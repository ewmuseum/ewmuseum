CCW (1998–2005) Card Archive

Show #30, May 30, 1999

30 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Full show script of a Cyberspace Championship Wrestling card from Washington, D.C. with a results list and match-by-match staging, dedicated at its head to the memory of a real wrestler who had died days earlier.
May 30, 1999 from The MCI Center in Washington, D.C. - Show #30 Supreme Machine vs. Brad Lamen - CCW Heavyweight & CCW Independent Championship Apocalypse vs. Niamouchu - CCW Trans-Atlantic Championship Bunny vs. Spit vs. The Goddess vs. Delilah vs. The Beast - CCW Women's Championship WAI vs. The Manics - CCW Tag Team Championship "Fat Ass" Phil Burner vs. Joquar - CCW Hardcore Championship The Konngo vs. Punjab "The Arabian Wonder" Thunder vs. Doomsday Evil Elf vs. Diablo Extreme vs. Ghettodogg Loki vs. Sam Frederick Dedicated to the memory of Owen Hart. From The MCI Center in Washington, D.C. CCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP - CCW INDEPENDENT CHAMPIONSHIP Supreme Machine vs Brad Lamen Brad came out along with Johnny Lamen as The Cramps blared over the loud speaker. The crowd booed at their entrance. SuMa came out, along with Thunder and Tim Inman. The crowd cheer loudly for the trio. Brad Lamen appeared to be no match for SuMa as SuMa bench pressed Lamen above his head and slammed him to the mat. Brad scurried around the ring evading the offense of SuMa and then catching SuMa in the back of the head with a spinning heel kick. Johnny Lamen climbed onto the apron and SuMa decked him with a right hook, sending him flying into the announcer's table. SuMa turned around to face Brad when Brad launced another spinning heel kick but SuMa ducked this one and swept the feet from underneat Lamen. SuMa picked Brad up and did the Supreme Bomb on Lamen. SuMa pinned him 1-2-3 to become the NEW CCW Independent Champion and retaining his Heavyweight Championship! All of WAI came out to celebrate the victory as The Lamens were run out of the arena by the presence of WAI. The crowd bawled incredibly loud as SuMa was carried back to the locker room on the shoulders of Thunder and Konngo. CCW TRANS-ATLANTIC CHAMPIONSHIP Apocalypse vs Niamouchu Apocalypse came out once again with Tim Inman and his customized belt to another enormous pop. Niamouchu entered the arena along with Jaysin Urban and the Goddess, who, was rather upset after losing in the Women's match. Niamouchu delivered an array of karate kicks to Apocalypse which eventually sent Apoc into the corner. Niamouchu wasted no time and delivered a running shoulder butt to Apoc. Apoc, still laying in the corner, moved out of the way when Niamouchu did a cartwheel into a flying elbow smash. Apoc took control and set Niamouchu up for a spinning back breaker. He then climbed ontop of Niamouchu and delivered a series of punches to his forehead. Niamouchu hooked the arms of Apoc with his legs and turned him over into a pin but got only a two count. As Apoc got up, Niamouchu leveled him with kamikazie bullet kick which sent him down to the mat. Niamouchu then climbed to the top and performed his Singapore Sault which allowed him the 1-2-3 pin on Apoc to become the NEW CCW Trans-Atlantic Champion! Apoc was rattled as he was helped up by Kaboink and TransSinister left the ring area with the custom TA title in hand. CCW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP WAI vs The Manics WAI came out first, along with Tim Inman. Inman had grabbed the mic as he entered the ring saying he had an announcement regarding the WAI stable. He formally kicked annouced he was kicking Samson out of WAI, then tossed the mic to the announcer. The Manics came out, along with Lady Cat and Bunny. The fireworks were bright with excitment. Klown started things off against Bob Burner as Phil rested from his previous Hardcore match. Things began slowly as Bob was being slowly worn down by Klown. Bob was able to do a quick tag to Phil as he was being thrown to the ropes and Phil nailed Klown from behind with a "Fat Ass" bulldog. Klown was down most of the match as WAI continuously kept Klown from making the tag to Pepper. Phil went to the top rope in an attempt to perform a HUGE flying ass flop but Pepper shook the ropes until the Fat Ass fell. Luckily, Klown rolled out of the way in enough time so Phil wouldnt crush him to death. Phil got right up from the fall as Klown made the tag to Pepper who went up top. Phil grabbed Klown by head and tried to get him up in the air but Klown blocked the suplex attempt as tripped Phil so that he fell on the mat and Pepper connected with a moonsault from the top rope. Bob came running in to make the save but Klown took him out with a well timed dropkick. 1-2-3.. NEW CCW Tag Team Champions, The Manics!!! The WAI sat in the ring in utter disgust as the Manics left the ring hugging and congratulating each other. CCW WOMEN'S CHAMPIONSHIP - 5 WAY DANCE Bunny vs Delilah vs Spit vs The Goddess vs The Beast The Goddess was eliminated by The Beast in the early going... Deliah was soon pinned after a beating by Spit... Bunny was pinned by The Beast after a Beer Buzz... Spit was the unfortunate victim of a super swami splash from the top rope by The Beast who pinned Spit for the 1-2-3. And NEW CCW Women's Champion, The Beast!!! CCW LIGHTWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP Apocalypse vs Mr. Gobstopper Apoc entered the ring with Tim Inman and his custom championship belt. The crowd went crazy as they chanted "three time champ, three time champ". Mr. Gobstopper came out to a more quiet crowd. Apoc went right to work on Gobstopper who was showing off his lightweight body to the fans. Apoc powerbombed Gobstopper as Mr. Gobstopper rolled himself out of the ring and onto the floor. Apoc came after Gobstopper and smashed his face into the steel railing holding back the fans. Apoc picked up a chair and clotheslined Gobstopper over the barrier with it. Once again, both men were counted out as the fans, this time, prevented either man from returning to the ring area. So, instead, they beat the living crap out of each other but it didn't end until Mr. Gobstopper ran off the other way and disappeared into the crowd. Apocalypse did not go after him as he felt that he didn't want to make a big deal of this count out, as he thought Mr. Gobstopper felt too. CCW HARDCORE CHAMPIONSHIP "Fat Ass" Phil Burner vs Joquar Burner came out with the fans cheers being louder than the music playing. He had a shovel and trash can in hand as he made his way into the ring. Joquar came out to a nice pop as he stopped at a ringside seat to give his mother a hug who was in attendance. As Joquar entered the ring, Burner nailed him with a shovel to the back. This sent Joquar flying back out of the ring to the floor. Burner went after him and tried to slam a trash can across his head but a boot to the mid section prevented that from happening as Joquar used the trash can on Burner. As Joquar remained in control using chairs, bricks, and glass bottles on Burner, Thunder made his way into the arena. As Joquar and Phil battled it out on the other side of the ring, Joquar's (and Thunder's) mother leaped from her ringside seat to stop Thunder from doing any kind of interference. While Thunder was arguing with his mother, Lord Gracemore came running out and took Thunder out, handcuffing him to the ring ropes and then beating him senseless. Joquar soon came over to Thunder and he, along with Gracemore and his mother, began taking turns whipping Thunder until his arse began bleeding. Completely forgetting about his match, Joquar was rolled up from behind by Phil Burner and was pinned 1-2-3. And NEW CCW Hardcore Champion, "Fat Ass" Phil Burner!!! Mid Card Matches Konngo vs Punjab "The Arabian Wonder" Konngo entered with Betsy the Camel. Punjab soon came running out and attacked Konngo on the way into the ring. Punjab was pissed as he kicked and punched his way all across Konngo's body. Konngo busted out with a loud lion-like roar and picked Punjab up and threw him down to the mat with a *thud*! The match was soon interrupted as the overwhelming sound of a camel cry filled the arena. Betsy was having a baby. As Konngo, Punjab, and all the fans watched the miracle of birth, Betsy popped the baby camel out onto the entrance way. After Betsy licked the birth fluid off of the baby camel, it was revealed that the baby camel was wearing a turban!! Punjab had the most puzzled look on his face as Konngo busted out in laughter. The fans began chanting "camel daddy" as Punjab shouted to the fans "Shut UP!!" Konngo rolled Punjab up from behind for the 1-2-3. After the match, Konngo walked by the camel, still laughing hysterically at the fact that the baby camel popped out wearing a turban. Punjab got his camels together and ran out of the arena. Thunder vs Doomsday Thunder entered the arena to a loud pop as he and SuMa, along with Inman, made their way to the ring. Doomsday soon came out, without Extreme, to a moderation of light cheers and boos. Doomsday tried to sneak up behind Thunder but Thunder was too smart for Doomsday as he grabbed Doomsday by the neck with a seriously mad look on his face. He then hoisted Doomsday above his head, as if to do a bench press, and launched Doomsday out into the crowd. Thunder went after him and the duo began wailing on each other as the referee reached the ten count, thus, calling for a double count out. The brawl continued into the locker room where SuMa, Inman, and the rest of WAI had caught up with each other and began wailing away on Doomsday. Under Card Matches The Evil Elf vs Diablo Diablo made his entrance into the arena and ignored the fans as he made his way into the ring. The lights went out and a green spotlight followed the Evil Elf to the ring as some mysterious music played. The crowd was mostly silent until the lights came back on and revealed an evil looking man in the center of the ring. The bell rang and Evil Elf went right to work on Diablo, sending him crashing to the outside. He then beat Diablo repeatedly with a 2x4 which was underneath the ring. Diablo was thrown back into the ring as Elf removed a pouch from his belt and poured some white powder looking dust from the pouch into his hand. He poured the powder into his mouth and amazingly, he spit out a green colored spew. As Elf crawled back into the ring, Diablo nailed with with repeated chops to the back. Diablo sent Elf to the ropes and delivered a spinebuster which put Elf down. Diablo went up top to perform a high flying leg drop but Elf rolled out of the way and Diablo landed ass first on the mat. As he got up in pain, Elf performed his Jawbreaker finishing move, pinning Diablo 1-2-3. Evil Elf quietly left the ring as his music played. Extreme vs Ghettodogg Prior to the Extreme/Ghettodogg match, Extreme and Doomsday entered the arena with The Cretin Family. As they entered the ring, Brad spoke into a mic. The Cretin family tag team had formed a Cretin Family stable with The Syndicate who revealed they were wearing Cretin Family t-shirts. The crowd booed as everyone climbed out of the ring except for Extreme. Ghettodogg then entered to a standing ovation of boos and name callings. Ghettodogg had a conversation about the fans on the way down the aisle with his Gangsta friends. Both Lamens had the look of evil in their eyes as they glared towards Ghettodogg as he was climbing in the ring. The bell rang and things got under way. Extreme surprisingly could not stand up to Ghettodogg as sent Extreme into the corner followed by a flying body press which brought Extreme out of the corner, dazed and dizzy, when Ghettodogg laid Extreme out with the Holocauster. 1-2-3, Ghettodogg gets his first ever victory in CCW. The Gangsta's, along with Ghettodogg, cheerfully exited the arena to the boos of the fans. Johnny Lamen went after them halfway up the ramp before turning back to join Brad in the ring along with Extreme and Doomsday. Brad angrily told The Syndicate that they were not Cretin Family material and that no one in CCW was worthy of being a part of the Cretin Family. The Lamens attacked The Syndicate and tore the Cretin tees from their bodies. Loki vs Sam Frederick Sam Frederick immediately went to work on Loki, sending him flying across the ring with a whip and bringing him back and meeting him with a flying clothesline. Loki was knocked to the outside, and tried to sneak a chair into the ring to use against Frederick but to no avail. Sam Frederick took Loki by the hair and slammed his face into the turnbuckle. This set Loki up for a bronco buster by Frederick but Loki moved out of the way, allowing Sam to bust his gonads on the turnbuckle. Loki dragged Frederick into the middle of the ring. Loki bounced off the ropes, accidentally knocking the ref off his feet for a few seconds. As the ref got back to his feet, Loki performed a leg drop to the gonads of Frederick, putting him in pain. Loki was able to cover Frederick for the 1-2-3. CCW Home
