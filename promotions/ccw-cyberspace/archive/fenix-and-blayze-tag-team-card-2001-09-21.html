What this is Tag team card for Fenix and Blayze listing their finishing move, double-team favourites and entrance music.
Name: Fenix and Blayze Members: Fenix & Blayze Combined Weight: 470 lbs. Theme Music: "Do You" by DMX Tag Team Record: 0-0-0 Championships: None Debut: September 2001 Finishing Move End Of The World - Blayze sets the opponent up for a backdrop and then Fenix hits an Elevated Inverted Front Facelock Swinging Neckbreaker on them. Trademark MovesUltimate Testimony - A Modified Severe Testimony in which Fenix hits the Cutter instead of Blayze The Eruption - A Stacked Version of the Lava Flow (Fisherman's Brainbuster DDT) off the top rope.
