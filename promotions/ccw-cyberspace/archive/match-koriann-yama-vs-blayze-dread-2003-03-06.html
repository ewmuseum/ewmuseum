CCW match: Koriann & Yama Tatsu vs. Blayze & Jason Dread

6 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Koriann & Yama Tatsu vs. Blayze & Jason Dread Date: 03/06/03 Show: Lockdown JD: Well, that definitely was quite the beating Devyl just took. And let me tell you, she's had that coming for long time now. BJS: Yeah right! You want to know the real reason why Ace attacked her like that? JD: **sighs** Fine. Enlighten me. BJS: Because Ace is a coward and NEEDS to hurt Devyl before the match. It's probably the only way he can win. JD: Well, either way, that match is coming up a little bit later on tonight. Now, we have our second to last Tag tournament match. BJS: Is there anyone good in this one? JD: Well... 'I'm Real' by J-Lo hits and Koriann comes walking out of the back in-between her golden spark shower. The fans boo the hell out of the jackass as she skips down to the ring, rolls inside, and pops back up screaming 'I RULE!!!' 'Meet The Creeper' by Rob Zombie hits next and Yama Tatsu walks out of the back to a decent-sized pop. Nayt Willems: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! First, weighing in at a combined weight of 353 pounds... KORIANN and YAMA TATSU! Yama enters the ring, glaring a bit at Koriann. Koriann returns the look, appraising her partner. She then rolls her eyes and can be heard saying... Koriann: Well, you're definitely no Brad Lamen, but I guess you'll do. Yama looks like he's ready to strangle her. BJS: Well, she does have a point. Yama Tatsu definitely is no Brad Lamen. Heh, he's no Koriann either. JD: Thank God. The two of them (Brad and Koriann) are almost more than any one federation can handle. 'War Ensemble' by Jedi Mind Tricks hits and the fans nearly go crazy and riot with boos and Blayze comes walking out of the back. He smugly struts down to the ring, ignoring the hate-filled fans. 'Just Got Wicked' by Cold hits next, and the jeers turn to cheers as Jason Dread exits from the back and heads down to the ring. Nayt Willems: And their opponents... Weighing in at a combined weight of 448 pounds... BLAYZE and JASON DREAD! The two men enter the ring, trading insults and trash talk with one another. Koriann laughs at one of the insults, and the two men turn and glare at her, causing her to shut up pretty quickly. BJS: Well... It's good to see that the tag teams we have out here are dysfunctional. JD: I was waiting for this match, actually. I'm curious to see how well Dread and Blayze will actually get along. BJS: And I'm waiting for Enigma to come down here and get some revenge on Blayze for what happened earlier. Did we ever found out if Enigma and Lacey are okay? JD: I heard that they were... But neither of them are in very good condition. Yama Tatsu gets ready to start for his team as Blayze gets ready for his. Chucky notes that everything is ready and signals for the bell. JD: Blayze and Yama with a quick lock-up... Blayze sends a knee up into Yama's stomach, and then wraps him up in a modified Sleeperhold of some sort... BJS: Looks like a choke to me... JD: Well, whatever it is, Yama is fighting his way out of it and... Tagging Koriann?! As soon as Yama broke Blayze's hold, he stepped over and tagged in Koriann. Koriann gives him a 'What the hell are you doing?' look as she steps into the ring. Yama ignores her as he takes her place at the corner of the ring apron. BJS: Yup... Koriann's in the match now. And check out Blayze! He's smiling from ear to ear. Blayze tells Koriann to come on forward and give it her best shot. And so she does. She steps right up to Blayze and punches him in the face. Blayze shrugs it off and grins at her. Koriann does it again, same result. And one more shot to the face... Blayze shrugs and laughs. Blayze turns his head to look at Jason... Blayze: Hey! Dread! I should tag you in so you can deal with her. You two are in about the same league! Haw, Haw, Haw! And a hard kick to the balls stops Blayze in mid-laugh as he falls to his knees in the ring, holding himself. Koriann quickly kicks Blayze in the face, knocking him onto his back, and then drops an elbow right onto his already injured groin. Blayze bucks around the ring, trying to shake off the pain. BJS: Woo-hoo! Have I ever told you that I like it when other people, not me, are hit in the balls? I think it's kinda funny. JD: You would. Koriann climbs up one of the turnbuckles and waits for Blayze to stop flopping around so much, and then flies into the air with and elbow aimed for Blayze's throat. She manages to connect, and Blayze rolls onto his stomach, coughing and gagging. Koriann stands up, and instead of pressing her attack, she places one foot on Blayzes backside, raises her hands into the air, and screams... Koriann: 'I RULE!!!' That is... Until Blayze grabs her foot and trips her off him. He stands, fire burning in his eyes, and drives his knee forcefully into koriann's gut, pinning her to the ground. His fingers wrap around her throat and he begins choking the life out of her. JD: Chucky's intervening now and pulling Blayze up and off of Koriann, and Blayze shakes himself free of the ref's grip. Looks like he's screaming something about how come low blows count now and choking doesn't. BJS: It's because ball shots are funny and choking isn't. JD: **sighs** Koriann is back up and punches Blayze repeatedly in the stomach. Blayze shrugs it all off and, no longer wanting to put up with this, plants Koriann with a Double Arm DDT into the canvas. JD: Nice Double Arm DDT by Blayze. And now it looks like he's trying to lock in The Last Breath on Koriann! He almost has in cinched in... BJS: Now would be a good time for Yama Tatsu to interfere and break up the hold. And... Action! Despite Slater's obvious attempt into goading Yama Tatsu to help his partner, he does nothing. He merely watches as Koriann tries to fight off the debilitating hold. JD: He almost has it... And Koriann reaches the bottom rope! Chucky forces Blayze to separate from Koriann. Blayze let's go, and then immediately leg drops Koriann before she even has a chance to catch her breath. JD: Well... Koriann isn't going to last much longer in there. She really needs to make a tag. BJS: I agree. But given the fact that Koriann and Blayze just locked up again, I seriously doubt she'll be making a tag anytime soon... Koriann, however, manages to maneuver her body to give herself a slight advantage, and then uses Blayze's momentum against him as she tosses him with an Arm Drag. Blayze immediately regains his footing, and then Backdrops Koriann as she foolishly charges him. Blayze shakes his head, unable to believe the morons he's constantly forced to wrestle against, and forcefully tags in Dread. Blayze: Get yo' ass in there. She's more your speed than mine. Dread climbs in, resists the urge to take Blayze's head off, grabs Koriann as she tries crawling for her corner, and then nearly destroys her with a Double Underhook Piledriver. JD: Dread is going for the pin on Koriann now... Chucky's down to make the count! 1... 2... 3... JD: NO! Yama Tatsu with the save at the last possible second! BJS: It's about time! Jesus... I almost thought Yama Tatsu was trying to throw the match... JD: Dread is up and he and Yama are exchanging lefts and rights! Yama's starting to gain the advantage, and here comes Blayze with a hard shot to the back of the head! Blayze starts stomping away at Yama Tatsu while Chucky once again attempts to restore order to the chaos. 'Meet The Creeper' suddenly hits and Kyle Thorne comes running out of the back, storming down to the ring with a chair in hand. JD: Now what the hell is this!? BJS: It's Kyle Thorne! Yama just broke this guy out of prison or something. JD: I know who it is, and Yama Tatsu did not BREAK this guy out of anything. Sylkk apparently got him released. But what...? In answer to Doyle's question, Thorne rolls into the ring and immediately smashes Dread over the head with the steel chair, right in plain view of Chucky. Chucky immediately calls for the bell, disqualifying Yama Tatsu and Koriann. Nayt Willems: Ladies and Gentlemen, your winners by disqualification... BLAYZE and JASON DREAD!!! The crowd buzzes about the result of the match. Blayze turns to see what all the commotion is about, and then takes a chair shot between the eyes as well. Yama Tatsu and Kyle Thorne quickly kick Dread and Blayze out of the ring. The winners stumble to their feet and, holding their heads, make their way up the ramp, trading insults with each other. Yama watches them go, and then signals for a microphone. Nayt tosses him one, and Kyle hands him the chair. Yama directs him to go and pick up Koriann, which he immediately does. Thorne applies an Arm Lock and jerks on her hair, trying to bring her back into the realm of consciousness. And once she's suitably awake, Yama addresses her... Yama: The reason I never bothered with this match was the fact I have an even more important match later tonight! .... And my partner was nothing but a piece of trash. Kyle pushes Koriann forward and Yama almost beheads her with the chair. The fans start booing Yama's brutality as he brings the chair down on her again and again and again. Yama: Women shouldn't be involved in Wrestling, but that's a different argument! She is a pathetic wrestler and the idea that she held the CCW Heavyweight title is a joke! You see Kyle and Me here, have decided to usher in the new era of this great sport! I found the best partner I possibly could and we plan to take this fed by storm! None of you in the back are exempt from our fury! Remember that Scorpion, and any other opponent the crosses OUR paths! Yama takes one more look at Koriann and smacks her unmoving body with the chair before he and Thorne exit the ring, the fans still booing. BJS: Jesus! What is it with all this brutality against women this show? I hope those damn women's rights organizations aren't watching. JD: No fear. This is wrestling. Those types don't watch us. Well, it definitely looks like Yama Tatsu is ready for his match later tonight. BJS: Yup. And here comes the paramedics to take care of Koriann. Speaking of women getting hurt, do you think Devyl's going to be okay for her match? View Koriann 's Biography Back
