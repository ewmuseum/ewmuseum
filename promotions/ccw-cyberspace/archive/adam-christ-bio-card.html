Title History: 1x CCW Tag-Team Champion Award History: Other Federations: Other Federation Awards: Quotes History On April 17th, 1977, Adam Christopher was reluctantly born in a hospital within Des Moines, Iowa. His mother, Joline Christopher, had been forced to spend months in labor and pregnant agony in order to have an unwanted child. The child forced into her from the penis of an unknown face. The child that would represent all that she hated. Joline was once beaten, raped, and impregnated... and Adam was the product of it all. Adam grew along with Joline's hate for him. Adam was never schooled as a child, so he had to learn human language through his mother. Joline, who has been abusing wellfare for a majority of her adult life, provided Adam with a trailer of which he will call home until he was 18. Adam didn't argue, of course, because it was all they had--and since he hadn't really seen the outside world beyond the trailer park and the grocery store a half-mile up the road, Adam didn't think it was that big of a deal. Adam was beaten by his mother and several other of her drunkard friends daily. He was raised to believe that he was the devils child, and was only allowed to drink water directly from the faucet and wait until everyone else was done eating before he could begin his meal. Adam wasn't allowed to pray, although his mother did all the time. Adam wasn't given any new clothes, so he had to make sure to take off his shirt before he got beaten in order to prevent blood stains. In due time, Adam got used to being beaten... it almost became as repetitive as waking up in the morning. It bothered him when he was younger, but when he reached the age of 6, he didn't care. He accepted that it was natural for him to be beaten, and that he was, indeed, Satan. Around the age of 8, Adam actually enjoyed the pain. There would come times that young Adam would remind his mother to punch him if he coughs too loud, or other mild things like that. And so, young Adam grew up to be a man. He grew into a, somewhat, redneck-like accent... but didn't really look the part. He cut his own hair when he had to, brushed his teeth with soap from time to time, and tried to keep his outside appearance clean while underneith his clothes, he was torn apart. In what seemed like a short time, Adam turned 18. Around that age, the police were beginning to suspect Joline of child abuse. It's a wonder why it took them 18 whole years to discover that something bad was going on, but one of the reasons was that Adam never told anyone. Adam let the pain sink in, and as time went bye, he got used to it... began to like it... and began to love it. To Adam, pain was love. Love was pain. The more pain his mother would give him, the more sin would bleed from his body. Adam loved pain. LOVED pain. When Joline became aware that the police were going to find out about Adam being abusive, she quickly turned her anger around and layed it all on Adam. She hurt Adam in places that she's never tried before. Places one would dare not think about. These new pains Adam felt caused tears to roll down his cheeks for the first time since he was a child. When Joline saw these tears, she told him never to cry. She told him that his tears were evil. Later that night, as Adam struggled to ignore the new pains and sleep, Joline shoved a butterknife in Adam's right eye. Adam quickly awoke, screamed, and passed out nearly automatically from the pain. When Adam woke up, he was on his back staring at a new ceiling. He was in the hospital in Des Moines once again. His head hurt. Badly. It was now 1995. A month or so went by, and Adam's flesh sealed itself over the empty socket that was once his right eye. Adam was forced to go into court and plead innocent over the murder of Joline Christopher. Adam was dazed and confused. According to the police, Adam used a butter knife to pry out both of Joline's eyes and let her bleed to death on the floor of the trailor. Then, once a neighbor called the police due to screams heard, Adam attempted to kill himself by prying out his own eyeballs, but passed out due to the pain. Adam was charged with the murder of his mother. When asked if he would plead innocent or guilty, Adam responded with a strong silence. By the end of the trial, the evidence was stacked against Adam, and because he was 18, he was sentanced to spend 8 years in prison. Adam never argued. At the end of Adam's sentance, he was 26 years old, and it was January of 2003. The first thing Adam did when out of jail was revisit the trailer park, but it wasn't there anymore. Adam was homeless. Around this time, CCW was reopening itself. Jason McCallister had just stepped into the office, and the whole place seemed chaotic. People were in frantic hurry and excitement to see Cyberspace Championship Wrestling stand up once again. Adam found a flyer on a telephone pole somewhere in Iowa, of which said 'CCW Lockdown - FIRST Show Since Reopening! See it NOW!' Adam went to the show, watched it alone, and was admired by it. ...It was just then that Adam had an idea. When the show was over and done with, Adam stayed behind as the crowd left. Adam snuck backstage, avoiding any guards, and found one of CCW's employees in the back. Adam told the employee of his interest in the job, and without question, Adam was hired. Adam didn't tell him anything about his past. Adam didn't tell him that he had any wrestling abilities whatsoever. Adam didn't tell him that he didn't have a wrestling background. Adam just told him that he was interested, and since CCW was in such need for new superstars, he was hired. Adam now has a home. Adam's home is CCW. Jason McCallister has no clue on what Adam has in store. CCW has no clue. CCW has no fucking clue.
