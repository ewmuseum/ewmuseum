Damien Nhyte Real Name: Damien Nhyte Birthdate: File Unavailable Hometown: The Trail Of Tears Height: 6'5 Weight: 265 Nicknames: The Dark One Status: Heel Entrance Music: Norma Jean - Memphis Will Be Laid To Waste Information Wrestling Style: Powerhouse/Brawler Set Up Move: The Sweetest Sleep (Damien Grabs His Opponent In A Standing Kata Hajime (Tazzmission) And Swings Them Around Like A Child And Throws Them Down On Their Stomach And Steps Over Their Fallen Body Into A Camel's Clutch Submission) Finisher: The Good Nhyte Kiss (Powerbomb From Inside Of Ring Over The Top Rope To Outside Through An Awaiting Table) Title History: N/A Award History: N/A Other Federations: GW, DIWF, AXA, AXW, MHWF, IWF, WCWF, WXWF Other Federation Awards: GW Platinum Champion, DIWF Most Under-Rated Wrestler Of 2002, DIWF Great Brittain Champion, DIWF Television Champion, DIWF Tag Team Champion, AXA Television Champion, MHWF Television Champion, IWF Television Champion (2x), WCWF United States Champion, WXWF Heavyweight Champion, WXWF Hardkore Champion, WXWF Tag Team Champion Quotes History Damien was born and raised in Charlotte, North Carolina to his loving father and mother. They were the picture perfect family that everybody dreamed of having. Not one flaw could be found in the Nhyte family. Until one Sunday afternoon, sometime when Damien was 12, his father got in a horrible car accident coming home from his job. Damien's father passed away immediately. This devastating event sent Damien's smiles and hopes down the drain. How can a family be perfect, like they once were, without a father? Damien had to be 'the man' of the house and he took some chores that his father used to do. His mother counted on Damien. But he wasn't too thrilled because his schoolwork and other work was falling from his grasp. He went to school and concentrated on his schoolwork, then went back home and did his chores. He never had time for any friends. So Damien became lonely and shy in school. When Damien was in High School, his only friend was the gym. He would constantly go in the gym to build his physical stature because he felt that maybe a girl would want him for his looks instead of him. So, Damien continued to lift weights each and every day after school. He lifted for years and became the strongest student in his High School in Charlotte. But still, Damien had no friends nor a girlfriend. Damien still hurt over his father's death...but when Damien was 17, his Mother passed away from Ovarian Cancer. He then gave up. He gave up on what he did have and gave up on his future. He had nothing to live for. He didn't have his father, his mother, or any friends. Damien moved in with his Uncle, who was a former professional wrestler who was on the verge of being in the WWF, but he never had the chance because he had a back injury. So he was forced to put his boots away. Damien graduated High School and attempted to get into a good college, but his grades were very low because of his troubles at home. He then thought about joining the United States Marine Corps. But his Uncle told him he would train him, but Damien wasn't too interested in 'fake wrestling'. So his Uncle nagged him until Damien agreed. His Uncle didn't want 'talent to go to waste'. Damien had the physical status of a Pro Wrestler, but he didn't have the moves...and that is what his Uncle wanted to teach him. He brought Damien down to his basement and laid out some wrestling mats, and everyday, he would train Damien and make Damien train to be stronger and healthier. Pretty soon, Damien was on the verge of busting out of this shell called containment in the professional wrestling business. His Uncle called up a federation called the IWF, The International Wrestling Federation. Damien had his first match against a newcomer to the ring named, T-Bone. In Damien's debut match, he debuted as a wrestler named Acid. He made short work of T-Bone and finished the match with a neck-shattering piledriver...his Uncle's patented manuever. From then on, Acid was feared in the IWF. He had legends offer spots to wrestle with them...but Damien declined. That he wanted to be a legend and not be known to wrestle with a legend. Damien won the IWF International Television Title and then lost it when he was jumped by the IWF Heavyweight Champion in a title match. Damien gathered all of his strengths up and went back and fought against Lujai Saduj, Acid's Rival and defeated Saduj for the IWF INTV Title once again. Acid was called upon by the new IWF Heavyweight Champion, Mr. Happy to be his tag partner. Damien accepted. And together, they felt that there was more to wrestling then fighting nobodies...so they left the IWF in search for competition. They found competition in an elite federation called AXA. Mr. Happy changed his name to Shale and together, Shale and Acid defeated numerous opponents, including Lujai Saduj for the AXA Television Title. The AXA could not afford costs and went backrupt. So Shale and Acid were free agents in the wrestling world. A legend in the wrestling world, Salaok, a man who led the GW in fear seen the work that Shale and Acid have done, so Salaok signed them up in The Nevermore, GWF's Main Heel Stable. Then on, Acid and Shale became the #1 Contenders for the GWF Tag Team Titles, but lost against Cold Blood, Jay Robson and Derek Frost. Shale got more attention then Damien, so they both went on their own ways. Shale changed to be Silent Wind in the GWF and Damien went from Acid in GW to his real name in the DIWF. In the DIWF, Damien was undefeated for many months. Nobody could stop him. Damien defeated Devastation and ended Devastation's 3 month long winning streak for the DIWF Television Title. Damien was noticed by the DIWF's main heel stable called, The Heartless Bastards. And in the Heartless Bastards, Damien was always the rookie and he was always in second place. In The Heartless Bastards, Damien's talent was contained because the leader of the stable, Brimstone was the Heavyweight Champion and another wrestler in the HB was the United States Champion, Kurt Wolf. So Damien went on a journey in the Tag Team Division with one of the best wrestlers Damien has ever seen in the world named Marcus Stanton. Damien and Stanton defeated Driscoll and Devastation to become the DIWF Tag Team Champions. A couple weeks later, the Heartless Bastards broke up and Damien went on his own way with Stanton. Stanton became the DIWF Heavyweight Champion and Damien entered a tournament for the new title, The DIWF Great Britain Championship. Damien won the GB Title and then left the DIWF and went back to the GWF. which is now known as the GW. He worked as a wrestler named Sage Sabbot, a man from Beijing, China. Damien defeated his former mentor, Salaok and Salaok's brother, Quartz to become the GW Platinum Champion. Sage never got another opportunity and lost the GW Platinum Title to Sean Price. Damien soon left the GW and laid dormant in his home for a couple months, where he met a lovely woman named Meredith. He loved her more than anything in the world. She filled the spot where his mother and father's love once was. Damien married her and now, Meredith manages Damien. Jason Dynasty, an elite wrestler in the GW walked up to Damien in the hall one day and asked him how he would like to leave the GW and to go to a better federation, the CCW, and to win some gold. Damien agreed, and now, Damien and Dynasty are in the CCW as the Disciples Of Death. Their future lies in their hands...what will Damien make of himself in the CCW? Wait to find out.
