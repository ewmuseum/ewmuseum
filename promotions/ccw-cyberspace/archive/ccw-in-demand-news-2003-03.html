CCW In Demand: News and Rumours, March 2003

1 – 21 March 2003 (four dated items)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
News / Rumours Stop Press -- Stop Press -- Stop Press Posted Friday, March 21, 2003 Folks the final card for next week's Pay Per View has been released and it looks to be one of the best CCW brand events ever. Matches signed are: Hardcore Title Match Enigma vs Jack Manson Mud Wrestling Match Melody Widmark vs Koriann Tag Title - TLC Match Adam Christ & Jack Manson vs Scorpion & Michael Stevens Singles Match Devyl vs Gabe Nyte Indy Title - Strap Match (Strapped to each other at the wrist) Scorpion vs Dan Logan Women's Title Lacey vs Sheena Trans-Atlantic Title Michael Stevens vs Gobbestopper vs Ace Hawk Heavyweight Title Blayze vs Dread We will have a special CCW ID Preview of Revolution over the next few days with our thoughts and predictions on all matches. Update Posted Monday, March 17, 2003 Welcome to another edition of CCW ID faithfull readers. All columns have been recently updated along with a few new additions to the ID fold. First up we are going to recap CCW's newest TV show CCW Answers. This is an exciting new prospect for CCW and means even greater main stream meadia presence for the CCW brand. Next up the editors in all their worldy wisdom have seen fit to offer column inches to one Larry Knowledge. Those familiar with Larry's previous publication the CCW Star will no doubt be fully aware of this mans work. To those of you who do not know him, all we here at CCW ID would like to point out is "the views of Larry Knowledge do not necessarily represent the views of CCW ID or its other employees". You have been warned readers. What with all that an awesome looking Lockdown card this week and Re-volutions just around the corner CCW is certainly generating some momentum right now; and we here at ID have a feeling that this is only just the start STOP PRESS -- STOP PRESS -- STOP PRESS Posted Monday, March 3, 2003 As the inaugural edition of CCW ID went to press we received word here that CCW president Sylkk has held a very lengthy meeting with his lawyers. The main topic of discussion it seems was none other than FORMER CCW owner Spider. Exactly what the outcome of the meeting is as yet unclear, but we will do our best to get more information as soon as it becomes available. Latest News Posted Saturday, March 1, 2003 This weeks Lockdown card has now been posted and folks what a show it looks set to be. President Sylkk has declared a Tag Team Lethal Lottery and the teams drawn have already started to cause a storm. Enigma & Lacey Skye have been drawn to face the combination of Forme Carlos & CCW's Hardcore Champion Brad Douglas. Toxic & GAbe Nyte take on the bizzare pairing of Adam Christ & Jack Manson CCW Legend Michael Stevens teams up with Scorpion to take on Sheena & Minx And in 2 matches that promise to be showstoppers Koriann & Yama Tasu meet Blayze & Jason Dread whilst Brad Lamen & Devyl take on Ace Hawk & The Gobstopper. What will happen when these combination of men and women get together? Who knows? But we here at CCW ID do know that you wont want to miss a minute of the action. Also this Thursday the Independant Title is up for grabs as both Scorpion and Yama Tatsu have to suck it up for a second time that evening in what promises to be a classic.
