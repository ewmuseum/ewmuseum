His dad committed suicide soon after Cyrus was he born. He was looked after by two brothers who took him in as their own. They taught him how to fend for himself as they lived on the streets. Cyrus was not educated like normal children, he learnt by watching television through shop windows and reading books in the library. He was told by one of the brothers he was built for fighting. This gave Cyrus the idea of fighting in pubs for money. He fought hard until he had enough money to get to America. Whilst over there he continued to fight until he had enough money to join a wrestling school. He did well learning all there was about wrestling. But he was dismissed, because of his split personalities. One minute he was cool, calm and collected. The next moment he was vicious and angry. One day he snapped whilst he was practicing some moves with his training buddy. He pounded him to within an inch of his life. He was thrown out of the school for not being able to control his twin personalities. He then started to look for a contract. He found one in the CCW.
