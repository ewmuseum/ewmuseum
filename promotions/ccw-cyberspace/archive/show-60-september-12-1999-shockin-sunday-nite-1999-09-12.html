Show #60 - September 12, 1999 (Shockin' Sunday Nite)

12 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Staged results script of the first Shockin Sunday Nite, show #60 from Madison Square Garden, with a twelve-man battle royal for the Independent title, a Samson-Chang match and the Heavyweight title main event.
September 12, 1999 from Madison Square Garden in New York City, New York - Show #60 Apocalypse vs. Supreme Machine - CCW Heavyweight Championship Kaboink d. Mr. Gobstopper, Pain Lord, Jim "Klown" Bowe, Pepper The Cat, Samson, Ben Frederick, Apocalypse, Carlos The Mexican Midget, El Mierda, Sid Powers, J.C. Superstar - Battle Royal - CCW Independent Championship Samson vs. Chang The Mongolian Giant Shockin Sunday Nite Results - Sept. 12, 1999 Sunday, 12-Sep-1999 23:22:07 [detail withheld] writes: Ladies and Gentlemen! Welcome once again to another great CCW event and welcome to the first ever edition of Shockin Sunday Nite! We are coming to you from Madison Square Garden in New York City, just a few minutes from the world famous Empire State Building and The Statue Of Liberty! Tonight, Samson will face Chang one on one! Also, the 12 Man Battle Royal for the Independent Championship AND the CCW Heavyweight Title will be on the line when Apocalypse defends his title against former champ, Supreme Machine! Folks, let's not delay any longer as here comes Samson!!! *Tunnelwalk begins playing as Samson emerges from the curtains* The fans explode in cheers as "Tunnelwalk" plays. Samson comes out, a look of determination on his face. He strides to the ring, pumping himself up, hopping up and down and hitting himself in the head and stretching his arms. *The Contender begins playing as Chang comes down led on a leash by SuMa* Chang now, making his way to this match. How can such a monster the size of Chang be controlled by such a smaller man as SuMa? The match begins and Chang lunges at Samson, but he gets out of the way! Samson then charges Chang! They lock it up...and it's a deadlock! Samson matches strength with Chang! Supreme Machine (who is at ringside with his remote control) can't believe it! He sends and electrical charge to Chang's collar, infuriating Chang and he grabs Samson! He's got him up in the air for a Gorilla Press Slam! Samson gets up off the mat slowly as Chang just stands there sneering. Samson slinks around the ring. Chang gets frustrated and lunges at Samson, missing him and Samson bounces him off the ropes and puts the monster on the ground with a cross body block! he just flew right into him! SuMa is pissed, and ups the juice on the controller. Chang grabs Samson by the legs and swings him around several times before he slams him down to the ground! The force of the slam was so big that Samson is almost out of it! Samson rolls out of the way of a stomp from the killer beast and retreats to a corner to recuperate while the dumb animal looks for him. SuMa yells at him, and Samson realizes that he has to do something. He charges at Chang, but goes past him and does a suicide dive onto SuMa, knocking him out cold! Samson grabs the controller from SuMa and smashes it against the concrete! Chang loses the dazed look and rips off the collar. Chang jumps out of the ring and starts toward the crowd! But Samson's in the way! Samson reels the big thing back with heavy rights and lefts! Then keels him over with a Russian Leg Sweep! Samson gets back in the ring and Chang angrily follows him. Chang takes a huge swing and connects with it! Samson should have been more prepared for that move. He could have had him down if he would not allow for Chang to reach his feet. Chang now, with Samson off the ropes, Oh my! Chang has him in a bear hug now! The ref checks... Samson is hanging in there! Oooh, Samson is slammed to the mat! He's being brought to his feet now, Samson rakes the eyes of Chang and Chang goes back a step. Samson with lefts, rights, lefts, rights, and Samson is gonna try... Can he do it? YES! Samson has Chang in the Samson's Vengeance but he's fallen over, his back must have given out from all the weight! Chang to his feet first and he has Samson by the neck! Up and DOWN! A choke Slam!!! The cover now by Chang... 1-2-3! Chang has defeated Samson, and this time, on his own without the aide of SuMa's little electronic devices! Could Chang be the next Trans-Atlantic Champion, or even Heavyweight Champion? All shall be revealed in due time! Let's take you now backstage where J.C. Superstar and GOD are standing by for an interview! GOD: Son, you gotta be a madman out there tonite. It's every man for himself and you've got no one to watch your back. Pick your spots in the ring and capitalize off any mistakes the others are bound to make. JC: I understand what I must do. You have trained me to become one of the greatest wrestlers in CCW history, and I WILL BE VICTORIOUS. I may not walk away with the belt, but I sure as hell will go down fighting. So gentlemen, you better say your prayers because your about to suffer a "Fall from Grace"... GOD: Whoa, whoa! Hold on there pal, don't give them any clue as to what you're about to unleash out there...just whoop some ass and take some names. JC: As you wish.....it is time. **JC rises from his seat and prepares to make his way to the ring for what will be one of the most exciting matches in the storied history of the CCW** Interesting comments from the religious ones! Nine men are in the ring now as our final combatants are about to make their way to the ring for the start of the match! *The arena goes black and the crowd is restless. Red flames erupt from each corner of the ring as "Hero of the Day" bursts from the speakers. Wasting no time, JC Superstar sprints to the ring ready for action.* The final contestants for the Battle Royal are coming out, and they just happen to be Apocalypse and Kaboink! The two enter to their theme music, "Crazy Train", and give fans high fives as they rush down to the ring. The brothers slide into the ring, and the referee signals for the bell to start things off! Wait a damn minute.... Apocalypse has just leaped over the top rope of the ring! He's taken himself out of the match! Wait a second, he's coming down to the announcer's table.... Apocalypse, what are you doing? This is your chance at being the first ever CCW Grand Slam Champion! Apocalypse: I've got more important things to do tonight, JR. Whoopin' SuMa's ass again, for one thing. Besides, I've got great confidence that my brother will bring the IND Title back to the family again tonight. ROSSENDALE: Oh! What a clothesline by JC Superstar on Pain Lord! Apocalypse: I've seen better. ROSSENDALE: A-Pac, everyone wants to know what's going on with you and Kaboink. Why have you suddenly turned your backs on the Tribe, the manics, and your women? Apocalypse: You're just like everybody else, JR. *Apocalypse starts talking in a whiny five-year-old's voice* Boo, hoo! W-w-w-why are you d-d-d-doin' this? (sniff, sniff!) D-d-d-don't you l-l-l-love K-kelly and L-l-lady Cat? *Apocalypse resumes his normal, sexy voice.* We're tired of it, JR. From now on, only one thing matters to us: ourselves. Now if you'll excuse me, I have a little business to attend to. ROSSENDALE: Wait a sec... where's he going?! Oh, no! Apocalypse is grabbing a chair from ringside! He's calling out to his brother! Kaboink turns around, and Apocalypse tells him something! Kaboink turns toward Gobstopper, who is beating on El Mierda! Kaboink spins Gobber around and is doing a number on the IND Champ! A left, a right, and now he slings him to the ropes! Apocalypse is up on the apron, and.... GOODNIGHT, GOBSTOPPER! A-pac leveled Gobber with a chair shot! Kaboink lifts Gobstopper's body up, and so long, Gobber! It's the Kablooie, and Gobber goes headfirst into the third row of the audience! Mr. Gobstopper has been eliminated by Kaboink! You know what this means! A new CCW Independet Champ will be crowned tonight here in NYC! JC Superstar charging Kaboink, who is facing Gobstopper on the outside, but turns in time to catch JC and fling him over the top rope! Kaboink has also eliminated JC Superstar!!! Pain Lord going to work on Pepper The Cat now and Ben Frederick has joined in a two on one against Pepper! Wait a second! Pain Lord has turned the tides and is wailing away on Ben! An atomic drop crushes the butt bone of Ben and now he's been scooped up! Ben Frederick is outta here! He's been eliminated by Pain Lord! Pain Lord and Pepper The Cat now snickering at Ben from the inside and look out guys! Kaboink incoming! Double clothesline from behind sends Pepper and Pain Lord over the top rope and onto the floor! Kaboink has eliminated two more! Hey! Here comes Scotty The Hotty from underneath the ring! He has no business out here! He's up on the apron and look out Scotty! Jim Bowe has tossed Sam Frederick toward Scotty, and woah! Scotty pulls down the top rope and Sam Frederick goes tumbling over! I guess you could say Sam eliminated himself! Sam gets to his feet and gives chase to Scotty, but Sam is clobbered from behind by a masked man from beneath the ring. Another masked man coming out from the back, and he's attacking the downed Sam Frederick! Scotty now calling the men off and the trio leave the arena. Kaboink with Powers now, and there goes Powers! Mierda charging the unbalanced Kaboink and over goes Kaboink! Wait! He's held on! Kaboink back in the ring now, Mierda thinks he's eliminated Kaboink, but think again! Kaboink rushes Mierda from behind, up, and DOWN! He lifts Mierda and tosses him out! Carlos trying to sneak up on Kaboink but is knocked silly by Klown! Klown tosses him to the ropes and back body drops him over the top rope! Kaboink now, locking up with Klown. Klown maneuvers around to the back of Kaboink but Kaboink lets out a ferocious fart! Good DAMN! Kaboink now, with Klown off the ropes and powerslam! Kaboink scoops Klown off his feet and after a few pushes and struggling, Klown goes over! Here is your winner! And NEW CCW Independent Champion, KABOINK!!! WoW! Unbelievable battle royal! We have a new Independent Champion! Surely Mr. Gobstopper is going to have something to say to Kaboink about how he was eliminated and how he would have been champion if not for Apocalypse and Kaboink! Well now, it's time for the Heavyweight Championship. The ring is now cleared and here he comes! *The Contender plays* Here comes The Supreme Machine, former Heavyweight Champion! He's looking to take down Apocalypse once and for all and reclaim his title! *Crazy Train plays* The ref rings the bell, and we're underway! Boy, no love has been lost between these two mammoths in the CCW! The two stride toward each toher and meet in the center of the ring, and here comes some trash talking! A shove from SuMa, and another from Apocalypse! SuMa goes for another shove, but A-pac grabs his arm and slams him to the mat! He takes the Armbar Takedown into a Crippler Crossface! Oh, goodness! The ref checks if SuMa can continue, and he can. A-Pac now lifting SuMa to his feet, but on the way up SuMa retaliates with a forearm to the genitals! Oucheez, that'll leave a mark! SuMa now with stomps to the sides and back of Apocalypse, and he's just ebating the Heavyweight champ down to the mat! SuMa lifts the man to his feet, and he's signalling that he wants it overwith! He's going for the Supreme Bomb, and A-pac goes up..... and counters with a Facebuster on SuMa! Where did that come from??? Both men are laid out on the mat, and Apocalypse slowly makes his way over to the turnbuckle, and he's ripping the pads off them! The ref is trying to make him stop, but this a no DQ match! The ref is powerless! Apocalypse makes his way back to SuMa, and he's taking him right over to the turnbuckles, as expected. A-pac grabs the back of SuMa's head, and he repeatedly slams him face-first into the exposed turnbuckle, three, four, five times! SuMa is dazed, but Apocalypse is back on the offensive, going to the middle exposed turnbuckle, three, four, and five times again! SuMa is in La-La Land, and Apoaclypse turns him around and delivers a vicious Floating DDT onto the botton exposed turnbuckle! SuMa's forehead is bleeding profusely. He seems to be unconscious, and blood is flowing onto the mat! Apocalypse is now sitting on SuMa's chest, giving him the one-fingered salute right in his face! What's he saying? "You wanted no DQ, motherf---er! I got your no DQ right here, you little shitball! You hear me, SuMa?!" WOW! Apocalypse is REALLY pissed off! Apocalypse is now on his feet, and he's bringing an apparently KO'ed SuMa to his also. A boot to the stomach, adn Apocalypse goes off the ropes and connects with a Swinging Neckbreaker! Here's a cover... 1... 2.... NO! Apocalypse let SuMa up! He's shaking his finger, and this man wants to really send a message to the Supreme Machine! That might cost Apocalypse later in the match, because he clearly had SuMa right where he wanted him! Apoc now with SuMa in a suplex, but SuMa blocked it! Oh dear! The Supreme Bomb! It's been reversed into the Supreme Bomb!!! 1-2-3!!! The Supreme Machine has become the NEW CCW Heavyweight Champion!!! Folks, what a night it's been! I hope you will tune in to TNME this week for more action and possibly, another night of awesemity! Good Night! [name withheld] CCW Home
