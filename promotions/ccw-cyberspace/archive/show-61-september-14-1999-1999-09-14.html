Show #61 - September 14, 1999

What this is Staged Tuesday Night’s Main Event results script for show #61, with Tag Team, Women’s and Hardcore title matches, a handicap match and several storyline interference angles.
TUESDAY NIGHT'S MAIN EVENT September 14, 1999 from The Nassau Veterans Memorial Coliseum in Uniondale, New York - Show #61 Hardcore Gorilla Mask vs. Chang The Mongolian Giant - CCW Hardcore Championship The Manics vs. El Mierda & Carlos The Mexican Midget - CCW Tag Team Championship Delilah vs. Kelly Bowe - CCW Women's Championship "Fat Ass" Phil Burner vs. Supreme Machine The Konngo vs. The Disciples - Handicap Match Thunder vs. Ben Frederick Hard Candy vs. Boneless Skeleton Freaks Bathsheba vs. Jezebel TNME Results - September 14, 1999 Wednesday, 15-Sep-1999 18:22:01 [detail withheld] writes: Welcome to another edition of TNME coming to you from the MCI Center in Downtown Washington, DC. Tonight Bill Clinton was seen giving head to Bob Dole, but that's no reason to stay away from the arena tonight as the Manics are set to defend their titles against El Mierda and Carlos TMM! But they really won't defend them, the deal is the titles for Lady Cat. In other title action, HCGM will defend the Hardcore title against Chang The Mongolian Giant! And Delilah will defend against Kelly Bowe! "Fat Ass" Phil Burner will face The Supreme Machine in non-title action! Let's take you now to the action! Bathsheba vs Jezebel *Bathsheba's music begins playing* We're starting the TNME card off tonight with a women's match. Bathsheba coming to the ring now. Some may wonder if the women's division is going to pick up now that we have an all-time high number on the women's roster. Well, all I can say is maybe. *Jezebel's music begins playing* Mrs. Punjab is making her way to the ring. No doubt that this little lady packs a powerful punch, and is gold in her early future? There's the bell and here they go! Smack! Jezebel delivers a smacking slap across Bathsheba's face! Look at that red mark! Bathsheba yanks Jezebel down by the hair and goes to work on her pretty little face. Bringing her to her feet now, Bathsheba slings Jezebel off the ropes and plants her to the mat with a spinebuster! Bathsheba, having better luck this show, has Jezebel up and DOWN HARD! 1-2-3! Bathsheba has gained her first CCW victory tonight over Jezebel! Hard Candy vs Scotty The Hotty & Carnage *Scotty's music begins playing* This is sure to be an interesting matchup! Scotty The Hotty, formerly High Flying Scott Waller, and his partner Carnage, are looking to make a name for themselves here tonight. If they can obtain a victory over the long-time CCW vets, they surely will move up in tag team competition. *Men In Tight's begins playing* Holy Hard Candy Batman! Here they com... wait a second! Someone is coming from the crowd and he's... THEY have blocked off the entrance for Hard Candy! There's four men dressed in black with masks and they're attacking Hard Candy! The beat down is over but they have helped Hard Candy into the ring and Scotty & Carnage go to work now on the limp bodies of Hard Candy. Scotty picks up Gobbstopper and puts him on the top turnbuckle. Meanwhile Carnage is punching the daylights out of Rancher, he picks him up into a hard powerbomb. Scotty then dropkicks Gobbstopper off the turnbuckle and onto the floor. Scotty turns to Rancher, and both Scotty & Carnage pick him up into a double suplex. Scotty distracts the ref and Carnage chokes Rancher. Back outside the masked men have the injured Gobbstopper surrounded and are taking turns punching him. Inside Scotty has climded to the top and Carnage has picked Rancher up into a powerbomb. Carnage holds the Rancher up still in the powerbomb and Scotty has signaled for his special, the Waller Legdrop. Scotty jumps and connects perfectly just as Carnage powerbombs the Rancher. But what is this? GOD makes his way ringside and jumps to the apron, drawing the ref's attention away from the match. Out of the crowd bursts JC with a foreign object in hand. With the ref's back turned to him, JC takes out Carnage with a quick right and does the same to Scotty. Unwrapping the top turnbuckle, JC repeatedly slams Scotty's face into the exposed metal cable, causing irreparable damage to his face. As GOD continues his distraction, JC goes back to work on Carnage on the outside until he is unconscious and then throws his battered body in to the ring. He's placing Jolly Rancher over Carnage! GOD leaves the ringside area and the ref begins his count! 1-2-3!! Hard Candy has won the match thanks to J.C. Superstar and GOD!!! This is merely a taste of things to come, I betcha! Scotty The Hotty is looking rather pissed and goes off chasing after JC Superstar along with the four masked men. Carnage in the ring now, Gobstopper is up and is kicking the crap out of Carnage! Rancher pulls Gobber away and they leave the ring together in victory. Thunder vs Ben Frederick *The Contender begins playing* Well, I haven't heard much out of this guy in the longest of times! He may have some ring rust but I'm sure compared to Ben Frederick, Thunder will have a cakewalk! *Ben Frederick's music begins* Ben may be an under carder at this point, and may prove weak against the likes of Thunder, but you can be assured that Ben Frederick will rise to the occassion later on in his third attempt at stardom in CCW. Here we go as the ref rings the bell! Thunder and Ben lock up. Ben reaching around and putting Thunder in a hold from the back. Thunder reverses it and delivers a back suplex. To the feet now, Thunder driving his knees into the leg of Ben with sky high knee drops. Thunder picking Ben up by the feet now, and, and, and... slingshot into the turnbuckle!!! Thunder with the pin... 1-2-NO! Ben barely escapes the pin as Thunder brings him to his feet. He sets him up, OH! The Thunder From Down Under!!! Referee out of position! 1...... 2...... 3! He got him down anyhow! Thunder has won this match by pinfall! Now that Thunder has gotten himself back on track, what is his next move? Is he going to go after the Heavyweight title, or go for les glamorous titles? One things for sure, he won't have an easy time doing so. The Konngo vs J.C. Superstar and GOD - Handicap Match *JC and GOD sit backstage before their match with Konngo reviewing strategy for their bout** JC: GOD, I don't think Konngo knows what he's up against tonite. I have proven myself a worthy opponent for any wrestler in my brief career here in CCW and now he wants to fight you too?? He's definitely not the sharpest knife in the drawer, seeing as how you're the biggest man in CCW. GOD: Well my apprentice, the Konngo will soon see what this 7'5" 500lb. man can do. And when he is unfortunate enough to experience the "Fall from Grace", he'll wish had never heard the names JC Superstar and the GOD. **both men begin to laugh at the thought of the Konngo getting choke slammed from the top rope** JC: Looks like tonite is the night that we send a shockwave rippling through the CCW....one that will strike fear into the hearts of any wrestler who dares oppose us. GOD: Patience my friend...patience. We have waited for our time and that time is now. This will be a night to rememeber. **both men rise from their seats and make their way to the ring as JC's theme music blasts from the arena's sound system* Well folks, earlier tonight El Mierda was seen waiting in his 1974 Green Buick with Carlos The Mexican Midget. JC Superstar and GOD drove up into the garage and when they stepped from their car, Mierda gained JC's attention while Carlos snuck up from behind and played a round of golf with JC and GOD's nuts, sending them both down in utter pain. Carlos and JC nailed their hands to their crosses they were carrying before calmly entering the arena backstage. Now, I dunno if their hands were nailed for fact, it was rather dark back there. But apparently, it had no affect on either of them because they seem to be in good health and spirits tonight! JC and GOD are now in the ring awaiting their opponent. *The Konngo's music begins* Konngo, the current TA champ, is now entering the ring and JC and GOD go to immediate work. Folks, keep in mind this match is following traditional tag team rules so one of these atheletes is gonna have to step! JC will start things off as Konngo whips him into a turnbuckle. Konngo dishing out the chops to the chest of JC before sending him to the mat. Konngo going up top, something unheard of from him! He leaps and JC rolls out of the way into his corner!!! The tag is made and here comes GOD! GOD with Konngo back on his feet whips him into the ropes and OH DAMN! Konngo has leveled GOD! He's got him in the African Grey Ape Skull Crusher!!! JC from behind on Konngo, but it has no affect! Look at the intense look in Konngo's eyes! He grabs JC and launches him out of the ring! Unbelievable strength! Back to GOD now and the cover, 1-2-3! The Konngo has lived up to his title expectations and defeated current #1 Contender J.C. Superstar. "Fat Ass" Phil Burner vs Supreme Machine - Non-Title Match *Sweet Home Alabama begins playing* Here comes the Porker's Champion! The fans really love this big tub of goo! What's this? No Shovel? That's odd. *The Contender begins playing* Here comes the Supreme Machine, the Heavyweight Champion of CCW! These two bohemaths have been long destined to have a fair match against each other. This one has begun as Phil Burner delivers the boots to SuMa as he slides into the ring and the bell sounds. Burner with several fists to SuMa's face but someone seems to be trying to break into the arena backstage! Looks like we have tons of security trying to hold back the intruders! Burner now with a whip to the ropes and he nails SuMa with a shoulder butt! Those guys trying to break in, well, they're probably some rejects from that federation up north. Phil now bringing SuMa to his feet but SuMa is fighting back with lefts and rights to the ribs of Fat Ass! Left, right, left and rights! SuMa off the ropes and a clothesline! Burner doesn't budge! Another one, Burner is forced back a step! A third one makes Burner stagger! Here comes a fourth one and PHIL GOES DOWN! SuMa dropping an elbow and another! The crowd is pumped, they don't know who to chant for! SuMa with Fat Ass to his feet and wait one hot minute! Two men have rushed the ring and are beating on SuMa! The ref calls for the bell and Phil Burner has been DQ'd! Officials now are in the ring with security and are ushering the two men out of the ring! Who are those two men!? They are wearing colorful masks and I can't tell without seeing their face! Security now has the intruders near the entranceway and... OH MY GOD! They've removed their masks! It's Brad and Johnny Lamen!!! What in the hell are they doing here!? Well, that was an interesting twist to things tonight. What is do we have instore for later on? Who knows but soon enough! Delilah vs Kelly *Delilah's music begins playing* Here comes the women's Champ. I seriously don't think that Delilah will walk out of this match without her title. *Kelly Bowe's music begins* Kelly now making her way out along side of her is her brother Jim Bowe. There's the bell and this one is underway. Delilah with a surprise dropkick to Kelly which sends her down on her bum. Now with a whip into the ropes, Delilah hoists Kelly up on the return and slams her forward to the mat, back first for a sidewalk slam! Delilah is going up top and it's looking like the One Night Stand! YES! 1-2-3!!! Your winner, and still Women's champ, Delilah!!! That was short and quick! Delilah leaving the ring with her title and Jim Bowe is in the ring helping Kelly to her feet. Awww, brotherly love! The Manics vs El Mierda & Carlos The Mexican Midget The Avengaliciamaniacs pull into the parking lot in their stretch limo as The Konngo was waiting their arrival. Just as Tim Inman exited, he and the rest of his stable were brutally attacked by four masked men who were armed with shovels and railroad ties. After several minutes of intense pounding, the masked men shoved Konngo and Tim Inman into the back of a Ford pickup truck, and two of the men drove off. The other two masked men took El Mierda and Carlos and dragged them to the CCW medical facility for treatment. The whereabouts of Tim Inman and Konngo are unknown. That is what happened out back prior to this match. I haven't seen Konngo since his match earlier tonight and haven't heard a word from Tim Inman. *The Manic's music begins* Here come the Tag Team Champs, ready to give up their titles in exchange for Lady Cat. *El Mierda's music begins* Here come the soon-to-be Tag Champs as they enter the ring, with contracts in hand. Klown and El Mierda seem to be discussing the terms of their agreement, and the ref calls for the bell. El mierda points out that all Klown has to do is lay down on his back (a la Ricky Coyote) and allow Mierda to pin him so that the Manics can get Lady Cat back. And Klown is agreeing! *"Sweet Home Alabama" begins to play* What the hell?! Here comes "Fat Ass" Phil Burner, and he's got a document of some sort in his hand! Both Mierda and Klown are staring at the fat man in dibelief! What business does the Porker's Champ have with this match? He's summoning the ref to the apron, and Tony Cloud goes over to discuss the matter with Phil! Wait a damn minute! Here come Kaboink and Apocalypse, out from the crowd! They've got railroad ties, and nobody knows that they're here! Kaboink is behind Carlos on the outside, and WHAM! Out goes the midget! Kaboink is handcuffing the Mexican Midget to the ringpost! Apocalypse is behind Pepper on the other side, and WHAM! Out goes Pepper! A-Pac handcuffs Pepper to the ringpost as well! Meanwhile, Tony Cloud is still being distracted by the Fat Ass, and the Crazy Train have slid into the ring unbeknownst to Klown and El Mierda! The brothers take a glance at each other, and Apocalypse nods, signalling both of them to swing their railroad ties full force into the back of Klown's and Mierda's heads! Oh my DAMN! Both men are out cold, and Kaboink drags Klown's unconscious body on top of Mierda! The former Tag Team champs have exited the ring now, and that's Fat Ass's cue to let the ref go. Cloud turns around and sees Klown on top of Mierda, and since he already rung the bell, here's the count! 1.... 2..... 3!!!! The Manics remain Tag Team champs, all thanks to the Crazy Train! Hardcore Gorilla Mask vs Chang The Mongolian Giant - Hardcore Championship *The Contender plays* Here comes Chang being led to the ring by SuMa. Who here thinks Chang will eliminate HCGM? I do. *HCGM's music begins* Here comes HCGM with a chair in hand! He's into the ring now and Chang has taken him out with a tree trunk!!! That tree trunk was laying by the ring all night and that is what it's for! The cover, 1-2-3!!! The NEW CCW Hardcore Champion, Chang!!! Can anyone stop this gigantic problem facing all competitors in CCW? We'll find out if the next opponent can put an end to him later on in the week! Be sure to catch Friday Fury this Friday from Bartlett, Tennessee and we'll see ya then! Good Night! [name withheld] CCW Home
