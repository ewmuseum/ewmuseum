Together, with Ryan Scott, you defeated the Kamikaze Ninjaz to become WMW's first ever Tag Team Champions. How does it feel? Lance Steele : Well it feels great to finally get some Gold. I mean it was my second title shot and with the dissapointment of my defeat against Fury it felt really good to know together with Ryan we are the best tag team around at the moment. wmw : Now, how did you and Scott finally decide to just run with the tag team? I mean, originally, you were opponents, with one goal in mind, the Midwest Championship. How were you able to put that aside and go for the Tag Team? Lance Steele : Well yes orginally we were opponents but after I got the Midwest Title shot it would have been rediculous to continue going at each other when yet another title was at my reach. So I just learned to deal with Ryan as a tag team and I think it turned out for the better as I have the Tag team Championship today. wmw : So, are you implying that you still don't like Ryan? Lance Steele : Not at all. I like Ryan although just as somone to aid me in being a tag team and I don't dislike him at all although it could have been nearly anyone who could have been my partner and I would probably still have dealt with it and gone for these titles. wmw : So, are you saying that even if your partner wasn't Ryan Scott, you still would be the Tag Champion, if put in the same situation? I dislike him more then I did Ryan and probably would never of made it with him with his deceiptful ways to the Tag Team Championship although probably the only reason I like Ryan is because I was put in the tag match with him. wmw : Overall, if you caught any of the matchups tonight, which ones did you really like the most? Lance Steele : Well besides the Tag Team Championship match if I had to decide then I would have to say I didn't mind watching Jeff Hardkore, someone I have been a little involved with in the past, taking the Hardcore Title showing that he will make an impact on this fed. wmw : Is there anyone in the WMW, past or present, that you would like to face? High Stakes: No need for A-1 sauce by Benny Tyler It seems that the bombs keep hitting at camp Johnson. No more than a month ago, we saw the hiring of Katy Hummer as Commissioner, brought in to help things; weed out the bad, if you will. However, instead of helping Johnson like planned, she did weed out the bad; Johnson, Matrix, Garvin, Donovan Merlot and Sub-Beast. She knew she couldn't do it alone, so she brought her entourage with her. And, with her help, Dess walked away from Ides of March with the WMW's greatest prize of all, the Great Lakes Championship. During the same time, another group was going after camp Johnson. His name was Volcano. But, after failing and losing time, he left to shoot a feature film. After months of being gone, without a word. To everyone's surprise, he came back on the 18th edition of Riot, and for good this time. It seems that the Court is backing themselves up as much as possible. And, with the seemingly strong bond between the Court, Dess, Archangel and now Volcano, it just seems a little over excessive. Nonetheless, at High Stakes, Dess and Sub-Beast will finally have the rematch, the rematch that everyone wants to see. From the sounds of it, there will be no outside interference. Dess claims that he didn't need the help of the Court that night, but Sub-Beast doesn't care. They were "victems of being in the wrong place at the wrong time," says the former champion, and at High Stakes, he will take the title back. This will be a huge match, definatly the main event of High Stakes, but the future looks uncertain. Take Matrix, for instance. Matrix and Sub-Beast seemed to have formed a partnership of sorts over the weeks. But, during the week, Matrix made a formal announcement that he would drop the title at High Stakes. No one is exactly sure why he would drop the title. Some are speculating that he will challenge for it once again, after the PPV is over, and just does not want to be in the match itself, because of the risks it brings. Personally, I am speculating that with the history of Matrix and Dess, he has other plans, which would include keeping Dess occupied after Sub-Beast wins the title, IF Sub-Beast wins the title. There is also a rumor that with the addition of Volcano, Matrix is being brought up in the ranks to help Sub-Beast fend off the Court.
