WMW Commish Katy Hummer aim: [detail withheld] WMW Rules and Regulations | Last updated: 13 May 2003 FAQ: WMW Basic Rules Original wrestlers only . Don’t make a rip-off character. Be creative, people. All rip-offs will be deleted. updated! Wrestling Midwest is a 75% / 25% roleplay to angle federation. If you roleplay, expect to win. If you don't roleplay, expect the winner to be determined using an angle that will be developed or expect to lose. If both participants do not roleplay, most likely the match will be scrapped, or your characters will be used to put someone else over. Basically, roleplay for your match or lose . If there is a problem, pm me or email me and I will not sign you up for a match. new! Handlers can only handle 2 wrestlers at a time. Any more than two could result in problems for the handlers in roleplaying, but if the handler is sure that they can handle it then we may allow more than two. This will be under the president's jurisdiction alone. updated! Strats or strategies can be sent in no later than Thursday at noon EST. Before the match, you can choose to do something specific to your opponent in the match. The more detailed your strategy, the more control you have over your match, and the greater your chances of winning your match. We want to emulate a wrestling show that would actually appear on television, and this is what we are using to help us. Strats include, but are not limited to: Backstage/In Ring Attacks Backstage/In Ring Interviews Any backstage segments including your wrestler What specific you'd like to happen during your match I suggest definitely to new players that they begin by challenging other new players and starting feuds with those who are close to themselves in the rankings. This prevents you from being out-matched at the beginning, and allows you to build up your character here. Think of RP and winning matches as gaining ‘experience’, which makes your character ‘stronger’. Titles will be defended once every thirty days. If you have a belt, you may have to defend your belt when you don’t want to, but only once in a thirty day period are you required to defend your title. There are two rosters. The main roster includes everyone who is considered a key WMW superstar, and who have a long term contract with WMW. These competitors have a full block, with all of thier vital information in it. On Riot, all of these men will get full results during thier matches. The "Dark Match" roster are new signees who are on a match by match, developmental contracts, if you will. Usually, competitors from the main roster will not drop down to the DM roster, unless they are inactive, or have been fined excessively. All in all, these rules are quite simple to understand and follow. If you have a suggestion for another rule, please contact me. FAQ: WMW Roleplaying Rules updated! WMW does not tolerate any backstage attacks or usage of other wrestlers, interviewers, or staff members without their permission in roleplays. If we find that you weren't given the permission to attack another wrestler, interviewer, or staff member you may be subject to a fine or subject to firing. new! A character must roleplay at least once a week. If we find that a character is not roleplaying for more than two or three events then they will become inactive and subject to firing. First they will wrestle in " Dark Matches " and then worse punishments will come if they still can't roleplay.. new! All roleplays must be done on the phpBB Forums (aka WMW Boards). new! Roleplays must be at least 20 lines long. The limit to roleplays at this moment for an event is 5. new! WMW won't tolerate any roleplays that contain pornographic images or material. No posts with racial or sexual slurs in them. Period. Cursing is okay. Bleep it out or not; it doesn’t really matter to me. Just understand that adult language can and will be used from time to time. If you feel the need to swear excessively, try editing it out. new! We grade roleplays on the subject of quality or quantity. A winner will be determined on: roleplaying ability, roleplaying creativity, roleplaying presentation, wrestler's contribution to the PTC and WMW community, and the general wrestler strategy. In the case of a tie, we will have a disqualification or a double count out. Either way, it will be a no-contest. Characters you can use in roleplays: John Roberts (Main Interviewer) Steven Squires (2nd Interviewer) Characters you can NOT use (without permission from their controllers) in roleplays: President/CEO Brad Johnson Commissioner (TBA) Jack Gene (Color Commentator) Bill Hughes (Color Commentator) Benny Tyler (Week in Review Host) new! FAQ: Community new! We advise all members of the community to post on the PTC Board. You can find a link on the main site. You can post in any of the categories, or even trash talk other PTC members. This helps our ratings and ensures that you have a great time wrestling here. new! We wish to keep a very friendly OOC atmosphere. Post frequently on the "Off the Wall" forum and also the "Feedback" forum. PTC also has the same "General Discussion" forums that keep you in contact with our community. new! Feedback is the key. Sometimes PTC offers Interfederation PPV events. If you wish to participate, just contact me and I will fill the PTC staff in with the information. I-PPV is a great way to get recognized. new! Let the staff help you. If you have a problem, don't be afraid to post on the OOC Board. We will do whatever we can do help. All questions, comments, and suggestions should go on there. new! Keep us informed. If you have to take a sick leave or attend the passing of a loved one, feel free to email the President any time you wish. If you have to take a vacation, we will come up with something to fit your schedule until your return. Your record will remain "frozen" until your return, so no cheap losses. new! Have a great time wrestling and just have fun in general! WMW offers more than just fedding. WMW offers a community to talk amongst of wrestling, as well as anything you'd like to talk about. FAQ: WMW Titles The WMW Great Lakes Championship is the top dog of the federation. Comparable to the WWE World Heavyweight Championship. The Midwest Championship is the secondary championship awarded to a great wrestler who isn't quite in the heavyweight division. Comparable to the WWE Intercontinental Championship. The WMW Hardcore Title goes to one who shows a special interest in Hardcore Rules matches. The WMW Tag Team Titles are awarded to the top tag team. Comparable to the WWE Tag Team Championship. FAQ: WMW Weekly Television Programs WMW Riot - Every Friday at 9pm. The main show. We try to fit every wrestler onto this show, however sometimes there just is not enough space. FAQ: WMW Weekly Non-Televised Events Dark Matches - Dark matches happen on Friday Night Riot. The participants of such matches are members of the Dark Match roster, which just means that they are new. This is only to see if these people are in it for the long haul, and not as 'punishment' per se. We want to make sure you want to be here, before we give you a big fued. updated! WMW holds unannounced house shows, atleast once a week. Participants include those who are active on the roleplay board. Winners are determined by quality and quantity of roleplays. Win/Loss records will be effected. House shows do not help or hurt storylines or fueds. This will enhance play because we don't have enough players for 2 big cards a week, but we need to have something. Results will be in (who won, who lost and why) format, and the main event will be in standard Riot Results form. The main event, however, will be written by the participants. Not all matches turned in will be used for main events, but will be considered, heavily. Here are a few ways to write results: On the welcome page, there are two links. Ring #1 and Ring #2. These rings are set up for wrestlers to fight via AIM chatroom. The rules are set by the participants, and they way it is done, is through turn by turn moves, etc. Winner is also determined by participants. The MidwestLive link can also be used for such matches. FAQ: WMW Monthly Television Programs Every month, usually on the last Friday, WMW holds a PPV. This event will have every title defended (unless storylines conflict) and every wrestler will be on the card.
