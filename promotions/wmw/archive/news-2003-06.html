NEWS 13.06.03 - New Signings, Roster Moves, Injury Update Comments [0] WMW signed 3 new wrestlers today. Mike Schwartz, who has been offered a Developmental Contract, will have his first match at the PPV in a dark match. NWI #1 Roleplayer for the last two weeks (discluding the latest results) was signed to a contract, but most likely will not have his in-ring debut til the Tournament. The Masked Wrestler was also signed, but his identity has not yet been revealed. Mark Matthews and Jayson Reil were both brought up to the main roster for the PPV. Umbrage is currently considering retirement, after being told he will need surgery on his neck. If he ends up retiring, WMW will carry the press conference live. -wmw 13.06.03 - Riot Results are up Comments [5] http://mwf.projectefed.com/wmw/riot061303.htm Riot Results are up. Rankings, Roster, Champions (if there was a new champion named), etc etc will be updated later on tonight. Enjoy. Good luck to all Riot competitors. There will not be a house show next week. All those who are not signed to the main card will be giving a dark match. -wmw 10.06.03 - NWI Top 10 Up Comments [7] NWI put up their weekly Top 10 roleplayers. But, anyways. >> Wrestling Midwest would like to announce the signing of the NWI #3 Wrestler, Josh Dyamond, to a long term contract. Dyamond will be pushed in the Great Lakes Division. We wish him luck in his future. >> WMW signed a tag team about two weeks ago, but their debut has been delayed. Rob and Rick Payne were signed and to be put in to fued with The Brothers Spartan, but will not debut in the tournament. >> Former WMW wrestler Crazed has signed a Developmental Contract. Crazed partnered with current WMW main eventer, Archangel, before leaving do to personal problems. >> It's being said that there was another huge signing over the weekend, but nothing has been released on names, or where they will go. But, it is being said that the new big name will debut this Friday. >> The first draft of the PPV card has been released, and it looks like every title will be on the line. So far, there are only two matche signed. The first is Prime Offender vs Tami Weber for the GLC. However, if Weber loses to Dess, it will be PO vs Dess. >> The 2nd is the ASC Championship match between Primetime and Jeff Hardkore. However, in a roster move, WMW signed a triple threat match during the House Show between Jayson Reil, Mark Matthews and Shawn Tryant. The winner faces Primetime this Friday. If Primetime loses, than there will be a triple threat match for the ASC at the PPV: Jeff Hardkore vs Primetime vs winner. >> Currently, Commissioner Merlot and President Johnson have been working hard to create the seeds for the tournament. Right now, its looking like a 28 man tournament. The finalized seeding will be released after the June 20th PPV. >> Matt Murdock is back from vacation, and look for him to write another column very soon. -wmw 06.06.03 - WMW Riot Results are Up, Roster Moves Comments [1] Riot is up and ready to read. Congratulations to all 5 of you. -wmw 31.05.03 - More signings, Injury Report Comments [2] WMW signed three more wrestlers to Developmental contracts today. That totals it up to 9 in the last two days. Today, they signed Hate Plauge, The Hornet and Mike "Iron Man" Matthews. Hate Plague will be in the Hardcore or Midwest Division when brought up while, The Hornet and Matthews will both be added to the Ace Superior Championship Division when they are brought up. Umbrage is said to have been seriously injured at Blood Factory. Fury's move, the Fear-Effect, which drops his opponent's neck into his knee, is said to be the reason for the injury. WMW is currently looking at the move to make sure its not too dangerous. Umbrage was not at Riot itself, but his segment was pretaped. No word on if Umbrage will be inactivated, or if he will start to work through the injury, however he has having problems breathing and may take a few weeks off to let his trachia heel. Original reports stated that Garvin would be out for another two months, but doctors have cleared him to wrestle, stating that he had heeled ahead of schedule. -wmw 1 2 3 4 NEXT
