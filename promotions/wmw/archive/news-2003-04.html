NEWS 28.04.03 - WMW: Injury Report Comments [0] High Stakes was not just a name, at Casino Windsor. Alot of the roster took thier bodies to the limit and paid for it. >> Myst injured is quad during the Midwest Championship match. The first set of MRIs doesn't show a complete tear, but Myst will be out for some time due to the injury. >> Donovan Merlot injured his right shoulder, and will be out for atleast 2 months. Original MRIs don't show a break, however, tests show that he has dislocated it. Further tests will be taken Tuesday morning. >> Newly crowned Hardcore Champion, Jeff Hardkore recieved a concussion at the end of his match, but has been cleared by doctor's to wrestle on Riot. >> Garvin will be out for atleast 2 more months. After having knee surgery on the 20th, he is healing a head of schedule. Original projections saw Garvin returning in 4-5 months. >> Volcano has been cleared the wrestle by Doctor's after tests taken two weeks ago showed that his shoulder was only at 85%. Doctors are now saying he is at 95% full strength. >> After being left off the PPV card because of a lingering concussion, Tami Weber has been cleared to wrestle at this week's PPV. -wmw (28.4.03) - WMW: Roster cuts, Interviews and Columns, Matt Scheffert, Umbrage Comments [0] >> WMW officially released the following wrestlers from the contracts today: - "Mad Dog" Briskout - Robyn T. - Mental Mikey - Hannibal the Cannibal - Matt Hardcore WMW would like to wish these wrestlers the best of luck in their future. >> WMW sat down with Fury, Lance Steele and Jeff Hardkore after thier historic matches at the PPV. Check out the column's page to read what they said. >> A new column's pages is up. Check out what Dess, JoeC, and Benny Tyler (all pre-ppv) and Rebnik DesTerek (post-ppv) have to say. >> WMW signed two new superstars, in Matt Scheffert and Umbrage. Currently, there are no plans with Scheffert or what his future will be. But there is rumor that he will help develop the base for a possible Lightweight Division. Umbrage, who made a big impact in the Midwest Division, is scheduled to fued with Fury and Lance Steele at the top of the division. We welcome both guys, and wish them good luck on thier careers here in WMW. -wmw PREV 1 2 3 4
