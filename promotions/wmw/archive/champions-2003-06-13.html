Last updated: 13 June 2003 10:15 PM EST Great Lakes Championship Dess Dess took back his title in a hard fought battle against Tami Weber. Former Champ: Tami Weber Longest Reign: Dess (2 month)(2 defenses) First: Sub-Beast (Feb 28th, 2003) 0 successful title defenses since June 13th, 2003 Midwest Championship Fury After weeks of trying to show he was still in the run, Fury finally earned the shot at the PPV to reface Myst, along with Lance Steele in a triple threat match for the title. It was a long, hard road, but Fury came up on top. Former Champ: Myst Longest Reign: Myst (3 weeks)(1 defense) First: Fury (Mar 28th, 2003) 1 successful title defenses since April 25th, 2003 Ace Superior Championship Jeff Hardkore The first decision of the new Commissioner, Donovan Merlot, was to strip Jeff Hardkore of the Hardcore Title, and hand him the Cruiserweight Championship. Former Champ: Vacant Longest Reign: Current First: Jeff Hardkore (May 16, 2003) 1 successful title defenses since May 16, 2003 Shoot Championship Maxx Spartan Maxx, in his first WMW Riot match defeated Garvin in a glass plate match to win the WMW Shoot Championship. Former Champ: Jeff Hardkore (4 weeks)(2 defenses) Longest Reign: Matrix/Jeff Hardkore (4 weeks) Jeff Hardkore (2 defense) First: Cy Cowart (02.28.03-03.21.03) 0 successful title defenses since June 6th, 2003 Tag Team Championship The Dream Team The Dream Team finally got their shot to take on the champions at WMW: Blood Factory, and passed the test in flying colors. They defeated the champs, becoming the first team to defeat Steele and Scott in a title match. Former Champs: Lance Steele/Ryan Scott Longest Reign: 1 Month First: Lance Steele & Ryan Scott (4.25.03 - 5.23.03) Noah Hanson and Johnny Blayze 3 successful title defenses since May 23th, 2003
