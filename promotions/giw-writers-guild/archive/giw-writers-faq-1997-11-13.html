GIW Writer's FAQ

13 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello fellow e-wrestlers, I have been in e-wrestling for roughly one and a half years now. I have in the past run two e-wrestling federations but unfortunately was forced to close them because of my server's annoying habit of mucking up my mail whenever I had a card due. I have sorted something out now and have made sure that nothing can get in the way of me putting out a card anymore. In the past I have participated in EWA, DPW and SJPW. I am starting up a booked e-wrestling federation called Global Internet Wrestling or GIW. GIW is a league based around angles. At the moment I am only accepting writers. Please if you think you might enjoy writing for an e-wrestling federation then read the GIW writer's FAQ included below. ------- Global Internet Wrestling’s Writer FAQ 1. Introduction to GIW. 2. What are you expecting from a writer in GIW? 3. So what cards will we write? 4. Lots of feds have a million titles, is GIW different? 5. If we want to be writers what positions will we hold? 6. What style will be writing in? 7. What should we write for a sample? 1: Introduction to Global Internet Wrestling. Global Internet Wrestling or GIW is an e-wrestling federation based in Richmond, Virginia. In the past people have known me for running two e-wrestling federations which I was forced to close because of continual mailer crashes but now I have sorted out all those problems by making sure I have two back up addresses in case my main server decides to crash. At the moment GIW is not accepting wrestler applications because we are first looking for people who can help write the cards and reports before we move onto filling up the roster. GIW is not a ‘hardcore’ or ‘extreme’ federation although we do intend to occasionally include a gimmick match for a pay-per-view event. GIW events mainly originate from the GIW arena on the outskirts of Richmond in Virginia. GIW’s emphasis is on angles. In GIW winning is not everything as handlers are encouraged to actively participate with other handlers in scripting feuds, choosing angles and keeping things interesting for people outside of GIW. Without angles GIW would become nothing more than a badly run federation doomed to die the life of a promising federation that never delivered. GIW is just an idea from the president [name withheld] who can be reached at [detail withheld] because he wants a break from hardcore wrestling and wants to provide a creative forum for handlers to use during their spare time. 2: What are you expecting from a writer in GIW? I am looking for between 5 and 6 people willing to write for GIW because the more people that write for GIW the less work there is for everyone. I am looking for a few people who would write a weekly report and then one match for the Saturday show and then one person to simply write three matches for the Saturday card. I myself will administer angles and feuds and write a dark match card as well as the Saturday card. I don’t want to pressure writers into writing tons of material for GIW so that matches in this federation really stand out. 3: So what cards will we write in GIW? The ultimate goal of Global Internet Wrestling is every day broadcasting. At the moment we will have one card full of dark matches or matches that aren’t being broadcast live. This show will broadcast on Wednesdays and will be a summary of GIW’s two house shows which happen earlier in the week. Most of these matches will be jobber matches showing of a wrestler’s skills with just one or two proper matches. This Wednesday card will be written entirely by me seeing as Wednesday is the day when I have the most free time. On Saturdays GIW hosts a 8-10 match card. All these matches are intended to be live and directly broadcast from GIW Arena in Richmond, Virginia. All cards are fully booked with some emphasis on handlers to decide the winners for feud starting matches by scripting screwjobs, sneak attacks and blatant cheating. The other two shows are a countdown to Saturday program in which the presenters go over all of the upcoming matches and analyse the matches and pick winners, etc. Also on this show small flashes will be allowed from handlers who want to try and sneak in some short comments before the Saturday match. On Monday a flash program for longer flashes from wrestlers who wish to rant about their wins or losses and discuss the Saturday card. 4: Lots of feds have a million titles. Is GIW different? Yes most definitely. I see lots of small federations starting with 7 belts. I think that that is just lunacy. A while ago on RSPWF in a debate about Championship titles one fed head said he had 65-90 wrestlers in his federation and 7 titles. To start with Global Internet Wrestling will only have three titles but as we increase in size possibly a tertiary singles and secondary tag title will be introduced. Currently our titles will be the: _GIW World Title: The primary title in GIW the world title is open to any wrestler regardless of weight. We intend to have this title defended once every 3-4 weeks on Saturday night against a top contender. _GIW North American Title: A secondary title open to all GIW singles wrestlers of 290lbs or under. This title is for wrestlers who think that lighter wrestlers are more realistic. Most wrestlers in this category will also be challenging for the world title. This title will be defended every 2-3 weeks. _GIW World Tag Team Titles: The primary tag team title in the GIW. Open to all tag teams with no upper combined weight limit. Lots of federations neglect tag team wrestling but GIW does not intend to do this at all. People wishing to have a tag team will generally be very welcome. This title will be defended every 3-4 weeks. 5: If we want to be writers what positions will we hold? Out of the writers I am looking for someone who will be able to help me with administration and angles to be my vice president. He or she doesn’t necessarily have to be the best writer just the best at helping administer a league. Unfortunately the vice-president will not be allowed to hold titles as he also helps determine who wins matches in the league. The other writers will hold the title of commissioner. This just means that they are a character used for formal occasions with little or no say on general GIW affairs other than writing. The commissioners will be allowed to handle GIW wrestlers. 6: What style will we be writing in? I have taken quite a long time to decide that one. In my opinion the style of writing made popular by the IIWF is what we use in GIW. We’re hoping to make the commentators realistic and not just cardboard and stereotyped people who just say what moves are happening. All writing in the federation except for discussions on the report shows is done with long bracketed writing telling the reader what is happening in the ring punctuated by a small dialogue between 2-3 of the 3 commentators. We recommend that you should read the archives at http://www.sisko.demon.co.uk/ to find out the style we’ll be writing in and the general length of Saturday night matches as well as the levels of description required in the writing. 7: What should we write for a sample? If you are interested in becoming a writer for GIW then send me a sample last half of a match. It should be quite short but still giving me enough room to see if you are for the job or not. As far as commentators go make your own ones up for the sample, just run wild. At the moment I have a few commentators ready but I’d just as easily replace them if a better one comes along. So what are you waiting for, get ready to be part of the future of GIW. _ /| |\|__| | | o o | \ / \ / \()/ Global Internet Wrestling November 13th 1997 - "All the action.. and then some" President [name withheld]
