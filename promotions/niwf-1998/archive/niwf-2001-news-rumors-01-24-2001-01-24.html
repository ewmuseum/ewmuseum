News & Rumors 01/24/2001

24 January 2001

Welcome folks, to another summary edition of news & rumors! The only place where you can find out what the hot talk is about the fed and maybe some of the inside or behind the scenes happening! Let's get right to it: -Irish Devil quit the NIWF. Apparently it "sux" and was a waste of his time. This was the comment by the handler. Whe President Lukiw tried to approach the handler to ask him why it sucked, he couldn't get an answer out of him. He was then reminded that he signed a 52 Card contract and that Irish Devil could very easily become the Irish Jobber. None too happy about this, the Irish Devil posted his little problem on RSPWF. Dirty Dan immediately came to the aid of the NIWF and defended it, but it only caused an uproar with the RSPWF telling him he was acting "immature" and questioned the rules of the fed. Lukiw was originally going to use Irish Devil as a jobber, but that thread alone on RSPWF created so much interest, many people joined the NIWF and Irish Devil is just being done away with, no questions asked. -It is rumored that yet another former NIWF superstar was inside the NIWF headquarters, talking with Dirty Dan. We couldn't get any info as to who it is but we did get comments from Dirty Dan who basically said if this man comes back, e-wrestling will be back to the way it should be and he could easily change the face of e-wrestling in general this year! -Axel Schmidt is not under contract with the NIWF and we are still not sure how he even got inside the building. We are not even sure of his motives for attacking "Dirty" Dan Lukiw. We know nothing about Axel Schmidt's appearance other then the fact that he appeared and attacked the president. Basically, we know as much as the fans but we are hoping to find out a lot more! -The Royal Rumble has been confirmed and every week, 5 participants of the Rumble will be named starting this week. President Lukiw is still looking for some of the "former" NIWF stars (hint hint) to come into the Rumble for just the appearance (or stick around if they want). If you are a former NIWF star, then please feel free to contact the president at [detail withheld] and inquire about the NIWF Royal Rumble. The first 5 participants are as follows: Smilin' Jack Ruby Dr. DooM Black Flagg "Heartbreaker" Matt Foxx Badger (more will be named in the weeks to come) -Apparently, President Lukiw is not too thrilled with comments he saw by one Roddy Taylor on Shootfire Pro. Our understanding is that the staff at Shootfire, some wrestlers at Shootfire and many wrestlers in the NIWF were none too happy about the comments geared towards the NIWF either. The comments will be aired this weekend on Hardcore Edge and we may even have some comments from the president about it as well. This should be a good one! -Who is [name withheld]'s "friend"? A former champ? The speculation is that the friend is Jason Starr, which is the best possibilty to show up to the rumble. However, other names have been thrown around such as Jimmy Cougar, Christian Hawk, Lawrence Buffy and even Bobbo the Clown! All we can tell you is the surprise is supposed to be huge and should shock the entire NIWF! -The first ever "Four Corner's of Death" match has been scheduled in the NIWF between: "Heartbreaker" Matt Foxx vs. The Bad Boy vs. "Superstar" Lawrence Steele vs. Crazy Chris Caruso. What this match does, is the following. Each wrestler brings their own weapon or "gimmick" to the ring and places it in the corner. All 4 men start out and wrestle each other. The person who brought his choice of weapon to the ring (barbed wire, baseball bat, explosives, etc) has to retrive his weapon and use it on one of his opponents. Once an opponent is nailed with that weapon or gimmick, he is eliminated. The winner is the last man standing with his weapon/gimmick. -Gwen has still not signed her contract with the NIWF and Dirty Dan is still waiting for it. Apparently Gwen is a little concerned about competing in the NIWF now thanks to the attack that occurred on her this past weekend. We would like to update you and let you know she is now in good condition to wrestle after healing from her bruises this past weekend. -Larry Gate's appearance looked like it may of been a one time deal. Right now we aren't sure if he wants to sign a contract or not with the NIWF. There haven't been any talks between him and the the president, so it does not look like he will compete in the NIWF. However, we did get comments from him and he told us that if the price was right, he would be willing to compete on his terms. -It is rumored that Kitti was taken out of her match this weekend because of Dylan Dice. Dice was concerned about Kitti's safety in the ring after this weekend's match between herself and Heidi Leick. After seeing comments from Sierra Browne, Dice apparently approached President Lukiw and demanded Kitti be taken out of the match because she would not be safe in a match with "a lunatic like that". -The NIWF has signed MANY new wrestlers this past week... the following are all new to the NIWF roster. Gravis "The being" Knight, The Wasp, "Babyface" Tom Collins and Crazy Chris Caruso. -Dirty Dan had a meeting with the announcing of the NIWF. While he was pleased with Billy Apollo's call of the action, he felt that Doug Hardy went a little overboard with the 14lb ham bit. He has asked Doug Hardy to tone down with the 14lb ham. While he felt it added some comedy to the show, he felt it was over done. Dirty Dan's comments were "A little less ham, a little more action!" -Many female wrestlers have been contacting Dirty Dan to try and get into the NIWF! While Dirty Dan does not mind having female wrestlers in the fed, he feels that too many may take away from the men's wrestling as well. If things get out of hand, he may put out a whole different card with just female wrestling on it. But right now, options are open. -The NIWF 3 Man Tag Belts have been retired. There is no need for these belts right now and there won't be need for these belts in the future. The TV title is still intact and the regular singles matches will be the determining factor to see who are most qualified for a shot at the TV Title at the Next Free Per View. The NIWF staff is currently working on a few ways on trying to implement the Cruiserweight and International Titles. As you know, the Diamond Title is still with Shootfire Pro. -Finally, Dirty Dan was pleased with the card that was put out this weekend and has received many compliments on it. He was especially pleased with those who put forth the effort to make this a successful card and tried but he was not pleased with those who didn't make an effort. He is hoping to get the same kind of effort for this weekend's card, or better. As far as we have been hearing, he hasn't seen the same effort as of yet... but that could change. Get those roleplays to him by 2pm Eastern on Saturday the latest! That's all folks!! Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/
