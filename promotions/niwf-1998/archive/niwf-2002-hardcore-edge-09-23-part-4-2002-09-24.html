Hardcore Edge 9/23/02 (Part Four)

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
====================== Eric Idol vs. Tommy Jackal Match Writer: Paul ====================== Billy: I'm really looking forward to this next matchup, Johnny. Johnny: Me too. This will be the debut singles match in the NIWF for both of these guys that are better known for their tag team exploits. Jackal as one half of the legendary PACK, and Idol as a former tag champion with T-Bone. Billy: The "Popstar" has always wanted to be known as a singles wrestler, though, and now he's finally going to get his chance to see what he can do on his own. Johnny: Yeah, but I'm sure that with Mad Skillz at ringside and Desmond Walker watching his back, he won't be totally alone. Billy: That's for sure! [The arena lights dim as a pounding drum beat begins. Lasers flash around the darkened arena, pulsing along with the rhythm. The disembodied voice of "The Popstar" Eric Idol bellows...]   "IT'S AAAAAAAALLLLLLL ABOUT ME!!!" ["BOOM" by POD blares as explosions erupt from the ring posts. Pyro flashes up around the entrance as Eric Idol emerges from the curtain with Mad Skillz following behind him. Idol is wearing white baggy pants and a red sleeveless T shirt. "POPSTAR" is printed on the front of the shirt while "It's AAAALLLL about ME!" is printed on the back. Eric pulls his shirt off revealing a chiselled physique. That draws a high pitched pop from the girls in the crowd but it is quickly drowned out by the overwhelming boo's. Idol smiles smugly and strikes a few poses before sauntering down the aisle.] Announcer: Introducing being accompanied by Mad Skillz, from Long Island, New York. Standing six feet, and weighing in at 230 pounds... "THE POPSTAR" ERIC IDOL!!! [Idol jumps into the ring over the top rope and Mad Skillz follows him in. Mad Skillz busts a move in the middle of the ring while Idol jumps up on the second turnbuckle. Idol raises his hands in the air and tosses his head back with a smug smile as if he's basking in his adoring Idol Worshippers. The crowd responds with various unflattering chants and jeers.] Billy: The crowd really letting Eric Idol have it here tonight, Johnny. Johnny: As always, but I think those jeers are about to turn into cheers, as K-Nine Tommy Jackal is set to return to NIWF action! ["Dog the Nine" by Bride blares as Tommy Jackal makes his way down to the ring. He howls at the fans, who howl back at him, as he approaches ringside. He jumps up onto the apron, and lets out a huge HOWWWLLLLLLLL, and the crowd echoes in unison, as the song fades.] Announcer: Introducing from Detroit, Michigan standing 6 ft 3, and weighing in at 270 lbs, TOMMY JACKAL!!! ["WELCOME BACK!!! WELCOME BACK!!! WELCOME BACK!!!"]   [DING DING] Billy: There's the bell and this one's underway, as the capacity crowd here welcome Tommy Jackal back to the NIWF! Johnny: It is great to see him, and he looks in better shape than he ever has! Billy: K-Nine challenging Idol to a test of strength, and Idol wisely backing off, as he's no match in the power department for the massive K-Nine! ["PUSSY!! PUSSY!! PUSSY!!"] Johnny: I think that's smart for Idol to not try to mix it up powerwise with Jackal. He's got to use his quickness to wear him down, and then move in for the kill. Billy: Idol now off the ropes, and he charges at Jackal, and Jackal leapfrogs him! Idol off the other side now, and Jackal surprises the Popstar with a wicked Frankensteiner!!!! ["K-NINE!!! K-NINE!!! K-NINE!!!"] Johnny: Jackal showing that he's no slouch in the quickness department either, and Idol's obviously a little surprised, as he rolls out of the ring to regain his senses. Billy: Idol now instructing Mad Skillz, but Jackal's not giving them any time to formulate an attack, as he comes thru the ropes with a suicide dive, and he barely grazes Idol! Johnny: Yeah, but he got all of Mad Skillz and it looks like we are down to one on one here! Billy: Jackal may have got the worst of that one, as Idol is to his feet first. He picks up Jackal and drives him into the ring steps! ["OOOOHHHHHHH!!!!"] Billy: Referee Shane Francis now admonishing Idol, but really there's not a heck of a lot he can do here. Johnny: Yeah, come on Francis, this is the NIWF! Let them go at it, and just count the pinfall!!! Billy: Idol shoves Francis out of the way, and he grabs Jackal, but Jackal nails him with a shot to the gut! Jackal picks up Idol, and OH, GUTWRENCH SUPLEX THRU THE ESKIMO ANNOUNCE TABLE!!!!! ["HOLY SHIT!!! HOLY SHIT!!! HOLY SHIT!!!!"] Johnny: That table shattered on impact, and I think Eric Idol might be broken into a few pieces as well!!! Billy: What's K-Nine doing now? He climbs up on the apron, and he's climbing the turnbuckle!!! This guy's nuts!!!! He's looking out to the fans in attendance!! Jackal: HOWWWWLLLLLL!!!!!!! ["HOWWWWLLLLLL!!!!!!!"] Johnny: The crowd echoing the wildman, and there he goes with a picture perfect moonsault! ["OOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!"] Billy: But Eric Idol rolls out of the way at the last second, and now both men are down. Ref down to check on both men. ["BOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: Here comes trouble. Billy: Yes, the Marquee Man Desmond Walker on his way out to ringside, and the fans are obviously none to pleased. Walker surveying the situation. He picks up Tommy Jackal and nails him with a spinebuster! ["BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!"] Billy: Walker with an evil smile, as he's taking pleasure in brutalizing K-Nine here. Walker puts Jackal's head between his legs, just as Idol starts to get to his feet. Oh, Walker nails Jackal with a piledriver!!!! ["YAAAAAAAAAAAAA!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: UNBELIEVABLE!!!!! Billy: Walker is posing for the crowd, and he thinks they're cheering for him, but he doesn't realize that Jackal sprang to his feet after that piledriver!!!! Walker turns around, and Jackal nails him with a clothesline!!! Jackal pounces on Walker, and oh my goodness, he's BITING him!!!! Johnny: I think he took a bit of flesh there, Billy!!! Jackal: HOWWWWWLLLLLL!!!!!! ["HOWWWWWLLLLLLL!!!!!!"] Billy: Jackal to his feet now, and he turns to go after Idol, and he's met with a vicious chairshot from Eric Idol! ["OOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!"] Billy: And another!!! And ANOTHER!!!!! Johnny: The Popstar is particularily vicious here, but I guess he has to be in order to take out Jackal. Billy: Eric Idol rolling Jackal into the ring now, and he follows him in! Idol to the second ropes, and he's waiting on Jackal. K-Nine struggling to his feet.... ["OOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!"] Billy: CORKSCREW DDT by the Popstar, and that might be it as he goes for the cover!!!! ["ONE!!!!!"] ["TWO!!!!!!"] ["THR.......YAAAAAAAAAAAA!!!!!!"] Billy: Kickout with authority!!!!! Johnny: Jackal must've thrown him three feet in the air!!!! Billy: Tommy Jackal springs to his feet!!!! Idol trying to get out of the ring, but Jackal grabs him!!!!! FUJIWARA ARMBAR!!!! Johnny: This has got to be it. Unless Idol can make it to the ropes, there is no way out of this hold. Billy: Shane Francis is right in Idol's grill, and the Popstar is in obvious pain, but he refuses to give it up. Idol raising a hand, and he looks like he's about to tap, but he won't quite drop it. Johnny: Eric Idol has to be in extreme pain, and there really is no way out of this hold. Oh wait, maybe I spoke too soon. Billy: Well, we knew he couldn't be down forever, and Mad Skillz is to his feet, and he sees that Idol's in trouble. Mad Skillz now grabbing Eric Idol's foot, and he pulls it under the bottom rope, and the ref didn't see it, as he's checking to see if Idol tapped!!! ["BULL-SHIT!!!! BULL-SHIT!!!!"] Billy: Ref now forcing Tommy Jackal to break the hold, as he notices Idol's foot. Jackal sees Mad Skillz and he's going after him now. Jackal finally grabs Skillz and he's got him by the neck!!! ["YAAAAAAAAAAA!!!!!!!"] Billy: T-Bone suplex by Tommy Jackal, and Mad Skillz is out cold again! But Eric Idol is back to his feet again, and he nails Jackal with a springboard plancha as K-Nine turns around!!!! ["OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!"] Billy: Idol first to his feet, and he rolls Jackal into the ring. Idol quickly to the top rope. He's turning around toward the fans, as they voice their displeasure. ["POLE-STAR!!!! POLE-STAR!!! POLE-STAR!!!!"] Johnny: He's wasting too much time! Jackal's slowly getting to his feet! Billy: Eric Idol with an unflattering middle finger to the fans, and he gets set, but Jackal is to his feet now, and he runs toward the ropes, and he's got Eric Idol. What's this going to be? A superplex? Johnny: I've seen this before, Billy, and it's not your standard superplex!!! ["HOLY SHIT!!!! HOLY SHIT!!!! HOLY SHIT!!!!"] Billy: Jackal reverses it in midair into a powerslam, and he's got him in a pinning position as Shane Francis drops to make the count!!!! ["ONE!!!!!"] ["TWO!!!!!"] ["THRE......NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!"] Billy: Eric Idol just barely getting a shoulder up!!!! I can't believe he kicked out of that!!!! Johnny: I've been watching tapes from Japan and he calls that move Dog the Nine, and I've NEVER seen anyone kick out of it. Despite all his bravado, Eric Idol is a very special athlete. Billy: Tommy Jackal obviously has to be very frustrated, but he's gotta get back on Idol. He bends down to pick up the fallen Popstar, but Idol nails him with a lowblow. Jackal doubled over in pain, and Idol struggles to his feet. He picks up Jackal and he's trying to get him up for a suplex, but Jackal won't budge. Jackal with a shot to the kidney of Idol, and Idol releases it. Jackal off the ropes, and he drops Idol with a shouldertackle. Idol back to his feet, and he's dropped with a clothesline!!! Johnny: Tommy Jackal with a second, or is it third or fourth wind, now, Billy? Billy: I don't know, but he really is the proverbial house of fire in there right now!!! Jackal climbs to the ropes, and this could finally be it!!! He's set, and wait!!! Look out!!!!! [THWACK!!!!!!!] ["BOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!"] Billy: Desmond Walker, we had forgotten about him, but he came too, and just nailed K-Nine with a brutal chairshot, and he fell face first to the mat!!!! ["YAAAAAAAAAAA!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: That brought out Jackal's partner T-Bone, and he goes straight after Walker. Walker hightails it thru the crowd though! Billy: And T-Bone is going straight after him. In the ring, Idol is first to his feet, and I don't even think he can believe what happen! He slowly makes his way to the ropes. ["POLE-STAR!!!! POLE-STAR!!! POLE-STAR!!!!"] Billy: Fans really giving it to Eric Idol again, but I'm not even sure he can even hear them. He's acting on instinct right now. Jackal is still out in the center of the ring from that violent chairshot, and Idol is in position, and OH, he connects with the 450 SPLASH!!!!!! ["BOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!"] ["ONE!!!!!!"] ["TWO!!!!!!"] ["THREE.........BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner... "THE POPSTAR" ERIC IDOLLLLL!!!!!!!!! [MASSIVE HEEL POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: Shane Francis raises Eric Idol's hand in victory, and as he drops the hand, Idol slumps to the canvas. I really think he was running on adrenaline for the last five minutes there, Johnny! Johnny: Yeah, he's right out of it, but he did get the victory, so you have to take your hat off to Eric Idol. [Commercial] [Scene opens backstage in Eric Idol's dressing room. Idol, Walker, and Skillz are all celebrating when Craig Kelly walks in.] Eric Idol: YEEEEEEAAAAAHHHH!!!!!! IT'S AAAAAALLLLLLL ABOUT ME, BABY!!! WHOOOO!!!!! [Desmond Walker pours a bottle of champagne over Idol's head.] Craig Kelly: Hey guys, can I get a quick word with you? Eric Idol: Sure come on in, Mark! Craig Kelly: Uh, actually I'm Craig Kelly. Eric Idol: Really, are you sure? Cause I thought for sure you were that Mark Hunter guy. Desmond Walker: Nah, Eric, Mark is the other one. Eric Idol: Whatever! It doesn't matter because tonight it's AAAALLLL about ME!!!! [Idol downs some champagne and dumps some on Craig's head as Walker and Skillz hoot and hollar.] Craig Kelly: Okay... gentlemen. You're obviously very happy about the outcome of your matches tonight... Eric Idol: Happy? HAPPY?! I'm freakin' THRILLED! Not only did I put away that big dumb hairy goof, K-Y, tonight! But two weeks ago, my pal Des busted up his tag partner T-Bag! Do you realise what that means, Mark? Craig Kelly: Uh it's Craig. No, what does it mean? Eric Idol: It MEANS that me and the Marquee Man are gonna kick their dumb asses at Bad Intentions! We've already proven that we can kick their tails in singles competition. In four weeks, we'll put them away for good in tag team competition! Mad Skillz: You da man Mack Daddy! Craig Kelly: Wait a minute. I wanted to ask you something, Skillz. [Skillz beams at the sudden attention.] Mad Skillz: Yo, ya'll finally want some o' da Skillz! What's da dilly. yo? Craig Kelly: I'm not sure what you just said, but I wanted to ask you about the last Hardcore Edge. You were attacked in the ring by Eric Idol and... [Idol snatches the mic out of Craig's hand.] Eric Idol: What are you trying to prove, Mark? Listen, don't rain on our parade! We're trying to have a party here and you're bringing it down! [Des Walker gets up in Craig's grill.] Desmond Walker: I think the interview is OVER! [Walker pie faces Craig right out the door and then slams it in his face.] Craig Kelly: Can't blame a guy for trying. Back to you guys. [The scene cuts back to Billy and Johnny at ringside.] Billy: Thanks Craig. Looks like their's trouble in paradise... again. Johnny: Not surprising. Just look at the egos in that dressing room. It's no wonder they don't get along.
