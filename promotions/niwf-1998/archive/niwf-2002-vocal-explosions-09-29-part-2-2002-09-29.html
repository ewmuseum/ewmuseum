=NIWF======== JACK RUSSELL ============= ( The scene opens up in the locker room. You see Jack Russell cleaning his brass knuckles, smiling the whole time.) Jack: Nice to get blood on the knucks eh? (He finishes wiping them off and puts them down) Jack: That felt so good, to beat the living piss out of some confused little mongrel want to be. He should have never stepped into the same room as me. He should have never spoke to me. He knew what was going to happen, he knew that fate had brought him to the Destructor. Now, he is in intensive care, paying for his lack of wisdom. (Jack smiles and adjusts his braces) Jack: Yes, folks, you got but a SMALL taste of the ruthless barbarian that is Jack Russell. You just dont know how I would like to do that to a few more pieces of shit in this federation. But that time will come, and I will be waiting patiently, like the hunter for his prey. I am the whip that cracks across the slaves back. I am your fear. (Jack starts putting his things in a duffell bag.) Jack: Folks, the way I see it, is there is alot more of what you have seen coming. Jack Russell has a match in 2 weeks. Against someone, I don't remember his name. There is no reason to waste too much breath on the man, it is just my stepping stone to bigger and better game. Oh, I will maul him worse than I did that Skills. And I dont think he will come back to this federation, part of fear, part of permanent wounds that I have inflicted on him. (Jack scratches his head) Jack: You know, folks, Jack Russell has probably angered some with his coffee serving skills. (He chuckles) Jack: Thank you, Thank you. I hope I pissed off EVERYONE. I don't care. Take everyone in this federation, line them up, I will fight them one on one, all I need is a glass of water in between. You will see total carnage, and Jack Russell will be standing on top of the pile of human remains that was the NIWF, smiling in blood lust. (Jack stands up and gets ready to leave.) Jack: Folks, the owners will soon open my cage and unleash the beast that is Jack Russell. Be afraid. (The camera closes up on his insane eyes.) Jack: Be VERY afraid! (The scene fades to black as Jack walks out of the locker room.) Johnny: I think it's YOU who needs to be afraid, Russell. Mark: There certainly are a lot of guys angry about what happened to Mad Skillz last weekend. Johnny: Yeah and Russell is gonna feel the backlash next week for sure. Mark: Here's the Prophet's newest disciple... Xanthos! =NIWF=== XANTHOS ======== [A new feed begins, the camera is shaky, similar to that of the Blair Witch Project type camera work. Someone outside of the camera's vision] The Prophet: Your training is over, your lessons have been learned. You are now at the apex of your life. My vision has brought you to this VERY moment. Your committement to my desires are the only thing keeping us from bring back the belief. In due time, they will all FEEL what I am speaking of. And you my brother of war, will be repaid tenfold. [Panning] The Prophet: And with our brothers in hand, Kid and Youth, we will finally bring back what is rightfully ours.... Johnny: Million dollar body and a ten cent brain. Mark: Here's a clip now of the Death Dealer! Johnny: I thought he was mute? =NIWF======================= "DEATH DEALER" RYAN ROBERTS ============================ (A lone light illuminates a small area. Roberts: It's a shame, really it is. Some one with my abilities has let his career fall down the crapper. After an impressive showing against Roddy Taylor I've let my career take a downward spiral. I make no excuse for anything I've chosen. It is my own demons with which I fight. I mean I have said nothing and taken a few beating since. Do not let my lack of communication fill you with glee; you had nothing to do with my lack of attention to the NIWF. What you need to concern yourself with is quite obvious. You see I've been pondering my next course of action, I've been doing a little soul searching as they say. Redemption is what I'm looking for. Revenge has run through my veins for and it's kept me up late at night. It's like a low knock at the soul. At first you can ignore it, but eventually, the sound drives you crazy. No, Roddy, I do not blame you for my hiatous. I don't blame you for anything. I'm not here to lay down the challenge to you Roddy. Yet, I speak of revenge, don't I? Yes you see Roddy. I blame you for what's about to happen to the NIWF, Oh you don't know it, but it's about to happen. It falls on your head, Roddy. (Ryan again lowers his head, looking at the ground) Roberts (in a soft monotone whisper): Lance. Oh Lance. How are you feeling? Do you feel ready to take on the enigma of the NIWF? Can you withstand the punishment that I'm envisioning in my brain? I hope so, cause I'm not here to end careers, someone else can take that moniker and run with it. I'm here to teach. What, you question my validity? Oh, I will teach you Lance, I will teach you in the art of pain and punishment. What's that running through your mind? What do I know about pain? (Ryan slowly raises his head and looks into the camera) More than you think. I leave you for now Lance. Realize that the NIWF is under attack. It makes no matter who stands across from the Death Dealer, They will feel pain and they will realize that I am more than capable of handing it out.
