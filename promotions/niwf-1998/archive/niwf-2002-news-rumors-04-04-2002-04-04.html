News & Rumors

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Boy oh Boy do we have some news and rumors for you today. -This past week, "The Extreme War Machine" Corey Irons was seen coming out of the NIWF Headquarters once again. However, this time Mr. Irons was not in such a great mood and he was mumbling something about a horrible contract. It is believed that Corey Irons had talks with Dirty Dan about returning to the NIWF but apparently had a difficult time reaching a deal. -"Wonder Boy" Tom Coleman has quit the federation. This leaves a little bit of a hole in our schedule and opens up another spot in the rumble for someone. It is believed that the rumble spot is filled and may be taken by either a current Shootfire Pro wrestler or a former NIWF Champ. There will have to be an updated schedule as well following these bits of news. -The Diamond Title division could heat up and become extremely huge within the next few months. It is believed that there will be some kind of deal work out where the Diamond Title may have to be defended at both the NIWF and SPW, but this is just a rumor. The next 5 participants for the Lunatic Fringe Match will be named next week. -Speaking of the Free Per View, the full schedule should be out after this weekend's card. It is believed that the #1 contenders for each title will be named and matches will finally be assigned for these titles. -Some wrestlers in the back saw Dirty Dan speaking to former NIWF wrestler, NaTaS. When asked if NaTaS was coming back to the NIWF, Dirty Dan said that is not what the meet was about. Apparently NaTaS is one month away from being a married man and has invited Dirty Dan to his bachelor party this weekend. Of course, we won't put it past Dirty Dan to convince NaTaS to come back to the NIWF. -There are some legal issues going on right now between the NIWF and former NIWF wrestler, Vile Vince Viper. Apparently it has a lot to do with the money that Vile Vince Viper won in the NIWF. He is apparently claiming he never wrestled for the NIWF so he doesn't have to pay additional taxes. The NIWF likes to refer to this as tax fraud and Dirty Dan has been in contact with VVV about getting this matter taken care of. Unfortunately, Vile Vince Viper refuses to return any of Dirty Dan's calls. Currently, VVV is in SPW. -The NIWG would like to welcome it's newest member to the roster, Daria Wakizawa. What we know about her right now is that she is a female wrestler who hails from Japan. Dirty Dan felt it was necessary to scout out some additional talent while he might be making some roster cuts in the near future. He would have to compensate for the losses. -Also, Dirty Dan has been in contact with a tag team known as MiB (Men in Black). Apparently they showed interest in joing the NIWF crew and wrestling for this federation. Right now we have 4 tag teams, but who knows where these tag teams will be in the future? Dirty Dan wants to make sure he has some fresh competition for his tag team division as well. MiB has not yet signed a contract, but they are in the process of negotiations. -There have been some free agents that have been in contact with Dirty Dan and also some other wrestlers who are not happy in the fed they are in right now. The most noted of these names is Andrew Davis, who Dirty Dan went out of his way to contact. However, the feeling is that Andrew Davis does not wish to compete any more and has called it quits. -HUGE NEWS! Dirty Dan has signed a former NIWF World Champ to a new contract. Apparently, the contract is not valid until the Royal Rumble event itself and it this wrestler will be a part of the rumble and will have the chance to win the NIWF World Title. When people see who this wrestler is, their mouths will drop! -Also, note the schedule changes (hopefully I didn't screw any up again) as not matches are changed around and newbies have matches in a few weeks. -Finally, we have all 30 participants named to the Rumble. Here is the last and FINAL list. It can still change if someone decides to quit like Tom Coleman did, but let's hope it doesn't happen: 1.Dirty Dan 2.The Masked Man 3."The Shocker" Brian Anthony 4.Thomas P.
