News & Rumors 03/07/02

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Let's get right to the point! -Rumors are swirling as to who the masked man really is right now. The masked man has caused some mass confusion, first denouncing SPW and then proclaiming his love for SPW as well. Many in the back are wondering what this is leading to. Some speculation is that the masked man is a wrestler who wishes to quit SPW by making the jump to NIWF. It is also a possibilty that the masked man is someone who already has quit SPW. Then there is the possibility that the masked man is a former NIWF star. Nobody really knows at this point. -Dirty Dan has been in some talks with with a former NIWF star. We recently saw former NIWF wrestler, Nightblade, make his way in and out of the NIWF headquarters. Nightblade could be a good edition to the NIWF. -Rumor has it that Sean Steele is not happy with the current direction of the NIWF and how some of it is being run. We are told that Sean Steele will be addressing this issue at this weekend's Hardcore Edge. -Dirty Dan is looking into many contracts for the NIWF to see which ones will be expiring on the minimum of 6 shows. We heard rumors that NIWF wrestler Ian Walsh may want out of the contract by then and is not happy in the NIWF. -This coming Hardcore Edge, it is rumored that current NIWF valet Heidi Leick may be doing commentary for the women's match which is the main event. This does not mean that Heidi is participating in any of the matches, just yet. -As you saw the past week, the NIWF has signed now former SPW Star Jeremy Colton Christ. The NIWF overlooked the fact that they also have another former SPW wrestler on their roster, and that is Elyse Lightfoot. SPW has done nothing but laughed at these two names. The NIWF feels these two stars are great additions. -Management has been disappointed with some of the wrestlers in the back as well. They felt that this past week, many of the wrestlers could of given more effort to at least show they care about their matches this coming weekend. -Dirty Dan has been satisfied with the communication between himself and the match writers and has even hired a new match writer. However, there is always concern that a match here and there will not get written. This is more of a reminder to the match writers to remember to get the matches in this weekend (since I know all of you do anyway). -The next two weeks, on Hardcore Edge, is predicted to be more violent and possibly more controversial than the card before. Credit has to be given to the wrestlers of the NIWF who basically are putting themselves at risk for these shows. They will all be greatly rewarded! -Dirty Dan spent some time this week with working on the website. However, he did get sidetracked and didn't get it completed. The website may stay a little basic so there won't be much navigation around it. We will inform you of any updates when they occur. -As you all know, this is the day 5 more wrestlers get added to the Royal Rumble. There are now 20 wrestlers total! This means only 10 more to be added before all 30 are announced for this great event! The wrestlers are the following: 1.Dirty Dan 2.The Masked Man 3."The Shocker" Brian Anthony 4.Thomas P.
