Patty Babes Carter vs. Assaf "The Terminator" Levavy to Headline NIWF LEGENDS!

3 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alright guys, it is time to give you an update on this spectacular event that we are going to have in the NIWF. I thank all of you who have sent me e-mails regarding what matches you would love to see take place in the NIWF. I was shocked to see many of them made but also loved some of your ideas. Unfortunately, there were so many different matches that were made that I can't have 50+ matches on one card. So right now, I have taken the popular matches that were voted for. I am looking to get this thing out on June 16th and make it a two day event with the normal matches taking place on the 16th and the Royal Rumble for the NIWF World Title on the 17th . I would save it for the next week BUT I will be going to KOTR on that Sunday. :) So, what I ask right now is that anyone that has not made the matches they want to see the most, send me the matches you'd love to see the most. The list of available characters to work with will be listed AFTER the matches I have scheduled. If you are reading this on RSPWF and want to participate, please e-mail me. Take a look: ================================================================= NIWF Presents LEGENDS Live from the NIWF Arena in Newark, NJ (yep, we are back there) June 16th & 17th, 20001 ================================================================= MAIN EVENT Purist's Dream Match Assaf "The Terminator" Levavy vs. Patty Babes Carter (everyone wants to see it apparently, Carter is also known as Patrick Lewis) Hell in the Cell Towel Match, BWF vs. NIWF [name withheld] w/ [name withheld] vs. "Dirty" Dan Lukiw w/ [name withheld] Exploding Ring Barbed Wire Match Havoc vs. "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice Submission Match NaTaS w/ Kitti vs. Amber w/ Todd Lucchesi Triple Threat Match for NIWF North American Title Jerry "The Bulldog" Jericho vs. Chaz Darby vs. Smilin' Jack Ruby Cruiserweight Title Match Ian Lightfoot vs. 'Asshole' Andy Simmons Hardcore Match Mr. Bungle vs. Adam Kuhl Old School Match Glamour Boys vs. 2 Exciting Dudes NIWF Tag Team Title Match The Specialists vs. The Greatest Team Ever Thumbtack Match for NIWF/Shootfire Pro Diamond Title Jeffery Dylan Marsh vs. Todd Lucchesi 4 Man Lunatic Fringe Match Angus MacGregor vs. Vile Vince Viper vs. Axel Schmidt vs. "The Extreme War Machine" Corey Irons Staple Gun Match for NIWF TV Title "Bad Ass" Tony Bettis vs. The Ferret King Also appearing the the show will be Rockin' Roddy Taylor! 30 Man Royal Rumble for NIWF World Title on June 17th, 2001 As for the characters left to choose from to make a match, here are the following characters that do not have a match. Make the best out of it and make some sort of match between them and see what happens. Here you go: Chad "Lightning Kid" Shade Poe Curmudgeon Attila Syrus O. Beck Survival Also, a few people have suggested that the following wrestlers be added as part of the event in wrestling their own matches: Christian Hawk XYZ Rockin' Roddy Taylor Feel free to use those guys as well if you need to make some unique matches. Another e-mail is coming your way shortly. If you know anyone else who wants to be in this event, please contact me!!
