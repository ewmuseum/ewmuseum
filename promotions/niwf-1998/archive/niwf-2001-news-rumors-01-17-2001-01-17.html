News & Rumors 01/17/2001

17 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to another Wed. Edition of the News & Rumors in the NIWF, where we talk about the hot topic of the week and the rumblings inside of the NIWF. But before we get to that I just want to remind everyone that roleplays are due to me this SATURDAY no later than 2pm Eastern. The sooner I get them, the easier it will be for me to get out Vocal Explosions. I thank all of you that have sent them in already. If you are confused as to who you face, e-mail me directly and I will send you the schedule. Now let's get onto things inside of the NIWF. -A former NIWF superstar was seen walking out of NIWF Headquarters the other day. Rumor has it that this superstar is looking to make his way back to the NIWF and make yet another impact in the sport. We are talking about none other than the Hollywood Kid, Jason Starr. There has been no official word as to why he was at NIWF headquarters and when approached about why he was there all he had to say was "no comment" -President Lukiw has been trying to get a hold of former NIWF Tag Team the Bomb Squad to help spice up the tag team division but he has not heard any word back to see if they were interested. He even attempted to contact former singles wrestler Hightower but no response. In order for President Lukiw to spice up the tag team division, he had to post bail for a tag team named 20 To Life to get them back in the NIWF. -'Asshole' Andy Simmons is no longer even being discussed in the NIWF. Last week there were some major rumblings about Simmons returning to the NIWF but all talks have ceased. Lukiw now feels that getting Simmons back in the NIWF would be bad for locker room morale and he feels Simmons is a "cancer" and would only proceed to kill the NIWF. -President Lukiw and a wrestler named Dewey were very close to signing on a dotted line last week. Lukiw even went as far as to almost schedule Dewey in a match but then things collapsed. Dewey felt the price just wasn't right to be a part of the NIWF and told Lukiw he would be in touch with him about his needs in the NIWF. -Rumor has it that a former e-wrestler who was somehow associated with Mr. Excitement Dropkick will be making his appearance this weekend to confront Dropkick. We have been trying to get to the bottom of this and the only thing we have learned is that this man is more of a foe than a friend to Dropkick. Things may not look too good. -Rumor has it that The Official Secrets are still a little perturbed over some happenings from their previous NIWF stint. We aren't sure exactly what these happenings are but we are lead to believe that there might be a title involved in it. -President Lukiw has signed two wrestlers to a developmental deal. Supposedly the deal is a one time deal where these two wrestlers will just wrestle on the first Free Per View. We are lead to believe these two men are here just to fill a couple of spots for the rumored Royal Rumble. The two men we are referring to are "Heartbreaker" Matt Foxx and Black Flagg -President Lukiw has signed multiple other talents in the NIWF such as "True 2 The Game" Shane Sharpe, "The Shocker" Brian Anthony, female wrestler "Black Jesus" Sierra Browne, Irish Devil & "Renaissance Man" Patrick Lewis That's all.
