Complete Schedule

25 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is our schedule for our first card back in the NIWF! This is a final one (well, always subject to change). Start with your roleplays and strats now! I'd like to have all strats in by Tuesday and all roleplays & in-rings in by next Saturday 10am Eastern the LATEST! For those who have joined and don't see yourself on the schedule for the first card, fear not for you will definitely be used on the next card after this. This card is booked and I still have more people joining. If you expressed interest in joining and still haven't filled out the app, do so now! If you don't have the app and need to request one then ask me. We are in need of tag teams and female wrestlers right now, those are the two best for all of us. However, singles wrestlers are always welcome!
