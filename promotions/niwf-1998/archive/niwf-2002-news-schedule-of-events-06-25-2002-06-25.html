News & Schedule of Events!

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, just a quick update here. Well, it seems like the NIWF has been very popular around the net now. I've gotten 3 more people to come in and join. 2 singles and 1 tag team, with a possible 2nd tag team and 3rd singles wrestler coming in soon as well. We also have a new match writer on the staff, we are hoping that he provides us with some much needed help in the match writing department (4 matches for me to write is too much). We also had a wrestler quit on us 2 days after he pretty much joined last week, but he said he will be back in due time. With that, the schedule had to be re-adjusted a little bit. For those who just joined, you won't be on the card this weekend unless you send me an in-ring or planned attack of some sort. Feel free to look through the roster at http://niwfwrestling.tripod.com and tell me who you wish to feud with. Otherwise, you will be on the next card.
