NIWF Closing! Free Agents Available!

24 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It is to my sad dismay to announce that after about 5 years of hard work and frustration :P that I have to finally close the NIWF. Since 1993, the NIWF has been a big part of my life and so has e-wrestling. Am I going to quit e-wrestling? No! I just can't handle the responsibilities and pressure of running a fed any more exspecially when I constantly run into problems. Therefore, the NIWF is no more and I will finally close the fed for good. No last card for us, no LEGENDS tournament.. I guess you can say I just went "cold turkey?" Anyhow, this leaves a large variety of free agents open to the e-wrestling community. I have a lot of great talent in my fed that has gone undiscovered and I think that they can make a lot of impact in the feds in the RSPWF. I will be sending out a message to some of the best RSPWF feds out there and will be telling them about some of the talent I have available and where and how to reach them. Also, I have two free agents available of my own. On guys is a crusierweight whom I created known as "Dirty" Dan Lukiw. Of course, that was when I was president, but I think I can change all of that and make myself into an awesome cruiserweight in some feds. The other free agent I have is my famous wrestler known as "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice. These guys will have sample roleplays posted on here and you can take a look and make me an offer or send me an application and rules or whatever else you have. I want to thank all of you who helped make my NIWF experience fun and I hope to see others in feds around the net. -"Dirty" Dan Lukiw- Handler of: "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice "Dirty" Dan Lukiw Former NIWF President & Owner
