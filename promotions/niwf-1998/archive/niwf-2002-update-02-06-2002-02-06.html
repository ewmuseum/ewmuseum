Update 02/06/2002

6 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, just here with another update to let you know I am still alive. What a busy week it has been for me with work and all. Sheesh! I haven't even had the time to even work on the website as I planned! But don't worry, that has no effect at all for the card! Just a reminder to the match writers, I'd like the matches by this Saturday (Sunday the latest). I've also been satisfied with the influx of talent. A pretty good amount to keep things competitive! Like I said, I wish I could let all of you win but I can't :( There are some characters that I absolutely love in the fed too that may not even win their matches this weekend, so don't let it discourage you! Everyone gets their fair chance. Oh yeah, if you by any chance know someone who wants to join with a female wrestler or another tag team, please send them my way! The tag team division is a little bit strong but the female division still needs help! There were a couple of you who asked about joining with a female wrestler! If you are one of them and still want in, then join already! NOW IS THE BEST TIME! Also, I'm happy that some of you could join the chat the other night, I'll highlight it tomorrow in the rumors. Finally, I realize that some of you are part of the @Home switch after they went bankrupt. This means that you will probably have a new provider, such as comcast. If you are aware of what your new e-mail addy may be, then forward it to me NOW before it's too late. Some of you just got to me this week which means you probably missed out on a roleplay show and various pieces of news. I'll give you the schedule again (which is updated) and it's not too late to do in-rings, however it will probably be too late to change my mind on any decisions. Don't fret about it, it's not your fault, and I am sure that you'll bounce back on it too! It actually worked out better for me that this happened with some because it made some decisions much easier.
