Hardcore Edge 9/23/02 (Part Two)

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=============================== Desmond Walker vs. Vile Vince Viper Match Writer: Doug =============================== ["Chitty, Chitty, Bang" by The Rezza Dons plays, as the lights in the arena go out, and "MARQUEE" appears in bold letters across the big screen, and pyro goes off. Highlights of Desmond Walker's matches play and blue and red lights flash thru the arena, as Mad Skillz emerges from behind the curtain followed by Desmond Walker. Walker confidently makes his way down the aisle, ignoring the fans, while Mad Skillz tries to keep up with TRD on the mic, which is thankfully muted.] Announcer: Introducing, being acompanied by Mad Skillz. From San Diego California, standing 6'5" and weighing in at 265 lbs. He is the NIWF Old School Champion... "THE MARQUEE MAN" DESMOND WALKER!!! Billy: Here comes Mr. Old School himself. Johnny: Oh brother, how do you say that with a straight face, Billy? The only reason Desmond Walker has ANY interest in the Old School division is because he can get himself DQ'd whenever he wants and save his belt. Plus it has the added bonus of taking any weapons out of the hands of his opponents. [The camera slowly zooms in, as "Paranoid Android" by Radiohead blasts over the PA system] "Please could you stop the noise I'm trying to get some rest? From all the unborn chicken voices in my head What's that, what's that When I am king you will be first against the wall With your opinion which is of no consequence at all What's that, what's that" [A few purple spotlights sweep over the crowd, and the crowd is cheering like mad as the King of Snakes steps out of the back. Vile Vince Viper is wearing a black, snakeskin vest, with brawny tanned muscles, snakeskin pants, and a black snakeskin vest. Some thong straps can be seen peaking out of his snakeskin pants. He smiles at all his adoring fans in surprise, before turning his attention to the main camera to gloat about how wonderful he is] "Rain down, rain down, come on rain down on me From a great height, from a great height, height Rain down, rain down, come on rain down on me From a great height, from a great height, height" [The smoke machines kick up, sending clouds of noxious vapor into the stadium and the crowd gives it up for the Snakeman. Vile Vince Viper stands on the metal stage, smiling in quiet honor as the fans who hated him for years are giving him a hero's welcome! The Virtuous One looks out across the waving crowd, and nods his head in appreciation] "That's it sir, you're leaving, the crackle of pig skin, the dust and the screaming The yuppies networking the panic, the vomit, the panic, the vomit God loves his children, God loves his children, yeah" [VVV starts down the aisle. He slaps hands with every fan he can, making sure to pose for every picture he can, flicking his tongue like a snake as the audience gets behind him. Triple V rolls into the ring and walks to the buckles, climbing to stand on the second, then throws his fists into the air as the crowd goes wild!!] Announcer: Introducing from Romania! Weighing in at 234 lbs... HE IS THE ONE, THE ONLY!!! VILE! VINCE!!!! VIIIIIIIPER!!!!!!!!!! [MONSTER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: Listen to these fans go crazy for Viper. Don't these people remember who this freak is? Billy: Well he is a bit of a legend around the NIWF. Johnny: He's also a cold blooded, EVIL man. Just wait and see, but remember that I told you so. DING DING Billy: Triple V just standing there face to face with Desmond Walker. The Marquee Man seems to be give him a tongue lashing there. Johnny: He's probobly giving him a lecture on the finer points of technical wrestling. Billy: You're kidding right? Johnny: You have to ask? Billy: Viper has heard enough and he unloads with some stiff right hands! Viper with an irish whip to the buckles... Hard knife edge chops from Viper in the corner! ["WHOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Billy: Viper with a whip to the far side... reversal by Walker... clothesline in the corner by the Marquee Man! Walker takes him down with a snapmare and goes to work with a chin lock. ["BOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: I'll give credit where it's due. Desmond Walker is really starting to pick up a style that, while may not be popular and exciting to watch, is extremely effective. Walker is trying to dictate the pace of this match from the opening bell. Billy: Viper trying to get back to a vertical base... elbow to the midsection... Viper nails him with another! Walker breaks the hold and Viper takes him down with an arm drag! Walker quickly back to his feet and Viper drills him with a hard clothesline! Triple V scooping him up... Big Samoan Drop by Viper!!! [CROWD ROARS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: Viper rolling to the outside now. Johnny: This is where you're gonna see his mean streak come out. Billy: You could be right as Viper has grabbed a steel chair! Viper sliding back into the ring... he lines up to waffle the Marquee Man... NO! Walker feeds Viper that chair with the Dezzinator! [THWACK!!!!] ["OOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: That's an awful fancy name for a dropkick if you ask me. Billy: Simple move, but it did the job. Walker now picking up the chair... [CRACK!!!] Walker wraps it around Vince's head! ["DESMOND SUCKS! DESMOND SUCKS!"] Johnny: Walker should stop grand standing and go for the cover. Billy: Desmond Walker placing the chair on Viper's face before climbing to the top rope. Johnny: This could be messy. Billy: The Marquee Man perched up on the top rope now... LEG DROP ACROSS THE CHAIR!!! ["OOOOOOOHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Billy: Walker rolls him up!!! 1............... 2................ KICKOUT!!! [POWER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: Walker is not happy with the count and he's letting referee Shane Francis know it. Viper getting up slowly... OOOHHHH!!! BRAINBUSTER DDT!!! Johnny: But look at that stupid ass strut around the ring gloating. That's the kind of crap that's gonna cost him this match. Billy: The Marquee Man is extremely proud of himself and he's taking some time now to let Mad Skillz towel him off. Johnny: He better smarten up and pay attention... Billy: Viper to his feet... He clotheslines Walker over the top rope! [POWER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: Skill helping up Walker on the outside... LOOK OUT!!! VIPER WITH A CORKSCREW TOPE!!!! ["OOOOHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] ["VIPER! VIPER! VIPER! VIPER!"] Billy: Viper grabs Walker by the head and drives him into the steel guard rail! Johnny: That just shows the inexperience of Desmond Walker. He had this match going exactly the way he wanted it, but then he had to showboat, and look what happens. Stupid rookie mistake. Billy: Viper pulling a table out from under the ring... [HUGE TABLE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: That's the most "over" inanimate object in the sport. Billy: Viper setting up the table across the ring and barricade. He grabs Walker and lays him out on top. Johnny: Viper's smart. He gave him a couple of shots to the head to make sure he stays there. Billy: Viper now climbing up to the top rope... [MONSTER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: OH MY GAWD!!! TWISTING SENTON BOMB THROUGH THE TABLE!!! ["HOLY-SHIT! HOLY-SHIT! HOLY-SHIT!"] Johnny: That was unreal! Billy: Vile Vince Viper putting his body on the line! Triple V now tossing Walker back into the ring... [The crowd erupts in boos as Roddy Taylor emerges from the back with a baseball bat. He ignores the fans and walks briskly to the ring.] Johnny: What the hell does HE want? Billy: VILE VINCE VIPER GOING FOR THE COVER!!!! ["ONE!!!!!!"] ["TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] [WHACK!!!] ["BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!"] Billy: Roddy Taylor in the ring just hammered Viper with a baseball bat! Johnny: Welcome to the NIWF, Triple V. Billy: Taylor still just pounding Vile Vince with that bat! Wat the hell is wrong with him?! Johnny: He's Canadian. There's something in the beer up there. [The crowd boos and throws trash in the ring as Taylor continues to hammer Viper with the bat. In the mean time, Walker slides over and sticks his arm out of the ring giving Skillz the chance to douse his wrist tape with ether.] Billy: This is sick! What is wrong with Roddy Taylor? He had no business coming out here! Johnny: Ah he likely drank too many Molsons, eh? [The crowd chants, "TAY-LOR SUCKS!" as he walks up the ramp with a big smile on his face.] Billy: Taylor leaving VVV in a puddle of blood! Desmond Walker now picking him up and putting him in the Sleepwalker! Johnny: I don't see the point. Viper is obviously already out cold. Billy: Francis checking the arm... he's out! Roddy Taylor has ruined Vile Vince Viper's return match! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner... "THE MARQUEE MAN" DESMOND WAAAAALKERRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!!! Johnny: Look at that jackass, strutting around the ring like he actually acomplished something here tonight. Billy: The EMT's down at ringside ow loading up Triple V. It's a shame this happened but you can be sure that Roddy Taylor will pay a VERY dear price for his actions tonight. Johnny: Oh, for sure! Viper is NOT a guy you want to cross. Taylor's a marked man now. [The crowd lets out a MONSTER FACE POP as "Wanted: Dead or Alive" begins to play.] Billy: Hey that's Lance Nelson's music! ["NEL-SON! NEL-SON! NEL-SON!"] [Nelson walks out onto the stage in faded denim and his old EPCW t-shirt. The dusty boots and old denim seem to fit him well as he scratches his chin an lifts his mic.] Nelson: Hey Marquee Man!   I couldn't help but here your showboat mouth overload your rowboat ass on Vocal Explosions. Listen up, rook! There have been many before you that felt they could put down this old warhorse, but let me make damn sure you understand that they were better men than you and they ain't around any more. It would seem you claim to know something about old school and that you did the job on your knees to get a title reign. It's important you understand, boy, I don't know something about old school. I BUILT THE DAMN SCHOOL!   So if you want to challenge the teacher...if you want a piece of the Journeyman...bring it on. School's in session. And the subject is Submission 101. ["Wanted: Dead or Alive" plays again as Nelson smirks and leaves the stage.] Johnny: Lance Nelson has called Walker's bluff! Billy: And the Marquee Man does not look to happy about it. Johnny: Hard to blame him. I sure as hell wouldn't want to wrestle Nelson. He'll stretch you in ways the human body was NEVER meant to bend. Lance Nelson IS Old School. Billy: You better believe it! [Commercial] To be continued......
