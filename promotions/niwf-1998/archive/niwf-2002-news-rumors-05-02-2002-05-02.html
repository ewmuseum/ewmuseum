News & Rumors

2 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here we go! -We are hearing in the back that CK is not even sure how he won the Diamond Title now, he just knows that he won it and that's it! Apparently the incident with the "masked man" has really messed up his train of thoughts and he doesn't know what is going on. Dirty Dan is concerned that CK came back too soon from his injury, but can't deny him from wrestling if CK feels he can do it still. -Apparently the whole deal behind Vile Vince Viper and the NIWF was done as part of an angle between the NIWF and SPW. However, Vile Vince Viper will not be wrestling in the NIWF any time soon now after the whole situation and what occurred. Dirty Dan did try to contact VVV into doing some spots for the NIWF, but he is yet unsure of any roles that may be played. For now, VVV will be keeping it low key but may decide to come into the NIWF in the future. -The NIWF would like to welcome some new members to the federation. Those members are none other that Lawrence Buffy and Solid Gold. If you recall correctly, Lawrence Buffy has wrestled in the NIWF before and his last stint was when he had a feud with NaTaS. Solid Gold is a team from the BWF looking to make a name for themselves in the NIWF. -Speaking of NaTaS, Dirty Dan would like to offer congrats to NaTaS who got married this past Sunday. Dirty Dan attended the wedding himself and was glad to see NaTaS so happy. NaTaS is now on his honeymoon in the Catskills in New York. -Apparently Dirty Dan was contacted by someone from MeW about joining the NIWF. This person was actually reccomended to the NIWF by SPW officials. Needless to say, Dirty Dan was skeptical as to why this person wanted to join but still offered a position because he wanted to spice up the Purist Division. The wrestler who contacted him was "Pure Talent" Adam Creed. Creed decided that he would not want to join the NIWF because he felt it was not the place for him. -Apparently an NIWF Superstar has quit the NIWF. "Saint" Ian Walsh gave his release to Dirty Dan the other night, asking to be taken off of cards because of his own personal reasons. Dirty Dan wishes Ian Walsh luck and will always have an open spot for him. This means that the 3-way dance for the TV Title will only be a one on one encounter between Brad "Buff" Baggs and "Handsome" Henry. Also, Ian Walsh's spot in the rumble will be taken by Christian Hawk. -Speaking of Christian Hawk, as you saw last week he came out and publically challenged Attila. Christian Hawk is now a part of the NIWF and is looking to get his revenge. However, Attila is not a part of the NIWF and neither are any of his associates. While the NIWF would like to have Attila back, there has been no way of contacting him to tell him about the challenge issued to him. -As you all saw, many former NIWF stars have joined the ranks of the current NIWF. The following wrestlers are now signed, sealed and delivered and a part of the NIWF family: Axel Schmidt, "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice, Roddy Taylor, Eric Henry, Todd Lucchesi... Justin Starr is not a part of the NIWF but did this for a one time deal. Apparently Justin Starr wanted to take a leave from e-wrestling. -As you all saw, Damage Inc. was attacked by MiB. While many are speculating MiB was the one who was sending those notes and send the bloody cow heart, the NIWF has learned that they are not the ones. Apparently the person who is doing it will continue to do it until "Natasha" shows up. The NIWF has no idea what the history is with all of this, but would like to finally see this angle unwind and finish. -Many people have been worried about the new talent coming in. They have been worried that their own positions may be sacrificed now and they won't get the necessary pushes or won't be recognized. The NIWF assures all of you that if you were in the fed since we re-opened, then you will be on the higher end of the stick for pushes as you have stuck with us since the beginning. The new talent that came in will have to work their way up to the top in order to get where the talent who started with the re-opening are. -Just a reminder, roleplays are due in by Saturday at 10am Est. the latest! There has been some active participation, but there are some that are still be to heard from which the NIWF would like to hear from. Also, if there is anyone on the list who feels that they can write a match for the Free Per View AND get it to Dirty Dan on time, then get in touch! We may need an extra match writer or two to make this card even better than it is planned to be. -Finally, Dirty Dan has been in some talks recently about re-opening up the NIWF website. While there is always talk about doing this, he feels that these talks are more serious than before. So serious that we may see a brand new NIWF website at the end of the weekend this weekend. On it will be all the old cards, roleplay shows, profiles for all the wrestlers, etc. The details will be finalized by the weekend.
