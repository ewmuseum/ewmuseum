Apollo Report

24 October 2002

Our version of the Ross Report! The Apollo Report: Wednesday Oct. 23 2002 Greetings, NIWF fans, and welcome to my thoughts and ramblings. I have long been the voice of the NIWF but this will be my first crack at writing a collumn. So without futher ado, let's take the Nestea Plunge... - All of us in the NIWF are extremely excited about the debut of Mayhem this Sunday. I think the format of the show is innovative and very entertaining. I plan on hosting the show with Jason Starr, while Mark Hunter and Johnny Simpson call the action from ringside. - Speaking of Johnny Simpson. He's been asked by managment to stop pulling the punches. Simpson has been taking it easy on the talent so as not to upset the powers that be. Well it turns out that they wanted him to be loud and obnoxious all the while. Look for a very different Johnny Simpson on Mayhem. - Doug Hardy was recently released from jail and has returned to work. However, he's being kept as a backstage interviewer because the front office is sick of his childish dick jokes. He may someday move back into his announce chair but he'll have to prove himself first. - The front office was very happy with Bad Intentions. President Doug [name withheld] thought the show was one of the best NIWF programs to date. All the workers did a fantastic job. - [name withheld] commented that he was overly impressed with the performance of Brad Baggs. Many people wrote in and said that Baggs didn't deserve a program with Steele. I think he's proven the naysayers wrong. He'll be getting a push along with a new moniker. Watch for big things from "The Total Knockout". - Jack Russell has also been turning a few heads in the front office. While his gimmick is contraversial, he's a great worker and a total heat machine. I'm really looking forward to seeing how he does against Eric Idol. - It sure was nice to see Andrew Starkey back last weekend. Look for him to become a major player in the near future. - Roddy Taylor is amazing. He's on the president's short list for World title shots. Also on that list are Desmond Walker, One Man Army, & Brad Baggs. - Eric Idol continues to be impressive. He'll likely headline the next Free Per View with Sean Steele. - The tag team ranks have thinned now as Solid Gold is taking a leave of absence. The Deities now get the dubious distinction of being champions of a one team division. Which is too bad because the Prophet's boys have really worked hard and are deserving champions. Watch for several singles wrestlers to get paired off so that the Deities can have some challengers. A couple of potential teams floating around are; Army/Taylor, Nelson/Jade, and Kuhl/Starkey. - [name withheld] is very unimpressed with Bad Element. The experiment is being givin one last chance to click. After that, it's off to SPW with them. - [name withheld], former BWF Promoter, was in talks with President [name withheld] this week. Insiders say that he may be brought on as an advisor. That's good news for the NIWF if that happens. Injury Update: - Lawrence Buffy suffered a minor neck injury at the FPV. He won't require surgury but will be out for at least 8 weeks. - Goldenboy is working with a broken jaw. Just one of the many facial injuries suffered at the hands of Jack Russell. - Mad Skillz is undergoing reconstructive surgery to his face. He hopes to return in a few months to compete in the new Cruiserweight division. - Shawn Osborne has a broken jaw (3 places) and he'll be out for an unknown period of time. - Desmond Walker has a broken arm but will continue to work through the injury. - Andrew Starkey will be cleared to wrestle just in time for Mayhem. Doctor's are concerned that he may re-injure his shoulder if he tries to do too much, too soon. - Adam Kuhl was lucky this weekend. Surgeons were able to re-attach his pinkie finger and he should regain full use of the digit. That's it for this week. See you in a few days on the debut of NIWF MAYHEM! Billy Apollo
