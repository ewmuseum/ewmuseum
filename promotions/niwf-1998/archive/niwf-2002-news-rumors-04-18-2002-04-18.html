News & Rumors 04/18/2002

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's Thursday night folks, you know what that means.. right? Here we go! -There is a big rumor backstage that an unexpected wrestler is about to get the biggest push he has ever dreamed and it may occur on the very next card. Many of the wrestlers and managers are not sure of who this unexpected wrestler is that will get the push, but we do know that he is currently already in the NIWF and is not a new wrestler. Though there are some that believe that CK is the first on the list to receive this push since he has been a good sport thus far and taken much abuse from the likes of The Masked Man. -This weekend on Vocal Explosions is supposedly going to be the first of two interviews by one of the mystery men in the Royal Rumble match itself. Some of the production staff had a look at the interview and thought it was a long and well thought out interview. It was told that the man doing the interview didn't get all of what he needed to say in this interview because he wanted to save it for later use, but the production staff and Dirty Dan are very happy with his efforts thus far as a mystery man and have some serious future plans for him. -The NIWF wants to welcome a new singles wrestler to the scene. That singles wrestler is Tyler "American Made" Johnson. Not much is known about Johnson yet, but the NIWF is looking forward to his debut wrestling match which is expected to be AFTER the Free Per View. However, if Johnson wishes to get involved in matches before hand then he is more than welcome to get involved or be active in the fed beforehand. -Seen coming out of the NIWF Towers: Mr. Excitement Dropkick. Not much is known between a meeting between Dropkick and Dirty Dan as of yet, but it is apparent that Dirty Dan wants the very first NIWF World Champ to come back to the NIWF in some way, shape or form. Whether it is as a wrestler, commentator, referee or whatever.. is yet to be seen. -The NIWF Card is scheduled to come out next weekend and it is the last card before the Free Per View. On this last card, it is rumored that there will be more angles than actual wrestling itself. The NIWF asks all of you be patient with these matches and if they don't occur for your character or are too short, just to let it be because what is about to happen is something that is great for the future of the NIWF. -The NIWF has also signed a former superstar in Adam Kuhl! Supposedly Adam Kuhl has signed a Free Per View only deal and has worked it out with the NIWF where he can be used to fill spots in regular cards when necessary but would like to only wrestle on Free Per Views. Supposedly Adam Kuhl is another participant in the Diamond Title match. -Speaking of the Diamond Title, rumor has it that the Diamond Champion.. Todd Lucchesi, will be doing some sort of segment about the Diamond Title and what it means to him. It will be airing on NIWF Television and SPW Television as well. Where this segment is to occur is unknown as of yet. -Since we are on the subject of titles, Dirty Dan is rumored to actually go through with activating two more titles in the fed once again. One title is that of the Cruiserweight Title, which may have an 8 man tournament starting after the Free Per View. Dirty Dan feels that this will attract even a better cruiserweight crowd to the NIWF and keep the wrestlers within their weight divisions (unless specified otherwise.) There also has been talks of bring back the International Title, which is only to be defended in countries outside of the United States.. but that has not been determined yet. -As all of you have seen, The Masked Man has signed an NIWF Contract effective at the Rumble for the Free Per View. He announced at that time, he will be out of Shootfire Pro Wrestling and will be joining the NIWF. What we know of the Masked Man is that he is a current wrestler in Shootfire Pro. Speculation on some names: Jeffery Dylan Marsh, "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice, Ian Lightfoot and 'Asshole' Andy Simmons just to name a few. Whether it is one of these men or none of these men is yet to be seen. -It is apparent that some former and current NIWF Stars will be appearing in a tournament called the GNCW Super 8 Tournament. Wrestlers from the NIWF, SPW and the now defunct BWF will most likely participate in this fed. However, there will be no "representation" for a certain fed in this tournament as it will all be just for the PRIDE of a character. If you are interested in showing up for the tournament and are one of the former NIWF GREATS then e-mail [name withheld] at this address: [detail withheld] -Finally, the rest of the Free Per View card will be announced after the next card. Dirty Dan is still trying to put all his cards into place and can't do this until after the next card when certain matches and certain angles all occur. One of these matches that is included is the Diamond Title match, which he believes will shape up after the next card. He will also announce the lineup for the card afterwards, where most likely all the new wrestlers who are going to or have joined the NIWF will make their debut. Let's not forget to roleplay this weekend and send me in-rings, attacks and strats for the next card ASAP!
