NIWF — Rules

Rules & Regulations

2 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
===================================== [INTRODUCTION] ===================================== Hello, some of you may or may not know me but let me introduce myself. [name withheld]. I ran a league called the NIWF a while ago which I eventually had to shut down because I was lazy and didn't want to do any work to make it a success. Well, I am back and now I am re-starting the league known as the NIWF, New Internet Wrestling Federation. Well, it won't be all extreme and hardcore like it was... it will be a big mix of things. I guess you can consider this as a sort of FAQ on this league for what it's worth. I'm going to try and do my best to keep this league alive and running. Here is some more info and some more rules. ===================================== [NIWF Staff] ===================================== Right now, the behind the scenes staff consists of me only! I think I could really use some help and hopefully some people will be nice enough to step forward and help me out. I would like to have a Vice President so if anybody is interested, e-mail me! I am looking for someone with a lot of experience and who knows what the NIWF is about. I am also in desperate need of match writers. I need someone who will be consistent and will be reliable and can write 1 or 2 matches on a weekly basis! Let's face it, I won't be writing matches on my own! [name withheld] Vice President: [name withheld] [name withheld] Writers: [name withheld] [name withheld] ==================================== [Cards & Free Per Views] ==================================== Well, like every fed, there will always be a card! The cards will only occur once a week, unless I get enough writers and enough wrestlers to put out a card twice a week!. The cards will usually come out on Sundays and will be known as "Hardcore Edge". Catchy name, huh? Sometimes I will delay the card if something comes up and I always let you people know ahead of time. It happens to all the great feds out there, but I will do my best to try and get each and every card out on time. As for the PPV... NO! We don't have PPV's because I don't believe in having to "pay" to read a match or a series of matches sent via e-mail! Instead, I call them Free Per Views! How often do they occur? Well, I will try to have about 4 a year and most likely, they will occur once every 3 months. The reason for this is because I want to help develop angles and story lines and I'm sick of seeing a fed bringing out a "PPV" every month! Which reminds me, don't expect to get a huge push about two weeks after an angle has just surfaced. I'm a strong believer of long feuds and long angles. I've seen so many feds that will just trash an angle after about a month. I'll make the angle last for 4 months. Most of you who already were in the NIWF know what I mean. ===================================== [Titles] ===================================== NIWF World Title- This title is given to the person with the greatest performance both in and out of the ring. It's the ttle that can be owned by anybody for that matter. It's pretty self explainitory but there is one catch. This title must be defended oce every 60 days. However, I do strongly encourage you to defend as much as you can so you don't fall out of favor with me. NIWF Diamond Title- This title is given to the wrestler with the most extreme and hardcore performance. This is a good title to hold if you make a hardcore wrestler. The title is laced with Diamonds and is worth more than the World Title because frankly, I think people who put their bodies on the line are the most hardest workers of all. NIWF TV Title- This title must be defended every week. However, this is the title designed for some of the *non* or *small* roleplayers. I think it's only fair that people get a shot and if you choose not to RP, it's fine by me but know you will not get too far and will have pretty crappy matches written for you. But, you still get to stay in the league. NIWF North American Title- The title that must be defended every 2-3 weeks. This title is designed for the average players. I basically decide who the average players are in this league, and you will all know who I am talking about. Don't be offended if I consider you an average player! I've actually had NIWF Superstars who have held the world title actually hold this title. It all depends on the angle. NIWF Crusierweight Title- The only wrestlers that may go after this title are the ones who are 230lbs and under. This is designed for some of the smaller, fast paced wrestlers in the league. I may make exceptions say if your guy is like 2 lbs over the limit or if it involves an angle. NIWF Tag Team Titles- I never had much luck with the tag team scene, so for now I am making only one tag team title. But, if enough tag teams show up, another Tag Title will be established. NIWF International Title- The only way you can win this title or even be a conteneder for this title is if you reside (character wise) outside the United States. This title will only be defended on foreign grounds. NIWF Six Man Tag Title- This title has been currently established. The reason why this title was established was only because of all the "gangs" and "stables" people started to create. It was getting out of hand, so I started this title. NIWF Purist's Dream Title- This is for the NON Hardcore Loving Fan! Also a new title which is established. Every match that happens for this title or for this division will of course have rules heavily implemented in the matches! NIWF has rules? You've got to be kidding me! These are for all the wrestlers who don't like wrestling a hardcore or extreme match. NIWF Women's Title: This is another new title to the NIWF. Seeing that the woman's scene in e-wrestling has grown popularity, the NIWF has decided that the best way for their women to demonstrate their ability is to do it in the ring! A lot of women wrestlers have been strongly interested in this title. Only the best woman wrestler will hold this title. =================================== [Announcers of NIWF] =================================== There are *two* main announcers here in the NIWF. Both of these guys work the shows, Free Per Views, and the Vocal Explosions. Boy, they must get paid a lot, huh? If the NIWF goes to a two show format, I'm sure they will be looking for some new fresh announcers to help them. Billy Apollo is basically the good guy announcer, the man who calls all the action as he sees it. Unfortunately, he is also the person who also gets knocked around by the wrestlers for his stupid comments and questions he asks. Doug Hardy is the comedic heel. The man is basically a bit like Joel Gertner, but a little bit heavier. He is obsessed with his 14lb ham and weighs in at a good 350lbs. But, he is also one to call the action as he sees it, in his own interesting way. ===================================== [RP Rules] ===================================== As of now, I am allowing *one* RP a week. That one RP is the RP you must keep for that week. Please don't tell me to keep on changing it and all that good stuff because I won't. It's not required that you roleplay, because I want to make this league open to everyone, but it does help if you do roleplay. If you don't understand what a Roleplay <RP> is, then you got problems but I will kindly explain it to you. A Roleplay is basically like trash talking your opponent. It's as simple as that. If you're a face <good guy>, then you get the crowd to cheer so then you must write up a *good* roleplay being nice a friendly and all that stuff. If you're a heel, then you write like you hate people and your a bad ass. Oh yeah, that's another thing... please, do *not* send me a *real* wrestler because I will just delete it. So, if you want to.. fill out the app. This league was even built around or the people who "don't have much time" As for the rules of the roleplays, I am not very strict on the content of the roleplays. I allow the cursing, and I will allow pretty much anything to go in a roleplay to some extent. For example, back in the NIWF, I had one character constantly do porno movies on there. It was pure brilliance. Why do I believe in being able to do these type of things? Because I know none of this is meant to be real! It's meant to be fun! What I don't like to see is using someone else's character in a roleplay, depending on how it's done. If it's supposed to be a video tape or audio tape of another character, I usually will allow it. However, if the other character is being attacked in a roleplay or is actually being used like the person who wrote the roleplay actually handles it, then I might have a bit of a problem there. Other than that, this is fantasy and pretty much anything can happen in a fantasy world. But why do I go away from the "realistic" approach? Because if we went in that direction, we would be like every other fed out there who restricts people to some brilliant ideas in a roleplay. Normally, handlers do keep things realistic, but you have the select few who are willing to do their part as well. Also, you can send instructions in for a match if you like. If you want to attack somebody in the locker room, or during their match, then let me know and I'll do what I can. There is no attacking others in their roleplays! Not is just plain dumb! If you are wrestling and want to tell me how you want your wrestler to wrestle, then tell me. Oh yeah, the list will be blind copied all the time so I won't expose e- mail addresses. ===================================== [Winning Matches & Establishing Titles] ===================================== Ok, here is a little hint to you. No matter how well you thought you did with your rolelay, and no matter what kind of instructions you send, I might see that the other person did a better job of it. So, even if you are a big name in the e-wrestling world, you are bound to lose a match every once in a while. Nobody can realistically win every single match unless I see they are really good! I'm actually thinking about getting a committee of people together so we can vote to see exactly who should win what match. But for now, I make the final decisions. {Winning Matches} Now, here is something new I want to try for winning matches... it involves money. Everybody starts off with a balance of $0. Don't worry, I'll do the math. Those who have been in the fed in the past, your balance stays with you from the last time around since I have all of that on record. For those that are new to the fed, I hate to say it, but you will have to work your way up to the top! Just like it is done in every other fed! At that point, here is what you get for winning a match <not for real, but for fantasy> Winning Main Event Match- $25,000 Losing Main Event Match- $2,000 Winning Title Match- $10,000 Losing Title Match- $500 Winning Regular Match- $1,000 Losing Regular Match- you owe us $100 If you lose so many matches where you owe us over $15,000.... then I'm gonna fire you because I won't feel you are getting the job done, unless it's part of an angle of course. I can give out fines and such if I choose, but that is only if you choose to do something real drastic. ===================================== [Contracts & Rules] ===================================== I'm going to enforce the contract rule here in the NIWF. Basically, you sign your contract based on weeks. I'll let you sign all the way up to 52 weeks <which is roughly a little over a year> and I will let you sign a minimum of 6 weeks. After your contract is up, I have the option of whether or not I wish to renew your contract with you or you have the option of whether or not you choose to work within the league any more. As for the rules of the matches... what rules? This is extreme and hardcore! Ok, except for the Purist's Dream Division. Keep it to a certain degree <no raping, masturbating or anything else like what was done in the past> There will be no DQ's and no countouts. You can win by Submission and pinfall only. There may be a certain level where we do call DQ's or countouts but it depends on the wrestler and the match writer. Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/
