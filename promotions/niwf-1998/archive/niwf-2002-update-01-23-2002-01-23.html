Update 01/23/2002

23 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well folks, another update for you guys! I am thinking of making a few changes to how the cards and roleplay shows are being run. I can't believe how many people have expressed interest in joining the NIWF and I really feel that if that continues, then this fed can be a huge success! For those that have expressed interest but haven't filled out an app yet, DO SO NOW!! The app is at the bottom of the message! The matches are filling up quickly so if you wait then you may not get on the next card. I'll talk about more in the rumors coming out tomorrow. Right now, we can use any type of wrestler. Singles are more than welcome but of course if you have a tag team or female wrestler then get them in here too. We need those to help fill the matches properly. Speaking of matches, here is what I want to do. On a Saturday I will have Vocal Explosions and the next Sunday (not the one right after the Saturday of VE, but the following one) I want to have Hardcore Edge, then the following Saturday it's VE and then the following Sunday it's Hardcore Edge. Etc. Etc. Follow me? The reason for this is because I want the fed to be competitive and I would hate to see someone send in two quality roleplays to beat out one quality roleplay. I want just ONE quality roleplay to help decide the match. You'll understand. This will start AFTER the first card. Speaking of the first card, here is what we have in store! Please don't let the tournament deter you away from the fact that there will be a world title as well.
