LEGENDS!!!!!

22 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sorry if the post seems personal. I want to make sure it gets out to anyone who once participated in my fed and I know people from the fed read the group. If you're not interested, ignore it.. if you want to know more, read on. ------------- Well people, there comes a time when a former e-fed president decides to himself that he needs to go back and look at how the fed folded. I did just that and decided to myself that the NIWF did NOT fold with a final card the way it should have... twice I have pretty much been forced to shut down. Well, I decided to make the NIWF's final card EVER and I will write it all by myself.... but it will take some time. I definitely want LEGENDS of the NIWF to participate in this final night called NIWF LEGENDS. I am including a list of e-wrestlers I want to participate in this event.... I know that ALL of you will really be up for it as it will be NIWF's final event EVER.. think of it like the way WCW had their final event. I will NOT make your character look bad in any way and pretty much put on the best show I can for you. If there are any attacks on your wrestler that people want to run as part of an angle outside the NIWF, I will make sure I run it by you in case you don't wish to particpate in an angle. I am going to assume the following wrestlers will participate and start to think of the best matches possible and make a little schedule in a few weeks... but, if you do NOT want to be a part of this.. PLEASE PLEASE PLEASE let me know and I will make sure you will not be included in this wonderful event to end it all so this way I will not use your character in anyway. Otherwise, if I do not hear from you... then I assume you want part of this (sending me a message saying it's a great idea would be good too so this way I know you are into it.) Also, anyone with a * next to them is someone I have not been able to get a hold of yet so if you have a past e-mail address for this wrestler's handler or a current one, then please send it this way. I would also like for all of you to vote for what you think will be the possible best matches. In other words.. YOU BE THE BOOKER! Tell me what match you would make out of the following list, the matches with the most people booking it to take place will happen. The only match that can't be booked and is set in stone is the NIWF Royal Rumble where the winner will be declared the very last NIWF World Champ for GOOD. Now, here is the list and following it is the list of matches I would make. P.S. If anyone from outside feds would like to get involved for an angle between your character and another wrestler or involved in the night of events somehow, e-mail me at [detail withheld] List of Participants: Mr. Bungle Assaf "The Terminator" Levavy Jimmy Cougar & Jason Starr (Glamour Boys) "Strangler" Shawn Osborne/Lawrence Buffy (Greatest Team Ever) Mr. Excitement Dropkick/Larry Gates (2 Exciting Dudes) [name withheld] Chad "Lightning Kid" Shade* Jerry "The Bulldog" Jericho* Syrus O. Beck* Survival Patty Babes Carter Jeffery Dylan Marsh "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice "Dirty" Dan Lukiw (Yeah, I know.. but try to use the other wrestlers first.. I'm sure every wrestler that was involved in my fed would love to wrestle me!) Adam Kuhl Attila Curmudgeon* Todd Lucchesi "The Extreme War Machine" Corey Irons Havoc Axel Schmidt The Specialists* NaTaS Amber* Poe "Asshole" Andy Simmons Vile Vince Viper "Bad Ass" Tony Bettis* Chaz Darby Smilin' Jack Ruby Ian Lightfoot The Ferret King* Angus MacGregor* If You feel someone is missing, tell me who and WHY. Scheduled Match: 30 Man Royal Rumble for NIWF World Title My Votes: Cruiserweight Title Match Ian Lightfoot vs. Chad "Lightning Kid" Shade -Possibly two of the best cruiserweights to walk into the NIWF. Ian Lightfoot went on a very big hot streak back in about 98 and won the Cruiserweight Title. Chad "Lightning Kid" Shade (ran by [name withheld]) has not been heard from in a while but basically brought the cruiserweight aspect into the NIWF Triple Threat Match for NIWF North American Title Chaz Darby vs. Jerry "The Bulldog" Jericho vs. Smilin' Jack Ruby -Jerry Jericho was the first original champ when the NIWF had this title as the US Title. He won it many times, exchanging the title back and forward between himself and Dylan Dice in their brutal barbed wire matches. Chaz Darby and Jack Ruby were two men who fueded over this title and exchanged many words and many fists. The NIWF fans never felt this fued ended, but now Jerry Jericho can be thrown into the mix to spice things up and make it interesting. Staple Gun Match for NIWF TV Title The Ferret King vs. "Bad Ass" Tony Bettis -For a title that was never meant to be hardcore, this title has proven to have many of the hardcore matches. The Ferret King started the wave of hardcoreness for this title and eventually dropped it when he quit the fed. However, "Bad Ass" Tony Bettis was the first ever TV Champ... he made the TV Title after he won the Global Title in a interfed PPV between the NIWF and AUWF, convincing many people that the NIWF was the place to be after trashing the unified Global Title and proclaiming it the new TV Title. Purist's Dream Match Assaf "The Terminator" Levavy vs. Patty Babes Carter -Carter, now known as Patrick Lewis, has always been a great technical wrestler... as has Assaf Levavy. But neither of these two have ever met in the NIWF and never have had any of these classic "wrestling" matches.. well, we want to give the fans what the deserve to see!! 4 Man Lunatic Fringe Match for NIWF International Title Angus MacGregor* vs. Vile Vince Viper vs. Axel Schmidt vs. "The Extreme War Machine" Corey Irons 3 of the 4 participants have been International Champ before... VVV has not been, but has never been defeated in the NIWF before. The rules of the Lunatic Fringe match apply the same way as the 10 man Lunatic Fringe match does.... but with only 4 people. Rules? There are no rules. You can beat your opponent any time and any where in or outside the building by either pinfall or submission. When a wrestler gets pinned, he is eliminated from the match. It continues until one man is left standing. 'Asshole' Andy Simmons vs. Poe Notice there is no gimmick in this match... why? Because both of these men are already gimmicks. The battle of the gimmicks, the battle of the two biggest perverts to ever step foot into the NIWF. The porn king vs. the man with the large cock! Who will come out on top here? Will egos clash? This is a match we could not miss. Submission Match NaTaS vs. Amber -We all know the little obsession NaTaS had with Amber for a long time, but Amber never gave him the time of day. This match should be made. Amber, a great female wrestler taking on the sick man in NaTaS. Will Jack Daniels help NaTaS win this match? Match for NIWF Tag Team Titles The Specialists vs. The Greatest Team Ever -Both of these teams were literally the best teams to ever step foot in the NIWF. Neither of them have faced each other in the NIWF. Once and for all the debate will end on which team is the best team to ever set foot in the NIWF with this match. Exploding Ring Barbed Wire Match Havoc vs. "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice -The fued started, it never ended. Havoc and Dice have never met one on one in the NIWF. While many may believe they have, at the time it was Devon Dice that was using Dylan's name.. after all, they are twin brothers. These two had a classic battle back in 1996 when Dice won the NIWF World Title in a similar 3 way match between himself, Havoc and Poe. For years after that, Havoc went around "copying" Dylan Dice and everything that Dice did in and out of the ring. Old fireworks will re-unite in this match I am sure. Return Match Curmudgeon vs. Todd Lucchesi -A battle of two former NIWF World Champions. When Curmudgeon lost the NIWF World Title, he felt he lost it without a fair shot. He got the title back and Todd Lucchesi wanted his chance to get back the title that he lost to Curmudgeon at the time (at the time, C-Mud 10). Good be a good old fashion "slobberknocker" as Jim Ross would say Thumbtack Match for NIWF Diamond Title Attila vs. Jeffery Dylan Marsh -Two former NIWF Diamond Champs going at it. Currently, Shootfire Pro is in possesion of the NIWF Diamond Title but I am sure that JDM would be more than happy to convince his buddies there to let him bring it to the event for one last time. Attila is one hardcore mother f*cker and can be very dangerous in the ring... JDM is not a seasoned veteran but an improved rookie who has gained a lot in the last few years. Both of these men can put on one hell of a show. Steel Cage Match Syrus O. Beck vs. Survival -Ahhh.. the battle of two of the toughest SOB's to ever step foot in the NIWF. And what better way to do it than to put them in a nice steel cage with steel cage ruling applied (climbing over the top or crawling out). Both of these men smoke and one of them has been caught drinking on the job before. Could be interesting. NIWF vs. BWF - Hell in the Cell Match "Dirty Dan" Lukiw vs. [name withheld] -The match that should of happened, but instead Lukiw got a hold of Diamond Doug instead. Bad blood runs between these two fed heads and the battle they have had between their two feds. It went as far as Lukiw coming into the BWF and getting FIRED because of creative differences between himself and another staff member. [name withheld] made the choice and ever since then, Lukiw has not fogave him. Then, [name withheld] brought in all of his BWF cronies and created a large stable in the NIWF and ran rampant through the entire fed before it was all put to an end at Seven Deadly Sins II. This fued between the two feds needs to be taken care of once and for all. Old School Style Match Glamour Boys vs. 2 Exciting Dudes Jimmy & Jason need to be coaxed into coming out of their retirement and putting the boots on just one more time. The 2 Exciting Dudes almost reformed a few months ago before the NIWF folded again. Mr. Excitement Dropkick has made it known that he has always wanted a piece of this team. Both of these teams were one of the first two original tag teams to ever step foot in the original NIWF (then known as the IWF) Mr. Bungle vs. Adam Kuhl -Just for the sake of making a match. Not sure why.. but I never saw either of these two fueding with anyone on this list seriously.. so how about they fight each other? Both are considered cruiserweights and both can go hardcore, especially KUHL who is a complete maniac. There you have it... you can roleplay for this event, but you don't have to.. you can send strats or an in-ring but you don't have to either. Send me your list of matches.
