Vocal Explosions 9-29-02 (Part Three)

29 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=NIWF========================= Ryan Roberts vs. Lance Nelson ============================== Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is set for one fall! ["Darkness" by Rage Against The Machine blares as Ryan Roberts walks down the aisle. He's dressed in a black leather trench coat and carrying a kendo stick.] Announcer: Introducing from Detroit, Michigan. Standing 6'4" and weighing in at 258 lbs... "THE DEATH DEALER" RYAN ROBERTS!!! ["BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: It looks like Ryan Roberts forgot to bring his cheering section tonight. Mark: Your right. Not a friendly reaction for the Death Dealer. But I imagine things are about to get real loud in here. [The arena goes dark as spots pan the crowd. As the opening twelve strings play "Wanted: Dead or Alive" the spots focus on the entryway and silhouette a man in black...wearing the silver of the EPCW/MCWA] Announcer: Introducing, from El Paso, TX, weighing in at 278 lbs, he forever remains the Lone Star Champion... "THE JOURNEYMAN" LANCE NELSON!!! [MONSTER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] ["NEL-SON! NEL-SON! NEL-SON!"] Johnny: I can't hear myself think, Mark. Mark: The Journeyman has arrived and this capicity crowd is on it's feet! [Nelson slaps hands with a few fans on his way to the ring. He removes his robe and Lone Star title belt outside the ring and then slides in under the bottom rope.] [DING DING] Mark: Roberts not wasting any time! The Death Dealer stomping away on Nelson. Johnny: He didn't even let the man get to his feet. Mark: All's fair in love and wrestling. Roberts with a handful of hair picks him up and sends him back down with a snapmare! Nelson back to his feet and Roberts sends him for the ride. Nelson off the ropes... leap frog by Roberts... Nelson from the far side... baseball slide through the legs by Nelson... Roberts turns around and Nelson drops him with a textbook dropkick! [CROWD ROARS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: Look at Lance going to work on that knee joint already. Mark: Nelson cranks the pressure with a spinning toe hold. Roberts kicks out of it with a boot to the face! Nelson is stunned. Roberts back up and he nails the Journeyman with a swinging neckbreaker! Johnny: Ryan Roberts seems much more focussed than usual. Mark: The Death Dealer continuing the assault now with a powerbomb! Roberts goes for the quick cover! ["ONE!!!"] ["TWO!!!!!!"] ["OOOOOHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!"] Mark: Only a two count! Johnny: That was real close though. Roberts is really getting it together here tonight. I'm impressed... and I don't impress easy. Mark: Roberts with a handful of hair... Woah, small package by the Journeyman! ["ONE!!!!!"] ["TWO!!!!!"] ["THREE, OOOOOHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!"] Mark: Kickout by Roberts! Nelson shoots Roberts into the ropes... back body drop by the Journeyman! Nelson grabs the foot... HE'S GOING FOR THE FIGURE FOUR!!! [CROWD ROARS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Mark: No! Roberts scrambles to the ropes and Nelson is forced to break. Johnny: That's some quick thinking by Roberts. He obviously has the Submission 101 scouted. Mark: Both men back up. Nelson with the whip to the buckles... Roberts reverses! Nelson into the corner and Roberts follows him in with a hard clothesline! The Death Dealer unloading with some hard knife edge chops! ["WHOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: Ouch. That's gonna leave a mark. Mark: Roberts climbing up to the second rope and hammering some closed fists into Nelson's forehead! ["ONE, TWO, THREE, FOUR... OOOOHHHHHH!!!!!!!"] Mark: Nelson scoops him up and nails him with a reverse atomic drop! Nelson following up with a West Texas Uppercut! Another one! Nelson runs to the ropes... Big Texas Lariat!!! ["OOOOOHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: Aka: The clothesline from hell! Mark: Nelson with the lateral press... ["ONE!!!!!!!!!!"] ["TWO!!!!!!!!!!!"] ["OOOHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!"] Mark: Kickout by Roberts! Johnny: Man, we're look at a new Death Dealer here tonight. I've never seen him this focussed! Mark: Nelson setting him up for a vertical suplex... Roberts blocks it... Roberts nails him with a Brainbuster suplex! Johnny: Ouch! He dropped the Journeyman right on his head! [Roberts takes the opportunity to slide out of the ring and grab a table.] ["TABLE! TABLE! TABLE!"] Johnny: The pace of this match is about to change drastically! Mark: Ryan Roberts setting the table up in the middle of the ring! Nelson getting to his feet... OOHH!!! ROBERTS WITH A DROP TOE HOLD AND NELSON'S FACE IS DRIVEN INTO THE TABLE! ["OOOOHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: His face knocked a big chunk off the corner of that table! Mark: Roberts now rolling Nelson onto the table... he's heading to the top rope!!! Johnny: HIGH SPOT! Mark: ROBERTS OFF THE TOP WITH A SWANTON BOMB!!!! ["OOOHHHHHHH!!!!!!!"] Johnny: He missed!!! Mark: Nelson got out of the way just in time and Roberts went through the table on his own! Nelson now giving the signal... Johnny: SCHOOL IS IN SESSION!!! ["1-0-1! 1-0-1! 1-0-1!"] Mark: NELSON WITH THE SUBMISSION 101! HE'S GOT THE FIGURE FOUR LOCKED ON TIGHT AND ROBERTS HAS NOWHERE TO GO!!! [The crowd lets out a huge heel pop as Desmond Walker jogs down to ringside.] Johnny: What the hell is he doing out here????!!!! [Walker grabs a chair and slides into the ring. Without hesitation he plasters Nelson in the head with it.] Mark: Desmond Walker just nailed Lance Nelson in the back of the head with a chair, and he was forced to break that figure four! Walker now pointing to his wrist pads, and I think we're going to see it! [MONSTER HEEL POP] Mark: THE SLEEPWALKER!!!! Desmond Walker's locked his signature maneuver in tightly, and Lance Nelson passes out immediately!!! He drops Nelson to the canvas, and Roberts makes the cover!!! ["ONE!!!!!] ["TWO!!!!!!"] ["THREE!!!!!!!!!!!"] ["BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: What a jerk! [DING DING DING] Announcer: Here is your winner... "THE DEATH DEALER" RYAN ROBERTS!!!!! [Desmond Walker grabs a mic and gets right up in Lance Nelson's face, but he's still out of it.] Desmond Walker: Lance Nelson, you just got a glimpse of what Old School is all about, and just why Desmond Walker is the Old School champ! You call yourself a warhorse, and you claim that you BUILT the school? Well, that tells me that you're an old man, Nelson! You haven't got what it takes to hang with a star like me, and soon, I'll prove it one on one in the ring. But, if were you, I'd just stay sleeping old man. There is a place for someone like you in this business, Nelson. It's in the back, cleaning the toilets, or washing out the real wrestler's jockstraps! ["WALKER SUCKS!!!! WALKER SUCKS!!!! WALKER SUCKS!!!!"] [Walker slaps on the Sleep Walker again, and a horde of refs come down to try and break the hold.] Mark: Come on, Walker! The guy's out cold! You made your point, now get out of the ring! Johnny: That guy's gonna have hell to pay when the Journeyman comes to. I wouldn't want to be Desmond Walker next week, or at any point in the future. He's really making alot of enemies here in the NIWF! Mark: Yeah, he just took away a certain victory away from Lance Nelson, but we shouldn't take anything away from Ryan Roberts either. Johnny: Well I just gotta say that Ryan Roberts really showed us something here tonight as well. Two weeks ago I would have dismissed Roberts as a guy who just didn't have "it". After his performance this week, I've changed my mind. I think, as long as he stays focused, that he can become a big star here in the NIWF. Mark: Well that's all the time we've got this week. Come back again next weekend where we've got a huge main event planned. Sean Steele & Bad Element are set to take on Eric Idol, Brad Baggs, & Desmond Walker. It's gonna be explosive. Johnny Don't forget the Diamond title is up for grabs as well when One Man Army defends against Handsome Henry in a Thumbtacks & Broken Glass match! Mark: And Lawrence Buffy will be in action defending the North American Championship against Vile Vince Viper! Don't miss a minute! See you next week! ["Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit plays as the show closes.]
