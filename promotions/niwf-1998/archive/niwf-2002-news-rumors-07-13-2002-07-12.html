News & Rumors 07/13/2002

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After finally relaxing, I get around to getting back to business. -We heard that Dirty Dan has been very pleased with the way the NIWF was represented in the RSPWF mid-years. As a matter of fact, he was so impressed and pleased that we are told he is going to be doing a better job of putting out BETTER cards for the whole NIWF. On top of all of that, we understand that the whole NIW Free Per View coming up will pretty much be one of the best Free Per View's in the history of the NIWF. -Rumor has it that Dirty Dan is working on getting Brad Zans a contract for working in the NIWF. As you all know, Brad Zans is the man who attacked Andrew "The Icon" Starkey a few weeks ago. Starkey has issued a challenge to Zans, and Dirty Dan really wants to see if he can sign the match or at least sign Zans to a contract. -There are rumblings in the back that Roddy Taylor may be getting a big push soon. Some of the wrestlers are not too happy about this because Taylor basically walked right into the NIWF and was GIVEN the International Title. Roddy Taylor has been seen in the back recently talking with Todd Lucchesi and Eric Henry. It is believed that Roddy is going to be announced as being part of something in the Free Per View coming up. -Where was Dylan Dice?! That is the question being asked in the NIWF locker room now! Aas you all saw, Dice never showed up for the World Title Match against Sean Steele. Instead, Steele was attacked by Eric Idol and company after winning by forfeit. Nobody has heard or seen Dylan Dice in the past week, but we are told that on the next Hardcore Edge, Dice will be making an appearance and may possibly cut some sort of promo in the ring. We are not sure what this means. Speculation has it that Dice may quit the NIWF before even getting to wrestle Todd Lucchesi! -When Eric Idol was asked about his #15 spot in the RSPWF mid-years, all that was said was "WHOOOOO!!! It really is AAAAAALLLLLLL about the Popstar!!!" -Supposedly, Dirty Dan may be doing some sort of out of character interview in the near future. Rumor has it that he will discuss issues such as the whole SPW thing, what happened between him and N.E.W. and what he feels about the e-wrestling community giving him negative heat, plus more! The only problem that the NIWF is having is to get someone to actually interview Dirty Dan! -There is rumor that the Women's Title match at Civil War may wind up changing to a 3 Way Dance of some sort between the 3 active female wrestlers on the roster right now. Dirty Dan feels that this division could easily get stronger, with the right push. -Speaking of an NIWF woman, it is apparent that we may soon learn the secret of just whom Sarah Mayson is really dating! While Doug Hardy claims it is him, we all know better than to believe a man who talks about his 14lb ham. -Axel Schmidt is said to be very happy with his TV Title win. So happy that he may dedicate his defense of the TV Title to The Ferret King. For those of you who don't know, The Ferret King was one of the first TV Champs in the NIWF and brought some respect to the title.
