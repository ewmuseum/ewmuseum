Ok, I am putting VE together today guys. As you all know, there has been no women's match. I will be doing a "press conference" today with the roleplay show explaining what has happened to the match and what will happen to it. I'll also address any other questions. As you can see, the schedule changed. We have had some guys who had quit the fed after the Free Per View. I know some of you roleplayed for the matches, but they will change. I'll count your roleplays as is since you didn't know what happened. If you still want to send in roleplays, go ahead and do so. You have until the time I get them out tonight to send me them. I got plenty now though, so it's all good.
