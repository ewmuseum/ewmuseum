Update 01/08/2001

8 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all! Well, the NIWF looks like it will be kicking around well. We don't have as many members as we would like to have but I have a feeling that will change in a short amount of time. Anyway, I am sending this message to those on RSPWF. For those who have joined, we have our first card already set (some changes may be made here and there during the week). I can't say much about it just yet except the schedule is on the webpage which went under the knife for some major surgery. You can view it and the webpage at http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling to see who has joined and when the first card will be and who you face. For those that haven't joined or haven't filled out the app and sent it to me, now is as good as time as any. While I can't guarantee that you will be on the first show I can assure you that you will be on the second show. If anyone tried to fill out the app online, it didn't work and I didn't get it. Now, I am debating whether to have two vocal explosions before the card itself or just stick with one. That will be up to you guys. If I feel I got enough roleplays (yeah you can start now) for a show this weekend then I will make one up. If not, then I will wait till next weekend as scheduled. Also, for those who are new let me explain how things work in the NIWF. You only need to send *one* roleplay a week per character. Please do not send 4 or 5 roleplays for a Vocal Explosions show. What will happen is I will send out an update like this every Monday but with the schedule intact. After you see who you are scheduled to face, you can then start on the roleplay and get it to me no later than 2 pm the Saturday of the same week. Got it? Good... if it's too confusing for you then please ask! Oh yeah, I know many of you use AOL or juno which limits to how you can see a large card. If you happen to not get a large card or Vocal Explosions show, it will *always* be posted on the webpage for you to look at. That's that. Feel free to send in a roleplay regarding your opponent and check out the page. Wed. will be Rumor day... I wonder what the rumors will be? Later. [name withheld] Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/
