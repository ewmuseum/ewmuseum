Ok guys, here is the updated Rankings. I did the best I could with it, tried to figure out who had what in the past. If you are wondering why I included all old wrestlers in the rankings, because I like to do stuff like that. Let's say one of those guys comes back, I don't want to lose their past record. I'll take into account their past record and their current roleplay status to determine where they fit best. Many of you are starting out as "TV Title" Contenders. Once many of you wrestled 3 matchs and have over $1,000 won then I'll start to place you in the proper spots for the larger titles. (though some may stay in the TV Title division to keep it competitive) If you feel that I missed out on a few guys in the rankings, please mention their names to me.. send me a list of those I missed. I know there might be at least 5 I missed, but tell me if there are any more and who they are because I want to place them in the right spots. For those newbies, you won't have any idea who wrestled in the past but give it a shot.
