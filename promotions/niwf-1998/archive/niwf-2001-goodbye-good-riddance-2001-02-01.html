Goodbye & Good Riddance

1 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, you know what? Fuck this. I am so sick and tired of running this fed. I did enjoy it but now things are just taking a turn for the worse and I feel I can trust *NOBODY* on the internet any more. Now, because I am afraid to even USE my name association with anything on the internet any more, I will no longer run a God Damn thing, make any more fucking posts about anything anywhere and will use the internet for only the two purposes I need to use it for after today. You are all saying "What the hell does this have anything to do with e-wrestling?" Let me explain. I got a phone call today.. from one of the handlers. I will refrain from using this person's name at all for I really don't want to expose this person. Let me just say thing, I *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* *NEVER* want anybody to call me at home regarding e-wrestling. That's just ridiculous. My phone is part of my personal life, e-wrestling was part of my online life. The fact that someone goes out of their way to find my phone number on InterNic or any site like that to just call me up to discuss e-wrestling because they are mad about things just shows me that they take things way to seriously. Why did this person call? Because they were perturbed I was not answering any of his e-mails or using any roleplays or angles sent to me, none of which I got the last week or so. So obviously maybe something is fucked up on that end, right? Because I have received everything from everyone. Maybe his proxy is blocking anything going to addresses that has the word "hot" in it? I don't know. Frankly, here is what happens: I get a phone call, I am shocked to hear who the voice is when they tell me who it is. They want to discuss e-wrestling. They harass me about what's going on with their characters. The last thing I need on my mind in my personal life is e-wrestling. I have other worries (and please, nobody call me at home EVER!!! If you do, I will not speak to you at all and just hang up on you.. it's only a fucking game). I live at home, my mom questions who it is. I have to tell her because I can't think of anything else to say. She gets very angry at me and threatens to rip my head off for even allowing this to happen because this is our personal life and shit like this needs to be left out of our personal life. I of course get upset because my mother is upset as well. Neither of us know what this person is like or who this person is, for all we know... this person could be a complete nutcase (which I am hoping is not the case and I apologize if I offended this person already). Hell, it gets so bad that both my parents want to take all my computers away from me and not let me have any contact with stuff like this. Can you blame them for being upset? Can you blame me for being angry? It's e- wrestling, I started it back up for the fun of it... not to be harassed by handlers calling me in the middle of the day because they are upset. I think many people have to distinguish between this being a game and what real life is all about. Therefore, with that decision I no longer choose to wish to participate in ANYTHING to do with e-wrestling or anything that can be fun on the internet any more. My parents are angry at me, I'm angry at everything that has just happened and feel there is no need for my name to even be ASSOCIATED with the internet any more. I think it would be a different story if we both agreed to speak to each other and just chat or whatever, because that has happened in the past. But the phone call took me by surprise. My parents weren't too please and I am in strong believe now that something like this, could force them to make drastic decisions for me, decisions which I will not want to hear. Frankly, I'm pissed about it and to that person, please refrain from calling me at home to discuss this or refrain from e-mailing me as well (I'm dead serious too, do not try me). I no longer wish to associate myself with any of this crap. It's too bad because I had some bright ideas, but I have to move on with my life and what I do with it. Finally, I want to thank the writers for helping out, the handlers for contributing and anyone else who could of been of some help. For any of you, please do not call or anything to discuss these issues at all. I do not need to be harassed at home about these things or anything else (I'm being dead serious. To that, I bid all of you farewell and wish you luck! Goodbye -Dan- Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/
