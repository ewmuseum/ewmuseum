News & Rumors 01/10/2001

10 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello all! Well, this is another message from the NIWF and to those who expressed interest. Every Wed. there will just be a little "hotline report" for the News and Rumors inside of the NIWF. Some things can provide great interest to some of you and help you work on some angles. Also, if I mention your e-wrestler or a conversation we had, don't get offended.. it's part of what I do to help keep the interest. With that, here are the following news items and rumors: -Apparently "Dirty" Dan Lukiw, the president and Owner of the NIWF, decided to invite close to 500 e-wrestlers to participate in the NIWF. As of now the NIWF roster does not have 500 e-wrestlers and really does not need that many. But we did expect more of a turn out. There is still time though and our president it still trying to sell the idea of the fed to various e-wrestlers and is still trying to contact some former NIWF Wrestlers as well. -Speaking of contacting former NIWF Wrestlers it is rumored that "Dirty" Dan Lukiw had some talks with 'Asshole' Andy Simmons in regards to returning to the NIWF. Simmons did express interest in returning and told Dirty Dan he would have to think about it. "Dirty" Dan Lukiw responded back and offered Simmons a contract but Simmons has yet to return the call. Frankly, we at the NIWF feel that Simmons is living up to his name and being an Asshole. -There have been some rumblings in the back about another former NIWF Wrestler returning on a part time basis. We have not yet learned any other information such as the wrestler's name but he could be making an appearance on the first card. We have learned that if the appearance is made, it will be to confront "Dirty" Dan Lukiw. What this is about, we are still not sure. -Apparently female wrestler Heidi Leick has not finished up the contract she was supposed to sign with the NIWF. "Dirty" Dan Lukiw has contacted Heidi Leick about this last week and asked when the contract should be signed. She told him it would be signed on Monday and as of today, he does not have the contract in his hands. We suspect that if the contract does not get signed, Heidi Leick may be pulled out of the 3 Way Dance she is scheduled for and will be replaced by "Alluring" Arielle Starr -The NIWF has signed a new superstar named Schiz. Currently not much is known about this superstar but we suspect that this superstar should help the NIWF in the talent department and the NIWF fans are sure to welcome him with open arms. -On our first show back, "Dirty" Dan Lukiw will come out at the beginning of the show to talk about the current state of the NIWF and what is going to be done with the World Title situation. We have heard various things about the World Title and the most interesting is that "Dirty" Dan Lukiw will hold a 30 Man Royal Rumble and the winner of the Royal Rumble will be the NIWF World Champ. However, the NIWF does not currently have 30 Wrestlers on it's roster. It is rumored some random wrestlers will get a tryout period in the NIWF so they can be entered into the NIWF Royal Rumble to fill spots. We aren't sure about what is going on yet. Dirty Dan will have a statement about it on the first card. -Speaking of titles, the $10 Million Dollar Diamond Belt is no longer in the NIWF's posession. The president is livid over this and will have something to say about it. Apparently the Diamond Title is with the rival promotion of Shootfire Pro. You can bet the NIWF will get it's hands on that Diamond Title and bring it back to the stomping grounds where it belongs. That's all for today folks.
