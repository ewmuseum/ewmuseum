News & Rumors 01/31/2002

31 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, here we go again with some news & rumors. -There has been talk behind the scenes that Ian Walsh & Damage Inc. do NOT get along that well out of the wrestling ring. This has caused a little dillema for Dirty Dan as he is afraid if he makes the wrong decision in their handicapped match, the egos between these two are going to overlook the match itself. That is why Dirty Dan has scheduled this match to take place in the beginning of the start of the fed so this way there won't be any serious problems and he can get a feel for what the future may seem like. -Speaking of the handicapped, it is rumored that this match is going to take an interesting twist and it could be main event material. It seems like Dirty Dan is going to have some tricks up his sleeve with this one. -There have been many people who have expressed interest in joining. If you join now, you will _not_ be a part of the North American Title Tournament as that has been finally filled out. However, if you join with a tag team or a female wrestler, you will still have the chance to participate for the titles in those divisions. -Since I mentioned titles, let me talk about what we are doing in the NIWF with the titles. Right now the Cruiserweight, International, 6 Man Tag & Purist's Dream titles have been SCRAPPED. They will eventually make their return back to the NIWF when we get more wrestlers, but right now we are going to stick with a select few to make things more competitive. The titles that will stay are World, North American, Television, Tag Team & Women's Titles. -It is rumored that on the first card, Dirty Dan is going to talk about how Shootfire Pro stole the NIWF's Diamond Title without the NIWF's permission. This has happened before in the past and as you all know, the Diamond Title is all about the NIWF's Hardcore Division. If this is what Dirty Dan talks about then we can expect SPW to retaliate with some words and who knows what else. It is possible that Dirty Dan demand that the Diamond Title be returned to the NIWF as it that's where it rightfully belongs. -Some wrestlers are not happy that they are not on the first card and have told Dirty Dan they will have some things to say about this. Some of them even used the word "quit" suggesting they want to have nothing to do with the NIWF any more. If any of you actually wish to quit, just know that because of story lines I will probably use your character in it's next scheduled match anyway if I can't get a replacement and then won't use that character after that. -There have been talks to bring N'Sync in the NIWF to do a small show... ok, just kidding! Haha. Seriously though, someone mentioned to me that it would be a good idea to have a handler contact list and this weekend I will work on putting that on the website. If there is some reason that you do not wish to be included on this list of e-mail addresses, please tell me why and I will not include you.
