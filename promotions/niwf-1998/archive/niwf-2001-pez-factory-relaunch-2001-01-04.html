We Used to Wrestle in a Pez Factory!!

4 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This was true. The NIWF used to wrestle in an abandoned Pez factory in Newark, NJ but after it closed down in '99 that building no longer exists. Well, we came back and this time are located out of St. Petersburg, Florida! We have had many old wrestlers come back and some new wrestlers coming in. For those that expressed interest in the fed, GET YOUR APPS IN ASAP!! For those that missed my message the other day (How could you?!?) then I will repost it again. I repeat, (*DO NOT TRY TO FILL OUT THE APP ON THE PAGE** Send the app directly to me!! ----------------------------------- You knew it was coming, and after a year long hiatus from e-wrestling and running a fed I have decided I miss the *fun* of this little game we all play. If you have no interest, then just ignore the message. If you are interested in becoming a part of the NIWF once again then continue to read on. Also, if you have any e-mail address of old NIWF wrestlers or know people that will be interested in becoming part of the fed then please contact me. I am having trouble locating some people who were once a part of the NIWF and may be interested. Also, pass this along to as many people as you feel may be interested. I am sure many of you remember what the NIWF was about, but if you don't I decided to put many things up on a web page for you to remember at http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling The main things on the page to focus on are the FAQ and the Control Center where If you want some good sample cards to see what we are about, then go to the following sites: http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/best_f_the_ppv.html http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/f_the_ppv_05_09_98.html http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/f_the_ppv_05_16_98.html http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/outback.html http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/No_Name_11_03_98.html And for an idea of how we do roleplays, it's a roleplay show that is sent out as one message (no roleplay board) so go here to look at a sample show: http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/ve11_03_98.html I'm sorry the website is in bad shape but it will be fixed once we get things rolling (please do not try to fill out the online application, it still has bugs to be fixed.) So basically, here is what is going to happen. Were you a part of the NIWF before? If so and you believe I still have your application, drop me a line with your e- wrestler's name and I'll confirm whether or not I have it. If I don't have it or you are a newbie to the NIWF and want to fill out a new application, I will attach one at the bottom of this message. Now, how are things going to work out? Well, first off I *will* need *reliable* match writers to write 2 matches per week (yes, I will be writing two matches a week as well) Once I can get about 3 writers, I will start the cards the next week. The first 16 people to join will be part of a tournament that will start to the North American Title. The first 8 cruiserweights that join will be part of the cruiserweight tournament, the first 8 or 10 females that join will be part of the Women's Title Tourney (1 already has joined as of now), the first 10 Hardcore Mother Fuckers to join will be part of the 10 Man Lunatic Fringe Match on the very first card (we already have one participant). The first 4 Tag Teams to join will compete in a series of Round Robin tournament matches (one tag team must face the other 3 tag teams, the one with the best record is crowned champ), and the first 6 International Guys will be part of a tournament as well. (TV Title matches will be held randomly). All final matches will take place on our first Free Per View of the year... just go with the flow. World title situation will be addressed on our first card. Our schedule will normally go like this: -Mondays: Updates for Schedule & Rankings will be given to you guys. - Tuesdays: Go ahead and work on your roleplays -Weds: Strats for your matches are due in, match assignments will be handed out. -Thursdays: News & Rumors -Friday: Work on roleplays and matches (try to get Roleplays to me this day) -Saturdays: Roleplays due at 2pm Eastern Standard Time the Latest, Vocal Explosions sent out, matches due to me by writers -Sundays: NIWF Hardcore Edge (weekly card), matches due at 2pm Eastern Standard Time the latest. There you have it. Things will fit in perfectly and you will just roll along with what will happen. Remember, it's what you make of the fed that determines how fun it will be and what it will be like. I've put up with many people bitching and other things in the past, the fed does not survive on just one roleplayer or one e-wrestler, it survives on all of us as a team. So, feel free to check out the page at http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling Here is the application!!
