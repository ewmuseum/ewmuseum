NIWF — News

News & Rumors 06/13/2002

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
More news and rumors! -It's been a busy week behind the scenes in the NIWF this week. Dirty Dan, once again, has been pulling out all the stops and trying to get as many people in the fed that he can. Unfortunately, not many are willing to get back to him. Two of the bigger names that Dirty Dan has asked to possibly come back are Mr. Bungle and Atilla. However, nothing has been heard and both of these men have not returned Dirty Dan's phone call. -Another rumor has it that an old fed, N.E.W, may be returning to the e-wrestling rankings. Dirty Dan has some old ties from this fed and may be looking to strike up some sort of deal with the president of this fed about becoming the "minors" for this fed. However, this isn't what you all think it is meant. Dirty Dan hasn't been approached or hasn't approached anyone about this at all. It is believed the reason for that is because Dirty Dan does not want the NIWF to be considered the "minors" but would rather want it to be considered a larger fed. -Lots of conflicting plans have came in this week to Dirty Dan. So many, that the NIWF is not going to gurantee that all of them get on the show this coming weekend. Not all the writers have every single plan as Dirty Dan is saving some to add into the show later. However, no more attacks or plans are to be sent. In-rings are still acceptable though. -As we were saying before, a lot of behind the scenes stuff has been happening. Apparently, some sort of quarrel between Axel Schmidt, Corey Irons & Derrik Weaver has occurred. We understand that it has grown out of control and it even involves a member of the Shootfire Pro staff as well. -Speaking if SPW, Dirty Dan is none to pleased about what he has seen the past few days. We will spare you the details, but let's just say that Dirty Dan isn't happy that some of the old NIWF legends did not choose to come back to the NIWF. He's so bitter and sore, that he is going to address this issue on the show this weekend in Las Vegas! Supposedly, what he has to say, is going to shock the whole locker room as he will be addressing different wrestlers in there two. We are told though that at least one wrestler will be coming out to confront Dirty Dan if he shows up in the ring. -As you saw last week, the NIWF is rumored to have a former Purist's Dream Champ come back and show up on the show this week. We still aren't sure who it is, but speculation now says that it might either be "The Stallion" Reginald Worthington-Smythe or Arthur Essex. We are hoping that the person shows during the weekend, but there hasn't been definite confirmation yet. -There are a couple of other wrestlers and maybe a tag team, who will be making their way into the NIWF for the first time. All of these wrestlers have been popular in feds that the NIWF has had ties with in the past. We were told not to reveal any names, but we will probably see them as early as this weekend. It should add a good element to the NIWF. -It is official, Dirty Dan has decided to go with the "Civil War" name for the next Free Per View. Expected to be on the Free Per View will be of course, a World Title Match, the finals for the Cruiserweight Tournament and a Diamond Title Match that might blow your mind! The show will take place in Raleigh, North Carolina. This doesn't mean that the International Title won't be defended. Dirty Dan is trying to work out a deal where he can have just one match occur outside of the U.S. just for this particular show. -Despite seeing that he is on the show, apparently CK really is gone from the NIWF. Dirty Dan finally got through to CK and told him that he either leaves or he gets jobbed until he quits. -We understand that Jermey Colton Christ's wife is expected to deliver her baby very soon. It could happen within the next seven days as well. We understand that it could happen during the weekend, when J.C. is scheduled to wrestle. In other words, it could be possible he misses the show to be with his wife. -Apparently, Lawrence Buffy is going to be wrestling injured this weekend. Not only is he injured, with a bum leg, but he is also wrestling while he is very sick. We heard it's just a common cold, but Buffy is going around in the back telling people that he has a nasty flu bug as well as a gastrial intestinal virus. -Despite what others have heard, the NIWF is really picking up and hopes to continue to pick up and become a top five fed on the net. Dirty Dan is encouraging EVERYONE involved in the NIWF to vote on the RSPWF mid-years.. he wants to get the NIWF on the map once again. We know Dirty Dan hasn't cared about the awards and recognition in the past and we understand he doesn't care too much about the concept now either. He is here just to have fun, but in the process, wants the NIWF to be recognized for all the hard work it's writers and handlers put in to it! That's it for today! Show to come out this weekend! Writers, send in your stuff!
