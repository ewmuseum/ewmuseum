NIWF — News

News & Rumors 02/21/2002

21 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, here is another edition of news and rumors followed by the schedule as well: -Dirty Dan is getting a little worried about the status of some of the match writers. He is worried we won't see the matches on time for this Sunday after he sent out the assignments. He does realize that a couple of the match writers are also participants in Shootfire Pro and is a little concerned that maybe someone in SPW management got to them and told them to dedicate themselves to SPW and forget about the NIWF. He feels if these writers forget about the NIWF, then they will not help out by having the matches written which puts a lot of strain on the president to try and get those matches done in one day to get the card out on time. We won't blame anyone just yet, but this is a reminder to the writers who have not contacted Dirty Dan or not sent him a match yet that the matches are due THIS WEEKEND. Dirty Dan knows who you are and will not be afraid to expose you to the fed if you drop the ball. -We are sad to announced that "The Greatest Show on Earth" Thomas DiArgo had to quit the fed. The reason being is because the handler had a serious death in the family and did not have the urge to continue after that at all. Dirty Dan was sorry to see him go and wished him and his family well. DiArgo was told that if he wished to come back at any time, he was more than welcome to. The NIWF always welcomes back any former wrestlers with open arms. -As you see, Thomas DiArgo was part of the North American Title Tournament. What this means is that a spot has opened up in this tournament. Dirty Dan has decided to give anyone who was in the tournament the first round and lost a second chance. This means, that if you lost in the first round, you can have another chance in the 2nd round. However, it only goes to one person only and that person will be the very first person who asks Dirty Dan to place him back in that tournament... first come, first serve. -Dirty Dan has been in talks with AJ Black about doing some possible exhibition matches. This means that we may see an SPW match on the the Free Per View for the NIWF. This also means that there will be an NIWF match at the next PPV for SPW. Who the NIWF has chosen to represent them right now is unknown but if you are interested, please mention it to Dirty Dan. However, Dirty Dan may decide to send a TAPED match to SPW to protect any wrestlers from being attack from SPW stars. -Dirty Dan has really been on edge all this week both personally and professionally. We were told that he told the locker room to turn it up a notch on the next show to push things over the edge. We are also told that he will be confronting certain wrestlers about their contractual status and whether or not they are serious about staying in the fed or not. Then there is the rumor he will be making the challenge for the Diamond Title from SPW. -There has been no date scheduled yet for the Free Per View but we expect it to take place in the NIWF Arena. We have heard rumors of certain matches that could take place. These matches will most likely include the 30 Man Royal Rumble for the NIWF World Title, a ladder match for the NIWF North American Title, some sort of TV Title Match, the finals for the Tag Team Titles, the finals for the Women's Title, a possible 10 Man Lunatic Fringe Match and an exhibition SPW Match. That would make 7 matches. Whether or not there will be 7 or 8 Matches is still undecided, as the Rumble match itself could be equal to two matches anyway. -There has been rumor going on that SPW Superstar Gregory Brians is none to happy with how he has been treated in SPW. Apparently Brians has made an offer to Dirty Dan to bring the Platinum Title from SPW into the NIWF but only for a certain price. We are not sure what this asking price is or if Dirty Dan is going to go through with it. -There were rumors circulating that "Lightning" Jake McCarey was quitting SPW to join the NIWF and the rumor was started by Lighting himself. However, these rumors were proven false after it was apparent that the Lightning was being impersonated. The NIWF was blamed for this and has been running into problems with someone trying to make a bad name for the fed both in RSPWF and now in SPW. It is being suspected that the person who is the masked man has been creating this controversy. -There have been 5 more people added to the Royal Rumble match Itself now. Ever week, 5 people will be announced until there are 30 people total. Right now, here are the first 10 Participants to be added to the Rumble itself: 1.Dirty Dan 2.The Masked Man 3."The Shocker" Brian Anthony 4.Thomas P. Harding "The Angelic Assasin" 5.The Wasp 6.Anrew "The Icon" Starkey 7.Dennis Windham 8.Desmond Walker 9.Shot 10.CK -Finally, word in the back is that a representative to Desmond Walker complained to Dirty Dan about Desmond Walker not being in the NA Title Tournament. Dirty Dan told him that right now, it wouldn't be fair to try and place Walker in the tournament with so many others deserving of a chance but he will find a good "place" for Walker in the NIWF very soon.
