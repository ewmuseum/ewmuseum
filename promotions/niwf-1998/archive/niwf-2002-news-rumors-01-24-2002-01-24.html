News & Rumors 01/24/2002

24 January 2002

Ok folks, let's get this thing rolling with many pieces of news items and many interesting rumors. News & Rumors --------------- -With the NIWF just opening up, there have been many people who want to join but only a few select few of them have actually filled out their application to join the fed. Those that have filled out their application are being placed on the very first show, which is pretty muched booked except for one match. That match is a tag team match. The NIWF needs one more tag team to join to make this card official. Right now there are 6 matches, all the first round of the North American Title Tournamentt plus there is a female wrestler's match. The tag team match would make the 8th match. As soon as another tag team joins, we can get rolling and I can send out match assignments to the writers. -Dirty Dan is very pleased with the amount of people that have offered to write matches for the fed. So pleased that there have been talks of a card every week if the help is there. However, those are just talks. It has been decided that the format the NIWF is going with the format that is discussed. One card every two weeks and one roleplay show in between those cards. The general consensous and feedback has been that this was the best thing to do for the fed and makes the most sense. There were talks of a possible house show on the off week but there has been no deal set in place. -With the announcement that Dirty Dan wants no Shootfire Pro wrestlers in the NIWF, at least 8-10 Shootfire Pro Wrestlers have been on contact with the NIWF about joing the NIWF. The feeling is that these wrestlers are not happy with the status of SPW and how they have to wait for things to come out on time and want an alternative so they have something to do while waiting for the stuff in SPW to come out. Dirty Dan is flattered that so many SPW Wrestlers want to join the NIWF but gave them the ultimatium: "It's either SPW or us, you decide!" The rumor has it that Dirty Dan will become more leniant on this matter come the first Free Per View called "Dangerous" and will allow some to come over to the NIWF. -Speaking of the Free Per View, the rumor has it going around that the Royal Rumble match is a match for the NIWF World Title. There has been no official announcement but it is expected that Dirty Dan will announce this on the first card and that the winner of the Rumble will be crowned the next NIWF World Champ. There is also rumor going around that the finals of the North American Title match which will take place on this Free Per View, will be a ladder match. -Dirty Dan has been in contact with many former NIWF Stars not in Shootfire Pro to see if there is any interest in returning to the NIWF. There has been a lot of talk from these stars but progress has not really been made. Many of these wrestlers want to come in but have not filled out the application. If you are one of those wrestlers, the application is at the bottom of this message. How will you know if you are not one of these wrestlers? Simple! If you aren't scheduled on the first card, it means you have no filled out the application yet. -[name withheld] was contacted by Dirty Dan to bring in some of his former BWF stars as Dirty Dan felt this would be a good addition to the NIWF. [name withheld] pondered the thought for a couple of days and finally reached a decision. He has decided against coming back due to time constraints. The NIWF wishes Paul well for his future and wants him to know there is always an opening for him. -Dirty Dan reached "The Extreme War Machine" Corey Irons about returning to the NIWF. Right now there are behind the scenes negotiations with Corey to bring him back to the NIWF. There have been some progressions but nothing has been signed. -It is rumored that Heidi Leick will have a strictly valet role in the NIWF. However, some people have been talking in the back and feel it won't be long before one of the other female wrestlers issues a challenge to her. -Currently there is not a complete website for the NIWF. The president and owner is working on this and expects a website to be up and running by the end of the weekend for the fed. One section that needs the most updating is the Rankings section. We have rankings from 1999 but do not have any rankings after that. There were matches wrestled for the NIWF in 2001 and if anyone has the rankings from our last stint in 2002, please send them to us! -The NIWF is expecting many more wrestlers to join in the near future. We are always open to any new wrestlers who wish to join us. Right now we are in need of mostly tag teams and female wrestlers but if you have a male singles wrestler who wishes to join then please feel free to join and fill out the application on the bottom.
