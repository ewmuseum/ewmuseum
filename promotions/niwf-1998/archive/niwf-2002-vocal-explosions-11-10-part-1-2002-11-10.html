Vocal Explosions: Nov. 12/02 ======================================= "The Popstar" Eric Idol ======================================= [Scene opens in the NIWF arena. The camera pans around the usually chaotic complex but finds nothing but silence. The place is deserted except for one lone figure standing in the center of the ring, "The Popstar" Eric Idol.] Eric Idol: I've been thinking. See, in one week I get to be a part of a history making event. In one week the fate of the NIWF World Championship will rest in MY hands when I ref the NIWF World title match. Will it be Sean Steele? Or will it be Jack Russell? Hmm, such a complex decision. [Idol scratches his chin and paces around the ring.] Eric Idol: Who deserves to be champion? Certainly not the "Man of Steele". Sean has been a disgrace to the world title. He's fought all the losers and stayed away from the man that could actually beat him, namely me. So in short... it would be an HONOR to count Sean Steele's shoulders to the mat next weekend. I'd really enjoy it too! [Idol smiles and then stops pacing. His expression turns to stone as he stares into the camera.] Eric Idol: But then there's Jack Russell. How could I help further this-this racist pig's career? Jack Russell disgusts me! I have never in my life met a more vile person and it would be extremely fun to watch him get beat. Just the thought of looking into Russell's glazed over eyes as I count to three and shatter his dreams excites me! But at what expense? My own career? [Idol resumes pacing back and forth in the ring.] Eric Idol: So you can see my dilemma. Do I help further the career of a man with no conscience? A man with no morals? A freakin' Nazi?! Or... do I help the man that has been sabotaging my own career? A man that has refused to give me what I deserve, a shot at the World Championship. So many questions... [Idol stops, cracks a sly smile, and stares into the camera.] Eric Idol: But the answer is so very simple. This match isn't about Sean Steele; it isn't about Jack Russell. It's about me! It's AAAAAAAAAALLLLLLLLLL about ME! I don't give a rats ass which of you losers comes out on top because the result will be the same; me standing over BOTH your sorry asses! You don't like it? Tough shit! I'm running this show and I'll be damned if either of you two goons are gonna steal MY spotlight. So Sean, Jack, best of luck to both of you. You'll need it! [Fade] ======================================= NIWF World Tag Team Champions: The Deities w/ The Prophet ======================================= [The camera fades in, while the erie sound of an organ is heard. We're inside the home offices of The Council, a run down, deserted church in the back woods of who knows, USA .... ] [Nailed to the walls, in the symbol of a cross, hang the NIWF World Tag Team titles. A black veil in hung overtop of the belts.] [Standing at his podium to speak, is The Prophet] The Prophet: Perhaps i'm just too close to the situation at hand, that perhaps it leaves my vision a bit 'jaded' .. if you will. But over the last 48 hours, my boys have been under the scrutiny of the naive, the uneducated, the biased warmongers that live within the dark, shadowy depths of this orginization. To say I'm hurt ... well .... That would be a lie. I don't care whether you sheep feel as if my boys won or not. Your all the wretched followers, while a man with vision, like myself, must conform to your ideals. But I won't. Let Bag o Steele lead you to the "truth" as they see it. But remember, do not trust the wolf who cries ... well, you know. You see, Steele, since his 'arrival' has been on a roll, he's had things his way. Throw a bit of what I like to call "The Unknown" in, and watch his resolve turn to butter inside a second. You see, Steele, in this business, nothing is safe, nothing is just, and nothing is ever what we want it to be. You can cry that the judges were biased, the the jury was bought, and your mom was a whore ... but in the end, what really matters, did you have the heart? Did you have the fortitude? Do you have the strength to forge a comeback? Or will you let your words prove what kind of man .. you are? [The Prophet comes from behind his podium, and stands next to the Tag Straps.] You see, your whining, bitching, moaning and complaining have done nothing but anger my boys. Your tantrums are laughable, but your dignity is in pain. You've shown yourself for what you are. But it doesn't end there, Steele. You've damned these belts. You've stripped them of all there worth. You've raped them of there exsistence. You've turned them into a shell of your image. [Wasted Youth and Suicide Kid come from out of the darkness, seemingly out from nowhere ... trailed closely by Xanthos.] And we don't want them anymore ...... [Youth hands The Prophet a can of gasoline, while Suicide hands The Prophet a box of matches.] [All four men kneel around the crusifixed tag titles, while The Prophet leads them in prayer] The Prophet: Dear Father, Yesterday is gone, Tommorow has yet to come. We have only today, Let us begin. Let my prayer be counted as incense before you, and the lifting of my arms as an evening sacrafice. Father, forgive Sean Steele, as he knows not what is asked of him, only of what he asks for him. The reason why sometimes you have asked and not received Sean, is because you have asked amiss, either inconsistently, or lightly, or because you have asked for what was not good for you, or because you have ceased asking. Tonight, we purge your sins, Sean. Tonight, we forgive your childish ignorance. Tonight, we wash away your pain. Tonight, we burn your troubles away..... [The Prophet lights a match and gently places the match upon the veil. The veil ignites and a flame engulfs the the belts.] With this sacrafice, Dear Lord, Sean Steele's sins are purged. We start anew, my father, and we thank thee...... [Camera focuses on the burning belts one last time, and then we ...... ] [FADE] ======================================= The Amazing Huang ======================================= It's late at night at a rather rough part of town. No one's out, save for a young woman nervously making her way down the deserted street. She looks rather lost. We hear her murmur something...a European language. Maybe German. She looks back. A lone figure is on the street as well, maybe half a block away. She keeps walking, hoping for a cab to pass by. She pauses when another man steps out from the corner in front of her. She tries to be cool about it, crossing the street right away. She glances back to find the man behind her cross the street as well. She quickens her walk. She starts running when both men follow her. She shouts for help, trying to stay out of their reach. She struggles as they grab her, but is quickly subdued with a punch to the stomach. She throws up as the two men drag her into an alley. They curse, hitting her for soiling their clothes. "We gonna cut you up good, bitch." They begin to tear at her clothes. She is too weak to fend them off. That's when something heavy dropped on them. The already-beaten woman panics, clawing at the thick canvas, lashing out at the men holding her down. She hears a struggle, some shouting. The men are leaving her. She crawls away from the commotion as quickly as she can, stumbling into a wall. She hears sirens, and throws up again. Her vomit stains her clothes... Then she is opening her eyes. She can make out a gruff-looking man through the haze and pain. "It's okay, miss. Please take deep breaths," the police officer tells her. She breaks down, sobbing and thanking the police for saving her. He tries to reassure her as best he can, not being able to understand German. Other cops are already checking on the two men. One is out cold, with a nasty gash on his head; the other is conscious, but does not resist as he is cuffed and dragged to his feet. He somehow manages to say his jaw has been broken as they read him his rights. "Take it easy, ma'am. The paramedics are on their way," he tells the tourist. She nods weakly, wiping at the vomit on her clothes with the golden yellow canvas that fell on them. The cop cannot help but wonder if he's seen the pattern on the material from somewhere. The Chinese--or is it Japanese?--embroidery looks out of place in this dump. [Fade] ======================================= 1/2 Owner of the NIWF: Dewey ======================================= (Scene opens at the Cue Club, a Karaoke Bar. Camera pans to the fifteen or so drunks sitting at the tables that could easily hold 100, and then to Paul Fontaine on stage, doing a surprisingly good version of "Come out and Play" by Offspring. The meager crowd claps their approval, as he finishes his song, and the next wannabe comes onto the stage. Camera pans to a table, where a John Bon Jovi lookalike is clapping, and he slaps Paul on the back as he comes to his seat.) Jon: Good job, man! Paul: Thanks. I swear, I do much better when I'm not drinking, you know! Jon: I hear ya! (Jon lifts his Molson's in the air, and toasts with Paul, whose drinking a glass of Diet Coke, and not looking to happy about it.) Jon: So, what's this big news you've got, man? Paul: Well, listen man, I'm gonna need your help next weekend. See, I'm in a bit of a jam, cause I lost about six grand betting on the Bombers over the last couple weeks.... Jon: Sorry, I got no cash. Can't help you out.. Paul: No, just listen. I talked to Doug, cause I thought I could get back to running the NIWF, since I've been sober for awhile now, but the prick said no, and I'm SOL. Jon: So, what do you need me for? Paul: I'm getting to that. I talked to Jason Starr, the new comissioner, and I tried to get him to step aside, and he basically laughed in my face, and told me that if I want to actually get paid by the NIWF, I've gotta wrestle!!!! (Jon starts laughing, and spits his beer in Paul's face. Paul takes a second to suck some of the foam in and continues.) Paul: So, basically, I need you to have my back. Hey, last time you seconded me, I won the AWA World title! Jon: Come on, that was some bush league with a bunch of has beens and never wasses! You're gonna get killed in the NIWF! Paul: I know, but I figured it you'd be at ringside, I'd at least have a fighting chance. Jon: So, who are you supposed to fight? Paul: Jade, the cruiserweight champion. Jon: Oh, man, he's tough. Title match? Paul: Yeah, that's the thing. I mean, the money I'll get for just wrestling kind of sucks, but if I can win the title, I'm in line for HUGE bucks! Endorsments, all kinds of stuff, man! And hey, I'll share the wealth. Jon: Well, I'll tell you something, though, man. As much of a better person as you are when you're sober, and as much as you sing better, I don't think you're going to have the guts to actually fight someone like Jade, or anyone else in the NIWF without a little ah, "liquid encouragment. (The waitress walks by and Jon grabs her arm.) Waitress: Hey cutie, what can I get you? Jon: Two Molson's please. (Waitress looks at Fontaine and a look of horror crosses her face.) Waitress: He's not going to be drinking, is he? Jon: Necessary evil, my dear. Thanks alot. (TWO HOURS LATER) (Still at the Cue Club and this time we see a shirtless Dewey on stage, belting out "Some Kind of Wonderful" with a beer in each hand, and the camera pans to Jon at the table with a sly smile on his face as the scene fades.) ======================================= Lance Nelson w/ Windham & Graves ======================================= (The scene opens in the Bar With No Name in Mud Creek, TX. Seated at the table is Lance Nelson, flanked by Dennis Windham and Tommy Graves. Cigarette smoke hangs in the air and a half empty bottle of bourbon sits on the table. Windham is flipping through a large book that the camera spots as the American Heritage dictionary.) Windham: Lance, here it is! (Windham hands over the book as Nelson looks into the camera) Nelson: Well, Walker, my little protégé, it would seem that the administration has passed down the curriculum for your next learning experience. An old favorite of mine, the Indian Strap match. Dennis, here, has been kind enough to get you the book definition since you never experienced the phenomenon before. It says...let me see...STRAP, a long, narrow strip of plaint material such as leather. A razor strop. A strip of leather used for flogging. To beat with a strap. Thanks, Dennis. That's a pretty good book you got there. But they left out the good part. Let's see if we can define it a little better. (Nelson grabs the remote and presses play on a VCR on the wall. A grainy video image plays of three men strapped together and beaten bloody. It becomes apparent that this is from the legendary Triple Threat Challenge matches between Nelson, Windham, and Graves in the early 90's.) Graves: Yep. I think that covers it much more thoroughly. (Graves rubs a noticeable scar from the strap on his arm) Nelson: You see, Desmond, the strap match is not just a means of securing two opponents together. Because it does that quite effectively. You won't be able to run and hide behind your little stunt man. It'll be me and you in a ring with 6 feet of leather between us. But you see its more than that. It's the assassin's loaded gun in my hand. It's the surgeon's scalpel in my palm. Do you hear the music, Desmond? The Executioner's Song is playing...and the bell is tolling for you, son. You see that strap as it gets slick with sweat becomes razor sharp. And as I drag you pillar to post in that ring and slap my palm down across each turnbuckle pad, do not think that my goal is only to win the Old School title. It's to watch you bleed. It's to stain that leather with the rich red of your life's blood. And when they stretcher you out of the ring, you'll be a little wiser, and you'll be ready to admit that I might be old, but I'm still the very best there is at what I do. (blows smoke into the camera as we fade) ======================================= NIWF Diamond Champion: Adam F'N Kuhl ======================================= (The screen is full of snow, and the sound of nails scratching a chalkboard can be heard in the background. As the picture starts to clear up, we hear Adam Kuhl cackling, and the camera focuses on Adam Kuhl in front of the NIWF banner. He's wearing a black T-Shirt with "Adam F***ing Kuhl" in red letters and torn black jeans. He's got the NIWF Diamond title around his waist.) Kuhl: As you can tell, the NIWF hasn't heard alot from the One Man Crime Spree in the last couple weeks, cause quite frankly I've taken a few weeks off after the hardest schedule of my f***ing life over the last couple months. I've fought in every type of match against every type of opponent I've been put in there against, and the end result of it all is this Diamond title around my waist. (Kuhl rubs the belt and a slight smile crosses his lips.) Kuhl: You see, it is and should be everyone in this industry's goal to win the World title and I will win the NIWF World title someday, or die trying, but this belt here is the next best thing, and in some ways, it's more coveted than the World title. This belt is the most valuable belt in all of professional wrestling in terms of monetary value, but also in terms of the gallons of blood and pounds of flesh that have been spent competing for this title week in and week out. And this belt here is the original. This is the belt that Adam f***ing Kuhl helped make famous back in the day, and I brought it back to the NIWF after a long absence. (Kuhl takes a second to pat himself on the back.) Kuhl: And I will defend this title again someday soon, but first things first, and the first thing that I've got to deal with is this tag team match next week on Mayhem. One Man Army, the man I beat for this title is teaming up with Roddy Taylor, a man I have unsettled issues with against me and a man that I've never seen eye to eye with in Andrew Starkey. Hmmmm, I'm teaming up against a guy I don't like against two guys that tried to kill me in the last month. Couldn't have anything to do with the fact that the comissioner of this federation is a gigantic dickwad, could it? I didn't think so! Well, it doesn't matter! Taylor, I'm making you this promise right now. Lightning will NOT strike twice and you will not pin the One Man Crime Spree twice in a row. One Man Army, I've already proven to you who the better man is at the Free Per View. Starkey, just stay the hell out of my way and we'll do just fine. (Kuhl pauses to collect his thoughts. He looks at his hand and the camera focuses on his deformed reattached pinky finger.) Kuhl: Oh yeah, Chris King, don't think I've forgotten about you. Just TRY and get involved in my match next week, just TRY, I'm begging you! I told you before and I'm going to keep telling you. I WILL take your hand in exchange for my finger. The only question is when, and if you want to stop the suspense, just show up next Sunday. I'm out. (Kuhl leaves the set, as the screen gets all fuzzy again, and the sound of nails stratching a chalkboard are heard.) ======================================= NIWF Old School Champion: Desmond Walker ======================================= ("Whatta Man" by En Vogue plays in the background, and the graphic "Q and A with the Marquee Man" plays in the background. The question "How do you expect to compete in a leather strap match with a broken arm appears on the screen, and then the camera pans to Dez Walker sitting in front of a blue background.) Walker: The doctors say I should take the week off. My trainers, my publicists, even my friends. But I won't give old man Nelson the satisfaction! I'll compete in that strap match, and I'll beat him, or I'll pass out trying! That's what old school wrestling is all about, isn't it? (The question "Is this fued sidetracking you too much from moving on to bigger things" appears on the screen and camera pans back to Walker.) Walker: Time and time again thru wrestling history, guys have been bogged down in fueds that they just got too involved with and promising careers were cut short, or completely ruined. Buzz Sawyer and Tommy Rich spring to mind. See, Lance, I've done my homework. Well, that ain't gonna happen to the Marquee Man. First of all, I've got plenty of other stuff on the go, and secondly, I've got my eyes firmly focused on the prize. (The question "The NIWF World Heavyweight title" appears on the screen and then the camera pans back to Walker.) Walker: Exactly. Sean Steele is fighting Jack Russell next weekend, and whomever comes out of that match with the big belt had better beware of Dez Walker! All the Marquee marks are screaming for it, and soon it will happen. When I get that elusive World title shot, I won't be denied. (The question "And what about Hollywood?" appears on the screen and camera pans back to Walker.) Walker: Warhammer 2, coming to theatres January 10, and who knows after that. The buzz has been good, and I wouldn't be at all surprised if more scripts come flying in. I've said in the past that I'll always have time for Hollywood and that wrestling is just a stepping stone, but I've gotta tell you something. I know which side my bread is buttered on, and I really doubt I'll leave wrestling any time soon. Any hey, who knows, the movie could flop, right? (Walker stops and smiles and lets out a little chuckle.) Walker: Nah, I didn't think so. (The graphis Lance Nelson, Desmond Walker, Sunday, Mayhem, Leather Strap match, Old school title appear one after another on the screen, followed by Don't miss it! and then "Whatta Man" by En Vogue plays as the scene fades.) ======================================= Roddy Taylor ======================================= [Scene opens with Roddy Taylor standing in front of a Canadian flag.] Taylor: You know, it hasn't been a great couple of weeks for me, but I figure that I gotta tell you people the way things really are! For the last year, I figured that myself and your wannabe champion, SEAN STEELE... would be able to co-exist. I figured that as long as I stayed out of his business, he would do the smart thing and keep his ugly face out of mine. Well, it appears that Sean Steele refuses to do the smart thing. He wants to live dangerously! He wants to let his jealousy of Roddy Taylor throw him into a rage, and attack me and screw me out of the Roddy Taylor International Title. Well Steele, phase one of your plan worked because I gave you too much credit! I didn't think you would be so stupid as to even attempt to set foot in the very same ring as I would. But here's where reality kicks in for you and your lacky, champ! You have awaken a man who has the most devestating finishing move in all of wrestling! You have awaken a man who has more talent in his little finger than you, your lacky, your lacky's mother and the rest of that entire locker room combined! You have awaken the man who has beaten every big name in this industry. You want to add something to your make believe legacy Steele? Allow me to do you the honours: You are over-rated, over-payed, you are ugly, and you have grown stale unusually quick, that's why you attacked me and made me look like a fool in that ring! The path you have taken is the path that will only lead you to sink into obscurity faster, and I will be the anchor that drags you under and drowns your legacy for good! [Taylor pauses to smirk at the camera.] Taylor: So, Sean Steele, get your little lacky to shine up that title real nice, and get someone from the office to unscrew your nameplate from that title belt, because that title will soon find itself around the waste of the one man who can raise that title to respectability. That title will find itself being carried to new heights by the only man in the NIWF who can deliver what the fans pay to see time and time again, and thatman is not Andrew Starkey, not your bed buddy Buff Baggs, and that man is certainly no longer Sean Steele. The man who will carry the NIWF world title with pride and dignity to new heights is the man who will destroy the make believe legacy of Sean Steele and the next NIWF Worls Heavyweight Champion: Roddy Taylor! And after the crying is over Sean Steele, you won't bow down and pay homage to the man... you'll be in the hospital [Fade]
