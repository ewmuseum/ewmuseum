Hardcore Edge 9/23/02 (Part Five)

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=============================== Diamond Title Match: Fatal Fireworks Korus Omen vs. One Man Army Match Writer: Doug =============================== Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, its now time for the FATAL FIREWORKS DIAMOND TITLE MATCH!!!!!!!! ["Dead Cell" by Papa Roach blares as One Man Army makes his way to the ring. His short blond hair is messed and his face is clean shaven. His tights are dark blue with a silver stripe at the waist and a silver and green spike design going down the left leg. To finish off his ring attire is white elbow pads, white wrist bands black boots. He slides into the ring while the announcer makes the introduction.] Announcer: Introducing, from Anaheim, California. Standing 6'4" and weighing in at 263 lbs. Here is... ONE MAN ARMY!!! Johnny: You're looking at the next Diamond Champion right there. Omen may be a monster but Army is the most violent, vicious athlete I've ever seen. He sort of reminds me of Adam Kuhl when he broke onto the scene. Billy: THAT's a scary thought. We have enough trouble controlling ONE Adam Kuhl. [The lights start to dim as smoke crawls over the stage and big screen. Soon candles are emerging from the curtain as "Gets me through" by Ozzy Osbourne starts to play. Soon the candles dim away as the bass starts to thump, with a flash of blinding light Korus Omen is standing on the stage his arms are crossed over his chest. He raises his head to reveal his pair of black sunglasses and a white face! His fingers are covered in heavy steel rings with sharpened tips! He uncrosses his arms to reveal bulky silver chains with upside down crosses! He starts walking slowly down the ramp followed by Nerris. He stops in front of the ring and climbs up and steps over the top rope and walks in the ring for a while. ] Announcer: Introducing, being acompanied by his manager, Nerris. From Parts Unknown, standing 7'4" and weighing in at 387 lbs. Here is the NIWF DIAMOND CHAMPION... KORUS OMEN!!! Johnny: Just look at that freak of nature! Billy: Omen really is a sight to see. That's one reason to come down and see us live. You don't get a good impression of how big these guys really are, watching on TV. Johnny: Then again it might be a good idea to keep a safe distance from these two. [DING DING] Billy: There's the bell and One Man Army is getting right up in the face of Big Korus Omen. He's not intimidated in the least. Johnny: I hope both men are prepared to get a little scorched because there are literally piles of Fireworks set up around ringside. Billy: Yes and as soon as the match spills out to the floor, Fireworks will start going off at random. Johnny: I'm starting to think we may not be safe here at ringside, Billy. Billy: Welcome to my world, Johnny. [Army stands right up against Omen in the ring and jaw jacks him. Omen completely ignores him and finally Army starts unloading some fists.] Billy: There they go! Army unloading with rights and lefts but Omen is just taking them! Army shooting himself off the ropes... shoulderblock by the Army! Johnny: No effect! Omen just won't budge. Billy: Army shooting himself off the ropes again... Omen catches him with a boot to the face! Omen quickly going to work here. He picks Army up by the throat! Chokeslam!!! Johnny: But look at how resilliant One Man Army is. Already he's back up and ready for more. Billy: Army charges... Jumping swinging DDT!!! Johnny: Wow, he had to really jump for that one. Billy: Omen a little shaken but quickly back to his feet... Army off the ropes... ARMY CLOTHESLINES HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE!!! [CROWD ROARS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: Time to see some fireworks! Billy: Army climbing up to the top rope as Omen stumbles around on the floor... [VIP-VIP-VOOOOOOM!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: MY GAWD!!!! ARMY KNOCKED OFF THE TOP ROPE BY A ROMAN CANDLE TO THE FACE!!!!!!! [CROWD ROARS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: Army didn't expect that! It looked like he was getting ready for a double axe handle off the top when BAM, he gets a face full of fire! Billy: Omen picking up Arm by the air now... POWERBOMB ON THE FLOOR!!! ["OOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: That'll loosen a few fillings. Billy: And look at Omen smiling. The members of the Black Faction are pure evil and.... [BOOM-BOOM-BOOM-BOOM-KABOOOOOOM!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: GOOD LORD! KORUS OMEN IS KNOCKED BACK ABOUT FIFTEEN FEET! ["OOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: Korus took the more violent impact there but thse exposions went off right beside One Man Army. Look at his arm, the flesh is just cooked on his left side. Billy: These men will both need to go to the emergency room after this one. Korus Omen is getting back to his feet. One Man Army is as well. Johnny: What the heck is Omen doing? Billy: It looks like Omen just pulleda roman candle from the bunch. He's gonna fire that thing right in Army's face! Johnny: [Chuckling] Sorry Billy, but I don't see that happening. Billy: Am I missing somethi... [CRRRAAACK!!!!!!!!!!] ["OOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Billy: OHHHHH!!! IT BLEW UP IN HIS HAND!!!!! Johnny: That's because it was dynamite! That blew his thumb right off his hand! Billy: My Gawd! This match needs to stopped! Johnny: One Man Army is nearly done yet. Billy: Omen is flailing around in serious pain! Army drills him with a forearm to the face! He rolls the big man back into the ring. Army scaling the ropes... off the top... DOUBLE AXE HANDLE!!! Johnny: But Omen is STILL on his feet! Billy: One Man Army with a boot to the midsection... Omen catches the foot... OWENZIGURI MULE KICK!!! AND OMEN GOES DOWN!!!!! ARMY WITH THE COVER... ["ONE!!!!!!!!!!"] ["TWO!!!!!!!!!!"] ["THR... NOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!"] Billy: Almost a three count there. Johnny: Army came within an eyelash of becoming the Diamond Champion. [The crowd roars as Handsome Henry emerges from the back with "Lucy". He stops midway down the ramp and pulls out a small bic lighter.] Billy: Oh this is NOT good! ["I-LOVE-LUCY! I-LOVE-LUCY! I-LOVE-LUCY!"] Johnny: I think that chair is more over than Henry. [Henry lights the lighter and sets the barbed wire wrapped chair on fire.] [CROWD ROARS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: That may not be a good idea to bring a bunch of fire down here! Billy: Henry charging down to the ring... [BOOM-BOOM-CRRAACK-KAABOOOOM-VIP-VIP-VOOOOOOOOM!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] [CROWD ROARS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: HENRY RUNS RIGHT THROUGH THE FIREWORKS AND SLIDES IN THE RING!! ONE MAN ARMY CHARGES HIM.... OH GAWD HE GETS A LUCY-SHOT TO THE FACE!!!! ["OOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: OMEN GETS ONE TOO!!!! ["OOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Billy: OH MY GAAAAWWWD!!!!!! OMEN'S HAIR IS ON FIRE!!!!!! [Handsome Henry jumps up on the second rope and raises Lucy high in the air.] ["LU-CY! LU-CY! LU-CY! LU-CY! LU-CY!"] Johnny: What can possibly happen next?! [Adam Kuhl runs out of the back with his Bag O Tricks.] [MONSTER HUGE FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: IT'S ADAM KUHL!!!!!!! Handsome Henry inviting the One Man Crime Spree into the ring by sitting on the middle rope! One Man Army is back to his feet... ARMY SENDS HENRY OUT TO THE FLOOR AND LUCY GOES WITH HIM!!!!! Johnny: OH SHIT! [Explosions engulf the ring as Lucy ignites a chain reaction that sets off everything around the ring. The popping and cracking of the fireworks continues for a full minute before stopping and leaving only a thick cloud around the entire ringside area.] [CROWD ROARS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] ["N-I-WF! N-I-WF! N-I-WF! N-I-WF! N-I-WF!"] Billy: [coughing] Johnny... you alright? Johnny: [Also hacking up a lung.] Yeah. I can't see a damn thing though. [The smoke starts to clear. Army and Omen can be seen stirring inside the ring while Henry lay unconcious on the floor. Adam Kuhl stands on the ramp with his bag, laughing his ass off.] Billy: Well I'm glad somebody finds this chaos amusing. Johnny: I think the whole arena finds this amusing, Billy. ["F'N KUHL! F'N KUHL! F'N KUHL! F'N KUHL!"] Johnny: No, don't encourage him, people! [Kuhl reaches into his bag with a big smile on his face and pulls out a...] Billy: HE'S GOT A PIPEBOMB!!!! [Kuhl laughs like a maniac as he lobs the bomb into the ring right between Omen and Army.] Johnny: OH GAWD NO! [BOOOOOOOOOOOM!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] [CROWD ROARS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] [Army and Omen both get knocked silly and sprayed with shrapnel.] Billy: This is getting WAY out of hand now! Johnny: I totally agree, Billy. But the fans are just eating this up! [Kuh walks towards the ring and uses Handsome Henry's face as a stepping stool to get inside.] Johnny: Okay now that was totally uncalled for. The guy's unconcious! Billy: Kuhl is surveying his damage. What's he doing? Kuhl is dragging Army over to Omen. Johnny: He's draping OMA's arm over Omen's chest! It's a legal cover! Billy: But the ref wisely jumped ship in the early goings of this match. Kuhl now dropping to the mat... he's gonna count the pin himself?! ["ONE!!!!!!!!!"] ["TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] ["THREE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] [CROWD ROARS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: Is that legal? Billy: It's the Diamond title, so isn't EVERYTHING legal? Johnny: Good point. Announcer: HERE IS YOUR WINNER, AND NEW NIWF DIAMOND CHAMPION... ONE MAN ARMYYYYYYY!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Adam Kuhl picks up the Diamond title from ringside and also grabs another Diamond title out of his Bag O Tricks.] Johnny: That's the belt that Jeffrey Dylan Marsh gave him on September 11th! Billy: It looks like Kuhl is going to present both belts to One Man Army. [Kuhl stands over One Man Army with the belts in either hand. But instead of dropping them on the champion, he raises them in the air to a huge face pop.] Billy: What the hell? He's KEEPING them? Johnny: Sure looks that way! Adam Kuhl has posession of both versions of the Diamond title and he's leaving with them. Billy: Well belt or no belt, One Man Army is officially the Diamond Champion... I think. Johnny: Yeah that sounds about right. [The scene cuts to the locker room area of Sean Steele. The crowd goes crazy at seeing their hero. He is lacing his boots up when he begins to speak.] Sean: WELCOME TO THE SEAN STEELE SHOW FOLKS! [The crowd goes nuts in reaction to what he said] Sean: Tonight I have a special guest for you, tonight Sean Steele beats the f*ck out of Lawerence Buffy. [The crowd erupts in agreement.] Sean: Yes, folks, he has been running his mouth for weeks now, saying this and that about how he is going to be World Champion. How I am a sit in, this, that, and the other. [Sean just smiles] Sean: Well, midget, if you REALLY truly believe that since you had the title before and had a successful reign and thing that you are going to win that back, well, just ask Axel Schmidt how that goes. [Sean smiles a shit eating grin as the crowd pops in agreement.] Sean: You see Lawerence, what you fail to realize is the truth. You see the truth of the matter is, is that you are a friggin' ant to me. Hell, if you were to walk up to me, face to face, well, face to stomach, I WOULDN'T EVEN BE ABLE TO SEE YOU IF I WAS LOOKING STRAIGHT AHEAD. Then you want to go off about your technical skills? [He laughs] Sean: Yeah buddy, tell me about them. Sean Steele will give you a school in technicality buddy. Sean Steele is going to take HIS World Title the ring, beat your ass, and walk away STILL WITH HIS WORLD TITLE. [Sean waits for the crowd pop and continues] Sean: TONIGHT [Sean pauses] Sean: SEAN STEELE.... [He takes a deep breath] Sean: TAKES OUT.. [Sean pauses] Sean: ANOTHER LEGEND!! [Sean finishes lacing up his boots and walks out as the crowd explodes in the arena.] Billy: Sean Steele and Lawrence Buffy are NEXT! [Commercial] [The scene opens backstage. Jack Russell is standing in front of a table getting himself a cup of hot coffee when Mad Skillz walks up.] Mad Skillz: Yo, welcome to da NIWF, G! I'm Mad Skillz, and yo, I run with da Popstar and da Marquee Man, yo! Dat's phat, and AAAALLLL dat, ya heard me? [Skillz sticks up his hand for a high five but Jack just looks at him disgusted. Finally after an awkward pause, Jack smiles and extends his hand.] Jack Russell: The name's Jack. [Skillz shakes his hand. Jack smiles and then drills Mad Skillz in the face with his free left hand, which happens to have brass knuckles. Jack pounds Skillz face with rights and lefts until he looks like ground beef. Finally Jack stops the horrible beating and wipes the blood on his knucks off on his jeans.] Jack Russell: Pleased to meet ya! [Russell is about to leave Mad Skillz in a bloody heap when he stops himself and glances back at the table. He picks up the big coffee decanter and pours out the hot liquid onto Mad Skillz' already bloodied head. Russell laughs like a madman as Skillz screams in pain. The scene cuts back to Billy and Johnny at ringside.] Billy: Uh... I'm sorry you had to see that, fans. I really don't know what to say. Johnny: That was SICK! Mad Skillz will be lucky is he lives after that beating. I think Starr needs to rethink his hiring of this Jack Russell. The guy is obviously unbalanced. He makes Adam Kuhl look like a Teddy Bear! Billy: Something has to be done, that's for sure. Hopefully Mad Skillz is okay... Johnny: Face it Billy, he's NOT! You saw what happened back there. His face looks like ground beef! Billy: You're right. You're right. I really don't know what to say. Johnny: Me niether. Let's just continue on with the show. To be continued......
