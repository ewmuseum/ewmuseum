NIWF — News

News & Rumors 02/14/2002

14 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Happy Valentine's Day Everyone! Here is the latest news and rumors around the locker room and the e-wrestling world. -Rumor has been going around that HALF of the SPW locker room has inquired about joining the NIWF. However, only 3 of those wrestlers were really serious and were made possible serious offers. Dirty Dan has been contemplating the idea of letting SPW wrestlers into the fed. He certainly should not deny them, as each wrestler has future superstar potential. I think that around the Free Per View Time, Dirty Dan will finally give in and let some SPW wrestlers make their way into the NIWF. There is no ultimatum that will be forseen and we are hearing that he could possibly allow these wrestlers to wrestle for both organizations! We can tell you this though... one SPW wrestler has joined and will be quitting SPW very soon! -As you have all seen by Dirty Dan's speech in the last card, there will be a Royal Rumble to decide the future NIWF World Champ. This Royal Rumble will take place on the first Free Per View back known as "Dangerous". Also on that card, will be the finals for the tag team titles, North American Title and Women's Title. Week after week in these rumors section, 5 participants will be announced to the rumble. With that, here are the first 5 names who will compete in the 30 man rumble: 1.Dirty Dan 2.The Masked Man 3."The Shocker" Brian Anthony 4.Thomas P. Harding "The Angelic Assasin" 5.The Wasp Look for 5 more to be announced next week!! -Speaking of the world title picture, there has been much tension in the locker room over who deserves this title. Rumor has it that some wrestlers caught wind that Dirty Dan has his world champ picked out already! Many of the wrestlers do not like hearing this, and hopefully this will drive them to try even HARDER to get that world title around their own waists. -The main event this past card was a huge success. Much compliments go out to all the participants in this event for creating some good angles and controversary. This was probably the hardest decision Dirty Dan has made in a main event, so he opted to make the decision based on future angles. He felt that all parties involved were well deserving of a win, but to build heat he would have to base the main event around an angle. -Many of you saw that Dirty Dan was on the phone with someone by the name of "Paul". It is verified, this person is definitely [name withheld]. From what is heard, Dirty Dan is looking for 2 more bookers to help him decide the matches so his decisions are not based solely on his own judging. The top candidates for the position right now are [name withheld], [name withheld], AJ Black & [name withheld]. It has been heard that at least one of those men may already be involved! -Speaking of [name withheld], here is another rumor going around about what him and Dirty Dan talked about. Dirty Dan is running into financial problems, and needs some investors to keep the NIWF running, and [name withheld] invested in the NIWF in order to keep it going, and in exchange for doing so, he made Dirty Dan promise that Desmond Walker would get pushed to the moon. Apparently Desmond Walker is former property of [name withheld] from the the previous BWF stint. -Sean Steele and Brad Baggs were seen cruising around Newark (who in their right mind would do that?). Word on the grapevine is that they are expected to start a tag team. This is only rumor and no superstar has been approached about it. More info as the story progresses or degresses. -Word is that the main event for the next card will be the TV Title match and it will be a brutal one! This title for the time being, is going to have some serious hardcore matches and may pick up right where the Diamond Title left off. -Speaking of the Diamond Title, two wrestlers who will not be mentioned, have approached Dirty Dan about the possibility of going right into SPW to bring back the Diamond Title to the NIWF! It has been also discussed that Dirty Dan will address the issue of the Diamond Title and may even CHALLENGE the current Shootfire Pro Superstar who has the Diamond Title or who will have the Diamond Title, to come in and defend it in the NIWF. -After her match last week, many of the wrestlers saw Medusa Rage really pissed off back stage. It is said that Medusa Rage was not too happy with the way her match ended, in a screwjob by the attack from Kitti. Medusa Rage was talking about extracting revenge and look out! She is the referee of Kitti's match this coming card! I have a feeling that this could turn ugly! -Many people in the NIWF have not been receiving cards or updates for reasons unknown. Dirty Dan is aware of this situation and has asked certain people in the fed to forward the cards to their fellow e-wrestlers. I hope the situation is resolved within time. There is talk that a few people may have some sort of mail filters on that may be blocking certain words or denying people from receiving anything from a BCC'ed list. This weekend, the website will be worked on VERY hard after Vocal Explosions, and sure enough all the past updates will be included on it. That's it, now here is the schedule! Remember, roleplays are due on Saturday at 10am Eastern! Even if you don't have a match, roleplay anyway, IT HELPS! ------------------------------------------------------------- NIWF Presents "Vocal Explosions" Saturday February 16th, 2002 Send in Roleplays by 10am Eastern on this date! ------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- NIWF Presents "Hardcore Edge" LIVE from the NIWF Arena in Newark, NJ Sunday February 24th, 2002 *Card Subject to Change ----------------------------------------------- 1st Round North American Title Matches (the last two): "Saint" Ian Walsh vs. Andrew "The Icon" Starkey "Blues Xplosion" Luke Denton vs. "SuperNatural" Simon Spinx 1st Round Tag Team Round Robin Tournament Knock Down Drag Out vs. Damage Inc.
