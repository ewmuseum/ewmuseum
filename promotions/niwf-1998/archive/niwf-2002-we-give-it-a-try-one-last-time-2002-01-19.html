We Give It a Try One Last Time!!

19 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You knew it was coming, and after a year long hiatus from e-wrestling and running a fed I have decided I miss the *fun* of this little game we all play. If you have no interest, then just ignore the message. If you are interested in becoming a part of the NIWF once again or for the first time then continue to read on. Also, if you know people that will be interested in becoming part of the fed then please tell them. And, if you have the latest set of rankings from 2001 before I shut down the fed, can you please e-mail it to me? Otherwise, it's going to have to be the 1998/1999 rankings I have to use. Before I get started, I just want to run by a few things with many of you: -I will not start this fed until I have reliable match writers. I myself will usually write one or two matches, but I need the match writers. If you can do this, it requires writing two (possibly only one) match every two weeks. The new format is every two weeks for a card. -I am not accept any CHARACTERS from Shootfire Pro wrestling. If you are a part of Shootfire Pro but have a different character you can use here, then I will allow it. The reasoning for this is because I participate in Shootfire Pro myself and like the competition and don't want anyone to stop doing what they do in Shootfire to be here. If you want in here, it's either you are here in the NIWF or there in Shootfire! In other words, if you want in the NIWF then do me a favor and take a leave from Shootfire so you can concentrate on what you do in the NIWF... and if I was promoting Shootfire ( http://www.shootfire-pro.com ) then I would say the same thing as well. I wouldn't want someone to take up space in the NIWF to concentrate in shootfire. But I will be fair, I plan on having a royal rumble in a few months and will allow some shootfire pro wrestlers to participate depending on how serious you want to be with the NIWF. Now, onto the other important stuff! (PS, I am working on updating the page to be better, so this is just the basics) I am sure many of you remember (or not) what the NIWF was about, but if you don't then go to: http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/rules.html however this still needs to be updated as well, but this is the basics. Also, cards will be once every two weeks unless I really get some realiable match writers to help me put it out once a week like I would like to have! If you want some good sample cards to see what we are about, then go to the following sites: (all sample cards are on the site as well) http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/best_f_the_ppv.html http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/f_the_ppv_05_09_98.html http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/f_the_ppv_05_16_98.html http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/outback.html http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/No_Name_11_03_98.html And for an idea of how we do roleplays, it's a roleplay show that is sent out as one message (no roleplay board) so go here to look at a sample show: (all roleplay shows are on the site too) http://www.geocities.com/NIWFWrestling/ve11_03_98.html I'm sorry the website is in bad shape but it will be fixed once we get things rolling. So basically, here is what is going to happen. Were you a part of the NIWF before? If so and you believe I still have your application, drop me a line with your e- wrestler's name and I'll confirm whether or not I have it. If I don't have it or you are a newbie to the NIWF and want to fill out a new application, I will attach one at the bottom of this message. Now, how are things going to work out? Well, first off I *will* need *reliable* match writers to write 2 matches every two weeks (yes, I will be writing one to two matches a week as well) Once I can get about 3 writers, I will start the cards the next week. The first 16 people to join will be part of a tournament that will start to the North American Title. The first 8 cruiserweights that join will be part of the cruiserweight tournament, the first 4 females that join will be part of the Women's Title Tourney , the first 10 Hardcore Mother Fuckers to join will be part of the 10 Man Lunatic Fringe Match on the very first card (we already have a few participants). The first 4 Tag Teams to join will compete in a series of Round Robin tournament matches (one tag team must face the other 3 tag teams, the one with the best record is crowned champ), and the first 6 International Guys will be part of a tournament as well. (TV Title matches will be held randomly). All final matches will take place on our first Free Per View of the year... just go with the flow. World title situation will be addressed on our first card. Our schedule will normally go like this: -Mondays: Updates for Schedule & Rankings will be given to you guys. - Tuesdays: Go ahead and work on your roleplays -Weds: Strats for your matches are due in, match assignments will be handed out. -Thursdays: News & Rumors -Friday: Work on roleplays and matches (try to get Roleplays to me this day) -Saturdays: Roleplays due at 2pm Eastern Standard Time the Latest, Vocal Explosions sent out, matches due to me by writers -Sundays: NIWF Hardcore Edge (bi-weekly card), matches due at 2pm Eastern Standard Time the latest. The Sunday there isn't a card, I may do some "dark matches". There you have it. Things will fit in perfectly and you will just roll along with what will happen. Remember, it's what you make of the fed that determines how fun it will be and what it will be like. I've put up with many people bitching and other things in the past, the fed does not survive on just one roleplayer or one e-wrestler, it survives on all of us as a team. Here is the application!!
