The Apollo Report 10/30/02

30 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Apollo Report Oct. 30th, 2002. Well it's been a hectic few weeks. We're just getting started with the weekly format and it's been a strain on us working stiffs. But the good news is that Mayhem was a huge success and everyone backstage is extremely happy with the current product. Hopefully you fans are diggin' it as well. But let's get to the real reason you're all reading this column... behind the scenes news and those oh so juicy rumours. NEWS & RUMOURS: The company is negotiating with a number of new talents in the wrestling world. [name withheld] (Executive in charge of Talent relations) has been working double time checking out the hottest athletes in the business. The cruiserweight division is the main focus at the moment. Speaking of cruiserweights; Dewey will be returning to action in the coming weeks. While the company is happy with Dewey as an "entertainer", he has been sent to the Starr Academy to tune up his skills between the ropes. T-Bone & Tommy Jackal, AKA Bad Element, approached me this week. It seems they were offended by my blunt comments in the last issue. Well boys, all you have to do is prove me wrong. Until then, my opinion stands. I got a chance to speak with Roddy Taylor after last week's Mayhem. Needless to say, he wasn't in a very good mood. In regards to Sean Steele's & Brad Baggs' involvement, he said, "Make no mistake, there will be hell to pay for the over pushed Sean Steele & Brad Baggs". Ouch. Last week I reported that [name withheld] was in talks with NIWF Owner, Doug [name withheld]. Well the talking is over and Dwain was officially brought in this week as a Vice President. [name withheld] will be working closely with [name withheld] as a creative consultant. What's up with the One Man Army lately? He burst onto the scene and turned the NIWF upside down when he arrived in August, but lately... not so much. I certainly hope he can regain his focus because, in my humble opinion, he could be NIWF Champion someday. Ryan Roberts has really impressed me lately. It's almost as if whatever One Man Army lost, the Death Dealer found. "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice was spotted backstage at Mayhem last weekend. Take that for what it's worth. Is it me or do the Red Bomb Dragons need a manager? They got all the moves but without the ability to speak english it's gonna be tough for them to get over. I really enjoyed the first edition of "Inside the Squared Circle" this evening. This will likely be a weekly feature and is the first pet project of [name withheld] along with [name withheld]. Look for an exclusive interview with Sean Steele next week. The next Free Per View will be entitled, "Season's Beatings". The main event will likely be Sean Steele vs. Eric Idol for the World Championship. But then again, who knows what could happen before then. Speaking of which. This weekend, Mayhem will present a four corners match with the winner getting a shot at the World Championship the following week on Mayhem. The four are; Eric Idol, Roddy Taylor, Andrew Starkey, & Jack Russell. I'm not even going to TRY and pick a winner for this one. Other matches planned for the FPV include; Desmond Walker vs. Lance Nelson for the Old School belt, Roddy Taylor vs. Andrew Starkey for the International title, & Adam Kuhl vs. Chris King for the Diamond title. INJURY UPDATE: Adam Kuhl is planning to work this weekend even though his doctor has advised against it. Desmond Walker continues to work with a broken forearm. Still nothing new to report on Lawrence Buffy's neck injury. He's supposed to see a specialist this week. Mad Skillz is recovering at home after his facial reconstructive surgery. Andrew Starkey's shoulder held up nicely under the abuse last weekend. He's not out of the woods just yet but it's a good sign. That's about all I've got for this week. Tune in to Mayhem this Sunday, it's gonna be a blast. Until then, Billy Apollo
