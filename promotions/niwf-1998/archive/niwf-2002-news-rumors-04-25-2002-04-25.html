News & Rumors

25 April 2002

What a bad week it has been for me! But that still doesn't stop me from sending out these great news and rumors!! -As you all saw, Shot has quit the NIWF. Dirty Dan is upset that Shot left because he felt that Shot had a legitimate future here in the NIWF despite the losses. The door will always be open for Shot to come back into the NIWF when the time is right for him. -A couple of former BWF guys were seen hanging around some NIWF officials and also seen entering the NIWF Headquarters. Both of these BWF guys have wrestled in the NIWF before and their names are Christian Hawk and Chaz Darby. It is expected that at least one of them has already signed with the NIWF and one of them plans on signing in the next few weeks as well. -This week's card is going to be a big card. It is expected that this card will change the direction of the NIWF and make it even better and stronger than it already is. Not too many details have been given about the exact happenings on this card but everyone seems to be talking about it and the rumor mill is swirling. -Apparently Dirty Dan is very happy with the participation that he has gotten from everyone in the fed. So happy, as a matter of fact, that he feels he can predict who belongs where in the fed and has also predicted future Free Per View matches for the next Free Per View. -As we stated last week, Mr. Excitement Dropkick was having some talks with Dirty Dan about returning to the NIWF. Apparently these talks have ceased and have not continued. It seems that the price was not right to get Dropkick into the NIWF so Dirty Dan is going to have a few talks with him to try and con him into coming back into the NIWF and make some offers he can't refuse. -This past week, there was a cheap shot taken at the NIWF. We are not sure if it was meant as a shot at the NIWF or if the person was just upset because they can't bring their own character into the NIWF. Basically, what was said in a personal message out of character was this: "As for NIWF, if you would take the band off of SPW characters coming over while still working with SPW then maybe a lot more people would come over. At one point I can see the reaosning but at others people like myself don't want to take the time to build a whole new chacater. Just something you should consider." The NIWF officials thought this was bad press as it made it seem like they were saying nobody was joining the NIWF at all and we had a weak roster. However, Dirty Dan does see the point this person is trying to make about joining the NIWF and will consider opening up some more roster spots and maybe allowing current SPW stars to compete in both feds, but right now he stands where he stands about SPW stars having to quit the fed before they join the NIWF or having the handler create a new character. -Apparently Sean Steele is not very happy with the way he has been misused in the NIWF and feels that he needs to be wrestling in more matches in singles competition. Dirty Dan is pitting him against a newcomer, Tyler "American Made" Johnson but Sean Steele feels that a newcomer is not a worthy opponent for him and wants a real challenge. It's doubtful that Tyler "American Made" Johnson will be taken out of that match, but instead he will be placed in the Royal Rumble himself and will get a fair shot at winning the NIWF World Title. -Finally, the schedule for the Free Per View is expected to be set after this weekend's card. The last few guys for the Diamond Title will be announced, the #1 contenders match for the North Anerican Title will be determined, as well as the finals for the N/A Title. There will also be the Purist's Dream Match which will be determined.
