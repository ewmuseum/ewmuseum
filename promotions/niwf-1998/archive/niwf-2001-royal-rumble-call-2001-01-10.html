We Need 8 Wrestlers for a Royal Rumble!

10 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You heard it here first. The NIWF is looking for 8 wrestlers, possibly more, to fill up spots for the Royal Rumble. This could either be a one shot deal for you or you could be in the NIWF before the Rumble on a tryout basis to see how you like it and enter the rumble and make your decision on whether or not you want to stay in the NIWF. Did I mention that the 30 Man Royal Rumble will most likely be for the NIWF World Title as well? That is right, the Royal Rumble will be for the NIWF World Title. But if you want only a one time deal with the NIWF just for the Rumble then chances of you winning that title will decrease. But if you decide on the try out basis and enter the rumble once it comes along, then you might have a good chance of winning if you can survive with the best in the NIWF! Are you interested?
