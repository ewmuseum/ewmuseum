News & Rumors 03/28/2002

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, let's get right to the news & rumors: -The next few weeks are expected to have a LOT of changes in schedule. So don't be upset if you see your name on the Free Per View on a certain match one week and the next week you see it somewhere else. It only means I am trying to fit the right people in the right situations. -Speaking of the Free Per View, there already has been a change. The Lunatic Fringe Match for the Diamond Title has been changed. Eric Henry will not be defending the Diamond Title on the show because he lost the Diamond Title on SPW's PPV known as Legacy. Instead, taking his place will be former NIWF World Champ, Todd Lucchesi. Rumor has it though that Dirty Dan may let Eric Henry participate in this match to try and win the title back for himself. What does this mean? It means we might see 2 SPW stars in the NIWF Free Per View! -Rumors has it that a former NIWF superstar may be making his return back to the rumble. The wrestlers have been talking in the back and heard some little tidbits from our underground source. Supposedly, the wrestler who could be coming back was a former NIWF World Champion. -Just yesterday, former NIWF wrestler "The Extreme War Machine" Corey Irons was seen coming out of the NIWF headquarters. Rumors have it that he is in negotiations with Dirty Dan about coming back to the NIWF possibly as early as 3 weeks from now. There has been nothing set in stone, however, but there is a sense of anxiety backstage with the return of one of the favorites in the NIWF. -There has been some outside interest from various wrestlers about joining the NIWF. Dirty Dan feels that the roster may get too big to handle the load and may re-evaluate the status of the contracts of some wrestlers after the Free Per View. -As you all saw last week, Desmond Walker made the pin during Sean Steele's match. Even though Desmond Walker was not a legal participant in the match, anything really goes in the NIWF. Therefore, the decision from the bookers was to let this pinfall stand and allow Desmond Walker to take Sean Steele's place and advance to the next round of the tournament. -Since we were talking about the Diamond Title, it has finally been announced who the first 5 participants are in this Lunatic Fringe Match for the Diamond Title. The first five are the following: Diamond Champ from SPW - Todd Lucchesi Thomas P. Harding "The Angelic Assasin" Jeremy Colton Christ Dennis Windham "The Shocker" Brian Anthony -A little news on the women's division. The next match that Kitti is participating in is her final match in the NIWF until further notice. Instead, she is going to be used as strictly a backstage role where she interviews wrestlers when needed. -You will all be glad to know that CK will return and wrestle on the next card. We understand that he is still feeling some effects of what happened to him with the masked man but has been cleared to wrestle matches. Dirty Dan is very pleased with how CK has recovered. -Speaking of the Masked Man, there is still no word as to who he is. But, Dirty Dan is looking to unmask in their up and coming matchup. It is believed that the masked man now is either a wrestler in SPW or a wrestler who was in SPW before and is showing some sort of support for them. -Finally, the next 5 wrestlers have been announced for the Royal Rumble. This means that there are only 5 more wrestlers to be named to the Rumble next week to make it complete. Here are the 25 wrestlers we currently have in the rumble: 1.Dirty Dan 2.The Masked Man 3."The Shocker" Brian Anthony 4.Thomas P. Harding "The Angelic Assasin" 5.The Wasp 6.Andrew "The Icon" Starkey 7.Dennis Windham 8.Desmond Walker 9.Shot 10.CK 11."Saint" Ian Walsh 12."Blues Xplosion" Luke Denton 13.Eric Idol 14.T-Bone 15."Dashing" Derek Lankford 16."Handsome" Henry 17."Wonder Boy" Tom Coleman 18.Sean Steele 19.Jeremy Colton Christ 20."The Heretic" Derek Weaver 21.Beanie 22.Robbie Dixon 23.Dr. DooM 24.Swift Nick 25.The Blaze And of course, here is the schedule.
