Hey gang, We're looking for cruisers. If you handle a cruiser, maybe you'd like to ply your trade with the NIWF? You can search google for our past events, or just follow this link: http://groups.google.com/groups?q=NIWF&ie=ISO-8859-1&hl=en I think we bring a pretty entertaining product to our handlers, with some pretty cool features: Apollo Report - Weekly Inside the Squared Circle - Weekly Mayhem - Weekly Free Per View - Monthly We currently have 12 active handlers, and 4 handlers on the edge of free ageny. We're a fair bunch of guys. Over the past six years we've built a pretty high work ethic to the feds we've run: BWF, NIWF, WIWO. So feel free to give us a try. I'm sure you won't be sorry.
