Former NIWF e-wrestlers?!

22 December 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, I am not sure if this is where I can reach all of you. First of all, for those of you in the states, Happy Holidays (or for those who celebrate whatever). Anyway, I hope everything is all well with everyone. I was thinking, and call me crazy, that I would love to have the one last "Free Per View" for the NIWF. Call it a once in a year thing, but it's an idea I am toying with to finally put an end to everything.. plus, I need my fix of e-wrestling and no way do I see myself running a fed again, so maybe I can start something here... just one Free Per View, with a big build up to it, with some of the biggest and most anticipated matches in the history of the NIWF we have been wanting to see. I'm looking for some of the former NIWF guys, especially the big players, to see who is interested and figured this would be a good place to start. If you are around, e-mail me at [detail withheld] and let me know if you want in. I do realize I have not been around for two years, and nor do I plan on starting a fed again but we never got to really end this thing the proper way, as I just gave away the fed for someone else to handle and they "merged" it with their own fed and another fed only to eventually do away with the NIWF name. There will be minimal involvement from everyone, just one or two roleplays and whatever in-rings and stuff you want, but I will write the whole thing myself. Let me know if you guys are interested and pass it along to anyone who may be interested to begin with. Here are some match ideas I am toying with, let me know... the good thing is, I will be fair in all this and probably just use it to have a little fun: 7th Heaven Match: Pick any 7 BWF Guys and get them involved, as long as they were in NIWF. If BWF is not around, let's make this for the BWF World Title. Special Grudge Match: Falls Count Anywhere Match, Axel Schmidt vs. Jeffery Dylan Marsh w/ Dirty Dan as special guest referee. Four Way Elimination, Match of Champions: Sean Steele vs. Corey Irons vs. "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice vs. Eric Idol... winner takes the prize of NIWF World Title Thos are just some ideas I would toy with... what do you guys think? I can go on and on with certain characters being involved in this one big "cluster f&*k". Which former NIWF guys are up for it? Let me know. Just contact me and I'll get you involved and for those who don't remember me, I apologize for sending this out to the group but this was the only way I could start reaching out to some people!
