Hardcore Edge 9/23/02 (Main Event)

24 September 2002

=========================== NIWF World Title Match: Sean Steele vs. Lawrence Buffy Match Writer: Doug =========================== Billy: It's that time once again, Johnny. The Heavyweight Championship of the World is up for grabs! Johnny: And this one might be Sean Steele's toughest challenger to date. It's one thing to talk about Lawrence Buffy and say that he's one of the greatest of all time. But just look at the roll he's on lately. Four weeks ago at Civil War, Lawrence beat Eric Henry for the NIWF Cruiserweight title. Two weeks ago, Lawrence Buffy pinned Christian Hawk and became the NIWF North American Champion. They say the third time is the charm... Sean Steele better hope not! Billy: It's undeniable the momentum that Lawrence Buffy has going into this match. Buffy has won many world titles for other organisations, but never the NIWF World Title. You take all that and combine it with Sean Steele's recent record, and the champion will have his work cut out for him. Johnny: That's true but let's not bury the champ just yet. Sure, two weeks ago he suffered an upset loss to Brad Baggs. That loss has GOT to be weighing heavy on the champion's mind. But we are talking about a guy who burst onto the scene and has backed down from nobody! Sean Steele is one hell of a champion and it's gonna take something special to separate him from his belt. Buffy may be the man to do it, but it's nothing I'd want to put money on. Billy: That's for sure. Let's go to our ring announcer now for the introductions. Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen this bout is set for one fall and is for the NIWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP OF THE WORLD!!! [CROWD ROARS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] ["Liverpool Requiem" blares as Lawrence Buffy makes his way to the ring. He is wearing his usual long glittering robe with "Lawrence Buffy" written on the back. Inside the ring he removes his robe and is wearing simple trunks with "LB" on the side. He tosses back his sandy blonde hair as the announcer introduces him.] Announcer: Introducing, from Liverpool England. Standing 6' and weighing in at 19 Stone... LAWRENCE BUFFY!!! [MONSTER HEEL POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: Lawrence sure looks confident going into this match. Johnny: Have you EVER seen him look anything but confident? Lawrence Buffy is Lawrence Buffy's biggest fan. Billy: I'm surprised he's coming out alone. After Shawn Osborne's appearance two weeks ago, I figured he'd be a fixture at ringside. Johnny: You can bet that he's nearby and watching very closely. Announcer: AND HIS OPPONENT... [INSANELY HUGE FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] [Rob Zombie screams 'I FEEL SO GOOD I FEEL SO NUMB YEAH' and the pyro goes off as Sean Steele's entrance begins. Two white S's go on the jumbo tron and red and white pyro go blasting through the arena. Sean Steele walks out in his wrestling attire and a t-shirt with the NIWF World Title strapped across his shoulder. He walks to the ring and slides under the ropes and goes to 2 opposite corners and pumps the crowd up.] Announcer: Introducing, from St. Louis, MO, weighing in at 289 lbs... THE NIWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE WORLD!!! SEAN STEEEEEEEEEELE!!!!!!!!! [FACE POP OF THE NIGHT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] ["SUPERSTAR! SUPERSTAR! SUPERSTAR!"] Billy: Listen to that ovation for the NIWF Champion! Johnny: What choice do I have? I can't hear anything else! Billy: This crowd is jacked and they know that tonight they are getting a match that other feds would be charging thirty bucks for! Johnny: This just goes to show how much of a fighting champion Sean Steele really is. He'll fight anybody, anytime. [DING DING] Billy: They circle eachother in the centre of the ring. A little stare down now as they stand nose to nose. Johnny: I think you mean nose to neck. Steele's got eight inches on Lawrence Buffy. Billy: ..... Johnny: What? Billy: Sorry, I've been working with Hardy for so long I was just waiting for you to make some sexual joke about his eight inches. Johnny: I feel for you man. Billy: OHHH! Buffy with a slap across the face of the champion... Steele fires back with a hard shove that sends Buffy crashing to the mat! [Steele stands in front of Buffy and flexes his traps.] [HUGE FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: Steele playing some mind games there. Billy: Buffy not too happy about that as he gets to his feet. Collar and elbow tie up... Buffy with a knee to the midsection... elbow to the back of the head by Buffy... Buffy with the gutwrench suplex!!! [Buffy pops back up to his feet and raises his hands in victory with a smug smile on his face.] ["BOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Billy: Some mind games from Buffy that time. Johnny: He's the master, no question. Billy: Steele back to his feet. They lock up... side headlock by Buffy... Steele backs him up and shoots him to the ropes... shoulder block by the champion! Steele runs the ropes... Buffy with a duck under... Steele misses the clothesline... Buffy with a standing dropkick takes the champion down! [Buffy once again raises his hands in the air and this time does a little strut as the fuming Sean Steele gets to his feet.] ["BOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: Nice dropkick by Buffy. I don't know that it warranted the celebration he gave it. But it was nice. Billy: Buffy seems to be really getting under the Champ's skin. Buffy putting up his fists and sticking his chin out for Steele to take the first shot. Steele with a right... Buffy ducks out of the way. Buffy taunting the champ by sticking his chin out once again... Steele fakes a right... Buffy tries to duck it... Hard left hand from the champion! [POWER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: Buffy quickly back to his feet... Steele drills him again! Buffy back up and Steele sends him for the ride... Buffy off the ropes... Steele scoops him up in a military press! Where's he gonna put him? Johnny: Awesome display of strength by Sean Steele! Billy: Steele walking over to the ropes.... HE DROPS BUFFY RIGHT OUT TO THE FLOOR!!!! ["OOOOOOHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Billy: Steele now climbing up to the top rope... what the heck is he thinking? Johnny: This is new! Billy: OH MY GAWD!!! FROG SPLASH ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR!!!!!!! ["OOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] ["HOLY-SHIT! HOLY-SHIT! HOLY-SHIT!"] Johnny: Steele got some BIG air with that move. Billy: Steele now whipping Buffy into the steel guard rail! Buffy is out on his feet! Steele backing up and taking a run at him.... STEELE MISSES THE SPEAR AND GOES HEADFIRST INTO THE RAIL!!! ["OOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: And look at Buffy, ever the opportunist! Billy: Buffy with a handful of hair... Steele is busted wide open... Buffy with a swinging neck breaker on the floor!!! Johnny: If you even make the smallest error, Lawrence Buffy will pick up on it and use it against you. Everything they say about Lawrence being a ring General is true. Billy: And he'll be the first to tell you that! Lawrence Buffy now whipping the champ shoulder first into the steel ring post! [Buffy grabs the mic from the ring announcer sitting at ringside.] Lawrence Buffy: YOU FILTHY NEW JERSEY SLOBS ARE LOOKING AT THE NEXT... OOOF!!! [POWER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: Steele has heard enough and he shuts Buffy up with a boot to the midsection! Steele pulls him in for a powerbomb... where's he gonna put him... OOOHHH POWERBOMB RIGHT THROUGH THE RUSSIAN ANNOUNCE TABLE!!!! ["OOOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: I sure am glad we speak english, Billy. They always go for the other guys. ["SUPERSTAR! SUPERSTAR! SUPERSTAR!"] Billy: Steele now rolling the Buffster back into the ring. He might be able to get a three count if he covered him right now. Johnny: I think Steele is looking to put and exclamation point on it. That may be a big mistake. Billy: Steele laying a few healthy stomps on Buffy while he's down! Steele picks him up... DEATH VALLEY DRIVER BY THE CHAMP!!! STEELE MAKES THE COVER!!! 1!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! KICKOUT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ["BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Billy: Steele picks him up by the hair... T-BONE SUPLEX!!! Buffy could be in serious trouble here! [The crowd lets out a HUGE HEEL POP as "The Strangler" Shawn Osborne strolls out from the back and starts making his way down the aisle.] Johnny: Not for long cause here comes the back up! Billy: Sean Steele sees Osborne coming! Steele telling him to bring it! But he's got his back turned to Buffy... SCHOOL BOY ROLL UP BY BUFFY!!!!! 1!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! KICKOUT WITH AUTHORITY!!!! Johnny: Steele powered Buffy right back up to his feet with that kickout! Billy: Osborne getting ready to insert himself in this match... [MONSTER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] [The crowd goes wild as Christian Hawk charges out of the crowd and smashes a whiskey bottle over Osborne's head!] ["OOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Billy: OH MY GAAAAAAWWWWD!!!! CHRISTIAN HAWK HAS TAKEN OUT THE STRANGLER!!! Johnny: Osborne is gonna need a lot of stitches after that! He's bleeding like a stuck pig out there! Billy: Hawk leaving Osborne in a puddle of his own blood! [Hawk turns to Steele in the ring gives him a small nod. Steele returns it as Hawk exits over the guard rail and disapears into the sea of fans.] Billy: Lawrence Buffy with a high knee to the back! He caught the champ off guard there! Buffy follows up with a German suplex... he hangs on and nails Steele with another one... still holding on Buffy nails him with a release German suplex! Johnny: Nice move by the Buffster. That really took a toll on the Champ. Billy: Buffy now dropping an elbow... Buffy rolls him over for some kind of toe hold... NO IT'S AN STF! THE BUFFY STRETCH!!!! ["BOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: Steele is in a world of hurt right now! Billy: Steele struggling to hang on! The ref is asking him if he gives up... Steele screams out 'NO' with a passion! The NIWF World Championship hangs in the balance here! Johnny: Buffy has it locked on nice and tight but he's not quite centered in the ring. Billy: Steele seems aware of that fact as well as he's trying desperately to get himself to the ropes! ["SUPERSTAR! SUPERSTAR! SUPERSTAR!"] [The crowd boos as Brad Baggs jogs out from the back with a steel chair in his hand.] Billy: The champion is hanging on by merely a thread! He's just inches away from the ropes.... HE GOT THE BOTTOM ROPE!!! WHAT THE HELL?! [Baggs slides into the ring, lifts the chair high over his head and smashes Buffy right across the back!] [VERY LOUD BUT MIXED REACTION FROM THE CROWD!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: What the hell was that?! Billy: The ref pushing Baggs back now and he's leaving voluntarily. The damage has been done... but why? [Baggs leaves the ring with a smile on his face. Steele looks up briefly from the mat and Baggs gives him a "friendly" wave. Steele sneers at him as Baggs walks back up the ramp laughing.] ["TITTIES-SAG-BAGGS! TITTIES-SAG-BAGGS!"] Billy: The crowd with an unusual chant for Brad Baggs as he leaves. Johnny: Ha ha, that's a line Sean Steele used on a recent Vocal Explosions. It's like these people memorise everything Steele says! Billy: Steele getting to his feet as Buffy gets to one knee... ULTIMATE KNEE STRIKES FROM THE CHAMPION!!! [POWER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: Buffy has a glazed over look in his eyes. I don't think he's all there anymore. Billy: Steele picking up by the hair and Buffy looks to be out on his feet.... WOAH BUFFY WITH A BOOT TO THE GUT!!! Johnny: HE WAS PLAYING POSSUM! Billy: Front face lock by Buffy... LIVERPOOL PLUNGE!!!!!!!!! LIVERPOOL PLUNGE!!!!!!!!! Johnny: WE'VE GOT A NEW WORLD CHAMPION!!! Billy: BUFFY DRAPES AN ARM OVER HIM!!!!!! 1!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 3!!!!!!!!! NO!!!! STEELE JUST GOT THE SHOULDER UP!!!!!!! BUFFY CAN'T BELIEVE IT!!! HE HOOKS THE LEG!!!!! 1!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! KICKOUT!!!!! STEELE KICKS OUT AGAIN!!!! [INSANELY HUGE FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: I can't believe it! Billy: Niether can Lawrence Buffy who's getting in the face of referree Shane Francis. Johnny: This may not be the best time for Lawrence to be filing a grievance with the official! Billy: Steele back to his feet... He charges Buffy from behind with the clothesline.... BUFFY DUCKS IT AND STEELE HITS THE REF!!!!! BUFFY WITH A BOOT TO THE MIDSECTION.... LIVERPOOL PLUNGE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! BUFFY COVERS HIM!!!! Johnny: But there's nobody to make the count! 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... Get a new ref down here dammit! Billy: Francis shaking off the cobwebs and crawling over to count the pin!!!! 1!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! KICKOUT!!!! [POWER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: Sean Steele is damn lucky because that was an extremely slow count. Billy: Buffy is furious and rightfully so. Buffy now backing Steele up into the turnbuckles... hard knife edge chops by Buffy... Irish whip to the far side... STEELE WITH THE REVERSAL...HE HANGS ONTO THE ARM AND PULLS BUFFY IN... EMERALD FUSION!!!!!!!!!! THAT'S "TOO MUCH!!!!!!!!!" [INSANE FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: STEELE WITH THE COVER!!! 1!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 3!!!! NO KICKOUT!!!!!! BUFFY KICKED OUT OF THE "TOO MUCH"!!!!! ["BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"] Johnny: You know what they say; 'Too Much' is never enough. Billy: Steele with a handful of hair picks him up... HE NAILED HIM AGAIN!!! ANOTHER EMERALD FUSION BY SEAN STEELE!!!! [MONSTER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Johnny: Now THAT should be enough! Billy: STEELE HOOKS THE LEG!!!! 1!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [UNBELIEVABLE FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] DING DING DING!!! Billy: Sean Steele has done it again!!! Johnny: He worked his ass off for this one though. Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner... AND STILL NIWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE WOOOOOORRRRRLD!!!!!!! SEEEAAAAN STEEEEEEELLLLE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [MONSTER HUGE FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Billy: Well fans that's all for this week's Hardcore Edge. Johnny: I don't think I could take anymore, Billy! Billy: Obviously we don't know the whole story regarding Doug Hardy's arrest earlier tonight. Hopefully we'll get that info to you on Vocal Exlosions next weekend. So in the event that Doug is back here in two weeks, I just want to say that it's been a pleasure calling the action with you tonight, Johnny. Johnny: Thanks, Billy. I really had a good time and I'd love to come back and do it again sometime. Billy: That would be just fine with me. We're out of time, so for Johnny Simpson, I'm Billy Apollo. See you next week on Vocal Explosions! [Show ends]
