Vocal Explosions 11-10-02 (Main Event Match)

10 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
======================================= World Tag Team Title Match THE DEITIES vs. STEELE & BAGGS ======================================= Mark: It's time for the match that everyone has been talking about. Last week on Mayhem there was a controversial finish to the tag title match. Johnny: I don't think it as all that controversial. After all, Steele and Baggs knew that the Prophet and likely Xanthos would get involved; they always do. Mark: Well I guess Jason Starr isn't going to let them get away with it this time. It's happening right now folks. Hugh Jass: Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is set for one fall and is for the NIWF World Tag Team Championship!!! [HUGE POP!!!] ["Til I collapse" by Eminem blares as the crowd erupts in cheers. "DBS" flashes across the jumbo tron as fireworks rain down around the entrance. Sean Steele & Brad Baggs walk out with Stacey following close behind.] Hugh Jass: Introducing first, the challengers, at a total combined weight of 576 lbs, Sean Steele & Brad Baggs... D-B-S!!!!! [MONSTER FACE POP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] Hugh Jass: And there opponents... [BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] ["More Human then Human" by White Zombie begins to play over the loudspeakers as The Prophet & Xanthos lead the Suicide Kid and Wasted Youth down to the ring. The crowd shows their disdain by yelling, booing and throwing things at the guys.] Hugh Jass: Introducing, being leg to the ring by the Prophet & Xanthos, at a total combined weight of 440 lbs. They are the NIWF WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS!!! Suicide Kid and Wasted Youth... THE DEITIES!!! Johnny: And once again the Prophet has his entire stable at ringside. Do they honestly expect the result to be different this time? Mark: You could be on to something, Johnny. It looks like Baggs is gonna start things off against Wasted Youth. [DING DING] Mark: There they go! Baggs and Wasted Youth exchanging rights and lefts! Baggs with an Irish whip... Youth off the ropes... Youth with a baseball slide through the legs! Baggs turns and drills him in the midsection with a knee. Baggs hooks him and nails him with a capture suplex! Johnny: Nice move. Baggs is doing a good job of asserting himself in there. Mark: Baggs makes the tag to Sean Steele. Steele climbs the ropes while Baggs holds Wasted Youth in an arm bar. Steele off the top with a double axehandle! Steele and Baggs no both overlapping their hands around Youth's throat... Double chokeslam!!! Johnny: The Prophet ain't happy about that one. Mark: Well that's too darn bad for him! Steele with a whip to the ropes... he nails Youth with a clothesline from hell! Steele going for the cover... 1................ 2....................... Mark: Suicide Kid breaks it up with a leg drop off the top rope! Johnny: Wow, that kid is quick. Mark: Baggs in the ring now and he drills the Suicide Kid with a shot to the face! The ref telling Baggs to leave the ring now. But Suicide Kid is now laying the boots to the world champion! Johnny: Classic. The Deities are talking advantage while the ref's back is turned. I love it! Mark: The Deities have Steele... Double DDT! Johnny: Baggs finally wised up and left the ring before they could do any more damage. Sometimes it's a good idea to do what you're told in there. Mark: Suicide Kid now stretching Steele a bit in an Octopus hold. Brad Baggs trying to rally the crowd behind Sean as he struggles to break the hold. Johnny: Here's where Sean's far superior strength will come in handy. He doesn't even have to do anything fancy to break this hold; watch him simply power out of it. Mark: Steele giving everything he's got and the Suicide Kid can't hold on. The Hold is broken and the Suicide Kid immediately lays the boots to the World Champion. Johnny: Yeah, Steele used up a lot of energy breaking that hold. I guess that's the drawback right there. Mark: Suicide Kid makes the tag to Wasted Youth. They hoist Steele up for what looks to be a suplex... no, it's a Double Brainbuster! Wasted Youth goes for the cover... 1........................... 2.............................. Kickout! Johnny: Steele certainly is resilient; I'll give him that much. Mark: Wasted Youth now picking him up and sending him for the ride. Steele off the ropes... Wasted Youth with a spinning heel kick... no, Steele catches him in mid air and slams him hard to the mat! Johnny: Wow, that was an impressive counter. Mark: Steele diving for the hot tag to Brad Baggs. The Total Knockout in in and he's a house of fire! Baggs nails Wasted Youth with a boot to the face! Suicide Kid comes in and Baggs nearly knocks his head off with a clothesline! Gosh, that was stiff! Xanthos up on the apron... Johnny: Here we go. Mark: Baggs sling shots him into the ring the hard way! Baggs now picking up the massive Xanthos... what on earth?! Johnny: He's putting the big man on his shoulders?! Mark: Holy Toledo! Baggs has the 500 pound Xanthos up on his shoulders! Good lord... STUDD GUNN!!! [The crowd goes wild as Baggs collapses to the mat, completely spent.] Johnny: Amazing display of strength by Baggs but at what cost? He's blew his tank on that one. Mark: Wasted Youth and Suicide Kid now double teaming Sean Steele. Double Irish whip to the ropes... Steele ducks the double clothesline! Steele puts on the breaks! Goodness gracious! DOUBLE CHOKESLAM!!! Johnny: These guys are juicers for sure. Mark: Well I know Brad Baggs is a juicer but I think Sean likes to stick with the usual protein shakes. Johnny: You're an idiot, Mark. Mark: Sean Steele now grabbing Suicide Kid and throwing him into the corner. Steele climbing the ropes... CHOKESLAM OFF THE TOP AND OUT TO THE FLOOR!!! Wasted Youth attacking Steele from behind. Steele with a boot to the gut... Death Valley Driver by Steele! Johnny: He's really kicking some ass now! Mark: Please, Johnny, this is a family show. Johnny: Since when? Mark: Here's Steele with the cover on Wasted Youth... 1....................... 2.......................... Mark: Prophet breaks it up with a chairshot to the back! Johnny: Nice aggressive move by the Prophet but now he's gotta contend with the man behind him, The Total Knockout! Mark: Prophet turns around and is begging Baggs for mercy! Baggs grabs him by the hair... OSAKA STREET CUTTER!!!! Johnny: That might be the last we see of the Prophet tonight. Mark: Baggs knocked him loopy! But look out! The Suicide Kid on the top rope... HURRICANRANA ON BRAD BAGGS!!! Johnny: NO! BAGGS CATCHES HIM! Mark: Suicide Kid is a very precarious situation... VICIOUS POWERBOMB BY BRAD BAGGS!!! Baggs with the cover... 1.................... 2........................... Mark: Xanthos picks up the ref by the back of his pants and tosses him into the corner! Johnny: The ref should have banned all these folks around ringside. This isn't supposed to be a handi-cap match. As much as I like the Deities; I think this is a cheap way to hang onto the straps. Mark: Here's Sean Steele with a spear to big Xanthos! He knocked him right out of the ring! Brad Baggs now with a clothesline to Wasted Youth sending him over the top rope... Wait a minute...ONE MAN ARMY!!! Johnny: Where did he come from? Mark: I think he came out of the crowd. Army trips Baggs and drags him out to the floor! Johnny: I never knew they had issues. Maybe this is just another random act of violence by One Man Army. It sure wouldn't be the first time. Mark: I can't keep track of all the bodies around ringside! Steele in the ring now with the Suicide Kid... EMERALD FUSION!!!! THAT WAS TOO MUCH!!! NEW CHAMPIONS; HERE'S THE COVER!!! 1!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! IT'S RODDY TAYLOR!!!!!!!!!!!!! [The crowd erupts in boo's as Taylor interrupts the count by nailing Steele in the head with one of the tag titles.] Johnny: Ahh, sweet revenge. Mark: Roddy Taylor just cost DBS the tag titles! Taylor now picking up Steele by the hair... RIPCORD!!! RIPCORD BY RODDY TAYLOR!!! Johnny: Okay let me get this straight; it's now 6 on 2, right? Mark: This is CRAP! Johnny: Hey! Family show, remember? Mark: Well gosh, I'm sorry but... Taylor now rolling the Suicide Kid on top of Steele! Here's the ref with the count! 1!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Mark: ARMY AND WASTED YOUTH ARE HOLDING BAGGS BACK ON THE FLOOR!!! 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Mark: The Deities will retain the titles with a lot of help from their friends! Johnny: I'm sorry but that match didn't prove anything. I'm all for cheating if you can get away with it but this is ridiculous. The Deities are a damn good tam and I don't think they needed all this extra help; it just makes them look bad. Mark: There has got to be another re-match, and let put them in a cage or something. Johnny: That's a hell of an idea, Mark. I'd pay to see that. Mark: Fans that's all for this week. See you next week on MAYHEM!!!!! [Show ends] http://community.webtv.net/niwfwrestling/NIWFWrestling
