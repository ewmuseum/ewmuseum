Mayhem 11.02.02 (Part Two)

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~~~~~ dEWEY ~~~~~~~ (Earlier today.) (Scene opens to backstage at the NIWF Arena. Jason Starr is talking to Eric Idol and Roddy Taylor and Dewey walks in. He's dressed in grey sweats, with a Winnipeg Jets jersey and he looks like he hasn't shaved in a week.) Dewey: Hey, Jay! Buddy.... (Starr looks in Dewey's direction and rolls his eyes and turns back to Idol and Taylor. Dewey walks up to him and puts his arm around the comissioner.) Dewey: Jay, you've done a great job here, but I think it's time that I took over. Thanks for all your help. Now, Eric, I'd like you put on this cap, backwards, and this wifebeater I have in my bag here. I've got great ideas for your character. And you, Roddy.... Starr: What the hell are you doing? Get out of here, you drunk, I've got a show to run! Dewey: HEY, you can't talk to me like that! I'm the damned owner of this company! Starr: Minority owner, and I'm the comissioner, so I'll talk to you however I want. Hey, I understand that you're looking for a job, though. Dewey: Well, I lost a pissload of money on the Bombers a couple weeks ago, so... yeah, that's why I need to take over running the show here. Starr: Well, that's not gonna happen, but nice try. Tell you what.....if you can dry yourself out, I might have a spot for you on next week's card. But, seriously, take a bath or something, and drink some coffee. Now, if you don't mind, I've got more important things to worry about. (Starr turns his back to Dewey, who glares at him, and then pulls a bottle out of his bag and takes a swig out of it as the scene fades.) Jason: What a jerk. Billy: You really let him have it, Jay. Jason: Well he deserved it. Can you believe the nerve of that guy trying to take my job? He's lucky I didn't slap on the Starrlock and knock his ass out right there. Punk! ~~~~~~~~~~ tHE dEITIES ~~~~~~~~~~ [Lights flicker then go black. An ominous chant takes over the crowd, while a procession of men in black make their way to the ring. In the front of the procession is the huge Xanthos, followed by Wasted Youth and Suicide Kid. The Prophet is a few lengths behind them, carrying the NIWF World Tag Team titles on each side shoulder] [On the jumbotron, the words: T H E C O U N C I L flash] [Xanthos holds the ropes open for The Prophet, while Youth and Kid await outside the ring, perhaps securing the ring in case an attempted strike persists.] The Prophet: Mere moments. That's what it's come down to, Sean. Soon, you and Brad will forge the path untaken ... well, nearly untaken. There have been those who've travelled these winding roads. But, don't doubt that this is a one way trip. There is no returning. At least, Not a pleasant return. I'm proud of you though, Sean. You've got guts. Youth wants to see them. [Youth stares at The Prophet with those words. Seemingly entranced.] The Prophet: He wants to see you bleed. He wants to swim inside your soul. He's hand picked you for his latest experiment. Don'tcha feel lucky, Sean? We wonder though, Sean. What's your angle? Why are you here tonight? Your already the holder of the holy grail. Why are you interested in our profit? [Prophet sets one of the Tag straps down on the mat] TP: Is it Brad? Has he gotten in your ear, and begged that you foster another dream, a dream not of your own, but of your faithful follower? Has he bought into the realities of his nonexsistance? You see, he's not visable when your not around. He's not amongst the "Elite" when your gone. He's not ... He's not anything, Sean. [Prophet lays down the other tag strap] TP: Does he need these titles to prove his worth? To prove his prowess? Is he unhappy playing your lackey? Tired of washing your jock strap? Or are you just greedy? [Prophet picks the straps back up] TP: There's ways of finding out if this is just greed, Sean. Usually, moral chaos provides us all the answers we need. If it is greed, Sean. We'll fix the problem. We'll help you through the rough times ahead. Though, deep down, I know it's not your greed that has brought us to this moment. No, it's Brad's jealousy. His jealousy has spawned an unfortunate future for you, the King of the NIWF. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ tAG tEAM tITLE mATCH tHE dEITIES vs. sTEELE & bAGGS ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The match opened up with a brawl that sent all four men out to the floor. The challengers dominated early by overpowering the much smaller champions. Xanthos and the Prophet soon interjected themselves into the brawl and Steele and Baggs found themselves outnumbered. Once the match settled into the ring, Steele and Baggs once again took control. Baggs wowed the crowd when he drilled the Suicide Kid with an awesome Osaka Street Cutter. He almost scored the win but Wasted Youth made the save after the two count. Steele came in and set Wasted Youth up for the Emerald Fusion but Xanthos entered the ring and nailed Steele from behind with a chairshot. Xanthos then proceeded to chokeslam Steele over the top rope while the Deities double teamed Baggs. The Deities, having Baggs isolated, were finally able to nail him with The End. Suicide Kid covered him for the three count and the champions retained the titles. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ wINNERS: The Deities, still Tag Team Champions ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Billy: Well once again the numbers game is played. When are you going to do something about the Prophet have his whole stable at ringside for every match? Jason: I'm not. Why should I? Steele and Baggs didn't have a problem sticking their noses in the International title match last weekend. What's good for the goose... ~~~~~~~~~~~~ jACK rUSSELL ~~~~~~~~~~~~ (The scene opens up backstage after Mayhem. You see Billy Apollo standing in front of a dented door. The camera closes in on the nameplate. It is Jack Russell's door. Billy Apollo is sweating as he stands in front of the door.) Billy: Here I am folks, Billy Apollo, standing here about to attempt to get an interview with Jack Russell. (He sounds like one of those CNN commentators in the middle of a country during a war.) Billy: As you can see by the door, it looks like we chose a bad ti.. (You hear MOTHER F*CKER! And then something crashes against the door. Billy jumps and he is visibly shaken. You see the camera trembling, indicating the cameraman getting a little flustered.) Billy: Well folks, I guess it IS a bad time to interview this monster. I'll wait. (As Billy turns to go. Jack Russell opens the door, topless. He is surprisingly chiseled and you see his white power tat's on his shoulders and chest. You see a few bruises around his rib cage area. His face is red from apparent rage. He smiles at Billy Apollo as he is turning to walk away. Billy obviously heard the door open and you hear him mouth, "Oh shit" as he turns around slowly. Jack Russell is continuing to smile.) Jack: Where ya going? (Billy turns around. He is trembling and sweating.) Billy: I was attempting to get an interview with you, but it seemed to me that you were not ready to be questioned. Jack: Naaaa, it's alright. I am just a little frustrated at my own mistakes. No big deal. I'm not going to take it out on you Billy. That would be wrong. Billy (looking surprised): Ok, well, let's get started then. Jack: Oh, please do. (Jack motions for them to follow him to his locker room and sit down at the table.) Jack: Have a seat and I'll be right back. (Jack walks out of camera view and comes back with his braces down and a shirt on. It is black and has a 'SS' skull and crossbones with the words "Blut und Ehre" at the bottom of the skull. He sits down across from Billy.) Billy: Jack, you are considered a monster in this federation for the way you view the world and the way you pulverize your opponents. What do you have to say about this? Jack: Well, Billy. The ones who are right are usually criticized by the masses. The sheeple, so to speak. You have to have some balls to come out and say what you feel is right every day. As far as me pulverizing my opponents, it is my nature. I do not take it easy on ANYONE. Man, or woman. That is not how I was taught and learned to fight. I learned to fight until stopped by authorities or stopped by unconsciousness or more. You see, Billy, I wasn't always the way I am. I used to take a lot of crap when I was a boy. I was scared not of the individuals, but of what I would do. But, one day I snapped. Before I tell you what happened, I will tell you what I was. I was a quiet kid, always made fun of because I was large. I took it. Then, one day, I was walking home from school. And our town was mostly mongrels. Anyway, I was walking home and about 4 of them, older boys started to harass me. They were from my school and they did not like the fact that I was pointing out them cheating on their exams, I was only in 3rd grade, I didn't know any better, my folks tried to raise me right. Anyway, they begun to harass me and finally I stopped and blacked out. Next thing I knew, I was standing there and they were all around me, on the ground, completely unrecognizable. I ran home and told my dad. He said to me. "Jack, son, you were only defending yourself. You did nothing wrong." I went to my room and thought about it. And the more I thought about it, the more I enjoyed the way I utterly destroyed those kids. And that began the saga of Jack Russell. I LOVE to fight. And I have been fighting my whole life since then. Billy: Jack, a lot of folks think you are sick, maybe this will shine some light on you. Now, onto your match against Eric Idol tonight. Why did you stop the pin, you clearly had the match won. (Jack snarls at this and Billy jumps. Jack, seeing Billy jump loses his snarl.) Jack: Don't worry old man; I am not going to lay a finger on you. You see, the reason I let up on the pin, was that I let my emotions get the best of me. As I heard the count, I started seeing him taking my brass knuckles. I wanted to kill him, but I made a rookie mistake. It will not happen again. (Jack looks at the camera.) Jack: ERIC, LISTEN TO ME!!! You see, I let you win. Not next week. I won't make the same mistake twice. You ass is mine and don't you worry, I have some for Roddy Taylor and Andrew Starkey as well. Billy: Speaking of that. Are you ready to possibly fight for the World Title? Jack: OH, I AM MORE THAN READY! You see, I know they will all be gunning for me next week. I know it. They will end up just like those 4 little mongrel boys that tried to jump me. They will be destroyed. I AM THE DESTRUCTOR!! I AM JACK RUSSELL!! Billy: Say you win next week's match, what do you think about Sean Steele and your chances? Jack (chuckling): Sean Steele. I would like to break his face in half! He is nothing to me and I will show it. I will destroy him with the rest and step atop this federation as the MASTER OF THEIR REALITY! Now, Billy, if you would leave, I must go. Billy: Sure, folks I am Billy Apollo, signing off from Jack Russell's locker room. (You hear him whisper as Jack walks away.) Billy: Alive, thank God. [Fade] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 4 wAY #1 cONTENDERS mATCH iDOL vs. sTARKEY vs. tAYLOR vs. rUSSELL ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The match opened up with all four men going at it. Taylor and Starkey paired off and went at it in the corner as Idol and Russell spilled out to the floor. Russell tried to pummell Idol with his brass knucks but Idol managed to avoid taking any shots to the head. Russell ended up hurting his hand when he tried to hit Idol and got the ringpost instead. Starkey and Taylor traded blows for a while with Taylor finally taking the advantage with a low blow. Idol made his way back into the ring and Taylor grabbed him in a heartbeat with the Ripcord! Roddy went for the cover but only got two when Russell hit him over the head with the ring steps. Starkey came back and nailed the steps into Russell's face with the Incredible. Starkey went for the cover but only got a two count. Taylor and Starkey started to work together briefly against Russell. They shot him off the ropes and sent him flying out to the floor with a double back body drop. Idol came back and nailed Starkey out of the ring with a clothesline before hitting Taylor with the JLT. Idol went for the cover but Taylor kicked out after two. Starkey tried to pick up Russell on the floor but he got a brass knuckle shot to the face. Russell slammed Starkey's head repetedly into the concrete floor until he was unconcious. Back in the ring, Taylor and Idol were trading weak lefts and right when Russell slid back into the mix. Taylor got a brass knucks shot to the back of the skull knocking him down. Idol charged Russell but the Nazi took him down with a spinebuster and then climbed on him and nailed him repeatedly with the knucks. Idol was quickly a bloody mess and he tapped out. The ref called for the bell and awarded the match to Jack Russell. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ wINNER: Jack Russell, #1 Contender ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
