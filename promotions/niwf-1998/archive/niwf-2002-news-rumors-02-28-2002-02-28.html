News & Rumors

28 February 2002

Hey all, got a lot of pieces of news and rumors to share with all of you as well as a "modified" schedule from last night which includes those I left out and some fixed screw ups for the next card (having a certain wrestler twice on the card). Here it goes: -Talks have been swirling about the Masked Man who made his appearance at the NIWF Show last card. There is a big rumor going around that the masked man is Axel Schmidt, hence the Axel Schmidt sign at the end of the show. However, this is only a rumor. For all we know, the masked man is just trying to make a name for himself in the name of Shootfire Pro wrestling. -The previous show was a big controversy, that is understood. There are some that felt that the envelope was pushed to the limits and more and didn't like seeing that. However, there are also others that felt that this is exactly what a fed needed to do in this sport, push the limits. It's a win/lose situation right now. -Dirty Dan is showing great concern over the condition of CK. He feels that CK may not be able to handle the NIWF both mentally and physically after what happened to him over the weekend. Dirty Dan plans on visting CK in the hospital over the weekend to speak with him to determine just how stable CK is. -Since there was no TV Title Winner determined this past weekend, Dirty Dan has moved the TV Title match to the Free Per View called "Dangerous". This means that every single title will be determined at the NIWF Free Per View. The participants for the TV Title are not determined yet, but Dirty Dan will name them at a later date. Rumor has it that if CK is stable enough, he will be one of the first people offered a shot at the title. -BIG NEWS... The NIWF has signed, sealed and will deliver a now former SPW Superstar! The man will debut this weekend on Vocal Explosions as well! He has already been scheduled for a match in the NIWF! -Rumor has it that NIWF Superstar, Shot, has been in contact with Shootfire Pro and is supporting their group strongly in the back. If Dirty Dan and NIWF officials find any truth to this rumor, Shot will be strongly punished in the NIWF. -What is the relationship between Sean Steele & Desmond Walker? Was there a reason why Desmond Walker saved Sean Steele at the last card? Are they aligning themselves or is it part of a sneaky plan by Desmond Walker? -Damage Inc. was not to happy with what happened to one of their team members this past week at the show. They felt that because of this, security needs to beefed up in the back. They have sent some nasty letters to Dirty Dan telling him that things are getting outrageous in the NIWF and they feel there is no place for stuff like this in the sport. Dirty Dan will take the necessary precautions to assure them that no outside wrestlers come into the arena without passing through security first. It was believed that a wrestler in the past, such as Golden Boy Brians, was the one who did the damage on Damage Inc. -There are questions regarding the status of Kitti in the fed. From what is known, Kitti has signed a one time deal with the NIWF for a wrestling match and after that match she plans on doing interviews backstage. However, there might be other plans for Kitti to wrestle one or two more matches before this takes in effect. -There is another rumor swirling in the back that yet another challenge will be made to SPW on the next card. However, this challenge may pit an SPW wrestler vs. an NIWF wrestler and may even occur on the NIWF's Free Per View! -As you all know, this is the day 5 more wrestlers get added to the Royal Rumble. There are now 15 wrestlers total, they are the following: 1.Dirty Dan 2.The Masked Man 3."The Shocker" Brian Anthony 4.Thomas P. Harding "The Angelic Assasin" 5.The Wasp 6.Anrew "The Icon" Starkey 7.Dennis Windham 8.Desmond Walker 9.Shot 10.CK 11."Saint" Ian Walsh 12."Blues Xplosion" Luke Denton 13.Eric Idol 14.T-Bone 15."Dashing" Derk Lankford -Finally, either the 6th, 7th & 8th or the 13th, 14th & 15th... Dirty Dan will be away on a business trip. This means that either a card or Vocal Explosions may come out a day later. However, Dirty Dan will do his best to get the stuff out on time. On a Wed. night Dirty Dan will be traveling to Cincinnati, OH. From there, on Thursday Afternoon he will travel to Chicago, Ill... then Thursday evening he will go to Nashville, TN and stay over night until Friday afternoon. If anyone wishes to meet with Dirty Dan in these areas, please get in touch with him!
