News & Update 01/30/2002

30 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I am here with another update for you folks in the middle of the most hellish work week I have ever had! But it's been fun, but busy. So here we are, getting ready for the NIWF to get it's feet off the ground. But before we do that, just some notes on my part: -The website has not been worked on, I plan on doing that this weekend. When it's done, you will know. There will be no roleplay board so just send the roleplays right to me. Some of you have asked me about strategy forms. Hmmm, that's the first time I was ever asked of that. Basically, you send me what your wrestler wants to do and I'll do my best to try and put it in the show depending on what your opponent has. There is no strat form. -I have been very happy and pleased with the ones who roleplayed and send starts this week, it seems like you all want a part of this thing and as long as you do, so do I! Remember, it's not 100% necessary to send a strat (I have been seeing a lot of the same strat from many of you) but it is necessary to roleplay. -Speaking of roleplaying, there are some new characters that joined the NIWF! Welcome!! Unfortunately, I won't be able to fit you in this first upcoming card, however, if you wish to stay active in the fed and want to keep things interesting, it doesn't hurt to send a flash for the roleplay show and it never hurts to do an in-ring or an unprovoked attack or anything of that matter. For those that aren't in the first card, I promise you that you will be in the next one! (Please, don't be turned off by having to wait 3 weeks for a match, just be patient) -Now, about the card. Like I said, this was the week from hell from me. I'm going to go to that roleplay one week, card the next week format. It's a 100% definite. What does this mean? The card that was scheduled for this Sunday will be rescheduled for the next Sunday. Don't expect this to happen a lot in the future either. I'm doing this for a few reasons. One reason being that I still haven't send out the matches to the writers only because at the beginning of this week, I only had 2 roleplays and it would of been hard to determine a winner in many matches based on those roleplays. Therefore, after Vocal Explosions comes out this Saturday, I'll send out match writing assignments right after that show. This way, the match writers have one week to work on their matches as opposed to 3 days. This will be the format from now on. Also, another reason is because I'M GOING TO ATLANTIC CITY BABY!! :) So, if I come back a millionaire then expect the NIWF to go even more full steam ahead!! That's a big *if*! -Finally, we are still looking for 3 good tag teams and probably 3 more good female wrestlers. Male singles wrestlers are always welcome. If you are on this list and have expressed interest and still want to join, FILL OUT AN APP! If not, I'll leave you on this list until you tell me you want off. If you know of people who might be interested then please, by all means, tell them to join. The more, the better!
