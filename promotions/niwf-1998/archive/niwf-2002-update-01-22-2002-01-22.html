Update 01/22/2002

22 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, this is going out to those who have joined our fed or expressed interest in joining the fed. If you want to join our fed and have expressed interest in joining the fed, please fill out the app. If you haven't done so then I will include the app at the bottom of this message. I must say, I am pleased with the amount who are interested but of course things are not rolling as of yet. The minute I can get 14 singles and two tag teams, I can get this thing rolling (or any combination of what I may find suitable.) I was hoping by this weekend I can start things but it seems there will be more time needed until we get the necessary wrestlers to make a good, solid card. Also, I appreciate all of your compliments on the sample cards I provided for all of you. I have debated that I may go with a card every week instead of every two weeks, but that decision has not been made yet. So far, I stay with a card every two weeks like I planned. I'll let you know if any changes are made. Also, let me let you guys in on a little secret. EVERYONE is going to be put in the World Title picture from the beginning! You'll understand when I get things rolling with the first card.
