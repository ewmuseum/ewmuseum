NIWF Schedule of Events for January

7 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, long time no post or talk to anyone here. Not even sure if some of the same people who were once involved in e-wrestling are still even lurking around. As you have seen the past few days, I want to get my last e-wrestling fix and have sort of a reununion show with the former NIWF Wrestlers who once participated in the fed. I obviously have a card set up, though I would like to see some more familiar faces try to get involved in this. If you want to get involved, then just e-mail me at [detail withheld] and tell me what you want to do or if you want a match or whatever. I'd like to hear from some of you guys, it's been a while! Here is a schedule of events I have planned for January leading up to the Free Per View. ----------------------- "Best of NIWF" Show #1 January 8th, 2005 ----------------------- A show to focus on some of the main aspects which made the NIWF succesful. This show will show some replays of some of the more classic matches from the NIWF as well as focus on the history of the TV Title, North American Title, Women's Title, International Title and Tag Team Titles. ------------------------ "Best of NIWF" Show #2 January 15th, 2005 ------------------------ The next Best of Show for the NIWF that will focus on mainly the Diamond Title in the NIWF and the history of what kind of controversary was caused behind the Diamond Title. The show will also focus on some of the craziest matches the NIWF has put together as well as show highlights of some of the best matches the current participants of the "One Last Time" Free Per View have been in. ----------------------- "Best of NIWF" Show #3 January 22nd, 2005 ----------------------- This show will concentrate on the NIWF World Title and the history behind it. It will include some matches of how the NIWF was won in the past as well as some great matches from some of the greatest NIWF World Champs of all time, ranging from Sean Steele to Todd Lucchessi. The show will also focus on some of the main angles that have taken place in the NIWF, from the battle between NIWF and SOW to the takeover of Shootfire Pro Wrestling. These angles are what made the NIWF the most controversial federation on the internet in the years it was up and running. ------------------------------------- Vocal Explosions Hosted by Billy Apollo & Doug Hardy January 29th, 2005 ------------------------------------- This show is basically a roleplay show, a show where the wrestlers get to hype up their upcoming matches for the "One Last Time" show. You will see comments from all those involved and maybe some from those who are not involved but would like to be involved. All roleplays for this show are due no later than 11:59PM Eastern Standard Time on January 28th, 2005 ------------------------------------------- NIWF Presents "One Last Time" LIVE from the NIWF Arena in Newark, NJ February 6th, 2005 *Card Subject to Change ------------------------------------------- PURIST'S DREAM MATCH Desmond Walker vs. Jason Starr In a battle of Old vs. New, the NIWF will put on a showcase of wrestling to start off the show. For those that don't know, the Purist's Dream Match involves actually having rules such as countouts, DQ's and whatever else can be named. Though Jason Starr has been out of wrestling for years, NIWF has kindly asked him to come back and asked Desmond Walker to participate as well in this wrestling spectacular. Cruiserweight Spectacular - BEST OF 5 SERIES Mr. Bungle vs. 'Asshole' Andy Simmons In what is sure to be another classic battle, we will see two of the funniest and greatest cruiserweights of all time take each other on in a Best of 5 Series. Basically, the match will last until one of the wrestlers wins 3 times either by pinfall or submission. BATTLE OF BEST NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPS Smilin' Jack Ruby vs. Chaz Darby Two of the greatest US Champs of all time will be duking it out on this one on one battle with no special stipulations. The only thing that is on the line for this match are bragging rights and pride. Who will come out victorious in this match and what surprises lie ahead for both men? 14LB HAM MATCH Doug Hardy vs. "Wild Animal" Sarah Mayson (or whoever Neal choses to wrestle him) Doug Hardy yapped about his 14lb Ham for almost 10 years. Now, a woman finally will get the chance to expose the 14lb Ham. If Doug Hardy were to lose, and that is a likely case, he will finally have to reveal his infamous 14lb Ham in front of the whole NIWF Crowd. If Doug Hardy were to win, then his opponent will have to have a one night stand with him. TABLES, LADDERS AND CHAIRS... OH MY! The Greatest Team Ever vs, 2 Exciting Dudes 2 Exciting Dudes, who have Mr. Excitement Dropkick and Larry Gates, were one of the first original tag teams in the NIWF and were a dominate force when the NIWF first opened before Mr. Excitement Dropkick went on his World Title Run. The Greatest Team Ever consists of Lawrence Buffy & "Strangler" Shawn Osborne, both members played an important part in the NIWF vs. BWF battle and as a team, they were really one of the best teams arounds. Will the Old Timers win this match or will The Greatest Team Ever preveal? ROULETTE OF DEATH MATCH - Match Is Chosen at Random by Roulette Wheel spun by Special Guest The Master of Mischief, Poe vs. Rockin' Roddy Taylor Both men want to face Vile Vince Viper and put his career to an end one way or another. Since Vile Vince Viper never responded to Dirty Dan's Invitational for this One Last Time Show. Since this is the case and since neither man has an opponent, both men will be forced to face each other in a match that will be as random as random will come with many options such as a Barbed Wire Match, Steel Cage Match, Ring of Fire Match, Purist's Dream Match, Last Man Breathing Match, etc. The options are endless and none the less, they are also dangerous. The question remains though, who will be spinning the wheel and what kind of impact will it have on the match? 10 Man Lunatic Fringe Match for BWF World Title (Participants Will More than Likely Change) Jimmy Cougar vs. Alex Cougar vs. Adam Kuhl vs. "Superstar" Steve Destiny vs. Davey Rumble vs. Christian Hawk vs. T-Bone vs. THE PACK vs. Dennis Windham (?) In a typical BWF style Match, 10 Men who once participated in both the BWF and NIWF will square off in an elimination style match for the BWF World Title. The rules are simple, there are no rules and it's every man for himself. Pinfalls and submissions can happen anywhere inside or outside the arena. The last man who is left standing with his hand raised at the end of the match, is the man who is victorious and the new BWF World Champ. MAIN EVENT - Triple Threat Elimination Match for NIWF World Title w/ Dirty Dan as Special Guest Referee "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice vs. Axel Schmidt vs. Eric Idol (Last NIWF Champ) In the main event, it will Dirty Dan who will decide the fate of the Final or Next NIWF World Champion. Will he chose the man who he has represented throughout his e-wrestling career and right a wrong in helping Dice win the World Title that he never lost? Or will it be the man who caused all the trouble for Shootfire Pro Wrestling and the man who led a new wave for the NIWF and Dirty Dan's fed by getting many wrestlers to commit to the fed and then complaining that he should be World Champ? Then again, Dirty Dan could do the right thing and keep the belt around the wrestler who was the last one to ever win the NIWF World Title in Eric Idol. It's all a matter of whether Dirty Dan chooses to be fair in the match. Plus, rumor has it of a special announcement by Dirty Dan and a Special Bonus or Dark Match in the Makings!
