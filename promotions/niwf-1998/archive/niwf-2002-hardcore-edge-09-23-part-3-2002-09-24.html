Hardcore Edge 9/23/02 (Part Three)

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=============================== Brad "Buff" Baggs vs. Jamie Courage Match Writer: Sean =============================== Billy: Here we are. All set for Brad Baggs and Jamie Courage! ["FLY ME COURAGEOUS!" flashes on the jumbo-tron as "Unbelievable" by Thousand Foot Krutch blares. Red, white, and blue pyro erupt around the entrance as Jamie Courage emerges. The crowd lets out a big face pop as Courage jogs down the aisle and high fives a few fans around ringside. Courage climbs up the outside of the turnbuckles to the top rope and moonsaults into the ring.] Announcer: Introducing, from Los Angeles, California. Standing 6'5" and weighing in at 265 lbs. The Grinning Poster Boy for Hardcore Airlines... JAMIE COURAGE!!! Billy: Jamie Courage headed down to the squared circle to take on a man that is on a streak. Johnny: Yeah beating the champ streak! Billy: That was a great match, one that will go down in the books.      ["The Intro" by Limp Bizkit blares as Brad "Buff" Baggs makes his way to the ring with Stacey. Buff wears a pair of blue Oakleys, a green t-shirt with a black bomb on the front and above it it says "and you thought we were through" on the back it just says in blue letters, "Brad "Buff" Baggs", and a pair of full legged blue tights. ] Announcer: Introducing from St. Louis, MO. Standing 6'8" and weighing in at 287 lbs... BRAD "BUFF" BAGGS!!! Billy: Man, we could be looking at our next champion here. Johnny: We certainly could but let's not get ahead of ourselves. Baggs wont be champion of squat if Buffy wins the belt later tonight. Billy: You can't count out anyone in this buisness. Johnny: That's for sure. Billy: Baggs into the ring and he is wasting NO TIME on Jamie Courage, HARD KNIFE EDGE CHOPS! Johnny: The sound from those chops echos through the arena as Brad Baggs irish whips Courage, OUCH, that looked like it almost took his head off. Billy: Viscious clothesling followed by a led drop by Baggs. Baggs hauling Jamie up, irish whip, reversal by Jamie. Johnny: CLOTHESLINE TO THE MAT. Jamie just evened the odds out here. Baggs holding his throat. Billy: Jamie Courage with the advantage now. Setting up Baggs for a nice belly to back suplex. Baggs holding his back. Johnny: Yeah Baggs probably isn't 100 percent seeing as he just had the match of his life with the president. Billy: Neither is the champ, as Jamie Courage chokes Baggs. Johnny: Yeah, that is where I can see Lawerence Buffy as having a SLIGHT advantage against Steele. Billy: Irish whip by Courage. NICE CROSS BODY FROM BUFF! Johnny: Yeah, Jamie got his clocked cleaned there. Buff off the ropes. NICE LION SAULT on the fallen Jamie Courage. Jamie writhing around in pain. Billy: The crowd is sure stirring up. Let's get a camera man out there to see what is going on. Johnny: Baggs sets up Courage for the Cover. 1........................ 2....................... Billy: Courageous kickout by Jamie. Buff sets up Courage for a suplex. Slammed hard into the mat. Johnny: Buff prancing around, doing his BUFF STRUT. Laughing at the fallen Jamie Courage. The crowd is still stirring and we don't know why. Billy: Buff attempts to lock Jamie in a figure four, but his boasting made him eat a boot to the mouth. Johnny: Nothing like a fresh boot in the morning to knock your senses out! Billy: Courage with an Irish Whip into the corner, Baggs goes hard, slumping down as Jamie left his feet. Johnny: That doesn't feel too good on the back.      Billy: Courage setting Buff up on the top ropes for what looks to be a super plex. It might be a little too early for that. Johnny: He's got Baggs hooked...WHAT THE HECK?!      Billy: TREMENDOUS DISPLAY OF AGILITY AND POWER BY BAGGS, HE IS UP ON THE TOP ROPE WITH JAMIE COURAGE ON HIS SHOULDERS, HE SMILES AT THE CAMERA. Johnny: This is going to hurt......... Billy: OH MY GOD! WHAT A SEXY DRIVER BY BRAD! HE IS SURE TAUNTING NOW, I DON'T THINK JAMIE COURAGE IS GOING TO GET UP. Johnny: Yeah, It doesn't look like that to me either. Especially since Buff spit in his eye and he didn't do anything. Billy: Buff picking up Jamie Courage and putting him up on his shoulders thecrowd starting to boo and stir close to the ring, still no camera out there folks. Johnny: Looks like an electric chair drop... OUCH! Billy: HE JUST RIPPED OUT JAMIE COURAGE'S THROAT WITH THE STUD GUNN....Courage rolling on the ground, clutching his throat. Johnny: The crowd is yelling to pin him but Buff just flips them off and stomps on Jamie to stop his rolling around. Billy: THIS IS CARNAGE FOLKS! Buff setting Jamie Courage up for the Buff Bomb, HE IS BROKEN ALREADY BUFF, STOP IT AND PIN THE MAN! Johnny: This is not going to feel too good in the morning! Billy: UP....AND DOWN!! COURAGE'S BROKEN BODY TO THE MAT. BUFF LAYS HIM FLAT ON HIS BACK, JAMIE IS OUT COLD. YOU COCKY BASTARD! Johnny: Buff flexing above a fallen Jamie Courage! 1.... 2..... 3..... Billy: This is sick! ABSOLUTLEY SICK! THAT WAS TOTALLY UNCALLED FOR. Johnny: But entertaining nonetheless! Billy: Brad is on the corner flexing and taunting this booing capacity crowd. Johnny: I THINK I KNOW WHY THE CROWD IS RILED UP! Billy: Wh... IT'S SEAN STEELE. BRAD DOESNT SEE HIM. Johnny: This is going to be nice. Sean slides in the ring and stands behind Baggs. Billy: Oh my god business is about to pick up. Sean Steele is standing right behind Brad Baggs who is still celebrating at that corner. Johnny: Brad is now wondering why everyone is cheering for him. But they aren't cheering for him, THEY'RE CHEERING FOR THE CHAMP!!      Billy: Brad's coming down, and OH MAN, FACE TO FACE. Brad looks like he's seen a ghost!!      Johnny: Brad looks like he's about to beat some ass is what it looks like. Those two are eyeing each other with fearsome looks. Damn this is going to get good.      Billy: WAIT!! WHAT THE.... Johnny: WHAT THE HELL?! [Sean Steele suddenly turns around and Nails Jamie Courage. He starts to pound on the already beaten man. Finally Sean picks him up and hits The Emerald Fusion.] Billy: SEAN JUST HIT JAMIE COURAGE WITH TOO MUCH. JAMIE IS LIFELESS NOW.      Johnny: WHY IS SEAN STEELE SIDING WITH BRAD?      Billy: I have no idea, maybe to psych Brad out? I don't know but Sean has just leveled Courage and the fans here are in shock. They don't know whether to boo or cheer their champ Johnny: Well Brad is standing in the corner and...well he's smiling at Sean. What? Billy: Sean is smiling back. You think maybe these two are on the same page?      Johnny: Highly doubtful with all the hatred they both have for one another. I just think Sean is making sure that Brad is 100% and to show Brad that he isn't the only one that can dominate someone      Billy: He could also be sending a message to Lawerence Buffy as well. And Brad is smiling probably to show Sean that he isn't scared of him. I think this was all just mind games. Maybe we went a little too far in thinking they are incahoots. Johnny: I dont think they are in cahoots, just sending messages. Anyway, Steele has exited the ring and our own Mark Hunter has caught up with him. Mark: Sean Steele, why did you nail a fallen Jamie Courage? Sean: Well, that is not on the subject of what Sean Steele wants to talk about, what Sean Steele wants to talk about is little Lawerence Buffy. (Sean sniffs and wipes the sweat from his forehead) Sean: In just a few minutes, Sean Steele will be going face to face with the man that thinks he is the man. (Sean sniffs his discontent at that comment) Sean: Well, Lawerence let me tell you something, in just a few short minutes you will find out that you are not close to the man, you will never be the man, and what you did in the past, is exactly that, THE F***ING PAST DUDE! You see, what you don't understand, midget, is that you are overweighed, and over matched. It says in your scouting report that you are a technical wrestler, the only thin technical that Sean Steele is going to see out of you is you laying on your back and getting the 1....2......3. (Sean snatches the mic) Sean: WELCOME TO THE SEAN STEELE SHOW! YOUR ON IN A COUPLE OF MINUTES. (Sean Steele walks to the back, leaving Mark Hunter looking befuddled.) Billy: Strong words. Johnny: He'll back them up. [Commercial] (A diminutive, fatherly Japanese gentlemen in a black suit and derby hat walks through the entrance way with the aid of a diamond topped cane to the tinkling bells of a very Asian theme. He requests a microphone.) Maxim: Ladies and gentlemen, I bring you warm greetings from the Land of the Rising Sun! For those of you who do not know me, my name is Maxim. And I have come to give you an invitation...to tune in to Vocal Explosions next week...for a surprise... (The Jumbotron flickers as the image coalesces to a home in Japan overlaid with rich silks. Seated comfortably in a matching black suit with dark glasses is a familiar face. And sitting next to him a Jade-green mask...) Jade: Greetings NIWF from the Son of the Sapphire, the spirit of the Green Dragon, the Kamikaze Bulldog...or as you know him...simply Jade. The next time you see me, I will be standing in the NIWF ring. My agent, Mr. Maxim, has gracefully arranged for me to defend the coveted but sadly inactive LSWA Light Heavyweight Championship against the very best cruiserweight talent in the world today, the NIWF elite. I look forward to seeing you all soon. Suka suka. (Jade bows his head in salute as the camera fades) Maxim: That is correct, friends, Jade has signed to face all comers for his title and to merge that title with the NIWF Cruiserweight Championship!! In perhaps one of the most anticipated bouts in recent wrestling history, Jade will meet the Goldenboy in a non-televised tournament in his old LSWA haunt, the Mud Creek National Guard Armory this Monday Night in an independent show promoted by myself, and the private funds of Robert Ladd. The winner of that match will be crowned the NIWF Cruiserweight Champion! If you are in the El Paso area, I invite you to take part in this special event! Until next time America...SUKA SUKA!!! (Princes of the Universe blares as Maxim-san exits) Johnny: I can't wait to see Jade in action again. Billy: Jade will be a great addition to the new Cruiserweight division. These smaller athletes can put on some of the most exciting matches you'll ever see. To be continued....
