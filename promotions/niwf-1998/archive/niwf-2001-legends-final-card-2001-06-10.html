LEGENDS Final Update

10 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok guys, here it is. The final card for NIWF LEGENDS. If you wish to roleplay, send them to me ASAP so I can get this thing rolling. If you have strats, I will definitely need them. Same goes for any in-rings. I think a lot of you will be satisfied with how this will come out. For those are not participating and asked to be on the list, if you still want to do something or know someone who wants to do something, let me know what it is!! If you are reading this on RSPWF and want to get involved then e-mail me! Also, for those who haven't voted on who you want to win the Rumble, please do so NOW. The list of Rumble participants is towards the bottom but remember, you can not choose your own character to win.
