NIWF — Recruitment

Attention All "Free Agents"

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Or for that matter, anyone who is interested in joining a fun wrestling federation. The NIWF is currently in some sort of restructuring process and will be looking for some good roleplayers and good handlers to join the NIWF. We are currently accepting singles wrestlers, as well as the much needed women and tag teams. The NIWF offers a great opportunity for those who want to have fun and offers great competition as well. We have many titles to be won in the NIWF. Titles such as the following: NIWF WORLD Title NIWF North American Title NIWF Diamond Title NIWF Tag Team Titles NIWF Purist's Dream Title NIWF Women's Title NIWF International Title NIWF TV Title Each Title has it's own meaning as well, you will find out when you join just what they are. I know it seems like a lot but with the competition in the NIWF, titles like these keep everyone happy. Currently, the NIWF will be weeding out all the wrestlers who are not giving their effort in the NIWF and it is expected that up to as many as 10 wrestlers will be let go by the NIWF within the next two weeks. This means a window of opportunity is opening for anyone! All you have to do is send a sample roleplay to [detail withheld] and he we like what we see, then we will send you an app to fill out and you will be part of our staff. The NIWF is also hiring match writers as well, so those interested in writing matches may get in touch with [detail withheld] and send a sample of your match writings as well. In case you are all wondering just how things are run, here is a quick breakdown. Cards occur every two weeks. Roleplay Show occurs in the weeks between the cards. Every Thursday and Rumor report is sent out. About once or twice a week, there is a quick update with the schedule and rankings. If you want to see a sample of how our cards are written, then check out the following site for some good samples: http://niwfwrestling.tripod.com/Cards.html Hurry up though! I'm sure the slots will be filling up fast!
