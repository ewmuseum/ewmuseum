News & Rumors

9 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, time for some news and rumors: -It is rumored that at least 3 members of SPW are not happy there and want to jump to the NIWF. As a matter of fact, one already has! The other one is a big name in SPW as well and of course another one feels he gets no recognition. I won't name names, but the NIWF will have no problems welcoming these wrestlers with open arms. While I do encourage them to continue with SPW, I can understand their concerns and why they want to be here instead. -The main even for Sunday is still a Hanicapped match, however the rumor has it that there will be a little twist in this match. Instead of it being 2 on 1, we hear that it may turn out to be a 3-2 handicapped match! It should provide for some excellent entertainement. -Two former female NIWF stars were seen walking out of Dirty Dan's office. For what reason, whether business or pleasure, we do not know. However the two wrestlers were none other than Kitti & Elyse Lightfoot. -The chat this past week was great! We had some NIWF guys and SPW guys join us. Among those joining us were the handlers of Ian Walsh, Damage Inc., Shot, Roddy Taylor & Ian Lightfoot. The highlight of the chat was the discussion of SPW vs. NIWF and the real life problems between the handlers of Ian Walsh & Damage Inc. Also, out of character discussion about where all the handlers currently lived and about the possibility of marriage with one of the characters! Great stuff! -Congrats goes out to the handler of The Wasp, who is expecting to have a baby with his wife! -Dirty Dan is pleased with the amount of matches that have come out to him on time. Only two more matches are needed, one is to be written by Dirty Dan himself! It's great to see such involvement with the NIWF by the match writers and the handlers. -If you read RSPWF, you have seen some trouble started on there by the NIWF.. well, not really by us. I approached the owner of RSPWF.com about some things but it was all straightened out. Now there seems to be a bit of problem with someone on there who wants to claim they are from the NIWF, trying to make the fed look bad. It is suspected that it is someone from another fed *cough* SPW *cough* that is trying to make us look bad. -As you all saw on the roleplay show, there was a masked man doing a roleplay about SPW and NIWF. At this time, I still do not know who that person is. It comes from an e-mail address that is not recognized to me and is nobody from SPW or NIWF. However, this creates a good angle and once that person reveals themselves, I am sure that person will get some major heat. -We are still currently looking for a couple more tag teams ad female wrestlers to fill out the roster (though it's always open) so if you know anyone who is interested still, send them my way! That's all! Not much else to report!
