News & Rumors

6 June 2002

Here we go, another news and rumors section! -Word has it around the NIWF offices that Dirty Dan has been in contact with and may have even signed a former NIWF Purist's Dream Champ. Only a few in the back know who this former champ is and he can be making his appearance as early as next card. -Speaking of possible returning wrestlers, we are hearing that Dirty Dan is going to go on a major manhunt this coming week and try to get many of the former NIWF stars to return. He feels that with the schedule being only once every other week, he can entice some of these stars to show up on his show. He is basically looking for some wrestlers to come in and replace those wrestlers who left or are leaving and he is also looking for some good tag team names to come in as well as a few female wrestlers. -Apparently this past weeked, Dirty Dan called Kitti back into his office. The reason for this meeting was to try and convince Kitti to come back into the ring and wrestle in the women's division again. Kitti, however, wanted to have nothing to do with it. We heard that Dirty Dan even tried getting Kitti's ex-bf and current wrestler, Dylan Dice, involved in the whole process. -Apparently Daria Wakizawa has dropped out of the NIWF. When asked why she has quit, it was mentioned that she felt the NIWF was not the place for her and felt that the character could not grow in the NIWF. It's unfortunate she left on such an abrupt notice but Dirty Dan appreciated the honesty. -Rumor in the back is that a few of the wrestlers are not happy with Derrik Weaver. With what happened this past weekend in his turn and alliance with Irons, I am sure there are more that are not happy at all as well. It has gone so far that a few wrestlers actually either want Dirty Dan to fire Weaver and make him leave the NIWF or force Derrik Weaver to quit the fed. We can assure you that Weaver is not going to go anywhere unless done so on his own free will. -Dirty Dan has no decided just where the next Free Per View will be or what it will be called. The toss up is between the names "Civil War" and have it take place in Raleigh, North Carolina or out of old BWF tradition, have another "Seven Deadly Sins" and have it occur somewhere in Europe. We will find out more details when they will be made. -CK has been pushed to the edge. After his loss to Axel Schmidt, CK burst into Dirty Dan's presidential office and totally trashed it. The damages have not been estimated yet. Even though it seems like CK is out of the fed, I have a feeling we haven't see the last of him just yet. -Rumors have it that "Handsome" Henry will have some sort of announcement next week when he defends his TV Title. We haven't heard anything official about this announcement but it is speculated that "Handsome" Henry has some sort of injury that may not allow him to wrestle. We shall see. -As we all saw, there was some sort of conversation going on between Doug Hardy and "Wild Animal" Sarah Mayson last week. We are not sure of the details of the conversation yet as the audio did not appear on the tape. The only 3 people who are well aware of this conversation are Doug, Sarah and the cameraman which apparently can not be found now. If you ask Doug, he will say he has a date with Sarah.. but we all know how true the things are that come out of Doug's mouth, right? -Who is this Brad Zans character that we keep on hearing about from Andrew "The Icon" Starkey and why did he beat up Roddy Taylor over him? Apparently Brad Zans is the former leader of the stable that Starkey used to be a part of and it looks like Zans may soon make his way into the NIWF full time. We are still not sure why Starkey took it out on Taylor but we do know that Roddy Taylor is going to be looking for revenge sometime soon. -As you all saw, many people got involved in Eric Henry's match last week. This has a lot to do with the fact that Eric Henry was a former Diamond Champ and everyone knows exactly what type of challenge he can be. Dirty Dan has pondered an idea which was brought up to him and that was a Chamber of Horrors match. We are not sure when the idea will occur, but rumor has it that it will occur right before the Free Per View. The stakes? Well, it looks as though the winner may receive a shot at whoever the Diamond Champ is at the time at the next Free Per View. There you have it!
