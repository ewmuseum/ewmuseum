Mark: Speaking of crazy people, here's the Prophet. =NIWF======= THE DEITIES ============ [ The camera shot pans into a field, far in the distance, a group of men, three to be exact stand in front of a cross built from tree. We are self serving. Unlike you peons, we have the heart, intelligence and desire to create our OWN destiny. We need no one to offer us a hand of help. Helpful hands come tainted, and inside that tainted help lies disease. You know the disease I speak of. Love. Friendship. Loyalty. They all mean one thing: The end. Throughout my years on this earth, I've met nary a man who could hold his head high within the throws of friendship. You see, NIWF, the reason the Deities survive, surprise and destroy is simple. He takes credit for me leaving SPW in the first place, but I say he should take credit for something else. He should take credit for Roddy Taylor rediscovering himself. You see, I owe an awful lot to Vile Vince Viper. Through his attacks, and our spilling eachother's blood, Viper taught me that I had let myself become weak, and that I was investing too much in the fans and not enough in myself. I left SPW not because he kicked my ass all over the place, but because I had to seriously re-evaluate my career and my future. I came to the NIWF to rediscover myself, and I can say compared to six months ago, I'm a more complete wrestler, and a more complete man, and a lot of that has a good deal to do with Vile Vince Viper. Therefore, I was merely doing Viper a favour cause I don't wanna see his efforts to break his back for the fans be all for naught, and I know that deep down in his slithery slimy heart that he enjoyed himself out there last week.
