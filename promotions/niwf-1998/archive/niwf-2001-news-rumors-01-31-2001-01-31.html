NIWF — News

News & Rumors 01/31/2001

31 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hmmm.. things are starting to heat up in the rumor mill for the NIWF. As we are nearing our Free Per View which will wind up being called "Dangerous" as per our Free Per View traditions and will most likely occur on Feb. 25th, 2001. Let's get to the rumors.... -"Renaissance Man" Patrick Lewis currently approached President Lukiw and asked him to be listed as an inactive wrestler. Lukiw has discussed some issues Lewis may of had with the NIWF and the agreement was made that Lewis is going to be working backstage and will be the one who will help spice things up and bring back the International Title. Lukiw is pleased that Lewis still wishes to at least participate in the NIWF, even if it is not as a wrestler. -The NIWF may go to 7 matches per card! The reason for this is because it is expected that many people may want to leave in the near future, which will leave a big hole in the roster and would be hard to fill out for 8 matches a card. However, nothing is definite yet. Many things could change and for every wrestler the NIWF could lose, they could also gain 1 wrestler back. Many people have expressed interest in the NIWF and things could remain the same. -Rumor has it that once Free Per View time rolls around, President Lukiw will either let the wrestlers take a break from action for one week before or one week after the Free Per View. The reason for this is Lukiw does not want his wrestlers to exhaust themselves, and frankly he doesn't want to suffer from exhaustion either. Taking a break for one week would do the NIWF, the writers, the president and most importantly the handlers.. some good. -The next rumor we are hearing is that the Free Per View could see many gimmick matches for each title that is on the line. We aren't sure which gimmick matches could be set for these matches and we aren't sure if these matches will appear as gimmick matches either. But we do know that President Lukiw will address this and put rest to the rumor. -As we said, every week we will name 5 wrestlers who will participate in the NIWF Royal Rumble for the NIWF World Title. Last week, you saw that Black Flagg, "Hearbreaker" Matt Foxx., Dr. DooM, Badger & Smilin' Jack Ruby have all been named as participants. The list now grows to 10 with the following 5 wrestlers being added to the Rumble: "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice The Steve "True 2 The Game" Shane Sharpe "Superstar" Lawrence Steele The Bad Boy -"Dirty" Dan Lukiw has supposedly called many FORMER NIWF wrestlers to ask them if they are still looking for work in e-wrestling and if they would be interested in working for the NIWF again. As a matter of fact, he claims that a few of these wrestlers will be some surprises in the NIWF Rumble among other surprises as well. When asked who he has been speaking with, all Lukiw could tell us was "These guys can add so much shock value that everyone will mark out over this spectacular card!" -Many rumors are running around about [name withheld]'s surprise for the Rumble still. Names have been thrown around, but the two biggest names that have been thrown around have been Jason Starr & Christian Hawk! It is believed that one of these two are going to be the ones who show up as #30 in the Rumble, as promised by Lukiw. Just for entertainment purposes, the other names that have been thrown around are: Dennis Windham, Bobbo the Clown, Jimmy Cougar, Alex Cougar, Lawrence Buffy, [name withheld], Davey Rumble, "Superstar" Steve Destiny and Chaz Darby... none of which have had any contact in the NIWF that we know about. -Roddy Taylor appeared in the NIWF this past weekend. Roddy Taylor is not a part of the NIWF and will not be a part of it in the near future thanks to the words that he said about the NIWF in Shootfire. We still are uncertain on how Taylor got into the arena, but the speculation is that he had one of the security guards paid off and that person let him in. Lukiw is doing everything in his power to assure Roddy Taylor doesn't show his face in the NIWF again. -The Official Secrets seem to be under some sort of protest. Gwen still has not signed her contract and was wrestling without a contract last week on Hardcore Edge. Gwen's lawyers contacted Lukiw about having her compete and they wanted her taken out of the match or else they were going to sue the NIWF. The president reminded them that Gwen had a verbal agreement to sign the contract and be in the match. Apparently the rest of the group is angry at the way things are run in the NIWF and feel that things are "out of place" in the NIWF. Personally, this rumor starter thinks the Official Secrets are mad that they don't have the titles right now and Lukiw refuses to let them have it until they prove they deserve it. As a protest, it seems The Official Secrets refuse to fight back with their opponents in any match they are scheduled to appear in. -Apparently Dr. DooM came storming into President Lukiw's office after his loss this past week. He demanded and threatend the president, asking him to give him a match with Dylan Dice..... a LAST MAN STANDING match! Lukiw is not sure what to make of this but I feel that Dr. DooM will get that match in the near future and it will happen before the Free Per View. This should be interesting. -Apparently female wrestler, Andrea Chandler, has been in contact with the NIWF about work. She is still not sure whether or not to bring herself into the NIWF. The women's division has been getting strong and getting much interest in the NIWF. If too many female wrestlers want to get into the fed, the president may turn them away and refer them to WHIP or another female federation. -Axel Schmidt made yet another appearance in the NIWF and brutally attacked the president. We are unsure of his motives, as we are not allowed to get words from him for he is not under NIWF contract. However, the president is going to say something about it this weekend at Hardcore Edge and promises that Axel will not be allowed to come into the arena and harm him! -Something seems fishy between Garnet & Mr. Bungle. Garnet's words were totally uncalled for and we are not sure of what her motives were when she said the things she said. However, we are told that Mr. Bungle will have some words this weekend and Garnet may be right by his side as well when he speaks. We are sure that Mr. Bungle might be angered by these words by Garnet and expect him to lash out on her verbally on either Vocal Explosions or Hardcore Edge. -The president received many compliments about the card this past weekend. While the president wasn't sure about how well it was put out because he had a late night working on putting things together (up till 3am), he got positive respones. He was glad they writers came through for him, that Doug Hardy toned down the 14lb ham talk a little bit and that the wrestlers contributed the way they did! That is all for today! Things are quickly heating up, as I said. The buildup is good for the Free Per View. Keep it up guys! Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/
