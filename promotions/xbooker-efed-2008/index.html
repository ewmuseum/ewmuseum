Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Xbooker E-FED's own founding sign-up thread, posted 31 July 2008, spun the promotion off the real-wrestling blog X Booker: members introduced themselves under a blogosphere alias and picked a gimmick to wrestle under, several borrowing a real wrestler's own ring name (CM Punk, Shawn Michaels, AJ Styles, HHH, Edge, The Game) as that alias rather than a claim to book the real athlete's own continuity — CM Punk's own sign-up post names a Shooting Star Press and a Canadian Destroyer as finishers, not the real Punk's Go To Sleep. Two brands split the roster, X-Brand and Ring Of Champions, each with its own general manager, roster and championships.

X-Brand's debut show, 6 August 2008, closed on a six-way “Royal Championship Brawl” for its vacant World Heavyweight Championship: CM Punk, Sub-Zero and Westlife were eliminated in turn before Duff Man ran in from the crowd to eliminate Westlife himself, and Dias Ferreira followed with a sledgehammer, knocking Dexter senseless before security swarmed the ring. A new general manager, The Game, walked out to declare the match a triple disqualification and the title suspended “until further notice.” A wrestler injured in the same show's own earlier, uncaptured card, KidMyst, was written out for good the following week, the promotion's own news post citing a shattered shin from being “brutally launched off the entrance platform.”

Ring Of Champions crowned MoonPlague its World Heavyweight Champion, Guilty Pleasure its European Champion, and Edge & Diogo X_Day its Tag Team Champions. Going into the brand's own first pay-per-view, Summer Bash, its general manager HHH fired the superstar MP for drug use days before MP's own scheduled title shot, then booked a replacement number-one-contender's match between Sabão and JakerTombstone that ended in a double count-out after Sabão put Jaker through the broadcast table — both men counted out together, with HHH turning the finish into a Triple Threat for the World Championship at Summer Bash instead.

Summer Bash's own advertised card, as built across both brands' final shows, also carried Shawn Michaels against PhantomLord, a Tag Team Championship defense pitting Edge and X_Day against Jesus of Suburbia and 619 Tritur, and a Hell in a Cell match between EVILution and Hunter; none of it is preserved to confirm how the card itself turned out.

A second season ran from 11 February to 13 April 2009 on a companion forum, restructured into two further brands, Dangerside and Battlezone, and built toward a pay-per-view of its own, Extreme Battle. Only that season's board-index pages survive — thread titles, authors and reply counts, never an opened post — and its own final thread title names the promotion's last show directly: “Resultados - 13 de Abril de 2009 ( Último Show ).”
