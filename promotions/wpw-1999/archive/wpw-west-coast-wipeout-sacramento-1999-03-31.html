West Coast Wipeout Tour — Sacramento Results

31 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Results of World Pro Wrestling's West Coast Wipeout Sacramento card: Interview w/Stone Cold. "I've said it before, Rock, and I'll say it again...cliches are cliches and an ass-whoopin' is an ass-whoopin', and
Stone Cold Steve Austin is gonna whoop your little candyass from one corner of that cage to the other."
Results from a 4-man tournament to crown a new Intercontinental Champion: Round 1: Barry Windham defeated Shane Douglas with a backbreaker in 18:45 **1/2 Round 1: Chris Candido beat Rob Van Dam with the Blonde Bombshell in 12:22 ***3/4 Finals: Barry Windham beat Chris Candido by disqualification due to Francine's interference in 14:14 ** Barry Windham wins the Intercontinental Title due to the disqualification rule being waived. Benoit and Malenko come out to celebrate with Windham...the Horsemen are reborn! After the match, Candido gets into a verbal fight with Francine.
Francine slaps Candido, and Tammy Lynn Sytch comes out of nowhere and beats the s*^% out of her. Candido's buddy Shane Douglas comes out for the save.
Douglas and Candido have words, which leads to pushes and shoves. Al Snow comes out to soothe the egos...
The Rock is interviewed in the back, where he delivers his greatest catchphrase to date: "The Rock will book your monkey ass into the Smackdown
Hotel, where each guest receives a complimentary fist in the mouth and foot in the ass!" Great stuff.
No-Time-Limit 2/3 Falls Match For The Intercontinental Tag Titles: The Horsemen (Benoit and Malenko) beat Stan Hansen and Justin Bradshaw in three falls: *Benoit beat Hansen via the Crippler Crossface in 29:11 *Bradshaw beat Malenko with the Lariat at 42:39 *Benoit beat Bradshaw by countout at 50:52 (The Horsemen retained the titles) Backstage, Douglas is in his own area with Francine. When asked about his relationship with Candido, Douglas says there's nothing to worry about, as long as Tammy Lynn knows her place. Francine adds, "If she gets in my face again, I'll beat the bitch's ass!"
X-Pac pinned Chris Jericho with the X-Factor to win the World Light-Heavyweight Title in 18:22 *** The Miracle Violence Connection (Steve Williams and Terry Gordy) defeated
Public Enemy by disqualification due to the interference of Chris Benoit and
Dean Malenko (The MVC retain the World Tag Team Titles)
-1/2* Huge brawl erupts between the MVC and the Horsemen. Eventually the teams are separated. Chris Candido and Tammy are interviewed in the back. Candido calls Douglas a chickenshit and Tammy says Francine needs to look in the mirror before she calls anyone a bitch. Douglas and Francine find Candido and Sytch and a huge brawl erupts between the four...officials break it up.
Abdullah the Butcher defeated Al Snow in a bed-of-nails match to earn a shot at the Hardcore Title DUD
Tommy Dreamer defeated Cactus Jack in a ladder match to retain the Hardcore title. After the match, Abby and Jack worked over Dreamer, until Terry Funk ran out and made the save.
First Blood Cage Match for the World Title: Steve Austin defeated the Rock in 28:10 after using a slapjack handed to him by Barry Windham (!) to draw blood from Maivia...Austin is the fourth
Horseman! No, because as the cage is raised, and Austin sees Maivia laying cold on the mat, he looks at Windham, and Benoit and Malenko who have joined him, and flips them the finger. Out from the crowd comes...HBK! He levels
Austin with Sweet Chin Music and starts beating the tar out of him along with Windham and the Vanilla Midgets. Screen fades to black after HBK opens beer, chugs for a bit, then pours the rest on Austin and Maivia's prone bodies!
Prelim Card For The Cow Palace: World Light-Heavyweight Title Match X-Pac vs. Chris Jericho The Rock vs. Barry Windham (non-title) Chris Candido vs. Shane Douglas Harlem Heat vs. The Nasty Boys World Tag Team Title Match: The Horsemen (Benoit and Malenko) vs. the MVC (Champs) Falls-Count-Anywhere; Loser of Fall Retires Permanently Abby/Cactus Jack vs. Dreamer/Funk
