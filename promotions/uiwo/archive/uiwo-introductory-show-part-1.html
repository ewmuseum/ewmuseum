Floyde Bennett Jr. Tonight Floyde is going to tell us a little about his wrestling career and then he is going to tell us just what he is going to be doing here in the UIWO.Floyde first I would like for you to tell us a little about your wrestling career. FBJ: Ok lets see here. I got involved in the sport of wrestling at age 11. I wrestled for my school. I won several awards including 2 time State Champion. At age 18 I started wrestling professionally. I wrestled under the name of Peacemaker. Throughout my career I wrestled for many organizations and won many titles.In 1990 I suffered a knee injury causing me to retire. After retiring I had to be involved in wrestling somehow. So I got involved in journalism in the sport of professional wrestling. For the last seven years I have been writing articles for wrestling magazines throughout the world. I have been the host of several wrestling shows as well. DP: I got a chance to watch you in a couple of matches in the later part of your career. I want you to know that I enjoyed watching you in action. I have read a lot of your wrestling articles over the years. You were a great wrestler and you are a great journalist. FBJ Thanks a lot. You dont know how much that means to me Derek. DP: No problem. Now I would like you to let us know what your job duties are here in the UIWO. FBJ First I will be doing most of the interviews here in the UIWO. I am the head editer of the UIWO Magazine. I am the host of the UIWO Hotline.And I am the cohost of Monday Night Mania. DP: That sounds like a work load. But I know you can handle it. You are going to enjoy working with the lovely Jenny Anderson. She is a great broadcaster. FBJ: I have spoken to her many times and I know that I will enjoy working withher. She is a lovely young woman. DP: That she is. Ok Floyde I am going let you take over the show and do your interviews. Then I will come back and finish the show with the releasing of the card info on the first Monday Night Mania. But first we are going to take a quick break for a UIWO commercial. FBJ: See you later on tonight Derek. [ Both men get up and they shake hands. Then Derek leaves the set.] ******************************************************* DO YOU HAVE WHAT IT TAKES TO BE A PROFESSIONAL WRESTLER? CHECK OUT THE OFFICIAL UIWO WEB SITE AT http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Arena/7651 AND JOIN TODAY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ******************************************************* FBJ: Welcome back to the show. Tonight I am going to interview a few wrestlers here in the UIWO I am going to have some live interviews and I will also have some clips some pre-recorded comments of some wrestlers as well. My first guess tonight is Whirlwind. So if you would Whirlwind please come out on the set. (screens opens to Floyde Bennet Jr. rushing to Whirlwind with a mic for an interview....) FBJ: Mr. Whirlwind, how do you feel being in such a great fed as the UIWO? WHIRLWIND: Well Floyde, its feels GREAT!! Especially since I am the first to sign up and I don't regret it a bit! FBJ: And what do you think of your competition here? WHIRLWIND: Hmmm....I don' want to reply to that question. If I can't say anything positive about my opponents then I won't say anything at all. FBJ: Oh!! Anyway,there has been a rumor that you have been engaging in secret talks with another wrestler here in the UIWO. Is the rumour true? WHIRLWIND: Well Floyde, it is true thatI have been contacted but no details will be given yet as there has not been comfirmation. FBJ: I see. Anyway what do you think of your chances in the tournament? WHIRLWIND: Floyde, I should win it and the belt quite easily. FBJ: What confidence! By the way, who is this bearded person with you? WHIRLWIND: This is my manager-cum-unle Zues! He taught me alot of thing about wrestling and will prevent anyone from interfering so don't even think about it. FBJ: Umm... Mr Whirlwind it seems that we have run out of time for this interview but Thank You anyways. (screen fades as Whirlwind walks out the door to sign autographs for his many adoring fans....) FBJ: Well we were glad to have Whirlwind and his manager Zues on the show. It will be interesting to see who he has been secretly been contacting. Ok now for our next guest tonight. This man is the son of one of the greatest wrestlers of all time.[General Red Lee Johnston] Lets hear it for General Lee. [ A man standing 6'2" with a goattee wearing a black leather trench coat comes out from behind the curtain. He meets Floyde on the set and shakes hands with him.] FBJ: General Lee it is a pleasure to have you here tonight on the show. GL: Yeah the pleasure is all yours.Lets cut out all the bull(BEEP) and get this interview over with. FBJ: Ok How do you feel about being here in the UIWO? And do you think that you will be in your fathers shadow in the early part of your career? GL: First of all. I didnt come here to talk about my father. My father was the best wrestler ever and that is the bottom line. Now as for me. I am the best wrestler that ever lived. Now as far as me being here in the UIWO. Well I guess that I will have to go look for some competition becuase as it looks now. The roster is full with a bunch of losers that want to be just like me. FBJ: I agree with you about your father. He was a great wrestler. But as far as you are concerned. I think that you have a lot to learn. You need to show a little respect to not only the UIWO but your fathar as well. I never seen your father carry on like this. So why should you be any different? GL: That was my father's weakness. He always cared about what the fans thought. Well not me. I am going to kick the crap out of everyone who steps in the ring with me. I dont care about the fans. You can like me or hate me.But I am going to do what I want to do and that is the bottom line. So if you dont have anything else to ask me this interview is over. FBJ: I guess we have ran out of time anyways. You better show some respect to the wrestlers here in the UIWO before you get that smart mouth of yours kicked right off your face. GL: I want everyone to know in the UIWO that I challenge everyone to a match. I dont care where or when it is. I am going to be the only champion around here. Soon I will have all the titles rapped around my waist. [ General points his middle finger up at the camara and walks off the set.] ******************************************************* FBJ: Well General Lee needs to show a little respect. His father would roll over in his grave if he knew what his son is doing. He will probally regret challanging everyone here in the UIWO to a match. I am glad that I am not in his shoes. Enough about him. My next guest tonight is a man that thinks he is god's gift to women. Lets bring out Libber Lipped Lenny. [ The song I'm to Sexy starts playing in the background while a man comes out in a pair of blue jeans and no shirt on. He is doing several poses in front of the camera. Finally he gets to Floyde Bennett Jr. to start the interview.] FBJ: I am glad that you took a moment out of your time of showing off your body to do this interview with me. LLL: Well Floyde when you got a body like I do you want to show it off. All the girls love me. Well I guess you dont know what I mean. Look at your gut. You need to go to the gym and work out a little. What being retired make you lazy or something? FBJ: I am not going to comment on what you just said. Well anyways where do you see yourself going in the UIWO? LLL: I am the sexiest person a live. And at WINTERBASH 97 I am going to be crowned the first UIWO World Heavyweight Champion in the history of the UIWO. And there is nothing that anyone is going to do about it. FBJ: Well it is going to be one hell of a tournament for that title. There is a lot of great talent here in the UIWO. Every match is going to be a great match in the tournament for the World Heavyweight Title. LLL: Let me tell everyone here in the UIWO that I am the best thing that could of happened to the UIWO. Not only am I the sexiest man alive but I am the greatest athlete as well. See you all on November 3rd.
