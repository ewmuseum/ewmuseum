A self-titled wrestling league whose surviving card splits a Triple Crown-claiming champion's open challenge and a title-vacating angle across two Usenet posts, backed by a roleplay-recruitment sample from its own top tag team. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Destiny Creations Wrestling League had been running since 4 March 1997 by its own account; a recruitment post that June, hosted on GeoCities, said it was looking for “New Blood” while “Dumping Non Role Playing Trash,” “Serious Inquires only.”

Its one surviving broadcast, Saturday Night Extreme, ran across two posts on 7 and 8 June 1997. It opened with newcomer Tony Samechi beating Corporal Punishment, then Austin 4:15 forcing Kensuki Kwan to submit to a claw hold in an “I Quit” match the broadcast itself called Kwan's best performance yet. The league's own president and referee, John Clapp Sr., then brought the returning Television Champion, Tazmanian Crippler, out for an open challenge against Bon Scott, an Australian wrestler who had spent his own pre-match interview dismissing a faction called the Independent Nation as “a sideshow distraction.” Billed on the same show as closing in on the promotion's first-ever Triple Crown, Crippler outlasted Scott across 31 minutes for the win.

A tag-styled brawl followed, one half of a team called Damnation against one half of a team called Elemental Force, fought with a cane, an umbrella, and a fire extinguisher through the crowd before the Elemental Force man won with his own finisher, delivered on the concrete floor.

The main event turned the promotion's own hierarchy over in a single match. Unified Heavyweight Champion Grandma Stagg came to the ring out of his wrestling gear, announced he was being forced to leave the league “for powers beyond my control,” and handed his own title to challenger El Juapo before lying down for an eleven-second pin. When the Independent Nation moved to block Stagg's exit afterward, a group of other wrestlers ran in with chairs to fight them off, and Stagg slipped away in the chaos — gone from the league's own record for good.

The only later document is a 17 June recruitment roleplay, a sample of trash talk from the league's own top tag team goading its rivals into a title shot. No further card is known. What became of the league, or whether the Independent Nation and Elemental Force ever settled their own rivalry, isn't in the surviving record.
