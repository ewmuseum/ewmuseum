Looking for Good Role players

17 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Im looking for good Role Playing Tag Teams! Here is an Example from a Top contender team in my league, The DCWL http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/1118/index.html The Elemental Force Maelstrom is actually laughing, watching some comments from DCWL stars on the television. Monolith, sitting nearby, does not look amused, which for him is typical. [Mo] Must you continue to chortle like some possessed hyena at these incompetents? Their words are ridiculous enough without hearing you guffawing at them. [Ma] I can't help it! These twits are too hilarous! Take Damnation, for instance! What a joke! They think they beat us...after we'd already put out the other two teams and wrestled for fourty minutes plus! And you chumps *still* couldn't get a win in less than half an hour! What a pair of cretins! Of course, on Tuesday, assuming Clapp has the guts to sign the match, your so-called victory will be quickly washed away, as we dispose of your puny bodies in con*vinc*ing fashion. Of course, since they still can't muster up enough brain power to pronounce our name correctly, I suppose we'll have to take pity on them and give *them* a rematch. [Mo] That is considered taking pity on them? By punishing them even more for their stupidity? I was under the impression that the definition was somewhat different. In any case, their destruction is guaranteed, much unlike the words of some other idiot... [Ma] You must be referring to the Canadian chowderhead. Listen, dunce, the only thing guaranteed from you getting into the ring with us is your quick exit on a stretcher! I don't care what meaningless *singles* belts you've held, you aren't a *tag team* wrestler...and thank God for that! We're already subjected to a new low in talent here, without you to make things worse! If *you're* as good as they come here, we should just leave before anyone finds out we signed a contract! It's embarrassing! [Mo] It matters not...his allies have already taken their cowardly exit from the fray. As you miserable sots observed, Damnation was sorely outclassed by us. We demolished both the Pack and the former tag team champions, and it was only after we had done all the work that the others could claim the glory. That oversight will be remedied shortly. You, however, realizing that your helpless little cronies would be eviscerated if they were to face the power of the elements, have chosen instead to run like the wind, as it were. Your expectations were far exceeded, and that frightened you beyond all comprehension. Thus, the only logical steps, those aimed toward the door, followed. [Ma] Bit late now, yellowbellies! You opened the door, and now we're going to drag your sorry carcasses through it! Clapp doesn't have any choice but to sign two of you scrubs to a match with us, so that we won't take it out on the actual tag teams. After all, if we put all of *them* out, what reason would Monolith and I have to stay? None, and thus Clapp would have to fold the fed due to lack of talent. Without us, this bush league would be worthless, and he'll protect his investment any way he can. Since all three of you wouldn't be enough to be on even odds with us, that means Don Flamenco there might as well butter you all right now, 'cuz you're toast. [Mo] You may run as far as you like. There will be no escape, unless you plan to do the DCWL a favor and leave completely. If that is the case, we have no intention of chasing you...your leaving will be proof enough of our superiority. If that is not the case, then your fate is sealed. Perhaps whatever gods you worship will be merciful, and simply strike you down with some debilitating disease, rather than force you to face annihilation at the hands of Elemental Force. But were I you, I would not place too much hope in that particular possibility. [Ma] It's very simple, scrubs. We already proved we're the best team in this meager league, and once we've claimed what already belongs to us, there won't be any arguing the point. If a bunch of snotnosed punk singles wrestlers want to get wasted along the way, well, pain is our business, and you'll suffer plenty of it. It's all there, in black and white...color, for those of you who can afford it. So come and prove just how right we are. There's the door, dipstick...use it. (fade out)
