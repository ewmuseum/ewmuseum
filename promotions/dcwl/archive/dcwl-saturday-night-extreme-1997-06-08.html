DCWL Saturday Night Extreme June 7,1997 (The schene opens in front of the DCWL Arena in Downtown Middle Town,New York earlier today with Chris Dietz doing an interview with Bon Scott.) Chris Dietz: We are here with one of the up and comers of the DCWL, from Australia, Mr. Bon Scott. Mr. Scott, what do you think of the DCWL? Bon Scott: Not bad, not bad. Some of the blokes are OK by me, but some stink like kangaroo dung...and they don't look much nicer. CD: As you know, this league has been invaded by the Independant Nation and.. BS: For crying out loud..give it a rest! You keep talking about a group and they are just gonna get bigger heads! That's my job, to shrink some of the egos around here....bunch of overblown trapeze artists... CD: So you align yourself in the group that opposes the Independent nation being put together by.. BS: No! I join no one in that small corner of the DCWL. It is a sideshow distraction. There are more important things to talk about. CD: Like what? BS: Like how the title is likely to fall right into my lap. CD: Fall right into your lap? How is that? BS: Well, unless there is a champion that can take a real man fighting them, I am bound to get a shot sometime, and when that time comes, it will be over before it has begun, because I am in their heads! They know I'm not gonna try any cutsie moves, they KNOW I'm just gonna stand there and BEAT them and BEAT them until they can't stand. That is the measure of a real man, Chris. And I don't see many here. I will separate the men from the ladies, cause the ladies will be the ones wearing the little pink tutus I put on em. I can tell you that Don Arturo's guy, El Juapo, won't be one of the tutu wearers. CD: Do you plan on stepping into the ring with El Juapo in a tag team match? BS: If the champs annoy me enough..or if I have to kick two people's butts at the same time..yeah. I just got one thing to say. If anyone thinks that they can hide behing tough words and then step into the ring with me and hide behind little back flips and twirly birdie moves, they are sadly, sadly mistaken. The DCWL is an EXTREME league, and anybody who steps in the ring with me is gonna learn that REAL quick. The thunder from down under is gonna CRASH over you and when you wake up bleeding and crying...don't say that you weren't warned.
