The World News Wrestling Federation Wednesday Live Slam.................... --------------------------------------------------------------------------- (The camera pans through the arena to president of WNWF, Todd Spencer, sitting along ringside)
Todd speaks: "Welcome fans to our first ever Wednesday Live Slam, a combination of the best of Tuesday Live Encounter and Thursday Nite Slam.
We have all the titles up for grabs in a special edition card...(The camera zooms to a telepromptor)
Singles match: Executioner vs. The Bodyguard Tag-team match: The Royal Knights vs. The Michegan Gangstas No DQ match: Soultaker vs. The king Guy Newman Tag-team match: The new Masters of Destruction vs. Canadian Carnage 4-Corners match: Christian North vs. Brian Wallace vs. Cotton-eye Joe vs. Mr. Excitement World Net championship match: Champ: Mark Bagwell vs. Natureboy Danny Kulba World tag-team championship match: Champs: The Sanctuary vs. ER The World Heavy-weight championship match: Champ: Jason the Terrible vs. Golaith "Well there you have it...That's one hot card...Now to our dream team, Gary Valis and Scott Pullman, Gary..." Gary: "Our next match pits 2 powers houses in the Bodyguard and the Executioner against each other for the very first time...Now to our ring announcer Ray Fuller.....Ray..."
Ray: "Our first match is for one fall...Introducing 1st weighing in at 254 pounds, Executioner (the sound of a lowering guillotine sounds) and his opponent from Los Angeles, Ca; the Bodyguard (the theme from the movie plays)
The match starts as 'Guard nails Exec with a chop to the chest...He kicks Exec in the gut...This makes 'Guard mad!!!He nails 'Guard with a massive forearm to the head...He motions for his finisher the Guillotine...He nails it!!!He gives him another and another...'Guard is out!!!He goes for the pin...The ref counts 1-2-3...He gets the pin!!!
WINNER--EXECUTIONER BY PIN 4:12 --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gary: "Wow that was a quick one!!!Almost beat Moo Kane's record..." Ray: "Our next match is a tag match introducing first weighing in at 595 pounds, The Cincinnatti Knight and Royal Guard, they are the Royal Knights and their opponents weighing in at 486 pounds, Gary Trent and Jerry
Gordan, they are The Michegan Gangstas (the michegan school theme is played in the from of a rap)
The match starts of as Cinci Knight battles Jerry Gordan...Gordan makes the 1st move...He kicks Knight and then gives him a wicked powerbomb...He is out!!!Knight tags in Guard...Gordan nails him as he enters...He gives him a powerslam...He picks up Guard and gives him an atomic drop...Knight tags in Guard as Gordan tags in Trent...Guard nails him with a clothesline!!!
Trent tags in Gordan...Guard gives him a wicked DDT...Gordan tags Trent back in...Trent clotheslines him and then gives him a nasty leg bomb...
Guard rolls and makes the tag...Knight rushes into the ring...Trent knocks him down and then gives him a top rope moonsault...He goes for the figure-
4...Guard comes into the ring to make the save...Gordan comes in and gives Guard a vicious DDT then sends him out of the ring...Knight is still down as Trent motions for his finisher the "drive-by" top rope brain buster....
He pulls Knight to the top rope and then brings him back down head first... He is out...Trent goes for the pin...Destruction Inc. rush the ring and start pounding on Trent...The ref motions for the bell and disqualifies
The Royal Knights...Gordan enters the ring and DDT's Assasin...Destruction Inc. quickly leave the ring as Gordan tends to his partner... WINNERS---THE MICHEGAN GANGSTA BY DQ 12:05 --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Rick: "That was a great match...The Gangstas had it won anyway!!!" Ray: "Our next match is is a no DQ match introducing 1st from Boston, Mass, Soultaker (a howl of a wolf is played) and his opponent weighing 244 pounds The King Guy Newman (is escorted by his new vale, the Queen) The match starts slowly as they size each other up...'Taker starts 1st as he nails him with a knee lift...The king answers back with a verticle suplex...'Taker sneers and gives the king his own verticle suplex...'Taker gives him another kneelift...He nails the king with a drop kick sending him out of the ring onto the conrete...He gives the king a fall away slam to the concrete as the ref counts 1-2-3-4...He gives him aother fall away slam as the King is hurt...The ref continues 5-6-7...'Taker rolls back into the ring...'Taker leaves the ring to attack the king again as the ref counts the king out and calls for the bell...'Taker pulls a set of brass knuckles out of his armband and nails the king in the jaw...He leaves for the locker room...The king is out as officials come to the ring...
WINNER--SOULTAKER BY COUNTOUT 16:24 --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gary: "Holy cow that was some match...I think the king is hurt bad!!!" Ray: "Our next match is a tag match...First weighing in at 580 pounds, The Boy Toy and Grim Reaper, The new Masters of Destruction and their opponents weighing in at 521 pounds Johnny Cannuck and The Mountie II,
Canadian Carnage (the canadian national anthem plays)... This match really never gets started as the former tag champs Raekwon and Tidal Wave attack Carnage at the while they are waling down the runway... They shove carnage into the ring...All of the masters of destruction are in the ring...slick rick comes down to the ring...Carnage tries to mount an offence but their are too many of them...Tidal Wave powerbombs Cannuck as The Boy Toy puts him in the figure-4...The ref calls for the bell disqualifying The new Masters od Destruction...Raewkon gives Mountie his finsher the "end" he is hurt....Slick Rick grabs the mike: "You see people we are the rulers of the WNWF...We decide everything here in this fed...
We will have all of the gold in this fed...There is nobody who can stop us... We challenge anyone and everyone to any kind of match...(Sanctuary appears on a large screen on the far wall...Destroyer speaks: "The masters of destruction...We have your belts...We stole you belts and left you only defeat in return...4 on 2 seems hardly fair...How about a 4 on 4 survivor series next week btween all of you against Canadian carnage and us...Are you guys man enough for this, probably not..." The screen goes black...
Slick Rick answers: "You're on punks!!!" as carnage are thrown from the ring...WINNERS--CANADIAN CARNAGE BY DQ 1:00 --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Scott: "Sounds like a great match-up to me!!!" Rick: "Our next match is for one fall introducing 1st from Harwick,Ma Invader and his opponent from Chicago, Brian Damage" Invader starts things off with a spinning DDT...He adds a running powerslam to Damage's troubles...Damage finds thr ropes...He gives 'vader a power- bomb...'Vader finds thr ropes...Damage nails him with powerslam...He nails
'Vader with an elbow to the head...'Vader rolls out of the ring...'Vader comes back into the ring and gives him an inverted atomic drop...He gives
Damage a chokeslam from the top rope...Damage is hurt!!!'Vader nails Damage with a belly-to-belly suplex...'Vader picks him up and gives him another inverted atomic drop...Damage finds the ropes...Invader goes for the bow and arrow submission...Damage breaks the hold...Damage nails him with a superkick, sending him into the ropes...He nails him with the
"Death sentence" flying clothesline...He goes for the pin and gets it!!! WINNER--BRIAN DAMAGE BY PIN 9:56 --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gary: "That match featured 2 of WNWF's powerhouses!!!" Rick: "Our next match is a four corners match with a new stipulation from president of WNWF...It will be for a shot at the national championship at next weeks Wednesday Live Slam...The combatants in order of entry are as follows: Cristian North, Brian Wallace, Cotton-eye Joe, and Mr. Excitement.
The match starts off fst and furious as all 4 wrestlers start pounding on each other...Wallace was the 1st one eliminated as Excitement nailed him with the "heartbreaker" and got the pin at 5:12...Excitment then gives
North a wicked DDT and gets the pin at 9:42...It's down to Joe and Excitment...Joe nails Excitment with a face-first powerbomb and gets the pin at 15:31 and gets the shot at the national championship Wednesday...
WINNER--COTTON-EYE JOE BY PIN AT 15:31 --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Scott: "What a match, sorry we had to cut out some of the action due to time constraints!!!"
Rick: "Our next match is for the World Net Title, intorducing 1st the challenger, Natureboy Danny Kulba and the the champion "Bad Boy" Mark
Bagwell (theme from cops plays) This match starts off slow as they size each other up...Kulba starts things off by knocking down Bagwell and dropping his leg on his head...Bagwell finds thr ropes...He suplexes Kulba...Kulba finds thr ropes...Bagwell nails him with a short clothesline...Kulba rolls out of the ring...The Sandmand appears from under the ring and nails Kulba with a wicked shoulder block sending him into the steel steps...Bagwell is distracting the ref as sandman punishes Kulba...He thorws Kulba back into the ring...Bagwell nails him with a back suplex...he motions for his finisher, the "Bad crab"...He puts Kulba in the bad crab as The Boy Toy runs down to the ring and gives
Sandman a drop kick sending him into the steel steps...He is out!!!Kulba submits as Boy Toy grabs the mike: "Bad Boy you have something that I think belongs to me...I will get my gold back (Bagwell looks at him speak and is caught from behind by Kulba)...I will get a rematch my friend, and then I will have my revenge...(he throws the mike) Kulba gives him a piledriver.
He leaves...Sandman hobbles to Bagwell's aid... WINNER AND STILL NET CHAMPION BAD BOY MARK BAGWELL BY SUBMISSION 16:42 --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gary: "Badboy made another enemy..." Rick: "The next match is for the tag championship...Introducing first the challengers; Dr. Roger M. Thomas and Dr. Matthew Mcmahon, the are ER (the theme from ER plays) and the champions, Destroyer and Olvan, they are the
Sanctuary... (the sound of a bomb exploding is played) The match starts off with Mcmahon facing Destroyer...Destroyer nails him with a spinning powerbomb...He nails McMahon with a flying legdrop...
Mcmahon finds his cormer to make the tag...Destroyer gives him a belly-to- back suplex...Roger tags in Mchmahon...Mcmahon nails him with a swinging neckbreaker...He gives Destroyer a slingshot suplex...Destroyer tags in
Olvan...Mcmahon puts him up in his finisher the "rehabiliatation" but Olvan escapes...Olvan finds the ropes...Olvan nails him with a wicked flying drop kick...Olvan goes for his finsher the "sweet dreams"...He is out as the ref calls for the bell...Dr. Thomas rushes in to revive his colleague...The Masters of destruction (all of them) with Slick Rick come down to the ring...Slick Rick takes a mike: "Lucky win pukes...You won't be so lucky next week in the 4 man tag match with us!!!We will crush you...
Then we will take you titles...You are powerless to stop us...(Canadian Carnage come down to the ring)...I see you made some friends...ER chase the masters of destruction off of the ring apron...See you next week punks
(he throws the mike) WINNERS AND STILL TAG CHAMPIONS---THE SANCTUARY BY SUBMISSION 24:07 --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Scott: "That was the match of the night!!!" Todd: "I'm sorry to inform everyone but the headliner main event for the world championship will take place next week...Jason brutally attacked
Golaith in the dressing room causing him to have to taken to the hospital.. To insure the wrestlers safety next week, they will be lowered to the ring in cages...This will be a cage match, so that there is no interference.
Jason that was a dispicable and cowardly act...Goliath you will get your title shot next week, and you can takle that to the bank...Well that's it for tonight's card"
