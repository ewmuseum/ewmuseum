WNWF Power Tour: Paris, France

15 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The World News Wrestling Federation....Power Tour...Paris, France.... -------------------------------------------------------------------------- This is a re-broadcast...Sorry...My newsreader was messed up... (The camera pans through the crowded arena with some of the crowd holding signs reading "Eiffel Tower: Le plus fort!!!(The strongest)"...It then zooms to Todd Spencer, Gary Valis, and Scott Pullman at ringside..." Todd: "Bojour fans de WNWF...Welcome to another action packed event...Let's chek out tonight's line-up...(The camera pans to a large screen on the far wall)..." Singles match: Jerry "Goon" Gordan vs Eiffel Towre Singles match: 1/2 of ER (Thomas) vs. 1/2 of M.O.D. (Tidal Wave) Tag match: Canadian Carnage (Destroyer/Olvan) vs. Canadian Carnage (Cannuck/Mountie II) (Again!) vs. Former champs: The Sanctuary (Destroyer/Olvan) Singles match: Former world champ Ruthless vs. Brian Damage Tag match: Midnight Warrior/Mr. Excitment vs. Executioner/"Imperium" Christian North Cage match: Former national champ: Moo Kane vs. Former world champ: Jason The Terrible Non-title street fight (Requested): World champ: Grim Reaper vs. Former net champ: Cotton-eye Joe Debut match: Mortician vs. The Bodyguard Main Event: Net championship! Champion: King Guy Newman vs. Challenger: Gary "OG" Trent Todd: "That looks like an awesome line-up...Let's get to the action" -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ***Look for part II for the matches...It seems that space is limited here. Power Tour Part II -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Todd: "We're back, encore...Let's get to the action!!!" Singles match: Former net champ: Jerry "Goon" Gordan vs Paris' own Eiffel Towre The action is heated at the start of this one...It looked like Towre was going to bring one home until he missed a crucial drop-kick...Gordan then capitalized on the situation with a wicked clothesline...He then nailed
Towre with his finisher, the DDT...The ref counted 1-2-3...The ref raised
Gordan's hadn in victory as the crowd Boo'ed...
WINNER JERRY GORDAN BY PIN -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gary: "What an impressive win for the ex-net champ!!!" Singles match: 1/2 of ER: Dr. Roger M. Thomas vs. 1/2 of M.O.D: Tidal Wave ***Special News report...It's lightning outside so I have to shut down... look for part III Power Tour...Part III -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Todd: "We're back!!!Sorry about the interruption...I'll try to get through this one yet!!!Let's get back to the card..."
"Gordan pinned Eiffel Towre in the first match..." Singles match: 1/2 of ER: Thomas vs. 1/2 of M.O.D.: Tidal Wave This match was brutal from the get-go...Wave was in complete control until he missed his finisher, the "big splash"...Thomas capitalized on this situation...He gave wave a vicious bulldog and then applied his finisher, the "deconstructive surgury" arm hold...Slick Rick, Wave's manager, rushed into the ring and nailed Thomas with his cane...The ref motioned for the bell as The Hermit, Thomas' manager ran into the ring and slapped Rick!!!
This prompted the rest of Rick's bunch to come to the ring...Raekwon and
The Boy Toy started pounding on Thomas...Dr. Mcmahon, Faith, and The
Scarlett Dagger rush to help their stable mate...The officials rush and clear out the ring...
WINNER DR. ROGER M. THOMAS BY DQ -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Scott: "What a battle!!!I think these two forces will meet again!!!" Tag match: WAR tag champs: Canadian Carnage (Cannuck/Mountie II) vs. Former WNWF tag champs: The Sanctuary (Destroyer/Olvan) This was an intelligent match as both sides made frequent tags...The tide turned when Destroyer nailed Cannuck with a spine buster...Cannuck failed to make the tag as Destroyer nails him with his finisher, the
"total destruction" powerbomb...The ref counted 1-2-3...Mountie then enters the ring and clotheslines Destroyer prompting Olvan to enter the ring...
The 2 go at it until officials clear out the ring... WINNERS THE SANCTUARY BY PIN -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gary: "What a match...Maybe Olvan and Mountie II will duke it out in a singles match-up..."
***Have to take another break because of groupspace*** Power Tour...Part IV -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Todd: "We're back fro some more action..." Singles match: The former world champ: Ruthless vs. Brian Damage This match was a little slower than the first four as both wrestlers sized each other up...Ruthless was in control for most of this match scoring several near-falls...Ruthless missed a legdrop and the tide turned...
Damage nailed him with his finisher, the "death sentence" and knocked him out cold...The ref counted 1-2-3...
WINNER BRIAN DAMAGE BY PIN -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Scott: "What an upset for Damage...His stock just rose 100%!!!" Random tag match: (I made the teams) Midnight Warrior/Mr. Excitement vs. Excitment/"Imperium" Christian North
This was an interesting match-up...Both teams were pounding on each as well as their teammates...The match was stopped as Warrior, Excitement, and Executioner (he was teamed with North sorry) started pounding on North.
the match was declared a no-contest decision...They continued to pound on
North as Warrior nails him with his finisher, the "warrior splash"...This prompts Bane, Kulba, and Destructo to come to the ring and drive out the perpetrators...
NO CONTEST -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gary: "Mr. President, that didn't work out well..." Cage match: Former national champ: Moo "Awesome lion" Kane vs. Former world champ: Jason The Terrible This was a classic battle between the martial art skills of Kane and the raw power of Jason...Neither was in complete control as both inflicted serious injury on the other...Both had opportunities to escape the cage but instead continued to battle it out...Kane nailed him with his old finisher the head-toe-kick and sent him down to mat...He then motioned for his new finisher, the "awesome bomb"...He then climbed to the 2nd rope and came down across Jason's chest...He then quickly made his way out of the cage and the bell was rung...Jason quickly got up and screamed...He climbed out and chased Kane back to the locker room area...
WINNER MOO KANE -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Scott: "Another classic upset...Jason is furious!!!" ***time for another break...look for part V*** Power Tour...Part V -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Todd: "We're back!!!That was the last interruption...I promise...Back to the action!!!" Non-title street fight: (Requested) World champ: Grim Reaper vs. Former net champ: Cotton-eye Joe This one starts at the entry way after both wrestlers are announced...You can get a pinfall anywhere in the building in this match!!!Paw, Joe's manager slammed Reaper's head off the railing in the bleachers...This makes reaper mad and he pulls Paw into the aisle and starts pounding on him...Meanwhile Raekwon and Boy Toy are pounding on Joe on the other side of the aisle...They eventially make it to the ring...Reaper is in control at this point and gives Joe his finisher, the "burial" pile driver...Joe rolls out of the ring before he can be pinned...Joe slowly gets up and nails Reaper with a chair as he exits the ring...He then gives Reaper his finisher, the face-1st powerbomb on the concrete outside the ring...Reaper is out as Joe goes for the pin...The ref counts 1-2-3...Joe gets the upset.
Wave, Raekwon, and Boy Toy come to the ringadn start pounding on Joe... Joe yells out "Souey!!!" and about 15 hillbillies rush the ring and circle
Slick Rick's stable...Paw takes the mic: "Looks like another ho-down tonight!!!"...They close the cirlce in and start pounding on Slick
Rick's stable...The officials clear ringside... WINNER COTTON-EUE JOE BY PIN -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gary: "Another upset...Joe has quite a big family!!!" Debut match: Mortician vs. The Body Guard Mortician dominated this one early as he nailed Guard with a wicked clothesline...He then gives Guard with a legdrop across the head...He motions for his finisher the "Burial plot" choke slam and nails Guard with it...He then gives Guard another burial plot adn then steps on his chest...
The ref counts 1-2-3...He then grabs the mic: "Grim Reaper...I have traveled far to get you into the ring...Now you give me more reason, since you are the world champ!!!I am the true master of fear...I saw your fellow stable mates in the NHBWA...Matter of fact I battled Raekwon in the ring there...Reaper it's time to pay the piper...The toll is that belt you wear.
Look into the true face of fear...I challenge you to a title match on the next stop on the Power Tour...Do you accept my challenge..."
WINNER MORTICIAN BY PIN -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Scott: "Mortician looks impressive in his debut..." Net championship match: (Requested) Champion: King Guy Newman vs. challenger: Gary "OG" Trent This was a great opportunity for the gangsta...Trent was in control for most of the match but both wrestlers had several near falls...King caught him with a clothsline sending him to the mat...The King then motioned for his finisher, the "royal finish" piledriver...Trent is out...The King then goes for another royal finish, sending him to the mat again...He was going fro a 3rd royal flush when Gordan came in to help his tag-team partner...
The ref motioned for the bell as Newman ran out of the ring... WINNER AND STILL CHAMPION KING GUY NEWMAN BY DQ -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Todd: "Newman manages to hold on his belt for at least a couple more days! That's it for another Power Tour card...Goodnight!!!" -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Guys, Sorry that this card is out late and broken up into 5 parts but this vax account really limits the way I can post and when I can post...Send me some requests and Trash talk...Thanks again...
