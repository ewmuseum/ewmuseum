WNWF Back-talk

20 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BACK TALK =========================================================================== (The camera aims at Gordan Low sitting at a neon-lit desk) Gordan: "Greetings fans and welcome to another edition of WNWF Back-talk... This is a chance to hear from some of the atheletes of the WNWF...Today we have Masked Mayhem responding to his title loss at Spring Fling, Pain
Factory talking about their debut loss to ER, and the stripping of
Emblamer's world title by president Todd Spencer...Looks like a hot one today...Let's get to the Back-talk..."
Former national champ Masked Mayhem to current champ: Brian Wallace: Manager, "Legend" Josh Brown: "You (beep)ing theif!!!If you think stealing the title is anyway to get a (beep)ing match then you got it!!!Mayhem is
(beep)ed off and you made the mistake of making it a 60 minute iron-man match...That gives Masked Mayhem 60 minutes to beat your (beep)...As for the cage thing, I say "no!"...I want a piece of you, you little (beep)!!!
PUt us in the same cage!!!" Masked Mayhem: "What is this (beep)...Nobody steals my (beep)ing belt...You and me; 60 minutes...That is going to be the worst 60 minutes of your
(beep)ing life!!!" Pain Factory to ER: Manager, "Neon" Leon Parker: "I don't believe it!!!I don't (beep)ing believe it!!!My boys were ready and willing to make loser quiche out of ER
It was our WNWF debut and the Pain Factory was looking to make an impact...
They get in the match; they dominate; and then due to biased officiating, my boys get diqualified...What is goin' on???ER was cheating like a couple of madmen and the refs looked the other way...However when Alex saves Andrew from being double-teamed, the ref gives the match to the two guys who use more illegal moves than the OAKLAND Raiders?!?"
Alex "The Hammer" Simpson: "C'mon ER!!!C'mon!!!We want another sho at you jerks!!!And if you're real men you'll accept our challenge!!!"
"Acid" Andrew Downey: "Breakin' arms has its charms and we intend to charm you 2 suckers until you are wailing like a couple of babies!!!"
Gordan: "Just from the president...The Masked Mayhem/Brian Wallace will take place this weekend in a special Weekend Warriors card...It will be a double-cage/no-DQ match for the title...Mayhem and Wallace will be in one cage while "Legend" and Starks are in a separate cage stacked on top of the first one...The first one to get a pin, then will get to enter the cage of his partner to help him to victory...Also signed is the much anticipated re-match between Pain Factory and ER...However, this will be a no-dq match to get sure winner...
(Camera zooms to monitor depicting after match mele between Embalmer and former 2x national champ, Mark Destructo right after Spring Fling concluded.
During this attack, no only did Embalmer severely injure Destructo but he also injured several ring officials and security guards...)
Todd: "We have good news for some in the WNWF...The world championship has just become vacant and I'm taking match requests for a shot at it this weekend...That attack on Destructo and the officials was totally uncalled for...Not only was his belt stripped, he was then placed in "jail"...We will not tolerate such behavior in the WNWF...Now we have The Boy Toy,
Grim Reaper, Tidal Wave, Raekwon, Slick Rick, and him are occupying the the jails cells...How about this...One big jailhouse match between them to]
see which one of them can be pardonned...This will be a no-dq cage match.... Should be fun considering Toy, Raekwon, Wave, and Reaper are in the same stable..That match will also take place this weekend..."
Gordan: "Just in...Newly crowned extreme champ, Moo Kane might also lose his title...He hasn't been in contact with WNWF staff since his victory and he is not meeting his contractual obligations for holding a champion- ship...Kane if you're out there please let us at the WNWF know what you are up to...Same goes for the rest of the belt holders...It seems that all
(beep) broke lose after the Spring Fling supercard and the champs are no where to be found...These titles have to defended guys..."
