WNWF Spring Fling Supercard

The World News Wrestling Federation...Spring Fling...Live from Hong Kong... --------------------------------------------------------------------------- (The camera pans around the stadium packed with over 100,000 screaming
WNWF fans...They are here to chear on their very own Moo Kane in his quest to bring the extreme championship home to them...The camera then zooms to the texedo clad Todd Spencer, president of WNWF; Gary Valis; and Scott
Pullman at ringside...)
Todd: "Welcome fans to another great supercard here in the WNWF...It's time for Spring Fling, coming at you like a run-away train live from
Hong Kong...Let's see tonight's awesome line-up...(The camera pans to
WNWF blimp hovering over the stadium)...
Spring Fling Part I: Debut: Saint Bruno vs. "Imperium" Christian North Debut: Chow Min vs. Assasin Bunker match: the Sanctuary vs. The Masters of Destruction w/Slick Rick 4-corners match: Morman Faith vs. Goliath vs. Brian Damage vs. Mark Destructo Todd: "Let's get to the action!!!" Debut: Saint Bruno vs. "Imperium" Christian North Bruno enters the ring to "Hard as a Rock---AC/DC" with his manager, Fred
Scuttle...He is a big man concealing his face behind a mask...North enters to the sound of trumpets harolding with his mamager/valet Lady Diana...He is a big man at 7'4"; muich larger than the 6'8" Bruno...The action starts at the ring of the bell with both wrestlers locking up...Bruno hits him wth a hard clothesline but the big guy won't go down...Lady Di cheers on...
He tries again and nails North in the gut with a boot...He then sends him to the mat head-frist with a piledriver...Bruno motions to the crowd for his finisher, the "crunch" torture rack...He puts North up on his shoulders and squeezes his legs and head...North submits after 15 seconds and the ref calls for the bell...Bruno throws him down to the mat...Lady Di runs into the ring to tend to her man as the ref raises Bruno's hand in victory...
WINNER SAINT BRUNO BY PIN --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gary: "That was quite a debut for the Saint...Now let's go to another debut from his sometime tag partner; Chow Min..."
Debut: Chow MIn vs. 1/2 of Destruction Inc: Assasin Bruno met Min at the ring with their manager Fred to the Japanese national anthem...Min is dressed in ninja attire and cartwheels to the ring...
Assasin comes to the ring with his tag partner; Destroyer to the sound of a wrecking ball hitting a brick wall...He is wearing red tights with green
Destruction Inc. letters down the legs...The bells rings and both wrestlers lock up...Assasin sends him into the ropes and connects with a perfect frankensteiner...Assasin picks him up and sends him back into the ropes...
He nails Min with a wicked drop kick...Fred and Bruno complain to the ref. Min slowly gets to his feet...Assasin runs toward him but gets a boot to the face...Min motions for his finisher, the "kamikaze" powerbomb and sends him to the mat back-first...He goes for the pin...1-2...Assasin kicks up...
Assasin slowly gets to his feet...Min kicks him in the gut and goes for another kamakaze and sends him back to the mat...He goes for the pin...
The ref counts 1-2-3...Destroyer enters the ring and challenges Min, while
Bruno steps between them...Assaisn reminds Destroyer of their tag match for the belts later on the card...Detruction Inc. clears out and the ref raises Min's hand in victory...
WINNER CHOW MIN BY PIN --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Scott: "Another impressive debut...I thought we were going to see D Inc. a little early...I'm glad they came to their senses...Our next match is one so brutal that president is leaving the building...He will not sanction this one...It's that bunker grudgematch between The former tag champs; The Sanctuary and the 2x former champs: Masters of Destruction.."
Bunker match: The Sanctuary (Olvan/Destroyer) vs. M.O.D. (Tidal Wave/Raekwon) Destroyer and Olvan came to the ring to the sound of a bomb exploding... Invader and former world champ and stable mate Ruthless came down to the ring with them...Wave and Raekwon were escorted from their cells by 15 security guards dressed in riot gear to "Enter Sandman--Metallica"...The barbed wire cage was lowered over the ring after the 2 teams were in it...
The cages with the weapons were lowered over the 4 corners of the cage... The bell rings and all 4 wrestlers go at it...The first one to get the pin via a camera 3-count wins...Wave catches Olvan with a clothesline and then suplexes him into the side of the cage...Olvan's leg scrapes along the barb wire around the top and starts bleeding...Raekwon sends Destroyer into the corner hard and then gives him a belly-to belly suplex...Olvan is limping and holding his bleeding leg...Olvan gives Wave a suset flip...Destroyer slowly gets to his feet and nails Raekwon with a boot to the gut...He then gives him a wicked spinning powerbomb...Olvan is climbing the cage and going for a weapon...He makes it to the top and opens one of the boxes...He pulls out a set of brass knuckles and puts them on...He jumps back into the ring and nails Wave with a right cross opening a huge cut in his head...
Destroyer gives Raekwona thrust kick, sending him into the side of the cage hard...wave slowly gets to his feet...His cut on his head is bleeding...
He runs at Olvan and gives him a wicked clothesline...He then drops one of his big legs across his head...He tries to take the brass knuckles off
Olvan's hands...Raekwon puts Destroyer in the cobra twist...He breaks the hold...Olvan jumps to his feet and nails Wave with an upercut causing him to spit blood and teeth...He connects again with a left hook...Wave is out...
He motions for his finisher, the "sweet dreams" sleeper...The camera zooms in to try to end this one...Wave is out...Olvan goes for the pin...The fans scream out 1-2-3...The bell rings but Destroyer and Raekwon are still fighting...They lock up...Destroyer kicks Raekwon in th egut and motions for his finisher, the "total destruction" power bomb and sends him to the mat...He goes for the pin...The fans scream out 1-2-3...The cage is raised and the battered M.O.D. are led back to their cells...
WINNERS THE SANCTUARY BY PIN --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gary: "What a brutal match...Here comes WNWF president, Todd Spencer...You missed a good one, Mr. Spencer..."
Todd: "I said it before I will not condone those types of matches (right). They are too brutal and could lead to the career end of those involved... I'm glad that one is over...Let's move on..." ***Look for Part II next*** Spring Fling Part II --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Todd: "We're back fans...Here are the matches that will be fought during part II:" Spring Fling Part II 4-croners match: Morman Faith vs. Goliath vs. Brian Damage vs. Mark Destructo Baseball Bat match: Typo*** How about Net championship: Champion: Natureboy Danny Kulba vs. Challenger: Ruthless Baseball bat match: Bane vs. Cotton-eye Joe w/Paw Extreme championship: Triangle of Pain Champion: Mortician vs. Invader vs. Moo "Awesome Lion" Kane Todd: "Let's continue the action!!!" 4-corners match: Morman Faith vs. Goliath vs. Brian Damage vs. Mark Destructo The bell rang as all 4 men finally entered the ring...Brian Damage attacks faith with a spin kick sending him spinning in the air...Goliath then grabs
Faith and puts him in a arm lock...Faith kicks Damage as he is trying to clothesline both out of the ring...Damage slowly gets to his feet and goes after Goliath...He nails Goliath with his finisher, the "death sentence"
flying clothesline...He goes for the pin...The ref counts 1-2-3...Goliath is the first one eliminated...He rolls out of the ring...Destructo goes after Damage...He nails Damage with a wicked powerbomb...Damage slowly gets to his feet and gives Destructo a pwerbomb of his own...Faith nails Damage from behind and gives him a headbutt...Faith slams Damage's and Destructo's heads together...Faith goes after Damage again...He gives him another head- butt opening up a cut...Damage slowly gets to his feet and motions for his finisher for Destructo, the "death sentence" flying clothesline and nails Destructo...He goes for the pin...The ref counts 1-2-3...Destructo is the 2nd one out...Faith and Damage are the only ones left...Faith nails him with a clothesline...Faith then gives him a belly-to-belly suplex, setting him up for his avalanche finisher...He crashes down on Damage's chest...The ref counts 1-2-3...The Hermit, his manager comes into the ring to congratulate him...
WINNER MORMAN FAITH BY PIN --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Scott: "Another great match!!!Now it's time for the 1st of the championship matches this evening...It will be for The King's net championship...Let's get to the ring..."
Net championship: Champion: Natureboy Danny Kulba vs. Challenger: Ruthless Ruthless comes to the ring in his trademark red and blue tights to the tune of "Eruption--VanHalen"...Kulba jumps out of the crowd and starts beating on him...He gives him a "royal flush" piledriver on the concrete...It seems that Ruthless is out...Kulba enters the ring and grabs the mic: "You see I am the best damn wrestler in the WNWF...Ruthless you didn't have a chance..."
Ruthless slowly gets to his feet...Kulba points at him...Ruthless tries to get into the ring but Kulba helps him in with a suplex...Kulba stomps on his chest...Former champ: King Guy Newman comes down to ringside...Ruthless finds the ropes and the ref calls for the break...They lock up...Kulba gives him a double hook powerbomb and sends him back down hard...Kulba plays to the crowd...Ruthless sets him up and nails him with running power- slam...Kulab rolls outside where he is met by Newman...Newman nails him with the "royal flush" pile driver that he stole from him...The King sends him to the concrete head-first...Kulba is out...Ruthless exits the ring and ends up behind Newman...He taps Newman on the shoulder and then nails him with an elbow to the head...He then powerbombs Newman to the concrete...He picks up Kulba and drags him into the ring...He goes for the pin...The ref counts 1-2...Kulba kicks out...Kulba finds the corner and gets to his feet.
They lock up...Kulba goes for a german suplex and puts him down back first.. The ref counts 1-2-3...Ruthless kicks out...He is mad...The ref raises
Kulba's hand in victory...Ruthless taks the belt and nails him with it, knocking him out...He leaves the ring in disgust...

Bane and former net champ: Cotton-eye Joe..." Baseball bat match: Bane vs. Cotton-eye Joe Bane enters the ring wearing his trademark black mask with his manager,
John Robertson...Cotton-eye Joe enters the ring with his manager/father,
Paw...The bell rung and the action began...Joe made the first attack...
He put Bane in a texas cloverleaf...Bane is screaming in pain...He throws
Bane into the ropes and connects with a clothesline...The bat is lowered into the ring...Joe ignores the bat and goes for his finisher, the face- first powerbomb...He goes for the pin...The ref counts 1-2..Kickout!
Joe picks him up...Joe gives him a DDT...He goes for the pin...The ref counts again 1-2-3...Joe now grabs the bat and starts beating Bane with it.
Paw comes into the ring and starts stomping on Bane too...Bane is bleeding bad as the officials clear the ring...Bane is taken out of the ring in a stretcher...
WINNER COTTON-EYE JOE BY PIN --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Gary: "That was dispicable...They should be locked up...Let's go to our match at Morticians request it will be a Triagle of Pain for the Extreme championship..."
Extreme championship: Triangle of Pain: Champion: Mortician vs. Invader vs. Moo "Awesome Lion" Kane This one started imediately after the bell was rung...Invader goes after the champ, nailing him with a savate kick...Invader slams Mortician's and
Kane's head together...Invader motions for shi finisher, the "invasion" and goes for the submission...Mortician gives up after 34 seconds...he rolls out of the ring...Kane and Invader lock up...The baseball bat is lowered into the ring...Invader grabs the bat and nails Kane in the head...Kane finds the ropes...Invader catches him with the end of the bat in the ribs...
Kane is in Pain...Kane slowly gets to his feet, holding his ribs...Kane nails Invader with his finisher, the head take kick...He then drags Invader over to the corner and gives him his new finisher, the "awesome bomb" and goes for the pin...The ref counts...1...Kickout! Kane sets him up in the corner...He then nails him with a flying sidekick...He goes for the pin again...The ref counts 1-2-3...Invader is out...The ref hands Kane the
Extreme belt and raises his hand in victory...Mortician's voice can be heard over the loudspeakers..."Lion we will meet again...I will take back my championship...Look into the ture face of fear!!!"

The ref counts 1-2...Kick-out!!!Executioner gives him a backbreaker and calls for the count again...The ref counts 1-2...Kickout!!!He puts Warrior in an armbar...They lock up...Warrior gives him a rocker dropper...

Warrior sends him into the ropes and nails him with a flying clothesline... He then motions for his finisher, the "warrior splash" and climbs to the top...He splashes down across Executioner's chest...He goes for the pin...
The ref counts 1-2-3...The ref raises his hand in victory...The crowd cheers... Wallace picks him up and gives him a DDT...Josh is yelling at the ref... Mayhem slowly gets to his feet...Josh is on the ring apron and Wallace goes over to yell at him but is caught from behind with a clothesline, sending him out of the ring...Josh begins to hit him with his cane...Tony
