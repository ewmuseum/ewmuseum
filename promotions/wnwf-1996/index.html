Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World News Wrestling Federation grew out of a December 1995 recruitment call and was running twice-weekly cards by January 1996, billing itself, in its own entry-sheet post, as somewhere “the animals play.”

Its early roster split into feuding stables — the Sanctuary, Slick Rick's Masters of Destruction, Destruction Inc., Canadian Carnage, the Michigan Gangstas — contesting five championships (World, National, a Net/television title, a tag title and an Extreme title) at once, on two separate weekly shows the promotion folded into a single card, Wednesday Live Slam, within its first month.

That March it launched a touring gimmick, the Power Tour, staging cards through Europe and Asia and having its own on-air president field mail-in title challenges from the roster city by city. The tour closed on Spring Fling, staged live from Hong Kong before a crowd its own broadcast put past 100,000, which put four of its five championships on the line in one night, including a “Jail House match” for the World Championship that Grim Reaper won from Embalmer. When Embalmer attacked ringside officials after his loss, the promotion's own president stripped his belt and had him thrown into storyline “jail” alongside three of his own stablemates, setting up a jailhouse cage match among the four of them to decide who could be “pardoned.”

Its own operator called a hiatus on 27 April 1996, admitting plainly that postings and interest had both dried up and floating a move to a private, e-mail-only format “like it was at the beginning.” No further card, and no return, survives in the record.
