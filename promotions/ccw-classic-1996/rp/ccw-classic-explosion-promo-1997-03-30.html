Classic Championship Wrestling — Roleplay

"CCW Explosion!!" Promo

25 March 1997 (for a card dated 30 March 1997)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[there is a black screen and no sound what so ever as out of the blackness, fades in the words, in white letters, "SUNDAY, MARCH 30th!!" Then they fade out as fades in the words "8 MEN!!" and in the same fashion they fade out, as in fade the words "1 BELT!!" and then the screen goes to black again and then suddenly the scene cuts over to a shot of what appears to be footage from the Hiroshima bombing as a HUGE mushroom cloud fills the screen and a deep voice is heard] CCW EXPLOSION!! LIVE Only on PAY PER VIEW March 30th, 1997, from the Joe Louis arena in Detroit, Michigan!!! 8 Men, 1 Belt!
[the scene then cuts over to a shot of the Explosion!! logo spinning around] 8 of the greatest CCW Superstars will step into the ring in a winner take all, single elimination tournament to crown the FIRST ever CCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!! Participants include...
[scene cuts to a shot of Jerry Anderson standing in the ring along with his manager, former wrestler, "The Rocket" Jeff Wrobelski] The lost horseman...JERRY ANDERSON!!
[then cuts to a shot of Terrence Daniels applying the Clover Leaf to "Roadkill" Len Griffon] The CCW's own Giant Slayer...TERRENCE DANIELS!!
[it then cuts to a shot of "The Fantasy" Brett Young standing victorious after his win over "Bad Boy" Mark Bagwell] The legend..."The Fantasy" Brett Young!!
[the scene then cuts over to a shot of Desmond Harrison applying the Heisman Piledriver to "GQ" Geoffrey Quartz] All-American...DESMOND HARRISON!!
[then shows X.Plosion leveling "Boardwalk" Bradley Warden with The Mushroom Cloud] World Champion Elect...X.PLOSION!!
[then a shot of "The Roadkill" Len Griffon yelling into the camera] "ROADKILL" LEN GRIFFON!!
[then cuts to a shot of "The Man of a Million Holds" Chris Kanne standing in the ring] The man who re-wrote the book on technical wrestling..."THE MAN OF A MILLION HOLDS" CHRIS KANNE!!
[and then cuts to a shot of "IQ" Ian Quartz eliminating his brother "GQ" Geoffrey Quartz from the Wild Card Battle Royal] And the man who eliminated 9 other men to be here..."IQ" IAN QUARTZ!!
[then another shot of the Mushroom Cloud from Hiroshima] All these men will compete one on one for the most coveted prize of all!!! The WORLD Title!!
[scene cuts to the World belt then another shot of the Hiroshima Mushroom cloud] Plus...a bitter score will be settled. When "GQ" Geoffrey Quartz...
[scene cuts to a shot of Geoffrey Quartz pinning Ricky Revolution to become the first ever CCW WEST COAST CHAMPION!!] .. puts his WEST COAST TITLE on the line against "The IronMan" Bill MacRagge!!
[scene shows both men on a split screen] While caught in the middle is Bill's sister the lovely Amy MacRagge!!
[the two split screens seperate and she is in the middle] GQ claims to hold the key to her heart. Is he truthful or does Brother Know Best?!?
[another shot of the Mushroom Cloud] Also...in a tag battle that's been talked about for months.
[scene shows Fire & Ice standing in the ring after defeating the East Coast Killers] FIRE & ICE are set to battle...
[cuts over to Murder Inc. standing with the rest of the House of Hurt behind them] MURDER INC. In a bitter rivalry between two of the greatest tag teams ever seen. And the score WILL be settled. But...now that Murder Inc. hold the coveted tag straps...
[scene shows them beating the Devestation Express to become the first ever tag team champions] ...the stakes have been raised to a NEW level!! FIRE & ICE VS. MURDER INC. FOR THE WORLD TAG TEAM TITLES!!
[again the Mushroom cloud is seen] Plus...one man claims to be the rightful champion...
[scene shows "The Bullet" Ricky Revolution laying on a stretcher holding his knee screaming in pain] ...while the other holds the gold!!
[shows "Boardwalk" Bradley Warden pinning Ricky Revolution to become the first ever CCW InterNational Champion] He's fought for a rematch!!
[shows Ricky Revolution pinning Robert E. Storm] While He's proven himself...a champion!!
[shows Bradley Warden pinning X.Plosion] And when they face this time, the rightful champ WILL be proclaimed!! "BOARDWALK" BRADLEY WARDEN DEFENDS HIS INTERNATIONAL TITLE AGAINST "THE BULLET" RICKY REVOLUTION!!
[another shot of the Mushroom Clould] And as a special bonus...a Special Edition of the "Fox's Den" hosted by wrestling legend "The Starfox" Curt Crane, with Special guest "Sexy" Sally McClane!!
[show's Curt Crane standing holding his ICW Wrestler of the Year trophy and then fades to a shot of "Sexy" Sally McClane standing, wearing a bikini decorated like the American Flag]
[then another shot of the Mushroom Cloud] And on the PreShow...a SPECIAL Women's Exhibition Match, booked by the GRAND DRAGON WRESTLING ALLIANCE Women's federation featuring two of their up and coming stars!! The athletic...CRIMSON!!
[shows Crimson talking into the camera] And the sexy and talented..."SEXY" SALLY MCCLANE!!
[another shot of the Mushroom Cloud as then the CCW EXPLOSION!! logo fades in] All this and MANY more surprises...don't miss it LIVE ONLY ON PAY PER VIEW!! Order Now by contacting [detail withheld] Don't miss out!!
[scene then fades to black once more as in white letters fade in that read "CCW Explosion!!" and then they fade out as fade in the words "8 MEN!!" and then in the same manner they fade out and in fade the words "1 BELT!!" as then the scene fades to black and the words "ORDER NOW" appear at the bottom and once again the address [detail withheld] is shown as it fades to black once more] Copyright 1997, Classic Championship Wrestling
