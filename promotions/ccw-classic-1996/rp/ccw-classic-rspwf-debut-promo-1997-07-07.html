Classic Championship Wrestling — Roleplay

Classic Championship Wrestling Promo

7 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[the scene sets for a few moments on a black screen, until the Logo for Classic Championship Wrestling fades in, and a slow instrumental version of Jimi Hendrix's "Voodoo Childe" begins to play quietly in the back ground, as then a deep voiced, monologue begins] "It all began SIX months ago."
[the logo fades out as, in white letters the words "Jan. 18th, 1997" fade in, and then they fade out, only to be replaced by the words "First Night"] "CCW's First Night, began a tradition in pro wrestling. It began a new era, of something different, and something new, but yet something completely and utterly....CLASSIC!"
[the scene then fades to a jampacked arena] "Fans from all over the world have witnessed the classic style, and awesome action that the CCW represents"
[the scene then cuts to a shot of former CCW World Champion Desmond Harrison standing in the ring with the World Title around his waist, then fades in to a shot of the current champion, X.Plosion] "And...NOW..."
[the scene quickly cuts to a shot of "The Bullet" Ricky Revolution and "GQ" Geoffrey Quartz fighting with Bill MacRagge and "The Shocker" Brian Anthony on the outside, as a part of the special tag team grudge match on CCW FanFest] "The ENTIRE wrestling world can witness that which is the greatness of Classic Championship Wrestling."
[scene cuts to a shot of the World Title, then fades to The International Title and then goes through every other belt in the federation] "Because for the first time ever, CCW will be broadcast LIVE on RSPWF, for everyone to view!"
[the scene then fades in to show a black and white shot of the former CCW West Coast Champion "GQ" Geoffrey Quartz, just after he won the title at First Night] "And the tradition of CCW, can finally be known."
[the scene then fades to black once more, as the CCW Logo fades in and then the screen goes black] Copyright 1997, Classic Championship Wrestling Watch our RSPWF debut card this Wednesday!! (special thanks to [name withheld] for writing this promo)
