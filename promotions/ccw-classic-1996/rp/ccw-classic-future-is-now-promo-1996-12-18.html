Classic Championship Wrestling — Roleplay

"CCW: THE FUTURE IS NOW!!!"

18 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
__ __ / / \ / \__ \__ \/\/ CLASSIC CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING THE FUTURE IS NOW!!! (*CCW LOGO FLASHES ACROSS THE SCREEN IN FRONT OF A STAR LIT BACKGROUND. AS THE A SOFT MELODIC TUNE IS PLAYED IN THE BACKGROUND. AND AN ANNOUNCER BEGINS TO SPEAK*) AFTER MANY DECADES OF THE SAME OLD IN WRESTLING, THE WORLD WAS IN NEED OF SOMETHING NEW, SOMETHING DIFFERENT, BUT YET, SOMETHING CLASSIC!!! (*LIGHTNING STRIKES DOWN THROUGH THE BACKGROUND, SENDING VOLTS THROUGH THE LOGO, UNTIL IT EXPLODES, AS THE SOFT MUSIC SEEMS TO ALMOST FADE INTO A HEAVIER BEAT, THAT IS ALMOST IDENTICAL TO THAT OF "THE BEAUTIFUL PEOPLE" BY MARILYN MANSON. AND AS THE LOGO EXPLODES THE CAMERA SEEMS TO RUSH THROUGH THE STARS AS IF THE VIEWER IS TRAVELING THROUGH SPACE AT HIGH SPEEDS, AND THE DEEP VOICED ANNOUNCER AGAIN BEGINS TO SPEAK*) CLASSIC CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING IS HERE, AND WITH IT THE FUTURE OF WRESTLING. EVERYONE'S JUMPING ON THE BANDWAGON OF THE GREATEST WRESTLING FED. TO COME ON THE SCENE EVER. TWO EXPLOSIVE CARDS EACH WEEK, AN UPDATE ON HOUSE SHOWS EACH MONTH, EXCITING PAY PER VIEWS, AND THE GREATEST ACTION EVER!!! CHECK IT OUT, WHEN IT TAKES THE WRESTLING WORLD BY STORM IN JANUARY OF '97!!! (*THE STARS BEGIN TO SLOW DOWN AS THEY PASS BY THE CAMERA AND AT A FAR DISTANCE, A BRIGHT GLOWING LIGHT CAN BE SEEN. AND AS THE CAMERA GETS CLOSER IT CAN BE MADE OUT TO BE THE WORDS "THE FUTURE IS NOW!!!" REFLECTING A GREAT LIGHT. AND AS IT GETS EVEN CLOSER THE CAMERA VIEW COMES TO A JOLTING HALT AND THE ANNOUNCER YELLS*) THE FUTURE IS NOW!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! *the following announcement paid for by Classic Championship Wrestling*
