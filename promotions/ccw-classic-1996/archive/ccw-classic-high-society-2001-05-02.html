Critique please... Tag Team.

2 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey RSPW-F'ers, Would really appreciate a critique for this new tag team that I am introducing into the CCW. Any opinions, views, suggestions, or advice and feelings are appreciated. On a side note, the CCW is seriously lacking a tag division. If interested, please e-mail me, I'll forward the info. We could use the competition.
The following roleplay was on the last flash show, split into 3 parts. Warning, it is rather lengthy.
***** JM: Welcome to the show, and boy did we witness a GREAT CCW PAY PER
VIEW. On all cylinders this show was shooting great matches one after another and this weeks show seems no different as we welcome a few new Superstars to the CCW Roster.
DO: You know, Not all the matches at the Pay Per View went off well. There was no tag team champions being crowned. Ethan Hoover never allowed the match to happen and I'm thinking he's holding out for more tag teams…this next tag team, the newest CCW tag team has a match and we now have a chance at hearing them in…THREE, count them THREE separate segments. Gotta love it when People want to be seen and heard.
I expect big things from these guys. JM: I have to agree with you there. This team is showing a lot of potential. Let's hear from the team that will face Fire and Ice…High
Society.
-------------- High Society The camera fades in to a rooftop of a high rise building with a helipad. In the far off corner there is a small waiting room structure made of glass with a windsock whipping in the wind, blowing to the north. Inside we see a woman, Ms. Eve, flanked by two men. We see her walking back and forth talking into a cellphone, carrying a portfolio nestled under her right arm. She has dark hair, that is properly tied up, wears thin glasses and is smartly dressed in a professional grey business skirt suit and high heels. Standing next to her are two men, one much larger than the other. The larger white man, Mr. Poe, is dressed in simple business suit attire, with a buzz haircut, and dark shades. Taking his large hands he gently taps the woman on the shoulder saying, "Ms. Eve..." Concentrating on the phonecall, she raises her finger to signal 'just a second'. The black gentleman, Christopher
Black, standing next to the larger one is dressed in very fashionable back dress slacks and a long sleeve, collarless black dress shirt. He leans over to the larger gentleman who steps back and folds his hands, patiently waiting for Ms. Eve to finish her call, and asks in a heavy
British accent, "Hey Poe, who do you think that is?" With no expression on his face he simply shrugs and keeps his hands folded and assumingly his shaded eyes staring straight ahead.
As the woman continues to talk and nod towards the cellphone, a loud noise can be heard and the camera pans just over the shoulders of the group to see a corporate helicopter coming closer to the rooftop and circling around for an adequate landing. "Yes sir..." she continues, "Of course..." she says, backing away into the corner, covering her ears so she can hear clearly. "You can most certainly count on us to raise the standards of your division."
The helicopter hovers over the helipad for a few seconds and then begins to slowly descend onto the pad. The black gentleman opens the door to the waiting area and begins to walk towards the helicopter as its rotors slow and the engine can be heard winding down. Behind him, Mr. Poe opens the door and waits for Ms. Eve to walk through.... her attention still towards whoever is on the phone.
Opening the door to the helicopter, the pilot waves the well dressed black gentleman over to the door as two women come out from the cabin giggling and behind them follows a well tanned and dressed blonde haired gentleman, Steve Riq, whose smile is as wide as theirs. "Ladies, ladies..." he laughs, placing his arms around their fancy gowned waists, "Didn't I promise you everything was first class?" one hand holding a bottle, the other a glass. The women stumble forward giggling, obviously a little tipsy from the flight over.
Ushering the pilot away, Mr. Poe holds the door open as the two gentlemen shake each others hands and greet each other, the woman standing behind them. Ms. Eve stands several steps away and finishes her conversation, "Thank you Mr. Hoover... you won't be disappointed." She flips the phone closed and then drops it in her suit jacket pocket.
Rolling her eyes in disgust as she watches the gentlemen flirt with the women, she stands there tapping her toes patiently waiting for them to get back to settle their business with the women. After a few seconds unnoticed, she glares at Mr. Poe, who then loudly clears his throat in front of the gentlemen. The two look at Mr. Poe, who then motions with a slight head movement towards Ms. Eve who does not look amused. The white gentleman sighs, and points to the glass building, "Ladies, we'll have the limo take you to your hotels...", Mr. Poe falls in behind the women and ushers them off as Ms. Eve stands, still annoyed.
She takes her glasses off and takes a H.S. monogramed silk clothe out to clean them, as she looks at the white gentleman straight in the eye, "Mr. Riq, you are always late - unlike your partner Mr. Black...
why is that?" "I know how to have fun..." he laughs as Chris Black joins him and lets a chuckle or two out as his partner slaps him on the back, rubs him on his bald head and they begin to walk to the building, Ms. Eve taking her steps in front and leading them to the elevator.
She places her glasses back on, and turns to Steve Riq, "Provided so long as you keep business and pleasure separate and them on even courses, you know I have no problems with your amusement and entertaining."
Steve Riq stops and waves his hands in the air, "Now wait a second
Eve..." "Ms. Eve." she exclaims. "We are business partners." He nods and continues, "Ms. Eve... but you know I wasn't in Japan to just have fun. For God's sake, you're the one who sent me there.'"
Ms. Eve stops and turns around and hands Steve Riq the large portfolio she held under her arms, just before they enter the building,
"And for good reason too. You're the newest tag team in the CCW."
Steve opens the portfolio and looks through the papers as the wind blows them and ruffles them in his fingers, "We are?" He flips through them some more, "When did we sign this?"
"She just finished the deal today, Steve." answers Chris Black, opening the door for his friend and tag team partner.
They continue to walk through the building and into the elevator as
Ms. Eve continues. "Mr. Black is right, you are the newest tag team in the CCW as of today." Chris Black leans forward and presses the button for the elevator to go down as Steve Riq continues to read the papers.
"High Society is the CCW's newest Tag Team.", Ms. Eve announces with a smirk on her face as the doors to the elevator close.
End of Pt. 1 ------- [They come back to the studios where the crowd waves their signs and cheers. Jason MacManaman begins to speak.]
JM: We're back and I do have to say the means by which Ethan Hoover is getting these new tag teams amazes me. I just hope he knows what he's getting into with this new team.
DO: Let's face facts, the CCW NEEDS TAG TEAMS, the better the wrestlers the better the product, who cares where you get them as long as they can wrestle, they're fine by me. Plus I saw nothing wrong with them they look very professional especially Ms. Eve. Whew….
-snip- ----------------------------------- High Society part 2... The camera fades in to an elevator as we see Ms. Eve standing in front of High Society International, Chris Black and Steve Riq.
"Now wait a second..." Steve Riq looks over one sheet of paper specifically, "I thought the deal was strictly for High Society to be an overseas deal only. This CCW is on the North American continent."
"At first it was." Ms. Eve reaches into the pile of papers and pulls out 2 or 3 sheets of bar graphs and points to them as she speaks,
Chris Black leaning over in interest as well. "Quite obviously we made more more money in the beginning as a traveling superstar team with no ties to any league for any specific time. We made our money by the bundles and left when there was nothing else to make."
Confused by what he's looking at, Steve shrugs, "So what's the point?" Ms. Eve lets out a frustrated sigh as she continues to point to certain parts of the paper, "The point is we can make more money now by being involved with a continental promotion. In fact, the leagues are rather open now to having their athletes perform elsewhere, for example... like I'll show you here - Mr. Lewis Spector has made his career blossom rather well from branching out from the CCW into other territories. Look at his earnings, and the rather impressive list of titles he holds, at once." Chris Black nods his head, "Makes sense
Steve."
"Wait, wait, wait... that's their singles roster. We're a tag team. Look, they only have, 1... 2... 3 tag teams?" He starts to laugh and shake his head. "3 tag teams? That's it? How are we going to make merchandising profits or generate any revenue wrestling the same 3 teams over and over? The fans will have no interest."
"You wanted a challenge. Well here it is." Ms. Eve folds the portfolio up and tucks it under her arms once more. "The fact is, the
Northern American continent is the last place we have yet to tour and make an impact on. We've made as much money as possible touring Europe, the Orient, Africa, Australia and the South Americas as we could. This is the best way for us to take the next step..."
"But..." "But? But what? What's the problem Mr. Riq? Are you worried about wrestling the same teams over and over? I can think no better way of proving your worth as a tag team than doing that." The elevator rings and the doors open to a large lobby, down the end of the hall is a large wooden double door. Ms. Eve quickly exits the elevator and picks up her pace down the hall.
Behind her Steve Riq quickly follows, "That's not the problem..." behind him, Chris Black follows as well, quietly laughing to himself. "Tell me it can't be the fact that you're scared of the Coast to
Coast Connection, Fire and Ice, or the Reagans, is it?" she says, smirking to herself.
Shaking his head, "Hell no... we're the best tag team that the CCW has never seen!" Steve replies, "The Coast to
Coast will be toast....and we'll be sure to handle Fire and Ice no problem and as for the Reagans...." looking confused, "What do ex-presidents have to do with this.... were we invited to a White House dinner?"
"Ah! So you did watch those tapes I sent you in Japan then Steve..." "Told you, you should have watched them all the way through.", Chris whispers to Steve. Mr. Poe stands before the doors and opens them up for Ms. Eve, as she thanks him with a wave and walks into a giant boardroom with a huge oak table in the middle and several plush leather seats surrounding it.
Against the far wall is a large screen tv, surrounded by many rows of books and videos and in the opposing wall, is nothing but a wide open view of the city of New York. Mr. Poe closes the doors behind Steve and
Chris as they walk in, taking their seats as Ms. Eve motions for them to sit. She picks up a remote control as they get comfortable and turns on the tv before them, a video snaps into view and she turns up the volume glaring at Steve RIq, "It's a good thing I didn't let any of your promos go to CCW for airing."
On the screen we see Steve Riq and Chris Black in their wrestling attire, in front of an interview podium. Chris Black stands behind Steve
Riq as he holds a beer bottle in his hand and yells at the screen, "As for you Fire and Ice - we've heard all about you... big, strong - monsters of the mat... unbeatable!" Steve pauses for a second and takes a sip of his beer and then gargles with it. He makes an awful and disgusting noise and the spits on the floor. "If you're so strong, pick that up!"
The video pauses as Ms. Eve coldly glares at Steve Riq who does his best not to smile. Sitting across from him, Chris Black hides his smile behind his hand, covering his face and tightly bites his lip. She leans against the table and shakes her head, "What in the hell were you thinking Mr. Riq?"
"Look, I may have not watched all the tapes, all the way through, but I saw enough to know that this is the type of crap that these fans want and expect." pointing to the frozen frame. "It's garbage wrestling nowadays with garbage wrestlers and it's all crap. That's why I asked us to be an overseas team exclusively...
they understand and appreciate wrestling for what it really is." He leans back and scratches at his chin, "Besides, you didn't tell me we were going to do actual promos."
"Thank God I didn't..." Ms. Eve snaps. Chris Black leans forward in his chair and looks to his partner, tapping his fingers on the table, "I don't think so Steve. Let's watch those tapes again and the matches and I bet you, the crowd is just as thrilled with wrestling as it is with the garbage - I think the problem is - they don't have many wrestlers to show them what wrestling really is. Sure, I may be able to count on my one hand the good, pure wrestlers they have... but this could be a good opportunity for us to go in there... and show them something very different and give them what they really want."
Steve ponders it a moment, "You think so, Chris?" "I know so, Steve I think the reason these people are cheering for the chairs, the tables, the silliness is because no one is giving them anything to cheer about wrestling wise. I think we can change that and make something of their tag division to be proud of."
Ms. Eve walks back towards the wall and gathers several video tapes in order, "Besides, I have it on good word from Mr. Hoover there will be more competition coming in."
Holding his hand up and politely interrupting Ms. Eve, Chris Black continues, "And as always, we can always make challenges to the singles roster to bring it in to the Tag Teams... you know how we love showing them tag matches are a whole new world and not side shows like they often suggest, or a waste of card time."
"Ok, I'm all for it then." Steve says, nodding his head in agreement. "Good then..." Ms. Eve says as she fast forwards the video. "We have a lot of good fortune on our side. First off, the team we're facing, Fire and Ice has no idea we're a real team.
They probably think we're a bunch of local tackle dummies." Chris nods, "That is a huge advantage." "Second, we're not even on their official website yet... we're only announced as an upcoming match next week in California..."
"Steve, you get to enjoy your home beaches!" Chris says with a laugh. "Maybe we'll even have the home crowd advantage!" "So no one knows anything about us, but we can learn plenty about them..." Ms. Eve stops the video and plays it, as we see the tag team of
Fire and Ice doing their old finisher. First we see the Boston Crab, then we see the flying legdrop.
Steve sits straight up, "Hey thats our..." Chris cuts him off, "Don't worry, they don't use it any more." Ms. Eve turns around back from the video. "You have one week left gentlemen to learn. We have the advantage, let's not let it go to waste.
One week to do one promo that is a class act, and not the drivel I saw. Steve, you want so bad to show them good wrestling and high class - this is your chance." Steve and Chris both pull themselves up from their chairs and begin to walk towards the pile of tapes on the far wall as Ms. Eve stops them one more time, "And know this gentlemen. I didn't go to the CCW. The CCW came to us. Sir Ethan Hoover scouted the globe and hand picked you gentlemen as his first choice to lead their tag team division to a renaissance. You weren't accepted, you were chosen... the first ones he wanted. Rather than have one of their 'own'
as champions... they held the belts up for later determination, they were that disgusted with their own roster."
Ms. Eve takes the portfolio and stacks of videos and hands them over to
Chris and Riq, "Take that knowledge with you to the ring."
End of Pt. 2 ------------------ DO: Wow the newcomers in the CCW, sure plan to heat things up around here. Did you see how well the High Society is scouting other tag teams here in the CCW? I mean that's just uncanny!!!
-Snip- DO: While MacManaman is changing his pants [he laughs] Let's hear for the last time tonight from High Society. Man these guys are starting to get on my good side. Here we go!!!
----------------------------------- High Society Pt. 3 A grainy video image comes over the screen and we can see Sir Ethan
Hoover walking away from the ring in disgust as the image flashes once more we see the Reagans and Fire and Ice fighting in the ring. They're going at it toe to toe as the we see a video from moments before a Sir
Ethan Hoover motions for both The Reagans and Fire and Ice to shake hands. They look at each other with confusion, then shake hands and seconds later, all ell breaks loose as they all erupt into a fight. The scene flashes back to a close up of Sir Ethan Hoover as he walks away from the fracas in the ring and gazes back at the ring one more time with a look of pure disgust. The image slows down to a crawl and freezes on the face of Sir Ethan Hoover - his eyes red with anger and his lips curled in a near snarl, his nose crinkled in disdain. Over the frozen image, we can still hear the loud boos of the crowd and the confusion of the announcers booth as they continue to repeat 'no tag team title match?' to each other.
As the image slowly begins to fade, we here a voice say, "When a line is crossed, where do you then go?"
The video quickly changes to the area outside an arena, at the front ticket area and box office. We see a black man with 2 small children by his side, one who is wearing a "Coast to Coast Connection"
t-shirt and another who is tightly holding onto a poster that says
"Coast to Coast is #1" that is larger than he is The man looks very frustrated and leans towards the box office trying to talk to the customer service agent inside. The camera moves in closer so we can hear their conversation...
"Are you sure that the Coast to Coast Connection is going to be here tonight, I heard over the radio that they might not show." He looks towards his children and then back to the person inside, "It's very important that I know."
She shrugs and doesn't know what to say, "I haven't been given any word sir, but there is a notice on your tickets that the card is subject to change. If they don't show up, I offer my sincerest apologies..."
We're taken inside the arena by the video as the announcer in the middle of the ring goes over his usual welcome and then begins to announce that the Coast to Coast Connection, sadly won't be able to make it to tonight's event. The camera zooms in on the father we saw before as his two children are seen crying and he is seen with a rather upset look on his face, trying to make his two children feel better. "I'll buy you a Coast to Coast poster kids, I promise to make it up to you." he pleads to them. One child pouts and cries as the other says loudly, "I don't want a stupid poster of Coast to Coast! They suck!" he yells, stomping his feet.
The image begins to fade away as once more, we hear the same voice,
"When you can't depend on those you look to most, who do you turn to?"
The scene slashes away to the interior cabin of a corporate helicopter and we see the lovely manager of High Society International,
Ms. Eve and the large and fearsome looking Mr. Poe sitting against leather seats on one side, and the tag team of High Society itself,
Christopher Black and Steve Riq seated on the other side. Mr. Poe stands up and opens the door to the helicopter and holds it open and escorts
Ms. Eve down the stairs with a helping hand. She looks down at her watch as she continues to strut, "We're an hour early."
Christopher Black stands up and holds his hand against his bald head to keep the sun out of his eyes, "California, we're here." He looks around taking in the sight and breaths in deeply as he steps off the helicopter and onto the airports tarmac.
Behind him follows Steve Riq who grabs his jacket and steps off the helicopter. Mr. Poe stands behind him and helps him into his jacket and smooths down his collar. Steve Riq brushes himself off once more as Mr.
Poe hands him a briefcase. Steve Riq falls in pace behind Ms. Eve and
Christopher Black. The camera follows in behind Mr. Poe and up along his side, as he brings up the rear. Reaching into his jacket, Steve Riq pulls out a pair of designer sunglasses and slides them on, "It's simple." he says as he stops and stares straight at the camera, "You call the professionals."
As we watch them walk towards a waiting limo, we hear Ms. Eve's voice over. "There's a reason that this business is successful, because
I'm the CEO. I take no excuses from my business partners or associates.
High Society International's goals are quality and success." Mr. Poe opens the door for the three of them and as they load in the limo and drive away, Ms. Eve continues her voice over.
"I'm not surprised Sir Ethan Hoover went around the globe looking for the best Tag Team to bring to CCW and chose Steve Riq and
Christopher Black. That's how I chose them myself - I went around the world." The limo is then seen pulling up to an arena as Mr. Poe steps out from the passengers side of the driver's seat up front and then opens the door, letting Ms. Eve out first. Steve Riq, and then Christopher
Black follows. We see them walking into the arena as Ms. Eve continues her voice over, "What does it say when a commissioner refuses to see
Championship on the leagues own men? What does it say when the fans even begin to lose faith in their own favorites, the men that were here from day one? It means its time for a change. Time for someone to come in, and do things right. High Society International was called in to do a job, and to add a touch of class to the tag team division. We're going to show Sir Ethan Hoover and the CCW that he made the right call, he made the right decision."
The camera continues to follow High Society International until they come to their dressing rooms, the file in one by one, save for Mr.
Poe... who stands outside the door and pushes the camera away as the door closes shut. End of part 3. -------------- Thanks for reading folks! Any and all comments appreciated! RTT
