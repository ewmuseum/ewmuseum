NCCW — Press coverage

A third-party column's own NCCW press-conference transcript, 10 December 1999 (excerpt)

10 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Excerpt from a third-party wrestling "sheet" column, "2 Pieces of Monkey Crap," by-lined "the Sports Fan" [name withheld]; the column's own second half is an opinion piece about an unrelated promotion's business and is not reproduced here.]

First, let me provide you with a transcript of Alison De La Cruz at an NCCW press conference, and as usual, she's lying through her f*$*in' teeth.

[The reporters immediately recognize De La Cruz and quickly begin to fire the flash bulbs, trying to capture pictures of her. A woman steps out from the back, and begins to make her way to the podium. She and NCCW President Edwards exchange a handshake and a few words, before Edwards steps back and allows the podium to this woman. The tall Filipino woman stands regally before the podium wearing a tan turtleneck with matching suit jacket and skirt. Camera bulbs continue to fire off as she's given the cue to speak. She smiles as she straightens and takes a deep breath.]

Woman: Hello everyone, welcome to this special New Classic Championship Wrestling press conference. NCCW President Mark Edwards and the championship committee thought it prudent to alert the public as to what has been discussed behind closed doors for the last few weeks. Especially as we near the premier pay per view of the Holiday season, Fanfest '99, this is the ideal time to make this major announcement . . . But first, let me debunk any rumors that have hit the internet over the last few days since NCCW Superstars.

[She looks about the room, acknowledging journalists with nods and a wink.]

De La Cruz: As many of you know, my name is Alison De La Cruz and I am the former GDWA Vice President. As you know, the promotion folded a little over 1 and a half years ago after the owners fell into financial problems with the I.R.S. But to quash those unfounded rumors, no, the GDWA is not reopening. And no, I am N_O_T attempting to buy out the NCCW's parent company.

[Bulbs flicker and a slight murmur ripples across the room as Alison draws a deep breath and continues.]

De La Cruz: Now, let's deal with the facts. I've been engaged in ongoing discussions with the NCCW in consideration of opening a N E W division in this dominant men's promotion . . .

[Silence. The media scoots forward and mutters in anticipation of her next statements.]

De La Cruz: Mr. Richards and myself are officially soliciting the Wrestling community to ascertain if there is GENUINE interest in Women's Professional Wrestling in the greatest men's promotion on the planet today . . . NEW CLASSIC CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING!

[An uproar as Alison lightly taps the podium and continues.]

De La Cruz: Now . . . now, nothing is official just yet. We need to hear from rookies, veteran's and would be grapplers interested in getting involved in a *hypothetical* NCCW Women's Division. If you are a woman from Japan, Mexico, Canada, Europe or the United States or any other hot bed for pro wrestling — and are SERIOUS about involving yourself in Women's Wrestling in the NCCW, please contact President Richards . . . That is all, thank you!

[Dozens of reporters jump forward thrusting mic's at Alison De La Cruz as she walks off the set and waves goodbye. President Edwards whispers a few words to her, and she responds with a whisper of her own, before she disappears behind the curtain. President Edwards returns to the podium, an obviously pleased grin on his face.]
