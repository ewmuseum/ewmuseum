Welcome to the home of the NCCW, or New Classic Championship Wrestling! The NCCW is incorporating many of the fine traditions of great leagues before it like the CCW, but don't think for a minute it will simply be content to coast on the glories of the past. The NCCW wants to be seen as a young, fresh organization dedicated to taking quality e-wrestling into the next millenium, with eventful cards and solid angles. But it will only be as good as the handlers make it, so come on and make the fed the best on the net!

APPLICATION NOTES: We're currently the most desperate for cruiserweight and tag-team talent, so if you apply for one of those you have a better chance of getting in. We're not really looking for heavyweights right now. Also, THIS IS NOT A GUESTBOOK FED. If you go to the trouble of designing a wrestler and then drop out of the fed after one week because you only now realized it's not guestbook-based, you just wasted your own time (and ours). Please, read the rules!

As of January 16, 1999 we have 9 face heavyweight wrestlers, 7 heel heavyweight wrestlers, 6 face cruiserweight wrestlers, 3 heel cruiserweight wrestlers, 1 face tag team and 4 heel tag teams. Offer a type of wrestler or tag team we have less of and you may have a stronger chance of getting in.
