* Ricky Revolution unable to defend belt due to injury. Title declared vacant on 7/25/98 and a tournament is held over two weeks to declare new champion. Superstar defeats Suicyco in tournament final to take title.
** Coast-to-Coast, with perfect 4-0-0 record, leave league and forfeit belts to Untouchables after defeating them at July 4 PPV.
*** New Austrian Powers throw away titles immediately after defeating the Untouchables and call a tag team tournament to remove all doubt of titleholders' legitimacy. They are defeated in final round by GQ & Don Cameron.
**** The Insiders soon unmasked, revealing themselves to be none other than Les Canadians (Alain MacDonald and Marc Denis) who renamed themselves Dominatrix. When they left NCCW in January '99 a lottery tournament was held to decide new champions at the February PPV.
+ Aaron Bagwell forfeited the Cruiserweight title when he left NCCW shortly after, and a 4-way match for the belt was held to crown a new champion.
++ Gabriel threw away his title in the first week of 1999, and Tyro Marvel vs. Brian Johnson was held to decide a new TV champ.
+++ Scott Douglas vacated the title when he became Heavyweight Champ at the same time. Fontaine won the vacant title in a four corners ladder match against Tyro Marvel, Ryan Douglas & Brandon Smith.
