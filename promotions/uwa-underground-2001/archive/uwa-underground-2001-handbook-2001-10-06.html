General Information Welcome to hardcore. . the uWa offers: - creative storylines and angles - a commited and friendly staff - complete play by play cards (nothing simmed) - flashes sent out by a member of our staff (to regulate junk mail) - original wrestlers only - the ability to get in contact with other handlers - a competitive, yet layed backed, environment Underground History 101 The Underground Wrestling Alliance first emerged after eXw (Epic Xtreme Wrestling) fell under after only a few weeks of operating. Suter Kain, a former pro wrestler and manager, was one of a few people in charge of the eXw when it closed, and, unfortunatly, was probably the one who lost the most cash after everything flopped. But it wasn't more then a month later that Kain and another member of the defunct eXw, Daniel Franchise, began construction on a smaller independant promotion that would be focused out of the tri state area
(New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, etc.), with a home base in Atlantic City, NJ. The orginal name for the league was going to be The Hardcore Underground, but Suter Kain later changed it to the Underground Wrestling Alliance. At their first show, broadcasted only in a few areas, It's Alive '98, the uWa immediatly turned a small audience into a cult of die-hard Underground Alliance fanatics.
uWa caught on fast and before most people even realized what was happening; the Underground was getting acclaim from other areas of the United States and even getting attention from some big name superstars. Their orginal roster, mostly refugees from eXw, would soon be invaded by some more prominant atheletes in the industry.
Things continued to grow for the Underground Alliance until things began to heat up on the inside. Franchise and Kain where at their ends with one another and couldn't seem to hold on. Franchise wanted to bring in more of his so called "respectable and trusted employees", while Kain saw them as nothing more then washed up friends in need of a favor or two. The fued inside the uWa escalated until finally Franchise went on his way - leaving Suter Kain now 100% in charge of the Alliance.
After a year in running, the uWa was looking strong - but unfortunatly things wouldn't remain so great. When Kain began to get a little too power hungry in the eyes of then uWa assitant commissioner Shawn Sancho, he stepped up and challenged the president. This of course would lead to a rather long and dirty fued between the two that finally ended with Sancho defeating Kain and becoming the new president of the Underground Wrestling Alliance - the only problem was, if Kain couldn't have the uWa - no one was going to have it. Suter had switched all of the uWa's cash into his own private banking account before his match with Sancho - so when Sancho did finally take over, he was left with a bankrupt promotion!
The Underground's long term hiatus finally ended when Suter Kain bought back the rights to his promotion and advertised that the uWa would be holding ONE
"last" megacard, Judgment Night. Perhaps the perfect name for the pay per view, not only would ever uWa title be on the line, but it was this card and the attention that it got, or failed to, that was going to make up Suter Kain's mind on whether to continue running the uWa - or to leave it behind him forever.
Now, after some incredibly rocky moments, the Underground Alliance is back - but Suter Kain isn't at the wheel. After being taken out of wrestling for good by current uWa Heavyweight Champion, Vincent Crow, the uWa was for a small period of time left in the hands of Charles "Phoenix" Inferno. According to
Inferno though, a new man is in the lead. And though he's completely unknown, Inferno assures us that not only is he supposedly incredibly business savvy, but he's also got the financial backing to bring the Underground Alliance back on the road to becoming an empire.
Enough Bullshit This is going to be the uWa's third run. For those of you that have been here since the beginning, or atleast long enough to know the pinnacle we once where, we invite you back to the Underground Alliance and hope you'll find that this time around things are going to be just as good, if not better, then our first run. As for those of you who weren't around when we first came into being - that's okay! The Underground Wrestling Alliance is a promotion that we pride on making interesting and creative storylines for your characters. Bringing in a new wrestler to the uWa doesn't have to be some nameless rookie against some nameless rookie. That of course isn't to say you should expect to be a top dog, but at the same time you will be given every oppurtunity to have a good time and see your wrestler advance.
Myself and the rest of the staff have a horde of ideas for this 3rd rebirth of the Underground Alliance. Expect to see more storylines! Unlike before, and in most other promotions, we aren't just going to have one big storyline for the main eventers - everyone in the Underground is going to have a niche.
Fueds and angles will be interesting and full of twists and turns - we're ready to motivate.

III. Weekly Events and Megacards The Underground Wrestling Alliance has one major card that comes out each and every week. As well as the big card, the uWa has a smaller show which airs at an irregular hour in the early morning and is just a few short matches - nothing major. Also, an Underground Insider (news letter) normally comes out about once a month - sometimes more - and is filled with facts, news, and rumors.
Thursday Night TRauMa (TNT): Trauma is, and always has been, the big show for the Underground Alliance. Every Thursday night the uWa puts on an incredible show of about five to seven matches - maybe more or less. This is where most of the major (and minor) storylines will take shape. Fueds will begin, fueds will end - nonetheless, always expect the unexpected. We're going to try to have the cards out by around 9:00 PM eastern standard time. And normally, soon after the card has been sent out, you can expect to see the lineup for next week's show. Trauma is written out in full play by play by myself and Mike - sometimes we may ask you guys to help if we're looking swamped. Never the less, TNT is written out in full detail by us - NOTHING is simmed.
HaRDCoRe HaNGoVeR: Hardcore Hangover is the uWa's late late night event which takes place sometimes Tuesday night - or EARLY Wednesday morning. It's only broadcasted on odd channels and isn't in full like Trauma. Lineups won't be sent out for Hangover - rather you'll just have to RP and keep active in the promotion. There won't be a lot of matches on Hangover, normally only a handful, but some minor storylines will be played out.
As for megacards and pay per views. The uWa does have a few cards that they hold annually, but for the most part the events just kind of come up when they're needed. If things start to look like they're about to pop - expect to a megacard to be announced soon. As for those that are held annually:
- Straight Through Hell (featuring the Dooms Day Tournament) - Lethal Injection (featuring the eight man lethal lottery brawl for all) - Into the Inferno - It's Alive (the uWa's Wrestlemania) - Warpath ** ** Warpath is very similar to the old WWF's In Your House ppvs. They pop up a few times a year, whenever they're needed, and though they're called Warpath - they normally have another name that sort of gives the whole theme to the event.
IV. uWa Personalities uWa Owner/President: Unknown. uWa Commissioner: Charles "Phoenix" Inferno has been with the uWa for a rather long time. He started his career managing the likes of Ashe Corvan, but soon moved over to the Model Employees Union and began to manage "Vicious" Vinnie
Vinson; he even made sure, due to a contract clause, that Vinson left Lethal Injection '99 with the uWa IC Title. For a time Inferno took over the duties of the uWa president, but with whoever is in charge now (nobody knows), Charles has been given the position of commissioner.
Suter Kain: Suter Kain was the Underground Wrestling Alliance owner and president, but after recieving a Death Drop DDT through a c4 wrapped table by
Vincent Crow he has been MIA. Recent reports say that people close to Kain are saying Suter has no intention of ever setting foot back into the uWa.
Color Commentators/Reporters Winstin Hues: (Thursday Night Trauma - all ppvs ) Winstin Hues is the face commentator. He's the good ol' JR of the uWa.. although a tad more vulgar.
Hues is relatively unbiased and calls it like he sees it. Incredibly educated in pro wrestling, Hues knows just about every move in the book - and if for some reason he doesn't, he does a good job of making names up.
Bobby Gold: (Thursday Night Trauma - all ppvs ) Gold is pretty much the asshole. He's the heel commentator and he lets just about everybody know it.
Bobby Gold is blunt, crude, rude, and VERY biased. He says what he thinks and doesn't think twice about who it may offend - more times then not he's TRYING to offend someone. Gold is pretty well educated in wrestling himself - though not as thorough as Hues, he knows what he's talking about.
Danny Holiday: (Underground Insider - HaRDCoRe HaNGoVeR) Danny Holiday is a smart talking, sarcastic, wise ass of the uWa. Though he occasionally does interviews with the wrestlers, more times then not you can catch him on
Hangover - or in his monthly uWa newsletter, the Underground Insider. Jeff Akkins: (reporter) Akkins is a timid, meek, mouse of a man and strictly does interviews. Normally picked on a taunted by most of the talent, Akkins normally just asks what he has to and then cringes - waiting to be hit.
V. Titles and Crowns * * Active Titles * * uWa Heavyweight Championship: Some of the biggest and baddest superstars in the uWa still never get their hands on the uWa Heavyweight Title. It's a title that means more then just being on top - it means being on top of a group of men that would literally kill to be where you are. This, the most coveted title in the Underground Alliance, has been held by only the greatest uWa superstars. Unlike in most promotions, the Heavyweight Championship is defened on a rather regular basis.. though not EVERY card, it's still hard to keep a hold of.
Past Champions: Vincent Crow (3x) Eric Aberle (2x) Lucius Lancaster (4x) Boston Brawler (1x) Ashe Corvan (1x) Mark Underwood (1x) Butch Carver (1x) Crash Karnage (1x) uWa Intercontinental Championship: The uWa IC Title has always had a lot of heat surrounding it. From the moment it was won by Devon Chapel in a grueling
Second Story Glory Cage Match, to the brutal wars Matt and Vinnie Vinson waged over it. Though technically the "second" best title in the uWa, make no mistakes about it, the IC Championship is no laughing matter. Getting a hold of this shiney strap is one thing, but keeping it is even harder.
Past Champions: Devon Chapel: (1x) The Dean (1x) Ice (1x) Shamrock (1x) Vincent Krowe (1x) Matt Vinson (2x) Vinnie Vinson (2x) uWa Tag Team Championships: Though not nearly as strong as the uWa singles titles, the tag team straps have had their share of fun in the uWa. The
Underground has never really had a strong tag team bracket, this time around we're hoping that changes.
Past Champions: Mark Underwood and Crash Karnage (1x) The Night Breed (1x) The California Kliq (1x) Ashe Corvan and Mark Underwood (1x) Sancho and Tsunami (1x) * * Defunt Titles * * uWa Hardcore Championship: The Hardcore Title was brought into the uWa as more of a rookie title. It got a lot of heat for being called the hardcore title, when every match in the uWa technically has NO rules anyway! Nonetheless the
Hardcore title saw some pretty good fueds in its day. Past Champions: Switchblade Suicide (1x) Perfect Pain (1x) Moonshine (1x) ..retired by Moonshine * * Crowns * * Dooms Day Championship: Every year the uWa holds its vicious spectacular "Straight Through Hell". It's at STH that the uWa has the incredibly brutal Dooms Day Tournament - an eight man, one night, tournament for a shot at the uWa Heavyweight Championship.
Past Dooms Day Champions: Ashe Corvan Eric Aberle VI. Surviving the Underground The Underground Alliance isn't like most promotions out there. So with that in mind you should remember on special thing before stepping foot into a uWa ring and hoping to stay alive. Every standard match in the uWa is no rules!
There's no such thing as a no holds barred brawl - because every match is just that! There are no DQs, no count outs, no nothing! Everything and anything is legal and the matches just keep on going until we have ourselves a winner. Pin falls and submissions are the only way to win a matchup, although once in awhile the referee may call for the bell if one of the wrestlers is obviously in REALLY bad shape.
Although we're not asking for a dozen roleplays every card - the uWa would like to see atleast ONE role play a week. If you can't get a role play in, then try and get i contact with me so we can maybe schedule some on air interview time at TNT or on Hangover. We also like to see strats sent in for matches.
Remember, this is YOUR wrestler - so YOU tell US what you want to see in your match - no promises - we'll try our hardest to comply. And please, please,
PLEASE - if you have an idea for an angle or a storyline.. RUN it by us! We're more then willing to listen and try to execute it. To put things simple..
without your participation, everything is for naught. We need YOU, the handlers, to make this thing work.
