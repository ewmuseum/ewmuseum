It's Alive 2001 "..a good day to die." (opening)

October 7, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[OPENING] I T ' S A L I V E ' 2k "..a good day to die." [Scene] Blackness. A faint drumb beat can be heard beating in the background. Suddenly a single spot lot is shown and in it's gentle golden hue a silhouette stands. Arms out as if in a crucifix, the shadowy figure suddenly speaks..
"..tonight, Eric Aberle, the Crow comes - for you." [Scene] The drum beat stops as suddenly "Down With The Sickness" blasts on. [Bird's Eye View of the Arena] "Down With The Sickness" continues to play as the camera opens up to a beautiful shot of the Underground Arena in Atlantic City, New Jersey. The jam packed venue is literally ready to explode with intensity as a few sparse fire works and pyro technics go off. The fans can be heard chanitng, "Un - Der -
GROUND! Un - Der - GROUND! Un - Der - GROUND! ! !" Suddenly we pan down to get a good look at the jam packed audience. The camera closes in on a few signs here and there, some read things such as: "Suter Kain lives in my closet!", or "Marry Me EA!", and of course - "100% PURE KaRNaGe! ! !"
(commentary) Winstin Hues: Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome one and all to It's Alive
'2k.. "A Good Day to Die'"! We've got one amazing line up planned for tonight so just sit right there and get ready to watch some of the most insane action this sport has EVER seen.
Bobby Gold: (ahem) Winstin Hues: (chuckles) Silly me.. and, as always, by my side to add some color commentary to the evening, the pug but lovable, Bobby Gold!
Bobby Gold: Why hello there fans, you didn't think I'd let this place go off the air for good without saying good bye, did ya?
Winstin Hues: The uWa NEVER dies, Gold.. NEVER! Bobby Gold: Oh shuttup, Hues.. this is our LAST show. Everybody knows it. The fans know it, I know, the superstars know it. Just give up with this.. "The uWa never dies" crap. We're done! [Ringside] The camera pans down to get a good look at the commentators' table where both
Gold and Hues are seated.
Winstin Hues: We've gone on hiatus before and come back and that's just what we're doing now. Nothing is gonna keep this giant down for good, Gold.
Bobby Gold: Please.. we don't even have a president! Ever since Crow took out
Suter Kain this place has gone to shit.
Winstin Hues: I hear Kain's coming back. In fact, I hear he's coming back.. TONIGHT! Bobby Gold: Keep thinking like that, Winnie.. but don't come crying to me when you're out of work.
Winstin Hues: Oh come on, Gold! How can you be grumpy with a line up like this! We've got osme of the most awesome matches of all time on the card for tonight! Crash, Huey Slasher, and Magna in a triple threat ladder match for the urn of Lancaster! A fatal four way ironman match for the IC Title!
Bobby Gold: I would like to see Lust.. Winstin Hues: Not to even mention Vincent Crow versus Eric Aberle for the uWa
Heavyweight Championship Title in a best two out of three falls grudge match!
Bobby Gold: And I am looking forward to Crow crippling Aberle. Winstin Hues: Plus lots more! Believe me folks, tonight is a night that won't disappoint. The uWa is going to deliver like always!
("Heartwork" by Cannibal Corpse) Winstin Hues: Hmm? [Bird's Eye View of the Arena] With his entrance music blaring over the PA system, Triple V, "Vicious" Vinnie
Vinson, comes strolling out of the curtains. The crowd gives a little pop as he holds his arms up high and then begins to make his way down towards the ringside area.
Bobby Gold: Is it time to begin already?! Winstin Hues: I don't think so, Gold.. Vinson is way early, the IC match isn't scheduled to happen for quite some time now.
[Inside the Ring] Vinnie Vinson climbs onto the turnbuckle and holds his arms up high again.. again - a little pop from the crowd. Triple V then grabs a microphone from an official at ring side and stands in the center of the ring.
"Vicious" Vinnie Vinson: You know.. it's hard to believe the uWa is going under. (crowd boos) This place has been my stomping grounds for awhile now and I think I've left a nice little scar. without a doubt I've been one of the most awesome Intercontinental Champions this promotion has ever seen. Hell,
I'm going to go so far as to say that Triple V is the second best IC champ in the uWa's history. So it's only natural that tonight I'm looking to capture that title ONE more time. But unlike the rest, I don't think this is going to be our last show. (crowd pops) We're a family here in the uWa.. one big disfunctional family. And NOTHING can tear a family apart. NOTHING. And speaking of family.. I did some talking in the back and I have some GREAT news.
Since this is supposed to be the best damn show on earth, I figured we might as well have the best damn Intercontinental Champion of ALL TIME residing over it. So allow me to introduce the special guest referee for the fatal fourway ironman match.. ("Indigo" by Aerosmith) "The Mangler" Matt VINSOOOOOOOON!
Bobby Gold: WHAT?! Winstin Hues: No way! Matt Vinson! Matt Vinson is back! [Entrance Ramp] The crowd goes totally insane as none other then "The Mangler" Matt Vinson steps onto the rampway. Using a cane to walk, Matt Vinson holds his right arm up high and smiles to the crowd before he begins to walk down towards the ring.
Wearing a pair of blue jeans and a referee shirt, Matt makes sure to shake a few hands on the way down to the ring.
Winstin Hues: I can't believe that Matt Vinson is back! We havn't seen him since Vincent Crow gave him a top rope brainbuster and broke his neck!
Bobby Gold: This can't be right, Hues.. how unfair is this?! Vinnie's BROTHER is going to referee his match!?
Winstin Hues: Matt isn't the kind of guy to throw a match his brothers way.. family or not. Besides, incase you forgot, Vinnie and Matt went through a miniature little hell themselves.
[Inside the Ring] Matt and Vinnie Vinson embrace inside the ring before Vinnie hands his older brother the microphone.
"The Mangler" Matt Vinson: I'm baaaaAaaaack! (crowd goes nuts) I bet you guys thought you'd never see this ugly mug of mine ever again after that prick
Crow drilled me out, 'eh? Well think again! Cause the Mangler is back and I'm gonna be the best damn referee this promotion has ever seen. Cane or not, you can bet your asses that the Mangler is going to make sure that when the smoke clears we've got ourselves a REAL Intercontinetal Champion. And bro, (looks to
Vinnie) we've been through a lot of shit. Hell, you broke me neck, mother fucker. But make no mistakes about it, blood or no blood.. this thing is going down fair.
("Indigo" by Aerosmith blasts back on over the PA system) [Bird's Eye View of the Arena] The brothers Vinson smile and shake hands before both men make their way out of the ring and back up the entry ramp.
Winstin Hues: Holy shit! This is amazing, Gold! Matt Vinson, the GREATEST
Underground Alliance IC Champion of all time is going to be the special guest referee for the fatal four way ironman match!
Bobby Gold: I smell something fishy, Hues.. I don't like it. Winstin Hues: Well like it or not, it's going down. But right now I'm being told that none other then Crash Karnage has arrived at the arena. We take you now to Jeff Akkins in the garage.
[Backstage] The camera cuts out and then opens back up inside the parking garage. An old beat up van seems to be the center of attention. We can see someone rumaging around in the back of the van, but the two large cargo doors cover the figure.
Jeff Akkins immediatly begins to walk over muttering, "Excuse me, Crash.. Crash
Karnage? Can I get a word with you?" One of the doors shuts and sure enough
Crash is standing there with a duffle bag over his right shoulder. His shoulder length black hair is still wet and slicked back behind his ears. A pair of dark shades rest just above his forehead as he stares over towards
Akkins with no real interest.
Jeff Akkins: Crash? Crash Karnage..? Could you.. uhmm.. could you please just tell the world what your thoughts on tonights match are?
Crash Karnage: (grabbing the microphone from Akkins) My thoughts..? Well
Jeff.. I'm thinking I'm just about ready to get down to that ring, pummel those two good for nothing assholes, Huey Slasher and Marcus Magna, and leave the
Underground Alliance with the ashes of Lucius Lancaster and the ass of that sweet little bitch, Lust. (he drops the microphone and shoves Akkins out of his way) Now get the fuck out of here.
[Backstage] Akkins just shrugs as the camera gets a shot of Crash walking into the Underground Arena. akkins starts to say something when out of nowhere both
Battle Ax and Switchblade Suicide start to pummel Crash!
Jeff Akkins: Oh my God! Are you seeing this, Hues?! The Night Breed is literally beating the crap out of Crash Karnage!
[Backstage] Suicide hauls Crash up while Battle Ax continues to hammer away on him. Ax then grabs him by his hair and sends him face first into the brick wall.
Jeff Akkins: I'm being told that security is on the way.. but it may be too late for Crash Karnage! Ooooh GOD!
(crowd gasps) [Backstage] Battle Ax hauls Crash up and drives him onto the hood of a car with a vicious running powerbomb that puts the back of Crash's head right through the wind shield! The camera then turns to see Battle ax and Switchblade Suicide smiling evilly - from behind them steps Huey Slasher.. grinning that toothy canine grin.
Huey Slasher: Next time.. you best to be paying your debts. (grins) Les' go boys. One down.. one more to go.
[Bird's Eye View of the Arena] The fans continue to go crazy as the camera pans back into the arena. This time the crowd is cheering so loudly because none other then The Flush is making his way down towards the ring with his Southern Dominican Republic
Woman's Title over his shoulder.
Winstin Hues: I don't believe this! Huey Slasher and those goons of his just took out Crash Karnage! I'm not sure what exactly the extent of Karnage's injuries are, but I don't think there is anyway in hell Crash is going to be able to compete tonight.
Bobby Gold: Did you see Crash's head go through that wind shield!? Winstin Hues: Talk about a hellacious beginning! Nonetheless, we're just about to get ready for the openning bout which pits none other then The Flush against his long time chum, Brimstone!
Bobby Gold: ..and that corny ass title of his too. Wooooo! Winstin Hues: That'll be up in just a moment, right now I'm being told that
Danny Holiday is trying to catch up with Ashe Corvan who should be getting ready for his BIG match against Mark Underwood. We take you there now.
[Backstage] The camera opens with none other then Danny Holiday standing outside a big wooden door which reads "Corvan". Holiday knocks on the door a few times,
"Ashe.. Ashe! it's Danny Holiday, are you in?" Danny continues to knock on the door.
Danny Holiday: Well Hues, I'm here, but it doesn't look like Ashe is in his locker room! I'm not sure where he could be, his match with Underwood is the second bout tonight!
Brimstone: (out of nowhere he grabs the microphone from Holiday) Just what the fuck do you think you're doing!? You wanna talk to a superstar? Well here
I am, mother fucker. Screw this (points to corvan's locker room door) faggot!
Now i got something on my mind, and I sure as hell an't waiting to get it off.. I've busted my ass off in this company for God only knows how long - and for what?! I get screwed over and over and over.. and when I do get my shots, it's just so those assholes in the back can put their "big boys" over. So now, at the last card EVER for the uWa, they've got the F'N nerve to schedule me in the openning bout against that waste of space - The Flush! I should be in the IC match! I should be in the urn match! Hell, I'd RATHER be cleaning the fucking parking lot then getting into the ring with the -
[Backstage] Without warning Corvan's door swings open and smashes BS right in the head! Ashe Corvan: What the fuck is going on out here? I got a freaking match in a little bit and I'm trying to get some meditation in.. could you please shut the hell up?! (looks down at Brimstone) ..and who the fuck are you anyway?
[Backstage] Brimstone snaps! he lets out a vicious roar and then charges right at Ashe and slams a big right hand into Corvan's face! he then grabs him by his hair and sends him flying into the wall. BS doesn't stop there, he continues to unload with heavy lefts and rights, until Corvan is crippled on the ground - dazed.
Brimstone: The name is Brimstone.. and don't your ass forget it! [Bird's Eye View of the Arena] The Flush stands in the ring and holds his title up high. The crowd seems to be having a good time - chanting "FLUSH FLUSH FLUSH!" Finally, the sounds of
Monster Magnet playing "Space Lord" blasts on over the PA system.
Ring Announcer: And his opponent, weighing in at two hundred and thirty six pounds - BRIMSTONE! ! ! (no reaction) Winstin Hues: Did you see that Gold?! BS just took out Ashe! What the hell is up his ass?
Bobby Gold: Didn't you hear the man? He's sick of being over looked! Winstin Hues: Sounds like a cry baby to me, Gold. Bobby Gold: Brimstone has been through hell and high water here in the uWa - and for what?!
[Entrance Ramp] Brimstone walks slowly down the rampway screaming at the fans, "What's the fuckin' matter?!" He even goes so far as to give a few fans the middle finger,
"Chant my name, you fuckin' pigs!"
