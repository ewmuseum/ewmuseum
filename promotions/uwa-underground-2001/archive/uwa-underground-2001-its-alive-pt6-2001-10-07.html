It's Alive 2001 "..a good day to die." (5)

October 7, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[FIVE] Bobby Gold: EA is back up! [Inside the Ring] EA charges in and starts to hammer away on Crow. He then turns Vincent around and slams a few fists into his face! The crowd continues to chant until -
Winstin Hues: Aberle with a big right hand..
Bobby Gold: NO! Crow ducks.. Winstin Hues: OUGH! (crowd gasps) Winstin Hues: Leaping hangman's neckbreaker! ! ! Bobby Gold: It was a good attempt by EA.. [Inside the Ring] Crow rips Aberle back to his feet and hoists him onto the top turnbuckle. He then climbs up infront of him and hooks him up for the Death Drop DDT - the crowd continues to chant EA's name.. but their silenced as Vincent Crow lunges off the top turnbuckle and drills Aberle head first through FOUR tables with his patented finisher!
(the crowd gasps) Winstin Hues: HE HIT IT ! ! ! HE HIT THE DEATH DROP DDT ! ! ! Bobby Gold: Say GOOD night. (referee) ONE ! ! ! TWO ! ! ! THREE ! ! ! ! ! ! ! (crowd boos) DING DING DING Ring Announcer: The winner of the second fall.. VINCENT CROOOOOOW ! ! ! ! Winstin Hues: Look at this! Crow is telling the referee to keep counting! This might be all she wrote for Aberle.. Bobby Gold: Count to three? Hell, Hues, he could count to a hundred - EA is OUT COLD. (referee) ONE ! ! ! TWO ! ! ! (crowd goes crazy) Winstin Hues: Nikki just pulled the referee out of the ring ring! Bobby Gold: That tramp just cost Vincent Crow the match! There was no way
Aberle was getting up!
Winstin Hues: Uh oh! Nikki, RUN! [Outside the Ring] The referee starts to scream and yell at Nikki, but all of a sudden the referee is thrown out of the way as Vincent Crow grabs Nikki by her hair and decks her!
! ! Winstin Hues: Crow just hit Nikki! Bobby Gold: Hit her?! He fuckin' knocked her ass out! Winstin Hues: Vincent Crow now throws the referee back into the ring and here he comes too..
[Inside the Ring] Vincent Crow starts to haul Aberle back to his feet - but EA rolls him up into a small package!
(referee) ONE ! ! ! TWO ! ! ! THREE ! ! ! ! ! ! ! (crowd goes insane) DING DING DING Ring Announcer: The winner of the third fall.. and NEW uWa HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION.. ERIC ABERLE ! ! ! ! ("You Could be Mine", by GnR) Winstin Hues: Aberle did it! ABERLE WINS! ! ! ! WINNER NEW uWa Heavyweight Champion ERIC ABERLE Bobby Gold: I don't believe it.. Eric Aberle beat Vincent Crow TWICE! ! ! Winstin Hues: FINALLY Eric Aberle has regained the uWa Heavyweight Championship Title! ! ! Eric Aberle: (holding the title up high) I TOLD YOU! (gasping for air) I TOLD YOU! Winstin Hues: Look at Vincent Crow.. he's on his knees in the middle of the ring and I don't think he can believe it. He had this match won.
[Inside the Ring] Aberle leaves the ring and helps Nikki up.. both of them hug and start to walk back uo the rampway when suddenly:
Vincent Crow: Where the hell do you think you're going, Aberle? You promised me a fair fight.. you PROMISED ME A FAIR FIGHT ! ! ! So come down here.. and face me - ONE on ONE.. without that bitch! I had you beat, Aberle.. I had you beat! ! ! Winstin Hues: ..uh oh. Bobby Gold: He's right, Hues.. EA never would of won that match if it wasn't for Nikki's interference.
Vincent Crow: Look at the tape you stupid son of a bitch! Look at it! (pointing to the tele screen in the back which is showing Nikki pull the referee out of the ring) ONE more MATCH! ! !
[Rampway] Obviously having no idea Nikki had interfered, Aberle looks shocked when he sees the tape. Nikki keeps screaming how sorry she is and trying in vain to pull EA back up the rampway, but Aberle shoves her off and makes his way back down towards the ring! The crowd is going absolutely insane.
Vincent Crow: That's right.. get the fuck back in here! Winstin Hues: What the hell? ..they're fighting again, Gold! Crow and Aberle are back at it!
Bobby Gold: ONLY in the uWa! DING DING DING Winstin Hues: The bell has sounded and this thing is BACK underway! uWa Heavyweight Championship Title standard uWa rules * ERIC ABERLE * Vs VINCENT CROW [Inside the Ring] Aberle and Crow start unloading on one another right off the bat. EA quickly gains the advantage and backs Crow back up intot he ropes, but he misses a BIG wild right hook and Crow counters with a HuGe elbow to the jaw that sends EA stumbling back into the center of the ring.
Winstin Hues: FOUR times in one night.. FOUR times in one these two are going at it! [Inside the Ring] Crow charges in and levels EA with a vicious clothesline from hell. Bobby Gold: It's just as much pride as it is the title for these guys.. EA wants to win this thing on HIS terms.
Winstin Hues: I don't know how either man has anything left in them to fight, but they're giving it their all.
Bobby Gold: Both men are back to their feet, but it's Eric Aberle who's getting heavy forearm shots to the small of his back. Crow is just hammering away on him.
[Inside the Ring] Crow whips Aberle into the ropes and goes for a back body drop, but EA rolls over him and lands on his feet! Crow turns and ALMOST gets a superkick, but manages to side step it.
Winstin Hues: Crow locks him into a full nelson annnd.. Bobby Gold: DRAGON suplex! Winstin Hues: That might do it.. (referee) ONE ! ! ! TWO ! ! ! Winstin Hues: NO! Aberle kicks out! Bobby Gold: Both men roll back to their feet, but again it's Crow who answers first with a few quick rights.
[Inside the Ring] Aberle finally blocks a left hook and smashes Crow in the face with a cheese grater! ! ! The cowd goes wild as Crow stumbles into the center of the ring holding his face in pain.
Winstin Hues: Diving lariat by Eric Aberle takes Crow down and here's the cover! (referee) ONE ! ! ! TWO ! ! ! Winstin Hues: NO! This time it's Crow who kicks out. [Inside the Ring] EA drops a heavy elbow onto the downed Vincent Crow. He then picks him back up to his feet and sends him into the corner turnbuckle.
Bobby Gold: Here comes an Aberle Splash! Winstin Hues: NO! Bobby Gold: Crow caught him! Winstin Hues: EA went for the big corner splash, but Crow catches him with a spear! [Inside the Ring] EA holds his ribcage area and screams in pain as Crow rolls back to his knees and fumbles around before he begins wrapping his boot with barbwire.
Bobby Gold: Aberle, despite probably having a few broken ribs, is fighting back to his feet.
Winstin Hues: He might not want too if he saw what was waiting for him! (crowd pops) Winstin Hues: EA gets up just in time to get a spinning heel kick to the face! Bobby Gold: With a barbwire wrapped boot nonetheless! [Inside the Ring] Eric is layed flat out. Crow smiles wickedly and then crawls on over and falls ontop of EA, barely having the strength to hook his leg way up.
(referee) ONE ! ! ! TWO ! ! ! THRE... Winstin Hues: SO CLOSE! SOOOOO DAAAAMN CLOSE! Bobby Gold: Vincent Crow is back up and look at this! He's calling for the
Death Drop DDT.. AGAIN!
Winstin Hues: If he hits Aberle with another Triple D, I think it's pretty obvious EA is done.
Bobby Gold: He's hauling his ass up. [Inside the Ring] Vincent Crow hoists Aberle back onto the top turnbuckle. He then looks over the crowd and smirks, spitting out a wad of blood and snot before he climbs up infront of him and hooks him up.
Bobby Gold: Look at this! LOOK AT THIS! [Outside the Ring] Nikki climbs onto the outside ring apron with a steel chair and is just about to smash it over Vincent Crow's head when out of nowhere some other mysterious woman comes up from behind her and grabs the chair out of her hands! Crow looks down to see what the hell is going on and -
Winstin Hues: Who the hell is that?!
Bobby Gold: I don't know but EA just hit Vincent Crow with a low blow! [Inside the Ring] Crow stumbles back down off of the top turnbuckle and Aberle hooks him up and nails him with a tornado DDT! ! !
[Outside the Ring] This new chick, whoever she is, is wearing a revealing leather bondage outfit and is BUFF as hell. Her head is shaved, but she still looks very feminine.
Nikki turns around annnnd - WHaM! She gets hit the chair! Bobby Gold: Whoever the hell that is, she just drilled Nikki! [Inside the Ring] Aberle runs to the ropes and starts to scream at the new girl. Winstin Hues: OH MAN! Bobby Gold: She just spit green mist into the face of EA! Winstin Hues: A LOT of GREEN mist! [Inside the Ring] Aberle stumbles back just in time to get caught with an inverted spinning brainbuster into a cradle pinning combination by Vincent Crow!
(referee) ONE ! ! ! TWO ! ! ! THREE ! ! ! ! ! ! ! (crowd boos) DING DING DING Winstin Hues: Crow beat him! Bobby Gold: Holy shit.. Ring Announcer: The winner.. and NEW uWa HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION.. VINCENT CROOOOOOOW ! ! ! ! ("For Whom the Bell Tolls", by Metallica) WINNER NEW uWa Heavyweight Champion VINCENT CROW [Inside the Ring] Crow staggers to his feet and snatches the uWa Title out of the announcer's hands and holds it up high. He then throws it back over his shoulder and looks down, out of the ring, at the new woman - whoever the hell she is.
Winstin Hues: I don't believe this.. Aberle won the match - but Crow wins the war! Crow is once again the uWa Champion!
Bobby Gold: Holy shit.. [fade out]
