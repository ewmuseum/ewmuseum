A December 2003 e-wrestling startup calling itself the Shaneo Wrestling Federation, recruiting an all-fictional roster around a weekly show and monthly supercard before announcing one New Year's card with no further trace surviving. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Shaneo Wrestling Federation opened for business 3 December 2003, pitched in its own recruitment post as an e-wrestling promotion “now in its 4th incarnation,” run by “an experienced fed head with years of experiance,” and open only to wrestlers who were “fictional.” It ran a weekly show with a monthly supercard, alongside Rankings and Hall of Fame sections carrying its own RP of the Week and Wrestler of the Month awards.

Its only other surviving document is a lineup for a New Year's card, King Of The Hill 2004, set for 3 January 2004: a World Title match between Fallen and Aiden Williams feeding into a battle royal for the rest of the roster, a six-way Internet Title match, a No Holds Barred bout between Shaun Munro and Jim Jack Jones, a three-way Women's Title match among Shadowcat, Stephanie Williams, and Torrie Electra, and a “6-Pack (of beer) On A Pole” Cruiserweight Title match between Nightmare and Shane Bibby. The same post flags a possible late change to the Internet Title lineup over “rumours” that Michael Tyler might resign.

Whether the King Of The Hill card was ever fought isn't in its surviving record; no further trace survives past this posting.
