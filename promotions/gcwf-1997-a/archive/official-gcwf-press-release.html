Official GCWF Press release...welcome to the next level !!!

Apr 5, 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A press release from the offices of the Global Championship Wrestling Federation: Scene opens to a crowded press room in the GCWF executive towers!! The asssembles reporter quickly come to order as a group of men enter the room! It is the members of the GCWF executive commitee, and they are followed by some of the signed talent of the GCWF...an incredible gathering of athletes..... GCWF Preident RJ Conroy steps to the microphone... "Greetings to the membership, and interested guests. Thank you for attending this press conference. This press release is to announce that the Global Championship Wrestling Federation is now ready to begin operations You see behind you some of the finest athletes ever assembled, thw world over !! And this is only the beginning!! Our first series of matches will be signed this week !! We will be drawing opponents for opening matches this weekend..I see a couple possibilities.. a simple elimination style tournament, a battle royal, or a mix...narrow it down to 4 or 8 then rumble for the winner...We will announce the format this weekend. We will have enough Tag Teams and Cruiserweights to begin, but plenty of room still in all divisionsThose of you already on the mailing list, will have till Sunday to get your last applications in...after that, they will not be eligible for the inaugural title tournament. This applies to all weight divisions. Also, those already signed up, please check out yourt wrestlers webpage on the website...if there are any errors or omisions, let me know and ill correct them. If there is a TBD next to an item, it means i have not recived that info from you...send it in asap! Those not yet signed up, check out the individual pages...a first from the GCWF The time has come, were ready to rock and roll ......whose gonna be the first champion ??? you think you can do it...well see!! Remember deadline for eligibility in the first GCWF title tournament is Sun 6th of April ...get them in..no extensions will be given, it wouldnt be fair to those who have already done it. We have set up a channel in the IRC chat.....on the EFNET server for the members to meet and get to know each other, make friends and enemies....you are welcome to trade email and such, but all league bussiness, ambushes, alliances, trash talks, etc must go through the league offices, another first from the GCWF. We will also be using the newsgroup rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy for postings and news...were gonna do it all!!! Well, thats all for now, any questions, comments, or problems, let us know...otherwise, if youll pardon the expression....Lets get ready to rumble!!!!!" Welcome to the next level!!! The GCWF executive commitee The Global Championship Wrestling Federation can be reached via email at [detail withheld] or on the GCWF website at http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Track/3339/ . The GCWF, Do you have what it takes !!
