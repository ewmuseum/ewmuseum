New E-Fed Open for New Wrestlers

17 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone. My name is Jason. I am opening a new e-federation, for those of you that don't know who I am, then I will tell you. I am very active in e-wrestling, and I have been involved in it for close to 2 1/2 years. I have been in only the top e-feds, now, for those of you that don't know which feds are the top ones, then I will tell you, they are, the EWA, PCW, DPW, PIW, and some would say Chaos. I used to write matches for some of these feds, and I have been doing it for a while, and everyone always tells me how good of a job I did. I used to run a fed called the WIF, it was one of the better new feds around, but then I had to close it. I reopened it, and did EVEN better. I then opened a new fed with my friend, and I almost ran the thing by myself because he was gone most of the time. Anyways, the fed I am opening is called the EIW What I have decided to do is mix it up a little bit, it will be a lot like the e-feds that are the best, and it will also be a little different. You will MAKE YOUR OWN WRESTLERS!!! All you have to do to join, is e-mail me telling me that you want a creation sheet, and then I will send you one, you fill it out, send it to me, and then after I get a certain amount of wrestlers, we will begin. Hope you enjoy it! [name withheld]
