Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This Extreme Innovative Wrestling introduced itself on 17 March 1997 through its own founder's résumé rather than a card or a roster: two and a half years in e-wrestling, matches written for several of what he called the era's top federations, and a promotion of his own, called WIF, that he had closed once, reopened, and run “EVEN better” the second time.

EIW itself he pitched as a hybrid, “a lot like the e-feds that are the best,” but original-characters-only — “You will MAKE YOUR OWN WRESTLERS!!!” — run by creation sheet, e-mailed on request, with the promotion set to begin once enough wrestlers had signed on.

No further document survives. Whether EIW ever collected enough wrestlers to actually begin isn't in the surviving record.
