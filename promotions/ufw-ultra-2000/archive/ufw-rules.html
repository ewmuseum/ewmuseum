RULES
The rules of UFW are simple and are easy to follow. We ask you to follow these basic rules so there will be no problems and UFW can run smoothly.

RULE 1: NO PROFANITY   The message board does not allow profanity.  If you must, do it like this...(A$$ , S*&t, F&#k etc).  Remember, it won't help you earn push points.  If you manage to find any words that we haven't banned yet and don't bleep them out, push points will be deducted, followed by firing if you keep it up.

RULE 2: NO SEXUAL INNUENDO  It will not be tolerated.  If you sexually harass anyone or use sexual language or descriptions there will be an automatic deduction of 5 push points the first time, 10 the second and you may be fired for a third.

RULE 3: NO RACISM To put it simply, if you use racially abuse someone you may be suspended or even fired if the offence is truly offensive at a personal level.

RULE 4: FAMILY IS OFF LIMITS Don't abuse members of your intended opponent's family in your RP.  

RULE 5: PUSH RATE CONFIDENTIALITY Don't ask to have your push rate increased or waste the Prez's time by asking what yours is, as you will not be told.  Any player who requests to have their push upped will immediately loose 5 push points for each request.

RULE 6: POST FREQUENTLY This is the most important rule: You must post two or three times a week minimum. We understand that sometimes this is not possible, and will not apply penalties if we are notified in advance.  We also recommend as a courtesty to other members that you post on the board that you will be away, to help prevent someone from wasting time RP'ing at you and you not being there to read and answer it.  If you do not post for fourteen days, you may be deleted from the roster, or alternatively used as a jobber for other characters to get cheap wins over.

RULE 7: RESPECT FELLOW MEMBERS If someone posts a challenge to you and you see it, answer it, even if to say no.  If it is obvious that you are ignoring a challenge from someone, you will be penalised.

RULE 8: TITLES To challenge for a title, you need to have won a certain amount of matches:

Heavyweight Title

 15 Matches

US Title

 12 Matches

TV Title

 8 Matches

Cruiserweight Title

 5 Matches (max. weight 225lbs)

Rising Star Title

 Less than 10 matches

Hardcore Title

  Champ's discretion
Woman's Title  Champ's discretion
Tag Team Titles  Champ's discretion
Stables Title (Six Man)  Prez's discretion 

These are guides only, to be used for selecting who goes on the contenders list (and for the champ to legitimately say no to someone who has not paid their dues yet) and may be wavered at the discretion of the champ or the Prez.

RULE 9: TITLE HOLDERS A Title must be defended on a regular basis.

Heavyweight Title

 Every 2 weeks

US Title

 Every 2 weeks

TV Title

 Every week

Cruiserweight Title

 Every 2 weeks

Hardcore Title

  Every 2 weeks

Rising Star Title

 Every week
Woman's Title  Every 2 weeks
Tag Team Titles  Every 2 weeks
Stables Title (Six Man)  Every PPV (only)

For periods longer than that stated, permission must be obtained from the Prez.

We are attempting to make UFW fun and enjoyable experience, and hope each and every member enjoys themselves and has lots of fun!

The UFW PRESIDENT
