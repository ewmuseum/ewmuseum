AUGUST 25 2000

MATCH 1 - PIRANHA TANK MATCH

Genocide vs X-Treme

MATCH 2 - HANDICAP MATCH

Scott Naket & Mystery Partner vs Revernd

MATCH 3 - LION'S DEN MATCH

The Joker Jack Frost Emerald Fusion "Demolition Man" Bob Ullrich Nick Taylor

Must win by submission or KO The winner gets the final spot at the PPV for the Rising Star Title The first to make a submission/ KO will win

MATCH 4 - TAG TEAM HARDCORE MATCH

Sythe & Emerald Fusion vs The Dominators

Which ever team wins, they get the two spots for the Hardcore title at the PPV

MATCH 5 - STANDARD TAG MATCH

Kaos & Revenant vs Myst & Mystery Partner

No interference If Revenant & Kaos win, Revenant and Kaos may enter the World Title Tourney later in the night and also... Revenant may get Myst in any match he likes

MATCH 6 - HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE QUALIFIER 1 - STANDARD MATCH

Biohazard vs B.A. Bruiser vs "Demolition Man" Bob Ullrich

If Biohazard wins on Thursday, Bob Ullrich receives a bye into Match 7

MATCH 7 - HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE QUALIFIER 2 - STANDARD MATCH

Kaos vs Revenant

If Kaos & Revenant loose the earlier tag match, Koas gets a walk-thru to the final Winner goes on to Match 7 to face winner of Match 4

MATCH 8 - STANDARD MATCH

Winner of Match 4 VS Winner of Match 5

Winner goes to the PPV Main Event as Challenger 2

MATCH 9 - TV TITLE QUALIFIER - BATTLE ROYAL

Genocide "The Scholar" Howard Porter Crazy Six "Demolition Man" Bob Ullrich Ming Groan Final two can challenge for the TV Belt 

MAIN EVENT - UFW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE QUALIFIER- STANDARD MATCH

Anguish vs Groan vs Mayhem

Winner receives Challenger 1 spot in PPV Main Event
