Final Roster / Member's list

This is as close as I can get to a list of all members of UFW (current and former)  who had at least one match (win or loss) while UFW was open, or did some good posts then disappeared before getting a match.

Members' profiles who left before March 2000 have been lost.   These include the first Heavyweight Champion Executioner, Slasher, Mystic, Dream Master, Dgenerate,  members of The Isolators and no doubt others

Other members' profiles have been lost since, including Cain Manson Crow, T-Money, Jaguar,  Kamakaze (the original one, not the jobber),  Biatch in Tights (before he became Insomniac),  Havok, JiN, Chameleon (one of mine), and a whole bunch of other guys who joined  and never had a match (I have over 100 profiles of guys like this that I can't be bothered adding)

I also don't have win/loss ratios of tag teams who got removed any time before the last day of trading.

To anyone else I left out, I apologise.  I'm happy to edit the list if you send me your name at the Prez hotmail account.

Two final things I can think of are: 1.  Damn!  I was within 3 matches of having two top ten characters on the final list 2.  DAMN!!! 1 more member to make 100 - curse those lost profiles!
