~The Info~

 

This is the Page where you can check out the latest and up-to-date news, rumours and information about the future UFW special Event, Live in London 2000, so heres it is so far:-

-Date :The 20th of August 2000

-Run by : Commissioner BioHazard

-Arena : Earls Court London (but all the Tournament match wrestlers shall be driven to Buckinham Palace to take place in their matches.)

-Wrestlers taking part - Everyone in the UFW (Bar Mimic and Mike Nightmare as they are having great difficulty making travel arrangements)

-The Card : Look at the card here

 

~The News~

We have recently heard that ALL the titles will go on the line in this event, bar the stables and tag-team titles, simply because the event shall over-run in time allowances if these matches take place. But there are still 2 open spots for singles matches for the event.

Recently, the UFW President, Jack Orion was rumoured to visit the Queen of England in order to aquire the allowance of having the 7 tournament matches take place inside Buckingham Palace itself! We have heard that the matches are to be Buckingham Palace Brawl matches... and they shall include such areas as 'The Throne Room, and The Crown Room' to name just a couple. This truly is a first for the UFW, and for wrestling as a whole for that matter, actually letting people wrestle inside a normally private building.

We will have more news and it comes through... Until Later!
