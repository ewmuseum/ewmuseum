UFW (Ultra) — Tributes

UFW Tributes

February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

This Special Section is designed to pay respect to UFW Champions who have for one reason or another elected to retire or those who have been injured and their career ended.

Myst - A brave, brave fighter!!

When Myst first made his debut in UFW during November 1999, he was new and no one knew what chaos he would later reek on this fed. Myst as every other guy did started to post and began to build up an impressive record of 10:0. Later in that same month a man by the name of Slasher teamed up with him and they formed a team by the name of Demonic Minds. As these two grew and began to win more and more matches a few eyebrows were raised and more and more people wanted to join them. They formed a stable, also by the name of Demonic Minds. Today its members include: Iceman, Slasher, Magnus, Pandora, The Body, Justin Sane, Insano and Krippler. This stable would grow in power and become one of the toughest most elite group of athletes this sport has to offer.

Myst is, by far one of the best wrestlers UFW has ever seen. He had a great record of 23:11 and held an endless amount of title. Myst's first title triumph was when he won the US title back in early December. Later that week he lost it to one of his tougher opponents but later regained it just before UFW's very first PPV - The Great Christmas Bash that was on December 24 1999. At this PPV Myst would prove himself to be the true champ that he was by holding on to his US title in a three-way dance. Later in that same evening he would be in the main event for the world heavy weight champion against The Body who was the champ at the time and Layeth The Smack Down. Myst wasn't successful this time around and Layeth The Smack Down became the third UFW World champion. Also in that same night Myst would tag up with Slasher and go for the tag team title belts. But yet again he would be unsuccessful. Never the less Myst was happy with his US title.

Over the next few months he was got into a couple of feuds with people causing him to have up to four matches sometimes in the one night. Being the true champ Myst was he would sometimes walk out to the ring with bandages wrapped around his arms and legs and still compete. Then he started to go after his dream, the world title. Layeth The Smack Down gave him a challenge and if he was successful he would get his title shot late one Friday Night. That came true for Myst and on that night he was successful. He now held the two most valuable belts in our sport today.

But this was short lived. About one week later he put his beloved US title on the line against Just Deadly "JD" Stalker and lost it. But for a consolation prize, during that same night Slasher and himself won the world tag team belts for the first time against The Isolators. Myst had lost a belt but gained another. This was approximately one week before his retirement in UFW.

The second PPV that UFW would ever see was on the horizon, exactly one week away. After Myst had won the world title from Layeth he gave him a set of obstacles that he had to pass to get another shot at the title at the PPV. Layeth passed and 2/3 of the main event from last month would carry over the following. The only problem would be to get a third competitor and a proposal of a royal rumble at the PPV from a wrestler by the name of Magnus would come in handy. The winner of the Royal Rumble would fill the third position for the main event.

Friday the 14th of January 2000. Some call it a bad luck day and it was for Myst. During the next few weeks a lot of big things were coming up for him. The PPV, the interfed battle for the interfed tag team titles with Layeth and of course his very busy schedule. On this Friday, Myst proposed an extreme hardcore match to anyone in the fed. Mr Hardcore himself came forward. He also offered to put his TV title on the line. Layeth The Smack Down had also put himself into the main event, Myst and Layeth defiantly made great main events at UFW. Myst had already fought Layeth twice that week and won twice. Layeth was going to make sure that he wouldn't win now or at the PPV.

The result was, Layeth The Smack Down won the match becoming the new UFW TV Champion. After the match Layeth and JD decided to beat Myst senseless. Layeth with his favourite weapon the kendo stick and JD with a chair. Smashed Myst across his back repeatedly fracturing his spine in three places. That night Myst was rushed to hospital and had an operation and the quick recover he is, he was back out on the scene the following day. On Saturday the 15th of January 2000, Myst made his retirement speech. It was emotional and reminded everyone of the great times. Myst you were a true champion.

JD Stalker - A great Roleplayer

JD Stalker announced his retirement from UFW on Februray 17 of the year 2000. JD began his career a little before UFW's first PPV, The Great Christmas Bash. It would be clear that he would make a big impact on UFW, but no one would have guessed the amount of impact that he actually made.

JD started off winning his matches with relative easy. After beating the no names, he went in search of more of a challenge and that form came from the man we know as Myst. At this point in time, Myst held the two most valuable titles in UFW at that time. It was the US and the World belt. JD and Myst roleplayed some several pages back and forth until reaching an agreement. Myst would put his US belt on the line sometime later that week.

As we remember JD won the match, and he now held the title. Myst being the true fighter that he is was quick to retaliate. He posted straight back at JD demanding that he get a rematch just before the PPV. But, it would not only be JD that Myst would face it would be a man who just beginning to establish himself in UFW, Layeth the Smack Down. When the title shot came, JD retained it and he and Layeth also ended Myst's career (see above).

After this point in time, JD would become more quiet not cause he injured Myst but because of illness. After the recovery he had a few matches that were non title and at the PPV, his US belt will go on the line and will be in the hands of a new owner. This was the career of JD Stalker.
