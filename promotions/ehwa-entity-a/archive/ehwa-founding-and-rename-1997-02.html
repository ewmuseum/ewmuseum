EHWA: Founding and Rename

6 & 11 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

6 February 1997 — "UWF starting soon"

Join up today for the Universal Wrestling Federation. All I need is your wrestlers name, what type of wrestler he is, his finisher.You are allowed
2 single wrestlers and 1 tag team. I have 3 singles and 2 tag titles. I'll hold matches during the week with the big ippv matches on friday posting results over the weekend.So e-mail me today and join up with the Universal
Wrestling Federation.

11 February 1997 — "EHWA formly know as the UWF"

Extreme Hardcore Wrestling Assocation was know as the Universal Wrestling
Fedration but has changed. Due to the fact that the name was already taken. So instead of causeing any problems I went ahead and changed the name. Right now I've got 20 wrestlers and 7 tag teams. So if you want to join up please e-mail for one of the youngest but hottest leauges around.
I need around 10-12 wrestlers and about 4-8 more tag teams. If you want to join up send me your wrestlers name( no real wrestlers) what type of wrestler he is (i.e. Power, high flyer, Brawler, Techincal) what his finisher is, if he has a manager and if hes a heel or a face. So join up now.
