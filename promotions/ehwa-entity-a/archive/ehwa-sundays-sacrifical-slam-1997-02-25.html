EHWA's Sundays Sacrifical Slam

25 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

THIS WAS A RECENT CARD HELD. IF YOU WANT TO JOIN IN AND BE APART OF THE HOTTEST FEDERATION GOING ON THE NET TODAY E-MAIL ME AT [detail withheld] AND I'LL SEND YOU ALL YOU NEED TO JOIN SO DON'T WAIT JOIN TODAY. "THIS LADIES AND GENTLEMEN IS THE SUNDAY SACRIFICAL SLAM. TONIGHT YOU WILL SEE THE NEW TAG CHAMPIONS DEFEND THIER TITLES. YOU WILL ALSO SEE A THREE WAY DANCE BETWEEN THREE OF THE TOP TAG WRESTLERS WRESTLING SINGLELY. AND YOU WILL SEE TWO SINGLES MATCHES BETWEEN THE THREE CHAMPIONS ALL TITLES ARE NOT ON THE LINE. SO FIRST LETS GET THE TAG MATCH ON THE WAY." The challengers come to the ring first but there way is blocked by Eclipse

and Cataclysm. As the Candians get ready to brawl Eclipse and Cataclysm

step aside. The Canadians go by verey tentivly. Then the music comes on

and The Masters of the Apocolypse come out. They to go by Eclipse and

Cataclysm tentivly. Finally after all four men are in the ring the bell

sounds. Martin and Fear start the match. Martin starts with several

european uppercuts backing Fear into the ropes. Then Martin slings Fear

into the far rope and dropped down for a backdrop. Fear though goes over

the top to get Martin in a sunset flip. After a quick two Martin kicks

out. Martin follows up with a quick clothesline to send Fear to the mat.

Martin grabs a handful of hair and tags in Jean. Jean jumps over the top

and kicks Fear in the chest. Jean kicks Fear several times in the small of

the back. Then he picks him up and sends him into the ropes. Jean goes up

and attempts a dropkick but Fear holds on to the ropes. Jean falls to the

ring at the time Fear jumps to his corner to make the tag. Pain comes

charging in and drops an elbow on Jean. Pain then picks Jean up and

delivers a piledriver to Jean. As Jean lays crumpled in the ring Martin

comes charging in the ring , but Pain sees him and ducks down in time to

backdrop Martin over the top rope. Pain tags Fear who climbs to the top

rope while Pain picks up Jean and drops him with a Powerbomb. As soon as

Pain gets outta of the way Fear comes off the top rope with a big splash. The ref makes the three count and The Masters of the Apocalypse get the

win. "NOW FOR THE FIRST MATCH OF THE BATTLE OF CHAMPIONS." First Maverick comes to the ring carring his television championship to

the ring. Ivan Thor Thunder follows suit with his Internet championship

strapped firmly in place around his waist. As soon as Thor gets in the

ring Maverick grabs the microphone. "Hey Thor, As far as I'm concerend

this is for the titles. But, some peice of trash in some comfy office has

seemed fit to say its not. I wish it was I've asked Commishner Powers to

make it a title shot and he said his hands were tied. So lets get this on"

Maverick goes to hand the mic back to the annoncer but Thor grabs it and

starts to talk. "Listen here Maverick, I respect what you;ve done and I to

want this to be a title shot and I to have talked to Powers and got the

same answer. So lets get it on and maybe next time we met it will be a

title shot." The announcer leaves the ring and the bell sounds. Maverick

and Thor lock up and struggle for postion. Thors power proves to much for

Maverick as he powers him into the turnbuckle. Thor delivers several shots

to Mavericks upper torso. Thor then whips Maverick to the far corner and

he hits hard. Thor follows Maverick into the corner but is met with a big

boot. As Thor drops down to his knees Maverick jumps up to the second

turnbuckle and comes of with a dropkick. Thor gets sent backwards and

Maverick climps back up the turnbuckle. Maverick waits for Thor to stand

up. As soon as Thor gets up Maverick comes off with a dropkick to Thors

knee. Thor drops to the ground and Maverick dosent waist time. Maverick

slaps on the sharpshooter and makes the big man give.

"THE WINNER OF THE MATCH MAVERICK. MAVERICK WILL NOW GO ON TO FACE PLAYBOY RONNIE D. AFTER THE THREE WAY DANCE." The three way dance starts with Vengence and Eclipse coming out and

hitting Agony with a steel chair. Agony drops to the concrete floor. As

Eclipse and Vengence celebrate Cataclysm jumps Vengence from behind. Both Cataclysm and Eclipse drag Vengence back to the ring. When they get back

in the ring they start to work over Vengence. Meanwhile Pain ,Agonys

partner has come out to help Agony to the ring. As soon as Pain and Agony

get to the ring Cactaclysm and Eclipse deliver a spiked powerbomb onto a

chair on Vengence. Agony and Pain climb intothe ring and start to brawl

with Eclipse and Cataclysm. Pain sends Cataclysm over the top rope and

then follows after him the to battle all the back to the locker rooms.

Agony doubles Eclipse over with a swift kick to the gut . Agony then

delivers a kneefift and sends Eclipse crashing to the mat. Agony then

grabs the chair and smacks Eclipse across the back. Agony puts Eclipse in

the boston crab and makes him give. Agony looks over at the limp form off

Vengence and shrugs his shoulders covers him for the win. "THE WINNER OF THE MATCH AGONY FROM THE POWERS OF DESTRUCTION" "THE NEXT MATCH IS THE FINAL MATCH OF THE EVENING PITTING THE TELEVISION CHAMPION VS. THE EXTREME HARDCORE WRESTLING CHAMPION." The Television champion Maverick comes to the ring puts his belt in the

center of the ring and then climbs up the turnbuckles and sits down. The

Playboys music starts up and The Extrene Champion starts to make his way

to ringside. The Playboy makes it to ringside where theres a beautiful

blonde sitting at ringside. Playboy opens his robe and starts to gyrate

towards her. The blonde throws a coke(tm) in his face. The Playboy starts

to take a swing at her when Maverick comes flying over the top rope

landing a haymaker to the back of Playboys head. Playboy is sent into the

ringside barrier cutting open his forehead. Maverick leans over the

barrier a gets a kiss from the blonde. Maverick picks up the Playboy and

bodyslams him into the ringside steps. Maverick then throws Playboy into

the ring. Maverick climbs up the turnbuckle and jumps off going for a

legdrop. Playboy rolls out uf the way and Maverick hits hard. Playboy

picks Maverick up and delivers a swift kick to Mavericks gut. Playboy then

hits with an elbow to the small of Mavericks back. As maverick stands up

Playboy sends him to the mat with a dropkick. Playboy climbs to the top

and points at the blonde. Playboy then drops from the top going for a

splash. But he waisted to much time Maverick had gotten up and he catches

Playboy powerslaming him to the canvas. Maverick then drops a series of

elbows and knees to Playboys right knee. Maverick then picks up Playboy

and sends him back down with a dropkick to the right knee. Maverick then

blows a kiss to the blonde slaps on the sharpshooter and gets Playboy to

submit.

"THE WINNER OF THE MATCH IS MAVERICK THE EXTREME TELEVISION CHAMPION"
