EHWA Pay-Per-View

4 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Here is the recent pay per view matches for the Extreme Hardcore Wrestling
Assocation. As you can see I goe in to a lot of detail and try to treat everybody farely. So e-mail today for a membership.
"WELCOME EVERYONE TO THE SPORTATORIUM HERE IN DOWNTOWN HENDERSON,KY. SO
NOW LETS GET TO THE ACTION. OUR FIRST MATCH IS BETWEEN BUDDAH LOVA AND A
NEWCOMER NAMED PSYCOTHIC." Buddah makes his way to the ring and then comes Psycothic. Both wrestlers walk towards each other and start jaw jacking at each other. Suddenly
Buddah slaps Psycothic. Psycothic thens lands an uppercut on Buddah sending him into the ropes. Buddah bounces back and clotheslines
Psycothic. Psycothic gets up and is clotheslined back to the mat. Buddah pounces on top of him and starts to choke him the ref counts to 4 and
Buddah lets go then he starts to choke him again. The ref then pulls
Buddah off of psycothic and tells him not to cheat again or hes disqualifed.While the ref is repremanding Buddah Psycothic slips up behind him and puts him in the sleeper.Buddah starts to slip fast and out of shear desparation kicks out and pushes of the turnbuckle. Buddah knocks over Psycothic and lands on top of him. Psycothic tries to kick out but cant and the ref makes the three count.
"THE NEXT MATCH IS THE DEBUT OF ANOTHER NEW COMER HERE IS THE RED PANTHER AND HIS OPPONET IS THE WESTSIDE GANGSTA." Gangsta attacks Panther before the match begans by clipping him in the leg. Gangsta then drops 3 elbows to Panthers stomach and several kicks.
Gangsta then picks up Panther and tries for a piledriver but Panther backdrops Gangsta. Panther then rakes Gangstas eyes and kneedrops him in the shoulder. Panther picks Gangsta up and delivers a brainbuster. Panther then climbs to the top rope and leaps off landing a leg drop. Panther makes the cover and gets the win.
"THE NEXT MATCH IS FOR THE LAST SHOT IN THE INTERNET TAG TEAM TOURNMENT. THE TEAMS ARE ASYLUM AND BLOODHOUND VS. THE DARKSIDE." As all four men enter the ring they jump each other. As Asylum battles
Wolf Bloodhound brawls with Reaper. Reaper gets the advantage over
Bloodhound and whips him into the ropes. As Bloodhound hits the ropes
Maverick comes from nowhere and pulls them down sending Bloodhound crashing to the concrete floor. Maverick then puts Bloodhound in the sharpshooter. Asylum sees this and starts to make his way over to help his partner but is stopped short when Wolf clotheslines him. Wolf picks Asylum up and gives him to Reaper who is perched on the top rope. Reaper sets
Asylum up and powerslams him from the top rope. As soon as Reaper moves out of the way Wolf drops a leg across Asylums face and covers him for the win. Maverick then lets go of the sharpshooter grabs the mic and says"NOW
WHERE EVEN YOU PIECE OF CRAP." "THE NEXT MATCH IS A SPECIAL CHALLENGE MATCH. IT PITS THE 6'7" 350 POUNDER JUSTIN BLAZE AGAINST THE 6'4" 252 POUND HADES." Blaze gets in the ring and awaits the arrival of Hades. Hades music comes on but he dosent come out of the dressing room. Both the ref and Blaze are staring at the dressing room and don't see Hades come from under the ring.
Hades gets in and jumps Blaze from behind hitting him with a dropkick. Hades then pick the big man up whips him into the far corner and catches him with a sleeper when he bounces out of the corner. Blaze reverse it so
Hades back is to the turnbuckle and then starts to ram him into the turnbuckles making Hades let go. Blaze stands up as Hades comes running at him. Blaze waits untill Hades is right in front off him and as soon as
Hades gets to him Blaze connects with a belly-to-belly suplex. This totally surpised Hades and Blaze gets the pin.
"THE NEXT MATCH IS FOR THE WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP. THE COMBANTATS ARE
THE KNIGHTS DARKNIGHT AND MIDNIGHT AND THE CHALLENGERS ARE THE DOGGPOUND
DOGGYSTYLE AND KURUPT." Kurupt and Darknight start the match off in a coller and elbow tie up. Kurupt gets the first strike and scoops up Darknight and slams him to the mat. As Darknight stands up Kurupt starts to run at him. Darknight catches him and lifts him high over his head in a gorrilla press. Darknight looks around and then throws Kurupt ontop of the ref. The ref immedtly DQs the champions. But a title can't change hands by DQs
"THE NEXT MATCH IS FOR THE EXTREME TELVISION CHAMPIONSHIP.FIRST THE CHALLENGER VIGLEN AND NOW THE CHAMPION MAVERICK." Maverick enters the ring and stands in the center of the ring looking around he then grabs the mic again and starts to speak."HEY BLOODHOUND I
KNOW YOU'RE AROUND HERE SOME WHERE SO JUST TO MAKE SURE YOU DON' DECIDE TO
INTERFERE IN MY MATCH I BROUGHT BACK UP."At this The Powers of
Destruction, Justin Blaze, Ivan Thor Thunder, Eclipse and Cataclysm come to ringside and stand around."NOW THEN JUST SO YOU DO KNOW BLOODHOUND I
DONT FEAR YOU I DONT SWEAT YOU YOU ARE A PIECE OF TRASH YOU ALWAYS HAVE
BEEN AND ALWAYS WILL BE. I AM THE EXTREME ICON NO ONE CAN BE AS EXTREME AS
ME SO IF YOU DONT WIN TONIGHT ON WENSDAY YOU GET A SHOT AT MY BELT ANY
TYPE OF MATCH YOU WANT." After this Bloodhound and the Knights come down the isle but stop mid-way. The bell sounds and the two wrestlers lock up. Maverick backs Viglen into the ropes and starts to pound on him. Maverick then points to a fan and the fan gives him his chair Maverick grabs the chair and backs away from
Viglen enough to get swinging room. As Maverick brings the chair up Viglen jumps and superkicks the chair into Mavericks face. Maverick drops to the mat. Viglen grabs Maverick and then slams him back down to the mat. Viglen kicks the chair out of the ring then splashes Maverick. Viglen picks
Maverick up and places him on to the top turnbuckle. As Viglen starts to climb up after him Maverick punches him in the stomach knocking him down to the mat. Maverick calls for another chair and gets one. As Viglen starts to stand up Maverick dives at him with the chair. But Viglen sees this and dropkicks the chair into Mavericks face again. Viglen then covers
Maverick and gets the upset.
"THE WINNER OF THE MATCH AND NEW EXTREME TELEVISION CHAMPION VIGLEN." Maverick gets up walks over to Viglen and shakes his hand. Then Thor gets in to the ring as the announcer signals its time for his submission only match against Bloodhoun. All the wrestlers at ringside disperse and let
Bloodhound through. As Bloodhound gets in the ring the bell sounds and the match is under way. Bloodhound tries to chop the smaller man down but
Thors pecs are just to huge to fell pain. Bloodhound then tries to whip
Thor into the ropes but Thor reverses it and hits Bloodhound with a deadly short-arm clothesline. Thor the picks the big man up like a rag doll and drops him back first across his knee not once, not twice, but three times.
Thor then locks Bloodhound in a bearhug from there its only a matter of seconds till the ref calls the match since Bloodhound has passed out from lack of oxygen.
"THE NEXT MATCH WAS TO BE FOR THE EXTREME HARDCORE WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP BUT
ASYLUM IS STILL HURTING FROM THE EARLY MATCH UP. SO INSTEAD OF TONIGHT
HE'LL GET HIS SHOT TOMORROW NIOGHT AT THE INTERNET TAG TEAM TOURMENT WHERE
THE TEAMS OF ECLIPSE ANS CATACLYSM, THE WARLORDS, THE FLYING FRENCH
CANADIANS AND THE DARKSIDE WILL SQUARE OFF. SO UNTILL THEN WE'LL BE SEEING YOU."
