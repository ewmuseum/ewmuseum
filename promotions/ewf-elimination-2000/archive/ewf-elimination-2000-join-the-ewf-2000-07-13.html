Elimination Wrestling Federation: Join the EWF

13 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Elimination Wrestling Federation (EWF) is getting ready to rumble. The web site is up, but we need to fill out the roster before kicking things off. My name is Danny, a multiple time champion in a number of federations over the past few years. Now I have taken up another challenge by starting the EWF.
The EWF is a roleplaying fed, with cards held on Tuesday and Saturday. All matches are simulated with TNM 6.2 plus. Better work in the studio, in card interviews and such give you a better chance at winning. However upsets happen, losing streaks come up, but over time consistent quality work will put the wins on your record.
To those that are inexperienced, I've been there. The EWF site has a number of pages with tips and help that will get you going very quickly.
Take a look, links to all pages are located within the table on the main page. To those who have experience, you will find the EWF to be a great opportunity to strut your stuff.
The first card is when we hit 25 men and the first card will consist of tournament matches for the titles. The link to the EWF application is to the bottom right hand side of the table which is on the main page located at:
