EFW (1997) — Recruitment

[EFW] Seeks Hardcore Writers

12 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EFW (Extreme Fantasy Wrestling) is a real wrestler fed that is currently searching for several writers. The job of writing for EFW is not particularly grueling as we only have one card a week. We're an established e-fed and have had a very successful run. We really only need about 2 or 3 writers, mostly for PPV purposes, as I have pretty much written almost every EFW card myself (I say almost because there are times when I've had help from friends and volunteers, and to them I thank you) since the fed opened, and frankly it's summer and all the constant writing is starting to burn me out. So there you have it. Send me a list of your credentials and experiance and I'll send you a match for you to write. If I like it, the job's yours. Note: You must be able to write hardcore matches. EFW has become notorious for its extreme nature (secondary only to its lateness which is soon to change) and the violent behavior of the wrestlers. It's also about the only real wrestler fed where wrestlers like Xanta Klaus, Who, and Los Chupos Rayadas could hold titles. :) -- EFW President, [name withheld] ------------- Big Yeti Cool Founder of the Yeti World Order and Public Enemy mark extrordinare. Can be seen often on IRC as the semi-sane TPEGrunge. "..........." -The Yeti (His greatest quote)
