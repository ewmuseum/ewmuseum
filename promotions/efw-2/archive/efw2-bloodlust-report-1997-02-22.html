[EFW] Bloodlust Report

22 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The EFW Bloodlust logo is shown as RiddleBox by the Insane Clown Posse plays. Mean Gene is standing in EFW Hardcore Central.] Gene: Hello ladies and gentlemen and welcome to the EFW Bloodlust report. With Bloodlust just one day away, here are the matches you can expect to see. First, on the free for all, we have Dennis Rodman to take on Rob Van Damme in what should be a good match. Next we have a classic: Rey Mysterio Jr. vs. Ultimo Dragon. Now don't forget it was Dragon who put The Great Muta out of wrestling. [screen shows the Great Muta crashing through the table on Wednesday Night Wars] Gene: Then we have the Pay Per view. There will be two battle royals. The winners will face each other THAT same night, to determine which man will get a shot at ALL EFW TITLES somewhere down the line. The battle royals will consist of the following people: 1. Hunter Hearst Helmsley 2. Rocco Rock 3. Johnny Grunge 4. Kevin Nash 5. Randy Savage 6. Ric Flair 7. Sting 8. Bret Hart 9. Vader 10. Dean Malenko 11. Dennis Rodman 12. Diamond Dallas Page 13. Ultimo Dragon 14. Scott Hall 15. Crush 16. Kevin Sullivan 17. Sycho Sid 18. Steve Austin 19. Mr. Perfect 20. The Yeti 21. Great Muta 22. Zeus 23. Raven 24. Honky Tonk Man 25. The Blue Meanie 26. "Pistol Pez Whatley 27. Disco Inferno 28. Cactus Jack 29. Chris Benoit 30. "Genius" Lanny Poffo _______________________________ 31. Blue Blazer 32. Bastion Booger 33. Mustafa Saed 34. New Jack 35. Brian Pillman 36. Shawn Michaels 37. Konnan 38. Damien Demento 39. The Goon 40. Hulk Hogan 41. Yokozuna 42. El Gigante 43. El Gigantito 44. Kronos 45. Saturn 46. Ax 47. Smash 48. Andre The Giant 49. Junkyard Dog 50. Arn Anderson 51. Tito Santana 52. The British Bulldog 53. Chupos Rayadas #1 54. Chupos Rayadas #2 55. Terry Funk 56. Mantaur 57. PitBull#1 58. Pitbull#2 59. Sabu 60. Abdullah the Butcher After that, The PitBulls take on Mystery opponents. Los Chupos Rayadas will take on Da Gangstas. Don't forget it was the Rayadas that stabbed Jim Ross and put him in the coma he's in now. Sabu will take on El Gigante. Kevin Sullivan of the Crips will take on The Mountie. The Crips/Horsemen fued continues in an Anaheim Highway Brawl between the Yeti and Junk Yard Dog. This match comes about after each of their last two matches ended in the highway. Both participants will start out on the median in the highway outside the stadium, and a pinfall can only occur on the highway. Steve Austin of The Lethal Alliance will take on Berzerker of the Stable. This match will be interesting because Austin is managed by Sunny, who had been kidnapped by the Stable. Berzerker was ordered by President Tunney to wrestle this match after Berzerker stabbed Lex Luger on Hardcore TV two weeks ago. Next, EFW TV Champion The Undertaker will defend against Dean Malenko. The Funkadelics, Ahmed Johnson and Flash Funk, will defend their Tag straps against two members of the Crips, Public Enemy. Anyone who interferes will be fined and suspended. In another fued that's been hot for sometime, LA member Diamond Dallas Page will attempt to regain his EFW U.S. Heavyweight Title from The Stable's Xanta Klaus. And, in what may very well be the most violent match of the night, Raven, Terry Funk, Abdullah, and Kevin Nash of the Crips will take on Horsemen Randy Savage, Ric Flair, Andre the Giant and Vader in an electric steel cage, barbed wire, broken glass thumbtacks match. The Horsemen/Crips fued is more violent than ever, especially since Raven attacked Vader with a baseball bat, costing him a win over Taz. Finally, the Main Event of EFW Bloodlust will be Taz of the Crips to defend his EFW World Title against the man who won Hardcore TV's Triangle og Terror Match. That man is none other than the Lethal Alliance's Giant. The Vegas odds makers are saying that TAZ is the favorite, mostly because of the Crips' habit of interfering. We'll have all this, plus a report on the condition of Jim Ross, this Sunday at the Duck Pond in Anaheim, at EFW presents Bloodlust. Until then, this is Mean Gene saying...so long.
