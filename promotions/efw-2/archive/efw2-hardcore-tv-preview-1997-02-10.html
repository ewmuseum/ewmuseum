EFW Hardcore TV Preview

10 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(For those of you who don't know, Extreme Fantasy Wrestling is a fed that uses real wrestlers and is without a doubt, a VERY extreme fed) _______________________________________________________________________________________ (Mean Gene Okerlund is shown in the EFW Hardcore Headquarters.) Gene Okerlund: Well, fans, the action in EFW has heated up to an unbelievable rate. We already have a very large number of wrestlers and Bloodlust is shaping up to be one of the greatest events of all time. Tomarrow night, on Hardcore TV, we have a bevy of extreme matches, which are as usual, anything goes. Let's go down the list. 1. Kevin Nash vs. Sycho Sid, 2. Da Gangstas to take on The Eliminators, 3. Andre the Giant will make his EFW debut against El Gigante, 4. The famed midget wrestler, El Gigantito will take on Mantaur, 5. EFW Television champion The Undertaker will take on The Giant in a non-title match, 6. Los Chupos Rayadas will debut against Public Enemy. Now don't forget that PE is still reeling from their loss to the World Leaders during the EFW Tag Team tournament. 7. The Yeti will attempt to continue The Crips' win streak, as he takes on The newest Horseman, Junkyard Dog. 8. The Crips' Raven will take on a mystery wrestler, who isn't even signed with EFW! 9. Here's one with alot of background. Lethal Alliance member Stone Cold Steve Austin will take on one of The Stable's most prominent people, Damien Demento. You will remember that this whole Stable/LA fued started when Clarence Mason, Xanta Klaus, and Bastion Booger kidnapped LA manager, Sunny. They then proceeded to make her their slave, and she finally escaped last Monday. Sunny's back with the LA, but the feud is long from over. Alot more blood's gonna be spilled over this one. 10. The main event will be EFW World Heavywieght Champion and member of The Crips, Taz in a non-title match with Vader, representative of the Horsemen. Again, the Horsemen/Crips feud continues. One can only wonder what will happen when they step in that electric, barbed wire, broken glass, thumbtacks, bring your own weapons cage match! Well, fans, I thought that was it, but I have just recieved word from President Tunney's office that another match will occur after the main event! Lex Luger of the Lethal Alliance will take on a mystery member of The Stable! Not only that, but the type of match will be chosen by the LA and wil be known to NO ONE until it occurs! Not only that, but we'll find out if and when new EFW US Champion Xanta Klaus will defend the title against the man he beat for the title just one week ago, Diamond Dallas Page. It's gonna be an action packed night of extreme wrestling, plus, we'll find out if The Great Muta's neck will heal in time to wrestle at Bloodlust. Until next time, this is Mean Gene saying so long.
