(Camera fades in on a mountain ledge. "Condor" Eric King is in street clothes, sitting comfortably with his legs dangling over the edge of the precipice. In a moment, he turns, puts his back to the precipice, and faces the camera.) [King] I feel calm now. Calm for the first time in a very long time. This whole business with Revi has had me on edge for a very long time now, along with the fact that I felt that I wasn't getting the respect I thought I deserved. Things have changed now. The deal with Revi will come to a head at Ultimatum -- and I think the two of us are content to leave each other alone until then, as both of us know full well that that match may be the most dangerous one of our respective careers. As for respect... I'll be the first to admit that, in hindsight, I may have demanded respect a little too soon -- before I actually had done something to deserve it. But you see, /I/ knew that I deserved respect, and when other people didn't seem to be giving it to me, I got a little angry. I apologize to Danny Boy McGill, John Robertson, and Mist Angel for anything negative I may have said. Even though I may not have deserved it, however, John Robertson decided to give me a match, so that I may prove that I could hang with the "Big Boys", so to speak. And I proved it. In a big way. I haven't checked the records, but I believe I am one of only a handful of people to ever pin John Robertson fair and square in the middle of the ring. And for that, I earned the respect that I thought I deserved. I have to say that I have the utmost respect for John Robertson, and I hope he now has the same respect for me. And I'm certain that if Robertson respects me, the rest of the Alliance for Order does as well. Respect (King's brow furrows a bit). It's a big word. Maybe not physically, but all the weight it carries... It's a big deal. I'm smart enough to know that respect isn't something that you earn once, and never lose. Respect is something that requires you to live up to a certain standard. I'd lose my respect for John Robertson if he started cheating to win matches, and I'm certain the same can be said for him about me. So I intend to keep on doing things to earn the respect of my fellow Light Heavy Weights. But right now, I think I'm going to step out of my boundaries once again. It may be a bit premature, but hey, I never learned anything from playing it safe -- I want a title shot. Dan Lea! You listening? John Robertson was the official Number One Contender for your belt. I know beating him doesn't automatically make me Number One Contender, but it should rocket me up to the point where I at least become a blip on your radar screen. So I'm giving you a chance to take on that blip right now. Hey, for all you know, pinning Robertson could have been a fluke -- maybe I'm just a flash in the pan, Lea... But I doubt it! And just to put a little added pressure on you, I think I'll sic Robbie Stevens on you as well! Are /you/ listening, Stevens? If Lea puts the title up for grabs against me before Ultimatum, and I win, I'll put the title on the line against your man during our match at Ultimatum. I hate the idea of Stevens and me being allies of a sort, but hey, you didn't exactly win that title on the up-and-up, Lea, so I don't feel so bad about doing something I wouldn't do to a more respectable champion. I guess that's all I have to say today -- as if that wasn't enough! Until the Condor flies again...
