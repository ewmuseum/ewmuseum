The camera pans across the crowd of USeW faithfull along with fans from the AWI, EWC, EWF, IeWS and VCW as the camera stops at the commentators' table, where we see Tim Hoss and Mr. Wrestling IV standing in tuxedos as the fans around them are going absolutely nuts.) [Tim Hoss] Welcome to the USeW and Leprecharnage! I'm Tim Hoss and tonight it's my pleasure to be announcing what can only be described as the biggest and most star studded Interfederation supercard in the history of the USeW. [Mr. Wrestling IV] And the biggest star in the Summit Wrestling Alliance is right here Tim. [Hoss] How can you say that? We have some the giants of the sport here tonight; the Honor Guard, 'Suicidal' Sean Altman, 'Mr Machismo' Hank Thompson and our very own 187. [MWIV] Oh, and I'm sure you'll also bring up the White Knights, the Equalizers, Blackjack and Jack Diamond. [Hoss] I don't have to but you forgot the two biggest stars of the Summit Wrestling Alliance; the EWC Heavyweight Champion Grim Weibaq versus the IeWS Heavyweight Champion and the USeW's very own Derek Machismo. [MWIV] Oh who cares! Tonight we're starting off with two of my favorite teams about to beat each other's brains out. Let's hear some previously recorded comments from Hojo Tojomoto and his Imperial Japanese Navy. (Hojo Tojomoto, the Japanese Sandman, and the Imperial Japanese Navy are stalking down the aisle of a prison or jail flanked by cells containing men in orange prison jumpers.) Hojo: So, Obayun Antonio, you choose to persist in your arrogant decision despite fair warning. Your two pitiful criminals are unworthy to face true warriors such as my Imperial Navy, and it is they who shall pay the price for your inability to percieve that simple fact. Prisoner: Hey sweet cheeks, you talk a good fight, but when the Don's boys get ahold of you I bet you start squealing a different tune. 2nd Prisoner: Yeah, lard-butt, Big Bruno will have your two bu-fu buddys here bawling fer mercy in no time at all. (BB Musashi and BB Yamato promptly silence the two men by grabbing them by the throats through the gaps in the bars. Musashi pulls his victim up against the bars forcefully and then slams him across his cell to crumple up against the far wall. Yamato reaches through another gap in the bars to grab his man by crotch and throat, reverses him, and slams him headfirst into the cell's concrete floor.) All: Banzai!!!BANZAI!!! B_A_N_Z_A_I!!! [MWIV] It's a damn shame when two classy teams like La Famiglia and the IJN resort to name calling and have to settle their differences in the ring. [Hoss] Remind me to cry a river later. We're just about ready to start, so let's head to the ring and hear from our own Motormouth. [Motormouth] Ladies and gentlemen... welcome to the USeW's first ever Leprecharnage in the beautiful FleetCenter. This first match is one fall with a twenty minute time limit. Being led to the ring at this time by their manager, Don Antonio, they are Big Bruno and Jack Mafia... LA FAMIGLIA!!!! (The theme to The Godfather fills the arena and is quickly drowned out by the boos of the crowd. Big Bruno and Jack Mafia smirk as Don Antonio blows smoke in the faces of the fans.) [Hoss] And it would appear the fans really hate the former Television champions. [MWIV] Who gives a rat's ass what the pions think. Don Antonio, can't we just settle this peacefully? [Antonio] No my friend. These fools need to be taught to respect their betters. [Motormouth] And their opponents... they weigh in at an impressive 1089 lbs and hail from Somewhere in the Former Japanese Empire. Being led to the ring by their manager Tojo Hojomoto, they are Battleships Yamato and Musashi... THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE NAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAVVVVVVVVVYYYYYYYYYYYYYY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The theme to Jaws floods the FleetCenter and the boos rain down from the crowd. Yamato and Mushashi waddle to the ring while Tojo Hojomoto taps his rattan cane against the guard railing.) [Hoss] And it's broken down already! La Famiglia has charged the IJN and are clotheslined into next week. Big Bruno up kickly and he's trading forearm blows with the big Battleship Musashi. [MWIV] Talk about a rundant statement. Yamato is pounding on Jack Mafia and the ref has started a ten count. Tojo over quickly and he convinces his man to take the fight into the ring. [Hoss] Mafia to his feet with a quick kick, but Yamato dodges and shoves Jack into the corner. Yamato charges in and catches a forearm to the face. Mafia to the top rope and he stuns the big Battleship with an elbow smash. Irish whip and Mafia ducks a clothesline... and another... AND YAMATO IS NEARLY DECAPITATED BY A DEADLY CLOTHESLINE AND COVERS! 1... 2... Battleship Yamato kicked out at 2 and a half and Mafia slaps on the Figure Four! Yamato is refusing to give up and Mafia has it really synched in! [MWIV] Musashi slides in and gives Mafia a face rack which breaks the hold and Big Bruno pulls the big man back out. Mafia catches Yamato with a small package and covers. 1... 2... and Yamato kicks out at 2 and a half again! [Hoss] And on the outside Musashi has blinded Big Bruno with powder and drops him across the guard railing. Yamato and Mafia lock up and Yamato shoves him into his home corner and makes the tag. Musashi in slowly and Mafia makes him pay with a thunderous right hook. Mafia and Musashi lock up AND MAFIA JUST BODYSLAMMED THE BIG MAN!!!! [MWIV] It was more like a hip toss you idiot. Mafia picks Musashi up by his hair and catches a kick to the gut for his trouble. Chop by the Battleship and Mafia hits the mat hard. Musashi hit the ropes AND MISSES A RUNNING SPLASH! Mafia catches him with a DDT and covers. 1... 2... And now it's Battleship Musashi that has kicks out at 2 and a half! [Hoss] Jack Mafia has been fighting this match pretty much on his own from the get go and he is holding up pretty well. Don Antonio has Big Bruno up and helps the big guy to his corner. [MWIV] A lot of good it's doing his partner. Musashi had Mafia in his home corner now and the Imperial Japanese Navy is pounding on Jack Mafia like there's no tomorrow. Musashi is setting up for the Pearl Harbor he makes the tag. Irish whip and Mafia goes into the corner hard. Yamato with the flying avalanche THAT MISSES! MUSASHI CAN'T STOP AND HE AVALANCHES HIS OWN PARTNER! [Hoss] Mafia with the hot tag to Big Bruno WHO CLOTHESLINES MUSASHI OUT OF THE RING! ELBOW SMASH ON YAMATO AND BRUNO COVERS!!!! 1... 2... 3... AND LA FAMIGLIA WINS!!!!! Winners: La Famiglia +3 Time 9:10 [MWIV] I can't believe it. I thought Bruno was done for the night and he comes up big in the end. And do you see Tojo Hojomoto? He's livid! [Hoss] That he is, but the decision stands for our first match of the night and what a way to start the night. Here's hoping the rest of the card measures up to this. [MWIV] Well you'll be measuring up without me pal. I just got word I'm needed in the back, so you're on your own. Don't choke man. (Mr. Wrestling IV takes off his headset and walks off as the fans boo like mad. And just as quickly, the fans go nuts with cat calls as Boni Blackmail joins Tim Hoss at the announcer's table.) [Hoss] And now I've been joined by our very own Boni Blackmail. Welcome Boni. [Boni] I am happy you boys decided to let a woman be part of the broadcast team. Considering I knew for the longest time that this is what the USeW needed. [Hoss] It couldn't hurt... the second match of the evening is Sterling Silver vs. Master of Metal these two men have yet to participate in a USeW card. [Boni] What a great way to start off you career in the USeW right here at LEPRECHARNAGE!!!!!!!!! [Motormouth] Being led to the ring by his manager Mr. Gold, weighing in at 235 lbs. from Seattle, Washington, here is MASTER OF METALLLL!!!!! (Master of the Puppets by Metallica fills the arena and the crowd gives Mater of Metal a pretty good pop) [Motormouth] Now coming to the ring weighing in at 224 lbs from Chicago, Illinois. STERLING SILVER!!!!!!!!!!! (The crowd roars with approval. Silver comes out with a smile and showing off his silver sequin vest.) [Hoss] Two fan favorites here in the USeW and they are about ready to face off in the ring. [Boni] Big deal neither one of them have actually done anything to warrant any real success in the USeW. Silver hasn't wrestled since the JCWF and Master of Metal has been quiet considering his stable-mate Metal Maniac has had a title shot and is out there trying to go places. (Hoss gives a dirty glare over at Boni) [Hoss] Well they are here tonight to get their careers going in the right direction. They are about ready to faceoff and the men lock up! Master of Metal gains control and goes for a suplex. Silver blocks it and rolls up Master of Metal! Silver only gets a one count! [Boni] What did you expect Silver to really get the pin. No use even trying for a move like that if it has no chance of winning the match. [Hoss] Have you ever heard of the element of surprise?!? [Boni] Master of Metal is a trained professional wrestler they are supposed to be prepared for surprises especially this early in a match. [Hoss] Very true. Both men in the center of the ring and Master of Metal bounces off the ropes and runs towards Silver. Master of Metal tries a clothesline which Silver ducks. Master of Metal comes off the ropes and Sterling Silver hits Master of Metal in the chops with a beautiful dropkick. Master of Metal gets up right away. [Boni] MoM bounces off the ropes again and Sterling Silver quickly follows him into the ropes and hits him with a clothesline of his own. MoM is down on the ground holding his throat. [Hoss] Sterling Silver goes to the top rope and waits, MoM doesn't see him. He gets up slowly, looks around and gets nailed by a double axe handle. [Boni] Silver goes for the pin!!! MoM kicks out at the count of 1. Silver tries to keep MoM on the ground but he gets up and they are facing each other in the ring. MoM appears to be taking a time out as he goes towards his corner and has a quick conference with Mr. Gold. Satisfied, MoM comes out of the corner looking very determined. [Hoss] MoM and Sterling Silver lock up and Silver with a knee lift to the stomach of MoM. Now they are trading blows. Silver seems to be the better man at brawling. Sterling Silver knocking Master of Metal to the ground and climbs up to the top rope again. [Boni] MoM to his feet and he quickly grabs Silver and slams him off the top rope. Then he drops and elbow and covers Silver. Silver kicks out at the count of 1. [Hoss] Both men trying quick manuevers to pull off the victory. Both men up in the middle of the ring and MoM tries to kick Silver. Silver dodges and grabs MoM and takes him down with an armdrag. MoM getting very frustrated. [Boni] Silver is impressive he can brawl and yet he is smart enough and good enough to be a very impressive technician. [Hoss] MoM quickly up to his feet and Silver bodyslams him down to the mat. Mr. Gold starting to shout encouragement to MoM to get him going as he knows his protege is getting badly outwrestled at this point. Silver is starting to dance in the ring as he is clearly in control at this point. [Boni] Silver quickly applies an armbar and then follows that up with a kick to the knee to bring MoM to one knee. It look like Silver is trying to apply leverage on the armbar from being in a higher position than MoM. [Hoss] Sound technical wrestling. MoM picks up Silver and tries to bodyslam Silver while in the hold to try to break the hold. Silver hangs on tight. (One minute passes by with MoM in the armbar) [Hoss] Silver lets go of the armbar and headbutts MoM to the canvas. Silver picks MoM up and does a standing dropkick. MoM is on the mat. Silver quickly in for the cover! 1!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!! 2 1/2 !!!!!!! and a kick out by Master of Metal! [Boni] MoM better get some offense going otherwise he will have a mark in the loss column very quickly. [Hoss] He must have been listening as he gets up and punches Sterling Silver in the face followed up by an elbow to the head. [Boni] MoM bodyslams Silver to the mat and goes for the cover. Silver out at the 2 count. Both men get to their feet but Silver takes control first and pushes MoM into the corner. The two men trade forearm smashes. [Hoss] Silver gains control and smashes MoMs head into the turnbuckle!!. MoM shoots an elbow into the midsection of Silver and falls down along with him. [Boni] MoM back to his feet and he picks up Silver and does a kneebreaker on Silver. Goes for the cover!!!! 1!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!! He kicks out. [Hoss] MoM picks up Silver and does a backbreaker. Silver really felt that one. As he gets up he is holding his back. MoM clotheslines him back to the mat. Silver gets up and locks up with MoM. Silver headbutts MoM down to the mat and stomps on his head. Silver goes for a leg drop and MoM rolls out of the way. Both men back on their feet and they lock up. [Boni] MoM bodyslams Silver and bounces off the ropes to gather up speed. As Silver slowly gets up, Master of Metal nails him with a DEVASTATING CLOTHESLINE!!!!!!! MoM then kicks Silver in the legs and goes for the Metal Braces (Scorpion Death Lock)!!!! Silver starts screaming in pain. Silver knows he's in big trouble and starts crawling very slowly to the ropes. (one minute passes by as Silver is very slowly getting to the ropes. Mr. Gold shouts encouragement to lock it on tighter.) [Hoss] Silver gets to the ropes and breaks the hold. MoM breaks cleanly and Silver gets up. MoM on him quickly with a punch to the ribs and a forearm to the chin. MoM whips Silver to the ropes. Silver coming back and is on the receiving end of an awesome spinebuster. Silver can't take too much more damage to his back. MoM going to pick up Silver and he gets poked in the eyes!! [Boni] Silver trying to survive uses desperate measures and is getting reprimanded by the referee. Silver gets up and kicks Master of Metal in the leg. MoM locks up with Silver and pushes him to the ropes. MoM is punching and kicking Silver. Silver being softened up and whipped to the ropes. MoM takes him down with a beautiful drop toehold. He then goes for the Metal Braces!!!!!! [Hoss] I don't think he can do it again, Silver is too far away from the ropes to survive as long as he did last time. The crowd is loving every moment of this as the two combatants are battling to get a win for themselves. Silver crawling as fast as he can to the ropes. He gets there! I didn't think it was in him to get to the ropes that quickly. MoM is very frustrated. He has sinched in that hold twice and can't put him away. [Boni] Silver showing alot of fortitude. Both men get up and Silver make MoM double over with a swift kick to the gut. Silver whips MoM to the ropes...it's reversed!!!!!!!! Silver catches MoM with a dropkick. Back and forth these two men have battled but yet neither can put the other away. Silver picks up MoM and bodyslams him to the mat. [Hoss] Silver taking control again picking up MoM. He sets him up for the DDT but Master of Metal shoves Silver away and falls to the mat. MoM and Silver are locked up again... MoM tries to suplex Silver and Silver blocks it as he takes MoM down with a leg sweep. [Boni] MoM down and trips Silver both men on their knees punching each other back and forth. Silver falls to the mat. MoM picks him up and whips him to the ropes. MoM catches Silver off the ropes and... WHAT AN AWESOME TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER!!! I think this match is over. MoM going for the cover!!!!! 1!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!! 2 1/2!!!!!!!!! [Hoss] Silver kicks out. This is incredible these two men don't want to lose. MoM going to pick up Silver! Silver tucks and rolls Master of Metal in a small package!!!! 1!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Winner: Sterling Silver +3 Time: 21:40 [Motormouth] The winner of this contest at the 21:40 mark of the match is STERLING SILVERRRR!!!!!!!! [Boni] Incredible match with an array of devastating moves and alot of willpower. Mr. Gold will have to build up the confidence of Master of Metal especially after coming so close and yet losing. [Hoss] I think you might have been right when you said a surprise move in the later stages of a match like the small package has a much higher chance of succeeding than in the early stages. [Boni] You doubted me. Hoss I am a well respected member of the wrestling broadcast community. I think that I know a little bit about wrestling. [Hoss] A little bit indeed. The night continues as we get ready for another debut here in the USeW. Professor Nickedemus Payne has been quiet upon entering this federation and tonight he faces a man who has made an impression with his violent attitude. "The Bodybreaker" Hal Johnston has already made it known that he enjoys hurting people and perhaps Professor Payne will be "broken" before he even has a chance to "break" in to the USeW. [Boni] Been practicing the puns, Tim? Get a life. Professor Payne has been successful in the indy feds and now hes got a chance to his the big time here in the USeW. Too bad its going to be all over for him in his debut match with Hal Johnston. [Hoss] The fans aren't thinking so as they are giving Professor Payne a mild welcome to the USeW! Here he comes! [Motormouth] Coming to the ring, from Winnipeg, Manitoba, CANADA!!! At 6'9", 295 lbs...here is Professor Nickedemus Payyyyynnnne!! (Professor Nickedumus Payne is walking to the ring slapping hands with fans and smiling as he looks around at the massive crowd at Leprecharnage. "Hey Stupid" by Alice Cooper is blaring from the loudspeakers.) [Boni] He's hit the big leagues. Time to show hes worthy of being in the Summit. My personal opinion...he doesn't have what it takes...especially not against "The Bodybreaker". [Motormouth] And his opponent, from Kitchener, Ontario CANADA....weighing in at 320 lbs and is 6'7"..."The Bodybreakerrrrrrrrrr" Hal Johnstonnnnnn!!!! [Hoss] We'll find out soon enough. The crowd has just started booing as Jo-anne McLoud is leading her big man, Hal Johnston to the ring. OH NO! It looks like he's getting in the face of a fan at ringside...we don't want another airport incident here at Leprecharnage! [Boni] Relax Tim. Hes getting in the ring without any problems and Jo-ann is now in the face of Professor Payne shouting at him through her megaphone. [Hoss] Professor Payne is not pleased at all! WAIT!! Hal Johnston has just hit the Professor from behind! What a distraction!! This match is underway!! [Boni] The two men are locked up and Johnston has just tried a quick pinning attempt after delivering an atomic drop. [Hoss] Payne kicks out at the count of one and Hal Johnston has gotten up and has smashed a big boot to the back of Paynes head. He's picking up the Professor and goes for the lock up but the Professor slides out and backs off. [Boni] Professor Payne locks up again and this time sends Johnston to the mat with a hip toss. Now he's bending down to pick Johnston up and Johnston surprises him with a quick DDT!! A pin attempt! 1...... 2....... 2 1/2! [Hoss] Nickedemus Payne kicks out and Jo-anne McLoud is enraged! She's cursing the ref for the slow count. Johnston isn't letting up though and delivers another big boot to the head of Payne. He's making this into a brawl that the more technically inclined Professor doesn't seem to enjoy. [Boni] Johnston is lifting the Professor up now and appears to be going for a spinebuster but out of nowhere the Professor has reversed that into a Frankensteiner!! [Hoss] It doesn't seem like there was a lot of strength behind it though because Johnston is back up to his feet and has just rushed Payne and is driving his head against the ring post. He's now choking him against the turnbuckle and the ref is warning him to back off. [Boni] Jo-ann Mcloud is on the ring apron jabbering in the face of Professor Payne as Johnston is being held back by the ref. [Hoss] Payne is resting in the corner oblivious to the rantings of McLoud. Johnston is now in the opposite corner and is charging the corner. Professor Payne has apparantly said something to McLoud who is still on the ring apron and she's swinging her megaphone!! Payne has ducked!! [Boni] Johnston has just been levelled by the megaphone! He looks like he's out of it! Payne has just collapsed on top of him and the ref is in position! 1..... 2..... 3!! DING!! DING!! DING!! [Motormouth] Your winner at the 3 minute mark.....PROFESSOR NICKEDEMUS PAYYYYYNNNEEE!!!!! Winner: Professor Payne +3 Time: 3:00 [Hoss] Jo-ann McLoud is visibly upset as she is standing in shock at what has transpired. It appeared that Johnston had the match wrapped up and that mistake just cost him that match! [Boni] Nothing to be ashamed of though. Professor had the luck of the Irish tonight...as simple as that..I'd like to know what he said to McLoud to get her to swing that megaphone. She's now helping Johnston who is coming around. I think he's looking for Payne and doesn't seem to be mad at McLoud at all. [Hoss] Well Payne has been dubbed the Professor for a reason. He's way too smart to be sticking around after that mishap. [Boni] Smart? Or scared? Anyways...Johnston is now making his way back to the locker room as the next match is about to get underway. [Hoss] Next up on the card is the return of another Machismo brother...who at one point in time was co-holder of those very belts the White Knights hold dear today. The tag scene has just been spiced up with the addition of the Smooth Criminals. [Boni] OOhh... we get to see some real men in Duane Machismo and Shane Matthews. [Hoss] Thats what you say about every wrestler. Don't you ever think of something else except your personal fantasies. [Boni] My personal fantasies are none of your business, I was just saying that these men could be the next World Champions. [Hoss] Yeah sure. I think that the tag-team scene in the USeW is heating up and with these guys coming in things are only going get hotter. We'll see how they fare against the team of White Tiger and Red Dragon. These men are known for there quickness. They will have to be slowed up by the more powerful Smooth Criminals. [Boni] You are forgetting they have an edge by having Tara Jensen as a manger. Also Matthews is a great martial artist in his own right. The Connection have never faced a martial artist before. [Hoss] Here we go as the Chinese Connection are running to the ring and getting this crowd pumped up.
