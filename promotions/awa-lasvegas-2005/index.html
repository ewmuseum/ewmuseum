A Las Vegas-based fantasy federation whose surviving record is the pay-per-view Blood Is Thicker Than Water, at which Cody Carson took the World title from Mike Young on 25 September 2005 under an interim boss, and a Saturday Night Adrenaline card of 19 November 2005, with the roleplays of five of its characters beside them. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The American Wrestling Alliance of 2005 survives in two cards and in the roleplays of the people who wrote five of its characters. Blood Is Thicker Than Water, staged at the Pontiac Silverdome on 25 September 2005, opens with Lexus Malone announcing that she is taking charge until the powers that be return and that a weekly Sunday show, Adrenaline, is now born. The night moves Cody Carson from the head of the A3 stable to the World title over Mike Young, splits A3 as Michael Manson takes the Continental title from Derek Cantina, unifies the X and Prime Time belts under Simon Sensation, sends The Rumbler through a table for the Chaos title, and has Rick Young leave his wife in the middle of her Fem-X title match.

Saturday Night Adrenaline, edition 5, from the MGM Grand in Las Vegas on 19 November 2005, puts Lexus Malone and Maxwell Williams at the desk, gives Michael Ryba a forfeit win over a missing Michael Manson, sees Brandi James beat Mike Young and Adonis beat Rick Young, and ends with Jimmy Stryker refereeing The Rumbler's Continental title win over Johnny Arnette. The roleplays beside the cards, stamped by the host between September 2005 and April 2007, follow Brandi James, Jimmy Stryker, Skylar Black, Jamie Brant and a manager named Hobo Nick whose client is a cardboard box. The federation's own site, its founding and its end are not in the record.
