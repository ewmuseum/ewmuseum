AWA (Las Vegas, 2005) — Roleplay (Jamie Brant)

Jamie Brant 007

August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*`*`*`*Jamie Brant*`*`*`* [ By now it's common knowledge that Jamie Brant is a bit....odd? Or shall we say, a bit of an over involved fan? Either way you cut it her success can't be denied. Today Jamie is doing what she does best. Yep that's right, she's spending her day doing her best to fit into the world of her idol Brandi James-Young. Jamie has spent the day at the Golden Gate Spa in Oakland, California. She's relaxing in a chair with a cucumber mud mask on her face, as she is getting a pedicure. Much to her surprise she hears a very familiar voice that makes her jump up with excitement as she hugs ..That’s right her idol Brandi James-Young. Brandi slightly nudges Jamie as and shakes her head.]
Brandi James-Young: "Hey calm down there..." [ Jamie takes a deep breath to gain her composure. ] Jamie Brant: "Wow...I'm sorry...I guess I'm a bit happy and surprised all at the same time to see you, but in a good way."
Brandi James-Young: "Well, I'm not here to see you. I just felt like a little me time." Jamie Brant:"Me time?" [ Brandi laughs at how simple Jamie's thinking can be. ] Brandi James-Young: "Sure Jamie, every woman needs a little me time. It's what keeps us sane."
Jamie Brant: "Oh? I thought.." [ Brandi puts her hand up and Jamie immediately stops talking.] Brandi James-Young: "Has someone been advising you differently?" Jamie Brant: "No....I know what to do if someone tries." Brandi James-Young:" Glad to hear it." [ Brandi looks around then notices a manicurist near by. She snaps her fingers and an oriental woman comes over with out a second thought. ]
Brandi James-Young:" It's about time! I was wondering if you people even understand the value of money."
Jamie Brant:" They do...but they call it yen." [ Jamie answers as she stands on her tip toes and sways as if she just clued Brandi into something that she did not know. Brandi raises her eyebrow and sneers at Jamie.]
Brandi James-Young: "Why don't we make a rule...." Jamie Brant: "Oooooo....like a game?" [ Brandi rolls her eyes. ] Brandi James-Young: "Right...like a game. It's called...Jamie only speaks when spoken to." [ Jamie puts her hands on her chest and is thrilled. ] Jamie Brant: "Oh My God! The game has my name in it? That's soooooo cool! I totally can do that!"
Brandi James-Young: "Ok...good! the game starts now!" [ Jamie tightens her lips and pretends that she is zippering her mouth, then tossing the key away. Brandi takes a seat at the manicure table. She puts her hands into the soak and leans back. She stops a worker walking back and demands as she points to Jamie. ]
Brandi James-Young: "Excuse me...Will you bring a box of crayons and some paper to that girl over there?"
Worker: "The girl getting the pedicure?" Brandi James-Young: "Yes, but don't speak to her. She's not allowed to talk for now." Worker: "Yes Miss...right away." [ Jamie leans forward and is making hissing sounds to get Brandi's attention. ] Jamie Brant: "Pssssttttt......Psssttttt..." [ With her back to Jamie, Brandi does not turn around but speaks to her. ] Brandi James-Young: "No...no...no sounds...We're suppose to be relaxing. That is unless you want me to go to another spa for my day of relaxing."
[ Jamie stops and sits back in her chair. She is brought the paper and crayons. She looks at the worker a bit unsure. The worker smiles and points to Brandi. ]
Worker:"The woman getting her nails done asked me to bring this to you." [ Jamie takes them for the worker and the worker walks away. Jamie can't help but yell. ] Jamie Brant: "Brandi James is the best friend ever!" [ Brandi's eyes widen as she whips her head to the side to look at Jamie. Jamie grins and mouths the words "I'm sorry." Brandi nods then continues to get her French manicure. Jamie takes the blue crayon and begins to write on the paper. As she does she's stopped by fingers tapping on the paper. Jamie looks up and waves her hands rapidly at Brandi's brother CJ. ]
CJ: "Hey squirt. What's going on?" [ Jamie bouncing her head from side to side as if to say to CJ that she is doing well. CJ frowns and questions her. ]
CJ:"Are you saving your energy for your match with Scarlett at Lost Souls?" [ Jamie puts her hand over her stomach and silently laughs. ] CJ:" So that's funny to you?" [ Jamie nods her head. As Brandi turns a bit in her chair. ] Brandi James-Young: "CJ leave her alone. Don't you have a blunt to blaze or something?" CJ: "Ha! Full of jokes today aren't we sister?" Brandi James-Young: " I can't help it you just make it that easy." CJ: "Look, all Im here for is to get as manicure and seaweed body wrap." [ Brandi shakes her head, as Jamie covers her mouth trying not to laugh.] Brandi James-Young: "You know what CJ...I'm going to forget you even said that." CJ:"There is nothing wrong with that." Brandi James-Young: "Yeah, if you're a woman there is nothing wrong with that.." CJ:" Just pay attention to what you are doing." Brandi James-Young: "As long as that means I'm not talking to you." [ CJ flips Brandi off without her knowing, and pulls a chair next to Jamie.] CJ: "So...exactly what do you know about Scarlett?" [ Jamie shrugs her shoulders, and flips the paper over, and writes then slides it to CJ who reads it aloud.]
CJ: "Does it really matter?...Yes it matters Jamie. Don't you think she's going to have something to prove after her loss to Faith last week. Not to mention you coming to gain up on her with the she-devil over there."
[ Brandi pipes up. ] Brandi James-Young:"I heard that CJ....and just a reminder that you are on very thin ice. Need I remind you that you live in MY guest house."
CJ: "Nope you don't. But lucky for me YOUR husband said that I can stay there as long as I'd like. "
Brandi James-Young: "We'll see about that..." [ Brandi mumbles under her breath. CJ swats his hand in Brandi's direction as if to say never mind her. He leans closer to Jamie and speaks a bit lower so Brandi can barely hear. ]
CJ: "Jamie. What you did to Scarlett on the Fiasco show...You should be worried. I mean do you really know what she's capable of?"
[ Jamie huffs and blows a bit of air from her cheeks. She takes a purple crayon and writes then slides it to CJ.]
CJ:""People who use the spitting on people method as a defense show they will go to desperate measure. It means you had them doubting themselves and they didn't know what to do." Ummm...that could be very true. But also some people view that as a way to insult someone."
[ Jamie shakes her head, and writes again. Then slams her crayon down. ] CJ: "No! The bitch knew Brandi had her dead to rights. She resorted to a desperate act! And No one gets away with spitting on my idol! The bitch is toast!" I guess...Brandi can never say that you aren't loyal. ":
[ CJ touches Jamie's hand. ] CJ: "Hun, all I'm trying to say is that if you find yourself in trouble when facing Scarlett what you should do is quickly grab her arm and..."
[ Jamie covers her ears fast, closes her eyes tightly and begins to hum "Wanna be" by the spice girls as loudly as she can. Brandi clinches her teeth hearing it, and stands up. She stands next to CJ and smacks him in the back of the head. CJ stands up and shoves Brandi. ]
CJ: "Bitch! Don't hit me." [ Brandi stands toe to toe with her brother looking up at him. As Jamie still has her eyes closed and is humming. ]
Brandi James-Young: "She's mine! " CJ:" So you want to make her as bitchy as you? News..flash..There is already one too many Brandi's in this world."
Brandi James-Young: "CJ...only when you move out of my guest house and get a life of your own can you start insult me. Right now you are biting the hand that is feeding you...and even you should know that's not wise."
[ CJ takes a step back and looks at Jamie then at Brandi. ] CJ""What the hell is she doing?" [ Brandi puts her hands on her hips and giggles. ] Brandi James-Young: "Ear muffs..." CJ: "Ear muffs?" [ Brandi walks close to Jamie and sits in the chair next to her, as she looks at CJ.] Brandi James-Young: "Exactly, I told her that if anyone tried to give her wrestling advice that she is to do just that...because I don't want anyone trying to de-rail my teachings."
CJ: "Nice to see that she listens.." Brandi James-Young: "At least someone does." [ CJ watches as Brandi taps Jamie on the shoulder. Jamie opens her eyes to see Brandi reading her paper. Jamie stops humming, then turns a bit red when Brandi flips the paper over to see Jamie's writings. Brandi points and raises her eyebrow. ]
Brandi James-Young: "You may speak to explain this." [ Jamie gulps as what Brandi see's is Jamie's name writing on the paper and Alex Wilkins under it as she was playing the "true love/flames" game. ]
Jamie Brant: "I wanted to see how well Alex's name matched up with mine. You know how to play ..you write your name in full then the name of your future boyfriend. And ..."
Brandi James-Young: "I know the game...but isn't that a bit ummmm...junior high-ish?" Jamie Brant:" I just wanted see how it would come out. I figured I'd do Alex's name and Simon Senation then figure out who I'm going to be with.."
[ Brandi rubs the sides of her temples. ] Brandi James-Young: "Jamie, you don't pick a man that way.." Jamie Brant: "Then how should I?" [ CJ chuckles. ] CJ: "Oh that's easy if your my sister. You find a happily married man, then get him to leave his wife by endlessly fucking his brains out...after you get him to leave his wife, you break all of his hopes and dreams and then him."
[ Brandi glares at CJ and licks her lips a bit annoyed. ] Brandi James-Young: "If you leave now...I'll spring for a full body massage. Deal?" [ CJ's nods and smiles from ear to ear. ] CJ: "consider me ghost..." Brandi James-Young: "Only if it were that easy.." [ CJ pays Brandi no mind and walks to the front desk to get his massage appoint. Once out of sight Brandi looks at Jamie then the paper. ]
Brandi James-Young: "Jamie what have I said to you numerous times?" [ Jamie lowers her head. ] Jamie Brant: "That boys will have to wait." Brandi James-Young: "Once you've proven to me that you can handle the responsibility of being the Fem-X champion then and only then will we focus on that.."
Jamie Brant: "But Alex is soooo cute, and Simon...well.." Brandi James-Young: "Enough...I don't want to know...Let's just take one thing at a time.."
Jamie Brant: "Ok....how about if I beat Scarlett and defend my title successfully. Then will you?"
[ Brand pauses then nods yes. Jamie squeals and tightly hugs Jamie, getting some of her mask on the side of Brandi's face. ]
Jamie Brant: "Oh shoot! I'm so sorry..." Brandi James-Young: "Just be more careful next time..." Jamie Brant: "Wow no yelling? Am I forgiven?" Brandi James-Young: "For now.." Jamie Brant: "Brandi what do men like better? Cotton briefs, lace thongs, crotch less?" [ Brandi gives Jamie a sly smiles and whispers in her ear. Jamie's eyes widen. ] Jamie Brant: "Really? But ..." [ Brandi gets up from her chair and steps away back towards her manicure table. ] Brandi James-Young: "I'm not saying all the time..I'm just saying that sometimes a man likes easy access..."
[ Brandi winks then goes back to finish her nails. Jamie looks around the room, and down at the woman that has just finished her feet. She notices her toes are red instead of French manicured style. Jamie blurts out..]
Jamie Brant: "What the hell is that? Do I look like a Latina whore? You must have me confused with Scarlett Williams! Now take that polish off and do what I asked for. I want a clean French manicure for my toes. Then when you're done you may do my feet! Now get to it. Peasant!...I swear all the manicurist in this joint and the village idiot ends up doing my feet."
[ Over hearing what went on Brandi smiles and casually says to Jamie. ] Brandi James-Young: "That's my girl...keep it up Jamie..." Jamie Brant: "Yes Ma'am..." [ Jamie leans back as the lady who did her facial returns with a warm bowl of water to softly take off her mud mask. Jamie let's out a sigh of relief knowing that she is well on her way to pleasing her idol as the scene fades. ]
Match Talk: Scarlett think about this...I whipped your ass already on last week's Fiasco show. You may say that what I did I had no right to do, infact it really wasn't any of my business. That's where you would be wrong. You see anything that involves Brandi James-Young is my business. You are the one who was out of line by spitting on the greatest woman's champion alive.
Your not good enough to pick out of Brandi's trash when it's on the curb the night before trash day. Do you really think that anyone in the AWA is going to think of you as a threat? I mean how many other feds have you joined and were either banished from? Rumor says it's lack of originality...hmmm...does that come with being a supposed actress?
I think it does...actually it would serve the theory very well.. You see the difference between a wrestler and an actress is ...we wrestler...we actually get in the ring to fight. We take all our own bumps that lead to real muscle aches and very real injuries. Actors, have their stunt doubles take all the pain. While a real wrestler, a true lover of the business wears each bruise as a badge of honor. Can you do that? Because I'm not convinced that you can. Especially since Actors lack the trait of thinking for themselves...It's not hard to memorize things that someone else has written. Yet in the ring if you think that you'll be able to mimic what you think you know....You're sadly mistaken and are in for a very rude awaking.
My job is a simple one at Lost Souls...to complete the job I started on the Fiasco show, and destroy your un-original ass...Go back to Broadway Scarlett you are not wanted or welcomed here. When you walked through the doors of AWA you were expected to compete at a higher level. You were suppose to rise to the challenge...but no instead you just gave everyone a nasty case of deja vu....by passing off promo's that people have already seen. Sure they weren't seen in AWA...but they were used before...
Now here's the question I seriously love...Scarlett is that fair? Is it fair that the rest of us have to work, and have our lives viewed day in and out for the audience to see...while you just turn in performances of past work. Dare I ask what you were doing instead of actually caring about AWA? Was something else more important to your fans? Perhaps you felt that your opponents...Faith Wilkins and Cyprus Lolita didn't deserve the work of a Scarlett Williams original....yes. That had to have been it..
Let me just run down how we run shit here....You are to be on your game at all times. Nothing but the best is exspected, and if you can't give that..don't let the door hit your ass on the way out. We bust our asses here...right from the newbie to the very top Champion. I'm insulted that you didn't give those women last week the respect they deserve...after all your life is so excited and fab....then why not let us into it? Your dead husband, your successful acting career....Hmm....could it really be that successful? Really...what successful person gets a job in a industry where you need heart to stay alive?
It's obvious Scarlett you have no heart! And it's going to be my pleasure to prove that to the rest of AWA and the world! Think you can get a victory on me? Think that what I did to you on the Fiasco show was my best? Sweets that's not nearly what I'm capable of....In fact I'm sure that what I got you can't handle. Step up and just try to pin my shoulder for the 3 count...Oh and incase you didn't realize it...I'm a heel...and just incase you didn't know....that's wrestler talk for ( bad guy) Do you even know what you are? Wait sure you do...all you are is an actress who DOES NOT BELONG IN MY RING!
Lost Souls Scarlett...will be something you will never forget. And as far as you're little skills and tactics that you think you're going to bring to the ring and boast about...frankly my dear...I don't give a damn...
