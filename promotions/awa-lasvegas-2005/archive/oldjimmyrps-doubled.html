Jimmy Stryker: "Can't we watch something else..anything else." Rhiannon James: "What's wrong with Dirty Dancing?" I guess it won't be that bad, nothing ever is when it's put next to dirty dancing. For now I have to get ready for my lunch date with daddy...as this is all for now.]
