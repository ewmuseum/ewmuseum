AWA (Las Vegas, 2005) — Roleplay (Skylar Black)

Shopping with Sky

November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Roleplay Title I've learned-that two people can look at the exact same thing,and see something totally different. I feel this is the case with Aspen. She has tons of mental issues going on. The poor dear, and while I feel just horrible about what her dad did to her and her mom . Which by the way is never excusable she's going to take her anger out the wrong way.
I'm looking at this match as excellent competition not some way to make another pay for the pain I've been put through in my life. I think Aspen may partly hate herself for the abuse her mother would take so she wouldn't be abused by her dad. I'm sure you're mother forgives you, and isn't even mad at you. Aspen it isn't always enough to
be forgiven by others,Sometimes you have to learn to forgive yourself. I think you should forgive yourself for feeling angry and bad about what your mom went through. She did it for you, because she loves you. There's nothing like a mothers love. And I know she wouldn't want you to feel this way.
Aspen we all don't have good days all the time. I know that I don't have a perfect day all the time, but when I'm angry I just say to myself. I have the right to be angry, but that doesn't give me the right to be cruel. It's a good rule of thumb to go by. It's pretty simple darling, either you control your attitude or it controls you. You must forgive and let go of your anger..we are responsible for what we do, no matter how we feel. you can do something in an instant that will give you heartache for life. But once you enlighten and set your anger free your soul will begin to feel at piece once you forgive yourself for all your sins and release your anger from your past. You can't control everything that happened. But you can focus on the posetive for your future. After the match if you need a friend, I'm here.
The SCENE: [ Our scene opens up to the middle of the Galleria Mall in Los Angles. Skylar Black is seen walking around with her dog peanut in her large pink Fendi bag. Skylar is wearing a pair of open toe pink heels with a heart on the front in silver rhinestones, a halter pink dress to match her shoes. Her blonde hair softly curled flowing down her shoulders. She walks by each store not sure which one to go in. She looks down at her dog. ]
Skylar Black:"Peanut...What do you think we should get for Johnny Arnette to congratulate him on winning his title. I want it to say....sucessful...but also say Champion at the same time."
[ Peanut lets out a bark, and Skylar pets the top of his head as they stand infront of a store called "music box."]
Skylar Black:"Good thinking peanut. Everyone loves music boxes, they're thoughtful, and people always look at them when they're inside your home. I'm sure they have something that Johnny would like inside here."
[ She walks into the store, and the sales clerk notices her looking around. ] Sales Clerk:"Can, I help you?" [ Skylar smiles. ] Skylar Black:"Actually yes....I was looking for something for a friend of mine. You know something cute and simple."
[ The clerk nods, and points to a corner of the store.] Sales Clerk:"Well, you have these trinkets over here." [ Skylar stares as she walks over, the touches the clerks shoulder. ] Skylar Black:"But it has to say manly...It can't be something I'd give to one of my soriety sisters."
[ The clerk laughs...and stands infront of a glass case. With about eight rows of music boxes. Immediately one in particular catches her eye. She points excitingly at it. ]
Skylar Black:"Oh, now that's really nice." Sales Clerk:"Which one?" Skylar Black:"The one with the Canadian flag on it." Sales Clerk:"So you're Canadian, eh?" [ The sales clerk chuckles as she opens the case for Skylar to take a closer look at it. ] Skylar Black:"No, I'm not, but a friend of mine is. And what says country pride more then a music box?"
Sales Clerk:"I guess." [ Skylar takes the box gentely and looks at it, then opens it up. O'canada begins to play, and she smiles at the clerk then closes the lid. ]
Skylar Black:"Does it come in pink?" [ The clerk give Skylar a funny look. ] Sales Clerk:"Pink?" Skylar Black:"Yeah....Pink..." Sales Clerk:"Are you serious?" [ Skylar smiles at her, as she hands the clerk back the box. ] Skylar Black:"It's just that red is so...well it's a very disturbing color. Always being compared to blood. And well Pink is my signature color. It's so much more happy and fun ! Don't you think?"
Sales Clerk:"Ummmm, is this for a boyfriend?" Skylar Black:"No, he's just a friend. He's actually moved to my hometown. I figured since we're neighbors and all a congraulatory gift was in order. Since he recently one the Continental title. He's really talented."
[ The clerk takes the music box and smirks at Skylar who's smiling at her. ] Sales Clerk:"Hunny, if pink is your "signiture color" why don't you just go to fredricks and buy some sexy lace thing to wear for him. I'm sure he'll appreciate that since you have a great body."
[ Skylar shakes her head and touches the sales clerk's hand. ] Skylar Black:"Oh, I don't shop there their clothes are too trashy for me...women like Brandi James shop there. Besides, I don't do that. I'm part of the silver ring soceity."
[ Skylar proudly nods. ] Sales Clerk:"Silver Ring Soceity?" [ She lifts her hand and on her left ring finger is a pretty silver band, with a rose design scrolled around it. ]
Skylar Black:"Yes...it's a club where you promise to remain pure till you get married." Sales Clerk:"You mean punish yourself." [ Skylar shakes her head once again. ] Skylar Black:"No, not punish Enpower yourself." [ She flashes a smile. The sales clerk looks as if she's about to be sick. ] Sales Clerk:"Riiiigggghhht....So do you want the box or not?" [ Skylar pauses then answers. ] Skylar Black:"Hmmmm....Well, Are you sure it doesn't come in pink?" [ The clerk glares at her. ] Sales Clerk:"No...Not in pink." Skylar Black:"Well what about blue.." Sales Clerk:"No, not blue...Look, do you want the damn box or not?" [ Skylar back up from the clerk as she seems to be loosing her temper.] Skylar Black:"Alright then, I guess I'll just take that one." [ They walk over to the cash register, and the sales clerk rings her up. ] Sales Clerk:"That will be $30 dollars..." [ Skylar takes out her wallet, and opens it. ] Skylar Black:"Is that the american or canadian price?" [ She giggles. but the clerk isn't amused. ] Sales Clerk:"It's just $30 dollars...Do you need a card?" Skylar Black:"Yes, please....Hmmm, I would like it say. Great job on wining with such style. Your Friend, Skylar Black."
[ The clerk rolls her eyes, and writes it down, then takes the money from her. She give Skylar her change then put the music box in a bag, and hands it to Skylar. ]
Sales Clerk:"here..." [ Skylar takes the bag, and looks at the side of the register with a frequent shoppers club card. Then she looks back at the sales clerk.]
Skylar Black:"You know, if you're were a bit nicer I would have joined you're frequent shoppers club. You know I suggest a good facial peel. They just seem to have a way of perking up your spirits. Or maybe some Yoga, you know smile and improve from the inside out....Ya know?"
[ The sales clerk puts her hand up and walks away. Skylar shrugs her shoulder, and looks at her dog peanut. ]
Skylar Black:"Oh well peanut..some people just aren't ready for self improvement...but you have been very good. I'm so proud of you...come on let's go to pawpoochie's...I think some little dog earned a new sweater."
[ Skylar pats the top of peanuts head as he barks, and the scene fades out with Skylar walking out of the store. ]
