It gives me a chance to think a bit more clearly to where I want my career to go, or what I would like to do within the next days maybe weeks. Either that or just sit and relax to forget about everyone that is bugging me for my attention. Rick is so understand of my "Brandi Time". You know how sometimes you just have a feel that you're being watched? Well, I had just that feeling. I looked up only to confirm my feeling was indeed correct. I smirked as I looked at the person. ]
Brandi James:" What the hell do you want?" [ I said. ...... The person I was looking at was none other than Rick's soon to be ex-wife Tiffany Phoenix or Teresa Young if you want to get technical about the name that's on the divorce papers. ]
Tiffany Phoenix: "Can't I get something to eat or is that only a luxary that the great Brandi James has? If I knew you would be here I probably would have just ordered from room service."
[ I raised my eyebrow, and set my pen down. The First thought that crossed through my mind was....How in the hell can she afford a room in the Bel-Air Hotel. They definately aren't cheap rooms, and I know for a fact Rick isn't paying her any alamony money yet. Still that didn't change the fact she was standing infront of me. ]

Tiffany Phoenix: "However, I guess I do have to say congratulations...I mean you ruined my marriage just like you hoped you would."
[ I couldn't help but sit back laughing, as I crossed my arms. ] Brandi James:"oh,no, no...my intent was never to wreck your marriage. I just wanted to fuck your husband a few times. "
[ I said smiling arrogantly from ear to ear. ] Brandi James:"It's not my fault he enjoyed it and wanted more.," Tiffany Phoenix: "Sure...and my middle name is mud." [ She said, as she placed her hands on her hips. ] Tiffany Phoenix: "Or sure just like you didn't mean to break up...oh what was his name...Oh that's right Damien Sparks marriage."
Brandi James:"No, that I meant to do. It was all for the common good." Tiffany Phoenix: "Oh so I see...so breaking up Rick and I were the common evil?" Brandi James:"No, your husband...oh wait excuse your soon to be ex-husband is damn sexy. I just pointed it out that's all. I'm telling you, I never intended to keep Rick...."
[ I sipped my drink slowly then set it down. ] Brandi James:" I just wanted to play with him for a while. Not that I'm complaining. Rick's fantastic, in everyway."
[ I could tell that my words were getting her as she looked down towards the floor trying to hide it from me, but it was too late. I had already see the pain in her eyes. ]
Tiffany Phoenix: "You know Brandi one of these days you are going to have to answer for all the wicked things you have done."
[ It was really amusing me how she thought she was getting to me, but this was my game, and I never lose at a game that I have started. ]
Brandi James:"Oh.....wait...wait...let me see....Do I?....Ummm...do I care?" [ I paused then said. ] Brandi James:"Nah, don't really care. See I have what I want now, and no one will take it from me. Unlike you Thersea, Im not afraid to fight for what I want."
[ She looked up from the floor to look at me again and I can tell that she wanted to hit me, but I could also see that she wouldn't either, because she doesn't cause a scene or she's afraid of me kicking her ass again. ]
Tiffany Phoenix: "You might not care about it now, but sooner or later when you are faced with it you will care about it."
[ She said, as she turned around to start to walk away. ] Brandi James:"Faced with what? My fucking life is Luxerious...baby.." [ It felt good to yell that for nearly everyone to hear. It was like a validation of sorts.]
Brandi James:"I have your husband and there is nothing you can do about it. But I do have one question though. Before you run away like the little girl that you are"
[ She stopped and turned to look at me again. ] Tiffany Phoenix: "What could you possible have to ask me?" [ with a cocky smile I asked. ] Brandi James:"You knew Rick for so long. He was your first everything, you were suppose to love him. So the question is if you know him so well inside and out, then how the hell do allow yourself to lose a man like that? How do you not see red, and want to kill me? How did you fuck up so bad that he lost interest in you?"
Tiffany Phoenix: "He was my first everything and it's quite obvious that I didn't know him as well as I thought I did..."
[ She said, as she raised her right hand up and ran it through her hair. ] Tiffany Phoenix: "Why don't I want to kill you? Maybe because I know that killing you isn't worth it."
[ Was she serious? Did she really exspect me to buy that shit? Hell no I wasn't going for that. ]
Brandi James:"What a bullshit answer! I'm worth more then you'll ever be. I'm smarter then you, more talented then you, sexier then you. Just over all better in every way. Infact I think you envy me, Thersea."
Tiffany Phoenix: "Believe what you want Brandi, but why would I kill you for a man that left me for you?"
[ She said, rolling her eyes at me so I thought, but soon I felt someone standing behind me. I turned her around to look over my shoulder and found Rick standing behind me. I stood up, and put my arms around his waste, and rubbed my nose against his cheek, then softly gave him a little peck on the cheek. ]
Brandi James:"Hey, baby...I was wondering what was keeping you." Rick Young: "I got ambushed in the rest room by fans wanting my autograph." [ He said, with a disgusted looked on his face thinking about it as I heard Tiffany chuckle a little bit at Rick's misfortune. ]
Tiffany Phoenix: "I hope one of them got an embarassing photograph of you." Brandi James:"Don't mind her Rick, not that you ever did..., I'm sure it's fine. If anything a photo of you with only result in men everywhere."
[ Thersea just froze as if she was taking a mental picture of the way Rick was looking at me. As I twirled my fingers around his hair that was pulled back. Then kissed his lips. ]
Brandi James:"You would know what I mean by that Thersea if you fucked your husband every once and awhile. Instead of making him, unhappy"
Rick Young: "Now now...no need to filet Teresa with your nails any more baby, just let her go running back to the hole she came from and cry her eyes out. It's the only humane thing to do."
[ Rick said, with a smirk on his face just before he kissed me on my lips. Teresa went to say something back to Rick, but Rick just shot her some dirty looks and then snapped at her... ]
Rick Young: "Don't bother saying anything Teresa to me your nothing, but a broken record." [ Rick smirked again as Teresa just stood there dumb founded. I was going to say something else but decided not to. Instead I just fully put my arms around Rick's neck and passionately kissed him infront of Thersea. Rick smiled, and pulled out my chair for me, then sat down himself. I handed Rick a menu then noticed Thersea still standing there with a few tears running down her face. I couldn't resist, I had to say something. ]
Brandi James:"Oh, you're still here thersea....shewww....go...or do I have to call secruity?"
[ It didn't take that much longer before she did just that turned around and ran out. It wasn't long before she was gone that Rick smiled and started to laugh a little bit... ]
Rick Young: "And she's off!" [ He's so cute and funny. It's no wonder I love him so much. I put my hand on Rick's, and looked at him. ]
Brandi James;"Baby, you are a Saint...How the hell did you put up with that basket case for so long?"
Rick Young: "To be honest...I have no fucking idea." Brandi James:"Well no wonder you came looking for me." Rick Young: "You can say that again." [ He said with his smirk on his face as he turned his attention to the menu I had handed him. He looked over it for a moment before turning his attention back to me. ]
Rick Young: "You ready to order?" Brandi James:"Yep....I even know what I want for desert..." Rick Young: "Well then let's order so we can get to desert." [ He said, before looking around for a waiter or a waitress. Once he spotted one he raised his right hand in to the air a little bit and called out... ]
Rick Young: "Waiter we are ready to order now!" [ See that's what I mean. What ever I want Rick gets me. What more could a woman ask for? A man that is so intend on giving you whatever you want. I set my menu down on the side, and winked at him. As I rubbed my foot over his from under the table. ]
Brandi James:"Rick, it's not on the menu..what I want." [ Rick put his arm down and looked at me with a smile on his face. ] Rick Young: "Well if that's the case...check please." [ He said, as he caught my foot inbetween the both of his. ] Brandi James:"but, I only had water.." Rick Young: "Well then I guess there is no reason to stick around here any longer. Unless you want something to eat before desert?"
[ Rick managed to say just before the waiter was standing besides our table. ] Brandi James:"Nope....don't want food.." Rick Young: "Never mind then waiter..."
