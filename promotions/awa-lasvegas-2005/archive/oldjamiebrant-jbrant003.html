AWA (Las Vegas, 2005) — Roleplay (Jamie Brant)

Jamie Brant 003

July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*`*`*`*Jamie Brant*`*`*`* [ The scene opens to the Bel-Air Hotel on Ocean Drive in South Beach Miami, Florida. Jamie Brant is seen pacing around her hotel room. A half opened suitcase is seen on the chair in the corner of the room with clothes scattered around it and the rest of the room. Jamie is wearing a pair of simple black shorts the go to her knees and a white tank top. Her hair is pulled back in a simple pony tail. Jamie undoes the pony tail and tossels her hair then pulls it back in the pony tail. She sits on the end of the bed and taps her hands on her knees a bit nervous. ]
Jamie Brant: "Oh god...I can't believe that she coming here..I'm telling you it was my promo. I knew I did..."
[ Jamie is interrupted by a knock at the door. She stands and smiles then walks over to the door and opens it. On the other side stands Brandi James-Young. Brandi looks at Jamie who can't wipe the smile from her face. Brandi doesn't wait to be invited in the room and walks right by Jamie. ]
Brandi James-Young: "You little miss have no reason to be smiling like that.." [ Jamie puts her hand over her mouth to cover her smile. She looks over Brandi, who is wearing white strappy sandals and a pair of white cut off shorts with a white v-neck halter top, and her cherry blossom Luis Vuitton pocketbook over her right shoulder. She notices the tour of the room that Brandi begins to do. To try to avoid a further uneasy moment Jamie speaks. ]
Jamie Brant: "Thanks for suggesting this hotel to me. It's really nice and the service people are really good."
[ Not knowing exactly where to stand omg the mess Brandi turns and adjusts her pocketbook. ]
Brandi James-Young: "How the hell can you sleep in this room?" Jamie Brant: "Easy the beds are really comfortable." [ Brandi shakes her head.] Brandi James-Young: "No you half witt. I mean this room is a pig pen. How the hell can you relax in a room that is so......So fuckin' messy!"
Jamie Brant: "I was meaning to pick it up. It's just that when you said you were stopping by to see me, I wanted to make sure I showered first. The flight back from Oakland sorta made me a bit tired."
Brandi James-Young: "That's no excuse at all. There is never an excuse for this." [ Brandi shudders as she picks up a strap of a bra that's on the table by Jamie's suitcase. ]
Brandi James-Young: "You're almost as bad as Rick is...And believe me, he's not someone you want to model your road habbits after."
[ Jamie nods and clears her throat.] Jamie Brant: "Ok, I'll make more of an effort to keep things tidy." Brandi James-Young: "It's for your own good Jamie. If you live like this on the road then you'll always feel like your missing something. It's much better to keep things neat that way you know where everything is."
Jamie Brant: "That does make sense." Brandi James-Young: "Ofcourse it does.." Jamie Brant: "I'll try harder.." Brandi James-Young: "You'll do alot more than that. Jamie it's not just your room that is a total mess."
[ Jamie looks a bit confused at Brandi who opens her pocketbook and tapes out a video tape. She holds it up and taps it.]
Brandi James-Young: "Do you know what this is?" Jamie Brant: "No...It could be alot of things." Brandi James-Young: "Yes it could be alot of things but it's not...It's your recent promo."
[ Jamie's eyes light up and tries to grab it but Brandi pulls the tape back.] Brandi James-Young: " I wouldn't be too excited about it..." Jamie Brant: " Why not? Didn't I sound confident?" [ Brandi laughs.] Brandi James-Young: "Yeah, way too much so." Jamie Brant: " Really? I only tried to makeself tough, you know like you." [ Brandi raises her voice and slams down the tape on the table.] Brandi James-Young: "No! You only sound tough Jamie when you actually can back up your statements. All you've managed to accomplish is to make yourself look stupid and piss off Angela Jameson."
[ A bit unsure if she should speak Jamie shifts her eyes then folds her arms.] Jamie Brant: " But...Brandi I did win." Brandi James-Young: " I know you fuckin' won you little twitt! But you took credit for beating someone that wasn't even there.....Yes you won the match, but you only beat Faith Wilkins....which believe me is nothing to brag about. "
Jamie Brant: " She's been here longer than I have though." Brandi James-Young: " I know she has but all people think of Faith Wilkins is as Alex Wilkins wife and tits and Ass...She's the kind of worker you use if you want a bra and panties type of match. Faith Wilkins serves no real purpose on this roster..she's eye candy and nothing more."
[ Brandi pauses for a moment. Jamie looks as if she's about to speak but Brandi points at her. ]
Brandi James-Young: "You will not say a God dam thing yet ! Understand me? Just nod if you do."
[ Jamie tightens her lips and nods her head. ] Brandi James-Young: " Jamie, I don't think you understand what you did. Your only goal should have been to talk about Jasmine. She's who you are fighting, and while you did that you did a huge no, no. You managed to piss off a woman who actually means something to this company. A woman that you are not ready to face yet, but now will have to deal with because of your stupid remarks."
Jamie Brant: " But Brandi, I only wanted to sound like you." Brandi James-Young: " What did I tell you? No speaking just listening..if you can't follow that simple instruction then I'm not wasting my time with you."
[ Brandi says not another word and walks by Jamie. Jamie turns around and throws herself on the ground at Brandi's feet. Jamie wraps her arms around Brandi's ankles and Brandi nearly stumbles flat on her face, but manages to catch her balance by place her arms on the door frame. Jamie begins to cry and squeel. ]
Jamie Brant: " Ok, ok...I'll be a good girl...please Brandi don't leave...I need to learn from you...Your my favorite woman wrestler ever...I grew up watching you...It's an honor to be around you...don't go.."
[ tries to shake her leg a bit to get Jamie to release her grip. Brandi places one hand on the wall steps one leg out of Jamie's hand. Brandi finaly steps out and Jamie is looking up at Brandi a bit exhausted with her hands still gripped. ]
Brandi James-Young: " Get a hold of yourself girl..." Jamie Brant: " Please don't walk out on me..I'm sorry for what I did..I didn't realize what I was doing.."
Brandi James-Young: " Ofcourse you didn't because you weren't thinking...half of this business is about being intelligent Jamie. Knowing when to hold back and when to expose your competiton."
Jamie Brant: " Ugh..." [ Jamie sighs and sits up with her knees bent to her chest. She stares straight at a blank wall then hits herself in the head with her hand over and over. ]
Jamie Brant: " I have so much to learn..Stupid, stupid, stupid...!" [ Brandi isn't sure exactly how to control Jamie or what to do so she whistles loudly to get Jamie attention. ]
Brandi James-Young: " Hey Cybil! Cut it the hell out!" [ Jamie stops and turns her head looking at Brandi. ] Brandi James-Young: " Yes, you have alot to learn..but acting like a retard won't help you. Now get your ass up, and pick all of your clothes up."
Jamie Brant: " What's the point? " Brandi James-Young: " The point is your going to travel with Rick and me. And we're leaving for Cleveland in about an hour...we're old schooling it and driving...and you know..."
[ Jamie jumps up wiping her tears, as she is smiling now. With glee she replies back to Brandi. ]
Jamie Brant: " I know you refuse to be late. " Brandi James-Young: " Exactly, atleast you remember that." Jamie Brant: " So does that mean that while Rick is driving you'll tell me how to beat Jasmine? Do you think Rick will tell me how to beat Jasmine?"
[ Brandi laughs and kicks a pile of clothes towards Jamie. ] Brandi James-Young: " For starters I'm going to be the one driving..If Rick drives we won't get there till tuesday and we'll miss D.O.A all together...Second I will be the only one telling you exactly how to beat Jasmine..Rick at the moment has taken up smoking trees again and can barely fight his way out of a wet paper bag...Good for Angela Jameson..bad for Rick..."
Jamie Brant: " Right...So Rick won't be telling me how to beat his ex-wife." Brandi James-Young: " As I said before the only other person you are allowed to take advice from about wrestling other than me is Jalie..Everyone else you must ignore and that goes double for Rick. His silly ass will only de-rail my teaching with you."
[ Jamie picks up the pile of clothes and shakes out a shirt that she begins folding.] Jamie Brant: " Gotcha...So I have to use ear muffs when listening to Rick and his wrestling advice."
Brandi James-Young: " Now your getting it.." [ Jamie nods as Brandi opens the door of the room and is startled by the AWA camera crew. Jamie begins to walk over but Brandi stops her. ]
Brandi James-Young: " You stay put." Jamie Brant: " But they want another promo from me for the match I have against Jasmine at D.O.A...Boss.."
Brandi James-Young: " After the last promo flub you did..I don't think so.." [ Brandi looks at the camera crew. ] Brandi James-Young: " Go away...she's not ready...You'll get a full statement from her before the match...nothing untill then..."
[ Brandi slams the door in the camera crews face, and snaps her fingers at Jamie to continue as she looks at her watch . ]
Brandi James-Young: "Chop, chop...time is a wasting..." Jamie Brant: " Right...almost done.." [ Brandi takes a deep breath and puts her head back. ] Brandi James-Young: " Well that's one diaster avoided..." Jamie Brant: " Umm, Brandi does that mean your going to teach me how to do a promo the correct way?"
Brandi James-Young: " It's not really like I have much of a choice. People now associate you as being taught by me. And I can't have you destroying everything I've worked so hard for. "
Jamie Brant: " Is that a yes?" [ Brandi rolls her eyes then gives Jamie a nod to say yes. Jamie's mouth drops and she begins to jump around the room clapping her hands. She hops over to Brandi and hugs her tightly. ]
Jamie Brant: " Your the best! I knew there was a reason I admired you! Your the standard! Oh this is gonna be so great! Like peanut butter and fluff great! "
[ Pushing Jamie back a bit Brandi backs away. ] Brandi James-Young: " Less talk more pack! C'mon I have to get back to Nolan, who knows what bad habbits his dad is teaching him. "
Jamie Brant: " Ok, Ok..." [ shuving the last article of clothing into her suitcase Jamie closes it up, and lifts it in the air to show Brandi she's done. ]
Jamie Brant: " I'm good to go!" Brandi James-Young: "What the fuck did you just say?" Jamie Brant: " I'm good to go! You know like the taco bell commercial..." Brandi James-Young: " I know, but it's not funny! So refrain from saying stupid gimmicky shit like that..got it?"
Jamie Brant: " Alright but I thought it was sorta cute.." Brandi James-Young: " There was nothing funny about it..Funny is pointing out the fact that Jasmine..has like 3 differnt names that she's known by and not one of them was good enough to keep her now ex- husband for constantly cheating on her, then finally divorcing her...Now that's funny. Not to mention the fact that she's about to lose Chris to that Pyscho Laura.. those are funny...it's ironic how the funniest thing are the things that are the most true...See what I mean?"
Jamie Brant: " Right boss, noted.." Jamie Brant: " So now exactly what is Jasmine's weakness?" [ Brandi laughs a bit amused by Jamie who walks close to Brandi as she shuts the door and the scene fades out with the sound of the door locking. ]
