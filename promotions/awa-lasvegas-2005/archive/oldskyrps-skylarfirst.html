AWA (Las Vegas, 2005) — Roleplay (Skylar Black)

The First Interview

October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The First Interview [ The scene opens our scene to later in the day at AWA headquarter. We see walking down the hall a bubbly pretty blonde. She's dressed in a pink business suit with a white collar shirt, and pink pumps. She looks around for a moment, then is tapped on the shoulder by AWA's own roaming reporter Jenny Tull.]
Jenny Tull: "Hey, Skylar Black! So nice to meet you!" Skylar Black:"Oh, hi...same here." [ Skylar gives Jenny a big hug, and warm smile.] Jenny Tull: "So I have a room all set to do your first AWA interview. [ Skylar jumps up and down clapping her hands. ] Skylar Black: "I'm so excited...this is going to be so much fun!" Jenny Tull: "Definately...come on." [ Jenny and Skylar enter the set of Jenny's new access AWA show. She and Skylar both sit down, and they begin. ]
Jenny Tull: "Well, I'm here with the newest AWA Diva...Skylar Black. Skylar how does it feel to have your first AWA match be at a PPV?"
Skylar Black: "Well first off, I'd like to say hello everyone! Greetings to my new co-workers, and AWA friends. I'm so looking forward to getting to know each and everyone of you. I'm so thrilled about my upcoming match. It's a huge deal that I get the chance to have my first ever match at a large event."
Jenny Tull: "It's true, it's a great honor....especially since it's against Brandi James. She's one of the top women here."
Skylar Black: "Yes, I know. I've actually saw one of her ...television thingies...and I have to say, while I think she's talented. I think she's forgotton a cardinal rule."
Jenny Tull:"What's that?" Skylar Black:"No fornication without procreation! It may take a little self restraint, but it's the way God intended it."
[ Jenny looks curiously at Skylar, and leans forward. ] Jenny Tull: "Are you trying to say that you're a virgin?" Skylar Black: "Yes, I am. I firmly believe in strict values. And let's face it, Brandi very well maybe a sex addict. I mean, I know her boyfriend must love it. But then again, I've heard stories where he's still married."
[ Jenny sadly looks at Skylar. ] Jenny Tull: "It's because he is." [ Skylar gasps and puts her hands over her mouth. ] Skylar Black: "No..." Jenny Tull: "Yes...it's a fact. Rick is married to this girl named Thersea..but her wrestling name is Tiffany Phoenix."
Skylar Black: "Oh, that's such a cute name...just sorta flows..Ya know?" Jenny Tull: "Oh, I completely agree..Anyway, Rick's cheated on his wife numerous times. And last PPV she put him up as part of her match against Brandi."
Skylar Black: "No!" Jenny Tull:"Yes! And Tiffany didn't think that Rick would go, but he did. And then he and Brandi. Well...they did."
Skylar Black: "Have mercy on that girls soul." [ Skylar pauses looking as if she's a bit sad.] Skylar Black: "What a sad story, that poor woman." Jenny Tull:"Tiffany is a sweetheart. She definately does deserve better than Rick. So I'm guessing put your man up is something that you'd never do."
Skylar Black:"Never, I don't believe gambling, drinking, smoking, or sex before marriage." Jenny Tull: "so what do you belive in?" Skylar Black:" I believe in hope, faith, trust, that everyone has some good in them. Sometimes it's a bit hard to get through to them, but I know if you just try really hard and reach deep down. You'll be able to help them see their potential and pull it out. Just takes alittle time patience and love."
Jenny Tull:"Awe, you know there should be more people like you in this world." Skylar Black:"That's soooooo nice of you to say ! I just want to lead with a good example. You know, be a good role model for the kids out there in this world. I want them to know that hard work does pay off, and that lying,cheating and stealing is no way to go. And will get you no rewards!"
Jenny Tull: "Well, I agree with that." [ Skylar proudly smiles.] Skylar Black:"Why thank you." Jenny Tull:"You're very welcome. Now you had a tiny run in with our newest star Johnny Arnette. What did you think about him?"
Skylar Black:"He seems a bit distracted when I met him. I realy don't think I had his full attention, but I'd love to sit down to talk with him sometime."
Jenny Tull:" Like...Talk?" [ Jenny looks at Skylar and winks.] Skylar Black: "No, I'm not that kind of girl. I really mean talk. I'm sure he's very intersting, and he sounds like he really needs a good listener. That Clayton Walker fellow seems to be a very bad influence on him."
Jenny Tull:"hmmmm, I guess I never thought of it that way." Skylar Black:"Not many people realize that the people around us have a huge impact on the everyday things that we do. Like how we dress and act. I'm not saying that you behave just like the person you are closest to, but you do tend to play off each other."
Jenny Tull:" I can see that." Skylar Black: "So are we done?" Jenny Tull:"Yes, I guess we are...atleast for now." [ Skylar stands up and extends her hand to Jenny.] Skylar Black:"Ms. Tull, it was my pleasure. Feel free to call anytime, I'll be more then glad to sit down with you."
[ Jenny shakes Skylar's hand, and smiles. ] Jenny Tull:"Thanks, I will." Skylar Black:"Looking forward to it, Thanks!" [ Skylar exits the room as Jenny shut her tape recorder off. ] Jenny Tull:"what a sweet girl!" [ Jenny rewinds the tape to replay the interview as the scene fades out.] People Involved Skylar Black and Jenny Tull Past/Future Victims None Next Match Brandi James Thought-provoking Nonsense "There is no such thing as a stranger.
