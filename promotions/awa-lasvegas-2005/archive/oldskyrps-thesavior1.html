Roleplay Title [ The holidays are over and everyone now is starting to get back to the everyday work flow. That means back to the basics of living. No more time off for the holidays...in Maxwells words "playtime is over kiddies." A message that will be heard loud and clear by the entire AWA roster. Today on a rather old January day Skylar Black steps out of the elevator at AWA headquarters. She's dressed warm in a pink jacket and pair of jeans with pink Nike sneakers. Skylar's carrying a cherry blossom Luis Vuitton bag with her trusty dog peanut inside of it. Peanut has on a jacket that matches the one Skylar is wearing. She walks up to the receponist and place the pocket with Peanut on the desk.]
Skylar Black:"Excuse me, I'm here to see Maxwell." [ The woman nods, and smiles warmly at Skylar.] Receponist:"Awe, Skylar how was your holiday?" Skylar Black:"Fantastic thanks so much for asking. How was your holiday?" Receponist:"Just wonderful...." Skylar Black:"That's great...So was Santa Claus good to you?" Receponist:"See for yourself!" [ Lifting her hand over the edge of the desk the receponist shows Skylar an engagement ring. Skylar claps then leans in to further examine the ring.]
Skylar Black:"Very nice...good cut...great clarity...I'm guessing a grade C? Nice choice did you pick it our yourself or was it a surprise."
Receponist:"It was a total surprise...and yes the grade is C...that's amazing how did you know that?"
[ Skylar let's go of her hand, and pets the top of Peanuts head.] Skylar Black:"Well back in Mississippi when I was a little girl I joined the girl scouts, but when I found out that mostly the only thing they sold were cookies and the patches they earned were a bit bland. I decided that I would make my own patches up. So I got a few of the other girls together who felt the same way and we earned our own patches, in shopping. My speciality is in jewerly."
[ Skylar said with a wink.] Receponist:"Well it's very impressive..." Skylar Black:"It's a lot more difficult then it appears to be.....So when's the big day?" Receponist:"Oh we haven't set a date yet. First we figured that we'd focus on a place to live. Then we would worry about the wedding details."
[ A look of concern came across Skylar's face.] Skylar Black:"A place to live first?" Receponist:"Yes...we were thinking..." [ Skylar puts her hand up stopping the woman.] Skylar Black:"Sweetie...back up...You have the ring already. I just think the wedding is just as important if not more. It the day every girl dreams about. I remember one time there was this perfect bridal dress I saw for my barbie doll. It was the dress she had to wear in order to have a perfect wedding day with Ken. You know satin with lace and beeds...Anyway I saw at Kendall's in the city. Mom my promised to get it for me but by the time she did it wasn't there. Mom spent months searching for it, and never found it. I was devasted...Luckily on my birthday my aunt ruth who has fablous taste had seen that dress for barbie and bought it for me. Ken and Barbie had their dream wedding everything was perfect. Do you see my point?"
[ Not exactly sure what to say the receponist shrugged and didn't speak a word. Skylar laughed and said smiling.]
Skylar Black:"The point is....don't act like one of the most important days of your life isn't that important. I mean you waited this long to be with each other don't rush things. I know finding the perfect dream house is also a top listed thing, but really...would you rather show your kids pictures of you in this to die for wedding dress or something that looks like it was just pulled off the rack?"
Receponist:"I do want my kids to see how beautiful I was on my wedding day when I got married sure. But Skylar Tim and I already live together...we just want a better place."
Skylar Black:"You live together already?" Receponist:"Yes..." Skylar Black:"Honey you need to move out! I mean the ring just maybe a ploy to bide Tim more time. I mean no man commits with full love when they're getting the milk for free."
[ Before anything else was said the door to Maxwell's office opens. Max is seen standing leaning against the door crossing his arms.]
Maxwell Williams:"Ah, Skylar, I thought I heard you out here." Skylar Black:"I was just waiting to be told you were ready." Maxwell Williams:"And giving Christy some advice I see." Skylar Black:"Just and opinion really." Maxwell Williams:"Well, I'm ready now. So come on in." [ Maxwell heads into his office and sits behind his desk as he waits for Skylar. Skylar picks up her bag with Peanut inside of it, and walks by Christy and touches her arm.]
Skylar Black:"Just think about it...If you need to talk let me know." [ Christy smiles and watches Skylar walk into Maxwells office. She shuts the door behind her and takes a seat infront of Maxwells large oak desk.]
Skylar Black:"So what can I do for you Maxwell...You sounded very serious on the phone." [ Maxwell folds his hand and looks at Skylar.] Maxwell Williams:"Skylar...I've asked you here today for something that has to do with one of my workers."
Skylar Black:"Alright...." Maxwell Williams:"You know who Mike Young is don't you?" Skylar Black:"Yes....the former World Champion...Rick's brother." Maxwell Williams:"yes,,,,,Unfortunately Mike has fallen upon a very hard time. Ever since Samantha left him for her bodyguard he's been down in the dumps. I've talked with a few people and we all feel an intervention is in order."
[ Skylar shakes her head as she stops smiling.] Skylar Black:"It's really too bad...Mike had a bright future...But what does this have to do with me?"
Maxwell williams:"Through the grape vine...It was brought to my attention that Seth Thomas had contacted you to help his wife Jalie with her problem with liquor."
Skylar Black:"No one was suppose to know about that Max..." [ Max just laughs and stands up behind his desk.] Maxwell Williams:"I'm sure..but I have eyes and ears everywhere. So needless to say when I found this out, I figured that if anyone would be a great influence on Mike..that person is you."
Skylar Black:"Mr. Williams...the thing with Jalie never happened because Seth wanted to take another method...but..."
Maxwell Williams:"I know that as well Skylar...." [ Max moves next to Skylar and shows her a picture of a pretty little girl. Skylar looks down and smiles at the picture.]
Maxwell Williams:"Pretty little girl isn't she?" Skylar Black:"She's adorable..." Maxwell Williams:"She's the daughter of Mike Young...Her name is Patience...Skylar if you won't do it for me...please think about how much this beautiful little girl needs her daddy."
[ Looking down at the picture Skylar sighs and runs her fingers over the face of the little girl. She looks up at Maxwell.]
Skylar Black:"Ok...What do I need to do and where do I need to be?" Maxwell Williams:"You're a good girl Skylar...I knew you wouldn't let me down..." Skylar Black:"Just to be clear..I'm doing this for his little girl...I don't condon drug use what so ever."
Maxwell Williams:"Which is exactly why I knew you'd be perfect. Hell. if you weren't afraid of helping Jalie, Mike's a walk in the park compared to that hellian."
Skylar Black:"I suppose..." [ Quickly Max writes down an address and hands Skylar a plane ticket. Skylar looks down and is shocked.]
Maxwell Williams:"Here...I'm flying you out first class...you're flight leave in about 3 hours. A limo will take you to get a few things you may need then to the air port."
Skylar Black:"Ummmm...Max....this address is in Miami..." Maxwell Williams:"I know...Mike is staying in Rick and Brandi's guest house. But the two of them are so wrapped up in their own stuff can't give him the attention he needs.."
Skylar Black:"Have you cleared this with them? Because Brandi James...well she hates me." Maxwell Williams:"Awe, don't take it personal Sky....Brandi hates mostly everyone anyone with the exception of Rick and Jalie... But yes..I've already spoken to Rick and he thinks this is actually a good thing."
Skylar Black:"As long as they know.......Arlight...wish me luck.." Maxwell Williams:"Keep me posted..." Skylar Black:"I will...have a good day Max.." [ With that Skylar picks up peanut and her pocketbook. She looks at Maxwell before exiting the office. Max nods as Skylar smiles and softly shuts the door on her way out, as the scene fades to black.]
