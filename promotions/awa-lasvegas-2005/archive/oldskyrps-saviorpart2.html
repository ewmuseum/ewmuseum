AWA (Las Vegas, 2005) — Roleplay (Skylar Black)

The Saint?

January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Saint? [ A brisk day in the northern country...gee who would have thought. But will cold wheather stop a woman on a mission to save a soul? Ofcourse not..Skylar Black is barely one miracle away from saint hood. Her mission now that was handed to her directly from Maxwell Williams is clear. She is to help get Mike Young back on track to be the once great champion that everyone remembers. Today Skylar's journey begins as her black super stretched limo pulls up infront of the Westin hotel. She gets out looking as beautiful as ever in a pair of black dressed pants and a pink jacket. You can see a fuzzy pink sweater at the top of the jacket. She walks through the hotel door and takes her hat off her head. Running her hand through her hair quickly she jogs to catch the elevator. Once inside she pushes the button for the floor Mike Young is said to be staying on. Skylar looks around and warms up her hands. She reaches her floor and steps off. She looks for the direction to head and quickly finds it before long she is standing infront of Mike Young hotel room door. Gently Skylar taps on the door that reads 1432. She waits for a moment then in a low soft voice says.]
Skylar Black:"Mike? Ummm Mike are you there?" [ After a brief pause the door to the door to the hotel room opened up and there stood Mike. He looked like a wreck as per his norm here lately...he just looked at Skylar for a moment before he stuck his tongue out to lick his pretty dry and chapped lips. ]
Mike Young: "I guess I am here...what do you want?" [ Taking a step back Skylar looks Mike up and down. ] Skylar Black:"Well first off, I think you could use a shower and some chap stick. You know you just look parched."
Mike Young: "Is that all you came here for? To tell me I could use some chap stick?" [ Mike just rolled his eyes a little bit still looking at Skylar glancing up and down her very quickly. ]
Mike Young: "That can't be the only reason why your here is it?" [ looking past Mike's shoulder Skylar looks at the table in the hotel with many liquor bottles. She smirks at Mike and then pushes her way into the room. Picking up the trash bucket Skylar immediately begins to toss the bottles into the trash.]
Skylar Black:"Entertaining last night?" Mike Young: "Nope...I was here by myself." [ Mike just watched Skylar as she started to throw away the bottles he didn't care as long as they were empty. Just as Skylar picked up a bottle of Jack Daniels that had maybe at best had a shot's worth left Mike snatched the bottle away from Skylar. ]
Mike Young: "Not finished with that one yet..." Skylar Black:"Yes you are!" [ Skylar grabs the bottle from Mike and puts it back in the trash. She pulls the bag up and ties it tight. Then sets it down, and stares at Mike.]
Skylar Black:"Now look.....I know you must think "I drink alone"by George Thorogood was written just for you, but you need to change that attitude."
[ She takes a glance around around the room looking at nothing but scattered dirty clothes and more empty beer cans and a few Jack Daniels bottles. She walks over to the window and pulls the curtain back then opens a window. As she pulls the curtain back, she looks back to see Mike shielding his eyes from the sun.]
Skylar Black:"Say it with me, Michael...SUNSHINE!" [ Mike still shielding his eyes from the onslaught of the sun to his pounding headache looks at Skylar with a grimmace on his face. ]
Mike Young: "That isn't sunshine...it's bright fucking light." Skylar Black:"Wow..it's been that long since you've seen the sun?" [ She giggles and walks over to mike. Skylar reaches for her pocketbook and takes out a bottle of advil then opens the mini fridge and takes a bottle of water out. She opens it then hands Mike 3, along with the water.]
Skylar Black:"Here take these...." [ Mike looks at Skylar for moment, but doesn't really hesitates in taking the Advil even though it will be a while before they have any real effect on his hangover. Mike chugs down half the bottle of water before he places the cap back on the top. ]
Mike Young: "Thank you, but can you please explain to me now why you are here?" [ Skylar kneels down infront of Mike and sighs] Skylar Black:"Well it's something Maxwell has asked me to do. Atfirst I must admit I was hestasting, but then he showed me this."
[ Skylar drags her pocket book over by her, and takes out her wallet. She looks through the pictures and pulls one out of Mike's daughter Patience.]
Skylar Black:"She's the reason....She's a beautiful little girl who needs her daddy Mike." [ Skylar says looking up at Mike with her innocent blue eyes that look glass like she's about to cry, as she touches his rough unshaven face.]
Skylar Black:"I want to help you for her sake." Mike Young: "How do you plan on helping me? There is no help for me..." [ Mike says as he looks does at Skylar hoping that she doesn't begin crying, because he doesn't think he could handle her crying in front of him. ]
Mike Young: "Samantha was the closest thing Patience has ever had to a mother and she turned her back on not only me, but her too."
Skylar Black:"I can't speak for Samantha's action Michael...but what I do know is that little girl needs you."
[ Skylar places her hand under Mike's chin and lifts his head.] Skylar Black:" I believe that deep down you are a good man, and I'm willing to help put you back together....but you have to atleast meet me even 20% of the way."
Mike Young: "I will try, but really what is there you can do for me?" [ Skylar jumps to her feet and claps....] Skylar Black:"Yay!..ok first....you have to shower then we're going out for some fresh air and a nice run....some excersie will do you good!"
Mike Young: "Don't really see how that is going to help me, but I'll give it a try." [ Mike says as he smiles slightly before he starts to dig through his bag trying to find something clean to put on. ]
Skylar Black:"It's simple Michael..Exercise gives you endorphins. Endorphins make you happy. Happy people just don't go around sulking, they just don't. Just wear something comfortable."
Mike Young: "I usually do were something comfortable...right now the problem is finding something clean. I haven't done lundary in a few days..."
[ Mike said, just before he found a pair of wind pants and a white wife beater. With those in hand he headed in to the bath room to take a shower. ]
Skylar Black:"I'll be right here.....Umm when did you eat last?" [ She inquired a bit concerned. Mike doesn't answer right away instead the first thing that is heard from the bathroom is the start of the water running. ]
Mike Young: "Last night...I had a candy bar." Skylar Black:"Not good at all...First shower....God knows you need one." [ Skyalr says as she scrunches her nose.] [ Ten...eleven give or take minutes Mike comes out of the bathroom clean with his hair still on the wet side of things and his face still unshaven wearing the clean wind pants and just putting on the wife beater as he walks. Mike looks at Skylar and smirks at her before saying? ]
Mike Young: "Better?" [ Skylar smiles and nods] Skylar Black:"Much.....oh I have something for you.." [ She reaches with a closed fist over to Mike and puts a tube of chap stick in his hand.] Skylar Black:"I'm sure that will help alot. I know I hate when my lips are dry...It's why I perfer the South to the North...the cold wheather is so harsh on your skin and lips."
Mike Young: "Thanks, but you keep it. Chapped lips don't bother me...when you've nearly had your body broken every night that you wrestle chapped lips are nothing."
[ Mike says as he starts to hand the chap stick back to Skylar. ] Skylar Black:"Sometimes MIke it's the little things that you don't realize that make you happy. The slightest...tiniest things. Like not having chapped lips..You keep it just incase you change your mind. Plus no girl wants to kiss a guy with dry chapped lips..That's just f.y.i"
[ Skylar walks to the door and opens it. As she does she turns back and flips her long blonde partly curled hair and smiles at Mike. Mike can't help, but smile at Skylar as he slips his running shoes on. As he starts to walk over to the door he slips the chap stick in to his wind pants pocket before grabbing his jacket. Cause if they are going outside for this run he would freeze to death with out it...with them currently being in Canada for their next couple of shows. ]
Mike Young: "Alright I will keep just in case I change my mind." Skylar Black:"Good.." [ Skylar steps into the hallway as Mike shuts the door to his room. Before she takes another step she looks at Mike.]
Skylar Black:"I'm so sorry Michael...You'll have to forgive my rude-ness....I know you are aware of who I am, but I didn't properly introduce myself to you since we've really have never formly met before...and that's just not very Southern of me...So..."
[ She straightens up and extends her hand.] Skylar Black:"Nice to meet you..I"m Skylar Black...your current AWA fem-x champion." [ Mike just smiles at Skylar as he takes her hand in to his and kisses the top of it like something out of old movie. ]
Mike Young: "Nice to meet you...I'm Mike Young...Former AWA World Heavyweight champion and Former AWA Legion Tag Team champion."
[ Skylar blushes a bit and looks tenderly at Mike.] Skylar Black:"Nice to meet you...I really am looking forward to knowing the REAL you...." Mike Young: "I don't think there is anything really interesting in the "real" me, but I am looking forward to getting to know you too."
Skylar Black:"Give yourself a bit of credit...You maybe surprised...come let's hit the gym."
Mike Young: "After you my lady..." [ Mike said, as for the first time in a while he didn't try to dull down his southern accent while letting Skylar take the lead. ]
Skylar Black:"Why thank you very much,...Sir..." [ she said as she existed the hotel.] Skylar Black:"There should be a limo that Maxwell is sending. I'm sure it will be right along in a moment."
[ Mike followed Skylar out the door as he slipped his jacket on and put his hands in to the pocket. Mike looked at Skylar and with smirk on his face... ]
Mike Young: "Please you don't have to say sir...it makes me feel older than what I really am."
Skylar Black:"Alright would you perfer Butthead then? Cuz lately that's how you've been acting."
Mike Young: "How about just plain old Mike?" [ Skylar giggled as she put her hands into her fluffy coat pockets.] Skylar Black:"Mike it is...." [ A black super stretch limo pulls curb side and Skylar leans to Mike.] Skylar Black:"That's our ride...Are you ready to begin...It's a New Year and I"m determined to help you turn a new leaf....Think you can handle it? I can be pretty touch when I want."
Mike Young: "I think I can handle it...and if by some chance I can't...I'll buy you dinner. How's that?"
[ Mike said, with a smile as he opened the door of the limo for Skylar before the driver could get out and back there to do it. ]
Skylar Black:"that sounds like a deal to me...Now hop in..." Mike Young: "Ladies first..." [ Mike said, still smiling and he didn't move until Skylar got in to the limo first. Once she was in he climbed in so the driver could shut the door behind him. As the scene fades to black.]
Match Talk: I actually feel honored that the AWA thinks I'm the best person for the new rookies to have their first match against. Last week it was Cyprus and this week Ivory. I'm really looking forward to seeing what he can do.
Cyprus finally gave me the challenge that I was so looking forward to, Unfortunately for me it did lead to a loss but everyone has an off night. And soon I'm sure Cyprus and I will see each other again. After all I'm well aware
that me having the Fem-X championship makes me a marked woman. But it's a good challenge that I am looking forward to.
This week match with Ivory will be just what I need to help sharpen my skills and get him ready for the competition he will face here in a fine fed.
