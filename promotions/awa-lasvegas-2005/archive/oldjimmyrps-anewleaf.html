A New Leaf

March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jimmy StrykerRhiannon James [ It's late evening and where is the Mouth of the AWA? If you've been wondering where exactly Jimmy Styrker has been you're not the only one. See Unfortunately do to the current going around of the flu Jimmy has been sick. Yes, Yes...some may have know that already but I'm not talking mental. I'm speaking of the physical body, you know the cough, sniffles..the whole nine yards. As of right now poor ole' jimmy is in bed with a fever. The door slightly opens and a crack of light shows. In walks Rhiannon James, Jimmy now girlfriend with medicine for him. Rhiannon puts on the night lamp by his bed and runs her hand through his hair.]
Rhiannon James: "Jimmy...? Honey are you awake?" [ Jimmy groans then slightly opens his eyes.] Jimmy Stryker:"Rhiannon...?" Rhiannon James: "Sweetheart...I brought you, your medicine..just sit up and take it.." Jimmy Stryker:"I don't need any icky medicine." Rhiannon James: "Stop being such a baby Jimmy...now sit up and take it." [ Slowly jimmy sits up and is handed a glass of orange juice and a few pills from Rhiannon. He pops them into his mouth and squints his eyes together as he swallows them.]
Rhiannon James:"I take it from your face that your throat still hurts." [ Jimmy smirks at her, then places the glasss on the side of the table.] Jimmy Stryker: "Gee, it's no wonder you graduated from college..You're a fuckin' genius." Rhiannon James: "You know with an attitude like that I might as well leave." Jimmy Stryker: "Oh is that so? And where do you think you're going to go?" [ Sitting in silence for a moment Rhiannon tries to mutter something but Jimmy interrupts.]
Jimmy Stryker: "No where is where you are going..." Rhiannon James: "Just shut up jimmy..." Jimmy Stryker: "You love me, you know it.." Rhiannon James: "Mmmmm,,maybe..." [ Jimmy smiles and slides back down and rests his head on the pillow. He pulls the blankets up towards his chin.]
Jimmy Stryker: "Baby can you turn up the heat?" Rhiannon James: "Are you cold?" Jimmy Stryker: "God damn freezing..." Rhiannon James: "Jimmy, it's like 75 in here.." Jimmy Stryker: "Not the way I feel.." [ Rhiannon rolls her eyes and rubs Jimmy's leg.] Rhiannon James: "If that's what you want I'll do it." Jimmy Stryker:"Thanks baby.." Rhianon James: "Sure thing..." [ Rhiannon stands up and begins to walk towards the door then turns around.] Rhiannon James: "Oh Jimmy...Jenny Tull phoned for you about your match Monday." Jimmy Stryker: "I'm not talking to any of them assholes.." Rhiannon James: "You sure?" Jimmy Styrker: "Positive.." Rhiannon James: "Maybe..you should call in sick Jimmy?" Jimmy Strkyer: "No! I'll be fine for Monday to take out..Salem Caprione...Hell even sick I'd still kick his ass.."
[ Rhiannon laughs and shakes her head.] Rhiannon James:" So that means I'll just hand you the phone when Shane or Jenny call." Jimmy Stryker: "Ummm No..but you can get over here and suck my dick...You're talented skills at the art of deep throating may make me feel a bit more alive."
Rhianon James: "ha..ha...You're so funny Jimmy..." Jimmy Stryker: "I'm not trying to be funny...get over here and wraps your lips around me.."
Rhiannon James: "Just relax..would you like some tapes of Salem..you know just to know what you should prepare for?"
Jimmy Styrker: "Hey Rhiannon?" Rhiannon James: "Yes?" Jimmy Stryker: "what part of suck me..do you not understand?" Rhiannon James: "Gee...all of it?" [ She says in a mocking tone.] Rhiannon James: "Do you want the tapes or not?" Jimmy Stryker: "What the fuck...whatever..." Rhiannon James: "Ok...I'll get them for you and then make you some nice home made chicken soup.,"
Jimmy Stryker: "Fine..do what you must.." Rhiannon James: "Alright...just rest and I'll be back in a bit.." Jimmy Stryker: "Good..Oh and when you bring me my soup...make sure you bring it to me naked."
Rhiannon James;"You're impossible.." Jimmy Stryker: "And you love me.." Rhiannon James: "For what reason I don't know.." Jimmy Stryker: "Because of my sparkling personality.." Rhiannon James: "Right...now get some rest.." Jimmy Stryker: "Yeah, yeah.." [ With that Jimmy snuggles up in bed and Rhiannon shuts the door as the scene fades to black.]
