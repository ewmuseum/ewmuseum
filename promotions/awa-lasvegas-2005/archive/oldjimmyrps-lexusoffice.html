AWA (Las Vegas, 2005) — Roleplay (Jimmy Stryker)

Lexus's Office

February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jimmy StrykerRhiannon James [ It's common knowledge that there are two people that Jimmy Stryker can do with out. First and fore most Rick Young, but running a close tight second is none other than AWA's World Champion Cody Carson. Once upon a time children not too long ago Jimmy Stryker was the lackey of Cody Carson. Sure Jimmy will argue that point and tell you that he was his assistant. Call it what you will..when you are forced to get your employer coffee among other things you indeed are a lacky. Really it doesn't get much more simple than that. Although Jimmy has gone on recorder saying the reason he even could stand being around Carson was to get closer to Brandi James. At Genesis we saw that Jimmy pushed Brandi down a set of stairs. This didn't sit well with commish Lexus Malone. Which bring us to today's opening scene. We join Jimmy and his new gal pal Rhiannon James standing infront of Lexus Malone's office door.]
Rhiannon James:"Now Jimmy remember you temper. You don't want to piss Lexus off the way you did last week, Remember you told her to go fuck herself?"
[ rubbing his chin he smiles.] Jimmy Stryker:"Remember? How the fuck could I forget the look on her face. Besides there's a bit of the green eyed monster there."
Rhiannon James;"With Lexus?" Jimmy Stryker:"Yeah..we used to have a little thing." Rhiannon James:"I see....Anyway I just think you should go in there be polite and see what she has to say."
Jimmy Stryker:"Rhiannon, I don't keep you around to think. I keep you around cuz your a hot piece of ass..."
Rhiannon James:"What a nice thing to say Jimmy." [ She sarcastically replies, as Jimmy mummbles under his breath.] Jimmy Stryker:"Beside your the next best thing to Brandi..that is till I can have Brandi all for myself."
Rhiannon James:"What was that?" Jimmy Stryker:"Nothing.." Rhiannon James;"Don't push it Jimmy, or else I won't come to the ring with you this week, and you'll be forced to deal with Carson on your own."
[ mocking her a bit.] Jimmy Stryker:"Watch it Jimmy..." [ Jimmy rolls his eyes.] Jimmy Stryker:"Do you really think I can't handle Carson? Gimme a break...C'mon on.." [ With out knocking Jimmy opens the door and enters Lexus's office. Lexus is just hanging up the phone. She folds her hands and pushes her chair close to her desk.]
Jimmy Stryker:"Ok Blondie..I'm here. Now make this quick." Lexus Malone:"Hey Jimmy....Don't worry I don't want you here any longer then you have to be."
Jimmy Stryker:"Atleast we can finaly agree on something." Lexus Malone;" Right...Anyway Jimmy..Do you remember our conversation last week? You know the one where you told me that I had no back bone? And then you told me to go fuck myself?"
[ Proudly Jimmy nods.... ] Jimmy Stryker:"Ofcourse I do..You're face was classic." [ Lexus pauses and smiles.] Lexus Malone:"Well I was just wondering how's this for back bone...? You may have won the match last week...but I'm voiding your title shot at Carson.."
[ Jimmy's eyes grow larger and he begins to stomp his foot like a child.] Jimmy Strkyer: "YOU BITCH! You can't fucking do that!" Lexus Malone:"Go on take your fit....but you see around here I believe in EARNED title shots. And other than you self-pro claiming yourself as the best you haven't shown it....Not to worry though I have a solution. You see there are exactly two weeks before My Bloody Valentine...with the PPV being on the third week of the 25th. I am booking you in a very high profile match at the PPV. If you win your PPV shot then you will be granted a title shot at the World Champion in March...be it Carson or Seth."
[ Jimmy rolls his eyes and nods as Rhiannon rubs his shoulder." Lexus Malone:"You see this has to do with the fact you think you can do and say whatever you wish. You've done nothing but torment Rick Young...really for no reason other then the fact he has Brandi and you don't. You nearly killed Brandi's baby by pushing her down the stairs two weeks ago..And I really became upset by that and felt something had to be done. You see...I have carefully made my choice of what to do with you, and I have full backing from Samantha and Maxwell...at MY Bloody Valentine it will be....Jimmy Stryker vs. Rick Young! And Rick gets to choose the match he would like..."
[ A smile crosses Lexus's face as Jimmy shakes his head no.] Jimmy Stryker:"You Cunt! Do you not realize how unstable Rick is lately?" Lexus Malone:"Awe,,,what's wrong Jimmy? Did you not think I wouldn't allow Rick his shot at you? If you can't beat Rick then there is no way you will beat Carson.or Seth....So now...how was that for backbone?"
[ Lexus smiles as she rolls back in her chair. Jimmy lunges at the ropes and screams ] Jimmy Stryker: "fuck you bitch!" [ Lexus shrugs and laughs. While Rhianon takes Jimmy's hand and begins to pull him back.] Rhiannon James:"Jimmy, that's enough! Come on before she does anything worse to you." [ Turning three differnt shades of red he screams.] Jimmy Stryker:"Worse? What could be worse then that. She took away my title shot! She's making me fight Rick Young at My Bloody Valentine! How could it be much worse?"
[ Giggling a bit..Lexus interrupts.] Lexus Malone:"Ya know Jimmy, I could always get Adonis back and you can have another strap match with him. Or would you perfer another mud wrestling match? Possibly tuxedo? Awe, who am I kidding. I know you'd want all three..I'd make it a best of three series."
Jimmy Stryker:"You wouldn't dare!" Lexus Malone:"I wouldn't? try me Jimmy!" [ Putting her hand over Jimmy's mouth Rhiannon begins to walk backwards with him.] Rhiannon James:"NO! He's fine Lexus...We're going now." Lexus Malone:"Good get him out of my sight!" [ Rhiannon and Jimmy walk out of Lexus's office and stand in the lobby. Jimmy rips her hand from his mouth and points at her.]
Jimmy Stryker:"Never and I do mean Never! Ever make me look bad like that again! I'm perfectly capable of handling that twitt."
Rhiannon James:"Jimmy...she.." Jimmy Stryker:"Just shut up please Rhiannon..You've done enough! Come on let's go." Rhiannon James:"Where?" Jimmy Stryker:"To find some way for me to get my title shot back." Rhianon James:"But if Max and Sam.." [ Jimmmy interrupts her.] Jimmy Stryker:"Hush! Not one more word from your doubting lips." Rhinnon James:"ah...ok..." Jimmmy Stryker:"good...now move it." [ Jimmy and Rhiannon walk down the hallway as the scene fades.]
