CJ Revenge [ Most of you know the James family by now. Know the backing spoiled bitch my sister Brandi is. You know that my sister Rhiannon is just doing her best trying to fit in, and is in love with a man that is just using her to get ahead in this company. And yes you are aware that my family is self serving to the point where is borders sheer evil. So what does that say about my mother? Well mom has given up trying to defend and explain the actions of her daughter and husband. Mom is the "good one" she's the one that wants the best for all of us. I've always considered my mother the balance of good in the house that holds more egos than anyone knows what to do with. So where do I fit in all of this? I wish I knew myself sometimes. I'm the one that they are disappointed in. I'm the one they exspect more of than the rest of my siblings. Instead I'm reminded daily how I've failed my father by letting OSW take a dive into the ground. How I failed in business and am nothing more than a two bit musican that smoke a lot of weed. Sure to them it's a bad thing, but with me...blah....I just want to relax smoke a bowl and chill. What's so wrong with that? If your feeling what I'm saying give me an "Amen"! Yeah you know what I'm saying. In the balance of good and evil in the James house hold it's hard to say what side one may want to take.
Over the last few months while staying with my sister Brandi in her gues house I have seen more than I've wanted. I've seen her humilate her husband, I've watched as she let a drunken Alex Wilkins sleep in the guest house, while he was trying to bed her. Sure nothing happen, but had he been more sober you never know. Then again I wonder just how interested in Wilkins she is, or if she just see's him as yet another pawn in her game. You know the game I'm talking about. The game where only Brandi comes out as the winner. It's a game she's played many times and has won. I see her doing to Rick what she has done to Sean Lane. God for bid a man shows some compassion for someone other than her. If you do that it makes you weak, you're branded as gutless. Sorry but not everyone is as self centered as my baby sis. After the week Rick and I spent in Vegas and I got to know him I felt two things. First his love for my sister is unconditional. Second because of his unconditional love for her, he's slowly being driven insane. This is where I need to step in.
Rick has his faults just like everyone else, but with that said. I'd much rather have him as a brother in law than some baseball player with an ego bigger than Texas it self. I'm sorry to say that my little sister has no clue what's best for her. Sure she lusts hard after Josh, she's even bought intot he fact they are destin to be together. Silly little girl believing in fate, and things. I need to do something that will get back at her and make my father eat his words. It finally came to me as I was watching clerks just chillin'. The easiest way to do this was to go pay a little vist to Doctor Brighton. If Rick is Nolan's father, Brandi will have no choice but to give him a fair chance. Not to mention that my father has stated that the only way Rick will have another shot at the world title is if he's the father of Nolan. It's a win, win situation for Rick and myself if that test says he's Nolan's daddy. What do I have to gain from him being Nolan's daddy...revenge. To prove to my father that I have the bone back he thinks I don't , and my sister not getting just what she wants. Hell over time she'll even learn to love Rick....maybe. I'll concern myself with that one later.
Right now I'm at Miami City hospital where I'm on a mission. To find the one man that hold the answer to everyone's future in a office folder. I walked into the main lobby area. Once I found his name on the board I headed straight to his office. I wasted no time walking past his secretary who seemed as if she couldn't have cared less. As she wasn't paying attention and talking on the phone, making what sounded like her nightly plans. Walking right up to his door, and not knocking was worth it. Dr. Brighton: "Please CJ...don't tell my wife." Cameron James Jr.: "Trust me, your wife is about fourth on my list..First I would tell the girls parents, then the police, then I'd notify the medical board of your perverse actions. After that, and only then would I have a chat with Mrs. Brighton."
[ A dirty way to get what you want? yes, but it's most certainly effective. He sighed and clicked his pen.]
Dr. Brighton: "Exactly what do you want me to write you script of?" [ I just chuckled as I crossed my arms. ] Cameron James Jr.: "That's not exactly what I'm after." Dr. Brighton: "Yes, I did..at the request of your father." Cameron James Jr. : "I want to see the file." Dr. Brighton:"CJ, I can't allow that." Cameron James Jr.: "That's a real shame. And being the nice guy that I am, I'm going to give you the choice of who you want me to contact first. The girls parents or the medical board?"
Dr. Brighton: "Be reasonable CJ." Cameron James Jr.: "On the contrary I think I'm being more than reasonable not phoning anyone sooner. What do you think?"
[ No sooner I said that he got up and opened the door. He looked at his secretary and reluctantly said. ]
Dr. Brighton: "Cindy, can you please get me the folder for Brandi James-Young." [ She smiled and gave a big nod. Only a few minutes of silence had passed before she entered the room with folder in hand. She handed it to Dr. Brighton, and he waited till she exited the room before handing the folder to me. I opened and looked at the results of the test. Just as my father has said. Nolan is the son of Josh Beckett. Brandi knew it, my father knew, even to a point I'm sure Josh has a feeling that Nolan is his as well. But Rick the poor bastard is definately in denial in thinking other wise. Dr. Brighton: "There are you satisfied?" Cameron James Jr.: " I will be satisfied once the results are changed." Dr. Brighton: "No..I'll lose my license.."
