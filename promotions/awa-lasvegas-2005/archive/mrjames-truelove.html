Mr. James: "It's not a crusade...I'm just trying to make things easy for you. You know the door isn't fully shut with you and Tiffany yet. I saw how she still looks at you. And face it Rick you never get over your first and true love.."

Really who would still want to be with someone that keeps cheating on her? Tiffany doesn't have a back bone and Brandi enough back bone for two women and I want a woman with a back bone."
