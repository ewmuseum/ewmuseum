AWA (Las Vegas, 2005) — Roleplay (Jimmy Stryker)

Enough

October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Once again we open to Jimmy Stryker's place in South Beach in beautiful Miami, Florida. Jimmy is relaxing by the pool of the hotel he's staying at while his home is being built. Jimmy leans over to the table next to him, and reaches for his drink. He reaches in the pocket of his navy shorts, and pulls out his cell phone that beeped from receiving a text message. Jimmy looks at it and smirks. He puts the phone on the table, as Kasey sits in the chair next him. Jimmy tilts down his sunglass to look at Kasey. ]
Jimmy Stryker: "what's up man?" [ Kasey looks at Jimmy's phone. ] Kasey: "Was that Brandi or Rick?" Jimmy Stryker:"You really need to fucking relax Kasey, I told you...I've taken care of Brandi already."
Kasey: "But...You said that Rick called you, and said I needed to be back Monday morning to them, and it's Tuesday, and well...I'm still here."
[ Jimmy sits up in his chair to face Kasey, he reaches over smacking Kasey in the head. ] Jimmy Stryker: "Smarten the fuck up! I told you I took care of it, and I did. Do you really think I give a fuck what Rick said?"
[ Jimmy lays back in his chair. ] Jimmy Stryker: "Kasey, People in glass houses shouldn't throw stones. And Rick Young is in NO position to be threating me at all. He thinks he's a bad ass, that I'm scared of him. Gimme a fucking break. He's got more to watch out for then anyone I know."
Kasey: "Why's that?" Jimmy Stryker: "Dude, have you not been paying attention." [ Kasey shrugs ] Jimmy Stryker: "Let's break it down like this. Rick has Brandi....believe as we speak Cody Carson's inside are torn up over that. "
Kasey: "I thought Carson was back with Carmen." Jimmy Stryker: "He is....but that doesn't mean a part of him still doesn't long for Brandi. I have to admit, that's one talent that woman does have."
Kasey: "Yeah, that I do have to agree with....Jimmy you should have seen the way she played, Lane against Sparks, then turned on them both by marrying Walsh."
Jimmy Stryker: "Yeah, the woman has a gift.....When she puts her mind to it, she's evil at it finest....God, how I admire that."
[ Jimmy pauses for a moment. ] Jimmy Stryker: "Any...relax dude...Brandi is delt with...there's no one she's going to screw with me. You're my assistant not hers anymore...No matter what that piece of shit Rick Young says."
Kasey:"How?" Jimmy Stryker: "How, what?" Kasey: "Did you get Brandi to agree to let me work only for you." Jimmy Stryker:"You can thank Hunter Davis for being a genius." Kasey: "Why?" [ Jimmy shakes his head.] Jimmy Stryker: "Fuck....Kasey, man...you ask too many dam questions...Is it ok I'm here? Brandi's gonna be pissed, I'm not back there yet ! Rick says this....You're acting like a God damn five year old...I'll just say this...Hunter is a genius... The night of Blood is thicker than water, he rigged Brandi's hotel room, and video taped her and Rick having sex. Before he left he gave the tape to me...A few nights ago I told Brandi that if she didn't give me you..I'd tell Rick that she had something to do with it....And then would send Tiffany a copy you know, incase Tiffany ever wised up and decided to take Rick to divorce court...."
Kasey: "Wow...that's pretty smart Jimmy." Jimmy Stryker:"No fucking shit it's smart. Brandi is in love with Rick, even if she refuses to admit it. Besides that she's also very afraid of him...which she again won't admit. So in a moment of her love and being afraid thinking no one will save her...She buckled, and allowed you to come work for me."
Kasey: "So why did Rick call you threating you for me to come back?" Jimmy Stryker:"Because Rick is a stupid red neck prick." [ Kasey relaxes back in his chair, and rests his head back. ] Kasey: "As long as you're sure..." Jimmy Stryker:"Which I am, now shut the fuck up, and enjoy the sun." [ Kasey smiles and takes one more sip of his drink. Jimmy laughs a bit at Kasey, then drifts to sleep. While he sleeps he dreams that he is on the side of a long desert road, and his car has over heated. He looks up and see's a sign that reads Las Vegas 18miles. Jimmy gets out of his car slamming the car door, he's wearing a pair of jeans and white tank top. He begins to walk up the road to get help for his car. As he's walking a black convertable pulls up along the side of him, with a goregous blonde driving it. She slows down, and beeps at Jimmy, he stops and looks at her. ]
Goregous Blonde:"Excuse...." [ Jimmy raises his eyebrow. ] Jimmy Stryker: "Yeah...." [ The woman licks her lips looking Jimmy up and down. ] Goregous Blonde: "Ummm, Are those bugle boy jeans that you're wearing?" [ Jimmy looks himself up and down, then back at the woman with a cocky grin on his face.] Jimmy Stryker:"Why yes,.....they are bugle boy jeans." [ The woman smiles and nods...] Goregous Blonde:"Thank You..." [ She then speeds away leaving Jimmy standing there as dust kicks up from her back tires. Jimmy yells to her. ]
Jimmy Stryker: "What the fuck was that? Get back here you fuckin' whore ! " [ As Jimmy get more fustrated another car creeps up in back of him. A white BMW...also a convertable...The driver doesn't say a word, but Jimmy can hear the car slowing driving next to him. He turns around, and it's Adonis...he yells over to Jimmy. ]
Adonis: "I'll give you a ride Jimmy....." [ Adonis smiles and blows a kiss to Jimmy. ] Jimmy Stryker:"Oh the fuck you will...." [ Adonis shrugs and speeds off...Jimmy all the sudden sits up in his chair breathing heavy. Kasey looks over as he can see Jimmy is obviously upset.]
Kasey: "Something wrong Jimmy?" [ Jimmy throw the ice from his glass on Kasey, and Kasey shoots up in his chair. ] Jimmy Stryker: "Ofcourse something is wrong man...I was having a decent dream till, it got fucked up by Adonis....That queer can't even leave me alone in my dreams....What the fuck...This has to end with him. Someone needs to stop think fucking shit from happening."
Kasey: "All you have to do is beat him this week, and I'm sure that will end it." Jimmy Stryker: "You would think that...what's in store next week..a leather strap match? The dude is a freak...who have issues with people's feet. I'm the brightest star here, and I'm not being put to use the way I should be. It's a conspiracy I tell you.......It's to keep me down so I don't show everyone else up."
Kasey: "Don't worry Jimmy, you'll get your shot." Jimmy Stryker: "My fucking shot should be at that moron Cody Carson....Fuck Carson thinking he's bad ass because he was handed Mike's title because that idiot wasn't sure if he wanted to stay or go..."
[ Jimmy pauses, and scratches his chin. ] Jimmy Stryker: "Know what's weird Kasey?" Kasey: "Ummm, Adonis?" Jimmy Stryker: "Well yeah but even more so then him." Kasey:"What's that Jimmy?" Jimmy stryker: "How shockingly similar Mike Young and Cody Carson look...I mean it's almost like their fucking twins man. Maybe the reason why Carson hates Rick so much is because they're family, and Big brother Rick is fucking the woman Carson wants. It's fucking insane how much Mike and Cody look alike..."
[ Kasey chuckles. ] Kasey: "Jimmy, I think you've had way too much sun today.." Jimmy Stryker: "Fuck you....hear me out..." [ As Jimmy begins explaining his theroy to Kasey, they notice Jenny Tull infront of them. Jenny smiles looking at Jimmy. He stands up next to hear leaning close. ]
Jenny Tull: "Hey, Jimmy..I was wonder if I could have an interview with you?" [ Jimmy smirks. ] Jimmy Stryker: "Here?" [ Jenny looks around then shrugs her shoulder.] Jenny Tull: "If you want...I don't care where we do it." [ Jimmy chuckles and looks at Kasey for a moment, who automatically knows what Jimmy is thinking. Jimmy puts his arm around Jenny.]
Jimmy Stryker: "I'll do it with you alright...but let's go back up to my room." [ Jenny's eyes light up. ] Jenny Tull: "Great ! just let me go to the van and get my microphone first." Jimmy Stryker:" No need for that....I've got my own that I'll show you." [ He winks at Jenny.] Jenny Tull: "You do?" Jimmy Stryker:"Yep....I'll definately let you do some talking, and wraping your lips around it."
Jenny Tull: "Well...ok let's go..." Jimmy Stryker: "After you, sweet ass.." [ Jimmy let's Jenny walk infront of him as he stares at her ass. Jimmy looks back pointing at Kasey.]
Jimmy Stryker: "I'll need about two hours...So make sure I'm left the fuck alone! Got it?" Kasey: "You can count on me Jimmy." [ Jenny hears Jimmy, and stops. As Kasey gives Jimmy the thumbs up.] Jenny Tull: "Wow...two hours? That's an awful long time.." Jimmy Stryker: "If you're a good little girl daddy just may let you stay longer." [ Jimmy winks at Jenny and again puts his arm around her as she giggles, the scene fades out.]
