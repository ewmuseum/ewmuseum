Stryker Intro

September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ The scene opens to a sound stage. It's pitch black, although some noise can be heard as if someone is walking around. Just then a few spotlights are turned on. For a few seconds all that can be seen is just a slightly light room. All the sudden a man standing 6"2 about 154 can be seen dragging a chair behind him. He has fair skin, blue eyes, and dark hair with blonde highlights. He's dressed in a pair of jeans with a black tank top, and black Jordan sneakers. As he put the chair into the center of the room one more light turns on. He sits in the chair, and takes a pack of Malboro's from his pocket. He taps the box, then pulls out a cigarrett, and lights it. He puts the lighter back into his pocket then leans forward as he notices the camera is on him. It's now clear that the man is Cody Carson's associate, Jimmy Stryker. ]
Jimmy Stryker: " What? Were you exspecting Cody Carson?" [ Stryker snickers. ] Jimmy Stryker: " Heh, The only reason I ever started hanging around that guy, was because he's a close friend of that hot piece of ass Brandi James. "
[ Stryker pauses, then takes a drag from his cigarett. ] Jimmy Stryker: " Speaking of Brandi James maybe you're exspecting her?" [ Stryker shakes his head. ] Jimmy Stryker: " She was here not long ago....." [ He pauses then sighs. ] Jimmy Stryker: "That was till Rick Young came looking for her. My suggestion if you want to find her, follow the sound of moaning. I'm sure Young has he pinned against a wall by now, and her legs are tightly wrapped around his waist...Fucking lucky bastard. Not that I can blame the man. It's not like, I wouldn't do the same thing myself. That's if she'd let me, but since her judgement is slightly screwed up at the moment. We all know the only guy that's going to be tapping that, is Rick Young."
[ Jimmy looks off to the side almost as if he's looking for Brandi, then takes another drag of his cigarett. ]
Jimmy Stryker: " It's a fucking shame that an asshole like Rick Young is able to even get the attention of a woman as fine as Brandi. I mean, C'mon now. Brandi James is smart,beautiful, sexy, seductive, does the cutest evil things. She's everything a man could want. Built, stacked and all that, and you drive it around like a cadillac. But am I ? No ! Why? Because she's with Rick Young. Actually not exactly yet. She, herself says they are only lovers. Stupid shit Rick is, if he's even thinking about staying with his wife. Rick's wife compared to Brandi..well Tiffany Phoenix...or Thersea which ever...can't compare to Brandi James. "
[ Jimmy stops and flicks his cigarett ash, then shakes his head. ] Jimmy Stryker: " Jesus, I'm loosing focus here. I suppose that's the effect Brandi has on me. God there isn't anything I wouldn't do for that woman, but I digress. The real reason for all of this, is to introduce myself. Jimmy Stryer's the name. I've decided that what AWA is a big kick in the ass. I've hung around Carson, who unsuccessfully tried to make me his whipping boy. The same way Sean Walsh did to him only about a year an a half ago. Once Carson learnt howerver that I'm not as easy to push around as he thought, the nature of our relationship changed."
[ Jimmy laughs, and takes another drag of his cigarrett as if he's remembering working for Cody. ]
Jimmy Stryker: " Wasn't he surprised when he found out, I was an even bigger dick than he is. Ah, good times....Now ofcourse there is the woman that I'm employeed by, thanks to Carson. AWA's own Fem-X Champion, Brandi James. Who unfortunately has all her attention focused else where at the moment."
[ Jimmy drops his cigarette on the ground, and steps on it with his foot to put it out...and sarcastically coughs. ]
Jimmy Stryker: ***cough*** "Rick Young"...****cough*** "Rick Young" [ He looks up and smirks into the camera. ] Jimmy Stryker: " So since my new boss is busy with her own agenda, and won't let me join in on the destruction of the current Mrs. Young. I have chosen to do what I should have all along. To enter the ring myself, and show the rest of you talent-less idiots exactly what entertainment looks like. To get all the glory that should be coming my."
[ Jimmy leans back, and place his hands behind his head. ] Jimmy Stryker: " My first challenge, will be against Nick Stellar at Blood is Thicker Than Water. That's if you actually want to give Stellar some credit. Me, I really can't see how you could. How can you give any type of credibilty to a man's who keeps his past "under-wraps" gimme a fucking break. What Nick, did ya do? Did ya kill someone? Maybe you raped a few women, or ran over the neighborhood cat. I wonder...."
[ Jimmy scratches his chin. ] Jimmy Stryker: " Is the lack of back ground suppose to make us scared? Oooooohhhhh,,,,,The Man of Mystery...He's done something so many bad, he doesn't want to talk about it....Oh, My God ! "
[Jimmy leans forward, and takes another cigarett then lights it. ] Jimmy Stryker: " It's actually pretty laugh able. Me on the other hand, I don't give a fuck. I'm Mad, Bad and Dangerous to know. I've been screwed over, and I've done my fair share of fucking people over as well. It's a live and learn type of deal. Sometimes you get caught, Sometimes you don't. The differnce is when you do get caught if you've learnt from the mistake, and make sure you don't repeat it."
[ All the sudden Jimmy is interrupted by a camera man. ] Camera Man: "Ummm,, Jimmy....We actually need this area for a AWA commercial we're doing with the members of A3. "
[ Jimmy stands up, with the cigarett half hanging out of his mouth pointing at the camera man. ]
Jimmy Stryker: " What the fuck if I was Cody Carson, or Mike Young you wouldn't be bothering me while I'm trying to cut a promo ! Listen, you pedifile...Yeah, that's right, I saw the way you were looking at those girls scouts that were selling cookies. Listen up fucknut, I have some news for you, I'm the new fucking star around here. When I'm finished you can have this shit hole for your precious "A3," shit stain."
[ The camera man gets flustered, and backs away from Jimmy. Jimmy flicks his cigarrett at him, hitting him on the shoulder. ]
Jimmy Stryker: " Oh, walking away? You sick fuck ! Those girls couldn't have been more than 11. You should know the fucking rule, dude....If there's no grass on the playing field, stay the hell away ! "
[ The camera man runs out of sights, and Jimmy picks up the chair and tosses it off camera. ]
Jimmy Stryker: " Fuck...I can't believe they can't get better help around here. Where the fuck is that hot piece of ass Jenny Tull to fucking interview me, or the other chump...Shane, Steve, Sean...whatever his fucking name is that no one wants to get interviewed by? I mean Hunter knew that I was cutting my promo for the Stellar match, and he can't even send him? What the fuck?"
[ Jimmy walks close to the camera, and sticks his face in the lens. ] Jimmy Stryker: " You think that Cody Carson is your next star that's suppose replace the pathetic Mike Young? Think again....You're fucking blowing it, by not giving me the respect I deserve ! You get one more chance.... MY advice....DON"T BLOW IT !"
[ Jimmy takes the camera and throws it on the ground, then steps on it, as all that can now be seen is static. ]
