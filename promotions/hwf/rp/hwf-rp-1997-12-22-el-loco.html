HWF: El Loco

22 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well Knight, you can remain silent about the Reign, but soon I shall speak volumes on behalf of the Giles. Ever since I first ventured to the ranks here, all I asked for was a friend. Its mean people like you who have ignored me. well the ignorance stops now. Now the blueblood smug snobby behavior stops now. For I have made yet another friend who has vowed to fight alongside me under the Giles. So yes I am happy. Who needs Dunatis anyway? Let him finish being the Packs whipping boy. How can he ever face the nirror again. I on the other hand wake up every day and do not fear anything. For while I am of no individual importance around here, the work and dedication I do in the name of the master does and I know I will eventually be in a better place. But first Knight, Batman, whatever the hell you are cuz frankly I dont give a damn, I must make yet another sacrifice in the shape of your carcass. You can ignore me no longer. If you dont respond soon vocally, you will never get a chance, for the destuction of your soul, and the tasting of your blood is imminent. Aint life great. Hehe. I am really a nice guy. All I wanted was a friend.....Do you feel the pain? bang bang!!! Tequilla Carlos The Messenger Faithful of the Giles EL Loco RoT
