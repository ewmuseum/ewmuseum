HWF: The Odd Couple

22 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Odd Couple, Andre and The MAN, are sitting in their tour bus, which is sailing down the freeway.) Man: WELL, our friends, the DUO, want this threeway tag title thing. SURE, we'll except. Andre: If you think about it, they might as well just give us the belts now. We already beat the crap out of the DUO until Deathstrike came down there and stopped the match. We KNOW we can beat the champs, simply because we know we can beat ANYONE. NOW, I see we have this match this Monday, against the DUO. But FIRST off, I'd like to compliment the DUO of the use of OUTDATED, and STUPID slams, or cracks or whatever you want to call them. That one about us needing to get on with our lives...WHOOPS, we don't have one, that was REALLY original, we've NEVER heard that one. I'm sorry we don't like to try and be cool like you, by making up cheesy places to be, that TRY to make us look cool. At least we don't copy other people by making up places to go or be. NOW, if there's ONE thing you should know about us, it's that we NEVER beg for mercy, let alone, beg for mercy at YOUR hands. Man: That's true, and I'm sure the LAST things Kris is worrying about, is a mid-card tag team like you two. BESIDES, I'm sure he's got his hands full worrying about us two, and Shango's unfortunate accident. HEY, why wouldn't he? We ARE the tag team division as far as I'm concerned, and pretty soon, we'll have those belts that Big and Smalls, or at least I presume those are your names. Apparently, the Duo can't remember your names as seeing as how they always get them wrong. So I guess that proves that the Duo is BARELY a mid-card team as seeing how they can't even remember tag team names. NOW, on to other things, about you guys not taking NO for an answer...WELL, we ALWAYS say yes to a challenge, so I guess there's no need to worry about US saying no, we're just afraid that YOU two might back out once tag teams like US and Big n Smalls accept. But if a mid-card tag team like you REALLY wants BOTH of us in the ring at the same time, HEY, we're all up for it. NOW, we have some words from the Bulkster to Ken Estes. (Camera fades out, then back in to the Bulkster) Bulk: HEY Mr. Estes, you seem to have this misconception that my mind isn't made up. WELL, it most DEFINATELY is. You see, when someone calls me a chicken for leaving because I was on bad terms with the president of the HWF, I DON'T take to it very kindly. And EVERYONE knows that I'd still be here, accept for the President, AND I have other opporunities to persue. BUT, just so YOU don't have the right to say I'm chicken, I WILL come back, and wrestle ONE last match. AFTER that match, I'll leave the building, and whether I win OR lose, I will NEVER come back. SO DON'T say I left the HWF because you beat me, because that's NOT the truth, and DON'T say it's my last match because I was scared of you, or ANY other crap like that, because THAT is a lie too. Just LET it be known. You KNOW, I got a BETTER idea, if YOU win, I'll leave, or I'll kiss your feet, or I'll be your personal servant for a month, or I'll re-sign longterm in the HWF, and only wrestle and lose to you, or ANYTHING like that. WHAT I'm saying, is that this match might as we'll be FUN if it's going to be my last one. SO, if I win, YOU leave the HWF, FOREVER, if YOU win, I'll do ONE thing that you say, nothing more than one, and you can't say the one thing you want me to do is to do more than one thing. You know, you CAN'T wish for more wishes, GET THE DRIFT? You don't have to accept, but I thought I might as well make my last match fun. If you have anything you want to change about what I do if you win, than name it. But for now, I must be going, just remember this one thing...BROTHER, WHAT CHA GONNA DO, IF THE BULKSTER, AND ALL HIS BULKAMANIACS COME RUNNIN' AFTER YOU !?!?!?!?!?!!! (Camera fades back to the Odd Couple) Andre: I think ALL of us are plum out of things to say. This has been the Odd Couple, and we'd just like to say, goodbye. (Camera Fades)
