HWF (Dec 1996) — Roleplay

HWF: John Austin (2)

23 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HWF Presents... The Greatest Champion in SCWF History... The Only Talent out of the WecW... He retired Ralph Austin... He retired Prototype... The next TV Champ... John Austin! <John Austin is seated alone in his living room at home. He is watching the itnerview segment with Batman earlier today. He stops the tape.> John Austin: Dark Knight, last Monday if I felt like sticking around I could have and would have kicked the crap out of you like I know I can. But I wanted to tune up for Bad *ss John in an upcoming match for the HWF Television Title. But last week was a fluke, that you won a match. Countout and Disqualification victories mean nothing to me or the rest of the HWF. Here is a little of the lying you had to tell everyone. <Austin rolls the tape> Batman: It seems Austin is gonna attempt to stay in the squared circle for at at least a couple minutes at our next fight <Austin stops the tape> John Austin: You see, you're completly misguided. I already told ya, if my heart was not in such tremendous pain I would have stuck aroud for the match. Well I will stick around for a few minutes this time and it will be a few minutes of living h*ll for you and then I will walk away with the win, after a few minutes ofcourse. <Austin rolls the tape> Batman: I guess Austin has figured out, his cowardlyness is not getting him anywhere in the HWF <Austin stops the tape> John Austin: I am not a coward. Infact I fear no man. And just as a point of order, "COWARDLYNESS" isn't even a word you moron. The proper word is "COWARDICE." Obviouslly our friend Batman wasn't and English major in school. <Austin rolls the tape> Batman: You probably know of how I was screwed out of a 1,2,3 pin victory over you the last time. <Austin stops the tape> John Austin: The closest you came to a pinfall over me was seeing two, not three, but two of my fingers saluting you as I walked out the door. You have had many pinfalls but you never can get one over me. <Austin rolls the tape> Batman: Batman is a nine time world champion, and holder of many other titles <Austin stops the tape> John Austin: Big deal! You never did jack in the HWF. You won your titles in all the Indy promotions taking on opponents like Ozzy for a living. Who can't do that? Now you stopped talking about me on the tape here which was a mistake cause there is alot more about me you could have said. So until you get your facts and vocabulary straight, you can forget getting a win against me. Batman, tonight you go down, and that's not the bottom line...it's the truth!! <Austin continues to view the tape as the camera fades to black.>
