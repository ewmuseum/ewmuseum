HWF: The Villain In Black, Tong Po

24 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He was never a champion in the SCWF..... He killed John Austin the WecW..... Ralph Austin's stablemate in the EWF..... He is the best cruiserweight in the World.... ::Tong Po is seen sitting in a room, as the cameras fade in, the screams of thousands of tortured souls can be heard we see the camera lens steaming up, it's as if they are entering the very bowels of hell. When the camera zooms in on Po he is sitting with his WecW North American Title in hand and what appears to be a framed picture in the other one. The camera stops zooming and Po begins to speak.:: Death Row presents..... "The Tiger"....... "The Villain in Black"........ Tong Po!!...... Tong Po:First up is Doc Bonghit....now, you talk like you ran the KoD out of the HWF.....but, I'm still here. Now, I won't run at the mere mention of your name, I might come in and take your head off making you love every second of it but, that's about it. You suck, you always will suck and no one will ever like you, even Ryan Carroll's mom has turned you down......even your mom hates you. Now, on to the subject of the title you used to walk around like you the greatest thing on the planet with....well....Socrates will be wearing it pretty soon......and that's just the way things are.....you'll get used to it. Okay, now let me talk about our Cruiserweight Champion for a moment.....James "Simply The Best" Pillow.....I don't really care how good of a Pillow you are, that makes no difference to me but, as a champion, you make this fed ashamed with those two queers you hang out with.....it makes me sick. Now onto what happened on "Banned in the HWF" with IrishDevil.....yes, we all know EVERY member of the Pack are pussies but, I wasn't aware that they were so afraid of me that Devil hit me in the eye with a bottle of Thunderbird.....of course, that's because he can't wrestle and he is as afraid of me as Mikey Donovan and the other members of the Pack.....I thought he would be the only one with any balls. Now, my final challenge to them, if they accept.....me versus Irish Devil, Bad @$$ John and Nightshade.....that should make it almost even, they'll still be at a disadvantage sure but, with three of them they should be able to at least make me break a sweat......probably not but, hey, we can dream. Anyway, I doubt you'll accept because I mean, you all know you can't beat me, I know you can't beat me and even your stable knows you can't beat me. John Austin.....the greatest champion in the SCWF? Gotta be ToP or Devastating Don.....there's no way it was you......loser. The only talent out of the WecW.....FLaSh Tucker, The Lake Shore Strangler, Rey Misterio Sr, Sebastian Jericho, myself.......and yes.....even Tazz. Now, how many of those people did you beat? Tazz.....you lost to Misterio, you lost to FLaSh, you lost to me, you even lost to Tazz four times before you finally lucked up and beat him. The man who retired Ralph Austin......well, I'll bet that's not an easy thing to say considering he's not retired and he's still wrestling in the EWF and one half of the number two contenders for the Tag Team Titles. That discredits pretty much everything you just said doesn't it? Yeah, I think it does.....oh well.....too bad for you. This newcomer, Deimos is coming after a certain title.....Godly Ken Davison.....you'd better be ready, Death Row will hold every title in this federation when we're done. Okay, Kris, I have an idea for a new title, a three man tag title.....I could bring together three men in this fed.....well....two and one new addition and they would dominate it......if you're wondering who they are it's Giant Condom Man, Master Bation and "Sadistic" Sam Hayte......they may end up taking the tag titles if you don't accept the idea. James Pillow, after I finish with the Pack....get ready to lose that Cruiserweight Title.....it WILL be around my waist before it's over.....now, Blade.....we have someone who will take that World Title away from you and you know it who it is but, you don't know it's them yet.....I'll keep you in suspense for right now though. Now, that's all I have to say right now, except for a few final words.......Pack.....you won't beat me....why? Because these are the eyes of a champion and you ain't got em!!!!! ::Tong Po smiles at the camera and the flames shoot up all around him, when they subside he is nowhere to be seen, as the camera turns around to leave the room they see Po standing around outside. The scene fades to a KWF commercial.:: He was never a champion in the SCWF..... He killed John Austin the WecW..... Ralph Austin's stablemate in the EWF.....
