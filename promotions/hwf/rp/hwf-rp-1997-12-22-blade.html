HWF (Dec 1996) — Roleplay

HWF: Blade

22 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The Blade sits in the training room of one of his Chicago mansions. He's punching a punching bag, and 3 of his beautiful followers cheer him on. He stops, and looks at the camera, with his usual, confident smile} Blade: First of all, I'm gonna speak very quickly about Natural Born Crybaby. For some reason, you wanna say that you got a 3 count on me?? What are you thinking, you little punk?? Look in the record books, you won by disqualification. The way I remember it, I was giving you a savage beating through the entire match, then that TOY of yours decided to get involved. So my friend Dunatis came out, and helped me pound you senseless. Then, as I was rejoicing in my beating of you, I somehow forgot to take my belt out of the ring. So then you held onto it for about a week, until, of course, I had to give you yet another beating. By the way, you still owe me $50 for disinfecting that belt that you messed up. Another point, ON BEHALF OF THE HWF, THANKS FOR BEING THE MESSENGER FOR THAT ANTI-HWF PROPAGANDA. AND YOU DID IT FOR $3??? THAT SHOWS ME SOMETHING ABOUT YOUR LOYALTY, KID. Anyway, I guess I should forget about that wannabe street thugs, bitterness towards the greatest HWF wrestler of all time, and focus on the match at hand. Malibu Mikey Boy Donovan. . .my next victim. Only a few hours away. I can guarantee that win or lose, you are gonig to take a savage beating. Everyone knows that the only reason that the PACK is still alive is because I have let them. Everyone knows that the only reason that Malibu talks about being the best is because nobody has shown him otherwise. . . UNTIL NOW. Tonight, you are going to get a taste of what it means to be a champion. You take part in about 5 different wrestling organizations. As many offers as I have been made, I am 100% H W F. You know why?? Because as long as I am on top of the top wrestlnig federation in the world, that means that I AM THE MAN TO BEAT IN THE WRESTLING WORLD. I've said it time after time. . . I TALK BECAUSE I CAN. Enligoddetor, Destrukto, Jakk Nyphe, Cabal, Super Patriot, Pennywise. . .I retired those so-called fighters, plus countless others. Raven Michaels, my friend Prince Gravis, Obsessor, Nightshade, Irish Devil, NATURAL BORN KILLER. . .. . . Nobody has been able to shut me up. Now, it's Malibu's turn. Another victim on my endless list of opponents. Everyone likes to talk. Everyone likes to say that I am a paper champion. But one fact remains, when people step into the ring with me, I ALWAYS WALK OUT WITH THE BELT AROUND MY WASTE. So Malibu, you better get ready, because I have something you want. But it's gonna take the performance of your life to get it from me. And when I'm done with Malibu, NBK better watch out, because his career is just about over. He loves to make these segments that looks so long, while half of it is just rap lyrics and huge symbols. That characterizes Natural Born Killer as opposed to myself. While he cares more about makign himself look good in interviews, I care about making myself look good in the ring, and STYLISH in life. Because I've got the style and I've got the POWER. Besides, I've faced street thugs 20 times tougher than you or your sidekicks, during my childhood on the streets of Chicago. until next time, Enough Said. {Blade begins hitting the punching bag as the camera fades.}
