HWF (Dec 1996) — Roleplay

HWF: John Austin

22 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HWF Presents... The Greatest Champion in SCWF History... The Only Talent out of the WecW... He retired Ralph Austin... He retired Prototype... The next TV Champ... John Austin! John Austin: Yo, Gotham City defender. Last Monday night was not me in the ring. It was [name withheld]'s version of me. Well that man is no longer. I am much more detirmined now. Now more than ever. I am much more detirmined to do away with the Pack. The plague to the HWF, the group of men who do whatever their messiah, Malibu Mike Donovan tells them. You can do as Mike says, but from now on I will not do as Kris says. This is my world, my life, and my federation. Since Kris doesn't have the guts to fire Prototype and Ralph, I had to do extra work and beat them in the ring. They were not easy opponents to defeat and I was quite angered having to put my former friend Ralph out of the business. Yes, former friend, not anymore. But still when you work for a man for so long it still bothers you having to put him away. Kris made me do that, just like he made me walk out last week against Batman. Well there is no more script, no more rules, and no more predetirmined outcomes to the matches in the HWF. I will see to it that if I do lose to Batman, it will not be because Kris told me to go out there and lay down, but because Batman was the better man, and I highly doubt that to be the case. So Bruce Wayne, you better bring all your friends to help cause it is going to require more than your utitly belt to defeat the Television Champion. And trust me by tomorrow night, I will be the Television Champion. Cause I can be the Television Champion, and becuase I must be the Television Champion. Bad John thinks he is going to walk all over a cripple, well he is going to be walked all over by this cripple in just a few short hours. I am the next HWF Television Champion, and I am the man. I earned it and now I shall have it. And the next night, Batman gets the business as well. That's not the bottom line....it's the truth! John Austin... The next TV Champ... He retired Prototype... He retired Ralph Austin... The only talent out of the WecW... The greatest Champion in SCWF History! Presented by the HWF.
