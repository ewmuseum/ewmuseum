HWF: Duo Of Death

23 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
>> THE DUO OF DEATH ARE IN THEIR LOCKER ROOM. CONSPICUOUS BY HIS ABSENCE IS KEN ESTES, WHO IS NOT AT THE ARENA YET. << CHRIS: WELL TONIGHT'S THE NIGHT. IT WILL BE THE NIGHT WHEN WE GET TO SHOW THE WORLD EXACTLY WHAT KIND OF LOSERS THE ODDS ARE. IF.....NO, WHEN WE WIN TONIGHT IT WILL BE THE LAST STEPPING STONE TO A SHOT AT TAG TEAM GOLD. TONIGHT, WE ARE MORE FOCUSED ON WINNING THAN WE HAVE EVER BEEN. THIS TIME WE WILL NOT LET SOME STUPID WANNABE SATAN INTERFERE AND COST US THE MATCH. WE GOT OUR FRIEND KEN ESTES WATCHIN' OUR BACK. IF THE UNLIKELY OCCURANCE THAT WE ARE IN A LOSING PREDICAMENT COMES ABOUT. WE WILL LET IT PASS THROUGH. WE WILL NOT HAVE KEN INTERFERE IN THE MATCH TO SAVE US. IF YOU, BEING THE ODDS, PROVE TO US THAT YOU DON'T SUCK. IF YOU PULL OUT A WIN THEN THAT'S WHAT HAPPENS. THERE'S NO STOPPING IT. BUT, THERE IS A WAY OF PREVENTING IT. THAT'S THE KEY TO STAYING OUT OF THE LOSING SITUATION. WE CAN PREVENT THAT SITUATION BY SIMPLY FOCUSING ON THE ODDS. NO ONE ELSE. NOT BIG 'N' SMALLS, NOT BULK ROGAINE, NOT DEATH STIRKE. THE ONLY LIVING THINGS WE WILL BE FOCUSED ON IS YOU, ODDS. AIN'T THAT RIGHT JUSTIN? JUSTIN: HELL YEAH!!! WE ARE AT OUR BEST TONIGHT. WE HAVE BEEN DOING SOME SPECIAL TRAINING WITH OUR PRIVATE TRAINER, WHOSE NAME WE WILL NOT REVEAL. SO WE CAN KEEP HIM TO OURSELVES. WE DON'T WANT HIM TO SPREAD HIS WRESTLING EXPERTISE THROUGHOUT EVERYONE. LETS JUST SAY HIS ABILITY AS A WRESTLER IS "STUNNING". KNOWING THAT WE HAVE THIS CERTAIN PERSON AS OUR TRAINER, AND NO ONE ELSES, ALLOWS US TO REVEAL ALL OUR STRATEGIES TO HIM. WE WON'T HAVE TO WORRY ABOUT OUR STRATEGIES GETTING OUT. HE HELPS US PERFECT OUR HIGH IMPACT MOVES AND ALL OTHER STUFF. WHAT? OH C'MON!!!!! CREW PERSON: YOU GOTTA FINISH UP WERE OUTTA TIME. JUSTIN: YEAH WHATEVER. TURN OFF THE CAMERA. >> SCREEN ABRUPTLY GOES TO BLACK. <<
