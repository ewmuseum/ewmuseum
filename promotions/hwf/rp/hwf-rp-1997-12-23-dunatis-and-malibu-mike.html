HWF: Dunatis & Malibu Mike Donovan

23 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{ The Packs music plays over the loud speakers and out walk Malibu Mike Donavan and behind him is Dunatis they enter the ring and Dunatis grabs the mic.} Dunatis: Ladies and gentlemen the next Heavy weight champion of the world.......... Malibu Mike Donavan.} Malibu Mike: Thank you big buddy!! Now let me start off with a few words about the present champ the Blade!! Blade come winter warz I.............................................. ..................What the hell!! {As Malibu Mike is talking Dunatis yanks the mic out of Mike's hands.} Dunatis: Mike shut your hole for 2 minutes would ya!!! {The crowd erupts in cheers as Mike's face is showed on the view screen as Dunatis is faking a punch and Mike flinches.} Dunatis: I know I have been silent the last few weeks and I just want to say sorry to all my fans that I have been quiet!! I have had nothing to say though I mean I feel I let the whole HWF down when I lost my 5 on 1 match with the Pack!!! MMD: Uh Dunatis there were 4 other guys on your team against the Pack. Dunatis: Mike I told you to keep your trap shut!!! {Dunatis grabs Malibu and sits him on the top rope and pats him on the chest hard.} Dunatis: Now you sit up there and look pretty for all the hard working photographers and let me finish up. If you watched the tape of that match you would see that I was the only one there that night taking on the pack by myself. Now I have been given the option of facing a Pack memeber of my choice. {When Dunatis says this Mike hops off the apron and gets on his hands and knees and begs for it not to be him} Dunatis: Get up, and don't worry its not you......yet. No I choose my tag partner at the Winter Warz PPV Nightshade. You see Nightshade has been out here saying that I won't be at our match at the PPV. I signed the papers to be there and I am also forced to be there because of quote unquote "MASTER" Mike over there, so I will be there to face so-crates and that other guy. So Nightshade I will get to see what you got and see if your good enough to be my partner at Winter Warz. Now as for Main Event at Winter Warz. Alot of people have been asking me what will I do, will I give up my carreer for the blade, will I help Malibu win the title. All I can say is buy the PPV to find out. The only hint I will give you is that (Dunatis looks directly at Malibu Mike when talking) someone is in for a RUDE AWAKENING!!!!!! Here Mike Now finish up your interview (As Mike comes over to get the mic Dunatis says one last thing) Know what Mike I have heard enough of you over the last 19 days!!! {Dunatis grabs the mic and rips the cord out of it breaking it so Mike can't talk. He then puts his hands behind his head and shakes his hips and leaves the ring Mike looks at the mic and tries to talk in it with no luck}
