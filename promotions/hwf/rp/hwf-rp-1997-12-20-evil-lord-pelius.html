HWF: Evil Lord Pelius (Burning Eye Of Rage)

20 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A pillar of flame twists angrily from a black altar in the center of a clearing of oaks, red and raging. Its grisly light plays upon the figure of a powerfully-built man dressed in a crimson cloak, his face contorted in pure hatred. His burning eyes glow redder than the fire, a gaze that is so frighteningly terrible that your heart instantly races in fear. It is, of course, the long-silent Evil Lord Pelius. He raises his clenched right fist, clad in metal and spikes, and speaks in a voice that sounds familiar, yet demonically altered. ELP: "Death Strike -- you coward. This past interference shall prove to be your last. Tomorrow night you shall finally receive the punishment due to you at my hand . . . . If, of course, you should appear at all. "I have prepared for this confrontation since the destruction of Ba'lzamon almost two months previous to this moment. Few distractions kept me from expressing to you my intent to end the contraversy surrounding our feud since that time -- and you may rest assured that I have meditated with ANGER upon it since your schizm with the Knights of Might & Honor. So much time to reflect upon the humiliations that your have inflicted upon me, all of which are now made worse ten-fold by this last, dishonorable, and ultimately FATAL error that you have made against me. "What strength shall you find within yourself to defeat me tomorrow night, Death Strike? I sense that you still exist, although your will to victory has dimmed to a smoldering pile of ashes. FACE ME, WEAKLING!!! FACE ME AND RECEIVE THE DOOM WHICH IS YOUR JUST RECOMPENSE!!! "FACE ME AND MEET YOUR DESTRUCTION!!!" Those words echo throughout the unholy forest. ELP: "This is the end of our feud, Agent of Entropy. When I look down upon your broken mockery of a body in the center of the ring of battle, I shall prove to be the superior. With your defeat, I shall have vindicated myself to be the true power amongst mortal men AND over even Death itself. Even now I have equalized a score that has needed to be settled for longer than I have been upon this world. Do you care to see what foe I have bested? Watch, and be informed of your forthcoming fate." The clearing seems to shimmer and then dissipate into mist. When it reforms . . . . ** *** ** The warrior pushes back his Elven Cloak, revealing him to be wearing golden armor over silver mail. His face is almost exactly identical to that of the Evil Lord Pelius, save for his short, black hair and absense of a chin beard. He reaches toward his back and draws out a sword -- a shiny, razor-sharp three-bladed sword quite similar to the one that the Red Knight has made so famous. He wields it easily in his right hand . . . . and awaits the arrival of his foe. ELP ::background:: "Behold the defeat of my father, the Paladin -- Pelius of Clamorus -- at MY hand." From behind a tall elm tree, a form appears seemingly from nowhere. The man's face is hidden in the shadow of his hood. His arm pushes back the folds of his cloak, revealing black armor over red chain mail and a silver shoulder shield connected to a dragon brooch by an iron chain. In his hand is a sharp three-bladed sword quite similar to that of Pelius of Clamorus. He wields it as easily as he does with his left hand. With his right hand, he lowers his hood, displaying his distinctive red and black dragonhelm. They appear to be brothers more than like father and son . . . . The apparent age difference might be but a few years if that! The two look absolutely alike, save for hair color and length -- whereas Pelius of Clamorus is clean shaven and wears his black hair short, the other man has a chin goatee and long, fiery-red hair tied in a thick battle-braid. There is also something inhuman about the eyes of the newcomer -- an unnatural red glow that expresses a frightful anger and resentment. The man speaks in his characteristically deep, rich voice. Except for his slightly foreign accent -- and the demonically menacing undercurrent inherent in the anger of his voice -- he sounds almost exactly like Pelius of Clamorus. It is without question the Evil Lord Pelius. ELP: "We meet again at last, FATHER." Pelius: "Yes, my son. You have changed much since I last saw you." ELP: "You have noticed? It pleases me to know that now I look less like you than I did before. However, I have not come to speak to you of such trivialities . . . . "I HAVE COME TO BEST YOU!" Pelius ::grabbing the overlength hilt of his sword with both hands:: "I am prepared!" ELP ::echoing the maneuver:: "You are not!" Like the clash of two fierce, raging tigers, the men of might lunge toward each other, swords arcing through the cold morning air. The rising sun reflects off of the blades, making them appear to flash toward one another. An immense ::CLANG!!!:: echoes throughout the forest -- soon joined by a dozen others. The Red Knight's eyes glow yellow with power and rage as he swings his sword with elemental rage; in contrast, Pelius of Clamorus meets every blow with a precise strike of his own, negating attacks so vicious that if they meet their mark, he will meet his end in an instant. In fact, the onslaught is so violent that it is surprising that neither combatant's sword has been notched or broken! Power swing is met by power parry. Suddenly, Clamorus shifts from a defensive stance to an offensive one; in a moment of channeled determination, he begins to drive the Red Knight back toward the tree from whence he appeared. ELP, forced to defend himself, finds that his adversary has gained the upper hand. Then, in a fit of utter rage, the Evil Lord Pelius roars a heart-stopping warcry, his eyes seemingly afire. Possessed by his anger, he slices his three-bladed weapon toward the Paladin's head with a force far greater than he should normally have. The blades of Pelius of Clamorus meet those of his foe, but ELP's might proves to be the stronger. The Sword of the Red Knight flings back the Sword of Clamorus in spite of the resistance given by its wielder. As a result, the Paladin Exemplar Ultimus is hit in the side of the head with his own weapon with so much force that he is knocked flat to the ground. His helmet saved him from having his skull split, but he is stunned almost to the point of unconsciousness. The Red Knight of the Fire Dragon raises his sword high into the air, shouting his victory cry at the greying skies. ELP: "THUSLY DO THE SANCTIMONIOUS FALL!" ** *** ** With another misty change of surroundings you return to the scene of the pillar of fire and the Evil Lord Pelius. The utter rage that etched his countenance is now tempered by a bitter satisfaction. ELP: "Death Strike, you know that you are already beaten. Do not hide like a cur from my wrath, for you shall not be freed until I have defeated you for your treachery and slander. "Tomorrow night, Minion, this conflict shall end at your demise." The Red Knight's laughter is sinister and cold.
