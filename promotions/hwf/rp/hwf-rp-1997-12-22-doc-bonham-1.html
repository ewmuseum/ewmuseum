HWF: Doc Bonham (1)

22 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Screen Fades in on Doc Bonham standing against a wall in his posh trailer in Ft Lauderdale Florida. He has a beer in one hand and a smoke in the other) Doc: Well, can't say he was the first... chances are he won't be the last. But none the less Dark Knight becomes the latest HWF superstar to tuck his tail between his legs and run for the hills at the mere MENTION of Doc Bonham. Look Knight, I never had anything against you, it was nothing personal. It would have been like any other Doc Bonham match.. You show up, I beat the cr*p outta you.. and we both go home.. SIMPLE ENOUGH? Sure, sounds it. But you wanna instead go out, trash the fed... Trash the House that DOC BUILT, and then have the audasity to not even bother stepping in the ring with me? <Doc shakes his head, takes a haul of the cigarette, then stomps the cigarette out on the ground> Doc: So thats that.. I really had nothing else to say.. Theres other stuff about you Dark Knight that I could have said. I coulda insulted a blue streak about ya.. But I won't.. I don't have to. For you see, you've insulted yourself, yer name, and everything you stand for by turning down the contract Kris threw out there to wrestle me. So Dark Knight..I AINT MAD AT YA... A little disaspointed maybe, but mad? Nah. My Career is gonna carry on after this match, whether you decide to show up or not. Its pretty insignificant really. Just a match for me to get my head of steam and make a run for another strap.. nothing more nothing less. SO Dark Knight, Do what ya want buddy, show up if ya feel like it. And if ya don't.. Well best a' luck to ya once ya find yerself a rock to hide under. But until next time, this is the messiah of the mat.. The savior of the squared circle.. and the man who put Dark Warrior Knight.. OUT of the HWF... This is, Doc Bonham. WhiteTrash Productions 97' Doc Bonham/MIke MacLean The views expressed here are not opions but mere fact. Brought to you as only Doc Bonham could. And if you don't like them, then yer not cool.
