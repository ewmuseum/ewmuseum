HWF (Dec 1996) — Roleplay

HWF: Crazy Jacob

24 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~ ••••• ~~~ Crazy Jacob ~~~ ••••• ~~~ XxThe Undercarder..xX XxThe Showstarter...xX XxThe Uncomparable Job...OOPS!xX He is....CRAZY JACOB! ( The Scene is Crazy Jacob's mansion in Palm Beach, Florida and Crazy Jacob is about to step out the door for a match he has to wrestle in another fed, when the second Crazy Jacob steps out the door, he is swamped with Reporters. ) Reporter #1: Crazy Jacob, may the press get a quick work with you about your upcoming match with Natural Born Killer? Crazy Jacob: Yeah, but make it quick. I gotta defend my AoLcW X-Treme belt against some loser claiming to be God. Reporter #2: Thank you, Jacob...First off, do you think you will be able to overcome NBK even though you are the huge underdog? Crazy Jacob: What do you think? I have only wrestled so many matches here in HWF and considering I have won all of them except one which was a draw, yeah, I think I can win. Reporter #3: Ok, Then on to our next question. Jacob, what is this surprise you have been talking about? ( Crazy Jacob smiles as he cracks his knuckles ) Crazy Jacob: Ahh, yes the surprise. Well, I can't say much, but I can say that this surprise will shock the wrestling world AND especially NBK. ( Crazy Jacob bursts out laughing ) Reporter #4: Ok, One more question, Jacob...We heard you talk about that belt on your shoulder before...what exactly is it?? Crazy Jacob: For the last time, it is the AoLcW X-Treme title. This title is one that Chris Moss never did win..hhmmmm..I wonder why? NBK, You are one of the most overrated wrestlers I have ever seen. I sang your praises last time I spoke, but I have thought it over...Yeah, you have won your world titles. But really, who couldn't? If I got a title shot, I could, and I would. Ya see, NBK, I have something you don't. I have my youth. I am a talented newcomer. You are a dinosaur. Compared to me, that is. And for exactly that reason, I will beat you. And I will make it look easy, because it will be. NBK, I know you, and you are a cocky, self loving bum, You underestimate me because I haven't got one hard competitor here in HWF, so you don't believe me. Yes, you have beaten me before, in one of my first ever pro matches over in CCW. I didn't know anything. I'll admit that. But I have changed a whole hell of a lot since then, NBK. I have held 3 different titles in 2 different feds. So, with that off my chest, NBK, SEE YA AT THE MATCHES! Reporter #5: Ok, Thanks for your time, Jacob. ( Crazy Jacob hops in his mercedes and drives off. ) ~~~ ••••• ~~~ Crazy Jacob ~~~ ••••• ~~~ P.S. Everyone Have a Safe and Happy Holidays!!
