Natural Born Killer NBK ~*~Natural Born Killer is sitting on a beach. He's wearing a blue hat, a grey, black, and red DKNY t-shirt, a black denim jacket, blue denim shorts, and a gold Rolex watch. Toy is standing over him. NBK looks up at her and grins. NBK then looks at the waves in the ocean. NBK gets to his feet and looks at a cameraman that's behind them. He stares at the cameraman in confussion. He just laughs and begins to talk...~*~ Natural Born Killer: Well, well, well, Blade lost the title to Malibu Mike Donovan. First off, congratulations to Malibu Mike Donovan and I'll see you at Battle Royal Rumble for the HWF World Belt! Now, Blade. You keep on saying I'm a crybaby? That's laughable. You go all out saying: "Your segments are just your logo and one line of talking. You didn't pin me one... two... three. You're this, you're that, blah, blah, blah." Now you're saying: "I got stabbed in the back by Gravis!" Boo hoo. Page me when someone gives a fuck! If I were Gravis and had to choose between the Pack and you, I'd choose the Pack! After he consistantly got screwed out of the HWF World Title by you and you consistantly kissed his ass, who would blame him? Now you're coming out here and kissing Dunatis' ass. You're desperate for friends and desperate for someone to watch your back. I don't ask people to watch my back. People watch my back cause they opt to watch my back and because they know if they have my back, I'll have their back too! And in case you didn't notice, for the latter part of the match, I kept Dunatis from interfering on your behalf. Blade, I strongly suggest you take those laxatives cause sooner or later the bullshit kept up inside of you will explode! And you talk about me being loyal. Well Baby Blade, I've done more for the HWF than send segment after segment out! And what was sent out needed to be said and it was said! By the way, are you mad cause I got $3.00 and you didn't? That's 300 times your life savings! I have better things to do. STAY TRUE TO THE BLUE! Shout out to the pinoys and pinays out there! SLC clique, represent! And....
