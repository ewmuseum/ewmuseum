WrestleCade II Preview

17 April 1998

SLAMMIN ACTION RSW RING SIDE WRESTLING ringside.simplenet.com ITS HERE, GO NOW!! HeartBreak Productions Presents . . . . . Heartbreak Wrestling Federation WrestleCade II April 18 , 1998 - Aloha Stadum , Honolulu , Hawaii TRIPLE MAIN EVENT MAIN EVENT HWF World Heavyweight Championship Match Evil Lord Pelius VS Godly Ken Davison CHAMPION CHALLENGER Guest Ring Announcer : Jack Nicholson (As Good As It Gets , A Few Good Men , Best Actor ) Time Keeper : Helen Hunt ( As Good As It Gets , Mad About You , Best Actress ) - Nobody would of expected this match to headline the biggest extravanga in the world. Ken Davison and Lord Pelius have had legit heat before in another organization and that heat spilled into the Heartbreak Wrestling Federation but due to the two making enemies from all over the wrestling industry the heat between them died down. Now will be the time , the time to show who the better man is. Crystal will be with her man all the way working in a training facility in Boston , Massachucetts while Dark Athena will be working her man in the Dungeons of the Obsidien Castle. - MAIN EVENT I RESPECT YOU MATCH Natural Born Killer VS Blade Guest Ring Announcer : Sarah Michelle Gellar ( Buffy The Vampire Slayer , Scream 2 ) Time Keeper : Jennifer Aniston ( Friends , Picture Perfect , Object Of My Affection ) Guest Referee : Dennis Rodman Outside Referee : Shaq - Finally this match will happen and this time there must be a winner. This war broke out in December of ninety seven and it spilled throughout nineteen ninety eight. They have had battles before but never with decisive finishes. This match has gotten to be personal and both men have agreed this to be a I RESPECT YOU match. The loser must say I RESPECT YOU to the winner of this match with the microphone. - MAIN EVENT Grudge Match Bad Ass John VS Irish Devil Guest Ring Announcer : Matt Damon ( Good Will Hunting ) Time Keeper : Kate Winslet ( Titanic ) Outside Referee : Evander Holyfield - The feud with these two man has gotten to far and over the edge. Both former members of the now defunct or so we think PACK. The heat has heated up and the time to settle the score is WrestleCade. - ******************** HWF Intercontinental Championship Match Stan ' The Man ' Riley VS Ken Masters CHAMPION CHALLENGER Guest Ring Announcer : Tom Cruise ( Top Gun , Jerry Maguire , Mission Impossible ) Time Keeper : Jennifer Love Hewitt ( Party of Five , I Know What You Did Last Summer ) - Ken Masters and Stan Riley will go one on one to decide the HWF Intercontinental Belt which is currently held by Riley. Both men have tremendous respect for one another so this should be a great match. - HWF Television Championship Match Morpheus VS John Austin CHAMPION CHALLENGER - Probably two of the most underrated wrestlers will go one on one to decide on who should be the TV champion. - HWF World Tag Team Championship Match The New Outlaws VS The Disasters CHAMPIONS CHALLENGERS - One year ago on April 19 , 1997 the Disasters lost the tag team championships and never got a fair rematch for the belts. There destination is at WrestleCade and to defeat the New Outlaws. Could they regain their belts ? - HWF Cruiserweight Championship Match - Triple Threat Rules James ' Simply The Best ' Pillow VS Psycho VS Crazy Jacob CHAMPION CHALLENGER CHALLENGER - The two challengers have been robbed of title opportunities before and both men are going to make sure that they don't get robbed again. The champion is not ready to let go as he has been the longest reigning champion in the cruiserweight division. - Revenge Grudge Tables and Dueling Canes Match American Heartthrob VS Dunatis - In a match which should be labled a main event match and a match where a title should be on the line but these two do not care as a grudge between the two must be settled once and for all at the biggest extravanga of the year. - Raven's Rules Match ' HeartBreaker ' Raven Michaels VS ' King of Swing ' Stevie Michaels - Almost everyone knew including these two that this match was going to be made. Both members of Raven's Flock the two will get it on one on one. Stevie has suffered several injuries and has been embarrased by Raven several times. Stevie will set out to even the score. - Extreme Match The Extremeist VS Chris Ekstreem - In a special contest added for the event these two men will go at it in a EXTREME match meaning anything goes. The winner of the match is the one left standing after a ten count. - ******************** 15 Man Over The Top Rope Battle Royal Bobby Bradley Polish Devil Big Fig Madman Moonlight : Wylde Star ' Hardknock ' Ralph Juvelic Kamikazzee The ' Amish Giant ' Rufus Yoder Ken Estes Prince John Gravis Bob ' The Backbreaker ' Thomas Batman Shadow Obsessor Headhunter - 15 of the HWF's finest will be stepping in the ring all at one time for a special Battle Royal. The winner of this match will recieve $100 , 000 ( $10 Will Be Offered To The Winner ) and a shot at any championship he chooses at the next pay per view. - Also part of the big event . . . . . The most popular talk show host , Jerry Springer , will be at WrestleCade II for a special segment which has not been announced yet but you can bet your bottom dollar that Jerry will bring one heck of a show. The Backstreet Boys will open up the evening singing the U.S.A. National Anthem.
