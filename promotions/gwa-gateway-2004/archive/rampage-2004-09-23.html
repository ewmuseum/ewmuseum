TBirdSCIL — 23 September 2004 RPer name: [name withheld] Real Name: Takaji "Tak" Fuyimoto Ring Name: Rampage Birthplace: Osaka, Japan Resides In: San Francisco, CA Height: 5'11" Weight: 255 lbs Alignment: Face Personality: Business-like, dead serious almost all the time. He's not necessarily loved by the fans, but they respect him for his dedication and ability.
Physical Description: Relatively short, but very powerfully built, with close cropped jet black hair and brown eyes. His in-ring gear is a pair of red half tights with the word "Rampage" written in white down each leg, and a pair of red boots with white laces. On his way to the ring he wears a t-shirt, any t-shirt, but he tosses it aside before fighting.
Wrestling Style: He is what could be described as a power technician, combining high impact striking moves and slams/suplexes with amateur wrestling skill and a dizzying array of submissions. He tends to zero in on one or two of his opponents weaknesses and relentlessly attack.
Set-Up: Hara-Guiri Description: Enziguiri into a modified Fame-Ass-Er Finisher 1: Rising Sun Description: A figure four crossface (Garden variety Crippler Crossface, except he uses his legs to cross the face and grabs the near arm with both his hands and pulls at the shoulder)
Finisher 2: Death Before Dishonor Description: Sit-out inverted cradle piledriver
