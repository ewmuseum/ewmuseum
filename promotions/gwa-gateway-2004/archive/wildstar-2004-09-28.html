A gifted high school athlete with a background in martial arts, WildStar burst upon the wrestling scene in the early 1990's, joining the CSWA and soon became Hornet's protege. WildStar soon emerged on his own as one of the mat's greatest high-flyers
and technicians. He is credited with being one of the first gaijin wrestlers to use the Japanese-style of wrestling successfully in a North American ring, and along with his longtime tag partner Tsunami is known as one of the sports all-time great high-flyers. In recent years, however, WildStar has developed a more old-school style as the years of crashing down onto mats have taken their toll on him physically. Always the bridesmade, WildStar now seeks his ultimate goal-- a chance to be the leading man as a singles performer
in a wrestling promotion and a chance to show the world he is, indeed, the sports only FIVE
STAR WRESTLER.
