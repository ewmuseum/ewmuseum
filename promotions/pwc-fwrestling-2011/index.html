A tournament league run on the fwrestling.com hub in 2011 by one owner, in which handlers' characters, many of them veterans of other feds, played judged roleplay rounds: a five-week round robin, The Legends, won by SJH, and the sixteen-man single-elimination Prodigy Classic, won by Hornet at Madison Square Garden. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Circuit began on 17 February 2011 as one owner's plan for a fantasy league run like golf or tennis: four major tournaments and six minors a season, with points, a leaderboard and judged roleplays. Its first major, The Legends, was a five-week round robin among five characters, which Alex Austin led until the last week and SJH won in a tie-break final. In June the owner added a one-off sixteen-man Prodigy Classic for the old characters of the hobby's earliest online days.

The Classic drew Hornet, Michael Manson, Joey Melton, Anarky and Cameron Cruise, and its write-ups, from Boston, Chicago, Columbus and Madison Square Garden, were the league's high point: Doc Silver arrived at the final to claim that he owned PWC, and Hornet tapped Joey Melton out. The last post held is a handler's thanks of 6 September 2011. The other majors and the minors never appear.
