P* Classic Semis

The Great Eye — 19 July 2011 (FADEIN: Reggie Schrader sitting in the PWC studios.) SCHRADER: The Semi-finals of the Prodigy Classic could not be any bigger as the opening match will feature two absolute legends of the sport as Hornet and Michael Manson meet finally to see who is the better man, with a spot in the finals for the winner, the stakes could not be higher.
And the other semi-final will feature Impulse, one of the break out stars in wrestling looking to begin building his legacy, and he will do so fighting another huge name from the past as Joey Melton looks to get to the finals and show everyone he can still hang with anyone in the industry.
The Nationwide Arena in Columbus Ohio will be jumping as we find out who the finalists of this epic tournament will be. Good luck to all involved and we hope you'll all tune in Tuesday to see who won these battles.
