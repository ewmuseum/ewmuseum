The Great Eye — 11 April 2011 (FADEIN: Reggie Schrader sitting in the PWC studios, ready to give us the latest news from The Circuit.)
SCHRADER: Fans, everything came down to the big showdown between Jonathan Marx and Alex Austin. Shawn Hart was the world's biggest Jonathan Marx fan and his faith was rewarded, Austin's apparent coronation was derailed as Marx, relishing the role of spoiler, kept on the pressure the way through and in the end with Austin desperately going for chokes, Marx countered into an inside cradle and got the pin and the win.
WINNER OF THE MATCH: Jonathan Marx (Small Package > Pin) 8:12 This leaves Shawn Hart and Alex Austin tied at 9 points each, this makes it a playoff for the title we'll see next week. It's going to be a barn burner as The Circuit crowns it's first major champion next week, till then take care everyone!
