The Legends Format and Schedule

1 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Great Eye — 1 March 2011 With 5 handlers I've decided that the best way to do this tournament is a round robin if there's a tie at the end, the two finalists will fight in a tiebreaker (If we have three people tied then a three way)
The matches will be set as twenty minute time limit bouts with points awarded as follows. Win by Pin/Submission- 3 points Win by DQ/Countout- 2 points Draw- 1 point Loss- 0 points Since having 33% of the tournament roster being Straw's characters would be a bit unfair, I'll ask Straw to pick one of his guys to be in the tournament.
Having said all that, here's the schedule for matches WEEK 1 SJH Vs Alex Austin Jonathan Marx Vs Michael Bastard Bye: El Cabron/Magnus Destructo WEEK 2 SJH VS Jonathan Marx Alex Austin Vs El Cabron/Magnus Destructo Bye: Michael Bastard WEEK 3 Michael Bastard Vs Alex Austin El Cabron/Magnus Destructo Vs SJH Bye: Jonathan Marx WEEK 4 Michael Bastard Vs SJH El Cabron/Magnus Destructo Vs Jonathan Marx BYE: Alex Austin WEEK 5 Michael Bastard Vs El Cabron/Magnus Destructo Alex Austin Vs Jonathan Marx BYE: SJH
