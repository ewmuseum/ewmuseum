Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World Alliance Wrestling Federation opened from a GeoCities address circa early 1997, its earliest surviving advertisement admitting “Only FANTASY wrestlers allowed!” A 7 April recruitment post, signed by its own president, opened that door slightly: “I might allow some of you to control one or two WWF/WCW/ECW superstars.” A fan's own cross-post later that month recalled a wrestler named El Phantasmo as its two-time Hardcore Champion, “the current hardcore champ” as of 27 April.

It ran a parallel German-language recruitment drive from the same address that July, and by 24 July its own president announced a rename: “I have changed the WAWF now to the USRW (United States Rampage Wrestling). It has been going strong since March,” pitching the renamed promotion against feds that claimed to be “the best on the net” or “the hottest around” with a plainer promise: “an e-fed that promises it's cards on time, and well written.”

Its richest surviving card, USRW Smashing September, ran 14 September 1997 from a sold-out Fleet Center in Boston and decided all five of its championships in one night: Mike Accolade over Top Gun for the Lightweight Title, Ace Diamond over Thunder Wolf for the Television Title, Axel Owens over Chang Khan for the United States Title, the Hounds of Hell over Juice and Jerome for the Tag Team Titles, and Maverick over Hunting Hamm Fox for the World Title. The card closed on a faction-war angle, debuting three new arrivals defecting from a rival fed, the RWA.

A further rename, into the World Internet Wrestling Council, is attested only from outside its own served record, and no closing notice survives.
