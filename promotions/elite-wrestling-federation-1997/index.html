The Elite Wrestling Federation, a TNM-run e-fed founded 1 December 1997 under two co-presidents, growing its roster to 35 wrestlers within a month while billing itself 'Not the EWF that posts 1000 times a day.' Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Elite Wrestling Federation announced itself on 1 December 1997, run jointly by two operators who said they had each run e-feds before and had to close them “due to computer problems,” now “guarented to not break again.” Run on the TNM simulator, it billed itself as original characters only — “no Shawn Michaels or Stone Cold Steve Austin” — though it allowed a wrestler to borrow a real name loosely, offering “Bret Michaels or something” as an example of what was fine.

By 17 December it had grown to roughly twenty-five wrestlers and was recruiting another fifteen, describing itself as mostly simmed but with roleplays weighing heavily on match outcomes, and dangling its own EWF World Title, to be decided at an event it called Seasons Beatings, as an incentive for anyone who joined in time.

Twelve days later it reported thirty-five wrestlers and drew a line against a same-named rival it didn't identify further, insisting it wasn't “the EWF that posts 1000 times a day,” didn't “rip off other feds' ideas,” and put more weight on “the wrestling element of the page than the graphic element.”

No card, roster, or result survives. Whether Seasons Beatings was ever held, or who if anyone held the EWF World Title it promised, isn't in the surviving record.
