[WAR!] War!Zone

WAR! Raw New York Proudly Presents WAR!ZONE Live from the Manhattan War!Dome 12 June 2002 War! Returned to the Manhattan War!Dome for some great action which was warmed up by two matches featuring Nick Cash pinning Ruben Clay, and Hammerstein scoring a victory over Rusty James. But they weren&#8217;t the matches people had come to see. NUMBER ONE CONTENDERS BATTLE JERRY GORDON Vs. TED VICKERS Vs. ADAM MITCHELL Vs. DESPAIR Morten Wyngarde made sure War!Zone started off with a big bang by scheduling what could be a main event anywhere as Jerry Gordon, Ted Vickers, Adam Mitchell and Despair were all placed into the ring in a one-fall, four-way battle for a shot at the War! Championship. The questions that had to be asked were though, would Despair even turn up, would Adam Mitchell be the competitor we know him to be, would Ted Vickers&#8217; brutal style prove effective against so many experienced competitors, or would Jerry Gordon continue his good form in Raw New York, winning the War! Title he has always coveted. First answer, Despair once again did not turn up. Fans are getting disgruntled with Despair being consistently billed but never turning up. Replacing him was Billy Cash, a long time favourite of the fans in Raw New York. This was a massive bonus to Jerry Gordon who would have otherwise had to face three people who wouldn&#8217;t have had a second thought about teaming up and putting him out of action. The four-way was as chaotic as any four-way can be, with no one able to really establish control, because the moment someone would deal with one person, they&#8217;d be taken out by another. There were many near falls though which made this match even more exciting. Jerry Gordon was the first to get a near fall, hitting Adam Mitchell with a fisherman suplex that would have got the three if Vickers hadn&#8217;t made the save. Later on, Ted Vickers had Jerry Gordon outside the ring and was about to slam him through a table, but Billy Cash ran off the ring apron and drop-kicked Vickers in the back of the head, saving Gordon. However, Cash had been sneaky and just when Gordon thought he could trust Cash, Billy got him with the Cashflow and made the cover, and he would have had the three, but the referee was dealing with Vickers choking the life out of Mitchell. Cash and Gordon began to brawl while Ted Vickers choked Mtichell until Mitchell went blue. The referee only just managed to get Ted Vickers off Mitchell before serious damage was caused. At that moment the ref turned around and saw Gordon hit the Spartan Smash on Billy Cash. Vickers charged Gordon, Gordon backdropped him over the top rope and then covered Cash for the 1-2-3. The fans went wild, and Jerry Gordon would get his shot at the War! Heavyweight title. After the match, Jerry Gordon and Billy Cash shook hands, which pleased the fans. Mitchell put in another performance lacking any effort and left the ring to chants of &#8216;You suck&#8217;. Ted Vickers showed promise and his big man brawling method will cause trouble for many of the stars of WAR! Result: Gordon by pinfall at 14:26 Billy Cash left the ring allowing Gordon to celebrate his victory and great performance. As he did though, &#8216;THE AMERICAN SUPREMACIST' REX AMERICANA emerged from the crowd. The 6&#8217;10 ultra-muscled man, dressed in a black and white stars and stripes tights and mask stared down at Gordon. Gordon stared back showing no fear. Tension built up and up. Americana, mic in hand, simply said. &#8216;Are you with us. Or against us.&#8217; Gordon didn&#8217;t reply. He began to walk around the ring. Gordon put out his hand and just as it looked like he was going to accept, he spin-kicked American in the gut and went for his Even-flow DDT, but Americana, so powerful, simply blocked it and rammed Gordon into the corner where he pounded on him with his fists before hitting his Final Solution powerbomb, leaving Gordon unconscious in the centre of the ring. Americana, an awesome looking figure in the ring then grabbed a chair and exposed Gordon&#8217;s throat, about to break Gordon&#8217;s windpipe. He lifted the chair and brought it down edge first but just in the nick of time Larry London came into the ring and pulled Gordon out of the way. American seemed prepared to end Gordon&#8217;s career. And it looks as though London could be next. IKE SAMPSON Vs. &#8216;LOVELORN&#8217; LARRY LONDON Larry London&#8217;s popularity shot through the roof by saving Jerry Gordon a few minutes previous, although London has no love for Rex Americana either. From one powerhouse to another though, London now had to compete against the incredible Ike Sampson. Ike defines barrel chested, but London, the West Virginia native, at 6&#8217;0 and 260lbs is no slim-jim either. However, the 6&#8217;6. 306lb Ike has competed in the best leagues in the world while Larry London is new to the sport, simply trying to prove that he is good enough for his girl who left him. This match didn&#8217;t break out of a brawl for several minutes, but when it did, the fight became mostly in the favour of Ike Sampson who slammed London with ease in a variety of ways, most devastatingly with a running powerslam which almost got the 3 count. But amazingly, London fought back. Ike missed a 2nd rope legdrop and seemed to be struggling with his leg thereafter. The two fought, but London fought with real heart and in the seventh minute he locked on a sleeperhold on Ike. Ike battled, Ike struggled, but with his leg in pain, and with London determined, Ike went down and out for the sleep and the loss. The fans were shocked, so was London. An amazing victory. Result: London at 7.28 MANHATTAN TITLE MATCH SAUL AKIVA [c] Vs. TALON VICKERS Talon Vickers has been getting in Saul Akiva&#8217;s face for weeks now, and even stole the Manhattan title claiming it as his own. Akiva won the title back in the 6man elimination battle last week, but now, finally, the two met head to head to settle their differences and clarify Akiva began his wrestling career looking for respect. His success with the Manhattan title has earnt him that. But not just that, he has won the supoort of the fans, and it&#8217;s something he has truly begun to appreciate. Talon cares not for such things though. Akiva was first into the ring and as soon as Talon started to come down the aisle, Akiva slid out of the ring and attacked Talon immediately. They brawled down the aisle and into the crowd Akiva was getting a lot of joy out of smashing Talon into the concrete, and before they even got into the ring, Akiva had bust Talon open. The support for Akiva was phenomenal. Akiva had scored an early victory in the mat &#8211; outfighting the hardcore highflyer, Talon Vickers. The match tussled backwards and forwards, with Akiva getting the better of it, with Talon&#8217;s agility and speed proving effective in dealing with the champ in response. Talon slowly began to edge control with eye-rakes and low-blows before carefully timing an irish whip, sending Akiva into the referee. Darius Smooth got squashed in the corner, and Talon hit Akiva with a half-nelson belly to back suplex. Then glee across Talon&#8217;s face as he left the ring only to return with&#8230;a Singapore Cane! Relentlessly he beat Akiva with it until blood was coming out of Akiva&#8217;s back. Then, with the referee recovering, Talon threw the cane out of the ring, went to the top rope and hit Akiva with the Death Row Drop &#8211; an off the top rope moonsault senton. He connected. Smooth counted. One. Two. But Akiva kicked out at the last moment. The fans went wild. It was a slow count from the dazed referee, but as Talon complained, it was soon he who was dazed as Akiva spun him round and hit him with the Dazed and Confused. Smooth counted. One. Two. Shoulder up! Akiva couldn&#8217;t believe it. He tried covering again, only for a two count, and soon the match was in full swing again, spending frequent spells on the outside with the two involved in some hardcore brawling, hitting each other with anything that wasn&#8217;t tied down. By the twentieth minute the two competitors were wrecked. This time Talon went up to the top rope for the Death Row Drop after slamming Akiva. But Akiva was up in time. He grabbed Talon, putting his head between his legs, spun around and powerbombed Talon into the mat. The ref counted. One. Two. Three. Crowd went wild. Akiva retained. Result: Akiva at 21.03 WAR! HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP STEEL CAGE MATCH MORTEN WYNGARDE [c] Vs. OMEGA A steel cage. Title on the line. A promise of no interference? Sounds too good to be true for Omega and his fans who really expected their hero to come out on top in this escape the cage match. But the last time Omega was in a cage he and Ike Sampson were mauled by Wyngarde&#8217;s Dogs of War. Certainly their seemed to be trepidation on Omega&#8217;s face looking at the cage &#8211; a rare sight indeed. The challenger had every right to be suspicious of the situation, especially since Morten Wyngarde was at ringside. Omega had a lot to do if he was to unseat the very unpopular two-month champion, &#8216;Nightshade&#8217; Richard Wyngarde. Richard Wyngarde started the match strongly, and continued for a few mniutes, using low blows to keep Omega under control, and Omega tasted more than just a few times. Soon his head was busted open. Blood flowed. Omega saw red. Omega zoned out. And in a cage there was nowhere for Richard Wyngarde to escape to, as much as he wanted to. Omega could not be hurt, no matter what Wyngarde did to him. Then Omega just destroyed Wyngarde with such incredible ferocity it was as if all that anger and frustration that has built up just exploded out of every pore of his body. The fans loved it. Omega began to climb. Omega climbed, and as he climbed, Richard Wyngarde lay still. But before he could reach the top he found his progress impeded by Morten Wyngarde who, from the outside, was holding his leg, and he did so long enough for Richard Wyngarde to climb up and back suplex Omega off the cage half-way up. It was Richard Wyngarde&#8217;s game again and is eight minute long assault resulted in the Nightshade for Omega who seemed far gone. Richard began his ascent. Omega was up quickly though. Bloodly flowing down his body, creating something like a map of the Mississippi in crimson. He pulls Richard back down and began introducing Richard to the steel, at one point bashing his head into the cage so many times and so fast that the fans lost count. Richard was on another planet and collapsed to the mat. The fans were going ballistic. Morten Wyngarde couldn&#8217;t believe it and demanded to be let into the cage. The referees wouldn&#8217;t allow it though. Omega put Richard Wyngarde over his shoulder and fireman carried Richard Wyngarde to the top. Once at the top, somehow he managed to balance on the shaking cage and jumped off, powerslamming Richard to the mat, using Richard as a cushion between himself and the mat. The mat caved in. T he boardings underneath the mat broke in half. It was three minutes until anyone stepped out. It was Omega. The fans were on their feet as he made his way to the door. The door opened for him, but Morten Wyngarde slammed it back in his face. Omega though saw this happening and kicked the door back lightning quick before it could hit his face. The door went smashing into Morten&#8217;s face. Omega stepped out of the door and claimed the WAR! Heavyweight champion to the delight of the crowd. The relief was incredible. Omega had finally done it. Ike Sampson was immediately there at ringside to help his good friend celebrate. The fans went home happy, the Wyngardes did not.
