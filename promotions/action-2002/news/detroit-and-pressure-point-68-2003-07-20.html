Detroit and Pressure Point 68!

20 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pressure Point 68 comes to us from the Joe Louis Arena in Detroit, MI, and what a show it was! Another match in the Action! Round Robin Tournament, another wrinkle in the developing feud between Max Danger and Coral Avalon, another match in Ramsey's and Duncan's Honor and Glory Series, and so much more! Check it out, now! link Tag Team Applications! ...too bad Joe Black's in charge of this. by "Legendary" Joe Black -- 07/16/03 In the interest of improving the Action! Wrestling Tag Division with new blood, you morons can consider this to be an official announcement for A!W. My remarks and personal feelings, like the fact that you're all morons, do not in any way reflect any opinions other than my own, and certainly not those of Action! Wrestling, even if what I say is true, which it is. Teams may feel free to apply at the below address. I'll be delighted to inform everyone that my responsibility regarding this is exclusively in the realm of rejection. You'll be notified of said rejection when you feel that certain sick feeling in your stomache when I dispose of applications that have been rejected. Non-rejected applications will be forwarded to the parties who make actual decision and who are not me. Applications not rejected by me may not necessarily go unrejected at a later date, but the possibility of acceptence is far greater than if they were rejected by me. If you'd like to submit and application for rejection, please put "application for rejection" in the subject line of your contact email. Postal applications will be rejected and burned. Phone Calls to me personally will be handled strictly according my telemarketing policy. Applications in person will be laughed at, at least by me. Really lame email applications will be be printed out and hung up in the Wrestling 101 locker area for our amusement. Should you be accepted, you'll be informed of the good news by someone who isn't me. That is all. Your Lord and Master Legendary Joe Black p.s. Rook Black sucks. Mentioning Rook Black anywhere in the email will trigger my autodelete finger. You have been warned. apply
