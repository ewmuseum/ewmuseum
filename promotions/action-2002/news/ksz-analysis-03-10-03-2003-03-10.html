ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 11.3.3 (taped 10.3.3.) The Big News: Joey Malone can't beat me. I cripple Airborn Tyler, make Jeff Garvin Bantam champion, and become the third man in Wrestling 101. Fuck you, James, waiting's for pussies. Title Changes: See above. Face/Heel Turns: Ruben Ross' mere presence (and timing in shutting up Daniel Phillips) essentially turned him, I suppose. Best Match: 1. So here's the deal: Joey shows up LATE, about 25 minutes before air time. He smells of whiskey. He has NOTHING. I script the entirety of the match on the fly in about 10 minutes, beg Reed for 10 minutes to let a WRESTLING MATCH go on first instead of SpongeFox, and then barely have enough time to change into gear (I was taping up as Joey came out!). Then, I drag him kicking and screaming to a **** on television. Wore my Bibby jersey for that extra-special super-cheap heat. That and the Kings > Spurs. So, the match breaks down into three parts. To begin, Joey keeps me on the mat and frustrates me, trying to wear me down with STFs after my anger leads me right into his arms. Second part sees me work over his bum left knee, dabbling in submissions. Third part was desperate times calling for desperate measures with psych behind it. Joey hits the full revolution tornado DDT (as previously mentioned in a CRD) and the CCR, but I kick out. I one-up him by using Anthason's Endurodrain. Aroma "mistimes" and I get off the Glass Ceiling Driverstrom for a 2 count, since every wrestler knwos how to kick out of his own finisher. 619/520 for 2, then a variant on the Eddy-Dean reversal series. Malone tries pinning me with the Magistral in a nod to Aroma. I hit him with a couple more nice moves and the System Crash, but only fight off the Super Malonestrom from the top and end up getting the super fireman's carry gutbuster. Time limit draw. I pulled an Aroma and tripped, leading to Joey getting the belt to the head. Phillips holds Allison hostage, leading to Avalon/Fletcher in a no DQ match later. F-bombs: 12. THAT is how you start a show. Worst Match: 3 was a squash, 5 & 6 were sneezes. Absurdity of the Show: Two hardcore matches consecutive, no belt involved. Moment of the Show: Me crippling Airborn Tyler's sorry ass. It's enough to make a hard rock smile. Breakdown/Highlights: 1) See above...Fox cut his retarded victory promo, and LLB's music has gotten even worse. He's staying around. Oh, joy. Cylear & Reed took out "LLB" & "Fox" outside only to find they'd been rooked by Action Team and were locked out of the building. Elements v. Batman & Robin will probably main...Jeff got ready for his Bantam Title shot...Le'Java will be working double duty...Zone cut a brooding Raven clone promo--he gets to smack Chambers around...Donell showed up...Bobby "I'm Still Alive" Minio & Steven "Me Too" Denton got in a fight...2) Weird match as the face-heel structure was reversed. Java was in brief trouble before hitting the Uncle Slam backbreaker to win; Donell went bat-shit afterwards. I slay me sometimes...Three-way between the Arcades and Le'Java on 53...Max will get his C Title shot on 53, too...Fiend made an innocuous debut...Chambers/Supreme is off--Garvin's still warming up...Joey gave Coral half-assed advice on hardcore matches--too bad he threw out the bottle of Jack he must've had that day...Ringer. "I'm new here, FEAR ME!" squash. Crossface finish...Fiend is the friend of the Mafia guys from last week--he faces Angel...Apparently Conrad's been getting with Max's mom. That won't be uncomfortable in the least. Also, old people sex````````...4) 7 Phillips F-bombs before "Cult Of Personality" fired up and out came Ruben Ross since Daniel ran him down last week. Apparently, he's still popular and is the big "mystery guest" Reed's been promising, though I hear there's a holdup in the contract signing. Ross began by beating Phillips up, BLACK WARRIORING (get it?) THAT SHIT DOWN, then whipped Phillips into the post. His overzealousness in the corner allowed Phillips a mule kick. Springboard single-arm DDT nicely done for a nearfall, and Phillips worked over Ross' left arm since he's a lefty. Nice sequence in which Ross went for a superkick (mirroring how he won the Asylum fight they'd had), Phillips stopped it and Ross fired off a combo of Owenzuigiri-->turnbuckle powerbomb-->Sliced Bread #2 (Shut 'Em Down) for a nearfall. Phillips countered the Spike into a Tombstone and hit the Perfect Halo (which neither man had been able to pull off in the fight) but Ross kicked out. Series of reversals led into a Phillips backslide with his feet on the ropes for the win. Ross superkicked him afterwards. Just as good as my match but I dragged a drunken Joey Malone to **** and I never put myself over in Best Match...5) Quick technical stuff before Fiend drilled her with an Anderson spinebuster, apparently putting her out cold, so they rushed to the finish and carted her off. Be interesting to see if gets depushed for that...Pretty Fly = FUHFORWID... 6) Total brawl led to a double DQ. Sneeze...Repeat earlier Ringer & Bill comments...7) Surprisingly, Java dominated early (because he fought earlier) and hit the Kick of Fear on Max, sending him out before sending him to the steps. He avoided the DANGER and bombed out a rana with authority. Bulldog slam on the ramp got a very hot nearfall. Max came back in-ring and went from the crossface to the STF but no dice. Modified STF finally finishes. Good stuff, and would be Best Match most weeks...See earlier comments about As & The Risk...Paulton got blindsided by Sharc and caught the chair beatdown after getting suckered in by his valet. This just in: Sharc RUUULES...8) I would've switched this match with maybe the Ross match (since he was the big surprise) to avoid back-to-back hardcore matches. And how trailer is it that there's two Hardcore matches and the champ's not even involved, let alone defending, in either? Sweet Pappy Johnson with an erection I hope The Ceiling lets Max take the belt next week and throws Seamen's worthless ass out on the street. Anyway, I hit you with this, you hit me with that. LIEBERMAN LIEBERMAN LIEBERMAN leads to RUN-IN RUN-IN RUN-IN, Fletcher cracks Avalon a few with the chair. Joey nullifies Daniel and Van Kleptonator (?) and sit-out Lionsault finish for Avalon. Chambers came to run interference but got the chair dropkicked back into his own face. Tyler DDT & he goes for the SSP, which is when I run out and fuck him up, leading him to drop right on his head and fuck up some vertebrae and now he can't wrestle ever again. Pity. Hammer Jammer. New champ. I join 101. And there was much rejoicing. Well, not by any of the rubes, but I ruined a man's life. Thus, I am supremely cool and > you...Batman & Robin got ready...10) Dew took out LLB early and Fox hit a pumphandle slam for 2. Lightning Bolt took out LLB & Dew, plancha took out Lightning. Dew put on the CFCW but Fox made the ropes. Lightning drew in LLB on the floor, leading to Montrose taking him out with a gigantic lariat. Dew took out Fox, LLB LIEBERMANed Lightning. Montrose chokeslammed LLB through one table, Fox somehow defied the laws of physics and planched him through the other. LLB and Fox recovered, hit their signatures, and Fox finished with a double Lionsault. Decent... Grade: A-. Quality, quality show, and I like to think I'm the main reason for it this time around. Proved I could hang with the alleged best, proved I had a meanstreak. THIS...will be the year of KSZ & 101. I hope you all have your bus passes. Bwahahahah. Muahahahaha. MUAHAHAAHHAHAHAHAHAH!
