KSZ Analysis 02/24/03

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 26.2.3 (taped 24.2.3.) The Big News: In the immortal words of Kurt Cobain, "Who needs action when you've got words?" Title Changes: None. And there was no Bantam Title match. Face/Heel Turns: None. Best Match: 8. Oh, just guess who. Max ran through Ramsey like a normal player away from a Clipper, culminating in an opposite-facing Walls Of Danger for the tapout. Referee got the Explodah, and Conrad got beat red by his own cane. Undoubtedly, a signal from Max to me saying he didn't like the fact Conrad never gave me my title rematch either. Worst Match: 7. Just a squash to reinforce the new EVIL Martin Yan. Absurdity of the Show: All the yapping that went on for eight matches, three of which licked balls and one of which was a double DQ (whoops). Moment of the Show: King Of Submissions, Max Danger. Breakdown/Highlights: LLB came out to start and got in his word before Reed & Cylear came out and mocked him. He walked away. Can I be the first to say this whole Fallen thing isn't working? I am the first? Even better...Russa's having a birthday party next week. What do you get for the man who's saddled with a foul-mouthed malcontent for a partner?...Martin Yan came out radiating evil. Bless Danny, 10 F-bombs in about a minute. If he ever works singles again, I can call him Sam Kinison. 10 more to run down the fWo tag teams. Wow, if he ever turned face again everybody could chant "FUCK!" like they do "WHAT?" for Austin. Not that he should work face, it just would amuse me to see a bunch of people yelling "FUCK!" at once. Cathartic AND fun. 18 more to run down tA, 21, Coral, and Joey, here's Joey. Chair, Fletcher. Fletcher, ground. He & Coral vs. Random Tag Team to earn the shot vs. WoC...Garvin, Chambers, & Julie showed up. Wrestling 101 was the shirt Garvin had on...Aroma whined and bitched about LLB not being a lawyezzzzzz...A contingent of Japanese guys showed up...Lactose & Russa talked about something...Lee was highly put out over losing the belts, Elements/Palmeiro tonight. Wait, they still work here?...Julius tried to smooth things out with Le'Java--I don't think it worked...Ran into Aroma. Debate leads to me vs. her maining Whatever. Once I win, she's my manager. And after one last unprovoked verbal attack on her part, I laid her out with a belt shot...Ramsey tithed at the altar of Max...1) A MATCH! I KNOW! I'm shocked too. I had Invalid beat until that chokeslam on the floor--then I felt like I'd been in a car accident. About the best I can do in a five minute match, and yes, I forgot to leap over the top Hennig-style...Airborn & Siobhnan hid...LLB packed...Yes, I am getting bored...LoD03 will use "Yummy Down On This" from the Bloodhound Gang...Julius got ready...Duel came out and made an open challenge, answered by some guy I've never seen, and they decided to fight for this title in an Evo match...2) You see how many people rip me off? Russa, Invalid, this guy, Simon Seaman wherever the hell he is. Ugh. Other Guy won with a straightjacket surfboard. Technically sound...DIE?! DIE?! I give you a golden opportunity on a platter and you want me to DIE?! All right, Tygress, come Friday night--YOU DIE...LoD03 will use a DDT Doomsday Device to finish. Not bad at all...LLB blew off J2...11 segments. Match. 4 segments. Match. 2 segments. Match. Getting better...2) Arcade countered out of nowhere with a pinning predicament. Match was a sneeze. Julius took a bat shot from Donell after shoving Java out the way...LoD will face the Stolen Glass Ceiling...Angel has a match next--uh, what the fuck is Avril Lavigne pizza? 4) Total squash. Ratings Spike and brainbuster finish. She got whipped, too...Garvin got ready...5) Stupid Garvin went for the Angle overhead and got SPLUT. Garvin actually hit the OrigiSlam and made him tap to the Jammer, so if I had to guess the Garvin/Danger program will continue in some form. And if it's not October & November what's up with the back-to-back squash?...They're continuing this Ringer/Bill shit? *sigh*...6) Flyer hit a slingshot SSP, and Dew Germaned him into the buckles. Flyer steals my tornado hangman's neckbreaker, and kicked out of the Lightningsault, so CHAIR. YOU. FACE...Kayden promo...Great American Tragedy promo--rumors are this is a guy from a recently shut-down big-name place. Looking into it...The teams prepared for the Dyad match...7) SUCK. Phillips put her in the Royal Treatment to end it about a minute in...The Japanese guys were signed to a contract...Joey officially left the Elements, which will help Lee's craziness...8) See above...LLB stared at the Risk but nothing came of it...Lord Daniel decreed the WoC will face the Arcades next week. Well, that's a smart move because Donell's nuts...Zone milled around outside--he, too, is new...The Dangers have left the building--that sound you hear is 7% of the watching viewers going CLICK...More Arcades dissension...Joey got driven nuts by Garvin...Stolen Glass Ceiling/Dairy Council never happened as WoC beat the fuck out of SGC...Fox cried as LLB left. Hey, maybe someone ELSE can have the spotlight now! Half a year without the belt and he's still the lead focus, holy fucking Danger... Grade: D. Good news: NOT the worst show ever. Bad news: Damn close. None of the four titles get defended, and way too much talk and not enough fight. Lightning & Donell are working the SAME EXACT GIMMICK, too. Anyway, tune in to Whatever. In the immortal words of Ike Turner or Daniel Phillips, "That stupid fucking bitch is going to get fucked the hell up."
