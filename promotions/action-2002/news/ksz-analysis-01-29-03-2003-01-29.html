KSZ Analysis 01/29/03

29 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 29.1.3 (taped 27.1.3.) The Big News: Jesus has finally betrayed me. Title Changes: *stares at you* Face/Heel Turns: --- Best Match: 10. Joey Malone. Jobbed. CLEAN. TO MAX DANGER. Well, between this, my match, and the Super Bowl, the Apocalypse is well nigh. Crowd was pretty dead early but these are the same people who helped make "Kangaroo Jack" #1 & "American Idol" popular so FUCK. THEM. Max suckered Joey into a Flair game of ¿Quien Es Más Macho? before poking him in the eye. Poor stupid new heel Joey. You don't ask Reed how he's doing, you don't piss into the wind, you don't ask Ken War to join the debate club and you don't try and out-heel Max. Malone spent the next five-ten minutes smacking Max around, and they traded Double Jeopardies. Joey rolled to the floor before Danger could finish the Trifecta Of Death, but posted his left knee fierce when he tried to hit the Muta Wizard. Danger worked the leg and hit the Wizard, Joey hit the really good kick, but Max kicked out. Ergo, it wasn't super. They played Quien Es again, but Max kicked out of the Magistral & the ADD. 10 Germans. Max scoffs at Joey's pussy-ass Benoit ass-kissing. He hit the Jazz/Steamboat facebuster. Max is bored. 360º Unprettier, but Max again kicks out. Finally, the sheep appreciate how awesome he is. Hope for 'em yet. Max evoided the Benoit, and they traded dragonplexes. Coral came out, and Joey turned into the ROOOOOOOOARING ELBOW~! And it WON THE MATCH! CLEAN! MASATO THAT SHIT DIZOWWWWWWWWWWWN! I now demand Fizzy do what's right for the company and let Max beat him for the title. Worst Match: 3, 5, 9. Pick one. Absurdity of the Show: I LOST! HELLO?! Moment of the Show: I know what it wasn't... Breakdown/Highlights: Fission came out to gloat and put forth an open challenge, answered by Neko Case. Ladder match for the strap...Team Martin Yan kept buttering up Coral Gables...The Bosses plotted...MAXITUDE~! stopped Granpa Simpson from retiring. That's got to be one hell of a glass ceiling to keep the Danger Man down like this...Minio & Nat X got in another fight...Donell said adios to Siobhan. *eats pack of Tic-Tacs*...1) Total comedy match. Pretty Fly's back together to add to the burgeoning tag ranks...Russa showed off a uKwtf? Title. I expect a royalty check soon, curtain jerker...The A-Star World Order, with Malone in tow came out. Elements (by which I mean Lee) started bragging before Team Martin Yan came out. There will be an eventually Dyad match. And now, this week's Phillips Report--F bombs: 4. One S-bomb for good measure. And Donell came out...Max was reading my report and bumped into Kayden...Jack Breaker paid Invalid for a shot...Montrose ANGRY! Montrose YELL AND SMASH LIKE SO!...2) Pretty much a squash for the big man, who won with the inverted brainbuster after a low blow...Lee Lightning rocked the suburbs just like Jon Bon Jovi did...3) Not even a minute before a double countout. Biters...Someone who sounded like Showcase wanted to rap to Case...Dirk took some wagers...4) Standard David/Goliath stuff. Focus point was Phillips using the arm, which was a nice change of pace from taking out at the knees. Single-arm DDT, and jujigatame were used. Fletcher and Lee cancelled each other out, leading to Phillips winning with a flying Diamond Dust. All that arm work for nothing. Best match so far, but that's not saying much...Max E. Coyote, Super Genius had the old "bucket of water on the door spot" accidentally reversed onto him by Paulton...5) You KNOW how I feel about Ken War matches! They're like Albert matches except I can't make my Chia Pet jokes. Seller won with some fat lady sitting on War. You took four minutes of my life and I want them baaack. Oh, I'd only waste them anyway...Airborn tried to cheer up Siobhan...The Bosses & Showcase planned...Next week is [C] Flyer/Seaman...Donell decided to solve his problems the old-fashioned way--with a bat dipped in glass shards... 6) Good stuff. Lee got in a couple of his high spots but Donell gave him the neckbreaker and the Lawler treatment for the win. What manly finishers. He piledrivered Lee through a table and went for the bat when Julius stopped him. Cue the dramatic dun dun DUNNNNNNNNNN...Max got ready to fuck up Joey. Heh, yeah right...Arcades argued, allowing the French Connection the opportunity to go LAPD...Disciple X On My Face was sad about his mask...7) Coral missed the Britney and posted his shoulder, so Tyler worked it over. Spectacular tope to the injured shoulder--psychology AND crazy lucha goodness! TIGER SUPLEX '85 INTO THE CROWD! History! ALL JAPAN THAT SHIT DOWN! Flapjack into the steps too but in-ring Coral got off the Britney Spear with the good left shoulder for a near fall. He came back with a standard tiger suplex but Tyler PROCURED THE CROSSFACE CHICKEN WING, making him tap. ***½ easy...Conrad confirmed he intentionally jobbed and got a late breakfast via belt waffle. GOOD. Not give me a title rematch you old bastard...Garvin got ready...8) Not one word. You hear me? NOT. ONE...Lactose answered Russa's challenge...9) Barely even a match, Cylear and Hawkins ran in. Probably some tag team match next week. # of clean Fission defenses remains at 0...10) See above...Was that Chris Fox? Ah, well. Grade: F-. Worst. Show. Ever.
