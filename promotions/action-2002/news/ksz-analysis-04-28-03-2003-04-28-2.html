KSZ Analysis 04/28/03

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 10.5.3 (taped 5.5.3.) The Big News: James Jack is even more hurt, PIW rules the day, and the BEST MATCH EVAH! Title Changes: -- Face/Heel Turns: -- Best Match: 8. How old school is this? When's the last time you saw guys fight for a minute over a headlock? Jeff escapes an early Crossface and hits the floor. Reverse chinlock causes Max to bail and Jeff baseball slides him into the rail (and we got one of them OLD SCHOOL steel jobs, thus probably fucking up Max's back another couple notches). Max lashes out with a rising palm strike (!) and Wild TURNBUCKLE Bombs Jeff. Danger has a guillotine choke for about a minute but I don't throw it in and Jeff eventually gets free. Crowd's appreciative, surprsingly, but then again we're in Pittsburgh and not Philadelphia. Max keeps it up with a hammerlock Northern Lights into the corner but Jeff snaps off the ROARING Dangerous III out of nowhere (heh). Both of them get chairs and standoff before opting to Conchairto SSI. Garvin gets the upperhand and pastes Max twice to draw color, and stretches him out in the ring with a backbreaker. Original series with a series of Tombstone reversals out of the Original Slam attempt but Jeff missess a move and gets sent to the floor by Ramsey. Ramsey attempt to get at Jeff and I lay his ass out with the markOUT on the floor. That'll teach you not to give me my title rematch, you old fuck. Garvin hits the spine on the pine but whiffs on the moonsault allowing Max to quebrada him in the back. ON the back, I should say. Double Jeopardy as the EMT's get rid of Granpa Simpson, and Max hits the Tombstone counter and SSP. Jeff gets him back with the LIEBERMAN III and whips out the ladder. HERE we go. Max uses the ladder as a weapon on Jeff for DOUBLE COLOR~! Max starts going off, hitting a springboard dropkick to the back where Jeff Rey-Rey sells with the slide out, then ¡TOPE! to the back and the Shining Wizard as a followup. YIKES. Jeff recovers enough to transition BLUE THUNDAH~! into the Boston crab. Max fights out of a straighjacket and pulls off the leaping swinging DDT. Jeff, second rope Russian legsweep. Max, Fujiwara. Jeff fights out but gets a cross-arm BRAINBUSTER into the cross armbreaker and the BEATINGS WILL CONTINUE UNTIL MORALE IMPROVES. Back to the Fujiwara but my sense of honor refuses me throwing in the towel. Jeff bombs into the Jammer but Max counters and gets the STF. Jeff bulldogs Max into the ladder and hits the Trifecta with a half-nelson instead of full dragon due to the arm work. I'm SO not worthy. Jeff uses the ladder as assisstance for a reverse surfboard and sentons Max on it. Max counters the Slam with a Crossface, superkicks Jeff on the apron and fucking RUNNING LYGER BOMBS Jeff to the floor. Dear. LORD. As both men wish to die, Max goes table-to-BACKUP announce table with the Original Slam. Sweet Lord Pappy Johnson with an erection, this is too much. Max now with the MURDERDEATHKILL bomb into the Jammer. Oh, I'm gonna throw it in. Then I'll hug some snakes. Yes! I will HUG and KISS some poisionous snakes! Jeff counters after two minutes that felt like 8 months into a Jammer of his own. Garvin Stomp, and he gets it back on, and then, and then....your Moment of the Show. If you have to ask what this gets, how'd you FIND this report? Worst Match: 3, 5. SQUASH. Absurdity of the Show: A first hour loaded with segments but two instances down the road with back-to-back quality matches. I have no idea how Sharc/KroW got over an Action Title defense and probably the best defense of the Bantam title ever. Moment of the Show: The whole Survivor Series '94 ending to the Bantam Title match. Everybody on the 'Net yelling should also know the Cornette 7-Year Statute and as such have free reign to blow me. Gives me great pleasure to play Owen, as some backstage have suggested I'm like. Breakdown/Highlights: Max came out with his mommy issues before we came out: Garvin/Danger for the Bantam in a towel match (me & Duncan in the corners)...Sanchis is...how you say? Ah, yes...ring announcing tonight...Conrad & the future Mrs. arrived fashionably late with the best seats...Reed came out and cut a bomb scare of a promo that went nowhere...Aroma snuck around backstage...Joey's got a half-sister...Fiend's associates got Paulton in a limo ride, go figure...Great Awakening & Mr. Magoo talked...Arcades got snippy talking strategy...Neil Marx offered help to Anthason...1) A MATCH! Some back and forth before an Accide run-in on everybody. Donell worked heel, Julius worked face, LoD worked comedy, and this clusterfuck worked my patience...KroW found out Cylear set up vs. Sharc tonight and slapped around Terry Jackson...2) Winner, of course, gets the prestigious Off The Side Of The Milk Carton Title. Minio started off hot and it took Anthason a while & the Hymn of the Fallen to turn the tides in his favor. Minio hit his sigs and had the match won but Marx came out and Anthason small packaged him for the win. More storyline, but the match was well enough for them; let's hope we get more Kent on TV with this Marx thing...Max met Joey's sister. THAT went well...JXS came out to say the Great Awakening had a small dick. No, really...Kayden no-sold a visit from the Mafia. That's so retarded it almost came all the way back around to cool...Max & Aroma exchanged pleasantries...Triple Threat for the Dyads is on at J2 with no DQ, they choose their opponents next week (LoD2k3 that is)...I read Aroma the riot act...3) Sneeze AND a squash. Then she burnt my belt. And now for the Ike Turnering--I have to call in a guy to do a favor for me next week... $eller's turning heel, I guess...Sharc cut a Ravenesque promo against KroW...I ran into Joey's sister; no wonder Max hates her guts...Julius & Rook Black are apparently friends...Jeff told me not to throw in the towel *eyes roll*...Fiend got Paulton on the outskirts of Pittsburgh...4) Julius did pretty well being old and on his second match but Rook mowed him down with the half-nelson suplex to end it...Max tried to get me to throw in the towel dun DUN DUNNNNNN...5) It's an Action!Man match, whaddya expect? Duncan finished with the DVD...Invalid's changed his name to HardCase! SMELL THE FEAR!...6) Very good match and they went back and forth instead of power v. speed and/or technical acumen. Another Rook run-in leading to a second rope Perfect Halo. I'd think it's safe to assume the LCWites will have their own thing at J2... 7) Brock dominated the match and put Fox through the announce table with the Hallaway slam before Invalid knocked him out--the Jerichoization of Fox continues...Jack's family got upset with Roland and knocked his bed over in the process...8) See above...9) Stiff & violent, both took huge bumps and hit a superplex all the way to the Spanish table. Natch, KroW kicked out of the inverted brainbuster and hit his big 2 for the win...Cylear got revenge with a chair...St. Daniel looked at the Malones from the shadows. Grade: B. Really a C+ but the Bantam Title match takes it above and beyond. I'd like to reiterate my "three segments before any match SUCKS" policy. Kinda confused as to why Julius had to work twice considering the roster depth. Anthason in 101 might save him--as it is, he'll probably be gone by the end of summer. Since I got my wish via Fox, you know what might be good for a larf is Kayden the Accidental Heel. Man, is J2 going to be LOOOOOOOONG... Show Results: 1) [DYAD] Arcade & Son dq. LoD2k3 2) Kent Anthason d. Bobby Minio 3) [TELEVISION] Sweet Aroma d. Dirk $eller 4) Rook Black d. Julius Arcade 5) Bradley Duncan d. Action! Man 6) Daniel Phillips d.
