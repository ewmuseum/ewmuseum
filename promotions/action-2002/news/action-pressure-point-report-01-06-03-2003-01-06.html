Action Pressure Point Report 01/06/03

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 8.1.3 (taped 6.1.3.) The Big News: Max Danger runs a fed like he's been there. I win (should've taken the Bantam Title with me for DOUBLE GOLD~! and ANGLED THAT SHIT DOWN). (Well, it's only big news because...actually, it's not news at all. I'm just a glory hog.) Debuts, debuts, debuts. Also, Siobhan...soooo hot...want to touch the hiney...off the market?! Come here, Donell. We'll treat you reeeeeal respectable-like... Title Changes: See Best Match Face/Heel Turns: Let's just put it this way: Joey Malone Fan Club spots are still available, in case there was any doubt. Best Match: Hey, surprise, Max Danger's in it! Hey, surprise, Max Danger doesn't win! Accidie d. Max Danger & Bradley Duncan (Non-Verbal Communication) to win the PIW World Tag Team Championships in their final match. At first I was upset I was seeing Max w/o N.E.R.M.F.D. but it was all cool since he and the deaf guy Raged Against the Machine with "Born Of A Broken Man". How goddamn awesome is Max Danger Chapter 47: he wrestled in his business gear. First time seeing the Frenchies & Duncan and did they ever rock the casbah here. Frenchie bust out the Midnight Express double flapjack and give Duncan the spike splash for 2. Max, being goddamn awesome, uses the WALLS OF DANGER~~~~~~~~ on one of them. Seeing as Duncan was obstenibly the only face, the crowd reactions were varied and bizarre, yet we weren't in Canada. Team France worked over Max's back as he played a great Ricky Morton despite the marks hating him. Frenchies Stage Dived Max for a near fall. FALSE HOPE TAG SPOT~~~~~~~ allows the increasingly-hated French Connection to double superplex him and steal the Guerreros del Infierno guillotine wheelbarrow/leapfrog straddle to the back spot. Shining Wizard allows the hot tag to Marlee Matlin, who busts out the Tiger Suplex '85 (RUAHH I TELL YOU)--wonder what Kayden thought of that. Turnbuckle bomb! Guillotine choke! Yakuza kick stiffing the U C K FAY out of that bastard KAWADA THAT SHIT DOWN, DEAF GUY! It's breaking down in Syracuse! CODE RED BY THE DEAF GUY! THE BEATINGS WILL CONTINUE UNTIL MORALE IMPROVES~~~~~~~~~~~! TILDE THAT THING TO HELL FOREVER! Reversed for the win. A total letdown via out-of-nowhere ending, but it's safe to say so far this is the Match of the Year. Danger completists, get thine asses to a Tivo. Worst Match: Well, there were a couple things that weren't matches that sucked enough to go here. Smut Factor: Negligible. Absurdity of the Show: Jobbing Cylear out, sort of, right after his impressive showing at Chess Game '02. I'd like What The FUCK for 2,000, Alex. Moment of the Show: "I'll 'WOO!' you!'--Donell Arcade, who proceeded to switch places with Lee Lightning, and in fact knife-edge chop him a bunch. Breakdown/Highlights: James Jack is back and the fans now despise him (Reed made the rehire at Chess Game '02)...Fission came out and cut his victory promo, but as he went to address Fox's challenge next month Cylear came out--both men have new themes. Cylear deemed himself #1 contender via the Pillmanizing of Fox's arm and winning their match but before that confrontation could go anywhere LLB showed up, too and ran him down. Cylear's response was to superkick Fission and leave untouched...Jeff Garvin bored some old man who was undoubtedly yearning for the icy embrace of death after that convo...Joey Malone, Public Enemy #1a, came out and wanted his rematch but before he could do anything, Daniel "Where's My Royalties Check You Fuck" Phillips came out and cut a FACEpromo. Welcome to the Bizarro Universe. Oh, wait, he called Malone a punchline for me, thus making everything equal. YAY DANIEL PHILLIPS. YAY, I TELL YOU! Anyhow, Malone kept ignoring him until he invoked the name of Keri and Malone toasted him with the Owen Hart Memorial Mic Shot, so there's Malone/Phillips tonight...MAX FUCKING DANGER~! arrived...The Endless Promo March continued with the Arcades & Elements. The family wanted their rematch but since Ramsey's run-in hurt Dew it was an impossiblity. Yankee's in as a sub...The Mothers are having a battle royal to see who'll face Steve...Team Raphael Palmeiro...HOLY SHIT IS THIS A MATCH?! A MATCH! I REMEMBER THESE THINGS FROM ESPN CLASSIC! *ahem*...Sweet Aroma d. Angelica Dawson. They came out together (rim job--er, shot--rim shot ) and worked a total face-face match, and Aroma countered the BACKSLIDE~! for a win. Woo...The Elements were milling around backstage when Granpa Simpson walked in, and got called a pedophile. Shocking, no actual violence broke out...Siobhan & Donell Arcade are now going out on a date. Hopefully nowhere in the South...PIW refugees Accidie arrived...Simon Seaman got a card from Tony Davis and belittled his grammer. GOOD...Lightning & Yankee d. The Arcades. Gee, who's going to win: the team in the ring already or the team with an entrance? Exactly. Julius played Ricky Morton and Dew GOOIFIED Donell with a gutwrench suplex onto the barrier to take him out in the middle. Julius slipped the former Union Bomb and dropped the Savage elbow but Lee pulled the ref out, and the Owen Hart Memorial Show continued as Dew hit Julius with the cast. Good, if formulaic... Danger found out Reed wasn't there and appointed himself in charge...Davis threatened to GET Seaman...Max welcomed Ramsey and put him in a TV Title match against yours truly--Max thinks I'm cool! SUCK IT, MARKS! AHAHAHAHAHAHA..*ahem*...Jeff Garvin d. Jordan X Shabazz via DQ. Crowd wasn't into it seeing as it was heel/heel and JXS was making his debut, but there was still good stuff there. Garvin got speared bringing in a chair and JXS hit a jumping brainbuster that had to hurt like like a OTHERMAYUCKFAY, but the ref recovered and thought JXS used the chair. Let the Nation of Domination jokes begin!...Simon wrote a threatening note to the Palmeiros...Kayden Paulton got the night off, then quickly named himself Max's assistant...Ramsey cut a "Don't try this at home and then turn around and sue us" promo with some Chess Game '02 Airborn footage...Davis & Seaman got into a brawl with Flyer coming out to calm the former...ME d. Conrad Ramsey. Hey, guess who's opening DIDN'T get fucked up this time? Let this be a lesson to the kids out there: bitch, complain and moan until you get what you want. The second the bell rang I was all over him like fat, ugly, and various drugs on Anna Nicole. Actually, all shit aside Conrad was a blast to work with (I know, I was surprised too) and very giving considering the amount of offense I got in--maybe because it was my first time under the big lights, I dunno. My Eddy, Jericho & Benoit tributes were all kicked out of. There's some joke there I'm missing. When I went for the double jump quebrada again, it missed and set up Ramsey. Let that be a message to the spot-repeaters out there. Quality Control after Ramsey hesistated and A WINNAH IS ME~! Surprised since QC is the lowest level of my name moves, but there you go. Match of the Night so far, but that's not saying much. Ramsey tried to lure me into the post-match handshake/beatdown after I celebrated with both titles but in the immortal words of Ashcroft on 9/12: I don't THINK so. Homey don't play that... Steve-O found out he ate the Pillsbury Doughboy. Reed, there's an Al Snow on line 1 yelling something about gimmick infringement...Neko Case came down to the Deftones in a nice nod to Fission having changed to a DT's song as well. Jesus Christ of wrestling? You're not the STEVE Christ of wrestling. Shouldn't you have matches and stuff? Hey, he dropped my name! You know who's cool is this Neko Case guy; oh, he's no Daniel Phillips but I see something. Anyway, since I made him realize his place, he called out Disciple X On My Face, Noah Hawkins. Reed will make him wear a mask, keep it on, and GIT. So he did. Way to be a face...Shabazz complained to Max and got his result "overturned"...The MANT Battle Royal was an setup to debut a new female character, Mrs. Shine, or as I heard a tech worker say, "Dear Lord, that thing's Rhyno with tits!". Steve-O took out Oprah and Shine earned her female Rhyno comparison by no-selling everything...Jeff Garvin unprovekedly attacked me and put me through the catering table, an act of cowardice by a COLOSSAL FUCKING LOSER who probably has a fun size Tootsie Roll for a penis...Max booked Aroma/Paulton for the main event next week. Forgive my sarcastic laugh. HA!...Set-up for the following...Accidie d. Max Danger & Bradley Duncan (Non-Verbal Communication) to win the PIW World Tag Team Championships in their final match. See above...The French Connection cut a heel promo after the match...Daniel Phillips d. Joey Malone via DQ. Phillips is your face, Malone is the heel, and yes, there's a guy in the background in black and white murmuring something about "wrestling isn't always real, and reality is always subject to change...in the Twilight Zone ." to people you can't see. Malone's so used to be a goody-good he broke at 4 instead of 5. Amateur. Joey uses Max's Double Jeopardy and drops the F-bomb for those of you slow-witted marks who don't realize he's the Big Bad. Release dragon suplex (!) connects but Malone offends the gods of wrestling by going for the GAYbrada afterwards so he gets the splat he deserves. Phillips comes back with a release tiger suplex and BLUE THUNDER so Malone gets a chair and himself DQed. Duncan Fletcher from tA made the save for his partner after Malone played a fifth-rate heel Austin with the chair. This'll probably be at the PPV next month so this was just an appetite whetter...The interracial Romeo & Juliet snuggled, causing some disconcert in this fair city of Verona...LLB chairshotted every referee in sight. Of course, next week they'll count fairly as if this never happened...Speaking of fifth-rate Austin chair beatdowns, LLB did the same to Cylear (bad enough on the same show but damn near on top of each other in a DQ and a no-match? YEEEEEEESH.) and teased the arm Pillmanizing before not going through with it. Foxorama through the table, WHORE WHORE WHORE WHORE WHORE WHORE (he did it twice) and Fission counts 3. Well, at least it's not a real match and I didn't pay $30 to see THAT abortion. Grade: B-. Six matches, two of which end in DQs, a few sort of matches, and about 583,173,278 backstage occurences. I realize it's the show after the PPV and people are hurt or fired or allegedly screwing Fission and getting caught in il flagrante delicto , but backstage segments don't really do it for me. What does it for me is VIOLENCE. Thank the lucky stars I had Ramsey to smack around and Max was on top of his game per usual, otherwise this would've been deeper into the D's than an inventory of Playmate chest sizes. Show Results: Sweet Aroma d. Angelica Dawson; Lightning & Yankee d. The Arcades (Yankee pinning Julius); Jeff Garvin d. Jordan X Shabazz via DQ (later "overturned"); ME d. Conrad Ramsey [Television]; Accidie d. Max Danger & Bradley Duncan (Non-Verbal Communication) to win the PIW World Tag Team Championships in their final match; Daniel Phillips d. Joey Malone via DQ. © Action Wrestling 2002-2004
