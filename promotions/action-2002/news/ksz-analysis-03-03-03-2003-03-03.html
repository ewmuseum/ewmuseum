KSZ Analysis 03/03/03

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 3.3.3 (taped 4.3.3.) The Big News: See below. Title Changes: Another Step Closer To Rollerball (™ Keith Olbermann): Action Champion...Christopher Fox. Face/Heel Turns: No. Best Match: 7. Fox ripped off my SHORYUKEN and BLACK WARRIORED THAT SHIT DOWN early. *shrug* I didn't tell him to. Foxorama through the Spanish table, Christian DDT on a monitor through the table to Fox. You can tell it's big when they sacrifice the tables on television. Anyway, Mexican standoff resulted. Savage Elbow got Cylear the first 2. LIEBERMAN and the frog splash, but Reed made the save. Lionsault got rid of Reed, but Fox turned right into the belt waffle. LLB, having gone an entire week without the spotlight and going into withdrawls, hit the LIEBERMAN on Cylear and got Reed out for good, Fox hit the Lionsault proper, new champ. G-rated Austinabration celebrated, apropo enough in Houston. Worst Match: 1, 4. Debut "get the newbie over" squashes. Absurdity of the Show: Sweet Aroma bitching about me not worrying another blown spot. That's funny, had you not blown that Lionsault I might not OWN YOUR SORRY ASS like the Raiders own the Broncos. Moment of the Show: I tell you what, I didn't like it, but the marks went batshit over the title change. Breakdown/Highlights: Cylear & Reed came out to brag about running off LLB and holding the silver when Fox came out and ran them down. He even used the f-word, and that was drawn up for the show. Nuclear heat here, esp. Arrested Development Boy...Garvins talked, and Jeff promised to reveal the new 101 member...Malone went to get the Dyad title shot but was informed the #1 contenders match scheduled for last week was back on...1) And now this NEW guy brings a belt! I damn well better be getting a raise after this month, Reed? You read me? Total squash, Supreme works more cruiser than Strong Style and finishes with the SSP...Max was wearing a Powderpuff Girls shirt--probably because the Mojo Jojo shirt was in the wash--and his mom came to visit...Russa waited for someone to show up for his birthday party...2) Show's tighter than last week, already a good sign. Paulton won the sneeze after being dominated, buuuuut....Sharc made his debut by laying him out. Gotta like a guy who uses Fear Factory...Aroma bitched and moaned about her duties--I was off getting a Code Red...Coincidentally enough, Dawson got named Bold's assisstant. Oh, for the glory days of holding the straps, eh, gals?...Stolen Glass Ceiling got ready to go over despite Jeff annoying them yet again...3) Hey, whaddya know, they went over. Lactose got the crap beat out of him while Russa handed out invitations early--why hasn't HE been fired? You'd think with all the newbies Reed'd be cutting some of the dead wood. Avalon played Ricky Morton and took a moonsault single-arm DDT from LtI. Hot tags allowed by a SUPER Klept-O-Matic (I kicked out of the standard issue). Double uranage, Avalon LIEBERMANED Russa, and a super urange/frog splash to finish. Good...Reed gave Pretty Fly a packing plant in Detroit for some reason...Dawson and Bold may have gotten kidnapped...Aroma tried to renege but found out why I'm Mr. Friday Night... 4) Another debut squash. Nate took a super TKO, Steve-O a Diamond Cutter and the Crossface...Chambers asked Supreme for a rematch...Accidie seemed to be coming apart due to Topex's wife's hospitalization...Duel demanded and got some matches against TBA...5) JXS quickly had rolling suplexes into the Perfectplex. He got most of the offense before falling prey to a flash pin; he passed out backstage...Ken War showed up for the party...Some guy who's mobbed up is going to debut, I guess...Me & Aroma settled our differences...Chris Lamborgh's coming--hell, who ISN'T at this point?...JXS railed against The Man. Y'know, that's why they fired D'Lo...Bill, Ringer, FUHFORWID...6) Donell's changed over to the Rage cover of "I'm Housin'". Arcade dissension discontinued, and WoC dominated for the most part with Donell tapping to the Treatment. One step up from squash...Daniel found some ex-wrestler out of the crowd and beat the shit out of him, including a shout-out to me followed by the markOUT. Two consecutive M's in Zimmerman on the check, Dan, and thanks much. He said Ja Rule sucks, but well, everyone knows that. Stolen Glass Ceiling came out, LIEBERMAN on Daniel...Mafia guy's in--hopefully it's The Don. I miss that guy...Garvin premiered a shampoo commercial and revealed the THIRD MAN shall be revealed next week instead. In addition, he & Airborn for the Bantam belt next week. I beat BOTH these fucks! Bah...Tyler Babylon came back from the side of the milk carton to wish the best to Russa...Fox got ready...7) See above. Grade: B+. Main event blew the roof off the mother, and the new guys made their impacts--now they have to not get lost in the shuffle as A!, in the new year, has suddenly become the happenin' place to be. Completely unrelated to my debut. Suuuuuuuuuuuuuuure it is. I don't like the fact they cut off Cylear at the knees but maybe he'll get it back next week. Boy, Joey really killed this belt dead, didn't he? Show Results: 1) Okiyo Supreme d. Chris Chambers 2) Kayden Paulton d. The Ringer 3) {Dyad Contendership} Joey Malone & Coral Avalon d. LoD2k3 (Avalon p. Lactose) 4) Zone d. Pretty Fly 5) Conrad Ramsey d. Jordan X Shabazz 6) [DYAD] Winds Of Change d. Arcade & Son (Phillips s. Donell) 7) [ACTION] Christopher Fox d.
