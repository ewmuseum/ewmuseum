Action Pressure Point Report 01/13/03

13 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACTION PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 14.1.3 (taped 13.1.3.) The Big News: Joey Malone's still a dick. Fission's still champ and Cylear's...still a dick. Title Changes: Nope. Face/Heel Turns: Not this time around. Best Match: Syphon Fission d. LLB. I never thought I'd hear LLB use a theme that made me long for the glory days of "Points Of Authority" but here you go. Fission worked over LLB's back early, playing subtle heel for some reason. It quickly went to the floor where LLB jumped off the announce table with PERRO AGUAYO~! onto a chair for a near fall. Out through the crowd, into the women's restroom (COMMODE, TONY, THE COMMODE!), through the garage, out onto the stage. How odd that these FCA matches usually end up on the stage or back in the ring, anyway. Testify but Fission powered out, Black & White but Fission kicked out. Down to ringside, where Fission exposed the floor and hit a running Awesome bomb onto it for his first solid near fall of the match. Back in, SyphHhon hit the high knee but Jack broke it up because his foot was on the ropes IT'S AN FCA MATCH, DAMMIT! THERE AREN'T ANY RULES! Oh, for the love of everything Portman...WHORE WHORE WHORE brought out Cylear, who superkicked both guys and teased a title change before letting Fission retain. Uh, Jack, you want to do something besides stand there as the biggest title in our federation gets decided? YEESH. Worst Match: Donell Arcade d. Ken War. Honestly, I watched this 5 times and I still have no idea what the FUCK that was. The Elements quickly followed with a beatdown and next week will see a falls count anywhere Dyad Titles rematch. Arcades lose and they get no more shots and Siobhan's back on the market. GO ELEMENTS!... Smut Factor: None. NO SIOBHAN! I'm going into withdrawls... Absurdity of the Show: People in the audience shocked at Malone attacking Avalon. That was so old-school Mid-South booking-by-numbers. Moment of the Show: The magically healed Airborn Tyler putting Conrad Ramsey in a wheelchair and pushing it off the stage. YOUTH~! Breakdown/Highlights: Winds came out to start the show. Fletcher's Malone hatred stems from Malone getting him blackballed. For a whole year. OOH. They challenge any team, and that brings out Malone. He promised to get a partner...LLB, taking a new tack this week, didn't beat the crap out of the refs...Accidie highlight package rolled...Cylear was stomping about backstage...ME d. Steve-O. Back-and-forth before I hit a string of moves culminating in the Online Onslaught (as a message to Garvin) for the near-squash win. 2-0. NEXT!...Donell Arcade got a T for tardy (gee, I wonder why?)...James Jack ran into LLB...Joey enlisted Coral Avalon as his partner. Poor stupid Coral...MAX FUCKING DANGER~! put me over. Jessica, don't be jealous of me. I'm just a really excellent writer. After seeing the chaos backstage they ran for the sanctity of their rental car...Steve-O scored some nickel & dime from a tech...Donell Arcade d. Ken War. See above...HA ha I heard the tech guy give the count before the next segment! Live TV, gotta love it. Anyway, Jack & LLB commisserated over the Niners loss. I hear they can get this Mariucci guy to do a cameo next week...Noah Hawkins d. Jordan X Shabazz. Pretty decent. JXS hit the Ice T for a nearfall and then said to The Man "Stop holding me down" and what have you. Hawkins countered a top-rope move with the 450 for the win. Too short. Neko Case came over and delivered a second-level Austin chair beatdown. He tried to take off Hawkins' mask but the zebras stopped him. Once that match gets signed, it can only end with Case hitting a Tombstone behind the good referee's back and the evil referee making the 3-count in .6 seconds...The Winds propositioned Coral, who said no. Malone lurked in the distance... Sweet Aroma d. Kayden Paulton. Aroma hit a great full revolution tornado DDT for a nearfall early before following it up with a nice tornillo. See, the problem for the faces right now is that all the champs are faces, too, so advancement's hard without going over to the Dark Side. Kayden busted out the BACKSLIDE~! before Aroma pinned him with the Magistral in a nice little bit of continuity. Again, good but a little short...Steve-O had a segment. I had a fast forward button...Cylear signed LLB/Fission for the title, falls count anywhere. I guess...LLB took the match because he wanted the belt. Where was Fission saying "Nah, I'd rather not."? Jack & LLB mended fences. I'm sure my pithy comments last week had nothing to do with this...Max congratulated Aroma on her win, and she noted she couldn't've done it without Max's trying the move. He congratulated Kayden for being such a cool dude and then BITCHSLAP. Muahahahahah. Owen mic spot and Max promised Paulton/Danger again. MAXITUDE~!, biotches!...The WoC d. Joey Malone & Coral Avalon. Joey made Coral start. Hint #1 your partner is going to turn on you, or at the least is a scumbag. Avalon/Phillips got all face/face technical Mexican standoff goodness before Avalon busted out a springboard Mysteriorana (hop on the guys shoulder, spin into a 'rana). Flecther did some Tajiri stuff and Malone tagged in to face him. Hint #2. Fletcher kicked Joey in the head and he tagged back out. #3. At this point if I was Coral Avalon I would've entered the ring and beat the glass ceiling out of Joey, but then I'd be a lower mid-card guy named Coral. So eh. Avalon took out Fletcher with the Dying Breath and Phillips got the WHORE! WHORE! WHORE! so Malone, of course, Malonestromed Avalon. Guy from UHF, what say you? GFVHF: STUPID! YOU'RE SO STUPID! Thanks. Pin followed as Joey tucked his pussy between his legs and ran. Meh...Jeff Garvin found out the Internet is an evil place...Ramsey came out for a promo and ran me down. I'd complain but he's due to die any minute now. He made an open challenge and Airborn Tyler wheeled down the ramp to accept. Of course, he wasn't hurt at all, so that's on next week. Jeez, Conrad, you could read your own site and find out he's not hurt. HOMEWORK, people! It's not just for fifth graders! Tyler beat the crap out of Ramsey, leading to the Moment of the Night. I just want to say I'm still #1 contender to that belt...Syphion Fission d. LLB. See above... Grade: C. Nothing much except standard issue angle advancement. There'll probably be a couple shows like that in the next few weeks with no end-of-the-month PPV this time (thank you, Paul Tagliabue). No Carnage Championship stuff this week--good, maybe they're getting rid of that. Hardcore belts are so '99. Good job of setting stuff up for next week, but don't feel bad if you missed it. Show Results: ME d. Steve-O [Television]; Donell Arcade d. Ken War; Noah Hawkins d. Jordan X Shabazz; Sweet Aroma d. Kayden Paulton; Winds of Change d. Coral Avalon (Fletcher pins Avalon); Syphon Fission d. LLB [Action!] © Action Wrestling 2002-2004
