KSZ Analysis 04/07/03

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 8.4.3 (taped 7.4.3.) The Big News: My first main event! And I even won! sorta. Title Changes: Actually none. Face/Heel Turns: None tonight. Best Match: 8. Ugh. Dead crowd for most thanks to the Moment of the Show. Don't you love how they sabotage me & Max with a dead crowd just in case the ratings suck, that way they can blame us and job us out to V.I.A.G.R.A.M.I.A. while LLB goes over half the roster again? Started off as a straight wrestling match. Shortly thereafter, I took a break. What was I thinking? Went for a pin before I remembered the new rules, and then a chopoff ensued. This reminds me: no one touch my chest. Double clothesline spot--nothing. Three Stooges--nothing. Double Roaring Elbows--well, that got something. More of that crazy wrestling with me fighting off a guillotine choke, Max sends me to the floor but whiffs on the ASAHI~! I fuck up my leg, but manage to pull off the Jericho combo before Max counters. Fight up the ramp as I catapult Max into the screen support and hit the Ricochet. Went up to FINALLY get the crowd into it, which meant I had to whiff the Benoit off the big screen. O. W. This reminds me: no one touch me. Double OW punch spot, chicken off the Tron! Well, we both managed to fuck each other up again. Backdropped on the ramp. OW. Literally, damn near everything on my body hurts at this point. GERMAN ON THE RAMP. I get the Eddy tribute on my legs into the half crab and it's almost a blessing at this point. Time for the claaaaaasic comeback: STONE COLD! QUALITY CONTROL! SLICED BREAD #2! EXPLODER '98! TRIPLE FREAKING JUMP QUEBRADA! Super DDT and the WALLS OF ZIMMERMAN! (Yes, I finally remembered about the subs. Pain hurt brain lots.) No-sold the superkick MASATO THAT SHIT DIZOWN and ROOOOOOOARING ELBOW DOUBLE MASATO THAT SHIT DIZOWN takes Max out to the floor. Shining Wizard and Max puts me in the Jeffshooter, so Jeff comes out, breaks it up, ORIGISLAM, one, two, three. I have won! I AM GOD OF THE SEA PEOPLE! Wait, no, I'm not, SUBMISSION! DUR! Bah feh fuckity fuck. I'm not in this for the battle--I'm in it for the WAR. And so, like a man, I restart the match. System Crash sets up the Crippled Airborn Tyler Crossface. Max escapes. Wild Bomb to the F4 Weekly. I escape. Down to the floor with the roll-thru counter leads me to flapjack Max onto the guardrail (a move I almost gave Chambers). Miss a chair shot on Max, take out Ramsey. Ah, well. Missed chair shot allows the Trifecta & Half-nelson suplex. That actually KOed me and Max practically spoonfed me the F1 so they could check on me. Aroma wastes Max with the chair, and I put him in VII. Max got out and put me in a dragon sleeper version of the Queen Angelito Stretch. Yeah, this was *****. But you knew that. Ah, I'm just kidding, more ****½. Worst Match: 1 or 2. Valet v. Paulton, sneeze, Sharc run-in with the Evenflow. He fucked up a superplex, knocking he and Paulton out vs. another in the "Get the new signee over with a squash series". Absurdity of the Show: Reed doing two things right. On the same show. Everyone into their bomb shelters! Moment of the Show: Fox using the GROSS POINTE BLANK SPOT (insert about 76 tildes)! Breakdown/Highlights: Started off with the Why, Fox, Why promo. Basically, almost getting chaired at Make It Or Break It took a whole month to set in--'cause he's SLOW, see? LLB came out to rebut and they did the security angle you've seen a thousand times before giving Fox the LIEBERMAN. By the way, since I know you're going to ask, it's OK to go forward with the rematch since there's an alignment change already...St. Daniel was off drinking--Irish?...LLB IS HERE AND HE'S REALLY MAD...SSI's doing camerawork...Conrad's taking off and watching the show so as to get next to Mrs. Danger...Cylear got ready to send KroW back from whence he came--Parts Unknown?...Dirk $eller--Eddie Murphy for a new generation...Someone refused to play along with JXS...Lactose stole the good half of the Dyad belts...Hi, Sharc! Thanks for the shout-out; just make sure you spell my name right on the check...KroW threatened to kill Cylear for real. Hard to believe someone would say "I'm about to commit murder!" on national television...Avalon used MY New Year's Eve comments to bash Jeff...Paulton cut his ™ promo with Sharc & Bait...I told Max not to underestimate me in a beautiful segment...The Arcades, Seller, and LtI had the first recorded clusterfuck of a segment...Some new guy threatened to GIT JXS... 1) A MATCH! I remember these from ESPN Classic and whatnot. See above...Coral just made Jeff's list of people he's gotta punk today...Russa & the Freedom Connection chatted briefly...Jeff came out and promised to defend the belt--PIW refugee #372,175 Sylo showed up so Angle-style Garvin threw Chambers in front of him first. 2 & 3) Chambers died, Jeff chaired Sylo for the win in 4 seconds, Jeff beat down Chris and got his sorry "I can't train with KSZ, he'll hurt me" rookie bitch ass out of our stable. This first hour's sort of sucking, ain't it?...Duel yelled at Misteriocito...Julius/$eller next week; the Donell/Freedoms saga continues...LLB IS HERE AND HE'S REALLY ANGRY...4) A great technical match, but the crowd didn't seem to be into much for whatever reason; new guy finishes with the FameAsser, which makes him the 3rd guy with that finisher here...Cylear put a bounty on KroW, who came up from under the ring and got his revenge before security chased him away; they's busy tonight... 5) Gee, regular tag team vs. feuding guys. Guess. Freedoms dust off the old Raven/Kanyon powerbomb neckbreaker to dust off...Coral, out for a month. Fletcher, gone. And who got to reveal this news? You guessed it, Frank Sta--Joey Malone. That's the problem, I had this thing set to McDonald...No Ringer/Scouts shit this week. Maybe I should turn in my atheist card...6) Face/face stuff. Brief Donell leg psych before CCR put his midcard ass away as Phillips was late with the run-in, $eller went after Donell but eventually the faces ruled the day...LLB IS HERE AND HE'S REALLY MAD...Russa got stuck with the cursed belt...Hendrix talked to some kid, sounding pretty heelish even though he's the face...Brock wanted Invalid but he got Fiend, the Anderson spinebuster of death and his hand smashed...Some cute redhead got hired--two good moves by Reed in a show? ZUH?...Fox beat the crap out of LLB backstage and see Best Moment. Happy happy joy joy happy happy joy joy...7) Surprisingly good stuff here. Heimdal actually carried the match (hey, when's the last time LLB let some rookie actually get halfway over on HIM? Don't think about too long, or blood'll shoot out of your nose--Lewis Black) before Fox Flair pinned him...SSI got fired from camera work...8) See above. Grade: B. Ugh, all that time without a match just offends me. But everything kept moving forward and the arly crap gave way to a pair of title matches that sort of rocked and rolled. I'll get Max someday. Experience won out tonight. Show Results: 1) Kayden Paulton dq. Bait 2) Sylo d. Chris Chambers 3) [BANTAM] Jeff Garvin d. Sylo 4) Rana Venenosa d. Duel 5) Accidie d. H.Q. Hendrix & Jonathon Scott (Topex pins Scott) 6) Joey Malone d. Donell Arcade 7) [ACTION] Christopher Fox d. Frank Heimdal 8) [CARNAGE] ME d. Max Danger; Max Danger s. Me © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
