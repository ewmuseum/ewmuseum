KSZ Analysis 02/03/03

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 4.2.3 (taped 3.2.3.) The Big News: The title match at BST was changed: Cylear/Fox/LLB/Fission. Stips are Last Man, blood, and submission. What do you mean there's a 21wrestling on line 1? Title Changes: WRONG! Face/Heel Turns: LLB, maybe? Best Match: 8. You'd think Joey would've come out with his boys instead of demanding seperate treatment, but that'd just make you a stupid mark. Joey pretended to start against Phillips--well, he's getting better. Fans were into Lightning/Fletch for what little bit there was of it early. Joey pulled down the ropes as Avalon ran them--I'm still surprised we don't have announced Malone/Avalon & Martin Yan/Elements matches named for the PPV. Ah, well, that's what happens when idiots are left in charge. Either that or they decide to bomb Iraq back to the Stone Age. Dew belly-to-bellied Avalon into the rail. That's his thing that he does--suplex small guys into the barrier. I wonder when the barrier's going to get sick of it and turn on him. Malone picked Avalon's scraps after Lightning got done--heeldom by osmosis? FALSE HOPE SPOT~! Can't believe they let Avalon kick out of a bridging dragon suplex--you get shot in Kyoto for pulling that mess. Avalon & Malone brawled to the back, where Malone knocked out Allison & talked shit to me. In the ring, the setup for the Dyad match I s'pose--Phillips hit the Spotlighter but it got busted up. Lightning went down to a Total Elimination leg lariat variation leaving Dew to clean house. YOU CAN'T POWERBOMB PHILLIPS but you can moonsault him, just make sure you've gotten rid of Fletch first. Perfect Halo, but Dew drew the ref (and Fletch to take him out with a Jerichoian Vulcan Snap) leaving Lighting the belt to waffle with for 3. Old school rules. Don't let the Russos of the world tell you any different. Worst Match: 1. Pure setup match, nothing even happened to get a one-count. Perhaps 5, a big gigantic squash. Absurdity of the Show: Danger getting arrested after the online hype of him getting his first title shot. Moment of the Show: It pretty much all sucked. Sorry, kids. Breakdown/Highlights: LLB ran down the roster and gave me props. Fox made his comeback but he's wearing the Bob Orton. Catchphrase bingo brought out Cylear which brought out Reed. See El News Grande. Fission/Danger (guess who's going to be the first sacrificial lamb?) & Fox/Garvin were setup, LLB took out Reed with the Whore Cubed and Fizzy lined Cylear to the floor, allowing Garvin the opening to get in some shots on Fox...Flyer & Invalid had a discussion that went nowhere. 1) See above. Elements hit their finishers & Joey the Foxsault before Martin Yan brought the chairs, setting up a six-man later on. Phillips Report: 8 Fs...Poor Jeff. Can't afford a car rental. I guess gay porn costs more than I thought...Ramsey & the Elements were milling about...MAXITUDE~! gave me my props. PINKY & THE BRAIN reference~! "I think so, Brain, but if the realms are so forgotten how come everyone's still talking about them?". Reason #72,329 why MAX 0WNS j00!...2) Paulton did a second rope frog splash followed by the F4 Weekly--why the hell would he do that? He got in a cobra clutch out of nowhere for the W--hey, you know what'd be a good idea is showing the brackets...Nick Arcade found his room trashed...Airborn was arriving late...Pretty Fly vs. Oprah & Shine & BST: Bathroom Break Match II...Nick Arcade came out with his new girlfriend the bat. Ran down Lightning, whatever, ran down Siobhan--well, that got me a little riled up--INSULT ME?! Dammit, I am upholding the Guerrero legacy! And I didn't even cheat to beat Christ OR Ramsey or Steve-O or the Joker, so guess what, Black Foley--do some homework and THEN talk shit. Sadly, the French Connection beat me down there, so I did a Doomsday Device. Big brawl between the Arcades, the Frenchies, me, and the Masturbator--shouldn't he be worried about Fox?...Conrad kidnapped Dew and put him in the Metamucil Lock...Reed had some sort of contract that kept LLB in service to him...Nat X put up some NOI stuff...Max got ready for the Man to hold him back some more--shit, are there going to be any more matches tonight?...3) Minio took a whiz on the posters, leading to KICK WHAM FAMEA$$ER for the win--ho-hum. He got the mic but some mysterious stranger promised to get him, tie him to a tree, and whip him. Or just the first thing...Conrad wants his title shot... 4) False finish off a frog splash and Invalid Aborts his way to victory. Good enough for governement work...Elements & Conrad bargained...Garvin & Julie argued...Julius has half a year left in him wrestling-wise...Again w/the Old vs. the Restless...Ringer/Scout Master. Woo...Kayden's easy-going nature foiled another Max plan...5) Squash. Tap. NEXT...Seaman/Flyer debate...Conrad/Montrose at some point, Elements laid out Granpa like a granola bar through his colon...6) Flyer worked over the ankle based on previous attacks, but Seaman cradled him out of nowhere. Another meh effort--hopefully I can get moved into a feud with him title for title so I can liquify that belt and take A! into the 21st century, however kicking and screaming it may be...Fox couldn't find LLB...Conrad promised revenge...7) Pretty ho-hum--JXS got his first win with the crossface...Max got arrested, thus Fission's # of clean defenses--survey SAYS--jack squat. Note to self: give show Z- for colossal bait & switch...Avalon & Martin Yan got set to do their thing...8) See above, but not that much competition tonight...Flying Frenchie is coming. Well, it's some guy using a cross & the letter D...9) Fox was prohibited from using the cast. Anyways, Fox taken out by Cylear taken out by Fizzy taken out by Garvin, who takes out Fox for good measure. 3. Yippee. LLB was just sitting there backstage, causing an en masse whuh-oh to close out. Grade: F. Top two titles not defended, Max gets shanked, a 123:1 ratio of promos to segs, no me...of course it's going to suck. A lot of the undercard got a lot of time tonight, but outside of 8 there was nothing that sat up and got my attention. Ah, well. One last show before the PPV--we better kick out the m'f'n jams is all I can say about THAT. Show Results: 1) Coral Avalon d. Jake Walker by DQ 2) Kayden Paulton d. Sweet Aroma {submission opener} 3) Dirk Seller d. Jordan X Shabazz 4) [IWO WORLD] Invalid d. Jack Breaker 5) The Ringer d. Scout Master Scott {submission opener} 6) [CARNAGE] Simon Seaman d. High Flyer 7) Jordan X. Shabazz d. Russa {submission opener} 8) Joey Malone & the Elements d. Coral Avalon & the Winds of Change (Lightning pins Phillips) 9) Jeff Garvin d.
