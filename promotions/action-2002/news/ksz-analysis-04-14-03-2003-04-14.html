KSZ Analysis 04/14/03

14 April 2003

ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 15.4.3 (taped 14.4.3.) The Big News: I AM GOD OF THE SEA PEOPLE! Title Changes: I tell you what WASN'T a title change 'cause I beat Joey Malone! Face/Heel Turns: None. Best Match: 1, 5, 7. Everyone says I'm unnecessarily hard sometimes. Translation: I'm not a pushover who toes the company line. So when I say this is the first time I can remember any federation having three ****+ matches on a free TV show, it's got some merit behind it. And two of them were just straight technical exhibitions. Worst Match: 6. Squash. We couldn't find somebody better to feed to Sylo than half of the Dyad Champs? At least it wasn't Russa. Absurdity of the Show: See above. Moment of the Show: Oh, guess. Breakdown/Highlights: 1) See above. Theme was that I was giving Joey a harder fight then the draw last month due to my increased aggressiveness (Tyler, Aroma, etc.). Joey made the standard issue comeback, but a series of reversals out of the Glass Ceiling Driver led me to hit TBL into the buckle followed by the markOUT for the "biggest upset in A! history". Heard the term MOTYC bandied about backstage and felt really superior since I booked this match too--really superior for about 40, 45 minutes...LLB hobbled in looking for Fox but found Reed, who booked LLB/Denton later...Conrad's engaged to Max's mother so he gets the sins of the father in the cage tonight...Jeremy was on the outskirts looking for a limo...The Garvins helped me celebrate the momentous victory...Fiend & Paulton got along in a said-the-spider-to-the-fly sort of way...Duel/Rana is on again tonight, and D wants the mask...Jeff will punk out Chris tonight...LLB Willis Reeded that shit down...2) LLB got in the spear and that's it. LLB flopped following a cross-corner whip and J2 called for the bell; obviously going for the Owen/Shawn November '96 vibe here. Fox came down and chaired J2, then delivered the Evil Austin Chair Beatdown © on LL...EMT's got rid of Jack & LLB...Chambers tried to get help from Avalon and revealed he doesn't know nothin'...Duel tricked Reed into signing the match he wanted...Sylo threatened Jeremy and got a match... 3) Went about as expected. Jeff offered Chris a chair, but he whiffed, Origislam and pay Grandma for the rubdown. Best part was the CHAIR YOU FACE...The redheaded chick came up to me and guess who's getting a shirt made now that he broke down The Ceiling? Who's house? RUN'S HOUSE! Erm, MY HOUSE!...4) Surprisingly awesome BRAWL from Paulton. Fiend hit the KVC backbreaker on Kayden but Sharc ran down and chaired Paulton, throwing the stench of gay all over a good match. Fiend seemed pissed Sharc got involved after...The Great Awakening choked out JXS... 5) Theme: Conrad controls Max early with back work, and keeps the advantage by submissions and occassional cheating. He also uses the cage first--Max release Northern Lights him into the corner. That spot's really catching on in the indys. Max hit the super gourdbuster(!) but got triangle choked while going for the powerbomb off the cage. Conrad sunset flip bombed him, but Bradley Duncan showed up to turn the tides as Max half-nelson suplexed him before the DANGEROUS MOTHERFUCKING I~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~! Damn Max usurp me...6) See above...St. Daniel promised to win the belt tonight...Limo arrived...Fiend was angry--maybe three-way dance at J2?...Coral had words with Jeff over how much Chris sucked the big one...TYKE'S joined A!. So, 21's dead, fWo's about to get shut down and lost everything with the "summer vacation" and overseas tour; we're #1! We're #1!... 7) Rana was revealed as being Rook Black, another LCW refugee. Can we get a history video package or something, because there are some people--by which I mean the vast majority of the audience--who never saw LCW. Really awesome technical match that Duel won with a modified Cattle Mutilation, probably would be ***** later in their careers with a hotter crowd, as is it's just really fucking awesome...8) Crowd didn't give a shit since it was heel/heel and of course since LLB took the stretcher ride Joey fucked it all up. Sorry, Daniel, I would've had your back but I was off to celebrate already. Phillips worked over the arm that was broken including a running armbreaker guillotine but went for the Pokesault and got kneed up. Watts bit it, Joey chaired Phillips. Decent match considering. Grade: A+. Best. Show. EVER. Get the tape from somebody. Oh, yeah. Bow before me! *shaking fist* Bowwwwww.... Show Results: 1) [TELEVISION] ME d. Joey Malone 2) Steven Denton rd. LLB 3) [BANTAM] Jeff Garvin d. Chris Chambers 4) Kayden Paulton dq. Fiend 5) {cage} Max Danger d. Conrad Ramsey 6) Sylo d. Lactose the Intolerant 7) Duel s. Rana Venenosa 8) [ACTION] Christopher Fox d. Daniel Phillips © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
