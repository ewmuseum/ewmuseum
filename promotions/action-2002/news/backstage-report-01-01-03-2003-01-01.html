Backstage Report 01/01/03

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FOR January 1st, 2003: With a Pay-Per-View out of the way, a number of injury reports should be noted throughout this column, because, other than that, it’s been a slow week backstage. Kicking it off, the condition of Airborn Tyler has circulated around the wrestling world as a serious condition, but I’d like to set the record straight and say that this was clearly something planned, and a work. Terry Jackson and company had agreed to have Airborn jump off the ladder one hour before ‘Players and Pawns’ actually aired... and Ben Tyler is resting at home with only a few bruises on his body. The in-ring work of Sean Cylear turned some heads. It was rumored as though Devin (Sean Cylear) couldn’t keep up with the regulars anymore (thus why he was not signed by any other Federation... even thought he wouldn’t have done so). However, after his Match against Christopher Fox, people’s opinions have brightened up just a little bit. "Ross is easy to work with." Tame said after the Match to reporters backstage. "But nothing should be taken away from the show that Cylear put on either." And after hearing the ‘Showcase’ on the microphone again, it was another event that turned for the better. "He’s still got it..." Tame went on. "And they just fed off of each other. It was great." Expect nothing to change in the LLB/ Cylear angle plans at hand, as the character in Sean Cylear is still a go and will be for some time. With that said, expect Ross (Christopher Fox) to be taking time off, as he’s been nagged with a toe injury. Writing him off in the Pay-Per-View Match with a broken arm was decided three days before the event. "I’ll take a short vacation." Ross said to reporters after the event as well. "But don’t expect it to be for long... the Chris Fox character is still very much into this feud, and will be in the storylines at the next Pay-Per-View... if not sooner." The Iron Man Match is being noted as one of the "show stealers"... which is nothing surprising at the thought of it. Jeff and Coral are two of the best physically fit wrestlers that Action Wrestling has to offer... and both of them have made a very strong impact again and again... however, this one might be the key. Are both set for "major pushes"? Rumors are going around backstage that they are... but even I, James Attwood, cannot dig up that type of information for you people yet. A few more injury reports to hand out... one in David Tyler (David Dew), who took a knock on the arm from a botched dragon screw... so he won’t be doing in ring work on this coming Pressure Point, and another in Trent Carson (Kayden Paulton), who after the Match against Max Danger, actually twisted his foot. "I landed on it funny during my Match with Marc." He said. "I’m not really sure when... but I think it’s best if I took the week off." That concludes this week in Action Wrestling. I’ll be back once again next week, with reports on how Keith and Daniel are fitting in backstage... as well as numerous other rumors going around this Fed. Until then, then. © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
