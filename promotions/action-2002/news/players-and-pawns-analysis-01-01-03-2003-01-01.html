Players And Pawns Analysis 01/01/03

1 January 2003

ACTION! PLAYERS & PAWNS ANALYSIS By: Keith Scott Zimmerman Email: [email removed] Date: 31.12.02 The Big News: I HAVE ARRIVED! Also, there's a new Action Champion and there are other new names in Action, people switched sides blah blah blah. Title Changes: Syphon Fission pinned Kent Anthason in the main event triple threat to win the Action Championship last held by Joey Malone in the lone title change of the night. Certain people predicted this days ago. Face/Heel Turns: Jonny Yankee's joined the A-Star Stable. Neko Case (to quote Mahatma Ghandi, ¿!) turned on Syphon Fission in the main. Best Match: Coral Avalon d. Jeff Garvin, 3-2. Fall one featured the Bret/Austin Survivor Series '96 spot before Garvin deliverd a low blow and front suplex to win it. Before fall two, there was a cute little scoreboard a la ABC/ESPN with GAR 1, AVA 0 and the time before going back away. Tres cool. Coral reversed Garvinator v3.0 into Ruben Ross' Chris Daniels ripoff for the second fall to even it up. Fall 3's ripoff of choice is Angle/Misterio at SummerSlam with Avalon armdragging out of the "Original" Slam. Announcers mis-IDed the Edge-O-Matic as the Dying Breath; Garvin kicked out and shortly after hit a Super Originangle Slam to go up 2-1. Avalon yanked down Garvin's pants in the fourth fall, and the ever-caring D.C. crowd chanted "Small Dick!" at the Original. Avalon did the Lionsault but Garvin kicked out, and down the road busted out an inverted dragon screw before transitioning to the Cloverleaf. Great stuff. Garvin chair shot evened it up at 2-2, so when Garvin went for the subsequent cover Coral got his foot on the ropes. Way to update a classic. Avalon came back with the Britney Spear (WHORE! WHORE! WHORE!) but couldn't capitalize, leading to a Garvin ½ crab and figure four weekly. Avalon hit the Diamond Dust (!) but Garvin got a foot on the ropes. Avalon hit the Curveball and the Golden Overblown Elbow to go up 3-2 with about 5 minutes left; Original Slam but Avalon got a foot on the ropes; Sharpshooter but Avalon doesn't give as time expires. I loves the professional wrestling. Worst Match: Boy Scouts over LoD 2k3. In a glorious masterstroke that probably set the industry back 40 years, they advertised this as a bathroom break match and then nobody cared. I Am In Shock. Russa's got to cut the dead weight and make a go of it here. I ran to the catering table and came back to see one of the Scouts pin Lactose. Boy, the Aroma/Minio matchup Sure Couldn't've Used These Minutes. Smut Factor: Negligible, hot chicks wearing tight things. Absurdity of the Show: Alex Brody's first PPV appearance is a loss and Danger jobs to Paulton, or the techs who didn't bring up the house lights. Choose one. Moment of the Show: Invalid mocking both PPV packages & Noah Hawkins with a one-sided setup video to his match with Hawkins to "My Heart Will Go On". Breakdown/Highlights: Reed Young got his mandatory face time to kick off the PPV, insinuating he was about to be in collusion with the refs and gaying it up...Package setting up the triple main event...Airborn Tyler arrived and was quickly greeted by Jon Bon Jovi & Richie Sambora (no, really), who sang some of their old songs as the youngster promised victory. What a great way to get heel heat, have a sucky band back you up...Noah Hawkins d. Invalid in a no-DQ match. Hawkins had an ugly X scar covering his face due to an Asylum match, but thankfully didn't come out to Uncle Cracker. Invalid, suitably disgusted, decided to New Jack the scar right off with a pizza grater. Invalid dominated the matchup, easing off a COCKY PIN~! after Retroactive Abortion, thus ensuing he'd lose. Sure enough, Hawkins got in a powerbomb out of nowhere and won, then collapsed shortly after. Nothing too offensive if blood doesn't make you squeamish...Sweet Aroma said some menacing things to Bobby & Angelica but before I could fall asleep backstage, MAX FUCKING DANGER~! came in and saved the buyrates...Elements promo in which I had to see Lightning in boxers. To quote the Sea Captain, that's going to replace the whale in my nightmares tonight. They ran down Siobhan and said this would be the last night she'd be around. Way to piss off The Demographic...Dawson tried to wish Aroma luck, but she blew her off...Sweet Aroma d. Bobby Minio. Aroma hit the (I can only assume) Spanish Harlem Jam right on the throat of Minio in a good near fall. Of Course Dawson Showed Up And In A Coincidence To End All Coincidences The Referee Bit The Dist Shortly After. If you couldn't tell from the capitalizations, that was sarcasm. Minio hit the At the Drive-In on a chair Dawson gave him, but Aroma still kicked out. Sufficently pissed, Minio cracked Dawson with the chair allowing Aroma the rollup victory. Minio beat her down, Dawson saves, and the Lesbian Alliance "reformed". Fans seemed to like it enough, but Minio needs to get his ass in gear especially with PIW shutting down... KVC, The Whole Fucked Up Show, lured Morg into a beatdown by his boy Brody, and made it an Alaskan chain match. Those of you wondering what makes an Alaskan chain different: it's really fucking cold...Boy Scouts over LoD 2k3. See above...The midget match quickly followed but before I could break the caps key with all the time I would've used in the booking decision to put those matches back to back Lactose had managed to lay them both out. And now, for something completely unrelated, one of my favorite segments from the Muppet Show--Stadler: This show is the cure for what ails me! Waldorf: What ails you? Stadler: INSOMNIA!...Elements promo...The Elements d. Arcade & Son. Note to the family: "Quality Control" > "What's Golden". Standard-issue plucky underdogs v. evil champions tag ensued, with it breaking down into an ECW outbreak of individual finishers before Airborn Tyler crashed the party by taking out the ref. 1. Slugfest interrupted by Jonny Yankee, who joined the Stable with a chair shot. 2. Steve-O tried to save the day, but it took Conrad "Grampa Simpson" Ramsey to save the day. 3 and 4. Everything broke down, allowing Siobhan a chair shot to the kid and the title retention was in the books with 5 run-ins. The match could've been nice but once Run-In Theatre started it got sloppier than Anna Nicole on Christmas. You mean to tell me with no less than three joke tag teams in the division booking five run-ins is a way to get the belts over? Yeesh...Thankfully, Yankee cut the promo of his fucking life explaining the turn, stating that America'd fallen into disrepair and no one had told him the truth after he'd sufffered the brain trauma. Elements chimed in...Kayden Paulton d. Max Danger. Jessica came back from the side of the milk carton to excort the most underpushed A! talent down to the ring. The original ref was abdicated for talking to Kayden, who let Max then pick a new referee, Who In A Giant Surprise Turned Out To Be Jessica. The Dangers did every Memphis two-bit trick in the book, but Jessica got "hurt" bringing back the original ref. Kayden stiffed the UCKFAY out of Max with a superkick in the best near fall of the night, then reversed the Dangerous I into a Repetition Of Angle off the second rope. Max quickly gained the advantage and hit the Roaring Elbow, but Paulton reversed the Magistral cradle for 3. Post-match, Max laid out some zebras and gave Paulton the Tiger Suplex '85 on a ladder before completing the Magistral. In a manly, hetero kind of way I want to have like 10,000 of Max's babies... Morg d. Alex Brody. Ump gave Morg a .9 Monica on the blowjob push scale before the match. I think the mic under the ring got turned up to 11 for this--then again they may've been stiffing each other. Total Rocky IV match as Morg whipped Brody with the chain at one juncture, causing front row people to flinch visibly. KVC came out as Morg hung Brody with the chain. Five Pill Frog Splash took out Brody allowing Morg the W. A whole lot of nothing wrapped in blood...Simon Seaman d. High Flyer and Tony Davis. Seamen took out the tag team early with a combo bulldog/Ace Crusher. Davis & Flyer came back with an second rope inverted DDT/guillotine legdrop combo to get themselves in the show's opening. Seaman busted out a ¡TOPE SUICIDA! HELL YES! BLACK WARRIOR THAT SHIT DOWN! Davis took the Snapshot to the floor but Simon didn't laugh like a maniac after hitting the move. Of course, the heels eventually fell into dissension, leading to the I Still Know What You Did Last Summer "gee, I meant to kill the good guy" spot via ringbell. Flyer got Judge Reinholded after that Low-Ki style for the retention. Team Raphael Palmeiro laid out the ref and Pillmanized Seaman afterwards. Nothing you haven't seen before done better....Ramsey & Tyler seperated, the latter putting a metal sheet around his leg. A brilliant move had he been a one-legged man...Steve-O & the Pillsbury Doughboy d. Oprah & Nathaniel. Now I shall pretend that 10 minutes isn't missing from my life...Jordan X Shabazz showed up, proclaiming KILL WHITEY--well, even though a race angle's never worked before, surely it'll work now...Garvin cut his textbook goofy promo before his match... Coral Avalon d. Jeff Garvin, 3-2. See above...At this point a certain handsome devil would've made the BEST DEBUT EVER had it not been for the idjits in the back, but what can you do except plot their gruesome demises? D.C. hated me, but they elected Marion Berry so the hamsters have been dead in the wheels for a good while now; ah, the joy of an arena full of morons booing you. It's quite breathtaking, really. I suggest you try it. *pinkie finger to mouth*... The Elements whooped it up and decided to let Siobhan hang around. The real winners: masturbating nerds. Hey, stop looking at me...Siobhan then bumped into Donell Arcade and insert mandatory "Jungle Fever" comment here...Conrad Ramsey d. Airborn Tyler. Tyler no-sold the modified anklelock and LIT UP DURING THE MATCH, then busted out a tornillo handless to the floor. REY. REY. THAT. SHIT. DOWWWWWWWWWWN! Tyler ran the barrier and legdropped Ramsey into a chair on the steps, then speared him into the steps. Ramsey got Airborn's "non-blocked" ankle in the anklelock and he tapped like an epileptic Jehovah's Witness, but it didn't count of course. Tyler blew an SSP or the ladder gave way and he hit the ground hard, leading to Ramsey retaining by referee decision. So, not only did the title not change hands but you took out one of the highest rising names in the company. Has anyone seen Reed Young and Vince Russo in the same place?...Invalid was seed talking to Vinny R..Reed... Sean Cylear d. Christopher Fox. Cylear was dominant early and it looks like he hasn't lost a step and gained a decent amount of muscle--a REAL amount, I should add. Sean immediately gets on my good side with the quebradora early before a kneedrop. Fox hit the 'Rama but SSC was in the ropes, then followed it up shortly after with the GAYbrada only for Cylear to kick out. Cylear posted Fox, drawing color, then gave him a piledriver on the floor. Boy, if this were held in Memphis the funeral for Fox would be Friday. Cylear jumped off the chess piece into a DDT. Both announcers used this key point to pronounce their love for Britney Spears, but after that Maxim shoot there's a crusty sock bent at a 45 degree angle that's declared my love for Christina Aguilera as long as I don't have to hear her. In fact, her mouth should be filled with my dick at all times so that happens. ANYWAY, that rambling allowed Fox to hit his frog splash off the top of the king piece in the entryway, popping the crowd beyond anything rational. A true holy shit moment--keep in mind Fox signed a new contract six days ago. Cylear kicked out of a series of legdrops and pulled the referee down onto him to avoid a standard frog splash in the ring; thus suitably distracted, Fox then took three superkicks. Great booking that made both guys look good...Cylear went after Fox but the Cell lowered. LLB came running down, stopped by Bold, then Invalid, then the referees (who were doing this to get Jack back). He went through them like fiber through an old man's colon, only for the Cell to shut on him. The sole obstacle being stopped, Cylear Pillmanized Fox's arm times two before LLB could get in there. So, with that having happened... LLB d. Reed Young. Young jumped LLB from behind with a chair to the back then followed up with the flying Shane-O-Mac shot. Cylear remained at ringside as Reed smacked LLB around and put him in his own Sharpshooter. LLB recovered, destroyed Cylear, then shoved Reed off the top through the casket. And a mighty roar went up from the crowd. Reed then took the WHORE! WHORE! WHORE! and the 10-count introduction to the buckles, thus causing him to pay a tithe to the technicolor church. Well, he did just get hit in the stomach at high speed. LLB made Bold come out with a new casket, then gooified HIS ass, then dumped Reed for the victory. Nothing special but well-received. Let the countdown to LLB/Cylear begin in earnest. They played dueling mics, leading to a match for the next Pressure Point and Reed's dumber than he looks and I dare even mock or dream if it actually even happens on a show instead of building this for two or four months and pimping it up to the highest rung of heaven on a PPV for 30 bucks. Neko Case (again, ¿!) pumped up Syphon Fission...Syphon Fission d. Kent Anthason & Joey Malone to win the Action! Championship. Match was no DQ which I don't remember being announced. Malone teamed with Anthason to take down Fission, drawing more heat than an oven door. He worked over Fission and handed him to Anthason, who hit the POST MOTHERFUCKING APOCALYPTIC MOTHERFUCKING CHILL on a chair to take him out almost in the first two minutes. Malone choked Anthason, DTHed him into a chair, gave him a Van Termaloneator (I guess) and put him in his own Endurodrain. Devol superkicked Joey to save. Kent put Joey in the Crossface--Fission saved. Fission beat the UCK-FAY out of Kent for a good two minutes and covered, Joey saved. Fission and Joey squared off, with Fission coming away with a Benoit string of snap suplexes. In the second-most business-exposing moment of the evening, Fission leaped off the BOTTOM rope so Malone could counter with the Exploder. YEESH. Malone gave Fission the John Fogerty but of course Anthason broke that up. Team Anthason beat down the other two guys as Kent gave Syphon his own Osaka Stunner, but Joey nutshotted Devol and he & Syphon stomped Kent. Fission quickly turned on Joey and Malonestromed him, but Malone as per Wrestling Rule #4 kicked out of his own finisher. So Fission Sure Shooted Anthason, but Devol saved again. This brought out Neko Case (who uses Radiohead's "Creep" as theme music, congratulations kid, you're offically gayer than a Harvey Fierstein movie festival on Haight & Asbury) to take out Devol and then turn on Fission. Wow. What A Brilliant Setup. Malone tried to get the cheap win, thus solidifying his heel turn, but if you guessed Kent broke it up, give yourself a cookie: the DNA Stardust Press soon followed. Kent decided he was the GAYME-UH and played Peter Gabriel with a sledgehammer, then gave Fission the Ender's Game in the fourth-most exposing occurence of the PPV. Third-most: the pin was broken up because Fission got his foot on the ropes. It's NO FREAKING DQ! Malone recovered to dump Fission and Malonestrom Anthason, but then MY FUCKING COUNTDOWN started up. I was very surprised, as I was loitering in the back drinking and watching Almost Famous for the umpeenth time. So needless to say, it wasn't me. Derik Shaw gave me some props before "Downfall" cued up and with a Vengeance an unidentified man flew through the air with a shooting star press/legdrop combination. Said unidentified man turned out to be Daniel Phillips, who after hitting that move on Malone and talking shit to him, hit an elevated Ace Crusher on Anthason. Fission, thus being the only guy not attacked by Phillips, hit a modified DVD across his back on Anthason to win the belt. Crowd loved it; I wondered where my fucking royalties check from Daniel Phillips Inc. was at. Grade: B. Despite some weird-ass booking decisions and incompetence, A! put on another good show with at least one solid MotY candidate. It's going to be interesting to see if Phillips can keep Malone from going back after his title, and what's going to happen to the other four titles as well. Hopefully they're not going straight to Case/Fission or at least Case will do something to justify his spot. Show Results: Noah Hawkins d. Invalid {no disqualification}; Sweet Aroma d. Bobby Minio; Boy Scouts over LoD 2k3 {bathroom break}; The Elements d. Arcade & Son [Dyad]; Kayden Paulton d. Max Danger; Morg d. Alex Brody {chain}; Simon Seaman d. High Flyer & Tony Davis [Carnage]; Steve-O & the Pillsbury Doughboy d. Oprah & Nathaniel; Coral Avalon d. Jeff Garvin {ironman}; Keith Scott Zimmerman d. Jeff Garvin {intellect}; Conrad Ramsey d. Airborn Tyler {chair} [Bantam]; Sean Cylear d. Christopher Fox; LLB d. Reed Young {Write Off}; Syphon Fission d. Kent Anthason & Joey Malone [winning Action! Title] © Action Wrestling 2002-2004
