KSZ Analysis 02/10/03

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 12.2.3 (taped 10.2.3.) The Big News: I MET MAX DANGER! Also, LLB's a pussy. And we have a PPV coming soon. And I MET MAX DANGER! Title Changes: 0. Face/Heel Turns: Nope. Best Match: 13. WoC busted out an double Owenzuigiri, impressing the crowd and even me to an extent. Topex used the Flatliner and a rolling Fit Finlay slam into a moonsault for a couple good near-falls. If the Frenchies hate America so much, why'd they ripoff the MX's double flapjack? Leaping Diamond Dust from Phillips lead to the hot tag portion, and he made a couple saves before Fletcher finished up with the double-legged somersault axe kick to finish. I Am In Shock. Worst Match: 8, 14. Absurdity of the Show: NO BRACKETS! Moment of the Show: Finally meeting Max Danger and it being all I thought it could be and more. "Though their choice of belt is HIGHLY illogicial, as the average Trekkie has no use for a medium-sized belt"~! Breakdown/Highlights: LLB blew off J2 on the way in...I met Max Danger, see above. Ah, if only Dawson had a mind like this and wasn't plowing SyphHhon...Coral/Joey's on for BST assuming he passes a physical. Let me put on my Carnac hat: he will...Once again Fox couldn't get chat time with LLB...1) MOVESET, baby! And I finally pulled off the System Crash. Proving Seaman wasn't the only one who could rip off Low-Ki I finished with the Dragon Clutch (dragon sleeper/camel clutch). It's kinda cool curtain-jerking because it gives me more time to bounce back here and do this...Newbie incoming vignette...Steve went to get busy, Nate followed a balloon...2) Max did Kidman's goofy-ass neckbreaker--WHY? WALLS OF DANGER~! were busted out, and DANGER won the match with his straightjacket crab. 3 easy...The Malone physical continued...3) Winner of the submission tournament gets shots at both the minors or at the big one only. Quick face/face that Fox won with the Bunnysault. G-rated Austin celebration followed...Arcades sat quietly waiting for their matches...SURPRISE! Joey passed the physical! Martin Yan/French Connection, winners get the Dyad shot @ the PPV. Well, who'll win that?... 4) Add this with the Carnage, IWO World and my belt and you've got a Triple Crown. Lactose looked better than usual but Russa won with his Fameasser...LLB blew off Fox...Max threatened bodily violence against Paulton at the PPV but Kayden exhibited his normal Polish no-sell...5) Donell worked heel and Coral hit the Malonestrom for the win. Afterwards Arcade went bat-shit with his new "girlfriend"...6) Good stuff. Airborn got the Jammer on Garvin after a second rope dragon screw, but Chris Chambers (who Jeff'd been talking to beforehand) joined A by knocking out Tyler, making the tap inevitable. And since I beat Jeff, I am once again the uncrowned Bantam champ...Aroma put forward an open challenge for next week...Fox & J2 went after LLB...8) Well, that was a joke...MANT thing--FUHFORWID...Bill/Ringer in the tournament @ the PPV...Cylear showed up fashionably late...9) Some new guy showed up and put away Seller with a Rings of Saturn/STF hybrid; said new guy is JXS' stalker... 10) Standard David/Goliath. Both men countered the other's finish, and Tyler's misfiring led to the MetamuciLock, which he also tapped to post-match. So he'll win at BST...Cylear took out J2 & Fox while LLB just sat there...11) What's up with all these same alignment matches tonight? Cylear was all sorts of crackhead tonight, with a stiff cradle piledriver and using an F4 attempt after two attacks to the head. Superkick finish. Fission layed out Cylear looking for the belt but Case dropped him...The Zone (?) is coming...The Garvins & Chambers celebrated...Dueling mic segment to further set up the Dyad contendership...Cylear attacked Fox & J2 again, then mocked LLB...12) JXS' new second (Jesse Jackson-type) slipped his charge a Bible, allowing Shabazz to Ice-T Minio for the win...The Stars whined...The Flying Frenchie promo played... 13) See above...some guy cut a brooding Raven clone promo with Columbine stuff...Ramsey promised victory this SUNDAY SUNDAY SUNDAY...14) LLB no-showed the match...MANT stuff...Ooh, it looks like Donell's not been listening to Grandmaster Flash...15) # of title defenses: 1. Against a guy old enough to worry about SS checks and a tag wrestler. What's next, Fizzy? Zybysko? Sammartino? The estate of the late, great Curt Hennig?...The heels looked at THE PAPER... Grade: B-. I feel generous. A LOT of stuff, almost too much, and all of it ranging from cop-outs to jokes to the mildly inoffensive stuff that cluttered the card tonight. Of course, if I met Max and this show is still barely above average, I suppose that tells you how it really is... Show Results: 1) ME d. Topex {submission opener} 2) Max Danger d. Airborn Tyler {submission opener, non-title} 8) Woodchuck Bill d. Action Man {submission opener} 9) Some Guy d. Dirk Seller {submission opener} 10) Conrad Ramsey d. Montrose 11) Sean Cylear d.
