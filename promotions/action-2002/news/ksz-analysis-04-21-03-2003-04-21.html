KSZ Analysis 04/21/03

21 April 2003

ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 26.4.3 (taped 21.4.3.) The Big News: Everyone who's still employed by A!W take a step forward--hup, not so fast, Syphon. Title Changes: No title matches, no changes. Face/Heel Turns: None. Best Match: 9. Started off contrasting, with Fission winning the wrestling and Daniel winning the wrestling. Then Daniel took it to the floor and became mayor of Chairtown before Fission surged to life. DTH on Phillips sent him into the ring bell he brought in so both men drew color (as it should be in such a match). Phillips shook off the Beastie Boys as Fission then shook off the leaping Diamond Dust. CRAZAY spot followed where Fission LIEBERMANed Daniel from table to table (Spanish, natch). But St. Daniel kicked out of that as well as the Osaka Cutter, leaving only the Final Word and/or the Death Plunge. Phillips ripped off the Vince-double-colored-bird-to-Austin-SVDM spot so Fission beat him about the face. Daniel came back to chair a shovel (probably THE shovel) in the face of Fission before chairing an arm and bad leg relentlessly. Chair shot to the skull took 2¾. Fission chokeslam through the American table, same. He even covered twice in a nice moment. Went for the Final Word and quickly got countered into the Treatment. Fission hung on a good minunte-90 seconds but Phillips somehow manuevered him into a Treatment/Tarantula hybrid and he gave it up. At least ****½. Worst Match: 8. Basically a squash to set up our angle for the night. Absurdity of the Show: A guy pulling a knife on TV. That sound you hear is Reed crossing his fingers as hard as he can hoping there aren't going to be lawsuits. That and LLB booking himself over the CAREER MATCH to end the show. Moment of the Show: The bell ringing at the end of 9. Breakdown/Highlights: St. Daniel started off the show by challenging Joey to a career match at J2 due to last week, but was interrupted by SyphHhon that eventually lead to a career match between the two of them to close out...Cylear sent Pretty Fly to do a man's job in dispatching kROw...James Jack showed up...Rook Black maneuvered his way offically onto the roster...The Great Awakening laid out JXS--he will surely be thugged and bugged upon later...Brock/Invalid tonight...Pretty Fly is HIGH! I'm Absolutely In Shock, Folks...JXS/Awakening next week...Donnie Daze finally debuted, only to get cutoff from newcomer/prodigy(?) Harley Cambria, so THAT'S on tonight...H.Q. Hendrix layed out Jonathon Scott...1) Match was about a minute, Brock won with his punchalong in the corner and the short-arm Angle overhead; quickly diffused the match by inferring Invalid had laid down for him...Fiend promised to GIT Sharc...The Ringer's back. In related news, a mighty roar went up from the crowd. *snicker*...2) One of Daze's managers turned on him and the kid D & Cd him on a chair for 3. Self-contained IWO angle to come, I presume. Match was perfectly serviceable...Fox got ready...LLB told Jack to get the fudge out of his life...LLB jumped Fox before he could even cut a promo...3) Babylon took punishment early with the Endurodrain but came back before some guy in a green cape chaired him. Cylear's got relatives? Match was OK, good heat... Pretty Fly got locked in a closet...$eller outsmarted Sanchis, playing security guard this week...Ringer and the Feud That Won't Die is set for JII...4) Possibly the best under five minute match I've seen--Black went for his mask and Scott caught Duel looking. Duel: Mask::Chavo::Pepe...5) Back-to-back MATCHES?! Must continue...shock straining heart...gah...anyways, Fiend came out real quick and KNIFED Sharc across the forehead. And here come the lawsuits...Fiend ran like hell...Bill/Ringer...Fox found James...LLB responded by bringing Almighty...LoD tried to get around the 30-day rule...6) Good stuff--of course Max ran the interference so JXS could win, then his mom & Jess stopped him from tearing Ramsey apart... Fox ran away...Brock jumped Invalid with the Power of the Punch...KroW called Cylear in one of those "magic phone" segments and had also laid out PF...Duel mocked Black being maskless...7) Russa joined in mid-match (short as it was), keeping the ECW spirit alive. Arcade superkicked Topex for the W...Fiend fought the law and he won...Hendrix/Sylo on for JII, hopefully to allow the latter's heel turn...8) Thanks to KroW not booking ahead with us, we had to cut the match up like a fat guy in for surgery due to what he did to the stoners. Kicked everybody's ass, pretended we were going to have $eller replace chmambers and beat him down too. EVIL~!...Jack was laid out by Fox...KroW laid out Reed and Cylear found him--the same thing in back to back segments? LAAAAAAAAAAAAME...LLB looked at Fox's Pikachu...9) See above..."Fox" got laid out with a sledgehammer but it was really Jack Fox had dressed up in his stead. Grade: B-. Career match saves the show and you can add it to the list of MOTYCs. JII's coming along nicely, but NO TITLE MATCHES= not good. That's why you have tertiary and secondary titles, so that when the "World" title isn't getting defended you can build up those other belts and put them in the spotlight for a tick. Some stupid, incomprehensible things happened on this show placement wise that should be fixed in the future. Someone has to step up and make a challenge to Garvin to help out the B Title. You can expect Fox/LLB & the Phillips/Malone to be some sort of double main event. Good job bolstering some of the undercard stuff tonight. Show Results: 1) Brock Shepherd d. Invalid 2) Harley Cambria d. Donnie Daze 3) Kent Anthason d. Tyler Babylon 4) Johnathon Scott d. Duel 5) Sharc nc. Kayden Paulton (Fiend intf.) 6) Jordan X. Shabazz d. Conrad Ramsey 7) {triple threat} Donell Arcade d. Topex & Russa 8) {triangle} Wrestling 101 d. Pretty Fly d. Action Team 9) {career} Daniel Phillips d. Syphon Fission © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
