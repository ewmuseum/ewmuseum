Phil Atken Analysis 01/14/04

14 January 2004

The "…" Recap For PP 89 Okay, so it’s a working title, screw you bloody smarks. Ladies and Gentlemen welcome to the first of many recaps from the one and only Sports Entertainment Savior, Phil Atken. Yes, that’s right, you great fans finally get to know what the smartest man in the history of wrestling thinks on the past weeks happenings of that great A! Show, Pressure Point. I’m not saying this little baby of mine is going to be weekly, because I can’t stand being around smarks too long (YOU SHOULD ALL WORSHIP ME DAMN IT!), but I’m here for this week, which is probably enough to make you jizz yourself. I’d like to personally think KSZ for worshipping technical wrestling, or as I like to call it arse groping but things are going to change around these here Pressure Point recaps. Pressure Point 89 was live from Hicksville and damn was that arena filled with too many she-males…oh and people who looked like they could eat a horse, literally…in one go. But that’s a story for a time when I don’t hate all your Benoit worshipping guts. 1) Pressure Point started off with a bang as Jesse Ramsey, who is nowhere compared to me on the scale of general coolness AND greatness as he paced INTENSELY! Oh my god, it was the most intense pacing in the history of Nitr…Pressure Point. THEN HE WASHED HIS FACE! HIS FACE! Oh my god, it was the greatest way to kick Pressure Point off EVER. I did offer my services for the first segment but oh no, Jesse really required the opening of the show. So he could walk around. Reed what the hell were you thinking? Do you think this intense face washing was going to bring the ratings in? For gods sake man, if you want ratings you use ME. Oh I forgot, he smiled at the mirror too, wasting more valuable TV show. Jesus Christ, he’s just lucky his bitch ass delivered later. 2) Oh my dear lord, we go from face washing (intense face washing at that) to PORN TALK. Reed for the love of god, stay away from the porn talk, I should know I had to deal with the problem when that moron Colin Gear tried to air streaming porn on air during thReat. You do not want to go down that route, learn from the mistake of others. Oh, that also means push me more, companies that don’t push me properly fail. You don’t want to be a failure do you? So yeah, this sets up a contest of asinine proportions for later on that night with Elijah Christ Stain taking on that guy with the shades. What’s his face, Kent Brockman, that’s it. 3) Then we went to a Comic Book Store… OH FOR THE LOVE OF ALL THAT IS PURE! Who the hell booked this crap? Are you WANTING the viewers to turn off? Is that it? Pressure Point is dying a slow painful death here. So far we’ve had porn talk, intense face washing and an autograph session of a guy not even on the freaking roster. Since I like Reed Young, I’m holding Jeremy Bold solely responsible for this mess. Yeah so apparently this un-signed moron found out that the true superstars, such as myself were in town tonight and went to stalk Reed Young. Or have his babies…or both. 4) Now here’s the point where everyone woke up from the boredom induced coma’s…or they’re long assed piss breaks, as I, the saviour of entertainment got the crowd up on there feet, chanting "Atken! Atken! Atken!". Well okay, so the crowd in the arena didn’t, but they seemed to enjoy the crap involving a porn magazine and a lazy wrestler but I know you wonderful smarks got off your chubby asses to do it. Oh yeah, being lazy as a gimmick really works…actually now I think about it, it does work. I should really give that a shot, paying me to not work my ass off. Of course the problem with that is, then I wouldn’t be the center of atten… be able to bring in the ratings. Now the live crowd treated me like crap and I honestly had huge news to tell them, that I would be recapping the show at A! Online. They would have went nutso, but noooooo they had to boo me. Well, look who had the last laugh. Me, when you all choked of your chicken wings. 5) More porn. Not even worth my time. 6) Some way of tying porn in to a wrestling match. I’ve seen photos of porn wrestling matches, porn wrestling matches are a bad, bad idea. Now I’m all for porn, but it just has no place in a wrestling ring. Unless of course, there are only females involved, then it can be the main event for all I care. Slap a "Porn Bowl 2004" on it, and you’ve got yourself a high buy rates PPV. 7) Our first match of the night! And it’s going to suck! Honest to god, what the hell was Reed Young thinking hiring this Christian guy? He isn’t one of those guys the fans hate because he’s all evil and such but rather because he bores the ever loving crap out of the crowd. Of course, I get booed because the fans just can’t appreciate a sports entertainment saviour, but hey, at least I get to make fun of these poverty problems. Where was I? Oh yes, Kent Brockman vs. Porn Guy McLazy, this match was really hard to concentrate on as the commentary feed was doubling over itself in a manner which made me run out of the room screaming "Kill the evil talking box"…did I say I did that? I meant my…girlfriend…yeah she did it! Did I mention how Liam Ump should be fired? His bitch ass constantly disrespects me. "Ooooh, Phil is soooo evil, he hit them with chairs. It’s a travesty of justice."…whiny fag. So from what I did see of the match, a clothesline was hit after a bulldog, oh an! d a spear. All the while Brockman stumbled around like Frankenstein, honestly man, have some pride if you work. So yeah, that took up a minute, and in this minute Brockman’s ENTIRE moveset consisted of a clothesline. Honestly, even I do more than that. Then my girlfriend ran around in circles screaming and the crowd got shocked at a freaking suplex. A SUPLEX! They were probably just amazed Brockman could do more than a clothesline. Then Liam Ump started sounding like JR, and some other crazy crap happened. Before the three minute mark, that Lazy McCumstain jumped out the ring, got a microphone got back in the ring and said more stuff about porn. I GET THE MESSAGE! YOU LIKE PORN! EVERYONE LIKES PORN! GO EVERYONE! Now stop talking about it for Christ’s sake, you’ve hammered the point home about twenty time over already. Oh, he also said he was a pussy and left the ring. PUSSY! If you’re booked in a match, you don’t back out! That’s my gimmick, stop stealing it! 8) Then Sergey went to Reed Young, because he’s a little ugly wankshaft. "Waaaah, I want to get my revenge on Atken, waaaaaaaaah". Ugly prick booked me in a match, despite that fact that I was not booked to wrestle on that night. Guy deserved the whooping he got. Why the hell did a nice guy like Reed Young book that match? I gotta to remember to talk to him about that. 9) I saw Jesse "Intense Face Washer" Ramsey, then I saw Tyler Davidson. THEN I changed the channel. Turned back in time to see that Tyler lost, I put this down to the fact that he flat out sucks and only won my Cruiserweight title as I had another, gruelling match-up before hand. Well that and he had a manager, who could hand him weapons in the No Holds Barred contest, which is cheap, cheap tactic AND the only way in which Tyler could ever beat me. 10) Oh joy, a guy called Kronos went all anti-hardcore on us. Actually, thinking back to that cheat Davidson beating me this guy could be on to something. I like his ideas and wish to subscribe to his newsletter. 11) This was the point were I found out about being booked in the Last Man Standing contest. Honestly, I’m sick of this crap being pulled on me. Not to mention I had to fight the match in my freaking street clothes because I quickly found out the match was on like two minutes after I spoke to Reed. That guy better not be trying to screw me around, I have ways of dealing with those who screw me around. 12) Last Man Standing: It wasn’t so much of a match, much more a case of me giving Sergey the beating of his life. Sure he managed to get a few (weak I may add) shots at me, but I wasn’t the one walking out the the arena will blood dripping all the way down me. Oh no, I left there smelling of roses compared to that oh so honourable wrestler crappy Sergey. Cool spots in this match included me kick the ever loving crap out of Sergey, me landing a picture perfect top-rope elbow on Sergey, me taking a sledgehammer to Sergey’s freaking knee. Oh yes I especially liked that one. I wonder how his knee is now? Okay so I lied, I don’t really give a toss. Anyway the match ended in a draw, which is good enough for me because to be honest Sergey is beneath me and not worth my time caring about. I give this match minus twenty stars because it was a waste of my valuable time. 13) Intense Face Washer and Chad Villain went back to the playground when insults were thrown and a line on par with "act your age not your show size" was used by Villain. Now "pencil dick", that’s a keeper. Oh yeah, Ramsey wish Villain luck, what a nice guy. Shame he’s just being sarcastic and no doubt going to get involved in the main event. 14) I would give this blackjack segment a recap, but not until Khristain Keller and Reed Young both learn the rules of blackjack. A QUEEN AND AN ACE IS BLACKJACK YOU MORONS! 15) Sergey gets a touch of the angst because he knows he can never beat me. That’s because I have an truckload more talent than him, not to mention my charisma is through the roof. Ha, his teacher bitched at him. Wonder if he got detention. 16) The crowd cheered on LLB as he managed to tie up his boots. I hear that’s the first time he’s done it with out mummy’s help. I cannot say this 100% certain, but it does explain why the fans were cheering the boot tying. Honestly, this show went from INTENSE face wash to INTENSE boot lacing. Next week on Pressure Point: INTENSE Sandwich making. You know it’s going to happen. If it doesn’t, I’ll make sure I do it. Hey, I might end up with a main event too if I have INTENSE sandwich making, live on air. 17) Main Event: Annnnd, I called it. Jesse Ramsey, and his intense face washing ways cost Chad "Pencil Dick" Villain the match against the boot lacer himself, LLB. You smarks are rubbing off on me too much when I can call the interference. Actually, you’re not, as even a new born baby who has no idea what the hell he’s doing in any matter could call Jesse’s involvement. I would recap this match point by point, but I do have a life. So yeah, screw you lot. Go read what drivel your smark friend has on his homemade website about this match. I’m sure it involves the phrase "BONZO GONZO! LOLOLOLOL" at least five times. 18) Chad Villain and Jesse Ramsey talk more, but I just can’t seem to care. Heh, pencil dick. 19) Roland Erman and LLB talk about things. Like identity crisis’ and…nope that’s about it. Apparently the boot guy was sick of this Roland guys crap. All in all it ends with a match for next week with LLB’s identity on the line. I would honestly care more if I understood any of this stipulation and name crap, but I was busy having a successful career to be watching A! when this crap started. The fans found the name changing stipulation shocking though, but they’re a bunch of hicks anyway. Plus, if I wasn’t so damn wrapped up in my life of being great, I’d jump at the chance to change my name. If this Roland guys wins, he should get to change LLB’s name. May I suggest "Frank the Homicidal Nun"? All in all, this show needed more me, and less face washing. © Action Wrestling 2002-2004
