The Blood, Sweat, & Tears Synopsis: 16.2.3 Posted by Keith Scott Zimmerman -Hennig's death affected me greatly, even though I never met him. You see, a lot of my character is derivative from some elements he brought to the table as Mr. Perfect. Matter of fact, alongside the Million Dollar Man, he was one of the first heels I liked--an early sign of the smarkdom to come. He was such an awesome athlete, something that stood out especially in Vince's steroid-riddled 80's and will probably go down in history as the best Intercontinental Champion of all time. So, from here on in, this is for Curt. And hopefully it'll be good enough...cause it won't be Perfect. -Live from the Sodom & Gomorrah of the Missip'. -Your hosts are Whatever Boy & Mr. Roboto. -Why The Hell Are Segments Clogging Up My PPV Theatre: LLB blows off Fox (yes, this is LIVE), Lightning runs down Airborn & Siobhan. -Curtain-jerker: The Ringer v. Bill makes the ropes, low blows, and goes off on Ringer, culminating in a long-distance SSP and a springboard splash. F4 Weekly finishes the job in a big upset, so I guess Ringer's on the line of pink slips upcoming.
