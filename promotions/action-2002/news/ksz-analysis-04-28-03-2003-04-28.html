KSZ Analysis 04/28/03

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 30.4.3 (taped 28.4.3.) The Big News: *grumble mutter* Title Changes: One. Face/Heel Turns: None. Best Match: 5. Nice wrestling v. power match. Jeff hits rolling suplexes for 2; Cylear gets the same off the Michinoku Driver/Savage elbow bang-bang. Cylear hit his Cha-Ching ripoff for 2, preparing Sweet Chin Music. Least in theory. Cylear for some reason followed it up with the Eddy slingshot senton bomb. Jeff put him on the floor and sidewalk slammed him into the edge of the announce table. NICE. STO & Tiger Driver 9fucking1 but Cylear gets out. The crowd is behind JEFF, showing you how completely worthless Cylear's become outside of name recognition. Cylear takes a leg with the superkick, then hits it. Jeff...kicks out. Cylear reverses the Origislam into a modified victory roll but no. Splash Mountain, Roaring Elbow, Garvinator; Cylear shrugs 'em off. He hit the Superfly but his own foot was on the ropes. Jeff goes spine on the pine and hits the superkick himself--of course, Sean got out. Who loses to their own finisher? Jammer but Cylear makes the ropes. Backdrop driver by Cylear...kickout. He goes for a moonsault press but Jeff counters via Tombstone and STOMP. They trade finishers and Jeff Origislams him into the corner, comes out with the super dragon screw in a spot I suggested and segues right into the Jammer. Nighty-night, Sean. Worst Match: 1. Let us never speak of this again. Absurdity of the Show: 1. Let us never speak of this again. Moment of the Show: Because pulling a Mass Transit wasn't enough last week, someone pulled out a gun THIS week! Breakdown/Highlights: Jack's in a coma, LLB visting...Heimdal threatened Max, the first time his back problem's been mentioned on air if memory serves me correctly...SSI's doing timekeeping this week...Arcades got ready...Phillips told a fat boy to shut it...Jeff mocked Klepto Boy...Cambria & Denton got in a dustup...$eller arrived HELLA late...1) Shut up. Just shut up... Max told his mom to make a choice...Another LLB in the hospital...Some guy pulled up trying to get a job...Fiend & Sharc played a game of "¿Quien Es Mas Macho?"...Max's mom (surprise) didn't pick him...2) Best Denton's looked since the comeback, and it was another sneeze as Rook went for the mask to draw Duel. Luckily, the backdrop driver finished a match--thanks for paying attention, Steven... Heh, Cylear got so rattled by the GO THE FUCK HOME chant he cut his interview two minutes short; Jeff'll eat his lunch later...Standard issue Paulton promo, this time with Fiend about teaming up vs. the Sharcs--would that make them the Jets? Hey, Officer Krupke...And in order not to kill you all, let's just say the Ceiling's come back with red-headed reinforcements...3) Fiend's switched over to Aphex Twin's "Come To Daddy" which is more awesome than if Britney Spears' tits pumped out Heineken. Standard tag with KP playing RM; combo Anderson spinebuster and Savage elbow took out the heels and Fiend laid out Paulton after the match. Decent TV match... Fox showed up at the hospital, presumably to mock LLB...Ringer/Bill at Juggernaut 2! SMELL THE BUYRATE!...Daniel tried to cut his trademark humble victory promo but the Overlord of the Ceiling hisself beat him up with the Ceilingstrom and we've got a freaking CAREER match in the form of Malone/Phillips at JII. If there was ever a way for God to work himself back in my good graces, it would be St. Daniel going over with the Glass Ceiling Driver...4) Stiff match, going shot to the back for shot to the back. Heimdal went for a straightjacket thing drawing Duncan. Max went for the Trifecta and hit it all, but only 2. (He'd given up on the tapout 'cause of Heimdal being good and the back problem.) Duncan got taken out with the fishbuster, and Jessica actually interefered. Max capped a nice series of reversals with a rope-assissted backslide. Maybe the injury IS bad, this was only 4 flakes... The guy from earlier (Joe) got hired as Production Manager...Fox--get this--mocked LLB for putting Jack in the coma. Anybody else think he's going over at the PPV? Yeah, me neither...5) See above...KrOw jumped Cylear hostage & held he and Reed hostage until he got a contract and a shot vs. Cylear at the PPV. Grade: K. You read it, K. Now die. Show Results: 1) Let us never speak of this again. 2) Steven Denton d. Duel 3) Kayden Paulton & Fiend d. Sharc & Bait (Paulton d. Sharc) 4) [CARNAGE] Max Danger d. Frank Heimdal 5) [BANTAM] Jeff Garvin d. Sean Cylear © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
