KSZ Analysis 03/31/03

31 March 2003

ACTION! PRESSURE POINT REPORT By: Keith Scott Zimmerman E-mail: [email removed] Date: 2.4.3 (taped 31.3.3.) The Big News: RUSSA~! Has a belt! FOX~! Grows some balls~! I~~! Fuck Aroma up OLD SCHOOL~! Title Changes: LoD2k3 won an elimination triangle match to earn the Dyads. Face/Heel Turns: Chris Fox? Wow, who'd they get THAT idea from? Best Match: 7. Weapons-filled cluster to start, and Fletcher canes Russa. # of participants bleeding: 1. White Russian leg sweep on Malone but Avalon saves. LoD with a spike wheelbarrow splash on Malone, kickout. LoD team up for SOMERSAULT PLANCHA in motion, taking out the WoC & Avalon. Malone with the half-nelson and release dragon suplexes, but St. Daniel saves Russa to take out Joey himself. Russa hit his FameAsser but got the Treatment AND the double uranage. Fletcher saved and hit a running SSP(!!!!!) but no. 630 SPLASH?! Holy S H I T. Fucking Malone. Lactose & Malone hit a double Stun Gun into a double back suplex elbow driver?! If this is anybody but Matt Hardy...version 1...you're stealing my bit! LtI took out Malone, Avalon LIEBERMANed LtI, and Phillips introduced Coral to a couple chair shots. Russa hit a piledriver on a chair to Phillips, and Fletcher Vulcan Snapped him out, as he's been wont to do recently. However, Fletcher fucked his shit up to the point where he's been cut from the active roster. So sucks to that assmar. LIEBERMAN DEVICE by the SGC. Wow. Malone superkicks Phillips, who bites it and gets Double Helixed. 3. Settles down to a tag, and the '03 work over Avalon's shoulder. Russa gets overconfident and gets Curveballed, and Malone gets his house of fire period. Russa goes for the Impale, but it failed. Phillips comes out, Impale, new champs. Lactose crying really sold this as A Moment. Phillips with the Halo on Joey--GLASS CEILING DRIVER through the table! RUAHH~! Treatment on Avalon. 3 refs bite it, and Phillips puts it back on for another minute. COOL. BEANS. Worst Match: 4. I'm thinking of declaring this feud too boring to live. Ringer won after some chair shots and Uncle Slam. Absurdity of the Show: See Worst Match comments above. Moment of the Show: Chris Fox beating the ever-loving shit out of LLB. And that's saying a lot seeing as I beat the shit out of Aroma and Malone lost to RUSSA~! Breakdown/Highlights: KroW came back and threatened LLB & Reed before stating after putting Cylear in a wheelchair back in the day, he's going to finish the job. Cylear came out and said due to the whole Zone thing, KroW himself wasn't on the roster and had him get the fudge out. Cylear/Paulton street fight booked, obviously for KroW to get his revenge...Young II came out and did a clipboard interview--Anthason's coming back way too soon to fight Heimdal, my hardcore challenge to warm up for next week, and Duel/Shabazz. Fox comes out and is retarded for a spot before lLb comes down and suggests LLB/Fox tonight, which happens. There's no way we're giving away our biggest PPV main event on free fucking TV, right? Right?! Oh, I don't feel so hot...Saint Daniel came out to brag and issue a challenge that was answered by LoD2k3 and Malone goaded him into including the SGC, so it's a triangle no-DQ for the Dyads...Heimdal & Duel pratted on about nothing in particular...Earlier Today, Max found out his mom was in love with Conrad (1...2...3...EWWWWWWWWWW), told her to fudge the fudge off, and got MOMSLAPPED for it...Sanchis is the young one's assisstant today...A match?! 1) Technical exhibition sneeze--Heimdal got Uncle Slammed and reversed the cover for the fall...JXS did I Bet You Will at MTV's Spring Break...Here's where the triangle no-DQ came from, Phillips...2) You didn't pay to see this on PPV, did you? Frogs (yes, frogs) interfered in this match leading to the heel rollup on Duel for 3, then Duel got spoken to in Spanish...Donell went to see his dad in the hospital...I got ready...Jessica tried to talk Max off the precipice... 3) Absolutely got the hell stiffed out of me (abrasions, busted lip & forehead), so I gave back what I took and threw in dessert 'cause I'm that kind of guy. Crowd was hot for the final 6 spots or so, as planned. Easy 3 flakes...Daniel punked a tech for candy...4) See above...Cylear threatened bodily harm, not like Paulton cared...Sanchis tried to stop The Seed from dropping the f-bomb; give him two years...Jessica got MOMSLAPPED too...Denton's gonna GIT Minio, just you wait...Cylear got ready... 5) Cylear carried most of the offense with Paulton hitting a moonsault off a car when, And I Am Shocked THIS Occured, KroW beat up Cylear until Paulton could drag his near-dead body for the pinfall. Have I mentioned enough how badly they've fucked Cylear over before? Once, maybe?...Some guy was watching Homeless Gus...Some guy named Neil Marx debut. Great, Kayden's spawned a clone...Conrad loves Max's mom, thus ensuring Fiend was going to DIE...Invalid mocked Shepherd...6) Fiend beat Max up and down the ramp and drew color before the match began. I don't get how Fiend's this dark, mobbed-up guy and steals JEFF FREAKIN' HARDY'S Drop Shot. Trapped headbutts and a couple postings occur before Fiend remembers the new rules (nice touch). Max pulled the mental equivalent of the ZOMBIE SITUP and hit the x-arm Iconoclasm segue to the cross-armbreaker. Max countered the Anderson spinebuster and got on the dragon sleeper but Fiend countered to the sleeper standard and backdropped Max damn near to the ramp in a horrifying bump. Razor's Edge to the floor, but Max counteres the Anderson with a DDT this time, buying him enough time to think about how much a corpse's spine goes for on the market. KO shot to the floor. Tornado DDT to the floor. Basically, I couldn't watch the rest of this since it scared me (though I should've been happy Max is weaker for OUR match) but Max won with the stump puller. Four...Donell made a baby speech back at the hospital...Jeff slapped Chris around... 7) See above...Accidie said hello to the rest of 101; I was busy stemming the bleeding from my right hand with the trainer, for those who care...LLB & Fox shook hands and agreed to be safe out there...8) LLB got Testify on in about 90 seconds. LLB with the Cactus clothesline, then introducing Fox to the steps. Fox got in a move and missed the Lionsault, then got introduced himself to the table. Foxorama through the table yet somehow LLB got a short powerbomb for the first near fall of the match. 3 more 2 counts for LLB before Fox superkicks and frog splashes him for HIS first two. Black & White for 2, after the double clothesline spot LLB gets the Crossface. Foxorama, only 2, LIEBERMAN only 2, and LLB hits a second B & W, drawing the Boss. He promptly gets booted, but Fox chair shots LLB for the win. Austin beatdown follows. JOY. Grade: A. Three superb title matches on free TV and the offensive stuff didn't grate me as much as it probably should've. Ah, well, maybe that's the aspirin talking. Anyway, Fox needs to burn the kiddy shit next week and curse like a Phillips. Yes. Show Results: 1) Frank Heimdal d. Kent Anthason 2) Jordan X Shabazz d. Duel 3) {hardcore} ME rd. Sweet Aroma 4) {cage} The Ringer d. The Big Malak(sp?) 5) {street fight} Kayden Paulton d. Sean Cylear 6) [CARNAGE] Max Danger d. Fiend 7) {triangle} [DYAD] LoD2k3 d. Stolen Glass Ceiling d.
