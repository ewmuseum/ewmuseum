A Tale to Tell

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.04.02 He woke up with drips off sweat pouring down in his face and a head that throbbed at every movement he made. He had awaken in the middle of the night from a nightmare, something that he still seemed to have problems with even though he was a grown man. He slowly dropped the blankets off of his bed, got up and headed out the door of his small room. The digital clock showed it was 2:47 AM and there was a gentle rainfall outside. Tyler headed straight for the phone, picked it up, dialed, and waited. It rang. He waited. It rang. He waited. It rang. He waited. It rang. 'Hello?' She softly said on the other side of the phone. 'Linz, we need to talk.' 'Tyler, it's three in the morning. I've got to get to work in the morning and I need my sleep. Can't this wait?' Tyler nodded and continued. 'Yeah, I'm sorry to wake you. I just... I'm sorry.' Lindsay smiled. 'I'll call you when I get home tomorrow. I'll have all the time to talk to you than, ok?' 'Yeah.' 'Goodnight.' She quickly said before hanging up the phone. 'Night.' Tyler replied back to an empty other line before hanging the phone back up. He sat there, alone on a small stool in the kitchen with only a small light to light the way. He got up and searched around a drawer for something, shuffling papers until he had a small case of asprin in his hands. He tossed it onto the counter and went back to bed, seeming to forget why he got out in the first place. He laid, looking up into the dark room. 'I love her so much!' He said to himself aloud. 'If only she felt the same way I did. It would make life easy for me.' A large grin crossed his face before he closed his eyes and fell asleep. Maybe tomorrow...
