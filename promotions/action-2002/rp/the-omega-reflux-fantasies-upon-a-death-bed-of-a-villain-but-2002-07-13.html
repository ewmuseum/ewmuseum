The Omega: Reflux Fantasies Upon A Death Bed Of A Villain…But I Am Near Death…So Leave Me Alone(5)

13 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
8:00 Am and I feel great. Man, this is weird. Out of the hospital in less then a week. Now, I guess it is time to think of what the hell the past really is. Not this fictional story I told people for years. The pieces are all around, scattered about like shattered cement from a highway reconstruction. So…where do I begin? Nowhere. It’s not time to think. It’s time for a cup of coffee. I walk into a Starbucks and grab a cup of coffee. My fingers wrapped around the circumference of the plastic cup with its cover, I bring the warm liquid to my mouth and sip. It feels so good. I have been cold for way too long, and the boiling coffee is curing my souls freezing shivers. After I stopped here, I went to a grocery store. Little thing. Not all that big. It was kind of dirty and dingy, with sevenities architecture. I must have gazed at the Asian lady at the express lane, with a pack of Marlboro Red Premiums clasped in my hand. She gave me a weird look, and under her apron, I could see a High Flyer “Just Intoxicating” t-shirt. Old school shit. Must be a wrestling fan. She barely looked at me as I checked out. Maybe she was expecting me to pull a shovel out of my asshole, make a war cry and play a game of home run derby with her cranium. When I dug into my ADIDAS Black Tear-Aways pockets, and pulled out a ten dollar bill, she didn’t look at me. Not even when she was giving me change. The tight black short sleeved shirt I wore showed off my arms, but also my scarred wrists. My blue hair tied in a pony tail, I lowered myself by her ear and whispered something. “I’m sorry” I took my pack of smokes and cup of coffee and sat on a bench. With a little lighter, I took one cancer stick out and began to puff down on it, letting the smoke penetrate my lungs. I would pull the cigarette out every so often and sip my Starbucks coffee. For some reason, this felt so good. I was free. I was alive. And, in another way, this hurt me so badly. I hated being like this. I do not want to have to whisper I am sorry. I do not want to be a loner. However, this is the life I chose. And this This is what I shall live. Do not pity me. I do not need pity. I only need reassurance. Yeah. Reassurance.
