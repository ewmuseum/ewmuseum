Saturday, February 16 - Superdome, New Orleans, LA King of Submissions Tournament Finals 'The Original' Jeff Garvin versus Max Danger *BOOM BOOM* Max Danger screaming out in pain, as Jeff Garvin takes a 'time-out'. *BOOM BOOM* Jeff Garvin gives 'The Danger Man' the Orignal Slam off the apron. *BOOM BOOM* Chris Chambers is knocked out by Danger. *BOOM BOOM* Jeff Garvin puts Max in the Hammer Jammer. *BOOM BOOM* 'Come on, baby!' screamed out the teary-eyed Jessica. *BOOM BOOM* Fans erupt as Max gets to the ropes. *BOOM BOOM* Garvin is reeling. *BOOM BOOM* 'The Original' escapes DANGER. *BOOM BOOM* Jeff is put in the Hammer Jammer. *BOOM BOOM* Towel is thrown into the ring. *BOOM BOOM* Max Danger kneeling beside trophy, triumphant. Max Danger, King of Submissions Saturday, February 22 - Dox Household, Leitchfield, KY 'The Danger Man' was laying on his stomach, like he had been ever since he had arrived home from New Orleans, on the black leather couch inside the living room. His face was turned towards the coffee table. Resting atop the glass table was the three-foot tall KING OF SUBMISSIONS trophy. Jessica came into the room and sat herself down between the couch and table. Max placed his arm across her chest and she turned and smiled at him. 'You did great, honey,' she told him. She had mentioned that several times since last Sunday. Max just smiled back at his wife. She didn't have to say it, but he was happy she did. The television set was alive just beyond the trophy. Playing was 'Graduation - Part One', episode 21 in season three for Buffy the Vampire Slayer. Danger had been watching all three seasons in a loop since he got home Monday morning. Jessica had allowed this. But neither were paying attention to the television. They were just sitting in silence. Enjoying being with one another. He knew it was her encouragement and faith in him that allowed him to be victorious in the tournament. 'You know, if you weren't with me, I would have lost to Kayden Paulton,' he had said to her. She kissed him, 'I know.' Marcus Alexander Dox, as Max Danger, had many accomplishments in his life. Titles won. Awards being given. But none compared to the one thing he got by just being himself, by being Xander. Jessica. It's the one thing he would cherish for the remainder of his life.
