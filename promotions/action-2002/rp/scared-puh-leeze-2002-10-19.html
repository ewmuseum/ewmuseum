Scared? Puh-Leeze!

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 19th, 2002 Gently he pressed the ice pack against his swollen cheekbone as he leaned back in an old chair positioned in the living area of his apartment. The envelope containing his monthly paycheck was torn from end to end and the check laid atop it. It had been quite a Pressure Point, indeed. Jeff had caused his arch nemesis, Coral Avalon, to lose his match against The Ringer on live television. An act that brought happiness to Jeff Garvin… But then that drunken Englishman had to stick his nose into Jeff’s business. “Joe Campbell.” Said Jeff, squeezing the plastic ice pack with all his might until it popped. The ice water shot back in his face and nearly knocked him out of his recliner. “Fuck! How dare he come to my show and attack me! That’s bullshit, complete and utter bull shit!” That was another problem that ‘the Original’ was going to have to deal with. Not only did he have Coral Avalon and Odium running around, now he had Campbell to watch out for. And why? All because Jeff decided to join up with a renegade group of professional wrestlers and burn down the Asylum video wall, then attacking their World Heavyweight Champion the week after and putting him in the… Deep breath. Single most devastating finishing hold to ever grace the squared circle. “And he has the audacity to come running down the entrance ramp and spear me?” Garvin shook his head as if not being able to believe some one would have the balls to do such a thing. “I’m the best damn technical wrestler in the world and I get blind sided by a fucking alcoholic!” But as much as it annoyed him to know that Campbell had got the better of him on that occasion, there was always the show to gain a little revenge with the rest of the Stranglehold. Though with the developments that the last Asylum Show brought, things had become even more complicated. All because of one man. Devoid. The man who attempted to KILL him on public television. “If it hadn’t been for my CAT LIKE reflexes I might have been a goner.” Cut to Jeff Garvin looking down to see a small red dot on his crotch. Then to him screaming like a four-year-old girl and leaping from the Asylum cage. “I wasn’t ‘screaming like a girl’…” Jeff shifted his eyes from side to side. “That was…Erm… A ‘Manly War Cry’. Yeah! If Devoid had just showed his face I would have had to go ninja style on his ass… EEEEEEEYAH!” He leapt up and gave a poor attempt at a karate, pump kick… Resulting in him falling flat on his ass. “Ouch!” He rubbed his ass. “Bah… It doesn’t matter anyway. Everyone knows I could take that piece of shit with one arm tied behind my back and a blind fold covering my eyes. All he is another lunatic with a gun. A no body… Like I would EVER waist my time with him.” Garvin’s tummy grumbled. “Woah. All this ‘talking to myself’ has me starved. I’m going out to go snag a cheese burger.” On his way to the door, Jeff slid open the closet door and grabbed a bullet proof vest… And put it on. Then a long black trench coat. He pulled up the hood, hunched over, and headed out the door. “Nope. Not scared of him at all.”
