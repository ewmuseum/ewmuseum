Life With The Malones: Day Eight

12 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/12/2002 Hey, everyone. This has been quite the interesting week for me, to say the very least. Joey's been staying up late reviewing tapes of Denton matches. I'm talking three AM late here. The guy doesn't seem quite right in the head at this time, but I suppose what's been going on in his life could screw anyone up. Joey had to go to the seVered pay-per-view over the weekend and call a match that he had a hand with for the last two years. To say that he was unhappy about doing it was an understatement, because he had promised Keri to take her to the movies, and... well, that didn't happen. She took it well, though, despite the random blasting of him that she told me and Ali when we decided to take her ourselves. For those of you wondering, I do end up going with Joey to his Uncut shows, seeing as though he doesn't quite yet trust me on leaving me to my own devices in his house. Mainly, I'm just there as a spectator that gets to watch the matches from the comfort of the crowded locker room. Not a lot of people knew who I was, though I did have an interesting conversation with Kinkade. He kind of just laughed and told me that I was doing his finisher all wrong. Heh, funny. Someone actually knew what I was doing! I also got to meet my childhood hero, Glen Miller, which was a trip in of itself. He's still looking good, despite his banged-up knee and his aged looks. We had a brief conversation that went something like this: Me: Huh... hi? Glen: Hello. Exciting stuff, huh? Yeah, I know, it really isn't, but it's Glen freakin' Miller! You think I'm going to be able to formulate whole sentences in his direction? Please. That's, like... college stuff. Now, I'd love to stay and continue to rant about my week, but I do believe that the bed in my room is calling my name. In a moaning, calling kind of way. So, join us next time, when I start to describe all of Joey's sleeping habits! -- Coral
