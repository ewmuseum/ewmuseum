New Year's Pain

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 1, 2003 The general consensus about New Year's Eve is that it is a time to reflect on the past, not to mention a chance look upon the future. The start of the future begins when the clock strikes midnight...or when the crystal ball drops in Times Square if one is in a Dick Clarkian mood. While others spent this time with their friends, family, and total strangers from who knows where, Simon's spent his in a different way. Sure, they were having fun, but so was he... At least, it resembled fun. 'Five...four...three...' Countdowns CAN be fun. 'Two...ONE!' exclaimed Simon as he dug a spoon into a can of Spaghettios. Yes, he was victorious at Players & Pawns. Yes, he was still Carnage champion...but at a price. Of course, victories for functional body parts isn't the ideal trade-off, yet it seemed to be for Team V.I.A.G.R.A.. Simon had been playing that attack over and over again in his mind. It was a broken record of an album he'd only want to listen to if his life depended on it. He was so caught up with his thoughts that it could've struck midnight a thousand times, but he wouldn't have a clue. What he wanted right then and there was to leave his room. What was going to happen would be anything but that. All he wanted to do was have a good time. In fact, just like Eddie Murphy, he wanted to party all the time, party all the time...party all the time. Suffice to say, that's a lot of partying. Though he couldn't. Physically and emotionally, this was it. 'Five...four...three...two...ONE!' he yelled before he took a sip of the finest drink available at the moment. Bottled water never tasted so...bottled. With the television on in the background, he'd occassionally glance at the screen. Anything involving nouns that were high or people named Tony would have set him off instantaneously, so he kept his distance. As he mumbled 'Auld Lang Syne' to himself, he was reminded that 2002 was a great year for him, though 2003 would be better. Much better. A year in which Flyer and Davis' actions would be rewarded... With a reaction.
