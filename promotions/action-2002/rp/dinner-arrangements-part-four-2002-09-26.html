Dinner Arrangements: Part Four

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 26th, 2002 Yeah, you guess it… Still Downtown. “I think I’m going to name him… Odium.” “No, Jeff, no… Don’t you name your fucking lobster after me.” “But you two look so much alike, all angry and what not!” Jeff laughed, pressing his face up against the outer wall of the plastic, zip lot bag that contained his lobster. Jeff and Odium were back in the waiting area again as Claire said goodbye to her father who was too busy tending to a disgruntled cook to meet them. Which was probably for the best in Claire’s case. She rounded the corner, repositioning her purse on his shoulder. “Okay, ready to go? Shouldn’t we call for a cab, I’m feeling a bit tipsy.” Odium’s face scrunched up, “Tipsy? You’re fucking plastered. I am too…” “I’m perfectly fine, alcohol is bad for the kidneys, the nectar of the nit-wit, that’s why I don’t drink. Milk helps build strong, healthy bones. Luckily I parked my ‘Shaggin’ Wagon’ nice and close.” “You mean your 65’ Pinto, Jeff? How in the hell is that a Shaggin’ Wagon, you’ve haven’t even got laid once.” “…Oh… I’ve had some ‘good’ times in that sex-mobile. Good times, indeed.” Jeff shifted his penis in his boxers and cracked a perverted smile. “Fuck that, I’m walking.” With that, Odium kicked the door open and exited the restaurant leaving Claire and Jeff all alone. Sha-WING!
