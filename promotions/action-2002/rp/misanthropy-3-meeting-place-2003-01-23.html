Misanthropy [3]: Meeting Place

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/23/03 The outskirts of Phoenix, Arizona January 17th, 2003 'Is this where you met Leigh?' Duncan Fletcher asked Daniel Phillips, as they sat in a bar/grill called 'Smiley's' on the outskirts of Phoenix, Arizona. Daniel had no idea why he was asked to come here, other than the fact that his crazy, off-and-on girlfriend decided that this would be a good place to come. 'Ehh...' Daniel said, pretty much blowing off his partner's question by turning around and looking out the window. Then he looked at his watch. It was twenty-five till one in the afternoon, and Daniel noticed with some alarm that Leigh was about five minutes late. 'What could be keeping her?' Daniel looked back toward Duncan, who just simply gave him a resigned shrug, before he dunked his cigerette in the ashtray. 'She'll show. She's a dependable girl.' Daniel merely returned his gaze to the window, before a car pulled up and a young, frizzly black-haired woman made her way out of the vehicle. She wore clear glasses over her eyes, a black short-sleeved shirt, long fishnet gloves, and black pants. Despite her clothing color of choice, she seemed very cheerful. She entered the grill, scanned the room, found Phillips, and immediately sat next to Fletcher, nearly throwing him right off of his seat in the process. 'Hi, Danny!' 'Hey, watch it!' Fletcher shouted, taken aback. This caused Phillips to smile, 'Man, she did a better job in getting a rise out of you than I ever could.' Then, Leigh pulled something out of her pocket, and smiled at Daniel. 'Happy birthday, Daniel!' She placed it on the table and poked it toward Daniel. It was a small box. Daniel looked at it oddly, then looked at Leigh oddly. 'Er... you know, I almost forgot that it WAS my birthday.' Daniel said, with an odd smile on his face. 'Well, aren't you going to open it?' Leigh asked, with a light smile. After a brief pause, Daniel untied the small string that held it open, and opened the box to find a small, golden necklace that would fit his neck. 'Aw, geeze, you shouldn't have.' Daniel said, giving her a smile. Leigh smiled back. Duncan Fletcher blinked in shock, almost frozen with a piece of steak still on his fork. It was mainly because he was surprised to find that his partner, the same man who he had shed blood with in the Asylum, the same man who had the attitude of a steel trap, actually had a softer side. He wondered what she thought of his wrestling side.
