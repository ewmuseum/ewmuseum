The Alpha: The End Of Innocence: 3

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/15/02 Before PP7's Main Event... Remember Me by Hoobastank played on in the locker room. There he sat. He was in his wrestling tights already. Syphon Fission sat on a bench, looking at the floor. I stand here face to face With someone that I used to know Used to look at me and laugh But now he claims That he's known me for so very long But I remember Being no one He wrapped the tape around his index and ring ringer of his left hand. He repeated the same task on the right hand. I wanted to be just like you So perfect So untouchable Now you want me to be with you Someone who used to have it all He threw the tape into the corner. He grabbed his pair of black elbow pads, and put them on. He flexed his arms, trying to get used to the rubber. To the constriction of his arms. Do you Remember now? You acted liked you never noticed me Forget it! Because the dawn has come around You're not allowed to be a part of me He put on both of his knee pads. Both were black, like you would think he heart was. You are wrong. He is doing what he feels is right. Did you know me Or where you too preoccupied With playing king in your small kingdom And now the real world Has stripped you of your royalty And from your kingdom you’re evicted After all the pain… After all the stuff he has been through in his life… He was going to see if he was really worth anything at all. I wanted to be just like you So perfect So untouchable Now you want me to be with you Someone who used to have it all Somewhere, deep down, he knows he is wrong. But his mind blanks it out. He is right. His word is God. He is a slave to the power. To the people. He wants to free himself from this self exerted bondage. And he is struggling against the chains. Do you Remember now? You acted liked you never noticed me Forget it! Because the dawn has come around You're not allowed to be a part of me Part of me Part of me Part of me! He stretched his arms. He wanted to make sure he had an extra snap on each punch he took tonight. He stretched his legs out on the bench. In his moment, he wanted to make sure his body didn’t fail him. You're never going to be a part of me You're never going to be a part of me You're never going to be a part of me You're never going to be a part of me You're never going to be a part of me You're never going to be a part of me It was up to him and Malone to decide who was really right. Was it Joey, a guy who plays the innocent role… Or is Fission… The man airing all the dirty laundry? Fission was losing his self. He was giving into the depression. The voices in his head taunted him, laughed at him, made him want to punch and kick harder. They were slowly killing Rashard Clark… And in his place putting a machine, ready to use all the power of Heaven and Hell to take out the competition Do you Remember now? You acted liked you never noticed me Forget it! Because the dawn has come around You're not allowed to be a part of me Part of me Part of me Part of me! The song was over. He could hear For You play throughout the arena. He heard the boos. The cat calls. Rashard Clark left that locker room… When he would enter it again… He wouldn’t enter it the same man. Right or wrong… Someone was going to leave that ring the victor. Nothing he could do would change that. Would it be him? -End of Chapter
