Brotherly Love: Prologue

26 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If you didn't watch the Blood, Sweat, and Tears pay-per-view, then you might not know that Coral Avalon had a hell of a lot of fans in New Orleans. In fact, it seemed as though someone had decided to bring every single fan he had into the arena on that night, because he had never expected that kind of heat in any of his matches before. Sure, he was kinda hurting, but then again, Coral Avalon had grown rather used to it. Coral stood in the backstage area, and was still trying to walk off his soreness. The pay-per-view had been over for a half an hour. His family had gone home after Coral's brother had been thrown out of the building, though Coral did receive an angry phone call from Irene several minutes after Joey had left his locker room. She pretty much yelled at him for giving her four heart attacks, having her 'lose her hearing'(an overexaggeration, of course), and getting her favorite son thrown out of the building even though Coral had absolutely nothing to do with it. Avalon had changed out of his wrestling gear and into a short-sleeved black shirt and a pair of jeans. He slightly hoped that merchandise would look at him and finally say, 'Hey, this guy might make a good t-shirt', but he's received nothing of the sort while guys like Airborn Tyler had several shirts. Then again, it wasn't like Coral needed the merchanidsing money anyway. Allison Lindum leaned against the wall in Coral's locker room, waiting for Coral to finally walk off his soreness. She smoothed out her long black dress as she moved out of her lean and stood in Coral's path, looking at him seriously. 'Are you going to be okay?' Allison asked, 'You don't usually take this long.' 'Yeah, sorry... it's not the soreness that's bugging me, though. It's what's waiting for me out there, you know? If you haven't noticed, I have a lot of fans here in town. I'm afraid that I'll get mobbed.' Coral said. 'You'll be fine,' Allison said, before she smiled and winked at him, 'Any bitch that tries to rip your clothes off will have to answer to me.' Coral smiled at this as Allison put on her leather jacket. 'Yeah, you're scarier than my mom when you get riled up.' he said, causing Allison to frown and give him a weird look. 'I bet I could beat you up!' Allison said, before Coral winked at her. 'Yeah, you're so big and tough when I've got a great big headache and had a thirty minute match with Joey frigging Malone in my hometown.' Coral said, smiling at her. He brushed a bit of hair out of her face and gave her a long, hard look. 'What?' Allison questioned. 'C'mon, sweetie. We should get going. We wouldn't want to keep your sister waiting, now would we?' Coral said, breaking his gaze into Allison's blue eyes by smiling at her and starting to walk away. 'HEY!' Allison shouted after him, 'You take that back!' She followed after him, content on making him pay, even if she was smiling in the knowledge that Coral just loved to tease her. °•·._.·•° Coral Avalon and Allison Lindum stepped out into the rainy, New Orleans night. Coral had thoughtfully brought an umbrella for the occasion, expecting the showers, and he held the umbrella above his head with one hand, and he had his other arm around Allison's neck to hold her close. Leave it to Coral Avalon to not take his car when he came here. Then again, he WAS driven here via his parents' limo, and had not even expected his family to rush out after the pay-per-view, thanks to his older brother being thrown out. Luckilly, he had a hotel room not too far from the arena. Even more luckilly, security had backed away some of the lingering fans who had come to catch a glimpse of the wrestlers after the night was done and over with, allowing Coral and Allison to avoid the legions of screaming fans for the moment. The couple travelled the mile distance between the arena and the hotel amid the pitter-patter of raindrops. Some people recognized them, and Coral was more than happy to sign a few autographs for a random passerby. Which, of course, was nice except that it made the trip a little bit longer. The first thing Coral Avalon did was collapse backwards on one of the handy sofas that they provide you in a hotel lobby. Allison joined him and tilted her head into Coral's shoulder. 'Man,' Coral said, 'I'm beat.' 'Tell me about it,' Allison noted, tossing her high heeled shoes into her lap that she had taken off midway to the hotel, 'That's the last time I walk a mile like that. Next time, you're carrying me.' 'Oh, come on,' Coral said, looking over at Allison, 'It was raining!' Allison gave him a playful shove. 'Man, what kind of chilvarous, shining knight in armor are you, anyway?' she asked. A man wearing a beret and an army uniform walked up to the happy couple and tipped his beret at Allison. The expression on Allison's face turned to confusion as she picked her head up off of Coral's shoulder. Then it contorted into surprise as she recognized the uniformed man, and then joy as she leapt up from the sofa and hugged him warmly. 'Marcus!' she cried, tightening her hug. The man in the uniform held her in his own warm hug, though he completely missed the venomous glance that Coral shot at him as he watched his love hug this guy right out of the blue. 'Hey, sis. Been a really long time.' Marcus said, brushing Allison's hair as she hugged him. They finally let go of each other and gazed at one another for a little bit. Allison's blue eyes meeting Marcus's brown, before Allison turned to Coral while still holding on to Marcus's arm. Coral, at this point, was trying to figure out a way to publicly kill this guy and then use his family's wealth to buy out the law, when Allison smiled at Coral in a simple, reassuring smile. Coral calmed down slightly, as Allison took a quick look at the soldier, then another one at Coral, before she introduced the soldier she had leapt into. 'Coral, this is my big brother, Marcus.' Coral blinked. 'Oh.'
