Action! — Tales

The Velcro Heart, The Twenty-Forth Part of

25 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The hard rain drilled itself into the motionless body of the attacker, as LLB and KroW stood there in silence, listening to the thunder crash over head. LLB snarled at KroW, before he reluctantly paced towards the beaten down man. From the looks of it he wasn’t breathing... and that did not vote well for him. Sticking his hand into the man’s chin, LLB figured out that there was no pulse to be found. Quickly digging into his pockets to look for some ID... he pulled out his wallet. And there, in the wallet, laid a police officer’s badge. It flashed sharply as a loud thunderous shock struck a tree a few blocks away, as LLB looked up at KroW, throwing the badge down. “Nice work asshole. Nice fucking work.” His hands began to shake. He wasn’t cold... hell, he had been fighting for at least an hour now. He was far from that. He was nervous. There, a man lay... at the souls of his feet. Beaten down and dead because of his ignorant behavior. “Way to fucking go!” LLB charged at KroW, exchanging a left hand for another right. But that took both of them to their knees... they weren’t about to do this again. KroW licked a wound off of his arm, as he spat it down to the cement floor. He, too, glanced at LLB... and he, too, felt the exact same way. KroW didn’t know what to say, at first. Maybe the cop was just knocked out... or LLB didn’t feel his pulse right. But it didn’t matter... KroW wasn’t going to find out for himself. He’d leave it like that. He had to. “Me!? Me Roland!? If you hadn’t have jumped on him there... I doubt you’d have killed him!” LLB didn’t understand. His version of the story was that KroW threw the harder punches... and KroW, was the man that was the cause behind this. The lawyer nervously rubbed the side of his head. “You’re an idiot Roland.” KroW started to speak in a louder tone of voice. “Are you that fucking blind!?” LLB rushed a shaky hand across his forehead to wipe the rain away. “In Action!, you get everything, and I mean everything handed to you. You’re the spoiledest brat I’ve ever come across! You can’t admit it Roland, YOU can’t admit it was YOUR fault this asshole is dead...” LLB walked up to KroW and got in his face. “Me!? I’m spoiled? You have to be the cockiest fucker I’ve ever seen. You don’t admit a wrong when you see one... This isn’t the battlefield anymore, KroW. Like you’ve realized... this is life!” LLB pointed at the dead officer. “Welcome to it.” KroW shook his head with an evil, yet uncomfortable grin. “No. No I can’t do this...” Turning away from the lawyer, KroW continued. “I can’t be a part of this anymore.” “A part of what?” “Of this.” He pointed to the cop. “I can’t be here anymore. I can’t fucking live with you anymore Roland. Your bratty attitude... your spoiled ways... it pisses me off! I’m leaving Action! Wrestling right when I can.” LLB took offence to the words that KroW spoke. He stormed over to him and slammed him in the shoulder, turning his rival around. “No. No you’re not doing that.” KroW shook his head yes. “I’ve burned someone’s HEAD in Action! Wrestling because of you... I’ve taken your body through the entire Arena because of you... and now, I’ve helped kill someone... because... of you. What the fuck is going to happen next Roland? I’m out.” Anger paced throughout LLB’s body. This wasn’t right. He was the law... and he made the rules. KroW was not going anymore. It was written in stone. “You...” LLB stared directly into KroW’s eyes. “You have some fucking nerve.” And KroW grinned. He started to speak again... as his voice gained from a loud, to a soft. “Why Roland? Why’s that? Because live on television... LIVE ON TELEVISION... you got your ass kicked last? And no way... not the law... NOT Roland Ermann... he’s got too BIG of an ego for that! He just has to get the last laugh every single time! Huh Roland!? HUH!? You’ve beat ‘em all... even me... EVEN KROW... but here... right now, you can’t understand... you can’t REGISTER that I... KroW... BEAT YOU LAST!” “Right. Considering...” He pointed to himself. “I’m still the fucking Champion!” KroW snapped his head backwards for a second, hearing a noise in the wind and rain. “You want to keep your fucking voice down!? If someone fucking hears us... our careers...” He pointed to both of them. “OUR careers are over!” There was a long, drawn out silence as KroW recollected his thoughts. “I’ll stay until the Pay-Per-View is over. Fight you clean in the ring... one final, fucking time. But none of this... NONE of this Roland... can be used for fuel.” “You’re the one that fucking killed him anyway.” KroW nervously looked back a final time. “I wasn’t the one who hit him last!” LLB could only try and calm down. Levine was right... what *if* someone would hear them? God only knew that outcome. “Fuck off...” He said with a very nervous twitch. “That- that didn’t even matter. He was motionless when I punched him...” The rain poured. The thunder cracked. KroW and LLB continued their stare at each other... It took a full minute or two before one finally spoke again. “Okay...” LLB said. “I don’t care. It’s done Levine, what’s done is done.” KroW evilly glared a hole through the Champion. “All I know is... we can’t tell anyone about this.” LLB looked up at KroW, and though rage was spiriting throughout each of their bodies... that fact was right. They couldn’t say a word. They had no choice.
