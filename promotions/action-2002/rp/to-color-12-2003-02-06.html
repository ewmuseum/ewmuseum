Action! — Tales

To Color. 12

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Paulton snapped himself into the ropes before running towards the sliding Bezel whom entered the ring. SMACK. The Fans went wild. For Mark Paulton had dropkicked him square in the face. And the mammoth screamed, too, as he tried to stand, swatting the hurt away from his eyes. “Can’t... see. Can’t see!” Paulton took advantage in dropping to his knees and tripping the big man up. The Fans kept cheering all the way, as Mark stood, and looked into the eyes of The Tiny Tiger. “Just try and stop me!” It was as if the entire Arena said his line along with him, as the building shook on end, and The Bezel started to rise. Not wasting anymore time either, Mark ran after him, taking them both out with a clothesline over the top rope. On the outside, Paulton snapped to his feet. He spun around just then... looking at every aspect of the Arena. The cameras flashed... as the goosebumps fled down his arms and spine. It was his world once again... and this time The Bezel was going to feel it. Paulton knelt down, taking his hands and pressing them under the giant’s body. He gripped it with all of his might... and then started to lift his knees up... For Mark Paulton now had The Bezel in a gorilla press slam. “PAULTON! PAULTON! PAULTON!” His teeth ripped into themselves... as the mere weight of The Bezel was twisting him apart inside. It was all going to be over soon, though... as Mark turned, red faced and all, directing the body of The Bezel to the announce table. Everyone, if they weren’t already... stood. Paulton’s eyes grew wider in taking one step forward... and... SMACK! The Tiny Tiger had ran across the way, and nailed Mark in the face with The Wrestling Federation’s First Title. But it wasn’t just that... in grabbing his face in pain, The Bezel fell... On top of Mark Paulton, crushing him into the cement floor. The Arena... for it now could have heard a pin drop. And because of this, The Bezel was slow to rise, but made sure he dug his every joint into the body of Mark Paulton as he began to stand. Then, getting on the apron, the giant rolled in, demanding the Referee to count MP out. “One! Two! Three! Four! Five! Six! Seven! Eight!” But Bezel put a hand over the Referee’s mouth... as he was stunned, shocked... even scared. Yet the big man just pointed... pointed to Mark Paulton whom tried to stand on his feet. A new hope had came... and with it every single seat in the Arena was fled for the people to stand up from. Blood dripping down his forehead and into his eyes... Mark took his right hand, clutched the edge of his tights... pulled them forward, and let them go back to his body, snapping in the process. “Just... try... and... stop... ME!” It was as if the last word had echoed throughout the Arena, too. ... ... ... The Bezel, all seven feet of him waited in the center of the ring for Mark Paulton to enter. He grinned evilly as he saw the former First Champion attempt to climb back into the ring... So the current First Champion ran after him. Low bridge. Down went the giant and to the cement floor. Paulton, relentlessly, put the boots to him. Boot after boot after boot after boot... the Fans didn’t stop screaming... “What the hell.” He muttered, and put everything into picking up The Bezel again. But this time he did know. Mark was smart enough to understand The Tiny Tiger would be pacing behind him, so letting go of The Bezel, Paulton turned... catching the old man by surprise. A right hand. The Fans lost it. He then rushed the manager into the steal stairs. “PAULTON! PAULTON! PAULTON!” Picking up the Champion once more, he turned to the announce table. And tossed him onto it. CRASH! “PAULTON! PAULTON! PAULTON!” And he hardly wasted a second in taking The Bezel back to the ring, entering... and waiting in the corner. Waiting for the Champion to rise... So that he could become a former one. The Bezel did. Running bulldog, also known as ‘Color’. “PAULTON! PAULTON! PAULTON!” After that, he just needed a count. One. Two. Three. Glory. Mark Paulton was a five time Champion... solidifying himself as the game’s main player, and the only one to win the Title this many times. Standing up and taking what was now his, Mark fell to his knees, fell to the canvas... and fainted face-first.
