A Danger to Society: Part 1

12 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It is often said that a picture is worth a thousand words… but being the in person, wouldn’t that be worth much more than a picture? This was the day that Nate and Steve-O were going to go look at the old Fish Packing Plant that Reed Young had given them to decide once and for all what it would be used for… It just never was that easy. The home of the brothers, Nate and Steve-O, was silent. After a party like they had thrown last night, there isn’t anything in the world that could get you up before noon. So they slept, reminiscing about what had transpired the night before; the women, the booze, the naked twister while dosed with vegetable oil… Nate awoke with a smile on his face, completely nude with only half a pizza box covering his ‘South America’. He peered around; looking for a female that might be in the same boat as him so he could sneak a few peeks but for some reason the house was bare. Oh, it was still trashed and all but he was the only one around. Nate shrugged and rolled onto his back to get more shut-eye before his big day filled with slacking and bodily noises when he spotted something on the ceiling. It looked like some kind of insect but upon further inspection it was determined that this was no insect… It was a ‘Steve-O’ of the Stoner-Fuck Family of Idiots! He was duck taped to the ceiling, also in the buff with cream cheese spread around his body. Nate waved and Steve-O waved back. After the duck tape finally gave way and Steve-O fell to the ground, shattering a table and crippling a goat that neither of them had ever seen, they started to try and put two and two together. So the team of Pretty Fly sat in the middle of their kitchen staring out at the mountain of trash and old carburettors that decorated their floor. “Dude, we did have people over last night, right?” Nate said, turning to his brother. “I’m not sure, bro… maybe.” “Cause I’m thinkin’ not, dude… I remember mackin’ some chick, but it might have been a bottle of Fruit Punch… It’s creeping me out, Steve-O. I’m freaked!” “How’d we make all this mess, think it might have been the hefty bag of weed we smoked or the speed you mixed into our pizza crust?” Asked Steve-O, now concerned. “Maybe… But I think we may have finally lost it dude. Seriously, I think we are like skitzos and shit… We’re a danger to all those around us.” “Yes, I mean… We were a deadly combination before but slap a mental problem on us and the public is screwed!” Now fully aware that they were ‘indeed’ schizophrenics, they knew exactly what they must do. “Dude, it’s off to Georgie Town…” Gulp.
