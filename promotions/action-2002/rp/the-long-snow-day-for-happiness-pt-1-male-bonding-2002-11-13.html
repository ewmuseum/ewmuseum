the Long Snow Day for Happiness - pt 1: Male Bonding

13 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.13.02 Bobby Minio and Max Danger were a tag team. They were the current Action Wrestling Dyad Champions, Danger led another life in PIW, just as Minio did, except Danger was a tag champion in PIW also, with a seperate man. Minio had always really detested Max, they never got along, Minio considered Max to be what many call, a Pompus ass. However Minio saw more in Max at the recent times, he saw what was a wounded man. Much like himself. He now sat outside Danger's house, it was about noon, and Minio had been in town and decided to call Danger out for lunch. He was honking for five Minutes when Max finally came outside and walked down to the dark blue 68' Mustang. Minio leaned over and unlocked the door, Danger sat down and nodded. He looked ok. He wasn't mumbling, bumbling, it was a nice vacation from the recent days of the Danger Man. 'You won't believe what happened Bobby!' Max blurted out as Minio drove the car away from the house. 'You told me ALL about it on the phone, Marcus.' Danger wasn't done yet. 'What about how she licked my a-' 'ALL ABOUT IT.' Minio had cut him off before hearing that digusting story again, and Max for once saw a fault and stopped at it. He turned to Minio. 'Why are you taking me out to lunch all the sudden, i've been the Dyad... We've been the dyad champs for over a month or so now, and we haven't done anything.' Minio thought about it for a while. He didn't know how to answer. 'You looked like you needed a friend, Marcus. I figured i'd help my tag team partner. Our tag team move worked on Pressure Point, were a team. And with all thats happening, I figured you could use someone to talk to, that has delt with this kinda thing.' Max laughed outloud at Minio. 'I'm sure YOU'RE the one who should give me woman advice? How are things with you and Dawson again? What? You haven't seen her for two weeks now aside from on Television? You must be an EXXXPEERR----' SCREEEEEETCH. The car came to a halt and Danger's head violently slapped the dashboard. He returned upright with a pained look on his face as he rubbed his forehead. Minio looked furious at the current state of thing. 'OWW!' 'Shut the fuck up Marcus! I'm trying to give you a shoulder man, don't fuck it up!' 'Sorry, damnit!' 'Alright.' Minio looked at him for another few seconds and Max angrily gestured to him to started driving again. They began going and Minio looked over at him. 'She'll be back.' Marcus Dox looked at Minio. 'Dawson?' Minio shook his head before replying. 'Jessica. Dawson won't ever be back.' Max tried to take the initiative. 'You lost her?' Minio shook his head again. 'I never had her.' Max turned with a confused look. Minio saw it on his face and let out a sigh. This was gonna be the longest lunch of his life.
