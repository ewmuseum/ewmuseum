The Time

21 April 2002

Date: 04/21/02 Title: The Time Date: 04/21/02 4:00 pm EST Place: Shopping Mall OPEN Ca-chin! The opening of a cash register; the smell of money. The scene is set, and it happens to take place in a nice shopping mall! Coming into the building from a walkway, The Ringer cracks his fingers together and takes a deep breath. Maybe I can find some peace and quiet here! I hate having to always think about Action! and my wrestling style. I haven’t been doing the best as of late either. Walking down the hallway, ‘Prototype’ sees an interesting store. Hmmmmm, No time for shopping! Looking down at his Rolex, Ringer concedes. Well.... it’s only 4:10! Besides, I don’t have to be at the airport till 9! And even when I get to the arena, Reed will say “Oh Ringer, where were you? GET ME A DAMN COFFEE OR YOU’RE FIRED!” You know.... I should just go into the store. Opening the door, The Ringer walks into the “Shack du Raddio.” He then walks up to the scared and tattered front desk. Ringer “Hey, what do you got here?” As the clerk turns around, he replied in a squeamish way. Clerk “Ah, j’ apple Jose, commons sa pa to?” Whatever the clerk said, it didn’t sound like English to The Ringer. Of course being better than everyone did have its advantages. Oh no. I’m in the French part of this town. Sure, first they send me on a flight in second class, then I land, my stuff is lost, and finally I walk into fucking French Ville! Ringer “I speak English!” Jose “Oh, I so sorry! Welcome to Shack du.... I mean Radio Shack! I’m Jose.... how may I help you?” Ringer “What kind of name is Jose?” Jose “A....” The Ringer cuts Jose off.... Ringer “Austrian?” As Jose nods his head The Ringer storms out of Radio Shack and out of the mall.... Boy oh boy! It’s not like I hate Austrians but isn’t it coincidental? Ugh. Today is just not my day. But lately nothing has been. As The Ringer walks on outside, the weather changes and all of a sudden it starts to rain. CLOSE
