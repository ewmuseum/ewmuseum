The Lessons Continue

31 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/31/03 He was beaten. He was battered. All at the hands of Airborn Tyler. Airborn Tyler. 18 Years Old. Just a child. And Conrad Ramsey, sixty-one years young. It was bordering on child abuse, or mistreatment of the elderly. It was strange. Yet, so perfect. Both men were teaching each other lessons. Though they probably weren't aware of them. Despite the constant beatdowns, Conrad still hasn't gotten violent with Tyler. He hadn't lifted a finger against him. He's only had words for him. He only wishes to teach him. About life. But as the old man sat there, inside the home of Samantha Dox. He had long since stopped going home to Kentucky. He had continued to fly to Augusta, Maine, to visit the mother of Max Danger. But he sat there, right beside the beautiful and elegant Samantha, and wondered. Had he done the ring thing? He just laid down for Airborn. He willingly gave up the Bantam Title, the only title he had ever held, the only one he'd likely ever come to hold. Was it wise? He had thought so at first. He was just trying to teach Airborn a lesson. He, in turn, had learned some things about Airborn. That the younger generation wasn't so inclined to accept things without working for them. It was clear the Tyler wanted to take the B-Title away from Ramsey on his own terms. Conrad had taken that priviledge away from him. And now, after severe beatings at the hands of the eighteen year old, he thought about what he should do. He had to be the better man. But he also couldn't continue to let himself be pushed around. He had to do something. Drastic. He was going to have to teach the young punk, Airborn Tyler yet another lessson. Don't fuck with old people!
