Action! — Tales

KSZ(and Nick Brendon)

2 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'The King of Submission' was laying back on the couch, hands behind his head, feeted rested in the air, while his wife Jessica fed him grapes. Only he wasn't resting on the couch(he was in the bed) and his hands weren't behind his head(they were at his sides), and his wife wasn't feeding him grapes(she was laying beside him, looking at him). Danger was smiling. Like he was having a pleasant dream, only awake. Jessica let out a small laugh and asked her husband, 'What are you smiling about?' Xander turned and looked at his smiling wife and just shook his head, turning it back and staring up at the ceiling, 'Oh, nothing.' Jessica rose up, putting her hand on the side of her head and letting her elbow hold her up. 'Oh come on, what?' She was very insistent. 'Okay, jeez,' Danger said as he sat up. 'Stop the interrogation!' He crossed his arms across his chest and huffed. She straightened out her arm, leaning on it and looking at her husband. He looked at her and then shook his head, 'Alright.' She clapped her hands together giddily and then sat indian-style, awaiting what was making her husband smile so. 'Keith.' Jessica still smiled but that's all Danger said. She gave the look of 'um, what?', but Max just turned and marveled at his three-foot tall 2003 Action! King of Submission trophy. He smiled. 'God, I'm great, aren't I?' 'Beep, beep, beep. Back the truck up,' Jessica said as she grabbed ahold of her husband's forearm to draw his attention. He smirked and said, 'Did you just back the truck up?' He let out a small laugh as Jessica gave an embarrassed smile and nodded her head. She then said, 'So, what about Keith? Who's Keith? And why were you smiling about him? Wait, no, why you were smiling about a guy? That's the more pressing matter. Oh - nevermind. Zimmerman, I get it.' She just shook her head in shame. She sat in her husband's lap, facing him and put both hands on his checks. 'Why were you thinking of him, baby?' He lowered his head for a moment and then looked back up into his wife's eyes. 'I was just... thinking. Thinking about the time we met.' **FLASHBACK!!! FLASHBACK!!!! CUE FUNKY FLASHBACK MUSIC WITH PSYCODELIC IMAGES!!!!** From February 10 Pressure Point... Max Danger had cleared his throat and the Television Champion and world renowned internet writer, Keith Scott Zimmerman, turned around. The two stood there, transfixed. Unable to speak. **BACK TO REAL-TIME!!!!** 'God, we talked for hours.' Danger said as he looked longingly at the ceiling, or into space, actually, he was just looking upward. Jessica slapped him on the arm. 'Idiot. It took like ten minutes before either one of you spoke.' Danger ignored her, 'Best. Meeting. Ever. Oh, fond memories. Reminds me of the time I met Nicholas Brendon at the mall.' **FLASHBACK!!! BOOM BOOM BOOM!!! CUE TECHNO MUSIC!!!** Max Danger walked up to a man. He cleared his throat. The man turned around. 'Xander!' Max whispered out in awe. The man just stood there, looking at Max as if he were a re-re. **BACK TO REALITY!!!! CUE ELEVATOR MUSIC!!!*** 'That wasn't him, honey. I'm sorry.' Jessica said. 'Was it his twin brother then? Kelly?' Danger asked hopefully. Jessica just shook her head. She stroked Max's shaggy hair and said, 'Sorry.' 'Liar! It was Xander! Alexander LaVelle Harris! You're just trying to trick me!' 'No, babe, it wasn't him. He was like seven-foot tall! Plus, blonde hair, not exactly the same as Xand- HEY! Wait a minute!' 'What?' Danger asked. 'Did you start going by Xander just because of him and that show? Huh? Did you?' She was poking and proding, while smiling evilishly. Danger said flatly, 'No.' But Jessica just started laughing and said, 'Did too. That's so lame.' 'Shut up.' 'No, you shut up.' 'Okay.' Then they kissed, while laughing, which wasn't an easy thing to do. Danger then let out a slight whisper, 'Zimmerman.' He stretch out his arm and opened his hand Mango-style.
