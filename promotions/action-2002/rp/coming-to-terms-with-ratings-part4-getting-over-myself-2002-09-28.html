Coming to terms with Ratings part4 - getting over myself

28 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.28.02 Two hours after the last transmission. Minio still sat on his car hood. The sun was even more enveloped in the clouds. Minio was opening his second double stack from Wendys. He took a sip of his open can of vanilla coke. He enjoyed the soda, one of his latest and last joys. A smile cracked. The sun peaked. 'Maybe when Action! wrestling goes under or fires me, I can be a vanilla coke spokesmen.' The sun was gone along with the smile. The bitter joke only angered Bobby, he took another sip of the soda can then lobbed it across the empty parking lot. He watched the traffic pass by around him. He was still angry. Still deep in his own mind. Recent events had shown KroW, the one man to reveal Minio as weak, was leaving Action! wrestling. Minio wasn't relieved. He wasn't happy. He was betrayed again. He had unfinished business with the man named KroW. He had a bone to pick with him. He had to get even. He would probably never get the chance. He knew that KroW was gonna be a dedicated roster member of Pinnicle now. Minio tried to get into PIW, not to leave Action!, but because he needed more wrestling. He needed more output. However Johnson and Minio were at ends, as they had always been. It was the mocking of Jim Johnson that had left Minio almost broken. The words always repeat themselves in Bobby's head when he fails at anything. 'You dropped the ball, Bobby.' He wanted to prove Johnson, and everyone wrong. But he wasn't bringing any fans in. He didn't even know if he was bringing any money in. Would his name on a marquee sell a ticket? Does his merchandise sell? He was a slave to the ratings, just like every other paycheck aside from LLB's on Action! Wrestling's roster. People would cheer sometimes for Minio, but recently it felt like it was out of pity. Minio had always got a decent amount of people behind him, but they never asserted themselves as real fans, just people who could holler along with a punch. Who could howl along with a slap. Who could scream along with a bodyslam. Who could clap along with a pin. It had been months since he signed an autograph. He began questioning if Reed could cut Minio's contract and fire him. The ratings issue was the only thing keeping his mind off of the sweet Curves of Sweet Aroma, of the bitter snarls and sneers from the Sexy, yet bitchy Angelica Dawson, or the hurling fist from Max Danger. It was some vacation, even if it was equally as stressful. What did Minio have to do to get over anymore? Kill someone? Rape someone? Kill himself for them? For the fans? And the real question sat in the fact that if he had to kill himself for them, are they really fans? And even more relevant then that, would he ever really know? Life is so rhetorical these days.
