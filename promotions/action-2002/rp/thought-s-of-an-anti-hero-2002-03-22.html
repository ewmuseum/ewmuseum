Thought's of an Anti-Hero

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-22-02 So here I lay. I wasn’t so much spent as I was just in need of a breather. I just won another match in Action Wrestling and…honestly…it felt odd. I had the thought of facing Malone the entire time in my head. Over and over, that’s all I could think about. Areumna’derin Rahl should have been on my mind. He should have been. I was instead…hoping for Remicho to go to Resentment for a rematch with the “Man-O-War”. But he lost to him. To Joey Malone. To my friend. To my superior. I am unable to blank out those IWO thoughts. How I was undeniably the franchise player…until he came. Until I encouraged him to let it all hang out like a warrior. That’s when he embraced this business. Behind all the smoke and mirror bullshit we did…we were true friends. At least at times. There were a couple times where I beat his ass to send a message. Those were unscripted. Yeah…like that’s much a surprise. Beyond the special effects filled promos we all were forced to do, we were nothing more then fighters. This is what the business is. Get away with fighting. Do not pull punches. If you pull the punches, you get fired. Malone became a car wreck waiting to happen in every match. He would get his ass kicked and still get up for more. The business was real…which made it more amazing. He wasn’t doing what the marks think is a blade job. Those shots from chairs, and whatever the fuck else he got hit with were real. Look at the bumps and bruises after those matches. Just examine them. If you do not notice somethings that should be there…thank the great make up people. They love to cover those stitches, those welts, the cuts. Joey Malone is wrestling. Rashard Clark is nothing. I am not even in his same league. In a fight, I could have so much more ability and knowledge wise on him, but he does something crazy. Something that throws you off. He is a risk, and he knows it deep down. And he uses that to beat you. Because even if we might hate each other, we do not want to see someone else die. We might lose our job. I do not want to fight Malone again. We have never fought in a singles match…but have in a tag team battle. God…I killed everyone that night. Everyone. Nobody in that match walked the same after the beating I laid on everyone. Except I. I felt so free in that match. I felt free of all the shit I went through as a child and my young adulthood. So much heart break. So much loss… I was free of it. I let the voices inside my head dictate to me what to do. I did some vile things in that match…but it felt so good… Until I seriously hurt someone. That’s when I silenced it all. I needed to get back into the real world. I am a powerful fighter as it is… When I let that come over me…I am unbeatable. If I let that side of me out…I would not only defeat Joey…I would retire him. I do not want that. But that is the only way I can beat him. If he beats me…I will just get more depressed about it. Because the IWO days will comeback. When Joey was on top and was the biggest asshole in the world. He wouldn’t give me a shot at him…or many others for that matter. He doesn’t talk about this…but he was the bad guy the entire time. His IWO World Title reign was him being a prick to everyone. And still…I called him my friend… I worry about his ego…sometimes. If he conquers Action…he might go back into that ego trip. I am powerless to stop him too. If only there was a way to stop thinking of his bad qualities… But my mind will not let me. I just keep getting angrier. I just keep getting more depressed. I just hate this business. I am back to being what I was in the IWO. A guy who will just back him up, even when he is wrong. Because he is my friend and that’s what friends do. Joey isn’t like me. He fights sometimes and doesn’t care about the outcome. I do though. I must beat him to save our friendship. Fuck friendship for Resentment. I can not lose to him. For both of our sakes. If I fail…I do not know what I will do. These are my thoughts as I lay here. I fear for the future. And no one cares.
