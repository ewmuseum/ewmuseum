Get Out Of Jail (For Not So) Free.

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.05.2003 It was twenty minutes after 'The Danger Man' Max Danger had been arrested for theiving(no, it's not so much a word)... from his own wife. He was in the car, in the back seat. Jessica was with him. Kayden Paulton was driving. 'You know, it's true what they say,' Danger muttered quietly to his wife. She looked at him curiously and then asked, 'What's true?' Max looked at his wife, as if he was going to reveal something great and grand, but all he said was, 'What they say, didn't I just say that.' Jessica just shook her head and playfully punched Danger on the arm. 'You know what I meant. What is it that they say?' 'Prison changes a man,' he said coldly. Eyes gazing down at the floor mat. There was a moment of silence. Nothing could be heard to the breathing. That and some strange music playing on the radio, something Kayden had explained as 'techno-rap-country-pop-punk'. Then Jessica spoke, 'You never actually went into a cell, Xand!' 'True,' he said quickly in response, 'but it doesn't matter. Just being there, knowing what was to come. You can't possibly imagine. I don't want to talk about it.' He turned his head and looked out the window. Jessica just faced forward shaking her head. She had a strange husband, there was no doubt about that. Kayden, up front, finally said something. 'I'm sorry, Max. If I had known it was you, I wouldn't have called the cops. It's all my fault.' 'Which is why you paid to get him out,' Jessica snapped. It was true, he had paid to get Danger's release, even though he had only made it to the front desk before Jessica and Paulton had reached him. They hadn't even started with him yet. There was more of the strange music. No one talking. Danger looked over to his wife on his left, then up at Kayden behind the wheel. 'You bastard!' he screamed. Jessica looked at him strangely, and Kayden took his eyes off the road to do the same. Jessica said, 'That was rude, honey. Apologize.' 'Why? He nearly got my ass intruded by some big guy named Bubba's man log.' 'This isn't the Asylum, baby.' 'Don't even get me started on THAT!' More silence followed. 'Wait,' said Jessica, 'Did you say man log?' 'Yeah.' There was chuckling abound. Kayden even joined in. Jessica kissed her husband on the cheek. Danger said, 'Sounded good at the time.' Everyone continued laughing.
