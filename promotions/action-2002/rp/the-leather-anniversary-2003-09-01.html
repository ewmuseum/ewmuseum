The Leather Anniversary.

1 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Love. Dictionary.com gives many definitions for the term. But in reality, everyone has their own meaning for the word. There wasn't just one set definition. To each person, it meant something different. Commonly, no matter what meaning you choose, the biggest show of commitment is marriage. Dictionary.com also gives a few definitions for the word, and there really isn't much up for debate on what it can be. But it is the ultimate commitment to another person. It is the one thing that can truly show to someone else that you love them. It tells them that you are willing to make the sacrifices and are ready to live your life with that person forever and ever. (Please ignore divorces. If you are truly, madly, deeply in love before getting married, there wouldn't be divorces.) For three years, Marcus Alexander Dox and Jessica Michelle Dox had been showing their love for one another. Through all the pains, trails, and tribulations, their bond has remained strong. Grown stronger, in fact, as a result of the struggles. Nothing, it seemed, was going to keep the two apart. They would be, as it was said in the vows, 'Until death do us part.' That was very much the only way the two were not going to be together. And now, three years after their marriage in Hawaii, they were inseperable. They continued to show the love and affection they have for one another on a daily basis. If it's only a passing glance. Even if it's just the two sitting quietly and doing nothing. No matter what... they were expressing their love. Showing to the world the kind of remarkable bond two people can have. And on this, the third year anniversary, they were going to continue to do so. It wasn't really a big deal for either of them. They had long since decided that these occassions, much like their birthdays, were just regular days. The only day that mattered more than the others was Christmas, but that was a good five months away. So, for the third year of their union. The third year since they pledged their hearts and souls to once another. They expressed their love with... Go Karts? Yes, Go Karts. It was so obsurd. There was no real rhyme or reason for them to be doing such a thing. They weren't big fans of them in the first place. But they decided, after a nice, quiet, pancake, eggs, toast, bacon, and orange juice(Sunkist for Xander), they weren't going to do anything fancy. So they wrote down several different things they could do on little slips of paper and placed them into a bowl. There were ten things, and each of them was going to be completed before the night was done. And that, doing odd things together, was how they were going to celebrating their three years of undying devotion. Without getting into big details about everything, here is an order, in which everything happened, and a small note about each: 1. Go Karts (Didn't help Xander's back a whole lot. 'Grumble, grumble,' was heard a couple of times from Marcus.) 2. Fishing (Fun was not really had. Neither were big on the fishing. 'Yuck,' Jessica said once, holding her nose. 'This fish stink!') 3. Mooning Local Government Officals (Jessica had actually added it in. Xander wondered why, but she wasn't really sure. But they did it and ran like the dickins.) 4. Sex (Yes, sex was had. After a thrilling mooning, it was very appropriate and very convenient that sex was next on the list. And, yes, fun was had.) 5. Helping An Old Lady Across The Street (It was easy to find one, right in downtown Augusta. But, she decided she wanted to tell her entire life story, so Jessica and Xand slowly backed away and then broke into a dead sprint. 'I so didn't need to hear about her visit to the gynocologist!' exclamed Jessica, as her husband shouted out obscure facts to rid his mind of her stories.) 6. Steal Change From Mall Foundtain (Only about twenty-six cents were gotten before mall security was after them. They hid in Victoria's Secret, in one of the changing stalls. Some underwear was bought while they were there. Fun for later.) 7. Yet More Sex (Luckily, this one came after the first, otherwise it would have been odd to have yet more sex without having the first sex. This one was actually Jessica's. Xander did not complain. New underwear was fashioned... for about ten seconds before they were removed.) 8. Prank Calls (Via a payphone at a gas station, Jessica called and asked Mrs. Fleming if her refrigerator was running. Xander asked Mr. Rogers if he wanted him to be his neighbor. Mr. Rogers was only ten.) 9. Play Chess, In Park, With Old Man (Jessica won and Xander lost. The old man was an interesting guy. Fought it World War II and just generally had a lot to talk about. Much better stories than the earlier old lady. The happy couple promised to visit him again soon.) 10. Ice Skating (Neither knew how, which was strange since Xander lived in Maine his entire life was lots and lots of rinks around. They fell, the laughed, they knocked little kids and old people over. Fun was definitely had. Xander tried a triple lutz... yeah, not good on the back when he fell flat on it.) And that was their wacky anniversary. Afterwards, they grabbed a nice, quiet dinner at a small restuarant, and just enjoyed each other's company. It was nice. They also exchanged their gifts there. Jessica had gotten her husband a leather journal. Xander had gotten his wife a leather purse and a big ole kit of goodness(pens, pocketbook, etc.). They both, at the same time said, 'I don't like the leather anniversary.' They smiled, they laughed. They kissed. Oh, how great it was to be in love... with each other. (Sucks when you love someone and they don't love you.) As they continued their dinner, they were being watched. From the window outside. Bradley Duncan. He growled in his mind and stomped off as he saw Xander feeding his wife a piece of cherry pie with whipped cream. But it wasn't about him. It was about the two people inside. Completely, madly, undyingly, just totally, in love.
