Action! — Tales

Black and White.

15 May 2002

Date: 05/15/02 He repositioned himself on the chair. Where was he going? What was he doing? LLB was as lost as anyone else to be honest. At the moment and maybe even throughout his whole time in Action!, he didn’t know if he should jump off the bridge or stay on. He wanted what Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling gave him. He had missed that. But it was gone. He wanted the victories... the pain and punishment he gave and endured. That was the only reason he was in Action to begin with. Reed Young had promised this... and LLB agreed to come on in. ‘Law’ shook his head. It was PIW to a point. Well, it was as close as things could ever get... Chris Fear. Christopher Fox. KroW. Shawn Steele. Bobby Minio. The core was all there... Though LLB still sighed. He was not the Champion, yet. Being pushed off a number of Title Belts in jOlt and for now the Asylum... let alone he was not the last PIW World Champion, there weren’t many minutes that went by where LLB could actually let them roll off his back. The only thing which kept him in tact: the date grew closer. ‘Juggernaut’. May 26th, 2002. Everything of PIW could finally be born again. Until then, LLB would keep his focus. He’d try and direct it away from Titles... and more so at friendships. He still wanted the old team. Fear. Fox. LLB. He nodded. Tonight was the night to have any hope of getting them back together. And though LLB didn’t do anything after Fission had smacked Fox with Payback, he wanted to. But LLB was smart enough to know when to object... and then was not the time. Pressure Point... the trial of family was about to kick off. LLB knew where he stood. Hopefully the others did too.
