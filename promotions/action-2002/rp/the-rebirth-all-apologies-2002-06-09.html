The Rebirth: All Apologies

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Me and Joey… Boys until the end of time. Until the end of the world. Through thick and thin…we have been there at least somewhere deep inside. I know that from my side. There is a part of me that wants him to just walk off into the sunlight with Keri, into some place, and enjoy the rest of their lives. But…we both know that is almost impossible. Money still is an issue in this world. Which is why I made Reed Young make an account for Malone…upon retiring. Something that would make going into seclusion much easier. Ten million dollars. Never again would he have to worry about working…or providing for his family. It was my gift to him…for our friendship. After all…he was a war buddy. The wrestling ring is not a place where I feel safe anymore. I guess it just doesn’t matter to me. It’s my home, and until I am able to get the crowd, the fame, the pageantry of the wars out of my system, I am not leaving. That could be tomorrow…or ten years from now. It really doesn’t matter to me. Joey has tried to conform to the real world’s rules, and has been mocked by those pencil-pushing fucks who need their pensions to be happy. Deep down…we both know we are nothing more then fighters…forced to beat upon each other. Whether its fake or real…people do not know. I feel a change in my attitude and my ways. And here, I will write my apology for what happens down the road. Currently, my mind is on getting ready for Action! Wrestling and being able to just avoid a scenario where Joseph and me get into a fight. It would mean an end to both of us. My past is unclear. My destiny is muddied. I can not explain the thoughts running through my brain. Why is he such a fixation in my motives? Is there a part of me wanting war? I hope not. Because I am getting sucked deeper and deeper into depression. The pills I was given do not work. I still have an ulcer. I hate how I am. So. Sorry for what will happen. Whatever it might be can’t be that bad. Right? -Rashard Clark
