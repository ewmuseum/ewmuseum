I've Gotta Plan

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/4/02 . . . Plane in the Sky | 21:35PM CST . . . MAX DANGER Come on, pick up! JESSICA and MAX DANGER are sitting side by side on the airplane. Max is apparently attempting to make a phone call. DANGER Damn it! I got her answering machine. JESSICA She must be out. She does like to do that. DANGER Yeah, probably out getting high. Jessica hits Max on the arm in a not-so-soft way. JESSICA She doesn't do drugs! DANGER Oh right. Oh right. Jeez. Max turns his head and rolls his eyes. JESSICA I saw that. DANGER Yeah, hey Angelica. It's Max. Just call my cellphone whenever you get in. I need to ask a favor of you. Ok, bye. Max hangs up the phone. JESSICA You think she'll take on Veronica for you? DANGER I hope so. JESSICA Speaking of Veronica and thus the meeting with had with Mr. Young asking for said match, you should have pushed the raise subject more. DANGER Why? I get enough per show to get us by. It's not like we have to pay for a house or anything. JESSICA That's true, but Conrad Ramsey did burn all your clothes. All you have is your ring gear. DANGER We have your clothes. JESSICA I'm thinking you can't fit into my stuff. Max reaches into his pants and pulls on the underwear, slapping them back against his skin. DANGER I don't know, you're panties are fairly comfortable. Max smiles as Jessica just looks on in disgust and confusion. DANGER Anyway, I've got plans for our good friend Conrad. He'll wish he had never of messed with me. Max begins to rub his hands together and laugh maniacally. FLIGHT ATTENDANT Excuse me sir, but could you please keep it down? Thank you. Jessica gives a look that says 'Ha! That'll teach you to act like an idiot.'. Max just continues to rub his hands together and give a softer evil laugh. He then remembers he needs to call someone about that said evil plan for Conrad Ramsey and picks up the phone. FADE OUT
