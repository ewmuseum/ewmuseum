The Velcro Heart, The Twelfth Part of

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He stormed through the curtain, a drizzle in blood and dark chocolate. Who knew how long he had been out for... because, by now, the whole Arena was empty. More than likely Reed Young or Jeremy Bold just told the Ring Crew to keep him there, in the ring... and not wake him up. LLB stormed with a limp throughout the backstage halls, as no one was to be seen anywhere... And all looked like it had been packed up and moved. ‘The Law’ turned a corner, slamming a locker room door open as he entered in, glancing around for a clock. He caught it with the corner of his eye. It was three in the morning. LLB could only grin sarcastically as he brought his right arm up and put it into the brick wall. That’s twice Reed Young had done that to him... and it was beginning to get dumb. If you were going to put the best out there, you at least had to tell the best what’s going on. And tell the truth. Finally realizing that everyone was gone, LLB limped to Reed Young’s office for the night's past, and entered. God it was sick and disgusting... the garbage Reed would leave behind. There was junk all over the place. Candy wrappers... Paper balls... my God the paper balls... Gum stuck to the floor... Tissues all over... Stupid Reed Young. Sometimes, to LLB... he was an ungrateful idiot. “You know Reed.” He said out loud to himself, however he was barely able to talk... for he was still shaken up. “One day... the Arena’s aren’t going to invite you back.” He walked forward, going up to his desk as he ran his hands across it... feeling the spilt Vanilla Coke upon the table. “Well, at least it ant Pepsi anymore... that was always worse. It had more sugar, too.” LLB stopped for a second. That wasn’t his voice. It was someone else. He turned around. An old man stood at the doorway. Short. Maybe about five foot one or so... as his gray hair was slicked back into a type of “scrub” manner, while he wore some battered clothes, and carried a mop as he leaned against the door. “Oh, h- hi... didn’t see y- you there.” ‘The Law’ obviously had trouble speaking, being shaken up a good deal. The old man gave a friendly sigh. “You’re telling me. Look at you... covered in blood... and... um... um... um...” “Chocolate.” “Oh, chocolate. That’s what it is. For a second I thoug-” LLB shook his head no. “Oh, alright then. Well, listen, I just cleaned up Chris Fox’s old locker room... so, if you’d like to take a clean shower... please kid, by all means... go ahead.” The old man smile. LLB couldn’t help but find a good likening towards him. He was calm and gentle... yet dirty at the same time. Brushing back his sticky gray hair, the man waited for a reply. LLB broke a half-smile across his face. “Thank you sir... but no, I’m okay. I don’t think all the Windex in the world could clean up Fox’s shower stalls.” The man seemed dumbfounded. “Sorry?” “Na... nothing. It’s okay... I need to wake up first anyway.” The man still didn’t understand what was going on, but being the kind, nice person that he was, he just smiled with a nod. He walked forward, taking his lean off the door as some dirt stuck to it, giving an unsticking sound that could be heard as he came from the wall. Sick, but if you looked at LLB's blood with chocolate... that was sick too. “Name’s Bill.” He said, as he stuck out his hand. LLB didn’t even think about it. He didn’t care. He shook it. “God kid, you’re worse than me!” The Champion smiled. “Heh.” “I mean... I’m not normally this dirty... it’s just I- I-” LLB patted Bill on the shoulder as he gave him a carefree face. “It’s okay. I don’t even care.” Bill nodded, making eye contact with the young lawyer. “Thank you... it’s just- we- I-” “It’s alright, honestly.” Bill nodded a final time. “Yeah.” He looked over at his mop, and sighed. “Sorry kid... I best be cleaning this room now. My job, you know. If you still want that shower, just take Joey Malone’s old locker room, three doors after a right turn. I just cleaned that one... wasn’t much to do, there, though.” ‘The Law’ was pleased. “That sounds great. Thank you.” He shook hands with Bill again, before limping to the door and turning around one final time. “If you need any help I ca-” “It’s okay. I got it kid.” LLB agreed, and not knowing what to say any longer, he limped off to use the shower stall, while Bill walked forward, straight into some gum on the floor. “Bah.”
