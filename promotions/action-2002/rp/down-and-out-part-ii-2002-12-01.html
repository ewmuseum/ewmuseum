Down and Out. (Part II)

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.01.02 A haze filled the dimly lit, foul smelling room. He sat limply against the wall in the corner, choking back vomit as the smell of his room mixed with the cigarette smog clogged up all of his senses. Yeah, cigarette smoke. Everybody does it sometimes. He lifted the cancer stick from his lip, and tapped it a couple times, knocking ash off its end. He watched as the pile of ash on the floor next to him grew. He placed the butt back in his mouth, inhaling deeply. Life was shit. *ring* Oh fuck. *ring* The phone was only a few feet away on the night stand, but this was definitely not a time for standing. His knee pain had steadily increased over the past day or so, and it now pained him just to stand. *ring* But, hey, maybe it was good news. He rose to his feet, using the wall for support. *ring* He hobbled towards the night stand. As his answering machine started up. 'Hey, this is Steven. Away on tour probably. Don't bother leaving a message, I won't listen anyway.' Steven smirked. He cleared the haze hanging around the phone, and picked up the receiver just as his machine beeped. 'What?' 'Steven?' The sound of his sister's voice rose his spirits. He loved her. 'Yeah, yeah it's me. How's it goin' Sarah?' 'Oh, same ol' stuff. Saw your match on that new Friday show...' 'Whatever.' 'What?' 'Whatever. The show, it's called Whatever.' 'Oh, uh, right. Anyway, you looked like you got kinda hurt during the match. The website said you have a strained back. Anything I should know about?' 'Oh, uh...' He really didn't want to tell her. She'd flip if she knew he had to go under the knife. 'Well, uhh... I'm fine. Just a few back spasms, wrestling-related shit. I'm gonna be out for a little while.' 'Oh, okay. Well if you need anything I can always drive down, since-' 'No, no. It's fine. I need some time to rest anyway.' 'Oh... well, good then.' Steven messaged his forehead with his free hand. He had to end the conversation before he let anything slip. 'Hey, I was about to step out and visit the gym, so...' 'Right. Well, I'll call back in a couple days to check up on you, okay?' 'Yep.' Steven placed the receiver back on the base. Without hesitation, he collapsed backwards onto the floor. He grabbed the nearby cigarette carton and withdraw another smoke. He produced a matchbox from his pocket and lit up. Steven inhaled as much as he could, making sure to get all of the cigarette's cancer-causing goodness. He exhaled in a sigh, as he placed a hand on his aching skull. Rest was not the answer. He rarely rested anyway. Something needed to happen and that was all he had on his mind. Something, anything to take him away from everything. Something to fill his void. Something he'd always wanted to do. Something he knew he would one day do... And then it hit him. Like a bullet to the brain, it hit him: Homicide.
