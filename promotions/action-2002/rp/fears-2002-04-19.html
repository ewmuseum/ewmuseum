Action! — Tales

Fears.

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/19/2002 Unstoppable. Chaotic. Destructive. Rashard... or maybe not Rashard. I don't know any more. Maybe he's dead, replaced by this monster that he's become. Maybe he's alive, and he changed. Maybe I was just a fool for believing that he was ever nice to me. My own delusions that Rashard was just misunderstanding everything led to me getting pasted, and Keri, too. Thanks to my ignorance, Keri was in the hospital. Thanks to my failure, Keri was in the hospital. Thanks to me, Keri was in the hospital. I failed her. I failed myself. I promised myself that I would protect her, and keep her from harm. I didn't know what I would do with myself if she got hurt. Well, now I know. I'm sitting at her bedside, moping about what I did wrong tonight. That monster. He wants to rip and tear at everything that I hold dear. He wants to destroy everything I stand for. He wants to break me. Let him. ... ... Because I fear him. Not because he's bigger than I am. Not because he could destroy me. Not because whatever I can do will barely put a dent in him. I fear him because of what he can do to those I love. He's already shown that he had no remorse for my wife, and if he feels the need to, he could easilly get to the rest of my family. So, I've left myself open to weakness through my love for my friends and family. Was it a mistake? I hope not. She stirs awake. 'J-Joey?' Keri asked, as she woke up from her unconsciousness. Though Joey took a far worse beating than she did, Joey's body was used to the punishment and he came around in the hospital two hours before she did. 'I'm here.' Joey said, holding Keri's hand. 'Are you alright?' There was a pause. 'Joey, what happened to Rashard?' she asked. 'I don't know. All throughout that match, he was saying stuff about how I 'screwed him' or how I would 'never use him again'.' Joey said, shaking his head. 'And all this while, I don't know what he was talking about.' Keri sighed and laid her head back on her pillow. 'I'm sorry.' Joey said. 'For what?' Keri asked. 'I was the one who got in the ring, Joey. You have nothing to be sorry for, it wasn't my place. I just... didn't want to see you get hit any more.' Joey sighed. 'Keri.' Joey said, not sure how to put what he wanted to say into words. He gave it his best shot. 'I don't think I can protect you from him.' Keri choked back her sobs and hugged her husband. 'Don't worry about it, Joey. I'll be fine.' 'I hope so.'
