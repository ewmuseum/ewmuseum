True Love

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/24/02 Beautiful, dark blonde, shoulder-length hair. The blueest of blue eyes, staring right back at you. Lucious red lips. Sparkling, white, teeth smiling at you. 'Jessica.' A hand runs down the picture. 'I miss you.' Something wet falls onto the picture. A person falls back on the couch, and kisses the picture. The picture is then moved away from the person's face revealing Max Danger. He has tears running down his checks, as he stares at the picture. From behind the couch we see Conrad Ramsey. He looks down at Max looking at the picture. 'So, tell me, how did you two meet?' Max looks up, seeing Conrad, then leans up a bit. Conrad walks around the couch with a coffee mug in hand, and takes a seat next to Max. He takes a sip of the coffee, then looks over at Max. 'Well?' 'It was a rainy day in California on the 12th day of May in the year 2000...' Max looks at the picture, as the screen gets all wiggly and fades away. There we see a younger, shorter-haired Max Danger. He has a slight limp as we walks. He has a long, black jacket on, with a black hat, black business shoes, and under the jacket is a black business suit, blue tie, gold watch, and a huge package possibly containing important documents. Max proceeds to the front doors and heads inside. Quickly, he removes his hat and walks up the the front desk. There a lovely young lady, in her early to mid-twenties, in a tight fitting red dress sits, with long dark blonde hair, baby blue eyes, and a pair of lips that you would want to kiss all day and night. She looks up, and notices a wet man standing there. This woman is obviously Jessica. Jessica: Hello, and welcome to Sunkist Growers. May I help you? Max Danger: In more ways than one, I'm thinking. Max flashes his million dollar smile, unfortunately she wasn't really looking. Jessica: Excuse me? Max Danger: Oh nothing. I'm here to see Mr. Jones, is he in? Jessica: Do you have an appointment? Max Danger: Yes. Jessica: Name? Max Danger: Yes, most people do. Max smiles again, as Jessica gives him a look to say 'come on, be serious'. Jessica: What is it? Max Danger: Tell me yours first? Jessica doesn't seem to happy with this game of cat and mouse, but her girlish smile says otherwise. Jessica: Jessica Barrett. And yours? Max Danger: Max Danger. Jessica: That's an odd name. Max Danger: It isn't my real name, just my wrestling name. I'm a wrestler. Wanting to get it out of the way at the beginning, he can tell she isn't a fan, but he had to know what she thought of it to start, rather than wait until it got serious and having her dispise him for it. Jessica: Oh, that fake stuff on TV. Max Danger: It's not fake! I lost my brother in the ring! Max got a little more anger than he should have been, and you can tell Jessica wasn't expecting the outburst. Max quickly let his anger fade, however. Jessica: I'm sorry. I didn't mean to offend you. I have never seen a wrestling show before, so I just went with the close-minded answer. Max Danger: Most uninformed, idiotic people do. Not saying you are overall stupid person. You are just stupid on this subject. My career. Jessica: Yes, I am. And I apologize. Well, you do have an appointment. But why would a wrestler be here at Sunkist? Max Danger: To make money of course. Max again flashes his smile, which gets a smile from Jessica as well, apparently putting that little outburst behind them. Jessica: Oh. How are you going to do that? Max Danger: I can't discuss it right now. I've got to talk it over with Mr. Jones first, then I'll tell you about it. Ok? Jessica gets a little suspicious with that answer, as he could be going to murder everyone and take their money. But just looking at Max, she could someone tell that wasn't the case. Jessica: Alright. Max Danger: So, what floor is he on? Jessica: Fifth floor, third door on your left. Max turns around, ready to walk out, before he stops. Max Danger: Thanks. You free tonight? Max turns back around, and Jessica raises her head up, after having looked down at some papers. She seems confused, but Max has the look of confidence on his face. Jessica: Y.. Yes. Why? Max Danger: Because I'm taking you out to dinner. Sound good? Max smiles his big, bright smile, as Jessica doesn't answer right away. Finally she snaps out of the shock of it all and answers. Jessica: Definitely. Max Danger: Cool. See you later, after this meeting. Jessica: Ok, bye. Max Danger: Bye bye. Max smiles as he turns away. Jessica is also smiling and watching as Max heads to the elevator. The screen gets all wiggly again and we fade back to Conrad and Danger on the couch, with Danger looking down at the picture of his wife. 'That's a very vivid memory you have there, kid.' 'It was the greatest moment of my life. There's no way I'd be able to forget it.'
