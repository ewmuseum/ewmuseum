Ship Wrecked - Part One

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27/02 Smoked consumed the room. In the center of the large, greyish cloud was the duo of Nate and Steve-O. The brothers were strung out on theier living room floor, passing a long, flat joint back and forth. Puff, Puff, Pass. Puff, Puff, Pass. And so on. “Dude, I’m not feelin’ so hot, are you sure you cleaned off all that oven cleaner you spilled on the papers?” Nate spoke up, attempting to pass it to his heavily stoned campadré, Steve-O. Though the fact that his vision was hazey and everything was in pink and swimming with gigantic elephants didn’t help the situation. “Um,” Steve-O shifted his eyes from side to side, “…Of course I did.” And took another long drag. “Okay.” This is about the same time the soft melody of beatnik bongo drums started. Their world began to spin and within the span of five measly seconds. They were out cold. Off to dream world, but not the dream world we know- a stranger, more trippy dream world where humans walk on their hands and the Humpty Dumpty chip company runs the white house. And pretty much everything else for that matter. Waves. Breasts. Two Surfers. Wow, it didn’t take long for their weed-abused minds to come up with some sort of drug induced scenario: a long, white cruise ship, the ocean, Pretty Fly, and what would this dream be without some hard core nudity. Half a dozen beautiful, super models loomed over the brothers who were stretched back in yellow lawn chairs. They wore knee-length Hawaiian shorts, personalised with their names written down the outside of their right thigh in white bubble letters. “Yeah, so then I looked up and was all like, I’ll Be Back.” Steve-O said as he finished telling a long draw out story closely related to the Terminator movies only replacing Arnold with himself. And for some reason, all the women, including the sly deck hand that wore a three foot tall top hat, bought it. Gasping and sighing with pleasure, just basking in the warm glow that is. Pretty Fly. “Half naked women, look what Dr. Nate has created…” He held up a lighter and flicked on the flame… “I like to call it… Gass Light that Hurts.” All the women gasped as if they had never seen it before. “You mean fire, dude?” Steve-O replied while he watched some big breasted red head suck his big toe. “Exactly.” At the other end of the ship, a disgruntled sea captain that slightly resembled Bruce Willis screamed in horror as he gazed through his long, black telescope. “ISLAND! And we’re headed straight for ‘er! I should warn the others…” He scratched his chin before taking another look through the telescope. “DEAR GOD! There’s no time… Fuck it!” And jumped ship… Ultimately striking his head on a rock pertruding from the water and dying a slow… salty death. Seconds later, it is revealed that the island is exactly seven point four miles away and that the Bruce Willis sea captain was just insane. Very, very insane. There’s no way that an entire cruise ship could crash into an island on a clear Summer’s day, is there? Many, Many Hours Later. CRRRRRRAAAAAAASSSSSSSSSSSHHHHHHH! “What the hell was that, dude?” Nate looked up. He was flat on his face after being knocked from his chair. The cruise ship had hit something. Suddenly Steve-O looked over board, turned back, and answered the question that had been plaguing everyone on the ship for a good fifteen seconds. “Um…” Steve-O stared down at the sandy beach in front of him, “I think we may have just hit some sort of island.” “Radical,” Nate sprang up from his chair, “see any buried treasured?” Pause to give Steve-O time to scan the beach for ‘buried’ treasure. “Nope, they must of like… hid it or something.” “Maybe they put the treasure in the ground for safe keeping… I say… We go looking for it! It couldn’t be that hard… I mean pirates aren’t that smart. I’ll need a bucket… a shovel… and five grams of weed STAT!” The next thing we see if Nate and Steve-O leaving the cruiseship and walking into the jungle with a child’s Star Fish Shovel and Pale... Off to whether their minds decided to take them.
