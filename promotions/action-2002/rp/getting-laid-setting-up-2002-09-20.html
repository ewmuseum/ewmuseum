Action! — Tales

Getting laid - setting up

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th September 2002 For Airborn Tyler, life was that big heffer that blocked the doors you so desperately wanted to get through. Whenever you wanted to accomplish something, there would always be something else to hinder your process. That thing could be a cheeseburger, a shotgun or a wrestler, but you could bet that it would rear it’s ugly head at precisely the wrong moment. Airborn often wondered why his life had taken the course it had taken. As a kid he’d been very much against violence, and it had honestly scared him. Why would people want to beat the guts out of each other and make another human being bleed for the sake of pure enjoyment? Instead, Airborn had preferred to get his enjoyment from sports like synchronised swimming. Synchronised swimming?!! Hardly sounds like a background for entering the skew whiff world of sports entertainment, but it had actually helped Airborn become the athlete he is today. One of the main skills required to become a successful synchronised swimmer, just like it’s a skill required to be a successful professional wrestler. In the swimming pool, you need to do everything with the exact same grace at the same exact time as your team mates, otherwise marks will be docked and you’ll be isolated as the weak link the team. Similarly, in wrestling, contrary to popular belief, the two athletes are actually working together as a team. If one person needs to turn around into a superkick, they need to swivel at the precise moment required otherwise they’re liable to either have a teeth knocked out, or even worse, make the offence comparable to a big pile of horse shit. Without timing, a wrestler is nothing. There are other small comparisons that can be made. Synchronised swimming is all about creating something easy on the eye, and therefore grace is of the utmost importance. If a participant chooses to flap around like a fish in distress then it defeats the aim of the sport. And Airborn Tyler in a wrestling ring tells a story. He doesn’t tell his story with words written on a piece of scrap paper, aided by a few crayon pictures. Airborn Tyler tells his stories with facial expressions and action. Every one of Airborn’s actions is graceful. Watch him in the ring someday. No one movement is wasted. Every muscle extraction or contraction has a purpose, and each facial expression gives off the emotions he is feeling at that moment in time. These ingredients had the internet fans raving about this young prodigy. Back during his PCW stint he had been marked as an unreliable worker who was too stiff and took too many liabilities with his opponent’s physical health. In EYE he was pulled out by the management because he was considered too green. In Titans of Wrestling he was finally given a little rub of the green when they handed him the Titans Championship, and gave him the ball to run with. The main thing about Airborn in ToW wasn’t his in-ring work (which remained rusty) but his backstage segments, which told a deep and enthralling story of a man on the run. Three months after Titans of Wrestling closed to contractual disagreements between the management and the boardroom, Airborn Tyler re-surfaced in Action! Wrestling, and his debut whisked up a storm on the internet. He was immediately compared to such prodigies as Joey Malone, Kurt Angle and Jonathan Conspiracy. What one element did those three sports entertainers hold above all others? They were technically sound in the ring. During his three months as a free agent, Airborn discovered a new work ethic that helped build him up as a smooth and visually acceptable wrestler. No one could believe that the Airborn Tyler whom appeared on 30th June 2002 at Action! Till Death Due Us Part was the same Airborn Tyler who appeared on 27th March 2002 at ToW Paranoia II. He’d retained his dramatic abilities, and debuted a brand new wrestling side to his a-game. He has finally shoved the critics damming words back into the keyboards they came from, and put the past behind him. Established as a credible threat in the world of professional wrestling, Airborn Tyler is opening up a new chapter in his career. With on-screen cousins ‘Luscious’ Lee Lightning and ‘Daunting’ David Dew (a tag team from WWR and jOlt) behind him, his talents are about to be pushed to the limits, in an effort to tell a story of three family members looking to change the world in a positive way. And on the flip side are three men who realise that despite their convincing argument, the A Star Stable are wrong in their beliefs. Jonny Yankee has been wronged by Airborn Tyler, but with his army backing him up, a moral war is about to break out. Six men are going to try and prove that their theory is correct. Who will win? *** “Go on! Skin him, WELL DONE MY SON! Come on, watch the tackle, SHOOT, SHOOT!” enthused Airborn Tyler, on the edge of his seat, eyes glued to the television set which flickered in the corner. On it was a soccer game, with the player in the red shirt skipping around the blue defence, with the ball seemingly glued to his feet. ‘Luscious’ Lee Lightning shook his head in the corner, laughing quietly to himself. His young cousin certainly had a passion for anything he did, but it was a passion that could perhaps be channelled a little better; channelled into something with more significance than a meaningless soccer game. Something like a Pay-Per-View match on Sunday with Jonny Yankee. “GOOOOOOAAAAAAALLLLLLLLL!” screamed Airborn at the top of his voice. Leaping out his seat, he lifted his shirt up to cover his face and ran around the room blindly, arms flailing around in the air. The television commentator seemed equally delighted, shouting about how the viewers had just witnessed one of those moments that would be repeated forever throughout history. Lee buried his head into his newspaper, trying to hide his laughter. He found his cousin’s actions quite pathetic really, and almost offensive. People were dieing all over the world, and instead of acting like a prat Airborn could be reading up on these tragic events and paying tribute to the heroes that kept the world turning round. Still, it was quite amusing at the same time. “Did you just see that?” said Airborn as he came to a jolting halt, running straight into the lampshade and crashing into the corner amongst a pile of loose papers that were sent flying into the air. Lee finally dropped the newspaper down onto the oak dining table in front of him, and turned his head right to see Airborn stumbling to his feet. “You’re going to clean that up right?” said Lee, half asking half ordering. Airborn shrugged his shoulders. “Yeah yeah, later, but did you see that goal? Honestly Lee, instead of depressing yourself about how some old biddy has a burst water pipe and is going to die of thirst, you should watch some of the soccer! I mean, he just took the ball around three people like they were invisible, and then lobbed it over the goalkeeper to complete a wonderful move. Honestly, goal of the year, right there!” “That ‘old biddy’ might well have been the cornerstone of our society for fifty years. People like her have children, keep our Earth going through good times and bad, and you know what?” Lee raised his arms and pointed at the window, “women are ten times better than men at maintaining democracy! They appreciate the vote. They fought hard for it, they use it. Most males cannot even be bothered to walk the ten and a half metres to the polling station.” Airborn didn’t know whether too look bemused or confused. How did his cousin manage to work democracy into every little conversation. “Hey, dude, we’ve left the arena now okay? You’re not preaching to a choir now, just an uninterested soccer fan who wants to see if Manchester City can come from behind to win! WOO!” Lee shook his head. “I don’t just say what I say on the shows for fun, I say it because I believe them.” Lee thought for a moment. “Hey, you want to go out tomorrow night?” Airborn thought for a moment, before nodding absent mindedly. “Sure, whatever.” Lee wasn’t entirely convinced his companion had heard him, but he didn’t care. He’d show Airborn how democracy was the way to go, and he would also show him how to get a girlfriend. He had a girlfriend himself, and her sister was very good looking.
