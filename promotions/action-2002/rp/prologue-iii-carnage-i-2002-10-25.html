Prologue III - Carnage (i)

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th October “Leon.” … “LEON!!!” Huh…oh, yeah. Leon was miles away, still thinking about last night’s conversation with Natasha. They’d had that arguments hundreds of times, but there was something about her tone last night, something that made Leon think that this relationship which only 24 hours ago had felt timeless, was perhaps reaching its conclusion. He knew he’d do almost anything for Natasha… but give up his dream? That was like self-sacrifice, and he didn’t know if he could do that for anyone, not friends, not family, maybe not even Natasha. “Ok, Here’s John” ‘Great’, Leon thought to himself, but managed to keep a straight face. This was the big shot stunt man that had worked with all the stars, and what a shocker he was another 6’2 plus, god knows how heavy, stuntman. Everything that Leon wasn’t. To say Leon was not enthusiastic about working with John was the understatement over the century. “In the scene he gets to grab you and jump off the rails before the train hits, ok?” Leon nodded, he hadn’t really said anything to the director today, cuz he knew he’d only say something insulting, and this film was important to his career. There was just something about the guy that rubbed him up the wrong way. Leon headed onto the rail and waited for his saviour ‘John’ to grab him and hurl him to safety. He did. Perfectly. “And cut!” “That was easy, you’re pretty light. Ya look like you’d weigh more.” John then let out some laugh that was somewhere between confident and cocky. To Leon he just sounded like an arrogant fucker. Yeah… The whole situation was beginning to be more embarrassing than Leon could handle. He was hoping that Hobson would have given him some good stunts to execute today, and maybe he could impress this ‘John’ guy, and then maybe he could get a break out of it. Maybe. Leon wanted to be a household name, he wanted to be the stuntman to the stars, and eventually… he wanted to BE the star. “Next scene!!!” Leon nonchalantly walked over to the director once again, almost the contradiction of John who strode over, and parked himself directly in front of Hobson, a fake cheesy smile on his face, and an eagerness that for some reason sickened Leon. “Ok, so Leon, you’re gonna be lying there in the road, and the car will be heading at you. Now John will jump in help and you to your feet, before turning round to see the car coming. He’ll push you out the way before jumping to safety. Ok?” Leon was beginning to hate this film more and more. Not only had he apparently not read the script very well, and found that the story was, well, shit, he’d found that his role could easily be played by a bag of potatoes in clothes. He was fed up with it, and he was gonna do something about it. “Action!” Leon followed the script perfectly until John helped him to his feet. Leon looked at the oncoming car and knew what he was about to do was probably stupid, but he couldn’t stop himself. As John turned round Leon grabbed him round the waist and hurled him out of danger, and as the car came careering closer to him he leapt up, straight up, just clearing the windscreen, and came tumbling down on top of the roof of the car, rolling down the back and onto the tarmac. Silence. Noone had expected to see anything like that, and when Leon got to his feet there was some stifled applause and congratulatory comments… but not for long. “WHAT THE FUCK WAS THAT?!?” Leon turned to see Hobson red in the face, spit flying out of the end of his megaphone. “While you’re working on my film, you’ll do my stunts… AND YOU’LL DO THEM WELL!!!” Leon stood there, unfaltering. He knew he was in the wrong, and there was nothing he could say to change that. “You’re lucky I’m in a good mood pal, or you’d be fired and replaced quicker than you can say ‘Angry midget’. Ill see you tomorrow morning, 6 am… make that 5, and this little attitude will have been gone, ya got me mister?” Asshole. The one word spinning round Leon’s head… but he knew there was nothing he could do. He’d let his anger get the best of him and fortunately barely got away with it. Hobson was the biggest director in the Miami area, and this was an opportunity he couldn’t waste. This film would literally make or break his career… and the way his life was heading at the moment, it looked like his career wouldn’t be broken. It would be eradicated.
