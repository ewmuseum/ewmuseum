Life With The Malones: Day One

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/01/2002 Hello. Ali suggested that I keep some sort of internet log or something so that I can stay in touch with my fans and give them insight on what it's like to live with one of the top wrestlers of the world, today. Now, I don't know about you, but I don't think I have too many fans. At least, that's what I figure when I overheared some fans after Down 4 The Count and they're like 'who's the guy with the strange hair?' or 'who's the kook with the funny name?'. I'd say that it lowered my self-esteem, but unfortunately, I had none to begin with. No, I don't own my own computer. I'm stuck using Joey's. And it's not exactly the greatest thing in the world, either. I guess I'm too spoiled with my internet cafes and whatnot, but I guess Joey's just out of touch with the technological world. So, anyway, I moved in with Joey a few days ago, into a neglected room in his house that neither he nor Keri could find a use for. So, needless to say, I rented it out. I must note that this is a vast improvement over what I was previously living in... a run down apartment in a hovel in downtown New Orleans. From what I understood, the building was on the verge of being condemned. The first thing I noticed was how CLEAN the place was. Whenever Joey and Keri can find the time in their busy schedules(they're usually home on the weekends and Monday), they just clean the damn place up. Since no one really bothers to stain the place, they're usually done with the cleaning after about an hour. They even did my room before they actually found a use for it. No wonder Joey hung out with janitors all the time, a few years ago. Another thing I noticed was the fact that they don't really have much in the way of 'organized family outings', mainly because of the fact that they're kind of out of touch with what to do. They had missed all of the good movies of the year because of what happened with Rashard Clark, and nothing right now interests them. Plus, with the baseball strike and everything, Joey's sports interests are also shot to hell. So, mainly, they just play Scrabble, and are extremely happy that I can get involved. The thing I noticed is just how much I suck at Scrabble, though. Keri can bring out these crazy words like 'nirvana', 'silver', or 'legend'... what have you. Joey tried to use things like 'sparta', 'zeppo', 'camelot', and other things that don't exist. Meanwhile... well, uh... I'm pretty used to using three letter words. Needless to say, Keri kicked our collective asses numerous times in the game until we switched to Monopoly, and at that point, we started to fare better. So, for those keeping score at home, don't ever play Joey's wife in Scrabble. Ever. And finally, I've noticed that Joey and Keri tend to take forever in actually leaving the place for their flight to whatever crazy place Reed Young books us. I think it's because Keri's a girl and they tend to take forever in choosing an outfit or five for their trips. Who knows. Anyway, I think they're ready, so I guess we'll see you all at Pressure Point. - C.A.
