Date: 6/21/02 Over the years, a person must undergo changes. Some for the better, some for the worse. In the wrestling business, everyone is constantly changing, always evolving, trying to become better, trying to obtain more success, trying to reach the top of the mountain. When one's personal life interferes with one's professional life, everything is flipped upside down, and nothing seems right or normal. Despite its simplicity, that explains Max Danger's life perfectly. 'What I have to do now, is something I've never had to do before. I don't think anyone in this business, has had to do what I'm having to do. I've been forced to live in a small town in Kentucky with my 'partner' Conrad Ramsey.' That's the voice of Max Danger you hear. It's not a happy or cheerful tone, but rather full of disdain. 'My sworn enemy, the man whom I defeated at Juggernaut. I broke the man's false teeth, for crying out loud. How does this arrangement make sense to anyone?' You can just tell how much Max Danger hates what Reed Young has made him do. He utterly hates it. 'My wife Jessica is a recovery drug addict, and I can't be there to help her through it all. She's at a clinic in New York, while I waste away from boredom in Kentucky, just waiting for the next plane to the next Action! event.' When Max mentions his wife, you can hear the saddness he has for her situation, and how much love he has for her. 'My adopted daughter Kayla was taken away from me, and she's going from foster home to foster home in Vermont, until the court hearing. Due to my career, and due to the situation, I will probably never get her back again.' **SNIFF** Max was beginning to break down as he mentioned Kayla. 'But this is my life. This is what I have chosen to do. Everyone has to go through changes. Everyone has to adapt. I am no different. So adapt I will.'
