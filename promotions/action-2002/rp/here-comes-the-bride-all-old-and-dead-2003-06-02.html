Here Comes The Bride, All Old and... Dead?

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.02.2003 Conrad Ramsey had lost, yet again, to Max Danger. He had never beaten the King of Submission. And he was never going to. The rivalry had ended. Juggernaut II marked the finish. Conrad didn't feel bad about it though. It wasn't important. He had lost to the better man. It was as simple as that. He had bigger things coming up, anyway. That little thing called marriage. To Danger's own mother, Samantha Dox. The two had quickly decided against a big wedding. In fact, they invited no one. Oh, Xander, Jessica, Bradley, and a handful of others knew when they were gettin' hitched. And they knew where. But they weren't going. 'Cause Samantha and Conrad were heading to Las Vegas. Sin City. Elvis was going to marry them. Hooky? Yes. But it's what they wanted to do. They had rooms reserved for the MGM Grand. The largest hotel in the world. (Fun Fact: If you stayed in every room of the MGM, and never the same room twice, it would take you nearly 14 years. No lie.) After Ramsey had 'kissed the bride', the newly married couple headed down the strip. They stopped in front of the Bellagio hotel and watched a water foundation show set to Tony Bennett. Beautiful. They shared a bike-carriage(only of those carriages, only with a person riding a bike to pull you - like the Chinese carts with the person, or the horse-drawn carriages of New York... y'know?). It was magical. The best night of their lives. They gambled. They made love. They gambled some more. They were in heaven. Couldn't be happier. It all came to an end when Conrad Ramsey woke up Monday afternoon, after the two took a nap from walking the strip all day long and hitting the shops in the Aladdin. He went to the bathroom. He came out, washed his hands, and then walked back to the bed in room 548 on the 10th floor. 'Samantha?' he called, trying to wake her. She didn't stir. He thought her faking. He got back into the bed with her and wrapped his arms around her. 'Time to get up, sweety. Big night ahead of us.' There was no answer. 'Are you cold? You seem cold?' The air conditioning wasn't on though, even despite the 100+ weather. He turned Samantha over onto her back. Her eyes were shut. She wasn't breathing. No pulse. 'Samantha?' Conrad said with a whimper.
