Toiletry Terror [Part 2 of 6]

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 14 The duo split, each running down a separate hallway. Some banging and clattering can be heard as the pair prepare themselves for the salvation of this loo. The first to come to attention is the John. He strolls down the hall wearing a silver body suit which matches his silver mask. He pulls on the second of his pink dishwashing gloves before straightening his cape. He steps forward in his pink cape. He makes his way over toward a bottle of Toilet Duck which sits on the couch. He lifts him up into the air and looks in its eyes……or the area they might have been rather. “Toilet Duck, though you’re a fan of the Bold and the Beautiful it pains me to tell you’ve been called into duty along the John’s side.” He pulls a small yellow cape from his pocket and ties it around where a bottle of Toilet Duck’s neck might be. “The Action! League has been called into duty and time is running out. My faithful sidekick…” He stops as if the bottle of Toilet Duck were talking to him. “…apologies. OUR faithful sidekick, Captain Obvious, will be prepared as will yourself and the John, Toilet Duck. A toilet beckons our duty and we must save her before it’s too late.” The skinnier man, Captain Obvious as the John had called him, stands in the frame of the hallway door. His arms folded across his chest as though he too were ready for action. His costume consists of a navy blue bodysuit with white gloves and matching white boots. On each respective boot and glove the letter “L” or “R” appear. His name printed boldly down the outer of his legs and on his chest reads “Captain Obvious”. He grins and nods to his partner in crime. “POOP” The John returns the favour before turning his attention to Toilet Duck. “Let’s roll!” The trio burst from the room, out a door which leads to the outside world and the safety of the thunderbox in need. Captain Obvious slams the door behind him. The trio are off on their adventure to help the helpless… …to save the needy… …to aid the… The door flies open and the John trudges in unhappily. He looks around and sees the coffee table for trudging along toward it. He snatches something off the table which jingles in reaction. “Well the John thought you had the keys, didn’t he? Sheesh, does the John have to remember everything?” Slamming the door behind him, his cape gets caught. We hear a loud thud as he falls backward into the door. It creaks open. The cape is removed. They finally get on their way…
