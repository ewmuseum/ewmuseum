Action! — Tales

Self doubt

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Airborn Tyler sat on the sofa in the A-Star Stable locker room, relaxing in his street clothes of blue jeans and a plain black t-shirt. The Pay-Per-View was just one hour away, and everywhere people were running around frantically, making last minute perparations, running over matches with their opponents. Airborn didn't need to make any such preparations, he knew exactly what he was going to do, and if it went off without a hitch, he would be walking out of Washington D.C as the Bantam Champion, only the second championship ever to hang around his waist in his career. The first one came from Titans of Wrestling, a troubled promotion which raised a few eyebrows and eventually shut down. On it's last show, a Pay-Per-View, Airborn was wrestling Jake Keeton for the Titans Championship, the ToW equivalent of the Bantam Championship. The match was back and forth, and like normal Airborn grappled a break neck speed which impressed everyone who was watching. However, it wasn't one of his high flying shenanigans which won him the match. Jake had bounced off the ropes with Airborn standing dazed in the middle of the ring, unaware of his surroundings after missing a big move. Jake flung himself at Airborn with all the might he could muster, and the spear that connected was devastating. The wind was driven out of the recipient by the high velocity shoulder that buried itself deep into stomach. The only problem was that Airborn had moved out of the way, and Jake had just speared his five year old kid, who had somehow found his way into the ring. What Airborn did next ate him up for a while, but as he told himself it was just part of the job. Jake cradled his son in his hands, praying for a miracle and that his son would be fine. Airborn saw a window of opportunity and rolled Jake up for the three count, before making a hasty exit so that the child could get the suitable medical help. For the next couple of weeks leading into the next show Airborn was terrified that Jake would dismember him when they next met, but that meeting never came, and they have managed to avoid each other since. So as Airborn refreshed all this in his memory, a shiver tingled his spine. He looked down at the special PPV gold and silver shiny tights that lay on his lap. Once a month those tights would come out of the wardrobe and replace his blue and green paintbrushed tights that he normally wore. For some reason, a wrestler takes great pride in his performance at a PPV and pulls all the stops. For Airborn, pulling all the stops meant some crazy stunt, or in the case of Last Laugh a month previous, three crazy stunts. Being chucked off the stage and doing two shooting star presses from a balcony in one night was insane. And it was something Airborn couldn't explain. He maintained that he didn't do what he did to entertain the fans and gain their support, but most were of the opinion that was his motivation. Whenever he pulled on the gold and silver trunks, the adrenalin was released from the brain, he stopped thinking rationally, and something inside him insisted on pulling some insane dive. Critics said that while this definitely entertaining to watch, fans expectations would get higher. Every month as the stunts became more daring and more spectacular, the pressure on Airborn to do them increased. If Airborn didn't do a major dive the event was considered a failure for him, and that is no pressure to be placed on an 18 year old. The chances are that he would be a cripple before his twentieth birthday, and that didn't bode well if he wanted a long twenty year career. Airborn had almost resigned himself to the face that he wouldn't last long in the industry, but if the price to pay to survive was cutting his style down then he wasn't going to open the coffers of his wallet. He'd just have a damn short career. Standing up, Airborn wandered over to a mirror, and looked back at the ugly face looking back at him. It was a face that showed he was definitely up for it tonight, in the mood to do something so crazy that people would wonder how he did it. The buzz inside him was rising from his toes and up his body, until eventually it would reach his mind and he would do something daring, most likely in the ring against Conrad Ramsey. Conrad Ramsey. That 61 year old fart was the only thing preventing him from emerging as the second top guy in Action, behind Joey Malone the champion, or Syphon Fission or Kent Anthason as the case may be by the end of the night. His brothers were Dyad Champions, so perhaps he felt a little like he had something to live up to. Could he beat Conrad? He was experienced and had a lot of ring savy, no doubt. The vast array of holds and submissions in his collection was outstanding, and to win Airborn would have to escape those wrinkly clutches. The chair stipulation would be an equaliser, but could his high flying win him the match, or would he be grounded by the Achilles Tendon Hold? With a shake of the head, Airborn dropped his tights on the floor and walked out of the room in search of his brothers, leaving the room dimly lit by a lamp. Marching down the corridor, he entered the pre-PPV chaos. Everyone knew something major was going to happen tonight, but what matches or segments would they happen in? What was going to happen? And who would be involved? What would the consequences be? Who would emerge as a major Player and who would droop as the Pawn?
