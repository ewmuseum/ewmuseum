TAFKADX I

31 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Artist Formally Known as Disciple X. A large black boot sharply cut through the air… and then another… The pounding of thunderous footsteps echoed throughout the facility. 'Oh, it's only you.' A pair of sneaker's joined the band, and the thunderous footsteps grew louder. 'Where are we headed?' 'Duh.' A deeper, crisp voice exclaimed. 'All I did was ask… asshole…' It was a given. Shaw sighed, and followed closely behind Liam, who rolled his eyes with relief. 'I thought you got lost… where the heck were you?' Derik mumbled. 'Got lost? Pft! I work here, remember?' 'Riiight...' Ump replied. 'Anyways, where were you then?' Shaw moaned, and hung his head low. 'I have no idea.' 'That's so sad…' Ump laughed, and continued to progress down the hallway. 'Shut up, fathead…' Shaw continued to follow Ump, with a dirty look spat across his face. Ump just shook his head, and ignored Derik's comment. 'Where the hell is this place?' Derik questioned. 'Beats me…' Ump didn't give up though, continuing to march without end. He looked back a few times, occasionally eyeing the markings on the doors, but none satisfied him. 'I wanna go home!' Shaw shrieked, throwing his arms over Umps back. 'Go then! I'm not forcing you to stay here...Reed ordered ME to do this job.' Derik looked to his left and then to his right. '…but… but… I don't know the way out…' Shaw stuck closely to Liam's side. Ump just ignored him, while at the same time, thanking God he wasn't born like Derik - that he wasn't born stupid. 'Oh look, it's Bold...' Ump gasped in relief as he progressed towards Jeremy. 'Yay! Maybe he'll know the way out, eh?' Shaw happily implied. 'Ugh, moron. Even I know the way out…I'm going to go and ask him if he knows the whereabouts' of Domino...' Ump sauntered over to Bold and cast his arm on top of Jeremy's shoulder. 'Why, hello darling...' 'Ewww...!' Ump pitched his arm off of Bold in an instant. 'WTMF?' Bold turned around, confused… His cheeks turned a bright red, as he hurled his hand over his head, brushing his hair back. 'I thought you were Reed...' 'Ewwwww!...' Shaw and Ump said, disgusted. 'I mean...um...I thought it was...Mae Young...!' 'Ewww...' 'Whitney Houston?' 'Too skinny.' 'Pamela Anderson?' 'Contagious.' 'Britney Spears?' 'Whore.' 'Oh, shut up...' Bold unrelentingly progressed towards Young's office, but Ump had every intension of stopping him. 'Hey, Jeremy… wait… ' 'Yea?' Jeremy replied. 'Do you have any idea where Domino might be?' Ump questioned, hoping Bold knew his location. 'Hmmm...' Bold scratched his head. 'Screwing pizza? AHAHAHAH! AHAHAH~!' Jeremy let out a historical laugh, and fell to the ground on his back 'Um…' 'Oh, not funny?… Damn…' Bold regained his balance, and dusted the dirt off his shirt. 'Last time I saw him, he was headed that way...' Jeremy flung his hand out, and pointed further down the hallway. Ump nodded. 'Thanks.' 'Alrighty, later hommies...' 'Hommies?' '.....' Bold intentionally turned the corner with speed. 'Bah, he really needs to get out more.' Shaw giggled, following Ump down the corridor. Sooner or later, they'll find him. …Let's just hope its more sooner, then later.
