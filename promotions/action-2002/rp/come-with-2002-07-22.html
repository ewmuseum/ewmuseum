Come With?

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/22/02 . . . Augusta, Maine | 20:30PM CST . . . MAX DANGER Haha! Man, that's great! MAX DANGER is laughing hysterically at the television. On the television is Max Danger beating the hell out of Conrad Ramsey from the 7/17 edition of Pressure Point. DANGER Honey, come in here. Watch me beat the piss out of that old bastard Conrad Ramsey. There is no reply. Max sits up on the couch. He turns, and hears something from the back of the house. DANGER Honey? What's wrong? More unnatural noises can be heard. Danger looks concered as he gets up from the couch. Danger makes his way down the hallway. DANGER Jessica, babe, what's going on? Danger pushes open the bathroom door. Hunched over the toilet is JESSICA, vomitting in the toilet. Very unpleasant sounds are made. DANGER Jess... JESSICA Xander. Jessica raises up. She is pale. Max quickly fills up a cup with water and kneels down beside Jessica. He holds the cup as she downs it. DANGER It's going to be alright, Jess. JESSICA What if I get sick on the road, with you? DANGER I'll take care of you. Jessica buries her head into Danger's chest. She begins weeping. Danger gently caresses her hair. FADE OUT
