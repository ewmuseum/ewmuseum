Action! — Tales

Saturday Morning.

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/06/02 Cartoons. The endless hours of watching quality kid’s programming, all day long. Reed Young sat himself down on his rotten, rusty old sofa, the one that saw his ass for almost hours on end... every, single, day. The world was good to Reed Young at this time. He had just gotten off producing a giant Pay-Per-View titled ‘Til Death Due Us Part’. One that saw LLB and Fox’s antics pulled a step higher... and the devastating LDEIII Match that took the outlook upon Action! to new heights. A!W was no doubt the top tier Fed in his mind right now. And the Owner would celebrate with a cold glass of chocolate milk and the Fox Kid’s next television show. “This is the life.” He said, leaning back and resting his head against the pillow. “I never would’ve guessed being the Owner of the bestest Fed ever would have been so easy.” He wasn’t able to ramble on much more. The phone had went, as it surprised Young. For hardly anyone would call his house at this time of day, let alone ANYtime. “Y’ellow?” He said as he lifted the handle up-side-down, only to realize his error and flip the phone back over. “Reed Young’s house... where women stay 24/7 and we party, party, party... even past 11!!” Reed looked around and smiled, even though the entire house was completely empty and silent. “Yes- yes hello? This Reed Young?” Young posed for the phone. “It is! But no, I won’t join your Fed.” “What?” “I won’t join your Fed. Goodbye-” But before he could ply his ear from the speaker, the man on the other line talked again. “Um... I’m not here to ask you that.” “Oh, well then... no, Action! Wrestling doesn’t want you to join, you no talent ASSHOLE!” “Um, are you sure this is Reed Young?” A dumbfounded Young looked annoyed. “Of course.” “Okay. Good. Reed, it’s Johnny. You have to stop being late with these shows. WB isn’t liking it too much, and nor is the whole Roster of yours. You may be pulled off this station, if you keep up your-” Young cut Johnny off with a hand gesture. How this was seen through the phoneline... will never be known... “Dude, man, it was like a few late shows. And WB cums over the fact that I have Pressure Point on their station.” Johnny huffed. “They may cum over it... but, if you’re thinking along the lines of that... then let’s just say they want to get off when it’s TIME to get off, and not wait. Get what I mean?” Do do do do do... Young began to think how cool it would be to actually sign Joe Campbell! Then he remember that Joe Campbell was that moron from the Asylum, from one of those inferior Feds. “Reed?” Do do do do do... “Okay, whatever. I’ll talk to you later.” Click. “Y’ellow? Johnny?” Young spoke into the phone again, waking up from his daydream. “Guess he hung up.” Looking down at the phone... and not knowing what to do... he threw the receiver into the wall and unplugged the cord. “AAHH!! The phone’s broken again!”
