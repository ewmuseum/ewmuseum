Courage - Act VI

26 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/26/2002 'This is not my day.' Allison was sitting on the bench directly behind Coral. She, herself, was not having a great day. She was blissfully unaware of Coral's presence, just like he was unaware of her own. They sat back to back and kind of apart from each other. Allison quietly sighed and spoke. 'Yeah, well, it probably can't touch my day.' Coral smiled and shook his head. It seemed that he really couldn't recognize her voice just yet, and so, they just continued on with their conversation as if nothing was happening between them. 'Oh, really?' 'Yes. Really. I think I missed Japanese.' Allison said. This was met with some laughter on Coral's part, before he retorted. 'Heh. I was fired from my job for not showing up last week.' he said, showing hints of a smile. He had received word from Rashard, back home in New Orleans, that the IWO had called and told him that they had released him from contract after not showing up on their last Hostile Takeover card. 'I had to see my big sister today!' Allison countered. 'I don't even have a sister.' Coral said. 'I have some long-haired guy living in my apartment back home, though. Does he count?' 'Yeah, well... you didn't spend a half hour looking for someone and not find him.' Allison said, obviously also unaware that she was talking to Coral. Coral just laughed, before he responded. 'I spent an hour looking for a girl around here.' Coral said. 'So, I win that one.' Allison scratched her head in thought. 'Yeah, well, this guy... he's a big dumb ditzy fellow. He loved his job, but then he got a promotion and wasn't around that much. So I had to leave him.' Allison said. 'Heh. Sounds like me.' Coral said. The earth-shattering irony was beginning to make Coral think about it. 'Maybe.' Allison said, sighing. 'How about your girl? What's she like?' It finally occured to Coral of the identity of this woman. 'Oh, she's a pretty girl. Long red hair. Blue eyes. Just a shade younger than myself. Thinks that Gavin Rossdale is a hottie. And she snores.' Coral said, a hint of a smile on his face. Allison was slowly beginning to catch on. 'Yeah, well, my guy is nervous all the time.' 'My girl likes to blare Wyclef and attempt to sing along with it.' Coral said. Allison giggled, before she came up with her own retort to that. 'My guy belches and farts in front of me.' 'So does my girl.' Coral said, with a smile. With that, Allison turned sharply toward Coral. 'I do not!' Busted. 'Oh, you do too!' Coral responded, before he turned to Allison and they embraced. They held on for a few moments before Coral looked at her now short black hair, utterly confused. 'Your hair. It's black.' Allison nodded. They hugged again. Needless to say, there were a lot of people staring at them on that day.
