Dreams were meant to be broken: Chapter 13

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Luscious finished his business inside the cubicle and went to pull the door open, but the fallen body of his guard Enrique fell into his thighs. Red poured from Enrique's nose as he lay there. Dead. His panic caused him to relieve himself yet again, though one could think by the smell he left one couldn't be relieved much more. His gaze settled onto Paul. Paul was still wriggling, but it was quite clear that he was not going to make it. He was making quite the mess inside of his red puddle. It was probably the last few bursts of command his brain would give his body. Ever! He noticed Fiend who looked as though he were trying to help Paul. Luscious was never weary of the mohawked man before him. He thought he was here to aid his companions. Here to guide them. Well, he was here to guide them in a way. You could say he was their angel... ...of DEATH per se. 'What happened to 'em?' grunted Luscious watching the final kicks come from his bodyguard. Fiend looked up. 'They got fucked,' was his reply. And they had been. Well and truly. Luscious then became weary, reaching for the gun under his coat, but before he knew it, he was just as dead. It was like it happened in 'slo-mo'...
