My Anti-Drug

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/18/2002 Has this ever happened to you? Let me set the scene: You're lying unclothed on the floor of your apartment ,scanning your nude body in the spider-web cracks of a full length mirror you've partly shattered the previous night in a coke and liquor induced stuper, deciding whether to either take up the crack whore's offer to go raw dog on her delapitated body for a few rocks or take the .45 from under your bed, and give God a piece of your mind, while the physical pieces lay spewed all over your shitty upholstry. And you just lay there. You've gone through this right? Bah. I've gone through it. That's all that matters right now. So anyway, I decided I was gonna try to do both, and time the trigger pull with my orgasm. That way I'd be going and cumming at the sametime. It's how every man should go. But my phone rang. It was my manager. A prentincious(spellings my bitch) passive aggresive little jewish twep. He tells me he's landed me a new gig. At Action. So like the good fed whore I aim to be, I ask how much. He says 6 figures for now. I say fine. He hangs up. I rip the phone out of the socket. Gulp down the last dregs of Johnny Walker from a bottle waiting eargerly on the couch, and quietly slipped into my ocean of self-pity, self-loathing, and self-absorbsortion. But that was a few days ago. I assume you want to know who I am. Actually, I assume you don't give a fuck, and trust me the feelings mutal, but I have to anyway. I'm Invalid. It is both my name, and my being. I don't feel like explaining it right now. But I will tell you a little about myself. In case you haven't noticed, I'm not particularly sunshine and lollypops. And the fact that I just included the words 'sunshine and lollypops' into a sentence sickens me. But that's not the point. After being around me a few seconds, you'll notice I fully intoxicate myself in one of the most addictive emotions humans are capable of. Apathy. I honestly don't know what a 'fuck' is. So I see no reason what so ever why I should ever give it. My past employers, and peers would say I'm what you'd call an 'asshole', or 'bastard' or 'cold hearted shithead' or as the authorities call me 'tall, black male assalient, armed and dangerous, proceed with caution.' I don't think so though. I mean sure brutalizing people exictes me....sometimes sexually. And sure I view the world through coke bottle glasses of white hatred. And you could say I'm the kind of person who would slit a baby's throat with a smile. And yes, I'll admit I have been known to visit cancer wards of local hospitals and shove the patients around just to feel tough. But if you disregard the heartlessness, the total disregard for human life, the sadistic tendency toward violence, and the complete lack of morals or decency... I'm actually a pretty nice guy. Atleast my mom thinks so. Bah. I've started to ramble. I'll wrap this one up. My name is Invalid My anti-drug is hurting people ...and sometimes Peruvian cociane. Anything less would be uncivilized.
