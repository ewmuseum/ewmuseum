A gift...

25 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 25th 'This is where I reveal myself, show that I'm not fine' Kine- Abney Park Shad Kroeger sat at a glossy oak wooden desk in a dark room which was lit by a single lamp, it was the typical office lamp, the gold base and rod to hold the lamp, and a green lamp shade. Shad sat shifting through his papers, looking carefully at a few of the documents, each one had a signature, each one gave him 12.6541% of their contracts. Every contract on his desk is what bought him the shiney Mercedes that sat in the office parking lot, with a sign that said 'Shad Kroeger Only.' Shad was living the high life, he was living good, but every now and then he'd have to look through the papers, looking for one that wasn't making him any money and give him options, options that Mr. Kroeger chose of course. Shad sat thumbing through the documents still, ocasionaly checking the computer making sure there wasn't a red number by any of the names on the grid sheet. Shad was checking the R's now, so far so good. Shad sat the manilla folder down, that contained the R's, and reached out grabbing the folder labelled S. The first contract Shad pulled from the file, he looked at the computer, then looked back down in shock at the contract. Mr. Kroeger quickly grabbed the phone and dialed a number... Brad sat silently on the edge of a bed, his bed that is. The walls of the room were a pale gray, casting a glooming affect over the room. Beside the bed a night stand sat with an alarm clock, and a phone on top. Across the room, on the other side of his bed a large white dresser sat, it's droors unable to close due to the amount of clothing in them. Across the room there were two doors, one was open, showing a rather dingy bath room, the other was shut, it lead to the hall way to head up stairs from his basement apartment. In the corner beside the door a pair of wrestling boots sat beside some tennis shoes, laying on top of the boots a pair of glossy black wrestling tights lay. Above the wrestling boots a replica of the World Wrestling Revolution North American title was framed in glass, the same title that was passed to him by Micheal Solo, the title that was supposed to make him a star. Brad sat wearing a pair of black Nike basketball shorts, and a tight white t-shirt, showing off his muscular build. His long black hair was pulled back into a pony tail. Brad finaly began to move glaring up at the North American title replica, he looked deep into the gold plating, the finger prints were still visible on the golden plates. His and Micheal Solo's, the minute he won the title, he was allowed to take that title home, and recieved a duplicate of it. It was standard WWR procedure, they couldnt have dirty titles. Brad layed down on the bed, stretching first, then closing his eyes, Brad rolled over, back and forth a few times before finaly becoming comfortable. Just as he was about to nod off into a well deserved sleep... Ring.. Ring.. Ring.. 'Hello?' Brad said after he reached over grabbing the phone off the hook. 'Hello Brad, this is your agent Shad Kroeger,' Mr. Kroeger said in a very buisness like manner. 'Hello Mr. Kroeger, whats up?' Brad said, as he awaited inevitable. 'Brad, what happened with WWR? You were on T.V. every night, two straight main events, what went wrong?' Mr. Kroeger asked. 'I missed a deadline or two,' Brad said sullomly. 'So they let you go?' Mr. Kroeger said. 'Yea, they told me my services were no longer needed, they had me job to Hornet, that was my last pay check,' Brad said as he waited for Mr. Kroegers answer. 'Brad, you've got a gift, you should be at the top right now, it should of been Chavez vs. Brad Schilling vs. Downfall at Wresolution 2002,' Mr. Kroeger said somberly. 'Yeah I know, I know,' Brad said, his voice fading into the distance, he had decisions to make, and decisions take time. Time Brad didn't have.
