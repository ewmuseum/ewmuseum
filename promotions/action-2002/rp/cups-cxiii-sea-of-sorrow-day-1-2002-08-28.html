Cups: cXIII: Sea of Sorrow (Day 1)

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 28, 2002 Cups: cXIII: Sea of Sorrow Day 1 'A day in the life of someone else would be great. The massive load has piled its weight off on me all at once. Wrestling, and trying to carry on a normal life. Why would I even attempt it?'          James picked himself off of the hotel bed and looked around the darkened and dampened room. Dust clustered up his nostrils with even the most futile attempt at taking in air. Sickened by the scene in which surrounded him, he felt a sudden urge to leave. 'It's amazing what people would do for money these days. Hiring someone to take the life of another. Ha, that's not even the worst of things to come! People sell rooms like these to normal people. It's sick .. This world is disgusting me more and more as the days go on. As a child, nothing was this bad? I'm sure that every body in the world knows what I'm talking about when I say it. This place is changing. Our five star hotels have turned into run down, beat-up trash dumps. Our architectural structures are crumbling before our very own eyes, but here ... there is still a survivor. This cruel world can unfold so quickly ... to me. It's nearly gone.'          James tucked in his shirt as he stood to his feet. A light in the corner of the room flickered nearly blowing the bulb. The attention was lost. He snatched the journal in which he received at the funeral and peered into its complexity. Deep thoughts of the utterly breathtaking panorama that took place in front of his very own eyes. ‘Day 1 The days seem to just float by. All of the drugs and painkillers that I'm on now are slowly eating away at me. By the time anyone reads the journal, I'll done be gone. I'm sure of it. Days end just as quickly as they begin. So has my life. I feel like I'm only a child in this world. Yet I'm twenty-eight years young. I couldn't be old ... look at me. I still see beauty deep down inside. No one else does. I'm just about over with. Everything is starting to change. I'm coming down, I guess. Slowly running out of the fuel to keep me alive. Guessing what to do from one day to the next. It's odd. A feeling that cannot be described. Within, I feel the pain eating me away. Outside, I see the beauty waiting to unfold. Hopefully everything goes through as planned. I should feel absolutely no pain. It's just the way I wanted it. He felt no pain when he went ... so I shall not either. I perceive myself as falling. Drifting away into nothingness. Slowly devouring myself in a world of hate. This isn't the way to go. I feel ... So... Different now. Everything in my world is gone. All that I have are the prescription pills laying on the table next to me. This journal is even getting hard to read or write anymore. I can't understand anything .. Maybe I need sleep.'
