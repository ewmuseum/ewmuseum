The Velcro Heart, The Eighteenth Part of

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/23/02 During the same timeframe as this. 09/23/02. It had been four days. Long days. And as LLB checked his answering machine again... he once again had no messages. Was Reed Young too scared to call him? Normally after every bad show on Pressure Point, Reed would at least call his best friend the night after. Calm him down... re-vibe his attitude... Make sure things would always go his way, and that, no matter what... LLB was the best Action! Wrestling had to offer. Normally LLB liked those phone calls. Despite always being pissed off in them, and always getting mad... deep down, it made the Action! Champion feel safe and secure... Regardless of the Owner and his HORRIBLE sense of smartness. It still beat Jim Johnson. Who’s only out there to make money and grab a name. It beat The Don. Who just plugged himself and the Mafia left, right and center. It still beat Joe Campbell. Whom was either too drunk or fucked up to care. You take what you can get in this world. And if that meant taking Reed Young... yes, even Roland Miles Ermann would take him. But today, again, as LLB looked at his answering machine... no messages were there. Young must’ve been horrified by KroW’s appearance... so horrified, he wasn’t going to call LLB... because even he, wasn’t sure... what to do with KroW now. LLB picked up the phone. A best offense is a good defense... right? He dialed Reed’s number. Starting to scream once Reed picked it up. “Thanks for the call... idiot!” Young replied, stuttering. “And I thought I ASKED you to STAY THE FUCK OUT OF THE MATCH!! If I was meant to LOSE... I wanted to LOSE!” Again he replied. “Then what the hell WERE you doing!?” Another stuttering reply. “Listen... I really don’t think I NEEEEEED ‘The Risk’ anymore... but if you want to stick me with them, that’s fine. I’ll side by you guys... but LET ME DO THINGS ON MY OWN!” More. “I don’t WANT people to think LLB’s not a fighting Champion. And I told you about the bar right? I told you what happened there... I do not and CANNOT have people talking like that again! It isn’t me, Reed. IT’S NOT WHO I AM!” Young continued to reply in his stupid ways. “You... are... A FUCKING IDIOT SOMETIMES!” Slam. LLB hung the phone up.
