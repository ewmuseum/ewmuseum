The Follower - Take 1

11 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.11.2002 Garrett Hollister sat up in his small flat in Hamburg, Germany holding the phone to his ear, the blank stare on his face showing that the words of his proverbial icon weren’t clear despite the fact that they were said with feeling. The loud and steady dial tone continued to blare through Garrett’s then empty head until she came into the room. “Who was that, rosebud?” He adored everything about her. She was so radiant, long brown locks and green eyes that any man would lose himself in. Her status was as his significant other and he was sure about that one thing, despite his lack of intellect. He knew that in the end there will only be three things left for him to depend on. Maria, which was her equally as beautiful name, David, their young child, and his father … Xander. Maria stood there in all her magnificence and once again called out the pet name for her large, but simple fiancé’. “Rosebud?” Maria said again. Garrett Hollister said nothing which worried her. For Maria had always thought that she knew everything about the man she was soon to marry. She never anticipated how wrong she was. They were together before The Brotherhood was formed in any aspect, inside or outside of the wrestling medium. In fact, they bonded on the streets where they both were forced to live by the upper class capitalists and governments of their society. It was still a classic love story. Maria walked down the dirty sidewalk of a corrupted neighborhood on that cold day and she wore nothing but a few rags. Garrett, who had been blessed with size, often worked minimum wage jobs for construction workers who were too fat and overfed to do things themselves and had just bought a jacket second-hand. He spotted her from a far, for even then you could tell that she was wonderful looking, and sweat began to run down his cold brow. He knew right then that for some strange reason, they were connected. She was the one who could guide him out of the mess his life was. Clumsily, he walked over toward her, his huge frame blocking every car in the street from moving. He didn’t care. For all the young Garrett Hollister knew then were he and her. “Hello.” Garrett said with a fake smile, trying hard to hide his nervousness. “Hi.” She spoke back to him meekly. It was like being back in high school. Garrett ignored these similarities, however, his smile widening after she actually spoke to him. “I was across the street and I noticed you.” He said. “It looks as if you’re very cold, would you like my jacket?” Please take it. Please take it. “N-No … I couldn’t …” She stumbled with her words. “Please.” Garrett said back with a hint of seriousness in his voice. “I insist.” Maybe it was because she was slightly scared of him, but she took that jacket. “Won’t you be cold?” She asked. “No.” He smiled. In all actuality, he was telling the truth. The warmth of finding a new love in his heart was enough. She was now smiling as well. “At least let me give you something in return.” She said. As a man, many things ran through Garrett’s mind at that moment. Some were made for the movie Titanic while others were common in those starring Jenna Jameson. It didn’t matter; it was a gift from her. She reached into her raggedy pocket and began to shuffle through the artifacts inside. A condom? A piece of candy? A coupon? No matter what the item, he knew that he would treasure it. She took his hand and placed something in the palm. Upon winking, she walked away, her brown hair flowing in the wind. He looked inside of his own grip and saw a small white rosebud. It could’ve brought a tear to his eyes. Then in a moment of awakening he looked up and saw her walking away … he wanted to call her back to him … but alas! He didn’t even know her name. “Wait!” Garrett called back to her. She paused in her tracks like a cornered animal. “Yes?” She asked, not even looking back at the man she would one day become engaged to. “What is your name?” He screamed back. “Maria.” She said before continuing her walk. Maria. It filled him with so much joy and today, it does the same. That is if he’ll ever listen. “Garrett…” Maria spoke loudly this time. “Talk to me!!” He wished he could. However, there was nothing he could say. Her rosebud was silenced by confusion. Her soft voice couldn’t even awaken him. “It was him … wasn’t it?” She asked. Xander. It all revolved around Xander and his sick game now. Their family was now a subsidiary of something bigger, a family built of maniacs and helpless followers. At the very tip of this proverbial iceberg were Xander Pollack and Garrett Hollister. The President and his lead secret service agent. Don King and his brutal prize fighter, Mike Tyson. Michael Corleone and his lead hitman. Garrett Hollister brought the pain while Xander Pollack speaks the truth or at least the truth as it is in his mind. “I hate him!” Maria screamed. “I fucking hate him!” Which way to go? Listen to his only love talk down on the man who made life matter? Or defend Xander to the fullest? Garrett closed his eyes in a mix of rage and passion, remembering back to what Xander had said over the phone. “Do it for your brothers, Garrett.” “Do it for your father.” It was him who he lived for and even though his wife saw him as a prisoner, Garrett didn’t feel that way. “Don’t you ever say that you hate Brother Pollack!” He slowly began to turn into Xander’s creation. Everything they had built as two people in a relationship was crumbling. “He gave us our new life and this new home!” Garrett screamed at her again, her whines echoing in the background. “He made it so our child can go to school.” In the back of his head, the brooding voice of his so-called partner spoke. Hissing louder and louder by the second. “I am your savior … Defend me … I am your rock … Cherish me … I will cure your ailments … Pray to me … I am God … Kill for me.” He lifted his hand as if to beat his true love. “Think of David.” Maria said through her tears. He brought down his arm to his side and collapsed back into a chair, putting on yet another helpless and blank look. Just when he thought he had life figured out, he met Xander and it was a beautiful thing. But beautiful can’t say beautiful forever. Sooner than later, it becomes ugly.
