In The Face Of Danger

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
She got the word. This Wednesday, she steps into the ring against Max Danger. No that's not short for maximum danger, because Danger isn't much of a threat. But he is one of the majority when it comes to this profession, so logic would tell you that she doesn't stand a chance. But her chances at pulling out the win increase because of who 'she' is. And who she happens to be is 'Sweet Aroma', good ole Veronica Aromadeus. The same woman that was thrown to the wolf, known as Syphon Fission aka Rashard Clark. She entered that ring blind, and left with some knowledge of what to expect the next time she entered a squared circle. This moment would come a week later in a tag match, however the chinanigans of Max Danger and Angelica Dawson showed her more of what to be on the lookout for, when she comes face to face with Danger tomorrow. Sure Max Danger isn't a threat like Clark was, but he's someone that needs some corrective action. The mouth, the meaningless beatings given to Conrad Ramsey. Sweet Aroma would have none of that, so she inquired to Angelica Dawson about the incident, only for smart remarks to exit her months, which led the two to go at it. And that's when Danger stuck his nose into business that didn't concern him. I mean yeah, I barely know Conrad Ramsey, but I know he's a better man than Danger, so I figured I'd help him out; and got a cane from behind for my trouble. And trouble or no trouble, I promise to help myself.
