Action! — Tales

Roster Spilt.

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/25/02 “So Boss?” “Sssooo...” Reed Young. Jeremy Bold. Reed’s house. 6:12 PM EST. Nothing on television. Nothing to talk about. And that Backstreet Boy song wasn’t playing on the radio yet. “So Boss?” “Sssooo...” Young looked over to his computer. Shame the internet was down too. “How the kids?” “Kids?” Young had to rub his head for a second. “I don’t have any.” Reed Young Jr, Reed, Reed Young Jr. Oh yeah... that’s right. Since Reed was divorced he kept sending ‘Y2J’ to a boarding school. What a Father. “So Boss?” “YOU ALREADY SAID THAT!” Young snapped at Jeremy. It was a fucking shame the baseball game wasn’t starting for a bit... Reed really wanted to see the Giants WASTE the Angels... Unless of course the Angels WASTED the Giants. Then he’d root for them. “I got it!” Young snapped. “The ‘in’ thing these days, as I read on ‘Wrestling Planet’... is to have a roster spilt!” Bold seemed confused at first... but once Reed hit him in the leg, suddenly, he caught on. “Oh yeah!” Moments of silence. “Yeah!” More silence. “Alright... so...” Young looked up at Bold. “Want to spilt the roster? We can have two shows... um... ‘Pressure’... and the other one can be... ‘Point’!” “Brilliant Boss, brilliant!” “I try.” “Can I take ‘Point’ and you can take “Pressure’ then?” “YES!! YES I CAN!!” “Okay... so, roster spilt. Let’s do it!” Reed pulled a quarter out of his pocket as he tossed it in the air. “Heads or tails?” “Um... HEADS!!” The quarter landed on the floor. It was heads. “I WIN!” Young cried... but he didn’t. “It’s tails!” Young quickly covered up the quarter before Jeremy could catch a glance. And believing that the Owner was fair, he let him choose first. “Okay. Go.” The Owner of Action! didn’t have to think much. “I’m taking... SYPHON FISSION!” Then he changed his mind. “Okay... wait, I’m taking... JOEY MALONE!! The Champion!” Bold slapped his hands. “FUCK! I wanted him!” “Your turn.” “K. I’m picking... CHRISTOPHER FOX!” Pressure: Malone. Point: Fox. “Fine. Next I’m taking my brand new enemy... LLB!” Young started to laugh. “And since we HAVE to be like the WWE, you, Jeremy, take the rest of ‘The Risk’ so that he cannot come back and fight them.” nWo/ Hollywood anybody? Pressure: Malone. LLB. Point: Fox. ‘The Risk’. Young followed it up with another pick. “Now I want... AIRBORN TYLER!” Bold thought long, and hard. He needed to outdo that. So he did. “I’ll take Jonny Yankee!” Good move. Really. Pressure: Malone. LLB. Airborn Tyler. Point: Fox. ‘The Risk’. Jonny Yankee. “SWEET FUCKING AROMA!!” Reed said, rubbing his hands together. “Fuck! I’ll take... um... SIMON SEAMAN!” Pressure: Malone. LLB. Airborn Tyler. Sweet Aroma. Point: Fox. ‘The Risk’. Jonny Yankee. Simon Seaman. “K. Now I want JEFF... GARVIN!! ... JEFF GARVIN!!” Big move. Jeremy had to counter. Taking all but a literal one second, he replied with his. “Action! Man!!” Tough. ‘Point’ was now officially somewhat unrendered. Pressure: Malone. LLB. Airborn Tyler. Sweet Aroma. JEFF GARVIN! Point: Fox. ‘The Risk’. Jonny Yankee. Simon Seaman. Action! Man. “Max fucking Danger!” This time, actually, Bold did something smart... however, not for ‘storyline’ purposes. “Conrad Ramsey.” Pressure: Malone. LLB. Airborn Tyler. Sweet Aroma. JEFF GARVIN! Max Danger. Point: Fox. ‘The Risk’. Jonny Yankee. Simon Seaman. Action! Man. Conrad Ramsey. “Kayden Paulton. That little fucks too nice...” “Kent Anthason.” Yep. Malone and Anthason wouldn’t see each other again. Pressure: Malone. LLB. Airborn Tyler. Sweet Aroma. JEFF GARVIN! Max Danger. Kayden Paulton. Point: Fox. ‘The Risk’. Jonny Yankee. Simon Seaman. Action! Man. Conrad Ramsey. Kent Anthason. “Nate.” “Steve-O.” Since when did Action! turn into that stupid inferior Fed? Pressure: Malone. LLB. Airborn Tyler. Sweet Aroma. JEFF GARVIN! Max Danger. Kayden Paulton. Nate. Point: Fox. ‘The Risk’. Jonny Yankee. Simon Seaman. Action! Man. Conrad Ramsey. Kent Anthason. Steve-O. “The Elements.” But wait... The Elements come together, yet Pretty Fly doesn’t!? Yes. That’s right. “Okay... um... Action! League.” Young interrupted. “They don’t come together.” Why not? “FUCK!” Bold picked again. “NOAH HAWKINS!” Pressure: Malone. LLB. Airborn Tyler. Sweet Aroma. JEFF GARVIN! Max Danger. Kayden Paulton. Nate. The Elements. Point: Fox. ‘The Risk’. Jonny Yankee. Simon Seaman. Action! Man. Conrad Ramsey. Kent Anthason. Steve-O. Noah Hawkins. “I want the GIANT... Brad Schilling!” “I want your Mom!” Pressure: Malone. LLB. Airborn Tyler. Sweet Aroma. JEFF GARVIN! Max Danger. Kayden Paulton. Nate. The Elements. Brad Schilling. Point: Fox. ‘The Risk’. Jonny Yankee. Simon Seaman. Action! Man. Conrad Ramsey. Kent Anthason. Steve-O. Noah Hawkins. Mrs. Young. “The number one newsmaker!” “Um... Coral Avalon!” Pressure: Malone. LLB. Airborn Tyler. Sweet Aroma. JEFF GARVIN! Max Danger. Kayden Paulton. Nate. The Elements. Brad Schilling. The Ringer. Point: Fox. ‘The Risk’. Jonny Yankee. Simon Seaman. Action! Man. Conrad Ramsey. Kent Anthason. Steve-O. Noah Hawkins. Mrs. Young. Coral Avalon. “Dude... man, like, are you bored?” Bold nodded his head. “Fuck yes.” “You wanna just, like... forget about this and keep it ‘Pressure Point’?” “Sure.” The two shook hands in agreement... and the scene went back to silence. “So Boss?” “Sssooo...”
