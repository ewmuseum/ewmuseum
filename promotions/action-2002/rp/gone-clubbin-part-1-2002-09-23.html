Gone Clubbin' - Part 1

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9.23.02 Ring Ring Ring 'Fuck,' said Odium in his normal, deep toned voice. He slammed his beer bottle on the table in his hotel room, and put his cigarette into the ash-tray. Slowly getting up, he groaned as if it was a monstrous task to walk five steps to answer the phone. ‘Talk to me.’ ‘Ody! Hey, what’s going on?’ Oh.My.God. It had to be the man Odium found most annoying. The Original. Jeff Garvin. ‘What the fuck do you want and how the fuck did you get this number? ‘Ody, you’re so silly! Silly guy! Reed Young gave it to me!,’ exclaimed Jeff. ‘Figures, he sweats your nuts like he was running a marathon on them.’ ‘Hahahahaha! That’s funny! You’re great Ody! Really great! Hey, I’ve got an idea! Why don’t me and YOU, go to the club tonight!?’ ‘No.’ Odium said within the very second Garvin asked the question. ‘Alright! I’ll be there in 5 minutes! Get dressed!’ ‘What the ---’ Dial Tone. Jeff only heard what he wanted to hear. Selective hearing they call it. Odium wiped his face with his hands and sighed. He had just come back from the store, so he was already dressed...in casual clubbin’ attire unfortunately. He wore a tight black shirt with some baggy black pants and white Adidas. Odium sat his is chair with a magazine in hand. ‘If he comes over, I’m going to shoot him.’ said Odium as he flipped the page. Knock Knock Knock Odium looked up from the magazine and glared in the “camera”. He ripped his magazine down the center and walked to the door, opening it with force. ‘GO AWAY! I DON’T LIKE YOU!’ ‘EEEEEEEEEEEEEEK!’ ‘Oh ma’am, sorry about that. What do you need?’ ‘Muh-Muh-More Towels?’ stuttered the maid. ‘No thanks.’ Just as Odium was closing the door, Jeff raced through. ‘Alright! Let’s go!’ Garvin did a quick jive. ‘Jeff, if you don’t get the hell out, RIGHT now, I’m going to be forced to throw you out.’ ‘You’re such a kidder! That’s what I like about you, we’re the same,’ preached Jeff. ‘...You’re so wrong.’ Jeff grabbed Odium by the wrist and pulled him out of his hotel room and posed in front of his “ride”. ‘Ta-Daa!’ ‘You’ve got to be kidding me.’ ‘What are you talking about?! This bad-boy will get all the chicks!’ ‘A ‘65 pinto?’ questioned Odium. ‘It’s a classic!’ Jeff said as he tried a Dukes of Hazard type slide across the hood and failing miserably. ‘Come on! Hop in!’ Odium went back towards his room and attempted opening the doors. Locked. He turned back to the car and Jeff held up Odium’s key. ‘I got ya covered man! I locked it. Don’t worry! Man, you’re such a cool guy. Safe. Now that’s cool.’ Odium trudged to the car and got in. ‘You ready for a night on the town? Well, you better be, because we’re hittin’ the city!’ “SMOOTH BROTHERS STYLE!”
