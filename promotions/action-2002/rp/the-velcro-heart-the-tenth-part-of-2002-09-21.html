Action! — Tales

The Velcro Heart, The Tenth Part of

21 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pressure Point came back to the ringside area as the Fans were still cheering from this awesome, intense night. There was a new Carnage Champion in The Joker... Christopher Fox was put in his place... LLB retained the Title Belt and basically killed ‘Lionheart’ Tyler Babylon once and for all... Jonny Yankee had lost... let alone the signing of Jeff Garvin, and no Morg to be seen. Tonight... was a night for a celebration. And unlike the night that was ruined after ‘Down 4 the Count’, this night wouldn’t be. For there was no one to get in the way. “Without Me” began as Reed Young strolled out, his hands waving high in the air as he still had Morg’s ear upon his, and one hell of a cocky grin upon his face. Young walked halfway down the ramp, before turning around and pointing to the entrance. Out came the Champion. LLB. The lawyer met Young in the middle of the ramp, as he smacked him a low five and continued the walk down. Both of them entered the ring, as Young leaped up in the air and screamed “RATINGS!” out loud as hard as he could. Everyone turned in disgust. Pat Hoskins entered the ring, handing the Owner a microphone as he motioned for his music to end, and his voice to begin. “WHOO! WHOO! And... WWWHHHOOOOOO!” Only jeers replied. “What a night it’s been. I’d like to thank everyone that’s been a part of it. fWo who!? the Asylum what!? Action! Wrestling... is the only Fed that matters, DAMMIT!!” Young looked over at LLB. “It looks like you’re well on your way to defend the Title at our next Pay-Per-View, Pay-Per-View... PAY-PER-VIEW... ‘Retaliation’ ending off this month Roland. Give yourself a hand!” LLB only marched around the ring, patting his Title. “That a boy.” Young started to pace around as well. “You see... last week at the end of Pressure Point, Jonny Friend stopped by... he was getting a lot of calls... a lot of complaints... about my show... about Reed Young. Some people want my show off the air... they, um... how do you say it? They don’t ‘like my style’.” Cheers cued up. “Or... they want my program off the air. I believe I’m preempting some Dawson’s Creek... or something.” A few people in the Crowd held up their “We want Dawson’s Creek back!” signs. Reed just ignored them. “Fan mail after Fan mail... week after week... they want my program off the air. And why? Because they don’t like it... because I’m late with getting the tapes into WB’s Headquarters. Well... as I always like to say... SUCK IT!” Liam quickly commented that he had never heard Reed say that line before. “Anyway... back on track here, this program will never...” Young cued up his valley girl voice, while half the Crowd even said the line along with him. “EEEVVVEEERR take it off the air.” Suddenly, a man from the audience burst through the ring as he slid under the bottom rope, escaping security. LLB ran over, about to smack him with a left hand... but Young stopped him. “Wait, Rowe. Wait just one second.” Reed looked him over. “Who the hell are you?” “Name’s Roberto.” He said, in a somewhat flamboyant voice. “Here... just take this.” And handing Young a video tape, the man bolted from the ring and fled through the Crowd where he came from. The Owner just shrugged, putting the tape in his large jean pocket. “I’ll save this for lat-” “Courage” by AAF began... as suddenly, the whole Arena woke up and screamed. LLB snapped around, ready to exit the ring in a second and beat the living hell of out Christopher Fox... but he wasn’t going to, yet. Then Fox appeared. Coming out at the exact same time “Courage’s” theme picked up, the Fans all jumped up and down, shaking the Arena. But after pulling a microphone from his hand, his song slowly died down, as Fox paced happily around the top of the ramp. You could almost see Young’s veins popping out of his neck, as LLB just stayed cool and calm, waiting for the right moment to attack. “Heto LL, Reed. I just saw you guys talking... and I couldn’t help but want to be a part of it because you said we’re cool now, Reed.” The people backed up Christopher with cheers. “Oh, hi LL.” The lawyer pretended he wasn’t there. “Me said hi LL. BIG-” Fox stopped. “Oh yeah... sorry Reed.” The Owner nodded in approval as the Fans just booed. They wanted Fox to finish off his line... but he wasn’t going to. “Thank you for the drive down to the A-” “OBJECTION!” “ERECTION!” “Let us never speak of that again!” “But you were so nice... and friendly. It was a real class act...” It was coming sooner or later. Fox & the Crowd: “BIG TIME!” Young’s veins exploded from his neck as he twitched a little. But then bringing the mic back up to his face, the Owner stayed very calm. “Chris.” He said, as Fox shook his head yes about a billion times. “Could you come here a minute.” Obeying the request, Fox skipped down and slid into the ring. “Hi Ree-” WHACK. The Fans booed. “I ASKED YOU NOT TO SAY THAT LINE... DAMMIT! THAT’S ALL I ASKED YOU TO DO... BUT YOU COULDN’T YOU FUCK... YOU COULDN’T!” Young whipped Christopher into the ropes. To Reed, it wasn’t even about the line, as much as it was that Fox was the reason the aftermath to ‘Down 4 the Count’ was ruined... as well as tonight’s. Young hit Fox with a clothesline as the Crowd just kept on booing. “You idiot... you moron... YOU GOT KICKED OUT OF THE MAIN EVENT... I THOUGHT IT WAS OVER... BUT THEN YOU BEAT STEVEN DENTON!” He hurled him into the ropes again, connecting with another clothesline. “Well that’s IT! For you’re gonna be written off too... next week, I’m releasing your suspension for fighting the Action! Champion... oh yeah, you heard me, dammit! Because next week... I have FULL FAITH that Roland will KILL you... just like he did Babylon tonight... just like he did KroW on Pay-Per-View... For he’s the ONLY man that I know WILL get his jobs done... ALL OF THE TIME!” LLB’s face grew angry. He wasn’t sure what Young was trying to say. But then it came. “YOU... NEXT WEEK... TITLE MATCH AGAINST... LLB!” The Crowd went wild, as LLB pushed Young into the corner and grabbed the microphone. “OBJECTION! What the HELL are you trying to do!?” Young almost pissed his pants. He didn’t know what to do... and luckily, he didn’t have to. Fox got up and rushed in, clubbing Young in the face with a right hand. Next... the ‘Foxoorama’. The Fans went wild, as Christopher paraded the ring like a moron, as he looked into the ropes... and pointed. It was time for the ‘Bunny Sault’. Everyone stood, as Fox charged himself at the ropes... SMACK. ‘Erroneous Conclusion’. They all booed, beginning to throw their waste in the ring, as LLB hip tossed Fox over the top rope and to the outside. He then began to fight him all the way up the ramp, until the two disappeared behind it. Young rolled around on the mat, as he dusted himself off slowly, with blood rolling down the top of his forehead. That stupid, stupid Fox. And with the Fans worked into an absolute frenzy, Young smirked his lips just a little tiny bit... Something was up. BBBBAAAANNNNGGGG!! He fell over in the middle of the ring, as the lights turned off... and the Action! Screen went completely red. Eerie, creepy music then began as pictures of blood and dead human’s flashed across the screen. It suddenly halted... as in place of it... came another... BBBBAAAANNNNGGGG!! Followed by the red light on the Action! Screen again... one word appeared upon it, that worked the Crowd into an even more uncontrollable state. That word... “KroW”. Chants began... as the people jumped up and down, and Young’s horrified face was seen, lit in red... as he rolled his eyes back... and just passed out. The Pressure Point logo followed, showing itself in the bottom left hand corner, as the final scene showed... the Owner out cold in the center of his dark red ring... while every single member of the Continental Arena that night, jumped around, forcing the entire building to shake.
