Power Set Aside.

21 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling Pay-Per-View ‘Write Off’ 06/20/00 LLB vs. Adrian Fernandez (Owner of PIW) Hell in a Cell **DING DING DING** You and I Adrian. You and I. Sin and the law. One on one. Wow... this Hell in a Cell is huge! Okay... come here Mr. Fernandez. Whoo! Into the corner you go. Come out... come out... dropkick! Roland, you’ve perfected it well. I “killed” Mary, Ad. But now... I’m really kill you. Testify... Suplex. Good. Now just pick him back up, Rowe. Snap suplex. That fucker’s got everything coming. Sure, boo me Jury... boo me. You can’t change this. Mr. Fernandez gets up... and I BLAST him in the face with my left hand. Again. Again... Reel it back... and SMACK! Good hang time, Ad. Good hang time from my fist. The law picks Fern up... power bomb! This is a prime example of why Boss’ are just fucking stupid. A suit can’t take a wrestler... --Ten Minutes Later-- Double clothesline... and we’re both laying here. That’s fine Adrian. You know you can’t hold out much longer... Get up Roland, get up. Now... throw him into the cage! Whoo! Again! Whoo! Again! Whoo! This trial’s so much fun! Lemme see if I can unlock the door... I know I left some bolt cutters under the ring. No one ever looks there... I have no clue why though. Ah! Here we go... But first, let me whack Fernandez in the head with it. Nice. All that blood pouring out makes me want to cry. Okay, to the door we go. Snap! It’s unlocked! Sweet! Now... for even more fun! Come here you sin... I’m so nice that I’ll even drag you out. Catapult! Face full of steel! Eat that... and literally too. Damn, I’m a fucking comic. Okay, it’s time to climb the cage now. --Five Minutes Later-- He’s almost up... take it easy Roland... take it easy... WHOO! He’s on top too! Come here... I’m right over here Ad. Testify... SUPERKICK LLB! And on the top of the Hell in a Cell too! Hahahahaha! Am I the only one laughing? Hahahahaha! Okay... lets do it Rolex. Lets do what your idle, Bret Hart does... SHARPSHOOTER! Wow, I did that pretty well... I should really turn this thing into a Signature Move of mine. I wonder what I’ll call it. Listen to him scream... damn, that must hurt. Sit back more, Roland. Sit back more. There we go. Move. Locked. In. --Two Minutes Later-- Fernandez on the mesh... Jury booing... as I stand here... Now, if Sam Creamer would just send down that ladder from the roof I can finally end this... and show Adrian a thing or two. This is what happens when you object to the law. Sweet! Here it comes. ... ... Just collect your thoughts. Roland, what you’re going to do is set that ladder up... climb it... and jump onto Adrian’s body. Lets just hope the top of the Hell in a Cell doesn’t give away. --Two Minutes Later--On Top of the Ladder-- Fuck, that’s high... Yep, that’s high... But I can do it. You can do it, Roland. You’ve done it all... Okay, on the count of three. Ready? One. Two. Three. Deep breath. JUMP! What... what the hell happened? I’m on the mat... in the ring... Dear God! I must have fallen through! Where’s Ad? I gotta find Ad and win this Match! Bitch... Hands... covered... in blood. I’ll rub my forehead. Jesus, it’s my blood! Where is he!?!? Where is Adrian!? Shit... quick... he could be okay. ... ... Over there! He’s right over there Roland! He’s out cold! Fuck, he’s bleeding more than me! Crawl over... you can do it. Lets go. Almost there. Damn! My leg is killing me. My head is dazed... Drape the hand over. My theme... it’s playing! I guess I passed out again... But I take it I also won. Okay, pull yourself up... Use the ropes. There. Finished. Now hold up those arms boy. Listen to the boos! In your face Jury. The verdict was proven. I dominated. I showed the guilt. I rest my case.
