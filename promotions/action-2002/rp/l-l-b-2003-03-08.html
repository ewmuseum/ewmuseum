Action! — Tales

“L.L. B. ”

8 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OPEN Fun and frolics was Chris Fox. Being a ‘Kid’ came naturally to Christopher, because, after all, he was just that. But at Blood, Sweat, Tears, Chris endured probably the most grueling match in his very career; in fact maybe his whole life! The ‘Kid’ who likes to play, plays rough. Chris Fox has now learned the meaning of pain.... Fox “HETOOOOOOOOOOOO.... BIG TIME!” ....or so we thought. Title: “L.L.B.” Date: 02/17/03 2:00 pm EST Place: Locker Room The empty halls of the arena were vague and dust was seen floating around as it settled. The dark passages of the arena were relaxed and hollow, ready for the upcoming Action Wrestling house show. The dark severity was no more as the scene switched to the man, the myth, the Pokemon master, Christopher Fox sitting in a locker room alongside his trusted companion Pikachu. Fox “Last night was rough wasn’t it Pika?” The still injured Fox rests his left arm encased in a cast on his leg. Fox “I like having fun with my friends.... I’m only three! I’ve still gots my whole entire life in front of me. Not only did I go in there with my hurted arm but I lost too.” Fox paused as he adjusted himself in the chair. Fox “Not only that but LL was told he’s guilty and not a lawyer! Everything was looking soooooooo good! Maybe even too good!” It’s not fair! I wish I could LOL it all away but that just won’t work! Fox “My bestest friend in the whole wide world was stripped down to nothing more than a ordinary Joe who is a fake. I won’t stand for it Pikachu, LL is still “LL”, not “LL....B”!!!” The custodian entered to do his usual clean up. Custodian “Oh sorry sonny, I didn’t know anyone was here....” Fox “Oh that’s otay, what’s your name?” Ben “My name is Ben Frills, I’m the oldest on the staff here at age 82.” Fox “WOW!!!!!!!!!! You’re fucking old!” .... Fox “I mean wow, heh, wow....” Ben “So what are you doing here?” Fox “82.... that’s old....” Finally Fox snapped out of his day dream and spoke up. Fox “I’m just trying to numb the pain that I suffered last night at Blood, Sweat, Tears, as well as the pain of losing a friend.” Ben “What was his name young lad?” Fox “L--R, R, Rotan.... He was my bestest friend.... ever. Heh, we’ve had some good times, I won’t forget them, I just wish this bad stuff would go away.... big time.” Chris gathered himself, picking Pikachu up and heading out the door. Ben “Hmmmm, he’ll be fine.” CLOSE
