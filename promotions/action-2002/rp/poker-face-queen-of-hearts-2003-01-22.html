Poker Face: Queen of Hearts

22 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Were you going to give her another chance?” Lyle asked as they got out of the Limo and walked in to the Hotel. The Bell Clerk took his bag and checked it in at the front as Dirk led Lyle in to the Hotel and to their room. “Nah, baby. Just use her for some stickin’ fun, if ya’ know what’I mean...” Dirk replied as he winked to his boddy, he just groaned in disgust. “Stickin’ fun?” “She was using you, Dirk.” Dirk pushed the elevator button for the 7th floor as Lyle stepped aboard. “I know. But so was I.” The elevator music was a much needed calm that overwhelmed Lyle’s body, but only annoyed Dirk. As Lyle slowly shut his eyes as he awaited their destined floor, Dirk moved over to the speaker where it was emitting tunes of Enya. He slowly raised his fist, pulled back, and slammed it hard against the speaker, causing silence, and startling his friend. Lyle looked at Dirk, who had a huge smile. “What the hell?” “See that, baby? I punched the shit outta’ that ga’damn shit-spewer.” “Wh-why?” “I’m a problem solver, baby.” The doors slid open as the bell rang, indicating that the elevator has reached the 7th floor. “When I see a problem, I solve it.”
