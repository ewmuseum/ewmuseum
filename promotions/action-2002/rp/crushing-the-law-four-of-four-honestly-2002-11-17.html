Crushing the Law. (four of four - honestly)

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/17/02 “Yes. Okay. Send him right in please.” And why Reed Young said that as he was actually leaving his office, we will never know. “It’s because I started out the other three parts like them.” What? “Four if you count The Ringer.” Okay... maybe we do know now. Anyway... Reed Young was leaving his office. Now he, too, had seen it. The golden glare. LLB saw it twice last week... So it wasn’t just his eyes. “It’s mine too!” Young added as he rubbed his eyes and exited the room. He looked around... Nothing. That’s when he saw it though. Just as he was about to turn back into his office, there was that golden glare. It sprinted itself across the hallway’s opening on the other end of the hall. Young squinted his eyes together, as he started to march towards it. The house show was about to start soon... maybe it was there for that? He got to the end of the hall, as Young reluctantly turned to the way that the golden glare went... Nothing. Yet, that’s when he saw something... he didn’t know what, but it stuck itself out of the corner. It was gold... and then it disappeared again. Young raced to it, as he turned the corner with mad speed. His eyes opened in shock... as he saw a figure draped over in a golden cape with a star on top of it at the end of the hall. It just kind of sat there... like the person under it was dead, or had given up. However... as Reed Young walked closer to it, giggling sounds could be heard. Evil, giggling sounds. Young got about ten feet away, as those sounds... finally stopped. An eerie feeling crept over Reed’s body... as goosebumps started to form on his arms. It’s funny how you can go from humor to horror in all but ten seconds. He tilted his head to the left, only to open up his beady brown eyes as they studied this... thing. “Um... hello?” Young asked nervously. However, the figure, did nothing. “Hello?” Sweat started to seep from the top of Reed’s head, as he took another step forward... but the giggling started again, so he backed off. “Um...” Young took some deep breaths in. “You just... um...” He stopped talking again, as the once giggling turned into a whisper of someone’s voice. “llb... llb...” The voice was low, and raspy. It hurled into your ears, making you want to spit out your insides. Young raised an eyebrow, as he nervously wiped the sweat from his forehead. He looked across the hallway’s opening, to his right. “Stay right here for a second.” Young marched off. He was going to go to his locker room, take a break, and then come back to see if what he saw was truly real or not. But he could feel it... as Young turned to head off, a gust of wind smacked him in the side of the head. He snapped back, seeing that the gold caped man had vanished from his former position. However, he could still hear the giggles... and reluctantly turning back, he saw the gold caped man at the end of the hallway. It stood there for only a split second, as once Young saw it, it ran behind the wall again... leaving a final, eerie whisper... that seeped into Reed Young’s ears throughout the narrow and silent hallway. “llb... llb...” An answer? Maybe.
