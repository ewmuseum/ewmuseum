Things

12 April 2002

Date: April 12, 2002 There he stood motionless in the doorway of his hotel room. He did those kinds of things on a regular basis. You know, the ones that regular people tended to do. Simon was the kind of person that likes to stand on certain places and then sit down for a while before standing up again. Just like every regular individual on the planet, he put on his metallic blue pants, one leg at a time, before realizing that it was on backwards and he had to do it all over again. He was the blue collar worker from the time a pen exploded on his button-down shirt. You'd think he'd have it easy, being the poster child of good looks for such heavy hitters as 'Versace' or the exotic 'JC Penney', but it just wasn't so. His experience in a new environment was becoming sour like expired milk that's been in the refrigerator too long or Cher's face that has probably been in the refrigerator too long as well. It was all being tainted by one person. By some guy who liked to hit things with other...things. 'I was reading Hardcore Magazine Weekly Tribune Daily and get this. In England, people hit others with objects all the time.' Simon said into his cellular phone with a hint of doubt in his voice . He leaned against the doorway of his hotel room awaiting a response from his agent all the way in Los Angeles by the name of Brooke Jace. 'Seriously Simon, what are you doing? I've got a meeting at four o'clock that I'm missing to leave early and this is what you called me for? You said it was urgent. I could've been late for my meeting that I'm going to.' 'It was urgent and it is urgent.' he uttered. 'For goodness sake Brooke, you know much they pay you in England if you make a living doing that? It's more than I make. I bet if I wrestled in England and struck someone in the head with a road sign, I'd not only have fame, but I'd be a millionaire too. People would see me in the streets and go 'Bloody hell, it's Simon and his amazing talent of hitting things with stuff. Crickey, let's give him money before he uses his gift on us.' She sighed and then lazily replied, doodling on her notepad shortly after. 'That's not a gift. You either have it or you don't. It's not a gift, more like a present.' 'What's the difference?' he inquired as he scratched his head. 'Earth to Simon. Gift and present are two different words. That's the difference.' He paused for a minute to contemplate her answer. Slightly confused, he moved on with the conversation almost immediately. 'The truth of the matter is that I need to defend myself. Fred Franks could be out my window with a No Parking, No Smoking, No Drinking, and No Sign Holding sign waiting to attack me. He's out there, no doubt. I can just feel it. It's starting to get chilly and English in here. Do you feel that? It's on me. I think I've got some English on me. Good around here is beginning to become undesirable to eat by the minute. That's it, I can't take this.' Simon began to pace nervously back and forth as his agent attempted to calm him down. 'Just breathe, Simon. Just breathe.' Beginning to hyperventilate, he reached over in his luggage and pulled out a brown, paper bag. Breathing into it, he sat in a chair in the corner of the room and tried to relax. 'Feeling better?' she asked as his heavy breathing into bag could be heard loudly on the other line. 'Now I am.' he replied. Odd chewing sounds were noticed by Brooke as she sat there trying to figure out what was happening. 'Are you eating something?' There was an awkward pause for a moment as Simon took a bite out of the sandwich he grabbed out of the paper bag he was using. 'No?' 'Put down the sandwich. This is supposed to be a business call.' 'It is a business call. I'm eating a sandwich and it's my business.' Leaning back in her cozy leather chair, she glanced at the ceiling while pitching a few suggestions to Simon's problem whether or not he was listening to her. 'I don't know why you'd feel the need to defend yourself this way. I wouldn't recommend it, but your my client and if you think that's best for you, that's what's best for you.' 'Thanks for noticing that for the first time in four years.' Clearing his throat, she waited patiently for him on the other end to continue. 'Look, I'm not afraid of Fred Franks. What I am afraid of is number one, that man sneak attacking me from behind like some sneaky attacker attacking me all sneaky-like from behind for a sneak attack that's no doubt going to be sneaky one. Secondly, I don't want him breathing on me or trying to give me dental tips because that's just not going to happen from an Englishman. Though most of all, if that man hits me with a sign again and decides to go all hardcore on the person who invented the alternative meaning to hardcore, there's going to be trouble. Once again, the new meaning of hardcore is one's undeniable admiration of George Michael and 'Wham!', but that isn't my point. If he even tries to do that to me, it's going to be tea time on his booty and I need something that will make it happen.' Simon's proclamation of enthusiasm was presented as Brooke suddenly had an idea pop in her head. 'You just stay right there. The equalizer is on it's way.' The next day, Simon is greeted by an individual who hands him a large, rectangular parcel. Giving his signature on the clipboard, he handed the man a tip and closed the door. Walking over to the bed, he set the package on the mattress and opened it. A look of approval was then seen on his face as he read the sign out loud. 'Kiss my ass before you go, go...' he said under breath. He stood there motionless for a while before reacting to it. 'I hope he enjoys a good remake.'
