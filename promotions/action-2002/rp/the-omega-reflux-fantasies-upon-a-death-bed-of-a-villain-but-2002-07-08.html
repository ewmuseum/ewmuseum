The Omega: Reflux Fantasies Upon A Death Bed Of A Villain…But I Am Near Death…So Leave Me Alone(4)

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You know what? I remember certain things. Such as a high ranking officer of the United States Military yelling orders at me like I was some sort of fuck. Yes, a fuck. I don’t really care, however. It’s just something that woke me up. A man standing over you while you do push-ups, spitting about how you lack manhood is not all that important to me. It’s just one of the bits and pieces that makes my life puzzle, which I am just now putting together. It is all coming back in flashes of utter brilliance. However, knowing that I was a military officer, that changes things a little bit. I guess I never did go to college. Not like it matters to me anyway. And I didn’t start my “career” as a wrestler. I started as a fighter. I went into the reserves. It was my way to give people the middle finger. I didn’t like authority, and people making plans for me. Ironic. I was doing this because I was running away. Man, I was a naïve freshman. Man. You have no idea what I am talking about. No worry. You will understand in due time. My wrists served as the gateway into my soul. Joey, Keri. I am sorry. I know that doesn’t change one damn thing. But I hold out hope that one day it will. When we started in the IWO, we were just two guys, drinking, lifting, joking, filming crappy films and calling them promos. It some guys recording studio that we would check out all the time. I just stopped coming. Now, so have you. After I get out of this, I promise to go back to our roots. For the both of us. I need to understand again.
