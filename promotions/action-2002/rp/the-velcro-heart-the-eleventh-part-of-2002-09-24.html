Action! — Tales

The Velcro Heart, The Eleventh Part of

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reed Young began to recollect his breath in the backstage area, as he rested his head against the wall and slowly sucked the air in... and then out. In... and out. Sometimes- or rather all the time... he wondered why in the hell he would even do this. He didn’t need it. He could just pack up and leave. After all, he *had* ran over twenty Feds before this one came into play. Quitting was his style. But... this time, Young actually stuck with it. Action! was collecting a hell of a lot of ratings and doe... and even the downer in Reed Young didn’t want to just throw that out the window. He breathed in again... and then out. For as he had learned moments ago... KroW was coming back. He didn’t know how. He didn’t know when. But he knew he was coming. That’s what the Action! Screen told him and it didn’t lie. Just... hopefully, KroW wasn’t coming for him. Besides the fact that it was *he* whom cost KroW the A! Title at the Pay-Per-View, and a column in the golden pages of beating ‘The Law’... what the hell *had* Reed done to him anyway? Right Reed, right. Young lifted his head from the hard, cold brick, as he continued to walk the Arena halls... going back to his locker room area. Long, long... lllooonnnggg day. Turning the corner, the idiot known as the Owner of A! found his locker room door and sighed in relief. Now, he was going to enter... rest, -possibly sleep...- and then take it easy for another whole week until Pressure Point again. He opened the door, as suddenly his leather office chair swung around. It was LLB. ‘The Law’ sat in it, rolling his right hand through his hair, as he eyed Reed Young without a blink... whereas Young, just stood there... not knowing what to do. He was scared. “Oh... h- hi- hey Rowe. Waz up?” LLB could only reach out and grab Reed Young’s football that always sat on his desk. He tossed it in the air a few times, before kicking up his feet while knocking over a ton of papers. God was the table filled with them. “Why’d you do that?” He asked, still paying attention to the football as his threw it in the air. Young could only rush his hands overtop of his forehead. Maybe it was better if LLB just stared at him... because at least then, he knew he was going to get through. “Do- do what?” Reed shuttered. LLB didn’t bother to change what he said. “Why’d you do that?” “Oh!” Young slapped his head in a friendly manner, but the Champion was far from that. “Look, I needed you to fight Christopher Fox... because, Rowe, I’m annoyed by him. He’s annoying me to no end... and since, like, you’ve been able to kill everyone in your path... maybe you could just, like, end him one final time?” LLB threw the football directly at Reed’s head, as Young was barely able to duck sending the football to slam itself hard against the door, sending small pieces of wood falling to the ground as it did. “What if I don’t, though?” He stated very sharply. “What if I don’t get the job done? You know, Reed, there'have been times where I wasn’t able to. Yes, sure, I am the law... but even past that... I still cannot win them all. That’s the way it’s designed.” Young flew back his wild brown hair, as he looked at LLB and frowned. “I thought you could do *anything* though, Roland. I thought you could do it all.” And getting a little annoyed himself, Young walked over to the paper-filled desk causing LLB to stand, his Championship Title Belt resting on his arm. Young tapped it with his index fingernail. “Remember how you won it?” ‘The Law’ could only growl softly. “It’s the least you could do for little old me and the ratings.”
