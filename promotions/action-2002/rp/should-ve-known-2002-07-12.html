Should've Known

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/12/02 . . . Leitchfield, Kentucky | 15:30 PM CST . . . Conrad Ramsey is walking up the drive-way of the house he was ordered, as per his contract, to live in with tag team partner Max Danger. His head is lowered and shaking back and forth. CONRAD RAMSEY I should have known. I should have known... (he trails off) Conrad walks up the steps on the front porch. He walks to the door and digs in his pockets for his keys. Conrad puts one hand on the doorknob and starts to insert the key when he notices that it's unlocked. RAMSEY (to himself) Just what I need, a burgular. Conrad pushes the door open slightly, and slowly pokes his head through. He slowly creeps through the foyer, then leans up against the wall. He peeks around the wall and looks down the hall, that leads to the bedrooms. He then turns and looks down the other way, towards the kitchen. RAMSEY Doesn't look like anyone was here. (shrugs shoulders) Oh well. Conrad stops the creeping around and just walks straight across and into the living room. On the television is a piece of paper, being held up by a picture of Ramsey and Danger posing in their ring gear. Ramsey walks over to it. He grabs the paper and reads it. Old Man, I'm sure you're wondering about what happened at Pressure Point, and I'm sure you would love to talk about it. You do like to talk things out. Me, I'm more of an action person, so you'll know soon enough why I did what I did. As you have probably figured, my wife was not in accident, but she was let out of rehab, and that's where I am. I'll see you next week at Pressure Point. Your Bestest Tag Team Partner, Max Danger P.S. Your dinner is thawing in the sink. RAMSEY You want action, Danger? I'll give you action! Ramsey crumbles the paper into a ball and tosses it across the room. It hits the picture on top of the television, connecting directlly with Max Danger.
