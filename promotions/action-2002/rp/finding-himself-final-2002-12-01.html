Finding Himself(final)

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-01-02 Long nights were recently the only type of nights in the General’s life. Tossing and turning in his bed, getting tangled in his sheets, cold as a criminal’s heart or beaded in sweat. Every night had different effects on him. Every ounce of sleep he lost, he gave up that much more stamina, or lost that much more sanity. It was like gambling. Jonny never really knew what reason his body would find to avoid sleep, but he knew that he would linger through the nightly hours simply waiting for the sun to make it’s daily arrival. That’s how it had been for months. It ended nights ago though. Jonny won’t ever be able to explain to anyone. The feeling he got on the night revolving around Action’s Last Laugh event. He won’t ever be able to put it into words, but he won’t ever forget the emotions that unfolded throughout that hectic yet melodic night. Jonny was pleased with how the night had turned out. Airborn Tyler was no longer an enemy, no longer a hangnail constantly being irritated and hindering Jonny’s life. Truth be written, Jonny would never like the kid. He was a pompous son of bitch that took things for granted. Not to be mistaken though, Jonny does have the utmost respect for the youngster. After the grueling battles the two shared, Jonny would be damned if he didn’t respect the kid for putting his heart into every match made mutual by the General and the Airborn. Both men had given their all in the match, and while Jonny was still aching in places he didn’t know could ache...he was glad he had sacrificed his body so many times. It was worth it when the fans voiced their opinions. So far, Jonny hadn’t come across a disappointed fan. Then, there was the virus match. Going into this match, Jonny didn’t really have the slightest clue what in the hell was going on. He didn’t know what download or upload meant. He didn’t know what the terms had to do with wrestling. Hell, Jonny wouldn’t have even known he had been eliminated if the fans hadn’t of voiced their sentiments when the time came. Jonny was one of the final four though. That was something to be immensely proud of. And he was. Jonny was proud. While he didn‘t win the match...he had proven that he was capable of going toe to toe with the most talented men in the business. It was a euphoric sensation Jonny had knowing that he was that good. Perhaps it was a stroke of luck, Jonny hoped it wasn’t, he knew sooner or later he would find out for sure. But as far as the General was concerned... He had found himself. And so tonight, he sleeps like a child again. Drawing long content breaths in, and slowly releasing them. He’s dreaming of good things, we don’t know what...but a smile is embedded on his weathered face. All is well in the Union camp.
