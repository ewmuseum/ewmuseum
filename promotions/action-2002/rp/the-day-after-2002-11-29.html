The Day After

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/29/02 What 'The Danger Man' was up doing. Thanksgiving dinner had been wild. It started off fine, but slowly began to unravel. It broke down, much like Samantha Dox did in the middle of it, and turned into utter mayhem. Iceman and Mo Ron versus Blast, Food Fight! Marcus Alexander Dox vs. Jessica Barett-Dox, The Who-Can-Shove-Their-Tongue-The-Furthest-Down-The-Other's-Mouth... uh... tha's it. Bradley Duncan and Conrad Ramsey watching from across the room. Gus continuing to eat. Then Samantha went crazy. Sounded like crying but was soon realized to be hysterical laughter. She said the things going on reminded her of the Dox Thanksgiving dinners before Max was born and when he was a young child. There was laughing all around after it was over. From everyone but Xander and Jessica. Jessica stormed off into her bedroom, as Danger slumped down in his seat and remained quiet the rest of the night. Clean up was handled by Samantha, Conrad, and Bradley. Iceman and Mo Ron had went outside, while Gus laid out on the floor, sleeping. Blast kept banging on Jessica's door, trying to get her to come out. She wouldn't. Max sat at the breakfast nook table, slumped as far as a person could, and looking out of it, not there. Distant. He remained there all night. Didn't move a muscle. When morning came, he had quietly entered his bedroom and gathered up some clothes and his wrestling gear. He stopped to look at his wife, sleeping soundlessly, looking beautiful as the moon shined through the blinds and onto her face. He left, leaving nothing but a note, the moment the sun began to rise. Everyone, We've gone. Signed, Xander, Iceman, and Mo Ron He had to get out of the house. Iceman and Mo Ron felt the same way. They didn't want to hang around any longer. So they all three went to Buffalo. Heading to the PIW show on Saturday. It was going to be a big night for Max, too, defending the Tag Team Titles against Accidie in the main event. A huge night. He didn't know how it was going to go. He and Bradley had been in the same house, but weren't near each other. They didn't even attempt to make contact. Now they were miles apart. Felt that way when they were nearby, but now, it was the truth. Iceman and Mo Ron occupied themselves by making lame jokes to the flight attendants. They laughed, despite the lameness. They couldn't be thrown off, right? Not while in the air. Right? RIGHT!? Right. Back at the house, Samantha and Conrad were reading the newspaper at the breakfast table. Jessica was keeping to herself, despite the constant yammering by Blast. He followed her everywhere. 'Why is she with him?' Samantha whispered to Conrad. He just shook his head. He didn't understand it either. Jessica was putting up Christmas decorations all around the house. It was looking nice. Outside, Gus was being aided by Bradley Duncan, with the lights. Duncan was smiling and having a good time, for the first time. Gus was doing a bang-up job. 'My house is going to win again, Gus!' shouted Mr Harris from across the street. He and his son were putting up their decorations, too. Gus shouted back, 'Oh no you won't! When I'm finished with this place, you will know that a new power has arrived on this street. There will be no denying our victory!' Both Gus and Harris laughed. It was all in good fun. All in the spirit of the season. Duncan motioned to Gus that he was going to get them some coffee. Gus nodded and continued his work. Back inside, Samantha was helping Jessica put up the Christmas tree, as Blast continued to be only a few inches away from Jessica. The two ladies spoke on everything that wasn't Xander. 'Jessica! I'm hungry!' Blast moaned. 'There's food in the kitchen, dear.' Samantha told him. She really didn't like him. Didn't help matters that he wasn't wearing a shirt. His big, fat gut hanging over his shorts, which if you looked from the front, you wouldn't know he was wearing any. 'But I can't make what I want.' 'What do you want!?' snapped Jessica. She, too, was getting agitated. 'Um.... toast.' Conrad nearly laughed out loud from behind his paper, but he fought it. Jessica sighed and lead Blast into the kitchen. Samantha sat back down next to Conrad. 'She'd make an excellent mother.' she said. 'Has a lot of love. She cares about everyone.' 'I know. She helped me when I was injured. I was in bad shape... thanks to your son, by the way... but, she helped, and I'm glad she did. She's a good one.' Ramsey responded. 'What do you think of this situation?' 'It's a game. That's my feelings. But he took it hard. Especially at first. Rambling, ranting, raving. It wasn't a pretty sight. But he's calmed over time. Still misses her, I'd wager. A lot of love for her, he has. I just don't get it, though. Doesn't make a whole lot of sense.' 'No, it doesn't. Kids.' she chuckled. 'Yup. Kids.' he smiled at her. They stared at each other for a moment, then Jessica returned, speaking to Samantha, 'Should we use the tinsel? I don't really like it. Xa... um... I don't really like it.' Samantha stood up, shooting Ramsey a look on the way up. Duncan had gotten the coffee, and went back outside in the background. Mom headed over to Jessica. 'Well, do what you want.' 'I LIKE TINSEL!' shouted Blast from the kitchen, who was eating his toast... with butter, syrup, peanuts, and whipped cream on top of it. 'No tinsel it is then.' Jessica said, with a smile to Samantha. She shared the smile. 'So, what time is everyone heading out?' It was a statement to all those in the room. Everyone had to be somewhere, and only Gus was going to be hanging around the house. He had to finish up the Christmas decorations. 'Bradley, Blast, and I will be heading out at six o'clock tonight. That's when we leave here. Flight doesn't leave until eight thirty.' She nodded her head and turned to Conrad. 'Oh, I'm here until Sunday.' She smiled. 'You know, I think I'll stay and finish up the Christmas decorations for you, Jess.' 'Ok.' They shared a smile. Samantha then turned back to Conrad. She winked.
