Vagabond - VII

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/05/2002 Previous Parts: 1, 2, 3, 4a, 4b, 5, 6 Awakened by the sound of knocking, the man known as Coral Avalon stirred awake from his sofa. He was really hoping that it was just his imagination that he had heard a slight thumping noise emanating from his front door of his apartment. Unfortunately for him, it wasn't his imagination, it was really being heard, and the knocking was louder and more incessant until he finally shouted 'I'm coming!' and left the comfort of his sofa. He groggilly rose to his feet, brushed his long black hair back, and wandered in the direction of the door. He opened it and was greeted by a familiar face and a not-so-familiar face. 'Hey, Avy.' Joey Malone greeted. Coral greeted him with a yawn, then acknowledged him. 'Been awhile.' he said. He looked at the girl standing behind Joey, and then looked back toward Joey. 'Oh, no. Don't tell me Keri kicked you out or somethi-' 'It's not like that.' Joey quickly interrupted. 'May we come in?' Coral stepped aside and nodded, allowing for Joey Malone and Allison Lindum to enter his small apartment. Coral didn't have a whole lot of money, so he was only able to afford a small, slightly run-down apartment on the west side of New Orleans. He was only able to maintain it through his wrestling in the area, and only barely. At just twenty years old, though, Avalon had an entire future in the business ahead of him. 'I suppose I should ask who the girl is?' Avalon inquired. 'Allison. I'm Joey's sister-in-law.' Allison replied. 'He asked me to bring him here, since he doesn't have a car right now. Or much of anything, for that matter.' 'Oh.' Coral said. He paused a moment before he decided to ask his next question. 'So, what're you doing here? Don't you have a job to do, like me?' That's when Joey sat down and explained his entire mess with Syphon Fission. The physical and mental torture. Keri's kidnapping. The stipulations of the match at Juggernaut. The fact that Fission had essentially caused Joey to cease to exist. Coral had but two words to respond to when Joey was finished. 'Holy shit. That... uhm. Sucks.' 'Yeah, don't I know it?' Joey said. 'I really thought I could handle it by myself, you know? Like I always have. I mean, this has become a regular thing with me and Keri. New situation, I handle it, we party. This is different. This is more than just a sister-takes-my-money type of deal, or even a talentless-hack-showing-no-respect type of deal. This is definitely a homicidal-lunatic-has-my-wife-and-I-at-his-mercy type of deal. I really didn't want to get my friends or my family involved in this, but when I was heading here, I suddenly realized that I am not the almighty, invincible Joey Malone that I used to be. I realized that I can't handle everything on my own.' 'I don't know how, when, or where I'm going to do it, but I am going to fight Fission again. And you, Coral, you're going to help me.' Joey finished, sighing as he did so. Coral blinked. 'Uh, me?' he asked. 'Me?!' 'Yes. You. You're my student, there's bound to be some things that you could teach me.' Joey said. 'There's a reason why you were my only student, Avy. It's because you would've always remained my best, no matter what.' Coral struggled to find the words to express how much that meant to him. 'Th-th... er... thanks.' Coral then thought about it for a second. 'Um. Wait. When're you going to start getting ready?' he asked. 'About three hours before I got here.' Joey replied, with a smile. Coral had to blink at that one.
