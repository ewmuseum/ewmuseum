Date: 01.02.03 Monday, January 8th, 1990. Jordan Copeland was now 14, and in the eighth grade. He was going to a school in suburban Toronto; Ontario Canada called Birch Cliff Heights. It was years after he was teased and mistreated by the other children in the neighborhood, it was a new era, so to speak. Now African Americans were accepted into the neighborhood, there were a few other African American families living in the neighborhood, but naturally, Jordan was the only one with white parents. It was the first day back at school after the 'Christmas' break. Jordan was very excited and very happy. The break had provided him with some above average gifts, some clothes, some shoes, some toys that were fit for a fourteen year old, like a remote control car, and a small portable game. But that was just a little part of the things that made him happy, the major thing, was that he was going to Quebec on a school trip for their French class. The Child Protective Services (Called Children's Aid) had paid for the entire trip to Quebec, including the hotel room, food expenses, and money for souvenirs. They were to be living in a very good and fancy hotel that cost $99 a night. They were to be set up in groups of four, some five, for people whom you would room with. Jordan decided to room with a few of his friends, Gordie Munro, Cody Downard, and Andrew Jacobs. The bus ride from Scarborough was long and fun, they watched movies, played games, and just talked about what they would do when they got to Quebec. When they finally arrived, nine hours later, they quickly went to their hotels and changed into their special 'formal' clothes. They were to be at a dinner in a fancy Italian restaurant and they were supposed to dress up as best as they could to look good for the night. Jordan made sure that he sat down at the table where the person he had a crush on, Hayley Smith, was going to sit. They were friends, but secretly Jordan wanted to be a lot more than that. Never before had Jordan liked a girl not only for her looks, but also for her personality. Jordan dressed up in the expensive suit that his foster parents had bought him, and he sprayed himself with cologne to make sure that Hayley would notice him in a way that she had never noticed him before. That night, Jordan, along with a host of his friends, had a great night at the dinner, and then later at a formal dance. Jordan danced with Hayley a few times, friendly dance of course. Then Jordan pulled Hayley aside from the rest of the group and asked to talk to her in private. 'Hayley, you know I've known you for a while now... and, uhm... Heh, well, I like you. I REALLY like you,' Jordan said in an awkward tone. Hayley looked Jordan in the eyes... and laughed, 'Jordan, you know I like you, just not like that,' she said. Jordan was crushed. He laughed it off and claimed he was joking, but they both knew that he wasn't. His heart was broken. Jordan spent the last few days of the trip moping secretly, and putting on a brave face for his friends and for everybody he would meet. It was another turning point in his life.
