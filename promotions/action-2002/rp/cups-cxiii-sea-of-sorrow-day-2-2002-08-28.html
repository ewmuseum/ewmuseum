Cups: cXIII: Sea of Sorrow (Day 2)

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 28, 2002 Cups: cXIII: Sea of Sorrow Day 2 - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - James continuously read away at the book. At the time, he started on the second day ... 'I don't see why she did this to herself! Why couldn't she have told someone? Why couldn't she have told me? I could have helped her.'         James seemed disgraced at his actions. As if he had done something wrong, he opened his eyes even wider and read away the next few days ... and last days of his love's life. ‘Day 2 Exhausted even more today. Sleep only seemed to devour me even deeper and deeper into the pits of destruction. How could I go on any longer? Sometimes ... I just wish it would end quickly. I just wish I could get out of this mess. I just ate another one. The small capsule that I took will take me in a few minutes. Up and down a road so bumpy ... no one could even imagine. It's great in a way. It's terrible in a way. It's destructive in a way. It's EVERYTHING in a way. It can be anything that you want it to be. For me, it's a form of escape. If I'm on the ride, I'm free. Indestructible to some, vulnerable to others. It's hard to tell what I am now. Days go by, and I don't even know it. I don't even remember it. Wasting away in my own little world. It was the way I wanted to go though. I guess there is nothing at all left for me now. I guess ... I'll just die like the rest. Friendships, I have none. Well, there was one. Just one ... only one. The funny thing is. The guy didn't even know it. Yet, this was before all of the drugs. This was before all of the hallucinations. This was before everything that unfolded my life before my eyes. This was when the bullet went through us both. Now that I think about it, I really want to see him. Maybe he could fix me. Maybe he could bring me out of this state of trance. My ability to function is fading. My life along with it. It ... seems like I've spoke to him before. Maybe in a dream. Maybe on a trip. It's hard to tell where I last pictured him. Although, he was ... beautiful. This is only the second day I've wrote in this junked up book, and I'm going off and talking about someone who I could have only imagined. Someone with the capabilities to save me. Take me down from the cloud at which I rest. TAKE ME OUT OF HERE!' The handwriting on the letter nearly faded away. James passionately stared at the book. He tried to bring some meaning out of it. Unfolding before him, still yet he couldn't see the truth. James realized that he could have helped her.         'I could have saved a life. I could have been a hero. I could have been someone to remember. Yet, I took the long ride across the sea of sorrows. I guess ... I'm fading, myself.'
