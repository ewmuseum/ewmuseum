Action! — Tales

The Champion

8 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OPEN He had done it. He had become immortalized. He won the Action Championship. He is Christopher Fox. Fox “Bluuuuuuuuuuurp!!!!! Excuse me! I guess I shouldn’t have drunk all those vanilla milkshakes!” Just a ‘Kid’ in a mans world? Maybe, but he’s a damn good kid. Title: The Champion Date: 03/03/03 11:00 pm EST Place: A limo Chris Fox sat beside his Action Wrestling Championship Belt, as the leather settled while Fox rested deeper into the seat. The destination was the next city for Pressure Point. The place where last week Christopher overcame insurmountable odds and defeated the ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear to become the new Action Champion! After winning the big money match, Chris decided to ride with a little more style. He could do whatever he wanted. After all he is the Champion.... Fox “Ah, hey Pikachu....” Fox pulled his plastic bud out of his duffle bag and set him on the title. Fox “Do you know what that is?” .... Fox “YES! It’s the Action Championship! I’m the Champ.... BIG-- I’ll save that for later.” Time to relax and enjoy that big win over Cylear. Those friends of mine sure were proud. Did you see the way they celebrated with me? Yes Chris I did, after all I was there. Tee hee, I love chugging milkshakes with my friends! I can’t wait till Monday! A beeping sound from a cell phone broke the thoughts of ‘Kid’ right away. Fox “Wha- wha? What’s that? ....Oh it’s my cell phone....” He picked it up. Fox “Heto, Christopher Elliot Fox the new Action Wrestling Champion at your service, how may I help you?” Ben “Hey Chris! It’s me, Ben, the janitor you talked to before your show two weeks ago, you remember me right?” Fox “Hey yeah! You gave me some great advice, thanks a lot! It helped me so much that I became the Action Champion!” Ben “I’m glad you won. Umm can I ask you something?” Fox “Sure ting!” Ben “Next time you come into town I want to see you again! Ok?” Chris shrugged his shoulders and gave a nod. Fox “Otay. I’ll come see you next time I’m in town! Until then, chow!” Click, and just like that Ben had become a member of a very big club, the Chris Fox Friend Club! Suddenly the mood changed as Christopher stared into his cell phone which he just clicked off. Fox “Hmmmmmm....” Heh, I shouldn’t be all that happy, I mean LL and everything, how could he ever come back full time.... Fox “Well Pikachu, I sure hope that my bestest friend in the whole wide world, LL WILL be at the next show....” .... Fox “Thanks Pikachu, I know he’s proud too. Big Time!!.... .....Big Time.” The scene ended with a proud but wondering Christopher Fox sitting in the limo, staring now at his Title Belt.... because he was indeed, The Champion. CLOSE
