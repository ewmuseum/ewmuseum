Action! — Tales

Desire.

31 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/31/2002 Action! Desire. Action! Desire? Oh, dear, this is one of Reed Young's ripoff tricks, isn't it? We see images of Joey, two years ago, as he was teaming up with ex-tag team partner Daniel Phillips. We get a few images of their team finisher, the 'Fated Hurricane', as it hits various people. Meanwhile, we're getting a voiceover from Joey himself. You know, one day, some guy... you know, just this guy. He came up to me as I was walking down a street in downtown New Orleans. And he was all like 'Ahahaha, you're Joey Malone! You're the lame guy who wrestles! Ha ha!'. And I was like 'Your mom'. Yup, this is the stuff that makes me want to be a wrestler. Hello, my friend we meet again, It's been a while, Where should we begin? Feels like forever. A few shots of Joey coming down the aisle to the ring, including his explosion entrance a la Jericho from IWO, and his normal entrance today. We see images of the fans as they cheer Joey while he's on the turnbuckles. Then, the day after that, a couple of girls snickered in my direction. I don't know, maybe they were just snickering at the ugly guy sitting behind me in the toupee while he was failing in his attempts to hit on some girls, but the point is that it lowered my self-esteem. Yet, I press on! Within my heart are memories, Of perfect love that you gave to me, I remember... We see a few shots of Joey Malone holding up various championship belts in victory. This includes: The IWO Intercontinental Tag Titles(along with Phillips), the IWO United States title, the IWO World Heavyweight title(with him bleeding), the LoW World Tag Titles(along with Doozer), the Action! Bantam title(with him bleeding and splattering the belt with his own blood), and the most recent shot where he held up both the Bantam and Carnage titles. Oh yeah. And this one time, I was playing Scrabble with Keri and she was kicking my ass because the entire English language was stolen out of my head, as if I was being forced to watch 'Feardotcom'. That was no fun. We see various shots of Joey Malone in action. Mainly of him hitting things with chairs. Donnie Daze? Hit with a chair. Sabastian Crow? Hit with a chair. Incessantly. Kent Anthason? Hit with a chair. Syphon Fission? Hit with a chair. And a shovel. And what can only be described as the entire Jules Asner High School Marching Band. The guy in Memphis that was standing on the corner of Summer and Stage who was trying to bum a ride? Hit with a chair. When you are with me, I'm free...I'm careless...I believe, Above all the others, we'll fly, This brings tears to my eyes, My sacrifice. Have I mentioned how many times some guy tried to hit me with a chair? My god! It doesn't even have to be provoked. Like, this one time, a guy screamed 'COBRA!' at me and charged me with a chair. Then he hit me, and I was all 'Owie' for a little while. Has it been said just how loved I felt after that one? We see more shots of Joey Malone in action. Hitting people with his Everest Cataclysm. First, we get pictures of guys being picked up in the move. Kent Anthason. Syphon Fission. Matt Remicho. AWS Man (also known as Bill). Donnie Daze. Syphon Fission(again, from a different angle). For those of you in attendance who suffer from elipsy, watch your back, because the screen flashes from black and white to negative quickly, while the people are still being picked up in the Cataclysm. Then, they start getting dropped. All of them. Anthason! Fission! Remicho! That Guy Who Says Freak A Lot! Daze! Fission(again)! Regis Philbin! Bob Saget! Even the guy from the Verizon Wireless ads who are on the phone and he's all 'Do you hear me, now? Good!'! We've seen our share of ups and downs, Oh, how quickly life can turn around in an instant, It feels so good to reunite, Within yourself and within your mind, Let's find peace there. I also love my fans! They bring me all kinds of stuff for me to sign. I've signed boring things like their T-shirts or their autograph books... then they give me wacky things to sign. I once had to sign this guy's cow, and he wanted me to write this big novel for him on the side of the cow. I had to write to his whole family, individually... and he had this long last name and about thirty cousins. We see a shot of Joey Malone getting injured. AWS Man (also known as Bill) superkicking him in the knee and injuring it. Joey missing a legdrop on a C4 pallet in the first ever Life, Death, and Endurance match. Joey falling off of a roof and cracking his tailbone. Joey taking Faded Dreams. Etcetera, etcetera. When you are with me, I'm free...I'm careless...I believe, Above all the others, we'll fly, This brings tears to my eyes... I was once at a party in my honor. I got drunk and passed out on the DJ's turntables. I'm sure you really needed to know that. More chairshottage. This time, Simon Seaman meets the chair. Donnie Daze gets incessantly beaten down. Syphon Fission, Dane Matthews, and Sabastian Crow all get the chair dropkicked in their face. Then this little kid gets hit with a plastic chair by another little kid... we're not sure where that came from. I suppose that what I've sacrificed is simple enough. I don't get to watch Friends any more. And, uh, when I do, I'm usually finding it difficult to move to change the channel, thanks to my injuries. You know, I might need to do that, in case it's the episode I really don't like. You know the one. It's... not terribly good. My sacrifice. We see the final image of Joey Malone celebrating a close win, with the referee raising an exhausted Malone's hand in the air. This fades out to the final two words of the video. Action! Desire. We are so going to get sued.
