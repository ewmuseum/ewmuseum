The Beta: Ejected From A Car: 1

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/24/02 Sometime between 11:30 and 12:00 PM, EST last night in Detroit. ----------------------- I have won the first battle. You do not know it yet, but I have. Syphon Fission was beaten to a bloody pulp, right? Only in body. NOT IN SOUL! I can barely see. My eyes are swollen shut. I can feel the burning emanating from my forehead, my nose, my mouth, both eye sockets, the top of my forehead. I do not know how many stitches they have put into my face to piece it all back together. Must have been a lot. I can barely see from my left eye. I do know I am in a hospital in Detroit. That is known. I can see they put me in a room with a low maintenance young lady. She must have had some sort of surgery. She keeps staring at my face. She cringes. I know. I can feel those compassionate eyes, giving sympathy to me. I love it. I am the good guy once I am out of the ring. I am just like anyone else in this world, in their eyes. STUPID IDIOTS! I banked on that sympathy. I knew they all would feel this way about me. After all, what I do isn’t real. MORONS! My face is a mess. It will take some time to rebuild into what it was. I wasn’t the epitome of beauty, but the ugliness fits. I feel ugly. I act ugly. I AM UGLY! I can hear her asking me questions. “What happened?” “Are you awake?” “Can you hear me?” Each time I want to SPEAK I can only speak mangled jargon. I expect this to seize in time for my plan to progress toward next week. Pressure Point Nine. It continues. The chess game is going great. I AM WINNING! MALONE IS TOO STUPID TO NOTICE! I have to go to Joe Louis Arena in a matter of hours. I MUST get my baby back. I left Payback there. I know he is going to be angry, but he understands. He screamed for Malone to stop, not wanting to hurt me. I am his master. A sort of father. He wants what I want. He NEEDS what I need. Freedom. Malone freed me earlier tonight. I am able to sleep for a couple hours now. I am not worrying and fretting over what could have been a huge failure. Kenneth wouldn’t be pleased. He talks to me as well. Syphon Fission, with his coach. Kenneth Rockefeller We think a like. He tells me how he felt when he pulled this stunt. He thought he shouldn’t be stuck there. He is too insecure. He is too willing to rush things and FUCK THEM up. Kenny made too many mistakes in his plan. I have already written in depth everything I planned on doing. It includes every scenario. EVERY SINGLE ONE! I pick myself up, and look through my blood shot left eye. The only one I can see from. I look for my clothes. I hear that girls pleas to stop. That I am only hurting myself more. I will be back in an hour, well before those people go and do their checks again. I have to get my child back. I have to talk to Kenneth, tell him everything is okay. I am showing Joey what forever means. And it is slowly dawning on him. Everything is coming around… Forming together… Just perfect.
