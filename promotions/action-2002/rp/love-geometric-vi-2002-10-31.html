Love Geometric - VI

31 October 2002

Date: 10/31/2002 October 23rd, 2002 Wednesday, Noon 'I don't know...' Keri said, quietly. After about ten minutes of looking for something for her to wear, Kent had managed to get her into a pair of leather pants and a white tanktop. 'I haven't worn this in a while, it seems constricting.' The two of them had also finally left the Malone residence and were now walking around Arizona Mills, a mall in Phoenix. It was Keri's favorite haunt when she went shopping, and it was also Joey's worst nightmare to go to, due to this. Kent was indifferent about it, either way. The two eventually stopped the rest at a bench right close to an electronic store and a few clothing stores. They were noticably sitting closer to each other than they were just a few hours ago in Keri's home. 'I think you look good.' Kent said with a smile. The two gazed into each other's eyes briefly, before Keri turned to face in front of her again. She was having the oddest feeling about her situation right now. With Joey. With Kent. Her life became more and more complicated as things went on. As she contemplated this, she saw the someone that could quite possibly ruin everything regarding her life if she didn't send Kent away. Someone that was supposed to still be in New York. Coral Avalon. Along with Allison by his side. Coral hadn't spotted Keri, yet, and probably wouldn't until he was close. However, Coral was walking toward area she was. She quickly tapped Anthason on the shoulder. Anthason turned quickly to Keri, who just simply pointed at Coral and Allison. Anthason went wide-eyed. 'What's he doing here?' he whispered to Keri, who simply responded with a shrug. Anthason realized that having Coral see the two of them together would not fare well with either of them, and he ducked into the nearby clothing store. Keri hoped and prayed that Coral Avalon had not seen Kent Anthason.
