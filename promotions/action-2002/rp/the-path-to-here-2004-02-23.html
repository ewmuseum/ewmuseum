Action! — Tales

The Path to Here.

23 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Years ago, people would have known Chad Villain as Seth Davis. A man who did so much for his father’s company, a man who tried so hard for it to be one of the best, but as every individual does at one point in there life, he failed. The New York Wrestling Organization was a small federation built around young and talented superstars only to provide the world with great excitement and it was just that for the first half of its existence. Seth’s father, Paul Davis, depended on his son to bring the company to the top and therefore he was given the main angle of the federation. It was supposed to be a simple, but exciting angle. Although, it turned into an uprising because of some peoples thoughts. The staff had disputes between one another, the wrestlers disliked the spotlight they were given, and others hated the fact that Seth was given the main angle. Seth’s father on the other hand wanted to give everyone there desired wants, but it was impossible to do such a thing. He could not please everyone, he could only do so much. The only thing he could do, was to tell everyone what they wanted to hear, “I promise each person on my roster that they will get the spotlight they desire, at one point in this federation, but we must take one step at a time.” Those were the words that settled and ended some disputes within the walls of the NYWO. But not all. Things went ok for the next couple of months. Everyone was getting along, everyone started acting like a family for a change. But an accident occurred. It happened on a Pay-per-view, ‘Collision Course.’ The entire show had went very well, Paul loved the ratings he was getting, and the profits he had made for the night. He thought nothing would go wrong. And that’s when everything began to go wrong. The main event was a match that was to end a long going feud between Seth Davis and Terrell Mason for the Heavyweight Title. The feud had been great and intensifying, the fans simply enjoyed it. The match was supposed to be Seth gaining the victory over Mason, but Mason disapproved. Mason wanted the title for himself, he was tired of watching the owner’s son get the spotlight, he wanted a change, and he thought that the black man should be in the spotlight for once. Paul realized the situation at hand as the match progressed. What they had planned, wasn’t happening. Instead, a friend of Mason’s made an unexpected appearance. The unnamed man and Mason had whipped Seth into the ropes looking for a back body drop but instead, they received a clothesline. Seth quickly position the unnamed man on his shoulders and dropped him on his neck. Immediately, he knew had injured him. Though, he had to finish the match. He did so by delivering the same maneuver to Mason and pinning him for the one, two, three. Seth gained the victory and the Heavyweight Title. But as he celebrated, Mason’s friend and partner was rushed to a hospital, only to find out that he had been paralyzed from the neck down. Mason was enraged, he wanted revenge. Paul Davis on the other hand, wanted his contract terminated, and barred from every event that was held. No longer would he be accepted in his federation. The next night, on there Monday Night Show, Mason obtained his vengeance. Seth had title defense against the number one contender, Shawn Fox. As Seth was about to pin Fox for the victory, Mason emerged. He ran down the steel ramp as fast as possible and slid into the ring as Seth had Fox pinned. Mason pulled Seth to his feet, punching him in the face. Each hard hit sent Seth farther and farther away from reality until finally, he fell from it. Mason used the same maneuver that caused his best friend’s career to end, wanting it to do the same to Seth. Security immediately rushed to the scene but Mason fled and was never seen again for quite sometime. Seth Davis had been temporally paralyzed and that led Paul Davis to his grave, leaving Seth in a puddle of guilt and shame. He blamed himself and Terrell Mason for the fall of his father’s company, and his death. He had failed, in his mind. He had failed to protect his father’s company. And overall, he had failed to protect his father. Several years afterwards, he was able to walk again. A miracle? Yes. So what was he to do, where was he to go now that he was able to walk again? That was the question he asked himself as he lived off garbage and slept in a box on the side of a street. He hand nothing, until he found the one thing he’d never thought he’d go back to. Wrestling… Action! Wrestling. And all thanks went Reed Young… And his alcohol.
