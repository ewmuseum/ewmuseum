Action! — Tales

Boring Battlefield

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 24th, 2002 A battlefield must have many qualities. It must be filled with so much emotion, and so much excitement, that a mere mortal would quiver in the mere thought of the battle. A battlefield must have challenging warriors, warriors that will challenge one another in a fight of blood, honor and respect. A battlefield must be filled with mayhem, an all out brawl between respected warriors. Action! is not that kind of battlefield. Action! is a battlefield filled with nothing but mockery. Its supposed warriors do not challenge me, and are the weakest excuses for warriors that I have ever seen. They bring in these constant weaklings, why? to anger me? The last time I actually had a battle was with Bobby Minio. Ever since, I have been dormant in this pathetic excuse for a battlfield. There is no blood, no honor, no respect in this battlefield. No, far from it. There is milkshakes, and teddy bears, and love, and matches entitled 'Til Death Due Us part'! This is a battlefield? It resembles a god damn childrens party! We have disgusting acts of sexuality on Action! We have throw-around weaklings on Action! Do we have challenging warriors? NO! Do we have mayhem? NO! I am left to face people like ERA, Bobby Minio, Disciple X, and other poor excuses for wrestlers. Action! is not what I expected. Action! was supposed to be one of the greatest battlefields I ever encountered, it was supposed to draw blood from my body, and make me LOVE the challenges ahead. But the only semi-challenge that has gotten into my path is one named BOREDOM. And that is not even a challenge in itself....it is a TERRIBLE emotion. I am BORED with this Action!, I am SICK of this Action!, I am TIRED of this Action! A warrior cannot be in a battlefield, when there is no suitable battle for him to take place in. A warrior cannot keep his savage heart beating when all he has to feed his hunger for a challenge....is weak hearted pathetic excuses for human beings. But what a warrior CAN do....is change his battlefield's setting. What a warrior CAN do is make his battlefield contain mayhem, emotion and honor. What a warrior CAN do is make his battlefield....interesting. And I am a warrior, one that thrives on the challenges of life. I am warrior, one that lives for the battle filled with mayhem. And if I must cause that mayhem...than I will. Action! is testing my patience....and my patience is wearing thin. And I will make Action! much more interesting. I will make it the battlefield that I want. I WILL fill it with mayhem. I will make it...my challenge. Because I am a warrior...and that is what a TRUE warrior would do. And I will do it. Because fate sees it to be that way. Fate sees it that I, KroW, must MAKE and FORCE Action! to be the battlefield that I want. And if that is the way fate sees it...so be it. I will have no remorse on Action! whatsoever. Absolutely No Remorse.
