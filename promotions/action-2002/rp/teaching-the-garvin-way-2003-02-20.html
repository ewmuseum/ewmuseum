Teaching the Garvin Way

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sometimes you have to think to yourself: Do I really deserve this? Jeff Garvin had been wondering this for quite some time. It didn’t matter how hard he worked things never went the way he wanted. He had the ability, the skill, and the heart to be the top man anywhere he went. Yet, he never could get the job done, and why? Because of things beyond his control; you can call it bad luck or even a curse, but it still resulted in things going to shit for him. ‘For too long, I’ve sat back and let things fuck with my dreams and I’m sick and tired of it. It happened in 21w and look where that got me; the streets, back to square fucking one. Well I’m through sitting back and taking this shit, it’s time to grab fate by the horns and ride that fucking bull to success…’ Monday, 3:45 PM… Location: Memphis, Tennessee. The recreation centre was almost completely lifeless with most working class folk still stuck in their dead end jobs. After the debacle of Blood, Sweat, and Tears, with Chris Chambers, student of Jeff Garvin, throwing in the towel right before Garvin reached the ropes while in the Hammer Jammer, causing him to loose the match… and the King of Submission trophy, Chambers hadn’t spoken to his mentor much. Things had gone sour between them and it was his fault. He jumped the gun, and while worried for Jeff’s well being, cost him something that meant more to ‘the Original’ than any championship: the right to be called the best submission based wrestler in Action! Wrestling. That night, he had driven Jeff and his wife to the airport… but left at different times. Jeff had told him he had no desire to stare at his face for the six hour plane ride. It hurt him that his teacher could be so cold hearted even after he apologised multiple times. What more did he want? He know how much he respected his in ring work and all that he had don in wrestler at only twenty four years of age… Never, not in a million years would he intentionally do such a thing had he not deemed it necessary. His muscles flexed as he pushed the bar bell up, breathing out and breathing in deeply, ready for another rep. Then the weights seem to grow even heavier, almost impossible to lift. “How ya’ doin’ pal?” Jeff glared down at his student, who’s arms had begun to quiver with this additional weight pushing down on them. Leaning against the bar, Jeff grinned and scratched the back of his neck casually. “You know, what you did was complete bullshit.” Chambers was desperately trying to keep a straight face and not show any sort of emotion. For if Garvin was testing him, he did not want to fail. He needed to succeed and show Jeff he wasn’t some punk who was going to quit when things got a little rough. His mouth open, slowly, now quivering like his arms, and he spoke a single word. “Yes.” Nodding, Jeff released his weight from the bar bell. Chambers, exhausted, let the weights slip off the end and dropped bar to the side. He gasped for air, his eyes closed. Boy what he wouldn’t give to just fall asleep right then and there… Maybe that would end the burning pain that plagued his biceps. The arrogance shown through his smile while he gazed down at his student: he wasn’t as happy as he was trying to make himself out to be. He turned and took a drink from the water fountain behind him “…You should also know, you haven’t been forgiven. You don’t fuck over Jeffrey Lucas Garvin and get hugs and kisses the next day. I’m not a saint, I don’t hand out second chances like a Red Cross Worker hands out fresh needles to heroin addicts. If you want my respect, you have to earn it. And Chris, judging by the BS you pulled last night; that may take you a while.” Chambers replied with a nod. “Normally, I would have smacked the taste out of your mouth for what you did… but I may need you. Good, loyal students are OH so hard to find these days, and I have a feeling that you won’t make a mistake like that again, you know that if you do, I’ll end your career before it even begins… Oh, and you’ll get your first chance to prove yourself against Airborn Tyler on the next Pressure Point. You win… You’re back on my good side. You don’t? Welcome to the hell that is being trained by me, ‘cause I will turn you into a human pretzel the second I get the chance.” Garvin chuckled and walked out of the gym, leaving Chambers contemplating what his mentor had just said. He knew then and there that he desperately needed to win this match…
