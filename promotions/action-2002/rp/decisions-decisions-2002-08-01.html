Action! — Tales

Decisions, decisions...

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/01/02 'What the hell is this crap?' Joey Malone wrinkled his nose at the package that came in the mail, the return address coming from Hollywood. His wife, Keri, looked over from the computer that she was sitting at, in the direction of Joey and said package. 'Oh, yeah... that Garvin fellow dropped that off while you were on the road.' Keri said. She had stayed home for the last few weeks, trying to get her mind off of the previous five months. 'I think they're screenplays he wanted you to view.' Joey groaned. How was he supposed to read through this stuff when he had to deal with the fact that he was also the Action! Bantam champion? 'Tremendous.' Keri would probably kill him if he said it, but he was starting to like the idea of being an actor even less and less. His body was finally conditioned to be on the road, now, after a while of being unconditioned. He felt like he could possibly give the whole world a freaking brainbuster if he so desired. He shrugged and opened the package. He flipped through a few pages of the screenplay, and groaned even worse. 'I'm supposed to be a slime demon in this. A freaking slime demon.' Joey said. Keri giggled. 'I guess they're looking for the teenage demographic.' she commented. Joey shot Keri a look and then quietly commented on it. 'No, they're just looking for the retarded people demographic that PIW looks for.' He sighed and looked through some more. He laughed uncontrollably, perking up Keri's interest. He finally calmed down and looked toward her. 'Oh man, Keri, I think I'm better off than you, at least I'm not a mermaid.' Keri turned toward Joey, and blinked. 'A merm- ...who writes this stuff?' she commented. 'I don't know about you, but I think that Whedon guy should sue for royalties.' Joey commented. Damn Allison and Coral for getting him into Buffy. Damn them both to Hell. 'Maybe Simon was right about the WB.' Keri said. Unfortunately, with Simon and Joey not being on the best of terms right now, Joey cringed at the mention of Simon's name, and bitterly decided to add his thoughts on it. 'Maybe.' He quickly changed the subject. 'Does television really need another show about supernatural elements or some other crap like that?' Joey said. 'Whatever happened to a good old fashioned police drama?' Keri blinked at Joey again. 'You don't like police dramas.' 'That's... not the point, Keri!' Joey said. 'Well, okay, it is the point. Er, I think...' He blinked and looked down at the screenplay again, before he groaned again. 'What in the hell? The main character's love interest is a two hundred and twenty-year old angel?! Alright. This is offically bunk.' With that, he tossed the screenplays to the floor with a thud. Becoming an actor was going to be harder than he thought.
