Reflecting On: Part Eleven.

19 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It had taken longer than expected to reach the hospital. It had donned on Xander that he wasn't sure where his mother's body had been sent. And the taxi driver had mentioned the fact that there were six different medical centers in Las Vegas. Xander thought for a moment and then just asked to be taken to the nearest on to the MGM Grand. That's where they were. Desert Springs Hospital. Jessica got out of the cab as her husband paid the fare. Eleven dollars. What a rip off. He then exited the cab and joined his wife. He took her hand in his own and the two of them stared at the front doors of the building. Both of them took a deep breath. And then they started forward. It hit Xander then, just as the doors slid open, that he had not been in Vegas since his brother had died back in 1997. All of it came rushing back to him. Hitting him like a mack truck. He had stopped, just in front of the wide open doors. Jessica had continued on, but when she was halted by the fact that her husband wasn't moving, she went back to him. 'I know this is a stupid question, but what's wrong?' she asked. It was the first they had spoken since arriving in Las Vegas. Xander just shook his head, 'Nothing.' 'No, it's something,' Jessica said. 'You have something face.' He smiled a little. She knew the way to his heart. Quoting Buffy. Worked every time. Unless used completely out of context, then it just upset him. But he shook his head again, 'Really, it's nothing important.' She didn't believe him for a second. Nor should she. He was there to see his dead mother, so it was bound to be important and a rather big something. She relented though, not wanted to push the matter. She, herself, was dealing with a multitude of emotions. None, she thought, on the level as her husband. But heavy nonetheless. The two approached the counter. It occured to Xander that they may be at the wrong hospital. He hadn't asked for specifics from Conrad. The news was just a bit too shocking at the them. They peered over the front desk. A woman sitting in a chair had just gotten off the phone and looked up at them. She could see the pain etched all over their faces. She saw it a lot. 'Yes, may I help you?' Xander cleared his throat and asked, 'Um, my mother was brought here earlier tonight.' It was just so hard. He could feel the emotions swimming in his veins, wanting to just burst out. He cleared his throat again, 'Samantha Dox.' The lady started typing away at the computer. She was scanning the screen with her eyes. She then looked up, saddness in her eyes, 'I'm very sorry for your loss.' Xander didn't respond. He wasn't here for condolences from strangers. Jessica, however, did reply, 'Thank you.' It was a quiet voice that spoke it. One that didn't seem too pleased with it either. Nice gesture and all, but they hadn't flown all the way across the country to hear people they were sorry. The lady continued, 'She's in the morgue, now. They performed an autopsy just a little while ago. Just take the hallway that way,' she said pointing right. 'There will be two double doors and then another stretch of hallway. There is a waiting room near there, for those awaiting autopsy reports. Just wait there.' Xander and Jessica nodded. They didn't even thank her for her help. They didn't much care either. Hand-in-hand they headed down the hallway. They had started out at a brisk walk, dodging other patients and doctors. Soon, though, their legs began to slow. Their thoughts were jumbled. They weren't sure what was going to happen. What they were going to do. And it scared them. Almost made them want to turn around and just leave. One scenario that Xander imagined was just that. He left and went home to Augusta and his mother was there, cooking breakfast. Far-fetched, yes. But it's how he wanted it. But the reality set in. And he was frightened. They reached the double doors and stopped. Xander looked down at his wife and she looked up at him. 'Ready?' asked Jessica, very unsure herself. Xander replied, 'Never.' A deep breath was had by both and the two pushed forward through the doors.
