For Worse.

13 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/13/02 Happy days were the current ones that came. The Key to the Courtroom stayed in tact; it was doing just fine. LLB sat alone in his house, resting up over the long few weeks. They were good. Far better than what the past had brought. ‘The Law’ rubbed his head, as to his indifference, the phone blew the sound waves off his charts, as LLB dove to pick it up, still in a calm manner. “Hello?” He said, as the man on the other line responded. “Hey Roland!” It was Reed Young. “Hey Reed, what can I do for you?” The Champion asked, oddly enough glancing over at his Championship Title Belt that sat on the table in front. “Well... I got some good news and some bad news.” Young said, starting to trail his voice off. LLB just sighed. “Give me the good news first then.” The Owner hesitated, but with another huff, he quickly got to the point, not wanting to piss Roland off at all. Yet, some would wonder... because the news no doubt would, regardless of the form it came. “After you left the Arena.” He said. “Fox basically took me by the balls and FORCED me to sign him a Rematch this coming show.” LLB’s eyes opened wide. “And man... you know how much sex I get... um... a lot. So, I was so worried, and I said okay.” Taking another look at his Championship, for some reason, LLB feared the worse. “And... it’s... um... it’s...” Young didn’t have to finish. LLB read him like a book. “For the Title. Fuck off Reed. I said I NEVER wanted to see him again!” “I- I know... but he had me by the balls!” Reed stated, trying to lie his way out of what really happened. Though if LLB ever found out the truth, Young might be killed. Anything to keep the ‘Key to the Courtroom’ in his sense. “You know how it feels... to think you’re DONE something, only to find out a few days later, it’s not over yet!?” Reed thought he knew, but then he remembered that he never finished anything, anyway. “Rowe, you gotta be more like me... and just DON’T finish things. Then, you’re not hurt.” He huffed again, this time a lot louder. “That’s not the way the law works.” “So change it-” “That’s not the way the law works.” “So change- oh... right. Well, don’t worry, cause I have a plan!” ‘The Law’ doubted that, so he just changed the subject. “Fuck Reed, you said that was the good news. What in Jesus’ name is the bad news, then?” Young smiled on the other line. “I got laid last night!” Shaking his head for the last time, LLB began to hang up the phone. “I mean... wait, that’s GOOD news!” Click. And taking another look at his Championship Title, LLB got up, grabbed it, and threw it to the ground.
