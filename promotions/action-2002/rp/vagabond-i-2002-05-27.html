Vagabond - I

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/27/2002 Beep... beep... beep... Hospital bed. Charlotte. Waking up in a hospital bed was nothing new for Joey Malone. A particularly brutal match sometimes left him here. When he blew out his knee, he was here. Both Life, Death, and Endurance matches had left him here. When he injured himself at the Mall Brawl match, he was here. Beep... beep... beep... But every time Joey woke up in a hospital bed, he always found himself looking at the beautiful face of Keri Lindum. She was always there with him, through thick and thin, chaos and disorder, and through better or worse. She wasn't here when he woke up. Fission took her. Far, far away. He didn't even know where she was, but he did know that she was with Fission. This chilled Joey to the bone, the thought of her being there with that psychopath. The thought made him sit up from his bed. He glanced at a clock, and saw that it was daytime, meaning that he had been unconscious for far too long. Beep... beep... beep... He began to take off the medical equipment that his body was connected to. He had to get out of this place. He had to... 'Mr. Malone!' a doctor yelled as he came into the room. 'You have to rest!' 'I can't. I have to go.' Joey said, getting up from his bed and looking at his surroundings. He had apparently been placed in one of those stupid hospital gowns. How long had he been unconscious? 'Please, stay here!' the doctor ordered, approaching Joey. A thought had come to Joey. Fission didn't just had his wife, he had his money. His house. His car. Even his autographed copy of the Hitchhiker Trilogy. He had no way to pay for the hospital bill. That's when he turned to the doctor. 'Doc. I need to make a phone call.' Joey said, rubbing his eyes. 'Where are the phones?' The doctor pointed to Joey's right. 'The payphones are down the hall, but...' Joey didn't even listen to the rest of what the doctor had to say, as he marched past him and headed for the phones. He suddenly stopped and walked back to the doctor. 'Um, hey, doc... you got a quarter or two? I'm kind of lacking in pocketage.' The doctor shrugged, fished in his pocket, and handed Joey a couple of quarters. Joey smiled and thanked the doctor, before he made his way to the pay phone. He put a quarter in before he dialed the number of Reed Young's cellphone that he surprisingly remembered offhand. The phone rang twice before it was answered. 'Yello?' the voice on the other end asked. Joey cleared his throat and spoke. 'Reed. It's Joey.' Joey said. 'I need a favor from you.' 'And what might that be?' Young asked. 'Are you showing up for the next Pressure Point? I need the ratings to roll in, you know. Gotta keep my top draws up there and whatnot.' 'Reed, I can't go anywhere. That match that I just fought was for everything I own, I don't even have a car, and I'm stuck in Charlotte until you can help me out.' Reed was silent for a second. 'Okay, what?' 'I need you to get me to Phoenix. Take it out of my pay if you have to, but I can't leave this hospital without someone's help. From there, I'll contact you when I can return to television. I just have things to sort out right now and it shouldn't take more than a week or two, alright?' There was a second period of silence before Reed spoke again. 'Um, um... okay... just, uh, pop the ratings back up when you return, alright?'
