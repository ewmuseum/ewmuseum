The Velcro Heart, The Fifth Part of

28 August 2002

Date: 08/28/02 It was a long, long week. And LLB hadn’t done a thing. He just sat at home... and wasted his mind away. On this. On that. It didn't really matter. He strolled down his staircase, so carefree... so careless... Reaching his answering machine, he awaited to see how many messages he had. And knocking over about five beer bottles as he approached the table, LLB replayed all twelve messages. All from Reed Young. “Hey Rowe!! Awesome news... I talked to Jonny and sucked him into keeping-” BEEP. “Hey ROWE!! Awesome news... I just signed J-” BEEP. “HEY ROWE!! Awesome news... I was talking with Jer-” BEEP. “HEY ROWEY!! Awesome news... I was thinking, how about we open App-” BEEP. “ROWE ROWE ROWE YOUR BOAT!! Awesome news... I was thinking about it and I thought hey Rowe why don't we-” BEEP. “ROLEX MY BROTHA FROM ANOTHER MOTHA! Awesome news... I called Sa-” BEEP. “ROL-E-O MY BROTHA FROM ANOTHER FATHA! Awesome news... I-” SMASH. LLB had slammed one of his beer bottles overtop of the answering machine, paralyzing the annoying messages he’d get every three minutes or so. The lawyer sighed, and walked into the kitchen. It was a hard walk, though. Everywhere you went, beer bottles lay. ‘The Law’... was just not a happy person anymore... but he didn't even care. He sighed again, only to huff out even harder as he bent down and opened up the cooler he had laying by the table. “Only three cases left-” Ring. Ring. Ring. Ring. Ring. Ri- “Hello? Roland here.” “ROLANDO!! Awesome news... I totally, TOTA-” Click.
