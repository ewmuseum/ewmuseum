Maxine: 'u got ur hands on mooney shit?'

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/8/2002 I had my “business” up and running since the day I’ve decided to take on the venture. But the cash gains weren’t adding up. The cost of manufacturing was beginning to take its toll and its was not being covered by crack profits. My pushers weren’t selling enough product. Other gangs were starting to strong-arm my people. I lost 3 men in the first week alone. And the fact that my two girlfriends and I are our own best customers probably isn’t helping either. I broke one of the most sacred Crack Commandments: NEVER GET HIGH ON YOUR OWN SUPPLY. Anyway, we cooked up 10 bricks, selling for 5,000 each. I should be pulling in at least 20,000 dollars worth of work a week. Even with the shortcomings I mentioned ealier. But I’m seeing less and less turnaround everyday. And I think I’ve located my problem. 2 pushers who seemed to be a little too much into their product. Pam Jerkins and Maxine. I went to their apartment complex and stood in front of the door. I could hear the two fucking fiends talking, even over the noise of the TV and the rumble of about 6 or more bastard children. “You got your hands and on of Moony’s shit?” “We gonna get hiiiiiiiight tanite!” That was all the proof I needed. Things were about to get noisy. I pulled out my Desert Eagle .50, and used the butt of my gun to bang on the door. *BANG BANG!* I heard Pam go “Oh shit!” then she whispered something to Maxine I assume. I was becoming impatient. “Bitch!! Open the door!!” I screamed through the plywood. “Maxine he go'n kill me!” Pam said anxiously and almost crying. “Chill I got a friend cop” Maxine tried to assure her “Girl you don't know it's beef!” I had enough. I stomped on the door ripping it off the hinges and pushed my way in. The 8 future convicts screamed. I pistol whipped Pam. Maxine came to the rescue, but the bitch soon found herself lying on the floor holding what might be a broken jaw. Pam was a caramel skinned woman, with hair that seemed to plead for a handful of Head-n-Shoulders ©, and eyes which pleaded for another fix. Maxine was obviously the “brains” of the operation...that is if you can call stealing crack from a violent unstable supplier and expect him not to notice at all brainy. Her skin was a rich chocolate complexion. She was meaty but quiet attractive, with full breast and an inviting rump. She lacked the glassy over glaze of hopeless addiction clearly apparent in Pam’s eyes. Made me wonder what the fuck she was doing here. Fuck that though. I held Pam’s emaciated frame in my hands, and shoved the gun into one bloodshot eye, making her look down the shaft of my Desert Ees. “Your gonna pay Pam!! You...” I was gonna continue on my little tirade, but I felt the glare of 8 snot nosed little runts burning the back of my skull. “Fuck that boo. Kiss your kids. Y’all get the fuck in the room!” They responded in what felt like rehearsed unison. “Fuck you! You ain’t are real daddy.” “Next time you see my caddy, don’t fucking flag me.”
