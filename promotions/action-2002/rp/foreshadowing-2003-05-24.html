Foreshadowing

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The latest Pressure Point, located in the cesspool known as New Jersey, had just concluded. The main event had come to a close and the fans were loading up their kids and various merchandise and were heading for the exits. Like a herd of rabid cattle. Bumping, pushing, hitting, and all that. It was vicious. A really ugly scene. But backstage, with the wrestlers, things were relatively calm. Max Danger was one of them. He had finished up earlier in the night, but like the majority of the workers, stayed until the end. He was out of 'Danger-mode' and back to himself. Marcus Alexander Dox. (Xander for short.) He and Jessica had gathered all their things, and along with the others, were about to head out. Xander always prefered to wait a bit, as not to get caught up in all the traffic. He held open the door for his wife and she exited the lockerroom, carrying a bag on her shoulder and a small hand bag in her left hand. Xander came out right after her, with his gym bag over his shoulder. He stuffed everything in there, though it was all packed extra neatly. Strange fellow. Jessica held out her hand and Xander took it. Together they entered the hallway, just as Coral Avalon and Allison Lindum were coming along. Coral shouted, 'Max!' Xander turned about and caught sight of the person who had called his name. He gave a head nod in response. Allison and Jessica shared smiles as well. Xander then said, 'Nice work out there, tonight.' All four started walking together. Coral was quite different from Xander. He was always Coral in anything he did. Which is to say that he was cool, calm, collected, and hated Jeff Garvin. His shoulder-length black hair was tied back in a ponytail, and he wear a leather jacket. Allison's fur coat covered most of her body, but she kept her arm around Coral's own arm. He smiled at Xander, 'Really? `Cause, I think it'd have been better if I had actually given Chambers a wedgie.' Xander had an inner laugh at that. He wasn't one for busting out and just laughing. Not his style. He usually just put on a smile and laughed on the inside. 'But would you really want to have to go to that area? Personally, I wouldn't. But I don't know what your feelings on that subject is. And for Allison's sake, I'm hoping you aren't too up-beat about it.' He cocked a smile towards Ms. Lindum, who quickly looked to Coral. 'Oh, please. I gave up that plan as soon as I got it in my head that pissing Keith off was a good idea.' Coral said, before he returned his look to Allison, 'Aw, c'mon, don't give me that look.' Allison rolled her eyes at him, 'Aren't you a little old to be thinking about giving people wedgies anyway?' Coral sighed, jerked his thumb toward her, and looked at Xander, 'Real character, she is. Sometimes she chews her food really loudly just so I can get annoyed by it.' 'I do not!' Allison said, playfully punching Coral in the side of his ribs. Coral laughed briefly, before he winced and held his stomach, 'Ow, come on, that just healed.' Xander's mind suddenly shifted into 'Danger-mode', making note of the injury. Could come in handy one day. He had a half smile on his face. Then put it back down into first gear and nodded his head towards Jessica. 'I know what you mean, Jessica here does this thing in bed, where-' THWAP! That was her hand bag flying through the air and connecting with her husband's head. He stumbled forward. Coral and Allison both held back their laughter. Xander turned back to his wife and stared her down. She put her hands on her hips and gave him a 'whatcha gonna do' look. Xander rubbed the back of his head, mangling his shaggy hair all up. He then stepped foward and grabbed Jessica's arms. He looked at her hard for a moment. Coral and Allison had tensed up. Xander bent his face close to his wife's. He then... FLICKED HER EAR! TAKE THAT! Xander smirked, 'NEE-NER-NEE-NER-NEE-NER!' 'You're such a dork, babe,' Jessica replied shaking her head. She then looked to Allison, 'Why do we put up with men?' Allison shrugged, 'Well, we need SOMEONE to yell at whenever there's no one else around to blame all of our problems on.' 'Well, that explains something.' Coral said, with a wink. Xander nodded in agreement to Avalon. He then rejoined his wife at her side, retaking her hand in his own. She whispered something, most likely something about him being an idiot. He could get that way at times. All four continued walking. Coral then said, looking at Xander's shirt, 'Are you excited for the finale? Allison and I can't wait.' Xander gave the 'ehhhh' sound and a shrugged, 'I haven't decided yet. I'm really looking forward to seeing it, but, then again, I'm not. Ya know?' Avalon, 'Definitely. Should be a nice show. It's just... well, the end.' Allison chimed in, 'I'm really not ready for it to end.' Jessica was at her husband's side, making coughing sounds. 'What's wrong, honey?' Xander asked. She just shook her head. It was well known that she wasn't the biggest of Buffy fans. She would tolerate it. Just for Xander. But she wouldn't watch it if she didn't have to. 'She doesn't like Buffy,' Xander told the others. They looked shocked as they headed out of the Continental Airlines Arena and into the parking lot. On a cold, chilly night. 'And you married this woman.' Coral said, sarcastically, as he shivered a bit. He was moreso used to the temperate climates of New Orleans than this weather, but he could deal. Allison, on the other hand... well, when you live in the Southwestern United States, which is essentially a desert, you tend to be taken aback by this type of weather as well. Which was why she clung on to Coral for warmth. 'Yuuuup.' Danger responded. 'How do you manage?' Coral asked, with a slight smile on his face as he turned toward Xander. 'Well, the sex is good,' he said, which was followed by a slap on the arm from Jessica. Xander then put his arm around her. 'It's alright though. She doesn't make me watch Friends or anything stupid like that. This one's a reader. Great for me, cause, hey, peace and quiet.' Another slap. 'I'm kidding, honey. Thems jokes.' Jessica put her arms around his waist and buried her head into his chest, trying to keep warm. 'There's a fatal flaw with your jokes though, babe.' 'Yeah, what's that?' Xander replied. 'They aren't funny.' She smiled up at her husband. He return a sarcastic smile and turned to Coral, shaking his head. Avalon smiled back and then looked to Allison. 'Well, I guess we should go, huh?' Allison, 'Yes. I'm freezing!' Coral concurred. They all then stopped. 'Well. Um. I guess this is goodbye for now, Max.' Coral said, 'Good luck with the old guy on the 25th. Don't hurt him too badly.' 'So, I should just not face him then? 'Cause, I think that's the only way I'd not hurt him,' he said with a sly smile. 'Yeah,' Coral said, 'True.' Awkward silence. Coral broke it, 'Well, okay. Let's hope the Buffy finale rocks hard.' Max nodded, and all four of them went their seperate ways. This has been a [name withheld] / [name withheld] joint. Pot power.
