Fucked up.

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.02.02 White lights. They shone brightly over the robed body of Steven Denton. He opened an eye, but quickly reduced it to a squint when he realized the brightness of said lights. Glancing around him, he saw he was in a private room, with one-way windows on the walls. No one else stood in the room with him. Steven opened a second eye, adjusting to the bright lights. Suddenly, as feeling returned, he felt a sharp pain in his head. As he came to further, he felt a pain in his neck as well. He attempted to mutter an obscenity, only to find his voice was crackled and distorted terribly. All he could do was groan. He noticed all those weird hospital wires connected to various parts of his body. He wanted to rip them off of his body. He wanted to hurl them to the floor and storm away from this cloudy place. He needed to... to... '...I can't remember,' Denton croaked, his voice clearing only slightly now that he spoke louder. It was enough to make him understandable, he thought. He jogged his memory for a moment, sorting through what he could remember. Fox... Down 4 The Count... losing... Pressure Point... scaffold match... Domino... and... 'Maybe I just fell on my head,' he guessed. A few minutes later, a doctor entered the room. He saw Denton's awakened eyes, and hurried his pace to bedside. 'How are you feeling?' Steven detected a Hispanic accent, which he would've assumed from the man's looks anyway. 'Back... neck... pain killers?' He tried to keep his words scarce, the sound of his voice almost embarrassed him. 'You won't need any. You suffered from a minor concussion from your fall, and a large cut across your neck from when, well... do you remember what happened?' the doctor questioned. Steven shook his head 'no', prompting a sigh from the doctor. 'Some of the other doctors had the show on a monitor in the lounge. I'm not a fan, but they convinced me to watch a match that was going on. I saw you and another man atop a scaffolding structure... wrestling, I guess. He started choking you with one of the support cables, then threw you off the edge. You... your neck got caught in the cable, and you were literally hanged about fifteen feet off the ground. The man who threw you went nuts, and got another, larger guy to come help snap the cable. I thought your business was fake, but it all seemed too real to me. You were brought in a few hours ago. I figure it was an accident...' he finished, trying to figure out what Denton's facial expression meant. 'When can I leave?' Steven had to see the tape of what happened. He had to get ready for the next Pressure Point. He had to get home and rest a rest he couldn't rest at a hospital. He had more important things to do. 'I'd recommend you spend the night and let us take care of you. But you're under no obligation, as your injuries were nothing that should keep you from doing your daily business, as well as wrestling. Your clothes, and some things your boss dropped off that you had at the arena, are over by the door. I've got other patients to attend to, so feel free to take it upon yourself to leave. Otherwise, try to get some rest,' the doctor smiled lightly, before turning and quickly exiting the room. Denton checked out of the hospital shortly thereafter, and caught a flight home to Chicago. He would rest there.
