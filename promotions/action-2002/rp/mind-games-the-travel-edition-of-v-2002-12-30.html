Mind Games, The Travel Edition of; V

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/30/02 Max Danger had been sent to the local jailhouse following his little incident at the mall. He had gone rather peacefully and only had to wait a few hours before his wife, Jessica, got him out. 'What were you thinking, honey?' she said in a very chastizing voice. Danger just smiled, 'Just having a little fun, dear.' He, of course, had every intention of trying to really get Kayden Paulton riled up. But he wasn't a person who you could just make angry. He was weird like that. Max didn't like it. 'Oh, you should have seen the kid's faces, Jess, it was great!' She didn't think so. She just looked at her husband, with a very sad face. She liked kids. 'Xan, that's so sad! You ruined their Christmas, you know that right?' 'They were going to find out eventually, babe. If not from me, then on the playground. And do you really want the youth of today finding out that there isn't a Santa Clause from some other kid on the monkey bars?' 'Well, no, but still. That should be something they are eased into, by their parents, or just letting them come to that conclusion on their own. Then again, there was a St. Nicholas many, many years ago, but that's besides the point.' Danger nodded, 'That it is.' The two headed to the car(Danger's silver 2002 Toyota Celica), and piled in. Danger peered into the backseat. 'So, you think it'll work?' He had reached into the seat and pulled out a book, 'How to Referee a Wrestling Match?'. He looked at his wife, who smiled. 'I think it is an excellent match, honey.' She then started up the car, as Danger smiled from ear to ear. He muttered to himself, 'Things are looking good for me now.' And off they sped.
