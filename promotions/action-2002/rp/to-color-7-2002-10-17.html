Action! — Tales

To Color. 7

17 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stepping off the plane never seemed so surreal in his life. When you get away from all the cameras flashing, the people screaming, and the pressmen constantly bothering you every step of the way, it seemed a little odd, with the change from order... to chaos. Mark Paulton stepped off the plane and on to the metal ramp below, as the cameras flashed, the people screamed... and once Mark got to the bottom step of the steel stairs, the pressmen bothered. Paulton marched steady as he smiled at his Fans, even stopping to sign the odd autograph or two. He always liked the people. They paid the checks for him... so he’d do whatever in return that he actually could. He turned down the catwalk again, as more cameras flashed in his face, while a couple of pressmen backtracked, attempting to get some words out of his mouth. Paulton didn’t speak. For *they* didn’t pay his bills. So he wasn’t going to help them collect theirs. Paulton kept his steady pace, as he rounded a bend, finding his black limo which waited for him at the end of the run. Smiling, Mark walked a little quicker, as the chauffer opened up the back car door for him. Paulton slipped in, nodding to the young man who then closed it, while the pressmen basically pressed themselves against the limo as it pulled away. Inside Mark leaned his head against the window, hearing the pounds and the hammers against it. “Mark... what about your Match with The Bezel this week? Do you think you can take him?” “Mark... are you going to resign with Forest Young’s Wrestling League?” “Mark... are the rumors true about you and ‘Dashing’ Leeann Smooth?” Paulton just let it roll in one ear and out the other. He then lifted his head from the cold tinted window, as he brought it across, over to the chauffer. “How long until we hit Madison Square Gardner?” He asked politely. “About an hour sir, about an hour.” Mark nodded, sitting back in his seat as he slowly closed his eyes. “Oh, and sir?” “Yes?” “Forest said he wanted to see you in his office right away.” “No problem.”
