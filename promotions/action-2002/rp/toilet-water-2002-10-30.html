Not that I’m not used to that state of being. Fuck, I’m so used to it, anything but it feels to uncomfortable and unfamiliar to tolerate for long. It’s like a security blanket. A daily wrapping of misery and angst. It fits me like a used condom. Oh yeah, I’m off that whole living like a bum shit. Fuck that. I like things. I like money. And I picked up my first paycheck from Action! after Pressure Point. So what do I do with it? Splurge it all of-fucking-course. I was in my hometown, the illest place on earth. New York-Fucking-City. I had to take full advantage. I won’t bore you with last nights exploits. I could, and I would very much like to, if only to annoy you and make you envy my lifestyle, but I’m a tad to worried about heaving my pancreas into the toilet bowl to talk more than necessary. My head hangs over the seat-for convenience sake- and I peer into the water. Swirling around along with the bile, vomit, and piss, was me. Me staring back at myself …and smiling. I stared for a while, watching chunks of baked zittie float around distorting the reflection. My eyes were trained on the soulless invalid sneering back at me. It was poetic almost. Through the puke, bullshit, and waste, was myself. Rippled but put reformed in seconds. And if the impurities really peeved me… I could just flush. Everything was clean. It was new. Pristine. Ready to be shitted on again, knowing it’s always a knob push away from being pure once more. That thought comforted me. Maybe it was all the retching, liquor, and narcotics. But at the point, at that moment. I deeply yearned to be toilet water. Open my self up to the most revolting aspects of life, flush it all away, and ask for more. It made me want to weep. “Ugh, uh…ungh…uhhhhhh!! Shit.” “Aiight. 50 dollars motherfucker.” Well what’d ya know. all this time I had company. I peered outside my stall, and I saw one of the strippers turning a trick in the corner opposite of me. She had one stiletto heeled foot up on the a urinal, parting an immaculate chocolate thigh, with the g-string pulled to the side for easy access. “Fuck that.” That was the guy she was letting fuck her. Fuck a description. I’ll just assume you have a vague idea of what a “guy” is. “What the fuck! Mothafucka, gimme my motha fuckin mon-” The “ey” was rudely slapped out of her mouth. She spun, hitting her head on restroom wall. The guy bent her over the urinal and was having a good old time. That’s when I chose to introduce myself. “Heeeeey maaaannn. How’s it goooooooann?” I slurred as I lurched toward them like a zombie. “What the fuck do you want!?! Fuck off!” He barked at me, in mid thrust. Oddly enough, the bitch never yelled or screamed for help or anything. She was in obvious pain. Not that I know form experience, but having your rectum jack-hammered can’t be all that pleasurable. Instead of an expression of pain, and terror, she wore a stoic face of boredom and minor irritation. It was an “oh well” kind of look. It made it seem like he was raping a slab of meat rather than a woman. I trundled further, and lay a hand on the guys shoulder. “Duuuuuuude!” This got on his nerves. “You fucking cocksucker!” he snorted at me, then landed an elbow to my face. I fell backward but he wasn’t satisfied. He knelt over me hammering away at me. He then lifted me up, and fling me into one of the sinks, my head crashing and cracking the mirror above it. “Hehe.” I giggled kind of girlishly, and wiped away a trickle of crimson. Bad fucking move. I wasn’t gonna do shit, I was just enjoying the show. Now I gotta kick his ass. Sure I was pretty fucked up and in no condition to fight. My coordination was way off, my balance was horrible, my head ached, and on top of all that I had double vision. How can I fight someone with double vision? … Fuck it. I’ll just kick both their asses. He came charging in at me, and my fist came charging in to say hello. The punch wasn’t solid enough to knock him off his feet, but it did disorient him for a brief moment. And that’s all the time I needed. I grabbed him by the head, and planted knee after knee into his face. When I was done, the guy was unconscious and probably dead. I’m not a doctor. You fucking decide. I turned his nose into a babbling brook of blood. His face was already beginning to puff up. I dropped him letting him drop face first, and lay there awkwardly. “Thanks.” The stripper said matter-of-factly, as if I just offered her a napkin rather than stopped someone from ravishing her. She checked herself in the mirror, and then dug into the guys pocket fishing out his wallet. “I owe you what…20?” She asked me as she held up a 20 dollar bill. I wasn’t sure what the going rate for stopping an anal rape was, so I just took the 20. I was about to thank her. But that’s when the lights in my head went out. And the floor made a bum rush to greet me.
