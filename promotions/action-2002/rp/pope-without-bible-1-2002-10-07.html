Pope Without Bible. 1

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/07/02 The cheerful Jeremy Bold entered his own locker room door with a smile. He walked in, seeing all the empty pizza boxes from before Pressure Point aired... as well as some rolled up balls of paper on the floor... a bag of chips, wait, while Bold stepped forward and crushed something underneath his foot... make that two bags of chips, and then some. “I can’t believe Reed and I are going to the strip club!” The truth was that Jeremy loved the strip club. Being in his mid-forties, with the odd gray hair forming on the top of his head, and that totally disgusting, homemade “B” t-shirt he wore, Jeremy didn’t get many women. The rumor actually was that his last girlfriend switched ball-clubs after him. Bold took a quick glance into the bathroom mirror. He winked. If you were to ask the VP, though, he thought he was dead sexy... That, and Gertrude was gonna come outta the closet sooner or later, anyway. Jeremy walked up to his small, little brown desk. It was pretty low to the floor. A funny sight because if Bold was to be sitting in his chair... he’d have towered over top of it. “Anyway, I just need to grab my hot pink binder and I’m-” Bold searched the desk, finishing off his sentence as he rubbed his head. “Off?” The hot pink binder... the one with all the angle in it. The one Jeremy Bold was going to bring to the strip club and chat with Reed Young about... Was not there. “Ah, I’m cool. I’m cool.” Jeremy started to panic. “I... I... I left it right there!” Now, normally, Jeremy wouldn’t travel without his pink binder... but even he, the VP of Action! forgot it now and again in his locker room. So many ideas... So many good angles! Sometimes, you just got caught up in it all, and Jeremy would just have to run and tell someone. Bold walked around the room, scanning it all over again. Still no sign. His hands started to quiver. “My... my... my binder?” He kicked one of the pizza boxes on to the floor. “MY BINDER!?!?” However, everyone else but he knew... that Tyler Babylon, during Pressure Point... snuck into the room and stole it when Jeremy was not there. ‘Lionheart’ was tired of the losses. Of being booked when he was dead. What better revenge... than this? After all, no binder, no booking. Jeremy started to travel throughout his locker room, back and forth at a rapid speed. He now twitched... sighed... cried... “HOT... PINK... BINDER!?!? WHERE ARE YOU HOT PINK BINDER!?!?” But after another search of the room, it was nowhere to be found. A tear fell from Jeremy Bold’s eyes. He couldn’t have lost it. He just couldn’t have! And after all that work... for fuck’s sake he had the new rape angle PLANNED! for Syphon Fission and Morg directly down to a tea! “NNNNOOOO!!”
