Did Someone Say Party III (Thinking About Him)

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-11-02 (happened 08-07-02) Part 1 Part 2 What a night she had. Celebrating her first A! win, with a night on the town. Her, some Action! staff members and lots of guys. The liquor was flowing, and Aroma's scent was being picked up by all the fine men at the club. However Veronica managed to keep herself under control, and managed to make it back to her hotel room... alone. Something that she didn't want to be, but there was only one person on this green earth that she didn't mind being with, and he was booked on the other side of the country. It was three in the morning where he was staying, so she saw no point in giving him a call. But she said once the time is right, his phone will be ringing, and she will admit that she loves him. And hopefully they live happily ever after.
