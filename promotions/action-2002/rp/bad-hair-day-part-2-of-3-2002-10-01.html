Action! — Tales

Bad Hair Day (Part 2 of 3)

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/01/02 OPEN “Otay, we’re here. I’m going to be about 10 minutes. Just the usual bowl cut-o and I’m back in the car!” Title: Bad Hair Day (Part 2 of 3) Date: 10/01/02 4:00 pm EST Place: Hair Salon Christopher Fox gallantly skids out of his beloved Pikachu Beatle as Fox can actually see through his hair today. Don’t ask me why, though. It’s a Christopher Fox story.... they make no sense! As a Fox dodges a couple of newspaper pages flying on the ground, a lady exits the hair salon with a poodle in hand. Hey! There goes that one hundred and one Dalmatian person! Except she isn’t wearing a fuzzy white fur coat! With Fox pondering upon the entrance, the salon goes dead quiet.... Then, properly wiping his feet on the carpet, Chrisy takes a seat in one of the leather chairs in the waiting area. The crackles from the seat scare a little girl who’s sitting across from Fox as he does. Let’s see.... why is everybody staring at me? Is it because I’m hot? Hey, look, I know I’m hot people, but can’t you keep your drool to yourself! The little girl’s Mother takes her daughter and exits, but not before speaking her mind to our hero. Lady “What are you doing?” Fox “I’m getting a hair cut! I think it’s my 3rd hair cut! Hey receptionist, do I get a sucker if I’m good?” Receptionist “Ummm no.” Lady “Diana! Are you going to let this MAN have a hair cut HERE?” Little Girl “Mommy, isn’t this place only for girls?” Lady “Yes it is dear!” Receptionist “Well it isn’t against the rules. Of course, he has to have money.” Fox “Hey girlfriend, don’t leave me outta the action!” Just then, a rather large woman walks up and whispers something into the receptionist’s ear. Receptionist “Ok.... Sir, if you’d like a hair cut please follow Jo.” Lady “Oh, I can’t believe this! Men invade everything! I am so out of here, come on Penelope!” The lady and daughter exit as Fox follows Jo like a running back with his blocker. Jo walks up to her seat and slaps it. Jo “Have a seat hun.” Just as Chris sits down, he finally clues in, looking around. Women, women and more women. Fox “Is this like a beauty parlor for girls, homes?” Jo “It’s ok, you’re our exception. So what’ll it be?” Fox “Well believe it or snot, I’m an Action! Wrestler, Christopher Fox is the name and pushing, pulling and pimping are going to be my games! Right now it’s just Twister, Risk and uh, oh yeah Monopoly! I love that game! I lose every time, but I love it!” Stunned for nearly three seconds, Jo reacts. Jo “I love Action! I watch it all the time! Chris Fox, I barely even recognized you!” Fox looks into the mirror. Hair practically covers his whole face. Fox “Otay, so what do you suggest?” Jo “Oh, well, I hear the Joey Malone look is in.” Fox “What you are looking at, girlfriend, is a failed attempt at a Joey Malone! I want something oh.... um, ‘not in my face’.” Jo “Well, Simon Seaman has short spiky sort of hair. It’s different from that bowl cut.” Hmmmmmm, look like a loser, or look like a loser with cool hair! Decision’s, decision’s! Fox “SIMON! SIMON!” Jo “Uh, it’s Simon Seaman and ok, I’m on it.” Fox “I knew that! I was just testing you!” .................... The scene closes and then reopens right away, as buzzing sounds can be heard while a bunch of dark brown hair floats towards the ground. The scuffing of Jo’s slippers can also be heard as she works away at the noggin of Fox. Jo “Ok, I’m done!” The scene shows all the women crowded around Jo and her patient. Indeed our friend has gained friendship with all the ladies here. Fox opens his eyes. From the mirror reflection, Fox clearly has a tan line right across his face. The lighter side was where his hair was. Now with a quick rub Fox jumps up and down in joy! Fox “I CAN SEE! I’VE BEEN CURED! I CAN SEE! THERE’S RED AND BLACK AND BLUE AND.... SOME MANY NICE GIRLS....” Uh oh............. Shaaaaaaaaaaaaa Wingggggggggggggggggggggg! Fox explodes right in front of all the girls! They all just burst out in playful laughter. Fox “Yes, I meant to....” Hehe.... LOL? Give me a sign! A Lady “Oh Chris, we could never make fun of you, now that you have nice hair and all.” Almost forgetting he has a new dew, Fox spins himself around in the chair and checks himself out. The picture is actually good, he doesn’t look half bad! Nice spiky hair with scented gel. Fox is a rejuvenated man! Fox “Thank you ladies, now I must go and buy everything that Jo put on this list!” A Lady “Why do you have to do that?” Fox “Well, to maintain my cool style I have to put all these things in my hair! So see ya!” Group of Women “By Chris!” Fox then moonwalks out of the salon like he is some cool dude. Finally unlocking the car, Fox jumps in and drives off. It turns out that the name of the salon was ‘Independent Woman’ and in subtitle ‘Reveal the lady in you’. Fox had done it again. He has broken another barrier. The scene shows Fox driving and chatting to Pikachu. Fox “So, you like it?” .... Fox “What do you mean, ‘who am I?’ I’m Chris!” .... Fox “Really!” .... Fox “Hey, well, thanks Pikachu, I know I’m cool.” The scene fades as Fox winks to Pikachu and speeds off into the distance. CLOSE
