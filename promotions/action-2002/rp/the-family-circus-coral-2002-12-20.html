The Family Circus: Coral

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/20/2002 'The truth is, I never agreed with Coral getting kicked out,' John said, 'That was my wife's doing.' 'What?' Allison asked. 'Well, Coral's always loved wrestling. He's been a fan for seven years. He loved it so much that he could have competed for the Louisiana state championship about four years ago.' John said. 'Why didn't he? If he loved it so much...' 'Irene, my wife. Coral kept the fact that he was on the wrestling team a secret from her, because he knew that Irene didn't like Coral's wrestling ambition. When she finally heard about it, she made a few angry phone calls on 'Coral's behalf', informing them that a family emergency was keeping him away. Then she yelled at him for an hour and a half about keeping secrets and made him miss the championship.' 'That's terrible.' Allison sighed. 'Most of the family agreed with her, except me and Emmy, and Emmy was living in a college dorm at the time. So, about three years ago, Irene gave Coral an ultimatium. She told him to either find a new direction in life, or Coral wouldn't be welcome in the Avalon household any more.' John said, with a sigh. 'Coral, of course, chose to continue to strive for his dream.' Allison stood silent. 'I saw Coral a couple of times after that, but he didn't have a lot of friends and eventually ended up on the streets. Irene wouldn't take him back no matter what I said,' John said, taking off his glasses and rubbing his eyes, 'So, I arranged a small meeting.' 'Anthason.' Allison said, with a look of shock on her face. 'Indeed.' John stood up, and placed his glasses back on his face. 'I met with Mr. Anthason, who lives in this area, and I paid him to find Coral, take him in, and train him to be a professional wrestler. I arranged it so it would be a chance meeting, and watched him from afar, and in secret.' he said. 'But, why?' Allison asked, 'You didn't seem to thrilled to see each other again.' 'Coral always thought I was the one who wanted him thrown out. The truth is, I didn't have a say in the matter,' John said, sulking, 'But you should have seen him. He always got so excited watching those old tapes. His room had shelves and shelves of tapes with wrestling from five different countries, possibly others. He always bought at least five tapes a month and would spend hours just watching it all. The kid always had that dream to one day be a wrestler, despite what Irene or the rest of them said. That's why I did what I did, Miss Allison... because family obligations are no reason to crush anyone's dreams.' Allison thought about it, and smiled. 'I wish you had been around for my family problems.' John smiled in his own right, 'Go and find him, take him to his room. Mr. Rosefeld will come by in a couple of hours and bring you for dinner.' 'Okay, then.' she said, before she departed John Avalon's study. Coral stood directly outside of the study, and seemed to have a look of clarity on his face. 'Heh,' he said with a smile, 'So that's how I got hooked up with that jerk.' 'You were listening?' Allison asked. Coral nodded. 'When you didn't follow me out, I figured the old guy had something to say to you.' Coral said. Then his expression turned to... was it excitement? 'Come on, now, let's go to see if they didn't sell my tapes on eBay!' he said.
