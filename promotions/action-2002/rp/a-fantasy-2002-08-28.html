A Fantasy?

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 28, 2002 Was it all a fantasy? Was my whole life a complete and utter fantasy? The words still ring in my head...the shouts of one, LLB. The shouts of mocking me. Or were they the shouts of truth? I can take what the COURTROOM has to offer. Not thus stupid, little...battlefield Those words continue to stain my mind, echoing every single second. I look around at me, the fans...they are still staring at me...wondering...why am I not attacking. I look at myself in the center of the ring as a river of blood slowly falls from my face. I wear a crimson mask, with my eyes wide open...but it wasn't in rage this time...it was in shock. I looked up again at the fans, those who had been cheering me for the first time in my life. They are in awe, complete and utter awe. They wonder why I haven't gotten up...I can still hear the footsteps of Jeremy Bold and Reed Young...walking arrogantly up the steel ramp. They are right there, fresh for attack...but why do I stay still in the ring? I kneel down and just allow my body to fall where it may. I kneel straight up, and just stare into oblivion...the words of one, LLB, echoing in my mind...staining what little sanity I have left... See KroW... if this was YOUR battlefield you fucking asshole... YOU'D HAVE WON! But I didn't. The odds were stacked against me...and I wasn't strong enough to battle them and become victorious. I lost in my own world...my own battlef...funny...the sound of 'battlefield' sickens me now. It makes me want to vomit, or hide in shame. Was my 'battlefield' really just a fantasy? An illusion? Was my actions of being a 'warrior' a fantasy? An illusion in my mind? Your stupid little fantasy world... THAT'S ALL IT IS!! FUCKING FANTASY!! A fucking fantasy. Is it true? Is there a 'battlefield'? Am I a 'warrior'? Or am I just some idiotic crazed man with absolutely no sense of reality in him anymore? And why do I get a bad taste in my mouth at the mere thought of the words 'battlefield' and 'warrior'? Why? Your stupid little fantasy world... THAT'S ALL IT IS!! FUCKING FANTASY!! Stop it. Your stupid little fantasy world... THAT'S ALL IT IS!! FUCKING FANTASY!! Stop it! Your stupid little fantasy world... THAT'S ALL IT IS!! FUCKING FANTASY!! STOP IT!!!!!!!! God fucking dammit! I heard your fucking words you son of a bitch! I heard them! Are you happy? Are you happy now? HUH?! You fucking bastard! HUH?!?! What's wrong with me...am I cracking? And these fans are still looking at me...they haven't moved...than again neither have I. I slowly collapsed to the mat and roll myself out of the ring...the people seem excited...they expect me to give chase to the rightful champion, to the sadistic owner, and his lackey. But no...not tonight. I don't feel like it. Instead, I'll slowly walk my path up the rampway. Taking time with every step I take, watching as my own blood falls upon the steel ramp that lay underneath my feet. It's funny, when I usually saw my own blood...I was happy. But now...it sickens me... I used to feel strong...but something inside is calling me weak. My battlefield was destroyed. Bleh, I sound like an idiot. Was it really all a fantasy? Was my 'battlefield' a figmint of my imagination? Was my 'warrior'ness a figmit of my imagination? Am I just some bummbling mentally challenged weakling? Am I? Am I really strong? Am I really powerful? Am I really...in reality? Your stupid little fantasy world... THAT'S ALL IT IS!! FUCKING FANTASY!! A Fantasy?
