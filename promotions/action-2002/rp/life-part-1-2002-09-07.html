Life - Part 1

7 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/07/02 Life. It's something that is supposed to mean something. It's the meaning of your entire world, your entire being, your entire body, your entire soul...you entirely. It's your life, your world, your fate, rests all in your hands. Your life is what leads you into what you want to be. Life is your reality. Life is what makes you real. But what happens...when you find out your entire life...was fake? Their voices still ring in my head. The voices of my opposition...the voices of LLB...the voices of Jonathan Conspiracy. Every single word that spilled from their mouthes stains my mind and infects my brain. It's a disease, it doesn't go away...it spreads. The words haunt me in my wake, and in my sleep. The words haunt me while I walk, and while I run. The words haunt me every single moment of my life. I stare into the mirror, and look deep into my own reflection, staring into my own soul. I was fake. I lived nothing but a fantasy...A FUCKING FANTASY. It was all an illusion, an image in my mind. Terms like 'warrior' and 'battlefield' were those that I lived by, that I believed was true. What I saw through this misguided eyes was a battlefield where blood was stained, and lives were destroyed. Through my misguided eyes I was a warrior, defending myself in the ongoing war in life. But... There is no war. There is no battlefield. There is no warrior. There is no meaning to my life. I lived, I thrived, I breathed, I fought all for my life. When I saw opposition...I didn't call them opponents...I called them 'weaklings'. When I saw the squared circle...I didn't see the ring...I saw a blood stained 'battlefield'. When I saw myself...I didn't see a wrestler...I saw a 'warrior'. Oh how that has changed... Now all I see is reality. The worst thing about it is...I'm totally new to it. I have never seen the reality of this world, all I have seen was my reality. My world and my life was nothing but a FUCKING FANTASY. Now, as I stare at myself in the reflection of the stained mirror...I see nothing...but a stained soul. I have been stained with idiocy, and stained with pure fantasy. Only now do I realize what I am... I am... Weak. And all my life I have lived by a code...that the weak must die to make room for the strong. That the weak must parish from this 'battlefield'. And usually I would agree...that was...until I found out that this 'battlefield' was just an imaginary world of mine. But I lived in this world for as long as I can remember. Which means my life...has been a waste. I lived for the imagination. I thrived for the imagination. And I lived in this fantasy. Only to find out it was nothing. My life means nothing to me anymore...this life means nothing to me anymore. This entire fucking life is completely idiotic. Watching my own self...cry. Looking at my reflection, and seeing salty tears slide down my face. I can't stand it. I can't stand watching myself fall victim to my emotions. Never have I let this happen...than again...never have I been through something this large. And I can't take it... I see my only solution, and it isn't a compromise of decisions. It's one simple decision, one simple solution. I'm now watching myself in the reflection, watching myself reach into my left pocket and pull something out. It's a long and shiny object. It shines in the lights of the darkened room in which I stand in. Amongst my blood, sweat and tear filled face...I can see clearly what I grasp in my hand. The one solution... The one thing that can solve it all. A knife. A FUCKING FANTASY [The voice of LLB echoes] YOUR LIFE IS JUST FUCKING STUPID [The voice of Jonathan Conspiracy echoes] YOU'RE A LOSER [echoes LLB] GET WITH REALITY KROW! [echoes Jonathan Conspiracy] One solution... One little object can solve it all. And it's time to put this life...to rest... What a shiny object it is...
