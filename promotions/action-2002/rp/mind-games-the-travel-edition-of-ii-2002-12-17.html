Mind Games, The Travel Edition of; II

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/17/02 It was still Sunday, but no longer in the morning. It was midday and the sun was shining. Kayden Paulton had exited his Dothan, Alabama home and hopped into his vehicle. He then sped off. Right behind him, was... MAX DANGER!!! DUM DUM DUUUUUUUUUUUUUM In his Lincoln Navigator, 'The Danger Man' followed Kayden Paulton through Dothan. Finally he stopped, at the grocery store. Max Danger pulled in behind him and parked at the very end of the row(of spaces), whereas Paulton parked at the front. Kayden entered the store, holding a piece of paper, which was obviously a shopping list. Danger entered after him. He was bombarded with southern accents. He tried covering his ears, but they just seaped through his skin and bone and went straight into the ear. Danger screamed out, silently, and headed on, trying to find where Paulton had gotten off to. Max was looking down each and every aisle until he heard, 'Can I 'elp ya?' Danger turned and gave the woman a raised eyebrow and a very confused looking face. She just smiled her bright smile and asked again. 'Can I 'elp ya?' Danger put his index fingers into his ears and wiggled them. He then said, 'Excuse me?' 'Can I 'elp ya?' Still, confused as hell, Max just stared at the woman. She wasn't bad looking, he just couldn't understand a word she was saying. A man then came over and stood beside her. 'Whatcha up ta, Mary Jane?' 'Justa 'elpin' out this youngun, Walter Ray!' Danger just looked back and forth between them. They stared back at him, waiting for him to tell them what they could help him with. 'Can I 'e-' 'SHUT UP!!!' Max had shouted at the top of his lungs. Both Walter Ray and Mary Jane were taken aback. Several other employees and customers stopped and stared at 'The Danger Man'. 'I don't understand hick talk! I'm fron the north! SPEAK ENGLISH!!!' No one spoke for a moment. Walter Ray and Mary Jane looked at one another, then back at Max. But then he saw him. Max had seen Kayden Paulton out of the corner of his eye. He made a mad dash down the frozen food section. Walter Ray and Mary Jane just looked at each other and shrugged, before going back to their duties. 'HEY!' Kayden Paulton pulled his head out of the case, where he was looking at pizzas. He looked through the glass door and saw Danger coming. He smiled. 'Max, how-' But Max cut him off. He spoke so loudly, that everyone had stopped shopping and were staring at him. 'Where do you get off? Huh? Who do you think you are?' 'I do-' 'ZIP IT! I'm talking here!!! You are going to leave me alone! You are going to stay OUT of my fuggin' life!' 'Fuggin'?' Kayden said to himself. 'SO.... GET AWAY!!!' Kayden just stared at Max. Danger had come and seen him, not the other way around. Paulton was lost for words though. He was about to speak, but Danger slammed the frozen door shut, so hard that the glass shattered and scattered across the floor. 'NO TALKING! JUST GO!' He pointed towards the front doors. Kayden tried to reason with Max, 'B-But it's a grocery store, Max. It's a public place. I-' 'I don't want to hear it! GET OUT! GET OUT! GET OUT!' He had opened another door and between each 'GET OUT!' he was slamming the door shut. The glass shattered, just as the other one had. Kayden then looked past 'The Danger Man' and saw security and store managers coming. 'Max, you should probably go.' 'What? Are you telling me what to do?' He snatched the collar of Paulton and slammed him against another door. Danger then let out a loud moan, 'OW! Did you all see that!? HE HIT ME!!!' Danger had fallen to his knees. Paulton didn't know what was going on. The security and managers had reached them. 'He tried to kill me! He tried to kill me!' Kayden was snatched up by security as was Danger. 'HEY!' Danger yelled. 'I was attacked! That bastard attacked me!' Kicking and screaming he was forced outside the store. Paulton had gone quietly. He then stood beside Max, looking at the store, as Danger was shouting obsenitites to the people inside. 'I didn't like that place anyway.' Danger looked at him. 'Bad deli.'
