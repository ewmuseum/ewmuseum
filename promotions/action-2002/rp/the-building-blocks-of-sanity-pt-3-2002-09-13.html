The Building Blocks of Sanity pt. 3

13 September 2002

Date: September 13th, 2002 August 21st, 2002 – Wednesday – 8:15 AM “Reed Young.” The owner of the widely popular wrestling promotion, Action! Wrestling spilled from the officer’s mouth as she loomed over a large, time grid in the centre of the department’s book of records. As it turned out, Reed Young had posted bail for Jeff not long after he was incarcerated. The officers just thought they would give the obnoxious, twenty three year old enough time to ‘make friends’ with his cell mates before releasing him. Surely, they did not count of Jeff Garvin’s increased level of homophobia, and of course, paranoia. The police department was often quite frantic on week nights, nothing to big, usually just teenagers getting picked up for holding illegal substances or shop lifting. Jeff Garviin stood in front of the brown, hickory desk as he waited for the women sitting behind it to finishing logging his name in the computer database. “Who in the fuck is Reed Young? Hmmm… Could it possibly be a hot young female that wants me to repay them with hours upon hours of sexual pleasure; starting with oils, add a little Barry, then off for a little rub a dub dub in the hot tub?” Garvin’s eyes lit up, his tongue moving across his upper lip, and pours beginning to drain sweat. A single eye brow rose on the woman’s face. “Sir, are you sure your not intoxicated? Reed Young is a males name.” Garvin frowned. “I know, just was, you know, hoping… I’ve never been with a woman before…” He began, peering down at the female officer, trying to see down her shirt while playing the sensitive side. Does he even know what the word sensitive means? Or even how to spell it for that matter? Jeff picked at his ear nervously, “Will you be my girlfriend?” “No.” A look of dissapointment crossed over Garvin. “Why? It’s because I’m homeless, have no job, like to kick defenceless animals, and cop feels on hot young business women as they walk by isn’t it? Isn’t it! Your racist!” She just rolled her eyes. “Do you even know the meaning of that word? Bah. I don’t even want to argue with you. There’s just a few things I need to run by you before you can go. So I’d appreciate if you please stop sexually harressing a police officer… in her own police department. You can be arrested for that.” “Are you saying you want to cuff me and do all kinds of dirty things to my body?” She didn’t even crack a smile, she just stared at Jeff. “Anyway… Reed took the court up on a few stipulations to help lower your bail amount. One being that you meet with him this Thursday at the Action! Wrestling headquarters…” Flicking through the papers and skimming over it a tad, she continued… “And second, you must attend councelling every Tuesday and Thursday for…” She read the next line, “Many months… Possibly years. The therapist was skimming over your file and she seems to think you have deep rooted emotional problems. Seems to think you’re a real whack job, I agree with her, and Reed figured he could save a little money for an electrical foot massager that he was babbling about… Kept telling the officers how ‘Fantastic’ it was in ‘special’ placed… So, you want out to walk out of this building, you must go to councelling.” Seconds later Garvin was already shaking his head in a ‘no’ fashion. “No way! People might see me! They’ll think I’m like crazy and I’m NOT crazy, I’m as sane as sane can be.” The officer stared back at Jeff; clad in his paper bag, pink knee socks that he found in the trash, smelling of fish, and chicken bone stuck in his nappy, curly brown hair. “Yes. I can see that.” She lied, taking her eyes away from the sickening sight and back to the computer monitor, “But you still are required to attend therapy. You don’t go, your back behind bars. It’s only until the therapist believes you’re mentally fit to stop attending. Until then, your ass is hers.” Her? Jeff’s eyes widened, his mind raced, and his man hood stood at attention. A female therapist? An actual woman who he was REQUIRED to sit and babble to for hours on end? Jeff’s mouth began to water. Maybe this wouldn’t be so bad after all…
