Dinner Arrangements: Part One

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 26th, 2002 The following takes place twenty-four hours after what transpired in the Clubbin’ series. “Okay then, so where were we?” “I was just about to tell you the good news.” Jeff answered, leaning over the desk with a smirk on his face, jumbling a pen back and forth through his fingers. Claire’s thin, blond eyebrow rose, silently insisting that he continue. “I actually made a friend!” “Really, well that’s great Jeff, I’m glad to hear your finally opening up socially. See what happens when you put on a smile?” “Yep. But I really think it was this new collogne I found beneath the floor boards in my new place. It is really awsome.” The collogne he speaks of is actually a bottle of human urine that the former tenent was saving just incase his porole office sprung a random drug test on him. But what Jeff doesn’t know can’t hurt him… Unless that mysterious rash manifests into something worse. *Shrug* Anyway, back to the story at hand… Claire studied Jeff’s face, she picked him apart with a simple glance, from only a few weeks of therapy and the little information they had in his personal file, she felt confident that she could pick up if Jeff was lying. Her original plan was to have Reed Young pay one of the female nurses to strike up a conversation with Jeff. Possibly leading to something more… But for the past week she had been in Cuba, and had yet to suggest it to the owner of Action! Wrestling, she had been doing a quick study on man accused of stabbing his wife to death. The evidence had been lost in a shuffle when a group of protesters, creating a human roadblock, began a full-scale attack on all police vehicles. They gave Claire permission to meet with him, delve into his psyche and see if she could possibly find out something that the law enforcement there had not been able to find during interrogation. The trip, though, had been a waste; the former husband and father of two hung himself in his cell two days before she was scheduled to leave Cuba for home. Her patience had begun to wear thin, as well as her faith in her own abilities. Jeff had started out as a ‘project’ but was quickly turning into a nuisance. Lie, after lie, after lie. That’s all it was with him. No matter how hard she tried, no matter how hard she pushed on the issue, Jeff refused to mention anything about his childhood… specifically his father’s death. Their therapy sessions had degenerated into an hour of silence, littered with weak ‘pick up’ lines on the Original’s part. Everyone had some good in them, even the most hardened criminals. Most of which were just fed up with life, looking for a way out. Looking to be cured. But when she looked into Jeff’s eyes, as much as she hated to admit it, there was no ‘want’ there. Was it that he liked the way he was? To be blunt; an arrogant bastard with no morals. Nevertheless, another thought had recently begun to flood her mind. Though it was more of a feeling than a thought, really. The feeling of love. *Gasp* But time after time, whenever their eyes locked and she felt that unmistakable ‘sensation’ in the pit of her stomach, she pawned it off. Justifying it with… ‘It’s mere pity, that’s all it is, it doesn’t mean anything’. But it did. Things were complicated. She had recently begun getting more serious with her boyfriend. Marriage was the ‘hot’ topic between them. They had begun setting dates to go look at engagement rings; he was a doctor, she was a psychiatrist, time was a precious commodity. Nick (her boyfriend) was the total package, or so she thought. He was smart, independently wealthy, caring, and wanted a family; he had his whole life planned. Then there was Jeff; barely scraping by, a bad haircut, the want to be hated in his profession, totally cluless to his own emotional problems. He ran on instinct; he was a goofy, moronic, pervert who lacked any sort of personality. And here she was second-guessing herself? Comparing the two? Maybe she was the real idiot out of the two. Meanwhile, Jeff hadn’t stopped talking, having no idea that Claire wasn’t listening. “So yeah, the Smooth Brothers, Ody and I, pulled up to the club in my snazzy ‘ride’. I swear to God, all the chicks eyes were locked on me. They all wanted the Gift of Garvin: my sweet ass, of course. But I just like totally strolled right by, playing it all cool, and OF COURSE they let me cut right in, but I had to bribe the bouncer to let Odium in. [Sure, Jeff, whatever you say.] The guy is cool, but he lacks my… uh… rugged style, my coolness, my smoothness. And so once we got in- Hey, you listening?” Claire looked up, startled. She blinked a few times, avoiding eye contact and replied, “Y-Yeah…” “Okay, as I was say-“ “Actually,” she said, interrupting him and checking her watch, “I’m going to have to cut this one a little short… I still owe you dinner, why don’t you bring your friend along, I’d love to meet him.” Really, she just wanted to make sure Jeff wasn’t making him up. Jeff quickly nodded and answered, “Well… I did kind of want to be alone with you, but I guess having him along wouldn’t hurt. Besides, I should get a full meal in me, I have a match against this little ‘monkey turd’ named Boral Tagalong, I think that’s his name, so I’m going to need all the energy I can get to give him a grade A ‘Original’, ‘Old School’ ass kicking… Garvin styyyyyyyyyle!” “Okaaaaaaaaaaay.” Claire said, confused as to why Jeff was hyping up a match in the middle of his office as there were no cameras around. Just another strange titbit she would get to add to her elusive, secretive note pad that rested on her desk. “Well I’ll see you at seven, okay?” “You bet! Whoo!” Jeff Garvin dropped the pen and exited Claire’s office, doing his ‘Pimp walk’ that he debuted at the Club last night. (Read Odium’s Clubbin’ series.) Something that we would most likely see for a long… Long… Long… … Long time. Cue the cries from children everywhere.
