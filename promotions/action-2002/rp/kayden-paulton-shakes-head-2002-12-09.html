Action! — Tales

Kayden Paulton(Shakes Head)

9 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/9/02 Ever since last Monday, when Max Danger sat down on a couch with Kayden Paulton, he had been receiving voice mails from him, 'Max! Max! Man, I need to talk to you! I'm worried about you! Please call me back!' Day after day, more and more messages were left on his cellphone. Thankfully he had taken to not answering his phone. Of course, he had missed a few important calls from his mother('I have something important to tell you, Marcus!' she had said excitedly in her message.), but he ignored them. He hadn't been much in the mood to talk. He had problems. Many problems. And now, he wished he hadn't laid out across the couch outside of Reed Young's office last week. Why did Kayden pry so much? 'Max! Mr. Danger! Please call me back. It's urgent. I think I know what's troubling you, and I think I may have a solution. You know my number! BYE!' He thought he was going to be able to just not listen to the messages, but after choosing not to on Wednesday and Thursday, he was overwhelmed when he found nearly fifty-six voice mails had been left. He just shook his head. He wasn't going to call him back. 'Hey Max! I went by your house, looked nice. But no one was there. I hope everything is alright. Please don't do anything drastic. I'm here to help you out. BYE!' He swore that Kayden had to be on drugs. How else would you explain how he acted? He was.... nice. It gave Max the shivers. No one could be that nice. There was obviously some other motives. 'The Danger Man' thought he knew. He had figured Kayden was just trying to throw him off his game. Trying ot fluster him and get him to concentrate on his personal problems and not his job. 'Mr. Danger, sir? Are you there? Please pick up! I can help you. I want to help you. Well... I'll see you at next week's show. BYE!' Of course! Max would get to see Kayden at Pressure Point on Monday. He didn't really want to see him so much as get him off his back. He had an idea... 'Two can play mind games,' he had said as he picked up his plane ticket at the airport, leaving for Monday's show.
