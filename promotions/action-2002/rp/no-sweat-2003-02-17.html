Action! — Tales

No Sweat

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OPEN The scene opened as Christopher Elliot Fox sat facing a computer screen, with his eyes wide open in amazement. Fox clicked, dragged and dropped until the screen displayed Blood, Sweat, Tears.... Title: No Sweat Date: 02/16/03 11:49 am EST Place: Christopher’s Basement Chris clicked the insignia and entered action.slike.com. Fox “Let’s see Pikachu....” Chris then clicked on the 2002 Archives and entered the Last Laugh page (surprisingly ‘Kid’ knows how to work this complex machine). Fox “Hey Pika Peek, look at what dat says!” The screen read ‘Virus Winner: Christopher Fox’ Fox “That’s me Pikachu.” The damp atmosphere was weakened when Fox bent over and turned on the heater. Pikachu was sitting nicely beside the monitor, which was covered in stickers, doo-dads and little Pokemon decals. Fox “Yeppers, that’s me Pikachu and do you know what that means?” Fox’s chair rolled back on the carpet as he stood. Fox “This month at Blood, Sweat, Tears I’m going to become the Champion! This will be my very dairy first time as Action Champion Pika Peek!” Fox started a little dance, waving his cast in the air as he did. It came to an abrupt ending though, when he fell over the chair! Fox “No Sweat eh Pikachu?! Tee hee, I’m going to become the Action Champion.... BIG TIME!!!! But you know Pikachu, I wonder what was up with LL, he didn’t seem right last time on TV.” I sure hope he’s otay. I want my bestest friend in the whole wide world to be there when I win the Championship! Then we can go around joking and jiving and chugging down milkshakes! Oh goody that’ll be grand! Fox rose and checked himself out in the mirror on the wall. He then flexed and, well, there was no big difference in appearance. .... Then came the Kamehameha! Fox “Oh yeah, Kayo Kan times five!” Sound effects supplied by Chris. Fox “Tou! Buuuuuuuoooomish! Ka....... Me.......... Ha......... Me........... HA!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Kiouuushshshsi!” Fox threw his free arm forward, like he was shooting a big giant blast with it. After that, he just stood normal. Fox “Did you see it Pikachu!? I think I raised my power level!” Silence. Fox “LOL? How could you say that to me Pika? I defiantly increased in strength there!“ .... Fox “Otay, how aBOOT this!?” Fox placed his right hand in a diamond shape, trying to use his left one as well, to finish off the look.... but that was pretty hard, with it still inside that cast and all. Fox “TRI-BEAM!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Beeeeeoooooooooooooik!” Silence. Fox “Thanks Pikachu, I knew you misjudged my power.” Complete silence from Pikachu was heard, as only the buzzing of the computer screen made a sound. Fox “I’m ready for my shot, I’ll have no sweat in winning! Especially at Blood, Sweat, Tears.... BIG TIME!!!!” Oh my gosh, look at the time, I’m late for Master Roshi’s lesson! He was going to teach me how to use the Destructo Disk! Fox “Instant Transmission.... go!” Fox put two fingers on his forehead and pretended to disappear. Then he quickly grabbed Pikachu and ran upstairs where the scene would end. CLOSE
