Did Someone Say Party V (Taking The First Step)

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-12-02 (happened 08-10-02) Part 1 Part 2 Part 3 Part 4 Veronica Aromadeus was sitting in a hotel room, still celebrating her victory over Max Danger, reading reviews of her recent performance in Action! 'Sweet Aroma is starting to get into it.' was a quote that she read on the internet, and it made her feel special, but she wasn't so sure about the forthcoming proposed angles that the fans wanted to see; even if it was better than Young's idea about having a lesbian love affair with Angelica Dawson and forming 'The Bowell Movement', she didn't want to step into the ring with Christopher Fox, 'maybe Angelica Dawson could take that one' is what she thought to herself as PIW's Drastic Measures 3 crept closer to the main event that put her trainer and prospective love interest Jonathan Conspiracy, in the ring against his nemesis, Rune Winters and Chris Mezz, the PIW World Champion for said title. And then the music played... 'Guilty Until Proven Innocent' by Jay Z featuring R. Kelly started to play and out walked her baby. Mr. Conspiracy, One Letter Better, The Attention Getter, JCON. Jonathan Conspiracy. The crowd boo'ed but she cheered. She was sure that others were watching and booing, one person booing was probably Bobby Minio, but he wasn't there for her to know for sure. Anway... 'Voilence Fetish' by Disturbed kicked in, and Rune Winters walked out from the back. Sweet gave a sarcastic clap of the hands for Winters. She liked his 'I'll fuck up your day' attitude, but she knew that he didn't stand a chance in that ring against her 'JCON'. Then 'By Myself' by Linkin Park started playing over the airwaves at the MCI Center, bringing forth... The last PIW World Champion The Franchise Wrestler Simply Crazy Chris Mezz Another 'good luck against Jonathan' hand clap occurred. And then the bell rang. The three went at it. Sweet looked around worried everytime it looked as if Rune Winters or Chris Mezz took the advantage, and everytime they had a pin she'd close her eyes, in hopes that one of them would kick out, or JCON would interject himself to keep his dream, and her's, alive of him becoming the World Champion. The match continued that way until Jim Johnson showed up. Sweetz DIDN'T like James one bit, especially after all she put Jonathan through. 'What kind of a man forces my baby, to hug a homosexual? I see nothing wrong with Jamal being gay, but please don't place Jonathan in a position to be viewed as a sexual orientationist' is what she said, as the match neared it's climax. Here take a look for yourself. While Mezz and J-Con engage into an exchange of punches, Jim Johnson pulls out referee Jerry Williams, and he apparently seems to be very mad with him. Brown: Wait a minute, why did he just pull out the referee?? Although the camera cannot pick up on what the two are saying, the referee seems to be explaining something, but out of nowhere, Johnson knocks him down with a hard right hand! Brown: HEY, HE JUST HIT THE REFEREE! Nerdstrom: So what? He's the boss, and as far as I'm concerned, he can do whatever the HELL HE WANTS! Back in the ring, Mezz blocks a right hand from J-Con, and that allows him to give Mr. Conspiracy The Tranquilizer! Nerdstrom: THE TRANQUILIZER! Brown: There it is, but the referee is out cold! After Mezz hits J-Con with his finishing move, Rune gets involved again, as he clotheslines Mezz from behind. He then goes for his finisher, Now You're Fucked, but Jim Johnson slides into the ring with the ring bell, and knocks Rune Winters with it in the back of the head! Brown: COME ON! THIS ISN'T RIGHT AT ALL! Rune immediately collapses to the ground after taking the bell-shot, leaving Mezz and Johnson alone to shake hands in the center of the ring. Nerdstrom: Now this is a Kodak Moment! As the two business partners are shaking hands, Jim Johnson shocks everyone by NAILING MEZZ WITH THE RING BELL!! Brown: Wait a minute! JOHNSON JUST NAILED HIS OWN MAN ON PURPOSE! Nerdstrom: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON HERE?! It looks like Mezz is down on the mat unconscious, and now J-Con crawls over for the pin. Meanwhile, Jim Johnson heads back outside, where he grabs a cup of water from the French announce table, pours it over the face of the referee, and that ultimately wakes him up. Nerdstrom: Now that's genius! Johnson rolls head referee Jerry Williams back into the ring, and after seeing J-Con's right arm over the chest of Chris Mezz, he slowly makes the count. Brown: It can't end like this!! 1...........................2............................3! Nerdstrom: NEW CHAMPION! NEW CHAMPION! 'Guilty Until Proven Innocent' by Jay Z featuring R. Kelly plays as the crowd is in shock. Most fans throw their garbage into the ring, after seeing the result of J-Con becoming the NEW PIW World Heavyweight Champion. Brown: Ladies and gentlemen, I am in shock over what we have just seen. Jim Johnson has turned on his own, or what we thought, was his franchise wrestler, and now, Jonathan Conspiracy, a man who can be said to be hated by everyone, is our new PIW World Champion. I still can't believe it. Nerdstrom: Well Mike, for one, I don't hate Jonathan Conspiracy, but this proves once again: You cannot trust ANYONE in the business.... even if it is your owner. Brown: Well, I don't think you could trust a sick, sadistic human-being like Jim Johnson anyway. As J-Con is handed the PIW Heavyweight Championship, he holds it close to his face while kissing it, and as he celebrates with Jim Johnson, he can barely even stand up. The man who claims to be 'one letter better' is literally being held up by his newfound friend, as they celebrate on top of the ramp, drawing boos from everyone in attendance through every second of it. Brown: Well folks, that's gonna wrap things up, as we are still in shock of what just happened. For Vinny Nerdstrom, this is Mike Brown saying have a good night, and we'll see ya next week on Massacre for all the latest PIW happenings! Vinny: AFTER All that complaining JCON did about not getting his shot.. HE WAS THE FRANCHISE WRESTLER?! Unreal.. SHOCKING! Brown: MASSACRE IS GOING TO BE HOT!! SO LONG FOLKS! Like I said, she DIDN'T like Johnson. Now she's one of his biggest fans; well next to Jonathan Conspiracy atleast. She watched the event until it faded to black, and the television showed highlights of the event. She continued to watch as a countdown to the replay started, and when it did she phoned up her brother Roberto, who was back in New York after his successful Manhattan Title win in 5 Boros Wrestling; and her cousin Benecio, who was relaxing in a Boston Hotel Room with his partner Benjamin after winning a Four Corners Tag Match on OSW Slam! that night, among alot of other family and friends; including but not limited to fellow Hell's Kitchen Graduates: 'Taylor Made' Robert Wade, 'Hot Head' Scot Redd, 'The Caddy' Jimmy Bradley, 'B.A.B.Y.' Madison Clarke'; as well as the expelled F.A.T.E. wrestler, Eugene Gregory Oswald (EGO) and his former tag team partner from EPIC, 'Self-Professed' Trent Daniels, and others including Jonathan's estranged brother, Romey Ransom. And once all the phone calls were done, she watched the replay, and once the replay ended, she phoned one more person... Jonathan Jerome Leary himself. But he never picked up. This is all Veronica heard: 'Yeah, this is JCON! The FRANCHISE WRESTLER FOR THE BEST FED IN THE WORLD! THE PIW WORLD CHAMPION! And I'm too busy for anybody to bother me right now, leave your message and I'll think about getting back to you, because I'm ONE LETTER BETTER and you're not! Did I get your attention yet! If not Ignorance Is Bliss! ONE!' as 'My Life' by Styles P (of LOX fame) and Pharoahe Monch played in the background. BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! 'Yeah, Jonathan??? This is Veronica; I just wanted to let you know that I saw you out there tonight, and you were awesome baby!' The word 'baby' brought a halt to the message, after four to five seconds of dead air, Veronica pressed on with the message. 'Anyway, I just wanted to say congrats on winning the PIW World Title, and thanks for getting me prepared for 'THE BEST FED IN THE WORLD'... ACTION! Um... give me a call, I love you!' I LOVE YOU! I LOVE YOU! I LOVE YOU! WAIT!!.... I.... LOVE... YOU??? 'Did I just say that???' is what Sweet Aroma thought to herself, while she pressed buttons on the hotel room's phone sending the message. 'SHIT!' Instead of rerecording the message, she mistakenly sent the message. 'Oh well.' she said as she hung up the phone's receiver. 'I hope he doesn't act like Ricardo or Everett when he hears that' is what she thought to herself, as she exited her room, enroute to the hotel's bar. She had to drink one for her World Champion. OH YEAH, IF YOU DIDN'T NOTICE... To be continued... ...but you already knew that.
