Subtle Dream

22 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/22/02 There's nothing you can say Nothing you can do Nothing in between You know the truth Silence. The thoughts were inevitable as he forced himself down upon the soft cushioned pillow. A man, tall in stature, looked up into the night sky. His eyes shown a different story than the look that he had. A man with justice, strength, and ability; laid there ... with a tear. He slowly allowed himself to fall back into a dream. ‘He watched as the hours passed and the wind blew away the remainder of his lifetime. Noises all around startled him, but not enough to make him worry. It was him, and only him. That was the way that he liked it. He laid in his place for days and days on end, just staring up into the sky. Hoping for a change, it continued. Life went on ... nothing happened. White paint, which blanketed his face gently, began to cascade down his face endlessly. The tears had ended, but the pain remained. Truth was the key to all torture. Past thoughts agitated his very key to existence. A beautiful smile, with a tormented soul. One day, he prayed that all of the unanswered questions would come to him. Nothing left to face Nothing left to lose Nothing takes your place Joker was the name that was placed on this character. His eyes, bloodshot. The veins were thickened and even bloodier red than imaginable. Whites taken by the over dominant flow of the heavy crimson veins. Was it even possible to see out of the bloody eyes. No movement whatsoever. No answer to come, either. His hair, short and scraggily. Beads of summer sweat dropped gently from his forehead down onto the ground. A puddle of salt formed, from the depressing stream of sweat, that made its home on a clump of sand that laid near his head. Not a single piece of hair on his head matched with the rest. Each individual strand of hair went its own separate ways, just like his stories. His body, weakened and beaten. Bruises were planted deep under the thick skin of the human being. Blood seeped gently from the open hole in his mouth. Lips cracked and began to chap. He didn't care. Entertainment for him was just laying there. Muscles so weak that he couldn't even stand. Joker was in very poor shape. ' When they say you're not that strong Well you're not that weak It's not your fault Once more, it was nighttime. Joker laid there, still. This time, moving about silently. His muscles twitched as he tried to rejuvenate the strength to pick up his lifeless body. Small sounds sprung from his mouth as he ventured into further movement. 'Ugh...' Once again, silence. Joker seemed to have given up already, he faintly deposited himself back where he was and tried again. The fuel for motivation flashed before his eyes. Past thoughts and former dreams took over his head. He nearly broke down into convulsions as the worsened. Energized, Joker elevated up instantly. In a sitting up position, the view seemed so much distinct. The detailed surroundings began to take play as he worked the muscles in his upper body hoping for them to come back. When you climb up to that hill Up to your place I hope you're well There's nothing left to prove 'Dammit, I should have known.' He repetitively said to himself over and over. Knowing the result of attempted sleep, fear took over. Everything had happened to him while he was sleeping; the dreams, the nightmares, the truth. He stood up from his establishment and looked around. It was still nighttime. The dream made him think he had slept for millenniums. If it hadn't have happened before, Joker would have been a little more worried than what he was. Revival had become one of the easier things for him in the last few months. Sleep, quite possibly, had to be the hardest. 'Just another day, I guess.' Retorted sentences took place, eagerly. His life was in the palm of his hands. Joker elevated himself enough to make it inside of his household. There it was ... a small sealed envelope laying on the seat of his chair. A chair in which he always installed himself. Smiling, he looked down at the letter in realization. 'Action!' In a low voice, he began to fade away as he read the letter continuously. Nothing I won't do Nothing like the pain I feel for you Nothing left to hide Nothing left to fear I am always here
