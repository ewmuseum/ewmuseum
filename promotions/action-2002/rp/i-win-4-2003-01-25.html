Action! — Tales

I win-4

25 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Haven’t heard from me in a while. I can only imagine how delighted you must’ve been. And you can only imagine how delighted I am to be polluting your auditory senses with my bullshit once again. Anyhow, I believe I left off with: getting punched in the jaw, receiving a virtual death sentence, sucker punching Noah Hawkins, and smoking crack. I actually don’t think I added that in my last promo, but you’ve known me for a little while, I think it goes without saying. *** The Pressure Point, following my unsolicited assault on Punk Bru-I mean Noah Hawkins-I did some fine mic work, flipping the roles of victim and assailant, adding my own brand of irritating arrogance that was sure to get the fans and Noah pissed off, building me up as a heel. Not the best method of “getting over”, but it suited my needs at the time. He came down to the ring, we traded a few rights and lefts, had an impromptu tag-match, won, and thus a feud (used in the most loose sense of the word) was formed. Besides the natural animosity I’m so good at invoking in people, our rivalry had all the substance of a street bawl instigated by a funny look and an abundance of bravado. Wrestling requires more drama than that, but I just didn’t have the time. I needed something, and quick. The whole thing depended on me being an irritating prick and Webst-I mean-Noah Hawkins’s underdog appeal to get the fans to give a fuck. Me the, conceited bully, and Noah, the innocent victim caught up in my random bullshit. Hopefully it would be enough. And it was...for a while. I kinda figured “I don’t like you, lets fight.” wouldn’t keep people interested for long. It got stale shortly after the initial confrontation. Flying across the country by day to wrestle in shows, then spending nights allowing shitheads slam my face into a chainlink cage, left little time to invest creative energy into my endeavor. Heh, I used the word endeavor. Applaud me.
