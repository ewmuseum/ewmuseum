Love? Geometric? The Fuck? THESE ARE MY REACTIONS!

8 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: This isn't a part of Mike's series. Far from it. This is a flaming pile of shit that was hit at 100 miles per hour. I just feel that the reaction by my character should finally appear. Anyway, enjoy, if you can. Which I doubt. My mouth was a gap. I just couldn’t believe what I saw. No way was that possible. Keri did not just do something that breaks the laws of all that is sacred in this world. She didn’t just kiss that scumbag fucktard shit faced tyrannical demon loving cock sucking asshole eating parasitic cum guzzling eleven-year-old raping punk ass bitch. SHE DIDN’T! NO WAY IN HELL! KENT ANTHASON AND KERI MALONE? BULLSHIT THAT IS REAL! I know better then that though. She just broke her husbands’ heart. No, ripped it out, showed it before him, and as he collapsed he saw her shitting on it. That fits this whole thing perfect. I swear a lot when I have just been shown something that is impossible. Like seeing someone crash an airplane into a mountain, then come out just fine. The chances of that happening are far greater then Keri breaking up with her love. That same bond forced those two to survive during that reign of terror. No matter how many times I, or we, used a shovel to pummel one or the other, that heart wouldn’t die. That bond is something unholy. At my worst, I could merely pull them apart. That will was still there. Anthason, somehow, mutilated that relationship. I was shocked. After fifteen minutes, I was sure to rewind and review the kiss. And I played it in real time. And that shock became a brooding anger. Anger that puts people away for a long time. Like having your whole family slain before you for some false reason. I was angry with Anthason. I know how that freak thinks. He thinks he is so damn cool, so much better then anyone else. I have been hearing how he is so psychologically sound. He can crush you with an ease unknown. Anthason doesn’t scare me. He is just the same little boy he always has been. He picks on the little kids, takes their candy, and when the policeman comes to pummel him to teach him a lesson, he cowers in fear. Can’t accept what he deserves. Always in the right, never wrong. Kent is selfish. He is angry. He wants to be some popular. To be a star. However, he isn’t anything but a loser. This is coming from a guy who failed to kill himself twice. That would really hurt, because in the most sincere of terms, he is more of a mistake then anyone I have ever known. Malone is going to be coming for you Kent. And if he can’t get the job done, then you might just bring more people into your holy crusade. Not saying my name or anything. It’s just a promise, wrapped to look like a warning. Keri, however, I can’t put into words what I would say to her. Fucking dumb bitch. Doesn’t know how things really are. She just wants to get Joey for putting his career first… That, Joey deserves, but Jesus Christ, see a counselor. Or talk to him. Chicken shit bitch! MAYBE IF YOU CARED YOU WOULD HAVE MADE AN EFFORT! Instead, you are out with some “bad ass” that wears all black, acts tough, and actually has no balls. It’s like high school all over again. Stupid people letting their emotions rule over everything. So confused about these different directions. Doesn’t make you want to shake them for going at this head strong with no intention of slowing down and thinking things out? Slap them? Professional wrestling just became like high school again. People too scared to have a voice and speak up. People too stupid to put life in front of a career. People too stupid to realize what they have just done. All we need is the teachers, a lunch break, and a two-story building, and you got the norm. Fucking retards. I know why this is all happening. Mixed feelings. A drift. And a dirty vulture smelling the blood. Through my looking glass self, I see history repeating itself. Another person heart broke for some dream. And I will plunge into the new gapping, bleeding wound in my world with two smoking barrels and a whole lot of piss and vinegar. Maybe I am overreacting. Joey better pick up the pieces and go after things while he still has a chance. Or else he’ll do something he will regret forever; LOSE EVERYTHING If he does, trust me. A shovel might make a reappearance.
