Action! — Tales

Bust A Garvin.

30 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 30th, 2002 Introduction It was only mere hours from Jeff Garvin’s pay per view debut in Action! Wrestling. For the past eight hours the Original was locked away at a local hole in the wall, a run down recreation centre, training for his match against Coral Avalon. As we join him, he has just exited from the gymnasium and was on his way to his hotel for a quick shower before heading off to Retaliation… Sunlight, an unexpected enemy on this day in question. It burned. It was the first sign of daylight that Jeff Garvin had seen in almost forty-eight hours. He had been living like a vampire lately. Sleeping during the day, walking the streets at night. It confused him as to why the human race worshipped the sun so much, when drawn children liked to sketch a cute little happy face in the centre of the huge orange ball in the sky. If that son of a bitch had a face he’d be laughing at Jeff’s pain right now. Just like everyone else. In a matter of seconds, Jeff had the ‘home made’ sunglasses resting on his the bridge of his nose, shielding his eyes from the mind raping light as his body adjusted to the atmosphere. He had always enjoyed a good down pour, cleaned the stench of the hicks that flocked to him every second of ever day for a simple autograph. Such sad people, he thought, readying himself for the large mob of pedestrians that he expected to run out from behind of various lampposts and move in on him. He had the pen already in his hand. Minutes passed and no one came. “Bah. Must be the cool new shades, they’re so snazzy the idiots of this town probably have no idea its me.” He reassured himself, sticking his hands in his pockets. Standing there, rocking back and forth on the curb in front of Roy’s Gym, he wondered if he looked stupid for wearing a rain jacket and rubber boots in the middle of the hottest day in all of September. In the distance a family, three dogs, and a shifty-eyed pigeon were laughing it up, pointing and cracking jokes at the Original’s expense. Really though, it wasn’t his fault, Steve the weatherman had predicted a tropical storm. What Jeff didn’t realise was that he was watching the forecast for Kuwait. “Steve… Your going on the frikken’ list.” Jeff grunted, digging out a crumbled up piece of loose leaf paper from his coat pocket and adding the local weatherman’s name down to the list of people that he planned to have tap out in the most respected and beloved finishing moves of all time. An elderly, blind man walked by, seemingly reading Jeff’s thoughts and grunted out… “The Golden Elbow!” Before jumping into a nearby trash can, confusing you and me a like. “No! The Hammer Jammer! The ______ ______ is the devil! Send in back to the fire pits of hell!” Jeff gritted his teeth and jumped up and down. He would never utter those two words if someone paid him. Well, maybe if someone paid him… He was poor… And VERY greedy. So yeah… “TESTIFY!” Screamed a random bible salesman in the distance. “Amen. I better teach that old man why you don’t mention… Grr… That name… In the Garvinator’s presence!” With that, Jeff cracked his knuckles and rushed over to the mysterious trash reciptical and peered down, ready to open a can on the crazy, blind man within. But all that was left was smoke… …And a half eaten cheese burger. Yum. Jeff licked his lips but a loud rap song stopped him as he was about to snatch it up. “FUCK THA POLICE! FUCK EM HARRRRRRRD!” His attention was averted to the town square where a group of African Americans were busy ‘free styling’ while others of the group break danced on the concrete below. Jeff smiled. “Negroes.” Five minutes later. Oh no… “Yo, dawgs. What’s up in the hizhouse, my brudda. Mind if I break it on down with yo’ crew?” The group of black men all stood in a giant clump, shocked at what Jeff had just said. One of them rolled his eyes and stepped forward. “Hi, I’m Greg.” “Yo, dawg!” Greg extended his hand and Jeff attempted a ‘stereotypical’ black hang shake. “Um… Yes… Do you mind, sorry, but we are rehearsing our parts for a play. It’s about a group of stereotypical black males who get mixed up in a gang. Though it has some serious morals in it, it’s meant to parody some people’s thick minded views on African Americans.” “Yah! Dat’ shat be wack, dogg! Holla’ back!” Jeff shouted again. Another poor attempt at trying to fit in. Tisk. Tisk. “Holler… Back? Sorry, I didn’t really understand that.” “Hey Greg, maybe this guy is mentally challenged or something, you know how I feel about helping the handicap. Let him stay.” One of the guys in the back whispered to Greg, who was standing at the head of the pack. “Okay. You can stay… What’s your name?” Garvin opened his mouth to reply, but paused. Jeff was so plain… He needed something with more spunk. Something that these ‘black’ folk could relate to. “Shanaynay, straight outta the hood!” “…Okay.” Greg replied. “Check dis’ shizzle out foos’! TIME TO BUST A GARVIN!” Bust a what? Jeff dropped down onto the ground and began flopping from side to side before getting back to his feet. “Listen dawgs, I need to go pimp dat shizzie somewhere else… So check you foos lata!” They were all stunned as to what they had just witnessed. Was it even real? Could a human being actually be that stupid. “Wow. I really showed them up.”
