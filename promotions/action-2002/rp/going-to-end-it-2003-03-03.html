Going to End It.

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Conrad Ramsey spent only one day at the hospital. He had a sore back, which he was told to rest, and a cut on his forehead. Everytime he crossed 'The Danger Man' he seemed to come away with stitches. This time had been no different. Thirteen of them closed the wound. But he walked out of the hospital under his own power. No one had called him. No one had visited him. He found a pay phone, the day he was released, and used it to call someone very special to him. Samantha Dox. 'No, I'll just be going home. The doctor said I need to rest myself. I'm very much ailing, Samantha. My knee is throbbing with pain, my back aches with great agony, and my head, it just hurts.' .......... 'I suppose you could, yes. I wouldn't mind you coming down to visit. But what will your son say?' .......... 'Well, we haven't told him about us. I mean, I haven't. Have you?' .......... 'Good. He seems to be gunning for me again, so I don't want to be adding any more fuel to the fire, you know? I just want to rest up and then, finally, put an end to it all.' .......... 'Did it sound cryptic? I'm sorry if my tone indicated something else, all I was doing was just putting it flatly. It's going to end one way or another. Either he walks away victorious or I will.' .......... 'That won't be a third option, Samantha. One of us will have to prevail, otherwise it will never end.' .......... 'Okay. I'm going to catch a flight to Louisville as soon as I can. When will you be arriving?' .......... 'Okay. I'll see you then.' .......... 'I love you, too. 'bye.' Conrad then hung up the phone. He stood there, leaning against the booth. He took a deep breath and then walked from the hospital. He had no intention of going home to Leitchfield. He wasn't planning on seeing his girlfriend, Max Danger's mother, either. He had only one thought on his mind. To end it. Finish things off with 'The King of Submission'. It had gone on for far too long. It was going to be over... Monday night at Pressure Point.
