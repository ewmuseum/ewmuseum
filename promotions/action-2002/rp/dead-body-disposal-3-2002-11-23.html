Date: 11/23/2002 you wonder why its difficult to understand and hear me. its because this is way it should me. if i was easy to read and decipher i’d lose all my worth. this is why i speak so softly. so cryptically. so faintly. like white on light grey. you are supposed to stain yourself to understand me. i don’t want it to be easy for you. but I do want you to try. if you aren’t willing to work to understand, you’re not worthy of understanding. this is why i do what i do. why a say what i say. it is because i don’t want to let anyone to close. i don’t want you to know. i don’t want your pity. i don’t want your acceptance. but the question begs... why then do i speak at all? its an inquiry which i haven’t answered to myself yet Ok, so now you’ve killed, cleaned, hacked, and packed your dead body. Its time to finally getting rid of this sucker. Oh but one more final point before I continue which I failed to mention in the last part of this tutorial. There is a simpler way to do all this. Only thing is it restricts your method of murder to strangulation with a thin rope or wire. Death by strangulation is a lot different then death by blunt trauma, shooting, or stabbing. Strangling someone puts immense pressure on the veins and arteries of the victim. This pressure causes blood vessels and capillaries in the face, brain, and rest of the body to pop, and traps blood in the system. This makes the body warm for several hours after death, and greatly slows down the rate of purification. This is a forensic scientist’s headache, because determining the exact time of death is impossible as long as you’ve done a good job cleaning the body. With this method you may want to keep the body enact. Just be sure to fold the corpse into a fetal position or into a strong sleeping bag quickly, because moving a body after rigor mortis has begun to sink in can be a pain in the ass. Now on to next, and final step in dead body disposal: Disposing of the dead body There is a great deal of effective ways to permanently dispose of a corpse. Some of the best in my opinion include: · hacking the body into a pieces as discussed earlier, and feeding them to starved animals, chiefly pigs, or dogs. Just be sure to bury the head and hair in a different location, as the animal digestive system wasn’t built to digest hair, and sometimes teeth. And like BrickTop says in the movie Snatch “You could do this afterwards. But you don’t wanna go shifting through pig shit now do you?” · Some of the more traditional methods are, burying and dumping the body in a deep body of water. If you’re burying, make sure the hole in the ground is very deep, about 4 to 6 feet. Bury it too shallow and a dog might pick up the scent and go digging up your handy work. If you’re dumping the corpse in a large body of water, make sure you dump it with something dense like chains or a cinder block. You wanna make sure it sinks, and stays sunk. · Dissolving the entire body with a powerful harsh coercive such as hydrochloric acid, sulfuric acid, or mountain dew. This method has many benefits. It destroys any detectable trace of the corpse and you don’t even have to leave your house. These are the methods I’d suggest to those new to dead body disposal. Myself, I’ve chosen a more creative method. I hacked up my corpse after deep-freezing it in a fridge for a few hours, then broke into few meat-packaging plants in the industrial districts of Manhattan and Staten Island and grounded up the body parts in a meat grinder mixing it homogenously with the cow, pig, and chicken beef. This way the body parts are efficiently disposed off, and I have the pleasure of fucking over everyone how indulges in the human herbivore nature. Think about that the next time you bite into a hot dog. You may have just served as an accomplice in a homicide. Thanks a bunch you fat fucks ;) I wouldn’t recommend such a method for novices though. I’ve been disposing of dead bodies for a while now, so I know what I’m doing and can afford to deviate from the norm every once and a while. You don’t have such a luxury. So just keep it simple, and once you got a couple “homies” under your belt, then you can experiment a little bit. Otherwise it’d be like 13 year old girl giving up the asshole before her first kiss. She could do it, but judging by the amount of blood and tears coming from her, I don’t think she enjoyed it too much. But the statutory rape tutorial is for another time :) And obviously use common sense. You don’t want to toss a body over a bridge in broad daylight, or bury it in playground. Use extreme discretion. I’d choose a late night time around 2 to 4 am. Train yards, abandoned farms, and construction sites a prime place to bury your dead body. Deep rivers, and lakes are the optimal places to dump ‘em off if that’s your thing. I wouldn’t use the ocean however. There’s too much risk of body parts washing ashore along with the tide. Also when moving the dead body, don’t ever use anything to numb your senses to the smell of the corpse. If it reeks, you want to be aware of that so you can do something about it. This is how some of the greatest serial killers got caught. Joel Rifkin for instance was stopped for having a broken taillight in Long Island, and the cops smelt the pungent aroma of death emanating from the dead hooker in his trunk. He didn’t however, because he masked the smell of decay by smearing acne cream under his nose. John Wayne Gacy was finally found out because of the offensive odor that enveloped his house from the 20 plus bodies he had stashed under the foundation. He was around the smell so long; he became numb and maybe even developed an affinity for it. You want to always know how serious the smell is. So you can spray some Lysol or get off the road. You should now know, in full detail, how to create, prepare, and dispose of a dead body. Have fun with it. Try not to kill people you know just yet. Random victims are prolly more reasonable since the lack of a real motive will fuck the authorities up some, leaving you room to fuck up a little bit if this is your first time. Like always, this is Invalid hoping someone uses these method on you. -Eat my ass, and God Bless
