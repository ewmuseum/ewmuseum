Dear Diary

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I’ll Be Waiting For one of the first times in almost 18 hours, Angelica Dawson was laying alone in the hotel room. The break from Bobby Minio wasn’t wanted, but valued. She just laid there, her nude, sweat-sheered body covered by the sheets. The sheets were twisted, creased and crumpled in every possible place. Ever since they had left Pressure Point together, they had been on a non-stop thrill ride, to say the very least. They had MANY ups, one or two downs, twists and turns into every position imaginable and then they came to a slow, rolling stop. Bobby had left to get new wraps for his wrists. He said he’d be gone for twenty minutes. Ha. She had a feeling he’d be back in about 10. He’d be speeding in his little Mustang to get back there and continue to fuck her brains out. She was going to use every second of the time he was gone to recover and clean up a bit before she got sweaty all over again. Angelica slowly rolled out of the bed, placing her feet on the light beige carpet. She moved the sheet, which still clung to her body, and stood up. Making her way to the bathroom, she stumbled a little bit. It was as if her legs were Jell-O™ and they hadn’t fully gelled up before she stepped out of the mould. She made her way into the bathroom and, once she got in, grabbed hold of the counter. She couldn’t keep her balance any other way. Her eyes slowly rose as she looked at her own reflection in the mirror. What she saw was actually something she liked. She saw her messy red hair. She noticed that her face gave off an exhausted look. She was a bit tired, but she was as physically and sexually awake as she had ever been. ‘Oh man.’ Angelica sighed and chuckled a bit. She was surprised with herself. She NEVER jumps right into a sexual relationship with somebody, let alone somebody she works with. But Bobby was different. Bobby was like a breath of fresh air. She had been waiting for somebody who was able to do what he did. That was to do something daring. To test their boundaries. And that’s exactly what Bobby Minio did when he followed Angelica as they walked out on their tag team partners the night before. Angelica reached over to the small towel ring and grabbed the facecloth off of it. She turned the tap on, adjusting the temperature so it was lukewarm, and soaked the facecloth in it. She rung the facecloth out a bit and then washed her face down a bit. The water felt cooler than intended on her red face. She dropped the facecloth and looked back up at the mirror. Her dark blue eyes stared right back at her, but they weren’t just there. There was a slight twinkle in her eye that she noticed. She smiled a bit, kinda like a teenage girl being asked to the prom by the guy she likes, and turned, walking out of the bathroom. She slowly crawled back into bed, but she didn’t go in alone. In her hand were a pen and a small book. It was a dairy Angelica had kept ever since she started in professional wrestling. She had written many entries in it, mainly about her title wins and joining a new federation. But this time, she was writing about much more. She opened her diary up and flipped to the first available page. She took the cap off the pen, tossing it aside. She started writing in her diary. Dear Diary: This has been one hell of a day, I can assure you that. I finally got what I wanted, though. I got Bobby Minio. Yay! Go me. But, yeah, Bobby and I were facing each other in a tag match. He was with Max; I was with Veronica as usual. But near the end of the match...it was as if I was taken over. My sexual urge for Bobby had gotten too strong for me to control. I stomped on the mat to get his attention. As soon as he looked over, I motioned to backstage. I wasn’t sure if he picked up what I meant, but I dropped down off of the apron. I didn’t want to leave Veronica all alone in the match. I mean, I thought she’d be able to handle Danger if Bobby and I both left...I guess I was wrong. But, as soon as I got behind the curtains, I saw the small TV screen...Bobby was walking up the ramp. My heart started beating a mile a minute. The butterflies in my stomach were going crazy and more seemed to be coming out of their cocoons every second. And as soon as Bobby came through those curtains, I knew that he wanted what I wanted. We came together in a passionate kiss, not wasting a single second. It was electric. And then we went to his hotel room. I’ve never had more fun in my life. But now...now I’m starting to regret leaving Veronica alone. Because of my hormones, we lost our Dyad titles. We WILL get them back, but...I still feel guilty. I guess I’ve just got to face the music when I see her. Bye. Angelica closed her diary and placed it on the nightstand. She put the pen down beside it right as the sound of a hotel card was heard. The door to the room started to open as the scene faded out. Black.
