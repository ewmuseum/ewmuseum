Love Geometric - IV

30 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/30/2002 October 23rd, 2002 Wednesday Morning 'What is it?' Keri Lindum asked, snapping Kent Anthason out of his personal reflection of Alyssa Cleeda. Anthason turned toward Keri and flashed her a quick, uneasy smile. 'Nothing,' he said, 'Just thinkin' about something.' There was a moment of silence before Keri broke it. 'What did you mean by 'knowing a few things'?' Kent blinked, then remembered what he had said, coughed nervously, and leaned forward, toward Keri. 'You remember Alyssa, don't you? My old girlfriend?' Keri nodded. 'Well, she disappeared about ten months ago. She just simply packed her things and left. The thing is that, well, Lucas told me that the last person that saw her was Joey.' Kent said, with a sigh. 'Aside from the title thing, that's pretty much why I'm pissed at him. He stopped talking to me and he wouldn't tell me anything about him and Alys.' 'Oh.' Keri said, suddenly forming her own story in her mind. 'So, you think that Alyssa... and Joey...' 'It's a thought.' Anthason said, hastily finishing her story before she could say it. Lord knows that Kent couldn't take the thought of Joey and Alyssa having a one night stand. He nervously turned away from Keri. 'A thought that I don't wish to contemplate.' Kent found it wise to quickly change the subject, and decided to ask the first question that came to his mind, 'You know what? Why don't you and I head out?' Keri blinked. She couldn't believe what Kent just suggested. 'Excuse me?' Anthason appeared to be regretting the asking of the question, but he pressed on with it. 'Well, you know, it's kind of uncomfortable to be here, not that you're uncomfortable or anything, it's just--' 'Let's go.' interrupted Keri. Kent Anthason actually did a double-take at what she said, and seemed really flabbergasted at her suddenly smiling face. 'Zuh?' Anthason asked. Then he regained his usual cool demeanor and smiled. 'Alright, then. The guy that brought me here is gone, so we could use your car, maybe?' Keri nodded, smiled, and got up from her chair. 'I'll take a moment to dress more appropriately, if that's okay with you.' she said, walking away from Anthason and up the stair into the bedroom. Kent looked up as she left and called out to her. 'Don't take too long!' Keri shut the door behind her, in such a way that she held her back against the door. She took a moment to breathe and sigh, locked the door(to avoid Kent walking in on her), and walked to the closet. After a few minutes of looking through her dresses and clothes, she emerged with a denim jacket, a striped shirt, and a long white skirt. She threw these clothes on and walked down to meet Kent, who stood at the bottom of the steps. Kent Anthason saw her, and decided to make a decision. 'I think it's time for a makeover, Keri.' 'What? You don't like it?' Keri asked. 'No, it's fine. I just think you should try something new for a change. Here, come with me, I'll see what I can do to help.' he said with a smile, taking Keri by the hand and leading her up the stairs and into the bedroom. 'Great. The bedroom. You don't waste any time, Kent.' Keri antagonized, with a smile on her face as well. 'Ha ha. Very funny.' was all Kent could respond with.
