The Sandman's Gift - Part 3

17 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday; July 8th, 2003. 'Jeff, wake up... Please, wake up.' The soft cries of Julie Garvin were all that he could hear. His eyes slowly opened to a welcomed sight. Julie leaned over. Her gaze piercing his soul and filling him with warmth. He was unsure of where he was. He hugged the blankets close to him, then explored his surroundings as his eyes darted from one area of the room to the next. A hospital, and a nice one at that. There was an IV in his wrist that he felt like pulling out had Julie not assured him it was there for a reason. Dozens of questions flooded his mind. What happened to the plane? Where had Paul gone? And most important: What happened to him? He waited a moment to collect his thoughts, then bluntly stated, 'I'm hungry.' At that moment, a doctor of what looked to be Italian heritage entered through the open doorway. Garvin pushed with his hands up to a seated position against the headboard. Though the physician in charge of his well being did not seem happy with this. 'You probably shouldn't be moving around a lot, Mr. Garvin,' he said, glancing up the clock in the corner of the room, and then scribbling the time he awoke down on a sheet of yellow paper. Jeff rose a hand up to his forehead. There was a golf-ball sized lump above his right eye and it hurt when he put any sort of pressure on it. But being the stubborn bastard he is- wouldn't stop poking and prodding at it until Julie swatted his hand away. 'Stop that.' 'What happened to me?' He said slowly, squinting as he tried to fully wake up. He was still very tired. 'We found quite a bit illegal narcotics in your blood stream- the drug known as 'Ecstasy' to be exact. You overdosed.' The doctor said. Julie looked shocked, and she had a right to be. The thought that her husband was a drug user sent her into a confused daze. She never had a clue. She was already crying. 'Why? Why would you do that!' She turned away, staring out the window while she wiped the tears away with her sleeve. Jeff shook his head. It hurt to do so. He felt like he was hung over and any small movement sent a painful chain reaction throughout his body. 'No, no I'm not. You know I'm not!' The doctor stepped back and focused his attention down on the clipboard he had been holding. He wanted to give them room to talk things through with a sense of privacy. 'Then how! How did this happen!' She waited for his answer. But he had none. Thinking back, he remembered Paul, the man that had been seated beside them, offering him what he thought were sleeping pills. That was the only explanation he could gather. 'I was drugged. That man, P-' he stuttered, 'Paul! He drugged me!' Garvin studied Julie. Though his story was true, she found it difficult to believe. She had not seen this man he referred to as 'Paul'. Though she hoped, for their marriage's sake, that he was telling the truth. He could have pleaded with her. But he didn't. He knew there was very little chance the police would find anything. What he had was a possible fake name and an appearance he could hardly remember. Everything made sense now: Why Paul had been so unwilling to release information about himself. It made sense... Though he wished it didn't. What he was left with was a feeling of betrayal. His trust in others had been severely diluted. He felt sick.
