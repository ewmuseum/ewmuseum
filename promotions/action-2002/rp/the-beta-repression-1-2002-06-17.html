The Beta: Repression (1)

17 June 2002

EVERY SINGLE BIT OF MY STRENGTH USED TO LED HIM DOWN THIS PATH! IT ALL FUCKING ENDS! ENDS! IN ONE SWIFT MOVE, HE KILLED IT ALL! ALL OF IT! Death is not acceptable for him. Or her. I was going to give them the easy way out. FUCK THEM! I walk down the cement pathway that leaves the Superdome. I am bloody. Confused. Beaten. I used to think I was doing this all so perfectly. I lied to myself. Who is the “I”, anyway? That Avalon was mentioning you, Kenny. He said I was stealing your thoughts or something like that. He said I was this Villam Ender character. Who the fuck is Villam Ender? These names all sound familiar, but reject their existence in my head. I am not sure who or what I am. I am just pushing through the motions. The walking dead, so to speak. I have lost my will to live. In one swift swing, I lost my control over Joey. Now, he is back, with more fight in his soul then ever before. I welcome that. I welcome his spirit. I now have a burning cesspool of hatred eating a hole through my stomach. Revenge, in its ultimate form. I have a plan. What good is a plan? I sit around here, sitting on this curb, staring long and hard at the sky. The stars, and full moon, the darkness. It all feels familiar. I usually lay around, staring at the sky for answers. For I might look like god when I am fighting on Wednesday nights, and when I am crushing minds. The thing is, I am even weaker then any of you. I am fragile. Every little bump in this plan has made me want to die that much more. The blood was drying all over my face. In my goatee. Swelling shuts my eyes. I can feel the pulsating of my nerves, screaming out in pain. Pain isn’t an illusion anymore. It is all too real. Pain, however, is the only thing keeping my legs churning, my arms punching, and my mind working. It is the only thing telling me I am alive, and not dead. I get up from the curb and walk. Where am I going? Maybe the stars will tell me.
