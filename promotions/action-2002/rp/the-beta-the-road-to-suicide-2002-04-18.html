The Beta: The Road To: Suicide

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/02 The world screws him… The world has maxed out his breaking point… This is where it gets interesting… For within this scene broods chaos… And chaos there shall be… Because chaos is only the beginning… --------------------------- There I sat, blood caked onto my face, my body bruised and battered. Even when I should have won I was forced to stomach a tie. A FUCKING TIE! TO HIM! TO THAT FUCKER! TO THAT FUCKING POSER SHITHEAD! It makes my blood boil so badly. My skin might be cold and clammy from lack of blood, but what is still left inside is hot. So hot that it’s burning my heart. I am finished. Tonight saw the end of everything sacred in this battle. When I had him beaten…time was against me. Time is such a prick. Malone has to be in his hotel room now, laughing at my misfortune. Him and Keri must be toasting to my demise. That piece of shit…he used me… Again… AGAIN! JUST LIKE IN HIGH SCHOOL! JUST LIKE THE IWO! I saw that fire in his eyes. Even as I hit him with that shovel over and over…even when I hit his bitch…even when I left the ring painted with their blood…he still had it in his eyes. The eyes of a champion…a man in control. I lost my edge against him. He is just too good. So I reached down and went through my duffel bag and grabbed her. My Colt M1911A1 was in my hands. I loaded a magazine into it and pointed the thing under my head. I thought of how Quinn would react to seeing me die. She would be happy…because I would be free from living in this predestined Hell. She always wanted to see me happy…even if it hurt her. I loved her so much that it was not even funny. I mean…come on…she was the only reason I did not implode before. I had kept her away from this business for awhile. She would be so happy to see my family there, as someone gives my eulogy. I would like to hear it. Maybe I can pull myself away from the abyss for long enough to listen. Wait… Wait one fucking minute. I am Syphon Fission. I am the beginning and the end. I am everything and nothing. I am necessary in this world gone soft. Malone isn’t. I have a job to do… I have to send him to the black abyss for his crimes. I am only trying to save him. He will thank me later…once we meet after death. He will realize it was for the best, because I am his salvation. I am his God. It is my duty to put the wounded animal out of its misery. Maybe I will add to its misery before I let it slowly… Painfully… Die. Death isn’t something he can beat. He can not cheat death when it is destined. He will realize that the blood that will be spilled is not on my hands and on my head. It’s on his. After all…he made me. He is responsible. I have a lot of changing to do. I have to make sure I map out the plans. Within my brain lies the key to my ultimate victory. He will understand. HE WILL HAVE TO UNDERSTAND! This is going to be easy… Almost too easy.
