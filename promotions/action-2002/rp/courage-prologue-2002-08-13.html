While Joey Malone travelled cross-country to New Orleans for a bit of soul searching, he had brought with him a young girl named Allison Lindum. Allison, a college student, was oddly fascinated with the always-nervous twenty-year old, and stayed with him for a month and a half. But alas, it didn't last. For Allison cared a lot for her education and for her future, and was forced to leave Coral's life as quickly as she entered it. Just when things were looking up for Coral, with his entry into the Internet Wrestling Organization and the first relationship he's ever had, it all came crashing down. He wasn't accepted in IWO. Many wrestlers didn't like him too much despite his positive attitude and ease to work with. They felt that he didn't work hard enough to get in the IWO, despite a year of busting his ass to even be considered as a member of IWO. Yet, despite this, Coral managed to get himself over with the crowd. His charisma and his near suicidal tactics earned his place in their hearts. But what use is the admiration of the crowd when you're not happy? Coral wasn't happy, and no matter how much the fans screamed at him, he couldn't shake his feelings of emptiness and depression. Coral felt as though there was a piece of himself missing, and he knew what that piece was. Yet he wasn't sure how he was going to go about finding that missing piece, let alone fitting it back into his life. As he drove along a deserted highway in New Mexico, headed west, Coral Avalon knew that the love of his life was just another two hundred miles from where he was, in Phoenix. He didn't really care about his job as a wrestler, the rent on his apartment, or anything else. His mind was focused on seeing Allison again, no matter what. The trouble is... ...would he have the courage to do more than just see her?
