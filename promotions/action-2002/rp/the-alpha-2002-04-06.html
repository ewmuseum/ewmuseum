The Alpha...

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-6-02 I walk amongst the crowd. They know me. They wave at me. They pull me aside. “What’s it like to work with a great person like Joey Malone?” “Joey Malone sure is charismatic, isn’t he?” “Joey Malone sure did put on a great match at Resentment, didn’t he Fission?” “Cheer up buddy, everyone loses to that guy.” I smile and lie. I say yes, and mention how great he is to me. I mention our friendship. The good times. I do not tell them much about 2001. Then I would get angry and hit them so hard in the mouth that I would need to pull my fist out of their esophagus. Joey Malone is so great. Cheer up buddy… FUCK OFF YOU FUCKING POSERS! They can not understand the hell I am forced to live because of that man. Not one fucking bit. They couldn’t possibly understand. How many of them have had something that is theirs stolen by someone else who takes credit for it all? How many of them have seen a friend screw them in the ass, and be expected to do nothing but pull their pants down, and let them ram them up the ass. They would react. I can’t react. If I do, I am a bad guy. FUCK OFF YOU FUCKING POSERS! Your hero is a liar. Your hero is a cheat. Your hero is nothing more then a scam…someone who is apt to using friends as a bridge to the top. We in the same boat? Huh? I do not think I heard you correctly. I used him too? What? Didn’t you use him too Syphon? “Life, Death, Endurance I. Didn’t you steal that spotlight from Malone?” You are a riot, aren’t you Rashard. Really, you are. Questions from deep within you own subconscious. I gave Malone his title belt and proclaimed the forming of We’re Better Than You 2. I stopped the wrestlers from getting into that ring. Most of those guys will go on record today saying that they hated that son-of-a-bitch. How did I steal his spotlight? If Malone wasn’t dead in the first place…he would have been given that microphone. He knows that. I know that. He just wanted to blame me for the failure of the project. How many times did he steal promo ideas made by me? Quite a few. He could not even come up with some of that special effects stuff. He took what was mine and stated he made it. What about in high school. I was going to finish ahead of him in the class ratings, but he just had to botch up our Physics lab. It got a B, and he finished one percentage point higher. He got the scholarship from the University of Arizona…the scholarship I BUSTED MY ASS FOR! The irony. We both were so intelligent, yet we picked this as a profession. What a joke. Joey was so smart, but he plays dumb. If he doesn’t, he loses an edge on you. He acts so stupid that at times, he makes himself look so innocent. Remember breaking that guy’s neck… Um…what the fuck was his name… It doesn’t matter. I was going to Death Plunge him. Joey just had to add his little touch. He knew full well what he was doing. I saw it in his eyes. That prick has a larger killer instinct then me, yet he plays this dumbfounded clown. He gets love and admiration. I had love… Then she said she needed to find herself before she came back. Before we could move out to that place in Arizona and spend the rest of our days near a pool or just relaxing. Before… We must work so hard, and yet get not a shred of respect from our peers. He has the fans in his left hand. In his right hand lays the world. He just grins that ignorant grin at me…plays stupid… While I suffer in this Hell he helped make for me. Everyone who has been treated unjust… This is for you. This is for our freedom from the insurmountable odds. This is our turn to revenge ourselves… No longer will I let him win… It’s time for my actions to speak louder then words.
