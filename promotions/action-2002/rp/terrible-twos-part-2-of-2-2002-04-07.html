Terrible Two’s: Part (2 of 2)

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/07/02 FADE IN Fox “So.... then I bumped into this business guy and he told me to go to the school and deliver these test results, and then....” Fox fills in his Mommy ‘aboot’ what happened as he tried to get to the next town. But the thing is.... Chris, Pikachu and Mommy are already in the next town! Title: Terrible Two’s: Part Two Date: 04/07/02 6:00 pm EST Place: Business Building, Other Areas OPEN Fox “Of course after I got out of the building, I needed to get a cab right? So....” The scene changes from Fox sitting in Jim’s Dawgs and back to Fox getting into a musty old greenish yellow taxi cab. --- Sitting down making a crackle on the leather seats, the driver asks.... Jamul “Yo dog, I’m Jamul, who are you and where you go?” Christopher, I mean “Chico” replies. Fox “I’m Chris Fox” HOLLLLLY SNEAKERS! I WASN’T SUPPOSE TO SAY THAT! Jamul leans and looks through the plastic separator from the driver and the passenger telling his stunning story. Jamul “Yo man, I’m Jamul Uhehskjfgkvidjkshshfdfkgski, my friends call me Jay-U. You can too bro! Now listen, I picked up an Action Wrestler before yo! His name was like The Onion Finger or something! Anyway, since you’re famous it’s a free ride, where too?” Shocked, Fox thinks, but it’s nothing aboot the topic. Hey Chris? Yeah what? Is Springer on tonight!? It better fucking be! Hey, you just swore! And it wasn’t beeped out! No duh dick slapper! Anything I say to myself can’t be fucking beeped out! Talking to himself still, Fox unwillingly blurts out something. Fox “Pie lately?” Covering his mouth, Chris blushes as Jamul turns around. Not surprised, because he is a cab driver, Jamul responds. Jamul “Oh yeah, I love pie! I eat apple! So where ya going? The moon homey! Of course not! I’m going to fucking Mars! Seriously, I’m heading to the school in the next town over, take me there! Oh wait, I gotta say it to him physically! Fox “The school in the next town pwease!” Make it snappy bitch! I got to make it there without any assholes attacking or seeing me! And after aboot a five minute silence of errr and brrooomss from stopping a starting at traffic lights, finally Christopher gets dropped off at the old ratty school. Jamul “See ya man!” With the cab leaving, Fox gets scared from the noise. With wind blowing and some newspaper pages flying around on the dirty parking lot, Fox walks up to the old damaged school. Only the noise of Chris’ shoes scuffing along the cement can be heard. ........ Holy, this is like as old as my Mommy! Well.... I’d better get this package to the Owner! Chris opens the door closets to him. It makes a creek, then.... DIIIIINNNNNNNNNGGGG Shrieking, Fox runs into the building and up the stairs! It turns out that noise was the bell to signify that Lunch Time was over. Then a bunch of loud thumps could be heard, but those thumps would soon turn into footsteps! And after it was found out that this was a Public school! Tons of “wee ones” ran up the same stairs Fox was on, and seeing them made Fox run up the stairs even faster! Holy Rodeos! Wee ones! I gots to find the office! Wait, I see something, there! Satisfied, Fox finds a sign that points down the hall way saying: Office -->. Yeeeeppppeee! After I hand this in I can go find Mommy, I think she said she be eating a place called Jim’s Dwags. Yeah! With Fox escaping the rush he wanders around, still knowing he has some time to kill. I don’t know why.... He likes hurting things.... (get it?) Thinking it’s the office, Chris walks into a classroom! In session too! Without noticing the kids, ‘Kid’ walks up to the Teacher. Fox “Hi, this is for the office, ok?” Smiling, the Teacher replies. Teacher “Oh, this isn’t the office but when I go past it I’ll drop it by.” As Fox walks away the Teacher repels Chris to stay. Teacher “I’m Mz. Greezal, my name is Austrian!” Austr........ Nevermind! Fox “Really, I got to go, bye!” Mz. Greezal “No way! No one leaves my class before they answer a question correctly! It’s my own rule, here is you’re question, What’s long Green and mushy inside?” Does she really want to know? Wow, finally someone wants to talk aBOOT my Balbasaur! Aka my Fireman! Fox “Uhhhh....” Mz. Greezal “Class, anyone know the answer?” A perky fat ass kid named Joel stands up and blurts the answer out! Joel “GUMBY! I watch him everyday on the Kids channel!” Mz. Greezal “You’re right! Now what do you say, should we let him go?” Joel “I’ll escort him out ma’am! Please!” Mz. Greezal allows Joel to take Chris by the hand and escort him out of the school. While walking down the hallway Joel starts a conversation.... Joel “So mister, are you going to rape me?” Tape you? No! I fucking hate Masking Tape! Not knowing, the naive Christopher Fox replies. Fox “Of course not! But if you’re bad then I’ll do it not only to you but your Dad too!” Joel “My Dad’s the one who fights off my Mom! She does it to me! You’re nice mister! Here we are, the stair case. If you want I’ll show you a cool move!?” Contemplating the offer Chris checks if the coast is clear then agrees. Fox “Sure Jell, I mean Joel!” Joel then climbs up onto the banister and slides down the railing like Bart off The Simpsons. Since Joel was fat, aboot half way down the railing bends a bit. Joel “Now it’s you’re turn Mister!” With Fox already doing some stretches, he gets ready. Taking more time to get on the railing than Joel, Chris starts to slide down! Jeeeeezzzzz, this is purdy fucking easy! Completing the slide Joel goes to high five Chris but accidentally hits Fox in his mouth, like a slap.... Fox “Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck, Shit, Fuck!” DON’T EVER HIT ME THERE AGAIN KID! Satisfied with a paragraph of swearing Fox pushes the kid over as The ‘Kid’ runs out of the building almost to get hit by a Red Geo.... That Mommy Fox and Pikachu are in! FADE OUT .... FADE IN The scene changes back to where this episode started, in Jim’s Dawgz store. With Mommy, Pikachu and of course Chris carrying on their conversation. Fox “That was a wild fucking ride! Oh Mommy, screw off would ya?!” Hurt, Mommy went to leave the store, but before she left, Mommy would say her farewell.... Mommy “I never knew it was in you to be sssooo bad!” As Mommy sounds depressed she finishes her statement in a good mood!” Mommy “If you change you’re attitude.... I’ll be waiting at home, bye!” Mommy leaves, Pikachu and Chris are left to finish their meals.... Fox “So don’t turn fucking sour on me now Pikachu! Damn that was a good ride, I guess Chris Fear and LL’s pep talk on how to be bad really worked! Ugh. Now I gots to go and tag with LL, AND KICK SOME ASS.... BIG TIME!!!!!!!” This might just be the start of a bitchy Chris Fox. The Terrible Two’s is upon us. CLOSE
