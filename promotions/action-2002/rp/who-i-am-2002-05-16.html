Who I am

16 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/16/02 Like what you see? Hello, I’m Dean Winters. The best thing that’s happened to Action for a very long time. I’m good, and I know it. Though I’m sure you do too though. I have an onslaught of wrestling moves, just ask anyone in this business that’s fought me. What? Can’t think of any? Get real. Soon you will. I will become the Action Champion. It’s as simple as that. I will become the Action Champion faster than anyone else in this game. I’m Dean Winters. I got my break in the best fed ever. I have to be good enough. No, really, stop laughing. I want you to clear your head for one moment. Clear it. Now, the next time you see me, I want you to keep this clean head. Weather I fight LLB, or Joey Malone, or even Syphon Fission, using this idea I have just asked of you, you will learn that Dean Winters has more skill than anyone of these pricks which I shall face. I said stop laughing. Soon you will see. Just like the owners will. The ones that fired me. I’m Dean Winters. You’re welcome already, for the endless enjoyment that I will provide.
