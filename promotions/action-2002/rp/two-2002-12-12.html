Two.

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/12/02 August 18th, 2000 Friday Fury, PIW Televised Event Arena Map. You are here. The small black dot positioned in the bottom right hand corner of the hard plastic diagram that illustrated where the many different locker rooms and areas were located. Enough time had been wasted wandering about in the back halls. It all looked the same, turn one corner and you’re lost. Sean’s finger traced a specific route from his point on the stadium’s central map to his destination. VIP Lounge. He had been told back in his dressing room by road agent, Dennis Allen, that it would be a wise choice for him to go and ‘shoot the shit’ with some of the others that he would hopefully get the pleasure to work with in the coming weeks. Back in the independent circuit, he had always kept to himself. It wasn’t exactly intentionally, as if he was trying to shy away from the rest of the rest of ‘boys’, he just wasn’t a very social person. He would compete, shower, and be at his hotel room in time to watch the late night news. At that point in his life, he was far to busy to make friends. His talk with Dennis had somewhat changed his attitude towards this new working environment. He had arrived at the arena that night, hoping to just get through his match without making some intricate mistake that would cost him his job. And now, his confidence was soaring high. Jim Johnson, who he had though didn’t even pay attention to what was going on in his own television program and just let his executives make the talent decisions, had actually gone out of his way to compliment him to one of his staff members. It wasn’t much, but it was a start. A boost of self-assurance that Sean Cylear desperately needed if he was going to survive here. He yawned and turned to leave when he saw steel name plate that was clinging to one of the doors parrell to him. Sean squinted. It simply read: Johnson. As was customary, Sean had jotted down a few ideas for his Showcase persona, some of which he actually deemed worthy of possibly showing to Mr. Johnson if the chance ever came up. If he wanted to get ahead, initiative needed to be taken and fast, while he still was under the watchful eye of his boss. While he still had his attention. He reached out to give a good knock when it swung open before he could get the chance. Jim was leaving for a quick massage at a local health spa across town. He chatted away on his sleek, black cell phone as he locked the door behind him with his free hand. “Yes, just make sure the Chris Mezz action figures are in stores by Monday. You hear me? If I don’t get confirmation that those plastic pieces of shit are sitting on toy store shelves within three days, your ass will be out of a job.” Click. He turned off the phone and sighed heavily. He looked up to see Sean standing before him; his long blond hair still damp from the shower, the area above his right eye red and swollen, and a look of ‘awe’ on his face. As if he had frozen and had no idea what to say. Jim raised an eye brow and gave a dry, “Um… Can I help you?” Sean shook the feeling of shock and anxiety from him so that he could at last get a word out. “Actually, Mr. Johnson, I have a few ideas that I’d like to run by you if you aren’t too busy.” Sean spoke up with an excited tone, digging in the pockets of his Levis and producing a small piece of crumpled up loose-leaf paper. Everything was going great, that was until Jim gave that look. That look of confusion and arrogance. As if trying to say- well, exactly what he said. Jim cleared his throat and replied, “Excuse me? Do you work here?” At that moment, everything changed. He felt dizzy. “Erm… Yeah…” he paused, still in disbelief, “I’m Sean… Sean Cylear, I debuted in my match with Darren earlier tonight. Don’t you remember?” Silence followed, Jim didn’t know what to make of him. “Sean Cylear?” He spoke as if he had never heard the name before, “Oh yeah…” He had no clue. “…Say, why don’t you call the office later on and schedule an appointment. I really have to run.” Jim quickly left through the nearest exit, not wanting to stay and cover up his blatant lie. At that moment, Cylear felt empty. His first assumptions were correct. Jim didn’t know nor did he care who he was or what he did to get his spot here. He was just another name, and until he was making him boat loads of money… He would stay as just a name. Sean turned and remembered why he had ventured down this way in the first place- to go and meet the others. Though all motivation to do so was no longer there. Sean stared down at the piece of paper in his hand and made a fist, crushing it in the palm of his hand, dropping it in a nearby garbage can. “Fuck this, I’m outta here.”
