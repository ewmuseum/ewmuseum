Baked Not Fried (Part 1 of 2)

Date: 03/23/02 Title: Baked Not Fried (Part One) Date: 03/23/02 7:00 pm Place: McDonalds OPEN Quite a nice night isn’t it? The wind whistles throughout the small town as very few cars pass by making little to no noise. Somewhat moist air, and soggy grounds as the little red Geo of Christopher Fox rumbles up the entrance way to McDonalds. A large fast food chain. Which tonight business was slow, believe it or not. And as Fox pulls into a parking spot, some of the water on the ground swishes and splashes upward. Chris looks back at his Mother, who is tucked up in a fluffy patterned blanket, asleep, and then checks on Pikachu, soon remembering he had left him in the hotel after all. Ugh. Where’s Joey? He said he’d be here. Fox ponders and scans his head around. For those that don’t know who this Joey is, he’s a high school friend of Mommy Fox. After a good wait, some smoke appears and then slowly fades, as the employee door opens up with a creek. A tall, slim, white bearded man starts to walk toward the Geo. Click, clock. Each footstep gets a bit louder than the last. Fox looks around, as he almost scares himself because Mommy had just turned herself to face a different direction in her sleep. Huh.... that’s Joey! Oh yeah! Hey, I should probably wake Mommy up. As the fog becomes clearer, a hat can be seen on the approaching man. The symbol on it reads a big “A”. Obviously standing for the name Action Wrestling..... or not. The man opens the Geo door, sitting down while taking a breath. It had looked as if the man tried to shave a short while ago, but failed because some spots on his chin still stood out. Man “Hey there, I’m Joey. I reckon that’s Buttercup.” As Joey speaks, he turns, pointing to Mommy Fox who was just opening her eyes. Fox “Wrecking? I am not going to allow Mommy to get hurt!” Joey takes a breath. He coughs hard then putting his hand on Chris’ right knee. Joey “Oh you kids’. I meant to say, I believe that’s buttercup in the back seat. Better known as Mommy.” Hmmmm. Is he coming onto me? Cause I ’m getting a woody.... Fox wipes his nose with his hand and looks out the window. What the hell am I talking about!? I like the pie, you know that! So.... I guess I should talk to him. Fox “Yea, umm, this was a bit out of our way to drive. I am sssooo hungry. Do you have anything good?” Joey takes his hand off Fox’s knee, opens the door and casually replies while getting out. Joey “Of course, come on in bud.” FADE OUT
