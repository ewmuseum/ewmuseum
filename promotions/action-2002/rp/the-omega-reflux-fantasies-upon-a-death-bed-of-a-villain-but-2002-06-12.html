The Omega: Reflux Fantasies Upon A Death Bed Of A Villain…But I Am Near Death…So Leave Me Alone(2)

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The light was so bright in my eyes. It hurt to look up. When I closed my eyes, the whole world froze. I was so cold, but I was warm. It was a weird feeling. It was extremely powerful. Like the first time you get that romantic feeling from a girl. It makes your stomach feel tingly. It was something so powerful that words could never describe it. Then, I woke up. Or maybe I didn’t. I do not know. It was all so surreal. I cut my wrists and my chest and collapsed. Nothing fancy about it. It wasn’t even the first plan I had. It was a last ditch effort. Keri saved me. Right when I was bring the gun into my mouth, she got enough force into her small, petite body to send that weapon flying out of my reach. When I was cuffed and taken up the ramp way, he was scared. I was starting to surface again, and he wanted to put an end to both of us. All my manifested hatred over the years, all the unknown beliefs and life stories, it was all purged from my mind in one decisive cut. Actually three. I felt the searing pain the entire way though. I was enough to make me vomit on the mat, which I did. Angel made the mind scream. That was his dying action. A simple scream. How fucking pathetic. He left the party before I could bash his brains into oblivion. Wait, we share the same brain. Wait, shared. When I looked up and saw the horror on their faces, I tried to cry and say I was sorry for this. It wasn’t my doing. It wasn’t Joey’s. It wasn’t Keri’s. It was the Angel’s. Am I hiding behind some excuse? If you think so, then fine. Not like it matters. I am in intensive care, having blood pumped into my decaying prison that you would call a body. For some reason, I heard a whisper. “Keep fighting.” Why? And walk back into Hell with open arms for my trial? Nothing sounded better. I think that’s when my heart started beating again. I think.
