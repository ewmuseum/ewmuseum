Boringest. Birthday. Ever.

10 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was the twenty-third day of the eighth month of the third year in the 21st Century. Marcus Alexander Dox was, at 12:01am on that day, 26 years old. He wasn't awake at that time, though. No, he was sound asleep with his arms wrapped about his beautiful wife, Jessica. The two had had a busy week when it came to special occassions. Jessica had her twenty-fourth birthday on the nineteenth, and the two of them celebrated their third year of marriage on the twentieth. So, money was spent, and good times were had (mostly). When Xander had awaken on that beautiful Saturday morning, he was greeted by the tender kiss of his wife. Xander then checked his breath... 'Um, hun, why do I not have morning breath.' She shrugged, 'I didn't notice. That's weird.' Of course, she wouldn't tell her husband that she had brushed them for him. Yes, very strange. But she wasn't going to kiss him like that with nasty morning breath. Ew. On Jessica's birthday, she had been given breakfast in bed. Xander recieved the same. Apple Jacks right in the box. Marcus just smiled at the entire ordeal. Now that was funny. Jessica made it a point to keep Xander in the bedroom as long as posible. She had to do a little 'convincing', if you know what I mean, a couple of times. Eventually, around noon-ish, the two emerged from the bedroom. Xander was blindfolded (again, rar!) and lead down the hallway. Why the blindfold? Well, there was the whole open hallway that looked down upon the living room. Anyway, they made their way down the spiral staircase. And into the living room they went, with Jessica holding her husband's hands. She stopped him in front of the television, and then slowly removed the blindfold. He looked about the room. He had guests. Brad Robinson. 'Happy birthday, Marc!' he said, lifting his Mountain Dew in greeting, or whatever. Conrad Ramsey... in a little birthday hat. His arms were crossed and he had a scowl on his face. 'Grrrrrrrr!' Jessica held onto her husband's shoulders, as he attempted not to fall on the floor laughing at the old man. Jessica tip-toed and then kissed her husband on the cheek, 'Happy birthday, baby.' But he couldn't hold it any longer. He doubled over, holding his sides, as he laughed out loud. His bellows echoed throughout the spacious living room. Jessica started to laugh as well, and had soon buried her face in her husband's back. Conrad shook his head in disgust and looked over at Robinson, 'Oh, I suppose you'll be laughing too, then?' Brad just took a swig of his Mountain Dew and shook his head, 'No, I'm good.' ...... Robinson, a renowned barbequer, had decided to fire up the grill out back and have a good ole fashioned summer B-B-Q. Jessica and Xander weren't big on the eating of BBQ, hell... they rarely ate meat. No, they weren't one of those people, but pretty damn close it seemed. Anyway, out on deck, Brad had brought along his chef's hat and apron. It didn't have any sort of witty quote on it or anything. It was just a simple white apron. He was flipping the burgers and other assorted meats on the table, as Jessica was preparing the salad on the inside. Conrad and Xander sat at the table. 'So, twenty-six... how's it feel?' asked Ramsey. Xander shrugged, 'Feels like twenty-six, I guess. I've never been big on the birthdays. Just another day, ya know?' Conrad nodded, 'Yeah. When you get to be as old as I am, well, it is just any other day, because you've long since forgotten when your birthday actually is.' He laughed. Xander laughed as well. Also, because he still wore the birthday hat. Why Jessica had made him wear it was beyond him. But he did, and really wasn't noticing it much now. ...... Yup, that was all of that scene. After the eating (know one wants to see a bunch of people eating, it's just disgusting), it was time to... OPEN THE PRESENTS!!!!!! The table was cleared, Robinson had untucked his shirt, unbutton and unzipped his pants. Yes, he had eaten that much. Xander patted him on the stomach, 'You keep it up, Brad, you're going to need to get a cart to push in front of you just to hold that up.' Robinson grabbed his belly and shook it, 'Yeah, but I kind of like it. All round and.... round. Plus, the chicks love it.' He winked, and then caught Jessica's eye. Probably wasn't wise to use the c-word around her. Robinson sheepishly smiled. 'Alright,' she then said. 'I suppose it's time for the gifts.' Xander slapped his hands together and rubbed them, 'Let's do this!' No one moved. Xander looked around. 'Well?' 'Oh, right, you didn't get anything,' Jessica said with a smile. The other nodded. Xander's jaw dropped. Of course, he would go on to be surprised by the generous gift giving of his friends. From Brad Robinson, he got a case of Sunkist, Futurama on DVD, and that was it. There really wasn't much to get the guy outside of DVDs. Conrad Ramsey had gotten him something he told him not to open until later. Until he was alone, or when he felt he should. Xander agreed to not do so until that time. Jessica's gifts were grand. All it was was a kiss and her agreeance to watch Buffy until they fell asleep. And that's what they did. All four of them. They sat there and watched hour upon hour of Buffy the Vampire Slayer on DVD. Conrad had fallen asleep in the chair, with the birthday hat still on. Xander, of course, had to snap a picture of the old man sleeping with his mouth wide open and drool coming out. Just in case he needed to blackmail him for something in the future. Jessica had gone next, sleeping with her head on her husband's chest, on the couch. In the other chair, Robinson had dropped. And Xander just kept watching and watching and watching. (There were other gifts from Jessica, some he had already gotten [wink] and some that were more private. So don't think Xander got nothing.) Eventually, though, he wanted a break. And he got up and walked out onto the deck. ...... Xander ordinarily didn't care much for birthdays. But actually having a loving wife to share it with, plus two close friends. Well, it was nice. But it had dawned on him that it was the first birthday without his mother. That was the only thing he looked forward to on the 23rd of August every year. Spending time with his mother. He sat there, just looking out at the horizon. The dark hills and the moon in the distance. And within a minute, he would be asleep.
