Who Is Jamal Wilson? -4

27 September 2002

Date: 09/27/02 It was the next day, I was still bored with University. My dorm roommates had given me my work assignments for the day that I missed. I did them quickly and had some free time on my hands. I didn't want to go to school for a second day in a row, and I didn't. I stayed home and watched daytime talk shows and soaps operas. After a few minutes of channel surfing I found myself watching a large man in tight tights walking down a ramp with a sadistic looking grin on his face. I'll admit it; he was one good-looking man. Definitely my type of man. Tall, rugged, and he looked like he was an animal in the sack.... WAIT! WAIT A FREAKIN' MINUTE!! WHY THE HELL AM I TALKING ABOUT ANOTHER MAN LIKE THAT?! This was queer, no pun intended, I've never looked at another man in that sort of manner, in my opinion, it always had been wrong. I knew for a fact that I didn't like women. They just... UGH! They were icky and I didn't want to deal with them. But this was crossing into the realm of homosexuality here, and I don't like to label myself. My entire life I've considered myself straight, a straight male. Up until this point, females had always been my sexual preference had always been female. But if I was straight, why was I liking looking at this guy? As these thoughts were streaming through my brain, I saw the commercial. 'Become a Professional Wrestler Today, Only...' after seeing 'become a professional wrestler today' I tuned out. I could become one of them... a big, well toned, in shape, good-looking man who gets to wrestle for a living. It was too easy not to try. *** I didn't know what I was getting into when I stepped into the hot, musty gym that day. I had my money in an envelop in my coat pocket. It would cost me $2000, but it was worth it. I would find out if I loved this 'sport', if you could call it that. As I walked into the front office, I saw a familiar face, Ricky Morton. I hadn't been the most avid of wrestling fans since my childhood, but I knew enough to remember him from the old NWA and the new WCW. As I walked into the office and took in the aroma of sweaty men battling it out in the ring, I realized two things. One: I'm in for a lot of pain. And two: I'm feminine... more so then other men.
