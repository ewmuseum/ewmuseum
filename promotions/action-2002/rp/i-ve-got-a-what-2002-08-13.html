I've Got A... What!?

13 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/13/02 Leitchfield, KY | 16:07PM CST CONRAD RAMSEY had stepped out of his white 1990 Chevrolet Lumina. He shut the door and looked over at the silver 2002 Toyota Celica in front of him. He walked up to it and wondered who it belonged to. Ramsey cautiously walked up to his front door and found the house key on his key ring. He grabbed ahold of the knob and was about to insert the key when he noticed that the knob was loose. He slowly opened it. CONRAD RAMSEY (To himself.) I don't need this aggravation today. Ramsey slowly entered the foyer and could hear the television on in the living room. Ramsey, with his new cane(which was made of plain old wood), began to slowly make his way towards the living room. Sitting there he saw MAX DANGER, and he was not pleased. He lifted up his cane, prepared to swing it. Max looked up and saw him. MAX DANGER Hey, Ramsey! Good to see you! Ramsey kept the cane up, ready to strike if he needed to. RAMSEY What are you doing here? DANGER I live here, remember? (Just noticing the cane.) Oh, come now, put that down. There's not going to be any need for violence. Conrad slowly put the cane down and walked around to where he was standing in front of Max, blocking the television. DANGER Um... could you move? I'm trying to watch the Powerpuff Girls. That Bubbles is so cute! Ramsey slowly turned to see Bubbles, Blossom, and Buttercup beating up on Mojo Jojo. He turned back to Max. RAMSEY How old are you? This is a kid's show. No, a little girl's show! Max jumped up at once, getting in Ramsey's face. DANGER I'm going to have to pretend you didn't say that. Max just smiled and patted Ramsey on the head, for some strange reason. He then sat back down. DANGER So, where have you been? RAMSEY What's it matter? I don't have to answer to you. DANGER That's true, but I was worried you may have gotten lost. I mean you are like 90 year's old, there's no way to be sure you would remember where you lived. Ramsey didn't seem to like that comment at all. He gripped his cane tighter, then noticed that Max had his had on Ramsey's old platinum cane. Ramsey slowly released the grip on his. Ramsey's attention then turned to a noise down the hall. It was water running. RAMSEY That your wife? DANGER No. That's someone who has been waiting here for you. Ramsey cocked his head and wasn't wondering who could be wanting to see him. The bathroom door then came open, and down the hall came a young man, about the age of Max, probably a few years older. He looked exactly like a younger version of Conrad Ramsey, only a few inches taller and with a full head of hair. THE MAN Dad!? The man's face was beaming as he held out his arms and embraced Conrad. Conrad didn't know what was going on as Max got up from the couch and stood beside the two guys hugging. DANGER Well, I leave you and your son alone to catch up. Danger slapped Ramsey on the back and strolled off with a huge smile on his face. Ramsey and his 'son' stopped their embrace. Ramsey looked at the boy with confusion. RAMSEY S-S-Son!? The boy stood there smiling and nodding his head approvingly. Ramsey was in shock as he just stared at his supposed son. FADE OUT
