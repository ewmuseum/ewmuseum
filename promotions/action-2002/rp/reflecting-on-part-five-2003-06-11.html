Reflecting On: Part Five.

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.11.2003 Xander and Jessica had just went through the security check. They weren't stopped for anything metallic or any sorts of weapons in their bags. They shoes were back on and they were off to the terminal. They only had a couple of minutes. The two were holding hands, but not really so. Xander eyes were darting everywhere, making sure he was going in the right direction. Southwest Airlines was the flight. The only one that had a plane heading to Las Vegas at that moment. The plane was boarding. Unfortunately, they had decided to carry their bags on. Though, faster in getting through the lines, slowed them down as they ran. Jessica still desperately wanted to know what was going on. But she couldn't ask. Not now. Had to wait. On the plane maybe? She didn't know. She really didn't want to open up a whole can of worms right there in the middle of the flight. But she needed to know why her husband had gone cold and distant. Why he was looking like a ghost. Wasn't going to be easy, but was going to have to be done. The terminal had been reached. They were a 'C' ticket, but they had been last onto the plane, so it didn't matter. They quickly had their tickets ripped and they rushed down the tunnel. They entered the plane. Jessica's hair was all over the place. She was breathing heavily. Xander was not. He was relatively calm it seemed. Mind on other things, obviously. They looked through the plane. It was full. Only two empty seats remained. One in the middle and one at the back of the plane. The one in the middle sat a large man and woman. In the back, college-aged students. Probably were college students, heading to Vegas for a fun week. Xander looked back at his wife. It was a ghostly look he gave her. She just put her hands on his face and whipsered, 'I'll sit with the couple. You head to the back. I'll come back and talk to you when we're up in the air.' He only nodded. A kiss was had. Xander stopped briefly to put Jessica's bag into the overhead compartment, and then headed into the back. Next to a muscle-toned kid and his girlfriend. Xander put his bag overhead and sat down without saying a word. As he sat there, with his head down, Jessica was watching, from her seat. Sorrow in her eyes.
