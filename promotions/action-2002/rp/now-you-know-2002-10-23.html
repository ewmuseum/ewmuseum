Action! — Tales

Now you know.

23 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/23/2002 So now you know who I am. Happy? I doubt it. Even if you did care, the fact that I’m Nuke does nothing but raise more questions. Why am I in Action!? Why the name change? What are my intentions? What happens to the IWO World Title? Those are some of the questions that may arise…and like all others will go unanswered. I know the answers. And I’m all that matters. Deal with it. I’m narcissistic, greedy, impulsive, ruthless, and deeply depraved. Get used to not understanding what I say or do, not liking what I say or do, and not knowing what I’ll say or do next. I’m here to annoy you. Irritate you. Confuse you to the point of hysteria. I’m the itch, that’s just out of scratching reach. I’m stalled car on the highway that keeps you in traffic an extra 2 hours. I’m the condom that perfectly times its rupture with your girlfriend’s ovulation. I’m the extra 15% you pay for taxes. I’m the turd in the toilet that just won’t flush. I’m everything in you life that makes suicide look more appealing every day that goes by. Know me. Hate me. And I’ll make you love me. I punched Noah Hawkins out of necessity. It’s a known fact that wrestlers involved in feuds are more marketable. I want more money. Therefore, I need a feud. Hence, I knocked Noah the fuck out. Simple as that. I don’t have the time, energy, or attention span needed to initiate and continue an authentic rivalry. “That seems to defeat the whole purpose of even having a feud” you’d comment. That’s just pointless violence against an unsuspecting innocent victim, you’d say. “You’re no better, than the fucking serial sniper!” you might add. At which point, I’d thank you for the compliment, and be on my way. I hope by now, if you know anything about me, it’s this: I. Don’t. Care.
