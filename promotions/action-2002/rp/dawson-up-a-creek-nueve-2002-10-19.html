Dawson: Up A Creek: Nueve

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was almost time for the A! Title Match, and Aroma still hadn’t found the Challenger for tonight’s big Match. It wasn’t everyday that a female got a shot at a Main Title. Sure there was Nerva, and the girls in Old School Wrestling, but from what was going around at ‘Wrestling Planet’, this was the first time that a female was getting a shot at THE Main Title. Well, that’s what Reed Young posted anyway, as millions of marks, as well as other Fed Owners disputed in the post. But every time they refuted something that Reed wrote in the topic, Reed would just come with something else, trying to convince the wrestling and fighting world, that this was indeed the first time. Needless to say, Sweet Aroma posted her own reply, letting Reed know that it’s the first time in Action! and not the first time in the wrestling/ fighting world that a female was getting a shot at the Federation’s top Title. All this mixed with her thoughts on the tonight’s Challenger, really played with the mind of Roni. Until she came face to face with the Challenger. Angelica Dawson. The Crowd gave a mixed reaction when they saw her on the screen. “Hey V.” Dawson spoke first. A confused Aroma just looked on. “I just wanted to apologize for leaving you a few weeks ago. I thought that if I got Bobby away...” “That’s enough Angelica; I know why you did it.” The two stood in silence. Aroma extended her hand for a handshake. Dawson accepted it. The two then embraced. Aroma took this moment to whisper something in Dawson’s ear. “Nolite te bastardes carborundorum.” The Crowd only had one response to what they overheard. “What?” Exactly.
