Action! — Tales

Simultaneous individuality 1 - Tyler

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th October 2002, 15:54 Airborn kicked the duvet of his bed into the air, before sitting up suddenly, a cold sweat spread across his face. He'd just had another nightmare. In his 'Barney & Friends' pyjamas, Airborn reached up to the chest of drawers next to his bed and took the glass of water. Breathing heavily, Airborn gulped down the liquid, although it wasn't cold any more. It had been when he'd poured it from the tap seven hours previously. Laying back down down, Airborn pulled the duvet back over his cold body and shivered, snuggling to his matress and pillow as much as humanly possible. No doubt he'd go back to sleep in a moment, but when he did, he prayed for the love of what is all democratic that he wouldn't have that same nightmare again. Four time in two days he'd had that nightmare. The visions of holding the Action! Championship high above his head to rapturous applause and multiple blinding camera flashes. Then the visions of Jonny Yankee sneaking up behind him and knocking him out cold with three subsequent crowbar swings to the back of the head. That solid, sadistic metal being cracked over his skull three times, the thud of brains cells being killed (although some might say that last consequence was irrelevant for Airborn). And now he had a concussion, and to make things worse, the flu. He'd have to miss Pressure Point on Monday, because there was no way he'd make such a significant recovery that he'd be fit to continue. One minute he'd been fighting Joey Malone in the match of his life, the next he was laid up in bed during the middle of a Saturday afternoon. The contrast couldn't have been anymore stark. The rain pattered gently against the window pane, and Airborn opened his eyes again. His own loss hadn't been the only casualty suffered by the A-Star Stable last Monday. His brothers The Elements had lost to Neko Case and Jonny Yankee in a cage match. Even with his own interference they didn't get the job done. Throughout the week Lee had been arguing that David should have broken up the count, and without actually saying it David's body language indicated he thought Lee's being pinned was an amateur mistake that should never have happened. Airborn didn't care whose fault it was. They'd failed to get the job done. He'd brought them in to back him up, but if they kept losing key matches like the afore mentioned then they weren't much use to him. As Airborn swallowed some flu tablets and closed his eyes, he prayed that he wouldn't have the same torturous nightmare again, and that The Elements would be able to turn things around Monday at Pressure Point.
