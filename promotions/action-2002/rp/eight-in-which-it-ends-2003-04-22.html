Eight, In which it Ends.

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“SOMEBODY, QUICK!” “Call an ambulance!” “Is she dead?” “She OD’d!” “Call 911!” The calls were random, no one would do anything. Donell stared in horror as his sister had lain lifeless in his arms. He sat on the couch dormant, not blinking, as she draped across his hoisted arms. Donell looked down on her and saw one thing that he had never seen before. Daisy’s eyes were soulless. There was no love, no compassion, and no happiness. The sister he once had was gone, but this was something new to him. Daisy was dead for over a year, Donell had only just realized it. A few moments later, the red-lights tinted the whole room as Paramedics arrived in the room with a series of cases and a stretcher. Everyone prayed that one wouldn’t be used. Two of the larger men pulled Daisy from her brother and set her on the ground in front of him. Most of the kids had already cleared out from the party to avoid any conflict with authorities. Donell watched silently as the EMTs checked her pulse, while another pointed a flashlight in to her eyes. The beam revealed her pupils to be dilated, while an EMT warned the others that there were no vital signs. In most situations of overdossage of a prescription drug, they would pump the stomach. But now, her heart has stopped completely. The EMTs tried CPR, but to no avail. With the announcement of the passing, many witnesses tried to flee from the scene, but were stopped by the approaching red & blue lights that flashed systematically. Many police officers blocked the scene from spectating neighbors, looking to catch a glimpse of the tragedy. Others began rounding up witnesses, trying to get a statement from them to get an idea of what happened. There was little success, as they received too many different stories from addicts or kids too afraid to tell the truth. Meanwhile, inside, Donell sat stunned as an EMT approached his sister with a body-bag. His eyes never left hers, until they began to place her in the black-tomb. “What’re you doing?” The EMTs looked at the confused man with question. They didn’t want to disturb him in his current state, but they had to do their job. The men went back to loading the body, but became sidetracked as Donell approached with great speed. Before he could come in contact with the paramedics, a Police Officer stepped in and forced Donell outside. On the path to the driveway, Donell screamed for his sister, calling for her to come back and that everything would be ok. It had sunk in, but he was trying to fight the tears with bargains and promises of happiness. As people began to give Donell some room, where he was swinging wild punches on the lawn, he slowly collapsed in the grass and began to cry. Before anyone could console him, a green Jetta pulled up and the passengers within burst through the yellow tape that now lined the scene. Julius Arcade looked down at his son, then in to the house to where he would see what was going on. Tyrone slowly followed after, but fell to the ground next to his nephew. He clutched him in his arms and kept the embrace to provide what little comfort he could. Minutes passed, but finally, a tearful Julius stepped outside, using the structure of the house for support. Tyrone looked up and saw his brother, crying uncontrollably, and approached him. The two hugged, while Donell slowly tried to get to his feet. From over his brother’s shoulder, Julius opened his eyes and looked at his son, who looked back. No words were exchanged, but Donell could see the hate in his father’s eyes. The sheer detestment of his existence sunk in deep. Donell looked away from his father, ashamed, and he couldn’t cry anymore. Donell turned back to the house, where EMT’s were now bringing out the stretcher with the black bag securely on top of it. As Daisy was slowly loaded on to the ambulance, Donell’s will to live disappeared. Julius had a big problem to fix, but did he want to?
