The Tar Mat, The Final Part of

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was long after hours. Pressure Point was finished, and ‘The Law’ got his own payback after beating down the decayed body of his former enemy, ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear. He was objected to. But the worst thing, the thing that sat up-side-down in LLB’s stomach... was the idea of *who* entered his Courtroom after. Scott Cylear. The dubbed Brother of Sean. He madly stroked the hammer that stood in his hands as he walked from hall to hall, room to room, in search of the new one. In search of the bonded last name, which tied Scott to the guilty and sinful prick. LLB walked slowly, his sneakers not making the faintest of sounds against the hard, still floor. He knew he’d find him sooner or later. For Reed Young was questioning him in the back. Turning another corner, LLB came upon what he was looking for. An office door. An office door open... with someone’s left leg sticking out from behind. He marched forward, pulling the hammer from his fists and smiling evilly. Voices could now be heard. “So sign me, dammit!” He said, then to be seen raising his hands in the air, ranting on like a fool. “I want a shot at that asshole.” The blonde continued to talk, as the figure behind him really didn’t seem interested. He just leaned back in his chair, beginning to fall asleep. “Fine! You want to be like that?” The long blonde shouted. “I’ll get him right now.” He turned around, and walked right out the door. Right into... The hammer. WHACK! Blood shot all over the walls and cement, as the now seen Scott Cylear laid out cold on the floor, red liquid rushing from his mouth, as well as his own teeth. “Too bad you’re not like your Brother.” LLB said, pulling Cylear up and eyeing him directly. “Now get out of my Courtroom.” And hammering Scott in the face once more, LLB chucked him out of the exit, leaving massive piles of blood over the grounds. LLB moved back around, about to go to his locker room. He was stopped. Reed Young leaned against the side of the wall to his office door. “Shit Roland, shit. Now I gotta clean this up! And I sent the Janitor’s home early!!” The two kept their eye lock, as both broke into a smile. “What am I saying?” Young exclaimed, shrugging. “I’ll just leave it for the next show to wash.”
