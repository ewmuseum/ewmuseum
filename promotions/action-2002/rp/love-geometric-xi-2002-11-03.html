Love Geometric - XI

3 November 2002

Date: 11/03/2002 October 28th, 2002 Monday Night Coral Avalon was at a loss of words. Which wasn't a big achievement for him, since he usually stutters and has difficulty finding anything to say, but this time he couldn't even begin to stutter. He had been watching the Halloween Summer Camp Match with Allison, so of course, has was quite stunned when Keri Lindum stuck Joey Malone with a steel chair. Twice. In the head. Then the passionate kiss with Kent Anthason. Coral Avalon, perhaps one of the closest friends of Joey Malone, turned to his girlfriend, Allison Lindum and spoke the first three words that came to his mind. 'Wh... what the HELL!?' 'I...' Allison tried to start. She normally had something to say for any situation, but this was a situation she had no words for, and it was folly for her to even try. Avalon, meanwhile, had a far more creative and justifiable way to get his point across. He toppled over a nearby table and screamed. 'Arrrgghhhh!!!' Of course, Allison jumped up, as she wasn't expecting Coral to even do that. She lunged for him and grabbed him by the arm to try calm him down. Almost upon physical contact, Coral dropped to his knees and sniffled. Allison knelt down next to him and felt a trickle of teardrops ruining her makeup as well. 'How could she...' Coral said. He repeated himself a moment later, finding no way to actually complete the sentence he was striving for. Instead, he sobbed into Allison's black shirt, and accidently knocked her red-haired wig from her head. Finding nothing to control her own emotions, Allison cried into Coral's shoulder. And there, they sat. Holding each other. Crying. Makes you wonder if Jesus's family cried like this when Judas betrayed him.
