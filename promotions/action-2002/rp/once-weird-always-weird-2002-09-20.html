Action! — Tales

Once weird, always weird

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2nd September 2002 Many, many years ago…or 8 A 10 year old Benjamin ‘Airborn’ Tyler sat on the floor of a living room. The living room was inside a small flat, which was housed in a 30 storey high tower building. Playing with his cha-cha train was such fun. There were so many things you could do with it. Ben grabbed the top of his plastic train, and tossed it along the ground as fast as he could, allowing it to smash into the wall. As he did so, he grabbed a handful of plastic figures, and smashed them into the ground as fast as he could. “HAHA! The train crashed, the replica people are dying! HAHA!” Ben skipped out into the kitchen and clambered his way onto the work surface. Looking all around him like he was trying to hide something, he slung the cupboard door recklessly, and began tossing random objects over his shoulder in his search for one particular toy. “Daddy’s medicine in a glass bottle that he needs or he’ll die? NOPE!” Smash. “Mummy’s peculiar vibrating pointy thing? NOPE!” Bang. “Best friend’s white powder that he calls ‘crack’ and I know nothing about? NOPE!” Thud. “Big shiny metal heffer that I found in the desolate garages that omits a flickering orange light that looks queerly like fire? YEP!” Benjamin held tightly onto the lighter as he crossed his toes and fell daringly off the work surface. What a big distance it was to fall! There was no way he would do anything involving flying when he was older! Happy again, Ben ran into the living room, stomped once on the ‘train’ wreckage, and organised all the ‘victims’ of the ‘crash’ in one pile. With a sadistic look that KroW would be proud of, 10 year old Benjamin Tyler flicked back the lid, pressed the button, and set the plastic figures on fire. What a horrible child! Ben stood up, looked up to the ceiling and raised his hands slowly to the heavens, before laughing evilly. It is rumoured that at that precise moment the whole world clouded over, and rampant strikes of lightning hurtled to the ground. As Ben’s nose sucked in the flame induced smell of burning plastic, two figures entered the room and stood in the door frame, unbeknownst to the future ‘A Star Student’. They were Ben’s cousin. The first one was Lee Tyler, aged 15. His frame was slender, and looked almost identical to Ben, except his face had an extra added edge. His natural look was conceited, but his blonde hair was the same, his build inseparable. The second boy was David Tyler, aged 17. His frame was totally the opposite: muscular. One could have been forgiven for thinking that he was a body builder. His face didn’t have any hint of a smile, just seriousness and aggression. His hair was a slightly darker shade of blonde, bordering on brown. “I cannot believe we’re related to him,” stated David in a mind frame of disbelief. Next to him, Lee smirked, crossing his arms in a way that stated he thought he was better than everyone else. “What’s he going to be, a pyromaniac? Pah, Benjamin will never amount to anything. I seem to remember hearing that everyone has their 15 minutes of fame, but Ben? He’s got about as much chance of fame as I have of being a 500lber.” Benjamin’s cousins; who were brothers themselves; walked out the room, returning to the spare room they were staying in for the weekend.
