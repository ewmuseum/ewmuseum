Action! — Tales

It will fall.

9 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jeff reached into his locker and pulled out one of the many W101 shirts he had stashed away. He used it to wipe the sweat from his face, exhaling loudly as he threw it in and locked the door. It was mid-week. He found himself growing restless each day. Rarely he saw his wife, which prompted him to bring her back on the road with him. He could only wonder how long it would be until her incessant nagging would have him filing for divorce papers. It was funny (or wasn’t, as it was a true mind-fuck to deal with); he couldn't concentrate with her at home, he missed her far too much. And he couldn't concentrate with her at his side- because she tends to be somewhat of a bitch, especially when she has to sit still for long periods of time. Traveling was not for her. Pressure Point's location was still a mystery to him, and he was beginning to believe that maybe it would be best if instead of sitting in his dressing room during the shows, helping KSZ torment Chambers, if he went and spoke with the road agents. But the road agents seem to forget an intricate part of personal hygiene- bathing. Jeff groaned at the thought of their sweat stains and never-ending ‘morning’ breath, walking out of the public dressing room and over to a set of weights. He had spoken with Keith earlier on the phone after he arrived back in Memphis. Apparently, there was a certain rule that could benefit his group of up-and-comers. The rule stated the holder of the Action! Championship must defend his title ATLEAST once every thirty days, or be stripped of it. And if memory served him correctly, that blow-hard LLB was last seen at Jugga' NUMERO DEUCE on May 25th. It was now July 4th, exceeding the 30-day defense guideline. Garvin smiled. He heard the cracks beginning to form. And soon the ceiling would come crashing down.
