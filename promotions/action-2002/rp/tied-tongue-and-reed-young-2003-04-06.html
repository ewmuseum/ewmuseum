Tied Tongue and Reed Young

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Great Awakening and Wendal slowly walked towards Reed Young, who stood a great distance from them in the office. As they confidently approached, Quinton slightly stumbled. His facial expresion turned to embarassment at such clumsiness. However, Young didn't seem to mind. They each shook hands, firm and strong. Reed held out his palms, a signal for them to have a seat. All three sat down, and Reed folded his hands on his desktop. He took one look at Quinton's beastly appearance, and smiled. He looked at Wendal, and frowned with confusion. Quinton read Reed's face, and swiftly explained: 'He's not my agent, or my valet. He's my manager and advisor.' 'Interesting...' Reed Young responded sarcastically. He quickly changed subject: 'I can see you're a beast, kid, an absolute beast. I've read up on you and it seems that you have some impressed references. All claim you are an amazingly talented wrestler, but I'm not so sure that you're a star.' Quinton gave him an absent glare as Reed spoke. Reed looked up and saw this face, and chuckled loudly. 'Are you bored, Mr. Grimes?' Wendal sharply turned his head and grimaced at Quinton's conduct. He knew 'the plan' they had devised would never work...Quinton was worthless. Quinton bit his lip and made a statement that made Wendal wince: 'Actually, I kinda am.' Reed was taken aback, and Wendal's jaw dropped in shock. 'Heh,' Reed snorted, 'You're not exactly speaking my language here, Quinton.' Quinton, very confused, looked for Wendal's assistance. Wendal shook his head and looked away. 'Obviously you are physically fit and right for the job, but are you dynamic? Are you passionate? Creative?' 'See?' Wendal whined as he looked at Grimes. He rubbed his wrinkled forehead and interrupted Quinton's response. 'Just Stop.' Wendal said with as he snapped his wrist in the air at Quinton. He switched his vision to Reed and led out a snort. Quinton shrunk in his chair 'What's with the twenty questions?' 'This is an interview,' Reed explained, his face contorted with the foolish question, 'This is just procedure---' 'Give me a break,' Wendal bitterly shot back. 'What do you want me to do?!?!' Reed barked, very angry. 'Jesus Christ, Reed. Just give him a chance before he messes this up even more.' Reed Young prepared to rant, but stopped in mid breath. He leaned back in his chair and looked at the beads of sweat flow down Wendal's forehead. It was clear to him that Wendal was nervous and intimidated, but he was passionate enough to fight for his friend. Mr. Young closed his eyes for a few moments, and opened them back up. 'Ok, here's the deal. I know it's sudden, but I'm going to place you in a dark match at Pressure Point. You will wrestle a mystery opponent, who is a prospect I'm fond of. If you get a win over him, you're as good as in. Ok?' Quinton and Wendal shared bewildered glances. They had fumbled around and nearly ruined the interview. But somehow, some way, Reed Young saw a small spark of potential in 'The Great Awakening'. Quinton looked back at Reed, and accepted his proposal.
