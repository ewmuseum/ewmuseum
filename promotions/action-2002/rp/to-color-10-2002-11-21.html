Action! — Tales

To Color. 10

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As he took his steps forward, he could sense the chill run down his spine. Mark walked to the door, pushed it back... and out he went. The camera flashes blinded him, as the media followed his every move down the backstage hall. They attempted to speak to Mark Paulton... but he was in his own world now. It was already too late. He turned a corner, as the media stayed closely behind him. They were almost as fast as he was... but that wasn’t a surprise. Anything for the press... anything to annoy the hell out of you. Paulton turned another corner, as he came upon the entrance to the ring. A black curtain... that was all that separated him between the world... and the world. Jumping up and down as he threw some left and right jabs, Mark sucked in a deep breath of air, pushed it out... and gave one final stare into the curtain. He could hear the people... the near silence of them as they awaited to burst out in cheers as he came down. It was one world, connected to the other... and it was only a few steps away. He walked out. Even more flashes crossed his determined face, as over twenty thousand people stood up to cheer him. This was what it was like to be famous. Everyone on their feet, cheering. The near silence awaiting to burst out in deafening louds. Mark couldn’t help but grin. Sure... he wasn’t the father he wanted to be. He wasn’t the husband he wanted to be. He wasn’t even the person he wanted to be. But did any of them see that? Not a soul. They saw this. It was a world, as stated already. A world where nothing else mattered but what happened in the squared-circle. It didn’t matter if you were a jerk outside this. They didn’t see; they didn’t care. Mark Paulton looked at his hands as he walked down the ramp. This... this was going to be fun.
