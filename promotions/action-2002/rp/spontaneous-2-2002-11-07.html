Spontaneous [2]

Date: 11.07.02 Russa had been singing Hero the entire car ride and showed no signs of stopping now. He had been driving all day and was making his way into West Virginia when something on the side of the road. “Russa,” he said to himself, “you are such a great person. Always helping others out, that is the Russa way.” He smiled before slowing down and pulling over to the side. He rolled down his car window and stared at the man with his hand out, thumbing sticking out, it seems that he was looking for a ride. “Need a lift?” Russa asked. The man only nodded. “What’s that smell?” Was the first thing Russa said as soon as he started to drive again. He held his nose up high and started to sniff all around the car. He sniffed over the head of the hitchhiker and every single muscle in his face tightened up. He looked the hitchhiker over. He had greasy, shaggy, brown hair, a dirty white T-shirt, with a pair of old faded jeans that were ripped all over. “Do you have a name?” “People call me Renner.” Was his reply. Russa thought to himself where he had heard that name before. It sounded so familiar, like the stalker he had back when he was in the ukw. This man had followed him everywhere and Russa had found plans of this man using ninja’s and vampire’s to steal his Hardway title that everyone wanted to hold. This was not the same guy. “Guess who I am?” Russa asked another question. “Umm…” Renner stammered out. It looked like guessing games were not going to be his strongest point. “That guy, you know, the guy from the thing who does things?” There was only one look for a response like that. WTFMF! ‘Riiiiigggghhhttt…” Russa shook his head in disbelief. “Actually the name is Russa, I’m a wrestler for such places as ACW and Action! I was also the most credible champion in the history of the ukw. The Hardway Champion to be exact. They tried to put me in the most grueling matches that the world has ever seen but I knew as soon as Jakks threw me through that glass window, I was never ever going to do that stuff again. So you know what I did?” “Slow down,” Renner demanded Russa, “I didn’t understand a word you said.” “Well, I believe I was telling you a story about me. Russa is the name and wrestling is the game. ACW, A exclamation mark W, and ukw…” “ukw?” Renner replied nervously and started to stare out the window. “Yeah, the Hardway Champion ‘twas me.” “Will you look at that!” Renner’s smile and laugh was fake and he playfully punched Russa in the arm. “That’s my stop right over there. Thanks for the ride, buddy.” “No problem, chief, it’s the least I could do.” Russa pulled over in the middle of the street and there was absolutely nothing in sight. Russa watched as Renner stepped out of the car and saw one of those tumbleweed’s blow past. He looked out his other window and his jaw dropped.
