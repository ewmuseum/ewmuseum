The Good, the Bad, and the Bad News part 2: the Hunt

18 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Flashback, 07.18.02 The surroundings were Dry. Dusty. Minio stood outside of the Cracker Barrel Resteraunt. He glanced at his watch. 5:46pm. He had heard of a Fear sighting in Arizona, and he was looking to find fear at any cost. Fear was the direct cause of his loss the night before. Fear tried to kill him, not only once with a knife, but twice by running an abulence into a ditch. If revenge meant busting into a family establishment and beating a man to death, so be it. He pushed the door open, stepping inside, glancing around. Fear was long gone. He walked to the back corner, a waitress was wiping up some spills. The table had cracks on the outside end. Minio sat, looking at the waitress. 'What Happened?' She replied very routinely. 'Nutty customer. Big fella. He was screaming about A Bobby Minino or somethin'.' Minio leaned forward in his table. 'Really, what did he say?' She sat up and shrugged. 'He flipped out. Said the war wasn't over, it was just beginning or something.' Bobby nodded to her, left a five on the table and walked away. He knew Fear was around, and Fear was losing it. Bobby wanted that. Bobby wanted to push Fear over the edge. He had a dark smile on his face as he walked out of the entrance, towards his blue 68' Mustang. He leaned against the side of it in thought. He was trying to push Fear to the brink of insanity, if not there. Fear was so angry at the drawn out fight between the two, just as Minio had once been. It was time to feel the pain of the victims. Minio had a plan. A Shotty one but a plan none the less. A female voice came from the car. The sun was just over casted to where it shaded her face. It was the same dark haired tan skinned woman from the Hospital. 'He wasn't there?' Minio looked visibly bothered. 'I told you to stay out of my life, why are you disreguarding that and getting into my car?' She grinned in the shadow. 'I'm your guardian angel Bobby. I'm just watching over you, and i'm sticking around for the ride. What are you doing anyway Bobby? Finding Fear away from the ring, thats suicide, he'll kill you!' He nodded, looking away from her, towards the resteraunt. He repeated her. 'He'll Kill me.' He paused then went on. 'And i'll take him with me.' She was silent for a moment then uttered coldly and straight. 'You're on a death Mission.' He replied, just as deadly. 'Its for the better good of many people.' There was another long silence. He finally opened the door and sat down. Glancing to her. He put the keys in the ignition, started the car, and turned to her. 'Just don't get in his way. Or Mine. When these Worlds Collide, I don't even think the ref will walk away. Fear is a dangerous man. I'm a man with a mission. I'm a man with an outlook. I'm a man with a Moral. All Fear is, is someone running away from the past. Running away from the Future. Running away... From Me.' She Mocked him, almost not believing how non chalantly he could be story booking a fued based on wrestling. 'Running from Minio?' Minio pulled out of the parking lot, then looked her straight in the eye. 'Running from Minio.' The car sped off down the road, in the same direction Fear's had a mere two hours ago.
