Foreshadows pt3 - Not what I had expected

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/25/03 They stared at each other for what must have been two minutes. Their eyes told tales. Robert Hicks Sr. had a pale skinny face, you couldn't even see the resemblence. He looked sickly to say the least. The silence was a novel(I was reading the emotion on his face, It told me that he had a different idea on how this was going to happen.) Finally Robert spoke, his voice crackled as if he hadn't spoke all day, dry, pained. 'Come inside Bobby, lemme see if TV does add ten pounds.' His father had been watching him, a vouyer into a life he never went through with(who the hell is he to watch me fail at every dream I've ever had?!) Bobby followed inside, and he took a short glance around, it was tidy, to say the least, boring and mechanical, dull and dead. Bobby reconized the smell, a formiliar smell from his past years, one he wasn't comfortable with. The stench of postponing the inevitable(this place smells like a hospital.) He eyed his seating arrangements, a single hardwood chair near a small table by the kitchen nook, a recliner which was covered in more dust then a masolium and a rather shoddy sofa. It looked worn in, worked out(a couch even I wouldn't sit on.) Bobby grabbed the hardwood chair, sliding it towards his blood father who sat on the edge of the couch watching Bobby like a reality show. Bobby didn't like his eyes on him, they felt undeserved, a Dad staring at his son that he refused to even raise. Granted, he liked attention as much as the next person but he had a grudge with the other man in this room, a grudge he has held for two decades, it wasn't something that he could turn off. Father and Son's fight and bond, these two ignored each other's existence(I wish he never had me.) Bobby took a deep swallow into his throught, he had waited nearly twenty three years to ask this, even before he was capible of understanding a family structure the question had burned the back of his natural brain. 'Why did you leave?' Bobby blurted it out as if he had rehearsed it. It wasn't the first question Robert expected, but it was one he would have to answer. 'What?' Robert wasn't ready, he stuttered out a word to get a bearing. 'Why the hell did you leave?' Bobby was demanding(answer me you fuck!) 'Son...' Robert had slipped, and Bobby would not allow him to use terms reserved for family members. 'Bobby.' 'Bobby, you may not know this but, your Mother, Dee, is a great person, but she is subborn as all hell. We didn't get along. Matter of fact, you were born when your Ma's boyfriend at the time, Louie, cheated on her at a party. We hooked up, and ... well, about a year later we were moving to florida in a sad state of wedlock. We didn't get along, she hated it, and when you were about one and a half, she was packing up to go back to Jersey. I wanted to go, but she said we wouldn't work out. I wanted to help with child support, but she was so goddamn stubborn that she just didn't want me in your life.' He lied(liar! liar! fucking liar!) And Bobby struck. The chair flipped backward, Bobby swipe the black shirt around Robert's torso into his grip, he pulled him towards Bobby, who was seething in anger. He knew the real story, he knew the truth. 'My mother, is a stubborn BITCH, but you, are a lying BITCH!' Robert choked out word through a cough, deep and rough. 'I... haaacccc, I am sorry... I'm sooo sorry...haaackkhaaaccckkk, things changed Bobby, I'm not... haacckkkhackhackhaccckk... that man anymore!' Bobby believed him, the tears in his eyes told Bobby the truth, and after being in the career he has been in for as long as he has, with all of the deception, he had learned to believe a man who was honest. Bobby knew he would lie, he DIDN'T know he would be sorry(fuck that, kill him! twenty three fucking years its time to kill him! fuck that fuck that kill him!) kwell the beast(fuck that fuck that fuck him fuck you fuck fuck fuck fuck fuck!! (silence) Bobby let the shirt slip from his hands, like dust, like life, like Robert let family slip from his hands. Robert fell, coughing on his hands and knees, and Bobby did what came naturally, and fell to his knees and comforted his father, long estranged, long decimated with the guilt of abandoning the only son he'd ever have. With his life drawing closed, all Robert could do was tie the ends. He was off to a terrible start. As everyone Connected to Bobby always was(I'm a worthless shell of a man...)
