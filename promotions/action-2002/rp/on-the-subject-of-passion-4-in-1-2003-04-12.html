On the Subject of Passion. 4-in-1.

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Quinton's Celica raced down the city street as if he had never heard of a speed limit before. The car passed streetlights in the April night, and they all passed by them in an indistinguishable blur. Quinton wove in and out of traffic as if he was the only person on the road. Wendal sunk deeply into his seat, with a queasy expression on his face. His face looked shocked, and his skin tone was very pale. He leaned over and asked something to Quinton. Grimes' head leaned closer to Wendal's. He didn't hear what Wendal had to say; After all, how could he in the midst of screeching tires and blaring car horns? 'I asked youWHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING?' Wendal shouted restlessly. Quinton cracked a smile. 'You told me I have no passion, correct?' Quinton started in rhetorically, continuing before Wendal could reply, 'I'm taking us to a place where I can show you that I have passion.' 'Please slow down! All I'm passionate about now is staying alive!' With a swift jerk of the steering wheel, the car turned into a busy parking lot. Quinton sped up and down the parking lot and finally stuffed the car in what looked like the last parking spot. Both got out and slammed their doors. Quinton headed towards the establishment, but stopped after realizing he was alone once more. He turned around and looked at Wendal, who stared at the fluorescent sign. 'Comin'?' Quinton asked, his breath making a slight cloud in the chilly air. 'That's a bar,' Wendal stated to Quinton as he pointed at the building. Quint smiled and headed towards the bar. 'Johnny, tell him what he's won,' Quinton joked sarcastically. Wendal looked around and then ran to catch up. 'I didn't mean this kind of passion Quinton!' Wendal explained. He circled Quinton as he headed towards the building, 'I was talking about real passion. Real interests. All that's here is low-lifes and failures drowning their sorrows.' It became obvious to Wendal that Quinton wasn't listening, so he made a last-ditch effort. He grabbed Quint and both stopped in front of the bar. 'Besides Quint, you know about my social problems,' Wendal whined in a hushed tone, 'Remember what I said in the hotel about getting thrown around by Quakers?' Quinton's head nodded with understanding. However, this plea wouldn't work, and Quinton headed for the front door. He opened up the door, and the faint music immediately poured out of the doorway and into the parking lot. 'Aren't you coming?' Wendal looked around and seemed to gag on his own anxiety. He nodded slightly and followed Quinton inside. Both stood in the entrance, and carefully surveyed the area as if they were 'casing' it for a robbery. Their speculative vision panned across the room, where their eyes saw pool tables, bars, and the drinks that flowed like water. 'These people don't look so bad after all,' Wendal said as he looked up at Quinton. 'I told you.' Wendal looked uncomfortable as two lovely ladies walked by in front of them. The duo's flashed Wendal a seductive smile. Wendal was clearly uncomfortable; he took his trembling hands and dug them into his pockets. Both walked on, and Wendal looked up for Quinton for advice. But this time it was Quinton who was gone. His eyes quickly bounced about the dimly-lit room, where he found Quinton at the bar...ordering a drink and introducing himself to a very attractive woman. Wendal shrugged, and headed off in an opposite direction. * * * * * * Wendal hadn't walked more than five steps before being pulled aside by a group of young women. 'Hey you,' The first said as she grabbed Wendal's arm. 'Aren't you a little old to be in here?' Another inquired. Wendal gulped nervously, but managed a smile. 'Ladies,' Wendal suavely stated. Nothing more, nothing less. They all shared girlish laughs. 'Is he,' the first started, in regards to Quint, 'your son?' 'No, actually, I guess you could say he's my 'client'. I am his manager.' 'Is he a rock star?' The first asked. 'Maybe he's an actor,' offered another. 'Or how about a famous model?' a third broke silence and inquired. Wendal reached over and snatched the girl's drink. He took a long drink, nervous of the women's reaction to the fothcoming answer. 'Actually, he's a professional wrestler.' They all shared awestruck glances, and it looked like they were just as intrigued as they would have been with the above professions. 'He can wrestle with me all he wants,' one murmured, her face soiled with excessive makeup. The three women laughed loudly, quite embarassed by what the woman had said. Wendal let out a nervous chuckle as well, just to fit in well with their emotions. Wendal elusively escaped their attention, and slid himself into the concealing protection of a group of people. He made his way to the bar and took a seat on the stool. He ordered a drink, and after getting it, turned around and observed a group of people who danced in front of him. He took a sip of his drink, and blinked with the strong taste that met his tastebuds. His mouth soon adjusted, and he took a brave drink of the translucent liquor. He looked at the happy faces that were gyrating to the vibrating music. The crowd slightly dispersed, enough so Wendal could see just who was dancing in the middle of it all: 'Is that...?' It sure was. Quinton was in the middle of the floor, dancing with an attractive woman. This surprised Wendal, for not only was he dancing...but he was looking good doing it at the same time. Wendal finished his drink, and the song concluded shortly after. He watched as Quinton tried to get away, but the woman grabbed his shirt. She pulled him towards her and kissed him firmly. Wendal nearly choked on his last sip. This was shocking to Wendal, for he never knew that Quint had such a profound affect on the opposite sex. He figured Quinton's face would gnarl with anger or react with rage at this suggestive action, but Quint smiled and walked off, sobered by what had occured. Wendal's sparkly eyes watched as Quinton walked across the room and into the restroom. Wendal turned around, put his empty glass on the counter, and paid the bartender. He walked away from the bar, and followed Quinton Grimes into the men's room. * * * * * * Wendal walked in the men's room, and was quite surprised that it was for the most part, empty. He saw that Quinton was relieving himself in a stall, so he indiscreetly walked up to the sink and began washing his hands. Quinton finished and walked towards the sink, where he was pleasantly surprised by Wendal's presence. 'Hey,' Quinton said nonchalantly as he leaned over and rinsed his hands. Wendal leaned against the sink next to Quint's. 'You never told me you could dance.' 'I haven't told you a lot of stuff I can do. Just because I'm not passionate around you, doesn't mean I'm never passionate. Besides, what's the incentive to act that way around you? You're two times older than I am.' 'True.' Wendal responded as he rubbed his chin in a ponderous pose. Quinton dried off his hands and turned around sharply. 'No offence...' 'None taken,' Wendal said with a smile. 'Yeah, I saw you out there with those girls,' Quinton commented as he poked Wendal in the chest with his wet forefinger. 'I doubt you guys were talking about politics...' 'We were talking about you.' 'Yeah, that's the thing,' Quinton said with concern, 'Like four people recognized me from Action and I've yet to get on T.V. Very strange. But then again, I've never been in a fed like Action before. It's really humbling. I'm not just a wrestler anymore...I'm an Action superstar. It makes me proud, but it also worries me alot, too.' 'Ah, don't worry Quinton. You're a good kid and I have no doubts you're gonna be big,' Wendal said sincerely as he slapped his hand on Quinton's shoulder. 'And the way you've carried yourself lately, is like night and day. You've proved to me that you give a damn about yourself, and me. I can't tell you how much I'll appreciate you taking out that Shabazz guy next week.' 'As long as he can keep his brown-nose out of our business, I have no need for force. I believe diplomacy is the best answer,' Quinton said with a chuckle, 'See, and you mean to tell me Bush was supposed to do this with Iraq? I won't mess with the guy unless he gives me a reason to.' Wendal smirked and headed for the door. 'Lets get going, Quinton, you've proved your point.' * * * * * * Quinton and Wendal said what few goodbyes needed to be said before heading back into the chilly night. They walked across the pavement, and a prolongued silence seperated them. However, they couldn't help but notice that their spirits were particularly high. Quinton had proved himself, and Wendal had discovered that he had decent social ability, should he ever have to use it. Both entered the car, and sighed in unison once inside. They looked into each others eyes, and laughed hysterically. At that very moment, they lost all restraint of themselves. The unusual outburst truly bordered insanity. Quinton revved up the engine, and their excitements finally subdued. Wendal dug into his pocket, and pulled out a ragged slip of paper. 'What's this?' He handed it to Quinton, who read it aloud. 'Candice... 413... this is a phone number, Wendal.' 'What's it for?' Quinton cut the engine and stared sternly at Wendal Caskey. 'Don't tell me you're serious.' Wendal smiled. Quinton threw the paper in Wendal's lap, frustrated that he had been fooled. He started up the engine once more, and backed up out of the space. He couldn't describe the utopian feeling of happiness he had achieved with the wonderful night that celebrated a contract, and life in general. Both had left the bar feeling better about themselves, which was a definite plus for the both of them.
