The Bind of Conduct.

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/04/02 You want to push me? That’s what happens. I’m Roland Ermann. A man with a work ethic like no one else... but people still view past it. Everyone in the Wrestling world doesn’t give me the authority I really deserve. You shout “Erection!” at my comments... cheer the blatant sins in this realm... You’ll do anything and everything you can to make sure *I* don’t get the ground *I* worked for in your eyes. Well... how do you feel after Pressure Point Five? Sad? Mad? Confused? You should be. Everyone overlooks the true power of the law... it should’ve never been a surprise. Yet it was. And now... now you’re forced to live with it. Yes, the true authority I have showed last night. The World Champion. The most popular icon... Together. With me. I raped your gold and you heart in just TWO minutes. I raped your draw. The man you all came to see. Now he’s with me. How does *that* verdict make you feel? I smile, oh yes... I smile. I’m very happy as a think over what has taken place. However, the greatest prize of all is that I actually trust these people. Fear and I go way back... we made it together. And Fox? Christopher has been my biggest Fan throughout the worst of times. I’d treat him like shit, yet he still loved me. No, matter, what. And they are with me now. Nothing can stop us. So, do you feel angry? Care to take a swing at me? Go ahead. I have two guys that would gladly step in front of it. Brace yourselves... it’s a whole new Courtroom now. [I, rest, my, case.]
