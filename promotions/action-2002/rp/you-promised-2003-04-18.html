You Promised

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Max Danger had just defeated Conrad Ramsey. Yes, again. But it had been different this time. He had done it inside a steel cage. Oh, and there was that whole Bradley Duncan run-in and help, but let's not let that overshadow the facts. Max Danger is your lord, your savior, and your, well, everything. He wasn't exactly feeling like everyone's everything though. He was aided to the back by his bestfriend, Bradley Duncan, as all the marks inside the RCA Dome booed him. Why? He won. Shouldn't they be throwing streamers and such? Whatever. Max didn't care. He could take the heat. The two former PIW World Tag Team Champions walked through the curtains. Once they had reached the back, Jessica was standing there. Worry covered her face. She ran up to her husband and put one of his arm's over her shoulder and placed a hand on his stomach. 'Are you alright, baby?' she asked. She knew he wasn't, but she still asked. What else was she going to say? Danger nodded, even though it was a blatant lie. Together, Jessica and Bradley helped Xander through the backstage area. There were long moments of silence. Not that anyone was afraid to talk or anything. They just felt the need to be quiet. There was no need in just talking. But after a moment. Just as they were reaching the lockerroom, Jessica stopped and faced her husband. She said, 'You promised.' Max looked at her with confusion. She read his look and went on, 'You said, if you were to beat Conrad, you would take next week off. Remember?' It hit Max. 'Oh, yeah. I'm sticking to my word, Jess.' He smiled and kissed her. She smiled back. 'Good,' she said. 'Well, let's get you home and into bed.' She then walked into the lockeroom. She was so happy. Now they had a long drive back home. Those always sucked. But for once, there was someone else... Bradley Duncan. Why was he there?
