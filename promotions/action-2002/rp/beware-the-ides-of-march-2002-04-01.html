Beware the Ides of March

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 1, 2002 On this bright and sunny early Spring afternoon, a large van is parked in the middle of a grassy field. The large white van has its doors open and loud rock music blaring from the speakers. On the side of the van it reads, “WROQ” with “We Roq!” right under it. In front of the van is a large wooden table with a banner hanging over the front of it. The banner says, “Get Your Shawn Steele Autograph for only Twenty Dollars!” Behind the table is Shawn who is eagerly waiting with a pen in hand and Marsh who is fast asleep. On the ground in front of them lay two men who are also asleep. Shawn puts his ear in the air and listens carefully. He then runs into the van and cranks up the volume. The sleeping men jump up, startled. Shawn: I LOOOVE this song! Marsh: Huh? Shawn wait’s a few seconds, then bellows out loud and off key. Shawn: Girl it ain’t no lie, bye bye bye! I don’t want to *something something* but I *something something* bye bye! Marsh: Shawn, I think you’re singing the wrong song. Shawn: How so? Marsh: I don’t remember Black Sabbath doing a pop love ballad. Shawn: Whatever. Shawn turns back to the van and does his best boy band dance impression. The two men who were laying on the ground get up and stretch. They look at their watches and stretch some more. Guy 1: Dudes, we’ve been here for three hours and not one kid has asked for your autograph. I say we chalk this up as a bust. Guy 2: Yeah, I say we do that. Shawn: No! We gotta wait for the Shawn-o-holic’s who are fighting traffic to come and see me. Won’t someone think of the children. Please, think of the children! Marsh: Shawn, face it, you’re not this big mega star you thought you were. Just because you wrestle doesn’t mean you’re popular. Shawn: Says you! I don’t remember you wrestling LL Bean in front of at least twenty people. Marsh: First, it’s LLB, second, it was more like thousands of people, and third, I would never wrestle LLB because he would kick my ass like he did to you! Shawn: You darn right! Marsh: Shawn, we’ve been here for three hours and how many people have asked for your autograph, none! Shawn: Nah-un! That crazy lady came by and asked for one! Marsh: If “Has anyone seen my rubber chicken penis?” means “can I have your autograph?” then you’re right! Shawn: Five minutes! Guy 2: Okay, five minutes, after that we’re going to that “Pearl Necklace Marathon” that sounds fun. Shawn: I don’t get it. Marsh: Don’t ask! The guys go back to their positions and wait out the five minutes. They continue to wait as on of the guys glances at his watch. He stands up and starts to speak when a little girl runs to the table. Girl: Please don’t tell me I’m too late! Shawn: You’re just in time little man. Girl: I’m a girl. Shawn: You can be whatever you want to be, no matter what anyone tells you! Girl: Listen, I just want an autograph, okay? Shawn: Okay, junior, give me your pad and I’ll fix you up. The girl hands Shawn her notepad and awaits for his signature. Shawn: So, what’s your name, sonny? Girl: Peggy-Sue. Shawn: God, what were your parents thinking? There you go, cowboy, have fun. The girl flips through the notepad and studies the signature. Girl: “Stay Cool, Shawn Steele”?!? You’re Shawn Steele? Shawn: The one and only. Well, I might not actually be the one and only, but for the purpose of this conversation-- Girl: Who the fuck is Shawn Steele? I thought you were Chris Rock, what kind of shit is that? Mother fucker! Shawn: Ch-ris? You got it wrong boy, I’m THE Shawn Steele. Girl: For the last fucking time, I’m not a little boy! God damn! The little girl storms off and throws her notepad in the trash. Shawn looks at the other guys in confusion. Shawn: What was his problem? I bet if we gave him one of those “Pearl Necklaces” you were talking about, he would have been a lot more social. Guy 1: I doubt it, I gave one to a little kid once and got into a lot of trouble… The other guy and Marsh look at the first guy, both with disgusted looks on their face as Shawn goes back to his seat and waits his next fan.
