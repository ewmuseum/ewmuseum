Scouts on Mission. [2]

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Woodchuck Bill walked with a purpose. He walked with speed throughout the hallway as Scout Master Scott tagged closely behind. Scott wasn’t really for the idea... there were still other badges to get, and other things to accomplish. And, to be honest, he was pretty much a pussy when it came to any types of sports. The odd thing was, Bill was too. Ah, those scouting boys... anything for a badge. Bill turned the corner sharply, and realizing they were near, Scott spoke out to him. “Let’s not do this, Bill.” He said, frowning. “Please. Can’t we just get our knitting badge? I’ve been working on that doily all week!” “No.” Bill snapped, turning around, as his giant buck teeth and massively chubby cheeks shined in the lighting above. Then, with his awfully “nerd-like” voice, he replied. “No, we have to get these. Imagine all the scouts if we got our wrestling badge! Imagine how jealous they’d be!” “No one else has these badges though. Teddy would’ve... but he moved onto Ventures before they came out with the badge!” Bill nodded. “Yes. See my point? If we got these...” Scott knew it was true. If they could get their wrestling badges... well, they’d be the talk of all of the 81st Oshawa Pack. Still, it was wrestling. Still, it was a sport. There was an utter silence for about another minute before Bill turned back to where he was walking, and walked. “Let’s go tell everyone.” “But... but...” Ah, those scouting boys... anything for a badge.
