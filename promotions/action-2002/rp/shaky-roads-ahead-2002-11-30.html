Action! — Tales

Shaky roads ahead

30 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lee Lightning stood outside the Corel Centre in Ottawa, Canada. Action! Wrestling was in town this Monday for Pressure Point, but later on that evening they’d also be housing a house show. On the card The Elements were scheduled to take on Max Danger and Bobby Minio as an afterthought to Last Laugh, but right now Lee’s thoughts were somewhere else. “Come on, where is he?” Lee asked to no one in particular. A crew member walked past carrying two lead poles and shrugged his shoulders before carrying on walking. Lee continued to tap impatiently on the clear glass of his Rolex. Behind him, trainers dully thudded against the concrete paving slabs. Lee turned around, and took a bottle of water from the hands of his brother David Dew. When they weren’t wrestling, their names were Lee Tyler and David Tyler. They’re cousins of Benjamin Tyler, known to Action! fans as Airborn Tyler. “I don’t get it,” said Lee, removing the cap from the plastic bottle and sipping a small bit of the cool water from inside. “Airborn should be here by now, Aunt Jane said she was dropping him off to make sure he got here. Afterall, we need to talk before the event starts. Business needs to be taken care of.” David stood next to Lee and looked out over the parking lot, in the hope that their Aunt’s familiar Hyundai would be parked in a corner they hadn’t noticed. David towered over his 6’2” brother, and he also had a much bulkier build on his bones. Both men were in casual clothes with black tracksuit bottoms, with Lee sporting the ‘Action’ t-shirt and David donning a tight black sweater that showed off his physique. Lee couldn’t have cared less what they were wearing though, he just needed to talk to their cousin. “You cannot leave that kid alone for one minute. We both get eliminated and leave the arena before we do something we regret, and what happens? Airborn goes and forms a one night alliance with Jonny Yankee. Jonny Yankee! Kent Anthason, hey, now he knows his politics. Perhaps event Steven Denton, because at least he’s a reasonably quiet and unassuming person, but Jonny Yankee?!” Lee shook his head in vulgar disgust. “He epitomises what is wrong with American society these days, and everything that is right about England.” David nodded his head I silent agreement, and raised his head when a white car pulled in, before turning away when he realised it was James Attwood. “Think about it. America wants to go to war with Iraq because of something as petty as oil. The senate can deny it as much as they want, but if Iraq wasn’t sitting on a potential goldmine then America wouldn’t even Saddam Hussain out of the corner of their eyes. In the beginning I always thought that only George W Bush thought that going to war was good, and that the people of this vast country just weren’t using their democratic rights properly, but in retrospect, that isn’t it.” Lee turned his baseball cap around, although it was a wonder he had a cap on at all. The sky was a murky grey, and the forecast was for torrential downpours of rain within the next eight hours. “These people don’t WANT to object to their President. It’s not that they don’t know how to communicate their feelings. No, they know how to communicate their feelings. They WANT to go to war! These ignorant, prejudiced Americans want to go to war over oil! And that’s where Jonny Yankee comes in. By openly encouraging people to be soldiers in public, he is promoting anarchy in the worst possible way.” A few drops of rain started to patter on the tin roof over the entrance of Corel Centre, under which the Dyad Champions were standing. “Jonny Yankee is promoting war, unruliness and anarchy, and what does our cousin do? He shakes his hand, he ALIGNS himself with the petty fool. Airborn needs guidance, he is too young to face this world on his own. We need to put a hand on his shoulder and show him the light, otherwise he will continue to make mistakes like this and dishonour the family name.” Lee took another swig from the water bottle and lobbed it at a bin. The container bounced off the rim and rolled into the middle of the road, but Lee didn’t go to pick it up. David Dew crossed his arms and looked up at the skies, from which the dripping had turned into a persistent shower of rain. Driving back to the hotel wouldn’t be fun after the event if this got any worse, and invariably it would be David driving their dark forest green Ranger Rover: Lee was always too busy studying the newspaper for any injustices in the world. Sometimes David got ticked off at Lee’s constant moaning, but he wasn’t going to argue. Lee claimed he was the brains behind The Elements costume, and although David knew deep down he was as intelligent as his sibling, if it allowed him to focus on the wrestling he was paid to do, what was the problem? Often he would be overshadowed though and say nothing. Now he felt like speaking his mind. “At Pressure Point, we need to make sure that this alliance with Jonny Yankee, is history. Our name cannot be tarnished by associating the family name with such moronic beings. We end it.” Lee nodded in agreement, before turning around and through the revolving doors back into the arena. Two hours until they were up, and they needed this as a warm up for their first televised title defence Monday. Airborn could explain himself when he bothered to show up.
