Action! — Tales

Showtime

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 19, 2002 The paint is still fresh. You can still see the shine on the tiled floors. The canvas still smells like canvas, and not the blood, sweat, and tears of men pouring everything that they have into that squared circle. But all that will change soon enough. A man is standing in the make-shift Action training facility, carefully examining the ring, and all its surroundings. It is quite obvious that he is a wrestler, you can tell becuase of his build. He is far more muscular than the guy from Nevada who throws the rings together every few nights would be. The shadows cast throughout the room by the moonline cascading through the broken windows makes the man look somewhat scary. But isn't fear the name of the game anyway? The man is approximately five feet, ten inches tall. More muscular than the average guy. He is wearing baggy blue jeans, and a tight red shirt. If you look closely, you can just barely make out the words 'Fear the Fear' printed in black on the back of the shirt. Thats the final straw in the puzzle. Yes, this man is obviously former maineventer of PIW, FSW, and IOW. But more importantly than that, this man is arguably the most psychotic man in the history of pro wrestling. An evil laugh rises into the rafters. It echos throughout the building. Fear turns around finally revealing his face to the world. He looks not much different than when he was last seen in IOW. Plenty has changed since the abrupt end. But he has not changed. Although the first installment of Pressure Point was the first time in almost three months that Chris Fear has been seen anywhere near a ring, it seems as though he never left. Slowly Fear walks forward, his head bowed. After several feet, he slumps down into an old wooden chair, covered in cobwebs. He quickly lifts his head, with a menacing stare in his eyes. 'You all forgot about me, didn't you!? But thats just fine. Forgetting about me is exactly what I wanted you to do. After all, the less you know about me, the easier it will be for me to win....wait, I already do that pretty damn easily in a promotion with guys like that foreign kid with the funny name and some adult who acts likes he's five and walks around with his mommy and that stupid cartoon show!' Yes indeed, Chris has not changed whatsoever in the last few months. He is still the same cocky, arrogant, no nonsense guy he was the last time he was seen on television. As Chris makes his way towards the back of the building, he passes a series of rings cheaply thrown together with bed matresses on top of a piece of plywood. There are also several weights, ranging from 5 pounds, up to about as big as you can imagine. Fear pauses briefly, and quickly jerks his head to the left. A few rats hurry past him, crumbs of food in their mouths. Continuing on his way, Chris hurries forward and makes a sharp turn to the left. Directly in front of him is an old set of steel steps, leading to the rafters some 30 feet above. He sprints up the stairs, showing how good of shape he is in. When he reaches the top, he jogs along the catwalks until he is in the middle of the building, looking down. 'This week at Presure Point, I have the chance to do something that has evaded me my entire career. I was always the 'next one'! In PIW, my big shot came, but the company closed before the match ever happened. Then in IOW, they had a 'hiatus'. When they open, I'm gonna get get my big shot! Right....wrong. IOW didn't last long enough for that to happened. It closed to. I have never been the champion of a major federation.' For the first time in a long time, you can almost feel sympathetic for Chris. His voice sounds so upset, like he's heartbroken. But then all of a sudden he begins to scream, 'But that's all going to change!! Pressure Point! I don't care if it's Chris Fox, LLB, or both of them! I've beat them both before, and I can sure as hell do it again. At this time next week, you will see that championship belt around my waist! You will see what I deserve! And more importantly what belongs to me! LLB is not a star! Chris Fox is not a star!! Chris Fear is where you go if you want to make money!! And the great owner of this company is such a genious, he knows that!! Only one man has the courage to head to head with WrestleMania! And that same man will make sure Chris Fear doesn't leave empty handed!!!' Chris Fear seems to be in a rage! He looks far below into the darkness, where you can barely make out the silohette of a ring. He turns quickly, and makes one final remark before briskly walking off into the blackness of the catwalks high above. 'Just a quick reminder...I've killed before. I'm not afraid to do it again....' As Chris disappears, one must wonder, is this the time when Fear will shine? Or is it the time when he will kill again. Chris Fox and LLB don't look like they're in the best position possible. There's no doubt about it, it's definately....... SHOWTIME!
