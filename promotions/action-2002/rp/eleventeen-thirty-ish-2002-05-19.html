Eleventeen Thirty-ish

19 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 19, 2002 Simon Seaman sat there in silence with his bright yellow shirt and sweatpants in the middle of a doctor's office. He stared at a bland clock on the wall utterly bored before clutching his head in pain from the brutal attack he recently received at the hands of Fred Franks. Simon cringed for a moment, but sucked it in as the doctor arrived and made his presence felt. He glanced at Simon for a moment, but then grabbed a chair and sat in front of him. 'How are you feeling today, Mr. Seaman? Good? Surprised and annoyed at the question put in front of him, Simon quickly answered. 'Does it look like I'm good? I might not be functioning mentally or physically at one hundred percent, but I am still aware of the incident that got me here. Long story short...I got struck in the face! You think that's fine?! How about I strike you in the back of the head with this weird bed I'm on here this sheet you a butcher uses to wrap meat in? Would you feel fine then? Said bed is mighty comfortable when lodged in your cranium, don't you think?' The doctor sighed as he jotted notes down. 'Very well, sir. Very well.' he muttered nonchalantly. Simon realized that his temper was getting best of him and wasted no time to explain himself. 'I'd like to apologize for any previous or future comments that exit out of my mouth that may offend or insult you, your profession or your current way of living. As you can see, I'm not in the best of moods. I don't even feel like pestering people today. All I want to do is get back to my job.' Simon continued to sit there intently placing his hands out as a gesture to signal for the doctor to reply. After about a minute of nothing, the doctor looked up from what he was writing and placed his materials down. 'By the way, Mr. Seaman, you told me that your injury was sustained at your workplace. May I ask what you do for a living that eventually caused this injury? He stroked his chin as he attempted to make something up on the spot that would be believable to the doctor. After a few moments of thinking, we switched over to a door of a hotel room accompanied with a 'do not disturb' sign. In the background, we could hear someone speaking. 'Now Simon, what you want to do in this scene is just let these three voluptuous ladies have their way with you. It looks good on camera and we can all go home before Oprah comes on, okay?' Umtempo music pounded inside the room as the door began to shake. Outside of the room, the sign fell off of the door and landed onto the floor below. Cutting back to the doctor's office, we noticed him staring at Simon in disbelief. 'You should know something, sir. I'm a doctor so I won't clean up any type of bull crap you give or may give to me.' 'Fine, I'll tell you. What I do is that I wrestle professionally. A professional wrestler, so to speak. I am a professional wrestler. Are you happy now?' Simon proclaimed as he started to feel a slight, shooting pain in his head and tended to it. While he did that, the doctor he answered without hesitation. 'No, not really. I'm more disturbed than anything else. Can I ask you a question though? When I see those wrestlers on television lift their opponents up over their heads, do they actually have to touch the, you know. Do you know what I'm talking about?' 'Tights? No, but can we stay on topic here? When can I get back to work?' Simon asked as he opened up a bottle of Tylenol, grabbed a handful and placed it all in his mouth. 'I'd recommend that you take a few weeks or possibly a few months so you may lessen the risk of further harm to you and your body. Although, if it means that much to you, now wouldn't be the best time, but...' Before Simon could even respond, the mouthful of pills scattered around the room as he tried to conjure up the right words to express himself clearly. 'Here we go again. You smart guys and your ifs, and or buts. First it's the brothas, then it's Sir Mix-A-Lot with his super funky and super fresh sound. Just when I thought it ended there, now this.' 'I understand that you want to go back to work as soon as possible, but where you work isn't the same as an office cubicle or even an office building for that matter. Think about it, Mr. Seaman. Can you even function normally in your condition right now?' the doctor asked as another memory popped up into Simon's head. A dance club surrounded by loud music and wild men and women on the dance floor was seen. Groups of dancers scattered to reveal Simon in his usual Saturday night attire on a stool, sipping a ginger ale and chewing on an Alka-Seltzer tablet. He threw one arm in the air rhythmically to the music as the dancers did their own thing back into place. 'You actually think you can even do the simplest duties without fault?' the doctor added as the scene switched over once again. In a hotel bathroom, Simon used a framed picture of Britney Spears for inspiration while doing his business on the toilet. Glaring at it from across the bathroom, he tried to do his best with what he had. 'You want to know what I think about your video game, Britney? You're cool, new, hip, video game. How about this for an opinion? You're going to need as much overprotection as you need.' Simon stated as he slammed the bathroom door next to him. Back in the office, Simon sat slouched on the supposed butcher paper and attemped to make a final decision. 'It's really your decision, but if you come back here with a body part missing or your head in the wrong place, I can't help you.' he told Simon with a scene of sincerity in his tone of voice. 'Can you suggest a doctor that will?' The doctor stood up from his chair and tucked his papers he had been writing on under his arm. He placed the pen back in his coat and replied. 'Hold on a minute. I'll get the phone book.'
