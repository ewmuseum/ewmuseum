Wands: wI: Carnage Abroad

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: sep 24 02 Wands: wI: Carnage Abroad James Madison sat dormant. He slowly inhaled the fragrance of life. Mysterious in its own way and frightening in another. The thoughts of all the events that took place in the last 6 months of his life were unbelievable. Finally, he realized what he had become. Carnage. Champion. 'Finally, redemption. It's all adding up now. Everyone doubt me, yes, do it all you want to. One day, it'll be a different strap around my waist. It's hard to tell when, but that's a promise. Please, hold me to it.'         James tossed the championship into the floor.         'It's a shame that all of my fighting; all of my blood; all of my tears; and all of my years have to go to scrap piece of metal. It's deceiving. The picture that was painted in my mind about being a champion is different outside of the ring. I'm no one than the ordinary guy across the street. In the ring, it's a different story. There is a little more respect in the picture. It's amazing to say the least how godly awful things could be at certain times, and others are fine.'         The man exhaled. Nothing happened.         'Another day in my life is gone. I feel no better outside of the ring having a title. Inside, my days have grown. It makes me feel like a home and I've got my own bed room. I don't know it's exciting yet doubtful all in the same sentence. How is it possible? The feelings of life have betrayed me. I now do not know what true pleasure is. Fine with me ... I'm just here. No one cares. No one listens. No ONE!'         He disputed the actions with himself. The title shimmered with joy, but it wasn't what James felt inside. He used his foot to nudge the title away from himself. It seemed as if he didn't accept the changes that were to come with the title. Life changed abruptly in the last few weeks. His minds drifted away from the girl which saved his life and nearly took her own. All that he could think about was the fame and fun he was having competing. Wrestling took his life to a separate level, away from all of the fear. He stared at himself and felt the vigor. War was at hand, and he was on the winning side. It seemed that the battle had been over for quite some time, but he continued to wage on in the drought of the war. 'Reed Young, LLB, Steve Denton, and everyone else that is supporting Risk sees the truth. They understand the actions. The understand the abolishing factor that is to come. We're planning to make an even more impact here at this pay per view. Retaliation- I'm bouncing back. My title is on the line for the very first time. The work that I have earned over the past 13 weeks. It seems like I've never mentioned it before ... but ... I've went 13 week without a single day off in Action Wrestling. I've gave it quite a bit, and no one has seen it. Here it comes. The truth is now in effect, The Risk will tear apart the others one by one. Denton will unleash on Joey Malone, LLB on Krow, and myself ... on Morg. It's time ...'         The Carnage Title lay vacant in the middle of the floor. Idle, James smiled as the first emotions came across his face. It's over. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
