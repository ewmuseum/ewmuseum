A Year Ago Today

31 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last year, September 11, 2001... Sweet Aroma was managing her brother Roberto Aromadeus, a wrestler with 5 Boros Wrestling. However, things changed that morning. 5 Boros Wrestling was canceled. Sweet and Roberto remained in their Spanish Harlem apartment, watching the news of the day. They couldn't quite believe what happened. Things will never be the same is what they thought. The rest of the country felt the same way they felt when they lost their parents, and it didn't feel good at all. Veronica and Roberto Aromadeus felt bad, that people had to feel the way they felt. Things will never be the same. She tried to call Jonathan, to find out where he was. She couldn't get through. She would days later, and they would move on, but they would never forget this day. Things will never be the same. But the more that things change... ...the more they stay the same.
