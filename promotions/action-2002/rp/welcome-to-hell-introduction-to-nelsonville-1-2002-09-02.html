Welcome to Hell: Introduction to Nelsonville [1]

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept, 2nd, 2002 The azure sky was slowly fading to a dark purple as Kent made his way down... Uhh... Some odd interestate. Actually, I'll just tell you now that he's not even really sure where he's going. His hair looks awful messy and Anthason himself looks ragged without having sleep for many an hour. A bunch of maps lay beside him in the GMC he's driving and he tries to peek at one while driving... 'Where the fuck is this town? And why did Jase go through all this trouble to get me here? And where is the map for this peice of shit part of the county? Where's my sunglasses? Oh, wait... WHERE'S MY COKE?' Anthason, at that exact moment in time got very frustrated and slamed his hand down on the horn. It blared loudly, but no one could hear it. Mostly because as what can be seen, to the outside of the SUV, there is nothing but dusk and a loung road leading to mountians that rise up from the horizon. 'I hate this. Here he is, practically begging me to come to some far off city in the middle of no where. And coming out here is about as interesting as listening to, uh, God?' All of a sudden, a booming voice from seemingly nowhere sounds. Kent, being as hyped up on coffee as he is jumps almost out of his skin. 'Kent Anthason, do not proceed any futher! Danger lurks ahead!' the voice screamed. 'Who the hell are you? And why shouldn't I go any further, if further ever ends, mind you.' Kent questions the voice who seems a tad angry. A flash of lightening races across a starry sky. 'They call me... Stephen Speilburg.' 'That's the most retarded thing I've ever heard in my life. A booming voice from nowhere sounds INSIDE my car and you expect it to be Stephen Speilburg?' A fork in the road split the high-way. Anthason stopped before proceeding. 'Hey, uh. Stephen? Which way should I go?' Kent looks around the SUV as if expecting to see something. 'I fear certian danger if you go left.' 'Alright!' And with that, Kent jerks the wheel to the left. A quick scene change takes us to be a place we believe to be Stephen Speilburg's residence. He's watching television, and eating some popcorn. 'Damn. That voice is sexy.' Another switch and Kent was still driving down the road. The voice of Stephen Speilburg has seemed to have gone away, and Anthason's starting to get drowsy. All of a sudden, without warning (and without any coherence in the world, mind you.) it starts to rain. Kent turns on his winsheild wipers and the lights. He sees a sign in the distance. The sign reads: 'You're going the wrong way, dumbass. Perhaps, just perhaps, you should turn around and save yourself.' Kent passes the sign without making a third thought. His second thought was 'Why do girls go all goo-goo gaa-gaa over Carson Daly?' Another road sign flies by, Kent looks quickly to catch what that one said. He turns around in his seat to see if he can't see something from behind. Right then, we hear a horn blare loudly. Kent turns around abruptly to hit the brake and barely miss a car that flies past him. 'Since when did this road get intersections?' Kent notices in the distance a dark, shadowed town. 'There it is! I knew it'd be around here somewhere! FUCK YOU STEPHEN SPEILBURG!' Kent drives through the intersection and proceeds to the city. To his right he notices a fairly large sign. He turns his head to read it. It appears as though someone had vandalized it. The sign read: 'Welcome to Nelsonville.' A shabby looking wooden board covers Nelsonville, however. Carved into the wood, and painted in the indentations in red is the word 'Hell.' Nelsonville is still readable, though. 'Nelsonville, here I come!' He pulls up to what appears to be the center of town. A sign in the middle of a patch of grass reads 'Center of Town' so, you know, why argue with the obvious? Anthason pulls out a small note. 'Dear Kent, I will meet you at the cafe down the street from the center of the city. Don't be late. 10:00 at the latest.' Kent checks his watch. 10:11, how predictable. The rain had let up, but thunder and lightening still proceeded to reign over the sky. Kent walks down the street, hardly anyone was out and the city looked bare. He didn't notice a cafe, but did notice a bar. 'This must be it.' He tells himself as he stares in the window... A bartender is wiping off a table, and there's a very, very attractive woman wearing a green halter-top. She has dark brown eyes, and a dark brown hair color. Her face is very defined and her skin is somewhat tanned. She's sipping a Scotch and soda, whilst reading a book. Kent suddenly remembers the Wyanes World scene where 'Foxy Lady' plays and Garth is all like, yeah, i'm getting this bitch. And that girl makes love to him and stuf-- Were we just talking about Wyane's World? *Shudder* Kent looks around slowly in the bar. Lucas is nowhere to be found. Kent steps up and walks through the door. One of those annoying cow-bells was attached to the handle. It rang very loudly as the bartender gave a sudden look up. The girl, however, was unmoved. Kent takes out what appears to be mouth freshener and gives a spray in his mouth. After a second of doing so, he spits out as much as he can, making even more of a scene. He glances at the bottle... 'Men's spray on calogne.' Go figure. He walks up and sits down at the counter. Throwing the girla look before speaking to the bartender. 'She's pretty, isn't she?' The bartender whispers to Kent. 'Been here all night. Never seen her here before in my life. If you must know, I've got half a bottle of viagra and a lonely night to spend...' Kent cuts him off, 'Masturbating?' The bartender threw his rag behind him and shrugged. 'Watch out, kid. This is a strange town and even the most innocent may be the most deadly.' Kent grabs his drink and walks over to the girl. He sits down in front of her and coughs lightly signaling his presence. 'I know you're there. Just calm down, will ya? The good parts coming up.' Her gaze was still locked onto the book. 'Do you mind if I buy you a dri--' 'I don't date, sorry. I'm a nun. Anyhow, I'm plastered as it is. I'm not even actually reading this book if you must know. I'm just kinda making the story up in my head.' The girl looked up at Anthason and cracked a drunken smile. 'Actually, I came here looking for my friend Lucas. Tall guy, red hair and beard?' 'Haven't seen him. And if I did, I probably don't remember. The name's Alyssa, and all the ice cubes melted in my scotch and soda.' Kent dumped the rest of his drink in her glass. She smiled. 'Hell yeah, more liquor! Wait, you don't have, like, genetal warts or anything do you?' 'Uhh. Why would it matter, anyway?' Kent gave a retarded look, one that you would give if someone just said the DUMBEST thing you've ever heard in your life. 'My name's Kent.' 'And, my name's Tonya.' 'I thought you just said your name was, Alyssa?' 'Oh! Oh yeah!' She laughed almost uncontrollably. 'My mind seems to slip me sometimes. Have you ever read this book?' She held up the book for Kent to see. It was a copy of the Catcher in the Rye. 'Most boring goddamn book I've ever read. I've been on the same page for two hours.' The Bartender cuts in for a second. 'Hey, mister. Could you take her home?' Kent looks at the bartender, then looks back at Alyssa. He nods, and tells her 'If I take you home, you've gotta show me around town tomorrow. I've got to get a hold of my friend.' 'Rest assured if your friend isn't in this town. If he is, though, he's probably dead. It's kinda like a spider web. You get caught in it and can't get free. This town is weird anyway.' And with that, she gave a loud hiccup. 'Perhaps you should show me where you live before you pass out?' Kent helped Alyssa into his car and they started to drive down the streets. The building became so close that they were a foot from touching, and then they just became one long row of buildings. Like a wall. 'This city, is like a big haunted house. It always stays dark here. It only appears to those who it wants to come. And I'm not drunk, mind you. I'm jussh a little tipsy. What I'm saying is... It's evil here, too evil. It's like, the devils playplace, or something. It's a huge city, mind you. And by the beach. Stop right here, this is it...' Kent picks Alyssa out of her seat. He walks to her door step as she tells him it's open and he can stay the night. Before they go in, however, a loud racket from next door sounds. 'THIS KING IS TOO ROYAL FOR YOU, BABY.' And out from the next door came The King himself, Elvis, all dressed up and fat as he appeared before he died. Alyssa looked over. 'Oh yeah. Once you come here, you can't leave. Elvis never really died, and everyone says aliens abducted him. He just got stranded here. Same with-- From the door across the street, comes Tupac Shakur carrying a loaded nine millimeter. 'If yall don't shut da fuck up, den dis nigga gonna put you to rest.' And after that, from the door next to Tupac's, which would be right across from Alyssa's came out Tim Couch. They're all standing on their door steps which are elevated from the ground by a good many stairs that lead up to their apartments. Tim Couch looks awful disgruntled, 'Do you know how many--' Before he can finish, however, a blast is heard and his door was suddenly painted red. He had been meaning to do it for a few weeks now, but not in the manner which he just did. And with that, the King raises his arms and yells to Tupac 'I aint got, uhh, beef my friend.' 'You better not, mothafucka!' And turns around entering his home and slamming the door behind. Elvis went back into his apartment, just after shouting 'I'M COMING BACK TO MY THRONE, BABY.' and closing the door. Then Kent carried Alyssa in and closed the door. Tim Couch got up, even though he had just sustained a mortal wound, and looked around for a second blinking. It was a miracle! He had survived with a bullet STILL lodged in his head! A split second later, Tupac Shakur came BACK out of his door and shot him once more in the face, finishing the painting job once and for all. Tim fell to his porch steps and Tupac went back inside... This time... He didn't get up.
