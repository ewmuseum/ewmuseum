Smell The Aroma (Part 8 - Just The Two Of Us)

18 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.18.02 1980 Four... ... ... ... 1984 Three... ... ... ... 1994 Two... ... ... ... 2002 Two... ... ... ... 2004 One? I sure hope not. Right now me and my brother are enjoying our careers in the wrestling business, and I don't want to see the day he leaves me; just like he doesn't want to be on this Earth alone without me. I know eventually it'll happen, but please don't take away the only person in this world that helped me when I needed it most. Roberto has been more than a brother to me. Rather he's been MORE THAN a father to me. To be honest with you, I don't think I'd be in Action without him, better yet I wouldn't be wrestling without him; AND to top that off, I wouldn't be alive without him. Roberto has constantly gone above and beyond the call of duty, when it comes to protecting his little sister. Never once did he object to Jonathan telling me to get into the ring. Never once did he hate me signing a contract with a major promotion before he did. And to top it off, Roberto has pretty much become my trainer, showing me things that I never would've learned. Cue Will Smith's 'Just The Two Of Us' or if you want to live dangerously Eminem's ' 97 Bonnie and Clyde' And now as I get ready to make my television debut, he continues to grind it out in the independents, and those dark matches for various federations throughout the nation. I've tried to put the good word into Reed Young, and hopefully one day he'll be smart enough to sign him. Then it'll be 'Just The Two Of Us' All over again. I love you bro.
