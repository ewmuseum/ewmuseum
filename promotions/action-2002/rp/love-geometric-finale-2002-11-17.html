Love Geometric - Finale

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/17/2002 October 31th, 2002 Early Thursday Morning They laid in what could only be described as the shattered remains of the bedroom of Kent Anthason's chateau, in New Orleans, Louisiana. What they had worn prior to this laid sprawled about on the floor. Keri's black dress and sandals. Kent's expensive silk shirt, leather pants, and shoes. The rug that generally greeted this room had been throughly dishelved by all of the stumbling around the two had done while embraced in a deep liplock. Equally destroyed was Kent's alarm clock, which had been smashed with one of Keri's sandals as it was thrown from her left foot. Almost entirely more destroyed was a lamp that was on that same table, the additional victim of said flying sandal. This had the rather convenient effect of also shutting out the only real source of light in the room, which was now only illuminated by the lights outside of the room. But perhaps the most destroyed thing in the whole bedroom was the bed, itself. Specifically the two individuals that were actually in it, Keri Lindum and Kent Anthason. The bed looked more like a disaster area than an actual place to rest. The sheets and covers were sprawled about and misplaced like something out of road construction on I-40 in Memphis, Tennessee. The only thing that the covers were actually covering were the naked bodies of Anthason and Lindum. There, they laid. Exhausted from quite a bit of sex. In truth, they looked like they had just survived an attack from a mob. Going all night tends to do that to you. Now they were tired and were seemingly finally going to sleep. At least, that's what it seems. Anthason sleeping perfectly fine, but Keri still laid awake. She laid on her back, next to Anthason, perfectly still. She didn't wish to disturb him, so she just laid there and thought to herself. For the briefest moment, she thought about her husband. She thought about what he was doing without her, and she thought about how he was coping with it. She had no idea that he was on a flight to Thailand and was quite drunk at the time. She had no idea that he couldn't even sleep because of her. She had no idea that he was looking for guidance from Kent's ex-girlfriend. She wondered, briefly, just where he was. Then she realized just what kind of effect her actions might have had on Joey. A thought came across her mind, simple and direct. What have I done? It left her as quickly as it came, and then she just stopped thinking about him, like she just silenced a nagging voice in her head. She turned her body around in the bed and went to sleep. -fin-
