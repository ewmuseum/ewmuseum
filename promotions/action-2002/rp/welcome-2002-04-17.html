Date: 04/17/02 LLB entered the Arena with force as he pushed the door back, whacking it up against the side of the wall. He squinted his eyes, trying to focus just a little more... but he was still incredibly happy. ‘The Law’ marched down the catwalk, changing views frequently to the scene around him. “Locker Room... I have to find my Locker Room.” He said to himself, turning the corner and catching a banner ad in front of him reading: “Action!’s Pressure Point”. “Insane?” He muttered for a second as he read that word instead of what was really on there. LLB shook his head afterwards. “Okay, whatever...” And looking past it, a Crewman then walked by, a pleasant smile on his face. “Hi.” He greeted, heading in the opposite direction. “Jack?” ‘The Law’ stopped there though, finding his Room and opening it up. But to his shock, in front of him sat... Jim Johnson. “What the fuck are you doing here?” LLB snapped, surprised yet pissed the former Owner of Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling had shown his face again, even after the closure of the Federation six months ago. “Uh, Roland, you okay?” He asked. “Get out of here Jim!” ‘The Law’ demanded in reply, but Johnson didn’t obey. Instead he just seemed puzzled. “Um... it’s Reed.” At that moment LLB’s focus became clear again... it was Reed Young after all. “Oh, hey. What’s up?” He said, changing the subject rather quickly. Reed smiled. “Ratings! Yes, that’s right. You and Fox, tonight... Tag... against ERA and Steele!” “My God!” Replied LLB. “Steele and Cylear... tonight!?” Young stood, placing his hand on LLB’s shoulder. “You okay? I said Steele and ERA, not Steele and Cylear. Sean’s dead, by the way.” “Right. Right. I knew that.” Reed smiled again, skipping off to the door as he turned back one final time. “Remember... give me ratings.” “Hey... that’s why I’m here.”
