Journey to Dreamland, Part I

16 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/16/02 Max Danger was watching cartoons and laughing madly('BUBBLES!' he would shout out in a childish voice while cutest Powerpuff Girl would fly across the screen). Bradley Duncan was on his laptop at the table in the breakfast nook. He was researching for some upcoming matches. The frustrated look on his face gave the impression that he wasn't having much luck. Jessica was piling dishes into the dishwasher. A hearty dinner was had by all and after making it, she was also having to clean it up. A woman's job is never complete.('Fix this! Clean that! Bring me some of those! I'm only one person!' she would mutter to herself, having to take care of three grown men now, since Duncan had moved in and despite her hatred for Ramsey, her human compassion and love overcame that and forced her to help Ramsey in his time of need.) And final Conrad Ramsey. After the turkey, his eyes began to feel heavy. Soon they would be closed and he would be taken to a whole new world. Dreamland, some call it. This time, his journey to Dreamland would take him to a place he hadn't been in years. His childhood. 1941... The first month of his life. Having been born on December 7th of that year, he didn't get to see much of it. What he did see, however, was his mother slaving to make ends meet and provide for herself and the baby Conrad. His father was dead. Died on the USS Arizona during the Pearl Harbor attack. A military man had been around to give his mother the sad news. Conrad didn't know anything. He was too young. Only a couple of days old when his mom found out. But he could sense the sorrow. His mother took on a second job in Paintsville, Kentucky. Her sister had moved in and was helping with the chores and the baby. Christmas Day the whole Ramsey family was there. Remembering his father. Honoring his father. A man he was never going to meet. 'Dad!' Conrad had awoken from his sleep and had drawn everyone's attention. He just looked around at them all staring at him, then laid his head back down. He just stared up at the ceiling and a single tear rolled from his eye and down his left cheek.
