Cups: cXV: Queen's Wisdom

30 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: aug.30.02 Cups: cXV: Queen's Wisdom ‘It had to have been a dream. I saw it all. The whole thing! The warmth of the blood that seeped across my hand was too real.'          James was unconscious. Imagination was completely in effect. Everything hit him at once as he realized that ... his love ... lived. ‘Being in a world so harsh with people to match, it gives you hardly no room at all to breath. I understand the perpetual events to take place. I understand the everlasting hatred that some people share for others. Taking the life of another individual is quite possibly the hardest thing imaginable. I swear, if I killed somebody ... I'd be gone.' ‘What is going on?! Why can't I control myself?'          James struggled slightly in his own little world, but in reality his attempts were futile. Not the slightest bit of motion took place. He felt the sudden urge to scream. ‘I open my mouth to release a scream all I get is a sigh. I want to talk, all I get are broke off words with absolutely no abundant meaning. What is happening to me? I know I've asked it numerous times, but if I knew the answer ... all of my problems would be solved.'          James settled the boat along the beaches edge. In reality, the tears crept down the side of his face. In his world ... there was no need to cry. ‘I've just ended my long ride down the Sea of Sorrow. It seems that all of the tears, all of the stories are beginning to add up.'          James looked at himself. He tried to make out what was happening but was in too deep of a state to make any valiant efforts. ‘I'm Confused! I'm aggravated. I ... saw her.'          His mind raced on as he could not place himself back into reality. The world in which he inhabited was far beyond belief. Not even he could imagine it again. Hours seemed to have passed, yet he didn't stop the pondering. ‘I believe what I believe. I know what I know. I see what I see. By God, I saw her!' - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - 'I've waited years to actually speak to you or so it seems. You've nearly lost yourself in tht little world of yours. Wake up and join the real world.'          A sharp young woman nudged James. Awaking from nearly a lifetime of sleep, he looked up into her eyes. 'This is the first time that ... I've saw your eyes. They're so much more beautiful in person. Yet, this is unexplainable. You're ... gone.'          James stuttered a little yet thought up a few words to say to her.          'Not quite. You're a little closer than what I am.'          James looked at her confused. 'I'm no where near gone.'          He felt so energetic but didn't have the motivation to get up and leave. He could have looked at her eyes forever.          'I know what they're up to.'          Still, he tried to piece together the puzzle.          'They? Who is they?' James scratched his head and raised up slightly. He got the aroma of the sweet young lady.          'Your ... brother.' 'That is the most deceitful human being alive. If people think I'm mean, HA! They've got another thing coming. That guy scares me.'          James put his hands together and looked at her.          'Thank god he's locked away.'          'He was supposed to get out not too long ago.'          James responded and added a little more onto her story. 'He's already back. This time, for good.'          James sighed in relief as he thought the whole time ... it was his brother.          'Thank god.'          'Yet, he's hiring people. James, that funeral ... they set it all up. That journal ... they set it all up. It's hard to believe, but look at me... I'm here aren't I?'          James crashed back down into the bed and peered up at the ceiling. He didn't know what was left to say. 'Earlier on ... you remember when that guy found you. He was supposed to take you to me.'          James admired her beauty once more.          'You looked so sickly back then. How were you cured?' Queen laughed jokingly.          'Bud, that wasn't quite me. Once more ... he was with them. If he wanted you dead though, he could have had you. I had to help you out there. You see, I paid him to get you away from ‘them.' He followed through.'          'Where is he now?'          'It's hard to tell. He could have already sided with them once more. The pay I have him is already gone ... so I don't have much time.'          'Much time? What are you talking about?'          'He led you to me. I actually saw you that day at the hospital though. That's all I wanted. He lead you to safety, but don't trust anyone now. I understand that you're wrestling. Keep yourself straight.'          'What do you mean? You can't go.'          'James ... I have explained all that there is to explain. Keep going. You went this long without me. I'm sure you can go longer. Just, know I'm living.'          James wept. He was speechless, but she placed some final words down upon him.          'The longer I stay, the more likely they find you. The less I stay, the better off you are.'          'I'm not worried about them though...'          'James, they carry the same power that your brother carries. I'm sure you know the capabilities that he had.'          'You mean ... he trained them. He wants me dead?'          'There ya go. Just ... think about things. It'll all make sense one day.'          James laid back and closed his eyes. As the Sea of Sorrow had dried up ... and the Cup of Life had been rejuvenated.
