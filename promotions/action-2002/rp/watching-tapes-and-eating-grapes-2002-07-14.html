Watching Tapes and Eating Grapes.

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 14th, 2002. Watching Tapes and Eating Grapes. The Saga Begins. Disciple X stood motionless, sitting still as he watched the large T.V screen, which was placed three feet before him. A large frown on his face, with a plate filled of green and purple grapes. He sighed, ripping off a grape and throwing it into the back of his mouth. Very out of blue for a wrestler of his stance, but honestly, he had nothing better to do. Just two weeks ago, Disciple X was slightly tossed around a bit too much. In the outcome to all of this, he was injured, and was ordered to take 4 weeks from his fighting career in Action! Wrestling. It was all that guys’ fault. You know, the man with the boobs. The man with a waist bigger then… well… anyone else’s. It was ‘The Ace of Spades’ Morg, who injured Disciple X. A lot of things have been going through Disciple X’s mind. He came to Action! To fulfill a dream career, but as of now, it’s turning out to be shit. More shit then Morg shitted last night. Oh shit. It just doesn’t get any shittier then that. “These grapes are fucking good…” Disciple X tried to act interested, but there was no use. He just looked at the bowl, and snarled. “I’ve got to get out of this place…!” Slowly, he lugged his body forward, but then once again, gently laid back. “Where the hell would I go?” He thought to himself, and nothing came to mind. With no intension, he just threw another grape into his mouth, and hit the ‘play’ button on the remote control. “Wow, this should be awesome!” Next to him, beside the plastic bag filled with grapes, laid a tape which said “Join the Army!”. Conrad Ramsey’s face was also, on the cover. It just doesn’t get any worse then that. So there he sat. Eating Tapes, and Watching Grapes. Vice-Versa.
