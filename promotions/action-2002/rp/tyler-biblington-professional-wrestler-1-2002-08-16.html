Tyler Biblington : Professional Wrestler (1)

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“There’s no way he’s even going to last a week inside that very ring.” Ralph Daniels, the head of the scouting department for Ontario Championship Wrestling, said to the other three men sitting at the wooden table near the ring as he quickly scribbled notes into a pad of paper. He watched as Tyler Biblington, a newcomer to the scene of wrestling, was wrestling with OCW premiere worker, Lunacy. Lunacy easily stood a foot taller and had about 100 pounds on the kid inside the ring with him. No matter how many times Babylon would try to mount an offence, Lunacy would just overpower him. “The kid’s got fight in him though. Look at this! No matter how many times Lunacy sends him down, he’s right back up to his feet. This right here is our underdog, the guy who the fans are going to want to root for.” Landon Walsh, VP of OCW, liked what he saw in Biblington. He saw something that no one else could see and thought this was a good choice for the new Lightweight Division that was soon to be introduced. OCW was getting ready to head to the States for the first time and the new division would add the extra spark that was needed. The third man who sat at the table remained silent. Peter Atkinson, the current OCW Heavyweight Champion, had a lot of say in how the promotion was run. His arrogance was always present and had endless lists of all the faults of every wrestler who was trying out. The first thing on his list for Tyler Babylon was underlined and in bold letters. ‘Too Small!” Biblington would be the last try-out of the day and Atkinson had already seen enough. He got up shaking his head and left the table with nothing to say. Walsh looked up and watched him leave. “I can’t stand this guy anymore! The only thing he does is bitch about this and that.” Daniels quickly finished scribbling a couple last notes. “What can we do? He’s daddy’s little boy. If he doesn’t like how his matches end, he changes them. If we even thought of screwing him over, our asses would be out of here.” “I think we have a winner here.” Walsh was quick to change the subject from getting any worse. He knew the two could go on forever about Atkinson and didn’t want things to get out of hand. Besides, there was the small Biblington in the ring who was just about to finish the match. Daniels was as quick to differ, “I don’t know. The guy is just way too small, is he even 150 pounds? I don’t need the risks of him getting seriously hurt in the ring. Something goes wrong and there goes the business, it goes right down the drain.” The two continued to watch as Lunacy lay on the mat and Babylon quickly got up to the top turnbuckle. He leaped off and came crashing down with a frogsplash onto Lunacy. After he hit the move, he rolled around the ring, clutching onto his stomach. He quickly managed to crawl over and cover Lunacy. After about three seconds, Lunacy pushed Biblington off of himself and sat up with a smile on his face. “Nice work, kid.” He said as he was getting to his feet. Biblington smiled and nodded at him in approval of his kind words. “Tyler Biblington, nice work in their kid. We’ll be getting back to you within the hour, so don’t go too far.”
