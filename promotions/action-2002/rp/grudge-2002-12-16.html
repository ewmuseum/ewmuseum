Grudge

16 December 2002

Date: 12/16/02 The cab slowly pulled into the darkened parking lot, navigating around an overturned garbage can. The rain was coming down heavy now; the windshield wipers could barely keep up with the downpour. The cabbie looked through the blurred window as the cab came to a stop in front of the boarded up, abandoned senior high school. His heart was beating hard as he watched a shadow move toward the car. Given the neighborhood and time of night, the cabbie expected to be mugged. Or worse. When the hulking brute of a man squeezed into the back seat of the cab, the Or Worse seemed like a more accurate assessment. He looked like he’d been in a fight. Hopefully he wasn’t looking for another one. 'W-where to?' asked the nervous cabbie. Alex Brody looked up with a tired expression on his face. 'Ramada.' With great relief, the taxi driver headed back toward the more well-traveled thoroughfares. This part of the city was dead, housing nothing more than the scavengers of society. Why on earth would anybody in their right mind come down to this cesspool? What the cabbie didn’t know was that, for Bodybreaker Alex Brody, this used to be home. Or at least, the birthplace of his nightmares. * * * The Bodybreakers were quite pleased with themselves. They'd run roughshot over all the tag team competition in the Southern Wrestling Alliance, and recently they'd expanded their resumes to include assaulting single wrestlers as well. Maybe they worked a little too stiff for some people's tastes. But the bookers weren't complaining; the Bodybreakers were putting fans in the stands. They were rapidly becoming the most hated heels in the company, if they weren't there already. More than once, Alex and David Brody had to be escorted out of the arena under police protection. Fans threw whatever they could get their hands on at the brothers. Riots were starting to become commonplace until the promoter pulled the Brody's aside and told them to tone it down. 'We're just being us,' David would tell them with a smile. 'But we'll try to behave.' 'Try,' Alex added with a smirk. Tonight, the Bodybreakers were planning a 'welcome party' for a newcomer to the SWA. He was a big man, far bigger than anyone else in the promotion. He was also pretty green, but he had a wealth of potential. The promoter saw money in this giant, and planned many great things for him. The Brody’s, however, weren’t quite ready to give up their top position in the company. Especially not to some rookie. As they stood in the gorilla position, Alex and David went over their last minute preparations. 'So you're going to hit him with the chair?' Alex asked his younger brother. 'Yep,' nodded David as he patted the folded steel weapon. 'I'm gonna swing for the hills and welcome the rookie properly.' 'Works for me,' said Alex as he turned back toward the curtain. 'Just don't go overboard. This guy is huge. You don't want to piss him off. Just ring his bell.' 'Yeah yeah.' David waved his brother off. 'I swear, some days you sound just like Mom. Hey, you think I’ll get detention for this?' Tonight they were wrestling in Washington High School. The irony wasn’t lost on the elder Brody. 'Probably. Detention for a month, smartass.' The crowd popped and the bell rang. The match was over, but the festivities were just about to begin. Alex gave his brother a thumbs up. 'Let's do this.' David pulled the curtain aside. 'After you. Age before beauty.' Alex forced his scowl up, fighting off the urge to smile. 'You prick,' he muttered as he stepped through the curtains and began walking down the aisle. In the ring, the giant who called himself Morg slowly turned towards Alex. He’d heard about the antics of the Bodybreakers, and was almost expecting them to show up tonight. The elder Brody started yelling insults at Morg as he slowly walked up the ring stairs. The referee placed restraining hands on Morg's midsection as Alex motioned for the mic. 'Hello Washington High!' he shouted amidst the boos from the crowd. 'Thank you for that warm, heartfelt welcome. I'm surprised you halfwits even knew how to find your way here. Probably the first time you people have even been inside a school, you dumbshits.' With the fans firmly against him, Alex turned his attention back to Morg. 'Hey freakshow, don't think I forgot about you. You've been throwing your weight around for a couple weeks now, acting like you're some kind of big shot. Like you're some kind of big deal. Lemme spell this out for you, in case the air's pretty thin up there: I don't care how tall you are; you're not that big of a deal.' Alex shoved the referee aside, sending the official tumbling under the bottom rope. He began walking around Morg, getting the big man to turn his back to the aisle. 'In fact, I think it's time someone let you in on how things work here in the SWA.' The crowd began booing again as David stepped through the curtains, chair in hand. He began running down the aisle, unbeknownst to Morg. 'You see Morg - ' David slid under the bottom rope, wound up, and slammed the chair across Morg's gigantic back. The big man flinched slightly, but turned slowly toward David. David looked petrified, staring at the ineffective chair. Morg swung a looping right at David, who ducked the blow and smacked Morg again. Unfortunately, David was off-balance when he swung, and put too much effort into the blow. The shot, which was supposed to hit Morg in the head, ended up smacking him in the face. He dropped to one knee, and David planted himself better as he delivered what he hoped to be the 'kill shot'. The chair bent over Morg's head and he fell to the mat. Alex cursed to himself. The chairshot had broken Morg's nose. He knew from the other wrestlers that Morg had a bit of a temper, still being too green and not yet able to keep his emotions in check.. He just hoped that - Before he could finish his thought, Morg had sat up. Alex and David looked horrified as Morg stood up, blood gushing from his broken nose. Alex took a swing at Morg, but the big man grabbed him by the throat and tossed him over the top rope. Distracted, Morg took another chair shot in the back from David. Only this time, Morg didn't sell it at all. Alex looked up from the gymnasium floor. 'He looks pissed,' he said to himself as Morg punched David in the head. David's head snapped back, a nasty welt rising from under his eye. Alex slowly climbed to his knees. David wasn't too happy about getting stiffed like that, and Alex knew that his brother was a bit of a hot head himself. With chair in hand, David swung for the fences as he rattled Morg's cage with yet another chair shot. Morg didn't appear to have even felt the blow. He snatched David up in a bearhug, engulfing the smaller man in his mighty arms. Alex watched in horror as David screamed in pain. Morg crushed the younger Brody in his grasp, shaking David's body like a dog would shake a small chew-toy. The fans were elated as Morg tossed David aside, raising his arms in victory. Morg stepped over the top rope and walked up the aisle, never once turning around. David was clutching at his back, writhing in agony on the mat. The fans pelted him with garbage. Alex rolled into the ring and grabbed his brother's hand. 'David!' he said quietly. 'Are you okay?' David's eyes were watering. 'It's my back,' he whispered in a trembling voice. 'I can't move my legs. He broke my back.' Attendants came down to ringside after seeing that the Brody's weren't leaving the ring. They gingerly rolled David onto a stretcher, the fans cheering louder at the sight of his obvious pain. Alex hated every person in that arena. They didn't know better, but he didn't care. He hated them. And if his brother's back really was broken, Morg was a dead man. They carried David to a waiting ambulance. Alex watched them load his younger brother into the back. 'Is he going to be okay?' asked Morg, an ice pack over his nose. Alex turned around. He didn't say a word. He just lunged at Morg's throat. The two men were quickly separated by security. 'What the Hell's going on back here?' Ross Barton, SWA Owner, demanded. 'That big fucking freak crippled my brother!' shouted an enraged Alex Brody. Ross stepped in front of the restrained Brody. 'That's not how I saw it. What happened out there was an accident. They happen in this business. Look what happened to Tony Consello over there in Florida.' Alex struggled against the restraining arms. 'This wasn't a botched brainbuster Ross. That big fucker lost his cool and deliberately hurt David. You saw it, and you damn well know that's what happened.' Ross pointed a finger at his employee. 'Don't tell me what I do and do not know Alex. You and your brother have been pulling the same kind of crap that I saw in the ring. You jumped Jeff three weeks ago and put him out with a concussion. Two weeks ago you broke Larry's fingers. You guys like to take things too far, and this time it caught up with you.' Alex couldn't believe what he was hearing. How could Ross compare a broken finger to a broken back? 'Are you out of your fucking mind Ross?' Several of the other wrestlers had gathered around the commotion. They all watched in silent disbelief at what was unfolding. No one spoke that way to Ross Barton. Especially not an employee. Ross clenched his jaw. 'Yes Alex, I'm out of my fucking mind. And you're out of a fucking job. Grab your shit, your brother's shit, and get out of my locker room.' Alex shook himself free. He was incensed. A cloudy red mist rolled in front of his eyes. Morg was still restrained, but wasn't struggling. If he was going to be fired, he might as well get his licks in. Alex thrust his leg towards Morg for a superkick, but Ross moved to block it. The foot caught Ross under the chin and snapped his head back, lifting him off the floor. Ross fell to the ground, unconscious. Blood was flowing freely from a nasty gash under his mouth, where his teeth had broken and punched through his bottom lip. His brother was crippled. He'd just accidentally KO’ed his boss. He's lost his job. But all Alex could think about was getting to Morg. He screamed a wild, primordial roar and lunged at Morg again. By this time, the police had arrived at the scene of the skirmish and tackled Alex to the ground. 'You're dead Morg!' shrieked a hysterical Alex Brody from under a pile of police officers. 'You're dead!' * * * The rain tapped against the window in Alex’s hotel room. He sat in the dark and looked out into the night, thinking about the past. Ever since that night in Washington High, his life had been ruined. His brother was a cripple. Brody was blackballed from every independent promotion in North America. Alex had no idea what kind of pull Ross had in the industry, but he learned the hard way. Meanwhile, Morg became a star. Alex was forced to wrestle in small promotions, tiny hardcore feds barely larger than some backyard wrestling leagues. He had to endure gimmicky blood matches against homeless people, junkies looking for a buck, and washed up wrestlers desperate to cling to their waning careers. David was stuck in a wheelchair, forced to pee in a bag or wear diapers. Morg became a star. Alex wanted him dead.
