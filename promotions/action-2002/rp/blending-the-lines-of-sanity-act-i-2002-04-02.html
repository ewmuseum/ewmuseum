Blending the Lines of Sanity, Act I

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04-02-02 I'm sorry Matthews, but you just don't have the size we're looking for in an up-and-coming prospect right now... You need more experience to make up for your lack of size, Matthews, before we can sign you..... Hey, how's it going shorty? 'FUCK OFF!' Tommy Matthews sat up in his bed in a cold sweat, a few moments after screaming that in his sleep. He wiped some of the sweat from his forehead, and quickly ran his hands through his hair. 'Montresor....' was all Matthews uttered as he got up from his bed and walked over to the bathroom. He leaned down over the sink and splashed some water on his face, trying to wake himself up all the way. Those voices still haunted him, despite his attempts to pretend they didn't exist. Tommy ran into the ropes, and leaped up in the air just as his opponent ran at him, leap frogging over his head. As his opponent turned around, Tommy gave him a stiff Sidekick that made an amazingly sickening snapping noise as it hit his opponent's chin. The crowd gasped with amazement as Tommy's opponent hit the ground. Tommy splashed more water on his face, trying to shake off these hurtful memories. He hung his head down, and closed his eyes tightly, almost as if he was trying to squeeze the memories out of his brain. Tommy signaled that he was going up to the top rope, as the small crowd that had packed this small civic center was cheering wildly, hoping to see an amazing manuver. Tommy went up to the top rope, looking down on the opponent he had previously dropped with the Superkick with a certain form of hatred, and leaped off with a Split-Legged Moonsault. The crowd cheered and clapped in amazement as Matthews hooked the leg and got the three count. He went onto his knees as he raised his arms into the air, and the crowd continued to shower him with praise. Tommy knocked a glass to the bathroom floor in frusturation, listening to the sound of it shatter onto the ground. Tommy looked down at the shattered glas, the broken, yet still dangerously sharp, pieces of it. Reminds me of myself. Tommy thought. He picked one of the pieces of the glass up, not caring that it sliced his finger quite a bit. He looked onto it with questionable, yet somehow angry, eyes. 'I want my life back.' Tommy whispered. He suddenly threw the piece of glass to the floor, raised and seperated his arms, and screamed upward. 'CAN YOU GIVE ME MY LIFE BACK?' Tommy quite suddenly sat down on the closed toilet cover, and put his head into his knees. He cried. The fans screamed in adulation for him as Tommy went to the outside. He leaned back onto the barracade, and allowed these excited fans to pat him on his shoulders and back, almost as if they were worshiping him. Fin.
