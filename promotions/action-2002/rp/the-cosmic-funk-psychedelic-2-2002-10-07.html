Action! — Tales

The Cosmic Funk Psychedelic. 2

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Take a step outside yourself And turn around Take a look at who you are It's pretty scary So silly Revolting You're not much You can't do anything” -Turnaround, Nirvana I placed that call to both. Akira was willing to do it if he brought his son along. Morris is more or less backed up with other things for the next week. Morris seemed extremely testy when I called him. Like he knew of what I had done. That’s funny, I never really remembered who the hell that man was. Just some trainer in Tempe, I believe it was. Don’t tell me he is another connector piece to the puzzle. Christ, it seems like I am destined to find piece after piece after my own manic plunge into my subconscious. Anyway, I am noticing a slow attitude change. I am moody, negative, and all that. However, I am starting to become someone else. Someone I used to know. I am becoming what I was when I was mid carding for the IWO. Why am I though? I don’t care about any of this… No. I can’t say that anymore. I just can’t. I can’t lie to myself to shield myself from the fucking truth. I care about everything. I want to become a good wrestler. Hell, a great wrestler. A great worker. I am none of those things, but I am perceived to be. I am the ultimate of all heels. My very existence is enough to make loyalist spit at my direction. I, however, was at one time a hero. A dreamer. A poetic man outside the ring, while violent inside against those who the fans seethed at. At one time thousands adored me. I always received a warm reaction when I raised my arms in the air. The adoration. The glee. The spectacle. The all. I want that again. When I was like that, I had friends. I had fun. I loved to wake up just to call up someone I knew and ask to go fishing with. I used to love the road with my boys. Now, my boys are either gone, or slowly going down the path I already traveled down. A path named self-destruct. Think about it. Anthason. Both Malone’s. Rivers. Jack. Sam. Harmen. One’s dead. Two have disappeared off the face of the planet. And three are in a constant battle for supremacy, while trying to maintain what little sanity they have. They might deny it, but it is almost frenzied. I see a dark cloud on the horizon. A cloud filled with tears, laughing as it pours its emotion over all to see. I picked up the first glass bottle and placed it inside my bag. The stench of alcohol was getting to me.
