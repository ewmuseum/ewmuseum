Cups: cIX: Cup of Life

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/26/02 Cups: cIX: Cup of Life ‘My only surroundings were darkness. For the only light that I had was on the inside. In that moment, and that moment only, is when I realized that I had a light lit in me.' Dazed and distorted, he crawled through the darkness. It seemed like he had a set destination. His navigation was precise and swift in movement as he made his way further and further into the distance.         ‘It was like I was crawling back in time. Trying to find the place where our blood met. Searching through the endless files stored away deep in my memory, I drifted away even more. There was nothing left to remember her by except that bullet.'         James snickered in the eyes of the man dressed similar to what he was.         'So, what party are you going to today?' James asked the man with an eager look in his eyes. The guy seemed to be suspicious about something.         Shakily, the guy backed up and responded. 'Here for back-up. Nothing big at all. I'm friends the birthday boy. I just had to come and with his a happy birthday. You know how it goes. I'm sure.'         'I've got you. Hey, I'll be around. I've got a little bit of entertainment to do if I plan to get paid for this job.' James extended his hand to the man and smiled         'No thanks.' Uneasily, the man directed his attention in the opposite direction.         James balled up his fist and took a few stout swings at the suspicious guy. He fell to the floor and so did his gun. Madison smiled and grasped the gun away from the man.         ‘It ... didn't happen like that. I guess it's not possible to wish these days, yet I knew that even if I did see that in the dream ... it couldn't be changed none the less. When in reality, this is what happened.' 'Hello, sir.' The polite guy shouted up to Joker who was standing in awe watching over the amazement of the birthday party.         'Yes?' James replied sternly and glanced over at the man. 'What?' He retorted in the face of the individual.         'What are you doing here? I thought I was supposed to be the clown for the party.' James extended his middle finger into the face of his brother.         'It's my party now.' James turned and walked away from his nicer brother. No emotions came from his face as he turned and nodded to his kin.         'It's over, James.'         Crack! Crack! Boom! ‘Yeah, that sounds more like it to me. It was over at that time. All I remember from that point on was darkness.' James stood in the darkness and opened his eyes. Finally, he came back to reality. He was about to leave the hospital. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
