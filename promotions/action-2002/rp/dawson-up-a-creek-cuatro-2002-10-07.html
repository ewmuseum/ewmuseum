Dawson: Up A Creek: Cuatro

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sweet Aroma had made it back to the hotel. It was the first time in her Action! career that she didn't travel with her partner in 'CnCrime' wasn't with her. Even before they became a tag team, the two traveled together. But Sweet thought to herself that maybe being alone was for the best. Danger told her on a few occassions about how Angelica Dawson didn't get along with females in gfwaOmega, but Sweety didn't heed the warning. I mean let's be real here, Angelica Dawson somehow procured a pink penguin. PP2 wasn't looking too good when Aroma stepped into the room. Maybe not having PP there saddened it deeply. Aroma was sort of sad that Dawson wasn't there either. However, it was something that she would have to get over. She had a nice career, an incredible man, and her name was all over the internet. A man known to the internet wrestling world as JoSo even stated that Clit 'N' Candy is the number one tag team in the wrestling industry today. Aroma took comfort in those that agreed, but that part of her life was over. Almost over... After looking at PP2 again, Aroma up and left the room. She turned a few corners and came upon a room, the one that the lady at the front desk stated that Bobby Minio and Angelica Dawson were sharing. She knocked a few times, but there was no answer, so she went back to her room and placed a call to the room. The phone rung for an infinite amount of time. Aroma hung up. Aroma hung her head. Aroma laid across her bed. PP2 hopped up onto the bed and waddled on top of Aroma, and layed on the 'Spanish Mamacita's' belly. 'I know, I know; you miss PP. I miss Angelica too.' PP2 jumped off of Aroma and disappeared for what felt like hours to Sweet Aroma, but they were just mere seconds in her life. Seconds that she spent reminiscing about her and her former Dyad Championship Partner. PP2 jumped back onto the bed, and in its beak was the A!W Magazine with Sweet Aroma and Angelica Dawson gracing the cover. She flipped through, seeing pictures of her in her match against Syphon Fission, Angelica Dawson pictures of her in her debut against 'Airborn' Tyler. Yet more pictures followed, Max Danger and Angelica Dawson against Sweet Aroma and Conrad Ramsey, Danger and Dawson against Aroma and Bobby Minio, Aroma and Dawson running down during the Danger/Ramsey match at Down '4' The Count, and ending with Aroma and Dawson holding the Dyad Titles in their possession while Danger and Ramsey were grounded. 'I have to find Angelica, I just have to find her.' PP2 started jumping for joy. For when Sweet Aroma find Angelica Dawson, PP2 finds PP.
