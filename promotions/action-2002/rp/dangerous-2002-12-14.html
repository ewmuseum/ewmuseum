Action! — Tales

Dangerous

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/14/02 The gym. An intrical part of a wrestler’s training. You had to maintain a certain level of physical fitness to be able to survive, let alone compete, in the business. Where being slammed against the ground, through tables, and being battered with steel chairs and steps were commonplace, your physicality was what helped you to make it through to the next day. On the weight bench, two men worked out with intensity. Two men who were near the upper limit of what the human body could be developed to. Translation: both buggers were freakin’ huge. To them, the gym wasn’t just a place to go to stay in shape. It was a shrine of steel, and both men were avid worshippers. Kodiak Vic Creed stood at the head of the bench, shifting the bar along the rack to make sure both sides were balanced. With over five hundred pounds of weight on the bar, a poor lift out of the rack could result in injury. “You ready, you little pussy?” asked Vic with a sarcastic smile. Alex Brody gripped the bar. “Maybe I should do this by myself. I wouldn’t want you to break a nail helping me get the bar out of the rack.” Vic lightly placed his hands inside Brody’s grip and helped him clear the rack.. He removed his hands and Alex held the bar up, looking at either side. With a sharp exhale, Alex lowered the weight to his chest and thrust it up again. Vic watched Alex perform repetition after repetition. The man was focused, no question about it. For most of his adult life, Alex was driven with an angry intensity. He was perpetually pissed off, but always managed to channel his anger into whatever he was doing. Weight training, sparring, fighting, Brody always did it at full force and head on. The weight slammed back into the rack. The clanging weights caused more than one head to turn and stare. “Your turn, sugar,” challenged Brody as he stood up. People began turning away as Creed leveled a harsh stare at them. He replaced Alex on the bench and tested his grip on the bar. Brody stood at the head of the bench, looking down at Creed. “I’ve got this,” Creed told him. Brody shrugged and stepped back. It wasn’t out of bravado that Creed didn’t want Alex touching the weight. It was out of preservation. He didn’t trust Alex as far as he could throw him. Years earlier, Kodiak Vic Creed and Bodybreaker Alex Brody had been locked in a heated feud. The blow off match came, and resulted with Vic lifting the corner of the wrestling ring and dropping it on Brody’s hand, crushing it. It took Alex several months of surgery and therapy to regain the use of his hand, but he never forgot that savage match. “Worried I might drop the bar across your throat?” Alex asked as he toweled the sweat off his arms. “Not hardly,” Creed lied. Alex Brody was more than a little unstable. Bat-shit crazy would be more accurate. Which was why Vic was so hesitant when Brody showed up at Vic’s training facility at the beginning of the summer. Creed had started training wrestling hopefuls, working them so hard that only the most dedicated remained at the end. Alex didn’t join because he needed wrestling lessons. He joined to take the worst punishment Vic could dish out, and ask for seconds. He joined to show up the Main Man. Vic almost respected that. Deep down, Vic suspected there was another reason that Brody had resurfaced from whatever hole he’d been hiding in. It wasn’t until Action had contracted KVC that it became clear. Brody not only wanted to show up the Main Man. He wanted to use him to get revenge. Now that, Creed fully respected. For some reason, Alex had a serious hate-on for Morg. The only reason Vic agreed to show up in Action was to help lure the big man out into the open. Once he did, the Bodybreaker made his move. Vic had no idea what Brody’s plans were, and didn’t really want to know. That could make him an accessory. “You gonna work out?” Alex asked. “Or are you just gonna stare at the weights?” 'You've got somewhere else you have to be?' Vic muscled the bar into position and began hammering out repetitions. Brody was big, strong, fast, and crazy. A combination that, under normal circumstances, would be dangerous. In wrestling, it was a formula for success. Vic just hoped that he’d be able to say, one day, that he’d trained a champion. Not a murderer.
