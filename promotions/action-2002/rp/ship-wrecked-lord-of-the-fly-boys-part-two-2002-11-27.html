Ship Wrecked: Lord of the Fly Boys - Part Two

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 27th, 2002 'Intruders!' Panic. Quick do something. Heart attack. Half naked children running around with rusty sporks. One with a giant backpack shell approaches Nate. 'Hi,' the little boy said. 'WAZZZUP!' The boy looked at Nate with a high five and scratched his head. 'Right mate, listen. Now that you're on my part of the island you're gonna have to listen to some rules okay?' he said in an English accent. 'Oh! Oh! Oh! Does he have any food?' said a fat little boy. He ran at Steve-O sniffing his pants and Steve-O slaps him. 'Get away!' Steve-O screamed. 'My munchies.' 'Where's the grown ups?' Nate asked. 'Grown ups?' whispered children in the background. The boy with the shell spoke again. 'You seem to be the oldest ones. And you look like smart lads, so here, you can be leader. You control the conch!' Everyone bowed before Nate's feet as the boy threw his shell backpack into Nate's arms. 'My name is Ralphie. I am at your service leader.' 'And my name is Pigney!' said the fat little boy. He rubbed his glasses and put them back on his face. 'My spectacles can create fire, and they are yours for your service!' 'Hail the new leader!' Ralph said. 'What is your name?' Nate scratched his chest, then his head. 'Master Nate.' 'HAIL MASTER NATE! HAIL MASTER NATE!' 'Dude,' Nate said as he looked around at everyone bowing before him. 'Steve-O, we're Gods!'
