The Beta: Ejected From A Car: 3

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Look at me. Be me. Drink from the river that is me. By some token, I won the first battle of this war. Joey Malone knows different. I KNOW DIFFERENT! Everyone thinks that the gun was all an angle. A joke. Some lie. They still ate it all up like some Italian cuisine. I have found no identity for myself. I have asked the questions when I get into the battleground. Who the hell am I? The questions are answered with questions, coming up with mangled ignorance that confuses me and leaves me dead. I am dead inside, you know. I hate who and what I am. I always look around as the world erodes, crumbling pieces of soil and shrinking the whole picture. I am lost in this deteriorating environment. My hatred for all people makes my stomach burn. I am crumbling, just like the walls of Berlin. Just like all other empires. And the weak see their chance. I need to fake my enjoyment of things not having to deal with Joey Malone and Keri Lindum. Every time they smile, I cry. Every time they cry, I smile. Every time I let them win, I go home. I shattered a mirror in my house with my fist. I bled ferociously. The shards of glass are still covering my floor, in large pieces. There is no reason to clean up my home, even though I can’t call it that. I am never there as it is. Every time I screw up my PLAN I pick up one of those shards of glass. I come close to cutting a vital part of my body, but lay the shard back down. I have the counter to what I have done written in a notepad. I always stare at those shards, showing me a reflection of what I am. An ugly, bruised, tortured, unkempt waste. When I put Payback in my hands, all that goes away. I feel warmth. My pillar of stability. Payback is my aptly named tool of destruction. Everyone fears Payback in Action Wrestling. They fear me. I used to have people talk to me. I used to share stories. I used to be loved. I used to. The only thing that could get me off now is a bullet, ripping the flesh of the Malone’s. Leaving them a bloody heap of waste. Of nothing. Just like me. The future that he robbed me, that’s what he thinks was my ego. I am crossing the line in his eyes. I am robbing him of his future. Of his life. That is something he can’t stand. He just is like a cheap trick bastard. He takes what he can, and then runs off before you can reclaim what he stole. I am currently wearing my pair of Hawaiian cargo shorts and my white beater, still wiping the sweat off my forehead after an intense conflict. I now go back into my mental prison, only to be unleashed in a weeks time. LLB, I am comfortable. I know you had another classic blackout moment thanks to Payback. Tell me Roland, did I scare you? Did I beat your brain and body up enough to make you understand? Those guys in the Asylum can’t fight like me Roland. Inmate, Nerva, Angel Dalton, KENNY ROCK, Ender, and Caprino. Those guys couldn’t beat your ass like I can. I am two hundred eighty pounds of muscle. I am more agile then that crew. I am stronger. And I will never stop. No matter what. They beat people for sport. I beat people to hurt them. I am not going to injure you for sport. I am going to injure you to satisfy my carving for destruction. I saw his eyes rolling to the back of his head. I saw the blood. I saw the carnage. It doesn’t matter though. I am the real GOD OF FIGHT! There have been many victims of Payback, and all were the same. They were all fodder for the greater cause down the road. Who cares? It is all so fucking funny, watching piece after piece fall in place. I am gaining more muscle mass by the hour. I am not as lean as I once was, but I do not care. I just need something to power these engines. I need something to give power to my body. To my soul. I have no identity. I can be any multitude of people. Rashard Clark. Kyle Gass. Michael Jordan. Kenneth Rockefeller. Jesus Christ. Diablo. Satan. I go under the name Syphon Fission. I am a lost soul in this vessel. I would cry, but I no longer have tears to shed. This is my war. THIS IS NOT ME! SHUT UP! Joey has a copy of the contract at his home. Good. He will have to read it as well. The English translation that is. I get out of my limo, and look at this bouncer before a nightclub. He is smaller then me, and he walks to the side. He is not going to try to get in my way. The limo will wait for whenever I comeback. I left my gun and Payback hidden in the trunk. I walk in and see the vibrant colors taking assault on my twitching left eye. My face is under some make up, of course. It has not fully healed from Malone’s redemption. I am surrounded by young women. They dance with me. They love me. They want me. They offer me sex, their lives, their minds. Quinn is so far away. She won’t know a thing. This is my world. My love. My gun.
