I Love You

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Conrad Ramsey had left Max Danger in the arms of his wife, Jessica. He had just revealed to 'The Danger Man' that he loved his mother, Samantha Dox. The old man walked faster than he had walked in years. At least since his early fifties. He rounded corner after corner until he had reached his lockerroom. When he entered, Samantha spun around and looked at him. She said, 'Are we leaving, then?' Ramsey nodded. 'Good,' she said. 'It's just... I really need to get out of here.' She looked unnerved, like she was about to go off on someone. Of course slapping her son and his wife normally would indicate 'going off', but she apparently had held back. Conrad just nodded his head, while smiling. 'What?' Samantha asked. Ramsey shook his head. Samantha just shook her head slightly and began to gather up her things. She had her back to the former Bantam Champion when he walked up to her. She grabbed her purse and another bag and then turned. Conrad was standing there, denture's shining brightly. He grabbed her shoulders and looked into her eyes. 'What's wrong with you? What happened while you were gone?' 'Ran into your son and,' he said, but she quickly cut him off. 'I don't want to speak of him again tonight.' 'Me either,' Ramsey told her. But he didn't let go of her as she tried to get them on their way. He looked deeper into her eyes. She looked into his. 'I need to tell you something. Your son actually helped me to realize something.' Samantha looked a bit stand-offish. Anything learned from Marcus probably wasn't going to be a good thing. But she listened to what her boyfriend had to say. 'I love you,' Ramsey said. She almost instantly began to cry. She quickly replied, 'I love you, too.' The two then embraced. She was crying happily. They shared a kiss and then Conrad said, 'But that isn't all.' 'It's enough though,' Samantha assured him. He just shook his head. 'I thought about this, in the five minutes it took me to return here. I thought, I could go on a long-winded spiel about how I've searched my life for that special someone. Tell you that I never thought I'd find that person at my age. That I figured I would die a lonely, old man. But I don't think you will.' 'But instead you'll give me a long-winded spiel about why you shouldn't give me a long-winded spiel?' she said with a mocking smile. 'Will you marry me?' he said flatly. He had left it all out there. Samantha just stared at him. Tears streamed down her cheeks. Her hands began to shake. She then wrapped her arms around Ramsey's neck, 'Yes!' The two held each other. Their love poured out of them, and engulfed each other while they embraced. They kissed a second time. A long, passionate kiss.
