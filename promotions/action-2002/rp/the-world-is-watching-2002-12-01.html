The World is Watching.

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/01/02 That’s what they said. Because one week ago, Reed Young had brought back a “dead” Sean Cylear. And why? WHY THE HELL WOULD REED YOUNG DO SOMETHING LIKE THAT!? THAT’S SO OLD... SO USED... SO RUN-OF-THE-MILL THESE DAYS!! The answer was simple. Ratings. Of course! It all makes sense now! Reed Young sat down on “Wrestling Planet” like the old days. He sat there, and he typed and he typed and he typed and he typed. He told those Fans just what he thought of them. No, fWo sucked. Bottom line. PIW always blew. And the Asylum... well it was going to fold in, um, three more days. Promise. Reed Young didn’t care that some people did not like the Virus Match (well, actually he did, he posted about 10 messages saying he was going to close Action Wrestling, but then remembered he brought back Sean Cylear, so it was all good again), and he didn’t care people thought Sean Cylear’s “death” was stupid. Because, dammit, they hadn’t heard the story yet! Wait, did Reed actually have a story? SHIT! Oh yeah, silly Reed, Sean told him it. It was brilliant. It really was. So there Reed Young sat, in front of Wrestling Planet, yelling at the Fans as he ran tomorrow night through his head. The world, would be watching. Watching... to know the truth about Sean Cylear. And at that moment, Reed Young grinned. It was an evil grin... a grin that told the world, he was ready. For tomorrow night, he’d walk to the ring with ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear... tell the story, and begin the start of an utter hell for all of Action Wrestling to swallow. Or... spit. It all depended which one you liked to do better. So, again, there Reed Young sat... on Wrestling Planet, yelling at the Fans for whatever reason. And tomorrow, at this time... he’d be in the ring, with Cylear... Bring home ratings. God never gave a bigger asshole more power in his life. Joe Crambell who?
