Same 'Ole Shit. Seven

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tuesday, January 21st, 2003 Donell paced through the halls backstage, clutching his abdomen. He passed his locker room, but that didn’t matter. He went to the check-in station, grabbed his father’s keys, and headed to the garage. AS he walked faster, it became apparent that he wasn’t holding his abdomen, but his arm. Donell didn’t stay for Siobhan, she left right after the match. He didn’t stay for Julius, who was probably just realizing that Donell was missing. Donell hopped in the rented Acura, quickly started it, and backed out of his space. In a quick moment, he sped out of the garage, under the watchful eye of Julius, standing at the Private entrance to the Arena. Donell quickly turned off of State Street and on to Northern Avenue, the pain in his arm became too much that he had to hold it close to his body while he used the other arm for steering. The light ahead had just turned yellow, and he was far too back to make it, even for 57 miles per hour. He accelerated just a bit more and almost hit the Explorer trying to turn left from the opposite direction. Red. Ran. It’s good a cop wasn’t there. On the right, Donell saw a small gang of young kids, about 17 and 18, waiting at the bus stop. Donell pulled in the junction where the bus made its pick-up, and rolled down the opposite window. “You guys sell?” A kid in a Raiders Beenie put out the cigarette he was smoking and put a tooth pick in his mouth. He approached the window while the others crowded around him. “How much’oo wan?” “Just a sack...” Donell opened the seat compartment and pulled out a bill: Benjamin Franklin. He handed it to the kid, who pocketed it, and gave Donell a white rock tied in a sack with change of a $50. Donell sped off while the kids celebrated their impromptu deal.
