Fox “Finally some relaxation!” Then the phone rings! With Pikachu laying on the other bed Chris snorts his wishes! Fox “Pikachu! Get the damn phone!” RING!!!!! Fox “PIKACHU!” RING!!! The base of the phone then falls as Fox opens the door up, thus hitting the bed Pikachu was on and Pikachu falling again. But knowing the number, Chris speaks up. Fox “316.” Manager “Ok, it’ll be about ten minutes.” “Hanging” up the phone, Chris drops it, letting it hit the ground. He then turns to see Pikachu beside it and starts to get irate! Fox “Errrrrr! Ding dong, the door bell rang, and as Fox turned to go toward it he slipped in the wiring of the phone and fell flat on his face! Then un tangling himself and brushing off some dirt, Fox grabs Pikachu and runs out of the room almost hitting the Manager with the Pizza! Manager “Hey, I got your Pizza!” Fox “Check pwease or whatever those movie stars say! I gots to go.... BIG TIME!” With Fox running to his car he drops Pikachu! And tired from sprinting Fox slowly picks Pika up and scoots to the Geo! Hahahaha, The sound of laughter is in the air as the scene shows a bunch of nice families sitting in a TGI Fridays restaurant. Then the scene shows LLB checking his watch constantly as Chris Fear gnawing on a French fry half awake. The scene switches again showing Chris Fox fiercely driving his red Geo into the TGI parking lot! Frantically finding a spot, Chris does! He leaves the car and rolls the window down a bit, hey Pikachu has to breathe too! Chris bursts into TGI and blurts out whatever he said the quickest. LLB “Yo Fox, over here!” Wiping sweat off his forehead Chris fixes his shirt and walks over. He takes a seat in another leather chair. Fear “Where you been?” Fox “I said I was cumming! Dude! Its ok, I’m here!” LLB “Yes. Now about this Wednesday, Chris.... you have an idea?” Finishing biting his hang nail Chris sits up straight and clears his throat. Fox “Yeah, I do like that new Pizza Hut commercial, especially when that Doug guy’s eating the Pizza! That’s so funny!” Fear “Hey! You called this meeting! Now speak!” Chris leans back in his chair and pull out his new Game Boy Advance, and starts to play Pokemon. The waitress walks by. Waitress “Are you ready to order?” Fox “Steak.” (a name of Fox’s Pokemon) Not realizing what he said Chris plays on. LLB “I’ll have the Halibut and Fries and uh just give Chris here an Apple Juice!” Fear “I’ll have the TGI Rib platter please.” Waitress “Ok, I’ll be back!” Fox “STEAK!” Waitress “Uh, ok.” After a few minutes, LLB starts the conversation again. LLB “Hey Fox listen, we have an idea, it’s about...” Chris cuts LLB off! Fox “Good, sounds great, where do I sign?” LLB turns his head in disgust! Chris Fear sits back and has a good laugh! LLB “What’s so damn funny?” Fear “Ha! You guys are fucking funny! Is this how he always was in PIW?” LLB gulps and answers. LLB “Yes, what’s it to you?” Fox “POOCHY!” LLB “What the hell is wrong with you?” Chris finally listens and hits pause on his game. Fox “Bros, my Bulbasaur just evolved! I call him Poochy! And then before that my Charmander or Steak as I call him evolved too!” With a stunned look on his face LLB glances over and sees the waitress coming toward the table with the food!” Waitress “Here you are guys.” Giving Fear his Ribs and LLB his halibut the waitress asks Fox something. Waitress “I’m sorry sir, we have no more steak. What else would you like?” By this time Chris was back into his game, and yes, this time his Squirtle evolved. Fox “Oyster! Oh I knew it! OYSTER!” Waitress “That we have plenty of! I’ll be right back!” Fox “Darn right!” Saving the game Chris turns it off. Fox “Ah that was good! Ok now I’ll have the apple juice pwease! Lady? Where’d you go?” LLB “She went to get you Oysters. Anyways shut up, we have to get a plan.” Fox “Pan? I’ll go buy one! See ya!” Fox storms out of TGI’s and runs into his car. Driving off Chris Fear finishes his meal and leaves. Fear “Uh, you got money right? Hey thanks! Bye!” LLB “But I don’t have money! Fox was suppose to pay!” FADE OUT .... FADE IN Fox “Oh well, looks like I got a pan now.” Chris turns back to his car and drives out of the TGI parking lot. Fox “It’s amazing that I’m gone for only three hours and yet they leave before I came back! Oh well, hey... look a red light!” Brrrrroooooooooooommmmmmmmmm Accelerating through the red light, the Geo swivels onward into unknown adventures. CLOSE
