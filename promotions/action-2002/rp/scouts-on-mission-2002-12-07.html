Scouts on Mission.

7 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They had sat back in their chairs, just relaxing after their half an hour hike in the woods. It was a good hike. They were able to see a bluebird and a whole nest full of beavers in the damn. Hell, Bill even found himself a brilliantly looking hiking stick. It was so good, that all of the other scouts had to use it. They just had to. Two of them even tried to fight it away from Bill during the hike, but luckily for him Scott, his bestest scouting friend was there to save the day. So, overall, it was a good hike. Scott closed his eyelids... he needed the rest. After you stayed up for a whole night earning your “shoe tying” badge, you were pretty tired after making breakfast and taking a half of an hour hike through the woods. Bill, on the other hand... well he just kinda sat there, with his hiking stick in hand. It really was a good stick. So thick... so brown... any scout would pay at least $50,000 bucks on ebay for it. At least. Anyway... they just sat there, resting. It had been a long day already. Minutes passed, as Bill set his hiking stick on the ground. For he, too, had just earned his “shoe tying” badge, and it was time to check it off in the book of badges that he always carried along with him. He flipped the pages... as he could only sigh. Scouting was getting too easy for him. The shoe tying badge... to what now? As the pages flipped, he and his best friend Scott had basically done it all. They were always the first ones to set out, and the first ones to accomplish. That’s when he saw it. Page six hundred and ninety-two. Column eight. Row ten. The wrestling badge. And it didn’t have a check mark. Bill had to do it. Tapping Scott on the shoulder, he began to open his eyes... and look over at Bill, whom held the book in front of him. “We...” He said slowly, making sure that Scout Master Scott was fully awake. “Are going to get that.” Fainting back to his sleeping position, Scott couldn’t believe what Bill had just said.
