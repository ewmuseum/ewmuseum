Ow! My Aching Back!

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He was laying in a bathtub full of icy cold water. That was how Max Danger had spent his days off since facing Keith Scott Zimmerman on Monday at Pressure Point. His back was killing him. It ached in places Max didn't even know were parts of the back. For the last few weeks 'The Danger Man' had been wading in the chilling waters of his bathroom. Each night he would go out to the ring, in front of thousands and thousands of Fans(not including all those goddamned marks), and put his body on the line. All the athletes in Action! did, but it seemed as if the current Carnage Champion would go a little further than the rest. He didn't show it in matches. But most wrestlers tended to not notice most things when in the heat of the battle. Your body won't allow for the pain to register. But once it's over it just hurt. What did he have to show for it? The terrible condition of his back. He hadn't gone to the doctor, nor would he. He just kept going. He'd wrestle, come home, soak, and then do it all over again(with a few stops off in the bedroom along the way *wink wink, nudge nudge*). So there he was, taking a dip. Relieving himself of the pain for a few hours. He would be joined by his wife from time to time, normally just to bring him something to drink(always a ice-cold Sunkist right from the can). This time, though, she sat down at the side of the tub. 'You're going to have to get it checked out, baby,' she said concernly. He didn't open his eyes. He just shook his head. He had refused repeatedly on going to the doctor. 'It'll work itself out,' Max would always say. Jessica the asked, 'So I guess you're going to go through with the cage match, then?' Danger nodded, 'If the old man accepts, then yes, I will.' Jessica sighed and looked down at her lap, where her hands were clinched together. Xander then opened his eyes and saw his wife's head hanging. He shifted in the tub and then put his arm around his wife's shoulders. 'It'll be alright, honey,' he said. He then turned her face to look at his, 'Tell ya what. Assuming Conrad accepts the challenge, the next week, I won't wrestle. I'll take it easy. Okay?' She slowly nodded. 'That's my girl!' He then kissed her on the forehead and started to get up. No, it wasn't easy. Jessica got to her feet and helped her husband onto his. He climbed out of the tub and into the warm towel his wife provided. She wrapped it around his waist as he took a second one and through it over his head and let it rest on his shoulders. Together, they scooted out of the bathroom.
