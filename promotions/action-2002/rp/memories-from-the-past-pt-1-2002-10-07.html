Memories from the Past pt. 1

7 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 7th, 2002 September 5th, 1994 Adolescence is a tough time for a teenage boy, the need to fit and be liked, and the sudden worry about clothing and your own personal look. Thoughts about the opposite sex starting to peek its way into their young minds. Until about halfway through his senior year in high school, Jeff Garvin was just another casualty of being one of the unpopular nobodies stuck in the shadows of a shabby, middle school dance. It was nine thirty and the night was winding down, the disc jockey was playing the last few songs in his collections. On the outskirts of the gymnasium, hidden away from the jocks, punks, and Goths stood the unlucky few of outsiders. The socially challenged, as they liked to refer to themselves as, nerds to everyone else. They sat on the bench, dressed in their Sunday’s best: black slacks riding up to their chest, button up shirts complete with pocket protector, and glasses. That was general look about them. Leaning back against the wall, checking his breath. Jeff had on his tight blue jeans, tight white shirt, and his Uncle Ron’s old leather jacket that he often wore down to the ring during his wrestling matches. Though much too big for him, coming off as if he bought it at a second hand store. Achieving the full affect that they had all been dubbed: losers. Jeff picked a piece of chicken from one the brackets in his braces and gave a deep sigh, staring out at all the other children. At the age of fifteen, Jeff had already experienced the wrath of his peers. The countless wedgies and swirlies came to mind. And even the legendary atomic wedgy that took place only last year. A moment he would never forget. As usual, Frank, Gordie, Mellissa, Kyle, and himself pretty much followed the same old routine; keep to them selves and not disturb the others. Thus not giving them the chance to make a mockery of them. That’s how it had been for the last two years, and that’s how it would most likely stay. “Yeah, so I was thinking, we could skip the rest of this nonsense and go and play some Dungeons and Dragons. We were almost at a heated battle between Warlock Garvin and Warrior Frankie. I’m itching to see how that epic turns out.” Gordie blurted out, snorting as he laughed. The others nodded, the last slow song of the night had cued up and the gymnasium was now almost completely pitch black. “Jeff!” Mellissa called, snapping her fingers in front of Jeff’s face. His eyes were locked on Meagan, the beauty of their school. She was his dream girl, way out of his league and he knew it. Fresh off of her break up with Nick, official ass kicker and captain of the school wrestling team. Wrestling; the only thing Jeff loved more than Meagan. His Uncle Ron spent countless hours teaching him the basics of amateur wrestling, he had the goods it would have taken to get on the team and even possibly knock Nick from his pedestal. Yeah, like that would ever happen. Imagine the medical and dental bills that would follow, quite simply, he’d have to pick out his own tombstone a whole week before the annual tryouts. It was the beginning of the school year though, his final year of middle school, and for some reason, maybe it was he had a death wish, or he was inhaling some of the smoke from the various joints seeping from one side of the gym to the other, but he wanted Meagan that much more. She was single and was ‘highly’ developed. “That’s it, men… I’m going in.” “Where?” Frank spoke up, before realising where Jeff’s line of vision was directed at. “Oh, no Jeff. I know you’re a level seventy two magic user and the most experienced swords men to ever grace the game that is…” He paused, allowing the others to finish it with him, highly over dramatic. “D&D.” They all proceeded to fish out their own character’s ID card and flash it in the air with their eyes closed. But when they opened them, their friend was already headed straight for Meagan… “Looks like we’re going to have to find a new warlock.” “Yep.” To Be Continued…
