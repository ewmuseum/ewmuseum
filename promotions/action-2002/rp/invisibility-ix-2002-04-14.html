Invisibility - IX

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Original: 07/24/2001. Rewrite: 04/14/2002 July 20th, 2001 Malone Residence: Mesa, Arizona, United States. And like that, Keri was back where she started. The hill. 'That accident...' Keri whispered. She wasn't sure what to believe. 'Yes. When you came to, you couldn't remember anything from your life for the last three years. You remembered your education, so you could graduate, but you couldn't remember who Joey was, who Rashard was, or who anyone was. It was an extremely unusual case of amnesia.' Bonham said. 'Joey was devastated.' he added. 'The two of you basically broke up, because your father never liked Joey to begin with, and told you that he wasn't good enough for you. Joey could've been with anyone in his life, he could've been married long ago. Believe me, I would know this. The thing is, he never wanted anyone else in his life except you, so all of his relationships after this didn't last.' 'He was in such pain after that accident. Not physical, but mental. It hurt him worse than when Shawn Arrows threw him off that scaffold, or when he wrecked his knee. I don't know how he survived these last seven years, but when he met you again, and you two started dating, he was the happiest guy in the world.' Bonham said. 'He was happy to be with you again.' There was an awkward pause. 'Christ. If Jimmy had this material, he'd be able to buy the whole bloody country.' Keri was confused. 'But what do I do about his behaviour?' Bonham chuckled and turned around, about to walk away. 'That's your problem. I am just the messenger.' he said, as he began to walk. 'You have two people to support. You have to support Joey through his time in need, and you have to support Anthason for what he'll have to do.' And with that, he walked away. Keri turned around, and saw Hendrix standing there. 'Ah, so the wanker's finally left you.' 'What do you want? I thought you were the evil one.' Keri inquired. 'I am.' Hendrix said with a smile. 'But see, you have a bond with that guy. Joey, I mean. You wouldn't even be meeting us if you didn't have it. So, you had better take care of that guy. For our sake.' Keri smiled, even though she was beginning to cry. 'I will.' Keri. 'Uhm, but if you die any time soon... uh...' Hendrix started. Keri... 'Put in a good word for us... won't you?' Hendrix finished, elbowing Keri in the ribs to try and coax her into agreeing. Keri? 'Sure.' Keri said. 'I guess.' Keri?! The world began shaking around Keri. Hendrix began to curse randomly as the shaking grew more and more fierce, until Keri was knocked out of the world, entirely. Back into reality. She had come to with Joey shaking her awake, at eight o'clock in the morning. 'Keri?' he asked for what seemed like the billionth time. 'I'm here, I'm here...' Keri said, sleepilly. The dream she had just had was still fresh on her mind, as she looked up at her boyfriend. She tried to say something, but what she wanted to say couldn't come out. 'Keri, why are you crying?' Joey asked. 'I'm crying? I... guess I am.' Joey got out of the bed and was heading for the door. 'Well, I'm going to make breakfast. Care to join me?' Keri nodded, and Joey left. Just when what she wanted to say came to her. '...I love you.' -- Nine days later, Joey Malone fought Kent Anthason for the Internet Wrestling Organization World Championship, in the fabled 'Life, Death, and Endurance' match. The bout lasted over an hour. In the gimmicky, tower-like structure that served as the bout's overtime, Joey Malone chose to save Keri's life over retaining the championship. With Anthason as the new champion, Julie left Joey's life, never to manipulate or use him again. Joey proposed to Keri Lindum a month later. Despite financial problems and difficulties in finding a job, Joey and Keri were married on February 3rd, 2002. The marriage took place just outside of Bank One Ballpark, in front of many of their closest friends. Simon Seaman was the best man. Rashard Clark could not make it. But neverthless, the newlyweds became Action Wrestling's favourite couple. This was their story. Now the story of how their relationship holds up begins. -fin-
