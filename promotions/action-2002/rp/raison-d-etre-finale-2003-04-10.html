Raison D'etre: Finale

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Let's recap our favorite Kleptomaniac for just a moment. Coral Avalon, age twenty-one, was a wrestler working for Action! Wrestling. In order to get there, the young Kleptomaniac had to go through quite a few trials and tribulations, and his decision to go to Action! Wrestling had nothing to do with the wrestling aspect of his work. Rather, he came here because of his love for Allison Lindum, as that was the main cause for his firing from the IWO last year, due to his missing some dates. Now, Coral Avalon's journey has been full of twists and turns. He tried to keep his rich kid past a bit of a secret, a secret that only Joey Malone and Kent Anthason knew. His relationship with Allison Lindum was no secret, though, and it was becoming entirely difficult to keep his head on straight around her. The decision to become his manager was hers and hers alone, but Coral Avalon hadn't exactly had the best luck since then, frequently losing, frequently getting hurt, and frequently watching Allison get hurt. Also mixing things up was the fact that all he really wanted to do was do something meaningful with his family, and have them accept him. So far, after the incident at Blood, Sweat, and Tears where his older brother, Elvis, was thrown out of the building because he tried to run into the ring to smash in Daniel Phillips' brains. So, anyway, the point is that Coral Avalon had once again returned to the Avalon family home, on the outskirts of New Orleans, Louisiana, along with his girlfriend, best friend, and manager, Allison Lindum. It had been a day since Joey Malone came here, and now he had a bit of a chance to see how he was doing, since Pressure Point 54 was four whole days away. Coral dressed as casually as possible just to annoy his family, wearing his new 'Steal This' t-shirt and a pair of jeans. Allison wore her favorite black dress for the occasion, mainly for the complete opposite reasons for Coral's choice in attire, so that she didn't annoy Coral's family again. 'You think that either of them are still alive after meeting one another for the first time in three months?' Coral asked Allison. Coral was first surprised to see his little sister, the quasi-gothic, depressing-looking, seventeen-year old girl that he had always known, wearing a white tanktop, blue jeans, and no dark makeup. She looked completely different as usual, and he actually had to question if it was, indeed, his little sister. 'Lily?' 'You're late.' she told him with a smirk on her face. 'What?' Coral asked, really confused at the moment. Lily simply smirked at him again and lead the two of them into what would be Coral Avalon's bedroom. Coral's old bedroom was largely unchanged since Coral moved out of the house two years ago. There was a wall that was entirely made of shelving, and it held a huge amount of wrestling tapes. There was an old television sitting in the middle of the room, and a table directly across from it. This room faced the north, so the sun never directly beamed into the room. However, the more interesting feature of the room wasn't the furniture in it, rather, the two people that happened to be on Coral's bed. Don't worry, they're still fully clothed. Keri Malone laid on the bed, curled up while Joey Malone was directly behind her with an arm over her stomach, and both were seemingly sleeping soundly. 'They spent the whole night just talking to each other. Keri fell asleep first, and he just simply joined her.' Lily said, quietly, not wishing the disturb them. Coral opened his mouth to say something, but before he could, Lily grabbed him by the collar of his shirt, and grabbed Allison by her left wrist, and pulled them out of the room. She let go of Allison to shut the door, before she turned to Coral. 'What?' Coral asked in response to this. 'I don't want you to wake them. They had a long night of working out their problems, and this is the first time they've had to rest. You should give them that chance.' Lily said sternly. Coral turned to Allison for an answer, but Allison simply shrugged her shoulders in that 'I don't know' kind of way. 'Alright,' Coral said, 'Then just tell him to hurry up and get on the road. It's not that far to Memphis, but I think it'll be easier on the two of us to plan our course of action. Okay?' 'I'll tell him.' Lily said. 'C'mon, Ali, let's go.' Coral said, gently putting his hand on Allison's shoulder. This caught Allison's attention, but Allison simply smiled at Coral. 'I'll catch up.' she said. Coral shrugged, said 'alright', and turned around to leave. With Coral gone, Allison turned to look at Lily Avalon, who gave her an odd look. 'You look like you have something to say.' Lily Avalon said, judging from the look in Allison Lindum's eyes. Allison leaned against the wall and adjusted her dress before she looked up at Lily. 'Why did you defend them just now?' Allison asked, 'You don't especially know either of them that well, right?' Lily leaned against the wall, next to Allison, and thought about it. 'They're just like me. They're searching.' 'Searching for what?' 'They're searching for a raison d'etre. A reason for being. For me, I look for a place to belong, which was why I was looking like Trent Reznor's offspring for a while. Joey helped me to realize that I didn't belong in that realm, and that I needed to find my own place in life. Which was kind of ironic, since at the time he was telling me all of that, he, himself, was having trouble finding a place to be. The real reason why I wanted to leave those two be was because I feel that Coral's yet to find his place to be, and that he doesn't admit it, yet.' 'What do you mean?' Allison asked, concerned about the words of Coral's little sister. 'He doesn't seem to be entirely focused on his job. It seems like he's got something else on his mind as of late,' Lily said, 'This is just me, though. We actually ordered that stupid wrestling pay-per-view just to see him wrestle.' Allison smiled, 'He'll be happy to know that.' 'Don't tell him,' Lily quickly said, 'Please. Don't tell him. He'll want us to come along with him, and after we had to go through the fun of bailing Elvis out of jail the last time we came out for one of his wrestling shows, I don't think we can take that.' Allison was silent for a few moments, 'Alright. I won't.' 'Thank you.' Lily said, before she paused and spoke again, 'I think you should catch up with Coral, now. Please, take care.' 'You, too.' Allison said, before they embraced for a quick hug. Then Allison turned on her heel and walked out of the Avalon family home to catch up with Coral Avalon. Lily Avalon smiled. 'This place just gets more and more interesting by the day.' She walked away.
