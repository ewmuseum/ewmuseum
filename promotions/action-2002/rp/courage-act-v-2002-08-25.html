Courage - Act: V

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/25/2002 'Listen, Coral... I, I appreciate the fact that you came all the way to Phoenix to see me. But, I don't know if it'll work out. You're an oddball at times, and I'm... I'm still in college, and... oh God, what am I going to do?' Allison, like Coral before her, was talking to herself, trying to figure out what she was going to say to him when they met. She was also on the second floor of the Arizona Mills Mall, and she was becoming frustrated. Joey had called her cell phone and informed her that Coral was coming to the mall, and now she couldn't find him. Of course, like Coral before her, people were looking at her oddly. She tried to ignore them as best as she could, unaware that Coral had spotted her and was trying to reach her, if only there weren't a bunch of people and obstacles in his path, and he was too afraid to draw any more attention to himself than he has already. Allison sighed, unaware that Coral just tripped over another garbage can in his dash toward her. 'Where IS he?' she asked herself. 'I've been wandering around here for a half hour by now.' She started to walk to the elevator that would take her to the first floor, still unaware that Coral was trailing her... albeit, still victimizing nearby garbage cans with his clumsiness. 'He can't be *that* stupid. Maybe we just keep missing each other. Maybe he's just wandering around, looking high and low for me.' she said to herself, as she entered the elevator and pressed the button for the first floor. She hummed to herself, hoping that she'd find Coral downstairs. As she descended, Coral reached the elevator, and sighed in disbelief, as his clumsiness and his inability to find the courage to yell out her name in public have made it difficult for the two to actually meet with one another. He looked around for a staircase, but blinked in disbelief as he found none in the general vicinity. He looked up at the celing and groaned in disapproval. 'Who in the crap built this damn mall?!' Many more eyes were cast in Coral's general direction, as some had been ignoring him during his Great Trashcan Knockoverathon. Coral ignored them and impatiently waited for the elevator to come back up. It finally did, and Coral went down to the first floor. Then he frowned in disappointment. She wasn't anywhere in the area. Frustrated, Coral sat down at a bench and groaned to himself. 'This is not my day.'
