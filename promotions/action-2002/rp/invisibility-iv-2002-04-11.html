Invisibility - IV

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Original: 07/23/2001. Rewrite: 04/11/2002 August 10th, 1990 (one year later) Mesa High School Cafeteria: Mesa, Arizona, United States 'So, did 'Mr. Wizard' find the dead squirrel in his car engine?' a fifteen-year-old Joey Malone asked to his best friend, Daniel Phillips, refering to their science teacher. 'Oh, you bet!' he responded, gleefully. 'You should've seen the look on his face when he popped the hood! He was all like 'AHHH!!!' and then he kicked a tree and broke his foot. It was hilarious!' The two teenagers, seated out of earshot from the faculty, began just cracking up for their prank. The two were seated on a long table that was close to the door, generally away from everyone else. It was a small school, Since Joey had met Daniel, the two had been pulling pranks on as many people as they possibly could. He had become a lot more bold as the weeks came by, and though they had gotten into trouble, they often got away with it. Bonham was sure to point this out for Keri. 'But, he doesn't act like this, now.' she responded. 'Of course not. It stands to reason that he and Daniel would have to grow up eventually. Being a prankster will only get you so far, before everyone begins to hate you. A person needs something, anything, to straighten them out. This is the moment that it occured for Joey.' Bonham replied. Keri just listened in silence. 'Well, I'll go get something to eat.' Daniel said, getting up from his seat. Joey decided to rest his eyes, so he leaned forward and covered his head with his arms. As he did so, a girl, fifteen years old, walked into the cafeteria. She seemed hesitant and confused, before she saw the seat in front of Joey and moved toward him. Keri blinked. 'Isn't that...' she started, but couldn't finish. Bonham decided to interject. 'That would be you, yes.' The younger version of Keri tapped on the table, which got Joey's attention. Sort of. 'Um, yes?' he asked, without picking his head back up from the table that he was resting his head on. 'Hello. Is this seat taken?' she asked. With his head still down on the table, Joey said his next few words. 'Well, Daniel was sitting there, but since there's no one around us for a six seat radius, feel free.' Thanking him, Keri sat down. A few seconds pass, before boredom quickly takes her, and she begins poking Joey in the head. 'Uhm, hello?' Joey picked his head up from off of the table. And when he did so and looked at the girl in front of him, his vocabulary suddenly dropped to one word. '...Hi... hi...' Not noticing Joey's sudden loss for words, Keri smiled. 'Hi. Do you always sit at a distance from everyone else?' she said, looking around at the vast emptiness of the rest of the table. Joey, himself, looked around, before he looked back at the girl. It was around here that he regained partial use of his vocabulary. 'Usually, yes.' Keri paused, before the look on her face changed to embarassment. 'Ohmigosh, where are my manners?' She held her hand out for a handshake and smiled. 'My name is Keri. Keri Lindum. Pleasure to meet you.' Joey looked at her hand before he decided that he'd better oblige her, and does so. 'Joey Malone. The pleasure is mine.' For a moment, the two paused, looking into each other's eyes. Then Joey spoke up, getting an idea in his head. 'You're new here, right?' Keri nodded. 'Mm-hmm.' 'Think I could show you around here, so you won't get lost?' he offered, not realizing that Daniel was probably going to yell at him later. 'Sure.' she said, smiling again. 'I wasn't hungry, anyway.' 'Right. Okay. I'm sure Daniel won't care where I'm off to, anyway.' Joey said. With that, Joey and Keri both stood up, then Joey hopped over the table. The two left the cafeteria, just as Daniel Phillips came back, a coke in one hand, a Snickers bar in another, and no Earthly clue that Joey had left. 'Hey, Joey, can you believe that stupid fucking Coke mach-' Daniel started, but didn't finish, because Joey was missing. 'What the hell?' As he looked around the cafeteria, the memory went into pause. 'This is where you first met Joey.' Bonham said, after the memory paused. Keri blinked. 'But... I thought I'd remember something like this.' she said, sighing. 'When you first meet someone you eventually love, it's like a memory that'll get etched into your head, right? I thought I first met Joey last November, in that cafe...' 'Mm-hmm... well, this memory isn't really finished, so come with me.' With that, Bonham exited through the doors. Keri stood there, before she followed him through the doors, all with one thought going through her head. What was Bonham showing her?
