Or somewhere like that. I had hitched a ride on a truck all the way from New York to Pittsburgh, and the taste of coffee and cigarettes kept me feeling immortally free in the vast landscape of the United States. I have a backpack of my belongings. The Action! staff was nice enough to gather all my stuff and send it to the hospital. I stuffed it all in a backpack and began hitchhiking. I had a destination.
