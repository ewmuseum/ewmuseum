Love Geometric - X

1 November 2002

Date: 11/01/2002 October 24th, 2002 Thursday Night Kent Anthason laid back in a chair in his hotel room, with his feet propped up on the bed. His sunglasses were still on for some reason, and a look of contentment was all over his face. His day, yesterday, had gone smoothly up until his former student almost destroyed everything, but at least he managed to give Keri the address and room number of the hotel he was staying at. He then heard a knock on his door, but was disappointed when the source came from the adjoining room, and not from the outside of the place. This was Lucas Devol, who came in directly after knocking, regardless of if Kent really wanted to see him or not, Devol sat down on the bed, right next to where Kent's feet were positioned. He had a serious look on his face, in that it looked more serious than usual. 'Kent, what's going on between you and the girl?' Lucas asked, his words seemingly encased in a glacier that was slowly burying a city. 'What do you mean?' Kent asked, innocently, almost the anti-thesis of the tone that Lucas just gave him. 'Listen, Kent, I know your love life is important and all, but this is Keri Malone you're trying to hook up with. The wife of your enemy. She's unobtainable, Kent! Shit, she's LESS than unobtainable!' Lucas pleaded. In actuality, Lucas didn't give a monkey's bollocks about Kent's love life, but it definitely concerned him given the fact that he was becoming distracted from preparing for his match with Joey. 'And yet, I had her,' Kent said, pointedly, 'For a few moments, Lucas, we felt something between us.' 'Doesn't mean that she'll just jump into your arms, Kent. If anything, she probably will feel guilty abo--' Lucas started, but was interrupted by a knock on the front door. Delighted to be moving away from the conversation with Lucas, Kent leapt out of his seat and started for the door. Lucas realized this, leapt out of his seat, and faced Kent. 'Hey, don't walk out on this now, Kent,' Lucas said. 'We still have to talk about Monday!' 'It can wait.' Kent said. He brushed past Lucas, but turned back to him before he reached the door. 'Now, please leave us.' Lucas grunted, but said nothing as he reentered his room and closed both of the doors. Kent smiled, and then finally opened the door of his hotel room. Keri was behind the door, as he expected. She smiled and waved her hand at him. 'Hi, Kent.' 'Hi, Keri.'
