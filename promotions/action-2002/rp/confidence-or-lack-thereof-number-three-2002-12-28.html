Confidence(Or Lack Thereof): Number Three

28 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/28/02 Christmas had come and gone for Conrad Ramsey with a whimper. He, despite Danger's objections, had been invited up to Augusta, Maine by Samantha Dox to celebrate the holiday. He had gotten a pair of socks from 'The Danger Man', after he had taken back his book on Pearl Harbor after what had happened Monday with lack of enthuisiam for his picture and the fact that Ramey's present was to hold his belt. From Jessica he was given various odds and ends, just general things(gift cards, watch, etc.), while Bradley Duncan gave him a polishing kit(which Max didn't like) to keep his Bantam Championship nice and clean. But, while they were nice gifts, they were nothing compared to being able to spend the holiday with Samantha. Max was still none the wiser about the two's relationship, and neither was really eager to jump at the opportunity to tell him. Jessica had suspected, but she never brought it up, she was still happy to be back with Max and didn't want to ruin it yet. But Conrad and Samantha, had spent many hours in front of the fireplace, talking about their lives and just... talking. It was just soothing to listen to her voice and to look into her eyes. He had not a care in the world when he was around her. But something loomed ahead. Something he had thought about while in Maine. Something he would surely want to think about before the Sunday. Bantam Championship Chair Match Conrad Ramsey(B) vs. Airborn Tyler That was coming up Sunday at Players and Pawns. Airborn Tyler had gotten the better of Ramsey on Monday, blasted him with a steel chair after Conrad had granted him a title shot. He hadn't thought about it until now. One day before the show. Was he ready? Could he defeated Tyler at such a match? It wasn't in his nature or best interest to be in a Chair Match. He was wanting to teach Tyler that he couldn't go far with his high-flying style. But a Chair Match certainly wasn't going to let him do that. Was his reign as Bantam Champion soon to be over? Everyone seemed to be predicting it. Everyone at ewPlanet had been saying so in the predictions poll, or so 'The Danger Man' pointed out at every opportunity. Did he have what he took? Would he be just one month, thirty-nine day champion? The shortest Bantam Championship reign in Action! history? Maybe.
