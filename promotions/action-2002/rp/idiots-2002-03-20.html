Action! — Tales

Idiots...

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-20-02 I have a problem with Reed Young. He is a dumbass. Sure…that’s common knowledge for anyone in the wrestling industry. He lacks the golden gift of brains. Come on…the guy could be dangerous if he found the brain God had given him. Or maybe God needed to play a cruel joke on the entire world. While that jack off is going on and on like some sort of half wit, he is forgetting to book his big time ratings grabbers in main events. It’s just that simple. He books this LLB guy for every single main event, while I destroy another one of his toys. Honestly, if LLB and me stepped in the ring, he would be run over like some sort of paper banner before a Homecoming Football game. Fucking posers…all of them. I think that this place is going to fold like nothing sooner or later. With Young and Bold, it’s a possibility. They need to hire some guys who know what they are doing. Simple as that. I honestly believe that Action Wrestling is in me and Joey Malone’s hands. Nobody in this federation are as seasoned at fighting as us both. Not wrestling. Fighting. If any “fan” is still lying to themselves, saying that this is fake, they need to experience what we really do. I think Remicho and Marx understand exactly what they will have to deal with if they climb to the top. Because I am going to be there sooner or later. I always am. By any means necessary. Look at me. A couple months ago I was looking at publishing a book that would destroy this industry. I would make it known that all we are doing is glorified fighting. Whether it is by choice or not, its agreed that we all fight. Asylum thinks they have some new idea. Stupid Joe fucking Campbell. Stupid fucking ignorant wanker Every single television company, every single sponsor would cut their contracts up. This industry would suffer something of Black Thursday proportions. Or maybe I am lying to myself. Either way, the truth would be out. It’s like a magician giving out his tricks. Showing the world what he does and how he does it. Only a magician isn’t slowly killing himself or herself, like those involved in this business. Wrestling and fighting go hand and hand. Remember that Rashard. I am back in this world no matter what. I am greedy sometimes, I guess. Or maybe there is something else. I sit here, taping my fingers, in my wrestling gear, getting ready for another car crash named Pressure Point. Why don’t you watch the idiots who run this place? You might even get to watch me crush another “superstar” in the process. Go me. Because one day…I will be their champion… By any means necessary. By ANY means necessary.
