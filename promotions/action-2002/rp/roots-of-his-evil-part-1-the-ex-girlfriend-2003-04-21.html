Roots Of His Evil / Part 1 / The Ex-Girlfriend

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In life, there are certain things that will always stick with you. Certain events so memorable that these single events help mold you into the person that you are. The single obsession in the life of Hurley Cambria, is respect. But what happened in his life to make him this way. Who is responsible for making him this way? Who drove him to the point in which he felt he must be respected by everyone and anyone he comes across? Who scorned him in such a way that made him feel as if he must feel the respect and admiration of everyone around him? --- The Ex-Girlfriend Port St. Lucie, Florida January 2, 1998 --- I was at work when I got that phone call. Though it was a little more than five years ago, I remember it was it was yesturday. 'Hello?' Hurley responded, curious as to who was calling him at work, and why they would do so. He rarely got calls at work. Most of his friends knew his work schedule considering it was the same every week, and would call his house when he got home. Either that, or page him. 'Hey, Hurley. What's up?' the strange person asked. 'Nothing much man. Just finishing up at work. Who is this?' Hurley asked. 'It's James,' he replied. 'Macormin?' 'Yeah,' James confirmed. When I found out who it was, I was surprised to say the least. James wasn't a close friend of mine. Or really even a friend at all. He was a friend of my brother's, who when I walked by in the halls at school, I would nod to and ask 'what's up'. He wasn't the kind of person I would hang out with. So why was he calling me at work, of all places? It had to be important... 'Well, what's up bro?' Hurley asked. 'Are you still going out with Kelly?' James continued. When he said that, I felt my heart drop out of my body. My stomach began to ache. I knew something was wrong. This son of a bitch knew something. And he was going to tell me, or I was going to beat it out of him. Kelly and I had dated a month before we became an official couple. We were well into our second month at the time. Considering I had never had a girlfriend longer than a couple weeks, this seemed like the real thing. 'Yeah. We're still going out. Why?' Hurley asked with a sense of anger to his voice. James was also picking up on the anger Hurley was feeling. 'Dude, I'm so sorry! She told me you two weren't going out anymore. Dude, I swear to god I didn't know. I'm so fucking sorry man!' James began to exclaim. 'I swear to god man, I'm sorry!' That did I. I was fuming. I was ready to kill. I would have killed. If James had told me this face to face, no matter who's fault it was, I would have broken his jaw. I was angry enough to kill. 'What did you guys do?' I asked. 'Well, we kissed and she gave me head. Dude, I'm serious. She said you two were broken up.' I hung up, walked up to my supervisor and told him I had an emergency. He was young. When I told him about the situation, he told me I could go. He told me to kick some ass. I left and went straight to her work to confront her... There was a line of people checking out at the counter. Hurley could see Kelly standing there ringing up items. There was nothing getting in his face. Before they began dating, he had never stepped so much as a foot in a girl's clothing store. He had no reason to. But now, he was ready to wrap his fingers around her neck and squeeze until her head popped off. He stormed through the store and to the counter, where he slammed both fists on the table, getting the attention of her, her fellow employees, and the four or so people waiting in line. 'We need to talk!' Hurley barked out at her. 'Excuse me. I'm at work right now! What are you doing?' she said, looking him straight in the eye. 'We need to fucking talk! We can do it right in front of everyone if you'd like!' he yelled at her. A manager approached the two. 'Is there a problem here?' she asked of the couple. 'Yes. Yes there is a problem. There's a big fucking problem!' Hurley told her. 'Meagan, can I have five minutes to sort this out please?' Kelly asked. 'Ok, but hurry up.' Hurley stormed out of the store with Kelly not far behind. 'You have a lot of nerve coming into my store and acting like that! Not even my parents give me that much attitude! Who do you think you are?!' she yelled at him. 'Fuck you! Don't you even fucking talk to me like that again you stupid fucking slut!' 'What's your problem?!' 'Hmmm... let me think about this? How about you sucking the dick of one of my little brother's friends! That's a big fucking problem now, isn't it?! It'd say it's a major problem. Would you care to elaborate on what the fuck happened!?' 'Well, he picked me up after work one day, and we just went back to his house. One thing led to another and it just happened.' 'It just happened? You told him we were broken up! How the fuck does that just happen?' 'I dunno.' 'What the fuck is wrong with you? If you didn't want to go out with me anymore, you could have just told me. You could have been like, look, I think we should break up!' he took a breath. 'Then I would have been like, Ok, whatever. And that would have been fucking that.' 'Well...' 'Well nothing. You obviously have no respect for me at all, you selfish cunt! No fucking respect at all!' 'Listen, don't fucking talk to me like this!' 'No, you listen, you slut. You have no respect for me or my feelings. So I'm gonna have no fucking respect for you or your feelings!' 'So what now?' 'What now? Peace the fuck out bitch. We're done!' With that said, Hurley was gone...
