Inhospitable Hospital

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/03 Coral Avalon had been in hospitals before, and often times, he felt horrible in each and every visit. However, he had never felt this horrible about being in the hospital up until now, and ironically enough, he wasn't even the patient. It wasn't the fact that Allison Lindum was in the hospital that bugged him, it was the fact that he had unintentionally put her here with cracked ribs following a messed up bump into a door. Perhaps Joey or someone should've remembered that Allison wasn't trained to take bumps, because Coral vowed to make them pay for the hospital bill that'll come because of Allison's cracked ribs. Allison, herself, hated hospitals. She remembered her older brother, Marcus, and a horrific broken nose that he had suffered while protecting her from some schoolyard bullies. The blood was difficult for the doctors to control and it made Allison very queasy. Which made her spend half of the time in that particular venture in the hospital in the bathroom to throw up. Ironically, Allison sometimes likened Coral to a big brother like Marcus, which sometimes made Coral frown. Mainly because his own older brother could only talk in grunts, moans, facial expressions, and sign language, due to the lack of a tongue. 'Ow...' she moaned, holding said ribs as the doctor taped them up. Her aqua blue tanktop was off and all that could be seen of her upper body was her bra and the bandages that were being applied. The shirt itself was hanging awkwardly from Coral's left shoulder, where she had left it. 'I'm curious...' the doctor said, 'How did this happen?' 'Well, t-that is... um...' Coral stumbled. Something told him that informing a doctor that he had caused the injury himself when he tackled her into a door in what was supposed to be a relatively safe spot. Allison sighed, 'Work-related.' Nice save, Allison. 'Heck of a job you must have, young lady.' the doctor said, finishing his patch work and moving away from Allison. 'I don't suggest that you move about that much. I don't know what happened, but if you had been hit any worse, you might have had broken ribs, and that would've been a serious problem.' the doctor said. 'I guess...' Allison said, passively, all while giving Coral a look that made him feel even more guilty. 'Just take a couple of pain-killers, take plenty of rest, and you should be fine. That should be it.' the doctor added, as he finished writing the prescription and handed it to Allison. Allison sighed and took it, before she took the tanktop from Coral's shoulder and put it back on, before she left the room. Coral quickly followed after her. 'Look, Allison, I'm...' 'Don't apologize.' Allison said, turning around to face Coral, her arms crossed in front of her now-bandaged abdomen. 'I can't help it. I feel horrible about it, okay? I should've objected to the idea of you taking a bump like that, and I probably shouldn't have done it in front of a door of all things anyway. I know that you wouldn't have done it if you weren't willing, but still... I shouldn't have thrown myself into it like that.' Coral said, 'So, I'm sorry.' 'Don't be,' Allison said, 'I can take care of myself.' She started to turn and walk away, but Coral lightly grabbed her shoulder, not trying to be forceful, since he was well aware that she was hurting. Allison turned back around to face Coral. 'Don't just blow this off, Allison,' he said, boldly, 'This wasn't just any old nick or cut. You passed out. That scared the shit out of me. I thought I had crippled you or something, which wouldn't have set well with my karma. You... you do realize, of course, that you're my best friend in the whole world and hurting you is about the last thing I ever want to do, right?' Allison fidgeted, 'Y-yes...' Coral was satisfied with the response, and gave her a wry smile, 'Good. Because if I had to do it all over again, I sure as hell would've hit Joey.' Allison nudged him in his own ribs as they walked out of the room, 'Yeah, right! You'd have missed and hit Reed or someone.' Coral chuckled, 'In which case, I'd have still won.' 'Touche.'
