The Beta: Eulogy Of The Human Spirit(2)

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Good times. Who needs them? I hate good. Good means you deal with the hand God gave you. I hate the deal God gave me. He cheated me out of decent deck. I vomit and bleed a lot. Those are the only good times I know. Those are fun. Because when you go do a mock snow angel in your own blood and vomit of the cheeseburger and fries you had going to the event, you do something nobody approves of. And if nobody likes it, then I like it. Because making you loath and hate me is all that gets me off. When I walk to the ring, that battery you threw meant for my head that missed barely, it gives me power. That trash and watered down piss you call beer that you throw at me, it gives me the ultimate power and rush. Your boos? Its like when you cheered for Hulk fucking Hogan. When you boo me, I “Hulk-Up” and become damn near unbeatable. If you knew this, you would still hate me. You would hate me even more since I am sick and twisted for thinking like this. I hate the normal world that is forced down my throat. I am abnormal. Who the fuck are you to judge me? You people are so two faced that it is not even funny. I am honesty. I am justice. I am patrotic. Did I mention I hate this country? Or that Muslims and Jews killing each other is something I laugh at like the funny pages? Or that being considered trash like you would be in China would make me happy? I hate all you people. My fans. My enemies. My family. My former lovers. I hate you all. I wish I could soak you in my own bile. You make me sick. You deserve to die. I looked at my wrists. I smiled. Keri is kinda tired. She is bleed from a self inflicted wound to her head. She banged he head into some pole and is laying there. Maybe I should take the blindfold off her, so she can see. Nah. Till Death Do Us Part. It WILL come down to that. I certainly hope so.
