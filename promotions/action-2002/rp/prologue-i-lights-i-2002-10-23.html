Prologue I - Lights (i)

23 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd October Crash Something shatters inside the café the camera is focused on, and is preceded by two men shouting inside the building. The camera zooms out and shows a kid standing outside, in an obscenely baggy jacket, presumably waiting for whoever is in the café to finish his business. The door suddenly busts open and a 6’ plus guy in jeans and a leather jacket comes running out, he grabs the kid’s arm and yells “there’s a bomb” as the two hurl themselves to the ground. “And… CUT!” The two get up and dust themselves off as the camera zooms out to reveal the film set. “OK, stuntmen on set please.” the director barked over his microphone. A guy about 6’2, and 240lbs walks on set accompanied with a young kid actor, the two with their back away from the camera, located by the side of the café. “… ACTION!” A pyrotechnic display goes off from the café, obviously simulating a bomb, as the two actors leap into the air, and land, rolling on the floor. “Perfect, nice one Leon, you too Dave.” The man gets up, gives the director a thumb up and walks off holding his shoulder; he obviously jarred during the jump. The kid gets up and looks at the camera for the first time and it’s here where we see he’s not a kid at all, but a young man. He dusts himself off and removes the flame retardant jacket showing his one isn’t at all baggy but he is amazingly well built in the upper body. “Any chance you can lose some of that muscle mass, it’s getting tougher to make you out to be a kid.” I dunno, I’d be weaker, then I couldn’t do the hanging stunts proper. “Heh, hanging stunts, still dreaming of being a major action stuntman, ey Leon?” That’s the dream…Fucker. He muttered the last comment under his breath. Leon hated being the little man. The guy who wasn’t big enough to do anything, he’d show them, one day. I gotta run, I be meeting Tash in ten. “Alright, we’re done here, see ya tomorrow champ, we got some more shots of the kid being grabbed out the way from a moving train, and we’re bringing in John Adams. He’s worked with Harrison Ford and other stars, so see if you can pick up some tips. Leon had stopped listening after “see ya tomorrow champ”, could that director being any more patronising? Leon was on his way to see his fiancée Natasha, he’d been going out with her since the end of high school, and 2 years later, the couple were planning to get married, but Tash had a major problem with his career. While she constantly moaned about not seeing each other enough, deep down she was scared for his safety, everyday was a lottery as to whether he’d come injured or not, and then there were the long periods without work. She needed stability, and Leon couldn’t provide that for her. She could break off the marriage, but she loved Leon too much, or she could go ahead and stay quiet, but she knew she’d end up resenting herself for it. She was in a tough situation, and she’d have to let him know… but not yet.
