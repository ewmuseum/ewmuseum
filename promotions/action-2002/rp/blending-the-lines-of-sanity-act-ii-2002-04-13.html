Blending the Lines of Sanity, Act II

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/13/02 'You have got to be kidding me.' Tommy's words echoed through the cavenous walls of his apartment at the site of Pressure Point Seven. In front of him was his laptop computer's monitor, at the Action website's preview page for Pressure Point Seven. 'This is complete and total bullshit!' Tommy said, slamming his laptop closed as it shut down. 'I had the only good feud in this craphole with Maverick Montresor, and this is how Reed Young rewards me? I was left off of TV last week, and now I'm left off the preview this week and I'm still not even told if I'm even going to have a match or not! What the hell kind of owner is Reed Young?' Tommy paused, realizing what a stupid question that was. He suddenly buried his head into his hands, and seemed to be shaking his head back and forth inside them. 'I'm a good wrestler, right?' Tommy said to himself. 'I try hard...don't I? I do good in the ring....don't I?' YOU'RE JUST TOO FUCKING SHORT! 'FUCK YOU!' Tommy screamed, and threw the table lamp into the wall, shattering it. Tommy stood up and raised two fists into the air. 'They're all wrong!' Tommy screamed. 'I'm destined to be a big-time superstar, I'm destined to be a commerical success, I'm destined to be the next big thing. YOU CAN'T CHANGE MY DESTINY, REED YOUNG!' Tommy began punching the wall with hisi right fist, gritting his teeth. 'It's...my...fucking...DESTINY.' Tommy said through gritted teach and in-between punches. 'You....can't....fucking...CHANGE IT!' Tommy put his head back between his arms as he pulled them away from the wall. He realized his entire body was shaking, and sat down on his bed, hoping to calm down. 'I hate this world.' Tommy said quietly. 'I hate everything about it. They won't love me. They won't adore me. They won't make me their hero. All they will do is reject me. Well, I'll make them accept me. I'll make them love me. I'll figure a way out.....and then Reed Young won't be able to hold me back, any longer.' Tommy grinned to himself. He had a plan. A full-proof one. He was getting his stardom....whether Reed Young liked it or not. Fin.
