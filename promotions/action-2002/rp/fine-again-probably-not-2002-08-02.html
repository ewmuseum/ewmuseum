Action! — Tales

fine again...probably not

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I hate ignorance. I really do. I stopped running. I stopped lifting. I just stopped caring about this sport. Whether it was fighting, or wrestling, or basketball, or whatever you think it is, it’s not something I give two fifths of a fuck about. Wrestling is in the shits now as it is. The fWo is on strike, from what I have gathered, Action! is one of the top federations around, and people are interested in everything. And I don’t care. Through my deeds, Action! Wrestling is now considered cutting edge. Who was the guy who garnered all those ratings? Who was the innovative one? Me. Not only that, but those times sucked. I was a suicidal revenge hungry shovel wielding clusterfuck who used the WB to get my message out. And what message is that? Violence is okay. Well, it’s not. I hate my job. I hate whispering to people about what happened. I am suffering from a case of depression. What am I going to do now that the entire world has its eyes fixated on my every action? How can I go back to wrestling like I used to when for so long I have been doing this shit for real? I can’t. See I am on a crossroads. One way is the right way, the other is the wrong. And I rather sit on my fat lazy ass, smoke a blunt, and become old and gray waiting for something to run me over. I need a steady love. I need something to care for. I need goals. And I have none. So, in the end, am I going to revert back to my old ways? Possibly. The names I see on the roster now were not around when I was holding everything by the balls. It’s all fun and games. Sweet Aroma knows Action! Wrestling isn’t somewhere you go to thrive. It’s somewhere to go to die. Reed Young used me so badly. He used my episode to funnel in all this money. Everyone did. I should be doing time in a prison cell, but people dropped charges. I nearly killed a man and woman. I injected a woman with heroin. I assaulted multiple people with a deadly weapon. I tried to kill myself in front of a live television audience. I ended careers, and alienated all around me. Daniel Phillips and Julie Malone were damn near killed. Joey Malone was inches away from being stuck with the image of my exposed brain splattered against his face. I do not know what happened with Keri’s psyche, but if she saw me in public, she would turn around and run away, I am guessing. I got off because I am a celebrity. I deserve to be castrated and fed my own dick. I deserve to be hung with piano wire, having it slowly slice my neck open until it completely decapitates me. I deserve to be locked in federal pound me in the ass prison for fifty years, forced to bend over for Bubba to ass rape me. Why does one wish these things upon himself? Because I lost that one thing that was more important to me than life itself. She’s walking around in the Asylum, forced to eat this rank business. She is being slowly circumcised by it, just like I have been. I want to grab her by the arm, get married, and ride of into the sunset. Elayna is a friend. Quinn is my love. And having lost her, it hurts me more than anything any of these wrestlers could do to me. I rather die than see her with another man. But I am too gutless to go and apologize for not showing the proper affection. The reason she’s there is because of me. I know it. She feels like she can carry the weight of the world on her shoulders. She’s not made for that. She’s made to be a warming smile. A everlasting kiss. A special moment. And all is bastardized in this world by myself. I lost my friends. My vision of a wife with two kids and a white picket fucking fence in the rich suburbs. My dreams of normality have died. Forever, you will point at me and say “That’s the man who tried to kill himself”. I will look at your sneers. Your hateful eyes. And it will hurt me that much more. I don’t care now. It numbs the emotional pain I feel. I want to press the reset button, and redo this entire act over again. But, it would end up the same. I hope to god I can get this all back if I try. Then again… Bah. I will shut up and do my job. It’s the thing you would expect, right?
