Enter The Grizzly's Den: Foreword

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08.29.02 (The Night After Pressure Point 27) A perverse symphony of sirens and screams encompassed the murky night air. Like a moth to the flame, the masses assembled expecting medical aid and asylum, but more often than not, it was death, not life, which welcomed them with open arms. Amidst the chaos that had come to epitomize places such as this, an old rickety sign made notice to all whom read it where it was they stood. Though noticeably worn, and undoubtedly hard to interpret at first examination, it soon became disturbingly clear to its reader the particulars of his or her location. Unmistakably inscribed on the frayed piece of wood were two plainly written words: EMERGENCY - ROOM. -SWOOSH- The revolving doors of the sickbay spun round, spitting out another of its weary occupants. At first glance, the departing creature might've almost passed as human, but those beady, lifeless eyes were a dead giveaway to its true origin. As a menacing storm stirred overhead the skies began to rumble. Countless beads of rain proceeded to beat down on the ogre's brow, as the torrential floodgates were released from the heavens. Gradually his hair became nappy and damp, causing it to hang low over his lethargic peepholes, only further cementing his monster-like appearance in the minds of those whom he passed. 'Please 'sa sir, you mustn't leave…' The peculiar character quickly whipped around, eyeing his pursuer through the slits in his long, wet mane. 'The doctor says you 'sa has'ta stay for mo' testin'… you 'sa deathly ill Mr. McClure…' This explicitly beautiful creature, which now stood steadily before Morg, sent chills up and down his spine, and even though it was most certainly apparent from her uneducated dialect that she was dim-witted, Morg instantly fell in love with her, as things of that nature made no difference to 'The Ace of Spades'. She was the epitome of perfection in his mind. Her mocha chocolate skin caused his blood to boil with excitement. She wore a bouquet of roses and cinnamon, which sent Morg's nose for a loop. He quickly took notice to the way her nurse's uniform clung to her budding breasts. Erotic fantasies tangoed back and forth in his head, as he dreamt of how he might have her. But he soon snapped out of his daydream, presuming that a woman of her description would never see anything worthwhile in him. His neck was draped tightly in a brace as a result of the fall he'd taken just one night earlier. The remnants of his ear were still bloodied and swollen. Scars still bedecked his body thanks to the war he'd waged with Domino. Not to mention the fact that he was freakishly tall by any standards. What could a woman like her see in a man like him? 'Nothing' He said to himself. More disenchanted then he had been before their encounter, Morg lugged her arm from his shoulder, and ensued to exit the premises. She hurriedly chased after. 'You 'sa has'ta come back inside, sir… you 'sa needs ya rest…' 'I'll get plenty of rest at my own home and in my own bed.' She continued to argue with him, but to no avail. Still clad in tattered and torn medical scrubs, Morg hurriedly made his way towards a taxi. As he sped away to an undisclosed location, the woman still trailed, screaming every inch of the way. 'I'll 'sa bring you 'sa back if its da' last thing I 'sa do…'
