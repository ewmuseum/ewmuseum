What Happened at WWC's Menace 12...

28 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Segment One : Break Down The Walls The aim for the night was simple. Survive. However, it seemed that such a concept in these times was far to hard to comprehend, as the mounting pressures and additional factors that were building up upon Khristain Keller seemed to be tying him down. He knew for a fact that the load on his shoulders was a very difficult one to burden. But he carried them none the less. He now wrestled in both WWC and Action, something that he had never experienced before in his life, but when it came down to the actual wrestling, he had managed to escape the task by only a matter of inches. Keller knew however, that today would be a momentous day in his career, as today, would be his first match in EITHER of his new federations. Add that to the fact that he hadn't properly wrestled in ACW for pretty much it's last month, you could imagine how he was feeling. But he deserves it doesn't he? Lying. Skiving. Deceiving. Khristain Keller is the scum of the earth. Remember that... Keep that single line in your mind for the rest of the night. Khristain Keller is the scum of the earth. This is a man whom broke another's neck in the middle of the ring, and said it wasn't his fault. Khristain Keller is the scum of the earth. This is a man whom single handlely turned a man against his fans and his family in ACW, just for his own personal gain. Khristain Keller is the scum of the earth. This is a man whom is mentally breaking apart his opponent for the night, a young man looking for a break in WWC, who would find himself in the ring with Khristain Keller on what would be his worst night. Remember... Khristain Keller is the scum of the earth. Now let’s see if you think that by the end of the night… - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Segment Two : Nothing To Lose, Everything To Win Khristain Keller tipped a bottle of water over his head in what had begun to become a pre-match ritual for the inring veteran. He didn't do it to make his body all shiny for the TV cameras, which some wrestlers of the bigger nosed variety had done for years. He done it for the rush. He done it for the buzz. He done it, because he had to. He fixed his newly purchased knee brace as it pinched his skin by his kneecap, it was a last resort to stop the pain, and it was something that had come highly recommended from the few wrestlers that he still relates with, and not only was it to stop the pain, but it was to try and stop anymore further damage being made. Keller opened his locker room door and began to walk to the technical area, every step with a new experience, as the brace shifted and moulded with his actions. His mind totally aware to the new metal and plastic accessory which was now wrapped around his right knee, he didn't even notice the ruckus which surrounded him. His mind was filled with cluttered thoughts. Will the brace hold? What if I need to take it off? And then his regular brain pulses took over. Can I use it as a weapon? Keller smirked for the first time in almost 4 days as he followed the luminous arrows which pointed the wrestlers to the stage way, but as he followed on, his path was blocked, by Paul Levine. Levine scanned his roster member, his arms folded and a small smile on his face. 'Are you ready for this Keller?' 'Not particularly...but when has that ever stopped me?' Levine nodded. 'Don't you even want to know who you are facing?' K² stared at the WWC owner, trying to show that he didn't care, he just wanted to get on with it. 'I'll just tell you then...I had one of the roster ready to take you down, but then I got a phone call, one very disgruntled youth who wants some revenge for what you did to him a couple of weeks back. I was kind of surprised, I heard that he was still in shock from what you did to him.' Keller clocked a figure coming from the shadows behind Levine, as the man then moved his way to the stage entrance as his theme boomed over the arena... 'You'll be facing Kevin Holland, safety man...and he's pissed.' As Levine walked away, Keller's hopes of an easy ride somehow vanished. It would have been OK to fight someone who had nothing to win... But now he takes on someone with nothing to lose... 'Remember the little talk we had last week Keller? You lose and that's strike one, but I thought I'd make it an even contest tonight, because you're the almighty Keller, isn't that right?' Keller refused to make eye contact with the WWC owner. 'Hardcore match, anything goes ok?' Levine left. Keller scowled. 'Fuck.off.and.die.' Should be interesting. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Match : Khristain Keller Vs. Kevin Holland Kevin Holland bounced in the centre of the ring as he looked at the masses around him. He didn't have a moment to think about the capacity crowd at his wake, nor did he even reminisce about the last time he left an WWC arena, in that ambulance, being made a fool of by Khristain Keller... He gritted his teeth. Begging. Pleading. Praying for Keller. The youngster was like a rabid bulldog waiting to come off the leash...and then came his whistle. 'Dirty Window' by Metallica. Cue boos. Enter... K E L L E R For his inring WWC debut, Khristain Keller was to take on someone whom nobody had even heard of, even though he had appeared on WWC TV just two weeks prior and had been bashed with a metal pole to the skull. Kevin Holland bust outside the ring as he sprinted up the ramp, just as Keller emerged from the backstage area, to quickly be hounded by the young rookie. The crowd screamed as Holland took Keller down with a Thou Thez Press on the metal ramp, Keller's back and head clattering off the hard meshed metal on his way down. Holland swung right hands as his temper for the better of his, as Keller lifted his hands trying to block or deflect the punches away from Holland's proposed target. Keller managed to push the young WWC challenger off of his chest, but was quickly pounced upon as he got to his knees, as Holland hit him with a sharp right hand, which had Keller rocking back on his knee. Holland then grabbed Keller's open head, and planned for a DDT on the metal ramp, but Keller had enough awareness and enough power to push the youngster off of him, and eventually of the ramp completely. Holland's bump had left him in the middle of the technical area after a small 4 foot drop onto the concrete, but it was a sufficient size to have left the promising talent a little winded. Keller rose to his feet as the capacity crowd looked, and boo'd on. Keller's 'I couldn't really give a fuck' snigger leaving the fans even more enraged as they watched him slowly take his time down the ramp. Keller swaggered down to the edge of the ramp before fixing his knee brace into a position, which didn't annoy him. He growled at it before making his way over to the technical area where Holland was now slowly getting to his feet. Keller made a small dash to Holland before he even knew Keller was there, and a club to the back of the head sent the youngster back to the concrete. Keller lifted Holland up and bashed his head with his left knee, marking the youngsters face. Keller had had enough, he yanked the kid by the hair and began and pull and tug, his target was the ring, as that's where all the weapons where. He's smart you know... Keller shoved Holland into the ring before pulling up the ring apron to reveal a galore of weapons, his choice? The trusty steel chair. He climbed in the ring, and the bell rang, with this being the first time either had jumped into the ring, but since it was a hardcore match, it seemed a mistake on the officials behave. Keller dropped the steel chair to his side and began to go to work on his opponent, a hard club to Holland's back had him on his hands and knees and then it seemed Keller's ego and temper got the better of him. He volleyed Holland's ribs, only for his leg to shudder on impact, and his knee to lock. Keller screamed in pain as he began to hobble, as the whole arena picked up the action to see exactly what had happened. But as Holland lay on the mat searching for his breath, Keller hobbled to the corner and clutched his right knee in pain. He grimaced as Holland began to get to his feet, but Keller could do nothing, as any movement to the centre of the ring would leave him totally immobile if the young Holland knew what he was doing, and it seemed he did. Keller immediate attention was brought to a wild right hand, which was thrown by Holland, which he Holland, but he didn't expect the leg sweep which followed. Rough is was, but it done the job as Holland took Keller's feet from beneath him and done around ten times more damage than any regular sweep move would have done in the circumstance. 1... 2... Kickout. Keller's leg might have been fucked, but he definitely was still able in the upper body department. Holland rolled off and then quickly got the steel chair which Keller had brought into play, but as he carelessly held it around his chest, Keller stretched out his left leg as it ricochet off Holland's face as he stumbled to the ground. The arena was now in a silent mood, as they watched Keller struggle to his feet with the use of the ropes beside him. Keller began to rip the knee brace off of his jeans, and then flexed his leg back and forth, but it seemed that even the removal of the new guard had no effect. He looked over his shoulder, as the now bloody nosed Kevin Holland was on his feet, slightly groggy but up none the less. With chair in hand, he swung again. Keller ducked...but stumbled as he moved past. Holland kept swinging, until he connected with something. Connect he did. Keller hit the mat with a thud as Holland smashed him over the back of the head with the steel chair. It seemed a direct hit to the backbone of Keller's skull, as a deep laceration was already evident on the bald head of the former ACW star. Keller's eyes opened, as he looked down the horizon of the mat and onward...the feeling of blood trickled down to his neck and flooded unto the canvas as Holland looked on at what he had created. Keller gritted his teeth and pushed himself up. Holland, with his fingers in his nose trying to stop his own blood flow ran over to Keller and jumped over his vertical body, before bouncing off of the ropes and trying an elbow drop, but Keller still had good function of his arms and body, and completely shifted his weight as he rolled out of the firing range of the elbow. 'Fuck this.' That was about all that Keller could manage as he rolled over to the turnbuckle and began the process of standing up, his knee physically shaking at the prospect. For a short moment, the path to victory was in front of him, as he looked on at Holland, who was rushing forward with harmful intent, his arms in the air clearly looking for a strike of some sort, but Keller jabbed Holland's belly with a sharp left hand, and then quickly hooked him with his right arm. Jackhammer... Or what Keller likes to call it. The Tranquilizer. 'None of this Goldberg bullshit.' Keller hoisted the young, and light WWC employee up and quickly down, as he felt his leg begin to buckle, but he had done enough to place the last spike on the drive down, as Holland's vertebrae crumpled on impact. Keller slugged his arm over Holland's body, as the referee stated the obvious. 1... 2... 3. The arena stood silent. No cheering. No booing. Nothing. As they watched Keller's arm lifted in victory, he shoved the referee away from site and clutched his knee in pain, as someone from the back had suddenly thought it was a good idea to get some medics on hand. Keller however, didn't agree with their being there. 'Need any help?' the young lady medic asked, to which she was answer. 'How about you go and fuck a goat?' Keller pulled himself out of the ring and crumbled down at the ringside barrier, the fans tapping him on the shoulder trying to get the perspiration memento which ALL wrestling fans which to receive when they pay for their tickets. Keller wasn't done however. Clearly exhausted and needing of medical assistance to his knee, he called for a mic to be handed over, and so it was, and with a few beginning large and huffy breathes he spoke. 'You know...Levine...you are a FUCKING CUNT, how about that for your strike one?' And so by winning, Khristain Keller would soon turn it to losing, as a clear fuck you to the WWC owner. As Keller's music prompted up, he let his head fall back to the guard rail, in what seemed like heaven in a moment. But his knee still prodded at his brain with every millisecond, and as he cursed under his breath, and swore and most of the fans nearby, he began the long and painful trip back to his locker room, as Kevin Holland was taken out of the ring in a stretcher and a neck brace. Keller could only just manage a smile. 'Could be worse...' he whispered under his breath. 'I could be like that cunt.' Every cloud... - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - Segment Three : Nothing To Lose, Everything To Win As he shut his dressing room door, he got what he had been looking for, for over 20 minutes, and that was the noise of silence. He leant back against the door and slowly slid down the doorway, a trail of blood following him from the back of his head, and finally stopping as he found the ground as he slumped. Blood still emerged from the wound on the back of his head. It trickled down his neck and down his collarbone, before dropping onto the floor with a splash, a splash you could hear only if you concentrated on it. This wasn't meant to happen. He was the mighty Khristain Keller. A man whom people feared when he was in the mood, a man who could strike most down with a single blow and make sure you were down with a kick to the head when you were down. This wasn't right. Keller breathed deeply as he grabbed the bench on his right hand side, as he struggled to raise himself to his feet. Slowly, but surely, he found himself erect, standing still in the centre of his dressing room. With a short pace forward with his right foot... He fell... His knee giving way to his weight, as he lay crumbled on cold and hard floor. He howled in pain, low enough for no one to hear outside of these four walls. He slammed the concrete with his bare fist, immediately damaging the skin and causing it to sting. Anything to take away the pain of his knee. Anything. His bag was within reach, and as he stretched, he managed to tip the contents over upon himself. Towels, a change of clothes and a toiletry bag fell upon him, the contents of which hadn't been touched for several months, as he had to reason to delve into it as he was never wrestling until now. Along with the contents however, he seen something that shook him to the core. A bottle of aspirin. The former addict looked on as they rolled along the floor with the familiar rattling sound to which he had grown to love and loathe for years. He looked hard. He stared at his memories of the places he had been, the places where he did not want to return, places, which he would wish upon his worst enemy. His inner self raged with greed, as he looked on to what would be the solution to all his problems. His right hand slowly moved towards them, his jerking reactions causing the contents to spill over the concrete. He was gone. He picked up four, then five, then six, and shoved them into his mouth, slowly chomping away on the white gritty substance. He closed his eyes and thought of happier days...his mind slowly becoming empty of such things...and as he opened his eyes and seen the spillage upon him, his broken thoughts could not repair the damage. The stains of time had come to ruin him once again. 'What am I doing...' A tear of regret ran down his cheek. This world is a drought when out of love. Please come back to us. For all of the above. I'm making a choice to be out of touch. Leave me be he said. Leave me here in my stark raving sick sad little world...
