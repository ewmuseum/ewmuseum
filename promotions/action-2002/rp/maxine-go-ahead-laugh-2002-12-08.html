Action! — Tales

Maxine: go ahead. laugh

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/8/2002 I didn’t die. You should’ve figured that out on your own. If I were dead, I wouldn’t be able to recount this story to you. But just the same... I didn’t die. How’d I get thrown out of an apartment window and survive to tell about it? I assumed you were expecting a description of me being hurled from the top of a building plunging several stories to my bloody demise. Instead, I was pushed from 1st story window tumbling several inches to my irritating annoyance. Sorry to disappoint you. I wasn’t even that bad off. The grits actually opened up my pores I think. And the grass I landed on was quite comfy. So other then the humiliation of being thwarted by 2 crackwhores and their illegitimate misbehaving children, I was pretty much feeling good. Here I am Mr. Big Bad Wrestler/Crack slinger/Gun Toting/Don’t give a fuck, don’t fuck with me, psycho killa, existential, rip off artist, asshole...getting his ass handed to him by pre-pubescent bastard kids and their strung out mothers. ...Yay for me. This isn’t gonna look good for my image. I sat up straight and dusted myself off a bit. I then pulled the .22 I had in my ankle holster and clicked the safety off. During the scuffle I was completely unaware of its presence. I should feel deeply embarrassed. I should seek revenge. I should take action. I thought about that for a second...and tossed the .22 down the gutter. Embarrassment is for those with a sense of decency and self-worth to feel as if those qualities in them have been comprised in some way. Revenge is for those who feel as if they’ve been wronged and feel passionately about getting the justice they deserve. Action is for those who are able to feel compelled into act. I’m don’t care about myself enough to feel embarrassed by anything. I’m to apathetic to be concerned with seeking revenge. And there are not many things in existence that can move me to action. I got my ass kicked my kids. I don’t give a fuck. Go ahead. Laugh, point, jeer. See if I care. I can still wipe the floor with you. Fuck it. I think I’ll stick to using. Dealing isn’t as fun as you’d assume. Hopefully this disgusts you. Eat my ass -God bless Invalid
