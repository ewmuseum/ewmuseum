Invisibility - III

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Original: 07/22/2001. Rewrite: 04/09/2002 September 5th, 1989 (thirty minutes later) Mesa High School: Mesa, Arizona, United States Bonham led Keri into the principal's office in the school. For the most part, the room was about as bland as you can imagine. There was a plant, a desk, and a few pictures. That was it. It was also completely devoid of adult life, because the principal, at that moment, was busy with something else, and had no time to deal with the two freshmen that had been banished to this room. One was, of course, Joey Malone. The other was a guy with short black hair, and was a tad taller than Joey was. He looked far more bold than Joey did, and he proved that with the bucket of water that he happened to have. 'Hey, you. Yeah, you. Could you give me a hand with this?' he asked, as he mounted a chair and left the door cracked. Seeing no one around, Joey helped the other kid out. He pulled the bucket up into his hands, who then placed it on top of the door, balancing so that the next unfortunate soul who opens the door will get an unwanted shower. The guy leapt off of the chair and looked at Joey. 'Thanks, man.' he said. 'I've been wanting to do this to that bastard for a while, now.' Joey looked up at the bucket, and put two-and-two together. 'Won't you get in trouble for that?' 'That? Not unless they catch me.' he responded, as he walked toward the window and opened it. 'You'd better come with me, unless want to get into any more trouble, yourself.' Joey took about two seconds to think before he followed the other kid out of the window. Bonham walked through the wall and motioned for Keri to follow, which she did. Joey and the other kid are now outside, and heading somewhere. 'Ah, thanks for the help there with the bucket and all.' the kid said. 'The name's Daniel Phillips.' And as if all of time stopped, the memory is frozen right there. 'Daniel Phillips... wasn't he Joey's best friend?' Keri asked. Bonham clapped. 'Bright girl!' he said. 'Yeah. He's the one that inspired Joey to stand up for himself. Without Daniel's intervention on this day, Joey wouldn't be the same person he is today.' Keri blinked. 'Really?' 'Yes. But the presence of someone else was needed to further shape him. Come. I'll show you.' Bonham said, before walking off, back toward the wall, where he disappeared. Keri turned around, shrugged, and followed him through the wall. So many images... ...but what did they all mean? Keri still did not know. But the next memory would surprise even her.
