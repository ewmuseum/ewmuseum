The Building Blocks of Sanity pt. 2

13 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 13th, 2002 August 21st, 2002 – Wednesday – 8:00 AM Laying on the damp, stained mattress, Jeff spoke aloud, trying to keep himself from falling to sleep. The cell was a tight, nine feet by nine feet, the floor nothing more than chipped cement with a toilet in the corner and bunk beds on both sides. With the regulated coarse, brown blankets folded neatly at his feet, propping them up as he stared up at the ceiling repeating the same exact sentence over and over again, much like he had been doing the last four and a half hours. Occasionally he would stop of course, just to glance over to make sure the two fat, obese skinheads where still asleep. And what disturbed him more than anything was that they were in the side bed, curled up in the fettle position… Spooning each other. Jeff shuddered, trying to cleanse his mind by thinking about a late night porno flick he had seen back when he had a television to watch. Wait, aren’t skinheads supposed to be against homosexuality? Strange. But not strange enough to keep Jeff from continuing his plot to stay awake through the night. He wouldn’t let those queers get to his hot, gorgeous body. Jeff had been aching to lose his virginity since he turned twelve years old, but that wasn’t what he had in mind… Not at all. He had the will, he had the endurance, and it didn’t mattered how heavy his eyelids felt. He would continue on, closing his eyes and taking a quick catnap was not an option. For being obese, those queers sure could move. “They all want my body. Can’t sleep. They all want my body. Can’t sleep. They all want- ZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ…” And off to sleep the Garvinator went, never reaching his goal, never achieving success; failing as always. Morning came and Jeff’s eyes opened to the sound of birds chirping outside his cell window, he smiled, hoping it was all a dream. Not just the part about being thrown in the ‘big house’ but about every single thing that had transpired over the last two months; leaving 21w, losing to Glen Miller, being attacked by fWo World Champion Erik Kelly on OSW Slam, and becoming one of those dirty, trash eating homeless people that he despised so much. Now he was one of them. The two guys on the top bunk across from Jeff yawned, stretching and gave each other a kiss on the cheek with a smile. One of them, refereed to as Andy, turned to Jeff and winked, “Hi, pumpkin. Sleep well?” Garvin’s eyes bulged from the skull. “You know why I’m in here? I KILLED A TOWN FULL OF PEOPLE WITH ONLY A SPOON!” Both the gay Klan members giggled like schoolgirls. “Oh, that ones cute.” The other commented on Jeff, Andy merely nodded. Oh how Jeff could feel his skin crawl, all he wanted to do was slap both them in the Hammer Jammer and listen to their bones snap as they screeched like women, he would simply laugh and dance on their broken bodies. Yes, The Original does have a sinister side, a side that could make a World War 2 veteran cry like a baby. Who can tell if Jeff would have snapped if another sickening comment had been dished his way because before he even had a chance to contemplate it, Sergeant David Breau stepped infront of the cell door. He wore his beige police officers uniform, complete with hat and gold badge. He reeked of an expensive coloyn that couldn’t be made out. A moment later, he produced a set of keys from around his leather belt and inserted into the key hole. The door slid open and David stepped in. “Bail has been posted.” Jeff’s jaw dropped to the floor, no not literally you fool, he’s not a leper, but who in God’s name would shell out the dough to get him out of jail. Most of the time it was the other way around… “But… But… I never even got a longitudinal visit! I never got laid yet!” Jeff screamed, almost falling off his bunk, if you look hard enough, you can actually see the tears in his eyes. Poor Jeff, why won’t some one just buy him a hooker? “Oh, we can fix that, baby.” The Original turned his head to see one of the homosexuals blow him a kiss. “Okay. Nuff’ chat, just let me the fuck out!” Garvin leapt from the bed and flew passed the guard. For those short, twenty four hours that Jeff was behind bars, sharing a sell with two horny gay men, the world was a better place. Then hell froze over, somebody actually wanted the Garvinator back out walking the streets. But the question was who? Who was stupid enough, who was dumb enough, who in God’s name was that dim-witted to do such a thing? Who was this moron in question?
