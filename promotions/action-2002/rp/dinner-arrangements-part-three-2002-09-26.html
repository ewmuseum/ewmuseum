Dinner Arrangements: Part Three

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 26th, 2002 Still Dowtown. Tap. Tap. Tap. “Please, sir, stop tapping the glass. It disturbs them greatly.” Garvin took his hand away from the large, glass lobster tank and looked up to see a tall, Italian waiter with a towel hung over his forearm. Looking quite ‘snooty’. “Think I can pet the little critters? I use to have a lobster as a pet when I was young, then he died because I neglected him and stabbed him with a pencil… I kind of did it too hard. So how about it, can I pet him!” Sad ‘Garvin’ face. “…No… We do not allow that here.” Replied the waiter. “Even if I was too… you know…” Wink. Wink. Jeff dug in his pocket and produced a nickel and two sticks of chewing gum. “Will this do?” “No. Sir, look at your hands, they’re filthy you could infect them.” Jeff stared up at the waiter, his hands in his pockets, where they had been since they had entered the dining area. They were filthy however, some of the oils from the painting had begun to rub off on him, leaving a large white spot in the middle of the art work. “…How did- Bah. Whatever.” Jeff grunted and sauntered off back to his table. He sat down as Claire and Odium read through their menus. Claire was the first to put hers down, knowing exactly what she was going to order even before picking up the menu. “Hello, are you ready to order?” The same waiter that refused to let Jeff ‘pet’ the lobsters appeared in front of them, a note pad in his right hand and a pen in his left. Claire smiled, “Yes, I’ll have the chicken alfredo and a glass of white wine.” “Excellent choice, Ms. Moore. And you?” He said, turning to Odium. “Hard liquor and the cheapest piece of steak you can find.” “…Okay.” He rolled his eyes, turning to Jeff. “A Happy Meal with a small milk. And I better get the toy, cause if I don’t, WHAM! HAMMER JAMMER!” “What the fuck, this isn’t fuckin’ McDonalds, stupid!” Odium spoke up. “Sir, we don’t carry those…” “Then give me a lobster… But I want it alive and inside a bag. If it’s dead, the Smooth Brothers will have to open a can of the W to the A… WHOOOOOOOOOOOOP ASS!” Sigh. “Fine. I’ll see what I can do.”
