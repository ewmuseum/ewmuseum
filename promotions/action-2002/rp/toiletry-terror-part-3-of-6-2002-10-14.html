Toiletry Terror [Part 3 of 6]

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 14 An old Volkswagen Combi van trundles along the highway, spitting smoke and God knows what from the arse end of the machine. “Oh ah, Chitty Chitty Bang Bang, Chitty Chitty Bang Bang, we love you! POOP Oh ee, Chitty Chitty Bang Bang, Chitty Chitty Bang Bang, yes we do! POOP” The John stares at his partner in crime who has taken the wheel. He stares at him as though he were about to erupt with antagonism. He taps Captain Obvious on the shoulder. His managed to stop him singing for a short while, as he drawn his attention as to what he’s about to say. Captain Obvious shoots glances at the John whilst he watches the road with an ounce of concentration. “The John says you should shut your plug hole or the John’s gonna plug it.” “POOPBut Johnny, it’s from the Walt Disney movie Chitty Chitty Bang Bang! It was made in 1968, y’know! And the director was Ken POOPHughes. He was rather lucky to have scored himself Dick Van Dyke who, incidentally, starred alongside Julie Andrews. That POOP movie was Mary Poppins and that came out four years earlier. But that wasn’t directed by Ken Hughes, oh no. That was a Robert Stevenson movie, y’know? It’s true. And that too POOP was a musical, I know songs from that movie too.” The John stared out his window as he waited for time to pass. He remembered how he first met Captain Obvious… Once upon a time there was a mental asylum. It wasn't maximum security, it was just where people went when life had been a little frenzied. There was a man, his name was Steven, but he preferred people to call him the John, Protector of the Lavatory. He was a big man. Little children often called him a giant. But he was a gentle giant. Unless people were naughty to their water closet. One day, Steven was eating his dinner. The mash was peas and steak, the solids were potato. This was Steven's favourite, he couldn't wait for the mashed ice cream for desert. It was his FAVOURITE DESERT! He ate it, and he enjoyed it. He picked up his fork and said, 'Here comes the aeroplane!' And he put the food in his mouth. Steven loved that game. He usually played it at meal time. But today, Agnes, the 64-year-old schizophrenic, didn't like Steven's game. She thought it would be funny to play a trick on Steven. She said to Steven, 'Follow me, Steven.' And Steven followed her, Steven didn't want to make Agnes the 64-year-old schizophrenic upset. Because she would have an episode. So he followed her, and she went to the toilet, she asked him to watch the toilet door for her. So Steven did. And Agnes came out and she said, 'Ha ha, Steven, I pissed on the seat!' Steven was upset. He was friends with the toilet, he didn't want wee-wees on the seat. He told her to wipe it with some toilet paper. Agnes didn't want to. This made Steven mad. Steven had once been a wrestler, before he got hit in the head too many times. The last time having had his head put through the porcelain of a toilet. Steven remembered his wrestling days, and thought that he should do one of his moves from his wrestling days on Agnes. As she was not particularly friendly to the toilet seat. So Steven did his favourite wrestling move. A spinning powerbomb. Smashing Agnes the 64-year-old schizophrenic through a dining table. This made the nurses angry. They used stun guns to control Steven. He didn't like stun guns. They made his hairs stand up and that made him feel funny. And they dragged Steven to solitary. Where they kept him there for a week. During this week, he had a fellow in-mate looking after him. A man named Patrick. Patrick was being rehabilitated. He had Tourette's syndrome. It made him say naughty words when he got excited or upset. But he had been practicing not to say naughty words. But Patrick also said the obvious. All the time. When they first met, Patrick opened the door and said, 'Doors open inward to avoid hitting the person who visits you. Most doors are made of wood, except this particular door is made of metal. Though on your side there is protective material, it's made from poly-urethane and rubber, so as if you get too crazy, you won't hurt yourself. Wow, what a door!' And Patrick and Steven became friends. Patrick and Steven would talk together. Often. And they found out that Patrick was once a wrestler too. But unlike Steven, Patrick was a Luchadore. He did high flying moves and daredevil stunts. Steven was impressed. He said to Patrick, 'One day, Patrick, you and the John,' he always referred to himself in the third person, 'you and the John will leave Shady Acres Psychiatric Facility. And when we do, Patrick, you and the John will become a wrestling tag team.' Patrick liked this idea. He knew that one day they would both be released. And because they were best friends now, they would become this tag team. They would become the best tag team. EVER! And now, this day has finally arrived, Patrick and Steven were let out of the Shady Acres Psychiatric Facility. And they have become a wrestling tag team. The best wrestling tag team ever. According to Steven. The End. The John smiled as his flashback ended. He sighed contently as he looked over at his buddy for life. “And a spoonful of POOP sugar makes the medicine go down! The medicine go down. Medicine go down…” His rather simple buddy for life.
