Five.

15 December 2002

Date: 12/15/02 Three days. That’s about how long I’ve had to sit at home, on my couch, thinking about what I did to Roland. Three days, not even a fucking week, and I can hardly keep my mind off the subject. The night had concluded, and once again, I kept to myself- wrestled, got changed, and left the arena as fast as I could. I went out of my way tonight to not come face to face with Roland, nor be in the same room as him. At this point, I wasn’t sure if Mr. Johnson had dealt with him following my selfish attempt to gain a bit of success- another week, another win. I was beginning to feel that Jim was feeding me the guys that he would be firing soon after. It was my job to walk down the isle, look good, and pin them one after another. I couldn’t help but believe that all this was no coincidence. My merchandise was rapidly becoming bigger- action figures, t shirts, and sun glasses were only the beginning. I got all of this for crawling to Jim like some sort of animal looking for scraps and snitched on someone who didn’t deserve it one fucking bit. How I’ll live with myself, I really have no idea. How I’ll ever be able to speak to Roland, ditto on that account. At this moment, I truly am fucked. August 28th, 2000 Mike’s Bar and Grill Shortly After PIW Monday Massacre Half a dozen empty bottles of Molson Canadian beer were lined up font on the old hickory table. From the looks of things, a Monday was not a record setting day for this tavern in question- only three or four of the regular drunks were lined up by the counter, drowning their sorrows in a shot glass while watching a local boxing match on Pay Per View. Sitting at the table furthest away from the crowd was Sean Cylear. He wore blue jeans and a black sweater. He leaned back in the chair and sighed heavily, staring up at the long tube lights hanging from the ceiling. “One more, please,” asked Sean, raising one finger up to get the bartender’s attention. One more beer couldn’t hurt. The house show for Tuesday evening had been canceled due to problems with the stadiums power generator. The plan was simple; drink a beer, take a shot of rum, drink a beer, eat some pretzels, take a shot, drink yet another beer and repeat this process until said person has no recollection of who or where he is. Mike, the owner and bartender, set down another bottle of Canadian brew. If there was one thing Sean liked about touring Canada it was their beer. While in mid-chug, a man walked in from the outside. It was only when he pulled out a stool and spoke that Sean’s attention was captured. “Yeah, a beer.” Sean put the down the bottle and locked over across the bar. LLB. He almost fell out of his chair, Roland had spotted him and he didn’t look happy. Jim probably slipped his name in while giving him a verbal lashing. “Hey, you mind if I sit here, bro?” Said LLB with a mildly friendly tone. Sean nodded. “Liked your match tonight, man, was well done. Jim must see something in you, he doesn’t usually push guys this fast. Took me several months to get where I am now and it looks as if I may be out of a job soon. Jim found out about a conversation I had with Fox a couple days ago and he’s making all kinds of fucking threats. I don’t know how he found out- must have cameras mounted to the walls or something,” he laughed, trying to lighten up the situation a tad. Sean gave a fake chuckle, grabbing his beer and downing the last bit of its contents. “Like, I don’t want to jump to conclusions, but I think one of the guys may have ratted on me. Don’t know who, I just get that feeling, you know? Like something isn’t right. The wrestlers are suppose to be a family, you didn’t fuck over your family. But hey, I don’t want to give you the wrong impression because you seem like a good hard working guy. PIW can be a great place to work, but if I were you, I’d stay as far away from Jim Johnson as possible. The guy is like a virus, he corrupts you… he uses you and then when he’s done, he chucks you to the curb. I know from personal experience,” he said, standing up, “I got to go, got an autograph signing early tomorrow, but listen, if you ever need some one to talk to if things get rough, you know where to reach me… Later.” With that, Roland left the bar, leaving quite a large tip on the bar counter before disappearing out the door. Sean didn’t say word. He needed to put an end to all of this. And soon…
