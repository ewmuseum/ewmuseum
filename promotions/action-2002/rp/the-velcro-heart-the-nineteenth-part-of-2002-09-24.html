The Velcro Heart, The Nineteenth Part of

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/24/02 The night after the phone call. Tuesday. And the night before Pressure Point would normally take place... The night... LLB would never forget. ‘The Law’ walked into the Nationwide Arena in Columbus, Ohio, home to the house-show tour titled ‘Ratings!’! A Reed Young creation... of course. LLB turned to his locker room door, opening it up as Reed Young sat there, smiling. “ROWE!” He screamed, jumping up in the air as he gave the Champion a hug. But LLB just pushed him away. “What?” “Tonight we’re gonna give everyone an OVERDOSE of ratings!” “But Reed, this is a house show. It’s not on TV-” “It’s alright. I thought about it... and don’t you want revenge upon what KroW did to you last show?” Young grinned. LLB could only nod his head. “Yes. Yes I do. I’ll kill that little fucker for objecting to the law like that!” The Owner walked up to LLB, as he patted him on the shoulder. “Well then, Rowe... I got it all planned out for you. Because tonight, it’s KroW and Christopher Fox... versus Steven Denton and... You.” However the shocker... was what was going to happen during this battle.
