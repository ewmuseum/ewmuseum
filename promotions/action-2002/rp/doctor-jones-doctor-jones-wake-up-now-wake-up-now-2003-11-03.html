Doctor Jones, Doctor Jones Wake Up Now (Wake Up Now).

3 November 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reed Young shrugged. “What do we do now?” He asked. No one was sure though. They all wanted to dance… but the door had flown back on its hinges and was killing this party. “Alright!” Yelled Thomas the Mohawk Machine. He had stopped making out with TIGERstripe and was flat out pissed off. The only reason he had stopped was because the party was over. Now it just wasn’t much fun anymore. Dammit! “Who’s the wise guy that did this?” Thomas looked around the room… but everyone seemed innocent. “I WANT ANSWERS!” And after a long awaited silence… their answer had come. He came in through the ceiling, as the roof collapsed on his entrance. Pieces of crushed dry wall covered the faces of everyone in attendance… as they stared this man down with much anger and hate. “Hi.” He said. This was a man of no more than twenty. Surely he loves to party! Everyone under the age of twenty does! So why in the hell was he killing this party? “Yes… I am the one who put the door back on its hinges.” He said. “My name is The Faggot. I am called The Faggot because no one likes me and I kill parties!” Reed shed a tear. “But wwwwhhhhyyyy…” The Faggot rolled his eyes. “I just told you.” Young fell to his knees. “But WWWWHHHHYYYY…” “I just told you.” “But WWWWHHHHYYYY…” “I just told you.” “But WWWWHHHHYYYY…” “I just told you.” “Oh.” Young turned to The Giant Bunny Rabbit of DOOM. “This sucks.” And all everyone could do… was just stand there and take it. Their party had stopped. All was not cool in the world of what was supposed to be cool! If you know what I mean. The Faggot leaned back in his chair. “So how’s everyone doing?” …Silence… “Well fine then. Give me the silent treatment.” SILENT TREATMENT. SILENT TREATMENT. SILENT TREATMENT. “Hhmm… well, boy that Daniel Sedin is a good pick up for my hockey pool. I mean what an awesome player! I first was thinking Henrik Sedin was the way to go… but not right now! DANIEL ALL THE WAY!” SILENT TREATMENT. SILENT TREATMENT. SILENT TREATMENT. “That Patrisha Bergeron has been awesome too. Same with that Zedlicky guy from Nashville. Yeppy I’m getting a lot of points this week.” SILENT TREATMENT. SILENT TREATMENT. SILENT TREATMENT. “So who do you all have in your pools?” Young spoke. “Oh, I have Curtis Joseph .” The Faggot laughed. “I don’t know why he hasn’t picked up points for me yet though…” SILENT TREATMENT. SILENT TREATMENT. SILENT TREATMENT. Finally, having enough, and looking over at TIGERstripe… Thomas wanted to make out some more… so he dove for the stereo player, and started to punch it. PUNCH. Every punch turned the music back on and made it louder. “No!” The Faggot yelled. “What are you doing!?” Thus the door was beginning to shake. It was going to fall off its hinges again. “I SAID: NO! WHAT ARE YOU DOING!?” The Faggot cried. Thomas stopped punching the stereo. “Oh, I’m punching the stereo.” He said. The Faggot nodded. “Alright, just making sure.” Thomas went back to his punching rage. “NO!” Cried The Faggot again. “You’re ruining everything!” Like Thomas was listening. He just looked back at TIGERstripe and saw those luscious lips. He HAD to get some more! PUNCH PUNCH FROOT PUNCH WATERMELON BLAST. The door was about to fall. But then The Faggot used his magical powers and made it stay! “NO!” Thomas yelled. “We have to do something!” It finally hit The Mad T-REX From the Planet ZORGTECH. “Everybody sing!” So they did. “We need to call for DOCTOR JONES!” “Brilliant!” D-Dopin’ Dan said. “Let’s do it!” Hence they began. “DOCTOR JONES, JONES, CALLING DOCTOR JONES…” “NO!” The Faggot screamed at the top of his lungs. “DOCTOR JONES DOCTOR JONES GET UP NOW!” Young provided the background lyrics. “WAKE UP NOW!” “NO STOP SINGING! YOU’LL WAKE DOCTOR JONES UP!” But that’s what they wanted. “DOCTOR JONES, JONES, CALLING DOCTOR JONES… DOCTOR JONES DOCTOR JONES WAKE UP NOW! (WAKE UP NOW)!” Again. “DOCTOR JONES, JONES, CALLING DOCTOR JONES… DOCTOR JONES DOCTOR JONES GET UP NOW! (WAKE UP NOW)!” And one… more… time!!!!!!!!!!!!! “DOCTOR JONES, JONES, CALLING DOCTOR JONES… DOCTOR JONES DOCTOR JONES WAKE UP NOW! (WAKE UP NOW)!” BBBBBOOOOMMMM!! The building shook. The Faggot buried his head. “I…” A voice said in the sky. “Am Doctor…” “Wait!” Young shouted. “Let me guess!” “Okayyy…” “Is it… uh…” Young had to think. “God?” “Nooo.” “Oh, my bad.” Young said. “Can I guess again?” “Okayyy…” “Is it…” Young thought and thought and thought and thought. “My Mom?” “Yes.” But this was a MAN’s voice! “HI MOM!” “HIIII.” Reed waved. “Come home for dinner.” “Okay.” Young looked to everyone else. “I have to go home for dinner. I think my Mom is calling.” But as Reed walked to the exit door, The Faggot came across and blocked the way out. “Uh… can you move?” “NO!” Reed didn’t know what to do. “DOCTOR JJJJJJJOOOOOONNNNNNNNEEEEEEEEEESSSSSSSSS!” He yelled. Once again the room shook. Finally someone appeared. He was dressed in white and hot pink, and he floated in the air. “I am-” Young cut him off. “Wait, can I guess?” “Sure.” “Uh… are you… are you…” He was really rubbing his head. “My Dad?” “Yes.” Wait. “Wait I mean no!” LOL. “My bad.” “It’s cool.” “I am…” He paused. “Doctor Jones.” “Wake up Doctor Jones!” Said Thomas. “I am awake.” “Oh yeah.” Thomas blushed because everyone was looking. “So you are.” “Anyway… I now banish The Faggot to GO… TO… SLEEP!” “YAY!” Went everyone in the hotel room. “NO!” Yelled The Faggot who then vanished into thin air and was transferred to Dream World. Phillip spoke up. “How can we thank you?” Christopher asked Doctor Jones. “Just party.” Was all the D-R said… before he faded into nothing again. “HURRAY!” Said everyone. And once more, the music blasted throughout the hotel room, and everyone partied. The Giant Bunny Rabbit of DOOM danced like mad, TIGERstripe made out with Thomas… and so on. “I love this.” Reed said with a grin. “These are my best friends and we’re having fun.” SNAP! Young woke up in his dark, abandoned hotel room. “So…” He said to himself. “This was all a dream?” Young paused. Then he shrugged. “Seemed pretty normal to me.” And going back to bed, Young turned over… but as he did, the scene went to his closet… where two eyes were shown glowing from inside it. The Giant Bunny Rabbit of DOOM just smiled.
