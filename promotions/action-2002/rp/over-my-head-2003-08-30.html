Action! — Tales

Over My Head

30 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OPEN New faces and some old ones too. Turning the pages, looking at the pictures Christopher Fox sat beside his beloved Pikachu. They were sitting in their car, checking out a scrap book that highlighted the big matches of Fox’s career. As the ‘Kid’ scanned through the book, pictures were passed of such events like Juggernaut I, when LLB went wild on Fox after the match.... while other photos included the Card Scramble battle against Steven Denton when Fox grabbed the last Pokemon card. Fox “Meh these matches weren’t so great! I wasn’t even a bad ass! I could so put a better show on for those ‘friends’ of mine, but I won’t because they betrayed me! The only person I can rely on is me!” .... Fox “Oh and the only Pokemon I can rely on is Pikachu! BIG TIME!” Title: Over My Head Date: Thursday August 17th, 2003 12:47 pm EST Mood: Optimistic Christopher Elliot Fox sat, unbuckled in his yellow Pikachu car, right beside his loyal companion. Fox reached back and placed the scrappy scrap book on the back seat and then relaxed in his seat. Fox “I’ve been talking too much, haven’t I Pikachu?” .... Fox “Yeah you’re right, it’s time for action! And after all I’m a man of Action!” One fact that he didn’t point out was that he was in Action as well. Fox “I wonder if I’ll show up to the arena tonight. I haven’t for awhile.... but if you think about it, its good because my ex-friends would just boo me and yell at me like they always do! Pikachu, I think I’m in over my head. I mean, I’m only one person.... and out there, they out-number me! And I mean big time out number me.” But if I don’t go to wrestle in front of those maniacs then I won’t get the bling bling, or as I call it the Cha-Ching! LOL! Hehehehe. Fox “No worries I guess. It’s time to show everyone that I didn’t just disappear, and it’s time to win the Action Championship! BIG TIME! BIG TIME! BIG TIME! BIG TIME! BIG TIME! BIG TIME! BIG TIME!!!!!!!!!!!! WOOOOOO!” Vrr....... Vrrrrrrrrrroooooooooommmm! The engine roared as Fox turned the car on and sped off down the road. A quick trip, as Fox had to stop at a close-by red light.... and that’s when....
