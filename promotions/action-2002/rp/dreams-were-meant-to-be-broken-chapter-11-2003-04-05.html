Dreams were meant to be broken: Chapter 11

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Snoooooooooooooooooooort. 'This is the shit, Luscious!' Enrique blurted out after tucking a line into the back of his brain. Paul moved across to the mirror, Enrique stepped aside to let Paul numb up. Luscious just sat in the crapper, empting his mind if you will. Snoooooooooooooooooooort. Paul stuaggered backward as that last hit really hit him in the spot, he slid down the wall and shut his eyes for a few moments. His last few moments on earth would be filled with bliss. Enrique would be hungry for more. Lots more. Fiend stepped into the bathroom. They did not know his face. Nor who he was. They were fucking wired and they were desperate for another hit. They'd just all their stash up their nose and were thirsting for more. Fiend walked to the taps, washing his hands, just ignoring the pair of stoned mullets with him in the bathroom, but Enrique caught him looking at him in the mirror. 'Who are joo, huh? Why joo fucking stare at me, punk?' Enrique bellowed at Fiend. Fiend turned and looked to their little piece of mirror and empty bag beside it. 'Looking at you, I'm staring at someone who wants their next hit, right?' Fiend queried. He knew that Enrique would try to use force to get fiend's 'coke' off him. He was prepared for this. But Fiend wouldn't fight Enrique. No. Enrique's greed for being more off his dial than the Stones in their day would be enough to kill Enrique. 'Joo fucking give it up, pal, or I just take eeet of joo, k?' he explained to Fiend. Fiend nodded. He could kill this dog without even touching him. Paul had woken up at the idea of some more nose candy. 'Two birds with one stone,' he thought as he watched the pair grow hungry for the blowy in his pocket. Their last sniff.
