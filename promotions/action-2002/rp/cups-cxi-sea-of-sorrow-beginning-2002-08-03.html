Cups: cXI: Sea of Sorrow (Beginning)

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 3, 2002 Cups: cXI: Sea of Sorrow (Beginning) The only remains were in his hand. He gripped the thought tightly and felt the keen edges of the glass penetrate through his skin. Layers of his hide slid apart easily. James Madison watched as the cuts widened and the blood began to seep out at first. He felt no pain. Nothing compared to what he felt a few days earlier. He did not care what it was. No amount of physical pain could add up to approximately the collateral damage that pierced its way through his heart. James beckoned on the thought of life. Seeming to have faded away with his love. He felt embroiled in the threads of love and never to get untangled. James pushed his way through the entanglement and into salvation. He rested his head by snugly against a soft cushioned pillow. Minds work in some extraordinary ways, yet his was nearly idle. He felt what had happened to him had nearly taken his life away. The wounds on his hands worsened as he chuckled about wondering what to do next.          He watched the blood trickle about. Hand in hand, the blood covered both. Caked in his own sorrow, there were no more tears. James was slowly drowning in a Sea of Sorrow. The only problem was his boat had already sunk without him. He battled on, kicking the waves struggling to hold on to the life that he lives. James lifted his arms up reaching for the raft which was afloat above his head. In reality, he extended his arms and grabbed the bandages which took refuge in the cabinet above him. He felt around until he finally found the right one of size to cover the wound. It wasn't a matter of pain, but he knew what would come if they were not necessarily covered. He peeled away the cover to the bandage and placed it eagerly upon his skin. The soothing feeling led him with a sense that there was a point in life. It led him with the reality that he may carry on. Still though, seeing the handful of events that he had saw. Two mass murdering processes unfolded before his eyes as he fell backwards looking into the mirror. There it was. The words clearly stated it across his forehead. Joker was insane and drowning in a sea of sorry. At this point in time, he began to feel sorry for himself. The blood still rained through the bandage as much as it did before. He saw the instinctive actions and pressed his weight down upon it. At the very least, he tried to slow down the bleeding enough so that he would not pass out.          'It's over. There will be no more tears. Where will I get if I allow myself to drown in the over-rated Sea of Sorrow? I've got my raft, it's enough to make it to sure, metaphorically speaking. You see, I'm finally getting somewhere in life ... I feel its time I move on up.'
