I'm Better Than You

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/14/2003 OPEN Fox “Err leave me alone! This is an autograph signing! Now show me your damn poster ma’am!” Woman “Why I’ve never been so mistreated before in my life! You should be ashamed of yourself mister!” The woman snatched her Christopher Fox poster and trotted off with her head held high. Fox “I should be ashamed? ASHamed? Have you looked at your ‘ASH’? It’s so fucking huge! What a stupid bitch! ....Next!” Title: I’m Better Than You Date: 05/14/03 1:45 pm EST Place: Autograph Session in a Chapters Book Store There sat Christopher Fox and surrounding him and his precious Championship Belt were dozens of security guards dressed in black suits. It was one of the last things on Chris’ good list that he had to do - a autograph session. Except, the thing was that Fox wasn’t in a good mood today. Just like he had been for the past weeks on end, the ‘Kid’ had become a cranky adolescent. The only people that were getting yelled at were the young children and women.... and look, a small lad just walked up to the table with a Pikachu plush toy in hand. Fox “Oh hidey freakin’ ho little kid, what do you want!?” Little Kid “Ummm heckto. I waz wund-ring if you cud sign my toy.” Fox “Not even a pwease? See?, it isn’t my fault! You guys are rude to me! I shouldn’t even be wasting my time autographing things for stupid people who disrespect me! Not LOL! Gimmie your damn Pikachu bitch boy!” The Champion ripped the toy out of the kids’ hand and signed it. Fox then threw it in a nearby garbage can. Fox “There ya go!” Little Kid “Uh, why you do dat sir? I though you had a Peekachuuuu!?” Weirdly, Fox started to foam at the mouth. Fox “Yeah well kid, I left him at the hotel! There can only be one Pikachu and he’s mine! Sorry I have to eliminate the competition! See ya! Fox stood, turning his attention to the nearby guards. Fox “Remove this pawn!” The suits advanced on the small boy until he wet himself and ran away crying to his Mommy. Yeah! I’m the big M F’er on campus now! I’m the Action Champion and I’m better than all of you! Three more fans came up to the stand for an autograph, but Fox had enough. Fox “Sorry, my wrist hurts, we’re leaving, but you can see my superstar face on TV with this superstar Action Title Belt!” Chris got up and snatched his precious prize, wandering off with his body guards close behind. Fox “Ha! Losers! Mega losers.... big time, big time.” CLOSE
