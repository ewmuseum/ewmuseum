Dream end of a feud

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th November 2002 “IT’S ON! IT’S ON!” screamed Liam Ump, as Airborn Tyler and Jonny Yankee frantically exchanged punches in the middle of the ring. Lee Lightning, David Dew, Neko Case and Kayden Paulton had already been eliminated, and were out of the Virus match later in the night, but this wasn’t about the virus match. This was about winning a feud which had been raging for four months, which had warmed up Airborn and Yankee and made them as popular as anyone in the company. But there could only be one winner. Airborn got the advantage by digging a knee deep into the gut of Yankee who doubled up. Thinking quickly, Airborn grabbed Yankee’s neck and ran up the ropes, before spinning off with a tornado flipping neckbreaker. Yankee’s neck twisted sickly as it smacked into the canvas. Instead of going for the cover though, Airborn ran to the ropes and came off with a springboard fist drop. The impact of the move jerked Yankee so much he jumped to his feet in shock, clutching his chest in pain, allowing Airborn to clothesline him over the top rope to the floor. And only one thing will ever happen when Airborn Tyler sees an opponent vulnerable on the floor. “SHOOTING STAR PRESS PLANCHA!” cried Ump. Airborn had ran across the ring, jumped onto the ropes and flung his body to the outside in a shooting star press which wiped out Yankee, who was now disorientated. Airborn’s flailing leg had smacked him right in the cranium. Getting straight back to his feet, Airborn pumped his hand in the air, looking around at all the masses of fans cheering for him. This was how it should be. All his career he’d been doing his best night in night out to impress these people, and finally he was receiving his dues. Airborn rolled Yankee back into the ring, and entered himself with a slingshot corkscrew leg drop across the neck of Yankee. Finally, Airborn knew this was the moment to win the feud, to end four months of torment and move on with his career. With a big gasp of oxygen, Airborn grabbed the elastic ropes and swung himself to the top. Looking out at all the flashing cameras, he knew this was his destiny and would earn himself many a title shot in the future. But the fans would love him for it. Airborn Bomb! The dazzling corkscrew swanton bomb landed right across Jonny Yankee’s chest, and Airborn remained on top of his opponent, reaching back only to hook a leg precariously close to the ropes. He was taking no chances. One… Two… Three! The crowd went crazy, as the referee rose his hand. Sure, he was battered and bruised, but the four months of physical strain was worth it for these ten seconds of glory. Then Benjamin Tyler woke up.
