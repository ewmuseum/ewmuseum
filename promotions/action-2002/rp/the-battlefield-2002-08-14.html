The Battlefield

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 14, 2002 Every human being lives in his own personal hell, lives in his own personal heaven, or lives in his own personal purgatory. Every life is different, filled with different forms of happiness and different forms of sorrow. Not one single life on this world is the same...every single life is unique...especially mine. My name...I don't know. My home...I don't know. My life...is The Battlefield. For as long as I can remember, I have always been on the battlefield, fighting and thriving for victory. For the past seven years I have continued to battle through all adversity, and in most cases...I have been the victor. I have fought through it all, and have beat it all. I have OWNED my Battlefield... From the moment I stepped in, I was victorious. I still remember the first opponent I ever had. He was named Shawn Shock...he was a big man, nearly eight feet tall...but with his power, his strength, and his veteran past...he still feel victim to me. I was too much for him, I was the almighty warrior that all feared, that all never wished to fight... I was born. It soon led to much much more. To bigger names, to names that were circulated throughout the world...names like Chris Thunder, names like Sean Cylear, names like Shawn Steele, names like Stephen Blackeheart, names like Extremist, names like John Headcase. Every single name was either erased from the world, or never the same again. John Headcase, my first victim. He was a veteran of the sport, he was a pro. He taught me everything I know now. He taught me the defensives of wrestling, he taught me the offensives of wrestling. He was like a father to me, he looked after me and made sure I did everything right. Which was the reason I had to kill him. He was always superior, I was just a simple lackey. I stood by his side through all the sins he committed, I stood by when he cheated on his own wife. I stood by when he backstabbed his own brother. I stood by when he sold out to the owner...and I was sick of him being the top priority. I WANTED TO BE THE ULTIMATE FIGHTER! And I soon became just that... When I watched...his life end...with a sound...of a crack. The next victim was Chris Thunder. A man who had the ego the size of the entire world. He believe his idiocy would overcome my power. He thought he was something big, something worth while, something like a warrior. But in reality, he was nothing like that. He was pathetic. And I don't let pathetic people live. Amazing what a sledgehammer can do...when you use it right... The following victim was Stephen Blackeheart. A man put through pure hell in PIW. I destroyed his heart, his mind, his soul, his body...his career. I remember it like yesterday, the screams of pain that came forth when me and my minions beat him to the ground, watching as his blood stained my battlefield...and watching me end his career...and take his Hardcore Title. I ended his active life...because he got into my path to victory... The next victim...heh...brings a smile to my face...The Extremist. My god, probably one man who deserved to die more than anyone else. He used me, just like I used John Headcase...but when I did it...it was justified...when he did it...it was treason. And NO ONE commits treason against me...and he did. And thus...he died. Revenge was the reason, nothing more. And than came Sean Cylear. A man with his whole career ahead of him. A man who loved the sport and loved to work every-single-day. I hate happiness in life...its a pure sign of weakness...and thus...I ended his misery of a life...when I broke his neck. He would never wrestle again...and without wrestling...he just couldn't live... He killed himself physically. But I killed him mentally. And in every single victim I have broken...I have never felt remorse or mercy. I have always loved what I had done. When the images of mangled flesh, broken bones and broken lives came into my mind...I smile, up to this day. I love the misery of the battlefield, especially when I cause it. And especially when it ends a man... When you cry, I laugh. When you are sad, I am joyfull. When you are depressed, I am angry. When you are weak, I am strong. It's just how the battlefield has gone...I am the victor, the victims are the losers. I was always the victorious warrior...and the one thing that remained the same in each... Was the prize. In every single battle..the prize was a Hardcore Title. But in this most recent battle... It's for a bigger prize. The World Title. Something I've never gone for. It doesn't make me fear LLB. But it makes me think. Am I truely ready for this? Am I truely ready for something this big? Am I ready for the big time? The questions race through my mind... as this battle is different from all the others. This time, I fight for something bigger...because fate sees me doing so. This time, determination and anger are not my only emotions going in this battle... Being in the big time...is added. But can I do it? Can I? Is this warrior truely ready to be a leading soldier? Is he?
