Courage - Finale

26 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/26/2002 'Coral, you're insane. Just absolutely insane.' Joey Malone said. The two Lindum sisters, Coral, and Joey were sitting in Joey Malone's house in Mesa. They were seemingly talking about stuff. 'I mean, you're not serious, are you? The guy's a loon. I've never met anyone more ridiculous than he is, and I was in IWO when Levine was pushing Crow to the moon.' Joey said. 'I'm out of a job, Joey. I sacrificed it when I came here to be with Ali.' Coral said. Allison smiled warmly at him in response. 'I mean, I know the guy's an annoying idiot... I had the displeasure of meeting him the last time you were in Orleans. But still, it's like the only place I can turn to.' 'But to work for REED YOUNG?!' Joey said, still stunned at Coral's idea. 'Come on, take it from Keri and me, you don't want this guy in your life.' Keri nodded. 'Just imagine a three year old kid with lots of money.' 'I've already made up my mind.' Coral said. 'I don't know how to do anything else than be a wrestler, and nobody else will take me in, okay? Action's the only place with an owner stupid enough to sign me up.' Joey had to smile at that one. 'Okay, okay, fine. We got a show coming up in Dallas, I'll talk to Reed about it beforehand. You should be there.' Joey said. 'If we do convince Reed, he'll want you to sign a contract. That's kind of the rule, y'know.' Coral nodded. Hopefully, nobody will steal his damn money on his first day, this time. Then he glanced down one of the hallways in the Malone residence, before something clicked in his mind and he had another crazy idea that Joey might not like. 'Oh yeah. I want to rent that room out.' Coral said, jerking his thumb at the empty room. Joey had a stunned look on his face, as he looked over at that room, then over at Keri, who just simply shrugged. 'Joey, it's not as if we've even tried to fill it in.' Coral smiled. 'You know I'm good for it, and I don't own much anyway. I think the only things I'd need are a bed, some clothes, and my autograph book that I will soon sell on E-Bay for loads of cash, and I'm set.' Joey thought about it. 'Heh. Okay. But as soon as you start hoarding our food, out you go.' Corla and Allison smiled at each other. Maybe everything'll go fine after all. Then again, knowing these people... probably not.
