Lonely March

9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 09, 2002 The General aimlessly wandered the dark streets of Oakland. He had simply felt like going for a walk, and so he let his feet lead him where they pleased. Nearly a week ago, he had proven that he had at least a little bit of talent in his body. He had escaped the gravity-defying flip that Airborn Tyler had committed his body to, and the General had proceeded to make his way back to the ring. He didn’t know why he did it, He just knew that once the referee got to the number six, the crowd started counting along with him. That made it seem like the counting meant something...it was important. Once the crowd got more antsy with the counting, Jonny decided it was best to rush back to the ring. He didn’t have anything to lose in the ring. As it turned out, Jonny gained a lot. He gained a victory. He gained a lot of fans. He gained a spot in Action! Jonny hadn’t planned on Airborn Tyler getting hurt...but causalities were an occurrence in wars. Airborn Tyler was a causality. “Hey, you’re kinda cute!” The woman’s shrill voice interrupted the General’s train of thought. She made her way towards him, working her hips. The short red skirt hugged every curve of her body, and it was quite a surprise that it didn’t propel up with her movements to reveal her unmentionable area. The matching top she wore complimented her chest. She kept walking... “Halt.” Jonny stopped her. She pulled the sucker out of her mouth and looked at him. “E’scuse me?” She wanted to go ghetto on him, she really did...but he would be her only hook of the night, and she needed money. She moved in on him and cupped her hand over his crotch. She whispered what she’d like to do to him, and she asked him where his place was. Jonny pointed behind her. She turned around and her jaw dropped at the site. In an alley between two skyscrapers was a doggie tent. A small fire was trying hard to survive. Tied to one of the tent’s stake was a rope. That rope led to a beautiful horse. The prostitute looked at Jonny once more and shook her head. “Fuckin’ weirdo.” She turned and left, leaving Jonny to melt in those words. Jonny was still just a soldier lost in time.
