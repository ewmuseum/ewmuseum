The Velcro Heart, The Fifteenth Part of

27 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He slowly walked down the parking lot road, as he approached the Manchester Stadium in Manchester, England. It seemed like a nice place. Classy. Very classy. It was a shame though... that LLB didn’t feel like he should be a part of it. Reed Young ran a hard schedule. Wednesday’s were the worst of times for anything. It was the middle of the week... and though ‘The Law’ didn’t do much past that, (the odd house show... the daily workout...) every Pressure Point he’d work to full capacity, trying to give Reed Young the best he could get. Because, to Reed... he gave him the Title anyway. And for some strange reason... LLB might’ve found sense in those statements. It didn’t matter though. What happened... happened. If LLB wasn’t able to appear on Joe Campbell’s Show, he had his reasons. It was recovery time... let alone the Asylum canvas flooring had already been dubbed unhealthy for his club feet. Sure, he felt bad... really bad. Because deep down inside, he liked the Asylum... and yes, he even liked Joe Campbell. Joe ran a good product. He might seem too open, too rude and too cold, but inside that frozen body, there was a velcro heart just like LLB’s. He cared. He worked. He had the love for his fighting. It was just something new... so people shunned it, and from that alone... Joe Campbell got his cold, heartless ways, able to retract his heart from inside his body. Well... it seemed right to LLB, at least. And that’s what made LLB want to turn away right then and there, as he placed his right hand upon the door handle to open it up... He could leave. No one would care. He could leave. For the same man would win. But to every end there is a start... and to every start, there is an end. LLB opened the door and walked in. He was going in the Iliad Match this night... to support the team name of the Asylum. He was going in to support Joe Campbell’s love for this world just like ‘The Law’. He was going in because he wanted to... But he was really going in... because deep down inside he just had to.
