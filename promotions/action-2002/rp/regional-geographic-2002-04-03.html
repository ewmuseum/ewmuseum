Action! — Tales

Regional Geographic

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 3, 2002 Simon sat in his natural environment, consisting of a hotel room that was fully equipped with a small television with cable, a miniature cabinet filled with miniature liquor bottles, and a miniature refrigerator. For leprechauns and the vertically impaired this hotel was not. Rather for those who were cheap and or scared of large objects. The warm glow of the television bounced off the walls of the room as he simply sat there with a blank stare, channel surfing while doing the strenuous task. With his forehead heavily bandaged and a crutch at his side, a deep, but loud English voice emanated from nowhere as he used his crutch to grab himself a bowl of chips near the television. 'The elusive Simon Seaman is a creature that defies all odds. In the wild that is known to some as 'the real world', one must fight to survive. By hook or by crook, in this world, you either are eating or you are the one that is being eaten.' Distracted by the mysterious voice, he knocks the bowl onto the ground as chips go astray on the floor below. 'Which is easier said than done.' We join him the next day shopping inside a supermarket on a wheelchair, moving through the aisle attempting to avoid pedestrians on this road of hunger. 'His appearance may fool the naked eye, but to the well dressed eye, his perfect complexion and above average bone structure is something that should be taken very seriously. For those are characteristics of this type of species that only scratch the surface of how complicated these creatures really are. Although they are known to retrieve resources like food and shelter much faster than others, there is no doubt that they earn it. Watch as a task as simple as retrieving milk from a supermarket in the wild is turned from a negative into a positive.' He comes to a halt several feet from the freezer door. Reaching with all his might, he is unable to retrieve the so-called oral delight that is skim milk. About to tip over on his chair, he pulls himself back at the last second and sighs in relief. Adjusting his lime green suit, which is ironically the same colour as his wheelchair, he attempts a second time, but fails yet again. 'This is where his good looks really come into play.' Several hours later, an elderly lady is seen helping Simon receive what he was looking for. Thanking her generously with a handshake, she responds to that kind gesture by handing him a napkin with her phone number on it. She walks away with great ambition as Simon crumples the napkin up and throws it over his shoulder feeling somewhat violated, moving on with his day. 'Yes, it has done the body good. Really good.' We fast forward to Simon at a dance club, sharing drinks with an attractive blonde. They share a laugh as the voice is heard over their conversation. 'As you may be aware of this, such livings things find it fairly easy to pursue and attain possible mates. Their overall attractive appearance and charm have boggled the minds of many scientists for several years. Questions like 'How do they do it?' or 'Is there something in the drinking water?' as well as 'Were there drugs involved?' might arise, but have not been answered as of yet. Whether or not this animal magnetism is temporary or permanent is not known, but it should be implied that for any meeting with Simon Seaman and a female species is a successful one.' Next thing we know, we are taken to the entrance of Simon's hotel room where a 'Do Not Disturb' sign hangs loosely on the doorknob. Muffled noises are heard inside as the sign sways back and forth. 'When injured or physically unstable, the mating habits of these individuals can be stressful and rewarding at the same time. It is imperative that one must avoid any confrontations with Seaman during these occurrences as the danger of getting involved in such an experience may possibly be fatal.' Simon and his lady friend are heard from inside of the room. 'What was that?' 'Oh, it's my crutch.' Simon uttered, as he tossed it aside. Seconds later, a loud thud is noticed as the woman screams in terrible pain. 'Now what?' She said, surely annoyed. 'Sorry about the wheelchair. You can just ignore it for now. I’m just using it because it gives me better mobility.' The woman is seen storming out of the room as suddenly, we move on to a scene of Simon by himself in his hotel room watching television. 'Many believe Simon Seaman is vulnerable to attack from its predators and in some ways, that is so. Though it should be remembered that all members of this wild kingdom have defense mechanisms.' Housekeeping is heard knocking on the door, but he ignores it. Several knocks later, he continues to sit in his room not wanting to move a muscle. 'Whether big or small, fat or skinny, kung fu master or not, everyone has distinct tactics used to defend themselves and their territory. Simon Seaman, although slightly immobile, is still able to attack with vigor and effectiveness.' The housekeeper begins to yell at the top of her lungs as Seaman rolls his eyes clearly annoyed. Pulling the plug on his television, he picks up the television and limps to the door. Finally opening it for the housekeeper, he throws the thirteen inch television towards her and into the hallway. After that, he locks the door and sits back down in that exact place to sit in silence and solitude. 'The enemy is left to taste the revenge that is sweeter than ten thousand chocolate bars dipped in honey, sprinkled with brown sugar, and encased in a box of marshmallows.' He double takes as the cameraman is about to leave the room. Grabbing the back of his shirt, Simon drags back and knocks the camera out of his hands. We then cut to static as shrieks of horror are heard. 'It is only a matter of time until this beast seeks to lather, rinse...and repeat.'
