Alterior Motive.

2 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Inside the Fleet Center, in the middle of it all, 'Til Death Do Us Part was going on. Backstage, Conrad Ramsey was walking through the hallways. Nodding and saying greetings to those that he passed. He was already dressed, in red and white, for his tag team match later in the evening. Once he had reached his destination, he knocked on the door three times. He lowered his head, listening for someone inside. People were whispering, but none had answered his rapping. He lifted his hand again, ready to pound on the door with his fist, but a female voice spoke through the door, 'Come in!' Conrad pulled his hand down and grabbed the door handle. With a quick downward push and a pull, the door came open. The sixty-one year-old entered the room, where he saw Jessica standing there. She was without pants. Ramsey quickly turned away. It wasn't that she was nude. She very much had a shirt on. A long one. And it covered everything. It was just the principal of the thing. 'Oh, don't be shy, Connie,' said his greatest rival from his seat. He then looked to his wife as she pulled hangers from a locker. On those hangers were her clothes, although the protective cover prohibited a look at what they were. Jessica then said to her husband, 'I'm gonna get ready. You should do the same.' He only nodded as she scampered off into the bathroom with her clothes and another small bag. Once she had disappeared, Xander turned to Conrad, 'So, what brings you to these neck of the woods?' Ramsey had finally looked back and cleared his throat. He shook his head, 'Oh, nothing much.' 'But...' Ramsey looked at Xander oddly and asked, 'But what?' Danger smiled slightly, 'Oh, you just had but face, that's all.' Conrad gave him an even stranger looking face. What the bloody hell was this kid talking about? 'But face? What the hell are you talking about?' 'But face. It look like you were going to say 'but'.' Conrad obviously didn't know Buffy the Vampire Slayer. Xander shook his head slightly. Conrad let all that seep in, 'Oh, right, yes. Well, I suppose I was about to say but.' He then took a seat beside Xander, 'It's just, I can't help but continue thinking about last week. It was awfully sudden of you to agree with my proposal. And...' Xander nodded his head knowingly, 'You want to know if I have some type of alterior motive.' Conrad looked at him, 'Well... yeah, that's exactly right.' Xander had a sly smile on his face as he left Ramsey to sit and think it over for a moment. He then leaned back against the lockers, crossing his arms in front of his chest. 'No, Conrad,' he said still smiling, 'I don't suppose I have an alterior motive. I meant what I said last week. And yes, I know it was part of the storyline. But it wasn't scripted for me. I said what I felt needed to be said. And I plan to hold to my word.' Ramsey was listening intently, hunched forward, arms crossed, with his head tilted sideways, to get a slight look at Xander's face. 'We aren't friends, Conrad. We never will be. And I don't necessarily like you either. But that's alright. Can't like everyone. But this isn't about our feelings for each other, Ram. It's about my mother...' he choked slightly, 'you're wife.' Xander took a deep breath and leaned back forward, uncrossing his arms and placing his hands on the edges of the bench. He, too, was leaning slighlty forward. 'We've battled on and off camera for over a year. And, well, I proved myself to be the better athlete.' He smiled slightly. Conrad only nodded, reaffirming that fact. 'But you showed you were the better man. And... I know she would not want us to continue fighting. So... I'm not going to turn on you tonight or anything like that.' He turned to the old man and smiled, 'I actually dislike those two more than you.' He was, of course, referring to their opponents for that evening, Topex and Le'Java. Ramsey had a smile chuckle at that. He then put a hand on Xander's shoulder, 'Well, I'm glad to hear that, Max. I know I didn't want to keep up our rivalry, in light of what happened and all that. Just ready to move on, ya know?' Xander nodded. There was a bit of silence between the two for a moment. Conrad looked down at the ground, at his feet, as Xander shot a look to the bathroom, where the sink water was running quietly. Without looking, Conrad said, 'That was some speech by Joe tonight, wasn't it?' Xander, again, nodded as he turned back to look forward. His Carnage Championship was sitting on the opposite side of the room, the lights shining off the silver plating. 'He was right, too,' Xander started. 'It's time for everyone else to shine. Too long we've had to sit in the shadows of Roland and Chris and the like. But... well... they aren't here, are they? So it's up to us to make this a special show and all the shows hereafter special.' Ramsey agreed, 'You're right. That's why, tonight, I say we leave it all out there. Bring everything we're capable of, and give these fans their money's worth.' He looked at his former wife's son, 'What do you say?' Xander smiled slightly and turned his head to look at Conrad. 'When have I ever not let it all hang out?' He had a small laugh, 'But I'm game.' Conrad smiled. The two stared at each other for a moment. They looked away, and then Ramsey asked, 'Have you seen Bradley around?' Xander shook his head, 'Haven't seen him since the meeting.' He shrugged, 'He'll show up sooner or later.'
