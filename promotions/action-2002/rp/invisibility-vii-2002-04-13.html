Invisibility - VII

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Original: 7/24/2001. Rewrite: 4/13/2002 April 20th, 1993 (months later) Mesa High School Gymnasium: Mesa, Arizona, United States 'What are you doing?' Keri asked to a frantic Daniel Phillips, who was holding a bag over his shoulder. Daniel, in his plain black and white tuxedo, was really surprised to see Keri in the prom, this early. 'K-Keri! What a... er, surprise to see y-you!' Daniel said, obviously stuttering and wanting to not even talk to her. He tried to walk past her, but Keri got in front of him. 'Why so frantic? What're you holding?' Daniel looked at the bag he was holding, before he realized that what he was doing might have been wrong. With that, he dropped the bag, spilling its contents on the floor, before he walked as fast as he could away from Keri. The memory paused. 'What was *that* all about?' Keri asked. 'And why can you show it to me? Joey wasn't there.' 'Well, to answer your second question, since you're in this memory stream, I can tap into your own memory, even if you aren't aware of it.' Bonham answered. 'As for the first one, well... Daniel wasn't particularly that happy with anyone. He was mad at Joey for spending more of his time with you than with him, and he was mad at Julie for being taken by Rashard.' 'And?' 'Well, he was fixing the prom king and queen ballots.' Bonham said. 'Then, it happened.' 'What did?' The memory started. Keri knelt down to see what Daniel had been doing, but as she did so, another arrival saw her. At the worst possible time. 'What the hell do you think you're doing?' a voice, belonging to a female, asked. It was Julie Malone. Rashard was apparently outside the building, talking to some friends of his, and was not aware of Julie entering the building ahead of him. She saw what was on the floor. And she seethed. 'Oh, I see. You're going to fucking fix the ballots in your favor. You bitch.' Whack. As Keri was standing up, Julie slapped her across the face with a smack that was heard throughout the gymnasium. Keri fell down, and Julie probably would've followed up, except that Joey Malone had come back from the bathroom and had pulled his sister away from Keri. 'Hey! HEY! Lay off of her.' Joey shouted. Julie didn't want to listen. She wiggled and flung herself around in Joey's grip, trying to get loose so she could hurt Keri some more. Instead, Joey spun her around and blocked her way from getting to Keri. 'Sis, the last thing I need you to do is beat up my girlfriend on prom night. Besides, Daniel's the one to blame. I saw this from the bathroom. If you want to continue this over a misunderstanding, then fine. Just not here, and not now.' Joey said. Julie seethed. 'What... ever. She's got you so whipped it's fucking unbelievable.' she said, before storming out of the gymnasium, never to be seen for the rest of prom. The memory paused. 'Sheesh. What a...' Keri started, but couldn't finish, because she was attempting to find a more political word than the one on her mind. '...bitch?' Bonham finished for her. 'Yes.' The memory fast forwarded to about two hours later. The prom had gone well, despite Julie's temper tantrum earlier in the night. Joey Malone was hanging around with a sophomore friend of his, whom Keri(the adult one) recognized rather quickly. 'Let me guess. That's Kent?' she said, pointing at the sophomore with the long black hair and the sunglasses, who would go on to win the IWO World Title eight years later. 'Kent Anthason, yes. Bright girl.' Bonham said. After a bit of dancing, Joey and Keri had settled down, and Kent had joined them. 'I can't believe how smashed my date became.' Kent said, pointing at one of Joey's fellow seniors, who was busy drunkenedly dancing the funky chicken. 'How many drinks did she have, anyway?' Joey asked. 'Like, three.' 'Lightweight.' Anthason nodded, before he brought up a new topic. 'How did the wrestling tryouts go?' he asked. Keri had convinced the unathletic Joey to try his hand at amateur wrestling, and he became rather good at it. Kent was refering to Joey's possibility of getting a college scholarship for it. 'Meh. The judges for U of A didn't think I had what it took.' Joey said with a sigh. 'But hell, I'm already getting straight As, so I didn't really need it, but I'm still disappointed.' It was around this time that the dork of a disc jockey that the school hired took ahold of the microphone. 'I guess it's time to announce the prom king and prom queen for this year's senior class. You know, it's not a very active life for me. I mean, all I get to do is tell you which two lucky people can call themselves prom royalty. I mean, that's just silly. I don't even get to choose, myself. It's chosen by a bunch of people who vote and stuff, and that doesn't sound like fun or anything. Especially counting the votes. And...' The entire senior and junior class, in unison, all yelled at the DJ. 'Get on with it.' The DJ almost pissed his pants. '...Er, right... um... yeah.' He pulled out an index card, examined it, and looked back up the crowd. 'And the prom king is... Rashard Clark!' Rashard stepped out onto the floor, toward the DJ, and the DJ gives him the prom king's crown. He raised it up into the air and let out a loud warcry. Erm, okay, he didn't really do that, but I'm sure he'd do that any other time. He put it on his head, regardless. The DJ put the microphone to his lips and examined the index card. 'And the prom queen is... Keri... Lin... uhm... how is this pronounced, again?' The entire building nearly shook with everyone in attendance yelling out Keri's last name. 'LINDUM!' 'Oh. Um. Right. Lindum.' With that, the DJ left to change his pants. Keri was a bit shocked at this, and she approached the dance floor nervously. Kent whistled, and looked over at Joey, who was open-mouthed with shock. 'Heh. Rashard spends all of one year in this school, and he's a frickin' prom king. Go figure.' Joey didn't answer for a few seconds, before he responded with a smile. 'At least Keri won. I'm happy for her.' The memory stopped. 'I was the prom queen?' Keri asked. 'I'm starting to question this flashback.' Bonham chuckled. 'You can ask Joey when you wake up.' 'So, where was Julie in all of this?' Keri asked. 'Well, you see... Julie still thought that you had something to do with the prom ballots. When she came back from her failed marriage in Egypt, she decided to intervene in Joey's life again.' Bonham said. 'To pry you out of his life. She never thought Joey could run his own life. Ever.' Bonham sighed. 'Then she realized just how well-known, and dare I say... famous Joey had become. So, she had a new mission. She still wanted to pry you out of Joey's life, but she craved the spotlight as well. So, using her sisterly influence, Julie began to seep into Joey's mind and give him false information. Just enough to cause a misunderstanding, and eventually, it would digress into violence.' Keri stood open-mouthed in shock. 'You now know why Joey's been acting strangely.' Bonham paused. 'I have one more thing to show you, before I allow you to go.' Bonham said, walking toward the exit of the gym. 'Follow me.' Something in Keri's stomach told her that what she was about to see was not for the weak of heart.
