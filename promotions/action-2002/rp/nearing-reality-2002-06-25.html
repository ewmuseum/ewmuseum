Nearing Reality

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/25/02 Forgotten thoughts of Yesterday Through my eyes I see the Past I don't know I don't know I don't know why I believe I believe in the truth from inside Go away go away go away from me Bright beautiful Scenes. Utterly Serene, Breathtaking sights. Everything that he wanted to see, seemed to be hid mischievously in a daydream. With a daily dosage of nightmares, a good dream seems to go well every once in a while. Every place he wasn't at, he imagined himself in the exact opposite. 'Once more, the grains of sand seeped up through his toes as he walked eagerly along the beach. Staring out at the beautiful, endless horizon. Clouds slowly cascade down towards the ocean. A perspective of sorts. He watched as the birds flew over head, but a smile covered his face. Sadly enough ...' It was another vision. A false prophecy of what he wants in life, what he dreamed to see in reality rather that the virtual world. He seemed to be as well coordinated with the world of near reality than in full-reality. False prophecies wished to be fulfilled, but only to be drug down deeper and deeper into his own pits of sadness. He used the visions to escape. The dreams to get away from everything. If gone, he would be what he wanted to be ... but not in reality. No matter how many wishes, how many great dreams, it won't change the true and inevitable past and future that is to be lived. When in reality, Joker was ... Sitting along the road. Looking out over the endless road at the way that humans are living these days. Trash scattered as far as you can see. He picked up endless amounts only to throw it back down and become even more furious. It wasn't the life to live, but it couldn't be changed in his eyes. He knew how things deserted. Past virtues can not live forever. They vanish eventually. Life seemed like a game in this place and time. Begging and begging to be played. Joker turned his head away from the disgraceful actions. Away from the turmoil that humans live with, and into the sky. 'No matter what. My fuckin' actions can't be changed. Laughing doesn't help anymore. Look at me, I'm a damn clown. Standing out here in the middle of nowhere, making a complete ass out of myself. It's my feelings, and my ass ... so I'll keep doing it.' He slowly crept out towards the horizon. Unwanted live my life in shame Who's to blame for my mistakes I don't know I don't know I don't know why I believe I believe in the truth from inside Go away go away go away from me Leave me alone 'Another wasted dream.' Joker retorted, as he had done so, day in and day out for years. His head latched onto his arm as he laid in anger and joy all in once. Joyful for the facts of life, and angry at the truth in the facts. As in, something that has happened, is over with. It can not be changed. Through no amounts of struggling is the vanquish over with. He looked up now ... at the placement of his house. He took refuge on an oblong and greatly cushioned couch. The two seater seemed to fit him perfectly. He established himself on his back and looked up into the ceiling. A fans blade swung around and around in small circles. Small gusts of wind supplied the sufficient breath for him to take in. 'Why? Why me?' Crying to himself, Joker rested even more. His head collapsed into a cushion as he prayed for more sleep. ‘Joker looked around at the beautiful environment. 'Ah, Finally, back to the place of dreams.' Once more, the beach. He sat down eagerly along the long tanned shore and cupped up small handfuls of the sand. The tall man watched graciously as the small grains of sand fell through his hands like the sand of time. A voice ... distracted him. Joker revolved from the sitting position. Only to look distinctly behind him... There she was. A short human with long hazel brown hair shining brightly as the sun's rays ricocheted off of each individual strand. His body, froze in astonishment as he saw the serene figurine. Joker smiled ...'
