The Good the Bad and the Bad News, Prologue: the insight of indifference

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8.28.02 ' I feel... A distant three sixty. Why? What will totally change in my life, only to go right back in the same direction? What will change in my life? ' Bobby Minio laid on a mound of dirt and rocks. His eyes were half closed. It was dark, night time, early morning, in the sky around him. Yet something was glowing. There was a fire ring in the sky. 'I hate Las Vegas.' He was HOME. As some might call it. Minio refered to it as a bear trap. He had the money to go anywhere else, but he couldn't leave. He could travel, but his HOME would always be Nevada. There was no nighttime in Las Vegas. The Casinos and the strip and everything made the sky glow. Romance was dead. The only romance in Las Vegas was the seduction of a worse life. 'The life I live.' He dragged himself out in a construction area where there were piles of turned dirt. Somehow, he fell asleep. He had an apartment not far of here. But he'd rather lie in the earth. He dreamed of the future. Things would change. Things would change again. He felt deja-vu of oncoming deja-vu. It was a vicious circle ahead and behind. History repeating. He couldn't tell if it was ups or downs coming. He really had no clue. But he wasn't scared. Just... Worried. 'What am I gonna do with myself?' He felt dead end at the time. Reed Young was more worried with his superstar to find a place for Bobby Minio. Minio was ontop of his game. One time. He was the top face in PIW, under Mezz, but he liked Mezz, he respected Mezz, and he didn't mind being the second man in the business. As long as he was in the business at all. But Jim Johnson's words still play back in Minio's mind like a tape recording, or rather, a Broken Record. ---- You Dropped the Ball, Bobby. ---- 'Jim was right.' Bobby did drop the ball. Through out his whole life. And everytime he thought he was ready to go the distance, he dropped the ball. 'Bio Bobby Minio? Bobby the Martyr? How about Butterfingers Bobby.' It was one of those days. Self doubt. And his career was going no where, providing no relief. It would be a matter of failed matches for him to get busted down to some Farm Fed. He felt fucked. He dropped the ball with KroW. Maybe, just maybe if he had beaten KroW, it would be him pitted against LLB. Just maybe it would be him with a shot at gold, a shot at conquest. A shot at something bigger. But he choked. He lost. And with Chris Fear, former A! champion, he didn't conquer in time. Fear fizzled out. Was Bobby Minio next? 'Here I am. I had it in my hands and here I am, screaming MORE.' Thats all he had. Thats all of the substance. Was what he didn't have at all. 'BRUTAL.' Brutal indeed. He dropped the ball. He dropped the ball in wrestling, in relationships, in life. If only the earth would just swallow him up. 'Fucking Brutal. Listen to me. Screaming MORE. MORE. MOOOOORREEE!' Respect was like the moon. You had to be a certain kind of person to get there. Minio just didn't seem to be that person. He thought about it. He thought long. He thought hard. 'Who do I have to KILL to get a shot at a fucking Title?!' It was a simple theory. KroW and Fear. Both Killers. Both with their hands either on the gold or grabbing at the gold. Maybe he should have choked Julie to death. Cut off her air supply. What did she ever do for him? Sexual Tension? Jealousy? Anger? Hate? Pain? 'NOTHING!' Nothing indeed. Nothing ever. He was left empty. Empty handed. Empty hearted. The slow buzz of the win with Sweet Aroma had already wore out. That was probably a one time thing. Nothing special. Nothing important. Nothing new. Where was his LLB? Where was his Syphon Fisson? Where was his Steve Denton? Where was ANYTHING? 'Where is MY Time?' ----Never Bobby. Just give up.---- 'Never. I won't give up. You'll have to kill me.' ----That can be arranged. You're tender from the Concussions.---- 'Go to hell.' ----I will, trust me. Just make sure you keep my seat warm down there.---- Who was he talking to? 'Myself.' Ah, the frenzy of failure.
