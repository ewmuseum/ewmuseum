Hostile Takeover.

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/23/02 It was Monday night. And Reed Young had gone throughout the Dawson’s Creek video tape at least ten times and back. For finally... it was time for a little, LITTLE break. “God damn I love Dawson!” He said, as he stretched his arms across the sofa in his massive estate somewhere in North Carolina (unknown, due to the last time his house was egged). But itching for the remote... and seeing it sitting RIGHT THERE... Young dove for it like it was a million bucks laying on the floor. “I WANT YOU DAWSON!! DON’T LEAVE ME PACEY! FRIENDS FOR(Valley Girl)EEEVVVEEERRR(/Valley Girl) JOEY!!” He stopped for a second. “And... um, Jack, I’m sure if I was gay I’d like you too.” He hit the play button. “Dawson... oh Dawson...” Young foamed at the mouth, ala this was JEFF GARVIN!! “Oh man... WHAT ACTING! WHAT STORYLINES... Jeremy Bold, TAKE SOME FUCKING NOTES!” Young’s mock to Bold was odd... to actually think there were better ideas than Jeremy’s out there... omg, that was just... sssooo wrong. Jesus... was the show at its climax. And though Young had watched it TEN times over... he acted as though he never saw it before. With his mind, people, and how small it was... we really wonder if he actually did, anyway. “KISS HER DAW!” Yep, he had nicknames; the whole eight yards. “KISS HER DAW... DO IT!! OMG... OMG... LOL... BRB... DO IT!!” Rrriiight. Suddenly... RING... RRRIIINNNGGG. “WTF!?” A very pissed off Owner jumped out of his seat, hitting pause on the remote control as he grabbed the phone. “YOU BETTER HAVE A GOOD REASON TO INTER-” Whomever was on the other line... could barely be made clear, as he was yelling. Yelling hard. It was the voice of LLB. “Oh... Rowe... dude... I’m sorry. I-” More yelling. “I-... you-... I-... I like, didn’t even do it... Bold did.” More yelling. “I- I-... was watching Dawson’s Creek.” And more. “Look, I’m at the climax in Dawson’s Creek... can I call you back please? I’ll make it up to you, I swear.” More. “Look, if they can’t understand... (sings like he’s P Diddy) that you, are NUMBER ONE... da do, da do da do... NUMBER ONE, (/sing) than I don’t know what to think... or even who to fuck... because you are the BEST...” Cue Valley Girl again. “EEEVVVEEERRR!” Some more yelling. And slam. “Rowe... you there?” Young shrugged, hanging up the phone as he seemed dumbfounded. “I guess he was cut off.” After hanging up the phone, he looked at the remote... and dove to it again. Unpaus- RING. RRRIIINNNGGG. And once more, a very pissed off Owner jumped out of his seat, hitting pause on the remote control as he grabbed the phone with great force. “YOU BETTER HAVE A GOOD REASON TO INTER-” A calm reply. “Oh... hi Jonny Friend. Didn’t know you were there.” More calm replying. “Look, yes, I’ve thought it over... and I WOULD like to change the time slot after all. Might as well... I mean, those people were right... about Dawson’s Creek.” More calm replying, as Young looked at the calender to see what day it was. “Yes sir... to Monday’s. Starting as soon as possible please.” Today was a good day, after all. More replying. “Okay, sounds good. I want to rename the show too... um, I’ll go do a logo for it right now and plug it on the website so people will know what’s up.” This time, though, some of the reply could be heard. “Well... okay. Do you know the name?” Young shrugged. “I’ll think of something.” Friend replied again. “Well, okay. But check it out with me first, when you do.” Reed nodded, though it could not be seen through the phone. “Okay.” Click. He dove for the remote again... but glancing up, and seeing his computer across the way... he sighed. “Fine. I’ll get this over with now. And then I can get Dawson all to myself again!” He almost literally flew on the computer, went to his normal site, Wrestling Planet... and read some of the threads. He couldn’t help but get caught up in one, as he said what he typed out loud. “ ‘No... you fucking suck... and so does your Fed. IT SUCKS!! SUCKS!! SUCKS!!’ WHOO!! POSTED!! Hehehe. Me so bad.” Then, seeing a link under someone’s signature... Reed clicked it, as it sent him to another Pro Wrestling’s website. He glanced back at the TV, seeing Dawson on pause, right before he was going to kiss Joey. Looking back at what he saw in front of him... He hit copy... And paste. For sir Reed Young... you just made the biggest mistake of your life... one that will honestly cost you... forEEEVVVEEERR.
