Want Me To Get Him 2

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sweet Aroma headed out of the locker room for her match with Max Danger Sweet Aroma exited the locker room, with her Dyad Title and prepared to go up against 'The Danger Man' Xander, as he is so fondly called by Jessica. Jonathan sat backstage, and it felt as if he was the one in the ring with Danger as Aroma and her opponent for the evening went at it. Jonathan didn't take too kindly to some of the comments made by Derik Shaw, but he figured all ex-PIW workers that currently work for A! are scum, he thought to himself what it would be like to rub his PIW World Title in the faces of LLB, Christopher Fox, Bobby Minio, Chris Fear and others. He quickly lost his train of thought, when he saw one of those men make their way down to the ring. Bobby Minio Moments later, Sweet Aroma was tapping out to Danger's Crossface Chicken Wing. Jonathan was on his way to the door, but when he yanked it open, he was face to face with Reed Young. 'No, no, no Jidiot, you don't leave this locker room until you leave the arena; or you sign this contract.' Jonathan slammed the door in Reed's face, before knocking over his seat and waiting for Sweet Aroma to return to the locker room. But let us rewind a little bit shall we Sweet Aroma was in that ring, fighting in one of the best matches that she's ever fought. She couldn't hear the comments made by Derik Shaw. She had no knowledge of how Jonathan was feeling backstage. The only thing she heard was the shots she was giving Max Danger. The only thing she had knowledge of, was the pain that Danger inflicted upon her petite body. Her 'I <3 NY' baby tee, was really getting drenched tonight. The sweat of Danger, the sweat of Aroma; you would've thought they had sex, if not for the wrestling ring. But Sweet Aroma and Max Danger would never have sex. Well, they'd have sex, just not together. You know what I mean. The two would continue to brutalize each other, until Bobby showed up. Bobby ruined everything. What could've went down as the 'match of the night' was turned into 'Bobby tried to be a hero, and screwed up... again night!' These words were never spoken at that time or place. And those words didn't exit the mouth of Sweet Aroma, they exited the mouth of Angelica Dawson. 'The PrimeTime Bitch' as she's so fondly known as, repeatedly told Sweet Aroma that having Bobby around was bad news. And Sweetz continued to tell Angelica that Minio was just a friend, that Minio is just an ally in their war against Max Danger, in their war against the bad men of Action! But once Aroma returned to the locker room, you know after the JCON/Reed Young incident, Dawson didn't say a word to Veronica, atleast not then and there, her look alone was good enough for four words. 'I Told You So.' The look from Dawson to Aroma stating that fact, and the one from Aroma to Dawson that stated... 'Yes You Did.' ...was broken up by five words from Aroma's man. 'Want me to get him?'
