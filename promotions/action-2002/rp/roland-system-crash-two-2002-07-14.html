Roland: System Crash: Two.

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Roland: Prelude System Crash Class. The horrible accents of work. The scene is a grungy old room with rust filling the roof and desks. The black board’s an off green, with a sense of rust on it as well. Roland entered the class, standing tall, but never actually meaning to. He walked to his desk. Forth row. Seat two. “Roland!” “Hey.” “Roland!” “Hey.” “Roland!” “Hey.” The class continued to pile in as everyone greeted each other, as a strong sense of affection filled the room at the time. Despite the bad grounds, the heart of the foundation seemed strong. “Get that work done?” A short haired, blonde male had said to Roland just two desks behind him while digging into his bag. “Yeah. I had nothing better to do anyway.” Stunned, the guy replied. “Riiight.” Both of them smiled, and took out their work. Just before the bell had gone off, in came the Teacher... pacing to his desk madly and looking through it. “One minute class.” He addressed. “I just have to find my lesson.” A few of the students sighed and began talking to one another. Then, and quickly too, without being noticed by the Teacher, a male standing around six feet with short light brown hair and light brown eyes entered. Running to his seat across from Roland’s right hand, he sat down and scrambled into his bag. Not finding what he wanted, he tapped Roland on the shoulder. “Hey... hey Roland.” The man said. “Hey Roland, I need the homework. I can copy it real quick.” “Christ Reed, this is the last time I do this for you.” He said, handing the kid his paper. Reed rubbed his head. “Uh... got a pen?” “Ugh. Here.” “Okay.” “Just another minute class.” The Teacher spoke once more even though no one was listening. “Just... gotta... find... it.” “He’ll never find his work.” One of the girls spoke up, sitting only two desks behind the football star. Roland turned, his brown eyes flickering a tiny bit in the lights. “Yep.” But before he could even finish... Reed had tapped him on the shoulder again. “Uh... Roland.” “Yeah?” He said, spinning around. “What now?” “Well... do you have any whiteout? I made a giant mistake.” The girl tossed some at him. “Here Reed.” “Okay.” Replied Young, his strange way of saying thank you to others. “So anyways...” Roland said rolling Reed off his back. “What’s new Carolyn?” “Oh nothing really.” She stated, then hitting Roland lightly on the arm. “It’s freezing today... ant it?” “Yeah-” “Uh... Roland?” “Reed?” “I put whiteout on the wrong page... I’m really sorry.” Roland looked up at Carolyn who just smiled. “Don’t worry about it.” Was his reply. “Okay.” “By the way, Reed.” He went on. “You going to be at football tonight?” “Naa. I got a date! Hot too! She’s got big fucking tits! Like holly fuck does she have-” Roland grabbed Reed’s shoulder and gave him a hard glance. “That’s good. Real good. I wish you luck.” Young stuck his head up, then jamming his hand into his best friend’s face. “Like I need luck.” And as the Teacher finally brought his head from the table, he started to write on the board with faded white calk. “Going to football tonight?” Carolyn inquired, smiling again. “Ye-” “Roland?” “Reed?” “Got anymore paper on you?”
