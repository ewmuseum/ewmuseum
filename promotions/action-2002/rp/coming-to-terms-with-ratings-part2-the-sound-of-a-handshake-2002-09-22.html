Coming to terms with Ratings part2 - the sound of a handshake

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.22.02 Yesterday, Minio sat icing his body down after the pummeling he traded with Max Danger. He contemplated his existence as an entertainer, his existence in Aroma and Dawson's lives, and his existence in general. Today, he was showing visible signs of distraught. Flashbacks hit him at awkward times. In the store buying new socks, flashback. Getting a burger, flashback. Stopping to get a bottle of water, flashback. Driving on the highway, flashback. He saw one scene over and over again. One single instance in his life. --------- Bobby Minio, young and wide eyed of the wrestling industry, stared at the contract with Pinnicle entertainment long and hard, with the paper mere centimeters from his nose. He wasn't even reading it. He was just acting so he didn't look stupid infront of the suits. He stared, and stared, and didn't know he would be selling his soul to Johnson and company with this. --------- The more he thought of it, the more undone he became. Sweat was dripping on his brow. He was sitting in his car, pulled over on the highway, and he had no clue of where he was. He felt lost, on so many different levels. He regretted the moment he didn't read that contract. But a bigger mistake was made. Almost a year ago, sitting across from Reed Young and Jeremy Bold, the two morons confused by their own sales pitches to hire Bobby Minio. He was already bitter, and all he cared about was having a steady job wrestling again. He didn't think someone this dumb could make a Martyr out of a mole hill. But low and behold, Minio was martyr'd for ratings. Bobby the Martyr Minio. He remembered the sound as he reached across the table and firmly gripped Reed's hand. The sound of money, the sound of fans, the sound, of a handshake. The sound of him signing his dignity to oblivion.
