The Beta: Influx: Venom

13 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
4:00 AM I am ready to finish my deadly game. All night, while she slipped in and out of consciousness, I beat her. The mighty Julie Malone was reduced to nothing. She could only cry and plea. I reveled in every single second of it. I can not wait until I get the one I want is in my hands. Ultimate mind fuck to Malone when that happens. I can only wait for that. It is coming ever so soon. Joey is going to enjoy this news so much when he finds that his sister has been in my possession for a good week and all I have done is broken her spirit. Joey is going to enjoy the news that his best friend was maimed by someone that he could not recognize. It is a Monday night, and I am bored. I have allowed Phillips to try and fight me, but his intoxication allowed for me to merely pound the blood out of him until I felt he was done for one session. I can see the moon ready to go down The night is almost over. And I am ready to finish. Phillips tries to stand up. He is laid out on the floor by Julie, who at this point, was drugged and in a deep sleep. In his pool of vomit and blood, he tried to make out who I was. I was thinking rather quick at this point, seeing as how I needed to make sure nobody would talk. I did not need Phillips on me right at the moment. It would throw my plans out of wack. He stood up, and stumbled. STOP HIM! QUICK! Taste his blood. Make him feel the burn. Show him what forever means…Fission. I remembered something I saw on a Sunday Show one time. Villam Ender did something nobody expected. I grabbed Phillips, and kissed him. Phillips tried to push me off, but it was too late. I bite down on his lips Until I tasted iron. I let go of the lock, and he damn near shit himself. He screamed the most primal scream I had heard since I had beaten Julie with Payback. I lifted Payback off the floor, and without a second though, I blinded his left eye. He laid on the ground, an ever growing pool of blood around his face. The sun had yet to take a peak of the world, and I knew I had a limited window of time. I loaded the two into my Kia Sportage, and went on my way. I it was merely an end to phase one of my ultimate end game with Malone. When I have the ultimate entity in his life at my whim… That’s when I will have won.
