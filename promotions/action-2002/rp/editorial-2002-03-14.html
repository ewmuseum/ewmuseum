Date: 3-14-02 When someone asks you to write...you feel like you have to. Wrestle World asked me to do this...so I did. I titled it this because my career was over just a couple months ago. But...out of the ashes, I came out. And I am not willing to go back into those ashes until I leave my mark... Wrestle World Magazine Editorial Like a Rising Phoenix… By: Rashard Clark (AKA Syphon Fission) I was just like every single child in this industry when I began. I wanted to become the proverbial God of Wrestling. Unlike most of those children, I achieved close to that status. I went from a nobody with nothing but athletic gifts to the top of the IWO, beating one of wrestling’s icons, Phelen Kell for the IWO World Title. I was the IWO’s answer to the Boston Strangler and Ric Chronos. I was IWO’s answer to Pete Borst and Kenny Rock. I was IWO’s answer to CWL’s the Don and basically their entire roster of superhuman combatants who changed the scope of the sport. Me and Joey Malone. We were the future of the IWO…which was one the same level as fWo and CWL, at least in many people’s eyes. I do not know if it was so much a fact that it was because of the history of the IWO. We had a rich tradition in there, which was both a curse and a blessing. But…the new blood in the IWO…man…the crop we had…it was something else. We all were main event caliber it seemed. But I was the champion. I had everything…yet…on the same token…had nothing. The IWO World Title is like almost every title in wrestling or fighting history; it was cursed. It brought out the worst inside me. To this day, I still get depressed about the results of the IWO Title reign did to me. Hope. Ultimate melancholy. Hatred for friends who were just doing their job. You see, the wrestling world is one of cycles. One of nothing but predictable routine. If you display above average talent or kiss enough ass, you will get into a good federation. The whole job is to push you along. To get the most from you. To make you worth money to the company. Eventually, you get a fan base, and you get a push. You get designs on the World Title of your federation, and the company gives it to you. You get into a massive feud with a guy who did the same thing as you did a couple months before. Jobbers are ones that just do not perform. In about a year, you have been made useful enough, and are released. That’s the way wrestling is nowadays. A business. Not a sport. My dream has become something along the lines of pre-1997 wrestling. Where your career was long, the pay was good, and you had insurance that you were more then a dollar sign from your boss. That’s impossible now. With people like Joe Campbell and others out there, wrestling/fighting utopia will never be realized. Of course…we can all dream of that. We, the men you tune into on a nightly basis, the guys who look so strong, so free willed, really just live for a title, a good lady, and a big paycheck. I have it all. Never has that been more true to me until now. My wife is my Goddess…I have made millions off of Jamie Kosoy and his IWO by his signing me to a record thirty-five million-dollar contract. And, I achieved the goal of my life…winning the IWO World Title over one of wrestling’s Untouchables; Phelen Kell. I earned it all. I earned that money by being more then a consistent guy. I earned the title because I earned it. I earned the trust of Quinn Morgan, which, in turn, became love. I did this all by NOT being the average Joe. I did it by being a moody, sarcastic, authority-destroying machine that I am in real life. I did it by telling my story, taking my shots, and then loading my gun and firing back at all of them. To make money off of our industry is not what I yearn for now. I just want to be in a wrestling ring, fighting like any normal guy. But, now, that can not happen. Because I am not going to ever be viewed as a normal, average Joe. I am going to be referred to as Syphon Fission, and instead of the nice mid-card level matches that I could be in, I am expected to fight in the main events. And I do not ask any less of myself. I have been making this bed that I am lying in for almost a decade. Sure…everyone pays his or her dues…but you know, nobody cares. All that matters is what you prove to yourself. I think of it this way…when you are on your deathbed…only you will care about what you did during your life. So…if you messed up, that’s your thing. If you paid dues like a whore…that’s your problem too. It all goes back to what you did to get ahead of the rest. And, in the end, we will all end up in the same boat…calling ourselves the best there ever was. In the end…I think it really doesn’t matter. My name is Syphon Fission. And I am Action Wrestling.
