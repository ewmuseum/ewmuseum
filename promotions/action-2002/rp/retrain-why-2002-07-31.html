Retrain...why?

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Long blue hair, braided. A black stubble beard forming under his lower jaw, along with a stubble mustache. I didn’t itch anymore, thank god for that. Sweat drench his face, his muscles tightened. He was wearing a pair of white cross trainers and a pair of Texas Longhorns basketball shorts, running up and down everything he could in New Orleans. This is Rashard Clark, or as you know him, Syphon Fission. Clark had gained quite a bit of weight the past couple months because of his lack of training. He was always the fitness nut. Since he started wrestling, he took the creatine and other supplements. He always drank Slim Fast. He put definition on his once medium build body, making him look like a ripped roid monkey. However, it was all natural. No human growth hormone, or any of the shit. The odor of sweat was in the air around him. The air burned as it forced its way in and out of his lungs. It had been maybe a year since he cross-trained. Since he did plyometrics. Women gawked at the body of the man, while men were different to it. Oh look, it’s a shirtless man running up and down all over the place. Big deal. Where is my Gumbo? The weight lifting caused me to vomit I dry hurled last night during my squats. What the hell. I used to be able to do this all with ease. Now? I am like a shell. Oh well. Training is always hard. And I am still waiting for Reed Young to make sure that my body wasn’t burned and my ashes spread somewhere. He would put those ashes in the ring, and be all “RANTINGS~!” The looks on all their faces when I walk back into an Action! Wrestling arena. There are so many cocky rookies, so many guys who mean bad. Not like I have much room to talk, but still. Man, when they see that I am alive, all the people will fall back into their places. The power struggle over who would be the top dog, no matter what title or anything you have, ends. LLB could be the champion, and might have been controversial. Nobody was even in my league. So, why the struggle? Someone wants to garner those dollars. The only person who could do that in Action is a sick prick. The warm are is burning my lungs. It is all working on its own now. I just keep the legs churning and the lungs do their thing. It’s like I am a damn cross country runner. Maybe I can become on of those. Nah. I still have a shot at that title LLB has. Everyone seems to forget about that. What better way to rebuild my credibility as a wrestler then to take down LLB and become the man. Really, who can stop me? Seaman is busy with the Carnage Title, and, well, Joey and me are probably not even going to look at each other. After what happened, I welcome that. We can both thrive in our own little ways now. Man, I am hungry. I need something to eat, and all I see is sit down restaurants. Damn. New Orleans is a great city and all, but please, I just want a Big Mac. I think I burned off enough calories to earn one.
