Foreshadows pt4 - Don't know why I'm even Here

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/25/03 Five minutes ago, the room was a snow globe of emotion, turned upside down to show the content falling(I'm the avalance, bye bye city below.) Bobby was seated back in the hardwood chair letting his father regain his compusure. Robert was in the bathroom coughing up blood that Bobby had no clue about. If these walls could talk(I wouldn't listen to them anyway.) When Robert reemerged a moment later, he took his seat back on the couch, not looking at Bobby. He stared at the carpet. Foot prints of years of living, dying, loving, hating. He saw everything in vivid colors, he wondered if his Son was so lucky. 'So, Robert, explain to me the real story. I want to know if my Grandpa added anything for impact.' 'I doubt it. I was a fucker then, I won't deny it. I denied you of more then money, of a Father.' 'Maybe thats why I'm so Fucked up.' 'Don't say that Bobby. I watch you, on television. You're a hell of a man. I couldn't do half the shit you do!' 'What the hell have you been watching? Its been ages since I've felt like a man in the ring.' 'How can you say that? You have strength, you have power, you have skill, you're one of the best in that damn sport!' Bobby was reeling for a second. His father called wrestling a sport... As supportive as his Mom was at first, she never called it a sport(I am still in control, he can't charm the hate away.) Bobby stood up over his father, for the first time in months, he felt like a man. Most Father Son relationships have respect, this one lacks that. Bobby Minio knew from the moment he entered this room that Robert's life was his to take. For two decades Bobby had fantasized about meeting the man who left him and his Mother for cash. Most of them, especially the later ones, included alot of breaking. This actual event is nothing that Bobby had forseen. 'What do you want Robert?' 'Bobby I... god! I can't ask.' 'Just ask.' 'Money.' 'Fuck off.' 'Bobby let me explain!' Every doubt, ever naysay in his head, had come true(How predictable.) 'Where is all the cash that your Ma' left ya?' 'Gone Bobby I need your help! you're my son! I just want to be apart of your life and I can't if I'm D, ' 'SHUT UP!' It had almost become a reflex for Bobby to swing any kind of chair from any position, and he lifted the hard wood chair sending it against the wall in a splendid crash of splinters and screws(it breaks likes bones.) Bobby was in motion, sending the back of his hand across his Fathers face, like an unloved stepchild(oh how the tides have turned!) Robert fell back against the couch holding his face, his cheek red and blistered. 'I don't know why I'm even here. I knew you would ask for cash, why else contact your son that you refused love to! FUCK YOU ROBERT! You're no father of mine. I hope you rot! I hope you die.' He paused. 'My only gift to you, is not taking your fucking life for ruining mine.' Bobby turned, and opened and slammed the door before Robert even noticed he was slapped. Bobby was long gone by the time the tears fell. Robert's last option had been exhausted. He had no one to turn to, he was done. Finished. Dead. 'W... Wait... please... I just... need... help...' He trailed off into tears, into oblivion. From Happy(to sad) from the Cradle(to the grave)
