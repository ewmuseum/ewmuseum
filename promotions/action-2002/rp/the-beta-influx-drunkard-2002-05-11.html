The Beta: Influx: Drunkard

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Daniel Phillips was a chronic drunk. He would make someone like Scott Hall proud. He drank when he was a wrestler, and drank even more now that he wasn’t. Daniel has just as many reasons to do exactly what I am doing as anyone does. He had to sit and watch his career take off while he was injured. He was fired, because the IWO didn’t need him anymore. If I remember right, Joey didn’t do anything to save his friends job. How pathetic? How fucking pathetic? I hated Phillips from day one, really. Always trying to be a big man backstage. Always playing his cards wrong. He was a prick. He tried to tell everyone to remember his name, because they wouldn’t forget it after his amazing matches. Right Daniel. Moronic dipshit. I laugh at you Daniel. I laugh as you stumble around, trying to walk home. I laugh at you while you vomit on the side of the road. Look at you, a waste of flesh. Of blood that some dying man who had some use in this world could have used before you poisoned it with your binge drinking. Look at you, paranoid as can be. Looking behind you. Talking to Joey Malone, standing up to him within the confines of your own drunken stupor. I haven’t had any of that make up put on my face, so you can see my stitches. I know that much from staring at mirrors. I got sick of looking at myself, and punched that mirror. I broke it. Now, I am breaking someone else. I follow him into a dark alleyway. He is still talking to himself, like some idiot. Staring at walls, talking his trash. PAYBACK GIVES THE ORDER! I SWING! I SEE BLOOD AS HE COLLAPSES! I pick up the passed out body of Daniel Phillips and sling him over my shoulder. The streets are empty. Nobody knows. NOBODY CARES!
