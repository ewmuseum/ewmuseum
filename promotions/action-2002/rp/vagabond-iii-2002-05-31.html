Vagabond - III

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/31/2002 Previous Parts: 1, 2 Click. Beeeeepppp... 'Ah, how typical of him...' Joey said as he put the phone back on its hook. He had walked three miles from Phoenix's airport and barely had any money left since Reed gave him a small bit of cash to make it from Charlotte to Phoenix. He was now wondering exactly why he had come back to Phoenix. His home was Fission's, and now, the only friend he thought he could depend on, Daniel Phillips, wanted nothing to do with him. He sighed and stuffed the rest of his quarters down his pants pocket. Reed had been nice enough to also allow Joey to get some clothes as well. At least he treated his employees better than most. Joey was about to walk some more, when he came across a place that had a lot of sentimental value for Joey Malone. It was a small place, built into the corner of the streets. It was mildly popular, especially amongst wrestling fans and wrestlers alike. It was also the place where Joey met Keri for the first time since they had left High School, nearly a year and a half ago. It was soon after he returned home from Tibet after spending a month to train, and he just found out that his ex-girlfriend, Rachel Milloy, had skipped town while Joey was in Tibet, so he needed to find a place to drown his sorrows. So, he came here, the Shaved Beaver Bar and Grill. This was the place that former wrestling legend and Joey's former trainer, 'Nitro Beaver' Kyle Morris, opened soon after he quit training wrestlers. He met Keri here, after she, herself, just broke up with someone. And that was how their relationship was restarted. This time, he came here to see if his mentor was in. Joey entered the establishment and paused to look around for his mentor. He didn't see him anywhere, and as he scanned the room, he saw someone whom he thought he had recognized, but then he did a double take. He thought he recognized that girl. 'Allison?' he asked, before he approached and prodded her in the shoulder. The red-headed young woman turned around and instant recognized Joey. Her face brightened quickly before she hugged her friend. 'Joey? Joey Malone? Is that you?' she asked. She examined him briefly before she tactfully added 'You look like crap.' to her statement. Joey chuckled, before glancing at a window's reflection to see his forehead covered in bandages from the match from Juggernaut. Then he looked around before he spoke to her some more. 'I suppose I should ask what you're doing here. I thought you live in Sacramento with your mother.' 'I moved here about three months ago for college. I think you and sis were still on your honeymoon at the time. I'm sorry I didn't call or anything, I just needed to take care of some school stuff.' Allison said. 'Say, where's big sis, anyway? Aren't you two always together?' Joey couldn't really answer the question at first, but finally, he choked up the words. 'He... took her. And everything.' Allison was a wee bit confused. 'Who?' 'Rashard Clark.' Joey said. 'To make a long story short, I lost a match to him, and he took just about everything I hold dear.' Allison's face was one of shock. 'That's why I'm here, Ally. I wanted to get Kyle's advice on the matter. Then I need to find a place to stay.' Allison scratched her head. 'Well, alright. I'll wait around for you, and I'll take where you need to go. Okay?' Joey smiled, before he caught a glimpse of the owner of the place. He turned to see if it's him, and when he's satisfied that it was him, he turns back to Allison. 'Thanks, Ally.' He walked toward Kyle Morris, but turned back to Allison. 'I owe you for this.' he said. 'Big time.'
