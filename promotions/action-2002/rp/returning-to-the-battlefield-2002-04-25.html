Returning to the Battlefield

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 25, 2002 The lone warrior awoke, his face feeling dirty, and his muscles feeling weak. He gripped the soft soil below him, the soil soaked with blood. He pushed his body up with the last amounts of power left in his body, and rose to his feet. He noticed the cuts, the bruises and the blood that stained his body. He noticed the pain and the hurt that stained his soul. But looking at himself didn't remind him where he was, it was what he saw around him. Bodies. All around him, young warriors laid on the soil, their bodies unmoving, and their souls either in heaven or hell. Staring at the corpses of what used to be, the warrior walked alone on the dusty battlefield. Limping, as his trail went past his comrades...and his enemies. He looked to his right and saw his friends, those he saw just the night before, laughing and smiling. But now, they were mangled and bloody. He looked to his left and saw his enemies, those who were screaming just moments ago, were now lifeless and staring into him. No chills overcame his body, for he felt no fear. He was alone, but it didn't seem to bother him. He was the sole survivor, but it didn't seem to bother him. He slowly wiped his arm across his forehead, but for some reason he felt nothing. He looked to wipe his forehead again, but still felt nothing but the cold sweat slowly moving down his brow. He wondered if it was because he was numb...and it wasn't. It was because his right arm was gone. He looked at the empty area, where his arm once was....but he didn't cry, not did he faint. He just stared as his warm blood trickled down his empty socket. He looked toward his other arm, which was still in tact, and noticed he still had his watch. He slowly looked down upon it, and smiled. Grinning in pleasure, and even showed a hint of laughter as he spoke softly to himself.... 'Funny how so much can change in a matter of seconds on a battlefield' ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The rain trickled slowly down from the heavens above. The clouds cried softly, for no anger ran through their being. The rain slowly feel onto the objects below, forming small puddles, everywhere it fell. The thunder was dead, for it felt no reason to come out. The clouds may have cried, but the storm was not angry...it was fulfilled, just as the warrior beneath its tears was. The warrior sat on a rock stone, his feet planted on the ground, and his elbows rested upon his knees. His face staring onto the wet ground below his feet. None of his figure could be seen, for his attire was all black, mixing with the night. His figure was hiden beneath a large leather coat, and long black hair. The rain continued to fall onto his body, as tears(raindrops) trickled down below him, forming puddles near his feet. The young warrior lifted his head from beneath his own body, and stared up at the sky. His eyes full red with anxiety. His pupils seemed fixed on the dark sky, as the rain drops continued falling on his face. The young warrior finally stood up, his hands crossed over his chest, and his face staring up at the night sky. And a slow grin forming on his face. His white teeth shining in the night's light. As thoughts began to run through his mind and out of his mouth. 'The battlefield..' He closed his eyes, soaking in his emotion.. 'I've missed the battlefield so much. I've missed the mayhem that is ensued, I've missed the pain that is suffered, and I've missed the blood that falls. The smell of blood and sweat, is a smell that indicates a battle has gotten good. And in my return to the battlefield, I've smelled it all, even the immense pain.' The voice of the one named KroW echoed in the night, as he spoke darkly, but softly, into the night air. 'It's been so long since I've felt the sense of battle. And I've missed it dearly. Especially since I haven't sensed the REAL battle since PIW. I've missed PIW, for it supplied the most immense competition that I've ever wished for.....but it closed, and with it, my immense competition stopped.' He sighed deeply, as grunted lowly, as he lowered his head, allowing the rain drops to fallen onto his cranium. 'But now, a new battlefield has restarted the war of PIW. A battlefield named Action! A battlefield that took the soldiers of PIW, and brought them into a new war. A war with their own comrades, and a war that shall not die so quickly. Thus, I enter...' KroW began to walk forward into the night, slowly talking to himself. 'I entered the battlefield, but something was new. My desire was new. My desire was different. My desire was to take an untouched soldier, and make him my personal prey. I would be the warrior, the predator, to enter the field of battle and attack the enemy, the untouched the warrior, 'The prey'. And that soldier, that prey was Bobby Minio.' The mere mention of the name formed a full smile on the face of the sadistic KroW. 'I've faced all the soldiers in Action! One time or another. Shawn Steele, LLB, Chris Fox, Chris Fear, all of them. But one man remained untouched, one man never felt the REAL challenge of facing a REAL warrior. And that man was Bobby Minio. Minio never felt such sensation of a true battlefield, or a true war. But now he will, because now he has been touched....by the Satanic God' KroW stopped his path and starred back up at the sky. 'Just think, one moment, Bobby Minio was on top of the world. He returned to his sport, and came in with a bang, attacking the A Champion of Action! Attacking The Law of Action! and attack the Kid of Action! Bobby Minio was on top of the world, as the fans cheered for his return, and applauded his actions as the big 'face'' KroW than slowly stared at the ground. 'And the next moment, he was on the floor of the battlefield, his warm blood trickling down his face, and the pain sensation filling his body' KroW than looked down at his watch, smiling as he did so. 'Funny how so much can change in a matter of seconds on a battlefield' And thus, KroW laughed, his sick laugh echoing in the night sky. His mind full of intentions, and his sights set on a target. The predator, the insane, the warrior and the Satanic God was back in his battle. And had no intentions of losing...but intentions of winning. 'Minio, all this happened because this is how fate saw it....not I' And thus the warrior walked into the dark of the night, sending the warning to Minio, that Minio would finally feel the sensation of a true battle.
