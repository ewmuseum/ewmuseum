Action! — Tales

The Velcro Heart, The Seventh Part of

21 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Action! logo faded as the gray Chevy Blazer of LLB’s slowly rolled into the parking lot area. The camera shot up, catching the Continental Airlines Arena in East Rutherford, New Jersey as the Fans went wild. LLB drove his car into a free parking space far away from all the others, as he reluctantly stepped out, looking left, looking right... and then looking left again. And then right. “Fine. Get out.” A voice could be heard from inside the passenger’s car door, as it unhinged and slammed shut again. The scuffing sounds of someone’s shoes could be heard, as the camera was only able to catch a mere inch of the man’s head. “Wait.” LLB snapped, and thus, the figure did... whom now had his hair slowly rolling about in the wind. ‘The Law’ looked to the left. To the right. Back to the left again. And then right. He opened up the backseat door, pulling the Action! Championship off of the seat. “Fine. But hurry.” The scuffing sounds were made once more, as the top of this man’s head came around to the back of the vehicle. “Wait.” The Champion snapped *again*, as he popped his head up, thinking he had seen someone far down there, at the Arena doors. The Fans had laughed at first, thinking this was a little funny... but then beginning to get restless, they started to jeer, just a bit. They wanted to know who this man behind the Chevy Blazer was. He was right there. Just one more step... and they’d see him. LLB looked to the left. To the right. And back to the left again. Oh... and then right. “Fine... but HURRY!” The man skipped up, coming forward... Christopher Fox. The entire Arena... went crazy. LLB looked back at Fox and snarled at him, as ‘Kid’ could only suck his thumb and begin to dance down the long parking lot, while LLB already walked a mile in front. “Oh LL... thank you for the drive...” Fox & the Crowd: “BIG TIME!” The chant was so loud, that LLB could actually hear it from outside the Arena. Yes, even if he was parked the furthest distance away. He snapped around. “SSHH!” “Oh, right.” Fox said, stopping his dancing for a second. “Sorry LL.” They continued to walk- well, Fox danced. “I’m just SSSOOO happy that you picked me up... I didn’t even know my car was gonna break down like that...” Fox & the Crowd: “BIG TIME!” He snapped back. “OBJECTION!” Fox curled his lips... You knew it was coming. “Erection? Oh jeez... do I have one already?” And storming off even further down the way, Christopher was left to eat up LLB’s dust. He coughed, looking down. “No. I don’t...” Fox & the Crowd: “BIG TIME!” Innocent smile.
