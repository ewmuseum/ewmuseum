Power Set Aside Again.

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling Pay-Per-View ‘Evil Intentions’ 11/19/00 PIW World Championship: LLB (C) vs. Jim Johnson (Owner of PIW) **Ding Ding Ding** Look at him... standing right there in front of you. He thinks he’s better. He thinks he has authority. He thinks he’s the child written in law. Error. I’m the ‘Child Written in Law’. C’mon Johnson... take a step forward. Object to me! Left hand! Left hand! Into the ropes... and a powerslam! I’m too much already! Is that all you have JJ? You’re spending a lot of time on the mat. A lot of time... Hip toss! Wow... this is going to be easy. --Twelve Minutes Later-- And now it’s time Johnson... it’s time for ‘The Right’. Reverse DDT. Just count him Ref, and I’ve got this won. One. Two. Three! You are GUILTY! Wait... the Jury, they’re booing. Glance around Roland, glance around... Where the fuck is the Judge? (Referee) Where the hell did he go? Did I not win? Okay... let me get up... Shit! That’s Bryant Covelli with a chair! Well... is it over? Did I lose? Open your eyes Roland... Neck... hurts... There’s Johnson, on the floor. I guess he’s still out! Good. Damn... he’s starting to get up though. I better do the same. A chair! In-between us! Okay, I’m vertical... and he seems to be as well. Now... for the chair... Hurry... he’s getting closer... Grab it Roland... grab it! FUCK! I take it he knocked me out. I should use the ropes to get up... There he is again! Thump. What was that noise? Sounds like- sounds like someone hitting the mat. Thump. I better lift my shoulders’... Hurts... C’mon... Brace yourself Roland... you’re not going to lose! ... ... Did I? Did I get them up? Cheers! I did. I got my shoulders’ up! And there’s Jim Johnson... going for the metal once more... If I can just pull it together... Use the ropes... I’M UP! Testify! Crap... he’s got the chair again! ... ... Ha! I ducked it! And into the ropes I go... Spear! You know, I do that move pretty well. Okay, take the chair Rowe. Whack! Whoo! Johnson... you had your fun... but now... THE verdict. ‘The Right’. Count him Ref! One! Two! Three! And Jim Johnson... you... are... GUILTY! I rest my case.
