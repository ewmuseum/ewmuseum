The Life of the Party: Part Two

26 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/26/03 Much time had passed since Jeff had initially spiked the bowl of fruit punch and served it to a group of party guests he barely new. Within that time, a large majority of his special concoction had been consumed at a rapid pace, leaving the others in a drunken state of rowdiness; lamps were tipped over, beverages spilled, and rooms preoccupied with men sleeping with ladies that were not their wives. Everyone was drowning in a pool of confusion and relaxation with every glass they downed, everyone except Jeff who had never drank in his life. He was a firm believer in keeping himself in top physical condition and becoming a blatant alcoholic, also known as Scott Hall, would not help his chances of becoming one of the great competitors in his sport. Few people remained, as some had passed out or others had just disappeared while in search of more booze. Jeff sat back on the coach; his feet propped up on the coffee table with the television remote, going from station to station, seeing what exactly this ‘cable television’ was. In retrospective, it wasn’t what it was cracked up to be, he thought, just a bunch of home improvement shows… things that he had no use for. Back in October, Jeff had mentioned to his landlady, a former hooker who purchased the entire apartment after suing a local cop who gave her an ‘extended’ strip search one time. What was most disturbing was the woman had to be a good three hundred pounds over weight with her two front teeth missing. Just the mental image of her naked body made him want to gouge his eyes out. The lights above him had been dimmed to a point where you really couldn’t see much of anything around you. He figured that one of those idiots must have been messing around with the fuse box because they were the only lights that worked… and that wasn’t to say they worked well either. Garvin felt the soft touch of a woman’s hand on his thigh. He gripped the cushion beneath him and gave a silent gulp as a head fell into his lap- long, gorgeous hair covering his entire crotch. Soon after, Jeff relaxed, he actually began to enjoy it. So what if she was under the influence of hard liquor and didn’t know what she was doing, this was the most action Jeff had received in his whole life! There was no way he was going to blow it now… Blow it, the Original thought, what a perfect choice of words. He sneered as his eyes tilted down to the woman. She lifted her head up and leaned back against the coach, her eyes glazed over and her lipstick smudged against her chin. Garvin knew who she was, it was Joey Malone’s sister, and she had come late in the party. Julie was her name. Julie and Garvin sittin’ in a tree! F-U-C-K-I-N-G! First comes love, then comes the sex! Oh wait watch out! Jeff’s gonna’ flex! Her eyes were on Jeff’s pectoral muscles as he flexed in and out, in and out. She giggled and gave a single hiccup. “You’re so funny, Jeffy! I love you SO much! We should get married!” The words didn’t come out as clear, due to the constant hiccuping and burping, but you get the picture. Jeff just stared into an abyss of nothingness… Which in suddently happened to be Julie’s cleavage… Go figure. No one had ever said that to him. Jeff was mesmerised, and the idea that maybe her being drunk might have something to do with her statement of ‘love’ to him, all he knew was that he needed to capitalised on this strange turn of events and fast. Garvin dug in his pockets and found the only piece of jewelry his possessed. A small gold ring that he had been given for getting second place in a Japanese tournament during his rookie year, Champions of Wrestling it was called. He had ultimately been beaten by Ninja K, who then went under the name Shinobi X, but that event still meant a lot to him. “Julie,” he spoke, holding out the ring which was between his thumb and index finger, “will you do me the honour of being my wife?” She giggled and laughed, took another sip of punch, and replied with a “Kay!” Probably the strangest response to a proposal ever, like she had no idea what he was even talking about. Not that it mattered. Jeff was already on top of the coffee table screaming as loud as possible. “YES! I FINALLY DID IT! YES!' Garvin leapt down and pulled his soon to be bride out the door… There was a ‘hitchin’ to be done! YAHOO!
