Who Is Jamal Wilson? -3

27 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/27/02 That day I stayed home. I didn't leave for any of my classes. I couldn't believe it, for the first time in my life I hadn't gone to school, and it didn't bother me one bit. Straight A student who always loved satisfying his brain by gaining knowledge relevant to the world... realizing that University wasn't all that a bottle of spooge, if you catch my drift. I sat in front of the television, leaning back on my leather couch, a couch that was bought due to my parent’s hard work. Due to the fact that my parents would bust their asses day in and day out, so I could go to a University like this one. A University of high standards, and one that would ensure me a good job when I stepped out into the real world. Was I in the wrong for not wanting to go to class? Was I in the wrong for deciding that I would rather look cute, dress nicely, and get all the sex I want then to look like a nerd, dress uncoordinatedly, and just be an all around loser? No friggin' way. My parents would understand. They would understand the queer situation I was in. Suddenly, my phone rang. Thoughts rang through my head, 'who could it be?!' 'what did they want?!' 'how did they get this number?!' Jamal answered the phone, with fear in his voice. 'Hello?' 'Hi. I'm Jacob Jenkins from student safety program here at the University of Memphis. Why weren't you at your lectures today?' came the voice. I was scared. My palms we're sweaty, I had to think quickly. And boom, it popped into my mind. I feigned a cough and replied, 'I'm sick. I think I should be in tomorrow *Cough*, I'm going to see a doctor right now.' 'Okay, you have a safe and enjoyable day,' he replied. Then he hung up. That was a close call, I had to do something that would completely relax me. I remember overhearing two girls in the cafeteria talking about how getting your nails done would relax you... so that's exactly what I did. *** As I sat in the salon, the only man there, I listened to the girls around me talking about how bitchy some of their friends are. I heard them say all these rotten things about other women beside themselves. And it got me thinking... how come women are so evil? They're vindictive, scheming, conniving bitches, who always seem to have something other than what they are doing/saying on their mind. After I had gotten my nails done and left the salon, I realized one thing: women are ICKY!
