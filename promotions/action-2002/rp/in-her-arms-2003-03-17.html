In Her Arms

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Conrad Ramsey had lied to the first woman who he had ever had strong feelings for. He had made a promise to her, but had broken it, knowing he was going to before the promise was ever made. The sixty-one year old had been in and out of the hospital for weeks, resulting from various injuries working for Action!. He was taking a beating week in and week out. He wasn't even near 100%, probably closer to 25% than anything. But he had someone to look after him. Samantha Dox. They had returned to her Augusta, Maine home. The same home Marcus Alexander Dox had grown up in. Conrad had even been sleeping in his old bedroom, with the Star Wars bedding and all. Trophies lined the walls, from various things that 'The Danger Man' had won throughout his life. Old championship belts from his days in EWCWF/FWO and GFWA. He hadn't taken them with him. He had left them with his mother. So she could look at what all he had accomplished in the sport she never wanted him to enter. The former Bantam Champion was in recovery, both his profession and his relationship with Samantha. Though it did seem as if they were growing more close than ever. All over a little lie. To repair that damage, both had to share things. Things they had never told anyone else in their lives. Like how Conrad spent three nights in jail when he was twenty-six years old. Or how Samantha and her ex-husband's brother(Max's uncle), almost had a child, but she had a miscarriage. Nothing was hidden. Everything was out in the open. And they were the better for it. No more lies. That was the foundation for which their relationship would flourish. Now, as he recovered, Conrad lay in Samantha's arms, listening to classic music. Letting the time slip away. Just enjoying being with the other. No thoughts of wrestling came into Ramsey's mind. Only one thing was floating around in his brain... I'm going to marry this woman.
