On The Road to Success (Overnight)

16 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/02 OPEN The scene opened and displayed the same place where our hero was last seen. The super big McDonalds with a play land. Of course by now the store had closed and everything was still; the balls in the ball pit sat motionless with moonlight seeping through the window and the jungle gym collecting tiny amounts of dust. The weather had since changed from a nice sunny afternoon to a drizzling dark crisp night. A little bit of frost could be seen forming on the grass. And as for the Pikachu Beetle, it was still parked outside. You can hear a voice, not one, but two.... it turned out that down the hall a light was still on. Christopher Fox and Onjin sat at the twenty-four-seven takeout window. Except, they haven’t seen a customer in about five hours.... Title: On The Road to Success (Overnight) Date: 11/16/02 11:00 pm EST Place: McDonalds Play Land Fox sat in a padded swivel seat as did his friend Onjin. If it wasn’t for Onjin, Fox would be in juvenile detention. lol. Onjin sat across from ‘Kid’, as he nibbled on a fry and looked out of the takeout window. Onjin “What a day, eh Fox? First I get transferred a couple of days ago, and then I save you from getting in trouble. Now we’re just hanging out. What next?” Fox “Hmm I dunno, I sure hope Pikachu is otay in the car though....” Onjin “I’m sure he is.” Wiping some major grease off of his face, Onjin rose from his chair. Hey, what’s he gonna do? Why’d he just grab his jacket? He wouldn’t dare go out in this kind of weather! Onjin checked his watch. Onjin “Quitin’ time.” Fox “But this is a 24/7 takeout window.” Onjin “Yeah, but there is a McDonalds policy here. If no service comes in five hours, then we have the option to close for the night. Anyway, c’mon Fox let’s go outside and have some fun!” Onjin proceeded to exit the small takeout booth and into the main part of the building, but before this could happen Fox had a burning question that he had to put out. Fox “Onjin, what’s your last name?” Onjin turned just after he opened the door. They stood, quietly. Onjin “My last name is Dyce.” Fox “Onjin Dyce. I like it!” Onjin “Ok? ....C’mon, let’s go.” Both “kids” headed to the main exit / entrance. Fox won the race and progressed outside; the air was just crisp enough not to make anyone too cold. Christopher had a t-shirt on and he seemed fine. In fact he was boiling hot! They walked out to the curb, as Onjin sat down and then Fox did too. Both gazed up to the beautiful three-quarter moon. Onjin “Darn, this would have been like a Disney moment if it was a full moon. I never have those moments!” Fox “I hear ya.” As they talked a slight bit of breath could be seen by both men. It was maybe a bit too cold now. Onjin “Can I ask you something Christopher?” Fox “You can ask me anything within my Dragonball Z powered body!” Onjin “Did you know a guy named Cory?” Fox “Nu-uh. Why do you ask?” Onjin “He was my Dad. He told me that he wanted to be cremated when he pasted on. That was a while ago. Just before he did he ran for an election to this new wrestling federation. He won, but then when he died the association was closed. He wanted to get the best wrestler too....” Fox “Well, I’m under contract you know.” Onjin “....heh.... yeah I know, he would have loved to see you perform in a match.” Onjin’s head lowered. Fox sat nicely and blinked innocently. Fox “What is it? You can tell me anything.” Onjin looked over to Fox. Onjin “You’re someone I can trust. Well, it may kinda gross you out but, uh, you’re greatly involved with this. You remember when we first met?” Fox “Yeah I do, my Mommy wanted to see Joey, an old school chum. You gave me a hot pocket that was filled with green stuff.... and then, we left!” Yeah, what was up with that green stuff anyway? Onjin “Well this is sorta ironic but here goes.... my dad accidentally was spilled out of his jar that night. I did it. He got into your hot pocket and turned it that ugly color! When you left I couldn’t help but laugh at how ironic everything seemed.” Fox “What do you mean bud-o?” Onjin “My dad, trying to be the best wrestling owner actually was eaten by the best wrestler ever.... and to add to it, my dad would have liked to sign you up!” The best wrestler ever? But LL isn’t anywhere in sight! LOL? Fox “You think I’m the best?” Onjin “Hell yeah! But I thought you’d be mad that you ate a bit of my dad....” Fox “Meh, but the best ever? Stand up Onjin.” Both men stood and looked at each other. Their breath was the only thing visibly moving until.... Fox “I’ll never be the best, not if I just sit here, I’m-I’m on the road to success and being the best, 1, 2, 3....” Onjin & Fox “BIG TIME!!!!!!” Onjin “Are you leaving?” Fox “Yep, but at least we ended this night like Disney, hehehe.” The two giggled as Fox’s mind was set on positively succeeding. The road to success had just begun. CLOSE
