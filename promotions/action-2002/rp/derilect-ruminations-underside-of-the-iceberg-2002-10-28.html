derilect ruminations: underside of the iceberg

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/28/2002 i am submerged, but you can hear me clearly. i know you can. i am more 'you' than 'you' yourself. the human psyche is like an iceberg. i am the larger portion which remains underwater. even though you are the surface, you are fully aware of me. i know you are. i know you are. we can't continue this battle. you attempt to discombobalate me with your false manifestations of schitzophinea. shitface and fuckhead aren't real. you don't have multiple personalities. only one. who endulges in lying to itself. you can force feed yourself your own bullshit till your stomach ruptures. but it changes nothing. diamond is dead. she killed herself. you must allow yourself to grieve. you must allow youself to heal. you must move on. this is not healthy. this in not healthy. you will destroy us both. you can hear me. you can hear me well. ironic that part of your sickness is this full awareness of yourself. you know i'm right. you know life will be better once you listen to me. you know this. you know this. you know this. you know this but will you going to act. ... nick? ...I'm sorry, were you saying something? nevermind.
