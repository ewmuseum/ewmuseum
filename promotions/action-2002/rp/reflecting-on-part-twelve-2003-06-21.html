Reflecting On: Part Twelve.

21 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was a bright summer day. Warm air. Nice, soft, breeze. It was the most perfect day in his life. Marcus Alexander Dox had been married for only two weeks to the beautiful Jessica Michelle. He was the happiest he had ever been. He was back home, with his mother, though. Together they sat out on the deck, overlooking the large backyard. They just sat there, no speaking. Xander had been through a lot in his life. Deaths, injuries, and heartbreak. But now... now everything was just right. His had a wonderful wife. He was speading one last time with his mother. Just alone, together, basking in the day. Feeling just spiffy. Samantha looked at her son. Seeing how grown up he was. The last of her children. She could only imagine what great things lied ahead for him. There was nothing more she loved in the world than her son. She looked at him and saw her late husband. Saw his stubbornness, his good looks. He, unlike any other, was most like his father. She just knew that one day, one day he was going to do something remarkable. She turned away from him and stared out in the blue, cloudless sky. A bright smile on her face. Xander just looked ahead, down at the bright green grass. At the birds that flew by. Just remembering. Remembering his life. All the things that had lead to this point. Lead to him sitting quietly with his mother on the deck of the home he grew up in. This one moment, the picture-perfect moment, would never be forgotten. Deep into old age, neither would forget this. They were just taking a moment. One last time before Xander went off to live with his wife. Start his own life. Create his own family. One last time for Samantha to just look upon her son. Remember all those years raising him. Knowing that she was about to release him into the world. Just a moment.
