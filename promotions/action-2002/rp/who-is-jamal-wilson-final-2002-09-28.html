Who is Jamal Wilson? -Final

28 September 2002

Date: 09-28-02 It was now 1999, and I had been training with Ricky Morton for almost a year. I was about ready to graduate from his school. All of my hard work wrestling on the grimy, cold, disgusting mats in his ring. I remembered all the days that I went home with bruises all over my body. The days that I spent my time in the locker room fantasying about the sweet sweaty bodies of my opponents and forgetting all about performing wrestling moves. We were set to graduate in a day, and that night, I decided that it warranted me going out to PARTY! I really didn’t have anything else to do, being as in I dropped out of The University of Memphis, and I was a loner for the most part. The club was called “Shooters.” Thank God it wasn’t a “Hooters”, I can’t think of anything more disgusting then so called ‘beautiful’ women running around in small halter-tops showcasing their large breasts. ICKY! I went into the club, looking pimped out in my stylish clothing. Some pink bubble wrap pants that I had designed myself, and a pink t-shirt, which had the word ‘Cutie’ on the front of it. I took a seat at the bar and quickly surveyed the dance floor, looking for somebody to get my groove on with. And there he was. The most beautiful thing I had ever seen in my life. He looked to be nearing 400 pounds, but he was sexy. He was Hispanic male in his late twenties dancing by himself, and doing it well. He wore a purple suit, and he danced with a cane. Under his suit, he wore a shirt that said ‘I’m With Gay’ and had an arrow pointing downward towards his lower regions. I had to have him. I got up from the bar and walked over to him. “May I have this dance?” I asked with much trepidation in my voice. “Jou wanna dance wit’ me?” he said with a heavy Mexican accent as I quickly nodded. That night, we danced and danced and danced. And also, that night, I didn’t go home alone. Chino came over to my home, and we spent the night talking to each other about things that we enjoyed doing… then we slept together. NO! NOT LIKE THAT! He was on one side of the bed, and I was on the other. Nothing sexual, he just held me. I felt so comfortable in his arms, and I had the best sleep I’d ever had outside of my native Canada. *** Weeks and weeks later, as I was grinding it out on the independent scene, Chino confessed to me that he was interested in becoming a professional wrestler like me. He said that I had spoken so fondly of it, that he HAD to at least try it. I hooked him up with some training, and before we knew it, we were both wrestlers. It wasn’t long thereafter when I had to move away from Memphis, where we were both living at the time, and I had to move into a house in Atlanta, where the promotion I was headed to work for was based. Almost a year after that, I was in PIW, and on top of world. I hadn’t seen Chino in 11 months, and I missed him greatly. We kept in touch through emails and through phone calls. He had told me he was still wrestling, but he was thinking of quitting because he really wasn’t doing much on the independent wrestling scene. I had remembered reading somewhere that Action! Wrestling had opened applications. They were mainly looking for tag teams. I quickly called Chino and asked him if he had some tapes of his wrestling matches from the Indy scene, he told me that he did. I asked that he send them to me via airmail, and he obliged, he didn’t ask me why, either. When I received his tapes, I took all of my tapes, and went down to a production studio in downtown Atlanta. I gave them the tapes, and told them to compile the good high spots for me. They worked with the company I wrestled for at the time, and were fairly good when it came to doing stuff like this. I paid them, and they told me to return in a week for my tapes. When I came back a week later, they gave me one tape, it was a compilation of the good spots that Chino and me had on the same tape. In the background the song “Dancing Queen” by Abba. I saw the tape, and loved it! I immediately sent it out to Reed Young, the owner of Action! Three weeks later, I got this in the mail. Jamal, Chino, we’ve seen your work, and we can say that we think you would a great addition to the Action! Tag team division roster if you are still interested. Please, mail us at [detail withheld] . Thank you for your interest, Action! Staff. The rest, as they say, is history.
