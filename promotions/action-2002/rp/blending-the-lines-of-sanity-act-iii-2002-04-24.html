Blending the Lines of Sanity, Act III

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/24/02 'I was jobbed....to Simon....fucking....SEAMAN?' Tommy Matthews threw the television of his hotel room to the floor, watching the screen shatter. 'JOBBED TO SIMON SEAMAN!' Tommy screamed again. 'WHO THE FUCK DOES REED YOUNG THINK I AM?' Tommy pounded his fists on the spot on the desk where the television once sat. 'I'm....supposed to be.....the next....big....THING!' Tommy yelled. 'Not Brock fucking Lesnar, as the WWF is going around claiming. All Brock has going for him is his.......' Tommy stopped his sentance 'GOD DAMNIT!' Tommy screamed angrily. 'THAT SHOULD BE ME! THAT SHOULD BE ME!' 'Excuse me, Tommy?' a voice came from outside the door. Tommy sighed with a bit of annoyance. One of Action's annoying road agents. 'What the fuck do you want?' Tommy asked him from inside his room. 'Can't you hear that I'm venting?' 'Yes, I do, Tommy, and that's why I came here.' he answered. 'It's three in the morning and you're waking everyone up.' 'I DON'T GIVE A SHIT!' Tommy yelled. 'I WAS JOBBED TO SIMON FUCKING SEAMAN! SCREW YOU!' Tommy opened the door of his hotel room, only to slam it in the road agent's face. He did this about three more times until the road agent finally walked away. 'USELESS FUCK!' Tommy screamed at him down the hallway. 'Sir, we're going to have to ask you to quiet down.' Tommy turned to see the bellman talking to him. 'Oh, now even the fucking bellman is going to tell me what to do!' Tommy said sarcastically. 'Can my life get any BETTER? CAN IT?' 'Sir, please quiet....' the bellman began to say, but found himself shoved into the wall by Tommy. 'Woops, I slipped.' Tommy said, and then slammed his door shut. 'And tell them to bring me a new fucking TV, this one's broke!' Tommy opened the door to yell at the bellman, then closed it again. He sat down on his bed in an angry heap, then pulled out a cell phone. Hedialed a number, and then waited. 'Matt?' Tommy asked him. 'Do you have the little bitch yet? No? Well, damnit, what are you waiting for? Ah, I see. Well, I'll keep in touch.' Tommy hung up his phone. 'I'm getting my fucking push....one way or the other.' Fin.
