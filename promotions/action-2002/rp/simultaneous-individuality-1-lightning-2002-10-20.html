Simultaneous individuality 1 - Lightning

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th October 2002, 15:54 The endless flicking and turning of thin paper was starting to grate on Lee's mind, but he did his best just to ignore it all. He'd been sitting in this cosy public library seat for two hours, engrossed in a book about the Cold War. He had been here first, everyone should have to adjust to him. The lights were down low, there was a sign on the door asking for quiet, couldn't people take a hint anymore? 'Ehem,' coughed Lee, just loud enough for everyone in the vicinity to hear. As he returned his gaze to the printed pages under his chin, he hoped that would be enough for people to turn their respective pieces of paper a little quieter. Still no change. Fed up, Lee looked down at the shiny brown table, and the interesting effect the dimmed lights had on it's reflection. Most twenty three year olds would be socialising at a soccer match on a Saturday afternoon, but not Lee. He knew that there were better ways to use his democratic rights, like reading up on those who confronted the Soviet Union and it's communist allies so valiantly. 'Ehem!' coughed Lee again. Those around him weren't taking the hint. Frustrated, Lee slammed the book shut, and the echo bounced off the library walls. A few people raised their heads and glared at the wrestler, but most couldn't be bothered. With small and deliberate steps, Lee walked over to the nearest bookcase, stroked the spine of the enormous written documentary, and lay it to rest on top of a row which contained soft back versions of crime authors such as John Grisham. It was totally the wrong section, but Lee didn't particularly care, as the overweight broad of a librarian now had something to do to earn her measly wage. What was the point of employing a smell woman to scan books in an out? A ten year old boy expelled from school could do the same job, without the same 'how dare you bother me' looks the current crop of librarians was insistant on giving. Lee was smartly dressed that afternoon, with beige khaki trousers and a black sweater that kept out the cold. Now the calendar had been flipped onto October, the wind was getting increasingly brisk and the showers of rain more frequent. Contemplating how he was going to get home without having to get on a horrible middle class bus full of middle class people and their lower class ways, Lee went into the toilet. No one else was in there at least, but someone had been. Three toilet cubicles, none of them flushed. What ever happened to common decency? If only people would use their democratic right to instate a law saying all chains on toilets must be pulled when someone came within a five meter radius of them. That'd solve a lot of unsavoury smells. Unzipping his trousers (being careful to make sure no perverts were lurking in the corners), Lee cast his mind back to the events of Monday 7th October, and Action! Pressure Point. He'd been pinned be Neko Case in a tag team match, causing his tag team The Elements to lose. 'David should have made the save,' moaned Lee to himself. Instead, he'd been in the embarassing position of being kept down to the mat for a three count by a communist, a Chinese one at that. Lee's uncle had often referred to the Chinese as the 'scum of the Earth', and right now Lee was finding it difficult to disagree with his elder. And then there was the main event, where cousin Benjamin (otherwise known as Airborn) fought Joey Malone for the Action! Championship. 'David should have been there to prevent Yankee from causing the DQ,' grumbled Lee to himself once more. Benjamin had actually put up a valiant fight, and he'd stood a fantastic chance of bringing wrestling's top prize home to the A-Star Stable. But someone needed to cut off Jonny Yankee, and David Dew was nowhere to be seen. That's how Lee saw it. Zipping up his trousers, and particularly anxious not to get mugged by any diseased homeless beings, Lee emerged from the library doors and made a dart through the streets towards the hotel the family was staying in.
