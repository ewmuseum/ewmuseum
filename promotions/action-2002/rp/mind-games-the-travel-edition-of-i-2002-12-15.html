Mind Games, The Travel Edition of; I

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/15/02 Saturday night, Max Danger had been in Boston Massachusetts, fighting his tag team partner Bradley Duncan at PIW's Massacre. But now, it was Sunday morning. He wasn't anywhere near Boston. He was in Dothan, Alabama. Home of Kayden Paulton. He was driving his rented SUV(Lincoln Navigator) down a long stretch of highway, glancing over at a small piece of paper, which looked as though it had been ripped from a much large sheet, which had a number and a street name written upon it. 'Just one more left turn, about a mile up, and I'll be there. You better be ready,' Danger muttered to himself, rubbing his hands together menacingly. The car nearly went off the road, but Max slapped both hands around the steering wheel, and righted the massive vehicle. He looked around, making sure no one was looking, and continued on, sweat slightly beading down his forehead. It wasn't long before Max Danger was stopping in front of a large white house, with grayish-blue roofing and shudders. He pulled into the gravel drive-way and could see a long bench swing on the large front porch. He stepped out of the SUV and headed across the cobblestone walkway and onto the front porch. He cracked his neck as he walked up to the front door. He looked down and read aloud what the welcome mat said, 'Home Sweet Home... pfft, whatever.' As he shook his head he reached his arm up and began to beat on the screen door. The sound it made echoed throughout the sky. He stood, waiting, but no one was coming. Max took a step over to his right and peered through the window, but he could barely see anything thanks to the curtains being drawn. He had only a small crease to see through. But then he say him. Danger pinned himself up against the wall between the door and window. The door came open and a voice said, 'Hello?' But Max didn't answer. The man pressed himself up agianst the screen door and looked both ways. He didn't see anyone so he shouted again, 'Hello? Anyone out there?' But still, Danger didn't answer. The man opened up the door and stepped out on the porch. As the door slammed shut, he looked to his right, but saw nothing. He then turned to his left and his mouth dropped open and his eyes widened. But it wasn't the reaction Max was looking for. It wasn't fear or horror that struck Kayden's face, it was happiness. Danger couldn't believe it. And before he could do anything, Paulton had lunged himself at 'The Danger Man' and wrapped his arms around him tightly for a big hug. 'You came! I can't believe it! You've come ot let me help you!' Danger tried to push Kayden away, but his grip was too tight. Thankfully for Max, however, Paulton released him on his own accord, and then pulled the screen door back open and beckoned Danger inside. 'Come in! Come in! I've just prepared breakfast!' Danger was about to enter. Kayden's smiling and good naturedness was very disarming. You almost forgot what you were doing. But Danger quickly snapped out of it. He spun around just as he was about to step foot in the house. 'NO!' Paulton jumped back, caught off guard by the shout. 'I didn't come here for breakfast! I didn't come here to be your pal! I didn-' Kayden cut him off. 'I know. I know. You're weary from your travels. Please, let me prepare you a bed.' Paulton tried to get into the house, but Max slammed the screen door shut. It bounced back and hit him in the back. He stood for a moment, smarting from the hit, but quickly forged ahead, cutting Paulton off as he tried to speak again. 'Are you-' 'I'm fine! But you... you on the otherhand,' Danger was pointed and shaking a finger at Kayden. 'You are NOT fine. You-' Danger was cut off again, 'My mom always said I was quite handsome. You should see my yearbook photos!' He, again, tried to lead Max into the house, but Max grabbed him by the front of his shirt and pushed him back away from the door. Danger then stood directly in front of it and sneered at Paulton. Then he began to speak calmly, 'You, Kayden Paulton, need to stay out of my life. You need to quit poking your nose where it doesn't belong. YOU,' he raised his voice slightly, jabbing a finger into Kayden's chest. 'YOU - NEED - TO - KEEP - YOUR - OPINIONS - AND - THOUGHTS - TO - YOURSELF!' He continued to jab his finger into Kayden's chest between each word. Kayden was now almost falling off the porch, as he stood on the edge of the top step. Max didn't seem to notice, not until Paulton snatched two handfuls of his Spongebob Squarepants t-shirt, and pulled him down with him. Danger was laying on top of Kayden as a car full of teenagers drove past, they shouted, 'HOMOS!' And drove away. Max quickly pulled himself up off Paulton, and just looked down at him. Kayden got onto his feet as Danger was pacing around the front lawn. His breath forming in the cold air, he was breathing so heavily. He then turned to Kayden, with a finger mere centimeters from his face, 'Just keep away from me!' After that stern message, he stomped back across the lawn and into his SUV. He did a U-Turn in the drive-way, shooting rocks all over the place, and sped back out onto the road, heading back into the drection he had come. Kayden Paulton just stood in the front yard, waving goodbye to 'The Danger Man'. Then, as he was about to head back into his house, he said to himself, 'That man. So much we need to talk about. But I'm gonna be here for him.' That wasn't something Max Danger would want to hear.
