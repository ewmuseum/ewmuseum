Roughing It ( Part One )

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 22nd, 2002 This Kent Anthason promotion is brought to you by... A picture of a couple making hamburgers on a grill, smiling, sharing a good ol' time with the kids is seen. The man is wearing an apron which conveniently reads 'kiss my ass' instead of the conventional 'kiss the cook'. No matter, however, since their Brady Bunch lifestyle is shortly interrupted by a falling figure. Yes, kids, he's falling alright... XM Radio... It's none other than Cleveland Browns Star Quarterback, Tim Couch! Ooh! He lands face first into the red-hot coals. The kids gasp in horror! The mother sheilds her daughters eyes from the scene! Tim Couch's face is disfigured from the burns and bleeding profusely all over the family's hamburgers. The eight year old son laughs whole-heartedly and asks his dad... 'Can we keep him, daddy?' Without warning, the father passes out and the scene fades as we see none other than the XM logo appear... XM Radio: Sports Broadcasts. The logo fades as we come right into the absolutely new, hot off the waffle press, Kent Anthason promo which fades in. What we first see isn't an exactly wholesome sight. We see a wolf pulling apart a dead rabbit. The wolf isn't exactly aware of the shadow behind him as he pulls the small intestines out and starts to chew as the juice... The scene suddenly goes to black as none other than Ben Stein comes in. It's just Ben Stein and a black background. It's almost fitting. 'Excuse me, but I'm going to have to intterupt the beginning of this promotion to let all the little kiddies out there know that this is rated TV:MA, for mature audiences only. If there's any children watching... Uhh... The boogie-man will probably eat your face off for watching this. Thank you, and have a great day.' The men in white suits carry Ben Stein off as we re-appear to the scene of the wolf eating the rabbit. But, it's kinda different this time. It's Kent Anthason ripping the brains out of the wolf and eating them. He's smiling a wicked sort of smile as the fake audience who suddenly gasps even though no-one is really there, gasps. Somewhere, in the country Tipper Gore is gasping also. 'TWISTED SISTER? TWISTED SISTER?' Her head explodes and George W. Bush laughs hysterically. As this promo is going no-where at all. Anyhow, Kent gets up from his mid-day snack and returns to the camp site. Oh, I guess I should inform you. While you were gone, Kent and Lucas and Kent's ex-girlfriend Alyssa had all went camping. I call Alyssa Cleeda Kent's Ex-girlfriend, but she's really not. I'd like to use the word (Explicit terminology) partners, myself, but TNN won't let me say (Explicit Terminology) partners. Oh well. (Explicit Terminology) them. I never (Explicit Terminology)-ing liked them, anyhow. You'll probably soon find out that Kent and Alyssa get into arguements once and a while but always end up in bed the same night. In other words, they're getting married. Just not now. They're (Explicit Terminology.)-ing. 'No, we're not!' Kent exclaims! 'Who the hell are you, booming voice from seemingly nowhere?' Well, I'm the booming voice from seemingly nowhere, stupidass. Speaking of which, where the HELL are you? You're camping, but where at? Alyssa speaks up, 'Yellowstone National Park... Kent, what's all over your face?' Kent shrugs. 'Cool-aide?' Alyssa kinda frowns at the thought of what Kent really WAS doing, but there's no need to get into that. Lucas has a marshmellow on a stick and roasting it over a fire. He's staring off into the flames as his marshmellow finally burns off and falls into the fire.... 'God, camping is so goddamn boring. We should go on an adventure or something.' Lucas tells Kent without actually looking at him. 'Do you know what happens whenever we go on an adventure?' 'What?' 'Something bad usually happens, I mean, we always over-look obvious clues of danger that's apparently telling us that adventuring is NOT for the standard man.' Alyssa gets up and walks over to the tent. Telling them both she's getting her make-up. Kent rolls his eyes at her behind her back as she goes off. Lucas is irate, though. 'Then what are we supposed to do? Sit around and whistle dixie while somewhere in the galaxy Aliens are taking over random planets?' 'I wouldn't whistle dixie. I hate that fucking tune. I'd probably whistle something like... Ooh-ee-ooh-aahh-ahh-ting-tang-walla-walla-bing-bang!' Lucas gives Kent a stare. Soon after, however, Alyssa screams. Kent runs over to the tent as Lucas plops another marshmellow on his stick and begins to stare off again. A loud thump is heard. As Kent starts to drag something to the fire. Alyssa follows close behind. 'Does this help you on your thoughts about adventuring?' Kent drops the dead carcass of Mark Whalberg, who has a sign stapled to his forehead. His eyes are in the back of his head as Lucas bursts out laughing. 'Hahahah! Now, I want to more than ever! I want to personally congradulate anyone who even thought about killing Marky Mark.' Alyssa and Kent stare dumbfoundedly back at him, as Lucas did to Kent a few seconds ago. 'WHAT?' Devol takes a big bite of marshmellow.... 'Alright.' Kent says... 'How about we go hiking?' The scene fades. I bet you thought it was over, didn't you? This Kent Anthason promotion was partially brought to you by... The Jetta... We see a man standing in the road, hands in his pockets. It appears to be a JOKER look-alike! Woah! What a coinencidence! He's even got the outfit on! He's whistling dixie (Go figure.) to himself as he rocks back and forth on his heels. Too retarded to know he's standing in the road. *whistle, whistle* All of a sudden, he flies into the air. Nothing seems to have caused it, but he lands hard on his face. Scuff marks scrape the sides of his face. Blood everywhere, as after a while he gets on his knees and in an emotional outrage tries to fit all his teeth back into place. Meanwhile, to the far left... We see a Jetta stop. The Jetta... So fast... They don't even see it coming. Kent Anthason steps out of the car as he gives a thumbs up. A wide grin comes over his face as light glints off his teeth making him the picture perfect role-model... ... Yeah, right. *To Be Continued*
