Action! — Tales

Limelight.

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/05/2002 'EVEREST CATACLYSM!' a raspy, loud voice yelled out, as we look at a video tape of Joey Malone, very weak and very tired, driving a man into the canvas with his signature finishing maneuver. That voice belonged to Greg Parker, a man considered to be the voice of the Internet Wrestling Organization. He was calling a match with his partners, JT and Nikki, between Joey Malone and a man called Donnie Daze. Most of you would know him as Matt Rivers. The two had been wrestling and fighting each other for more than an hour, in a match that could never be for the weak of heart. A grueling iron man match. Life. Death. And Endurance. Now in overtime, and with a pair of cages around the ring, and weapons inside the ring, Malone and Daze were no longer fighting for any mere championship. They were fighting for survival. The fans were on their feet, yelling and screaming for Joey to have one last gasp of air. 'AGAIN, THE EVEREST CATACLYSM! MALONE FUCKING DRILLS HIM WITH IT AGAIN! BOTH MEN ARE DOWN AGAIN, BUT MALONE IS CRAWLING TO GET TO COVER DAZE!' he yelled, watching in anticipation as Malone inched his bloody carcass toward Daze's own. The fans continued their chants, as Joey finally managed to barely put an arm over Daze's chest. 'MALONE HAS AN ARM OVER DAZE! GUNNAR SMITH SEES THE COVER IN THE CONFUSION! HE MOVES OVER INTO THE PINFALL!' Parker yelled, again, seeing the IWO veteran begin to count the fall. He brought his arm up and slammed it down on the canvas, the fans chanting with the count. One. The chaos on the outside continued, Keri Lindum trying to avoid all of the people around ringside, while Syphon Fission was dealing with Daze's managers. Gunnar brought his arm down again, the fans totally behind the count. Two. There were no signs of life left in the bodies of Joey Malone and Donnie Daze. Gunnar brought his arm down again. Three. The bell had rung. The match, the war, and the era of Daze... was over. 'OH MY GOD! IT'S OVER! IT'S FUCKING OVER! WE HAVE A NEW IWO WORLD CHAMPION!' Parker yelled, though he couldn't be heard over the noise of elation from the fans at ringside, who were on their feet, yelling, screaming, and clapping their hands in happiness, and in respect for the two athletes that put their bodies on the line for the title, and for the fans. The title lay on the ground, and Rashard Clark, who just entered the ring, sees it. He looks at it with sadness in his eyes, knowing that his best friend just accomplished the impossible. He draped it across the chest of an extremely exhausted Joey Malone. Joey stirred awake, and even though every single muscle in his body ached, every single bone in his body creaked, and every single thought in his head drifted, he still fought to his feet. With the title in his hand, he raised it up for all to see, and they loved him for it. The day before, he was a nobody from Phoenix that no one thought could have ever made the big leagues. That day, he became a world champion. That was almost a year ago. May 27th, 2001. Sacramento, California. The Arco Arena. Joey's first and only world championship came and went in two months, losing the belt in the same kind of match to Kent Anthason. Joey watched this match in his silence, a match he had seen, himself, numerous times. He always felt immense giddiness, watching himself win that title. He had never, ever had a victory that occured in that ring that meant as much as that night. The happiest moment of his life came in that building, in front of a crowd, some of which were friends and family who made the trip from Mesa to Sacramento to watch him fight. And who stole his thunder? Rashard did. Many wrestlers tried to come down to the ring to help Joey celebrate his triumph. It was no secret that Joey was well-liked in the IWO's locker rooms, and they wanted to see to it that he was given the proper respect as the new champion. Samuel Potright and Kent Anthason entered the ring, but as others came down to celebrate, Fission cut them off. 'Hold up.' he said, holding his hand out to prevent people from coming down. Some men, like Ben Archer and Shawn Arrows, ignored him and headed toward the ring, but Anthason and Potright lashed out at the two, forcing them to back off. 'Don't any of you even THINK about getting in this ring... this is the territory of the better men. Of the heroes... of the legends of the future... the kings of the ring.' Rashard yelled. It was on this night, with an exhausted Joey watching on, barely able to stand with the support of Keri Lindum and the ring ropes, that the team of Fission, Potright, and Anthason, known only as the Mass Media Movement, formed 'We're Better Than You 2'. Rashard had once again stolen the well-deserved spotlight right out of Joey's hands. And he placed it upon himself, once again. Once he was done with that, he bailed out on WBTY2 a week later. Joey watched this, knowing full well that Fission's time had passed him then, and that it wouldn't come back for him for a long time. He tried *everything*... *EVERYTHING*, to make sure that the limelight was on him. He took it from him during the happiest moment of his life. He took it from him when he intentionally injured Joey's friend, Kent Anthason. Every time Joey's accomplished something, who was there to take it away and make him feel insignificant? Rashard. Now that Joey overcame him at Resentment, there was only one question on Joey's mind. A question that had been eating away at him since he defeated him on that night. What would Rashard do to take the spotlight, again?
