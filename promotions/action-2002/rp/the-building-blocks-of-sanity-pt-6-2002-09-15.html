The Building Blocks of Sanity pt. 6

15 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 15th, 2002 August 21st, 2002 – Wednesday – 7:30 PM Roots. Stiches. GAP. West 49. Wigger World. The shopping mall on the East part of town was bustling with shoppers, people scurrying from one outlet to the next with bags in their hands and boxes stuffed under the arm pits. The struggled from place to place, occasionally bumping others who were just as stressed as they were, ready to push right back. Jeff stared up at the diverse array of signs plastered in all directions. A dumbfounded look on his face. Moments later a pretty young brunet, around the age of twenty-one, walked by. Garvin stepped in her path and opened his trap to speak. “Hi, could you recommend a retailer that may suit my… um… Style?” The girl looked him up and down with a laugh, fixated on how ugly his face was. “Murry’s Masks… That would defiantly help you out.” She gave a cackle and continued on her way, leaving Jeff standing there confused. Garvin scratched his head, “I wonder what they sell there? Oh well. Looks like I’m going to have to settle this the best way I know how.” Jeff closed his eyes and his arm outstretched, his finger pointing out. …Poking a five year old girl in her eye. The little girl shrieked in pain and ran back to her mother. “Ennie…” His finger moved to the next clothing store… “Meanie…” And again, to the next one in line, working clockwise. “Minie… Moe… Catcha tiger… By the toe… If he hollars… Let him go… Ennie…” The pace began to slow. “Minie… Moe.” Jeff’s eyes opened to reveal that he was pointing to a store apparently selling woman’s lingerie. Any other day Jeff would have been fair game for a little, ‘Grab and Sniff’. He saw in a catalogue, one he found in the trash, that the new panty line was out, this brought a smile to his face. But he wasn’t here for pleasure, he was here to do as Claire had instructed… And that was… Uh… Um… Jeff pulled the list from his pocket. ‘Buy new clothes, go out, make friends, and feel good about yourself.’ Garvin frowned and moved his hand a step to the right so it was inclined with the next shop. Wigger World. He watched as a bunch of laid back teenagers exited the store; wearing baggy jeans that clung to their knee caps, an NBA jersey, plenty of chains, and a side ways, upside down sun viser… How could the sun viser protect their eyes from the harmful ultra violet rays when it wasn’t even in the right position. Their wacky concepts freaked Jeff out to say the least, he wanted nothing to do with a place that promoted the wrong way to wear a sun viser… That was just sad. Jeff turned… His mouth opened, his eyes widen… “OH… MY… GOD!” What could it be? “COW BOYS!” Jeff Garvin screeched at the top of his lungs, beginning to make his way towards a store that sold Country Western Clothing… Cow Boy’s amazed Jeff, if he hadn’t of made it in professional wrestling, he would have liked to own his own dude ranch and breed horses… *Clears Throat*… Erm… …With chickens. SUPER ANIMAL! Garvin stood in front of the mirror, ready to enter when he realised that Country Western clothing was not what the kid’s these days would call ‘Hip’. Sighing, Jeff turned towards ‘Wigger World’ once again and decided he better jump on the bandwagon before he spent the two hundred bucks his therapist had given him at some strip joint across town. Sure their girl’s ‘assets’ were drooping but it was still tempting. Seconds later Jeff disappeared inside the store…
