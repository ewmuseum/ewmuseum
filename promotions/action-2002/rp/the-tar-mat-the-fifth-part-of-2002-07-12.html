The Tar Mat, The Fifth Part of

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As mist and dust roamed throughout the night air, LLB was about to enter a frightening graveyard. But even before he could open the gate, it already removed itself out of the way... yet there was no wind, so how did it open? Leaving that mystery to be untold, the lawyer walked on into the foggy wetlands in search of something. Swamp. Broken trees. It was not a pleasant sight. Looking at the face of ‘The Law’ though, no one could tell... He was so calm and modest... Nothing was going to stop him. Not wind. Not the worst sense of scare that could be thrown in his path. Stump. He came upon what he was looking for. LLB tilted his head back evilly, knowing that the answer to sickness... to pure disgusting thoughts which could be edged into time... lay right at the souls of his feet. Taking the shovel from his resting, silent hands... ‘The Law’ began to dig. Throwing the dirt over his shoulder, he couldn’t help but become covered in it, head to toe, all the more that he worked. And he liked it. Each new plant of the silver tile into the rotten grounds of death, it made him feel more powerful... spike his lips just a little bit higher... Send him to that uncanny God-like stage. Stump. Before he even knew it, the goal was reached. --Later-- LLB stretched the wooden handle back, and pulled it forth to the ground. The view was horrifying. It was sick. It was the lowest LLB had ever gone before. He pressed his boot slowly against the side of it... as the soft, paper thin layer caved in. “Welcome back to the testify.” He kicked it, hard. “Because I literally have ‘Showcase Style’...” LLB glanced above to the heavens. “And you... don’t.”
