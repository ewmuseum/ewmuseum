Oh Man!

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/12/02 OPEN Jeeeessssss I gotta scratch it! Yes it is him. The one the only Christopher Fox wandering down the empty arena hallway before the start of Action Wrestling’s show: Pressure Point. The air is a bit misty. The lights are off making it somewhat hard to see. Fox looks up, glancing at the window above him which shines in a small tint of light, but without a washroom close to Fox.... he just had to scratch IT. Fox stops dead in his tracks to check if anyone is coming. The cost is clear! I can scratch it now! Without further hesitation Chris rips at his private area, of course on the outside of his pants sicko. Ahhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh! Ohhhhhhhhhh!! Yummmmmmm! That’s just what I needed, and Mommy says only touch there when having a shower. Puh! like I am going to resist this type of pleasure! Suddenly outward from the shadows a noise is heard. A waft of clean smelling air hits Fox in the face! “Ah!” Shouts Chris, “I suspect someone clean is near me! Show yourself!” Following his words all the lights in the hallway turn on as Mommy Fox appears beside the light fixture, cleaning it. “Oh hi hun,” Mommy says in a weird voice, “I saw what you did. You scratched, down there...... didn’t you?” Fox drops his hands from his face in amazement from who he has just seen. “Momma?” Fox asks, “I, I was just ummmm trying to find my pockets, but it was sssooo dark I guess I missed them! Tee-hee.” “I think you did more then miss Chrissy! Now what did I tell you about doing that?” The air tenses, but then softens because Mommy Fox sprays a bunch of Pledge Smell Stuff in the air, making that nice smell you really hate to breath in...... “Awwwwww! Mommy don’t spray your shit in here!” Yells Fox, soon to correct himself. “Oh, I mean don’t spray your Pledge stuff in here that I don’t like, okay?” “You’re lucky I don’t smack you for saying those things!” She says walking over to the closets door, “I’ll let you go this time Chris, but just don’t let me catch you again doing something like that.” “Yes Mommy.” Smirkes Fox, fidgeting around with his hands swinging back and forth. Mommy Fox opens the door saying one last remark, “I’ll be waiting for you in the car after your show tonight.” “Okay.” Fox replies. Boy, I gots to watch what I am doing more carefully from now on... “BIG TIME!” Fox yells as he jumps in the air. “Oops did I say that out loud? Oh well, soon everyone across the globe will hear that fa-mouse statement again.” Fox continues to skip on, as he sings a Barney tune. A good one of course. CLOSE
