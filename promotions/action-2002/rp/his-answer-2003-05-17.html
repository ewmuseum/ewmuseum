His Answer.

17 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.17.2003 Max Danger had himself a headache following the latest Pressure Point. It was throbbing from the chairshots he took at the hands, of well, himself more or less. Xander Dox also had the same headache. Then again, they were one in the same. Even several days later, he was complaining about a slight headache. 'JESSICA! MY HEAD HURTS!' he would shout at random points throughout the day. She'd always answer, 'What do you want me to do about it?' Or something as smartass-ish. Xander would just pout, folding his arms in front of him on the couch. His back was still bothering him. Wasn't something that was just going to go away over night, of course. And he had been able to keep all the information regarding its condition from his wife. Not that she didn't keep trying to trick him into talking about it. Throwing in little subtle hints from time to time. Xander would almost start, but never say anything on the matter, just wanting her to let it go. He'd tell her when he was ready. When he was certain of what the future might hold for him and his condition. 'I bet Julie doesn't complain this much about Jeff's bad back,' he told her at one point. Yeah, okay, not friends at all, were the Garvins and Doxs, but still a point Xander was trying to make. She would only scowl at him and remark, 'Then I guess she doesn't love him as much as I love you.' Two points for Jessica. But she had been keeping a secret from him as well. She hadn't told him about Conrad Ramsey wanting to face him at Juggernaut II. Oh, it was already on the website and already being promoted, but she had never told her husband about what happened after the Duncan/Ramsey match the past Monday. Then, as Xander was packing things for a flight out to New Jersey, for the upcoming Pressure Point, he casually said, 'Well, I guess it's on more go around with Conrad Monday.' She stood up straight and became slightly weird(and had stopped her humming). You can always tell when someone is holding a secret and another person mentions something about that secret(even if they know nothing about anything being a secret), because they get a bit jittery. Avoid eye contact. Just generally act strange. Xander noticed. He asked straight out, 'What's up, Jess?' She just shook her head, keeping her lips tightly shut. 'Don't give me that, honey, I know when something's up. What is it?' She shook her head again, 'Nothing is wrong. Just, you know, packing.' 'I see that. But you were humming until I mentioned Conrad's name. Why is that?' 'Song was over. Stopped humming. Jeez, quit giving me the third degree, Xand!' She zipped up her suitcase in a hurry and then stomped out of the room. Xander slowly left the room, as Jessica was darting in and out of all the rooms in the hallway. Xander smiled. 'She's so cute.' 'I don't know anything about Conrad!' she shouted. 'Um, Jess, baby, I didn't mention he name.' She stopped and looked back down the hallway at him, 'Oh.' The two stood there for a moment. Marcus leaned against the bedroom doorway and folded his arms in front of him. Jessica put her hands into the back pockets of her jeans and, with her head down, sluggishly walked back towards her husband. She stopped inches in front of him and looked up into his gray eyes. She loved it when his eyes went gray. They tended to go from blue to green to gray and various points. Gray was her favorite. Xander stared back into his wife's crystal blue eyes. He lifted an eye brow to say 'well, what is it'. She sighed and then went into it. 'I'm sorry, Xand, I meant to tell you. I wanted to tell you. But I was worried you'd get all moody.' 'I don't get moody,' he interjected. 'Oh, believe me, you get moody. Just like a woman.' Off the subject. Xander really wanted back on. None of this was helping him at all. 'Anyway,' Jessica continued, 'while you were laid out, um, well, he kinda asked for the match to be at the pay per view and not on Monday.' She gave him a worried smile. Hoping he wouldn't get all moody on her. Xander kept his cool. He was actually a bit more peeved that no one in the front office had told him about the change. Of course, Reed Young was running the offices, so that explained that. He looked up at the ceiling a moment. Letting all the anger pass through his system. Then he looked back down at his wife. 'Oh, alright.' And then he walked back into the bedroom. She sighed in relief and went to follow her husband into the room the shared, but he slammed the door, nearly right in her face. She put her hands on her hips and shook her head. 'See, moody,' she said through the door.
