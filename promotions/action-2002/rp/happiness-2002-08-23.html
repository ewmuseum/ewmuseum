Happiness

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/23/02 Harry Randall Smitch(age 29) is my son. Since being introduced to him I've come to know a lot about his life. What he's gone through and what his intentions for his own future are. Unfortunately, not all things I learn are good. His mother, Elanor, died four years after his birth and he had to live with his abusive step-father. It saddens me to realize that he could have been living with me, if Elanor had only told him about me or me about him. At the age of 16 Harry finally left home and went to Texas to live with his aunt. The step-father was not happy and went looking for Harry. He got Harry back and forced him to live with him until he was eighteen. But while he was living with his aunt, she told him about his father. Me. Told him where I lived(Colorado) and what I did(wrote for local newspaper). Once he had gained his independence on his eighteenth birthday he came looking for me. His search turned up empty, as I had left town and moved to Florida to continue my wrestling training. He knew my name, so when he stumbled upon an Action! television broadcast he saw me. He looked up Action! on the internet at a public library and found out where Action! would be holding shows. He met my arch-nemesis, Max Danger, and he brought him to 'our' home. That was the single most happiest moment of my life. Everyday just becomes better than the last. There is nothing on this planet that can bring me down off of this high.
