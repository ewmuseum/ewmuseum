Flashback : Love Geometric? Love's not geometric, it's more like a circle. [t]A circle is Geometric Fly. [f]Quiet Tony. Who's subject is this again? Yeah, that's what I thought.

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 19th, 2002 People always say that shots ring out clear throughout the world. In fact, it's been catch phrases for numerous wrestling promotions largest chair shots and violent acts of hardcore destruction. 'The (chair)shot heard round the world.' They weren't kidding. High Flyer sat in his living room recliner, a fifteen pound dumbbell in his left hand a bag of dorito's in his right. Kate Young-Harmen sat next to him on the set's arm rest, Flyer's hand around her waist and still inside the bag of chips. Flyer eyes never left the television set while Kate slowly processed their finacial numbers. 'For the fiscal...' Kate started before she was immediatly cut off. 'AH! Joey's on!' Flyer said, pointing to the television with his dumbbell. 'No talking about fiscal numbers!' 'I was going to talk about the last fiscal year.' she placed her pen in her mouth and tilted her head to the side. 'None of that either!' Flyer's still didn't turn toward Kate while saying this, his eyes locked into the Action!'s main event. Kate pouted some more before Flyer turned his attention to her. 'It's Joey! We gotta give our support! He's the freakin' Champion Kate!' There was a clang in the background. 'I know... but I just wish you paid that...' She was cut off, taking a look at the television screen, her jaw dropped and her eyes bulged. 'What is it?' Flyer said. 'You saw a mouse didn't you!?!? I told you I got rid of it!' Flyer said, before placing the dumbbell on the ground and tugging at his shirt collar. He began to sweat, and then turned to television screen to be calmed down. That's when he saw Keri Lindum with a chair, Joey Malone's bloody face sprawled on the Action! canvas and Kent Anthason locking Malone in the “Endoadklfjapa.” Well, that’s what Flyer would call it. 'Oh... My... GOD!' Suddenly, Tony Davis, High Flyer’s bestest friend in the whole wide world, walked into the room carrying a soda. He didn't notice the shocked faces of the friends around him, and he didn't notice the television which was turned to full blast. 'You're out of soda Fly, better go get some.' Davis took a look at the shocked couple before him. 'Dude, what's wrong with you?' Davis turned to where they were looking at, and saw Keri Lindum, the chair still in her hand and Joey Malone down. His head tilted in confusion. 'Why's she got a chair? And why is Kent Anthason STILL up if she has that chair?!?' Davis asked Flyer, who simply pointed towards the television screen. 'GET... THERE... FASTER!' Davis tilted his head to the side, and began to mumble to himself. He went through the steps. Keri Lindum has a chair. Kent Anthason is up, but Joey Malone is down, his head bleeding as if he's been hit by a blunt object... That's when Davis' eyes went wide and he started pointing like mad towards Flyer and the television set. 'OH MY GOD!' 'I KNOW!' And that's when the bell was rung, and Keri and Kent locked lips. 'OH MY GOD!' Davis shouted. 'OH MY GOD!' Davis turned to Flyer sharply. 'What, do you have a different God than me?'
