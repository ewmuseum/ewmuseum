Early Morning Mayhem

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.14.02 Tuesday morning. Outside. Summer dew was dripping from the plant outside of the hotel room. It was 4:34am, the sun was barely brewing on the surface of a fierce and violent morning. The clouds looked like a whirlwind had blasted through a cake and a tampon and pissed out a vile combination of yellow fluff and pink squish that was romantic in a strange sense of overkill. Inside. The 'fli-CLICK!' of the lighter screamed across the soundplane. The flame flickered in the shadows, under something. A can. Vanilla frosting. Minio sat frusterated in the dark, trying to warm the can of sugar enhancement. His brain ached for some- thing sweet. The Hotelroom lacked a stove and a microwave, Minio was roughing it. 'I feel like a fucking cracko.' He licked the icing from the can, and gave a disgusted contortion of his face. He brought up a slight film that grew on this cake's parchment when the can was left out for a while. He HATED that film. He frowned then dipped a finger in, ripping it from his fleshy diget then slammed the can on the night table in a fit of aggravation. He stood from the bed, then walked to the sliding glass door overlooking the pool side of the mariot. 'Its gonna be an interesting morning.' He wasn't preparing for his match with KroW. He wasn't trying to compensate for whatever was lost in his concussions. Minio had a secret that he didn't let on to, a secret that not even the master of mind games, KroW, had known. 'Every scar is an artificial insemination of HATE.' His face landed softly against the glass. 'Every loss makes me a little bit stronger. Every Set back is a gratifying acceptance that just makes me push harder. Every time i'm hurt my drive gets alittle faster, alittle bit more determined. Torturing me is the best way to help me win. Injuries teach me to fight with one less adaption of myself. You're SUCH a fool KroW.' He spun quickly, pivoting in place, his fist slamming backwards against the glass. His face was in a smile. 'You think that because you worship satan, you're Different then People?' He smiles and walks towards the dresser, pulling the bible from the top drawer. 'I'm much more dangerous then any fan of the Devil. Because you know who I follow KroW? No one. I'm agnostic. I take it as it comes along. I rely on myself. And only myself. I dont expect anyone else to give me power, to save me in a time of need, to make the pain become nourishment for my hunger. All I know KroW, is that every time i'm knocked down, I have to get back up. . . Because atleast on my feet I can fight back, i'd take the same beating on the ground accept I can't fight from a face down position.' He opens the bible, then tears it down the center, flinging both sides in opposite directions. 'Its called a will to live, KroW. No doctor can tell me I will die. I know that I will NEVER die. I am pretty confident that I won't ever hear a 'told you so Bobby!'. I won't accept death. I won't accept giving in. Every time you've beaten me down, its just be another reason to beat you in the ring. Fair. To show you that you are nothing without a knife KroW. Nothing without the drop. You're a coward and you can't face a man straight on. And thats why I'm the real warrior KroW. I'll accept a fight straight on. I won't hide. You're gurilla warfare doesn't make you a warrior, it makes you a coward without any full facial skills in combat.' He walks back towards the glass, opening the door. 'And thats why I will win.' He climbs up onto the railing. Teetering awfully close to falling. His balance was a fail safe that not even a physic would trust. He grinned as he looked at the fifth floor overview. 'I'll just, wait until you're infront of me. . . and let my instinct go. Krow. . . you're fucked.' He fell forward, the camera rushed to look over the railing. Minio's body was a lawn dart in a dive position. He peirced the pool water like a junkie's needle hitting the fix. He swam to the opposite side of the pool. One early morning riser, still tipsy from the club scene the night before gasped over his bloody mary as Minio hit and swam. Minio crawled onto the deck, ran a few steps, slopping in his soaked clothing, then hopped and shimmied over the wall, into the parking lot. Disappearing into the ocean of plastic and steel. Vehicles. Out of sight. Gone.
