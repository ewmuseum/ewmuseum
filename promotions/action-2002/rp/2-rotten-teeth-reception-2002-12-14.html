2 Rotten Teeth (Reception)

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/14/02 OPEN Click, creeeek.... The door opened, and Christopher Fox entered with.... MOMMY! Title: 2 Rotten Teeth (Reception) Date: 12/14/02 10:00 am EST Place: Dentist Office Mommy “Heto.” Mommy saluted the secretary as Fox muttered to himself. They both sat down. Secretary “Dr. Blufinland will be with you in a second.” The lady secretary went back to her filing. The scene was nice and clean for a change; the dental office had some calm music in the background as Fox and Mommy talked back and forth. Fox “Now why am I here again Mommy?” Mommy “Don’t you remember? Before I went on my trip your Dentist told me you had two cavities. I said to operate on them when I’m back with my son. And here I am.” Fox “But you’re not following me to the ring anymore, right?” Mommy “I already said ‘right’ but if you don’t take a bath with Pikachu every week then I’ll be escorting you around. You’re older now son, I expect more than this from now on.” Shut up, shut up, lalalalalala, shut up. Mommy “I don’t want to have to bring you here anymore. In fact, the only times I’ll expect to see you again is Christmas or any other holiday.” Fox adjusted himself and grabbed a Winnie the Pooh magazine, entertaining himself as Mommy just huffed. A few moments passed until Dr. Blufinland came from the back. Blufinland “Ok Christopher, we’re ready.” Fox “Bye Mommy.” Mommy “Yeah, this better not happen again, you hear me? You don’t brush....” The rest was just a mutter as Mommy was relentless with her verbal assault that only Fox heard. She was still going too as Fox sat down the a dental chair in a totally separate room! Luckily, Pikachu was getting a massage next door.... or he would’ve been scarred for life! Three chairs, the middle one was empty, as Fox was on the far left and a little girl and her Mommy on the far right. Fox tried to be friendly. Fox “Heto there.” The Mommy stood in front of her child screening any communication. Blufinland came in. Blufinland “Ok Chris, I’ll be with you in a sec. In the meantime my assistant, Jill, will prepare you.” Jill came over as Blufinland walked towards the little girl. Jill “I’m just going to put this on you and then you’ll have to wait hun.” Jill placed a bib on Fox. No, he wasn’t going to be eating any time soon. Fox “LOL?” Fox’s comment was in vein as Jill went over and helped Blufinland. The curious ‘Kid’ looked across the way and watched the little girl, white knuckles and all, take her retainer out. Jeez that little girl is weak and her Mommy is kinda annoying asking all those questions. Hey, she must be having here quarter just like my Mommy, SMP season! Chris talked softly to himself. Fox “I wonder what teeth are rotten? I bet it’s these!” Fox pulled on his two front teeth, obviously it wasn’t them. At least five minutes passed as the little girl ripped away at her retainer and Fox watched. Finally and I mean FINALLY the little girl, who was crying by now, popped here retainer out all by herself. Blufinland “Ok looks good, meet back with me in a couple of months.” Girl’s Mother “Oh thank you Dr. Blufinland what would we do without you?” What are you smoking woman? Dr. Blufinland didn’t do jack! Blufinland “That’s why they pay me. Ok see ya.” The family left and all eyes were on Fox’s two rotten teeth. Would Fox have a mouth full of work? Or just a quick examination? Find out next time! CLOSE
