Unforseen Consequences

12 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: [Nov. 12. 2002] The Blueprint 2 Baby! The wave of cheers blew up in the arena, as Michael Hunt entered the ring. The fans all knew what the next contest would be, and couldn't stand the anticipation. Michael Hunt smiled at the millions in attendance and slowly brought the microphone to his mouth. The moment he did so, the fans stood on their feet, awaiting the announcement that they were waiting for the entire night. The entire night was filled with action, seeing many titles on the line and many surprises in the end, but everyone knew...they would all pale in comparison to the contest about to be introduced. Michael Hunt stood firm in the center of the ring and took a deep breathe, as his hand motioned to the crowd in proud... Sweet Aroma sat backstage in Jonathan Jerome Leary's locker room. She was watching this on the monitor provided. Sylo was the first one out to the ring, and KroW soon followed. The two then engaged in a war that was a month or more in the making. There was no sign of Jonathan. Veronica didn't even know where he was. KroW got to his feet and looked out at the crowd, who were on their feet cheering like wild for the Satanic God. KroW let out a sadistic grin as he looked down at Sylo, and realized that the big man was getting to his feet. KroW quickly grabbed hold of Sylo's head and body and rolled the big man into the ring. KroW was just about to roll in afterwards, when suddenly a theme song blasted across the arena. It was a very familiar theme song to the thousands in attendance, and especially familiar to KroW. KroW immediately stopped dead in his tracks as he heard 'Guilty Until Proven Innocent' blasting across the arena. KroW slowly turned around and noticed the World Champion sprinting his way down the ramp. KroW grunted and quickly began to sprint up after him. The fans were on their feet, as the two meet in the center of the ramp. J-Con looked for a clothesline, but KroW quickly ducked in and reversed into a neckbreaker! J-Con smacked the steel hard! KroW immediately pulled J-Con right back to his feet and pulled the World Champion off his feet and held J-Con on his shoulder. J-Con looked in horror, as KroW charged the ringside area and smashed J-Con head first into the turnbuckle pole! Veronica felt that, and had to cover her eyes at the sight of her man getting his face rammed into the turnbuckle pole. DING DING DING! Finally the bell rung, and now Jonathan can become the former PIW World Champion, or he can retain the title now. It was all on the three men in the ring. Sylo. KroW. JCON. The match would continue on its 'merry' way, as Aroma just watched on nervously. The chants were deafening, as KroW slowly inched over onto his stomach and began to crawl towards the fallen J-Con. J-Con was down and out, his eyes closed, and his body remaining still. KroW slowly inched over, and before anyone knew it, he had placed a single arm over the fallen body of Conspiracy. The official dropped to the mat, as he slapped his hand down... 'ONE!', roared the fans. 'KICK OUT BABY!' shouted Veronica to the monitor. 'TWO!', counted the fans along with the official... 'COME ON BABY, YOU HAVE TO KICK OUT!' repeated Veronica, as she closed her eyes. 'THREE!', roared the fans... 'NO!!!!!!' Brown - 'THAT SON OF A BITCH! He did it again! Just like he did at Evil Intentions, that big blue son of a bitch just pulled the official out of the ring and screwed KroW out of the World Championship for a SECOND FUCKING TIME!' Nerdstrom - 'HAHAHAHA! All's fair in love and war Mike!' After hearing what the commentators of the event said, Aroma opened her eyes, and saw that the match was still going on. The fans were deafening with boos, throwing numerous items at the bloodied Sylo, as Sylo just looked at the fallen official and smiled in accomplishment. But little did Sylo know, with the official down and out...anything goes. And he soon found that out...as suddenly a sickening SMACK! was heard around the arena. Sylo suddenly felt a sharp pain in his back, and heard the fans cheer wildly...he felt himself fall to his knees in utter pain...and soon it became clear. For KroW quickly stepped in front of him, steel chair in his grasp. KroW looked down in disgust and spat right in the face of Sylo... 'YOU MOTHER FUCKER!', roared out KroW as he reared back the steel chair... WHACK! With a sickening thud, the chair connected directly over the skull of Sylo...flooring the big man with ease...and forcing Sylo to bust wide open...reopening some scars on his forehead...just like what had happened to KroW. It was a bloody mess outside the ring, as KroW stood over the fallen Sylo, bleeding wildly. Blood was dripping onto the concrete, and a pool began to form around Sylo's head. KroW just looked down in disgust and threw the chair down, and rolled back into the ring...but just as he did... WHACK! Another steel chair was brought into play, and smacked KroW across the skull. The Satanic God fell viciously to the mat, as J-Con stood over the Mind Manipulator with a steel chair firmly in his grasp. J-Con was in a great deal of pain, his legs felt like jello, and he strength seemed down to a minimum, but it didn't stop him from lifting the chair once more and cracking the steel over KroW's face! Suddenly, a trail of blood surfaced on the chair, as KroW's face became a bloody mess. The stitches were opened, and KroW was wearing nothing on his face...but an all natural crimson mask. J-Con grunted in an insane manner, as he slowly placed the chair across KroW's face, and gingerly pointed to the top of the turnbuckle... 'YES! Do it baby, do it!' shouted an excited Sweetz. J-Con slowly walked his way to the turnbuckle, stummbling as he did so. J-Con was slowly reaching the top rope, pulling himself up with all the power he had left in his body. KroW remained out, bleeding freely in the ring and on the chair that lay on top of his face. On the outside, Sylo remained out cold...but the official was getting to his feet. J-Con took one last look at KroW, making sure he wasn't going to move...before using the last bit of his energy to soar off the top rope. It was almost like time had slowed down, as the fans watched J-Con slowly extend his leg...continuing to look down at the Bloody KroW, steel chair still covering his face. And just as time returned...he landed the Conspiracy Theory right onto the steel chair!!! ONE!, the officials mat slapped the ground, as the fans grew frantic...cheering for the Satanic God. 'COME ON!' TWO!, it was nearly over...one more second at the match would be over. The fans were going wild, as were the announcers at ringside... 'ONE MORE! ONE MORE!' But KroW would kick out, and the action would progress, until this moment. Jim Johnson had just screwed KroW out of the title, pulling a Shane McMahon. And now Johnson was faced with paying for the crime. And in a matter of moments, Sylo had elbowed KroW in the back of the head. Johnson fell to his feet and quickly rolled out of the ring, as KroW staggered into the ropes. KroW slowly turned around to face his assailant, but the moment he did, he was hit with one of the biggest spears seen in the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling. The impact was so greatly, that KroW landed right on the back of his neck! It was the spear Sylo called 'The Sylent Slaughter'! The fans blew up in jeers, as KroW was floored once again. Sylo stood up with strength encompassing his entire body...or so the people thought. For the moment Sylo got to his feet, the blood loss began to get to him...and the blue monster soon fell... On top of KroW. The official slowly gained consciousness and crawled over to the scene... ONE!, the official slapped his palm to the mat. Jim Johnson looked on in horror...this wasn't part of the plan... Aroma nervously kept her eyes glued to the monitor. TWO!, Johnson frantically called Sylo to get off of KroW! Johnson screamed at the top of his lungs...but the blood loss rendered Sylo unconscious. And with that in mind...Sylo remained on top of KroW... Aroma was shouting for Jonathan do to something. WHACK! I think he heard her. Johnson eyes were wide, and his jaw dropped to the concrete. For J-Con was back to his feet and literally obliterated Sylo with a vicious chair shot right into the Sylencer's ribs! Sylo screamed out in agony as he rolled off of the fallen body of KroW and began holding his ribs in utter torture. J-Con lifted the chair up again, and... WHACK! Jonathan seemed to have lost it. J-Con: 'You son of a bitch! I knew it! You want my fucking title! I KNEW IT! I knew I couldn't trust a big mother fucker like you!' 'You tell him baby!' Aroma cracked a smile, as she saw her man taking control of the situation. J-Con - 'STILL WANT MY TITLE YOU SON OF A BITCH?!?!' But suddenly, J-Con was cut off, by the big hand of Sylo! Sylo, with all the strength left in him, grabbed J-Con across the throat. J-Con kicked and gasped for breathe, but Sylo began squeeze the life out of the World Champion! Sylo suddenly began to get to his feet, holding his ribs with one hand, and holding the Champion in his other. Johnson began to go wild on the outside and warned Sylo not to do it! Sylo didn't hear Johnson and looked to lift J-Con off his feet, but just as he was about to do so...J-Con punched Sylo square in the ribs. Sweetz just loved the way Jonathan took advantage of others' weaknesses inside the ring. Sylo cowered in pain and released J-Con from his chokehold. J-Con held his neck and took a deep breathe of air, as Sylo staggered to the ropes. J-Con had enough of this screwjob, and looked to connect with a sidekick, but suddenly, something stopped him...he was twirled right around, and when he was turned around...he was looking right in the eyes of Jim Johnson. Johnson stared at J-Con and began yelling at the World Champion to stop the assault. J-Con protested that Sylo was looking to screw him over...but Johnson protested that accusation and said it was a complete mistake. It was an on going argument in the center of the ring...the ring filled with weapons, blood, and mayhem. Jonathan didn't plan on stopping, and neither did Sylo or KroW for that matter. The three continued to wage this unholy war. KroW stood firm, a face covered in blood, staring down at the fallen Sylo with insanity in his widened and red eyes. But suddenly, the Red Vision had died down, as J-Con snuck up behind KroW and immediately clocked KroW with the Co-Conspirator, the falling reverse DDT! And directly after the impact, KroW was KOed...or so we all thought. But KroW was quick to get to his feet and floor J-Con with a hard right hand! The fans blew up, as J-Con charged again, only to be floored by a body slam...followed by an roaring elbow drop. Suddenly, Jim Johnson had enough and sprinted over to the time keeper's table. J-Con crawled away from KroW, as Johnson quickly handed J-Con his World Title... KroW stomped over to J-Con... THWACK! And was floored by the World Title shot straight into his skull! The official was still recuperating after the pain he had suffered, and didn't see the Shot to the skull. The blood of KroW's was stained up the championship gold, as KroW remained on his back in the center of the ring. Johnson smiled ear to ear, as the scene upon him was nearly perfect. Although Sylo was floored, it was a compromise for the destruction of KroW. KroW was knocked out, and standing in the ring was the World Champion...Jonathan Conspiracy. J-Con tossed his title to the ground, and kicked a few chairs out of the ring...it was all set...exactly how it was planned. J-Con was the sole survivor, and immediately...J-Con hit the mat and covered KroW. Johnson rushed over to the official and woke him back up, and ordered the pinfall... ONE!, the officials hand met the mat... 'My baby's going to do it!' TWO!, again the officials hand hit the mat... One count away from victory. One count away from escaping defeat. But it never came. After all the screwjobs KroW had endured, there was just one for Jonathan Conspiracy! The official was yanked out of the ring, and standing at the ringside area, was the man that did it. His head was shaven cleanly bald, and his long brown goatee hung over his face. A look of disgust was over his face, as he stared right at J-Con and yelled out those symbolic words... 'NOW YOU'RE FUCKED!' Rune Winters. He would nail 'NOW YOU'RE FUCKED!' and Jonathan was on his back in the ring, meanwhile all hell continued to break loose. KroW slowly pulled Sylo to his feet, and locked the front face lock on. The fans screamed for KroW to turn around, as KroW looked to plant Sylo with 'The End', just as he did to Jonathan Conspiracy. Jim Johnson roared like a tiger and charged with the steel chair in his grasp. KroW suddenly heard the roar and dodged to the left, and within a split second... WHACK! Johnson had cracked Sylo right over the skull! KroW then took out Johnson. Sylo had finally fell from the chair shot... Landing on Jonathan Conspiracy. Sweet Aroma shouted in unison with the crowd, 'NO!' but for different reason. She didn't want Jonathan to lose the title, the fans didn't want the title to stay in Team Johnson. ONE! TWO! THREE! DING DING DING! From the looks of it, Jonathan was a former PIW World Champion. Mike Hunt - 'The winner of this contest...and NEW PIW WORLD CHAMPION...SYLO!!!!!!!' That announcement made it official. And now it would be Veronica Aromadeus' job, to calm down JCON. How would she do that? She didn't even know.
