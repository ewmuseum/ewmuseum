Derelict Ruminations: life's little joys

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/24/2002 i'm nothing but a farce. a fucking FARCE. i won't tell them why I'm doing this. no. i'm a farce. why’s are we saying that? never you mind i'll lie to them. lie and tell them something else. something to feed my 'don't give a fuck about nothin' bravodo, won't i. won't i! i don't care huh? i'm the indiffent huh? i'm above everyone else with their worries and concerns, right? i'm fucking apathy incarnate aren't i. NEWSFLASH FUCKFACE: if i really was i wouldn't be reminding everyone that i don't give a fuck every 5 seconds. people who REALLY don't give a fuck, don't give a fuck if no one else knows they don't give a fuck. stop it, shitface. you’re making me sad. that’s no big feat. i'm too insecure. i'm afraid to make people like me. so i beat them to the punch. i tell them not to like me. i feel powerful in that capacity. i'm screaming. SCREAMING. validate me. i'm weak. i suck stop it. stop it!! please!! cease!! stoooooop!! rape me i killed--*THWAK* With a pathetic moan he collapsed unconscious. His mouth, a mangled mess of shredded lips, and jarred teeth. A beautiful sight, is it not? At times, you have to stand back and enjoy the little joys this pointless existence provides us. Such as, receiving a grateful smile from a homeless man, after you drop a few coins into his half empty cup. And the joy it brings watching that smile contort into a scowl of agony as you send your knee smashing into the poor man’s faces face. This is what makes life worth living. ...for me anyways. I don’t need to, but I think I’ll take his pathetic little cup. Sure wrestling, winning titles and shit makes me rich, but I could always use the extra change. You think assaulting and robbing a bum is deplorable don’t you. I think I’m doing the guy a service. I mean, it’s bad enough I knocked this poor fuck’s teeth through his nose. What if he were to wake up, and find that he still had all his change. How bad do you think he would feel knowing some asshole mashed in his face just for the simple joy of the act. So, out of common courtesy, I robbed him. At least now he’ll think I had a reason. And I was glad to do it. Cuz that’s just the kind of guy I am. I don’t mean to toot my own horn, but I really am a dynamite individual. Funny thing happened this mourning. I woke up, in my plush hotel room, with my expensive nightclothes strewn around the room, a gorgeous Latin bitch laying on the bed next to me, all these ornate decorations and items all around me, and I felt sick to my stomach. I immediately hated it. I despised it. I loathed every shred of dollar I owned. It paid for all of this. Wrestling, and wrestling well, got me all of this extravagance. And I fucking HATED it. If I have to live, it’s time I lived without all this. It’s time I survived rather than lived. It’s time I experienced life in all its glorious misery. I don’t deserve opulence. I will no longer indulge myself in material goods. Never mind the fact you just witnessed me robbing a destitute beggar, I don’t need money. All that I need is neatly concealed in this ebony organ of dead cells. So, after playing a 5 minute game of “in-and-out” on whatshername’s chocha, I trashed the hotel room, and here I am. A true derelict. Ain’t life grand?
