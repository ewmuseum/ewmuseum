Confidence(Or Lack Thereof): Number Two

21 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/21/02 Conrad Ramsey hadn't been at his Leitchfield, Kentucky home at all since last Monday's Pressure Point. He had flown directly from that location to Augusta, Maine. What was there? Surely Action! wasn't going to Maine, were they? Of course not. Reed Young probably doesn't even know Maine is a state. Ramsey had other business there. Not Action! business. Not wrestling business at all, as a matter of fact. It was personal. *heart* Samantha Dox. *heart* He had first met her at Thanksgiving. They had kept in touch ever since, but not a single word was spoken to Marcus Alexander Dox. You might know him better as Max Danger. Samantha was his mother(for those who don't know, and if you didn't, SHAME ON YOU!). She was a widow(for several years), and had had many suitors before, but none quite like Conrad Ramsey. He stayed at Samantha's. They just talked. Too long walks. Just enjoyed each other's company. Sure, they're slightly elderly. So what? Old people deserve love, too. It wasn't just for the young. But something still crept in the back of Conrad's mind. 'You're not worthy...' 'You're too old...' 'You don't deserve it...' All things echoed in the furthest reaches of his mind. Did he deserve the Bantam Championship? He did win it. Sure, some slight help from LLB, but nothing that really impacted the match in a major way. He still would have gotten the Achilles Tendon Hold. He would have still gotten the tap out victory. But was it too good to be true? Was his number almost up? He needed the help of Airborn Tyler to retain the silver against The Ringer. Would he need help every time out from now on? He wasn't sure. It continued to swim in his head. He just hoped it would drown him. 'It's your's for a reason.' whispered the sweet and calming voice of Samantha. Was it?
