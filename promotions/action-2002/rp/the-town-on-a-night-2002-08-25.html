Action! — Tales

The Town on a Night.

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/25/02 Ratings. Money. Fame. Always the fame. Reed Young and Jeremy Bold were on top of the world tonight. They had just come off their brilliantly produced Pay-Per-View ‘Down 4 the Count’, and despite the Fans not going home happy in the end, the actual hype they produced from the show... the skills of the wrestlers... the overall quality of the product, it had never been better. And all thanks to one man. Reed You- Rather, one thing. Reed Young’s massive pocketbook. But the Owner didn’t really know that. He thought it was him and Bold. They did it all, dammit! They did the giant leaps off of the stage. They did the wild TLC Match... they did the Card Scramble Bout... they took off each other’s ears... Hell, they even booked the Title-“4”-Title Match. Everything was going SO well. fWo who!? They had fucking no-namers in their house-shows!! No one gave a fucking hell about them. the Asylum!? Can anyone say... SLUMP!?!? 21w!? Rrriiight. PIW? Okay, okay... that Chris Mezz is a show-stopper. Snicker. Snicker. Snicker again. Wait, another snicker. Snicker. Snicker. Reed just laughed like an idiot. Regardless of how great those Fed’s were... to him, they were fucking shit. Action!... was the bomb. Young was number one. Those Fed’s were falling behind. So it was time to celebrate... BITCH!! It was time for the town on a nig- the night on a tow- town on nigh- night on the nigh- whatever. ZZZZZZZZOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMMMMMMMM. The beaten down, rusted-covered wagon paced itself wildly throughout the lonely streets of a town in Texas tonight. “HARDER BOLD... HARDER!!” Reed Young commanded, riding SHOTGUN!! He was, well, totally hyper. But we already knew that. “C’MON BOLD... PRESS THAT GAS BITCH!!” ZZZZZZZZOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMMMMMMMM. That wagon could totally fly when it wanted to. The rust spilled off it, onto the dirt roads below as Jeremy squeaked it hard while turning a very sharp corner, going at least 80 as he did. The wagon almost tipped over. “OOOHHH... WHAT A RUSH!!” LOD, folks, was in the house tonight. Bold and Young just jammed to “Can’t Get You Out of My Head” as they continued to roam the streets of this little town. “La la la la, la la la la la, LA LA LA LA... LA LA LA LA LLLAAA!! I just can’t get you out of my head...” Young sung to the music as the two of them just smashed their heads around, ala Dr. Dre and Eminem in the music video of “Without Me”. “Boy that Eminem can sing when he wants to, eh Boss?” Jeremy said, taking his eyes off the road as he looked at Young and his terribly ratting light-brown hair rolling around in the nighttime wind. Bold almost hit a pedestrian as he jetted the wagon through a red light. “Yeah.” Young agreed. Oh yeah, they honestly thought this song was done by Eminem. “He really has talent with that voice. Man... can he change its tone whenever he wants!” The Owner reached over, and turned the song up even louder. ZZZZZZZZOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMMMMMMMM. Bold rounded a corner, coming upon a teenage couple walking the streets at 1 in the morning. “WHOO!” Thus... Reed Young emerged from his seat just a little bit, bending down and picking up... Eggs. He stuck his head out the window, beginning to hurl them at the couple as they drove past. “WHOO!! I’M EGGING YOU BITCHES!! MUHAHAHAHAHA!!” Whack. He caught the male straight under the chin. “Fuckka! I ‘ill you!” This dubbed ‘hommie’ yelled at the wagon as Reed still hung his head out the window and looked back with a smile on his face, screaming. “What ya gonna do bitch!? I’m the best Owner...” Oh God... That cued up Bold to speak simultaneously with Reed. “EEEEVVVVEEEERRRR!!” Kill me now. Please. “Whoa... whoa... ANOTHER person walking down the street at night!” For this time, Young didn’t throw the eggs. He just looked at the overweight man, approximately three hundred or so pounds, and whooped. “MCDONALD’S IS THAT WAY... ... UM... ASSHOLE!!” Jeremy almost crashed the car, breaking down in tears. “Good one Boss!! W00T!!” They rounded another corner, clearly getting into the deep parts of this town’s core. But it really wasn’t that big. More deserted than anything else. Another man, walking the road at the late time of night. Young pulled out an egg. Smack. Right in the face. “HAHAHAHAHA HAHAHAHAHA HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA HAHAHAHAHA HAHAHAHAHAHA HAHAHAHAHAH AHAHAHAHAHA HAHAHAHAHAHAHA HAHAHAHAHA HAHAHAAAAHAHAH AHSAHDWJKGF WREFWOF ERJGLH GH;LSLNSFTJ HL JE;HJRT OHJOPIREIYTPOEJHO YTREUJOHILJREG9IAUE OGHNGJKBBM DBFD JBB !!!!!” Oh Jesus... if you were Reed Young at the moment, this would be the time of your life. Though really, please don’t try this at home. ZZZZZZZZOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMMMMMMMM. Another corner taken at a very fast speed. “Can’t Get You Out of My Head” cued up again. It was on repeat play. Houses. And lost of them. The zoom alone would wake up some. It was the bitter and bad end of town now. The disgustingly kept houses alone proved that fact. Kids. Two of them. Playing outside. On the driveway. Young stuck his head out the window again... and cued out his eggs. Whack. Bulls-eye on the three year old boy. Smack. Bulls-eye on the five year old girl. “TAKE THAT YOU CRACK INFESTED CUNTS!!” Inside the car, Jeremy Bold broke apart... in half. These, were the two Owners of a Federation that people said were going somewhere? These idiots? “LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! LOL! DILWOFREOHG 53UGYT 93 G9PERGJE GLJDLH JBRLITJG LFDMFDS;V SFG;LDFJ GLDGJL EL O; !!” ZZZZZZZZOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMMMMMMMM. The better end of town now. The houses were somewhat decent. No one walked the streets down here. No one was outside. No kids. No nothing. They all slept. Then Reed Young spotted something. “HALT THE CAR HOMMIE!!” SSSQQQUUUEEEAAAKKK. The tires almost burnt off. And Reed Young jumped out of the driver seat. He looked directly at an object in a driveway. A truck. But the bumper sticker told the story. “the Asylum: Following one year of pain, agony and death with another.” “An Asylum fan!?!?” Young & Bold: “WTFMF!?!?” It didn’t even have to be said. Jeremy Bold exited from the car, and opened up a whole new case of eggs. Young took a handful, and began to cover the black truck in yoke. “More... more... MORE!!” With that, twelve eggs dripped all over it. Bold went back to the “ratings mobile” and handed Young another carton. This time, though, the Owner hurled them at the house. “LOL!! That’ll teach you to mess with me!” Young said, before suddenly... the window to the top bedroom opened up. “WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU DOING!?!?” The man shouted, as he exited the room and sounds of him racing down the stairs to get to the door were heard. A scared Bold pissed his paints, literally, as he jumped back into the wagon. But Young... Young just kept on egging the damn house. He never learned. BANG. The door opened up... and a giant, well-built man, mid twenties, stood... breathing heavy. “You BETTER have a good excuse!” Reed smiled. “What you gonna do...” He looked at the tA logo again. “Rape me?” The man just grunted. He rushed at Young. The Owner didn’t even have time to react. SMACK. Right hand to the face. Young toppled back, crashing into the civic just as another shot met his face. SMACK. Blood began to draw. “I won’t rape you.” The man said. “Just kill you.” SMACK. SMACK. SMACK. Bold panicked. And not knowing what to do, he nervously cried out for help. “SUPERMAN... WHERE ARE YOU SUPERMAN!?!?” SMACK. SMACK. “OKAY... OKAY... BATMAN!! I’LL EVEN USE YOU RIGHT NOW!!” Reed was laid out against the back of the truck, as the man pulled back one last time, taking a look at his face, absolutely drenched in blood. “Asshole.” SSSQQQUUUEEEAAAKKK. WHACK! The man flew forward, dead into his own truck as Young was barely able to sidestep across. Jeremy Bold had rammed the “ratings mobile” right into the guy. “Holy shit.” Was all he could say. For he didn’t even know he had that in him. Getting out of the wagon, Bold picked up Young and propped him back into the rusted-wonder. But before getting in himself, Jeremy looked back at the truck, and the tA sticker. “Pft.” He said very cocky. ZZZZZZZZOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOMMMMMMMM. The wagon took off down the street, only to round another corner at a frantic speed, and leaving a loud ssssssuuuuuurrrrrr of the tires behind in the nighttime air. The scene that was left... A man’s wonderful house, and black truck, covered in egg... as he lay unconscious at the floor of his driveway.
