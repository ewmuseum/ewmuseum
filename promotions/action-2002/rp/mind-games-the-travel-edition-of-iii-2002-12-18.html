Mind Games, The Travel Edition of; III

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/18/02 Pressure Point had ended. It was another excellent showing for Action! and the fans had been very pleased. Max Danger, though, was a bit mixed. Sure, he was happy, but also sad. Why? It's Max Danger, he's always running the gamut of emotions. Actually, it was because of two things. First off, he had lost his chance at Conrad Ramsey and the Bantam Championship(which he KNEW he would have won, he's always had the old man's number). Then, he got a second match with Kayden Paulton. The outcome? Another loss. Two in one night. Max wasn't exactly what you'd call 'on a roll'. He was the complete opposite of that. That's why he went to Reed Young. He got himself another match with the Dothan, Alabama native. The man who he had gotten kicked out of the local grocery store. But the announcement by Young had lifted the spirits of 'The Danger Man'. That's why he was standing outside the arena following the show. He was waiting to tell Paulton the good news. At Players & Pawns(on December 31st) they would face off again. He waited and waited. It wasn't the warmest of nights. Danger had a blue and gray scarf around his neck, and blue and gray tobbagon on his head(also covering his ears), a black, leather jacket(with a gray shirt underneath, possibly Old Navy), black gloves, khaki pants, and brown dress shoes. His breath formed in the cold air in front of him. He was thinking that maybe he should have just pointed his car in the direction of the door, and waiting for Kayden there. He certainly could use some warmth. But he stayed. Waiting. It was taking so long for ANYONE to come out, that he was hoping that Homeless Gus would make an appearance. But he probably had some previous engagemnts. Danger bounced back and forth on the toes and heels of his feet, waiting, anxiously, impatiently, for anyone, ANYONE to come out. Finally the doors swung open. Danger smiled so large you would think he were wearing fake teeth. He stepped over, without looking at who it was and began to speak, 'You think you're clever, don't you? Think you're SOOOOO good. Well, haha, I've got you. I've got your number now. You and I are going to be-' Then he looked. It wasn't Kayden Paulton at all. But rather Sweet Aroma. She just looked at 'The Danger Man' the same as she always did, like he was a frickin' retard. Danger quickly stammered to cover up, 'S-Sorry, thought y-you were s-someone else.' Aroma's reply was quick and full of sarcasm, 'Really? Never would have guessed.' She just rolled her eyes and shook her head. She was about to walk off, so she could leave, but Danger stopped her by grabbing her shoulder. She turned back towards Max and had the expression that said 'what?'. 'I saw what happened.' She just shook her head and shrugged her shoulders wondering what Max was going on about. 'I your match with Angelica. I saw what happened. That no good scum, Bobby Minio.' He cursed under his breath at Minio, something about losing the Dyad Titles for him or something. Then he looked back up at Aroma. She spoke first though. 'It's nothing I can't handle, Max.' It was as though she knew he would ask to help her. How could she? Danger never wanted to help anyone. Wasn't in his nature, especially if there was nothing to gain from it for himself. But right on cue, 'But I can help you, Veronica.' Wow! Didn't see that one coming. Danger continued, 'I can help you out. I have some unfinished business to settle with Bobby, anyone. Besides, I never liked that Dawson girl much anyhow. Always parading around like her shit didn't stink. Her big, voluptous ta-tas. I mean, who does she think she is? You've always been the one who carried that team! I mean... look at you!' Aroma wasn't sure exaclty HOW to answer that. She just muttered 'Thanks'. Danger wasn't through though, 'I brought her into the gfwa OMEGA. Did you know that?' Aroma shook her head. 'I did! And then she has to go and follow me here. What's up with that? Between you and me,' he leaned in closer and whispered to Sweety. 'I think she has a crush on me.' He uprighted, shaking his head up and down, as though everyone woman should really have a crush on him. 'Get out!? Seriously?' said Aroma, looking the least bit geniune in her shock. Danger just continued shaking his head, 'Yeah, I never mentioned it to Bobby though. Didn't want ot disrupt the team-unity, you know? Figured it would lose us the belts. But,' he looked down and shook his head back and forth, 'Guess that didn't work out too well, eh? He lost them for us. BASTARD!' He began to shake his fist into the air, like Minio was up there. Then he turned back to Aroma. 'You could use my help. I'm ready to go!' he clapped his hands together and began to hop around, throwing punches into the air. 'Just let me help! Come on! I can be of service.' Aroma was fighting back the laughter. She then reached out and grabbed Danger's hands to make him stop. 'I'm flattered, Xander, I really am.' she smiled slightly, as though laughter was going to come bursting out at any moment. 'But I can handle it. But... thanks.' She gave Max a small smile and then turned. She was nearly out of view when Max shouted, 'If you need me, you've got my number!' He then turned around and was punching the air again. Thinking it was Bobby Minio. He was headed away from the back door, having forgotten all about Kayden Paulton and the gloating he was going to do. But the moment he touched the door of his rented SUV(Ford Exploder, this time), he remembered. 'NOOOOOO!' he screamed. He had turned and was looking wildly all around the parking lot. Then he saw him. Kayden Paulton was getting into his rental car. Danger broke out in a sprint towards him. Paulton had pulled out of his space and was slowly moving along the lot. Then he stopped. He was searching the floor for something. That gave Max time. He leapt out from behind more cars, and onto the hood of Kayden's car. With a shocked look, Paulton looked up and saw Danger staring at him through the glass. Paulton rolled down his window and poked his head out. 'What are you doing? Are you hurt?' He then opened up the door and stepped out. 'Do you need a ride? Tell me, Max. What can I do, I'm here for you.' Danger rolled off the hood, holding his stomach. Jumping onto the car hadn't been the most wise thing to do. He was bent over, leaning against the car, coughing heavily and trying to regain his breath. Kayden knelt down beside him. 'Do you need a lift to the hospital? To your car? Home? Anything Max! Anything!' Danger couldn't answer though. Kayden figured he should just take the initiative and help Max into his car. He was leading him around the front and went to open up the door when Danger straightened himself and shouted, 'NO!' Paulton looked up and at Max, bewildered. 'No, what?' 'No, you're not helping me.' 'Why not? You're hurt. Someone must help you.' 'NO! I don't need help!' 'Oh, ok... but, you looked as tho-' 'I don't!' Kayden just went silent. He just waited for Danger to continue, being polite and all that. 'I talked to Reed Young.' Paulton's face beamed. 'Did you? Nice fellow, isn't he?' Danger just gave Kayden a strange look that said 'what the hell are you smoking?', and just shook his head of it. 'Yeah, sure, whatever. Anyway, he's done me a favor.' 'That's great! Mr. Young is the greatest boss! What favor did he do for you?' He was waiting eagerly to hear it. Though Max was sure he wouldn't be so happy about what he had to say. A smile crept across Danger's face before he said, 'Another match with you!' He began to cackle. What? Yes, cackle. Danger was strange, shut up. Kayden, however, didn't really catch why Danger was laughing, and with a smile said, 'That's great!' Max stopped laughing. 'What?' 'I can't wait to face you again. Though, it seems, I do have your number, doesn't it. I've beaten you twice in a row now. Funny, isn't it?' Danger was baffled. Why wasn't Kayden trembling at his feet. Why wasn't he begging for Max to get Reed to change his mind? Danger just didn't understand. 'So, is it a match at the PPV? I think that would be grand. Kayden Paulton vs. Max Danger at Players and Pawns. Paulton looking for a third straight victory over 'The Danger Man', while Max tries to stop a losing streak.' Danger was just stunned. He couldn't move. He couldn't speak. Then, as Kayden was about to say something else, he snapped, 'YOU KNOW WHAT!? IT'S NOT GOING TO HAPPEN! I'M GOING TO GET REED TO CANCEL IT!!! NO MORE MATCHES WITH ME!!!!' Kayden, though, looked only slightly disappointed. 'Oh, well, that's fine. Maybe we can team up?' His face was beaming. Danger was horror struck. He shreiked, 'HELL NO! MATCH IS BACK ON!!!' Paulton smiled again. 'Ok.' Danger started to jump up and down in a fit of rage, screaming, 'SHIT! SHIT! SHIT! SHIT! SHIIIIIIIIIIIIT!!' at the top of his lungs. Kayden looked worried. 'You should take it easy, Max. Your hair will fall out. No need for all this stress. Here, let me take you over to your car.' He had reached out to help Max into the car, but Danger pulled away. 'NO! I can manage!' He was breathing heavily, and his face was blood red with anger. Kayden just shrugged. 'Well... see you next week!' He waved and walked back around the car and got in. He sped off, leaving Danger alone out in the cold. Danger dropped to his knees and placed his head into his hands, as if he were crying. The he saw tail lights coming back through the cracks of his hands. He looked up and it was Kayden Paulton. He lowered his glass again, and looked down at Max. 'Here, I got you a Sunkist. I heard you like them best.' He handed Danger a bottle of Sunkist. Max took it, in disbelief. 'Well, bye!' He then sped off again, leaving Danger alone, on his knees, in the cold, and holding a Sunkist.
