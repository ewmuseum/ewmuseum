Vagabond - VI

4 June 2002

Date: 06/04/2002 Previous Parts: 1, 2, 3, 4a, 4b, 5 Mind. Drift. Think. Now deep in the heart of Texas, several hours away from reaching the state of Louisiana, Joey Malone has had time to think. Think, Joey did. The more he thought, the more he began to question his own decision to come to New Orleans. He should have been back in training, preparing for what he knew would be his next horrible, bloody war with the man he used to call Rashard Clark. He should have been preparing. Instead, he was taking a road trip to meet a student of his that he hadn't seen in months. It was a gambit that Joey Malone was wondering if he'd regret. Especially since he ended up all over the News after his brawl in New Mexico. He thought about Keri. While it was comforting to know that he was with a sister-in-law who was willing to drop what she was doing to help Joey, it was NOT comforting to know that his wife was in the hands of a psychopath who would do anything, whatsoever, to break Joey's spirits. Joey knew that Fission would use his own home to hold Keri. Which was exactly why Joey wanted to get out of town. But why did he come back? Why did he leave? He was beginning to wonder why he made the decisions he made. Maybe it was predetermined. Maybe the belief that fate decided everything, despite Joey's belief in otherwise. If so, was the love he had for Keri predetermined, too? Or was there some divine deity up there that didn't forsee it, and put up as many roadblocks as he/she could to prevent their love from lasting? Or maybe Joey just had bad luck. The more Joey thought about it, the more his mind drifted away. He thought about the house. He thought about the house's old gaudy wallpaper and how he went through a lot of trouble to remove it. He thought about its shrubberies and its small, neat-looking, and easy to mow lawn. He thought about the hundred or so pictures that he and Keri had carefully hung around the house, celebrating Joey's life, triumphs, and even failures. If a picture were worth a thousand words, how many words would his house have represented? Has Fission taken down those pictures? Torn them apart? Burned them? Joey secretly hoped he had. He was having an epiphany. In a way, Fission was actually doing something good for Joey. In destroying Joey's life, Fission has given Joey a reason to live and a method on how to live. Rather than just hide away whenever something comes along, Joey was now feeling the confidence needed to tackle his fear. Joey suddenly had the courage to visit his friends again. Was it because he needed them? Or was it because he just missed them? He lost contact with a majority of them after his honeymoon, and hadn't felt that he needed their help. As it turned out, he needed their help more than anything else in the world. Except Keri. Joey's thoughts would have drifted further, but at that moment, Ally swerved to avoid a raccoon and startled Joey from his thoughts. He turned to look at Allison as she quickly moved back into the lane she previously occupied, thankfully raccoon-free. Allison looked at Joey, shrugged, and smiled. 'What?'
