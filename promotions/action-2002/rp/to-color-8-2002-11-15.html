Action! — Tales

To Color. 8

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He was working hard. Maybe even a little too hard. It’s like the work never stopped with him... he just kept on going and going, producing the same quality almost every time. His office door opened up, as the talented individual known as Mark Paulton stood at the doorway. He smiled politely before he was offered a chair across the desk. Then doing so, Mark took his seat. He waited for his Boss to finish, but once he did, he started up. “How are you today Mister Young?” “Mark.” Young stated. “You can call me Forest. You know that.” Mark nodded. “I understand, but I perfect to be proper. At least let me do so.” Forest, nodded too. “Alright.” Young paused for a second, as he picked up the few sheets of paper off of his desk and tucked them into his manila folder before filing it under the drawer. Mark had a look around. The office was so nice, so neat, so well-kept and clean. How did he even do it... with so much work on his hands to do. “Sorry about that.” Said Forest, as he placed his arms quietly in front of him on top of his brown oak desk. “Now, why I called you here... are you sure Mark, are you sure you’d like to go through with this?” Without much hesitation, Mark shook his head. “Yes sir, I would. I feel it’s the next step up the ladder.” Forest reluctantly grinned. “Well, okay. Just remember, Mark, you’re already a legend... and the best that I have. Please, please do not go to extremes out there.” Paulton thought about it for a second... but the mere thought of it made him want to hurl. There was the man that made his career, that did everything for him. The least he could do, was tell him yes. He could obey that order. Nothing out of the ordinary. It would be as normal as any other battle would be. “Of course.” Mark stuck out his hand as he stood up from his chair, shaking Forest’s hand hard and stern. Mark said his goodbye then, as he exited the office to go get ready for his big main event later on that night... the one where everyone would be watching. Forest paused for a second, as he smiled and neatly un-tucked his hands as he reached back into his drawer to take out his manila folder.
