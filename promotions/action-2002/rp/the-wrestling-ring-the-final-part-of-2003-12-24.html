The Wrestling Ring, The Final Part of

24 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Could- could- could-” “You want me to sign this?” LLB said to eight-year-old Erica. She had been suffering from cerebral palsy since birth… but you couldn’t tell. The happiness that LLB brought her made her determined to try her best and hand LLB her photo. And the former two-time Action Champion just smiled as he took it and signed his signature as best as he could. “There you go.” She smiled and walked towards her mother, who also smiled back. LLB took the next autograph sheet… and then did the same. “Uh… LLB…” The boy in the wheelchair said, as ‘The Law’ looked down. “Yeah?” “Why weren’t you on Pressure Point today?” And that set off a reaction… not a major one, but one that did make everyone in the room open their mouths and also ask the same thing. LLB looked back towards the ring… he really didn’t have anything to say. He was not in the dominating position like he was six months ago. If he went back, he thought, he’d just fall right back down again. Contemplating retirement suddenly wasn’t just contemplating it anymore… “I- I-” But seeing the looks on their faces… and what LLB had meant to them in the past… in a way, it got to him. ‘The Law’ took another glance at the ring… while Bill and Robbie were now lowering the canvas. But the ring didn’t care. Through LLB’s downtime he had realized this. He had realized that no matter how long he had been in wrestling… the ring continued to stand there. It was like a revolving door. Six months ago… there he was… inside the ring, working with its magic. And now? Now he had nothing. He was outside the doors… but they still carried on. They still let new people in… and revolved like they had never been stopping. Even with LLB still outside the building. This wasn’t what LLB thought. Sure, he would be remembered… but cared for? In the same light as he was six months ago? There was no way. Now the wrestling ring displayed Jeff Garvin… Frank Heimdal… Rook Black… Max Danger… and others. And when their time would go… the ring would display others yet again. LLB couldn’t have that. Sure… his status might be lower. Sure… maybe no one wanted to see him return. But LLB could. And LLB would. Taking one last glance at the ring, he turned to Tommy Wright. “Next week…” LLB started. “How about I give you all front row seats?” The looks on their faces sprung with joy. They knew what this meant. And they knew they were happy. All LLB did… was smile. The wrestling ring would have him back. Like it or not.
