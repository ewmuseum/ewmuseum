Precursor (Numero Uno)

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/08/2002 It hits you without any notice at all... A picture of a car smashes into the side of a house. Glass and metal go streaking across the picture with a blizzard of red, hot, scalding flames shoot from what was once a nice looking Ford truck. It's effects cause sheer agony... We see a picture of a teenagers head being slammed repeatedly against a power-sander in shop class. Bone and blood fly everywhere as the boy screams in pure horror... It's... The spilt milkshake conveniently placed in the middle of the shopping center... All of a sudden we see a ground view of the celing of a mall food court. Everything slowly fades back into exsistance as a man with short, spikey red hair and a long gotee offers a hand to his fallen friend. 'Dude, man, you just slipped right the hell over that milkshake and busted your ass. You alright?' Kent's eyes tingled as he gave a hard blink and sat up. Quite a few people were staring at him. 'Yeah... Just dandy. Where the hell am I?' Lucas gave Kent a very akward stare. 'Kent, tell me, truthfully... At this present moment, how many arms do I have?' Kent smiled. 'Two, dumbass. Everyone has two arms... Except for those weird experiments and those mutated southern asian hardcore porno chicks... but you aren't either or.' 'How many are you really seeing though?' 'Five.' 'Good enough. Stand up, you look like an idiot sitting there on the mall floor.' Kent did so. His legs felt alittle like jello but he managed. A splitting headache rushed to the inner most part of his forehead. Anthason slammed a hand on his head and screamed quite loudly. 'Would you shut the hell up?' Lucas advised Kent. 'I can't help it. I just stood up and this splitting fucking migrane grabbed me by the hair and made me it's bitch.' Lucas steadied Kent ever so slowly. Now... Where were we? Oh yeah. If you didn't know, which obviously you don't since you're just now getting around to actually meeting Lucas and Kent, we're currently shopping for Kent's third cousin's friend who's distantly related to Ritchie Cunningham from Happy Days' half uncle whose birthday so happens to be tomorrow. 'Where were we going?' Kent questioned as he started to try and walk a few steps. 'Let's go buy you a new shirt first, you should take a look at the back of yours.' Kent reached over and pulled his shirt over his shoulder so he could see what was on him. Milkshake... everywhere. 'Goddamnit!' All of a sudden, none other than the big, beefy bald white guy known as Mr. Clean runs into the mall. He slams the gliding glass doors to each side of the wall shattering them into a million peices. He moves his head back and forth as if scanning the immediate area for something until he comes across Kent and Lucas who stare at him in stunned disbelief. 'Is that...?' 'I think that's Mr.- Fucking-Clean. JESUS GOD! IT IS MR. CLEAN!' Kent almost bent over and cried... 'He's here to clean this mess up for me! I knew he was such a noble man.' Instead, Mr. Clean walked over to Lucas and Kent and stopped for a second. 'Excuse me.' He said as he pushed Kent and Lucas out of the way... 'Excused.' Lucas said as he gave Mr. Clean a horrible stare. 'C'mon Kent, let's go get you some new clothes.' And with that Kent and Lucas walked off to start their mall trip from scratch. But, wait! What about Mr. Clean, you ask? Well, let's find out. We move the camera over to where Mr. Clean took off to. We see him stalking over to some large football player who's apparently signing autographs. 'Excuse me, Mr. Couch?' 'Yeah, son. You wanna an autograph, too, kid? 'Not really.' And with that, Mr. Clean cleanly laid out one Tim Couch of the Cleveland Browns. People gasped, people cried, and somewhere in God knows where, Shaq is eating a Grilled Sourdough Bacon Cheeseburger, with king sized fries and a king sized coke with some cheesy fry dip to go with it. But, anyhow, we return to the scene of the outlandish attack. We see Mr. Clean slamming Tim Couch's head against the mall floor. With each sickening thud Tim's eyes start to water up with a pinkish liquid. His nose starts to profuesly bleed as his face becomes more and more unrecognizeable. Then, all of a sudden, Mr. Clean pulls out a mop and some of his pattended cleaning solution and demonstrates the wonders of MR. CLEAN for all the people around. They 'Ooh' and 'Aah.' as they soon forget about their football hero and flock to Mr. Clean. Some random boy calls out, 'I WANT TO BE JUST LIKE YOU WHEN I GROW UP.' The group leaves with Mr. Clean out from where they came as Butch Davis, Coach of the Cleveland Browns walks up with a milkshake in his hand. 'Hey, Tim, I meant to get both of us a milkshake but I accidently dropped one and figured I was too lazy to get another so you can have this on-- Tim? Tim? TIM?' Coach Davis nudged the corpse of Tim Couch with his foot a little, shrugged, and walked off sipping Couch's milkshake. Meanwhile, we zoom back over to Kent and Lucas who are walking down the mall again. Kent has seemed to regain his stature as they seem to be shooting the bull... That is, until Kent trips over Tim Couch's dead corpse and lands hard on the concrete floor. Lucas just kinda stares, and puts his hands on his hips. He gives a glance to his watch as he taps his foot impatiently. 'Goddamnit...' (Fade.)
