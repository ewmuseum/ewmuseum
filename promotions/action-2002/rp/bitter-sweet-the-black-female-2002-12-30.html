Bitter Sweet: The Black Female

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ransom pointed in the direction of a sexy young black female as cameras faded away from the scene. Right into a close up of the black girl talking to Veronica Aromadeus. What was going on you ask, listen up. 'We miss you at school girlfriend.' 'I know, maybe one day I'll go back and get my degree, but I love what I'm doing Vic, and I have a good man to share it with.' Veronica was talking to her former roommate Victoria Chambers also known as Very Vivacious, or V&V among other names. 'So what's going on with him anyway.' Vic pointed over into the direction of Romey and Jonathan, who had by this time stopped pointed and continued their conversation, which will be recapped at another point and time. 'Oh we're engaged! Haven't really set a date yet though, some things are being discussed over where he wrestles at about Jim Johnson shutting the place down, and it looks like he might be a free agent.' 'So why don't you recruit him? I'm sure that would boost ratings.' 'Well, considering the big New Year's Eve pay per view coming up, you never know, he might show up.' 'You got him signed didn't you? Jonathan's going to help you beat that Minio guy isn't he?' 'No, Jonathan knows I can fight my own battles, and wouldn't want to make himself out to be a bully.' 'But he plays on on TV.' 'Exactly, he's a good actor.' 'Well for your sake, I hope he's not acting like he loves you.' Victoria winked in the direction of Romey Ransom.
