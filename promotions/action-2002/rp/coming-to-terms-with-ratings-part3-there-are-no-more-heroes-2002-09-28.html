Coming to terms with Ratings part3 - there are no more heroes

28 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.28.02 The sun peaked through the holes in the clouds onto a dark blue classic Mustang. Bobby Minio was sitting on the hood, staring at the overcast. He had a smirk on his face. The day was bland, Like his mood. He felt a storm brewing, in his stomach, in his mind, in his life, and in the sky. It was coming. Coming together to fall apart. The dysfunction of the past was a manual for whats in store. He got his hops up, and for what? Was his life being dominated by a woman again? Does she even know it is? Bobby's smirk played into a sneer. He lifted the cheeseburger he had sitting beside him to his face, taking a bite almost forcefully. His appitite had been non-existant in the past days. Ever since the first confrontation with Angelica Dawson, Bobby had been troubled. He hadn't smiled in quite some time now. He had spent every moment deep in thought about what he, what she, were doing. Max Danger was also now a problem. Danger was a catalyst. He created more problems then those that lay before Minio. Minio was looking to win the Dyad titles with Veronica Aromadeus. He wanted to make her happy, to make her proud. But now, right now, he felt betrayed. He felt torn between love and hate. One would have to be dominant soon. One would have to overcome. And Minio reluctantly knew exactly which it would be. He took another forced bite of the ninety nine cent double stack. He makes alright money, for a wrestler its not great, but its not bad. He's a nomad, he doesn't spend to much on bills. But still he found himself pinching pennies. He felt like he was on a budget. What was he saving up for? Reed Young, his boss, had left a child in charge of a Wrestling federation a few days ago. Was the business collapsing? Minio had a bad feeling about the direction of his job, and his business. He made a huge mistake with PIW. It was making great ratings, he was making great Money, but he pissed it away on a trip to the orient. He thought alittle martial arts would calm his nerves, would refine him as a better person. He was wrong. In recent weeks, he had found a new martial art. Krav Maga. Israli Self defense. He found a new outlet for his anger. It was helping, barely. But he felt like his punches were much stronger even after a few lessons. He felt like a machine already. He just needed to get his head on straight. Reed Young was supposed to pay for a trip for Minio to chill out. Take a small vacation. Even the fewest of days would help. Reed forgot about Minio. Like always. Minio wasn't grabbing the ratings Young had expected. There were no Minio marks. Was anyone even watching Minio's life falling apart on television? History repeating. Minio was dropping the ball. Minio was forgotten. He used to think of himself as a hero in the days of Jim Johnson and the evil empire. He figured himself a hero against the likes of the three musketeers and KroW. Minio had a rude awakening recently. There are no more heroes.
