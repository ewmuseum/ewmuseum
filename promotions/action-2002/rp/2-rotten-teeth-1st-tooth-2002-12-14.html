2 Rotten Teeth (1st Tooth)

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/14/02 OPEN Blufinland sat down; the leather chair crackling for a second as the Doctor settled in for a long operation. It was time to put Fox’s money were his mouth was.... literally. Title: 2 Rotten Teeth (1st Tooth) Date: 12/14/02 11:00 am EST Place: Dentist Office Blufinland “Ok Chris, now our procedure is going to be harmless. I guarantee that you won’t feel any pain or discomfort during the fillings.” Yeah I take your word for it, Doc. The only thing that will be painful is getting scolded by Mommy for letting two of my pearly whites rot! Only songs from a PA could be heard as the nice Dr. Blufinland went to work in his office. It was time to fill the first tooth. It was located at the upper back of Fox’s left side. The very last one. Of course Mommy choose Chris to have the white fillings instead of the silver ones. Blufinland “Ok now, all I’m going to do is put some freezing in your gums.... both upper and lower, seeing as the two bad teeth are directly across from each other.” Blufinland grabbed the needle with about a two centimeter tip. He squirted it and then proceeded. Blufinland “Ok Chris.... I’ll need you to open your mouth, this won’t hurt and if it does I’ll give you two suckers instead of one.” Knowing how ‘Kid’ acts like a kid, Blufinland understood how to handle the situation. Fox abided by the rules and un-cranked his jaw. The foul odors emerged as bats came flying out (ok we’re lying about the bats but it wasn’t a pleasant smell). Anyway, Blufinland pricked the top area of Fox’s gums and injected the freeze material.... then followed that up on the lower gums. Blufinland “All done the freezing, you can just wash your mouth out....” Fox sat up vigorously and dumped out a bunch of red saliva. Seeing the red in his spit.... he freaked out! I’m bleeding! Only girls bleed! Hey, wait a minute, it’s from that needle.... I never liked needles.... BIG TIME! Well, I’ve ventured this far, I might as well go the distance and that’s definitely not an LOL! C.E.F. relaxed in the chair as phase two commenced.... time to drill. Blufinland snatched the tiny but powerful drill, tested it with one pull of the trigger and hammered away. Of course by now Fox was so intoxicated with the freezing that he was almost sent asleep. The Doctor did a clean dig up on the top tooth as all Fox could feel was a very slight pins and needles sensation. Blufinland “Ok Chris, that’s the drilling part. Now the next step may take longer. We’ll insert this white casing inside your tooth and gel it in place.” Heh.... he, teehee, Teletubies are my buddies! The very calm Blufinland and very calm Fox entered into the filling stage. Blufinland inserted the small capsule sized with white filling and chipped at it, securing it into place. Fuzzy wuzzy was a bear.... hey, wait!! I’m not wearing underwear! By the time Chris was on his fifth poem, as Blufinland had already finished the first filling and was preparing another needle, this time to freeze the bottom. Blufinland “That first one went well, Chris. Now just open your mouth and we’ll get started on that second one.” Fox opened his mouth alright, in a circular donut shape. Blufinland “I know you’re intoxicated....” Blufinland spoke with a heavy English accent. Blufinland “But just open your mouth normally please, there’s a bar of soap in it for you.” Christopher cooperated with big bright eyes as the second needle headed to the lower jaw.... Will Christopher ever get off of the freezing drug? Does the second tooth go as smoothly as the first!? Find out next time! CLOSE
