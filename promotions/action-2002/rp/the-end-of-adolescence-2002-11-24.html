The End Of Adolescence

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
How does it feel to watch things change right before your eyes? How does it feel to see this old dog back in his old home? How does it seem? The fans cheered for me. It was a decent pop, after all. Not like that isn’t a big achievement. I was literally pelted with fruit when I came out, and have been damn close on many occasions. However, you can ask who got the last laugh in on that unforgettable night. I would say it is I. Even in my loss, I can say that I don’t care about this persona. It’s not me anymore. And to be honest, I am over myself. No more sleepless nights in this business. No more bullshit. For months, I have been trying to find a reason to keep at this. A reason to have fun again. When I finished my chair shot parade, well, that was it. I found my fun. I found my desire. That’s why there are no more demons in my closet. No more creepy things to startle me when I sleep. The old nightmares are over now. It’s time to grow out of this post adolescent funk and become a man. I am twenty-nine years old, and for the past eleven years of my life, I have not made a decision. I made that decision when I entered the building. I wasn’t going to be a tool anymore. I wasn’t going to be doing this for all the wrong reasons. Life’s too short to be as bitter as I have become. That I had become. You see, this is the end of this long chapter. In my mind, I have to finally live in the here and now, not hate everyone for something that happened years ago. We have all changed so drastically, that this world is totally different then just a year ago. It’s time that I evolve like the rest of you people have. I’m so sick of faux ideals that have no true meaning. So, I close my eyes to drift off into heavenly sleep, not a shred of guilt in my mind, not a single tear in my eye. It’s the first time since I was a young boy I could say think this. I am finally healed of my own dementia. -fin-
