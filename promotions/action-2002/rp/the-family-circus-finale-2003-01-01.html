The Family Circus: Finale

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/01/03 Coral Avalon found Allison Lindum, sitting at the stairs that came before the front doorstep of the Avalon mansion. She rested her arms on her lap and stared at the star-filled night sky, still trying to shake the events of the dinner that had gone wrong from her head. Coral sighed and walked over next to his girlfriend. 'I'm sorry.' he said. Allison hummed to herself, and sighed. 'It's not your fault,' Allison whispered, sighing to herself, 'It's mine. I shouldn't have brought you here if I had known that this was going to happen.' 'Well,' Coral sighed, sitting down next to her, 'You thought that what you were doing in bringing me here was the best thing to do. You thought that by meeting my family, you'd come to understand me better. Right?' 'Yes.' she said, simply. 'I didn't handle myself in there that well,' Coral said, 'My family always brings out the worst in me, especially when we get into a squabble like that. I dunno, I just can't seem to ' Emmy Avalon, who had been listening in, walked toward her younger brother and his girlfriend. This was surprising, since she rather liked to stay away from people in general. 'You know,' Emmy said, as she walked forward and sat next to Coral, 'What we do... we-well, it rips us a-apart.' 'What do you mean?' Coral asked. 'I mean that... well, we're... we're all kind of s-strange. Sometimes, we ju... we just work to be like that. I, I mean, how many people have you met that're like, you know, Elvis?' Emmy asked. 'I always thought that David Dew should be in anger management, myself.' Coral said, idly, making a mental note to find him something for Christmas that would help him with his anger. 'How about Auntie Cleo?' Emmy asked, surprisingly studder-free. 'Okay, got me there,' Coral said, 'What's your point?' 'It's just that, it's... well... um, how do I put this? Overrated. All of it.' Emmy said, taking her eyeglasses off. 'I mean, we're all just k-kinda living off of Uncle Albert and Dad. We, we don't really do anything wo-worthwhile,' Emmy said, 'None... none of us, we d-don't have a right to judge you. You weren't trying to be strange or... or anti-social. You chose a path that's... you know, what you truly wanted to do. You didn't let what Mom, Lily, or Auntie Cleo stop you... it's... it's what sets you apart from us, you know?' 'I'm still kinda peeved that Mom still holds a grudge.' Coral said, with a sigh. Emmy said nothing, mainly because she looked up at the six foot, eight inch, tongueless freak of a big brother in Elvis Avalon. Coral and Allison both looked up at the giant, surprised to also see him there. Elvis merely nodded toward them and sat down next to Emmy. He, too, had apparently gotten bored of the bickering inside. He said nothing, but sat down and looked up at the stars. 'Don't be,' Allison said, before she flashed him a sly grin, 'My dad's still mad that I'm with you, anyway.' Coral laughed, 'Your dad wanted me to be in the military and be a 'real man'. Then he chased me around with a plastic chainsaw for a half an hour when I said I wasn't up to it.' Allison latched onto Coral's arm and rested her head on his shoulder. 'So, what now?' Allison asked. Coral breathed, before he spoke again, 'I don't know. Part of me felt like that if I came here and confronted my family about the past, I'd come to understand myself. Unfortunately, the only thing I've understood since I got here is that most of my family holds really long grudges.' Coral sighed. 'Thing is, being a wrestler was a lot harder than I thought. There's constant pain and drain from being on the road so much, and it was a lot worse when I didn't have anything to look forward to when I came home,' Coral said, with yet another sigh, 'Then I met you, Allison. You're someone I look forward to seeing every single time I come back home. You're there when I'm hitting hard times, so, you know, that's a big, big plus.' Allison smiled. Coral closed his eyes, and thought to himself. Unbeknownst to all four of the people sitting in front, John, Cleo, Lily, and Irene Avalon stood at the doorstep. The only one who spoke was Irene, 'Do you think that we may of misjudged him?' None of them said anything, but they did nod in silent agreement. John Avalon then approached his son, while the other three female members of the Avalon family left sight of the front door. 'Coral,' John said, getting the attention of Emmy, Elvis, Coral, and Allison, 'I know that we've been inhospitable to you and Allison, but you're both welcome to stay here tonight.' Allison looked at Coral, and both of them shrugged, 'What the hell, it's not like I paid for a hotel room.' With that, Coral and Allison both stood up and followed John inside. They were then followed by Elvis, and then Emmy, who shut the door behind them. The Avalon family, for the first time in three years, would stay in the same household for a night.
