Down and Out. (Part III)

28 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.28.02 Bells chimed as Steven pushed the shop door aside with his right crutch. He worked his way in slowly, eventually ending up on the inside with the door slamming behind him. The man at the counter looked his way halfheartedly. 'You want me to help you or what?' 'Nope. I'm just gonna look around for the moment,' Steven replied. 'Good. The man turned around and entered a door which read 'employees only'. Steven chuckled at how the man acted. What's up his ass?, he though, before turning and looking at the items around him. He didn't want anything too powerful, just something that could do the job. Nothing fancy. Nothing flashy. Just something quick and inexpensive. He didn't plan on keeping it long anyway... 'Come on, pick one. Loitering ain't allowed,' the cashier had returned to his post, the same lighthearted look still covering his face. 'I'm not loit-' 'Whatever. Just pick one.' Steven choked back his emotions. He didn't need to get into any trouble at this point, just wasn't worth it. Besides, he was on crutches, what could he possibly do anyway? So, he continued browsing through the store, checking every price for the cheapest he could find. Temporarily, he forgot about his crutches and tried to step on his injured leg. A pain shot through his injury, all the way to his head. He stopped to regain his composure, trying not to show too much agony. Surgery was the last thing Steven wanted to begin with. All surgery seemed to do was pain him further, and make him even less mobile. But regardless, he'd do just about anything the doctor told him to. Wrestling meant a lot and he didn't want to ruin it by not receiving treatment for something. The doctor had told him he'd torn his meniscus, and that it would require surgery. End of story. No way around it. It sucks, but life sucks a lot nowadays. So he had gotten his tear surgically repaired a couple weeks prior to this day, and now had about a month left before he could walk again, so sayeth the almighty doctor. 'Would you hurry up? I want to close the store down.' Damn, this guy knew how to get under your skin. 'Yeah, fine. This one,' Steven signaled to an item near the counter. The cashier quickly typed its identification number into the computer, before removing from the case and heading into the back. After another couple minutes, which Steven spent reading various signs that hung around the shop, the clerk returned. 'Now, you understand the risks of...' 'Yeah, yeah, yeah. You want to close down, don't worry about giving me that speech,' Steven said. He was just as eager to leave as the man was to see him leave. 'Okay. Hold onto this card and bring it back up in five days, and you'll be able to pick up your gun then.' 'Thanks,' he took the card and placed it in his pocket, before turning and edging his way towards the door. He was one step closer, the only thing standing in his way at this point was his mind, and battling his mind would be the most difficult battle of all.
