The Last Time.

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.02.2003 Madison Square Garden was going nuts as Daniel Phillips and Joey Malone fought for their very careers. Many of the boys and the various other workers, too, were watching any monitor they could find. It was a big moment. An even bigger one, to a handful of people anyway, was going on behind all that. Max Danger's back was pure agony. His leg was ailing him. His neck wasn't feeling too great, either. But he didn't care. The pain was all worth it. He had gained victory over Conrad Ramsey... again. No small feat. Oh, sure, people think it was easy. But it's never a simple thing to beat the same person multiple times. It took everything Max had this time. Took the Dangerous IV. The crowd was jazzed throughout the whole match, and for good reason. Both men had brought their A-game. Both men put it all out there. Sure, there wasn't any big table spots or anything of that nature. That was the nature of it though. No Gimmicks Necessary. They didn't need 'em. They didn't use 'em. Just straight up wrestling. The Danger Man over the Old Man. For the final time. In his lockerroom, Xander had showered up. He had changed into his street clothes. He and Jessica were going to head out and watch the rest of the show. It was a big night for Action!. As they were about to join the others, there was a knock on the door. Jessica opened it and there stood Samantha Dox. Xander's mother. 'May I come in?' she asked in her gentle voice. Jessica wasn't sure. She looked back to her husband, who rose up and looked at his mother. He studied her for a moment. She wasn't upset. She was... happy? How could that be? 'Yes, mom, you may come in,' Max said. Jessica stepped aside, holding the door open further. A group of wrestlers in the hallway were shouting at the top of their lungs at the monitor in front of them. Jessica shut the door, drowning them out. Samantha walked to her so. The two had made up recently. Put their differences aside. Xander had relented, accepting her engagement and future wedding to Conrad Ramsey. The future Mrs. Ramsey stood in front of her son. She wasn't there to talk about the previous match. She wasn't there to scold her son for beating her future husband. She was there to tell him goodbye. 'Marcus, my son,' she started. 'I have learned a lot about the type of man you are in the last few months. And I realize more than ever that you are exactly like your father was.' Xander smiled. That was a great thing to hear. His father was a great man. 'I know the concerns you had in the past. I know how you feel about Conrad. And I respect that,' she continued. 'And I just want to thank you. Thank you for caring so much. Any mother would be proud to have such a great son like yourself.' Xander blushed. Jessica was beaming behind them. As happy as could be. Xander said, 'Oh, mom. Come here.' The two embraced. Hugging. They kissed each other on the cheeks and then seperated. 'Ramsey told me, while I was dropping him on his head, that you two were heading to Vegas. Is that true?' Xander asked. Samantha nodded, 'Yeah. We're going to have Elvis marry us.' She smiled. 'I'm glad you're happy, mom,' Xander added. 'Me, too,' she replied. The two hugged again. And then Samantha said, 'Goodbye, my dear. Take care of yourself.' 'I will, mom. Have fun.' As Samantha walked past Jessica, she whispered, 'Look after him, okay?' Jessica nodded. Someone had to look after the poor boy. And Samantha was gone. Jessica walked back to her husband and she put her arm around his waist, while he put his arm across her back. Together, the two exited the room. His mother was at the end of the hallway, kissing Conrad Ramsey on the cheek, just as the two headed through the double doors and out of sight. Xander turned to Jessica and kissed her on the forehead. They walked over to the group of wrestlers, and partook in the rest of Juggernaut II.
