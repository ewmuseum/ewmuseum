Action! — Tales

Suffer.

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/09/2002 I suffer from my job. I could have just as easilly have been a lazy-as-hell worker who has no idea what he's doing in the ring, but instead, I pushed my body to whatever limit it had. God didn't bless me with an incredibly great body, but he definitely blessed me with a tolerance for pain. However, it's been proven that Action Wrestling has been a witness of some truly spectacular kickings of my ass. Especially in the last couple of weeks. I don't know. Maybe I've just become lazy and unprepared because I got married. Maybe I just can't cut it in this bloody industry any more. Maybe I lost the killer instinct I once had. I don't know, any more. I suffer from my worries. Watching that man act shows me that he's a man that has no weakness to exploit. Whereas, I have more than necessary. I don't just have Keri to protect, I have all of my friends. They need to be warned. Simon, Daniel, Jack, Kate, Scotty, Tony, Quinn, Kent, Coral, all of them. I am a threat to them. Not because of anything I can do to them, but my mere presence in their lives will infect that man upon them all. I don't... want to be responsible for that. I don't need to be responsible for that. It's supposed to be my battle, and yet, he wants to invite everyone I know and love in on this, too. I suffer from my guilt. I admit that at first, I had no idea what he was talking about when he said that he'd 'do to me what I did to him' when he kidnapped Keri. But as time went on, little bits and pieces began to fall together in my head. I, at one time when I was IWO world champ, promised him that I'd give him a shot at the title down the road. I didn't know I was being put into a feud with my friend, Kent Anthason. He found out about it, and he lost it. He intentionally tried to injure Anthason at the following PPV, but that plan backfired when the injury Anthason received wasn't as bad as it was thought, and he was able to face me. I lost the title that night. He was not thrilled. The night afterward, he yelled and screamed at me, while I was still incapacitated from the most emotionally and physically exhausting night of my life. We haven't exactly been best pals since. It's why I asked Simon Seaman to be the best man at my wedding, instead of him. I suffer from my paranoia. Because he could be lurking anywhere. He's like a vampire that doesn't have to be invited. He is my shadow. Wherever I go, he follows. I can never get rid of him. If I turn around, would he be there? If I turned my head back around and walked some more, would he be standing in front of me? Or would he have snuck up on me to stab me in the back again? I suffer from my feelings of betrayal. He's betrayed me. He's betrayed Keri. Quinn. Kent. Everyone. And for what? What does he have to gain out of this? Revenge. And what does revenge get you? Nothing. I know from expierence through my battles and wars with such people as Stone, AWS Man, Crow, Daze, Anthason, and Denton. In my attempts to gain my revenge on these people, I got my ass kicked a lot. I blew out my knee, suffered some concussions, bled like victims in a vampire's assault of a hemophiliac's convention, and scared quite a few people, including my now-wife. I suffer. Because Syphon Fission is my enemy. A relentless, inhuman, revenge-minded enemy. I stand little chance, but I'm going to have to fight him. It's the only way to save me, or anyone else I ever cared about.
