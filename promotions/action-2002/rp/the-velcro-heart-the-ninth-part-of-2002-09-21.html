The Velcro Heart, The Ninth Part of

21 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“How’s it look, Jeremy?” Young twirled around in front of a mirror, admiring himself from head to toe. “You don’t think it’s a wee bit tacky, do ya?” “Oh, no sir... it’s hip. Very hip.” In a most ironic turn of events, Reed had ordered Morg’s ear to be fashioned into a one-of-a-kind, gold plated earring, which he’d now wear in attempt to get Morg’s goat and subsequently draw him out of the woodwork. “Like they always say, Boss, three ears are better than one!” Bold earnestly attempted to reassure Young of the striking affect his hood ornament held. “But really, do I look good in it? I think I do.” Bold blushed. “You do. I can hear the girls screaming right now.” The Fans booed. “If I could just-” The door opened up, as LLB stood, rifling his eyes right through the Owner of A!W. “Reed.” He addressed in a stern manner. “I have a Match tonight!?” Young nodded. “Yeah. I told you to get ready for o-” “Objection!” The Fans replied with “Erection!” as LLB again, huffed heavily. “I’m not ready for a Match tonight... hell-” “It’s okay.” Bold said, as LLB snapped around, almost scaring Jeremy to bits. “It’s only against Tyler Babylon. We, like, need him to die... and you’re the ONLY one that can kill him, Rowe. THE ONLY ONE!” Bold’s pathetically appeared. LLB turned back to Young and walked up to him. He squinted his eyes together. “What the HELL is on your ear!?” Young grinned. “Morg’s ear.” “Morg?” Bold said. “Where has he been as of late?” Shrugging, Reed gave a reply. “I don’t know, really. Maybe... in plastic surgery... getting a new ear! LOL!” Young & Bold: “LOL!” ‘The Law’ held up his fist to Young’s head, stopping their fun internet talk. “You’re an idiot sometimes... you know.” Reed only smiled again, as he dangled his ear around, showing it off. “Well... at least I didn’t CAR POOL with Chris Fox!” “Whoa... whoa. OBJECTION!” “Erection!” “The kid’s car broke down... he needed a ride... I- I could fucking care less about him... that’s why I didn’t do a thing when you smacked him across the face.” Young nodded, putting his hands on LLB’s shoulders. “Yeah. I know... it’s just, eeerrr... he gets me SO mad with his stupid ‘Big Time!’ saying... and he survived Steven Denton... let alone two weeks ago beat me with a LOW BLOW!” It was a right hand Reed... a right hand. “It was a right-” LLB didn’t even want to give it a try. He just looked at Young, whom flicked the literal “ear-ring” around as he shot a glance at Bold. “Just... go out there and beat Tyler Babylon... write him off. He’s another one of those annoying little pricks. Just do that in your Title Match... and all is cool like always Bro. Oh, hey, by the way... did you get all of my messages at the house this week?” LLB started to walk away, but then snapped back. “A TITLE MATCH!?” “Yeah. If that’s okay.” “No... it’s no-” Slowly but surely... LLB could only remember what happened in the bar just one week ago. “Fine. Title Match... but stay out of it. I can take Babylon on... hell, I can kill him.” Young laughed. “YESUMS! RATINGS!! I LOVE YOU ROWE-TEN!” “Rowe-ten?” “Yeah. Row ten... a dude, and a good looking dude at that. Check him out tonight!” “But Reed, you haven’t even been to the ring yet.” “I- um... hey I signed Jeff Gar-” “I know. Good for you.” LLB exited the room as Bold ate his right index finger like a nervous school girl. “Babylon is gonna DIE tonight... and then, I’ll get Linz!!” A small rise began in the center of Jeremy Bold’s pants as the Owner flicked his earring once more. “Lucky, lucky man.”
