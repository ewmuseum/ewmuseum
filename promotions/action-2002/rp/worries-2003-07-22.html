Worries.

July 10th, 2003 'No one would think any less of you if you just took the show off. I'll even make the call for you.' Julie chased her husband around their upstairs. Through doors; in closets. He was busy packing clothes- Intent on being in Toronto for Pressure Point. Though his body ached, he knew he couldn't let this incident control his life. He couldn't let it affect him. For the most part, he had done a successful job of tuning out her nagging. He turned around, stopping as he walked quickly down the hall, and demanded her to, 'Stop.' He ran a hand through his short, brown hair, and continued, 'Just stop. I don't need this right now, Jewels. What I need is a pair of cleans socks, but you seem to have hidden them from me.' True. They were currently stashed away in the (downtairs kitchen) oven. Jeff couldn 't cook. He didn't even attempt such things. No one wants to be responsible for burning their home to the ground. She knew they would be safe there. Julie wrapped her arms around Jeff's neck and hugged him close. 'I'm just concerned, that's all,' she responded, kissing him, 'I just don't trust airplanes, or the people on them, and I don't trust your judgment. You’re tired, you need rest.' Julie took her eyes away from his. 'But if you feel you need to go, that's your choice, isn't it?' Jeff nodded slowly. 'If you're concerned about my judgement- come with me. I'm sure the writers could use you in one of their many LLB DEppreciation Night segments. We'll speak to them, alright?' 'Thank you.'
