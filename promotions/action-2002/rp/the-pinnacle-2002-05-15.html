Action! — Tales

The Pinnacle.

15 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Five months ago... “Hello.” “Hey Roland. It’s Reed.” “How’s it going?” “Good. You?” “I’m okay. What’s up?” “Not much. Say... about this Fed I’m going to run, Action Wrestling...” “Yeah I know Reed. You want me in it. Look, we’ve known each other for a long time. Basically since we were born... I understand you need me, but-” “We signed Chris Fox.” “You’re joking.” “Na. We did.” “Really?” “Chris Fear too.” “Who else can you promise?” “Bobby Minio’s a maybe. Sean Cylear... Shawn Steele... hey, there’s a whole pool full of them. What do you think?” “It’s interesting, Reed. Interesting.” “What is?” “How deep your pockets run.” “I know. This is how bad I want you in. I remember PIW. You ruled there... but I know you weren’t finished either. Johnson pulled the plug on you, and I- I won’t!” “Reed, you’ve ran over twenty more Feds than Jim.” “Yes, however... this is Action! If I get you in, along with these guys, and mind you, Joey Malone and Syphon Fission have signed too... I’LL GET RATINGS!” “I- I-” “World Championship Title Belt. I can give you it.” “You can?” “I’m the Owner. I can do ANYTHING I want.” “...” “Laughing? Is that what I hear Rowe?” “Okay. It’s a deal... but I want the Gold.” “FUCKING YES!” “That is the only reason I’ll join. To continue what I did in the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling... got me?” “I promise.” “By ANY means necessary.” “Hey... I’m Reed Young. You can trust me.”
