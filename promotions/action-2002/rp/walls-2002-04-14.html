Action! — Tales

Walls.

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/14/02 It seemed like it was happening all over again. First the feel... then a best friend in the Owner... the new bond... and now Bobby Minio. It was beginning to look like the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling to Roland Ermann... to LLB. Everything was coming into that form... and just the way he had left it. LLB couldn’t help but smile at that moment. He broke the ice cold picture upon his face only a handful of times a year, and this was one of them. For if PIW was reforming, he’d be able to end it off after all. “Testify.” He said, standing from his bed and turning towards the window. “Testify.” The media was right, but anyone could really see it. Bobby was left the same. A loner. Against the old team. On other days, maybe LLB would dwell. Maybe he would get mad, pissed off... a total image of pure fire, but today he wasn’t that. Minio could have his day to stand. To rule over all. To become a man in the Courts again. “It won’t last long.” He knew the verdict. What had made LLB more bitter, even taken him off his game in jOlt and somewhat of the Asylum was the fact he wasn’t able to finish PIW’s Case. He, after all, had everyone right were he needed them. Welcome Action. Welcome PIW Era Three? LLB rubbed his fists together, the smile still over his face and a determined look like never before. And closing his eyes LLB raised his right hand, still in a ball... crashing it down moments after. “Begin again... and this time, there’s no fucking stopping. No fucking stopping for anything. Let them all be given their verdicts’. We’re right where we fucking let go.” An evil laugh, a last glance into the window... and LLB was off. Off to end what could now finally be.
