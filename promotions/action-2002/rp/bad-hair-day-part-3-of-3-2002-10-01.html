Bad Hair Day (Part 3 of 3)

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/01/02 OPEN The stage was set, every hair was in place. For the “coolness” was most certainly there. With the hip new hair cut, Christopher Fox was once again speeding down the highway as his sweet silver Pikachu Beetle glimmered in the red-orange sun. Title: Bad Hair Day (Part 3 of 3) Date: 10/10/02 10:00 am EST Place: Train As Fox bashed his head to his newly bought Moist CD, Pikachu sat in the back “looking out the window”. or so we thought. Fox “Boy oh boy! This is a really goooooooooood CD Pika Peek! The name of the band is just like my ice cream! Moist! Hehehe! Ah, the sun feels good, don’t it bud-o?” .... Fox “Yeah, it is nice out! Anyway, today me and you are going on a trip to the mountains! Yeah, it’s gonna be fun! We get to ride on a train and stuff! It’s a perfect place to show off my new style of hair!” .... Fox “That’s right, I meant hair style.... BIG TIME!!!! Boy, I’m hyper! I guess I shouldn’t have had all those free coffees back at that gas station!” With a few clicks of the volume button, the music floods any chance of further conversation. Fox just jams his fingers on and off of the wheel as Pikachu sort of vibrates from the shockwaves. Fox “I love this song!! We are, we are silver!!” Little did our hero know, but he was less than three minutes away from exiting the highway to the train station. Fox turns the volume down, as all the way from the far left lane Christopher skids into the exit one! At least a dozen horns could be heard from this reckless driver. Who gave him his license anyway? Fox “Heto people.” Fox flings his middle finger to the cars as he rolls through a red light, nearly causing two trucks to collide with each other. ‘Kid’ finishes his safe car ride as he comes close to hitting a parked car while he rolls in the train lot. Fox “Here at last, Pikachu!” Grabbing his best bud in the whole wide world, Chris ejects himself out of the driver side window. Fox “I really need to get that door fixed!” Fox walks into the station ticket booth, as suddenly, a shady character wearing all black walks up to the Beetle and puts a crowbar to the window, but before he could bust the door open, it fell off! The thief is so freaked out he just runs in terror! The scene goes back to Fox, smiling. Fox “Works every time! Pft, and they think I’ll buy the club!” .... Christopher makes his way to a teller and pleads his case. Fox “Ummm heto.... I’m lost and I don’t have any money, can I ride the train for free?” Teller “Come with me, ya!?” As the teller speaks, the scene displays the tellar. He was a tall, muscular, oriental man.... in his mid-thirties. Teller “Follow me, ya!?” The teller exits his booth and escorts Fox to the men’s washroom. Tick, tick.... Finally the teller emerges from the washroom first, as he zips his pants and climbs back into the booth with a big smile on his face. Shortly after Christopher Fox comes out, with Pikachu in hand and a train ticket in the other. As he passes by the teller he says goodbye. Fox “Bye bye Mr. Jeep! Thank you for the ticket!” Fox winks as he goes outside to wait for the train. Boy that was fun.... but it was worth it! I got a free ticket! One final man limps out of the washroom, as he paces towards the teller booth. This man was holding his jaw. And with a rude attitude, he goes up to women teller to plead his case. Man holding jaw “What the f*** was that? I have been violated!” Women Teller “What happened?” Man holding jaw “I was just fixing my hair and this teller and some kid holding a Pikachu come in and violate me! The kid held me as this dude right here, f***ed up my **** until he ****!” The whole crowd within a 10 meter radius reacts by “ewwing” and “ahhhing”, as once Fox heard this he slips Pikachu into his pocket. Boy, calm down! People these days, their just so cheap.... wait, was I just cheap? No comment. And as the man kept throwing his fit to the women teller, the train had come in. Fox races to it as he grabs a seat as quickly as possible while covering Pikachu. Starting to day dream, a pretty lady walks over and takes a seat beside the self proclaimed pop star of the century, even though he listens to rock. Fox “H-hi.” Fox vibrantly sniffs his nose in. Lady “Hello, what’s your name? I’m Hillary.” Fox “H-hillary.... I-I’m C-Chris, you can c-call m-me C-Christopher.” Fox extends both of his shaky cold hands toward Hillary. H-Hillary.... H-huge bone! BIG TIME!!!! Hillary shakes the right hand of Fox. YES!!!!!!!!!!!! She picked the RIGHT hand! She must be a slut! Whoo! Fox “S-so where are you head.... heading too?” Hillary “I’m going to a village. It’s a few hours south of here.” Fox “Wow.... BIG TIME!” Just as Fox was getting settled in, a bright, tall man walks on board and sits across from both Fox and his new found gem. Oh what the f*** do you think you’re doing? She’s mine! I’ve claimed her! Or rather the fireman has rose.... I mean spoken! The train sets off as the man and Hillary begin to talk now while Fox begins to lose interest. Some time passes as Fox finally sets into a deep sleep and comes close to falling directly on to Hillary. Luckily she pokes him to wake him up. Fade Out. Fade In. The scene fuzzes as Fox manages to open his eyes. The car is almost empty! Only Hillary, the man, and a few other remain in their seats. Fox had been out for a couple hours as the train stops. Engineer On Speaker “Village of Drane. I repeat we’ve entered the village of Drane.” Hillary “Oh my, Christopher this is my stop. I’m afraid this is goodbye.” With Fox half-awake, regretful things were said. Fox “Oh fuck a cow bitch!” Luckily Fox muttered this vulgar and rude comment.... and Hillary took it as something else. Hillary “You’re too kind. See ya around.” The man across from Hillary stood and finally spoke. Man “C’mon honey, enough playing around with these boys.” The couple exit as Fox was left a half-awaken embarrassment to himself. Since the doors were open, a couple of bugs manage their way in. A fruit fly lands on Fox’s nose which fully wakes him up. Fox “AHHHHH! Where am I?” Sturdiest “Sir, you’re on a train. The next stop is the city.” Fox “Huh? No way Jose! It’s the mountains! Isn’t this the 612?” Sturdiest “No, it’s the 512.” Fox “WHAT!? I’m on the wrong train!? Does this train go to the mountains?” Engineer On Speaker “All sturdiest please report to car 6, this is a Code Bar. Repeat, Code Bar.” Sturdiest “Oh, I’m sorry I have to go. It’s a Code Bar, someone in first class spilled their martini.” Leaving Fox in an empty car the train puffs on, as each time the car stops and the doors open, Fox was looking but every stop they made wasn’t the mountains.... in fact they were going in the opposite direction! Fox grabs another nap between two stops, as the next one he’s fiddling with his hair. And after a few more stops of messing with his hair, the final stop comes as the doors open and a fairly peculiar face emerges. Indeed, Fox’s hair was back to its original state. All the rubbing and thinking had returned it to normal. The cool Fox was back to being a bowl-cut Momma’s boy. Not that he changed that much but.... his confidence has decreased noticeably. Fox trots off the train. He was back at the station where he “bought” his ticket. He then walks inside. Mr. Jeep, the teller whom Fox got into an adventure with was perching in a standing position right beside the door. Mr. Jeep “Hey, it’s you again, ya! I’m on my break, wanna have some fun?” Fox stares straight ahead, foreseeing his next adventure, and since he isn’t that cool anymore.... well, he accepted it. Fox “Washroom, be there in five.” Fox’s new style might not have lasted that long, but the moral of his story is change is bad. For Fox.... he just had a bad hair day. CLOSE
