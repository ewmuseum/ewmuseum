The Family Circus: Prologue

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/12/2002 'Just for the record, my parents suck.' Coral Avalon told Allison Lindum, as they approached the doorstep of the Avalon mansion on the outskirts of New Orleans, Louisiana. With college over, Allison had more time to be with Coral and his silly occupation, and she had managed to convince Coral to spend some time with her in New Orleans before he has to fly back up to Canada. Coral was wearing a pair of black slacks and a gray button-up shirt. He was thinking about wearing a shirt from Action!, but the truth was that the bastards at the marketing department still haven't made a Coral Avalon shirt, since they all thought Coral was just some jobber who hung around Joey Malone a lot. In actuality, Coral has had little to no contact with Joey since Keri left, and whenever Coral could speak with him, he didn't say anything more than two words before he was gone again. Not to mention the fact that Coral had been busy dealing with the King of All Idiots, Jeff Garvin. Allison, herself, was in a short-sleeved black shirt and a pair of jeans. Her shoulder-length black hair blew idly in the slight breeze. She seemed a lot more relaxed about this whole idea than Coral did. 'Why?' Allison inquired. It was pretty much her idea that Coral come to see his parents, anyway. 'It's kind of difficult to explain.' Coral said, closing his eyes and trying desperately to not remember what he was remembering. It all came in flashes, each a little harder than the rest. 'Get out of this house! You're a disgrace!' 'You're not worthy of the Avalon name!' 'I didn't want the Avalon name in the first place!' 'Coral?' Allison asked, putting her left hand on Coral's shoulder, snapping him out of his bad memories. 'What's wrong?' 'No-nothing,' Coral lied, 'Nothing at all.' 'Well, whatever. We're here, aren't we?' 'Unfortunately.' Coral said under his breath, which Allison couldn't decipher, and decided she was better off not knowing what he had just said. Allison decided to knock on the door, since she knew that Coral wasn't going to do it. Coral sighed, and awaited the presence of someone from his constantly strange family. A girl, aged seventeen, opened the door. She looked almost like the polar opposite of Coral. Coral looked neat, very organized, and casual to a fault. This girl almost looked gothic, with dark makeup, dark hair, and all black clothes. 'Hello, sis.' Coral sighed as he saw her, not really surprised that his little sister hadn't gotten over this phase in her life just yet. The girl frowned when she laid eyes on Coral, and sulked. 'Oh, great, it's you,' she said, 'You have a lot of nerve to show your face back here, Coral.' Coral was quick to respond, 'Wasn't my idea.' Allison elbowed him in the rib in response to this, which drew the girl's attention to Allison. 'Oh,' was the girl's response, 'I didn't know you finally met somebody,' she said with a sigh, 'Well, I guess I can go get Mom, but don't expect her to greet you warmly.' 'I'm not.' She left to go find her parents, as Allison turned to look at Coral. 'What's her deal?' 'That's my younger sister, Lily,' Avalon explained, 'She's the least creepy person from the rest of the Avalon family, believe it or not.' Allison was surprised. If the goth girl was the least creepy person in his family, who knows who else she'd meet here? Coral smiled when she saw the change of expression, 'Yeah, I'm as shocked as you.' A few moments later, an older woman, in her mid-fifties, approached the door. Whereas Lily Avalon was black and dark, this woman was so multicoloured that she hurt the eyes of both Coral and Allison. She wore a multicoloured dress, had her hair all swirled up from the top of her head, had the worst-looking makeup in existence on, and generally looked like a freakshow. She frowned upon seeing both Coral and Allison, making her makeup look even worse than it did before. The woman, Irene Avalon, was the law and order in the Avalon household. 'Come grovelling back, have we?' she spoke in a condescending, demeaning tone, 'It figures that you couldn't make it on your own, Coral. After everything we have taught you!' Coral sighed. This was going to be a long day. 'It's Christmas, Mom. Look into it.' 'Nobody informed me.' Irene said with a roll of her eyes and an arrogant smirk on her face. 'Well, since you're here, and I guess since it's the holidays, I can call Rosefeld to show this... girl... around. Who is she?' 'She's my girlfriend. Allison Lindum.' Coral said. With that, Allison held her hand out for a handshake. 'Pleasure to meet you.' she said. Irene merely stared at her hand for a moment, as if she didn't know what a handshake was. As a matter of fact, she didn't. She was taught in her upbringing that bowing was the proper way to introduce oneself. Nonethless, she ignored Allison and turned to call on the butler of the Avalon mansion. 'Rosefeld! Come and show our guests around!' A minute later, an aged man walked to the front door of the Avalon residence. He looked to be pushing eighty, and he wore a tuxedo with white gloves. His name was Alan Dodge Rosefeld, a man who has worked as the butler for the Avalon family for three generations. He might not have looked like much, but he was a welcome member of the household no matter who greeted him. As he walked up, Irene casually walked away from them, content with getting the hell away from her estranged son. Coral greeted Rockfeld with a wave. His only friend in the entire residence. 'Hiya, Rosey.' he greeted. 'Ah! Good sir Coral. Welcome back,' Rosefeld said in his kindly, gentle manner, a welcome change from Irene's meanness, 'How have your travels fared?' 'Lots of bumps and bruises, and not much else. I do want you to meet somebody, though.' Coral said, before he reached over and pulled Allison in front of him, holding her by her shoulders. 'This here's Allison. She's my guest, my best friend, and pretty much the reason I've come back,' he said, 'Before we go see Dad, I want her to meet the rest of the family.' 'I see,' he said, scratching at his chin, 'Come with me, then. Lady Emmy is the closest to us.' He walked right past Coral and Allison, heading for a greenhouse right in the middle of the courtyard. Allison looked at Coral in confusion. Coral simply sighed. 'What did I tell you about my family, Ali? They're all nuts.'
