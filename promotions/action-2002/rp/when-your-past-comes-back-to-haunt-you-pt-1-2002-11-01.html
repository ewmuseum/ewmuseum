When Your Past Comes Back To Haunt You. : pt. 1

1 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov. 1, 2002 [foreword.] It had been a long time since the world has been blessed with the presence of Lactose the Intolerant, the runt member of the Legion of Dairy. Lactose had never had his share of the lime light that his colleagues had. Lactose never really caught on like his friends did, while he arguably had more talent than any other member, the fans just didn't like his sarcasm and odd sense of humor. He felt that wrestling was no longer the place for him, that he just didn't belong. So he left. Without giving it a second thought, he packed his backs and went home. You never how great something was until it's gone. Lactose changed his mind. He would come back. Just like that the ball began rolling. [end.] The shot opened up to living room inside the small home of the man that would become one of Action! Wreslting's latest stars, Lactose the Intolerant. We caught a short glimpse of one of the most 'controversial' men in wrestling as the camera panned over the room showcasing all that Lactose's living room had to offer. No trophies, just one plaque from his day in the Specktacular Wrestling League and a painting of a boat on the water hanging to the wall adjacent his plaque. Lactose was relaxing in his recliner as usual, doing what he had seemingly become accustomed to since his retirement from wrestling all that long time ago. Watching soap operas. As Lactose continued to watch his soaps on TV, a load knock was heard at his front door. Lactose grumbled, 'who could that be?' Lactose slowly pulled himself from his chair and staggered over to the door. He glanced through one of the windows fixed on the door and caught sight of the United States Postal Service logo on the hat outside. 'Ah, mail's here!' an enthusiastic Lactose cried out. Lacotse quickly threw the door open to see that the mailman only had only one letter in his hand, this brought the total amount of mail that Lactose had received since his retirement to: one. The mail man smiled, 'excuse me sir, but I have a certified letter that I'm going to need you to sign for.' Lactose nodded, trying to hold back his excitement. 'Right, right. So, uh, where do I sign?' The mail man held out a clipboard and pointed toward the bottom. 'Uh, right there sir.' Lactose pulled a pen out from the top of clip board and signed anxiously on the line and snatched the letter from the mail man, only to slam the door shut in his face. Lactose laughed as he held the envelope up towards the light for further inspection. He could make out the contents of the envelope so he, instead, ripped it opened as the contents spilled out onto the floor. One plane ticket and a Post-it note. Lactose leaned over and picked the note off the floor, reading it aloud: 'See you soon exclamation exclamation exclamation Reed.' Lactose sighed as he started down the hall. That sudden feeling of déjà vu overcame him. to be cont.
