Fond Realization (3 of 3)

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/10/02 He walked conceivably along the constrict trail, taking in every solitude of energy neighboring him.          The surreal thoughts of; demons, death ghouls, and angels of the underworld, filled his head quickly. They confiscated all of the space and confided it into his sanctum. A world unlike his. He took place in a dream that felt so real. The utterly thought of it ending ... frightened him. Even with the atmosphere which trapped him, he breathed. Large gasps of air circulated vagrantly throughout his entire body. He achieved strength quickly, enough so to pick up the pace. It went from a slow walk to a quicker jog.          The ground in which was covered seemed endless now.          Carelessly, his thoughts treaded on. The dream proceeded as planned. Nervously, he opened his eyes even farther. The images faded and became one with reality. Wires, cords, cables, and filaments obstructed his view. The drowsy feeling allowed him to step in and out of realism. A scurry with death had taken place.          'Is it true?' He watched his life pass him by, seeing nearly everything that had happened to him in his past. The lights flickered on and off in the background allowing him to see portions of the visions. Then ... there was darkness.          'Another sad day in what I call hell.' He rose drenched in sweat. His hair, strung out even more. His eyes looked unwieldy as he tried to force them open and go on out into the daytime world. 'Reality is becoming harder and harder to find nowadays. Seems every time I close my eyes, I'm gone. To a world unlike this, a world that I'm free to live, but not alone.' The eyes focused on the letter like a magnet. Locked on and holding tightly, he stood to his feet and grasped the letter. The seal ... still sealed. The handwriting seemed to get sloppier and sloppier by day.          'I guess it's time.' Meeting up with old family in reality or dream wasn't the greatest thing in his life. The immortal thoughts flogged his mind with fear. The letter needed to be open. The truth needed to be left out. He only had a few ideas on what could be concealed in the envelope. 'The letter inside should tell the truth. It should give the reasons to the bloody killing spree that he went on. It should tell all of his little secrets that took place in the process. Everything ... should come to me once I read it.' He held the envelope up to the light and stared through. It seemed empty. There appeared to be nothing on the inside. The man peeled the letter open as abruptly as possible.          'Do ... what?'          There it was ... the picture. A beautiful female stared back at him from the small photograph. His eyes were glued, literally. He took no smooth movements to take his eyes away. Staring into the eyes that aren't staring back anymore. Looking deep down inside of someone that ... he hadn't saw in years.          'It's her.' The man seemed to fall apart from the inside out. He laid the picture down on the table and laid back into the chair. Soaking up the thoughts, soaking up his reaction from just a glance at the picture. He faded. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
