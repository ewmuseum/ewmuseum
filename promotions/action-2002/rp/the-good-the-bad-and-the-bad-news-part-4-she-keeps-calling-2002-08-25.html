The Good, the Bad, and the Bad News, Part 4: She Keeps Calling

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 7th, 2001. The past. He sits in his locker room, staring at the Bobby Minio shirt on the rack. A PIW logo at the bottom. He missed her birthday, independance day. What a joke it all was. That was the main thing that tore them apart. Her independance. He remembered when they first met. The coincidence that her birthday was a month after his. It all seemed so petty now. The damage was done. He ended the relationship. He cut the cord on that ugly infant that was their love. If only it had the chance to grow. 'Yea fucking Right.' He utters the words, slouched forward with one elbow on his knee, his fist clenching in the air. He was over her now. He even made jokes about her amongst his friends, coworkers, even opponents. She was just a bitter memory. And then, when everything reached an odd level of even, there was a knock on the door. Bobby turned, opening it. Her red hair was the first thing he noticed. That cautious look on her face. She held an envelope infront of him. 'Just... please Read it.' He snatched it from her hand, he felt like a bashful teenager. Her face made his stomach turn. After all that had happened, his only physical reaction was to close the door, and set the letter on the folding table by his locker. It sat there. And it sat. And he watched it. And it sat. And he watched it. About twenty minutes went by of Bobby staring at the letter. He finally gave in to his concious and stood up, grabbing at the envelop, tearing it open. A picture fell out. It was her. And him. They were happy. He watched himself smiled forever in the picture. He almost mimmicked it. 'Perfection through Silence.' He watched the picture longer, almost expecting the two to stop smiling, release each other and battle as they had for the past few months. He expected her to walk off and find someone better ofr her time. he expected for her to rip his chest clean open and pull his heart out, then drop it into a vat of acid. It just sat there. The two of them finally alone in a photograph. He pulled the letter out, reading it over. In summary, it was everything she missed about him. Every little aspect. Down to the last niche. Every curl, every smile, every scent. By the end of the three page letter, Minio was shaking. His hands rumbled. His face fought both tears and impending doom, he looked as if he were about to explode. He calmed. He was settled. He folded the letter, and as he was about to put it back in the envelope, something came over him, something washed over his body and he tore the letter in halves, then again, then again. He tossed it carelessly on his suitcase beside him.
