Action! — Tales

Over.

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The night was young. There LLB sat, in his Locker Room... testify complete. Finally the verdict was told. Christopher Fox was guilty... now he knew it. And even if Fox levelled LLB, stealing the key to the Courtroom, at the moment there would be no worries. The law had proven Christopher’s guilt, going by all the doubters in doing so. LLB was on top. He was where he belonged. The very way the law wanted it to play out. And right now, the system was exactly on track. LLB raised his arms and slammed them both down in victory. ‘Juggernaut’ really couldn’t have been better. It was a given though, that later on, tomorrow or the week after, the media would be all over LLB’s actions. They would trash him. Put him down. Yet in the end they were wrong. The law could not proscribe a verdict that everyone agreed with, all of the time. But was LLB too harsh? Was it all worth it? Follow the footsteps of the lawyer for his past two years. From the law’s standpoint, Fox was the most annoyingly sinful person in the world. Not a care at all. Just love. “Objection.” He muttered. Love had no right in the book of justice; in all which was right. There would be no dwelling. No thinking back. It was straight ahead. Forward with no stopping. The next to object would get the same. Hopefully for them, it wouldn’t be as crude. After all, a lifetime of “love” had to be devastating to throw away... for the party that believed it. LLB broke his frozen stare with a smile. The law ruled all. The night was young.
