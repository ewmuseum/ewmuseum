The Beta: Fission Mailed(3)

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I bet you are wondering why Clark is doing this. Am I not right? Of course I am. I am Fission. I am never wrong. You see, Syphon Fission is in all of you people. From your little babies to your forgotten grandparents, to the everywhere in-between. He is Clark’s made persona to hide weakness and to give “the man” the finger. Remember the old IWO? How many times did you see Syphon as the figurehead behind a rebellion against the suits? Too many. Syphon was unbeatable and was a favorite. You all loved Syphon Fission. When Syphon Fission lost to Samuel Potright, he lost his edge. Clark has never been the most stable of men. He suffers from many mental breakdowns. You could write a novel on how his brain uses them to make up a world. Clark has many personas. After seeing his best friend let hate fester within him and push him away, Clark lost himself. That’s when he became moody. Clark hasn’t found that Fission persona since then. All he has had was the Angel persona. The Angel persona is a anti-hero persona. It does whatever it wants. It saves those he really cares for and does what it really needs to. An Angel persona is not complete without another persona to bond with it. We all have this persona, but in many people, the persona is incomplete. Clark’s career was over, no doubt about it. His character was old and not needed. He was getting replaced because he wasn’t producing like he once did. He hated Joey Malone for that. The IWO World Title reign of Syphon Fission was supposed to last for six months. He was going to defend nightly, and then lose to someone. He was however going to be a fixture in the division for the rest of his career. When Dane Matthews used real fighting tactics to defeat Clark, it forever changed Kosoy’s stance with him. He looked old. He looked beaten. Kosoy went behind closed doors and stated that Fission fight Matthews at his own game. Fission totally decimated Matthews and took back his title, defeating him and Malone. Irony was there. Malone’s showing was enough to make Kosoy take the title off Fission later that week against Potright. Potright was supposed to fall to Donnie Daze. And right afterward… Joey Malone was supposed to bring the title home. Give it to the hero. The rest is blurry. Malone could explain better how he stopped talking to Clark, not wanting to accept that the company was going to run him dry. That’s when I came in. Ender Fission. I am the ultimate revenge. I want fame. I worked so hard for that title. I am Clark’s rage. I am Clark’s fight. I hate everyone and love no one. Clark knows what happened afterward. He threw me away after I injured Kent Anthason. He didn’t want to see more people get hurt in real fighting. Well, Clark was depressed. He dwelled on what had happened. We followed Malone to Legends of Wrestling, but it closed. Action! was the last place. If Malone had just apologized… No. Wouldn’t work. He threw us away like a used fucking condom. Clark is a forgiving one. We are not. So I made a pact with someone. Angel-Ender. We synthesized and became one. After fighting Joey in a real life fight, and getting shafted the way he did, Clark went into relapse. We came out to play. We know all his weaknesses and all his strengths. We know how his mind works and how to manipulate him into decisions. Fission hasn’t let Malone win one battle he didn’t want him to. You see, anytime Malone won a measure of revenge, it was because we wanted to raise his hopes up. Where is he now? Probably along the streets, looking for a rope to hang himself with. No? Who cares anyway. Fission has beaten Malone. All that’s left is the end. I will get the ending I so desire. I will give you all something to remember me by. Hate me still? Yes. Good. Keri is bound somewhere in her own home. I am depriving her slowly of her sanity. I whisper in the most human of voices that Joey failed her. The knight isn’t coming back. She is starting to believe me. She isn’t hard to crack. I have the ultimate weapon to put him where he needs to be. He doesn’t know yet though. He has one last rebuttal that I need him to do. So Joey, if your still alive, heed my warnings. Do not open Pandora’s Box. There is no hope left inside.
