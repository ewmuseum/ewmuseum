This Is The Big League.

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/14/02 Prior to the start of PP35 | Oct 14th, 2002 ------------------ You know how you have those debuts that will just rock your socks off? The kind of debut that just has you saying: 'oh my God, how did they get this guy?' or 'him?! Here?! WHOO!' Yeah, well I regret to inform you... This is not one of those debuts. We were in the thick of it all. Standing dead center in the ring. The fans were growing anxious awaiting their gladiators to come forth and fight the good fight. Just for them. Their mumbles slowly filled the arena as anxious fans were slowly becoming annoyed ones. A faint cry of crackles and guitar slowly came into play. 'it's time.... (it's time... it's time... it's time...) ... it's time... STEP ON UP!!!' Flaw's 'Only the Strong' kicked in as the fans responded the only way they knew how. Utter confusion. If I were to describe the sound the fans began making for you, it would sound something like *rabblerabblerabble*. People were curious. Answers had to be made. Who could it be? Did I leave the lights on in the car again? Do these jeans make my butt look fat? Well sit tight true believer, your answer is walking out through the curtains now. The curtains shifted as a lone figure slowly strolled out onto the stage, in his hand, the almighty 'stick'. His brown hair put up in a spiked doo, his goatee trimmed and well groomed, neatly sitting on his face. His black tights drawn up with a green and gray flame-like design, and a large green skull on the front of the tights. Black tape covered his arms, not like the norm, but in his own, unique style. He was smirking, slowly lifting the microphone to his mouth. But he stopped. He looked over the crowd and raised a hand in a show of silence. Only one thing was wrong in this situation. The crowd hadn't made a peep since his music began playing. 'If you will, please.' He said calmly, 'I have an introduction to make and no time to waste. I have more... important places to be.' The crowd wasted little time responding to the comment. A shower of boos littered the man standing on the stage, still smiling, soaking it all in. Oh how he missed this. This was the way things should have gone. 'I guess it's understandable that you people can't remember the great human being standing before you today....' He paused as he thought for a moment. 'No, now that I think about it, it's not excusable. Too often you get off the hook. Too often the blame gets shifted. Well, no more!' The man's face was turning a dark shade of red. The smile, gone. Times were hard for him. He didn't end up like the others, they were loved. He, on the other hand, was a skapegoat. The 'whipping boy' whenever they did wrong. He had his fill. He reached his boiling point and if wasn't going anywhere, he didn't want 'in' on the business anymore. So he quit. Just like that. Never to return. Sometimes.... You just don't know how good you had it. And you get pulled back in. The man cleared his throat and pointed to the crowd. 'You think you're so f**king special. Like we're here just for you.' he chuckled, 'I'm not. Never was.' 'I risked so much for you people. And for what? Boos? Jeers? To be ignored? I've given my body to the business and I've gotten nothing out of it. I never could as long as I was trapped in the shadows... behind them.' He paused once more as he put a hand on his hip and tried to hold back some tears. 'I've been doing this for the better part of five years. Did you people know that? Did you know how many belts I've held in that time?' He raised his hand in the air and formed a tight fist. 'Zero.' 'Natta. None. Zilch. In five years, I've never know what it's like to be a champion! HOW DO YOU THINK THAT MAKES ME FEEL!?' He was on the verge of breaking down again. Fighting to hold the tears, that were so obvious on his face, back. 'How do you think it feels to never be someone? To know that when you wake up, it's just going to be another punch line in the joke that is your life?' 'And nothing is worse... I mean nothing, than the feeling of when you need a shoulder to cry on and you look around and you see your alone. And that you've been all alone the whole time. You suddenly feel better off dead. But you can't do it. You know that it's just a passing phase. Things will change, it just takes time. But it doesn't. Days turn into weeks, weeks into months and months into years, and you're still that same walking punch line that you were all that time ago.' He lowered his head, running his fingers through his hair. 'Maybe I brought all this upon myself. Look at the name, heh, real clever.' He turned away from the camera and started to leave. Stopping at curtain, turning back around. 'No, I'm not selling out like that. Look at where I am now. Action! Wrestling. And you, you think you're so high and mighty but you don't know anything. You're just jackass losers and that's as good as you'll ever be. Me? I'm going places. Try as hard as you like, pray as much as you want, you can't stop me. Not now. Not ever.' 'I'm Lactose the Intolerant, and I'm in the Legion Of Dairy.' 'Only The Strong' was playing once more as Lactose threw the microphone down. With a look of disgust etched on his face, he pushed the curtains aside and walked back. In a few short seconds the thirty-fourth edition of Pressure Point would go on the air. No one would be the wiser to the actions that just took place. Once again it would be just wrong place, wrong time.
