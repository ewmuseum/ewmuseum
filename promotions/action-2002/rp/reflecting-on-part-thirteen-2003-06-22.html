Reflecting On: Part Thirteen.

22 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Words were being spoken. Words from a preacher. No one heard them though. Their own thoughts were clouding the voice. Everyone's eyes were fixated on the beautiful casket. It was ready to be lowered to the ground. Those those weren't, were crying. On opposite sides of the grave, Conrad Ramsey and Xander Dox stood. Both with their arms behind them, looking down at the one thing they shared in common. Loving Samantha. Only thing they could ever agree on. The two men had not spoken since her death. Not even when Xander and Jessica had gone to Las Vegas. Conrad was there, in the waiting room, but they didn't approach him. They had kept their distance. Both worlds had been shattered. Now, as they stood mere feet apart, they didn't look at each other. Didn't acknowledge that the other was there. Jessica was leaning against her husband, tears in her eyes, holding him around the waist. It was painful. Neither Conrad nor Xander shed a tear. Both had no more left to give. Not a single drop of wetness. Nothing would come. Their pain was far beyond that of simple weeping. It was inside them. Eating away at them. In their brains. Going to be there forever. They were going to be changed men. They didn't know how or what exactly, but they weren't going to be the same. It was going to affect them more than anyone else there. More than the family members that hadn't seen Samantha for years. Not caring. Not wanting to see her until she had died. The preacher had stopped speaking. The Bible had been closed up. Xander and Conrad both stepped forward. Jessica stood back, watching through tearful eyes. They both dropped laid flowers on the casket. They didn't look at each other. Not even a glance. Not even at the flower they were putting down. And then the casket began to lower. Dark clouds began to fill the sky. Rain had been expected but had held off until Samantha was in the ground. Everyone had begun to leave. Feeling their obligation to the dead finished. Xander, his wife, and Conrad were all that remained as the rain began to fall. Sprinkles at first. Heavier with each passing second. None of them had an umbrella. None of them cared. Xander had pulled his wife to him and under the protection of his suit jacket. The rain didn't exist to him. To Conrad either. They just stared. Thoughts drifting to various moments in the past. It's all they had left to carry on. The memories. After hours in the rain, Conrad had gone. Left to return to Kentucky. To try and live his life without the one person he had loved more than any other in his life. If he could. Xander and Jessica had gone home. Not to Kentucky, but rather to Augusta. The home Xander had spent his entire life at. The rain had stopped. The night air was cool. Marcus had walked out onto the deck. Sat in the chair he had sat in many times over the years. He just looked out upon the night sky. 'Goodbye, mom,' he said in a whisper.
