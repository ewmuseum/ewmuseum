An Early Christmas

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-17-02 A friend is a gift you give yourself. -- Robert Louis Stevenson A soft smile came acorss his lips. His plan had worked perfectly, and now he could just sit back and take in the atmosphere that he missed with all his heart. The smell of sweat in the locker room, the roar of the crowd in the arena, the slight bounce in your step from the mat. He remembered it just as it was tonight. The cast had changed a bit, the colors of the ropes even had a subtle change to them. But it was all so similar, and so reminiscient. Tony bent over to untie his shoes. 'I really need to take a shower,' he thought to himself. 'Tony!' Tony looked up to see Flyer walk into the dressing room, white towel draped around his neck, small beads of water still on his skin and in his hair. Tony had to let out another smile, it's been so long since he had seen Flyer in an arena. 'Man, what gives? I thought that you weren't coming back for another year or so? Isn't your back still all messed up?' He was right. Tony did tell him that he wouldn't be able to join him in Action for quite some time, that he was still trying to nurse his back into 100% condition. Fact of the matter is, his back has been healthy for quite some time. 'Yeah man, I did tell you that. But, the day before you told me about signing on with Action, Tammy came and told me. Don't know how she found out before me, but I figured you just wanted it to be a suprise. Anyway, I called Reed later that day about signing up, and we worked things out.' Flyer looked around confused, not sure why this was all kept a secret. 'But why didn't you tell me? You kept acting all bummed about your back and stuff, I thought...' Tony stood up. 'Fly, I talked to Reed, I set you up for that title shot against Joey. And, damn, you were impressive. But I wanted you to have the oppurtunity to lead this place into what I know you can. This was all a gift for you. Merry Christmas Fly.' Tony took a few more steps forward and hugged him. Flyer was shocked, not really sure what to say. A semi-awkward silence engulfed the room for a few minutes, before Tony broke it. 'Thanks for being such a great friend man. The best.'
