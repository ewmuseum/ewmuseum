The Family Circus: John

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/2002 Jonathan Avalon is the patriarch of the Avalon family, at fifty-eight years of age. While his abilities in parenting are indeed questionable given the fact that he allowed for his sons and daughters to grow up and be incredibly strange, he was a firm leader. Plus, a lot of what happened to his family was not his fault. John Avalon was a successful business man, running a multi-million dollar restaurant chain in the Southern United States. However, he wasn't known for this, which is actually the amusing thing about him. He was more known as a professional, world-class fencer. John used his restaurant business to maintain the family fortune, while at the same time perfecting his fencing art. John has won more than his fair share of state fencing competitions, a couple of national titles, and even qualified for Olympic fencing at one time, before a family emergency forced him to pull out. John still fences despite his advancing age, but has been disappointed when he could find nobody to pass on his heritage to. Lily was an artist and didn't like sports. Emmy was a recluse and couldn't interact with people. Elvis was in anger management and would probably just simply throw the sword down and start beating the piss out of people with his fists. And Coral? Coral's ambitions certainly didn't lie in following in his father's footsteps. Currently, John Avalon sat in his study, his favorite place to be, and flipping through one of the many books in the private library. Since reading was the biggest passion(after fencing) of the Avalon family patriarch, this private library had the tendency of putting plenty of public libraries to shame. Of course, JUST when he's settling in for some light reading, his butler, Rosefeld, walked in. He was trailed by Coral Avalon and Allison Lindum. 'Master Avalon, sir,' Rosefeld said, 'You have some guests.' 'Can it wait?' John asked. 'Actually, no, it can't,' Coral said, speaking up, 'Dad.' John looked up. His jaw actually shook as he tried to process the name of the young man standing in front of him, with his arms crossed. '...Coral?' 'Yeah. That's me,' Coral said, 'Been awhile.' 'It has.' A long, long pause soon followed. Rosefeld took the time to excuse himself just before John broke the silence. 'Why are you here?' John asked, 'It's been three years. You didn't want anything to do with us any more.' 'Yeah.' Another long, awkward pause. This time, Coral broke it. 'Allison's the one who convinced me to come.' 'Allison?' John asked. He turned to look at the lovely young woman who stood next to Coral. She merely waved at John. 'Oh.' 'Yeah.' Another in a long series of long, awkward pauses. 'Nice girl,' he commented, before poking his face back into his book, 'If you don't mind, I have some more time to read.' Coral sighed and walked right out of the room. Allison was about to follow him as well, but John grabbed her arm. Allison spun around, surprised, but John put his finger to his lip to keep her quiet briefly. 'Miss... Allison, was it?' 'Yes...' 'There's something you should know about Coral. It's about what happened between us all,' John said, 'It happened about three years ago... please, sit down.' Allison sat down. She prepared herself for a good, long, explaination.
