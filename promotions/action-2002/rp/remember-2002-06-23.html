Remember

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/23/2002 I know how you feel, I'm feeling it too... I hold my heart, I dream of you. I see your face, I feel it, too. Searching skies. I need you. I miss you. Sitting in a hotel room, listening to a borrowed portable CD player, Joey Malone was reflecting on his love and his relationship with Keri. Remember when I first played this song? I'm sure you do, I played it when I first took you to my apartment, about a year and a half ago. I don't even know why I bought the CD; I had never even heard of the guy. Sort of a spur of the moment type of deal, but when I listened to the song, I cried. It kind of fit my situation at the time. I consider it my song. A song for the lovelorn hero. Take this and hold my love for you. In separate times we think as two. In paradise I'll drown in you. Still searching skies. I need you. I want you. And do you remember when I last played this song? I certainly do. I proposed to you while dancing with you to this song. In your apartment. Maybe it was a silly couple relationship type of deal, but I didn't care. I loved you, I wanted you to be mine. And you became mine, just as I became yours. Simple as that. The best day of my life, even if I had to fight your dad. I love you, love you, I love only you... Love you. I wonder if Rashard remembers anything positive, now that he's become the soulless machine he's become? Does he remember happiness, or does he know only anger and despair? Huh. He's in my thoughts more than I'd like him to be. He's like a freaking tumor, and I'd probably name him Rashard. The day when I'm close to getting Keri away from that maniac, and Fission takes her just when I'm about to leave. Well, that sucked. Remember me near, there may be times when it's not right for me to be there. But remember me near. Remember me near, there may be times when it's not right for me to be there. But remember me near. Does Rashard remember when we had that post-Desperate Measures party, last year? It was me, you, Rashard, Simon, David, Kent, and Quinn, and we all went and got absolutely smashed. Kent sang kareoke, and he chose 'My Way' by Limp Bizkit as his song of choice. It was kind of funny to see a bad singer slur his way through a worse singer's horrible atrocity of a song. Not to mention the fact that you, Keri, were our designated driver and you had to deal with six drunkened maniacs all the way to our hotel. Searching for strength. Can I face this day. Blinded by your love we stay. You're always there, you're in my head still chasing skies. I need you. Oh, I miss you. Maybe, maybe not. Maybe I'm just wishfully thinking. But Keri, I'm sorry. I'm sorry for failing you. And I'm sorry that I'm about to break the promise I made last year. I promised you that I would never, ever enter a match like this ever again, for the sake of you and for myself. But as they say... desperate times call for desperate measures, and these times are desperate enough for Carrot Top right now. So, I'm sorry. Just love you, only you, only you... I love you, I love you, I love only you...love you. Till Death Do Us Part. It might just come down to that. I certainly hope not. Remember me near, there may be times when it's not right for me to be there. But remember me near. Remember me near, there may be times when it's not right for me to be there. But remember me near. - 'Remember', BT
