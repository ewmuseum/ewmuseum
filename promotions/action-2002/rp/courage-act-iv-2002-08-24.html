Courage - Act IV

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/24/2002 'Listen, Allison... I know I haven't known you for very long, but... it's just that ever since you left, my heart's been aching. My work ethic that was once without equal has now faltered and given way. I can't go on like this, Ali... and...' Coral hesitated. 'Aw, crap. Where did that come from?' he asked himself. He was reciting to himself what he was going to say to Allison if he managed to meet with her as he walked along the second floor of the Arizona Mills mall in Phoenix. Of course, upon his reaction to what he had said, he had quite a few people looking at him as if he was insane. They might be right. 'Man, nobody's gonna want to love me if I keep saying stuff like that.' he said to himself, softly. His point was further buried in him when people were walking away from him as quickly as possible. Coral frowned at this. 'Why, oh why, did I come here?' he said to himself. There were young people all around who were just kind of snickering in Coral's direction, mainly because Coral was talking to himself. Coral wasn't sure if he should continue to gain attention by doing something manly, like ramming his own skull into a nearby pillar, or go out and play in traffic, or even just randomly scream 'COBRA!' and jump off the second floor and into a nearby fountain. That might not have been a bad plan. Finally, after an hour of walking through the god forsaken mall, Coral decided to sit down and contemplate what it was he came here. He came here on a hunch, and he was beginning to have a growing suspicion that his hunch had failed. He stood up and started to walk away, and that would've been the end of it had he not caught a glimpse of a young black-haired woman on the other side from where Coral stood, that looked much like Allison. There was only one way to find out. ...and tripping over a trash can on your way over to this woman wasn't it.
