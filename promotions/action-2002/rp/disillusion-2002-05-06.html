Disillusion

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/06/2002 Seems you created your own illusion, Fuelled by an image of me. Well, I couldn't stay at your side, It wouldn't be right, A picture that I just don't see. Fantastic. Fan-fucking-tastic. It's bad enough that a psychopath, who kidnaps my wife at gunpoint, wants to fight me for no reason other than to 'do to me what I did to him'. That, I can live with. I have no problems with him carrying a shovel in the ring with me, either. Maybe I deserve it for something I did in a previous life, that's fine. He didn't have to do this, though. That contract that I signed, no, was forced to sign... I can't even think about it. I don't want to think about it. Sometimes, I wish I could just lie to myself and say that the stipulations for this match that I'm forced to fight in weren't all true. But that's just lying. Running away, like Rashard does with himself. I decided to be upfront and told Keri what the contract stipulated. She just freaked out, not believing it. I showed her the copy of the contract I have, with the English translation, and she couldn't take it any more. She ran into our bedroom, slumped down against the door so I couldn't open it, and started crying loud enough for me to hear her in the creepy old basement we have. She couldn't take it. I really don't blame her. Now I have to face Rashard, again, in the ring. I've beaten him before, but now, I'm not so sure. In fact, I'm seriously doubting my chances at a time when I absolutely must stay confident. The way he had just jumped on me and pounded on me last week was something I wasn't used to. At all. I didn't know he even had it in him. How am I supposed to beat someone who takes what I can dish out and come back at me twofold? He took my best shots the last time we met and he still came at me, full throttle. I barely came out of that encounter with my head intact. How am I supposed to fight him? How am I supposed to fight him, knowing that I'll lose everything when I lose? It's too much. Even for me to take. Why do you have to make it so complicated? Can't it just be beautiful? -'Disillusion', Badly Drawn Boy
