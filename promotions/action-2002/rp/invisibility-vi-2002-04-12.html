Invisibility - VI

12 April 2002

Date: New Part: 4/12/2002 October 16th, 1992 (two years later) Mesa High School: Mesa, Arizona, United States The last year. The senior year had always been said to be the most fun, and the most memorable. It is a time for goodbyes and new beginnings. And as Keri and Bonham entered this memory, Bonham spoke up about the situation. 'This is the completion of Joey's evolution.' he said. 'His senior year.' There was only one person that were presently walking through the halls of Mesa High School on that day. Joey Malone walked down the hall to his favorite hangout during lunch. He never ate lunch in the school cafeteria any more, prefering to hang out together with Keri in 'Mr. Wizard''s room. But today was a different story. He opened the door. And standing there was the high school senior equivelant of a beast. His name was Rashard Clark. He was new to Mesa High. In the first few months since he started in Mesa, his football acumen had led Mesa's football team to a tremendous string of victories. He was a lot bigger, and a lot stronger than Joey was at the time. But that didn't matter for Joey, because Rashard had done something that bothered him. Immensely. He had his hands on Joey's girl. 'What the hell?' he asked. With Rashard's attention diverted, Keri slips herself out of his grip and moved toward an exit. She left, leaving Joey alone with Rashard. Well, Joey wasn't one to just go off like that, and he assumed that it was Rashard who made the advance. 'What're you doing?' Joey asked Rashard. Rashard just grunted. 'Why do you want to know?' Rashard asked, rather oblivious to the fact that Joey and Keri had built a strong relationship between each other for the past two years. 'Is she your girlfriend or something?' Joey nodded. 'I see.' Rashard said, before brushing past Malone. Before he left the room, though, he offered Joey one last piece of advice. Advice that still bothered him to this day. 'Take care of her, then. You never know when someone's gonna take her from you.' And with that, the memory froze. 'Rashard Clark? That was Rashard Clark!?' Keri asked, very, very confused. 'Yes.' Bonham answered. 'Gridiron god and wrestling superstar. Syphon Fission. He has had an impact on Joey's life even back then.' 'I don't... seem to remember this.' Keri said. 'I thought I would, though.' 'Well, I can't really show any memories or anything, but Rashard fell in love with you, knowing full well that you were with Joey. They were competing for your affection, but by the time the day for prom came, you had to make a decision.' Bonham said. 'What do you mean?' 'You had to choose between the two of them for the senior prom.' The memory continued, but it was not the same one from before. It was just Joey sitting alone in 'Mr. Wizard's' office, reading a book, but generally seeming nervous. A lot of thoughts seemed to be drifting in his head. He was hoping, so hoping, that the girl that he loved so much would choose him for prom. He didn't think that his heart could take the idea of Keri choosing Rashard. His fears would be answered as Keri walked into the room and sat down in the desk in front of him. She then turned around and sat on her knees, now facing Joey. 'Hey.' she said, softly. Joey looked up from his book. 'Hello, Keri.' 'Joey, I'm accepting your offer for the prom.' Joey was about to go back to his book, but then he does a double take. 'You're kidding! I thought you were going with Rashard!' Keri giggled. 'He's tried everything to 'win me'. Flowers, chocolates, everything. I can appreciate that, but there's more to it than just that, you know?' Joey took a shot in the dark on what she was talking about. 'Friendship?' Keri smiled. 'Friendship.' Memory pause. We're used to it by now. 'So, Rashard got the shaft. So, Julie Malone caught him with the question after he was told, and he was probably drunk enough in his sorrow to say 'yes'.' Bonham said. 'This is where the problem comes in.' 'Problem?' Keri asked, eyebrows raised. 'Daniel Phillips.' Bonham answered. 'He had this major crush on Joey's sister at the time. After Julie asked Rashard for prom, Daniel asked her for prom. She told him that Rashard asked her. Daniel was not happy, and he plotted revenge, and... ah, hell. I'll just show you.' Bonham grabbed Keri by her hand and walked out through the door of the classroom. And this is where things turned for the strange and unexplainable.
