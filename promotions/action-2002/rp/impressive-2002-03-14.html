Action! — Tales

Impressive?

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03-14-02 It was a new feeling for Rahl, a feeling he couldn't describe. He had a horrible showing, but it was his first match in such a major wrestling promotion. Everyone else in Action had already had a career going for them Chris Fear, LLB, Chris Fox, Joey Malone, Syphon Fission, Shawn Steele and Matt Remicho. *** Rahl was still in the backstage area even though the show had been over for a couple of hours. The janitor had started to clean the once half-full arena up. The seats were tarnished, the floor was covered with nacho trays, and empty coke bottles. Rahl was just getting ready to leave for another city along for another match at an Action house show. Areumna'derin grabbed his big gray bag and walked out of the locker room that he was sharing with 2 or so more wrestlers. Areumna'derin wasn't big enough for his own locker room like some of the other Action wrestlers. Rahl slammed the door shut behind him and made his way to were his taxi was waiting for him when he saw a sign posted on the wall. ATTENTION ACTION WRESTLERS! You will be paid every 2 weeks depending how good your showing is. If you produce low quality work, then you will receive a low quality paycheck. If you perform like LLB does each week, well you won't receive an LLB paycheck but you know what I mean. So don't screw up! Rahl couldn't believe it. He had a horrible showing, the bookers already hated him for his looks, and the wrestlers hated him for the way he acted. The fans hated him because they don't even know who he is. Was it cause he didn't allow anyone to see his face? It wasn't his fault. People don't and won't give Rahl a chance they hate him for 2 things, his race and the way he acts. He doesn't belong in Action, he doesn't belong anywhere. *** Rahls eyes grew large in size as his head felt like it was about to explode he was dizzy and he had images running through his head. ARGHH! Rahl let out a bellow he remembers the firing blaze rushing out from the car. Rahl began to sweat heavily and started to breath harder then normal. Yet again Rahl flashed again his eyes grew big again. YOU... FUCKING... PRICK... I’LL... GET... YOU!! Rahls eyes shut on him and he collapsed to the ground.
