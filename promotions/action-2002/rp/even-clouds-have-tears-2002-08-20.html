Even Clouds Have Tears

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 20, 2002 The sky was gray, a little bit symbolic of how his life was. Jonny’s life that is, our General is letting his loss get the best of him. Yeah, he won his match against Logan Snyder, but he lost his only friend he had in this foreign world. He lost his horse. Has your dog ever been hit by a car? Has your cat ever gotten out of the house and failed to return? Have you ever experienced the knife-in-the-heart that is the experience of losing a pet. In many ways, a pet could be a better friend than a human...pets don’t have opinions, pets don’t have the pitiful ability to hurt feelings, pets can’t insult you. Jonny had consider Ginger a close friend, and Airborn Tyler had taken her. He had taken Jonny’s close friend. He sat on the roof, pondering over all this and still trying to get used to the neverending pit in his heart. Still trying to cope with his lack of an appetite. Still trying to get over the constant needling headache that pierced the area regarding his temple every occasional second. Jonny might have lost his close friend, but he was slowly gaining a new acquaintance--Pain. This was the first time Jonny had ever taken a seat on the top of a skyscraper. He thought that Airborn Tyler would be somewhere in the city, and Jonny could see the entire city from where he was sitting. He was a cloud. He had to keep on telling himself that because he knew clouds didn’t fall. Jonny felt like falling off the building, and he had to fight the urge to let the weight on his shoulders cause him to fall. He was a cloud. He was a cloud without a friend. Jonny stood up on the ledge and looked down at the street below him. So many advances in technology. Cars, bikes, lights. It all muddled his mind so much when he really thought about it. A gunshot rang out in the distance...murder had been evident in his time, but in this age the sin was committed daily and seemingly without consequences. Men would take women into dark alleyways, rape them and plunge themselves into their fruit, and then kill them. It made Jonny sick to his stomach that the United States had become such a place. He was a cloud. He looked at the ground and stuck a foot out off the ledge. Clouds don’t fall. Jonny sighed and took his foot back. With a final look over the city, he turned around and walked to the stairwell. Clouds don’t fall...they slowly release their droplets and disappear. Jonny was going to go release his droplets now, his tears. As much as he wanted to though, he couldn’t disappear.
