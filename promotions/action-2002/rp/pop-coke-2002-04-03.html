Pop! Coke?

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/03/02 Title: Pop! Coke? Date: 04/03/02 9:00 pm EST Place: Oldies Diner OPEN Ok, here! Chris Fox slowly but surely gets out of his parked Geo on the way to Pressure Point Five, wanting to know if there were any hotels’ nearby so Mommy, -who was already asleep- could feel more comfortable. Fox, the playful guy he is, stops in a small town with a diner and a dance club. Have no fear though, Chris would make sure he’d hit that before the night was through, but first he had to find a hotel. So ‘Kid’, decides to ask for directions at the diner, which they were parked beside. Chris walks to the door taking in some of the fresh air. The moon can clearly be seen as no stars are in the sky tonight. Oh boyz! It’s awfully late! I hope these hooligans can give me directions! Erection Chris? No, silly conscience! I said direction! Jeeeeez! I already told you, I will go to the lady dancing place later! Chris goes to open the door just as two hefty, bulky men with long beards walk out, shoving Chris aside. The two men kiss before getting on a Motorcycle and driving off. Ewwwwwww! Homo-Lesbians! Well, Mommy says they make the world go round. I think she was cow crapping me, though! Wait! I didn’t mean to say a bad word! Hold on.... No one can hear me! I am talking in my head! Boy, if someone was like, reading what I thought, I’d go mad! Chris opens the door and walks into the misty smoky diner, taking a seat in front of the bar on a swivel chair, waiting to be served. Fox “Whoa! These chairs spin! Weeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee!” Fox amuses himself until the one they call “chef” with the messiest apron approaches to Fox. It seems like time stood still for a minute or two as Fox stopped swivelling ‘round and looked eye to eye with the chef. Chef “I am da ma’in Chhuef h’ere. W’hatt’ll it bee?” Fox looks confused and tries to answer to the best of his ability. Fox “You are the madam of the house here? What’d you say?” Chef “I’m just barkin’ ya! What do ya want Hun?” Uh oh. Another Homo-lesbian! He even called me Hun. Oh man! Oh, I know, just play it cool. Make friends and beat the directions to a hotel out of him! Erections Chris? No no! Stop that! LATER! Fox “Ugh…. I’ll, I’ll, I’ll have a Pop pwease!” At least half of the people in the diner look over to Fox in a mysterious way. Chef “Hey n’ow, wha kind?” Air Tenses. Nerves Excel. Fox…. Ice creams. Fox “I’ll have a Coke!” The weather changes drastically all of a sudden and a light rainstorm starts. The sound of the rain hitting the diner creeps Christopher out. Then everyone in the diner looks over to Fox. They all get up and walk toward him. Creek, Crack…. Creeeeeeeeek, Crrrrraaaack…. The floor almost sounds like it’s out to get Fox too, but by that time the 20 of them get to Christopher, Pop! Chris is amazed. Everyone’s cheering for him.... but why? Hooligan #1 “He’y Chrtoo’fur Ox right? He this t’oown al’wayz watchez you on the Acti-on Box! We calll it dthat, but is’ a TV.” Hooligan #2 “YEAH! You rock th’sat Chrt Feers ass at the Rentsentmint pay p’er view!” Fox in surprise, replies. Fox “Hey, What’s going on?” Then, another person walks into the Diner. Wearing a black business suit. The air tenses a bit more. He walks up to Fox and explains. ??? “Hello Christopher, My name Yusuki Yogoslavisskison. A Japanese Action film star. My friends call me “Debby”, you can too. Anyway’s I was sent here to get my next big star to join me in my new movie: Debby Strikes Back! And I told the people here to corner a famous person if he ever came to this town. Everyone was acting so weird because they weren’t sure of who you were at first.” Chris, stunned, nods his head and checks out Debby’s suit. Chris rambles to himself as Debby talks. Debby “I want to build an empire of awesome Japanese action movie stars! I want you to be in a movie wi….” Really nice hair. It’s cut nicely. Blue eyes, I like blue eyes. Fox picks up a word that Debby says. Debby “….ass….” Yeah! I can’t see yours but my bottom is fine, thanks! Speaking of bottoms! Nice pants! Haaaa! Are those Dockers!? Lugz eh? I knew he is a homey. Hmmmm, not the original shoe laces thought. That’ll lower the price. Finally Fox clues into what Debby says. Debby “So, do you want to kick some ass? Chris? Are you ok? I have been asking you if you want to kick some ass now for almost an hour!” Fox blushes and replies. Fox “Uh…. No! I gots to go to a hotel room so I can sleep, then I gots to wake up and find the arena, then entertain my friends! Also known as the crowd! Oh they Pop, I mean Coke for me all the time! I have to go!” Fox runs out of the diner and sprints into the Geo quickly. He turns the keys but no response from the car is made! The people try to run out after him until Debby calls them off. Debby can be seen on the diner roof, were he then runs and jumps off the roof a good 15 feet to the ground landing about 5 feet short from the car. Fox rolls his window down and screams in joy. Fox “Ha! Where’s your big action moves now joy boy? I’ll tell you! You don’t have any Action moves! I have all the Action you could ever want! Catch the Action live on Wednesday’s on WB! Yeah! They play new Pokemon episodes! Oh! It’s like Midnight! Pokemon late night shows start at 12:30! Where’s Pikachu?” Fox reaches to the back seat beside Mommy and grabs Pikachu. Without hesitation, well.... you know what’s going to happen, oh boy, when will Fox learn? Fox “Piakchu, give it a mother bumping, THUNDER BOLT!” Tick, tock, oh man that’s lame. Yep, Pikachu does nothing. Then Fox holds Pikachu out the window. Fox “Kachoooooooo!!! Bham! Explosion! Yeah! Pikachu gots you! See you in heck Debby, BIG TIME!” Fox pulls Pikachu back in and hits the gas, within minutes Fox is on the road going to find a hotel. Let’s hope he makes it, safe and sound, well maybe just safe.
