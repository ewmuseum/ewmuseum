The Omega: Reflux Fantasies Upon A Death Bed Of A Villain…But I Am Near Death…So Leave Me Alone(3)

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It’s weird. You know, death. It is just a crazy afterthought for some of us. For others, it plagues our thoughts. We all come up with our own version of what death really is like. The world you become a part of. All your philosophical bullshit, however, doesn’t scratch the surface of what it is. What is it? I would tell you, but that would ruin the surprise. I have never been afraid of death. Or suicide. Hell, I was a military boy. Just found that out after my heart started beating again. I have drunk my sorrows away once with cheap vodka and a loaded shotgun clasped in my hands. So, what’s the big deal? I died. That’s very important. I live now, but for a couple of seconds/minutes/hours/days/months/years/centuries, I was dead. My stay in this dead zone seemed to last past my current life time. It’s nothing like those morons tell you. Religious propaganda aside, death is the end of a beginning. That’s all you get. You have to die to know what it’s like. However, suicide isn’t the preferred insertion method. So, my heart was beating again when I woke up the next morning. They had the windows of my room open, letting air circulate. It gave me the bittersweet taste of a freedom I currently do not have. The cream walls and bland ceiling show this. The constant beep that comes from the machines around me also have proven freedom impossible. At least for now. I opened my eyes for the first time in I don’t know how long. However, I felt cold, tired, and hungry. Even the hospital meat and potatoes sounded good. However, I am not a real big fan of potatoes, for some odd reason. Anyway, I was in that crap cloth they give people. I pulled down the shirt, and saw a stitched cut across my chest. My wrists were both stitched over, and healing. There was a mirror across the room. Looking into it, I didn’t know what I would see. I saw my blue hair. That’s all I knew was myself. My face had almost grown a full beard and mustache, both my eyes were black and blue, and my lips were swollen shut. At least, until I decided to open my mouth to speak. My left eye socket had a nasty looking gash, but other then that, my face was just fine. Sarcasm? You’re damn right. My whole body ached. I didn’t care. I wasn’t going to get up and walk out of this place. I do not know if that would be right. I do not want to die again. That would be too messy. So I went back to sleep. I needed to catch up on the hours I missed. Insomnia does that to a body. And the mind.
