Sleeping in the gutter

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st December 2002 Airborn Tyler was laying in the middle of Ottawa, propped up against a dirty white van and fast asleep. On the back window of the van someone had scribbled ‘DAVE WAZ ERE’ in the dust. Airborn was still fast asleep, but was soon woken up when someone opened the door to the van with a pair of keys and started up the engine. “Hey pal.” The man banged on the door, and Airborn awoke with a start. Airborn looked decidedly scruffy for he hadn’t shaved in a while, and his clothes had gathered dirt from lying in the gutter against the van. “You’ll be moving now, unless you want to get run over.” Airborn grumbled and got to his feet, holding his head. How had he got here, why had he been lying in the middle of the road against a car, and why was everyone obsessed with running him over? Coral Avalon had already done that today. “Huh, I get it,” groaned Airborn, realising what had happened. He’d fallen asleep in Coral Avalon’s car, and Coral thought it funny to leave him in the middle of this torrid city. “Have a cent!” chuckled the van driver, tossing a bronze coin at Airborn. It bounced off the side of the kerb and down into the sewers. Airborn looked around, wondering exactly where he was. Somehow, he had to get to Pressure Point. He needed to be there. The previous night he had pinned LLB, Simon Seaman and Christopher Fox. He had to capitalise. And of course he had to ask Coral Avalon exactly why he’d left him lying in the middle of the road. Sighing, Airborn tried to find a restaurant before searching for the arena. His stomach comes first.
