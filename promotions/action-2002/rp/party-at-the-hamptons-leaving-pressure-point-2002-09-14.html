Party At The Hamptons: Leaving Pressure Point

14 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thanks to Bobby Minio she lost her match against Max Danger, and she wasn't in the best of moods. Thank the heavens for Sweet Aroma, and this trip to The Hamptons. 'Calm down Angelica, you'll get your revenge on Xander.' Xander wasn't who Angelica was worried about though. 'I'm not worried about Xander! It's YOUR BOYFRIEND BOBBY WHOSE ASS I'M GOING TO KICK!' Veronica looked apalled at the comment. 'My boyfriend? Girlfriend, my man's Jonathan Conspiracy; Bobby Minio is just a friend.' 'Yeah, I know how those end up.' 'Angelica, what's wrong girl, talk to me.' 'I don't want Bobby around. It's his fault I lost to Xander.' 'Alright, how about we just forget about wrestling for a minute, and get inside this car; and head up to The Hamptons. Shouldn't be that long of a ride.' 'Alright, I guess I'll get to the car then, come on PP.' Angelica Dawson and the Purple Penguin exit 'Clit 'N' Candy's' locker room, while Veronica Aromadeus and the other PP continue to pack Aroma's bags. Enter Bobby Minio. 'Hey darlinlg. Looking good.' 'Thanks...' Aroma looks up and notices Bobby. 'Bobby???? What are you doing here?' 'Just wanted to see if you were alright, you took a bad spill during the Danger/Dawson match.' 'I'm fine Bobby, now if you'll excuse me I must be going.' 'Oh yeah, where you headed?' 'The Hamptons to visit my buddy.' 'Oh cool, listen I'll just run to my locker and get some things.' Bobby turns to head for his locker room, but Sweet grabs him by the arm. 'No Bobby, it's a different type of buddy.' 'Oh, OH! So whose the buddy?' Sweet ponders if she should let Bobby in on her private life, as much as she already has. She decides to do it, just in case what Angelica has been feeding her turns out to be true. 'Jonathan Conspiracy.' 'Oh, good ole JCON, I haven't seen him in like forever. Listen, I'll just go get my bags. It'll only take a moment.' 'No Bobby, you don't understand; he's not just a buddy, he's my boyfriend.' 'Oh I won't be in the way, I'll probably just keep Angelica company, penguinsit, I can do anything you want me to, I just want to have some fun away from the ring.' 'Bobby, I'm sorry, maybe some other time. I'll see what Jonathan says, and maybe I can take you up there some other time, but there just isn't enough room in Angelica's car.' 'Oh okay, yeah, maybe some other time.' 'You're not mad at me are you Bobby?' 'Me mad, no. Just a little bored. But that's alright, you go without me.' 'OKAY!' Sweet scooped up all of her belongings and exited the room, enroute to the parking lot for Angelica Dawson's car, next stop THE HAMPTONS!
