The Alpha: Interlude: 1

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/13/02 I am a pawn on this… The chessboard of life. I forever wanted to be more then a pawn, but he wouldn’t let me. Joey Malone wouldn’t let me become more then some pawn. He stole my vision of perfection from me when he knew I was about to hit the home run necessary to have her forever in my arms. Without Malone, I might be in the NFL right now, running the ball for someone, or shedding off blockers as an outside linebacker much like I shed skin now. I would be bigger then anything Joey could ever dream of being. She would be with me. She wouldn’t worry her pretty head about finances, her family’s future, any of that shit. It would be taken care of. She could do whatever she wanted in this world. Whatever she would want, I would give it to her. No questions asked. But no. I lost her to him. It would never be the same. I never had the chance to tell her what I felt when I saw her. I never had the chance to tell her why she was the brightest light in the crowd while all others were dark and dingy. I never had the chance because he found out about the letter… The place I would tell her everything… He broke into her locker and stole that note I left, saying to meet me in the parking lot. I was going to drive her someplace I can not remember now. I was so nervous that I could barely stand myself. She was a Goddess. I wanted her to be mine. The way we talked, the way she knew I felt deep down inside, she knew I was one hundred percent real. I wasn’t a liar, a cheat. She had been lied to so much, and cheated out of so many things, I wanted to show her what a real gentleman could do. All he could offer was a sense of security in laughter. A way to make her laugh if she felt down. A way to bring a smile to her face. Outside of that, Malone was a poser. He was a maggot. He was nothing… Yearning for something. Joey had to break into the locker and steal the letter. He had to have. I remember it clearly. After about an hour of waiting by my 1987 Mercury Cougar for Keri to come out, I thought something us up. I didn’t even give a fuck how much class I missed. It was my Senior year, I had already put in enough credits to graduate early, but stayed and helped the Mesa High Jackrabbits win regionals in baseball. We didn’t get to the state title game, not like we did in basketball and football. If it weren’t for those damn Phoenix schools recruiting those black kids…we would have been state champs in basketball… WHAT THE FUCK AM I THINKING ABOUT? THAT HAS NOTHING TO DO WITH YOUR LAST TRACK OF THOUGHT YOU MORON! I hate when I let my mind drift. I came back into the school and looked around. It was just after lunch and I knew where Joey would be. I went to Biology room… Well… Was heading there. The bell had rung, and the halls became rather crowded. That’s when I saw him with his arm around her as they were walking to their lockers. I saw one of my buds bump into him. Gabe Landers, the guy who had been the most popular guy in the place until I showed up. He was the All-State quarterback who was going to the University of Arizona to QB the Wildcats. We expected to be the kings of Tucson as the dynamic duo who led the University of Arizona football program into prominence, but those morons had given all the scholarships to Mesa that they wanted. They spent the last one on Malone. See… He had a higher Grade Point Average then me. I was a top ten student, a testament to the constant change of environments I faced through my young adult life…but Joey was the Salutatorian. They thought he had something to offer to the program. Still think he is really that stupid? No…that act grew old long ago… Joey got a little worked up with Gabe, and Gabe threw him against a locker. I SHOULD have came in and delivered the knockout punch to him, but I stayed my distance. Joey used some wrestling move on Landers, and punched him out. I was surprised nobody stopped Joey. Not even the teachers or other staff. Gabe was a bit of a wild child. Remember Gabe… A drunk driver killed him in 94. When the tussle was all over, Joey had grabbed a paper from his pocket and wiped the blood off of his nose. He then dropped that paper. I decided to walk over there and pick it up as Joey dragged Keri away. I opened it up and read something. THE VERY THING I WROTE TO KERI! For the longest time, Joey could never look at me face to face. Even when we became slight friends, he seemed to never be able to look me eye to eye. He didn’t think I knew. I did. So, after I picked up the blood stained letter, I left. I went to my car and went the fuck home. I didn’t play in the baseball game that night either. Didn’t matter to me. It was all coming to me then. Joey Malone decided that he was better then me, so he did things to slight my chances of getting things done. He would grade papers I did in Government harder then everyone else. He would always try to fuck up a project I was working on. He had made it seem that the only thing behind my madness with Lindum was chocolates and charm. Please. I am not that fucking passé. I think that is when I stopped caring. I didn’t like football anymore. Actually…all sports. I stopped believing in a God…in a reality. I have lived some surreal moments in this life of mine…but I never believed them to be real. Somewhere, right now, I am still an eighteen-year-old kid getting ready to go to Prom with my girl… Keri Lindum That has kept me going in a way. It is the only thing I think about now that Quinn is out trying to find herself. Julie Malone, the head cheerleader, asked me out shortly after. Funny…the hand of death dealt me two hands from the same fucking family. One was a fucker who made my life Hell; the other was a girl with a drinking problem and personality issues. So, we went to Prom together and that stuff. Who cares about the good memories anyway? The bad ones always overshadow them. Man…will that lady ever get me my coffee? I am sitting here in some dingy New York Restaurant named Smilthy's, and they take forever getting me my meal. Damn breakfast.
