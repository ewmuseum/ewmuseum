Brotherly Love: I

26 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a short bit of formalities between Marcus L. Lindum and Coral Avalon, which involved Marcus inevitably making fun of Coral's name, Coral had elected to bow out quickly. Mainly because he was dog tired and needed a lot of rest. As did Allison, so she also chose to go with Coral. Unfortunately, their restful slumber didn't last but six hours before they heard a loud commotion coming from outside their room. 'I KNOW YOU’RE IN THERE, SIOBHAN AND BEN! YOU CAN’T HIDE FOREVER!' Coral Avalon snapped awake, startled by the incessant screaming. He had recalled that Benjamin Tyler had a room next door. This had led to a wonderful exchange between the two rivals which inevitably involved Benjamin using a lot of name-calling in the direction of the Kleptomaniac. Most notably that damn 'Boral Tagalong' line that made absolutely no sense to Coral whatsoever, not to mention the cracks about Coral's driving abilities(which were actually fine). He looked at his girlfriend, Allison, who slept across from the bed, rolled up in a little ball while in her silver nightgown. She was stirring a bit, so Coral sat up and listened carefully to see if the voice would continue. 'C'MON, YOU LIL' BITCH! YOU COST ME MY...' the voice continued, before pause occured for a loud hiccup to be let out, and then the sound of the owner of the voice loudly collapsing onto the floor, 'MY... TITLE!' Allison had also been fully disturbed by the voice, not to mention Coral's movement in getting out of bed. 'What in the hell is that?' she asked quietly, as Coral put some jeans on over his black boxers. Coral opened the door of his hotel room and caught a glimpse of Lee Tyler half-laying, half-leaning against the door right in front of Ben Tyler's room. Coral frowned as he looked down at Lee Tyler from the door of his room. 'Are you trying to wake up the entire hotel, Lee?' he asked, before he caught the strong smell of alcohol from the younger half of the Elements. He had apparently been drinking away his sorrows, which would greatly explain how loud Lee really was. Lee, however, did not even acknowledge Coral aside from the random mumbling about how Coral isn't patriotic enough to see the wrong that had been committed. He awkwardly stumbled away before he entered another hotel room and violently slammed the door. Coral sighed to himself and closed his own door. Allison looked at Coral questioningly before he jerked a thumb behind him, 'It was just frigging Lee Tyler deciding to give us our morning wakeup call.' 'Oh.' Allison said. 'Great. Now I'm all fully awake,' Coral complained, sighing to himself, 'I don't think this could get much worse.' KNOCK, KNOCK, KNOCK. 'Aw, crap, why'd I have to go and say that?' Coral said, as he sighed and walked back to the door and opened it. Standing at the door, in the same army gear that he had worn just seven hours ago, was Marcus Lindum. Coral sighed and rolled his eyes. 'Okay, look, the mess hall is right over there.' Coral sarcastically said. 'Sir, I don't believe I like your attitude,' Marcus said, glaring into the face of Coral Avalon, 'I came to talk with my sister.' 'At...' Coral started, before he paused to glance back into the hotel room to look at the alarm clock that sat at the nightstand next to the bed, which read '6:33 AM' in bright red letters, 'Six thirty-three in the morning? You, my friend, hate your sister more than I expected.' Allison got up out of bed and pulled Coral out of the doorway, showing a little bit of surprising strength coming from such a slender figure, 'Coral. Stand down.' Coral shrugged as Allison turned to her brother with an inquisitive look on her face, 'Mark... what's up? Did you want to talk or somethin'?' 'Yes. I've been away for a while and I feel that we have some,' Marcus said, before he briefly paused to cast an eye at Coral before looking back at Allison, 'Catching up to do. Would you like to come with me for breakfast, ma'am?' Allison considered it briefly before she nodded, 'Sure. I'll be right down.' Marcus tipped his hat at Allison and smiled, before he walked away. So, of course, Coral simply glared at his girlfriend, not really happy with how she's starting her day. Allison winked at Coral, 'Oh, come on... my brother's been in the Middle East for the last two years. I think I deserve to talk to him.' Coral sighed, 'Yeah, whatever, Ali. I'll just stay up here and try unsuccessfully to sleep again.' Allison clasped her hands in happiness before she gave Coral a quick peck on the cheek and entered the bathroom to freshen up a bit. Allison emerged ten minutes later with a white tanktop, an unbuttoned purple shirt, and a pair of jeans. Her chocolate-highlighted black hair was tied back in a ponytail, and her gold pendant hung loosely from her neck. 'I'll see you in a while, sweetie.' Allison told Coral as he laid sprawled out on the bed, not at all capable of sleeping. An open and a close of a door later, and Allison Lindum had left the room. Not a minute thereafter, there was a knock on Coral's door. Sighing to himself, Coral stood up, put on his white shirt from the night before, and walked over to the door. There, he was greeted with the sweet face of one Siobhan Tyler, in her white Precious Shamrock shirt and a pair of jeans. Coral rubbed his eyes to make sure he was sure it was her and he hadn't fallen asleep. 'Sher. What do you need?' Coral asked of the young Tyler. Siobhan gave him a weak wave, 'I need you to take me somewhere, before my brothers decide to do more than shout at a door.' Coral rolled his eyes, 'Oh. Yeah. Lee. Don't worry about him yet, Sher. He might be drunk now, but he'll be so hung over from the alcohol that he won't do anything more than yell at a table to shut up.' Siobhan frowned, and the look in the Irishwoman's eyes made Coral forget about his bitterness of his girlfriend spending her breakfast with her stupid brother. He instead focused on the problem that was faced by a friend in Siobhan Tyler. 'Please,' Siobhan said, 'I need you to take me to go find Benjamin. You won't have to stay and endure his insults, once you drop me off, I'll be fine by myself. I just... really need to see Benjamin right now.' Coral nodded and smiled. 'I can drive you.' Then Coral gave Siobhan a wily grin, 'Ben's cracks about my driving are so far from the truth that they took residence in China, anyway.'
