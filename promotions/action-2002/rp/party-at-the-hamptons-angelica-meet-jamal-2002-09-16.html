Party At The Hamptons: Angelica Meet Jamal

16 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We finally made it, me and Angelica Dawson. THE HAMPTONS! But where's JCON's car? 'Hey, Sweet; didn't you tell me that your man had a silver Chevy Impala?' Sweet looked over to her partner, she didn't see Massacre; so she didn't know what the reasoning was for a Hot Pink Volkswagen Buggie being parked in the Impala's place. 'I don't know, but I guess he'll tell me, if he's there?' 'Better hope he doesn't have company?' Sweet shot Angelica a look, like 'that mother fucker better not be!' 'Jonathan isn't like that. He knows to keep it in his pants.' Angelica gave a shocked look at her partner. 'You two haven't fucked yet?!?!?' 'No. He's a patient man. And I love him for that.' Angelica gave a look of confusion towards Roni, and after some thought she addressed her situation with The News' Newsmaker of the past week. 'He is??? You're not worried about his past with Jenny Jiggles and those other girls?' 'No, I'm not worried. Jenny was a slut, and Jim Johnson's dirty ass is married to her, so me worried about Jonathan's past 'skeets', not on your life.' Angelica seems to be steamed by Aroma's rebuttals, but she just smiles at her fellow Clit 'N' Candy Express member. 'You do look better than that skank.' The two shared a laugh that seemed to go on forever. Aroma finally broke the laughter. 'Come on, lets go inside.' Angelica Dawson and Veronica Aromadeus both exit Dawson's car. They get to the door, and Aroma rings the bell. The instrumental for 'Guilty Until Proven Innocent' starts to play, and moments later, Jonathan Conspiracy opens the door. Sweet Aroma jumps into his arms, and kisses Jonathan on the forehead. He lets her down and the two lock hands. 'Angelica come on.' Dawson followed her inside, she didn't seem to happy with the situation, as she saw how great it is to be in a relationship. 'Romey! Whose that?!' Jonathan turned his head over his shoulders, and noticed Dawson. 'Oh, um, Roni; whose the fine lady you brought with you.' Jonathan could tell that Dawson wasn't too happy, but he knew how to make a ladies day. 'Oh, Angelica come here.' Angelica picked up the pace, especially after JCON brightened her day. 'Angelica Dawson, meet; um, Jonathan whose that?' Jonathan whispered in Aroma's ear. 'Oh that's Jamal Wilson, the gay guy I was telling you about.' Aroma gave a startled look and whispered back. 'You never said he was gay, I would've brought Jeremy Bold if that was the case.' Angelica gave the happy couple a look, wondering when she'd be introduced. 'ROMEY!' 'What Jamal!' 'Who the bitches be!' Sweet Aroma seemed ready to cold cock Jamal in the face, but Jonathan grabbed her arms and held her back. Dawson took the time to get in Jamal Wilson's face. 'THAT'S PRIMETIME BITCH TO YOU!' Jonathan Conspiracy and Sweet Aroma got in the middle of the two. Aroma holding back Dawson more than JCON held back Wilson. Wilson's gay is what crossed Jonathan's mind, and too much contact might give Wilson the wrong idea. 'Nice to meet you Primetime Bitch.' 'Same here, um...' 'Jamal; Jamal Wilson.' The two seemed to actually like each other. Sweetz and JCON released the two 'lovebirds' thinking that everything would be okay. 'So Romey, hows about that dance my nigga!' 'No Jamal!' 'Come on homey, lets get jiggy wit it.' Jonathan looked over at Aroma and Dawson. 'It's okay honey, I'll just take Angelica and we'll go unpack.' 'Alright, take the steps up to the second floor, third door on the left, Angelica you can stay in there, Roni my rooms at the end of the hall on the right.' Sweet hugged her man, and Dawson gave the two a look; she didn't seem jealous or anything, just looking at something that she might want someday. In the meantime Jamal begin to dance calling out his pet name for JCON. Aroma and JCON ended their embrace and she and Dawson headed up the stairs. Jonathan then turned to Jamal. 'Alright, but stay at an arms length.' Angelica Dawson, Jamal Wilson, Veronica Aromadeus and Jonathan Jerome Leary in one house, together, in... THE HAMPTONS This was going to be some party.
