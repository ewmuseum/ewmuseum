Date: 11/21/2002 i killed him out of jealousy. not for his woman. not for his material possessions. for the one thing he had that i know-i meaning the other me, the me that irks you out joy-i’ll never have. as long as i live i’ll never be happy. as long i’m divided in this fashion. as long as i’m not allowed to grieve. as long as i refuse to accept. as long as this charade continues. as long as this self-inflicted pseudo psychosis thrives. as long as- If you’re watching this right now, I’d like to congratulate you. Being that your actually following this piece of shit series, its obvious that either you’re a twisted fuck, or you’ve just murdered someone. Whether you’re a psycho or a killer looking for the best way to cover your tracks, you’ve come to the right place. Your mother would be proud ;) For this, the second installment in my dead body disposal tutorial I will walk you though the process of: Preparing the dead body And no it’s not what you might think. Through I thoroughly endorse and encourage all of you to engage in savage acts of cannibalism, I will not-repeat- NOT be teaching you how to cook human beings. But if you stick around, maybe I’ll air some of my “Cadaver Cuisine” special that seemed to do very well in more of the underdeveloped parts of the world like tribal Africa, tribal South America, the aborigines of Australia, and most of South Carolina. I have really appetizing recipes for eye ball stew, and placenta patties. Nummy nummy. But I’ve deviated from the topic of discussion. Let’s get back on task shall we? First of all, you need to have the following items available: rubber or latex gloves coercive liquid ei: bleach, chlorine, ect several rags incase things get messy hefty, durable, opaque black garbage bags Lysol, or an equivalent odor and germ killing spray If you’re working alone you’ll almost always have problems moving a corpse whole. The best thing to do is drain the body of liquids, then hack off the hands, head, and legs. Also make sure you’re wearing clothes that you won’t mind destroying later. What I’ve done with my corpse is hack the neck half way off, then tip the body over the tub and allowed blood and other liquids to flow down the drain. After doing this be sure to scrub down your tub thoroughly with a brillo pad, and pour some Draino or another plumbing blockage remover down the drain to eliminate any trace of liquid from your tub. After I did that, I took off the rest of the head with a hacksaw, and lopped off the hands and legs. What you want to do at this point is place the head, hands/legs, and the torso in separate bags. This is to ensure that even if some of the body parts are found it will be very difficult to correctly identify the body, and trace it back to you. At this point, the body is now set for removal. We’ve have now reached the end of part 2. Join me next time when I go through the next step in dead body disposal. This Invalid hoping you’ve watched this promo after eating a large meal -God bless No Body Loves You
