The Alpha: Blood Soaked Hands

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/11/02 Writer's Note: All my roleplays in Action! up to this point fall in this first of three chapters. This chapter is currently titled 'The Alpha'. Until I name otherwise, these roleplays/tales are all included in this chapter of the progressing story of my character in Action!. Enjoy reading this, and thank you. Malone is going to react the way I want him. Even though I had to go and get my hands dirty to do it, I still got my retribution. At least in a small quantity. I left him for dead in front of those morons. They probably just talked some idiotic wrestling trash. It was expected that Malone would get a talking to from Reed Young… But my appearance… Pure spur of the moment. Malone was surprised. Couldn’t you see it in his eyes? Couldn’t you feel it in his quakes? Couldn’t you see that fear? That stupid look of amazement? Fuckhead! Look at me, the man who made the hero of the hour into a poster child of hypocritical immortality. I sense that a war is brewing. He probably can not get the venom out of his head. He knew I was right. I could see it in his eyes. He knew that the days of using me were over. He knew I was bringing reality to the table in a business where reality is called fake. Some people call it simple crowd heat for saying what I said. They don’t know that what I said was really true. I feel good... No… I don’t. Wait… What the fuck is going on? What exactly is going on? Why am I looking behind myself every single moment, even though there is nothing here but I? Why am I washing my hands over and over in this sink, when my hands are not dirty? Why am I thinking my career was ruined? Why? FUCKING ANSWER ME! Relax Rashard… This is you we are talking about here. You can not stand being used like a whore and then get thrown away. Joey did it to Anthason. Joey did it to Rivers. Joey did it to Seaman. Joey did it to me. Rather, he did… Meaning past tense. I am pushing back Joey. I am fighting it Joey. I am pushing back Joey. I am not letting you ruin it all again. You did it in the IWO. I am not letting you drain the life out of me one more time. NO MORE! FUCK YOU! It’s time to justify those who died… And were used… FUCK YOU! Joey… Why do I wash my hands? I wash them… To get the blood of you off of them. No matter what I do to separate from you… You stay. This blood will not go away. I can smell you. I can feel you. I can’t get you away from me. You stay! You have taken everything away from me. Now all that is left is my mind. YOU GREEDY SON OF A BITCH! YOU WANT TO BECOME ME! Malone… You can take everything from me… But now you want my most personal of possessions. My mind. And that… I will not let you take. NEVER!
