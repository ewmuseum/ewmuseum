The Good, the Bad, and the Bad News, Part 6: Nightmares, Past and Present

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 25th, 2002 August 25th, 6:42 am, 2002. The Present. Morning sun was peaking through the hotel room window. It had been days since he escaped the grasp of Julie. His ex love. His first love. His only love. She had the car in her posesion. His very favorite vehicle in the world, but he didn't care. Being away from her was enough. It hurt just to smell her. Just to breath her reproduced air. He had a dream the night before, which was why he was sitting up in his bed with a cold sweat on his face. He dreamt of the day Julie gave him that letter. Over a year ago, and then of the day Sean Cylear tried to comfort him. Back when everyone Bobby wrestled were friends. Before the real bad blood. Back when Roland, otherwise known as LLB was giving him tips on his finisher and the business, when Cylear and he were drinking Buddies, where Chris Mezz and Bobby wrestled on weekends for charities and enjoyed it. Those days. Before Cylear died. Now, LLB beats up dead bodies, Cylear being the bodie, Mezz wrestles for the new blood of PIW, never returns any calls, and Minio feels more alone then a wet tree in Colorado. Minio did have one thing to half smile about. His tag team win with Sweet Aroma. The young woman and him had good ring chemistry. They kicked alot of ass. Her being spanish and he being italian led them to be not to far off. Both had that edge. She wasn't much younger then him but she had the energy, she had the charisma. He saw it in there. He knew Johnathon Conspiracy really chose his pupils well. The young woman named Veronica really put a smile on his face in all of this bullcrap. It took his mind off things for quite a while. He had the distinct feeling he would see more of Veronica Aromadeus then Julie. He wasn't quite sure if he was pleased or disappointed yet. But... he was sure he would figure it out soon enough.
