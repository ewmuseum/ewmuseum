Invisibility - I

8 April 2002

Date: Original: 07/21/2001. Rewrite: 04/08/2002 [name withheld]'s note: This is actually a roleplay series that I wrote for the IWO in July of 2001, only rewritten. I have been wanting to rewrite it, and make it much clearer. Since elements from what I plan to do with Malone in Action are within this series, I might as well post it for Action. -- July 20th, 2001 Malone Residence: Mesa, Arizona, United States Keri Lindum had been increasingly worried about Joey's behaviour, as of late. Following the appearance of Joey's sister, Julie, in his life, everything had been turned upside down. He was becoming more and more distant, as if there was something else on his mind. It didn't really take a rocket scientist to figure out that Joey's behaviour change and the appearance of his sister was related. How it was related, Keri didn't know. He didn't even say anything to her when he went to sleep. He just turned out the light and went to sleep. She couldn't find the words to say to him. The sheer exhaustion of the two days she spent thinking about it took over, and she drifted off, herself. -- She woke up and found herself in the most surreal enviroment she could've possibly imagined. Spatula-shaped raindrops were falling here and there. The sky was colored brown with what appeared to be blades of grass. The ground Keri was standing on was sky blue, and the grass was replaced by a puffy white substance. There were trees, but the wood was neon purple and the leaves were neon pink. To make a long story short, Keri wasn't sure if she was dreaming, or if she was having the worst acid trip this side of Jimi Hendrix. It was ironic that such a thought came across her mind at that moment. Sitting at the top of a hill was a a man with a black afro and a stubby little moustache. He wore a shiny purple suit with a Hawaiian shirt under it, and wore several gold chains around his neck. To make a long story short, he looked exactly like Jimi Hendrix, himself. Keri found herself walking toward this man, but then she noticed that he was arguing with someone. 'I was just mindin' my own business, and you had to fuckin' get all anal at me.' Keri was confused, but she approached the man. She then saw a second man, a short man with a thick beard and long hair. Unlike the man that he was arguing with, he was dressed far more casually. He was seated on a neon purple log. 'Minding your own business?!' he yelled. 'Hendrix, you told him to hit him with a chair! I'd hate to see what you call 'taking action'.' Hendrix frowned before he began mumbling. 'Fuck you, Bonham. You and your damn stairways...' Keri finally reached them and held up her hand. 'Um... hi?' Bonham was just about to respond to his companion, but he hears Keri's voice and slowly turns to her. Hendrix, as well, turns to her. Bonham blinks before he realizes who she is. 'Ah. Miss Lindum. How good of you to join us, today.' Keri blinked. 'Huh? You know me?' Bonham stood up from his seat and approached her. 'Yes. Think of me as a sort of a conscience. My name is Bonham, and I happen to act as Joey Malone's good conscience.' Bonham said, before turning and pointing towards Hendrix. 'The dimwit with the gaudy suit over there is Hendrix. He's the evil one.' 'Do you even look at yourself in a fucking mirror, Bonham?' Hendrix quipped. Bonham just sighed. 'Ignore him.' he said. 'Where... is this?' Keri asked. Bonham shrugged, looked around, then looked into Keri's eyes. 'The alpha and the omega, my dear.' The look on Keri's face suggested bewilderment. 'Well, nevermind. You wouldn't get it.' Bonham said. 'Anyway, Hendrix and I watch and observe through Joey's brain. We got dumped in here after both of us died. Apparently, all of our drug and alcohol abuse made the powers that be...' Bonham points upward. '...question our morals.' 'You can believe what you want. You're the one that's dreaming. But you're here for a purpose, Keri Lindum.' 'I am?' Bonham nodded. 'Yes. But I figure that I can show you some things. From the past. You wouldn't happen to remember anything relating to Joey before your high school graduation, would you?' Keri shook her head. 'Most of what I remember about high school is rather blurry.' 'Then this shall benefit you. I should warn you, though. A lot of these memories were rather painful for Joey. Since you were a major factor in Joey's life, they could be painful for you, as well.' Bonham said. 'I shall be your guide, since Hendrix probably just wants to get shitfaced while I do all the work.' 'You damn right.' Hendrix stated, before he sat down and began to play Voodoo Child on his guitar that he just happened to have. 'So... with that said... follow me.' Bonham said, before he walked down the hill. Keri followed, but stopped as Bonham just simply disappeared. She was confused for about five seconds, before Bonham's hand reached out from where he had disappeared from, grabbed Keri by her arm, and pulled her in. Things were about to get interesting.
