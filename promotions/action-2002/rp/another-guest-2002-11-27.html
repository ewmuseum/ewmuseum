Another Guest

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27/02 Max Danger was off renting videos, while the others were off getting a tree. In the house, all alone, were Conrad Ramsey and Homeless Gus... though being together wasn't necessary alone. Of course, Gus had fallen asleep, at the breakfast nook/dinner table. He had been eating ice cream and drinking orange juice. Weird combo, but he was homeless, he didn't know any better. Snore he did, as Conrad tried to watch television. Kansas had been THUMPED by North Carolina. He didn't know much about college basketball, but knew something was going on when a highly ranked team like Kansas was being dominated by UNC. Now, he was just flipping through the channels. Nothing appealed to him. He had a strange urged to stop on the Home & Garden channel, but couldn't bring himself to. He pressed forward. ABC, UPN, FOX, FX, FOX Family, USA, MTV, Nickelodeon... Danger had a lot of changes skipped. Knock Knock It was a slight knock. Not too loud, not to soft... juuuuust right. Conrad Ramsey heard it instantly and rose. He was hoping it was the others or at least Max, because he wanted to get back to his work. Little did he know, though, that he wasn't going to get back tonight. He was only going to have one thing on his mind. Someone he had never seen before, but someone he instantly felt a connection with. Someone he knew he would be able to talk to. Samantha Lynn Dox. Doesn't sound familiar, does it? Probably not. She hasn't been around for awhile. Not since the gfwa OMEGA had she really been seen. She, of course, was the mother of one Marcus Alexander Dox. 'The Danger Mom' so to speak. 'Hi.' she said, softly and elegantly. He was in love. In that instant. Wasn't the way she said it. It was before that. The moment he opened the door. The moment their eyes meet. He was in love. What he didn't know, though, was that he was standing there for quite a while. Too long. She was freezing out there, and had a load of luggage. 'May I come in?' she asked, smiling and laughing on the inside. 'Oh, yes, please do.' he stammered on the words, 'I'm Conrad Ramsey.' 'Samantha Dox,' she said, shaking his hand. 'I'm Marcus' mother.' 'You sure?' he said. She laughed. He smiled. He helped her in with her things. Placed them in the living room. He offered her something to drink, but she declined. She seemed to want to know why a man was passed out with his face in his ice cream and one hand on a glass of orange juice. 'It's a long story.' Ramsey told her. 'Tell me. Looks like it may be awhile before everyone gets back.' she seemed to share an affection for the old man, and neither of them really knew the other. They sat down on the couch, and he told her everything from Last Laugh on. He didn't feel it necessary to go ALL the way back to when he first met Max... er, Marcus, and all the problems he's had. 'I haven't seen Marcus is such a long time,' Samantha told Ramsey. 'Hope he's doing alright.' But that was the last his name was brought up. Conrad couldn't answer it for her. He could give his opinion and what he's observed, but nothing solid, nothing factual. So, they talked about themselves. About their lives. Laughs, silence, compassion, sorrow. It was all shared. Looked like something was in the air, and Blast was out of the house. Must be... LOVE.
