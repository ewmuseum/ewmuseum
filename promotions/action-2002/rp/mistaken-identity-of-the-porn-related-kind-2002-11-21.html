Date: 11/21/02 Sigh. Things weren’t right. He had made his appearance in the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling weeks ago and for some odd, strangely bizarre reason- no one looked at him as if he was a threat. His air time on Saturday Night Massacre was dwindling down week by week, he had things to say but no way of saying them. He didn’t know what the problem was, the people hated him for dismantling the return of their former United States Champion but yet, Jim Johnson didn’t seem to want to cut him the break. Meanwhile, in Action! Wrestling, his on going feud with Coral Avalon and Odium was about to come to a head at the next big pay per view event- Last Laugh, which was taking place this Saturday. Things weren’t really going as he had hoped, instead of having the Triple Threat match he had so wished, Coral, in an attempt to once again get under his skin, signed the three of them up to be tag partners in a Virus Match qualifer. Mistaken Identity of the Porn Related Kind That son of a bitch… It was now three o’clock in the afternoon; Jeff had scurried back to his crampedapartment on the corner by Pizza Hut. He had spent his day like he normally did when he wasn’t scheduled for a house show or booked for an autograph session, come to think of it, he never really was at an autograph session. Today’s agenda included an ice path, grabbing a Caesar salad from the restaurant across the street and peeping in his female neighbour’s windows while they changed. Uh… You never heard that. Jeff leaned back in his old, brown leather recliner and inserted a pornographic tape that he had rented called, ‘Beauty and the Beast’. Sounded rough and tumble. He eased himself into the nicely carved ass groove he had created over the many months and pressed play on the remote. The screen blurred and then began… His eyes were now as wide as small pools of water when he reached for the popcorn beside him. Oh yes, this movie sounded Naughty. Very, very naughty- this brought a smile to Jeff’s already sweaty face as the previews quickly skipped by and the ‘hard core’ pornographic feature presentation began. “What the hell is this bullshit,” he groaned, staring at some sort of shoddy Disney cartoon with talking tea-cups and a beast that Jeff figured got ALL the bitches skipped around singing a song. Either way, it was a cartoon, what a disappointment. Jeff frowned, tossing the half-empty snack bowl aside and reaching for the remote to turn off whatever ‘this’ was. Then the main character appeared- Bell was her name. “Woah! What a fox!” Jeff quickly dropped the remote and began watching whatever type of ‘adult video’ this was. The hour and a half flew by, revealing that this movie contained no nudity whatsoever… Not even the occasional boobie! Jeff Garvin in a rush to get home and watch, must have grabbed a children’s video that had been accidentally placed on the adult rack in the back room. You’d think ‘the Original’ would be furious but for some reason, the nephew of the former WWF champion Ron Garvin, the man who beat Ninja K in a thirty minute submissions match at Titania… was crying, or at least on the verge of it. “Woah, got a little dirt in my peepers,” Jeff said, trying to pass the tears off as some act of nature instead of him being upset with the seen in front of him. The wolves were attacking ‘the Beast’. Finally, Jeff broke down… “YOU MOTHER FUCKERS! LEAVE… HIM…ALONE!” …And chucked the remote at his tiny, fourteen inch television set- the only thing he could afford. The edge of the plastic remote just nicked the power button and the television clicked off. Jeff looked around frantically; making sure no one saw him. “…Mother fuckin’…uh… dirt.” What? It was!
