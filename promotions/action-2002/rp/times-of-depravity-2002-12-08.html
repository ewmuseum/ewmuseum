Times of depravity.

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/08/2002 It had been a long and difficult road for Joseph Tyler Malone to follow, and if he had a choice in the matter, he probably would've gone down the road more taken. He stepped into his home in Mesa, Arizona for the first time in a few weeks. He had mainly stayed out on the road and had not even bothered coming home for the whole time. With no one to come home to, there wasn't much of an incentive to return. Besides, he figured that Coral took care of the place while he was away, anyway. So, anyway, Joey Malone stepped into his home, took six and a half steps forward, and simply collapsed onto his sofa. He laid there, exhausted and empty. Which was pretty much how he felt at the moment, all things considered. He reflected on every single horrible atrocity that had happened to him in the last month. His wife leaving him for Anthason. Nearly drowning. Getting mugged in Thailand by a bunch of kids. Coming back. Getting tossed out of the Asylum of all places. And most importantly of all, not getting back at Kent Anthason at Last Laugh. He had failed. As he thought this, a familiar smell crept into his nostrils. At first, he tried to ignore it, but soon, it was eating at him. It was the familiar smell of Keri's perfume, and Joey noticed with some alarm that it was a strong smell, and not the kind of a leftover smell that accumulates within a place of residence. Joey leapt out of his sofa with a renewed vigor, and tried to figure out where the smell was coming from. He deduced after a moment that the smell was coming from upstairs, and so, he climbed up the steps that led to three rooms in the Malone residence. A bathroom, a guest bedroom that was used as a sort of a computer room/study, and the master bedroom that Joey and Keri had previously been using. Joey quickly deduced that the smell was indeed coming from the master bedroom, and he slowly and quietly opened the door. The master bedroom of the Malone residence was a large, spacious place. Along the wall where Joey walked in was, of course, the bed. We'll get to that in a moment. The rest of the room was covered in a light brown wallpaper and dark brown carpet. There was a dresser with a circular mirror right across from the bed. Right next to that was the door to the third room of the second floor, the bathroom. Across from the bathroom door was a closet door that was opened, with several women's clothes sticking out of it. Apparently, Keri hadn't cleaned the whole closet out when she left Joey, because it was still relatively full. Next to the closet door was a second dresser, which had a small, antique-looking clock on it. This clock was a wedding gift from Joey's trainer, Kyle Morris, which is why it had a special place here. Right next to this was a second closet, which housed Joey's clothing. But what struck Joey was the bed. The bed was a large spacious bed so that Joey and Keri had room to sleep together. It was covered in several different blankets and sheets, all of which were quite disjointed, since the present occupant of the bed had apparently moved them. The occupant of the bed was a young woman wearing a blue dress. Joey didn't think. He was probably off in some sort of euphoric 'la-la land'. He slowly walked into the room, walked over to the other side of the bed, and planted a kiss on the sleeping woman's lips. Immediately did the girl wake up, surprised. Her eyes fluttered open, and she tried to cry out, but, of course, being locked in a kiss, her cry of surprise became somewhat muffled. However, it did cause Joey to open his own eyes, and it was only then that he realized his mistake. The girl had blue eyes. Keri's eyes were green. He quickly let go of her and moved backwards, surprised and most of all very awkward. The girl whom he had just kissed sat up quickly and shot a very bewildered look at Joey, before she spoke, 'What in the hell are you doing?!' 'I, er... I, uh...' Joey fumbled. It was really hard to come up with an explaination for anything when you had just kissed your wife's little sister, not to mention your best friend's girlfriend. She brushed her presently messy, shoulder-length black hair out of her face and looked at Joey. She suddenly realized who it was, 'Joey?!' 'Um, hi... hi, Ali,' he said to Allison Lindum, very awkwardly, 'Um, uh, you're prob-probably wondering why... yeah... um, that is...' Allison frowned. She put two and two together and figured it out on her own. 'You, you thought I was Keri.' Joey said nothing. 'Okay, look, um. The perfume, uh, well, uh... I kinda, you know, tried it. And, um, well, I kinda fell asleep here, and, uh... you know,' Allison fumbled. She was in just as much of an awkward position as Joey was, but then she suddenly snapped her head in the direction of Joey, 'It still didn't give you the right to kiss me!' 'Sor... er, sorry.' Allison calmed down a bit. 'Look,' Allison said with a sigh, 'I promise I won't tell Coral about this. But please, Joey, do try to watch what you kiss.' With that, Allison stood up and walked out. Joey listened to the pitterpatter of her small, bare feet walking down the stairs and toward the direction of the room she now shared with Coral Avalon. Once the sounds stopped, Joey's legs collapsed out from under him, and he wept. He was beginning to accept the idea that Keri was never going to come back.
