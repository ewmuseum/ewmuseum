Cups: cXIV: Sea of Sorrow

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: aug.29.02 Cups: cXIV: Sea of Sorrow The tires squealed across the pavement as the engine revved up over and over. Fog had just began to rise over the mountains as he peeked out across the open land. Smooth rolling mountains accompanied by the man made roads was all that was in sight. Looking harder, James browsed down over the balcony to only see the tail lights fade into the horizon. He felt the piercing pain cascade through his body and nearly collapsed. Strongly, he held himself up and looked down into the pool below him. A stagnant look had taken over. The greenery floated amidst the oxygen in the water. James browsed down at the steam rising easily off of the road. Weather in his area had began to change. The hot humid climate had drifted away, more or less, he drifted away from it. Continuing to go North East but with no real goal. All he wanted was time. The thinking he had done in the last few minutes wouldn't change the fact that he has an envelope in his hand. Sealed. There was no return address nothing. No name on the outside. All he could see was some very distinct handwriting, but it was too faded to make out any of the words. James felt the urge to open it. Inside he declined. He felt it was hard to tell what would happen if he opened it. Strongly, he pushed down on the envelope. Testing it out to make sure there were no pranks or anything involved. He couldn't resist any longer. The seal was released. He felt the fiery aura burn away at his hands as he attempted to open it further. For some reason, it felt hot. James relieved himself by letting loose of the letter. Pain was all he felt. Pain was not a factor to him. Graciously, he snatched the letter back up and looked inside. The eyes he had envisioned. The body he had envisioned. It was ‘her'. James felt the fear as he predicted a game being played on him. Surveying the area, it was unsuccessful. There was no one left in the empty hotel parking lot. His car. That was it. The last vehicle around was the one he saw fade into the distance... from two bright crimson lights into nothing. Fed up with all of the mind games, James set off. Solving this could be his redemption. All the games had been played on him. All of the shooting deals ... after a while, things like that would get to a person. He leaped down the stairs and peered into his car. A bloody hand print rested in the exact area where the picture had laid. He had pieced the puzzle back together. Now, he looked at his hands. If his predictions were right then ...          'Blood.'          Just as he had expected. His hand had droplets of blood just waiting to be released. A darkened shadow overcame the small car of his. He turned ... nothing. As he turned back around, he galloped in fear.          'Q .... u .... e.... e.....n?'
