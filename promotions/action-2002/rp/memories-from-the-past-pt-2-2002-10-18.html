Memories from the Past pt. 2

Date: October 18th, 2002 “Hello there, beautiful.” Jeff stood there, in the middle of the school gymnasium. Meagan turned around, smiling after just finishing some meaningless conversation with one of her shallow cheer leader friends. Her smile quickly turned to disgust as she stared into the eyes of the ‘Human Women Repellent’ himself… Jeff Garvin. He bobbed his eye brows, licking his lips as he looked her up and down. “What do YOU want?” She said, crossing her arms and tilting her head slightly. To her, Jeff was no better than an insect. An insect she wanted to squash beneath her high-heeled, designer shoes. “I was just…erm… thinking… maybe you and I could go see a movie on Sunday. Or like, you could, um, let me feel your boobies. That’d be cool too. I have money.” Slap. “Ew! Yuck! Get away from me, you pervert!” Jeff stumbled back into a wall, his cheek bright red. Wait. Wall? Turning around, Jeff looked up to see Nick glaring down at him. Smiling, but it wasn’t a happy smile, it was a ‘You’re going to be wearing your ass as a hat for the rest of the fucking school year’ kind of smile. He cracked his knuckles and tapped a few of his friends. In seconds, Jeff was looking straight into death. Death multiplied by four. Four of his school’s toughest bullies, and you know, this would be a great time to tell you all that Jeff stood his ground and went down fighting… But we all know that isn’t ‘Garvin’ like… So lets just stick with what we all know REALLY happened. The next ten minutes were filled with girlish screams, cries for mercy, a swirly, a wedgie, and a meeting with a nearby garbage can. But hey, he got his shots in too. “That little fucker bit me!” Nick grunted, grabbing the garbage can that contained Jeff and kicking it several times until Jeff stopped whining. “Don’t feel bad, dude, he stole my shoe.” “That sucks, your shoe rocked.” “Yeah, it did.” “Want my shoe?” “Yeah.” “Okay.” Yes. That’s pretty much all… Cue the random porn images of well-known celebrities. Hmmm… We all know that’s what you came to see.
