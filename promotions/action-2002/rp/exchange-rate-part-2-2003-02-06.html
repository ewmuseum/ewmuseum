Exchange Rate - Part 2

6 February 2003

Date: 02/06/03 Women… They knew exactly what strings to pull to bring a man to their knees. The thought of no more sex could turn a guy like Jeff into a complete wimp, he had only lost his virginity a few weeks before and he couldn’t go back to his old life. Julie had left to go shopping, taking three hundred dollars from her ‘willing’ husband’s wallet to occupy her for the day while he stayed home to try and make up for his stupidity. Garvin had gathered whatever cleaning products he possibly could find and laid it out on the kitchen counter for examination. Perhaps he would be able to distinguish what each was used for if he stared at it for extended periods of time. “Well, I know what this Windex is used for… But what the hell is Javax?” Jeff was in way over his head, house cleaning just wasn’t for him. It was the women’s job to look after that. He didn’t even know where he kept the damn vacuum cleaner. Already he missed his wife’s handy work. She could whip up his daily protein shake and salad like lickity-split, but he on the other hand didn’t have a clue. You’d have to wonder how the guy survived all those years by himself. He use to just sweep it all into the closet but Julie kept certain antiques in there and if one of those broke, it would be off with his head. First things first, before any ‘work’ could take place, he needed to get some grub into him. People really shouldn’t work on an empty stomach, Jeff inquired, as he walked over to the refrigerator and pulled out about ten different things. Then, the blender… Jeff quickly rolled up his sleeves; this could get messy. Now how did Jewels make that shake? Jeff’s carefully pondered this. His stomach growled. There was no time to fire up the stove, she could be back at any time, that woman could go through a hundred bucks in five minutes. Yes, he’d have to find a shortcut to a nutricous breakfast. His body needed to be kept in top physical condition, skipping breakfast wasn’t an option. With no time to spare, Jeff frantically chucked in whatever wasn’t a cleaning product; eggs, bacon, milk, vegtebles, hash browns, cheese, and half a piece of coffee cake. By the time he was done, there wasn’t anything left in the ice box. Garvin slammed the lid down on the blender and pressed ‘Power’. The Original watched in amazement as all the bits of food turned to liquid and spun around like a whirl-pool. He couldn’t help but smile. So this was what independence felt like, eh? He popped the top and began chugging the concoction. Jeff gagged, his face becoming red. It tasted disgusting, but how could this be? He created it. Garvin hurled the blender’s cup over his shoulder and into the sink. “Tasty.” He lied before going off to begin some house work.
