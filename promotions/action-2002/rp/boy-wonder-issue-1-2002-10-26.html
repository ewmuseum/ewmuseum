Boy Wonder. Issue #1!

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/26/02 Jeremy told me everything. The Entire story. Although his misjudgment threw me off a few times, I still ended up understanding every word that came from his mouth. The bad news sprung to my head, like a frieght train. Morg had dissapeared from the face of this Earth. I was alone. Once again, alone. 'Hey, you okay?' Jeremy asked. I nodded, and lugged my body in an upright position. 'Yeah, I'm fine.' I said. 'Alrighty then. I'll catch you on the flip-side brother!' Jeremy arched his eyebrow, and threw out his hand. It was the least I could do. 'Bye...' Boy Wonder. Issue #1! 5:00 P.M. Action! Headquarters. 'I can't beleive it...' I whispered to myself. Lawless had already fallen. The uprise of the most powerful tag-team in the world of wrestling had failed, without one clashing battle, or one moment to keep treasured. I begin to walk down the corridor, and through the main enterance to the Action! Headquarters. I pushed the door open with my fist, and stepped out into the cold. The wind blew through my face, and my nerves tangled. What was I doing? I had no idea. ----- I walked a few blocks myself, watched the wide variety of people minding there business. Birds chirpped above me, and I turned the next corner. 'Hey...you...' I sprung, and turned around. 'You..you look familiar.' It was middle-aged, slim, blond-haired boy. He was in his mid twenties, and stared at me, wide-eyed. He gasped, and uttered for words. 'Uncle D-X? IS THAT YOU? OH MY GOD!' The boy jumped on my shoulders, and clutched onto my back. Who was this kid? What did he want? 'Don't you remember me? I'm...I'm..The Indomitable WHITE-LIGHTING! YOUR BEST FRIEND!' I felt dizzy. It couldn't be Whitey. It just couldn't. 'Holy fucking shit. Kid? Is that you?' I eyed him carefully. He smiled, and happily jumped off my back. 'This is Soooooooo Great!' I laughed. Who else could it be? Nobody else in the world was that optismitic. 'So, What have you been up to Kid?' We began to walk, parallel to one another. 'Not much Uncle DX-..' 'Disciple X? You mean Domino.' 'Huh?' 'Disciple X is dead kid. It's Domino. Got it?' 'Whatever you say, Uncle Dommy.' I looked at him. 'Dommy?' 'Well, I have to call you something don't I? Where going to be together forever, and ever. Like we were in ThReat! Right? Right? RIGHT?' I took a deep breath. 'Yeah, sure.'
