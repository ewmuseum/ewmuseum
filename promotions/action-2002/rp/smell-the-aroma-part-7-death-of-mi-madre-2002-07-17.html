Smell The Aroma (Part 7 - Death of Mi Madre)

17 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.17.02 It was 1994. Not a real important year for some people. But this was the year that left me with only an older brother to guide me through my teenage years, and he was only 16. I was really scared. I mean watching my mom go out the way she did wasn't pretty. Yeah sure she died in her sleep, but to me it was far from peaceful. I came into her room because I was having a problem, my first of many problems that I would get once a month, and she was the one person that I knew to help me get through it. There was one problem though... ...SHE WOULDN'T GET UP! After shaking her repeatedly I burst from the room, and pounded Roberto's door down. I then barged in and heard 'Don't You Ever Knock!' I hurriedly explained that there was no time, and we both made it back into the room only to still see our mother laying out cold on the bed. 'Why didn't you call 911???' barely muscled out of Roberto's mouth, making it close to muted, rendering me death to his words. I just sat there on the edge of the bed, with a cooling hand in my palm, and tears, which are forming right now, streaming down my face. An ashtray full of cigarette butts and ashes rest on her nightstand, and a phone was pressed to Roberto's ear. 'The ambulance is on the way.' I didn't hear him, but managed to mutter 'I think she's dea... gone.' My voice quivering on each sylobol. The autopsy said cancer and loniless took her away from us. And we are all we have left. Roberto Aromadeus is my last strand of sanity. May God help us. Maria Aromadeus, may she rest in peace. February 26, 1964 - October 8, 1994
