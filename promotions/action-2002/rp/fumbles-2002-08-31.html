Action! — Tales

Fumbles.

31 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/31/02 It was a good day... this past Sunday night. It was a great day! I proved to everyone... that dammit, I could put on a show! PIW sets what weekly? Fuck off and blow me. the Asylum Show's are all that *and* the bag of chips? Rrriiight. fWo... pft, they've sucked since day one. Action!, is the only Federation that matters. PERIOD! I mean, we SIGNED Donaven Winters himself! Our main Champion STILL remains in LLB... boo yeah! The Joker picked up the ball and TOOK our team to VICTORY! Let alone I forced that quitter Morg to go back and wrestle... EVEN with his stupid little ear missing! Yes, that's right... *I* just pulled Action! up another step. But still, sitting here... with the results written out in my hand... I cannot help but see some FAILURES upon the words. Those people I gave HAND-OFF'S too... yet they dropped the football! THEY FUMBLED! And didn't recover! Hehehehe. It doesn't matter though... for I'm on a roll now... I've got this bitch literally JETTING past Federation after Federation to the TOP of the world! This Pressure Point... beware... For those that fumbled... will pay the price. And for those with the ball in their hands, I will STRIP it dry! Because I'm Reed Young... the best Owner... EEEEVVVVEEEERRRR! Stop laughing.
