Cups: cVII: Cup of Life

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/26/02 Cups: cVII: Cup of Life 'Are you okay?'         I peered down into the eyes of the perfect young woman. A smile came across the lifeless face, leaving the body out of perspective. Abundantly, the seconds that he spent there filled the void immediately. He didn't worry about what to do next. James was where he yearned to be; Next to her. Her eyes were bloodshot. Apparently all of the drug usage had already kicked in. The drugs abandoned an artificial being laying on the bed. Immune to pain, James never claimed to be. For the Joker was not laughing, he was crying.         'Queen, come to me! Speak!' Madison beckoned to the life stolen body. Still endlessly, there seemed to be no response. James uttered her last name over and over, hoping for some form of movement or lifelike gesture. 'Queen! Come here!' He gently rubbed his hand across her face. A sense of horror filled his mind. The most frigid thing that he had ever touched was right before him. Immobile. It wasn't the same person that called him. It wasn't the same person who gave him the cup. It wasn't the same person who took the same bullet as him a few years back.         James kneeled down beside the bed. Slowly, he directed his attention upward to the heavens. 'I know ... you'll help if there is any help. You'll fill the needed space. Look at her, she's nearly gone. Lying right before my eyes and there is nothing I can do.'         Adding in a few religious words, Madison collapsed to the floor. Flashbacks of the day, memories of the past, they all haunted him at once. They seemed to have built up and released themselves upon him at quite possibly the worst timing ever. Fighting off the cruel demons, he stood to his feet and reigned over them all.         'Queen, I took the bullet before you. In an attempt to save you, I gave what little bit of life that I could spare. You couldn't have given more. I was hit first, you can't go first! It'll be me! Fucking take me!' The most emotional side of Joker ever spilled out all over the hospital floor. His tears flew as vagrantly as the winds over the deep blue seas.         'A grown man, crying. It's pitiful. I know!' James sat down easily in the chair. Reminiscing over what, he had left in life. Nothing. 'Since that day, I've been looking, searching for a reason to find you.'         Feelings of sadness mixed with exhaustion and James was nearly out of it. He leant his head back against the top head of the chair. Once more, Madison peered over her body. The smile was gone. Her whiteness continued to fade easily to an even more pail coating over her body. He extended his hand and snatched hers up. Feeling the immense cold, a final tear fell down his face. James squeezed her hand, and prayed for a response. No attempts.         'Queen, I've came this far and not given up. Pick your ass up off of that bed and get to feeling better.' He nagged on, hoping that she would catch on and listen, but his attempts were futile.         'That was my last tear.' - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
