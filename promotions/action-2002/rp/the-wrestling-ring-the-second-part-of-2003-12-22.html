The Wrestling Ring, The Second Part of

22 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Jesus…” LLB said as he worked at the ring rope. It was still connected to the ring post… but with all of that rust covering it, LLB was having a hard time scrapping it off. “Why can’t Reed just order a new one?” ‘The Law’ rolled his eyes after he said that. The answer was simple. And everyone knew it. LLB finally detached the ring rope from the buckle. “Well… that was hard.” He dropped the rope to the ground, wanting to take a closer look at the rust and dirt mashed into the metal that kept the ring rope against the corner for so long. And it was a sickening sight to say the least. One would have never thought a ring post could be that dirty… but it was. Rust covered all of it… with little traces of dirt caked all around. LLB laughed just then… although he was positive this was some other mess (it had been a year)… a small trace of brown and light brown clumps sat on the edge of the metal. “Na…” He said to himself. “It isn’t…” A flashback to last year’s Players and Pawns appeared in LLB’s head… the time where ‘The Law’ slammed Reed Young’s head up against the turnbuckle so many times it made him throw up. Clearly, a year after, this was not what LLB was seeing. He was sure. ‘The Law’ shrugged it off, and looked to the center of the ring. So many memories… so much fun… and so much sorrow. A mixed reaction hit LLB then… as he continued to stand there and observe the ring which had been his life for five good years. But recently only failures came to mind… and LLB just sighed at the thought of them. He walked to the middle of the canvas… feeling its power. The power of when he would stand there on the microphone… demanding everyone’s attention. Allowing everyone to hear what he had to say. But with his recent downfall… LLB got to say no more. This was left to the Keith Scott Zimmerman’s… the Levine’s… and to anyone and everyone else that wanted to trash him, others, and then brag about themselves. Not many more thoughts passed through LLB’s head just then… as he looked up, seeing Robbie and Bill heading back to the ring. “Boy that Reed…” Robbie said jokingly. “You know what he wanted?” The two workers entered the ring again… but Robbie didn’t continue. He could see something was bothering LLB… he was going to wait and see what ‘The Law’ had to say first. “No idea.” LLB smiled, snapping out of his trance. “And you know… maybe it’s best I didn’t find out.” He patted Bill and Robbie on the shoulders, as ‘The Law’ turned to the ring rope he detached. “Guys… I’m sorry… I’m afraid I didn’t get much done.” Bill smiled. “It’s alright Roland. You help us out enough, anyway.” “Yeah.” Robbie said, looking at LLB. He still knew something was wrong. “You okay by the way?” He asked. LLB nodded. “Yeah… was just thinking about the great times I’ve had in this place.” He said. “You guys construct a really good ring you know… trust me, I’ve seen worse in PIW.” He smiled again at Robbie and Bill… and even though it might have seemed like a harmless comment… it really did mean something to the both of them. Sure, not everyone (hell, hardly anyone!) admired the work that they did… but that compliment from LLB… well, to them, it didn’t even just come from ‘The Law’. It came from a wrestler… a wrestler that had performed on their ring since the beginning of Action. It came from a wrestler that, to them, spoke for the rest of the roster at that time. Hey, when you don’t get daily compliments… that kind of statement does this to you. “Thanks.” Bill said to LLB. “And I hope you come back soon… to wrestle many more matches on it. We enjoy it you know.” “Yeah.” Robbie stated. “At least you won’t jam the turnbuckles so hard. I don’t see you use them so often.” LLB laughed… as he headed towards the steel stairs, and then walked down them. “Have a good night guys… I have some other business to attend to.” “You too Roland.” They both said as they went back to work. And just before he reached the curtains, LLB turned around… to take one last look at the wrestling ring. Maybe… just maybe… it could go on without him.
