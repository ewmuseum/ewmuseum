The Beta: Repression (3)

19 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This bus never stops traveling. It might for two hours in the day, but other then that, it will keep going. It is not important if I am really here or not. The thing is still going to travel from point A to point B. Don’t make a fucking difference if I am between. This is when I really consider that this life is overrated. You always have to rebuttal and cause shit to happen. If you don’t, your not really a good, successful person in this world. So, why am I getting all this money? Why am I on television? Why I am here doing this? The sure is more people in this world more deserving of this then me. It is like, watching all the people more qualified for a job get passed over because there is something that makes you stand out amongst them crowd. Might be your large tits. Maybe your six pack build. Maybe it was something else. The way you said you were naughty to lock that job up. All of that connects in the end. You get the job, and find you are in a deeper pile of shit then you once thought. My pit is deep enough to take Payback through the other side of the world with a light jab at the ground. Seeing someone like Sean Cylear out there, all dead and that, it kind of set something off inside my pool of scattered brains and fragments of a human being. I have been broken down. No need to continue on this road. I, however, need one last great hurrah. That’s why I kind of came back. I needed to prove myself as a dangerous human fucking being. More then a waste of blood, flesh, and bone. I need to prove to all enemies that I am forever unbeatable. That I will stop at no length to make them understand. Joey never has understood. He wasn’t robbed of his will to live. I have been. After so often, you go a little nuts. You start to hate. It all boils. It is like a pressure cooker. My hatred is all bottled up, and slowly, it is being released. I have yet to scratch the surface on how deep this hatred is. People might be surprised. I don’t care. I am not content of living anymore. Hatred is the only thing that keeps me going now. Hatred for one. For him. The bus stops. I stumble off. I collapse. I am trash. Just like you.
