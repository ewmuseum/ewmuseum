Action! — Tales

The Velcro Heart, The Twenty-First Part of

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Left hand. “I can take it...” Left hand. “I CAN TAKE IT...” Left hand. “I CAN FUCKING TAKE IT!!” The thing was, he could. KroW received shot after shot to the jaw line, as LLB reeled back and threw them as hard as he could. So hard that blood drew from KroW’s chin... and from ‘The Law’s’ hand. Left ha- Blocked. KroW fired a roaring elbow into LLB’s forehead, forcing himself to jam against the side of the wall... thus KroW took a run. LLB moved, hence KroW smacked into the side of the brick, tumbling back and into LLB’s arms. ‘The Law’ upchucked yet another left hand... nonetheless ‘The Satanic God’ did duck it. The Action! Champion’s palm went dead into a pillar. “You fuck!” He cried... as LLB looked at KroW. There was that stupid little prick... the one that played by “the battlefield” or now, “life” as he said. It didn’t matter to LLB... KroW was a sin that needed to get his. Play by ‘The Law’s’ rules... or prepared to fall from it. KroW looked back at LLB. The big, giant fuck himself... the center of attention, but the one not deserving to be. It’s like LLB needed his hand to be held in the wrestling world these days. Before... he was not like that. Before KroW could even respect the “court system”... Times change, and so do the people along with it. That Title Belt would shortly go across the arms of a real Champion. Anger rose and tension built; the two started to exchange rights and lefts again. There was no wrestling to it anymore. It was a fight... A fight where you were lucky to survive... and most people... Just didn’t...
