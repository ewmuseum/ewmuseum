Love Geometric - VII

31 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/31/2002 October 23rd, 2002 Wednesday, Noon 'I am *so* glad to be done with that stupid promotion.' Coral said, idly, to his girlfriend, Allison Lindum. He had managed to get an early break from the promotion of the Halloween Pressure Point, so now he was home in Mesa, he was going to spend as much time as possible with Allison as possible. Maybe he'd even find some costumes for himself and Allison. 'I know. Especially since you wanted me to be at the Halloween show,' Allison said, 'And I think I'm just a wee bit too old to be trick-or-treating.' They walked along. They found a little temporary costume store in the center of the mall. As Allison looked through the various ridiculous-looking adult costumes, Coral found a ghost costume that looked more akin to a white blanket with a few holes in it than an actual costume. Jokingly, he threw it over his head and jumped out at Allison. 'Booga booga!' was what the ridiculous image of a grown man with a sheet over his head yelled at Allison. Rather than screaming, Allison simply whacked Coral across the shoulder with her right arm. Startled, but laughing, Coral stumbled backwards and threw the sheet off of his head. 'Coral,' Allison lilted, 'Aren't you a little old to be doing stuff like that?' Coral merely laughed, adjusted his black Joey Malone t-shirt, and put the ghost costume back. He had actually wanted to have a Coral Avalon t-shirt, but Reed Young apparently thought that having any merchandise relating to a guy who's sometimes refered to as 'the Kleptomaniac' would have a bad image or something. This, of course, made Coral a tad upset, but he figured that the demand would increase one way or another. 'Hey,' Coral laughed, 'You're the one who dragged me here. I can have a little fun, right?' She smiled at him. While their relationship was rocky at best, since Allison was studying to major in physics and therefore didn't have the time on her hands that Keri did, they were almost as close as Joey and Keri were. Of course, if they had any idea what Keri was doing right now, they'd probably question this comparison. Finally, Allison snapped her fingers. 'Hey, wait a second. Why don't you just go as the guy from Firefly? The nice-looking fellow with the brown coat?' 'You want me to go as him?' Coral asked. Then he thought about it. Easiest costume in the world, and he owned a brown coat somewhere in the house. '...Okay, fine.' Allison smiled and declared, excitedly, 'And I can find a red wig and be Syndey Bristow from Alias!' Coral rolled his eyes, as he forgotten how much she loved that show. 'Sure,' he hastily said, 'Whatever you want, Ali.' They both laughed, and held hands as they left the little costume shop and walked along the crowded sections of the Arizona Mills mall. They were happy to be together on a slow day like today, and just be carefree. She clung on to Coral's left arm, and rested her head on his shoulder. They walked along like this for a little while, before Coral noticed somebody. 'Hey, isn't that Keri?' Coral asked, pointing in the direction of Allison's older sister and Coral's housemate. 'Y-yeah, it is.' Allison said, slightly surprised to see her in the mall. The young couple wandered over to Keri, who was dressed very differently from usual, in a white tanktop and a pair of black leather pants. Allison was the first to notice this when she and Coral approached. 'Geez, sis, raid my closet much?' Keri looked up sharply, obviously distracted by something that neither Coral nor Allison could detect. 'Wh-what? No... no no,' Keri said, looking down at her clothes, 'There are mine.' Allison giggled. 'Okay, then, I'll take your word for it.' she said, sitting down next to her big sister. Coral joined them, sitting beside Allison. There was a short pause, before Keri asked her next question. 'Er, Coral? Why are you back so soon? Why didn't you call to tell me?' Keri asked. Coral fidgeted a bit, before he answered her question. 'We... well, I wanted to spend a little more time with Ali, here,' Coral said, putting his arm around Allison as he did so, 'So I think it just sort of slipped my mind. Besides, I'm just as surprised to see you here. Aren't you supposed to be with Joey in Uniondale?' Keri nodded and sighed in response to this, 'I was tired and wanted to go home. Is that so wrong?' she said, batting away a few strands of her hair as she did so. Coral and Allison looked at each other, and both of them just simply shrugged. 'I guess not.' Coral said. 'Yeah.' Allison added, a moment later, mimicking the same hair-batting motion that Keri did a moment ago. She didn't know why she did it, but she did it anyway. Finally, Allison got up, which made Coral, too, get up. 'Take it easy, sis, Coral and I are going to wander around here some more. We'll probably hang out for a while longer, so I guess we'll be back by five.' Allison said. With that, she resumed holding on to Coral's arm, and they left Keri by herself. Keri watched as they left, and almost wished that she could be as happy with Joey as Allison was with Coral. Instead of thinking about how to make things happier for she and Joey, though, she quietly waved Kent back over to her and advised him to take her home.
