Coming to terms with Ratings part1 - Depicting an artist

21 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.21.02 Bobby Minio was not a happy man. Contrary to how he might have felt two months ago, he wasn't angry either. He was dosile. Holding an ice pack on the back of his head with his right hand, his left in a bucket of ice. The war with Max Danger last night had taxed him, and just when the brawl was pushing to the limit, Angelica Dawson had came out of nowhere and laced Bobby with a boot to the head that he wouldn't live long enough to forget. The worst feeling of all, was that he knew he deserved it. But on camera, at night, he was a different person then now. A different entity. He didn't know why. At the moment he didn't, but the silent time on ice, which some might go as far as to consider as meditation, put the picture clearly infront of Minio. He was a puppet. Years ago he dedicated his life to wrestling. He dedicated his life to the Jim Johnsons, the Reed Youngs. Safety was NOT an issue. It was all about one thing. Ratings. It was subconcious, as it was for most men and woman in the industry. Always go over the top on camera. Always go the distance on air. Granted, Minio's actions were always Seventy Five percent based on Veronica, he never conciously made a descision. It was always decided by the camera. Behavior science would prove the fact that life is based around a pay check, and most sane people won't fuck that up. Minio's supposed insanity was all caused by his sane instincts to live life. The agents of PIW entertainment had said it best, at the time, Minio was much to naive to catch the warning signs. 'We've never heard a thing about you Bobby, but we gotta catch you on the television.' He had the look. He had the build. He was the subject of exploitation, and Action! wrestling was the same. Now, in september of 2002, Minio sat freezing parts of his body to numb the pain and stop the swelling, the conversation translated itself. He was much wiser now, if only a few years older. 'We depict an artist to break, we wanna break you. Yea, we wanna break you. Yes we wanna breka YOU, and we want to make millions in the process.' He felt anger in his belly, but it wasn't enough to get a rise. He only saw one thing in his mind. Sweet Aroma's lips in a smile. He wanted to make her smile. Even if he had to be an ass on television. He would come to terms with his boom mic'd reality soon enough, but now he had the smell of woman on his mind. The scent of a jackass.
