Cups: cXIII: Sea of Sorrow (Day 3)

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 28, 2002 Cups: cXIII: Sea of Sorrow Day 3 - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - ‘Day 3 Alone. I'm falling. No one is there ... No one is extending their arms! After a few hours, the falling began to end. The world I was in had finally reached its peek. The brim of the cup was full. I sense it. Yesterday ... I believe it was. I couldn't even recall meeting the guy ... now I remember assigning him a job. The Cup of Life. I feel it overflowing, yet he doesn't realize it. I've got to see him once more before I go. Killing away at each and every individual organ, it's hard to set something straight before you go. That's for sure. Being so calm, but ready to go. Being so alert, but ready to fight. Being so gone, and willing to stay. You begin to wonder if it's the right thing to do. In this situation, my options are limited. The choice to continue on or die right here is not in my hand. Once the thread has broke, there is no turning back. Once he releases me ... it's all over. Taking advantage of what you go these days is becoming rare. I look at the world from my perspective and see no intentions to live. I see no fuel to keep me alive anymore. I see nothing. A world with people as blind as a bat. Not realizing their actions and going on carelessly. It's hard to tell what'll come of this world after I'm gone. I know it's going to happen. All hell will break loose. I feel it. It's not too far. Not too long at all after I'm gone. The bullets. I feel the breeze coast by me, like it had something to do with me. I felt the screams. I felt the pain that everyone else will feel. Distracted in a world of my own, I sense it. I know he's going to come. Yet, I don't know why. I don't know how. I don't understand. It's all becoming foggy to me now. My time is almost over.' James couldn't handle it anymore. He slammed the book closed and stood to his feet. The tears fell over the cover of the book as he read the words plainly. The tear-drops faded the distinct lettering on the binder of the book... James cleared his head and began to talk.          'I now ... have something. These words actually mean something to me. I know that to others they have no meaning at all. I could care less. My time is now! Get it?'          James arose to a knocking on the door. He tip-toed his way over and looked out the peep hole. There was nothing. He swung the door open only to find a letter taped to the back.
