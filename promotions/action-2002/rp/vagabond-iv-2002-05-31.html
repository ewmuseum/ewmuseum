Vagabond - IV

31 May 2002

Date: 05/31/2002 Previous Parts: 1, 2, 3 'I can't believe you've come back to me like this, Joey.' 'Well, I...' 'Don't give me excuses, son.' Kyle Morris, former wrestling legend and current owner of the Shaved Beaver, said. 'You look like a train ran you over and every single box car smashed the shit out of you along the way.' 'That isn't too far from the truth.' Joey said, sitting down at a table next to his old mentor. 'Nitro Beaver' Kyle Morris was a long-time staple of the western United States wrestling scene in the 1970s and 1980s. Born in Canada, Kyle became one of the most well-known wrestlers in the area, long before Vince McMahon came in with his exciting new WWF product. Eventually, though, his increasing age and his deteriorating health caused him to settle down in Phoenix and train other wrestlers. Joey Malone and Daniel Phillips were just two of them. Not long after Malone and Phillips were trained did Morris quit training wrestlers to start the Shaved Beaver. From time to time, his former students come back to listen to his words of wisdom. For while Kyle never finished high school, he was far more wise than a large majority of the wiseasses that did. This is why Joey was here. 'So, what in the blazes knocked you around so badly, anyway?' Morris asked, eyebrows raised, with his eyes mainly focused at the bandages around Joey's head. 'Rashard Clark. Or Syphon Fission, as he's more commonly known.' Joey said, with a sigh. 'I fought him last Sunday, and he beat me. Pretty much kicked my ass doing so. If you've seen him before, you'll know that he's built like an ox and was given clearance to use a shovel in his matches. I haven't missed a training day since I got back from my honeymoon, and my head was into the match. So, I'm not sure what's wrong with my technique, and that's why I've come to you.' Kyle scratched his graying black goatee before he commented. 'Joey, you're not going to win every battle.' 'I know that! But Kyle, this match was perhaps the most important one in my life. I look pretty horrible, but that's because I had to borrow these clothes after I got put in the hospital, and the guy I borrowed this stuff from didn't have the greatest fashion sense in the world.' Kyle stared at Joey, who was wearing a short-sleeved plaid shirt and a pair of khakis. 'I can see that.' Kyle said. 'But as I said. No one can win every battle, no matter how great they are. This works both ways. I don't know about this Rashard Clark, but I do know that you'll face him again, and if your head's into it, you'll have a much better chance.' Kyle paused to let Joey soak up those words. Once he did that, he spoke some more. 'A good student will almost always surpass his teacher, Joey. You listened and learned to what I had to say, and still come back to hear my words. Which is more than I can say for any other student I ever had. You're a far better wrestler than I am, and that's why I'll always regard you as my best student. You can beat this guy, he just caught you on a bad day. You hear me?' Joey nodded. 'I hear you. Listen, I've gotta go and deal with my problems, now.' 'Take care, Joey.' 'You, too.' Joey stood up, and started to walk away, but as he approached Allison, Kyle's words echoed in his head again. They continued to do so until they clicked, and then he realized where he had to go. 'Allison, are you up for a drive to New Orleans?'
