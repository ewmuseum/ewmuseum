Becoming. Became. Undone. Part 2 - A new attraction

29 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9.29.02 Became. Bobby Minio sat in an empty locker room. Holding an icepack on his forehead, and another on his crotch. He just arrived in his room after his match with Angelica Dawson, where she apparently forgot he was in the match, and only targeted his manhood, if it even existed anymore. Minio felt like less of a man. He set off yesterday to make something of himself, and now here he was, stumbling around in a stuper, in extreme pain, probably sterile, after being beaten for twenty minutes by a woman. His balls felt like scrambled eggs, his brain felt like eggs over easy, and his eyes probably looked something like eggs over hard. Dawson had did a number on him. For a short amount of time, he found it hard to even try. He didn't know why. Whenever he tried to get his mind on straight, all he had was regret for nailing Aroma and causing her to fumble the match. But there was something else. He didn't know quite what it was. He couldn't put his finger on it. Moments passed. His iced meditation for the second time in two weeks once again brought him a revalation. Before he could even make sense of it, he blurted it out. 'Damn Angelica looked good tonight.' And then the shock sent in. What did he just say? Bobby found Dawson attractive, but still, not like that. Or did he? 'What the fuck did I just say?!' Bobby was caught off guard by himself. Was he losing his attraction to Aroma? Maybe getting impatient? He had no clue! His thoughts dangled out to when he was able to force an offensive against Dawson. He leaping from the top rope, crotch in his face. He remembered a split second stall, then his instincts dropped her into a sitdown powerbomb. He tried to focus on the rest of the match, reviewing it as he did every match, he hadn't even gotten to the pleading from Aroma, causing him to get schoolboy'd. His mind dwelled on the 'powerbomb'. Moments passed, then Minio looked down at his lower regions. 'Well, apparently i'm not impotent. Hmm, can't let this go to waste...' He grabbed the icepack and set it on the table beside him, picking up the Action! Magazine with Clit and Candy posing on the cover in their bikinis from a recent swim suit photoshoot. A mischievious grin played on his face as the camera faded to black.
