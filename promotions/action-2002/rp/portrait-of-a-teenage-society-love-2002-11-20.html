Portrait of a Teenage Society : Love

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 20th, 2002 There was a girl. There's always a girl. She's the reason my door's been shut. Her name was Sarah. I would go on for days with my bitter sweet description, but I won't bore you with the details. All you need to know is she was perfect for me. Too bad I wasn't nearly as perfect for her as I needed to be. She helped me through the tough times in my younger life, you know? There may have been a lot of them, but just taking one glance at her put a smile on my face for the rest of the day. And if I would have just had her once, I'm sure I would still be happy today. Too bad I wasn't nearly as important to her as she was to me. We sat together in Chemistry in eleventh grade. We were friends of friends beforehand but this was the first time we really communicated. Although I don't remember how, I do know that I was a different man around her. I was a better one. I'd wake up on school days with a spring in my step and a smile a mile wide, just because I knew that in a few short hours, I'd be there, and she'd be there, and for that moment, we'd be together. I'd like to say that moment lasted forever, but they didn't. In fact, they were some of the shortest times of my life. I know this is stupid, but time flew by when I was with her, and I just prayed and hoped that time would just stop for a moment. Time listens to no man. All that bullshit about time stopping and moments lasting forever is a crock of overdramatic bullshit. It's supposed to be romantic. It's supposed to be heartfelt. Lies are romantic? Lies are heartfelt? No wonder I never got love. But I got her. And I swore she got me. Too bad I was wrong. Too bad thse walls are locked now. Too bad the keys been lost. Too bad you can't get in. 'Who's gonna love you when you hate yourself?'
