Action! — Tales

More Aftermath from Players & Pawns

3 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It hadn't been the best way to spend his New Year, for either man in fact. The hospital wasn't a place you ever wanted to be. And being there because someone got seriously hurt, well, that just made it suck even more. But Conrad Ramsey had come. He had come with Airborn Tyler. Doesn't make sense, does it? The two were fighting in a Chair Match just hours earlier, and here the sixty-one year old man was, making sure the eighteen year old, was going to be okay. But he wasn't convinced. He had been unable to convince himself that everything was going to be okay. He need reassurance himself. He needed some comfort. There was only one thing to do. Call Samantha. Conrad had made his way to the pay phone. He inserted the proper change and dialed the number. She picked up after just one ring. She sounded as if she hadn't slept for a long time. There seemed to have been tears in her eyes. Hello? she whispered from the other end. 'Samantha? What's wrong?' Conrad asked. There was a sniff on the other end and then Samantha said, It's... I saw.... I saw what happened. What happened with that kid. It's... I didn't... She was having a hard time continuing. Funny, Ramsey thought, he had wanted her to comfort him, but it was he, who was having to comfort her. 'It's alright, Sam. It's alright.' She wasn't listening. It's just... I've been through this before, Conrad. I've lost a son in the ring. When I saw him fall... she was trailing off. I could only think of Marcus. I don't want that to happen to him. He... He... I can't lose him, too, Connie! She burst into tears, but she pushed them back and continued. You make sure he's alright, okay? Will you do that for me? He wasn't sure who exactly she was talking about. Airborn or Max? But he figured he knew. She meant both of them. Don't let that boy go, Conrad! Be at his side. 'I will.' he assured her. But he wasn't sure there was much he could do. It looked bad for the youngest Action! superstar. And you look after Marcus, too. You hear me? Look after him. 'I will.' he again assured her. She had begun to cry again. Conrad just listened, wanting to hold her and tell her everything was going to be okay. But he was pulled away from her. A doctor had come up to him. 'Mr? Sorry didn't get your name.' the doctor said. 'Oh. Ramsey. Mr. Conrad Ramsey.' 'You are Mr. Tyler's father, correct?' He could hear Samantha growing quiet on the other end. She didn't know that. Conrad wasn't his father, but he had said he was. He was also seeing the doctor's quizzical look. 'Yes, I am. Divorced. He took his mother's name.' The doctor just nodded. 'Well, we're going to need to ask you some questions about your son. How everything happened, some background info, stuff like that.' Conrad nodded his head, 'I'll be right with you, just let me finish this phone call.' 'To the mother?' the doctor wondered. Ramsey shook his head, 'No, my girlfriend. Though concerned as a mother should be, for the young lad.' He smiled weakly. The doctor just smiled and then pointed at his office, where Conrad would talk with him. Ramsey nodded, and then turned his attention back to the phone. You're his father!? I had no idea. 'I'm not, Samantha. I-I just said I was. Don't ask why. It just came out that I was.' Oh. 'Well, I've gotta talk with the doctor. I'll see you when I can. I really wish I could have spent the New Year's with you.' Me too. But you take care of him. Of them. 'I will.' I love you. 'I love you, too.' Bye. 'Bye.' And with that he hung up the phone. He took a deep breath. What was he going to tell the doctor? He knew nothing about Tyler. Nothing at all. But he had made a promise. One he intended to keep.
