The Wrestling Ring, The Third Part of

23 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lawyer’s Lounge. To the average person, Lawyer’s Lounge wasn’t known… or understood. No one was sure what it meant when they saw the little “LL” logo at the bottom of every ticket. And no one really put that much thought in it either. That was okay to LLB. He never wanted to look for fame on his brand new project. In fact, the less people knew… the less cheap shots he might take… and the more enjoyable his work would become. So what is Lawyer’s Lounge? Lawyer’s Lounge started one month ago, from the spendings of LLB. It was to support those of the mentally and physically handicapped. It was a chance for those that were born without perfection… to sit back and enjoy a wrestling program, in box seats… with popcorn, pizza… you name it, they had it. The event happened every Pressure Point. Ten or so kids, along with their parents… would be able to spend a show at the wrestling event. They were treated to a movie before… then the two hour wrestling show… and after, a tour throughout the backstage while everyone left. It was now two hours after Pressure Point had aired… and everyone up in Lawyer’s Lounge would soon be packing it in. After the tour, the “final surprise” would come. LLB himself would appear… signing autographs for free, and getting a chance to brighten everyone’s day. To meet those of the less fortunate. ‘The Law’ walked up the flight of stairs to get to the second floor. It was a successful program so far… and it really made LLB feel at home, like he was accomplishing something. Something more than anything else before. However, with ‘The Law’s’ lack of appearances on some of the recent Pressure Points… there always seemed to be somewhat of an emptiness left with his guests for that week. Nonetheless, the program was successful… everyone was happy… and so was LLB. A sense of urgency struck the “lawyer” a moment before he always reached the door handle. Just to see the looks on their faces was good enough for him… Some would say the feeling he got was more than a Title. Some would say the feeling he got never equaled to anything else in LLB’s life… And as LLB opened that door, and those children, who thought the night was just about over, had seen him… well it was worth more than a Title. And it was the best feeling in LLB’s life. “LLB!” The children shouted. A few of them couldn’t… but their disabilities were evidence of that… and LLB still believe he made them happy. “Hey guys…” ‘The Law’ said. “Has Judge Joe shown you a good time today?” Stupid name… but to a bunch of children who were having the time of their life, who really cared? “Yeah!” They shouted… as Rory stumbled across the floor to give LLB a hug. “Now before you guys leave…” He said to them all. “I’ll autograph anything you would like…” The response was almost deafening… but it made LLB happy. He took every t-shirt… every photo… every odd and end… and signatured it as best as he could. From where LLB stood… he was able to look out and see Bill and Robbie still working on the ring. LLB didn’t take long to move his eyes back from the ring… and over in the direction of his new photo to sign.
