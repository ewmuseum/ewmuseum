Action! — Tales

Get ready.

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/29/2002 Madison Square Garden. Otherwise known as THE arena. Eighteen thousand screaming fans. This is what every wrestler dreamed of. To wrestle in front of the most famous arena in the world, and electrify the capacity crowd with every ounce of talent that they had in their body. It was an arena full of blood, sweat, and tears. Resentment. Two days. More blood, more sweat, and more tears shall be etched into the hollowed halls of the Garden. It was becoming a sort of a consciousness, manifesting itself into the electricity that only wrestling in Madison Square Garden can bring. Much history has been made in this arena, and now, more shall be made. For Joey Malone, there was something that needed to be done. The last time Joey wrestled in this arena, he suffered an injury that put him out for a month, causing him to lose the title he held at the time. Although he came back to action and exploded onto the scene, he never forgave himself for blowing his performance in the most important arena in the world. That was fifteen months ago. Today, Joey Malone is a different man. He's married, no longer financially secure, and optimistic for the future. And in two days, he had a chance to redeem himself. He had a chance to make up for the injury that kept his match from stealing the show. He had a mountain the climb to acheive his goal, but he had the tools and the iron will to make the trip. That mountain was Syphon Fission. His friend. The prize for climbing the mountain? A shot at the Action Championship. It was worth it to him. No wrestler should not have a goal to one day become the top champion of the business, after all. So Joey had only one thing to do to prepare for his mountain climbing. Get ready.
