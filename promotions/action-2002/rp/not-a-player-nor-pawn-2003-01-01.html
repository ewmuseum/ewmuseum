Not a Player Nor Pawn

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/01/03 OPEN Where were you? At a party? With your partner? Where were you when the clock struck 12? Well, we know where one person was.... In the third year of the 21st Century, a “three year old” ‘Kid’ ran into the high profile of Dr. Blufinland’s house, in brutalizing pain! Title: Not a Player Nor Pawn Date: 01/01/03 2:12 am EST Place: Dr. Blufinland’s House Chris rushed in and jumped on top of Blufinland’s glass table. Early our hero encountered a fatal “arm breaker” thanks to Sean Cylear at the Players and Pawns pay per view. Blufinland’s house was nicely dressed with high tech gizmo’s and whatnot, besides.... he is a rich doctor. Fox “Oww, oww, help me Bufinkand! My army hurts! I think Shawn broke it!” Blufinland “Just calm down. I’ll never be able to stop the pain if you keep squirming!” Cooperation. Fox lay. My left arm, that’s my ice cream arm! Look at it, all bent around like that! I could probably go on the Maury Povich show.... BIG TIME! Blufinland “Oh crap.” Fox “What?” Blufinland “It’s broken. Shattered in two places! You’ll need to wear at least a cast with iron strengtheners.” Fox “You’re going to put rods in my arm?” The glass table cracked a bit. Blufinland “No, they’ll be in the cast to straighten your arm.” Shattered! Just like Fox’s arm. The glass table collapsed and with it came Chris crashing down and cutting his arm open a little more. Fox “IT HUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUURRRRRTS!” The once peaceful, clean, high price looking home of Dr. Blufinland had now become blood stained and quite messy. With each step Blufinland took towards Chris, the crackling of glass could be heard. Blufinland “Well, I’m calling for backup.” Blufinland thought to himself: “Not a good way to start the new year Chris. I feel sorry for you bud.” Just as Blufinland snatched the cordless, Fox prepared to accept his fate. Fox “Well Blufy, if I had to spend new years with someone. I’m glad it’s you.” Blufinland “LOL?” Fox “Nope, heh.... big time.” Then, without hesitation, Blufinland made the call to his comrades. Now the only thing that Fox has to prove is whether he is just a pawn and will quit.... or a player and return to the battle field, stronger, faster.... and better. But Fox isn’t a player nor pawn; he’s a master! And he’ll be back with a vengeance to conquer and rule.... BIG TIME! CLOSE The scene reopened for just a second to show Pikachu in the back seat of Blufinland’s car, sitting there in the night....
