Crossing the Border

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 24, 2002 The man...was Simon Seaman. Destination...unknown. Well it sounded pretty neat to say it, but to state it as true would be lying. He sat in his car bopping his head to the catchy music that was playing in his brand new Volkswagen beetle. Currently in a traffic jam on his way to the United States border, he took a moment to take a load off and simply get down with his supposed bad self. Really getting into the song, he suddenly became distracted by something in his peripheral vision. He turned the music down and scooted over to the passenger seat. Leaning against the window, he attempted to get the attention of a group of attractive female vacationers stuck next to him in a convertible. 'Any of you ladies dig Enrigue Iglesias? I know I do. Forget Anna Kournikova because his beats are beautiful enough.' Simon proclaimed with confidence as the ladies ignored him and tried to stay awake in the blistering sun. 'I'm down with the hip music of the seventies, eighties, nineties as well as today. Trust a connoiseur such as myself. You blast this song up real loud and you'll be dancing to it as well. You should hear the remix too. Enrique featuring AC/DC...now who wouldn't listen to that?! Some say it would never happen, but it did and I'm glad it did. Who's with me?' As he failed to create a conservation with the group, he sat back in his seat and thought of other means to get their attention. Fiddling with something in his glove compartment, he leaned over the open window and sounded a signal horn. Although annoyed, they finally noticed Simon across from them. In turn, one of the girls spoke to him in an attempt to get rid of him. 'What the hell do you think you're doing?' the driver of the car said with a hint of anger in her tone of voice. 'Sitting in my beetle, ladies. Blue lagoon if you'd like to know the colour. So what brings you to the border?' he inquired. 'Um, we'd like to go back to the United States 'cause we like live there. Does that answer your question?' she responded sarcastically. Simon then rummaged through his glove compartment a second time while the girl proceeded. 'I'm sure you're a nice guy and all, but we're not really in the mood for your, you know, charming demeanor. Do you get what I'm saying?' 'Oh, I'm not trying to hit on any of you...nothing like that. I'm just a single guy with an above average physique who is not afraid to cry if the time calls for it. A simple, but popular champion of a successful wrestling organization who makes a good living and does his job because he enjoys it.' Simon replied as the women glanced at each other in confusion. 'Then why did you talk to us?' the lady in the passenger seat asked. He paused for a second while he picked up something. Facing the group, he glared at each one of them and then exposed what he had in his possession. 'Spanish fly, anyone?' Simon answered with a bit of hope in the delivery of his question. With expressions of shock on the girls' faces, we would join the Carnage champion fifteen minutes later as he inched closer and closer to the border. A big smile was seen on his face as he checked himself out in the rear view mirror. Tapping his palms rhythmically on the steering wheel, he nodded his head in agreement of what he was looking at. 'Simon, you animal.' Tilting his head to the left, we noticed the other side of his face completely red. 'Sometimes too much female contact is bad for you, my man.' he proclaimed. He attempted to touch his face, but drew his hand back quickly it as his face continued to sting. Moments later, he drove up to the border and greeted the official who was manning the lane. 'How's it hanging, officer? Peachy keen with no caffeine?' 'Sir, you know the deal...' the portly gentleman with a thick southern accent replied. 'I know, I know. I watch the news. More CNN Headline News than the actual CNN. I get my news faster that way. Just the way I like it. Plus, the women are more attractive, but that's another story.' Simon stated. With Simon's vehicle parked to the side, he stood there with his arms folded as the officer checked his car for anything suspicious. 'Everything looks perfectly fine.' 'Really?' Simon asked the officer. 'Well, except for the pinata shaped like a donkey holding a steel chair, the fireworks, the jugs of Mexican water, the flaming pieces of wood strapped to the top of your car, the super soaker filled with I don't know what, and the two illegal immigrants carrying boxes of jagged taco shells in the back seat, you're good to go.' Glancing at his car and the contents inside, he laughed it off and then tried to explain himself. 'Oh, those are all for my job that I have. You see I wrestle and I would like to use all this stuff in these little things I have called matches. Yeah, I need those tacos especially. You know how hard it is to find food for physical use? I can't go to Taco Bell since apparently they only think tacos are strictly for eating. What do they know anyway?...' Simon exclaimed as the officer glanced at him in disbelief.
