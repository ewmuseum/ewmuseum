Finding Himself(1)

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-4-02 The golden sun leaked down through the clouds and soaked Jonny Yankee in its auburn rays. Today was a good day for the General. Retaliation had come, gone, and favored Jonny Yankee. He had beaten that aggravating little thorn, Airborn Tyler. He knew his victory wouldn’t end the battle though, Jonny was still bitter with Tyler, and Tyler was still bitter with Jonny. He knew that deep down. On the surface though, Jonny was jovial as ever with his victory over Airborn Tyler. He walked down the city streets of Washington D.C., dressed in his union outfit, humming an old Union tune of “Old Dan Tucker.” People stared at him as he passed by them with his hands in his pockets. But Jonny merely smiled at them and nodded his head like the gentleman he was. Jonny was used to being stared out by now, he understood that he was out of place in this modern world. But Jonny was who he was, and nothing could change that. He had spent some of his hard-garnered money on a train ticket, and he had made his way here to his great nation’s capital. He had come here to see the sights, to pay his respects to casualties of other wars. He also came here with a plan though. Jonny stopped walking, and he stopped humming, and he stopped nodding at the people who stared at him. He looked up at sign of the building he stood in front of. “Social Security Agency” Jonny was tired of walking around in this bizarre world without any proof of identification. Today was a good day for The General
