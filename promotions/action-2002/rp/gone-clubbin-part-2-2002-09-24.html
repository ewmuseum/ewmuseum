Gone Clubbin' - Part 2

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCREEEEEEECH! BOOM! POP! Those were the sounds of the '65 pinto rolling up to club G-G's. Smoke filled the area as the tailpape was emitting it. Garvin's old, worn-out factory system bassed the theme to 'Ducktales' as he put the car in park. 'Ducktales! OOOUUUUUOOO!' 'I'm not getting out.' said Odium with an expression on his face that reads, 'I can't believe I'm going to a club with this fool'. 'Come on! Look! People are looking at my ride! And smiling, too!' 'How can you be so fucking dense?' Odium said as he lit a cigarette. 'Put that out! I don't want that smell in my bitchin' car!' 'Man, your car smokes more than a group of weed addicts in a gasoline fire. Don't tell me what to do. I can't believe I've even let you drag me this fucking far. You're nothing but a waste of time.' Garvin looked at Odium. Hurt. Jeff was hurt. Odium, Ody. His best friend called him a waste of time. Jeff's bottom lip started to quiver. 'HAHAHAHAHAHAHA! Man! You're a riot! Let's go!' Odium's jaw dropped and his cigarette fell onto the floor. He stepped on it and got out of the car reluctantly. 'Would you like me to park your car sir?' a valet man asked as he met Garvin getting out of the driver's side. 'Sure! Be careful and NO JOY-RIDING!.' 'Uh...Yes sir. Might I say, you're looking snazzy tonight.' said the valet driver sarcastically. 'First time at the club?' 'Nah, I'm a regular.' said Garvin trying to fit in. Jeff brushed off his unbuttoned hawaiian shirt and dusted off his faded, black leather pants. As he approached the door, he drug Odium with him. Jeff proceeded to walk past the bouncer but was halted. 'Hey! You on the list buddy?' 'Yeah, it's under 'Jeff' or 'Garvin' or 'The Original'. I come here so many times, I forget what my home-dawgs call me...word.' 'End of the line, pal.' Odium thought to himself, 'Yes! The embarrassment has ended!'. 'And take that big oaf with you.' MISTAKE 'Big what?' said Odium, getting into the bouncers face. Before the bouncer could respond, Odium gave him an uppercut from hell, knocking him out. Odium pushed the doors open, knocking a waiter down, drinks and all. Jeff looked at Odium walking into the club. 'Now this guy knows how to party! Smooth Brothers Style!' Jeff strutted up to Odium doing his 'pimp-walk' as the doors swung shut.
