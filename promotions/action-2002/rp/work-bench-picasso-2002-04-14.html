Work Bench Picasso

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/14/02 No Regrets. 'My return was a success. I've put Fear in its place. And in HIS place.' No Remorse. 'My attack was well planned. He looked me square in the eye before I levelled him. It wasn't cheap in the least.' No Mercy. 'I won't let up from my onslaught. Every punch has to draw blood. Every kick has to leave a bruise. Every chairshot has to dent steal and every bodyslam has to leave a crater in the canvas.' No Worries. 'Things will definitely gradually get worse. As much as LLB can rant, this isn't the Pinnacle. This is Action. It's unfriendly ground. I'm behind enemy lines. I have no allies, no back up. It's Minio versus the Three Musketeers. . .' No fucking way?! 'Three Musketeers? What the hell kind of Name is the Three Musketeers? You'd think Fox could have found something better to name their little posse then a Candy Bar.' No kidding. 'Well, as good as he is, he never was to0 bright. The kid can't even wipe his own ass.' A hand reaches forward in the darkness, there is a *cli-CLICK* heard, and a light flashes the screen for mere seconds. As the bright intruder recedes, Minio's hand still grips the chain from the lamp beside the wooden chair he sits upright as a rocket ship in. His fingers slide off of the lamp and his hands rest in his lap. He looks slightly . . . Distraught. A Tipped soda can lies spilling liquid on the table which the lamp rests on. Minio is sweating. His hair is plastered to his forehead and his eyes are the only determined thing in the frame. The camera slides back, showing from behind as Minio looks at a Mirror. 'There's holes in this canvas. I'm a real piece of work.' He pauses bitterly. 'A geniune Work bench Picasso.' He stands, then kicks the mirror away from the wall in which is was propped up against. It doesn't shatter. It doesn't crack. It just rolls awkwardly then lies on its face. The back has a message, scribbled in the jelly from a pastry which is now in view. A Half eat doughnut which was squeezed beyond edibility. The message reads plain and clear. 'Theres nothing to Fear, but Fear itself.' Minio's shadow is overcastted on the wall behind the Mirror. He stands, his arm reaches under the lamp shade. Darkness. Footsteps are heard walking away from the camera.
