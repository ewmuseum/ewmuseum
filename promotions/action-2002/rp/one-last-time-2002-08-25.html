One Last Time

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/25/02(Right After Danger vs. Ramsey @ D4TC) MAX DANGER is bleeding all over the place in the backstage area. He had just finished his match with CONRAD RAMSEY, which turned out differently than anyone had thought. Sweet Aroma and Angelica Dawson rushed the ring, Aroma pinned Danger, and were awarded the Dyad Titles. Bizarre. But now Max is being taken care of by a medic, who's trying to close the wound given to him by Ramsey. MAX DANGER Fuckin' old bastard! I'll get him for this! As Max continues to vent his frustrations to the medic, Conard Ramsey comes walking through the curtains, limping badly. He looks completely worn out both physically and mentally. One of the assistants gives Ramsey his old wooden cane to help him. Max hears the tapping of the cane and turns. He tells the medic to stop for a second as he walks over to Ramsey, with the plantimun cane in hand. DANGER Who do you think you are? Look what you did to me! Danger pointed at his head, where some stitching was hanging down, as the medic didn't get to finish up. Ramsey didn't say anything, as you could see the pain in his face. DANGER Well? WELL!? Are you going to apologize for this!? HUH!? Ramsey slowly looked up at Danger, then rammed his cane into his stomach. Danger doubled over and Ramsey clocked him across the back with the cane, snapping it in half. This gave Danger a little time to recover and take a major swing with the platinum cane. Ramsey ducked it just in time and punched Danger in the face. DANGER HELP! HELP! Crazy old man on the loose! Danger was tripped up and Ramsey got on top of him and began to press the platinum cane against Danger's throat. Danger was having trouble breathing. He was kicking his feet as several assistants ran over and pulled Ramsey off of him. Danger slowly sat up catching his breath. Both men sat on the ground staring. DANGER You know what old man? We were screwed out there. Those two bitches kept up from beating the living hell out of each other. Ramsey didn't say anything. He was breathing heavy as he kept his eyes on Danger. DANGER I say we team up one last time and take those bitches out! What do you say? Just one more time, then we can go back to trying to kill each other. So? Ramsey was helped up onto his feet and he limped over to Danger. They stared each other down, then Ramsey extended his hand. Danger took it and was helped up by his nemesis. Ramsey got close to his face. CONRAD RAMSEY Just one more time, then I finish you off. Ramsey limped past Max and headed down the hallway slowly. Danger just smiled as he watched Ramsey leave. The medic came back over and finished up his work on 'The Danger Man'. FADE OUT
