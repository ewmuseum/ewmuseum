Friendly Phone Call

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Noon, 8.28.02 BRRRRIIIING. BRRRIIIIING. BRRRRIIIIII* 'Hello?' - 'Veronica?' 'Oh. Hi Bobby. Whats up?' - 'Congrats on the win last night Ver. I'm proud of you guys.' 'Oh thanks, thanks Alot. We're proud too.' - 'Ya. Now if I could just win something, hah! Well. My time will come around for something. I hope.' 'It will. Don't worry. I heard you had some arguement in the parking lot last night but you were a changed man.' - 'Yea, I feel much better this morning. Sun is shining in a different tint kinda thing. Corny, but true. Ah. I dunno. Maybe i'm just feeling high off of that Pay Per View.' 'I guess. Alright, i'm gonna head to the gym. I'll see you at Pressure point!' - 'Aight. Definately. I can't wait. Peace.'
