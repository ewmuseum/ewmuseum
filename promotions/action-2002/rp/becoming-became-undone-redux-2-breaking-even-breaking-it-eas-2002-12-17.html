Becoming. Became. Undone. Redux 2 - Breaking Even, Breaking it Easy

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/17/02 'You always come close but this never comes easy, I still know everything.' - Taking Back Sunday He sat on the floor of his relatively empty apartment. He was bleeding on the inside, he was bleeding out his dilemas. He lived two lives. He worked in Action! Wrestling, and he was now back in Pinnicle of Insane Wrestling. He was torn to loyalties. On one hand, Reed Young had given Minio a chance when no one else in the industry would even look at his tapes. On the other, Reed has put Minio through a number of hells. Minio got his upstart in the mainstream from Jim Johnson and the PIW. He wouldn't know people personally like Roland and Sean if he hadn't worked in PIW. He wouldn't be in Action! Wrestling. With the resurfacing of Sean Cylear, finding out his once long time opponent and friend was still alive, he was hit with various amounts of shell shock. There were alot of things already going on, with his fear of relationships, his fear of commitment, his fear of being alone... Every weakness he had was so far away from the wrestling ring... Why were they effecting his abilities? But they were beginning to overlap. They were climbing ontop of each other. He needed to somehow 'break up' with Angelica, but he did like her, he did have real legitimate feelings for her. He was just afraid... Afraid of being left like every man in wrestling eventually does. There was a horrible trend in the Wrestling Industry. Every man with a female companion that is anywhere near that Ring, ends up bitter and broken by that woman. Weather its on purpose, or inadvertant, weather its out of spite or out of confusion, it happens, and Minio was hell bent on not ending up like that. He had enough problems with Woman in the real world. The house is on fire, am I going to get burned? Magic Eightball says: More than likely.
