Messed Up Logic

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-5-02 I took Prozac to deal with my problems. My mind overpowered the stuff. I went to therapy. My mind overpowered the stuff. I can not stop looking at the images on the television screen. They are burned into my eyes. I will not forget them. Ever. Even if I wanted to let go of them, I couldn’t. Samuel Potright and Joey Malone. Two different creations. Two different forms of torture. Sam took away MY title and MY pride. He took away everything I had worked so hard to achieve. Potright was a bitter enemy of mine. He made me miserable because of his apathy. He was a pathetic human being. He was vile. I would hit him with everything I had, and he would still stand up. He would laugh. He would spit. He would violate the bounds of what it took ME a decade to achieve. He was so greedy for the spotlight that he broke the rules. He stopped trying to play by a script and started fighting me with a lot. IWO CEO Jamie Kosoy gave me assurance that if Potright broke the rules, he would be fired from the IWO. He was not going to see that LEECH steal my spotlight. Then… Nothing! NOTHING! Jamie loved his new champion. And he slanted my best friend as the next to crumble his perch. Not that lazy Donnie Daze. Joey Malone. Malone had to know that I had practically died after losing to Potright. He promised me that when he won the title… I would have my shot. In his pathetic excuse of a match with Daze…I think it was called Life. Death. And Endurance. That young man asked me to be in his corner, even though I was taking a break from wrestling. Slowly I was gaining back my pride and sensibility. Then he won the brutal thing, and couldn’t stand on his own two feet. I entered the ring as a friend to hand him that title. I left that ring as an enemy. I wanted so much to wipe the grin off that FUCKERS face. He had brought me back to see him with it. That title. That’s when he had overcome me. He had surpassed me. He wanted me to see that. To realize that he would not let me get back my spotlight. The fans loved him. I became the anti-hero. And I damn near broke his student’s back. Kent Anthason was nearly dead in that ring after my Faded Dreams move. That move was made for one person. JOEY MALONE! I was meant for him for one reason… Because of him, my dreams forever would become fading glimpses of what could have been. Joey was a little bastard. That move was meant to KILL him! But…I relented. I let him get away…and hit it on Anthason instead. I had gotten too lazy to take back my spotlight. After that…Malone came crawling back… He knew that I had a big bull’s eye on his back. No… I came crawling back. No… HE DID! No… I DID! I am losing myself again. I can feel this awkward feeling…the desire to ruin him. I do not want to ruin him… I just want to beat him. Then I will have my pride back. Then I can retire. This feeling…it’s weird. It’s familiar. I felt it that night before Beach Party…the same night I nearly killed Anthason. Under that ring… That Colt M1911A1 with a pair of handcuffs… It had a clip in it. And all the bullets had one name on them. Joey Malone I made him… I… Would break him. I… DAMMIT RASHARD! STOP THIS! GET A FUCKING HOLD OF YOURSELF! HE’S MY BEST FRIEND! HE’S… my only friend. I just wish they would remember me. Remember Syphon Fission… The Forever Champion.
