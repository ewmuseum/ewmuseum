Love Flushed Away [Final]

17 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 17 “OH WOE IS THE JOHN! OH WOE!” The wailings of the large Action! League leader can be heard from the men’s room of O’Leary’s Tavern. People in the bar are gathered around the door, trying to open the door, but something is forcing it shut. Security guards bash it with their boots and fist, but to no avail. And their saviour arrives. “POOP” The crowd turns to see Captain Obvious making his way across the dance floor. The Bee Gees, for some unapparent reason, strikes up. “Well you can tell by the…” Definitely the wrong song for Captain Obvious. With a spin and point from the ceiling to floor, he strides up. He pulls Toilet Duck from his pocket and motions for the crowd to let him through. In Biblical terms, it looks like the parting of the Red Sea. But Cap ain’t no Moses. “I heard that there was a problem because my partner is jamming the door. Jamming it somehow but I’m POOP not sure, y’know? But I do have some theories on how he’s done it. If the door was a push door, right, he’s placed POOP himself at the foot of the door so as there are no entries into the men’s room. Or he’s wedged some sort of device POOP at the foot so whenever you push it gets POOP stucker, y’know? Or if it were a pull door he might have wedged something in the handle. But it’s a twist handle, so he might POOP have tied some string or rope to it, if he had any on him, right, and therefore he’s POOP tied the other end to something else, or himself. Or he holds the string or rope. I can’t be sure if it’s string or rope, it may be POOP twine, definitely not wool because that would snap, y’know?” The crowd blinks as Captain Obvious rambles. [blink] Crowd: “SHUT UP!” “POOP” Captain Obvious walks up to the door. KNOCK! KNOCK! “What do you want?” replies the voice of the John. “Toilet Duck is here! POOP” Placing the John’s confidante at the foot of the door, he walks away to allow them some privacy of some description. The crowd blinks again. [blink] There appears to be some sort of conversation between the pair through the door. The John is heard to be saying the likes of “Uh huh”, “Yep”, “The John knows”, “Sure”, “Right” and “OK”. It seems like Toilet Duck is doing all the talking. Finally, the door creaks open and the John stands there. The crowd gasp as the behemoth steps through the door frame. He looks to them all with a tear in his eye. “The John has been mourning the loss of a lavatory. A drunken villain approached the porcelain, cursing of his problems with a loved one. Angered by her infidelity. Unfortunately, Rosa was the wrong toilet, in the wrong place at the wrong time. The villain kicked her. He shattered her porcelain skull and she flushes no more. Her life ended abruptly over the disasters of another’s life. Who deems fit to take another’s life over the mishaps of their own? It’s like the disgruntled postal worker on another wave length. The John thinks this is not right. The John thinks too many villains get away with too many crimes and all this will finish. All this will come to an end and the John predicts the time is nigh. Monday night, at Action! Wrestling’s Pressure Point, Captain Obvious, the John and Toilet Duck will venture into the unknown to bandy a crew together to protect those whom have had crimes committed against them. Who have suffered the injustices of other’s frailties and misdemeanours. The John says that those who shine the light of the dark side will have their wicks pulled from their waxy bodies, for the John’s tolerance is no more. The time to act is now. Captain Obvious, Toilet Duck, fine citizens. On Monday night, the John and his partners will bandy together to find more superheroes. More superheroes who too are tired of the injustices in this world. The greatest superhero team on earth will form to protect the citizens from these injustices. Prepare yourselves. Prepare yourselves for… …ACTION! LEAGUE” The crowd roars its approval as the John scoops Toilet Duck under his arm. He and Captain Obvious make their way across the dance floor and as timely as it is, “Hero” by the Foo Fighters cues over the room’s speakers. There they go. The greatest crime fighting duo EVEEEEEEEEEEER! [/valley girl] Action! League. The John and Captain Obvious. Look out criminals. Your time has come!
