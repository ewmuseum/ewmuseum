Cups: cV: Cup of Life

20 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 20, 2002 Cups: cV: Cup of Life 'How did you ...' James stopped the car and turned on the dome light immediately. A man was there. The same voice, the same attire, the same everything as what he had pictured. A deep dark suit, black in coloration. His hair hung down a little past his shoulders with a brownish tint. The face of the man was beautiful, precise marks took up a great look upon his face. His nose slightly rounded, his eyes widened and with a shallow blue coloration to them.          'Trust me, I can run rather quick. James, I was sent to deliver a message. Nothing more, nothing less.' He eyed Joker uneasily and then continued on. 'I'm sure you know who it is about.'          'Her...?' James questioned with an evil grin. Pondering the possibilities of what he could expect to hear from this guy's mouth.          'I've got to explain a few things first.'          'Yes, go ahead.'          The man looked at Joker and felt a bit rushed, yet continued on. 'That match with Babylon, hell of a fight if I can say so myself.'          'Oh, I've got to give you props for getting my ass beat. I appreciate that greatly. Thanks.'          'I had nothing to do with it. Since when is it a sin to watch a wrestling match. I was just another fan. I had planned on telling you nothing.'          James now became furious. 'Then why the HELL did you do something like that. You looked too much like my brother. You still do. If I didn't know he was in jail, I would be questioning you on that matter right now. Seeing that I know better than that, there would be no point. Now, on with your little story and enough about my match.'          The man slammed his hand down on the seat. 'You shall not command me. I'll go at my pace, this is my job. You're making it more complicated.'          'Well, I guess my job is to make things more complicated.'          The man scaled Joker and grabbed his shirt collar. The white muscle shirt cringed as the mans tight grip seized it easily. 'Listen up!' He demanded. 'This is no laughing matter. You've got to help her.'          James suddenly went in a straight line, changing all of his further output on things.          'What's the matter with her?' He asked eagerly. Wondering what is wrong with a love is hard. Seeing that he thought she was dead until recently, he would have done anything to help her.          'It may take a while to explain. Are you still interested?' The man frowned knowing the truth behind what had happened. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
