Dreams were meant to be broken: Chapter 8

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Now, Vincent, you got anything you'd like to mention to me before you go where ever the Hell it is people like you go?' Fiend asked his victim. A lonely, blue tear rolled down the cheek of Vincent. He still couldn't talk. His tears just choked him. All Vincent could do was attempt words, but due to Fiend's lack of patience, this would not be the case. 'I'm afraid, Vinny, I don't got all day to chat with you. So, I'm gonna end this conversation here, Vin.' Fiend removed the knife from digging into the neck of Vincent, it was time to make Vincent very red. Very. Fiend pushed Vincent to the ground, straddling his shoulders. He pushed the knife into Vincent's mouth and nicked a half inch cut in the corner of Vincent's mouth. He then did the same on the other side. 'I'm a little uncertain as to how this works,' Fiend said as he stood up off of Vincent's chest, 'so I guess we'll both find out as we go along, right?' Vincent tried to hold back the scream, he tried with all his might. 'Oh, there's a second part. You didn't think you were getting off that easily, did you Vincent?' he asked. Fiend stepped to the side, raising his foot above the fallen body of Vincent Palazzio. 'Say 'Hi' to God for me, Vinny. Tell him Fiend sent ya,' he remarked. His foot came down with amazing force and speed, expelling all the breath from Vincent's body. The only thing that came to Vincent's mind was scream in pain as Fiend's foot twisted in his mid-section. '<>AAAAAAAARRRRRRRRGGGGGGGHHHH!' But screaming wasn't the best plan. The corners of Vincent's mouth began to rip apart as he screamed loudly. Red began to saturate the asphalt. The red began to run down his neck, soaking his body as he lay there, not knowing what to do. Not knowing where to go. Not knowing how to prepare for the next place. The next place. Was it heaven for Vincent Palazzio? Or was he being sent to the same place as the man who just put an end to his being. Was he going to end up in Hell with the man called Fiend? His life force was draining onto and soaking into the asphalt. Fiend looked down onto the asphalt of the road and he knew that this red was blood.
