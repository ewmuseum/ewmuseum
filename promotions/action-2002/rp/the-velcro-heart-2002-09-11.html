Action! — Tales

The Velcro Heart.

11 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“And I had him Roland... I HAD HIM RIGHT WHERE I WANTED!” LLB rubbed his head. It was already a long, long day. Reed Young sat across from him in the locker room area, icepack on his head... and a heavily wrapped bandage around his waist. “I mean... I couldn’t believe he hit me with a low blow!” LLB just marched around the locker, rolling his eyes. A low blow? It was a right hand directly placed into the jaw. Sure, Fox could hit. He really could. LLB was there once... and had fallen from a mere right hand too... But never rendered out cold. Sometimes... listening to Reed, was so horrible you just wanted to take a gun and shoot his head off. The lies. So bland... never thought over. A million people could see the truth, but inside Young’s mind... he honestly believed his lie was the outgoing truth. Sad, really... “That fuck! That prick! This coming Pay-Per-View, Rowe... you’re gonna fight him for me... in a Rematch! Title on the line and all!” ‘The Law’ snapped back. He wasn’t paying too much attention until then. His ears were open... not on, but always enough to pick up what he didn’t like. “What did you say?” He asked, actually unsure of what the Owner had demanded. It was never even a suggestion with him. It was a demand. This. That. He says. You do. “I said... this Pay-Per-View, next month... you... Fox... for the Title. You’re gonna get him back for me-” “Objection!” Young jumped back, only to flutter his eyes right after. Pissing Roland off scared the fucking hell out of him. “He’s been banned from fighting Championship Title Matches for three months!” LLB said. “There’s one month left!” Young was freaked out... but his stupid mind never thought it over. A word came... and he just said it. “I will drop the suspension, then.” Rolling his eyes for another time, ‘The Law’ walked up to Reed and took him by the neck. “Um... no. How about this... you fight your own battles. I’m done with Fox. I wasted KroW... I took him out... Fox is old news too... Now I’ll find someone new to bring down.” Nearly smothering Young’s neck to bits... he could only pound out a squeak from his stomach and then just nod his head, while his beady little light-brown eyes rambled around. LLB placed him back on the bench. “But I would’ve had him, Roland... if he just didn’t hit me with that fucking low blow!”
