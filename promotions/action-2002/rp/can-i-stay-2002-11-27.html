Can I Stay?

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27/02 The groceries had been bought and stored away. Max Danger and Conrad Ramsey were the only one's home. Bradley Duncan, Jessica, Blast, Iceman, and Mo Ron had left. They went to get a Christmas tree. Because the day after Thanksgiving, before the majority of them left for Buffalo, they were going to put it up and decorate it. 'The Danger Man' was sitting in his chair, watching Kansas take on North Carolina on ESPN. He enjoyed college basketball, but hardly got the chance to watch it anymore. He lived a very busy life... and it was in much chaos at the moment. Conrad Ramsey was in his room, compiling notes and watching tape, to try and learn some new moves. Bradley Duncan had taught him about knee, leg, and palm strikes. He had already gotten elbow smashes(which Danger and Duncan were very adept at) down and was waiting to use them in action... or rather Action!. Now he was learning a bit more about other strikes. Quick ones. Hard ones. A flurry of them. He needed moves to wear down his opponent. Punches weren't going to do it. Wrestlers shrugged those off nowadays. He needed something else. Something that wouldn't cause pain to himself... or would cause less pain than most other things. As he did all that, someone began banging on the door. Max Danger just sat in the chair, unmoving. He assumed it would be the 'gang' back with the tree. Conrad Ramsey remained in his room, not hearing any of it. Finally, after he got completely tired of it. He got up... groaing and cursing as he walked. He reached the front door and opened it. HOMELESS GUS! 'HI MAX!!' he shouted out, just as happily as Iceman had earlier in the day. 'What do you want homeless Gus?' Danger said, agitated. 'You can just call me Gus. I don't mind.' 'I do.' 'Oh.' .............. 'Anyway, how's it going champ?' That wasn't the wisest thing to say. Sure he was a PIW World Tag Team Champion(and if you didn't, just ask, and Max will tell ya), but Bobby Minio had lost the Dyad Titles for him, and to top it off, Conrad Ramsey was a champion... by himself. It was sacreligious. Something was wrong. Perhaps the word was coming to an end? Usually it was Danger who was picking up wins left and right, but it was Ramsey. Sure, Max had used the Achilles Tendon Hold on Ramsey and eliminated him at Last Laugh. But it just didn't make him happy like it normally would. Just too much going on right now. 'What, exactly, do you want Gus? I'm kinda tired.' Gus tried to wedge his way in, but Max didn't leave much room between the door and door facing. 'Can I come in?' Gus said, still trying to squeeze through. Danger pulled open the door and Gus tumbled onto the hardwood floor of the foyer. He pulled himself up. He stunk to high-heaven, but he was homeless, so it's not like he had much of a choice. 'Do you want to take a bath or something?' Danger said, getting queasy at the smell. 'Nah. I was just wondering... if...' he put his hands behind him, lowered his head, and twisted the toe of his shoe on the floor. He looked up innocently at Max. '...I could stay here... for Thanksgiving.... I'm all alone.' It was sad. Danger, of course, couldn't care less. But he didn't say no. He had a thought. If Jessica sees that Max let Gus stay, due to his homelessness, then she'd definitely want him back. 'Sure, you can stay. But we're going to clean... I mean, you're going to clean yourself up.' Gus grinned. 'We're going to start with those.' He then lead him down the hallway, and into the bathroom. There he showed him where everything was. He left him there and went into Conrad's room. He didn't pay any attention to the old man. He went directly to the closet. 'What are you doing? Those are MINE!' Ramsey screamed out. Danger wasn't listening. Or didn't care. 'Don't worry about it. I'm just helping someone... IN NEED... out. Geez, you can be so insensitive sometimes.' He grabbed a pair of courdaroy pants and a sweater. He opened up the underwear drawer. He grabbed a ruler from the dresser and used it to pull out some boxers and socks. He exited, as Conrad got up and limped out of his room. He followed Danger down the hall. Danger stood in front of the closed bathroom door. 'Who's in there? And why are they using my clothes?' Ramsey demanded to know. 'Just Gus.' 'GUS!? That homeless guy!?' 'Yeah, so can he barrow your stuff? Just through Thanksgiving. Help a brother out.' 'No he can't borrow them... he can KEEP 'em! I don't want them after he's had them.' Conrad then spun and headed back to his room, still a bit pissed. It was for a good cause, but still, they were his. Before he entered his room, Max yelled out to him. 'I'll be back, old man. The others should be back at any time. Just come out, sit in the living room, until they do. I'm gonna go rent some DVDs at Movie Gallery.' Ramsey sighed and reluctantly did as he was told. He went into the living room, sat down in the same chair Danger was in, and watched the halftime report. Maryland beat The Citadel 97-49. Not that big of a deal, but just figured it should be known. Danger then left. The old man and the bum were alone. God knows if Gus tried to steal anything that Ramsey wouldn't stop him. Was too old and had to protect himself for Action!. Let him take anything... or just Max's stuff.
