Portrait of a Teenage Society : Reunions

4 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 4th, 2002 I wonder what my High School reunion will be like. Not that I ever want to go back to high school. That's a time in my life I'd rather forget. I just want to head back to that dilapidated gymnasium and rub my dream in all of their faces. 'I'm a professional wrestler! What are you?!? I bet it's not something you've dreamed about since the age of 12. Stockbroker. HA! Like any 12 year old dreams of THAT!' Of course, I doubt anyone would even remember me when I returned. That's life for you. Time not only heals all wounds, but it erases all marks as well. Doesn't matter what you've done in the past, it's a world filled with people asking the question, 'What can you do for me now?' What I can do for you now is fly through the air with reckless abandon. What I can do now is fight with every fiber of my being just to make the fans rise to their feet. What I can do now is manipulate the people into a suspension of disbelief and lure them to cheering and booing me at a moments notice. And what I can also do is beat the every living piss out of that fuckwad Invalid. This petty little grudge he has with me is just like high school. We get along when we talk but he really hates my guts, talking about me behind my back. Eventually, shit like that comes back to you and then he turns his back on you completely. Then there's the years of bitterness and torment and desire to beat the fuck out of him. No, I've never had this happen to me before... Never. And in life, in high school, you can't really do anything about it. You can't just go up to his face and challenge him to a fight, because christ, high school's more talk than anything. You can never get into a true fight, and even when you do, it's just a matter of time before it's broken up. Thing is, here, it's expected. Here in the world of professional wrestling, every single heel turn of life is expected to have a definite conclusion. That huge fight that happens in high school and gets broken up by rent-a-cops and daytime security? Here in Action!? The fans thrive to see that. They expect it, and Reed Young uses this to make a profit. And in the end, all that matters to me is my hands around Invalid's neck, slowly squeezing, ever so slowly until he's a lifeless corpse in my hands. And then I'll get a few live pigs and a hacksaw... I'm sorry, I've been watching his dvd set for the past couple of days. Really fuels the hatred.
