Your mental bull - Prelude

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th June, 2002 Yes, you're right, it's me. Benjamin 'Airborn' Tyler. And yes, you're right again, I'm 17 years old. Got a problem with that? I damn sure haven't, because it means I have longer to live than you! It's weird you know. 17 years old, only 1 year removed from when I left my secondary school with 10 GCSEs and 1 Intermediate GNVQ in ICT. I did pretty good as well. 6 As, 4 A*s and 1 Distinction. Grades that most of you could only wish for. And grades that most of you probably DO wish for. And for someone so young I am oh so experienced. First I showed up in a tournament. Yes, a wrestling tournament, against 63 men all much older, bigger and stronger than me. Yet I was smarter. I did pretty well, got to the final 8 before bowing out in style. Got caught by a schoolboy roll up as well, which made for some interesting comments from those internet reporters. You all know that they secretly want to be superstars themselves, but they can't you know. They're not good enough. Anyway, my showing was more than befitting of someone still technically known as a wrestling virgin. Someone obviously saw shit in me, as I was wrestling just one month later in Primetime Championship Wrestling, the PCW. I did OK suppose, got a Rising Star Championship shot, but I was doomed to fail in that hell hole. Walking down the ring someone chucked a soda at me, and it caught me on the head. What kind of drunken redneck hurls a soda at a wrestler's head? I was there to entertain that heartless bastard, to put on an airborn spectacle that Rey Misterio Jr would have admired. Well, if they don't want my expertise and vision, stuff them. They needed me more than I needed them, so I was out of there. Next stop: EYE Wrestling. The 'EYE' stood for 'Eliminate Your Enemy', and as tough as it is to admit it, I wasn't capable of eliminating anyone. I was just this skinny runt who thought he was a big shot. Heck, I still am a skinng runt who thinks he is a big shot, but this time I have the knowledge of what I did wrong and how I can put it right. EYE was a very satanic environment, every match taking place inside a steel cage, and against all these 6'10' 350lb giants I didn't stand a chance. I didn't have the know how about ducking and evading, but I guess that is all changed now as well. Next but far from final stop: Action! I may be mentally scared, and I'm damn sure physically scarred from when that brute Rio Nexan raised me onto his shoulder and drove my head straight into the wire mesh. Yes, that's it, that small cut on the top right of my forehead. Well when I've finished here I intend to rebuke some revenge on every single person who crosses me, and I'll be dishing out plenty scars of my own. Take that as gospel and swallow it. I may have suffered many mental traumas, and no doubt that national press will feel the need to dig it up and dust it off, but hey, everyone has their own story, and it's just mine. Are you ready to see some more stories unfold in front of your very eyes? I've got the pen in my hand. Come and get me.
