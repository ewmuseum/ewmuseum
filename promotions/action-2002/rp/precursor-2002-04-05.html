Precursor

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-5-02 “CHRIST AIR ONTO SYPHON FISSION! POTRIGHT WITH THE COVER!” Greg Parker screamed at the top of his lungs. One... Two... Three!!!!!!!! NEW CHAMPION!!!! SAMUEL POTRIGHT HAS FINALLY BEATEN HIS HATED RIVAL! HE IS THE IWO WORLD CHAMPION!” he roared. The fans yelled and screamed. They thought they had only saw a beginning. The fans never walked into an IWO event where Syphon Fission would be World Champion. Ever. The fans never watched an IWO Pay-Per-View where the two elite warriors of their trade, Fission and Joey Malone, would exchange blows for that IWO World Title which meant everything to Rashard. He would just be there. Not in the funny loving sense of the term. He was just very detached from the locker room he had become a leader of. He cut IWO storylines by leaving a stable with three of the men that would forever haunt Fission as being better then him. Potright was the past ruler of Fission. Malone was the present ruler of Fission. And Kent Anthason would dominate the fallen man sooner or later…just to make things understandable. Fission gave so much to the IWO, to the wrestling business for that matter. He had ulcers from the painkillers he needed to take. His body was failing him. His free flowing matches, where he would pull off moves with such grace and style that you thought he was a God on Mount Olympus had morphed into a roughneck style where he would put too much power into his moves. He ended a couple careers because of his careless nature. It didn’t affect him. Potright was on his knees, kissing the IWO World Title, as all the pageantry that was celebrated as IWO lore came pouring over the crowd, the fallen champion left the ring. He left quietly, without a sound. He just hung his head low. Nobody caught the exit, for nobody cared. They had a new icon. Rewind… I failed everyone… Fission sat in that chair, watching his collapse time after time. It was something he had to do. It reminded him why he is nothing. It reminded him how the fans throw people away when they are needed the most. Nobody cares about me… Nobody except me… He rested his Colt M1911A1 onto the wooden cabinet by his plush leather chair. He walked the same segment five times… Or was it twenty times… Maybe fifty. He had ejected the tape. If you noticed, the quality of the IWO show had become significantly poor compared to what it should be. Well… That’s what happens when you rewind and play the same segment for over a year. A new tape was in the VCR and was being played. “DEATH PLUNGE! BOOYAH, MOTHERFUCKER! I GOT IT IN BEFORE YOU! I OUT CALLED LIAM! WOO WOO WOO!” Shaw screamed. “You're impossible.” Ump added “Malone is down, and Keri is definitely concerned at ringside! Fission drops down into a cover!” Ump yelled, seeming concerned himself. “One... Two... Three!” “Finally!” Shaw blurted out. “WAIT! WAIT WAIT! NO! Malone got the foot on the ropes! I don't bloody believe it!” Ump yelled, sounding shocked as all hell. The fans were shocked too. Their hero had escaped a pin from the anti-hero. “Gah! Now all of the Marks’ watching this show will complain about how long this Match is! Dammit! Dammit all! Ratings are going down...” Shaw said, sounding somewhat agitated. “The Referee is informing Fission, and I don't think he can believe it, either! Fission is now arguing with the official about the three count! Malone is slowly getting to his feet behind Fission, and now Malone hooks Fission from behind!” Ump said, getting louder as Malone reached and finally pinned the massive Syphon Fission. “Damn back fighter!” Shaw screamed. “A rolling reverse cradle with a bridge! Malone's trapped Fiss here! Ump: One! Ump: Two! Ump: ... Ump: Three!” “Thank GOD. Now, for some Fear! FEAR, DAMMIT!” Shaw said, trying to hype Chris Fear up as a dominant heel. “This bout is over, and the Crowd is going crazy! Joey Malone has managed to pin the giant, Syphon Fission, and secure a shot at Chris Fear at a later date!” Ump ended up stating. Rewind… I let everyone down… The same segment is showed over and over again. So many times that we lose count. Why can’t they love me? Why can’t they understand me? I am a giant…yes… But I have a heart. I need their cries. Their elation. I NEED IT TO LIVE! Fission used to be a fun loving guy until Potright ended his life in a sense. He has fought to gain it all back. Rashard Clark worked so hard and ended up with nothing. Malone… You cheated me again. Next time… You won’t cheat me. Never again will I let you use me. CAN YOU HEAR ME? DO YOU REMEMBER ME? DO YOU REMEMBER NOW!
