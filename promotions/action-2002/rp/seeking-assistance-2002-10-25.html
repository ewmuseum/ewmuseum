Seeking Assistance

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/25/02 Mid-day of Friday. The house was relatively empty. Save for Bradley Duncan and Conrad Ramsey. Duncan had his bags packed, ready to head out to Los Angeles for his PIW commitments. Max Danger wasn't around. No one was really sure where he had ended up, but an email to Duncan had assured the big man that he would meet up with him at LAX. Ramsey, however, was just limping around the house, just wasting time before he left to make his Action! dates on Monday. A long weekend was ahead for him, having the house all to himself. Conrad had made his return the previous Monday, in New Jersey, and had made an open challenge to anyone. He was ready to go. Or that's what he told the fans anyway. Secretly, he wasn't even close to thirty percent. Bruised ribs, a destroyed knee. It was bad. Especially for an older man such as him. But he was determined to get back in the ring and give the fans want they deserved. Give them someone to cheer. He loved performing for the people. That's the only thing he had left in his life. Can I ask you something? the old man signed to Duncan, as the deaf/mute crossed through the living room. Duncan was surprised. His own partner, Max Danger, hadn't even taken the time to learn sign language, but the old man, Danger's nemesis, had. Duncan got over the shock of it and signed back. Yes, anything. I want your help. Help improve my ring work. Bradley smiled. No one had ever asked for his help and assistance before. He'd always wanted to. Max taught him, and he was eager to try and teach someone himself. I want to become a better mat wrestler. My knee is basically shot, so I need to develop a more submissive style. Ramsey signed. I will gladly help you, Mr. Ramsey. When would you like to start? Without hesitation Ramsey replied back, What time does your flight leave? Four hours. Duncan signed back. Now works for me. The two smiled, and then Duncan got out a pen and paper to help demonstrate the moves and give them names, while also showing Ramsey the holds on himself. Conrad Ramsey wasn't going to rest on his laurels. He was going to improve. With the help of his arch-rival's tag team partner and best friend, Bradley Duncan.
