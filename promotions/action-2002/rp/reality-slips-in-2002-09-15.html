Reality slips in

15 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.15.02 'I'm just bored.' The words played back over and over. The lies replayed in his head. Here he sat, Bobby Minio, everyone's least favorite tragic hero, in his locker room realizing slowly he wasn't wanted, needed, or appriciated. Events replayed themselves in a sense from his previous ventures in the valley of a love life. He felt it boiling up again. The hate. The anger. The jealousy. The Hate for others and himself. He sat wrapping his knuckles, dripping with blood. He turned, sneering at the mangled remains of a locker he defaced by unloading a shelling of fists onto it as he found out the truth. As he found out that he was only second best. Second best to Johnathon Conspiracy. JCON was everything Minio wasn't. He suceeded where Minio choked. He surpassed Minio and became a champion. He had everything Minio didn't. Minio hated him for it. A while ago, Minio and Conspiracy worked together, and Minio actually enjoyed One Letter Better's company. Now... all he could think about was finding a way to stand face to face with JCON and make him into the locker he crippled. Was Minio going to be a hypricrite, make a martyr out of someone else for his problems? Of COURSE. He's human after all. 'I'm human. I'm nothing but flesh, bones, and chemical reactions. Chemicial reactions. Chemical... Reactions...' He was clenching his fists so hard blood was squeezing out of his palms. He tried to convince himself that life was that simple. 'I wish.' He wished it was. But he wasn't gullible. He was just impressionable. And there IS a difference. Life was losing that light. Losing that shine. That glow that it had for the past few weeks. It was all going gray again. He was feeling flawed. Feeling worked out by his daily life. He could only recite a lyric to calm his nerves. Only simple thing to pass the time until he could try to make Veronica appriciate him for what he is. 'Fumble outta bed and I stumble to the kitchen, pour myself a cup of ambition, and yawn and stretch and my life is a mess and if I never make it home today, god bless.' He stood up, and began punching another locker viciously as the camera faded to black.
