Bored: Happy Halloween

28 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/28/2002 *ding, ding, ding, ding, ding, ding, ding, ding, ding, di...* The man of the house, we’ll call him Al because I’m just that lazy, came to the door to examine who the hell was ringing the bell so insistently. He had expected to his in-laws. His wife’s side of the family was the impatient obnoxious type. He swung the door open; ready to punch uncle Earl in the face for waking him up so fucking early, but 3 children wearing costumes and rubber masks stood on his porch. He was bewildered. “TRICK OR TREAT!!” the three youths yelled in unison. “...What?” inquired Al. “TRICK OR TREAT!!!” the youths replied more enthusiastically. “Ok, you got the holidays mixed up kids. Go home.” Al turned around back inside his house and was closing the door behind him, when one of the kids stuck out his foot blocking the door from closing. Again, but this time in a much more menacing tone they yelled: “TRICK...OR...TREAT!” Al was becoming angry, but he didn’t want to yell at little kids. Especially on the holidays...even though Christmas was a few days ago. Regardless, Al knelt down to be eye-to-eye with these children and attempted to reason with them. “Look. I know, and I believe you kids all know it’s not Halloween. You’re pulling something, and I just don’t appreciate it. It’s not nice to be doing this esp-” “Are you giving us candy?” the oldest one interrupted. “Well, no I’m not gi-” “DADDY!” one of the children yelled. That was my cue. I emerged from behind the bushes and stomped onto the porch. “What’s the problem here son?” I asked, doing my best to put on a “concerned fatherly” tone of voice. “That man won’t give us candy.” Al was appropriately flabbergasted. What exactly was going on here? “Look. I don’t know what the hell is-” “Have you no holiday spirit sir?” “What the fu-” “Looks like I’m just gonna have to teach you a lesson.” Before he could say anything else, I shoved my way into his house, and walked out with PS2, a few games, couple articles of clothing, his wife’s gold chain(ripped off her neck), and went back to drag out the Christmas tree. The kids grabbed the shit and stuffed it in the Navigator. I was going to walk away without saying a word, but Al stopped me and spun me around. He was pretty heated. His face went a raging shade of red, and he was nearly foaming at the mouth. I looked down at him indifferently for a moment and I held contact with his now rabid eyes. He cocked back and punched me in the stomach, lunging all his body weight forward to add force. He stayed like that for a while, until he realized his punch did nothing beside crunch his fist on the brick like muscles of my abdomen. “...Are you done sir?” “Well...yeah.” He hung his head in shame and went back inside. I lugged the tree to the SUV and shoved it in the back of the Navigator. “Well...it’s been fun kids.” “Wait! What are we getting?” They all stood there confused. They were expecting to have the toys I stole. Normally I would’ve just told them to fuck off, and smacked the leader of the bunch around a bit. But seeing how its “that” time of year and all, I thought against it. I mean, I wouldn’t want people to get the wrong impression and think I’m a bastard. So I lowered my zipper and pulled out “Bad Andy”. “Here’s what you get faggot. Merry fucking Christmas.” I wagged it around a bit, and they ran off horrified, and probably scared for life. I like I said. I don’t wanna give the impression that I’m a bastard. People should realize I’m a perverted, twisted, demented, bastard with little to no sense of decency. Happy Halloween.
