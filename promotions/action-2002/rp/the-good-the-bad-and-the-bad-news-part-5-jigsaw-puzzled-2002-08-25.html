The Good, the Bad, and the Bad News, Part 5: Jigsaw Puzzled

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 12th, 2001. The past. Minio sat in shambles. Tears streamed down his face. He was in some half life motel room, scrambling through his suitcase for the peices of paper. He peiced them together like a jigsaw puzzle. Parts were missing. He was emotionally distraught. It would show in his performance, in his wrestling. A phone was sitting haplessly next to him, off the hook. He was panicing. He lifted the phone, pounding on the reciever. He was calling her. Over and over. Calling her, getting nothing but a busy signal. The truth had hit him like a brick reentering the atmosphere. She found someone knew. Already. She had her rebound. She, a nobody. And he, a super star wrestler, had nothing but tears and a half assed tore up letter. He felt stupid as all hell. He felt dead. He felt numb and dead inside. 'How long will this last?' Minio was pleading with himself. A formiliar voice rang out. 'It passes. Trust me Minio, it passes. Eventually.' Minio turned to see Sean Cylear sitting in the seat by the business table of the hotel room. Cylear was keeping him company and he snapped, griping around at the letter like it was gold. Like it was life itself. 'Showcase' Sean Cylear watched it all unfold. He would be enjoying it if he didn't enjoy Minio angry, and not depressed. Cylear would poke at Bobby to get his blood flowing, get him ranting and raving, not crying and whining. This was almost... painful for Cylear. He had to give Some bit of reasurance to Bobby Minio. 'Sean, it never passes. I feel empty. Like theres a hole.' Cylear was upbeat with a cocky return. 'Of course theres a hole missing. We can fill that hole with a few pretty blondes from a bar. I'll cover the tab... Maybe. Lets get a drink Mini-oh.' Bobby shook his head. 'Go ask Roland or Mezz. I'm not in the mood for a drink man. Go ahead without me.' Cylear stood, looking at Bobby surprised. 'Minio turns down a free drink. Well, you know where we'll be if you need some company man. Keep up. And remember, one day, when you dont have sand in your vagina, were gonna have a rematch. And I will win this time Minio, i'll just crack a few jokes about this incident.' Cylear watched Minio ignore the entire comment which would have usually made Minio return something in attempt of wit. It was gone. It was all gone. Cylear walked out. Minio had no clue that the last time he and Cylear would spend hanging out would be the last. Months later Sean Cylear was killed in an automobile Accident. The pangs of moping.
