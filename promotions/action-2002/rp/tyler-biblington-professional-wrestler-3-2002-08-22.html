Action! — Tales

Tyler Biblington : Professional Wrestler (3)

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mann showed up at about 10:00 AM that morning. He was dressed casually, wearing a pair of jeans, a baggy sweater, he wore a pair of sunglasses, and a Toronto Blue Jays hat to try and stay unnoticed. He was starting to gather popularity and was becoming noticed almost everywhere he went. He stepped into the room of Tyler Babylon who was changing into some clothes that his friend, Lindsay Russell, had dropped of for him last night. “How you feeling, kid?” Mann said to get the attention of Babylon who was staring out the window. “Meh… I’ve been feeling better. I still can’t believe he did that to me, I could’ve been killed last night!” Mann shut the door slowly and quietly behind him. He took off his glasses and his hat and placed them on a small table that was in the room. His weak smile was trying to show innocence, but Tyler soon realized that something was up. “ Is everything alright?” Tyler slowly asked. He was hesitant because he knew that something was going on but wasn’t quite sure what to make of everything. “Sit down,” Mann said as he pointed towards the bed. “I didn’t want to do this here, but you and me have some things we need to work out.” Tyler stared at him for a moment before quickly slipping on to the bed. Tyler demanded, “What’s going on?” “You’ve got a lot of heart, kid. You’ve got the heart of a lion and that really means something in this sport. You’ve got talent, that’s for sure. The only thing that I worry about is the size of you and after what happened last night…” “After what happened last night? What the hell are you talking about? Dewey fucking made me land face first onto a steel chair and it could’ve killed me. And you’re blaming this on me?” Tyler stood up and was in the face of Mann. He was now furious, a side to him that he was never really known for. “I’m not blaming anything on you. So just sit back down and let me finish.” Mann calmly said. “Last night wasn’t your fault but you’ve got to wonder how much you’re going to be able to take. You were tired, the move was sloppy and that hurt you even more. I think we’re going to try you somewhere else, in the tag division for a while, just to see how things work.” Tyler’s emotions were running wild inside him. Thoughts danced throughout his mind and for now he was silent. “Who’s my partner?” Tyler asked a lot more calm than he was seconds ago. Tyler took a deep breath and looked at Mann who seemed like he had run out of things to say. He couldn’t even look at Tyler and just stared down at his feet and sighed. “You can’t be serious? There is no way that’s going to work.” Mann continued to stare at the floor, he knew everything seemed unbelievable but it was for the best in the long run. Lunacy had trained Tyler hard for past three months but the powerhouse style of Lunacy wasn’t what Tyler needed. Jefferson and Biblington had similar styles inside the ring and they would benefit from each other. “You didn’t fire him? That wasn’t part of the match and you know it wasn’t. Why the hell are you doing this to me?” “You were supposed to get your hands up to protect your face. You know you’re not going to hit every single move you try. It was all too simple but a rookie like you screws it up. If you want to go anywhere in your career, you’re going to do what I tell you. You got that?” The two were furious now and their screaming could be heard out in the hallway. An elderly nurse who happened to be walking by heard this and peaked her head in the door. Babylon was pissed off and took it out on the bed as he gave it a quick and hard kick. His whole face scrunched up and he tried his best not to show how much it hurt. “Excuse me gentlemen, but I think it’s time the both of you leave.” The nurse told them. Mann grabbed his hat and a glass, quickly put them on, and was soon out the door. Babylon sat on the bed and gazed out the window for a few more moments. Maybe Mann was right. Maybe he wasn’t cut out for wrestling.
