Angel Calling From Hell

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Hello?” “Mark….it’s Angelica.” Angelica had since gotten out of her father’s bedroom and was now in the living room. Her cheeks were flushed and her face was distraught. She had washed off her make-up, but her eyes were still heavy. Tears still pooled in her eyes as she spoke to her brother for the first time in about five months. “What’s going on? You don’t sound too happy already.” “It’s Dad, Mark…he’s dead.” Angelica burst into tears again, emotions flowing with her tears like an exploded dam. “Oh my god…how?” “I don’t know. I came to visit him like I promised to do. When I came in, he was just laying in bed. I tried to wake him up, but he wouldn’t wake up. Mark…it was just so sudden.” “I know, Angelica. I wish I could be there for you. Look, I’ve got to go right now. I’ll catch the first plane out there, I promise. Take care of yourself until I get there, K sis?” “I’ll try, Mark. See you in a while.” “Bye.” Angelica hung the phone up, sitting down on the black leather two-seater. She sighed heavily, placing her head in her hands. She cracked her knuckles before standing back up. She made her way over to a small table underneath the wall-mounted phone. She picked up the phone book and quickly flipped through the Yellow pages. She stopped suddenly, finding the phone number she was looking for. She picked up the phone and dialed the number. After a couple of rings, the phone was picked up on the other line. “Hi, I’d like to speak to Jeremy, please…yes, I’ll hold.” Angelica paced around the room, her feelings taking her places she wasn’t prepared to go. After what seemed like an eternity, the person she was looking for picked up the phone. “Hey, Jeremy. It’s Angelica. How ya doing?…Not too good…My father just passed away…thank you….Yes, he was a good man. Look, do you have any appointments this afternoon…No? Good. I’m coming in…I’ll tell you what I want when I get there…Price is no option, Jeremy. I’ll make sure I’ve got plenty of money…Thanks, you’re amazing…See you in a bit…Yep, bye.” Angelica hung the phone up and made her way over to the couch, grabbing her jacket. She slowly slid her jacket on, heading to the door. She looked to the key shelf and grabbed her father’s house keys. She opened the white wooden door and exited the house. She turned and closed the door, locking it behind her. Angelica reached up to her right eye with her hand and wiped a tear away before making her way to her car. His little angel was saddened. But would you expect her to be happy about this? Didn’t think so.
