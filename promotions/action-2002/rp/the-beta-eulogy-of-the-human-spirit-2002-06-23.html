The Beta: Eulogy Of The Human Spirit

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Positive? No. Negative. Happiness? Yeah right. My mind is failing me. Right now, when he reappeared, he almost let them go. No price to pay or anything. I can hear him screaming for me to stop before it happens. Fucking forget him. He was too weak. I was too busy surfacing, feeding off his view of this passionate existence. I have been doing this for twenty years. It’s not something new. I am the negative feelings you all have. Only, I am more controlling because this world is so full of hate. Soulless machine? No. I just accepted the fact that the second I flatline, I am going to wake up in the pits of Hell. Or nothing at all. I am not scared of death like the rest of you. Death isn’t my warm embrace, either. Its just there. It’s like birth. I probably just didn’t care either way, because it was going to happen. So is death. It is just all this religious bullshit that has been force fed your entire lives that makes you fear life. Life is what you make of it. I have no regrets. I have nothing to gain and nothing to lose. That’s life now. A game with nothing to gain and nothing to lose. So why not exit the game? Because I rather use this life, this body, as my weapon. A more powerful weapon then any gun, or knife, could ever be. If I strapped a bomb to myself, and walked around, and blew a crowd of people up, I would see nothing wrong with it. I am doing them a favor. I hate this weak, “I don’t want to die” shit. The old people in this country take up believing God, the younger people don’t care, and life as a whole is just a mass of jumbled retarded jargon. You can’t decipher one original thought from the next. You all are not that great until you accept all the possibilities. Maybe Joey is the bad guy. Maybe I am just the good guy. Maybe Keri is nothing more then a chess piece, a toy in all essential terms. Maybe death is life, and life is death. Think about it for a second. Instead of giving me that fucking look of “You are stupid”. That means your perceptions decide your affairs. That would make you lower then scum in my eyes. If you could excuse me for this eulogy of your human spirit… I have to beat a woman who is crying because she can not accept the fact that she is a pawn. Nothing more and nothing less. Good bye. Loser.
