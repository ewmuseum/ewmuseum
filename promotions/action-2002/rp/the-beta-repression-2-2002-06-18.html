The Beta: Repression (2)

18 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I sat in the dimly lightened bus stop. I see the trash of this world collecting by me. Hookers. Bums. Coke heads. Drug dealers. Goth punks. Want to know what is worse? I am the most evil person in this booth by quite a large margin. I have started questioning who I am. Why I am doing this all again. You know, the simple stuff you take for granted. I am scared to think about who I was before all this. And I am even more scared now that my mind keeps looking within itself to find the answers. The people all know who I am. They think this is a publicity stunt, to make what I do seem real. They were giving me props and that shit. I wanted to vomit. This trash was praising my unworthy ass. They thought my stuff has went unhitched since the fucking “angle” began. I am still gripping Payback. They are running their hands all over its spade. It is crying. It is scared by this trash. I do not open my mouth, but it knows that I am telling it to calm down. The blood all over my face is keeping them away from touching me. The dead expression compacts my feelings. I feel dead. Numb to all these people. Not just these people though, I have been getting a true feeling of hatred for the human race. I am not even in this industry for the fame of these people and their money. I am in it now for… For. For. I do not know anymore. I could go and kill Joey Malone without worrying my head about him and his toy. And it would be the same thing. Why did I do this all in front of the people I hate so much? Why did I wish this all upon us all? I know now. I wanted to tarnish everything that fuck stood for. For all the times he used me. Is that even true, Fission? I look at the glass reflection of what I have become. I see the ugly face of hatred. It is all blood, scars, and bile. The unkempt blue hair glistening with my own crimson. My goatee and stubble soaked with the liquid that keeps my heart beating. My body, once so beautiful and young, now deteriorated to rubble. My knees are hurting me. My joints and tendons cry in pain. I can barely put forth the effort to walk and talk, unlike the way I used to be. I have grown lazy. A decade of war has killed my body. The bus comes, and we all enter, me being the last one. In my loose fitting clothes, I look at more scum, and age. Grandmothers who can’t remember their names. Grandfathers who can’t wait to die. Drugs. Death. Pain. This all fits me. I sit in the back seat alone. Nobody pays any attention. I am just a face in the crowd, indecipherable from the next. I just wish I could figure out who was behind the face you pay no attention to.
