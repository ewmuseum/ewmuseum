Players & Pawns Aftermath

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/2/03 Players and Pawns wasn't quite finished. In fact, the Conrad Ramsey versus Airborn Tyler, Bantam Championship Chair Match had just ended. Abruptly. Airborn Tyler had ascended a ladder, inside the ring, and had prepared to hit Conrad Ramsey(who was sprawled out on the announce table with a chair on top of him) with a Shooting Star Press. He didn't. The ladder had begun to wobble, and he wasn't able to steady it. He fell. And fell. Even fell some more. He hit the cold floor on his head. He laid motionless as the Fans stood in silence, trying to see what was going on. Conrad Ramsey had won. But he didn't care. No one cared. All they were concerned with was Airborn Tyler. He went at break-neck speed at all times. He always did the most outrageous stunts imaginable. And this would might have cost him. Backstage, Airborn Tyler was laid out on a stretcher and being pushed towards the ambulance, which waited out in the parking lot. Conrad Ramsey was at his side, talking to Airborn. 'Don't you give up, kid! Don't you dare give up!' Were they rivals? Yes. But this was more important than wrestling. This was life. The youngest man in Action! could lose his life. Even if wrestling was life to everyone in the lockerroom(though it may not be), you still didn't want to see anyone lose their's in the ring. Especially someone as young as Tyler. They continued pushing him along the hallways. Everyone backstage was watching, silently. Max Danger, who had been yelling at the top of his lungs just a few seconds earlier, had even shut his mouth. He had lost a brother in the ring. Once they reached the ambulance, Airborn began to stir. 'Son! Son, you alright? Speak to me.' Conrad shouted. 'Please, sir,' said the medic. 'We need to get him to the hospital.' Ramsey stepped back. They loaded him up into the ambulance, and then the medic turned to the Bantam Champion. 'You going to ride along?' Ramsey just nodded his head and jumped into the back. The doors slammed shut and the ambulance sped off, sirens blarring and lights flashing. Inside the vehicle, Conrad Ramsey was holding Airborn's hand. His eyes were open, but he wasn't speaking. He didn't look good. 'It's going to be okay, kid. Everything is going to be okay.' The medics were checking him out. Conrad's mind was swirling. He was giving Tyler encouragement, but he wasn't even sure he was there. But he had to stay strong. Then the medic asked, 'Are you his father?' Silence. Conrad was caught completely off-guard by the statement. He thought, for a second, that the man must be an idiot. He had just seen him walk away from the wrestling ring with Airborn. He saw that he was wearing light-blue wrestling trunks(w/ kneepads and boots). And he had a belt with him. But then, he thought... I'm old. He rubbed the top of his nearly bald head, and then looked at the medic, who had been waiting for an answer. 'Yes.' He didn't know why he said it. He wasn't even sure if he had said it. He knew he wasn't Airborn's father. But, on the bright side, at least they didn't ask if he was his grandfather. He was old enough to be. But he looked down at Airborn, and under his breath, he muttered, 'Foolish boy.'
