Action! — Tales

Ten miles to go

30 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Boral, the only newspaper I can find under my seat is the Toronto Times, where’s The Sun? Come on, enough of this broadsheet crap, I want scandal, sex, and lies!” As Airborn continued to scrimmage under the passenger seat in Coral Avalon’s Ford Fiesta, the kleptomaniac tapped the steering wheel impatiently with his right forefinger. It was just his luck that out of all the people in Toronto he could have knocked over it had to be Airborn Tyler. And it was just his luck that they were both heading for Ottawa. “BORAL! You disgust me, you really do!” said Airborn while screwing up his face. In front of Coral’s face he dangled an old McDonald’s milkshake carton he’d found lodged under his seat. “MOVE IT YOU IDIOT!” shouted Coral, swiping the carton out of the way just in time to see the corner. He turned the steering wheel sharply and disaster was averted. Coral would rebate Airborn even more, but what good would it do? Sighing, and resigned to arriving at the arena for a house show late, Coral slapped on the right indicator and carefully turned into a road side bar. Airborn twisted his head left to stare longingly at the road they’d just left. “But Boral, we’re only ten miles out of Washington.” “Ottawa,” corrected Coral Avalon as he removed the keys from the ignition and swung his body out of the car. Airborn followed him. “WASHINGTON! I’m telling you, I got 102% in my Year 9 Geography test, and WASHINGTON is the capital of America!” Coral didn’t break out of stride and pushed open the door to the bar. The 20 and 18 year old slender wrestlers were greeted by an overwhelming smell of smoke and booze, and the less than inspiring sight of obese truck drivers with their bellies sticking out under their shirts. “Airborn, we are not in America, we are in Canada. And it’s impossible to get 102% in a test. Waitress!” Coral sat down at a table in the corner that was meant to be non-smoking, but so small was the bar it didn’t make any difference. The nicotine odoured smoke still clung to the air. Airborn sat next to him as a waitress clad in a knee length skirt and pink t-shirt made her way out from behind the mahogany bar armed with a notepad. “Well I got 102%. Mr.Thomas I think it was who gave me such a mark. Great man, he clearly saw my potential as a geographer. He once told me I was one of a kind.” Coral couldn’t help but snigger at this, that last part was definitely true. Content to let any further discussion slip away, he raised his head and looked at the waitress who removed a pen from her shirt pocket. “Yeah?” she asked, less than politely. This bar obviously didn’t like to keep it’s customers. “I…” started Coral, but Airborn Tyler cut him straight off, his high pitch voice echoing loudly enough around the bar to turn a few annoyed heads. “I would like a double cheeseburger, chips with extra fat, and a beer. Oh yeah, don’t forget the salt and vinegar. And if you don’t follow my orders PRECISELY to the word, I will kick you shitless.” So two minutes later Coral Avalon frantically accelerated out of the car park and back onto the highway with a lynch mob armed with wooden planks and beer glasses behind them. “What wankers!” commented Airborn, climbing into the back and pushing his nose against the back window like that truly annoying child who loves to taunt anyone who cannot reach them. Coral just shook his head. Not only had Airborn ordered the fattiest meal available when he was (believe it or not) a young promising athlete, but he’d nearly got them both attacked, he’d threatened to beat up a woman, and even worse, they now had no meal. Only ten miles Coral thought as his stomach rumbled. “I KNOW A SONG THAT’LL GET ON YOUR NERVES! GET ON YOUR NERVES! GET ON YOUR NERVES! I KNOW A SONG THAT’LL GET ON YOUR NERVES! ALL, DAY, LONG!” Ten miles too many.
