Action! — Tales

Brewing over a workout

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd December 2002 ‘Daunting’ David Dew was down at the local gym situated inside the arena in which Pressure Point would be hosted tomorrow evening. The wrestlers hadn’t been told if they had matches yet, but that wasn’t of any significance, for he had to prepared to have a match any time. At any time someone could jump him from behind, and when they did, he would be waiting with as much strength as he could muster. Last week’s Pressure Point had left a sour taste in his mouth, for The Elements had competed in their second Dyad Championship defence, this time against the unlikely combination of Simon Seaman and his ex-IWO foe High Flyer. These days Action was looking like an IWO retirement ground: Joey Malone, Kent Anthason, Coral Avalon, High Flyer, Simon Seaman, Syphon Fission, Invalid, Noah Hawkins, and the latest addition, Tony Davis. Post match Davis had run in and attacked The Elements to save his IWO buddies from a beat down. The match itself had been a standard affair, certainly nothing for an end of year compilation tape. It had been fairly even, and an argument could be put forward that if Seaman and Flyer had been a more cohesive unit they might have walked away from the arena with the silverware. The deciding factor in the match though had actually been none other than the Tyler brother’s sister Siobhan Tyler. Lee ‘Lightning’ Tyler had caught High Flyer in an inside cradle, and just as Flyer was about to kick out eighteen year old Siobhan (who has never been involved in any sort of wrestling) grabbed Flyer’s leg and prevented him from kicking out. After the bell had rung Lee and David had wanted to get their point across more and started beating down on their opponents. “Bloody democracy,” cursed David as he put down the weights he was using for bicep curls. The only reason they had done the beat down was to teach their opponents not to abuse democracy by getting championship shots they don’t deserve. But then Tony Davis (High Flyer’s long time friend and tag team partner) made his Action debut and sent The Elements retreating to high ground with a few chair shots. David didn’t like getting beaten, and being forced to retreat was a moral defeat no mistake about it. With rage he lay down on the bench and started piling more weights on, raising them high into the air with sweat dripping from his brow and onto the floor. And then of course there was the issue of Siobhan Tyler. It was no mistake that the Tyler family were close and took great pride in looking after each other, but there was tension there. Until she showed up on Pressure Point two weeks ago unannounced (in a way which didn’t endear itself to her siblings) Siobhan hadn’t been seen by her family for two years. “This is bad, stupid bitch,” murmured David under his breath while wiping his forehead with a towel.
