Cups: cX: Cup of Life (Ending)

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 31, 2002 Cups: cX: Cup of Life (Ending) 'My time is over.'          There he lingered. His head hung low staring peacefully at the ground. The tears just propelled from his eyes. Clashing echoes nearly slowed down time as he felt the small trickle run down the side of his face. ‘Be strong.' That's all he could think. The pool began to build up below him as his eyes faded into vermilion. Every remain of the face paint was dissolved. Shattered dreams, mislead thoughts, it's all that he could conceive as he squinted his eyes. Still looking over the dreaded mausoleum, he felt incomplete. A portion of his life had been misplaced as his future with a woman dove from the sky. The collision after the fall was anything but soft, yet for a moment ... his heart was soft. No longer he thought he was immortal. No longer he thought he was perfect. He now felt a sense of pain. Suffering was a sensation that all humans faced at one point or another. It started at the beginning.          The threads of time fell thin as the body laid there in front of him. Lifeless, this time he could tell. Her hair hung so graceful down to the side. Moments passed when he felt he could endure no more. All he wanted was to be free. Now it was impossible for that feeling. Loose from all of the others in the room, he detected the envision of being singled out. There he stood, alone. He had no shoulder to cry on. He had no hand to hold. What he came for was not to see any other people. What he came for ... was gone. The hands of the man collapsed free from the grasp of love. He slung his arms through the people, prying his way towards the front of the line. He wanted to be the first to see her. All he had with him was what she wanted. Deep down inside, he knew that the only thing she wanted with her was what he had in his hand.          Her cup rested in his hands. Her dreams rested in his hands. Her life rested in his hands. Reminiscing on the thoughts, he remembered: ‘All I want when I'm gone is this ... above me.' He delineated her character handing him the long slender cup. A goblet of her life rested upon the palm of his hands. Still yet, he forced his way to the front of the line. Begging for the opportunity to peer into her eyes. The placement of his cup would be his final mission, then he would be off. By this time, he was at the nose of the line. James recollected the screams and moans as he passed by person by person and finally made his way to the destination of choice.          There she was. Just as he imagined, only a little ghostly. The sudden urge to cry fell over him, but being the stronger man he did what was right. Madison placed the cup above her head and carried on. He didn't stop to say goodbye. He didn't stop to mourn. He didn't stop to take one final look into her eyes before she was officially gone. It was over.          'Look at me, what am I going to do with my life? The girl that I so dearly wanted to depart with has now departed on her own train. I don't blame her for death comes and goes. One day ... it'll be my turn and I'll get the opportunity to see her once more. That day, I do not dread. That day, I can't wait for.' James slammed open the door to the holy house. He nodded his head showing respect for his mischievous actions. Mournfully, he walked on towards his vehicle. When ... his arm slammed into another. He turned quickly, but didn't catch a glance of his face. The man was a brute in stature, yet James didn't mind. It didn't bother him at all. A second later, he was at his vehicle. He inserted the key and turned the lock. There was a clicking noise then the door swung open. James cushioned himself as he sat down. Exhaling deep, he pressed his head back easily against the head of the seat.          'What the hell?' Suddenly, there was rapid fire. Gun shots blared and he heard screams. He leaned back, realizing that it was just in his head. James felt the intense urge to enter the church one more and take a final look into her eyes, but he didn't. It seemed like hours he sat there pondering his next move in life.          The doors of the church swung open and out came the large man. He wielded a gun in his hand. James released the door as quickly as possible and took off towards the church yard. By the time that he made it near the surroundings, the man was gone. The Joker opened the door to the church and saw the crimson stained walls. Demons surged through his once thought evil body as he saw true evil unfold before his eyes. There were bodies laying all over the floor. A few survivors crawled to the nearest corner seeking refuge. James felt helpless as he fell to his knees.          Blood seeped through the small threads of his dress pants. The man slowly crawled towards the open coffin. Not knowing what to think, he bowed his head and began to pray. Minutes passed as he still couldn't build up the strength to approach the open tomb.          'This couldn't be happening. It must be a dream.' He tried to convince himself as the strength was at a tee. James stood to his feet and walked suspiciously towards the coffin. He was now within its radius. He felt the pain once more that he had felt when walking that close. There was no way he could forget the other deceased laying right near him. He stood to his feet.          'How could this have happened!?' James screamed at the top of his lungs. The screech was horrible.          'It's gone...' James titled his head towards the floor and looked down at the remains. The Cup of Life was shattered. R.I.P Queen: Cup of Life
