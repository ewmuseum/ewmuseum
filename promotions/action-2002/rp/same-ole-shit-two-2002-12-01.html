Same Ole' Shit. Two

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 1st, 2002 “Fuck you.” Julius was appauled. He couldn’t believe the trash he was hearing. “Oh, boy, no you di-” “Fuck. You.” Donell just pointed at his dad. Julius was gripping the ring ropes tightly. All that could be told was that Donell was lucky he had gotten out of the ring, because to his daddy, he wasn’t to big to be put over the knee, and then get the shit slapped outta’ him. “Boy, get in the damn ring and prepare for a beatin’.” “You still think this is a damn joke!” Donell screamed. He shook his arms in frustration. Once again, father wasn’t listening to him. “I am sick of being here. I am sick of this place.” Donell turned away and sighed. “I’m sick of you.” Donell sunk his head low and walked away, leaving Julius looking on in disgust. Donell left the small arena and took the small walk to the guest house. The garden path on the way there was beautiful. So peaceful. The highlight in his mind were the soft purple shade of the Lilacs that spread throughout the scenery. Exactly like the ones that he took to Daisy’s plot, once a month, ever since she left. That is, until he moved to this dreadful place. If only she was still around. She would make it seem right. Through out it all, whenever Donell got himself in too deep, or whenever he had another problem on his plate, Daisy was there for him. They all said Daisy asked exactly like her father, then why didn’t Julius understand him? Why didn’t anyone understand him. No one but Daisy.
