Jeremy’s Angel.

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was Saturday night. A lonely night for Jeremy Bold... as he sat there, in his beaten down red station wagon, thinking of what could have been between him and Angel Dawson. Tomorrow night, at this time, he would be sitting down to face her in a Match... a Match where if Dawson won, she’d be set free of being his personal slave. But then again, if she lost... if Angel Dawson lost to Jeremy Bold, the ‘Angle Man of 2002’ and soon to be the ‘Angle Man of 2003’, would get a date. Yet, Bold wasn’t *that* stupid this night. He knew his wrestling skills were a hell of a lot weaker than Angel Dawson’s were. He knew she had been in wrestling for a while... and he, he only had four Matches under his belt. And even those fights were shotty. Bold leaned forward in the driver seat, but then leaned back... only to once again lean forward and rest his head on the wheel. What would he do... and how would he do it? Bold was up against a corner... he could only hope he’d just make the turn. Ding... ding... Bold looked up, hearing the church bell ring throughout the area, as he glanced across the driver seat, to the church which stood in front of him. Knock knock... “Hey, hey Boss!” Bold looked back to his side of the window, and there they were. Desmon. Eric. Grace. Jamul. And the one they called ‘Jigga’. All waiting for Bold to roll down his car window. “Boss, we’re here.” Said the one entitled ‘Jigga’. “And we’re ready.” Desmon stated. Jeremy looked the five men over a good deal, dressed in gangster street clothes... well, okay, it wasn’t *hardcore* gangster stuff, because one of them wore a nice jean jacket (that was Eric)... and another wore a flashy long sleeves shirt (Grace), but the pants were baggy... the hats were sideways... and, well, you get the idea. Finally rolling down his window, Bold nodded. “Okay Jigga.” He said with a nod. “Let’s do it.” With that, Desmon opened up Bold’s car door as he stepped out... and the scene would change. *2(&^%*35^%$#%32$@@45)* “Do you, Trisha McGregor, take James Taylor to be your husband?” It was to be the time of their life. Their wedding... their joining together as one in the idea they called life. Trisha had known James for twelve years now. They had gone to High School together... and just finished college. They were starting a new life... and it was time they joined together in the eyes of the law, even though they had been one for some time. “I do.” “And do you, James Taylor, take Trisha to be your wife?” He smiled... his blue eyes shining like diamonds in the lighting above him. “I do.” “Then by the power invested in me...” The Priest spoke. “I pronounce you husband and-” SLAM! “BBBOOOOOO YYYEEEAAAHHH BITCHES!!” Everyone snapped around, for whom had just taken the church’s door and rifled the twenty foot piece of oak back, was none other than Desmon. Eric. Grace. Jamul. And the one they call... ‘Jigga’. “IT’S ON!!! BBBBOOOOOO YYYEEEAAAHHH!!” With Jeremy Bold standing behind them, Desmon took out a fucking rifle, and started to shoot everyone... With blanks. “DIE BITCHES DIE!! BOO YEAH!” And everyone screamed, running for cover as they believe the gun was actually loaded. Trisha had already broken down, as James, being the pussy that he was, ran off through the exit door to never, ever come back. The Priest had also been scared, but with faith in God he stayed there with Trisha, trying to drag her away for cover. The five men were dressed in hoodies, so their faces were not seen... as Jeremy Bold rushed in... the little gray hair that he did have blowing across his forehead while he did. That’s when he saw it. The wedding cake. “BBBOOOOOO YYYEEEAAAHHH!!” He shouted, looking back at his boys while they beat up some random people. “JIGGA! JIGGA EYE NEED U!” So looking up from an old grandpa he was tearing apart, Jigga ran towards Jeremy within all the chaos. “What do you need, Boss?” “Wheel this out for me.” He nodded... doing what he was told. This cued Jeremy to jump up on a table, commanding everyone who was not getting their ass kicked at the time’s... attention. “Hello!” He shouted. “I’d just like to thank you for the cake! It’s going to god use!” With that, and a little dance he had completed while up there, Bold hopped off the table, collected his men, and they left the scene... with everyone else laying on the floor, gasping for air. The scene then changed to Trisha and the Priest, who were hidden behind one of God’s statues. “Are you turned on?” He asked, making a move to her breasts. “Because I am.”
