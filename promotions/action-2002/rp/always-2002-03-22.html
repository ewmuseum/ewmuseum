Always

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/22/2002 Pressure Point 3 was done and over with. And Joey, still in his wrestling gear, was in his post-match ritual of laying on the bench and groaning. Now, don't go thinking that everyone doesn't do this after a match, because they do. They pour their hearts and souls for the crowd, to put on an entertaining match, and it takes a lot out of a guy. Tonight was no different, but tonight also brought about a thought that began to itch in the back of Joey's mind. I have to face Rashard... The thoughts continued in the back of his head. Poking at him and causing other thoughts to drift in his mind. What's going to happen when we lock up? It began to bother him. It was noticable in his expression, as he frowned and sat up. The idea of fighting one of his friends in the ring bothered him immensely. Emotions run wild in matches, especially ones where titles or contenderships were on the line. Could emotions run so wild that the friendship would be shattered again? If I beat him, would he take it personally? That thought drifted for quite some time. Rashard took wrestling almost too seriously, kind of like a devout zealot of the sport. A loss for Rashard could signal the end of something resembling a holy war against Joey. And the last thing Joey needed in his life, after finally finding some semblence of a future in the business, was Rashard Clark as an enemy. 'What's wrong?' a concerned female's voice asked. Joey turned toward the origin of the voice, which belonged to his wife, Keri. She had stayed in the locker room for Joey's match, mostly at Joey's request. 'Ah, nothing. Just a bit concerned about something.' Joey responded. 'Tell me.' So, Joey explained to Keri about the fact that he would have to face Rashard Clark, otherwise known as Syphon Fission, at Resentment. He told her about his worries about facing him. And when he was finished, Keri spoke. 'Well, I don't know, Joey, but just remember... I'm behind you, one hundred percent. Always.' she said, smiling. Joey had to smile at that. He smiled in his response. 'Always.'
