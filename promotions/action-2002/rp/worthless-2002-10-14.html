Worthless

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 14th, 2002 It had been a couple weeks since Hawkins made his appearance at Retaliation and almost became Action! champion. Almost is the key word. Hawkins was in his bed, face up, staring at the textured ceiling before it. Rough and pointy. It was amusing, trying to find some sort of pattern. It was all he could do bed-ridden. For the past couple weeks, Hawkins had been nursing a concussion he had gotten at the hands of LLB. Hawkins was unable to wrestle for weeks because he put all he had into ten minutes with LLB. And he almost came out victorious. He almost became the best. Almost. Hawkins hated that word now. That word cost him the joy in his life. That word forced him out of the ring 'for his own safety.' Hawkins just wished he was Joey Malone or Chris Fox. Reed wouldn't have let them nurse their injuries. Reed would have forced them to compete, because it's RATINGS BABY! But no, Hawkins isn't a draw. Hawkins isn't important. Hawkins doesn't matter. That's what ran through his head on repeat. Pathetic. Pointless. Worthless. Hawkins would return within the next week to Action! television, and he was going to make a name for himself. No matter how much of a price it took.
