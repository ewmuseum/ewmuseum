Action! — Tales

Superstar?

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Knock. Knock. Knock. Joey heard the knocking from his seat on his sofa. He was talking into his wireless phone, at the time of this knocking, and he groaned. It had been a few days since LDE3, and he had gotten out of the hospital about a day ago. Since Action!'s Pressure Point was to be in Phoenix, Joey wanted to stay at home for a bit and talk to some friends of his over the phone. 'Hold on a second, somebody's at the door.' he said into the phone, before he wandered over to the door, and opened it. 'Uh, hello?' he asked, finding himself looking down at a five foot, nine inch man in a black suit, white undershirt, and blue tie. In stark contrast, Joey was in his common attire of a Diamondbacks jersey and blue jeans. 'Ah, yes. You wouldn't be Mr. Joey Malone, would you?' the man asked, his black ponytail being knocked back and forth by the light breeze that punctuated Mesa's present and rather cheery weather. 'Yeah, that's me. What do you want, chief? I'm kind of busy.' Joey asked. He then thought over his words for a few split seconds. '...doing not much of anything.' The man coughed and cleared his throat, before he asked his next question. 'May I come in, Mr. Malone?' Joey thought about it. Guy in black suit, kind of odd-looking, but certainly nice enough. What the hell. He turned his attention back to his phone, and said something else into it. 'I'll call you back, Jon.' He hung up the phone and turned back to the man in question. 'Sure.' The man came into Joey Malone's small, white house, which was in a subdivision in Mesa. He had had the house ever since he came back from his honeymoon, after he and Keri pooled their money and resources together to move out of their respective apartments. When the man first entered the room, he noticed that there were a brown tile floor and an archway that led to the living room. To the left were the kitchen and the hallway that led to the other rooms of the house. In front of them was the living room, which had a white carpet and wallpaper, a sofa, a television, a VCR, a few potted plants, and a recliner. Joey showed the man to the recliner, and proceeded to sit down at the sofa. 'So, what do you want to talk about with the likes of me?' Joey asked, rather curious as to why this man wanted to talk to Joey. 'My name is Roger Garvin.' he began. Joey decided to interrupt with a question. 'Garvin? Any relation to that Jeff Garvin idiot that I saw over in 21W? Before he quit?' Roger laughed. 'No, no no. I have never even heard of this... 21doubleu thing. Actually, I work for the WB. We air your Pressure Point show for Action, and we have taken a special interest in you and your wife.' 'So, what do you want out of me? If you want us to tone down the viol-' 'No, no no. You aren't even remotely as severe as the dreadful fighting thing that TNN airs every once in a while.' Roger said. 'We were interested in you because we wanted to offer you a deal for a sitcom on the WB.' Joey was about to have a smartass remark for this, but as soon as the words reached his brain and his brain processed it, he got as far as pointing his index finger at the man and opening his mouth, with no sound coming out. Surely he couldn't be serious. Could he? 'Actually, we wanted both you and your wife.' he added, a moment later. 'We caught some tapes of your promos in the federations you've worked for before, of both you and your wife doing things for the sake of doing them. You filmed just about anything you felt like filming, and no matter how silly-looking your props looked, it was always the two people mainly involved, you and Keri, that made it all worth watching. You're both naturals at showing emotion and conveying a story, even with the low budget films and cameras that you've been working with for the Internet Wrestling Organization.' 'Er, that is... uh...' Joey said, at a loss for words at the sheer elation of Roger's comments. 'I understand that you're contracted and all, but I'm sure you can work something out with your boss.' Roger added as he stood up. 'Whatever works for you.' Joey blinked, appreciating the kindness that this man shockingly displayed, despite the fact that he worked for the television industry. 'Um, can I think about this for a few days?' Joey asked. 'I think I might want to talk to Keri about it.' Keri was, at this moment, sleeping in her bed for once. 'Sure. No problem. If you come to a decision, you can page me, since I'll be in town for a while.' Roger said, handing Joey a slip of paper. 'That's my number. Glad to have talked to you, Mr. Malone.' 'The pleasure's all mine.' Joey said, escorting the man out of his house. As he closed the door behind him, he quietly wondered what this meant for his wrestling career and where it would take him. He wanted to ask Keri, but there was someone else he wanted to ask for advice, and Joey would see him at the next Pressure Point. He wanted to ask his best friend.
