Down and Out. (Part I)

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.29.02 The Crowd: ERECTION! Young stopped dead in his tracks. Before he could even turn around he was hit. Clothesline by LLB! Ump: YES! YES!! LLB!! LLB IS HERE TO SAVE THE DAY!! HE JUST- JUST NAILED REED YOUNG WITH A CLOTHESLINE!! THERE'S A COVER RAMSEY!! Ump: ONE!! Ump: TWO!! Ump: KICKOUT!! MY GOD HE KICKED OUT!! Shaw: Thank the lord! Stupid LLB! He twitched. LLB continued to ringside. Ramsey and Denton took some time, but eventually reached their feet. Denton kicked at Ramsey, but Conrad caught it again. Steven was then able to reach back and hook his arms onto the ropes to prevent another dragon screw leg whip. Shaw: He's a frickin' genius! He's blocking the dragon screw! Ump: Very smart. But he forgot about LLB... A chill shook his spine. LLB reached up and was able to pry Denton's hands from the ropes... 'Holy shit!' Ramsey hit the dragon screw. His eyes fluttered open and shut, his body no longer under his control. Achilles tend hold. Tap... Tap... Tap... He couldn't shake the images from his head. 'Steven Denton?' The sound of his own name brought him back to reality. He glanced around him, slowly remembering where he was. Oh, right, the doctor's office, he thought. Only one other man sat in the waiting room with him. His eyes were baggy and his skin wrinkled in every possible place. The man's coat seemed to cover his whole, maybe 5 foot frame. Steven looked down at himself, and sighed as he realized he too would probably look the same one day. 'Mr. Denton... is that you, sir?' He glanced up at the young nurse that stood at the head of the waiting room. She stared right back at him. 'Yeah, that's me.' 'The doctor will see you now,' her voice sang a happy tune, but her eyes told a different story. She wasn't a very patient nurse. Steven climbed from his seat and began walking towards the door. On the way, he noticed a new pain in his knee, but shook it off. He felt so much pain every day that a little nag in his knee didn't mean a thing. He opened the door at the head of the room, leading him into a white room. He hated going to the doctor. 'Hey, Steven! Been awhile since I've seen you here,' the doctor said, a smile on his face. He hated the old guy, but he was a good physician. He knew his shit. 'Action!'s got trainers. They wouldn't know what biceps are if they had any themselves, but I never get hurt enough to care,' Steven sat on the small hospital bed in the middle of the room. Another thing he hated, those tiny fucking beds. 'Hah. Well, what brings you here then?' 'Back pains. Young told me to go see a company doctor, but those guys are worse than the trainers.' 'Hm. Well, why don't you step into that closet and put on this robe. I'll run a few tests and whatnot then.' Ugh. A hospital robe. This visit would never end. --- Now back in what he'd worn to the office, Steven sat on the edge of the bed. Doctor Forguson (he has a name now, I got tired of saying 'the doctor') looked over the notes from the tests he'd just ran. At first it seemed standard, just a few glances and stupid doctor noises. But after a moment, his middle-aged face frowned. 'Well, you know that bad elbow you've always had.' 'Yeah.' 'It's fine. I don't know that a brace is even necessary anymore. It's healed perfectly.' Steven shrugged. Lucky him. 'That all?' Steven asked, ready to leave. 'Not quite...' He took a deep breath. Bad news stood on the tip of Forguson's tongue. 'Hmm... um, can you lay down on your stomach for a moment.' 'I guess.' Steven turned around and laid on his stomach, his legs hanging off the edge of the bed. Doctor Forguson bent Steven's leg, and twisted it inward and outward, which brought several shouts of pain and protest from Denton. 'What the fuck is wrong with you?' Steven screamed, as the doctor finally relinquished his hold on Steven leg. Anger filling his mind, he quickly rolled off the bed and onto his feet. He stood up straight... but then almost collapsed as his knee gave out completely. 'What the fuck did you do?!' Forguson quickly helped Steven regain his footing, and seated him back on the hospital bed. 'Steven, there's no easy what to say this. I'll have to take a few x-rays, but it looks like, well... it looks like you've torn your meniscus. And judging from the amount of pain it seems you just felt, the tear is probably quite large...' A Meniscus Tear is a injury to the cartilage inside the knee. This cartilage normally acts as a shock absorbing cushion between the thigh bone (femur) and the shin bone (tibia). Meniscus tears can result from a sudden traumatic injury such as a violent twisting of the knee. Tears may also occur without significant trauma.* Steven already knew this, but when could he have possibly hurt his knee? The Fans still believed, though. They were stomping their feet, clapping their hands. No chanting. Just stomping and clapping. Denton was trying to power the old man up and then bring him down onto the back of his neck with the Denton Driver. Ump: If he hits this, it will be over. Shaw: Hell yeah it will. Steven Denton is walking out of this arena with the Bantam Title. Denton had lifted Conrad's feet off the canvas. He was almost able to get him over... His knee gave out. Oh. 'How long?' Steven said. He wasn't even listening to what the doctor was saying. 'Well, you'll need surgery, but only 6 to 8 weeks after that... and in your condition, you should recover quickly...' 'I'm fucked.' 'No, no. You'll be back...' 'I. Am. Fucked. And I can't remember the last time I wasn't.' Steven collapsed backwards onto the bed. All he wanted now was revenge... *Credit http://www.emedx.com/.
