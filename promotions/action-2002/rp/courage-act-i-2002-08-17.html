Courage - Act I

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/17/2002 'What do you MEAN she's not here?!' Coral yelled. He had just driven a couple of thousand miles only to find that Allison wasn't even in her dorm room at the moment. The news was delivered by her roommate. 'That's what I said. She took off. Probably went to the mall or somethin'.' she said, chewing on her gum. She seemed to be quite the airhead, but Coral didn't want or care to analyze the girl right now. 'You wanna take a message or somethin'?' Coral blinked. 'Er, um... no. I'll swing by later.' Coral hastilly said. He had the feeling from the way this girl smiled and looked him over that she was a bit of a slut, and that kind of thing was not gonna be needed when he desperately needed to talk to his girlfriend again. 'Well, whatever.' she said, rolling her eyes. She shut the door in Coral's face. Well, at least some things don't change in his life, with people shutting doors in his face. Coral quickly walked away. This trip was going to be the death of him. --- Five minutes later... Allison Lindum quietly walked into her dorm room. She had just gotten back from a quick walk around the campus. 'I'm back.' she cheerilly said. 'Well, hey.' her roommate responded, lying on her bed and staring up at the celing. She didn't seem to care too much about anything at the moment. 'Anything exciting happen while I was gone?' Allison asked. Her roommate didn't really move. 'No, not really. Some guy came over, though. Lookin' for ya.' 'Guy?' Allison said, confused. 'There was a guy?' Her roommate sat up. 'Well, yeah. Tall. Odd hair coloring. Kinda stuttered.' Allison's jaw dropped. She dropped her purse. She wasn't believing this in the least. 'You're kidding!' 'You just let him go?' Allison asked. She couldn't believe that Coral came all this way to see her. Maybe there was a show and he was in the area. 'Well, yeah. Thought he was a stalker or somethin'.' 'Oh, you big... idiot person!' Allison said, before running right out of the door. Her roommate just rolled her eyes and laid back down on her bed. 'What... ever.'
