Cut the Ribbons prt2 - Goddamned Cell Phone!

14 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.14.03 'Yes but I need you to find it now! Its a classic, custom, one of a kind! Everything in it was chosen especially by my Fath- hello? HELLO? GODDAMN THIS THING!' Bobby heaved the cell phone across the bus, it hit the Plexiglass window and fell to the floor, the shock of the hit causing the lights under the dials and the reading panel to blink inconsistently. It began beeping and Bobby tried his hardest to ignore it, to ignore the stench, to ignore the scenery around him. The bus driver called out a street and Bobby knew his stop was quickly approaching but he tried his hardest to hold out for these last few seconds. It was like waiting for the bell to ring on the last day of school but the monotone teacher kept talking and talking and talking, and droning on and talking and droning on and talk--- SSCREESSSH! Bobby Minio lost all self control and slammed his foot down over the recently crippled cell phone, now leaving it completely out of commission. It died with a sickening electrical hum, it gave a soft cry as the life was taken from it... The inanimate object. However Bobby felt more remorse in the idea of explaining how the phone died to Sprint to get a replacement then the issue of its death rattle. He now had two objects of his every day life taken away from him in his own carelessness. His car was stolen when he left it unlocked and running in ten minute parking earlier in the city when he was asking for directions to his Mother's house, and the phone when he lost all control over his human impulses to crush technology. To hammer the laptops, to smash out the monitors, to crack and leak the flat screen plasma displays, to take a knife and decimate every sub woofer in an import vehicle with an electric unit in the dash, to take a baseball bat and smash every television until the black lifeless screens were an orgy of white shattered glass dancing on the edge of the insanity, to fire a sawed off shotgun at every LCD display alarm clock, to drop kick every Compact Disc Walkman with auto control over the skipping which made the goddamn things SO HUMAN... To take it all away. Bobby Minio, twenty four year old gladiator of the squared circle, found himself weeping on a bus infront of two dozen strangers. Why? Because he was unhappy with his life. Why? Because he was lonely. Why? Because he didn't know what in god's blazes he was going to do when he saw his Mom. Why? Because the past twenty four years have been a big fucking waste. Why? Because I'm tired of answering to you.
