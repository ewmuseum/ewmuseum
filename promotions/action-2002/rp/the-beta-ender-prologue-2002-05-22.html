The Beta: Ender (Prologue)

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The ender is Fission. The fission is Ender. He walks around, killing, destroying, TOUCHING RUINING INVADING! He hates everyone one. Everyone hates him. Fission is Action! Wrestling’s first and foremost authority. He is Action! Wrestling’s biggest draw. What will he do on this show? Will he invade the IWO and tie the old story together? Will he torch another set? Will he pull a gun on someone? Reed Young is one of those people who believe he controls everything he owns. Problem is that he doesn’t own Syphon Fission. He only shields his venomous parades on the roster of his federation as nothing. Who is Syphon Fission? We saw him once as a big funny guy. We saw him as a manic depressant. We saw him as a caring man. We saw him as a used man. Now, we see him as a mass of complexes. Anything that gets in his way gets crushed now. He doesn’t seem to care who or what it is. He ends it by wrecking life. He wants payment from a world far removed from the one that cast him away. One person triggered a chemical, that festered inside this man’s brain, and now he stands as damn near unbeatable and with aims at revenge. Revenge doesn’t pay off. It soothes the soul for a little while. Syphon Fission knows fewer things then you ever could as to who he is. He believes he is a dead man, walking for the sole purpose of doing away with a enemy. Fission doesn’t even know why anymore. Fission would rather die now then anything. Except he has to tie loose ends. Joey Malone has had a rather easy life. Syphon Fission is the example of the hard route. He is a survivor. But so is Malone. That, he can’t stand. Fission thinks of it as a two man mountain that he was ousted from thanks to the powers that be. Fission used to fall into line. Now, he hits those in line with a shovel. Make enemies? What threat is that when you are already dead… Or veering close to it. Fission, does he believe in God? Maybe But if he does, He hates that fucker.
