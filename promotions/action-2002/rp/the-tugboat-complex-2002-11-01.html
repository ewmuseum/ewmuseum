Action! — Tales

the Tugboat Complex

1 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.01.02 Darkness spattered with the green glow. An alarm clock. Blinking. 4:00am. Minio shot up in his bed, the covers pouring into his lap. He eyed the clock, warily. He had that dream. Again. It always started out the same. Digging his feet into the dirt. A rope over his shoulder, the twisted grains tearing into his flesh. He dragged the weight of the world over his back. Blood poured down his left side like a dysfunctional fountain... The beauty was lost in the metaphor. The beauty was drapped in a sheet of feigned pride. The beauty was hidden behind a brick wall with one word tagged in an urban message, 'Honor'. Spelt with an H the artist stole from heritage. Minio blinked himself into clear thought. He turned to the side to his left. His skin was clear of cuts, of gashes, of wounds. He stared at the emptiness of the matress beside him. He missed sharing it with Angelica. He missed her warm body. He missed the affection. He spun his body in the bed, slinging his legs over the bedside. He was wearing blue boxer shorts, his legs looked red, stretched, and torn, as if he was dragging the empire state building on a sled behind him. He stood, walking through the distant hotel room. He had never been here before. He had never been in this city before. But for some reason, he could navigate every inch of his cell. He knew where to find the shower cap. The bible. The complimentary soap which was the size of a modest chocolate. The shotglass shampoos. The coat hangers. The remote control glued to the bed post. The post its. The pens. The towels he would steal if he gave a damn. The mirror. He dragged along in the half asleep state his body was in. The only concious item on this cluttered mass of flesh and bones was his eyes. He stumbled through the bathroom doorway, flipping the lights on unenthusiastically. He stared into his own eyes in the wall sized mirror provided by the Hyatt. He stared deep and hard, expecting something to twitch. Expecting something to move. He wanted to find that he was in a dream. In a fantasy. He didn't want this to be real. He wanted to wake up in a new location. Fifteen minutes passed and not a single item moved that he did not have control over. He finally appeared broken in his own staring match. He turned towards the towel rack, slapping both of his palms over the railing. The undersized towels slid down, falling onto the polished porcelin toilet. He tugged, and one of the side peices gave way from the plaster wall, a white dust puffing out as the screws popped from their former molds. He pulled the bar, shaking the end peices to the tiles, letting loose an empty scream of aluminum clanking against white ceramics. He stormed into the room, the dark cave's only sources of light was the off glow of the bathroom and the green alarm clock. He took a strong ground, and slammed the bar against the wall. He then carved. He chissled a message. He didn't seem to care about noise, but the whole ordeal wasn't that loud. After a few minutes he stopped, he was finished with his massacred masterpeice. He stood, breathing hard, huffing and puffing, staring at what was left of the defaced plaster. Carved into the wall was a simple statement. It said enough. It said it all. 'You're gonna carry that weight.' He dropped the bar, then dragged himself back to the side of the bed. He laid down, not even taking the time to pull the covers over his body. The tugboat was laid to rest. Within seconds he was dead asleep. Off to dream again, of dragging himself his rock. His weight. His world.
