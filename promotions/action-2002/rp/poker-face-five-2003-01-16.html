Poker Face: Five

16 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Thursday, January 16th, 2003 “I swear, Joey, you’ll ge’cha money!” Dillon was pleading in to the reciever of the pay-phone. “Ok, Ok, I know a guy. He’ll forward you half of it, and I’ll get you the other half in a week. Ok?” “Good talkin with ya, baby...” Click. As Dillon waited for a good-bye, Joey Riggs hung up on him. “Nice guy...” he muttered to himslef, before hanging up, then putting in another couple quarters. He picked up the reciever and dialed some numbers. *ring* *ring* “Hey, Jer, it-” “DILLON~!” was heard on the other side. “Yeah, shut up for a second. I need a favor.” “Yeah, well, can you get backstage at one of your shows?” “C’mon, remember when you dropped me on my he- oh yeah, that was me to you.” “Yes I have experience! We went to school together, until you dropped out.” “Yeah, well, there were problems with some wages, which is why I never got my shot.” “Well, that sounds good.” “Yeah, that’d be great.” “Ok, see ya soon Jeremey.” Dillon hung up the phone and returned to the bus stop with a huge smile on his face.
