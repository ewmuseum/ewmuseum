Action! — Tales

The Leader - Take 1

11 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.11.2002 So many things come to mind when one thinks of family. Love. Kindness. Trust. Yet, what about the dysfunction which constantly runs rampant in today’s society? Do people choose not to associate words with those situations? Obviously. For life on Earth in this day and age is all about image, and hatred between relatives just isn’t something positive for people to look at. With every beaten child and every addicted parent, these brutalities trapped inside something which is told to stand for love are hidden from view. Kyle Black grew up in one of those unspoken environments, and eventually decided that he would rather live on the streets then conform to what anyone else wanted in terms of his young life. Never once did his father pick up a baseball and attempt to play catch … choosing to spend his time at the bottom of a bottle than with his son. Nor did young Kyle ever experience the warmth of his blanket after his mother tucked him in … for she looked at Kyle and everything about him, including where he slept, as an expense not needed. So each night, Kyle Black slept silently on his bare bed, while inside he struggled to put words to what he was feeling. Night in, and night out … the boy became an orator … able to cast a spell on only himself, for nobody cared enough to listen. His parents hid what they did to him until the day they mysteriously disappeared … the words associated with the unloving family echoing through his brain as the child transformed into a man. Darkness. Torture. Pain. Yet, as he scrounged on the streets, the man he became was slowly transforming again. Years later, this new creature wakes up in a cold sweat, facing the same darkness which for so long was related to the whip of his father’s belt and the black stare of his mother. The slight flicker of a match is heard, a small light erupting against the darkness of the room, illuminating his face. But who? None other than Xander Pollack, who in body is Kyle Black, but in spirit … something went wrong. “Kylie…” He whispered. Nothing. Not even a slight grunt from the woman he loves. Xander is no stranger to nothing, however, for he has always been marked as just that. “Kylie…” He whispers again, this time nudging the beautiful woman next to him. “Please wake up.” Finally she rises next to him, only the tired drooping of her eyes being shown by the firelight. Her name is Kylie Pollack, and her past is unknown. What is important, at least to Xander, is her future. A future which he hopes to mold, just like he has already done for so many others. “What is it Xander?” Mumbles the woman. “I had the dream again.” He says to his spouse, shuddering with every word. This was indeed uncharacteristic for Pollack … who has been watched since inception and made out to be a strong willed individual. Who wouldn’t be, with the backing of thousands, and more importantly that of his partner Garrett Hollister? Now, laying in bed and obviously thinking about his tortured childhood, he was weak. “Listen Xander…” Kylie speaks clearly. “You know I care for you … but there is nothing I can do for you when enduring this situation. The past is yours to control, and I cannot help you to overcome it, for that will isn’t that of our higher power.” Xander Pollack seems to erupt in anger, slapping his wife across her face. “Who are you to tell me what my will is?” She seems to look down at herself in shame, almost as if admitting that she created reason to be abused. “For I am the higher power in which you speak.” He speaks in a mixture of pride and fear. “And my children, including yourself Kylie my dearest, answer to me because of the emphasis I put into that very fact.” He kisses her cheek, red from his palm. “I’m sorry.” She silently speaks. “You are right, you always are.” “Right or wrong isn’t the problem…” Xander speaks, getting up from his position of rest and standing before his wife. “It’s my mind state. I cannot project two images for the people, for they are the tools to spread my message. They see me mold words and insecurities together in strong bursts of confidence, but what will happen when they see me scared at the very thought of my former family, resorting to beating a meek woman to ease my pain.” Former? They are his blood. Complex is his story. “You need not have fear of your past, Xander.” Kylie reassures him. “But how?” He looks on in disgrace, no doubt hurt by the fact that he has enough knowledge to captivate the human mind, but must go to his wife for advice when it comes to his own. “Your past consists of brutal memories, a family that you were born into.” “Yes?” He answers back quickly. “But what about the family you built yourself?” Kylie asks. “They are the only relatives that matter … realize that … and forget the ones who never cared. You and Garrett are strong as a tandem, and you’re together because of a bond stronger than the dirty blood which you and your father shared.” His smile begins to shine through the wear on his face. Almost as if his next speech is coming to him. Xander Pollack’s ability to provoke each wrestling fan by using their own ignorance begins to flow through his body, almost like the blood which is only cleansed by his status as a father who replaces his own in spirit. “Remember your new roots, and forget the horrid ones you were born with.” Kylie speaks again. “Remember the positive with your status as a “Leader”. If all else fails … talk to your most important follower for guidance and a look into just how important we see you as. Lose your mind … and lose what you’ve accomplished.” Minutes later, across the ocean in Germany, a man receives a call and breaks from his lunch to answer. “Hello?” He answers slowly. “Garrett?” The brooding voice of Xander Pollack answers. And so the two begin to chatter, forming a plan which will slowly unfold, much to the chagrin of professional wrestling fans and competitors alike. The two key members of what one writer called “the most complex cult since the Manson Family” plotting to use sports entertainment as a gateway. Not for stardom. Not for fame. But to spread a distorted message. Why is that certain message distorted, anyone would ask? Simply because this “family” could be just as dysfunctional as Kyle Black’s, only in a more mental way. In Garrett Hollister, Xander has that single follower, who is so mislead in life that he’ll do anything for his father. The details of his past are very thin, but there is one clear thing which Xander Pollack can boast in his favor when it comes to Hollister. Seven feet and one inch. Three hundred and sixty-four pounds of pure muscle. He is the beating heart behind The Brotherhood. He is the brutality. He is the enforcer. Without him, Xander would be nothing but a mouthpiece, not a world class wrestler and leader of men. Perhaps Garrett knows this, at that is exactly why he hangs around. And Xander certainly knows this, for he pumps the immigrant’s mind with those very sentiments. Yet, between the abuse given to his wife and the empty promises to his so-called children, greed took over what were once the ideals of a proper family life. His gain will bring equality amongst his people for an instant, but when he’s leeched all he needs from them, it will be worse than that beating a father gave a son … more treacherous than the neglect given by the mother to her daughter who only seeks approval. The followers will be left outside. The leader will reign supreme. “Do it for your brothers, Garrett.” Speaks Xander slyly. “Do it for your father.” Xander Pollack hung up the phone and with that slight click, there was no more sound. Neither the slight breath of the man’s wife nor the breath from his own mouth emerged through the thick air. An eerie silence was all that stood. Yet, with the glare of his beady black eyes shining through the darkness, it was evident that in time the eerie silence would turn into a brooding violence. For Xander Pollack’s words can only speak certain volumes. It is time that the words which made him popular turn into what is always louder. Action!
