On The Road to Success (Alternate Ending)

16 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/02 OPEN This is an alternate ending to the On The Road to Success episodes. This has nothing, or will have nothing to do in further Christopher Fox adventures. Writer’s note: Some of the following material contains explicit content. Readers discretion advised. The scene opened and displayed the same place where our hero was last seen. The super big McDonalds with a play land. Of course by now the store had closed and everything was still; the balls in the ball pit sat motionless with moonlight seeping through the window and the jungle gym collecting tiny amounts of dust. The weather had since changed from a nice sunny afternoon to a drizzling dark crisp night. A little bit of frost could be seen forming on the grass. And as for the Pikachu Beetle, it was still parked outside. You can hear a voice, not one, but two.... it turned out that down the hall a light was still on. Christopher Fox and Onjin sat at the twenty-four-seven takeout window. Except, they haven’t seen a customer in about five hours.... Title: On The Road to Success (Alternate Ending) Date: 11/16/02 11:00 pm EST Place: McDonalds Play Land Fox sat in a padded swivel seat as did his friend Onjin. If it wasn’t for Onjin, Fox would be in juvenile detention. lol. Onjin sat across from ‘Kid’, as he nibbled on a fry and looked out of the takeout window. Onjin “What a day, eh Fox? First I got transferred a couple of days ago, and then I save you from getting in trouble. Now we’re just hanging out. What next?” Fox “Hmm I dunno, I sure hope Pikachu is otay in the car though....” Onjin “I’m sure he is.” Wiping some major grease off of his face, Onjin rose from his chair. Hey, what’s he gonna do? Why’d he just grab his jacket? He wouldn’t dare go out in this kind of weather! Onjin checked his watch. Onjin “Quitin’ time.” Fox “But this is a 24/7 takeout window.” Onjin “Yeah, but there is a McDonalds policy here. If no service comes in five hours, then we have the option to close for the night. Anyway, c’mon Fox let’s go outside and have some fun!” Onjin proceeded to exit the small takeout booth and into the main part of the building, but before this could happen Fox had a burning question that he just had to put out. Fox “Onjin, what’s your last name?” Onjin turned after he opened the door. They stood, quietly. Onjin “My last name is Dyce.” Fox “Onjin Dyce. I new it! I mean.... I like it!” Is he related to who I think he is? Onjin “Ok? ....C’mon, lets go.” Both “kids” headed to the main exit / entrance. Fox won the race and progressed outside’ the air was just crisp enough not to make anyone too cold. Christopher had a t-shirt on and he seemed fine. In fact he was boiling hot! They walked out to the curb, as Onjin sat down and then Fox did too. Both gazed up to the beautiful three-quarter moon. Onjin “Darn, this would have been like a Disney moment if it was a full moon. I never have those moments!” Fox “I hear ya.” As they talked a slight bit of breath could be seen by both men. It was maybe a bit too cold now. Onjin “Can I ask you something Christopher?” Fox “You can ask me anything within my Dragonball Z powered body!” Onjin “Did you know a guy named Cory?” Fox “My Mommy was high school sweat-harts with him! Why do you ask?” Onjin “He was my Dad. He told me that she is all grown-up and has a kid who wrestles. I’m glad I found you, I was worried when I was transferred.” Fox “Why did you want to tell me that?” Onjin “....heh....” Onjin’s head lowered. Fox sat nicely and blinked innocently. Fox “What is it? Listen, all I know is that my Mommy said goodbye to your Daddy when your Daddy quit High School, if I remember so much!” Oh boy, how could I forget!? Mommy always told me her bedtime nightmares. I mean stories! Stories! Onjin’s eyes seemed different. His head came up as grease was still dripping down his face, but a knife was in his hand. Onjin smirked evilly. Onjin “That’s a lie! Remember when you first met me? I wasn’t sure that it was your family. Joey never told me he attended the same school as your Mother. Do you want to know what really happened.... or for me to cut to the chase?” Fox “N-No.... take your time.... BIG TIME!!!” Onjin “Ok, you asked for it.... let’s begin. It was in high school. Your Mommy wasn’t the hottest but she caught the eye of my Dad. My Dad’s name was Cory. Cory Dyce, the jock, well at least of the loner group. Cory was friends with your bitch Mom, and then Cory asked her out and she agreed, yada, yada, yada and then my Dad got a different girl pregnant, so then your Mommy left my Dad and made good friends with other people like Joey and stuff. Anyways, the night of the prom my Dad took that pregnant girl. I was just happy to be that kid. Mommy and Daddy had a good time until they left. The school sports jock, I believe his name was Bryan or something, was drunk and “accidentally” ran over my Dad with his car as they crossed the street....” Fox “What does this have to do with me?” Onjin “Since your Mom was the only witness, she said Bryan didn’t mean to and Bryan got off scott free! It was on your Mom’s head that my Dad didn’t receive any justice! I believe she wanted Cory to die because he got my Mom pregnant! That’s why I’m going to kill you, then I’ll track down your Mom.... RAPE HER and keep her head as a door prize!” With a quick flinch, Onjin’s eyes opened tremendously! Onjin “You think I was transferred here? I don’t think so! I realized back when you wanted a hot pocket that you were the family. That’s why I injected it with poison!! Hahaha!! Then I killed Joey, the blood skids still slash across my apron too. Now right here is were you meet your demise, your road to success ends HERE!” Strife, Fox.... dodged it! The knife crashed violently in the curb as it bounced up slightly. Fox rolled to his feet, got up and gave a swift kick to the head of Onjin. Only a crack could be heard, as Onjin fell and laid.... motionless. Not dead. Terrified, Fox jumped into the Pikachu Beetle and grabbed his cell phone that came with the Action! Wrestling Contract he signed a while back. However, the phone was frozen and had no battery power left. Onjin rose, dragging himself slowly toward the car, as Fox kept pressing on but it wasn’t working! Desperately, Fox mashed the cell phone with his hand.... B-Bee.... BEEP! ON! Fox “LOL? IT’S ON! NOW DOES ANYONE KNOW THE NUMBER FOR 911!?” Onjin “Arrrrrrrrrrrgh!” Onjin hurled his knife, scraping it against the wind shield! Onjin “C’mon out! I want you to meet Mr. Machete, muhahahahaha!” Fox “Heto? There’s a m- I can take care of this! I’m a very successful person!” Fox turned the car on and drove until he nicked Onjin’s left leg, causing him to fall! Fox “Oh drat! He’s getting up again!” Onjin “I don’t need to kill you, just as long as I don’t get killed by a Fox! Goodbye Chris!” Christopher closed his eyes, as only one sound could be heard, a body hitting the ground. Fox opened up his eyes.... only to see Onjin there, stabbed by himself. Dead on the ground. 911 Lady “Hello? Mister, should we send an ambulance?” ‘Kid’ talked to himself. Fox “Well, that sure wasn’t a Disney Moment....” The scene faded, as it showed a car surrounded in mist as Onjin laid across the way, on the cement. Dead. CLOSE
