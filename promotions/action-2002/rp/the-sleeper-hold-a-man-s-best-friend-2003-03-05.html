The Sleeper Hold: A Man's Best Friend

5 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
03/02/03 It was good to be back in the comfort of his own apartment. The heavy work load he had been carrying lately, what with competing on both Action! and Asylum shows left him fatigued, especially after his brutal match up with Keegan at Bloody Valentine last Sunday… A bout that left him bloodied and beaten, with a possible broken nose. Back in his home, late Sunday night, Jeff sat back in his favourite chair sipping his favourite soft drink, that being a nice, cool Dr. Pepper. With his feet soaking in a bucket of warm water that Julie had prepared for him, Chambers stood in behind the recliner massaging his mentor’s temples. “Oh yeah,” said Garvin, his eyes closed as Chris’s tiny fingers slowly made his splitting head ache fade, “that’s perfect.” Chambers couldn’t help but roll his eyes. He had decided to let Jeff train him for one reason: So he could be trained! Yet all he had been doing for the last month was the man’s chores… How was it HIS duty to clean Jeff’s house? And God knows how many times he had been asked to pick up his laundry! “Jeff, don’t you think we could, I don’t know… get in a few hours of ring time tonight? I mean, I have no problem with your methods but you spent three hours teaching me the wonders of a Sleeper,” Chris interjected as he stopped moving his fingers. “Oh, Chris, dear boy… will you ever learn. These things take time and until you learn the basics, you’ll never get to the half-level mark in the ‘Original System of Wrestling’. A Sleeper Hold can become your best friend in a match. It wears the man down; it puts you in control. As I said, we start with the basics and work our way to the harder stuff.” “Yeah but couldn’t we skip a few steps? Just a couple, maybe?” “Chris… if you try and tell me how to do my job again I’ll slap the taste out of your mouth. I’m God to you- you do not challenge my teachings.” “Fine.” “Now back to work, slave! Er… I mean, Chris…” Chambers did as he was told and continued the massaging of Jeff’s temples. “Tomorrow night, Chris, we reveal the fourth member to our little group… and we break the big news to the Bantam Champion, Airborn Tyler… …I’ll show you how we deal with blonde haired little sissies that don’t deserve what they have.”
