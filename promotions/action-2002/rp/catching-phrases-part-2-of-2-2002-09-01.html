Catching Phrases (Part 2 of 2)

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/01/02 OPEN “Yay! Mommy, can we go on that ride?” The scene turns and shows a ideal adult sized male jump into a very small bumper car. “Umm, no dear, there’s a strange man in that car. Doesn’t he know that these bumper cars are for kids?!” The view changes to the man with a guard standing over him. “Sir, you, uh, have to leave.” Title: Catching Phrases (Part 2 of 2) Date: 08/31/02 9:00 pm EST Place: Carnival Christopher Fox slams his hands on the wheel of the bumper car that he barely fit into. Tap, tap, tap. Fox calms himself down by tapping his fingers on the wheel. After counting to ten, Fox then gets out of the mini car, turns his head and scampers over to Joey Malone. Fox “That didn’t work. I was only trying to have fun too!” Malone leans his back on the railing and looks to the sky, while Fox hunches his back and cracks his Super Mario wallet open, beginning to count his money. Malone “Chris will you ever learn?” With a 5 dollar bill in his hand he replies. Fox “I learned my A B C’s!” As Chris talked he waves his hands in the air. Already a bad sign. Fox “A, B, D, E, C, F, G, H, I, J, K, L, L, M, NO, P, Q, Rust, T, U, Double V, X and Zeeeeeeeeee! Now I know my A, B, C, D, F, 7, 3, won’t you cum play with me?” I think it’s that way. Or is it A, B, E.... ? Humph! That’s the lastest time I watch Sesame Street! They only review one letter per day! Malone’s eyes widen with some sort of feeling. Malone “Uh yeah.... maybe I should go home or something, after all it is way past my bed time.” Malone “I think that’ll catch him! I’m pretty sure Fox’s Mom gave him a curfew.” Fox “No, actually she didn’t!” Malone “What?” Fox “Since your with me I can read your thoughts! Hehe!” Malone “O... k....” Fox “LOL! Gotcha!” Relief is the only action Malone portrays after Fox finishes his sentence. Fox “I’m not letting you go home yet, homes! I gotta catch you a phrase! Ummm how ‘bout ‘Big Frog’! Yeah! You can call everyone a ‘frog’! Frogs go hop, hop, hop! Hehehehe!” Malone “Ummm no thanks.” Fox “Well just to let you know bud, I’m not leaving your side until I find you a phrase!” After taking in a huge gulp of air, Joey shrugs his shoulders and heads for a bench. Fox dances his way over and leaps himself next to his new found bud. Fox “Otay, I’m going to tell you some catch phrases and you can chose one! Here we go!” Fox clears his throat and starts the list. Fox “Big killer, big goal, big move, big mama, big rapper, big molecule, big telephone repair guy, big Pokemon cards, big Pikachu punk destroyer, big sumo stupid trash can slash ass bum, or big time.... BIG TIME!!!” It seems as if Fox yells the last two words as the people stare straight at Malone and Fox. Joey just sits there with his right eyebrow twitching a bit. The crowd then moves on laughing and pointing at the “losers”. Fox “That went well. So what do you think?” Malone “Ummm go-od.... I really need to see my wife. Really, I had fun-- never mind....” Joey gets up and starts to walk toward the exit. The scene turns and showed Malone’s face still trembling from all those weird things Fox said. Speaking of Fox, he could be seen behind Malone waving bye bye. Then a strange lady walks by Joey. Fox “Your Mom!” Just then Malone raises his head a bit and turns to see Mommy Fox run up to Christopher and give him a big hug! Fox “Hi Mommy! I didn’t know you were here!” Mommy Fox “Well Christopher I just wanted to-- your hair! Its all out of shape! Come here and let me fix it.” Fox “NO!” Fox stands and stamps his feet at his Mom’s demand. Mommy Fox “Ok but you’re going to get it cut soon right?” Fox “Yes Mommy....” With that Mommy Fox walks on into the distance, as meanwhile Fox scratches his head wondering why she was at the carnival. “Hey.” Fox turns. Fox “Jo-D!” Malone “Your Mom. Thanks Chris.” Fox “Fuck you!” Malone “Excuse me!” Fox “That’s my way of saying you’re welcome! Aren’t I good! Wait a minute! Why are you thanking me?” Malone “For ‘arranging’ Mommy Fox to show up and give me my catch phrase! I use ‘your Mom’ all the time. It’s too perfect, thanks again! See you later bud.” Malone walks out of the carnival and into the distance while Fox was left talking to himself. Fox “Yes ‘arranged’....” Fox takes a seat. Fox “Did he just call me bud?” As Fox spoke, a couple of ladies walk by. Fox “Whoa! Ever since I got a bud I’m pretty popular!” The girls just walk by talking to each other. Fox, thinking he is cool and all, puts his arms on the top of the bench and crosses his legs. Yep, things are looking up for our hero. He has one bud. CLOSE
