The Tar Mat, The Second Part of

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There he was. Just standing there. Not a care in the world. Not a fear at all. Throughout his entire body, it seemed as though nothing at all mattered. He was blinded by love. “I’d like to join forces with you... BIG TIME!” Fox screamed, but ‘The Law’ just stood there, showing no emotion whatsoever. “No. Get lost.” Roland fast forwarded the tape. The whole thing made him sick. To think he’d even sit there and listen to what that sin had to say... And for most of the people, they would take a likening to Christopher. They would open up their hearts... and bring him in. Almost instantly too. Turning it around, the law was cold. It didn’t care. If you were guilty, it mattered not how much you loved the others, or how much they loved you. The verdict stood. It fucked you over. ‘The Child Written in Law’ was the same. No expressions ran throughout his face. He could’ve cared less. In fact, he wished that he had. He stopped the tape once more at a certain spot. “I love you LL!” Fox ran to hug the lawyer during another Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling show, but LLB just backed off, as Fox smacked into the wall. “Oh LL. You so silly.” “Fuck off.” LLB stormed out of the area... the Fans, cheering the evil sin on. “Oh LL. I love you...” “BIG-” The Crowd... the sly Fox... that stupid catch phrase... it rang in and out of ‘Law’s’ head enough. The pointless rambles of fun were very annoying. LLB didn’t want to see much more. However his hands controlled him at the moment. Time would tell where they’d take him next.
