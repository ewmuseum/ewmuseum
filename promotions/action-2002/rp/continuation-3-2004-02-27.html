OPEN Title: Continuation: 3 Date: Thursday November 28th, 2003 7:00 pm EST Christopher stood there. Fierce frothing at the mouth. His lokcer room was totally trashed and little did he know but he was the one who caused all of this carnage. It seemed that his morphine buzz faded ever so slightly, ever so slowly. Christopher limped his way out of his locker room while removing the froth from his mouth. He had that dazed look pasted on his mug. He had no clue what the hell happened and as he emergred as quiet as a mouse, almost every single Action staff member was stuck in their position staring at Fox wondering what the hell happened in there. Action Staff Member Joe “Does he know what he just did....?” Action Staff Member Chris “I don’t think so.” Joe “I think we should get going because I don’t want to get beat up eh?” Chris “Yeah I hear you buddy, lets just keep working.” The two staff members were the only two to start to go among their own business again. But that wasn’t pleasing to Fox. Fox “HOLD TITS! Fuck..... I mean stop.... you know.” Chris and Joe freeze right in their tracks as ‘Kid’ makes his way right into their faces. Fox “Alright you two! Who trashed my locker room? WHO?! Fucking tell me!” Joe “Don’t hurt us, it wasn’t any of us! It.... it was, ummmm, him!” Joe pointed to the next person who walked by. Woodchuck Bill. Fox “Oh you son of a wangsta! Hasn’t he had enough of me? You still want some more bitcha don’t ya!?” Chris turned his attention to Woodchuck who was occupied with his back turned to Fox, getting some coffee. SMACK! A right hand of Fox bouncing right off of Bill’s skull! He hit the table knocking all of the coffee over. Bill’s face was covered with blood and coffee. Fox “I’ve been working on a new moew buddy and seeing as you didn’t break the table, I’ll break it with your FACE! BIG FUCKING TIME!” Fox threw Bill onto his shoulders and delievered a Fox 5 right through the coffee table! The Action staff members all scrambled away for help. Fox “Hey it works pretty good huh Billy Bob? I hope you’ve learned your lesson this time bitch! DON’T EVER FUCK WITH THE POKEMON MASTER, KID DISASTER! .... BIG TIME!” CLOSE
