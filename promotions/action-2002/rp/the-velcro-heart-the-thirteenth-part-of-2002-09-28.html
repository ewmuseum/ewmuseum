The Velcro Heart, The Thirteenth Part of

28 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He limped the halls of the Arena, turning each corner at a slow, controlling speed. For as much as he hurt, and how dirty he felt, he was still going to keep his patients. It was always better to get there later, than never at all, anyway. Continuing to pace down the hall, LLB stopped for a second to catch his breath. Wrestling took so much out of you; this “behind the scenes” stuff was not that easy. With kids these days... and the critics. All they thought about was the show that they were going to see. Period. Nothing underneath. They could care less if you couldn’t walk after the contest was over... hell, they could care less if you couldn’t walk BEFORE the contest either. The bottom line, to them, was in the ring... they wanted to see you walk. Those that had it in them... walked. And those that didn’t, fell behind... jobbing for some senseless Indy Fed. LLB sighed. It’s a shame... it really was. For if people could only see *this* part of the wrestling side, maybe... maybe there’d be no hate in this game. Maybe people wouldn’t be ruled by the actions and words that they said... but by the huge heart in the body... to get the job done. No matter what. It’s a shame... it really was, to know that someone the Fan love... someone they think is just great... whom really, backstage, don’t do a damn extra thing, enjoyed. They don’t work out... they don’t stay late. And they don’t fight in crazy, on-the-spot ideas like a Chocolate Fountain Match. LLB did. But no one... from the Fans, to the Roster... to the Owner *himself* didn’t stay till three in the morning to care. It was a “thanks for the two hours of fun, do it again next week... or I won’t fucking like you” deal. Finally reaching the end of the hallway, the lawyer stopped, turned, and opened up Joey Malone’s old locker room. He walked in. So clean... so nice. Regardless of Bill’s look, he did his job well. And LLB could only sigh. Another hero of the “backstage” environment that took no credit. Jesus, compared to ‘The Law’ himself... he had nothing on top of him. The Action! Champion climbed into the shower stall, removing his clothes before turning the tap on. It was a long shower, and it seemed like it too... however LLB’s mind didn’t race through things like it normally would. He just kinda stood there. ---Scene Change--- Bill continued to clean Reed’s office as hard as he could. The stupid gum... it was stuck to the floor hard. It took some of Bill’s best work to get it off. The door opened up... as Bill turned around, seeing LLB standing there, all clean, in some blue pants and a blue shirt with a gray stripe running across it. “Hey... I know it’s pretty late, but I’m sure something’s got to be open. A donut store... maybe?” He rubbed the back of his neck. “Want to go get something?” Bill nodded. “After I clean up th-” “Na. Forget that. You did your best... if people stop inviting Reed to the Arenas... it’s all for the better, anyway.” He smiled, dropping his cleaning objects. “Sure.”
