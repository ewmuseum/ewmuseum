Action! — Tales

To Color. 13

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Forest Young was celebrating with the most expensive bottle of champagne he could find. Twisting off the top of the corkscrew, it popped open and began to fizz everywhere. Then the man of the hour came in, Title Belt in hand, a towel over his head, and absolutely drenched in sweat. Mark Paulton huffed as he made his way to the seat across the way from Forest Young, and once there, almost collapsed into it. The Owner of The Wrestling Federation smiled, though, as he took his bottle of champagne and poured Mark (and himself) a glass. “Would you care for some?” His voice was so polite, and understand of what Mark was going through. He knew that any wrong gesture could set the man off... so he tried to make very little of them. After all, he had just completed the fight of his life. “I’m fine, thanks.” Mark said, flipping the Belt across his left shoulder. “Ah... alright. Hand it over.” Mark smiled, as Forest did what was asked of him. Mark then took the champagne and chugged it back, before placing the plastic cup down on the ground. “You alright?” “Yeah. I’m fine.” Mark said, looking at his Title. “Nothing I can’t handle.” The compassionate silence was then interrupted... as Forest’s door whacked open, and Mark stood, in a fighting stance, ready and willing to take out what was about to come through. Sighing, he sat back down. For it was only four year old boy Reed Young. “Daddy!” Reed cried, as he ran forward, only to ram his head into his office desk and falling on the floor soon after. “Owy! My heady!” Mark watched on in amazement as Forest stood up to check on his son. “He... well he does that a lot.” Forest picked up the four year old boy and went back to sit down, but Reed had other ideas, running right into the bookshelf knocking book after book off of it. “Son, you shouldn’t.” Warned Forest. “Da de da da da ddee!” And down went a hard-covered book, dropping right on Reed’s head. “Da OWY! My heady!” Sighing himself, and standing up once more, Forest went to pull Reed away from the books. Mark watched on, but reassured his Boss that he didn’t even care by saying it was okay. “Anyway Mark...” As Forest sat back down, Reed had positioned himself right in front of Mark Paulton. “Now about your new Title run... I’d like to-” Reed stepped on Mark’s foot. “OW!!” “Owy?” Was all that the four year old’s questioned in reply. “No not ‘owy’ Reed, say sorry to Mister Paulton.” Reed only bit his thumb, as drool started to seep from his mouth, making a puddle in the floor. “Do de do do deeeeeeeeeee!!” “Right. Anyway Mark... I was thinking...” This time, though, Reed had started to tug on Paulton’s injured leg... and Mark had to swat the kid away. “You were saying Forest?” Mark said, not knowing that now Reed Young was trying to crawl up the back of his chair. “I believe you were at what we were going to do with my Title run.” “Ah, yes.” On top of Mark’s chair, Reed took a handful of his hair and tugged it back. “AAAHHH!! DAMMIT!!” And having enough of it, Mark took the kid from his head, placing him on the ground. That cued Forest to get up, take Reed and sit him on the bench outside. “Wait here. Got it!?” “Da do do do dddaaa!!” So Forest went back in the office, closing the door. “Yeah. About the Title run I-” SMACK!! Another thump on the door was heard, as Mark turned to open it... and there Reed Young laid, after ramming his head full-blast into it. “Da do do do DDDAAA!” He said squirming on the floor with drool falling out of his mouth. Mark smiled. “Cute kid.”
