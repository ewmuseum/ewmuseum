Suffering From V-D

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02.05.2003 Jessica 'Danger' Dox was laying in bed, continuing to slumber in peace and happiness. She was curled up slightly in a ball. Knees tucked into her chest, and back bowed forward. She looked beautiful, her husband thought as he looked over her. Marcus Alexander Dox(aka Max Danger aka Xander aka The Danger Man aka Your Lord and Savior aka [Insert Other Alias Here]) held in his hands a food tray. On it sat a rose, a single red rose, inside a crystal vase. Along side that was a plate stacked high with pancakes. A glass of Orange Juice next to it, and a small cup full of syrup(because, much like himself, Jess liked to dip her pancakes into the syrup, not cover the pancakes in it... made it soggy). He then woke his wife abruptly, 'Happy Valentine's Day, baby!' She woke with a startle, kicking her legs out from under the sheets, and nearly causing Max to drop her tray. Her eyes darted around the room momentarily, completely unaware of where she was. She caught sight of her husband and he smiled. She smiled back and then ran her fingers through her hair, removing them from her eyes. She sat up, wearing her blue pinstriped pajamas(matching button-up long sleeve top and pants). Her hair was in disarray, but it made no difference to Xander. 'Hey, honey,' she said to him. He then leaned forward and kissed her on the forehead. He then placed the tray across her lap. She looked up at him, eyes beaming. Her beautiful smile was shown again. 'Thank you!' she said with all the love she could muster. He sat down on the edge of the bed beside her. She leaned forward to kiss him, but he moved back. 'I don't think so, Jess,' he said shaking his head. 'You haven't brushed those pearly, not so whites, yet.' She playfully slapped him on the arm and still went in for the kiss. But he put a finger on them. 'No, seriously,' he remarked. She seemed slightly hurt. But then he leaned in and kissed her. She then smelled of the rose, 'It's lovely.' Though she cared little for flowers, which is why Max never bought more than one at a time. She would just end up neglecting it and causing it to die anyway, was his reasoning. They sat there for a moment, Max looking at his wife, her slowly cutting up her pancakes with her fork and dipping them slightly into the syrup. Nothing was spoken. Just two lovers enjoying being in the other's company. Finally Danger said, 'I've always found it amusing that the day that's all about love has the initials 'VD'.' Jessica took a drink of her orange juice and then replied, 'I never thought about that. Hmm. That's kinda strange, isn't it?' He just shrugged his shoulders. He wasn't really going to ponder the thought(since there wasn't much left to ponder). Soon Jessica was finished with her breakfast in bed. It wasn't the most original or the most elaborate Valentine's Day gift. But that wasn't the point. Jessica and Xander had long since past the point where they were trying to impress the other with lavish gifts and surprises. All that they required was each other's company. No words were needed to be spoken. Just lying in the other's arms was enough. Smelling them. Feeling them. Looking at them. That's all that mattered. 'I've got the water ready, with the bubbles and all, so if you'll join me.' Max said, as he stood holding out his hand for his wife to take. She lifted the tray from her lap and laid it on the bed. She then took Danger's hand into her own and got out of the bed. Max then lifted her up into his arms and carried her off into the bathroom, where a nice, hot bath full of bubbles awaited. Okay, so the silence and such was enough, but a hot bubble bath didn't hurt either.
