Getting laid - Badass Airborn Tyler

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“WOO! CALL ME MR.WEDNESDAY NIGHT, CALL ME THE SEX MACHINE, CALL ME AIRBORN TYLER! THE A STAR STABLE HAS ARRIVED!” Airborn Tyler flung open the door to his locker room, ran in and dived over the back of the sofa onto the pile of cushions like a little boy on Christmas morning. Closely behind was a slightly calmer ‘Daunting’ David Dew, arms crossed and looking totally serious as always. Last in was a sweaty ‘Luscious’ Lee Lightning, with a giant smile spread across his face. “First we preach to those ungrateful fans about democracy, and then David and I defeat Team Action! Man, we need to party!” Airborn lifted his head up onto the back of the sofa and leaned over. In fact, he leaned too far over and fell down the other side. Picking himself up off the floor, he dusted his knee cut off jeans down, and raised an eyebrow. Well, he tried to raise his eyebrow, he just ended up looking like a retard. “Lee, did you just say, party?” ‘Luscious’ Lee nodded, one side of his mouth still twisted. He then disappeared into the bathroom to get changed out of his ring gear, and ‘Daunting’ David Dew strode back out to the corridor to find a drinks machine. The camera focused in on Airborn Tyler, and more specifically his butt-ugly face. Slowly, his face organised itself into a heinous smile which would more than scare the scariest child in the land. “Well we all know what happens when Airborn Tyler goes to a party. In a freak of nature and a ‘Kodak’ moment, he transforms into the party animal they call ‘BADASS’ AIRBORN TYLER!” Airborn lifted his head to the sky and ran off camera, like someone out of a famous movie. Unlike the dude in the famous movie, he fell over, and out of shot someone ‘hmffed’. “Oww, my nose.” *** A very loud booming noise rocked the club, as ‘Luscious’ Lee Lightning and Airborn Tyler struggled through their hordes of fans to enjoy a night on the town. Okay, perhaps that part was in their imagination, but nonetheless there were a lot of people around. Strangely, they weren’t accompanied by Lee’s brother and Airborn’s cousin ‘Daunting’ David Dew. The powerhouse was back at the apartment, probably doing some weight training to warm down from The Elements’ match earlier. Lee had asked him if he wanted to come, but he only got a grunt in reply. ‘Daunting’ David was a man of very few words. Lee looked very sharp in a fine suit, which actually helped him live up to his nickname. He liked to think of himself as a ladies man, and in truth this wasn’t far off the truth. The second he got onto the dance floor and strutted his stuff he was surrounded by women, some attractive, some at the other end of the scale. Still, they were women, and it was more than Airborn got all night. “Man, I don’t get it,” moaned Airborn whilst swirling a straw around his alcoholic cocktail, “here I am, looking my best, yet no women want to get a piece of the A Star Student!” Lee paid only half attention, cagouling with a very fine blonde woman with a suspiciously short skirt. Airborn didn’t like it, and tapped him on the shoulder. “You listening to me?” “Sure,” replied Lee in a ‘I’m busy’ manner. His cousin didn’t take the hint. “I actually washed my hair today! For the first time in 3 months I washed my hair, so you’d think the chicks would be all over me like, like, like I was a very hot man!” But Airborn turned around, and Lee was gone. He caught a quick sight of him walking out the double doors with a woman on each arm. Sighing, Airborn asked the barman for another refill. “So much for becoming ‘Badass’ Airborn Tyler. Perhaps another transformation is needed, ‘I want to get drunk’ Airborn Tyler is born! HAHA!” However much Airborn drunk though, he couldn’t help but forget the words of God when they’d spoken a couple of weeks ago… God: Well, Airborn Tyler, I’m going to give you a challenge. By the end of October, I want you to have a girlfriend, and get laid.
