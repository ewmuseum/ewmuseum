Outhouse: II

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As it might be expected, Sanchis Guilherme hadn't exactly had the most pleasant of times since he was forced out of 21st Century Wrestling thanks to Kellen Kinkade. Instead of finding new work, Sanchis tried different things. First, he tried his hand at construction work, but after he had 'accidently' hit the foreman's thumb with a hammer... um... four times, the construction crew had determined that Sanchis wasn't cut out for construction, and they booted him out. Next, Sanchis joined a band, Three White Guys and A Mexican, and played drums. However, when the band attempted to play something other than Livin' La Vida Loca, he, of course, just simply randomly hit the drums. This actually gave the band a hardcore edge. That was, until Sanchis scared away all of the girls and accidently poked out the guitarist's eye when one of Sanchis' drumsticks flew right out of his hand. That's when the band kicked him out. His tenure lasted all of three weeks. After that, Sanchis tried his hand at stand up comedy. Most people couldn't understand just what in the hell Guilherme was saying, and some people booed him off the stage. He tried to verbally attack the audience like insult comics do, but this usually involved Sanchis getting frustrated and 'accidently' spilling stuff on people. He was, of course, kicked out of the bar he was working in after that one. Sanchis, desperate for money, tried his hand at being a male stripper. He was, of course, thrilled at this whenever the girls wanted to see him. Unfortunately, being Sanchis Guilherme, and being one of the more unattractive being on planet Earth, could only attract gay men. And Sanchis... he didn't like that at all. Often times, Sanchis will 'accidently' trip such a man and then 'accidently' kick them in the nuts while trying to 'help them up'. Sanchis did not last that long as a male stripper. It should be noted that Sanchis used this expierence to try and become the new Naked Cowboy of Dallas, playing guitar for random people. This, of course, backfired when he did something that the real Naked Cowboy didn't do, which was to actually BE naked. So, Sanchis was arrested as a result and spent two weeks in the big house and had to do twenty hours of community service. After that, Sanchis became a wanderer. He went from place to place, took the random odd job, and generally lived in a cardboard box. Once in a great while, he'll pull off a stunt like he did at the bar. Today was one of those days for Sanchis Guilherme, because a limo slowly pulled up next to him. 'Hey!' a voice shouted at Sanchis as the limo slowly pulled in next to the red-headed Mexican, 'Hey, you!' The window of the limo rolled down, revealing a brown-haired man. Though you couldn't see him, there was another man in the limo with him. Sanchis stopped and turned toward the man in the limo while continuing his walk, '`Ey! Are chu talking to de Sanchis?' 'I guess you could say that.' the man, Reed Young, said. He didn't know Sanchis Guilherme, nor did he know that underneath the appearance of a smelly, red-headed, Latino bum was an extremely talented wrestler. 'Would you like to do something for me? I might even pay you if you bring me the RATINGS!' Sanchis thought about it, 'Ehhh, de Sanchis, `e wants ta know how match chu would pay `em.' 'Um...' Reed said, not sure what to say, so he turned to the other person in the limo, 'Jeremy, help?' The other man poked his head out, 'Um, we'll, like, pay you... um. A lot?' Sanchis thought about it. For all of two seconds, 'Aha! De Sanchis, `e agrees to chore terms!' With that, the door of the limo opened and Reed Young adjusted his seating so that Sanchis would be free to take a seat, 'Then come on in. We'll explain on the way, and...' Reed said, before he paused to sniff the air, 'Hopefully, get you a shower. Sheesh.' The limo drove off into the night, headed for some place else in the town.
