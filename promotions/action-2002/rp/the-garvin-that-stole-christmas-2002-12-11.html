The 'Garvin' that Stole Christmas

11 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/11/02 “No talking! When I point to you, that is when you’re aloud to speak! It’s not rocket science!” Jeff Garvin sat watching one of his favourite prime time television shows, Judge Judy. It went to a commercial shortly after but not before giving the phone number and mailing address. Quickly, Jeff jotted down the digits that were displayed on screen and tucked the piece of scrap paper back in his jacket pocket. Two weeks ago on Pressure Point, Coral Avalon assaulted Jeff Garvin. He tore up his newly purchased lime green tuxedo with white stripes running down the front and back and then, if that weren’t enough, he ruined his top hat. Who the HELL ruins a top hat!? Just like that, an entire ensemble was trashed and he wasn’t about to let Coral get away with something as heinous as that. He stepped over the line and he messed with him one to many times. It’s time to take legal action. Jeff thought to himself, nodding in agreement. Let’s see, that’s destruction of property, aggravated assault- No! Attempted murder, yeah, even better. Mr. Avalon is a criminal that must be brought to justice. He shouldn’t be aloud to walk the streets where he may harm other magnificent pieces of clothing at will! And there’s only one lady that can bring him to justice… Judge freakin’ Judy. She’s the real law and order of public broadcasting, not that fool LLB. I’m sure that once she here’s the TERRIBLE things that Coral did to me she’ll have pity on me. She’ll understand, she has to… I’m going to sue that idiot for everything he has! Garvin sneered with glee tenting his fingers and cackling like Dr. Evil. He wasn’t exactly sure what Coral had to take but whatever it was, he wanted it. When everything was finished, he wanted Coral to be left with not even the shirt on his back or the shoes on his feet. His television, his dish washer, his napkins, the family cat, a bag of thumb tacks, left over insulin… ALL OF IT! He’d take the Christmas tree, too. The light bulbs and tinsel. Heck, he’d take the deep fryer and the log from his fire! He’d CLEAN out the deep freeze- have fun with the looting; taking the pie… …AND the plumb pudding. Oh, he’d take it ALL without a penny to spare! And leave Coral with only one thing. A Garvin-like glare. And if this weren’t enough, we’ll he’d take Allison too and watch as little Coral… went… Boo-Hoo.
