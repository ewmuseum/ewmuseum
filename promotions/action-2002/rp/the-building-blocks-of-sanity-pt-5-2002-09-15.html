The Building Blocks of Sanity pt. 5

Date: September 15th, 2002 August 21st, 2002 – Wednesday – 6:00 PM Let’s describe the seen a bit, shall we? A small, cosy room… pillows, white lacy curtains, and two people. One a beautiful, university graduate with a doctorate in psychology. The other? Jeff Garvin. Slicking back his eyebrows with saliva and checking to see how bad his BO is. In case you’re stuck on details, yes, it is indeed VERY bad. The door was only half opened as Jeff Garvin stopped in his tracks only a few feet from the entrance to ‘her’ office. He stuck his head under each arm pit and took a deep whiff. And choked. And gaged. And swallowed his own vomit. “Fuck. Oh well.” Jeff stepped in. “Well hello, I’m glad to finally meet you.” Claire Moore, his court appointed therapist, rose from her chair to shake Jeff’s clammy hand; drenched with sweat and his own lung butter from moments ago when he attempted to make a few adjustments to his hair. His heart skipped a beat. And instead of the usual pain Jeff was use to experiencing when such a thing took place, this time all he felt was utter happiness. She was even more beautiful than Jeff could have ever dreamed of; long silky blond hair, peaches and cream complexion, and a perfect form. Her face, don’t even get him started on her face; as soon as their eyes locked… they stayed locked. Gulp. “M-m-me too.” He replied, stumbling over his own words. Look, Jeff, boobies! Wait… He’s not looking… This isn’t right. There’s a perfect view of cleavage and Jeff didn’t even take a quick glance! Hell just froze over… And a generic football team that no one ever thought in million years could win the Super Bowl just did! Hey, I don’t watch much football. Sue me. “Please sit. Lets get started.” Claire turned and set back her timer and plunked herself down in her seat, smiling at Jeff as he fidgeted with his hair before at last taking a seat. “Jeff, I’m not going to lie to you, I’ve read over your file… several times… and I must admit, I’m worried about you.” The Original scratched the back of his head and responded, “I get that a lot… Things like… ‘Jeff your scaring me’, ‘Jeff stop touching me there’, ‘Jeff get out of my window’. It’s all in good fun! Really, I’m perfectly fine!” Claire’s eyes shot to her desk, knowing full well that getting Jeff to open up wouldn’t be as easy as many of her other clients. He was hiding behind layers upon layers of psychological baggage. To put it rather simply, Jeff was just too damn stubborn to let others know of his problems, his worries… His weaknesses. “I’ve been calling around the last couple hours, trying to gather whatever little background information on you that I can… From what I hear, you’re living on the streets because you refuse to make amends with your uncle?” “My uncles a fucking loser, he didn’t see anything in me. He didn’t believe in me… So he had to be left behind so that I could aspire to NEW heights!” Garvin gazed up, pointing towards the ceiling. Claire blinked. “You’re homeless. How is that aspiring to new heights?” “Don’t be so short sighted… Everything works out in the end. Two days ago I was sleeping on a bench in the middle of a park guarding pop cans with my life. Now, the Garvinator has a job interview! I’m on my way back to the TOP!” “May I say something?” “Sure.” “You seem to be solely focused on one thing… Professional Wrestling… And also-“ “Your damn straight, if wrestling had tits and a vagina I’d fuck her good and hard… And I’m not just saying that cause I’m a sexually frustrated, twenty three year old virgin either.” “Rrrrriiiight.” Claire paused and wrote the word ‘Pervert?’ down on her paper. “Don’t worry, I’ll delve into your infatuation with sexual intercourse later, but now… I just want to know, aside from your work, and… women… is there anything else you want in life? A home? A car? A family, maybe?” “Oh, you mean like a wife I get to fuck every night for free and a son that will fetch me things? Yeah… I could see that being pretty damn cool. Nothing better than free fuck and a midget butler!” Claire shook her head. Her reason? She was getting NOWHERE! “No Jeff… I mean a family that you can love and grow old with. I find it really sad that a person as dedicated as you has no goals in life other than what takes place in the ring… or in the bed room. I think that’s one of the reason’s you have no friends… You push them away with your one-track mind. That’s why I’m going to cut our session a little short today. Usually I don’t give ‘homework’ assignments, I’m a therapist not a teacher, but this is something I feel will do you a lot of good… Go out, buy some new clothes and then try and open up… Try and make ONE friend. That’s all. Just one. I’m even am to give you a bit of money to go shopping, money that Reed Young sent me in case I needed anything extra. I think all you need is to learn that there are more important things in life, like friendship.” Garvin tented his fingers and squinted. “A friend you say? I don’t need one of those! Friends cause emotion, emotion clouds the mind, clouding of the mind leads to losing focus of the big prize… I don’t know, I’m willing to do a lot of things, but letting some ‘stranger’ leach off my ‘coolness’ is something I’m a bit weary about… Unless…” An evil smile appeared. “How about if I do as you say, we have to… you know… Get down. Get busy. You know… S…E…” Claire cut him off. “No Jeff, I’m not going to have sexual relations with you. That is just wrong. But I will have dinner with you as friends… My treat?” … “Fine.” Jeff reluctantly accepted the deal. Dinner could always lead to OTHER things, right? Okay, maybe not… But still… FREE MEAL! He got up from his chair and headed directly to the exit. “Jeff… I think, under your gritty exterior, there’s a shy, loveable guy just waiting to be let free… A guy that any woman would want.” He stopped. And thought. “Even you?” Claire giggled, smiling as she waved. “Goodbye, Jeff.” “Bye.”
