Its 4:30am, know where your head is at?

4 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.04.02 His eyes blinked into an open state of confusion. Blurred visuals. Dripping water seemlessly dangling overhead. A blanket strewn about his body. Steering wheel. Trees. Glowing sky. Its morning again. He sits up, coughing out the cobwebs. The where am I's and why am I here's fading away with his sleep. The setting was simple. His car. Under some trees. Morning. Alone. But that doesn't get him down. A smile slowly plasters on his face. He opens the door, stumbling out of the car. Cool morning air. Its reassuring. Its not the desert of Las Vegas. Its not the sweat glazed stench of the wrestling ring. Its sustained comfort. Bobby Minio is twenty four years old, and he feels like he's lived his life. Usually, this day would be filled with bitter half backed jabber about his pains and trials of daily life. Not today. Not recently. He grins. He smiles. Every day feels closer to contentment a little more. Like the days when his bed wasn't empty, when he was on top of his game. He walked a few steps to find he was on a hilltop over looking plains of grass and trees. It felt right. It felt right at home. He walked back to the car, found a bottle of water and an apple, and walked to a tree by the hillside, sat down, and took a bite of the apple. Something began feeling out of order. Was the apple rotten? He checks. No. Warm water? He checks. Nope. --- Bobby Bobby, Bobby. Having fun? --- 'You again.' --- Things aren't right Bobby. What are you without the hate? Nothing. You're a pawn Bobby. You're a servant. --- 'You're a pain in the ass! Stop trying to kill the moments.' --- Its been weeks Bobby. You can't hide me forever. --- 'You're not hidden. You're defeated.' --- You can't defeat the perpetual feeling of defeat. Don't kid yourself Bobby. Your life is just a series of bad days. Take that back. Don't let woman take that from you. --- 'Woman gave it to YOU. It was bad days with woman that created you. It was my own courage that took you down. I'm happy now. I've got things. I've got People.' --- What people? --- 'Her. I've got her.' --- Oh do you? --- 'Its dark out. The sun is coming up. Can't I enjoy the moment and think to myself?' --- You are thinking to yourself. --- 'I'm talking to myself.' --- Tomato, Tomahto. --- 'I can't believe my concious is mocking me.' --- What am I here for? --- 'For hard descisions. For forks in the road. For regret.' --- ... If I had a face, i'd be rolling my eyes at you. --- 'I appriciate your concern for my love life, well, not really, but I have things under control. I'm sure she's into me. I'm not a bad looking guy. I'm smart, clever - ' --- Crazier then a shithouse rat... --- 'Get off my case! I'm not crazy. I just have no one to talk to. You're around. You're supposed to argue with me, but not mock me. Make things right. Not wrong. And I know you're wrong about her.' --- I think we're both wrong about her Bobby, reguardless, you're in for trouble, and where will I be when you come crawling back? At a bookie getting the cash I bet on you crashing down like a soviet jet liner. --- 'When I was... you know, angry, I didn't talk to you. We were one person, which is how we should be. So please...' Bobby bites into the apple, then speaks with his mouth full. 'Be GONE.' --- Bobby you're nailed it right on the head. We were one person back then. As we should be now. --- 'I refuse to give you an audience any longer.' He winged the apple off the hill, sipped the bottle of water, then poured it into his hand the wiped it across his face to hide the sweat. Was he really talking to himself and getting nervous? Bobby used to have a motto, back as a teenager, back when life was fun. 'Nothing makes sense until the morning after anyway.' It used to seem so true, now... Nothing makes sense... Ever. Bobby stood up walking to the car, then turned, looking at the breaching sun. 'Another day, another dollar.' He flashed a smile, then ducked into his car, starting the engine. Turning his back on this conversation that he was convinced didn't take place.
