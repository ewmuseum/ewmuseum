Invisibility - VIII

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Original: 07/24/2001. Rewrite: 04/14/2002 April 21st, 1993 (two hours later) The corner of Highly and Brown: Mesa, Arizona, United States The hour of the clock was a peace sign. 12:50 AM. The road of Highly in Mesa was not presently occupied, save for one car, an old black Tempo that quietly drove down the city streets. The occupants were Joey Malone, and Keri Lindum. They had just gotten changed out of their prom clothes, and were now headed for a post-prom party at the house of a friend, Craig MacFadden. Craig wasn't an especially bright fellow, and he was borderline from failing all of his classes in high school. But where he failed in school, he made up for by hosting some of the most killer parties in all of Mesa, Arizona. One such party featured Daniel Phillips vomiting up a goldfish on a bet, and the fish ended up all over Tabitha Stevens' new dress, along with whatever Daniel had eaten that day. Another party featured Gabriel Landers getting so drunk that he climbed up to the roof of Craig's garage and jumping onto an unsuspecting Andrew Cool. That incident broke Andrew's leg. Though it was funny at the time, since nobody really liked Andrew, Gabriel wasn't laughing after his family was given a lawsuit from the Cool family. Bonham and Keri were in the backseat of Joey's car. 'This really isn't the best place to watch this memory.' Bonham said. Keri wasn't sure what was going on, so she just watched. The car stopped at a stoplight, on the corner of Highly and Brown. 'What do you suppose Craig's gonna do, this time?' Joey asked, putting his turn signal to head left on Brown. 'Wait for one of us to get drunk.' Keri suggested. 'That's usually when the 'fun' begins.' she added, doing quotation marks with her fingers as she said 'fun'. 'You may be right.' Joey said. As he said it, something caught his eye on his right side. It was moving fast, and as he turned toward it, he realized what it was. A vehicle that had careened off of the road, onto the sidewalk, straight toward them. 'Keri!' he yelled, pulling her away from the right side of the vehicle, which was where the car was headed. He attempted to protect her as much as possible, but both of their seatbelts restricted such an action. Both Keris, one in the memory, as well as the adult one, screamed out as the car impacted, shattering glass everywhere and twisting the car to one side. The cars moved and screeched toward the center of the intersection before they finally came to a rest directly in the center. The memory went blank. 'Wh-what happened?' Keri asked, concerning the blankage. 'Well, both of you had been knocked out from the crash, so it's impossible to show what had happened directly afterwards. I'll go to the part where Joey came to.' Ten minutes later... Joey Malone came to, with him barely hanging out of the driver's side of the door. His left arm had gone through the glass, and it was cut rather badly. His right arm had been broken when Keri's head slammed into it, which caused his arm to hit the steering wheel. Joey did not care about himself at this moment. 'Keri?!' he called out, noticing that she wasn't in the car. He cradled his broken arm with his left, as he stumbled around the wreckage. The driver of the other car was missing, possibly to dial 911 or something. And that's when he saw her. Laid out in front of Joey's car, a cut on her head, several injuries to her stomach, and possibly a broken leg. She looked like she took the worst of the accident, despite Joey's attempt to protect her. Joey did the only thing he could do as he cradled her head in his arms. He cried.
