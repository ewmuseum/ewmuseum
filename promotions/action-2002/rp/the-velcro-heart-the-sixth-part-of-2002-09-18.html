Action! — Tales

The Velcro Heart, The Sixth Part of

18 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To the Arena he went, as the Chevy Blazer sped down the highway with great speed. The week had passed... but it really couldn’t have gone by any slower. Such long days... by yourself. With yourself. Being ‘The Law’, and the current Action! Champion... everyone wanted a shot at you. Face, heel... undecided... it didn’t really matter. If you had something someone else wanted, it all went out the window. And this business would prove truth in that statement time and time again. In the past LLB was a likable guy though... regardless of his PIW Titles or not. It felt as if there... he always had some friends to hold onto... to pass the slow days by. Chris Fear. Chris Fox. Sean Cylear. MUD. To even Jim Johnson himself... The Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling brought him a family... and one that LLB didn’t even realize... Until now. For every time he’d enter through the Arena doors on Wednesdays... he saw the looks, the glances... It was as though the respect had been lost. For maybe those kids in the bar were right... that being the best friend to the Owner seemed like the cheap and easy road to take. Let alone he was the Champion. Flashback to bar scene: “Man that Christopher Fox sure can wrestle.” “So unlike that one guy... um, whomever Fox used to fight.” “Steven Denton?” “No. He’s good.” “LLB?” “Yeah... that’s the one.” “Yep. I hear ya.” “I mean... he’s best friends with the Owner... AND he’s the Action! Champion. Like, wow, I didn’t see that one coming.” “No kidding.” “Did you see his last battle with KroW on Pay-Per-View.” “Yeah. ‘I’m number one... but why am I number one!?’. News flash, idiot, you’re number one because you cheat... and you’re friends with the worst... Owner... EVER!” “What ever happened to the good old LLB? The one in the Asylum... the one in PIW...” “I don’t know. He didn’t have what ‘it’ took anymore?” For if they saw it that way... maybe... just maybe, the others did too. But in the heart of the young lawyer, he knew he had what ‘it’ still took. He knew he had the talent... and deep down, regardless, he knew he’d have his Championship Title Belt... sooner or later. Maybe it wasn’t meant to be at ‘Juggernaut’... but ‘The Law’ knew it was only a matter of time, without Reed’s help. Sighing, there really wasn’t much LLB could do now. He just drove on, in hopes of setting the record straight for whenever he’d fight next. Surely not this week... he was beaten and battered already... but when the time would come... he’d be there. And win it on his own. Suddenly, LLB spotted a yellow beetle down the far ends of the highway road, on the shoulder. He squinted his eyes together... nearly coming over to the middle lane and smacking the car beside him. Luck for him, though, that didn’t happen. “That’s...” He didn’t have to finish. It was Christopher Fox... pulled over, as smoke came rapidly from his engine. “Pft. Fuck him.” Yet, within a blink of the eye... LLB had pulled over in front of the Pikachu Beetle... and exited his own car. “JESUS CHRIST ROLAND... WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU DOING!?!?” LLB thought to himself as he tried to get back in his car and drive away... he really did... but some force... it was taking him right to Chris Fox! Fox rested his head against his hand, as his arm was down upon the top of his roof. “LEAVE ROLAND... LEAVE!! HE HASN’T SEEN YOU YET!” “Hey.” Fox looked up... He almost fell over. “Need a lift?” “LL!” Christopher ran to hug him... but ‘The Law’ just held his hand out in front. “No hugs. No kisses. No talking.” The Champ pointed to his car. “Need a lift?” Fox nodded excitedly. “Enter.” The two walked into the car, as LLB slammed his door shut and began to make his way back onto the highway. “I called the two-trick.” Fox said, though LLB knew what he meant. “They said they’d be coming by to take it away shortly.” LLB nodded. ... ... Silence. He changed to the middle lane. ... ... He changed to the far lane. ... ... Silence just kept on playing throughout the entire car... as a very faint sound of music could be heard from the radio. Yes, even LLB himself wasn’t for music this night. Fox jumped forward, turning it up. It was “Brother Down” by Sam Roberts. “YAY!!” He clapped, as he looked over at LLB... whom just turned off the radio. “Mine. Do not touch.” Christopher didn’t seem to understand. He just sucked his thumb. “Mine.” LLB pointed. “Do. Not. Touch.” Fox nodded excitedly. LLB sighed. “Let’s sing ourselves then, otay LL?” Making sure the coast was clear, LLB took his eyes from in front of him and looked over at Fox, shooting him the rudest glance anyone could give. “Otay... I start. The wheels on the bus go round and round... round and round... round-” Another rude glance. “Oh, right... sorry LL... this ant a bus.” After a giggle, Fox cued up a new song. “One hundred bottles of beer on the wall... one hundred bottles of beer...” Rude glace: number three. It did nothing. “Take one down... pass it around... NINETY NINE BOTTLES OF BEER ON THE WALL!!” It was going to be a long, long... hour. And he thought the week went by slow.
