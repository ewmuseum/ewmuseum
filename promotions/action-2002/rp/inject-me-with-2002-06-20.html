Action! — Tales

Inject me with...

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.20.02 Steven Denton sat on the ground, cross-legged, leaning against the foot of his hotel room bed. The television was tuned to some random infomercial. This one featured Bill Goldberg trying to sell some crappy gym equipment. 'You could be next,' he says... how cute. My how the might have fallen. Steven compared Goldberg and this commercial to his life. When he first wound up in jOlt, he had everything. He was making money, getting exposure, and exploiting his incredible in-ring work, which was that of a technical genius. He had nothing to lose and everything to gain. Things actually looked up, for once in his life. Steven enjoyed life when things looked up. Then he had fallen. He left jOlt, which drew a bit of media attention, but soon his hype had died down. Soon he was nothing, again. So he returned to jOlt, only to find it to be a haven of bad booking and pour ownership. He fled once more, still looking for a stable, well run organization that he could grow and prosper in. Finally, Action! offered him a spot on their roster. A step in the right direction? Yes. But he still pondered if he would only end up like Goldberg: selling shit gym equipment to fat kids who actually think they might be next. That is not where he wanted to be. There is no way he will let himself fall that far. In Action!, he once again has everything to gain. Respect. Exposure. Championships. And he had nothing to lose. No image vital to his career that he must uphold. He's set no standard, and in Action!, he only has another opportunity to set that standard. Steven smiled. After the start he had gotten, destroying Fred Franks in his final match with the company, letting him and everyone else know that he was a force to be reckoned with. Franks' career here ended at the same moment Steven's began. His second step now, was to really prove himself. He would go out there at Pressure Point, and he would do what he does best: Wrestle. Steven clicked the television off, shutting Goldberg off from his thoughts. He climbed upwards and into his bed, rubbing a hand over his buzzed head. He stared upward, still with a look of hope and eagerness in his eyes. 'I'll have my night. I'll have my time. I'll get my piece of the action (no pun intended). And I'll show everyone who doubts me just why I got a job here. I'll dominate. I'll destroy. I'll ruin. I'll run amuck through a federation without stable ownership. I'm a renegade in a sea of conformity. I'll never be afraid to let go of what's normal and do what no one expects. I will lead. I will win.' Steven shut his eyes.
