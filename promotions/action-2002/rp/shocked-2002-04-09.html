Shocked

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 9, 2002 Chris Fear is asleep in a hotel bed. The room is much nicer than most hotel rooms you see. It has large paintings above the oversized king bed. Expensive looking oak furniture is spread neatly throughout the room. A comfortable looking chair, and a table sit beside the window. The curtains are open and the bright morning sunlight is shining through. A terrace complete with its own chairs and a small table sit just outside the window. Meanwhile, back inside the room the television blares. Chris has obviously left it on from the night before. It is tuned to Fox News, and the news is blaring throughout the room. Suddenly Fear jolts up in bed. He does not look fully awake, but he manages to stagger out of bed and into the chair by the window to turn the television down. He places the TV remote back down on the table, and takes a seat. 'And to follow up on a previous story, the investigation into the death of a professional wrestling is still ongoing.' When Chris hears this he immediately looks wide awake. He also looks somewhat surprised that he did not already hear this, after all, they are calling it a previous story. 'At this point we would like to take you back to our original coverage of the tragic incident,' the reporter says solemnly. 'Earlier today, two gun shots were heard in the south suburbs of Chicago sending Homer Township, a small town, into a frenzy. Local authorities were called. After investigation, billionaire Johnathan Daniel Phantom as well as his girlfriend, Jessica Jane Craven's bodies were found each with a single shot to the skull. Johnathan Daniel Phantom also known as Johnny Phantom, a popular professional wrestler and corporation owner of Phantom Enterprises was pronounced dead only moments ago. Currently, Phantom's attorneys and insurance agents are looking into his will, as there is a company's ownership at stake as well billions of personal dollars held by Mr. Phantom. More updates as we receive them, on this story.'* As soon Chris hears this his eyes begin to water, and he slowly shakes his head. A man Chris has worked for, and known as a friend has aparently been killed. He is obviously dead. Chris buries his face in his hands for a few seconds before jumping up in a fit of rage and punching the television set, shattering the screen. He then picks up his travel bag and throws it at the wall, knocking down one of the pictures. Quickly Chris picks up his bag and throws on a clean t-shirt and jeans, before he runs out of the room, bag in hand. 'Son of...' Chris can't even finish the sentence. He slows his pace and leans up against the elevator door. 'I can't believe he's gone. I'm no stranger to death, but I was closer to Johnny and Jessica than I was with my own family. This one's definately harder to take...' Fear's voice trails off. Chris pushes the down button on the elevator and steps inside. When the doors reopen, he slowly walks to the reception desk, and buzzes for a clerk. 'Just put my room damages on Reed Young's bill. He'll take care of it,' Chris says. 'Yes, sir,' the clerk quickly replys in a surprised voice. Eventually Chris makes hsi way outside of the hotel and to his blue Honda Civic rental car. He throws his bag in the trunk and climbs in the drivers seat. 'At least from now on I know something like that won't happen to me. Not after 'Killer', 'The Kid', and 'The Law' united. To get to one of us, you'll have to go through two others.' Chris pulls out of the parking lot and speeds off into the distance, still in shock over what he has heard on the newscast. * News coverage courtesy wt-online.com
