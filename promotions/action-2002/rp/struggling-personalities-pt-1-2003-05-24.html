Struggling Personalities (pt. 1)

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-24-03 “You really are weak, you know that don’t you?” A deep hate filled voice flowed through shades of darkness until it struck upon the ear of one Jay Edward Sylo. He stood outside the shadows inside a dim light that protected him like a holy barrier. “Who are you,” He growled back at the voice but the answer did not come. Only the sound of a deep and dark laughter hissed back. Sylo growled and clinched his fist once more. He could feel his old hatred brewing inside him but he wouldn’t let it go…no…never again. “Who am I?” The voice hissed. “Who am I?” It repeated in a tone that sounded offended. “Jay, you should know me very well. But who I am or why I’m here isn’t important. The reason I’m here, if you’re wondering, is to see what kind of pathetic being you’ve become. You had it all you know…the fame…the glory…even respect but you threw it all away after you killed some little nobody,” The voice hit home with that and Sylo leapt forward but the shadows seemed to reject him and throw him back. The voice found this amusing as his hiss of laughter echoed through Sylo’s soul once more. Sylo quickly picked himself up to his feet, fist still clinched and bellowed out, “Fuck you!” It was only two words, but it was the only two words he could think of. Sylo knew he had it all and then lost it…but he killed a man and it didn’t matter who he was because to someone he mattered. “Tsk, tsk, tsk, Such language. Didn’t your mother ever tell you it isn’t nice to swear?” That was the straw that broke the camels back. “I’LL KILL YOU!” Sylo roared after hearing what the shadowy stranger had said. It was no hidden fact that Sylo’s parents had died at an early age and anyone who talked ill of them or even hinted at it, became an enemy. “YOU WON’T DO ANYTHING TO ME!” The voice shouted back with force. “DO YOU NOT GET IT YOU FOOL!?” “Step forward you coward!” Sylo roared out trying to leap forward once again, but once again the shadows knocked him back. “Now that’s the Sylo I remember,” The voice sounded euphoric now, like Sylo’s rage was a drug, a drink, or even guilty little pleasure. Whatever it was, it brought a feeling of great happiness to the shadows. “Do you not understand? What you had still lives deep down inside your soul. You may run but you will NEVER escape who and what you are. You are a freak, inhuman, cold, and uncaring. You will never be anything more than that. You are a machine designed by God himself to wipe out everyone you come in contact with. Your human side died long ago and this sad existence you call a life now is nothing more than pitiful,” Sylo’s anger had grown, he began to shake violently and his fists were tight enough to crush stones. Everything this voice had said…was true but Sylo couldn’t face it, he wouldn’t face it. He wasn’t that person anymore and he wasn’t ever going to be again. The light around Sylo clicked off and soon his eyes were forced shut. Images of pain and suffering ran through his mind over and over. Tears began to form and the beast fell to his hands and knees. “Stop…this,” He cried out, his whole body shaking in a seizure type way. Everything stopped and everything became quite…just then a light came on and standing in it was a large man. He was covered in tattoos; he wore no shirt, and wore baggy pants. His head was still hidden in the confines of the shadows though. Sylo slowly stood up, his body trembling, ready to break down at any moment. “You need me,” Whispered the figure. “You need us,” And with that he stepped into the light. Sylo looked in shock as he saw himself…except with blue hair and a devilish grin. “You can’t win without me” And with that Sylo arose from his dream, drenched in sweat. “I will never go back,” He whispered.
