Action! — Tales

Topsy turvy diary

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th September 2002 Dear Diary… I must confess that at the moment I’m going through an identification crisis. When I walked into the supermarket though, I kind of, well, I don’t know how to say this. I looked at a women’s chest. I know, I’m a sinner. Mummy always told me that women are people as well, they have emotions, they are there to respected and not stared at. Was I staring? I don’t know, I’m confused. I’ve never felt these feelings before. Not recently away. When I was 15 I fancied a girl, and it was kind of cool actually. She didn’t like me back, but that was my first ‘boy likes girl’ experience, but I’ve never found anyone else since. I didn’t fancy this woman of course, she just had a nice chest. WHAT AM I SAYING? I’m speaking about this poor woman like she is an object! I wouldn’t have an arm to write this with if she’d known, she’d have probably given me an arm wringer from hell, followed up by a spinebuster through the conveyer belt. Heh, wait a sec, not everyone’s a wrestler right? Either way, these feelings aren’t the nicest. They’re confusing, and the worst part is that I’m actually starting to like women. I’m Airborn Tyler, I’m not supposed to like anyone, and damnit no one is supposed to like me. So moving on from women, and onto the being liked theme, why am I being cheered by the fans? I mean, I say something, and the whole crowd says it with me. I have a catchphrase, I HAVE A F###ING CATCHPHRASE! I was hired as jobber, I’ve not won a match for two months, yet I’m more popular than half the roster. You know what, I think the cheers I get when I come to the ring are as loud as KroW, as Chris Fox, as Bobby bloomin’ Minio. I listened to my cheer with the volume on the TV full blast, and it was a sound to behold. It’s a pity that by the time I got to Chris Fox the neighbours had made me turn down the volume, but my cheers were louder than his! I think they were cheers anyway. In this modern world, language confuses me. The language totally confuses me. I always used to think ‘shit’ was a word described to use something bad, something so bad it stunk. But you know what? I stood in the shopping centre in England a few months ago, and in the shop display window was a new episode of ‘Winnie the Pooh’, and lets face it, who doesn’t like Winnie the Pooh? That rotund bear has it all! Anyway, a posse of ten years old walked by (the type I used to lead yeah? Everyone said I was jonah, but secretly I had thousands of people walking behind me. By the time I arrived at the train station the place was packed!), and as they saw the Pooh Bear, one of them shouted out ‘YOU DA SHITS WINNIE!’. That big orange teddy bear was positively beaming at that. Come to think of it, teddy bears never stop smiling, but if that phrase was bad it would’ve frowned right? Anyway, back onto weird language. When I’m in the ring and someone shouts ‘YOU SUCK AIRBORN’, does that mean they’re my number 1 fan? I mean, shit is a bad word, but now people use it in a complimentary fashion, so if someone tells me I suck is that a good thing? And if racism is considered the worst insult around, if I shout at a bunch of ten blacks in Harlem and call them a bunch of ‘nigger assholes’, will they proclaim me as the second God and hug me? Language is confusing. Everything is topsy turvy these days. Finally for now diary (it is getting late and I need to eat my carrot before bed, it’ll help my ingrown toe nail, although I hear it’ll make my eye sight worse if I eat too much), I have to confess, I’m a mental mess. I mean, how am I going to handle Action! Man on Pressure Point? His top notch offence of continuous left and right fists might be too much to handle! I got knocked out of the Global Turnbuckle Tournament on Saturday. Yeah, I know, it’s a travesty. In my dreams, I dreamt that the whole nation was pulling for me. Well, the previous dream I had was that JFK would get assassinated, and we all know what happened in the 20th century! However, Donny Diamond decided to be a coward and not let me pin him, so we fought to the time limit. It went to the judges, and you know what they went and did? The assholes only went and voted for Donny?!! So I was eliminated. As I walked up the ramp they were chanting “YOU SUCK!” at me, so at least my dream of everyone being behind me was right. Man, I’m bushwhacked now. You know how long it took me type this? Thirty three minutes. Thirty three f###ing minutes! And you know, it couldn’t possibly be my fault. The keyboard, yeah, it’s very slow. VERY slow. Goodnight diary, sleep tight, don’t let the night bugs bite. I think that’s the phrase.
