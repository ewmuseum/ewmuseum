Heading Home.. A Mess, But A Champion

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/27/02 The Referee was still groggy, slightly, and wasn't watching Denton's arms. LLB reached up and was able to pry Denton's hands from the ropes. He had a look of surprise and 'holy shit!' on his face. Ramsey hit the dragon screw! Ump: ACHILLES TENDON HOLD!!!!! Shaw: I can't watch. Ump: TAP!! TAP!! TAP!! Shaw: NOOOOOO! Ump: NEW CHAMPION! CONRAD RAMSEY IS THE NEW BANTAM CHAMPION! The crowd went ballistic. They were jumping off the walls. LLB had headed back to the back. Conrad Ramsey hadn't seen him. The Referee hadn't seen him. Steven Denton and Reed Young hadn't seen him. He got past Young before he even got up. In the ring, Conrad Ramsey, a bloody mess, was handed the equally bloody Bantam Title belt. Ump: Oh my god! What a moment! What a match! Steven Denton has lost his title to Conrad Ramsey! Awesome! ****************************** It had been a moment. Conrad Ramsey's greatest moment ever. He was happier than he had ever been in his life. He was the new Bantam Champion. Champion. It was a great honor. Even Max Danger thought so. Of course, he had lost his belt, his Action! belt anyway, and also had to see Conrad winning one for himself. Didn't set well with 'The Danger Man' at all. But Conrad didn't care. Why should he? It was his moment. His time to shine. He proved, to everyone, that it didn't matter how old you were. All that mattered was the size of your heart, how determined you were, and just how much you wanted it. He wanted it. He was determined. He had waited for that one moment, that one time to show what he had. Reed Young had provided that opportunity. He had signed him, despite his age, and let him do the one thing he wanted to do... wrestle. He had a lot of thanks to give Reed and had to give him some credit. Despite all the idiotic things he's done. He still gave Ramsey a chance. And he took advantage of that opportunity. Bantam Champion. Even now, driving through Leitchfield, Kentucky passing Burger King, McDonald's, Dairy Queen and many other restuarants, a bloody mess and aching all over, he was smiling. Stitches in his forehead thanks to Arcayin, but still smiling. His knee killing him and feeling as though it could fall off at any moment... smiling. Ribs, brusied and sore, pain was great... still a smile on his face. It didn't matter his condition. He was a champion, and he was proud of that. He had finally arrived at his home. He parked the car in the driveway. Another vehicle was there. Wasn't anything he had seen before. It was a SUV. He didn't pay it much attention. He just gathered his bags and his belt and headed up the walk, up the steps, and to the front door. He opened up. He could smell the food. Jessica was back. He knew it instantly. The spagetti smell filled the house, and it was intoxicating. He entered, putting his bags down in the hallway, and headed into the living room. Blast rose up, and smiled his big yellow-teethed smile at the old man. Ramsey just gave a small wave and turned, facing the kitchen, looking over the bar/counter top. Jessica was there, setting out the plates. She looked up, and smiled. 'CONRAD!' she screamed out. It had scared Blast, causing him to spit out his Sunkist(or rather Danger's). She ran over to the old man and hugged him. He hugged back. It was good to be home.
