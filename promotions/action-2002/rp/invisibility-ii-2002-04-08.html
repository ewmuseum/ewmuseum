Invisibility - II

8 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Original: 07/21/01. Rewrite: 04/08/2002 September 5th, 1989 Mesa High School: Mesa, Arizona, United States We all have nightmares of school. Somewhere, some time during our meaningless hours in the accursed halls of a school, something horribly embarassing happens to you. It's a known fact that your mistakes are much more easilly remembered than your accomplishments. We all wonder what would happen if something had never had happened. Like that day the school bully gave you a wedgie, or that day a seven-foot-tall, retarded, freak-of-nature, black girl beats the shit out of you. For Joey Malone, this day was pretty bad. Joey hadn't even started to really grow at the age of fourteen. He was rather short at this point, and that left him rather vulnerable to the bullies. Biology class on this day was the breaking point. Keri and Bonham stood in a neglected corner of the room. Everything seemed to be on pause, all motion stopped, as if the memory was on pause. 'I might as well warn you, Keri. Since these are just memories, it's fairly obvious that you can't manipulate anything. Henceforth, to everyone in these memories, we are invisible. We are merely the observers, and nothing more.' Bonham said. Almost as soon as he said it did the action within the memory began. It seemed that since the biology teacher was out of the room for whatever reason, there was basically utter chaos amongst the freshmen in the room. Paper airplanes were flying about and the noise level was rather deafening. As this went on, two boys approached another, familiar-looking one. Keri recognized him. 'Hey, isn't that...?' 'Yes, that's Joey.' The two boys approached Joey and grabbed him by his shirt, pulling him to his feet, and shoving him against the wall. Since the rest of the freshmen were busy being random, they didn't see this. 'Alright, Joey. It's time to give up your money for today!' the short, fat one said. 'I... don't have any money for you, today.' Joey said, but this just made the tall one punch him in the stomach, doubling him over. 'Bullshit, kid!' the tall one said. As most would know, most kids at this age curse whenever there's no authority figures around to punish them. 'You have money! Cough it up!' 'But...' Wham. A hard fist to Joey's forehead knocks him into the wall and very nearly knocked him out. This got the attention of the other freshmen, but before they could crowd around and chant 'fight, fight, fight', the teacher came back. At the worst possible time. Because Joey decided to use the distraction to his advantage. He had gripped a broom and swung it at the tall one. *CRACK!* The broom broke upon impact and the tall bully fell to the ground in a heap. And, of course, the teacher saw this. 'Joey Malone! What do you think you're doing!' Joey wasn't even sure what he did, himself. He looked up at the teacher, before the broken broom handle that he was holding slipped out of his hand and fell to the floor. 'Gah! What the heck hit me!?' the tall bully asked. Notice how his speech changed as soon as the teacher had shown up. It didn't take the teacher very long to make a decision. 'Joey, it's time you went to see the principal!' Keri looked on as this occured, in shock. 'Isn't he going to do anything about those two guys that were picking on him?' 'Nope. That tall kid happened to be the principal's nephew. At the most, he would've gotten a slap on the wrist. Joey, on the other hand... well, this was when he met the guy that ended up changing him around.' Bonham said, before following Joey on his long trek to the principal's office. He turned back to Keri. 'Follow me. You'll see.' Well, what else could she do but follow?
