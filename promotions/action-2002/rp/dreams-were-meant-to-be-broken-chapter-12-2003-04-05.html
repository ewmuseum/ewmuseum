Dreams were meant to be broken: Chapter 12

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He pulled two devices out of his pocket. To elongated devices with a hole in the top and a button on the side. Enrique and Paul were intrigued about these devices. Fiend smiled as he held them out. 'This is the shit,' he admits to the pair. They stared at it in awe, not knowing what it was or how it worked. 'What happens is I press these buttons on the side and you sniff, it shoots the powder up further, nearly direct contact with the brain,' he explains to them. They both let out an impressed 'wow'. These guys were really quite dumb. 'Really?' asked Enrique. He tried to reach for the device, but Fiend held his hand back away from the dirty mitts of Luscious' henchmen. He had them, they were dumb enough to believe him, so they would learn for their stupidity. They would learn. Class was in. 'Sure, why not?' Fiend answered. 'You gotta work it for us,' Paul demanded. 'It looks pretty tricky.' Fiend nodded. It was tricky. It was a complete and utter trick that would ensue their demise, but they wanted just another hit. They wanted to do their best Keith Richards impersonations and Fiend would assist. He would make them higher than Joe Cocker. The floor was dry. It was not red. It was time for Fiend to comfort himself in red. They both stood, guiding the device in Fiend's hand to their nostril. He looked at them both, they seemed as ready as they could possibly be. 'Say 'Hi' to God for me, gentlemen. Tell him Fiend sent ya,' he smuggly told them. Their eyes widened as the recognised the mythological name with the mythological dead eyes, but it was too late. He had pressed the buttons.
