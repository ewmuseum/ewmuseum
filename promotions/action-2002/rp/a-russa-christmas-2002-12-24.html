A Russa Christmas

24 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.24.02 Russa walked through a mall all by himself. It was Christmas Eve and with Andrea not making the trip for the Christmas holidays, there was nothing better for him to do. He wondered what he would get for Christmas when he flew back home tomorrow. Being a nice guy and giving presents to his best friend and tag team partner, Lactose The Intolerant and A! champion Joey Malone. Surely there would be tons of gifts waiting for him when he got there. Than it suddenly sunk in. Russa didn't tell anyone he was going home. He would have to wait until Players and Pawns to get his presents. Life always seemed like it was out to get him. Russa came up with a great plan to help cheer him up. He walked around the mall until he found a huge map of the mall. He walked up to it and studied it carefully, of course rubbing his chin because not only did it make him look smarter, but he came up with some of his best ideas while doing it. 'You are here.' He read aloud, 'now where am I going to find a store that will carry the new 'tose t-shirt?' He scanned the entire map until he found the perfect place. 'Here it is, Al's Pro Wrestling!' He hurried throughout the mall until he found exactly what he was looking for. Al's Pro Wrestling was one of the smaller stores in the mall, but it was completely full of wrestling t-shirts. He stepped inside and looked for the t-shirts that seemed familiar. He came across shirts for Action!, LLB, Joey Malone, and even Jeremy Bold's homemade 'B' t-shirt. Unfortuneatley there was no 'tose, Russa, or LoD 2k3 shirts in the building. 'Excuse me,' Russa pouted trying to get someone to come over and help him, 'I can't seem to find a specific shirt. Can you help me?' A man from behind the counter looked up from behind the magazine he was reading and sighed. He dropped his magazine and reluctantly made his way over to Russa who looked rather concerned. 'Yeah? How can I help you?' The voice didn't seem to care. Russa hated it when the person he was talking to didn't care. His biggest pet peeve in the world. He shook his head as he looked down at the man trying to help him. 'Yeah, I was looking for the latest Lactose The Intolerant shirt but can't seem to find any 'tose shirts at all. I really think we have a problem here.' 'To make things simple for you, nobody cares about Lactose The Intolerant or the damn LoD either. Dairy is out now.' Blink. 'You know what? I'm just going to take this shirt right here and leave.' Russa grabbed the 'B' t-shirt and stormed off, leaving the store in a hurry. 'Good.' The man screamed, 'No one likes that shirt anyways.'
