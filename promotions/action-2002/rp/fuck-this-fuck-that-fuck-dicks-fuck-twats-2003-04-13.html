Fuck This, Fuck That; FUCK DICKS, FUCK TWATS~!

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On April 7th, 2003, Jeff Garvin drove to Pressure Point in his shitty care, with a shitty casset player. All night, while not out beating the royal fuck out of little dip shits who think they don’t owe anyone anything, Garvin was jotting down random notes on what he thought was generally wrong with everything. 1. Coral Avalon sucks. 2. Max Danger sucks. 3. LLB sucks. 4. Chris Fox sucks. 5. Cylear sucks. 6. KroW sucks. And all those new guys suck too. Let’s just cut to the chase, the whole fucking roster, minus himself and Zimmerman, suck. Fuck, Garvin couldn’t believe how fucking pissed he was with his whole situation. His back fucking hurt. His hip fucking hurt. His neck fucking hurt. He was generally a bitter dick to be around, especially after the whole thing when Chambers-thingy ma’jig came about. There’s another one that sucks, too. That ungratefull fucking shit. Fuck him. Christ, fuck, fuck fuck fuck… FUCK THEM ALL. He didn’t know why he was so pissed. He just was. Action! sucked. He was better than it. It was now… uh… whenever it was. Maybe Thursday? Garvin stood outside Pizza Hut, the cold being a prick and making his face ache. “I wish Reed would get shot in the head, that way I could fuckin’ get out of this dump.” Jeff sighed. Maybe he’d just not show up for the next show. Or maybe he would, he’d didn’t know. Heh. Shrug’ity shrug. It’d be grand, walking in the week after like nothing happened. What a bunch of shmucks.
