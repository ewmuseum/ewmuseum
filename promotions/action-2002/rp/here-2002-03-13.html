Date: 03/13/02 Wrong. All of them. And they got away with it. It may have been old by now, but in a way it was still brand new, because no one saw it, or at least they didn’t comment on the matter. Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling LLB put his trust into... it died. jOlt Wrestling LLB put his trust into... its Ownership died. Sure, the testifies could become annoyingly long, stressful, a real shot to the head, but deep down inside Roland still enjoyed them. It was fun. Hell, that’s what he was composed to do after all. However LLB didn’t call the end to either of those places. They had called the end on him. And maybe, just maybe they had the authority in that department, but LLB still should’ve been let in on it. He *was* friends with Johnson and Lee. Friends don’t do that. LLB could’ve wandered onto another Wrestling Federation quickly, but he didn’t. Due to the past he waited. He waited for Reed Young. His long-time childhood friend. For no matter what they said about Young and his “pathetic” ways, ‘The Law’ himself stood on his feet. There would be no escape even if Reed wanted one. The facts were, regardless, that Young and LLB were just too close. Action Wrestling: The new Wrestling Court LLB would work at, having all the time in the world. “I promise you,” Young said, on the other end of the cell phone conversation between him and LLB, “I promise you the top.” “I know.” ‘The Period’ replied in his demanding type of voice. “I know.” Clicking off the cell, LLB turned to the window of his Hotel Room. “This’ll work out after all. No matter what.” He stated. “The past is gone, and it doesn’t count. None of you favourites’ can get to me now.” ‘Law’ laughed, as even the faintest of little smiles crossed his cold rotten face. For now he was resting in the Wrestling world. He was resting without a worry at all. Because this time he could play the favourite. And he’d get away with it.
