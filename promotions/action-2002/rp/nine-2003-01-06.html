Action! — Tales

Nine.

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/06/03 January 10th, 2000 PIW Monday Massacre One week before WAR PPV Another Monday had come, the first Monday since Countdown to Insanity had played host to perhaps the most savage thing ever to be seen on PIW television, the end of former champion, LLB. Cylear skipped the Friday show and flew down with Jim Johnson to celebrate in style at his summer house in Florida. IT was there they slowly let the memory of their enemy slip away with every glass. Now PIW had a fresher, slighly more sadistic champion then ever. As the arena doors opened and Team Johnson enetred, with Sean leading the way, all eyes were on him. They were disgusted to even be in his presence. He scowled, holding his world championship belt closer to his chest as they marched through the halls demanding respect. “Here comes the champ, baby!” Jim announced to all, as he walked beside his ‘money maker’, pointing and yelling with pride. All Sean could do was smile and pat his precious belt, the one thing that meant so much to him that he could possibly end the life of a wrestling icon. James Jack, the head referee at the time, stood over the catering table, grabbing a bottle of water. He felt Jim’s breath on the back of his neck and quickly spun around to see his boss… and his champ. Cylear gave him a shove, knocking him back against the edge of the table. “So,” Cylear began, snatching the water from James and taking a drinking before chucking it over his shoulder, “you think you’re so kind of hero DQing me at the pay per view, huh? You think you’re some kind of tough guy standing up to me? Is that it?” All James could do was glare into Sean’s eyes and shake his head. He hated that man, there was no two ways about it. Those many months back, when he first saw him begin to bud into a true professional in the ring, he would have never thought Jim Johnson could have clouded his mind so much that he was no longer a shadow of the man he use to be. Sean swung his hand and smacked Jim across the face violently, once again sending him back into the table… this time with much more force. “You want to say something, James? You want to take a shot? Come on, tough guy, hit me… give me a reason to snap your fucking neck. LLB is gone… I’m the man now…” “Man? All I see is a child that can’t see when he’s being used.” Cylear gave a slight chuckle and slammed the referee against the brick wall beside him. “Child? I’m the champion now, mother fucker! You will RESPECT me!” In the background, Jim Johnson’s faint laughter could be heard. “Come on Sean, we need to go get ready… drop this fuck stain… we have more important things to tend to.” Jim spoke up. James Jack fell to the ground. “See you around, James…”
