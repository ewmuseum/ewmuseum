Baked Not Fried (Part Two)

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/24/02 Title: Baked Not Fried (Part 2 of 2) Date: 03/24/02 4:00 am Place: McDonalds FADE IN Fox along with Joey and Mommy (who is awake now) are talking inside McDonalds. As a pimpled teen wearing their corporate shirt walks toward them, Fox shrieks like a girl at the “horrible” sight and intrudes on Mommy and Joey’s conversation. Fox “Aghhhhh! What is that?” Joey calmly peaks over his shoulder to understand what’s going on. Joey “Oh, Chris calm down. That’s our night shift worker, Onjin. Also known as “Grease Pit”. He makes the best potato salad around! Now, Mrs. Fox as you were saying....” Onjin looks oddly at Chris when he arrives to the table. Onjin “Hey, I think I know who you are! You’re Chris Fox! Yeah! Joey’s talked a lot about you, but even more about your Mommy, Mrs. Fox! Hey, what do you want to eat?” Fox gulps a whole bunch of saliva down, then responding as his heart rate increases. Holy man, I hope he doesn’t come closer, he is one god damn greasy kid! I don’t even use that much lubrication when I am making ice cream! Damn, he should go into making car oil or something, he’d make a fortune. Fox “I’d like the Bacon double Grease burger please.... I mean! Can I just have a Hot Pocket?!” Onjin’s forehead scrunches up in confusion as more grease spews out. Yum.... Onjin “We don’t serve Hot Pockets, but I do have a couple in the back for a situation just like this. I’ll make one for you. Uh.... do you want it baked or fried?” Fox squints his eyes and tries to hold back on popping jokes. Yes, popping. Fox “Err, uh, friend.... no wait, baked! I’m on a diet! No grease! Uh baked!” Onjin starts to walk to the back when he slips and plants his face right into the floor. He gets up, moving on as Fox notices a puddle from where he had fallen, maybe of.... grease? Ugh. He should try Oxi Clean. That salesman on that commercial is really convincing! Joey turns his attention back to Chris, as the McDonalds swivel chair he’s sitting on makes a squeaking sound, scaring Chris. Joey “I can’t wait for you to go out there on live TV and kick some ass. I have to say I am a fan of Action. I was talking with your Mother about the old, fun times we had in high school. I appreciate you driving here. I’ll be watching you.” Fox starts to sweat trying to think of a good reply. Fox “Oh, thanks. It wasn’t that hard of a drive. Especially with Cruse Control. I didn’t know these things could drive themselves!” That should shut him up! Mommy Fox yawns and mutters something to Joey. M. Fox “We almost got into 6 accidents if I didn’t tell him that cruse control wasn’t automatic driving!” Joey “So Chris, you excited about Action?” *Sarcastic* Oh no, I am not. I hate wrestling, I am only 22 years old and I want to quit. Say, I’d like to work here, when can I start? *Normal* Of course jackass, wrestling rules! Joey “Well? If you’re not, a job is always open here! You know you’re only 22.” Ugh. Fox *Sounding Happy* “That’s ok. I am really excited aBOOT Action! BIG TIME! Oh goody gum drops and a half a bag of Skittles! My Hot Pocket!” Onjin makes his way to Fox, Mommy and Joey. He sets down the Hot Pocket.... Onjin “I hope you like it. Hey, can I ask you a question? Did you actually catch Pikachu?” Fox looks at the Hot Pocket, then over to Onjin. Fox “Listen, I am a Master, POKEMON MASTER! Of course I did! Now would you leave you sick, sick man. Go eat a chocolate bar or something. Actually, that would add to your problem. Go eat a Zest soap bar, could clean you up a bit.... you dirty slut!” Fox bites into the Hot Pocket as it bursts open, shooting this green goop everywhere! And Chris, with a mouthful, spits it out and yells.... Fox “THIS WAS SUPPOSED TO BE BAKED! NOT FRIED!” Onjin “Oops. Well the baker was broken so I thought the fryer would be ok.” Joey “Uh oh. I was cleaning the fryer with Oxi Clean Green. Umm, it must have gotten in your hot pocket.... sorry.” With eyes wide open, Christopher takes Mommy by the hand and leaves McDonalds. And once they are at the Geo, rain starts to trickle down, but Joey runs out in an attempt to stop them. Joey “Will I ever see you again Mrs. Fox?” M. Fox “Maybe.” Fox “You, Joey, can take that greasy freak and you can all go to heck! Ahh! I said it! Well I hope your happy! Sister, I am out!” Fox throws Mommy into the back seat as he sprints over to the driver side, and within seconds they’re out of sight. Joey “Goodbye! Hey wait a minute. Now that I think about it I wasn’t cleaning the fryer. It was the fridge! Fryer, fridge I get them messed up! Then.... what was that green goopy stuff?” Joey hears laughter from in the kitchen.... Could it have been Onjin? Who knows. CLOSE
