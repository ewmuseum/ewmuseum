I'm Sorry

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/28/02 'I'm sorry.' Those are the words that come from Max Danger's mouth as soon as Conard Ramsey opens up his front door. Conrad just looks at Danger, who looks dead and beaten. His eyes are bloodshot, his hair is a mess(more so than usual), his clothes are all wrinkly. He just looks completely out of it. Conrad just takes a step to the side. Conard Ramsey: Come in, man. Max steps through the front door and into the foyer as Conrad shuts the doors behind him. Max then turns to Conrad, as Conrad is looking at Max. Max Danger: I'm so sorry man. Conrad Ramsey: You should be. You gave me a concussion. Max Danger: I've just had so much going on in my life right now... I just lost it. Can you ever forgive me? Conrad Ramsey: We'll see son, we'll see. But right now, I'm going to finish my breakfast. You hungry? Max Danger: As a matter of fact I am. Conrad Ramsey: Well, there's a McDonald's in town about seven minutes from here. If you start now, you might get there before they stop serving breakfast. Max just smiles, knowing it's a joke, or at least he thinks it's a joke. Conrad walks back to the kitchen, as Max is right behind him. Conrad sits back into his chair and puts a spoonful of Crackin' Oat Bran into his mouth. Max grabs an orange from the bowl in the center of the table. Conrad Ramsey: So how's the wife? Max looks up shocked that Conrad would ask him. Max is just full of glee, causing Conrad to wonder what's up. Conrad Ramsey: What? Max Danger: Nothing. I just didn't think you cared enough to ask. Conrad Ramsey: Just making conversation. I know, even if I wanted to, I wouldn't be able to get you out of my house, so might as well talk. Max Danger: Good point. Well, she's doing much better.... I think. Conrad Ramsey: You think? You have visited her in the hospital right? Max Danger: Well... Conrad Ramsey: Jesus! She's your wife. You always yammer on and on about how much she means to you, and how you couldn't imagine a life without her, and you don't even go see her in the hospital. Why aren't you there now? Max Danger: There's problems, Conrad Ramsey, problems. One of my wife's friends, Angelica Dawson, is always there. I think she's the one who got Jessica hooked on the drugs to begin with, so I don't want to be anywhere near her. Conrad just shakes his head as he puts down his second spoonful of Crackin' Oat Bran. Conrad Ramsey: Son, she's your wife. You need to be with her. Cancel everything and be with her. Max Danger: I can't, Conrad Ramsey. I need money to pay for the hospital bills. If I cancel all my bookings, then we'll be up the creek without a paddle. Plus, I've had to hire a lawyer to try and get Kayla back. Conrad Ramsey: Have they set a court date yet? Max Danger: Not an exact one. My lawyer said it should be around the end of May or so. I just want her back so much. Conrad Ramsey: Well kid, I don't know. You should be with your wife, she needs you. How cares who her friend's are, she's your wife. Max Danger: I know. I'll be heading back after the TVs this week. It's going to be hectic doing TVs on Tuesday and Wednesday, but I really need the money. Conrad Ramsey: Well, ok. Max Danger: Would you mind if I crashed here for a few hours before heading out so I can get back to New Hampshire? Conrad Ramsey: Sure, but do you reallly trust me not to try and get my payback for the attack you laid on me last week? Conrad just smiles as Max gets wide-eyed. Max slowly backs away. Conrad Ramsey: And son, apology accepted... Max just nods as he heads into the living room. Conrad sits back in his chair and smiles. He then says something to himself. Conrad Ramsey: But I will have my revenge, boy.
