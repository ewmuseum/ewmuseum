Vagabond - IX-A

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Previous Parts: 1, 2, 3, 4a, 4b, 5, 6, 7a, 7b, 8a, 8b It wasn't a particularly long walk between the gym and the chateau that Kent Anthason resided in. Well, that is, if two and a half miles wasn't that long for you. Which is wasn't for Joey, usually, but having just wrestled a short match with his student, Joey was a little worse for wear when he finally reached the doors to the place. Joey quietly wondered exactly how Anthason had such a place and how he had all of the luck, it seemed. Whereas Joey struggled to even be able to keep his job and his life afloat, Kent seemed to truck along just fine. Nothing seemed to get to Kent. Neither rain, nor shine, snow, sleet, hail, or even one pissed off dog could hope to stop Kent Antha- 'GOD DAMMIT, BOY, GET OUT OF THIS PLACE BEFORE I INVITE MANNY OVER TO PUT A BOWIE KNIFE THROUGH YOUR EYEBALL!' came a voice from inside the chateau. Almost immediately thereafter, a small, black, shifty individual was literally tossed out of the chateau's front doors, rolling along the ground before coming to a halt at Joey's feet. Joey glared at the guy before he scurried away. 'Ah. So nothing's changed.' Joey said, smirking at the scene. 'Except that the guys trying to break in his house are a lot shiftier than usual.' Joey shrugged and headed for the door. He knocked on the door, and almost immediately, it was answered. 'LOOK, I thought I told yo-' Kent Anthason yelled, before he realized that the person was a person he hadn't seen in a long time. 'Joey.' 'Kent.' There was a brief, awkward pause, before Anthason broke it. 'What're you doing here?' 'To talk. Can I come in?' Anthason stepped aside and allowed Joey to walk inside. When he stepped inside, Joey turned around and looked at Anthason, before he pointed past him. 'Who was that guy?' Kent looked behind him, then back to Joey, before he shrugged. 'Termite.' 'Pretty big termite, there. You should hire an exterminator.' Joey commented. 'So, who was he? I mean, seriously?' Anthason blinked. 'Some guy. Came in off the streets.' Anthason said. 'So, are you going to answer my question? Why are you here?' 'I came to apologize.' Joey said. 'I've been a really bad friend, haven't I? I gave you the silent treatment for eight or nine months, left you with something you didn't want, and kicked you off the roof of a building. That's not a good sign, is it?' 'And, so, you came all the way to New Orleans to tell me this? Good show.' Joey shook his head. 'I came here because of Coral, Kent. The guy I've stood up for, for more than a year. Your student. My student. I came here because I felt I could learn from him, just as he's learned from us. I came here to find a path to beating Rashard.' 'So, this's about the lunkhead, huh? The same lunkhead that injured me in an attempt to get at you? So, it all comes back to you, in the end, doesn't it?' Anthason said. 'That upsets me, Joey. Everything you do always has to do with your own benefit. I mean, even before you got married, you were always so distant from me after the incident over in IWO. You ruined my career, man, thanks to your crazy family.' Joey glared at Kent. 'Listen to me, I...' 'No, you listen. I appreciate what you've done for Coral since I let him go, but maybe he's still too young and naive to realize just what kind of asshole you really are. I know about what Rashard did to you, but instead of going to help her, what do you do? You just sit here and try to make amends with me? You waste time with your student?' Anthason said. 'What's your problem?!' Joey said nothing. 'Cowardice has never been a trait of yours that I've ever seen, Joey. Why now? Why don't you help Keri? This is a time that she needs you more than anything, and you're just dicking around. Where's the action? Where's the Joey Malone I used to know? Who is this pussy that wears a Joey Malone mask?! Answer me!' Joey couldn't find the words to say what he wanted to say, but he realized with great concern that Kent was right. In his own, blunt, insulting kind of way, Kent Anthason was right. 'Kent...' Joey started, his eyes not looking in Kent's until a second later. '...you're right. You're absolutely right.' 'Good.' Anthason turned around for a brief second. 'Oh, and Joey?' Joey tilted his head to the side and gave Anthason a funny look. 'What?' Bam. Two hits. Kent hitting Joey, and Joey falling to the floor. A third hit followed soon after that, which involved Kent pulling Joey to his feet and tossing him right out of his chateau. Joey hit with a thud on the outside, nearly hitting his head on the bottom step. Anthason stood at the top of the steps and grunted. 'Don't show your face in this part of town, ever again, or I'll split your skull open. Got that?' The door slammed shut. Joey pulled himself to his feet and found that his left leg was hurt in the fall, and his nose was bloody. He turned back to see the door to Anthason's chateau shut, and he just sighed. 'Scratch one more friend.' Joey said, limping off back toward Coral's apartment. 'They're dropping like flies.'
