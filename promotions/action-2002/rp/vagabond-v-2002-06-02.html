Action! — Tales

Vagabond - V

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Previous Parts: 1, 2, 3, 4 Allison Lindum was Keri Lindum's little sister and Joey Malone's sister-in-law. When Allison was just ten years old, Keri and Joey were involved in the traffic accident that changed both of their lives. She always looked up to both of them, but when they were seperated by Keri's loss of memory, everyone went their seperate ways, leaving Allison alone. Eventually, about eight years afterwards, Joey turned up in their lives again, and restarted his relationship with Keri. Allison was twenty years old, now. She had fully matured into a beautiful young woman, ready to tackle the world as she knew it. Of course, she still had to deal with the fact that Joey Malone needed her help in getting to New Orleans. She accepted his offer, and as they travelled down an interstate in New Mexico, headed toward El Paso, Texas, Allison decided to ask Joey the golden question. 'Why New Orleans? Couldn't you have picked a closer spot to stay? Like, say, right across the street from the Beaver?' Allison asked, rather irritated. 'I have a friend there, an old student of Kent Anthason and myself. He hasn't broken out into mainstream, so I assume that he's still living in New Orleans until he can find a federation that'll take him.' Joey said, scratching at an inch in his head as he said so. 'His name's Coral Avalon.' 'What kind of name is 'Coral'?' Allison rhetorically asked. Joey didn't answer her question, so Allison just sighed. 'Well, I suppose it doesn't matter.' she added. 'Shouldn't you call him in advance, though?' Joey knew that he forgot something. He looked around along the sides of the interstate before he saw a small pub on the side of the road, up ahead, and he informed Allison to stop. She did so. The two of them exited the vehicle and entered the pub. The pub, which was simply named 'Tommy's', was a sort of a tough guy's favorite sort of hangout, which definitely made the enviroment less than stellar. Two such 'tough guys' were playing darts, while another six were at the bar, and another four were surrounding the pool table. It was a nightmare waiting to happen, and that's why Joey told Allison to stay with her car. Joey saw the payphone near the corner of the bar, and so, he walked over to it, making sure not to piss anyone off in the process, and reaching the phone. He popped in a quarter and a little extra, and dialed the number that he remembered offhand as being the home of his former student. The phone rang twice before a young man answered the phone. 'Hello.' 'Hey, I'm looking for a Coral Avalon, is he in?' Joey asked. There was but a brief pause before the man on the other end answered. 'Yeah, this is him. Whattya want?' 'This is Joey. To make a long story short, I need a place to stay, so I'm swinging by. I'm somewhere in New Mexico, so I'll be there by tommorow if I can make it. Do you think you can find room for me and possibly a friend of mine?' There was a pause. 'Er, I think so. Er, I... sure.' Avalon said. 'Alright, great. See you tommorow night, if we can make the trip. Bye.' Joey hung up the phone. He spun on his heel and started to walk out, but unfortunately, three of the men in the place recognized who he was and walked up toward him, blocking his path. Undaunted, Joey tried to walk around them, but the leader of the three grabbed him by his shirt and pushed him back in front of him. 'Okay, what'd I do this time?' Joey asked, looking at the leader, who stood at a respectable six feet and six inches and he had probably ate the Ultimate Warrior or something. The other two guys were roughly the size of Joey. The man looked at him, spit out his tobacco into a nearby pot, and looked back at Joey. 'Yer Joey Malone, aintcha?' he said in his ignorant, tough guy tone. Joey seriously didn't have time for this, and an extremely depressed and generally pissed-off Joey Malone was not a Joey Malone to get into a bar fight with. Even if you were backed up by two equally large human beings. 'And if I was?' he asked. 'Well, me an' the boys, we don't like ya.' the man said. 'We thinks that that Fission feller's gonna maul ya's again.' Joey quietly picked up a nearby pair of pool balls. His sixth sense told him that he was about to get into a fight, which started when one of the three threw a wild right hand that Joey saw coming a mile away. He ducked and then jabbed the man in the stomach with a left punch before whacking him across the face with one of the pool balls, which he accidently let go of upon impact. As soon as one went down, though, did the other two jump on him and pick him up. They charged with Joey in their grasp until they hit a wall, which Joey's already sore back took like any other blow to the back. With great difficulty. 'How's ya like that, punk?' the leader of the trio said, before he left the position for whatever reason, allowing Joey to kick the other guy in the nuts and then use the pool ball to knock him away with a shot to the jaw. Joey stood up, just in time to hear the breaking of a Heiniken bottle. He turned to meet the direction that the noise came from, but he couldn't react in time to stop a stab to his shoulder. Part of the glass broke off in the new wound, and Joey yelled in pain for a second. He held his wound with his right hand and dodged a second cutting blow aimed for what was apparently his neck. He then grabbed the guy by his shirt and shoved him face first into the wall. He pulled the bottle-holding hand behind him and held the fellow in a hammerlock until he dropped the bottle. He let go of the hammerlock, and when the man turned around, Joey met him with a hard left hand that knocked the guy out. Joey looked around and saw that all eyes were on him. He ignored their collective burning glare and left the place as quietly as he had entered it. Allison was the first to notice the blood stain on his shirt. 'What happened?' 'Let's get out of here, I'll explain on the way.' Joey said, calmly. As calmly as a man who was just stabbed with a beer bottle can possibly state. The News would report the story in the morning.
