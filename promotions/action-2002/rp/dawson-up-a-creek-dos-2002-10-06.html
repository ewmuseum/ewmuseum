Action! — Tales

Dawson: Up A Creek: Dos

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cameras once again were focused on one half of the former Dyad Champions. Veronica Aromadeus, aka Sweet Aroma. She was standing out in the hallway in front of a door marked “Reed Young”. “I hope this guy actually has some sense tonight.” She took a deep breath, and exhaled it. Knock. Knock. Knock. Moments later... the door creaked open. “Who is it?” And hearing the clicking of heels, he took a guess. “Angelica Dawson?” The ‘Prime Time Bitch’ it is not, however, but when Aroma is a “bitch”, it’s anything but Sweet. “Reed, it’s me!” Reed turns in his chair to reveal a Vanilla Coke can in his hand, in anticipation of seeing a pair of 38C breast. His dream didn’t come true. “Angelica, what happened!” “I’m not Angelica!” “Then get out of my office!” Sweet Aroma barged past the door and stood face-to-face with Reed. “But I do need to know where she is!” Reed gave Sweet his trademark “WTFMF” look. “Where is Angelica Dawson? I know you had to book her, after that plot twist you had her and Bobby pull on Friday.” Reed had no clue what the ‘Spanish Mamacita’ was talking about. “Where is Angelica!?” Sweet Aroma was really starting to get impatient with the DUMBEST. OWNER. EEEVVVEEERRR! Finally Reed answered her. “Oh, she’s somewhere around with that guy... with all those Titles... um... um...” Sweet had a pretty good knowledge of Reed Young conversations, and tried to correct him. “You mean Bobby.” “No, I’m sure she’s with that... Xander. You know, I didn’t like the fact that those two walked out on that Match on Friday.” “Me neither Reed.” “To think, Sweet Aroma and that Xander Minio would do such a thing. I wanted Bobby and Aroma to keep those Dyad Titles FOREEEVVVEEERRR! They would’ve been the best Tag Team in the history of Act- of WRESTLING, PERIOD!!!” Sweet saw that she was getting nowhere with this ‘interrogation’, but for some reason, people always seemed to always try to correct Young, instead of just walking away, and Aroma was one of these people. “First off, I’m Sweet Aroma; and I didn’t leave the Match. I was the one that was forced to lose the Match, because Angelica was nowhere around when I went to tag her. Now I know you booked her tonight Reed, where the fuck is she?” “I don’t know where she is, honest. But I’ll tell you what though.” Sweet stood in front of Young’s desk with her arms folded. Awaiting Reed’s response. She continued to wait. Finally she spoke up again. “Well?” “Well what?” Sweet just couldn’t believe this guy. Part of her wanted to leave, but she just couldn’t. “Aren’t you going to tell me something?” “Oh, I am?” “Yes.” Reed just sat there. He took another sip, or two, or ten, of his Vanilla Coke. “So you gonna tell me!?” Reed finally nodded at Sweet Aroma, and signaled for Aroma to hand him a paper off his desk. Bold wasn’t around to take notes... he was off to get his coat and leave with Reed in a moment or two. She snatched the paper, handing him it... reluctantly. Young cleared his throat. “AHEM. AHEM.” Sweet placed her arms on her hips, and begin to tap her foot, staring a hole through Action!’s Owner. “I want to book another four way Match between you, Sweet Aroma, Robert Danger, and Xander Minio.” “No! That won’t work.” Young looked up and then begin to finish off his Vanilla Coke, before chucking it under his desk. “Look, you book whatever Match you want to book, but I don’t have time to be wrestling in the ring with those three anymore. I want to know where Angelica Dawson is. Because Dawson’s up a creek, and I plan on smacking her 38C’s with a paddle when I see her.” Sweet was about to leave, when Reed shouted out something else. “DYAD TITLE MATCH! HOW ABOUT ONE OF THOSE?” Sweet stopped in her tracks. “When?” And Reed just fumbled with his words. “Um, I don’t know yet. The first ‘Hostile Takeover’.” Sweet knew that Reed planned on having the first ‘Hostile Takeover’ tonight, but for some reason or another the show was still called Pressure Point, so she thought nothing of it, and just exited the office, to continue her search for Angelica Dawson. The camera faded to black, but not before getting a shot of the dozen or so cans of Vanilla Coke, resting under Reed Young’s desk.
