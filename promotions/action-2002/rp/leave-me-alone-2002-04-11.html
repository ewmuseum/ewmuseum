Leave Me Alone

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/11/02 As another stellarly booked show by Reed Young has come to a close, Conrad Ramsey is walking through the parking lot to his rental car. The old man looks tired and a bit upset as he slowly strolls past several other parked cars. He has his gym bag on his left shoulder and he is digging into his right front pocket for his keys. Conrad Ramsey: No respect. No damned respect. I show up early each and every week. I do as I'm told, and I still can't get a televised match, while this no good bum, Mitch Ranger or whatever his name is gets an Action Championship match. Reed Young is a fool. Conrad finally is able to dig his keys out of his pocket and stops buy a red Ford Escort, no it's not fancy, it's just a car to get from show to show. Anyway, Conrad unlocks the driver's door and tosses his bags into the passenger's seat. 'Omph!' That is the sound of someone being unexpectingly hit with Conrad's gym bag. Conrad slowly bends down and looks in the passenger seat. Sitting there with a smile is Max Danger. Conrad groans as he straightens back up. He then bends back down and looks over at Danger, who tosses Conrad's bag into the back next to his own. Conrad Ramsey: What the hell are you doing? Max Danger: I figured I'd bum a ride to the airport, since someone stole my car. Conrad Ramsey: What makes you think I would share a ride with you? Max Danger: Well, I mean we've shared tights, what's the big deal about sharing a car? Conrad Ramsey: Get out. GET OUT! Max is like 'easy, calm down old man, don't have a heart attack', and gets out of the car. Conrad motions for Max to come over and Max does so. Conrad puts his arm around Max as they begin to walk. Conrad Ramsey: Now boy... Conrad quickly stops talking and jumps into the driver's seat and quickly starts up the car. He puts it in drive and pulls out of the parking lot, leaving Max standing there. In the distance we see the brake lights on Conrad's car shine, and Max smiles slightly. Saddly the smile does not last, as Conrad tosses a bag out of the car and speeds off again. Max lowers his head and slowly starts to make his way towards his bag. Max Danger: Stupid old bastard.
