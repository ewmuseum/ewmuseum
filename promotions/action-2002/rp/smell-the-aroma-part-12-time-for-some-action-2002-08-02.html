Smell The Aroma (Part 12 - Time For Some Action!)

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-02-02 Morning... BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! The clock read 9:17AM. The date was July 24th, 2002, a Wednesday. But not just any Wednesday; this Wednesday was her debut on Action!'s Pressure Point. She rolled out of bed and hit the snooze button. 9:18AM. Next stop, the bathroom. The 'Spanish Mamacita' had to get prepped for her big day. 10:07AM. Veronica emerges from the bathroom. She is naked, with the exception of a towel covering her from the middle of her breast to midthigh. She heads over to the dresser in the hotel room, and squeezes some lotion out of her Suave (c) bottle. She rubs the white substance into her brown skin. 10:11AM. She pulls the top draw of the dresser open, and unveils a blue thong. Right foot in, left foot in. She pulls them up, and takes out a matching blue bra. She places the bra on the bed, and pulls a pair of black jeans from the middle draw, and begins to put them on. 10:15AM. She then goes over to the bed and gets possession of her bra. She places it under the towel, and once the cups cover her nipples. She holds the bra in place, while releasing the towel, which falls harmlessly onto the hotel room floor. 10:17AM. She then snaps the bra locking it in place, and securing her breast. She grabs a blue 'Mets' t-shirt from the middle draw and throws it on. She then grabs a rubberband from the top of the dresser and ties her hair back with it, before grabbing her keys, and the hotel room key card, and exiting the room. Afternoon... 2:00PM. Veronica Aromadeus arrives at the Arrowhead Pond in Anaheim, California. She sits across from A! Owner Reed Young. They conversed over what role Veronica 'Sweet Aroma' Aromadeus would have with the company. Reed told her that he would love to see an angle involving herself and 'Prime Time Bitch' Angelica Dawson. His exact words were 'make out with Angelica Dawson in that ring tonight, PUPPIES EQUAL RATINGS!' 2:15PM. Reed Young finally stopped hyping up A! and allowed Sweet Aroma to speak her mind about things. Veronica didn't say much, but did thank Reed for allowing her to entertain. 2:20PM. Veronica Aromadeus exited the office, and the arena, and made her way back to the hotel. Evening... 7:30PM. Veronica Aromadeus sat in her locker room backstage, getting focused for her match with Angelica Dawson. 8:00PM. The opening to Pressure Point began once again inside Reed Young’s Office. A desk shot was seen. The dirty, rusty old black leather chair... the table absolutely covered in work papers... the millions of rolled up sheets on the ground... yep, it was the Owner’s Office alright. Sweetz watched on wondering what the hell it was that Reed Young was doing. 8:05PM. The arrival of Carnage Champion, Simon Seaman. 8:07PM. She watched as Airborn Tyler's 'mum' wiped toothpaste from his face. This brought a snicker from the lips of Roni Aroma. 8:10PM. Morg and his drugs, not one of her favorite things to watch. 8:14PM. 'I can't believe this!' was what Sweet Aroma blurted out when she saw Max Danger arrival with a bond and gagged to a wheelchair-Conrad Ramsey. 8:17PM. 'CHRISTOPHER!' shouted Sweet Aroma, as she got to see first hand the type of things that JCON told her, that Christopher could do; even though not all of JCON's views of Fox where positive. But then Steve Denton ripped his Pokemon cards. 'Denton sucks!' 8:20PM. Tyler Babylon and Linz arrived. 'I never knew these guys were allowed to show up late.' Ofcourse things got worse for the pair when Jeremy Bold told Babylon about the match he would be in later. 'That's just not right!' The night would continue with arrivals, and matches, with challenges in between. Until 8:47PM. It was at that time that Conrad Ramsey was subject to a beating at the hands of Angelica Dawson and Max Danger. The night still continued on the way Pressure Point continued on months before Aromadeus' arrival. Until she got a knock on her door from Jeremy Bold. Time now... 9:01PM. 'Veronica, you're up!' Roni went and opened the door. 'Who am I facing?' Bold answered with two words, 'Angelica Dawson.' So Sweet Aroma made her way to the guerilla area. 9:28PM. 9:05PM. 'A Woman's Worth' by Alicia Keys cues, and Veronica nervously makes her first appearance in front of an A! crowd. 9:07PM. In the someday what's that sound? In the someday what's that sound? And out of the entrance, there he stood. Just two weeks ago, this man was dead. Everyone was sure he was gone. And now, he stood right before all those in attendance. In a pair of extremely baggy black jeans, no shirt and a slightly faded leather trench coat, and a black pair of Nike’s. His skin wasn’t pale. He was marked up, sure, but it didn’t matter. A cancer stick protruded out of his lips, a cloud of smoke covering his face. A face full of stubble, his now faded blue hair braided, he seemed very different. Even his walk was weird. Sweet Aroma just stood in that ring, her mouth hung open. The Announcers didn’t say a thing. And as Rashard Clark walked to the ring, the Fans were silent. Nobody knew this was going on. It was a complete shock. Before everyone stood the epitome of evil. A man who cut his own wrists to give his former friend the ultimate mind fuck. He walked up the ring steps, flicking the cigarette to the ground. The embers of the half smoked cigarette burned out as it hit the floor. He stood in the ring, before Sweet Aroma. She was now timid. The man who stood before her left a trail of bodies in his wake. He was the most feared of the creatures in the promotion. He never was in kayfabe. He always fought. Her game plan changed as quickly as the bell rang. Damn that Reed Young. From 9:10-9:16PM, Aroma fought for her life, against a man that should've lost his and she lost. 10:08PM. Sweet Aroma regained consciousness, and was receiving ice treatment in the medical room at Arrowhead, for the aches and pains throughout her body, from the punishment Rashard Clark aka Syphon Fission inflicted on her. Indeed it was a 'Time For Some Action!' and Veronica has 'Smelled The Aroma!' It wasn't that great, but she promised to make Action!'s fragrance 'rosy'.
