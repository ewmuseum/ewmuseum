The Velcro Heart, The Second Part of

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/28/02 Music always calmed him down. But then again, outside the ring... LLB was normally calm. It was weird, if you were ever to meet the lawyer outside the dubbed Courtroom, he was a very different person. Much nicer. Much more human than anything else. But it wasn’t even a type of act LLB tried to put on in the ring. There, he needed to be loud... cruel... uncaring. If he wasn’t, people would walk all over him... and he’d be nowhere. It was as if LLB had a retractable heart. He’d pull it off when he entered the Arena doors... he’d pull it off whenever anything had to do with the business of wrestling, or his real lawyer jobs... but other than that, he’d put it back on. He’d actually care about everyone again. The job he was in. It’s cutthroat. To play the game... you be the game. Period. Throwing his Action! Championship Title in the back seat of his 2002 Chevy Blazer, it didn’t take an idiot to realize that LLB was the game inside and out. He turned on the radio. It didn’t matter what was going to play. It was gonna calm him down... ease his soul... and take his mind off all the stressful things wrestling could bring up, in just two small hours. Rock, rap, hip-hop, alternative... even country. It was a form of art and expression, just like his jobs were. You had to have respect for that idea alone. LLB put the truck into reverse and began to back out of his parking space, as he couldn’t help but catch a quick glimpse of the Arena that hosted Pressure Point. Sometimes... it hurt. Knowing that every night would end without anyone giving a flying fuck for him. Whereas the other times, it felt great. To actually be able to object to so many against you... and prove them wrong... the feeling... well it was an all-out overload. LLB shrugged as he put the car into drive. It was damn confusing when you had so many thoughts and different outcomes ring through your head. LLB was there before... when everyone liked him. And let me tell you... you’ll never feel more alive until you have thousands and thousands chanting your name. Cheering you on. Hanging on your every word... like you were a God, or something along those lines. For that alone, made LLB want to drop it all right then and there. He didn’t need it anymore. He had beaten KroW... when all doubted him. They all said KroW was going to win. Reed Young... Jeremy Bold... or not, the final hand that’s raised is really all that matters. For if KroW had friends, LLB was damn sure that they’d be helping him too. He was merely just playing the exact same thing KroW would do... if the roles were reversed. Yet the people missed that. They never understood the other side. ‘The Law’ was sure, that if you’d have asked KroW this... he’d agree. For he’d try and use his friends to outdo the Champion... to become the Champion. Anything for that title. The car sped off, out of the parking lot and into the night as LLB looked up at the Arena passing him by again, while the reflection flied across his gray Chevy Blazer. “Whatever.” But he was right. What hadn’t he done? He took all the Titles in PIW after a lot of hard work. He took jOlt to new limits. He caused as much havoc as possible in the Asylum. Action!’s story didn’t even have to be noted. But now... he couldn’t help but think he was falling apart. Where was he in the Asylum now anyway? He was doing nothing... And with everyone against him, thinking that KroW was going to beat him at the Pay-Per-View, the lawyer thought that maybe, just maybe... he was losing a step. For after the top of the ladder... where in God’s name do you go? Turning a corner, the dead roads played off as the gray Chevy Blazer drove on into the night.
