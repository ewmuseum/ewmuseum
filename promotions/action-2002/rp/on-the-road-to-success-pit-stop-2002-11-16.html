On The Road to Success (Pit Stop)

16 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/02 OPEN “No way Jose, Kirby is better than Chase from Fighting Foodons!” “Hugh, don’t you know that Ultra Man Tiga is the best!?” Title: On The Road to Success (Pit Stop) Date: 11/16/02 4:00 pm EST Place: McDonalds Play Land The scene was in place, as usual, as the man you expected entered into view.... The air was crisp and clean as the beautiful blue sky lit up a bright afternoon at a highway rest stop. Buzzing out of his favorite Pikachu Beetle, Christopher Elliot Fox intercepted the scene’s spectral. Fox “Look Pikachu, it’s a McDonalds! AAAHHH! It even has a play palace! I’m hungry.... are you?” .... Fox “Then let’s go get some grub in our tub! After all I’m on that new diet plan to make myself win even more matches and be even better than before.... BIG TIME!” The quite worn soles of Fox’s runners scuffed along and took Fox inside, as he encountered two youngsters arguing just inside of the McDonalds play palace. A sign read “Adults Not Allowed.” But since Chris had a curiosity that not even Madonna could satisfy, Fox walked right on in. Hmm, I wonder what these young boys are doing all alone in a play palace arguing? Fox “Hello boys. You guys are looking sharp today. Say, what’s all the fussing aBOOT?” Jose “Hello mister, are you going to take us?” Fox “Well.... um, where’d you wanna go?” Hugh “I’m not going with any strangers until Jose here says he likes Kirby and Fighting Foodons, not that crappy show, Ultra Man Tiga!” Whoa! These show names are even more fucked up than Pokemon! Fighting Foodons? What’s that? Fox “Well, I think that Jose can like whatever show he wants too. You know I like Pokemon!” The ‘Kid’ boasted about his fascination and skill at being a pro Pokemon trainer. Fox “Yep, I even have a real Pikachu!” This is a warning, Fox is probably going to get into his technical stuff, so you’re warned on confusing yourself with this next set of dialog. Hugh “Really, well tell me how good you are!” Fox “Well, you know first I bought Pokemon Blue for Game Boy, just plain Game Boy but of course I had Game Boy Pocket which gave me an incredible edge against opponents because I could see more of the screen. Blue was good though, I had a Blastiose with 344 hit points, but of course Pikachu had 500 hp, but if you were to take into account the damage that they can do to each other than Blastiose’s hp would be at least 15. Pikachu is electric, so he has the obvious advantage over the water Pokemon. Then there was Pokemon Red, I didn’t get that one. Then there was Pokemon Yellow. You’re stuck with Pikachu and that was a cool experience because I could trade a bad Pokemon I had and get two Pikachu’s! Of course there’s then Pokemon Gold and Silver. I got Silver....- are you with me still?” The two kids eyes were flickering up and down a bit. Fox “Good, you’re still awake. Usually this far in the people I’ve talked to fall asleep.” Hugh “Pwease stop. You’re even more messed up than us! You’re not even supposed to be in the palace! Get outta here.” Fox “Hey, I’m not leaving that easy. Did you know I watch Dragonball Z as well!” Jose “Then if you won’t leave, we will!” Not so fast boys. Here’s my Solar Flare! It’ll blind you so you won’t know where the exit is.... BIG TIME! Fox “Hey boys -- SOLAR FLARE!!!!!” The loud scream of ‘Kid’ echoed in vain, as it made some kids cry and a security guard come running. Security “Hey sir, you’re not allowed in the play palace unless you’re 3! *And* your loud scream has scared everyone, so please leave at once!” Fox “But I’m a kid! And I’m gonna play with my friends.... BIG TIME!” Hugh and Jose ran out of the building as quickly as they could. Security “Right, you have five minutes to exit sir.” Fox “.... lol?” A figure walked up beside the guard. “Security, I know this guy. Let me handle it pwease.” Security “Jeez, okay.” The guard takes a good look at this “guy’s” face. It’s dripping with... um... something. Security “Do you need a cloth?” “No, I’m always this way.” The guard walked off. “Hi Chris, remember me?” The scene just shows an apron, and then it shows Fox rolling around in the ball area, tackling some kids until finally the sound vibes hit ‘Kid’s’ ear. He turns.... Fox “.... Onjin!” The greasy face of Onjin smiled. CLOSE
