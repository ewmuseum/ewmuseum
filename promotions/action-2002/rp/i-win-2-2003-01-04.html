I win-2

4 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/04/03 Remember my brief stint in the drug trade? Well I took a crack, and selling crack, and just it just didn’t crack up to my expectations(all puns intended, as cheesy, forced, and unnatural as they sounded.). You know of the little altercation with Maxine, Pam, and her hellion kids. That pretty much soured me on the business. I only wish I would’ve been soured before I decided to run consignment for the Italians. “Consignment” is when you agree to take and sell a certain amount of product in a certain amount of time. I was wide-eyed and stupid when I first started slinging. So when my smack connection told me he might have work for me, I skipped at the chance. It was for the mafia. Which family you ask? I could tell you, but then I’d have to kill you. Yes. I’d track you all down via the broadcasting signal coming form your antennae and kill you. Don’t think for a second I won’t. They gave me about 100,000 dollars worth of heroine. My girls and I shot up about 10,000, I ended up selling maybe 50,000. Which left 40,000 left laying idly under my bed. Consignment, as I’d come to find out, wasn’t for freshmen to this shit. When you took consignment with someone, you better be fucking able to pay them back. They want their money, rain, sleet, hail, snow. They don’t take it installments, or on credit either. They want it in one lump sum. When you took consignment in the drug trade, you put your life, and the lives of your family up for collateral. So when I only had half their money, they were more than a little pissed the fuck off. But these people wanted their money more than they wanted to create another dead body, so I got some extra time. Which I dwindled, doing about 12,000 more bucks work of smack. Yes. Yes I am an idiot. I expected broken legs, amputated thumbs, a bullet in the back of the head executioner style. Tony, however, was a bit more creative. *** “Fucking cunt queer nigger boy!!” Following that colorful string of insults was a bare-knuckle fist to my jaw. I saw it coming the whole fucking way, so I braced myself and absorbed the hit, selling it as best I could collapsing to the cage floor holding my jaw gingerly in my hands. The 300 or so, who came to watch, roared all around me. Some cheering me on, some wishing my death, all having a good time at my expense. My opponent, was a hairy looking skinhead, about 6-5, definitely well over 250 pounds, and had an eagle tattooed on his chest with the caption “white power, war skins” under it. We were in a makeshift cage, in the middle of an abandoned warehouse. A chainlink fence closed off an area about 20 feet in diameter for us to fight in, while bleachers were set up all around, so wealthy businessmen could sit comfortably with a beer and a whore if they’d like and enjoy the carnage. With deliberate sluggishness, I rose to all fours, giving the skinhead an opening to my ribs. He attacked with a boot, and sent me crashing into the fence. He lifted me up, and I opened up on him with a stiff left. The pussy went wobbly and stumbled to one knee. For fucksakes. I feigned exhaustion and hung onto the fence, while I waited for him to get back to his feet, and start pounding me. He did so, stumbling a bit, and pummeled me with lefts and rights. The crowd roared with approval. He planted a knee in my gut, and slammed me down to the mat with an elbow to the back of the head. “Fucking cocking sucking eggplant!” he growled and hawked a thick loogie, which hit me in the face and produced a slime trail down my cheek. I laid there, hurt, but not as hurt as I made it seem, as he raised his arms to the crowd. He came back to me, and I let him place me in a chokehold. He crushed my throat with one huge bicep, and squeezed the fucking life out of me. While on the unforgiving concrete, being strangled by a foul smelling skinhead, and steadily but surely approaching unconsciousness, I looked up into the stands. Looking for the sign. Just give me the fucking sign. And moments before blackness overwhelmed me, I got the nod from Tony. Thank you. I fiercely tensed the muscles in my neck, and took the brief breath of air this action allowed. I then rose to my feet, the skinhead still clutching my neck from behind. I got up to a vertical base. I reached behind me, and grabbed the skinhead by his head. In one motion I bent over and pulled harshly, flipping his body up over my head. On the way down I shifted my hold to an inverted facelock, and put out a knee for him to land on. His spine, met my knee with a crunch, and landed in a picturesque dragon sleeper. I swiftly kicked my legs back behind me, slamming him down in a diving reverse DDT. I held on to his neck, and violently ripped it upwards while coming down hard on his chest with my free forearm and heard that satisfying crunch once more. Much louder this time. He and the crowd alike went silent at this sudden turn of events. It took a moment for the pain to sink in. “AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!” He then let out a pathetic screech, which made me wince and the crowd explode with glee. I climbed out of the cage, his girlish howls still bellowing behind me and walked to the parking lot. They would probably have to take a chainsaw to the cage and pull him out. He wasn’t going to be able to climb out himself anytime this decade, or the rest of his life if I wasn’t too sloppy with the “Example Setter”. If he had no reason to hate niggers before, the numbness bellow his Adams apple gave him plenty now. I was only glad to help. I changed clothes, while I waited for Tony. The wife beater and jeans I wore in the cage were blood drenched. Chilling winds spurted goosebumbs on my exposed flesh, but I didn’t mind really. As I pulled a black shirt over my head, I saw Tony approach me. Same black shoes, same silk shirt, same 2 muscle men at his side as when I met him in the alley the other night. “You fucking prick.” “Well, hello to you too mister.” “What the fuck was that?” “You don’t like my work? I’m hurt.” “You fucking prick!” “I do believe you used that one already.” One of his muscle guys moved in and buried a ham like fist into abdomen. I keeled over holding my sides on the concrete. “Don’t get cute with me dickhead.” I had a curt little response for that, but I was to busy trying to get my lungs working again to spit it out. “That was fucking bullshit. We’ve been through this before. The nod means you and fight back. The cutthoart is when I want you to finish him. I want sustained violence. Not 5 minutes of entertainment you fucking prick.” I got my wind back, and answered him. “Violence? I Christopher Reeve’d the fucking peckerwood. How much more violent did you want?” “Sustained violence piss brain. I need the fights to go at least 15 minutes each. More if possible. I want my clientele good and fucking drunk. So I can take more and bigger bets, and my girls and do their work. I explained this to you before. What the fuck is wrong with you?” I thought about that for moment...then decided he probably doesn’t want to spend the 5 hours or more it would take me to thoroughly answer that question. “I won’t happen again.” He said nothing. He motioned for the boys to follow him in. As they left, one of his muscle boys, the one who decked me in the stomach, turned and raised a threatening fist. I made an exaggerated flinch showing how absolutely “terrified” I was of him then went into my SUV. I pulled a pipe from the glove compartment, with a decent sized rock still in it, and toked up. After I felt thoroughly fucked up, I made it a point got on the highway as soon as possible before my high wore off. I’m a dynamite individual, aren’t I.
