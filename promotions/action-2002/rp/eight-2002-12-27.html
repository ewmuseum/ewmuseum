Date: 12/27/02 December 31st, 2002 PIW’s Countdown to Insanity 11:35 PM The crowd was ready. It was the end of a long night and the main event of the last pay per view of the year was just minutes from taking place. The other performers that had taken part in the fatal four way match before it piled out into the backstage area. Near the Gorilla position where the technical crew sat ready, was Jim Johnson and his pawn, the PIW World Heavyweight Champion, ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear. The belt was around his waist. He wore ring attire and his long, golden blond hair was tied back into a pony-tail. He was nervous. Jim could sense it. He gave Sean a slap across the side of the head and stared him straight in the eyes. “We’ve talked many times about this, you’re ready right?” Jim asked intently, nervous him self, if one thing went wrong it would blow what he had planned. There was no way that was going to let that happen. “Sean!” He shouted, snapping ‘Showcase’ out of the trance he was in. “Yeah JJ, I’m ready, ready and willing…” He said, grabbing his Nike Sport water bottle from the table beside him and dosing himself with it after taking a long drink. Johnson nodded and patted his prodigy on the shoulder. “Excellent… Now let’s go make it happen.” December 31st, 2002 PIW’s Countdown to Insanity 11:55 PM “Get this mother fucker in the car!” Sean drug LLB toward a black sports car. He was unconsious and was bleeding badly. Moments before Jim Johson had run him down with that vary same sports car. The door was unlocked, allowing Cylear to open it easily and chuck his foe inside the back seat. Cut to PIW Footage… Brown: Cylear’s tossed LLB into the side of that car! Shaw: LLB’s trying to fight back with a few left hands, but the south paw’s taken down with a rake to the eyes. Brown: Cylear... Jesus! Now Cylear’s just chucked LLB into the back seat of that car. He’s locked the door too! Cylear: Do it JJ! Shaw: Holy shit! Brown: Above us... a wrecking ball! JJ’s driving that damn construction machine! Shaw: Cylear backing out of the way! Brown: Unlock the door Sean! UNLOCK THE FUCKING DOOR! Shaw: Hey... it’s almost midnight! It’s almost midnight Mike! Brown: JJ’s got the ball directly over the car... Cylear: Ten... nine... eight... seven... six... five... four... three... two... one... SMASH! Shaw: Gold! Shaw: ... Shaw: GOLD! Brown: LLB has to be dead! Shaw: It’s new years! Cylear: Well, at least I got to see the ball drop. Haha! Cut from PIW Footage… By now the cameras had shut off and the audience were leaving their seats in disgust. Paramedics came rushing to the car wreck, where shards of twisted metal and a possibly dead former PIW World Champion lay. The gold belt was lifted high in the air by Jim Johnson. Cylear, meanwhile, was pacing around, that cocky look on his face wiped clean. Replaced by that of worry and insecurity. “What the hell is wrong with you? The plan went off without a hitch!” Sean paid no attention. He walked closer to smashed-up sports card, preparing himself for if LLB was to jump out. “Come on you mother fucker! COME ON! YOU WANT ME! COME THE FUCK ON!” Cylear screamed, begging LLB to come at him… But nothing emerged from underneath the wreck. Nothing at all. Jim grabbed his champion from behind and pulled him away. “He’s gone, Sean… He’s gone…”
