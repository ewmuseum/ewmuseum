Title: Bondage Date: 10/7/02 9:00 pm EST Place: Howard Johnson Hotel OPEN As Chris turned his Game Boy Advance off, he took in a deep breath of freshness as he walked around the deluxe suite. Chris opened the blinds as sunlight burst into the room. The suite was very classy.... and expensive.... Fox 'Ummm, lady bitch, can you tell me how many nights I can stay here?' Lady 'Um, it's 50 dollars a night sir, and did you call me a bitch?' Fox 'Otay, I'll take it! Me and LL will have so much fun together! BIG TIME!' Lady 'I'll go get the paper work.' As Chris spun around his view became blurred. Fox 'Yay! This is a perfect place for me and my doll, LL, to enjoy breathtaking sunsets and other moments! Oh.... don't get excited now Christopher!' As he sat on the crackling bed, Fox took off his shoes and lay still for a few moments, waiting for the lady to return with the check. After a couple of minutes passed, Chris then looms off into sleepy land, wondering how LLB will react to this nice surprise. Little did Chris know, he was getting nudged by the lady who had already returned with the check. What do you fucking want? I was getting to the good part of our peanut butter moon! Oh boy, I better cover up down there, I feel something growing! Lady 'Here you go. Enjoy your stay.' Click.... 'Buy this skin cream, now 19.99!' Click.... 'Don't miss the all new episodes of Pokemon, on WB!' Off.... Fox 'Ha....... I thought LL would call by now! Damn bitch! He runs me ragged! He doesn't even talk aBOOT our marriage anymore! Well that is that! I'm going to get LL and slap him until he acknowledges me.... BIG TIME!' Knock, knock! Lady 'Its me, Miss....' But before the Lady could say anymore, Fox had rushed out of the hotel room and down the hall, knocking some old people down too! Fox 'LL, this has only started! You and me.... well we're going to get to know each other a bit more.... BIG TIME!' CLOSE
