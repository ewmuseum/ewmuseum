Date: 04/07/02 Flash Back: --- Action Hero? The precipitated air lingers in a big city as Christopher Fox, alone, tries to grab a Taxi cab wearing an odd brown body suit while holding a package. That guy was weird. I feel like I’m in a time warp. The Terrible Two’s.... here I cum. What’d he say again anyway? --- Title: Terrible Two’s (Part One) Date: 04/07/02 5:00 am EST Place: Business Building, Other Areas OPEN As the scene fizzes out and then back into a tall building, Fox rushes down the halls really fast as people chase him from behind. Passing business men and women, Christopher has no time to stop and chat. It turns out our “Hero” of Action has just exited the Arena in where the Terrible Two’s have started. Just last night, in front of the world, the loveable jerk Christopher Fox turned into just a jerk... backstabbing everyone! And running away from a mob must be hard... so Fox finally gets some peace as he quickly opens the dented creaky door to the men’s washroom. What’s wrong with that mob? Isn’t change good? Oh well, now I can go.... Fox stands in a stall, as he remembers what he said to Mommy and Pikachu before he left the arena.... Another flash back: --- The scene changes to Fox running out of his locker room with the Geo car keys. Fox “I gots your thingy guys! I’ll meet you at the next town.” ‘Kid’ says to Mommy after Mewtwo (a Pokemon) tore off her keys. Fox “Return Mewtwo!” He screams, making a silly laser sound and running out of the arena. --- The scene changes back to Fox in the washroom. Fox “I hope Pikachu is ok.” Quick Note: Chris, being older now can play the Pokemon games for Game Boy. Of course he wanted a Mewtwo, another favourite Pokemon of his.... and he got one! So that’s two Pokemon.... no badges. Anyways.... back to the story.... Chris had to find a way to get to the next town and not be caught by that mad mod of former fans. I must not be seen. For, hey.... Fox washes his hands and sees a window. Astounded by the view he can get, Chrisy exits the washroom and looks out. What a bleak morning. Neato! I can see a school. It looks to be in the next town. Without hesitation Fox moves out. Oh, I should have told him to watch for that business man walking by. Fox bumps into him. Man “I’m sorry sir. Wait! It’s you!” Fox “I’m Ch....” If I tell him I’m Chris Fox, I’ll be dead! Fox “I’m Chico!” Man “We were looking for you! Here, you see that town over there, deliver this package to the school. Ok? Here’s the brown suit and package. Change in the washroom.” Yes sir! Lalalalalalalalalalala! It’s a space waster, what do you expect? Chris makes his way back into the washroom. Into the same stall, Fox changes quite fast. I guess he’s all grow’ed up since he can dress himself. Ok, I’m cocked, locked and I’ve already unloaded! In my other pants of course! The door to the washroom opens with a squeak, flustering Fox, because he is under cover you know. After a good ten minute walk Christopher finally makes his way out of the high rise, Corporal building. Looking for a cab is tough in a big city. Chris walks up to the curb as the precipitated air lingers and Fox, alone, tries to grab a Taxi cab. FADE OUT
