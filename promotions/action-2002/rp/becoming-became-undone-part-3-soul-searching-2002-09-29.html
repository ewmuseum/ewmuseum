Becoming. Became. Undone. Part 3 - Soul Searching

29 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9.29.02 Became. Fifteen Minutes prior to the last transmission, Bobby Minio was sitting, looking relaxed as ever. He placed the magazine down, replacing the icepack on his crotch. Without the repeating image of Dawson's crotch in his face the rest of the match slid into his mind. Sweet Aroma, Veronica, on the apron pleading with him, begging him not to level Angelica with the title. He remembered dropping it, then the pain. The low blow. The uppercut that sent him to the mat in a heap. His eyes tearing up, the schoolboy, then watching Dawson and Aroma standing over his head raising the titles. He remembered rolling to his side to watch them walk up the ramp, Aroma turning her head to him. That moment he felt no love for Veronica. His eyes didn't stare at her lovingly. It was only disappointment. Not so much for her, but himself. He was disappointed at the whole situation, at the match, at the fact that he was rolled up in the fetal position holding his balls watching the woman he wanted to hold walking away with the woman he wanted to fuck made him feel like a bastard. But the irony was all there. He went out of his way to make Veronica happy and all he got was a shot to the beanbag. RIGHT IN THE MEAN BEAN MACHINE. The fact that it happened over and over again just added substance to deserved punishment. Concrete for the sidewalk, for people to walk all over him. Bobby didn't know if he should feel betrayed, or if he should have saw it coming. He remembered sitting around talking to himself, the voice in his head warned him. He was so naive. He SHOULD HAVE KNOWN. Something else bit at him too. The reality that its not over. There was more pain, more punishment to come. And he felt like he deserved it all. Why? Because he was a man. Why? Because thats how life works. Woman constantly kicking men in the balls. Making up for the earlier days in history where woman were slaves, and had no rights. Now, in the 21st century, Woman were mounting their revenge, and Bobby was being unrightfully attacked in vengance that he had no part of creating. There was no smoking barrel. No bloody glove. Nothing but a dick and an over active sex drive. Did being lonely make him a bad person? If not why did every woman in his life make it an objective to make him regret ever wanting something more then nothing? Bobby was just a soul searching merchant selling nothing but a reflection, himself. But he caught the ass end of the deal. He always caught the ass end of the deal. Which is why he was the one curled up holding his reproductive organs on the floor. Right now the only thing he knew in stone, was that he could still get it up and everything was still working, barely. But thats better then nothing, and thats what its always been about, right? More then zero? More then what he usually had? Slowly breaking character, losing his deep thought, his eyes wandered back onto the magazine, the two woman in his life scantly clad, and his eyes closed. Remembering the simple physics of the powerbomb a few short hours ago. His hands wandered down his chest as he grinned, there was still one thing that made him happy besides Panda Express after all... And the camera faded out.
