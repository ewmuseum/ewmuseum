The Wrestling Ring, The First Part of

21 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Bill, do you need help with that?” It was another late night for the ring crew after the 82nd Edition of Pressure Point. The show had ended a couple of hours ago, and the Action Wrestling Crew was hard at work bringing down the ring. Everyone had gone home by now. Keith Scott Zimmerman’s Bantam Title celebration was over… Kenny Heckurt and Reed Young continued their PARTAY~! elsewhere… the entire Action Wrestling roster had left… leaving their problems in the exact place that was being taken down now. The ring. Bill glanced up to his coworker and long time friend, Robbie. Bill and Robbie had been a part of the Action Wrestling Road Crew since it began almost three years ago. Jeremy Bold had hired them, basing his judgment on their history in car mechanics. They were both struggling to find a job at the time… and whatever set Jeremy off to believe they’d be good for the job, well, that was why we liked Reed and Jeremy to begin with. They weren’t too clear on any of their methods. Regardless… Bill and Robbie were the right picks. They were hard working men, that suffered through a job which saw not a lot of money… not a lot of fun… and little to no credit. No one ever thought about it when they sat down in their box seats… in front of the television… or just picking up the results on the local wrestling website. No one ever said to themselves “wow, look at how well that ring is constructed”… or that the ring crew was doing a fantastic job, day in and day out. They sat down… watched the program… cheered for those they liked… and booed those they didn’t. But this scenario was okay to Robbie and Bill. They didn’t mind. After all, they were happy to be employed and realized that no one would be patting them on the backs for the work they had done. And unless you were with them, it was hard work. Sure, they had got the job thanks to Jeremy… and they owed him a lot. But the way things were ran backstage… sometimes Reed wouldn’t order new springs for the canvas… or demand that the red ring ropes should be exchanged with purple ones for the night… one hour before the event would start. Oh well, they both thought. It only made them better at their work. Robbie helped Bill detach the top ring rope from the corner. It seemed to have been stuck against the buckle… more than likely from all the Irish whips against it. It was funny, in a way… to see how solid the ring was before Pressure Point… and then see the end result. Wrestling was clearly not something they wanted to be into. “Thanks.” Bill said to Robbie, as Robbie went back to the opposite end of the ring. But not much more work was going to be accomplished… not now anyway. A figure slowly walked down the ramp… as Bill heard a noise, raised his head, and smiled. LLB slightly grinned back at Bill, and then at Robbie. Over the years LLB made sure he had a strong friendship with everyone backstage. It was good to be nice… you never would know when someone you helped might end up helping you. “Hey Roland.” Bill said as LLB approached the apron. “What can we do for you?” LLB just smiled politely as he looked at both Bill and Robbie. “Reed actually hasn’t left yet…” He started. “I happened to be walking by his office… he ended up yelling at me, told me to get you guys…” Robbie rolled his eyes. “Neon blue ring ropes this time?” He said looking at Bill. “Something like that.” Bill nodded and exited the ring, with Robbie close behind him. “Uh… good luck guys.” LLB said jokingly to both of them as they walked up the ramp. “I’ll do a little work for you while you’re out there.” LLB waited for the two men to leave… as he was left in silence… staring at the ring he had spent so much time in. And where was he all this time? LLB had returned after being sidelined by Jeff Garvin four months ago… but after a short stint with Chris Mezz, he occasionally appeared on the odd Pressure Point now. The media had heavily covered this. Rumors of LLB leaving were strongly reported. Some said LLB was washed up… but most wondered if the former Action Champion was even coming back. LLB sighed as he rolled into the ring. He himself wasn’t even sure what direction he was taking. A loss to Coral Avalon had set him back… and a loss to Keith Scott Zimmerman had pushed him even further. They hurt to be honest. It wasn’t as though LLB couldn’t take a loss… he had lost many times before, to many different wrestlers… on many different occasions. But these two losses seemed to kill the once titled lawyer. Now he was gone… down at the bottom with no real direction to take. And as LLB reached for the ring rope… he couldn’t help but think that the rumors of him leaving Action… and wrestling in general, might even be true.
