Date: 01-07-03 Thursday, June 4th, 1998 Jordan Copeland sat in a cold, metal chair in a room with metal walls and a window that was obviously used for the police officers to stare in at their suspects. Jordan Copeland couldn't afford a lawyer yet, and he didn't want to burden or bother his foster parents, so he didn't call them. He didn't want them to know that he was incarerated, but he was sure they'd find out through the news soon enough, there was nothing he could do about that. His counselor's name was Jake Jackson, apparently he was famous for defending people that were guilty, and getting them off with things like a hung jury, or having a mistrial, and for those who don't know, in Canada, you cannot legally be tried for the same crime twice. Jordan sighed heavily as he looked at the metal walls around him, he was angry with himself. He wasn't sure if he had killed the DJ, but he was sure that he had hurt him. The man had gotten on his nerves on a day when he wasn't going to put up with anything. Suddenly, the door opened, and in stepped a plump, Cacusian male in his early 30's. He had shortly cropped and uncombed brown hair and blue eyes, he looked at Jordan Copeland and he grinned. This kid was going to make him even more of a celebrity in his hometown of Toronto then he already was. 'Mr. Copeland, I'll promise that you'll be acquited on the charges, which are involuntary manslaughter,' Jackson said with a huge grin on his face. Involuntary manslaughter? Tarzan Dan the DJ had died, and it was at the hands of Jordan Copeland. Jordan put his head down and thought about all of his options. Here came a supposedely big time lawyer, who is doing work for people who can't afford lawyers. It made no sense to Jordan. 'I know you're sad that the guy died, but there's nothing we can do about that now man, keep your head up, except in court, show remorse, that humanizes you to the jury,' Jordan almost blurted out that he wasn't sorry the DJ died, he didn't mean to kill him, but he figured that the world was a better place without him anyway. 'We'll talk strategy more when the actual date for the case is announced. We'll definitely work this thing out, don't worry about a thing,' Jake Jackson put his hand on Jordan's shoulder. He felt that the man had an ulterior motive. All white men, and especially lawyers, have ulterior motive's when they do good things for young black people. Jordan just couldn't figure out what it was. *** Three Months Later. Jordan Copeland smiled as he walked out of the courtroom a free man. Jake Jackson was a little lighter in the wallet because of it, but he knew that in the end, the money that he had used to pay off a few of the jury members and a doctor who had falseified documents and reports that Tarzan Dan had a preexisting brain hemorage that could have caused him to die suddenly at any point, would come back to him when he stopped doing work for criminals who couldn't afford council. Not only that, but if Jordan Copeland ever made anything of himself, he was sure that Jordan would take care of him. Jordan didn't care if Jake Jackson had any uliterior motive, he was a free man.
