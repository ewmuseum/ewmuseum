The Beta: Ejected From A Car: 2

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I walk. I live. I breathe. I die. If God is our Father Then Satan must be our cousin. Why didn’t anyone else understand these important things? Fear stood before me, with MY SHOVEL, PAYBACK in his bastard hands. I could hear Payback cussing me out for letting the infidel hold him. How did I know that it would not be near the locker room, or out in the ring that had yet to be torn down? Payback knew this all though. Payback is God. I am Satan. Actually, I do not know who or what I am anymore. I do not have time to figure these simple things out. It gets in the way of my slow, methodical killing of him. Fear says that he wants to help me, if only for one show. FUCK HIM! THIS WANKER WOULDN’T UNDERSTAND MY PURGE! HE COULD NOT POSSIBLY BE OF ANY HELP! Wait. Yes he could. He could be the diversion. He could be the person that can be my special little helper. For one show, we have an alliance. Until I say we don’t. Payback can see it in my eyes… Wait… They are swollen shut. Except for the left one. He asks what I have to say, to letting Malone feel some of our pain. I nod my head. Payback laughs silently as Chris Fear hands him back to me. I am reunited with my tool of redemption. He knows what Fear and Fission mean. For one night, Chaos is only the beginning. God, who the fuck am I now? Am I still Rashard Clark? Am I Syphon Fission? Am I Kenneth Rockefeller? Am I Reed Young? Am I Jesus Christ? Am I God? Am I Satan? No. I am Joey Malone. Fear is speaking, trying to get me going. I do not listen. I do not even know who or what I am? All I know if that whatever the fuck I am, I had better keep going. If I do not, my mind will slip even further away from where it is already. I can not afford to lose more valuable time. I nod my head, and walk away from Fear once he is done talking. He doesn’t know what he has gotten himself into. GOOD All these thoughts. All these illusions. I want the doctors to slip too much morphine into my body. I want them to kill me. But I can’t request this. Not until I build up Malone. And then. BREAK HIM!
