Dreams were meant to be broken: Chapter 6

12 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Please, don't do this to me, I pay you,' he pleaded with Fiend. those words echoed through his head. The last time he heard those words come from this mouth he shrugged them off. He slapped them aside and finished what he'd come to do. He always finished what he'd come to do. Always. Nobody could ever do it the same way as he'd do it. He was menacing and damn good. He was the best there was and nobody could touch him. Because nobody ever knew who he was. Ever. He just ran through the motions, he'd done this guy before and he'd do him again. Because that's what the dream wanted. And he knew the rules. He knew he wasn't supposed to break the rules of the dream. Because if he broke the rules, he'd be haunted. Haunted was a perfect word. He took the man by the throat and rammed his back into the wall. He bounced off the wall and down flat on his face. 'You never paid, Vincent. You always got to pay me,' he explained as he dragged him up onto his feet once again. 'When I want the money people put it in my hand, Vincent. You never put the fucking money in my hand. You fucking bit the hand that feeds you,' he continued to explain. He slapped him across the back of the head and Vincent began to cry. Vincent always cried. But this time, it was different. This time Vincent had reasons to cry. This time, Vincent would die. 'I hope you gave your wife a kiss, Vincent. Coz that's what I'm gonna do once your cold, dead body hits this asphalt, Vin,' he said as he looked down to the asphalt. He noticed it had dried. He knew he had to wet it. The asphalt wasn't red. And Fiend was green. Green equals greed. He'd come for DiMarcio's money and Vincent hadn't paid for the third time. DiMarcio was a baseball fan, Fiend was the referee. Vincent was out. Vincent couldn't talk. His voice was taken over by tears. He was scared. And he should be scared because he fucked up and now Fiend had to make it all better. He had to make everything better. 'Please...' was all he managed to get out. Fiend smashed his nose in with a backhand shot. Vincent's body took to the air and he spun before he hit the asphalt, face first. His face scraped across the asphalt. It was beginning to turn red. 'C'mon, Vinny. Don't you wanna play with me?'
