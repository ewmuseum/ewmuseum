Misanthropy [1]: Arrival and Departure

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/02/03 (hey, cool!) The airport in Washington D.C. was a lively, though heavilly secured place. Security was tight due to the fact that some idiot had decided to threaten the US with a bomb threat or something. One had to wonder if they just did this for a laugh as the people and visitors of the US were being thoroughly inconvenienced thanks to this. There was a single man amongst to irritated folk who stood out. Mainly because he was just a bit more irritated than the others were. His flight had been delayed by about thirty minutes, and now he had to deal with the task of finding the suitcase that the stewartess saw fit to take from him. It wasn't his fault that the overhead compartments were too small for the fucking suitcase! But, then again, it was fairly easy to irritate Daniel Phillips. Finally back on solid ground, the self-proclaimed King of the Cruiserweights had been silently cursing the entire airline industry for the entire walking trip from the gate to the baggage claim. He chose this moment to flip open his cellular phone, as it had been vibrating along the side of his belt. Considering the fact that only two people knew his cell phone number, and only one of them bothered to call him, he already knew who was on the other side. 'Ryan?' A pause, though murmuring could be heard from the phone. Phillips simply waited for his suitcase to appear. 'So, how did the shit go with the Shithole's excuse of a pay-per-view?' Daniel asked, after a moment, before he picked up his suitcase. 'Were your collegues... or fuckheads... or whatever you called them mad?' Daniel asked. Another pause. 'Okay. I didn't think you'd find Campbell, what with him getting hammered in the ass by Universal all the time, but Cheno... eh, never liked the prick.' Daniel walked right out of the baggage claim, happy to be away from it. 'Oh, about that?' Daniel said, 'I'll talk with Young about it, if I see him. I might have to kick him in his fucking balls a couple of times to get you in, though.' A pause, more murmuring, as Phillips headed for the exit of the airport. 'Oh, come on, Ryan, Reed's got to have weak balls. He let Brad fucking Schilling into the fed, for Christ's sakes. I'm sure he'll let you in, and if he refuses, well... I'll just tell him he's missing out on some fucking ratings or some shit like that.' Phillips said. Through the pair of sliding glass doors did Daniel Phillips go. 'Right. I'll see you there, then, Ryan. We'll really surprise the fucker.' Daniel hung up, and he waved his hand. 'HEY, FUCKER! Pull that taxi over, right the fuck now!' It zoomed on by. Daniel sighed. 'I really need to find a better approach to that.'
