Your new champion, ladies and gentlemen.

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/03/2002 Joey Malone. Action! Wrestling's champion? Hard to believe, isn't it? A year ago, Joey Malone thought his future in the wrestling business was done and over with. Yet, with the aftermath of the most recent Action! pay-per-view, Retaliation, it became all too obvious that Joey Malone had finally captured his first world title in over a year. A year of injuries. A year of idiot owners. A year that Joey Malone would most likely rather forget. Joey Malone sat in his locker room, two hours after the pay-per-view had ended. He was the only one of a few people still in the building, though he was positive Roland was still in the building. He was also still in his wrestling gear. All of this time, he had been staring at the glimmering piece of tin called the Action Title. In the entire time that she's been together with him, Keri Lindum always thought that Joey Malone was an odd one. He liked Creedence Clearwater Revival, he had a notoriously bad taste in friends(Jason Nuclear came to mind almost immediately), he had an unusual sense of ethics and morals, and he had this unyielding ability to get himself absolutely killed doing his job. But this might have been one of the odder things she's noticed. 'Joey?' Keri asked, coming into view. Her day wasn't quite as busy as Joey's, as she chose to stay in the backstage area and check up on Coral while Joey did his work. She smoothed out her white dress as she sat down next to Joey. He was still staring at the title belt, before he spoke to Keri without moving his head toward her. 'Hey, Ker.' Joey greeted. 'You've been staring at that thing for long enough. Coral's getting antsy on leaving, and I am, too.' Keri said. 'It's just another scrap of tin, Joey, okay? You have plenty at home.' Joey shrugged and casually dropped the title into his duffle bag. 'The champion. I never thought I'd do it again.' Keri smiled and playfully punched him in the arm. 'And who was it that talked you back into this? Huh?' Joey rubbed his arm and commented with an 'ow'. Keri quietly leaned her body against Joey's and sighed to herself. 'Doesn't this occasion call for a celebration of some sort?' Joey used the same arm that Keri was leaning against to put his arm around her, and then spoke. 'Usually, yes, but first, I must will myself to be... less sore than I am.' 'How long will that take?' 'I don't think the human lifespan would cover how long that'd take.' Joey said with a shaky smile. A smile that was hit in the face with a ladder one too many times in the night. 'I hope you're not too sore to come back to the hotel.' Keri said. 'Because SOME of us need the sleep.' 'Yeah, sure.' Joey said, getting up from his position and picking up his duffle bag. Keri, as well, stood up, and they left through the opened door of the locker room. What does the future hold for Action's new champion? We're not sure, either, so until then, enjoy this new commercial. 'DIALING DOWN THE CENTER IS EASY!!!' Actually, on second thought... don't.
