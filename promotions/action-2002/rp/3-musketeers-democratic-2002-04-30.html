3 Musketeers: Democratic.

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/30/02 We bleed the same blood We cry the same tears We have the same fears We pass the same years We see the same stars Under the same skies We pass the same time We all live and die Cause friends and family wash thicker than blood And if ya never felt love than I feel for you Cuz lives pass above us in 747's Deceased dwell below us Before they go to heaven Deep like seven leagues life intrigues intelligence Got no time for clutter, your late if there's hesitance Put your foot forward and there will be a helping hand Take a step back and your stuck in the quick sand Ingesting toxins keeps us locked in, a mental jail trap 2002 where's your family at How long has it been? Weeks? Months? Maybe even a whole year? Chris Fear has finally returned “home”. Chris has not been in the Boston area for a very long time. One place in particular he has not been to since way back in his days as an employee of the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling. Right now, Boston is a city with something always happening. How couldn't it be? After all, the Boston-based New England Patriots are the SuperBowl Champions, the Boston Bruins won the Eastern Conference and are looking to move on to the semi-finals, and the Boston Celtics are just about ready to move on as well. Being the best in the world is a common occurrence in Boston, isn't it? As flags supporting the local sports teams fly high on the city streets, and the Star Spangled Banner is proudly displayed on almost every building, Chris Fear makes his way towards a place he is very familiar with. The cold spring air is getting to Fear, who is dressed only in jeans and a grey SuperBowl Champions t-shirt. His hair is blowing in the wind, as he begins to shiver violently. After a few moments, Chris arrives in front of an eight foot height, rod-iron gate. He pushes it open with authority, and hurries along a dirt path. A sign a few feet inside of the gates gives away his destination. Chris Fear has returned to the Boston Cemetery, the burial grounds of his entire family. When Chris reaches his families tombstones, it is clear that nobody has been near them in a very long time. They are battered and cracked, and the grass around them is nearly as tall as the tombstones themselves. They are beginning to fall over as well - they are all leaning in all different directions. Chris walks up to one of them and kicks it as hard as he can. The top six inches of the tombstone completely shatter and fall to the ground. The name on the tombstone says Michael Fear, Chris' father. “You know dad,” Fear says aloud, “even after all these years I still hate your sorry ass! I guess 10 year olds have more power than you ever thought! I killed you all when I was 11. And Reed Young's pathetic son is 10 and he's running a company!” Chris kicks the tombstone again and again until it finally breaks in two, and falls to the ground with a thump. “Now I'm gonna do something I should have done a long time ago!” Fear screams. Out of his pockets, Fear pulls a number of lighters. He lays one down near every grave and steps on it, cracking the plastic covering. The lighter fluid begins to spill out of them, when Fear pulls out a box of matches. He strikes one and throws it at the grave of his mother. Instantly the flame spreads throughout the graveyard, faster than anybody could have expected because of all the tall grass. Fear begins to laugh hysterically, a very evil laugh. Suddenly he looks at his watch. “Son of a....” his voice trails off. “I have to meet LLB at TGI Fridays soon.” As soon as Fear says that a tree no more than twenty feet from him explodes in flames. The fire is quickly spreading throughout the cemetery, so Chris makes a dash for the gates. Off in the distance sirens, likely of fire trucks, can be heard. Fear jumps a fence to get out quicker, but in doing so he cuts his left forearm pretty badly. Blood begins to spread over his shirt as Fear continues to run down the street. He just makes it to a bus stop as the bus arrives, and quickly climbs on. “Wow,” Chris silently says while recovering his breath. “Looks like I'll have just enough time to get home, clean myself up, jump in my Mustang, and speed to meet LLB. Just enough time...”
