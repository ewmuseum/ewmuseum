What Now?

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/24/02 Read Baked Not Fried by Chris Fox first Title: What Now? Date: 03/24/02 4:00 am Place: Highway OPEN Come on, come on! I don’t have all day! The Ringer, as perfect as he is happens to be caught in a traffic jam on a wet soggy night. He looks around, and out of boredom starts to rate the cars around him. Ringer “BMW? No.... it’s only a Volvo. Nothing even compares to my car. Try a 1999 Jet Black Corvette. I hope these people don’t get any ideas to trying and teal this thing because in two seconds, I’d have them tapping out in the camel clutch or trying to find a paramedic when I nail the Steel Fist on them.” Broom, broom, brrrrrr...... Ringer “What’s that? Probably that red Geo that stuck up behind me. Who would drive such a shit box like that?” Errrrrrr, brrrroooooommmm, crack! Little to The Ringer’s knowledge it was his high performance corvette’s engine breaking down. A big cloud of smoke rises from his hood. Ringer “What the....?” The Ringer steps out of his car and pops the hood in the middle of the congested highway. Ringer “Errr, damn radiator! Sure, last week it was the catalyst converter, now this!” With all his “buff” strength he pushes the car to the shoulder. Ringer “Just my luck. My car breaks down and the god damn highway happens to get going again! Hey, car stay here, I’ll get some radiator fluid at the next town...” Such the bold man that he is, yes, he’d actually walk to the next town, amusing himself with the cars that pass by. Hey, it’s fun... Ringer “89 Shit box by Honda. Oh look a Hummer, wait it’s actually a corvette! A black one! Looks to be a 1999 edition. Huh? Wait a minute.” To The Ringer’s surprise he looks behind him... Ringer “YOU’RE KIDDING! AHHH! MY CAR! I THOUGHT THE RADIATOR WAS OUT! DAMN IT! Ok, just calm down! You’ll go to the next town and call a damn cab. Come on! You’re better then everyone, Ringer! Pull yourself together! You know you’re the best, so act like it!” The Ringer tries to calm down, but walks through the rain with a bit of fury. And just in a T-shirt that’s tight on his body, and blue jeans The Ringer tramples through the highway on the shoulder, his breath starting to become visible. .... And finally, after about ten minutes of swearing and walking in the rain he reaches the next town. It’s a small town as a McDonalds is seen nearby. Ringer “Oh man, Ringer, I want you to take this fury out on those stupid Action promoters for making each wrestler find their own way to the arena’s! Save money... fuck that! And don’t forget those Amateur wrestlers.... give them hell too! I’m the Prototype of wrestling! No one can beat me!” As The Ringer walks up to the window of McDonalds to see what’s going on, he finds a red Geo parked in the west side of the lot. Ringer “Maybe I should steal that thing! No.... it’s crappy, whoever owns it must really like GAP products!” The Ringer walks up to the window and looks in. A kid is seen biting into a pizza pocket of some sort, as green goop shoots out of it! Ringer “Who are these people? This is just not my night!” The kid and an older women run out of McDonalds. The Ringer only catches a bit of what the kid says.... Kid “You, Joey, you can take that greasy freak and you can all go to heck! Ahh! I said it! Well I hope your happy! Sister, I am out!” The Ringer mutters to himself. Ringer “Sister? I must be in Dallas! Whatever. Hey there’s a pay phone! I’ll phone a taxi.” The Ringer dials a taxi number he found in the wet ripped phone book. Ringer “Yes, hi, I need to get to the arena that’s 5 km north of here. Can you get me there?” Taxi Manager “Yeah! Yeah I can! I’ll get my best man on it. Hey Jamul go to the McDonalds! NOW! He’s coming.” The Ringer hangs up and tries to stay under the roof of the pay phone as a greasy sort of kid walks up to him.... Greasy Kid “Excuse me sir. I need to use the phone. My shift is over. It’s almost 1:00 am.” Jeez these kid needs some damn Zest! And he works at McDonalds!? That’s the last time I ever order a filet-o-fish anywhere! Ringer “Do you know who I am Grease Pot?” Onjin “I am Onjin, who are you?” Ringer “I am The Ringer, the Action mega Superstar! What kind of name is Onjin?” Onjin “The Ringer? Whose that? My name is Austrian. So there!” The Ringer spits to get that bitter taste out of his mouth. Ringer “Listen, do you like your teeth?” Onjin “Yes.” I wish this kid would just leave! Well.... he made me do this! Ringer “Then run away before you have dentures!” Onjin “You wouldn’t punch me out.... would you?” Ringer “Damn right I would! Now get lost!” Luckily for the first time today The Ringer gets a break as the Taxi pulls in the parking lot. Ringer “I have to go, that’s my cab. You win this time. Oh and by the way if you want to see what I can do, just watch Action Wrestling soon. You’ll see me there!” The Ringer gets in the cab as Onjin walks off. Ringer “Hey cabbie, what kind of name is Jamul?” Jamul “Austrian, man.” The Ringer blatantly stares ahead of him as the cab pulls out of the parking lot. CLOSE
