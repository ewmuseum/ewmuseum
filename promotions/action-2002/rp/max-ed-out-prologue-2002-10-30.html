Max-ed Out: Prologue

30 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: [October 30.2002] Danger charges from the corner, but Aroma leapfrogs him, but just barely clears his head. Danger stops on a dime and turns around. Aroma attempts a superkick, but Danger catches it. He spins her around and tries a clothesline, but she ducks. She tries one of her own, but he ducks that. Superkick by Danger, but she catches it. Ump: Enziguri! She ducked! Shaw: He landed on his foot though! Cool! Aroma releases his foot and locks him behind in a full nelson. Danger drops down onto his butt and kicks upward, catching Aroma in the face. She stumbles backwards as Danger kips up again. He charges at Aroma, but both sensed a move coming from the other so they dodged. Aroma jumped and Danger slid. He nearly crotched his ownself on the ring post. He just stopped an inch away, and scurried to his feet. She caught him with a superkick to the face, knocking him back into the corner. Ump: Awesome action, Derik! They've been going at it for a long time, and they are still going full-speed! Shaw: They aren't two of the best for nothing. Danger is lifted up onto the top turnbuckle, but Aroma can't get the superplex. Danger elbows her in the stomach and looks to be going for a sunset flip powerbomb of his own! She holds on for dear life, as Danger lands on his feet, still holding onto Aroma's thighs. She then pushes off and flips in the air. She lands on her feet and ducks underneath the Danger lariat. Ump: Oh my god, this is great stuff! Shaw: I whole-heartedly agree. Aroma has her own clothesline blocked and Danger knees her in the gut. He lifts her up for a powerbomb, but he doesn't go for the Wild Bomb. That allows Aroma to spin around on his shoulders and have her crotch in the back of Danger's head. From there, she slips down onto her feet, and hooks Danger around the waist. Danger gets a standing switch. Ump: Crossface chicken wing! Shaw: This should do it! The Fans are going rabid for Aroma. Dawson is pounding on the apron with all her might. Minio is doing the same, but his motives and who exactly he's cheering on is unknown. Maybe he's just reall wrapped up into the match. Aroma tries to fight it. She runs fowards towards the corner and pushes her feet off top turnbuckle. Ump: Look at this! She has Danger pinned down! He isn't releasing the chicken wing! Shaw: Let Go! Ump: ONE! Ump: TWO! Ump: THREE! Shaw: She won? NOOOOO! 'A Woman's Worth' begins to play as Dawson jumps up and down in her purple penguin suit. She is going nuts as are the Fans. Danger releases the hold in the ring as Aroma rolls away. She is on her knees, breathing heavily. The Ref lifts her arm into the air from that position. Ump: What's he doing? Shaw: He's.. he's... HUGGING HER!? Danger wrapped his arms around Aroma. She doesn't know what to do. Tears are filling his eyes. Danger: Jessica. She left me! I need help! Aroma still is confused and taken aback. She just pats him on the back as he continues to cry on her shoulder. The Fans just applaud. Dawson joins the red M&M-dressed Bobby Minio, and the two seem to be wondering what's going on in the ring. Ump: This... is... weird. Shaw: Very. Aroma pulls away from Max and grabs his hand. She helps him onto his feet and raises his arm into the air. He's still crying and embraces her again. Ump: Guess he just needed a woman's touch. Shaw: I could really use one of those myself. Aroma pulls away again and just smiles at Max, who's still crying. She leads him out of the ring as the Fans continue to cheer. All four walk to the back, Aroma has her arm around Max's shoulders, as he holds her around the waist. Minio is carrying Danger's costume and Dawson is carrying Aroma's. Ump: What a weird conclusion to a great match. Shaw: I'll say. But maybe he gets the girl. Ump: I doubt it. She's still with JCON, ya know? Max just wants help in getting Jessica back, me thinks. Shaw: I hope so. I love looking at her. Ump: I have a feeling this one may be a candidate for TheNews' Match of the Week. Neither Aroma nor Danger is a stranger to that. Shaw: Just... WOW! At the beginning of the match, Sweet Aroma could only think of proving a point, a win over Max Danger would finally shut his mouth. A win over Max Danger would finally close the book, on who was the better of the two. 'The Rock Star' vs. 'The Woman That Has A Worth' 'The Danger Man' vs. 'The Sweet Woman' Xander vs. Roni Marcus Alexander Dox vs. Veronica Aromadeus That's what it all boiled down to. Max Danger vs. Sweet Aroma By the end of the match, Sweet didn't know what to do, Max seemed to be unstoppable, and a win wasn't really important to her anymore. But at the last moment, when defeat was looking her dead in the face, she saw victory through her periffial, and did what she had to do. Suprisingly Max didn't release the chicken wing, which was the main reason for Sweetz doing what she did. But when Danger broke down, the win flew out the window. And Sweet Aroma was Max-ed Out.
