Lack of Respect

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/28/02 Conrad Ramsey: Absolutely no respect. Conrad Ramsey scoots across his kitchen floor inside his Boulder, Colorado home. Conrad sits down at the kitchen table with a bowl of Cracklin' Oat Bran and a tall glass of whole milk in front of him. Conrad dips his spoon into the Cracklin' Oat Bran and pulls it back up, but it never reaches his mouth. Conard Ramsey: No damn respect. I give, give, give and never get anything in return. I try to get along with everyone. I try to be a professional, but it's hard. It's hard when you know you're going to continue to be disrespected. It's bad enough that Action! is run by an imbeccle, now he's put his kid in charge. Things are not going to get better. Conrad puts his spoon into the Cracklin' Oat Bran but doesn't pull it out just yet. Wanting to rant further rather than eat. Conrad Ramsey: I have a hard match going at the last TVs and that damned Max Danger whacks me with a steel chair. Sure I get the victory via disqualification, but I did nothing to warrant that type of attack. So I made a little joke, big deal. People make fun of me for being a 60-year-old wrestler, but I don't bash their skulls in with a chair. Oh, believe me, I'd love to sometimes, but I know if I prove to them that I'm worthy of being in the ring, then it will mean more and make a bigger impact. Conrad puts his hand around his glass of whole milk, apparently choosing over the delicious taste of Crackin' Oat Bran. Conrad picks up the glass, and just holds it there, and stares forward talking to the air. Conrad Ramsey: I mean, this Danger fella does nothing but pester me. Pester, pester, pester. I don't get ten seconds alone, unless I'm in the ring, but apparently that isn't solitude for me either. I don't know what the deal is, but I'm not going to worry myself with it. I have me some Cracklin' Oat Bran to eat, to start my day right. Like they say, 'An open box, is an empty box.' Conrad smiles to himself, and gets a spoon full of Cracklin' Oat Bran, having put his milk back down. Just as Conrad is about to put the Cracklin' Oat Bran into his mouth the door bell rings. Conrad puts down the Cracklin' Oat Bran and curses to himself. He gets up from his seat and walks through the kitchen. He passes through the living room and into a small hallway. There, he turns right and goes through the foyer. He walks up to the door and looks through the peephole. He takes a step back with a look of shock across his face. He then unlocks the door and standing there is... MAX DANGER!! *dum, dum, dum*
