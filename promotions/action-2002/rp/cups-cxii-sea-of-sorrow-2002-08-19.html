Cups: cXII: Sea of Sorrow

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 19, 2002 Cups: cXII: Sea of Sorrow The road was long and narrow. Miles went through the tires cleanly as the tread slowly rubbed away on the terrain. He dove into the temptation and gave way as the vehicle coasted easily down over a hill. Pictures of his love, his life, had been painted in his mind all day. Still, he concentrated and carried on. No worries No lies No love anymore. Things had all worsened. James peered into the rear-view mirror and saw his bloodshot eyes. Slowly, he pulled over out of all harms way and began to think about what had happened one more time. He felt if he thought it through ... it could be changed. Life had began to fade away. No trust No establishment No pleasure left Madison felt as if he'd had one too many beers, yet ... he hadn't drank any. His physical conditions must be worsening. Soon enough, he felt it would be time to visit in with the worrying doctors. Disregarding all of their pleads, he carried on. Flashbacks kicked in of the immense pain they placed into his hands. ‘Sir, you've got to allow us...' ‘It's for your own good.' vStill, he refused. The treatment may have cured him, but ... that's not what he wanted. Living a life of being normal ... not being picked on or hated was his main goal, although ... it had faded at this time. Now, he was living a life of secrecy. ‘Love is ... what I've got.' James felt the words pry their way through him. ‘It's inside of me. The love I felt for her could never fill the void in this lifetime. Inside and outside of me, I sense the love. A feeling is mental. I look at myself and I see something that not every other individual sees. I see myself as who I am ... what I have made of myself. I have no made a f*cking loser! That's for sure. I've made a human being worth living. What I started off with was nothing short of a piece of sh*t. I see what is happening. This sea I am swimming in; I'm not alone. I'm starting to see the other people paddling along beside me. Look in the mirror and tell me the truth ... do you feel that love is physical or mental. I know .. It's all in a person's heart and soul. Nothing comes from the physical appearance. If it does, then you're paddling along side me in the sea of sorrow for the wrong d*mn reason.' James floored it and spun the tires wildly as he made his way back on the interstate and towards a hotel.
