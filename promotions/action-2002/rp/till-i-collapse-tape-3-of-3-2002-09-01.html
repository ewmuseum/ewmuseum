Action! — Tales

Till I Collapse (Tape 3 of 3)

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FUZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ Jeremy slowly pushes it into the machine, wondering what awaits him and Reed Young.... Bold “Holy caked rusted metal, Boss! What’s going to happen to our dear Chris Fox!? He’s the only Chris left! And each good fed needs at least one Chris!” The scene was once again in Reed’s office, where the Staffers sat. As ‘The Boss’ took off his sneakers and placed his socking feet on the soft carpet, he replied. Young “You forgot one thing Jer.” Bold “What’s that sir?” Young “Chris is Christopher! Since he’s called Christopher it accounts as two Chris’! So.... we’re fine.” Bold “Oh, ok” The remote scuffed off the table as Bold grabbed it and pressed play. FUZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ Title: Till I Collapse (Tape 3 of 3) Date: Recorded Place: Tape Thump, thump, THUMP! The sounds got louder as it approached the door of the police station. By the way, you could hear the thumps.... you knew it was Chris Fox. Only ‘Kid’ walks to a tune inside his head. The scene switches and indeed reveals Christopher exiting his silver Pikachu Beatle as he heads over to the entrance of the police station. His keys jingle in his pocket as he whistles.... as finally when our hero, -and I use that term loosely,- gets to the police station doors, he stops. Ok, so one quick practice on what I’m going to do! I jump in and demand Pikachu, or I give bail. Ok, let’s go! Fox grasps the cold silver handle and swings it open as he attempts a somersault.... but fails miserably flying right into the receptionist’s desk. Startled the old woman, she gets up and sees what just made that noise. Woman “Excuse me sonny, but WHAT THE FUCK DO YOU THINK YOU’RE DOING!?” Fox “AAAAAAHHHHHH!” Woman “AAAAAHHHHH!” Fox “You swore! All women who swear are fucking whore bitches.... BIG TIME!!!!!!!!” Stunned, the old lady tries to swing at Fox. But luckily ‘Kid’ gets up and races down the hall, thanks to the shiny tiled floor. As Fox scrambles around the police station looking for Pikachu, he walks by a room, seeing some weapons just laying on table.... unattended! Fox “Well happy fucking birthday Christopher Elliot Fox! Let’s play! I haven’t had a play day in sssoooooooooooooooooooooooo long!” Fox walked into the room and grabbed a nightstick. Fox “Whoa! I’ve seen these before on my tapes! Yeah, the guy takes it and sticks it up the girl!” ‘Kid’ makes a poking motion forward and then sets the stick back down. Bold “Hey, I think I’ve seen that video as well!” Young “Shut up Jer!” After that interruption, Fox picks up a pepper spray bottle. Little did the dumbfounded Fox know that it was full. Fox “Wow! A bottle! I wonder what happens if I push this button!” As he says that, Chris pushes the button as the pepper spray goes all over his shirt! Fox “Wow! This sprays just as good as my ice cream maker! I’m keeping this.” Christopher slips the can into his pocket, but before he could pick up that nice looking lemon shaped object with a pin in it, he hears a voice. Voice “Yes, so if you’ll follow me, I’ll show you our new weapons. The grenades, pepper spray and nightsticks!” Fox “Oh no, those peoples are coming in here! Time to bolt!” ‘Kid’ dove out of the room and out of the police station. Whew, he almost got caught. But knowing Chrisy he got some sort of memorabilia for his efforts, and he did. Along with the pepper spray, Fox grabbed a paper off the old ladies desk on the way out. Fox “That should piss ‘em off!” Chris then jumped in his car, turned it on, and went as fast as he could out of the police station. As cars honked at Fox for jumping red lights, the child of Mommy Fox read the letter that he ‘took’ for no reason. Fox “Hhmm. The letter says that Pikachu was taken by the police to the grand canyon! I have to go to the fecking grand canyon to get my Pikachu back!?!? Pft! I bet that Stevie Dentist was behind this terrible thing! Oh well.... I guess I’m off to the grand canyon to settle this once and for all!” FUZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ The scene shows Reed Young in his chair. Young “Damn, why did Jer have to go to the washroom right at the good part!?” Bold walks into the room. Bold “What’d I miss, Boss?” Young “Uumm, nothing much.... except the ending of Tape 3. It was weird. It’s like there’s suppose to be another tape.... but there isn’t.” With that same dumbfounded look on his face, Jeremy Bold replied. Bold “How so Boss?” Fox “Hey Boss-a-cho!” Bold “Boss, was that you?” Fox “No silly buns!” The scene turns.... as Christopher Fox is shown at the door while he walks into the room. Young “Hey Fox!” Fox “I see you found my home movies!” Young “Home movies?” Fox “Yeah, I bought this new digital camera, you know, like all of those Chinese immigrants get when they enter the country! They always get the high tech crap.” Bold “And they become rich very fast as well!” Fox “That’s right Jaaaar. Anyway, I wanted to film a day in the life of myself, and it turned out that I lost Pikachu! But I ran out of film when I got to the Grand Canyon.” Bold “Anything interesting happen there?” Fox “Naaaaaa. But did you know that there are no trees at the canyon!? It’s true!” Bold “Neat....” Young “Yeah. Neat.” CLOSE
