Misanthropy [4]: Miss tier ee ous.

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/05/2003 Smilthy's. Duncan Fletcher hadn't set foot in one of these in a while, ever since he decided to walk into the crazy happenings of the restaurant franchise's mothership, the Asylum. He stood in an empty basement of a Smilthy's, the setting of the underground fighting ring that served as the Asylum's minor leagues. He knew why he was there, he was just waiting on something. Duncan Fletcher, or rather, the man who chose this as his identity, Ryan Maxwell Jr., remembered it like it was yesterday. Fresh off of being fired from LoW, and fresh off of being banned from all IWO events, Ryan came to a Smilthy's in the Portland, Oregon area, seeking guidance and wisdom. Ryan didn't remember how it all happened, and before he knew it, he had joined the fighting ring. Within several weeks, Ryan had refined his already skilled fighting skills for one-on-one, hand-to-hand combat. Yet, Ryan knew there was something out of place about his style. Elements of jujitsu, karate, and even frigging capoiera had popped up in his style, and not even Ryan could explain it. Sure, he had been shoot fighting in Japan, but there, he was trained to grind his enemies into submission, not pummel them into oblivion. His cell phone buzzed in his trenchcoat pocket, so he took it out, flipped it open, and answered it. 'Maxwell.' Fletcher frowned. He knew who the hell was calling him, since there were only two people who knew his cell phone number, and only one who would bother calling him. His boss and true employer. 'Mr. Campbell.' Fletcher took his sunglasses off and tried to mentally prepare himself for this. Joe Campbell hadn't been too thrilled that one of his better prospects and hired guns decided to take off for personal matters, but Ryan still retained his close ties to the corrupt Asylum owner. 'I'm in Houston, sir. Smilthy's, actually.' A pause. 'Yes, I heard about the incident down here. Almost had an officer down here. I managed to clear the operation out before he came down, though, thanks to a secondary exit. I have to credit the owner, he definitely prepared for the worst.' Another pause, as Fletcher visibly grimaced. 'Phillips? That loudmouth decided to take his girl out for a date or something. Can you believe that he has a girlfriend?' Fletcher then did something that he almost never did. He laughed. 'Ah, don't say that about him. After all, he did assist in taking down those Stranglehold fucks, which was helpful in the long run for us.' Fletcher said. A pause of reflection. 'Joe, if I could, I'd still be there. I'm glad you got Aragon to help, he seems to be more effective than an old fox like me,' Fletcher said, 'Anyway, I've got the Smilthy's in Dallas to check up on, then I'll head to wherever Idiot Young is booking the next show.' Fletcher thought about it and smiled. 'Oh yeah, and if that fuck ever bothers stealing your material again, you know who to call.' Fletcher began to walk out of the basement. 'I'll be in touch.' He turned off the phone, then flipped it closed. He sighed to himself and finished climbing up the stairs. He did not see the man in a similar black trenchcoat step out of the shadows, watching him leave.
