Bad Hair Day (Part 1 of 3)

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/01/02 OPEN Vrrrrrrr, VRRRRROOOOOOOMMMMMM!!!!!!!!!! “Let’s burn rubber Pikachu!” Title: Bad Hair Day (Part 1 of 3) Date: 2:00 pm EST Place: Highway The super crisp silver Pikachu Beatle zings down the highway at 200 km per hour in a 100 zone. Of course, it’s Chris Fox.... but can you tell? The scene switches to the view of our beloved stuffed warrior, Pikachu with Chris, who doesn’t seem to look the same.... what’s that in front of his face? Ha, hair? Indeed, Christopher’s hair is covering his whole face! What a scrub! No wonder he’s going so fast, he can’t even see what’s in front of him! ZZZZOOOOMMMMMMMMMM! Right past a police car! Now you’ve done it Chris! Not only did you just get the car repaired, but you also managed to awake a sleeping cop! Sirens can be heard as Fox has no clue what’s racing to catch up with him. Fox “Dickie D Man! I’m so pulling over!” Nearly hitting another car, the Pikachu Beatle slowly rolls to a stop on the shoulder. The police car then pulls up ahead as a dark sunglass wearing, macho bearded police man emerges from the cruiser and power walks to the silver car. Fox somehow manages to roll the window down as the shady police man starts his spiel. Police Officer “Hey Chief, do you know how fast you were going back there?” The big burly voice of the police man stops and waits impatiently for Fox to reply. The only thing is, Chris didn’t! Police Officer “Sir?” Fox finally spat something out. Fox “Yessir?” Police Officer “Answer the question.” Oh, he wants me to answer that question? Well he should know that one of Pikachu’s nicknames is Chief! Fox “I wasn’t aware you were talking to me Mr. Dickie D. By the way I’ll have a triple fudge smudge sludge chocolate shake pwease! I sssooo love chocolate now, it’s unreal.... BIG TIME!!!!” The police man lowers his glasses and opens the driver door. Police Officer “I see I caught a funny guy! Exit the vehicle right now sir and please take that wig off, I can’t see your face.” As he was told, Fox grabbed Pikachu and jumped out of the car. Police Officer “Take that wig off.” Uh oh.... I don’t know what he’s talking aBOOT! Police Officer “TAKE IT OFF!” Fox “Ummmm LOL.... ?” Taking a breath of air the police officer puts his hand on the top of Fox’s head and pulls as hard as he can. Do to the lack of vision, Fox doesn’t even know what’s happening. Fox “OUUUUUUUCCCCCCCHHHH!!! I remember that chocolate shake didn’t hurt this much! Nor did my Mommy! I mean....” With a reluctant jolt backwards, the officer’s glasses fall completely off. Police Officer “Now you listen here boy, I don’t want to hear that type of language! I see that your hair has impaired your driving! Therefore I’m going to drive you to the nearest town to get it cut and then I’m writing you a ticket!” Fox “To a carnival?” Police Officer “NO! A ticket for breaking the speed limit!” I don’t use drugs Mr. Weird Dickie D man! Hey wait a minute, I thought I told Pika Peek to stop dealing! That little pusher! Fox “But I was only growing my hair to match my new bud, Jo-D Malon-E.... BIG TIME!!!” Police Officer “Tony Soconie? You mean the bug drug king pin? I thought we bagged him along with his groupies!” What, is this guy def? I said JO-D MALON-E!!!!!!!!! Fox “Well since you don’t look like you’re selling shakes, I’m gonna go. Bye bye!” Now where’d I put my super special car starting thingy’s? Of course, Chris was talking about his car keys. Everything seems like a game to him. Fox “Hmmm, where did I leave them last?” Police Officer “Sir, what can I help you with?” Fox “Oh I seem to have lost my.... ummm, you know, vrroooom, vrrommm thingy’s. You know, the thing that starts a car car!” Police Officer “Your keys?” Fox “That’s the word! Yeah, can you help me look!?” With no alternative, the Police Officer agrees to look for them only if Christopher promises to get a hair cut in the next town. With a quick ‘otay’ Fox and the Police Man search the Beatle. Eventually the Police Man had to eject himself from the car because of the wretched smell of certain types of sweat. Police Man “Well sir I can’t seem to find your keys. How ‘bout you, found them yet?” Fox emerges out of the car coughing and waving the fresh air in his face. Fox doesn’t have the keys, but he seems to get something else out of his car. Fox “I wonder what this is man? Hey can you open this? I can’t see....” Huffing and puffing, the Police Officer opens the box, but just as he did a huge blow up doll exploded out into the open. The sounds of inflation made Fox know exactly what the item was. Blushing under his hair Fox just grabs the doll and throws it in the nearby ditch, then walking back to the shoulder. Fox “You saw nothing!” Police Officer “Trust me, I don’t want to know.” That’s right! Unless you want to hear me say how many times I ice creamed on that lady, you heard nothing. With almost a light bulb flashing above Fox’s head, our hero remembers, or thinks he remembers where he left his keys. Fox “Wait! Look over there! Are my keys there?” The Police Officer looks over as Fox’s hair swaggers as he walks up to the police man. I betcha those little devils are in here! Thinking he has just opened the glove box, Fox un-zips the pants of the police officer and dives his arm full-force into the police officers pants! Not realizing anything yet, the Police Officer can’t see anything in the direction Fox pointed him to. Fox “What’s this?” Fox tugs on the man’s leg hairs and really get his attention! Police Officer “WHATTHEFUCK!?” Ignoring the profanity, Fox penetrates the boxers. Fox “What’s dis squishy thing? These aren’t the little devil’s I was looking for.... BIG TIME!!!” Police Officer “You’re in my pants!! YOU’RE IN MY PANTS!! GET OUT!! GET OFF OF ME YOU HOE!!” Fox relinquished his tight grab on the two ‘devils’ and scurries into his car, almost getting hit by some speeding cars. Little did Fox know, his keys were left in the ignition. With a simple turn Fox speeds off in fear of the police man following him. Back to the Police Officer.... Police Officer “Look, my pants! He ripped them! That boy better get a hair cut! Jeez, and I thought Reed Young was a scrub!” The scene ends with a split screen of the officer standing on the shoulder in his boxers and Fox driving down the highway with his wild hair flapping from the door.
