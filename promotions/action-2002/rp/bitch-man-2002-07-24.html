Bitch Man

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/24/02 OPEN “It’s time. I’m going to tell him fucking straight up! It’s over! I never knew it would come to this!” Title: Bitch Man Date: 07/24/02 2:00 pm EST Place: Arena Hallway The empty arena stands still as dust can be heard softly touching down on the floor. The silence is then broken as the man known as the “idiot” in this story walks down the dark estranged hallway. That’s right, it’s LLB. He’s the one who locked criminals away and yet some people still liked him. In a different part of the arena, Christopher Fox is aBOOT to face his fear, calling his “beautiful” marriage to an end with LLB. Fox “It’s time. I’m going to tell him fucking straight up! It’s over! I never new it would come to this!” You can do this. Just say goodbye! I have bigger things to fuck aBOOT, like Stevie Dentist! He ripped my favouritist Pokemon card! Now he’s going to pay! No Dentist with a PHD hurts Christopher ’Fucking’ Fox and gets away with it! Chris starts to walk around the arena hallway. Fox “I know LL is here! He always shows up a couple of days early for a show! And he always walks around the hallway like the fans do. I never did understand my ‘wife’.” The scene then switches to LLB walking with his hands in his pockets and his Action! Championship on his right shoulder. And as luck would have it, LLB and Fox were heading towards each other! Time past with both stars just walking... but finally Fox saw LLB and ‘The Law’ saw ‘Kid’ too! With disgust LLB just kept on walking after Fox ran over to his hero. Fox “Wait! I have something to tell you!” Surprisingly LLB turned around, and stood up just a bit taller than before. LLB “What do you want? You’re wasting my time!” Fox “Oh sorry, but I didn’t realize that walking around an empty arena was so important!” LLB “Don’t push me!” Resenting that remark, Fox continued. Fox “I know I cannot get a title shot for three months but LL, but I will be relieved of that stip and when I am, I’ll be after you like Jenna Jamenson on the nearest guy that weighs 120 pounds or less!” LLB “You’re pathetic! You just can’t except I kicked your fucking ass! Look at you! You’re wearing a fucking 7 11 uniform! You’re a jackass!” Fox “Hey! I tried to make our marriage work! But I have something to tell you! You know, our marriage, it was fake! I never signed our papers declaring us Mister and Miss Christopher E. Fox! Well.... I also noticed you didn’t sign either! So I’m done playing couples! LL, I have other things to deal with! I’ll see you in three months!?” Fox raises his right hand for LLB to shake. LLB “That’s if you can survive.... BIG TIME!” Fox jolts back as LLB slaps hands with Fox! LLB then gives Fox and evil smirk and walks into the darkness. ‘The Law’ mutters some last words that Fox didn’t hear. LLB “Bitch Bo--- Bitch Man. We’ll see how far you can go.” Fox falls to his knees. Fox “I must beat Dentist to show everyone I deserve another title shot! And to show LL I mean business.... BIG FUCKING TIME!!!!” Shit, the final part of Debbie does Dallas is on at 3!! I gotta go. CLOSE
