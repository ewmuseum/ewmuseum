The Building Blocks of Sanity pt. 4

13 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 13th, 2002 August 21st, 2002 – Wednesday – 5:40 PM The fire place was lit, the white lace blinds slighty parted in the middle just allow a hint of sunlight peek through. Jeff Garvin laid sprawled out on the leather coach, firm beneath his body as he struggled to get comfortable. At the current time he was still clothed only in dirty, retro clothing that he picked up at the Salvation Army, a second hand store that Jeff shopped at regularly even before he became a poor, homeless wreck. It wasn’t like he was dreadfully short on funds like he was now, he was just incredibly cheap. He tried to slick back his hair, yet the French fry grease that he picked up for free at a local fast food restaurant didn’t really do anything except attract flies and various fat children as he made his way down the street. His destination? The therapist. The WOMAN therapist. *Random Giggling* To be truthful, Jeff felt like a fool, a devilishly handsome fool, but a fool none the less. He didn’t need counceling, he was fine, he was better than fine he was great. Better than ever, well, if you don’t count have miskeeto larva nesting in his curls and a pocket full of apple cores for supper, than yes… Better than freakin’ ever. Peeking around the room, his eyes wandering checking out the different paintings and sculptures. Damn was this fine as far as waiting rooms go, the door to his therapist’s office was shut and locked so that none of the ‘crazies’ or random ‘gangstas’ could wander in looking for their ‘fiddy dollas’ that those people always seemed to be on the hunt for. Normally, these ‘gangstas’ or ‘underground rappers’ were nothing more than dim witted black people that couldn’t cut it mopping floors at the Highschool around the corner… But when equipped with their notorious weapon of choice, the Glock 9mm, those idiots could be quite dangerous… Atleast when the gun was pointed the right way. In addition to checking out the works of art around the room, he was also checking out the others. Most of which Jeff had never seen before; some were young, some were old, some were rich, some were poor… Damn did they have some lunatics gathered here; a guy that for some reason resembled Jerry Seinfeld and another young lady who was obssessed with a small bottle cap that she refered to as ‘Fran’. And finally, there was a ‘the blue haired freak’, nick named by Jeff. He had a slight beer belly, dark bags under his eyes, a bald spot in the middle of his vabriant blueish hair. Oh god, Jeff thought, he’s coming over… Garvin turned away and grabbed the first magazine he could find… Without even realising it, he was staring directly into ‘Gay Pride Weekly’, specifically an article about reducing the pain during anal penetration. Finally Garvin’s eyes adjusted and he gritted his teeth in disgust. It was either put down the magazine and face the psycho, or don’t put down the magazine and…erm… ‘Face the Anus’. Jeff sighed and chucked the book away and stared up at the blue haired retard that stood before him, his hand extended like he wanted to shake. But that hand could have been anywhere… ANYWHERE. The Original took a moment to examine the hand further and finally answered back, shaking his hand. “Hey! Yo! What’s up! Sup! What’s your name!?” The man blurted out, scratching his lice infested hair and midly shaking like he had some sort of ‘nerve’ desease. “Um… Hello.” Jeff rolled his eyes, massaging his temple, already feeling a migraine coming on. “You have a name?! Cause I do! Wanna hear it?!” He began bobbing from side to side, smiling. “Actually, I don-“ The guy cut him off. “It’s Lowell! Your Jeff Garvin right?!” “Yes.” “Sweet! That is soooooooooo cool, cause like, I’m a wrestler too… Or I was, I mean, before they said I wasn’t mentally stable enough to compete. Now I make structure designs for Lego! It’s a lot of fun! Though I sometimes go through this black out period where I wake up and I have a knife to my throat or my head through a noose! My girlfriend left me! My parents don’t speak to me! And for some reason… No one wants to be my friend…” Holy fuck this ‘tard could ramble. “Oh really, that’s too bad…” Garvin replied, not trying very hard to sound interested in a thing ‘Lowell’ had to say. For historical purposes, Lowell was a wrestler in Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling back in 2000 who resided in the lower-mid card and often tagged around with fellow jobbers. With the exception of his best friend, ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear. After Sean’s death in February of 2002, Lowell snapped. Transforming into the bumbling baffoon that stands before Jeff Garvin. “Yeah. I’ve been coming to therapy for the last three months, Claire is really nice, she’s the one who finally convinced me that I wasn’t being hunted by a large majority of the NBA Bull’s first string line up that I thought were after my testicles and right ear lobe. I used to be afraid to step outside my house and I would go bizzerk whenever I heard the squeek of a shoe… But I’m better now, I’m moving on with my life.” Jeff tapped away at the side of his head, naming every wrestling hold he could think of to deter the mind numbing jibberish that was spewing from Lowell’s mouth, hoping that the door would open and he would be chosen to leave this carnival side show. Moments later Jeff drifted off too sleep, still terribly drained from being up most of the night when he was locked up with those Nazi faggots, only to awaken three hours later with Lowell still flapping his gums… “… And then I would just sit there slapping myself until the voice would tell me to stop… SO! Wanna’ maybe go for a drink sometime! Maybe catch a movie with two hot females?! I like women… I’m not gay… I’m not… Seriously. That one time meant nothing! He forced it on me! I was just a FUCKING child, god damnit!” “Umm… Yes. I better-“ He began to rise from his chair. Target: the exit. The door to ‘her’ office finally clicked open and a voice came from within. “Jeffrey Garvin, please.” Thank you, God! Jeff exclaimed in his head, leaping from his seat and heading for the door… Leaving the nut case behind. Little did he know, he was about to head down a new road… A road… to recovery. … Or not.
