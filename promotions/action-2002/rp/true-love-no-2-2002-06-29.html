True Love(No. 2)

29 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/29/02 Broken. Battered. Destroyed. Probably the best way to describe the teeth you see before you. The jar containing them, is filled 3/4th full with water, to preserve the destruction one Max Danger caused to the once-perfect dentures of Conrad Ramsey. A few of the teeth are seperated from the 'gums'. 'I miss you so much.' The voice of one Max Danger. Saddened and sullen. He isn't speaking of the teeth, but rather the picture of his wife. In a beautiful, white, wedding dress, her hair all up, and all that stuff that makes a woman seem even more beautiful, is Jessica. She has her arm around Max Danger, who is in a sleek, black, tuxedo with a blue tie and cumberbun, and he has his around her. Max is just staring at the picture, a tear rolling down his cheek. Someone then walks into the room, but Max pays him no attention. 'Oh, you just can't get enough of those teeth, can ya? Ok, I'll admit, you got me good with that one. But do you have to keep them around?' There is no reply on Danger's end. Conrad Ramsey steps forward and leans towards Danger's ear. 'Hey, boy! You alive!? Talk to me, son!' Despite the close proximity to his ear and the loudness of the voice, Max doesn't flinch. Ramsey just straightens up and tries to think of something to get his attention. Finally Ramsey just touches his shoulder. Danger spins around and gives Ramsey a hard elbow smash to the mouth. Ramsey stumbles back, as Danger quickly gets on his feet. 'Oh, damn! I'm sorry, Conrad.' Conrad shakes his head, then checks his mouth for blood. There doesn't seem to be any, as he rights himself and stares down Max. 'Oops. Dude, I'm sorry. You just startled me.' Conrad just shakes his head. 'Boy, go home. You obviously would rather be with her, than here, and I don't blame you.' Max sits back down on the edge of his bed and looks over at the picture. 'It's not that easy, Conrad.'
