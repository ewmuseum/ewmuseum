Novella of a Precious Shamrock - Blood, Sweat and Tears Analysis

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, that was a weird week and a half! Oh, and hi everyone, the Precious Shamrock here. As I sit here now, the Action Wrestling circus has moved out of New Orleans and onto the site of this week’s Pressure Point. And I would be lying if I said anything else but the truth. And that’s that Airborn and myself are scared boneless of setting foot in that arena this week. I guess if you didn’t order the Pay-Per-View weekend you might not know what happened. But the chances are that even if you didn’t order it you have heard by now, because it was pretty big, and LLB’s bombshell might have been an earthquake, but ours was at least a strong tremor. Things were going fairly rosy for the A-Star Stable, and Airborn Tyler engaged in a fantastic match with Conrad Ramsey. Everything was put on the line, and credit to both men for putting on the show that they did. Perhaps as the match wore on, Airborn’s youth gave him an advantage and I wouldn’t be at all surprised if that was the deciding factor. Anyway, after his obligatory big PPV spot, something happened that I never saw coming. No one saw it coming. Lee Lightning (my brother for those not in the know) stepped into the ring and gave this big speech to Conrad’s face about basically being a loser. The surprise though when he turned around to speak to Airborn and said “the same applies for Conrad.” As I sat in the book, I couldn’t believe it, and no one else on the roster could either. Not everyone likes Airborn, but they couldn’t understand Lee’s motivation for this. Out worst fears were confirmed when David Dew (also my brother) flattened Airborn (my cousin) with a clothesline from hell. A massive speech ensued, and basically, Airborn was kicked out of the A-Star Stable. So where did that leave me? Officially I was still a part of it, but I helped Airborn to his feet backstage nonetheless, with a crowd technical hands offering support. We found our way into Reed’s office, and Airborn wanted to let him know of our displeasure. So naturally Airborn got banned from ringside. After that we had an argument off camera, and Airborn stormed out. What was I supposed to do then? I love my brothers, always will, but at this point in time I also despise them. I have no idea what got into me, and I have a feeling that at a not too distant point I will regret it, but I did something big, something that sent shockwaves through the tag team division. I hit my brothers with a chair, cost them the match and the Dyad Championships. So that explains why the Winds of Change just changed paths. And then I ran. I ran as fast as I could and as far as I could. Or, at least back to my hotel room anyway. What happened next is detailed elsewhere on the site, but Pressure Point is going to be a biggy. I have no reason to believe that the whole saga wouldn’t have been over with if I’d not pulled that crazy stunt. Now, Airborn and myself might feel the consequences of those actions. Anyway, with my death warrant aside, the show was very impressive! Max Danger’s guts throughout the night were most commendable, and after the quarter final I spoke to his wife Jessica. Very nice woman, despite what some people would have you believe. Max was hurt, and they didn’t think he should continue. Okay, it was more down to the antics of one Mr.Chambers that Max won the King of Submissions Tournament overall, but he fought through the pain, and that alone deserves a reward. Talking of the tournament, the final was perhaps the best match of the whole damn thing, which is how it should be. It takes two men to tango, and ‘The Original’ Jeff Garvin, as much of an asshole as he is, was outstanding. Insert and other superlative here. Chris Chambers throwing in the towel was perhaps rather stupid. No, change that, it was stupid. Change it again, it was AMAZINGLY stupid. But then, look at his associates. By the way, Reed Young has ordered Airborn Tyler to defend the Bantam Championship against Chris Chambers on Pressure Point. Someone needs to change the system by which contenders are decided because, last time I checked, Chambers hasn’t even wrestled a match in Action, let alone won one. What makes him more deserving than someone like, Max Danger, or Jeff Garvin, or Kayden Paulton. These are people who sweat and bleed for their careers, and for Chambers to walk in and be GIVEN this opportunity is a massive slap in the face, not only to those three men, but the entire roster, to anyone who has ever held the Bantam Championship, and most importantly, Airborn himself. Words shall be exchanged with Reed. Is he a corporate slug or does he actually know something about wrestling, because at the moment I cannot tell the difference. Ehem, sorry. Elsewhere, match of the night goes to Joey Malone and Coral Avalon, surprise surprise. I didn’t see it live, because by that time I was already running like the scalded dog an Oklahoman keeps referring to, but I ordered the replay last night, and it was way off the charts. If it was a tornado, it would have been an F6, no, F8, change that, F bloody 1,000,000. I’ve heard others say that Joey has had better matches, but quite frankly, I can’t find them. And if I’m using such phrases to describe the winner, imagine how Coral felt! There were other good matches, including Accidie against Arcade & Son, but I’m tired and want sleep so I’ll only comment further on the main event. Who would have thought Sean Cylear would nip through and win huh? Sorry to Syphon Fission, everyone had wrote him off, and he just couldn’t keep up with the pressure, the lofty expectations or the pace. In fact, most thought the same would happen with Cylear, and it’s come down to LLB and Christopher Fox. No siree, Sean Cylear, new Action Champion. Most don’t like him, me included, but he’s good for the company whether we like it or not. He’s someone fresh, and his character is a different spectrum to the rest of the roster. And, well, LLB, not a lawyer? I cannot see where that is going to go. So make sure you tune into Pressure Point on Monday night! I hope you enjoyed the event more than I did. But then, I’m not telling you anything you didn’t already know! Man, I love that phrase! Take care, -Siobhan Tyler The Precious Shamrock
