Action! — Tales

The Courtroom.

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/16/02 Time. That’s what you have when you enter through the giant oak doors. Time. And whenever I feel it’s up... it’s damn sure up. Some people in my past wouldn’t have given a kid like me the time of day to believe I had something great inside of me. To believe I was special... different... Not the normal face in the crowd. Not a member of the jury. For some people in my past... wouldn’t have considered me anything. Thin air... the kid with all sorts of time on his hands, but no bloody use to put it forth. Well they were wrong. When you enter my room, I’m here to prove their wrong... hell, your wrong for entering, too. Once you step into my Courtroom... I’m ready to go. There’s no backing down. I don’t care if you’re three feet taller or twenty feet wider. You’re subject like the rest. You’re a mere sin in my path to relentless and utter victory... to prove those that doubted me... that said I could do nothing... that left me alone for someone else whom they thought had a mere, slim hope of promise... of special... guilty. If you come through those ropes, I’ll take you apart. For you’re in my path to the verdict I want. The verdict I worked for. And the verdict I know I damn well deserve. It hurts... it does, when people object to me... saying I hide... saying I’m not made out to be what others have spoke on. It does hurt. But normally, it never lasts too long. Like a good court system, my room, my testifies need proof... facts if you will, so everyone knows I get the job done. I am not a shallow man. I need my points. It’s the way to fight. Overdose. Chris Fear. Chris Fox. Throw them at me... throw them hard. It’s not a coincidence that I’m able to walk away from everything. Current and only Grand Slam in PIW. Former Five Time Champion. Jim Johnson wouldn’t have bitched about me that much, if I didn’t mean a leg and an arm to his company. Even he would agree. And that makes me smile. Because ten years ago... no one would care like that. Not a soul. It makes me feel special, ‘cause I know I now am. Asylum Fighter. And if it wasn’t for a few of those people... objecting to my trial, I’d have held the Fighting Title as well. Current Action! Champion... The biggest icon here. I’ve proved my point over and over and over again... I win. You lose. I am not the one that is guilty. For the doubters... are that. August the 25th, live and only on Pay-Per-View... a man named KroW thinks he’s something special. He thinks he’s different. Does he forget my proof? My facts? Does he forget about the law... and what it has done for me? Does he just... forget? Once again, it hurts... but it won’t for that long. Look at my history... look at my soul... a kid like me, as I said... wouldn’t have even made it this far. But I did. *I’m* the one that’s special. So I stand up in my trials at this very moment... and I ask. I ask the doubters to think again. Submit me new thoughts. I ask the sinners to think again. Submit your new thoughts. But most importantly... I ask my current opponent... the most recent objecter... to stand up in this trial... look me in the eyes... and submit to me what his gut is telling him... as he glances behind my face of difference... my history flashing around his blind eyes. And I ask him... in the middle of my Courtroom... to tell me how he thinks this trial will honestly end. Laugh. You can still have your doubts. For history cannot change... and what I bring there, comes forth into now. For the law cannot die. So... still have your doubts? The time ticks. And you will be proven guilty again. I promise. ... I promise. Because I am special. Because I am the law.
