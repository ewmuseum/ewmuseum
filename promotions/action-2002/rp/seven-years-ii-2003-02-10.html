Seven Years: II

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/10/03 On the same road travelled by Joey Malone and the Tyler Family, just ten miles behind them, a second car cruised along. Sleek, black, and obviously new, the car belonged to Duncan Fletcher. Daniel Phillips, who rode in the passenger side of the vehicle, didn't ask questions about where in the hell Fletcher got his damn new car. Daniel tried to engage in conversation with Duncan at certain points, like asking him about the weather, or about how that Vancover basketball team was doing(to which Duncan only responded by pointing out that they were in Memphis now), but Duncan either replied with short, non-committal phrases, or didn't respond at all. After a while, Daniel just simply gave up and started to think about what got him here. First topic? His injury. November 3rd, 2000 was the date it occured, and it happened when the Misunderstood Family, a short-lived stable consisting of Sam Potright, Mike Extreme, and Donnie Daze, had run in after Extreme had managed to beat Joey. After Joey had been ejected from the ring, Daniel took a 'Dazed and Confused' reverse facebuster(think Edge's Edge-o-matic) onto a toaster. Unfortunately, the force of the bump had cracked a vertebrae in his lower neck, and then he had to take the bump a second time, which didn't give him a much better time of it, what with ending up with a concussion and all. Daniel wasn't quite the same after that, as the self-proclaimed King of the Cruiserweights had to take about six months off. When he came back, it was for a brief run in Thunder Pro Wrestling as a heel, which got him over enough to become the promotion's Cruiserweight champion. Unfortunately, his match with Johnny Nova, who was quite the sloppy wrestler if you took him away from high spots, had reaggravated his injuries, and he took another year off to go under the knife and bide his time until he could come back. Which, of course, brought him to the Internet Wrestling Organization. There, he took his vengeance out on Donnie Daze, taking his Cruiserweight title from him with a toaster to the head and the shooting star legdrop that he used once under a blue moon. Seven years. He's been in this profession for seven years, now, having broken in at the same time Joey did. Joey had caused Daniel a wealth of problems, both personal and professional. For one thing, Daniel never even got close to holding a World title before in his career, whereas Joey has held two. Daniel's obsession with getting back into the game had alienated him from both his wife, Tina, and his daughter, Erin, to the point where they got divorced. People always wondered why he sounded so bitter and jaded, so this may be a good start to figuring it out. He would've continued his train of thought, but the motor broke when Duncan actually said something to him. 'Hey, Daniel,' he started, 'After we get to town, I've got to take the car to Shreveport.' 'Huh?' Daniel asked, confused. He often wondered why Duncan disappeared from time to time since the two entered Action, but he never could get around to asking why. 'Yeah. Just a bit of business,' Fletcher explained, 'I should be back in no time flat.' 'Why're you going there?' Daniel asked. 'Business.' Duncan said, before resuming the silence that Daniel so very resented him for. Why was it that this tag team, despite how cohesive of a unit they were, couldn't even so much as trust each other?
