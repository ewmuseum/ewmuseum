Did Someone Say Party I (First BIG Win)

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-10-02 After getting attacked by Max Danger at the tailend of their match, Aroma wasn't that upset. You see, it was her first victory in Action!; in wrestling period. After the fans got a glipse of her potential in a lose to Syphon Fission, they wanted more of the 'Spanish Mamacita'. But she wanted to just relax and cut a rug, maybe even get a drink. She is 21 after all. So she went back to her locker room, and got packed. Her next stop, her hotel room, where she would change for a great night of celebration. LLB wasn't the only who would be partying. But before she left the arena, she had to meet with Reed Young and Jeremy Bold. Too bad they were busy with LLB at the time. So Veronica took a seat, and tuned into the rest of Pressure Point. 'When Jonny Comes Marching Home' started up, as Aroma sat patiently, and begin to treat her knee with ice, as Pressure Point continued.
