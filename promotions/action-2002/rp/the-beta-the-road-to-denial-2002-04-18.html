The Beta: The Road To: Denial

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/02 It’s two in the morning and I haven’t left the arena. People must think I am weird. I don’t care. No…let me rephrase that… I DON’T GIVE A SHIT! There. I got that off my fucking chest. I felt a rebirth in that ring tonight. After the former Rashard Clark spilled tears of frustration… I came. Syphon Fission came. Syphon Fission stopped crying in that ring. Syphon Fission knocked out that piece of clusterfuck shit referee with all his might. Syphon Fission grabbed that shovel. Syphon Fission maimed that fucking poser zebra with it. Syphon Fission decked a standing Joey Malone with said shovel. Syphon Fission KEPT HITTING MALONE WITH THAT SHOVEL! Twenty… Fifty… One Hundred… I lost count of all the shots I gave him. Syphon Fission was stopped by Keri Lindum. Syphon Fission crushed her skull with the same chair that opened Clark up. Thanks in part to the shovel that left her husband a bloody and concussed heap…a waste on the mat. Syphon Fission left to his retreat in the locker room…after all the garbage that hit him as he walked up that ramp. Rashard Clark died there. He was merely the vessel. He is too weak to beat Malone. He cared too damn much. Fucking ignorant shit. Malone used him… HE RAPED HIM! Through his crimes…he created us. He created a new God. Thanks to him…the dream of Rashard Clark has become a reality. No more pussy games. No more fucking back and forth battles. From here…it’s all Fission. Clark was too weak… HE’S DEAD! Chaos is only beginning…HA! Compared to what is in store… Chaos is a fucking joke. My name’s Syphon Fission… I AM ACTION WRESTLING!
