Mind Games, The Travel Edition of; IV

28 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/28/02 'And what would you like, Jimmy?' asked the very familiar voice of Kayden Paulton, who was decked out in a Santa outfit. The eight-year-old brown-haired boy, on Paulton's lap, answered with much enthusiasm, 'I'd like a firetruck!' A big smile crept across Kayden's face, underneath his fake mustache and over the fake white beard. 'A firetruck?' he exclaimed excitedly. 'That's great! I had a firetruck when I was just a wee boy, too. I must say, you have made my dad little Jimmy.' Jimmy put his arms as best he could around Kayden's stomach(which had been padded a bit to give the impression that he was a tubby man), and gave him a squeeze. 'Ah! Thanks Jimmy!' Jimmy jumped down from Kayden's lap and waved at him(Spike Dudley-style), and walked over to his mother. It was then that we could see the great set that Kayden was on. The local mall had really done an excellent job on their Santa Clause display. A large house stood behind the large throne of a seat Paulton sat at. Candy canes for archways down the snowy path(really cotton and LOTS of it), with large toys scattered about. Two elves standing guard to the left and right of the entrance. Each handing out candy canes for the young tikes. A large sleigh sat out by the house, large sacks filled with god knows what inside it. If that hadn't been enough, in the background of it all, a large Christmas tree stood. It's very tip nearly touched the high ceilings of the mall. All around the base of the tree were presents. Children and adults alike stood in awe of it all as they made their way around the path towards Santa. The line stretched for what seemed like miles. But as Jimmy walked away with his mother, someone else. Someone more familiar than even Kayden Paulton stood. He arms folded in front of him. An evil scowl on his face. His upper lip curling as the happy children walked away, telling their parents all about their meeting with Santa and what they had asked for. Max Danger just loathed the scene. It had, for years, been his favorite holiday. But now, he couldn't stand it. It disgusted him. Why? 'The Danger Man' himself probably didn't even really know for certain. It was just a feeling he had. He just couldn't bring himself to be cheery and happy. Even with getting back together with his wife and tag team partner. 'Right you are, Ms. Valerie! A jolly red nose, I do have!' shouted 'Santa' Kayden from his grand perch as a little blonde girl, no older than six, kissed him on the nose. 'And don't you worry, I foresee a great, white, pony in your future.' Valerie's mother didn't much care for that, as she stared disapprovingly at Paulton. He winced and gave the 'I'm sorry' expression. But it wasn't long before he had another child jumping up into his lap. This time it was a much larger one. One rather large boy, who instantly whipped out a long bit of parchment and began to read aloud his list. 'I want a PlayStation 2. An X-Box. A new computer. A 35-inch flatscreen television. A brand-new DVD player. Hockey sticks. Basket...' The voice trailed off into the distance as Max Danger began to walk away from the scene and towards the opening of the candy cane-arched walkway, where kids upon kids stood, waiting anxiously, some slightly scared, for their chance to meet Santa Clause. Danger slipped in behind two twin girls. He bent down where his face was between them and he whispered, 'You realize, of course, that that man up there, is not Santa Clause.' They both looked at Max, horror struck. But he continued, 'He is nothing but a great fraud, tricking kids into believing that they will receive, on Christmas morning, everything that they tell him they want. But that is not the case.' The girls were nearly in tears, but Danger just tredged onward, 'His only intentions are to disappoint you. He knows that you won't be receiving nearly any of your wishes, and loves that fact. He enjoys bringing pain and misfortune to others. I know, because he has done it to me.' Max then stood up straight. He didn't look down at the girls. Their eyes were filled with tears, their faces looking as though they had just been told Santa Clause didn't exist(oh, wait...). Max walked on past them, as the girls' eyes followed him, hoping beyond hope that what he had said wasn't truthful. But they knew that adults wouldn't steer them wrong, that's just the way it was. They looked at each other and both let out a great scream. 'AAAAAAAAAAAAAH!!!!!!' Their parents, who had been standing back talking to another couple, had come running over. They scooped up their children, who were sobbing uncontrollably. As they made their way out of line and back through the mall, they looked up at 'Santa' Kayden, who sat, lap empty, looking at them, frowning. But they just let out a much greater scream, one which would have made a deaf man hear again. Kayden just looked over at his elves, who shrugged their shoulders and shouted, 'Next!'. What Kayden was expecting was a kid, around the range of nine or ten. But unfortunately, that kid had 'left' the line. In his place stood a six-foot tall, two-hundred eight-pound, shaggy-haired, blue-eyed, sneering, twenty-five year-old man. A man who he knew fairly well. 'MAX!' Kayden shouted full of cheer. 'What are you doing here?' Danger just walked up to Paulton, staring him dead in the eyes, and a wicked smile crept across his face. Kayden was smiling back at him, but in a more playful and happy way. Max did not speak to Kayden. He just spun on his heels and faced the huge hoard of children and parents. 'EVERYONE!' he shouted, drawing their attention. Kayden tugged at the back of Danger's leather jacket, but Max didn't pay it any attention. He just kept looking out at his audience. 'I have something to say to you all here.' Kayden smiled brightly, obviously thinking Max had come down, knowing Paulton was there, and wanted to wish everyone a very Merry Christmas. Of course, he was dead wrong. 'This man is a fraud!' The children gasped. Kayden just looked on. The parents quickly tried to settle their kids down. 'He is NOT Santa Clause! Nor is he an assistant or helper of the man!' The only defense the parents had to calm their children down('No, he isn't Santa, he's a helper though. He relays the wishes of the children to him at the North Pole.' they always said when this particular situation arose.) was gone. Max Danger stood there, looking out at all the children, unable to fight back his smile. 'This man... he's... EVIL!!!!' The kids screamed out in horror. A few fainted. Danger just continued on, this time walking to the side of the great throne on which Paulton sat. 'This man doesn't even have a beard!' He ripped off the white beard that had been placed on Kayden. The children had begun to cry uncontrollably, as Paulton just stood there, watching as the parents herded up their kids. 'He's not fat, either!' Danger pulled the rubber fat suit out from underneath Kayden's suit. He tossed it aside as the entire line was running away from the candy cane-arched walkway. Security was beginning to come forward. 'He's a theif! He has stolen from me, many times.' His eyes looked deep in Kayden's. He turned and saw the security officers walking up from behind the house. Danger smiled his evil smile. 'This man... he's.... he's.... I don't have the words to describe the torture he's inflicted upon families all over the world. I've seen him. Going from home to home, acting as a broken down man, with neither food nor money. He is let into these homes, because we all show weakness to those less fortunate than us, and then he takes. He takes and takes and takes until there is nothing left to take. Leaving the families broken and sometimes.... DEAD!' Danger was really not making much sense, though he rarely did. He looked at the last remaining children(teenagers were laughing hysterically at the whole scene). 'What I'm trying to say, kids, is that Santa Clause is not real.' He said it in such a calm way(while everything else was shouted and said with great hate), that the kids just stared, mouths open, tears streaming from their eyes, the parents cursing him. They would definitely have emotional problems as they grew older. Danger then turned to Kayden, who was watching the entire scene. Parents carrying their crying children away. Security grabbing ahold of Danger and dragging him away. Teenagers rolling on the floor with laughter. Other adults glaring up at him and at Max. He said but one thing, 'Wow...' Indeed.
