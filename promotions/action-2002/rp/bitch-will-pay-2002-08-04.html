Bitch Will Pay

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/4/02 . . . Reed Young's Office | 15:45PM CST . . . MAX DANGER Nice banner. MAX DANGER, who's sitting in a leather chair with his wife JESSICA in a chair beside him and holding an ice pack on the back of his neck, is remarking on the large banner behind REED YOUNG's desk. The banner reads 'Jim Johnson and PIW SUCK!'. REED YOUNG (Chuckling slightly.) Yeah... It's true, too. DANGER I wouldn't know, Mr. Young, I wouldn't know. YOUNG Oh, just call me Re.. no, Mr. Young is fine. I kinda like that. Reed Young offers a cheesy smile while Max just gives him a half-hearted smile back. Reed then winks at Jessica, who gives him a grossed out look. DANGER Right. Well the reason I asked to meet with you, is because I need a favor. YOUNG Yeah? Well I think it may be too early to give you a raise, but if that's what you- DANGER (Cutting Young off.) No, I don't want a- JESSICA (Cutting Max off, and whispering to him.) Xander, honey, he's offering more money. Might as well take it. Max nods his approval. He turns back to Young. DANGER How much did you have in mind? YOUNG How much what? DANGER Money. For the raise? Remember? We were just talking about it. YOUNG I don't remember money being brought up. So, did you want your tag team to have a match on Pressure Point next week or something? Max looks at Jessica, who just shrugs her shoulders and shakes her head. Max turns back to Young, figuring it best to just push past that questioning and just get to why he wanted the meeting. DANGER I want a match with Sweet Aroma. YOUNG Yeah? I thought you two had a nice match last week. You two make a good team... and a cute couple. Max gives him a look that says 'What the fuck are you talking about?'. Then it hits him. DANGER Oh, I think you mean Angelica Dawson. We worked together last week. We made the cute couple. Jessica hits Max on the arm. DANGER That's what he said. I don't really like her that much... or at all really. YOUNG So you want Sweet Aroma next week? DANGER Yeah, that's pretty much the ideal. The phone then suddenly rings. YOUNG Excuse me, while I take this call. Max and Jessica both just nod, as Young picks up the telephone. YOUNG Hello? Hi! YOUNG How much time off? YOUNG Alright. A few weeks it is. YOUNG Toodles. Reed Young hangs up the phone. Jessica and Max give each other a look, wondering how gay Young must be for using the word toodles. YOUNG So, where were we? DANGER Um, you were about to grant me a match with Sweet Aroma. YOUNG Right. Well before I do, may I ask why you want to face her? DANGER She damn near re-broke my neck, sir! I want to make that bitch pay! YOUNG Well, as long as you have a good reason. You may have your match! DANGER Thank you. Max and Jessica stand up. Young does the same. They all shake hands. Max and Jessica leave the room. Young looks at his sign hanging behind his desk, then pushes a button on the telephone. YOUNG Ms. Peters, get the banner guy in here. My 'DICK' is missing. FADE OUT
