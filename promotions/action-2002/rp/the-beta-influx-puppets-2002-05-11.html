The Beta: Influx: Puppets

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I kept her there before me. I beat her. I maimed her. She tried to scream through a gag. Nobody heard her screams. I locked her up in an abandoned building in Mesa. Fitting. I had to transport her over all this terrain, having drugged her up and causing blackouts thanks to Payback. She is trying to cry, but I have officially numbed her in fear. Fear that I might end her WRETCHED life. What fear I must exhort. I live for the day when I show Malone exactly what I am all about. He is too naïve to know the true outcome of what is to come months down the road. Nobody does. This is just another jigsaw piece to the puzzle. I am winning this thing. I could die right now and I would win. Not to say I won’t. I kind of with I could DIE. I took her gag out of her mouth. She tried to remove herself from her bondage, but I merely laughed. My eyes, with rings of sleep deprivation tattooed around them, looked deep in her soul. She sounded so damn pathetic. “Please…please…” “Stop this Rashard…” “I am dying Rashard…” “YOU’RE KILLING ME!” “LET ME GO…PLEASE…OH GOD PLEASE…” I merely kiss her. She tries to relent, but for some reason, I win her over. JUST LIKE HIGH SCHOOL! I bite that lower lip. I taste blood. I swallow. I hear her muffled scream. How erotic… I put the gag back in, looking at the blood flow. She passes out from the pain. Or was that blood loss. She has quite a bit of it. So I sit back in my chair. I wait for her to come to. I dream to play this game again with my prisoner. That’s when Payback reminds me of something. Joey’s friend, Daniel Phillips. He is out there somewhere…probably drinking. You remember the bar you three got drunk at when you were on some off time all that time ago? I do indeed. He is there. Go pay him a visit. I rose up from my seat, and exited my Heaven. I plunged into this Hell. Chaos is only the beginning.
