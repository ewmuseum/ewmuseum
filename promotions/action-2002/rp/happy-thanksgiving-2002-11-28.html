Happy Thanksgiving

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/02 The house was filled with the smell of a delicious meal. Turkey, mashed potatoes, corn, biscuits, and any and everything you would expect to be at a Thanksgiving dinner. Around the table, sat the crew. A very different bunch. The majority of them didn't even like each other. But there they were, celebrating Thanksgiving together. A new wooden table had been bought by Samantha the day previous, and had been delivered earlier that day. The living room was cleared out, all the chairs, couches, and tables were moved to the back wall, around the entertainment center. The table ran from the kitchen bar/counter top, which was open to the kitchen, to the center of the living room. The food was being passed around. Everyone seemed to be happy. Having a good time. Samantha sat at the head of the table. The matriarch of the 'family'. That's what they were acting as. Not everyone within a family liked one another. There were always issues between family members. At the other end of the table, Conrad Ramsey sat. He and Samantha eyed each other. Completely unbeknownst to Max. He was just trying to get along. Trying to act happy. But there were problems. Just wasn't the time to bring any of it up. He sat to the right of his mother. Next to him was Iceman, Mo Ron, and Homeless Gus. On the left of Samantha was the daughter-in-law, Jessica. Next to her was Blast and Bradley Duncan. There was much talking going on. Several conversations. Bradley Duncan, like Danger, was being quiet, though it wasn't his fault really. He kinda had to be. 'Thiff if a graff meaff, mom!' said Blast, stuffing his face with turkey, a biscuit, and mashed potatoes. Samantha smiled and thanked Blast. But told him not to talk with his mouth full. 'Then he'll never talk,' remarked Mo Ron, with a hint of hate in the tone, though everyone laughed at the comment. They continued talking. Neither Jessica nor Max would look at each other. There was more love shooting back and forth between Conrad and Samantha than the actual married couple. Max started to slump in his chair and play with his food. 'Don't play with your food, honey.' Max looked up. Jessica and Samantha were looking at each other. They had said it at the exact same time. They smiled slightly and then turned to Danger. He chuckled to himself and just shook his head. 'So, how are you two holding up?' mom asked. The entire table went silent. Iceman pulled his spoon of his nose, Blast swallowed a chuck of turkey without chewing, Duncan was drinking when Conrad drew his attention. Gus was snatching up other people's food, while they looked. Mo Ron had just stopped what he was doing. Silence. Not the best thing to have at a Thanksgiving dinner. Not when everything was going so well. Max and Jessica looked at each other. Neither knew what to do. They just looked at each other, then at Samantha. She was swallowing her food, and looking back and forth between the two, waiting for an answer. It hadn't been that long into the dinner, and alright there was the awkward question, that no one was expecting to hear, and no one knew how to answer. Max finally spoke up, wanting to kill the deathly silence, 'Great, mom. We're doing great.' It was a blatant lie. They've only seen each other once outside of when they are at the arena. She had flat out walked away from him with Blast in front of a live crowd and millions watching at home. But he wanted to make his mom happy. He lied. He had to. Jessica didn't fight him on it. It wasn't until Blast spoke up that Samantha pushed further. 'She's with me now.' he had said. He had been told NOT to bring it up. But his stupidity got the better of him. Iceman gasped, as everyone else just went quiet again. Samantha stared down Jessica. 'Is this true?' she asked, sternly. The softness of her voice never wavered. She was still the calm woman. But there was a sternness to it. Her eyes would break anyone. She was burning a hole through her daughter-in-law. Luckily, Jessica didn't have to answer anything. Her husband did, 'Everything is fine. We are still married. We're just...' 'Just what?' she demanded. Danger sat silent. He wasn't sure what to do next. This time Jessica came to the rescue, 'We're just taking time apart. That's all.' That's when the shit hit the fan. 'WHAT!?' Blast yelled out. 'I thought you loved me!' He jumped up. Jessica remained seated. Iceman came up next. Things were coming to a head right then and there. 'Love? She doesn't love you. She's just using you! Anyone can see that! I've been trying to tell you that since day one. But NOOOOOO, we won't listen to best buddy Iceman. Nope, no need for that.' Blast fired back, 'Right. Best buddy. You've been jealous of Jessica ever since we got together! You were so lonely that you went and got another friend.' He pointed at Mo Ron. He didn't respond. He was caught off guard. 'Yeah, and he's been a better friend than you ever would be!' Iceman folded his arms in front of him and spun around, turning his back to Blast and the table. Samantha was directing all her comments to the married ones. 'Why? That's all I want to know. Why?' she asked the two of them. There was no chance of an answer. Blast had flung some mashed potatoes off his spoon and hit Iceman in the back of the head. His hand went up, touching the back of his head, and pulled off the potatoes. 'Who did it!? TELL ME WHO DID THIS!?' he screamed out. Blast pointed at Bradley Duncan. Duncan wasn't paying attention to anything that was going on. He just continued to eat. He picked up his glass of water, and Iceman threw a roll. It hit his glass, spilling it all over him. He looked up. Iceman nearly pissed himself right there. All it took was a look. Duncan stood up, as Ramsey did the same thing. He began to sign to him. 'Let it go, Bradley. Just let it go.' Duncan sneered. He was ready to beat some ass. He's had a lot of pent of frustrations lately and needed to just hit someone. Conrad lead Duncan away from the table, calming him with signing. 'That's one reason,' said Jessica, facing Max, and completely forgetting about Samantha. 'One reason, what?' Max replied, wondering what the hell she was talking about. 'You've known Bradley for years. YEARS! And you couldn't take it upon yourself to learn sign language. Conrad learned. He wanted to be his friend. You, though. oh, you've been a terrible...' Danger jumped up. 'I don't want to hear it!' Jessica stood up. They were steaming. Iceman and Blast had begun to fling food at each other. Mo Ron caught hit with an errant corn cob, and began to join in. It was Iceman and Mo Ron versus Blast. Conrad Ramsey returned to the living room. Food was flying everywhere. Jessica and Max were shouting at each other. It was so loud, that no one could make out what exactly they were saying. Ramsey was trying to calm them down, but they weren't having any of it. Danger had his palms on the table and was leaning forward. Jessica the same. They were nose to nose, continuing to shout. In the middle of all this, Gus was continuing to eat. He snatched some food out of the air and shoved it right into his mouth. Iceman ran to the counter top and picked up the pumpkin pie. One of four pies they were going to have for dessert. He chucked it over Samantha's head, and right over Jessica and Max. It connected with the side of Blast's head. Iceman then rolled on the floor and pulled down his chair, using it as a shield. Blast was throwing the food fast and furious. Mo Ron put his chair down beside Iceman's and they ducked low, out of the line of fire. 'You're going to pay, fat man!' Iceman shouted. Blast steamed. He grabbed the entire bowl of gravy. He chucked it. The bowl shattered as it hit the chair. Gravy went everywhere. On the tops of Iceman and Mo Ron's heads, and all over the white carpeting. Jessica and Max had stopped the yelling and now were just staring, menacingly at each other. Samantha slowly got up. Conrad went over to stop the food fight. Iceman got himself up. He dove over the table and tackled Blast to the floor! They started to roll around, the food that covered them, rubbing off onto the carpet. Conrad just threw his arms up in the air. There was nothing he could do and he wasn't much caring anymore. He just plopped down in a chair that rested against the wall, out of the way. Gus continued eating. Blast and Iceman were all over the floor, scratching and pulling hair like a bunch of girls. Bradley Duncan came back out. He stepped over Iceman, who was on top of Blast, flailing his arms with his eyes closed. Little girlish hits. He walked past the table and sat down beside Conrad. Gus continued to eat. Mo Ron had pulled himself up. He had begun to cheer on Iceman. Danger and Jessica didn't move, just stared. Intense. He snapped his hand up and grabbed the back of her head. He pulled her forward and began to kiss her. Not gentle kissing, more rough. Jessica started kissing back. They shoved things down the table, or off it, they didn't much know where it was going. Both were on their knees on top of the table, passionately kissing one another. Their hands were all over the place. It was utter chaos. There was a crying sound. Sounded likely crying anyway. Slowly, from beneath her hands, Samantha puled her face. She was laughing hysterically. Uncontrollably. Everyone began to stop. Conrad was looking over, Duncan had seen the madness of Samantha's face and was looking, strangely. Mo Ron had stopped cheering on Iceman, who was still on top of Blast. Iceman jerked his head around, and Blast rose his head from the ground. All were looking at Samantha. Mo Ron slapped Gus in the back of the head, getting him to stop eating, and even he looked. Max and Jessica stopped kissing. Their heads still together and turned simultaneously to face mom. Both with weird looks. 'Oh...(laughter)...my...(laugher)...god!' she was having trouble speaking between all the laughing. 'This...(laughter)...is just...(laughter)....like the...(laughter)...Thanks...(laughter)...giving...(laughter)...we used to...(laughter)...have when...(laughter)...you were a...(laughter)....kid, Marcus.' She took a deep breath. Still laughing inside, but not so much on the outside. She looked up and around at everyone, who were giving her strange looks. She smiled and said, 'What?'
