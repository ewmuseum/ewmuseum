I'm With Conrad Ramsey!!!

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/02 It is just after Pressure Point 7, and inside the red rental car are Conrad Ramsey and Max Danger. They are headed to the airport. In the passenger seat, Max Danger is smiling from ear to ear, then he gets a look on his face like he just remembered something. He kinda leans backwards, pushing his pelvic region forward and digs into his front right pocket. He pulls out his checkbook and flips to behind the checks and deposit slips, and thumbs through the cash there. Behind the plastic guard used to seperate checks when writing them, are photos. Max pulls out three photos and turns to Conrad. Max Danger: Hey, buddy, I forgot to show you these earlier. Take a look. Conrad Ramsey: I don't know if you've realized this or not, but I am trying to drive. So, if you don't want to die tonight, I suggest you wait until we've reached the airport to show me anything. Max seems dejected at first, and slowly puts away the photos. He keeps the checkbook out on his lap, then a thought pops into his head. Max Danger: Yeah, I guess you're right. It's bad enough that you have to deal with old age when driving, as your eyes and ears, and just general coordination skills are slipping, so it's best not to hinder those further with you having to look at photos of my beautiful wife and child. Conrad sneers and kinda tilts his head to the left, and says something to himself. Max just nods his head like he knows he said the right thing, as Conrad just shakes his head and has a look of disdain on his face now. There is a few minutes of awkward silence until Max breaks it, predictably. Max Danger: So, where are you headed to? Conrad Ramsey: Whereever you aren't. Max Danger: Haha, funny. That's a good one. But seriously, where are you headed? Conrad Ramsey: Home. Max Danger: Where's that? Conrad Ramsey: Colorado. Boulder, Colorado. My new house. Max Danger: Cool. Cool. Conrad looks on the side of the road and sees that the airport is on the next exit. He adjusts himself in his seat as Max is staring at him and acting as if he's waiting for Conrad to say something. Unfortunately for Max he doesn't, so he has to break the silence again. Max Danger: Yeah, I'm going to Jersey. That's New Jersey. Gonna work a show there. Just a couple hundred bucks, nothing much, but it'll sure help out my family. Conrad Ramsey: Right, so was I supposed to care? Because I don't. Max Danger: You're so silly. I bet, some fifty, sixty years ago you got all the chicks, didn't ya? Just so alive and vibrant. You're a very charismatic man, Conrad Ramsey. Conrad Ramsey: I'll be sure to let you know when I give a flying- Max Danger cuts him off before he can finish that. Luckily for Max, he wasn't really listening to what Conrad was saying. Max Danger: You remind me of Bradley Duncan. You're both so charismatic and likable. But you can hear and talk, unlike him. Right? You can hear and talk, can't ya? Conrad can't believe the stupidity that is Max Danger, though he's thinking it's just a ploy. But he slaps his forehead anyway. Max Danger: Fly? Yeah, I hate it when they land on my forehead, I'm always squashing 'em, and I get fly guts all over me. Rather disgusting. Max looks over at Conrad's forehead and makes a face that says 'alright, clean head, good job'. Max Danger: But it looks as if you made a clean kill. Niiiiiiiice. Conrad just shakes his head in disbelief and turns off the expressway and onto the airport exit. Max Danger: I hate saying goodbye, and it looks like in a few minutes I will have to. Conrad Ramsey: Don't say it, just go. It'll be easier on both of us if we just part ways. You can just feel the sarcasm dripping off that statement. Max doesn't lead on that he notices it though. Conrad heads down the road and pulls into the airport parking lot. He just parks at the nearest open space, and hops out of the car. Max follows him out quickly, grabbing his bag from the back seat and catching up to Conrad. Max Danger: I guess we'll see each other next Wednesday, eh? I mean, if you don't die between now and then, of course. Conrad Ramsey: Right now, I'm thinking that wouldn't be such a bad thing. Max Danger: There you go again. You're a funny guy, and that's why I like ya. Conrad Ramsey: Whatever. Conrad Ramsey heads through the front doors and quickly heads off to his gate, of course he looked up to see where his gate was. Max Danger is behind him quite a bit, so he screams out. Max Danger: I LOVE YOU, CONRAD RAMSEY!!!! Conrad doesn't stop moving, he just keeps going as everyone looks at Max. Max waves goodbye to Conrad, as everyone know carries on with their business. Max Danger: There's no sense in saying goodbye now, I'll just go to Conrad's and stay there until next week's Pressure Point. We'll really get to know each other now! Max's face is beaming as he runs off to get a ticket change.
