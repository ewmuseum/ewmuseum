Going Home Again.

3 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was mid-morning on the Monday following 'Til Death Do Us Part. Sitting alone in the lobby, on the dark green couch, was Conrad Ramsey, sipping his black coffee. In front of him were his bags and in the lap of his crossed legs was a folded newspaper. He had yet to peer at it as he watched people coming and going in the lobby, taking sip after sip of his morning wake-me-up. He had said goodbyes to several other Action! performers as they bustled out of the hotel, heading to the company's next stop, or even home. Conrad wasn't sure where that was for him yet. So he sat there, just glancing about, not really paying attention to anything too closely. He checked his watch and noticed that it was nearly 10:00am. As Ramsey finally put down his mug of coffee and began to unfold the newspaper, the gold elevator doors sprung open in front of him. Holding hands, Xander and Jessica Dox emerged, carrying their bags with their free arms. 'Conrad!' Jessica exclaimed as she noticed him sitting there. 'Good morning!' He smiled slightly. Xander was pulled with Jessica as she headed over to him. Ramsey put down his paper and returned her greetings, 'Yes, I suppose it is a good morning.' He looked out through the windows and glass doors, as the sun was shining brightly. 'I take it you two are heading out, then?' he said, looking at their bags. Jessica nodded, 'Yeah. We've just got to check out, and then we're off.' Ramsey gave an 'oh' expression with a slight head nod. He then picked up his burning hot coffee again and blew away from of the steam before taking another sip. He let it slide down his throat before asking, 'So are you headed straight to Canada or...' Xander chimed in this time, 'Home. We're going home, actually.' He had said it a bit more harshly than he had intended. Jessica gave him a look and Xander then continued, 'Our stuff was just delivered there, I mean. Have loads of unpacking to do.' Ramsey just nodded again, 'Oh. We'll, good luck with that.' He smiled slightly and Xander did the same, 'Yeah, I own far too much stuff. Plus, with mom's things...' He trailed off, obviously not wanting to head down that path this early in the morning. Conrad had perked up at that mention and stared at Xander. The Carnage Champion shook his head, 'We've got a lot of rearranging to do, is all.' Cue the uncomfortable silence. 'So, yeah, I'm just going to get us checked out, Jess,' Xander said to break the silence. 'Okay, honey,' she replied. He then walked off towards the front counter. Jessica put her hands into her back pockets and began to bounce up and down on the heels and toes of her feet, watching her husband. 'It's great,' Conrad started. 'The love you two show for each other. I can't tell you how happy it makes me to see two young people in such happiness.' Jessica had turned and looked at the old man. She smiled to him. She wasn't sure if she should be flaunting her and Xander's love and happiness in front of Ramsey. But he had brought it up. 'Thank you,' she said and before she could stop herself, 'I honestly don't know what we would have done if we hadn't had each other after Samantha's death. But luckily...' She clammed up quickly and stared at Conrad like she had just done something very bad. 'I'm so sorry,' she pleaded. He shook his head and waved his hand, 'Don't be. I'm glad. It's important to have someone. I'm just happy that you two were able to get through it.' He then took one finally chug of his coffee and let out a refreshing sigh, 'I'm dealing. It's hard, ya know? But I'm doing alright. I've even began talking with Julius again, after years of bitter silence.' Jessica smiled, 'It's good that you're starting to socialize again. Get some friends and just talk about things.' 'Yeah,' he said, looking away. 'Say, have you seen Bradley anywhere? I was hoping to chat with him.' Xander was returning at that moment and quickly shook her head to Conrad and, with her eyes, told him to not mention it again. She then turned to her husband, 'Hey, honey!' 'No, that isn't suspicious at all,' he told his wife with a sly smile. 'What's going on?' She shook her head, 'Nothing.' He looked at her hard and she put on her most innocent face. Xander just shrugged. 'So, are you ready to go?' She nodded, 'Yeah, I suppose so.' She began picking up her bags and then looked back to Conrad, 'Bye, Conrad. We'll talk to you soon.' 'Goodbye, Jessica,' he replied. 'Have a safe trip.' She then threw her bag over her shoulder as Xander threw his own over his. He then grabbed her hand and gave a head nod to Conrad as a goodbye. Ramsey returned it and sighed. He was now all alone. As he sat on the couch, Xander and Jessica were heading through the front doors of the hotel. Jessica turned to her husband, 'Conrad doesn't know what happened to Bradley either.' She looked concerned. Worried. Xander looked at her, the same concern etched across his face. He then looked away and shook his head. Where had Duncan gone following the PPV?
