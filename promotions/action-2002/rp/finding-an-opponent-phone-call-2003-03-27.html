Finding An Opponent... Phone Call

27 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Ring, Ring, Ring) 'Come on pick up, this could be a great opportunity for you.' (Ring, Ring, Ring) 'Come on, pick up, I know you're there.' (Ring, Ring...) 'Hello???' 'Hey hunny, how are things going with the contract talks.' 'Fine baby. I should be back on the tub in a few weeks, maybe even days if this guy hurries up and gives me what I want.' 'Have patience Jonny, you're one of the biggest free agents on the market right now, hell people have been asking me if I was going to try to recruit you into Action!' 'You know I hate that place V.' 'I know, but KroW's back, and I know you and him have some unfinished business; and right now he's up on his high horse, because everyone found out that he didn't kill Showcase.' 'Wait? 'Showcase' Sean Cylear, the man whose brother hightailed it out of PIW when he found out I was the new star. That Showcase?' 'Yeah.' 'He was better off dead than going to that rathole.' 'Hey I happen to wrestle in that rathole!' 'I'm sorry, but you know someone the caliber of JCON can't be seen in a federation headed up by an idiot like Reed Young, who somehow surrounded himself with all those PIW rejects. And besides you don't wrestle there, you MANAGE!' 'If you don't stop with the insults, I'll have to manage to not invite you to Juggernaut for your birthday!' 'Oh great, the NonWrestlemania of Action! I saw the first one with you backstage remember, and it sucked more than KroW winning the PIW Title. And what's with that Keith Scott Zimmerwoman, what's stuck up his ass?' 'I'm kinda of hoping your foot will be. That's why I'm calling.' 'Sorry babe, would if I could, but when I showed up at Winter Warfare in support of Romey, I almost killed off all negotiations with quite a few dollars on the table... not as much as my PIW or j0lt deals, but its the most this fed can afford to give me, for atleast right now. So unfortunately I can't help you out.' 'Not even for little old me???' 'Maybe I'll see that Y2-Reject somewhere down the line, but right now my hands are tied. Sorry babe.' 'That's okay, I guess I'll have to go to plan B.' 'So we still on for this weekend?' 'I guess, as long as that idiot Keith isn't wrestling on Whatever.' 'That show still exist???!... Nevermind this is Reed Young we're talkin' bout. Listen babe I gotta run, hopefully I'll see you this weekend. Love ya. 'Love you too.' And with that the conversation ended, and Aroma wasn't sure if KSZ would be pleased that he wouldn't be wrestling Jonathan Conspiracy, or if he'd be in a 'midjerk' mood after Aroma couldn't close the deal with her fiancee.
