Cups: cVI: Cup of Life

20 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 20, 2002 Cups: cVI: Cup of Life 'She honestly needs your help, James.' The man had a look of deep desperation on his face. Madison peered into his eyes endlessly and located his thoughts. 'She's almost gone. Her final request was to see you.'          James slammed his hand onto the steering wheel and drove off. Not wondering, he asked no more questions. 'Does she need the cup?'          'It's nearly that bad, though, don't speak of that matter. There may be some helping to her.' James didn't care what the man said. He floored the vehicle. 'You'd better take it easy, or you're going to be laying next to her again.'          Once more, the vehicle was stopped. James stared resonantly into him once more. A glaring grin took refuge upon his face as he dynamically stuck a stern middle finger directly between his eyes. Swiftly, he turned his attention back around and concentrated on the road. The darkened night skies made it harder to drive than normal. He squinted in the darkness and road on, worried about what was to happen. The life of a girl was obviously in his hands. All he wanted to do... was see her.          Time clicked by the slowest that it ever had for James Madison that night. He continued to peek in the rear view to make sure that the man in the back wasn't trying anything weird again. Then, he was all eyes on the road. The minutes seemed like hours, even though the hospital was only a little over an hour drive away. The radio was silent ... then it jarred on. He listened to the words of a local band as they echoed through his head. Nothing mattered to him, he was on a mission. Leaving the local area and going into the bigger city, just for an attempt.          'You shock me by seeing you at ringside, you jump me on the river's edge, you meet me in my car. You tell me all of this shit. Something is up with you.' James eyed the rear-view once more as the man smiled.          'Why do you say that? Can't an old friend help you?'          James ignored him and drove on. 'I have no friends. No one has ever trusted a word I said. I work off of people, they make the mistakes, I move in.'          'I already know that. You'd have trouble telling me something about you that I don't already know. The studies that we carry out are pretty hardcore. Trust me on that.' James felt uneasy, but continued on.          The hospital was within sight now, and Madison was nervous. 'Well, it's time ...' - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
