Four Titles

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/05/02 PIW's Massacre had just ended. Max Danger, Jessica, and Bradley Duncan were all walking through the Compaq Center parking lot in San Jose, California. Duncan was in the rear, carrying four large bags. The lights in the lot shined of his bald head. Next in line was Jessica. Looking ravishing. By looking at her, you never would have guessed she had spent time in drug rehab. She was holding a piece of white paper in her hand, folded up. Up front, was Max Danger. 'The Danger Man'. He was covered in gold. One on each shoulder. Two around his waist. 'Damn it!' uttered Danger under his breath. 'What, honey?' asked his wife, as she walked up to him. 'I've forgotten my keys. Bradley, could you go back and get them?' Danger asked his NVC partner. Jessica had to sign what Max had said, and he just nodded. Duncan sat down the bags and was about to head off when Danger grabbed his arm. He gave him a warning. 'Don't you DARE come back without my Bubbles key-chain!' Duncan had read his lips and nodded. He headed off as Max stood there, holding all those titles. Where had he gotten so many, you ask. Well, it's really simple. October 28th, NVC took out Dynamic Duo to obtain the PIW World Tag Team Titles. Was was Danger lugging around both belts, then? Good question. Duncan didn't like how Danger had cheated. Max had pinned both Iceman and Blast despite being the illegal man. 'It's the Ref's fault, Bradley. He made the count knowing full-well that I wasn't he legal man. Blame him.' Max had told him. He wasn't going to have any of that. He fought for competition, not winning by any means necessary. That's where he and Max differed. Danger was all for cheating like a madman if it meant winning. Winning was essential. That's how that happened. October 4th, Bobby Minio and Max Danger were teamed up. Pressure Point. Sweet Aroma and Angelica Dawson defended their Dyad Titles against them... and lost. Danger made Aroma tap out after both Minio and Dawson had left the match. He had four pieces of hardware. He couldn't give Bobby his, because he hadn't seem him since he tagged himself back into the tag match on Friday. 'The Danger Man' was now 'The Danger Champ'. Love him or hate him, he produced... by any means necessary. After a hectic weekend, where NVC won the PIW Tag Titles, and then Max Danger defeated Sweet Aroma at Retaliation, it would seem things wouldn't have gotten harder for Max. It had. This weekend, he obtained the Dyad Titles from the Clit 'N Candy Express. That was Friday. Tonight, on Massacre, he and Duncan retained the gold, when Danger hit Blast with Duncan's belt, drawing the DQ, despite Duncan having the 'Dangerous V' cross-arm breaker hold on him. Now he has to prepare for Monday. The new for Action! television. Who would he face there? Would he be able to add MORE gold? Or would he find some way to get out of losing his? Either way, I'm sure it'll be an excellent capper to an already wild weekend for 'The Danger Champ'.
