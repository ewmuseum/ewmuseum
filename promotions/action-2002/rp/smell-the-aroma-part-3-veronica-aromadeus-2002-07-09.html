Smell The Aroma (Part 3 - Veronica Aromadeus)

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.09.02 Well here I am, alone in my own little world, surrounded by a bunch of crybabies. I'm hungry, I'm dirty, I have to use the bathroom. I mean come on, you'll be getting breast fed any day now, don't interrupt my beauty sleep. I mean come on, I spent nine months floating around in a bubble, I'm enjoying the fresh air. Guys shut up; here comes the nurses. Every last one of them taking care of all of our needs, and one is going around with markers, while the gleeful family members stamp their heads on the glass to peek in to see they're own bundles of joy. Wait, give me one second, here comes the 'marker nurse'. VEE - E - ARE - OH - EN - EYE - SEE - AY! AY - ARE - OH - EM - AY - DEE - YOU - ES! Veronica Aromadeus... I think I'm going to like it here.
