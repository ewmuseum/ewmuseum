More Suspicions.

1 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When Jessica Michelle Dox awoke on Saturday morning, she draggered herself out of bed at 4:00am. She was dead tired, not to mention had bed hair. Never a pleasant sight. She groggily entered the bathroom, where she did her daily morning routine: Use the toilet, brush her teeth, floss her beath, take a shower, dry off, comb her still-wet hair and put it in a towel, wrap a towel about the rest of her body, and get dressed. The outfit of choice was a very comfortable pair of jeans, and pink shirt, with red sleeves. She began to gather up her things. She had to be at the airport at 7:00am for a 7:15am flight. It was always nerve-racking preparing for a flight. As she was putting her shampoo and other toiletries into her little clear bag, her husband rolled out of bed. He was going to be seeing her off on this fine, sun-filled morning and then picking her back up the next morning. He sat on the edge of the bed and yawned. After he rubbed some sleep out of his eyes, he saw his wife pacing around in the bathroom, grabbing things that she needed. She still had to fix her hair, but it seemed to be a bit lower on her agenda at that moment. Xander got up out of bed and walked into the bathroom with his wife. He muttered in his sleepy state, 'Good morning, hun.' He leaned in for a kiss, but she was really not paying attention to him. 'Morning, babe,' she said without looking at him. She stopped once, to think about what else she needed. Then she grabbed her deoderant. 'Slow down, Jess,' Xander said. 'You've got over two hours.' She shook her head, 'Well, I don't want to forget anything, now do I?' Xander sighed, 'You packed a clean pair of underwear, right?' Jessica stopped and looked at her husband. 'Of course I did.' He shrugged as he turned around to look in the mirror, 'Well, that's all you really need then, isn't it?' She smacked her husband on the shoulder, causing him to turn around. She shook her head at him with a smile on her face. He smiled as well and then went to kiss her again. She put both hands into his chest and kept him at bay. 'Ew, no, Xand, you haven't brushed your teeth yet.' He sighed again. He then turned and grabbed his toothbrush and the toothpaste. As he brushed, Jessica left the bathroom, and was stuffing shoes into her bag. Lots of them. It was a one day stay. Wasn't even overnight. Xander leaned back from the sink and watched his wife. He shook his as he spit out the contents of his mouth and then rinsed out with water. He then exited the bathroom and watched his wife piling what was probably the sixth pair of shoes into her bag. She usually didn't pack like a girl for such things. Xander said, 'What are you going to do? Change shoes on the flight, in the cab, and during the show? Seriously, Jess, one pair is sufficient. 'Cause, really, you're only going to be there for, what, one interview and that's it, right? So, why the need for large quantities of footwear?' She didn't answer. She just kept piling various things into the bag. He still wasn't even sure why she was taking ANYTHING with her. She was going to arrive in Louisiana just as the afternoon began, and would be leaving there at 11:27pm the very same night. No hotel, no need for clothes changing. Jessica had gone back into the bathroom and grabbed the remainder of what she needed. When she stuffed that all into her bag, she stopped. Xander walked over to her and put his arms around her from behind. 'You realize you stink, right, dear?' she asked her husband. 'Yes,' he replied. 'But isn't it a rugged and handsome sort of stink?' 'Not really,' she said with a smile on her face. She then turned around, 'It's really quite gross. I think... yes... yes, I'm going to pass out.' She began to sway in his arms, as if she was going to drop to the floor. Xander seized her and held her. 'Well, guess what?' Jessica stopped the swaying and answered, 'What?' 'You stink again, too!' And then he planted a big ole kiss on her. She pushed him in the chest as he laughed. 'Guess I'm going to have to take another shower then!' 'Oh, come on, babe. Stop worrying, you're going to be around a bunch of sweaty, smelling guys all night anyway.' And, first the first time, he stopped and thought about that. She WAS going to be surrounded by a bunch of sweaty, smelly, big-muscled, small-brained, perverted, wrestlers. 'Course Bradley was going to be there with her. 'Don't worry,' she said. 'Bradley's going to be there to protect me.' Danger scoffed, 'Yeah, great comfort that.' She hadn't really heard him as she had grabbed a book out of her nightstand drawer. 'What?' she asked. He shook his head. Thankfully he didn't have to explain what he meant by that. 'Well, let's go grab some breakfast,' he said. She nodded in agreement. And together the two of them headed out of the bedroom and down the long hall. They reached the spiral staircase, wooden mind, and headed down them. Across the beautiful, spacious, living room, past the double doors leading into the pool room, and not a pool table aka billiards. No, an actual indoor swimming pool. They past those doors and then went left and into the state-of-the-art ktchen. It was massive. Xander went over to the cupboard. 'Ooooh,' said Jessica. 'I'll be right back. Gotta check for something in the laudry room.' And off she went. Marcus didn't pay it much attention. He was searching for something to eat. 'Hmmm. Apple Jacks, Honeycombs, Frosted Flakes...' he mumbled to himself. 'Or, maybe, toast? Cinnamon rolls? Yes! Cinnamon rolls!' He moved away from the cupboard and went to the futuristic-looking chrome refrigerator. He pulled one of the doors open and began to scan the contents. Finally he spotted them. Cinammon rolls. Delicious. Now, if only he knew how to make them. He frowned and then turned, 'JESSICA! COME HERE, I NEED YOU!' Jessica, meanwhile, was rummaging through the dirty laundry. Things were all sorts of scattered about. She seemed not to be able to find what she was looking for. While sitting on her knees, she huffed while she held a pair of her own underwear. (Pink lace, if you're wondering... perverts.) She then threw them down and got back up onto her feet. She headed back out of the laundry room, unhappy that she couldn't find what she was looking for. Back in the kitchen, Xander was sitting at the counter and spinning a spork around with his finger. Passing the time as boredom was fast consuming his soul. Luckily, Jessica entered then. 'Honey, have you seen my SQUEAKY CLEAN CARWASH shirt?' she asked him. He rose up and looked at her. He thought for about ten seconds and then answered, 'Nope. Haven't seen it.' 'But that's not the most pressing matter.' She looked at him strangely, 'What is?' 'Well, asides from my inability to make cinnamon rolls, there also seems to be no Bradley Duncan in this house. Or, probably in this city.' She lifted an eyebrow, 'Um... what?' 'I can't make cinnamon rolls.' She shook her head, 'No, not that. The other thing. The Bradley thing.' 'Oh, right, he's gone. Car's gone. His room hasn't been touched. I went and looked for him, see if he could make the cinnamon rolls. Not around, babe.' She sighed, 'Well... what are we going to do then? Should I go anyway, just in case?' Xander stood up and shrugged. 'Well, personally, I wouldn't. But it's your call. But, again, if it were me, I'd stay and make cinnamon rolls. And of course, all those 'I's I'm using... I mean you.' He smiled. She looked kinda disappointed, Jessica did. She then just threw her arms into the air, 'Alright, cinnamon rolls it is.' Her husband smiled from ear to ear, which made her do the same. 'Though, it is weird,' she said. 'Duncan leaving. Plus, my shirt. Where the hell was that at?' 'Kinda like the underwear you lost, remember? Maybe we have some underpants gnomes in the house. 'Course, wouldn't explain the missing shirt, unless... yeah, that's it... they're just expanding their business. Underpants and shirts.' Jessica looked at him and shook her head, 'You have got to be the biggest dork I have ever met.' Xander smiled proudly. She then tippy-toed and kissed him on the cheek, 'But that's why I love ya.' Of course, Xander's underpants gnome theory was just an afront for his growing suspicions of Bradley. Comes in, wants Jessica to go with him to another state, at the bottom of the country, then leaves and she has a shirt missing? Very odd.
