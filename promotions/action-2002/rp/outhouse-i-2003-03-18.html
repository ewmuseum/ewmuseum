Outhouse: I

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dallas, Texas. In one of the less than graceful establishments in the city, a certain red-headed embarassment to the human race was sitting as close as humanly possible to a small group of girls seated in the corner of the place, attempting to talk one of them into giving him a lap dance. Unfortunately, he was far too rowdy, and on top of that, he spoke in an obnoxious Mexican accent. 'C'mon, bebe! Give de `ol Sanchis a lap dance, holmes!' Sanchis 'Su Idolo' Guilherme, former 21st Century Wrestling Cruiserweight, Pacific, and United Champion, having been cast out of the federation by Kellen Kinkade, had been reduced to trying to get lap dances from a bunch of college girls that wanted nothing to do with him. So, of course, he was failing absolutely miserably. 'Go away.' one of the girls told him. 'Chu know chu want de Sanchis! De Sanchis iz de icon among icons, bebe! Hembras want to BE with de Sanchis, while de men, dey want to BE de Sanchis!' Sanchis said, thrusting his pelvis with every use of his name. 'You're disugsting.' the second girl said, trying to pretend that she didn't know this guy. 'Cha! Chu reject de Sanchis?!' Sanchis said, having to blink at least five times to try to register that his 'brilliant pickup lines' weren't working, 'Fine! De Sanchis, `e `azn't det inter'sted in chu hembras, anyway!' So, Sanchis turned around, walked about five steps, and tapped another girl on the shoulder. She turned around, and a look of disgust immediately came over her face before Sanchis even spouted off his pickup line. '`Ey, bebe! Chu wearin' de mirror pants, holmes? `Cuz de Sanchis, `e can see `emself in them!' The girl 'accidently' splashed her drink into Sanchis' face, and stormed away from him, leaving Sanchis to yell out at her. 'EH, BEBE! CHU GONNA PAY FOR DE SANCHIS' COSTLY SHIRT!' he yelled, wiping away the wine from his ten dollar 'Breatholizer: Blow Here' shirt that he borrowed from that Steve-O guy and never gave back. No matter. There were many fish in the sea. He turned to another girl. '`Ey, bebe! Mind if de Sanchis floss wit' chore pubic hair?' THWACK! 'OWWWW! Why chu go an' hit de Sanchis like det?!' Sanchis shouted, toward the big brawny guy that happened to be the girl's date, 'Now chu've gotten de Sanchis all riled up, holmes! Chore gonna regret chore mo-- OOF!' An uppercut greeted the face of Sanchis Guilherme, and sent him to the floor. Now, naturally, Sanchis did the one thing that he knew how to do the best. He got the hell out of that club as fast as his hands and feet would allow him to crawl. Once he was out, Sanchis kissed the ground, then spat back out. He looked up at the night sky as he stood up, and sighed to himself. 'Ey... stupid hembras... dey musta been lesbians or somethin'.' Sanchis said, sulking. 'I should've stayed `en de 21W. De weird eye was much'a de easier for de Sanchis to deal wit' den THIS.' Sanchis said, putting his hands into the pockets of his heavilly tattered jeans, and walking away.
