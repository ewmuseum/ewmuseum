Lies

10 July 2002

Date: 7/10/02 A face in shock. A face in pain. A face in utter disbelief. The tears freely flow from the eyes and down the cheek, dripping off the chin. The phone hangs barely in the hand, near the ear. It slides free and drops to the ground. Head lowered, sobbing commensing. CONRAD RAMSEY Hey, I just got off the phone with Jeremy Bold and... Conrad Ramsey entered the room with his head down. Once he looked up, he saw Danger with his head down. Sobbing. Conrad touches Danger on the arm. RAMSEY What's wrong? What happened? Danger slowly looks up. Eyes filled with tears. He doesn't answer. RAMSEY Tell me. What's wrong? Danger's breathing is filled with pain and suffering. MAX DANGER She... Jessica. She's.... She's... in the hospital and... Danger can't continue as he begins to cry uncontrollably. He plops down on the side of the bed and puts his face into his cupped hands. Ramsey remains standing. RAMSEY Pack your bags. You're going to go to her. Danger raises his head slightly. DANGER I can't. We've got... RAMSEY Don't worry about us, worry about your wife. I can just face Morg one on one. Go to her. Danger looks around, not really able to find himself or his bearings. Finally he stands and looks into Ramsey's eyes. DANGER I'll make it back for the match. I won't let you down again. Danger grabs Ramsey and hugs him. Danger's eyes remain open and staring straight forward. The tears seem to be drying up and a smirk slightly forms. FADE OUT
