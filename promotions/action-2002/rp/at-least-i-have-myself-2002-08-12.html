At Least I Have Myself

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 12, 2002 He woke up in the middle of the night in a cold sweat and glanced around in a state of panic. Completely speechless, he looked up at the night sky from his bed and noticed everything was perfectly normal. Simon switched his attention over to the walls of the hotel room and saw nothing even remotely peculiar. He put on his slippers and calmly walked around for a bit and pondered just what had happened. He tip-toed to the door and almost stumbled and fell, but was able to maintain his balance. Simon crouched down to observe what object and sighed in relief. As he discovered a piece of his luggage on the floor, he promptly placed it aside and procceded. Once the search grew tiresome, Simon decided to go back to sleep. He kicked off his slippers and was about to move under the covers until the alarm on his radio started to go off. Simon reached for it, but the annoying sound suddenly stopped. Although confused towards the occurrence, he shook his head in disbelief and simply plopped his head onto his pillow and tried to rest. With the look of satisfaction on his face, he closed his eyes, but soon felt a strange sensation against his face while doing so. Unwilling to discover what it was, he ignored it and turned to his side. Weird thing is that it didn't stop and finally, after much debate, he sat up to see what it was, only to glare up at a crack in the ceiling above him. Several drops of water then hit him on the head and trickled down his body. He scooted over to the edge of the mattress to avoid, but shot right back up from his bed when his alarm went off for the second time in three minutes He headed for the bathroom with clock radio in hand and took it upon himself to take care of the situation. Without hesitation, he dropped the radio into the toilet and walked back to his bed a happy man. He kept himself to the edge of his bed and dozed off, yet the sound of the alarm made itself know and grew even louder than ever before and triggered the people in the next room to bang violently on the wall in protest to it. Shook up from all the noise, it caused Simon fall off his bed and onto the floor below. The next day, he checked out of the hotel room exhausted and drowzy from the previous night. Nevertheless, Simon perservered, kept quiet, and entered a vehicle parked in front of the building. Once inside, he proceeded to give the driver an address to a special meet-and-greet where he, along with several other members of Action!, would be attending. The two of them then headed for the destination. With a coffee in one hand and a cell phone in the other, Simon spoke to the driver for a moment before he checked out his surroundings. Straight ahead, he was surprised to see a fairly large poster at the end of the street. Simon squinted to see what it was all about, but it was merely too far away. As the green light lit up, the driver sped up at Simon's request. Once they made their way up to it, he was shocked to see something that he truly didn't want to see. 'Action! Wrestling presents superstar Joey Malone' he mumbled while he read the caption under the large picture of Joey, whose goofy smile and big eyes stared right at Simon and reminded him of just about everything that had gone on between the two recently. Not only did it get his immediate attention, but it also infuriated him to a great extent. 'Stop!' Simon screamed as the driver put on the brakes in an attempt to get a better look at the poster. Though, another unfortunate incident would occur as the cup of hot coffee fell into Simon's lap followed by the cell phone which he held in his other hand. In intense pain, he tried not to do or say anything to further aggravate what had already been done. Unaware of what to do next, Simon opened the door and stepped out as the driver glanced behind him and noticed he was nowhere to be seen. 'Something wrong?' the man inquired. 'Oh, Mickey you're so fine. You're so fine, you blow my mind. Hey, Mickey...' Simon sang to distract himself from the immense discomfort he had to endure. With his white pants absolutely ruined, he couldn't speak and stood there silent for a good minute, but would finally duck his head into the vehicle and answer the driver's question. 'I guess, but can you do me a favour? Do you by any chance have an extra pair of pants in that glove compartment?' 'Don't think so.' the driver replied. Simon glanced down at the ground and slammed the car door in frustration. 'Great. Just great.' he said under his breath. The traffic light turned green and the driver attempted to coax him back into the car. As several drivers showed their displeasure towards him, Simon walked ever slowly towards the vehicle. Baby step by baby step, it seemed as though he'd be all right and would make it to the door more sooner than later, Well, that was until he stepped into a large, yet muddy puddle. Sort of a set back when you think of it. On the other hand, at least he had that grunge look down pat.
