Road to Juggernaut // II // New Friend

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/28/03 “’Eer ya go,” Dorris said, slamming down the plate of French Toast in front of Donell. His Napkin was already placed on his lap, and his fork in his hand. As soon as her hand left the table, Donell’s fork had already cut a small piece of the Egg-Battered Toast and shoveled it in to his mouth. She turned around, facing the bar where Rook Black and Julius Arcade sat. Julius drank his coffee religiously, whereas Rook hadn’t taken a liking to caffeine. “Refill on the or-aange juice, hun?” Rook shook his head, not looking up from the swirling pulp, as Dorris turned to Julius. “Mo-rah Coua-fee?” Her thick Ohian accent made Julius recount the words in his head, before he finally understood. “No, thank-you, I’m fine.” She nodded her head, then turned away to return to kitchen. Being the shy dope that he is, Rook finally lifted his head from his sorrows to return to the conversation he and Julius were having. “So Reed hired you just because Donell said ‘EVVVEERRR’?” Rook’s impression of the Valley Girl wasn’t great, but in this situation, who cares. “Yeah. I had never dealt with a promoter like him. I was shocked at his stupidity, but Don took the reigns and landed us some contracts.” “Reed is such an idiot.” His anger with his hopeful-boss was getting to him. “Aw... man, don’t let that shit get to you. Lately, he has been hiring some good people like Parker, Pleh, but then Sanchis got a job and fucked that streak up...” That comment brought in a few chuckles, but Rook went back to wallowing in his pit of orange juice. “So, Action!’s treating you good?” “I guess. Aside from tonight, everything has been great. Sure, it’s run by an idiot,” Rook let out a laugh. “But it’s getting me what I want.” “Oh? And what’s that?” “Well, experience for my boy.” Julius gestured behind him, where Donell had already finished his first sliced. “I’m not going to be here forever, but I am glad that when I can’t make the trip, there’ll be a lot of people to take after him.” Rook smiled, and glanced over at Donell, 'I think my dad had a similar idea. I more or less ruined that with an independent streak. However, when I finally met up with him again, it seemed like I became more grown up than he did.' Julius cocked an eyebrow at Rook, trying to imagine Donell making that transition hurt his head. Rook smirked, 'Of course, on the other hand, he also somehow became a lot more fun than me. I'm sure you'll meet him eventually, his name's Joe and he tends to show up when he's looking for mischief.' Julius looked up. “Joe Black? SWA Joe Black?” Rook shook his head, 'No, I sure that's not him. Joe is mostly a promoter around Texas. He and his brother run the Black and Black Technical Conservatory Of Technical Whatever Wrestling. They change the name every year I think for some reason.' A nod of recognition, if that's what you'd call it, was exhibited from Julius. 'Ah, yeah. I knew it was from one of those Texan Schools. Good school. Thought about taking Don there, but thought that Stu could do him better, just because Stu helped me. But your dad is a good man. Loose Cannon, at times, but he always puts a smile on ma' face...' Rook smiled, 'Stu's a great man. I think he punched Joe once.' Rook nodded his head in Donell's direction, 'So, how is he doing anyway?' A sigh came from Julius, as he mixed some more cream in to his coffee. 'Uh oh...' 'No, it's fine. He could be doing better, well, at least I thought. Truth is, since Daisy, he hasn't been himself. We started wrestling, you know, to set him straight. It worked, but not for the right reasons. He began seeing some Siobhan woman, and she broke his heart. 'He would go in to these depressions, not talking to anyone or even smile. He got Accidie on our asses, which gave me a beaten' or two. But now, I think he realizes that he can't screw up in this world. This is the real big time, and they don't play up here. You know that.' Rook nodded. 'And now, it seems like he's trying to make it up to me. I act like I don't care, but inside it touches my heart, you know, to see him try so hard for my affection. I just hope that, when all this is done, he could be a better person.' Rook nodded, 'I'd want the same for my son. Were I ever to have one of course.' Rook's eyes got a little bigger and his hands left the counter, he was getting an idea, 'The thing is, that I find under these circumstances, there's a lot of lessons that you just can't teach people. Anybody. They really need to find out on their own.' 'Sorry to say, man, that that's the oldest lesson on the road.' Rook tilted his head, slightly confused. 'You see, people in general are ignorant, and conceited. They like to think they're right, no matter how much you force your opinions or actions on them, and no matter what, it won't get to 'em. You have to... let life run it's course... if you know what I mean.' Julius looked back on his son. 'But I hope to teach him what I can, and hope he straightens out from there.' Rook nodded, again, 'The rest is up to him.' Donell looked up from his now empty plate, and hailed the waitress. “Check, please!” Credit goes to Ben for helping with the Rook Black dialogue.
