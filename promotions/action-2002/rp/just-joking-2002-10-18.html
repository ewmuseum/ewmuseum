Action! — Tales

Just Joking.

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 18th, 02. It’s been two months. Over sixty days. Past two-hundred hours. You thought I left, but you’re wrong. You have no respect for me, but you should. Why? I’ve decided to prove it. The mutations have come to full stop. I am one, not two. I am Domino. My left eye twitched endlessly, as I sat myself down in Reed’s office. I had so much to do, and so much to catch up on. Action! Had changed repetitively during my absence, and I could tell, by just looking at my surroundings. “Glad you could make it…” “Oh. Hey Young…” I replied, nodding my head. Reed had a grin on his face, and sat himself down beside me. Excitement built up in my eyes, I had a good feeling about this. I knew this was the right move to make. I just knew it. “So, what have you been up to lately Reef?” Reed slid the mug from the left side of the table in his direction, and clutched it in his hands. He looked at me, wide-eyed. “Well, I haven’t been here. That’s for sure.” I implied. “Um, right, I kind of noticed.” Reed said, in a blunt tone. I looked around, grinning sheepishly. The man was right; I suddenly had disappeared, without even noticing it myself. “You know, a lot of things have been happening back-....” “I know Reed, I know. Although, I don’t think it’ll be hard for me to get adjusted, seeing I have Morg to help me out…” Reed nodded. “Morg. Right.” “Yeah.” I said, hoping he would continue. A sadden look came over Reeds face. My heart skipped a beat. “Well, I’m sorry Jay.” He said, compiling his papers. “I can’t help you out. Find somewhere else to betray. Maybe the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling? Or the Asylum? You know… something lower class.” He shuffled his papers, and began to stand up. I spoke, in shock. “What? You mean, I can’t…come...come back?” I said, hoping for a miracle. “No.” “But-“ “No.” “Jus- “Fine” Reed got up, and smiled. I stood there, in shock. Had I been accepted or declined? I hadn’t had a clue. Reed progressed towards the door. “Cheese is rice! It was only a joke! Welcome back, Reef! Man…I love that name…Reef…I should name my kid Reed Reef…or maybe Reef Reed…Who knows. Anyways, pack your backs. Welcome to a little place I like to call...” He paused. “ACTION!” The door slammed behind him. I realized nothing had changed. Nothing at all.
