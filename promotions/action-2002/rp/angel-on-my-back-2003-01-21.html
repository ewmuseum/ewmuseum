Angel On My Back

21 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Angelica arrived outside of a shop in downtown San Angelo 20 minutes after leaving her father’s house. She had stopped at a bank on the way there, withdrawing 600 dollars. She looked up at the sign above the shop’s door and entered. The sign read “Tri-Star Tattoo”. Angelica looked around, absorbing the once-familiar surroundings. This was the location where she got her first tattoo, a US flag with the name “Dylan” underneath it, dedicated to her brother who was killed during 9/11. She noticed Jeremy approaching her and nodded a bit. Jeremy was wearing a black bandana, a white “Tattooed And Proud” shirt and slightly ratty blue jeans. His arms were covered in tattoos and his ears had multiple piercings. Jeremy extended his arms and brought Angelica into a tight embrace. She hugged him back, the occasional tear still falling from her eyes. “How you holding up?” “About as good as anybody who found their father dead could be doing.” “He was a great man, Angelica. Now, onto business. What do you want done?” “I want angel wings on my back. I want them to cover the entire sides of my back. And in between the wings, I want “Angel” in cursive writing. Dad always used to call me his tough-as-nails angel. I guess growing up with all guys kind of makes you tough.” “That’ll be easy to do, Angelica. You’re going to be so angelic, your name should be Angel or something.” “It will be. Now, let’s get started shall we?” “No problem. Right this way.” Angelica and Jeremy made their way back into the tattoo area. They both sat down and Jeremy grabbed a pad of paper and a pencil. He started to sketch out a pair of majestic angel wings, thick and full at the top, but tapering down to a point at her lower back. He then did fancy cursive writing between the top of the two wings and wrote “Angel”. Angelica nodded in approval, smiling a bit for the first time today. “This may sound bad, but you need to take your shirt and bra off.” “I expected you to say that.” Angelica stood up, sliding her jacket off. She placed her jacket down on an empty chair beside the tattooing chair. She then moved her hands to the front of her black silk button-up shirt. She undid each button, sliding it off. She placed it on top of her jacket, exposing a white silk bra. She then proceeded to take her bra off, standing topless in front of Jeremy. After placing her bra on top of the pile, she turned and sat on the tattoo chair. She laid on her stomach a bit, back-up. While Angelica had done all this, Jeremy had his tools set up. “Is this going to be black and gray or coloured?” “Basically black and gray. But I’d like the wings lightly highlighted white and a blue shimmer on the end of the curl of the A.” “No problem. Just relax and it’ll be over soon enough.” Jeremy moved towards her back, the tattoo needle humming as he began the outline. --TWO HOURS LATER— Jeremy was sitting in front of a masterpiece. The wings on Angelica’s back turned out perfect despite the small amounts of blood finding their way onto the white on the edges of the wings Angelica sighed as Jeremy sat back, enjoying his work. “And there you go, Angelica. Do you have a backless shirt or something that won’t irritate this?” “Yeah, I brought one…we’ll call it a shirt.” Angelica smiled a bit as she reached into her purse. She pulled out a black halter one-piece bathing suit. There was absolutely no back to it until the thong, so it wouldn’t touch her tattoo at all. She slowly stripped completely, wincing a bit when she bent over and stood back up. She slid the bathing suit on, hooking the top part around her neck. The bottom part of the bathing suit was then covered up with her blue jeans. She smiled and turned to Jeremy. “Is there a mirror I can look at them in?” “Yep, right over there.” Jeremy pointed towards the mirror and Angelica made her way there slowly. She turned around, facing her new wings at the mirror. She turned her head around and was speechless when she saw the wings. She sat there for a few minutes, simply staring at the wings. She turned back around and smiled, walking over to Jeremy. She gave him a huge hug, which he carefully returned. Angelica picked up her shirt, bra and jacket. Her and Jeremy made their way out to the studio entrance. Jeremy walked over to the cash register. “Well, Angelica…I’d normally charge around 500 for that. But due to the circumstances, our friendship and the fact that I actually made you speechless, we’ll drop the price to 350. Is that okay with you?” “That’s…amazing.” Angelica pulled her wallet out of her purse and took out $450. She handed it all to Jeremy. “Keep the change, babe. You deserve it.” “Angelica, you’ve got to be one of my favourite customers. On your way out, why don’t you sign the guest book?” “I think I’ll do that. Thank you, Jeremy. Until we meet again.” Angelica smiled and turned, seeing the guest book sitting on a table near the door. She made her way over to it and picked up the blue pen sitting on top of it. She began to write. “Jeremy : You’ve made this miserable day a bit better by gracing my back with your art. My wings help me commemorate my father and for that, I love you more than I can express in words. There’s a special place in Heaven for you and I hope I’m as good of a person as you are so I can meet you there. Signed, Angel Dawson.” Angel placed the pen on top of the guest book and, smiling at Jeremy once more, stepped out the door of Tri-Star Tattoo. The cold wind hit her back, but Angel just started walking towards her car. Inside the studio, Jeremy walked over to the guest book and began reading what Angel had written. He smiled to himself and sniffled a little bit before looking out the window. He watched Angel pass by and sighed. He then turned around, making his way back to the cash register. (NOTE : Jeremy is a fictional name and is not an actual tattoo artist at Tri-Star Tattoo.)
