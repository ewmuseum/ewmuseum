The Alpha: The End Of Innocence: 1

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/15/02 We all have someplace we call home. For me, it is many places. This, the Mellon Arena backstage locker room is one of those many places. I used to show up to wrestling shows the day of, later then most others. I would be out with friends, courting a girlfriend or something like that. I would show up, get the show run down, and be on my way. I would follow a schedule that the stagehands gave me. I now get here before the stagehands. I have since I lost to Potright all that time ago. Now, I sit in this locker room, knowing this is the end of my innocence. This is the end of everything I have worked hard for. If I win or lose… He will still be there. He will try to make amends, and will coax me into accepting him again. This time, it’s different. Joey Malone versus Syphon Fission, this time they are enemies. I have seen it everywhere. The IWO legends, always friends, now enemies, fighting to prove the other wrong. Stupid Reed Young. This is me, breaking away from my bondage. This illusion I have been living, I want it to end. I want to stop crying myself to sleep. I want to stop thinking Keri is mine. I want to stop hating. I want to stop being like this. But it is the only way I can fight back. Joey doesn’t see it that way. He sees it as me stealing his spotlight. He has always thought that way of me. Well…he should know something. He stole the spotlight away from me. He gets revered for doing something to make his life better. But if I try and do the same thing, it is unacceptable. Why? Because they tell you I am a bad guy while Joey is the good guy? Is that it? Then you are all stupid. If only you knew what I have thought all these months. You would realize the world has switched us places. Malone has been shafting me at every turn. I have just tried to put on the game face and accept it. Well…I am done accepting it. This is my message Joey. What you have stolen from me after all these years… It comes down to Wednesday night. It all comes down to Pressure Point Seven. It all comes down to Fission/Malone II. We are going to fight a war where only one man is leaving this building whole. Pray for me… Pray that that man is me. If I don’t win… I do not know what I can… Or will… Do. Innocence is ending… I wish I were there to see it all crumble down.
