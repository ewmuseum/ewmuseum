Action! — Tales

The Call: 180°

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.04.2003 In room 548 on the 10th floor, Conrad Ramsey stood. Silent. Eyes filled with tears. They had stopped flowing finally. After several long minutes. The paramedics were there. Samantha Dox had a sheet over her. Completely covered. Unmoving. Cold. Dead. It had happened while they napped. Conrad was horrified. It took time to call the front desk, alert them of the situation. Even then, he stammered through the call. He was shaken. Shaken bad. But he had another call to make. On the other side of the country, Samantha's son, was in New York. At the Nassau Coliseum. Action!'s Pressure Point. Conrad dreaded the call. But he had to make it. It dailed twice. Still no answer. But he was going to stay on until someone picked up. He didn't know Max was in the middle of a 'funeral'. But the Danger Man had left the ring. 'Marcus?' Conrad said into the phone. Xander had to know something was up. Conrad always called Xander 'Max', never his real name. Always 'Max'. Conrad was lost for words for a moment. Quietly, he mumbled, 'Your mother. Something's happened.' Xander didn't know what was up. Conrad's eyes were staring as the paramedics wheeled Samantha out of the room on a stretcher. His mind began to wander. The tears began again. Streaming. He couldn't tell his stepson what had happened. Could he? It was just too much. He hadn't yet grasped what had happened. But he had to. It was his obligation. 'She's dead.' Flatly stated. Lifeless was the voice that spoke it. On the other end, there was a crunking sound. The line went dead. Conrad looked around, not knowing where he was. 'Are you coming with us?' asked one of the medics. Conrad shook his head, although his brain had slipped through his ear and made a run for the window. He wasn't there. It wasn't happening. The medic grabbed Ramsey's arm, and lead him from the room. Was this real?
