The Family Circus: Emmy

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/12/2002 Emmy Avalon wasn't named this because it was a girl's name. After all, this is the same family that decided that 'Coral' was a good name for a boy. Emmy was named this because of the Emmy Awards. Of course, she didn't take it too well, nor did she receive it too well either. Despite graduating with honors from the University of Louisiana in biology, specifically the biology of plants, she didn't seem to want to do anything more than laying around in the courtyard of the Avalon mansion and study the exotic plants that grew in the greenhouse. When Coral and Allison walked into the greenhouse, they saw her with a magnifying glass in her hand, studying a slightly withered rose bush. Emmy was a tallish sort, and very tanned considering the fact that she hung around outside all the time with her plants. She seemed kind of unkempt in general, seemingly never wanting to waste time in her day with actually trying to keep herself clean. Her hair was tied back in a bun using chopsticks, she wore an unbuttoned white shirt with a black tanktop underneath, and a pair of jeans. Light glinted off of her eyeglasses, as she made notes into her little clipboard. 'Oh, not good, not good...' she said to herself, to no one in particular. 'This bush hasn't had enough water! What happened there?' 'I apologize, my lady,' Rosefeld said to her, as he stood motionless while Emmy panicked around her plants, 'I had thought that it had already been watered when I made my rounds.' She sighed, and glanced over at some overgrown vines in the corner. 'Yet, the kudzu is all overgrown. I knew we shouldn't have planted that, it simply grows way too fast. Wasn't it only halfway up the greenhouse yesterday? Now it's invading the celing.' she said, again, it was as if she was having a conversation with herself and not actually speaking to the other three in the room. Emmy turned suddenly, and stumbled back in surprise. 'Ahhh!' She went behind a table as if she didn't want to be seen. Allison turned to Coral in confusion, and Coral shrugged, before he called out to his older sister. 'Hey, Emmy.' 'I-I, er... um... go away!' she said, seemingly frightened by the presence of someone other than Rosefeld. 'Emmy, it's me. Coral. Your brother?' Coral said. Once the words settled then, Emmy poked her head out from behind the short, squat plant with giant leaves. 'Oh,' she said with a bit of confusion. Then she remembered who he actually was, then she spoke with a bit of worry in her voice, 'Oh.' She saw Allison, which made her slink back behind her plant and point, 'Who... who's she?' 'Allison,' Allison said, 'I'm Coral's girlfriend.' 'Oh,' Emmy said, before she decided to bite the bullet and step back into full view of the two visitors, 'I a-apologize, I, er, I wasn't... wasn't ex-expecting company.' She tried desperately the rub some of the dirt and grime from her body, but Coral just held his hand up for her to stop, 'Well, you want us to come back later? Or...?' 'Ye-yeah... I'll, uh, try to make it for di-dinner or s-something.' she stuttered. She seemed to have retained the same stuttering problem that her younger brother once had. Coral slowly turned to Allison and nodded at her to say that she should leave for a little bit. He then turned to Rosefeld. 'I think we should leave.' he told him. 'Certainly, sir.' Rosefeld said. He walked past Coral and exited the greenhouse. Coral turned to leave, but glanced back to see that Emmy had come out from her hiding and was now going through more of her plants. Coral turned around and spoke, 'You know, you don't have to be so reclusive around me. You're my sister after all.' 'Ummmm... weren't you... like, w-worse than I am?' she asked. 'Yeah, but I got over it, didn't I? I met someone, fell in love, and am now achieving the dream I've always had,' Coral said, 'I'm happier than I've ever been.' 'Must be great,' she said, 'I, uh, ...sh-should get back to work.' Coral simply shrugged, and walked out of the greenhouse. Emmy sighed to herself, 'He has all of the luck.'
