Dreams were meant to be broken: Chapter 4

6 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Listen here, Chino,' said Valenzzio. Valenzzio? That mother fucker WAS the one who did it. He felt relief for a part, then more anger than he had felt in his whole life. Valenzzio had ended his father's life and he never made him suffer more. Fiend stood beside his father, who was tied into a chair. Valenzzio stood in front of him, nobody else in the room. It was empty. And it felt empty. 'You fucking cunt! YOU KILLED MY FATHER!' Fiend screamed and lunged at Valenzzio. He leapt for a shoulder tackle, but he never touched him. He just went straight through him. Straight through his father's killer. He was just like Ebenezer Scrooge in a fucking Christmas Carol. Bah humbug. Fiend threw punches. Tried to pick up lead pipes. Tried to snatch away Valenzzio's gun. He tried with all his might to stop this man from hurting his father but he couldn't lay a finger on him. He never could. He never did. 'Chino, you fucked my wife, you dirty bastard. You fucked my Goddamned wife and what became of it? You became dead, capische?' Valenzzio told him. His father just stared at Valenzzio, peering deep into his eyes and looking into his soul. Fiend's father cringed and looked away. He always tried to see the good in people, but here he was today. On trial before the devil himself. 'You became dead, Chino. And I am gunna do this personally, capische? I'm gonna top you myself. I ain't got nobody to do this shit for me. I'm just gonna put a gun in your mouth and blow your fucking brains over the floor here,' he informed Chino. He nodded. Without a word he nodded and accepted his fate. 'You get nothing to say to me, you fucking cunt? You got nothing to say to my face after sticking it up my wife for the last three years?' Valenzzio interrogated. '23 years, asshole!' Chino spat at Valenzzio. Valenzzio's face distorted into rage. He spat in Chino's face and prepared his gun to fire. He jammed it into the forehead of Chino, busting him open as he jabbed him with the gun. Red trickled down his face. Fiend tried to pull Valenzzio away but he couldn't grasp him for the life of him. He couldn't grab the gun or his jacket to pull him away. He still couldn't touch Valenzzio. BANG!
