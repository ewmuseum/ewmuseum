The end to Joey, whether he lives or dies. Keri Lindum was the ultimate peace. I now have his home, his belongings, his wife, his everything. He doesn’t even have insurance. Too bad for him. That hospital bill is going to kill him before I get the chance to. His keys to his Nissan Ultima are in my hands. I basically walked here, having crashed the Sportage somewhere. In my backpack was everything. Tapes, the notebook, everything. I threw Payback in the back, and Joey’s IWO World Title as well. Keri sat in the front with me. I couldn’t help myself from taking all of Malone’s compact discs and throwing them out of the car. I even ripped out his photos. I started the car and started driving. Keri was going to get drugged up down the road, until I could do things to her that Joey would hate me for. He thinks I care. “Good job back there. You made us all very proud.” I looked at the face of Kenny Rock, riding shotgun in the Nissan Ultima, and smiled. “Kenny…we still got a month before the road ends.” “I know…I know…give em hell kid.” With that, he was gone. I only was able to listen to Payback as I drove to the nearest five star hotel around this place. I have to celebrate… After all… I did achieve God-like status. Everyone hates me. Now…if I could find the right drug to pump into her blood stream.
