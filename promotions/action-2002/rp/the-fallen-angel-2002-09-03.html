The Fallen Angel

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/03/02 Crouching down in the dark alley to pick up a small stone he had dropped; he looked out towards the street ahead of him, watching cars speed by like the apocalypse was coming. The haste that was always drawn upon their faces brought much disgust to himself, but there was nothing he could do. Bitterness of everyday life had consumed him since he found himself back in Detroit near the end of August. Between Cyberslam and Down 4 the Count, he remembered next to nothing. Looking down at his bare chest he saw the only reminder; scars crisscrossing over his chest in the shape of an ‘X.' There was also a tattoo on his right pectoral, the Greek symbol for Omega. He didn't understand how it got there, or what it really meant. It was just there, and his other tattoos remained intact, for the most part. The bar code that was once on his wrist had become a marred look of scars from where it appeared like someone had tried to rip it off of his wrist. It was all very confusing, but he knew there was no point in searching for an answer. Only one person would have anything to do with any type of answer; it was also the person he hated more than anybody else who breathed air on this planet: Elijah Flair. A sneer came to his as the name ‘Eli Flair' swept across his mind. That was how his entire world turned into nothing. Flair nearly killed him, and for from Cyberslam until now, he had been dead. Or at least he felt dead. It seemed as if he had been in a prolonged coma, finally waking up and finding a new target: Kent Anthason. Something about Kent's carefree and almost comedic attitude brought extreme disdain to him. It was almost like Flair's seriousness, it just found a way to wriggle into his body like a worm. Slowly creeping its way into his brain. That's what Anthason had done, tunneled a way into his body and his fumes were being released into his hardened veins. It was all setting some sort of time bomb off. No one understood why he appeared at Down 4 the Count, let alone Action! Wrestling. Only he knew. But soon they would all find out why Anthason was firstly targeted. A Fallen Angel who had seemingly died months earlier had returned with a vengeance upon Action and post importantly, Kent Anthason. There was going to be a large amount of bloodshed in the coming weeks, most of it will belong to Kent Anthason. The great big white world was going to be painted crimson.
