Life - Part 2

14 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/14/02 Life. I am a waste of life. A worthless pile of molded flesh and bones. When I continue to hear the voices of LLB and Jonathan Conspiracy, hearing them mock me and tell me what a fantasy I live...I realize I have grown weak. I have grown into the one thing I have always hated, the one thing I have always fought against. When I watch my own tears slowly traveling down my face, and watch as it mixes with my blood....I can see, feel and taste my weakness. And it tastes sour and bitter. I watch as the glistening of the knife shines into my eyes, rendering me blind for a few moments. And for those few moments, I couldn't see myself...my weak, pathetic self, in the mirror I stood in front of. All I could see was the light of my boarded up room...bouncing off the the shiney knife, and hitting my eyes. It was like looking into the end of that tunnel when you reach the other world. And it felt like a good place to be... To bad I would only see fire where I was going. Finally, I take a deep and long breathe, as I move the knife down a bit, so I can look at myself once again. It has been nearly two weeks since I have entered that ring...not battlefield...and destroyed people. It has been nearly two weeks since I have shown my true power as a wrestler...not a warrior...in that ring...not battlefield. It leaves a bad taste in my mouth, knowing that the words I used for my entire life...are now words I feel ashamed of. What was I? Some kind of black knight in a fairy tale? What? Did I think the young Princess was going to let down her golden hair for me to climb upon? Or did I think the Wicked Witch of the West was my worst enemy? What the hell was I thinking living in a world of battlefields and warriors? I guess Jonathan was right... 'DON'T YOU SEE HOW FUCKING STUPID THAT SOUNDS?! You sound like a damn kid, stuck in fairy tales...GET WITH REALITY KROW!' It burns. It hurts. The truth burns and hurts. I was a little child...living in my own fantasy world. I might as well have been in a children's show and dance around like a demented ape, I would've looked more appropriate. I still can't understand how I became such a hypocrite for seven years. I would attack these people because they were weak, because I felt they were living in a fantasy world...a world I would pull them out of. I would remember telling them 'It's Time for a Reality Check'... When instead of doing this to others... Maybe I should've done it to myself. I think it was me who needed a reality check. I have no idea how I let it all continue for seven full years...and only realize it recently. All my life I heard people laughing at me, mocking me, pointing the finger and joking about me....but I never gave it a second thought...for moments later they would lie in a pool of their own blood. But now I watch my strength, my power, myself...slowly eating away at me...and making me into the one thing I never thought I'd be... A weakling I can feel my heart beating faster and faster into my chest, hoping to pound right through my body. It becomes even faster as I begin to bring the knife up more. I looked at the mirror ahead of me, watching myself lift the knife in front of me. I could see the tears forming once more...as they slowly traveled down my face once more. I could see my blood continue to travel down my face. I haven't cleaned myself ever since the attacks of my two sworn enemies. So I watch my blood and my tears fall to the ground... Especially with the knife getting higher. I look directly at myself...and slowly point the knife at my neck. I stare into my deep black and red eyes once more...watching the pain resurface in my eyes. Watching as two singular drops of blood fall from my chin and only the floor. Watching as my eyes swell up from the previous tears that came forth. I hate it. I can't take it anymore. I can't watch myself become weak. I can't watch myself literally die over finding out my life was a big lie. I can't stand it. I can't take it. I must end it. And at that moment and drew the knife forward...into myself. Watching myself look on in pain... Watching my blood... and my tears.
