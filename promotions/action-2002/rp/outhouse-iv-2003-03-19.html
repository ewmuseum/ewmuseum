Action! — Tales

Outhouse: IV

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another day, another cardboard box. Sanchis' previous night was quite the interesting one to say the least. He had ended up in a bar full of lesbos(according to Sanchis), then spent the rest of his night in another bar with a bunch of wrestlers from some organization called Action, where Sanchis pilfered the PPV of the Year award, as well as the money that Reed Young had foolishly paid him in advance. However, what he didn't count on was the fact that Reed Young would find him eventually. Hence why he was kicked awake with a few nudges of someone's foot. Sanchis stirred awake, a newspaper still on his face. Without removing the newspaper, Sanchis could be heard, his voice and tone groggy and irritated. 'Ehhh, five a'more minutes, de Sanchis, `e is resting.' 'Get up, you.' Reed Young said, nudging Sanchis further. Finally, Sanchis got just a little riled up, and he pointed up at whoever it was that was nudging him. 'Look, mang! Do chu know who chore messin' with, holmes? Chore messin' wit' de Sanchis! Sanchis Su Idolo! CHA! Det's right, de one and only Diablo Disfrazado! De Sanchis `es not to be messed wit', man! `E's torn up Meh-hee-co! `E's conquered de 21st Century! Chu don't wanna be messin' with de Sanchis, or chu'll be layin' up in de hospital bed!' Sanchis said, before he tore the newspaper off of his face to really strike the fear of God into the man he was talking to, 'So, holmes, chu'll be packin' it up and gettin' out of de Sanchis' fa--' Sanchis blinked, before he finally recognized the man he was yelling at. '`EEEEEYYYYY... de Sanchis recogn'ze chu! Chore det Reed guy from last night! Me hombre!' Sanchis said, before digging himself out of the cardboard box and standing up. Before Reed Young stood a short Mexican man with long, unkempt, and dirty hair that may or may not have had rats nesting in it at some point. He wore a very dirty set of clothes, which consisted of an extremely tattered and worn out black trenchcoat, a tattered t-shirt, and tattered pants. He wore this stuff whenever he didn't 'dress up' like he did last night. Basically, Sanchis looked like a total and complete loser of a bum. Not to mention that he smelled horrible, which the expression on Reed's face could attest to after Sanchis embraced his newfound friend. 'Um, yeah... hello to you, too, Sanchis.' 'OH, BEBE! DE SANCHIS, `E'S BEEN MISSIN' YOU, HOLMES!!!' Sanchis said, his embrace tightening. 'Okay, okay! JESUS! Let go of me, will you?!' Reed demanded. Reluctantly, Sanchis let go of Reed, who dusted his shirt off, and made a mental note to have this thing dry cleaned. '`Et was'a horrible, man! De Sanchis, `e's been jobless for `a many months! Si, si! A-and, de Sanchis, `e's workin' on a way to get a job!' Sanchis said, before going into his fake tears routine to try and sway Reed over to his side. Shocking the hell out of even Sanchis, Reed Young brought out his own tears, these being legit tears, and he hugged Sanchis even tighter than Sanchis had hugged Reed, 'Oh my,' Reed sniffed, 'GOD! You poor, poor man! The atrocities you've been through! No, no, don't frown, sir, I'll find you a good job! Really!' All the while, Sanchis' evil smile grew under the dirty, matted mess called his hair, while he was simultaneously thinking about what kind of dope that he was dealing with in Reed Young.
