Cups: cIV: Cup of Life

20 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 20, 2002 Cups: cIV: Cup of Life 'This is exactly what I expected.' ‘The Joker' James Madison cushioned himself easily along the brink of darkness. He gaped off into the middle of the night. There was nothing else there, no one, nothing. The expression he put forth was eccentric. It appeared to be eating away at him.          He browsed at his surroundings. The grass along the floor was built up nearly to his knees if he would have been standing. In the situation that he was in, it was up above his sitting fashion. The blades of grass shaved through the darkness, leaving him with a trail. If it wasn't for them, he would have never made it down the hill as far as what he did. Awaiting the arrival of her, he because anxious. Once more, he thought back of what happened to him on the previous Pressure Point. The man in the crowd ... it was all too familiar.          Immediately, James stood up and surveyed the nearby area. Through the extremity of quietness, the sound of ruffling pants and a voyage through the grass broke through the silence. The man grasped a hold of the nearest tree and continued to scale the atmosphere with his eyes. A feeling of hatred, abandonment came to his mind.          'Oh shit ...'          He whispered to himself as the sound became closer and closer. By this time, he was so greatly hid that he could pick out who the instigator of this freaky scene would be. If he chose not to come out, he wouldn't have to. If he felt it was the best ... he would come out from his great hiding. With the darkness one his side, the being was there. His heart fell as he saw what was before his eyes.          'James, where the hell are you?' Shouted a voice from not too far off. It wasn't what he expected. It was the voice of a male, a familiar voice. Still worried, he did not move a muscle. It all seemed to be a big set-up in Madison's point of view.          As he realized it, all of the thoughts of betrayal; hate; and acts to destroy him flashed before his eyes as he galloped up off of the hill behind the man. Staying out of reach, he continued to hear the man shouting, 'There you are!'          James made it up the hillside before the man, luckily enough. Exhausted from the fight at Pressure Point and the long run up the hillside. He leaped further on and into his car. Without any further thought, he started it up and gassed it. Out of site he went, he watched as the headlights of the other car continued to stay on. There was no movement. The car remained stationary.          'Well James, I never expected us to meet like this.' A deep voice said from the back seat. Madison slammed on the brakes and peered in his rear view. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
