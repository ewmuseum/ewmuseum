Action! — Tales

Before

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-31-02 I sit here In this locker room It’s like I am going off to my execution I will not win this match tonight I know it deep inside my body. I am going to Death Plunge him, and he’s going to kick out or get his foot on the ropes. He always does. He always fucking does. I sit here…in Madison Square Garden. I do not think of the thousands of fans that have flocked to see Action’s first Pay-Per-View, Resentment. I more or less am thinking about what it’s going to be like to let them all down. If I were in the ring with anyone other then Joey Malone, I wouldn’t be thinking this stuff right now. I would be thinking of a way to celebrate. Simply put, that’s the only way I can think when I face other people. If I lose…it’s not that big for me, but it is for everyone else. I let people win many times. Matt Remicho… HA! You think he won that on his talent? Nothing against him, but he is not even in MY LEAGUE! Think about it. I kind of only injured him with relative ease. I kind of only advanced his career five years down its path of destruction. Matt is going to implode one day…and I am going to be part of the reason he does. Well… I do not think I have much room to talk. My internal clock has only been ticking since I beat Phelen last January. Joey has just been there, advancing the timer further and further. Every main event he did, every title he won, every single promo that garnered rave reviews… Those were supposed to be mine. Then he took them from me. He’s the only true friend I have in this crazy world… Yet he has taken everything I could ever want and made it his own perch for his career. Why do you think I care so much Joey? Because, unlike you, I bled in those bingo halls in unknown towns for a sandwich and a place to stay for a night. Unlike you, I had bones broken, ligaments tattered and torn, tendons stretched and sore, and still fought. I fought with a broken jaw. I used masking tape to keep my jaw from moving. I fought with dislocated ankle for six months. I could barely walk, but the painkillers helped me pull off excellent matches. I fought with both of my shoulders separated and dislocated. My god, it was so painful. But I FUCKING did it. I did this because, if I didn’t, I would be thrown away into the streets and be made into nothing. Joey, you have taken the easy way to the top. You have gotten all these lucky breaks. We traveled down the different roads to get to the top. You were able to use and step over people because you were a work rate machine. I had to prove myself again, and again, and again… AND FUCKING AGAIN! Joey, you’re a good friend… But horrible when it comes to using me. I need to win tonight to stop you from doing this. Before you hurt another friend. I can hear my music. That’s my cue. Pray for me.
