Dinner Arrangements: Part Two

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 26th, 2002 Downtown. The ‘Smooth Brothers’ stood in the waiting room of the small Italian restaurant. The carpet was a dark shade of red, the walls a tan colour with various abstract paintings on the wall, all done by famous French painters that Jeff had never heard of before. He leaned over the black, velvet rope and poked at the painting. Odium, in the mean time, was leaning back with a ‘cancer stick’ stuck between his lips as he inhaled the cigarette’s deadly, yet addicting, toxins. Above a small sculpture sat an old fashioned, wooden clock. The hour hand was in between the seven and the eight, the minute hand was on the six. Odium listened to the ticking, playing a monotone song in his ears as he exhaled a large cloud of smoke in the air near Jeff Garvin, who was still preoccupied with the painting at the moment. “That bitch said she would be here by now, she’s thirty fucking minutes late.” Called Odium, who groaned and fixed his attention on ‘the Original’ Jeff Garvin. But Jeff hadn’t heard him; he merely continued his fascination with the piece of artwork from eighteenth century. “If she isn’t here in two seconds I’m leaving. I can’t believe I’m actually here, I can’t believe I actually let you drag me here. I don’t even like your stupid ass.” The glass door opened and shut, and in stepped the lovely Claire Moore. She had her blond hair down, a red sleeveless dress with a slit up her right thigh as she wandered in and placed her long, black coat on the rack to the left of her. She smiled Odium, realising that he resembled Jeff’s description to a tee. She was a little surprised to actually see him there in front of her. Quite frankly she was expecting… Actually she really wasn’t expecting anything. Jeff turned to see Claire. “Hey, glad you made it.” “Finally.” Odium commented under his breath. “Thanks, the traffic was horrible, there was an accident on the highway.” She answered, shivering from the sudden change from cold to warm as she walked down a short few feet to where Jeff was standing, staring at the painting. “Some guy said that the owner of this place paid twenty grand for this piece of shit. I mean look at it, it’s a god damn finger painting for Christ’s sake. A five year old could take a crap on a sheet of paper, step in it, and it would still look better than this. Whoever the guy is that bought it is a fucking chump.” Claire cleared her throat and crossed her arms, “That would be my father.” Gulp. “Haha! Smooth move, Jeff, you dumb fuck.” Odium shot back, taking another drag from his cigarette. Cue the signature Jeff Garvin shifty eyes. Jeff looked over to see the ‘No Smoking’ sign and quickly pointed back to his ‘friend’. “…Odium’s smoking!” Screeched Jeff. The tattle-tale that he really is. “You little fucking-“ Odium started, clenched fists and all. He moved closer to Jeff when Claire stepped in and put her hand on his chest. “Put it out.” Claire demanded. The cigarette fell into the ash trey; the flame flickering until it was completely out. Odium cracked his knuckles and looked over to Jeff who was smiling with glee that he was able to find a scapegoat. “Okay.” She sighed, leading them to the dining room. “Shall we?” “You damn right, I’m fucking starved.” “I wonder if they’ll let me pet the lobsters, those ‘rush-sta-shons’ are cool dudes.” A deep ‘Odium’ like sigh. “You’re an idiot.”
