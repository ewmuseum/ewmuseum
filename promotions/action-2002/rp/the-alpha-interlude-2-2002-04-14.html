The Alpha: Interlude: 2

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/14/02 I finally got out of Smilthy's. Even though this crazy fuck kept asking me to go to the basement. I do not like anal sex…so he can go hit up another depressed nobody who just wants some coffee and some scrambled eggs. You would think there was a fight going on down there. I sit here, in my Prowler, driving away from New York to Pittsburgh. This is my freedom. This is how I live. Going place to place looking for a fight. For another chance to ruin the man who ruined me. I was so close two times. At Beach Party, last year, I was going to take out all the people in the match, cuff Malone to the ring ropes and blow his brains away. It was for using me. It was extreme. I chickened out. I fell for the Malone charm. He made me feel guilty. I feel guilty. I should have ended him, even if it would have ended me. I could at least know that Joey would never hurt me again. He could never crush me again. I would end him. Yeah… Deep shit…isn’t Clark? You were going to kill the son-of-a-bitch and call it a mistake. A fuck up in the angle. And you had the money to get the job done. Instead…you let him live and pollute her. SHUT UP! That was the second time. The first time was prom night. I was coming back from Lander’s party. I put down about eight beers and called it a night. Julie had about six and was blitzed out of her fucking mind. I was taking her home; I did not want to get her pregnant, after all. Wouldn’t Malone love that? For his blood to poison my youth. It would get him off every night. He gets off on using me…I know it. And deep down… So does he. She was fucking with the steering wheel, and I stopped paying attention for about a second to stop her before something serious happened. It happened anyway. I had hit some faggot car at about fifty-five. I immediately got out of the Cougar, and jumped to see who the fuck it was. I saw Joey Malone’s body, probably dead from the impact. I ripped off the door. Really…I did. I saw my angel in that seat, broken and bloody as all hell. I pulled her out of the car and checked her pulse. In a way… She flatlined. She was dead. I didn’t shed a tear. I, instead, performed CPR to get her body going again. She, to this day, owes me her life. I got her breathing again, but after that, I could do nothing. I had the drunkard bitch who caused the incident to call 911. I let her lay there, knowing I could do nothing anymore. She was probably deep in a coma. I then noticed Malone for the first time. His body was broken. He was still breathing. For some reason, a voice in my head roared in my brain. “YOU LEFT HIM BREATHING! FINISH HIM NOW! BREAK HIS NECK! CUT HIS THROAT SLIT HIS WRIST! JUST FUCKING END HIM!” I woke up noticing a piece of glass in my left hand, and Malone’s wrist in my right. I dropped the fucking piece of glass and ran. And ran. And ran. To this day, that voice haunts me time to time. I do things that I wouldn’t normally do. Maybe that’s why I feel the way I do. Maybe this is all a dream. Joey doesn’t know how close to death he was. Keri doesn’t know she was dead for a span of about a minute. Maybe more. I left the corner of Highly and Brown as a hero in a sense. A villain in the other. Keri didn’t remember me. Joey needed my help because both his arms were useless. So I defended him if he needed it. We became good friends because of that night. He doesn’t know it was me. Why do I feel regret for the crash? Why do I feel regret… For not killing him?
