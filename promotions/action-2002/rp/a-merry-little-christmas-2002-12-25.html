A Merry Little Christmas

25 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/25/02 It was December 25th. The snow had fallen, the children had woken, and happiness was contagious. As was expected on such a day, it was after all… “Christmas!” Garvin shouted with glee, rushing down the stairs in his flannel pyjamas, his short curly brown hair still in bed-head mode. His one bedroom bachelor apartment was a complete mess; dirty clothes strung over furniture, half eaten pizza laying on the coffee table, and a raggedy old evergreen tree leaning in the corner with puke green tinsel to compliment it’s hideousness. There were five medium sized presents wedged under the tree, all from Santa Clause. Three days before, Jeff ventured out to the nearest shopping mall with twenty dollars in his pocket and bought as much cheap shit as he could find. He didn’t have any friends, so he needed to have something to unwrap. He picked up the first one and smiled with glee. “I wonder what this one could be,” he said while giving it a shake. After which he tore through it, throwing the paper across the room to reveal the gift inside. “A moustache trimmer!” Garvin screamed, happy with the gift that ‘Santa’ had brought him even though he did not have a moustache. Five more minutes passed in a fury of ripping and tearing. On the floor in front of him laid all of his presents. A potted plant. A shirt that reads, “#1 Dad”. An extension chord. And an Atari video game system. Fast forward an hour or so in actual ‘fast forward’ speed, so Jeff is running about scratching his ass, trying on his new shirt, whipping some blue jays with his extension chord, and using the Atari as a paper weight. When finished, Jeff sat at the kitchen table with a bucket of KFC in front of him that he tried to trick himself into thinking was a ‘turkey dinner.’ He said a quick grace and piled all the food on his paper plate and began digging it. “Oh yeah, this is so good! Wait…” He stopped, a chicken leg hanging from his mouth. “Please pass the gravy.” Jeff waited, but the gravy did not move. “Oh well, I guess I really don’t NEED gravy.” And with that said… Jeff Garvin’s ‘Merry Little Christmas’ had concluded. Thank God.
