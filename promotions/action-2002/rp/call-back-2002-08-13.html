Date: Aug. 13/02 RING.....RING..... She had heard the phone ring earlier. She was in the middle of her shower, go figure. People always called her when she was in the shower. It was getting to be a curse. She had every intention of calling the person back...really, she did. She continued to dry her tanned, fit body off as the phone rang for a second time in 20 minutes. Man, this person is rather persistant. They could easily leave a message, but that seemed easier said than done for the caller. 'Let's see if I can get there in time to pick it up THIS time around' She muttered to herself, shaking her head as she walked towards the cordless phone, which was placed on the edge of the bathroom counter. She had moved it there after the first phone call just in case. She picked up the phone at the end of the fourth ring. 'Hello?....Hello?' She barely missed them. The person hung up right before she picked up. Angelica sighed hard, hanging up and placing the phone down. She really wanted to know who was calling her, but feared they didn't leave a message. She'd check later, though. 'Hmm...I wonder who it was...oh well, I'll find out later. Now it's time for my full body massage. Oh, Gunther!' Angelica grinned from ear to ear as she slowly made her way out of the bathroom. Two seconds later, a muscular, brunette man with a tight white shirt and white shorts followed her down the hallway, smiling as well. Gunther would be dealt with now. Phone call could wait for later.
