Courage - Act II

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/21/02 'So, she wasn't there?' Joey Malone asked. 'Nope.' Coral said, dejectedly. He caught Joey in the Shaved Beaver and decided to talk to him for a spell. 'And her roommate probably thought I was... er, some sort of stalker person... thing. So I didn't stay.' Joey laughed before he took a sip of his water. 'Man, you really need to work on your approach.' Joey said. 'Ali's not gonna like the fact that you're too nervous to stick around. You've gotta get in there and not let go.' 'You're giving me advice.' Coral said, in shock. 'You, Joey Malone, who couldn't be arsed to marry the girl of his dreams for six months after his proposal, are giving me advice.' Joey cringed. 'I'm just saying.' he started in response. 'Allison's a college girl. She's got like a thousand hormonal time bombs literally bulging in their pants for a shot with her. You can't just dawdle with Ali. She might like you a lot, I can't really say... but if you don't go see her any time soon, some idiot's gonna get to her first. And then what? You'll have lost your chance!' Coral shook his head. 'But... I dunno. Maybe, ...maybe she already found someone.' he said, nervously, and as if he really didn't want to say what he just said. 'Don't say things like that, Coral. She hasn't had time to find anyone. It's been... what, a month since she left? She couldn't have possibly have found someone else by now that she's serious with.' Joey said. 'We were only together for a month, Joey. And, well, I'm... just not sure that she's the one. Do you know how often I've been dumped and cast aside? ...Quite a lot, unfortunately. Maybe this is just God's way of saying 'screw you, Coral'.' Coral responded, with a sad face. 'Geez. I think I could've felt those pessimistic vibes when you entered this place.' Joey said. Coral shook his head and finished off his water. 'Yeah, I know. I have that effect on people.' Coral said, dumping two dollars on the counter. 'Just ask mom and dad.' With that, Coral stood up. 'I'm gonna hit the mall. Maybe I'll catch her there.' He walked out. Joey just chuckled to himself and went back to his drink.
