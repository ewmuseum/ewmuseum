F.R.I.E.N.D. ?

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/08/02 OPEN Red light. Stop. Wait. Look. And then turn. Chris Fox is on the road again, as he calmly races around town. Title: F.R.I.E.N.D. ? Date: 1/6/02 1:00 pm. EST Place: Pikachu Beatle The number one contender drives on. A nice sunset can be seen over the horizon. A shade of purple blares form the sun. Being a surprisingly un busy afternoon in this town, Christopher decided to take the car and clear his mind. After driving for a bit and listening to Motivation by Sum 41 on the radio, Fox pulls up in a gravel parking lot. Just ahead is a huge open field with hills and a great view of the city across the lake. Chris rolls down his window for Pikachu in the back seat. He was getting hot. Then Chris exists the vehicle. He takes a stroll down a path nearby which leads into trees. With his hands in his pockets and face looking down, ‘Kid’, with all his ‘spare’ time makes use of it. Fox “Ah, fresh air.” Fox pulls out a small Scope Mouth wash bottle, shaking it around. Fox “I won’t be needing this.” Undoing the cap he pours it on the ground below.. Fox “Fa la la la la. La la la la.” Fox continues to walk down the path until he hears a voice. “Hey kids. I have something for you!” Fox turns his head and sees a dark man with a long jacket talking to a couple of kids! Look at those two boys. Both having fun. Well I’m not. LLB sure kicked me to the curb! I don’t have any real friends now. Pikachu’s not even a friend.... he’s just a Pokemon! Fox then sees the dark man pull out candy and taunt the children. Man “Here, there’s more in my car. We can by FRIENDS forever.” Fox starts to walk away but after hearing the word FRIENDS he goes back, seeing all sorts of candy! Chrisy thumps with his feet as he jolts toward the Man! Fox “Take me! I want fucking candy! Oh I could use some friends right aBOOT fucking now!” The man looks around like he's embarrassed or something. Man “Hey guy, get lost.... I want the fucking kids.” Fox “Hey, my car can fit all of us! C’mon!” Man “Fuck, what will get you out of here?” Hmmmmm.... Condoms? No I have enough of those. But I am running low. Hmmmmm.... I could really go for a Vanilla Milkshake!.... BIG TIME! Fox “Do you have any milkshakes?” The Man, yet again, looks around. Man “Fuck dude.... I have some pot. Here.” Fox takes it and skips off.... but as he does his feet hit the Man knocking him out cold! The two children then play around, jumping on top of the dude with the sweet leather jacket. Fox “Wow! I gots some Pixy dust! Weeeeeeeee!” Fox dances back and forth, throwing the “dust” up in the air. Just then an old man walks by and accidentally takes a whiff! He falls to the ground and is presumed dead! Unlike the old man, Fox is very much alive jumping up and down all the way to his car. Fox “Oh who cares aBOOT LLB! I gots plenty of friends all overs the palace! I think I’m in a new era! Yes! The era of.... FUN IN THE SUN.... BIG TIME!!!!!” CLOSE
