[detail withheld] or AIM - [detail withheld] ::: 'Now this looks like a job for me So everybody just follow me Cause we need a little controversy, Cause it feels so empty without me ' -Without Me by Eminem June 25th, 2002 Just another girl. That's what most people think when they see my name on the roster list. That's what most of the other wrestlers think when they first hear my theme play and I walk down to the ring. That's what I know you thought when you saw this letter in your damn mailbox. As you opened it, you got curious about what's different between me and every other girl. You think that nothing would be different and as you read this, your judgment is clouded by the typical stereotypes. THAT'S NOT WHAT I AM! I'm not just another girl. Why you would think this is beyond me. I'm more than your regular girl. I'm a professional wrestler. I may not seem like much, I know. I may not look like I can be as good as I say I am. I probably don't look like much of a winner to you. LOOK DEEPER! You'll find the heart of a warrior, the spirit of a champion, the mind of a genius, the guts of a daredevil and the strength of a gladiator. But if you don't bother to look deeper, all you'll see is the body of a hot, young female that you'll think can do no damage to you. The only thing you'll be doing by not looking deeper is fucking yourself over. Get to know me before you make any assumptions and you'll be better off. If you take the time to look at my past and my abilities, you'll know what to do. STAY OUT OF MY WAY! Don't try to stop me. Don't even fucking think of laughing, either. I know I'm good and once I kick your ass, you'll know it too. Sure, you may have faced other girls in the past and gone through them like Britney Spears goes through condoms on a slow night in downtown L.A., but I'm not like that. I'm kind of like that dull-looking knife that ends up stabbing you. I'm like that one person in the race who surprises everybody and finishes in first place. FUCK YOUR STEREOTYPES! Fuck them straight to hell, you dumb bitch. Oh, you don't want me to call you a bitch? You want to call ME a bitch? I'm a bitch because I tell the truth, right? Right. I've heard it from every single person I've beaten. They all call me a bitch, either behind my back or to my face. Hell, I've heard it from anybody you could ever think of. I've been accused of shit that wasn't my fault. I've been lied to straight to my face by people who were supposedly my best friends. I've had people flat-out calling me a lying piece of shit. There's an ironic part to that particular incident...I was the only person telling the truth. WHY WON'T YOU FUCKING LISTEN TO ME?! I'm telling you the fucking truth, bitch! Nobody listens to the person who knows the whole truth, have you ever noticed that? The one person, the ONLY person who knows everything that you need to know is the one person you don't even ask. The only person that knows what you want to know is the one you don't believe. And you don't believe me. But I know something you don't NEED to know. And all the things you don't know and NEED to know? I know them too. ALL THAT YOU NEED NOT KNOW, I CAN NOT FORGET! Sincerely, Angelica Dawson. Fucker.
