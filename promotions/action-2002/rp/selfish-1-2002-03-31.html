Selfish - 1

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, March 31, 2002 'And so, we meet here today with the hopes of not mourning the loss of a fallen man, however celebrating the life of a cherished friend whom we shall remember for the rest of our lives.' I can't believe you, Sean. Matt Remicho stood among the endless sea of faces, as the man in the black robe stood before a casket in the backyard of Remicho's mansion. The day had taken a colder turn, the temperature now dropping, the winds coming off of the Shrewsbury River of New Jersey. The word had spread so quickly as to the death of 'Showcase' Sean Cylear, that the number of wrestlers seen was astounding. Why would you do this? There stood LLB, with ice pack wrapped around his head from dental surgury. The man once known as Eron The Relentless came, but stood off to himself, as nobody really wanted to be there next to him. Overdose was mixed into the crowd, and the retired Steven Shadows, also known as Graphic Violence, was mixed upon the crowd. Look at all these people...you had plenty to live for. 'Growing up well liked amongst his peers, he grew up to many people's fantasy job; professional wrestling. Out of character, there were very few people who could ever say an ill word of Sean, as he was renowned from his hard work ethic, and easy-going nature as I'm sure everyone knew.' No kidding man...that's why he was one of my best friends. XXXstasy had come in from the fWo, seated near the back in a blue suit. Billy Lovemuscle made an appearence, cowboy hat and all. Not to mention, Sterling Silver himself. The list of indy wrestlers from the days of Sean's growing career could be lead as a list of the roster of a superfederation. Not to mention, of course, 'The Saint' himself was holding the funeral, and had paid for all of the arrangements out of his own pocket. 'And now, we come today to give Sean Cylear, known to many as 'Showcase', his last rites, and final burial.' It's not fair. You're so fucking selfish.
