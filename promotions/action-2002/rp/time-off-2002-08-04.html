Time Off

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/4/02 . . . Leitchfield, KY | 15:45PM CST . . . CONRAD RAMSEY is sitting on the couch, holding the bandage on the top of his head. His right leg is propped up on the coffee table, with a large brace on it. CONRAD RAMSEY So much pain. Conrad tries to stand up, but he clenches his side and lets out a small scream. RAMSEY A sprain knee. Two broken ribs. Twenty-plus stitches on my head. A black eye. I need some damn time off. Ramsey grabs the cordless telephone laying next to him as he dials someone's number and holds the phone to his face. RAMSEY Hello? Mr. Young? RAMSEY I'm in bad shape here, sir. I think I need some time off. RAMSEY A few weeks. Just to heal a little bit. RAMSEY Thank you, sir. Thank you very much. Ramsey hangs up the phone, and rests it on the couch. RAMSEY A few weeks is all I'll need to recooperate. I'm not anywhere near finished with that little punk, Max Danger! FADE OUT
