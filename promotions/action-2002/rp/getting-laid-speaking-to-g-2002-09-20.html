Getting laid - speaking to G

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th September 2002 (Airborn Tyler sits in a dark room, with a light shining down on him from the ceiling, so the only thing that can be seen is his head. Kind of like those funny pictures of floating heads on the event summaries posted on this website, except much uglier. Airborn looks around, confused. Raising his eyebrow, he breaks the silence.) Airborn: So, uh, you gonna say anything? (Slowly, the light on Airborn’s head intensifies, and Airborn looks up to the ceiling, covering his dazzled eyes. Then, a deep husky voice emanates out of the roof.) Voice: Waddap Airborn? How’s da ‘hood dealing with ya dawg? Airborn: God? Is that you? God: *invisibly shrugs shoulders* Yeah. Airborn: What happened to your VOICE? Man, the last time I heard a voice that husky Daddy was slaughtering the Labrador! God: Times change. No one’s interested in a fuddy duddy anymore with a grey beard. Instead, I dyed cut off the beard, but kept the moustache and died it an ‘out there’ shade of green. Hey, I even got some hair gel to spike up what’s left up top. Airborn: Up top? What about down low? God: Hohoho, God lost his virginity the time Eve was created. Airborn: You didn’t?!! God: Hey, why do you think perverts exist? Airborn: What was she, well, you know, like? I’ve heard so many rumours about Eve. Can you confirm any of them? God: Hohoho, where do you think the phrase ‘doing it doggy style’ came from? Airborn: Oh yuck. That’s like telling me my Grandad is a sex machine. God: He had his moments. Airborn: Oh dear God… God: Don’t use my name in vain! Airborn: …don’t tell me you… God: And where do you think homosexuality came from? Airborn: You disgust me. Mummy said you should be my role model, well now I can say that Patrick Kielty is a better role model than you ever were! God: Whatever. (Airborn looks down at his feet, and an awkward silence is heard.) God: Anyway, up here in Heaven HQ we have something way better than web cams… Airborn: Oh God. God …no, sorry, I’m 100% straight these days. Anyway, I couldn’t help but notice that you’re 18 years old and still a virgin. Airborn: *like a schoolboy* Hehe! God: What? Airborn: You said ‘virgin’! God: Children these days. Anyway, you need a girlfriend, and bad, and then you need sex. Do you have any idea what it’s like to love someone? Airborn: Of course. Mummy kissed me on the cheek every night and tucked me underneath the covers. God: Well, Airborn Tyler, I’m going to give you a challenge. By the end of October, I want you to have a girlfriend, and get laid. Airborn: Laid? God: That’s slang for having sexual intercourse. Jesus… Airborn: Hey, don’t use your son’s name in vain! God: …what has my world come to when I’m teaching 18 year old wrestlers what ‘getting laid’ is? Airborn: I don’t know, what has the world come to? God: I have no idea. Anyway, Mary needs some lovin’, then I’ve got to go speak to Chris Fox about his masturbation problem. Airborn: What did you turn into, the sexual problems help line? God: It’s a topic I’m well informed then. Airborn: Oh, OK. See ya later G, keep in touch my man! God: Didn’t I tell you once already, I’m 100% on the straight and narrow these days! Keep the faith Earth child. (The spotlight fades, and the light in the room turns up.) Airborn: That was totally bodacious. I think the A Star Student had better find some A Star Action!
