Cups: cI: Cup of Life (Beginning)

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/10/02 Cups: cI: Cup of Life (Beginning) 'Like the old saying goes, a picture is worth a thousand words ... but a picture like this couldn't be described in a lifetime.' He spent most of his day just looking, studying every crisp detail that extended out the slightest bit in the picture. Love was the hardest thing to find as a child. His parents gone, mistreating his youth, but that's a different story. Cups. 'How could someone so precious not know that I was smiling back at her?' He questioned as he convened immobile.          The man never even concluded to revolve the picture over on the flip side to analyze a type of lettering. All that he craved, was there on the surface. Her long brown hair scattered in the mist, beautifully down her shoulders and out. A foggy brown that seized his heart from the beginning. There were no deliberations of lust, ever. For once, he pondered the fact that he may have found someone that he truly loved.          'How ... could she have slipped away?'          He repeated to himself over and over. Begging himself to come back into realization and assess the truth behind it all. Dereliction. Failure. Inadequacy. Those were the only thoughts. They circulated vagrantly throughout his mind, praying to find a place to nest. Everything that he predicted, contradicted what he thought. 'I never expected to see those eyes again for the rest of my living life. I could've only imagined seeing the eyes of the angel.' He soaked back into the mitigated sofa and took in a few large puffs of air.          'Once more, I failed her. I tried so hard that day, but ... it never worked. I guess you could only do so much to try to save someone. Then it's over with. You're born with nothing at all, yet you leave the world with everything you've worked for.' Peeling his eyes away from the nothingness, he glared into the picture once more. 'All you ever wanted me to do was to lie a cup above your tombstone. You told me it for years. You never asked me to visit you. You never asked me to come and weep. All you wanted to do was for me to fill my love in that cup and place it there. You knew when it rained, and when it snowed, that when the cup was overflowing ... that signified my love to you. Amazing how I could remember something like that when I don't remember what I ate a few minutes ago.'          At that moment, he realized his point. The final task was at hand, or so he believed. He stood from his idle state and stared out the window at the pouring rain. 'It's time for the cup to become full.' Perception took effect. He realized what he was talking about. He actualized who he was talking to. 'Her name ... as precious as the angel's themselves.' Still yet, he never spoke of the magnificent name or the story behind it. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
