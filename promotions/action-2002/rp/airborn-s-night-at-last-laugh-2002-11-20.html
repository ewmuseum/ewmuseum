YANKEE TOOK THE ONRUSHING AIRBORN TYLER AND BELLY TO BELLY THREW HIM OFF THE STAGE! AIRBORN TYLER JUST GOT THROUGH OFF THE STAGE THROUGH TWO TABLES AND THE TECHNICAL EQUIPMENT! MY GOD, HE HIT IT! THE ‘BALCONY OF HOPE’ SHOOTING STAR PRESS ONTO A STANDING JONNY YANKEE! RATINGS REED YOUNG, DO YA’ SMELL THEM?!! So hold me close better hang on tight Buckle up, baby, it's gonna be a bumpy ride We're two kids hitching down the road of life Our world, our flight Ump: THE FINAL HOORAH! YES! HE HIT IT! Shaw: The Final Hoorah through the French announce table? Sweet mother of Jesus… Ump: THAT WOULD BE MARY, AND THIS ONE MIGHT BE OVER! Yannick Pavarel: OH là là! Thierry Pires: AIRBORN TILER EST MORT! If we stand side by side (all night) There's a chance we'll get by (and it's alright) And I'll know that you'll be alive In my heart till the day I die Ump: They look at each other, snarling, feel the hatred! Feel the passion of four months of beating the shit out of each other! Airborn rears back and… Shaw: OFFERS HIS HAND? WHAT ARE YOU DOING!?!? Ump: Both men have shaken hands and raised them, and every single person inside this arena is on their feet. What an incredible moment. Cause you were born to be my baby And baby, I was made to be your man We got something to believe in Even if we don't know where we stand Only God would know the reasons But I bet he must have had a plan Cause you were born to be my baby And baby, I was made to be your man Ump: HOLD ONTO YOUR SEATS!! AIRBORN AND FOX ARE ABOUT TO COLLIDE!! Shaw: They do! Fox with a right hand... but now Airborn with a right hand... Fox... Airborn... Fox... Airborn... FOX... AIRBORN... FOX... AIRBORN... FOX... AIRBORN... FOX... AIRBORN... AAAHHHH!! Ump: FINALLY FOX DUCKS A SHOT... BACK DROP!! Christopher jumps to his feet as he goes into the ropes... NO!! THE ‘BUNNY SAULT’ MISSES!! Airborn was able to roll out of the way! He’s gonna die another day in this one! Shaw: Pft. Nice takeoff... Ump: Tyler’s getting to his feet... he walks over to Chris... ‘TYLER DDT’!! THE ‘TYLER DDT’!! AIRBORN WILL PIN!! Ump: One! Ump: Two! Ump: Three! My heart beats like a drum (all night) Flesh to flesh, one to one (and it's alright) And I'll never let go cause There's something I know deep inside Ump: Please... PLEASE AIRBORN!! I’M SURE REED YOUNG HAS ENOUGH BUY-RATE FROM THIS MATCH ALREADY!! Shaw: He can’t hear you, Liam! AIRBORN TYLER IS CLIMBING THE BALCONY!! Shaw: Airborn’s measuring Fox on the ramp... Ump & Shaw: I CAN’T LOOK!! (The Fans go wild.) The Crowd: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! Ump: AIRBORN TYLER’S F’N DEAD!! HE’S F’N DEAD FOLKS!! BROKEN IN HALF... JUST FOR YOU!! I HOPE YOU’RE ALL HAPPY TONIGHT!! I HOPE YOU’RE ALL FUCKING HAPPY... Cause you were born to be my baby And baby, I was made to be your man We got something to believe in Even if we don't know where we stand Only God would know the reasons But I bet he must have had a plan Ump: AIRBORN TYLER HAS PINNED LLB WITH TEN GERMAN SUPLEXES!! Cause you were born to be my baby And baby, I was made to be your man You were born to be my baby And baby, I was made to be your man Ump: Testify time... is now. (A light turns to Pat Hoskins on the outside, as he sits in front of the laptop. He picks up a microphone, stands, and begins to speak.) Hoskins: The Virus... is... Hoskins: AIRBORN TYLER! As those words echo over and over again, the music comes to a sudden stop. The picture decolours to black and white, and then a still image of a bloodied, battered and bruised photo of Airborn Tyler saluting the crowd is shown. Quickly followed by Airborn Tyler giving the first backstage worker he comes across the middle finger.
