Hmm. Interesting.

14 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/14/03 He lay there. Motionless. The crowd is concerned silence. People of all sorts(Refs, Medics, etc.) were around him, talking, seeing if he was alright. That was what happens when you get pushed off of a ten foot high ramp while sitting in a wheel-chair. Once you've splatted on the cold, unforgiving, concrete floor you were pretty much unable to do anything on your own. The 61-year-old Bantam Champion, Conrad Ramsey, had the eighteen-year-old Airborn Tyler to thank for his current situation. He was unconscious, a pool of blood forming underneath his body, having begun to flow from a cut over his eye. But the fall kinda caused his mouth to bleed as well. He may, in fact, need to send in an order for new teeth. But with him on his stomach and his face down as well, it was hard to tell. It wasn't something he hadn't had to do before. Max Danger once broke his teeth. Though he just knocked the teeth out by hitting him and stomping on them. He never pushed the old man off a stage. He had more guts than that. Airborn Tyler, on the otherhand, obviously did not. He had climbed back down from his ladder, and thrown the Bantam Championship down to its owner. The silver belt had clanked against the steel of the wheel-chair and rested at Ramsey's side. The young bastard had then left the area. But Conrad Ramsey wasn't going to be able to go anywhere at the moment. Though he really didn't seem to mind. He needed the rest. He had been having quite the difficult time sleeping since Players and Pawns. That was where Airborn Tyler had fallen sixteen feet to the ground. But all that time Conrad spent, feeding Tyler, making sure he was alright, just generally caring for him, was for not. It was all pointless. Airborn had faked his own injuries. He had conned the old man. Tricked him. Played upon his emotions. But that was all a distance thought to Ramsey at the moment. He was thinking of Samantha Dox. The lovely mother of Max Danger, who he had grown quite fond of since he first meet her at Thanksgiving last November. If his body would have allowed it, he would have smiled. She was a nice lady, he thought. But soon he was been turned over. Blood covering his face. The fans in attendence cringed at the sight of his face. It was worse than how Danger looked, and he lookd brutal. The paramedics, who had just arrived in an ambulance, lifted the old man up onto a stretcher. One of the referee's picked up the Bantam Title and placed it across Ramsey's stomach. The medics rushed Conrad backstage and through the halllways, which were still reigning in chaos. No one paid any attention to the stretcher being wheeled through. No one except Airborn Tyler. He stepped out from around a corner, and watched, with a smile on his face, as Conrad was pushed through the doors and into the parking garage where the ambulance waited. He then disappeared again. Out in the garage, Ramsey was being lifted up into the ambulance. Just at that time Homeless Gus came over. He asked, 'Can I have a lift?' No one answered him. He was just pushed aside. Inside the ambulance, Conrad just laid there, not doing a thing. Oddly, he didn't feel any pain. He wasn't hurt at all, or so his brain was telling him. 'Hmm. Interesting.' he said inside himself, and a smile formed across his face.
