Losing It.

6 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.06.2002 So much for the big return. 'Mother fuc---' And so much for the class act. The match was over, and Pressure Point 1 had been completed. After leaving wrestling for the past six months, not to be seen in any place with the small exception of Five Star Wrestling, Matt finally reared his face back to the industry that he had helped to make popular. Matt had essentially built the wrestling industry and it's hardcore ways with a few of the other guys from UWA, but on Pressure Point 1 he faced another legend in his own right. Syphon Fission was a big motherfucker. Matt gave up almost seven inches and over fifty pounds of muscle to Fission, and Matt's ring rust more than showed in the match against him. It wasn't so much Fission kicking the crap out of him, although that was a very big factor; Matt was beating himself by not preparing for this match, thinking it would just be the cakewalk that the other promotions had all been. Now he sat in his locker room, feeling the effects of going under .500 in any promotion for the first time since he wrestled in the ECWF over in Japan. Even if it was only 0-1, he still was reeling. Two hours had elapsed since his match, and he just sat there with the towel over his head, the TV set sitting across the way. The remote dropped out of his hand, crashing on the ground with a loud smacking sounds. The video tape was labeled in black, with 'Fission v. Saint, PP1' on the label. Ryan Kelly slowly opened the door, and looked at Matt. She wasn't completely in the room, but half in the doorway half in the room. 'Matt, are you okay?' '...' Matt really wasn't in the mood for talking. Ryan peered under the towel, looking at Matt's bruised cheekbone. 'Matt?' Matt looked up at Ryan with his blue eyes. 'Do you think I've lost it Ryan?' 'Matt, that's crazy.' Matt sighed deeply, looking up at the screen again. 'Every time I watch this tape, I see five more mistakes that I know I shouldn't have made. Every time I see this match over again, I see an old habit that I am reverting back to.' Matt picked up the remote and set it on the TV. 'I've been doing this for almost a decade, do you think I'm starting to lose my touch for the ring?' Ryan walked over next to Matt, and sat down on the bench in her gray sweatpants. 'Matt, please. It's one match, you need to work off the ring rus---' 'I DON'T HAVE RING RUST!' Matt screamed. 'After doing this my whole life, you don't get ring rust.' 'Right,' Ryan sighed out. 'Just get back into the flow. You haven't wrestled since last August. Now come on, they're starting to worry about you.' 'They are?' Ryan smiled. 'Of course. Now let's go, get dressed, we'll go to dinner.' 'Okay,' Matt replied. 'Just need to get dressed.' Ryan left the room, and Matt ran the white towel through his hair. He got up, and walked into his bathroom. Soon after, he opened up his medicine and pulled out a bottle of painkillers, putting three into his hand and popping them into his mouth. He looked into the mirror, and stared right back into his own eyes with a stare that would eat right through most men's souls. He softly whispered to himself, 'Don't let me lose it now...'
