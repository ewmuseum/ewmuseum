Love Geometric - II

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/29/2002 October 23rd, 2002 Wednesday Morning 'Kent, what in the hell are we doing here?' Lucas Devol asked, as Kent Anthason pulled up into the driveway of the Malone residence. Despite the fact that it was a really sunny day in Mesa, Arizona, it was also a fairly breezy day. 'If you're here to beat up Joey, you and I both know that he's in New York for 21st Century Wrestling.' 'I'm not here for him.' Kent said, dialing a number into his cellular phone. He waited for the person on the other end to pick up the phone. When the person did, Kent spoke. 'Hello, Keri.' It took her a full ten seconds to realize who it was. 'Kent? What do you want?' Keri asked, in a worrying tone. She didn't expect a phone call from Anthason. Not now. Anthason put his hand on the phone's bottom end and turned to Devol. Devol looked at him questioningly, before Anthason spoke, 'Go back to the hotel, I'll call you when I need you.' Devol was surprised, but he nodded. Kent stepped out of his vehicle, a black sporty number, and walked the stone path to the front door of his house. As he did so, Lucas drove Kent's car away from the house, and Kent spoke into the phone again. 'I suggest that you open the front door.' Kent reached the door just as it swung open, revealing Keri standing in front of him. The breeze blew into the home, throwing her long blonde hair and the skirt of her light blue flowery dress around a bit. The look on her face suggested a combination of bewilderment, worry, and, strangely enough, a slight hint of happiness. 'What are you doing here, Kent?' 'I came to be with you.' Anthason said with a crack of a grin. He took off his sunglasses and looked into Keri's green eyes. She had to blink and turn away to make him stop. 'Why? Don't you know what Joey's going to do to you if and when he finds out that you came by to see you?' Keri asked, pointedly. Anthason simply shrugged in response to this, and smiled. 'What's he gonna do about it? He's three thousand miles away in Uniondale. While you, for some reason, decided to come home. Why?' Anthason asked. 'Are you tired of being on the road so much?' Keri nodded solemnly and waved for Anthason to come in, as the breeze had begun to bother her. Anthason strolled inside the home of who is actually his enemy. Once in, Anthason walked over and saw the family couch before he sat down on it. He looked over at Keri, who sat down on the other side of the sofa, as far away from Anthason as possible. After a short period of silence, Anthason spoke again. 'Keri, I know I'm not the type to give advice, but if you're tired of all of the travel, you could always not come with him.' Kent suggested. 'Nothing's making you go.' 'That's the thing!' Keri cried, 'I have to go! He's my husband, we want to be as together as much as possible!' 'But you don't like the travel?' Anthason questioned. He, himself, had travel problems when he was in both Action and Asylum, but Joe Campbell had previously made it clear that he wasn't presently wanted in Asylum. 'I didn't mind it at first, but... but... when he signed that contract to 21w, it made things a whole lot more messy for me.' Keri quietly said. 'I... I don't know. I've done a lot for him. I helped make him a better person. I just don't know if... if...' '...if it was worth it?' Anthason finished for her. 'If he appreciates it.' corrected Keri. Kent took a moment to pause and think about it, before he came to the only conclusion that he was capable of, and proceeded to speak it, 'Oh.' Keri turned slowly toward Kent, 'What do you know about this, anyway?' Kent sighed and reflected on his relationship with Alyssa Cleeda. A strong and carefree young woman, she was. A pretty woman, too, who resembled Keri in a few ways. Their love was an unusual one, frought with a few perilous adventures and figures in the reckless abandonment that passed for a relationship. Unfortunately, it didn't particularly last more than a year, because she had abruptly disappeared. Not to mention that Joey Malone was the last person to see her. 'Let's just say that I know a few things.' was all Kent could say in response.
