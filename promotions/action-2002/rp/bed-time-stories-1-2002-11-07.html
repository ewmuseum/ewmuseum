Bed Time Stories [1]

7 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.07.02 “Hurry up and I’ll tuck you in for the night, bud.” The father says as he opens the door and waits for his young son to follow him into his room. He runs past his father with his little legs moving as fast as they can. He is wearing his Monsters Inc. Pajamas, his favourite pair that he always has to wear so he can get to sleep. He builds up speed and leaps onto his bed and bounces a few times while giggling. His father can only smile as he pulls his Monsters Inc. bedspread over him. “Goodnight son. Sweet dreams.” “Daddy?” His son asks with the biggest puppy eyes he had ever used. “Yes son?” “Could you pwease read me a good night story?” He asks his dad knowing there was no way he could say no. “Sure, a quick story isn’t going to hurt anyone tonight. What kind of story do you want to hear?” “One with dragon’s and bad guys all over the place and only someone special can save the day!” His son excitedly says to his dad. “Okay,” he softly says as he brings his finger to his mouth letting his son know it is time to settle down. “This story takes place a long, long time ago. Everyone was afraid of Youngnius and Boldificus, the two meanest rules in all the land of Action. They lived in a spooky castle outside of the city and were guarded by the scariest dragons anyone had ever seen. They were known as the Risky Dragons because you were taking such a big risk if you tried to mess with them. All there terrible ideas came from one book. The Pinkius Book of Evil.” “W-w-what was in-inside?” His son stutters out, already scared of what his father had told him. “Well, this wasn’t any normal book you see. It had all the evil secrets and bad ideas that Boldificus could come up with. You see, people always tried to steal the book from him because he wouldn’t have any special powers without it. Many people tried and tried to steal the book but no one was ever succesful. One day though, the smallest man in all the lands knew he was the only hope to get the book.” “Daddy, what was his name?” his son asks before his dad had any chance to continue. “Everyone called him Lionheart because for such a little guy, he was very brave. They said he had the heart of a lion but knew there was no way he could save them. So one day he packed a few of his things and left Action in search of the Pinkius. The biggest problem to start was going to be getting through the Asylum Forest. Youngnius always said it was the worst forest eeevvveeerrr!!” “What happened next?” “You closed your eyes and went to sleep. This story is a long one and we will not be able to finish it in one night. So sleep tight and don’t let the bed bugs bite.”
