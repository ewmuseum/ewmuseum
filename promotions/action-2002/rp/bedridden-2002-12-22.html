Bedridden

22 December 2002

Date: 12-22-02 Have you ever had a moment in life that totally drove you down another road? A moment that made you realize that you were fully capable of making something of the aforementioned life. Reality strikes you harder than a brick glove and you come to the conclusion: You actually have some talent. Jonny had succeeded in grasping hold of this particular feeling. On the latest episode of Pressure Point, Jonny had beaten LLB. LLB! One of the biggest names in Action, quite possibly the biggest name in the world of wrestling, and Jonny had gained a victory over him. It was a certain euphoria that Jonny had felt upon beating him, an unexplainable ecstasy. After the match, when Jonny got back to his apartment, he decided he was going to go out and buy a television and VCR. He was going to buy a phone and call Reed Young and have a copy of the match UPS’d to him. Jonny was going to relive that match as many times as he could. So, he did all of this, and he popped the video into the player. What a great match it had been up to a certain point... That point? The tarnished win. Sean Cylear had been the reason that Jonny was able to execute The Union Bomb on LLB. Sean Cylear was more or less the reason Jonny had been so ecstatic with himself. AND more to the point, Sean Cylear was the reason Jonny hadn’t left his bed in two days. Jonny was angry with himself more than anything else. He was angry that he’d taken advantage of the situation like he had, albeit unknowingly, but it wasn’t fair nonetheless. Then, after the match, Jonny should have come back down and saved LLB from the wrath of that dastardly Sean Cylear. Not because Jonny and LLB were friends, but because LLB was already beaten down and had no chance of fighting back. Jonny wasn’t really sure now what he was going to do about the situation, God knows he had one hand full with his friend Airborn Tyler. The other hand was full of dealing with The Elements. Jonny didn’t a free hand that he could use to resolve the affair that pertained to LLB and Sean Cylear. He reckoned it was best to let the two men deal with it themselves. Could he though? Oh Jesus...what’s a soldier to do?
