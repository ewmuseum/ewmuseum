Seven Years: VI

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So, here we are again, in the basement of Smilthy's restaurant. Duncan Fletcher, or as he's known by his actual given name, Ryan D. Maxwell Jr., was being held at gunpoint by a Lieutenant Commander from the United States Navy. 'So, what's your deal old man? You look to be fifty. You tellin' me you're still a member of the Navy, at your rank AND at your age? Who'd you piss off to be demoted?' Fletcher asked. 'You really have bumped your head, haven't you, Ryan? I retired seven years ago.' 'You said 'we' just a little while ago, asshole. Who're you working for?' Fletcher asked, 'And why do you give a damn about me?' 'We'll get to that,' Rivera said, 'But let's talk about you. Lieutenant Ryan Maxwell Jr., born on August 17th, 1967. Formerly an American citizen, now a Canadian citzen residing in Vancover. Former member of the Navy SEALS. Expert in hand-to-hand combat and marksmenship.' Fletcher blinked. 'You, my friend, were a product of one of the Pentagon's black projects back during the Gulf War. A team of seven soldiers instilled with the sole purpose of killing. The Seven Sins, they were called. They were responsible for taking down many Iraqis. After the war, we started using the Seven Sins for many anti-terrorism and assassination missions, many of which never even made the news. Well, unfortunately, in 1993, one such assassination mission in Somalia led to an ambush, and four of the members were killed. Two managed to escape, while another was listed missing in action.' Rivera said, nary a word hesitated. 'It's taken us eight years to even hear about you after you went missing off the failure in Somalia, and by then, you had found your way to Canada. You've gone out of your way to stay out of sight, but then you stick your head out for wrestling, and then this Asylum thing.' 'So, why do you have such an interest in my affairs?' Fletcher asked. 'There was a glitch in our system, Ryan. We used a lot of stimulants and depressants to keep the Sins in line when they weren't in combat. Some of them caused the remaining soldiers to snap and start killing civilians, hallucinating into believing that they're enemies or demons or something. We eventually had to put them in asylums.' Rivera said. 'And you think I might be affected by this?' 'Of course. You think we didn't hear about the incident in Colorado, my friend?' Rivera said, tightening his grip on his Beretta, 'Three men were found dead in a warehouse last October. Two men were shot in the head with nine millimeter bullets, the third had his neck broken with a chain. There weren't any fingerprints, but an eyewitness vaguely saw a man emerging from the warehouse wearing a trenchcoat, much like yours. It turns out that these three men used to work for the Asylum and potentially had information that could finally get a lead on Joe Campbell's arrest. With their deaths, Joe Campbell's name goes free. Now, if I'm not mistaken, didn't you work for Joe Campbell... and continue to do so?' Fletcher laughed. 'Oh, come on. You think hallucinations had anything to do with what I did? I think you're just trying to cover for your mistake.' 'Mistake?' Rivera questioned. 'You want to know why I went missing? Because I knew that you were just gonna get rid of the rest of us. I haven't hallucinated jack shit, Rivera. Chances are, neither did the other guys before you went and got rid of them. I work for Campbell because he protects me, and in return, I protect him. I protect his investments. I still have my own battles to fight. You, on the other hand, you're just a politician with some skeletons in his closet. So, who are you working for?' The sound of a click was heard from the gun held by Lcdr. Alec Rivera. 'You are not at liberty to ask me that question.' He fired. He missed, because Fletcher performed an emergency roll to the left. Surprised by not just the roll, but by the kickback of his gun, Rivera couldn't evade a tackle to the ground by Fletcher. A brief struggle for the gun occured, before Fletcher managed to emerge with the gun, aimed at Rivera's throat. Fletcher, with the gun firmly in his hand, slowly got up off of Rivera. Then he reached into Rivera's coat and pulled out the Desert Eagle as well. 'Get the fuck up.' Fletcher said, backing away from Rivera. Rivera, slowly and warilly rose to his feet. Now the ball was in Fletcher's court. 'I'm not gonna ask you again, god dammit. Who the fuck are you working for?' 'Do you think it matters? Shoot me. What he'll do to me if I told you will be ten times worse.' Rivera snidely said. 'You think?' Fletcher said, with an evil grin on his face, as various painful methods of torture started to come to his mind.
