derilict ruminations: bucket full of stillborns

27 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/27/2002 here my life stood. a bucket full of stillborns. how would that feel? experiencing death before, breath? others fly out of nothingness through the open skyroof of life you, by no fault of you own take the revolving door. and forget to get off. you’re back inside. y-ou-self abbreviated. and then- 3 days, 20 grams of coke, 4 shots of smack, 3 rocks of crack, and a partridge in a pear tree. ...minus the partridge in a pear tree. I don’t even if I said that line correctly. But guess what? I don’t give a fuck. i’m sure you don’t It’s been 72 hours. 72 fun filled hours of destitute depravity. Sure I smell like shit, look like shit, and fill like shit. it’s only fair that the outside mirrors what’s within But it was worth with. Being homeless, it was quite an arduous task, but I persevered and was able to score shitloads of fun silly willy drugs to pass the time. See kids, if you put your mind to it, you too can harvest gratuitous amounts of illegal substances too. And remember: drugs are cool! Besides getting shitfaced, I had time to think. What right do any of us have to be born? What right do we have to live? You’re the product of one out of millions of sperm cells and hundreds of eggs. There are over 12 billion people in the world. Who decided your mom and dad were to be your parents? If a blind midget and crab handed hermaphrodite have a baby, is that baby just fucked? What right do you have to impose your inferior genetic material on someone completely innocent? That’ll change once I’m in charge. I’m having everyone screened. You fail the screening process, you lose your balls or ovaries. Fuck natural selection. If natural selection where still doing its jobs, the world wouldn’t be filled with so many fucking fucktards. I climbed out of the dumpster this mourning and before me was a mass of defective chromosomes, as far as the eye’s can see. Bustling to and fro as if they matter. People make me sick...that and the dirty diapers I used as a pillow. Defiantly the dirty diapers. But defiantly people. “People = Shit” as the song goes. You fucks don’t even know what you have. You all run around taking, and taking. Fucking each other over for poorly made green paper emblazoned with deadmen. You eat, sleep, piss, fuck, then die, only for your seeds to continue the cycle. You take for granted the gift you were given. Do you realize you can hear, see, touch, taste, and smell. Do you realize the awesomeness behind your senses? Have you ever once stopped to think about the fact you are you? Think about it. What if your mom and dad never met? What if you mom really liked giving blowjobs? You’d end up coming out of her intestinal track instead of a womb. Think about that. The next time you jack-off. You just took millions of potential lives and wasted them on a 2 dimensional image of some slut, or a projection of pussy on a computer screen. What if your father was as much of a loser as you were? You would’ve been floating down some septic tank on a napkin, instead of here listening to my words. I want you all to fully comprehend this statement. There is no reason why I should exist long enough to finish this sentence, or you exist long enough to finish hearing it...yet here we both are. Alive and well. I’ve realized this these few days. I was with nothing. And needed nothing. You trivialize life with wants and needs. You live, barely, but you’re not alive. No one is anymore. . . . . . . . . . . . . Meh. Being misanthropic and hypercritical requires to much caring. Forget, all I’ve told you. I return you to your regularly scedule lives... already in progress.
