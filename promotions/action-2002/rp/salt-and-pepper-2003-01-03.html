Action! — Tales

Salt and Pepper

3 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/03/03 Intertwined with ‘The Domino Effect’ Series… Previous Parts: Prologue, A Day in the Drawers Amsterdam, Holland (The Next Day) Mick was normal, and like all normal people, he had his good side and his bad side. It was common for Mick to ruin ready made plans, even after negotiating on the phone for over twenty minutes nonstop, to be exact. Yep. It wouldn’t be the last time Hunter spoke to Mick that night. Actually, he ended up phoning him back in a matter of ten minutes. After spending A Day in the Drawers, he had to live up to Mick’s childish behavior. ‘I’m so (x10) sorry.’ Or ‘it’ll never (x5) happen again’ were his most common catchphrases. What else could Hunter say but ‘It’s okay’ (translation: fuck off) or ‘I understand fully’ (translation: what the fuck?). You get the point. After all, Mick was one of Hunters best friends. He respected his flaws, even if they were more common then usual. Instead of meeting at Hunters hefty abode, they’d meet at Mick’s favorite place. Macy’s Truck stop: ‘Drinks, gambling and girls.’ (Flashing with bright lights) Hunter had already driven thirty kilometers before seeing any indication to the truck stop. He really had no clue why he was driving there in the first place. He wasn’t in the mood to get drunk all over again, even if he tried to ignore the drinks, it wouldn’t do him any good. Good reddens, he was hanging with Slick’ Mick for crying out loud. *** After driving another fifteen kilometers, he pulled into a dust filled parking lot. The majority of cars parked looked like piles of shit. They WERE piles of shit… An old rickety sign barely stood, reading: ‘Macy’s Truck Stop’ in bright pink letters. ‘Mick’s really lost his class.’ Hunter sighed. He walked into the door-less building. Smoke from lit cigars surrounded the room. A diverse amount of people sat on stools, and benches, enjoying their cheap beer. Peanut shells made a substitute for expensive tile. **Crunch! Snap! Pop!** everywhere he walked, he stepped on another shell. A blonde, sluttish, large breasted woman walked across the room, into a private bungalo. ‘HELLLLLLLLO MACY’ Hunter strutted towards her. At first, the door from which she entered wouldn’t open. Hunter thought it was locked. The sign above it read ‘Keep Out!’ Now that he had a better look, it was only the hinges that were ‘fecked up. After all, this place looked like it was going to collapse in a matter of seconds. (Meaning: half of the furniture was either destroyed or broken.) **CREEEEEEEEEEK! BANG!* ‘Um, Mick?’ Hunter questioned, walking into the room. Nobody answered. ‘WHO THE HELL ARE YOU? GET OUT! GET OUT NOW! THIS IS A PRIVATE ROOM FOR OUR MOST TRUSTED CUSTOMERS ONLY! GET OUT OR I’LL CALL THE COPS, I SWEAR, I’LL CALL THE COPS…you must leave now!’ ‘Jesus!’ Hunter flung backwards, hitting a small button on the wall. The lights suddenly turned on. ‘Holy shit.’ Hunter muttered to himself. His surroundings were extremely different. It was as if he was in a five star restaurant. Everything looked perfect. Absolutely perfect, incept for… ‘OH…MY…GOD…YOU…YOU…YOUR NOT SUPPOSED TO BE HERE!’ Macy yelled so loud, no pun intended. ‘YOU SON OF A MOTHER FUCKING, SHIT, ASS, LICKING BITCH’ She charged towards Hunter, who dashed out of the way. ‘Whoa, LADY…CALM DOWN’ Hunter screamed just as loud. ‘I’m SORRY…I DIDN’T MEANT TO…’ He added, being chased around the small bar. ‘I’M LOOKING FOR SOMEONE…HIS NAME IS MICK, SLICK’ MICK…I THOUGHT HE’D BE DINING IN THIS PRIVATE ROOM…’ She suddenly stopped. ’Slick Mick?’ Gasping for breath. ‘Yeah.’ Hunter extended his arms in the air. ‘Jesus, your one tempered bit-, gorgeous lady…’ He stuttered for words. ‘Why did you go insane like that? You could’ve warned me.’ ‘Oh, sorry.’ Macy laughed. ‘Well, I got frightened…I mean, look at this place, and then look at outside…’ She stopped, and then continued. ‘As stupid as it sounds, we spent all of our money to fast. We ran out in the process of making this room, and this room only. It actually happens to a lot of ‘first-timers’, after all, I’ve never run a restaurant before. I wanted everything to be absolutely perfect, but the tables turned, everything’s a disaster. ‘Ah, I see.’ Hunter muttered. The room covered only a little area, but for what it was worth, it didn’t matter. Purple velvet curtains, red mahogany tables, leather chairs, pool tables, fancy drinks. Everything was in perfect position. It was all there. ‘I need you to do me a favor.’ Hunter implied. ‘Is Mick dining here by any chance? He’s expecting me.’ ‘Slick? Yeah, he’s here. Name please.’ Macy’s suddenly switched back into her ‘working’ tone. ‘Hunter.’ ‘Hunter Levy Mackenzie’
