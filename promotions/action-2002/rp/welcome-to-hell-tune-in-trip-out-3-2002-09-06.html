Welcome to Hell: Tune in, Trip Out [3]

6 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept 6th, 2002 Before we go any further into this random mess of excitement known only as the most desolate, meaningless town on earth... I just want to tell you some things about a certian person who lives there. His name is, Don the Drug Addict. Some commonly refer to him as Drunken Don, or Downer Don, Overdose Don, or even Drown in your own vomit Don. Names are meaningless, however. You must really meet him to get the full effect of Don the Drug Addict. What's more, is Don the Drug Addict is quite a common voice in Nelsonville. People hold him of high regards because of his visions and interpretations. He has no real talent, however, he's just tripping. As far as anyone knows he doesn't do drugs. Everyone thinks he has some kind of disease that came with having his special powers. He's quite the manipulator when he wants to be. So, if you ever talk to anyone in Nelsonville, just call him Don Quixote. Anyhow, Alyssa tells Kent about this wonderful oracle of God, known only as Don Quixote. She takes him to his apartment. Outside the wind is cold and bitter as Alyssa hits his apartment intercom button. All of a sudden a voice is heard over it. 'Yes?' 'Hey, Don. This is Alys. Can you open up the door for us so we can come in?' Alyssa questioned. 'Who-- Who are you, again? And what's the password!?' 'This is Alyssa. Remember, I saved your life last year from the man eating Saint Bernards.' 'OH! Oh, Alyssa Cleeda?' 'Yeah! That's right, Don!' 'How are you doing?' 'Fine. Could you open up the door? It's freezing out here!' 'What's the password?' Before Alyssa can even think about a password a booming voice from the background yells in Don's general direction. 'YO, MANG. YA GONNA HIT DIS OR WHA?' The voice sounded remarkably like... Eddie Cheno? As fast as Cheno said it, Don hushed him. 'What are you talking about? Hit what? Your mom? Hahahah! Get a life, Eddie!' Meanwhile, Kent and Alyssa are freezing on the doorstep. 'I dunno wha ya talkin' bout, mang. Ya passed eet to me, now i'ma passin' it back.' 'Hey, Don. How about the door?' Alyssa intterupted. 'Oh yeah!' He said, as the door suddenly flung open and knocked Kent unconcious. Alyssa stood there for a second, looked at Kent, then looked at the surroundings. Then, the unexpected happened. From nowhere, and I mean absolutely nowhere, came good ol' Cyanide. Anyone remember Cyanide? IWO, anybody? He's dressed up in shorts and a very bright tank top. His sunglasses are halfway slid down his nose, and there's bright green sunscreen ran across the bridge of his nose, and on both cheeks. He blew his whistle very, very loudly. 'KENT ANTHASON! IT IS A VIOLATION OF THE BEACH BY HANDBOOK RULES FORTY TWO AND A THIRD THAT NO ONE CAN BE HIT, OR HIT AN AUTOMATIC OPENING DOOR! NOW PICK YOUR LAZY ASS UP AND GET OUT OF THIS COLD, OR ELSE I COULD SLAM YOU WITH ANOTHER VIOLATION.' Kent doesn't move. Alyssa nudges him with her foot. 'Uhh. Cyanide? Nice to see you and everything, but I don't think he's getting up.' With that, Cyanide takes his foot and drags him in the apartment complex. Leaving him on the cold floor he gives a simple nod to Alyssa and walks back out the door to parts unknown. Alyssa picks up Kent and takes him to Don's apartment. The door is shut, but there's smoke pouring from the crack below the door. Alyssa knocks quite loudly. There's a loud rustling, a vacuum cleaner sound, sudden smashing of several things. Don the Drug Addict opens the door with vampire eyes and smiles a crooked smile. Alyssa fowns. 'Have you been smoking marijuana, Don?' 'Absolutely not, never touched a drug in my life. Might jeapordize my visions and foretelling.' 'Whatever.' Kent kinda comes to during this point, and groans quite loudly. Alyssa sits him down in a chair. Eddie Cheno, Don, and a guy whose name is supposedly Viovis are sitting around. Viovis is smoking a cigarette and laughing at about everything while making extremely crude outbursts. The thing is, nothing he says makes sense at all but it sure seems to crack him up. Apparently Viovis and Cheno were having a discussion. 'Eh, Vio. Ya evah seen tha movie Half Baked?' 'Orson Wells and the eluded tsunami from frying pans?' Viovis then starts to laugh uncontrollably. Don looks at him with that 'Shut up, you're going to give me away look.' 'So, how have you been lately Alyssa?' He asks her in a nonchalant voice. 'Oh, fine. I just brought Kent here so you can help him escape Nelsonville and give him your almighty guidance.' 'That I can do, Alyssa. BUT WAIT! WHAT THE HELL IS THAT! IN THE WINDOW!' Kent's still kinda half asleep so he doesn't look, but Alyssa turns around abruptly and stares at nothing, really as Don wraps something around his arm, sticks himself with a needle while smoking a pipe and swallowing a few pills and dropping a few sheets. She turns around and Don's back to laying back. 'Oh, I guess I just saw a.. Bird or... Something.' Kent sits up in his seat and speaks for the first time. 'So how the hell do I get out of here?' Viovis, at that statement cracks up again. His giggling gets on Alyssas nerves as she gives him a sign to stop it. Cheno, red eyes and all, is sitting back puffing on a blunt. 'The, uh, thing is... Sometimes people can't get out of this city. There IS a way, though. If you go downtown there's a man named Big D. He's the only one who really knows the way out. He works at Burger King. ' 'Ah, hah hah hah! AhahaHAHAHAHAH!' Viovis is laughing. And George Bush is still an idiot. 'Anyhow, can you read his fortune while you're at it? I'm kinda interested.' Alyssa asks Don the Drug Addict. Or Don Quixote. 'Sure.' Don grabs Kents hands and acts like he's all in a trance and stuff. Which is really just a show. 'I see... Many... Uh... Clouds, and rocks... And a bunch of blue liquid.' 'Water, you mean?' Kent asks. 'Wait. I see... Drugs. Lots of drugs.' With that, Don snaps back to a normal state and screams 'AAAHHH! EVERYONE LOOK THERE'S A GIANT BAT!' Kent and Alyssa turn around to look as he whips out a paper and some white powder. He snorts it quickly up with a straw as Kent and Alyssa turn back around. Paper and straw are gone, as he just shrugs. 'Must've been my imagineation.' He snorts quite loudly. 'Yeah. Uhh...' And with that, he passes out. Alyssa sighs, gives Cheno a high five and helps Kent up. 'Let's go find that Big D guy. I'll see you later, Cheno... And Viovis.' 'HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHHAHAAAAAAHAHAAHHHAAAA!' 'Right.' They walk out the door. Don suddenly sits up as soon as they close the door behind them, looks around, and shoots up some. He sighs quite loudly, then proceeds to fall into a drug induced trip. All the LSD leads to somewhat of a bad trip, as Cheno stares at him while Marijuana smoke clouds his eyes. Don screams 'OH MY GOD!' Cheno stares with a wondering look. 'What ya seein', mang?' Don replies, 'You'll never believe it. It's... It's... Tim Couch, man. Oh my god.' 'Hahah. Tim Couch, mang? I think ya had too many tabs and too many hits, mah bro.' 'No, I'm not tripping out! Look!' Don points and Cheno looks. There he is, in all his might and glory. Tim Couch. 'Hey, guys. You mind if I take a hit?' Cheno hands him the blunt, as he warns him to be careful. Couch takes a hit and suddenly starts to cough. Cheno laughs quite loud. 'Good shit, aint it?' Tim proceeds to cough, harder this time. 'Aint it?' Couch falls to his knees and grabs his throat. 'Huh? What'cha think? Ya high as a kite?' Tim suddenly throws up his internal organs. And I mean all of them. Eddie Cheno's eyes get big and his eyebrows round off with a surprised look. Don just stares at him with a stoned look. Viovis has gone quiet for the moment. 'That was the trippiest shit I've ever seen in my life, man.' Tim falls over and dies. His heart is still pounding on the floor. Blood stains most of the carpet as Tim's eyes roll to the back of his head. Cheno nudges the body with his foot, and gives a disgusted look. 'Mang, ya nothin' but trouble. Ya decayin' corpse is already startin' to smell like sheet. Now we havta get rid of ya, ya sonofabitch! That would suck, ya know, dying from drug overdoses?' 'Yeah, man. No shit.' Don replies. They both look over to Viovis who's staring intently on Tim Couch's corpse and internal organs... 'HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAH!!!' And then, they ALL started to laugh.
