end of the [P]urple [C]olored [P]eople:

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/20/2002 I assume you want to know who I am. I’ll tell you, though I know you won’t believe me. I’m a mirror. Yes. A mirror. “But inanimate objects can’t talk.” You say. Well I say, I’m a mirror. Do not try to rationalize this. Do not try to make sense of this. Do not dismiss this. Accept this as fact. Believe in me as you believe the sun will raise and fall. I am a mirror. Now listen to what I have to say. You may be wondering just why I’ve decided to talk to you right now. I’m sure you’ve seen how Invalid has polluted your airwaves with promo, after enigmatic, meaningless, empty promo. And I’m sure you’d like me to elucidate these works of art shit for you. I will not make sense of them. This is not because I do not know what they mean, or represent. Rather, it is out of respect for the man who has created them. They are his. He will tell you when he is ready. I will however, shed some light on their creator. He harbors a great deal of pain, mental anguish, sorrow, remorse, and worst of all emptiness. Peer inside his heart and you’ll most likely come across a piece of loose-leaf reading I owe you: one soul Signed, God I feel for him dearly. I emphasize with him greatly. I-like him- am invalid. As a mirror I exist only to reflect. I am not myself, but what ever is set before me. If an apple is in front of me, I am an apple. If an orange is set in front of me, I am an orange. You can never see me. Whenever you look at me, you only see yourself, but never can you see me. For this reason there is no me. I am. Yet I am not. Such is the case with Invalid. But he deals with his absurdity, with alcohol, marijuana, cocaine, and all their other mind altering friends. His favorite being “angle dust”. This is wrong. Not because he is killing brain cells at an exponential rate. It is because all these substances only disillusion. Distract from reality. They are like taking aspirin for a gapping shotgun wound in the gut. It may allay the pain, but the problem is still very much alive, and getting worst by the second. It pains me to see him agonize so much. The drugs will not end his pain. Only make it even more devastating once the high wears off. I have to make him see the truth. And yes, at times I bring him to reality quite harshly. I will do what I feel is necessary. I will speak to him: your pain is a harsh pain but your pain killers are flawed this kind of aguish far exceeds the boundary modern medicine can not aid you all the angle dust in the world can’t help you phencyclidine can not raise the dead and she is dead you have to come to terms with that you can’t run away from that who you were drove her to it you sickened her you were her cancer the world made you malignant you metastasized in her that’s what killed her that is what killed her they were carcinogenic they ALL were carcinogenic resume your vendetta resume your vendetta resume your vendetta resuym eouv rendetta reyome suve urndetta yeome venu dettaseu mvrrse eeyo dttauneu!! “ARHHHHHHG!!!” Invalid let out a primordial roar, as he slammed his fist into the mirror splintering it into a web. He then dropped to the ground, a pitiful sobbing mess. And there he laid for the next 15 hours. …until the phone rang.
