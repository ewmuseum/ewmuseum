Bitter Sweet:A Very Aroma Christmas

24 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The snow covered town of West Hampton, New York was once again host to another great Aroma/Conspiracy party. This one was limited to various family and friends. Taylor Robert Wade, Scott Redding, Jack Jackson, Madison Clarke, Jimmy Bradley, and even Jonathan's brother Jerome Leary were all in attendance. Roberto Aromadeus, Benjamin Bannon and Benecio Aromadeus were present from Aroma's side of the fence. Of course the female to male ratio had to be tweaked a little bit, so quite a few anonymous females were in attendance as well. Veronica sat in the recliner while Jonathan entertained the other party guest, and looked down at her engagement ring, the one that Jonathan gave her at exactly 12 midnight to officially get their engagement going. 'Hey V, come her baby.' Veronica snapped out of her trance and made her way over to Jonathan, squeezing through the crowd of guest that were present. Jonathan hugged V, and wrapped his arm around her waist. 'For those of you that don't know, I just wanted to let you all know that Veronica and I are getting married. We haven't set a date yet, but it could be as early as spring 2003, so keep your eyes glued to your mailboxs for wedding invitations.' 'A toast to my brother Jonathan,' stated Jerome Leary aka Romey Ransom. Glasses clinged, sending some cristal over the edge of the glass and to the floor, but Jonathan's lucrative PIW contract would cover the bill, the only thing he had to do was keep 'V' smiling. Thoughts ran through Aroma's head, while Conspiracy and Ransom walked off to discuss Ransom's IOW career.
