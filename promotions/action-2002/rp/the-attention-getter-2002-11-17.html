The Attention Getter

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: [Nov. 17. 2002.] Unforseen Consequences had ended, and it was very unforseen that Jonathan Jerome Leary would lose the PIW World Title. After all Jim Johnson said that Sylo was just there to take out KroW. But we all know what happened. Sylo saw Rune Winters give Jonathan 'NOW YOUR FUCKED!' and proceeded to pin him. Sweet Aroma remained backstage not really knowing how Jonathan would react, but she prepared for the worst, and hoped that his slight set back wouldn't hurt their relationship. And Jim Johnson tried to play it off, by screaming for Sylo to get off JCON, but that fat mother fucker with a small brain didn't get off 'One Letter Better', and in the process ruined any chance of the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling remaining at the Pinnacle of the wrestling world. Sweet Aroma was never a fan of Jim Johnson's, she didn't like the way he conducted business, and felt that Jonathan's title reign wasn't getting enough attention. Veronica felt that Johnson was more concerned with watching Sylo and KroW bring ratings in by killing each other, and suddenly threw Jonathan into the equation, just to add some name value to the war, but JCON was never really into it... UNTIL HIS TITLE WAS BEING JEOPARDIZED. 'I wouldn't be suprised if Jonathan walked out of PIW over this.' stated Sweet Aroma backstage. Jonathan then entered the locker room, and grabbed his Silver Jansport Duffle, and exited the room, not even acknowledging his girlfriend. This definantly got V's attention.
