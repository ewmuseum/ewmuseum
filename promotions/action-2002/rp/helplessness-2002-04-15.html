Helplessness

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/15/2002 Eight years ago, I was in a traffic accident. I'm sure most of you know that sad story. My girlfriend, the love of my life, lost her memory. But I lost something else on that night. For a month afterwards, I couldn't use either of my arms. My right arm was broken and my left one was cut so badly that it required a large amount of bandages. Enough to keep my elbow from flexing all the way. My left arm also had a broken thumb from the accident, and since I'm left-handed, I couldn't write particularly well. So, I had to take all of my tests orally. Not to mention the fact that Gabriel Landers hadn't forgiven me for the locker incident. A few months before the accident, I saw Gabriel putting a note in Keri's locker. Well, I obviously wasn't very happy with this, so I confronted him about it. I took the note out of the locker and put it in my pocket, and we got into a fight. My nose was bloodied, so I used the note to wipe some of it off. Gabriel was a tad peeved at me after the accident. So he attacked me when I was in my helpless state. I needed someone to help me defend myself. I am the same way right now. Keri tells me that it's all right, that Rashard's just going through a phase and he'll get over it. I don't believe her. Rashard's the guy who flattened all of the tires to my car, so I wouldn't be able to see her for a basketball game. Rashard's the guy who told Keri that I hated Bob Seger, her favorite musician. Rashard's the guy who built up so many lies and fallacies between me and Keri that I thought I would never see our relationship go through. I thoughr he changed when I saw him in the IWO. He certainly seemed that way. But no, he didn't change. He continues to lie to himself to this day. Maybe that's what started this. Maybe I'm just helpless to stop it from happening. Whatever it is, Rashard's intent on fighting me. What I'm afraid of is the fact that he's going to end up dragging Keri into this, too. I don't care what happens to me. I can't let anything happen to her.
