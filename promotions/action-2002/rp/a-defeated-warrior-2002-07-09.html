A Defeated Warrior

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 09, 2002 Human beings are put onto this earth, not to care, love or honor. They are put on this earth to compete, to prove who is the greatest of all human beings. Constantly humans are battling amongst themselves. Whether it be a simple game of hockey, or a world war...humans are always thriving to be the best. Losing is not an option when a human has the mind frame when entering a contest, he MUST win...it's his wish...his ONLY wish. When you enter a competition, their is always a broken man. With a winner, their always comes a loser. You cannot escape that plain fact...it is written in stone. For one man to feel happiness, another must feel sorrow or rage. It's the law of nature, the law of human kind...the law of the battlefield. Shaw: KroW is really out of it, but Seaman isn’t done yet. He now takes the wrench and... and YES! Slams it into KroW’s face! Ump: The Crowd is going crazy... as Seaman whips KroW into the car... KroW bounces off as Seaman takes hold of that hubcap once more... **Whack!** Shaw: DRILLED! JUST DRILLED INTO ‘THE SATANIC GOD’S’ HEAD! But in the battlefield, no matter what, a human must continue to try. He must thrive and push onward into victory. Even when the odds are against him, he is forced by his pride, to push onward. Losing isn't an option...remember? You are here to WIN. You didn't travel all this way to lose a fight, a game, or any competition to LOSE...you came to WIN. Ump: Seaman looks around... hearing the cheers from the people inside... he takes KroW... ‘Vice Versa’! It’s DONE! Shaw: Dammit! C’mon KroW... kickout. You’re SUPPOSED TO WIN this Title! Ump: One! Even when the odds are against you.... Ump: Two! You mustn't give up... Ump: Thr- Shaw: HAHAHAHAHA! KICKOUT! KICKOUT! Ump: No one, and I mean NO ONE can believe that shit! KroW kicked out... as the Referee tells this to a dumbfounded Simon. You continue...for victory. Because you know...only warriors win. The defeated are merely human beings that are wastes on this earth. Victorious humans are warriors. And the most important place to be a warrior...is in the battlefield. Ump: Seaman takes a step back... Shaw: HE’S SCARED! Ump: Wait! Simon just fell! He tripped over something. Shaw: An- an eight ball!?!? Ump: AN EIGHT BALL! Simon’s shocked... as he picks it up... Shaw: KROW’S UP... Ump: IN HE GOES... Shaw: MOVE KROWY! FATE TELLS YOU TO MOVE!! **CRASH!** Ump: THE EIGHT BALL BREAKS ALL OVER THE CHALLENGER’S HEAD!! THIS IS ECSTATIC! THE PEOPLE ARE GOING INSANE! Shaw: NNNOOO! Ump: ONE! Only.... Ump: TWO! WARRIORS.... Crowd: THREE!! Ump: THREE! Shaw: NNNOOO! **DING DING DING** Ump: The Fans are going insane... SIMON SEAMAN HAS DONE IT!! Woot! Win. ------------------ The darkness poured into his surroundings...was it the dead of night? Or was it his feelings? He had fallen. He had fallen for the three mere seconds that he could've easily kicked out from. The heart of KroW was filled with depression and rage. He ran his fingers across his numerous scars. Head;scar, Arms:scars, Legs:scars, Face:scars. He ran his finger through each and everyone of them. He pressed on the biggest one on his face, allowing small amounts of blood to spill onto the floor. He felt the warmness of his blood...and the feeling it gave him. Anger. He lost. To a warrior named Simon Seaman. 'I lost. Warriors don't lose. I couldn't have lost. I didn't lose. NO! I didn't lose! I don't lose! Because I am a WARRIOR. Warriors DON'T lose!' The echo of his voice traveled throughout the darkness of the unknown area in which he sat himself in. Some could say he was sitting in his own depression and rage. Some could say he was sitting in his own little hole. Or some could say, he was just sitting....a defeated warrior. 'I didn't lose. I didn't lose. I didn't lose. I didn't FUCKING lose!!!!' And as one warrior once said... A Defeated Warrior... is a dangerous warrior. Fin.
