Action! — Tales

I. Steven Denton.

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08.25.02 A young Steven Denton, still in college at the time, dropkicked his opponent to the mat. The man who he faced on this day of days in California wore a red singlet with solid red hair, aptly being named 'Big Red', quickly kicked back up to his feet. He struck Steven in the in the midsection with a boot, before backing him off the ropes and shooting him across the ring. Once he bounced back, Steven ducked a clothesline and reached behind, dropping Red with a neckbreaker. He rolled into a cover, only to have Red kick out and to his feet at two. Again, Big Red struck with a boot to the midsection, this one catching a few ribs and rattling Denton's insides a bit more. Red then hooked his head for a DDT, but Steven, no one to go down easily, quickly tosses him toward the corner with a northern lights suplex. Red, seemingly unaffected, was back up quick, along with Steven. They rushed each other once more, this time Denton swung around behind and applied a rear waistlock. Denton flung a helpless Red overhead for a German suplex, only to have him flip over and land on his feet. Steven's momentum sent him sprawling onto his back, and his feet up over his head. Big Red quickly grabbed them and pushed downward, pinning down Denton's shoulders. The official counts one, but Denton is out quickly and kips back to his feet. Back up, Big Red throws yet another kick to the midsection, only to see an expecting Denton block it. Red, thinking on his feet, quickly threw his opposite boot up towards Steven's head. However, instead of harming him, Red simply found himself being caught and wrapped up by his opponent in a leg capture suplex. After dumping Big Red on his head, Denton swiftly kicked back up to his feet and whipped around, only to see his slightly smaller opponent down on the mat in anguish. Around him, the small indy audience cheered heartily, giving the two young men credit for the show they had just put on. A small, hardly noticeable small crossed Denton's lips. He was happy that he was entertaining the people who paid to see him. Seconds later, Red was back up, and Steven had to refocus himself. After a moment of circling, Denton lunged forward, only to have Red dodge and nail him with a knee to the stomach. Caught in a moment of breathlessness, Steven didn't notice Red reach around and apply a gutwrench on him. What he did notice was when Big Red lifted him off the ground and held him upside down, preparing for his finishing move. Without panicking, Steven smartly wrapped his shins around Red's neck, quickly pulling him over with a catlike hurricanrana. He was no lightweight, but Steven did what was necessary to grab the advantage. It was things like that that got him where he is today. Fast forward a few minutes, where we see Steven absolutely destroy Big Red an out-of-nowhere backdrop driver. Steven, now noticeably bleeding from his lower lip, called out to the audience: 'Time to fuck him up.' Steven knelt over, grabbing Red by his thick red hair, and bringing him slowly to his feet. Red threw blind punches, trying to fight back, only to have Denton nail him square in the gut. Steven then applied a standing headscissors, before hooking both arms behind his back. Steven looked at the audience, who stared back, anticipating what was to come next. A Tiger Driver '91. A move rarely use in the US. The crowd awed in appreciation of the move, and the devastation of its impact all at once. Steven Denton rolled his opponent onto his back, and hooked the leg for three. Match over. Denton victor. --- Big Red. Big Red was a challenge. He threatened a win over me on more than one occasion. He caught me off guard of high risk moves, suplexes, bridges, holds... the works. He almost dropped me on my head with his finishing move, which I would not have been able to kick out of. He was a formidable opponent. He produced a significant challenge and proved himself as a true warrior and tough competitor. He won my respect in minutes and made the feeling of not just wrestling with him, but defeating him that much more grand. He pulled out all the stops, and used every move in his arsenal, as well as some from my arsenal. However, in the end, I just had more stops to pull, and I was crowed victor. I. Steven Denton. Christopher Fox. He is not a formidable opponent. He cannot wrestle, and cannot put on a show that can entertain an in-house crowd like I can. He can be cute and stupidly-hilarious in his own immature little way, but he cannot touch me in the wrestling ring. Card Scramble? Just a crazy stipulation that gives him a better chance of winning. It demeans the match, his credibility, the grandness of capturing a victory over him, and wrestling as a whole to create such an... inept stipulation. His moveset? His moveset rivals that of a small infant... and by small infant, I mean small infant cockroach. And just like a small infant cockroach that won't get out of my bathtub, I'll step on him. I'll step on a 'grown' man and crush him like the little fuck that he is. It won't be long until he finds himself falling victim to a Harbinger. And, as one should know, a Harbinger foreshadows that of which is about come. And what is it that's about to come? Well, you'll have to wait and see. For it won't be long. I. Steven Denton... I win.
