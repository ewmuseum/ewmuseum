At The Airport

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 20, 2002 Date: May 20, 2002 Time: 2:17 p.m. EST Place: JFK Airport | OPEN | The one thing I hate about this business is all the flights. I can't stand waiting around at all these airports for hours at a time. Waiting for the people who work here to call my flight number. And I can't tell you how many times all the different airports have lost my luggage. You'd think that when somebody as important as me...somebody with the celebrity status that I have...the World Champion!! You'd think that they'd at least make sure I'm treated how I know I deserve to be treated. Look at all these fools. They all walk past me without even saying 'You are the greatest!' They never tell me I'm their hero. They never ask me for my autograph. Maybe they're scared. Yes, that's it. They are scared of me because they know what I've done in the past. They are scared of me. Just like LLB, Chris Fox, and even Reed Young are scared. All three of them know that at the biggest pay-per-view of the year I will go in as Champ, and I will walk out as Champ. Quite honestly, I'm not ready to put somebody elses name in the record books. I like the pages saying 'First A Champion - Chris Fear. There have been no other A Champions.' What a great feeling that is. Ever since I started, people said 'He's too small' or 'That guys crazy...he'll never last!' Well, look at where I am right now. In my opinion, I work for the greatest company in the world, with the dumbest boss in the history of mankind. I hold the number one championship in that very company. And at that company's biggest event of the year, I will be in the main event. Now, in that main event, I will be wrestling two of the greatest wrestler's of all time. I hate both of their guts and think they are complete and total cowards, but I'll get to more on that in a minute. Truthfully, LLB is probably in the top three for most known wrestlers in this entire industry today. And Chris Fox isn't far behind. And when I beat them both to within an inch of their lives, well, I guess that's going to put me pretty high up on that list too!! Another thing that I can't stand is people asking me 'What's Chris Fox like?' or 'What's LLB like?' I used to think they were alright. But in that past two weeks they've both stabbed me in the back more times than Homer Simpson's strangles Bart! LLB...what a coward. He never fights you face to face. He tells you one thing, then goes and does the exact opposite. If he ever, and I mean ever, tries to fight me like a man, he knows he has no chance. So he doesn't. He'll run in and hit you with the Erroneous whatever the hell he calls that thing when you aren't looking, and then run away before you have a chance to get up. Then there's Chris Fox. The man talks to an imaginary Pokemon!! I think that's just about all I have to say on that subject because if you don't get the picture already you've got just as many problems as he does!! Now boarding American Airlines Flight 3497 Yep, that's my plane. Finally, it's seems like its been forever. I can't wait for this weeks Pressure Point. That's where I'm off to now. Just watch and wait. I'm not going to say if I'm going to take care of some things at Pressure Point or Juggernaut, but at this time next week LLB and Chris Fox's families will be praying for them...because they, just like anybody else who gets in my way will FEAR THE FEAR!!!! | CLOSE |
