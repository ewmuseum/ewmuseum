Action! — Tales

To Color. 1

9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mark Paulton. He was respected. The king of the house... as he finally took a well deserving seat on his black leather sofa that sat in front of the television screen. He picked up the remote control, turning on the television. He hardly got time to watch, being on the road so much. It was hard... A lot of work. Everyone wanted the top, but when you actually got there... You wanted out. And you wanted out fast. Yet Mark Paulton could live with where he was. He enjoyed it. Nothing abandoned his mind setting as a young 20 year old kid with a giant, surreal dream. His body stayed in check. His youth stayed in check. His popularity stayed in check. But most importantly... his ego stayed in check. Mark was a man that demanded his work show throughout the people that it served. He worked like no other... or so they did say. Therefore he wanted it like no other as well. He was Mark Paulton. A man... that could do anything. A man that was everything. And a man that was whatever you thought him to be, well...
