Controversy

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/08/02 OPEN Without Me? How could.... ? Action could not function without The Ringer according to the man himself, of course! Title: Controversy Date: 25/5/02 Noon EST Place: Denny’s As the scene opens on a rather sunny noon hour, The Ringer sits back in his chair sipping his coffee. Just as he swallows, some big, hefty, 360 pound man with his skinny family walks in. The two kids are scratching at each other and making one hell of a ruckus. The Ringer violently puts his coffee down and covers his ears until the family goes into the smoking section away from The Ringer. I’ve seen a lot of things happen in my life. A lot of people move on. Ugly people and annoying ones like Reed Young. This world is fucked. Not me though, oh no! It’s those imperfect scrubs that are fucked! Take a minute and think what the hell is happening in the world right now! People disregarding rules and drinking too much beer not to mention any names, Jeremy Bold! And to top it off these ugly fucks mess up the world even more! Constant controversy will continue to run and ruin the god damn universe for me! The Ringer looked around, seeing a toothless waitress wearing a ripped Denny’s shirt and holding a foggy disgusting pot of coffee. But before the waitress could offer any coffee to him, The Ringer smiled and waved on by. Totally fucked. I don’t mean to swear but it’s the only way to get people’s attention! Enough with the others. Look at who ran from you. Conrad Ramsey, Tyler Babylon and bicthes like that! All of them are imperfect jerks trying to show their awesome “talent” when they know they just get signed on for the money and try to win a few matches until quitting. Well I’m not quitting. I’m the best Action has to offer! All of this controversy has gone far enough! It’s time I make my own destiny come true! I’m the prototype of wrestling and the perfect most precise and accurate sharp shooter! I’m not going to let anyone hold me down. In other words, I’m going to kick ass and take Action by the throat to chop it the fuck off, raising supreme as the best in the world. The Ringer gets up and dashes some money on the table. Before he pushes the exit door, he glances over and sees that family arguing. He stares. Then leaves. CLOSE
