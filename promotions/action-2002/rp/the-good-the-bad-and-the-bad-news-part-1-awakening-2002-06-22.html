The Good, the Bad, and the Bad News part 1: Awakening

22 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/22/02 Beaming Lights. Beeping machines. -I've been here before.- A Hospital bed. Location: Unknown. Minio's eyes blinked towards conciousness. He got KO'd Again. He rose, like many times before, in a woozy stuper. His face darted side to side. -There was no way I could be living like this.- He slid off the side of the bed, his blue gown catching on the bed post. He yanked forward in a stumble, and lifted up his chart. His sight was blurred, but he made out the words. 'His... er... My, concusions are nearly fatal?' Minio felt betrayed by his body. By his head. By his eyes. By this chart. Why hadn't any doctors told him this? Why hadn't anyone let him aware that he could die? Had he really wrestled to the edge of death? 'WHY DIDN'T ANYONE TELL ME?!' He was panicing. Screaming like a madman. 'I'll tell you why Bobby.' A woman's soft voice spoke behind him. His face eased in a slight surprise, slight relief, the voice was formiliar to him, if not the viewer. She stood behind him, he face was blurred by the backrounding of the camera view. All that could be seen was tan skin and dark curly hair. Someone of Minio's private life obviously. 'When have you ever stayed around for a doctor to tell you anything? You always wake up in a rush and escape from the facilities! You never let anyone help you Bobby. You never let anyone help you.' She spoke to him, almost down on him. He grew quickly frusterated. 'I don't need help. Just... Just stay out of my life. You said you couldn't keep up with it, well stay out of it.' The woman's face dropped in sadness. Minio was already changing into his street clothes. He was deep in thought. He was Sinking in trouble. Babylon had been able to knock Minio out, KroW could knock Minio out, and Minio was much more threatened by Fear then either of those men. The future looked grim. The unknown woman behind the Martyr knew what he was thinking. 'Fear is quite the obsticle, isn't he Bobby?' 'Fear itself or Fear HIMSELF?' Minio's reply was sharp, he knew the answer. Did she? 'Maybe both?' Her soft voice was reassuring even if the answer was not. 'How about Neither. Its something I have to do. Its closure. Hell, maybe after this, I can retire.' He sounded more like he was convincing himself then the woman. 'Maybe after this, you'll HAVE to retire.' She replied, well thought out, hitting all the right nerves. 'You left me. Remember that. YOU LEFT ME.' He was growing emotionally hostile. He stomped out of the room, his mind clearing, before the arguement got any worse. The camera panned with Minio, never showing her face. Never revealing her identity. Minio wasn't as straight forward as some had thought. There were skeletons in the closet. Yet again secrets and FEARS were revealed in another hospital room. Once again Minio awoke in a hospital bed. Once Again Minio was so confused it made his head hurt. Or maybe that was the concusions. Fade to Black
