The Pain.

9 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05.09.2003 Marcus Alexander Dox was back at home, in Leitchfield, Kentucky, in the home that Action! owner Reed Young had provided for himself and Conrad Ramsey after he had forced the two men to be tag team partners. It was a nice home, despite the fact that he had to share it with that old bastard. It was nice, then, when Conrad had started spending more and more time with his girlfriend. The bad news, though, was that his girlfriend was Xander's mother, Samantha Dox. Worse yet, they had become engaged. Oh, how Xander's life was spinning out of control. But his relationship with his mother was the least of his worries. Xander and his wife, Jessica, were planning on heading out for dinner. Bradley Duncan was set to join them, but he had cancelled. So the two-years plus married couple were all set on a nice dinner for two. They were going to have to drive an hour to get the place, of course, but it would be worth it. As much time as they spent with each other on the road and all that, it never seemed like they had any time alone. Just to talk. Jessica was in the bathroom, getting her make-up on. She rarely wore make-up, and what she did, well, wasn't much. That's how Xander liked it. A more natural woman. She was going to be heading out in a nice white dress. All one-piece. Showing a lot of back. It had a slit up the right leg. Oh... *drool* Her hair was all done up. She was a knock-out. It often amazed Marcus how he could have snagged such a woman. Luckily, he had a big hard-on for Sunkist, otherwise the two would never have meet. He was in the kitchen. He was wearing himself a suit. That's right. A nice blue suit. Their intentions were dinner, a movie, and some dancing. Not that young club dancing, but some actual, you know, dancing(no, that isn't a term for sex, though, that would probably happen, too). Xander was looking in the refrigerator. He said to himself, 'Should I drink a Sunkist? Decisions, decisions, decisions.' He thought about it for all of three seconds and reached in and grabbed a can. 'It's always time for a nice, refreshing Sunkist.' He smiled to himself, 'Hey, I should do a commercial. Wiat...' He had forgotten. He HAD done a commercial for Sunkist. Just not, you know, for the soft drink. It all tied in to how he had met Jesica, but that wasn't the purpose of this story. That would be saved for another time. He straightened himself up, his face contorting slightly from the pain. Oh, yeah, the back. That was still hurting like a bitch. And, plus, a long-ass match with Jeff Garvin wasn't helping the matters. He just couldn't slow down. But on this night he was. He wasn't Max Danger. He was Xander Dox. Husband to Jessica Dox. She had called out from the bedroom, then, 'Okay, honey, I'm ready.' She had to get her purse and scarf(to be worn across her back and her elbows - you know?). Xander was in the kitchen, trying to open up the Sunkist. Jessica entered with a broad smile. She was a knock-out. Just so fine. Everything about her was perfect, in Xander's mine anyhow. She was the dream girl. The one everyone wished they could be with. Her smile soon faded though. Xander had opened up the Sunkist and dropped inexplicably. He grabbed his arm with his other and held it. He looked up at his wife worriedly. 'What's wrong?' she said, concerned for her husband. He shook his head, 'I dunno. One minute I was opening up this can and the next, I couldn't feel my arm.' He looked up at his wife. 'I told you! You need to see a doctor! But nooooo, I'm Max Danger, I'm invincible. I don't need a doctor, because I'm Max Danger. Look at me, I'm Max Danger! I can break my back and not feel a thing!' 'Jess, stop. Please.' 'Why? I've told you time and time again that your back is a wreck. Everyone has told you this. Anyone with eyes can see how bad of shape you're in. Oh, sure, you're good to go when it's match time, but before and after, it's a whole other story.' 'It's alright, Jessica.' 'No it's not, honey. It's not alright. You're going to end up paralyzed just like dad and I'm going to have to take care of you day in and day out. I don't want that, Xander. I don't want that at all.' 'You won't. I promise.' 'Oh, what, then are you going to give up wrestling? Are you going to stop killing yourself every night? 'Cause that's the only way you can promise me that. Otherwise, you'll just be a liar. And...' Xander walked up to Jessica and put his arms on her shoulder. The feeling had come back into his arm quickly. He then put his hands on her cheeks. 'I'm not giving it up, Jess, but I can still promise you... no, I do promise you that I will not end up in a wheel-chair. I won't need you taking care of me all the time. I won't end up like you father. I swear to that.' Tears were beginning to form in Jessica's eyes. She was just so damn worried. She didn't know how she would be able to handle her husband becoming a parapaligic. Xander then pulled her in and held her. 'It's okay, honey. Everything's going to be alright.' In between sniffs she said, 'How can you be so sure? You won't even see a doctor. Who knows how bad you're back is.' Xander then pulled her away, holding her shoulders. He looked at her and said, 'I know how bad it is. And so does Dr. Felt.' She couldn't form the words, but you could tell she was saying, 'What?' 'I went to him a few weeks ago, after my match with Conrad in the cage. I didn't want to tell you, because I know you'd worry too much. And I didn't want you to over-react when you knew what exactly was wrong with my back. That's why I'm not going to tell you anything now.' 'What? Why not?' 'Just trust me, okay? Everything's going to be alright. I'll be seeing Dr. Felt every week for a check-up. He'll be monitoring the situation. But, as of right now, I'm not in any real harm.' 'No real harm? You just feeling in your arm, baby? I think that constitutes as 'real harm'!' 'Okay, so a bad choice of words. But, Jess, there isn't anything to worry about. I'm going to be fine. I'm not technically cleared to wrestle, but that's alright. That's never stopped me before. So...' 'I'm stopping you.' 'What?' 'You heard me. I'm not going to let you wrestle. Not until you're cleared. That's the way it's supposed to be anyway.' 'You can't do that.' 'I can. I'm putting my foot down on this. See,' she stomped her foot down on the kitchen floor, 'putting the foot down. It's a contractual obligation, you know? If the wife puts the foot down, then it's the law.' Xander kissed her on the forehead. She was really stretching to get him not to wrestle. She knew it was useless though. Xander smiled at his wife and kissed her again, 'It's going to be alright. I promise.' He then held her. She wrapped her arms around him. Tight as she could. It hurt, yes. The back was bad. Real bad. Worse than Xander was letting on. And the hug, well, it didn't feel too good either. And yet, it felt so good. So right. He wasn't going to make her let go. He couldn't. She needed this. So did he.
