The Return to Pansy Palace

9 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OPEN Fox “I’M BACK! THIS TIME AS THE CHAMPION.... BIG TIME!!!!!!!!” Title: The Return to Pansy Palace Date: 03/30/03 3:23 pm EST Spring Break Vacation! Place: Pansy Palace The gallant Fox dove right into the action as a crowd of people waited in line to buy entry tickets. But Fox had a plan! Hehe, little do these people know that I have a way of getting in for free! Fox walked up to a man in a pink shirt. The shirt read “Hello, welcome to the Pansiest place on Earth, my name is: Doug”. Fox “Oh hi Doughy! See me stamp on my hand? That means I can go “back” into the park right?” Doug “Did you just call me Doughy? My name is Doug!” Fox “Sorry, so can I go in?” Doug “Your stamp is valid. Okay kid, you’re in.” Doug pushed a door open and allowed Chris, Pikachu and The Championship Belt into the Palace. Oh yeah! It worked! I knew if I didn’t wash the stamp off from the last time I visited they’d let me back in! You sly fox, you. You rock! Christopher had actually pulled it off and instead of paying a measly two bucks, ‘Kid’ was a hard ass and got in for free. The scene was pink and fluffy, tons of cotton candy being made at different stores. Tons of pink colored chocolate displayed in windows. And a new addition, a big waterfall right in the middle of the building!.... as the water slurped out of a huge mechanical pink Panda Bear! It was like heaven for Christopher. He could barely control himself. Fox “Whoa, it’s even better than the last time I was here.... and the last time I was here seems like yesterday! Even though it really wasn’t yesterday.... yeah, yesterday was fun....” You’re getting off topic Chris. Wait, he can’t hear me.... .... Fox “Right Pikachu, let’s go see the waterfall!” Thanks to Pikachu, Chris had begun his epic encounter back to Pansy Palace! Scuffing along the pink tiles Christopher walked up the stairs, taking in the sight and the mist residue from the big pink panda. Just then a nice man in a blue shirt and pink pants started to talk with our hero. Man “Hey there young fellow, how are you today?” Fox “I-I’m good sir!” Man “Know don’t call me sir, I have a job! Say, how old are you?” Fox “I’m three!” Man “Wow! You sure are a big bastard! I mean.... a big three year old!” Fox “Thanks a bunch, what’s your name?” Man “Huh? Oh, it’s Eddy. Yeah, I’m the longest serving employee here at Pansy Palace and I’m only 34!” Otay Chris, think back to this morning! You were reading your horoscope and it said that ‘you will have a monkey on your back today, lure the monkey off with a banana!’ Okay.... well.... here goes nothing! Literally! Fox “Umm guy, yeah, umm do you like pixy dust?” Eddy “Why yes, all employee’s here at Pansy Palace love pixy dust! Only if it’s pink of course!” Fox “Oh it’s pink.... solar flare!” Just then Christopher tossed his belt aside, stuck his hand into his pocket and threw some good old sand in Eddy’s face! But then as he turned back to get his belt.... it was.... GONE!? Eddy “Hey you’re quite the playful one aren’t ya? You little scamp!” Eddy wiped and rubbed at his eyes but still the sand was there. The solar flare ‘worked’. Fox “Oh no my Action Wrestling Championship!” Chris went to the railing, only to see the belt suck in the lower torso of the pink panda bear! Fox “Skank! Give me my title back!” The machine mad a funny noise as smoke started to rise out of the panda’s eyes. Fox “Have no fear! I’ll get you back my belt!” With that Fox scurried off down the stairs in the search of his Championship, leaving Eddy all pissed. Eddy “Okay could someone just, like, throw water on me or something?” The scene dashed to Christopher Elliot Fox who was madly rushing down a pink hallway, then a red hallway, then a green hallway and finally one more pink hallway when he finally stopped and was staring straight at a big pink mechanical dolphin. Al “Hi I’m Al, can I help you?” Chris turned and looked at a tour guide who was sitting in one of these dolphins that was floating by the water.... Fox “Wow! A big giant floating pink dolphin!” Al “Aren’t they wonderful?” Al just sat there patting the spot next to him like the fruit cake he was. Fox “Hey maybe you can help me? You see I lost my Belt and I want to get it back!” Al “Oh I ssssssssssssssssssee. Well if you ride with me then I’ll drop you off at the security office mister!” Fox “Gee thanks! I wish there were more people like you out in the world!” Al “Oh thanks hot buns. Could you sign my petition to legalize gay weddings?” Fox “Anything for a friend.... big time!!!!!” Fox jumped in as the dolphin swayed a bit. Satisfying Al’s sensitive areas. Al “Ooh, you’re feisty aren’t cha!?” Fox “Hehe.... yeah, now let’s go!” Al mumbled to himself.... Al “Hmmm, what a cutie....” Fox “This is a nice ride Al!” Al “Ooh, it’s just about to get really hot in here.” Fox “It is? Should I take off my shirt?” Al “Oh you tease! Sure thing.” Christopher proceeded and threw his shirt into the river, the only thing was that Chris wasn’t getting any warmer. Fox “I’m not feeling the heat....” Al “Oh, well just relax.” Fox “Otay.” Al placed his hand on Fox’s forehead and started rubbing softly, smoothly, gently.... annoyingly! But definitely pleasing to ‘Kid’. Al “Mmmmmmmmm.... how does it feel?” Fox “Ummm otay I guess.” Al “Ummmmm, glurggggggggggggg!!!!” Just then things got weird as Al threw himself on top of Chris! The Action Champ had been played! Al “I want you! Scream my name bitch! Oh yesssssssssssssssssssssssss!” Al, Al, AAAAALLLLL!!!!! Fox “I MEAN GET THE F$%K OFF ME YOU TREE HUGGING HIPPIE!” Al “Oh I love it when you talk dirty!” Fox “Errrrrrrrrrrrr!!!!!!” Chris overpowered the fruit cake and tossed him off of the dolphin and into the freezing water! Al “Oh my gosh! Like total shrinkage!” Fox “Fruit cake! Thanks for the dolphin!” Chris put the petal to the metal and he rocked the dolphin forward to the security drop off. Fox “Whew! Looks like I lost that gay boy! I always thought that they hired homos at Pansy Palace... now I’m sure of it!” The Action Champion pulled up to the edge and scampered out, back to a huge cathedral-like opening. Fox was astounded as he checked out the paintings of pink and purple pandas and green fuzzy snakes! Fox “Heh, that’s one long snake! He looks hungry. And speaking of food I could use some....” Just then a security officer approached the cold and hungry ‘Kid’. Officer “Excuse me sir? You don’t have a shirt on. Is there something wrong?” Fox “Yes there is, that dolphin ride employee, Al, tried to make sweet, but bitter, love to me!” Officer “Oh....... yeah that happens a lot. Al is gay, he takes advantage of a ton of people around here. Don’t let it bother you, he did it to me.” Fox “Did you resist?” Officer “I couldn’t, he had me handcuffed! Besides it wasn’t that bad.” It wasn’t that bad??!!! You tortured soul, I’m so, so, so sorry. Ugh, graphic image! Fox “Ewww.... umm, do you like, have food or clothes.... or something?” Gil “The name’s Gil. And yes I can give you a Pansy Palace t-shirt.” Gil mowed through a crowd as Christopher followed close behind. They made their way to a t-shirt stand that wasn’t near any crowds. Gil “Whew, I’m gather-phobic. I hate crowds and tight spots!” So you hate tight spots? I bet Al loosened some up for ya! Hehehehe. Gil “Okay, here.” Gil tossed Fox a pink t-shirt that said “I went to Pansy Palace and all I got was this gay shirt”. Fox “Hey, it tells the truth....” Gil “What does?” Fox “The shirt!” Gil “Anyways.... let’s go get some food.” Christopher and Gil walked over to a Restaurant called ‘Pansy Pancake Express!’. They sat down in the fluorescent blue chairs. Gil was a big build with a beer gut and balding hair, as Fox just couldn’t get over the earring that Gil wore. Fox “Hey, is that real?” Fox asked as he reached over the table and pointed. Gil “Yeah it is! Don’t touch it though. It’s my prized possession!” And that’s when Fox remembered what his prize possession was. Fox “THAT’S IT!!!!” Fox burst up, knocking the chair and table over as the surrounding people immediately looked to see what was up. Fox “Sorry, ummm............... boo yeah! Heh......” Gil “What’s wrong with you? We haven’t even been served yet and you want to leave!?” Fox “Close your eyes Gil, this will work!” Gil refused but had no choice when Fox slapped his hand across his head. Fox “This should let us escape.... SOLAR FLAREEEEEEEEE!!!! Kaaaaapowww! Cuchuuuu! Whhhhhhhussssssh!” Giggles emerged from some of the people as others just stared at Fox surprised... even a little concerned. Fox “RUN!” Chris pulled Gil by the hair and made a break towards the security office. Dodging through waves of people Gil was loosing precious hair by the second. The glossy floor posed as no threat for Fox but it was trouble for Gil. Why? Because Christopher still had his hand across his eyes and on top of that, he was pulling Gil’s hair! Finally, they made it. The wood door was cracked open by the body of Gil, as everyone’s favorite ‘Kid’ threw him into the entrance of the security office. Into the security office the scene went as Fox tossed Gil and what was left of his hair onto a table. A few things were knocked off as Gil tried to wake up. Fox “You there Gil?” Gil “........Ugh..... yeah, I think my head got hit or something....” Fox “Yeah, and someone tore out your hair too!” Gil “What did you say?” Fox “I mean, speaking of prized possessions, I was wondering if you guys in security happened to pick up my Action Wrestling Championship Title Belt! You see I lost it when I came here and I really need it back.” Gil “Is this it?” Slowly Gil pulled an object up from the ground. Indeed, it was the Action Championship! Fox “How did you find that?” Gil “I patrol inside the big panda and this thing came through it. Do you want it?” Fox “Yes pwease!” The moment of truth - Gil handed Fox the Action Title! Fox “Yes! Reunited at last! You’re the greatest Gil.... BIG TIME!!!!!!!!!!!!” Gil “Hey, thanks....” The moment of truth turned into anger as Gill felt his head. Gil “My h-hair! Where’s my hair?! Who did this to me?” Fox “Ummm, that guy! BYE!” Fox randomly pointed to an adult male walking past the office window, just before Fox sprinted out of the office and out of Pansy Palace all together through the fire exit doors. Outside the sun was almost down. A beautiful horizon ahead as Fox had recaptured his long lost title. Fox “Hey, I parked right around here!” The happy go lucky ‘Kid’ trotted off into the sunset until he...... walked into a parked car. Fox “FUCK! Someone should move that stupid car! It’s fucking parked in a fire zone!” Fox kept walking, then stopped, and turned around to let out a cry. ? Fox “%$#@^&% *^#$!!! That’s my car!” CLOSE
