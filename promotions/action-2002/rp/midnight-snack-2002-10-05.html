Midnight Snack.

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 5th, 2002 Single water droplets fell from the small cracks in the apartment ceiling, landing with a sudden clanging noise in the pots and pans below. The cable had been down for hours, or at least it felt like hours. The living room lit only by a miniature, red lava lamp. It’s movements were almost hypnotic as he laid in front of it, his hands busily piecing together a plastic model aeroplane, his eyes blood shot and twitchy. Never had Jeff looked such a mess, like he was torn between two feelings. His hand shook as he reached out to insert the final piece, the last chrome hubcap. He slowly extended his arm, his tongue outlining the in side of his lips as if he was in deep concentration. In a single shift of body weight, Jeff collapsed onto the small silver jet, crushing it like Godzilla might crush a skyscraper in down town Tokyo. “NO! That took me nine fucking hours to build!” He shrieked in anger, his jaw quivering as he pulled himself back to a kneeling position and stared down at the wreck before him; shards of the thin windshield still caught in his forearm. Nine hours building a foolish model, you’d be crying too. Though the box probably wouldn’t contain the phrase, ‘For Ages Five to Thirteen.’ Jeff Garvin was a long way south of being anywhere near that age limit, but it didn’t matter, he’d never get to show it off. It was in pieces, much like his life was nowadays. Everything had been taken away from him in one night, he had accomplished everything he set out to do at the pay per view, Retaliation, but it was all taken away shortly after. Coral Avalon was a human pretzel by the time he had finished with him and then someone had to come along and push him from his pedestal. “Odium.” His hand formed a fist and his teeth gritted together. “We could have been the best of friends but he had to go and ruin it. Odium is a destroyer. He polluted my mind and tried to end me. Most likely sent straight from 21st Century Wrestling, sent here to kill me… “But psychical death wasn’t good enough for my ‘enemies’ they wanted my sanity. That’s right, little Jeffrey, its all a conspiracy to make sure that you don’t succeed. They want you to fail. Well we can’t let that happen. Oh no, we must strike back at the right time. The right moment.” The words ran through his mind, spilling out verbally as he rocked back a forth clutching the remains of his model airplane to his chest. Paranoid? Indeed… “Everyone wants to see to it that I fail; just like Glen Miller, just like Mark Knight, just like Jason Tronic, and just like… Uncle Ron. Though I have bounced back from all their feeble attempts, and I will bounce back once again. Stronger, faster, and more focused… More lethal.” Reaching around in back of himself, Jeff produced a large silver platter with a silver lid covering whatever gourmet meal was below it. Maybe the Original secretly snuck out and cut off Odium’s head for revenge? Okay, so we all know there’s very little chance something of that nature took place. But you’re wrong! Lifting the covering from the platter, Jeff revealed Odium’s head. Complete, and Utter Shock. “Yes… This is going to be good.” He said, licking his lips and bending over and picking IT up and lifting it to his mouth. A loud crack is heard followed by Jeff biting into Odium, sucking the insides clean and licking his lips. “Delicious.” He slowly finished off the rest of Odium’s ENTIRE body. Since when did Jeff Garvin go from comical dumb ass to full, fledged cannibal? … Jeff looked down at the few pieces of lobster left on the plate. Burrrrrrrrrp. “Oh, Odium Jr. I knew you well…” Jeff declared, wiping his chin off. “Delicous. You’re the best ‘symbolic cleansing’ lobster I had eeeeeevvvvvveeeeeerrrrrr had!” With that, Jeff took off to the bathroom for a little alone time. You actually thought Jeff cut off Odium’s head, served it on a silver plattered, and devoured it with his ‘Kiss The Chef’ bib hanging around his neck? Sick bastards.
