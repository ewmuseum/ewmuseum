Smell The Aroma (Part 6 - Death of Mi Padre)

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.16.02 It all happened so quick. I was four, and he was no longer around. I really can't explain what happened, because I don't really remember the details. But the only thing I can tell you is that my dad's no longer here. Yeah, I'm sure he's watching over me, but I wish he could've been there to see me graduate high school, I wish he could've seen me get accepted into Princeton. I wish he could see me wrestle my first match in Action! Wrestling. But he won't be there in the physical form. The Lord has taken him away from me. Maybe it was too soon, but I'm not going to doubt God; I'm just going to take what he has given me, and I'll do my best to cope. When I finally asked why dad was no longer around, she broke down and started to cry. I didn't know how to handle it, I was just a teen, when I asked her; and sometimes I blame myself for her death, because it wasn't long after that she was gone too. But let's not get ahead of ourselves. My mom told me that it was just his time to go, but the details she tried hard not to get into. From what she said it was a case of mistaken identity, and my dad is a very stubborn man. He got stopped by the cops, and when the cops screwed up, they offered no apology, let me back up a bit. My mom and dad went out on the town to celebrate with my older brother, his team won the 7 Year Old League Title in baseball, and he had the winning hit. I was home sick with the chicken pox, and couldn't attend the game. The cops stopped my dad with the reason being that a car fighting that description was involved in some drug trafficking that they were monitoring. They stripped the car in search of drugs, and turned up nothing. The cops turned to leave as if it were no big deal. But my dad didn't appreciate the way they went about their business, especially with his wife and son in the car. And most importantly he wanted an apology. One that he wouldn't recieve. And when my dad's been wrong, he gets very loud. So then the cops tried to really fuck with him, and it lead to my dad trying to reach into the car to leave, but he got pulled back by one of the officers, and accidently elbowed him in the jaw. And when things like that happen, cops will treat you like Rodney King. He then ushered my brother and mom back into the car, and was going for the spare set of keys in the glove compartment, and when he came back out the car, one of the cops fired shots, 'three or four' as my mom put it. Reynaldo Aromadeus, may he rest in piece. June 12, 1960 - July 18, 1984
