Becoming. Became. Undone. Part 5 - The person I am

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.01.02 Became. 'The person I am.' Jim Johnson is shown leading out his evil empire. LLB. Chris Fear. Prisoner 109. Bobby Minio is shown being beaten by Fear and 109. Minio is shown being thrown around by 109. Minio attacking Chris Fear. Attacking Johnson. 'Once, I trusted Chris Fear, Overdose and I trusted him. And he turned on us for Johnson. Then...' Minio racing down the streets of Las Vegas on foot. He hits a corner at about twenty miles an hour, and there he stands, outfront of a corner house, windows broken in, neighbors gawking at it. Minio cautiously moves towards the doorway, goes to put his hand on the door as it slowly swings open. Minio steps in. The broken voice of his mother laying under a broken table and two chairs catches his attention. He screams for help. 'Johnson and LLB had 109 attack my mother at her own home. She lived, but went into hiding from me... I was to much of a danger to her life. I don't blame her. One day i'll finish wrestling, and she'll contact me. I hope.' Minio is shown beating the life out of Prisoner 109. Knuckles bloody. He looked like an animal. 'The first time I really lost my temper. 109 didn't wrestle long after that.' Minio is shown with a girl. Pale skin. Redhair. They look happy. Partially. Minio is holding her tight to his side as they wall, she just sits beside him. 'Julie. Wow. I dunno if i'm ready to explain this. We had been together for a year during PIW, but she eventually made some mistakes, as did I, and around this time, I left her, but I felt like shit from it. Life went on I guess.' Minio and Chris Mezz are seen attacking the evil empire together. Mildly sucessful. There however was a kinship between the two, a loose trust, both had been burned, both wanted revenge. 'Mezz and I were a decent team, as rare as it was. Overdose was eliminated from the picture. They injured him and sent him packing. I always looked up to him, and when he disappeared, I felt like I had lost a mentor. I think Mezz may have been the same. But, I can't speak for him.' Then nothing. 'PIW goes under, for a second time. I was jobless. For a while.' Shots show Minio outside of the jOlt building, putting an application in the mailbox. Then coming back. Again. Again. A secretary shaking her head at him. He walks off defeated. Then it shows Minio talking to IOW's Owner. They shake hands. Minio watches from backstage as Chris Fear and Mezz beat various opponents as team PIW, but he never does debut, the owner keeps shaking his head when he asks to run down. Then Nothing. 'IOW went under before I could even show my face.' Reed Young is shown sitting across from Minio grinning. Minio signs a contract, they shake hands. Minio becomes the official Action! slave for ratings. Minio is shown going after Chris Fear, finally, then it happened. Minio is in the ring, a dark body rises behind him, KroW. A baseball bat slams across the back of his head. Minio stumbles then drops like a rock. Another Montage of Minio getting concussion after concussion. Waking up in hospital after Hospital. Beating after Beating. Then Julie comes back into his life. 'Eventually KroW beat me. I guess I wasn't enough for him. I guess. I'd like another go at it. Julie was around then, which I think tolled me out a bit.' Shots of Minio and Fear having a few small squables here and there. Fear flipping out in a resteraunt, Minio arriving an hour later. Julie tagging along as he searched for Fear, then, it all falls apart. Julie and Minio at ends. Fear is fired. Julie tries to convince Bobby she is pregnant with his baby, he shuts her down, and smiles. 'Life felt different then. Very different.' Bobby is shown watching Sweet Aroma, staring at her with Loving eyes. Then, Aroma and Dawson team up. Aroma and JCON falling in love. Minio alone. The Dimensia Minio falls into. Slowly falling insane. Minio and Dawson's hate slowly mounting. Bobby Minio beating Danger and opposite, the two beating each other straight to hell. Minio winning the four way elimination match. Minio accidently hitting Aroma against Danger, then Aroma 'accidently' causing his loss to Dawson. Low blow after Low blow is shown consectutively. 'Was the ref even working that night?' The hate. All of it refering back to one primal instinct. Hate. Anger. Revenge. Loss of control. Replacement of intuition and hate. 'This is who I am. A Ball of fire. I'm a dragon incarnate. All hate, all day. All night. Nothing ever works out. Now I simply have to scrape the walls to survive, to not go completely out of my mind. I have to work my hardest to stay human, atleast to some extent. And everyone wants me to fail. Wants me to die.' The projector stops. 'And what now? Where do I go from here? I wish I knew. I wish I had a clue. I wish I had a track of videos to tell the future. But I don't. So what happens now? I don't control that. I have to just hang on for dear life and watch it all unfold. Good luck, me.' Minio stands, walking past the camera, pulling the projector screen down, then letting it fly upwards, showing an empty brick wall. He walks out of camera view again, light is cast as a door opens, then complete darkness as it slams shut.
