A not so lending ear

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/15/2002 Yeah, so this Noah guy's on my case now. I was walking down the halls one day, pissed off that some kid recognized me as Nuke, so I felt i needed to let off a little agression. And I saw Noah, I didn't know who the fuck he was then, just some guy that had nothing to do with me. And I decked him in the face. Being the cool guy I am, I decide to forgive him for smacking his face up against my fist, let bygones be bygones and let him tag-up with me. So blah, blah, blah We get a Dyad Title match. HE loses it for us. He loses a bunch of other matches for me, so I punch him in the face at the PPV and go home. I'm figurin' that's the end off it. But the guy has the nerve to ask to be in a match with me? As if I the have fucking the time to waste on him...well I do, but still...what a fucktard. So I send him a message and smash his face in. A normal person would've gotten the hint: When someone bashes your head into a toilet bowl and flushes your face a few times, that's usually a strong indication your not getting along so well. But get this... He STILL wants to fight in a match against me. The fucking audacity of this character. So anyway I put him on 'time out' by place kicking him into la la land. But I'm pretty sure he's still gonna want to fight me. So know I don't know what I'm gonna do. ... Hello? '...Dude...its been THIRTY mintues. Now are you or are you not going to switch long distance services?' Nah. I rather enjoy simply stealing pre-paid phone cards. Thanks for listening though. *...dail tone...*
