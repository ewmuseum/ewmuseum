Action! — Tales

Turning 3

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/02 OPEN Birthday cake, candles, hats, music, balloons, streamers, games, plates, drinks, television and Christopher Fox were all ready for a fun day! For it was Christopher’s 23rd Birthday Bash.... BIG TIME! You’re ALL invited! Come on in! Title: Turning 3 Date: 12/19/02 2:00 pm EST Place: Chris Fox’s House Yes sir, the scene was set in Chris’ very own house! This is the first time he has opened his pad up with others including you so come on in! There will be dancing, rapping, eating, presents, poems and stand up comedy with Drew Carey! <---Well.... we lied about that and the poems, but the rapping real! And as a usual party, people are standing, having a drinking and talking in their own little group. While some are watching a Chris Fox highlight tape on the big screen, some are enjoying a nice game of I-spy. I know what you’re thinking, and not everyone at the party is 3. There are only a few little munchkins scurrying about playing tag, until.... The man. The myth. The Pokemon Master himself walks in.... CHRIS FOX! With a little pointy party hat on and a grin the size of New York, Christopher Elliot Fox enters, and since he doesn’t want a big ovation (as noted on the invite card) Fox paces around and shakes hands with the people. Chris first makes his way to three young men in suits all sharing a laugh. Fox “Tee hee, heto gento-men yeah! Who are you guys?” The men turn as the laughter dies right from their voices like a deer after it has been shot. Man 1 “Umm you’re not suppose to talk to us, we’re extra’s! You hired us just to fill space, remember?” Hmmmm, I recall something about that.... but wasn’t that last year!? Fox “Extra’s? What makes you guys so extra? Are you guys extra good, extra cool, AND extra muscular?” After the muscular remark, the three men trot off. Fox “Hmmm, what a nice group of extra’s.... BIG --” Everyone stops to help out. Fox & Company (Mostly extra’s) “BIG TIIIIIIIIME!” Fox “Yeah!” Then all the people go back to talking and leave Christopher alone to concentrate. Now that I’m thr-- another year older I can do so much more! Like go snowboarding or buy cigarettes, or start my own posse.... hmm all of those things sound super but I’m too lazy to do anything aBOOT it. So I’ll stick with entertaining my friends.... BIG-- Everyone stops to help out, again. Fox & Company “BIG TIME!” How did they know I was saying big time? The wonderful birthday bash scene fades with the shifty eyes coming from the original ‘Kid’ himself, Chris Fox. Who is now ready to get busy because he is.... 3 CLOSE
