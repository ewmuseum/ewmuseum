Love Flushed Away!

17 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 17 “Mine will be the only hand you ever need to hold…” The juke box in the bar wails out one of the latest Dolly Parton classics. All the white folk begin to dance around like it’s the latest Peter Andre smash hit. “I'll be your umbrella when it rains…” Some sing along as we follow a man, who trundles into the men’s room of this fine beer consortium that is known as “O’Leary’s Tavern”, which lies in the heart of Salt Lake City. “Good God, man, what the Hell are you doing?” The John is sitting in a cubicle. With a mug of beer in his left hand and a strawberry daiquiri in his right. He hovers the daiquiri over the bowl, tipping a little in every now and again. “What do you think you’re doing, pal?” The John looks up at the man, clearly not to happy having been interrupted in…what ever it was he was doing with the toilet. The John stands, but before moving toward the man, he looks down to the lavatory. “This won’t take a minute, honey.” He takes the man by the arm, moving him to the far end of the bathroom. He shoves the man against the wall. “What do you mean what is the John doing?” “I wasn’t talking about the john, I was talking about you. What are you doing with that can, pal?” The John looks over to the toilet, waves to it before returning his gaze to the man, a dirty look through the silver eye holes in his mask. [sigh] “The John thinks you should take more pride in the lavatories.” “You’ve been talking to the toilet?” “What are you talking about, citizen?” The man and the John both stare at each other looking rather confused. The John sips his beer and the man blinks. “You just told me the toilet thinks I should take pride in toilets.” The John shakes is head. Resting a hand on the man’s shoulder, as though he were about to give him some mildly intellectual advice. “The John says that you urinate all around her seat. And the John thinks that is a down-right injustice to lavatories everywhere!” “The toilet said that?” “No, the John did.” “That’s what I just said.” “You said the toilet said it, but the John said it.” “You’re making no sense, buddy. I’m gonna go get security.” The John takes his mug and rests it beside the sink, he moves closer to the guy, bending down to get in his face. The man steps back a little as the massive 400+ pound man moves forward. The John doesn’t look too happy with his current situation. “The John says this, you turn around you keep walking otherwise you just might smell what the John is flushing! You wish to create injustices to lavatories? How about the John pisses on your seat? How about the John goes to your house and turns around the roll of toilet paper so it’s against the wall, and every time a visitor comes they wipe their shitty fingers against your fine walls, citizen? How about the John takes your head and cleans out the bowl with it, because citizen, you are an enemy to the porcelain. And the John, the Water Closet Warrior, has no problems with tearing out your arms, sticking them in your ears and playing motorcycles!” The guy looks up at the John. He takes a deep breath and prods the John with a finger into a fat man mammary. “You know what buddy…I should…I’m gonna…I er…I’ll be going now! That’ll teach ya! YEAH!” The John watches as the man exits the men’s room, white as a ghost. And the John turns, he looks to the toilet and grins cheekily. He swaggers up and pats the toilet on the seat. “We can finally be alone!”
