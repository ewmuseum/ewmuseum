Reflecting On: Part Two.

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.06.2003 For Marcus' fourth birth the Dox family had decided to travel to Orlando, Florida. And for a kids there was really only one place to go: Walt Disney World! His father(Stuart Parker Dox), mother(Samantha Lynn Dox), brother(Charles Abraham Dox, 9 years older), sister(Amanda Rachel Dox, 3 year older), and himself all were piled into the car for a week long journey. After a couple of days travel - hey, it wasn't easy getting from Augusta, Maine to Orlando, Florida - the Dox family unpacked at the hotel and went right to Magic Kingdom. Oh, it was a joy. A blast. Even for a four year old. All Marcus wanted to do was meet Pluto. After a few hours at the park, that time arrived. Several other children had their autograph books out, which they had bought at the various Disney gift shops, and were getting Pluto to sign and take pictures with them. Amanda and Charles both had their father take a picture of them with the cartoon dog. In his mother's arms, Marcus, wanted desperately to join them. 'You want his autograph, Marcus?' asked Samantha in her beautifully soothing voice. She could always make the most dire of situations less-so, just be speaking. He didn't have an autograph book though. Samantha dug into her purse. She had ripped off a piece of paper, grabbed a black pen, and asked Marcus again, 'Here, take this over to him, and he'll sign it. Daddy will even get a picture of it.' Stuart smiled, 'Go on, son. He won't bite.' Then, out of the side of his mouth, he said to Marcus' siblings, 'He is a dog, so he might.' They both laughed as Stuart turned back and caught 'the eye' from his wife. He zipped up his mouth and threw away the key. Which, well, was stupid, because he didn't lock it, and how many zippers require a key? Anyway... Marcus, with his piece of paper, feeling slightly embarrassed even at the age for four. Hey, had to keep that cool image. He slowly walked towards the massive yellow dog. Tongue hanging out, big, long nose. Oh, it was fuckin' scary. Pluto saw the little tyke coming towards him and bent down. He held out his arms. Marcus was horrified. He dropped the pen and the paper floated down to the ground. The four year old was too shell-shocked to move. His bottom lip quivered. Before long he was going to start wailing, everyone knew it. Samantha quickly rushed for her son as Stuart snapped the pictures. Oh, that was going to haunt Marcus when he brought his first date home. Samantha lifted Marcus up off the ground and spun him away from the evil yellow dog. Pluto stood up and held out his arms in a 'what?' manner. Amanda and Charles began to laugh, but Stuart quickly got them to stop after another look from his wife. But, secretly, they smiled huge smiles behind them. Samantha lead Marcus over to a bench and sat down with him. 'I never liked that dog anyhow,' she said, trying to cheer her shaken son up. 'I much prefer Mickey or Donald, don't you?' Marcus quickly shook his head. Samantha always seemed to be able to make you feel better, no matter what. Marcus was now over his fear of Pluto. Oh, he wasn't going near the damn mutt again, but he was no longer trembling over the encounter. Of course, the same thing would happen later when he met Mickey Mouse and Donald Duck. Stuart got pictures of that, too. But Samantha was able to calm him down. With ease, in fact. It was amazing. Thank the lord, however, that they didn't see the Mad Hatter.
