Love Geometric - XIII

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.28.02 Another night. Another dark hotel room. Another warm body. The New York skyline haunted the open draped window in the backround, and the television glared a dim glow on the faces of Bobby Minio and Angelica Dawson, the man and woman sharing the bed below blankets, watching the rebroadcast of Presure Point halloween edition. Lucas Devol had just kicked a ref in the stomach. 'What a cheap shot. Attacking refs is soooo last year.' Minio joked, then glanced at Dawson, who gave a slight giggle, just as Keri Malone began climbing into the ring. 'She's gonna get hurt.' Dawson managed to say before the most menacing sound Minio had heard since KroW cracked a metal bat over the backside of his head. SMAAAAACK!!! '... Holy... Shit.' The television mocked as Minio's eyes stared at the screen in disbelief. *Swerve, Baby! Swerve!* Keri Malone had just hit Joey Malone, the Action! champion who was wrestling Kent Anthason, in the face with a chair. 'No.' Minio tried to wish the television to lose power. He tried to wish time to stop. An icon in his life, his sanity, had just unwrapped itself infront of his eyes. Malone was fighting against a submission towards the ropes, Minio was there in his mind, trying to drag Joey towards the side of the ring. 'Fight it goddamnit, FIGHT IT!' Dawson was now watching, looking scared, almost horrified at Minio's angered, his hurt face. He forget she was even there. He sat up in his bed, shaking his fist. 'GRAB THE FUCKING ROPE!' SMAACK! A second chairshot. Minio felt that one. He felt it in his heart. Joey tapped. Minio sat back against the headboard. 'Bobby, you alright?' Dawson asked, dragging her soft fingers over his bare shoulder. 'No... I'm not.' He stood up, in a pair of blue boxers, and walked into the bathroom, slamming the door behind himself in a rage. He stared at himself in the mirror, looking deep into his own eyes. 'Where am I going? What am I gonna do, what are WE gonna do...' He said gesturing towards the direction of Dawson in bed through the wall. 'When the only couple that ever made sense has turned. Has broken up. Has been torn? What the fuck can I expect in life? Are ALL WOMAN THAT SHIESTY? FUCK!!' He put his fist against the mirror, sending a shard above it flying through the air and against the tiled wall. A large spiderwebbed crack surrounded his fist, as blood trickled down the mirror. The door burst open, Dawson, wrapped in the bed sheet, sat glaring at Minio, frozen in the punch, breathing visibly hard, staring himself down. 'Bobby... whats wrong?' He took a long time to answer, before pulling his fist back from the mirror. He turned to her, and coldly spoke the words. 'Everything Angel. Everything is wrong. But who cares. Lets go back to bed.' He stomped past her, she followed him with her eyes, then looked at the mirror, then followed, climbing into the bed beside him. He glanced away from her as his eyes bubbled up with moist tears. None dripped, none fell, but they were there. He hurt for the last man he really respected. He hurt for the idea of the Malones. He hurt for himself and his future. He hurt for womankind. He hurt for every mistake a female had ever made. He hurt for watching helplessly as Joey Malone was assaulted by the woman he trusted. At that moment, one thing sat on his concious. Will anyone be there when Angelica stops loving me? And the answer was apparent. Yes, the third degree. 'Me... Myself... and I.' Dawson looked over to him as she draped the sheets over his side. 'What babe?' Bobby replied with a stuttered quickness. 'Er, uhh... Sorry, thinking outloud.' He leaned over, planting a kiss on her lips, hoping alittle physical attention would help her forget his outburst, and maybe... just maybe, help him forget his dismal future with her. The woman. The warm body.
