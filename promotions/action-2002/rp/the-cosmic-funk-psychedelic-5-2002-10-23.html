Action! — Tales

The Cosmic Funk Psychedelic. 5

23 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I found myself in the air. From the top turnbuckle. Right on my back. The sweat was rolling off my body like crazy, and it was mixing with this opponent of mine. He is perhaps the fastest athlete I have ever seen. The way he closes in on things is incredible. His strides in the ring are unruly. I flipped off my back, and saw him, white hair flowing down his face, eyes of cold steel piercing a hole right through me. I went for a clothesline. He cartwheeled. The next thing I knew, my head was ringing from a reverse DDT. Mercifully, the bell rang. The kid held his hand out and helped my fat ass up. I was barely able to stand on my own two feet, my legs wobbling back and forth. I had to prop myself up in the corner as Akira handed out the judgment. “You were a World Champion? That was a pathetic display. In all honesty, how were you ever though of so highly.” I could only shake my head. “We have a lot of work ahead. Shower up. Tomorrow, show up at five. In the morning. We are going to get rid of this fat and bulk muscle of yours.” And with that, I was off walking, this kid following me. When I entered, I sat my butt on a bench, and laid back. I took a breath of relaxation for the first time in what seemed like eons. “Don’t worry. He is usually worse on the first day. Usually, they are never as proven as you.” He said. “You kicked my ass.” “Only because you are out of shape. I’ve seen you man. And Syphon Fission is one of the most insane creations I have ever had the pleasure of witnessing. Once we work you into shape and actually work some new moves into the works, you’ll be unstoppable.” He said. “We’ll see.” Was all I could gather. “By the way, the name’s Alastair Nagatsuka. And I have been doing this for a long ass time.” I only laughed. I quickly showered and went home. It was three in the afternoon. I hit the hay immediately.
