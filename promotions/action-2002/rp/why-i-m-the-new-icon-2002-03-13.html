Why I'm the New Icon

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/13/02 'Hello, world!' The slightly-high-pitched voice of Tommy Matthews indicated that this special presentation was about to begin. Those followers of Action Wrestling, the ones who have been awaiting its debut, were being treated to a special streaming-video broadcast, live from the home of Tommy Matthews. Matthews' pretty-boy face came on display. The young man, wearing a white shirt that read 'The New Icon' in red letters on the front, and black leather pants, sat on a large chair inside his own home. The fans had been awaiting this presentation anxiously, and they could only hope Tommy did not dissapoint. 'As you know, Reed Young was kind enough to give me my own series of streaming video broadcasts here on the Action Wrestling website.' Tommy said. 'Of course, a smart move by Reed, since everyone knows that I'm the real reason everyone's been following this otherwise-crap fed. If it wasn't for Tommy Matthews, if it wasn't for The New Icon, Action's site wouldn't get the hits it does, and Action wouldn't have so many people awaiting its television debut. Reed Young knows that, so that's why he gave me this series of streaming video shows. After all, what better way to get publicity for Action than putting the Star of Action Wrestling, Tommy Matthews, on display?' The fans watching this at their computers seem a bit confused, and some appalled, at Matthews' attitude. Tommy Matthews is just some 20-year old kid fresh off the indy circut. some of the fans thought. He doesn't have the star power of LLB, Chris Fear, Christopher Fox, or any of the others. 'Now, before I go to the phones and e-mails, I'm going to answer the main question on everyone's mind.' Tommy said. 'Why am I the New Icon? Well, that is a simple question, if you think about it. In fact, I'm not even going to answer that with words. Instead, I'm going to take you to a match I had three weeks ago in Deleware with the Eastern Championship Wrestling Federation, against Lo-Ki.' The picture suddenly changed from Tommy Matthews' home to what looks to be a high school. Tommy Matthews, in his wrestling gear, was shown posing in the ring, then he was shown running into the ropes, grabbing them with both his hands, and sommersaulting over the top rope and down onto Lo-Ki below. The picture changed to another clip from the same match, and showed Tommy giving Lo-Ki his Split-Legged Moonsault through a table. Tommy leaped back onto the top turnbuckle right after pinning Lo-Ki's shoulders to the mat, and posing with his hands raised in the air to the cheers of the small crowd. The picture changed back to the room, where Tommy has a smile very evident on his face. 'Now THAT is why I'm the New Icon.' Tommy said. 'I'm the biggest up-and-comer in the wrestling buisness. Reed knows he is damn lucky to have gotten someone like me. And he knows who the real star of this show is, and he's not LLB, he's not Christopher Fox, he's not Chris Fear, but he's Tommy Matthews. And the sooner all of you start realizing that, the better.' Now the fans watching this presentation seemed split on Matthews. It was obvious that he had a lot of wrestling talent, but his arragoant attitude didn't make him exactly likeable. 'But, unfortunatley, we are now out of time.' said Tommy. ' I will see you all on the next edition of my show, when I'll be taking all of your calls and e-mails. Until then, never forget that I am the New Icon.' The picture on the streaming video broadcast fades out.
