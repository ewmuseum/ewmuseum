The Defining Moment: Part 4

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/02/03 May 25th, 2003 - Juggernaut II Immediately after the Bantam Championship Match. Look at him. Just look. Watch his slow, cold saunter through the curtains. Disappointment- that's what it was. That undeniable feeling that though you tried your very best to go the extra mile, you ended up veering off course in the long run. Clad in his wrestling tights, Jeff stood with his hands firmly on his hips. He breathed profoundly with a slight soreness in the back of his throat. His final match as Bantam Champ and he blew it. They asked him if he could out due his performance he put on in the famous Iron Man competition last December, and he assured them it wouldn't be a problem. Tonight, Juggernaut, everything meant so much more. And he wanted to feel happily. He did. He wanted to feel proud of himself. He helped to create an out standing contest with the new champion, Coral Avalon. But, for whatever the reason, he felt sick inside. Like he let himself, the fans, management, and the entire roster down. Depressed? Hardly, though he did find it more difficult as the days slipped by to smile. How different is that, though, than the first twenty-four years of his life when he felt alone ninety percent of the time? It wasn't until he married Julie that it slipped away and he was finally able to focus, in-ring and out, on what he wanted to achieve in life. But still, come on... even he could tell something was wrong. Sure, he felt alone before, but he was still happy. Now, well... he wasn't exactly sure how he felt. The best he could do was assure himself that it was a temporary deal, and that when he woke up tomorrow, it will all be a distant memory. He'll have Julie cook up some flap-jacks, a bit of bacon on the side, and he'll sink back into his chair and watch television all day. For the next week, he was officially off duty as a professional wrestler. Working out would, for the first time since he became employed by Action! Wrestling back in August, take a back seat to rest and relaxation. Which he missed terribly and could vaguely remember. But all of this would have to wait. For the time being, he was irritated. Really irritated. He didn't show it, but he was... His disenchanted feelings toward the quality of his match (despite the fact that it was fine work) were hidden in the back of his mind now. 'What was that?' He said out loud for everyone to hear. 'When did Kenshiro sign?' On camera, every fan knew and followed the long-running hostility between Jeff Garvin and Coral Avalon, dating back to his entrance into the promotion. And off camera, a couple of dirt-sheets and rumor web-sites had reported that there was reported disagreements and unfriendliness between the two. Neither saw eye-to-eye. Coral's girlfriend, Allison, didn't care for Jeff since the day she met him. He seemed arrogant, not 'heel' arrogant like he portrayed on television, but arrogant in the sense that he thought way to high of himself. Garvin held resentment towards many of Action!'s top-level wrestlers, the ones that currently were given spots in the main events. Although it was silent, meaning he only spoke about it with close friends, like Keith Scott (who happened to be his current running-body off camera as well), he couldn't stand LLB, or Fox, or Cylear… Or especially that loud mouth KroW, who was always trying to worm his way, back into the title picture. He didn't like any of them. This didn't mean he was not a good guy at heart, he was… nice to his wife, friends, and polite most of the time. Jeff was just a -little- paranoid when it boiled down to when he was going to be given his shot at stardom. This a cut-throat business, you have to take the opportunities when you can because there will always be someone else watching over your shoulder, looking to steal away what you earned. Like stated, he disliked quite a few people (most of which were commonly know to create problems in the lockerroom, so he DID have his reasons) but he did not hate any of them. Hate's a strong word... But not strong enough when it came to a certain someone whom ruined his entire Juggernaut performance. Ruined? Yes, Kenshiro ruined, TAINTED, the straight-up wrestling match he had with Coral. Through all the blood, through all the risky-bumps that took place on many of the events held in wrestling, Jeff had suggested they go the 'old school' route. They would not 'juice' tonight. It was going to be a wrestling match- a physical contest between two rivals, with possibly a handshake after the match depending on the crowd's reaction. That's what he wanted. That's not was he received. 'Who's big FUCKING idea was it to send him out there?' Garvin called out again, his voice growing in volume. To him, that was a show of disrespect for their wishes. And they (management) did not even have the stomach to mention it beforehand. No, just send the trained martial-artist, who, Jeff has shared a hatred with off-camera, out tp the ring, let him sneak up from behind and 'shoot' on him for ten minutes. That's what it was, a 'shoot'... and he had the bruises, the lacerations, and the swollen lump on his forehead to prove it. MotherFUCKERS. He went out and wrestled with an injured back for nearly THIRTY minutes, and management hands him that in return? His hands rose up and he shouted, 'Thanks ALOT! Oh, thanks a lot, guys!' He already knew what the agenda was for all of this. The booking committee knew that a vast majority of their viewing audience followed 21w and they wanted to run the long awaited 'Garvin/Ninja II' angle. Nope. Wasn't gonna’ happen. Then he heard the concerned voice of one of Action!'s many writers, walking towards him. He laughed, rose his eyebrows up in an innocent and cheerful manner, and said, 'Wait, Jeffster, we can explain... Ya' see, we had this great idea-' There it was. The 'GREAT IDEA'. But Jeff didn't give a damn. 'Chow.' He waved back with a middle finger stuck out to add a little zing. Then left. He need to get far away before a shit-storm began.
