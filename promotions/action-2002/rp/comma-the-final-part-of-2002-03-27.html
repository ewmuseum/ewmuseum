Comma, The Final Part of

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/27/02 Other Part: Comma, The First Part of Comma, The Second Part of Comma, The Third Part of Date: March the 27th. 12:12 AM EST. “Mr. Ermann. Wake up, Mr. Ermann.” “What? It’s done? That fast? Ha!” LLB spoke to himself while rolling around on the bed underneath him. “That was easy. I better open my eyes.” Shaking off the spider webs in his brain, ‘The Law’ rose from his laying down position. There stood Nurse Sarah, watching over him. “We’re all done.” She said smiling. “Your teeth came out very easy. You shouldn’t even have that much swelling.” “Well... I’ll be dammed. Talk about easy!” LLB snapped around, checking his arms and entire body. “Everything... off?” He asked, glancing up at the Nurse, barely able to talk, but he could. She smiled again. “Yes. Everything’s off.” “Good.” He muttered to himself, trying to get his feet out from under him, still feeling a little woozy. “How are you-” “Fine. Just fine.” And even though he didn’t seem like himself inside, Roland wanted out of there as soon as possible. He’d lie, if he had too. And lie he did. “My jaw... it feels like my whole bloody lip.” LLB stated, placing his right hand over his face and feeling around while doing so. “Ah, I’m sure it’ll wear off.” Putting both feet out and standing, LLB headed towards the door, a little unstable, but hiding it well. “Am I all done?” He asked nearly killing his dry throat when speaking. Nurse Sarah checked the clock. “Yes. If you feel fine enough, you can go.” “Damn. Good. Just get me the fuck out of here legs... I’ll do anything.” LLB pushed the door back and staggered out, making his way to the elevator. “I don’t feel *that* bad.” He mentioned. “Regardless, get home. Get sleep. And then Roland, get your mind focussed on the law again...” LLB smiled, throwing his right hand into the air as a small type of victory salute. He was ‘The Child Written in Law’ again. He was LLB. He had authority. Case Closed.
