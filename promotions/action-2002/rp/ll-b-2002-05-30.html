Action! — Tales

LL. B.

30 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OPEN Air filters into the hallway as Action staff members try to organize wardrobes and other essential belongings.... “Look, it’s Chris Fox!” Title: LL.B. Date: 28/5/02 Place: Arena Hallway As some staff turns to actually see Fox, others keep to their business. Fox, with Pikachu in hands walks down the floor.... and though usually smiling, he’s not today! Fox also skips down the hallways too, but not today either! For some reason Fox wasn’t himself at this moment. Could it have been because he was crying in front of everyone? Or was he so over excited that he was now rid of LL................ B. He stops, coming upon his locker room door reading “Chris Fox”. Fox “Well, this is us.” With a push on the handle and a long creek of the joints, Christopher enters. Once inside all Action Staff members go back to work. So, Fox walks over and sits on the only chair in the whole room. Setting Pikachu down on the floor beside him, Chris seems to spill content. Fox “Pikachu.... On Sunday.... I was.... I was humiliated in front of a lot of people. I, Christopher Elliot Fox should not get his ass raped in front of my Fox’s Ox’s! All this time. I knew it. Some friend he was. It’s over. It’s over.” Chris crosses his legs. Fox “You know what Pika Peek?” .... Fox “That’s exactly right! I’m on my own from now on! No more being jobbed to “The next icon in sports” Or being beaten up by stupid fucking punks like Frostman and Cast!” That’s right! It’s all aboot me! Freg yeah! Fox “I’ve fucking had enough being bad and singing stupid ‘Motivation’ songs! I’m going to kick bottom, guzzle Milkshakes with my Ox’s and oh heck yeah, be the most funniest, annoyingest Dandelion in the universe.... BIG TIME! Why? Because I’m Christopher Fox and I do whatever I want to do from now on!” Just then an Action Staff Member walks in. Staff “Hey Chris, I know you hate makeup but.... you need it!” Ok, stick to your plan of doing whatever you want! Fox “Be there in a sec!” Wait! You hate makeup.... Yeah, I know, but he told me to go and get some on.... Ok Chris, we’ll let that one go but next time I expect more! Fox “Yeah, ok, deal! Time to get ready to go out there and kick some butt and show every Action Stupid Star what I’m made of! And one stupid Star in parts-icular will get a special butt kicking....... The Action Chump, L L........ B!” CLOSE
