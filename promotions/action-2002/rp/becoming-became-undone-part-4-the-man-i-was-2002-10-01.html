Becoming. Became. Undone. Part 4 - The man I was

1 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.01.02 Became. 'The person I was.' A voice over what looked like a projector image of a little boy running around a grass New Jersey front yard. Firebird in the drive way. Chainlink fences and blue above ground pools. He was round faced with brown hair and brown almond eyes. A young Bobby Minio. 'Time progressed. I got older...' Bobby, now looking about ten was wandering around the streets of Atlantic City. Saying goodbye to his birthplace. A red Raider jeep pulls up beside him. A woman with long brown curly hair and brown almond eyes pulls up. She calls out to Bobby, he smiles at his mother, climbing into the jeep. 'I moved to Westerly Rhode Island for two years, where even as a child I found that you can't trust anyone. Between overcoming a bully and then being seen as a bully, then being beat up by a bunch of like aged eleven year olds on the play ground, I found that every one will kick you when you're down, even children.' Bobby is then seen, a teen ager, sixteen to be exact, setting up a wrestling ring then smiling into the camera. A banner behind him read 'Cherry Hill Wrestling'. 'Cherry Hill Wrestling... When I was still clueless. I eventually moved back to New Jersey and got a job putting up the ring for a small shindig called Cherry Hill Wrestling. One of the Wrestlers, Henry Hammer, saw me bouncing off the ropes and decided i'd make a good wrestler. Hammer trained me for a year, then, passed on from a coke overdose. Cocain was big in those days.' Bobby is shown as an eighteen year old, riding around the Las Vegas strip in his blue Mustang. A wide grin on his face. Bright lights. Big city. 'Las Vegas. I moved there when I was eighteen. Still wide eyed and wet behind the ears. I met up with Lance Brazil and Luke Fuentes. Two men who both had the same wrestling expirience as me. We joined up with...' A black ring with an indy sized crowd is shown. A 'Fire Wrestling Alliance' banner hangs high. Bobby is perched up on the top rope, a mexican with a big smile and an agile white man with a short haircut and goatee are holding down a masked man in red as Bobby hits a shooting star press on the masked man. A montage of Bobby, Luke, and Lance as the Masters of Apocalypse beating various people in the indy ring are show, Lance picking a blonde man up in a bench press as Bobby springboards off the ropes with a legdrop in air. Bobby hitting a half nelson suplex when Luke Scoops the man up for another suplex. Then it fades. 'FWA went under. Luke and Lance and I still talk when I swing by Vegas. Both have retired. The wrestling game was fun to them, but to complicated. Lance is now a Shao-lin lohan and Luke pretty much just gets high, but they're old friends and anything they do is cool to me. I miss them. I miss having people I could trust.' A large ring is shown. A Large crowd. The ramp has a 'PIW' logo above the entrance. Minio is in the ring jocking for position against Lowell. Another shot is shown, Bobby Minio throwing Cell out of the ring. 'PIW was my big break. Era one was great. Until ... Overdose.' Overdose is standing in the ring when Minio comes from behind and levels him with a chair. Various shots of Minio wrestling the PIW hall of famer are shown. Overdose beating on Minio quite a bit, then the Pay Per View. Bobby and Overdose both bloody faced, when Minio finally pins him. He leaps in the air with celebration. 'I couldn't believe I beat him. I just couldn't believe it.' Bobby Minio is shown parading around the ring with the PIW Television title. 'It all went down hill from there on out.' The projector stopped, the sound of the track being changed was distant.
