Welcome to Hell: The Demi-God of Wal-Mart [2]

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept. 3rd, 2002 The alarm clock goes off beside Kent Anthason who is lying on the couch of his newest friend, Alyssa Cleeda. Anthason groggily gets up, and stretches. His bones crack under the pressure that he, himself put on them. He slams down on the alarm clock, which stops buzzing. Kent yawns and proceeds to get up. He's still in his clothing from last night. He pulls out a comb, and combs his hair so it's not sticking up every-which-way. About that time, Alyssa walks in. 'A little late getting up, aren't we?' 'Sorry, I wasn't the one soo plastered I couldn't stand last night.' Kent replied. 'Take a look, outside, Kent. It's STILL last night.' And, with that, Alyssa slapped him upside the back of the head. 'Com'on. We've got things to do.' With those last five words... 'We've got things to do.' A sudden shiver ran up Kent's spine. Somehow, someway, he knew his life would be in jepoardy. We suddenly switch to Alex Trubek... 'Today, on Jepoardy...' He takes a long breath, building the suspense... 'KENT ANTHASON'S LIFE!' And, now, back to our regularly scheduled programming. Alyssa and Kent strap into the GMC. Alyssa's car had been out of operation for a little bit and she was liking the idea of not having to walk places. *I might keep him.* she thought lightly, and chuckled. 'Now, where we're going may seem like a regular place for a newcomer to this town, but it's totally different. A spirit rests in the supermarket we are going to attend in a few moments. We're going to Wal-Mart, Kent. And the soul of Hitler's ex-wives' second cousin, twice removed lives there. He strikes down all who pay dis-respect to him. Be wary, alright?' 'And why are we going to Wal-Mart, Alyssa?' 'I need some pads.' A very long, uncomfortable silence fell upon them, that is, until Kent broke the silence he usually does... 'You're going to make me buy them, aren't you?' Kent questioned as he glanced at Alyssa. 'You're damn right.' * * * Kent and Alyssa make their way back, back, and further back into the town where they pass a bum-looking person, he's carrying a bottle of scotch, and walking down the darkened street. Giggling to himself, he drunkenly staggers down the walk toward a large, looming building. 'Kinda reminds me of someone just a few hours ago...' 'Shut up, and stop the car. We need to pick him up.' She replied to Kent. Kent didn't have a thing against bums, he even trained one, but as to pick one up while he was drunk and let him in the car? Nonsense. 'Pick him up, you hear me?' Alyssa looked at Kent with that psycho-PMS kinda look you get every once and a while from your girlfriend when you accidently drop soda on her new shirt at the movie theatres! Did you hear that, Joanna! I'm fucking sorry! Anyhow, Kent stopped beside the bum. He whistled quite loudly. The bum turned sharply around with a grimace on his face, but with the sight of an attractive woman looking at him, his frown turned upside down. 'Need a ride, stranger?' Alyssa asked. * * * They stop in front of Wal-Mart. Alyssa warns Kent not to let the bum run off. Anthason jerks the bum out of the back seat... 'Isshh dat any way to treet ur guests, mothafucka?' Kent slams the bums head against his GMC. Alyssa warns him again. 'If you let him go, I'm going to remove your manhood manually. Got it?' 'What exactly is he for, anyway?' 'You'll see.' As the beaten, and worn man looks at the massive building which is secluded from the city, he cringes and tries to run off. Kent has a firm grip on him, and pulls back hard, flinging the man in front of him. The bum gulps loudly, and takes a swig of his whiskey. 'Im gunna need to be reaeelly drunk fer dis one.' 'You can practically smell the evil roll off this building.' Alyssa states, as she puts her hands on her hips. If anything would be odd to you, it'd be seeing a Wal-Mart in the middle of the dark woods, especially during night-time. 'I think that's the bum.' Kent stated back to Alyssa. She shrugged and beckoned Kent to follow. 'Peesh, Mistah! Pleesh don' make mea go in thar... Peesh!' The bum pleaded with Anthason, almost to his knees crying. 'Don't let him go. If you do, I'll kick your ass.' She turned around and gave Kent a quick, and spiteful look. 'You and what army?' All of a sudden, a huge roar emits from the huge grey building that looks like any other ordinary Wal-Mart. The slight rumble continues to get louder as Kent and Alyssa stare in all the Wal-Mart's massive glory. 'I won't need to do it by myself, either.' Alyssa says, with a smirk. *Gulp* The building shakes at it's foundation as the roar gets louder, and louder. The setting sun glares into Kent's eyes, as he squints to see the door way-entrance to the massive department store. The doors open, automatically, and a huge gust of wind barrels through the opened entrance. A small man, with a brief-case comes hurdling at speeds unknown out, and slams against a pick up truck. Blood slowly pours from his nose, as a booming voice is heard. That like a semi-trucks horn. '...AND YOU SHALL NEVER SEE THE LIGHT OF ANOTHER DAY, AGAIN! AH HAH HAH HAH AHAH AHHAH HAH!' The voice stops, and the doors close. The wind dies down, and Kent looks at Alyssa longingly. 'Holy..... Shit......' Kent's jaw is ajar, his eyes almoust bulging out of his sockets. Saliva hangs down from his lower lip, and with a slight shake he snaps out of his near comatose state. 'Come on! And bring that bum with you!' Kent slowly makes his way with the defiant bum toward the entrance to the store. The bum twitches and pulls, trying to escape Kent's grasp. Alyssa looks calm as ever, but Kent is scared shit-less. The first automatic doors open, the two step through, and the doors close again, trapping Kent and Alyssa between two sliding doors. They won't open as Kent steps in front of them. 'We're trapped. We're going to die. I know it!' Kent cries. He looks horrified. 'No, we're not, Kent. Calm down. The Demi-God of Wal-Mart has mercy for those who bring him human sacrifices.' 'You were going to use me! You're using me to get in the department store! How dare you!' Alyssa points at the bum, squirming in Kent's grip. The bum gives a blood curdling scream as he finds out his fate. 'Oh.' The booming voice returns from a speaker on the ceiling where Kent and Alyssa are. 'WHAT IS YOUR BUISNESS HERE, MORTALS? DO YOU HAVE SOMETHING FOR ME?' The Demi-God of Wal-Mart, himself, roars. 'Here's a human sacrifice, dear God of Wal-Mart. Spare us so we may shop in your wonderful store!' Alyssa screams, and grabs the bum from Kent's hands and throws him onto the ground. 'HAH. YOU MORTALS THINK YOU CAN LURE THE GOD OF WALMART WITH HUMAN SACRIFICES? I CAN STRIKE ONE PERSON DOWN WITH SWIFT JUSTICE, AND BOOMING WORDS! I CAN MAKE YOUR HEART STOP WITH JUST THE SOUND OF 'CLEAN UP ON ISLE FIVE.' AND YOU THINK YOU CAN TEMPT ME WITH SUCH PITIFUL SACRIFICES?' He roared louder than ever. He was quite a cocky Demi-God when it came down to it. Kent was jealous. 'Hopefully?' Kent questioned. The gum-ball machines suddenly open, and multiple gumballs shoot from the opening. They pelt the bum extremely hard. One hits him in the head, and splits his skull wide open, revealing the brains. Another hits his prostrate. And another nails his stomach, spilling his organs. Kent looks mortified onward at the carnage. 'That was the most horrendous thing I've seen since Will Smith's last music video.' Kent squeaked. 'Well, looks like he accepted.' Alyssa concludes. Kent notices a security camera watching it all. That must be how he see's things. If he is really he or something. Kent's very confused and terrified at the moment. The corpse of the bum, suddenly erupts into fire, and smoke smolders from the body. The ventalation system comes on, and sucks the smoke into the gaping holes in the celing. The automatic doors in front of Kent and Alyssa open. 'Thank you for choosing Wal-Mart, your discount kings. Enjoy shopping, and have a GREAT DAY.' The sliding doors open. The soft, inviting voice of the welcome as compared to the Demi-God's voice almost sounded cynical, psychotic, and sarcastic. Alyssa had to pull Kent foward. 'All this for pads?' Kent screamed. Kent and Alyssa walk up and down the isles. Looking at the signs that hang above them. Some say 'Laundry Detergent' some say 'Rat Poison' and some say 'Human Fetus'' But that's not what scares Kent the most. The next scene he's about to see is quite horrific. Kent and Alyssa finally find the isle where the pads are. A woman looks at them shiftily. Before she picks up a bottle of shampoo, and sticks it in her purse. 'Did you just see that?' Kent asked Alyssa, concerned to say the least. 'Unfortuenately, yes. And if we saw it, someone else saw it. I'd advise we hide.' 'OHH DEAR--' Suddenly, a whirl-wind picks up around the woman. The wind rips at Kent, and Alyssa's clothing. The booming, all so familiar voice, comes once again to collect it's dues. A single tear drips from Kent's eye... 'ATTENTION WAL-MART SHOPPERS. ATTTENTION WAL-MART SHOPPERS. IF YOU ARE IN THE IMMEDIATE VICINITY OF ISLE SEVENTEEN, PLEASE REMOVE YOURSELFS IMMEDIATELY. THANK YOU, THAT IS ALL.' Kent and Alyssa drop whatever they were holding, and run to the end of the isle, they duck behind a shelf and peer at the lady who now has a full force tornado spinning round-about her. The woman's clothes come off suddenly, and the twister becomes darker, and darker. 'OH MY GOD!' Kent screams quite loudly. Alyssa puts a finger over her mouth. 'YOU CALLED?' The booming voice replied. 'Oh, nothing. Just checkin'. You know. Keepin' tabs on my homie.' 'I HEAR YA, DAWG.' The tornado begins to get lighter, and lighter until a full view of the woman that was just surrounded can be seen with the naked eye. Although, we wouldn't call it much of a woman anymore, because all of her skin has been ripped off, and massive amounts of blood sucked up. The twister dissappears, and it begins to rain blood in isle seventeen, as the woman's body falls to the ground, dead. The corpse begins to burn, and the ventalation system turns on, as Kent and Alyssa leave the row. 'Wait, hold on.' Kent runs back to where they were just hiding, and picks up his jaw and catches up with Alyssa. 'That had to be one of the most repulsive things I've ever seen in my life.' Kent states. 'I know, wasn't it great? *wink*' 'You're sick, Alyssa.' 'Well, at least I got my pads. While we're here, we should pick some more stuff up. I don't feel like working my way around, you know, him again.' 'How about we rent a movie, you know, to watch when we get back to your apartment?' 'Good idea.' Kent and Alyssa make their way over to the video rental section. A lady with a huge smile on her face waits at the counter. She's tapping her fingers against the table loudly, as Kent and Alyssa approach. Alyssa smiles and waves while saying 'Hello.' Kent just smiles as though his mind were somewhere else. 'What movie do you want to see?' Alyssa interrogates Kent. *Click, click, click...* The fingernails hit against the counter. The Wal-Mart clerk blows a big bubble with her bubble gum, and it bursts all over her face. Still, the clicking continues. 'I don't know... errr. you choose.' Kent replies. *Click, clicky, click, click...* 'No, I insist.' Alyssa nudges Kent and winks at him. 'How about something... Hot?' *Click, cliiiiiiick.... cliiiiiick...* 'Umm... how abou--' *CLICK!* And then, as we all could've seen this coming ages before, Kent loses all control and bursts into a rage unlike seen by the Gods... Well, most of them, anyhow... His face becomes flushing red, and his teeth are pressed together quite hard. 'DEAR GOD. WILL YOU STOP THAT INFERNAL TAPPING. IT'S DRIVING ME MAD. I SWEAR TO GOD THAT IF YOU DO THAT ONE MORE TIM--' The wind picks up. Alyssa also becomes quite angry. With, Kent, though. She slaps him over the back of the head. 'You had to do that, didn't you?' She yells. The smile from the movie ladies face drops, and she ducks under her counter. No more clicks. 'YOU SWEAR TO WHOM, MORTAL? YOU SWEAR TO GOD?' The voice gives a glass shattering scream in the general direction of Kent and Alyssa. 'I was just annoyed by the tappin--' Kent pleads, 'You don't understand!' 'SILENCE, HUMAN. WHY DO YOU THINK I HIRED THIS LADY? SHE IS AN ASSET TO MY PLAN FOR WORLD DOMINATIO---- I MEAN... SHE IS AN ASSET TO THIS STORE, AND YOU MUST GO ALONG AND REFUSE TO RESPECT MY HOME? I CURSE YOU TO DEATH, PUNY MAGGOT. IT'S TIME I ROLL BACK MY SLEEVES AND DROP THE PRICES ON YOUR WORTHLESS CARCASS.' Kent suddenly breaks down crying. Alyssa grabs his arm and bites her lower lip. A bunch of basketballs are hurdling toward them in one direction. Hundreds of them. Pencils come out of their wrappers and become airborne. Kent and Alyssa glance at each other, then take off in a sprint. The Demi-God laughing his ass off over the whole store. Kent grabs Alyssa by the hand. She gives him a surpised look, and then let's loose and runs with him down an isle way where the toys are located. Toy helicopters away Kent and his partner, as well as many barbie dolls with pellet guns, and G.I. Joe characters fully ready for combat. 'Obviously not the way I wanted to go.' said Kent. Alyssa confirmed, 'Obviously not.' The two retrace their steps, and take off down the isle. Kent slams through to swining doors, and much to his dismay, he finds himself in the Farm and Garden section. 'Are you familiar with the term, out of the frying pan and into the fire?' Alyssa looks at Kent questioningly. An elderly lady standing at a counter looks at them. She smiles and looks down. She bends over and picks something up. When she returns to regular stature, her eyes glow red, and she's weilding a chainsaw... which is currently screaming 'RUN FOR THE HILLS, KENT AND ALYSSA!' but in it's chainsaw voice of 'VARRROOOOMMMM!!!!!' Kent and Alyssa, without even speaking chainsaw language, knew what it meant. Certian doom if you didn't take the hell off. 'Alright, remind me not to mess with old people again.' Kent states. 'I'll hold you to that.' Alyssa says back. The lady jumps over the counter, and rushes at Kent. Several screwdrivers, and appliances fly at the duo, also. Anthason catches a crow bar out of mid air, and hits the ground, crawling through the doors as fast as he can. Him and Alyssa stand back up and start to run. The elderly lady chases after them. Kent can only think of the sports center. Suddenly they make their way toward that section of the story. The demonic lady searches for them over hill and under dale. 'Hmm. What do we have to work with, here? Tennis racket... Won't do. But a home tennis ball shooter will.' Alyssa looks surprisingly at Kent. 'Wow, did you find your brain?' The elderly lady grabs a hocky mask from one of the isles and puts it on, before she reaches Kent's she turns around, and looks behind her. Alyssa hurls a foot ball which hits the woman in the back of the head. The elderly lady turns back around, and revs up the chain-saw. 'Yeah, somehow. This situation looks very dark, indeed.' Kent mumbles. He wheels the huge cannon out, and starts to pelt the lady with tennis balls. The old hag hits the floor hard, and Kent grabs a baseball bat, immediately crushing her ribs, and then slamming it across her face. 'GOOD JOB, KENT. ALTHOUGH, YOU STILL WONT MAKE IT OUT OF THIS STORE IN ONE PEICE. OR TWO PEICES. LET ALONE ALIVE! HAH HAH HAH HAH! I'm so evil, Clarance. Did you hear that? Huh? What? The Mic is on? OH SHIT.' Kent looks questioningly toward the speakers. Alyssa does the same. Then, the speakers click off. 'Alright, I'd like to know what that was all about.' 'I don't know, but let's move. We're sitting ducks.' Kent and Alyssa run as fast as they can, but they end up in the electronics department. All of the televisions have the words 'You're going to die a slow, and low-priced death.' And on the stereo's the song 'The Whistling Smiley Face Whistles Dixie' plays loudly. The CD cases begin to split open, and the compact discs themselves fly at Kent. One cuts his arm, as it begins to bleed, the two people duck behind a television. 'Kent, are you alright?' Alyssa says as she grabs his arm and rips some of her shirt to stop the bleeding. Kent gives her a sad puppy dog face. 'Aww. Let me kiss it and make it all better.' Alyssa smiles. Alyssa bends down to kiss the injury, but Kent meets her with his lips, and they kiss passionately. Just for a second, because just as they split, a CD flies between their faces. The CD players suddenly play 'Get down tonight' by KC and the Sunshine Band. Kent knows, obviously, that the Demi-God is watching. The two run some more. They keep running, and escaping the near-death explosions. People's bodies lie everywhere, bloody and bruised, from mis-fires and being in the line of fire. The two make it to the hunters section. Kent grabs a shotgun, and a revolver. He loads the revolver up. He grabs a box of Shotgun shells and sticks them in his pocket. Kent loads up the shotgun. While he does so, he catches movement out of the corner of his eye. The deer beside him is moving. Alyssa is looking at Kent. 'What? What is it?' Alyssa looks quite worried, as... ...The deer jumps and kicks Alyssa in the side. She hits the ground hard, and Kent drops the gun. He jumps on the deer, and starts to nail it in the head. The deer kicks, but Kent rides him down like a Bronco. After a while, the Deer tires, and Kent loads a round into it's head. Alyssa is groggy, and bleeding from the side. Kent loads another round, and finds a security camera. Blam. It blows apart, and nothing moves in the Hunters department. 'What he can't see, he can't control, I guess. Let's go, Alyssa. We're out of here.' Kent and Alyssa run along, Isle to Isle, blowing apart cameras. The voice of the Demi-God is heard throughout the store. Winds rip papers, and things fly around violently. 'NO! THIS CAN'T BE! I'M MEETING MY FATE LIKE MY DEAR FATHER, THE GHOST OF WOOLSWORTH! THIS CAN'T BE! NOO! I'M MELTING! I'M MEEEELLLTTT-- Wait, a Minute. What the fuck am I saying?' The Demi-God of Wal-Mart suddenly becomes silent. Kent and Alyssa pass a door that says 'Employee's only.' Kent pushes the door aside, and a small man sits in a huge chair, in front of a big control board, and multipule screens. Some of them are black, from where Kent shot the camera out. Kent coughs quite loud, signaling his presence to someone for the second time that day. 'Not right now, I'm busy. If you're looking to pick up your check, do it later.' The man replies to Kent's cough. The man picks up a microphone. 'YOU! ATTENTION WAL-MART SHOPPERS. YOU WILL ALL DIE IF YOU DON'T FIND THIS MAN. GET HIM. FIND HIM. DESTROY HIM! OR I'LL HAVE A BIG DEMI-GOD OF WAL-MAR TEMPER TANTRUM!' Kent coughs quite loudly for the second time. 'Oh, what is it?' The man turns around to face Kent and Alyssa. He squeals like a pig, before grabbing a Nurf Ball and hurling it at Kent. Kent knocks it away in mid-air. 'Take that! You mean old man, you! I'm the Demi-God of Wal-Mart, you faggot! I'm also the owner of multiple IRC Porn chat-rooms! Feel my wrath!' The man throws a Game Boy at Alyssa who snatches it, and throws it down. The man squeals, before he grabs the microphone. 'SECURITY! SECURITY! SECUR--' Kent grabs the microphone, and throws it behind him. He then grabs the man by the collar, and the scene fades to black for a moment. We switch scenes to Ben Stien. 'Yes, kiddies. I just wanted to intterupt this slaughter-fest to let you know that violence is actually bad for America. If you like to indulge yourself in horror without the blood, guts, and gore... Why don't you try watching some of my old shows? The Wonder Years, is a good example. You can even win my money on primetime television. The point I'm trying to make here is that--' Tim Couch runs up, dressed in full uniform and holding a pigskin football. 'Hey, Ben Stien! What's up, man!' Ben Stien replies by pulling a Smith and Wesson twelve gauge sawed-off shotgun and spreading the brain fluids of Tim Couch all over the screen. Ben Stien walks over and wipes off the camera lense. 'Violence, as I said, is indeed very bad.' The scene re-opens to the sounds of 'Dose' by Fuel which is playing around the terror stricken store. Kent is shown bouncing a basketball on the Man's face. Then we see that the man is tied to parts of the shelves by bungee cords. Alyssa hits him with a ball bat in the stomach before they drop their items and leave. 'Let's go, Kent.' Alyssa says, and pulls Kent's arm. 'WAIT! I want to do one more thing before we go.' Kent runs off, and Alyssa waits for a few seconds before the speakers come on, once again. The Demi-God of Wal-Mart makes his laugh triumphant statement! 'O'DOYLE RULES!' Kent comes running back, and smiles as they make their way out of the store. Bruised, beaten up, and clothes torn. The two smile at each other, as Alyssa pulls a box out of her coat pocket. 'Hey, at least I got my Pads...' Alyssa winks, but Kent didn't find it quite as funny. The scene fades as Kent and Alyssa get into Kent's GMC. When it goes to black, we see the Man who was/is the Demi-God of Wal-Mart run across the screen. Following him is a disgruntled shopper with a box of Christmas Cards, who is throwing them like mad at the Man. They run past, and we will continue this adventure later... Brought to you by...
