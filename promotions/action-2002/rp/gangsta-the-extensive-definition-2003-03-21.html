Action! — Tales

gangsta: the extensive definition

21 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Before I share my life of crime with you, I think I should explain something. Because I’ve killed, maimed, trafficked narcotics, and such and such (which would be fucking retarded, had it not been for the legal loop holes which won’t allow these promos as evidence in any case against me...you gotta love lawyers) doesn’t mean I’m part of any criminal organization. You see folks, I’m not a gangsta. I am gangsta. There’s a difference. Observe: John Gotti: The former boss of the Gambino mafia family. He killed, ordered hits, ran many illegal rackets all over New York, Jersey, and where ever. He was part of an organization with very defined rules and parameters, but completely devoted to illegal activity. John Gotti; is a gangsta. George W. Bush: The current president of the United States. He and the government don’t chiefly engage in illegal activity like a crime syndicate would. However, Bush has fabricated facts, manipulated countries and the public at large, disobeyed the wishes of most of the World, and demanded that Saddam Hussein disappear within 48 hours or he and his boys are fucked. Not because Saddam poses an immediate serious threat, but because there’s something about that sand monkey that rubs him the wrong way. He’s willing to have most the World hate him, send thousands of Americans to their deaths, slaughter innocent Iraqis in the process, just to wipe that smug expression off Saddam’s face. George W. Bush; ladies and gentlemen is gangsta. Much like myself. Hope that clears things up. Anyways... The next name on my shitlist is Antonio Ramirez. He’s a coke dealer. A pretty successful one at that. He’s greedy, ruthless, and absurdly paranoid. Like’s to think he’s fucking Scarface. Which is bullshit, but he is about to get lit up like Tony did. Guess you can call me Sosa. Like I said he’s paranoid as hell. His house is guarded 24/7. He travels no less than 20 deep. He rides in one of those security cars all the stars got now. A bullet proof, bomb proof, Bentley, with several holes to shoot out of and a rotating machine gun turret, hidden in the back. Short of a tank, nothing can get to him in this thing...from the outside that is. *** “So you won’t give it.” “No.” “No?” “What is there an echo?” “So YOU are telling ME...you won’t give it?” “That’s what I’m saying.” “Not even if it’s flying?” “Don’t care if it’s flying.” “What if it’s fat?” “Don’t care if it’s fat.” “Ok. What if...it’s the mother kind?” “No.” “...What if-” “Look! I won’t give it to you, or anyone, or anything. I simply DON’T GIVE A FUCK!! Get it? I don’t give a fuck. I don’t give a flying fuck. I don’t give a fat fuck. I don’t give a motherfuck. Deal with it!” “Alright, alright. Geez, your pretty touchy for a guy who doesn’t give a fuck.” They were going on like that for about 30 minutes. You don’t know what willpower is until your hiding in a backseat with a high-powered automatic rifle which could easily shut these two shitheads THE FUCK UP. How I got in? I would like to tell you I concocted a brilliant scheme to distract and disable his guards. Or how I penetrated his well-guarded compound employing a magnificent display of stealth. But the truth is...I hopped in, with my weapon of course, completely unsuspected and laid low. I guess when your looking out for multi-man kill teams raiding your place from helicopters and shit, little things like locking your back door slip your mind. “Who the fuck-” That was Antonio talking. He was cut off by a couple hollow tips, courtesy yours truly. I lit up a few of his crew (including the two fucktards in the front seat, I made sure of that) and made my escape...by stealing the Bentley of course. I was pretty deep inside the compound, so it wasn’t exactly easy. I’d tell you all the graphic details of my daring exit. But I feel pointless depictions of violence have no place on TV. ...atleast not in my promos anyways.
