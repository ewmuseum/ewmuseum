Date: 8/23/02 Dallas, TX | 20:55PM CST MAX DANGER Honey, this has been the best birthday of my life! And I'll be right back to enjoy even more of your wonderful, WONDERFUL gift after I make a quick phone call. MAX DANGER is standing with his head out of view, due to the bedroom door. He is barely holding onto a sheet covering the lower-half of his body. He shuts the door behind him and slowly makes his way through the hotel room kitchen and into the living area. He picks up the phone on the coffee table and dials ten digits. DANGER Hey boss, it's me Max. .....(indicates Danger listening to a reply) DANGER What? Who? No, No. Max Danger, from Action!, remember? ..... DANGER Well anyway, Mr. Young, as you may or may not know, today is my 25th birthday, and- ..... DANGER Why thank you, sir. Anyway, what I was wondering is is if you could give me a present? ..... DANGER Yes, I realize it isn't polite to ask for gifts, but this is a special occassion. ..... DANGER No, a stripper will not be necessary. I just want to ask something of you for the PPV tomorrow night. ..... DANGER Yes, it's called 'Down 4 The Count'. (Shakes head in disbelief.) ..... DANGER Right, 'Down 4 The Count', it's tomorrow night in Dallas, Texas. Anyway, what I was wanting was a match with Conrad Ramsey. ..... DANGER So? I can assure you that he will be out of retirement come tomorrow night. ..... DANGER How can I be sure? Just trust me sir. ..... DANGER What? No, we are not a team any longer. Haven't been for awhile sir. And before you say anything, no Angelica and I are not a team or anything for that matter. ..... DANGER I'm sure we did make a cute couple. (Rolls eyes.) Anyway, can you book the match? I can guarantee you that Ramsey will show up and he will wrestle me. Just book it and advertise it. It will happen. ..... DANGER What kind of question is that? I haven't told anyone about that. ..... DANGER Mr. Young sir, I can promise you that I am not going to the competition. I assure you that. ..... DANGER Ok, thank you sir. Bye bye. Max puts down the telephone and holds the sheet tightly to keep it from falling to the floor and exposing his privates. DANGER How did he know about that? Max just shakes it out of his head and heads back across the living area and through the kitchen. He opens up the bedroom door. DANGER Having sex for each year of my life is SO much better than blowing out candles! The sheet was being dropped as the door closed behind Max. FADE OUT
