Action! — Tales

The Velcro Heart, The Twenty-Fifth Part of

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An unknown man paced the hallways of the Hara Arena, as he turned each corner with speed and diligence. He looked scared... somewhat nervous. And more than likely he was, as he walked himself right into the wall. “Shit.” He said, turning back and finding his way through the maze again, trying to get to where he wanted to be. He wore a white hoody, that basically covered his entire face except for the mouth area, as his pants were taken from a blue tracksuit. Not a good combo, but it didn’t really matter. The “man in the white hoody” turned another corner... walking even faster. “Must find it... must find it.” The voice seemed so familiar... Yet no one could put their finger on it. The “man in the hoody” then stopped. “Aye... yes, here we go.” He looked up. LLB’s locker room. The Fans sat there... wondering what was going on. Maybe this was an answer to why KroW was leaving Action! Wrestling after this coming Pay-Per-View? The “man in the white hoody” didn’t know, though... he just stormed in as LLB shot from his sofa and literally clotheslined the man to the ground... beginning to kick him wildly. “I DIDN’T, YOU IDIOT!! GET OUT!! OUT!!” He stopped, tilting his head. “It’s me.” The “man in the white hoody” scrambled to take off the hood... but it was going to take a while. “Go. Go to the door and close it!” Recognizing the voice, LLB did what he was told as he turned back around. The figure got up, face pointed towards the wall so he could not be seen just yet. “Fuck. I didn’t know it was you.” The “man in the hoody” finally got it off his face as he turned around. The wild brown hair... and beady brown eyes. Along with that pathetic looking grin. Reed Young. “I didn’t know you were coming tonight, Ree-” Young put his finger to his lips, even making a louder noise than LLB had. Stupid Reed. “SSSHHH! People can’t know I’m here.” LLB sighed, as he went back to his sofa to rest before the Main Event. “What’s up?” Having a hard time getting the words out at first, Young took a deep breath and spoke. “I’m getting sued.” LLB seemed shocked. “Who? Why? And how?” “IWO. The ‘Hostile Taekover’ logo... and, because... they said I STOLE IT!” Young started to scream. “BUT I DIDN’T STEAL IT!! I MADE IT MYSELF!” ‘The Law’, as well as everyone else knew Reed Young was telling a lie... but there were two kinds of people in this world. People that told lies and believed them. And people that told lies and deep down inside, knew the truth. We figured, Reed’s in column A. LLB shrugged. “What do you want me to do about it?” Young slapped his head. “WHAT DO *I* WANT *YOU* TO DO ABOUT IT!?” Reed seemed pissed off. “I WANT *YOU* TO FIX IT! You’re the lawyer... Get me out of this! GET ME NOT SUED!” Great grammar Reed, really. “Lower your voice.” LLB stood up and walked over to Young, putting a hand on his shoulder. “There’s nothing I can do. It’s theft. You took the logo... and used it.” “They- Rowe- they-” Young almost broke down to cry. “THEY WANT ME TO CHANGE THE NAME ROLAND!! THEY WANT ME TO CHANGE THE NAME BACK!!” “You have to. IWO had it before you.” Young broke free from LLB’s arm as he gave him the evil eye, still sobbing, though. “But- but Rowe... I MADE THE NAME!! I DID THE LOGO!! If anything... they stole it from me!” Alright... even the smart ass lawyer didn’t know where to go with this one. “I can’t be your lawyer. You have no fight.” “What do you mean!? YOU’RE LLB! You’ve won over 87% of your cases as PIW says! If you could get Kenny Rock OUT of jail... you can get ME *OUT* of this!!” “Reed, Reed... Reed.” The Champion sat back down on the sofa. “I... nor any other lawyer could win this one for you. No matter what.” “YOU DON’T MEAN THAT!” “Jesus... fuck Reed... keep your voice down.” “YOU DON’T MEAN THAT!” Young started to cry. “YOU DON’T MEAN THAT... BECAUSE, ROWE, IF YOU DO... I’LL OWE THEM MONEY AND I CAN’T USE THE NAME EITHER!!” He dropped to his knees. “I NEED THE NAME ROWE... YOU DUNNO WHAT IT MEANS TO ME!” LLB just stared blankly into him. It took Young one minute to think of the name... he couldn’t have wanted it that badly. “Um... go find a different lawyer then. I can’t waist my time with this... and I’m sure, Reed, I’m sure... they can’t and won’t either.” “Vineal Plutz. He’s the only other lawyer I know, Rowe... and he ant even that good!” Young brushed a tear from his eye. “I mean... hell... HIS OFFICE IS IN A SHACK ROWE... A SHACK!” The Action! Champion rolled his eyes. “Did you not listen... to a bloody thing I’ve just said?”
