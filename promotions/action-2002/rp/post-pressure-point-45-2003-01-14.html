Post-Pressure Point 45

14 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/14/03 Max Danger had just walked through the curtains after having his confrontation with Kayden Paulton. He was smiling from ear to ear, unable to hide his delight. His intentions were simple: Get Kayden pissed off. It really upset Max that Paulton always took the high road. Never got angry. He just went along with life, without a real care in the world. Always smiling and waving. It made Danger sick to his stomach. But he always told himself, 'Anyone who walks around that happy must be seriously fucked up on the inside. No one can be that happy.' But the Dothan, Alabama native seemed to be. Well, not so much at the moment. He was, of course, being helped backstage by a couple of referee's. Danger didn't really do anything. Sweet Aroma had done a good deal of the punishment, really weakening the man's ankle. He was grateful for that, though it was never planned to happen that way. 'I like her,' Danger muttered to himself as he past by several crew members playing spin the bottle. 'She's a good girl.' He was, of course, referring to Veronica aka Sweet Aroma. He continued on and then he stopped. From a side hallway, water was flying through the air. Several women came running in front of 'The Danger Man' soaken wet. They screamed as two other men chased them down. They stopped and spotted Danger. 'Oh no! Don't you even think about it!' he said sternly. But there was no stopping it. His chocolate pants, gray Adidas running shoes(he needed to be comfortable), light blue(almost UNC Blue) button-up shirt, with white t-shirt underneath, and his chocolate demin jacket were drenched in the H20. He cursed loudly, as the men with the hoses ran off after the women again. He tried shaking off the cold, wet(as it was want to be) water and continued on his trek through the corridors of the arena. He just wanted to get out now. All the joy he had for humiliating Kayden Paulton was drained from him. He wasn't the least bit happy. He didn't even pick up his feet as he walked, something that he hated when others did the same. It just annoyed him. But, finally, after coming across nothing else really out of the ordinary, he pushed the doors open and entered the parking garage. That was when he was blindsided by someone. He was knocked to the dirty ground. 'Great,' he thought. 'Now I'm wet AND covered in dirt.' He pulled himself off the ground and tried his best to clean himself off. But the wetness of his clothing forced the dirt and such to stick to him. He then looked around to see who it was that had knocked him down. He just shook his head and rolled his eyes. 'Jesus, Gus! Why'd you do that for?' Homeless Gus had gotten himself up and didn't even bother dusting himself off. How would he know what was new dirt and what was old dirt? He rarely washed up. But he looked at Max and smiled. 'Reed You-' 'No he didn't!' Danger snapped, interrupting him. He of course had heard the story a million times. His excuse was always that Reed Young wanted him there. Max knew otherwise. 'He's not even hear, Gus.' Homeless Gus nodded his head in agreement, 'I know. He wanted me hear to be in charge tonight.' Danger just shook his head. He didn't care if it was true or not. He just wanted to get to his car, be with Jessica, and leave. He wasn't have the least bit of fun now. He stared impatiently at Gus and demanded, 'What do you want?' Gus just rubbed the toe of his flip-flops on the ground, with his arms behind him, and looked up at Max innocently. 'I was wondering, if, maybe... I dunno... you-' Danger cut him off. 'What? Give you a ride? Let you live with me? What? Just spit it out, I'm not in a very good mood, as you might have noticed.' Gus stood silent for a moment, Danger huffed, wanting the homeless man just ot 'HURRY THE FUCK UP!', which were the words Max shouted out. They echoed inside the garage, as Gus whimpered in fear. He managed a weak, 'Sorry.' Danger sighed deeply and was about to put his arm around Gus, then pulled his arm back. He just motioned for Gus to follow him. And together they walked along the parking garage. Max then opened up to Gus, 'I'm sorry, man. It's just, I was having such a great night. I got to run down Kayden Paulton verbally. That's always fun. And I was just on a high, you know? But then, I get sprayed with a friggin' water hose backstage, and then you come and knock me down. I'm just tired, exhausted, my nose if fuggin' broken... or something, I dunno. I just want to go home. So what is it, Gus, that you wanted?' Gus was getting teary-eyed. He sniffed and rubbed his nose with his dirty yellow raincoat. He looked up at Max and smiled his green-toothed smile. 'Just wanted to say hello, Max. That's all.' 'The Danger Man' threw his arms up in the air and let out a low growl, more like a frustrated groan, or something. He then stomped off away from Gus. Then the homeless man yelled out, 'Oh, and I needed a lift!' Danger didn't even turn around when he shouted back, 'There's an elevator inside the building!' Gus smiled and started to walk off. He then stopped and looked after Max, who had climbed into his car, where Jessica waited. He noticed her grimace at the sight of her husband, his clothes were a mess and all that. He then wondered what the hell Danger was talking about. How did he get elevator out of lift? He just shrugged his shoulders and walked off. In the background, the Dox's could be seen pulling out of the garage, off to wherever it was they were to go. Most likely the hotel. A change of clothes(and maybe some lovin') would do 'The Danger Man' some good. Especially after the two nights he's had. As their taillights disappeared in the distance, an ambulance was seen pulling in.
