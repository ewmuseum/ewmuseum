Action! — Tales

Reflections- The Injury [2]

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A drowsy ‘Lionheart’ Tyler Babylon rubbed his eyes and tried to get them focused on the darkness that covered the entire room. The medication that he was taking, because of the pinch nerve he had sustained in his match with Russa only a week ago, caused his drowsiness and often made him sleep for a few hours at a time the entire day. The bright red numbers of the alarm clock across the room said it was ‘3:47’ AM and there was absolutely no way that Babylon would fall back asleep anytime soon. Grabbing on to the top of the sofa he had fallen asleep on, he pulled himself up to a seated position. He was now able to see where everything was in the room, partly thanks to the light coming through a window emitted from the full moon. Still holding on to the top of the sofa, he slowly got up to his feet and hobbled across the room, holding on to anything he could get his hands on to make his walk that much easier. He got to a small-reclined chair where he just stared at Lindsay Russell, who was curled up in a ball in the chair. She was wrapped in a small wool blanket and also had to sacrifice some of the blanket to use as a pillow. Tyler Babylon silently stood there and just watched her as he listened to her breath. It was obviously made known to everyone how much that Tyler loved Lindsay. It was also just as obvious that Lindsay and Tyler were just best friends. She didn’t want to ruin their friendship so it was a topic not often talked about. He quietly sighed and remembered what happened when he tried to tell her how he really felt. “Look Tyler, I’m sorry!” Lindsay’s voice called down the hall at the ‘Lionheart’ as he was walking down the hall backstage. Tyler kept walking, pretending he couldn’t hear her. “Look! I know you’re mad right now but come on, you really think you stand a chance against Fission? He’s gonna…” Tyler never even gave Lindsay the chance to finish. He quickly stopped and turned around screaming at Lindsay. “WHAT? DESTROY ME? YOU NEVER DOUBTED ME BEFORE! WHY ARE YOU NOW?” Lindsay didn’t know what to say, as she never heard the soft-spoken Tyler talk to her like that. Tyler was quick to realize what he had done and knew that it was uncalled for. “Look Linz, I’m sorry. But it’s just like any other match I’ve ever been in and Fission is no difference.” “Tyler I still care about you, you know that. It’s just that I can’t watch Fission do what he did to you last week. I don’t want you to get hurt. But I should go now, you’re not going to listen to me anyways” Lindsay said before turning and leaving. Tyler just scratched the back of his head and watched her walk away. “Well, I’m going to win this one for you. Show you that you don’t have to worry about me and I’ll be fine. Lindsay, I love you…” Tyler mumbled at Lindsay so she couldn’t hear what he had to say. Tyler sighed and headed off. If it didn’t work last time, how was he going to tell her this time? The two of them had spent the entire week together and would spend a lot of time together in the future thanks to the injury. Maybe this time would be different? Babylon stood there, trying to remember the last guy that Lindsay had been seeing. He even tried to remember who the last girl he had been with was and how long ago that had happened. Action! had turned his personal life into a new realm. Apparently, the women seemed to love him and were always trying to seduce him after the show. That always caused him to stay in the arena, usually for at least a few more hours. Often he had Lindsay right by his side for the entire length of the wait. He gently reached for his cheek and softly touched it with the tips of his fingers. ‘Good night.’ He quietly said as he turned around to head back to his bed on the sofa. Surely, she felt something too.
