Vagabond - VIII-A

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/06/2002 Previous Parts: 1, 2, 3, 4a, 4b, 5, 6, 7a, 7b If one wished to beat Joey Malone, they had to get the job done. Syphon Fission did not do this. I'm sure you don't think as such because he used a pair of Death Plunges and the Faded Dreams to win a match, but that was an awful lot. Fission expected Joey to just roll over and die. He expected Joey to just give up. What he didn't count on was the fact that Joey was a quick healer, and the fact that he can come back from a lot and dish it out all the same. Coral Avalon just remembered this as he was sailing through the air of a gym. Landing on his back with a thud after taking a back body drop, a perfect bump, Coral dizzilly shook his head and rose to his feet, only to eat a right hand from Joey. Though Joey was notoriously left handed, it was surprising that his right hand looked just as good as his left did. He then shot Avalon into a corner and came charging in for a lariat, but Avalon ducked it and ran off the opposite ropes, straight back at Malone, before spearing him down. Both guys laid next to each other, and even though they were in great pain, they laughed. 'Still... stealin' the moves, I... see.' Joey commented, refering to Avalon's use of the spear, which was actually delivered in the style of the Erroneous Conclusion. Avalon used the assistance of the nearby ropes to pull himself to his feet. 'Heh.' was all Avy said, before he leapt up to the top rope and prepared for his next move. But Joey knocked him off and crotched Avalon on the top rope, before grabbing his neck and delivering a running neckbreaker that took Avy from the top rope to the middle of the canvas. Both guys just laid there. Finally, Avalon spoke up, still laid out in the middle of the canvas and staring idly up at the celing. 'Okay, can we please spare the truly spectacular kicking of my ass, now?' Joey stood up, knees wobbilly. They had mutually decided to try to improvise a wrestling match for the sake of doing one, and for the most part, their joking aside, they had done a pretty decent job constructing something watchable, as the other people in the gym could attest to. Joey was impressed, and idly wondered just how long it would be before Coral ended up with that contract. Joey grabbed a towel that was hanging over one of the turnbuckles and used it to wipe off the large amounts of sweat that had built up from their match. He tossed it to Coral, who had just gotten up, and then left the ring. He sat down at a chair and began to think about how his training was going. A few seconds later, Coral sat next to him. There was a few seconds of silence before Coral spoke up. 'Hey, Joey? Why haven't you gone to see Kent, yet? It's been a few days since you got here. I thought you two go way back.' Coral asked. At first, Joey didn't feel like responding, but then he thought about it. 'I would feel uncomfortable with talking to him again. We kind of got into an argument the last time I saw him. A loud, screaming one.' Joey said. 'He wasn't too happy with what happened at Mall Brawl, last year, and yelled at me over it. He was at my wedding, but he didn't say a word to me. So, we haven't spoken to each other in eight months.' Coral looked over at Joey, then just shook his head. 'I think you should talk to him.' Coral said. 'Clear the air, so to speak. It's better than to let things fester and grow.' Joey thought that sounded familiar, and realized with growing concern that this was how his situation with Syphon Fission all started. He stood up quickly. 'I'll meet you at your apartment, then, Avy.' He started to walk away, then paused and looked back at Coral, who was still seated where he was. 'It's time that I met an old friend.'
