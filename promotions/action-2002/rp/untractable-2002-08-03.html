Action! — Tales

Untractable.

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He could barely see... he could barely walk... and he could barely move. ‘The Law’ did not know where he was... or what he was doing just prior to. No clue. LLB pulled a shaky hand over his face as he began to wipe the blood from his head downwards and into his lips. He could taste it. Something wasn’t right. He tried to open his eyes... he tried to look around... but they seemed to take forever to ply apart from each others. Where in God’s name was he? And what in God’s name was he doing just before? Trying to think, despite the incredible power that it took to do so, the lawyer could only trail his mind back to the square-circle. The Action! squared-circle. ... He saw Reed Young. He saw the KroW. And then he remembered what had taken place... A Match. LLB & Reed Young vs. KroW. For a shot, at a shot... to LLB's Gold. But everything went straight to black after KroW kicked him in the gut. ... For LLB knew nothing else after that. He finally opened his eyes, realizing his was laying in his own locker room area. Alone. He tried to turn his neck to the window that sat high and close to the roof. He caught a glimpse. Dark. It was late. What had happened? Surely he was not able to pull out the verdict... but did Reed Young do it for him? Did Reed have his plan in full force? He have to’ve. He promised his best friend that he would. And even though Reed was a horrible liar... Roland couldn’t help but deep down inside have belief for the Owner sometimes. LLB attempted a weak, scared smirk. Hoping... praying... that Young was able to get out the victory. To take home the verdict. NO WAY... was ‘The Law’ going to fight a loser, a sin, an error like KroW on Pay-Per-View. He battled him once a long time before... and he won. Case closed. ... Longing to get up, and taking an entire ten minutes to finally do so... stiff neck and all, the lawyer marched himself around the room, blood still dripping down his face. Nothing. Nothing was there. Just his black duffle bag. And nothing... was all the sounds that he heard. LLB bent down, while he back cracked wickedly. He grabbed his watch. It was way past midnight. A confused lawyer threw it down... and walked across the way... To the door. He opened it up... A note lay on the floor. Slowly but surely he took it and started to read it out loud... “Dear Roland.” He began as his jaw popped. It was Young’s writing... that was for sure. Spelling mistakes all over. “The plan worked!” LLB tried to smiled... but just read on. “However...” His eyes shot open. “Well... um... KroW was too strong for it. He broke free... and he just rolled me up. AND THAT BIAS REFEREE COUNTED!!” ‘The Law’ couldn’t even respond. “AND KROW WON! I am SO sorry, Rowe. I AM SO SORRY! PLEASE DON’T HURT ME! The Ref... he was laughing after... so I hit him... and then I fucking broke his neck. That’s what I’m not here, Rowe, I went to the hospital.” For a second... LLB wondered why in the hell Reed would go to the hospital with him. “Look... I promise you... I’ll make it up... I PROMISE you... This coming week... I’m throwing you a tribute. I fucking love you man... I am so sorry. Bye, Reed.” Tossing the paper to the floor in a ball, LLB looked up and latched his hands onto the door. SLAM!
