Comma, The Second Part of

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/26/02 Other Part: Comma, The First Part of Date: March the 26th. 10:00 PM EST. **Ding** “Excuse me please. It’s my exit.” LLB pushed his way past a couple and an older lady, filing out of the elevator as he looked at the wall ahead. “I want to go... that way.” And taking off in that direction, LLB reluctantly walked forward. He scratched his head... then putting his hands in his pockets... only to take them out a second after. No surprise he was nervous... but it was a little much for him. Passing by a few doors and making two right turns, he then came upon a rather larger one, opening it up. “Brace Yourself...” “Hi, my I have your name?” the Young lady said sitting behind the desk a few feet away. “Yeah, it’s Roland.” “Nice name. We’ll be with you shortly.” “Uh... thank you.” LLB quickly took a seat across the way. He didn’t take compliments too well. Hell, why did he always get that from people in the first place? It was always the number one thing they’d say, and he didn’t name himself... his Parents did. Mind you he liked his name; it was different... but still. Why wouldn’t anyone bother to say “My, your teeth are very white. You brush well.”? Or “Good job cutting your finger nails... they are nice and short.”? Ha, laugh... go ahead, but no one speaks on those types of things, and there are people that don’t shower, brush, let alone keep themselves’ clean. People only compliment you on the things you have no control over... or so it seemed. Everyone in these testifies’ judge too wrongly. It’s how you look, not who you are. It’s the tone of your voice... not the content of it... And it makes LLB sick. “You’re not a fucking pussy, are you?” He said to himself... over and over inside his head, replying the word: “No”. Taking a magazine that sat in front of him he opened it up, but only read one page, then placing it back on the table. “Yeah, well... tomorrow at this time it’ll all be over and done with.” Of course, only if he’d survive...
