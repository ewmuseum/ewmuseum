Love Geometric - XIV

7 November 2002

Date: 11/07/2002 October 24th, 2002 Thursday Night Keri Lindum sat in Kent Anthason's hotel room, her eyes closed and her head tilted backwards. She quietly wondered to herself exactly why she was here. She didn't want to be anything more than friends with Kent Anthason, and yet, she dressed in one of her more expensive dresses. A silk black number with a skirt just above the knees. Not even her own mind could determine what had driven her impulses. The only present source of light in the room was a lamp that was directly next to her, which highlighted her facial features. Her long, curly, blonde hair. Her bluish green eyes. Her smooth face. Keri looked at Kent Anthason, who was laying on a bed and thinking in his own right. She interrupted his thoughts, 'Kent, we have to talk.' 'I know. You said that five minutes ago,' Anthason said, 'So, talk.' She thought for a moment. 'Kent, I can't do this.' 'Can't do what?' asked Kent, rising up into a sitting position, before positioning himself so he'd face Keri. 'I can't just be with you. Like this. I'm a married woman, after all. What would Joey say if he found out that you were with me? Er, well, he'd probably just thrash you and stuff.' 'What are you saying?' Kent innocently asked, not especially taking her hint too well. He leaned forward toward her. 'I'm through. This is over, Kent. It's all over. I'm going home.' she said. She quickly stood up and attempted to leave, but Anthason sprang up to his feet and got in front of her, grabbing her by her shoulders. 'Aw, no. No no. I don't think you get it. I'm in love with you, Keri,' Anthason said. Keri's eyes went wide, she couldn't believe it, but Anthason continued, 'Okay? I said it. I spend every waking moment thinking about you and thinking about how to protect you.' 'But...' Keri tried, but Anthason continued. 'Joey couldn't even do that properly, you know. He'd always slip up,' Kent said, 'Like with Daze, or with Fission. I can do the things he can't do. I can be what you need me to be, if you'd just let go.' The expression of Keri's face was a mixture of confusion, shock, and just a little bit of surprise. It was Kent's designated job to make sure that the surprise turned to more surprise, and with that, he did what would usually result in a slap to the face under normal circumstances. He kissed her. He kissed her long and he kissed her passionately. When he was finished, he let go of her, somewhat happy to get this off of his chest. Her face was contorted by shock, surprise, and just a bit of smeared lipstick. Then, in what had to be the third most highly improbable event on Earth, right behind the Red Sox winning the World Series and the Buffalo Bills winning the Super Bowl, Keri did something that not even Kent expected. She returned the kiss. They embraced, stumbled around awkwardly, and fell onto the bed. She ended up on to, and continued the liplock. Anthason unconsciously began to unzip the back of her dress, which she didn't even acknowledge, because she was too busy taking his shirt off. A few moments later, Keri's expensive dress laid crumbled on the bed, and Kent's shirt was, with some difficulty, taken off as well. A quick reversal roll ended with the heel of Keri's sandal crashing into the single light source in the room, breaking it. All was black, the only sounds emanating from Kent Anthason's hotel room, that night, were the sounds of sex. Lucas Devol would have a hell of a time sleeping that night.
