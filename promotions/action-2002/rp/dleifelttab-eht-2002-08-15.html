Action! — Tales

dleifelttaB ehT

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Every human being lives in a place called reality. Reality is a multitude of things. It could be your 2.5 kids, your house, your wife, and your job. For others, it’s a severely diluted alcoholic beverage. Or that belt you wrap around your left bicep, followed by an injection of heroin. Everyone thinks they are different. All our lives are the same. We all go for a simple goal. Fame, money, happiness, world conquest, etc. The paths we take are different, yet the same. You don’t understand? No life is more unique then the other. That’s like saying two blue M&M’s are different from each other. Obviously, they are not. They both taste the same, right? They both look the same, right? Human beings are the same way. One isn’t different then the other when you get right down to it. You just think that because it gives you a warm and cuddly feeling that you are in control of your life. In any second, your entire world can come crashing down. And you just hope to god it isn’t you today. It might be tomorrow. It might be next week. But it’s coming. You know your home, name, and life. Unless you are oblivious to these simple things, then you understand. It’s all just one big act. People like KroW just shill you because they think they have it so rough. Wow. Arrogance is a great thing…isn’t it KroW? Without me, you’re shit. Bottom line. Name off all your victims. Big deal. You deserve to die. And you deserve it for a reason. You’re too fucking stupid to live. THIS IS MY BATTLEFIELD RUAHH~!!!!!!!!!!!!!!11111111111111111111111111LOLZ~!!!!!!!!!!!1111111111 Bullshit. You’re waste of money, rags, and toilet paper. Or are you too hardcore for toilet paper? The term warrior is kayfabe bullshit. The are no more warriors. There won’t be. They are all gone. Stop considering yourself a notch above everyone else. You’re nothing more then a joke compared to me. Tried and true. I would control you. I would crush you. I would mind fuck you. And I would kill you. Simple? You best hope to God and Satan we never cross paths, you little bitch. There is only room for the real deal. And you, the fake, can go home and listen to your death metal and kill yourself. Thanks for playing. -Excerpt from: Fission, Syphon Date: 6/24/02
