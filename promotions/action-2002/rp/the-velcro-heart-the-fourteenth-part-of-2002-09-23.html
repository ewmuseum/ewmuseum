The Velcro Heart, The Fourteenth Part of

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LLB took a seat, clutching his cup of coffee in his right arm as he slid his donut across the table to where he was going to sit. Bill followed, doing the same... except the plate basically stuck to his hand as he did. A few people around the store looked over, seeing a clean young man in ‘The Law’ and across the table a dirty old janitor in Bill. But Bill didn’t care. He was used to it by now... and in truth, it actually seemed odd when people treated him normal. People such as LLB. The Champion grinned before taking a bite out of his chocolate raised donut. “I thought you had enough of those already?” Bill said, pointing to what LLB was eating. ‘The Law’ didn’t catch on at first... until he looked down. “Oh. The chocolate.” Chocolate donut. Chocolate Fountain Match. Just for those that were slow tonight... like ‘The Law’. LLB laughed. “It went right over my head.” Bill didn’t respond, though. He just ate his donut and finished it off by guzzling his coffee. There were some moments of silence that followed... but it was nothing that crept either of them out. It was a long day in both of their lives... a normal and recurring theme. LLB sighed, finishing off his own coffee. “How long have you been wrestling for?” Asked Bill, as LLB could only shrug in a quick reply. “I really don’t know. But my professional career started in PIW... March of 2000 I believe. I stayed there until last year at this time, when it basically folded.” Bill nodded, cutting into what the lawyer was saying. “Yeah. I remember that Silver fellow... you two didn’t get along well.” ‘The Law’ agreed. “Yeah. We didn’t... but God knows where he is now.” LLB stood up, throwing out both empty coffee cups. “Then I joined jOlt... and after that went out of fashion... I took to the Asylum and Action! Wrestling. I wasn’t going to sign with Reed, I really wasn’t... but then he gave me an offer I couldn’t refuse at that time.” Bill didn’t seem to understand. “What’s that?” LLB could only make a reference to his Belt, as he pretending to strap it around his waist. “Let’s just say, Bill... it’s something I’d rather regret.”
