Love Geometric - IX

31 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/31/2002 October 24th, 2002 Thursday Night Allison Lindum and Coral Avalon burst into the Malone residence. Not because there was a situation to deal with that needed the bursting to set the stage, but mainly because they were in the middle of a passionate kiss and didn't have time to knock or really do anything relating to the common etiquette of letting the people inside the house know what the hell was going on. Fortunately for Ali and Coral, nobody happened to be in the house when they burst in. They ended up awkwardly slamming themselves into a wall in their kissage, but it wasn't enough to make them stop. They did stop stumbling around so they could catch their breath a bit, but it was brief because they got right back to the kissing. They exchanged positions on the wall a few times, neither of them really bothering to stay in the new one they found themselves in for very long. Eventually, their last attempt at exchanging positions along the wall ended up going down the hall where Coral's bedroom was. They ended up slamming themselves into the door that seperated Avalon's room from the rest of the hall, and after a bit of blind searching, Allison found the doorknob and opened the door. Immediately afterwards, Coral and Allison came into the room, still engaged in their excessively long liplock. Coral blindly kicked the door and shut it behind him, and the two blindly stumbled around in Coral's room before they tripped over an incredibly large something and fell onto the soft corner of Coral's room called his bed. This made them let go of each other and awkwardly fall next to each other. They took this moment to rest. Coral glanced over at his twenty-year old girlfriend, whose hair was sprawled out everywhere on his bed, and whose black dress was currently trying to figure out how to return to its original position. Meanwhile, Allison looked back at him, mainly at his eyes. This went on for a few seconds before both of them laughed to each other. Finally, after a pause, Coral spoke. 'I've never felt this way before,' he started. 'It's... it's kind of a buzzing feeling in my stomach. Not specifically butterflies, but... I dunno, something that buzzes. It's... you know, not something out of nervousness. I... I guess it's a good feeling to have. It's... I dunno...' Allison smiled at him. 'Your point being?' 'I love you, Ali,' Coral said with clairty, 'I love everything about you. Your hair, your eyes, the way you dress, the way you make fun of me, the odd little smile thing you do, the belly button piercing, everything. You... you make me feel like a better person just being around you. You know?' Allison thought about it, before she answered with a smile, 'You have no idea how corny you sound right now.' Coral blinked, opened his mouth for a response, but found nothing. Allison giggled and kissed him again. 'It's what I love about you.' she said, before she got up on top of him and began to unbutton his shirt. Before the night was over, Coral and Allison would finally take their relationship to the next level. They were vastly unaware that on the other side of town, another relationship was being taken to the next level.
