Dieing? WTF!

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/09/02 He jogged on the spot. He jumped up and down. He rolled his neck from left to right. He itched his- Okay, Reed Young stood before the gate and curtain. He stood before his deadly press conference. “Those fuckhead’s in the media.” He muttered, smacking himself across the face, trying to get his mind ready for those stupid, pathetic questions each one of them would ask. “I’ll show em.” He licked his lips, as a small, midget lady, standing about knee height to Reed (okay, a little higher than that... just trying to make the story funny) walked over to him. “You’re wanted out there, sir.” She said, her pudgy face bouncing up and down while she talked. Young patted her on the head like a little boy or fluffy kitten, as he charged towards the curtain. “Fucking asshole.” The midget lady replied as she snarled towards the Owner. Young leaped on stage, as the entire array of media began to flash their cameras and started to speak. “I’m here... MOTHER FUCKER!” He screamed, as Reed jumped into his seat and rested his boots on top of the table. “Yes, you sir.” And pointing to a rather ugly, ugly women, she began to question Young. “Reed, is it true there are some backstage problems right n-” He didn’t even let her finish. “Backstage problems? Yeah... between me and your Mom! Oh... LOL! LOL! BURN BITCH! I burned your ass!” All were dumbfounded. “LOL, sir?” She wondered, not accustomed to that dammed internet slang. He rubbed his temples and actually calmed down for a reply. “Dear, there are no problems at all. Everything between me and my hand have been cleaned up with some nice vas- shit; did I say that out loud?” Hoping he did not, Young quickly pointed to another person. “Word has it.” A young man stood up in the back row. “That you’ve signed some big stars. Care to comment?” “Well...” Young went on. “I do plan to contact ‘Stone Rock’ Steven Jericho from WWEWCWECW2K1 Fed... but other than that, wtf you talking about?” “WTF, sir?” Stupid people. GET WITH THE TIMES! It’s the internet age! Okay... back to the story... “Yes, Reed, is it true Action! Wrestling is slowly dieing inside?” The best Owner EVER, answered that with ease. “Dynginzde? What the hell is Action! dynginzde?” “Dynginzde, sir?” Those people... I tell you... If you haven’t caught on to one of these pathetic jokes, go follow Mega Job, or something... Back to the story... “Action! isn’t dieing. We just got a whole new slutting of a roster, bitch! Let alone my internet problems are fixed-” “Internet problems, sir? What does that have to do with anything?” “It doesn’t.” Young shrugged, as the man grew a small, faint smile across his face. Why? Well... maybe he thought he made a fool out of Reed Young. But the Owner of Action! Wrestling... was never a fool? “ARG!” He snapped, realizing that yes, the man *did* make fun of him. Jumping over the table and slamming into the reporter with an elbow drop, countless people tried to ply the Owner away from the man... And everyone else, could only hang their heads in shame.
