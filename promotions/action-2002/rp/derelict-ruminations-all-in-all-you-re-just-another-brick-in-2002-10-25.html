derelict ruminations: all in all, you're just another brick in the wall

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/25/2002 confidence is something innate. those who have confidence don’t need to say they have confidence. they show they have confidence. i have no confidence. only faux apathy. i need your acceptance. i push you away, in hopes you’ll try even harder to get close. i can lie to you till my face is blue. but how can i lie to myself? its irrational, it’s immature, it’s self-defeating, it’s impossible, it’s ridiculous, it’s detrimental... yet i persist. I slept well last night. The park bench served as a surprisingly good bed. It was just the way I like it. Just the way I want to be in my new life. Hard and unforgiving. My spine abhors, but my soul requires this treatment. I must be battered, to remind myself I still exist. you don’t understand. I enjoyed 8, dreamless, hours of sleep, and when I awoke, I got to thinking. What am I in Action Wrestling? My intensions on coming to A! were to develop the run or the mill “badass” attitudes that’s worked so well for others. I’m to apathetic and lethargic to create my own original identity. I figured I’d just rip off Rave Caprino, or Villam Ender. You know who those 2 are don’t you? Sure you do. Everyone in the wrestling community does. People love, or hate them. Just which emotion is irrelevant. The fact that they elicit such passion is important. That’s what makes money. So I was all set to be the next Rave, but sadly, even after unprotected sex with 10 Haitian prostitutes, not wiping the needle after shooting up with a large circle of strangers, and shaking Erik Blake’s hand, my blood still tested negative for HIV. :-( I doubt that would’ve mattered. I probably would’ve beaten the virus anyway. I’m a devote believer in home remedies. Fuck AZT and chemotherapy. Bowl of chicken soup and a good night’s rest is all I need. I’m just way to lazy to adopt his attitude. It might not look like it, but hating everybody takes a lot of energy, I’m just not willing to invest. Anyways... After I aborted that plan I figured I’d rip off Villam Ender. I was real big on this idea. I figured I could pull it off easy. We’re both black, we’re both psychos, we’re both violent... we both have dead lovers... And I could see myself sodomizing another man. Sure I could. Besides, it’s only gay if you’re on the receiving end...or you can’t keep a secret. Whatever. So I sat down with a straightedge razor held to my penis, and I decided maybe this wasn’t right for me. Remember that movie where that guy lost his hand, and the hand went nutz and started killing people? Trust me. You don’t want my cock out there on the loose. No hymen on the continental United States would be safe. I did all your virgin daughters an enormous favor. Then I looked to emulate other superstars, Graphic Violence, Kenny Rock, Neo, Steve Douglas, Archangel, Carnage, blah, blah, blah. I just don’t have the drive to put any energy into being them. i wanted fans to like me. to cheer for me. i want people to respect me. i want to be loved I easily could have pulled it off if I wanted to though. What? Are you upset that I trivialize your heros/anti-heros? ...You are? Good. I am not deep. I am not philosophical. I am not profound in thought. I am not poetic. I am charismatic as a stillborn. I am not intimidating. My misery is no better or worst then your own. My existential anguish is no more important than yours. My thoughts and words carry no more value and meaning then your own. My being is no more then your being. In other words: me, them, you, are completely and undeniably equal. Equal in that we all exist in the same nothingness. The only difference between us is that I am aware of that quality in myself. I accept, and adore all this absurdity. Others constantly search for meaning. You can’t find, what’s not there in the first place.
