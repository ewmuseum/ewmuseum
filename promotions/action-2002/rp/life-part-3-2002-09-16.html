Life - Part 3

16 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/16/02 Life. It's an on going fight. No matter what second, what minute, what hour of life you are living in...it is always a fight. You fight to survive and be the best. You never give up. Because if you give up, than you aren't worth being on this earth and wasting all the good air. If you give up, go ahead and die already, so we can all get on with our lives, as we sneer at your grave and spit on your tombstone. I don't want that happening to me...so I didn't give up... All I did... Was kill the old me... I watched as the picture of myself lay against the wooden wall, with the knife deep within the center of my forehead (in the picture). No, I didn't end my living self, I just ended the self I no longer needed. The side that was slowly eating away at my soul, and making me into the one thing I've fought against since I can remember. Weaklings. I refuse to be remembered as the one who threw in the towel, who gave up when his world came crashing down. I WILL be remembered for being the one who would never give up, who would prevail over all...no matter what. I am not a weakling, nor have I ever been. I have seen what weakness does to me, and I will NOT allow it to overcome my body. I will NOT allow people like 'The Law', or people like Jonathan Conspiracy to mock me any longer. I have watched too long, watching as LLB and Reed Young continue to mock me in their league of Action! Wrestling. I have watched too long, hearing Jonathan Conspiracy constantly say he ended my career. Oh, how ignorance can be blissfull. What a surprise they have coming... I look at myself in the mirror, and no longer do I see the weakling I once saw. No. Now I see what I really am. A reality check hit me hard, and almost knocked me out of the game...but it didn't. And it helped me in the process. It helped me grow as a fighter, not a warrior. And it helped realize I was fighting in a ring...not a battlefield. Yes, I was living a fucking fantasy. Yes, I was pathetic. Yes, I was in a fairy tale. But no longer. Those days are dead to me now. The old KroW died on those faithfull days. When I heard both LLB and Jonathan Conspiracy yell into my face, A FUCKING FANTASY. I watched my blood spill to the mat, I watched my tears fall from my face...and I watched the reaction from the millions around the world. All I saw was shock... That was my reality check. A fucking fantasy was exactly what I was living. And I had to put an end to it, without ending my life. And I did. That picture in front of me with the knife in the center....that isn't just a normal picture...it represents something. It's a picture of myself from back in the GCW, where I began...where I believed I was a warrior in a battlefield. And that knife...represented the death of what I once was... No longer am I pathetic. No longer am I weak. I am strong. I am powerful. And a strong and powerful man...never lives anything down. I will never forgive... and I will never forget... And now the only thing left in my vision... Is Red. Red Vision. Of Anger, Rage, Revenge, Vengeance... And of Death.
