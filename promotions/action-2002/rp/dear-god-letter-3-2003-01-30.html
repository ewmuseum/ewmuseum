Action! — Tales

Dear God. Letter 3.

30 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/30/03 Yo yo yo, God, wassup? I thought I'd give you a day or two off. You know, so you could sort through everyone else's garbage problems and then work your way back around to fixing mine. I guess it's a pretty big task since I've been waiting for some sort of change for a while now. A change will happen won't it? God? Anyway God, I guess I should talk about something else for once, seems mighty greedy to just want you to change my silly little problems, right? So I thought I was going to get some work none too long ago. You know, maybe get a program and start my rise to the top of Action(~)! Alas, no luck. Reed couldn't sell the idea to anyone, it seems that no one is willing to work with a man of my talent. Their loss I suppose, after all, I have you in my corner to make me a king among men, who can beat that? Right God? So I went to talk to Russa, but he was asleep. Strangest thing, he was actually stroking his goatee as he sleep. Kinda freaked me out, you know? So I went from there and did my daily routine, which was the four s'. Of course you know what those are so there's no need to share, right? You know, I'm glad I wrote this. You seem to really listen to what I have to say, everyone else is just in one ear and out the other. Really frustrating, but not you... you listen to what I have to say. You got my back. Like I've got yours. We're, like, hommies, God. Like Biggie and Puffy. Suge Knight and Tupac. Simon and Garfunkel. The Captain and Tennille. We're there for each other, ain't it great? I have to be going now, I can fell one of my many tasks coming on. Or 'out' in this case. Later God, 'tose.
