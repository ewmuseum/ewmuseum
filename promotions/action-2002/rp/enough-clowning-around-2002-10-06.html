Enough Clowning Around ..

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: oct. 6 .02 Alone. Engulfed by the eternal flame, my scars slowly healed. I felt the pain at first, then it went by rather smooth. Deja vu, for sure. The time was at hand. Make-up slopped onto my face, how amusing. I felt as if I was a child at the time waiting for maturity. Looking back at life, all you wanted to do was grow up. Now that you're there ... you want to go back? Right, not in this case. I wanted to grow more! To hell with being younger.          The state of mind was at its peak. It felt as if the transformation was creating someone ... completely different. Fresh blood coasted along the lining of the veins which entwined their way through my domain. Feeling the inevitable changes, there was no need for a test. Intense thoughts crept about tempting me to devastate someone or something. Still, I held back the force. I sat there alone.. Just thinking. I'm a normal human being now ... I'm allowed to think.          Enough with the face paint, enough with the lies, enough with life period, in that waxwork called the Joker. It's amusing how human beings can be so blind. My brothers all warned me ... they all told me that there was no need in being a freak. I felt tempted, as if that's what they wanted me to do. Pushing me over the edge slowly ... Then, it just happened.          I outlined my face in the crimson shade. Dreading what would happen, I left it alone. I pictured myself where I am right now ... Not any more. You live, you learn. That's all there is left to say.          I rubbed the remains of the black off of my face. The difference was already felt. Staring at the smeared rubble on my hand, I began to laugh. After a few chuckles, it was all over. I got all of the laughing out of me. No more CLOWNING around. You can have fun being a freak like I was. You can have fun being made fun of for how you dress, look, act, and live. Go for it. There is a better life. There is a life where you can be yourself and not care what people think of you. That's what I'm finding. The key to salvation lies within. You think hard enough and for a long enough time, the door will eventually open up to satisfaction.          Shove me away for now. Hate me while you can, pretty soon I will be owning you. Pretty soon I will seek the salvation. There is nothing better than fulfilling a promise and that's where I'm on my way. This promise will make me ... or break me. If it's reached, say goodbye to your heroes. A new one just rode into town.
