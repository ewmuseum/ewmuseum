Bobby's Inferno Book One

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I hear it in my sleep. The crowd. The slamming sound of a body hitting the ring and the supports shaking. I hear the despicible booing and the respectable cheering. I hear the marks on their feet and the fans in their seats. I hear it all. I love that, I really do. Thats not the source of my grief. Thats not the heart of my anguish. The pain exists in my bosses. My coworkers. Jerks and assholes. Liars and conmen. I think about Christopher Fox, even with the innoncence of a child he is still tainted and corrupted. I think about Sean Cylear, so driven by his ego that he faked his own death when he thought that people would view him as a weakling. I think about LLB, so involved with his gimmick that he doesn't care what side of the line he fights for. Does he even know? Its not about motives or causes anymore, not about good and bad, its about if you're being cheered or booed. I think about KroW, a man so vile, a murderer. If I didn't admire him I would have killed him by now, in his sleep. But he is the better man, and I hate him for that. I think about his mind games, yes, they still scar my life even a year later. I think about how much the fans love him and what he does to Reed Young and Cylear, its confusing. He's done so much, so much bad, and people still cheer for him. If Manson and Dahmer were in a boxing ring would either of them get cheered? I think so. Whoever had a popular haircut. Its disgusting. It makes me want to walk down the ramp, with something classic playing, Ludwig Van or maybe just Van Morrison, put a pistol to my chin and let the world be beautiful. I hate to be so melodramatic but, thats my job. Beyond those obvious cases I think about Angel Dawson. Her sex symbolism, I wonder if anyone knows just how talented she really is? I'm afraid that we'll both die alone and unadmired. Its horrible. Atleast we spent our time together, we dove together, face first into the fire, and we came out at the other end alone. I'll burn before I play the crowd game. I'll burn to a crisp before I swallow my pride and my morals for a pop.
