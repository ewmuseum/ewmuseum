Novella of a Precious Shamrock - Prologue

20 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16th January 2003 Hello everyone! I am Siobhan Tyler, an 18 year old female from Cork in Ireland. Many complicated things have happened in life, but I've never forgotten my roots. If I was to die tomorrow the autopsy would show the blood in my veins to be green, white and orange. My family tree is complicated to say the least, but I won't confuse you by giving explicit details. Basically, Lee and David Tyler are my older brothers, 23 and 25 respectively. Our past is rough to say the least, and no doubt stormy times are brewing in the oceans once more, but for now I just want to be there for them, because lord knows I've made some a few mistakes in the past. We have the same parents, as you would expect for brothers and sisters! However, where as they were born in London England, I was born in Ireland. By the time I arrived into the vast world, they had sadly split up, and so I didn't get to live around my brothers much. My Mum (Jane) lived in Cork, my Dad (Joseph) lived in London. Once every few months we'd all meet up, and I spoke to Lee over the phone all the time. In some ways Lee was my best friend, because we would tell each other everything. As for David, well, we've never seen eye to eye and we probably never will. I think he has problems feeling true emotion, especially after what has happened over the past 5 years. But I won't go into that now: some things are better left unsaid. And then there is my dear cousin, Benjamin Tyler, or as the fans on this website will know him, Airborn Tyler. I'll tell the truth, I'm concerned about the way he portrays himself on screen. He has it in his head that to be popular he needs to keep up this charade of being an ugly, cocky, cheating, canniving and insane teenager who doesn't know how to like anyone or anything. That's not the Benjamin I know. Ben's parents died in a car crash when he was younger. He hasn't told anyone in Action Wrestling this, and I don't blame him, because it was awful for him. They were returning home from a New Year's Eve party when he was only 6 years old, and everyone was cheerful because they'd just welcomed in the year 1990. Someone forgot to tell an oncoming car that it's wrong to drink and drive, and the collision was head on. Ben's parents didn't stand a chance bless them, and they were killed instantly. Even if he was sitting in the back, it's a minor miracle that Ben came out with his life, but he came out with barely a scratch on his body. I guess those were early signs of Ben's resistance, and I still think that tales of how he survived this tragic accident convinced him he is immortal. Anyway, the Ben I know isn't the Ben you all know. On screen he is evil, and I don't like the niche he's created for himself. Yes, you all my love to jeer him, and love to see him get beat, and I guess from that sense he's been successful because his whole job is to be booed. But you love to cheer him when he's standing on the top of a balcony or ladder don't you? You love him when he's putting his body on the line. Ben's a caring person. You know that we used to go out together? We'd walk around hand in hand, canoodling like teenage couples do. No matter what the world threw at us, we had each other. Ever since the car accident he'd lived with us, and we just became really close. We grew up to each other, and we loved each other. I don't know if we still do or not, only time will tell. Whenever I got hurt he would always be there to heal the wound, whenever I was upset his shoulder would be there to cry on. And he was good looking. Have you seen the picture of him on this website? It is gross! He's gone out of his way to look ugly and revolting just to make you people hate him just an ounce more! I guess that's typical of him, because if you give him a job to do he will throw his all at it. Whether he admits it or not (and he won't, he thinks he's a cockney to the bone), that's the Irish in him. Us Irish never do anything half hearted, whether it's riding a horse, playing a violin or downing a Guiness. And don't you believe I can down an alcoholic beverage with the best of them! The first thing that people notice about me is my face, or so I've been told. Lee once told me that if he was ever to drive a car on the other side of the carriageway to me he would crash the car because of the sun gleaming off my teeth. They're white as milk, and I never stop smiling. Why hate life? It could all end tomorrow, and who wants to be having regrets on their death bed? Men may notice my ginger/red hair, or my small legs (and they are small, I'm only 5'4'!), but I'd like to think it's my caring nature that stands out the most. I'm only in Action Wrestling to re-establish a relationship with my brothers, and to try and make Benjamin a more caring person on screen. What I do intend to bring is a bit of fun, even if I have to do my brothers accounts to get the opportunity! The crew at Action Online will eventually add some chronicles detailing my life, and I hope you feel you understand me a bit better after them. The only thing left to address is my relationship with Donell Arcade. No, it's not just a show for the cameras, an opportunity to show my flirty side, I really am dating him. He's the sweetest bloke, who just needs to let his hair down a bit more on occassion. I don't know if I love him or not yet, as love is a strong word. But if my brothers can let it slide, we can have a lot of fun together! I have no problem with them beating Donell up in the ring, as it's their job. Just make sure that he comes back in one piece! I have no idea who is going to win the Dyad Championship match between The Elements and Arcade & Son on Monday night, and to be honest, I don't care! Three people that I care dearly about are in that match, and I just hope they all come back healthy. Perhaps I'll come to ringside to make sure everyone is alright! Take care everyone! ~Siobhan Tyler The Precious Shamrock
