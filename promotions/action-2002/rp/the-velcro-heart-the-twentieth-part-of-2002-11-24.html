The Velcro Heart, The Twentieth Part of

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LLB entered the ring, handing Pat Hoskins his Champion Title as he went to his corner and waited on Steven Denton to begin the Match. Being the last one to come down was pretty cool to ‘The Law’. It made you seem important... and indeed, if you did come down last, you were. Reed Young always had a rule. Challengers first, Champions last... and that was regardless if it was a Title Match or not. LLB nodded inside his head. Even the village idiot had the odd, good idea. KroW rested in his corner, as Denton locked together with Fox in a grapple. The Fans went wild as Denton tossed Christopher into the ropes... but on the return, Chris ducked a clothesline and smacked Steven in the side of the head with a right hand. Denton fell back, into the ropes as Fox paced forward... taking him and hurling him into the next corner. LLB sighed. “C’mon Denton! Get up!” But as Fox hit a roundhouse kick, he knew that Steven wasn’t going to. LLB never liked to tag-up with people. It meant he wasn’t in control. And regardless if Steven Denton *was* a ‘Risk’ member or not... LLB didn’t have full jurisdiction in his Courtroom with a partner. By now, Fox had total control. He had taken Steven down with a scoop slam and a kick to the back. Christopher dragged Denton to his feet... and that’s when LLB tried to get Denton’s attention with his arms. “Hey! Steve! Low blow him!” Denton looked up, seeing LLB’s hand gesture as he did... Thus, a low blow was hit. LLB grinned. “At least he isn’t slow like my old partner.” ‘The Law’ made reference to Christopher Fox; they were former PIW Tag Team Champions as well. And with both men down... Denton tried to get to LLB’s extended palm... while Fox did the same to KroW’s. Smack: Fox to KroW. Smack: Denton to LLB. Then it started. The something... no one wanted to know. LLB nailed KroW with a left hand, as KroW returned the favor with his right. Back and forth it went... until LLB went of the ropes and was caught with a clothesline. He pounded the mat in anger. Not the revenge... the retaliation that he wanted to seek. KroW walked in, but as LLB looked across the way, seeing Denton whom was now making reference with his hands for a low blow too... LLB did it. “Smart-ass.” He said, snarling in the direction of Steve. LLB didn’t want to admit that he needed the aid of someone else to get KroW down... but there, he did. Both men tried to get up... as KroW looked at LLB, diving for his leg. ‘The Law’ jumped it. Thus beginning to exchange lefts and rights on the mat again. The Champion threw a hard left hand, one that would always hook at the last possible second. Opponents hated it... as they never wanted to battle the lawyer in a shot-for-shot contest. And though he only stands at five foot eleven, and a mere two hundred and ten or so pounds, LLB’s left fist was as deadly as they ever did come. He rose first, going off the ropes and splashing KroW on the ground. KroW twitched for a second... but then started to recover. He was near the other side of the scale compared to the Action! Champion; no one would fight him for his sheer insanity at taking whatever you gave him... and getting up from it. Standing tall, KroW, was doing just that. Left hook LLB. Down went KroW. ‘The Law’ marched himself into the corner, as he quickly lifted up to the second rope. He looked at Denton. The soon-to-be Bantam Champion Challenger to Joey Malone’s Title motioned for an elbow drop. He was right once... He was to be right... again? LLB jumped. Nothing. And as LLB rolled on the mat in pain, he looked over at Denton... who shrugged. “Sorry Roland.” His mouth motioned. “I didn’t know he was going to do that.” The enraged lawyer muttered under his breath too... but was cut off by an elbow to the back of the head. Tag: KroW to Fox. The Fans roared as LLB could hear the bouncing of Christopher Elliot entering the ring. “Not this... fucking Christ, not this...” He rolled on the mat, barely out of that tricky little Fox’s grasp, whom had bent down to get him. Not taking much time to think... LLB rolled into KroW’s corner, got up, and walloped him across the face. KroW fell off the apron. He was drilled, and drilled hard. Maybe the hardest to date. LLB looked down at his hand... blood started to pour from the knuckles. That’s when KroW entered the ring. The Referee tried to stop him... but that was clearly NOT going to happen. KroW almost went through the Ref’s body, as he caught LLB with a clothesline from hell that spun him right around... as LLB practically landed on his head. Just a spilt-second less in the air, and he would have. Cue: Steven Denton... Who was fought off by Christopher Fox in a spilt second. LLB rose, getting smacked across the head again. This one sent him over the top rope and onto the floor below. KroW followed. The two began exchanging lefts and rights on the ramp... as the Referee watched on, only to signal for the bell soon after. A double count-out. The Fans were not pleased. But as LLB and KroW started their battle behind the curtains, they were truly not pleased... And the two, would suffer greatly from it...
