Toiletry Terror [Part 1 of 6]

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 14 A rather huge man and a rather scrawny man sat on the couch watching their television. Each wearing unusual costumes as they gazed upon their idiot box. The obviously larger controls the remote as they surf. Their blank and fried commercial television faces just peer into the beyond of their television set. Well, they would look like that but they’re both wearing a mask and underpants as they watch the tube. RING! RING! The skinny man’s concentration is broken with the first tone of the ring. He turns to the larger man, taps him on the knee. “Hey, John, it’s the Action! Line. You’d best pick it up because when the telephone rings it means somebody is POOP trying to get in contact with you. Therefore answering it would greatly increase your chances of speaking with them at this very moment. Sunday, June 25, 1876, was the day of the Battle of the Little POOP Big Horn, or Custer's Last Stand, if you will. Far away, in Philadelphia, it was also the day when Bell demonstrated his new invention at the Centennial Exhibition. Alexander Graham Bell was a gifted pianist POOP and did you know the first person to be prone to his genius was his mother, who was deaf. POOPSo he put his lips on her forehead to talk and resonate the sound of voice through the bones of her skull an-“ “Cap…” “Uh-huh?” “…shut-up.” “POOP OK.” RING! RING! The Action! Line rang loudly for the fifth time. The larger man turns to look at the brightly flashing red telephone. It flashes as it rings. RING! RING! “Hello?” Voice: “Is this, uh, the John?” “Yes ma’am, the John is on the other end of this telephone. How can the John be of service to you?” Voice: “Well, uh, I know this sounds strange, but…er…my…erm…toilet is on the roof and we think it’s going to jump!?!” [gasp] He gasps. The skinny man on the couch turns to him, he knows there’s some trouble in paradise. The John doesn’t gasp like that for no reason at all. “Oh my God, a code 4! Address lady! Give me it now, this is critical!” Voice: “Um...er…eh…it’s [---censored---], how long will you be?” “No time for idle chit-chat, citizen. There’s toilet to save!” Slamming the phone back down in its cradle, the large man, the John as he calls himself, stands. Places his handles powerfully on his hips and looks out all starry eyed. “Cap, when duty calls the John attends his beloved lavatory in its time of need. And that time is now. Now, as we speak, a water closet is in dire straits and it needs our help. The John says we should get into uniform, hit the Action! Mobile and come forth to protect this innocent flusher before it’s peril is nigh.” “Huh?” “Let’s roll!”
