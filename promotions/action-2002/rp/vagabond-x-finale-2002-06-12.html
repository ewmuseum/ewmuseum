Vagabond - X (finale)

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/12/2002 Previous Parts: 1, 2, 3, 4a, 4b, 5, 6, 7a, 7b, 8a, 8b, 9a, 9b 'J-Joey, are you s-sure you want me to do th-this?' Coral nervously asked as Allison drove her Grand Prix into the parking lot of the Superdome. Coral was a tad nervous, jittery, and excited after Joey finished going over their plan for tonight's events. 'I, I mean, if this were anyone el-else... I'd be all for it. But this is SYPHON FISSION here! That... that guy's like frickin' Angel! He's a broody, depressed guy when he's good, and when he's all, like... e-evil and stuff, he's killing vil-villagers and being generally the baddest hombre this side of the Rio Gr-' Coral stuttered, before he realized that Joey had no idea what he's talking about. He calmed down a bit. 'You haven't a clue on what I'm talking about, do you?' 'Well, no.' Joey said. Allison turned to Coral. 'Oh, yes! Angel. Well, to translate for our unfortunate person-who-knows-not-what-Coral-is-saying, Angel's a vampire cursed with a soul. Incredibly cute and dreamy in a broody, wears-black kind of way.' Coral looked at Allison rather oddly, though she didn't pick up on it. 'Basically, he lives with the guilt of the atrocities that he committed. He can only ever become evil again when he attains true happiness.' Coral nodded, and turned back to Joey, who sat in the backseat of the vehicle. 'I think Rashard's generally the same way. Except for perhaps the whole 'true happiness equals no soul' thing. He was nice, if only a bit shy and brooding. Then he changed into this monster that he's become.' 'I get the gist of what you're saying, but you've never even met him.' Joey retorted. 'That's why you have an advantage. He doesn't recognize you as a friend of mine, so you can just go in and take it.' 'Won't he come looking for it?' Allison asked, as they entered the parking lot of the building. 'That's what I'm banking on. You'll hand it to me, I'll take care of the rest. Just...' Joey began, sighed, and tilted his head backwards until he looked at the celing of the car. '...don't follow the show. Stay in New Orleans, you'll be safer here, and I won't have to worry about you guys getting hurt. I know I seem like I'm using you guys to help me, but believe me, I'll make it up to you.' Coral turned around and looked at Joey, sporting a grin. 'I already owe you, Joey. So I'll just consider us to be even after tonight.' Joey nodded, before they shook hands, knowing full well that this would probably be the last time they saw each other for a few months. 'Good.'
