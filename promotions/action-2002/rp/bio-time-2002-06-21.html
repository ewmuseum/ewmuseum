Date: June 21, 2002 Title: Bio Time Date: June 21, 2002 Time: 6: 23 p.m. EST Place: Orlando, FL | OPEN | Ah, the bright sun, the nice weather, and some great beaches. Orlando, Florida, U.S.A. Its amazing how things can change so much in such a short time. I don't believe I've talked on television since I lost my A Championship. But thats okay anyways, I had nothing to say. But yes, quite a lot has changed in such a short time. First there is of course the genoius owner of this fine company, Reed Young. The man's a genious. XHL?! Nobody has ever came up with anything like that. And I know for a fact that Mr. Young will make sure the XHL lasts much, much longer than the XFL. Then again if the XHL lasts 15 weeks it would have lasted longer than the XFL....but Mr. Young is still a genious. In fact, I have it on very good authority that he is planning to launch the XHL right now and thats why he hasn't been around lately. Then there is of course LLB and Christopher Fox. Honestly, you two make me sick!! Your friends, then you hate each other, your friends, then you hate each other! I mean come on, you two are worse than Dawson's Creek!! And what makes it even worse...a Merry Me Match for the A Championship!! What a waste of time. A Marry Me Match on a pay-per-view. Wow. If you really want ratings throw me in the ring and I'll put on a clinic for whoever has the guts to try me....which takes me to my next topic. Bobby Minio. Who the hell do you think you are. Me and you, we've known each other for just about as long as I've know LLB and Fox. We've had our battles in the past. Do you remember those battles.......BIO!!!!! Haha who about a little trip down memory lane Minio? Way back when I first hit the 'big time' in PIW, you were a newcomer there too. And while I was Chris Fear then, you were the 'great' 'Bio' Bobby Minio!! Hell, I've got to hand it to you. You must have guts to go on TV with a name like that! But Bobby, you want to attack me from behind. You want to cost me matches. You know me better than that. You know I will do absolutely anything to get back at you. I've burned people alive before. I've ran in on peoples matches and thrown them off ladders when their back was turned. I've attacked people with everything from crowbars, to sledgehammers, to blowtorches. There's a reason they call me 'Killer', Bobby. And you will find that out. You have to learn the hard way, because thats how I've chosen to teach you this lesson. So 'Bio' its time for 'Killer' to teach you a lesson you'll never forget. And its time for you to finally know the true meaning of that phrase I use oh so much. 'Bio' you will, and this is a promise, you will........FEAR THE FEAR!!!!! | CLOSE |
