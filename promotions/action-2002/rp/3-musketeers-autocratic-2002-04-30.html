3 Musketeers: Autocratic.

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/30/02 Some call me a loser Some call me a cheater Some say I'm a selfish untalented dreamer Cause all's I try to do is keep my dream alive But it's so hard to do when your working 9 to 5 I've never been one afraid to die But I'm afraid to leave behind The precious life that grows through the sands of time Cause for my son and daughter I know they need their father To help them make it through all of the devious things that people do Cause every moment counts from the good times to the bad I don't have time to envy those that got things I've never had Cause the one thing most important is the one we take for granted And until your life is on that line I think it's the way god had planned it To open up your eyes and make you realize That to some life is money But what's money without life Cause all's I need is the air that I breathe And my friends and family to believe in me. ‘The Law’ rose to the sound of his buzzer going off, hitting it lightly on its head and then tossing it to the floor. He got up slowly, cracking his arms and legs forward, trying to stretch out the night time rust. LLB pulled himself out of his bed. He wanted more sleep, but he had a big day ahead of him already. He walked into his bathroom, turning on the tap and soaking his face in a puddle of water throughout his hands. “Wake up.” He said to himself, eyes still glazed over a little. “Big day.” He left the washroom and continued down his stairs, finding a sheet of paper on the kitchen table reading: “Things to do: - Take the 10:00 AM flight to the Fleet Center tonight and meet with Jeremy Bold about your new wrestling tights. - Press Conference about your sore groin. - Dinner with Christopher Fox and Chris Fear.” He tossed the paper in the trash. “Ugh… each time I see Bold I think I get stupider and stupider by the second.” LLB began to search for some pots and pans throughout his cupboards. “Breakfast. Then I’ll go and do my routes.” He took out a pan and turned on his stove. Next he reached into the fridge and brought out two eggs. Smacking them onto the table he broke the shells and poured the contents into the pan. He began to cook. --An Hour Later.-- LLB pulled back the door from his house and closed it shut, jamming the key in the lock and turning the handle of it. LLB took the key back out and stepped backwards, getting a good look at his fairly large quarters. “The home of the law.” He said. “The best place there is.” And turning around to face his Chevy Blazer LLB unlocked his driver door and entered. “To the Arena.” He backed out quickly, putting the car into drive and sprinting off down the road. A few residences whom sat outside in lawn chairs watched the Blazer speed by. The Wife looked towards her Husband. “The lawyer’s in a hurry again.” She said. The Husband just rolled his eyes. “Who isn’t these days?” --Twenty Minutes Later.--Airport.-- “Now boarding flight 401 to Boston.” LLB paced quickly down the halls passing by people left and right, trying to make his way to the plane in which had been called. “Out of my way. Can you walk any slower? Jesus… my Grandmother walks faster, and she honestly has no legs. No really… she doesn’t. Assholes. All of you are guilty.” --Ten Minutes Later.--Plane Ride.-- “That is like totally cool. I’ve never met a Professional Wrestler before.” ‘Law’ sat on the plane, not that happy. A child, aged ten or so sat beside him- well… he rather fidgeted beside him, talking non stop. “Well kid, if it wasn’t for Reed Young and his ‘find your own way to the Arenas so I can save money’ you’d have still not met a Professional Wrestler.” The kid didn’t catch on. “Oh… neat. I am so lucky. Thank you Reed Young.” “Ugh.” “So… LLB, do you like to do… um, stuff?” LLB humoured the kid. “Oh, sure. I love stuff. Sometimes I get a whole group together and do… stuff.” Poor ‘Period’. The child once again did not catch on. Though he was a kid after all. “Neat! Maybe one day you can do stuff with me!” “Oh, that would be swell mate.” ‘The Law’ rolled his eyes. “Now Timmy, don’t bother the nice Wrestler too much.” His Mother said leaning over. “I’m not Ma. We are talking.” “I think you better listen to your Mother, kid.” --One Hour Later.--Bus.-- “I can’t believe that boy.” LLB said to himself taking a seat on the back of a bus. “He didn’t shut up for one minute. ‘What does LLB mean?’ ‘Lawyer degree.’ ‘Why do you wrestle?’ ‘Uhh... because I like to.’ ‘Oh neat.’ ‘Yeah... sure is. Sure is. Brother, it sure is.’ Idiots.” --Three Minutes Later.--Fleet Center.-- LLB zoomed up the stairs and into the Fleet Center wanting to get his little meeting over and done with as fast as possible. It was a given his attitude had changed since the moment he woke up. Other times he’d enjoy his day, but not these... the people he saw daily, the people he saw weekly, the people he saw once in a life time... they were all becoming too much to handle. He wanted out in a sense, but of course that wouldn’t happen. He paced into Bold’s Office seeing the VP sit up from his paper filled desk, head on hands. ‘The Law’ rolled his eyes. It was no surprise how disorganized the team was. Let alone how lazy they were. “Hey Rolly.” “Roland.” LLB corrected. “It’s Roland. Or LLB in your case.” “Sure Rolly.” Bold went on in his annoying ways. “So, here’s what I thought.” “Yep.” LLB relaxed in the edge of his chair. “Okay. Well... we need more ratings to compete with 21w. So... I thought: tight change. You like Bret Hart right Rolly?” “Like him? My style is based after him.” Bold smiled. “Good. What about this: pink tights! Eh... eh?” LLB stood. “Umm... right Jeremy. Good try. You’d kill my heat if you did that.” ‘The Law’ walked out of the Office. “That was a fucking waste of time.” He said to himself looking down at his “Things to do” sheet. “Press Conference... bah. What am I going to tell them? Media. They’re worse than pink tights and kids put together, I swear.” --Ten Minutes Later.--Fleet Center Press Conference.-- “Just answer whatever you like, Rowe.” Pat Hoskins explained as he covered up the microphone in front of him with one hand. “K?” “Umm... yes.” “Alright people. If we can just go one at a time please. Yes. You sir. Over there.” LLB rolled his eyes and watched on as a small black haired man wearing glasses stood up in the back row and spoke. “Roland, you’ve been suffering from groin pains, correct? How will this affect you in the long run?” “Well. It really won’t. I’m not going to take time off now. We’re one month away from ‘Juggernaut’. You don’t know how hard I’ve worked to play a major role in a Pay-Per-View. It’ll really be my first Major Event to be honest. And I’m not going to take time off.” Rubbing his neck ‘Law’ waited for the next question. “Yes, Roland, will this affect you in the ring over the next while?” “Sure. Any injury will. But I’ll take it easy for now. I won’t be wrestling much until the PPV. And that’s in the Asylum and Action. I’ll be on the shows, still creating my heat. But I shouldn’t wrestle too much.” “Did your Match with Fission last week make the situation worse?” “Na. Fiss and I talked about the fight before hand. It was basically just a few clotheslines and choke slams. Rather weak showing again on my part. But I’ll save up for ‘Juggernaut’.” “Will you be taking time off after ‘Juggernaut’?” “This all depends. Reed and I have to sit down and discuss a number of things regarding the Pay-Per-View and what comes after for myself. If I see he doesn’t need me for a month -which is impossible by the looks of it- I’ll take time off. If he needs me, I’ll be here. Is that all? Okay. Thank you guys.” --Later on During the Afternoon.--Bus Ride.-- He breathed heavy. It was a long day again. Bold ate him up... and though the Media wasn’t half bad, they still did. It killed ‘The Law’ inside that right now, wrestling wise, he was somewhat at a disadvantage. And all before the Main Show... The show he worked three years to get at. Not too many people could have said that. Most of LLB’s past had left. All but two. “Shit. I hope I’m not late.” ‘Law’ said to himself looking down at his Rolex. “Good. Got time.” Fox and Fear. Since day one they were together in PIW. And here they were together in Action. It was a weird feeling. You look around and ask outsiders, others, how their wrestling lives went and you’ll only meet a few that actually have a few old friends/ enemies left in the game. Most people quit... But the trio didn’t. And they wouldn’t. Maybe it was one man drove the other... and it went around in circles. Or maybe it was luck. Hell, bad luck. It didn’t matter too much. The three stood together... throughout it all. LLB pulled the handle to stop the bus. He exited. “Thank you.” He said passing the driver. And though he was a prick in the ring, ‘The Law’ couldn’t help but remember to be kind to people... sometimes. Well, as long as they obeyed the law. He walked into TGI Friday’s. “Hi. Umm... reservation. Table for three. It should be under: Fox-Ermann.” The waitress giggled looking into LLB’s deep brown seemingly attractive eyes. “Right this way. The other two aren’t here yet, but if you’d like a drink to pass the time, I’ll go get you one.” LLB took a seat and glanced up. “Sure. Three Scotch’s on the rocks.” She squinted. “Long day.”
