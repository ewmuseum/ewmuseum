The Alpha: The End Of Innocence: 2

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/15/02 Into the short future…at PP7 This is it. The night of. The whole night has seemingly geared towards one match. My match. Joey Malone is probably telling Keri it is okay. I know he is. Keri doesn’t know how serious this is. We are not pulling punches. This is something beyond mere combat. It is our element. No matter what Joey thinks or wants to believe, we were meant to do this. The day we were born, this was our lot in life. Fight the other. We are so alike… Yet different. Joey commented on me that I had grown up a lot since Mesa High. We became the best of friends. We were both cool guys in the backstage arena of the IWO. I stayed over in Phoenix to help Joey rehab his knee, and Joey was the first in my corner when I needed back up. Then, he changed. He wanted more from me after Potright beat me. He wanted his shot at the title I was slated to get a rematch with. When Jamie Kosoy handed down the decision to push me down to mid-card status, after everything I did for him and his damn company, I felt alienated. When he told me Malone was to take my place… That’s when I knew he had merely used my friendship to elevate his career. And he kept piling his stuff on top of me, to make himself look better then I. He was bitter because he didn’t retire a legend. He was a bastard, and tried to ruin my career by telling Kosoy that he wouldn’t defend against me, even though it was in a friendly manner. He ruined Anthason, because of the ending to LDE II. The ending wasn’t supposed to be fixated on him. It was supposed to be on Anthason. Kent still feels betrayed like I do by him. He wanted us out of his life and tried to get people who would smile as he climbed over them. Joey left Anthason for dead. He left my student for dead. That was the reason I followed him into Legends Of Wrestling, but I didn’t have a chance to fight him before it shut down. I tried to follow him into jOlt…but their staff did not like problems I presented. Reed Young wasn’t going to have Syphon Fission unless he said a couple other words. Joey Malone has joined. I am avenging Kent. I am avenging myself. I am avenging Keri, for she doesn’t know the truth. I am avenging my sanity, because I have stretch the line of sanity so thin that I might explode. Joey Malone thinking of me as a mountain. I think of him as the system, stopping others from freedom. It is my job to crush that system. In a couple hours Joey… I will run you through. For your own good.
