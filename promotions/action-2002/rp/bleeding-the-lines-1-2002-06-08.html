Action! — Tales

Bleeding The Lines 1

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He is starving me. I am tired. I can not sleep. I can not wake. I can not escape. This is Hell. My own home. Doesn’t make sense. The pictures are not around. Fission burned them all. Memories of good times. Not around. Fission burned them all. My wedding gown. I sowed it myself. Took forever. Fission burned it. Fission doesn’t care. Joey isn’t a hero. Joey is villain. Fission is anti-hero. Joey never wanted to save me. Why hasn’t he come back? Why hasn’t he come back? Oh father, why hasn’t he come back? I close my eyes. This is mere dream. Nightmare. I open eyes. The same. FUCKING THE SAME! Kenny Rock? His bangs look like Kenny Rock. His face looks like Rock. It’s Fission. I see the scar. Fission is depriving me of my very thoughts. I try to cry, but my tears are dry. I am dehydrated. Fission hasn’t fed me. I am dying. I am losing blood and losing sanity. His eyes are always there. Piercing me. He is always there, killing my soul. I tried to escape a couple of days ago. Fission was sleeping. He faked. Fission never sleeps. Look at eyes. Bloodshot. He is gone. Nobody home. Noticed this before. Now understand. My thoughts are mumbled jargon. Because those eyes haunt me. That smile separates me. He has conquered me. He is going to conquer Joey. Joey isn’t a warrior. Joey is a wrestler. Fission is ender. Fission is warrior. Fission is unbeatable. I am going crazy. What is Fission doing with a syringe? He is taking my blood. I am too weak to fight. I do scream. Scream why. My tears come down as I try to stop the blood. Fission smiles and walks away. But those eyes are still upon me. And I am still dying. Joey… Save me.
