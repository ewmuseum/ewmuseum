'Welcome to Action! Wrestling...' (1)

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'...Hello! Welcome to 'Action!' Wrestling.' A large black boot, and then another, stepped feirecly down on the tiled floor of Action! Wrestling. A band of men circled repeadivly around the center of the hallway, exchanging jokes' and passing faint smirks back and forth. The door lugged forward, hitting hard against the wall behind it. A tall physically boasted man entered with a faint grin across his face. One hand in his pocket, and the other holding his jet black bag, which was dangling in the air. He proggressed toward the 3; 4 men which were currently 'ON - HOURS'. 'Welcome to 'Action! Wrestling'. How are you? Good, thats nice. May I see your permit?' One of the larger security guards stepped forward, ahead of all the others. A large, fake, smile on his face, as his eyes glimmered in the ray of sunlight, from above. 'Sir? Do you have a tag of somesort that I can see...?' 'This is one of those jokes isin't it. Ha!... Very funny, no-' Disciple X was cut off, with no intension the large man stepped forward once again. 'No, I'm sorry. You'll have to leave the building right away sir...' 'This is a joke... this is a joke... this has to be a joke. Listen, I've had to ride the hard way in Action! already, this is just plain stupid. I'm Action! Wrestling superstar, Disciple X. Remember, the guy who kicked Mor-...' '...Oh, It's you! Your the guy who got his ass kicked by my man Morg! Ha! He reall-' 'Yes, okay. Can you please just, move!' 'No, I can't. Like I said, I need to see some sort of ID card before I let anyone pass. This apply's for ANY circumstance. So please Disciple, back away.' The security guard backed away, leaving Disciple X 3 feet behind him, as he countinued to ramble on with his co-workers. 'This is completley upserd, I'm taking this up with Mr. Young when he get's back!' Disciple X said with a stern voice, staring straight ahead at the men. 'Trust me boys, he'll be back sooner then you could ever imagine!' The same guard glances up at DX with a smirk mellowing on his face. He gave out a unusual chuckle, and came within a couple inches of Disciple X, as he breath tringled around his face. 'Is that so Mr. X?' The profound guard who'm we know as Joe, due to his golden name-tag questioned. 'How would you know such a thing, when you JUST became a active superstar on the roster? Huh!?' '...You make me sick! ---' Disciple X takes a quick guard at Joe's name tag, and then countinues to look face to face with him. '...Joe!'. '...And you my friend, you make me vomet. ...Now get out of my face boy'! I don't want to have to slap you around or anything. ...Little boy's like you running around Action! Wrestling backstage hallways. GEEEZE!.' Joe's sqeualed ended his comment, as Disicple X backed away and implied his heavy body on a concrete bench. 'Action! Wrestling.. damn, it's a whole lot to get used to'... His words echoed through the building, as the scene slowly faded to black. It's just getting harder and harder. Despite the glory.
