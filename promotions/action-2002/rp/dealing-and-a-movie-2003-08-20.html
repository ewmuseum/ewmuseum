Dealing and a Movie.

20 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was relatively warm outside on the Wednesday morning. But not too warm. Overcast slightly, though the sun would shine through from time to time. Outside, in their backyard, the Doxs had completed their garden. After much explaining at the Pressure Point tapings, Xander still was unable to understand what Rook Black was talking about. In fact, Rook had to get a pen and paper and right it all down for him. Luckily, even without the paper, Jessica knew exactly what was to be done. And it was. Right down to the last detail. They had all sorts of vegetables growing in their backyard, by the line of trees just behind the back porch of the home. Xander had been worn out from his work on Monday, but he had gotten in an early morning frozen soak in the tub and was good to go in helping his wife set up the garden. In fact, he did most of the work, allowing Jessica just to sit back, make sure he was doing things properly, but most importantly think about what had happened Monday. She had been told, by Bradley Duncan, that he loved her. To say she was wigged out from that claim would be an understatement. She was positively wigged. Wigged like no one had ever been wigged before. But she had been given time. Xander didn't push. He didn't prod. He just used the garden - though it hurt his back a great deal - to occupy his mind. When it was all finished, Jessica had fixed up some Kool-Aid. Cherry, to be exact. Xander was still a big kid at heart. Hell, they both were. For people who were about to celebrate their twenty-sixth and twenty-fourth birthdays respectively, as well as their third year anniversary, they weren't quite into adulthood yet. Which was kind of funny. They were mature, very mature for people their ages, but yet, still... well, they were dorks. After all, they had asked Rook Black for gardening tips. If that didn't make you a dork, nothing did. But as Xander sat back against one of the trees, in the cool shade, Jessica handed him a glass of Kool-Aid, which he took a small sip from. She sat down at his side and leaned her body into his chest. He wrapped his left arm around in front of her stomach and kissed the top of her head. 'You want to catch a movie tonight or something?' he asked her. She shrugged, 'Doesn't matter to me. What's playing?' 'Well... American Wedding is out. How about that?' She looked up at him, and smiled, 'You just want to see it because of Alyson, don't you?' Xander looked away, trying to play coy, 'Of course not. I'm sure it'll be a lovely film.' 'If I remember correctly, you could only take the first two movies in spells.' There was some shrugging from Xander. Jessica kissed him on the chin, 'It's alright. You can have your little crushes.' He looked at here, wide-eyed, 'Really? 'Cause, um, Elisha Cuthbert... wow!' Jessica sat up, moving away form her husband, so that they were face to face. 'I was kidding, babe.' Xander smiled and kissed his wife on the forehead. 'I know. But just you remember, you do me wrong, and there's two women I'm ready and willing to try and form a relationship with.' He winked drawing a small smack on the arm from Jessica. And it was her, then, that asked, 'So, do we want to go see this? 'Cause, it doesn't matter to me.' Xander shrugged, 'Yeah, sure. Gonna have to take another soak again, before we leave. Otherwise, the back will stiffen during the movie and I won't be able to leave.' Jessica frowned. Max stroked her hair, 'Oh, don't be like that, Jess. Yeah, okay, my back is very much messed up, but I can deal. It's not getting worse... or it doesn't feel worse, so it's nothing to worry about.' She still ddin't like it. She wanted him to see a doctor again, only this time with her, but he refused. She wouldn't push it though. She then sat down indian-style beside her husband as he took a drink of his Kool-Aid. He then said, 'Speaking of dealing. Have you thought anymore about the Bradley situation?' She just shrugged her shouldes, sighed, and looked off into the distance. Nothing but grass and tress for a long while, before you could see the neighoring house in the distance. She then looked back at her husband. 'It's weird, ya know? Him liking me. It's odd, and I haven't really come to terms with it yet.' 'He doesn't like you, Jess. He loves you.' 'Yeah, yeah, he says that but I don't know if I believe it or not. I mean, how can he love me? It's not possible.' 'I love you.' 'Well... yeah, but, Bradley? No. I just don't see how it's possible.' Xander sighed, 'Jess, have you ever looked at yourself in the mirror?' She looked up at him as he continued, 'I mean, just on a physical level, you're very attractive. I know, I know. You don't believe it, but it's true. And couple that with just how you are. Who you are. You're personality. Well... let's just say I'm the luckiest man in the world.' She blushed slightly at this. Max pulled the hair from her face and continued. 'So, it's not surprising in the least that he has developed strong feelings for you. I mean, we were all pretty close. Feelings are going to form. Whether that's love or not, I dunno. It's up to him. But it is something we're going to have to deal with. Something we'll do together. 'Cause, anyone that loves you, well, is sort of my business. What with being the husband and all.' She smiled for a moment and then asked, 'What should I do?' Xander could only shrugged. He really wasn't sure. Smacking Duncan had crossed his mind once or twice, but that wasn't going to be a valuable solution to the problem. She sighed, 'I just don't know what to do. I mean, I like Bradley. Or, I did like him. He's a little...' 'Strange?' 'Yeah. He's been very different lately. I mean, we've all seen that. But now, saying he loves me. I don't know. Just weirds me out. I'm not used to the whole 'guys loving me' thing. Heck, or guys just being attracted to me. It's all foreign and I don't like it.' She pouted a bit. Xander scooted over and pulled her head into his chest. 'The curse of the beautiful, my dear.' She rose up again. 'So... should I just tell him, flat out, that I don't share his feelings?' Xander replied, 'Again, I shrug.' And he shrugged. Funny how that worked. 'I dunno. But it's something that should be taken care of immediately, so that he doesn't get too attached, or get the impression that you love him or not.' A thought, then came to Jessica. She studied her husband for a moment. Just staring into his green eyes(the darkness of the shade had made them green) and then she asked, 'How come you haven't asked if I was in love with Bradley?' 'I didn't have to. I know you don't.' 'Oh,' she said. Then followed up, 'How did you?' He shrugged again, 'I just did.' She only nodded. And then she decided. Yes, just like that, she had decided what she wanted to do. 'I'm going to tell him. Tell him the truth next week. I figured it's safer to do it with you and a bunch of people in close proximity.' Xander only nodded. 'So, next week.' She took a deep breath. Xander repeated, 'Next week.' And then he started to get up. He grabbed Jessica's hands and pulled her up as well. She began to dust him off as he asked, 'Seven o'clock showing or ten?' 'Ten.' Xander nodded. And together, hand in hand, holding glasses of Kool-Aid in their free hands, they started back towards the house. Leaving their newly planted garden behind, ready to grow.
