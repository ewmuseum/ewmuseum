Troubles in the Air

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 2, 2002 It is a large plane. The American Airlines logo can be seen throughout the interior. Passengers sit calmly in their seats as the pilot announces they are preparing to take off. Row after row is filled. From up front all you can see is the tops of peoples heads, and occasionally a face or two that stands out from the rest. Sitting in the aisle seat of about the eleventh or twelfth row, one of the faces stands out. This particular face looks like it has been attacked by a rabid pitbull. This man has a black eye, has not shaven recently, his face is stained with blood, and stitches are in more than one place, holding the battered flesh together. It is clear that this man has taken a terrible beating at the hands of some person ... or something. Upon taking a closer look, you can barely make out that this man is Chris Fear. The man who gained fame and fortune as one of the longest serving members of the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling never to hold a title. A man that seemingly complains more than he actually wrestles, even though professional wrestling is is chosen profession. For some odd reason he continuously brings up the past, for some reason not wanting to focus on the present, and surely not the future. Slowly you can see the man painfully turn his head towards the woman beside. She is a very attractive young woman with long blonde hair pulled up in a pony tail. Chris painfully extends his hand to her, 'Hi. Do you know me?' With a look of surprise on her face the woman shakes her head. 'Well you should!' Fear screams. 'I am the A Champion! That's right the Action Champion. First and only! I get more television time than Homer Simpson! What's the matter with these people! The man who owns Action is quite possibly the smartest man in the world! Who else but Reed Young can pull off ratings of 0.3 and 0.5 almost every week! The man is a genius!' 'O....K...' the woman says slowly, not having any clue what The crazy guy beside her is talking about. That crazy guy beside her, may be just that: crazy. Quite honestly, he endured more punishment on Sunday night at Resentment, than most people endure in their entire lives. There is no doubt that Christopher Fox beat Fear from pilar to post. But in the end, none of that matters. What matters is that Chris Fear did kick out of the Foxoorama. What matters is that Chris Fear did kick out of Fox's Frog Splash. And what matters, is that Chris Fear did retain the A Championship, all by himself. 'Listen lady. Don't blame instand replay. A few years ago instant replay proved that the Bills lost to the Titans on an unbelievable play by the Titans. You know the one. The big pass across the field that had all the controversy! Well, instant replay showed Sunday night that I had my foot on the ropes! Yes...I know, people make mistakes. Luckily every arena in the world has instant replay booths in them now thanks to Reed Young!' The plane hurries down the runway and begins its liftoff. After a few moments Fear begins grabbing his ears and complaining that they are popping. Obviously, this is normal on a flight. Perhaps the A Champion was just too cocky to think about something so small. Fear honestly believes he won that match on his own. To the rest of the world, LLB helped him. To Fear, he just say Fox lying in the ring and made the pin. Chris yells at the woman beside him once again, 'I'm the A Champion, damn it! I deserve a better plane than this!! I shouldn't have to sit beside somebody like you!!' Ah, yes indeed, the Chris Fear we love to hate has returned!
