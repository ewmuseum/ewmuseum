Mother Knows Best

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/02 The turkey was still a few hours away from being ready. Everyone was awake. The house wasn't large, but they had managed to squeeze everyone in. Conrad Ramsey and Bradley Duncan used their own beds, while Samantha used her son's(and daughter in-law's). In the living room, everyone else slept the night away. Blast on the couch, Iceman reclined in the recliner, and Mo Ron lay sideways in a chair, a not so big chair. Gus actually slept underneath the kitchen table. No one knew why. No one really cared. On the living room floor, Max Danger and Jessica, slept. A coffee table between them. It wasn't until in the wee hours of the morn that she decided it wasn't best to sleep at the bottom of the couch... not with Blast up there. So she moved. Next to her husband. She slid into her sleeping bag and stared at Max. Just stared, as if she hadn't seem him in years. After long minutes of that, she turned, her back to him, and fell asleep. When morning came around, Conrad and Samantha were the first two up. They had toast and orange juice, while they waited for the others to awaken. Jessica and Bradley were the next two up. Jessica and Samantha went to work on the turkey. It was a big one, too. Twenty-six pounds. Iceman woke up, followed by Mo Ron. Gus crawled out from underneath the table, and went outside to use the restroom. He hadn't gotten used to the whole pissing in a the house thing. Blast got, falling to the floor. Jessica had been wise to move. The last to rise out of his slumber, was 'The Danger Man'. He hadn't known his wife slept at his side all night. When he stood up, he saw all the bags around the house, full of Christmas decors. Plus a huge box containing the Christmas Tree. He just shook his head as he made his way out of the living room. 'Good morning, son.' called his mother, in the most gentle of voices. He hugged her and kissed her on the check, before heading into the bathroom. Jessica was already in there. 'Sorry,' he said, turning his back and facing the other way. 'It's alright. I'm finished.' she said, putting down her comb. She walked out, looking at her husband, and putting her hair in a ponytail. Max then entered and shut the door behind him. In the hallway, Jessica was stopped by Samantha. 'Is he alright?' she asked. No one had the heart to tell her that the 'happy couple' wasn't so happy. But she could tell, especially with Blast directing comments towards Jessica. Jessica just shrugged her shoulders, but her expression told Samantha everything. She didn't press the issue though. They two ladies just went back to preparing the big Thanksgiving dinner. In the living room, Blast, Iceman, and Mo Ron were sitting on the couch. In the recliner was Conrad Ramsey. He had tried to offer his assistance with the meal, but the ladies had shunned him. He left reluctantly and sat with the others. Bradley Duncan sat in the chair that Mo Ron had slept in. All were watching television. Buffy The Vampire Slayer. 14 Hours. FX. Should be a holiday tradition. But the man, who was the biggest Buffy fan, Max, went straight out the front door after he left the bathroom. Gus was out there, just wondering around. 'What are you doing?' asked Danger. 'Oh, nothing. Just looking around. You have the perfect house for Christmas lights and decorations on the outside here.' he said, looking all around the house. 'Really?' Max replied, walking off the porch and into the front lawn beside Gus, looking at the roof. He explained how Santa and Reindeer could be placed across the roof, and various other things. He was really just like Clark Griswold. Having him around could prove useful. 'I can do that for you.' he said. 'I would love to do it. I used to do it all the time for my kids, as did my father for me and my sister.' There was a moment of silence there. Gus just reminiscing his past. Danger ddin't want to interrupt that. He then turned to Max. 'You can do it.' Danger told him without even looking at him. 'You can stay here, as long as you want, and you can do it. This weekend. Everyone will be gone, you'll have all the time you want.' Gus was beaming. He couldn't believe it. 'I'll tell Mr. Harris, across the street there,' he pointed to the red house, 'that you will need a staple gun, and maybe a few other things. He was telling me the other day that he always put up decorations.' Gus was just happy as could be. Then Danger added, 'Just make sure you out-do him, okay?' With a smile to Gus, he turned and headed to his Celica. Gus just kept looking at the house, imagining everything he could do with it. He headed back inside, as Danger pulled a plastic bag out of the back. A Wal-Mart bag. He didn't go rent DVDs, he bought 'em. He didn't go back inside though. He sat on the steps of the porch. The cold concrete. But he didn't let it bother him. He had on an American Eagle pull-over hooded sweatshirt(gray) and ragged blue jeans from Old Navy. Was that wrong? Wearing two things from two different stores? Those were some of the things Max was thinking about. Back inside, people were laughing. Iceman and Blast were telling jokes. They laughed and laughed. How they could do such a thing while watching Buffy(Becoming, Part One, even!), is anyone's guess. The door came open. Danger didn't turn around, because he already knew who it was. His mother. She put her arm around her son and sat down beside him. 'Having fun out here?' she asked. It was a rhetorical question, of course, because she continued, 'I know everything, Marcus. Well, not everything. But I know you and Jessica aren't together at the moment.' Danger turned and faced his mother. Her gently, soothing eyes, still strong despite all she's suffered in life. 'I don't know what's going on, mom.' he told her, 'Everything is just turned upside down.' She nodded. She knew. 'She misses you.' That caught him off guard. That was the last thing he expected to hear. 'How do you know?' he wondered aloud. 'I can tell. I'm 54 years old, Marcus, I've seen these things. I've felt these things.' She smiled at her son. She rubbed his shaggy hair. 'It will be alright. Everything will work itself out. She still loves you. Don't ever forget that.' She kissed him on the forehead, and then headed back into the house. More laughter could be heard before the door was shut behind her. Danger sat there, staring out into space. His life was just a big mess. But taking himself away from it all wasn't going to help matters. Mother knew best. He stood up and entered his home. 'MAX!' Iceman and Blast shouted at the same time. Through the window, everyone standing around and laughing could be seen. The holidays were here, and no matter your differences, you put them aside. Tomorrow you could hate again, but not today. Today was the day for togetherness.
