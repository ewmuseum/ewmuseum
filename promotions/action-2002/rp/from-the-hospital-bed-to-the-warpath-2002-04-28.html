From the Hospital bed to the Warpath

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/28/02 Flashback. April twenty fourth. Two thousand and Two. Flashbulbs popped in unison as A baseball bat was brought across the back on Minio's head. The one man revolution lived to his surname as the arena spun around him and him only. He reeled in surprise to see the unkempt hair of KroW grinning down on him as dark red blood rolled down his forehead. One moment, he saw the Grin. Time slowed down around him. The 30 second encounter became a seven minute romp against his body. He was now in shock. His unconcious didn't even have enough sense to fight back. He had LLB alone in the ring, he was ready for his fight, then before he knew it his whole body seized up from a shot to the back. Before he knew it, he was staring upward. The lights. The grin. The law. The... eyelids? Darkness. Fast forward. Twenty Nine hours later. Eyelids. Light. Cameras? He awoke in a hospital bed. Cameramen were all around him. He was at first delusional. His grip on reality was lost about a day ago when he felt the cold slap of a baseball bat stroke its stiff fingers through his sweat ridden hair. He jumped from the bed, various tubes and needles hanging on for all of their unanimated life until each and everyone had fallen to the floor. Minio's fist came across the jaw of a med student who was staring wide eyed at Minio's awakening over a clipboard. The next punching bag was a camera man snapping pictures for a local wrestling magazine. He got a great motion capture of a knuckle bearing down on the lens. Minio hauled ass out of the room as he finally came to and knew what he had to do. Fast Forward. Three hours later. Minio is changing into a pair of jeans and an At the Drive-In tshirt in his 68' Mustang. He presses the button on the CD player. The song Ursa Minor by At the Drive-In begins the play. 'permission to land ... all systems go... propeller snapped, decapitated while you slept upon your pillow. inertia kisses those around me.' He speeds off along the road with a fury, ace bandages formed into a bandana over his wound. Fast Forward. Three days and Twenty Minutes later. Reed Young is sitting at his desk, with a dumbfounded look on his face. 'Answers Young! I WANT ANSWERS.' Minio barked orders across the pine vehicle of mass career destruction as Young did as he was expected to do. Bumble and stutter a response. 'I... er... What... I dont understand what you want from me Mr. Minio.' 'I want to know why KroW is here. Why did you authorize this... BULLSHIT?' 'You might want to talk to LLB about that Mr. Minio.' 'I would LOVE to have a chat with LLB but I can't get within three feet of the law without having my head being used as a wiffle ball!' 'I could arrange a meetin-' 'Young, you're a fucking welp. I can't turn my head without feeling like my brains are gonna spill because of this garbage and you're gonna give me false promises?' 'I wouldn't promise something that couldn't be taken care of Mr. Min-' The desk was already being flipped vertically against the wall. The barrier that seperated Reed and Bobby was now leaning on the wall, splitters spitting in every direction where the corner crossed the brick. 'I don't want your promises Young. I want all four of them. I want Fox. I want Fear. I want LLB. I want KroW. I want them in the ring. All of them. All four of those fucking cowards. Let them know Young. I can still walk. I can still swing. And goddamnit I can still kill them ALL from trying to Cripple me!' Young's face now showed an uneasiness as Minio sat mere inches from his nose. Minio grew frusterated just by being there and stomped out of the office. Fast Forward. Two hours. Current time. The view panned from bloody pressings against a brick wall. It spun to show Minio. His fists were crimson and raw. He had clearly lashed out on the sedimental enemy of the walls with his canned rage from his most recent concussion. 'KroW thinks he's some kind of fucking warrior. Some kind of great man of battle. What a crock of shit. Warriors are men like Overdose and Chris Mezz who dont give in to ANYONE'S bullshit. They don't give into ANYONE'S offers. I'm a Warrior! You're a wanna-be. Thats why you came from behind with a bat KroW. You're a wanna-be. When I attacked Fear. He saw me coming. For a LONG way. You... you had you're little vignettes... your little quotes... your little music and your little lighting parlor tricks. You're NOTHING KroW. NOTHING. Nothing but a fuckin Puppet. But no, not a puppet on strings. You got the Law's hand so far up your ass he's tickling your earlobes with a feather duster. I'll show you KroW, just how useless. . . How alone you are when your little buddies don't come to rescue you from the unmerciful onslaught you've just ordered! IT'S OVER KroW. It's over before its begun and you can't do a thing about it.' Minio slides down the hood, slamming his fist down on the side of the car, then with quickness he slid into the drivers seat, slammed the door, and sped off into the foreground.
