Party At The Hamptons: Party On

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Here Jamal; have a drink.' 'No thank you Romey!' 'Come on Jamal, one drink.' 'I pass.' 'OK.' Jonathan Conspiracy walked over to his stereo, and begin to blast 'Pass The Couessier' by Busta Rhymes featuring P. Diddy out of it. 'PASS THE COUESSIER! JUST PASS THE COUESSIER' 'If you say so.' Jonathan gave Jamal a glass of couessier. Jamal downs it with no problem. 'Taste better then semen don't it?' 'Huh, Romey???' 'Nothing. Here have another.' Jamal reaches out for another plastic cup full of couessier and pepsi. And down the hatch it went. 'I have to use the bathroom.' A tipsy Angelica Dawson made her exit, to relieve herself of the alcoholic beverages that entered her system. 'So Jamal, you want some Alize? Cristal? Smirnoff? Bacardi Limon?' 'I'll take 'em all, ERRRRPPPP!' Jonathan hands over a full bottle of 'Red Passion' Alize, and watches Jamal chug it down. Jonathan then gives him a 24 oz. bottle of Smirnoff. Another bottle gone, the contents deposited in the pit of Jamal's stomach. 'Hey Jamal, want some Tequila?' 'Yeah Romey, my niggy, hip-cup!' This went on for what seemed liked days, but only a few hours past. 'I have to use the bathroom.' Sweet Aroma knew where the bathroom was. 'Oh it's right up the steps to the ri..' 'Honey, its to the left.' 'You sure???' 'Yes Veronica, I'm sure.' Sweet Aroma knew where the bathroom was, but Jonathan didn't plan on sending Jamal to the bathroom. 'Jamal.' 'Yes Romey.' Jonathan cringed. 'Forget it. Listen I'm hitting the hot tub, Roni you coming?' 'Sure baby.' Jonathan held out his hand to help an alcohol induced Aroma up off the couch. 'Hey Jamal.' 'Yes Romey.' Jonathan cringed again, but he managed to regain his composure to tell Jamal what he had to say. 'If you want to crash, just head to the second door on the right.' 'Okay my niggy, homey, nahmeanuheard.' 'Yeah, whatever.' That was JCON's last reply as he and Aroma left for the hot tub, leaving Jamal downstairs all alone with another two bottles of Tequila. Once outside, Sweet Aroma had to get something off her mind that was lingering. 'Isn't the bathroom the fourth door on the right.' 'Yeah.' Sweet was ready to respond, but Jonathan's tongue entered her mouth, and the liquor took over.
