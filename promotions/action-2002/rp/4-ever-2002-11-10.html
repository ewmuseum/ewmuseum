4 Ever

10 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/11/02 OPEN “Pika Peek I so mad! I so, so, so mad that I could watch an XXX video and ice cream in my pants! I know that’s a no, no but I am just so mad!” Title: 4 Ever Date: 10/11/02 3:00 pm EST Place: Movie Theater One Fox. One Electric Mouse. Waited. Fox “You know, I told you to grab my ID before we left the hotel didn’t I Pikachu? Now we can’t see the new Pokemon Movie!” Ticket Clerk “Oh, I thought you said jackass, the movie! You can see Pokemon 4, here’s your ticket. Enjoy!” Fox grabbed the ticket and made his way inside the movie theater. Fox “Pft, Pikachu look at what we almost gotted into! I call you a jackass and we almost weren’t allowed to see the Pokemon 4 Ever Movie!” Quiet frankly I dunno why I brought you. You don’t even like Pokemon! The carpet was lightly scattered with popcorn remains and ticket stubs. Fox “Hm, just like home, eh Pikachu?” The gallant lad surfed his way over to the ‘confession stands’ as he calls them. ‘Kid’ popped Pikachu into his pocket in exchange for his Super Mario wallet. After waiting for a few minutes the joy-filled Christopher had his chance to order. Fox “Heto.” Sally “Hello, I’m Sally, what can I get you?” Fox “I’ll have a large popcorn, extra butter, hold the toppings and I’ll have a large coke-eh-da-cota pwease.... BIG TIME!” Sally “Boy you sure know what to order. I’ll be right back.” Ah fuck you bitch! Fox coughed up twenty dollars and hustled into the specific theatre that Pokemon 4 Ever was playing. Chris squeezed through the small door and picked himself a nice front row seat. While he journeyed to his seat a small boy and his mother wondered why an adult was doing here all alone? Boy “Mommy, why is that man alone? This movie is s’post ta be for kids like me.” Mother “I don’t know, but just keep quiet.” Fox “Ah, all se-attled in. Otay, you can come out Pikachu.” Spilling about a quarter of cola, Fox pulled Pikachu out. Fox “Now just time to relax and focus on something other than Action! Wrestling, but if the movie goes beyond 5, I’ll have to leave. I have an Action! meeting with Reed.... LOL! I’m just poking you Pika Peek.” The lights dimmed. The crowd hushed. Yet Fox didn’t. Fox “SHUT UP PEOPLE!” Like he needed to say that. Scene faded. Scene opened. Fox “Look Pikachu, it’s........... Pikachu!” The yellow companion jumped around on the big screen as the scene opened in the middle of the movie. Fox “C’mon Ash, you have to rescue Celebi! Make Pikachu use Thunder!” Ash “Pikachu:....” Fox “Thunder!” Ash “....” Fox “THUNDER!!!!!!!!!” Ash “Thunder-” Fox “THUNDER!!!” Ash “Shock!” The word “shock” echoed throughout the movie theater. Christopher stopped running up and down the aisles and took his seat. Oh thanks a lot Ash! You should have used Thunder, everyone, even I know that’s Pikachu’s best attack and his weakest is Thunder shock! Fox “NOW THE BAD GUY WILL DODGE IT!” The crowd booed at Fox.... and as predicted, the bad guy dodged it. Fox “I guess I’ll be quiet from now on.” On the screen, the bad guy kicked Pikachu off a cliff! Fox “WHAT THE FUCK MOTHER FUCKER!?!? FUCK THIS BAD GUY IS TOO DAMN STRONG HE’S IMMORTAL NO ONE CAN BEAT HIM!” Fox turned. Everyone booed, throwing some popcorn until Fox sat down. Once more the scene fades. Once more scene opens. This time at the end of the movie. The scene focused on Chris’ face for the remainder as all, but Ash’s voice could still be heard. Ash “Pikachu.... THUNDER!” The reflection turned Fox’s face a shade of yellow as electricity could be heard hitting someone. Ash “Pikachu we did it! Come here buddy!” A tear crumbled down the cheek of Christopher Fox as the lights came back on and everyone exited. Fox “Pikachu, that was sssooo magical! That was truly awesome!” An usher made his way to the front row and started to clean up. Usher “Excuse me sir, the movie is over.” Fox “POKEMON 4 EEEEEEVVVVVEEEERRRRRRR!” Usher “Umm, if you liked it so much why don’t you see our t-shirt stand? We have tons of new Pokemon toys!” Fox “Hey Pikachu, do you have Reed’s cell number? I’m going to need a raise.... BIG TIME!” CLOSE
