When Wars Collide

13 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/13/02 KroW slowly ran a dry white towel, with red stains, through his drenched jet black hair. The red stains were blood, blood that spilled, blood that spilled by himself. The water ran down his neck, across his lips. As the water slowly dragged across his lips, a slow thick red substance drooled down his chin...it was blood. He ran the white towel, with red blood stains, across his face, leaving another big red stain across the white towel. KroW starred at the ground below his naked feet, a million emotions running through his mind. Not one of these emotions...was fear. Not one emotion feared the sight of his two enemies forming an alliance. Not one emotion feared the fact he was laid out by two enemies. But one emotion that did run through his mind...was anger. Anger that he allowed himself to be vulnerable like this. Anger that he fell to two weaklings, to two unworthy opponents such like Erik Rave and Bobby Minio. The peons he wished to receive a challenge from, had formed an alliance and went against him. And for that, another emotion went through his body. Pleasure. He was pleased, that finally, these men were becoming warriors. Only a warrior would form an alliance with a man that they never met before. Minio and Rave never met before, yet they found a common goal. To Destroy KroW And for that, they alligned and took out their number one enemy together. The emotion of pleasure was great. It made him proud, for all the torture he placed those two men through...they prevailed with such a plan, that not even KroW sensed it. And for that, he was pleased. And due to that, he cracked a grin across his face, pleasure seething throughout every particle of his body. But for the pure fact that they did something that he didn't detect, sent a jolt of shock throughout his body. His eyes widened, and his grin disappeared. He realized that he was surprised, he was attacked...and the attack was actually successfull. He didn't expect it, he didn't see it...the war was beginning to slip from his grasp. It seemed he no longer controlled the war as much as he used to. Maybe he shouldn't have made two enemies at once... Maybe he shouldn't have underestimated them... Was he growing weaker? Were his abilities of sensing the future of his opponents slowly fading? No, it couldn't be... The only reason he was questioning all this...was because... He cracked a smile. For he was questioning all this because he had finally gotten a challenge, but more importantly, he had gotten a War. He had gotten Two Wars, and both of them were getting interested. Both wars, KroW v. Minio, KroW v. Rave, they both were going the way he thrived...surprisingly. And for that, he looked up in front of him, a smile stretching across his face, and a cold stare looked into the darkness. His eyes grew wide with excitement, as he began to laugh. 'Anger, Pleasure, and Surprise. They all are the effects of When Wars Collide. Never have I felt such a sensation of emotion...and I like it. I guess the wars are truely out of my hands...only fate controls these wars. And that's. just. fine.' The War Was Getting Excited. The Warrior Was Getting Excited. The Platform Was Ready. On Tuesday he would end Erik Rave in the war in jOlt. On Wednesday he would end Bobby Minio in the war in Action! He would win the wars, and he would win them in the name of a true warrior...in the name of KroW.
