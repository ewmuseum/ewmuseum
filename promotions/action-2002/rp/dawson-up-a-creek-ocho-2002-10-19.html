Dawson: Up A Creek: Ocho

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sweet Aroma had not too long ago escaped the moronic ramblings of Max Danger. He said something about meeting her boyfriend. Sweet Aroma’s personal life wasn’t the topic of conversation tonight, but in a way it was, except she wasn’t having man troubles, she was having woman troubles. And Jamal Wilson was the least likely person you would talk to in a predicament such as this one. Sweet Aroma had finally reached the locker room of the men that love “Hot Gay Action!”. She knocked a couple of times, and after hearing Jamal scream “Come in honey!”, she obliged. “Hey Veronica, how you doin’ girlfriend?” “Not so good Jamal.” Almost as if on cue, Jamal signaled for Chino to exit the room, which he did. “What’s wrong girlfriend?” Jamal was all ears, he might not have been attracted to females, but hearing they’re plight made him feel a little better about not dating any of them. “You see it’s Angelica...” Jamal burst out in laughter. “THAT SLUT...” “Hey Jamal, don’t call her that!” “But she is, girls are so fickle. YUCK!” “Jamal, please don’t start this, I’ve been through enough these past few weeks.” “I bet she’s the reason I can’t do my hot gay action!” “I’m serious Jamal, can you cut it out?” “Why would I be serious about a ho?” SLAP! BAM! Sweet Aroma had slammed and punched at Jamal, knocking him to the ground. And once he was on the ground she kicked at him. Suddenly Chino burst back into the room, and Aroma seemed to almost instantly stop her assault on the ‘Hardcore Homo-Sapien’. “Wha happened esse?” “Go on without me.” Jamal wasn’t that hurt, just a woman blowing off steam, and Jamal knew that Aroma meant nothing by the attack; she might’ve been on her period, and he did badmouth her friend, Angelica. “But vatos homes. We have to be together amigo.” “I’m... sorry.” Jamal Wilson passed out in the locker room, while Chino held him as if he was dead. Chino let out a loud feminine roar “NNNOOO!” Let’s get out of here.
