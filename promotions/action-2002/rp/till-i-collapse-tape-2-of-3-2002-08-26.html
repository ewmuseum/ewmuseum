Till I Collapse (Tape 2 of 3)

26 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FUZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ “Umm Boss, are you ready for tape 2?” It was Jeremy Bold of course. Only he calls Reed, ‘Boss’. Bold “Umm Boss, you should watch them in order! That’s tape three! Usually you go one, two, and then three, sir!” As Reed flushed the sound out, he hit the Mute button. The Owner then nodded and allowed Bold to switch tapes. The VCR jerked back as Bold pulled out tape 3. Bold “Are you ok? You seem sort of spaced out today.” Young “I don’t know but I have a bad feeling about this.... I mean what if something bad happens to Fox? There goes our ratings!” FUZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ Title: Till I Collapse (Tape 2 of 3) Date: Recorded Place: Tape As Bold popped in tape 2 he sat down as Reed Young flicked the lights. The screen went on and played tape 2.... ‘Cough cough....’ “Ugh........ It was cold out there last night.... BIG TIME! But I’m better, I think! Now it’s time to find Pikachu!” As we left Fox in tape 1, he was standing out in the rain after he forgot Pikachu in the diner. Now it’s morning, and Fox feels somewhat better- except he doesn’t have Pikachu. ‘Kid’ rose from his comfy Hotel sperm filled bed and jumped out into the washroom. As the soft feeling of cold tiles gave Fox the goose bumps, he undressed himself to get ready for a shower. Ok Foxo, here’s the muther fecker plan! I gotta get cleaned up in the shower, then I can check out, go down to the Police station to grab Pikachu.... and then go to the arena, arriving just in time for the show! Fox stepped into the shower, spreading the curtains across the grounds. ‘Kid’ could still be heard, though. Fox “Oh yes, it’s aBOOT time I took a shower! The last time I did was when I fought LL in a cage match back in PIW for the belt. This should keep me clean for at least another year or so.” .... Fox “Well.... I think I’m done!” Just for a split second, water could be heard running. Then Fox turned it off and jumped out. Fox “Ah, refreshed!” Christopher grabbed his old dirty clothes and was tempted to wear them. However the smell drew him further away. Luckily before he could do anything else, Fox then remembered that his Mommy gave him, like, ten pairs of the same outfit! Injected with joy, Fox rejected some ice cream and got dressed into his nice new cloths. Holy Whack! I gotta gets going! All my stuff’s in the car already. Well time to get Pikachu! I figure since the police were there, that they took him to jail! Now it’s Kanbu bust out time! The loud thumps of Fox’s feet could be heard throughout the floor. As Chris whaled the half broken door open, he scrambled down the hall nearly pushing over a man wearing a skin tight t-shirt and black leather pants that has a fairly sized red R on the right pant leg. Man “Jeez! I get no respect around here! First I get FIRED.... and then I’m trampled by some kid that looks like Chris Fox! Wait a minute....” The scene paced side to side as it tried to keep up with the juggling Fox. ‘Kid’ didn’t even bother to open the exit doors! Hey just jumped through them! Luckily the glass areas were replaced by cheap cardboard. That just gives you an idea of how Chris lives.... Fox “PUTITONMYTAP!” Fox screamed as he bolted to his car. The receptionist hardly heard him and asked an elderly woman who was walking by what he had said. Receptionist “Excuse do you know what that young man said? Old Woman “Something about putting you on his lap....” Bold “HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! That’s not funny.” Young “Shut up Jeremy! You’re ruining the movie!” After that interruption passed, Fox was already in his car and speeding away to the nearest Police station. FUZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ Tape 2 popped out as Jeremy Bold hurled to grab it before it fell onto the floor. The lights faded on and off and then back on again as Reed sat up and grabbed tape 3. Young “You know I haven’t seen Chris around lately. I wonder if anything happened to him!” With a confused face, Bold answered. Bold “I don’t know.... I just hope Pikachu is ok too!” The scene fades as Reed walks over and hands Bold Tape 3. Jeremy slowly pushes it into the machine wondering what awaits them.... FUZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ
