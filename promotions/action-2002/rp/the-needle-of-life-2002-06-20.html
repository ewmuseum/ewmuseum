Action! — Tales

...the needle of life.

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.20.02 'I'm alive again.' Steven Denton is alive again. 'No one can stop me.' No one can stop him. 'I'm young, charismatic, athletic, and blessed with incredible skill in the art that is professional wrestling.' He believes in himself. Steven wailed away at a punching bag, rocking it back and forth with incredible strength and force. He pictured it as so many things. Everything he hated, he saw in that inanimate bag. Everyone who has ever opposed him rocks back and forth with the sack, as Steven hit it again and again. His muscles grew tired, his knuckles turned red, but he didn't show any signs of stopping. He bit his lower lip until trickles of blood ran down his chin. His blood was red, full of life. The taste of it in his mouth drove him to hit harder. Droplets of blood split onto the bag. He hit the droplets. Blood began to smear over his hands, as well as the bag. Crimson filled his mind. And then he stopped. Steven backed a few steps in reverse. He surveyed the scene. Bloodstains smeared across the bag. Bloodstains on the concrete gym floor. Bloodstains on his hands. Steven smiled a wide, toothy grin. He'd let himself go, something he had not been able to do in so long. Steven was free again. The emotional (and physical) outburst he'd just had on the punching bag allowed him to find himself again. Steven was never insane, but only know was he beginning to come to grips with himself and who he was. Since the incident with his parents when he was younger, Steven had been a lost soul, drained of life. He couldn't find himself. He couldn't figure out what he was here for. He didn't know where he was supposed to go. Now... now he didn't need to know. He found himself, and that was all that mattered. He glanced around the gym, and noticed that no one was there. He, alone, had been there to witness himself revelate. For an instant, Steven was glad no one was there to see him. He must look horrible, he thought. So, he headed for the showers to clean up. The last thing he needed was to show up for Pressure Point stained with his own blood. As he walked, Steven spoke to himself, much like he did the night before: 'If it's possible to ever be at peace, I think I am finally there. Life has simplified. And now that I'm simplified, I can act freely. Nothing holding me back. No falling like the mighty often do. I am at my peak, and I will remain there. This is my time. This is my world. This is me, and there has never been a better time to be me. The wrestling world might not know it yet, but I am coming. I have been released. I've always been dominate physically, now I'll be able to dominate my opponents mentally. The world is my oyster, and I'm going to eat it.' Steven washed the blood off his face. As the crimson water swirled towards the sewer... so did his former self.
