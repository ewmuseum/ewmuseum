Prelude, In which a Family is born.

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Come on, Jackie! One more push!” Julius held his bride’s hand tightly. He had taken an early flight from Houston to be here, and that didn’t sit quite well with Promoter Vincent McMahon, Sr. But he had not choice. Jackie was in Labor. “Ok, it’s coming. Keep going, Jackie!” Now, she held his hand tighter... She let out a final scream, her dark skin was now bright red. But after that agonizing push, she gave birth to a beautiful seven pound, eight ounce boy. Julius kissed his wife on her forehead. Both had great looks of relief, Jackie more than others. “Mr. Arcade.” Julius looked up from his wife’s beautiful face to the Doctor who had delivered the baby. “Would you do the honors, again?” he said as he presented Julius with a pair of sharp medical scissors. Julius took the scissors and cut the umbilical cord. The nurses took the baby to the back where they cleaned him off. After a few moments that let Julius and Jackie soak up their new lives, two nurses returned. One carried the newly born boy, while another carried another child. The nurse placed the boy in the arms of Julius, while the other set the other child on the bed with Jackie. Each parent stared in to their child’s eyes, when one of the nurses finally spoke up. “Congratulations, Mr. and Mrs. Arcade, you are now the parents of a beautiful girl and boy.” Julius and Jackie gave a warming smile to each other, then back at their new children. “Have you decided on any names?” the nurse asked. “Are we going with those two?” Julius asked his wife. She nodded, and spoke softly as she stared in to her daughter’s eyes. “Daisy May.... and Donell Ray.”
