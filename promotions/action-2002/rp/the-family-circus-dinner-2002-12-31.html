The Family Circus: Dinner

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/31/2002 One can only stand to watch Mitsuharu Misawa being dumped unceremoniously on his head for so long before they get bored. Sure, it's damned entertaining to see Misawa's iron skull being smashed, crashed, and burned onto All Japan Pro Wrestling's mats, but that's aside from the point. Anyway, eventually, Allison had pulled Coral away from his still-remaining wrestling tape collection in his room, and into the dining room of the Avalon household. The dining room was extravagant, with many paintings(most of them Lily's earlier, more peaceful works) hung from the walls. A beautiful series of cabinets were along the walls, while a red carpet lined the floor. The long table that was centrally positioned in the room could fit at least sixteen people, though in this case, it would fit nine Avalon family members and a special guest. Irene Avalon frowned upon the sight of Allison Lindum, who had dressed causally for the occasion. Actually, she usually frowned, so it wasn't exactly the most noticable of events. Emmy Avalon, the botonist and oldest of the four Avalon siblings, meekly slinked into the room and quietly took a seat. She seemed very uncomfortable in general to be amongst the rest of the family, which Coral took in as deeply ironic, since he usually felt the same way. She has changed out of her jeans and tanktop, and wore a black dress, but still kept her lab coat on. Coral shrugged at this. Lily Avalon, the youngest of the four Avalon siblings, didn't seem to really pay attention to what was going on in front of her. Her long black hair hung carelessly from her head and her black dress flowed behind her as she walked into the room. She had a pencil in one hand and a large sketch pad that she pinned against her abdomen as she drew something on it. She dropped down onto her chair and put her sketch pad onto the table. Coral noticed with some alarm that the picture was a vivid picture of a man being stabbed in the throat with a knife. Elvis, Coral's giant of a big brother, simply strolled in, picked up a chair, and sat down. He had definitely gotten out of the workout mode, and changed into a suit. Yes. A big guy like Elvis wore suits. Mainly to show off how rich he is to afford clothing to fit his large frame... but mostly so he can look professional when he pounded your face into immaterial. Albert Avalon was the next person to enter to room, three minutes later. He, like his nephew Elvis, was clad in a suit. He had also apparently got back through to his companies in North Dakota, and was throughly berating them in a brutal, obscenity-laced tirade that would make Kellen Kinkade wet himself, make Joe Campbell shit himself with fear, and make Ruben Ross cry like a bitch. Cleo Avalon filed in next, though she still carried a bucket of popcorn. Even more embarassingly, she wore a Jerry Lee Lewis hat. She sat down with her little portable DVD player and played a custom-made telethon DVD silently to herself. Though she wouldn't be silent for long, before she started to hoot and holler, and chant for the Jerry's Kids to show up. All of these combustable elements were in one room, just waiting to go off. Finally, John Avalon pulled himself from his studies, and entered the hall, before he took his place at the very end of the table. He called everyone to order with a simple ringing of his glass from his fork. This had more effect on the family than any one word that could have been said to one another. Almost simultaneously, Lily stopped drawing, Albert stopped yelling into his phone(bringing relief to whoever the hell he was talking to), and Cleo stopped watching her telethons. 'Yes... I do believe everyone is all accounted for.' John said. There was a pause, as John chose this moment to clear his throat. 'Distinguished members of the Avalon family,' he began, 'We are here to...' This was the part where the interruptions came in, Albert being the first as he pointed the antenna of his cellular phone in the directions that firmly belonged to Coral Avalon and Allison Lindum, 'Who the hell are they?' Albert, of course, had a short memory when it came to things that didn't relate to his businesses. At any rate, John gave a cough of embarassment. 'Um, that's your nephew, Al.' John pointed out. 'Pssh.' John sighed. This wasn't going to go well, especially since the family tended to be very polarized about certain issues. And right off the bat, things weren't going to look good. 'I thought we disowned him.' Lily said, bluntly. 'I think we did.' Cleo said, not really caring about the banter. 'I'm harder to get rid of than Cher, only less ugly.' Coral said, sulking back in his chair, not enjoying the direction this was heading. 'Cher's not ug--' Cleo started, as she started to defend a known infomercial star, but couldn't finish. 'Ahem!' John said, ending the argument quickly and hastily. He paused, looking about the cornucopia of freaks that make up the Avalon family. It was becoming brutally obvious that the family was polarized with regards to Coral. 'We are gathered here to honor our distinguished guests. Could we not have all of these petty indifferences, spats, and interruptio-' 'Distinguished guests?' Irene interrupted, bringing about the groans of just about everyone in the room, 'Pah! Look at them! The only place they'd look distinguished is in a barn.' 'Mother,' Emmy said meekly, 'Don't be r-rude...' 'PAH!' Irene shouted at Emmy, nearly sending her off of the chair in fright, 'Rude?! I don't have to watch my tongue when I'm around these... barbarians!' 'Barbarians isn't a strong enough word to describe them,' Albert said, 'I mean, you could at least call th--' He didn't have a chance to finish before Elvis punched him in the arm to shut him up. Albert turned to look at Elvis, content on trying to return it, but he was giving up an entire foot to the big brother of Coral Avalon, and he quickly stood down. Needless to say, Coral snapped his head toward the direction of his mother, 'Geez, after all of your etiquette lessons that I stopped paying attention to years ago, you still can't afford to be nice to me. How ironic.' 'Hold your tongue!' Irene shouted, bursting from her chair, 'You have no right coming into this house and insulting me!' 'And yet, you would insult me in mine,' Coral said, unflenching, 'Your double standardizing is seriously out of control, mom.' He was thinking about tacking on a crack about the shockingly absurd haircut his mother had, which tried to be a ponytail and an afro at the same time, but thought better of it. 'How dare you... why do you insult me? Your own mother?' 'Irene,' John said, 'Get off of his back, already.' 'Only when he stops insulting me.' Irene said, thumbing her nose into the air and acting very regal-like. Coral raised an eyebrow. 'What are you, six, Irene?' John asked. 'Mind your business!' 'This IS my business.' John snapped, standing up, 'This is my family. My family, my blood, and my son. Of everyone in here, Irene, you're the person who has the LEAST right to judge him. Coral might have done some pretty spiteful things to his siblings when he was trying to learn the alphabet, but he did it because he was young. In the eighteen years that Coral's lived here, though, Coral's never done anything to you.' 'That isn't true. He's forsaken everything this family has stood for.' Irene shot back. 'Everything we've stood for? What DO we stand for, Irene? Etiquette? Wealth? Greed? Absurdity?' John asked, his tone rapidly becoming more and more bold, as he stood up from his chair, 'We haven't functioned as a family in years, Irene. Emmy spends so much time in her greenhouse that she might as well live there.' 'I, uh, d-do live there, Dad...' Emmy said, weakly. John blinked, but pressed on, 'Elvis is usually out of the house because of his job or his anger management, and whenever he IS home, he's always pounding something into dirt,' John said, as Elvis grinned and pounded his right fist into the palm of his left hand to emphasize the point, 'Lily probably hasn't set foot out of this house in years, given how pale she looks now... same with Cleo, especially given the fact that all she does is watch telethons and infomercials all day. And... Albert! Hey! I thought we were gonna have to surgically remove that cell phone from your ear!' Albert grumbled. 'What's your point?' Irene demanded. 'The point is, Irene, that this family hasn't been a family since Coral lef- I mean, was kicked out.' John said. 'He wanted to participate in a barbarian's sport! He deserved whatever he got.' Irene said. 'What do you know of it, mom?' Coral asked, shooting out of his chair in his own right, 'Would you like for me to practice a safer, more cost-effecient job? Hey, I know, why don't I pick up a bucket of popcorn and join Cleo in her wonderful adventures in infomercials!' 'I've got no problem with that!' Cleo shouted, as she popped open her miniature DVD player and started playing her shows again. Coral rolled his eyes, 'Sorry, mom, but this is what I am. It's what I do, and what I've always wanted to do. I didn't want to own a bunch of useless oil companies, I didn't want to paint violent pictures, and I sure as hell didn't want to have a fashion sense that would make Kelly Osbourne look like Jessica Alba. I wanted to be a wrestler. It was a simple wish, at least, I THOUGHT so. Apparently, some people thought otherwise.' 'Have you no loyalty to this family?' Lily asked, sternly, 'You know that your, ahem, JOB, doesn't allow for very many visits to the family.' 'Amazing what getting kicked out does to one's morale.' Coral said, idly. Lily stood up and pointed an accusing finger at Coral, 'You're terrible! Just terrible.' Emmy then stood up, and meekly held her hands behind her back. 'U-umm... he... he does have... a, a point...' 'Point shmoint!' Albert hooted, picking up his cell phone as he went, 'I have better things to do than to listen to you saps.' With that, Albert left, dialing a number back to his companies. This, of course, had no real effect on making things better with the family, as it had turned into an all out verbal brawl. Lily, Cleo, and Irene took one side, while Coral and John bravely took the other side while poor Emmy began to walk away. Perhaps Coral didn't know it, but as he was arguing with his family, his girlfriend, Allison chose that moment to storm off. As Coral was about to launch into a long tirade against his mother, he noticed this. 'Hey...' he said to no one in particular, 'Where did Allison go?' Emmy saw this, and tapped Coral on the shoulder. Coral twirled around, surprising Emmy with a yelp. This was ignored by the remaining arguing members of the Avalon family, but eventually, Emmy calmed down, 'She, um... she... left... t-that way.' She pointed off in a direction. Coral shrugged and followed her out.
