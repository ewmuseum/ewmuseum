Put your Quarter up.

14 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9.14.02 Only a Sunny Afternoon in Lower Eastside New York... Bobby Minio was sitting in his dark blue Mustang. It was his only home. His only keep. His only shelter. He was tapping his palms across the steering wheel to match the jazzy beat of the aesop rock song on the cd player. He looked confused yet content. Like he didn't know, but he didn't care. -Good Morning, my sweet vision of morbid disorder- The lyrics were pouring from the speakers and all Bobby could do was think. Think about the rumors he heard of Dawson trying to second hand him, trying to get Veronica to Second guess him. Some people thought Bobby had a 'thing' for Sweet Aroma. Some people thought he was just trying to get in her pants. Bobby himself, didn't know what to think. Between his past, his present, and hopefully, his Future, he didn't know what promise things even held anymore. One thing was for sure. If he was gonna be the glimmer in her eye, he would need to shine. He moved with the gracefully slowed broken flow of traffic. It was an urban orchistra of horns, hollars, engines and highway car lagged moans past Starbucks lava cups. A veritable orgy of people more confused, angry, and or depressed then Minio himself. That scared him more then any middriff shirt wearing psychopath could ever wish to do so. Minio was caught in the replay of what made him haul off and lay Dawson out. He didn't really have anything against her. He actually was quite fond of her, he admired a woman with a love for animals. But she was to much of a liability. Her rumors, her lies, her pokes and prods to Veronica had gone to far. What did Dawson care if Minio had something for Veronica? They're all adults, or close enough atleast. 'Girls love to Gossip I guess. Well, Curiousity killed the cat.' The Honks from the angry line of cars behind the blue Mustang holding up the intersection finally peirced the thick brick wall of thought Minio had laid around his head. He leaned his head out of the window looking back at the first man, a large New York fellow in a 99' Dodge Ram, and yelled... 'I'm moving, keep your pad on!' This Prompted the man to glance to someone else in the truck, then open the door and begin stomping a trench towards Minio. Minio watched the man approach in the rear view mirror. 'Oh lord.' Minio uttered low. So tired, so exhausted, so caught up in his own problems. The man was finally getting close when Minio let out a very Nick Noltesce Sigh, and opened the door, standing to face the man. 'What sir, do you want to `fight` me because of a comment?' Emphasis on the word 'FIGHT'. The man looked Minio over with an angry grin and replied simply. 'Maybe I do, bitch.' Bobby laughed, very holier then thou. 'Put your quarter up.' The man was reaching into his pocket when he caught himself, stopped, and sneered. 'What the fuck is that supposed to mean?' Bobby was laughing at the man as he was sitting back into his classic muscle car. 'Go to an arcade and ask sir, because this entire two minutes has been juvinile.' Bobby closed the door, and peeled out, leaving the man in the burnt stench of hot rubber. Bobby sped around another corner, then pulled into an alleyway. He needed to sleep. He needed to think. He needed to sort things out. He needed to find out how to explain to her, to them, to everyone, how he really felt. He needed to set things straight. God knows how hard this will be.
