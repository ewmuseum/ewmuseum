Action! — Tales

Pope Without Bible. 3

12 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/12/02 Jeremy Bold rested his head against the side of his desk. He couldn’t think. He couldn’t speak. For Jeremy Bold could not get one mere idea out of his head. He was sitting in his apartment, at his “working desk” which looked a hell of a lot like Reed Young’s. He sat there with a hot... pink... pen... and a... Navy... blue... binder? WTF... All together now: MF!! Bold ripped out a sheet of paper. “THAT’S NOT A GOOD ANGLE! ... THAT’S NOT A GOOD ANGLE AT ALL!” The sheet fell to the floor, as it opened back up slowly. Jeremy was so weak, he had been up all night thinking of great angles that even he could not crush a small paper ball like he used to. And as the ball of paper opened, the angle could be seen. “Joey Malone... battling Kent Anthason in a Mall Brawl Match at the next Action! Pay-Per-View.” Actually Jeremy, that wasn’t a half-bad ide- “THAT’S NOT A GOOD ANGLE AT ALL!” Right. Whatever. Bold began to brainstorm another idea. He thought it’d be cool if one day Reed Young fought Joe Campbell in a Match. Then, he punched the table, thinking that was another shitty idea. “THAT’S NOT A GOOD ANGLE AT ALL!” Jeremy Bold slammed his head against the desk, beginning to cry onto the paper which sat in the... Navy... Blue... Binder. Cracking it in half, and throwing the Stables Business Depo product across the room, Bold broke down in more tears. “Action! Wrestling is going to HELL if I can’t get new angle ideas!”
