Prologue II - Lights (ii)

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th October “Leon… I need to talk to you.” It didn’t sound good. They had been having the perfect evening together as far as Leon was concerned, in fact there was less nagging than normal, he should’ve read more into it. He just assumed that Tash was finally getting used to Leon’s occupation. He hadn’t always been a stuntman though. He’d done office work for a bit, and got tired of that, and for a long time he’d done building work, working on his physique… but it just wasn’t challenging enough. Leon needed danger in his life to keep him going. Moving bricks and lumber around was hardly the deadliest profession on the planet, and soon enough he’d grown tired of that profession as well. It was here that Leon had gotten it into his head that he wanted to become famous. Except deep down he knew he didn’t want to become famous… he needed it. He needed the drive, the thrill of entertaining people. He needed to be recognised when he walked down the street. He needed the praise from any random person, who ten minutes later he wouldn’t be able to tell apart in a crowd. It sounded sick when he ran it through in hid head, what kind of twisted individual would want to live like that? Leon Powers. “It’s… It’s about your job… I hate having to bring this up again, but I hate it. I hate not knowing whether you’re gonna come back in your car or in the back of an ambulance. I hate the fact that one week I don’t see any of you, and the next you’re unemployed and we struggle with the rent. I need some security Leon, I can’t go on like this.” … “Say something!!!” What you want me to say? “However you’re feeling, I need to know what you’re gonna do.” We both know how this gonna end Tash… Natasha’s head dropped slightly, that wasn’t what she wanted to hear, but she knew it was inevitable. It felt like rehearsing a play, the amount of times they’d had the same conversation, each making the exact same points. This is my dream. D’ya get what I mean when I say that? This and you are my two reasons for living. Without you both I couldn’t go on… I’d have nothing to live for. “Leon… I can’t… we can’t go on like this…” Leon choked on his meal… he was incontrovertibly shocked. He hadn’t expected her to drop this bombshell… especially during Chilli Con Carne… this was his favourite meal, just turned bitter as hell with one sentence. You… you what? “I… I don’t know. I can’t carry on like this Leon, but I can’t live my life without you by my side. Just promise me you’ll at least look for some other work, and if it pays better, you’ll take it.” I’ll try Tash, but ya knew when ya met me I weren’t no 9 to 5 man. “Yeah…” Their relationship was going through it’s toughest test to date, and with Leon’s increasing frustration with work it seemed like the weight of the world was on his broad shoulders, giving him the ultimate test.
