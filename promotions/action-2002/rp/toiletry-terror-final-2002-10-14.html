Toiletry Terror [Final]

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 14 He takes the boy by the hand. He drags him across the lawn and toward the Action! Mobile. He snatches a newspaper from the letter box. He opens the side door of the Action! Mobile and draws the dark curtains. We hear the boy scream. “NO! NO! I’M SORRY!” But it all gets muffled out. The screams get drowned out as we hear the John grunt. The people have gathered around to see the saving of the toilet now begin to gather around the van. All trying to peer in and see what the fate of the boy is. [THIRTY MINUTES LATER] The side door is pulled open and the boy is thrown to the lawn. He is bound and gagged with gaffer tape. His shirt is missing. Stepping out of the vehicle, the John stands by the fallen body of the boy who looks as though he’s rather ill. The John has the boy’s white shirt in hand. He throws it to the mother of the boy. “You’d better wash it.” Dropping to one knee, the John is by the child’s side as tears flow down his face. “Let’s see how you like having a turd in your mouth for three hours!” The John looks to Captain Obvious, who appears to be talking to the toilet and Toilet Duck about the compounds of grass. He whistles and makes his way into the passenger seat of the Action! Mobile. Captain Obvious lifts the toilet and makes his way to the van, placing the Gaylan pull-chain in the back and securing it. He then hands Toilet Duck to the John who has his window down. The John glances over all the faces and grins. “The John is the protector of the potty and that’s all there is to it. If an injustice takes place against the lavatory, the John will be there. And yes, there will be repercussions.” The motor turns over as the John nods to his sidekick, Captain Obvious. They slowly begin to pull out onto the road as the John shouts back to the gathering of spectators who begin to clap. “The John is number one. You’re number two. And you know damn straight the John is gonna flush you!” The sun begins to set the Action! Mobile makes it’s way down the road. The Action! League begins it’s trek home and to the Gaylan’s safety. Our heroes have saved the day again. But who knows what injustices may happen? Who darn well knows?
