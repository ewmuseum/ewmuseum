Reflecting On: Part Seven.

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.13.2003 The plane had reached an altitude so high that no one really wanted to think about it. It was just really high. The seat belt sign had been turned off and the passengers were allowed to move about the cabin. People were getting up and using the lavatory and things of that nature. In the back of the plane, sat Xander. Head lowered, mind wandering. He was just... gone. A group of about twenty college students were all turned around, standing, and talking to one another. Joking and talking about what they were going to do once they landed in Vegas. Xander just sat there, hands on the arm rests, head lowered and staring at the seat in front of him. It was around 9:00pm CST, as they had not yet reached the mountain time zone. Jessica was still up sitting next to the large couple. She desperately wanted to see her husband, but wasn't sure it would be wise. Too many people, too much going on. It would not go well, she thought. The girlfriend of the muscle-bound guy had shook his forearm. Her boyfriend had went to take a piss. Xander turned his head slightly and looked at the dark-haired college girl. She asked, 'What's your deal?' She was remarking on his suit and the fact that he was all sad and shit. He didn't answer her. Didn't even regard her. He just closed his eyes and turned his head to face the seat in front of him. The college chick just scoffed, 'Fine. I was just trying to be nice.' She turned back to the window and watched the night clouds rush past it. Her boyfriend soon returned, stopping to have a laugh with his buddies and then stepped over Xander's legs and back into his seat. Whispering, she told her boyfriend about Xander's rudeness. The guy turned to Marcus and put a hand on his shoulder. 'Hey, buddy, do you have a problem? My girl was just wondering what was wrong with you,' he said in a deep, oafish tone. Xander eyes slowly opened. Just looking into them would scare you shitless. He turned and faced the big guy. 'I'm sorry I'm not a chatter-box. But losing one's mother tends to do that to a guy. So... fuck off.' Okay, not the best choice of words. Jessica was on her way to the back as well, although she had not heard that little conversation. 'Listen, man, no need to get pissy with us,' the brute responded. Xander did not reply. He just turned and saw that his wife was making her way back. She bent down to him and kissed him on the forehead, 'Xander, baby, how are you doing?' He didn't answer. He only shook his head and made a quick glance to the couple beside him. Jessica looked at them, not knowing what had gone down, and then turned back to her husband. She just looked at him for a moment and then turned back to the couple. 'I hate to bother you like this, I really do, but do you think you two could switch us seats? I know we're full here, but a little trip around the plane could find you two seats. And you could just send one person back here with us.' The big man looked to his girlfriend. They seemed to recognize the situation. They weren't pleased with the tone that Xander had used with them, but considering the circumstance, they were going to oblige. 'Sure,' the big guy said. He turned to his girlfriend, 'Come on.' Together the two stood up as Jessica moved back for them to move out. The big man then stopped and said to Jessica, 'I'm sorry to hear about his mother.' Jessica looked shocked. Horrified even. She hadn't been told what had happened. It took her a moment to let it sink it. She muttered, 'Thank you,' to the man and then slid into a seat by the window. She just stared out it, watching as they zoomed through the night sky. Xander had moved a seat over. He hadn't told Jessica what had happened and was going to right then. He grabbed her hand and held it in his own. He then turned her face, by her chin, to face his. 'Jessica,' he started. But she stopped him, putting a hand up and shaking her head. She knew. He knew that she knew, just by the look on her face. In her eyes. He lowered his head, as tears began to stream down her face. He pulled her head into his chest and put a hand on the back of her head. He just let her weep. No more would come for him. Not for a good while longer.
