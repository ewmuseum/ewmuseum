Spontaneous [1]

7 November 2002

Date: 11.07.02 Russa eyes slowly opened up and focused on his alarm clock that was beside his bed on the night table. The bright, lime green numbers were all Russa he could stare at right now. 7:58 It was always the same. Every morning at exactly 7:58 AM, Russa would wake up and stare at his alarm clock until it reached 8:00 AM where the annoying screech would begin. Russa would shoot up, slam his fist into the alarm clock and start singing his favourite song, ‘Eye of the Tiger.’ Today was going to be different. It was a special day for Russa. It was only days ago that the ukw had finally closed its doors for good and Russa had finally returned to his old apartment back in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It was completely empty except for a few of the things he had brought back or left there before he went away. His first day was going to be special and he had just the idea of what he could possibly do. 7:59 He sat straight up in his bed and ripped the cord from his alarm clock out of the wall and tossed the cord to the ground. “THEY SAY THAT A HERO COULD SAVE US, I’M NOT GONNA STAND HERE AND WAIT!” Russa sang completely off tune as he pulled his blankets off of him and headed towards his kitchen. He opened one of the cupboards that revealed boxes and boxes of Wheaties cereal with an older picture of Russa on them. (Russa just got a lot of empty cereal boxes and reuses the box for a new cereal each time.) He grabbed a bowl and poured the cereal into it. To his surprise, out came Lucky Charms. Today was going to be his day. Russa was on a strict cereal diet and would be the only thing he would eat for days at a time. He quickly drained down the cereal and headed to his bedroom to get dressed. He quickly pulled some blue plaid pants on and a black LoD T-shirt and was soon in the bathroom. His hair seemed to have its natural powers and beauty, as all Russa had to do was rub his dry hands through it and presto! The perfect style. He looked into the mirror and got a glimpse of himself. He quickly reached for the goatee that was snug on the bottom of his chin and turned his head to the sides, staring at himself. He reached down for the tap and turned the cold water tap on and waited for the water to fall. He cupped his hands and let the cold water hit them. He brought the water to his face and it relaxed him. He shook the water off of his face and turned the tap off. He left. Back to the bedroom. He grabbed a few pairs of shorts, pants, shirts, and his wrestling gear including his ukw Hardway title and he stuffed them into a duffel bag. He left his apartment and raced down the stairs towards the lobby. He left the building and headed to his car, a light blue, 1995 Ford Tempo. He threw his things into the back and drove off.
