Dinner Arrangements: Part Five: Final

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 26th, 2002 Doesn’t really matter. The automobile came to a sudden stop in front of a large house at the end of a secluded, gated neighbourhood. Children were fast asleep, mommies and daddies with thoughts of passion. Roads glistened after a fresh rain, the moonlight reflecting off the water contained in small potholes in the middle of the street. In the dark, around midnight, Jeff yawned and turned off the headlights to his 65’ Pinto. Claire laid back in the lumpy, torn passenger’s seat, giggling uncontrollably like a high school cheerleader on her prom night. Drunken laughter was what it was; brought on by the fix or six glasses of expensive alcohol she had single handily downed one after another. Odium the Lobster swam in his small, temporary home in a Glad zip lock baggy. Though it didn’t really swim as much as it just floated in the same spot, not that it matter, Jeff wasn’t looking at his new friend anyway. All his interest was on the beauty that was Claire, she squirmed around in her seat trying to get comfortable, completely unaware that this was her stop. She turned her cute little head and smiled at Jeff, blowing him a kiss. “Hey, you know that if you kissed me right now, I probably wouldn’t stop you. I’d probably like it.” She giggled again, leaning over and rubbing her nose on Jeff’s cheek. Boy did her the warmth of her breath feel good. All he wanted to do was plant a lip-lock on her, but he couldn’t bring himself to do it. He lacked morals, this shouldn’t have been so hard to do. Just kiss her and let her lead you to other things. But something just told him that this was wrong, he had never, ever cared for someone like he did for Claire. It wasn’t just a sexual thing, sure she was beautiful, but it was more than that. It was love. He couldn’t do it, not with her intoxicated like that. Not with out knowing how she really felt about him. “You, maybe, wanna come in.” My god, she was actually trying to seduce him. This was his chance, the chance he had been waiting for, the chance to finally loose his virginity and become a real man. “I can’t.” Jeff softly blurted out, placing both his hands on the steering wheel. “Not like this. I’m sorry.” A look of disappointment appeared on Claire’s face, she simply nodded and hiccuped and opened the passenger’s side door to get out. She slammed the door, but turned back and leaned through the opened window. “Thanks Jeff, I had a real nice time...” “Me too.” She stuck her head through and gave Jeff a gentle kiss on the cheek before heading off to her door, leaving the Original shocked at what just happened. And what was going to happen in the coming weeks…
