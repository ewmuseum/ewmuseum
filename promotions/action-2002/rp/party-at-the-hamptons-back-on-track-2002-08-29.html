Party At The Hamptons: Back On Track

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JCON's 'Back On Track' Veronica and Angelica were relaxing in their hotel room, the morning of September 11th. Tonight they would possibly wrestle on the Pressure Point card. Roni would wrestle with a heavy heart, but nevertheless she was positive that she would be able to handle her duties and put on a good show. A much needed break would soon arise, as she will be taking Angelica Dawson away with her, to meet her soon to be boyfriend, Jonathan Conspiracy, PIW's World Champion. 'I think Bobby likes you.' Words from the mouth of Angelica Dawson. 'No he doesn't. He just despises the way Max Danger treats women.' The reply straight out the mouth of the 'Spanish Mamacita'. 'Whatever you say girl, but I think he has the hots for you.' Angelica continued to press the issue. 'Whatever you say girlfriend, but I don't think Jonathan would like that too much.' That's what Sweetz response was. 'I'm just fucking with you girl, let's just get ready for PP, and then...' Sweetz joined Angelica in finishing the sentence. '... THE HAMPTONS!' They each highfived each other, as a pink penguin and a purple one continued to pack bags.
