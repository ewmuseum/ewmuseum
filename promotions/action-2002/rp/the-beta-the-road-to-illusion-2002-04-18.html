The Beta: The Road To: Illusion

18 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/18/02 This pain… It is all just an illusion. The cuts on my face are not real. It is surreal bullshit. Lies. All of it. The mental anguish I felt wasn’t real. If it was, why did it go away so quickly? Why did the pain that came when I bled go away? BECAUSE HUMAN EMOTION ISN’T REAL! It is just a bunch of signals triggered by chemicals in your brain. Your reality is based on something that is theoretical. It can be proven wrong at any given moment. Chemistry makes peoples lives. So some chemical tells you that some chick gave you a wink. So some chemical tells you how to feel and what to do. How quaint. Morons. All of you. Look beyond the searing pain of cutting yourself and allowing these chemicals to lose their control. Why does it hurt when you bleed? Because those chemicals tell you so. If I had only three tablespoons of the red ooze, I wouldn’t feel anything. I would be God. That is when your body fails you. It can not stand the perfection. I stopped cutting myself a little while ago. It was boring. It didn’t get me off anymore. I just want to hurt something important. Someone, to be more exact. I stopped accepting pain and emotion as real entities. That’s why I can stand up, even though my legs tell me not to. I can open my eyes, even though they scream to stay shut. I do not feel, therefore I am perfect. Joey opened me up to this great possibility of revenge. I can hurt you in the most vile way, throwing my body into cautions way. If I held you as a speeding truck came along, and hit us, it would kill me. But it would kill you too. See…that is how you play a game like this. Show the person you are fighting who wants life that you do not care about your own…as long as it is in duty to ending the life of the other. Fucked up logic? How so? Revenge is something that is a never ending chess game. One person moves, the other counters, the first person counters what the second person countered. It lasts forever. Unless you want to stalemate the opponent. Then all that matters is the amount of pieces you took away. That really determines who just won. Joey, our fight is like a game of chess. You have countered everything Rashard did to beat you. I am still waiting for your counter. And more then likely, the counter will not make me worry. For it is something that plays right into my game. Fear me… Love me… Hate me… Destroy me… It doesn’t matter… Payback’s opinion is the only one that matters to me. We think a like. How could that be? A shovel is inhuman. I am inhuman. Draw your conclusions. Because in the end… I win. Payback wins. We will win while you lose. Accept it. Because this pain… IT’S ALL AN ILLUSION!
