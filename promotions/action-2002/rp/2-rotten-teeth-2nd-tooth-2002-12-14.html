2 Rotten Teeth (2nd Tooth)

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/14/02 OPEN The scene was familiar by now.... and if you didn’t know where Christopher Fox was then you shouldn’t be allowed to have children. Anyway, for those of you who missed the nailed grinding action last time, Chris Fox is in the Doctor seat getting his fillings! Now it’s time for the second tooth and a big surprise.... Title: 2 Rotten Teeth (2nd Tooth) Date: 12/14/02 Noon EST Place: Dentist Office Blufinland “Ok Chris this will freeze your upper gums.... and stuff.” Otay Doc, you know, heh, the only thing that is scary is the extra ‘stuff’ that will happen. Blufinland grasped his needle, half empty, and then used the remaining fuel and pinched it into Chris’ gums. Christopher’s grip tightened to the chair as the pain exceeded like a normal hardcore match, but of course for Chris he enjoys hardcore matches. Blufinland “There. All done. It should be kicking in quickly because I added some freezing mechanisms to it, can you feel this?” Blufinland touched Fox’s gums and waited.... .... .... .... Blufinland “Umm I’ll take that as a no. Let’s proceed.” Fox “I though you were going to touch my gums!” Blufinland “I did.” Fox “Well blow me a new cock! I MEAN! Yes, proceed.” Christopher laid still as the scene displayed a bird’s eye view from the Doctor’s high volt light. It was time to drill. Blufinland grabbed his tool and mistakenly pushed it on. The shock of this miniature but powerful tool made the good intending Doc drop the drill right in the mouth of the ‘Kid’!!!!! The most gruesome sound of flesh getting twisted up with metal could be heard.... it was almost like nails on a chalk board. Oh yeah, and one big scream! Fade out. Fade in. Chris Fox could be seen walking out to the front desk of the dental office only holding the left side of his jaw. Mommy stood. Mommy “Chris are you ok?” Fox “I otay Mommy, Dr. Bufintan just dropped the drill in my teeth....” Just then the Doc himself walked out and stood beside Chris. Blufinland “Hi Mrs. Fox. We had a slight accident, it has actually benefited your son though. You see I dropped the drill and it rearranged Chris’ teeth.... well to put it correctly, straightened them. More good news: not only did the fillings fit fine but Chris won’t ever need braces. I think you’ll agree, Chris, when I say the pain was worth it.” Fox “Yeah it only hurted for a second.... BIG TIME!” Mommy “Oh shut up Chris! I suppose if this didn’t happen then I’d have to pay four thousand dollars for braces! I’m not happy, I’ll never be! Now c’mon! I’m hungry, drive me, oh and you’re going to give me a back rub later for saving me money!” Fox “I thought you’d be happy!” Mommy “Oh I am, but just the fact that I have to cancel the orthodontist is a pain that you are held responsible for!” They started to walk out, as Mommy left, but before she did Fox turned around to Dr. Blufinland. Fox “Lol.... hehe.” CLOSE
