Starting Over

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/16/02 . . . Augusta, Maine | 23:30PM EST . . . MAX DANGER Jessica! JESSICA Marcus! Max Danger(Marcus Alexander Dox - often referred to as Xander) and Jessica Barrett-Dox run across the front yard. Max tosses down his brown satchel as he runs. They embrace. A long, passionate kiss is shared. DANGER I've missed you so much! JESSICA I've missed you even more. Max and Jessica kept their arms around each other as they begin walking towards the house. DANGER I have so much to tell you. JESSICA Xander, honey? DANGER (smiling uncontrollably) Yes, Jess. What is it? JESSICA Where's Kayla? Max's smile immediately turns to a frown. There is silence amongst the married couple. Jessica looks at Max, wanting an answer, but the look on his face is answer enough. JESSICA Oh... Max pulls Jessica's head into his chest and kisses her on the top of her head. DANGER They just took her away, and I haven't heard anything from her or where she was taken since. JESSICA I loved her. DANGER I did too. The two continue walking. They step up onto the small covered porch. Both take a seat at the top step. Jessica puts her hands together and puts them through her legs, and rests them on her feet. Max has one arm around Jessica, and on the porch, as the other rubs her leg. DANGER Would you like to come with me? Come with me on the road? JESSICA I.. I... I dunno. I just... just got home.... and.... DANGER I understand, Jess. Has my mother come by to see you? JESSICA Not yet. I called her, but she wasn't home. DANGER Jess, you know I'm going to have to leave again, right? JESSICA I know, and I understand. DANGER But I'll be back after each show. JESSICA I know. DANGER I love you, Jessica. JESSICA I love you, Marcus. Danger rubs her cheek, then gives her a kiss on the lips. The kiss lasts for what seems like an eternity. The kiss finally ends, as Max starts to get up. DANGER Bye. JESSICA Bye. Jessica is starting to tear up, as Max strokes her hair. He kisses her on the forehead, then turns around. Max reaches down and picks up his satchel. He puts it on his shoulder, letting the strap cross his body and letting the satchel rest on his hip. DANGER Now it's time to take care of business.
