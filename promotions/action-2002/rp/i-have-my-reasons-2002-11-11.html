I Have My Reasons

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have my reasons for remaining a heel. I have my reasons for being a jackass who, to this day, is almost unattainable (unless your name is Kent Anthason) when it comes to gaining your hatred. Everyone in this business hates who and what I am. What I do. What I feel. I am a bitter superstar with all the tools and all the intelligence to fight the doers of evil. I chose not to. How many times have I needed to have my ass kicked by upwards of three to five large men (sometimes women like to jump into the mix) to learn that simple fundamental. I can put five more years worth of profit thanks to the decision to absorb your verbal punishment then to absorb physical torture. I have a bad back, and my neck and right knee make it almost impossible, somedays, to function. This is from my years of absorbing all that punishment. Anyway, I hate to break it to you, but being a damn good heel takes more then being a damn good face. You have to do more then show up. You have to bring your top-level game every night to maintain that status. And while fucktard Kent might be the top heel in the promotion, I am still continue to give you a reason to want to puke. Is it because I just am almost impossible to beat? Or is it my rather callous manner? By not saying a word since my return, I have gained more heat then Anthason, the entire rest of the Risk, and Airborn Tyler and the rest of his fuckwits combined. They had to talk to get under your skin. To hit you in special areas. Me? Talk is real fucking cheap. Talkers carry a small penis and have little talent. Look at Anthason. Can you tell me he is just that badass in the ring to make you hate him? No, you can’t. It’s his games that piss you off. Just like all the rest of them. They shy away. Me? Bring your best shot. Heels these days are too quick to run away. I can not remember the last time I picked up my carnival and ran into the sunset as fast as possible. When I speak, my words a like needles, with the ability to pierce your soul. Because if I say I am going to go into row five, seat twelve, and kick whoever sits there’s ass, then you might just take an early leave from the show. That’s not that bad though. If someone else said that, you would laugh it off. They just talk. I follow through. I am a face merely in sheep’s clothing. If I was doing this to Kent Anthason, you know how much you would love me? Or Reed Young? You would jump out of your skin and cheer. You would suck my dick. Because badass Fission just kicked one of the worlds most detestable beings in such a vile and crude fashion that you would morally love it. I am nothing more then what I need to be. You don’t need a hero like me yet. You wouldn’t accept me as I am now. You still remember me as a suicidal, psychotic, masochistic bastard who wanted to prove a point at everyone’s expense. Now? Why should I do the unthinkable and turn on a fiendish core of sick sociopaths who have no goal but to keep their small time fights with the likes of LLB and Christopher Fox, two people I hate because their work ethics in this business are terrible? Because… Um… Because you want to? Shut up.
