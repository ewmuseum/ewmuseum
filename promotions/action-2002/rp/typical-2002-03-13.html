Typical

13 March 2002

Date: 03/13/02 'Ugh...' A familiar sound when you're in a locker room after a match is over. A wrestler is often sore as hell from all of the bumps taken, and he often needs to lie down and do whatever he can to make sure that the soreness goes down. Which was exactly what Joey Malone had to do. It's even worse when you haven't been in active competition in almost two months thanks to feds closing and his getting married. Nice for his life, hell on his wrestling career. He let himself get ouf of shape, and it was definitely killing him as an in-ring performer. He still had the skills necessary to have a good match in the time he's given on television, but he was definitely going to have a problem if he was expected to have an extended match any time soon. What a moron Reed Young is. Joey quietly wondered to himself if Reed had any sort of patience whatsoever. He had heard stories about how Reed organized Action from some of the managers in the locker room. How he had run countless feds before this, and that the decision to start Action had come out of nowhere. Not to mention the fact that Reed didn't want to wait for Joey to get himself back into shape so that he could wrestle better. No, with Reed, he wanted instant results. Ratings. This was going to be a problem if Joey didn't want to become a danger to himself and his fellow wrestlers. Impatience was quite a threatening trait for a wrestling promoter to have. That thought went through Joey's head just as one of Reed Young's coordinators came into his locker room and tapped Joey on his shoulder. Groggilly, Joey stirred out of his thoughts and looked up at the guy. 'What do you want?' he asked. 'Well, sir, Reed was wondering why you didn't wrestle to your full potential tonight.' the coordinator asked. Joey paused to sit up from his bench. 'Has it occured to Reed that I'm out of shape?' Joey inquired. 'Well, he also said that you should've gotten back into shape before you came.' Joey grunted and stood up. At six feet and four inches, Joey was significantly taller than the guy. 'And maybe you should tell 'the boss' that he caught me the *day* I came back from my honeymoon with my wife, and told me to come to Carolina in *three* days. I haven't even been an active wrestler since I left jOlt almost two months ago. Do you really expect me to suddenly get into wrestling shape in the length of time that Reed gave me? I doubt it.' No response. Instead, the guy just turned around and left. Joey shrugged and just assumed that the guy will just tell Reed what he said, and Joey will be out of yet another job. Typical.
