Catching Phrases (Part 1 of 2)

Date: 09/01/02 OPEN “Two pwease.” “There’s only one of you sir.” “Heto!? Pikachu at nine fecking o’cock!” Title: Catching Phrases Date: 08/31/02 8:00 pm EST Place: Carnival Fox “Look, I need two tickets! Pikachu can’t just enter for free now can he?” Ticket Master “Yes he can, he’s not a living thing that has values or even a job!” PIKACHU HAS A JOB! And if you don’t shut up I’ll rip your head off and ice cream down your neck! Fox “Oh, I’m sorry for the mix up sir. Thank you!” Oh, oh.... you “really” told him off Chris! Pussy! Chris hopped out of line and pushed with all his might to turn the attendance wheel to enter. Scuff, scuff, scuff. Christopher Fox had finally made it to the carnival! Fox “Well Pikachu, let’s go have some fun.... BIG TIME!” Fox walks around the merchandise area and checks out all the ladies’ legs, and then with his head down he walks right into someone’s waist! Guy “Hey! What do you think you’re doing?” Other Guy “Get him Bubba!” Fox looks up with puppy dog eyes and a small little spot starting to form on his pants. Fox “Ummmm, are those latex?” Guy “Is what latex?” Oh no, so this isn’t Richard Simmons.... but damn what a bod! Ha! How could I say such a thing! I’m not gay! Isn’t that right Pikachu!? Fox “Pikachu? Isn’t that right!” .... Bubba “Ha, look at this guys! The kid has a toy! Yoink!” Bubba snatches Pikachu from the weak right hand of Fox. Now only if Chris had Pikachu in the left hand, man you’d need a tank to open that one. After waving Pikachu high above his head, Bubba places the yellow fellow on his hair, which of course, is too high for our hero. Jumping and missing, Chris isn’t about to give up yet. He just got Pikachu back from the Grand Canyon, dammit! Fox “Hey you big bully! I’ve been watching Dragonball Z.... so don’t make me do a move on you!” Bubba just ignored Fox as he kept on talking with his friends. Fox then walked all the way across the pedestrian street and made everyone in between him and Bubba clear a path. Fox “Ok Bubba, you asked for tit!” Some people actually ran off after Fox said that. I think it was the tit part that scared them the most. Chris tucked his eyes closed and began his secret move. Fox “Ka......... Mae........... Ha................ Mae................ HA!!!!!!!!!” Fox lashed his hands out in a forward motion. He then slowly opened his right eye, followed by his left. Total silence, not a single sound.... until.... ‘Cough, cough’ ‘Creek, creek.’ The two sounds of a man coughing and a cricket could be heard. Those obviously meaning that something didn’t happen. Why is everyone staring at me? And why is Bubba still there? Fox “Whoa, did it go too fast!?” Bubba turned and looked strangely at Fox. Bubba “Dude, you must be on crack or something. Here! Take your freakin’ toy and scram!” Before Fox could tune himself into what Bubba was saying, ‘Kid’ tried another move. Fox “Oh sure so you’re big and stro-- SOLAR FLARE!!!!!!!!!!!!!” Fox turned and put his hands up beside his face, as Pikachu came and hit him square in the kisser. The crowd got a rise from that as Fox was sent back, almost into the roller coaster line! The crowd went among their business again, but as for Chris he felt like crying, because Pikachu got some dirt on himself! Fox “Are you ok, Pika peek?” “Hey, he’ll be alright!” Fox looks up in amazement and sees.... nothing?! “See he’s fine.” He whips his head to left and sees Joey Malone talking with another person. Malone “I’m sure your son will be alright on the ride.” Man “Oh well thank you Mr. Malone for building up my sons confidence.” Malone “Hey, just call me Joey.” The scene turned back to Fox as a grin slowly curled onto his face! Hey, it’s Karl Mailman Malone! I work with that guy! Hmmmm, doesn’t he have a wife? Okay, then why is he flirting with that man!? Clearly Malone was just chatting with this man, but in Chris’ eyes it was a different story! Enraged with fury, Fox stashed Pikachu in his pocket and ran over to Joey. Fox “Hey! What do you think you’re doing Karl? You’re married! You just can’t go and flirt with any guy you like!” Man “Oh, well sssssorry girlfriend! I was just trying to be prim and proper!” Fox turned and looked at the other man. Fox “Excuse me?” Karl “Yesssssss, my name is Karl and I wasn’t trying to pick up your friend, sicko! C’mon sssssson let’sssssssss go bobbing for bull nuts!” The man grabbed his kid from the ride exit and raced off into the distance. Fox “Whew, now it’s just me and you Karl!” Malone “My name isn’t Karl, Chris!” Fox “So your name is Karl-Chris?” Joey turned and looked back at Fox. He blinked. Malone “Walk with me....” Joey started to walk as Fox followed beside. Scuff, scuff, that was Fox. Click, click, that was Malone. Fox “I’ve never walked with someone before! Well, if you count my Mommy then I have!” Malone “Christopher, listen! I’m not a basketball player!” Fox rubbed his nose and looked at Joey. Fox “Karl -- wait homie, you’re not black!” Sighing, Malone stopped and took a seat on a bench. Fox then jumped down beside his new friend. Malone “Well you could say that....” Fox “Ok.... you’re not black.... BIG TIME!!!” Malone “Huh....” Joey sat back and looked at the beautiful stars.... Malone “.... Big.... Time.... ?” Yeah bitch, I said big time! Malone “That’s making me think.....” ? I mean.... Fox “?” That’s better. People walked back and forth as some of them dropped their garbage off in the bin beside the bench. A short moment between Joey and Chris seemed like eternity as finally Joey blinked and regained his composure. Malone “How does it feel to have people react to those two words?” Fox “Ummm, good! I create all types of words, like the other day I made this word up, it’s lol! Get it?” Malone “Heh. Yeah I get it. Hey Chris.... ?” Fox “Yeah Jody.” Fox said trying his hardest to pronounce Joey’s name right. It was tough. How many people are named Joey after all? That’s right. Malone “Let’s go on some rides.” Malone looked over to Fox and gave him a thumbs up, but Fox knew something was up. Joey then rose from the bench and started walking to a coaster called “Loop Catcher.” Of course Fox put two and two together to make nineteen and ran after Joey who was already in line. Fox “Hey Joey, I know it! You need a catch phrase!” Malone “Err, uh, n-o I don’t....” Joey then scratched the back of his head. Fox “I know you do! Why else would you be all gay when I said big time and why else would you pick a ride that has the word catch in it? Face it, you want to be like me and have a simple line that everyone will know! I’ll help you get it, too.... BIG TIME!” Malone looked down at Fox with sparkles in his eyes. Malone “R-Really? I mean, no I don’t need one, it’d just be nice to have something I’d say often.” Fox “C’mon, I’m helping you.... --” The people in line “BIG TIME!!” Fox grabbed the arm of Malone and pulled him out of line, only to look back at the crowd. Fox “YOU GUYS WATCH TOO MUCH TV!” Christopher then whispered something Malone couldn’t hear. Fox “Thanks guys, the checks are in the mail.” And with a wink from Fox the scene faded to black. CLOSE
