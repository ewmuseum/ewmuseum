Reflections- The Injury [4]

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It seemed that ‘Lionheart’ Tyler Babylon was going to, once again, defy what people were telling him. There was still another month until Babylon was supposed to make his return and here he was, going through the last parts of his workout for the day. If there weren’t any problems, Babylon would be able to start working some house shows for Action! next week. There was only problem. Lindsay. It had been nearly two months since the injury and Babylon had spent every day with Lindsay. If he came back to the ring, he would still be traveling with Lindsay, but the chance of being alone with her was very unlikely. If she wasn’t with Tyler, she would be laughing with some of the other wrestlers, where others would try and hit on her. If Babylon could stay off the road, even if it was just for a few more weeks, he was sure that there would be some kind of future between the two of them. Once again, he was met with another challenge that he would have to overcome. Lying to Lindsay and than telling her the truth. It turned out to be two tough challenges for Babylon. These would potentially be things that he would have to live with for the rest of his life. He had been thinking of this, ever since the doctors told him he could possibly be back in the ring real soon. Now, it was time to make a decision. He was in the ring with Jacques LaFleur, ‘The French Flower.’ A kid that Babylon himself had recommended to the crew at Action! to possibly work with in the future. Action! had kept their eyes on for the past few months. The 6’2, 230 pounder was one of hell of a technician; with a strong move set of suplexes. He picked up a downed ‘Lionheart’ and brought him up to his feet. He quickly had Babylon in a front face lock and quickly sent Babylon back down to the mat with a snap Suplex. ‘OH SHIT!’ Babylon screamed as soon as he hit the canvas. He immediately rolled on to his stomach and clutched onto back. ‘Get ze hell in here.’ LaFleur called in his heavy French accent as a few people quickly hurried into the ring. ‘What happened?’ A doctor who was watching over asked, as he was the first to reach Babylon. ‘It’s my back,’ Babylon cried out, ‘I can’t move.’ ‘We’re going to get you to the hospital. Make sure everything is okay and that you’re not seriously hurt. If you are, there’s a chance you won’t be back in the ring for a long time, maybe even never again.’
