Invisibility - V

11 April 2002

Date: New Part: 04/11/2002 August 10th, 1990 (ten minutes later) Mesa High School: Mesa, Arizona, United States Keri(the older one) and Bonham walked into the empty hallway where Joey had taken his newfound friend. 'And over here is 'Mr. Wizard's' office. We like to call him that.' Joey said, waving a hand toward the room that would lead to where the biology classes are held. 'Ah, I see.' Keri commented. Joey turned back to her. 'Should be everything. You shouldn't get lost, now.' he said, smiling. 'If you do, indeed, get lost, I'm always here. Usually. Unless I'm sick. Then you're in trouble.' Keri giggled. 'I'll be okay.' They looked into each other's eyes again. There seemed to be something that connected with the two of them. The two of them weren't really sure how to express their feelings, but in a moment, they wouldn't have to, anyway. 'There you are!' a voice from across the hall said, belong to Daniel Phillips. Joey broke out of his gaze into Keri's eyes for a moment to turn toward him. 'Daniel. What's going on?' Daniel didn't seem all that happy. 'Dude, you just ditched me. What were you doing, anyway?' Daniel asked, before he turned and saw Keri. 'Oh, I see what's going on. Meh.' Joey paused, and looked over at Keri, who was looking rather confused. It hit Joey as to what Daniel was talking about, and he quickly turned back to him. 'I think you're getting the wrong idea, here. I was just showing her around the school.' 'Whatever, man.' Daniel said. He pulled Joey out of Keri's earshot and spoke in a whispered tone. 'So, what do you intend to do with her? Worms in the hair or a squirrel in the locker?' Joey blinked. 'Nothing. Okay? I don't want anything to happen to her.' he said. 'And if you happen to do anything, then I'm going to kick your ass. Are we clear?' Daniel thought about it. After all, Joey had grown about four inches over the summer break and he was now quite taller than Daniel was. He probably could have kicked Daniel's ass. 'Crystal.' Daniel amended himself a moment later. 'I just don't see why you're going after her. I don't think she's in your league, man.' Joey shrugged and walked back toward, before whispering to himself. 'A man's gotta do what a man's gotta do.' The bell rang to indicate the end of lunch. There was a flood of humanity coming through the halls, but they wouldn't get the chance, because the memory froze at this time. Bonham spoke up. 'When Joey met you, he sort of developed a crush on you, and you felt the same way. When Daniel suggested to pull a prank on her, it was here that Joey realized that staying as a prankster wasn't going to get him real far. He grew up.' Bonham said. 'Because of you. And from here on, Joey devoted more time toward helping you out and getting you used to Mesa High. Daniel resented him for that. After all, his best friend was slowly losing interest in the thing that drew them together, and he spending time with 'some new girl'.' 'I did that for him?' 'Yes.' Keri scratched her head. 'I don't remember this.' Bonham chuckled. 'You'll figure it out.' He then turned around. 'Come. We are going to the next memory.' Bonham walked into the crowd and disappeared. Keri shrugged, before she followed him out of this memory, and into the next.
