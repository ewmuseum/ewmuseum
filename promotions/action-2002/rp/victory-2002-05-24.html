Date: 05/24/02 Victory The essense of winning, of defeating an opponent. You are choosen into a contest where you must face an adversary, one who wishes to challenge you. You have two choices in the matter of this contest...to win, or to lose. You have the choice, your fate rests in your hands. Do you wish to train? Or do you wish to be cocky? Do you wish to fight? Or do you wish to wrestle? The final battle arises, where to warriors must enter into one final battlefield. A battlefield stained with blood, controversy and hate. A battlefield filled with passion. These warriors who fight through the stains of crimson blood, and power through the controversy, and fight with one thing driving them to go on...the hatred for one another. And in the end...there is only one victor. There is only one Victory. ------------------------------------------------------------------- I won't let you win Bobby. I can't let you win. This is MY war, and although you have pulled some surprises...it doesn't change the fact that this is MY war. I created this war, I placed the feul on the proverbial fire. I made this war the hated fight that is it...and only I shall win it. With every step he took, he intentions grew more insane. He looked down upon the sleeping warrior near him. It was the dead of night, you could tell by the silence of the dark. Only the shadows could be seen in the staring moon light that cracked through the spaces between the blinds. And there he lay... Bobby Minio. You are a fool for thinking I would remain down because of injury. You dare to mock me by resting, RESTING? You dare to rest, as if nothing was wrong in this world? As if YOU were going to defeat ME? You are either insulting my strength, or you are just completely ignorant. But it doesn't matter to me Minio...I shall send you a warning...I shall make sure you are awake every single night before our final battle... KroW, hidden behind the dark shadows, crept his way closer and closer to the sleeping warrior known as Bobby Minio. KroW could taste the flesh and blood already, he could see the hurt and pain already, and he smiled in joy at the mere thoughts. He imagined Minio's corpse laying in the center of the room, blood trickling from his open neck. He imagined Minio's insides finally introduced to the outside world, being spilled for all to see. And he imagine himself, bathing in Minio's own blood, licking the remnants of what once was...Minio. And the mere thought of it all...made him smile. KroW finally had reached the edge of the bed, and nothing else around him mattered...all he cared about was the figure that laid asleep. KroW looked down, and slowly stroked the right cheek of Bobby Minio with his forefinger. KroW slowly licked the finger, and smiled, as thoughts raced through his mind once more. The taste of sweet flesh is symbolic in a way. Notice at how smooth his skin is before the final encounter, notice how there is not one hint of blood, not one scar...nothing. His face is perfectly clean. Its very symbolic indeed...for it represents what one looks like BEFORE his most ultimate challenge. KroW slowly placed his left hand down upon the shoulder of the sleeping Bobby Minio, KroW patted his shoulder, smiling sadistically at his prey. That smooth face of yours...that shining hair...that glimmer in your eye...it will all fade away. The final battle nears closer and closer. Every minute, every second, every milisecond...I thrive the battle to come...and it will. I may have been down for awhile, but I shall return....just for you. And I will show you....what a true battle is all about KroW took his hand off of Minio's shoulder, and slowly reached into his black denim jeans pocket. His face suddenly went straight, and his eyes went wide with insanity. He searched around his pocket, as he stared down upon his prey. His face resembled one of a savage beast before it leaped upon its victim. It resembled animals such as a tiger, a lion, or a bear. It resembled the face of men like Adolf Hitler, or Stalin. It resembled a heartless beast with cruel intentions. Suddenly, his hand had found what he was looking for. He stroked the pointy edge of the object with his pointer finger. He could feel his warm blood slowly breaking free from his human flesh and trickling into his pocket. He slowly rolled his finger down the spikey edge of the object and finally reached the rubbery grip. KroW's face remained still, as he slowly brought object out of the stuffy pocket, and into the free air of the outside. The moon light shimmered off the object, as KroW brought it up to his face to observe it. He brought the object to his face, and licked the edge of it.... His face remained still as he felt the trickle of blood run down his bare tongue, which now sported a gash that gushed out crimson blood. But KroW didn't move...he felt no pain...it was only pleasure. He looked down at Minio and brought the object to his side...the object...was a knife. The knife glimmered once more in the moon light, as KroW rested the object slowly upon the side of Bobby Minio's neck. Poor fool. Poor ignorant fool. Resting, while his life is ready to end. This war will end Minio, I feel maybe it should end sooner. Maybe you shouldn't be given the chance to defend yourself. Maybe your pathetic body should just be slashed to tiny bits, and allowed to rot in the night's sky! Maybe I should end you now Minio, maybe I should end you now! KroW's hand began to shake in excitement as he brought the knife closer to the neck of Minio. But his face remained still, as Hitler would look while holding a gun to the skull of an innocent jewish man. The face was expressionless...so what if Minio died at the hands of KroW? It would just be another waste of life thrown into the heavens....it would make the world better....it would rid the world of just another weakling. KroW finally felt the time was near. He brought the knife to his side, and readied himself to jab into Minio....he could already feel Minio's blood run down his hand and onto the wooden floor of the room. KroW could already see the final look in Minio's eyes as he feel into the hands of the heavens. He wanted to make sure Minio saw his own death...because KroW was his death...he wanted to be the last thing Minio saw...he wanted to be Minio's own personal death! He finally felt the time was here, and sent his positioned hand into Minio. KroW's eyes closed upon impact, he always loved the surprise of the look of his dead victims. He loved how John Headcase looked in WWL as he died. He loved how Chris Thunder looked in dW as he was thrown into the heavens. He loved how Extremist looked in jOlt, as he was stabbed to death. He loved the death of others....but this time...he wanted to be surprised... And he was. He felt no warm trickle of blood run down his hand....he felt no pain corsing through Minio's bad...he felt no death...but most importantly... he felt no knife. KroW's eyes opened in complete shock, as he looked down at his hand....to see no weapon. His eyes went wide, and his jaw dropped in horror, as he looked across to where his arm was to see Minio standing up in front of him...the knife now in his grasp. KroW looked directly at Minio's face....Minio was grinning insanely, holding KroW's knife near him.... Suddenly... The blood. The pain. The death. It all rose up...in KroW himself. He felt the cold edge of the knife being dug into his chest, as Minio rammed the knife in deep, he wanted KroW to suffer, he wanted KroW to bleed...he wanted KroW to die. KroW looked up at MInio's insane eyes in shock, as he coughed up a puddle of blood from the pain. Minio laughed a bit at KroW's pain as he whispered into KroW's ear one last thing... 'I'm glad...I'm the last image you ever see' KroW's legs grew weak, and his vision became blurry. The night was becoming even darker, and his pain was slowly going away. He felt lighter, and his mind became more freer. But his mental anguish was not gone...for all he saw was Minio's final gleam in his mind. The last image he would ever see... He felt his heart straining to pump blood through his body, eventhough a cold knife was dug into the center of it. The heart tried to remain strong, pumping with a steel edge pointed in the center of it...but it soon grew weak....pain began to resurface...this time it was only mental. KroW let out one final war cry as he felt his heart pound its last pulses... ba-bum ba-bum b.a.-.b.u.m. b.a.-....... Here lies KroW. Born - ???? Died - May 24th, 2002 R.I.P. ------------------------------------------------- 'FUCK YOU MINIO!!!!' Suddenly he awoke, beads of cold sweat running down his forehead. He was alive, but not living. His eyes were wide, and his mind was filled with anger and rage. His teeth were clenched and fists were tight. Every muscle in his entire body had tightened. He looked around this room he was in....he saw the hospital bed he was in...he saw the bandages wrapped around his arm...and he saw the figure that laid next to him on the other bed....Erik Rave. KroW ran his open hand across his shocked and shaking face, he rubbed the sweat from his brow, as his eyes remained open, staring into the darkness of the night. He couldn't move for some reason, possibly from injuries...or possibly from shock. He looked into the distance, staring into nothing...as he yelled out... 'MINIO!! YOU DARE TO HAUNT ME IN MY DREAMS!! YOU DARE TO TAUNT ME MINIO!! I WILL SHOW YOU!!! YOUR DEMISE WILL COME MINIO!! YOU WILL DIE!!!' ----------------------- 'What are dreams? Some say that dreams are actually visions...of the future. What are they around for? That is why they are there...to warn us of our horrid future.'
