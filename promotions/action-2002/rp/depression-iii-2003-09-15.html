Action! — Tales

Depression. III

15 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It had been an hour. Action Man was waiting patiently for his food to arrive. It was a toasted cheese sandwich for God sake. How long would it take for the chef to make a bloody toasted cheese sandwich? Action Man looked at the ceiling while he waited. Time was really messing with his mind, and now, he was beginning to become just a little ticked off. He looked back to his table, without a bloody idea of what to do next. He had already been through every advertisement of food that the Relaxation Café had attached to the stem of the rotating holder, the one that held the salt and pepper in place - twice. Standing up just then, as Action Man finally had enough and was ready to leave, did the waitress emerge from the kitchen… food in her hand. She smiled at the Action hero. In truth, she had been waiting behind the kitchen door for a rather long time… ready to see how long she could hold off Action Man until he was fed-up. Action Man sat back down in his seat, as she placed the food on the table and stared directly at him. “Was there a problem?” She bickered. “Was there too much of a wait?” The attitude she was sending, was one that Action Man just didn’t want to get into. Indeed he had every right to be mad… but was it really worth it? He wasn’t going to get through to her, anyway. “Oh- no.” He stuttered. “I was just getting up to go- to go to the bathroom.” She knew this was a lie, and Action Man knew that she knew this was a lie, too… nevertheless, the waitress just grinned. “Good. Enjoy.” She walked off, once again swinging her ass around like it was meant to be used as a play-toy. Action Man only groaned as he saw this… and then looked back down at his meal. There it was, his toasted cheese sandwich… burnt around the outside. Action Man had it. But in looking up, and seeing the waitress enter through the kitchen doors with a smile on her face, as she grinned to the other two waitresses that were in there as well… he was not going to let them get the better of him. He looked back down at his food, picked it up… and started to eat. Sick, is what the taste of black toast tastes like. Yet it wasn’t all that bad… or the middle parts at least. They were not heavily burned… yet they, too, were dark enough not to be called a toasted cheese sandwich. Giggling was heard from inside the kitchen, as Action Man just looked up, and somehow he knew that this talk was about him. Of course, it helps to come to that type of conclusion when you see one of their heads sticking out of the door, for a split second or two. Action Man glanced back at his sandwich… and took another bite. “There.” He said after one more mouth-full. “The first half is done.” Action Man’s attention was taken away though; as he saw a teenaged couple enter through the doors at the front of the Cafe. Almost instantly did one of the waitresses emerge from the kitchen, helping them to their seats. Gaining ground towards the back of the room, they saw Action Man in the far-off corner, as the male teenager turned to the waitress. “Can we… possibly sit near the front?” She didn’t catch on at first, but in looking back to see Action Man only a few tables away, she nodded. “Oh yes, please.” She walked them to the front. “What was I thinking?”
