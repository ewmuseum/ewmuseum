Down and Out

18 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 18, 2002 Title: Down and Out Date: July 18, 2002 Time: 4:59 p.m. EST Place: Cracker Barrel Restaurant, Arizona | OPEN | The restaunt is full, actually there is a small line forming at the doors at the other end of the restaurant. Most tables have many people sitting around them. Laughing, talking, just having a good time on this sunny Thursday afternoon. But there is of course the one exception. Just as he always seems to be. Chris Fear, more often than not, is the the exception and not the rule. For Chris is sitting alone in the corner. He has a terribly frightening look on his face. It appears that he is ready to snap. At anytime. In anyplace. On anybody. The look on his face almost makes you believe that pulling a knife on Bobby Minio and driving his ambulance into a ditch was just the beginning. Chris opens the menu. Slowly his hand moves towards the knife on his table. Suddenly he grips the knife tightly and starts banging it on the table! 'Can I get some damn service over here!' Fear screams. Everybody in the restaurant looks at him. He stands up and knocks the table over. 'What the hell are you people looking at!?' he yells, his eyes burning a hole through each and every person trying to enjoy their meal. 'If any of you dumbasses see Bobby Minio, I want you to give him this message! Tell him that he may have won the battle at Til Death Due Us Part, but the war is far from over! Tell him that Pressure Point 19 was just the beginning! And tell him he should probably see a doctor, because he went for quite a ride in that ambulance! Hahaha!!!' After Chris is done his evil rant, he storms out the door. Everybody else is in a shocked silence. Perhaps they are frightened. Or perhaps they have no idea who that crazy guy was. After a few seconds they return to what they were doing before. As if nothing happened. Unfortunately for Bobby Minio, this is all to real. Chris Fear sprints to a red Corvette and jumps in it. He quickly starts the car and speeds onto the highway. As the car disappers into the distance, one has to wonder what lies ahead for Bobby Minio. It appears as if Chris Fear has snapped. He has finally crossed that very fine line he has been walking for many years. Chris Fear no longer seems to belong in our society. A psychiatric hospital maybe, but definately not in an environment where he can cause any more damage. Chris Fear has seemingly finally snapped. And that can not be a good thing for one man in particular. Bobby Minio is in for the fight of his life. Who knows, but this battle may not end until 'Death Does them Part'. It may take the death of one of these men to finally end this insane battle. This battle has been in the making for years. Everybody knew that some day it would come to a head. But this battle is far more brutal than anyone could ever have imagined. Bobby Minio vs. Chris Fear. The former is down and out. The latter is one very scary man. But this war is far from over. | CLOSE |
