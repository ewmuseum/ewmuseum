Date: 7/8/02 Smile.         'I guarantee you've never looked in the mirror and saw a face like this staring back at you. As I peer endlessly into the vast world behind me ... I read the words clearly. It's no longer a blur to me. The meaning of my life is no longer backwards. ‘InSanE' I read it plain as day. Kinda ... worries me, but with a life like this, what's not to worry about.'          He fell back into the inclined sofa and peered. Not blinking, not moving. The raven hair covered his head like a silk blanket, peeking out every so often to take a breath.          'A! Wrestling. How great things are going for me. It's surprising to actually see me win a few wrestling matches. I've not been getting quite the practice that is needed. It's hard to tell who they'll put me up against, considering I knocked down three of the newest wrestlers to step in. I guess, I gave a bit of proof of my existence. Maybe now someone knows where I'm at. It's about damn time! Lifetimes being ignored, and minutes in the spotlight. It's definitely out of proportion.'          The man sit back, once more. Thinking about the pay per view that just past, thought about the life that was nearly taken.          'My match was everything but easy, but I still ended up with my hand raised. Not saying that I didn't get jarred a few times after the match ... I walked away the fuckin' winner that's all that matters. You get nothing with an attack after a fight, at least that's the way I look at it. Shows a weakness in the spotlight.' He slowly lowered his head. Patiently, it came back up. The letters 'Insane' written backwards across his forehead seemed to fit perfectly as he took a long hard glimpse into the mirror. A diminutive portion of the room slowly began to appear hazy. The steam rose sluggishly, not bothering the individual too much. 'The time is at hand. Publicity is at its peak. If I don't have what it takes to bring in the beating, then there is no point for me to attempt to wrestle here. I've got to continue at the pace I'm going in order to establish myself. My past is ... not important at this time. When there is more of a chance, I'll fill the whole world in, but what they don't know won't hurt them.'          The mirror was completely glazed over. A minute layer of dew persistently continued to takeover the reflective glass. Portion by portion was taken over ... only the extensive objects in the background could be located. He ran his finger hesitantly across the glossy surface, trying to gather piece by piece the surroundings and the actions. His life was like that mirror, all foggy, except at one point in time ... he could take a swipe and discover more and more.          'Wrestling is the fuel. The motivation to live another day. If it wasn't for the things that I look forward to in the wrestling ring ... I'd probably be in the same situation as Syphon Fission. It ... would go to that extent. He played his cards right and may have another day to life ... I'm the Joker in the deck. There are only two ... but it's gone. I had the pleasure of removing the other one. The Joke is over...'          The man broke out into a sense of deep laughter. After a few seconds, it immediately stopped. He peered into the open line across the mirror. Seconds later, shattered. Shards and edge remains filtered their way across the room as his fist was swiftly placed through the center of the mirror. A trickle of blood was all that could be portrayed from the scattered remains of the glass. On the floor, rested a piece of glass. Peacefully, it had the reflection of Joker staring ... eyeing it grimly with a smile across his face.
