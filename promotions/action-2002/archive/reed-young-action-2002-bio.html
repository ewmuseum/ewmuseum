Reed Young Biography Nicknames 'Your Boss!' Hometown New York, New York Birthday December 17th, 1976 Height 6' 3'' Weight 233 Federation Awards Owner of the best, Fed, EEEVVVEEERRR!! Biography Failure is Reed Young. Money is Reed Young. Reed Young is Reed Young. It seems as though no one else has come close to the persona that this man carries. He’s a moron. If you could think of the prefect business man... well, Reed Young would NOT be him. Hell, he’d be on the BOTTOM of the list. Or even lower if that’s possible. Money is the only thing that keeps Reed high. He’s ran over 30 Federations (yep, that many) and he’s closed them all... some due to fair reasons, but after the 5th Fed went under... all of them got stupid. He wants one thing, and one thing only. Fame. Ratings = Fame. Sure, laugh and call him pathetic... but with money he can do anything. He had come from a rich family after all... and so far, this time, it may just work. His best friend since Kindergarten, Roland Ermann, stood beside him for some time, a man hell bent on having Action Wrestling become his new Courtroom. Although their friendship has folded, if Young wanted to close AW now... This time it seems that he couldn’t. For he’s getting EXACTLY what he wants. Action Wrestling is growing. Young’s quest to the *very* top will no doubt be hard and very trying. It’s been proven Young cannot take that... his weak and easy to sway mind doesn’t do him much good... fWo. the Asylum. 21w. WWEWCWECW2K1 Fed. How can Young keep up with all these places? His track record already cost him some of the best talent, even for all the money in the world. Or maybe, just maybe the asshole will be able to overcome everything... making Action the best possible Fed... EVERRRRRR. /Valley Girl Yeah, and the CFL will expand to the United States again.
