Pressure Point 54 The Pyramid — Memphis, Tennessee ---- PRESSURE POINT. PRESSURE POINT. A Short Lived Return. Starring: KroW & 'Showcase' Sean Cylear. The fans were quite excited inside the arena tonight, not only for the usual Action Wrestling program called Pressure Point, but for something else. In the wrestling business, everyone who watches or is apart of the business knows that you always expect the unexpected. At Make It or Break It the unexpected went down, and the fans would go wild for it. Pressure Point didn’t start out with the usual pyrotechnic show, where the fans would stir and go wild, but rather the Action Screen would come alive. Clips of the Pay Per View came forth, and suddenly the ending to the Main Event popped up, and the fans currently in the arena began to cheer wildly. The clips soon died down to darkness, and the fans continued to stir with excitement. It was semi-silent, until the lights dimmed down. Immediately, the fans blew up in a wave of cheers as a very familiar theme song came forth from the PA System. A theme song that hadn’t been heard since September of 2002, a theme song that no one thought they would ever hear again. “Thoughtless” by Korn. The lights remained dimmed and the fans remained bursting out in cheers, and even more so when an instant boom of fire came bursting from the top of the stage and standing amongst the smoke was the man that no one thought they would ever see in an Action Wrestling arena again. The arena’s roof nearly blew off, as the Satanic God stood at the top of the ramp, allowing a bright red spotlight to shine upon him. He didn’t move an inch; he just stood in one spot and slowly surveyed the crowd that he hadn’t seen in about half a year. The former PIW World Champion finally began to make his way to the ring; now staring at nothing but the squared circle in front of him. He slowly made his way down the ramp, the music still blaring and the sounds of cheers still as loud as ever, as the Satanic God named KroW finally reached the ringside area. He leaped upon the apron and looked at the logo in the center of the squared circle, the logo of Action Wrestling. KroW sneered in a disgusted way as he grabbed the top rope and flipped over the rope and into the ring. It had been six months since KroW had been in an Action Wrestling ring, and it the sensation still felt the same to him, as he let out a small grin at the fans and raised a single fist above his head. KroW was soon handed a microphone, and upon grasping the microphone, the lights returned to full luminosity as the music died down. The cheers from the crowd overcame the Satanic God, as he closed his eyes and allowed himself to soak up the fanfare, something he hadn’t been around since the closure of the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling. Many say that they aren’t in the business for the fans, and KroW was one of them, but the excitement of the crowd never hurt anyone. He slowly opened his eyes and raised the microphone to his mouth, the cheers were deafening. “Seven months ago, I grabbed my bags, I packed up my gear, and I left this business by telling the entire company to take their roster spot and stick down their throats,” he said rather harshly as he stood in the exact same place, continuing to stare at the Action logo beneath his feet, “And up to this day, I regret doing that. I regret taking my resignation, I regret taking that final path down the ramp, and I regret turning my back on Action Wrestling” “But every reason I have for regretting that…you won’t like” He suddenly looked up at the crowd, who went silent with curiosity, and stared blankly at them all. His voice was just as dark as ever, and his voice was filled with much more emotion (mainly a mixture of anger and sorrow). “I regret leaving Action for these following reasons. I regret leaving because I didn’t finish my battle with LLB and didn’t prove to the world that he is NOTHING compared to me,” the fans gave a very mixed reaction to this, but many were actually cheers for the Satanic God. “Another reason was because I never did what I said I would do…and that was to rid the wrestling world of a man called Reed Young…” this received a very positive reaction from the crowd. “And the final reason I regret leaving was only two months after I had left. A certain face suddenly popped up from a grave that I had thought I buried him in. A man that the world thought I rid from the world had returned…conveniently when I was gone. What’s his name, you ask? Sean Cylear.” The fans immediately blew up in cheers as KroW just snarled in disgust at the mere mention of his sworn enemy, “It was in the year of two thousand, that I faced Sean Cylear in jOlt Wrestling, and it was in that very match where I forced Cylear into a wheel chair and forced him to become a fucking vegetable!” His voice got louder by the second. “And when I believed the coward was dead and buried next to men like Extremist and Chris Thunder, I see him return…when I’m not there. I believe it’s quite funny on how great the timing was on behalf of Cylear. I was in Action for nearly six months, and in that entire time, he never thought of coming back…when I was still around. No, he waited until I left, so he could pick apart a much weaker enemy in LLB. Oh, how little Cylear knows who I truly am. For you see, when my target is set, I do NOT let it get away…I just wait, and allow my prey’s attention to pass by me…and than, I attack” The fans could only cheer wildly for the Satanic God, as he grunted beneath his voice. KroW was ready to continue speaking, to explain more, but just as he raised the microphone to his mouth again, a familiar theme came over the PA system. The theme song was none other than “At the Drive In”, by Arc Arsenal. KroW’s eyes immediately went wide with rage as he stared up at the entrance ramp and saw his prey make his way from behind the curtain. Sean Cylear had arrived. Much different from the previous week, Sean Cylear seemed unafraid of KroW. KroW, a man who nearly ended Cylear’s career, stood at a firm stance as he stared blankly at Sean Cylear, who made it his own choice to stay at the top of the stage. Sean Cylear, complete with his red sunglasses and his street attire, brought a microphone from the back of his denim slacks. He took another look at KroW, before looking over the crowd, and than returning to KroW once more; a grin plastered over his arrogant face. “KroW, can I ask you a question?” KroW didn’t respond, he just continued to give the same cold stare to Cylear that he did just minutes ago, that he did at Make it or Break It. The fans looked on, awaiting for something to happen, but all KroW did was throw his microphone aside and clench his fists. “I’ll take that as a yes. Well, my question is this…what the hell do you think you’re doing in that ring?” Cylear asked, “I mean, only Action Wrestling superstars are supposed to be in that ring, and you currently are not an Action Wrestling superstar. KroW immediately grunted loudly as he stomped to the ropes and quickly flipped over the top rope and to the outside. Sean Cylear’s confidence and cockiness suddenly disappeared, as KroW began to stomp around the ring and towards the entrance ramp. “Whoa, stop right there you freak!” KroW didn’t listen, his eyes remained glued on Cylear as he stomped his way up the ramp, now just about fifty feet away from The Showcase. “I’m warning you KroW, stop RIGHT THERE!” Again the Satanic God refused to listen, as the crowd began to stir with excitement, as KroW got closer and closer to Sean Cylear. The fans begged for KroW to just wail on everyone and attack Cylear, but KroW knew that it wouldn’t get him far. If he were to be arrested, his revenge on Cylear wouldn’t be successful, and in the end Cylear would win. With that fresh in his mind, KroW slowly lowered his arms and allowed the officers to escort him up the ramp. KroW looked over at Cylear and snarled at the grinning Cylear. The grin on Cylear’s face would soon disappear as KroW tilted his head backward and spat a large amount of saliva into the face of Sean Cylear. Cylear grunted and began to carry on like a little child throwing a temper tantrum as Cylear began to pace on the stage, fuming with anger as he spoke. “KroW, you.are.FUCKING.lucky you aren’t a superstar, because if you would, I’d take your ass on and kick the living hell out of you…in fact, I think I’ll show you exactly how I’d kick your ass,” Cylear snorted out, “Because tonight, I’ll take a victim of my choosing…and KroW, I’ll give you a BIG example as to how I’d beat you down like the dog you are.
