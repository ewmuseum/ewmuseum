Kayden Paulton Biography Nicknames 'The Silver Jet'. Hometown Dothan, Alabama Birthday January 1st, 1978 Height 6' 4'' Weight 266 Biography I try. I figure you can’t get everything in life. I mean... it is called life. If you got everything, what does that leave the other people with? Hi, I’m Kayden Paulton. In my world... we should all just get along. And sure, yeah, I understand the mere thought of that might sound funny... but is it really? A bad day never gets to me... because I don’t know what the word bad means. No doubt it’d be nice to have a wonderful and loving wife, true to blue friends... a corvette, and all the other odds and ends that make life even richer. I could dwell on it. I could get upset when these things do not go my way. But hey, I’m a young kid... I’m not sick, I’m not dieing. I’m living. And I’m going to make the best possible outcomes from that. Sure... I don’t have a lot of things. But sure... my life doesn’t depend on them either. Because this life... is just worth living exactly as it is. Hi again, I’m Kayden Paulton... and welcome to the ultimate journey that we people live, day in... and day out. Wrestling Style Technical Finisher Move 'In Color' Finisher Description Pearl River Plunge Special Moves Swinging Neck Breaker, High Elevation Cross Body Block, Low Cross Body Block (tosses self into a ball and at the opponent’s knees), Three Point Tackle, High Angle Superkick, Powerful Hip Toss (sending opponent at least half way across the ring), Flying Elbow Drop (long hang time), and a Scissorkick.
