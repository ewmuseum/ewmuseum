Giovanni Ollivander DeNucci Biography Nicknames He was often referred to as Gio, Ollie, Vand, or some even took the G.O.D. abbreviation. Hometown Born in Florence, Italy. Lived just outside Dublin, Ireland most of his childhood and early teens. Settled in Orlando, Florida with his parents around sixteen. Birthday The Seventeenth Day of the Fourth Month of the Eighty-Fourth Year in the 20th Century. Height Five-Feet, Seven Inches. Weight One-Hundred Seventy-Eight Pounds. Biography Some things to know about Giovanni: - He was born on April 17th, 1984 in Florence, Italy while his parents were visiting relatives. - His father is part Italian, part American. His father was Italian and his mother was American. - Giovanni's mother is part Irish, part Canadian, and part Japanese. Her mother was Japanese, while her father was an Irish-Canadian. - Giovanni has family in Japan, Ireland, Italy, Canada, United States, and England(but they never speak of them). - Giovanni spent most of his years in Dublin, Ireland, where a lot of his mother's family was. - He also goes by GOD(his initials), Ollie, Gio, and Van(d). - His father, Montgomery Ollivander DeNucci, is an billionaire international businessman. It isn't important what he does. - Giovanni was trained by Francisco Mungro, who is part African, part Japanese, but lived his entire life in America. - Giovanni does not believe in God(not from any religion). He thinks of himself as God. - He's a smart-ass, with an Irish accent. - He can speak Japanese, Italian, English, and Irish(it's English, but slightly mangled). - He was taught Karate and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu by Francisco, along with dabblings in other styles. - Is a slacker. - His family moved to Orlando, Florida when Ollie was 16, which is where he completed high school. - Has worked a few MMA tournaments and has very little professional wrestling experience(read: none). - Works stiff.
