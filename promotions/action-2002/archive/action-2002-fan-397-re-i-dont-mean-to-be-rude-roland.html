Re: I dont mean to be rude (Roland) Posted by Roland [detail withheld] on October 10, 2002 at 21:16:23: In Reply to: Re: I dont mean to be rude (Roland) posted by Joseph on October 10, 2002 at 20:57:43: : Well now, what it means, most likely, is that you haven't been accepted this go round. They've already begun phasing in the new wrestlers in the pressure points.. So, unless Roland has been extremely busy.. Sick. Busy. All in one. Yes, we've had some people come in, but, like I've said... I haven't done reading all of them... and, I will bring in a few more. : Don't worry too much though, I'm in the same boat as you, I think...unless I somehow got an email and accidently deleted it... Damn 30 spam emails a day... Like I'm really going to buy smokers insurance, or am interested in an internet credit card! I bought one yesterday. =:) : Anyways, apps will open in a couple months, mabye even late november, early december, etc. : Um, yeah, so... : *Runs off into the sunset, chasing the walking cactus from the Taco Time commercials* : ~Joseph Well, that's true. I like to keep apps open like that, because, I DO see a lot of good things... but, then, I DO see characters that just don't fit. And I'm not about to eMail someone who's writing is good, but I want a different character. I may, oddly do that once or twice... but I don't know what people want. In truth, and I say this as honest as possible... what I've read, I've liked. ::Shrugs:: Some of it I don't feel fits exactly into what I'm looking for. And I'm not basing this as you Joe, or you Haze... right now, with my horrible cough I'm even forgetting if I read your apps or not. But, take it for whatever it's worth, and this is to anyone reading this message. If you don't get accepted, try again. Unlike the first time I ran through applications, there hasn't been one where I cannot say "this... doesn't have talent". 'Cause they all do. Cheers, - Roland <--- again, isn't done mailing. <--- is, though, trying to do the best he can. I have football, school, eW work, school... and God knows what else too. =:)
