Pressure Point 8 Joe Louis Arena — Detroit, Michigan ---- PRESSURE POINT. 04/24/02 PRESSURE POINT. 04/24/02 Warning. The show started outside of Joe Lewis Arena in Detroit as the pouring rain came down hard. The Crowd cheered, viewing the Video Screen with ‘Action!’, as the shot scanned from street to street. But even before the startup to Pressure Point Eight would start… a Limo had pulled up. The door opened from inside as Christopher Fox skipped out, jumping up and down. He saw the camera, and smiled at it. “Fuck you… BIG TIME!” He screamed, the Fans’ jeering in return towards their former hero. Fox held the door back… and… The Action! Champion, Chris Fear stepped out giving Fox a high five. Next… LLB. Standing tall, ‘The Law & Order’ gave a determined look towards both of his team mates’. Fox slammed the door, and the three began to walk into the Arena until LLB grabbed the camera, pulling his face right into the lens. “Tonight…” He stated sharply. “Tonight Bobby Minio will get his verdict.” And laughing, LLB motioned to Fear and Fox who stood behind him. “It’s a shame… because just like Shawn Steele… the verdict won’t be handed by us.” He pushed the Cameraman back into the wall as the scene went fuzzy… the sounds of only laughter left to hear. ---- Segment The Making. It has been a week in the making. Pressure Point 8, no matter what the main event would be, the show was all focused on him. Last week, he was screwed and snapped in such a violent manner that Reed Young would apologized for the content. It was something you would see from the Asylum. It did give Action! the edge in the ratings, however. No matter what the women’s rights activists said, no matter what the wrestling in the backstage thought, the vision down the road was clear. Fission and Malone would hit head on again. This time, it would be even more violent. Many Fans’ were coming out. They were buying tickets for ‘Juggernaut’, a Pay-Per-View going up against an epic federations premier event. Action! was taking on 21w in the ratings and were fighting for fans. Reed Young owed it all to Fission and Malone’s match, in many respects. The other names were great. But Fission and Malone sold seats as cold-blooded enemies. They treated us to conflict worthy of a Pay-Per-View last week. Now, Action! was reaping the benefits. Just before the show came to focus, Fission entered the building. He was in street clothes, and carrying a red handled shovel. He never stopped looking at the ground. ---- Segment Answers that are Questioned.
