And Fear jumped, hitting a corkscrew moonsault! But his only connection was with the mat. Everyone by now stood, as LLB, waiting in the corner looked to pull the 'Erroneous Conclusion' out from the testify next. And he did. But then something else became erroneous in itself. The outcome? Maybe. But no doubt a turn in the road that wasn't seen. Reed Young, and that lead pipe in hand. Young looked on at his friend LLB, who was just seconds away from winning the Title. But walking right on the apron, Young still tossed LLB that pipe. "Take him OUT! You've got this won. Now END him!" He commanded as LLB picked the pipe out of the air. It felt soft to him though. It wasn't lead, that's for sure. Pushing it together in his hands, LLB spoke… "This isn't lead!" He screamed, raged at Young for wasting his time. "It's not?" Reed replied. Shawn Steele ran out, and the people went crazy. Steele levelled Young with a dropkick, then getting on the apron, taking LLB's neck and hanging it on the ropes. LLB fell back, and Fear… Small packaged him. One. Two. … Three! The bell went. And in shock, a Referee came down, slid into the ring, handing Chris Fear the very first Action Championship Title Belt. Steele danced up the ramp, happy he had outsmarted Young again. LLB got up. But he didn't hit Fear; he didn't do anything towards him. LLB just ran up the ramp, in an attempt to catch Shawn, who was the reason he wasn't the Champion right now. Fear hit the turnbuckle, holding the Title up in the air, the Crowd giving him a mixed reaction as Pressure Point Three's Credits rolled, and the show came to a close. The verdict for this night? Reed Young was the stupidest man of them all. Outcome: Chris Fear via Pinfall & First Action Champion. ---- Credits Credits Results by Rowe The Ringer vs. Maverick Montresor by Tomer © Action!
