Players and Pawns: The Law vs. The Master

December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The site's own story page for the Write Off Match on Players and Pawns (31 December 2002), a build-up piece signed by the staffer James Attwood, from the Wayback capture of 14 January 2003. The show itself is served on this wing.
June 20th, 2002. LLB fought co-owner of PIW, Adrian Fernandez, in the first ever Write Off Match (Hell in a Cell Casket Match). And now... two years later, he’ll battle his new Boss, Reed Young. The history between these two is clearly evident. Reed was the reason why LLB had won the Action Championship... and Reed was the reason why he lost the Action Championship. He’s also the reason why the ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear is back... which has already made LLB’s life a living hell. Come the last night of 2002, a night in which has not served worthy for LLB in the past... and a month that has brought him the worst of his times in losing to Sean Cylear two years ago, losing to A.D. at Pain last year in the Asylum... and not forgetting his last minute loss in both the ManHunt and Cataclysm contests last year... maybe Reed Young is due for the victory? Statistic wise, and just mere mat skills, there is no way that Reed Young should be able to hang with LLB. But he wouldn’t have agreed to it... if there wasn’t a plan, right? On a night of firsts, ‘The Law’ will meet the Owner... and there will be no escape. But for who? -James Attwood ggg
