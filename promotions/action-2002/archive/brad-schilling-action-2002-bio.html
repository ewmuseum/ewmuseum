Brad Schilling Biography Nicknames The Immovable Object Hometown Harlem, New York, New York Birthday Believed to be: August 7th 1978 Height 6'10' Weight 285 Biography Written by Mr. Jamal Fredricks, an ex-history professor at P.S. 124, now residing in a small mental institution a few miles outside of New York City. He knew Brad Schilling from age 14-18 and found out about the boy's past. There are things that make people go crazy, there are so many things that can happen that make people in this world go crazy. One thing could be terrorist attacks; yes, those can make tons of people burst into panic, and flee from the nearest building. Another thing is constantly being turned down, or being called different, or being called fat. But one thing sticks out from my mind. Hello, I'm a History teacher at P.S. 124 in New York, more specifically in Harlem, New York City, New York. I've only seen a handful of white boys walk through the doors of this school, enough that I could count them on my hands. Some of them were considered 'one of the boys' in school, and hung out with all the rest of kids getting into their gang fights. Then others were picked on, called 'cracker', 'whitey', 'pasty ass' - the usual names for a white kid. But one, he was a part of neither sector, and he still haunts me from the very first day I ever saw him. He's the one that put me in this place; it's his fault. It's his fault I was thrown into this shithole of a mental institution. He's the one that caused the dreams; he's the one that made me crack. More on this boy? When I first saw him, he was only 14 years old, and for some odd reason I was stuck with him throughout his stay in high school. At that time, I had looked through his records, expecting to see what was the usual for a white boy of his age in a place like Harlem. I expected a fighter, some one who was swinging at the other kids all the time, who'd be fighting the African American population of P.S. 124. That's not what I saw. What I saw was a relatively quiet young boy who lived at a local orphanage. He was never in trouble at school, but the teacher's notes from the past year had been left that the kid was quite disturbing, and he had always scared the other kids. I instantly wondered why as I continued my search to learn about this enigmatic young man. I turned up at the orphanage, and as I was speaking to its co-ordinator, he told me of nightmares the boy had as he was growing up. Horrible, horrible things - how his parents abused him. They used to play a cruel game with him, try to see who could make him cry first... These people were sick people, but they had no idea of what they had the set the fire for, no idea. But those eyes, I still see them peaking through the curtains at me... Written by an ex-McDonald's manager, now a New York City official, who's asked that his identity is not revealed, for endangerment of public opinion. Knew Brad Schilling from ages 19-21. Brad walked into the McDonald's on my shift and asked for an application. So as common courtesy, we gave him one, by the looks of the kid, he didn't even have the brains to finish the thing. The next day the kid turned the application in at the same time as he came in the day before. I looked at the application, figuring he was just a young kid, but that was wrong. He was actually a 19-year-old, going to school at a local college after earning his high school degree. This type of kid is usually a dream employee so we hired him after a brief interview. One thing I did make a note about were his eyes. They looked like a man's eyes I had seen on T.V. Now, as I have watched the numerous hearings for this man's probation, they remind still to this day of the piercing eyes of Charles Manson. I also noted how he might be a distraction to some of the female workers, with him standing 6'5' and weighing 255 pounds, with the build of a linebacker. Brad worked a lot, requesting some late hours, and was usually our steady closer. When someone couldn't finish their close he was there to close the shop. Always seemed like a nice quiet kid to me, but there were times, when I'd see him get angry. Those eyes seemed to illuminate, you could tell rage was flowing through his body as the muscles in his arms began to protrude from his usually baggy clothes. That's something else - this kid had a very weird sense of style. Ranging more to the gothic genre of style, always baggy pants. Occasionaly a home made sweat shirt with some patches sewn onto the back or something. But there's not really much else to say, I had to calm him down a few times, that's it. We did find out he lied to us about his status as a student - he wasn't in the college we thought he was, he was in a wrestling school. As this fact made its way around the work place, Brad quit almost immediately. It seemed like he was kind of embarrassed about it. Written by Jamal O'Connor, a student at Killer Kowalski's gym in New England, also trained there after finishing school. Now a wrestler in Combat Zone Wrestling, based in New Jersey. Knew Brad from 20-24. Well, I had been training at Kowalski's for about two months when the kid came into the training room. The kid was built like an ox, really reminded me of some big mean linebacker. He even had those linebacker eyes; well, actually they were worse. I used to play tailback in high school, and I went up against some tough linebackers, and they didn't even have eyes like those. Anyway, he came right into Kowalski's and became the quietest kid in the wrestling class. He was big, and strong, and did what he was supposed to do. He only talked when he had to, Kowalski always would ask him to repeat what he just said, because he looked like he was staring off into space. But he always had the right answer; I guess the kid was just weird like that. His style was also kind of weird, not the usual style you'd expect of a kid who had the money to come to wrestling school almost right out of high school. He always had on a pair of big baggy jeans and a baggy sweat shirt, but to wrestle he'd change into a pair of swishers as Kowalski used to call them and a tank top. The kid wrestled his heart out, then would go back to being calm and cool, but with those eyes, you never could tell what he was thinking. He had a temper though - I remember one time a fellow trainee got a little stiff with him, and he began swinging. It took about five of us to hold him back. Man, that kid had a wicked right. The trainee never came back, last we heard he broke his cheek bone from a punch and that was that. Kowalski had to sit the kid down and remind him this was a training school, and he couldn't do that in a real ring etc... Overall he had the makes for success, unless of course his temper heald him back. Written by Brad Schilling, his take on these people. Mr. Fredricks took an interest in me in high school; I don't know why. I tried to make it so people wouldn't take interest in me; I tried very, very hard. Which is why I used to sneak out of the orphanage and go look through his windows at him in the night. He was always up late, always shifting through manilla folders. I knew the contents of my history laid in those folders, which is why I stole them before he got too far into them. My past usually scares people off, and for some odd reason so do my eyes. That manager, who threatened legal action if I mentioned his name in here, yeah that guy's a punk and a half. He always tried to calm me down, by saying 'so what if they called you freak.' Yeah, so what if they called me freak? How would he have liked it if they had abused him at school, which is why I dropped out. I was tough, but not tough enough to fight ten knuckle dragging jocks at once. Then he found out I was in wrestling school, and spread it around the work place. Of course it got out of the work place, and now I was the wrestler freak. Yeah, thats why the jackass didn't get my vote. Jamal is now waiting for the clock to tick in his fortune and fucking kill him. The guy literally puts his body on the line every show. Why? Because Kowalski decided to put me over on one of the shows we would do for WWF recruiters. Jamal wasn't too happy about it and missed some spots. So he went right back, trying to embarass me by getting a little stiff with me. And yeah, Jamal walked out of school that day with a broken cheek bone and ran over to Combat Zone. These people, they've all played roles in my life, but they all have something to hide, I was the only one who let it all show in the end, which is why I'll be a success...I hope.
