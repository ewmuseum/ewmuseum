Their relevance to Fiend is unknown, but as fans we thought you were entitled to the information we had. The only information we had... New York Deli owner found dead At 5:37 this morning here in New York, the body of deli owner Vincent Palazzio was found. In the alley behind Palazzio's Delicatessen, Vincent Palazzio was found in a pool of his own blood. His hands were tied behind his back and his feet fastened together. Police told us that it was a torturous death to the 38 year old deli owner. There was talk on the scene that there was a connection to the Laviticus De Marcio's family and the arguments over protection money. It is commonly known that Palazzio was in the heart of De Marcio territory, yet relating the murder to the family is only speculation. - 15th of April, 2002 Young Indy Wrestler Charging to the Top Having only appeared on the scene one month ago, the man only known as Fiend has made an impression on the Indy scene. Having worked only exhibition matches from time to time, he has created some sort of a cult following amongst New Yorkers. Using the fabled Butterfly Effect as his finisher, Fiend has destroyed three of New York's top indy workers. Many promotions trying to tie Fiend up in contracts have failed in their trying. Fiend continues to sign exhibition contracts at his own accord and own free will. We tried to contact Fiend for a comment but we were unable to contact the young superstar. - 1st of March, 2002 Three Murdered in New York Taverna Standers by could only watch in sorrow as police took away three bodies associated with the Valenzzio family. Of the three, Donny Valenzzio's son, Luscious, was found dead in DiChini's Taverna. The bodies were taken from the building, the murders having taken place in the facilities of DiChini's. Police were baffled as how three of New York's mythological hit men could be killed without any signs of a struggle or bullet entry. The police commissioner was unavailable for comments. - 3rd of May, 2002 Valenzzio Dead Crime boss, Donny Valenzzio, was found hanging outside DiChini's Taverna in the heart of De Marcio territory. Found at 6am this morning, the body was dangling from a street light. Only eye witness of the hanging was a jogger, who takes the route every morning. Being unnamed for safety reasons, the jogger told us 'I saw one man. He was huge. He looked like he could push a truck on it's side. 'He had no weapons, he demanded that Valenzzio remove his belt and put it around his throat. He made Valenzzio hang himself. The man didn't even touch him,' told the jogger. Police were again baffled by the latest in the demise of the Valenzzio family's deaths. 'Donny Valenzzio was a powerful man. If the eye witnesses testimony is correct, then whoever this man is has to have information or something to do with the murders two months ago,' NYPD Police Commissioner told us. - 6th of July, 2002 Fiend Cult Hero to Hated Zero The Indy wrestler, Fiend, came to New Jersey yesterday to participate in a World Wrestling Allstars event. The sold-out crowd came for Fiend, the arena was littered with signs for the man that every feared, but now they loathed him. He destroyed 'the Tank' Ivan Wright within three minutes. The packed crowd were on their feet behind Fiend when Wright tried to attack him after the bell. But the Jersey crowd were soon treated to a bloodbath at the hands of Fiend. Details of the incident are withheld from print as they were too gruesome to be expressed in a public newspaper such as ours. - 28th of July, 2002 Ivan Wright dies in Hospital WWA wrestler Ivan Wright died in hospital yesterday. Police inquiries told us that this was mob related. The death of Wright had nothing to do with the injuries he suffered at the hands of Fiend on the 28th of July. Wright was found with laundry detergent in his stomach and his had been sewn open. Nurses had no idea how an intruder had gotten into the building and done such a thing without them hearing Wright scream. An investigation is now taking place after police found a nurse's uniform outside of the New York General Hospital. 'We have reasons to believe this has connections with the murders of Donny Valenzzio and his men.
