Bourbon and Tears. Morg gazed aimlessly into the empty syringe, readying himself for its insertion. He filled the hypodermic needle with insulin and proceeded to compress the plunger a bit; squirting out any excess fluid it might’ve contained, so as to meet his prescribed dosage. Morg squeezed his left hand firmly into a fist, constricting it repetitively. Morg, unlike most diabetics, had to inject the drug directly into his veins for it to properly take affect. Unfortunately, his condition was discovered so late into its development that traditional treatment was no longer an option. He would have to take massive doses of insulin, multiple times a day, to keep the condition under control. Obviously, this would put an enormous amount of strain on his body; specifically his kidneys, liver and heart, but it would keep him alive... for now at least.
