Not as exhausted as Joey Malone though, who knew he was in trouble. ‘Forget about Kent Anthason’ he thought, ‘you have a problem right here.’ Airborn popped those hips one more time and lifted Joey into the air, but just like earlier Joey caught the head of Airborn Tyler and moved forward for a bulldog. But Airborn Tyler prevented the forward motion, and fell back, dropping Joey on his head. Four times the former IWO Heavyweight Champion had been dumped on his head. Head trauma was something all wrestlers suffer from time to time, and Airborn Tyler homed in on. He brought Joey up and hooked his head, looking for the Tyler DDT. He started to swing around, but Joey pushed him into the ropes. The crowd had fresh impetus, delighted to see their hero fighting back. Airborn flew onto Malone’s shoulders for a crucifix pin. But Joey Malone caught him. Airborn was held there for a few seconds, and everyone knew that the Malonestrom was coming. The plucky kid slipped down though and hooked Joey’s head. Quickly he jumped and spun into a swinging fisherman’s neckbreaker. The ‘Place For Your Head’. Someone had finally hit a big move in the match, and contemplating the fact that he could be the unlikely Action! champion, Airborn hooked Joey’s leg! One! Two! Three No! Once more at the very last second Joey Malone thrust up that arm, and kept the dream alive. He wasn’t giving up the title, not after all he’d been through throughout his career to earn it. Not yet. Airborn Tyler finally lost it though, and this time he did protest to referee James Jack. In disbelief and desperation he pleaded, lifting three fingers to indicate he thought the shoulder was lifted after the third count should have been made. Hey, when was the last time arguing changed a sport official’s mind? **POP** Cobra clutch reverse DDT, and Airborn Tyler never saw it coming. Airborn Tyler had made a rookie’s mistake by disputing the referee’s decision, and Joey Malone was showing his experience by taking full advantage. Once more Joey Malone headed to the top rope, although this time he did it with more haste. There wasn’t anyway he was going to miss this move. Bad Moon Rising. The crowd was elated, because Joey Malone had hit it, his swandive headbutt. Unfortunately, in using his head as battering ram Joey had also taken a lot out of himself and he couldn’t make the cover, at least not immediately. After ten seconds though he crawled over to the decimated Airborn and draped an arm loosely over his chest. One! Two! Airborn put his foot on the bottom rope. It wasn’t entirely unexpected, but the crowd was still flabbergasted. Still a professional though, Joey got straight back up and battled on. Grabbing Airborn by the hair he forced him to his feet and body slammed him to the mat. Arms lifted high, Joey looked longingly at the middle rope. Oh how he loved this move. And the Malone fans around the world loved it as well. Bunny Sault! But Airborn Tyler lifted his knees. Just as well Joey Malone had landed on his feet and not Airborn then. Joey grabbed Airborn’s leg and tried to lock in a single leg Boston crab, but Airborn wouldn’t be shifted. Instead, the challenger used his leg strength to kick Malone into the ropes, and then take him down with an inside cradle! One! Two! No! The fans over the arena touched their hearts. That was too close for comfort. Malone was straight back to his feet though and lunged with a clothesline. Airborn ducked. James Jack hit the deck. Joey Malone instantly regretted that and nudged JJ to try and bring him around again, but no movement was forthcoming. Shrugging, Malone pushed the head official’s body under the bottom rope with the soul of his boot and turned around. Into an Airborn Tyler spinning heel kick. Malone was straight back up to his feet, but he was wobbling all over the place. His vision was just good enough to avoid a crossbody block though, at which Joey went down to one knee. The two matches last Sunday, the gauntlet and subsequent assault on Friday, and now this. It was overwhelming. Not that Joey was about to complain. It went with the territory. “BBBOOOOOO!” Airborn Tyler screwed up his fist and thrust it straight into Joey’s nether regions. Keri’s husband collapsed down to the ground immediately. “No mini-Joey for the Malone’s!” chuckled Derik Shaw. Now was his time. That’s what Airborn Tyler thought as he slid outside the ring and shoved the time keeper off his chair. The suited man hit the ring steps hard, and his clean blazer became covered in dirt. Not that the ‘A Star Student’ cared, his eyes were fixed on the Action! Championship belt that lay on the table. The lights shined off it’s gold plates, and a reflection of Airborn’s face could be seen. Seemingly in a world of his own, Airborn picked up the title and rolled into the ring, where he stood in the corner, knees bent and poised, ready to pounce. Joey Malone climbed to his feet. Something didn’t seem right. He turned around. **CLANK** Airborn PLASTERED the title belt over Joey’s face, and the champion hit the deck straight away, with a little blood trickling down his nose. The footsteps of someone running down the ramp echoed around the arena as Airborn made the cover. The crowd erupted into cheers. The person hit the mat once. The person hit the mat twice. The person hit the mat three times. Airborn Tyler leapt to his feet, ecstatic. Still holding the Action! Title, he went straight to the second turnbuckle, not even bothering to turn around. He’d done it. He was the champion, the best in the business. He was the democratic best. Something seemed wrong though. There was no booing from the crowd. There was no ring bell. There wasn’t his theme music playing out the speakers. Slowly, Airborn turned around. He didn’t see what happened next. Jonny Yankee hit him over the head with a crowbar. And again. And again. The first hit had knocked him off the turnbuckle and down to the mat. The second hit had busted him open badly. The third hit had knocked him clean out. The crowd was going bananas. Joey’s title had been saved, although the way it happened might not be appreciated, but heck, he had problems of his own with Kent Anthason to worry about. James Jack’s head popped up from under the apron, and he’d seen everything that happened. Disgusted, he waved his arm at the time keeper, before collapsing again. “The winner of this Match...” Announced Pat Hoskins. “By disqualification... AIRBORN TYLER!” The crowd were disgusted that Airborn had won the match. He was the wrestler who’d originally cheated, but at least Joey still had the title. The cheers for Yankee soon turned to jeers as The Elements ran down to the ring. Jonny Yankee scampered, not wishing to fight the three-on-one odds. The Elements checked on their stable leader, as Yankee retreated through the crowd. Jonny Yankee had cost Airborn Tyler the Action! Championship (in their eyes). Jonny Yankee and Neko Case had defeated The Elements in a Tag Team Cage Match. It hadn’t been a good night for the ‘A Star Stable’. But at the top of the ramp a man and his wife quietly disappeared behind the curtain. Blood trickling from his nose, Joey dragged the Action! Title, his attention already away from the previous match, and back onto the man he detested so much. Kent Anthason. Outcome: Airborn Tyler via Disqualification; Joey Malone Retains A!
