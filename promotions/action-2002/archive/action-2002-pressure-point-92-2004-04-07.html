Pressure Point 92 Action Wrestling Headquarters — Charlotte, North Carolina ---- ACTION WRESTLING PRESENTS ACTION WRESTLING PRESENTS PRESSURE POINT: EDITION 92 Action Wrestling Headquarters, Charlotte, North Carolina 04/07/04 The Show Must Go On. Starring: Reed Young & Jeremy Bold. Writer: Roland. It should really be over. Reed Young had failed once again in his attempt to run a Federation… and the second time he failed to go on with Action Wrestling. It was nothing new to anyone that knew Reed personally. He was… bar none… one of the laziest, useless Owners of all-time. Hell, he was one of the laziest and most useless people of all-time. However, with the past success Action Wrestling had gained in its first two years… things were looking different for a change. Reed Young was actually running something that mattered to people. And despite his questionable job as the Owner… AW was a hit. That was, of course, until he decided to take a leave… without informing anyone before. From there… problems started. Joe Schmidt took over. Other insiders voiced their opinions. Ideas clashed. People argued. And to make matters worse… Reed Young returned. All hell broke out behind the scenes. It had turned Reed into a failure… from his recent successes. He couldn’t take it. Chaos was normally caused when he was around… but not to this point. It was chaos no one had ever seen before. Power-hungry chaos. And to change the problems… Reed drank them away. He drank them away to the point of firing the entire roster… but all of them were ready to walk out anyway. So from what was heaven two months ago… went to hell. From last year’s mark in this current year, Reed might have even been semi-respected. But fast forward, one year later… and no one… no one could give a f*ck about him. Rightfully so? More than likely. But in Reed’s mind it was just one mistake. It was a huge mistake. It deserved the rest of his roster wanting to leave. Yet to him, it didn’t mean he should be looked upon as the scum of the world. Young had seen scum, he thought… and though it looked kind of cool to act like such scum… he wasn’t sure if the name would suit him or not. Reed Scum Young. It just didn’t have the flow. Back to the rebuilding process. Back to where Young gathered a new roster together… and kept some of the old ones. … It just didn’t work. Move forward to the present time… and due to his new, one-year TV deal with the Fox Broadcasting Network… Young has been forced to sign, resign… and try to recruit new talent. With his name in the mud though… and his status so low… it looks almost impossible. This wasn’t like his other 30 Federations. They were low-key. To close them down broke the hearts of a couple. To just abandon Action broke the hearts of the millions. AND MILLIONS… ALL CHANTING THE ROCK’S NA- I mean… As Pressure Point aired, on the 7th of April, on a dark Wednesday… the scene opened to the Action Wrestling Headquarters. The beautiful multimillion-dollar building, surrounded by a magnificent landscape in Charlotte, North Carolina, as the scene went inside… to Young’s office. He stood, standing in front of his giant ceiling-high window. This wasn’t the Young of before. It wasn’t even the Young of the Christmas incident. The man stood with a glaze in his eyes and a downward slump in his posture. He had been standing there for a very long time now. Even he wasn’t too sure how long. Young was never, after all of his Feds had gone down, been one to dwell on the past. Basically… to be blunt, he was too dumb… to ignorant, to see his own faults. Somehow, throughout the opening seconds of Pressure Point, you could already tell this was not the same Young as before. A knock on the door followed after a whole minute of silence… but there was no answer from Reed. Another knock followed, and after Young brushed back his ratty brown hair, the door had just opened. Jeremy Bold knew what type of state his best friend was in. He knew silence had meant the words “come in” today. Bold seemed in better spirits. Hell he had to be. He was the soul reason Young’s mind tingered on the edge of sanity these days. He carried a tray of coffee with him… one for himself, one for Reed Young. The Vice-President placed them on the table… which was, surprisingly empty of paper. The entire office was clean… but really, what did Reed Young have to pretend to do anymore, now that his roster was gone? Jeremy paused as he gazed at the Owner. He was hoping Reed heard him… but after another minute of silence, Bold spoke very cautiously, as if blood from insanity was about to spill from Reed Young. “Boss.” He still said. Bold always had, although dumb, liked to give the proper respect to the people above him. Even in his heelish attitude, Jeremy gave off a way of respecting his enemies. No one ever saw that in Bold though. Today it shined like never before. Young turned his head. His neck had bitterly cracked as he did. And it was almost scary to see Reed in the form he was in. His eyes had heavy bags under them. His hair, all ratty and rough messed into his forehead. His cheeks were covered in prickly hairs… a clean-shaved Reed was all anyone saw in the past. This Reed looked odd. It was a double take for anyone to make sure it was truly him. But the worst of it all… was the way Young shook. His hand tried to brush back to top of his hair… yet it shivered in nerves as he brought his hand back. Bold smiled. He could always brighten the room. He was doing his best today to try and do so too. Young fully turned his body as Jeremy spoke again. “Everyone’s here to see you, Boss.” Bold said, again smiling. “And there are some excited people too!” The VP said that comment in hopes it would lift Reed Young’s spirits. It didn’t. Young nodded as he walked towards his desk and pulled back the chair. “Uhhh…” He looked towards Jeremy. “Can you help sit in on this… maybe ask the questions, lead the interviews?” Bold’s eyes opened. He was shocked to say the least. “I’d be delighted!” Jeremy said with a grin, as he grabbed one of the chairs sitting in the corner… only to drag it behind the desk and beside Reed Young. “Look Boss…” Jeremy said. “I think you should be excited for another opportunity! I can’t wait to get RATINGS again!” Young slightly grinned… although it seemed more like getting the VP off his chest. “Can we call in the first person?” Young asked Bold. “Hey, you’re the Bos-” He huffed. “Well can we?” Jeremy nodded. “Yep.” And Pressure Point faded to a commercial. ---- Taking Charge.
