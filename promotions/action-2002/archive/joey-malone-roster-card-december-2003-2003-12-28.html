Joey Malone Birth Name: Joseph Tyler Malone Also Known As: Tempest Malone, the Village Idiot Date of Birth: June 29th, 1974 Age: 29 Marital Status: Married, to Keri Lindum Hometown: Mesa, Arizona Height: 6'4' Weight: 245 lbs. Hair Color: Brown Eye Color: Emerald Green Blood Type: O+ Status: Face Personality: Honor-bound, kind of chivalrous in a way. A backstage leader in a loose sense of the word, he's more or less a loner that people do depend on at times of problems. He can come off as cold and sarcastic if provoked. He's fueled by a strong bond of love(via family) that, if disturbed in any way by another wrestler, causes him to react in a violent and somewhat unpredictable manner. Being such a strong family man caused him to inevitably retire from the sport of wrestling in May of 2003. Theme Music: 'Denial' by Sevendust Wrestling Style Trained By: 'Nitro Beaver' Kyle Morris Detailed Style: Suplex-based technical flyer Strengths: Adaptive to his opponents' styles. Though he doesn't like it, he can wrestle hardcore. Decent flying capability if his knee's working for him. Able to pop off his suplexes from any position, and sometimes regardless of personal injury. Weaknesses: Easilly angered by his opponent. His strong fury at his opponents if they've bothered his family leads him to be less careful. His left knee has always been a weakness for him, as has his usually-involved wife. Absolutely refuses to hit women, even if they're his opponents. Signature Moves: Springboard Dropkick Exploder suplex Japanese Ocean Cyclone suplex Rolling Germans Dragon Suplex Half Nelson Suplex (sometimes executing by first pivoting 180°) Bad Moon Rising: Top rope swandive headbutt. Executed normally, but he can do it with a steel chair on top of the victim, or off of a cage. He flies far with the maneuver, and it's hard to avoid. New Arizona Heatwave Elevated double chickenwing into a sitout facebuster. It was his IML2 finisher and occasional finisher in Action. He stopped using it soon after using the Malonestrom. Everest Cataclysm: Double underhook over-the-shoulder piledriver. Was his primary finisher up until mid-2002, when he switched to the Malonestrom for his opponent's safety. Rared executed after the change, and nearly impossible to kick out from after it's executed. Trademark Name: The Malonestrom Trademark Move: Fireman's carry into a Michinoku Driver (Amityville Horror/Whiplash) Injuries Current Injuries: None, thank God. I mean, he's retired, why would he be injured NOW? Nagging Injuries: Deteriorated left knee. History Years Pro: Eight. Manager: None, previously Keri Lindum Allies: Coral Avalon, Allison Lindum, Simon Seaman, Airborn Tyler, High Flyer, Tony Davis, Jon Crisp Nemesis: Daniel Phillips, Keith Scott Zimmerman, Jeff Garvin, Kent Anthason, Jax Stone, AWS Man (also known as Bill), Duncan Fletcher, Syphon Fission, Steven Denton Current Team: None. Past Teams: w/Coral Avalon(Action!), the A-Star Stable (w/Airborn Tyler, the Elements, Montrose, Siobhan Tyler, Jonny Yankee/Jonathan Scott: Action!), The Cult Heroes (w/Jon Crisp, Chris Tyson, and Jason Nuclear: 21w), w/ Doozer(LoW), The Winds of Change(w/Daniel Phillips: IWO, IML, IML2), Team CGI(w/Syphon Fission, the Mysterious Birdman, and Donnie Daze: IWO) Action Wrestling Title History: Action! Title, Bantam, Carnage Title (is the first-ever ABC champion and the first ever Bantam champion) Action Wrestling Award History: Wrestler of the Year, Feud of the Year(w/ Fission) Other Federations: 21W, IWO, jOlt, LoW, SWL, IML, IML2 Other Federation Awards: IWO Hall of Fame(2001), LoW World Tag Titles(w/Doozer), IWO World Heavyweight, IWO North American, IWO Pacific, IWO United States(2x), IWO World Tag(w/Team CGI), IWO Intercontinental Tag(w/Phillips), IML World Tag(w/Phillips), IML2 World Tag Titles(w/Phillips). Attributes Intelligence: 7 Strength: 6 Toughness: 7 Speed: 8 Endurance: 10 Courage: 6 Charisma: 10 Skill: 10 Biography The career of Joey Malone is that of legend. Whereas most only dream of being a legend, Joey Malone has accomplished it in spades, as a two-time World Heavyweight champion, four-time tag team champion, twice inducted into a Hall of Fame, and the Action! Wrestling Wrestler of the Year. But his road was long, and he endured much to accomplish what he did. He suffered through emotional trauma involving his beloved wife's brief affair with another man, his father's death, and the introduction of his little sister. He fought through the pain of a deteriorated left knee that eventually would be a factor into his retirement. His success was marred by the presence of other faces of his past, especially Kent Anthason and Daniel Phillips, who broke Joey emotionally and physically, respectfully. At the cost of the status of 'legend', Joey Malone can barely walk on his left knee, still suffers through his emotional problems, and has a fifteen-year-old girl to care for with another child on the way. While Joey Malone has newer and more important issues in his life, his last little bit of legacy in the sport of wrestling still carries on in the unsteady hands of his greatest student, Coral Avalon.
