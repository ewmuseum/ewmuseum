Halftime Whatever, 26 January 2003

26 January 2003

Halftime Whatever 1st Mariner Arena — Baltimore, Maryland ---- Syphon Fission vs. LLB vs. 'Showcase' Sean Cylear Halftime? WHATEVER! Syphon Fission vs. LLB vs. 'Showcase' Sean Cylear (The scene cut to the empty Arena [somewhere], where the next fifteen minutes plus of Warner Brothers’ Television would host a special Whatever Halftime show. The Action Championship would go on the line as Syphon Fission would defend against LLB and the ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear. The Referee for the night’s event was James Jack, as he strolled around the squared circle, waiting for Pat Hoskins to call the three members down to the ring.) Liam Ump: Welcome everyone to the special Whatever Halftime!... where it’s right now 20 to 3 for the Tampa Bay Buccaneers over the Oakland Raiders! Derik Shaw: It’s WB! It’s my show! It’s Whatever and here we go! (The layout was in the normal Whatever style. The word “Whatever” laid across the stage ramp, as the wrestlers would come out of the “te” part of the word, only to walk down the ramp and get into the ring. The ring mat was made of a normal canvas, as it had black posts and black turnbuckle padding, topping it off with red ropes and the “Whatever” logo for aprons. Thus completing the view, Pat Hoskins rolled into the ring and spoke on the microphone.) Pat Hoskins: This Match is for the Action Championship and it is a Triple Threat Match! Introducing first... LLB! Ump: There’s “Still Waiting” by Sum 41, and folks, here he comes! His chance to become the Action Wrestling Champion once again has appeared... as LLB may be wrestling in his best chance to capture the Title to date! Shaw: Ah, who cares!? It’s Cylear’s night! No one picked the Bucs to do so well... but dammit, they are right now! No one is picking Sean Cylear either... but he’ll show you. He’ll show you all! Ump: LLB enters the ring, shakes James Jack’s hand and goes about the ropes... Shaw: And that’s *another* thing! What’s the deal with LLB and James Jack anyway? Did you see how James acted during the whole LLB, Fission Title Match a few weeks ago!? Because I sure did... and it WASN’T normal! Ump: That’s true. And if James doesn’t shape up... he may see himself out of a job again, this time because of his own faults! Shaw: I... -oh my God...- agree. Ump: Really? Shaw: ... Ump: ... Shaw: ... Yeah. Ump: ... Hoskins: His opponent, and Challenger, the ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear! Ump: And as Cylear’s theme starts up... here he comes! **WHACK!** Shaw: NOT FAIR! NOT F’N FAIR! Ump: We’re off already... as Syphon Fission has just come from behind the Whatever logo and literally nailed Cylear in the back of the head with his Title! Shaw: LLB’s just waiting in the ring, as Fission takes Cylear... and will now run him down the ramp and directly into the apron! **DING DING DING** Ump: So Fission with relentless boots to the gut of Cylear here... and folks, if there was a Crowd tonight, they’d be cheering. Shaw: Well... I really shouldn’t argue too much, anything beats watching that Twang bitch sing. YUCK! Though, you know, I would like to bone her- Ump: That’s enough Derik. Shaw: And finally Fission picks up Cylear... **THUD!** Ump: Only to hurl him into the ring post! Shaw: But wait! That actually cues LLB now! He’s going off of the ropes... and a baseball slide to the back of Syphon’s shoulder! The Champion falls into the guardrail, dropping his Title as LLB exits the ring, picks up the Belt... **WHACK!** Shaw: And DRILLS it right across Cylear’s face! Ump: Now tossing the Title into the ring, LLB’s gonna walk over to Fission... CLOTHESLINING HIM OVER THE RAIL! Shaw: ::Acting like Liam:: That’s cement! CEMENT! Ump: Take it easy tiger. Shaw: *You* never do! Ump: Ugh... Shaw: LLB follows, as he picks up a chair- HEY A WOMEN WAS ON THAT CHAIR DAMMIT!!! A NICE... HOT... WOMEN!! Ump: What? Shaw: Oh, yeah, empty Arena. My Dad. Ump: What? Shaw: My bad. **WHACK!** Ump: LLB slams the chair over Fission’s head as he falls to the floor! Now he’s calling James Jack over, as the Head Referee hops the top rope and will make the count! Ump: One! Ump: Two! Ump: Kickout! Shaw: Barely! Ump: LLB was almost the Champion there, as he now gets up and takes hold of that chair... Shaw: JESUS LIAM!! LOOK!! LOOK!! Ump: Cylear’s on the top rope... LLB TURNS! **SMACK!** Ump: And the lawyer gets a face full of steel right in the kisser! Sean Cylear was able to regain his composure... get on the top rope... and dive, hitting LLB with a dropkick that forced the chair right into his own damn skull! Shaw: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! Ump: ... Shaw: Someone has to do it! Ump: But Cylear’s OUT too! As he did that leap of faith, upon the landing Sean crashed his gut into the guardrail that he hangs from now! Shaw: All three men are out. Totally totally out. Ump: Who’s going to get up first!? Shaw: Um... let’s see... um... um... ::shrugs::. Ump: Derik Shaw everyone! (Shaw claps.) Ump: But it is Syphon Fission, the Action Champion to rise to his feet first, as he looks around, and will walk over to Sean Cylear! This could spell trouble... Shaw: NO! DON’T DO IT! Ump: Suplex off of the guardrail and onto the cement! Down down down is that God damn idiot Sean! Shaw: Fission now dragging Cylear across the floor, as he’s bringing him deeper into the stands. James Jack follows, after he does check on LLB to see if he’s alright. Fission picks up Cylear... and fires him a right hand... another... another... and one more for good measure... Ump: Rake to the eyes: ‘Showcase’! Shaw: HA! HAHAHAHAHA!! DDT ON THE CEMENT! DDT! DDT! Ump: Cylear will pin! We could have a new Action Champion... !! Ump: One! Ump: Two! Ump: Three! Shaw: No! LLB made the save! He picks up Cylear now... looking for a- JESUS! A German suplex and Cylear goes right through two Arena chairs! Ump: With the ‘Showcase’ down, the former Action Champion picks up the current one... having plans to run him all the way back to the guardrail and over! He does just this, as Fission FLIES over the top and lands on the opposite side of the floor! Shaw: LLB gets on the guardrail, measures... and... AND... Ump: ELBOW DROP! Shaw: Pin baby! Ump: One! Ump: Two! Ump: Kickout! Shaw: Good! Ump: Fission was able to *just* get his leg up there, and power out at the last possible second! LLB doesn’t let this bother him though, and will roll Syphon Fission into the ring! Shaw: But not before seeing a groggy Sean Cylear marching towards him down the aisle! Ump: LLB hops back over the rail, lowers his shoulder and sends Cylear flipping right over with a back drop! Sean’s head almost crushed itself off of the floor upon the landing too... but now LLB will also roll him into the ring... Shaw: He’s going under the apron! Ump: LLB’s taken out... a kendo stick!? Shaw: Yes! A kendo stick! Ump: Into the ring ‘The Law’ goes, as Fission’s getting up... LLB SWINGS... **Swoosh!** Ump: Miss! Kick to the gut Fission... AND A DIVING DDT!! LLB’s out of it! Shaw: Fission takes that kendo stick... waits... waits... **WHACK!** Ump: It almost breaks in two as LLB is once again blasted from hell with a shot to kill! This time Syphon Fission will pin and get his first attempt of the night! Ump: One! Ump: Two! Ump: Cylear! Sean Cylear just saved LLB from losing as he pulled Fission’s leg and turned him around... Shaw: Figure four!? Ump: NO! The Champion has kicked Cylear out of the ring as he lands across the way! Now Syphon’s gone to the outside, taken Cylear... and a gut wrench powerbomb into a neck breaker! My God what a move! Shaw: He’s not done either! Fission’s a pissed off little boy... and in taking Cylear’s neck... HE’S BRINGING HIM OVER TO US! Ump: ::Sarcastic:: Great... Shaw: Fission hammers Cylear in the stomach just to make sure he’s down... it looks like it’s time for the choke slam! Also known as the ‘Muted Faith’! Ump: Fission lifts Cylear... Shaw: LOOK THE FUCK OUT!! **WWWHACK!** Ump: LLB slid out of the ring, ran around the corner, and just SPEARED the f’n shit out of Syphon Fission!! Cylear is dropped onto our table in the process... but not with nearly enough force, as it doesn’t even break!! Shaw: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! ::Breath::. Well the Champion left the entire midsection exposed when he went for the choke slam... it does only make sense you know. Ump: Everyone’s out! Everyone’s down! And with only minutes remaining until the Halftime Show is over... we still have no idea whom is going to become the Action Wrestling Champion! Shaw: C’mon Cylear! Get up! GET UP! Ump: Cylear’s getting up! Shaw: The former PIW Champion now takes hold of the Action Champion, and rolls him back into the ring... as LLB is left to lay, trying collect himself from the spear he just hit - that was from everything he had left. Ump: Cylear may look for the superkick if Fission’s going to get up- no! Instead he’s taken Syphon and tossed him into the ropes! In comes the groggy Fission... and a belly to belly suplex! Now Cylear gets behind Fission... and a high angle backdrop! Shaw: The ‘Showcase’ isn’t done either, as he lifts up the A Champion... and looks for a dangerous brain buster!! My God this is over! Pin him! Pin him!! Ump: One! Ump: Two! Ump: . Th- Shaw: KICKOUT!? Ump: Yes! Fission kicked out! But didn’t it seem like the count was just a little too slow, Derik? Nothing to really get pushy about... but it did seem as though James Jack counted a bit faster for LLB and Fission than the ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear! Shaw: Either or, it IS a pushy thing for Sean! He’s gotten right in the face of James Jack and backed him into the corner! Ump: Wait! It’s LLB! He taps Cylear on the shoulder... and Cylear turns! Shaw: STONE COLD STUNNER!! Ump: Cylear’s head not only snaps back, his body lands right into the turnbuckle! There’s Sean standing there, resting against the buckle... Shaw: Fission’s getting up... and now, Jesus, Fission is running in! Ump: SPLASH TO CYLEAR! Shaw: Hip toss to follow! Ump: And finally, a hangman’s neck breaker! Shaw: YET HE FORGOT ALL ABOUT LLB!! LLB, WHO’S IN THE CORNER!! Ump: The lawyer charges forth! Shaw: ... Ump: ... Shaw: SYPHON CAUGHT HIS NECK!! HE KNEW IT ALL ALONG! Ump: Choke slam!?!? Shaw: Low blow LLB! Ump: ‘The Law’ into the ropes... and instead of a spear, this time he hits a dropkick to the knee of Fission! The Champion doubles over in pain, as LLB finishes the job with a DDT... and now... now, will add on a submission! Shaw: THE SHARPSHOOTER! Ump: The ‘Testify’!! Shaw: Could we see a new Action Cham- Ump: Cylear with an off-the-ropes leaping bulldog! It’s not over yet! Shaw: And to add more to this, he’ll continue to stomp the crap out of LLB’s body! If there were Fans, they’d be booing. BOOING! Dammit I want Fans here! And I want them right now! Ump: Calm down! Shaw: NO! Ump: Yet, once more... the unknowing happens again as Fission is rising... and yes! A Russian leg sweep to Cylear! Now Fission hurls Sean into the ropes... BIG BOOT! Blood is starting to spew from the ‘Showcase’s’ head as he tries to gather himself up for some air on the top of the canvas... Shaw: A relentless Fission pulling Cylear to his feet... Voice: I’M COMING! I’M COMING! MY GOD... I’M COMING TO SAVE YOU! Ump: This isn’t fair! This isn’t fair at all! Reed Young’s just appeared from behind the ramp, wheelchairing himself down! Shaw: And THIS cues LLB to exit the ring and run up the ramp to meet him. **SSSMMMAAACCCKKK!** Ump: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! Shaw: God dammit stop that! Ump: LLB just CLOTHESLINED Young right out of his chair as he was rolling down, full blast, repeat: FULL BLAST, down the ramp to save Cylear! Shaw: I have to go see if Reed Young’s okay! Ump: Regardless, Fission’s got what looks to be his Trademark to end the one Challenger!! Shaw: ‘The Final Word’! Ump: LLB’s a little too late to make the save, so instead he’ll just look down at his work! A destroyed Cylear waiting for the three count! Shaw: But no count!? NO COUNT!?!? What’s THE FUCK is going on!? Ump: I think Fission knows that LLB just helped him out here, so, instead, he’s KICKED Cylear to the edge of the apron and is calling the lawyer... on. Shaw: Here we go! Ump: LLB CHARGES DOWN THE RAMP... ROLLS INTO THE RING... Shaw: He ducks a right hand... now with a left... LEFT... LEFT... Ump: The pace quickens as LLB shoots to the ropes... CROSS BODY BLOCK INTO A PIN! Ump: One! Ump: Two! Ump: Th- KICKOUT! Shaw: That seemed like a somewhat fast count to me dammit! Ump: LLB gets up, as does Fission... both of them grapple and Syphon overpowers the lawyer as he chucks him into the ropes... BIG BOOT!?!? Shaw: DUCKED! LLB off of the next set... Ump: CROSS BODY BLOCK NUMBER TWO!! PIN!! PIN!! Ump: ONE! Ump: TWO! Ump: KICKOUT!! Shaw: A SOMEWHAT FAST COUNT... AGAIN! Ump: But this cues the two of them to get up once more... and LLB ducks *another* clothesline... pulling Fission into a back drop! He literally leaps directly to his feet, going off the ropes as Syphon rises... Shaw: YET A FUCKING ’NOTHER CROSS BODY BLOCK!?!? Ump: NO!! THIS TIME FISSION CATCHES HIM... FALL AWAY SLAM!! Shaw: The beaten down Syph will wait for the lawyer to get up... and he does! Fission towards him... YES! A right hand! LLB slams into the corner as Fission will hammer the reigning shots down upon him! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! EIGHT! NINE!... Ump: VICTORY ROLL LLB!! Ump: ONE! TWO! Shaw: REVERSED! Ump: ONE! Ump: ..TWO! Ump: THREE!! Shaw: DID HE GET IT!?!? James Jack is getting up... and saying... Ump: HE DID NOT!! Shaw: HE DID NOT!?!? Ump: A stunned Fission looks back to the canvas... but LLB rolls under his legs, gets behind him and... AND... AN ELECTRIC CHAIR DROP!! Shaw: LLB is now going to go all the way up here! What’s he looking for this time!? LLB: I’m going to win... BIG TIM- OBJECTION! The Crowd: ... Ump: ERECTION! (LLB looks across the way at Liam Ump.) LLB: Thanks. Shaw: AND LLB JUMPS... Ump: ‘BIG TIME’! ‘BIG TIME’!! NEW ACTION CHAMPION... BIG TIME!! Ump: ONE! Ump: TWO! Ump: CYLEAR!?!? Shaw: CYLEAR!! Ump: Cylear just broke up the count... picked up LLB... and drilled him with a implant DDT! Now he takes Fission... tossing him into the ropes... **SMACK!** Ump: THE BIG BOOT CONNECTS!! Fission will pin Cylear! Ump: ONE! Ump: TWO! Ump: THREE!! Shaw: FISSION RETAINS!! FISSION RETAINS!! SYPHON FISSION IS STILL THE ACTION CHAMPION!! Ump: And as “Be Quiet And Drive(Far Away)” by the Deftones airs over the PA... the A Champion is still Syphon Fission!! (Pause.) Ump: Halftime is almost over too!! Shaw: And I’m predicting an Oakland comeback by NINE MILLION POINTS!! Ump: ... Right. (The scene cuts.) Outcome: Fission via Pinfall (pins Cylear); Retains A Title. © Action! Wrestling 2002-2003
