What this is Keith Scott Zimmerman’s card from the fed’s own roster page, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 28 December 2003. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Keith Scott Zimmerman Birth Name: Hattori Hanzo Also Known As: KSZ, the Dean of the Digital Mafia, the Doctor of Smarkanomics, the NEW Ratings Draw, the Whole Workrate Show, Version 1.5... Date of Birth: 30.10.82 Age: 21 Marital Status: single...as far as everyone but one knows... Hometown: Berkeley, CA Height: 5'8' Weight: 163 of twisted steel & two title toting appeal Hair Color: black Eye Color: green Blood Type: A positive Status: Heel Personality: Asshole. Smark. Genius. Demanding. Anti-social. Egotistical. Worships Jeff Garvin as a minor deity, for in his world, he is above all else. Except Natalie Portman, his future wife. I know, it's very sad. Also, he becomes quite the moron around Allison Lindum, for some reason. Theme Music: When wrestling as half of the International Workrate Connection or as a part of Wrestling 101, he'll enter to their theme 'Back To School' by the Deftones. In singles competition he uses the Hives' 'Main Offender'. And fuck Levine because KSZ was doing this WAAAAAY before him. Wrestling Style Trained By: Michael Modest Detailed Style: 70% cruiser, 30% American Strong Style Strengths: Keith's life revolves around wrestling and his gameplan of attacking the head and neck is technically proficient. He does a lot of homework on his opponents, so while he's not a counter wrestler per se he knows what to look out for. He likes doing moves in twos to fives, slowly setting up an opponent's demise and getting (and keeping) momentum. Also, due to the general crusier weight in the company, can lift most of his opponents. Keith is both highly intelligent and very fast, so if an opponent makes a mistake they'll usually pay for it. Weaknesses: Keith is easily the smallest man in Action and if someone can catch him, they can beat him up fairly easily. Keith is egotistical, meaning he's prone to distraction leaving him open for a move. If an opponent is unprofessional or uses a move he doesn't like, he won't sell it and/or will stiff them silly. Signature Moves: leaping uppercut (SHORYUKEN~!) swandive headbutt back suplex elbow driver rising palm strike STO the Best Brainbuster in the Business: The biggest momentum shifter in the Zimmerman arsenal. Can be executed in a myriad of fashions (see super variant vs. Garvin in the Round Robin Tournament, and/or onto the stairs vs. Avalon at TDDUP '03), and is self-explanatory. For the maximum effect, there is the Trilogy, rolling brainbusters with some sort of variant for more effect on the third. The Trilogy is also an alternate finisher, but is far rarer than the Breakdown or Driller. Communication Breakdown: Suggested by a reader for Keith's love of Led Zeppelin and Chris Jericho, it is the latter's hybrid full nelson front Russian legsweep mixed with a song name of the former. Is one of Keith's major alternate finishers, right alongside the... KSZ Driller: Renamed Mitchinoku Driver (and once named the System Crash), from the Mitch Wilson playbook: a hiptoss transitioned into a Michinoku Driver II. Numerous variants on this move as well, and is also usually a nearfall with finishing potential. Inciter III: Until Daniel Phillips comes back from the side of the milk carton, Keith has co-opted his move. (Daniel being the only non-101 friend Keith's had to date) It's not going to show up in every match, but what Keith loses in strength he'll make up for with speed to execute it. 'It' is a running Diamond Dust, and has an alias under the name Throwback when John Cena uses it. triple jump quebrada: Not a named move, but a Zimmerman signature nonetheless. He leaps to the middle rope, springboarding off of it to hit the top rope, and then hits the inside-to-inside Asai moonsault more commonly known as the Lionsault. 'Inspired by' both Jericho again and independent demigod 'Fallen Angel' Christopher Daniels, who played a minor role in training him. Zimmerman CI: Discus forearm smash to the face. Has KO possiblity, and does a Rockesque version of this (though it has yet to hit in A! to date) called the People's Roaring Elbow. Name a takeoff on the Dangerous III, with 101 replacing the 3. International Workrate Connection: double brainbuster double dropkick to the temples of a victimized opponent double flapjack tornado bulldog into a facebuster to the knee Delacroix Device: Doomsday Device except KSZ uses a missile or springboard dropkick in lieu of a clothesline. There has also been a belt shot variant used in the match vs. Honor & Glory. The most easily countered of the IWC's named moves, it is also the premier momentum shifter. La Deluge: Frenchie hits the (delayed) Time (power)Bomb; as the opponent is being driven into the mat KSZ spikes the victim with a springboard leg lariat that's usually inside-to-inside. Outside-to-inside is a possibility given enough time to pull it off, and if the IWC had such a thing as a main secondary finisher this would be closest due to the Frenchie's powerbomb portion of the move. STIWC: Before Zimmerman executes an STO, the Flying Frenchie superkicks the poor victim's head back into the Stone Age. Due to their honing as a team they may use this move more than once during a match;whether it'll be done to punish inferior opponents, change the momentum of the match, or (given enough set-up work) spell the end of the bout will vary. Wrestling Renaissance: Cut Keith and Pierre's lives into pieces for this, because it is the last resort. Unveiled in their defense vs. the Legacy, it is a variant on the Stackplex with Keith hitting the opponent with a release German that given enough strength can be a release Power Hoist. Needless to say, this move is a hyperare superfinisher that would be top 10 in causes of death if they were to do it all the time. Trademark Name: (IWC) Denouement (singles) the markOUT Trademark Move: (IWC) modified Tidal Wave: Frenchie gullotine legdrop and KSZ swandive headbutt from same corner (singles) Orange Crush: suplex turned sitout pinning powerbomb Injuries Current Injuries: mild post-concussion syndrome, misaligned nose, sprained neck, sore leg, chipped tooth Nagging Injuries: everything's sore History Years Pro: 1+ Manager: -- Allies: Wrestling 101 and the Digital Mafia Nemesis: Any of the Arizona Cardinals not named above Current Team: International Workrate Connection (partner: Flying Frenchie), part of Wrestling 101 Past Teams: no lasting ones Action Wrestling Title History: current Bantam Champion, half of the current Dyad Champions (first person in Action! history to hold these belts simultaneously) Action Wrestling Award History: nominated for Most Improved Other Federations: Bay Area Wrestling, IWO Other Federation Awards: -- Attributes Intelligence: Keith is nine credits away from a UC Berkeley degree in English. His IQ last tested at 171. Anything above 140 is considered genius. Guess. (10) Strength: He's 163 pounds, so this is obviously one of his big weaknesses, though he can with a little luck pull off the Power Hoist on anyone within 75 pounds of his weight. (6) Toughness: He'll fight, but he'd rather outsmart someone and wear them down with his speedy move strings. (6) Speed: See earlier remarks about being 163 pounds. Goes quicker than a paycheck. (10) Endurance: This is on the rise. Has participated in a near-35 minute cage match with Max Danger, an half-hour iron person match, and is now up to the point where he's generally built for a 20-minute match. (7) Courage: Keith's a prototypical cocky heel who while thinking he can just brandish the sword and charge, also can flip-flop to absolute cowardice if he sees it as a window of opportunity. Can be goaded into foolhardy charges, but will wait for backup and/or have some sort of illegal object to change the tides. (6) Charisma: Love him or hate him, Action fans and followers have spared their indifference. (9) Skill: The reason the Flying Frenchie came to Action was to work with Keith. Living, breathing prodigy. Already really good, so imagine him with multiple years under his belt. (9) Biography It's mercy, compassion, and forgiveness I lack. Not rationality. --The Bride, Kill Bill volume 1 My early life was damn boring standard middle-class existence. I skipped kindergarten, which was a mistake because if I'd known there'd be naps, cookies, and milk with only four hours of 'education' I would've been all over that like LLB on the book. Anyway, I liked wrestling since I was a kid, with the first match I remember being the ***** WMIII Savage/Steamboat classic. I was almost never a fan of the big guys (keep in mind this was the 80s so they'd proliferated across the WWF landscape like bunnies) and I was also a fan of the bad guys too (still team up as the late great Mr. Perfect & Ted DiBiase when I play WrestleFest--the Perfect Millionaires! ), and no one else at my school was. I guess once you're a smart, there's no way out. Obviously, like many smarts, I got into the Torch & Observer. I started up a site when I was 14 (Miracle Ecstasy Weekly [MEW]) and in the course of my travels of covering shows, managed to piss off a shitty roidhead excuse for a wrestler. You know, 'Blah blah you can't do any better like to see you try you fuck mispelled word incorrect punctuation'. I was pretty bored and it was summer. So I learned. Turns out I'm pretty decent. Scrap that--I'm fucking good. And for everyone who only sees a writer, a poseur, a string bean, a geek, or a nerd? You're about to get a harsh lesson in the realities of the new millennium. Because I'm part of Wrestling 101. So get on the bus... 'cause we're taking your ass to school. Off Stage Information Name: Butch R. AOL Instant Messenger Screen Name: WON'T join your fed. Before you find out my s/n, I demand...a SHRUBBERY! And no, I WON'T join your fed. Interests: Jessica Alba and Eliza Dushku. Sure, there are other things, but who cares? If either one of those women bumped into me somewhere and said 'Excuse me.', I'd quickly say, 'Yes, I will marry you and father all your children.' I would... if I'm actually able to speak after seeing such beauties. But if you must know, the other interests I have are writing, being lazy, stealing mp3s, being lazy, and being lazy. Favorite Pro Wrestler: Angle, Matt Hardy VERSION 1-UH, WORD LIFE THIS IS BASIC THUGANOMICS...you know, the usual guys smarks like. Oh, and I back the Mack against Whitey. Ya feel meh?
