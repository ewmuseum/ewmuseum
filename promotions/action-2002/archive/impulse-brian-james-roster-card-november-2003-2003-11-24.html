The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Impulse Brian James Birth Name: Brian Michael Spaes Also Known As: IBJ, The BLONG Mastah, The Phoenix of Wrestling, 'The Eternal Journeyman', 'The Unluckiest Man in All of Wrestling' (Not to his face, of course) Date of Birth: First of July, Nineteen Seventy-Seven Age: 26 Marital Status: Widower (Jennifer Alicia Spaes) and currently in a relationship with Denise Caruthers Hometown: Fort Worth, Texas, U.S.A. Height: 6'3' Weight: 225 lbs Hair Color: Black with Red Tips on the ends of his hair Eye Color: Dark Grey Blood Type: B+ Status: Heel Personality: Brian is intensely loyal to his friends, however is much more reserved than in recent years due to personal tragedies (Wife and Brother's death, being on trial for rape of a minor). Brian is friendly to strangers, however his opinion of people shifts drastically in a snap. A light drunk (PP 67) and intensely vindictive. An instigator. Theme Music: 'Here' by Vast Wrestling Style Trained By: 'Emerald' Jim Schwartz and Minoku Kitsguari Detailed Style: Innovative, IBJ does not rely on one style of Wrestling, rather, he adapts himself to his opponent via trial and error, although IBJ primarily targets the neck, and Head and Shoulders (TM) region. Strengths: Acrobat, able to do the 'WOOO KICKOUT!' when needed, innovative style of wrestling Weaknesses: Quick to anger, gets in fights easily, not extremely bright, not as skilled as a technical wrestler as other styles Signature Moves: [Rare Moves in Italics] Seraphim Fire - Backflip Kick Seraphim Thunder - Front Roll Thrust Kick Seraphim Earthquake - Front Snap Kick, Back Snap Kick, Turning Roundhouse Kick Finishing with a Stepping Side Kick. Deus Ice - Inverted Facelock Front Suplex Deus Crash - Dragon Suplex Deus Bomb - Belly to Back Flipping Throw Suplex Cataclysmic Bash - Somersault Leg Drop Cataclysmic Blizzard - 1 ¾ Rotation Moonsault Cataclysmic Device - Springboard Corkscrew Splash Anarchia Devastation - Elevated Texas Cloverleaf Anarchia Slash - Step-Over Facelock Anarchia Slice - Stretch Plum (Usually used as a transition from Anarchia Slash) The Rusty Warlock - Jumping Slap to the Face (Shining Wizard parody) Exodus - Reverse Body Scissor Powerbomb Engulf - Top Rope Springboard Spinning Heel Kick to the Back of the Skull Trademark Name: 1. The Firestarter 2. The BLONG Clutch Trademark Move: 1. Stalling Brainbuster 2. Dragon Clutch Injuries Current Injuries: Gash in forehead and weaken knee (PP82) Nagging Injuries: Weak ribs and replaced right knee cap History Years Pro: 9 (First Professional Match: 7/1/94) Manager: Does not require one. Allies: Wrestling 101 (Jeff Garvin, Keith Scott Zimmerman, Chris Chambers, Joe Black, Flying Frenchie) Nemesis: Jon Crisp, Derecho, Coral Avalon, Ninja K, Ben Sythe, Jeremy Spaes (Disappeared) Current Team: Wrestling 101 Past Teams: The A-Team Action Wrestling Title History: None Action Wrestling Award History: None Other Federations: Over seven places of employment. Notables: Toronto Championship Wrestling, X Wrestling and 21st Century Wrestling Other Federation Awards: A 6-1 record in Cruiserweight Tournaments (1 Championship, 1 Elite Eight Preformance,) 4 Crusierweight Titles, 3 Tag Team Championships, 3 World Titles, Various Awards, One Time Hall of Fame Appearance (X Wrestling) Attributes Intelligence: Outside the ring, Brian Spaes is probably the 2nd least intelligent person on the Action payroll (to Reed himself), he is poorly educated and has little common sense. When he does step foot in the ring, though, he is a ring general, he has a savant knowledge of what to do in the ring. (5-) Strength: Having average strength, Brian relies on his tactics to take out heavier/stronger competitors. However, against wrestlers of around his weight and lower, he is uneffected. (6+) Toughness: Full of grit and determination. Due to his experience in the ring, it often takes more working of the knee, more time and more effort to put him down and out. If it wasn't for his ribs and knee, this would be a ten. (8) Speed: One of the top cruiserweights in the world, proven in his preformance in tournaments and competition. Incredbily agile and flexible wrestlers in the ring. (9+) Endurance: Almost in the twilight of his career, Brian has done the iron man and the 45 minute matches, and still has the capibility to do so. (7) Courage: Brian will not step down. Is set in his morals. He will do everything and anything to reach his goals. (10) Charisma: One of those who ooze natural charisma, he can rocrk the mike and entertain in the ring. (9+) Skill: Brian has the most innovative move set in Action Wrestling, Bar None. He blends martial arts, luchadore, strong style, wrestling and mat skills into one package, however he is the weakest, by far, in mat wrestling and the skills of the techncian. Poor against superheavyweights. (7) Biography Things to know about one Brian Spaes: He ran away from home to Chicago in the early 1990's, not the wisest move ever done. He was a street rat much of his teenage years in the Hyde Park suburb of Chicago. Trained by 70's-90's wrestling superstar Jim Schwartz. An unknown incident occured at his debut match against the above Jim Schwartz that left him out of favor in Evanston Wrestling. He will not speak of the match against Jim Schwartz. Has 2 younger brothers. One of his younger brothers commited suicide, the other he hasn't seen him since he was attacked by him. He is still recovering from his brother and wife's death. He will not speak about the child that his wife was carrying at her death. Brian's greatest joy in his life is his son, Jack Alexander, age 6. Brian has a growing reluctancy on alcohol. Brian's girl friend, Denise Caruthers, recently resigned from a powerful Dallas-Fort Worth law firm to homeschool Jack. Rumors have been spreading about the possibility of marriage on the horizon.
