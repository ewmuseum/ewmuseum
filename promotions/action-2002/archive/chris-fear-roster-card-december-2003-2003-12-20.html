Champion Action Wrestling Award History: N/A Other Federations: WCF (World Championship Federation) ; PIW (Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling) ; FSW (Five Star Wrestling) ; IOW (Innovation of Wrestling) Other Federation Awards: x5 WCF World Champion ; FSW World Champion Attributes Intelligence: 8 Strength: 5 Toughness: 8 Speed: 7 Endurance: 7 Courage: 10 Charisma: 9 Skill: 10 Biography Chris Fear was born Christopher Michael Fear on February 29, 1975 in San Jose, CA. When he was only four weeks old he moved to Boston, MA when his father, who was a professional baseball player, was traded to the Boston Red Sox. Chris grew up in Boston just like any other child. He graduated high school winning an athletic award before heading south to UCLA on a football scholarship as a running back. He completed university but was not drafted into the NFL. It was at this time that Chris Fear decided to follow his childhood dream and become a professional wrestler. There was one road block throughout everything however, and that came in 1986 when Chris was only 11 years old. On Christmas Day while his entire family was having an enormous Christmas Dinner somebody poured gasoline all over his house and set it on fire. Chris, being the smallest, was the only one able to escape. He stood at the end of his driveway and watched as his entire amily perished in the blaze. After university Chris went back to Boston, where he was trained to wrestle by the legendary Killer Kowalski. Killer Kowalski is the same man who trained such stars as Triple H, and Chyna. He wrestled for about a year in a very small federation called the World Championship Federation, winning a number of titles, which included the WCF World Title on six different occasions. Suddenly, out of nowhere the WCF closed it's doors forcing Chris Fear into unemployment. He remained unemployed for several months before he heard that the PIW was opening positions. Chris Fear was accepted by the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling and quickly moved into the upper card. He has headlined numerous pay per views and televised events, and often challenged for the world title. Unfortunately Chris came up just short in each attemt for the title. Chris Fear had been trying to find the person who killed his entire family for several years. At one point the 7-foot monster, Prisoner 109 came out and said he was the killer. Upon a further investigation by the FBI, it was believed that Prisoner 109 only said he was the murderer to gain instant recognition from the fans. Chris went on a brief hiatus from the PIW, and when he returned at Drastic Measures '01, he make one of the most shocking announcements in wrestling history. Fear, who had been given immunity many years earlier to help in the investigation, shocked the world when he announced that he had killed his entire family. Their was much specualtion leading up to DM as to who the previously announced masked man would be...it turned out to be Fear with his announcement. Fear went on to explain that Jim Johnson (PIW owner) had came up with the plan when Chris told him about his hatred for his family. LLB, who was always interested in law, said he would be able to keep suspicions away from Fear, and Prisoner 109 did indeed set the house on fire after Fear had stabbed his parents. Not long after that PIW closed, and Chris Fear went on to Five Star Wrestling. He quickly won their World Title, but once again that promotion closed a few months later. He then went to the Innovation of Wrestling and was united with many former PIW employees in what many considered PIW v.2. In IOW Fear was a member of Team PIW, but again, IOW closed within a few months. Since then Fear had remained out of the ring until Action! Wrestling was created. Fear was once again reunited with many former PIW'ers including the man who conspired with him in his families murder, LLB. Chris became the first ever Action! Champion. Chris held the A! Championship for several months before eventually dropping the title to 'The Law', however Chris will be etched in the history books forever as the first Action Champion. Only Chris knows for sure what the future holds, but it seems clear that many more people will FEAR THE FEAR!!!
