What this is Memphis Gray’s card from the fed’s own roster page, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 14 May 2004. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Memphis Gray Birth Name: Memphis Steven Gray Also Known As: M.S.G, God's Caliber Date of Birth: June 25th, 1980 Age: Twenty Three Years Marital Status: Single Hometown: Harlem, New York (by way of Newark, New Jersey) Height: Six Feet, Two Inches Weight: Two Hundred & Twenty Pounds Hair Color: Black Eye Color: Brown Blood Type: B+ Status: Neutral Personality: Like the colors employed by a chameleon. Changes when the need arises. Theme Music: 'What More Can I Say' - Jay Z Wrestling Style Trained By: Shawn Michaels, Jim Cornette, Kleptomaniac's, and Classy Freddie Blassie Detailed Style: Technical - High Flying, although he is a hybrid of karate and power moves as well. Strengths: The all around wrestling ability, along with his mannerisms to get himself out of a tight situation, although previous head and trauma has caused him to snap and be AWOL on companies for up to two to three weeks. It's been said that Memphis sometimes cares about the sport as a smark would, only loving for the wrestling portion, while at others he cares like a child, more for the spectacle, and glam and somewhat realism it brings. Weaknesses: Women, he's easy on them more than he is on the men, and of course we can't forget the injuries, such as a slowed down knee (broken at age 15), and torn rotator cuff (in New Japan during the summer of 2001) Signature Moves: Aerial Maneuvers: Hurricanrana, Springboard Tornado DDT, Somersault Plancha, Asai Moonsault, Twisting Pescado, Somersault Splash via Catapult, Top Rope Frogsplash, etc. Power Moves: Double German Combo with a Reverse Flapjack Finale, Lariat, Shoulderbreaker, Brainbuster, Double Underhook Tigerbomb, Double Underhook Piledriver, Fisherman's Buster, Whiplash Spinebuster, Tombstone Piledriver, etc. Submission Moves: Indian Deathlock, Surfboard, Achilles Tendon Hold, Inverted Full Nelson, Half Crab with Armlock, Hammerlock w/ Step Over Armbar, STF, etc. Named Moves: Da Phuck Up - Superbomb, Closer To God - Arabian Facebuster, Infinite - Flash Magic, Lost Cause - Shooting Star DDT Trademark Name: God's Caliber Trademark Move: Jumping Awkward Screwdriver Injuries Current Injuries: None, he's as clean and illness free as the baby who came out of the woman with the HIV virus, or something. Nagging Injuries: Some cases of the flu, not to mention a deteiorating knee which he rarely ever even thinks about, but he should though. History Years Pro: 3 Years on January 1st, 2004 Manager: None Allies: None. Business Is Business Nemesis: None. Business Is Business. Current Team: None Past Teams: Propaganda w/ JD Lawson and Johnathan Collins Action Wrestling Title History: None Action Wrestling Award History: None Other Federations: SWF, TZW, The X, PURE Other Federation Awards: SWF National Champion, SWF Tag Team Champion (w/ Lawson), PURE Junior Heavyweight Champion, When Worlds Collide 9 Finalist, When Worlds Collide 10 Quarterfinalist, Reign of Power 3rd Place Finish Attributes Intelligence: 100% Strength: 75% Toughness: 90% Speed: 80% Endurance: 100% Courage: 75% Charisma: 100% Skill: 100% Biography Nothing, there's no history. I'm God Caliber, ladies and gentlemen. I'll come back from the dead and show you bastards how we do it.
