Giovanni Ollivander DeNucci Birth Name: Giovanni Ollivander DeNucci. Also Known As: He was often referred to as Gio, Ollie, Vand, or some even took the G.O.D. abbreviation. Date of Birth: The Seventeenth Day of the Fourth Month of the Eighty-Fourth Year in the 20th Century. Age: Marital Status: Single and possibly a little gay. Hometown: Born in Florence, Italy. Lived just outside Dublin, Ireland most of his childhood and early teens. Settled in Orlando, Florida with his parents around sixteen. Height: Five-Feet, Seven Inches. Weight: One-Hundred Seventy-Eight Pounds. Hair Color: Brown. Eye Color: Brown. Blood Type: B Negative. Status: Heel Personality: Giovanni was a quiet guy. Didn't talk to anyone really. Always kept to himself. He paid a lot of attention to the things around him though. He picked up on everything. When he did talk, he was a smartass. Just an all around dick about things. Theme Music: 'Angel' by Massive Attack. Wrestling Style Trained By: Francisco Mungro in Karate and Jiu-Jitsu and an unknown man taught him wrestling. Detailed Style: Giovanni was nothing more than a shootfighter... or so it seemed. He grew up learning Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Karate. So he worked extra-stiff. Actually, he would just knock the fuck out of you. He did, however, possess wrestling knowledge, but his quick firing never allowed him to establish anything beyond the 3/4 Turn Back Flip Bulldog. Strengths: His shootfighting ability. Despite his size, he was very tough. Could take some serious blows and give them back, only harder. Knocking out Payne Fosters, legitimately is proof of that. Weaknesses: His lack of wrestling experience. Didn't really know when or how to tone down the stiffness. Ended up costing him in the end. His size, though he would never say it was such. Signature Moves: For those with the access, you can view his whole list of moves by clicking here. Here are just a taste of the moves he used to try and win matches with. Or would have, had his career lasted long enough. Roundhouse Kick, is what it says it is. Usually to the face. Jumping Knee Strike, was the jumping knee strike. Done either standing or running. Belly to Belly(or Back) Wristlock, which is just the technical name for Key Lock. Very deadly when applied by Giovanni. Back-Mounted Inverted Facelock, was a move not even used. But it was a devistating hold to be locked in. It's popular amongst wrestlers these days. But Giovanni did it for real. 3/4 Turn Back Flip Bulldog aka Sliced Bread #2. The only wrestling move he really busted out and it was during the Payne Fosters Shootfight at 'Til Death Do Us Part. Trademark Name: Divine Stretch EX. Trademark Move: Crossed-Arm Knee Backbreaker Hold. Injuries Current Injuries: None. Nagging Injuries: None. History Years Pro: Four, not years, but months. Manager: Holly Famke. Allies: His only allies were those in his camp, Holly Famke and Francisco Mungro. Reed Young was but only because Ollie's father was paying him to take care of his son. Nemesis: Joe Schmidt was his main enemy. He ended up firing Giovanni on Pressure Point 68. It was a glorious day. He also ran into problems with Payne Fosters and Donnie Daze. Plus the Advantage Wrestling front office, due to his walking out on them. Current Team: None. Past Teams: None. Action Wrestling Title History: None. Action Wrestling Award History: None. Other Federations: Advantage Wrestling for 2 months, before Giovanni just quit before officially debuting. Other Federation Awards: AW Co-Wrestler of the Week for the week of April 22nd through April 28th. Attributes Intelligence: Seven. Strength: Three. Toughness: Nine. Speed: Nine. Endurance: Nine. Courage: Ten. Charisma: Seven. Skill: Eight. Biography Some things to know about Giovanni: - He was born on April 17th, 1984 in Florence, Italy while his parents were visiting relatives. - His father is part Italian, part American. His father was Italian and his mother was American. - Giovanni's mother is part Irish, part Canadian, and part Japanese. Her mother was Japanese, while her father was an Irish-Canadian. - Giovanni has family in Japan, Ireland, Italy, Canada, United States, and England(but they never speak of them). - Giovanni spent most of his years in Dublin, Ireland, where a lot of his mother's family was. - He also goes by GOD(his initials), Ollie, Gio, and Van(d). - His father, Montgomery Ollivander DeNucci, is an billionaire international businessman. It isn't important what he does. - Giovanni was trained by Francisco Mungro, who is part African, part Japanese, but lived his entire life in America. - Giovanni does not believe in God(not from any religion). He thinks of himself as God. - He's a smart-ass, with an Irish accent. - He can speak Japanese, Italian, English, and Irish(it's English, but slightly mangled). - He was taught Karate and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu by Francisco, along with dabblings in other styles. - Is a slacker. - His family moved to Orlando, Florida when Ollie was 16, which is where he completed high school. - Has worked a few MMA tournaments and has very little professional wrestling experience(read: none). - Works stiff.
