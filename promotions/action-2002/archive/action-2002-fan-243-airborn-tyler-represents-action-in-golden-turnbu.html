Airborn Tyler represents Action! in Golden Turnbuckle Tournament Posted by James Attwood [detail withheld] on August 19, 2002 at 14:40:23: It has been announced that our own superstar Action! superstar Airborn Tyler is one of the play-ins in Round 2 of the prestigious Golden Turnbuckle Tournament.
