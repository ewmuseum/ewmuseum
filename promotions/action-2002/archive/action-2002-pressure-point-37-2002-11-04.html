A giant running clothesline out of the corner. Surprisingly though, Max shook it off. So he got another. Stupid ‘Danger Man’... didn’t even know when the “Danger” was coming. He got up again. A third and final clothesline from hell. The lawyer madly stormed around the ring. It was all him during the beginnings stages of this Match... and each and every ‘Jury’ member loved it to death. “WHOO!” LLB screamed out; he was much more outgoing than ever before... and it helped. The Crowd had never made more noise in their lifetime to date. They were suddenly silenced. Low blow: Danger. Then they booed, about as loud as they openly cheered. Danger stood up... the most cocky grin ever playing overtop of his face. “OB- WHAT!?” He smiled. And started to kick the literal crap out of the former A!W Champion. He picked LLB up, and still with that Dyad Championship Belt on his waist, he chucked the lawyer into the ropes. Waiting... waiting... Thus it came. A massive hip toss. “WHOO!” This time Danger mocked LLB. He was getting into it just as much as LLB and the Fans were. Max smirked a final time before heading up to the top rope... and that’s when he laughed. WARNING!! WARNING!! DANGER... INCOMING! Frog splash. One. Two. Kickout. With the Crowd cheering, Danger stood up... but that’s when LLB executed what he did during the Steve Christ contest. He leapt directly onto his feet, starting to scream in the face of Max Danger. “OB- OB-” LLB & the Crowd: “ERECTION!” Danger deeply took offence. He ran to the ropes and shook them like LLB had done at the beginning of the night and like he had just done during the Match. “SEE!” He screamed. “I’M CRAZY TOO!!” LLB nodded. He was glad Max Danger was going to be as outgoing as him. The two both charged in... Double clotheslines. But both leaped right back onto their feet. This time they went off the ropes. Double shoulder block. Not one person had sat down the entire Match yet... as LLB looked around the Courtroom at the millions and... Well, he didn’t want to get sued. Danger ran at him. Low bridge... and the D Champion, Title Belt and all still on him, fell out of the ring. LLB rumbled around the squared-circle again... as the Fans just played on him and played on him some more. He then stopped dead in his tracks... only to slowly turn and look at where Danger had fallen. He just couldn’t stop grinning. Danger slammed the edge of the apron. He slammed it hard before reentering. LLB waited there... and being the nice, gentle lawyer that he was, he even greeted the PIW Tag Team Champion. “OB- OB- ERECTION!” And then he waved, ala Christopher Fox. Danger progressed to his direction... but LLB caught him with a leg trip. The Fans went wild, as he reached down... and pulled the Dyad Title off of his body. ‘The Law’ looked at it. It looked a little weird... but then he remembered it came from MD... so he threw it out of the ring. Danger got the better of that, too, as the Crowd and LLB were wrapped up in the moment, Danger thought it was best to hit the lawyer with another low blow. He did. LLB had fallen. “TAKE THAT!!” Danger yelled as he walked around the ring, hands on his hips as he nodded up and down. “TAKE THAT!!” Again, a reoccurring theme. LLB jumped directly upon his heels... shaking his entire body as he screamed and looked dead into the brown eyes of Max Danger. In a way he was like a Brock Lesnar... just, without the Brock, or the Lesnar. Or that big of a body. Danger built on it, as he stared right back into LLB’s brown eyes. They walked forward... and grappled. The red-faced lawyer got the better of Max with a knee to the midsection, but Danger showed why he was a hated man. He was good... very good, no matter what they said. He was able to take things and hand them out tenfold. And dammit, he did just that. He absorbed the knee to the gut and stood up, slamming LLB right across the head with a right hand. LLB stumbled back... as Danger took into the ropes and hit a brilliant, five-star cross body block. The pin was made. One. Two. Kickout. He had come close. Very close. And as LLB stood up blocking a speedy right hand, only to fire back with an uppercut and a stunner... maybe it was a little too close. LLB was more awake than ever before. He took the D Champion and tossed him into the ropes... as Danger came off and got hit with a hurracanrana. Though LLB didn’t pin... the move really took a lot out of him. Having the club feet he did... something of that nature did hurt his legs. But he was willing to take it. Anything... literally anything to get the ‘Jury’ cheering. He went to the ropes and shook them again, as he lifted Danger up and smacked an atomic drop. Max cried out... and the former A! Champion smiled. Exactly the reaction he wanted. LLB walked to the second rope, measuring off his opponent as he debated on what move to pull. Seconds after... he jumped. Yet, it didn’t matter... Max Danger had rolled out of the way and whatever move LLB was trying to pull, concluded itself into the mat. Danger shot up, as he took LLB by the hair and chucked him out of the ring. LLB slammed against the ground below, as he even rolled into the side of the announcer’s table. Danger followed. An idea popped up inside his head. Why not fucking break the damn thing? It was there for a reason... and this Match was no DQ after all. Danger picked up ‘The Period’ and threw him on there, as he went up himself. The Fans still watched... not booing, not cheering, but chanting and believing in their fan favorite lawyer to get himself out of this mess. Max laughed though... they could chant all they wanted. It was not going to change a bloody thing. He had THREE Title Belts. And finally picking up the lawyer for the ‘Dangerous I’, as he placed him in-between his legs, he tried to actually count the number that LLB had. Oh... wait... He didn’t have any. The widest beam crossed his evilly played face. Shouldn’t have done that, Max. CCCRRRAAASSSHHH! LLB back dropped Max Danger over his shoulders, as Danger crashed into the Spanish announce table on the landing. Shaw: “At least they got it before us!” Liam could only hang his head. LLB jumped off the table... with every single member of the ‘Jury’ still cheering their verdicts and supporting the law. That warm feeling crossed his heart... giving him goosebumps thundering around his arms and neck. This was fun... this was a lot of fun. He was going to make up for that month he was gone in Action! Wrestling for... and the ions he was gone for in the Asylum. He was going to make it up... Like never, ever before. He picked up the fallen Max Danger and hurled him into the steel steps... BUT, Danger was able to reverse it at the last possible second, only to collapse on the ground. It was funny, how the momentum could change like that, so drastically... so quickly. But it did. And now it was anyone’s game again. Just how the both of them liked it. There wasn’t that much of a break in the action, though... not when the Crowd was this wound up. Not when everyone stood... not when everyone cheered... and not when everyone wasn’t ready to go home. The adrenalin... it got to you no matter what. The loud noise drove through you... and almost physically picked you up and walked you back to what kept it going. This adrenalin... for it did just that. Danger stood. LLB stood. Both with limps and pain running around their heads... they slowly marched to each other; their fists clutching together, tighter and tighter with every step it took. A few steps away... they ran at one another. CCCRRRAAASSSHHH!! Ump: “POWER- POWER FUCKING SLAM MAX DANGER TO LLB... THROUGH- THROUGH OUR GOD DAMN TABLE!!” You knew it had to be intense if Liam Ump was screaming like JR. Danger reeled back and into the edge of the apron as he rested. The Fans booed and tossed garbage at him... but Danger didn’t care. Hell, he didn’t even know. All he knew was that he was breathing really hard... and it was going to be a few weeks until this type of pain left his body again. But if you were to ask him if he cared... he’d pull an LLB. No. Fucking. Way. He was going to make this Match famous. Danger marched over to LLB, picked him up... and threw him back into the ring. However, not before getting himself two things to better his chances against the court system. A chair. And Bradley Duncan’s version of the PIW Tag Team Championships. “WHOO!!” INCOMING DANGER!! INCOMING DANGER!! Danger tossed the lawyer into the ropes, picking up the chair... Dropkick!! The chair met Danger’s skull with luminous impact, as suddenly, the Fans detonated with cheering that shook the camera and the Arena. LLB shot to his feet... fighting off the pain. He, too, was going to be in a world of hurt for about a month or so. Maybe he’d even go back to the hospital after this Match... Maybe. But maybe meant shit nothing in his world. Facts were what got you the verdict. LLB took the chair and pasted it on Danger’s fallen face... as he went up to the top rope. Facts were going to do just that. Still, the people cheered... as the real hardcore ones had cameras in their hands... but wait... LLB shook his head no. This wasn’t right. All the evidence was not there... just yet anyway. LLB got down from the top rope. The Fans did not boo though, they trusted the lawyer... which at the thought of it is morally wrong, but they did... and he went under the apron. Okay, so lawyer’s aren’t that evil. The ladder. Neighbors grabbed fellow neighbors in attendance, shaking the living shit out of them in the sheer tout for what was to come. LLB put the ladder in the ring, set it up... and started to climb. “OBJECTION!” The lawyer rubbed his head. He didn’t say that... he was almost positive. No. No he didn’t. That sound came from below. It was... Max Danger. SMACK! LLB fell off the ladder, and this time Danger marched around like the cocky brat he was. He took that PIW Tag Team Title and waited on LLB to get up. “PIW WELCOMES YOU BACK ROLAND!” He yelled, as he rushed towards LLB and totally CRUSHED his cheek with the imprint of Duncan’s Title. Everyone booed... that had NO RIGHT to go into the face of the ‘Child Written in Law’. But they could do nothing. Just boo. Just watch. Danger waited on him again. LLB rose. WWWHHH- Swoosh! ‘The Law’ ducked. Kick to the gut... and Danger had dropped the Title. Too bad for him... as LLB DDT’ed him directly on it. The former Champion got up, and bounced his head like a madman around the ring... and the ‘Jury’, they followed his lead. They did the exact same. LLB went to that ladder... But he was stopped again. Reed Young. Young hammered the shots down on the lawyer’s face... as LLB was backtracked into the ropes. Reed kept it going, screeching as he did. “HUH ROLAND!?!? WHAT!?!? NO YOU WILL NOT MAKE THIS YOUR FUCKING NIGHT!! I WANT MY RATINGS... AND I WANT THEM RIGHT!!” Young tossed LLB into the ropes. ‘ERRONEOUS CONCLUSION’. Young, meet your new hell... and it ant going away. LLB climbed that ladder. Got to the top. And couldn’t help but think what some of the critics would say. “LLB goes to extreme to beat Max Danger!”... or something like that. The lawyer’s reply was simple and honest. “ASK ME IF I CARE!” The words almost echoed throughout the Arena. “BECAUSE... I...” LLB & the Crowd: “DON’T!!!!!” Off he went. “YYYEEEAAAHHH!!!!! LLB!! LLB!! LLB!! LLB!! LLB!!” He was out. The lights were off. And LLB laid directly on top of Max Danger for the pin. James Jack did his job. He counted. ONE. TWO. THREE. “Still Waiting” by Sum 41... and a hell of a lot of screams. He made this Courtroom... and as the camera’s flashed, and the Pressure Point logo showed in the bottom left hand corner... the Courtroom... Was making him.
