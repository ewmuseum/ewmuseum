What many of the fans hadn’t noticed as Reed Young made his way to the ring was that in his hand he was carry a title belt. This wasn’t any old title belt mind you, this was THE title belt. Yes folks, Reed Young had the Action Championship in his hands. As he climbed the ring steps and went into the ring the fans began to buzz again wondering if this mystery opponent and Phil Atken were going to fight for the prestigious A Championship. Then it happened… Reed Young held out the Title towards Phil, who gladly accepted it. Reed and Phil both smiled at each other as Young walked off, to what was now dead silence from the Crowd. Atken looked at the Title. Then he surprisingly held it in the air. The Pressure Point logo showed in the bottom left-hand corner… and just like that… the night was over. Leaving Phil Atken… the new Action Champion? © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
