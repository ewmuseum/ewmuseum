Was all he could ask himself as he continued to pull himself up the ladder. The fans looked on, expecting Sharc to show signs of life. EMTs readied themselves to charge the ring. Levine continued up the ladder, his hand was just barely touching the leather briefcase that held his PIW World Championship and the wallet that held his long lost past. He could feel the end was near, the end of his miserable days of life; and the beginning of a new Levine would begin, an era where he knew his life and his past. An era of a real man, not just a wrestler. Levine reached up, now feeling the straps that held onto the briefcase. Sharc slowly began to lift his head, reaching his arm out to the ladder. Levine placed both hands upon the briefcase…and yanked down. *SNAP* The bell rang, and the fans (for the first time) began to actually cheer Levine as he fell from the ladder and onto the mat. “Introduction to Mayhem” returned to the audio system as the ring announcer announced that Levine had indeed won the battle. After about forty minutes of pure body brutality, the battle was over. EMTs made no second thoughts as they all rushed the ring and began to examine each wrestler. Levine refused the help, as he began to unlock the briefcase. Sharc, however, was far too weak to resist and was immediately placed upon a stretcher. Levine, who could barely walk, was opening the briefcase anxiously, as if he were a child opening a present. Sharc was soon carried from the ring, and began to roll up the entrance ramp; as some EMTs remained in the ring, ready to assist Levine to the backstage area for stitches. Levine quickly popped open the briefcase, and the moment of truth was upon him. He quickly retrieved his PIW World Championship…and threw it to the side…as he looked hypnotically at the wallet at sat inside. After 8 long years…it was finally over. His past would be revealed, he would no longer have to live his life as a simple dark cloud hovering over the earth. He would now know his name, his family, his forgotten life, and everything about HIM. Not about KroW, not about Levine, but about the man that hid those characters within his mind. He opened the wallet…and quickly snatched out the pieces of evidence inside. … … …. … … Nothing was in it. Levine looked through it angrily…but nothing was in it, except a white paper note written in blood. And what did it say? Sucker. Levine roared out angrily and fell to his face, slamming his fists into the mat. As the camera viewed in on the KOed Sharc being carried away on the stretcher. His eyes were closed, his body didn’t move, but a wide sadistic smile stretched over Sharc’s face as he whispered to himself. “It isn’t over Levine, not by a long shot.” Outcome: Levine wins. © Action Wrestling 2002-2004
