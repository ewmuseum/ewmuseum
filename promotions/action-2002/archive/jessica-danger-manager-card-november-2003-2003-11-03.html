What this is Jessica Danger’s card from the fed’s own Managers folder, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 3 November 2003. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is.
Jessica Danger Real Name: Jessica Michelle Dox. Also Known As: She isn't 'also known as' anything other than cute pet names and variations on her actual name. Date of Birth: Nineteenth Day of the Eighth Month of the Seventy-Ninth Year in the 20th Century. Age: Twenty-Four. Marital Status: Happily married for three years. Hometown: Currently residing with her husband in Augusta, Maine. Although, she was born in Sacramento, California. Height: Five feet Eight inches. Weight: One-Hundred Twenty-Two Pounds. Hair Color: Dark Blonde. Eye Color: Baby Blue. Blood Type: A Positive. Status: Face. Personality: Jessica is fairly friendly. Likes children. Will talk to most people, but generally stays with the same group as her husband. Enjoys reading and likes lots of quiet time, since there's so much noise out in the arena, it's nice to be away from that. Doesn't get Buffy references. Can be goofy at times. Theme Music: N.E.R.D.'s "Rock Star". Managing Style Trained By: Her husband taught her what she needed to know to be a manager. Other than that, she has no formal training for it, or in-ring action. Detailed Style: Jessica isn't your typical female manager. She doesn't use her looks and she doesn't get physically involved. She's more of a psychologically manager, in that her being there drives Max a little harder, and that can really dampen the opponent's spirits. She does a lot of palm-pounding on the ring apron, clapping, and shouting of encouragement to her husband. Strengths: Her love for her husband. Just by being there it gives Danger boosts of adrenaline. Her words of encouragement through the matches puts extra life into him. Also, according to some people, quite the stiffener. Weaknesses: Hasn't really got the physical strength, and isn't intimidating in that way, to harm opponents. Has the looks, but fails to use them for the reasons of distraction. Injuries Current Injuries: Nothing but a small scar on her right hand, from when it was cut on Sharc's barbwire 2x4. Nagging Injuries: None. History Years Pro: Three. Manages: Max Danger. Allies: Her husband, Sweet Aroma, and Conrad Ramsey. Max has been friendly with Rook Black, Frank Heimdal, and Allison Lindum as of late, which, in turn, means she has been. Nemesis: She shares the same dislike for Jeff Garvin and Keith Scott Zimmerman has her husband. Doesn't feel the same about Coral as Max does, though. Is also very much wigged out by Bradley Duncan at the moment. Formerly Managed: Bradley Duncan, alone and as part of Non-Verbal Communication, as well as Blast. Other Federations: OWA(front office role) for three months, GFWA for five months, PIW for 5 months, the Asylum for 2 months, and Spinebuster Wrestling for two months. Attributes Intelligence: Ten. Cheating: One. Speed: Five. Physical Harm to Opponent: Two. Psychological Harm to Opponent: Nine. Courage: Eight. Charisma: Seven. Importance: Eleven. Biography It would probably be best to withhold all information on Ms. Jessica Michelle Dox, in the best interest of all parties. If one were to give out the fact that Jessica used to work for Sunkist Growers in California, it made let some pervert break in, go through their records, and find out all sorts of other information on her. Or that her parents live in a large, white, estate in Sacramento, California. Sure, that'd be vague information, but the fact that it has DOX FAMILY written on the mailbox, oh, and where the finest topiary garden in all of California(or so says the workers who, ya know, actually make them). If someone were to know that, then they could go to her house, sneak over the brick fence(using a handy ladder that one fired worker left, and everyone else was too lazy to put up), sneak across the back yard and up the latice with vines and various flowers growing about it. (The latice goes up the back wall a bit, and stops just underneath a window.) Though that window would lead you into the guest bedroom. No one stays there, but it's decorated with enough odds and ends that you would think a avid fan of the Golden Girls roomed there. (A Bea Arthur life-sized stand-up rests in the corner.) Go out the door and to the left, along a creaky, but seldomly occupied hallway, and into the fourth room on the right, passing the guest bathroom, the second guest bedroom(decorated in various A-Team memorabilia), and then past a room no one has been in for twenty-seven years, long before Ms. Jessica was even born. In any event, head through that fourth door on the left and you will have come upon a very odd room. A room with nothing but a pole in it. A fireman's pole, to be exactly. Slide down it, and through the wide hole in the floor, and you will have entered the childhood and teenaged-years room of one Jessica Michelle Dox. There you will find all her old personal belongings that she never felt to take with her as she went to college, then got married, and moved to Augusta, Maine. No, we won't be telling any of that information. There are stalkers about, and if they got their hands on such viable information... well, we just wouldn't be able to live with ourselves. So bugger off! The life and times of one Jessica Michelle Dox is one of great pain and suffering through the early years of her life. Her existence lacked meaning before she met one Marcus Alexander Dox. She was directionless. Lost. Not a hope in the world. But he changed all that... Okay, that's just bullshit. I'm just too lazy to write it up at the moment. Someday, though, I promise. Off Stage Information Name: Michael D. AOL Instant Messager Screen Name: [detail withheld] Interests: Elisha Cuthbert. Also, writing in my blog, and just writing in general. Favorite Pro Wrestler: My Lord and Savior, Chris Jericho. © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
