The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Max Danger Birth Name: Marcus Alexander Dox. Also Known As: The Danger Man and the King of Submission. Date of Birth: Twenty-Third Day of the Eight Month of the Seventy-Seventh Year in the Twentieth Century. Age: Twenty-six. Marital Status: Married, happily, for three years. Hometown: Augusta, Maine. Height: Six feet, Zero inches. Weight: Two-Hundred Ten Pounds. Hair Color: Dirty Blonde. Eye Color: Blue, Green, or Gray. Depends on what type of environment he's in. Blood Type: A Positive. Status: Face Personality: Nice to wife and close friends. Tends to come off as more of a smartass and dick towards those he doesn't know to well. But, generally, will talk to if spoken to, and will be mostly nice about it. Doesn't hate anyone(besides Garvin). Won't go around shaking hands and having chats with everyone, though. Theme Music: N.E.R.D.'s 'Rock Star'. Wrestling Style Trained By: Greg Foster, Charles Dox, and Willis Chamberlain. Detailed Style: Max Danger, aside from his limited high-flying arsenal(though, in the past, it was extensive), is one of the most complete wrestlers on the planet. There's a reason he's had the success he's had and how come a lot of his students turn out to be excellent wrestlers as well. Strengths: His all-around wrestling ability. Adaptable to any situation. Tough. Very tough. High endurance. Possibly the best submission wrestler in the promotion, hence the 'King of Submission' moniker. And Jessica. Weaknesses: His bad back. Takes a lot of punishment. And Jessica. Signature Moves: Max Danger has a whole hell of a lot of signature moves. Not all are used often, or at all. But he has them. So see how and when such things should be used, head over to the Match Writing Aid. Dangerous I, is a Middle Rope Tombstone Piledriver. Dangerous II, happens to be a Top Rope Brainbuster. Failing that, it can be converted into a Top Rope Fisherman's Brainbuster. Dangerous III, would be the Roaring Elbow. Dangerous IV, see below. Dangerous V, would be Bradley Duncan's Cross Armbreaker. Dangerous '99, this would be one of those rare changes to what the 'Dangerous' was. It started out as the top rope brainbuster/fisherman's buster, but it did change completely once, which was here. It was a simple vertical suplex into a stidown piledriver. It is hyper-rare that Danger would ever bust it out, however. DangerBuster, is a Crossed-Arms Brainbuster. Danger Splash, is the Shooting Star Press. Double Jeopardy, can either be a seated or elevated Surfboard with Dragon Sleeper. Double Jeopardy, Code Alpha, why the odd name, 'cause orginally 'Double Jeopardy' was a running Liger Bomb followed by an Elevated Boston Crab. (The version with the knee or foot on the back of the opponent's head.) So, Danger being who he is, he named it thus. Hammer Jammer, would be Jeff Garvin's Sharpshooter, which Max has swiped, and likes using against the Original. MX-Bomb, is a Reverse Crucifx Powerbomb, with a variation that's a Reverse Crossed-Arm Crucifix Powerbomb. MX-Varietas, is a STF, the regular, along with it's many variations. Hence the name. RAMYOUFACE, is the essentially retired Shining Wizard Running Knee Strike. It's all but been eliminated from Danger's moveset and won't be busted out often. Trifecta, is Danger's take on the rolling suplexes. Only he hits a German, Tiger, and then Dragon Suplex. Can also be coupled with a fourth suplex, but that's hardly ever. Wild Bomb, is the quick, stiff as all hell Powerbomb that the Danger Man performs. Can also be changed up into a sitdown or running version. Trademark Name: Dangerous IV. Trademark Move: Chicken Wing Piledriver. Injuries Current Injuries: If you don't know by now, you must have been living under a rock deep within the recesses of the Earth, with earplugs and eye patches, while tied in a burlap sack, and held in a small container of water. Or something like that. But in actuality, the Danger Man suffers from a major BACK injury. It doesn't tend to affect him too greatly during the matches, but he feels the agonizing pain at every moment he is is outside the squared circle. Nagging Injuries: For the majority of Danger's career, he was injury free. A lone knee injury was all that he had suffered. (And one that does not nag him today.) But back in the fall/near winter of 2001, Max suffered a horrific injury; form of... broken neck! He was given the tombstone piledriver off the top rope and through a table. Yeah, it went horribly wrong. He was out for only five monhts, however, so it actually wasn't that serious of a break. But, it does still nag him somewhat, especially if you start dropping him on his head, which he has no problem with. History Years Pro: Nine. Manager: Jessica Danger. Allies: His wife Jessica is the most obvious ally. Also the Aqua Teen Hunger Force, well, Coral Avalon and Rook Black anyway. He hasn't had much time spent with the other two. Nemesis: He finds Keith Scott Zimmerman to be a general nuisance. He absolutely hates Jeff Garvin.. HardCase is a fuckhole. And he's slightly troubled with Bradley Duncan. Wrestling 101, in general, won't be receiving Christmas cards from him. Current Team: Aqua Teen Hunger Force with Coral Avalon, Rook Black, Frank Heimdal, and Duel. Past Teams: Non-Verbal Communication with Bradley Duncan, Canadian Violence Connection and B³ with Conrad Ramsey, M&M's with Bobby Minio, and Instant Ratings with Brad Robinson. Action Wrestling Title History: Dyad Championship with Bobby Minio for 51 days, Carnage Championship for 198 days, and Bantam Championship for 76 days. Action Wrestling Award History: 2003 King of Submission at Blood, Sweat, & Tears and Voted Most Improved Wrestler at 1st Annual Awards. Other Federations: EWCWF/FWO for 7 months, OWA for 8 months, GFWA for 15 months, FSW for 4 months, PIW for 5 months, gfwa OMEGA for 8 months, Advantage Wrestling for 1 match, and the Asylum for 2 months. Other Federation Awards: EWCWF/FWO Television Title for 28 days, EWCWF/FWO Cruiserweight Title(2 times) for 0 days each, EWCWF/FWO World Tag Team Titles(2 times) as Instant Ratings for ??? days and 3 days, EWCWF/FWO Extreme Hardcore Tag Team Titles as Instant Ratings for 29 days, EWCWF/FWO Intercontinental Title for 72 days, GFWA US Heavyweight Title for 12 days, EWCWF/FWO World Title(2 times) for 24 days and 6 days, GFWA Lightwegiht Title for ??? days, FSW Rising Star Title for 48 days, PIW World Tag Team Titles as NVC for 100 days, Action! Dyad Titles with Bobby Minio for 51 days, and Action! Carnage Title still being held. Non-Title awards include The News Match of the Week(2 times) for the Clit 'N Candy Express vs. Max Danger & Bobby Minio Dyad Title Match at Pressure Point 32 and versus Brad Schilling at Pressure Point 35. Attributes Intelligence: Outside the ring, Danger is fairly smart. He did go to college for like five days. Inside the ring, there aren't too many more intelligent than he is. He just knows exactly what he's supposed to be doing. (9) Strength: Max isn't a very strong man. He is able to distribute his opponent's weight and his own to lift guys bigger than he is for his suplexes and such. But he isn't going to be winning any weight-lifting contests in the near future. His back also plays a large factor in his struggle to really lift people. (6) Toughness: The Danger Man is extremely tough. He is just a skosh below Coral Avalon on the scale of how much punishment he can take. And he can dish out just as much as he takes. Besides, he's given up just the once despite the fucked up back. You gotta be tough to do that, eh? (10) Speed: He used to be extremely fast. Blazing all over the ring. But now he's a little less-so, but not too much. His quickness is almost unmatched. He can transistion from move to move in the blink of an eye. So he's fairly quick, but his foot speed is a little slower than it used to be. (7) Endurance: Would it surprise if I said Danger had never been in an Iron Man Match? Well, he hasn't. But that's not to say his endurance isn't off the charts. He's wrestled long, long matches. He's wrestled over an hour before, but never in something that was called an Iron Man Match. He, generally, will go upwards of 30-40 minutes nightly. That's the type of endurance he has. Not the ability, for one night, to go long, but to go long night after night. (11) Courage: Danger isn't afraid to go at it with anyone. No matter the size or how crazed the opponent is. Plus, he goes out and wrestles nightly with a nearly broken back. That takes courage. (9) Charisma: Max is, like Coral, a very charismatic person inside the ring. Kinda like Chris Benoit. He, however, unlike those two is much more charismatic outside the ring as well. He isn't on the level of a KSZ or LLB, but he can hold his own on the microphone. He's much better with backstage skits rather than out in the ring, though. (8) Skill: Do I even need to say anything here? (10) Biography Masked. Worked many dates in Japan and Mexico under the guise of saidaigan KIKEN. A masked wrestler. He would often times pull double-duty. The last appearance of the character was in early 2002, when Max returned from a broken neck in the gfwa OMEGA. Atheist. Xander has absolutely no belief in the existence of God. It is not a major character trait. He just is, pure and simple. Jessica is agnostic, if you must know. Xmas. The abbreviated word for Christmas, Danger's most beloved holiday. Birthdays, Thanksgiving, Easter... all of those things paled in comparison to Christmas. His enitre life, he loved it the most. I mean, who wouldn't? Outside of the Jews, who was opposed to celebrating Christmas? (Okay, so there were others, but for the sake of this, only the Jews exist, okay? Okay.) Everyone loved getting gifts! (And damn those Jews for getting multiple days to give gifts. See, they know how to do it right.) Well Max never gave any care to the religious aspects of the holiday, he does firmly believe that the man who was supposedly born on this day, December 25th, will be coming back as a zombie to eat everyone's brains. You telling me Futurama would lie? That's impossible. Futurama is truth. DVDs. The Danger Man owns a very large collection of DVDs. Upwards of 400, and with the total growing by the day. Aqua Teen Hunger Force. The Adult Swim block is the home to many a great cartoon, like The Brak Shaw, Space Ghost Coast to Coast, Sealab 2021, among others. But the one staring a giant milk shake, a floating box of fries, a lump of meatball, and their human neighbor Carl, stands out as the most fucked up one of all. It also happened to be the name that a small collection of wrestlers had called themselves. The group consisted of Rook Black(Master Shake), Duel(Meatwad), Frank Heimdal(Frylock), Coral Avalon(Carl). Two new members were brought into the fold given their friendship with Rook and Coral. Yup, that's right, Max and Jessica Danger. They were dubbed Inignot and Err of the Mooninites respectively. Never had a single job his entire life. He was always financially supported by his mother until he started working independent wrestling shows. Supported himself through various tours of Japan and Mexico. Now is financially secure due to his inheritance from his mother's death. So, he and Jessica won't be strapping for cash anytime soon. Plus, Max has that insurance policy with Lloyd's of London. Gear. As in Max Danger's extensive wrestling gear. There is no way of telling exactly how many pairs of tights the Danger Man actually owns. He has entire outfits. The most recent addition being his Halloween costume of Spike from Buffy the Vampire Slayer Angel. Please, excuse his photo above, because he doesn't wear that attire at all. Engaged once before. Just a few short months before meeting the love of his life, Jessica, Xander was engaged to be married to his bestfriend during his childhood all the way through freshman year of college. Sarah Haplan was her name. They remain friends to this day, and live very close to one another in Augusta. She broke it off. She, also, was Xander's first sexual partner. (There have only been three.) Renaissance Man. Not the 14th-17th centuries in Europe, but rather to mean that Max is 'refined and educated' as per the words of one Ben Pants. See, Danger knows things. More specifically, he knows Japanese. He can read, write, understand, and speak the language. It was almost something he had to do when he was spending a lot of time wrestling in the country. Oddly enough, he never could be bothered to learn sign language during all the years of training and partnering with Bradley Duncan.
