SMACK. ‘Erroneous Conclusion’. They all booed, beginning to throw their waste in the ring, as LLB hip tossed Fox over the top rope and to the outside. He then began to fight him all the way up the ramp, until the two disappeared behind it. Young rolled around on the mat, as he dusted himself off slowly, with blood rolling down the top of his forehead. That stupid, stupid Fox. Chants began... as the people jumped up and down, and Young’s horrified face was seen, lit in red... as he rolled his eyes back... and just passed out.
