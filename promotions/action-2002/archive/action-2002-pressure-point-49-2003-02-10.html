Mark Out. Starring: Max Danger & Keith Scott Zimmerman. His Action! Championship Match against Syphon Fission had been taken away all because of a prank. A stupid little prank. For he was pissed off enough, already jobbed out of getting back his Belt in thanks to Neko Case. Regardless of the rules at which Reed Young and the Action Staff had set out, Syphon Fission marched down the ramp and rolled into the ring. No theme song played, nor did one need to. The Fans cheered lightly... still caught off-guard by the events of LLB, and of the whole show in general. Fission grabbed the Ref’s shirt, telling him to get Julius down to the ring as fast as possible. A crank of his theme, “What’s Golden”, and Julius Arcade came marching down to some cheers. He rolled into the ring, right before Fission dropped the elbow on top of his head. He then tossed Arcade into the ropes... and crushed him with a giant elbow to the forehead. Another toss to the ropes was then seen, as Fission lifted up his knee and caught Julius square under the jaw line. The Fans cheered as Syphon roared to the Crowd, taking Arcade and lifting him up again... in looks for a chokeslam. But Arcade did come to play too, as he grabbed Fission’s face and mashed his eyes in, to snap into the ropes soon after and connect with a European uppercut. The Fans also cheered at this, as Arcade took Fission’s head and locked it into a dragon sleeper. Smack. The ring-smarts of Fission played off, as he backtracked Julius into the corner. An elbow to the head after that, and Fission was well on his way to a good roll. A bufferfly suplex kicked it off, as the A Champion took hold of Arcade and this time hit a snap suplex... only to hold it hitting two more. Then, he pinned. One. Two. But for an older man, Julius could still do it. He was hanging in there with a highly raged, pissed off, machine of madness. Syphon got to his feet, tossed Arcade into the ropes, and drilled him with a high knee to the side of the head. Straight up Arcade went, as he stood there, waiting to be planted on the ground... And he was, via a gut wrench suplex into a neck breaker. Once again, Fission roared when he rose, taking Julius along with him as well as the final stages of the Match. A overhead belly to belly suplex... and Syphon went for the victory... ‘The Final Word’. One. Two. Three. The bell went, thus cueing his theme song, but Fission stayed in the ring, kicking Arcade out of it and taking Pat Hoskins’ mic. “Now...” He said, as the Fans cheered in hearing his voice. “Now I’m going to make this short and sweet. Cylear... I don’t care anymore. You can have my Title on your waist... because at the Pay-Per-View, that’s where I will win it back from your God damn hands. It’s this simple. At ‘Blood, Sweat, Tears’ I walk in the Champion... and nor you, LLB, nor Christopher Fox are going to stop me.” In seeing Julius roll around on the outside, Fission dropped the mic, picked him up and put him back into the ring. I will retain my Title.” Tossing the mic to the floor, Fission stepped over the top rope and walked all the way back up the ramp. He stopped at the top, turned to look at everyone, and roared. On Sunday, he would do his best to destroy the ‘Showcase’, LLB, and Chris Fox... as well as anyone else in his path... to retain his Action Title.
