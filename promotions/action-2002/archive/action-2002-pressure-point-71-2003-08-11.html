Pressure Point 71 Target Center — Minneapolis, Minnesota ---- PRESSURE POINT. PRESSURE POINT. Setting Up The Main Starring: Max Danger & "the Original" Jeff Garvin Writer(s): Michael K. D., Mike R. & probably Devin W. Pressure Point, live from the Target Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota, was just hitting the airwaves. Everyone in attendence was screaming at the top of their lungs and holding their signs high, so that the camera may focus on them and have their 'creativity' shown throughout the world. Of course, little did the fans know, but they normally, a good percentage of the time, weren't creative at all. But it didn't matter by the time N.E.R.D.'s "Rock Star" began to blast through the speakers. The fans all turned their attention to the entry way, as Max Danger, the Carnage Champion, walked through the curtains, title shining brightly around his waist, and beautiful wife, Jessica holding his right hand. They stopped at the top of the ramp and soaked in all the cheers. It hadn't been long since Danger was succumed by the Good Side and reverted from his dark, scheming, asshole ways. And the fans were very appreciative of the matches he had given them. Putting his body on the line and all that. Together, he and his wife walked down to the ring. Taking their time. Why hurry? Not long the show was going to run long or anything. Danger, however, despite the goodness that he had finally decided to show, still wasn't one to slap hands with the fans. Jessica, as a result, didn't do so either. Instead, they walked down the center of the ramp, made a long turn around the railing, and then Jessica started up the steps, with Danger hold her hand. She went across the apron and stopped, waiting for her husband to join her. As he got to her, he turned and looked out at all the people. He pointed out a couple of signs, which made Jessica laugh a little bit. The King of Submission then held the ropes open for his beautiful, stunning wife, allowing her to enter. Once she had, she walked over to the adjacent ropes and was handed a microphone from Pat Hoskins. Danger, meanwhile, had walked across the rings and climbed onto the ropes, putting one foot on the top and the other on the middle. The lights shined brightly off his polished silver Carnage Title. The fans continued to cheer wildly, as Danger stepped down off the ropes, wincing ever-so-slightly, and rejoined his wife in the center of the ring. He thanked her with a kiss on the temple for her getting the microphone and then addressed the Minneapolis crowd. "Hello!" There was still a bit of his smartass-ness inside of him. That would never go away. It's just who he was. And his greeting to the crowd wasn't the most sincere of things. But, really, outside of getting a cheap 'mention the hometown' pop, there wasn't are real comfortable way of welcoming the people, so to speak. They didn't notice anyway, shouting back, "HI, MAX!" Danger smiled at that. That was cute. Jessica, too, had a smile on her face. Danger then went right into what he wanted to talk about, "As you all may or may not know, as of last week, when I lost to Keith Scott Zimmerman, I was mathematically eliminated from the Round Robin Tournament." The fans booed loudly. They didn't want to see the Wrestling 101 bums reach the finals either. Danger held up a hand, "It's alright. I'm still the Carnage Champion afterall." He said with a smirk. "And I fully intend to bring the title back up to it's previous standards, which were also set by me, when Instinct rolls around, because not only will I hold this title, well, I will also be in possession of Coral's Bantam Title." Cheer or boo? Well, it was a bit of a mixture. The Danger Man got that. He did. "I know that all of you aren't going to be happy with that, and that's alright. I'm sure, if he were to say the same thing," he then mumbled off-microphone to Jessica, "Not that he would, the man's terrible on the mic." And then he returned to his original thought, "You all would give him the same mixed response. If not... shrug. No big. You cheer who you like, boo who you don't. And if it comes down to it, well, you're going to choose sides or remain neutral. Nature of the beast. But that's not really my point. No, later tonight, as I'm sure you all know, I will be having my final Round Robin Tournament match with Mr. Jeff Garvin." The fans booed at the mere mention of the Original One's name. They did not, just like Max, like the former Bantam Champion. "Now, while it is correct that there isn't a slimmer of hope of me going to Instinct to compete for the Action! Title... there is for Jeff. I, however, am going to do everything in my power to see to it that he doesn't get that opportunity. My job is to play spoiler. Last week I failed. I couldn't get the job done against Keith. It happens. And I'm not going to use Garvin's run-in as an excuse. I was done. I wasn't going to be able to come back." The fans were confused. Danger was usually very, very confident. Max took a deep breath, "My game has been off a bit as of late. I know this, and I'm sure you all have noticed this as well. Losing to Frank and Russa were not big things. They are talented individuals, and were just better on that night. Just as I was better earlier this year when I beat Heidmal... though just barely, and last year when Bobby Minio and myself successfully defended our Dyad Titles against Russa and his dairy-unable-to-haver partner. But, I vow before you, that I will be the better man tonight. After he was gone, Danger tossed the microphone into the hands of Hoskins and his music kicked up.
