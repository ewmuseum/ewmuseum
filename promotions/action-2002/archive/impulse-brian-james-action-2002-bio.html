Impulse Brian James Biography Nicknames IBJ, The BLONG Mastah, The Phoenix of Wrestling, 'The Eternal Journeyman', 'The Unluckiest Man in All of Wrestling' (Not to his face, of course) Hometown Fort Worth, Texas, U.S.A. Birthday First of July, Nineteen Seventy-Seven Height 6'3' Weight 225 Biography Things to know about one Brian Spaes: He ran away from home to Chicago in the early 1990's, not the wisest move ever done. He was a street rat much of his teenage years in the Hyde Park suburb of Chicago. Trained by 70's-90's wrestling superstar Jim Schwartz. An unknown incident occured at his debut match against the above Jim Schwartz that left him out of favor in Evanston Wrestling. He will not speak of the match against Jim Schwartz. Has 2 younger brothers. One of his younger brothers commited suicide, the other he hasn't seen him since he was attacked by him. He is still recovering from his brother and wife's death. He will not speak about the child that his wife was carrying at her death. Brian's greatest joy in his life is his son, Jack Alexander, age 6. Brian has a growing reluctancy on alcohol. Brian's girl friend, Denise Caruthers, recently resigned from a powerful Dallas-Fort Worth law firm to homeschool Jack. Rumors have been spreading about the possibility of marriage on the horizon.
