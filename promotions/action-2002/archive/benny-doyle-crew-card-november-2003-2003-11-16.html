What this is Benny Doyle’s card from the fed’s own Crew folder (referees, doctor and other staff), as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 16 November 2003. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is.
Benny Doyle Real Name: Benjamin Francis Doyle Also Known As: Benny Date of Birth: September 3, 1982 Hometown: Quin, Ireland Height: 5'8" Weight: 167 lbs. Favorite Wrestler: Sweet Aroma Least Favorite Wrestler: Giovanni Ollivander DeNucci Favorite Trademark Move: Dangerous IV Favorite Catchphrase: "OBJECTION!" -- Classic. Attributes Intelligence: 7 Bump Taking: 5 Counting Speed(out of 5 - which is the fastest): 3 Leniency: 7(He won't count you out, but he won't allow weapons and the like.) Ease of Retaliation: 4(Won't retaliate much, but will DQ you.) Eye Sight: 5(Sees a lot, but not everything. Good luck, though.) Biography Benjamin Francis Doyle, a native of Quin, Ireland, was born to Martha and Francis Doyle on September 3, 1982. He wasn't a big wrestling fan by any stretch of the imagination during childhood. In fact, like a lot of his fellow countrymen, he was big on football, or soccer to you American bitches. His father and mother were farmers. That's what he did when he didn't attend school and play football. His dream was to become a professional football player, but unfortunately, he never really went for that dream. He was caught up in the wrestling scene by the time he finished up high school. He figured he wouldn't have a chance in hell of being an actual wrestler, so he decided to become a referee and work as a manager a little bit. Referee, he found, was extremely fun. One day, he read about a wrestling company looking for good young officials. That company was Action! Wrestling. Benny Doyle flew in, tapes in hand, ready to show Reed Young what he could do. He didn't have to though as Reed hired him right away. At first, thinking he was a wrestler, then realizing he was a referee. That wacky Reed Young. He has now become the unofficial official Max Danger/Conrad Ramsey referee. He does ALL their matches, and has come to actually befriend Conrad Ramsey. Max Danger, though, he's a tougher cookie to crack. But, Benny Doyle is having the time of his life. (He's just happy he was able to get a work Visa. Would have been embarrassing otherwise.) His goal is to become the best referee of all-time. And Benny thinks he can do that. And he thinks that reffing the main event of Juggernaut III would be the best way to do that. *crosses fingers* Here's hoping. Definitions of Attributes: Intelligent - Pretty self-explanatory. How smart is the Referee? Bump Taking - The ability to take bumps. A higher number means he takes better bumps and sells them well. A lower number means, well, he no-sells like the Undertaker and won't go down unless it's a big chairshot or something similar. If it's somewhere in the middle, then it'll take more than a simple "knocking into the guy". A move will likely need to be done. Or a vicious bump to the floor. Counting Speed - (Out of Five) How fast he counts(includes ring out count). The higher the number the faster he counts. The lower the slower. In the middle, just right. Leniency - How much he will allow you to get away with. The higher the more brawling he will allow. Also, with a higher number, he won't use the count out, unless completely necessary(like if only one person is outside the ring). The lower, the less he'll let you get away with. Stupid by-the-books cock. Ease of Retaliation - Will he retaliate if pushed(and so forth)? The higher the more likely he is to strike back and THEN DQ you. Funny how that works. The lower, the less likely he is to hit back, and might be a bit to scared to call for a DQ. Eye Sight - Not 20/20 or anything. What he sees. Does he see the feet on the ropes? Interference? Other forms of cheating? That sort of thing. The higher the number the better chance you have of being caught, you cheating bastards(I'm looking at you KSZ!). The lower the less likely you are to be seen. So, have fun! © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
