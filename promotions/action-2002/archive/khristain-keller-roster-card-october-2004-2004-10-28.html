What this is Khristain Keller’s card from the fed’s own roster page, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 28 October 2004. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Khristain Keller Birth Name: Kyle Keller Also Known As: K² Date of Birth: July 13th, 1970 Age: 33 Marital Status: Unknown Hometown: Miami, Florida Height: 6'4'' Weight: 255lbs Hair Color: Hair? Eye Color: Blue Blood Type: AB+ Status: Heel Personality: Keller is selfish, egotistical, rude, ill-mannered, disgusting, violent, unforgiving and undeserving of any praise he gets...but other than that, he's a really nice guy. Theme Music: 'Dirty Window' - Metallica Wrestling Style Trained By: James Tarbuck and Doug Finaly Detailed Style: Benoit maybe, but he is really aggressive to boot. Strengths: He would say everything... Weaknesses: He would say none... Signature Moves: Favourite Technical Moves: Keller has a wide array of moves spanning from his days in Japan, his days in the US, and creations of his vile mind. Spiked Piledriver is a favourite of his ever since he crushed young Buckley’s neck back in 1988. Figure 4 Leg Lock was his “specially” in Japan, but he used to accompany the reality old school leg lock with hammering his opponent’s knees and things with hard shots from his fists, just to add to the effect. Favourite Speedy Moves: Multiple counters...some don't even have names yet, and baffle any play-by-play announcer, he even left Jim Ross expressionless on a WWF trial…which isn’t a hard thing to do I guess. Favourite Brawling Moves: And it all depends on the area in which he is fighting in, Keller will use anything to help him in victory, a chair, table, bat, pole, dust, walls, cage and even a mix of the previous with a kick to the balls for added effect. Trademark Name: Tranquilizer Trademark Move: JackHammer with a spike at the end, which leaves your spine and neck crumbled to the edge of paralyses…none of this Goldberg shit. Injuries Current Injuries: None that have been brought to the attention of any promoters. Nagging Injuries: The usual back and neck problems that effect every wrestler in the industry today. History Years Pro: Since 1988 Manager: None. Allies: Is there such a thing? Nemesis: Alias Current Team: None. Past Teams: None. Action Wrestling Title History: None. Action Wrestling Award History: None. Other Federations: aWc, ACW and WWC(Current) Other Federation Awards: Attributes Intelligence: 8 Strength: 8 Toughness: 10 Speed: 6 Endurance: 8 Courage: 8 Charisma: 8 Skill: 9 Biography You're damn right I'm a bad man, I've been a bastard ever since I came from the womb, but hey, life's a bitch, you might as well be on the same side as her, right? OK, OK, a lot of people say that I changed in 90', I'll agree whole heartedly. Buckley showed me that one mistake by anyone can end you, and your career, but my theory does have some flaws yeah. I lived on in wrestling and have the persona as a mean mother-fucker. Buckley eats through a straw and can't move anything below his neck. Shit happens. It's how you smell coming out of it that determines were you are going in this world. You'll see... Off Stage Information Name: Ben S.
