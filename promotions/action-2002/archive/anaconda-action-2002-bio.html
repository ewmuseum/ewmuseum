Anaconda Biography Hometown Tucson, Arizona Birthday April 17th, 1957 Height 5'9' Weight 200 Biography Where some may wonder how such a man of this stature is able to provide a sense of fear and doubt into the minds of opponents when facing this submission specialist, all doubts are put to an end when meeting Anaconda in person. Once a former TWF wrestler for Forest Young’s federation, Anaconda has decided to make a return to the wrestling ring. At the age of 46, he does not look like, or feel like a man of extreme age... but rather a man to accomplish some of the things he had not done in the past. Bounded by no limits, the figure that is Anaconda will make as much impact in Action as he once did in The Wrestling Federation. He will be strong, tough, and mentally capable of going up against a seven foot giant... as if he was only a mere three feet. His task: to gain a higher rank than when he wrestled 10 years ago. His determination: already has him right there. Beware of the one they call Anaconda. Beware. For if he lurks near you, stay still... or suffer in the grip of his talent. Other Feds The Wrestling Federation (TWF). Other Fed Awards TWF's 3rd Title (x3); TWF's 1st Tag Team Champion (x2 - w/ Seeker - w/ Mark Paulton); TWF's 2nd Tag Team Champion (x1 - w/ Cowboy Carson). Wrestling Style Submission. Finisher Move 'The Coil'. Finisher Description Scissor Hold. Special Moves 'The Inhaltz' (Anaconda stands behind the dazed opponent, and by grabbing both of the opponent's arms, he crosses them from one another [as if the opponent was in a straightjacket]. By arching Anaconda's back, he begins to pull the opponent backwards [and, if possible, to lift him off the ground]). Entrance Music ''Suffocate'' Cold
