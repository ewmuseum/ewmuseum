What this is The site’s own Crew page: the owner, the vice-president, the referees, the doctor, the announcers and the reporters, each in a short write-up, from the Wayback capture of 10 August 2004. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the page’s text is.
CREW. THE OFFICE: Reed Young, Owner/President The Owner of Action Wrestling. Reed Young is the son of a billionaire, and Action Wrestling is his thirtieth or so wrestling federation… not to mention other ideas like boxing wrestling, football wrestling, and wrestling wrestling. So, over one-hundred failed projects more than likely. But Reed keeps trying. His goal: fame. Hey, at least he’s being honest. He wants RATINGS and will think about closing Action Wrestling about fifteen times a day if he feels this place is not giving him just that. Young is not a Chris Universal. He’s not even a Joe Campbell. He’s an idiot, plain and simple. His drunken firings of the old Action roster proves that. Can he ever be wrong? In his mind, no. So Young continues to run Action Wrestling… more stupid than ever before. But in his mind, this is the best… Fed… (Valley Girl) EEEVVVEEERRR (/Valley Girl). And no one, not even Zeus, can change his mind on that. Jeremy Bold, Vice-President The Vice President of Action Wrestling, Jeremy Bold is in his forties… and still searching for that special someone (aren’t we all?). He’s just as ratings-smitten and stupid as Reed Young is, if not more-so. Jeremy carries a hot… pink… binder… with him, containing what he would believe to be ratings-making plans, and the average person would think that they’re the stupidest things ever thought up. Jeremy normally steals his ideas from other federations or other feuds… or, if you’re lucky, he’ll totally pull them out of his ass. Now that’s a treat. THE REFS: Ryan Watts, Head Referee The Head Referee of Action Wrestling after James Jack was paralyzed in the month of April, 2003, at the hands of LLB. Watts is very talented. He has a keen eye-sight and is a Referee that is in demand these days in the wrestling world. He can actually take a chair shot. Or a DDT. He’s young (only 19). He’s athletic. He’s everything any Referee would want to become. There’s just one problem. That being he’s *only* 19. This is a guy who would still like to party. This is a guy who is only Refereeing because his Dad passed away just one year ago. Watts likes to mingle. You can always find him backstage. And he’s normally talking about the girl he slept with last. Also, don’t be fooled… he seems to miss a lot of “cheating” in the ring, as he’s always checking out the cuties in the Crowd. Hey, at least you don’t need to distract him… Ref Reffington, Referee After being the “default” Referee for sometime in Action, Ref has moved into the second position next to Ryan. Ref married young… to 17-year-old Uma Umpire (she had kept her maiden name). Together they have three wonderful little Refs. Ref’s sister, Lyines Judge (she married to a man named Back Judge), is the only known woman who is a line judge in the NHL. The Reffington’s are a very family oriented bunch… and their goal is to bring you professional refereeing, at an affordable price. ‘Big’ Mark Mann, Referee You don’t want to mess with ‘Big’ Mark. Or maybe you do. He is over 300-pounds of pure fat. This is a major weakness in his refereeing… since it may take him ten or so seconds to drop to the mat and begin counting the pinfall. So why did Reed hire him? Well could you eat twenty-four donuts in a record fifty-three seconds? Didn’t think so. Nancy Frasier, Referee Believe it or not, Nancy Frasier is actually pretty normal (except for the name). And… well, aside from the fact that he thinks he’s a super hero. Sometimes, in the middle of a match, his cell phone will ring… and he’ll just walk out. Normally Ref Reffington will come down to take his place. No one’s sure though what Frasier is doing. If the match is a long bout… he may even return later on. Referee at night. Hero during the day. Does this make sense? Does anything make sense anymore? We’re not sure… THE STAFF: Dr. David Nurse, Federation Doctor Dr. Nurse is fully qualified… for broadcasting. So why is he the Doctor? Um… well… Reed needed someone to be after he fired Dr. Dick (very original name, Reed Young thought). So, Nurse, straight out of Carleton University in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada, with dreams to become the next Liam Ump or Derik Shaw, is now the one and only Doctor in Action Wrestling. Does he mind? No. He’s getting paid. He’s getting paid a lot. Does he know what he’s doing? His BA in Journalism says he doesn’t… THE BROADCASTERS: Liam Ump, Play-by-Play Announcer (English) Liam use to be a wrestler for Reed Young back in the day… in one of his very early Federations. No one’s really asked which. Ump now finds himself the play-by-play man for Pressure Points and Pay-Per-Views. And you know what? He’s a lot better than JR… that’s for sure. Ump’s the normal good-guy. He gets angered when wrestlers are not moral. And he does not get along with Derik Shaw, his partner. Hell, who would? Derik Shaw, Color Commentator In what has to be the most colorful, fearless, sharp yet stupid idiot on Action Wrestling’s crew, Derik Shaw is a heel at heart and yet one of the most personal people you can find. He likes to get under Liam’s skin. He loves it when wrestlers take the cheap way out. And if you’re one of his favorite… he may even start cheering for you over Liam’s very own words! Shaw hosts a weekly show titled “Whatever”. It’s a flashback show where Derik does his thing. And weekly on Pressure Points, and on every Pay-Per-View, if you’re lucky… you may even hear him say his famous catchphrase. “That’s gold…” Long pause, until he comes out screaming… “THAT’S GOLD!”. Oddly enough, even the faces like this quote. Life without Derik Shaw… what would it be? James Attwood, Interviewer Just because his last name has one more “t” in it than Margaret Atwood, oh well. James is very professional, and maybe the “most-normal” (if that’s a word) person that works backstage. He’s good at his job. He asks the hard-hitting questions… and even the heels seem to give him respect. Ten solid years in this job could do it for you. Bobby Blank, Interviewer Blank has been signed on after the big roster firing in December of 2003. Young wanted one more man to go around and get interviews. There’s just one major problem with Bobby Blank. He forgets EVERY QUESTION he’s about to ask. Action Wrestlers, have fun with this guy… you may be asking him more questions (eg. “are you an idiot?”, “you gonna talk?”) than he would be asking you. Tom Clark, Reporter Tom has reported news on the Action Wrestling front page since its beginning with James Attwood. However, if ANYONE has paid close attention… Tom’s posts seem to get a little dumber and dumber with every new day that passes. No one is sure why this is. But rumors from other crew members has it that Tom’s been spending a lot of time with Reed Young and Jeremy Bold. Brainwash, anyone? © Action Wrestling 2002-2004
