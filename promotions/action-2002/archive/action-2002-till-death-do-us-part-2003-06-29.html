Till Death Do Us Part Fleet Center — Boston, Massachusetts ---- TILL DEATH DO US PART. TILL DEATH DO US PART. June 29th, 2003. Things looked grim. Half-way in to the year, it was going good. Was, being the key word. It was obvious on the faces of the talent who happened to be in the green-room of the Fleet Center. Everyone looked nervous, and no-one knew why they were called here. Up front, Coral Avalon sat on a backwards steel-chair with Allison hanging on his arm. Close by were Rook Black, Duel, and Frank Heimdal. The Arcades stood behind, Donell involved in a conversation with Frank Heimdal while Julius waited for their reason for being there. Off to the side, more notably, was Keith Scott Zimmerman, Jeff Garvin, Chris Chambers, with the Flying Frenchie in the back of the group. Max Danger was lost in the crowd. He sat in a chair with his head slunk down, Jessica at one side and Conrad Ramsey on the other. They all looked grim, but Conrad tried to make it less visible. Bradley Duncan cast a shadow over his former partner with his large frame, standing behind the group. By his lonesome, leaned up against the refreshment table, was Ninja K with a towel over his neck. He had some sweat dripping off his body, making the preparations for his match with Garvin notable. And finally, in the back of the room, hidden in the shadows, was Daniel Phillips. The empty look in his eyes was hidden by the thick lenses of his sun-glasses. He was in his wrestling gear, ready to go, but he didn’t look near ready for his match, although he had been prepping all week. No one in this room knew why they were here. They had all been called to this room 20 minutes before the show began, but there hadn’t been a meeting like this since Juggernaut I. Then, the reason for this meeting walked in to the room with an also tired look carried by the bags under his eyes. Joe Schmidt. He stood in front of everyone, calling for silence among the groups of people so he could speak. “Listen, everyone. Here it is. Our First Pay-Per-View since Juggernaut. And here we are, without an Action! Champion, without a Leader, without any direction.” Some seemed to shuffle in their seats, while others turned to see what their friends reactions were to the obvious statement. “Since the beginning, I have noticed the ethic, the work-rate, the determination of those in-front of me. Some of the best talent that I have EVER seen in a ring is right before my eyes, right now. Fans were expecting to see the four-star matches from the LLBs, the Foxs, and the Cylears. They wanted to be thrust in to the middle of a lack-luster program between the those above the barrier. “They wanted to see a big battle from Joey Malone. They wanted to see him overcome the odds placed against him. But sadly, last time it happened, he didn’t. And now, the fans aren’t getting that. “It’s true, ratings are down, and this Buy-Rate may be as well.” Joe took a long pause as he rubbed his face with his hands, giving a deep sigh before continuing. “Ok, I’m not going to bullshit you. Things suck right now. LLB has been fuck knows where, Krow is back, and now Malone is at home.” Most of the crowd, especially Coral Avalon, didn’t like the fact that the father figure of the backstage has since gone home. “It seems, right now, this is as low as we can go. Well, that is exactly right. “Right now, at this show. Til Death Do Us Part. This is the turn around. Tonight, there is now Glass Ceiling. Tonight, we have the chance to turn this around. Tonight is ours, it belongs to us.” Joe let out a slight laugh as he scratched the back of his head. “Well, I guess it would be really corny to say ‘carpe diem,’ but you know what I am getting at. Tonight, forget all those thoughts in your mind. Forget the ratings. Make the fans want to see you. Make the fans NEED to see you. Send those bitches through with-drawls ‘til Monday. “Fuck LLB, fuck Fox, fuck Cylear, and fuck Krow. Tonight is about us.” Everyone’s faces tend to light up, as their postures corrected and they began to group tighter together. “Go shine,” Joe said, and with that, everyone stood up and headed back to their lockers. Once alone, Joe headed to the table where Ninja K once stood, taking a disposable cup and filling it to the brim with cold, black coffee. He didn’t care of the temperature, despite it being opposite of the proposed preparation. He gradually moved him over into an inverted facelock. Ratings Spike. Countered. Reversed. And KSZ, for the third time in his career, procured the Dragon Clutch on a surprised victim. Once, he'd used it to win. Another time, he used in a victorious match. And considering it was a dragon sleeper camel clutch on a man with an injured neck, well, things didn't look too sunny in Kleptoville. Coral desperately thought of ways to counter. He could almost feel strings in his neck coming apart and for a brief horrifying second had a daydream in which Zimmerman literally ripped his head off with this move, leaving a twitching bloody stump on the canvas. Unbeknowst to him, though, Zimmerman was really struggling to keep it on due to his .7 Muta job. His head slumped back a little, and he focused to snap it forward. But a couple seconds later, he fell back and released the hold. Avalon fell limply to the mat. Very reluctantly, Shields began the count. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. Seven. Eight. Niiiiiiiiiiine...and here we went again. Coral's elbow strike was blocked. KSZ grapevined him and tried a short lariat but Coral ducked under him and took off for the ropes. Tired, KSZ telegraphed a back body drop; revitalized, he was able to pivot and backflip over Zimmerman. KSZ turned to see Coral come back at him, and immediately side-stepped him before firing off a leg lariat to the back of the head. Avalon was necked on the middle rope shortly after. Time to dial it up with the MORTAL~! KSZ bounced off of the ropes, looking to come off with a swinging dropkick to the mush. 101. Nah. Avalon de-necked himself and rolled over to the apron, but KSZ had adeptly landed on his feet and decided to get the Copycat's boat by stealing one of his mentor's siggie moves. KSZ bounced off of the perpendicular ropes and as Avalon stood up on the apron the challenger fired off a springboard dropkick to the mush, knocking Coral down to the floor chest-first. KSZ slid under the bottom ropes to the floor, and suddenly realized what was wrong: Shields wasn't saying any numbers. "CO?" Zimmerman asked, wondering if there was going to be a count made. Mark merely shook his head no. And with that, KSZ smiled, and brushed aside the timekeeper. He grabbed the ring bell, and waited for Avalon to get up. DING There wasn't a called disqualification. Discarding the ring bell to a chorus of boos, he climbed up to the apron and then re-entered the ring. Shields almost slipped on someone's blood and had to stay upright via the ropes. Then he began the count, for KSZ was slowly climbing the turnbuckles, the power for the one leap to the top drained from him for a few days. He finally positioned himself up top. Another angle, from the turnbuckle cam: vibrating and settling still as KSZ drove Avalon down to the ground, the crowd immediately coming unglued. Suddenly, a chorus of boos erupted. The EMT's were coming down the aisle. Down the ramp they came, and this burgeoning epic title match was ruined. Would we ever know who won the match? Sure. They stood on the rampway, watching and waiting. They didn't understand. They couldn't possibly. KSZ leaned against the apron, looking at the lights. He stumbled back towards Avalon before tripping and falling down face-first. Fuck it. He'd come too far and given too much to let that hitch stop him. Avalon's breath came out in ragged spurts. He felt like he'd been in a car wreck. Maybe ten pounds of platinum wasn't enough to get anyone's heart in a tizzy anymore, but for KSZ it did the job and it did it just fine. Avalon got up.
