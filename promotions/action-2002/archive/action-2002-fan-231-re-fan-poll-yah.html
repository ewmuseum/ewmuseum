What this is A post on the fed's own Fan Board, 11 August 2002: staff poll answers on the roster, champions and matches. From the Wayback capture of 15 June 2003.
Re: Fan Poll....YAH! Posted by Wayne [detail withheld] on August 11, 2002 at 08:34:48: In Reply to: Fan Poll....YAH! posted by Paul L on August 09, 2002 at 15:03:53: 1) Who are your favorite Action! Superstars? [changed it a bit. ;)] I love LLB, Action! Man, Reed Young and Jeremy Bold. (The Whole Rowe Crew.) Gotta' give props to Ross for making Fox such a hilarious and indelible character. More recently, I've really started to mark out for Jonny Yankee  his segments and role-plays are great. (GO WIL) 2) Who is your least favorite Action! Superstar? Morg.hehehej/k.n/a. 3) Who are your favorite champions of all time in Action!? [changed this one too.] LLB and Joey Malone 4) Who do you feel will become the next A! Champion? KroW. 5) What got you following Action!? I had a conversation with my long time friend Rowe over a character idea, and after a few conversations it was decided that he'd be brought into Action!  And I've been hooked ever since. (I really love this place.) 6) Who do you feel are the next rising stars of Action!? [you guessed it, I changed this as well.] Jonny Yankee, The Joker and Disciple X. 7) Which matches have been your favorites thus far? I am gonna have to plug myself here for a second. I have gotten a lot of rave reviews for the first match in my series, which aired on the Special Play-by-Play version of Pressure Point. It's the first and only piece of my work I've ever marked out for. (Go check it out) As far as other peoples work, I loved the Marry Me Match, the HIAC (Each starring LLB and Fox), and the Ladder from this week's show (Morg vs DX)  all three of which were done by Rowe. Almost forgot; Life, Death, and Endurance III: Syphon Fission vs. Joey Malone. (A Kick ass match done by Aaron.) 8) Which angle do you follow the most right now? I too am currently placing a lot of my attention on KroW/LLB. 9) What match-type would you like to see in Action!? 3 Ring Battle Royal. WWWHOOOOOO!!!!! (Only Rowe Could Make This Match A Good Read.) 10) What is your dream match (Wrestler A v. Wrestler B) for Action!? LLB vs. Conrad Ramsey in an Action! Championship vs. Fixodent Dentures Match!!! -Wayne
