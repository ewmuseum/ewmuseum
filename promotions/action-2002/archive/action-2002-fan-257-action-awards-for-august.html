Action! Awards for August Posted by Alex M [detail withheld] on August 30, 2002 at 03:22:44: OK, I've seen the guys over at EW24-7 do this and I must admit it's pretty cool. Copy and paste the following into and E-MAIL, send it to [detail withheld] by Monday morning, and then I will post the results on the boards. Perhaps there's a section on the website it can g (Roland?). Anyway, here they are, remember E-MAIL them to me, don't reply. Members and fans alike can do this. All awards are for the month of August, so say that Airborn Tyler had a bad month but normally he's one of the best (*snigger*), then you wouldn't put him down as the best wrestler. Base your picks on the past month only. OK, lets go... Wrestler of the Month Which 5 wrestlers have had a particularly good month? 1. 2. 3. 4. 5. Match of the Month Which 3 matches made the hairs on your neck stand up? 1. 2. 3. Angle of the Month Which 3 feuds/storylines captivated your imagination? 1. 2. 3. Swerve of the Month Which 3 unexpected things happen that you never saw coming? 1. 2. 3. OK, that's it. Obviously, in all the sections 1 is your first choice. Remember, e-mail them to [detail withheld] by Monday morning. Peace, Alex [name withheld]
