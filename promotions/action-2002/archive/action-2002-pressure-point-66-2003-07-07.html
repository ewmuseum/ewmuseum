Canadian crowds were the best. Witness this Montreal one. Everyone standing, cheering, showing their support via signs for their favorite wrestlers. The cameras panned over them as Pressure Point had started up. With "Headstrong" by Trapt blarring through the speakers, filling the Molsen Centre, everyone was anxiously awaiting to witness the potential fall-out of 'Til Death Do Us Part, that had happened just one week ago yesterday. After all that nonsense, the focus was on the entrance, because the crowd was about to have a guest speaker. One in the form of Max Danger. The Carnage Champion made his entrance to a chorus of cheers with "Rock Star" by N.E.R.D. playing overhead. He had the silver title belt wrapped around his waist and his lovely wife, Jessica, at his side, holding her hand. The cheers only grew as the Danger Man slowly made his way down the ramp.
