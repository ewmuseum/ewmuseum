What this is The fed’s own Ring of Gold page for Joey Malone, a short list of the shows that made his name, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 20 May 2004. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the page’s text is.
MALONE: GREATEST HITS. Pressure Point 1 In the first Pressure Point, Joey Malone picks up a victory over Shawn Steele. Resentment Joey Malone in the arena that had given him the greatest of pains, Madison Square Garden, manages to defeat Syphon Fission in his Action! PPV Debut. Till Death Do Us Part 2002 Life, Death, and Endurance III, culminating the end of the "Feud of the Year" for 2002. Pressure Point 19 Joey Malone defeats the Joker and Steven Denton in the same night to become the first Action! Bantam Champion, and starting an 81 day reign as champion. Down 4 The Count 2002 Joey Malone defeats Simon Seaman and makes history as the first ever double champion in Action! history. Retaliation 2002 On the same night that he loses the B title to Steven Denton, Joey Malone defeats LLB during his 24 Hour Challenge to not only become the new Action! champion, but also the first and currently only ABC champion in Action! history. Pressure Point 36 In one of the most shocking moments in Action! history, Keri Lindum, Joey Malone's life-long soulmate, best friend, and wife turns her back on him by blasting him with a steel chair, forcing Joey to inevitably tap out to Kent Anthason's Endurodrain. Last Laugh 2002 In the most violent Action! title match in history, Joey Malone and Kent Anthason take each other to a draw, destroying the Last Laugh set, their own bodies, and their own lives all in one stroke. Pressure Point 43 This would be the card where Joey impregnates his wife in a closet. Not sure if it's a "greatest hit" per se, but look who's updating the freaking site. :-P Pressure Point 45 Joey Malone turns his back on his best friend and student, Coral Avalon. Blood, Sweat, and Tears While losing to Coral Avalon in their four star encounter at BST, Joey Malone still shows a sign of respect to his student, until the Winds of Change turn on them both. The 2003 Action! Awards Joey Malone wins Wrestler of the Year. Rejoice. Pressure Point 61 Joey makes his final televised appearance in Action!. Juggernaut II Joey Malone is retired by Daniel Phillips. © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
