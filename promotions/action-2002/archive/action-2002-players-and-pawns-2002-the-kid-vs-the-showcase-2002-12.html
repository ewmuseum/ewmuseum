Players and Pawns: The Kid vs. The Showcase

December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The site's own story page for one of the matches on Players and Pawns (31 December 2002), a build-up piece signed by the staffer James Attwood, from the Wayback capture of 14 January 2003. The show itself is served on this wing.
It will be two exact years to this date when the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling saw ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear defend his newly won World Championship off of the same man he beat it from, LLB. During this time, ‘Showcase’ and Jim Johnson double teamed LLB, as they rushed him into an automobile, and dropped a wrecking ball on top of the car as the countdown to the year 2001, kicked off. However, Reed Young says this is weak, very weak, compared to what his plans will be before 2003. The dropping of the wrecking ball took LLB out of PIW for a month and a half, only to return, costing Sean Cylear the Championship Title against Overdose. Following that, it was soon to be booked. When the PIW began to slow down, Overdose walked out of the company, dropping the PIW Championship along the process. The PIW name slowly began to change to the TWE (which is a story within itself)... but regardless, it was jotted down on paper. LLB vs. ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear for the vacant PIW World Championship at their formerly named Grand Show, ‘Rumble in the Bronx’. This, was not meant to be though as PIW folded almost directly after the two signed. When PIW returned, opening its doors in April of that same year, LLB battled Sean Cylear in a Steel Cage Match, and though it was not booked for the PIW World Heavyweight Title, after LLB’s victory over Cylear, Johnson sided with the law... and handed him the Gold. Cylear then took to drugs, slowly fading out of the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling picture, and wrestling in general. Over his short stay in jOlt, Sean was found "dead" in his apartment in the late 2001 winter. That’s when it happened. Last Laugh, Action Wrestling Pay-Per-View. After numerous appearances backstage, running around in a "golden cape", Sean Cylear returned, costing LLB the Virus Championship and a chance to fight the Action Champion at the February Pay-Per-View. The odd thing was, that Sean cost LLB the Match against his best friend... and ‘Showcase’s’ up and coming opponent, Christopher Fox. Two years after the exact date and time of the "wrecking ball" incident, Sean Cylear will battle ‘The Kid’, attempting to send a message to the lawyer. But Christopher Fox can almost find his way out of anything; for he IS one sly Fox. However, will he be able to escape a madman, whose come back to seek a long time revenge upon ‘The Law’. In Sean Cylear’s return to the ring, December the 31st, the story will unfold... -James Attwood ggg
