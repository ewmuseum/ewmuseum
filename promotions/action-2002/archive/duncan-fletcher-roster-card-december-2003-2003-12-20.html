Duncan Fletcher Birth Name: Ryan Duncan Maxwell Jr. Also Known As: Ian Maxwell(alias) Date of Birth: August 17th, 1968 Age: 34 Marital Status: Unknown Hometown: Vancover, British Columbia, Canada Height: 6'1' Weight: 219 lbs. Hair Color: Brown Eye Color: Gray Blood Type: AB- Status: Heel Personality: He is a cold, calculating individual. Works well either by himself or as someone's lackey. Rarely shows emotion. Enjoys participating in violent acts. Theme Music: 'Stray Bullet' by KMFDM Wrestling Style Trained By: Loosely trained by Horito Yakama Detailed Style: Fletcher's style is mainly him just beating the shit out of his opponent with kicks. He has few actual wrestling maneuvers, aside from those he stole from his trainer. His style is stiff, brutal, methodical, and somewhat predictable. Strengths: Has the strongest kicks ever seen in Action! Wrestling. Former shoot fighter. Weaknesses: No wrestling ability, he just kicks people. Easilly manipulated. Signature Moves: Vulcan Snap Springboard hurricane kick. He either executes it inside-to-inside, or uses a more dangerous inside-to-outside version, which had actually injured him and put him out of wrestling for good.
