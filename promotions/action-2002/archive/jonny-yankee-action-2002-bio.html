Jonny Yankee Biography Nicknames Jonny Yankee Hometown Gettysburg, PA Birthday sometime in July Height 6'2' Weight 265 Biography He doesn’t know much about himself. Why should he? The most he knows is that he woke up in a hospital to see a man watching wrestling. He was a grown man, not an infant in a hospital...a grown man with no memory of anything. Seeing his uniform on the chair next to him though, that worked the General’s memory, and he recalled all the battles he had fought under the 28th Regiment of Pennsylvania. He was a soldier, a Union General. He was lost in a world that wasn’t familiar to him, a world he wasn’t familiar to. This, fans, is the true biography of the man you know as Jonny Yankee. How did we acquire this information? Sources, acquiring things in life is all about having sources. Here we go. _________________________ While we still haven’t been able to dig up the exact date of his birth, we know that it is sometime in July and that the man we know as Jonny Yankee is approximately 30 years old. Most people are surprised that he isn’t older. Our General was born an army brat, living on the Fort Knox base in Louisville, Kentucky until he was about 16. Around then, his parents went through a nasty divorce and Jonny chose to live with his mother in Gettysburg, PA. He was considered a momma’s boy when he was young, if his mother was still alive today and he knew her...he would still be a momma’s boy. He was living in the Civil War capital of the country, so it was hard for him not to become infatuated with it. Jonny began collecting figurines and making models celebrating the past battles. Gorgeous models that he often entered in art fairs, models which usually won those very same art fairs. He started reenacting them with his mother’s position once he turned 18. He worked his way up from the rank of a Private just like everyone else. His schoolwork showed the effects of the time it had lost to the reenactments. He excelled in US History, but he was failing everything else. He managed to pull his grades up and graduate from Conestoga High with a 2.0 GPA. Nothing to brag about, but it was good enough for Jonny, now he could spend more time doing what he loved. He couldn’t really explain why he had such strong feelings for the battles fought....Battle Of Gettysburg....Picket’s Charge...Devil’s Peak...Little Big Hill. He respected both sides in the war for the simple fact that they were each fighting for something they believed in, but he was a Union soldier without a doubt....he was fully against slavery. Jonny never had much of a love life, he dated for awhile, but he was just spending too much time entertaining the Civil War fans and that intervened in every relationship he ever had. He soon gave up, there would be a time for women. The fans. That was another big reason Jonny participated in the fake battles....people loved it. The blank rounds being fired, the fake blood being spilled, the cannons shouting their loud booms and giving off rings of smoke. They loved it. Jonny loved being loved. His mother died when he was 27, and the majority of the people at the funeral were people Jonny had met through his reenactments. He found a family in this hobby of his, and that was something he would hold onto forever...until they forgot about him and he forgot about them. He forgot about them when he took a stiff butt of a rifle to the temple. It was pure accident, but it knocked him into a coma nonetheless. An ambulance took him to the hospital...his friends would have checked up on him, but they didn’t know what hospital he was taken to. They forgot about him, he woke up without any memory of them. He woke up knowing only two things... he was a solider and Action could use a soldier in its ranks.
