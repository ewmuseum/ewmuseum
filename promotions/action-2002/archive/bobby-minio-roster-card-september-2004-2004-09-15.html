Bobby Minio Birth Name: Bobby Minio Also Known As: Formerly: the Martyr, Bio, One Man Revolution Date of Birth: June 4th, 1978 Age: 25 Marital Status: Single Hometown: Las Vegas, NV Height: 5'11 Weight: 218lbs Hair Color: Brown Eye Color: Brown Blood Type: B+ Status: Neutral Personality: Socially detached young wrestling Veteran Theme Music: Untitled by Finch Wrestling Style Trained By: 'the Mile High Madman' Mill Collins Detailed Style: Innovative Highflying, Technical Strengths: Powerful launcher for arial, strong endurence, relentless Weaknesses: Overzealous, pushes himself to far, dangerous arial moves leave room for disaster, known to be easy to knock out Signature Moves: One Armed Scissor; An upside down reverse DDT An Old Religion; Hangover Leg Drop No Room in Hell; Minio baseball slides through the opponent's legs, his arms dragging the opponents feet out from below them Dear John Letter; Opponent is slammed to the mat from the top rope into an elevated armbar Shotgun DDT Trademark Name: A New Religion Trademark Move: a springboard backflip to a dropkick Injuries Current Injuries: Nagging Injuries: Bobby was very Prone to concussions during his first months in Action!, that seems to have settled down lately. History Years Pro: 7 Manager: Allies: Nemesis: Current Team: Past Teams: M&M w/ Max Danger, Minio & Lowell Dot Com Action Wrestling Title History: Action! Dyad Title (M&M w/ Max Danger) Action Wrestling Award History: Other Federations: PIW Other Federation Awards: PIW Television Title, PIW Hardcore Title, PIW Tag Titles (w/ Lowell Dot Com), The News Match of the Week for the Clit 'N Candy Express vs. Max Danger & Bobby Minio Dyad Title Match at Pressure Point 32 Attributes Intelligence: 7 Strength: 5 Toughness: 8 Speed: 7 Endurance: 8 Courage: 9 Charisma: 6 Skill: 7 Biography Bobby Minio, the troubled High flyer, driven steadily towards his own destruction by the female species. ------------- Daddy was a rolling stone and Momma was a space cadet. With that being said you know enough about my family life for me not to bring it up. Lets go to where it really starts, you know... My downfall. Julie. Simple. I don't need to tell you a story, no description, anyone who knows me, knows about Julie, and already knows more then I would like anyone else to. I've had better days. So I was a broken shell of a man, but no matter, I liked that feeling, it made me forget the pain, forget the hurt, forget the concussions. She attempted scamming my hard earned pay checks. She got shutdown. I upgraded. Between a love triangle with Sweet Aroma and Angel Dawson, PIW Superstar Johnathon Conspiracy eagerly waiting to crush my skull for advances to his girlfriend, and Max Danger blaming me for the loss of the Dyad Titles... Life was pretty exciting. But with progress, comes failure. I have always been emotionally charged. Certain things move me. At the time a backstage leader for us 'good guys' was none other then Joey Malone. One of my last remaining idols. I had been 'seeing' Angel Dawson for a few months, things were alright, the laws of wrestling hadn't interceded to destroy us as lovers. One night as Angel and I laid in bed after a hard night for Action! Wrestling, we were watching the television broadcast, and I saw as Keri Malone turned on her husband for Kent Anthason. I never looked at Angel in the same light. I have never trusted woman, my Mother left that gap in my life, and from there on, I learned they were decietful monsters, driven by vanity. By greed. By Jealously. Sure, that is human. But woman are cruel and relentless. Watching the last relationship I ever trusted crumble in the ring, destroyed my last ounce of trust. And it hurt me. And it hurt Angel. Atleast I thought so, she was out hunting for a man again quickly. I don't blame her. Life slowed down significantly. Until, my father, the late Robert Hicks... Sought me out. I met him, things didn't go well. Weeks later a phone call made me aware he was dead. He had overdosed on prescription drugs, he was dying of cancer, it was deemed suicide. A figure from my past, KroW, took credit. And attempted to bring Father and Son victim to the same fate. I however am not weak like my Father. I'm weak emotionally, but strong willed. I'm broken. I'm beaten. But I'm a survivor. I proved it after a hellacious Buried Alive match, where KroW rigged the grave to really kill me. Kayfabe anyone? But I escaped. And Sylo mixed into the fray. My life was kicking, my blood boiled, my career was gaining new heights of fan awareness. When I got out of the grave I was a new man. But being vocal about the Action! backstage roster got all of us introuble. While we all honestly hated each other, Sylo, KroW, and I, we were banded together in a socalled revolution. Sylo has since quit Action Wrestling. KroW is on his way out too. Our war? It didn't end in heroics. It didn't end in a big bang. It ended in a muffled opinion, and a strangled gasp for air. Quietly. Dead. I am now struggling, to prove myself as a Wrestler, the Wrestler I was trained to be. Good? Bad? Its your opinion. I'm afraid to voice my own until I show these bastards what I'm made of. ------------- Forget me not... I've got more mud to sling.... Shot, through the heart, and you're to blame. You give love, a bad name.
