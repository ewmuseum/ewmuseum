He was splitting the red sea once more, leaving his opponent at Juggernaut to tremble in a mixture of fear, shock, and anger. It was all too much for Cylear, but as KroW walked behind those curtains to sign the deal, Cylear muttered one thing to himself as he slowly gained composure. “This isn’t over.” © Action Wrestling 2002-2004
