Tanya Isama Birth Name: Tanaya Loohknanhan Also Known As: n/a Date of Birth: May 4th, 1985 Age: 18 Marital Status: Single Hometown: Bear Forest, Wyoming Height: 6' Weight: 203 lb. Hair Color: Black Eye Color: Brown Blood Type: O+ Status: Neutral Personality: Shy, quiet Theme Music: 'Severed' by Kittie Wrestling Style Trained By: Armin Krieg Detailed Style: Technical wrestling focusing on suplexes/brainbusters, DDTs, and submissions focusing on the head, neck, and back. Her wrestling style is more ground-based as a compliment to her cousin Xiao's high-flying and martial arts offense. She employs many lifting suplexes; her training enables her to lift a moderate amount of weight for the moves that she employs, but she maxes out at 260 pounds. After that, she relies on non-lifting offense, and her above-average speed. He was the best wrestler. The Gorgeous George's and Freddie Blassie's, Dorn Funk Jr.'s ... they couldn't hold a cande to him. Of course, they got the fame. They were the All-americans. 'Armin grew up in Wisconsin, growing to a height of 6'9' by the time he was eleven. He was regarded as a freak; after all, how many giants were walking around in the 1960s? The other kids were always picking on him, wanting to fight him to make themselves feel big about themselves for beating him up. They never won, though. Five at a time, it didn't matter. He dispatched them with ease. There was something that felt very natural, very right, about fighting. With that in mind, he triained to be a boxer. That was all that heknew about, since that was the only form of combat competition that was aired in his hometown of Madison. 'He got his start in the wrestling business at a passing carnival. There was an event: 'Beat Garganto and win $100.' Garganto was 6'4' and 260 pounds. To th e average person, he was massive. But Armin... all 7'6' and 512 pounds of him; he was simply too much for Garganto. He was easily defeated. The promoter who ran the event, was impressed not only with Armin's size, but with his technique and ferocity. Armin was quickly offered a job at the age of 16. 'He toured the regional scene, defeating and actually retiring many wrestlers. In fact, he became a liability to promoters because he was too violent. They were losing wrestlers too fast, and even though he would win the regional championship, no one would want to face him. Things were different back then; there was dedication to the spor, of course, but people weren't willing to give their lives or livelihood for it. 'Armin became a myth. No one was willing to face him, everyone stayed to the undercard... he couldn't get a match signed, and the bookers wouldn't put their other workers in danger. It was the wrong time for the kind of match, the kind of hardcore style that he would wrestle. It wasn't about beating the opponent to Armin, it was about at least crippling them. 'He was cut off in his prime. Without being able to wrestle, he couldn't perform and impress scouts. He couldn't find new work, and the federations he'd worked for were unwilling to take him back, knowing that he would not tone down his in-ring style. Defeated, he purchased a few acres of land in Wyoming, naming it Bear Forst, and retired there. At the age of 23, Armin Krieg had already beem pushed out of the wrestling and had become a recluse. 'Wrestling, boxing, fighting... he couldn't get work anywhere. He took to alcohol to drown his unfulfilled yearnings to pummel his fellow humans; to make him forget about his unachieved success. 'While making the rounds in the touring, Krieg slept with a 16-year-old girl named Miranda Mills from Detroit, Michigan. She bore him two identical twin sons, but he would not take them, or her. She could not return home with the children, so she registered them for adoption. 'At this point, we're still not sure about the circumstances of how Krieg came into custody of us, but apparently his sons were quite lecherous. They had children out of wedlock. Though they never knew each other or us, both were ironically incarcerated on very similar drug-charges. Apparently, my cousin and I were without mothers or near family. someone found that Krieg was a blood relative, and had him take us. We're his granddaughters. 'Tanya Isama; my mother was of black and ethnic Indian descent, a citizen of Trinidad. She was apparently caught up in some voodoo affairs. She didn't want children. 'Xiao Suarez, she's my cousin. She can tell you about herself. 'We're Armin's last chance at having a legacy in the wrestling industry. We've been living with him for the last 14 years. Xiao's 19, I'm 18. All this time, he's been preparing us for the wrestling industry. He's disappointed in us that we can't match his strength, but neither of us is much of a powerhouse. 'I manage well enough on my own, but Xiao's under 160 pounds. She can't do too much. She uses a high-flying base, but Krieg hates that. Doesn't think that it's the way wrestling was originally intended to be. 'If you're gonna flip around like a monkey, go join the circus! He says. 'We're all he's got, and he's determined to make us succeed.' Off Stage Information Name: Skylar G.
