Payne Fosters Biography Hometown Arkansas Oklahoma Birthday 1/26/71 Height 6'11 Weight 345 Biography After Payne fell from the ActionTron during a dreadful match between himself and Russa everyone thought his career was over. With nothing left after his recovery he went into street fighting, earning little and giving alot. A big fight was promoted on the streets and high baller mobsters placed their bets, but it was fixed, Payne was set to fall. Payne didn't fall though and won his match he let pride fuck with his head. Later that night he was assaulted by a group of thugs hired by the mob that fixed the fight. Payne's eyes were scarred where his paint marks had been so bad infact that one side couldnt be washed off and was melted to his skin. Now Payne wants back in the game and he want's his revenge on all who have done him wrong.
