Atty's Survey #1 Posted by James Attwood [detail withheld] on October 25, 2002 at 09:19:28: Today I was bored, and Reed doesn't pay me enough to do something creative, so I thought I'd do this, and have a survey on two of Action!'s best feuds. For each feud I would like you to: -a) List your favourite moment in the feud to date. -b) Say what is/has been good/bad about the feud. -c) Detail how YOU think the feud will end. The two feuds I have decided on are: The A-Star Stable (Airborn Tyler, Lee Lightning and David Dew) vs The Army (Jonny Yankee, Neko Case and Kayden Paulton) This one started way back in June when Airborn Tyler and Jonny Yankee first entered the A!W leagues. First off it was about Airborn being fired, then Jonny's horse being shot, until it got to a point where they fought merely because they detested the other's guts! When Tyler's cousins The Elements helped him win the TLC match, this became a six man affair. LLB vs The Risk Ever since being dumped at Retaliation by The Risk, LLB has been a massive fan favourite, but now The Risk are trying to replace him. LLB might have a few choice words when he re-emerges after his injury, but what do you, the fans think?
