Tony Davis Biography Hometown Philadelphia, PA Birthday January 14th, 1977 Height 6'2' Weight 254 Biography The whole story began one cold January morning in the dangerous New jersey city of Camden. Dr. Thomas Matvichuk handed a crying baby boy to Estelle Davis while he cut the umbilical cord, and Estelle, alongside with her husband Salvatore, smiled together for a few minutes before their newborn son was taken for normal routine tests. This would be the best moment of Tony's childhood. Over the next 15 years, Tony lived with the hell that was his family. Salvatore was a hitman for the Italian mafia, long before the Sopranos ever beame 'reality'. He never had time for his son aside from trying to get him involved in the family 'business', which Tony never wanted. This lack of interest in the mafia angered Salvatore, and he took it out on his family through many painful nights of physical abuse, even giving Tony his noticable scar above his right pectoral. Tony had always loved his mother when he was growing up, until he was about 14. That's when he realized how weak she was, how she let all of this happen to her and how she just accepted it all as a part of her life. Tony, being so immensly depressed and suicidal while living at home, finally too all that he could take, and ran away from home and school five months after his 15th birthday. Hitching a ride, he made his way to Philadelphia, a 15 minute ride across the Delaware River. He worked odd jobs, whatever he could find, so that he could afford food and clothing. He, however, had no place to sleep, and took shelter at night in a bus stop or at a subway station, wherever he could find a little shelter against the elements. Cold was always a problem though, and many nights he woke up, feeling like he had hypothermia. All of this changed on November 19th, 1995, when a man by the name of Dino Yagich took pity on him. He was a boxing trainer, and owned a small gym on the corner of 7th and Passayunk. He offered Tony a place to sleep as well as food to eat if he would help around the gym, doing jobs that needed to be done. Tony jumped at the oppurtunity, hoping this would be the start to getting him out of such extreme poverty. Over the next few months, Tony spent his days cleaning bathroom stalls, wiping down the mats, and tending to the boxers. Every once in a while he would get a few spare minutes, which he would spend taking advantage of the equipment and building on his tone a bit, or watching the boxers. He soon fell in love with fighting, though he never could see himself as a boxer. Then, one day in the spring of 1997, he was watching television on the cheap black and white TV that Dino owned, and he flipped to a WWF program. Immediately, he knew that was what he wanted to do, to be a professional wrestler. He started searching for someone that he could talk to about performing in a wrestling company, and after weeks of searching, he was referred by one of the boxers to a man named [name withheld]. He was the Creative Consultant for an up-and-coming federation based in South Jersey called the FWL. He met [name withheld] for an interview, and a small tryout, and was soon wrestling in his first wrestling promotion. At first, Tony had a hard time devloping his character. He began somewhat cocky from his first few wins, but then he started to pick fights with guys that he just couldn't handle. However, some good did come out of his cockyness. He was approached, just a few months after joining the federation, to join the most powerful stable in the FWL, the Ultimate Horsemen. Tony, without a doubt in his mind, joined the group, and soon, he would be holding up the FWL United States title belt high above his head. His first title reign. His time in the FWL, however, was short-lived. He began a fued with Pierre Le'Mort, which eventually involved losing his US title to him in a Bastille Match. Tony challenged for a rematch, which was accepted. However, while he was looking toward a victory in the match, his back was badly injured, and his time in the FWL was concluded. After a few months of rehabilitation, things were a little different. The FWL was no more, and [name withheld] was now owner of a new federation, the IWO. This is where Tony would eventually make his biggest claim to fame. He started off slowly, afraid of reinjuring his back, and took a couple of losses. Soon though, he started a small stable with Joey Rappaport, Jack In The Box, and Chris Anthony called Four Badass White Guys. He would capture his first title as a member of this stable, the IWO Intercontinental Tag Team title, with Chris Anthony in a rigged match against the other two members of the group. This didn't last forever, the titles were lost, the tandem broke up, and Tony was once again left without a gimmick. Things wouldn't be bleak forever. It was August of 1999 when he would capture his next title. The scene: Heatstroke. The opponent: Capital Punishment. A heated rivalry had developed between these two, and the love-hate relationship culminated with this IWO North American Title match. After an amazing showing by both competetors, Tony eventually came out the victor (with a little brass help) and now help the North American title, signifying him as the #1 contender to the World title. Which his reign as champion didn't last to long, he would eventually get that World title that he longed forever to get, albeit not in the fashion he would have liked. After a long meeting with current champion Dane Wilt and the IWO board, it was decided that Wilt would job the title to Tony, who would then job the title back to Wilt a few weeks later. While Tony, at first, loved the idea, he eventually grew to despise it. In an aftershow interview two months after these events, he was quoted as saying: 'No, I really did love the idea at the time. I respected Dane Wilt very much, and I still do, even to this day, and I am so grateful for his support of my career. However, looking back on it, it seems to be that the administration of this company don't like seeing me with the belt, sill view me as a mid-carder. Well, let them think what they want, eventually, I'll show them how much of a mid-carder I'm really not.' And Tony was true to his word. It was early 2000, and Tony began to form a friendship with fellow wrestler High Flyer. Both of them, while enjoying the singles division, decided they wanted to dip their hands into the tag division. And this is where the legendary tag team was formed. Team V.I.A.G.R.A. They immediately dominated the division. The won their first titles a short time after forming, and held on to them for 6 months, equalling the longest title reigns in the history of the fed. The eventually gave up their titles, but not after making their marks as one of the best tag teams in the history, and securing both of them a spot in the IWO Hall of Fame. The New Year brought about a horrible tragedy however. Flyer needed a few months off for personal reasons, and the IWO decided to use a car crash with Tony for an injury. However, things became way too real, and Flyer had reinjured his knee from the crash, bring Tony back into his depressed state. The next few months really ate away at him, and he didn't deal with it all too well. When Flyer eventually made his way back from injury, Tony had created a story in his head that the injury was all Flyer's fault, and that he couldn't forgive him. His dilusions too the two all the way to Beach Party, where they fought one-on-one in one of the most violent matches either man has ever, or will ever, compete in. But, in the end, Tony snapped out of it, what doctors diagnosed as post-psychotic amnesia, and the two embraced as the best friends that they are. However, out of all this tragedy, a bright spot did come out of this situation: Tammy Cradle, Flyer's sister and now long-term girlfriend of Tony's. The two have been practically inseperable since then, and wouldn't want it any other way. Together they tried to become a force in the tag division again. Aside from the IWO, they both joined jOlt, where they quickly became tag champs for most of the federation's duration, before it closed. They then won the IWO tag titles again, only to get them vacated and having the current distinction of being the IWOs last tag team champions. But with the attack on The Elements and S2, it seems the Team V.I.A.G.R.A. has returned, this time to Action! Wrestling. Who knows what's in store? But one thing can be assured. Things will never be the same.
