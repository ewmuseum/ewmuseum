What this is Anaconda’s card from the fed’s own roster page, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 11 May 2004. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Anaconda Birth Name: Private Also Known As: Date of Birth: April 17th, 1957 Age: 46 Marital Status: Married Hometown: Tucson, Arizona Height: 5'9' Weight: 200 Pounds Hair Color: Black and Grey. Eye Color: Light Brown. Blood Type: O Positive Status: Face Personality: Where Anaconda may lack the size of being such the dominant power that his move arsenal, name, and intelligence do mean him to be, Anaconda coves these “size” flaws by having such a methodical approach to the wrestling ring. He has put forth countless hours into conditioning his body and his mind to overcome many different obstacles. His personality, is, what some would say to be very self-motivated and strong. Theme Music: ''Suffocate'' Cold Wrestling Style Trained By: The Clutch Kid. Detailed Style: Submission. Strengths: Determination and will-power. Weaknesses: Size. Signature Moves: 'The Inhaltz' (Anaconda stands behind the dazed opponent, and by grabbing both of the opponent's arms, he crosses them from one another [as if the opponent was in a straightjacket]. By arching Anaconda's back, he begins to pull the opponent backwards [and, if possible, to lift him off the ground]). Half-Nelson Submission (With the opponent on the mat, Anaconda will apply a Half-Nelson Submission as he screams out 'Half-Nelson! Half-Nelson!' as if he was a commentator). 'Bow and Arrow' (By placing one knee on the canvas, Anaconda takes hold of the opponent's neck/ head with one arm, and lower thighs/ upper legs with the other. By putting the middle of the opponent's back towards his knee, he then attempts to break the opponent in half as he forces both of his hands backwards while keeping his knee stuck in the back of the wrestler). Trademark Name: 'The Coil'. Trademark Move: Scissor Hold. Injuries Current Injuries: Sore back. Nagging Injuries: None. History Years Pro: 18 (retired; came back). Manager: N/ A Allies: None. Nemesis: None. Current Team: None. Past Teams: None. Action Wrestling Title History: None. Action Wrestling Award History: None. Other Federations: The Wrestling Federation (TWF). Other Federation Awards: TWF's 3rd Title (x3); TWF's 1st Tag Team Champion (x2 - w/ Seeker - w/ Mark Paulton); TWF's 2nd Tag Team Champion (x1 - w/ Cowboy Carson). Attributes Intelligence: 8 Strength: 10 Toughness: 10 Speed: 6 Endurance: 10 Courage: 10 Charisma: 7 Skill: 6 Biography Where some may wonder how such a man of this stature is able to provide a sense of fear and doubt into the minds of opponents when facing this submission specialist, all doubts are put to an end when meeting Anaconda in person. Once a former TWF wrestler for Forest Young’s federation, Anaconda has decided to make a return to the wrestling ring. At the age of 46, he does not look like, or feel like a man of extreme age... but rather a man to accomplish some of the things he had not done in the past. Bounded by no limits, the figure that is Anaconda will make as much impact in Action as he once did in The Wrestling Federation. He will be strong, tough, and mentally capable of going up against a seven foot giant... as if he was only a mere three feet. His task: to gain a higher rank than when he wrestled 10 years ago. His determination: already has him right there. Beware of the one they call Anaconda. Beware. For if he lurks near you, stay still... or suffer in the grip of his talent. Off Stage Information Name: Rpac AOL Instant Messenger Screen Name: [detail withheld] Interests: Triple A Select Football (QB), Sports (Football [Go 49ers!], Hockey [Go Devils!], Baseball [Go Jays!], Basketball [Go Raptors!], Swimming, etc.), Girls, Hanging Out, Listening to All Kinds of Music, Working Out, Video Games (Metal Gear Solid Series [Even the old Nintendo ones], Mega Man Series), eWrestling (No Kidding), Wrestling (What!? I said Wrestling...) School (Gotta Keep My Marks Up!), I could go on and on... Favorite Pro Wrestler: Real wrestling? Whatever! Get with the times, Vince. Put on a good show for a change.
