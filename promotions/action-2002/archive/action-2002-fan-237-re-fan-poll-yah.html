Re: Fan Poll....YAH! Posted by Roland [detail withheld] on August 11, 2002 at 16:51:35: In Reply to: Fan Poll....YAH! posted by Paul L on August 09, 2002 at 15:03:53: : Time for one of Paul's Polls, those polls I'm so famous for in PIW, XWF, OSW, Asylum and Action!....here we go..... : 1) Who is your favorite Action! Superstar? Everyone... really. Or else they'd be fired. =:) : 2) Who is your least favorite Action! Superstar? Not a soul. Jeremy Bold's an IDIOT though. =:) : 3) Who is your favorite champion of all time in Action!? Thus far I'd say Simon Seaman... Malone and LLB just aren't as kool as him right now. : 4) Who do you feel will become the next A! Champion? Reed Young or Action! Man... or Lassie. =:) : 5) What got you following Action!? The IC Owner rules... the OOC one's an asshole! Muhahaha. : 6) Who do you feel is the next rising star of Action!? Honestly, everyone. Max Danger continues to make me mark out... same with Conrad, though I must say, Danger is now better in my eyes... and I never thought that'd happen. Airborn Tyler can work the mic like a bitch! I mark out to everything he says. Just goto his Quote section in the Bio... MY GOD I mark out. Yankee is brilliant. Great gimmick. Sweet Aroma is starting to get into it. Morg vs. D-X is picking up big time... I know I'm forgetting others too. It's all getting strong. : 7) Which match has been your favorite thus far? I'd have to say, tossing out the Main Event at Juggernaut, based on ONLY the history of Fox/ Fear and LLB... Steven Denton vs. Joey Malone for the B Title... and Malone vs. Fission at Juggernaut. Sure, the other Malone vs. Fission shit was great too... I guess, I just marked out A LOT for Juggernaut. : 8) Which angle do you follow the most right now? All of them. : 9) What match-type would you like to see in Action!? Something I don't know, but it really good. =:) : 10) What is your dream match (Wrestler A v. Wrestler B) for Action!? Airborn Tyler vs. Christopher Fox Kent Anthason vs. Reed Young : , Paul L Cheers, - Roland
