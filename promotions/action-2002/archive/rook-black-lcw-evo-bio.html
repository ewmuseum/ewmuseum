Rook Black Biography Nicknames Rana Venenosa, The Great White Dope Hometown St. Joe, Texas Birthday 12/02/75 Height 5'11' Weight 233 Fed Titles None! Fed Awards None! Biography Rook's dad, Joe Black, runs more or less a wrestling school and a limited territory out of St. Joe, Texas. Rook was exposed to wrestling when he was little as he'd follow his dad and uncle around, help put the ring together, carry towels around, and in general try to be useful and unabtrusive. Rook's mom, an intellectual, wanted a different life for Rook, and pushed the scrawny little kid towards academics. Rook at 13 was a chess master. It was the last time he'd compete professionally, and he'd never become a grandmaster. After divorce, and Rook's growth spurt, he began to appreciate the finer aspects of athletics and his dad's passion. He went to college at Texas A&M, where he was an up and coming amature wrestler, chess team captain, fraternity brother, and 4.2 gpa ... and a very mean drunk. And eventually, he was only a very mean drunk as all social interaction became strained, he was put on academic probation, and kicked out of his fraternity. Rook had to attend AA meetings, his continued enrollment at Texas A&M was contingent on it. At AA, Rook discovered that the higher power was a load of shit and became a devout atheist. Because of atheism being incompatable with the structure of AA, he was no longer forced to attend, but any drinking incidents would mean immediate expulsion. So Rook continued to go to school, work out, and train with his dad's school every weekend. Rook graduated with a degree in mathematics. Upon graduation, Joe Black had planned to push his talented son in his own little territory, and then move him into the indy circuit where he would build him up to join a prominent national promotion. Instead, Rook joined the MMWF against his father's wishes, before he thought Rook was ready, and Rook's ride in the federation that would eventually become LCW began. Rook wrestled well, and his rise to fame drew his father's ire, who returned to fued with Rook, and eventually relaunch his own career first as a wrestler then as an announcer. Rook career was successful, and his prowess as a technical wrestler was only getting better. Then, Duel, who had been with LCW's original incarnation as well (longer than Rook actually) crushed Rook's right hand under a full keg of beer at Club Evo, LCW evo's mix of night club/wrestling show. Rook was not seen again on television in the states until he was unmasked as Rana Venenosa in Action! Now that's a lot of chat.
