Thank You's

15 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A post on the fed's own Fan Board, 15 July 2002: writer's thanks after the Fission/Malone angle and the IWO crossover. From the Wayback capture of 28 February 2003.
Thank You's Posted by Aaron [detail withheld] on July 15, 2002 at 00:33:43: Hey, this angle is finally over. Fission/Malone is finally come and passed. Hoped it lived up to expectations. I have some people to thank for making this possible. Roland, thanks for letting me into Action. This place is just great to be a part of. It wouldn't have felt right if I had done this somewhere else, even the IWO. You were patient and put our matches over the World Title ones. I appreciate it. Good luck in future endeavors. I am still here. To Tom [name withheld], for allowing this to cross promotional waters. Without you, LDE III wouldn't have had the same effect. You let me come in and become World Champion, like I wished. You are the man Tom. Thanks for being the board member from day one in the IWO that cared. Thanks for being a friend man. To John [name withheld], for being cool with the idea of Fission being champion. I always liked Tod, and the fight Fission/Schitzo Tod will be one of my fave matches I have ever written. Your a cool guy. Don't stop. To Val, for being one of the first fans that I knew of for this angle. I was sketchy on you at first, but now you're in Action and are getting better. Thanks for all the kind words over the months, even as life has grinded me down. You fucking rule all. To all the fans of the angle. I like doing stuff for an audience. Thanks for reading. To EW-News, for making this feud of the week. With Ender/Caprino going on, I did not know how much press we would get. To the rosters of both Action and IWO for accepting this. Thanks Jermz for all the Kenny Rock usage. Thanks to Steved and you for making Rock/Douglas, which I kind of based around this. And most of all... Thanks Mike [name withheld], for giving me a character I have followed since you came from the IML. I always knew you would be the man, always doing it for the people instead of yourself. Your characters are awesome, and have given me so much inspiration on days where I feel like I can write nothing. Thanks for letting me run wild with this. Thanks for being there these last couple years as a handler...and most importantly...as a friend. You do not know how much just your friendship means to me. Well...I won't get sappy. To those I haven't thanked, thanks. And you guys who said I would amount to nothing in my first fed all those years ago, in ICW, yeah, fuck you. Looks like I was good after all. Thanks. Aaron W. [name withheld] And most of all...thanks Mike. This
