Wrestling Style: Violent Favorite Moves: Whatever hurts, is what Invalid is most likely to pull on his opponent. Invalid has intensive instruction in various styles of high flying, technical, and powerhouse wrestling. But mainly he just sticks to roughneck and powerhouse wrestling spots, along with a large variety of jujitsu grapples and holds, and striking moves. Invalid has received rigorous training in kickboxing, and began to learn jujitsu, in an attempt prepare himself for the “fighting” craze he intends to pimp for all its worthwhile the fad’s still popular. Needless to say, this added training, along with is natural sadistic nature has made him a dangerous presence in the ring. One arrant punch or kick, and Invalid can quickly capitalize playing an orchestra of agony on his victim’s joints and limbs. A momentary lapse in concentration, and Invalid will send a lighting fast barrage of kicks to the face, that’ll leave you wondering if your head can still call your shoulders home. Invalid fancy’s high-impact moves. Which include a great variety of drivers from tiger drivers to drivers of the Death Valley persuasion. He will occasionally pull a belly-to-belly as a counter or a high-impact suplex to soften up his opponent, though he will not resort to that often in a match. Other wrestling spots include: a wide amount of DDT’s, brain buster variations, trapping head butts, drop kicks to the knee, and superkicks. Invalid generally stays off the top rope, unless he’s looking to seriously injure someone, and with that he’s usually looking to pull a flying knee drop to the throat, or a splash or another top rope move looking to smash a steel chair into his victim. Invalid is a violent person. Anything he’s capable of doing and using to inflict pain, he will happily employ. Choking a fucker with a cable wire, tying up some fool in the ropes and opening up on him, wrenching a victims back and neck using the ring post, whatever. Biting, scratching, spitting, and a relentless amount of stomping and punting to ribs or face, are not an uncommon occurrence when Invalid is involved. Show any hint of an injury, and Invalid will pounce like a pit bull working over that vulnerable spot to his hearts content. He’s also been known to tap dance on his opponents while they are down. There are turning points in all our lives. Tunnels, if you will. You live your life as you have since birth, as you were taught. Then you find yourself in a tunnel. Enveloped by darkness. Isolated. Frantically scrambling to freedom. Then you emerge on the other side. Pre-Tunnel you and Post-Tunnel you can no longer co-exist. You must re-create yourself. No. I haven’t gone through this transformation. I am still confined to my tunnel. Though I’m not scrambling. I fear what awaits me on the other side. I’ve dug myself a nice niche. I will interact with the world from within the precincts of my nothingness. I am forever fixed in transition. I am neither the old me, nor a new me. I am Invalid. My past before assuming the moniker of ‘Invalid’ should be of no concern to you. Take me for what I am now. Not for what I was. Or what I will be. Don’t let my lack of substance deter you. Don’t allow my apathy to disgust you. Don’t dismiss me because of my embracement of nothingness. There is no value in my current state. From nothingness, the universe was created. From nothing, Hitler assumed control of a good portion of Europe, and snuffed the flames of 12 million lives. From nothing, Albert Fish brutally tortured, murdered, and consumed. From nothing, I will eventually surface. I’m just in no particular hurry.
