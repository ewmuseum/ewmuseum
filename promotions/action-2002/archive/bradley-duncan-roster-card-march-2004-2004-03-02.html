The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Bradley Duncan Birth Name: Bradley Allen Duncan. Also Known As: The Silent One. Date of Birth: Second Day of the Third Month of the Eighty-First Year in the Twentieth Century. Age: Twenty-two. Marital Status: Single. Hometown: He was born in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Now resides in Leitchfield, Kentucky. Height: Six feet, Seven Inches. Weight: Two-Hundred Seventy-Six Pounds. Hair Color: Brown. Eye Color: Green. Blood Type: O Positive. Status: Neutral Personality: Quiet. Keeps mostly to himself. Is a reader, studier. Has very few friends, and doesn't have big problem with that. But is nice, and will 'talk' to anyone if they want. Theme Music: When alone, Bradley uses 'Nobody's Listening' by Linkin Park. When he's teaming with Conrad he utilizes Agent Provocateur's 'Red Tape'. Wrestling Style Trained By: Max Danger. Detailed Style: Bradley Duncan has the rare combination of power, technical, and submission-style of wrestling. Similar to that of the Danger Man, only with more power and no high-flying. Also, is quite stiff with knees, elbow strikes, forearm strikes, etc. Strengths: His size. Ability to adapt to most situations. Tag Team wrestling. Weaknesses: Isn't as comfortable in one-on-one situations. Is deaf and mute, so communication can be a problem. Signature Moves: Like his trainer and ex-partner, Bradley Duncan has several signature moves. Some are more recent additions, as he tries to make an identity all his own. Please check the Match Writer Aid for usage of these moves and, well, other moves in Duncan's repetoire. Burning Hammer, just so happens to be the exact same move that Kenta Kobashi weilds. It's technical name is the Argentine Back Breaker Rack Brainbuster or Reverse Death Valley Driver. Dangerous III, is the same move done by Max Danger, and is the 360° Spinning Elbow Smash or Roaring Elbow. Hush is the chinlock and key lock combination submission hold which is viewable here. Lightning Fire Driver, is just a fancy name for the Powerbomb and ALL it's variations. Perilous Vision, is another fancily named move for something so common... the 3/4 Turn Neckbreaker. All variations apply and go under the same name. Shizukesa, is Japanese for 'silence', but is also the new name for Duncan's version of the Cross Armbreaker. Trademark Name: The Soul Destroyer Trademark Move: Belly-to-Back Half-Nelson and Chicken Wing Suplex Injuries Current Injuries: Currently isn't suffering from anything. Nagging Injuries: Has no past injuries that can crop up and bother him. Lucky man. History Years Pro: Three years. Manager: He doesn't have one. Allies: Conrad Ramsey would be Duncan's lone ally. Nemesis: He hates Donell Arcade with a passion. Doesn't care too greatly for his father Julius either. Both Max and Jessica Danger aren't technical enemies of Bradley, they aren't is friend either. Current Team: Honor & Glory with Conrad Ramsey. Past Teams: Non-Verbal Communication with Max Danger. Action Wrestling Title History: None. Action Wrestling Award History: None. Other Federations: PIW for 5 months, EYE for three months, gfwa OMEGA for 4 months, Advantage Wrestling for 1 match, and Spinebuster Wrestling for three months. Other Federation Awards: PIW World Tag Team Titles as NVC for 100 days. Attributes Intelligence: Eight. Strength: Nine. Toughness: Nine. Speed: Six. Endurance: Eight. Courage: Nine. Charisma: Seven.
