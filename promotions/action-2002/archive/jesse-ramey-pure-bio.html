Jesse Ramey Biography Nicknames 'Bad Attitude', 'Mr. Action!' Hometown Harts, West Virginia Birthday January 6th, 1976 Height 5'11' Weight 205 Biography One man can only live one life, or at least that is what you have been told all of your life, but the truth be told this is the tale of a man that has been made to live many in a very short time. From his humble begins as a child on into his haunting present. His story is not for the weak or faint at heart. Rittled with memories of the death of his parents, women, drugs, money, the people who he thought he could once call friend, and the only real love of his life taken from him. A career in professional wrestling that has been no accomplishment to him, as close to five of his twelve year career was devoted to having the management of whatever federation he was in holding him down. The only places he was safe was overseas and even there he had his enemies in the community itself. His decision rested upon one of two things, coming back to the states and hoping to find a promotion that wasn’t ran by underhanded monopoly or to stay overseas and risk his life wrestling in communities that were nothing short of anti-American. Fate, is something that comes swiftly, and now, the fate of this still young man’s career rests in the hands of Action Wrestling.
