About Action Wrestling: the opening statement

c. March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is (written in 2026) The federation site’s own “About Action Wrestling” page, as the donor’s database holds it (lot 0159): its opening statement, written as the federation opened (it calls Action a new federation, describes the weekly shows it is about to run, and remembers PIW as gone), so in or about March 2002, when its first card ran. The page carries no date. The donor’s copy follows it with a later statement by the same writer, which is not served here. The bracketed “[email removed]” is the donor copy’s own. No capture of the page is held. The page’s navigation and the markup of its headings are not reproduced; its text is.
Greetings and thank you very much for visiting the site. I’m Roland E. My former work has been in Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling, jOlt, Realm, and some Indy Federations some time ago. I was the Vise-President of PIW, and under that headline I did numerous things. I wrote some of the Cards, Booked, work with angles (more so the last month of the PIW era I was really able to get a hold of them), and other things as well. However PIW is gone, but it will always be remembered. I come to you now with a new Federation ran by all the Handlers’ here, Action Wrestling, in which I founded but in no way run or own the show. Each and every Member of Action I am happy to have join me in creating our version of a Wrestling story as they do just as much as myself in terms of creating and working hard. This Federation just couldn’t be possible without the work ethic and dedication they have and therefore is why I state this is our Federation and not just mine. However, not just the Handlers’ have played a role in being a huge part of this program, I would also like to thank Lewis J. for being a great friend to me and helping me make this Federation in order to have some real fun. He has done the Site and I think it looks just great. Tons of thanks as well goes out to [name withheld], who has always been there, giving me the pushes I need to succeed in my eGoals and life goals. Also I’d like to thank Lonnie C., Tom F. and [name withheld] for their help with CGI. Not to mention many others I’ve come to known over the Internet. I thank you all for being the great people that I know. And, I also thank you the Fans’. Without Fans’ in eWrestling there would be nothing. It’s the community that makes this hobby fun, and makes fun, even more worth while. Thank you for visiting this eFed. As you all know, there are lots of Federations’ out there that clam to be the best on the net, or the next "CWL" (R.I.P. Ed Wilson), "fWo", "21W", "Asylum", etc, etc. Well, we are not one of those Federations’, and I can proudly state that. There are lots and lots of other great eFeds’ out there too. Almost all of them are worth while to join, to visit, to read and to watch. We are just another version of that, and in turn, just here to do what we want to do: HAVE FUN. I suggest that after your visit here, to view any other Federation on the net I’ve listed in my "Federation Folder", or any Federation you’ve heard about. Any of them are worth a look at and rule just as much as all of the others’. Now, to what we are about. Here in Action it’s just about fun, like I said before, and telling our version of a Wrestling story. I have thought and thought of many new ideas in which could make Action Wrestling different but let me tell you, it’s hard to do. New ideas don’t come easy. And again, thank you to those that have helped me out, and to those that have really been supportive. You guys are great. Action is an Angle Federation. I for one am just not knowledgeable when it comes down to any Role Playing Fed. I have more-or-less an Angle type of mind. So therefore, this Federation is Angle based, fully, and totally, one hundred percent. Our shows are once a week. They will be done in Summary Style with the odd Special Show that happen in Play-by-Play here and there, simply because I, unlike some people, love a Play-by-Play Card and find it a little more interesting than Summary. We have built many different types of gimmicks into Action. One being our shows are based after plays/ movies. At the start we have a "Previously...", our commercials are called "Intermission" , our segments during the shows are "Scenes", and at the end we have a little "To Be Continued". That’s just our way of doing it. Our Role Plays are titled "Tales". Our Roster is called a "Cast of Characters". As it makes sense, that our Cast of Characters tell tales, which to me states they are telling stories about themselves to us. If you happen to be confused by any of this, don’t worry, it’ll be more straight forward in the shows. Our "Episodes" are to be held on the WB Network. Also, "Tales" will be broadcasted, but not normally. Think if it like a movie, or a soap opera, where every night at 11 PM EST WB Networks will broadcast the "Tales" recorded that day, but when really, to the story, there are no cameras’ filming. Again think of it just like a movie. The "Cast" do their tale while pretending the cameras’ aren’t there, staying in Character and acting. It’s just a way to get into a Wrestlers’ head and view what they think and do, like any movie or soap opera out there. I’ve always felt there was no real reason as to why something big could happen in an RP and following that up in the show, everyone would still "know" about it. At least to me I’ve fixed that now. And that’s about all, without really getting into it deeper and having to take up another long page. If you have any questions you are more than welcome to mail me. My address is as followed: [email removed] . However I do suggest that you please view the Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) before mailing me first. It might help to clear up your problems. Once more thank you for stopping by and making Action a part of your time. It’s really appreciated that you’ve come by and checked this Federation, out of many, out. We hope you enjoy it here, and if not, we are sorry we did not meet your expectations. Good luck to all, Fed Heads’ and Handlers’. Have fun in this hobby. We here are just another form of it. Cheers, Roland J. E.
