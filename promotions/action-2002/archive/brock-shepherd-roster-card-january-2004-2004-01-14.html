Brock was brought up in front of his computer. By the age of nine, Brock had made three computer viruses which tore into FBI, CIA and Pentagon computer systems, changing their documents to be downloaded by all. He was stopped from using the internet and all programs accessible to him in which he could make these documents. Brock's career in changing the world was over, or so he thought. At school, Brock saw there was opening's in the wrestling at age eleven, he started training at home and by the time he was in high school, Brock had configured every move that was fast and brutal to an opponent. He joined his high school wrestling squad to win the high school championships in Washington. By age sixteen, Brock was wanted by majority of independant federations but he held out. Brock started to date the head cheer-leader at the school, Bonnie Tucker. A hot little blonde piece that made Brock live every second of his life. He found more reason to live. At a wrestling meet when Brock was 18, his girlfriend found out she had contracted a discease which within 24 hours killed her. Causing Brock to lose track of his life and quit from the wrestling world. He was to never step foot into the ring again. Then, one day, living off of Social Services, Brock recieved a letter from the University of Washington, stating that the tests on him showed the disease had a differing effect on him to his girlfriend, Bonnie. Instead, it sped up muscle growth allowing Brock to tone up faster, by the rate of six point nine times. Thus making Brock somewhat of a superhero. Brock saw this as Bonnie's gift, her last wish, and decided it was time again to get back in the ring. After 6 months of training, now 21 years old, Brock was back to his physical peak. Even further along the track, Brock was startting a cult following in the backyard federations. Where Louie Miranda found him... Brock continued from BMW, leaving after a week when he was offered steroids from a trainer backstage, he was immediately picked up the Elite Wrestling Alliance. Where he spent six months there. His second appearance he had the EWA Intercontinental title wrapped tightly around his waist, being the quickest EWA rookie to win the title. He went on to hold it an amazing three times in EWA before he left, now Brock has signed on with AWE, which soon became defunct. Chuck U. Farley paid out his six month contract in full, leaving him high and dry, but a top prospect to be signed. Now, Action! Wrestling has taken an interest. As has the gigabytes of Brockoli...
