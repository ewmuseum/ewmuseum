The Ringer Biography Nicknames 'Prototype'; 'TR' Hometown Calgary, Alberta, Canada Birthday June 18th, 1980 Height 6' 2'' Weight 200 Biography The Ringer. Not really what we would call an anticipated debuting superstar these days. Although promising, The Ringer has never been the “talk” in anything. Not in wrestling. Not even in a form of life itself. The Ringer originated into ball sports as a child, but by accident we believe he had gotten into the wrestling world and found some success. He quotes “When I watched wrestling I began to like it more and more each day. I thought I’d give it a try. I didn't really like the other sports I played. They weren’t a challenge.” This could've been a lie however, we don't know... His child life is too unclear as well. It’s rumored he came from a horrible house of ranting and raving. A house that everyone was out for only themselves. The type of house that if you weren’t at the dinner table within two seconds, all the food would be gone for the rest of the night. It’s also taken that The Ringer comes from a large, dysfunctional family, but again, non of this has been proved. Real Name? No one really knows. He’ll lie to you just to get you off his back. He has many false pieces of ID, which leads us to, once more, not even have the faintest of facts about who he really is. Only Truth We Have: Training under Mason Hillon, The Ringer began an awesome wrestling career. He was unbeaten in his first five months with a record of forty and zero, in Syphon Wrestling. In fact The Ringer would end up being Syphon's best superstar as they closed a year and a half into their promotion. Yet critics never paid much attention to him. They wanted the big names in the WWF, fWo, the Asylum, etc. That didn’t stop The Ringer from winning though, as he just kept going. He couldn’t wait to get in the ring and show how pathetic his critics are. And Reed Young saw this. His dark side for critics was in mud too. The Ringer now had his 'break', or at least that’s what Young told him. Ringer believes he has a “special power” and is greater than everyone else. Will this mentality work in Action? Or will he suffer something he hasn’t before, failure? Or has he?
