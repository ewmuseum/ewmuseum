Daniel Phillips Birth Name: Daniel Alexander Phillips Also Known As: the King of the Cruiserweights, Philly, Danny, Xander(*flips Max Danger off*), Philly D (anyone calling him this will be subject to a severe beating), Riot Phillips Date of Birth: January 17th, 1975 Age: 28 Marital Status: Divorced Hometown: Phoenix, Arizona Height: 6'2' Weight: 212 lbs. Hair Color: Hazel Eye Color: Brown Blood Type: AB Status: Heel Personality: Misanthropic, but faithful. Theme Music: 'Downfall' by TRUSTcompany Wrestling Style Trained By: 'Nitro Beaver' Kyle Morris Detailed Style: Submission. Cruiserweight. Scumbag. Strengths: He is an eight year veteran of the ring. He is an excellent submission wrestler, especially in relation to the arms. His attention to the details of his opponents is rather outstanding and can use their mannerisms and habits against them. He is a manipulative bastard and uses any and all means to gain the upper hand. He cheats like nuts. He often makes his opponents make the first mistake. He can take to the air, but only rarely. Weaknesses: He has an extremely weak neck and a badly scarred right arm, both of which are prime targets. He can lose his temper just as easilly as he can make his opponents lose their temper. His penchant for pissing people off tends to blow up in his face. He doesn't react well to an unprofessional opponent. Signature Moves: Inciter I Blue Thunder Powerbomb Inciter II Styles Clash; rarely executed because it's difficult to apply effectively. Inciter III Somersault Ace Crusher. Resembles a Diamond Dust, except that Daniel's running from behind when executing it, instead of standing on the ropes. John Cena uses this, if you're wondering. Nagging Injuries: He has a bad neck injury from an incident on a Hostile Takeover about three years ago. He's so fearful of reinjuring it that he doesn't let people attack the neck all that often. Severely burned right forearm three years ago in a flaming table scaffold match against the Unknown Elements, an injury covered up by his arm gear. History Years Pro: Eight. Manager: None.
