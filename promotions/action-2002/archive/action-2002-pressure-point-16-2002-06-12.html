Pressure Point 16 Mellon Arena — Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania ---- PRESSURE POINT. 06/12/02 PRESSURE POINT. 06/12/02 Opening. Another day... another show. Pressure Point was beginning to no doubt open some people’s eyes. For it already lasted three months, even with the horrible Staff at hand. Wrestlers like LLB, Christopher Fox, Syphon Fission and Joey Malone were making an impact, while new stars were forming one. Everything was right on track. Ratings were going up... ticket sales were getting higher. It was all Reed Young wanted. And then, for no given reason... he goes missing. Pressure Point put off till now; the new blood whipped to a panic. This should’ve been expected though. It’s Reed Young after all. So... with the Owner still missing... the show would go on. Who would take to the extreme this time? ---- Syphon I. Syphon I. Syphon Fission. The man who had run roughshod over Action! Wrestling as of late, leaving a wake of destruction, chaos, and fear. He dragged Payback in with him. He was intent on spreading the word of Fission, one shovel blow to the skull at a time. He bumped right into a Management Officer, and grabbed him by his shirt. “You there, what are you doing?” “Er, er... um, I... I’m heading to the p- parking lot.” He said. Fission just sneered. “Why?” “B...b- because I was t- told that Joey Malone was going to be in a- attendance.” Fission tossed the man aside and yelled out loud, smacking Payback across random inanimate objects. The walls. The large travel cases. Anything within reach. The Officer quickly scurried away, not wanting to be beheaded by one of Syphon Fission’s wild swings. “DAMMIT! DAMMIT! He’s NOT here! HE’S NOT HERE!! GRRRRAAGGHHHH!!!” Fission still slammed Payback into various objects, before he stormed off in the direction of his Locker Room, attacking anything that happened to move on his way there. Something was going on. Something that Syphon Fission might not have control of. ---- The Care that Worries.
