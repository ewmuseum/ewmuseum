Coral Avalon Biography Nicknames the Kleptomaniac Hometown New Orleans, Louisiana Birthday December 22nd, 1981 Height 6' Weight 224 Fed Titles Bantam Fed Awards Match of the Year(vs. Jeff Garvin @ Players & Pawns) Biography In the ongoing effort between [name withheld] and [name withheld] to turn ourselves into a two-headed beast based on universal domination, I'm now stealing his whole thing with the bios and listing it with every letter of Coral Avalon's name. Chess. Coral loves chess. Whenever Coral Avalon is not wrestling, he is probably playing chess with his favorite chess partner, Rook Black. Mind you, he's not at Rook's level, but he's trying. Oh, god, is he ever trying. O Rivalry. Coral Avalon is pretty much the permanent worst enemy of 'the Original' Jeff Garvin. The two men have clashed on three pay-per-views(a fourth at the upcoming Players & Pawns event), including one Match of the Year. Also, both men have had their clashing in backstage incidents, and Coral has also clashed with Wrestling 101 more often than any other member of the Action! roster, including significant matches and feuds with Keith Scott Zimmerman and 'Impulse' Brian James. It is likely that Coral will continue to provide adequate resistance for the dominance of Wrestling 101 for many months to come. Aristocracy. Coral Avalon grew up in an affluent family on the outskirts of his hometown of New Orleans, Louisiana. His particular family of multi-millionaires that he lived with included his Irene Avalon(mother), Jonathan Avalon(father), Emmy Avalon(eldest sister), Elvis Avalon(older brother), Lily Avalon(little sister), Albert Avalon(uncle), and Cleo Avalon(aunt). Not to mention that he has other family members not accounted for. The family has earned its wealth with several oil claims in Texas and a restaurant chain in Louisiana. Coral was meant to be the heir of the restaurant business run by Jonathan Avalon, but he ditched the family based on his disgust with having his life predestined, to become a wrestler. L Agnostic. Unlike his friend, Marcus Dox, Coral only doubts the belief of God, he does not deny his existence entirely. The fact of the matter is that his family was never deeply religious, and therefore, this rubbed off on him as well. Vagabond. Ever since Coral Avalon left his family, he has pretty much wandered the country rather than staying in a specific residence. This is a self-inflicted punishment on his part for living so well in the first eighteen years of his life. Aside from semi-permanent homes in the residence of Joey Malone in Mesa, Arizona(whereupon he was kicked out to make room for Joey's sister, Alexandria Malone and Joey's daughter, Holly Malone) and the Heimdal Halfway House For Wayward Wrestlers in Ohio, Coral pretty much remains a vagabond and generally just goes wherever his federation goes. Amateur wrestler. He was on the varsity wrestling team in his high school for two years, keeping it a secret from his mother for as long as possible. He was slated to be a part of the big state-wide tournament, but by then, his mother found out and had him pulled from the team. However, Coral's amateur background remains one of the strongest aspects of his wrestling style, and often gives him an edge over many of his opponents. L O N Wrestling Style Adaptive all arounder, with a flair for stealing finishers from others. Hence the kleptomaniac gimmick. Finisher Move The Spiritbreaker Finisher Description Spinal Shock/Widow's Peak (Gory Special into a stiff hangman's neckbreaker; rare, has only been used three times) Special Moves A good 25% of Coral Avalon's moveset are finishing maneuvers that he's stolen from other wrestlers. For whatever reason, however, Coral has cut back on the stealing(it's limited just to counter moves at this point) and only really uses three moves as his primary finishers(the Spiritbreaker, mentioned below, is his hyper-rare finisher). These are the moves he's mastered and they have essentially become his own. Entrance Music 'Won't Back Down' by Fuel
