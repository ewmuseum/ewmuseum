Simon Seaman Biography Nicknames S2 Hometown Burbank, California Birthday March 20, 1975 Height 6'1' Weight 233 Fed Titles Carnage Championship (2) Biography March 20, 1975 would be the day that Simon Seaman would never forget. Why you ask? It's his birthday. You think he would forget his own birthday? Use your head for goodness sake. No, not that one. That's right, the other one. Anyway, onto the story. Seaman was raised in Los Angeles, California by his mother and father. Some say he was the kind of kid that was incredibly fortunate. He was good looking, he was good looking and did I mention he was good looking? He realized this on the first day of kindergarten as for the entire day, as kids were learning their ABC's and 123's, Simon was learning how to get a good hair care regimen going and speaking with his future agent on the Flinstone phone. Friends called him 'yucky'. He called them 'blech'. It was a little thing they had. Most likely his most important trait, offers from several companies came pouring into the Seaman family mailbox. They wanted Simon in their television commercials and were willing to do quite a bit to get that task done. Though Simon decided that he didn't want to go that route. Eating watery soup with a person he didn't even know and running around the yard with a dog in a commercial wasn't his cup of tea. It was his cup of mocha latte, but that's not the point. Let's just move on. At age 9, Simon would watch professional wrestling for the first time. He was captivated by the elaborate costumes and the athletic ablitily of these performers, but he watched it for what it was and that was entertainment. As years went by, his interest in wrestling would dwindle and he would go on to use his looks to his advantage, gaining a temp job at a Los Angeles television station. Months later, we would see Simon as co-anchor of the nightly news for a year. Funny thing was that Simon was supposed to be the one doing sports, but I guess that went out the window. From the start of college to the age of 23, his home was at that television station. That was until he thought that he wasn't being used to his full potential. All out of ideas of alternative careers, Simon would try something that completely risky. He would attempt to become a professional wrestler. He remembered watching them on television, but he never thought of actually being one, until now. He wanted something different and something new. He wished for New Coke when there was just Coke. He wanted Crystal Pepsi instead of regular Pepsi and damn it, he was going to do what he had to do to get it. Learning the ropes at a local wrestling school, he would get to know the basics and improve on those skills. He might not have been the most co-ordinated student, but he was willing to do what he could to succeed. Perseverance? No, not really. Will power? Next. Basically, he didn't know what was good for him and just did what he was asked to do. Though this would pay off when he was brought into an independent federation based out of southern California by a talent scout looking for up and coming wrestlers. His skills weren't great, but he was brought in for his look. He realized the reason why he was brought in and others believed that was all he was good for, but he proved them wrong. Excelling in promos and improving on his in-ring performance, his hard work would give him the company's television title. Although, the downside of this was mere weeks after he won the title, the company folded and Simon was left to find work elsewhere. Rethinking his new career path, he almost quit right then and there. Then came an offer from the IWO Minor League (IML). He was in the right place at the right time. This was good because he had problems with taxi drivers and clocks with no batteries before. Given a second chance, he thrived in that company, later got his big break, being promoted to the Internet Wrestling Organization. People everywhere took notice and Simon didn't disappoint. He may not have been the most decorated wrestler in the company, but he sure acted like he was. The fans stood behind him until he won the IWO World Heavyweight Championship. Then everything changed. He shouldn't have had that cup of coffee that morning because it didn't keep him regular at ll. Other than that, for whatever reason, the fans turned their back on him. Maybe because they were jealous or envious that he was in fact the world champion. Maybe some didn't want him as the world champion. Well...maybe they shouldn't have treated him like they did before he was champ. They didn't think he could amount to anything more mid card status, but he did all that and more and they thank him by booing him. It's typical, but it doesn't matter much to Simon for he will continue to do what he does best. No, not getting a high score in Ms. Pac Man. Well, then the second thing being wrestling. Yeah, that's it. In Action, Simon Seaman plans to thrive in a whole new and different environment. Attempting to capitolize on his previous success, he strives to make Simon Seaman a household name. He will try to bring his own flavour into a federation that will most likely be filled with angry individuals who have nothing to be angry about and appear like they need to go to the washroom and wrestlers with a billion 'death defying' moves that will surely lead them to actual death. Whether in the ring or on the mic and fan favourite or not, Simon is out to prove that he belongs. That and you can dance if you want to. You can leave your friends behind. Cause if you don't dance and if you can't dance...well, then you're aren't no friend of his. Damn skippy.
