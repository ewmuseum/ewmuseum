Sweet Aroma Birth Name: Veronica Aromadeus Also Known As: Roni Aroma, Sweety, Sweetz, V Date of Birth: August 25, 1980 Age: 22 Marital Status: Engaged To Jonathan Conspiracy Hometown: New York, NY (Spanish Harlem) Height: 5'8' Weight: 145 lbs. Hair Color: Black w/red streaks Eye Color: Brown Blood Type: Status: Face Personality: So Sweet Theme Music: A Woman's Worth - Alicia Keys Wrestling Style Trained By: Jonathan Conspiracy Detailed Style: High Flyer/Technical Strengths: Speed, Courage, Intelligence Weaknesses: Lack of strength, doesn't know when to back down Signature Moves: 'The Closest You'll Get' (Figure Four Sleeperhold/Sweet places a chokehold on her opponent by making a figure four leg grapevine around the opponents neck.) 'Conspiracy Theory' (Guillotine Legdrop) 'Crazy In Love' (Asai Moonsault) Trademark Name: So Sweet Trademark Move: Reverse Twist of Fate/Sets up for reverse DDT and then spins it into Twist of Fate neckbreaker Injuries Current Injuries: none Nagging Injuries: on again/off again neck problems no thanks to Max Danger & KSZ History Years Pro: close to 1 year Manager: n/a Allies: Roberto Aromadeus (brother/guardian), Jonathan Conspiracy (trainer/fiancee), Benecio Aromadeus (cousin), Angelica Dawson, Conrad Ramsey, Max Danger Nemesis: Ricardo Verez, Everett Johnson, Bobby Minio, KSZ Current Team: Past Teams: Clit 'N' Candy Express w/Angelica Dawson Action Wrestling Title History: Dyad Champion (August 25th 2002-October 2nd 2002) Action Wrestling Award History: Other Federations: Other Federation Awards: The News Match of the Week (Dyad Title Match w/Angelica Dawson against Bobby Minio and Max Danger) Attributes Intelligence: 9 Strength: 3 Toughness: 7 Speed: 9 Endurance: 7 Courage: 7 Charisma: 7 Skill: 6 Biography Sweet Aroma, born Veronica Aromadeus, is a young girl turned off by the way guys act. She's been through all the 'I just wanna get some' guys, and they never got any. Sweet just wants a guy that completes her, but that day seems far off. On two occassions she thought she found the guy, but on two occassions the guy was just really good at hiding his 'I just wanna get some' motive. August 25, 1996 Sweet Sixteen for Veronica. And a boy she liked took her brother up on the invite to the party. The boy was two years older than V, but he was also her brother's best friend, and was always around; she even walked in on him using the bathroom once. But back to August 25th 1996. They're in her bedroom, they managed to escape the rest of Roni's friends and her brother. But it was almost time for Ricardo Verez to leave for a pick up game at Rucker Park with Roni's brother, Roberto; but he was preoccupied with Roni. [withheld — a passage held under the museum's content rule] But back to August 25th 1996. Roberto re-entered the house, and made a b-line for Roni's room. There V sat on her bed, with tears streaming down her face. It was then that Roberto finally became a big brother, and V became a woman. Robbie held her head on his shoulders, comforting the birthday girl until she feel fast asleep. Robbie tucked V in and kissed her on the forehead before heading down the stairs to get her gift. A platinum bracelet that Robbie had worked hard for, and it was on layaway for quite sometime; the inscription on the inside 'Happy Birthday, My Sweet Aroma'. Two years later... ...Veronica Aromadeus, High School Graduate from Stuyvasent High School. She wasn't the valedictorian, or salutatorian, but she was a very bright girl, making the honor roll on numerous occassions and gaining numerous scholarships to pay for an education in Princeton, New Jersey. She considered New York University, but couldn't pass up the opportunity to finally leave New York City, and felt that Jersey was close enough to stay in contact with her brother, and to still see Roberto on occassion. Besides, soon after the worst day of her life, Roberto showed her something that he said he would only show a brother, but with no more parents, there wasn't going to be a brother. You see Robbie would work in the day, but from the time he got off work until the time he had to be home to have dinner ready for V and himself, he would be at the famed 'Hell's Kitchen Wrestling School'. And all the training has finally paid off, because Robby finds himself getting offers to wrestle in dark matches and at numerous house shows, for a variety of federations. But back to 1998. Forbes College, room 405. A guy from her calculus class has walked her home from the late class. Everett Johnson's the name, and he's done it all, and he'd like to do something with Veronica, he is cute. But Veronica's not that type of girl, and she thinks Everett for the walk and the talk, and kissed him on the cheek and was set to send him on his way. But Everett took the kiss, like a speedy baseball player takes the third base coach's sign to run for home. And forced himself into the room, closing the door behind him, and then knocking V onto the bed. But a few nights back in Hell's Kitchen Wrestling School with Roberto helped Sweet out of this situation. A well placed knee to the private area of Mr. Johnson put him down and out, until Campus Security hit the scene, and hauled young Everett off to the Princeton Police Station. However alot of girls didn't like how the events of October 14, 1998 went down, and Roni Aromadeus soon found herself alienated from the girls at school, and would drop out and return to live with Roberto in Spanish Harlem, while working to help Robbie pay the bills, as well as train alongside him to become his manager for whenever Roberto got his big break. Little did she know that Jonathan Conspiracy would notice the young sultry diva during one of Robby's sparring sessions and urged her to step into the ring with him. A few pointers from 'One Letter Better' and Veronica wasn't training to be a manager/valet anymore. She was side by side with Roberto taking the bumps and gaining the bruises to start her own wrestling career, and now Veronica finally wants some 'Action!', but not the kind you think.
