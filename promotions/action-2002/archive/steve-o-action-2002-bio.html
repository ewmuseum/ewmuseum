Steve- O Biography Nicknames 'Dude', Steve-O the Sex God Hometown In a hospital with bright lights Birthday Uh dude I can't remember. Height 9 inches Weight 5 lbs Biography Nate and Steve-O were born in Sydney Australia. At that time there mom and dad thought, ‘These boys can be anything they ever wanted’. They were so happy with there two beautiful baby boys. So proud. In grade one the two kids were at the top of their class. They were nice to everyone, paid good attention, and never once caused trouble. As years passed ey slipped… a lot. Surfing was a big part of their life, they would skip school just to go and ‘catch some waves’. Their parents feared that there two loving children were on the old ‘maryjane’ , the ‘maryuchie’, ‘Marajauna’… But the time they were in grade Ten they spent just about as much time skipping school as they did in it. But their parents kept paying off the teachers to move them up the ladder of learning because in their mind if they got into college they one start to work hard. But there was one thing standing in the way… they had no plans of going to college. They even once tried to be let into Disney World by flashing the lady at the ticket both, cause they thought she wanted them. They would wake up at 2:00PM , watch TV till 4:00Pm and then hit the waves till 1:00 AM. There life was just a bunch of nothing. And their parents got fed up. They kicked the two out of the house, but they were 19 anyway, and said ‘Go Find A Job!’ So determined they set up to do what ma and pa said, ‘Find a job’. Steve-O found a job at a Surf Board shop. It paid well, he could get a free board, and he knew all about it. But the thing was, since you were going to be working at the register, you had to be able to typed more then 1 word per minute. And since Steve-O was almost always half baked he couldn’t type in one word in the gave him 2 hours. Nate wanted to run for mayor but he decided to settle for Women’s Clothing. But yet, he got fired for trying to help a women out in the changing room. So both guys were out of a job and needed money… why? To buy food? To buy water? To rent an apartment? No, they needed to buy wax for their ‘boards’. They decided to crash with a buddy other theirs, Andre Goden… A guy the made fun of a lot in High School (when they went). He was a computer genius he was trying to hack into the Nasa computers to see if the US really did land on the moon. His parents were just as weird, when most families went away to tropical islands for vacation, his parents took his to try and find Area 51. But as much as they made fun of him he still worshipped them like gods. One night while watching UFC on PPV (Andre was looking for clues to find out if it was fake) Nate and Steve-O thought it would be a cool way to put some green in their pocket. But they needed someone who was smart, someone who was good with strategy, some one like… Andre Goden. Andre was really excited to be apart of the team but Nate and Steve-O had a few conditions. One, he could never been seen with them in public. Two, he had to pay for all their expenses. And three, he had to take a bath every two days. Andre , even though he didn’t like the last one, went a lot with them… So the Trio set out for the number one fighting organisation in North America… the Asylum. The dynamic two known as Pretty Fly went on to have a great successful career in the Asylum however the fighting style didn't match their type of personality and didn't shine their 'cray-zay' talent. They left Asylum, not on good terms with Joe Campbell...but hey, who cares? They went to 21wrestling and found themselves leaving under the spotlight... So here we are...the Fly Boys 'Narly' Nate and Steve-O now in Action Wrestling!. How? No idea. Some say Reed Young saw them at a party in the boonies and asked them to join.Some say they just came to a show and got the job! None the less, a la RVD like...no one can get 'higher' than Pretty Fly!
