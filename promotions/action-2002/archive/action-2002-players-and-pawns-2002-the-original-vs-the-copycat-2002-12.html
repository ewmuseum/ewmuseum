Players and Pawns: The Original vs. The Copycat

December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The site's own story page for the Iron Man Match on Players and Pawns (31 December 2002), a build-up piece signed by the staffer James Attwood, from the Wayback capture of 14 January 2003. The show itself is served on this wing.
Since the beginning of September, Jeff Garvin and Coral Avalon have been at each others throats. It was at ‘Retaliation’ when everything looked to be over for Avalon. Garvin not only beat him; he battered him and in the end... made him tap out. The thirty minute affair left many scars and bruises on both men- but neither were finished just yet. Four months later, the two have engaged in one of the most memorable feuds in recent history. At times it made the Fans laugh, while at others they couldn’t do anything else but sit in awe. At ‘Players and Pawns’, they look to finally settle the score. In only the second Iron Man Match in Action! Wrestling history, sixty minutes of blood, sweat, and even tears; it will all stain the canvas on December 31st of 2002, when two of the biggest up-and-comers of this year lace up their boots to go one more time. And end this thing the only way they know how... In the ring. -James Attwood ggg
