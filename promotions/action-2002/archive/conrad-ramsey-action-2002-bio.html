Conrad Ramsey Biography Nicknames Does he look like Jennifer Garner to you? Hometown He currently resides in Leitchfield, Kentucky. He was born in Paintsville, Kentucky but spent the majority of his life in Denver, Colorado. Birthday Seventh Day of the Twelfth Month of the Forty-First Year in the Twentieth Century. Height Six feet, One inch. Weight Two-Hundred Thirty-Seven Pounds. Fed Titles Bantam Championship Biography C O N Running gags. In literature, film, and television, some characters, directors, writers or whatever have running gags in all their work. That's what I'm going to have with Conrad. There will be three running gags; 1) He will go Bruce Lee on your ass. If he's pushed just a little too far. If he's just sick and tired of having his ass handed to him. And if he's just damn frustrated with things, he will snap. The spirit and mind of the legendary movie martial artist will converge within Conrad, thus UNLEASHING THE TIGER! 2) Each and every match from here on out, Ramsey will attempt a kip up. The operative word there being 'attempt'. See, he's not going to hit it. Not for a long time anyway. He will try and try and try, but continue to just fall right back down onto the mat. But one day, in a galaxy far, far away... er, sorry. He will nail it one of these days. But who knows when. Just pray to whatever deity you believe in that it doesn't happen against you. 3) The final running gag will be Conrad's middle name. For the life of me, I cannot remember what I originally gave him. I know it started with a 'D', since all my characters usually have one of their names starting with that letter. I assumed it was Darryl for the longest time and then changed it to David when I couldn't think of it. So, from now on, he just won't know his middle name. Every time the subject is brought up it will be a different name, starting with the letter 'D'. A D R A M S E Y Other Feds Spinebuster Wrestling for 1 show.
