He looked to the corner of his eye, and saw a darkened figure slowly walk his way towards Sharc. Sharc gritted his teeth and clenched his fist as hard as he could. He slowly slipped the wallet into his back pocket and readied himself for battle. The footsteps got louder, and the figure got closer; and Sharc was more ready than ever for the attack. Sharc, acting impulsively, quickly darted forward. His closed his eyes and roared angrily as he threw a wild punch into the air, connecting with a glass jaw that shattered beneath the weight of his fist. Sharc grunted loudly and towered over his fallen victim, ready to continue the assault. But something was wrong. He looked down at his victim and realized that the man he had just leveled was not who he thought it was. Instead of the man being Levine, it was a road agent (unfortunately not Joe Black) dressed in a heavy leather coat. The road agent’s eyes remained closed and his body remained motionless, as an angry Sharc cussed at himself beneath his breathe. His hair remained hovered over his eyes, as he spoke lowly to himself. ”Where is that mother fucker?” … “Looking for me?” The voice came from behind him, and before Sharc could turn around he felt two large hands grab him by the back of his head and send his face crashing into the brick wall before them. Sharc could feel his warm blood trickle down his broken nose the moment his face met the wall with a great amount of velocity. Behind him was none other than Levine. Levine starred down blankly, as Sharc began to cough harshly, leaving a puddle of blood below him. Levine didn’t care for Sharc’s well being, and the moment Sharc looked up Levine had no mercy in lifting his boot and kicking downward into Sharc’s face. Levine looked down at Sharc disgustedly as he knelt beside Sharc and rolled him over. ”After this whole big hunt, you can’t even handle what you asked for Sharc. You’re like a stupid fucking female, or better yet Keith Scott Zimmerman, you never know what you want…and when you get it, you’re fucked,” Levine said with an evil bit of arrogance as he reached into Sharc’s pocket. Levine removed the wallet from his grasp and immediately held it to his face. It was almost a hypnotic trans for Levine, as he starred at it with a great amount of joy. Levine sighed deeply. It was over, finally over. The moment that wallet was opened, his life would be complete. Sharc had made the fatal mistake in testing Levine, and now the test was over and Levine had passed with flying colors. No one was as happy as Levine was…for the first time in his life, Levine had smiled. And it wasn’t the usual sick and sadistic smile, it was a smile of pure joy…one that a man would share with a woman, one that a little boy would share with his parents after receiving his Christmas Present, and one that a mother would share with her son after he did something good. He hoped he could now share those types of emotions; see his family, live the life he always wanted, and stop this life of pure living hell. It was the moment of truth. He opened the wallet, and quickly began to scan its contents. But what he saw, wasn’t what he wanted to see. The wallet had no driver’s license, the wallet had no proof of identity; it only had a large piece of paper that read “SUCKER”. And there it was, the low and sick laugh of Sharc as he slowly began to scale to his feet. His mouth opened with a bright red smile, gladden in his own blood. Levine looked at the wallet for a long time, and with every second he stared at it, the more he became enraged. Sharc continued to laugh, as he leaned against the brick wall where his nose was broken just moments ago. ”You’re predicable as all fuck, you know that Levine?” said Sharc as he hung his head low, “I know you like a god damn book, you have done the same things over and over for the past eight years; I think this is a hint to change your act a bit, wouldn’t you say?” ”There is no wallet, is there?” Levine asked harshly, taking his eyes away from the wallet and staring up at Sharc. ”Oh, there is…but did you think I’d let you have it that easily?” But before Sharc could laugh any harder, he heard the wallet get hurled onto the floor. He lifted his eyes slightly, just in time to see Levine curl his fist and send it directly between his eyes. Sharc’s vision became blurry for a moment, as his head snapped back and his body fell backward onto the floor. Levine had had enough of the mind games, no one would fuck with his mind this much and get away with it; he was going to make sure Sharc never played another head game again. Levine reached over to lift Sharc off the ground. He grabbed this foe by his long crimson hair and yanked up as hard as he could…but Sharc was not lifted off the ground. Levine looked over at his hand to reveal a hand full of crimson red hair. Levine cocked an eyebrow up at the hair in his hand, as he looked down at the figure he had just leveled. Levine looked back at the crimson hair in his hand; no fucking way he just ripped Sharc’s scalp off his head… And he didn’t. The man he had leveled, was an imposter. Levine grunted angrily, and threw the hair piece down. He soon scooped the man from the floor and hurled him into a nearby refreshment table. Levine was angrier than all hell, as he grabbed the stooge by his collar with one hand, and began to send stiff right hands into his face with the other hand. “Sharc” continued to be beaten, more blood began to spill from his nose and mouth, as his face began to engorge in blood after the brutal punches he received. ”Where is he?!” shouted Levine as he stopped his assault to pull up the imposter and headbutt him to the ground, “Tell me where he is, damn you! Speak now, or sacrifice your fucking pathetic life!” Levine shouted as he reared back and soccer kicked the actor directly into the side of the ribs, causing the man to roll over onto his back and begin coughing violently. The man began to pull himself up and beg for forgiveness, almost getting into a prayer formation in front of Levine, “I don’t know! I was only hired to do the job…” ”The job?” questioned Levine. ”I was paid to play the part of Sharc, to lure you in. I was only supposed to mentally hurt you, and get the hell out of here; please, I’m sorry,” he begged Levine. ”Who paid you?” roared Levine. “I can’t say” ”FUCKER!” With the one word that poured from Levine’s mouth, an all out assault would begin. It began with another soccer kick to the ribs, followed by a few more mounted UFC-Style punches to the face. It would continue on to an even worse fate, where Levine would grab this man and slam him as hard as he could THROUGH the exit door. The hired actor, flew through the door and stumbled onto the cement, where he lay flat on his face. “I didn’t get paid for this” he whispered to himself, “Levine! It was Hertz and someone else…they paid me to do this…please, no more; that’s all I know” ”Fuck you…you know what you did,” Levine said, “You knew the risks you were taking when you entered a job with this much danger before you, and you still took it knowing full well who you were fucking with. Did you take mercy on me when you decided to play along with this mind game? Did you take mercy on me when you decided to take the one bit of happiness I had and rip it to shreds as if it were some simple piece of paper?” No response, just a violent cough that ended in a puddle of blood. ”Well, than I have NO mercy on you…I’ll give you a message to send to Hertz for me, a message that will make it clear to him what he has gotten himself into” Levine spoke no other words, and lifted the man from his feet. The actor, “Sharc” so to speak, did not stand up; but dangled underneath Levine’s hard grip which held the man up to his face. Levine starred harshly at the man before grabbed him by his throat and hurling him into a nearby Sedan. The back of the “Sharc” imposter slammed hard into the hood, forcing the man to stumble forward and fall flat on his face, his limbs sprawled out. Levine simply spat upon the victim and looked over at the Sedan. ”Give him this message for me, never enter a danger when you aren’t prepared for the risks” With that said, Levine had slammed his fist through the window of the Sedan and opened the door. As he entered, he reached beneath the wheel and began to rip out a few cords…before the engine roared in the night, and Levine quickly entered the hot wired vehicle. No sooner did the engine roar, than did Levine press hard upon the gas pedal… The fans gasped… The horror was evident… And “Sharc” screamed bloody murder. For the car drove full force…running over the actor’s legs and crushing them beneath the tons of weight in the Sedan. Levine said no words, no threats, he just drove off into the night, as the fake Sharc screamed bloody murder, looking at his crushed legs. Where once proud and full legs remained, now was a crushed mess of skin and blood that formed around his body. He screamed for help, as medical personnel rushed to the scene. The message was sent.
