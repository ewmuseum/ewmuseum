What this is Coral Avalon’s card from the fed’s own roster page, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 10 December 2003. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Coral Avalon Birth Name: Coral Dylan Avalon Also Known As: the Kleptomaniac Date of Birth: December 22nd, 1981 Age: 21 Marital Status: Single and broody Hometown: New Orleans, Louisiana Height: 6' Weight: 224 lbs. Hair Color: Black Eye Color: Blue Blood Type: B+ Status: Face Personality: Taciturn and quiet. Generally avoidant to conversations and especially doesn't enjoy speaking in front of people. He is genuinely selfless and puts other people in front of him. Tends to overthink. Tends to brood. Tends to be awkward in standard conversation with new people. Theme Music: 'Won't Back Down' by Fuel Wrestling Style Trained By: The Kent Anthason Academy of Ass-Kicking and the Joey Malone Medical School of Mesa Detailed Style: Adaptive all arounder, with a flair for stealing finishers from others. Hence the kleptomaniac gimmick. Strengths: Agility. Speed. Unpredictability. Adaptiveness. Endurance. Weaknesses: Being social. Lack of promo skills. Naive. Standard-issue finishers(ie: the ones that aren't his big three) tend to be weak and easilly countered. Usually takes an ass-kicking. Often emotionally confused. Signature Moves: A good 25% of Coral Avalon's moveset are finishing maneuvers that he's stolen from other wrestlers. For whatever reason, however, Coral has cut back on the stealing(it's limited just to counter moves at this point) and only really uses three moves as his primary finishers(the Spiritbreaker, mentioned below, is his hyper-rare finisher). These are the moves he's mastered and they have essentially become his own. 1) Ratings Spike, stolen from Ruben Ross Inverted facelock swinging neckbreaker. 2) Curveball, stolen from Scott Slugger Swinging double arm DDT 3) The Golden Elbow, stolen from Golden Glen Miller Opponent is down and centrally positioned. Coral bounces off both sets of ropes to the sides of the opponent before he comes back with a forward falling elbow drop to the forehead or face. Trademark Name: The Spiritbreaker Trademark Move: Spinal Shock/Widow's Peak (Gory Special into a stiff hangman's neckbreaker; rare, has only been used three times) Injuries Current Injuries: None. Nagging Injuries: None. History Years Pro: Three. Manager: None. Allies: Max Danger, Jessica Danger, Rook Black, Frank Heimdal, Duel, Joey Malone, Conrad Ramsey, E.M. Pleh, and Patricia Grayson Nemesis: 'the Original' Jeff Garvin, Keith Scott Zimmerman, 'Impulse' Brian James, Daniel Phillips, and Airborn Tyler Current Team: None. Past Teams: w/ Joey Malone Action Wrestling Title History: Bantam Action Wrestling Award History: Match of the Year(vs. Jeff Garvin @ Players & Pawns) Other Federations: None. Other Federation Awards: None. Attributes Intelligence: Coral is rather smart by himself or among a few close friends(ie: Rook Black, Max Danger). Unfortunately, his nervousness around other people can lend Coral to be a little awkward and dumb to the casual observer. (7) Strength: Coral is strong enough to lift people of the same size as he is up for moves, but against larger opponents, he has to fall back on his non-lifting moves. Luckilly, his moveset consists of many of those sorts of moves. (4) Toughness: Tough enough to survive long enough to find the weakness, but not long enough to hold out if his opponent is relentless. (6) Speed: Fastest and most agile male on the roster. End of story. (10) Endurance: He has wrestled one iron man match in his career and can literally go against people willing to take him to long matches. (9) Courage: Brave enough to face anyone one-on-one, reluctant to face more than one person. Is absolutely afraid of cutting promos without support(ie: Danger). Is also absolutely afraid of his family. So, most of the courage is reserved for the ring. (7) Charisma: Charasmatic in the ring, and in the ring only. (8) Skill: Incredibly skilled and talented for a man of his expierence. (10) Biography In the ongoing effort between Mike R. and Michael D. to turn ourselves into a two-headed beast based on universal domination, I'm now stealing his whole thing with the bios and listing it with every letter of Coral Avalon's name. Chess. Coral loves chess. Whenever Coral Avalon is not wrestling, he is probably playing chess with his favorite chess partner, Rook Black. Mind you, he's not at Rook's level, but he's trying. Oh, god, is he ever trying. O Rivalry. Coral Avalon is pretty much the permanent worst enemy of 'the Original' Jeff Garvin. The two men have clashed on three pay-per-views(a fourth at the upcoming Players & Pawns event), including one Match of the Year. Also, both men have had their clashing in backstage incidents, and Coral has also clashed with Wrestling 101 more often than any other member of the Action! roster, including significant matches and feuds with Keith Scott Zimmerman and 'Impulse' Brian James. It is likely that Coral will continue to provide adequate resistance for the dominance of Wrestling 101 for many months to come. Aristocracy. Coral Avalon grew up in an affluent family on the outskirts of his hometown of New Orleans, Louisiana. His particular family of multi-millionaires that he lived with included his Irene Avalon(mother), Jonathan Avalon(father), Emmy Avalon(eldest sister), Elvis Avalon(older brother), Lily Avalon(little sister), Albert Avalon(uncle), and Cleo Avalon(aunt). Not to mention that he has other family members not accounted for. The family has earned its wealth with several oil claims in Texas and a restaurant chain in Louisiana. Coral was meant to be the heir of the restaurant business run by Jonathan Avalon, but he ditched the family based on his disgust with having his life predestined, to become a wrestler. L Agnostic. Unlike his friend, Marcus Dox, Coral only doubts the belief of God, he does not deny his existence entirely. The fact of the matter is that his family was never deeply religious, and therefore, this rubbed off on him as well. Vagabond. Ever since Coral Avalon left his family, he has pretty much wandered the country rather than staying in a specific residence. This is a self-inflicted punishment on his part for living so well in the first eighteen years of his life. Aside from semi-permanent homes in the residence of Joey Malone in Mesa, Arizona(whereupon he was kicked out to make room for Joey's sister, Alexandria Malone and Joey's daughter, Holly Malone) and the Heimdal Halfway House For Wayward Wrestlers in Ohio, Coral pretty much remains a vagabond and generally just goes wherever his federation goes. Amateur wrestler. He was on the varsity wrestling team in his high school for two years, keeping it a secret from his mother for as long as possible. He was slated to be a part of the big state-wide tournament, but by then, his mother found out and had him pulled from the team. However, Coral's amateur background remains one of the strongest aspects of his wrestling style, and often gives him an edge over many of his opponents. L O N Off Stage Information Name: Mike R.
