You cheer for guys like Max Danger, guys like Frank Heimdal…but only because they say what YOU want to hear. But I, on the other hand, say whatever the hell I want. I will say what goes on backstage, I will comment on what the smarks have been saying…and I will do whatever I have to…to piss you people off” Levine stated as he eyed the crowd behind his dark shaded glasses, “I mean, how do you people THINK I’m going to react to your comments? I’ve been doing this business for eight years now, more than anyone else in this idiotic company. I’ve been wrestling WAY before Joey Malone, Coral Avalon, or any of those overrated actors have been around. And yet, I hear YOU people say that I can’t wrestle?” The fans, for the first time in the night, cheer the man formerly known as KroW. ”When did wrestling become a mixture of gymnastics and fighting? The last time I checked, the only rules about wrestling is that you have to either make your opponent submit, or pin him to the ground for three seconds. I never heard the rules of wrestling were to perform a bunch of fancy moves, lock a submission for three minutes, and than commit the most used move in Action history…BLUE THUNDER BOMB”, Levine stated. “And who made these rules?” ”The people, of course. The smarks of the wrestling world stated that wrestling isn’t about grounding your opponent, or just making your opponent feel the worst amount of pain in the world. No, because the smarks got sick of seeing that and began to say, “Oh, well anyone can do that””, Levine suddenly paused for a second, stopping mid-sentence as he pondered what he had just stated, “Wait a damn second…if anyone can do that…than why is it that only a choice few are doing it? I mean, if everyone can do what I do in this ring…why am I making close to $250,000 a month…while you’re struggling to make that amount in two years?” The crowd in Kansas City continued to express their hatred for Levine. ”I’ll tell you why. Because every single last one of you couldn’t last a second doing the kind of shit I do in this ring. You couldn’t swing a chair right, you couldn’t take a nasty bump, and you couldn’t perform any sort of Piledriver, DDT or any move that slammed the opponent on the tip of their head,” Levine said arrogantly, “But yet…you seem to know more about wrestling than me….but I’m the one getting paid to do what I do…” He rubbed his chin for a moment and than smiled brightly. ”But enough about what you think wrestling is, because that is far from my concern. What’s been troubling me lately is the fact that I was not on last week’s show. Do you know why I wasn’t on last week’s show?” Levine asked the crowd, as the crowd began to respond with statements like ‘cause you suck’ and such to that extent…all of which Levine simply shrugged off, “It’s because I was approached by Action executives last week, and was given two grand just to stay away from the arena…so what would you rather do? Come and try to entertain people who don’t care for you? Or go visit the night life and get PAID to do it?” The crowd continued their jeers, as Levine continued to smile. ”Let me tell you what I did last week, just to boil your blood a little bit more. While guys like Max Danger, Jeff Garvin, hell even Roland Miles Ermann were wrestling their hearts out in this ring…I was at a night club, enjoying the night life of hard liquor and loose women…bet you never thought you’d hear ME say something like that…huh?” Levine began to swagger around the ring, making sure all the fans caught sight of the PIW World Championship that was wrapped securely around his waist. ”But that is how Action is, they use money to solve all of their problems. They bought out Chris Fear’s contract when he was causing so much hell backstage. They bought out a time slot so they could put over their superstars with their show called ‘whatever’. And they use money to constantly keep Reed busy with things other than his own company...but the one problem that Action can’t get rid of with the almighty dollar bill… Is me” ”Notice how they could only afford enough money to get rid of me for one show…but the next show, I’m fucking ending it with my oh so wonderful rant,” Levine said as he looked out to the crowd in attendance. ”And since I really have nothing more to say than the usual ‘Action sucks’ rant, I will bid you adieu…and leave you to comment about how terrible my segment was…and how it should’ve never ended an Action Pressure Point. I bid you adieu…and leave Keith Scott Zimmerman a chance to rant on me in his little articles. I bid you adieu…and allow the entire Action roster to bitch and complain that I was even given TV time. And finally, I bid you adieu…,” Levine suddenly released his trenchcoat from his shoulders, allowing the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling shirt to flash before the crowd, who immediately jeered their brains out. ”To allow you to spread the rumors of the BIG PIW takeover. Because you ALL know I get along with ALL of my PIW comrades…right?” Levine said as he flashed one last smile, before removing his PIW World Championship and holding it above his head…before the sounds of Primer 55 returned to the PA system. It was a segment that bounced from one bit of information to another…it was a segment that constantly changed from one topic to another. He knew the writers would be very angry that he didn’t follow the script of Levine turning back to KroW and commenting on the release of Sylo…and he damn sure knew the fans were not satisfied with this segment. But did he care? Fuck no. It was Action Wrestling…and Levine never cared about Action Wrestling. So thus he rolled out of the ring, holding his PIW World Championship across his shoulder. And as he continued up the ramp, he couldn’t help but look over to the corner of his eye…and see a fan holding one of the imitation Action World Championship…Levine quickly snatched the belt, which could’ve cost the fan well over 100 dollars…and slowly began to drag it behind him…allowing the silver plated title to get scratched and dent on the steel rampway. So thus the biggest rebel of Action left, the one man who was destroying the Action shows…by doing what every one hated. He stopped at the top of the ramp, allowing the “KroW-Sucks” chants to get louder and louder. Levine just snapped a smile as he held his PIW World Title in the air….and spat upon the imitation Action Title. Four people would be fighting for that title…and in many people’s view it was an opportunity of a life time. But to Levine, those four men; Danger, Heimdal, Garvin and Zimmerman; were only fighting for a ten pound piece of shit. Action shit, that is. Don’t you love this segment?
