Austin, Texas, 27065 Record number: 32423-5543-432423-134 Patient #364-71-9762: Jay Edward Sylo Date: February 14th, 2003 Time: 10:00 am I’ve recorded that the patient has went through many traumatic situations, which has caused this nervous break down. As I write this I feel extremely sorry for him, but I know that deep down inside there lies other troubles. Recorded Statement #19347-049: “Life hasn’t been easy for me since I was five. I’m not sitting here trying to bitch and complain to you but you asked so I’ll tell you. Try witnessing your parents brains scattered across their white walls. It is, in fact, very disturbing but now, well now it just doesn’t bother me.” ---- It seems he’s became cold to human affection. The childhood was outlined with many foster families, none in which worked out. He shows minor signs of remorse though; maybe I can break through this hard shell of his and find out what makes him tick. This will be my biggest challenge to date. Patient #364-71-9762: Jay Edward Sylo Date: February 17th, 2003 Time: 4:00 pm It seems he holds bitter feelings toward a former girlfriend who cheated around on the patient. He holds an extreme hatred for the female and even goes as far as to blame her for the death of his grandmother. Recorded Statement #12364-869: ”I hate that bitch. My grandmother didn’t like her and I was blind with love…Fuck love. I know it was the stress of me dating Jade that did it…y’know killed my grandmother. Fucking bitch, I hope she rots in hell. Her and that dick fuck she cheated on me with.” ---- I then asked the patient to tell me about his teen years. He seemed normal enough except for the death of his parents. Recorded Statement #20758-375: “I was your typical guy I guess…I liked getting ass, I liked playing sports, I liked when girls looked at me. I pumped weight getting my muscles as big as possible because I liked the attention I got from people…it felt good. Now I realize it was all for nothing, people usually end up being nothing more than shit and friends? Fuck friends…there is no such thing as friends. I’ve never needed anybody or anything but me and that’s it,” ---- The patient shows bitter animosity towards the human race. I’m starting to figure it out though; He just wanted someone to love him. Patient #364-71-9762: Jay Edward Sylo Date: February 20th, 2003 Time: 9:00 am Today I wanted to go through his profession and that was, of course, wrestling. I asked him many questions about the “sport” as some would call it and his replies were very insightful. He told me of how he trained in the PWA and soon found a job with a man by the name of Jacob Yoder. I bold this name just incase it becomes of some importance, I shall do this with all names stated by me. Sylo was angered with the untimely closing of NTICW and spoke out against it, this of course angered the “higher up’s” Recorded Statement #57436-510: “Yeah I was pissed when NTICW closed. The head branch thought some funny business was going on but if you ask me I think they just wanted the talent. I wasn’t about to go work for them when they were killing off something I actually enjoyed. So I rebelled, I dyed my hair blue, I put in blue contacts, and I became from that day forward a monster. The Sylo you see in the ring and the Sylo you see before you are very much the same person,” ---- The patient went on to list that he went to a federation named Global Wrestling and won his first title there. He soon departed for what was known as PCW and became a champion there as well. Being on top doesn’t seem to affect him though, he seems like he’s never happy. Patient #364-71-9762: Jay Edward Sylo Date: February 23rd, 2003 Time: 2:00pm Note: This is the final session with said patient. In my final session I finally ventured to ask him what sent him over the edge, what made him come here. He told me of how he was in PIW and finally became a world champion, every wrestlers one goal. He told me of how PIW closed and of how Jonathan Enders offered him a job in aWc. Sylo took it and began work and after a run with top star Jamal Wilson it looked like Sylo would have a healthy future. Recorded Statement #91037-884 “I thought everything was finally going to be ok. I missed PIW, sure, the money was good and I was on top. No I don’t have the famous “Triple H” syndrome but you know, it was nice. Then I went to work a house show, no big deal really. They booked me against a new guy, it was supposed to be a squash and as I went to pile drive the kid he didn’t tuck…and I snapped his neck. Why did it send me over the edge? Because I’m tired of this kind of shit, I’m tired of it all being my fault…I didn’t want to kill that kid…hell he had a young wife and a kid for gods sake. I know what it’s like to grow up with out either parent…and I just didn’t want that for that kid,” ---- It appears that the patient finally shows human emotions. Maybe they aren’t as clear as many would like but they are there…deep down inside. I deem Jay Edward Sylo to be clinically sane. On a side note though I do feel he is emotionally insane but I do not feel it will conflict anymore with his profession or personal life. Best wishes to Mr. Sylo in Action!
