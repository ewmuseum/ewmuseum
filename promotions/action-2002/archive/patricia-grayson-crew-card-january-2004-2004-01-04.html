What this is Patricia Grayson’s card from the fed’s own Crew folder (referees, doctor and other staff), as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 4 January 2004. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is.
Patricia Grayson Real Name: Patricia Anne Grayson Also Known As: Pat, Trish, Trisha Date of Birth: The Fourteenth Day of the Second Month of the Eighty-Fourth Year in the Twentieth Century. Hometown: San Diego, California has been her home all her life. Height: Five feet, Five inches. Weight: One-Hundred Fifteen Pounds. Favorite Wrestler: Coral Avalon Least Favorite Wrestler: Keith Scott Zimmerman Favorite Trademark Move: The Spiritbreaker Attributes Intelligence: As a former student of San Diego State University, Trish is pretty intelligent. She's not your average blonde, that's for sure. Her desire was to major in communications and/or mathematics. She's also a bit of a logic geek. (She enjoys the logic puzzles.) So, yeah, she's pretty darn smart. Bump Taking: She's the daughter of a wrestler, and she trained for a couple of years to become one, but opted for the less painful job of officiating. But because of her training, she can bump. Not like Coral(which is why he's her favorite, plus his 'never die' attitude) or anything, but adequently enough. Because she's so thin, it won't take much to knock her down, but it'll take a big hit to keep her there. Counting Speed(out of 5 - which is the fastest): Trish is one of the slower counters in Action! Wrestling. On the scale of one to five, she'd be about at a two, which is very slow. But she's consistent. Her countouts and countdown(when both men are down after a double KO spot) are at the same speed. Leniency: She's stingy about the rules. She will enforce them to the highest extent of the law. She will count out you out. She will call for the DQ if you're cheating. She makes her five counts. She's fair, if you count fair as going by the rulebook, but you probably won't like it. Ease of Retaliation: She's very feisty, but she doesn't really have the strength to fight back. She will push back though, all the while calling for the bell for the DQ. Eye Sight: Perfect 20/20 eye sight. She does not miss a single thing. There's no cheating on her. She won't miss those false tags. So, make sure you have about twenty people to distract her(she isn't easily distracted either, if you don't get in the ring, she'll ignore you), if you want to try and get away with something. Biography Patricia Grayson is the daughter of a former professional wrestler and current referee. Since at very early age, she always wanted to get into the business. Despite the fact that it was a business that split her parents, apart, it was in her, fueling her very existence. That's why she answered the open call of Joe Schmidt for young referees, skipping out of San Diego State University between her freshman and sophomore years. She had been working the indy circuit in the southern California area, getting work where she could and picking up tips and advice from all over. But her main consultant was her father, who she calls daily. Now, as the newest referee for Action! Wrestling, the blue-eyed, short-haired blonde bombshell is ready to prove that women can do just as well as the men... possibly even better. She's also going through some very real, very big personal changes. Long had she thought she would end up marrying some big muscle-bound man, but ended up falling in love - so madly so - with a woman; E.M. Pleh. © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
