What this is Edguardo’s card from the fed’s own roster page, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 22 November 2003. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Edguardo Birth Name: Edguardo Rodrigo Guiterrez Also Known As: Beaner, spick, bean dip, la imigra...unlike Diablo, Edguardo doesn't care because he lacks the mental capacity. Date of Birth: 5-12-1982 Age: 21 Marital Status: If he finds something to hump...that's his marital status. Hometown: Tijuana, Mexico Height: 5'10' Weight: 205 Hair Color: Puce Eye Color: Brown Blood Type: A positive Status: Heel Personality: Either crazy, or pissed-off much like his brother. It all depends on how his head gets hit. Theme Music: 'Violent Mood Swings' by Spineshank Wrestling Style Trained By: The television. Detailed Style: Lucha Libre/whatever Strengths: Strong for his size, always gives full effort Weaknesses: Crazy, can't focus on matches all the time Signature Moves: Mexican Fortune Cookie -- Hangman's neckbreaker to Evenflow DDT (Edguardo sets up the hangman's neckbreaker, but snaps their head back and drops them into the DDT. This move is known to cause whiplash of the type most commonly seen in car crashes.) Head humping -- Head humping (This is the most brutal submission of all time. BEWARE.) The 6T9 -- catapult leg drop ring rope clothesline (In mockery/homage to Rey Mysterio, Edguardo coined this move. He sets it up similar to the 619 with a drop toe hold onto the ropes, then he bounces off the ropes and springs off the top rope, dropping a leg on their neck. Pronounced 'sixty-nine'.) Trademark Name: The Spick Kick Trademark Move: Bicycle Enziguri kick Injuries Current Injuries: None. Nagging Injuries: Some bizarre form of mental retardation that causes him to act normally when you hit him on the head. History Years Pro: 3 Manager: The Lesbian Colony (think about the Ho Train with hot, bi-curious Lesbians) Allies: Diablo, Daniel Phillips Nemesis: Whoever Diablo or Daniel tells him to attack Current Team: Those Damned Mexicans (with Diablo) Past Teams: The Knights of the Landfill (with Diablo and Daniel Phillips) Action Wrestling Title History: None Action Wrestling Award History: None Other Federations: IWO Other Federation Awards: Attributes Intelligence: 3 Strength: 8 Toughness: 10 Speed: 8 Endurance: 9 Courage: 9 Charisma: 10 Skill: 9 Biography (For more information, see Diablo's biography.) Edguardo was born somewhat messed-up. But nobody has been able to figure out why... He is slavishly loyal to Diablo, but he has the mind of a 12-year-old. As a result, Diablo has to think for Edguardo most often. Edguardo's two main interests are sex and sex. This is why he humps stuff and is managed by a colony of Lesbians. But strangely, when Edguardo goes into a wrestling match, he becomes a sometimes unstoppable, always amusing force. Whatever causes Edguardo to be messed-up has no effect on his in-ring abilities. EDGUARDO'S PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION Edguardo has puce-dyed dreadlocks. He wears Hanson Brothers-like glasses, just because (think about the Dudleys). He always wears a white 'Insufficient Memory At This Time' T-shirt with light blue shorts, green argyle knee socks and brown leather cowboy boots. Off Stage Information Name: Marc S. AOL Instant Messenger Screen Name: Coming soon Interests: Look at Diablo's profile because I'm too lazy to add it right now. Favorite Pro Wrestler: See above.
