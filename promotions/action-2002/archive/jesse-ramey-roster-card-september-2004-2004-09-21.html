The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Jesse Ramey Birth Name: Jesse Tyler Ramey Also Known As: 'Bad Attitude', 'Mr. Action!' Date of Birth: January 6th, 1976 Age: 28 Marital Status: Single Hometown: Harts, West Virginia Height: 5'11' Weight: 205lbs. Hair Color: Brown Eye Color: Baby Blue Blood Type: O+ Status: Neutral Personality: Jesse is a true veteran of the sport and is an entertainer through and through, no matter what type of mood he is in. He vary seldom loses sight as to the reason he has been in the business for as long as he has, the Fans. He doesn't take kindly to newcomers of the business who automatically think they are hot merchandising or those that flagrantly disrespect the fans. Though he may be cocky from time to time, the fans don’t mind as they have grown accustom to his ways over the many years that have seen him perform, which makes him a very big fan favorite, and yet clearly makes him hated by a majority of the roster. Theme Music: 'Last Words' by Thousand Foot Krutch Wrestling Style Trained By: (Brawling, Psychology, Stealth, and Stamina Training) July, 1986-December, 1990 Juvenile Military Boot Camp, Drill Instructor Major Charles Alexander III; (Basic Professional Wrestling Training) January, 1991- March, 1992: Killer Kowalski's Pro Wrestling School; (Martial Arts Training) April, 1992- March 1993:Tiger Mask in Japan during Jesse's short stint in Japan; (Submission and Technical Training) April, 1993- July, 1994: Stu Hart in the Dungeon Detailed Style: Hardcore High-Flyer Strengths: Quick attacks and Submission moves Weaknesses: The fact that he isn't as big as everyone else has always placed him in a very negative roll when it comes to matching up strength with most of anyone. Signature Moves: Set-up Move: The Hangtime (Tornado DDT from any position) Trademark Moves: Touching the Sky (Split-Legged Moonsault), Touchdown (Rolling Thunder), Extreme Measures (Jump Spinning Heel Kick to the Face), Culture Development (Asai Moonsault). Aerial Moves: Flying Clothesline, Flying Headbutt, Guillotine Leg Drop, Shooting Star Press, 450 Splash, Corkscrew Plancha, Cross Body Block, Flying DDT, Frog Splash, Swanton Bomb, Skytwister Press. Submission Moves: Achilles Tendon Hold, Ankle Lock, Chicken Wing, Crossface, Dragon Sleeper, Figure Four Leg Lock, Greco-Roman Arm Lock, Headlock, Indian Death Lock, Nerve Hold, Reverse Chinlock, Rings of Saturn, Sharpshooter, Side Headlock, Sleeper Hold, STF, Texas Cloverleaf. Other Common Moves: Arm Drag, Arm Scissors, Baseball Slide to the Outside, Running Bulldog, DDT, Double-Arm DDT, Drop Toe Hold, Dragon Screw Leg Whip, Eye Gouge, Facebuster, Headscissors, Hip Toss, Insiguri Kick, Japanese Arm Drag, Leg Sweep, Monkey Flip, Neck Breaker, Reverse Neck Breaker, Running Clothesline, Running Senton (Back Splash), Savat Kick, Short Arm Scissors, Side Kick, Side Russian Leg Sweep, Snap DDT, Snapmare, Snap Suplex, Spinning Toe Hold, Standing Drop Kick, Superkick, Swinging Neck Breaker, Thrust Kick, Tornado Punch. Trademark Name: The Attitude Adjustment Trademark Move: Elevated Boston Crab w/ Right Knee In Opponents Back Injuries Current Injuries: Bruised Right Eye; will heal fully in about four weeks. (Sometime around the middle of February it shouldn't be noticable at all) Nagging Injuries: None History Years Pro: 12 Manager: Previous: Kid Lightning (UWL), Michelle Marie-Ramey (CCW,OSW), Chass Loraine (OSW), Fubu (OSW); Current: None Allies: Previous: Blackheart (UWL), Fubu (UWL,EWF,CWA,JEW), Kurt Ragnarock (JEW), Cianan (CCW), Asa Fountain (FSW); Current: None Nemesis: Previous: Blayze (CCW), Cianan (CCW), Sebastian Toomes (OSW), Steven Lee (PURE); Current: Chad Villain Current Team: none Past Teams: Bad Fortitude (w/ Asa Fountain) in Five Star Wrestling Action Wrestling Title History: none Action Wrestling Award History: none Other Federations: UWL, EWF, CWA, JEW, CCW, OSW, FSW, PURE, PWC, tA, thReat Other Federation Awards: UWL Hall of Fame Inductee, UWL Tag Team Champion (w/ Blackheart), UWL Television Champion (First to win and last to hold) (x 2 and Longest Championship Reign), First EWF Hardcore Champion, CWA Tag Team Champion (w/ Klown), CWA Hardcore Champion (x 2), CWA World Heavyweight Champion, JEW Cruiserweight Champion, JEW USA Champion, CCW Independent Champion, CCW Lightweight Champion, FSW Tag Team Champion (w/ Asa Fountain), First and Only PURE Heavyweight Champion. Other Federation Backstage Positions: EWF Owner and CEO, CCW Public Relations Advisor, Owner and Head Trainer (When not signed to a Written Wrestling Contract) of Future Wrestling Talents based out of Chicago. Attributes Intelligence: 9 Strength: 4 Toughness: 8 Speed: 10 Endurance: 10 Courage: 9 Charisma: 9 Skill: 10 Biography One man can only live one life, or at least that is what you have been told all of your life, but the truth be told this is the tale of a man that has been made to live many in a very short time. From his humble begins as a child on into his haunting present. His story is not for the weak or faint at heart. Rittled with memories of the death of his parents, women, drugs, money, the people who he thought he could once call friend, and the only real love of his life taken from him. A career in professional wrestling that has been no accomplishment to him, as close to five of his twelve year career was devoted to having the management of whatever federation he was in holding him down. The only places he was safe was overseas and even there he had his enemies in the community itself. His decision rested upon one of two things, coming back to the states and hoping to find a promotion that wasn’t ran by underhanded monopoly or to stay overseas and risk his life wrestling in communities that were nothing short of anti-American. Fate, is something that comes swiftly, and now, the fate of this still young man’s career rests in the hands of Action Wrestling.
