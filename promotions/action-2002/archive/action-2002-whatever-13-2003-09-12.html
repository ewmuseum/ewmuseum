Jeff Garvin stood, a scowl accompanying a steady glare that traveled through the stands. In his right hand he held a water bottle, in the other, a sack... black; velvet; weighed down by his contents. He walked to the ring, climbed inside, and collected a microphone from Pat Hoskins. He then sat the bag down at his feet, making sure the opening was not in plain view as to give away his surprise prematurely. The microphone was lifted to his mouth. He stared out amongst the crowd for a moment, they continued to boo. He stared blankly, scratched the area above the three inch long bandage that covered his forehead. "I don't get it," he shook his head, "I don't understand what it is I have done that calls for such disrespect. I defeated Frank Heimdal clean for this championship belt and you act as if I'm the devil. News flash, this is professional wrestling! You win some, you lose some. It just so happens that I win when it counts, while Frank, he'll pick up a victory here and there, the marks will praise him and call him as the next huge draw in this business." Garvin laughed at the notion. Frank? A huge draw? Frank couldn't draw a grizzly bear if he spread honey over his testicles and wandered out into the forest. Him? Responsible for leading the promotion? It was laughable at best. Heimdal had far too many personal issues going on in his life. He simply wasn't the man for the job. Garvin smiled, and continued from where he had left off. "Frank, he's a damn good wrestler, I'll give him that. He took me to a place that I had never been before. He busted me open, he drained the life from me, and for a moment, I actually doubted my own abilities. But then it hit me. You're Jeff Garvin. If you begin to doubt yourself, then others will too. So what I did was, I bled that negativity from my wounds and I proved once again that I'm the best wrestler in the word today." He unbuckled the silver-plated championship belt and held it in the air, spitting his success in the faces of all his detractors. "This is what it's all about. Proof of just how good I am." The smile passed, a stoic gaze remained. Professional, but at the same time, angry. "But, as I watch my reflection stare back at me in its polished exterior, I feel as if this championship is below me." Oh, that did it. The World Arena was filled to the rafters with loyal Action! Wrestling fans that had followed it since its debut in March of 2002, and here he was, beaming with a smug grin, stating that the very championship that meant so much to all of the boys in the back, was BELOW him? Talk about arrogance. "As I stare into my reflection, I see those that have held this title before me. Joey Malone. Sean Cylear. Syphon Fission. Christopher Fox." Garvin paused for a second, cringed, then added one final name to the list. "LLB." The crowd popped instantly. A chant quickly replaced their cheering as his name pounded inside Garvin's head, bringing him to clench his eyes shut and think of said person being put their a giant meat grinder. Ah, all better. "Please, don't you EVER mention his name in my presence, again. I am the only one who may do so, and believe me when I say this, that will be a very rare occurance."
