Tyler Davidson Birth Name: Tyler Davidson Also Known As: The Future, The Career Assassin, The Next Generation Date of Birth: March 11th, 1978 Age: 25 Marital Status: Single Hometown: New York City, New York Height: 6'3 Weight: 219 Hair Color: Black Eye Color: Blue Blood Type: A+ Status: Neutral Personality: Tyler was until recently one of the most popular faces in the wrestling business. Always one to have an attitude and ego the death of his longtime girlfriend had been enough to push him over the edge. He returned with a much sharper edge and a mission to make everyone feel the pain which he has to live every day with. It’s been well over a year since that time and the hatred that has burnt within Tyler has not disappeared in the least and has apparently begun to consume the sanity of Tyler as he has over the past several months begun to show some signs of possible multiple personality disorder. He has been seen on numerous occasions talking and even arguing with himself over some of the choices he has made in recent months. One side of the personalities seems to have the personality traits of the Tyler of old, the Tyler that actually cared about the fans and the business while the other side is the Tyler of present, the man who hates the people and everything that they represent for this business. Theme Music: 'Unstable' By Adema Wrestling Style Trained By: Scott Daniels and Daniel Somers Detailed Style: Tyler is quite an intellectual wrestler and depending on the opponent he will start the match off in one of two ways. If he is severely overpowered Tyler will go straight into flight mode and begin using his incredible speed and agility to try and stay one step ahead of his opponent. Otherwise he will focus his attack on a specific body part of his adversary and work to wear his opponent down piece by piece. Strengths: Incredible Agility and Speed, Highly intellectual, always has the constant presence of Daniel Somers ringside Weaknesses: Tends to be reckless in the ring and can often cost himself the match, lack of strength and size Signature Moves: Trademarks: Time Spike: Cradle Piledriver Lockdown: Sharpshooter Flashback: Dragon-Rana Five Star Slam: Michinoku Driver Davidson Plex: Top Rope German Suplex The Assassination: Superkick Beyond Belief: Rolling German Suplex followed immediately by a Dragon Suplex and the bridge. Submissions: Armbar Dragon Sleeper Figure Four Aerial: Hurricanrana Missile Dropkick Flying Cross Body Phoenix Splash Shooting Star Press Suplexes: Brainbuster Northern Lights Suplex Snap Suplex Multiple German Suplexes Dragon Suplex High Angle Back Suplex Strikes: Series of chops Kicks to the head and midsection Spinning heel kick Shining Wizard(running kick to the temple) Power Moves: Liger Bomb Emerald Fusion Spinebuster Bomb Reverse DDT Sitout Powerbomb Sitout Tombstone Fire Thunder Driver Trademark Name: The Future Shock - Sanity's Imperfection Trademark Move: Future Shock: Crippler Crossface - Sanity's Imperfection: Double Powerbomb Followed by an elevated boston crab Injuries Current Injuries: None Nagging Injuries: None History Years Pro: 4 Manager: Daniel Somers Allies: None Nemesis: Phil Atken, Sergey Kovhachek Current Team: None Past Teams: High-Intensity Carnage(w/De'Andre Walker) Action Wrestling Title History: None Action Wrestling Award History: None Other Federations: thReat, PRIME, FSW, IOW, eVolution, MCW, AWF, JEW3, $W, CCW, WCCW, WWCF Other Federation Awards: IOW Extreme Champion, IOW Cruiserweight Champion, thReat Cruiserweight Champion, WWCF World Champion, WWCF Rookie Champion, WCCW Cruiserweight Champion, WCCW World Champion, AWF Digital Champion, MCW American Champion Attributes Intelligence: 9 Strength: 4 Toughness: 7 Speed: 9 Endurance: 9 Courage: 6 Charisma: 7 Skill: 7 Biography Tyler was born March 11th, 1978 to Hunter and Marie Davidson. Both parents had worked for many years for a prestigious law firm in New York City and as a result found themselves with an abundance of money to say the least which they used to ensure that they and their children could have the best possible life growing up. It was with that money that the purchase of what would later become known as the Davidson Estate in Danbury, Connecticut was made and it was there that Tyler spent the majority of his youth growing up under the supervision of nannies and relatives while both his parents continued on with their careers just a brief drive away in New York City. For the majority of his early childhood Tyler led a rather uneventful life which he saw little actual attention from either of his parents but they never hesitated to make up for their absence by showering him with gifts and of course the best education that their money could buy for him as well as it was their hope that their son would one day follow in their footsteps and become a lawyer as well. Things never did work out very well for Tyler even at such a young age however. By the time he was ten Tyler had found out first hand what it felt like to lose someone that he cared about as his mother had secretly been diagnosed with breast cancer a year earlier. She had fought valiantly against the disease for a year in secret but after passing out during dinner at home one night there was no way she could continue to hide it. Her husband and Tyler quickly rushed her to the hospital that night and it was then that they found out about the diagnosis but by that time it was already to late for them to help her as she never again left the hospital alive. It was the first time that Tyler had felt any kind of loss and he had little notion as how to deal with it especially when his father had done his best to keep up a strong front for his son. It was with that image that Tyler began to really look up to his father as a truly strong man and spent the majority of his remaining childhood trying to find a way to truly impress his father all up until high school. It was in high school that Tyler threw himself into various athletics while also picking up the desire to break away from that which his father had wanted for him. At first his true interest laid in baseball and football and for his first two years of high school he had great years becoming one of the top players in the state in both sports. In his junior year of High School Tyler got his first taste of the wrestling world when his friends forced him into trying out for the team. To Tyler’s surprise he wound up making the varsity wrestling team. He immediately began training harder for the wrestling competitions than he did baseball and football and after a few weeks he quickly began to fall in love with the sport. He continued on the wrestling team for his remaining two years of High School in the meanwhile to the chagrin of his parents he dropped his baseball and football careers along the way. After his graduation from High School Tyler managed to convince his parents to put him through wrestling school in South Carolina while he also attended college there at Coastal Carolina University. Tyler eventually packed his bags and headed off for the sunny beaches of South Carolina where his life would take a totally new turn. While training at the school this is where he met the man that would go on to have the biggest impact in his so far short career. That man would be “Suicidal” Daniel Somers, a man who would later on go on to win the World Heavyweight title on four occasions. He was about two years ahead of Tyler at this point in time but the two sparked up a very strong friendship that carried on through the years. In his remaining time there Somers would often work extra with Tyler trying to give him an extra edge over the rest of the students. By the time Somers graduated Tyler was far ahead of the rest of his class and was already receiving offers from small promotions around the region to begin competing but with his promise to his parents of remaining in school he opted to turn them down and continues his training there. During his schooling at Coastal Carolina he also met another person that would influence much of his life. That person would become in time his girlfriend Heather. The two struck up a friendship when sharing a journalism class together, which in time blossomed into more. Heather also from the Connecticut area has remained with Tyler for the past three years and now continues to support him in his quest to become one of the best in the business today. After college Tyler made his debut wrestling debut. The owner of the fed he was part of at the time took a look at his background and upbringing and decided that it would be best for Tyler to play the role of a rich snob. It was a role that he didn't fill very well and resulted in him picking up a streak of losses. Things continued this way for Tyler until the return of his friend and trainer Daniel Somers. Somers came back into the promotion for the second time and joined with Tyler helping to break him free of the horrid gimmick and allowing him to be himself again. Since then Tyler has traveled the country and Japan training himself to be the best wrestler that can be. At the age of 24 Tyler made his debut for $oldout Wrestling and rapidly propelled himself into one of the companies top stars. Just as he seemed on the verge of capturing the Global Title however tragedy struck. While in California preparing for his big match his girlfriend Heather, left behind in New York City with the wife of Daniel Somers, was killed. With so much on his mind Tyler would come up short in his match and depart shortly after with the help of Victor Blood and T3 assaulting him and stuffing him in the back of a car. That was the last Tyler had been seen by anyone. Until now. Now he is back but as a much different man than he once was. Taking his place atop his father's company of Davidson Enterprises, it was Tyler who has since financed some of the newer financial acquisitions of Daniel Somers. Since that time Tyler has fluctuated quite a bit as a person and a performer having stints in Five Star Wrestling, Icons of Wrestling, and thReat and now he finally finds himself in Action Wrestling. A place where he hopes to at last find some peace of mind and a place to last. Off Stage Information Name: Daniel S.
