Jamal Wilson Biography Nicknames The Hardcore Homo Hometown San Francisco, California Birthday March 8, 1980 Height 5'11 Weight 222 Biography Jamal Wilson was born on March the 8th, 1980. He was born in a suburb of Toronto, Ontario Canada, called Scarborough. He was born to Jordan Wilson, and his wife Jennifer Wilson. To say Jamal had everything a boy could want would be an understatement. His parents owned a chain of large black diners through the province of Ontario, and they were raking in the big bucks. Jamal grew up in the mainly white area of Scarborough. He lived in one of the biggest houses in his area, and he was one of the most respected students in the area. Though Jamal was respected in the area, it didn’t mean that he was immune to being made fun of. The area was mainly white, and the fact that Jamal was an African American, with parents who grew up in an age where most African Americans weren’t as well off as they were now, gave the kids ample ammunition when it came to telling jokes. Also, Jamal was different than other boys for the fact that he enjoyed playing with Barbie’s. While all the other kids were out playing baseball, football, and basketball, he was in his house, cooped up in his bedroom playing with the most famous dolls on the planet. His parents really didn’t want to discourage him when it came to the Barbie’s, because they didn’t want to anger him. They thought that if they brought it up, maybe he would feel as if there was more stress on him to be perfect. Jamal excelled in school, he brought home straight A's every year of his schooling life. He got every honor that a student could get. His favorite subject was history. He loved reading about the Civil Rights battles of the sixties. He was the type of person who would help the old lady across the street, the type of person who would stay after school to help the teacher tape banners up on the walls for a school concert. Many of the students would make fun of him for stuff like that. Calling him as suck up, and a goody two shoes. Jamal always acted as if when kids said things like that, it didn’t bother him. When in reality, everyday he would go home and cry himself to sleep. He had a group of friends who treated him like he was a God. But they were nerds. Jamal’s life was contradicting itself. And Jamal wasn't happy. Something was missing. His parents loved him, his friends loved him, and the teachers loved him. But Jamal just couldn't place his finger on the thing that was missing. Universities heavily recruited Jamal across North America, he was an exceptionally good basketball, and the Universities wanted him for their teams. Jamal settled on the University of Memphis. A school known for it's tremendous Division 1 basketball program. Jamal also wrestled in high school. A bit of amateur wrestling, he wasn't really too good at it, but he thought it was fun, and something that he wouldn't mind doing later on in life. When Jamal went to University, things changed. His attitude was completely different from that it had been before. On the first Friday night at his University, Jamal decided to go out clubbing with his roommates. After hanging around the bar for a few minutes, and after drinking a few Long Island Ice Teas, a female approached Jamal. She asked Jamal to dance, and Jamal verbally thrashed her because he felt that her clothing wasn’t matching, and the fact that she would even think about trying to dance with him dressed as she was, made his stomach churn. Where did that come from, Jamal thought to himself a moment after telling her off. Only gay guys care about their clothes like that…. The next day, Jamal cut class for the first time in his entire life. He decided not to go to school so he could spend the day in front of his television, watching day time talk shows and soap operas. Very feminine, ain’t it? The next day Jamal went out and got his nails done for the first time. He liked the feeling of being pampered and treated like a woman normally would have been treated. Jamal began following into this pattern/trend. He was missing school days left and right and he wasn’t happy. He knew that no matter how good an education he got, he wouldn’t love life until he found a job that he would love doing. After seeing a professional wrestler on his television screen that got his juices flowing (So to speak), Jamal decided that he HAD to become a wrestler. This is when he became more and more infatuated with men. Jamal scrounged up the $2,000 it took to get training, which really wasn’t much for him, because his parents are loaded. After paying Ricky Morton for the training, Jamal went through some intense beatings, and he learned a lot about himself. First off, Jamal decided that he should drop out of University. It was too detrimental to his budding professional wrestling career. After training for a good year and a half, Jamal graduated the school and began taking Indy bookings. On the night of his professional wrestling debut, Jamal was supposed to be in a battle royal. The fed was adamant about being old school, so they wanted all of their wrestlers in the ring at the same time, they didn’t want it to be like the WWF(E) with somebody running in every 90 seconds. But the problem was the ring was old and shoddy, and there was no way in hell that it was going to hold the 20 wrestlers that were supposed to be in the match. So the bookers decided to just pair everybody from the battle royal up with a random partner, and stick ‘em in the ring with another random pairing, and have some mayhem. As luck would have it, the gay, Jewish, African American, was drawn to be the partner of the KKK member. This KKK member’s name was Jack Otto. He was your stereotypical redneck who didn’t know all that much, but what he did know was that he didn’t like Jamal Wilson. In fact, he knew that he LOATHED Jamal Wilson. After wrestling as a tag team that night, the bookers were surprised at the reaction the crowd gave them, and how they worked together in the ring, and they decided to pair them together. This Indy was run as a part of the school, and they handled the Indy bookings for all of the wrestlers that they trained, and they decided to bill Jamal and Jack as a tag team. They were called, The Hate Brigade. After a few months of toiling in the independent scene, Jack and Jamal were introduced to a 35 year old Japanese man, who happened to be an Islamic Militant who followed Allah as best he could. He became their manager, completing the odd couple. After sending their video tapes to every major company they could find, they were finally accepted into Christian Keaton’s Universal Wrestling. Jamal Wilson and Jack Otto were new to the tag team division, and got themselves over with the fans as Jamal made appearances on Keaton’s radio show, regularly. Jamal and Jack Otto got themselves embroiled in a violent feud with the fWo (Funky World Order) and fought valiantly, even after being attacked from behind and spray painted. Through Keaton’s radio show, Jamal began to make friends with some PIW officials. And after appearing on Vinny Nerdstrom’s radio show, Jamal was granted the opportunity to become Jim Johnson’s personal assistant. After showing up at Massacre 3, Jamal was given the job, and a contract with the Pinnacle Of Insane Wrestling as the personal assistant of the owner, Jim Johnson. Jamal made his wrestling debut in the PIW when he pinned current Action! Carnage Champion, The Joker, with the help of his two buddies, Iceman and Blast. Jamal signed with Action! after he and his partner, Chino Hernandez, sent their demo tapes to Reed Young, whom apparently loved it. Jamal now resides in San Francisco, California.
