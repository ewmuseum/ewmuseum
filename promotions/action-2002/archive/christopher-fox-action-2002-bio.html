Christopher Fox Biography Nicknames 'Kid' Hometown Ottawa, Ontario, Canada Birthday December 19th, 1979 Height 6' 1'' Weight 218 Fed Titles Action Wrestling Champion x1 Fed Awards Top Face 2002-2003 Biography Throughout the history of wrestling no one has pushed the envelope like Christopher Fox has. He doesn’t take no for an answer, bottom line. Well, maybe it’s because he actually doesn’t understand what the word no means, but that’s for another time. Fox pretty much had it lucky from the start. Basically you’d have to be if you were Chris Fox! No one in their right mind would want to train this piece of work. Seeing as Fox and LLB were actual cousins (oh it’s true), Chris got ticket after ticket to PIW events. And the more Fox went to the events, the more Fox would rant and rave about how LLB needed a partner. You have to understand, Fox REALLY liked LLB. Therefore, following around one of the most famous wrestlers for literally months and months and months and months (bored yet?), Fox got his chance to wrestle in some matches after numerous begs to Roland himself! And from that point on, Chris would embark on the greatest story known to man - himself (yeah right). The very day Fox started out he was already popular, because like I said, Chris pushed the envelope. Not only in his matches but in his character too. You see no one has ever encountered someone with such a “persona”. He had something not too many people held: charisma. And lots of it too. Lets not forget the courage either, but maybe it’s not courage after all, just the fact he’s too stupid he WOULD fight a killer whale if one came. Regardless, Fox was almost like a pioneer, except he said he was Indian. Whatever. Moving on...(you sleeping yet?) Chris would eventually build up to what he is today. A milkshake guzzling, happy-go-lucky, Pokemon loving, Mentos eating, fun in the sun...... child. Quick Fact: People say Fox is the most used household name across North America while on the subject of Wrestling. We doubt this, but will go along with it anyway. Fox has held various titles but he says it doesn’t matter what has happened in the past, it's what will happen down the road! So people out there in playtime land, turn on your Action Box and get ready for Chris Fox again.... BIG TIME! Transmission On- Out.
