What this is Conrad Ramsey’s card from the fed’s own roster page, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 28 December 2003. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Conrad Ramsey Birth Name: Conrad (Insert Random Name That Starts With 'D') Ramsey. Also Known As: Does he look like Jennifer Garner to you? Date of Birth: Seventh Day of the Twelfth Month of the Forty-First Year in the Twentieth Century. Age: Sixty-One. Marital Status: Conrad is a widower after Samantha Dox passed away. Hometown: He currently resides in Leitchfield, Kentucky. He was born in Paintsville, Kentucky but spent the majority of his life in Denver, Colorado. Height: Six feet, One inch. Weight: Two-Hundred Thirty-Seven Pounds. Hair Color: Gray and White. Eye Color: Hazel. Blood Type: AB. Status: Face Personality: Conrad is a nice guy, who will talk to almost anyone. He's a grumpy old man at times, but generally doesn't burden you with that attitude. Is a big reader and player of the chess. Theme Music: For singles competition, Ramsey uses 'Tomorrow' by SR-71. When he teams up with Bradley Duncan, he opts for Agent Provocateur's 'Red Tape'. (If he's wrestling a singles match now, he will still use the tag theme.) Wrestling Style Trained By: Baxter Moline and Bradley Duncan. Detailed Style: Conrad Ramsey possesses only the basic of wrestling knowledge, when it comes to his moveset. All the submission holds that he knows were taught to him by Bradley Duncan. His basic style, however, is very much like Ric Flair's. There isn't anything fancy or flashy about it. His style is more based around him selling as much as possible for his opponent and making well-timed comebacks. Chops and leg work is the basis of his attack. Strengths: His ability to take a beating and keep coming back. His ability to work over the leg. He's become almost a master at it. And most importantly: his heart. Weaknesses: His old age and various injuries(arm, knee, ribs, head) that slow him down a tad in the ring. Lack of real experience despite being a long-time pro. His stamina is a bit of a problem as well. Match goes for too long and he'll suffer from it. Signature Moves: These are just a handful of Conrad's most potent of moves. For the rest of his moveset, please wander on over to the mighty MWA. Trust me, it'll be worth the trip. Knife-Edged Chops, are his main weapon. With his dislike for punching (not that he won't do it) he opts for the chest-caving chops. No one does them harder. Figure Four Leg Lock, isn't used as often due to the pain it causes in his own knee when he does it. But a viable finisher. Ankle Lock, is a very viable finisher for him. Can lock it on at any time, and uses all the variations on it. Please check the aid for its usage, when writing a match. Dragon Screw Leg Whip, quick and effective knee-hurting maneuver. Can even end matches with it. Hurricanrana, the most rare move and out-of-place looking in his entire moveset. But it's a nice counter move that he'll bust out only when he's desperate. Can also use the top rope version, although that takes a serious toll on his body. Trademark Name: Achilles Tendon Hold. Trademark Move: Achilles Tendon Hold. Injuries Current Injuries: His soft skull. Constantly having headaches. He's very easy concussed. Nagging Injuries: He has tender ribs, which bruise far too easily, and also has a held-together-by-a-thread left knee. It's partially protected by a brace, but it isn't that big of a help. History Years Pro: Twenty. Manager: He doesn't have one. Allies: Conrad is friends with Max Danger, Jessica Danger, Coral Avalon, Bradley Duncan, Julius Arcade, and Frank Heimdal. Nemesis: Oddly, he doesn't have too many enemies. Wrestling 101 would probably be about the only group of people he would consider as such. Donell Arcade, however, is fasting moving up the list. Current Team: Honor & Glory with Bradley Duncan. Past Teams: Canadian Violence Connection and B³, both with Max Danger. Action Wrestling Title History: Bantam Championship for 59 days. Action Wrestling Award History: None. Other Federations: Spinebuster Wrestling for 1 show. Other Federation Awards: Spinebuster World Title which he held from July 19th, 2003, until the company closed. Attributes Intelligence: Eight. Strength: Five. Toughness: Eight. Speed: Two. Endurance: Six. Courage: Ten. Charisma: Eight. Skill: Seven. Biography C O N Running gags. In literature, film, and television, some characters, directors, writers or whatever have running gags in all their work. That's what I'm going to have with Conrad. There will be three running gags; 1) He will go Bruce Lee on your ass. If he's pushed just a little too far. If he's just sick and tired of having his ass handed to him. And if he's just damn frustrated with things, he will snap. The spirit and mind of the legendary movie martial artist will converge within Conrad, thus UNLEASHING THE TIGER! 2) Each and every match from here on out, Ramsey will attempt a kip up. The operative word there being 'attempt'. See, he's not going to hit it. Not for a long time anyway. He will try and try and try, but continue to just fall right back down onto the mat. But one day, in a galaxy far, far away... er, sorry. He will nail it one of these days. But who knows when. Just pray to whatever deity you believe in that it doesn't happen against you. 3) The final running gag will be Conrad's middle name. For the life of me, I cannot remember what I originally gave him. I know it started with a 'D', since all my characters usually have one of their names starting with that letter. I assumed it was Darryl for the longest time and then changed it to David when I couldn't think of it. So, from now on, he just won't know his middle name. Every time the subject is brought up it will be a different name, starting with the letter 'D'. A D R A M S E Y Off Stage Information Name: Michael D.
