LLB Biography Nicknames 'The Law' Hometown Vineland, New Jersey Birthday May 27th Height 5' 11'' Weight 229 Fed Titles Action! Wrestling Champion. Biography Authority. These days it’s about power, right, and the authority over another. The power to give and the power to take away, which can be applied in two different forms: for or against. Born under the identity of Roland Miles Ermann, on May 27th, 197?, around the 10:31 minute mark, son to Margaret and Russell Ermann, a club-footed child was molded into this World. And with all of the bones in his feet in a big puzzle, instant surgery took place after birth. Roland grew up a different life. Due to his clubfeet he couldn’t walk until age three, nor run until age seven. He also had to wear leg braces for more than half the day. So, what would you do with a kid like that? Bring him outside, or keep him inside? Would you want your kid to realize he’s different from all the others too? Mr. and Mrs. Ermann’s answer to this was, -authority in hand,- to keep him indoors, closed and locked away from all other viewing eyes until surgery could corrected his problem as much as possible. Good move at first, bad after. The bottom line was they went a little too far... Along age three came a Brother to Roland, and in that: Raine. Age five, came Sister: Madison. As a kid, life for Roland was really easy... although on the first Wednesday of every forth Month a ‘Sleeping Man’ would put him asleep, and when he’d wake up a cast on each leg appeared. Mr. and Mrs. Erman told Roland it was just protection, since his feet were so prefect they needed to be kept that way. Again, authority they did have over him at that time, and he had no way of knowing what the real reason was. Home Schooled until age nine, in that way of life he was prefect, and although he never really understood why his Brother and Sister disappeared during his Home Schooled hours, he was still very happy... well, until he was ten, until his Parents’ brought Roland to a Public School. Their reason for this was that around this time Roland was a lot more functional. Surgery fixed up most of the problems in walking normally. Plus, Roland could run a little faster (even though club foot was still noticeable while doing so). Public School was fair. The people that didn’t know Roland laughed at him while he ran and sometimes walked. Roland wasn’t really sure of their motive, but those that were his true friends didn’t bother poking fun at him anyway, which was all that mattered. Around the age of fourteen was the date he was actually, and finally told by his Parents’. And to say Roland took it well wouldn’t have been right. He was mad he was never told beforehand. After all this was his body. He had the right to know... and still kept pondering why his feet turned slightly in, why the people laughed... why the people stared... it took time, but Roland did end up accepting what his Parents’ did in the end, basically he forced himself too, assuming they had only kept the truth from him for the better all that time. Roland also took his problem as a mountain to jump over. If his Parents’ wanted to hide the facts from him because they thought he couldn’t handle it, he’d be sure to prove them wrong. He could handle it; he would handle it. When it came down to the academics for Roland in public school he was very smart and accomplished a lot of things. He had to be one of the top in his Grade Eight Class; he was running for Valedictorian after all, only to be beaten out by one person though, in a vote of the Class Roland swore had to have been rigged. Rowe (nickname) was a popular kid no doubt, a lot of people did like him and his fearless attitude, but when he got voted out for a Girl (which was okay), the thing was that she happened to be the Teachers’ kid. Weird. None the less, Roland took the loss. There was no way he was going to fight the Teachers’, as they had more power and authority over him, and Roland didn’t even know for sure the vote was rigged, but it seemed to fit. It just seemed to. Sports also came into play for Roland around that age. His friends’ were getting into them and he wanted to join as well. He also wanted to show his Parents’ he could do something and this was that chance, because like said, he still believed that deep down inside they thought he couldn’t, that Roland would just be an academic kid, end of story, smiling and nodding happly with that result. [[//ERROR\\]]. And back a few years ago too it looked like Roland would never have been able to run. But at this moment in time Roland could run, fairly, and he could walk almost normally too. That in mind, Rowe joined a Baseball and Football Team, even getting into Wrestling which happened to be his Uncle’s sport for a very long time. There his Uncle taught him a number of things, training him hard and well. Roland also built himself up in Football liking the intensity a little more than Baseball, which he dropped soon after. Down the road Roland turned into a Rep Football Player, and one of the top all around Quarter Backs’ in the league. There were only a few problems though, and those were his Coaches’. Roland didn’t come across one Coach in his time that actually believed he could carry the team to the top of the mountain with his clubfeet. He had the talent in the arm, in the head, but he did lack some speed, so the Coaches’ made sure Roland got the odd break in Games, and that had pissed him off. He worked hard to become a top QB, but the Coaches’ just didn’t want him in there all the time. Instead, they put their own Kids’ as the Quarter Backs’ for replacements then and there. Authority they did have like others, so Roland didn’t bother to fight a problem like that either. But while under his Uncle in Wrestling training, Roland saw a good Coach in him. It was maybe the only Coach he ever had that was a totally great and honest man. His Uncle never even bothered to talk about Roland’s feet. Not once. Instead he believed Roland could do anything and everything in Wrestling if he put his mind to it. The fact was he saw Roland’s attitude which was all that mattered to him... and it was great. With that in mind and a lot of time devoted, Rolex (another nickname used) was becoming a rather good Wrestler indeed. High School (followed by College) then left Rowe and Law School was next. He had the marks for it and directed himself towards the world of the law. Still training in Wrestling and Football though, Roland just felt that Law might be the real way to go, and there still stood a chance with his feet that he could become badly injured, so something to fall back on wasn’t a bad idea at all. Also he did like the law a lot, however couldn’t put his finger right on the reason why. Regardless, there he got his LLB; he was a lawyer. After a couple of months into the world of being a lawyer, a failing Wrestling promotion was in need of new blood. Roland was offered this opportunity to try out, seeing as his Uncle was a “fair” Wrestling name, and having him as a trainer looked pretty good on one’s application. That, and Roland was looking pretty damn good himself. So, he said the hell with it, took the chance, and offered his help towards the Federation. Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling, three days later then signed ‘Ragin’ Roland to a short-term deal. At first, he did okay. Roland had the skill and heart for Wrestling, and he was doing well. But something was missing. It couldn’t be pointed out as to what. Roland figured his name wasn’t right, it rubbed off as more or less a “funnyman” or, “not to be taken seriously” as they said. Roland didn’t want that; he didn’t want to be laughed at like some did at his feet. He didn’t want to be overlooked for someone else like in his past. He wanted respect; he wanted authority. Fact stood: Roland worked very hard to get to where he was, over some odds others’ had never gone through before in their life, so he shouldn’t have been judged so basically. The degree was then born into his name, and Roland was LLB. Believing a job is only as good as you want to be, and your work is only as good as you live it to be, he dressed in his normal lawyer wear, and kept speaking his normal ‘Courtroom’ speeches. Roland began to get good at it too. He began to see the sins in people; the wrong doings they did; the laws they broke. It got him mad, too. It got him very mad. If everyone was just one big person, then, then the world would be a better place. He used this against them. It worked. Roland could make the rules; he could set the tone. The bottom line was Roland could lead, and lead he would. There was a lot in his past he didn’t care for, but the future and the present held nothing in stone. And LLB would actually be able to write his own. He’d tell people what to do. He’d change their ways to his liking. But most of all, he’d get the job done right. Roland would make sure that all the wrongs in his life didn’t come out anywhere else, as well as all the other wrongs too. Errors. Errors sucked. After a two year run with Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling, and winning a grand total of Five World Championship Titles’ there, -among other things,- PIW closed, due to a disrespectful and guilty Jury (Crowd) during a tapping of ‘Inraged’. LLB and his friend, Jim Johnson, also the Owner of PIW, were insulted by the people that night and took it to themselves to shut them up, basically jumping the whole first row, causing total chaos and a Locker Room clean out that ended up in both Television Stations killing the deal with PIW. Leaving LLB with no Court in session, he walked around, from Room to Room, trying to find a place that could actually suit him. A place that had guilty people in them. A place... named jOlt. LLB watched this Trial, and realized that this Room was unlike any other. You see, jOlt had a man named Eron, and Eron’s actions did nothing but make LLB want to get more into his past, and the more into his past LLB went, the more he saw how truly guilty Eron was. Action was taken. LLB entered the jOlt Courtroom. And enter he did, proving to all the verdict of Eron, rushing him out of jOlt under a “fake” reason, or so he says. However, LLB still stayed in jOlt, as he would hunt more and more guilty and sinful souls down to a.) give them hard time b.) prove their guilty verdict c.) and write them off from their wrongs LLB as well appeared in a Fighting Federation, the Asylum, alongside now dead friend Kenny Rock, in which they prove the sins of Douglas and A.D., another big two that stood out in the heading of Guilt. But there... well that’s how LLB expanded the Courtroom into Fighting. Though A.D. and Douglas’ guilt was proven, it seemed as though the Jury did not think it was, because when LLB left, A.D. had won the final battle against him... Pondering over this for sometime, LLB took his leave with the new jOlt Ownership and headed towards another Wrestling Federation ran by his long-time good friend Reed Young in an attempt to stop all horrible Wrestling shows once and for all, and to hand LLB the Title, where he didn’t get it in jOlt... but also towards a Fighting Federation. the Asylum. To fix the errors and restart the trial of A.D. As well, contacted by long time childhood friend, Reed Young, LLB took up an offer to head over to another Wrestling Federation. He wasn't going to let up there at all. The story of LLB still goes on to this day and will for some time, but no matter what may happen in the future: He is the law, and he is what’s right. Natural Law, Positive Law, or any of type of Law is handed out by him first. In a world where once there was no authority, well, now there is, and the right authority at that. LLB continues to write the verdict on Action Wrestling... The Case isn’t closed just yet. Other Feds the Asylum (Court Currently in Session); PIW (Case Closed); jOlt (Case Closed).
