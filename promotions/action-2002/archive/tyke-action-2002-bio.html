Tyke Biography Nicknames Da Tykester Hometown Leeds, England Birthday 11 February 1976 Height 6'1 Weight 230 Biography Was born in Leeds, West Yorkshire England to English parents. Named 'Tyke' by his trainer and mentor Mr Ming, and 'Da Tykester' by the rest of the world, the man is a multiple world champion, record breaker and record maker. He began training in Mr Ming's Gym in Leeds at the ripe old age of seven years and by the time he was 12 was already making headlines in the local press. Never one of the conventional approach, he deviates from the norm of young high-profile sportsman and always has. While he doesn't drink, gamble or smoke, he is also noted for sleeping in, chomping on chocolate and turning up for post-midnight training sessions. His early years are peppered with fight after fight with bigger, more experienced men. Although he learnt a great deal from stablemates and trainers, you can't teach the kind of raw talent and unbelievable speed that are his and his alone. In a stroke of fatherly love that can be seen as a genius move for the wrestling world his father, John, took him to the gym, fearing that his small lad could suffer from abuse. Some chance!! He was raw in the early days, but not so raw as to fool his trainers and many of his training partners. He's wrestled for England, although he moved out of the amateur circuit before he had a chance to score an Olympic gold medal. He's won belt after belt and still the wrestling world deprived him from entertaining people. Whatever the rest of the world thinks of him, his record stands all scrutiny and he will wrestler on!! Tyke relationship with Jordan happened when he turned 20. Tyke was in Milan with his father and he was introduced to Jordan who was working as a model at the time. The two hit it off, and were together for a year before Tyke asked Jordan to marry him. The two got married married in September 1999. Soon after Tyke and Jordan got married in Jordan's hometown of Brighton the two decided it would better for both their careers to move across the Atlantic. After months of preparation the two newly weds made the biggest move in their short time together. Tyke and Jordan moved into their new home in Miami Bay and quickly became popularly around the neighborhood. While Jordan quickly found out been a model, it took Tyke a little longer to settle down to his new home, and his new life. As this was the first that he had left his family behide. Tyke went and about his normal day trying to get his break into the wrestling world. Tyke first break came when Jordan was working to advertise a small time fed called ECW, Tyke having nothing else to do went to keep a eye on his young wife and while she was happily taking pictures with some of the so called big names Tyke got chatting with the owner of ECW Mr Gower. They arrange a try out for Tyke and with that Tyke wrestling career in the US started. He quickly became a fans favourite due to his ring entrances and his never die attitude. This earnt him his first title shot. He was to face X-Plosin (soon to become one of Tyke worst enemies) in a ladder match for the vacant ECW World Crown. The fight went back and forth until Tyke was just about to reach the belt when X-Plosin somehow blow the ladder up and costing Tyke his first really belt. After this Tyke and went on to become ECW first IC Champion and later to beat X-Plosin for the World Crown. During his time in ECW Tyke was drawing in bigger wrestling companies looking at him as his contact with ECW ran out in the Summer of 1999. Tyke had already made up his mind that he would have to leave ECW to become a bigger and brighter star than he already was, so he began to talk to the other companies. He later signed a pre-contract with Tornado Wrestling [TW] but when his contact with ECW ran out, TW was no where ready so Tyke was shipped off to another small organisation in SWF where he captured the European belt in his home town Leeds. Once TW opened it doors to the public many where shocked to see that they had signed Tyke as the word on the street was he was waiting to join either the WWF or WCW. Tyke joined TW and became the Cruiserweight champion after having a high profile ladder match with Kaid Mann. This was no normal ladder match this match up, it was a very personal to Tyke as the weeks leading up to the match had seen Tyke and Kaid got to hammer and tongs over Tyke's son Robbie, whom had been kidnapped by Kaid. Once the night of the fight came, Tyke made a very close friend in former fWo Heavyweight Champion Primetime who was guest referee. Also During his time with TW the first time round Tyke had a very serious neck injury which kept him out of the ring for well over 2 months, with Primetime by his side and helping the young star back to the ring all looked well for Tyke UNTIL! The TW management team decided that it was about time they used this young guys talents and make him the star he always wanted to be, but he was going have to turn his back on his family, his friends and his fans by going heel and attacking close friend Primetime. This cut the young Englishman up inside and soon started to feel lonely, wanting to return back to his wife and child. Tyke took it upon himself to cancel his contract with TW and go looking to better himself. After not working since he quit TW, Tyke and the TW have decided that it's about time to bring back the worlds favourite face and Tyke will be once again be a TW legion. Sadly during Tyke second regain in Tornado Wrestling, Tyke did not reach the heights he and fans hoped he would, due to the fact TW was running with a new management. Tyke long time friend Chris West (Owner of TW) was involved in a serious car accident in which he handed over control to his CEO. Tyke and the “new” CEO never saw eye to eye and Tyke was to play bit parts in TW only sometimes heading out a main entertainer, which TW had promised him. When TW shut down due to management problems Tyke was left with no employer, later in February 2001, Tyke was in deep talks with a promotion called 21st Century Wrestling, after nearly of month of discussions Tyke joined the team. In his first year in 21W Tyke has been plagued with a lot of pain and suffering. It looked very promising for Tyke, when he became the promotions very first champion, by becoming the Cruiserweight title, he has also won the tag team titles on 3 times with partner Coby Wright. The two forming the very popular tag team the British Lions, but the untimely death of his wife Jordan on Christmas eve 2001, played on Tyke’s mind and he went on a spree of causing havok to himself and others, taking the best part of 2002 to get him back online, after suffering another careering ending injury Tyke is now looking forward to 2003! Where he has promised his fans and himself to return to his formerly glory, and reclaim that place amongst the worlds elite!
