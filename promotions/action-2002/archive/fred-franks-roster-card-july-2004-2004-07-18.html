A Proud father watched on as his wife gave birth to a 7.5lbs baby boy. September 1978 - Fred Franks started his 12-year education in Hackney. It was a pretty normal school life. Throwing pieces of paper at the teachers, not doing homework and fighting in the corridors were all on the agenda. May 1979 - Fred's father died of a heart attack while having a row with his wife. 'Them two were always at it', Fred recalls. Some people still believe that Fred's violent nature came from living in a rough area of London and tensions within his family. June 1981 - Fred had his first run in with the law for stealing a Mars Bar from the corner shop. In a recent interview Franks recalled this as, 'the only time the coppers ever treated me proper'. July 1985 - Franks was expelled from school for splitting another child's head open whilst 'showing him' his favorite wrestling move, the DDT. July 1990 - Franks finished school and, 'fucked off to get a job'. He joined one of the few wrestling academies in London, AEW (All English Wrestling). They would train teenage lads up to watch and put up the ring. No Wrestling. It was what Fred wanted to do, but not yet. January 1993 - Fred met his long-term girlfriend and 'love of his life', Laura Jones. The two are yet to marry and when she's not traveling with Fred. She's living in Hackney. December 1994 - Franks was arrested at a football match for Racial Abuse, Rioting and resisting arrest. He was jailed. August 1995 - Franks groveled his way back into AEW after 8 months in the slammer. In a recent interview he referred to most of the inmates as 'queers'. May 1996 - With a history of violence, Fred Franks was again taken into custody and questioned by police after the brutal attack on a fan outside Wembley Arena. It was here he met his friend and lawyer, Adams. Police never found the man that was responsible but Franks was released and no charges were brought forward. He refuses to talk about it. January 1998 - Franks was publicly warned by the AEW about the use of excessive violence in his matches. His game was getting more and more extreme. This was brought forward after Franks won the Hardcore Title in late '96 in emphatic fashion. The opponent, Kid X, was FDT'd through a table and onto Fred's Hackney station sign. September 2000 - The ten years of graft paid off and Fred Franks was awarded the AEW Heavyweight Title after a win over The Highwayman. The match was one that went down in AEW history and will not quickly be forgotten. Franks lost the title the following Christmas Eve, to Shawn Gold. July 2001 - Franks won the AEW Title for the second time via submission. Locking Steve Smith into his modified Boston Crab made the Champion submit to the East Londoner. September 2001 - Franks was brought out of AEW after 12 years of 'loyalty' to the federation. Legends of Wrestling was an up-and-coming federation in America and Fred was contacted by the owner Zac Hawkes, to come to the federation to represent England. It was here Fred met his nemesis... Great Dragon. The two were sworn enemies from the start, both bringing titles into the federation. Although Dragon said he had respect for Franks, it wasn't mutual and Franks beat Dragon on the first Legends show to take the KOYASHI Title from him. This meant nothing to Franks, he wanted more. Fred went into league with Hawkes to FDT and beat Sean Cylear for the LoW Heavyweight Championship. The three belts were merged and the Legends Championship was born. Franks later lost this title to Great Dragon. October 2001 - Franks decided to come back to his roots and return to London. America was a shithole which was full of shitheads. While walking past an arena drunk one night, Fred somehow fought his way through security and smashed a chair over ukw Champion Alex Plant's head! He was caught and arrested by police. Franks found out afterwards that he had interrupted the October PPV, Red October. He went on to make a huge impact one of the largest wrestling companies in the UK. December 2001 ? On December 29, 2001, Fred Franks competed in his greatest match ever against the ukw Champion Alex Plant. The match lasted for ages but in the end, ukw owner Simon Webbe made the count, forcing Alex Plant out of ukw and making Franks the undisputed ukw Champion. February 2002: After swearing he?d never go back to America, Fred was forced to as his life in ukw crumbled around him. It seemed like everyone was against him and he lost the ukw Title. Looking for work in the USA was like looking for a whore in London. With his record? Bah!! March 2002: Fred joined US fed 'Rival' and became a familiar face among fans. Mostly for the downfall of many superstars. Fred was unbeaten in his two month stint in Rival and was schedualed for a Grudge Title match before the federation closed down.
