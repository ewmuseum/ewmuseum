What this is The site's own Report page (“Updated once a week”): an in-fiction backstage report on the roster, quoting staffers on how each wrestler is being received and pushed, from the site as it stood in the Wayback capture of 1 October 2002.
Note: Updated once a week. Action! Man Airborn Tyler One of the fastest rising people in the books of Staffers, has to be Airborn Tyler. "No regrets on this kid." Exclaimed James Attwood. "At first we were worried about him taking another fifteen foot dive... but he was able to do it." Though new, Airborn’s been praised by backstage talent such as Christopher Fox and LLB himself for his recent showings. And Fox, who was known back in the very early stages of his PIW career as doing wild and crazy things (jumping off a Hell in a Cell... taking a stage dive through fake glass, etc) has regarded Ben Tyler highly around the locker room area. It’s a given that Chris Fox and LLB have a lot of swing and say in Action!, so continue to look for Airborn to be put over as much as possible from his current positioning in the cards. He did receive some good bumps from the dives, so wrestling (may) be out for a few weeks in the ring. Angelica Dawson Christopher Fox The man himself seems back on track in the ring after his Hell in a Cell Match with LLB. Getting off the Marry Me Match he seemed to hold his own, but he then was complaining about a few small problems regarding pain in his back... and some booking things as well. "Chris can be very easy to please." Attwood stated. "But then again he can be very hard to please too." Only LLB has a higher swing within the company, yet despite Fox having some problems with one Staff Member, he is still very much for his angle with Steve Denton. And, if anything, the Staff Member will be looked down upon, before Christopher Fox himself. Conrad Ramsey Disciple X Joey Malone Fans were really over after the recent antics of Joey Malone during Pressure Point 23. "When he brought up the idea..." Said James. "I almost fell over in my chair." ‘Wrestling Today’ stated that the moment Seaman put on the scout hat, it was an instant mark out and a time for the ages. Malone, couldn’t be happier. Within status, Joey’s more than likely one of the top three right now on the Action! Staffer’s lists. Jonny Yankee The debut of Jonny Yankee went much better than first though. Tom Clark commented after Pressure Point 23 about it. "We weren’t worried really... it was just, how to work someone such as Yankee into the show? Well, after some thought, I think we did it just right. The Reed Young segment where he met Yankee for the first time, really got over well with the snickers." "If Reed didn’t snicker that much." Yankee recalls. "I think the attention would’ve been taken off me for a bit. It was really well done. I was trying not to laugh myself!" Another impression was made upon a highly regarded wrestler in the ways of KroW, whom laughed when he watched that part play out on television. Kent Athason Kent Anthason’s received major praise backstage for his "Lassie angle" during the special Pressure Point show. Joey Malone, Simon Seaman, Syphon Fission and even some new people Kent’s never worked with before have all put in a good word and really helped him become part of the team backstage. "Kent’s fit right in. He fit in so fast it was almost scary." Said James Attwood. KroW LLB LLB’s been battling with a little bit of depression as of late. He stated at a press conference just one week ago, "I don’t really feel like myself lately. Life crisis I guess... but I’ll get over it in time." Expect Reed Young to carry ‘The Risk’ on the microphone a bit more due to this, even though LLB will still be his normal self rambling on the microphone. Also, expect Reed Young to be drawn into the feud LLB and KroW for the time being. "It seems like Young’s really shown he can be a hardcore jackass... and a total idiot at the same time." Said another Action! Staff Member. "He’s doing really well... and to kick him away now would be way too premature." Logan Snyder A somewhat lost character in Logan Snyder is finding his way now, after wrestling countless Matches. "He hasn’t actually messed up in the ring yet." James Attwood said. "Which is a very good sign." Look for Logan to really take over in the mid card scene very soon, as he’s proven his worth to the A!W Staff wrestling wise. They’re only worry now... is can he handle the mic? Max Danger A lot of people are becoming in tune with Max Danger. The feud against Conrad Ramsey, the old man, has done him a good deal. "We were worried at first." Said Attwood. "Would Max actually be able to do something without his Tag Team Partner?" Low on ideas, they stuck Ramsey and Danger together, creating an overall feud that’s done really, really well. "I think one of the best moments we had was just two weeks ago, when Danger and Dawson beat the hell out of Ramsey in that wheel chair. Major heat." Morg Morg’s level of intensity and work ethic has vastly been improving, but his backstage attitude seems to have taken a bit of a downfall as of late. Against wrestlers such as Chris Fox, along with LLB don’t help in the long run, even though Morg on the television screen has not been any higher. The rumor is Morg’s been missing a lot of work out sessions, and he was currently booked with both Fox and LLB at the same time. LLB, who’s normally the most calmest person you could meet in the back, was irate after Morg supposedly missed his forth straight session. Simon Seaman Everyone seems to be very happy with Simon Seaman’s attitude as of late. He was never known for causing any problems... in truth he was actually one of the overall top guys to get along with. This has helped him get even more over with the Action! Staff as Seaman’s ongoing feud with Joey Malone is picking up a lot of heat and TV ratings. Do not expect this feud to last too long, though, as Action! Officials hope to keep this one short and sweet to maintain a high deal of interest... to leave the doors open down the road... and to keep the Fans wanting more from each character. Steven Denton The word is that Denton’s push is getting over well, the very second he hit Christopher Fox with a deck of cards during HIAC. "I never had more junk tossed at me in my life." Said Steven during a meeting with an Official after the show. The edition of Pressure Point 23, Steven quoted as saying, "I could really hear the Fans pop when I put my hand in... well... you know. That’s exactly what I wanted." Expect Good things from Steven Denton within the next few months. They are very pleased with what he’s been doing.
