What this is Phil Allensby’s card from the fed’s own Crew folder (referees, doctor and other staff), as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 4 January 2004. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is.
Phil Allensby Real Name: Phillip Daniel Allensby Also Known As: Phil, Dr. Oddball, and Keeper of Death. Date of Birth: The Twenty-Five Day of the Twelfth Month of the Seventy-Fifth Year in the Twentieth Century. Hometown: Born, raised, and still resides in Lava Hot Springs, Idaho. Height: Six foot, Three inches. Weight: One-Hundred Ninety Pounds. Attributes Intelligence: 10 Head Knowledge: 10 Leg Knowledge: 10 Arm Knowledge: 10 Other Body-Related Knowlege: 10 Stitching Ability: 10 Wrapping Ability: 10 X-Ray Reading Ability: 10 Charisma: 10 Penmanship: 10 Biography Phil knows EVERYTHING there is to know about the medical profession. He didn't get degrees from Duke University and Harvard Medical School by being stupid. He's fuckin' brilliant. But like most geniuses, he was a little eccentric. Coming from Idaho, well, he had a lot of free time. So he would take care of animals(yes, he has experience and knowledge of being a veternarian, but that's a whole other story, and his degree in THAT field is unimportant to his current profession as Action!'s trainer) and just sit around and read, watch television, movies. He's a pop culture junky. Also, he keeps a crow by the name of Death. He finds the name comical, but you don't touch Death. Get it? Personal Handler Note: While Dr. Dick can be used by anyone(you'd have to ask Joe about Nurse Rick) if they so choose to, Phil is personally going to be mine. It's not that I have major plans for him, but I do intend to use him much more so than Dr. Dick. So, if you want to do a segment or even a tale with him, please get with me, so that we can collaborate. Thanks! © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
