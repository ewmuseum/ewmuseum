Max Danger Biography Nicknames The Danger Man and the King of Submission. Hometown Augusta, Maine. Birthday Twenty-Third Day of the Eight Month of the Seventy-Seventh Year in the Twentieth Century. Height Six feet, Zero inches. Weight Two-Hundred Ten Pounds. Fed Titles Dyad Championship Fed Awards 2003 King of Submission Biography Masked. Worked many dates in Japan and Mexico under the guise of saidaigan KIKEN. A masked wrestler. He would often times pull double-duty. The last appearance of the character was in early 2002, when Max returned from a broken neck in the gfwa OMEGA. Atheist. Xander has absolutely no belief in the existence of God. It is not a major character trait. He just is, pure and simple. Jessica is agnostic, if you must know. Xmas. The abbreviated word for Christmas, Danger's most beloved holiday. Birthdays, Thanksgiving, Easter... all of those things paled in comparison to Christmas. His enitre life, he loved it the most. I mean, who wouldn't? Outside of the Jews, who was opposed to celebrating Christmas? (Okay, so there were others, but for the sake of this, only the Jews exist, okay? Okay.) Everyone loved getting gifts! (And damn those Jews for getting multiple days to give gifts. See, they know how to do it right.) Well Max never gave any care to the religious aspects of the holiday, he does firmly believe that the man who was supposedly born on this day, December 25th, will be coming back as a zombie to eat everyone's brains. You telling me Futurama would lie? That's impossible. Futurama is truth. DVDs. The Danger Man owns a very large collection of DVDs. Upwards of 400, and with the total growing by the day. Aqua Teen Hunger Force. The Adult Swim block is the home to many a great cartoon, like The Brak Shaw, Space Ghost Coast to Coast, Sealab 2021, among others. But the one staring a giant milk shake, a floating box of fries, a lump of meatball, and their human neighbor Carl, stands out as the most fucked up one of all. It also happened to be the name that a small collection of wrestlers had called themselves. The group consisted of Rook Black(Master Shake), Duel(Meatwad), Frank Heimdal(Frylock), Coral Avalon(Carl). Two new members were brought into the fold given their friendship with Rook and Coral. Yup, that's right, Max and Jessica Danger. They were dubbed Inignot and Err of the Mooninites respectively. Never had a single job his entire life. He was always financially supported by his mother until he started working independent wrestling shows. Supported himself through various tours of Japan and Mexico. Now is financially secure due to his inheritance from his mother's death. So, he and Jessica won't be strapping for cash anytime soon. Plus, Max has that insurance policy with Lloyd's of London. Gear. As in Max Danger's extensive wrestling gear. There is no way of telling exactly how many pairs of tights the Danger Man actually owns. He has entire outfits. The most recent addition being his Halloween costume of Spike from Buffy the Vampire Slayer Angel. Please, excuse his photo above, because he doesn't wear that attire at all. Anyway, Danger is about to unleash an entirely new line of wrestling attire come Players & Pawns. (A complete list of the color combinations will be on the MWA eventually.) These are just the templates for you all to know what they look like. He will obviously be color-coordinated. Engaged once before. Just a few short months before meeting the love of his life, Jessica, Xander was engaged to be married to his bestfriend during his childhood all the way through freshman year of college. Sarah Haplan was her name. They remain friends to this day, and live very close to one another in Augusta. She broke it off. She, also, was Xander's first sexual partner. (There have only been three.) Renaissance Man. Not the 14th-17th centuries in Europe, but rather to mean that Max is 'refined and educated' as per the words of one Ben Pants. See, Danger knows things. More specifically, he knows Japanese. He can read, write, understand, and speak the language. It was almost something he had to do when he was spending a lot of time wrestling in the country. Oddly enough, he never could be bothered to learn sign language during all the years of training and partnering with Bradley Duncan. Danger can also speak a little Spanish, but he really didn't have to know it much when he was working tours in Mexico. Other Feds EWCWF/FWO for 7 months, OWA for 8 months, GFWA for 15 months, FSW for 4 months, PIW for 5 months, gfwa OMEGA for 8 months, Advantage Wrestling for 1 match, and the Asylum for 2 months. Other Fed Awards EWCWF/FWO Television Title Wrestling Style Max Danger, aside from his limited high-flying arsenal(though, in the past, it was extensive), is one of the most complete wrestlers on the planet. There's a reason he's had the success he's had and how come a lot of his students turn out to be excellent Finisher Move Dangerous IV. Finisher Description Chicken Wing Piledriver. Special Moves Max Danger has a whole hell of a lot of signature moves. Not all are used often, or at all. But he has them.
