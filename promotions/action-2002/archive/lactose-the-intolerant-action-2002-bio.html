Lactose The Intolerant Biography Nicknames Mr. Controversial, Planet Lactose, Lactose the Intolerant Hometown Dairytown, West Virginia Birthday Aug. 15, 1974 Height 5'10 Weight 189 Fed Titles Dyad Championship (x1 w/ Russa) Biography < PRELUDE > He's the proverbial 'runt of the litter'. He's the Spike Dudley to the Buh-Buh Ray and D-Von. Lactose has never been held in any regards like his LOD brethren. He spent the early part of his career shying away from the lime-light that his friends learned to embrace and love. He soon felt that wrestling wasn't his cup of tea and decided to let it all go and hang up the tights. Minutes turned into hours, and hours soon turned into days and no one seemed to ever notice that Lactose was gone from wrestling. Now he plans on a comeback... sorta. < Early Years > Lactose was born and raised on the outskirts of the booming metropolis called Dairytown. While it would be a long time before he ever actually stepped foot in the town, Lactose was raised by his parents to become one of Dairytown's finest. By age two Lactose was well ahead of most children's standards, prompting his parents to believe that he would grow to be a child genius. It proved to be only a fluke. Lactose went on to go to Preschool and that's when his downward slide began. Dairytown has never been held in high regards for it's education programs, just it's wrestlers. Lactose made his way through a second-rate education and upon graduation from high school, found himself with nothing to do. Soon Lactose was presented with two options 1) college and 2) wrestling. Looking back on his secondary schools education, Lactose came to a quick decision and was soon enrolled in one of the finest wrestling schools in the county. < Catching A Break > Lactose wasn't the best student in his class, nor was he the most dedicated. Still, he managed to get through the class and was soon a bonafide 'wrestler'. Lactose's first federation was the lowly AwF (Akron Wrestling Federation), where he was placed under the guise of 'El Diablo Mehecano'. Forced to wear a ridiculous mask, Lactose soon found out that the fans weren't connecting his character and he was soon released from the company. One night while he was sitting at home, he received a call from his friend cHEESE of the Legion of Dairy, inviting him to come join a booming federation called the Net World Order or NWO for short. Seeing an opportunity, Lactose jumped on it and was soon repackaged as Lactose the Intolerant, member of the Legion of Dairy. < Early Troubles > Lactose was enjoying the NWO, it had a nice atmosphere and everyone seemed to get along... or so he thought. As he would soon learn, many of the NWO faithful had already formed an opinion of the new stable and soon pressed their opinions to the boss. One night Lactose suffered a defeat that many believe he should have been victorious in. He was soon talking to the boss and almost immediately was shown the door. Soon the other members of the Legion were fired or quit and all five were without a job and in desperate need of work. < Golden Opportunity > It was almost like Deja Vu as Lactose found himself once again sitting at home with little to do, and almost like before he received another call from a member of the LOD. This time it was egg NOG pitching the fast rising EHWF, with boredom at the wheel Lactose was quick to say yes and was soon back to work in wrestling. < Not Such A Sweet Deal > While the LOD were soon on their way to the top, it seemed that the train to success had left the station without Lactose on board. While egg NOG, MiLk and cHEESE were earning all their accolades, Lactose was left with nothing. While he didn't mind this at first, it soon started to get to him and he was finding him more and more turned off to wrestling as a whole. Lactose only wrestled in three matches over a six month period and was soon giving the president his retirement notice. Once again, Lactose was without wrestling. < A Change of Heart > Months had past since Lactose had left the sport of wrestling, and all had seemed well. He never thought about wrestling much anymore, and when he did it was only about the negative things that he'd seen during his numerous stays. One day Lactose found himself thumbing through the channels and happened across the SWL, the federation that he had once worked for and now found international success. It just so happened that in the ring at the time were his two friends, also of Dairytown, cHEESE and egg NOG. He was surprised to see them since he thought that they too had left wrestling for good. Suddenly Lactose got a odd feeling inside, something he'd never felt before, it was as if his body were wanting him to return to the sport he'd once loved. Deciding that he need to get his own spotlight, Lactose was soon out-and-about looking for work. < Hard Times > It wasn't easy for Lactose, many federations had never heard of him and really didn't want to run the risk of him leaving the company in a short period of time as he'd done in years past. Lactsoe felt that his luck had run out and all was lost when he came across an up and coming federation called iCon Wrestling. 'Surely they'll want someone like me', he thought as he made his way into the building. 'They need me.' And just like that, Lactose was back in the game. < What Does The Future Hold? > I have brought up to date on the life and career of Lactose the Intolerant, where he goes from here is anyone's guess. What he'll do is a mystery unto itself. Will he break from the omnious shadows that are cast by the other members of the Legion Of Dairy? Time will tell.
