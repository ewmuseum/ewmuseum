Le Java Birth Name: Henry Resuex Also Known As: Date of Birth: July 7th, 1981 Age: Marital Status: Hometown: Calais, France Height: 6'8' Weight: 311 Hair Color: Eye Color: Blood Type: Status: Heel Personality: Theme Music: 'Gasoline' by Audioslave [Face]/'Just So You Know' by American Head Charge [Heel] Wrestling Style Trained By: Detailed Style: Powerful/Innovation Strengths: Weaknesses: Signature Moves: Trademark Name: French Envy or The Napoleon Lock Trademark Move: “French Envy”: This move can be performed in an instant or it can be built up to after performing a trademark move. For example, if say Le’Java had just connected with a kick to the gut or a move which may stagger an opponent momentarily, he could grab them by the shoulders, shove their head between his legs, hoist them into the Powerbomb position, keep a hold of their legs as their body dangles down towards the ground, step over the opponents arms so they were locked behind his legs and then fall forward, connecting with a Reverse Pancake Piledriver, sqaushing the opponent’s body and mainly their shoulders to the mat. -- “The Napoleon Lock”: Unlike “French Envy”, “Napoleon’s Revenge” has to be performed in unison with one of Le’Java’s trademark moves, “Illegal Immigration”. After the opponent has hit the chair edge, edge of the steel steps, etc, Le’Java proceeds to set the opponent up into a unique submission manoeuvre, having the opponent still lying on their chest as he pulls their legs up behind them at his sides, almost like an inverted Walls Of Jericho. To add additional pressure, he may well sit down on the opponent’s back and possibly hook his feet under their arms. Won on Pressure Point in One-Off Interfed Match] Attributes Intelligence: 7 Strength: 10 Toughness: 10 Speed: 6 Endurance: 9 Courage: 8 Charisma: 5 Skill: 8 Biography Coming from a poor area of the famous port town in France, Le’Java went under another moniker as a youth: Henry Reseux. Compared to his current weight, the 8lbs baby that would later become a mysterious wrestling superstar shown early signs that he would be a large child. His parents, Frank and Missy, a farmer and an actress, were extremely proud of their son, even though he would later become an oversized bully during his tenure in primary school. At the grand old age of 5, Henry started at a local primary school, and once again amazed elders with his wit, knowledge, but most of all...hate. Even though he did make people laugh with his little boy jokes and could tell them just about all there was to know about his dad’s farm or his mother’s acting roles, the hate and anger he used to show towards another child who disagreed with him was astounding. By the time he reached 11, the Reseux kid was known throughout the school as the boy you didn’t want to mess with. At this age, a child wasn’t expected to know such feelings as love and hate, but Henry did: he loved to hate. Weighing in at approximately 14 stone and measuring a massive 5’7” tall when he left primary school, a child of this size made a massive impact when he entered secondary education. Without even having to do anything when he arrived, Reseux’s reputation had already spread amongst other newstarts in his year as well as teenagers in the years above. By his 3rd day of secondary school, Henry had managed to set up a lunch money racket along with kids from 3, 4 and even 5 years above him who had already tried and failed the system. This, of course, led to much victimisation, both from Henry but also towards him, from older kids who didn’t agree with his ideas and additionally from teachers who knew about his love for intimidation. While his mass and reputation faired him well in the playground, in the classroom, he was in a league of his own. Even the teachers who despised him couldn’t keep him down, his wit, intelligence and general knowledge blowing any questions they thought might puzzle him out of the water. This mix of bullying, intimidation and excelling in class continued throughout Henry’s secondary school career, finally graduating with a multitude of first-class grades for English, Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Physical Education, amongst others. At the age of 17, the young Reseux was at a loose end, with a handful of grades in his back pocket and the build of a powerlifter. Destiny would strike upon Henry 3 days after he finished school for the summer. As he messed around in the garden, playing with one of his only friends, Jacke Geffen, the kid next door, Henry and Jacke began to scrap in the usual schoolboy manner, running about the garden, trying to toss each other around. But as Henry caught Jacke for the second or third time during their game, he lifted the smaller boy and held him above his head in what is called a Military Press in the wrestling business. Both Jacke and Henry were amazed by Reseux’ strength and the two boys stood in slight shock for a few minutes, obvious after Frank lowered Jacke to the ground. Leaving his next door neighbour’s garden around about 5pm, Frank made his way into his own house and to the living room where the television was switched on, an unusual sighting seeing as no one was watching it and the family kept strict rules about wasting energy, leaving the television on being one of the key faults. A promo flashed across the screen, a pair of leotard-clad men grappling together in a ring in front of a crowd being the main attraction. An 80’s style voiceover kicked in, the English being spoken with a Northern American tone: “Come see the CWA, this Saturday night, live at Calais Nationale Stadium! Catch all your favourites from Dr. Death and Steve Wright to K.C. Cool and Fuming Frank!” With that, the promo flashed through various clips of the wrestlers, powerbombs, suplexes and other common moves demonstrated for advertising purposes by some of the aforementioned wrestlers. Henry stood in awe, watching these men, these massive men, of similar build to him, entertain people and get credit and applause for slamming men on their heads or knocking them off their feet...Never before had something appealed to him more... This is the generic “Becoming a Wrestler” part that I’m sure everyone knows by now: boy goes to wrestling event, falls in love with sport, devotes live to becoming famous, lady-da. Henry’s wrestling uprising was no different from most other wrestling superstars these days, his mass and intelligence playing great roles in his development as a wrestler. By 20, he had donned the moniker “Bulk”, apparently representing his size advantage and dominance over other wrestlers. The name obviously didn’t catch on in the independent federations he was working in, so a name change and a different look were all that Henry, or should I say “Bulk” needed... Enter “Le’Java”. The name came to Henry as he fell asleep on the bus back from an Indy show in a Paris suburb. Jolting himself awake as the bus hit a nasty pothole, Henry awoke and his eyes caught sight of a billboard selling some French coffee named “Morning Java” and directly under the billboard was a small French bakery by the name of “Le’Crust”. Somehow, the two thing seemed to merge and so it became....Le’Java... His mind suddenly clicked and Reseux immediately started thinking about attire, a gimmick and everything else essential to his development of the Le’Java character. The next morning, Henry had drawn up ideas of how he may look, what attitude he would play off, and then it struck him...The Le’Java character wasn’t created...It was him. The attitudes of cockyness, of superiority...That was Henry Resuex. Only then did he realise that the character he had created was nothing more than a mirror image of his own personality. This really screwed Henry’s head. He knew he was sadistic, he knew he was slightly evil...but only when he actually saw it all first hand did he realise how f*cked up he really was. As you may expect, Henry tried to sort this and tried to act like a nice guy, helping people, not being sarcastic when he spoke, etc, but it didn’t last long. Before long, he was back to being the big bully he always was. Now, he realised he couldn’t change what he was and suddenly something clicked: it was like he’d worked out what he was and was happy with it. From then on, he didn’t regret attacking people when their backs were turned or hurting someone who got in the way. All the time Henry was trying to work out things in his head, he’d been missing shows on the Indy scene and had started to gain a reputation as a no-shower, so the agency he was signed to had no regrets when they let him go. Now a free agent and with his head in order, Henry looked for work on a larger scale, not in the Indy scenes but on in one of the major feds. But an experienced wrestler by the name of Jacques Fazaro, or “Topex” as he was called in the ring, would soon slow up Le’Java’s leap to stardom, but also strengthen Reseux as a wrestler and as a person...
