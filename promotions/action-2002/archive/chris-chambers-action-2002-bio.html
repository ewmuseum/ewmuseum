Chris Chambers Biography Nicknames Rudy. Hometown Chicago, Illinois. Birthday January 17th, 1984. Height 6'0' Weight 219 Biography Okay. Let us give you a quick run-down of what Chris Chambers has achieved during his stay in this promotion: ... What? Expecting something? Chris Chambers is a failure. He doesn't win matches. He doesn't carry championships. He loses. And that's about the only thing he does right. He currently wears a brown paper bag that covers his entire head, believing that his face is horribly disfigured after botched surgical attempt from Dr. Dick. He is also a member of the Wrestling 101 B-Squad. So... His chances of winning a match in the next few years before he is recruited by a latex-suit wearing cult that promises peace and happiness on a far off planet? Slim to none. Other Feds aWc.
