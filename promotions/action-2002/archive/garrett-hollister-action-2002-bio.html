Garrett Hollister Biography Nicknames The Follower or Brother Hollister Hometown Munich, Germany Birthday 01.01.1970 Height 7'1' Weight 364 Biography Just like his mentor and God, we know little about the past of the man who came into this world as Matthew King. He lives to serve and will no doubt be the driving force which takes his figurehead to the forefront of professional wrestling. For he, Garrett Hollister, is the muscle of the tandem now known as The Brotherhood. ------------------------------------------- There are two types of people in every society, whether primitive or modern. You have the leaders, those who have power. Then you have the followers, those who crave just a small ounce of that power. This blueprint for humanity is the cause for much of the Earth’s turmoil. For when one race has one sole leader, there may be benefits for that certain race. What happens, however, when each leader comes together. Egos clash. Empires falter. And in the end … who suffers? The followers. Yet, humans never seem to grasp that fact. We elect them. We coddle them. But do they care about us when it’s them on the line? Of course not. As Matthew King came to Boston, a naïve immigrant, he never once considered that fact. Stumbling across a man known as Kyle Black, he thought nothing of the relationships between a leader and his pawns. Slowly, Black who was a mere child compared to the large and elder King, started to preach out on exactly why King met him that day. For where did they cross paths? 'The streets!' Screamed Kyle. 'You found me in poverty, Matthew! Just as you will be in one day, my friend!' '…' Matthew King seemed confused. 'You come from Germany…' Black continued, 'You know nothing of America, yet expect opportunity?! Go to George Bush as a border jumper and ask him for some opportunity. You’ll end up washing the car of some pig while he laughs at your misfortune, collecting his mounds of money and recognition.' What was King thinking listening to a child? Answer unknown. But as the German began to follow Kyle Black more closely … it all became clear, at least to the witnesses. Neither subject had any idea of what was happening between them. This twenty something could speak, however. He captivated hundreds in every cold alley and junkyard he could find. Seeking out the poor and hungry, and singling out their weaknesses, blaming them on the leaders which they themselves gave power? 'I was born on the day of independence.' Kyle ranted. 'Where is my liberty? In the trashcans where I scrounge for food? I’d think not.' And as he spoke out against authority and the conventionalism of leadership, he slowly began to build his own foundation to lead. He knew, just like Hitler did in the 1930’s, how to make people think about their fears and insecurities. And if he knew how to make them think, he could surely provoke them with those same fears. With this, in his head, Kyle Black realized that what better way to stop the world’s leaders then to fight them with their own medicine. Not only that, but the very same material advances he had never been exposed to became clear. With power comes respect, and with respect comes money. He would be the one supported, while the people who drove him to be what he is sit back and try to imagine what it would be like to be him. It wasn’t about a mission to destroy anymore, it was about a mission to please one man. This was all kept from each person who donated time and energy to the 'cause' including Matthew King, who was now in Kyle’s company simply to protect him from the law, who the brawny alien evaded in brutally beating and thieving for his leader and supposed friend. He saw Black build power and did nothing to stop it, for he knew that there would be some sort of share between them involved. Dubbed a family, Black reversed his plans and began speaking of not acting violently towards the establishment, but acting kind to the 'brothers and sisters' around you … thus beating each government with unity. As coverage of this rising in Boston broke out, a press conference was seen nationally … bringing Kyle Black and the group of zealots who he now called 'The Brotherhood' to the front lines. 'I assure you…' He said softly, reassuringly. 'I assure the President…' Echoing the first tone. 'I assure each world citizen…' He smiled. 'That if you realize that the way we do things as a country aren’t the answers, and you drop your guns, joining me. I’ll take you all home…' 'Home?' One nameless reporter shouted out. 'To the promised land … brother.' Thinking that this was some sort of eerie prediction of a mass suicide, The Brotherhood was dubbed a Cult … many people comparing them to the Manson Family of old. But how? They claimed to be non-violent. The morgue’s records said differently. Men who openly opposed the cause, turning up dead with symbols carved into their bodies and organs taken. Is this the work of Kyle Black and his 'family'. Of course it was. Yet, these were never proven to be factual toward the convictions of Black. Then, on the night of his 21st birthday, Matthew King walked into Black’s office, transfixed by his stare. The last murder, dubbed 'Operation True Freedom', had obviously been carried out. Blood soaked the hypnotized elder‘s brow, while the smaller and weaker Black sat in front of him, knowing fully well that he ordered death on another man. 'We cannot continue to kill.' '…' King had never said much. Almost as if acting as the enforcer to something much bigger. From the sulking look on the scorned immigrants face, it was clear that he had developed a taste for violence, at the accord of his mentor. 'Don’t fret, brother.' Kyle said with a smirk. 'For I have found something mainstream, both to cure your thirst for blood, and to spread our message.' It had to be violent. It had to be watched by millions. 'Professional wrestling.' Changing his name to Xander Pollack, clearing his record with the swift erasing of a computer, he organized a nationwide office for The Brotherhood … the 'family' which to this day still collects 'children' by the dozens. Publishing diaries and newsletters, in support of the 'cause' which was actually his personal gain, the group which was once limited to ragged clothing and garbage for nutrition, was slowly becoming a business. And the smallest chapter, but the most powerful, began to search the Earth for a proper breeding ground. Meeting Kylie Yazvac, Xander quickly made her think his way, taking her as a wife and creating a manager … so nothing suspicious could be suggested. She quickly found the perfect place. Xander’s plan was coming full circle now … seeing the perfect wrestler in both himself and Matthew King … known now as Garrett Hollister. Now … The Brotherhood is on it’s way to a new world, one which has never been dominated in the ways Xander & Garrett have planned. Cult or tag team … they’re dangerous … and accepting members with punch thrown from the fist of 'the Follower' and every word from the mouth of 'the Leader'.
