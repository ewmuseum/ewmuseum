Dyad Championships (with Julius Arcade) [current] Action Wrestling Award History: Other Federations: IWO [current] Other Federation Awards: none Attributes Intelligence: 6 Strength: 8 Toughness: 9 Speed: 7 Endurance: 7 Courage: 8 Charisma: 7 Skill: 6 Biography The Team of Arcade & Son was formed in early January of 1999. It is composed of Julius Arcade (48), and his son, Donell (26). Julius Arcade had been a wrestler in the times of Vincent McMahon Sr., wrestling with Tony Atlas as a team in the Late 70s - Mid 80s. Five years after his wife gave birth to a set of twins, Donell and Daisy, she passed away. The departure hit the family hard, but as Julius was on tour his brother Tyrell would often take care of the kids. When Donell and Daisy were 17, Daisy died of a pill overdose in her brother's arms. The death had hit Donell harder than anyone, as his sister was his closest friend. Since then, he has received a Tattoo on his left arm that reads 'DAISY' in Old English writing. After the death, Julius had taken a long needed departure from wrestling and came home to be with his Son, who was now getting heavily involved with drugs himself. After dropping out of High School and loosing a Basketball scholarship to NAU, Donell became a drug dealer. Julius let this go on under his nose, as he was beginning to lost patience with his son, until he got busted by the DEA at a major deal. With his money, Julius paid the best lawyer he could afford to help his son, who managed to get him Six Months in a Minimum Security Prison and Three Years probation following. After serving his Six Months, Donell left a humbled man. Convinced that his son would fall back in to that track, Julius took Donell to Canada where he trained him to wrestle as he did. Once fully trained, no promotion would give Donell to a contract, as he was a liability. But because Julius was a well accomplished athlete, they would pay the two on a Pay-Per-Appearance basis. In March of 2002, Donell's Parole was finally up and he was able to get a contract. Seven Months later, the two got signed to a promotion called Action! Wrestling by an idiot named Reed Young, and they appeared on the November Pay Per View called Last Laugh. Since then, the two began a heated rivalry with a former jOlt Team, and at the time Dyad Champions, The Elements. The tension became rather intense when Donell began to date the brother's younger sister, Siobhan Tyler, but it all ended when the Elements beat Arcade & Son in a Last Man Standing Match which forbade Donell from seeing the youngest member of the Tyler family. After that, memories of Donell's sister Daisy began to resurface, and Donell slipped in to yet another depression. As Julius knees began to wear badly, Donell would always leave him to rot when he was suffering beatings from the newly arrived French Team of Accidie. After an altercation where Donell mentioned to his father that Daisy was the reason for his actions, Julius turned away in disgust and lost trust in him, as well as all feelings. The move hit Donell hard, and after being forced to watch a beating given to his father with a Glass-Covered Baseball Bat at the hands of Accidie, he snapped out of his trance. Donell was Hell bent on winning the respect of his father, again, and that included taking new challenges. After capturing the Action! Dyad Championships, the two decided to take on some other challenges, that being the IWO. With two promotions to juggle, the weight of being champions, and an ever-growing tension between them, it is a matter of time before the Arcade Dynasty will collapse.
