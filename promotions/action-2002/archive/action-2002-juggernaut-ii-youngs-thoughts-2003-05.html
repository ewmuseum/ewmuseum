Juggernaut II site: Young's Thoughts

May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The Thoughts page of the fed's own Juggernaut II website (the 25 May 2003 pay-per-view): an in-character message from the Owner persona, Reed Young, from the Wayback capture of 16 June 2003. It is a promo on the event's microsite, not a post of the roleplay board, so it is filed class other. The show itself is served on this wing.
Reed Young, Action Wrestling Owner: Okay, I’m gonna be blunt here. Other Federations, take note. This is how to run the biggest event EEEVVVEEERRR /Valley Girl. First off, I didn’t get the Backstreet Boys this year because they aren’t around anymore... but Justin Timberlake will be in the house with I Mother Earth, Trapt and The Hives! Oh yeah, I’ve reached into my pockets big on this one. Anyway, aside from that... the card itself is taking a great shape. Look, see, I’m staying the HELL OUT of some of these people’s feuds. Although Fox will win, I haven’t bothered them too much. I could care less about Joey Malone and Daniel Phillips... but it’s still going to be a bloody high draw. I’ve gone out there and signed so many people YOU want to see, haven’t I? I am a fucking God. Sylo. Cylear. KroW. Tyke. Sharc. Rook. Just some of the many moves I’ve made over the month or two... and some MORE people will be coming too, ones that draw in RATINGS like never before! This is a big "I’m way better than you" message to all the other "Owners" out there. Not only will Juggernaut II kill the critic’s expectations, it will even put other Federations like the Asylum out of business. Sure, I’ve reached big time into my pockets to pay for all these fools, but dammit, you all want to see them anyway. fWo, take notes Mr. Aqua Fox, and you too Mr. Joseph Crambell... as well as anyone else who wishes they were ME. This is going to be the event that rocks the body which rocks the party that rocks the body. And I know what you want to tell me, and, you’re welcome. This world does need a monopoly, and thankfully I’m that guy in the black suit and little top hat.
