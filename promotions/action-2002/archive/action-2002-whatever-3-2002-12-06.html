Action! — Episode

Whatever 3, 6 December 2002

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Whatever 3 1st Mariner Arena — Baltimore, Maryland ---- WHATEVER! WHATEVER! Introduction. (It was that time again... Whatever. The scene flashed over from the Action Wrestling Signature Introduction to the Whatever startup video, which showed different wrestler’s shots for over two minutes, as “Breathe” by Nickelback played throughout it. The scene then switched, as Derik Shaw smiled, sitting behind an Action! Screen with the Whatever logo jumping randomly upon it. He sat in front of a black desk, with red writing that said “Whatever” across the side. “Breathe” then started to fade out, as Derik Shaw readjusted his tie, smirked, and then spoke.) Derik Shaw: Welcome everyone to Whatever! Tonight, Sweet Aroma will be interviewed, and we will recap Monday’s past events with our Match of the Week, and Segment of the Week as well. The upcoming Pay-Per-View, Players and Pawns has an official Match now, as ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear will make his return to the ring on December the 31st, to battle Christopher Fox, LLB’s best friend. No doubt that more on this and other Matches will pick up this coming Monday... as I’m sure the card will grow. Speaking of which, before we move on... I will go over what’s lined up for this coming Monday. I *will* be opening that package later on, the one I received during my broadcast of Pressure Point. On it, it read “do not open until Pressure Point 41”, and, since I can add a little, 40 plus 1 is 41, so, Monday it is. Alright, now... the important stuff. Conrad Ramsey will in some way, shape, and form defend his Bantam Championship on this show. Reed Young also says he has a statement regarding this Championship Title. Now, I don’t know what that means -no one really does- but will it effect Conrad Ramsey, or not, is the question at hand. Pretty Fly will see the ring against the new tag team in Arcade and Son... and it looks to be for the rights to face the Dyad Champions at Players and Pawns; anything there could be possible. Finally, Christopher Fox will take on a hand picked opponent of Reed Young’s choice... as well as Joey Malone has plans to defend the Action Championship again... but no opponent’s been named yet. I’ll assume, a lot more will go on within these times too. (Derik looks across the camera and smiles.) Shaw: And after this, we’ll take you back to Monday! ---- Segment of the Week: Dead. Segment of the Week: Dead. Starring: LLB, Reed Young, 'Showcase' Sean Cylear & Christopher Fox. And almost instantly off the commercial break, it came. Without a warning, without a notice, without any buildup whatsoever... it came. “Still Waiting”, Sum 41. The Fans lost it. They stood. They cheered. They waited for their lawyer, the one that was jobbed out of the Virus Championship last week. Then he marched out. He walked the ramp hard, fast, and with a purpose. It didn’t take a million people to realize that LLB was indeed pissed off for the first time since his one month absence. He entered the ring, taking a microphone as his theme song cut off right away. He didn’t waste a second. “OBJECTION!” Some of the Fans replied with “Erection!”, but most of them were too concerned on what LLB was going to say next. “First...” He trailed off. “First I’d like to say what a night Sunday was. What a night indeed. From seeing Syphon Fission turn on ‘The Risk’, to German suplexing Airborn Tyler, to fighting Simon Seaman, my friend tooth-and-nail... to coming down to LLB and Christopher Fox in the end of the Virus Match. And regardless of the outcome, I’d like to congratulate Fox for winning that Match.” Pause. “OBJECTION! It was all well and good. There we were... LLB, CHRIS FOX... one-on-one! And I didn’t care if he beat me clean... but that did not happen! I had Fox lined up... NAILED with the ‘Erroneous Conclusion’! I had him down and out... and then, I went for... The ladder.” The Fans booed. “I SET up that ladder... I CLIMBED that ladder... I was going to jump off and CRUSH Christopher Fox because OF that ladder! I was going to hit the ‘Big Buck$’ [note: reference to LLB’s first ever Trademark move; sometimes still used to this date] and pin him... one, two, three. OBJECTION! THE VIRUS WOULD NOT BE LLB!” LLB marched around the ring with a sneer on his face, as he stopped in the center, and jumped up and down frantically, just like his old heel days, yelling and screaming. “BUT THEN... SEAN... FUCKING... CYLEAR!!” Ottawa booed like never before. “OBJECTION!” Pause. “Where in the HELL did he come from!?” LLB rubbed the back of his neck, taking some deep breaths in, and then out, to calm him back down. “I... want answers.” Pause. “OBJECTION! AND I WANT THEM NOW!” “LLB! LLB! LLB!” The Crowd stayed behind ‘The Law’, as the Fans kept cheering... for the wait, it was long, but it did come. Just as LLB was about to exit the ropes to go back there and find the answers himself, a theme cued up. “Without Me”, Eminem. Reed Young strutted out, as he began to dance on the ramp like it was the disco days. The Fans booed hard and loud; for no one got the reaction this idiot did. He stood on the ramp, but to everyone’s surprise, no one else came out. Young stroked his neck as he grinned. He couldn’t stop grinning, all day and all night. Hell, if you were an idiot, and you upped one on the entire world, twenty bucks says you’d be smiling like him too. “E-WHATEVER-THE-FUCK-YOU-SAY-JECTION!” “Without Me” came to a close, as the Fans just jeered while LLB marched around the ring. “Answers!?” Young was dumbfounded. “What the fuck Mother fucker are they!?” The grin never left. “Roland, what the fuck are you smoking!? Has Villam Ender and those FUCKS given you drugs and raped you a few times in the Asylum? They must have... because I have NO Goddamn idea what you’re talking about, and, Roland, *I’m* an idiot, too!” The Fans booed. LLB just walked the ring, screaming at Reed. He didn’t have an idea what he meant. “Roland, I don’t know HOW you could say Sean Cylear cost you the Virus Match... when he’s dead.” Confusion. “YOU HEARD ME YOU MORONS... SEAN CYLEAR IS DEAD!” Young took a breath. “Even *I’d* like to know how someone can cost you your Virus Match when they’re dead. Tell me that lawyer... TELL ME THAT!” And just to piss Reed Young off, the Fans started one of their chants. “Your Fed sucks! Your Fed sucks! Your Fed sucks!” “Not this time faggots.” Reed went on. “I think you’re ALL seeing things, because-” Young kept talking, but the attention wasn’t on him anymore. It was on the Golden Caped Man, whom had appeared from under the ring, rolled into it, and snuck behind the lawyer. Waiting... The Fans tried to warn LLB, but it didn’t work. “-I don’t know a Sean Cylear... I never met a Sean Cylear. I mean, like, what’s a Cylear? A fan, or somethin-” LLB saw Reed Young’s mouth begin to grin heavily. He sensed it... and no matter how many Fans yelled at him not to, he did. He turned. SMACK. LLB walked right into a superkick. “YES MO-FUCKER!! YES... YES!!!” Reed Young ran down the ramp as he slid into the ring. The Fans kept booing, and that’s when the Golden Caped Man took off his mask. Sean Cylear. Young acted surprised, screaming into the microphone. “OH MY GOD!! IT’S SEAN CYLEAR!! HE’S ALIVE!! HE’S ALIVE!! SEAN CYLEAR IS ALIVE!” Ump: “What an IDIOT!! We KNOW it’s Sean Cylear!” Shaw: “OH MY GOD!! CYLEAR... HE’S ALIVE!! AND HE’S THE GOLDEN CAPED MAN!!” Ump: “WE KNOW!!” Cylear bent down, and began to hammer LLB with hard right hands into the side of the head. There was no stop, he just kept on going... and Reed, Reed kept on screaming. “SEAN CYLEAR!! BUT YOU’RE DEAD!! YOU’RE FUCKING DEAD SEAN!! I DON’T BELIEVE IT!! OMG... OMG... O, M, G!!!!” The boos were loud; they had reached their climax, as blood drew from LLB’s head, and Sean Cylear showed no intention to stop. “I DON’T BELIEVE IT... THE EYES... THE HEAD... IT’S YOU!! IT’S SEAN FUCKING CY-” “Courage”, AAF. And as Reed Young snapped around to the entrance, “courage” it was going to take to actually object to this duo of power. ‘Kid’ appeared from behind the logo as the Fans erupted. They went even crazier as he pulled a mic up to his lips. “Heto!” The Crowd replied back with an enormous “Heto!” of their own. “Reed-o, stop this madness now! The people want it stopped, so stop, in the name of love!” Fox begged. He actually did a little dance too. Young shook his head in the motion of no. “No? Hhmm, well then, heto Seen! I mean Sean!” The Crowd giggled as Cylear sneered, going to pick up LLB again... “Not a good idea, Sean.. BIG TIME!” Just then, Fox placed the microphone down softly and stormed to the ring! The Fans all stood out of their seats, watching as Fox slid into the ring, and ducked a ‘Showcase Shot’. Right... RIGHT... RIGHT... Fox had the Fans going mad, as he flipped himself into the ropes... and went to spear Sean Cylear... WWWHHHACK!! Ump: “REED YOUNG- YOUNG HAS JUST CRUSHED CHRISTOPHER FOX WITH A STEEL FUCKING CHAIR!!” Young dropped the chair, in place of the mic. And acting like the last two minutes didn’t even exist, Young shouted on the speaker again. “OH MY GOD... IT’S SEAN CYLEAR!! HE’S ALIVE!! ALIVE!! A-FUCKING-LIVE!! BIG TIME!! HAHAHAHA!! LOL!! LOL!! L, O, L!!!” Young eyed Cylear. “Now kick his ass!” To the boos and cries of the Crowd, Cylear did. He bent down and hammered Christopher Fox until blood spat from his forehead, as it started to cover the ring. Shaw: “SEAN CYLEAR’S ALIVE!” Ump: “SHUT UP! WE KNOW!” “HE’S ALIVE!!” Young laughed like never before. “KICK HIS ASS DEAD-MAN... WAIT, ERECTION, KICK HIS ASS... ALIVE-MAN!! HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!” More blood spat out, as LLB tried to save his friend. He got to one knee, but Reed saw this, tapped Cylear on the shoulder, and pointed to him. ‘Showcase’ shook his head, stood up, and took the chair... SSSMMMACK! Ump: “THIS IS A FUCKING ONSLAUGHT! THEY SHOULD STOP THIS!!” The Fans still booed, as Reed Young lifted Christopher Fox up. “Hey, Sean... you forgot to superkick him!” WHACK. “That’s better.” As Cylear finished off his beatings against LLB and Fox, Reed had to play it for all it was worth one more time. “IT’S SEAN CYLEAR!! HE’S BACK... FROM THE DEAD!! HAHAHAHAHAHA!!” ‘Showcase’ closed the book on his beatings, as he lastly kicked LLB in the ribs and took Reed Young’s mic. The Crowd stopped booing, although the hate was still there. They just wanted to hear his voice again. “-” Young slapped the microphone out of his hand, to scoffs of the Crowd. “They can wait, Sean. THEY CAN ALL FUCKING WAIT!!” “Without Me” cued up again, as Young picked up the speaker, exiting the ring with Cylear. They left LLB and Fox there, to lay out cold... in giant puddles of their own blood. The Owner and newest member to the Action Wrestling tree got to the top of the ramp, before Reed turned around, and spoke a final time. “Muhahahaha. Welcome to your new hell Roland. And just to show you what you’re in for, at ‘Players and Pawns’... your best friend, Christopher Fox, will die first. Will die... facing Sean Cylear.” Young pounded the microphone to the floor, as it made a loud thumping noise over the PA. The Fans kept their jeers towards the two of them, as Young held up Cylear’s arm for all to see. The image faded; the evil grin of the ‘Showcase’ Sean Cylear was back. And as LLB and Christopher Fox lay motionless in piles of their own blood, it was badder than ever before. ---- Match of the Week: Dyad Championships Match of the Week: Dyad Championships Pat Hoskins stood in the middle of the ring and looked around at the crowd, the people of Ottawa had certainly put in a good showing. Apart from a few isolated seats in the upper deck, the arena was sold out and the fans that had paid their hard earned cash to be entertained were sure making a lot of noise. Off the back of Last Laugh it was becoming a night that no one would forget in a hurry. “Ladies and gentleman, the following tag team match is scheduled for one fall and it is for the Action! Wrestling Dyad Championships!” “Rock Star” by N.E.R.D. started to play, and this brought two well known faces from the back. “Introducing first, the challengers, at a combined weight of 400lbs and a very bad smell, Homeless Gus and Max, DANGER!” Somehow, even though Max had tried to clean him up over Thanksgiving, Gus was still smelling worse than ever, and fans held their nose as the harmless tyrant power walked to the ring. And tripped up over the ring steps. Max shook his head and climbed into the ring from the other direction. Winning back the titles he held so dear was going to be an arduous task. “And the Champions.” Announced Pat, while the lights dimmed down to a murky green over the stage. The music changed to “Freedom Fighter” by Creed, while water droplets fell onto the stage, illuminated blue by lights shining from underneath. “From London, England, at a combined weight of 486lbs, ‘Luscious’ Lee Lightning, ‘Daunting’ David Dew, the Action! Wrestling Dyad Champions, THE ELEMENTS!” Lee Lightning and David Dew pushed aside the curtains, and into the dark green they walked with the Dyad Championships around their waist. It was noticeable that David had the battered belt that Bobby Minio once held, but he didn’t care. It was what the belt represented that mattered. For five seconds both men looked up into the water that felt onto their faces, and Lee raised his arms to the sky. Finally, they advanced down the ramp and shook their faces to get rid of the water. Somewhere backstage the production team was congratulating themselves on the pun with the team name. The Elements both slid into the ring, and Max Danger slid out the back, not wanting any part of them until he absolutely had to. However, when he realized what he’d done he climbed onto the apron and tried to get back in, but the referee held him back. Sniggering, Lee Lightning also went out onto the apron. Max had just let Homeless Gus start off against ‘Daunting’ David Dew. “SHIT!” yelled Max Danger in anger, at both himself and Gus. He could already see his big chance to win back the Dyad Championships slipping away. Gus wasn’t about to back down. Clad in clothes not worthy of even being sold in Oxfam, Gus raised his right hand to the fans’ amusement. “Come on big man, not strong enough to take ol’ Gus?” taunted the homeless man. David, unlike everyone else in the arena, didn’t laugh. He just clutched Gus’ hand, and squeezed. And squeezed. Gus quickly dropped down to both knees and begged for mercy, while Max held his head in his hands on the apron. David Dew lifted Gus over his shoulder, and dropped him straight back down with a whiplash spinebuster that shook the ring. Shaking his head in pity, David lifted Gus straight back up and put his head between his legs. Why toy with the person when you can secure the match there and then? In one foul, swift motion, David Dew lifted Gus into the air and brought him straight back down with a powerbomb, that not only shook the ring, but the foundations it was built on. With a traditional cover aided by a forearm across Gus’ face, David went down to pick up the easy victory. One. Two. Not quite yet. Max Danger wasn’t prepared to give up his dream again, and he broke up the count with a stiff boot to David Dew’s face. He also made an equally quick exit, and stomped his feet on the mat, willing his unwilling tag partner to make the tag. Gus crawled across the ring, knowing that a few more steps would do it. On hands and knees he humbly made his way towards the corner, and reached up for the tag. But there was no hand there. ‘Luscious’ Lee Lightning had darted around the ring and pulled Max Danger’s legs out from under him, before hurriedly returning to his own corner and grabbing the tag rope before the referee could see anything. “Hehe, like you wouldn’t do the same to keep those titles!” retorted Lee to a front row man who disagreed with these tactics. And it was hard to argue with Lee’s argument. David Dew had used the time to drag Homeless Gus back into The Elements corner, and he followed it up with a tag. David held Gus in place, and Lee kicked him in the side, inflicting more pain. Gus wasn’t a trained wrestler, he wasn’t conditioned to take this kind of punishment. Lee followed it up with constant jabs to the rib sections, and whipped Gus into the ropes. Determined to do something useful, Gus ran as fast as he could towards Lee. And was beheaded with a clothesline from hell. “HOME RUN!” cried Lee, before ascending the ropes. If the clothesline didn’t end it, this moonsault certainly would. The ‘I’m Better Looking Than You’! Missed. Max Danger pulled Gus out of the way by the arm, and into his corner. Danger stepped back out onto the apron, slapped Gus on the back, and entered again. Ironically, the fans were cheering. ‘Luscious’ Lee Lightning charged, but was sent down with a flying knife edge chop. David Dew entered with a roar, and went back out just as fast after a dropkick sent him over the top rope and onto the floor below. Lightning was back to his feet, but walked straight into a superkick that connected hard with his jaw! One! Two! But David Dew dragged the referee out, before re-entering himself and taking aim with a massive lariat, that Max Danger ducked! DDT! Max went for the cover again, and waited for the count. Looking behind him though, the referee was standing idly with his arms crossed. Furious, Max nipped to his feet and questioned this bovine official. “David’s not the legal man Max, you know the rules! Not legal? No count!” Cursing, Max turned around and kicked David out of the ring, returning his attention to Lee Lightning who was struggling to get to his feet in the corner. With a head of steam, Max crushed him in the corner. Lee slumped down in the corner, sitting plump on his ass. Seeing the opportunity, Max dived forward with a shining wizard kick. And hit it! One! Two! Standing elbow drop! ON LEE LIGHTNING! Max Danger had rolled out the way and David Dew’s elbow pierced Lee’s forehead. Regretful about this, David looked to rectify the situation, and walked straight into a Max Danger Trifecta of suplexes. German suplex! Tiger suplex! Dragon suplex! David Dew rolled out onto the apron, wishing to have no more of the hot Max Danger. Danger didn’t want a part of Dew either though, as he wasn’t the legal man, and he couldn’t win the Dyad Championships off him. The man he could win them off of, was Lee Lightning. “Come on you democratic son of a bitch, you’re crap!” taunted Max, choking Lee on the bottom rope. The referee used his five count, and Max broke the illegal hold at four, but the damage was done. Still taunting, Max whipped Lee into the ropes. And missed a running senton. Max Danger immediately turned to tag in his partner, but his facial expression turned to one of horror when he saw the gleeful face of Homeless Gus awaiting him. Danger held out his hands as if to say ‘no way, I’m no tagging with you’. Gus pleaded, but it didn’t make any difference. Behind Max, Lee had crawled into his corner and tagged in his brother and co-champion, ‘Daunting’ David Dew. “No, I am not tagging you in! I want to win this match dammit!” stated Max Danger. David Dew sneaked up behind him, and pushed Max into Gus. “TAG!” announced the referee, as Max fell into Gus’ legs. When he stood up, he threw a tantrum. “BUT IT WASN’T MEANT TO BE A TAG!” shouted Max, going red in the face, but the referee slapped his hands together, indicating that the tag stood. Crestfallen, Max walked away, and straight into a David Dew big boot! Max fell down, and David Dew smelt blood. Homeless Gus didn’t want to come in anymore, but David dragged him over the ropes and into the center of the ring. He assumed a position behind Gus and applied a hold, before turning it into a throw. Gutwrench suplex! Gus’ body bounced limply off the bottom rope, and David Dew made the nonchalant cover. One! Max Danger ran to break up the count. Two! But Lee Lightning cut him off with a hip takedown. Three! The bell rang instantly. “The winners of this Match and STILL Dyad Champions... THE ELEMENTS!” The confrontation wasn’t over though, because as the Referee brought the Championship Belts into the ring, Max Danger went toe to toe with The Elements, who both stood taller than he. The situation was all set to explode when Max took both of the Dyad Championship belts and slung them around his wrists. Then he offered them to The Elements. And they accepted. It was like the passing of a torch, and it signaled the proper start of the Action! tag team divisions, with actual tag teams. “Take care of them, especially that clean one.” Max Danger pointed to the spotless belt that Lee held, rather than the scratched one that rested on the hefty shoulder of David Dew. Solemnly, Max Danger walked up the ramp, not looking back. The Dyad Championships were just not meant to be his. Sadly, he walked under the big screen and backstage, while “Freedom Fighter” by Creed played once more and The Elements celebrated. They’d just made their first successfully Title defense. Outcome: The Elements via Pinfall; Retain D Titles. ---- The Countdown is On? The Countdown is On? 25:02:43:25 25:02:43:24 25:02:43:23... ---- Interview: Sweet Aroma & Angelica Dawson. (The male contingent jumps to their feet hooping and hollering at the potential three letter acronym becoming an actuality.) Shaw: Please welcome one half of the ‘Clit ‘N Candy Express’, the first Dyad Champion, SWEET AROMA! (Aroma storms onto the set and slaps the taste out of Shaw’s mouth.) Shaw: ::Rubbing his cheek:: That was kind of a turn on! (Aroma gives Shaw a look of warning which prompts Derik to get back to the interview.) Shaw: And now, I’d like you all to give a warm welcome to, the other half of the ‘Clit ‘N Candy Express’, ANGELICA DAWSON! (Dawson storms onto the set and motions with her finger for Shaw to get close to her, which he does. He walks over to her, and just as quickly as he did, he drops to the ground grabbing his crotch, as Dawson’s knee was sure to meet the little Shaws’. Dawson then takes her seat while the female portion of the Crowd cheers at the actions of ‘CnC’.) Sweet Aroma: You go girl! Angelica Dawson: You go too, girl! (The two exchange a high five, while Derik Shaw starts to gather himself, while the male part of the Crowd goes over what they just witnessed.) Shaw: Girls, I just want to welcome you to... Aroma & Dawson: WHATEVER! (The girls giggle as Derik Shaw sits behind his desk a little ticked at the early goings of this interview.) Shaw: Yeah, yeah, yeah, Whatever! Can we just get started!? Aroma & Dawson: WHATEVER! Shaw: Funny, but can we get started please!? Aroma & Dawson: WHATEVER! Shaw: Enough of that! No wonder Bobby didn’t want to be on your team! Dawson: For you’re information, Bobby wanted to be on our team, but Veronica wanted Max Danger. Aroma: Hold on there Derik, don’t get me started on you. Number one, Max was a great partner, and Bobby would’ve been a great partner, but me and Max found some middle ground in the weeks leading up to ‘Last Laugh‘, where as I still hadn’t forgotten about what Bobby did to me the night Angelica and I lost the Dyad Titles. Aroma: Well, surprisingly this was something booked out of the “Hot Pink Binder”, but I’m sure Reed and Jeremy didn’t expect it to get over, seeing as the rest of the stuff in the “Hot Pink Binder”, like “The Groovement” never got off the ground, and that “HLA” they wanted us to do, was definitely not going to happen. Dawson: I would’ve loved to do it though. Shaw: How about now? Aroma: No. No. No! Dawson: Why not!? Aroma: Because I don’t swing that way! Dawson: I don’t know about Jonathan, but I’m sure Bobby wouldn’t mind. Bobby’s so understanding, he loves me, he really loves me. I love Bob- Aroma: Enough about Bobby! That’s it, I’ve had it, I want him in a Match this coming Pressure Point! Dawson: You beat Bobby? WHATEVER, girlfriend. Aroma: WHATEVER! WHATEVER! Shaw: Hey, that’s my li- **SMACK!!!** Aroma & Dawson: WHATEVER! (The tow girls then storm off the stage while Shaw is rubbing both sides of his face.) Shaw: WHATEVER! Just go to commercial! ---- Signoff. Signoff. Shaw: Well, there you have it. I guess Bobby Minio will take on Sweet Aroma in a Match that, well... it *could* see the break up of Dawson and Aroma, considering whose side I think Angelica will be on this coming Monday. That’s not one to miss. (Pause.) All in all, this is only an hour show... -I’ll have to bother Reed about more time-, but next week, I sit down with Coral Avalon... and give a full report upon this pending show, as well as many other things soon to come to the television screen. That’s all for now. Have a great weekend, and see you Monday. Boo yeah! (“Breathe” begins as Derik Shaw tosses his papers in the garbage and walks off the stage.)
