What this is Mark Shields’s card from the fed’s own Crew folder (referees, doctor and other staff), as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 7 January 2004. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is.
Mark Shields Real Name: Marcus D. Shields Also Known As: n/a Date of Birth: February 29th (leap day), 1981 Hometown: San Diego, California Height: 6'1" Weight: 223 lbs. Favorite Wrestler: Daniel Phillips (due to the fact that Phillips trained him) Least Favorite Wrestler: Anyone who happens to piss him off at the moment. Favorite Trademark Move: Royal Treatment Attributes Intelligence: Mark is more or less a brawn over brains sort of guy. He's muscle bound, and he's good enough to count to three. That's really all you need to know, outside of the fact that Mark makes some intensely dumb moves and tends to become very intense when people try to push him around. Bump Taking: Mark's an ex-wrestler, and he can bump pretty well. Counting Speed(out of 5 - which is the fastest): 2 Leniency: Mark won't count people out, but he also will not allow for weapons to be used. Ease of Retaliation: Mark is pretty much the short fuse of the six referees of Action! Wrestling. He's a fairly intense individual and he becomes real biased REAL quick if people attack him. And mind you, Mark is the most durable of the referees. Eye Sight: He's blind as a fucking bat sometimes, and easilly distracted. He sometimes might miss a foot on the ropes or blatant cheating. Biography Was trained by Daniel Phillips as a wrestler. Wrestled extensively in the Southwest Wrestling Alliance, alongside fWo's Allen Speck and Leigh Landers. Injuries have forced him to take a job that requires less wrestling. Like refereeing. © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
