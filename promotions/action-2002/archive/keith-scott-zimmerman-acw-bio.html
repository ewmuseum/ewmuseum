Keith Scott Zimmerman Biography Nicknames KSZ, the Dean of the Digital Mafia, the Doctor of Smarkanomics, the NEW Ratings Draw, the Whole Workrate Show, Version 1.5... Hometown Berkeley, CA Birthday 30.10.82 Height 5'8' Weight 163 of twisted steel & two title toting appeal Fed Titles current Bantam Champion, half of the current Dyad Champions (first person in Action! history to hold these belts simultaneously) Fed Awards nominated for Most Improved Biography It's mercy, compassion, and forgiveness I lack. Not rationality. --The Bride, Kill Bill volume 1 My early life was damn boring standard middle-class existence. I skipped kindergarten, which was a mistake because if I'd known there'd be naps, cookies, and milk with only four hours of 'education' I would've been all over that like LLB on the book. Anyway, I liked wrestling since I was a kid, with the first match I remember being the ***** WMIII Savage/Steamboat classic. I was almost never a fan of the big guys (keep in mind this was the 80s so they'd proliferated across the WWF landscape like bunnies) and I was also a fan of the bad guys too (still team up as the late great Mr. Perfect & Ted DiBiase when I play WrestleFest--the Perfect Millionaires! ), and no one else at my school was. I guess once you're a smart, there's no way out. Obviously, like many smarts, I got into the Torch & Observer. I started up a site when I was 14 (Miracle Ecstasy Weekly [MEW]) and in the course of my travels of covering shows, managed to piss off a shitty roidhead excuse for a wrestler. You know, 'Blah blah you can't do any better like to see you try you fuck mispelled word incorrect punctuation'. I was pretty bored and it was summer. So I learned. Turns out I'm pretty decent. Scrap that--I'm fucking good. And for everyone who only sees a writer, a poseur, a string bean, a geek, or a nerd? You're about to get a harsh lesson in the realities of the new millennium. Because I'm part of Wrestling 101. So get on the bus... 'cause we're taking your ass to school. Other Feds Bay Area Wrestling, IWO Wrestling Style 70% cruiser, 30% American Strong Style Finisher Move (IWC) Denouement (singles) the markOUT Finisher Description (IWC) modified Tidal Wave: Frenchie gullotine legdrop and KSZ swandive headbutt from same corner (singles) Orange Crush: suplex turned sitout pinning powerbomb Special Moves leaping uppercut (SHORYUKEN~!) Entrance Music When wrestling as half of the International Workrate Connection or as a part of Wrestling 101, he'll enter to their theme
