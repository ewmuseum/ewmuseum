He took to the ropes... and nailed Anthason with another ‘Sure Shot’. But as the Referee fell to the floor... did he? Anthason had actually pulled James Jack in front of him, and at the last possible second was able to catch Fission’s boot, too. Damn... what luck. Anthason roared forward, grabbing Syphon off-balance at he did, nailing him with another knee lift. Fission snapped back, as Anthason took hold of him and planted him with an implant DDT. The Fans moaned... now it was time for Anthason to try and take the victory. ‘The Slight of Mind’. Cue the new Referee. One. Two. Three. “NO!” The Ref screamed at the top of his lungs. “NO!! Fission kicked out.” So there was still life. The both of them rose, as Fission looked at Anthason, and Anthason looked at Fission. Not knowing what to do... they just stood there. There was no way either man was going to move in for a grapple... there could be another low blow. After a second or two passed, both of them went into the ropes... Jumped... And crushed each other’s necks with a double flying clothesline. The hang-time was a brilliant thing to watch, in this, as both of them literally stopped once their opponent’s arm was draped around their necks, only to crash down to the mat in a heap. Anthason put his hand over Fission. But Fission did the same to Kent Anthason. The Referee shrugged. He couldn’t count this... for he would only count them out. Once at ten, he called for the bell... and that cued nothing but boos. He left the ring in a hurry... he didn’t want either of the wrestlers to know what he did... so it was best to hightail it as quick as he could. Hoskins spoke. “This Match... has been ruled... a draw!” There was really nothing more that could be said. In the case of Joey Malone and Kent Anthason at ‘Last Laugh’, once again, nothing had been proved. Nothing. And that damn well sucked.
