Merely shifting his weight to one side and rocketting a shoulder off the canvas was painful. Frank stood up and moved Garvin back into the ropes. He Irish whipped him to the ropes. But as 'the Original' rebounded off, he collapsed. Frank bent over to pick him up off the canvas but just then, he came to life, rolling him up in a small package for a quick 2¾ before the kick out. Garvin slowly got up and swung a right. Heimdal ducked, then rose up to catch him in the set up for the Full Tilt Uranage Suplex! The fans went nuts. The finish was mere seconds away. Garvin elbowed him in the side of the head, spinning out. As he came back around, Heimdal fired a standing front kick. Jeff caught it and countered with a Dragon Screw Legwhip. He stood up, wishboning the legs. It was time a headbutt to the abdomen. Like clock work, right on cue. Garvin spread the legs apart, but as he dropped his forehead down, Heimdal shut his legs and clamped on a vice; scissoring his head in a very homo-erotic MMA-style submission hold. Frank rolled over. Now with Jeff on his back, and Frank mounted on his chest, he punched him in the nose. Then again in the upper lip, followed by three hard head-butts. More blood, please! Still mounted, Frank decided to go back to his old shoot fighting days. He slid both hands behind Garvin's head, locked them together, and violently pulled Garvin's head up to his chest; bending it in a way that it strained the neck and lower back. The triangle neck lock had Jeff's hand hovering dangerously close to the canvas, ready to tap out if neccessary. He pulled his head up father, torquing it as he arched his own back. Garvin groaned loudly, before he kneed Heimdal in the lower back repeatedly with bent knees. In a laying position, they were rendered very weak- but they worked. Heimdal dropped his head, stood up, and waited for Garvin. Jeff stood up, still keeled over holding the back of his neck and clutching his back. Heimdal stepped forward to take him by the hair. No. A chop from Jeff Garvin lit him up and tanned his chest blood red. CHOP! CHOP! CHOP! Three more. They had moved across the ring and now were standing in front of the ropes. Jeff whipped him- Reversed. Garvin rebounded off. He leapt in the air as he came back, looking for a Cross Body. Heimdal drop kicked him in the chest, dropping down to the canvas on his side/shoulder. A very realistic looking bump taken from a man who's back seriously needed surgery at this point. Frank wasted no time, quickly pulling Jeff off the mat. He suplexed him over, looking for a Spin-Out Dangerous Brainbuster, only to have Garvin slip out the back door, landing on his feet behind him. Garvin snapped him onto his shoulders with a Dragon Suplex straight into the bridge. 1! 2! Dropped? Faint cheers rumbled in the crowd. They were confused. Heimdal's shoulders were pinned to the mat, and he drops the bridge? Jeff rolled away, mumbling something about his back. When he bridged the move, it curved his spine- a real no-no when you're in the shape he is. Staggering to his feet, Heimdal backed into Jeff... Bad move. ROARING ELBOW! Dangerous III. As Frank stumbled backwards after getting up from the rapid Dragon Suplex, still a bit shook up, Garvin turned the ol' 360 degrees and BOOM! Dangerous III to the back of Frank's head. The crowd gasped. Frank could have had a possible concussion. Upon being nailed with the stiff elbow, his body went fully limp, his head shot forward like a rag doll, and he collapsed to the mat. Sickening to watch. Jeff was slow to his feet as he had been the entire match. He staggered backwards into a nearby turnbuckle. He pushed up to the second rope and came back off with a Second Rope Front-Elbow to the noggin of Frank Heimdal! Cover. 1! 2! ¾ Heimdal kicked out, causing the Conseco Field House to irrupt in cheers. Sweat glistened on Garvin's upper body. He couldn't remember when he had been this exhausted. The constant pain was zapping his strength. Never-the-less, Jeff bent over and peeled the crowd favourite off the mat. He spat some blood out, then took Frank by the hair and walked him to corner. Happily, Jeff grinned, whispering something in his right ear before ramming his head down into the turnbuckle pad. After seating him on the top rope, Jeff stepped out onto the apron and climbed up. With both men up top, Heimdal started to recover. He pounded the Original's body with rights. Garvin began crawling back down, because, well, when the going gets tough- the Garvin gets going! He was standing on the second 'rung' of the turnbuckle, reaching down with his left foot for the apron, when Frank turned and SHOVED him off! "OOOOH!" Was the crowd's only response. Jeff had landed on his back, out in front of the announcer's table. His head bounced off the thin black mat. Frank climbed down to the ring. Nearly half a minute later, Garvin just began to stir. His head ached, his back- well, you know the story... Anyway, he grabbed ahold of the announce table and stood up. He didn't move. He stood next to the table, one hand planted on it to steady him in case his legs gave way. Heimdal reached through the ropes as Jeff came back to the ring. Garvin hit him with an elbow smash for good measure, then climbed up onto the ring apron. Groggily, Jeff threw elbows into Heimdal's face- bouncing them off his jaw, nose, and forehead. Reaching over, his hand held the back of Heimdal's neck. Turning him, he ran with Frank... WHAM! Head meets turnbuckle. Frank staggered around, until he came back around to face the leader of W101. Through the ropes, he thrusted a shoulder into Heimdal's mid section, and bent him over. Grabbing his head with both hand, Jeff pulled his upper body through the middle rope. He pulled his entire body out onto the apron and stood him up. Deep breath now, and liiiiift... Up- and throw! He lifted him up with a Kneebreaker, ya' know... with the knee bent and all, then took a single step and slammed Heimdal's knee into the outside ring post! Thud. THUD. The first; the sound of Jeff toppling over, tired and in no shape to stand, and hitting the mat below. The second; Heimdal falling off to his side after having shin forced into exposed steel, then landing on his head below. There was no quick way to recover here. It had taken more than enough out of both competitors. History would show that under normal circumstances, after landing such a big momentum-shifter like a Kneebreaker into the turnbuckle, Jeff Garvin would follow up with something as equally painful. Not tonight. First things first, like an old woman with a severe slouching problem since kindergarten, Jeff moved over to the announcers’ table, hunched over. He was searching for something. A steel chair, maybe? Ring bell? DING? No. A glass of water. "Gimme," Jeff uttered in a dry, raspy toned. He grabbed Liam Ump's bottle of mineral water and screwed off the top. While leaning against the table, he chugged down the water down as Derik Shaw laughed it up. Garvin turned his head. Heimdal was standing. He was holding his knee, but he was standing! Garvin moved around the table cautiously, hands up- one open, one with the water bottle clutched in it. "Wait now, Frankie... We can call this a-" Water bottle- ducked. It ricocheted off the turnbuckle post and flew into the crowd. Back to Garvin, Frank turned. WATERYOUFACE!? Jeff had stored a cheek full of water. As Heimdal turned to face him, Garvin sprayed it in his face- blinding him for only about a second. But it was a second too late. Garvin charged in and chop blocked Heimdal's knee- the one he had Grapevined earlier, and also drop kicked. Frank fell flat on his face. Garvin stood up, grabbed it, elevated it, and drove it into the floor! You better believe the fans didn't appreciate that. Garvin picked him up and set him up for a suplex. Or did he? He moved him back, his head tucked under the arm, until they were standing beside the steel steps. Garvin closed his eyes. Gordbuster on the steps! Garvin couldn't fully invert him upside down, so instead he just made sure his feet were off the ground and fell forward. Heimda's lowerbody struck the awkward shaped steel structure. The edge of the top step gouged the side of his knee joint and he screamed in pain. Garvin, himself, turned and slumped down against the steps, his back to the steel. Holy F'N Christ. He needed sleep. The crowd chanted 'HOLY SHIT!' then simply booed. Garvin sneered, flipping off the crowd. Jeff marched up the steps. He grabbed ahold of Heimdal's ankle like before- lift... UP! DOWN! UP! DOWN! He wasn't content with just driving his knee into the steel once, no... twice was better. And you know what? So was three! UP! DOWN! Heimdal's knee was a wreck. Jeff brought him over to the announce table and snap suplex him against the side, so that only his bad knee hit the edge. Liam Ump screamed like a woman and called Jeff a horrible bastard. You know, the usual? Frank laid on the cement floor in the fetal position, grasping his knee. Garvin rolled Heimdal back in the ring and propped himself onto the apron. He laid there, breathing. Then rolled inside. He went straight for the lateral press. 1! 2! Kickout at 2½. Not bad. Jeff stood Frank up. Inside leg trip, followed by a stiff elbowed to the inner thigh. Garvin stood up, but paused. Fuck, the back pain wouldn't let up. He exhaled and said 'Fuck it', then drove another elbow down into the joint. Finally, he stood up and crossed the legs. HAMMER JAMMER! He just needed to turn him over and wait for those three beautiful taps. Heimdal wrapped his arm around Garvin's step-over leg. He couldn't budge it. So he switched legs, and Heimdal used his other arm to do the same to that leg. They were deadlocked. Garvin dropped the legs, leapt into the air and came down with the FISTS OF STONE. Jeff's fist was driven deep into Heimdal's heart. Frank convulsed. The crowd knew it was over. He was out cold. Garvin had never used a Fist Drop variation of the FoS. But now was as good of a time as any. Jeff collected the legs, one by one... Cross 'em, turn over, and sit back. The Hammer Jammer was synched in. No escape. Garvin wrenched back on the legs and sat down on the lower-back. He punched at the injured knee of Heimdal, taking special care to grind into the knee-cap after ever strike. But wait. No. NO! Indianapolis was coming unglued. Heimdal was pushing up from the Jammer. After almost a minute inside the devastating hold, Frank actually found the strength to push-up. But it wasn't from inside himself. It was from the people. They were feeding him their energy; their strength... And HEIMDALMANIA WAS RUNNIN' WIIIILD! Garvin toppled forward onto his stomach. He struggled to his feet, but got dropped with a lariat. Back up. BOOM! LARIAT! Dazed, Jeff again, despite being knocked off his feet two consecutive times, was fighting to get up and recieved again. Frank came running. Lariat. Blocked. Countered with a Tornado Single Arm DDT! STF Submission. Seconds afters the DDT, Jeff applied the STF. He wanted to make Heimdal tap out in front of all his fans. It felt like an eternity inside the dread hold. Few people could make a near three minute submission hold interesting, but the fans were so into the match, they hardly noticed. Heimdal broke loose in a single burst of adrenaline. His knee wasn't in good shape, but he didn't exactly care at the moment. He crawled on his hands and knees away from the expert submissionist who suddenly just seemed to have revived himself. Heimdal was up. Garvin, without a thought in his head, sprinted towards him. New plan: knock Frank Heimdal out cold with a second Roaring Elbow. Actually, no. Not a 2nd Roaring Elbow... A 360 Spin Fists of Stone! Death was on its way. He spun around, and knew things hadn't gone his way. Heimdal had stepped to the side and extended his weak knee. KITCHEN SINK! Jeff ran into it and flipped violently over. Frank listened to the fans. They were once again chanting his name. And he liked it. It fuelled him. He reached over and pull Garvin up to a vertical base by his tights. He lifted Jeff up, took a few steps, and dropped him to the mat with a Sidewalk Slam. Luckily for 'the Original', he head landed beside the ropes. Jeff knew that all Frank had to do was apply a simple Camel Clutch and he'd be tapping. It was either that, or risk a broken back. Jeff grabbed a hold of the ropes and dragged himself out to the floor, out of Heimdal's reach. Safety, thank God. Not for long. Heimdal stepped outside and walked over to his opponent. He collected Garvin off the mat. LOW BLOW! Garvin stood up and capitalised on the doubled-over Heimdal. MEMPHIS. DEATH. CERTIFICATE. Piledriver on the floor. Neither man moved. Jeff was still trying to recover from the Sidewalk Slam and Frank had just had his head spiked into solid cement. Jeff had changed the MDC back from the Ganso Bomb to the Piledriver after taking heat backstage for using it. It made sense, though. In Memphis, Jeff's hometown, a piledriver was once as lethal as a gun shot wound. At least, in Jerry Lawlers hay-day. To his feet, Jeff climbed. He wasn't sure of his next step, but he was pretty certain he wanted it to involve a little table breakage. He laid Heimdal up on the English announce table and climb up himself. Heimdal was stood up. Jeff behind him. ORIGINAL SLAM! Heimdal slipped behind him. He spun Garvin around, and into the... CRAAAAAAAAAAAASH!!! The table exploded. Full Tilt Uranage Slam. "HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!" Oh, and... "HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!" Much better. Jeff was sprawled out on the floor; a piece of the table covering his face. Meanwhile, Heimdal wasn't moving either. Blood was leaking from Garvin's mouth, staining the ground. Heimdal got up, and pulled Jeff by wrist, dragging him back to the ring. He rolled him inside. Cover. 1! 2! 3! ... NO! SHOULDER! Heimdal had taken far too much time getting Jeff in the ring. Garvin’s eyes open. He blinked. “I want to die.” Frank’s face came into his view. He smiled. “Please do.” Though Jeff did infact get his shoulder up, that does not mean he is able to stand under his own will power. Frank bent down and brought him to his feet. He had no idea where he was; no sense of ring awareness. Frank sunk his fist deep in his stomach, knocking the wind out of him. He then scooped Jeff up onto his left shoulder. He took a few steps, attempting to drive Garvin's head into the mat with the Cranial Smoke. Before he could complete the Fire Thunder Driver, though, Jeff struggled out from his grip and landed behind him. He kicked Heimdal's bad knee out from under him and he fell down onto his stomach. Garvin quickly stepped in and applied the Hammer Jammer with his last bit of strength. There was no need to turn Heimdal over, as he was already lying flat on his stomach. He struggled, pounding the mat as he tried to reach the ropes before Garvin finished crossing his legs over one another and synching it in. He was only inches from the ropes, when Jeff walked him back to the center of the ring and sat down. The fans were absolutely roaring; they were booing so loud. In the bleachers, they stomped the flooring. Praying that Heimdal would some how hold on until he could reach the ropes. But every time he began to crawl, Jeff walked him back. Sat down. And synched it in tighter than before. Like he did earlier, he pummelled the kneecap with his fists. Heimdal's leg was a mess. He knew there was only one option. Three lethargic pats with an open hand to the sweat-stained canvas. Tap. Tap. Tap. It was over. Ryan Watts called for the bell. "Champion" by Grinspoon cued up. Garvin dropped the hold and fell to his knees, exhausted. He'd need medical attention. The referee raised his arm in the air. The crowd were upset that Frank had lost, but understood that submitting was the only choice he had. They respected him for it. The Conseco Field House were on their feet giving both men a standing ovation for the outstanding match they had just witnessed. Two men had given their bodies, hoping it would pay off in the end. Jeff had been lucky. It could have gone either way. He didn't offer Frank a handshake as he got to his feet. It wasn't in him. But he did respect Frank. And having his respect meant something. Jeff collapsed from the ring. Julie, his wife, and the rest of Wrestling 101 had filed down the ramp. They offered to help him to the back but he refused. The fans hated and loved him at the same time. It was a moment of pure ecstasy for Jeff Garvin. As he staggered to the back, he turned to face the ring. Heimdal was standing, glaring at him. He was leaning to his side, keeping his weight of his possibly injured knee. Still selling it. Jeff had won the match, but he hadn't killed his spirit. Garvin smirked, then left through the curtains. Victory was his tonight. But the road to the Action! World Heavyweight Championship was still far from over... Outcome: Garvin via submission.
