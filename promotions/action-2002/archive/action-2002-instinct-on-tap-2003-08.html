Action! — Pay-per-view preview

Instinct: On Tap

August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The site's own On Tap page for Instinct (31 August 2003), the announced card with a build-up for each match, from the Wayback capture of 26 October 2003. The show's own card page on this wing records its results as not located.
Pepsi Arena in Denver, Colorado 08/31/03 'The Original' Jeff Garvin vs. Frank Heimdal Coral Avalon (B) vs. Max Danger (C) Confrontation. Why has he chosen this time to strike? Everyone knows the story... once upon a time, in the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling, LLB was the World Heavyweight Champion. But Chris Mezz, one of LLB’s many rivals, ended up beating him for it. And even though, when PIW closed, Chris Mezz was able to walk out with the World Title, it was LLB who got the last laugh on him, at the Pay-Per-View entitled ‘Drastic Measures 2’, where LLB teamed with Christopher Fox to beat Chris Mezz and Bilko the Enforcer. This, clearly, upset the ‘Crazy One’. He was unable to finish LLB off once and for all - a task that many have tired (Chris Fear, KroW, Sean Cylear) but all have failed. So why has he chosen now to strike? Two years have passed since there was any animosity between LLB or Chris. Yet, Mezz has brought it upon himself to appear three times in the past four weeks to somehow interfere with LLB, including a brutal attack on the last Pressure Point. This Sunday, they will meet. Face-to-face for the first time in two *exact* years. May the Jury be seated... this trial isn’t going anywhere. Daniel Phillips vs. Rook Black Conrad Ramsey vs. Bradley Duncan Arcade & Son (D) vs. International Workrate Connection Ever since Juggernaut II, the father/son tandem out of Los Angeles has made their mark in the tag team division by defeating all comers who've opposed them from any league. But the greatest challenges come from within, and at Instinct Wrestling 101 hopes to hold two championships by the end of the night. They've sent living legend the Flying Frenchie and hot rookie Keith Scott Zimmerman after the D belts, and in brief flashes they've shown impressive teamwork. At Instinct, can the IWC win the belts in their first tag team match together? Or will the Arcades add the biggest notch on their belt to date? Sharc vs. Sylo, Trey Vincent & Reed Young Reed Young, retaliating to unprofessional behavior by Sharc (mentioning PIW! How dare he!) has taken it upon himself to make Sharc's life a living hell. Trey Vincent has proved to be a man Sharc can't beat, but to make things even harder for Sharc, he must now fight Sylo and Trey Vincent. Possibly even somebody else before he can get to Reed Young. You'll have to find out at the PPV if anybody else might get thrown in. 'Impulse' Brian James vs. Ninja K IBJ. Ninja K. Battles across 2 years come to a head at the PPV. Brian James has been attacking Ninja K under the eye of Jeff Garvin. Ninja K found out and now the two will fight at Instinct! Ninja K leads the all time series 4-3. Can he end the series at 5-3, or will "The Firestarter" tie the series and leave the wrestling community questioning who is the better wrestler? Christopher Fox vs. Woodchuck Bill Sometimes it’s not just winning or losing that makes or breaks a wrestler. Sometimes, it’s just actually doing something. Come Instinct, that’s what Woodchuck Bill will be trying to do. Who’s Woodchuck Bill? What does he do? But most importantly... who has he beat? Not many if anyone is counting. The legend in Christopher Fox returned two weeks ago, getting in the way of Woodchuck Bill’s Match against Action Man. This, in turn, meant nothing to Christopher. He saw there was a chance to grab the spotlight... so he went for it. Angered, Bill has now challenged Christopher Fox to a Match at the Pay-Per-View. But will Bill even last a minute? Come Sunday, Christopher Fox will be acting as a wrestler, and a Judge, in the all-new Impression Match. If Christopher feels that Woodchuck Bill is not putting up a fair-enough battle... he can call the Match, and award himself the victory. An odd stipulation to say the least, but it’s the only way that Fox would accept Woodchuck Bill’s offer. This Sunday: A legend against a nobody. In Bill’s mind, it’s time to make his impression. Hardcase vs. Deft Bobby Minio vs. Jonathan Scott Clayton Prodigy vs. Sweet Aroma Ever since Sweet Aroma made her return she has proven to be a thorn in the side of Clayton Prodigy. Clayton has tried to do everything in his power to eliminate Aroma, but has had no luck so far. Prodigy has promised to rid Action of Sweet Aroma, but thus has failed in every attempt. At Instinct, Clayton Prodigy meets Sweet Aroma in a ‘2/3 falls’ match that will prove to be the culmination of a hate filled feud. The Ringer vs. Action Man Guest Referee: Kayden Paulton! In what has to be said as one of the most interesting Matches on the card, The Ringer will do battle with Action Man. In what has become utter hell for our hero, AM has been countlessly put in his place by The Ringer. The ‘Prototype’ of Action has made it his job to ridicule and torment Action Man’s problem... the fact that he is unrendered. And this does not sit well with AM. Before, he was a joke... but there is only so much one man can take. Attempting suicide, countless emotional fits, and Action Man is, if not past the verge of insanity. In entered Kayden Paulton, the ultimate nice-guy. Hell, he’s so nice, he tried to cure Action Man’s depression as he sold every single right hand that Action Man threw at him during their Match last week. That was until The Ringer got in the way, beat them both up, and allowed Paulton to beat Action Man... something in which Kayden would not have done if he was in control. But it didn’t stop there. The Ringer then booked this Match at Instinct, and made Kayden the Guest Referee. Why? Look at it this way. Paulton LOVES everyone. He is sure to call the Match right down the middle. If The Ringer pins Action Man (and he will - AM has a arsenal of one move), Kayden will count to three. This, no doubt, will anger Action Man. It will be another loss, and it will turn Action Man against the ‘Passive-Aggressive’. To add to this, rumor has it that Action Man has asked for Kayden to be bias towards himself, and let AM steal the victory. One can only wonder how the hell this Match will end up. But at least, on Paulton’s end, he’ll have a lot of fun! © Action Wrestling 2002-2003
