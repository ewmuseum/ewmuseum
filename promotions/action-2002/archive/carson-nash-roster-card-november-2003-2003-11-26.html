Injuries Current Injuries: N/A Nagging Injuries: Lower Back Problems (stemming from match with General David Sanders), Neck Surgery in 2000 History Years Pro: 1 Manager: N/A Allies: Colin Gear Nemesis: Mitre Gooding (of Risk fame) Current Team: N/A Past Teams: N/A Action Wrestling Title History: N/A Action Wrestling Award History: N/A Other Federations: thReat, Risk Other Federation Awards: Uncrowned Risk World Champion Attributes Intelligence: 8 Strength: 7 Toughness: 8 Speed: 5 Endurance: 7 Courage: (stupidity?) 6 Charisma: 8 Skill: 8 Biography Christopher Carson Nash was born to Ronald and Jaime (Taylor) Nash at St. Joseph’s Memorial Hospital in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada at 12:31 PST on July 1st, 1981. After 8 hours of labor, the Nash family were proud parents of what was to be their only son. They made a pact that morning to do their best to have the best for their son. Jaime had worked as an aerobics instructor before quitting after the birth of her son and Ronald worked as a judge. The isolated mansion in Vancouver was usually the sight of the Nash family as Ronald, Jaime, and their son, whom preferred the name Carson instead of the common name of Chris, as they stayed together, closely knit as a family. As started previously, the mansion that Carson grew up in was isolated, away from the city life. Carson started kindergarten at the age of 5, where he first experienced interaction with children his own age. He treated these kids as if they were full-grown adults showing nothing but courtesy and respect but the actions were not returned. Carson was looked upon as a “loser” whom never went outside. On Carson’s tenth birthday, he waited outside of his school for 2 hours as nobody showed to pick him up. Mrs. Nash was nowhere to be found. Carson waited and waited until finally his ride showed up. As the police car drove Carson Nash home, he spotted tons of ambulance, police and fire vehicles. Immediately, Carson broke down into tears. While preparing for the party that was to be thrown for their son, Ronald and Jaime Nash both were the victim of glock shots to the head. Daniel Vance, their butler, quickly turned the gun on himself after murdering his employers. Carson inherited 350,000$ for himself and the rest of the money that belonged to his parents was never seen again. Nash moved in with foster parents in Ventura, California where he adopted a “I don’t give a fuck” attitude. He was angry about everything that had happened. After 6 years with his foster parents, Carson rented an apartment in Valley Christian, California. While flipping through television channels, Carson happened to see an advertisement which advertised a wrestling school. Carson wanted to do something physical where he could let out his anger, but he didn’t want to hurt anyone. He figured that he might as well just give wrestling a shot. Nash went to Valley Christian Wrestling School where he met the Legend of Rick. To this day, Carson still doesn’t know much about tLOR but is he glad he met the guy. Rick trained him, got him in great shape, and got him a job with his first local indy fed. After 2 years under Rick’s guidance, Carson finally was ready for wrestling. While training, he met fellow wrestler trainee Amy Michaels. During his 2 years of training, Amy stopped training, moved in with Carson, and caused him to fall in-love with her. But Amy broke off all ties with Carson and mysteriously vanished. Carson became a bitter, angry man. Nash now wanted to hurt someone. However, he was the one that was hurt on a backdrop went wrong. Nash landed on his neck, and was rushed to the hospital. Two days later, Nash had surgery. After training to become a boxer with cousin, Xavier Nash, Carson decided to give wrestling one more shot when he was contacted by promotion Risk. There, Nash met Steven Hunt, and became very good friends with him. After a stint and a feud with Mitre Gooding, Risk closed down as Nash became a well liked indy wrestler over the internet. During his time in Risk, Carson developed a cocky attitude as he was overly pushed by Steven Hunt. Nash debuted with the company thReat facing thReat World Champion, General David Sanders. Nash came within .2 seconds of becoming the thReat World Champion, but fell short to David Sanders that night. Now, Nash, still bitter and angry, has been brought into Action! Wrestling by Reed Young, who was looking to get a ratings push much like thReat had after signing Nash. However, Carson Nash wishes that all Action! fans forget about his past as he is trying so hardly to do. The future, Nash insists, is what matters. Action!
