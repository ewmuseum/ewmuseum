The Action! Awards 2003 Bill's Tavern (basement) ---- The First Annual Awards Show. The First Annual Awards Show. The Action Wrestling Signature Introduction played, followed by the Pressure Point theme song “Boom” by P.O.D.. The cameras cut to the basement of Bill’s Tavern... a rotten, disgusting, hole in the wall (no, literally, as there were about ten or fifteen holes in the walls) with dirt covering everything... and rust practically thriving off of the metal chairs. And there, all the Action Wrestlers sat. Some with their families, some with other wrestlers... but all with sick looks on their faces, as if they still didn’t believe the first annual awards were held in a place like this. But then again, when Reed Young left the booking of this show to the last minute (the very last minute) what was to be expected, anyway? The stage was even worse than the area below it. The roof hung lower than normal, and every so often big chunks of dirt would fall from the ceiling and create a pile of dust (along with a massive dust cloud) throughout the stage. And if it was a big pile of dirt that had fallen... the dust cloud might have even worked its way into the seating area. “Boom” still played as the cameras just panned around the room, catching everyone in sight. This basement was the home to Pressure Point 35, where Reed Young held auditions for anyone to become the “next LLB”. And now, now it was the host to the Action Wrestling Awards. “Boom” slowly died down, and then the room went to a complete and utter silence. All the wrestlers just looked around at each other. LLB shrugged when most of the eyes fell on him... Christopher Fox then giggled when most of the eyes fell on him next...
