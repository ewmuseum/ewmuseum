Edguardo Biography Nicknames Beaner, spick, bean dip, la imigra...unlike Diablo, Edguardo doesn't care because he lacks the mental capacity. Hometown Tijuana, Mexico Birthday 5-12-1982 Height 5'10' Weight 205 Biography (For more information, see Diablo's biography.) Edguardo was born somewhat messed-up. But nobody has been able to figure out why... He is slavishly loyal to Diablo, but he has the mind of a 12-year-old. As a result, Diablo has to think for Edguardo most often. Edguardo's two main interests are sex and sex. This is why he humps stuff and is managed by a colony of Lesbians. But strangely, when Edguardo goes into a wrestling match, he becomes a sometimes unstoppable, always amusing force. Whatever causes Edguardo to be messed-up has no effect on his in-ring abilities. EDGUARDO'S PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION Edguardo has puce-dyed dreadlocks. He wears Hanson Brothers-like glasses, just because (think about the Dudleys). He always wears a white 'Insufficient Memory At This Time' T-shirt with light blue shorts, green argyle knee socks and brown leather cowboy boots. Other Feds IWO Wrestling Style Lucha Libre/whatever Finisher Move The Spick Kick Finisher Description Bicycle Enziguri kick Special Moves Mexican Fortune Cookie -- Hangman's neckbreaker to Evenflow DDT (Edguardo sets up the hangman's neckbreaker, but snaps their head back and drops them into the DDT. This move is known to cause whiplash of the type most commonly seen in car crashes.) Entrance Music 'Violent Mood Swings' by Spineshank
