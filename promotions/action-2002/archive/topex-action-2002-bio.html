Topex Biography Nicknames Jac [But only to Le'Java] Hometown Provence, France Birthday February 17th, 1970 Height 6’2” Weight 237 Biography Unlike his tag team partner, Jacques Fazaro’s/Topex’s history is a bit less screwed up. Raised by a working-class family, who would later become one of France’s richest family’s, Fazaro lived in a hard-working, honest environment. An average student in both primary and secondary school, he entered the world of work at the age of 18 with a couple of decent passes from his final exams. His father, a carpenter in Paris, asked him to come along with him to work prior to him finishing school. The day after school ended, an 18 year old French boy made the transition from school boy to working man, travelling for 2 hours each day to Paris and working at least 10 to 12 hours a day. Before long, his small schoolboy frame had filled out and he looked more like an apprentice than a recently educated teenager. The experience with his father was enjoyable in a couple of ways, fact being he learned a good, honest trade and could spend more time with his father, an act he didn’t have much time to do as a child. The bond between the two men grew and after a year or so of working with his father, Jacques felt like he’d known his dad forever. This feeling, unfortunately, was cut short but a few weeks later...Working on the site of a newly constructed housing estate, the father and son team worked on laying floors and other general carpentry jobs around the site. But as he went to cross the room to the window to let in a fresh breeze, the freshly-laid floorboards under Mr. Fazaro Snr.’s feet gave way and as he thrashed his arms in the air, he fell two stories to his disastrous death. All Jacques could do was watch his father plummet as he tried uneventfully to get to his father’s aid. Dying instantly, the father-figure of Jacques’s life was gone in an instant and all the anger and depression in the world was imbedded into Jacques’s mind, reason being he thought he was to blame for the poor floor condition. Isolating himself from the rest of his family, except for his father’s funeral, Jacques became determined not to let his father’s death plague him. As quickly as he started, Jacques left the carpeting trade and focused his energies on providing for his mother and two sisters. Working 2 jobs, one during the day and one at night, Jacques aged extremely quickly, 4 to 5 hours of sleep per night not helping the situation. By 25, Jacques had secured a position in a building firm [Funnily enough, Jacques returned to the building trade a few years after his father’s departure]. Working 9 hour shifts each day, Monday to Friday, Jacques became his father. This began to bug him, and the fact that his mother and sisters were relying on him really put pressure on the young man. Deciding it was time to do what he wanted to do, he gave up his position in the building firm for a smaller, part-time office job in the same company. In his spare time, Jacques followed his boy-hood dream of becoming a professional athlete. Trying his hand at football/soccer, athletics, and numerous other sports, Jacques never found his true calling until he stumbled upon an amateur wrestling club just a few miles from his family home, the sport he had enjoyed as a boy at school. Training vigorously, the now 28 year-old had a body of a professional swimmer, but his height restricted him slightly, now standing at 6’0”, he wasn’t the tallest there was. But his training paid off, and by the time he started competing, he weighed in just under 220 lbs and stood at nearly 6’2”. His name began to become common on the amateur wrestling circuit and soon, the young man from Provence was a popular local competitor. This is when it all changed... Competing in a national tournament, Jacques reached the semi-finals, only to be knocked out by a professional wrestler-turned-grappler. Fuming at his loss, Jacques marched out of the venue, only to be caught up with by a wrestling promoter from a popular French wrestling federation, FLWF. Marco Des’Tori, promoter from Francias Lightweight Wrestling Federation, approached Jacques and offered to make him a star on a national and international level. This, compared to his “local competitor” status, shocked him and he snapped up the offer right away. The following week, he began training with the FLWF. Weeks flew by as his skills were tailored from the mats to the canvas, and Jacques was quickly becoming a fan of Professional Wrestling. In his near-30’s, Fazaro was getting on a bit and decided it was make-or-break time and pushed for his professional debut. On his first show, his aerial and mat skills blew the crowd away and within a matter of weeks, Fazaro, or “Topex” as he chose to be called, was a fan favourite. After a couple of months in the federation, Fazaro had topped the bill on events numerous times and was a big time player, but had changed his allegiance from the fans to the money. The pain full-time working had caused him during his mid-20’s made him hungry for money, and selling out seemed a great way to make some. Obviously, the fans hated him for this, and soon did the management, his power-hungry attitudes creating extreme tension backstage and soon, he was forced to leave, not after making a hefty sum of money. Now a free agent, with years of experience, and a large bank account, Jacques looked for work on a larger scale, not in the Indy scenes but in one of the major feds. But a eager wrestler by the name of Henry Resuex, or “Le’Java” as he was called in the ring, would give Jacques the boast he needed and a student to tutor...
