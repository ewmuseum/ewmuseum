Lee Lightning Biography Nicknames The Conceited Juvenile, Luscious Lee Lightning Hometown London, England Birthday 13th July 1979 Height 6'2' Weight 219 Fed Titles Dyad Championship (11/22/2002 - 16/02/2003) Biography Not much is known about the past of 'Luscious' Lee Lightning, except that he is the brother of 'Daunting' David Dew and the cousin of Action! breakout superstar of 2002 Airborn Tyler. With his brother David, he forms the intelligent yet slimy tag team The Elements. However, around the globe they are despised by fans. If it was down to their in-ring ability, nothing would stop them from becoming the best tag team in history, but their out of the ring beliefs prevent them from doing this. Their hobby horse is that the fans do not know how to use democracy properly. Like many people in this world they hate communism, but they hate it for the wrong reasons. Lee Lightning believes that the demons in this world are down to the public's inability to invoke their right to a democratic opinion. The shootings in Washington could have been presented if people had made a stand against legalising weaponary. Hunger in Africa wouldn't exist if we requested more aid for the helpless. And everyone would protest that The Elements should be the Action! Tag Team Champions. And that is how 'Luscious' Lee Lightning believes things should be. People cannot see why he is the best looking young stud in wrestling, and they cannot see the world through his eyes. He wants to change that. By winning the Action! Dyad Championship.
