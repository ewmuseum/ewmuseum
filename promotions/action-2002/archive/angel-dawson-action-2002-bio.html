Angel Dawson Biography Nicknames Prime Time Bitch Hometown San Bernandino, California Birthday March 16th, 1975 Height 5'10' Weight 158 Fed Awards Dyad Tag Titles w/ Sweet Aroma Biography March 16th, 1975...if you were alive on this day, you were probably handed a cigar by the not very well-known, but everloving Devin Paul Holmes. The man was a proud husband, father of 2 sons and now the father of a gorgeous baby daughter, Angelica Lynn Dawson. She weighed in at a healthy 7 pounds even. The mother who had just gone through 15 hours of hell, Lindsay Ann Dawson-Holmes, was smiling ear-to-ear at the end result of the pain and agony she had just endured. Angelica seemed to be worth everything they went through in the past 9 months.All of their past fights, miscarriages and counselling seemed to fly right out the window as Lindsay held the bundle of joy known as Angelica in her slightly weak arms. As a very young child, Angelica was brought up like one of the guys. Never introduced much to feminine tendencies, she hung around her two older brothers, Dylan and Mark. From them, she learned the necessities of a guy's life - sports. Angelica was a hard tackler when she played football with her brothers and their friends. She was never picked on for her size or the fact that she was a girl, mainly because Dylan and Mark were two guys that people knew not to cross. She could tumble with the guys as if she was a guy herself and was considered a guy half of the time. At the age of 8, Lindsay and Devin got into an argument and got a divorce. Devin got custody of Dylan and Angelica, while Lindsay had custody of Mark. This was one of the last times Dylan and Angelica had seen Mark. Devin and his two children quickly re-located to San Angelo, Texas. In their new location, Angelica went through a depression, which was never a normal occurance for your average 8-year-old. After Angelica's battle with her mental issues, she began reflecting some of her mother's best qualities. Angelica acted more ladylike and cut her football playing drastically to close to nothing. 6 years later, Angelica was in high school. Now was a different scene than there was when she was younger. Angelica had become a type of an outcast, not really fitting in. She was outspoken, but didn't fit in with the funnier people. She had a lot of acquaintances, but wasn't considered popular. Peer pressure got the best of her, which started Angelica's addiction to cigarettes. She picked up smoking and, for the first time in a while, had a solid group of friends. She was still obviously a lady, but started dressing more tomboy-ish. Angelica's early blooming made the fact she was a girl rather obvious. At certain times during Angelica's self-titled 'Dark Period', she tried to hide the fact that she was a woman by taping herself down as best as she could. Devin had noticed Angelica's behaviour change and started to get worried. Dylan tried to talk to Angelica, but she would just smile and nod, not really taking in what Dylan told her. Her father and Dylan came to the harsh realization that it would take a traumatic event to break her consuming habits. This realization couldn't have come at a more ironic time. Word came quickly from their hometown of San Bernandino that Angelica's mother had committed suicide. As soon as Angelica received this news, she was too traumatized to even move. 24 hours later, Angelica was being checked into the San Angelo Psychiatric Hospital, reportedly becoming suddenly insane and suicidal. 2 months passed, seeming like 2 years to a now 15 year old Angelica. She finally got released from the hospital, being deemed sane and ready to be a productive member of the community. From day one back at home, she was a changed person. Angelica had become a very feminine, polite person. Angelica quit smoking and got a better group of friends. For the next 3 years, Angelica worked at improving her life and becoming the type of person she always wanted to be - somebody who was respected for their mind, body and soul. It had worked. Angelica became popular, even having a boyfriend for all 3 years of her high school days. Angelica and Taylor had gone through everything together and were eventually named Prom King and Queen at graduation. Graduation hit like a ton of bricks. Angelica and Taylor were forced to break up as Taylor was heading off to Harvard and Angelica was staying in Texas. The break-up brought Angelica's spirits down, but they were brought back up when she received an acceptance letter from Texas A&M. Angelica went on to Texas A&M to get a degree in journalism. During her university years, Angelica's horizons expanded and she quickly found professional wrestling. Her male friends in university would invite her over to watch wrestling every night it was on, which got her female roommates pissed. Angelica was happy and that's all that mattered to her. Angelica quickly found independent shows around the campus and attended them all. She got to know some of the wrestlers and this got Angelica even more interested in a career in pro wrestling. Angelica graduated from Texas A&M with her degree in journalism, but by this time her sights were firmly set on pro wrestling. She got a job in an indy fed called SWA as a valet. Named Serena Murphy, she accompanied JD Murphy to the ring and helped lead him to SWA gold. During her time here, she picked up valuable information from JD and other co-workers. After 6 months in the SWA, she got into a local wrestling school, training hard to advance her role in the SWA. Angelica worked hard to get better and once she finished at wrestling school, she advanced in SWA and became a successful female competitor. After a long run there, she left and went on to YW under her real name and has used it ever since. Angelica did rather well, learning a lot from her new co-workers. After spending 8 months there, Angelica moved up to the infamous GFWA. Her brief, 1-month stint was cut drastically short due to a surprise call from Mark, her long-lost brother. She left GFWA to try to heal old wounds that their parents had created. In late August of 2001, Angelica came back to GFWA, now named gfwaOMEGA or gO for short. Only 2 weeks into her contract, Angelica received terrifying news. Her brother Dylan, working in Tower 1 of the World Trade Centre, had been killed during the 9/11 attacks. To commemorate him, Angelica donated $10000 to the World Trade Centre Relief Fund. About a month after Angelica came into gO, she met up with the soon-to-be GHC Champion, paYne. They hit it off instantly and got together after a couple of weeks of paYne begging. They were a happy couple and other wrestlers sent many wishes to them. Suddenly, Angelica broke up with him, saying he was holding her down. This started a feud between the two which ended when paYne lost the title to Frostie. Two and a half months later, Angelica Dawson made history. She became the first woman to hold a title in gO, capturing the OMEGA title from Jiraiya at Stage 1 of Two Days Of Hell on December 31st, 2001. Four weeks later, she was cheated out of a title defense by her then-boyfriend Joshua Michaels, losing her title to Remy Resnick. 4 months after her title loss, Angelica moved forward in her wrestling career by joining Action! Wrestling, thanks to a few kind words from an unlikely source. Max Danger helped Angelica get in, obviously not knowing the shit she'd dish out to him. Angelica had never been one to enjoy other females in the federation she was in, so fans of hers were taken aback when she teamed with Veronica Aromadeus, more commonly known as Sweet Aroma. Collectively known as The Clit N' Candy Express, they were awarded the Dyad Tag Team Titles. Angelica had it all. She was doing what she wanted to do, she had tag title gold but something was missing...something she'd never had. A sidekick. But where could she find a sidekick that was totally cool? A newspaper contest. No, really. Just listen. One plain and simple day, Angelica picked up a plain and simple newspaper. She was flipping through it and right beside the 'Cathy' comic, she saw an advertisement for a contest. The winner would get a fuzzy purple penguin. Of course, Angelica's love for penguins rekindled as thoughts flew her head of owning an actual penguin. She sent an entry away as soon as she could and waited anxiously for the next 2 weeks until the winner was to be drawn. 2 weeks later, Angelica got a phone call. She had won the purple penguin! The newspaper explained that the toy would be shipped on ground...toy? Angelica didn't know she was getting a TOY penguin. So, Angelica argued with them for an hour, stating that she was led to believe she'd get an actual fuzzy purple penguin. After another hour of geing transferred to different people, Angelica was going to get an actual fuzzy purple penguin. She arrived at the radio station two days after the argument. She walked in and in front of her was the most gorgeous baby purple penguin she'd ever seen. It was standing there, its round eyes mesmerized by its new owner. The penguin was not fuzzy yet, but Angelica was assured the fuzz would grow over time. She walked up to it and scooped it into her arms. Of course, she had to sign lots of papers cause it's not every day that a woman gets a purple penguin for a pet. On the papers, she had to write the penguin's name. Angelica thought about it and many names came to mind. Pengu? No, already used. Colin? Nah, too gay. Stampy? No, too big. PP! The very initials of the show she appeared on every week in Action were now the name of her baby penguin. But this PP didn't stand for Pressure Point. It stood for, of all things, Purple Penguin. Recently, though, tragic events have made not only PP, but the duet of Clit n Candy, disappear. The death of Angel's father, Devin Holmes, turned Angel into a depressed, singled out individual. PP has not been seen and Clit n Candy seems to have disbanded ever since Angel's life took this turn for the worst. What she plans on having next is a mystery. The return of PP? The reformation of Clit n Candy? Or maybe a complete dismissal of everything she once knew. Other Feds GFWA, gfwaOMEGA (gO), Advantage Wrestling Other Fed Awards First female champion in gfwaOMEGA, first female competitor in gO, Former Advantage gO Champion Wrestling Style Extreme/High Flyer but Technical when needed Finisher Move Falling Grace / Painful Reminder Finisher Description Superkick / Alabama Slam Special Moves DDT, Bulldog, Hurricanarana, Release German Suplex, Short-Arm Clothesline, Missile Dropkick, Guillotine Legdrop, Tornado DDT, Sliding Dropkick, Springboard Moonsault Entrance Music 'Angels' by Vinyl Sun
