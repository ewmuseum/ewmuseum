Out of Control Cox Arena — San Diego, California ---- Opening Contest Action! Title #1 Contendership Coral Avalon vs. "Impulse" Brian James OUT OF CONTROL. September 28th, 2003 The Cox Arena in San Diego, California Opening Contest Action! Title #1 Contendership Coral Avalon vs. "Impulse" Brian James Writer: Mike R. Referee: Mark Shields Ever since Instinct came and went, the wrestler known as Coral Avalon had been in a bit of a funk. The proud Louisiana native had been both emotionally and physically beaten, either by family problems, girlfriend problems, or a morally wrong New York City native breaking his nose with a heavy knee strike on the last Pressure Point. Coral had been on a major losing streak ever since he lost the Bantam title to Max Danger. At the core of this losing streak, however, was his disenfranchised love life and the presence of Wrestling 101 member, "Impulse" Brian James, who still had it in for the Kleptomaniac following a tough loss to him in early August over the Bantam championship, and who wanted to prove himself as one of the finest members of Wrestling 101. The way to prove that was to destroy the biggest thorn in Wrestling 101's side. It was no secret to anyone that Coral Avalon hated Jeff Garvin and vice versa. Additionally, Coral has also had issues with Keith Scott Zimmerman, the other franchise member of Wrestling 101. It was also no secret that Coral Avalon wanted a chance at Garvin's title. Playing off of that, "Impulse" Brian James laid out his challenge: The only way Coral Avalon could ever get a title shot against Garvin is to go through "Impulse" Brian James, and take his shot at Players & Pawns. "Here" by Vast would kick things off, sending the crowd into a frenzy of boos. The lights faded to black and red strobe lights shined in the entryway. The fans continued to boo until the pulse-pounding guitars of "Here" hit and "Impulse" Brian James appeared at the entryway. The loudmouthed Texas native, "Impulse" Brian James, had come to play. James was in a situation where he had nothing to gain and everything to lose, in theory. However, James was here to make sure that Coral didn't make it past this pay-per-view event with any sort of chance at Jeff Garvin, and he didn't come alone. The fans erupted in a frenzy of X-Pac heat for Chris Chambers as he followed IBJ out. Chambers stopped, brushed his lilac hair which was somehow poking through the paper bag over hiead head, and held his arms out as if to say, "What? What's wrong with me?" And thus, the resounding chants of "CHAM-BERS SUCKS! CHAM-BERS SUCKS!" echoed throughout the Cox Arena. Brian wore the alternate red version of his tights with the grayscaled flames, and black tape. He looked determined, tonight, to do Wrestling 101 proud and take Coral Avalon's ass to school. Brian entered the ring, playing to the crowd in his usual dickhood manner, stretching his arms out and pretty much asking the crowd to "bring it, bitches". Luckily, the crowd didn't have to bring it, because "Here" was soon replaced by the loud, delayed blasts of guitar that signified the theme song od his opponent. "Won't Back Down" by Fuel. No song signified a wrestler's attitude more than Coral Avalon's theme song. Never backing down from a challenge, no matter how much he was hurting, Coral Avalon had endeared himself to the fans with his "talk is cheap" attitude and his abilities to make to impossible seem possible. The problem was, of course, that Coral wasn't in the best condition. He was going in with a broken nose from the HardCase match and some hurt legs courtesy of a post-match beatdown by IBJ. In fact, the broken nose needed to be protected on this night, which is why Coral Avalon had a nose guard on top of his head, so that you can see the tape on Coral's nose. Coral also wore the black and red variation of his tights. It was, of course, the nose that would most likely be IBJ's primary target, rather than the legs, which were just struck in order to weaken Coral's greatest advantage of his high amounts of agility. Coral was in this alone, and he didn't seem to mind too much as he walked down the aisle. Sure, Coral was battered and beaten and broken, but he was still here to prove to the Action! Champion, Jeff Garvin that he was the one true enemy that Garvin hasn't been able to truly conquer. Coral cautiously entered the ring, making sure that the Eternal Journeyman wasn't going to attack him from behind. However, IBJ just seemed to be arrogant and standing and mocking the Kleptomaniac's noseguard in that "Ha ha, you have an injury and I'm so going to exploit it." sort of way. Coral sighed and pulled the guard over his face and adjusted it for the most comfort he could afford. Referee Mark Shields, in his striped muscle shirt, stood between the two vastly different individuals. This was his hometown of San Diego, and he was psyched to be here. Therefore, the hometown referee quickly called for the bell to ring, and got this match started. Ding ding ding. Chris Chambers tried to lead on an "I-B-J! I-B-J!" chant, but a fan behind him smacked him in the back of the head with a flying milkshake container. Chambers looked around to see who did that, pointed at him, threatened the guy, and chanted "I-B-J! I-B-J!" again. He was thusly overwhelmed with the following chant: "AV-A-LON! AV-A-LON! AV-A-LON!" The two men faced one another, in their completely different worlds. Coral was taciturn and cool-headed, whereas Brian was hot-headed and loud. Coral preferred his style of adapting to his enemies and finding a weakness, whereas Brian preferred to beat things until they stopped moving. Coral had his European uppercuts that he had styled after Fit Finlay, whereas Brian had his kicks of jaw-wrenching fury. Most importantly: Coral had an amateur wrestling background. Brian did not. And that was key for when IBJ came out, guns ablazin', trying to knock Coral Avalon's head off with a roundhouse kick, and Coral ducked that and grabbed a waistlock.
