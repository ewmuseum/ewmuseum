They had come to no finer place for such things, as Action! Wrestling was the number one company in North America(fWo who?). Why? Because their night was going to start off with someone they hated. Someone they loathed. That man was the reigning Carnage Champion, Max Danger. He had walked from underneath the Action!Tron, with his music("Rock Star" by N.E.R.D.) blarring overhead.
