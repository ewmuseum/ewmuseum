What this is Allison Lindum’s card from the fed’s own roster page, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 17 November 2003. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Allison Lindum Birth Name: Allison Lilith Lindum Also Known As: Ali, Lilith Date of Birth: April 13th, 1982 Age: 21 Marital Status: Single (but secretly dating Keith Scott Zimmerman) Hometown: Sacramento, California Height: 5'9' Weight: 136 lbs. Hair Color: Black Eye Color: Blue Blood Type: O- Status: Face Personality: She's Coral Avalon's polar opposite in terms of personality. Whereas Coral is quiet and taciturn, Allison is outspoken and somewhat stubborn. Can come off a bit tomboyish, but she's also very sweet and compassionate when she's not trying to come off as 'one of the boys' like she does with the 'Aqua Teen Hunger Force'. Theme Music: 'Won't Back Down' by Fuel Wrestling Style Trained By: Marcus Lindum, partially trained for the ring by Marcus Dox. Detailed Style: Allison, like Jessica Danger, isn't just your average pretty face. She is an Aikido blackbelt, as well as a semi-athlete(at least until she concussed herself playing soccer). If she gets angry, she will try to get physically involved, and she usually leaves people wondering what hit them. Strengths: She is an Aikido blackbelt. She's not fragile and can get up after someone slams into her(unlike a lot of WWE referees). Aside from her defensive Aikido discipline, she has picked up a few other moves(ie: superkick). She's at least smart enough to know when to back off. Weaknesses: Her firey temper can get the better of her(leading her to superkick the beejesus out of people, or challenge people like Max Danger to matches). She, like Jessica, doesn't use her looks to distract Coral's opponents. She proves to distract Coral more than she distracts her opponents. Is generally in the shadow of her older, more famous sister, Keri. Signature Moves: Kicks of multiple varieties. Takedowns. Momentum-based trips and tosses. Kicking people in the nuts. Being pretty enough to distract people. Trademark Name: Superkick Trademark Move: Superkick Injuries Current Injuries: None Nagging Injuries: None History Years Pro: n/a Manager: Coral Avalon Allies: Coral Avalon, Max Danger, Jessica Danger, Keith Scott Zimmerman Nemesis: Daniel Phillips, Jeff Garvin Current Team: None Past Teams: None Action Wrestling Title History: None Action Wrestling Award History: None Other Federations: None Other Federation Awards: None Attributes Intelligence: Allison Lindum is a graduate of the University of Phoenix, with a degree in physics. She's working on her pHD over the internet. She's not a dummy, but she also thinks more in terms of mathematics than she does with her English. (10) Strength: Allison's strong because of her martial arts background. Most of her strength happens to be in her legs, however. She lacks a lot of upper body strength and weight to the other ladies of Action!(with the exception of Jessica). However, Allison's kicks are as lethal as they come and they save her from a low score. (5) Toughness: Allison can survive a lot of hard hits. She's not the kind who will just be KOed off of someone merely running her over or ramming her into things. Hell, she'll survive wrestling moves as well. (7) Speed: Allison is an athlete and used to play soccer. Quite well, actually, at least until she got herself concussed. But pretty much, Allison is difficult to actually hit because she's fast and evasive, partially because of her background in Aikido, and partially because she's a good sprinter. But I guess that depends on if she's wearing heels, now doesn't it? (8) Endurance: That's a 6 if she has to wrestle, and... well, try to guess what it is in the sack. (6) Courage: Allison's not afraid of anything. She'll stand up for herself long before Coral ever does. She has actually wrestled Max Danger without backing down once. She's a brave little soul, isn't she? (10) Charisma: Charismatic to cheer for her man, but she's not expected to cut a promo. (8) Skill: Aikido black belt, and loosely trained by Max Danger for the ring should she ever need that. Enough said. (9) Biography A L L I Sacremento. Allison was born and raised in Sacremento, like most of her family. Her family moved to Phoenix when she was eleven, however, and it's there that she would go to college. O N L I N D U M Off Stage Information Name: Mike R. AOL Instant Messenger Screen Name: [detail withheld] Interests: In Wily's Defense, my comic. Internet comics. Web technology. Web design. Angel. 24. Alias. Favorite Pro Wrestler: Chris Benoit, among others. See Avalon's bio for a bigger list.
