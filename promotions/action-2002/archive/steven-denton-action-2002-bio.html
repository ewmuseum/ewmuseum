Steven Denton Biography Nicknames Tommy (only to close friends), Steve Hometown Chicago, Illinois Birthday May 26th, 1977 Height 6'5' Weight 245 Fed Titles None, yet. Fed Awards None, yet. Biography ~May 26, 1977~ 6:13 PM; May 26th, 1977. Steven Denton took his first deep breathe of air. Born to Rose and Larry Denton of Chicago, Steven was originally weighed at 7 and ½ pounds, seventeen inches tall. Despite the crying, spluttering, and overall loudness of their second child, Rose and Larry felt as warm inside as doves. The very next day, Steven's sister, Sarah, met her new little brother for the first time. She giggled playfully as her mommy held Steven close, letting the two exchange a quick 'handshake'. The following months and years turned out to be those of clarity, where life, and everything about it, was beautiful. ~July 07, 1980~ Rose gives birth to a third child. Despite the joy of bringing another life into this world, Rose and Larry agree to put the infant up for adoption due to money constraints. They kept the baby as private as possible, and when the time came, a young Japanese couple adopted Valkyr, as he'd been named, and whisked him off to Japan. To this day, Sarah does recall having a second brother, but nothing about him can be recollected by herself. Steven, while knowing he does have a brother, knows as little as Sarah does. He remembers calling him 'Val', but nothing more. Sarah and Steven have looked for evidence of his existence throughout their lives, but have yet to turn up anything useful. ~October 21st, 1983~ Rose Denton first discovered her son's knack for reading on this day. Though she hadn't paid much attention before, she now saw her son enter their home with a children's novel. Astonished, Steven's female parent began to question him. 'Stevey, honey, I see your big book there. Isn't it too hard for you?' she had questioned Steven. 'No, mommy, reading is fundamental. I'm gonna read a whole series of books published by this author,' Steven had replied, before entering his room. From this point on, Rose knew her son would be one of the more intelligent people when he grew older. 'He'll be a professor, or a doctor, or a scientist! I can't believe I've bred such a knowledgeable young man,' she had proclaimed, as Steven's door slammed shut. ~April 1st, 1990~ Troubles arose for the Denton's, mainly money troubles. Though it seemed rather common for those in the neighborhood, Rose and Larry had always managed a healthy budget. However, things became too overwhelming, as Steven had gotten hurt playing football recently and needed thirty-two stitches in the back of his right shoulder. You can only imagine how hefty the bill turned out to be worth. Both Denton parents were under a lot of stress as tax day, April 1st, rolled around. As various add-ons multiplied the amount of taxes due, tension mounted between the adults. First they seemed to just be letting of steam. 'It's natural, Steven, they'll get over it,' Sarah had reassured her brother. 'Are you sure?' Steven said back. 'They seem awfully raged.' As the tussles between both parents became more and more abusive, an across-the-street neighbor set up a camera in her window. She, unfortunately, was a feminist, and set out to prove that Mr. Denton was no more than an abusive husband and a horrible father. Things only got worse, as money dwindled further, and Larry Denton began to lose control. The Denton household was covered in tension, seeing as Sarah and Steven were the only two who ever spoke anymore. Finally, an ending to it all dug itself up: ~April 27th, 1990~ Just a mere month before Steven was set to turn thirteen, he stood on a basketball court in Chicago. Steven was having a good time outside of his gloomy home... little did he realize that things were crumbling at his home, just two blocks away. Rose and Larry were rummaging through the mail, when Mr. Denton stumbled upon a bill from a messuage parlor. 'What's this?' Larry spoke. Rose glanced, saw the ball, and yanked in from Larry's hands. 'Oh, nothing,' Rose commented, sliding the bill into her back pocket. 'So this is where our money is going? To your messuage bills? What the hell is wrong with you?' Larry shouted at his wife. 'Honey...' before Mrs. Denton could begin to explain herself, Larry grabbed the table by it's edges, and sent it flying to their wooden floor, scattered papers in the process. 'Calm down!' Rose shrieked. '...calm down? I've had it!' Larry bellowed. He swung at his poor wife, sending her backwards, and into the wall behind. Still conscious, Rose began to sob, while groping at the back of her head. In his rage, Larry glanced towards the kitchen. The drawer. The knife. Back at the basketball courts, Steven smacked his head as he realized what time it was. He waved goodbye to some nearby kids, and began jogging towards his home. Little Steven reached the steps, and climbed hastily up them, taking deep-breaths as he did so. Larry Denton approached his downed wife. Shouting obscenities, Mr. Denton gripped the cleaver in his hand; raising it high above his head. 'Sorry I'm late,' Steven jumped through the door. He slammed it shut, turned, and saw the occurrence. Larry stabbed his wife, twice, sending his wife to her horrible end. ~April 28th, 1990~ The next thing Steven Denton remembered is awakening on his bedroom floor, curled up next to his sister; he shook her slightly. 'I'm awake,' she had whispered, rolling over to peer at him. 'What happened?' Asked a troubled Steven. 'Steven... God... I love you...' she pulled her little brother tightly close for a hug. As it all comes back to him. As reality set back in, Steven recollected the happenings of the night before. Steven's dad, Larry, was arrested the following morning by police. Two months later, he was sentenced to a lifetime in prison, after the court viewed the Denton's neighbor's tape. From then on, Sarah and Steven began a life together. She began working a part-time job to help keep the apartment, as well as keeping Steven healthy. The neighborhood, pitying the poor children, donated nearly two thousand dollars to the two, which they lasted very long on. After that nights happenings, however, Steven began stagnant from his friends. He drifted off by himself, spending all his time on schoolwork. Denton set a goal for himself; he'd become educated, and bring good to the family name. He swore it on his mother's grave. ~May 26th, 1993~ Steven turned sixteen, and celebrated with his sister by venturing out and purchasing his first automobile. Along with a car, Steven also captured a job. He began working as a backstage attendant for the Fire Thunder Wrestling promotion, which was an independent wrestling federation in the area. At the time, Steven had little, if not any interest in the art that is professional wrestling. Strangely, as he worked on keeping order in the backstage area, he began to become more and more intrigued at what was happening in the ring. When the owner of the promotion, James Warner, finally noted his interest, he offered to train Steven, and get him in the ring. Steven, up front, accepted. ~August 20th, 1993~ On this faithful Friday night, 'Maximus Escabar' debuted for the FTW. Steven wore a mask, golden tights, and a black cape. Warner had trained him to be a technician, and suplex artist, despite the company's high-flying edge. As the match began, Denton lit up the crowd with dazzling exploder suplexes and breathe-taking Germans. The audience was astonished, and they roared for the six foot, five inch competitor. Sarah seemed most proud of him, however, as she bought him a nice cake for celebration, despite the costs. Maximus Escabar entertained crowds for months. As Steven became better and better, other promotions began noticing him. Before long, Steven was being offered much higher paying contracts. However, Denton turned them all down. He set out to finish his education first. Wrestling on Friday nights, and schooling during the rest of his free time, Steven kept up his near one-hundred averages in every class. ~May 19th, 1995~ Steven's graduation day. On this day, he received several offers from several colleges around the United States. It was a tough choice, but Steven decided to attend Stanford University, in California. It was tough leaving his sister, but she was near moving in with her boyfriend. Steven figured he'd get out of their way, and graduate from a school he'd wanted to attend since childhood. For three years, Steven studied hard at Stanford, and continued to wrestle in local promotions, never leaving the area, for fear of jeopardizing his education. He wrestled as Maximus Escabar, Denton, and Nails in three different company's during the time. He loved every minute of the action. He wanted to spend his life wrestling, and he knew it. ~April 15th, 1998~ All he'd worked for had finally paid off. Steven received his bachelors degree from Stanford. His sister and her husband-to-be flew in, and took him out every night that week, announcing to everyone they saw that he'd graduated. If you ask Denton a question to the extent of 'What was the most enjoyable moment in your lifetime?' Steven will respond, 'Well that's simple. When I graduated from college.' After graduating, he was now looking to make a serious commitment to a wrestling promotion. His largest and most promising offer came from a rather large federation in Japan. Steven easily accepted, and moved himself to Japan. There, he wrestled against the likes of Kenta Kobashi several times, lighting crowds up with multiple four and five star matches. For three years, Steven perfected his skills in Japan. Now known as himself, Steven Denton, he has decided to return to the United States in hope of working for the WWF, or a company of that caliber. He had the skills. He now carried the charismatic heel mentality, and is now ready to jump-start his career. It's September, 2002. Steven Denton is twenty-five years old. He's at his peak physically. But where does he stand mentally?
