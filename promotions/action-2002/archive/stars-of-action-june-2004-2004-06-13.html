What this is The site’s own Stars of Action page: a short write-up of each wrestler it was pushing, from the Wayback capture of 13 June 2004. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the page’s text is.
STARS OF ACTION. WRESTLERS: Phil Atken, Action Champion The self proclaimed ‘Entertainment Savior’ arrived in Action after the mass firing at Christmas time. Phil Atken is known around the wrestling circle as a man who only cares about himself and has be known by many to be a huge backstage problem. A former major member of the thReat roster, he managed to make a joke of its Cruiserweight Championship by trying to avoid all his Title defenses. It was clearly shown in thReat that he only cares about the major Titles, even taking pot shots at the thReat World Champion David Sanders on air even though he wasn’t involved in any sort of feud with him. Even though he does have the intelligence to be able to hold his place on the spotlight his actual wrestling skills leave a lot to be desired. Yet Phil tends to try and hide this by avoiding as many matches as possible. He’s one of the few roster members to care about the ratings, so long as they show him in a positive light. He’s also known to be a backstage politician which was seen from his first arrival in Action when he managed to convince Reed Young to assign him to special guest referee duty. Despite nearly everyone he’s come across having problems with, Atken’s star still manages to shine bright in every company he’s been in. Now the Action Champion, he’ll be able to show his star even more. Action Man, Unrendered Wrestler Action Man is unrendered. And this drives him crazy! WHY IS HE THE ONLY ONE WITHOUT A GOOD POSER GOD DAMMIT!?!? Hell, can he not be like the other half of the roster and just NOT have a poser!? But AM has changed. He’s not an idiot anymore. Now, he’s depressed. Depressed that he is not rendered. And you know what… that’s as good of a problem as anyone else. Maybe, one day, Wayne will make him a poser. Maybe… one day… Action Woman, Unrendered Wrestler She once was Action Man’s teammate until last February Sharc did the unthinkable and broke AW’s neck. She’s been recovering since… although you may find her in a match or two after, that was her stunt-double, Michael, who was already unrendered. AW remains loyal to Action Man as a teammate and a good friend… but she’s developed a massive crush on the ‘Passive-Aggressive’ himself, Kayden Paulton. Anaconda, Wrestler Former wrestler in The Wrestling Federation (ran by Reed Young’s father), Anaconda tasted some success before stepping away from the game. Now, at 46, he’s returning to the ring. No one is really sure why… some say it’s to give it one more go, because Anaconda has never been to the top of an industry, and he had served in TWF for so very long. But Anaconda will be patient. He knows that there are people here which are more talented… a lot younger and quicker… and have better relationships with the Owner and Vice-President. But patients is a virtue… says Anaconda. He has to be one of the wisest wrestlers in Action… we guess, that comes with his age… Chad Villain, Wrestler Before Action Wrestling, Villain wrestled for his father’s company, which was located in New York City. He was only there to help his father out with the company but failed to do so because of an injury he sustained caused by his long term nemesis, Terrell Mason. Mason struck Villain with a baseball bat to the knee, which took him our for several years. Villain fully recovered from the injury and signed with WWC, ran by Paul Levine. There, he met up with Mason, injuring him at ‘Road to Gold’. After the event, Reed Young approached the young superstar and offered him a chance to run with the best, but what Young didn’t tell him was that the best had been fired because of lots and lots of alcohol. Yet that didn’t matter to Villain. All that mattered to him was crushing his opponents and going home with a paycheck. As far as titles went, if grasping one meant a raise, than he would gladly take one. Christian Elijah, Wrestler Lazy. Christian Elijah is the laziest wrestler on the Action roster. However there is just one problem… Reed Young LOVES him. He thinks Elijah’s great! But Young has never seen a Christian Elijah match. Every time the Owner attempts to watch one… something happens. He’s distracted. So, Reed lies to Elijah. He keeps telling him he’s seen his wicked matches and thinks Elijah finishes off his opponents early… when the truth is Christian can’t even get through one minute. Yet, Elijah goes along with this, and Young believes him. Hopefully, Reed won’t push him or anything… Christopher Fox, Wrestler The biggest happy-go-lucky kid in the world! Sure, he’s 24... but in his mind he’s about 3 or 4. Fox loves everyone and everything… except when he went mad last year against LLB. Fox was the reason James Jack, ‘The Law’s’ best friend, had turned into a vegetable. Now Fox is an angry kid. The terrible twos have started… and Fox will not rest until he gets what he wants and he gets it right now! Watch the ‘Kid’ go on some awesome outrageous adventures in his tales… and take to Pressure Point only to become the biggest brat you’ve ever seen! Fox has been in Action since the beginning, and remains only one-half of the most loyal wrestlers to Reed Young (the other being LLB). So Fox will make sure all the new blood knows his name… BIG TIME! Hosni, Wrestler One of the newest ‘stars’ to the industry, the man who goes by the name of HOSNI (pronounced HORSE-KNEE) is a very colourful individual. Not much of his present lifestyle except for the fact that he’s gay is known, but his past has been well-documented during his short stint in Carnage Wrestling. His father, who was born in Syria, left the family when he was eight. His mother, who liked to act as if she was Dutch, had to raise the family all by her own. Herzy (his real name) wasn’t too fond of his younger brother, so as the years passed by, he created four imaginary sisters. They influenced his life so much, that he started acting like a girl. And before he knew it, Herzy was bending down in dark alleys, biting his lower lip as some stranger’s cock pounded his hairless arse. Surprisingly, during each buttfuck, Herzy thought of his father, and the story of ‘The Evil Empire’ that his father used to tell him many years ago. So, Herzy changed his identity to ‘Emperor Hosni’, and he now wishes to take over the world, to promote his ideals of gayism. Will he achieve his goal? Nobody knows. One thing’s for sure; he’s actually becoming quite the popular mofo. Then again, stranger things have happened. Jesse Ramey, Wrestler A seasoned veteran with over twelve years under his belt and at the very young age of twenty-eight. You would easily know him from everyone else on the Action roster, because of his superstar good looks and the charisma that almost oozes off of him. His only problem is his past, something that has haunted him for the better part of his wrestling career and what normally causes him the most problems in life. His only escape, or at least he believes so, is taking to the ring for a good bout. Many aren’t very intimidated by Jesse because of his size, but he has made nearly all of them at least respect him once they enter the ring. Ramey plans to make all of Action respect him; already proclaiming himself ‘Mr. Action’. Does the future hold glory or demise for this young star? Kayden Paulton, Wrestler The nicest guy on the planet. Care to object? Just watch Paulton in action. He loves everyone, and everything. You could be the biggest asshole to him and somehow Kayden will find your inner beauty and love you even more! The ‘Passive-Aggressive’ is just that… passive on aggression… and maybe a little too much. He has never gotten mad. He has never lost it on anyone. In his mind, this world is full of joy… and joy, is his middle name. Khristain Keller, Wrestler Khristain Keller is the newest virus to sweep through professional wrestling, and his latest stop is that at Action Wrestling. After convincing Reed Young to give him a job through a game of Blackjack, he is now attempting to take Action by storm. Keller is better than you and pretty much everyone else... what else do you need to know? LLB, Wrestler Legend. ‘The Law’ is a former 5-time PIW World Champion… and former jOlt and the Asylum superstar. Many critics have said he was the best tA wrestler not to hold the World Title. LLB is also a 2-time Action Champion… and his legacy continues. He shows no signs of retiring… and although his age is not known, he is rumored to still be in his twenties. Last year, Young broke the almighty rule between their great friendship. Reed told everyone that LLB was not a lawyer. How this could be? No one is sure… as the story has not come out in full detail just yet. Nevertheless, ‘The Law’ remains in Action. Reed and him don’t see eye-to-eye anymore… but LLB would never leave. His job isn’t done here yet. Action, in turn, has not been proven GUILTY. But it will… it damn sure will… Matt Rose, Wrestler Signed to the Action roster because he’s hardcore… Rose is a favorite to contend for the Carnage Championship and maybe make a division out of it again. Nevertheless Rose has a lot of work to do. For some reason his hardcore title has floated around backstage… and many wrestlers want to prove this theory… and see if he is as crazy as they say. Memphis Gray, Wrestler Memphis has been trained by many greats… which makes his overall bio look astonishing. Although not much is really known about Gray and Action together as one, he’s smart, he has charisma… he has courage, endurance and skill. That, right there, is dangerous in itself. Morg, Wrestler Massive. He’s a giant in his own right. Morg made his first appearance in Action two years ago. With over five months under his belt, many different sides of Morg were able to be seen. He’s very depressed sometimes. But don’t get this guy mad. He has the power to destroy anyone in Action Wrestling if he wants to. Just ask Reed, who hired him to be his bodyguard for some time in 2002. Roland Erman, Wrestler Don’t be fooled by his name. Roland Erman is formerly known as Scott Cylear, the younger brother to Sean. Sean and LLB’s history is too long to explain. But it was filled with hate. And after Sean faked his killing… only to return to Action and seek out LLB, and then paralyze himself after another attempt at beating ‘The Law’ failed, Scott legally changed his name… to Roland. He doesn’t want LLB to destroy his entire family, so Scott is playing mind games. Scott is OBSESSED with ending LLB’s life, just like he ended his brothers. Can you feel the love tonight? Sergey Kovhachek, Wrestler Sergey Kovhachek is being touted as a definite star for the future. Only in his early 20’s, the man born in Ukraine is one of the many and more commercially exploited students of Darren Bencini. Darren helped to train Sergey’s father in the latter’s rise to fame in the mid 1990’s, and now Sergey is benefiting from the same brand of wisdom. A multi-talented young man, Sergey also specialises in internet journalism, in the area of professional wrestling. His debut in thReat left fans wanting to see more of Sergey, and within time, this young man will surely mature to become one of the fiercest and most talented competitors the squared circle will ever see. Still a raw talent, but with tons of potential and the guidance of a experienced veteran, Sergey’s future definitely looks bright. (First Name) ‘The Jobber’ (Last Name), Wrestler… he wishes (First Name) ‘The Jobber’ (Last Name) is written like this because his name seems to change every different show. The only thing that remains constant is his title, ‘The Jobber’, because that’s what he is. Watch out for gimmick changes too. This guy can be really odd at times. “Hey ‘Jobber’, I need a win… so get down on the mat right now!” is his least favorite catchphrase. “Hey ‘Jobber’, he’s ten dollars for jobbing”, is his favorite. The Ringer, Wrestler The Ringer is so full of himself he thinks everyone else is inferior. They’re all idiots he says. The Ringer is off and on in programming but maybe someday he’ll take it upon himself to keep showing up every week, and prove his worth to all of those idiots out there. The Ringer, however, is very talented. He’s beaten LLB. That in itself is his claim to fame. Keep reading the rest of these short bios, idiots, The Ringer thinks they are stupid. Tyler Davidson, Wrestler Tyler was born into the life of privilege that few people ever achieve. For him however, that still was not enough. He wanted the adoration of people worldwide and for a time he had achieved it. One of the top stars during the height of $oldout Wrestling’s success, Tyler’s life took a change for the worst when his fiancée of two years was murdered by a man who was actually an employee of his. Tyler disappeared from the wrestling business for several months after the murder before he reappeared a changed man. Something inside of Tyler had snapped after his fiancée’s murder and when he returned it was clear to everyone he was different. Davidson made no secret that it was the fans and their ever-growing lust for blood in the wrestling business that he blamed for their death. Despite some occurrences where it seemed the old Tyler personality resurfaced, he still to this day blames the fans and coincidentally has only enjoyed moderate success in that time with his biggest claims to fame being the final IOW Extreme and Cruiserweight Champion, as well as the thReat Cruiserweight Champion. A title that he by hook or by crook managed to defeat Phil Atken for at the height of their feud. Tyler joins Action along with several other former thReat stars and has quickly restarted rivalries that had begun in thReat with both Sergey Kovhachek and Phil Atken.
