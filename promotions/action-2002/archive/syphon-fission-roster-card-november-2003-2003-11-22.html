What this is Syphon Fission’s card from the fed’s own roster page, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 22 November 2003. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Syphon Fission Birth Name: Rashard William Clark Also Known As: The Negative God, The Avenging Angel Date of Birth: May 7th, 1973-November 8th, 2003 Age: 30 Marital Status: Single Hometown: Saginaw, Michigan, USA Height: 6'6 Weight: 255 Ibs. Hair Color: Black Eye Color: Cobalt Blue Blood Type: AB+ Status: Neutral Personality: A helpless romantic with a violent streak. A martyr without a cause. He very much plays the part of loner in every sense of the word. He's been a vagabond since a very early age, so he does not trust many people in this world. Comes off as cold and sarcastic in times of crisis. He is fueled by trying to make up for the wrong doings in his life. Theme Music: IWO: 'Down With The Sickness' by Disturbed, 'Papercut' by Linkin Park, 'Cosmonaut' by At The Drive In, 'Synthetic' by Spineshank Action: 'Papercut' by Linkin Park, 'After Me' by Saliva, 'For You' by Staind, 'Dumb Waiters' by The Rapture, 'Capricorn' by 30 Seconds To Mars, 'Be Quiet And Drive(Far Away)' by the Deftones Wrestling Style Trained By: Victor Riley Detailed Style: Powerhouse Strengths: His offensive talent is explosive, is perhaps the strongest wrestler in Action history, has a high tolerance for pain Weaknesses: Due to his long career, Rashard is a shell of his former self. He is very vunerable when he meets a top notch technical wrestler. Has had two major reconstructive surgeries on his knees, along with a degenerative elbow condition as well as multiple microfractures of the neck and back. Signature Moves: Death Plunge Double underhook face driver. Throughout his career, this would be the primary move done by Syphon Fission. Was rarely kicked out of and was feared from coast to coast. Trademark Name: The Final Word Trademark Move: Full Nelson Slam into a DDT Injuries Current Injuries: Death Nagging Injuries: Death History Years Pro: Twelve Manager: None, previously Quinn Morgan, Carmen Jackson Allies: Most of Fission's allies ended up becoming mortal enemies.
