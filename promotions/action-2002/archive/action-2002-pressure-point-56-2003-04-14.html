The move had knocked the Champion to he mat, as Phillips took him, walked him into the corner and began to climb the ropes. Tornado DDT. Fox. Was. Out. Then came some mad stomps into his right arm. Fox continued to cry out. It was times like these where he wish he had a real manager, or, well, anything other than a stuffed little toy. Regardless, Phillips lifted Fox up, and hammered him down with a last DDT. The Fans booed... and the ‘King of the Cruiserweights’ pointed upstairs with his middle finger, before climbing up. This looked to be one of the key turning points in the Match... and with Phillips’ first ever World Championship Match... this was going to be a key turning point in his career. A high elevation moonsault. And it drilled itself into Christopher Elliot. “It’s over... mother fucker.” He could barely say the words, as Phillips muttered it ever so lightly under his breath. But there Fox was... knocked out, in a prime position for some... ‘Royal Treatment’. Yet, a very quick blast of wind came within the ‘Kid’, as he used his absolute last bit of energy in grabbing the ropes before Phillips could do anything. Daniel sighed, taking the Champion and picking him up. He was just going to land another move, and then, then Fox would be prime for the hold. Phillips hurled Christopher into the ropes, hitting him with a powerslam. “There!” Shouted the Challenger, as he looked down and whipped Fox into the Buffalo sleeper submission. There was just one problem. That Referee, Ryan Watts, who was in place of James Jack due to the events at hand earlier in the night, had made another rookie mistake, finding himself too close to the action. So he got hit with Fox’s boot. And Fox, was out of it. The Match was over... and if it wasn’t for this sense of bad luck, Daniel Phillips would have been the new Action Champion. Yet, just then, as Phillips realized what had happened... did there come another problem. In the form of Joey Malone. In the form of sixty thousand Fans standing up to cheer. Malone rolled into the ring, ducking Phillips’ attempt to knock him out of it. And as Joey waited for Daniel to turn around, he held up the steel chair in his hands... WHACK! The Fans went wild. Out of the ring went Joey, as if it was a piece of cake. Fox started to stir, crawling across to the fallen body of Daniel. He then dropped an arm over him... as Watts started to recover, and made the slow count. One. ... Two. ... Three. The Fans didn’t cheer. In fact, they just booed again, but as “Courage” began and Fox was handed his Title, the ‘Kid’ basically rolled himself out of the ring, and collected his breath until the end of the show. That left Malone on the top of the ramp, looking down at Phillips who was just starting to recover himself. “Courage” came to a close, as the Fans drew silent. Daniel got on his feet... and, at first, he didn’t seem too mad. He didn’t seem to angry. He didn’t seem... like Daniel Phillips at all. Until he realized what happened. He looked outside of the ring, as Fox laid there with his Title. He looked into the Crowd... which started to cheer. And then, then Daniel Phillips looked to the top of the ramp... to find Joey Malone standing there. Smiling. One deep breath, and Phillips went from rather calm, to absolutely irate. He kicked all of the bottom ropes, swearing and shouting like never before. In fact, he was swearing so much, his former segments were made to look like Barney. Phillips tossed Ryan Watts right out of the ring, only to kick the living shit out of the bottom turnbuckles. And there, with as red of a face as ever before... did he spit in the direction of Joey Malone... who calmly just turned around, and went behind the Pressure Point logo to the sound of his theme song. The credits showed in the bottom left-hand corner, leaving Phillips to go back to kicking the ropes, and screaming into the Crowd. His first ever World Title Match was fucked. Fucked by the man he hated the most. And Daniel Phillips... couldn’t fucking stand it. Outcome: Fox via Pinfall; Retains A Title. © Action Wrestling 2002-2004
