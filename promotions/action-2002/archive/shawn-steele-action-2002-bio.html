Shawn Steele Biography Nicknames 'The Real Deal, The Big F'n Deal, Mr. Cheater, Wrestling Sensation, Idiot, Stupid, 'Tard, and Shawn-ster' (only to himself) Hometown Seattle, Washington Birthday November 15, 1977 Height 6'2' Weight 235 Biography My past as Shawn Steele began the day I changed my name. You see, I wasn't born as Shawn Steele, I was born Shawn Steel. I know this may seem like a small change, but it was the last thing to complete my full change. A lot of things in my life I cannot tell you. For many years, I went underground to a fighting promotion where I trained with some of the most dangerous men in the world. I saw moves that crippled and people that killed. But, that's enough of that. After I resurfaced, some might say I was a little more cocky that I once was. This was for good reason though, I mean, I was a well trained killing machine (sort of) and I was about to take the wrestling world on by storm, whether they like it or not. But, I didn't just jump to the top, I started where some have never been, at the very bottom. When I started wrestling, I had my eyes on the top, Tornado Wrestling 2000. I waited in line for hours to get tickets to the show and shelled out all kinds of money to gain a little backstage access to see what it took to be a TW superstar. After watching numerous shows, I found out that the best way for me to get on, well, was to become a seriously serious heel. I tried out, sent in a couple of tapes on my 'cocky heel-ness' and had them all rejected. Yeah, that made me mad, and yeah, I wanted to give up, but I didn't. What made me continue on was one person in particular in TW. His name was Chris Titan, and he was like an ideal to me. I saw him on the television and saw what kind of person he was. Chris wasn't the sharpest tool in the shed, one might say, no offense to him, but he wasn't. I realized that people loved it, so I started slowly changing again, this time to an idiot. By the time X:Wrestling opened, the change had been completed. I was very honored to be in X:Wrestling because a lot of big names were involved in it. Sadly, after only a few days of being open, some circumstances forced it to close. Once again, I had been let down. Normally, some would have quit, but I didn't, because I knew if my name had gotten out to at least one person, I'd always have a home somewhere. After sitting at home for a couple of months, I got a call from the same people at X:Wrestling, only this time it was X:Wrestling The Tournament. Of course I jumped on the chance, and once again I was surrounded by big names. This was my toughest challenge. This was nothing but a dog-eat-dog game and alliances were the key. I was lucky to form one with a fellow XCW wrestler in Brandon Blade. Together, we managed to make it to the final three, when I was eliminated. Brandon went on to win it and the contract to TW. Now, this was a major blow to my career. I had made it to the final three and being predicted as moving on to the final two when some fWo dude beat me to it. After this loss, I stopped wrestling for a while. I had to get my mind together and just think things through. I did come up in a few feds, but only to keep myself from having ring rust. Most recently, I was seen in RWO and dW, two feds which shut down without any given notice. Then my career took a turn for the good. Not only was I accepted into an extremely popular organization by the name of jOlt, but also in PIW. I was faced with a huge dilemma. Since I was only one man, I couldn't 'whore' myself to two feds. After long considerations and many different contract negotiations, I decided on PIW. I wished everyone good luck in jOlt and am still on good terms with the wrestlers. Now I was in PIW. At first I had a somewhat slow start. After feuding with the goody-goody Bilko, he went on to the main event and I stayed at the bottom. Then I made a decision, I wanted to win a title. I wanted to start at the bottom so I set my sights on Sepiroth Du Luc's Television Title. After doing everything I could, including dressing up and going by Shawn Du Luc and kidnapping Sepiroth, I won my first title. Then, as I was on a tremendous losing streak, PIW suddenly, and with any prior knowledge, shut down. Again, I was without a home. So, I wandered around sending my resume to as many people as possible. No one seemed to be interested in a person of my 'type.' I soon realized something I'd been over-looking for a while: 21 Wrestling. I'd always admired their vast array of wrestlers and felt I could probably do some good their. I sent them my papers and within a few days, I was the newest wrestler of 21 Wrestling. The Real Deal has a home once again. After not really finding my niche in 21 Wrestling, I once again went underground. And for a long time, instead of actually being part of any particular federation, I was just a fan. I did try out for a few places, fWo Survivor II being on of those. When I failed to make the final cut, I was left without a place to go. Although I didn’t make it into Survivor II, the recognition I got from being a runner up was great. I was now receiving offers from many different places now. One place that really struck me was Action Wrestling. After only a few short days of negotiation, I was a part of one of the top upcoming promotion, Action. Other Feds PIW, jOlt, 21W Other Fed Awards Last ever PIW TV Champ Wrestling Style Hybrid Finisher Move 1. Steelekick. 2. Steelemission. 3. Steelesault. 4. Steeledriver. Finisher Description 1. Forceful sidekick to opponents face. 2. Elevated single leg boston crab with Shawn's other knee digging in opponents skull. 3. Reverse moonsault and legdrop combo. 4. Screwdriver suplex. Special Moves Striking Assault: Punching, kicking, biting, scratching, groin abusing, pinching, chair shot, Irish whip onto guard rail, elbow strike, choking, rolling elbow, high dropkick (to face area), eye gouging, spiting.
