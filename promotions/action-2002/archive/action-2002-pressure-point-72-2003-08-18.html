But Keith Scott Zimmerman, in another sign of the apocalypse had however weakly kicked out of Frank's pinfall finisher off the second rope. Frank looked at Trish questioningly and held up three fingers. She shook her head. Some of the Heimdalmaniacs chanted "SLOW COUNT" as Frank brought Keith to his feet once again. Jeff Garvin suddenly staggered out on stage, to massive boos. Frank turned around and saw him there. Pointed. "Message RECEIVED!" SPLUUUUUUUUT. A drawn-out massively delayed CHAIN Smoke. If Keith kicked out of this, he was a cyborg. One. But as it turned out... TWO. He had a broken body to match his broken heart. THREE. Garvin slapped his arms against his thighs as Nick Cave kicked in barely perceptible under the massive roar of the Des Moinesians. It wasn't really Keith's fault, but still. Heimdal's arms weakly came up over his head and Trish did her job and pointed to the top face in Action to signify him as the winner of the match. Frank sat there, brushing back hair, blood, and sweat from his forehead as Garvin got to the bottom of the ramp. He got to his feet as quickly as the beating would allow. Garvin pointed at Frank. With his right hand, he cupped an imaginary belt around his waist. Frank shook his head, and repeated the motion to a much friendlier crowd reception. It was official: Frank Heimdal. Jeff Garvin. In just 13 days one of the two would become the next champion of Action.
