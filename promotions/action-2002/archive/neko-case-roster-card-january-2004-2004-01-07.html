What this is Neko Case’s card from the fed’s own roster page, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 7 January 2004. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Neko Case Birth Name: Alastair Nagatsuka Also Known As: The Outlaw Star Date of Birth: May 28, 1980 Age: 23 Marital Status: Single Hometown: Los Angeles, California Height: 6’1 Weight: 227 Ibs. Hair Color: Black Eye Color: Cobalt Blue Blood Type: 0 Negative Status: Neutral Personality: Neko needs the approval of others or else he withdraws from society. As such, Neko is currently a loner. He is the son of a semi-famous wrestler, and the expectations to match his father's brillance and his own hype weigh extremely heavy on his shoulders. He is confused with the emotions he has and seems geared toward his own self destruction, much like his father. When lonely, he's a heavy drinker as well as a heavy smoker. However, if he's accepted, it is almost the total opposite. Theme Music: Just Because by Jane's Addiction Wrestling Style Trained By: Self Trained Detailed Style: Suplex Based / Technical High Flyer Strengths: Is extremely fast (perhaps even the fastest man on the roster) and uses it to his advantage. Is quite agile, but is a notch below the likes of Coral Avalon attributed to his gained muscle mass. He is a multi-faceted performer in the ring, with his ability to wrestle many styles. He possesses quite a bit of strength, especially in his lower body. He has no existing, putting him ahead of a lot of people on the roster. Also, he has youth on his side. Weaknesses: While heavily trained, he is still very raw. While he has quite an arsenal of suplexes, the technical aspects of his wrestling still need work. He is still adjusting to the pure wrestling style Action has decided to go with, and has a bit of trouble with some of it. Oh, and he can't stand hearing about his performance from smarks.
