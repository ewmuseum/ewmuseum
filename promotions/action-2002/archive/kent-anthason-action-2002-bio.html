Kent Anthason Biography Nicknames The Structure and Method Hometown New Orleans, Louisiana Birthday 2/8/76 Height 6'4 Weight 241 Biography I'm not really going to lie to you... I wasn't different like every other goddamn superstar to ever set foot inside a squared circle. I didn't have an abusive childhood. Actually, I spent most of my childhood playing Atari and watching the Wonder Years with my eighty year old, dying, cajun great aunt who always used to smack me and tell me 'You'll never be anything, Kent! Never!' But, unlike everybody else's family members who condemn them to eternal loss, my aunt was cool on the side. She gave me a joint or two every once and a while to smoke. Hmm. What other interesting things do you need to know... I hit puberty at the ungodly age of sixteen and grew ravashingly beautiful... Yes, even more than you. And was prom king three years in a row, AFTER I got out of high school. Not everyone can say that, can they? Oh, yeah! And there was this one time, I was sucking pudding through a straw and my friend punched my other friend square in the jaw and all this brown pudding shit with green stuff on it came out my nose. It was soo embarassing. I swear to God, I'll never do it again. Then there was this one time I was in some other federation and I was a champion, then I went to another federation with the same letters only different arrangements and was a world champion again! Go me! I'm afraid you'll find me alittle different than the normal wrestler, I guess. I mean, I wasn't sexually abused as a child and grew up to be some silent monster who beats everyone up for the sheer hell of it, and I wasn't some brainiac who drove himself insane with trig theroms. I kinda was just some kid with a really whacked out imagineation who decided to pro-wrestle and really kick ass at it. Therefore, I am cool. Hence, you are not. Sincerely yours, - Kent Anthason.
