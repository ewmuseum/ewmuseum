Jemal Davis Biography Hometown Chicago, Illinois Birthday November 11, 1979 Height 5'10' Weight 211 Biography Jemal Davis had a very tough life growing up. He was not fortunate enough to live in the area of the Windy City that people enjoy visiting. The Sears Tower was not something he lived near. Unfortunately Jemal grew up on the streets with his twin brother Ray. Jemal's mother had him when she was only 16, but she gave him up for adoption soon after his birth. Jemal lived in foster homes for several years, until he eventually ran away. His many foster parents describe him as being a trouble child, and somebody you wouldn't want to meet up with in on a dark night. However many of them also felt that his potential was unlimited if he ever decided to do something with his life. Jemal was in and out of gangs his whole life. He joined his first gang when he was only 11 years old. He lived on the streets, was a drug addict, and committed his fair share of crimes along the way as well. Somehow he also managed to stay alive, stay away from the police, and remain loyal to his friends. According to Jemal, 'loyalty is what keeps you alive on the streets. If everybody is watching everybody elses backs, you'll be fine.' When Jemal was 19, his brother Ray was shot and killed in a drive by shooting. Ray and Jemal had done everything together. They were the best of friends, and they had kept each other alive for so long. That one, very unfortunate incident, was what changed Jemal's life forever. He made a decision to make something out of himself. He entered a rehab center, and began to work out. By age 21, Jemal was clean. He was a new person. He had left the gangs, moved into an apartment, went back to school, and found a decent job. It is unfortunate that such a tragic event had to occur for Jemal to realize his life was in a downward spiral. But he did manage to break out of everything that had plagued him for so many years. For the first time in his life, Jemal had a future that had just a little bit of gold at the end of the tunnel. Then one day in early 2001, after watching some old wrestling tapes he had purchased for $0.99 at the local video store, Jemal realized what he wanted to do with his life. He was going to be a professional wrestler! He decided he needed to enter the world of sports entertainment. He had the opportunity to be trained by a former PIW Superstar and the man who eventually became the first Action! Champion, Chris Fear. Fear was out of work at the time and took just a few students under his wing, Jemal Davis was one of them. He was a natural, easily picking up new moves. He mastered the 450 Splash just as easily as an armbar. It was clear one day he would reach the big time. His relaxed attitude, and his willingness to work as hard as he has to to reach the top will no doubt be an asset that will help him on his journey. However, he has also come to a realization that he believes will help him on his journey to the top. It is the one thing that almost everybody agrees upon about Jemal. While he is willing to put in a lot of hard work, he is also willing to do anything to make it to the top. Jemal has been known to be very polite, almost to polite, to those above him. But he will stab them in the back faster than you could imagine. He is known as somebody who will say one thing to your face, and the exact opposite behind your back. At times this can get Davis in a lot of trouble, but it can also help him climb the ladder that much faster. Jemal Davis will do whatever it takes to be the best. Jemal is now 22 years of age. He has seen just about everything in his short lifetime. He feels he is justified in everything that he has ever done, but also realizes that he has walked a very fine line for much of his life. He is on the right side of that line. Not too much can surpise him anymore. For there is only one thing left for Jemal to see. There is only one more thing that he wants to see. That is the World Championship belt around his waist. Someday he knows he will get there. Someday...
