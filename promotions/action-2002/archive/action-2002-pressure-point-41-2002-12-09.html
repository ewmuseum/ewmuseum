Simon Seaman, decked in a blue, pastel, leisure suit sat there in silence. He didn’t seem to be doing much at all. He just sat in his chair and stared at the wall for a good while. Sure, some could say that he was doing absolutely nothing at all... or was the rest of the world just doing too much? Nope. The former was correct. You could hear a pin drop in that locker room, though it’d probably disturb the beautiful silence that surrounded him. If you put that sound of silence on a CD, it wouldn’t top the Billboard charts, but it seemed to fit in this situation. ‘S2’ felt calm. A serene scene it was. Was he thinking about his life? Was he thinking about the future? How about potential opponents for that Championship he currently was in possession was? “All right. Here we go. This is going to be sweet.” He stated casually as he held the Carnage Title up in front of his face. Simon glared at it and ever so slowly lifted a toothbrush to his mouth. If this moment had a sponsor, it would be Colgate. “Thssis an ashummm... ashom...” Only a dentist could comprehend those words. Unfortunately, there wasn’t one in sight. So, he paused for a second and repeated himself. “This is awesome. Who else could have guessed that this Title wasn’t only for wearing and hitting people over the head with? I’ve got to write this down.” He set the brush aside and retrieved a pen and a pad of paper. “Use number two... mirror.” He proclaimed with glee until it was time to proceed. The Champion... of Carnage by the way... brushed in the most appropriate manner. Not back and forth, but in picture perfect circles. It was like ballet. A sad and mind numbingly boring ballet to the extreme. It is no wonder why he has that Title. Then, things got interesting. Things referred to in the previous sentence... basically... things. In the corner of the room sat a fairly large present, adorned with elaborate wrapping paper and a bow to match. “Whashtha...” Was all he could mutter. Simon dropped everything on the floor and walked around, attempting to find a place to spit the contents of his mouth into. In a rush, he swallowed it all down in an uncomfortable fashion instead. “Mmm. Toothpasty.” He said sarcastically. He made his way to the gift and picked it up. S2 shook it a little and then shook it again. “Do I dare?” Simon asked himself. Seconds later, the answer was as clear as crystal Pepsi. The tag attached to it stated it was meant for him. In addition to that, it was from anonymous. Definitely not suspicious in any way, shape, or form. “Oh, I double dare. Mark Sommers-Nickelodeon-game-show-style, baby.” Just to make sure, he threw the gift against the wall. A Seaman tradition if there ever was one. It was go time and Simon did not disappoint as he carefully opened the early Christmas gift. “Do not open till Pressure Point 41?” He stated with confusion. “Crazy Christmas gift, Pressure Point 41 is happening right now! Stop being so stupid... oh, you.” It was his mission to keep the ribbon and gift wrap in tact for the following holiday season, or die trying. He popped the top open and rummaged through the contents of the box. Chicken dance Elmo this gift was not. Simon was speechless as he caught a simple glimpse inside. Placing the cover back on, he did as best he could to restore the packaging to its original condition. He set it back where it was and then took a seat. “La la la... la la dee da...” He mumbled in order to distract himself from what he just saw. Did it work? In a union labor sort of way, yes. With the Carnage Belt in hand, he left the room to get himself some fresh air. “It always has to be a surprise. I asked for a bike when I was seven and I got a surprise. Neither man moved much except for Flyer throwing his leg up into the air and slamming it down into the mat repeatedly. Whether he knew he was doing this was truly anyone’s guess. At the count of six, Malone began to stir. He hobbled to his feet, breaking the count at eight. Flyer wasn’t so lucky, as when he began to stir; Malone already grasped him by his hair and pulled at the roots. Flyer was pulled to his feet, and Malone swatted his arms away from his chest once more before delivering another shot of Canadian Violence to Flyer’s already blood red chest. When he realized that Flyer had ducked and was now in the center of the ring, it was too late. A Superkick delivered square in the jaw, sending Malone over the top rope and landing on the apron. Malone ducked a flying forearm, and then proceeded to rake Flyer’s eyes. Flyer stumbled around blindly, as Malone sprang up to the top rope. Malone went for another, but it was blocked, and Flyer choose to slap the taste out of Malone’s mouth to the shock of the audience.
