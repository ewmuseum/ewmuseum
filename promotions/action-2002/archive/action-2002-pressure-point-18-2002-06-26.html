Denton called for the Denton Driver toward the crowd that instantly booed him, but as he turned around to execute it, he was met with Silencer from a surprisingly-recovered Simon Seaman. Seaman shook off whatever effects that the Death Plunge had on him, and rolled over into the cover. One. Two. No. Because Syphon Fission was back up, and he was pissed off. He immediately deposited Seaman on the outside like he was nothing, and then picked up what he brought into the ring before he broke up the pin. A table. He set it up in the center of the ring, to the protest of the referee. Fission kicked the ref in the gut and nailed him with the Death Plunge. Laughing at the carnage he just brought, Fission got back to his feet and then he pulled Joey Malone to his feet. He then placed Malone up on the top rope and climbed up. The fans all let out a massive chorus of boos, and they all knew what Fission wanted to do. Faded Dreams. No. Malone slipped out before Fission could hook it, and landed behind Fission in the canvas. As Fission tried to turn around and counter whatever Joey was doing, Joey leapt up to the level of the ropes that Fission was on and hooked a waistlock before he proceeded to German suplex himself and Fission through the table, to a tremendous pop from the crowd. Both men were down and out, and as the second referee entered the ring, he realized that this match could not continue, and called for the bell. With the fans abuzz over what just happened, they immediately booed the decision when it was announced by Hoskins. The cameras faded away from the ring, with the two men that would go into the biggest undertaking in their lives in just a few days down and out in the ring. ---- It'd Been A Long Day. It'd Been A Long Day. It'd been a long day, and Simon Seaman was ready to pack it all in. The Six Man Tag Team Match took a lot out of him, no doubt. Placing the C Championship lightly in the back of his car, he closed the door and headed to the driver's seat. He stopped. For the handle looked to be broken. Shaking his head and thinking that he saw the image wrong, 'S2' opened the car door up. Wham. The Crowd booed, as Seaman's head flung into to the side of the van right behind him. KroW emerged from the automobile... black trademark baseball bat in hand. "I won't kill you yet." He said, grabbing hold of 'S2's' face. "I'll let fate take me into the Pay-Per-View to do that." ---- Out Cold. Out Cold. The Crowd was electric as the show came back on for one final time as they stood, just waiting for what would happen next. All of a sudden... they started to boo. LLB walked down the ramp, carrying Christopher Fox on his shoulder. He rolled the Challenger into the ring, then entering himself and pulling a microphone from his back jean pocket. "Objection!... or should I say erection!? I don't really know..." 'The Law' said, mocking Fox who was long gone on the canvas. "Fox... I was going to let you arrive at the Pay-Per-View in one piece. I was going to let you show up for one final testify... I was going to let you TRY and prove my guilt. The key words: I was going to. Now... now for fuck sake I won't." LLB laughed as he picked up the fallen Fox and threw him into the corner of the ring. "But you just pissed me off too much. The guilt is SO clear... I just can't let you live another day, without hard time..." The Fans began to cheer against LLB, as they wildly filled the ring with as many shouts as possible. Not even caring, LLB picked up Fox and tied him up in the top and middle rope. Then, ripping off his "Brace Yourself... it's time to testify." t-shirt, 'The Law' revealed himself to be wearing the Action Championship. "You want to know how long it took me to wash this? FOREVER! You want to know how long it took me to wash this!?" LLB pointed to the side of his face. "ETERNITY!" Slowly taking the Title away from his waist, and dragging it up in the air with his left hand as a few camera's flickered... with Fox completely helpless... LLB got right in his face. "Asshole! Objection! Repeat: OBJECTION! You stupid brat... you skip around... so carelessly... so cute... you have the whole fucking 'Jury' at your fingertips... you have it ALL Fox, you really fucking do... But then you have to BREAK all that. Then you have to be a stupid fucking loser. You object to me... you get in MY way. And I do not have the TIME to deal with you. I can't stop the trial for a mere little boy..." He looked at his Title. "Not for long, anyway." And clubbing Fox in the side of the head with all he had, imprints could basically be seen from the Action! Championship as it drilled into Fox's cheek. He smashed him again. Blood began to spill from his mouth and nose... so much that some deep Fans of Fox tried to jump the rail, only to be held back by security. He smashed him once more. "OBJECTION 'Kid'... I dare you to get off to that." He untied Christopher from the ropes as he held him up... only to take one step back and hammer him with the 'Erroneous Conclusion'. "Black and White" followed up the move, as Fox laid back down in a pile of his own blood, totally wiped out. LLB grabbed his Championship again as he went to the turnbuckle corner, bouncing off the bottom rope while holding onto the ring post. The credits showed... leaving one final view in the middle of the ring... Christopher Fox out cold, covered in his own blood... and his former best friend, the Action! Champion, LLB, standing on the bottom rope... face to the Crowd... now raising the A! Title high in the air... with no intent of losing it.
