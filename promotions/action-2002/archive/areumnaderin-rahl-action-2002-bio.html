Areumna'derin Rahl Biography Nicknames The Pillager, Rahl, ‘The Pillager’ Areumna’derin Rahl Hometown Acbanistain, but is now living in Rome, Italy. Birthday December 25th, 1975 Height 6'4' Weight 246 Biography In order to be the character you gotta get inside the character right? Thats what Caleb Mecca aka Areumna'derin Rahl did, he just took it to a whole new level. Caleb Mecca was born in Acbanistain a place not many people know about. Thats just one of the many secerts that Caleb holds to himself only. When Caleb first discovered his love and passion for wrestling he was only at a young tender age of 7 years old. Caleb wasn't your normal 7 year old child though, he wasn't hyper like the rest of the kids were; he wasn't outgoing like any other 7 year old was; and he wasn't stubborn like the rest of his age group. Instead Caleb didn't want friends, he didn't want toys, all he wanted was to be left alone and at first he was granted that wish by the other kids, untill he got to the 4th grade. Once he got there his peers soon started to notice that he wasn't like the rest of them, for onething he didn't have one single friend, and he was the smelly kid in the class and his face was messed up in more ways then one. The teachers even made Caleb feel like an outcast by always placing him in the back of the room. Was Caleb a troubled child? Never; for Caleb had gotten good grades all through his school career and graduated with his class. Caleb's life took a change for the worst though. Place: Rumored to be somewhere on the west coast It was a cool, calm night in December when a tragic accident happened. It involved 2 cars and Caleb himself, there he sat in the drivers seat looking at the havoc that he had caused. A guy rushed to the seen of the accident and pulled Caleb out of the car with blood rushing from his hands dripping onto his hands, and all the guy could understand was 'Everybody hates me now, so fuck it Bloods on my face and my hands and I don't know why, I'm not afraid to cry.' Caleb was sitting straight up looking straight into the 2 other cars that had hit head on. Only Caleb knew what had happened that night as the authority's couldn't find out how the car crash happened. All Caleb cared about was his life he didn't care that possibly a mom and a dad died and that a child would be coming home to find a place that he called home with no mother or dad. From that day on Caleb considered it a new life for him. His house was gone; why? Cause he hadn't paid his taxes. His car was gone why? Cause he had totalled it, he was running low on food why? Cause he had eaten all his food and hasn't gone shopping for weeks now. He needed money and couldn't get a job, but a low paying job as a wrestler in a indy fed called West Coast Wrestling Federation. There he struggled from match to match with no gimmick, no fans, and no talent skills. Of course he had the moves but the fans didn't care for the moves they wanted gimmicks, and cutting edge promos something Caleb lacked in. He was soon cut from the indy fed due to his gimmick-less/boring promos that he tried to save his job with. Caleb soon fell into the drug scene and was a drug abuser for 2 or 3 years surviving off of drug money and the affects of weed and crank and all that other fun stuff. After getting his act back together he decided that he needed to get a steady job and came up with his gimmick for Areumna'derin Rahl and spent the rest of his drug money to go to wrestling school and that is where he learned all his moves and his trademark mask. He soon learned about the finer things in life, and that would be wrestling. Aremna'derin was born again. Say what you want to say about Aremna'derin but what it all boils down to is Aremna'derin Rahl will always be the Pillager.
