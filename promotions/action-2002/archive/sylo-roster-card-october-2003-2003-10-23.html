What this is Sylo’s card from the fed’s own roster page, as the site showed it in the Wayback capture of 23 October 2003. The site’s menus and footer are not reproduced; the card’s text is, field by field, with blank fields left blank. The block headed Off Stage Information is the handler’s own entry on the card.
Sylo Birth Name: Also Known As: 'The super beast','The one man wrecking crew','The destroyer' Date of Birth: Age: 27 Marital Status: n/a Hometown: Somewhere inside the state of Georgia Height: 7'1' Weight: 380 lbs. Hair Color: Blue Eye Color: blue Blood Type: o- Status: Heel Personality: Monster Theme Music: 'Sun dosen't rise' - Mushroomhead Wrestling Style Trained By: none Detailed Style: Powerhouse-brawler-hardcore Strengths: His power Weaknesses: He's slow in the ring and has a bad neck Signature Moves: This guide was written for the soul purpose of giving information as to how Sylo’s system of moves works. This is not a guide for others to take and use as their own and some moves can be credited to The big book of wrestling moves. Submission moves: Ankle Lock: The victim is on their stomach, Sylo grabs one leg by the ankle pushing it forward so the knee is bent and twists at the victim's ankle. Argentine Back Breaker: Sylo lifts the victim up so that they are laying across his shoulders. Sylo hooks the victim's neck and leg and applies pressure. Variations: • Reverse Death Valley Driver: dropping the victim head first on the mat. • Systematic Shutdown: Sylo swings the victim up high in the air and drops them down with a reverse DDT (almost like a reverse F5) Rib crusher/Bear Hug: Sylo wraps his arms around the victim and lifts them off the mat squeezing with all his power. Variations: • Hang man: Hanging the opponent up on the top rope • Belly to belly suplex: Throwing the opponent over Sylo’s head and to the mat. • Spinebuster: A hard slam to the mat. Technical Moves: Suplex: Sylo hooks the victims arm under his arm and lifts them up dropping them straight back onto their back. Variations: • High stalling suplex: Just like the suplex except the victim is held in the air longer. • Jackhammer: Instead of being drove straight back the victim is turned at an angle and driven to the mat. • Front suplex: Reverse of a suplex Belly to Belly suplex: Sylo grabs the victim almost like a bear hug but instead tosses the victim over his head onto the mat. Variations: • Reverse Russian leg sweep: Sylo pushes forward driving the opponent back first onto the mat. Belly to Back suplex: Sylo grabs the opponent from behind in a bear hug type manner then slings them over his head driving the opponent down on their neck/shoulders. Variations: • Russian Leg Sweep: Sylo drives the opponent face first into the mat. 3/4th bulldog (or neckbreaker): Sylo grabs the opponent by the neck placing it on his shoulder and leaps forward. Power/Roughtneck moves Big boot: Sylo charges and drives his boot into the face of the opponent. Choke slam: Sylo puts his hand around the victims neck and lifts them high into the air only to drive them down hard onto the mat. Double handed sitdown choke powerbomb (Baldo bomb): Sylo lifts the opponent up by their shoulders only to drop them into a sit down power bomb. Flying shoulder block: Sylo charges toward the opponent and drives his shoulder into their chest with impact. Body press slam: Sylo lifts his opponent in a body press and then slams them down onto the mat. Gorilla press slam: Sylo lifts the opponent high in the air and then steps away letting the opponent freefall to the mat. Big punches: Sylo uses big lefts and rights to stun his opponent(s). Snap power bomb: Sylo places the victims head between his legs, lifts at their waist and then drops them down hard on the mat. Running close line: Sylo rushes forward and nails the opponent with a hard close line. Signature moves The slaughter:Sylo charges his opponent nailing them with a massive spear. Malicious Nightmare : Sylo places the opponents head on the second turnbuckle (face toward the turnbuckle). He then steps back and charges forward driving a knee into the back of the opponents head. War cry: The opponent tries to nail various punches and kicks on Sylo which doesn’t phase him. He roars out and nails the opponent with a hellacious close line. Downfall: Sylo shoots the opponent off the ropes and on the return lifts them up into a massive spine buster. Blood Loss: Sylo tosses the opponent up in the air and as they come down he grabs the back of their head and drives his knee up into their face. Sick: Sylo lifts the opponent up in a power bomb position but as their going down he choke slams them down hard into the mat…thus making the landing…sick. Tap the fuck out: Sylo grabs one of the opponents legs and locks it under his arm. He then grabs the opponent’s head and locks it under the other arm and pulls (Sort of like a modified dragon sleeper) Variations: • Reverse DDT: The victims head is driven into the mat. • Rolling reverse neck breaker: The victim is rolled and slammed onto the mat with force. Ring tendencies: • His neck, legs, and right shoulder are his weak points. • He goes for the early kill that way not to use up too much energy • Sylo uses his power over anything else, including his head. • He can take an extreme amount of punishment. • He has great ring awareness. • He hardly ever does any flying moves • Relies on power but can use technical when needed. • He can be very intimidating. Trademark Name: Trademark Move: The Systematic Shutdown: Sylo scoops his opponent up in a reverse DVD position and then swings the opponent around (still holding on) to where their body is almost completely vertical (almost in suplex fashion) but soon drives them down head first into the mat. Injuries Current Injuries: None Nagging Injuries: Neck problems History Years Pro: 2 Manager: none Allies: None Nemesis: KroW, Wippit Guud, Jamal Wilson Current Team: None Past Teams: Team Johnson (PIW), The Celestials (PCW) Action Wrestling Title History: None Action Wrestling Award History: None Other Federations: NTICW, PCW, GW, PIW, aWc Other Federation Awards: PIW World Champion(x1), GW International Champion(x1), PCW Rising Star Champion(x1), GW Tag Team Champion(x1) Attributes Intelligence: 8 Strength: 10 Toughness: 10 Speed: 5 Endurance: 8 Courage: 9 Charisma: 6 Skill: 9 Biography Mosley Mental Institution 487 Mosley dr. Austin, Texas, 27065 Record number: 32423-5543-432423-134 Patient #364-71-9762: Jay Edward Sylo Date: February 14th, 2003 Time: 10:00 am I’ve recorded that the patient has went through many traumatic situations, which has caused this nervous break down. As I write this I feel extremely sorry for him, but I know that deep down inside there lies other troubles. Recorded Statement #19347-049: “Life hasn’t been easy for me since I was five. I’m not sitting here trying to bitch and complain to you but you asked so I’ll tell you. Try witnessing your parents brains scattered across their white walls. It is, in fact, very disturbing but now, well now it just doesn’t bother me.” ---- It seems he’s became cold to human affection. The childhood was outlined with many foster families, none in which worked out. He shows minor signs of remorse though; maybe I can break through this hard shell of his and find out what makes him tick. This will be my biggest challenge to date. Patient #364-71-9762: Jay Edward Sylo Date: February 17th, 2003 Time: 4:00 pm It seems he holds bitter feelings toward a former girlfriend who cheated around on the patient. He holds an extreme hatred for the female and even goes as far as to blame her for the death of his grandmother. Recorded Statement #12364-869: ”I hate that bitch. My grandmother didn’t like her and I was blind with love…Fuck love. I know it was the stress of me dating Jade that did it…y’know killed my grandmother. Fucking bitch, I hope she rots in hell. Her and that dick fuck she cheated on me with.” ---- I then asked the patient to tell me about his teen years. He seemed normal enough except for the death of his parents. Recorded Statement #20758-375: “I was your typical guy I guess…I liked getting ass, I liked playing sports, I liked when girls looked at me. I pumped weight getting my muscles as big as possible because I liked the attention I got from people…it felt good. Now I realize it was all for nothing, people usually end up being nothing more than shit and friends? Fuck friends…there is no such thing as friends. I’ve never needed anybody or anything but me and that’s it,” ---- The patient shows bitter animosity towards the human race. I’m starting to figure it out though; He just wanted someone to love him. Patient #364-71-9762: Jay Edward Sylo Date: February 20th, 2003 Time: 9:00 am Today I wanted to go through his profession and that was, of course, wrestling. I asked him many questions about the “sport” as some would call it and his replies were very insightful. He told me of how he trained in the PWA and soon found a job with a man by the name of Jacob Yoder. I bold this name just incase it becomes of some importance, I shall do this with all names stated by me. Sylo was angered with the untimely closing of NTICW and spoke out against it, this of course angered the “higher up’s” Recorded Statement #57436-510: “Yeah I was pissed when NTICW closed. The head branch thought some funny business was going on but if you ask me I think they just wanted the talent. I wasn’t about to go work for them when they were killing off something I actually enjoyed. So I rebelled, I dyed my hair blue, I put in blue contacts, and I became from that day forward a monster. The Sylo you see in the ring and the Sylo you see before you are very much the same person,” ---- The patient went on to list that he went to a federation named Global Wrestling and won his first title there. He soon departed for what was known as PCW and became a champion there as well. Being on top doesn’t seem to affect him though, he seems like he’s never happy. Patient #364-71-9762: Jay Edward Sylo Date: February 23rd, 2003 Time: 2:00pm Note: This is the final session with said patient. In my final session I finally ventured to ask him what sent him over the edge, what made him come here. He told me of how he was in PIW and finally became a world champion, every wrestlers one goal. He told me of how PIW closed and of how Jonathan Enders offered him a job in aWc. Sylo took it and began work and after a run with top star Jamal Wilson it looked like Sylo would have a healthy future. Recorded Statement #91037-884 “I thought everything was finally going to be ok. I missed PIW, sure, the money was good and I was on top. No I don’t have the famous “Triple H” syndrome but you know, it was nice. Then I went to work a house show, no big deal really. They booked me against a new guy, it was supposed to be a squash and as I went to pile drive the kid he didn’t tuck…and I snapped his neck. Why did it send me over the edge? Because I’m tired of this kind of shit, I’m tired of it all being my fault…I didn’t want to kill that kid…hell he had a young wife and a kid for gods sake. I know what it’s like to grow up with out either parent…and I just didn’t want that for that kid,” ---- It appears that the patient finally shows human emotions. Maybe they aren’t as clear as many would like but they are there…deep down inside. I deem Jay Edward Sylo to be clinically sane. On a side note though I do feel he is emotionally insane but I do not feel it will conflict anymore with his profession or personal life. Best wishes to Mr. Sylo in Action!
