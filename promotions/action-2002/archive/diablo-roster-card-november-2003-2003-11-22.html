But there's some relevant information for each wrestler in here. TDM. They've had a roller coaster of gimmicks. From crazy Mexicans to vicious heel Mexicans to crazy Mexicans. Well, looks like they're back at the vicious heel Mexicans. Or are they? Diablo and Edguardo were born in Tijuana, and fled to San Diego with their mother at the ages of 8 and 6, respectively. They never knew their father--the bastard left a month or two after Edguardo was born. Their mother was killed by a thug when Diablo was 11, so he took care of Edguardo and himself by stealing everything from cars to food. Diablo and Edguardo didn't even live under a roof until they were 15 and 13, and this was when they slept in a small arena outside San Diego and worked there as janitors. Every day, Diablo and Edguardo would seek openings at any number of wrestling schools. But they never had enough money. So Diablo and Edguardo, mimicking the famous luchadores that they had seen as children, trained with each other. One day, fate dealt TDM the hand that they needed. A small independent promotion was coming into town and had advertised an open tag team invitational. With nothing better to do, Diablo and Edguardo decided to enter. They stunned everyone; who would have thought that two Mexican teenagers would be able to compete against grown men? Diablo and Edguardo's reckless style earned them the nickname 'Los Matadores' (The Killers). They wrestled independently and, once they had enough money to buy a car, did some traveling. But then, something terrible happened. In one of their early matches as Those Damned Mexicans, Diablo and Edguardo faced a tag team known as Los Tecnicos. During that match, one of Los Tecnicos delivered a DDT to Edguardo on a chair. That DDT put Edguardo into a coma for three weeks. When Edguardo emerged, he was legally insane. He humped things at random, tended to emit senseless commentary, and was a danger to anyone other than Diablo. So then, TDM entered IWO. TDM has had quite possibly one of the strangest and most memorable matches in IWO history. This was the TLC Match featuring TDM (double tag champs), the Suicide Kings, and the Jack Daniels Connection. The actual TLC part featured some sick stuff, like a Clockwork DDT off a ladder through a table by Jack Breaker, and the absolutely sick double-team that TDM called Go to Mexican World (it has now become the EMP (yes, I know I used this for a different move, but that name's been changed too), or the Experience Mexicans Project). After the IWO shut down, Diablo was out of work and needed a job to support himself and Edguardo. This is when he received the call. A man only known as 'Deep Throat' offered Diablo a job as hired help. All Diablo had to do was return to his teenage occupations as a car booster and drug dealer, and Diablo would make himself some good money. The question remained: would Diablo accept? Initially, Diablo said, 'No fuckin' way, man,' but then Deep Throat dangled the ultimate carrot: information about Diablo's father. So now, as TDM has earned a job in Action! Wrestling, Diablo must decide: how badly does he want to meet his father? (A Note from Marc: I'll have two styles of roleplay. One is the wild and crazy style I've used for most of my career. The second is one where I actually try to continue this storyline I've started. We'll see how it develops.) TAG FINISHERS (they are all craaaaaaaaazy) 1) M.I.A. (Mexicans in Action) –stacked powerbomb. Edguardo flips the victim up in a powerbomb, and then Diablo picks up Edguardo onto his shoulders. Diablo seems to deliver an electric chair drop to Edguardo, when in reality, Edguardo is powerbombing the victim. This move can be done (theoretically) from the top rope, too, but won’t that be the day? 2) Burnout–reverse Russian legsweep/DDT. Can be done from the top rope, and is very effective when delivered. 3) EMP (Experience Mexicans Project)–top-rope 450 splash/top-rope somersault leg drop. Absolutely nuts. 4) M3 (Mad Mexican Move) –combination of Diablo’s Border Kick (charging thrust kick) and Edguardo’s Spick Kick (bicycle Enziguri). Special. And the name’s abbreviation sounds cooler than the actual name. 5) Barrio Bomb –Electric chair drop/top-rope leg drop. This is akin to the Dudley Boyz’s use of the Doomsday Device (a really good setup move but not quite finisher quality). But it still hurts like a bitch…just ask the garden hoses it’s decimated. 6) The 1238--a double 619. It is possibly the second-coolest-looking move in TDM's arsenal (after the EMP). Designed to pay homage to Rey Mysterio Jr. TDM'S PHYSICAL DESCRIPTIONS Diablo and Edguardo, being semi-identical twins, look almost exactly alike. Diablo has about shoulder-length brown hair, which he often dyes red, and Edguardo has puce-dyed dreadlocks. Why he has dreadlocks, I don’t know, and why he has puce hair, I don’t know. Diablo and Edguardo are both about six feet tall, and Diablo has a bit more muscle on his body, so he weighs five pounds more (210) than his brother (205). But, with Edguardo humping things so vigorously, wouldn’t he have more muscles? I don’t know. Why am I talking to myself? I don’t know. Diablo often wears street clothes to the ring, with his attire generally consisting of black DC skateboarding shoes, extremely baggy black cargo pants, and a red t-shirt with the sleeves cut off. Edguardo wears brown hiking boots, magenta tights that say ‘Value Village’ on both sides of them, and a turquoise sweater vest over a green-apple-colored shirt. In other words, Edguardo is painful to behold.
