This is the Diary of Chino Hernandez. December 25th, 1980 Dear Diary, Santa got me this awesome new book thingy. Mommy says it’s a journal, but I like diary better. Mommy says it will help me to become a wealthy author, and one day I can help her with her debts. That’s all I really got for Christmas, but I am really excited. I can’t wait to record all the wonderful things that happen to me! July 14th, 1981 Dear Diary, Why did they take me away from Mommy? The goofy looking man in the black suit told me it was because she was a bad person. I think they are lying to me. I don’t think Mommy loved me anymore. I must have been a bad boy. September 2nd, 1981 Dear Diary, The foster home isn’t very nice. All the girls keep picking on me. They call me a dirty Mexican. It’s not my fault I am a different nationality. I hate women. I hope they can find me a new home soon. December 16th, 1981 Dear Diary, They moved me to a new home! It’s a very nice place, much nicer then my old house. What’s even groovier is that I get to live with two Daddies! Thank goodness. I wasn’t sure how much longer I could live with those pesky women. September 7th, 1988 Dear Diary, Today is the first day of High School. My dads are sending me to a catholic school. It’s obvious they want the best for me, but I hate these dumb uniforms we have to wear. It’s also an all boys’ school, which I don’t really mind. Well, wish me luck! September 8th, 1988 Dear Diary, School is awesome! I made a whole bunch of new friends. There is Darryl, George, Jimmy, Colin, Keith, and Steve. We all have so much fun together. They are even talking me into joining the football team! October 2nd, 1988 Dear Diary, I joined the football team. I am a defensive lineman. The coach says I will be a great advantage with my build. It’s hard work, but definitely worth it. We all get really sweaty, but take showers together after practice, so it’s not that bad. October 18th, 1992 Dear Diary, Well my last season of football is over. I’m really kind of upset. I know it sounds weird, but I was really beginning to like the quality time I was spending with my friends. I look forward to the showers that we take together. Well I guess I’ll just have to find some sort of substitute. May 4th, 1993 Dear Diary, School is almost over! Things are going so groovy. I found a place that is for guys only. Good thing, because girls are so icky. It’s called shooters. My Saturday nights will never be the same. April 27th, 1999 Dear Diary, I met such a wonderful African American last night. His name is Jamal, and he is such a dreamboat. He told me he wrestles hot sweaty men. He must have noticed my groovy dancing, so he just came up to me and started grinding. Well one thing led to another and I ended up having a slumber party. Let me tell you, you haven’t lived till you’ve played twister with an African American. June 4th, 1999 Dear Diary, I have been researching Jamal’s profession of wrestling. I absolutely love it! He is so lucky. He gets to run around a ring all day with other hot sweaty men in tights. What an amazing job he has. Tomorrow I am going to ask him if he can teach me a few pointers. August 22nd, 2001 Dear Diary, I feel so stinky. Jamal is in PIW having a blast, while I’m stuck here on the Indies. Granted I should be appreciative of everything he gave me, but I can’t stand being without him. Sometimes I cry at night, dreaming of him. Maybe one day I will meet my African American friend again. September 21st, 2002 Dear Diary, JAMAL AND I MADE IT! A few weeks ago I received a call from him asking for some independent footage of me. Without a second thought, I sent him the wrestling, along with some other personal footage. As it turns out, he sent Action! Wrestling a tape of us, and now they want Jamal and I to wrestle together for them. I am so ready for some hot gay action! Gay meaning happy of coarse.
