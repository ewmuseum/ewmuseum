Duel Biography Nicknames Alex, Lex Hometown Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Birthday July 15th, 1974 Height 6'3' Weight 286 Biography Alexander Lawson was born to Alex and Amelia (Scaramuzzo) Lawson on July 15th, 1974, in metropolitan Philadelphia. Alex, a police officer, had rapidly risen through the ranks of the Philadelphia Police Department, and he was less than a year away from being promoted to detective when he was shot and killed in a routine traffic stop on February 15th, 1981. After the funeral, Amelia found young Alex in a state of blind rage, driving his fists time and again into the sides of a blue post office box. He spent nearly two weeks in the hospital, floating in and out of consciousness with no real signs of what was wrong with him. Just as suddenly as his symptoms came on, however, they disappeared, leaving a seemingly healthy young boy. However, things weren't easy for young Lex, as his mother had to find two jobs just to be able to make ends meet, and he often found himself the victim of childish teasing and bullying at his school. In spite of his marked intelligence and athletic ability, Lex Lawson's high school record was marred with one other thing - a history of fights that the boy neither remembered nor even seemed to realize. He eventually went on to Penn State and graduated with a degree in political science before progressing to Dickison Law College. While in law school, he worked nights with a local federation and eventually was trained there, falling in love with the sport. After earning a law degree, Lex went on the Philadelphia Police Department, becoming the fastest promoted to detective in the organization's history. After leaving the force in 1999, he trained at Dominic DeNucci's school in Freedom, Pennsylvania, before earning a shot with LCW (which was then known as the Mark Madden Wrestling Federation, and was financed by wrestling fan and commentator Mark Madden). After Madden's involvement with the promotion ended, Lex, wrestling as Lex Laww, ended up flourishing, until he ran across his nemesis from the MMWF days, Mike Static. Static was a vicious and uncaring man who once killed Lawson's dog and carried its head to the ring on a pike. He stalked Lawson, eventually getting into his head and forcing him to put his contract on the line in the first and only Lethal Combination match in LCW history. During the match, which featured both men's contracts hanging from the arena roof high above a Hell in a Cell structure, Lex ended up being put through the top of the cell with a ladder wrapped around his head, which caused severe trauma to his brain. While he was never expected to wrestle again, it had become a moot point, as he had lost his contract to Static in the match. Several months later, a masked newcomer debuted, saving Static from a beatdown. Known only as Felony, the newcomer briefly held the tag titles with Static, but a stipulation that they had demanded saw them lose the titles a week later when their stablemates were pinned in a match that neither Static nor Felony were involved in. One week later; however, Felony would have his revenge, as he was booked to take on the most accomplished world champion in LCW history, Used, in what was to be an easy defense for the popular superstar. However, Felony shocked the world first by winning the title, and then moments later, he revealed himself to be Lex Laww. Over the next two years, the fans of LCW saw two sides of Alex Lawson: the decent human being he had once been, and savage, inhumane brute he had become. While he was away from the ring, he had gained over thirty pounds of muscle, and his arsenal became even more devastating, as he focused on suplexes to brutalize his opponent's head and neck, leaving them ripe for his judo choke submission. Often, he would lose matches for refusing the relinquish his hold on his hapless opponent, but it didn't seem to bother Alex. However, after German suplexing an opponent off of the side of a cage in the spring of 2001, it was again believed that Lex's last match was behind him. Surprisingly, he rebounded and returned less than three months later, in late June of 2001, his warring personalities unable to maintain even tenous control of his body, as they had in the past. With the constant struggle of Disassociative Identity Disorder pulling him apart, he took the name Duel and sought help from David Chambers, a psychologist who wrestled for Evo while suffering from amnesia. And while Chambers did help to bring about some improvement in Duel's condition, the man himself admitted that his work was more of a side effect, because Duel had finally found something that made him *want* to be cured: Olivia Heimdal. The younger sister of one of Duel's fellow wrestlers, Frank Heimdal, Duel spent the night at Frank's apartment after the two men had had a few too many to drink one night, and upon seeing Olivia, who had come to pick them up at the bar, he had fallen head over heels in love with her. Even drunk, he had made a point of spending the entire night talking to Olivia, and the two had finally fallen asleep in one another's arms, their lives indelibly changed. For nearly a year, things were perfect for Lex - his wrestling career was flourishing, his love life was wonderful, he had found a new friend in Frank, and his mental health was vastly improved. However, one man ruined it all for him - Mike Static. Returning to Evo, Static had adopted a caring attitude, and he apologized to Duel numerous times for what had happened between them in the past. Duel was reluctant to believe him, however, and when the two ended up sharing a hospital room in Philadelphia after they had each taken particularly nasty spills during a show, Lex finally snapped, and his Felony persona was once more unleashed. However, realizing that he'd quickly be caught if he announced his return, the dark side of Alex Lawson still called himself Duel, but his mannerisms had changed completely. He drove away everyone whom he cared about, including Olivia, en route to winning the Evo Championship. But it was what he had done in the match that would eventually bring out Evo's downfall, as he had crushed his opponent's hand with a beer keg in a falls count anywhere match. His opponent, Rook Black, was perhaps one of the most gifted technicians ever to set foot inside the squared circle, and the damage that Duel had done had surely cost Rook his career. Now finding himself in possession of both of Evo's titles (he was also the Madhouse Champion), Duel went on a rampage until losing both titles - the Evo title was lost to former stablemate David Chambers less than a month later, and the Madhouse championship to Frank Heimdal two months later in a cage match at Evo's only supercard event, Dawning, in what both men would later call the best match of their careers. However, because he had so grievously injured Rook Black, the city of Philadelphia, from which Evo ran all of its shows, had cracked down on Evo, putting much more strict limits on the promotion, which not only hurt their business, but also the business of Club Evo, the dance club/sports bar complex that was the home of their shows. With revenues failing, Edward Olmstead and Tim 'Gravity' Hall, the co-owners of Evo, did what they could to bring in business, even signing talent from the other half of the former LCW, Mercury, to bolster crowds. The signing of the tag team of Entropy Crusade (and later the rest of their stable EEI) proved to be the final straw, though, as Mercury owner David Beaumont filed a suit for breach of contract on the superstars, even while they continued to appear on Mercury programming. Facing a lawsuit that they couldn't win and dwindling interest in the promotion, Olmstead and Hall put it on hiatus, hoping for a suitable financial backer. And while Club Evo flourished with the lifting of the wrestling-related restrictions, it hasn't been the same, and neither has Alex Lawson. For deep down, with his mind now fully healed, Alex has been forced to deal with the knowledge that his actions began the chain reaction that led to the closure of Evo. Without Olivia or any friends to speak of, which Alex Lawson will Action see? The fallen hero trying to atone for his sins, or the deranged madman who uses his anger as fuel to destroy his competition?
