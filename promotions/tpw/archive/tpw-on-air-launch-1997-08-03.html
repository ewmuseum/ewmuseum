Parent Fed's On-Air TPW Launch Segment, 31 July 1997

31 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The theme from "Star Wars" gallantly bursts through the studio as the lights rise in the studios of "Etc..." Bruce Lord and Lindsay Jackson are still seated on the stools as Lindsay is looking into the camera laughing and Bruce is looking at her with a questioning face) BL: I just don't get it....Why the "Star Wars" theme? LJ: (Smiling now, she looks at Bruce as she replies) Because Bruce, the *stars* of tomorrow reside at Toronto Pro Wrestling. BL: (Now realizing...) Ohhh. The TPW. (Shaking his head and looks at Lindsay) Great fed, bad song choice. You picked that out didn't you, Lindsay. LJ: (Slight nod) Uh- huh, I did. I never said I was a DJ, man. I am an "LJ".......get it?.....LJ.....Lindsay Jackson? BL: (Still shaking his head in mock sickness) Leave the jokes to me, babe. (Back to camera) BL: Fans, the TPW is off and running with the stars of tomorrow and Lindsay and myself will bring you the wild coverage of the TPW first hand with the premiere of "TPW Midweek" next Wednesday night. LJ: There ain't nothin' out there like it! You'll have to see it to believe it. BL: (Looking at Lindsay) Not bad, LJ. Two crappy cliches in a row. LJ: (Playfully to Bruce) Here's a third; *Shut up*. BL: (Looking at Lindsay) ...and a fourth; *OK*. (To camera) Fans, the TPW is brought to you by the creators and producers of the best fed in the land, the NPWA. I have no doubt in my mind that this little fed, the TPW, will be just as exciting and unpredictable as the big boys are in the NPWA. LJ: The TPW will act as a stepping stone to the "big leagues", so to speak. And I will tell you this, the early reactions have been all positive. BL: I am really excited about this and all the credit has to go to the braintrust in the NPWA towers. It's nice to see that they don't sit around all day on some dumb on-line chat room or sit on their duffs eMailing each other all day. LJ: They don't do that do they? (Bruce Lord sarcastically shakes his head "no") LJ: I didn't think so. (Quick pause) Fans, drop on by the TPW because I have been told that the TPW will be dropping by the big boys of the NPWA. BL: Changing the subject--- LJ: (Smiling as she interrupts) ---it's about time. BL: (Surprised)...What? LJ: Never mind. BL: (Shaking his head) Women. (Quick pause) Fans, the following video was dropped off our studios after last Thursday Night Thrashings. Let's take a look. (Studio lights fade out as Bruce and Lindsay turn around to view the screen on the wall behind them and the following illuminates) The shot fades up on "Blackheart" Brian Masters, standing in the back of an arena, halfway through the back door. He wears a pair of sunglasses, an ASU t-shirt and blue jeans as he leans against the door jam, a gym bag over his shoulder.] BM: Do I got a challenge I wanna make? Well, I through down the damn gauntlet on Thursday, but since my phone isn't ringing, I'm just going to assume nobody was listening, so I'm gonna start to name names. [Masters removes his sunglasses, folding them over the collar of his shirt and stepping out of the doorway. The door shuts behind him, and the scene becomes a lot darker.] BM: How about The Silent Striker. If you can tear yourself away from your search for a love lost [pauses in an overly dramatic sarcastic swoon], how about you and I climb in the ring on Saturday evening, and get it on. I've been disappointed that our paths haven't crossed thus far, Striker. We've got a lot of similarities. We can both be sullen and moody. We both like to talk about shutting up and getting down to business, and yet we both talk a lot. [Snickers a little bit.] And we both do it for the same reason. Because we can, plain and simple. We both thrive on competition, and we both spent a lot of time eating English hospital food this April. So isn't it appropriate, then, that you should be the first man to get a crack at my Television Title, a Title which I have already earned many times over... and which I will earn many more times over again before it's all said and done. So here's the deal. You sign the papers, we climb in the ring and see which one of us can really back up our mouths. We'll see just how deafening the silent is when the bones in your arm break. [Masters turns on his heel and walks out the arena door.] (The studio lights rise as Bruce and Lindsay once again turns towards the camera) LJ: (Surprised) Wow! Brian Masters challenging Silent Striker? (Shaking her head) Wow. Right here on "Face-Off"? Wow.....when? BL: (Smiling) Assuming the Striker accepts, which I bet he will, "Etc.." will bring that match to you on Saturday August 9th. It will be "Etc..." first title match . LJ: If Striker wins his match at Armaggedon, then he will be the North American Champion, then what? BL: Good question and I already checked with the chatters in the towers and they told me that *both* titles would be up for grabs--- winner take all. LJ: I heard that this is the match everybody wants to see: Masters versus The Striker and the fans get it on the first Saturday after Armaggedon...assuming they are both physically able to perform. BL: (Looking beyond the camera) I have been just told that The Silent One has been contacted and because he is in a meditative zone right now, he will respond before the showdown with Masters. (Slight pause) The Silent Striker faces "Blackheart" Brian Masters on Saturday August 9th and at least one title will be up for grabs. LJ: Next up are Bruce and Lindsay's fearless predictions....we will be right back. (The lights fade and the camera goes to black then reappears to the theme song to "The Olympics". As the horns blare, James Earl Jones thunderously rich voice speaks) "Faster..." [The scene changes to Dante Powell and the Masked Men chasing Dave Diamonddown the aisle of a wrestling arena.] "Stronger..." [Now the picture shows Valtharius The Mad tossing an official out of the ring, over the top rope and into about the 8th row of the audience.] "Dirtier." [Finally we see Gan Green in the woods, just off of a fairway. He looks left. He looks right. He then picks up his golf ball from it's bad lie and hurls it as hard as he can toward the green.] [The picture again returns to the NPWA flag crackling in the breeze.] "The NPWA Summer Olympics. Athleticism at it's finest." (Fade to black..)
