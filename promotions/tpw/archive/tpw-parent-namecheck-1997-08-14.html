Further Parent-Fed Namecheck of TPW, 14 August 1997

14 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SK: Okay, they haven't found anyone yet, so let's go to these pre-recorded comments by Reynoso Torres. ******************************************************************** [The camera pans across a darkened Capetown street. There are very few people on the street at this late hour. One lone man stands in the middle of the street. As the camera pans closer it focuses on the man. It is Reynoso Torres, clad in black jeans, black boots and a red and grey poncho thrown over his shoulders. His black hair drenched from an earlier rainstorm hangs over his eyes. He raises his head slightly just enough for his eyes to be seen through the straggly hair.] RT: This land has seen many a war. The people of South Africa have dealt with one of the greatest physical and emotional struggles in the recent history of man. It is something that I can most definitely emphasize with. For over six months I dealt with the oppression of the NPWA. Throwing me into the lion's den to be ravaged by many of the biggest cats in the NPWA. It didn't matter what the odds I always survived in one way or another. Finally revenge came and it was oh so sweet. It was like the nectar of the ripest fruit. Now the NPWA has taken this away from me. They turned the rest of the Theatre of Pain against me. Then when there was only one to rely on they took him away as well. These are not tears in my eyes. There is no remorse, no change in who I am. There is no need for change. I am... and always will be the most unpredictable, most deadly wrestler in the NPWA. Now, onto business. For some reason the NPWA seems to not want to sign me to a match. Maybe they feel that I can't wrestler with this arm in a sling. Maybe it's just no wrestler wants to get in the ring with me. With all the big talkers around here I know that isn't possible. I mean everyone comes out here saying they want anyone anytime yet no one has my name on their contracts. NPWA, I don't care who it is, I want a match next week or I will not be responsible for what happens. Don't know who to put me up against?? Let me give you a few suggestions. John Hunter... The man that can sink ships and make censors quiver with just a few words. Well instead of beating up on a woman or some primadonna pretty boy, why don't you take your extreme beer guzzlin' ass to the ring next week and get it on with a man that doesn't get scared and hide just because someone shaves his head and talks like he's some loser who can swear. You don't have to give me 'fair warning'. I know exactly what to expect from you. A lot of bad breath and a whole lot of excuses after the match. [camera closes to only show him from the waist up] Steve Sampson... Hey Champ, you want another night in hell... I'm right here waiting for ya. All I ever hear from you is, my wife left me, my kid's in reform school, my brother just crashed my car, my girlfriend has a problem with my wife. The only reason you have that World Title is that Masters got sick and tired of hearing you whine and decided that if you had the World Title you'd shut up for a minute. Hey, if you wanna be a martyr that's fine with me. Bring that cross that you like to carry with you. I'll bring the hammer and nails and finish the job. [camera closes in to only show him from the shoulders up] Brian Masters... Well Brian, I'm not quite sure what you thought I said last week but apparently you didn't get it. You keep saying hey I'll take on anyone but no one is stepping up. 'Blackheart' if you haven't quite figured it out yet, this is me stepping up. This is me saying, I'll take you on and give you the ride of your life. Don't be wasting your time with yesterday's news like Amoral. The man was an embarrassment before and he's even worse now. The idea of him retiring is just too much to imagine but let's get real. You want your title reign to be memorable. I'll make it memorable. I'll also make it very short. [camera closes in to only show his face] Tyr... Apparently I have gotten your attention. That is very good. Problem is that you don't understand the situation. You say my soul is worthless. Wrong my poor Nordic enemy. My soul does not exist. It may have at one point but it started to slowly die almost 9 years ago and it stopped breathing earlier this year. I must pay for my sins?? I have committed no sin. Sin is a consequence of having a conscience. I have no conscience. I do what I do because I want to and for no other reason. Thoughts of retribution never enter my mind. Your revenge means nothing to me. At one point you may have gotten into my mind and gotten the best of me but that is over. The mind games are a thing of the past. You have nothing left to torture me with. Your mistake was to destroy everything that mattered to me. Now I am free and can do whatever I want to do. You have seen your final resting place, Tyr. It was not in the cemetery where my soul was destroyed, it is in the ring where we meet next. I am the black spot on your sun. Man fears the evil that men can do. I am evil. [camera only shows Torres' cold eyes] I am your fear, I am everyone's fear. I am fear! [fade to black] ******************************************************************** SK: Folks we are back! And we are very fortunate, in that the legend, Tobias Hall, has agreed to help out with the commentating for the rest of tonight's card. Welcome aboard, Mr. Hall. TH: Don't welcome me quite yet, Sayonara. The last time I did any commentating, my two co-host mysteriously disappeared! SK: But the just went for a walk through the woods! They told me so. TH: Yeah, okay. You believe your version, and I'll believe mine. SK: In any case, let's go to the Question of the Week while we let Mr. Hall settle in. ******************************************************************** QUESTION OF THE WEEK Every week we will ask a question that the superstars of the NPWA can answer. Last week we asked: What was your favorite match at Armageddon? Here's what the superstars of the NPWA had to say: Bret 'The Quit-man' Hutt: "Speaking as the Worst there is, the worst there was, and the worst there ever will be, my favorite match had to be the World Title match with that great Canadian hero against the dead guy. When that Boy Toy nailed the stiff with a chair, and the mighty Canuck pinned the Reaper, well, it brought tears to my eyes." "Blackheart" Brian Masters: "WarGames was definitely the place to be, and a great match with a not-so-great ending.... but we have no one to blame but ourselves for that. Tell you the truth... I intently watched the North American Title match. The Striker and Toast are two of the few guys around here I can watch and actually feel I'm getting something out of it." Hollywood Duttley: "Well, me and the rest of the dWo loved them all, bra'! Why? Cause we didn't get beat up in ANY of them! Bite me! No! Wait! Don't!" "The Wrecker" Mick McGreggor: "Well that is not too hard to figure out is it. My favorite match was the War Games match, when I drove the remains of ever rapid thinning spine of Steve Sampson right into the mat. I said from day one I wasn't walking no Hardline. Instead rumor has it that the "Superstar" took a little walk himself, straight to the hospital to repair a bruised ego. And I expect a trip to the Chiropractor is not far behind." Chunkotuna: "Um...I missed the show. I got up to fix a snack during the pre-game show, and didn't get back from the kitchen until it was over. Hey! Is that your lunch? Are you gonna eat all that? Dave Diamond: "My favorite match at Armageddon? It should have been War games, if our glorious leader, Jarrett, hadn't blown the whole deal. So I'd have to say it was Mike Mitchells and The Mighty Boss. Everytime I watch it on videotape, I never cease to laugh uncontrollably. Something about a bunch of no-talent losers blowing up really cracks me up. WHHHOOOOOOO!!!" Food Love: "Like, wow man! Like, 'Armageddon' was a show man? Groo-oovay, Daddy-o! I thought the world was going to end, so I got some wicked cinnamon buns, and smoked them until I was seeing vapor trails. Can you dig it?!? Bing-bing! I mean..Bong-Bong! Bong? Hey cats, that's not a bad idea. See-ya!" The Cougar: "Though most would pick their own matches...a true warrior respects the prowess of those around him. At Armageddon...the War Games gathered 12 of the best warriors...that the NPWA had to offer... n a brutal contest...to see who would remain standing. The ancestors watched the match with great pleasure...as did I." Here is the question for this week: Which do you prefer: Boxers or briefs? Wrestlers, send your answers to [name withheld] at " [detail withheld] ". And fans, if you have any questions you'd like the superstars of the NPWA to answer, send it in and we might use it in this weekly feature. Special thanks to all who participated, and an extra special thank you to [name withheld] for bringing Food Love to the NPWA so that he could answer the Question of the Week! ******************************************************************** TH: What in the hell was that fool, Bret Hutt, babbling about? SK: Apparently, Mr. Hutt was watching the wrong Pay-Per-View. TH: Apparently, the boy doesn't have a brain in his head! The only reason I bother with a television set at home is to watch NPWA, and occasionally that new farm league you guys got, the TPW. Otherwise, it's just a $300 plant stand. SK: I'm honored, I think... TH: Why?!? You ain't got nothing to do with it! SK: I meant...well...never mind. And I thought Alexis was difficult! TH: Me?!? Difficult? Sonny, you don't know the meaning of the word difficult! If you want difficult, you should try tangling with Boris Krabkov's dad, Shmirnov Krabkov! He had this one submission hold, I swear your arm bent in three new locations by the time he got that thing on ya. And that's the truth! SK: I'll take your word for it. Folks, before we go to our next match, let's hear from two men who are the talk of the town here in the NPWA, "The Franchise" Steve Stone, and "Fair Warning" John Hunter. ******************************************************************** [Fire in the Hole blasts over the sound system, John Hunter and Steve Stone walk arrogantly down the aisle, spitting and threatening to back-hand many of the fans along the way. They are pelted by dozens of pop cans, and various other items. Finally, reaching the ring, they both climb in] [Stone and Hunter have a brief conversation, and Hunter picks up the microphone] FW: Hello idiots. Everyone from Amoral to Valtharius has been wondering what the hell the two of us are doing. I have heard the whispers, I have heard the questions, and now we're here to address some of you peons. I was walking down the street after a house show a while back, and I saw Steve here fighting four guys in the parking lot of a local watering hole. I didn't help him, as a matter of fact, I just stood there with my beer. When it became apparent that he was going to kick all four of their asses, I chugged the beer, and the four of them shared the shards equally. [Stone laughs in the background, yelling at the fans occasionally] FW: We have no game plan. It's all rather simple. When we decide we want to kick the crap out of someone, we are going to do it. Nug, Dane, Lester have all been ripped up, and the list is getting longer. This is not gang warfare. This is not persecution. This is not at all similar to Josef Stalins purges. This is pure, unadulterated, fun. It's time to establish The Franchise, and my own place in the NPWA. We have been screwed, and passed over time and time again. It will not happen anymore, we will show you idiots the definition of fear and loathing, and in time, you will call us Champs. FW: Before I pass the microphone over to Steve, I just want to quickly address the situation that is coming. The whole NPWA has been waiting to see it for many months. John Hunter vs Brian Masters. Well, you idiots won't have to wait much longer. The promoters are trying everything possible to get us in the ring together. But we both have our own agendas right now. Just rest assured, that when the old man meets me, he will retire, and he will never bother you with his senile ramblings again. Masters, this is your first warning. [The crowd boos lustily, and hammers Hunter with programs, soda cans, and a beer bottle even whizzes by his temple. He just laughs, and passes the microphone to Stone] STONE: Why don't all you idiots do me a favor and shut the hell up! [Fans boo loudly and Stone continue to argue with a few of the fans in the front row.] STONE: Just like the president of this Federation you're all pieces of trash! You have no idea when you see talent and as far as I am concerned you people can blow me! [Hunter laughs in the background] STONE: I wanna address something right now. I wanna address The Southern Rocker.. [Crowd pops and Stone just looks at the crowd in disgust] STONE: Rocker seems you've gotten that yellow streak off your back and found your way back into the NPWA. Well boy if you think I have forgotten about you, you've gotten another thing coming. You ran outta here like a little girl and now you come back thinking everything is just gonna be a cake walk.. Well boy if you think your just gonna walk back in here and make some sort of comeback you've got another thing coming. I will personally dedicate my career in making your comeback a living hell! As far as I am concerned you and me had a match and I want you in that ring next week. I don't care if it is on MNM I don't care if its on Thrashing the fact of the matter is you and me have to settle this and what better way than on live Television were millions of people of TV Land can see me beat you like the no good trailer trash that you are... Moving to bigger fish in the pond, Steve Sampson you came out here last week and you seemed to have issued a challenge if I am not mistaken. Your either pretty brave or just really stupid. Pretty boy you name the time and the place and I will show you what heart ache and humiliation is all about. You'll realize that when I kicked your ass in the PCW the first time that I was only playing with you. This time boy I am gonna show you why I am the rightful man that should be wearing that belt! [Fans boo] STONE: You see when I look into the mirror in the morning I see someone that has earned every single thing he has achieved. I see someone that gives 110% ever time I get in the ring and unlike you chump I don't hide behind anyone when I'm talking! Your nothing more than a pre-primadonna living a fairy-tale and as far as I am concerned your nothing but trash. I will take that belt from you not cause I can but simply cause you don't deserve to wear it. You nothing more than a puppet Sampson and when me and you meet you'll see why The Franchise is the greatest thing ever to hit the world of pro-wrestling. You'll find out first hand why when you look into these eyes why I put the fear of god in ever wrestler I meet. Shine it up Sampson cause your time is coming. FW: And if you people don't like #$@!##!@#!#!$#@$!$$%&^&$%#*@^$*&%^#@! SK: We apologize for that fans.. STONE: The era of a new dynasty is upon the NPWA and whether you like it or not Hunter and me are gonna make life for everyone in the NPWA a living hell! [Fire in the Hole blasts over the sound system] ******************************************************************** SK: Strong words by two of the toughest men in the NPWA. TH: You got it right. Words. All words! Where's the action? Tunney drags me out here to do commentary for a couple of matches...I've been here for 20 minutes, and I have yet to see even a single headlock! If you're going to waste my time like this, I'm outta here! SK: All right! All right! Dave Perry is in the ring to announce the next match. ******************************************************************** DP: Ladies and Gentlemen!! This contest is a No-Time-Limit, Falls-Count- Anywhere, Chicago Street Fight. It is scheduled for one fall. Refereeing this bout, is referee Jamal Patterson. And now, the competitors. Introducing first, from Kokomo, Indiana, weight 235 pounds, here is BOWA THE SNAKE MAN!!! ["Until It Sleeps" by Metallica plays. Bowa walks out wearing a black leather trenchcoat over his wrestling tights. He has his python Blake wrapped around his shoulders as he makes his way down the aisle. When he reaches the ring, he places Blake inside a canvas bag and lays it down in the corner.] TH: This must be a street fight. SK: Why do you say that? TH: Looks at all those morons at ringside with chains, bats, hammers, and various other weapons that have no business in wrestling. In my day, every match was a street fight, but only because we literally fought in the streets! We didn't have any fancy rings. We'd just find a dead end alley, park a few fold-up chairs there for the fans, and beat the snot out of each other. Now that was wrestling! SK: Did you have to walking uphill both ways through the snow to get there? TH: How'd you know? SK: Never mind.... DP: His opponent, accompanied by his manager, Jeremy Stevens, weighing in at 245 pounds, he comes from Columbus, Ohio, here is "The Wrecker" MICK MCGREGGOR!!!! ["Hard Charger" by Porno for Pyros starts to play over the speakers and a video montage on the jumbo screen starts to show various clips of Mick finishing off opponents mixed in with a wrecking ball destroying a building. Suddenly, McGreggor charges from the back and immediately begins to attack Bowa.] SK: Patterson signals for the bell, and this match is under way. McGreggor is dominating early as he rains blows on Bowa. TH: The boy looks a little peeved. SK: Well, Bowa called McGreggor on. He's getting what he asked for, although I don't think it is what he expected. TH: You're telling me! I also notice that Stevens is nowhere in sight. Perhaps he thought it best if he just kept out of the way. SK: You could be right. You know Tobias, seeing these two go at it, it kind of reminds me of some of your classic battles with the patriarch of the Hutt clan, Stewed Hutt. TH: How so? SK: Well, you've got two guy, who are fairly evenly matched, pound for pound, in an all-out brawl to see who will survive. It's almost exactly like some of your matches with Stewed that I used to go to see with my dad. I mean, you guys would just beat the living tar out of each other, then turn around and do it again the next night. You inspired so many of the wrestlers of today. I'm sure you are a hero to many of these youngsters. TH: Truly fascinating. Now, can I ask you something? SK: Shoot. TH: Can we get back to calling the match? SK: Hunh? Oh! Yeah! Okay! Bowa has finally turned thing around a bit and has started firing back a few blows of his own. He whips McGreggor to the turnbuckle, reversal! And Bowa hits the corner hard! McGreggor follows in quickly with a backbreaker. He hits it, and holds it down, bending Bowa over his knee. TH: That's what I like to see! The man saw how hard Bowa hit the corner, and goes for where he believes Bowa might have been softened up; the lower back. From there, he should go to suplexes that land on the back, and follow that with a Boston Crab or a Camel Clutch for the win. SK: Apparently, McGreggor agrees as he applies a snap suplex on Bowa. He whips Bowa to the other corner. Bowa with the reversal this time. Bowa follows in with a splash, but McGreggor gets the boot up in Bowa's face and the Snake Man staggers back. McGreggor grabs Bowa for a vertical suplex, or a brain buster, I'm not sure...OH MY! TH: That's original! SK: McGreggor had Bowa in the air, and fell backward, nailing Bowa's lower back into the top turnbuckle! Bowa falls down and rolls out of the ring trying to regroup. TH: And McGreggor, like any good wrestler should, follows him to the outside. Once you inflict damage like that, you got to keep at it before he has a chance to recover. SK: McGreggor grabs Bowa now, and rams his head into the steel ring steps. Now, he whips him intothe guard rail, causing even more damage to Bowa's lower back. Bowa rolls over and drapes himself face-first over the guard rail. TH: If that doesn't say, "Please beat me some more.", I don't know what does! See! What'd I tell you? There goes McGreggor to the ring apron. SK: Flying axe-handle to the back of Bowa. McGreggor goes to grab Bowa by the shoulder, AND IS MET WITH A FISTFUL OF CHAIN! TH: And the Street fight has officially begun! Some fan must have slipped Bowa a chain while he was down. SK: However Bowa got the chain, it has evened the odds somewhat, as McGreggor staggers back from the blow. Bowa adjusts his grip on the heavy steel chain and proceeds to whip McGreggor on the back with it. McGreggor is trying to find an equalizer of his own. He picks up a monitor from the Gambian Broadcaster's table and pitches it at Bowa. Bowa blocks it with his arm, but it looks like he may have hurt his arm doing it. TH: That was an instinctive move by Bowa. When something that big is coming at your head, it's duck or block. Ideally, you want to duck, but Bowa chose the latter, and I'm sure his arm is going to feel it in the morning. SK: It certainly bought McGreggor some time as he grabs a 2x4 from a fan at ringside. Bowa whips the chain at McGreggor again, but the "Wrecker" blocks it with the board. The chains has wrapped itself around the board and is stuck. the two men are having a tug-of-war, each man trying to disarm the other one. AND MCGREGGOR PULLS THE CHAIN FROM BOWA'S HANDS!!! ["Closer" by Nine Inch Nails begin to play over the P.A. system.] SK: What the hell? [The Cougar comes down the aisle carrying a microphone in one hand, and forcing Dan Barclay to the ring by the hair with the other. He climbs into the ring with Barclay in tow, and begins to speak.] Cou: On Monday night...half the NPWA...where allowed a chance to speak their mind. Now...it is my turn. DB: B-b-b-b-but Cougar, there's a match going on! Cou: I care not for a match...between a snake...and a worm. SK: Somebody get him out of there! TH: So, you're the broadcast veteran. Do let him talk, or ignore him and call the match? SK: Well, I can't see McGreggor or Bowa from here. We might as well let the Cougar speak, and get a camera crew to track the two grapplers down so that we can call the match from the monitors.
