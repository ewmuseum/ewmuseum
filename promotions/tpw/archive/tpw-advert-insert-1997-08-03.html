TPW's Own Advert Insert, 31 July 1997

31 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
("This is It" by Kenny Loggins plays through a darkened studio as two silhouettes are seated on stools in the foreground. The person on the right is obviously a female due to the length of hair and the feminine facial outline as she chats with her counterpart. The lights slowly rise and the viewer is greeted by a smiling Bruce Lord and Lindsay Jackson. As usual, the two are outfitted identically. This time they each wear black sweatshirts with a block lettered "MORE" and the contemporary fonted "Etc..." below it. Both Lord and Jackson seem rather highstrung as Lindsay Jackson speaks.) LJ: (She speaks in her usual articulate and very pleasant tone as her dazzling smile radiates into the camera) This **is** it! Welcome fans, to "More Etc...". A special Pre- Armaggedon celebration, and joining me as always, is Bruce Lord. Great to see you again, Bruce. BL: (Speaking to Lindsay) Lindsay, it is great to be here, (turning to camera) and fans...do we have a *bitchin" show for you tonite. (Upon hearing Bruce Lord say "bitchin':, Lindsay Jackson cracks up in laughter as Bruce continues) BL: (Gesturing with his thumb towards the back) Back there in our special "green room", we have the "War Games" captains from each faction and they will be Lindsay's special guests at "The Round Table". That is, if they don't kill each other back in the "green room" first. LJ: (Still smiling from Lord's comment and shaking her head slightly) I am going to don my own war helmet and fatigues for that segment, that is for damn sure. Fans, Bruce and I will also give you fans our own fearless predictions of each and every match that Armaggedon has to offer.......you'll hear it from the experts. BL: Call your friends. Call your bookie, and if you live near Vegas then run on down and place your bets 'cause LJ and I are gonna tell you what *should* happen. We will have that just a bit later, but first we have been trying to get one of the participants of Armaggedon on the phone (turning towards Lindsay)...is he on the line yet? LJ: (To Bruce) Yes, he is. BL: Good. I am being told we have "Sadistic" Steve Stone on the phone live from his hotel room in Berlin.. Steve Stone are you there? STONE: Hello?! Am I on? LJ: Yes you are.. STONE: Who am I talking to here? BL: Its Bruce Lord and Lindsay Jackson your on the air Live and if we could ask you a few questions about August 3rd and your match against the man they call Nug for a shot at the World Title currently held by "Superstar" Steve Sampson. STONE: First things first lets get something straight here... This match shouldn't even be happening. I shouldn't have to go into any match to prove I am the number one contender to this title.. This is all just conspiracy that is all apart of all the political Bullshit that has been holding Steve Stone back for the past three years. You see when I get in the ring with that moron I will prove without a shadow of a doubt that I am the Franchise of the NPWA. BL: Some may say you have a point but we have a little footage courtesy of Saturday Night Nightmare where you faced Nug lets take a look.. (Shot fades to the screen behind them) BB: "Steve Stone whips Nug into the ropes, but Nug reverses it. Steve Stone bumps into Denny Larson." AH: "Obviously, since Larson did nothing the first match, the top brass decided he should do a double-header tonight." MS: "Actually, the only header he did was into the mat. He's out cold!" BB: "Nug misses with a shoulderblock and Stone capitalizes with a kick. Steve Stone is going for the pin, but the ref is out! Steve Stonedecides to punish Nug some more and takes him down with a fist to the midsection.He goes for a spinebuster slam, but Nug counters it with a swingingneckbreaker. Nug whips Stone into the ropes. Nug hits Steve Stone with ashoulderblock." MS: "And Denny, 'Sleeping Beauty' Larson is back on his feet." BB: "Nug takes Stone down with an atomic drop. Now a flurry of punches from the Man They Call Nug. Nug picks Stone up and tosses him outside ofthe ring! Stone hits his head on the steel guard rail! He's out cold!" (Shot fades back to Lord and Lindsay) BL: As you can see "Sadistic" Steve Stone, Nug has had you beat so maybe you shouldn't take him so lightly.. STONE: Ha.. very funny but it looks like you forgot something idiot. Who walked out of that match the winner? Why don't you tell the people that?.... I did! I won the match, not Nug, it was me! And another thing, Lord, from now on when you talk about Steve Stone I want you and the rest of the NPWA to refer to me as "The Franchise". Let's cut the crap with this "Sadistic" stuff. We all know I am the best thing in this place, and I want everyone to recognize the fact that I am"The Franchise" of whatever organization I compete in. LJ: Steve Stone you seem to be taking Nug very lightly.. STONE: You see when your as good as me you can do whatever you want.. Let me ask you something sweatheart, who is the man that was part of the most dominating Tag-Team in the NPWA history? Me. That is who. I held the Tag-Titles for 11 months and I did it all on my own.. BL: You did have some help from Mr. ATM.. STONE: Shut up when I am talking, Lord.. I was the anchor of that Tag Team and it was because of me that we held those belts for so long.. Let me ask you something else. Who is the one that went through 15 so- called wrestlers to become the first ever NA Champion?... and if it wasn't for the ToP I would still have that title around my waist. You see, the whole NPWA from top to bottom is jealous of "The Franchise", and on the 3rd, in Berlin, I am gonna show Nug just why I am the brightest star in the NPWA. He's gonna get into the ring with me and realize he just doesn't stand a chance. You will all see I will do whatever it takes to get that title shot and Nug will find that out first hand.... And if he thinks eating hot dogs gives him heart burn, I hope to God he has got alot of Tums on hand cause my fist is gonna be even worst. You piece of garbage... (You hear the phone hang up and Bruce and Lindsay look in utter shock) BL: Umm yeah.. "The Franchise" as he wants to be called from now on, Steve Stone who will face The Man They Call Nug. For the victor a guaranteed shot at the World Title and for the loser will have to work his way back up that ladder. We will be back after these messages.. ( The volume to "This is It" by Kenny Loggins gradually increases as the studio lights fade to black....) ################################################################### T P W (Toronto Professional Wrestling) "We're next in line and we're hungry!" *Only found at http://npwa.simplenet.com/tpw/ Hey NPWA, come and check out your future opponents! --------TPW is a regional federation and is a subsidiary of the NPWA. ################################################################## (fade to black)
