Parent Fed's Farm-System Announcement, 11 August 1997

11 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JBN: The ref's checking to make sure he can continue... he's losing a lot of blood. EW: Masters has had a lot worse and still come out on top, he is one of... what the hell are they doing here? JBN: Steve Stone and John Hunter have just come down the aisle... Stone picks up Wicked Lester... Hunter smashes a beer bottle over his head!!! EW: And Steve Stone executes the face-first powerbomb right there on the concrete floor! Lester is busted open even more than Brian Masters! Stone and Hunter roll Lester back into the ring and head back to the dressing room. Masters stumbles back across the ring, he doesn't realize what's happened to Lester. JBN: Oh bull, he was probably behind the whole thing. EW: Will you stop?!? He rolls Lester over and falls on top of him. 1... 2... 3! Brian Masters retains the TV Title, and loses a lot of blood in the process. JBN: He's not the only one... there's enough blood in the ring right now to take care of those Red Cross blood shortages back home! They're both being helped out of the ring. EW: Fans, we're almost out of time, but I understand the NPWA tag team champions have something to say. So let's head up to the ring with Skip Wailen one more time. JBN: I wouldn't do an interview in that ring right now, it's a gory disgusting mess in there! ************************************************************************ [Skip Wailen is seen standing in the middle of the ring.] SW: Ladies and gentlemen, introducing at this time, representing the Asian Triad, the tag team champions of the NPWA, "Asian Assassin" Akira Sasaki and Kenshin Nobunaga. [The theme from the anime "The Lost Wolf" starts to play over the PA system as lights dim to pale blue and sakura petals starts to rain down from the ceiling. "Asian Assassin" Akira Sasaki steps out of the curtain first, wearing a white outfit, carrying a pair of steel kendo sticks and one half of the NPWA tag belts over his shoulders. Kenshin Nobunaga comes out next wearing a black samurai outfit and carrying his Dragon Sabre in his left hand, and the the other half of the tag belts in his right. Chinami Kuramoto and Noriko Sakai, dressed respectively in blue and pink kimonos, follow with Chinami carrying Shinya's urn in her hands. The triad members walk towards the ring area slapping the hands of selected fans.] SW: Congratulations Mr. Sasaki and Mr. Nobunaga on an impressive win over the Lords of the Ring and the Hellrazors at Armageddon'97... AS: [Grabbing the mic from Skip and putting on a big smile on his face.] Well Skip, before the match, we told everyone that we were going to bring the tag belts back into the Triad, and we have done that. We told everyone that we're the best team in the NPWA, and we have proved that. Akira Sasaki sticks to his words, and today, I'm going to tell all of you out there that no one, absolutely no one in the NPWA is going to be able to take those belts away from us. SW: What are you thoughts on your recent victory, Mr. Nobunaga? KN: [With a sorrow look.] These belts are of no meaning to us since Shinya is no longer able to share them with us. We do not need those belts to prove that we are number one. But it was both Shinya's and my wish that one day we would together bring those belts back into the Asian Triad. Even though Shinya is no longer with us, both Akira and myself will defend these belts honorably in his memory. SW: Great to hear that, but there are many other teams here in the NPWA that wants a piece of that gold. Resident Evil, Lords of the Ring, Hollywood Studs, Drama, and the Hardline, just to name a few. AS: Now, we don't really need to talk about the teams that we've already kicked the sh<bleep>t out of, namely Resident Evil, Lords of the Ring. Drama, you are pretty good at playing mind games, but Akira Sasaki is the master of mind games, so we don't to worry about them either. Regarding the Hollywood Studs, the only thing that we've seen them done so far in the NPWA is beating each other's c<bleep>ks in those porno movies of theirs. And about the Hardline, Masters, you pulled a win over our teammate the Silent Striker on Etc., but there is no way in the world that you and Jericho can get a 1-2-3 on Sasaki and Nobunaga. KN: To all tag teams here in the NPWA, the tag titles are now the possession of the Asian Triad once again, seek not to dispute that claim. But to any of you who do not mind another loss on your records, Sasaki and Nobunaga will put these titles on the line against anyone here in the NPWA. SW: Thanks for your time Mr. Sasaki and Mr. Nobunaga, I'm sure Shinya is looking on proudly somewhere up there. [Sasaki and Nobunaga raises their belts along with Shinya's urn. The crowd cheers to the theme from anime "The Lost Wolf" starts to play as Sasaki and Nobunga make a trip around the ring shaking hands with the fans before returning to the locker room area.] ************************************************************************ [NPWA President Billy Joe Tunney is sitting at a large oaken desk, presumably in his office, where a large NPWA banner hands on the wall behind him.] BJT: Hello, I hope you have enjoyed tonight's show. Before we go off the air, I have a few announcements to make concerning the state of the NPWA. First, The Theatre Of Pain. Pain Train wrote and signed all of the contracts for his wrestlers while acting in the capacity of Interim President of the NPWA. This placed him in a conflict of interest position, and our legal council has informed us that all managerial contracts signed at that time are negated. In layman's terms, The Pain Train is no longer the manager for any wrestler at this time, and the Theatre Of Pain as a contractual entity is officially disbanded. If The Pain Train does not leave the NPWA immediately, our attorneys *will* be filing lawsuits, both criminal and civil, against you for these illegal contract signings. Second, I have received many cards, letters and phone calls asking why we have allowed The Southern Rocker to return to action in the NPWA. I have been assured that The Southern Rocker has recieved councilling, The Rocker has personally apologized to me, he has paid a substantial fine and has agreed to donate a percentage of his earnings each week to various charitible organizations here in Toronto. In light of his service to the NPWA, both against the IRCEWF last year and against the New Wave earlier this year, and the reasons I stated moments ago, I agreed to lift the indefinite suspension against The Southern Rocker. Third, regarding the actions of Resident Evil on last week's Thursday Night Thrashings. That type of behavior will not be tolerated and you are hereby suspended immediately. You may appear before this office in 30 days to apply to have the suspension lifted, and gentlemen - your arguments had better be mighty persuasive. Finally, I hope you will all support the NPWA's new farm system, Toronto Pro Wrestling. My son, Bobby Joe Tunney, is the acting president of TPW, where you will see many of the future stars of the NPWA learning their craft. Goodnight, and godbless. ************************************************************************** Monday Night Mayhem is (C) Copyright of the NANet Professional Wrestling Association.
