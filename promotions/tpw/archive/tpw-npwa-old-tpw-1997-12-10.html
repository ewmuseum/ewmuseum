Parent Fed's Own December Script Frames TCW as "The Old TPW", 10 December 1997

10 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*********************************************************************** <Camera opens inside the DRAGON DOJO in Minnesota. A young and handsome Asian man is looking through the window and is watching the cold winds of Winter freeze every water. He is wearing a white sweatshirt with black swaetpants. He shudders from the cold> Cesar: Brrrr... It's cold here AGAIN!! Is it just me or is it cold in Minnesota AND CANADA!!??? I mean... in my cournty, Philippines, was quite warm and hot. And this is my first WINTER and I hate WINTER!! But everything else.. I love.. LIKE WRESTLING and.. <A knock is heard comign from the front door> Cesar: Who is it?? voice: It's THE MAILMAN!! <Cesar runs up to the door and peeks through the peep hole. A white caucasin man is standing infront of the door wearing the thin mail man uniform. The man looks like he is freezing to death. Cesar quickly unlatches the door and motions the man to enter the house> man: WHOA ITS CO..COLD OUT THERE!! Cesar: What are doing out in the cold?? <The man sharply replies without noticing who he is talking to> man: CUZ IT'S MY JOB!! <Cesar frowns at the man> Cesar: Why don't you stay in the dojo for awhile and wait till the winds die down?? man: Look.. I am tired and I need some sleep.. so.. <He man looks at Cesar, finally recognizes him> man: You're the GREAT DRAGON!!! CESAR.. MONTANO!! <Cesar just nods and smile at the man> man: Man... You're my favorite WRESTLER!! Cesar: And what's your name?? man: OHHH!!(shakes Cesar's hands) I am David Taylor.. But you can call me DAVE. Cesar: Why don't you have a seat and a cup of hot herbal tea to heat your froze body up?? Dave: Okay.. thanks!! <Cesar gives David a bamboo chair and a herbal tea> Dave: So..*sip*... I have been wondering.. how come you are wrestling in NPWA?? I mean... You are more successful in TCW. Cesar: Well.. I never thought about joining any fed except TCW but I really wanted to make the NPWA when I signed with the old TPW. But then they broke their ties and changed their name. So I decided I have no chances of making it to a good fed such as NPWA. Then when TCW got real big... NPWA recruited me then I joined after a lot of thinking but I am still with TCW. Dave: Oh yeah... that reminds me.. HERE's your mail!! <David gives Cesar a bundle of mail. Some are frozen stiff due to the harsh weather. Cesar carefully looks throught them> Dave: But I barely see you in NPWA. I mean they barely give you matches.. I MEAN A WRESTLER like you don't deserve a couple of matches. You should be their main event!! MAIN ATTRACTION!! You are the Great DRAGON!! <Cesar unintentionally ignores Dave and keeps looking through his mail. He takes out a mail with the NLWP Seal on it. He carefully reads the mail then he folds it to his pockets> Dave: WHOA!!! Was that from NLWP!!?? <Cesar nods in response> Dave: WHAT DID IT SAY!!?? Cesar: *sigh* To make this short. NLWP wants me to be one of their many wreslters. They are recruiting me. Dave: Man.. everyone wants you!! Are you gonna join?? Cesar: Hmmm... you'll find out. Dave: Uh huh.. What about your match with Kris Stallion on NPWA?? Cesar: What about it?? Dave: Well.. what do you think about wrestling a jobber.. I mean a low class wrestler such as Kris Stallion?? <Cesar looks at Dave in amazement. Dave bows his head in embarrasement> Cesar: Well... I don't think Kris Stallion is a bad wrestler. Actually I admire his courage and drive. He actually goes out in the wrestling ring and tries his best. No one can take Kris Stallion's pride away from him. He is a great wrestler.. he might lose all the time but losing is not always better than winning. You gotta remember that. Dave: Sorry.. I didn't mean to say that. I just too nervous cuz I am sitting here with one of the GREATEST. Cesar: Stop it with the comments. I wish I was great as you. Your courage even surpasses mine. You go out there rain or snow and still deliver that mail. You go out there either sick or well. I can't do stuff like that. You are GREAT. <Dave looks amazingly at Cesar then smiles proudly> Cesar: As for Kris Stallion.. I wish him luck and I will give it my best to beat him so should he. And for your comments about me not getting matches....well its probably because NPWA has a lot of newcomers and they don't have any room for matches for everyone. So you will wait.. soon.. I shall become champ of this fed. Dave: Oh.. yeah.. I need to ask you a question. Who do you wanna wrestle the most in NPWA?? I mean you do wanna test out your skills. Right?? <Cesar thinks for a moment then grins> Cesar: Hmm.. I would say.. I wanna meet either Dave Diamond... or Brian Masters. Dave: WHOA!!! Two champions!! You wanna wrestle against the two champs. Man.. now that's gonna make a sell out crowd!! THE WILL BE THE MATCHES OF THE CENTURY!! You versus Dave Diamond OR YOU VERSUS Brian Masters!! Man.. that would be exciting!! Cesar: Well... Dave Diamond went to TPW and tried to destory its sacred tradition and I wanted to make him pay!! He also thinks he owns my friend Lindsay Jackson like a toy. I can't stand what he stands for. But Diamond has a great skill in wrestling. As for "Black Heart" Brian Masters.. he is a great wrestler that shall be a hard test. He is the man to beat if you wanna prove you are the best and that's what I aim for. Dave: Hmmm.. Oh look at the time and the winds stopped! I gotta go home and tell the kids who I MET!! <David says good bye then rushes to door then stops on his tracks> Dave: Thanks!! Cesar: Here... <Cesar tosses his wreslting bandana to David. David looks at the black bandana in suprise and shock. He smiles at Cesar and tears are rolling down his eyes> Dave: THANKS!! Cesar: No..thank you!!! Have a Merry Christmas!! *********************************************************************** JBN: Ooooookay... another one for the clueless patrol! EW: A very nice man indeed, Mr. Cesar Montano! Alright, without further wait, let's go up to ringside! *********************************************************************** DP: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest is set for one fall with a fifteen minute time limit. Introducing first, he is known as "The Great Dragon".... [Perry shuffles through his cue cards and can be heard cursing] he is Cesarrrrrr Montanoooooo!! [Montano emerges through the curtain to a huge face pop and swiftly makes his way down the aisle and into the ring as "Triumph" by Wu-Tang Clan plays.] DP: And his opponent [Perry again shuffles through his cue cards, and evidently has lost his info on Stallion too!] he is... does anyone know who Cesar's wrestling? Crowd: Kris Stallion! DP: He is Kris Stallion! [Massive cheers from the crowd as Montano pats Perry on the back. Stallion comes to the ring to "Psycho Killer" by Talking Heads, intent on taking Montano out.] EW: What a mess up by Dave Perry! JBN: The guy's always sucked, puke, let's face it. This is just the final straw. Fire him! EW: You'd fire everyone given the chance wouldn't you...? JBN: Everyone but me... and you... EW: Well thanks alot Johnny. I'm touched. JBN: I was joking, puke. You'd be the *first* to go. Well, second. That son of yours would be out the door before he could say "Help dad!" EW: Although I'd love to talk about my family with you all night, Johnny, we've got a match to call. And Stallion's not waiting for the bell, unloading on Montano with big rights and lefts. I think this one may be over quickly. JBN: So do I. But I've got this strange feeling Stallion isn't going to be winning! EW: Really? Your prediction's not looking good at the moment, Johnny. Because Montano's getting himself choked on the ringropes and there's no way out. Now Stallion dashes across the ring, and lands on Cesar's neck! This is bad... JBN: But bad is good. EW: No it isn't. JBN: It is as far as we're concerned. EW: True..! Stallion's really not pleased for some reason, Johnny, and now he's taking it out on the fans, telling them to shut their mouths! JBN: Forget the fans, Stallion, just finish the match off, or I'll just be proven right sooner than any of us expected. EW: Stallion helps Montano to his feet via the hair and double chops him to the throat. Montano drops down to the mat like a sack of potatoes, and now he's rolling out to the floor! JBN: This kid sucks. He won't even stand up and take a beating even though he knows he's going to win. EW: Well if he does, I'm sure he'd rather do so without getting injured in the process. Stallion goes down to find Cesar, but the little guy's disappeared! JBN: He's under the ring you idiot. EW: Stallion can't find him, so he's getting back in, asking Jamal Patterson to make the count on his opponent. Jamal does so, but Cesar turns up on the opposite side of the ring, and he's climbing to the top! Stallion turns round to see what the fans are shouting about, and gets nailed with a perfectly timed, perfectly placed dropkick. JBN: Can't the fans show their glee by applause rather than this ape-like howling? EW: No, and why should they?! They're just supporting their hero! JBN: Hero my ass! This kid's got about as much charisma as a bowl of Kellogg's Cornflakes, but he doesn't taste as good of a morning, or any time of day, drenched in cold, or warm milk! EW: Did you get asked to plug that? JBN: Yep. EW: What's it worth? JBN: Not telling. More than you earn in a year though! EW: I bet it is... Cesar helps Stallion up to his feet. JBN: Have you noticed that *our* Stallion's about as impressive as that guy from Italy? EW: No, I don't take any notice of other federations, Johnny, and neither should you! Cesar sends Stallion for the ride and catches him on his return with a hurricanrana! And listen to the fans now, Johnny! JBN: STOP TELLING ME TO LISTEN TO THE FANS! EW: There's no need to shout! JBN: YES THERE IS! I CAN HARDLY HEAR MYSELF THINK! EW: I think the end's near, fans.... Cesar's going up top. He leaps off with a somersault splash and makes the cover. 1.....2.....kickout by Stallion. Cesar lifts Stallion up, bounces off the ropes and bulldogs him into the mat! Now he's lifting him up again. JBN: Stallion's out on his feet! EW: Cesar leaps up top and flies off with a top rope sunset flip! Outstanding! JBN: This one's over. Told ya Montano'd win! EW: 1.....2.....3! You're right, Johnny, this one's over. Moving right along, let's take you to two participants in our next match... The Fear Machine! *********************************************************************** Skip Whailen : Ladies and gentlemen, accompanied by their manager Black Sarah, "The Shadow" Michael Monroe and "The Ivory Tower" Justin Cross, The Fear Machine. ("Just One Fix" by Ministry blares over the arena PA system as Black Sarah leads her men to the interview area. Monroe seems as agitated as ever, but Cross looks uncharacteristically steamed.) SW : Black Sarah, let me be the first to welcome you to the NPWA. Your men bring an impressive list of credentials to the table. Black Sarah : Thank you , mister Whailen, it's good to be here. Everyone knows that to truly prove yourself as a superstar in professional wrestling, you have to prove yourself in the NPWA. This is the home of the legends and where the hottest young talent competes, and we have come to take our place among the elite superstars of wrestling ... where we belong. SW : Justin Cross, your first real challenge in the NPWA is the famous Siberian Express, whom you face this evening. their manager, Alexander Gudonov had some pretty harsh words for your team. Justin Cross : Gudonov, you portray yourself as a sophisticated man, and yet the more you try to show off your intellect, the more ingorant you appear. First, you insult my football background. Have you any idea how many great wrestlers ae former football players? Wahoo McDaniel. Dusty Rhodes. Ric Flair. Tully Blanchard. Ron Simmons. Steve Williams. Lex Luger. Terry Funk. All of the, either former college players or former professional football players. All of them holders of world, US or world tag title holders. Then you go on to insult my Cnadain heirtage. Agin, the more you talk, the more ignorant you look. Does the name Gene Kinisky mean anything to you? Whipper Billy Watson? Mad Dog Maurice and Paul the Butcher Vachon? Rowdy Roddy Piper? How about more recent names ... names like Chris Benoit. Chris Jericho. Lance Storm. And far from the least, the men whose family helped train me , Bret and Owen Hart. I seem to recall that the few Russian wrestlers who ever amounted to anything ... weren't even Russian! (Cross' normally impassive features settle into a deep scowl.) I have no reason to be ashamed of what I have accomplished. I've bean a fromer DEWF Intercontinental champion and world tag team champion. I've gone into the Cage Of Fear with one of the toughest submission wrestlers in the world. And now I've arrived in the NPWA at a time when the tag team scene has exploded and we are faced with you, Gudonov, and your pair of reprobates. When I get either of them in the Falling Tower, you will know the true meaning of White Death! Michael Monroe : Alexander Gudonov? What are you, a Russian Ballerina? Or a character from a Rocky and Bullwinkle cartoon? Or just a pathetic excuse for a manager with a talent for shooting his mouth off and not much else? You sure as hell are no judge of talent, because look at the pair of Slavic lummoxes you chose to manage! And further proof is that your idea of scouting you opponents is to read half of their bio sheet ... and that's it. Did you miss my mastery of the submission style? Did you miss my matches with some of the greats of Super Junior wrestling? did you overlook my legendary matches with the second Crow? And you even go so far as to call me fat? (Monroe opens his robe to show his muscula, Chris Benoit-like physique) Do you call this fat? Would you use the word fat to describe the body that has put out men with a handspring moonsault? Would you use the word fat to describe the bady that has locked men in the Shadowplay until they have screamed for mercy? But your so called scouting technique has done one thing successfully ... it has angered me more than you could have ever imagined! Nobody, and I mean nobody, insults my faith and lives to gloat about it. You hateful and specatcularly ignorant slurs will not go unpunished, and when we have finished disposing of your tow washed-up pathetic has-bbens that you call a tag-team, we will take our umbrage out of your worthless hide! We are not called the Fear Machine because it looks good on our corporate letterhead! We came to the NPWA to earn glory and win titles, but we also came to break bones. And when we look for volunteers, who do we see in the front row with their hands raised but the Siberian Express. Well, boys, we are your one way train ticket back to Vladivostock ... in a body bag! Make peace with your gods, little men, you are next! *********************************************************************** EW: Sounds like Fear Machine are ready! JBN: I don't think so, puke! EW: Why not? JBN: Look at 'em! They know their match is up next, and yet they're heading back for the dressing room. I guess they wanna do the whole ring entrance thing again, huh? EW: Uhhh... I guess so. So, who are you liking in this match? JBN: Either or... The Siberians have shown me a lot, but then, I kinda like these two dark souls, Cross and Monroe. If you ask me, though, the only really good-looking prospect in this match is that Sarah chick. She may be goth, but that's okay with the Nastee man! EW: Oh please! Let's get up to ringside before my partner gets TOO carried away! *********************************************************************** DP: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following tag team confrontation is set for one fall... introducing first, team number one... to be led to the ring by Alexander Gudonov... at a combined weight of six hundred and eight pounds, hailing from the Commonwealth of Independent States... Ivan Drago, "Russian Bear" Andrei Bolotov.... The Siberiannn Expresssss!! [Big heel pop as Gudonov, carrying a gym bag, taunts the fans. The Express emerge through the curtain and the pop increases in magnitude. "Just One Fix" by Ministry blasts over the PA. A deathly silence hits the arena.] DP: And their opponents. To be accompanied to the ring by Black Sarah, at a combined weight of five hundred and twenty pounds, first, from Los Angeles, California, he is "The Shadowww" Michaellll Monrooooooe! And his partner, hailing from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, he is "The Ivory Tower" Justinnnnn Crossssss... collectively known as The Fear Machiiiiiiine! [The three individuals make their way to the ring to a very strange silence.] JBN: This is a very strange group of individuals, puke. EW: They certainly are, Johnny, and that's why they're known as The Fear Machine. JBN: Oh, obviously I'm not afraid of them... [The two men and their manageress enter the ring, and as Cross takes off his robe, he stares down at the announce table.] JBN: Uhhhhh...!!! EW: What's wrong, Johnny? JBN <in a deep voice>: Nothing. EW: Good, because I think we're ready to get under way. Wille Feldman's calling for the bell. DING! EW: And it's Cross to start off against Bolotov. The Bear! The two big men, locking up, and it's Bolotov who gets the better of things. He backs Cross into the corner. Big clubbing forearm over the top, which misses as Cross moves out of the way, and simply tosses Bolotov down to the mat by use of the hair. JBN: Blatant cheating, Feldman. DQ Cross. NOW! The sooner that guy leaves this arena the better. EW: Are you sure you're not scared of him? JBN: Not at all. EW: Okay... well, fans, Bolotov doesn't look too pleased, and he's gotten involved in a pushing match with The Ivory Tower. Both men are pushing away and neither's gaining the advantage, but here comes Ivan Drago to make it two on one, and Cross gets manhandled out of the ring, and down to the feet of Alexander Gudonov! JBN: I wanna know what he's got in that gym bag, puke. And I wanna see him use it. EW: He's not going to get the chance just yet at least, Johnny, because here comes Michael Monroe to help his partner back to their corner, and he rolls him in under the bottom rope and makes the tag out. Monroe in and goes to lock up with Bolotov, but a big boot to the midsection doubles him up. Bolotov lifts Monroe up onto his shoulder, and he's locked on a hanging backbreaker, Johnny! JBN: And that hold can break a man's back, puke. EW: Hence the name backbreaker I suppose? JBN: Don't get cheeky, puke, I've told you before. Yeah, it's a devastating hold... EW: But Monroe's wriggling free, Johnny! JBN: No he ain't! EW: Oh dear oh dear... Bolotov just turned the backbreaker into a powerbomb, fans, and I'm as sure as I can be that Michael Monroe is officially out. Feldman counts the cover: 1...2... kickout by Monroe! JBN: You're as sure as you can be.... I despair! EW: Bolotov's livid, and he helps Monroe up by the hair, then plants him with another powerbomb! And he tags out to Drago, to do some more damage. JBN: I'm surprised the Express are having it so easy, puke. EW: Drago drops an elbow, but Monroe moves out of the way! Where's he getting this energy from?! JBN: Obviously he's been drinking the Lucozade, puke... EW: Probably. Hey, how much did you just earn for saying that? JBN: Twenty five thousand... EW: Sheesh! I'm sure Mr Dutt won't be too pleased about that though. JBN: Yeah, Dutt worships the ground I walk on, puke. He'd've fired you for less than that though! EW: You're probably right. But in the ring, the tag's been made, and Justin Cross is in, and he's showering Drago with right hands. He grabs a handful of tights, and lifts Drago high in the air with a vertical suplex... and he's just holding him there, Johnny! JBN: Yes, the blood's rushing into Drago's head now, puke, and if he doesn't let him down soon, I think we could have a death on our hands. EW: Never one to over-exaggerate, our Johnny. But thankfully Cross brings Drago down to the mat with a humungous crash! JBN: God, we're thankful for that! EW: You know what I mean. Cross covers: 1......2......Bolotov in to make the save, and now we've got a four-way going here. Monroe in with Bolotov. MAN! Bolotov just threw Monroe out over the top rope! Surely this is a DQ.... nope, Willie Feldman's saying he didn't see it! JBN: He didn't see anything! Well, he did, but it's called "black." EW: Sarah? JBN: Who? EW: Black Sarah? JBN: Is that meant to be funny? EW: No. I mean yes. I mean no. JBN: Well whatever you mean, I'm telling you it's the opposite. EW: It's funny? JBN: No. EW: Oh. Bolotov goes to work on Cross, but Cross nails him with a massive clothesline which knocks the big man down to his knees. Cross now scoops Bolotov up, and slams him down to the mat, to the delight of some of the crowd! JBN: But most are just sitting in silence. This is weird, puke, weird! EW: Cross scoops up Drago, and tosses *him* over the top rope! JBN: And Feldman didn't see that either! EW: No.... Cross is setting Bolotov up against the ropes, and he nails him with a flying clothesline that takes *both* of them down to the concrete floor. And Willie's the only one left in the ring. JBN: But he probably thinks he's out on the concrete! EW: Well he can't think he's alone, as he's ordering the ringpost into the ring! JBN: Ha! This guy just cracks me up every week! EW: I'd noticed. JBN: And now he's counting the ringpost out! EW: No need, as all four men are getting to their feet and continuing their brawl *inside* the ring rather than out. Both Fear Machiners tossing the Siberians back in. But the Siberians are waiting for them now, and both execute perfect outside-in suplexes, and Cross and Monroe are in big trouble. Double Irish Whip, and now they're both caught in sleeperholds. This is impressive teaming by the Express, Johnny. JBN: It is, the Express are looking good. But I can still see the Machines coming back into this one. EW: Bolotov releases his sleeperhold, sends Cross for the ride and shoulderblocks him down to the mat, and he rolls to the outside. Drago releases *his* sleeper, sends Monroe for the ride, and Bolotov catches him in a bearhug, and Monroe bounes off the ropes with a big clothesline -- The Iron Curtain! This one must be over... JBN: But here comes the Pain Squad! EW: And Alexander Gudonov's waiting for them... he's taking a taser out of his gym bag! This could be... oh my! He *just* grazed Terror, but Torture dodged it, and now he's pounding away on Bolotov and Drago. JBN: I don't believe it!! EW: What?! JBN: Feldman's actually calling for the bell! EW: Indeed he is.... DP: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners of the match as a result of a disqualification due to outside interference, the Siberian Expressss!! EW: Well, fans, this one's far from over, because Wyldside are making their way out here! My goodness! Torture, Bolotov and now Justin Cross and Brian Taylor are all going at it, while Terror's just performed an airplane spin on Alexander Gudonov, and he's in the crowd! Drago's over to meet Terror, but he's intercepted by Jimmy James, who nails him with a dropkick! And now Michael Monroe's out there to make it a four-way thing on the concrete as well! JBN: Watch out for Black Sarah! She's got Gudonov's gym bag, and now she spraying yellow paint in the direction of *anyone* other than Cross and Monroe! EW: Terror and Torture are making their way back to the locker rooms fans, as are James and Taylor, but those four have not gotten themselves involved in a brawl! They're fighting all the way back through the curtain, and probably into the car park..! JBN: They'll probably be brawling all the way to Siberia! EW: Erm, neither of those teams are from Siberia, Johnny! JBN: But you gotta admit, it sounded pretty damn good at the time! EW: No, not really! And now Bolotov and Drago are coming for the Fear Machine, but Sarah's ordering her boys out of there! But what's happened to Gudonov..? JBN: Take a look in Row 23! EW: Oh my goodness! As these teams brawl their way back to the dressing room, let's take you back to the ring where my son has an interview with The Impact Player... JBN: The man who does NOT fear the Cougar! EW: Whatever. Here's Skip with Mustafa. ----- [name withheld] Chair, NPWA Booking Committee http://npwa.simplenet.com The NPWA - just another SHEF gone bad.
