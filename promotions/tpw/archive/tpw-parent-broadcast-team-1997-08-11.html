Parent Fed's Own Studio Show Confirms TPW's Broadcast Team, 11 August 1997

11 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The shot fades up on "Blackheart" Brian Masters, standing in the back of an arena, halfway through the back door. He wears a pair of sunglasses, an ASU t-shirt and blue jeans as he leans against the door jam, a gym bag over his shoulder.] BM: Do I got a challenge I wanna make? Well, I through down the damn gauntlet on Thursday, but since my phone isn't ringing, I'm just going to assume nobody was listening, so I'm gonna start to name names. [Masters removes his sunglasses, folding them over the collar of his shirt and stepping out of the doorway. The door shuts behind him, and the scene becomesa lot darker.] BM: How about The Silent Striker. If you can tear yourself away from your search for a love lost [pauses in an overly dramatic sarcastic swoon], how about you and I climb in the ring on Saturday evening, and get it on. I've been disappointed that our paths haven't crossed thus far, Striker. We've got a lot of similarities. We can both be sullen and moody. We both like to talk about shutting up and getting down to business, and yet we both talk a lot. [Snickers a little bit.] And we both do it for the same reason. Because we can, plain and simple. We both thrive on competition, and we both spent a lot of time eating English hospital food this April. So isn't it appropriate, then, that you should be the first man to get a crack at my Television Title, a Title which I have already earned many times over... and which I will earn many more times over again before it's all said and done. So here's the deal. You sign the papers, we climb in the ring and see which one of us can really back up our mouths. We'll see just how deafening the silent is when the bones in your arm break. [Masters turns on his heel and walks out the arena door.] (.......fade to black) (As the lights rise in the studios of "NPWA Etc..." Lindsay Jackson and Bruce Lord are standing side by side, front and center. Lindsay is her usual striking self in a white t-shirt that reads "NPWA/TPW Family Feud". Her faded blue jeans match Bruces although he has on a black "I Survived Armageddon.....I think" sweatshirt.) LJ: (Dazzling smile) I am Lindsay Jackson and joining me, as always, is Bruce Lord. Welcome to NPWA "Etc...". BL: Fans, as this week began, some feuds were put to rest. A couple feuds were rekindled, and all hell broke loose--- Armageddon has come and gone. Instead of resting on our laurels, as many of us wish we could do once in a while, our opening sequence showed that in the NPWA, there is no such thing as down time. LJ: As all of you saw, "Blackheart" Brian Masters wants a piece of The Silent Striker. The Silent Striker is more than happy to oblige. Here's what The Striker had to say... (Bruce and Lindsay turn around to face the wallhanging screen behind them...) [A quiet, pitch black room appears on the screen. It's impossible to tell where the floor and walls meet or any other details of the room. Suddenly, a burst of white light appears as the camera pulls back through an overhead spotlight. After a moment, the camera stops several feet in front of the spotlight to reveal the contrasting image -- a radiant beam of light shaped like a cone bursting against the darkness. An equally radiant voice breaks the silence.] SS: Brian Masters . . . the 'Blackheart' of the NPWA. Your rise to the top has been heard throughout the world, whereas my ascension has been relatively silent. I prefer the sancitity of the shadows in contrast to your need to bask in the... [pauses] ...spotlight. And now, you have beckoned me to do battle... [steps into the circular brightness] ...and pulled me into YOUR spotlight. [Silent Striker lowers his head, bangs draped across his face. Under the brilliant light, Striker's face appears ghost-white, his black hair glistening.] SS: Yes Masters, the two of us share similarities. I fight for love, you fight for justice, and vice-versa to a certain degree. However, do not drag my personal affairs into this as I have no interest in yours. Cross the line, and you shall be in permanent need of a wheelchair courtesy of the Striker. I will even provide you with one that matches your wife's. [An evil grin flashes and disappears as quickly as it appeared. It is an expression never before seen coming from Silent Striker.] SS: You and your Hardline, me and my Asian Triad . . . both equipped with their fair share of NPWA gold. The title shots have come fast and furious lately and I have let them slip bye. Truth be known, you will probably find a way to hang on to that precious title of yours, the one you worked so hard for by defeating Mark Bagwell. Big deal. I defeated him the week prior to much less notoriety. [Silent Striker pulls out his duct tape and plays with it.] SS: And if that is the way it winds up, so be it. But before the two of us are through, it is you who will be venturing into MY realm of darkness. You will feel the unrelenting need to scream . . . only you cannot. The time has come... [rips off a piece of duct tape and applies it over the camera lense] ...where actions speak louder than words. [Darkness prevails once more as the deafening silence fills the air . . .] The studio lights rise to face Lindsay and Bruce's faces) LJ: A few weeks ago, the NPWA asked their fans which two wrestlers did they want to see in a feud, and their answer was The Silent Striker and "Blackheart" Brian Masters. Will this much anticipated feud begin here on "Face-Off"? We will find out later on in the show. But first, Bruce has a little news about this week's "Round Table". (Bruce Lord face shot) BL: Thanks, LJ. On last week's "Etc...", we told you that we would begin a multi- part interview session with the NPWA World Champion Steve Sampson. (Smiling) Well, it seems as though Mr. Samp- son and the "Etc..." were a bit overly agressive in scheduling such a thing so close to an event like "Armageddon". Steve called our offices a couple of days ago to reschedule so he may continue to re- cover from that wicked "War Games" match. Of course, we were more than happy to comply. So, look for the exclusive "Round Table" segments with Steve Sampson and Lindsay Jackson to commence next week. LJ: I don't blame him one bit, Bruce, we have had quite the week ourselves. Armageddon last Sunday, the premiere of the NPWA's newest and littlest family member, the TPW on Wednesday, and the TPW's first live action last night. I think we are a little burnt out ourselves. I have *no* idea how Masters and Striker will manage on "Face-Off' to- nite. BL: LJ, Brian Masters has promised a defense of his Television Title on every single show the NPWA has to offer. *This* is most definitely an unprecedented move by any champion in any fed that I have heard of. And assuming he does, in fact, win the first couple of times....how long will he last before he completely physically and mentally ex- hausts himself. LJ: There are less than a handful of stars in the NPWA that would even consider such an action, and if anybody can pull it off, it is Brian Masters. Personally, I wish the man all the luck in the world. BL: (Smiling at Lindsay) Well, Lindsay. I hope that your new boyfriend doesn't become jealous because of your well wishings of other NPWA stars. LJ: (Quick sharp look at Bruce) Ummm.....what are you talking about? (Bruce continues the devlish grin and points quickly to the screen on the wall as he turns towards it. Lindsay turns as well...still looking at Bruce.) (Video plays of Dave Diamond sitting in the back of a limo with three beautiful women - a blonde, a brunette and a redhead] DD: So, let me get this straight, I can challenge anyone I want for this Face Off thing, right? Girls: That's right, Dave! DD: Alright, I'm issuing a challenge right now... and it ain't who you think it is... nah, I've beaten you enough for now Powell, I've got other fish to fry at the moment... Girls: Ooooh, who is it Dave? DD: I'm glad you asked... this guy struts into the NPWA a few weeks ago, and I thought I was looking in a mirror! The robes, the strut and even the hip gyration... yeah, I'm talking about you "Midnight" Michael Dane, you steroid-induced, Dave Diamond wannabe!!! You ain't me and you could never *be* me, no matter how hard you try! And big deal, you have Alexis Gold in your corner... hey pal, I've nailed her already and she wasn't that great. Blonde: I heard she dyes her hair! Brunette: I heard she stuffs her bra! Redhead: I heard she's over 40! Girls: Ewwwwwwwwwww!!!! DD: Dane, you march in here, thinking you're gonna be the next "Hollywood Heartthrob?" Forget chump, I ain't ready to give up the title. Strut your wannabe ass down to the ring and I'll show you how it's done, junior. It's like I said to the high school students when I starred in 'Dead Poets Society' - "Carpe diem - seize the day." And I'm going to do that if you have the balls to step into the ring with the Heartthrob - or has Alexis taken those from you already? WHHHOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! [fade to black] (As the studio lights return, Bruce and Lindsay are again facing the camera. Bruce still has the same grin as when the camera cut, meanwhile; Lindsay's sensual light skinned African American face has grown hard) BL: (Still grinning..) Ummmm....LJ? You..didn't..really ..know about that tape and those other women, did you? LJ: (Not looking at Bruce and hiding her anger only sort of well) I wish Mr. Diamond the best of luck in the NPWA. BL: (Eyes widening and teasing now) Oooooohhhhh.....the "canned" answer, eh? Hmmmmm....I smell a lengthy phone call on the horizon to "The Heartthrob". (Rubbing his chin and speaking sarcastically) Gee, I sure hope Dave isn't "busy". Hehehehehe. LJ: (Angrilly) Shut up, Lord...cut to the damn commercial! BL: Umm...no commercials this week, LJ, shorter show means no commer- cials, remember? LJ: Get to the match, then. BL: (Still smiling) Can't do that either, LJ..that's the last segment, but I will do you a favor, I was just told that we will cut to a little break *just* for you...... (As camera fades to black, Lindsay storms out of view of the camera's view as Bruce continues smiling) ******************************************************************** THEY SPIT ON HIM *AND* KICKED HIM WHEN HE WAS DOWN. NOW HE WANTS REVENGE The Maltese Falcon squares off against former teammate, "The California Stud" Christian Steele ONE'S A TRUE TELEVISION CHAMP THE OTHER IS JUST PLAIN DEMENTED "Blackheart" Brian Masters defends the Television Championship against Wicked Lester. PLUS We'll see the genetic wonder, the Kaiser in action ONLY ON NPWA'S MONDAY NIGHT MAYHEM, AND ONLY ON WTN AND THE LIFETIME CHANNEL ******************************************************************** (The studio lights rise to meet only Bruce Lord who is shaking his head and smiling) BL: Well fans, this makes four out of four. Lindsay has yet to make it through a complete shows. Usually she bails because of the violence and mayhen the NPWA offers, but now it is something near and dear to every- bodies hearts------ (mock drama) a torn love affair. Hahaha. (Bruce changes positions then continues.) BL We now bring you to this week's "Face-Off match between Brian Masters and The Silent Striker that was recorded last evening. Calling the match was the unlikely duo of Alexis Gold and Johnny B. Nastee.... (Lights dim and the screen illuminates....) AG: Well, Johnny Boy, everybody is in the ring and we are ready to get it on. JBN: "Ready to get it on".....Gold, those are the five words that you live by. Hehehehe. AG: You're a pig, Nastee, and whomever's idea it was to match your's truly up with a over the hill washed-up slob like you should be canned. JBN: Listen toots, this ain't no picnic for "the Nastee one" either. AG: Let's just try to get through this experience with as little pain as possible. JBN: Alexis Gold not wanting pain is as bad as the pope not wanting to to splash himself with that holy water stuff..... AG: (Ignoring him....because she knows he is right) This is the first Television Title defense for Masters and he couldn't have picked a tougher opponent in The Striker. JBN: Masters is "Hardline", Gold, they re the toughest around, just ask everyboy that participated at "War Games". AG: Whatever.....this Patterson guy just called for the bell and Striker and Masters lock it up mid-ring. Master easily slides Striker into a side headlock. Both Masters and Striker are right about even in the heighth department, Nastee. JBN: Affirmative, Gold, but the Asian one has thirty pounds or so on Masters and can use that weight very effectively. AG: Point well taken, Nastee as Striker just slipped backwards out of the headlock and ....Wow! Three consecutive karate thrusts to the throat of the surprised Masters and Striker has Masters retreating early. JBN: Striker is lightning quick, Goldie, but it is still *way* to early to even consider this an advantage. AG: Striker grabs the arm of Masters and ...irish whip by Striker... here comes Masters...BIG elbow by Striker that caught "Blackheart" flush on the jaw. Masters went right to the mat......Whew, I am getting tired of talking, Nastee...this play-by-play stuff is tiring. JBN: I've heard that you don't like to talk much, Goldie, especially when have a mouth full of------ AG: (Interrupting) -----if you finish that sentence, Pigbreath, I will take this *very* hot cup of coffee and dump it right *there*. JBN: ..........ok. AG: Striker has taken advantage of the downed Masters with an elbow- drop and is picking up Masters by the hair......DDT!!! WOW! JBN: *That* hurt the champ. AG: Yeah, it did, Nastee. Striker is not letting up as he is again picking Masters up by the hair. I sense a different air about Striker tonite. JBN: For once, we agree, Goldie....there goes Masters into the ropes... AG: Another strong irish whip by Striker.....WOOOOOO!!!! WOWSA!! JBN: SHEESH! Striker nailed Masters with a CHOKESLAM!! Nice move by Striker.....HE'S COVERING! AG: Whatever his name is is counting! ........................1 ........................2 MASTERS KICKS OUT!! AG: Striker is on the attack again. He is pulling Masters up by the hair and these fans are just *too* noisy. JBN: Yeah, I agree, I hate all this screamin'. VERTICAL SUPLEX!!! AG: OHH!! Masters hit the apron hard. This really isn't the way Brian Master wanted to start his championship run... JBN: Somehow Masters has positioned himself so he is now sitting and at the same time, leaning against the ropes..He looks quite dazed, Goldie. Not as dazed as you do, on many occasion, but dazed. AG: Shuddup. Striker is on the prowl again and approaches....HEY!! JBN: WHADDA MOVE BY THE CHAMP!! That's why he is Hardline. AG: Gotta agree with you, Nasteeman. Striker was bending down over Masters and the champ grabbed the tights and used his leverage to pull Striker right out of the ring. JBN: The Silent one unexpectedly toppled headfirst onto the cement. Now we got both guys down. G: Blackheart is using the ropes to help him up while Striker is not showing much signs of life on the floor. I can feel the tide turn- ing. JBN: Masters is up and now has hopped onto the floor. This guy is a great technici---THATTA BOY!! AG: The champ is laying some heavy boots into the rib area of Striker. Jamal Patterson has come over to warn Masters....like that'll stop him. JBN: What is Brian doing? He is walking away from Striker.... AG: Yeah, he is walking all the way to the other side of the ring and Patterson is following Masters while still in the ring. This sure is an odd one, Nastee. JBN: Gotta agree, Goldie. Ohhh....Masters is getting a chair from the crowd, who don't seem to happy to be in the same company of a champion. Of course, ol' stripes ain't gonna go for that and is reprimanding Masters for grabbing a chair. Masters is chewing right back at Pat- terson now.... AG: BRILLIANT!! ABSOLUTELY BRILLIANT!! JBN: *That* is why Masters is not only the champ, but why he is "Hardline". AG: As ol' Jamal was bickering with Blackheart, Sebastian Jericho has come out and even though the youngster is being bombarded by all sorts of treats, he has now picked up the hurting---OOH WOW!! JBN: Hehehe...I just love when you say it like that. AG: Pig. Sebastian Jericho just executed a perfect Kalamazoo Facebuster on the defenseless Silent Striker and Jericho is now sliding Striker in the ring. JBN: The kid slipped under the ring just as Masters slipped under the rope.....hehehehehe...gotta love the Hardline....Masters with the cover....Hell, he could frickin' take a nap and it wouldn't matter. .......................1 .......................2 .......................3!!!! Masters defends his title. AG: Yeah, he did, but Striker definitely held his own. (Pause) AG: Are we off the f<bleeping> air? Nastee...you are a God-damned pig, and if you ever get within ten feet of me yu will be talkin' to my man Michael Dane. JBN: Awww, shaddup you bi<bleep>....Go to the store and by yourself a cucumber and go home and shove----- (SIGNAL BREAK............) (Studio lights rise to Bruce Lord) BL: A successful title defense by Brian Masters with a little help from his friends. It wouldn't surprise me if Striker pays Masters a visit in the very near future and I think the fans may get their wish: a full blown Striker/Masters war. (Pause) BL: Fans, my lovely colleague is still trying to contact Mr. Diamond and we are about out of time for today. Next week, we expect everybody to be healed from Armageddon and rarin' to go. For Lindsay jackson, I am Bitchin Bruce Lord saying adios.
