DARK HORSE EXCLUSIVE SCENE: Before the Show The alien race of cyborg hunters known as Cynines have had a cloaked unit spying on DHW events for quite some time now. However, on this day... that spy unit has been discovered.
The Cynine unit is pulled out of its cloaking field and made visible by his attacker. The Janitor - " WE came across this world and its super powers first. It shall be our race that takes this planet whole and uses it and its people in galactic take over. "
Unit 7 - " The Symbiotic's of Planet MK213134 have never been able to bond with a Cynine, let alone defeat us in any respects. You simply lack the ability on your own to over power us. "
The Janitor reveals Tony McDessel's sword. His eyes charge with power as it is generated into the blade of the ancient sword not of his own.
The Janitor - " The host beings of this world shall change that. " The Janitor swings the blade in all directions at the Cynine who generated force fields to block the weapon. The Janitor starts to fire at him with lightning bolts and takes his part of the battle airborne. The Cynine keeps up so The Janitor doubles his efforts and finally sneaks an attack with the sword right through one of the Cynine's energy shields and drives the blade from the top of its cyborg skullcap down and to the side of its neck.
The Cynine murmurs gibberish as half it's face slides right off it's head and neck. The Janitor using the ability of flight and super strength again and makes one more swing that lops the Cynine in half.
The Janitor - " It would seem I am not the only being here with an interest in the DHW or its immortals such has Tony McDessel and Blacken Blue. I must speed up my efforts and summon the home world with instructions for the hostile take over before the Cynines do just the same. "
