Match #1 Robert Mula vs. West (w/ Joanna) Referee: Miguel De Pinna Appeared on: DHW Sunday Night Skirmish, February 27, 2000 Written by: Wasteland Format: ABS [The Show opens with a wide-angle shot of the arena. The seated front row and the rest of the standing room crowd have packed themselves into what little space they have. The ring, a plain blue canvas with red ringposts and white ropes. Tracer and Steele, dressed in their usual attire, sit at the simple announcer's table upon the stage.]
AT: Good evening everyone, and welcome to another SUNDAY NIGHT SKIRMISH!!! I'm Alex Tracer, and with me as always is the enigmatic "Stainless" Ray Allen
Steele! RAS: "Enigmatic?" I thought you knew me better than that! AT: I know you, but I don't know why. RAS: Oooh. Ouch. Let's get to our first match, before my rapier wit decides to strike you down for all you're worth.
AT: Right, our first match of the evening: West versus Robert Mula. RAS: I like West, he's a scoundrel after my own heart. AT: You have a heart? RAS: Shut up and call the match. [The dance beat of "Blue" by Eiffel 65 comes to life as West walks out of the
DHW entranceway, accompanied by the ever-popular Joanna. The low mist-like smoke creates the illusion that the two is walking on a cloud. The crowd favorites come out to a massive face pop by the small crowd. The Icon of DHW hops up onto the ring apron and flips into the ring, while Joanna steps lively between the ropes. Another face pop as the two pose for the photographers.]
RAS: Wow. Joanna is looking hot tonight! AT: I know what you mean. That smoke effect makes it look like she's an angel walking on a cloud.
RAS: Why, Alex, don't tell me that the cold armor of yours has a weak point! AT: ANYWAY, West is on a roll, having lost no matches against his opponent. RAS: Ignoring me, eh? AT: No, I just hate you. [The "Adults Only" mix of White Zombie's "Super-Charger Heaven" works it's way onto the speakers. The crowd erupts into another face pop as Robert Mula storms out through the entranceway. He scans the crowd quickly before heading towards the ring. Deftly, Mula rolls into the ring.]
AT: Let's get this underway! [Joanna steps out of the ring, much to the delight of the Boathouse crowd. West and Mula size each other up, as Referee Miguel De Pinna signals for the starting bell. The bell rings, and Mula and West lock-up. West takes control early with a double-leg takedown.]
RAS: I think West is gonna take this one. He's faster, more talented, and in better shape than Mula.
AT: Think what you want, but Mula has proven that he can go toe-to-toe with some of the fastest guys on the planet.
[Mula darts to his feet, and answers West with a kick to the midsection and a scoop slam.] AT: One of the most memorable matches I've ever seen Mula in was one where he was wrestling Fixxxer and the Big Fix caught Mula in an anklelock. Mula was stuck with a hurt ankle and little energy, but for some reason, the guy was laughing!
RAS: I don't think he realizes that it's supposed to pain he's feeling... [Mula and West lock-up once again, this time West comes out the victor and locks on a rolling leglock.]
RAS: There's why West is DHW's biggest superstar. He's smart. He went after the hurt wheel that you've been talking about. Hopefully, he breaks it off!
[Mula overpowers West and drags him, on one foot no less, over to the ropes to break the hold. Mula catches West with a vicious lariat across West's throat.
Helping West up, Mula takes him down quickly with a snap suplex, rolling over for the cover. Referee de Pinna hits the canvas for the count.]
AT: ONE! TWO- RAS: Kickout! [Mula follows up with a vicious splash across the chest area.] RAS: Mula sucks. He looks like a human baked potato. AT: Well, whatever you think of him. He's got control of the match. He's just put West in a camel clutch.
RAS: He's a human baked potato made out of flubber. That's it!!! He's a FLUBATO!!! [The camera angle shifts to ringside, where Joanna is cheering on her wrestler,
West.] AT: Hi. RAS: Man, why don't you go down to talk to her? AT: NO! That would be unprofessional. RAS: So??? [In the ring, Mula has West caught in a camel clutch. West's legs kick as he tries to find a way out.]
AT: This doesn't look too good for West. RAS: I think that might be an illegal chokehold that Mula's got on him! AT: What??? RAS: Nevermind, look! [West manages to get one of his knees under him, and then the other. West slowly works up onto his feet, somehow lifting Mula up on his shoulders. The crowd goes wild at the sight of the 300-pound Mula on the shoulders of the
165-pound West.] RAS: Check that out! West might be small, but he's got the strength where it counts. [Mula slides off the back of West and catches West with a belly-to-back suplex.] RAS: BOOO! Mula's a cheater! AT: A cheater? Mula? You must be kidding! [Mula stands West up, before whipping West into the ropes. West holds onto the ropes to break the run. That doesn't stop Mula, however, as he charges into
West with all of his body mass. The two are sent over the top rope and onto the unpadded concrete floor.]
AT: GEEZ LOUEEZ! I don't think their health insurance covers that. RAS: Mula just made a big mistake. Arena brawling is West's specialty. [As if on cue, West kippups to his feet and immediately goes on the search for a weapon. West hops over the guardrail and grabs a steel chair. Meanwhile,
Mula is still working his way onto his feet. He makes his way towards West, but is cut off by Joanna, who then makes the effort to leap onto the ringside timekeeper's table and soccer kick Mula in the throat! The crowd erupts for
Joanna.]
RAS and AT: DAMN!!! [Mula stumbles around, gasping for air. He stumbles too far, however, and is greeted by a chairshot across his forehead. West tosses the chair at Mula for him to catch, but as if this were a game of hot potato, Mula tosses it back right into West's face!]
RAS: See? Mula is using foreign objects! AT: No crime in that in DHW. He countered the Van Westinator with ease! RAS: The what? SHUT UP, AL! [Mula drags West back into the ring, tossing the still folded steel chair onto the canvas. Mula stands West up, and proceeds to run against the ring ropes.]
AT: Mula can end it all right now! [Mula hits the ropes and proceeds full-force towards West. He launches himself into the air, in an attempt for a flying bulldog. Expecting West's back to be turned, Mula can't change his momentum when West quickly spins around. West catches Mula, and quickly spins around again and drops him with the So Fap spinebuster slam... onto the steel chair!]
AT and RAS: DAMN!!! [West grabs Mula's legs and rolls over for a bridge pin. Referee de Pinna is on the spot with the count.]
ONE! TWO! THREE!!! [de Pinna calls for the bell, as Joanna slides into the ring to congratulate her boy. "Blue" plays on the audio system, as the crowd applauds for West.]
DS: Your winner... WEST!!! [For some odd reason, Joanna feels like dancing, and she does, and the crowd loves every second of it. West takes a breather before doing anything else.
Mula gets back to his feet, aware of the win and stumbles over to West. He extends his hand for West. It's a moment oblivious to Joanna, but not to the crowd. Hesitantly, West shakes Mula's hand. Mula then rolls out of the ring and quietly leaves.]
RAS: I don't like him much, but I'll say this for Mula: he says he's a sportsman, and he acts like it. Don't you agree, Al?
AT: ... RAS: Al? Oh, Al? Are you watching the pretty girl? Why don't you go up there and show her what dancing is all about? Nudge, nudge, wink, wink.
