A message-board-based promotion whose one surviving full show script staged a multi-round tag-team tournament between costumed super-powered wrestlers and a literal cat. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Dark Horse Wrestling posted a recruitment ad on 26 November 2004, pitching a message-board-based promotion running three to four hand-written cards a week from its own site, dhw2.0catch.com, and signed off with a line that set the tone for everything that followed: “When I do finally go to Hell... I am kicking Satan's ass & taking over!”

Its one surviving full broadcast, Wednesday Night Warzone, aired 29 December 2004 as a billed comeback show, and opened before its own credits rolled: an alien cyborg spy is discovered lurking around DHW grounds and cut down by a mysterious figure called the Janitor, who names two of the promotion's own “immortals,” Tony McDessel and Blacken Blue, as targets of a coming invasion before teleporting off to summon reinforcements.

The card itself opened the first round of a Dark Horse Tag Team Turmoil tournament, nine matches deep, run through a roster of costumed and super-powered wrestlers — among them a team billed as Roy Roy Junior and EVIL, EVIL turning out to be a passed-out cat whose one action of the match was to lie in the corner while the referee counted a pin anyway. Elsewhere on the card, Victor von Draco retained the Hard-core Championship against Sandman, ending a twenty-minute brawl through the crowd, over the announce table and through six ringside chairs with a Crucifix Powerbomb he called the Tormetorium.

The main event, a Last Man Standing grudge match between Blackwolf and Max Palermo, ran nearly fifty minutes through a steel chair, a folded ladder and two tables, both men shrugging off finishers that would have ended any ordinary match — Palermo's wounds visibly sealing themselves shut, Blackwolf absorbing blow after blow without staying down — before a false finish gave way to Palermo left standing as the true winner, chanted over by the crowd while Blackwolf walked out under his own power once the cameras cut.

The broadcast closed by teasing its next pay-per-view: a first Elimination Chamber tag title match, another Hard-core title defense, and X-Treme Island itself staked on a match, held for the moment by EVIL the cat. No further show survives; how the Tag Team Turmoil tournament finished, or how far Dark Horse Wrestling carried on past this broadcast, isn't in the surviving record.
