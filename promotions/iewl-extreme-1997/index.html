Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The International Extreme Wrestling League introduced itself on 18 May 1997, in block capitals: “I AM THE PRESIDENT OF THE IEWL…THIS STANDS FOR THE INTERNATIONAL EXTREME WRESTLING LEAGUE, IT WILL HAVE 5 TITLES AND CONSISTS OF FAKE WRESTLER'S ONLY NO REAL WRESTLER'S WILL BE ACCEPTED.”

A reply the next day, signed by a fellow newsgroup regular calling himself The Jammer, mocked the pitch on two fronts at once — the block capitals, and the choice of name itself, chasing what he called an already outdated trend: “Extreme e-feds are passe. They are doomed to fail since anyone who sees the word 'extreme' expects it to fold in a month.”

Whichever way the reply landed, no title name, roster, card, or result of the IEWL's own survives beyond its founding post. A near-duplicate ad still ran on the newsgroup six weeks later, and a prospective member was still asking for its home page address on 30 August 1997.

What, if anything, the IEWL ever staged isn't in the surviving record.
