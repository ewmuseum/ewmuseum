BSWF MONDAY MAYHEM Match Results This Page will be updated the following Tuesday Morning. All Results Are Final INTRODUCTION HEY YO!!!Check this out, as far as I can tell the only match on the agenda is Merchandi$e versus The Clarke for the BSWF World title, so that's all that's going to happen, after the new year thursday Night thumpfest will be back up in full effect but until then whatever. Also I might have a TNTF this week instead of Mayhem next week. How's that sound???? E-Mail me. BS-TV Nick Hart is seen Hobbling down the hall with his leg in a cast. Hart As Hart leans against his crutch to open the door, the Mista and the Made Men Body Guards walk by and shove him to the ground and walk away laughing. Debo vs. Mi$ta and the Made Men Body Guards. This Match (if you can call it that, started out of nowhere. Mi$ta and the MM BG's were hanging around the ring waiting for the title match to begin and making sure that noone was around waiting to interfere. Out of Nowhere Debo came barreling out from the crowd and nailed one of the Body Guards with a chair, from their Debo smashed the Other Body Guard into the rail, and when Mi$ta came rushing over to stop Debo, Debo Grabbed him and chokeslammed him onto the concrete. Debo then made sure Mi$ta and the BG's weren't getting up bybeating on them some more until security ran down to stop him....... What the hell is Debo doing?? BS-TV Santa Clause is seen on the BSWF monitor telling everyone that he has a big surprise in store for the BSWF'ites in the next few days... Hey! That Santa looks a lot like.......... WORLD TITLE MATCH The Clark vs. Merchandi$e This Match started with Di$e coming down to ringside. Merchandi$e was visably pissed off due to the fact that his BG's and main man Mi$ta were jumped by Debo and couldn't watch his back. Harper then came down to ringside wearing the gold for the millionth time in BSWF history. Right Away Di$e flew over the top rope and nailed The Clark with a cross body splash. This knocked both men over, but adrenaline was flowing and each man recovered pretty quickly. The Clark began to pound shots at Di$e stagegering the challenfger back into the steel barricade until Di$e connected with a low blow and was able to whip the Clark into the ring. Di$e then grabbed the Clark whipped him into the ropes and nailed him with a dropkick that floored the champion before going upstairs and nailing a diving headbutt. Di$e then went for the win but only got a two count before the Clark powered out of the pin. The Clark then got up and drilled Di$e with a kick to the midsection followed by a lethal DDT. The Clark then bounced off of the ropes and planted the knee to Merch's forhead before scooping him up, bouncing off of the ropes and delivering a devestating powerbomb. The Clark then signalled for the Clark's Elbow, but as he stood over Merchandi$e, Di$e's arm shot up smashing into the groin of The Clark, and crumpling the Champion in the center of the ring. Merchandi$e then pulled the Clark over to the corner and pulled him up nailing a superplex, leaving both men dazed. Di$e wasn't quite as dazed and managed to get up but when he shot back to bounce into the ropes, ended up nailing the ref through the ropes to the outside. Di$e tried to revive the ref so he could continue with the momentum, but the Clark got up and as Merchandi$e turned from yelling at the ref on the outside, he got planted with the Clark bottom. The Clark then nailed the Clark's Elbow but the ref was nowhere around, so the Clark went to the outside to get him, as he did Wrestletech came flying out of the crowd and nailed the Clark with a Clark Bottom of his own. Harper then picked up teh Clark and brough him back into the ring before locking on the dreaded TECHsas maple leaf. By this time Merchandi$e had recovered and climbed to the top rope and nailed the Clark with a guillotine leg drop while Harper had him tied up. Harper then nailed Merchandi$e with a right hand, but Merchandi$e could taste the gold and fired Harper over the top rope with a drop kick. From there the groggy ref was just climbing back into the ring and counted the three count for, the first time BSWF world champion, now truly a made man, Merchandi$e. See ya Thursday.
