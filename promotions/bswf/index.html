A GeoCities-hosted fed at Colosseum/Bleachers/8000 billed "the Best Fantasy Wrestling Fed on the Planet," whose own tournament PPV, "Super September," crowned its own World Champion in a six-man bracket that changed hands twice more before the surviving record runs out. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Big Stick Wrestling Federation ran under the tagline "If You Can't Run with the Big Boys, Get the Hell Out!," a roster of original characters staging weekly "Monday Mayhem" and "Thursday Night Thump" results alongside its own Pay Per Views.

Its own "Super September" PPV, a six-man tournament, crowned the BSWF World Championship's own founding titleholder: Ceesalo won the tournament final over Nick Hart to become champion. The Clark held the belt next, though his own winning of it went unrecorded — the surviving record picks him up already champion. Wrestletech's own interference cost him the title against Merchandi$e on the fed's own "Monday Mayhem" broadcast, the fullest surviving account of any change of the title's own hands. Merchandi$e still held the belt as of the fed's own undated Rankings page and its own "Thursday Night Thump-Fest" broadcast, where Debo carried off the physical belt in a post-match angle without the title itself changing hands — the last-attested state of the championship in the held corpus.

The fed's own Rankings page also names the Made Men as Tag Team Champions and lists a Solar Championship (vacant) and a Lunar Championship, neither ever awarded to a champion anywhere in the surviving record. The Made Men's own only attested defense, against Kadafi and Scarface at Thursday Night Thump-Fest, ends without the fed's own text ever ruling a winner.

Storylines

The surviving record carries two consecutive weekly broadcasts in full, and between them one arc: Merchandi$e's own takeover of the World Championship, and the resistance it drew.

The Made Men's turn, and Debo's answer Written by AI, 2026

Monday Mayhem opens on Nick Hart already hurt, hobbled on a crutch and shoved down by Mi$ta and the Made Men's own bodyguards for the trouble of just being in the hallway. Debo answers in kind before the main event, ambushing the same trio at ringside. Then the World Title match itself turns on a run-in: Wrestletech, ranked the challenger right behind The Clark on the fed's own Rankings page, interferes on Merchandi$e's behalf, and Merchandi$e leaves champion. By Thursday Night Thump-Fest, Wrestletech's own favor reads as the Made Men closing ranks: The Clark's promised mystery partner never shows, Merchandi$e raises his hand only to turn on him, and the Made Men and their bodyguards beat him down until Debo and a still-hurting Nick Hart run in to make the save. Debo closes the night without a match at all — he simply walks off with the physical BSWF World Championship belt, telling the fallen Merchandi$e where to find him.
