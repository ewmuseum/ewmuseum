Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The InterNational Wrestling Alliance opened in March 2001 as a simulator-run promotion, its matches produced on the TNM engine and its winners weighted by the roleplays handlers sent in; a handler who did not roleplay was jobbed and eventually released.

Its handbook made a point of flexibility. It allowed both real wrestlers and original characters, asking only that real ones be played true to form; it let a handler sign two wrestlers, a wrestler and a manager, or a manager alone; and from its second edition it chartered stables of three or more.

It ran on numbers: three points for a pinfall or submission, two for a count-out or disqualification, one for a draw, and a purse for every result that could be spent on raising a wrestler's work rate, push, stamina or charisma, on paying fines, or on buying other wrestlers' contracts.

One weekly show, Friday Night FlatLine, was called by a heel commentator, a neutral one and a face, and a calendar of monthly pay-per-views ran from a January Royal Rumble to a December Seasons Beatings. Six titles were sanctioned, from the World Title to a Hardcore Championship defended every fourteen days.

The handbook, in two editions posted the same day, is the whole of the surviving record.
