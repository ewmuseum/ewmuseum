INWA Is Now Recruiting

Welcome to the InterNational Wrestling Alliance, we are one of the newest federations on the 'net, but we have a great deal of potential. You may be asking why am I creating an E-Fed (electronic federation), when there are so many out there as it is. A good portion of the E-Feds die away after a month or so, and more often than not, it is not that the Fed-Head lacks dedication, but lacks experience. Too often, federations wither away because the Fed-Head, or President, does not know what it takes to efficiently run a federation or he/she is a kid and has to put everything on hold whenever they are grounded Well, I am no teen-ager, I am 28 yrs old, so there is no way for me to get grounded, so no need to worry about that. Also, there are are federations out there that have been running for 2+ yrs and are still going strong. I have been involved in Fantasy Wrestling for the past 10 yrs and began my "career" in the Imaginary Wrestling Association (a Play-by-Mail federation), long before anybody knew what an E-Fed was. Besides from wrestling, I also worked behind the scenes as a card writer, booker, and was a VP of a small federation and took over as acting President while the President was recovering after an operation, so as you can see, I know my way around. The INWA is based on ideas that I saw in other federations and have decided to use the ideas I liked in different federations and create a new federation. Also, you will not see my hype up this federation. I will not boast and brag about how we're this or that, because I do not believe one should sing his/her own praises. I want you to find out and judge for yourself. Also get involved in the INWA. Get involved in the feuds, Get Involved in the alliances. Get involved in the plots, because this is all about participation and any federation is only as strong as its members. 2. The InterNational Wrestling Alliance, or I.N.W.A is an E-Fed where you can take the persona of your favourite wrestler, or create you own unique wrestler. Have you ever dreamed of taking on the likes of Ric Flair or Bret Hart? Well now, you can. You can book matches against other competitors and one match may be with a person across town, and the next match may be with somebody across the world. The I.N.W.A. is run using TNM7, which is designed by the legendary [name withheld], and in my humble opinion, is the best simulator out there, hands down. Just because we use a simulator, don't think you do not have to roleplay, because RP's are is what this is all about. If you do not RP, you will be jobbed, and eventually removed from the roster. Your RP's do not have to be 10,000 miles long, but corny, meaningless one-liners will not do either and I take quality over quantity any day of the week. 3. The INWA is unique in the fact that we allow both real/fantasy wrestlers. All we ask, is when you sign a "real" wrestler, please roleplay him accordingly. For example, do not roleplay Hulk Hogan as an Ivy League prep, because that in one thing he is not. We will allow you to sign up to 2 wrestlers with no manager/ 1 wrestler with 1 manager/ or 1 manager (I will get to this in one second). So you want to be a Manager: The INWA is also unique in the fact that you can be just a manager if you wish. Since a wrestler spends a great deal of time training, wrestling, public appearances, ect, he cannot be bothered with the day-to-day details of match negotiations, public appearances, ect. That is where a manager comes in. A manager is one who takes care of the day-to-day details for his charges and also acts as an official "spokesperson". A perfect example of a manager would be Bobby Hennan and Jimmy Hart. A manager is also at ringside during the match for moral support and for the occasional "cheap shot". Also managers are expected to pay for some of the training that a wrestler goes thru, as well as some of the fines that may be levied. But a manager, like anybody else, expects to be paid for his or her services. The INWA states that 15% of the purse is "average" payment, but as always, the manager and his protégé, may negotiate the payment. (We will get to the money system in a few seconds) 4. The INWA does allow stables. To have an officially chartered stable, you need at least 3 members, and after that, you can have as many members as will join. 5. Right now we have one weekly show, entitled Friday Night FlatLine (our flagship show). The commentators are "Majestic" Tom Majesty, Dan Harrell and Lance Richmond. The interviewing is done by the INWA's own "Mean" Mike Greene. (I will at least write the results of the cards in a "summary" fashion, while the main events and PPV cards will be done with full commentary) Here is a short biography of each of these people "Majestic" Tom Majesty: He is a retired wrestler and former 3 time World Tag Team Champion and a former TV Champion as well. He is a heel thru and thru and broke every rule written (and even some that were not). He cheers for the heels and constantly tries to provoke the face loving Lance Richmond Dan Harrell: "Dynamic" Dan as well is a wrestler, semi-retired and still wrestles on occasions. He is a former US and Hardcore champion and is neutral when it comes to commentating on the matches. He usually finds himself in the middle of Tom and Lance's verbal warfare. Lance Richmond: Lance is a face who marks out for the fan favorites and jeers at the heels. His verbal warfare with Tom Majesty has become legendary and millions turn in around the world to hear them go at each other. "Mean" Mike Greene: The INWA's own "house interviewer". Mike is a veteran interviewer and knows how to ask the tough questions, but also is smart enough to know when to shut up, as well. 6. The INWA will have Major PPV (Wrestlemania, SummerSlam, ect) and will have minor PPV's (IYH, ect) as well as "Supercards" (Main Event, Clash of Champions, ect). The Friday before a major PPV, there will be a special Friday Night Flatline, in which there will be no matches, but there will be a run-down on the PPV card and will discuss the events leading up to the matches taking place. Here is a rundown of the cards… January- Royal Rumble- 3rd Sunday of the month February- Minor PPV3rd Sunday of the month March- Supercard- 4th Sunday of the Month April- WrestleFest- 3rd Sunday of the Month May- Supercard- 3rd Sunday of the Month June- Minor PPV- 2cd Sunday of the Month July- Revolution- July 4th August- Summer Heat- 2cd Sunday of month September- Supercard- 3rd Sunday of Month October- Halloween Hell- October 30 November- Minor PPV- 3rd Sunday of Month December- Seasons Beatings- Sunday before Xmas Eve 7. The INWA will employ a unique rating system that goes like this… Submission/Pinfall- 3 points Countout/DQ victory- 2 points Draw- 1 point each Loss- No Points Awarded 8. The INWA will incorporate a unique money system. For every match you win, you will earn some money. Money can be used for many things, such as raising your stats, paying fines, and you can even purchase other wrestlers' contracts and from time to time, I will create a new wrestler and allow you to bid on his contract. Here is the signing bonus… A- Sign 2 wrestlers, receive $1000 each wrestler B- Sign 1 wrestler/1 manager- $1,000 each C- Sign as manager only- $3000 Pinfall/Submission Victory- $1,500 Countout Victory- $1,000 DQ Victory- $500 If you are pinned/submit you will receive- $150 for making the effort If you are counted out- $100 fine If you are DQ'd- $1000 fine Here is what you can use your money on- Raise Work Rate by 1- $750 Raise Push by 1- $750 Raise Stamina by 1- $750 Raise Charisma by 1- $750 Create New Custom Move- $50,000 per move 9. Here is a run-down on the current titles, sanctioned by the INWA World Title- Only the elite of the elite hold this title. This title must be defended at least once every 28 days and on all PPV cards and all Supercards You must be within the top 3 to challenge for the title. You win $10,000 for every title defense InterNational Title- A very respected title in the INWA and must be defended every 21 days and must be defended on every Major PPV card. You must be within the top 7 to challenge for the title. You win $5,500 for every title defense US Title- Defended only on US soil and must be defended at least every 21 days and must be defended on every Minor PPV and Supercard. You must be within the top 10 to challenge for the title. You win $3,000 for every title defense TV Title- Defended only on live TV or Taped TV shows. This is the one title where the Champion decides when to defend and against whom. There is no "minimum defense" date. You win $2,000 for every title defense Hardcore Champion- Defended during "gimmick matches", no DQ and no count-out. This title must be defended at least every 14 days and anybody may challenge for the title. You win $1,000 for every title defense World Tag Team Titles- Follows the same rules as the World Title and the purse is divided between the winners. The money for title defenses is in addition to the regular "purse" money for the match. 10. Here are a few simple rules A. No racial slurs/remarks of any type, whether in character or OOC B. You may lose a match from time to time and if it happens, it happens. Please do not be a chronic crybaby. C. If you do not RP, you do not wrestle that week, simple as that. If you are going on VC, or know in advance you will not be able to RP, we will work with you. If you do not RP the following will take place….. 1- If you don't RP within 1 week, you will not wrestle that week 2. If you don't RP 1 week later we will send you a reminder 3. If you do not RP 1 week later, you will be suspended for 2 weeks and fined ¼ of your earnings 4.- If you do not RP 1 week after that, all titles held will be declared vacant and you will be jobbed (push, work, and Charisma will be reduced to 5) 5- If you do not RP 2 weeks later, your contract will be terminated. D. All transactions between wrestlers will need to be cleared by the CEO (that's me) E. Don't demand title shots…..earn them. F. I will not challenge the referee's decision, but if I feel the situation warrents, I may order a rematch. G. Do not attack the handlers themselves (keep everything in good fun) H.
