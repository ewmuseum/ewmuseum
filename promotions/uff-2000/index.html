Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Underground Fighting Federation opened under an on-screen president who introduced himself with an origin story rather than a press release: a former Australian kick-boxing champion, in his own telling, who built a promotion for “the fighter of no specific origin” because the sport he loved had none of its rules left in it. “The fighter which may be driven by the need for greater competition,” his own mission statement reads, “or just the need for blood.”

Its own first card, Saturday Night Rage, filled a bar called Loui's Saloon to a reported 500 to 600 people and opened with a house band playing before the president walked out to declare the promotion's revolution underway. Three titled matches followed on the night, closing on a beatdown that set up the promotion's own next card, Revelations.

The UFF billed itself as a fighting organization rather than a wrestling one — no rules beyond a request to clean up after yourself, an invitation to use an invented fighter, an existing wrestler, or one pulled out of retirement. It shared its initials with an older, unrelated AOL-hosted promotion three years its senior; nothing about either promotion's own roster, title history, or operator connects the two.
