The UFF ... a legacy of Peter Phoenix

13 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Mission Statement of the Underground Fighting Federation is - "A federation which is made for the fighter of no specific origin. The fighter which may be driven by the need for greater competition or just the need for blood." I can end our lesson here, because that is the basics of the UFF, but if you want to know more, then read on. Let us take a few minutes time out from your training, feuding, airbreathing - whatever. Allow me to give you an insight as to how the U.F.F. came about.
Back in 1990 I became the Australian Kick-Boxing Champion. My dedication to the sport was unmatched, and no other single point in my life was greater then the point when the
Championship belt was wrapped around my waist. But not untill last year did I stop to think as to why I loved the sport so much. What drove me, why was it that I showed so much dedication to being so good at being able to beat an opponent into a stupor. Well I figured it out.  You see, we live in a society where we pride on ourselves on helping others. How many times have you been bombarded with advertising bullshit to help your fellow man who is less fortunate - "SEND YOUR DONATIONS...". Look at every religion and society, well to my knowledge, they found themselves on being good to your brothers and so on. WHY?? That was partly the question that was burning in my mind."Why did you love to fight?".....ANSWER?? Because it is our nature. Before society's were formed, what were humans doing? They were beating the shit out of each other with sticks and stones. Why?? For supremecy. To mark your territory like a wolf pisses on his tree. That's why. To make a name for themselves as being the baddest mother around and for noboddy to fuck with them or they would soon find themselves being beaten to a bloody pulp. Sure they could have learned to negotiate - but the natural instinct of every living thing is to fight if you can, run if you can't. Kill first, reason later. Supremancy.   In '93, I relinquished my title to the number one contender, and gracefully retired much to the releif of my mother. I was the King Of The Hill.
I had a name as being a decent person, but with the temper of a cobra - hence my nick name. After retiring, I helped a freind of mine start up a cafe, which I knew he was using me as a draw card. After a month or two, I was going crazy. I would get agitated easily. Cobra to Phoenix in a second. I undertook counseling to try and understand where this was coming from. It wasn't stress because buisness was good - personally and socialy (if you know what I mean). It took
'till the second session that we agreed that I was having withdrawal symptoms of some sort. Impossible I thought. I don't do drugs - that includes alcohol, and smoking. Smoking was limited to a cigar every now and then.   After a few months I realised that my withdrawal symptom were not that - but urges. The urge to fight. I had been out of the ring for so long and training was reduced to crawling pace. I wasn't feeding a part of me that was seemingly my driving force. I spoke to some people, mostly people who had experienced a few fights before. They all agreed that at some point in their your life that they were driven by the thrill of a fight. You can't tell me that you have never gone out with the notion that if you didn't get pissed and had bit of a brawl, that the night was uneventful. I've thought it, and I've seen it.   So after speaking to some people, we decided to have a small social gathering every month or so. We would get together - have a few drinks, go into the basement and put on the gloves. Never had we had so much fun and content.
Soon we were taking off the gloves and fighting bare knuckled, then we were inviting others to join. That was the begining of UFF.  Technicly, it wasn't illegal. Just immoral. But who gave a shit. We heard of other factions popping up, our "club" was becoming popular. So we decided to turn it into a business. We got all of the club leaders that we knew of together and gave them this - The Underground Fighting Federation. Apprehensivly they agreed, based on the fact that they wanted the U.F.F. set up and to see what the public opinion would be before they made any active involvment. So I agreed.   I spoke to a guy I knew whom owned his own bar - Loui Sahinidis. At first he was apprehensive of the idea, but he knew buisness was slow and getting slower. He needed an edge. So after some time, he got back to me and allowed me to have use of his main room on one condition - he gets half of the nights takings at the door. The rest was ours. The deal was made. That is the U.F.F. as we know it.   Looking into the future, we are hoping to go big. Make a name, shake the world up a bit. With some help and from freinds and the allmighty greenback, we are hoping to be able to run the U.F.F. independantly. Our own headquarters. Our own training facilities. T.V. coverage - PPV's,
Fans,Merchandise. But untill then, the dream continues...     This was compiled by the President of the UFF, Peter Phoenix. Impressed? Why shouldn't ya be? Join the UFF today, to get regconized, to break away from rules - to have NO-HOLDS-BARRED FUN!!
