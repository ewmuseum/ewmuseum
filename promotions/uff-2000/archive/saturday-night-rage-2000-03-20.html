[UFF Saturday Night Rage]

20 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The first ever UFF card, Saturday Night RAGE promised to be big, and it was. Loui's Salloon held a capacity croud of 500-600 people, with a line heading down the block. The show opened with the lights dimming out, then the Gorilla flashed to life. It showed images of all the superstars promos as special guest
houseband - "ELEMENT", played "Sleep Now In The Fire". President Peter Phoenix came out to a huge roar from the crowd. He had this to say. "Ladies and Gentlemen...welcome to Saturday Night RAGE!!!" The crowd exploded at this. "Welcome and thank you for taking part in the revolution of sports entertainment history. Tonight, on our first card of many, three UFF Titled are up on the line. We will also have for your entertainment, a ten-man Battle Royal for the number one contendership for the Ultimate Heavyweight Championship." Phoenix went on for another 5 minutes or so about promotions and drink cards, and that tonight's event was being broadcast live via the Internet. The crowd gave a cheer of appreciation as the lights went out and the announcer introduced the first match. UFF Street-Fight for the Lightweight Title -- Yung Syko vs Stevie Styles Syko and Styles came out to a rowdy crowd, itching for the first fight. They were introduced and it was announced that the match would take place along the main road. The competitors moved out to the main road which was blocked off, along with a
500 strong crowd. The match started with Phoenix calling "FIGHT" and the two men went at it. Styles hit out like a flash with a right hand, catching Syko off guard. He threw another but was ducked and Syko followed with a heavy kidney punch. Styles backed off and Syko charged at him, ramming Styles onto the bonnet of a nearby car. Syko then jumped up onto the roof and attempted a leg drop but Styles moved. Styles grabbed Syko and put his head into the windscreen. Syko was busted open. The crowd went wild at the sight of blood. With Syko dazed, Styles wipped him into a lamp pole.
Syko collapsed and went down for a count. The referee got to five before the match continued. The two men went at it again, Syko taking the advantage after a low blow. After 5 minutes of brawling, Styles then backed off and lead Syko to the side of Loui's Salloon. Syko went to charge Styles again, but was leapfrogged and crashed into the side of a dumpster. Styles set him on top of the dumpster and hit the Soldier Drop into some bins below. Styes followed up with the Slidin' Down Southside and it was all over. Syko was then counted out making Styles the first UFF Lightweight Champion. 4-Way-Dance for the Foreigner's Title -- Jakks vs "KOS" Simo Fletcha vs Freak vs Richard The crowd returned inside for the next match. The fighter with the biggest ovation was Richard. He came out with a duffle bag full of dildos and began waving them around. As he was doing so, the match began and Jakks and Freak began to double team Richard while Fletcha sat back in the corner and watched. After Richard went down, Fletcha mounted the turnbuckle and hit flying clothes line on Freak and Jakks. They went down, and so did Fletcha. The referee began a count out, but Phoenix came ring side and yelled at the referee to stop the count. Fletcha and Richard were the first ones up, with Fletcha holding his side. Richard then began to go to work on Jakks and Freak who were still getting up. Freak delivered a punishing uppercut, and Jakks got up in time to give Richard a club handle smash to the chest. Richard collapsed into the corner and Freak began to work on him with a few punches and then a headbutt. In the meantime, Jakks and Fletcha were trading blows untill Fletcha went for a sidekick but Jakks caught it and threw him into the corner. With Fletcha stunned, Jakks called for Freak who grabbed Richard. The both whipped thier men, the crowd "OOHHING" at the sickening thud. With Richard and Fletcha in a bad way, Freak and
Jakks
rolled the men out of the ring and began to go one on one. A brawl then began as both men traded blows, soon blood was dripping from thier noses and above thier eyes. Freak suddenly ducked and delivered a low blow, and let loose with a flurry of kicks. He then gave Jakks the Poison, sending
Jakks
to the ground. Jakks, not giving up so easily, got up on the cont of
8. As this was going on, Freak mounted the turnbuckle and hit a double leg drop on Richard and Flethca as they were getting up. With the two men down again, he grabbed a chair from the crowd and smashed it over thier heads.
Brutally, Freak began to stomp on the chair on the two men & the crowd wnet wild. Back in the ring
Jakks was getting to his feet and Freak dived back in. Jakks, with his eyes swollen, attempted a spear but missed and hit the ropes, on the rebound,
Freak was ready and then played "Hyde And Seek". With Richard and Fletcha seemingly unconscious, the referee began the count. Freak was then declared the Foreigner's Champion to a huge roar! While celebrating in the ring, Freak was attacked from behind by
Richard
with the chair. Styles came out and clobbered him with his belt. To the crowd's surprise, Bloodthorn came bursting through the crowd and hit
Freak with the Thorn. Styles went to hit him with his belt but Bloodthorn jammed it back into his face with his foot and gave Styles The One-Two. He helped Jakks to his feet and announced to the crowd that he is accepting Jakks' offer to join as tag-team partners. A ten minute intermission was called and at the bar, Mr X and Phoenix were being interviewed by the Internet broadcasting crew. Phoenix and X were beaming at the fact that the event was going so well. The crew asked who
Phoenix was to be fighting in the Special Match, but Phoenix and X were keeping quiet. 4-Way-Dance for the US Title -- Styles vs Slade vs Syko vs Bloodthorn The match for the US Title started off fast and furious, with Styles and Syko gettng into it before they had even entered the ring. Slade came in ramming thier heads together. In the meantime, Bloodthorn was casualy making his way down to the ring. With Styles and Syko seeing stars,
Slade
began jumping around the ring and screaming for Bloodthorn to get in. Bloodthorn threw down his jacket and dived into the ring. Slade then gave Bloodthorn the Lights Out and he dropped. With Syko and Styles once again going at it, Slade began to mount the tunbuckle. Syko suddenly gave Styles the Juke 'N' Jive and followed with a swinging neckbreaker. With Slade posing on the turnbuckle, Syko ran in and pulled Slades legs out from underneath him, sending him face first into the guardrail. At this point, Bloothorn was getting to his feet. Syko then jumped onto the top rope and went for a moonsault. To the astonishment of the crowd, Bloodthorn caught Syko mid air and gave him a running powerslam. With blood streaming from his head, Slade came rushing into the ring with a chair. As he was raising it for the swing, Bloodthorn delivered The Thorn. While he was on top of Slade, Jakks came down and rammed Syko's and Style's heads into the corner posts to keep them down. Slade then began slamming the ground wildly with his hands signalling submission. The crowd were on their feet and going crazy. Bloodthorne shook Jakks' hand as he was being awarded the US
Title. Special Match -- Peter Phoenix vs Mystery Man Phoenix then entered the ring, draped in a silk robe. He grabbed the mic and announced this... "As you all know, it is know my turn to get some action in the ring. I will not have you in suspende any longer. Tonight, my rival of the ring shall be...YOU!!" He says, pointing his finger at the crowd. "That's right. But I will not be facing 1, not 2, not, 3, and certainly not 4. BUT 5 members of the crowd in a gauntlet match."
The crowd was shocked, but they soon changed when it was announced that $200 dollars was on the line for the person who could get
Phoenix down for a 3 COUNT!!! They managed the numbers but the competition was not there. Phoenix disposed of his first challenger with in 15 seconds of him entering the ring. With his experience in the ring of Kickboxing, he got through the gauntlet in 5 minutes, disposing of his last challenger with the
Rising Phoenix. The kick sent the challenger out of the ring. After the match he awarded the challengers $200 dollars each for taking part and being such good sports. 10-Men Battle-Royal -- Sex Symbol vs Syko vs Styles vs Jakks vs Bloodthorn vs Freak vs Simo Fletcha vs Richard vs Slade vs Shane Moore **the winner will compete for the Ultimate Heavyweight Championship at UFF Revelations** Still in his robe, Phoenix announced the final match of the night; The 10-Men Battle-Royal for the #1 Contender slot for the Ultimate Heavyweight Title. Phoenix explained the rules for the match, being that there are no pinfalls or submissions.
The only way of winning is by being the last person in the ring. At this, the lights dimmed and each fighter was introduced with thier music being covered by the house band - ELEMENT. The lights flashed back on and Phoenix called "FIGHT". Syko was set upon by
Styles and Freak. Bloodthorn
and Jakks went to work on Shane Moore. The rest were either going one on one or gang fighting. Shane Moore was the first to go although he struggled furiosly to get out of the grips of Jakks and Bloodthorn. They sent him crashing onto the bar floor. Sex Symbol was then set upon by Slade as
Fletcha and Richard went toe to toe. Style and
Freak were were trying
furiously to throw Syko out, but he wouldn't give. He had his arms wrapped around the ropes and kept  managing to hold on.
Freak then held held onto Syko's legs as Sytles began to hammer at his biceps with his elbows. Syko was screaming out in pain at each blow, his grip loosening every time. Bloodthorne and Jakks then came over to his aid. Jakks slammed a fist into the back of Freak's head, while Bloodthorn kicked at the back of Styles' knees, sending him to the mat. Bloodthorn turned Styles into a boston crab while Jakks was holding Freak from behind so that Syko could deliver his payback. Sex Symbol was then whipped into the ropes by Slade and was met with the Lights Out. The force of the kick sent him back over the rope - possibly a tooth or two less. Slade then went to Richard who was giving
Fletcha some hell in the corner. He lined him up for the Lights Out, but as he was excecuting the move, Richard dodged it and Fletcha recieved the full brunt of it. Richard gave Slade an elbow to the face and rolled Fletcha out of the ring. Richard then turned to the other side of the ring, where Syko,
Freak, Styles, Jakks, and Bloodthorn were trading blows. Standing in the middle of the ring, he calls out to Bloodthorn. As he turns, Richard gives him the Out Of Breath and sends him to the canvas.
Slade them came from behind and delivered a merciless atomic drop on Richard. With Bloodthorn gasping for air and on his knees, Slade mounts the turnbckle and delivers the 5 Star Frog Splash. He gets up and sprints across the ring at Richard seting him up for the clothesline, but he ducks and throws Slade out of the ring. He crashes onto the Internet announcers table
(at this second, the tranmission was cut, and only available to those who were at the event.) Quickly to his feet, Slade get up and runs ringside and grabs hold of Richards legs. He yanks them out from underneath him and drags him out of the ring onto the floor. A full on brawl erupts ringside between the two.
Meanwhile in the ring, still dazed from the splash, Bloodthorn gets up in time to see Jakks being put down by Freak with a low blow with the heel, then a DDT. He and Styles then turn thier attention to Syko who is on the top rope about to deliver a flying clothesline. But Freak grabs him in time, and with a running Gorilla Press, launches him into the crowd. Outside the ring, Richard and Slade are swinging chairs and dildos at each other.
Back in the ring, Bloodthorn is charging Styles for the Thorn but due to the speed of the move, he crashes into Styles and they both go through the ropes. Freak and Jakks, once again face each other as the last men in the ring. Freak suddenly lashes out with a hook to the face put Jakks ducks and goes for a low blow but Freak locks his knees together just in time. With a smile on his face, Freak headbutts Jakks and gives him a toe kick to the mid-section. With Jakks holding his stomach, Freak sets up Jakks with a running powerbomb into the turnbuckle. He raises his arms in the air and salutes the crowd. They give him a roar of approval. With great ferocity, Freak swings Jakks up onto his shoulders and begins to run at the corner. Suddenly, Jakks comes to life and reverses the move into a facebuster into the turnbuckle. The crowd goes wild and Jakks picks up Freak and delivers the Inverted Piledriver. With the crowd chanting his name, Jakks ends the fight with the Jakked-Up. Jakks removes the unconscious Freak from the ring and the night ends with Jakks automaticly getting his chance at the
