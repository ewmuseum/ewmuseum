Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Underground e-Mail Wrestling set out its plans in a founding handbook dated 17 February 1999, describing a federation based at the Elks Lodge in Somerville, Massachusetts, with weekly visits to small venues — high school gyms, other Elks lodges, even parks — in the spirit of the “underground” independent scene it said had inspired it. Its house style was deliberately loose: no ten-count on the outside, and no disqualification for using a chair or a table.

UeMW named three championships out of the gate — the UeMW Heavyweight, Hardcore, and Lightweight titles — and a weekly television card, “Furious Friday Night,” hosted by two fictional commentators, “Mindblowing” Max Newbern and “Thunderous” Tyrone Rice, under a fictional commissioner, Jason Jeromine, and a small in-fiction officiating crew.

The last surviving document, dated 22 February 1999, describes a newly finished website with no matches yet signed. No card, title change, or further update from UeMW survives, and whether it ever staged a show isn't known.
