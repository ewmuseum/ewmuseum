These federations were small time un-known federations that had die hard fans, after seeing what type of action these feds brought in, Chris decided to open a federation of his own. Based at the Elks Lodge in Somerville, Massachusetts, he opened what he believes is the best underground federation known, or unknown to the world. The UeMW makes weekly visits to small places, such as high school gyms, Elks lodges, and sometimes even gyms. Why hell they will go to a park if they can find some fans. - [The Style of UeMW] - The style of UeMW is simple, there are hardly any rules. There is no 10 count on the outside and there is no disqualifications when it comes to weapons. [chairs and tables.] You may be saying that this will be too extreme, but this is what the fans want, and as far as we here at the UeMW are concerned, the fans are _very_ important. __________________________________________________ - [ S E C T I O N 2 : A B O U T T H E P R E S I D E N T ] - *********************************************************** *********** - [About Me, [name withheld]] - Well I was born in the 80's. I have two dogs and I live in a small apartment in Medford, Massachusetts, is that good enough? No, anyways, I have been in the e-Wrestling world now for almost 2 years. I have handled many characters, iNSANiTY, HaRdcOrE, "Cutthroat" Craig Cypress, Dr. David Diversity, just to name a few. But the one that really got me well known is "Sadistic" Shane Alexander. He has been in many federations now, and still competes. I have also run two federations. I started out running the ESWA, the East Side Wrestling Alliance. But mobody was participating so I just closed it. The next fed I ran was the SSW, along with [name withheld]. This was open for a good 4 months, then my computer would no longer allow me to use America Online, so I had to use my sister's computer, this made it very hard for me to do anything, so I had to leave. Well, I am back on my computer now and ready to run the UeMW. With your help I know we can make this fed a great one. ___________________________________________________ - [ S E C T I O N 3 : R U L E S A N D G U I D E L I N E S ] - *********************************************************** ************ - [Rules on Role-playing] - In the UeMW, there are not that many rules when roleplaying, there is just these simple ones. - [Do not have racial slurs in your roleplay.] - [When doin scenes, use [ ] at the beginning and end.] - [You may swear in your roleplays but keep it down to a minimum.] That is it. Now tell me that you can not live with that, huh? ________________________________________________ - [ S E C T I O N 4 : C H A M P I O N S H I P B E L T S ] - *********************************************************** ********* - [Championship Belts] - The UeMW has three championship belts thus far, they are as follows... - [UeMW Heavyweight Championship] - This belt is the cream of the crop in the UeMW. When you have this title, you are on top of the UeMW. You must defend this title at least once a month, and MUST defend at a PPV. Any weight class is acceptable. - [UeMW Hardcore Championship] - This is the second highest belt in the UeMW, when you have this belt you better be ready for some HARDCORE action, you must defend this belt in a no dq, pinfalls anywhere type of match. That is a normal match with this belt, any type of match is acceptable, as long as it is HARDCORE. Any weight class is acceptable. - [UeMW Lightweight Championship] - This is the highest ranked belt for the light guys. We here at the UeMW know it is hard to beat a 320 pound man when you are only 190 pounds. That is why this belt is here. Any weight class BELOW 270 is acceptable. ___________________________ - [ S E C T I O N 5 : S H O W S ] - ************************************** - [Shows] - Shows are things that are sent out to keep you, the handler informed on what is goin on in, and out of the ring. These are sent by me from time to time. Here is a run down of the types of shows in the UeMW. __________________________________________________ - [ S E C T I O N 5 : S H O W S , F L A S H S H O W S ] - *********************************************************** *********** - [Flash Shows] - Flash Shows are something that I send out that contain the roleplays that you the handlers have sent me in for the week. We are under license agreement with WUNI Channel 30. That is the station that televises our shows, we have one weekly card, that is called, "Furious Friday Night." This is hosted by Max Newbern and Tyrone Rice. There is usually 6 - 8 matches on this show. ____________________________________________________ - [ S E C T I O N 5 : S H O W S , M O N T H L Y P P V ' S ] - *********************************************************** ************** - [Monthly PPV's] - Each month the UeMW will put on a supercard or a PPV.
