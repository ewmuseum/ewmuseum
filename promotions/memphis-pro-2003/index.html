Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Memphis PRO Wrestling, 'True Memphis Wrestling', set up on the fwrestling.com hub as a small fantasy federation of created characters: an owner who had wrestled as Genesis and was a Hall of Famer of another e-fed, WIW, a broadcast staff of four, fifteen wrestlers, two managers and five stables, with three belts (the Memphis PRO, Memphis Heritage and Memphis PRO Tag Team titles) and four pay-per-views named and never dated. It promised old-school, technical wrestling with few storylines.

No page says who held a title, no pay-per-view has a date, and the one card, Saturday Night Slam!, is listed on its archive page for 22 July 2006 with six matches and no results. The site's template carries a 2002 copyright line, which no dated page bears out. In 2007 a visitor to its message board asked whether the fed was still running, and no reply follows.
