Laguna was Chris Storm's next door neighbor when they grew up together. Despite not being born at the time, Laguna likes to refer to himself as the Disco King of 1979.
To this day, no one knows what Groovy Toovy Tom actually means. Despite his goofy nature, Laguna is an experienced tag team wrestler. 'It's time to celebrate! One more time.'
