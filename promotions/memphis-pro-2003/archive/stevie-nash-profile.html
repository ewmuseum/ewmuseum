Nash's family is big in the oil industry and as a result he was able to buy training from some of the top talent across the globe.
Memphis PRO is only Nash's third promotion. Despite now allied as Ratings Boost, Paige and Stevie Nash use to be heated rivals in a local Ohio promotion.
Nash has never held a title in any wrestling promotion. 'When you're as talented as me, it just comes easy.'
