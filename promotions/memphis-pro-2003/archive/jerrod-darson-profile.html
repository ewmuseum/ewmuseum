Jerrod Darson is the 45% owner of Memphis PRO. The other 55% is divided up among investors.
As Genesis, Jerrod Darson is the first ever WIW Hall of Fame member. The only other WIW Hall of Fame member on the roster is Chris Storm, who was trained by Genesis.
Swearing off in-ring action, Jerrod looks to focus on the business side of wrestling. Fear this.
