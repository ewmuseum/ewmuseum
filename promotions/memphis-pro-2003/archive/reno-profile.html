Reno is the older brother of fellow Memphis PRO star, Laguna. This is Reno's first wrestling promotion. Original Reno never had plans of being a wrestler. He was content with being a bodyguard for the head of a major company. It was Laguna who talked him into, and trained him, to be a wrestler.
