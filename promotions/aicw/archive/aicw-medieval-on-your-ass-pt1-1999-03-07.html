AICW Medieval On Your ASS -- Part 1 of 41 (7 March 1999)

7 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a dark match, Polka Tolk defeated "Big" Mark Brickowski, a popular local worker from New Jersey, in 2:14.
AICW Medieval On Your ASS! Sunday, March 7, 1999 Live from Medieval Times, Lyndhurst, New Jersey < "Silk Road" by Kitaro begins to play in the background.> Dan Chan (V/O): We have travelled a long and memorable road. [Shot of an AICW tour bus driving down an empty highway.] Dan Chan: Some of us have been here since the very beginning. [Sensuous Samantha leads the Perfectly Perfect Duo down the aisle October 11, 1997 at the Nassau Coliseum.] Dan Chan: Others have joined us at different points in between. [Shot of "The Cadet" Lance Marshall stepping into the ring May 21,
1998 to make his in-ring debut at the Hartford Civic Center.]
Dan Chan: There were even a few who left us along the way, in manners both good and bad. [Johnny Payne gets carried out on a stretcher, January 5, 1998 at the
Key Arena.] Dan Chan: We had plenty of good times... ["Hard Line" Byron Brady holds up the AICW World Title for the first time, after defeating "The Demon" Kyle Bane at New Year's Revolution.]
Dan Chan: ...and even suffered through some bad times. [Mark McMahon nearly suffers a career ending neck injury at the hands of "Pretty Boy" Troy Walcott on an edition of Pretty City, March 9,
1998.]
Dan Chan: There were some who claimed great victories along the path... ["Big D" Devon Burke wins the Inoki Cup in Japan on March 29, 1998 at the Tokyo Dome.] Dan Chan: ...and others who suffered defeat and frustrations. [Mr. Y loses to prelim wrestler Steve Dallas in an impromptu best-of-seven October 20, 1997.] Dan Chan: Yes, we have gone on a great journey. And tonight, no matter what their agenda, or their cause, these superstars, the finest athletes the world has ever seen, will have their chance to place their names in the annals of history. Because whether they are a legend...
[The Knick Man walks down the aisle confidently, tagging the hands of his fans.] Dan Chan: ...an enemy... ["The Demon" Kyle Bane storms down the aisle May 25, 1998 to face "Hard Line" Byron Brady at Madison Square Garden for the third time.] Dan Chan: ...or a hero, ["Hard Line" Byron Brady acknowledges the crowd at Steel City Meltdown.] Dan Chan: they have come in search of a common goal. Immortality. Whether they have tasted success in the past, or whether they are looking to rise above past defeats, one thing is for sure. Tonight these superstars will come together in the greatest spectacle the wrestling world has ever seen. The time has come to let memories remain as such, for tonight, for each of them, means one of two
things--bitter defeat... ["8 Ball" Vic Garibaldi hangs his head after dropping the International Title to Mista Smith.] Dan Chan: ...or triumphant glory. [A shot of the fans cheering "Hard Line" Byron Brady after his victory over the Saint at Steel City Meltdown.]
Dan Chan: Tonight, the AICW converges on Lyndhurst, New Jersey. To the most unusual of places, on perhaps the most unusual journey, on what may just turn out to be the most unusual night in AICW history.
Tonight we get Medieval On Your ASS! [We get a shot of the exterior of Medieval Times. Fans and press have collected along the sides of a red carpet. We focus on Dan "The Man"
Chan, who is standing on the sidewalk, where the limousines pull up.] Dan Chan: WELCOME EVERYONE! I'm Dan "The Man" Chan, and we are LIVE from Medieval Times, and you can see that the limousines are beginning to pull up! Many of the superstars have already arrived! I saw the
Stone and Erotic Erin earlier. Alex Raptor showed up just a few minutes ago, as did The Knick Man! Anyway, we're about to see who this next one is. WHAT THE HELL IS THAT?!
[Shot of a red carriage driven by two donkeys pulling up. Dan opens the door, and Dima "The Russian Powerhouse" Lerminskiy walks out. He is met with a chorus of boos from the crowd.]
Dan Chan: Ladies and gentlemen, my broadcast colleague, Dima Lerminskiy has arrived! Dima how are you feeling tonight? Dima Lerminskiy: The great DIMA feels like million rubles! They even rolled out red carpet for DIMA because they know that Communism is taking over tonight! These puny Americans, you call "press" will witness first hand, with their cheap Japanese photo cameras, the
DESTRUCTION of the capitalist system that the AICW has run under for far too long. The proletariat will rise tonight, and the great DIMA, the Red Scare himself, will ascend his rightful throne!
Dan Chan: It sure is great to be here, and right now we've got another limousine arriving! OH MY!! It's "HARD LINE" BYRON BRADY AND KELLY
LYNN!! [The sound of a car horn honking in the distance rapidly increases in volume.] Dan Chan: OH NO!!! OH GOD NO!!! ANOTHER LIMOUSINE HAS JUST REAR-ENDED THE BRADY LIMOUSINE!!! I THINK THEIR BRAKES MUST'VE WENT
OUT!!! Thank God that Byron Brady pulled Kelly Lynn out of the limo just in time! She would've been mauled!!
Dima Lerminskiy: Like I told you, Dan. Puny American parts cannot hold up in car! You need RUSSIAN STEEL! The greatest construction in world.
[The door of the other limousine opens up.] Dan Chan: OH MY GOD!!! THAT'S NICKY DE NIRO'S LIMO!!! "Honest" Nicky
De Niro has rear-ended Byron Brady's limo! Something must've been up!
De Niro now apologizing to Brady, and now he's coming this way! Byron and Kelly heading inside the building! "Honest" Nicky De Niro: Our brakes went out! OH MY! I could've been killed! Look at the damage on this limo! It's totaled!
Dan Chan: Mr. De Niro, get a hold of yourself! What the hell was that stunt all about? Kelly Lynn could've been seriously hurt, if not killed!!
"Honest" Nicky De Niro: What are you talking about? Kelly? WHAT ABOUT ME?! I got the two biggest matches of my life, and I'm not gonna spend them filling out paperwork in a hospital bed! I've had enough of talking to you! I've got to get a new suit on, so if you'd excuse me!
Dan Chan: "Honest" Nicky De Niro looking a bit flustered! The limousines continuing to arrive, and the superstars will just sort of have to work their way around the wreckage right here. There's Zeek
Monkey, and I think that Reba Kola is going to try to catch up with him!
Reba Kola: Thanks, Dan! Here I am outside waiting for the wrestlers to show up... This looks to be an exciting evening, and I personally can't wait to get things started.
[Arriving in an olive green Toyota 4-Runner is Zeek Monkey and Nikki
Electra. Zeek is wearing a black shirt that says "Guns don't kill people, I kill people." and some jeans. Nikki is dressed in a bright red sweater and tight jeans. They get out and shake the hands of the fans as they walk up to Reba.]
Reba Kola: Hi Nikki, hi Zeek. What are your thoughts on the last ever
AICW show? Nikki Electra: I'm sad to see it go. Tonight, though should be good. We're just going to try to sit back and relax tonight. I had a long talk with Zeek, and he promised to behave himself tonight.
Zeek Monkey: No I didn't. I said that I would try not to snap anyone's neck tonight. I got a little problem with that, you know...
Reba Kola: Well, Zeek... you're nominated for- [Reba is interrupted by a loud horn blaring from a long stretch limo as Billy Kane and his nameless/faceless date arrive to the show. Billy is wearing a black polo shirt along with tan khaki pants. His date is dressed in a long blue gown that shows a LOT of cleavage. Billy, ignoring his date, runs right up to the camera, flashing his trademark grin.]
Billy Kane: This camera just loves me, you know. Hey there, Zeek. Can you go away please, you're scaring away the viewers.
Zeek Monkey: Hello, Kane. Reba Kola: Billy, that was rude! Zeek here was in the middle of an interview. Billy Kane: You see, Reba... it's stuff like that which got the AICW closed down. You see, they didn't put the BDK on camera enough, and when you're stuck looking at guys like Lance Marshall and Zeek
Monkeyface over here, you lose viewers. Now, see... if the BDK was the champ, and I was on TV every week, the AICW would have more viewers than that
Seinfeld bloke. Why? Because I'm Billy Kane, and I'm the man.
[Billy looks over and notices Nikki.] Billy Kane: Zeek, how'd you get a girl like this? I'll tell you what hon, why don't you dump the freak, and I'll do you a favor and make you a Kane flavor of the week.
Nikki Electra: No thank you. I like going out with men, not mice. Zeek Monkey: Step off, Kane... Billy Kane: You see, once you've been with the Big Daddy Kane, all other men look like... monkeys.
Zeek Monkey: I SAID STEP OFF!!!! Reba Kola: Zeek just tackled Kane. He's brawling it out with Kane, and security is going to have to break these two apart. Zeek is calming down, and is heading inside. Wait, here comes Billy again...
Billy Kane: Who does this guy think he is? Look what he did to my pants (pointing to a small dirt mark on his pants). That's it... he got me angry. I'm going to get that freak show and his little girlie tonight, mark my words.
Reba Kola: Billy just stormed off with his little bimbo... Well, if that's any indication of how this night's going to be, then I can't wait. Back to you, Dan.
