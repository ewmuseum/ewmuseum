AICW In Your Face World Tour, 7 August 1998

1998-08-07
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AYCW In Your Face World Tour Friday, August 7th, 1998 The ARCO Arena, Sacramento, California <An aerial shot of Sacramento> <We see the streets of the city, filled with activity. Headlights of the cars below illuminating the streets. We move faster and lower, until we are merely 40 feet above the ground. DAN CHAN's voice is heard over the shot> DAN CHAN: Here we are. In the streets of Sacramento. Where just moments ago, the AYCW invaded the jam packed ARCO Arena! We are with you live to bring you the exclusive updates and results of this memorable event! Here we are, in the heart of the city, sitting down in one of the most exclusive nightspots on Franklin Boulevard, McDonalds!! <The aerial shot slows to a halt, and hovers over a McDonalds restaurant. The camera takes us inside, where we see DAN CHAN sitting with "THE MACK" JAMES MACK and DIMA "THE RUSSIAN POWERHOUSE" LERMINSKIY. Dan is having a 6 piece Chicken McNuggets meal. Dima is having a cheeseburger happy meal. And James is having a Big Mac. Dan turns to look at the camera> DAN CHAN: Hello everyone, and welcome to the In Your Face World Tour! I'm your host, Dan "The Man" Chan, and with me tonight are Dima "The Russian Powerhouse" Lerminskiy, and "The Mack" James Mack! JAMES MACK: Yo Dan, you know why the call this the Big Mac? <James holds up the sandwich box to Dan's face.> JAMES MACK: They named it after ME! Because I'm "THE MACK"! And because this sandwich is the BOMB, know what I'm sayin'? Burger King can't floss with "The Mack"! <James goes back to eating his sandwich and Dan continues:> DAN CHAN: I'm glad you're enjoying your meal, James. Anyway, I know you folks may be getting hungry just watching us eat, so why don't you get yourself some popcorn or something, and relax, 'cos we got a show to put on and we'll be back, right after this important paid advertisement. Stay with us! <Everyone goes back to eating their meal, and we fade to black.> <An advertisement pops on screen. It is a view of the Flatiron Building in New York City. A familiar voice appears on screen:> VOICE: They are the criminals. <The image of "8 BALL" VIC GARIBALDI and JAMES "THE DON" GARIBALDI appears on screen.> VOICE: They are the ones running this city into the ground. <The image of the skyline of New York City appears on screen.> VOICE: It is against my principle to let these men continue their foul ways. <The image of Vic Garibaldi stomping "Big D" Devon Burke is shown in slow motion.> VOICE: They are responsible for 43 mob-related deaths between 1983 and 1996. This man is a crook. In EVERY sense of the word. <Vic Garibaldi's face appears in an extreme close-up.> VOICE: And he has tried to ruin... <A familiar, yet sad looking face looks up to the camera.> VOICE: ...my honest name. <Nicky De Niro.> "HONEST" NICKY DE NIRO: Vic, let's do business... <Shot of "Honest" Nicky De Niro raising his arms in victory.> "HONEST" NICKY DE NIRO: ...one more time. <Fade to black.> <We return to McDonalds, where Dan Chan is ready to begin the broadcast. James bobs his head up and down to some unheard rhythm, and Dima plays with a toy he got in his Happy Meal.> DAN CHAN: Fans, we're back. It was only just about an hour ago, when the AI.. sorry..YCW took over the ARCO Arena. It was a sell-out crowd, and in the first contest, MIKE MARCONI made his AYCW debut! Before the contest, however, our cameras picked up these comments! (The Tarantella starts to blast throughout the arena. Mike Marconi comes out from the curtains wearing a black Armani suit, and black sunglasses. He walks briskly down towards the ring, ignoring the people. He then calls for the mic, standing just outside the ring, in front of the steps.) (V)arconi: Ok, look, I'm gonna make this short and sweet, cause thats all you people deserve.... (The crowd reacts with a torrent of boos and catcalls.) (V)arconi: Oh, why don't you idiots shut it...where I come from there's a little thing called RESPECT...but its obvious none of you ingrates have that. But I'm not out here to waste my time talking to you people. I joined AICW for a few reasons. One of the reasons I'm here is to be a problem solver. You got a problem...I fix it...plain and simple, for the right price, or course. Another reason I came here to AICW...well its no secret as to how dangerous a wrestler I am. I've put down bigger and better than the best this league has to offer. And whether or not you have seen what I can do is irrevelevant..simply because TONIGHT, you will see FIRST HAND why people do thier best to dodge Mike Marconi. So come on AICW, quit wasting my time and send down whatever you have to offer, cause time is money...and don't you forget it. <Mike Marconi throws the mic down as our hand-held camera shows us a shot from inside the ARCO Arena. A man by the name of Meat Johnson stands in the ring, awaiting his opponent. Marconi looks confident as he places one foot on the steel steps and looks out at the crowd, which is giving him a heated reaction. Dan's voice is heard over these scenes> DAN CHAN: Mike Marconi, the acting "bounty hunter" of the AYCW of sorts, made his in-ring debut in the opening contest earlier tonight. Marconi was impressive, showcasing a versatile arsenal of power manuevers! He proved tonight that not only can he get the job done for someone else, but he can also take care of business for himself! <Mike Marconi covers Meat Johnson. Referee James Roma makes the three count. Marconi stands tall as Andy Burton makes the official announcement. Roma moves to raise Marconi's hand, but Marconi pulls away from James as if he had some disease. Mike Marconi walks to the corner and stands on the second turnbuckle raising his hands to the mixed approval of the fans.> DAN CHAN: What a match that was! Dima, what was your first impression upon seeing Mike Marconi in the ring? DIMA LERMINSKIY: BAH! He was pathetic! Nobody can get the job done, unless it is done the RUSSIAN way! This Marconi will fail when it counts most! DAN CHAN: And James, what about you? <James pauses for a brief second.> JAMES MACK: Who's Mike Marconi? DAN CHAN: It's no use.. Fans, after that match, it was time for "Southern Gentleman" Tommy Lane to step into the ring with the GWO's Notorious GS! This match was one of the most surprising of the evening. Tommy Lane got the early advantage, and came close to victory several times! <From the handheld camera, Notorious GS bounces off the ropes and is caught by a devastating Tommy Lane spinebuster. Lane covers. Only a two count by referee Ryan Gentle. Later in the match, Notorious GS goes for a big boot to the kisser. Lane ducks and connects with a superkick. GS goes down like a ton of bricks and Lane makes the cover, hooking the leg. Gentle counts. ONE. TWO. Kickout!> DAN CHAN: The turning point of this one came when the Notorious GS, in control, distracted the referee!! Tommy Lane was recovering in the corner, when all of a sudden--THIS happened! <Tommy Lane leans in the corner, while referee Ryan Gentle argues with GS. Lane shakes his head, clearing the cobwebs. From behind, Kato steps up on the apron. Before Lane can do anything, Kato connects with a roundhouse kick to the back of Lane's skull. Lane collapses holding the back of his head and GS moves in to make the cover. ONE. TWO. THREE. Gentle calls for the bell and raises the Notorious GS's hand in victory.> DAN CHAN: That had to be one of the toughest upsets in the history of the AYCW. DIMA LERMINSKIY: Tough?! You Americans always complaining about tough!! Back in Russia, I sleep in snow for THREE DAYS! THREE DAYS!!! What do you know about hardships!! I am STRONG LIKE BULL!! <Dima pounds his chest.> DAN CHAN: After the match, Tommy Lane was reportedly VERY upset about the officiating in this contest and was overheard saying he "had enough" and was "going home." Is this the last we see of Tommy Lane? JAMES MACK: Nah, yo. He'll be back. Booty call, that's all. DAN CHAN: In the third contest of the evening, "Excellent in Everyway" Mike Robinson made his debut. I talked to some fans after the show, and each and every one of them told me he was a mountain of ego. Nonetheless, he was impressive in the ring, defeating local star, Robinson Caruso. <Mike Robinson is seen in the ring, whipping Caruso to the ropes. Caruso comes off lightning fast and gets nailed by a superkick, dislodging a tooth. Robinson stands over his opponent arrogantly as the fans let him have it. Robinson turns his attention back to his opponent, and he connects with the Perfect-Photo Flash. Referee Ramon De La Cerveza attempts to raise Robinson's hand in victory, but Robinson ignores him and celebrates on his own.> DAN CHAN: That man will certainly go very far in the A*Y*CW. JAMES MACK: Depending on how far the AYCW goes. With Mr. Y in control, we're all bound for bankruptcy. But that's all cool with the Mack. Because I gots the skills to pay the bills! DAN CHAN: After that it was time for some tag team action, featuring Entrepeneurs Inc. against All You Can Eat. Brian Walters and Scott Howard hit the ring first, to a particularly unreceptive crowd. The fans were behind All You Can Eat for this one, and they took the early advantage. <Big Bob Twinkie whips Scott Howard to the buckle and follows in with a HUGE avalanche. Howard, crushed under Twinkie's girth, falls face first on the mat. Twinkie makes the tag to Fat Daddy, who enters the ring, and drops a tree-trunk legdrop across the back of Howard's neck.> DAN CHAN: Surprisingly, Entrepeneurs Inc was able to withstand the early onslaught of All You Can Eat. However, as the match neared the ten minute mark, All You Can Eat appeared to tire out! <Brian Walters reverses an Irish whip by Fat Daddy. Daddy stumbles off the ropes, huffing and puffing. Walters connects with a pinpoint dropkick to the jaw, stunning the Fat one. Walters follows up with another, backing Fat Daddy against the ropes. Still stunned, Fat Daddy gets sent for the ride by Walters, who connects with a clothesline. Fat Daddy slowly flips over the ropes backwards, and hits the ringside floor with a splat.> DAN CHAN: After that, it was only a matter of time before All You Can Eat succumbed to the speed and ring savvy of Entrepeneurs Inc. <Big Bob Twinkie misses a big splash attempt as Brian Walters rolls out of harms way. Twinkie is still down as Brian Walters quickly applies the 4.0 GPA. After a few seconds, referee Peter Kaplan calls for the bell, signaling Andy Burton to announce Entrepeneurs Inc. as the winners.> DAN CHAN: That was certainly some great tag team action, as Entrepeneurs Inc. continue their winning ways. JAMES MACK: Everyone knows that James Mack is the only madd money makin' machine in the AICW. DIMA LERMINSKIY: AYCW. DAN CHAN: After that, we were interrupted by none other than "DIE HARD" DARREN SHAW! Shaw had something to say, and a challenge to throw! Our cameras picked this up! <"New Skin" by Incubus begins to play through the arena, all of a sudden, "Die Hard" Darren Shaw emerges from behind the curtain wearing a pair of baggy dark blue jeans and one of the new LA Kings away jerseys. He comes out arms raised. He makes his way towards the ring, slapping some fans' hands along the way. He spots one fan, apparently his wife, Shannon, in the first row, and hugs and kisses her. He walks around the ring to slap more fans' hands, then steps into the ring as the ring announcer hands him a microphone.> "DIE HARD" DARREN SHAW: Okay, I appreciate the welcome folks, thanks. There are a few things I need to talk about, however. <Darren looks around at the fans. One of them says to Darren, "YOU DA MAN!!" Darren smiles and looks back at the camera to talk.> "DIE HARD" DARREN SHAW: First, words can't express how happy I am to be in AICW. I was in some deep stuff in the indy feds. I was going nowhere fast, then I found AICW, or it found me. I'm really glad to be here. I want to thank all of the fans out there for trusting me and I want to thank the AICW Executive Committee for letting me in. I'm sure I'll enjoy AICW... <Darren blinks a little to keep himself from shedding a tear. He looks back at the camera once more, ony with a stern look on his face.> "DIE HARD" DARREN SHAW: Next, I'd like to tell a certain somebody what I think of him. He's a current contender for the International Title. Now before I go on, I don't want anybody to think of me as some brash rookie automatically going after the champ, I just have a real problem with this guy...we go back when I was a mid-carder His name is Brandon Matthews. Remember me, Brandon? Of course not, you were a big main-eventer then, while I was in everybody else's shadow. I didn't have a problem there, but I could never understand why anybody would ever think of you as anything special. You have no talent, no brains, and you can't wrestle for s%*&!!! Well Brandon...you and I are equals now and I can bring the secret grudge I had against you back into the light. I've always hated cocky a$$holes like you. I look at those types of people and I notice that they had everything handed to them on a silver platter, I never got anything that way bud, so you're gonna have to take a page outta my book and start workin' on your poor @$$ just like I did...See ya in the ring chump... <"New Skin" by Incubus plays again as "Die Hard" Darren Shaw leaves the ring area and makes his way back to the locker room.> DAN CHAN: Darren Shaw has made a challenge to "Hot Property" Brandon Matthews! We'll keep you posted as this situation develops further! What a match that would be! DIMA LERMINSKIY: BAH! No different than any other match featuring two PATHETIC, NO-TALENT American capitalist pigs! DAN CHAN: Well, after that, fans had an opportunity to see "The Modern Day Outlaw" Ryan Thorne in action! This man showcased his take no prisoners attitude, winning his debut contest against local superstar "Inbred" Johnny Sacramento! <Handheld camera focuses on the hard look of Ryan Thorne, making his way down the aisle. Without even waiting for the bell, Thorne goes to work on Sacramento. Thorne whips him hard to the ropes, and Sacramento comes flying off, only to get caught with a vicious looking spinebuster. Sacramento wriggles around clutching his back, and Thorne wastes no time in executing the Gutbuster, a gutwrench suplex into a powerbomb. Referee Ryan Gentle makes the three count and Ryan Thorne is declared the winner.> DAN CHAN: Ryan Thorne going out and getting the job done! JAMES MACK: I know all the ladies are wondering if I can get the job done. DAN CHAN: Well anyway, the next matchup of the night was Adrian Polonsky against "Camel" Joe Banno! Before the contest, however, Polonsky had these comments! <Adrian Polonsky is in an AICW locker room. He has on a plain black t-shirt, and snakeskin pants & boots. He has something small in his hands. He suddenly notices the camera and looks up.> ADRIAN POLONSKY: "Camel" Joe Banno. That 'ad me thinkin'. <Holds up a picture with a young Adrian Polonsky riding a camel.> ADRIAN POLONSKY: Lil' Jimmy once 'ad a camel down in Australia. We fed it some beer an all great stuff, but then 'e stepped on one a me gators- that's somethin' ya don't do! So I beat that son a camel... <Holds up another picture with him choking the camel while a group of people stand in awe.> ADRIAN POLONSKY: ...and we 'ad camel stew. <Shows a picture of some Australian rednecks eating some stew and laughing.> ADRIAN POLONSKY: Ol' Adrian Polonsky is going up against Joe Banno. This 'as t' be a good match t' get me a win, cause I've beaten men bigger than ye, men stronger than ye, hell, I've even beaten a camel before! Just look at me! <Makes a muscle and poses for the camera.> ADRIAN POLONSKY: I have th' power and th' ability to take down a week fool like you. Cause one way or the other, this match is gonna change th' AICW. DAN CHAN: <Back at McDonalds.> Strong words from Adrian Polonsky, who was able to back those up to get a win against "Camel" Joe Banno! <Handheld camera shows Adrian Polonsky connect with a diving headbutt. The crowd gets on it's feet and cheers as Polonsky, sensing victory, applies the Australian Spike. Banno struggles to break free, but is unable to last very long before passing out. Referee James Roma calls for the bell and raises Polonsky's arm in triumph.> DAN CHAN: Adrian Polonsky back on the winning track with a big win over the struggling Joe Banno! JAMES MACK: But he'll never be as mackin' as the Mack! DAN CHAN: After that match concluded, something very bizarre happened in the locker room! Our cameras were attempting to get a word with Juicy Fruit, however, when they opened the door to his locker room, he was nowhere to be found! <Cameras show an empty locker room. In the background, "Der Kommissar" by After the Fire can be heard blasting in the arena, signalling the entrance of Alex Raptor.> DAN CHAN: It was time for the first match of the first round of the Inoki Cup tournament, and the Blue Chip, Alex Raptor, patiently awaited the arrival of his opponent, Juicy Fruit! Problem is, Juicy Fruit failed to show up! A ten count was given, but when Juicy didn't appear, Alex Raptor was declared the winner by forfeit!! That means that Raptor will advance to the semi-finals! <Camera shows Alex Raptor raising his hands in victory.> DAN CHAN: The question in everyone's mind: Where was Juicy Fruit? Well, our cameras picked up this shocking footage, just outside the door to "Excellent In Everyway" Mike Robinson's locker room!! <Camera shows the outside of Mike Robinson's locker room. Standing next to the door, holding a box of chocolates in the shape of a heart and a bouquet of red roses is none other than Juicy Fruit. Juicy is knocking on the door, and muttering sweet little nothings. We cut back to Dan, James, and Dima in McDonalds.> DAN CHAN: I would hate to be Mike Robinson right now. Apparently Juicy Fruit liked what he saw earlier and is now courting the Excellent one. Although fans didn't see that footage in the arena, they were getting ready for the MAIN EVENT!! First, the ringside enforcers were introduced! <"Bullet in your hand" plays over the loud speaker. Devon Burke steps out from behind the curtain. He is wearing a pair of black Nike shorts, a pair of Nike sandals and a white t shirt bearing a characterized picture of Alex Raptor asking a "Mama" duck "Are you my mother?." His mop of hair is held back by a thin black headband and his gottee is neatly trimmed. He grins and nods to the audience. He is holding a sign that reads, "Three WOPS and a WANNABE." He slaps hands with some ringside fans and grabs a directors chair from ring side and makes himself comfortable.> <"Great American Nightmare" by White Zombie plays as "Hard Line" Byron Brady and Daron Foley make their way down the aisle. Byron is dressed in jeans and an Alliance tee shirt with two black and white, referee styled armbands around his biceps. Brady and Foley grab chairs and join Devon Burke at ringside.> DAN CHAN: After that, the Triple Threat was introduced! <Fans jeer as Chandler Stone, "Hot Property" Brandon Matthews, and "Devastating" Daniel Lewis emerge, accompanied by Erotic Erin. All three play up to the crowd, Stone drawing the most boos by giving fans the People's Finger.> DAN CHAN: T3 and the ringside enforcers exchanged words briefly, until it was time for the Firm to be introduced. However, all was not right with the Firm! <The arena goes quiet as "8 Ball" Vic Garibaldi and George Muldoon make their way to the ring. They bumps fists with as many fans as they can reach, and when they reach ringside, Muldoon gives his bandana to a kid at ringside and bumps fists with him. They then steps in the ring and raises their fists in the air as the crowd pops loudly. Abscent from the group is "The Convict" Oswald Bates. Muldoon asks for a microphone.> GEORGE MULDOON: Well...As you can see, Ozzy Bates ain't here, so I guess it'll be just Vic and I, just like the old days. BUT...This also shows us what kinda man Ozzy is. He did this to me when I first started, and left me to deal with the T3 alone. Now, he's done it again, but this time, I have Vic to help me out! DAN CHAN: <back in McDonalds> Muldoon started things off against Daniel Lewis, and the Firm took the early advantage. Muldoon, the fastest man in the ring, kept Lewis off balance in the early going. <Handheld camera shows George Muldoon duck under a clothesline. Muldoon comes off the ropes fast, and catches Lewis with a spinning heel kick. Muldoon makes a quick cover, but Lewis springs up before Peter Kaplan can make the count.> DAN CHAN: Soon after that, Muldoon made the tag into Vic, who was caught by a thumb to the eye. Lewis took advantage of this, and tagged in the STONE! <Stone enters the ring and plants Vic with a swinging DDT. Stone drops a quick elbow before flashing the People's Finger towards George Muldoon. Muldoon attempts to enter the ring, but referee Peter Kaplan cuts him off, allowing T3 to double-team Garibaldi.> DAN CHAN: T3 succesfully cut the ring off with their decidedly underhanded tactics. Brandon Matthews was in control over Vic, when all of a sudden, "Hard Line" Byron Brady lent the Firm a helping hand. <Matthews nails Vic Garibaldi with a kick to the groin behind Peter Kaplan's back. Byron Brady springs out of his chair and heads for the announcers table. He grabs the AICW World Title and ...PUTS IT ON! Brady shows the belt off to the crowd, which is going wild. Stone complains to Kaplan, giving George Muldoon the chance to enter the ring and take Matthews down with a dropkick to the jaw. He drags Garibaldi to his corner, and just as Kaplan turns he makes the tag! Muldoon comes in and unloads on Matthews, eventually connecting with a piledriver.> DAN CHAN: It seemed as if things were all over for T3. Muldoon was working on the Stone, and after nailing his with a dropkick to the knee, tagged in Vic once more! <Vic Garibaldi comes back in the ring and the crowd goes wild as Stone backs away on his knees. Garibaldi nails Stone with a right hand, and pulls Stone's legs out from under him. Garibaldi drags Chandler to the corner, and nails him with the CORNER POCKET! Erin looks worried on the outside of the ring, as Matthews and Lewis look on. Garibaldi looks out to the crowd. The fans stand up and call for Vic to end it. Vic signals for the SHAKEDOWN...and applies it! It looks like it is all over for the Stone. Stone, obviously in pain, looks towards Erotic Erin for help. She shouts instructions to Matthews and Lewis, who immediately charge into the ring. Lewis dropkicks Vic from behind, freeing the Stone. Brady and Foley call for Kaplan to disqualify T3, but nothing is done. Muldoon enters the ring, and a full scale brawl breaks out. Muldoon fends off Matthews and Lewis, while Vic and Stone trade blows. Seeing enough, referee Peter Kaplan calls for the bell. Moments later, Andy Burton announces the decision... ...A DOUBLE DISQUALIFICATION!> DAN CHAN: That's how it ended folks. The Firm was able to do surprisingly well, considering they were one man short! After the brawl was broken up by the ringside enforcers, Stone turned his attention back to "Hard Line" Byron Brady, who was still wearing Stone's title! <Brady, refusing to hand over the title, dares the Stone to fight him. Stone teases, but then whispers something to the referee. Kaplan notifies Andy Burton, who announces that if Brady does not hand deliver the title to the Stone, he will be SUSPENDED!!! Brady, after contemplating for several moments, hands the title to Stone, who gives the Alliance members the People's Finger as they leave the ring.> DAN CHAN: What a night it was folks! How did you enjoy tonights card, guys? JAMES MACK: It was a'ight! DIMA LERMINSKIY: In Russia, our cards are much more exiting than your pathetic American cards! I missed it all, because I fell asleep, Dan! DAN CHAN: I for one had a great time, and we hope you did too! Fans, we'll see you again on Monday for WAR! Be with us then! So long! <Cameras fade to black as Dan, Dima, and James throw out their trays, and leave the McDonalds.>
