Guru won the very bizarre cage match where special guest referee Pseudo Mecca apparently sided with Phantom the whole match,only to turn and count Phantom's shoulders with the fastest count in wrestling history. Phantom is pissed and he wants his
Extreme title back. Guru is immediately defending the XPW Extreme title this week in a match with former XPW World champion Misfit Chris Douvavlas
Superstar Sean Copeland withdrawals from elimination match Copeland did not give a reason for pulling out of the match,but it looks to make the match even better as new star Whiplash faces two of wrestlings biggest heels Jason Cline and Chandler Lewis in this three way match.
Dalton to compete for second title Flamethrower Rob Dalton , current XPW US champion looks to add to his trophy case as he and Devilman Christian Alicea team against the undefeated champions,Havok and Collosus,the Marvel Corporation. Dalton is at the top of his game right, and the Devilman should show up for a classic title bout.
Jason Cline causing heat with bookers already Although XPW president Johnny Lords sent out a memo addressing his opinion of
XPW strats,the "Prodigy" had to argue heavily about how important his strat was to the Prez. After careful consideration,Hellkid gave Cline the benefit of the doubt. It's clear though that Hellkid doesn't have patience with anybody who badmouths the XPW. Insulting XPW while employed here could result in serious humiliation. Insult the President...he likes that. But don't insult the fed.
We've got dope graphics, a dedicated roster, and shows that come out every week! I believe XPW to be the premiere federation on the internet......and don't forget,we have porn!!!! Cline is expected to wrestle Chandelr Lewis and
Whiplash at WoW.
Jack Mannon arrives in XPW,dissappears Legendary "Good Guy" Jack Mannon arrived in XPW and promptly vanished. Apparently his nephew Brian Mannon will wrestle Jack's dates on the schedule until Jack returns....
