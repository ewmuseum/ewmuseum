Well, it seems as if WET has gotten its SHIT together. =) WET's first card, _FIRST PENETRATION_, will be held on June 27, 1999. The final day for apps (which is posted below) will be on June 20, 1999. WET is a European based federation, mainly running cards out of England. A good percentage of our cards will be run out of the WET Arena in Manchester, England and Wembley Stadium would be our big arena (ala WWF with MSG) . . . we will have annual dated cards there (ala NJPW with the Tokyo Dome). EVERYONE who turns in a WET app by June 20 will be included into a battle royal. The final 20 will remain in WET. The final seven will be put in the World Title tournament with the winner of the battle royal getting an automatic bye into the second round. The others who don't make the 20 man cut will be placed on the waiting list. The battle royal will be in World War 3 format. If you don't know what that is, it is a three ring battle royal with a certain amount of men in it. The final man in each ring then move into one ring for a three-way-dance. The winner, simply stated, is the last man standing. For First Penetration, one RP will be used, but two can be done . . . only the best one of the two will count . . . NO IN-RINGS OR STRATS WILL BE USED!
