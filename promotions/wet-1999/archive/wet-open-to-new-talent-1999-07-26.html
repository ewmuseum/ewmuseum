My loyal WETites... We at the proud organization, Wrestling's Eminently Talented, would like to invite ALL of you to become part of our roster. Now, realize that you won't definitely get in, all members of the WET committee are VERY picky...but before I tell you how to join, let me give you some info on the fed that is WET:
WET was created June 8th, 1999 in the #WET channel on Starchat. In that channel, 10 of e-wrestling's best handlers met to plan what would hopefully be one of the greatest, most original feds the e-wrestling world has ever known. Instead...we thought up WET.
No. WET is _NOT_ a parody fed. In fact, we're exactly like your average fed...except we have a Beaver as our mascot, a kick ass acronym, and our fed is ran by a committee, as opposed to just one single fed head. Oh yeah....I forgot to mention, we _OWN_ you.
We have 3 shows as of now: WET TV- our flash show Saturday Night Splash- Weekly card The Update Show- a special livewire type show BUT...the best part about WET is...once you're a member..you _OWN_ EVERYONE who isn't... so... If you want to do the Happy Beaver Dance with the WET Beaver, enjoy 3 high quality shows a week, and _OWN_ almost EVERYONE...
JOIN WET! How? Simply go to the WET Website and fill out the application. It's not hard. Really.
