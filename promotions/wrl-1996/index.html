Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WRL introduced itself on 18 December 1996 with a short founding post: “im starting a fed called the WRL. It is a roleplay fed. To win you must roleplay.” It planned a WRL World Title, defended every three cards, and a WRL US Title defended every card, three wrestlers per person, no tag teams yet, and a weekly schedule of Monday Night Mauling, Thursday Night Trashings and Saturday Night Rage, plus one pay-per-view a month.

Eight days later, a press-conference promo for its own first-ever card introduced a German-nationalist heel gimmick, “3rd Reich Alexis Windhelm,” billed against a face opponent, Bishop, promising “total extinction.”

A community post the following March, from one of its own co-owners, says their partnership ended when his co-owner quit without notice — the clearest account on record, thin as it is, of why the promotion stopped. How that first card against Alexis Windhelm played out, or anything else about the promotion's own run, isn't in the surviving record.
