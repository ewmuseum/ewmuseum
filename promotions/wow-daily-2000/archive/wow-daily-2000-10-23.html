WOW Daily: October 23rd, 2000

23 October 2000

This is for the World of Wrestling, the new Interfed Frontier. The WOW can be found at [detail withheld] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Hello wrestling fans and the WOW is really really starting to heat up with tonight's UWF's Monday Night Maul. The show was incredible and looks to be the standard that we are going to have to live by in the WOW. We've got lots of news today so let's get going.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- UWF Monday Night Maul Recap ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- For the full results, go to http://www.geocities.com/uncensoredwf/maulresults.html this is only a recap and it will be treated in that fashion. The evening opened up with the beginning of the World Title Tournament, Sean Simmons taking on Steve Austin and like expected, the Stone Cold one defeated Sean Simmons with the SCS
(Stone Cold Stunner). From there, Tazz came out, bashed Miami and got ready to take on the Miami hometown hero, the Rock. And boy was Tazz ready for the
Rock as he upset the Rock by Tazzplexing him over the top rope and Rock suffering temporary paralysis by landing on his neck. This angered the Miami crowd but does the black hearted Tazz care? No!! Tazz then assaulted the
Rock while he was on the stretcher, angering the crowd even more! Go Tazz
Go!! Austin made Tazz pay though by coming out and attacking Tazz and pleasing the crowd with a SCS!! Then, two of the underdogs, Ice and X entered the ring to fight each other with Ice winning the match with the
Iceplex!! It was then announced that Raven, tha Punisher and Sting had all no showed so the 4th and 5th matches were scrapped and Suicidial Maniac therefore got a bye. We are into the semi finals now and Tazz took on Austin and upset him by Submission!! Ice and Suicidial Maniac locked up with Ice beating SM to set the stage for the finals! The finals took place and everyone was looking for Tazz to pull the win but Ice managed to roll up
Tazz and get the 3 count with Ice becoming the first ever Uncensored World
Champion!! Well, nobody expected Ice to win and maybe they should of but
Tazz was definitely furious about this loss and he beat up Ice after the match. That concludes the first UWF Monday Night Maul and it was quite a great card!
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- UWF Thursday Night Thrasher ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- With the first card finished, the UWF has unveiled it's second card and it's looking quite good as well and maybe could top the first card cause hey, can anything really top a tournament? Maybe, we'll have to see! This Thursday, from Phoenix, Arizona, the UWF stars are out to shine in a 6 match lineup.
here is the lineup. Hardcore Badasses vs No Bullshit in a standard tag team match. This match has alot of potential to be a great match and the first ever UWF Tag team match. I'm picking No BS to win!
New Jack vs Cid Smack in a hardcore match. Both newcomers to the UWF but New
Jack in a hardcore match means that it's going to be great!! I'm picking New
Jack to win!
Ace vs Mr. Ass in a standard match. Both newcomers, another great match. Go
Mr. Ass! Thunder, Nightmare and Will Starr will go at in a triple threat match. Another match with great potential and I think Willy Starr is going to be able to pull the victory off here.
The next battle has two of Tazz's victims from last week and it's the Rock vs Stone Cold Steve Austin. Another great match but I think Rock is going down again, this time to the hands of Austin.
The main event of the evening is going to be a classic, actually, it's going to be a rematch from Monday night and the rematch of the Tournament finals as Tazz takes on Ice for the World title. Ice beat Tazz once, he may have been lucky but can he do it again? I don't think so! Go Tazz Go!!
All in all, this should be another tremendous card produced by the UWF, we'll have to see though.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Sting released from UWF ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Yes, it is true. The UWF was not very pleased with Sting's no show and they released him following the card. No word has been given on Raven and tha
Punisher but rumor has it that they could be headed out the door very shortly as well because of their no shows.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Impact Players speak ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The following announcement has been made by Justin Credible of the Impact
Players. "Listen all you cherry punk asses, this is Justin Credible from the
Impact Players daring all of you punks to try and test your stuff in the most EXTREME enviroment, ECW!! If you've got what it takes, we're looking for some competition so head on over and don't leave your chair behind!"
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- UWF bounces back by signing the Sandman and Balls Mahoney ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The UWF inked another two deals today by signing both the Sandman and Balls
Mahoney for two month deals. Things are really looking good for the UWF right now while ECW and WWF are staying low with the wrestlers, WWF hasn't even signed a contract yet but people expect things to get better soon.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Thats it for now, talk to you all later! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
