WOW Daily: October 22nd, 2000

22 October 2000

This is for the World of Wrestling, the new Interfed Frontier. The WOW can be found at [detail withheld] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Welcome back wrestling fans and what a great day today was at WOW Headquarters with many new happenings so let's get right into it! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ECW gets a Promoter: ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Yes, finally, ECW now has some ownership!! Justin Taylor and Alan Stephens accepted the duties of ECW Promoter's today and they have claimed that they have big plans for ECW. Well, let's hope they do and the WOW is getting closer and closer to getting started. The Url for the ECW is
http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling2/ECfnW so go check them out. So far, Alan and Justin have signed the tag team of the Impact Players, Justin Credible and Lance Storm. They have also announced that they will only have one card a week and that will be their ECW Hardcore TV. Things are looking up.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- WWF also finds a promoter: ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- WWF announced today that Edward Nesbit will be running the WWF shows. Nothing was really commentated today but once again, things are looking up! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Signings: ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- A couple contracts were signed today. "Bad Ass" Billy Gunn signed a 2 month contract with the WCW. Ace, Edward Nesbit, New Jack, Bobby Coley, Will "The
Thrill" Starr all sign with the UWF.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Possible Tag Teams forming: ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- The word in the WCW locker room is that there are numerous tag teams forming. Buff Bagwell and Scott Steiner are a rumored tag team and also,
"Road Dogg" Jesse Jammes and Billy Gunn have also been rumored to be teaming up again. Nothing has been confirmed yet but these could be two great teams reforming!
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- UWF Monday Night Maul Predictions: ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Alright, now, I'm going to predict the winners for tomorrow night's first
Monday Night Maul and the entire World Title Tournament.
First Round: Steve Austin over Sean Simmons Tazz over the Rock Ice over the X Sting over the Suicidial Maniac Raven over tha Punisher Second Round: Tazz over Steve Austin Ice over Sting Raven gets a bye Third Round: Tazz gets a bye Raven over Ice Finals: Tazz over Raven Those are my predictions and I'm sticking to them. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- That's it for the news today so I'll see you all tomorrow. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
