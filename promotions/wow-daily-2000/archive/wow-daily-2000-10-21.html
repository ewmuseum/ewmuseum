WOW Daily: October 21st, 2000

21 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is for the World of Wrestling, the new Interfed Frontier. The WOW can be found at [detail withheld] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Hello everyone, Buzz Stone here, one of the many WOW Reporters and this will be the first of many WOW Daily's to come. I hope to do this segment every day giving you all the news going around the WOW. So let's begin.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- WCW Signs Numerous Wrestlers: ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Today, WCW signed 4 more wrestlers, upping it's total to 8 wrestlers. Today's new signings, all for 2 month contracts, were Rikishi, Kurt Angle, Buff Bagwell and "Road Dogg" Jesse Jammes. All 4 wrestlers look like they can really help WCW take off. UWF picked up no wrestlers today.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Still no WWF and ECW Promoters: ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Hmm, this is odd. We still haven't found anybody willing to run these two organizations and we also haven't been finding many wrestlers. Many people are staking this all towards the fact that the webpage isn't completely done but don't worry, once [name withheld] gets Frontpage 2000 (Probably Monday), the webpage will be finished and the WCW page will be launched.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- UWF Opening Card this Monday: ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- UWF has their first huge card planned and it will be this Monday from Pro Player Stadium in Miami, Florida. There is a 10 man tournament for the UWF World Title and this will all happen on the very night so at the end of the night, we should see a new World Champion. The Ten Participants are Sean
Simmons, "Stone Cold" Steve Austin, The Rock, Tazz, Ice, The X, Sting, Raven, Tha Punisher, and the Suicidial Maniac. All 10 men are destined to win that World Title and this should be an incredible first Monday Night
Maul and a great way for the WOW to get going. For the full Maul Preview, go to http://www.geocities.com/uncensoredwf/maulcard.html . ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- More Tourney Action: ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- WCW also released their first lineup today and their first WCW Nitro will take place on October 30th, 2000. They will have their 8 man World Title
Tournament featuring Buff Bagwell, Scott Steiner, Triple H, "Road Dogg" Jesse James, Tommy Nightmare, Lost Wolf, Kurt Angle and Rikishi. All 8 men look great and this should also be a great night of tournament action. Also,
WCW will also unveil it's new President. Nobody is sure who it is yet but people are pointing their fingers at either Russo or Bischoff.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- That's all for tonight, good night fans. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----
