CPW Saturday Night Blockbuster (26 June 1999)

Saturday Night Blockbuster 6.26.99 [The scene opens up showing two very large buildings. Below them two MAC trucks colide and catch on fire causing explosions and acidents everwhere. The two large buildings crumble below themselves and dust settles everywhere. Out from the clouds of smoke comes the word
"BLOCKBUSTER" with the words "SATURDAY NIGHT" following behind it, forming above it.]
[We are now taken into a sold out Alamo Dome where millions of screaming fans await. The camera pans around the arena as a "Saturday Night
Blockbuster" logo apears in the left hand corner of the screen. There are thousands of signs everywhere. Some of the more visable ones read
"Let's Go Bret" "CPW 4 Ever" and "Why Wade Why?"]
[The scene now cuts to the announce position where "JD" Jack Davis and
"Rowdy" Ronny Smith are standing with mics in hand.]
RS: Hello everyone and welcome to another great edition of Saturday
Night Blockbuster! I'm "Rowdy" Ronny Smith and with me as always is "JD"
Jack Davis! [Jack nods his head.] RS: We are here live in the Alamo Dome in San Antonio Texas! The home of the World Champion San Antonio Spurs! This could very well be the biggest Blockbuster ever in the history of CPW!
JD: I don't like you but I couldn't agree with you more! RS: Tonight we will se... [All of a sudden "Stitches" by Orgy hits the PA system as President Uss,
Wade Wilson, Emulator, and Amanda MacCloud strut down to the ring. As they all enter Uss grabs the mic.]
Uss: "I told all of you that I would be adding people to my wonderful team of the greatest wrestlers ever. Tonight I unveil my latest addition.
Here he is The Masked Marauder." [Cold Hard Authority laughs as a tall well-built masked man comes to the ring. It is the same mask that Wade Wilson wore just one week ago. He gets into the ring and stands next to Uss.]
Uss: "(haha) Ok that's not who you are so lets take this off." [Before Uss can grab the mask a voice tell him to stop. Everyone turns and sees Nygen in the entryway.]
Nygen: "President Uss, You and I need to talk. You have turned your back on the fans and you have screwed over your best people. Now I'm going to give you tonight this chance to redeem yourself. To me and to all these fans. You have Emulator put that belt on the ground. You all walk up here and apologize to every fan that you have ever meet for being the
@sses that you've been, Stop your fall of Destruction, and I'll let all of you walk out of here tonight."
Uss: "(Laughing) Oh you will little man. You and what army?" [Everyone in the ring begins to laugh out loud. Nygen just raises his hands and except for four spotlights around him, the arena goes black.
After a couple of seconds four separate pyros go off in different parts of the arena. Nygen drops his hands, and the lights come up. The crowd erupts as they now see that behind Wade Wilson is Duncan MacCloud, behind the "Masked Marauder" is Storm, behind Emulator is Bret Masters, and behind Amanda is Tygress. They are all wearing black clothes and shades. Uss and the others don't see them and start to wonder what is going on. Uss points to Nygen as in an almost Eire unity the group grabs the person in front of them and spins them around. Wilson is hit with the Quickening; Storm throws the "Masker Marauder" up for Thunderstruck.
Emulator is meet with a hard DDT, and Amanda is almost decapitated by
Tygress's Tiger Swipe. Uss looks and quickly exits the ring. He back pedals to about mid walk way and stops. The four slowly point to the back video wall as the lights go down.]
[One the video waal, the Scene is a small mountain area. The camera is near the base of the mountain. A small grove of trees and a house are there at the base. Out of the low grass comes a large tiger. It stops center screen and roars. After the echo stops it transforms into
Tygress. To the left a tornado comes out of the sky. It strikes in the grove of trees and begins tearing the trees in all directions. As it clears the trees the tornado breaks up as Duncan MacCloud walks out. He stops behind Tygress. A huge lighting strike hits the small house. It bursts into flames and quickly burns to the ground. Out of the flames walks Bret Masters. He stops next to Duncan. The whole scene shakes from the thunder of the lighting. Behind all of them, as if coming out of the sound itself, Storm appears. Black clouds appear behind all of them.
Eyes can be seen in the clouds, Nygen's eyes. "A great insult was occurred. An insult to all those that have come before. As the cries of the many are heard, the warriors call for their champions. With them they send there blessing and there code." The camera goes to a close up on Storm's face.]
Storm: "Tradition" [Next Duncan has a close up.] Duncan: "Honor" Tygress: "Courage" Masters: "Heart" "This is their battle cry. This is their purpose. This is the Dark
Knights of Victory." Duncan: "Here to save the CPW." Masters: "From itself." [The clouds roll over the whole group blacking the screen. In a blood red the words "The Dark Knights of Victory" are left.
As the screen goes black the Dark Knights of Victory are gone and Uss is extremely upset.] RS: WHAT A WAY TO START BLOCKBUSTER people. A new powerful force has just made themselves felt. Wow what a way to kick this off.
JD: What the hell was that Ronny? RS: I think Cold Hard Authority just got a taste of their own medicine at the hands of "The Dark Knights of Victory"!
JD: Yeah well you can bet your bottom dollar that Wade Wilson and
Emulator are going to punish Bret Masters and Duncan MacCloud TWICE as hard now!
RS: Don't forget that those four men will be locked inside a solid steel cage, there is no getting out and nobody is getting in!
JD: Don't worry, CHA doesn't need any help. RS: Well we will see about that but we are going to take our first break then we will be back for our opening bout on Blockbuster!
[Commercial] RS: And we are ready for our opening bout! Don: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the opening contest on Saturday Night
Blockbuster! [A huge explosion is heard as Nuker makes his way down the aisle followed by Exterminator. He is wearing a black mask, and a black shirt with yellow and black nuclear danger sign on. He has black leather trousers with Nuker written down the sides.]
Don: Introducing first, he weighs in at 323 pounds and comes to us from
"The Future"! He represents the Legion of the Damned, he is NUUUUKER!!!
[Thunderstruck blares thorough out the arena. Chaos comes out on to the entrance way. The crowd doesn't know how to react to Chaos alone.]
Don: On his way to the ring at this time, weighing in at 212 pounds from
Tampa Florida CHAOS!!
