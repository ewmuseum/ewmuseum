Card line-ups CHAMPIONSHIP PRO WRESTLING http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Gym/8445 ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*LETTER FROM THE PRESIDENT ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* CPW is now back on track! This monday marks the one month anniversary of Championship Pro Wrestling! I am planning some big things for this event! To see the run down of the things, please read the news. And soon, CPW should be getting lots more talent to fill our roster! That's all for today! ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*NEWS & RUMORS ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* - Monday, Febuary 1st marks the one month anniversary of Championship Pro Wrestling. President Jonathan Uss has some big things planned for this day! Here is a quick run down! * The CPW website will be "spiced" up a bit with a new logo and new designs to the pages! * All current inactive wrestlers will be terminated to leave room for the new wrestlers that are coming to CPW. * On Madness, there will be a new title announced. This should really heat things up a bit more now with a new title! There is no word yet on what it will be called but rumors say it will be the second most prestigous title, behind the World Heavyweight. * The Roleplay Reviews will also be "spiced" up. Instead of just listing the wrestler's and then the roleplays it will be like a "talk show". It will be hosted by CPW announcer, Ronny Smith. This will add a new twist! - Mammoth and Dmitriy Gurov were suposed to fight each other this Saturday in a cage for the Television Championship. But due to a death in the family of Mammoth, the match has been cancelled. - Speaking of Gurovs, Nikolai Gurov has decided to hang up the boots and just manage his older brother, Dmitriy. - Tommy Hayashi was injured Monday night after Kerre Leisey hit him with the Total E! ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* QUOTE OF THE DAY ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* " And these, yah lard-ass-strawberry-tart-eating-joe-hoe, you dont have" - Jean Lawson commenting on Mammoth's skills ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* INJURIES ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* [Tommy Hayashi] Suffered a broken neck to the hands of Kerre Leisey on Madness.
