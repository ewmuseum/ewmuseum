A federation dedicated to bringing you Quality Cards,Quality Wrestlers and just flat out a
Quality Federation. Hello, My name is [name withheld] but most of you know me as the handler of "Magnificent" Mike Douglas. I have been in the e-wrestling world for awhile and I always wanted to start up my own federation. So here I am, Giving it my best shot. Take a look over the handbook and if you like it fill out an application.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= II. About the SWA™ =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The SWA™ is a booked federation. There is no simulation involved at all. To win you must roleplay. Strats also help out your wrestler a lot. Before a card I will set a deadline for all strats. The SWA is only for original wrestlers. No, Steve Austins or Undertakers here. We at the SWA are trying to bring you the best in e-wrestling today and I will try my hardest to try and bring you that.Currently, I am looking to pick up some writers, So if you are interested in being a part of the SWA™, Give me an e-mail at
[detail withheld]
. As far as the writing style of the SWA goes, We use the ABS system. Meaning that all the action goes into brackets. To give an example, We write matches like GLOW,NAAW and HHWO. I just feel ABS is the best way to go.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= III. Rules & Regulations =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Well, Like every federation, There has to be rules. Here they are: ROLEPLAYING RULES: Here are the rules for roleplaying. There is NO limit on how many roleplays you can do a week. If you want to dish out 5 roleplays a week, I don't care. But remember we are playing by the QUALITYover QUANTITY rule. One good flash is better than three bad ones. Also, To prevent some lack of roleplaying, You MUST roleplay at least ONCE every two weeks. The REAL superstars of the SWA will obviously do more than one every two weeks But I am just making this rule. If I haven't gotten a roleplay from you in two week, I will give you a warning. After that if you continue not to roleplay, You WILL be jobbed. I don't care who you are, Big name or little name. You WILL be jobbed. Also, If you really want to get into the thick of things in the SWA then you have to be consistant. My definition of CONSISTANT is at least ONE
QUALITY RP a week.If you are consistant, You will be rewarded, If you are not then you will suffer the consequences.
If their is a reason why you cannot roleplay like Vacation, School, or anything else just let me know via e-mail. Just tell me that you can't roleplay and I will take care of it. If I don't get notice from you then you will be jobbed.
Strats, In-Rings are ENCOURAGED. I love to see a lot of strats and In-Rings. It just makes the show much better when a lot of things are going on. But remember, In-Rings are NOT counted as roleplays. They are just something EXTRA
. But In-Rings and Strats both REALLY help your wrestler out and it also shows how much participation you are putting in. And, Participation = wins.
CHARACTER RULE: Each and every handler in the SWA can only handle ONE wrestler within the federation.This just makes it a lot more fair and a lot easier for everything else. So if you handle more than one character, Pick which one you would like in the SWA.
OTHER THINGS: Do not expect to be undefeated in the SWA. EVERYONE and I mean
EVERYONE will lose at some point. It may not be clean but EVERYONE will lose.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= IV. SWA Broadcasts =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Here at the SWA, We currently have one weekly card , As well as a flash packet sent out every few days Here they are:
SWA™ MONDAY MAYHEM: The SWA'S biggest card of the week occurs on Monday Night from arena's all over the world. This card will feature 6-8 matches all play by play, Not to mention interveiws with SWA top stars. Titles will be defended, announcements will be made and much more. When Mayhem hits the airwaves you won't want to miss it.
SWA™ TRASH TALK: Every time I get enough flashes, A flash packet will sent out entitled SWA Trash Talk. This will come out a few times a week.
SWA™ SPECIAL SHOWS: Sometimes before a ppv we will have a preveiw show or something like that. Special shows will only occur when I have enough time to do them. But most likely we will have one before every _BIG_ event.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-= IV. SWA Pay Per Veiws =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= No, We don't have monthly Pay Per Veiws. Here at the SWA we have bi-monthly Pay Per Veiws. So that the card will have more build up. Feuds end,
Feuds begin, Appearances are made, as well as just about anything else you can thing of. The names for the PPV's will be made as we go along.

SWA™ Titles =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Here at the Superstar Wrestling Association. We currently have 4 singles titles. Here is a little description for each:
SWA™ WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: The grandaddy of em all. The main prize of the SWA. The most prestigious belt in the SWA. The man who wears this belt has the right to call himself the best in the SWA. Do you have what it takes? Step into the SWA and find out.
SWA™ NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Hey, Second best isn't bad. This belt comes runner up to the world title. It is also a very prestigious belt and the man who carries this is right up there with the best of them.
SWA™ TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: This belt is defended weekly on SWA™ Monday Mayhem against a worthy contender. Can you hang onto this belt for a long time? If you can, you have the right to have this belt wrapped around your waist. And the right to be called champion.
SWA™ CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Think your the best wrestler under 230 lbs.? If you hold this title, You are the best in the SWA.
* There could possibly be a Tag Team division in the SWA. After all the singles titles have been decided I will decide whether I want a tag team division in the SWA. If there is a REALLY big responce it could happen.
