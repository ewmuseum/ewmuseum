Join the SWA! The Superstar Wrestling Alliance is now accepting applications. The SWA existed for years on the Prodigy service and [name withheld] and
[name withheld] are now partnering to bring the SWA to the web for the first time on Shootangle.com. Both of us have years of experience in
Fantasy wrestling and are taking several steps to make the SWA fun and unique. Here is a brief outline of the way we will be running things.
- Cards will be held bi-weekly. We have two shows, SWA Saturday Night and SWA Carnage, which will alternate. Each card will be small (5-7 matches) and the league will be storyline and feud intensive, run in many ways like a weekly television program.
- Roleplay will be taken into account much like a wrestler's workrate. Wins are based on RP, wrestler's overness with the audience (in this case, the bookers and other RPers) and angle/storyline as decided by the RPer and brass. Often it may seem that we're pushing for a feud to go in a certain direction, but good RP can and will cause use to change the direction.
- The roster will be no more than 20 wrestlers, all singles, and we will be having only two titles. These are the SWA World title and the North American Title. In the interest of keeping things focused we will be having no tag team titles and no full-time tag teams (although there will be tag matches.)
If you are interested in joining with a singles wrestler, fill out the following application and return it to
[detail withheld] or [detail withheld] with "SWA" somewhere in the subject line. As we said, the roster will be small and space is limited so apply now for our remaining slots!
