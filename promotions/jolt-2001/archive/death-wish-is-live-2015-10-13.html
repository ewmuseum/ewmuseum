Derecho — 13 October 2015 The final show for jOlt Wrestling is now live. Witness Champion vs Champion as Jeremy Ryan and Omega defend the World and Fearless Titles inside of a steel cage in a winner take all match. Vogue Gonsalvez defends the Starlet Title against Tammy Lynn Foster, The Natural Athletes clash with The New Breed for the Tag Team Titles, and so MUCH more.
Here is the card for jOlt's final event. Career vs Career Alyssa Corliss vs Raevynn For the Rights to The House name The Roebucks vs Derrick Huber & Charlotte Last Man Standing Frank Silver vs Mack Brody Starlet Championship Vogue Gonsalvez(c) vs Tammy Lynn Foster Fearless Rules Alex Reyn vs Darren Best World Tag Team Titles The Natural Athletes(c) vs The New Breed Steel Cage Match - Winner Take All jOlt Championship & Fearless Championship Jeremy Ryan vs Omega You can stream the event here: http://www.joltwrestling.com/joltv/deathwish2015/index.shtml
