J The Ripper — 26 July 2012 Ever wanted to be a part of jOlt? Now's your chance! We're currently seeking Starlets, Tag Teams, and Flyweights. Not sure about jOlt? Here's some information.
Q: Will I be stuck in JUST this division the entire time? A: At first, yes. That's the whole purpose but things are always growing and changing. There will be opportunities along the way to branch out.
Q: Angle or RP? A: Angle. Q: So, I'm new, does that mean I'll always be stuck under the proverbial glass wall? A: No. In fact jOlt doesn't play favorites. The more you work, contribute, and submit things (apart from your writing) the more staff takes notice and will put you in bigger angles.
Q: I write in present tense, I noticed you guys use past tense, I'm willing to try but what if I need help?
A: Everyone in the back is always willing to help. Any questions will always be answered by someone.
Q: I'm a guy but I think I could handle a chick, does that make me "light in the loafers"? A: No...unless you are...and that's cool. Writing is writing. Q: What if I wanted to do a tag team made up of a female and a male? A: Go for it! If you're interested in joining, just fill this out below, and shoot it to the e-mail attached at the bottom of this post!
