Sunday iNtense 41 Back In The Seat Damien Lee sat in his chair for the first time since he was fired by The Don. Damien looked good in his chair and jOlt looked even better with him in it. He looked down at his papers neatly stacked on his table and smiled. He tossed them around his desk and threw a few on the ground. He kicked his feet up and slid his chair back. "It's good to be home." Damien smiled and the camera's faded out of his room. iNtense 41 was underway. Misery Erik sat in a pensive stance. His face was buried within his hands. The low drone of others walking around and talking was in the background, but Erik didn't look up. He felt a hand on his shoulder. He sighed and looked up. A doctor stared down at him, but did nothing but nod. As quietly as he came in, the doctor left. Erik looked over to the bed next to him. Amidst the technology lay a single woman, breathing shallowly. Anna. She wasn't in need of life support, but she was slipping in and out of conciousness even since Erik got her to the hospital. As of right now, she was resting peacefully. Erik placed a hand over hers, and looked into her face. She had been cleaned up, but she was still slightly bruised from the injuries KroW inflicted. But still... she was beautiful to Erik. And he knew that he had to be strong for her. He needed to be there for her... because he couldn't before. As the lights began to dim outside, Erik simply sat, staring into her face.
