Kenjiro Ito Defends In PRIME Time! Posted by Mark Mixx on 03/19/05: As fans of Jolt well know, the stipulations of the Jolt Ambassador Championship allow for the title to be defended outside of Jolt Wrestling. Champion Kenjiro Ito made a historic first defense of the Ambassador Title out of the confines of Jolt on March 16th, taking a red-eye flight from Providence to Anaheim, California, the site of PRIME Wrestling's ReVolution 58 show. Once there, he defended against one of professional wrestling's most decorated female competitors, former fWo United States Champion Amy "Aimz" Campbell. Despite a stiff challenge from the redheaded competitor, Kenjiro Ito caught her attempted hurricanrana, and put away the contest with a vicious Ito's Ice Age. When approached after the contest, Ito had a good deal to say, both about Aimz and his open challenge for the Retort. "I intend to take this title EVERYWHERE," Ito said, "and defeat anyone insolent enough to think that they can take it from me. Aimz gave a valiant effort, but she simply was no match for the Ice Age. No man or woman is. With Aimz vanquished, I look forward to the Retort, where I will face my second non-Jolt challenger." Indeed, internet message boards have lit up with possible suggested challengers for the Osakan Ambassador Champion. Some names include former tSC Evolutionary Champion and current fWo superstar and net reporter Keith Scott Zimmerman, fWo Internet Champion Rana Venenosa, and even former IWO World and WMW Great Lakes Champion AWS Man (Also Known As Bill). When asked why he didn't accept the challenge of Wippit Guud, Ito simply walked away, referring to the reporter as a "(BLEEP)ing moron" and refused to comment.
