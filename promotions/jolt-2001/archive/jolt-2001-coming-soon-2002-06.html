What this is The preview page of jOlt Wrestling’s second season, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 4 June 2002: the card for Sunday iNtense 5, which follows Unwanted.
In this area, you will find the previews of the upcoming iNtense's. This is what we know about next week's iNtense thus far. ---------------------------- Unwanted set a standard for the rest of jOlt's era. Unwanted brought upon a new start into jOlt. Unwanted crowned all the current champions, and set the new tone of jOlt's Second Season. And already, Jason Winters (who was actually missing at Unwanted's broadcast) has set up the card for iNtense #5. A card that will get the wars going, and the matches started. The opening bout places two underrated superstars in the ring. Lawrence Clamentia is coming off a tough loss at the hands of The Magi, and looking to bring back his victorious ways by taking on none other than Miles Blunder. Miles Blunder shocked the world after eliminating numerous amounts of people in the Unwanted Battle Royale...and staying in the rumble longer than any other wrestler...AND becoming part of the final four! Miles has changed greatly, but still seems to have a bit of idiocy inside of him (look how he was eliminated at Unwanted). Will Clamentia be so determined to be a winner again, that he will defeated Blunder? Or will Blunder surprise us once again, and begin actually...dare we say it...WINNING?!? At Unwanted we saw two debuts to the jOlt ranks. One being a wrestler by the name of Calamity. Calamity made an impressive victory, being one of the final six in the battle royale at Unwanted, and giving a good fight all the way through. Calamity makes his singles debut in jOlt wrestling, taking on Mark Pearson in a exhibition bout. After Unwanted, it seems that somes wars just won't end. Their is just too much hate and revenge on the mind of these enemies, that they wish to continue their war all over again. Jason Winters has signed a SIX MAN TAG TEAM CONTEST for iNtense #5. It is signed to have The Elements teaming up with the NEW Revolutionary Champion, Dave Gibson, to take on the team of Deadly Sins and Sam Potright. The hatred between The Elements and Deadly Sins has not disappeared, especially after The Elements took Aubrey of Deadly Sins, and powerbombed her through a table. And the hatred between Potright and Gibson has just started to heat up, after the controversial win for Gibson at Unwanted, Potright is far from done with his war with Gibson. Jason Winters has also signed a REMATCH for the International Championship. A match placing the Current International Champion, Jonathan Conspiracy, against the man he defeated at Unwanted...the self-proclaimed "Best Sports Entertainer of All Time"...Trey Vincent. TV was very angry at his loss to J-Con, and seems more pumped than ever to capture the International Title. And during this match...someone will be in attendance....Wippit Guud! And in the MAIN EVENT, one of the greatest wars may be coming to the very end. The Current World Champion, Erik Rave, has challenge none other than KroW in a World Championship bout. What a main event to start off jOlt after Unwanted. Everyone saw the reaction by KroW after being eliminated at Unwanted...there is no telling what he will do at iNtense #5....and there is no telling what Erik Rave will do himself. Also in attendance will be Brandon Blade, a man who changed drastically since Unwanted. After attacking Rave, he has alot of explaining to do. Also in attendance will be the new jOlt Tag Team Champions Derecho and Blazer. Not only that but Jason Winters has much to talk about...considering the contracts of many jOlt superstars. All this and more, LIVE! on jOlt's Tuesday Intense #5!
