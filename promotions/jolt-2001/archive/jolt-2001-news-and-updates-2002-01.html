::News & Updates:: iNtense 37! Posted on 01/06/02 by The Don Ladies an' gentleman, iNtense has justa been posted... Yer buddy John Roccott is noa longer on da jOlt Wrestling staff so he will noa longer post here on da board. I will finda someone to update all of dis garbage lata' when I have da time. Who knows, we may not have a website very longer... I dont tink I like dem'. New Riegn! Posted on 01/05/02 by The Don Ladies an' gentleman, it'sa histo'ic times here in jOlt wrestlin'. Yes, da' mafia, run by none otha' than myself, will be takin' ova'. Have no fear do', we'sa gonna' make jOlt even betta' than befo'. Now, I got some real business to mention right now. Yes, we gonna' have a year end awa'ds show, but it'sa gonna' be happenin' on Sunday iNtense. Now if you don' like it, I don' care, because, it'sa my jOlt Wrestlin'. Da' secon' piece o' business is'a somethin' you all been askin' a lot about since my easy victory. I got people everywhere askin' me, "Don, what are you going to do with Mr. Lee? Please take it easy on him. He's a very nice man!". I don' care. He ain't a nice man. He fucked wit' me! What kinda' idiot be fuckin' wit' da mafia? Shit, you wanna' know what'sa gonna' happen ta' him? I'll tell you.......... On iNtense... Dat's it'a, fa' now. Until next time, Im'a leave you kids wit' a piece of advice. Don' be sneakin' into my shows ya' hear. Pay fo' a damn ticket, so I don't gotta' whoop ya' ass when I catch, den' leave ya' on ya porch bloodied an' bruised! Punks... jOlt's Cataclysm!
