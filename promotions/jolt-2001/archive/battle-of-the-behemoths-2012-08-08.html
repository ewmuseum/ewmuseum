Battle of the Behemoths!

J The Ripper — 8 August 2012 jOlt Head 2 Head presents: BATTLE OF THE BEHEMOTHS! Kodiak Vic Creed vs. Sylo vs. Grendel vs. KroW vs. Penance vs. Alexander Jerusalem jOlt and LoC Monsters of the Squared circle face off! Who will walk out alive? FIGHT~! (Poll closes in one month) Ernie — 8 August 2012 I've always been a big fan of both Penance and Alexander Jerusalem, but I think Sylo will probably be booked to win.
Russel — 8 August 2012 I can't not vote for KVC. If I'm not mistaken he got his start in jOlt, right? Then his big push from there landed him in CWL which was a short road to the FWO and the evolving and going through all sorts of amazing stories. For longevity, impact and generally bad-assery, is there any other monster better suited for this wrestling world... then Kodiak Vic Creed?
