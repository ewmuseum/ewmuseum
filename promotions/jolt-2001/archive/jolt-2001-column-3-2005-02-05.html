The Mixx Report (02/05/05)

The Mixx Report (02/05/05) Posted by Mike Mixx on 02/05/05: Hello, and welcome to the Mixx Report. I am Mike Mixx, who most of you know as one of the voices of the Jolt Wrestling broadcasts. There are a few of you who might remember my Professional Wrestling career, though I really hope you forget, because I’ve tried to do my best doing so. However, what very few know, is that I am the Director of Talent Relations in Jolt Wrestling. I deal with the Jolt Wrestling superstars on a day to day basis, which is why on the last Tuesday Night Intense broadcast I looked much drained. I wanted to be interested in the show, and watching it on tape Thursday night, I thought it was an excellent show (I’ll get to that later), but I just wasn’t up to it on Tuesday. Ask any wrestler on the roster, every commercial break I was back to talking to wrestlers about how they were feeling and what they planned to do. It was a raucous affair. Tuesday Night Intense was an excellent event for all those involved. I would like to thank New Orleans for being an excellent home for Jolt Wrestling. We have always appreciated the generosity the Louisiana community has shown to us. We first felt, planning the Mardi Gras Introduction event, that there would be a backlash from our fans. However, we seen people proudly wearing t-shirts of the old generations, and excited to wear t-shirts for the present generation. It is nice to have such appreciative fans for Jolt Wrestling. Mark Cuban was very pleased with the first episode of Tuesday Night Intense. “We were taking a huge chance with Jolt Wrestling,” said Cuban, in an interview with TV Guide. “They have a shaky past and putting a fresh product as our first primetime show on Tuesdays is a huge risk. Myself and Todd (Wagner) were worried this could get our network to a bad start if this didn’t work out, but hey, we were proven wrong. Damien (Lee) and associates put out an excellent show and we are proud to have them on Broadcast TV”. The internet was certainly “buzzing” about Jolt Wrestling. There were a lot of positive comments on the show. A bit of “heat” on Xias’ comments about his promotion past, but it is nothing the Jolt Wrestling management worried about. Xias is an interesting situation for Jolt Wrestling. He is currently “optioning himself” to several different Wrestling promotions, and chose Jolt Wrestling as his first “option”. Many believe Xias wants to continue in the footsteps of his “hero” Eron the Relentless, who was a huge part of jOlt Wrestling during our first season, but others question whether Xias wants to “deny” his career comparison to Eron and will join All Star Wrestling. Jolt Wrestling management was not pleased with Xias’ interference in our main event, but was happy at the reaction he gave to the viewers. Whether Xias shows up next week and gives a decision, that’s his prerogative. A huge star like him, in my opinion, should be given the free time to make a choice. American Hero III didn’t like him at all, but American Hero III has an old school mentality. Brandon Youngblood’s neck was tight after the work it took against Kenjiro Ito in his wrestling debut for Jolt Wrestling. Management was disappointed at Youngblood’s performance, due to his long list of credentials, but were much more impressed by Kenjiro Ito. He has the potential to be a huge superstar in Jolt Wrestling, and the audience has already taken big attention to him. He’ll surely be ready to continue his impact in Detroit next week. Jolt Wrestling management has been worried that Back Alley Brawler’s recent “gambling” in the locker room could lead to illegal activity. While no plans have been made to shut down the project, they will be taking a very close eye to how things develop. Jolt Wrestling management was very pleased with Persephone’s interview on Tuesday Night Intense. Expect more interviews from classic jOlt Wrestling superstars, followed by a classic match from our archives. We have been currently talking to erØn, LLB, Rush, The Don, Reno Davis and more to give interviews. Currently, none of these superstars have gone into talks about contracts. I’m sure the old school fans would love to see any of them back in the Jolt Squared Circle. A lot of “Predictions” have been made on the Internet about the Triple Crown Tournament. While I will not give the match ups for “Block B”, and let everyone wait for next week’s show to find out, I will confirm that The Rock is not entered into the Tournament and will not be making any guest appearances. The same goes to other rumoured names in erØn, Brandon Blade, Villam Ender, Mike Randalls, Mike Bear and Brock Lesnar. Sorry folks. Coburn P. Valencia seems to be enjoying North America and Jolt Wrestling. He told me his only problem was with Josh Boodreau, who seemed to be following him everywhere, trying to convince him he was British. Boodreau has been a bit of a nuisance on the roster, but his qualities as an interviewer are unquestionable. Kelly Andrews is without question a sight for sore eyes, but has the talent to back it up. The company wants to one day position her as Damien Lee’s replacement for announcing. She is well liked with the company except the wrestlers (and Josh) who have tried to flirt with her. Very professional, she wants none of it. Seems Kelly was smart enough to check up her history and see how Samantha Bevins was treated. Dave Meltzer can kiss my ass. Even I could out wrestle Ric Flair on the mat. If he has a problem, I’ll be glad to go out of retirement to teach him a lesson with the Spicy Mixx Leglock. Dave Meltzer I mean, not Ric Flair. Tickets for Wrestlesault have been selling very well in Montreal, PQ. Jolt’s last appearance in “Mount Royal” was for Rise of the Legends. Do not expect any screwjobs, guys. The “Total Impact!” tour has been selling very well. Jolt Wrestling.com will be adding more tour dates soon. Expect some special guest appearances down the road, including Persephone in Plymouth, Rush in Windsor, and erØn in Chatham. The announcement of erØn was one of the big reasons for the sell out in Chatham, with the “Chathamites” being very vocal for their hatred of erØn. I just think they need something to do. Zing! Injury Report: DDR was sore after the post match attack by Lusus at the Baton Rouge show, but I spoke to her this morning and she felt good. Chris Mezz sprained his ankle after missing a thrust kick and having his leg caught in the ropes in Baton Rouge. He still feels pressure when walking on it, but said he will be fine in time for Block A of the Triple Crown Tournament. What a trooper! And that is all for the first edition of the Mixx Report. Expect these every Friday or Saturday, when I have some time to relax. Thanks folks! Mike Mixx, Esq.
