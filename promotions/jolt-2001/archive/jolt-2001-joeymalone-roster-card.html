Bio Joey Malone. In a world filled with depressive dark guys, easilly angered tough guys, steroided freaks, retarded people who yell 'I'll kick your ass!' a lot, and guys named 'Joone Carrule' asking you to join his fed, only to say he's just kidding, Joey Malone is the odd man out. A proud native of Arizona and a second-generation wrestling star, Joey Malone never really originally found it in his heart to be a wrestler. He eventually found it by chance and trained under wrestling veteran(and current owner of a restaurant) 'Nitro Beaver' Kyle Morris. From there, Joey Malone and then-partner Daniel Phillips fought into the IWO, but soon afterwards, Phillips was out of the picture, and Joey Malone scratched and clawed his way before he bested Matt Rivers in a difficult and brutal Life, Death, and Endurance iron man match and won the IWO World Title. Everything just sort of fell apart from that one truly great moment for Joey. He lost the title to Kent Anthason two months later and a combination of a tailbone injury and high medical bills caused the IWO to stop compensating for Joey's injuries, and Joey left the wrestling in September. Or so he had hoped. Joey's manipulative sister, the medical bills, and a coffee shop took its toll on Joey's finances, and a month after his retirement from the wrestling world, Joey Malone found himself nearly broke and fearing that he might not be able to care for a family he's about to start with his fiancèe, Keri Lindum. Thus, he is forced back into the business he once loved, but now dislikes. No longer fighting for titles, Joey Malone is here to do one thing, and that's fight for the future of his family.
