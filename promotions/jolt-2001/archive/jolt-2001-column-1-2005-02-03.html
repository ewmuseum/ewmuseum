The Powell Tuesday Intense Stream of Bad Attitude Mixx, Really Obvious Mystery Men, Random Matches, and Stuttering Heroes Posted by Scott Powell on 02/03/05: The Powell Tuesday Intense Stream of Bad Attitude Mixx, Really Obvious Mystery Men, Random Matches, and Stuttering Heroes Welcome to the first-ever "Powell Stream of...." for Tuesday Intense. Probably because this is the first-ever Tuesday Intense. Yeah, that makes sense. So we begin with highlights from the Mardi Gras show. No, I'm not doing a stream for that. Why not? Cause I said so, bucko. Mezz is OUTRAGED~! with that dastardly JCON and challenges him to a match for the first Tuesday Intense. Hey, that's right now! Damien Lee and Mike Mixx introduce the show for us. We have a bunch of random matches, plus a MAIN EVENT GRUDGE MATCH~!, and the announcement of the A Block of the Triple Crown Title Tournament. Lucky us. Mixx is giving us an attitude.....BUT WAIT~! It's the SUPER-MYSTERIOUS MYSTERY MAN! He's followed us from Mardi Gras with his Japanese music. Who in the American wrestling world uses Japanese music? I'd say it's a fairly short list. Hint: there's only one guy on it. But I'll play along for now. Who could it possibly be?!? I have no idea! Hey, look, it's Survivor 4 loser Shawn Arrows! And he's got some Mexican with him! Joy for us! The match between these two tag teams is actually a really good 20-minute opener that sees Lopez get the pin on Ramon to give the Unknown Elements the win. Great opener. JCON comes out next to announce the winner of his contest for the jOlt International Title (because he was the last champion, see?). It's some local guy, but since he's Cajun JCON won't give it to him. OHSNAP. Good way to get some cheap heat, Mr. One Letter Better. Meanwhile, Mittens the Cat tries to get the Back Alley Brawler to agree to be his ninja partner. Yes, that's exactly what happened! Really! I'm not making it up! 'the Original' Jeff Garvin and Brandon Youngblood argue mostly about another promotion. Yawn. Don't care about PRIME, guys. But Garvin does threaten to break Youngblood's neck, which sounds like fun to me. Basic takes on King Cobra next in a battle of puroresu-influenced wrestlers. I've seen enough New Japan young lions' matches to know they're usually boring as hell, but luckily this isn't. Basic wins in a good 10-minute match with the Amethyst Driver, which definitley beats out a Boston Crab as a finisher. Back Alley Brawler is still taking bets, so Kenjiro Ito shows up to put 5K on himself over Brandon Youngblood. Brawler is less than convinced on Ito's chances, but Ito's watched a lot of after-school specials on BELIEVING IN YOURSELF~!!!, so he doesn't mine giving up the bling. We get an interview with one of the wrestlers from the first era of Jolt, Persephone. Not much to note here, but it does lead into a fun 4-way tag from September. God I miss Team VIAGRA. Polar squashes a jobber by the name of Back Draft, which is just a set-up for Carson Nash to challenge him to a match for next week's show. Sounds good to me. Dawson James comes out to basically blab about how great he is. He calls out an interviewer and gets the chick, who he proceeds to hit on. Wow, he's good at being an annoying prick. Anyway, he makes fun of the other zTw alumni in Jolt, Geo Vacton, which draws Geo out for a quick impromptu brawl that Dawson ends up on the wrong side of. Dawson promises REVENGE~! Meanwhile, an old British guy complains about not being booked. Well, okay then. Dawson James comes back out to bitch about what just happened to him, uh, two seconds ago, but gets interrupted by the AMAZING, COMPLETELY UNKNOWN MYSTERY MAN~! again. Still no sign of him, though. Dawson James beats, uh, himself.....actually some idiot he paid to dress up as himself. Lovely. Meanwhile, Ninja K arrives. Yup. Ken Kaze arrives and makes an idiot of himself in front of Geo and the BAB. Wow, the BAB's all over this show tonight. But anyone who claims to be the "Turquoise Ranger" is cool with me. Polar thinks he'll beat Carson Nash next week. I'm shocked he has that opinion. Kenjiro Ito faces Brandon Youngblood next.....and absolutley destroys him. I mean, he MURDERS him like no one's ever been murdered before! Wow. That was quite the debut match for Ito.....but what the hell's up with Youngblood?! Could have sworn he was gonna be a player in Jolt. Oh well. Ito then goes off to collect his mucho winnings. Well, when you're right..... Elsewhere, Carson Nash explains that he doesn't hate the fans anymore. Well, not THAT much anyway. American Hero III makes his grand appearence. No one outside of New Orelans cares. Although, his constant gasping for breath is pretty funny. He announces the following Block A matches of the Triple Crown Tournament for next week: 'Impact' Rowdy Burns vs. Lusus, Chris Mezz vs. Phil Atken, Kenjiro Ito vs. Geo Vacton, and Jeff Gravin vs. Coburn P. Valencia. Not bad, all in all. Finally, the Main Event pitted JCON vs. Mezz in a match, umm, I'm sure a few people were waiting for. But the match ended up being nothing more than a set-up to the SHOCKING MYSTERY MAN being revealed. I'm sure you'll never believe this, but......IT WAS XIAS ALL ALONG! GASPEH! Xias takes out both men with some help from a chair and cuts a nice promo that basically says "Hi, I'm here, kind of, now bye." Intriguing way to end the first Tuesday Intense. So that's all for now. We had some great matches, some good matches, some squashes, and a memorable ending. Can't really ask for more from a first show. See you back next week for Tuesday Intense 2!
