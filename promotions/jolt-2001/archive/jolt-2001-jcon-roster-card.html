Roster Page | 'J-Con' Jonathon Conspiracy 's Biography | 'J-Con' Jonathon Conspiracy 's Transcripts 'J-Con' Jonathon Conspiracy Real Name: Jonathan Leary Birthdate: May 25th 1980 Hometown: West Hampton, NY via Brooklyn, NY Height: 6'0' Weight: 208 lbs. Nicknames: JCON, The Attention Getter, One Letter Better, True One And Only (from days as John 'Inferno' Blaze) Status: Heel Manager(s): Current Teams: JCON's Team (Membership: 1) Entrance Music: Guilty Until Proven Innocent - Jay Z feat. R. Kelly Wrestling Style: High Flying Technician Signature Moves: The Attention Getter (Canadian Crossface) Set Up Move: Co-Conspirator (Reverse Falling DDT ) Finisher: Conspiracy Theory (Guillotine Legdrop) jOlt Title History: none...yet jOlt Award History: Winner of Jason Rau's Austrialian Open Invitational Other Federations: PIW Other Federation Awards: Bio I'm not a man that you can get to know. I'm 'One Letter Better' than you all. If you have a problem with me, then you definantly have a problem with knowing that I'm better than you! Check my resume and you'll see that Jonathan Conspiracy isn't someone here to be a 'jobber'. Jonathan Conspiracy is here for that j0lt World Title. But until Damien Lee notices the superior talent I have, I'll have to settle for making j0lt a place no one else wants to venture into. Jonathan Conspiracy has decided to make j0lt his home, and if anyone dares kick me out of that home, 'I will get their attention!' For starters, I only came to j0lt after The Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling closed. j0lt was just a place for me to continue making ends meet, a way for me to continue to live my lifestyle, one that all of you want to live. You all want to sleep in my bed, you know the one in West Hampton, New York. You all want to drive my car, you know the 2002 silver Chevy Impala. You all want to wear my clothes, you know Sean John, Roc-A-Wear, FUBU. You all want to be me, well you can't be me, because someone else is already me. Who might that be? Well... ME!!! May 25th 1980- Jonathan Jerome Leary came into the world. I was almost aborted, but the doctor told my mom that it was too late. Now aske me how I felt when I found that shit out. 1980-1994- I lived my life in Brooklyn, New York the way I wanted to live it. There was only one problem, I was forced out of New York City and moved to a suburban town in New Jersey on September 26th 1994. September 26th 1994- I'm here in New Jersey, and I must say that I'm rather pissed off about these turn of events. I guess I'll have to deal with it, NOT! I'm outta Jersey the first chance I get! 1994-1998- I enroll in Sayreville War Memorial high School in new Jersey and graduate by skating through four years of hell. I mean really, Jersey really blows! June 21st 1998- Graduation day, I finally get my release from New Jersey, but my mom wants me to go to college at the State University. She wants me to go to Rutgers University. But throughout my time in New Jersey, I had one thing on my mind... wrestling! July 14th 1998- Ten days after America's Indepence Day, this is the day I left New Jersey behind and entered... Hell's Kitchen Wrestling School, ran by legendary wrestler, 'The Flame' Robbie Flamingo. 1998-1999- A year of training and a lot of scouts from various wrestling federations came to the school to check out new talent, and I was at the top of their list. Unfortunately none of the big ballers of the time came calling, no fans Wrestling organization, and no Tornado Wrestling to call home, so in October of 1999, I decided to take matters into my own hand, and go to the next federation that came calling... Ultimate Professional Wrestling Federation! Jonathan Conspiracy, no he didn't start out like this. He wasn't a big name in wrestling when his career first jumped off... Jonathan Conspiracy was 'Mr. Conspiracy' Jonathan Leary... But it didn't start there either, he was known as John 'Inferno' Blaze... But before that he was simply 'Inferno'... That's right! I started out as part of the Ultimate Professional Wrestling Federation. Thinking that all I learned in Hell's Kitchen would be put to good use. But it wasn't, I was put on the backburner in favor of the friends that ran the place. This led to me getting made fun of, and forced into losing matches. I would lose one match with the stip that I had to dress up as a hillybilly and go by the name of 'Billy Bob'. With nowhere else to turn, I was locked into a five year contract, I couldn't take anymore and begin to wrestle stiff. This would lead to me getting fined, and suspended on various occassions. However, my actions did garner me the Cruiserweight Title, a title that I still have til this day, after the federation closed its doors in November 1999, and officially shut down operation in January of 2000. Thankfully the place did close, because upon closing in November, I left UPWF's BeatDown 99 after having to stupidly join the 'Hot Boyz' stable. I was on my way to my first Championship Wrestling Federation match, a number one contenders Four Corners Elimination match for a shot at the Cruiserweight Title. Needless to say I was the first person eliminated but would wind up wrestling the Cruiserweight Champion, Erik Darkstorm, the following show, NON-TITLE ofcourse! I would go on to defeat Darkstorm in the match, and continued my success against champions, when I defeated Kid Knight, the then-TV Champion, in a street fight. That match was also known title. I promised myself that if I ever captured CWF gold that I wouldn't be afraid to put it up for grabs, cause I'm a gambling man. So there I was finally with a shot at gold. The CWF Cruiserweight Title. Triangle Elimination Match, involving myself, former champion, Erik Darkstorm, and current champion, Ike Arumba. I would win this match, but my 'fighting champion' motive was quickly derailed as I dropped the strap to Kid Knight in our third and final street fight. A bond would form between myself and Knight, but would get ruined when Knight, at the time known as Kid Karnage, he blew up over not getting a World Title push in a federation that will remain nameless. Needless to say alot of federation jumping occured for me, until I landed in another Championship Wrestling Federation, where I feuded with the likes of Moondog and Doobage. I would go by John 'Inferno' Blaze, and truly shined, before getting put out of action. I recently attempted a return to the federation, but my ties to j0lt and other prior commitments prevented me from running the angle to completion. Then came the Global Wrestling Association, a place where I landed after the closing of the fed that was marred in 'conspiracy' over the Kid Karnage non-World Title push incident. Ironically enough Kid Karnage was being pushed to the World Title here, and I didn't like the fact that the crybaby was being rewarded, so I set out to get into the fed, but John 'Inferno' Blaze letters went unanswered...but one letter asking to audition for the federation from 'Mr. Conspiracy' Jonathan Leary led to me making my GWA debut. I would quickly become the Cruiserweight Champion, and attacking Kid Karnage at every turn, before getting injuried, well faking an injury to wrestle for the big time, The Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling. During my first stint in PIW, I tried too hard to get noticed by the Television Title contenders, and everything I did was to gain their attention, and get a shot at them and that lovely title. I would start out by attacking the lame duck tag team champions, The Dynamic Duo, Iceman and Blast From The Past. I'd beat them up take, their titles, and on the next show; defeat them in a handicap match, they were the one's handicapped; and become a Tag Team Champion, WITHOUT A PARTNER! I'd soon fight stiffer competition and drop the straps to Sepirtoh Du Luc and TPS. PIW would then close down for some time, leading me back to the Global Wrestling Association. Taking off the mask I wore as 'Mr. Conspiracy', I revealed myself to the champ, Kid Karnage, after another attack. I would then never receive a World Title shot, but fought in two of the top five matches in GWA History. A Fatal Fourway TLC Match for the GWA Cruiserweight Title, and a 'One Last Time' match against Kid Karnage for the GWA World Title Number One Contendership, atleast that's what it was supposed to be for, but the fed shut its doors right before the big event, and me and Karnage treated the fans to a spectacular last match. But not my last match, PIW RETURNED! When I returned to PIW, I was looked at as a star in the making, and got booked against 'The Poisoned Soul' Stephen Blackeheart, and the feud revolved around the Television Title, a title I craved since wrestling that dark match oh so long ago. Needless to say, I unveiled 'The Attention Getter' and got TPS to tap out during our submission match and captured the Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling Television Title, a stepping stone to the PIW World Title. I would continue to dominate the competition until I got screwed by the duo of SDL and TPS, and lost my title. Frustrated I wrestled just about every champion in PIW. I was on a quest for gold, but I never seemed to have it that night, or my opponent beat me fair and square; okay so maybe I didn't have it that night. These latest happens led me to Jim Johnson's office, and I demanded that I get a shot at the United States Title; which ofcourse I won, but that's besides the point. Had I lost, I would never receive another title shot. I would then go on to defeat Joey Starr, and once again all was right with the world. But all that's right with the world, quickly became forgotten when Griff Truxell arrived and burned every title, even my United States Title. So you know that didn't sit well with me. I'd begin to wage a war with Griff Truxell that was sure not to end. But then Griff defeated me for the US Title, and I wasn't too happy about it. However, what got me more pissed was Truxell choking me out, sending me to the hospital and putting me out of action for awhile. I never got a chance to return to a PIW ring, as the company went under, and I wasn't cleared by doctors in time for the last card in PIW history. This would prompt the Damien Lee's and Jason Winter's of the world to come calling. Jonathan would participate in the Jason Rau Invitational and would win his tryout match with j0lt Wrestling. He was finally there, a place to call home after the demise of PIW. But something was missing, because his archrival, Griff Truxell had left j0lt for PIW and never returned to j0lt for reasons unexplained. But then JCON heard the news, 'I know where Griff Truxell is!' Jonathan then met face to face with Jason Winters and negotiated Jonathan a contract with IOW! Jonathan would handle his business with Truxell, and move on to bigger and better things within the IOW, one being Jason 'Shocker' Elliott, and the IOW Cruiserweight Title. After multiple run ins with The Tein Faction, Jonathan secured himself a match against Elliott, and pulled off what many described as an upset, and holds the IOW Cruiserweight Champion til this day. A couple of programs were wasted due to IOW having money problems. They weren't able to afford the contract of former fWo wrestler Insane Wonder,and after a temporary layoff, they signed James Bradley III to a deal, but he came aboard at a time where IOW was bad off, but he will get a shot at Conspiracy's Cruiserweight Title at the end of era one in IOW history. Which brings me back to j0lt, the only federation I'm ACTIVELY wrestling for, we have a little idiot running around by the name of Wippit that thinks he can stop me, he wants me to be less serious, but how can I not be serious when I'm beating the smile off his face. But what happens if j0lt comes to an end, like so many feds before it, like the UPWF, the CWF, PIW and IOW! Where will I enjoy the thrill of the... ... ... ... ... ... ... ... ... ... ... ... ... BIG MONEY CONTRACT!!! Broadcast Archives Junk In The Trunk Dude, Where's My Car? IV...Fourthquel? or Fourthology? Part 4! Dude, Where's My Car? Part III...Trilogy...The Best Things In Life Are Three...Uh Dude, Where's My Car, The Sequel??? Dude, Where's My Car? ...no I'm better! I'm Tiger Woods... Memories of my own j0lt's New World Champion Appointment Setting Rematch Next Week Not Gone, Just Forgotten Quotes "But wait one second, Cataclysm is around the corner, and what better way for me to get those '3ood' wanna-bes back. Jonathan Conspiracy, the j0lt World Champion; has a nice ring to it. Sterling Silver, Wippit Guud, and Crude don't stand a chance. It's my show, it's my world, and on New Year's Eve, j0lt goes in a new direction, j0lt becomes 'The Attention Getting Federation', j0lt won't just be known as a fed full of 'Survivor Runner-ups', because JCON will bring j0lt to the promised land, whether you all like it or not, j0lt will get your attention!" "But Mr. Lee obviously forgot to tell Titan, Register, Flyer and Davis that, so to hell with 'The Four', to hell with Mr. Lee, and to hell with jOlt. Jonathan Conspiracy is here to end this pathetic little federation, and then I'm going to watch fWo Sunday Slaughter!"
