Roster Page | KroW 's Biography | KroW 's Transcripts KroW Real Name: 'Levine', or so he heard. He usually refers to himself by his own made character 'KroW' Birthdate: Unknown, even to himself Hometown: The Lonely Streets Of Nowhere Height: 6'4' Weight: 268 lbs. Nicknames: 'The Satanic God' Status: Neutral Manager(s): None Current Teams: None Entrance Music: 'Boiler' by Limp Bizkit Wrestling Style: Hardcore Technician and Power Signature Moves: StraightJacket Driver (Old Finisher, a double underhook piledriver), Reverse DDT, Front Dropkick with a Chair, Depression Downfall (old finisher, Vertaebreaker), Strong DDT, Piledriver, Top Rope Elbow Drop, Top Rope Leg Drop with a chair Set Up Move: Prelude (Release German Suplex Through a Table ) Finisher: The Paralyzer (nicknamed after ending the career of Sean Cylear) (A Powerbomb quickly turned into a snap sitdown Piledriver) jOlt Title History: jOlt X-Treme Championship jOlt Award History: None as of yet Other Federations: Current Federations: Currently None Previous Federations: GCW (Global Championship Wrestling), WWL (World Wrestling League), SW (Shockwave Wrestling), RWO (Revolutionary Wrestling Organization), dW (digital eWrestling), PIW (Pinnacle of Insane Wrestling) Other Federation Awards: Numerous GCW and WWL Championships, Wrestler of the Year 1999 (GCW), Match of the Year 1999 (GCW), SW Hardcore Champion (never defeated), RWO Genesis Champion (never defeated), dW United States Champion (never defeated), PIW Hardcore Champion (never defeated) Bio **1997** Many people here talk about their REAL past, the past of their family. A loving mother that would cuddle you beneath her chin whenever you needed a shoulder to cry on or just try to cheer you up. A brave father who would make you into a man of all men, and allow you to become brave and strong. Many people's past reflect on who they are. That is not the case with me, because, I have no past...well, not one that I can remember anyway. I can only remember up to the day I first saw my first big break called the WWL. I think I was knocked out or attacked by someone, it may have knocked my memory out of my mind. I remember staring at my bloodied self and then looking up at a wrestling federation, to this day I'm not sure if I was there on purpose, or was brought there by someone. But when I awoke, someone was there to help me get to an ambulance. I later found that man to be WWL Veteran, John Headcase. John showed me much of the sport, and some of the reasons I was there in WWL (All that I picked up along the way of his speeches). He told me my name, well, half of it at least, he kept calling me 'Levine'. I figured it must be my last name or first name, either way, I finally had a name. John Headcase taught me much about High Flying skills, and speedy wrestling. He taught me many moves to commit during wrestling matches, and soon enough, he had me ready for my first match...... **One Year Later - 1998** WWL was a great experience for me. I had won numerous titles, almost every single one of them, as a lightweight. I had faced my once mentor...ECW Star Raven, and I won. On top of that, I embarrassed him by taking his own signature move...the Evenflow DDT. I used it constantly to taunt him, before I retired him from WWL. Everything seemed right in the business world, but in my mind, everything was a blur. I began to see visions, I wasn't too sure of what...whether my past or present...but it were some visions. Visions of a young woman screaming while she pushed me out of the way of a bright light. Visions of a bloodied body that stood before me, and visions of a young boy who smiled at the younger image of myself. I was insane, I thought to myself. There I was, a man with half a name, who usually called himself by his ring name 'KroW'...but this ring name, would soon be a part of myself. KroW was a rageful gimmick given to me after WWL had closed down, and GCW hired me. KroW was that one person I always wished to be, that evil power that could take all that came to him, and would never give up on what went down. KroW became a part of me after two months of playing him. I became a rageful maniac who looked for revenge on all, and mostly, on the man who I thought was a friend, but who never tried to get me another job or contact me after WWL ended. That man, that black hearted bastard, was John Headcase. He would be my number one enemy, and he would be the one who I would kill in an Ironman Match. After his death, which I had a vision upon, came to be, I was confronted by GCW Staff, but was pardoned of all charges because earlier on, I was reported...mentally unstable by doctors. In this case, GCW would be held responsable....and because of this, GCW would close. *One Year Afterward* I was desperate to fight, but my mind was still not ready for violence. The violence feared me, for some reason, I did not like to kill people, it hurt me dearly. I was afraid of killing another. I was given a call from someone I knew back in GCW, I was actually found and called...it was a former stable mate of mine named Michael Mantle...better known as Madsexy Mike, current PCW Star. Mike told me he had found an opening at a federation...this federation was named E.WF. The worst mistake of my life, or I think it was. E.WF was filled with alot of staff and backstage bulls--t. E.WF was nothing more than handing wins to the main eventers constantly, until I had enough of it. I went against the E.WF's backstage area, and injured their Hardcore Champion on purpose. I thought I would kill him, until one man entered the fold, a man who would be a new comer to E.WF, obviously a man who was sent to restrain me from hurting E.WF's Hardcore Champion. I was immediately fired after breaking their Hardcore Champion's arm, shoulder and jaw. *One Year Later - 2000* A year of going to one federation to another. I must admit, not many federations were interested in signing, because of my violent past, and how my mental state was no where near 100%. But finally, a federation signed me, Shockwave Wrestling. On the first event, I was signed to go up against a man named Matt Williams for the SW's vacant hardcore title. I won the match and the title, but not two days later, the SW closed its doors. Once again, I was left with no way to release my anger, so it built up inside, until I received a call from Eddy Gordo in RWO. He wished for me to join his federation along with stars like Shawn Steele, Chris Thunder, Hellphyer, Leprechaun, and much more. I entered, and on the first event, defeated Chris Thunder for the RWO Genesis Title. But not a moment later, the RWO also closed, with me as their Genesis Champion. *One Year Later - 2001* I was contacted by Chris Thunder, a man I hated, to join dW. I wondered why he wanted me in, I found out it was only to screw with my mind, which was messed as is. There, I killed yet another man. Chris Thunder was killed in dW, after being fired by them. On that same night, I met with another RWOer named Shawn Steele. He was fearfull of me, and couldn't believe he would face me, but I did not take him lightly. Although his mental status was low, his in-ring abilities weren't bad. But though he tried, I ended up destroying Shawn Steele and capturing the dW United States title. Right afterwards, dW closed its doors. **Three months later** It was late April, where I found myself walking around my home, my new home, and my only home that I knew. The lonely streets of no where. I was a lost cause, a man with one single name...and character that portrayed him. I was Levine, simply Levine, with the mind of KroW. I killed two men, and was so afraid of doing it again. I couldn't control my anger, or my feelings. I did what I did because...I felt it....to be right...and to be fun. As I pondered through those lonely streets it led me to a big building. A bright tall building, much fancier than the previous federation buildings I've seen. This one read PIW, and under those big letters it said 'looking for wrestlers'. I saw my opportunity...and I took it. *On August 2001 Through all the hard times in my career, through all the bullshit of suicidal tendencies, I formed a group, a group of misfits of the world today. A group of killers, who just needed more training on learning to fight with more vengeance and gain much more respect from the PIW offices. Extremist and K.C. Dukes are my students, and I teach them well. And in the process I teach myself more and more during training. Learning new techniques of high flying and speedy type fighting. And it finally paid off as I finally defeated one sick individual brought to this company by the name of......'The Poisoned Soul' Stephen Blackeheart. And in the process I became the PIW Hardcore Champion. *On November 2001 I had returned, from a betrayal. It was only in August where I was betrayed by my own students. Those students of Extremist and K.C. Dukes. I have lived with such hatred within my being to EVER forgive them, so I have trained for so long to the day I should meet them. I had found out K.C. Dukes had committed suicide, the weakling took the easy way out of my hell. The hell I had him prepared for...he would've burned in all hell, but he got out easyily. Extremist wasn't so bright...I have found him, and have sent him the warning. That warning was the career ending of Sean Cylear. He didn't understand, so I made it clear for him....I told him in my own way...that he would be destroyed...and I shall do just that. Extremist had crossed me once, and it shall never happen again, no matter what obstacles he may throw at me...I shall become the victor of this war. And I shall end a creation that I created myself. I shall destroy and rid jOlt of Extremist. And Extremist knows what I told him before hand.... 'I Warned You' **The Start of 2002** The war has ended, my horrid past in PIW will no longer haunt me. During the final confrontation with Extremist, behind the steel cage, I forgot the one other man in this equation of KroW's Nest. KC Dukes. I thought he had killed himself, but I was wrong, and instead, he killed the man who killed him mentally.....Extremist. And now I am left alone, no more enemies, Extremist is destroyed, and I have won. Dukes is gone, hiding from the world....he is destroyed, and I have won. And now, as the victor, and capturing jOlt gold....I have a mission. A mission to take the weak of jOlt and make them stronger, but not for their own benefit. I do this, because it gives me a challenge to beat them back down to the ground! And shall do so, Believe In Me, I shall do so.
