Pay Per View

as captured 8 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The pay-per-view page of jOlt Wrestling’s 2005 site, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 8 February 2005: Wrestle Sault announced for the Bell Centre in Montreal, and Mardi Gras, the introduction to the third season, on 23 January.
The biggest event in the history of Jolt Wrestling is coming to the Bell Centre in Montreal, PQ, Sunday, February 28th, Live on Pay Per View: Wrestle Sault. Watch as the Triple Crown Tournament comes to a head as the Semi Finalist Victors get a shot at the Jolt Triple Crown Championship, while the runner's up get a second chance by wrestling for the Ambassador Championship. Also, the feuds that have transpired during the Triple Crown Tournament and outside come to a head. Contact your local cable or satellite operator and order Wrestle Sault. The Bell Rings here! Jolt Wrestling Presents Mardi Gras: Introduction to the Third Season of Jolt Wrestling, rocked and thrilled the streets of New Orleans on January 23rd for an amazing event. The C.E.O. was named, the Triple Crown Tournament was announced, and the partnership deal between Jolt Wrestling and Broadcast TV was made public. While not a Pay Per View event, the show was put on Joltwrestling.com for viewing. Watch it here!
