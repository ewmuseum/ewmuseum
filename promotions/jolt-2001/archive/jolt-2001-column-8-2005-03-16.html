Wednesday Night Dynamite Posted by Geo Vacton on 03/16/05 Heeey, ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the most explosive Jolt Wrestling column EVER. Yes sir, this is Wednesday Night Dynamite and its hosted by me, yours truly, Geo Vacton! I’ll be letting you guys know the low down on everything going on in Jolt every Wednesday night. I’ll recap Tuesday Night Intense, and let you know what you should expect to see on the next Jolt event, the reactions to some of the things that happened on the show, and, of course the things that you didn’t see on television and the things that you wouldn’t know about anywhere else if you DIDN’T read this bad boy right here, Wednesday Night Dynamite! So, not only did we just have an AMAZING Intense last night, but in just FOUR days its gonna be THE RETORT coming at you LIVE from the Fleet Center in Boston, Massachusetts, baaaabbbyy!!! And, it is there where we’ll see Brandon Youngblood defend the biggest title, or should I say TITLES, in wrestling… THE JOLT TRIPLE CROWN!!! Against the one and only, JEFF GARVIN!! In addition to that as the main event, there is going to be a NUMBER ONE CONTENDERS MATCH featuring the dual winners a la last week’s BAT-TALE ROY-ALE, “UNTOUCHABLE” CARSON NASH VS …WHAT?! CJ NEWFIELD!!! Out of no where, CJ NEWFIELD could very well go on to face the champ for the Triple Crown!! I gotta tell ya, I’d kill to be in his boots right now… BUT, I wish him only the very, very best at The Retort! The rematch of all rematches, XIAS VS JCON!!! I know JCON might not be the most popular guy amongst the fans, but c’mon. He’s going up against XIAS! This guy INVADED us on some of our first shows!! Whether he’d like to admit it or not, this guy is helping us out. I’d say we give him a round of applause, but I won’t push it ;-) either way though, its going to be a HELLUVA match!! My buddy Ken Kaze is gonna be taking on his now arch nemesis, POLAR in a WINNER TAKES GEORGE match! You know, these two have been screwing with each other like rabbits, and its gonna be AWESOME to finally see those two in action against one another. Then, of course, there’s going to be MY match J Yes, I’m finally gonna get my hands back on Dawson James and show him what’s up! To think, this all started cos he had to come out and rap about himself being the best wrestler to come out of ZyberTech Wrestling, well… Who’d of known? Honestly, who has he wrestled around here that gives him any amount of credibility? He’s wrestled himself! I guess we’ll see though, because by the way, Dawson ACCEPTED my challenge for a CAGE MATCH this Sunday, at THE RETORT! Now, lets get down to the nitty gritty of this thing, the recap of last night’s TUESDAY NIGHT INTENSE!!! The show came to us LLIIIIVVEE from the Dunkin’ Donuts Arena in Providence Rhode Island! And you know what? They proved that size doesn’t really matter, because lets face it. THE SOUND OF THAT CROWD WAS HUUUUGE!!! It all started out with a little recap of last week, and then followed up with a word from CJ Newfield, or should I say Carson Nash? Yep, Nash came out dressed up as Newfield and basically humiliated… Hmm, does this sound familiar to anyone else but me? I guess Nash learns from the best… Or the worst, whichever way you look at it, really. Obviously none to pleased by Nash’s performance of himself, Newfield came out and let him have it, which totally violated the rules that American Hero III had set down a week ago, which stated that neither man touch each other until The Retort. Oh boy, that sounds so dirty… Anyway, for their sheenanagins AH3 had them tossed out, and said they wouldn’t get paid for the night. Pretty stiff, but c’mon guys, don’t cross the boss ;) Ken Kaze appeared on screen looking a little disheartened. As if losing George last week wasn’t enough to bring the poor guy down, he also came face to face with some caution tape and a big metal beam, which he was only able to save himself by using the powers of the Buzzed Bunny! After coming to the conclusion that the bull dozer had been hired by Polar, he tried to kick its ass… That didn’t work so good, and I’m pretty sure his toes were taped for the rest of the night. Heh, expect a limp. Jeff Garvin came out and sassed the crowd up a bit, proclaiming himself the savior of the supposed ratings drop since Youngblood became the champ, and said he was take Youngblood within an inch of his life at The Retort. It didn’t take long for the champ to have enough of it, and so he made his way out to beat the crap out of Garvin until Jeff was able to fend him off with a low blow. As hard as Garvin tried to take Youngblood out with a chair, Brandon fought back. Youngblood laid the smack down on Garvin, and left him in a heap of himself on the floor. Girl power ruled again as Michelle Micks and Kelly Andrews both showed concern for DDR, who was still down about being embarrassed last week by the Brawler and Ito. They found her stomping away at the floor in her locker room, apparently realizing some stress. Being a hero to teenage girls all across the country, she let Michelle and Kelly know that she’s straight edge, and so is trying to stay clean and not make any destructive decisions. XXX is the way to be, Dee! Oh, man… I hate myself for making that rhyme, but I had no other choice… Anyway, she said stomping is what keeps her away from drugs, alcohol, and sex. Good move. Heh, no pun intended. She then said she would get revenge on Ito and Brawler. She then left to go ready for her match with the Brawler. “Impact” Rowdy Burns knew that he needed to make an IMPACT The Retort, since so far its going to be HUGE. In what has to be the most disgusting thing to be on Jolt television yet, Burns dropped trow and relived himself in a paper bag! Oh my God, honestly man. He then put the bag in front of a locker room door and set it on fire… As he ran away, the words “Unknown Elements” could be seen on the locker room door. Shawn Arrows fought to put the fire out, and then realized the cards he had been dealt. Uh-oh. DDR VS Back Alley Brawler. To say these two were a mismatch in sizes would be understatement. As usual, however, DDR put up a great fight and gave Brawler a run for his money. After several pin attempts and finally getting Brawler down, DDR took off her top and wore only a black tank top. Oooh man, following it up with a move called the Dynamite Rave, I’m thinking about giving her my number. Wait a minute, she’s like 17 isn’t see? GUYS I TAKE IT BACK. I DIDN’T MEAN IT. I NEVER GAVE HER MY NUMBER AND I NEVER WOULD C’MON DUDES THATS SICK. As valiant as her attempts were, BAB caught her with a Winston Special and then locked on the Big Bronx Sleep after a seven count. She passed out, and Jared Alexx called the bell. :-\ Moving onward we had a little piece about this little piece right here… Basically Magick Man and I let y’all know I’d be comin’ at ya every week ;) Polar showed up and walked passed that construction site that Kaze had bumped into earlier, and he tried to act like he didn’t know anything about it. The quick thinker that Kaze is, he constructed a plan to try and take Polar out. A distraction caught Polar’s attention, and he was almost ran over by an unmanned car as it came speeding towards him. After just barely dodging the car, Polar punched open the car window and went to destroy the driver. Too bad for him, however, there was no one inside! Just a note stating “George Was Here.” Heh, dayum. Polar, with what seems to the theme of tonight, vowed revenge. But, wait a minute… if Polar already has George, then what’s left? Oh! Yeah! KEN KAZE!! Nash had some beef with Ito after Ito stormed the ring last week during the battle royal, but Ito told him he was only doing it to make things interesting. Then he insulted CJ Newfield, which didn’t really matter that much to Nash. Tension was rising, but Ito didn’t wanna fight Nash backstage since he wouldn’t be getting paid for it. Nash didn’t care though. He decked him and told him to enjoy watching Nash in the main event tonight. Ohhhh yeah. I started to feel not so bad for thinking about slipping DDR my number when I saw that Mittens was actually going through with his date with her. Hahaha, honestly guys. Love him or hate him, well, you gotta love him. A big cat suit with a tie on? C’mon. Give the guy some props, maaaannn… Anyhoo, the duo watched “Hitched” and Mittens was looking to score. DUDE. SHE’S 17!!!!!!!!! He tried to put the moves on her, but she refused. Brawler, who was chaperoning the whole thing, made her hold his hand and then returned to his seat. Someone didn’t like Mitten’s cat suit head, but the Brawler took care of him and took his milk duds. Mittens tried to show some class by schooling himself up a little bit on the DDR game by reading a strategy guide, which is an EXCELLENT strategy, by the way ;) but then he admitted all he really wanted to was have ass sex with her after their date. Once again, and I know its getting redundant, SHES 17. As the bodies hit the floor, the Brawler stole some food for himself and for Mittens, only further humiliating DDR. Poor thing. The one and only Kodiac Vic Creed appeared to give an interview with Mike Mixx. He said Jolt had a lot of potential and that it could only go up in success. He said he wouldn’t frown upon coming back to Jolt someday, but didn’t mention any plans on doing so. He said maybe he’d show up if someone talked smack on him, since he’s been known to not take any of that. So, uh, hey, boys. Get started on that, ok? The crowd was then treated to a little treat. A Jolt vintage match from December of 2001. It was actually the Cataclysm Activity match from Cataclysm, which Creed won by getting ten points and was awarded the jOlt Heavyweight Championship. Ken Kaze likes to say Dildo. A lot. In fact, a little over 98 times. No, I didn’t count it… The FCC did. They said 15 more and we’d of hit our limit, which would’ve meant bye bye Jolt! Kaze made his way back into his locker room and found that Polar was at it again. His television screen had a message on it, much like Polar’s did last week. It was an arrow pointing up, and when Kaze finally found what it was pointing to, he was astonished to find such a big and mesmerizing word as “Redemption.” Enough was enough for Kaze, and he decided to go after Polar. Having been kicked out of the arena, CJ Newfield was outside loafing around. He tried to get a guard to go fetch Nash, but he refused. They came upon a car with the oh so clever license plate: HANDSOFF. Who would have a plate like that? I dunno, but I bet he’s UNTOUCHABLE. OOHH!!! Newfield decided that if he couldn’t get to Nash, then he’d get to his car. He said there were two options. Heh, looks like Jolt likes to harbor guys who give two options to everything. CJ said that the guard would either do nothing, or like it… No, not really. He said he’d either destroy the car or go inside and destroy Nash… Since he couldn’t decide which one he liked better, he did ‘em both. What a man. So, he took a lead pipe and took it to it. When the guard couldn’t stop him, CJ headed inside. Phil Atken came out and talked about how Carson Nash (who’s first name no one can remember) won the battle royal, and then made mention about threats he was getting from one Chris Messiah. He called him out to the ring, but he didn’t show up… Instead, Jolt was treated to something almost as strange as Mittens T. Cat and Ken Kaze. Hosni. Not too many people were familiar with him, but he let us know that he’s a relative rookie from Egypt who’s here to finish what he started with Phil Atken in Carnage Wrestling. Atken made a lot of queer jokes and tried to demean Hosni, but c’mon… The guys is wearing a pink shirt and purple pants. There’s not much more to take away from him. Hosni told Atken that they had a match, and if Atken tried to back down he’d be FIRED. Despite the crowd’s hopes that Atken would back down, he didn’t. After roughing Hosni up a little bit, they had a match. Phil Atken VS Hosni. Hosni’s pretty new to Jolt. Actually, he *JUST* started… so, no one really knew what to expect. However, he brought it to Phil Atken and had a really strong connection with the crowd right off the bat. Hosni’s efforts all came in vain, however, as Atken took the lead and drilled Hosni into the steel steps with a pile driver. Jared Alexx called this a disqualification, however, and so called for the bell. Hosni got the win, but it didn’t come without a price. DDR and Mittens were continuing their date at a restaurant when the Brawler showed up again. Still trying to make this a good night for Jolt’s newest couple, he laid the smack down all over the restaurant, eventually getting the two a meal together. DDR wasn’t pleased. Polar was again been tortured by the apparent presence of Ken Kaze. Ya know, if you think Dawson plays some weird games… Well, then apparently your out on a potty break every time that Polar and Kaze are on television. These two are driving each other NUTS. Polar’s lights kept flickering, and then they eventually shut off completely. When they turned back on there was a note waiting for him, and he found what had been left behind in Kaze’s room as well. REDEMPTION. Uh-oh. Kenjiro Ito came up to the same ol boos he’s been getting since day one, so what the hell did he care? He came out and said he was gonna finally answer the question on a lot of people mind’s (including my own, if you really wanna know): Who will he be facing at The Retort? The crowd wanted to hear Wippit Guud’s name, but Ito refused. Instead, in a total shocker, he said he’d be going up against any professional wrestler who WASN’T in Jolt. Oh, man. Do you guys realize how huge this is? Well, it’s HUUUUUUGE. He also said to tune into Prime tonight and catch his match with Aimz. Then I came on television and announced that my match with Dawson James would be a cage match. I have an update, actually… Dawson has accepted the match, and it’s completely official… We will have a cage match at The Retort, and extra security will be at ringside to make sure that Dawson shows up… This time, its it. Dawson can’t escape now. Hehe, tune in. Jolt’s top dog Brandon Youngblood appeared on screen, and it seemed as though American Hero III wanted to have a word with him and Jeff Garvin. Garvin showed up on screen too, and of course tensions rose. AH3 appeared live from New Orleans and announced that Youngblood and Garvin CANNOT touch each other until The Retort. As creepy as that sounds, it was meant to preserve the main event at The Retort. Both men were forced to agree. RUSH VS BRANDON YOUNGBLOOD. Rush and Youngblood met in a non-title match. Youngblood came out to a huge entrance, and then the match was off. They went back and forth for the majority of the beginning of the match, and both men were in equal position on the very end. Rush brought Youngblood up into the air on his shoulders and looked to be about to take the win, until Youngblood reversed the whole entire move and locked on the Gridlock! Rush tapped, and Youngblood walked away with another W under his belt. Garvin came out, but hey, he couldn’t touch Youngblood. They’d have to wait until The Retort. Impact showed up again and let Unknown Elements know that he was the one who left the flaming poop bag earlier. They weren’t pleased, but all parties agreed to a tag match at The Retort… Who would Rowdy’s partner be? Dunno yet. Hey, Rowdy, you know I’d have your back and everything, but I’m gonna busy kicking someone else’s ass. Sorry, dude. Mittens and the Brawler were back on the screen, this time heading for a club. Mittens thought DDR wanted to get freaky, until he realized where he was. I think I saw Dawson James in the background, cos they were at a gay bar! OOHHH BURRRNNn!!! DDR left them a note and asked if Mittens wanted to wrestle DDR in a “You’re My Bitch Match,” a match where the loser would become the winners bitch for a month. Mittens accepted and then men 10-inch Tony. Garvin and Youngblood shared some more confrontation with one another as Garvin hit on Youngblood’s wife. Youngblood couldn’t do anything though, because he’d lose his titles of he did. Ken Kaze came out and Polar soon followed. The two argued over everything that was happening and about just who had George… Their argument was going no where but bad, so AH3 had to step in. After getting over the shock of seeing Zordon, Kaze listened to AH3 announce that he and Polar would face each other at the Retort in a no DQ match where the winner will get George. Oh, and Kaze has to stop saying Dildo. Too bad for you, Kenny. I can say Dildo all the damn much I want! DILDO DILDO DILDO. I’ll stop at three though. Youngblood was paranoid about Garvin, but Melissa assured him that he had nothing to worry about since Garvin couldn’t touch him. Brandon had to go start the Hummer, so he told his wife not to leave the locker room. But hey, she was thirsty. The scene faded into the… The… The… THE MAIN EVENT!!!!!!!!!! Kenjiro Ito ( c ) VS Carson Nash. The Ambassador Title was on the line, so its no surprise that these two put up a HELLUVA show. Both men came out with their superstar entrances, but points go to Nash for basically telling Ito he might as well hand the belt over to him. Ya know, if Nash could win this match, and he could beat Newfield, and he could then beat Garvin/Youngblood, then gee wilikers!!! He’d be a quadruple champion!! Knife Edge chops entertained the match a few spots in, and they continued to enjoy themselves with some great power moves by both men such as the PerfectPlex, and an Angle Slam and MULTIPLE BLIZZARD SUPLEXES!!~!!!@2!!!!! Jeeez!!! …LIKE A GODDAMN VAMPIRE WHO BITES MOUTHS INSTEAD OF NECKS CARSON BIT ITO IN THE LIP… Now that I got that out of the way, lemme type W like forty times. EWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW… Ito and Nash exchanged some more moves, and then Ito tried hit Ito’s Ice Age… it would be over, but there was something going on up at the entrance ramp… bodies were flying everywhere. Who was responsible? What the HELL? IT WAS WIPPIT GUUD. Then, out of nowhere, and how he got back to the arena so fast, Back Alley Brawler came out with a chair and tried to take Wippit out but it backfired. Lusus came out too, but Wippit took him out as well. After Nash hit the Epilogue on Ito, Wippit made his way into the ring. Newfield appeared. Within moments there was nothing but chaos. Brawler was in the ring, Lusus, DDR!!!, Wippit, and more. Bodies were flying left and right, and the referee eventually called for the bell. Yeah, it was a no contest. Backstage Melissa was going for a drink when the Garvins appeared behind her. Wrong place, wrong time apparently. They tossed her around and brought her down to the ring where they messed her up some more. Jeff Garvin locked in the Hammer Jammer, and it was only a matter of time before Brandon would show up. He had to make a decision, save the girl or keep the titles? Titles are important, but aw, Youngblood’s a softy!!! He chose love!! AWWWWW!!! How romantic!!! Well, he might’ve chosen love but… That didn’t change the ass kicking he gave Garvin ;) Garvin was soaked in his own blood by the time the credits rolled and a preview for The Retort aired. Ladies and Gentlemen, you totally don’t wanna miss this upcoming Pay-Per-View. WrestleSault was only a warm up, I’m telling you. Now, obviously there were a lot of other things going on last night. Something that didn’t make it to television, for instance, was a little a chat I had with Carson Nash. I think I asked all the questions y’all wanted to know. His responses are written in BOLD. "What was up with all those notes, man? Do you really think you can kick my ass?" My anger management counselor made me write notes to people, expressing my truth through notes and not violence. Of course, my notes started some violence, but, that matters none now, because it's over. And as for you, I think you'd wipe the floor with me. Oh, just kidding, you're a poor excuse for a wrestler who bases his gimmick off of one HORRIBLE song. And if anyone out there that thinks otherwise, I pity them. "Hows it feel to be a step away from wrestling for the Triple Crown?" It hasn't quite hit in, yet, but I think once I get out there at Retort, it'll sink it. The whole feeling of the crowd buzzing, popping at your every move...I love it. Ask Youngblood how it feels knowing that he'll be losing it at One Night In Winnipeg. "How surprised were you when Newfield pwned you last week?" Excuse me? "How surprised were you when Newfield pwned you last week?" You're lucky this isn't Tuesday Night Intense or.. "You're right, it's Wednsday Night DY-NO-MITE!" ... ... Yeah. Kay.. Uhh, CJ Newfield, uhm, hid outside the ring. I'm sorry, but weren't you like the first non-enhancement talent that was eliminated? Ouch. Looks like YOU got pwned. Final Words... Watch Retort. Watch Nash. Watch Nash beat Newfield. Watch Nash beat Youngblood. Peace up. A-Town down. Nash, you’re a big jerk. But, I’m gonna let it slide. We’re on the road again right now, in fact I’m typing this from our tour bus. Kaze and I were at a diner last night before we left, and scribbled something down on a menu… Uh, he said he wanted to make sure I got it on here, so here’s my big favor to him: If you can help him out, call that number… Or whatever it is. Anyways, thanks a lot for checking out my column!!! I’ll be back again next week, but until then, DO NOT FORGET TO TUNE INTO THE RETORT, LIVE THIS SUNDAY AND ONLY ON PAY PER VIEW!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Yours Truly, Geo Vacton PS – Oh, by the way, today’s date is 3/16… Hmm… Just something to think about!
