What this is The write-up of Mardi Gras, the street show that introduced the third season of jOlt Wrestling, as its 2005 site carried it in the Wayback Machine capture of 9 February 2005. The site’s own events page dates the show 23 January 2005, in uptown New Orleans.
Prologue It was the beginning of something new, and at the same time, familiar. A brisk, January air blew throughout New Orleans as the Mardi Gras festivities continued their merry way. In uptown New Orleans, over a 1000 people gathered around a very high maintenance set up, including a wrestling ring and two large Plasma screen televisions. It was the exciting debut of Jolt Wrestling. It had been three years since Jolt Wrestling disappeared in a disappointing "second season", which left a bitter taste in a lot of wrestling fans mouths. However, with time comes healing, and the healing begins with this exciting event, in the heart of Jolt Wrestling's territorial home, New Orleans. This was to be a new Jolt Wrestling, the right Jolt Wrestling. All of the mistakes of the past to be repaired by the present, to make way for the future. And tonight was the first step... Begin Ceremonies Surrounding the ring were a thousand of Jolt Wrestling’s most loyal fans. No wind chill or aborted promotion plans were going to stop these fans from cheering on this debut for Jolt Wrestling. Many of them were just 18 when they watched Jolt Wrestling. Now, many of them were able to drink alcohol to cheer this eventful occasion. Many in the audience wore the old jOlt Wrestling souvenirs they picked up while jOlt Wrestling was hot: Brandon Blade t-shirts, Graphic Violence Halloween masks, Team VIAGRA “Funny Pills”, Donaven Winters “Whine Kazoos” and even LLB “Foam Gavels”. There was even a special section near the ring of fans who all wore Season 2 jOlt Wrestling t-shirts and held up a large sign shaped of the jOlt logo reading, “Lost since 2002: Season 2 Fans”. There was a distinct, positive vibe within the crowd, brightly displayed by the 52” Plasma TV screens high above the ring. Inside the ring, two tables were set up, along with a podium that well recognized jOlt Wrestling announcer Damien Lee was standing against with a smile. Across the table included jOlt Wrestling colour commentator Mike Mixx, former PG13 Radio DJ Josh Boodreau, Dallas Mavericks owner Mark Cuban and his former Broadcast.com partner Todd Wagner, former Louisiana wrestling promoter turned senior referee Mark Lenoux, former professional wrestler turned ring announcer Clippie Da Clown and a midget who looked oddly reminiscent of Graphic Violence. The crowd died down as it seemed Damien Lee was ready to begin talking, and with this began the ceremony. “Wow, what an exciting turn out,” exclaimed Damien Lee, a smirk still on his face. “I was expecting around a dozen people throwing tomatoes at us!” Forced laughter followed, like it does at most conventions when the speaker makes a joke. “Anyhow, I am going to make this as brief as possible, because we are running on a tight time schedule, and I want to make sure that everyone gets a lot of wrestling action tonight. Welcome to the introduction to Season Three of Jolt Wrestling!” The audience cheered, before breaking out into an expected “Jolt! Jolt! Jolt!” chant. The chants died down as Lee continued. “For those who do not remember, my name is Damien Lee. I was the regular announcer for Jolt Wrestling and played the role of part owner in 2001. Behind the scenes, I was the one who for the past three years have been trying to make this happen. Trying to revive Jolt Wrestling and properly. I feel that no better time is now. There will be no delays, no pre-emption’s, it is now or never, and I prefer now. We have a great crew behind us, we have an incredibly talent roster assembled, maybe the best you will ever see, and best of all, we got all of the tools needed to once again become the most powerful force in the wrestling world!” The cheers continued, but were less excited because fans wanted to get to the details. “As many of you know, because I am sure a lot of you are ticket holders, Jolt Wrestling will present its first arena show on February 1st.
