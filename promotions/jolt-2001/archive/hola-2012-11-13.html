Kaze — 14 November 2012 Hola Dan, Josh, and Trev! Definitely glad to see you guys again. How goes it for y'all? Life's been pretty good for the most part for myself. I cannot complain overall. Military basically sapped all of my interest in ewrestling a few years ago, but I've had those lingering feelings of nostalgia and "How are those guys doing, I wonder" creep up on me a couple of times per year. I definitely get the itch to write something regularly, but never find a comfortable topic or medium to write about. Maybe one day soon I will sit down and write out some material with Kaze to see how it goes.
