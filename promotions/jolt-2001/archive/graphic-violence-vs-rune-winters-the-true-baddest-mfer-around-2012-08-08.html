FIGHT~! (Poll closes in one month) Russel — 8 August 2012 Rune is a dangerous and incredibly fun character, and all... but in terms of bald sociopaths, he and GV are still my three and two all-time favorites.

Cheers for the Head 2 Head! We have to get some of these on the website, Dan! Derecho — 9 August 2012 I was a big fan of Graphic Violence. I'm going to give him the nod here. Sorry, but Rune Winters will not WHOCK out of this one on his own power
