What is Jolt Wrestling? “jOlt” Wrestling began in 2001 in the ashes of the recently departed Tornado Wrestling. The promotion was started under [name withheld]. They created the ground work and enlisted people to work under the project, such as flash animator [name withheld], and rounded up eight wrestlers to begin the promotion. The original eight of jOlt Wrestling were Brandon Blade, Kodiak Vic Creed, Donaven Winters, erØn, Griff Truxell, Jason Warrior, Erik Rave and Reno Davis. At a2o, the first jOlt World Champion was crowned when Brandon Blade defeated Griff Truxell with the Cutting Edge, with the help of a lead pipe legswipe by Erik Rave. More and more talents were assembled, as jOlt gained momentum in the E-Wrestling world. This climaxed at the Power Struggle pay per view, where Brandon Blade defended his title against Graphic Violence. It was at this point, the politics and ramp shod running of jOlt Wrestling caught up to itself. When erØn made his exit as jOlt World Champ in October instead of defending the belt against LLB, things deteriorated faster and faster before the promotion decided to end “Season 1”. Plans were then set up to re-open the promotion in 2002, but nothing was corrected. Mismanagement, egos and quick departures lead the closure of jOlt Wrestling for “Season 2”, and most felt jOlt Wrestling would never make a return. With years going by, Jolt Wrestling has decided to make a much needed return to the E-Wrestling world, hoping to bring a taste of “throwback” to those desperately needing it. It is important to get a taste of the atmosphere by reading the Mission Statement. The logo may look throwback/vintage to you, but that is the purpose. You’re supposed to think back to the times where there were walls around your writing, when writing was fun. When you spent a lot of time in this hobby because you liked it, not because it was a necessary chore and you didn’t want to disappoint people. Here is everything you need to know about the Triple Crown Championship. All of the rules, the defence system, questions, etc. Q: What is the Triple Crown Championship? A: The Triple Crown Championship is the top singles prize in Jolt Wrestling. It is also what the most important storyline in Jolt is always revolved around. It is three separate belts. The First belt is the Circa 2001 jOlt World Heavyweight Title. The second belt is the Circa 2002 jOlt World Heavyweight Title. The third belt is the Circa 2005 Jolt Triple Crown Title. If for some reason thats too much gold for your wrestler to handle (LAZY LAZY LAZY!) they can just wear the Circa 2005 Jolt Triple Crown Title. However, holding a belt in each hand with one around your waist looks cool. Q: What are the restrictions? A: You must weigh at least 1lb ;) The only restriction is where you filled out "Years Professional" on your Jolt Wrestling application, you have to have at least a "1" there. If you put a "0", you cannot hold the Jolt Triple Crown. Q: What is the Defence system? A: This is how it works: When someone wins the Triple Crown Championship, the next night, either at a Pay Per View or a weekly event, before they wrestle, they must decide what sort of Champion they are. Once they decide, they cannot change their mind. If they lose the title and win it again, they can work a different system. In Pro Wrestling, this system is usually just unreferenced and only referenced when looking "back" on a Championship. This also helps make sense to why some wrestlers become the number one contender, and why others get an immediate shot. The systems: King of the Road: King of the Road works on a Number One Contenders system. The belt is only defended once a month, at the Pay Per View, against the designated number one contender. The night this is set on the Triple Crown, a number one contenders match will take place on who will face the Champion. Every Pay Per View, a Number One Contender match will take place to decide on who’ll face the champion next month. If a new Champion is decided that night and they change to the second system, the Number One Contender gets their shot that night. This means there is only one person who can win that belt in a given month: The Number One Contender. Being the number one contender is just as constant as being Champion: You are only stripped of that when you lose against the Champion. The Number One Contender is decided either the night the new Triple Crown Champion confirms their Defence system, or at the Pay Per View of a Triple Crown defence. The advantage is a much more pretigious Champion. You have to fight through everyone else to get to the Champ, and you either take their place, or go back to the end of the line. The disadvantage is you cannot receive two straight Title shots. If you lose, you have to build yourself up the next month to become the next Number One Contender, win and face the Champion the following month. Road Warrior: Road Warrior works on an Endurance system, in that the Triple Crown Champion helps decide who he/she'll face. The Champion *must* defend the title at least three times a month, as well as at least six house shows. The Champion is stripped of their title if they are unable to make the proper amount of defences. The decision as to who faces the Champion is decided by the Jolt Chief Executive Officer and/or the Triple Crown Champion themselves. The Champion will receive a lot more opponents, and opens the opportunity for the belt to change hands during the week. While it doesn't include the prestige of the King of the Road, they do earn the respect, having to defend their position every night if called. The advantage to this is it works better for feuds. You can run a three month feud with opponent without an interfering Number One Contenders system. The disadvantage is that facing more opponents means a lesser valued Championship. You likely will not hold the Triple Crown as long as a King of the Road Champion. Back to Top How does the Ambassador Title work? Here is everything you need to know about the Jolt Ambassador Championship. All of the rules, the defence system, questions, etc. Q: What is the Ambassador Championship? A: To be Ambassador Champion is to represent Jolt Wrestling. If you think back to it, back in the early 90's, the WWF Intercontinental Title was the representation of the "Wrestling" in the World Wrestling Federation. The wrestlers who could work were given that title. In Jolt, this is similar. To be the Ambassador Champion is a step under the Triple Crown but you earn a different level of respect, since the person who holds the title is supposed to be "What Jolt Wrestling is about" so to speak. It is also just a fancy name to stray from geographical title names. Q: What are the restrictions? A: There are none. Yes, even if its your first year professional, you can hold the Ambassador Title. Q: What is the Defence system? A: A lot less rule heavy then the Triple Crown Title. The title must be defended in 30 days or you are stripped of the title. Opponents can be decided by the Champion or the C.E.O. (Either verbally or through show set-up). Q: Is there any "strings" attached? A: Yes. If another Wrestling Promotion wants someone from Jolt to come and work for them on a show, as a Special Appearance, it is the Ambassador Champion who gets sent. Just like a real ambassador, you have to represent Jolt in another "nation". You won't be sent to do this all of the time, since you like everyone else has storylines to run, but it is something you get to do. The best part: This gets your name out around Wrestling. If you tour often, there's the chance you can become even a bigger name then the Triple Crown Champion, since your name will be coming up on everyone else's cards, and on Jolt. Back to Top What is the Jolt Tag League? Q: What is the 2005-2006 Jolt Tag League? A: The Jolt Tag League is a new twist to the Tag Team Divisions in E-Wrestling. Instead of the classic championship, you now have a more “sports” themed system. Wins matter, teams look to the long run and the partner you work with is who you end with. If you have ever watched the AJPW Real World Tag League or NJPW G-1 Tag League, you’ll have an idea as to how this works. The League lasts from May until February, and the prize is the Jolt Tag League Championship. What makes this so different? 1. No Championship. No belt to be passed. Only one thing to win, one time to win it, and nothing to lose. Becoming the 2005-2006 Tag League Champion means only you can be given that title. No-one can take that year from you. 2. Everyone begins at zero. If you enter the Tag League in May or August, you begin at a record of 0-0-0=0 points. Beginning in August may give you a disadvantage to the teams who’ve already wrestled several matches, but that does not mean there is no possible way you can make the Playoffs. Those who make the playoffs are those with the most wins, highest percentage and best time average. 3. Season and Playoffs. The first half of the season runs from May until August. Anyone can take part at this point, and while records are kept store, teams can drop out. Once September rolls around, the teams who are still in the Tag League are there until Season end in December. No leaving. You go as far as you can. The Tag League is designed to breathe life into the struggling E-Wrestling tag divisions by giving them a purpose and style away from regular singles wrestling. There is a much more competitive edge to the Tag League then singles wrestling, and a team can take much more pride in being the 2005-2006 Tag League Champions then being Triple Crown Champion for a few months. You only have to beat one person to be Triple Crown Champion. You have to beat several people to become the Tag League Champion. Q: What are the restrictions? A: There are no restrictions to the Tag League other then you have to be a tag team and that if you are still in by September, you must stay in the Tag League until you are eliminated. If you choose to leave Jolt, you give Jolt the right to run your characters until elimination. Q: What are the rules for a “Tag League” match? A: A match in the Tag League is the first to one pinfall on a twenty minute time limit. Wrestlers can only spend 10 seconds in the ring after a tag. If there is no winner after twenty minutes, the match goes into a five minute “Tornado” style overtime, in which all four wrestlers are in the ring. Scoring is as follows: 0 points – Loss before 20 minute limit expires 1 point – Draw after 20 minute limit and 5 minute overtime, loss after 20 minute limit expires 2 points – Win before or after 20 minute limit expires If you are disqualified or counted out, you do not receive any points. If you are disqualified or counted out after the 20 minute limit expires, you do not receive a point. If a double pin occurs, the partners involved in the double pin are eliminated from the match, and the partners not involved much complete the match as if it were a singles competition. Time limit still applies. If those two somehow double pin each other, then no points are rewarded, for they eliminated each other, thus ruling a No Contest. If both members of a team cannot participate, only 1 point can be rewarded in a win (for either team). Q: How long is the Tag League? A: The season lasts from May 22nd, 2005 until February 26th, 2006. The League is broken down into four categories: Season A – May 22nd until August 22nd Potential Matches: 38 (Tuesday Intense, House Show, Pay Per View) Details: Between the teams who move into Season B, the two most successful teams receive an automatic position in the Playoffs. Season B – August 23rd until December 18th Potential Matches: 37 (Tuesday Intense, House Show, Pay Per View) Details: The Top 8 (Or Top 4) teams through the remaining teams of the Tag League will move onto the playoffs. This is determined by who has the best records through Win-Loss, highest win percentage and average match time. Highest win percentage is the most important statistic, followed by win-loss and average match time. Playoffs – January 3rd until February 26th Potential Matches: Quarter Finals (Best of Three), Semi Finals (Best of Five), Finals (2/3 Falls) Up to 9 matches Details: Highest Rank wrestles Lowest Rank in seeding. The Quarter Finals consist of a Best of Three series. Matches now consist of a 30 minute time limit. If the time limit is exceeded, the match goes into “Tornado Style” elimination, where both members of a team must be eliminated for the match to end. The Semi Finals consist of a Best of Five series. If the time limit is exceeded, the match goes into “Tornado Style” elimination, where both members of a team must be eliminated for the match to end. The Finals at Wrestle Sault is a first to two falls. There is a 60 minute time limit. If the time limit is exceeded, the match goes into “Tornado Style” elimination, where both members of a team must be eliminated for the match to end. Break – February 27th until May 27th The Tag League goes on a break. Q: When is the Jolt Tag League finals? A: Wrestle Sault II. It will be the second to last match on the card. The winning team also receives their own shrine on the website and is allowed to take their coveted Championship anywhere they please during the vacation. A graphic trophy for the Jolt Tag League will be made, and their names will be etched as the first to win. History, baby! Back to Top What are the Fatal Arcs? The Fatal Arcs are the five last Pay Per Views before Wrestle Sault. September's "Power Struggle", October's "ONI", November's "Rise of the Legends", December's "Cataclysm" and January's "ONI: Showdown". The purpose of these events are to establish potential number one contenders to face the Champion in February. Once you win your spot in a Fatal Arc, you are set to wrestle in January against the other number one contenders. The event matches are as follows: Power Struggle Special Match: The Power Struggle Elimination Games Description: Twelve contenders are given random time entry positions. Each man enters the ring, enclosed with a cell cage at their time intervals, beginning with two until all are in the ring. You can only win the match by pinfall or submission. The last man to survive the eliminations is declared the winner. They receive the first position in the January Showdown. ONI: October Special Match: The Hell's Kitchen Match Description: Ten contenders are seated by when they won qualification matches. The match begins with two men. After one of the men are eliminated, the third man comes out and a five minute clock is set. If the man who has been in the ring the longest cannot defeat the recent entry in five minutes, he is considered "Burned" and eliminated out of the match. This continues until there are only two men left. If the last survivor cannot defeat the last entry in five minutes, he is burned and the last entry wins the match. The last man to survive the eliminations is declared the winner. They receive the second position in the January Showdown. Rise of the Legends Special Match: The Rise of the Legends Tournament Description: A sixteen man tournament (similar to the original Triple Crown Tournament) takes place in November. Rise of the Legends will present the Semi Finals. The winner of the tournament is presented with the Beyer Cup (A tribute to Dick "The Destroyer" Beyer) and proclaimed the "Legend of Jolt Wrestling". They receive the third position in the January Showdown. Cataclysm Special Match: The Cataclysmic Activity Match Description: Thirty men will enter the ring in a "Royal Rumble" style time entry, beginning with four men. Each talent in the match is given a "Target". This means that every target is also being targeted. With pinfall or submission rules only, every pinfall or submission you make gives you points. If you eliminate someone who is not your target or targeting you (I.E. Random victim) you receive one point. If you eliminate someone who is your target (I.E. Assassinating them) you receive two points. If you eliminate someone who is targeting you (I.E. Your assassin) you receive three points. No-one knows who they are supposed to assassinate until they enter the match. They will only know their assassin by hearing it when the assassin enters the ring. The winner is decided when twenty nine men are eliminated, and the point scores are tallied up. Whoever has the most points wins. If there is a tie, the tie breaker is indicated by: (1) Who eliminated the most, (2) Who stayed in the match the longest and (3) Who eliminated their assassin and/or target. They receive the fourth and final position in the January Showdown. ONI: January Showdown Special Match: The Showdown Description: The main event is a Fatal Fourway Showdown between the four previously mentioned winners. There is no countout or disqualification and the match will go as long as it has to. The last man will find themself facing the Triple Crown Champion in the Wrestle Sault main event. If it goes down to two men, and they eliminate each other (double pin, double tap out) then both men will find themselves at the Wrestle Sault main event against the Triple Crown Champion. Back to Top What is an ONI? An ONI is a "One Night In" event. These take place every April, July, October and January. These events take place in special locations that are not usually visited by Jolt Wrestling. The importance of an ONI is they always precede a major event (Mayfall, Wrestle Festival, Rise of the Legends, Wrestle Sault) and present a much different atmosphere to the wrestling fan. Think of the ONI's as the "In Your House" of Jolt Wrestling. Back to Top Who deserves Credit for what?
