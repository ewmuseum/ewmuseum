What this is The write-up of Sunday iNtense 30, which jOlt Wrestling’s own site carried on its front page when the Wayback Machine captured it on 15 November 2001. The page has no date of its own; the weekly Sunday count back from iNtense 35, which dates itself, puts the show on 11 November 2001.
Sunday iNtense 30 Introduction jOlt Wrestling would enter its 30th iNtense tonight with much speculation. Nobody truely knew the direction that jOlt Wrestling was going in, but they knew that there were troubles, and lots of them. The Don and Damien Lee looked as if they were moving in their own directions, and if this was true, then something had to be done and fast. Damien has faced many challenges since his arrival. Bagaran, Bevins, Salary... But nothing at all matched the problem he would face if The Don did indeed have intentions on leaving Damien's side. The Don owned 49% of jOlt Wrestling. A percentage that was so large, Damien could only go to court to fight for. He couldn't issue matches for percentages of the company anymore, because The Don had the power to deny them. The Don was now a player in the financial game. He was no longer just a player in the game of entertainment. He believed he was entertainment now and he has already shown signs of using his power for the wrong things. Although Erik Rave didn't like The Don being the catalyst to keeping Erik in jOlt, The Don did just that. He used his power to keep Erik in jOlt. Erik later that day would go onto taking out former jOlt Wrestling Champion, Brandon Blade and long time friend, Bane Loneheart. These events could only bring up questions whether or not jOlt Wrestling would survive under split leadership once again. The odds are no and Damien knows this. Would it be in his best interest to continue to baby The Don to keep the company alive? Or fight for the company? Tonight... he would try one. Taking a Chance with Death. Chris Register and Damien Lee Not too many people understood the history behind Fueled By Ignorance. Their friendship was always very tight, even though they couldn't really trust eachother. It seemed that everytime the relationship got rocky, one of the Chris' would turn on the other so they wouldn't go down too, but this time... Things were different. Chris Titan was always the one to put things to an end, while Chris Register would be the victim of Titan's plans. When Register wanted to leave and would approach Titan un-televised, it was always to end things on a high note, but when Registers back was turned, Titan would knock him down to the floor on camera and keep him down and out, sometimes for months. Yes, things were different. This time it was Register who came up front on camera telling Titan he was nothing but self centered. When one thought a brawl would occur, Titan just dropped his microphone, shed a smile and turned his back on Register. No... not too many people understood the history behind Fueled By Ignorance, but now they would. Chris Register calmy knocked on Damien Lee's door and entered his office. Damien acknowledged him with a nod as Chris entered the room. He took a seat in front of Damien who took off his reading glasses and looked up at Chris. Register smiled and nodded, looked in Damien's face and jumped right into what he wanted to get at. "I want a match... Tonight. I need to prove myself." Damien looked at Chris with a cocked eyebrow, questioning his motives. To what extent was Register going to go through to show that he wasn't just Titan's toy. That was what Damien was urging to know ever since he told Titan there was no more F.B.I. Damein knew Register wasn't a big man. Nor a small man for that matter, but Register hasn't had too much singles success outside of the Cruiserweight Division. A division that hadn't been created in jOlt. "But..." "But nothing, Damien. I'm fed up with being Titan's puppet. Three years of this shit. It has to end and I'm going to end it today. I want Titan in a match." "I'm sorry Chris, but that can't be done. Titan hasn't been seen in the arena yet and hasn't called to explain his absense. He will be fined, but I'm sure that isn't what you wanted to hear." Register closed his eyes and shook his head. "Fine. I won't prove anything to myself then. I'll prove it to you. I'll prove I'm not a push over. I want KroW." Damien looked away from Register as his eyes widened. Damien took a drink of water and took a deep breath as he focused back on Register. "Chris. KroW is dangerous and he is the last man I want you to go against because you're a little steamed. Wait for next Sunday, we'll book you again Ti..." Chris Register interupted immediately. "KroW... Tonight... Make it happen." Register got up from his seat and left Damien's office. Damien raised his arm to call back Register, but had a lack of words. Nothing would change Register's demand. The Belt will be held up in some type of Match down the road. Right now, I fell that no one needs-” LLB rose his hand up, and cut Lee off in mid-sentence. “Look, jOlt Wrestling hasn’t been the best place in the world Lee, lets face it. You’ve had Wrestlers’ killing themselves, to a ton of Wrestlers’ leaving and walking out on you... etcetera, etcetera. I’m being VERY blunt here when I say this: jOlt Wrestling is going in the shitter. With a horrible Roster such as KVC, Rush, KroW, Crude, J-Con, Wippit Guud. Sterling Silver... for fuck sake Damein, who the hell are you letting into jOlt? PIW sucked, I was the only star. Next thing I fucking know it’ll be Idol Blockbuster, or IWO’s Ken War, or that crappy Indy Fed’s Avo Chavez, or Blast From the Past... God knows what you’ll do next, but what God himself doesn’t even know, is how bloody good I am, and how I was, and ONLY was PIW.” There was a moment of silence, as Lee took it all in, replying after with what he felt was the only right thing to say. “Roland, with all due respe-” However he was cut off, and LLB kept his rave going. “Chris Mezz talks about how he was the PIW, and Chris Fear... and Chris Fox... blah blah blah. I’ve been providing the fucking Law and Order in Wrestling for a long time now. So what if it was PIW, I deserve the right to have that evidence carried over into jOlt...” Once more the heat was back on Lee, as LLB sat in his chair, nearly smiling from ear to ear telling himself over and over there was no way Damien could get out of the last few words just said. “Please, please, I don’t want this to end up in a fight. I know you’re good, you’re VERY good and one of our best new talents, but c’mon, KVC? Rush? Wippit Guud? Sterling Silver? Those are some fine talented men. KroW and the others are good too, and they will keep working on their skills here... but there are other good people, just like you. LLB, you need to stop realizing it’s all about you. The fact is, it’s not. Most of jOlt’s success will come from you now, but not all... not all.” Very shocked, LLB snapped his head sideways, and frowned. “Objection!” he screamed, making Lee go up in shock himself. “This will be the last time I speak with you on this, and if you know good enough, you’ll hand me what’s mine. Give... me... the... key... to... the... Court Room.” “I’m sorry. It wouldn’t be right.” replied the Owner, in a sad, almost distant voice. And with that LLB got out of his seat, stormed off to the door, and looked back. “For this is one big sin you’ve pulled Lee, and it’ll testify. You wait and see, it’ll fucking testify and be proven... GUILTY!” Slamming the door behind him, Lee put his head down on his desk again. “Well, things sure are going good, aren’t they?” Rematch ‘Kodiak’ Vic Creed vs. LLB As the camera panned into the Arena, “Walk” was heard, and the Fans boomed up out of their seats for KVC as he power walked down the ramp, and into the ring. Following that came “Points of Authority” by Linkin Park. The people at that moment gave a rather mixed reaction for the so-called ‘Law & Order’ of Wrestling. He walked down the ramp, shouting some smack and calling himself the next jOlt World Champion. LLB walked up the ramp, and saw Creed resting on the corner across from him. LLB entered, as Creed smiled, finally having LLB in the ring after what had happened last week. The bell then went, and the Match was off. LLB looked at Creed, as KVC simply replied by tossing his bicep into the air and flexing it. LLB jumped back a small bit, almost becoming reluctant to grapple with him after that. KVC took a step forward, as LLB again took another step back. KVC then for a second time, took a step forward, and LLB went back, hitting his back into the turn buckle. Trapped, Creed laughed and charged into the corner, ramming his shoulder into the gut of LLB. LLB fell, and Creed stomped at him a few times before hitting the ropes and dropping the elbow. At that moment, Creed rolled LLB onto his backside, and pinned him to the floor. One... Two... The Crowd seemingly booed as LLB got his shoulder up, then starting a chant of “KVC, KVC”. A little upset, thinking he indeed got the win, Creed took LLB up via his short black hair, and hit him with a suplex. KVC then went to the ropes, hammering LLB with first, a standing splash, and second, some right hands. The ring shook, as Creed pulled himself up to the top rope, and jumped off, only finding nothing but mat as LLB moved just a second before hand. Both men lay, as the Fans started getting to their feet, really finding themselves into the Battle. Creed looked up, and found himself close to the ropes. He grabbed them, and pulled himself up to one leg, as on the other side of the ring, LLB did the same. After that, Creed was up on both feet first, proving he was truly the more powerful of the two. KVC turned to face LLB, who got up fully as well. They locked eyes once more, walked closer together, and grappled. A head butt sent LLB crashing down to the mat, only to get right back up. Another head butt sent LLB down, and this time he didn’t move. Vic Creed smiled, finding the ropes, and ramming LLB with a shoulder block. KVC pulled LLB back up, and hooked him for a snap suplex, which was hit, however LLB’s right boot was able to catch the Referee in the side of the head. Creed covered LLB, not realizing what had happened yet. KVC glanced around, finding the Referee out cold. Creed shrugged, got off the cover and went over to him checking on the man to see how badly he was hurt. In the time being, LLB slowly got up, and pulled some object out of his paints. They were brass knuckles. Creed turned around as the Crowd went up in gaps seeing the knuckles shine on LLB’s left hand. *Wham* LLB nailed KVC with the brass, sending him to the mat in a pile of his own blood. Some of the Fans booed, as a few cheered, but most were silent. LLB took a look down at Creed, then at the knuckles, only to toss the evidence of cheating away outside the ring. LLB then hooked KVC’s leg, and pinned him. It took a moment or two, but soon after the Referee counted the fall, finally wakening from the non intentional boot to the head. One... Two... ... Three! The bell went, and LLB sat up in sounds of it. He smiled and laughed, pulling himself on the top rope and holding his hands high above his head. His theme hit, but was only taken back off the airwaves as Creed got up, and pulled LLB down from the top rope, hitting him with a right hand. Creed rolled to the outside of the ring, and grabbed the brass knuckles. He slid back in, hit the ropes and among return decked LLB in the head with them. Whipping the blood from his forehead ‘Kodiak’ Vic Creed knelt down and hammered more and more shots into LLB’s skull. Referees’ and EMTs’ came rushing down only to be scared away by KVC getting up and running at them on the apron. He then went back to work on LLB, hitting him with a power slam, and more and more shots with the brass knuckles. Finally, after his theme was played over the PA, Creed dropped the brass, and headed off to the back, as the Referees’ piled into the ring checking on ‘the Law & Order’ and iNtense going to black. Winner: LLB via pinfal. The face could not be determined, but everyone knew it could only be one man. The Don. The Don continued took a long drag of his cigar and blew the smoke into the camera until nothing could be seen. The Don had made his presence felt. iNtense faded... to grey.
