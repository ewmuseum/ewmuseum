Roster Page | Erik Rave 's Biography | Erik Rave 's Transcripts Erik Rave Real Name: Erik Ryland Birthdate: 8/21/79 Hometown: Stanton Island, NY Height: 6'1 Weight: 220 lbs. Nicknames: Status: Neutral Manager(s): Anna Current Teams: Entrance Music: 'Gets me through' Ozzy Osbourne Wrestling Style: High Flyer/Brawler Signature Moves: Flying Head Scissors, Spear, Neckbreaker, Senton Bomb, Standing Side Kick, Reverse DDT Set Up Move: ( ) Finisher: True Hell (Falling Top Rope DDT) jOlt Title History: jOlt #2 Championship jOlt Award History: Other Federations: Tornado Wrestling Other Federation Awards: Bio The beginning of Erik's life was just an omen of things to come. He was born to a fifteen year-old mother, who didn't have the means or ability to support him. She tried, but living poorly in the middle of New York, there was little chance. And his father was hardly around, and had no intent of fathering Erik. When Erik's mother became pregnant for a second time, Erik's father was done with it all. He beat her severely half-way through her pregnancy, and left her for dead. Erik was only two. She lived long enough to deliver Erik's sister, Monica. But due to health complications, his mother didn't survive the process. Erik was now orphaned, with his younger sister as his only link to a family. A family sympathized with the situation, and adopted both Erik and Monica. Despite the efforts of the foster parents, however, the relationship hadn't worked out. Erik and Monica were again up for adoption. But Erik was now old enough to be concious of his sister's existance. And despite his young age, he refused to go to any home without her. The years passed, and Erik and Monica went from home to home and back. But no family was capable of dealing with the two. Often, many considered Monica an ideal child, but Erik was protective, isolated, and respected no authority. The foster life was a plague to him until he was thirteen years old. Erik struck out from the homes with his sister. They ended up back right within their home town. The streets of New York became their home. Erik was a realist about his situation. He was a nothing more then a white child tossed into the concrete death trap of the inner city. And on top of that, he felt it was his duty to protect Monica, and provide for her. So he did the only thing he thought possible. He joined a gang. He needed no home, no school, and no job. It was the perfect solution. He started small, and was considered nothing more then a street punk by most. But as the years passed, Erik grew stronger, and his name became bigger. Monica became worried about Erik. As his reputation grew, so did his enemies. She begged for him to give up the gang life. But in his logic, it was the only way he could provide for her. She had it well. They had a roof to sleep under, good food, good money (as long as the cops didn't find out), and due to the threats from Erik, all the guys kept their hands off of Monica. Erik prospered on the streets for four long years. But tragedy follows Erik like thunder follows lightning. As Erik's rivalries grew more and more heated, Monica became more worried about him. But especially when he would confront his biggest enemy, a rival gang leader named Xavier. Erik grew suspicious. So one fateful night, he made it his business to follow her. Hiding in the shadows, he stalked silently until she made it to her forbidden destination. She had a boyfriend. And that boyfriend... was Xavier. Erik snapped with rage. He pulled his gun out, and decided it was time for a 'break up'. Xavier returned fire. But gun shots weren't the only sounds in the air that night. Monica's screams filled the air. When the smoke cleared, she was bleeding to death on the cold ground. Erik watched in horror as she died in Xavier's arms that night. Something in Erik snapped. He pulled the trigger until the magazine was emptied into Xavier's body. Erik's life was torn asunder in one night. The years passed. Erik haredened to the street. He was forever known as the man who killed Xavier. He made some enemies with that action, but scared off most of them. He was the king of his domain. But he wasn't satisfied.
