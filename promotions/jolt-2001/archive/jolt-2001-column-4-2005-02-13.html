The Mixx Report (02/12/05)

13 February 2005

What this is A column from jOlt Wrestling’s 2005 site, posts/4, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 20 March 2005.
The Mixx Report (02/12/05) Posted by Mike Mixx on 02/13/05: Hello everyone and welcome to the Mixx Report. I’m still Mike Mixx, so don’t worry, the name is sticking and so am I. I’m sure you could see I was in much better spirits last Tuesday then I was the Tuesday before. Damien (Lee) just so happened to let me have a swig of some dwarf spirits before the show, which is usually deemed unprofessional but he knows the stress I’m in, and what some spirits can help me in that state. I was fine for the show, and we were ready to roll! Everyone has been very pleased with the route Tuesday Night Intense has been headed. A lot of new stars are being created in only the first few weeks of our company. A lot of people were afraid that Jolt Wrestling would take the boring route of allowing developed stars to steal the spotlight, but as you seen last Tuesday, we closed with a Triple Crown Tournament match between the six foot nine monster Lusus and former IOW TV Champion “Impact” Rowdy Burns, which did a fine job in “carpe diem”ing their careers. Good show to the both of you. It was also nice to see the return of the Magick Man, who seemed to be in great spirits, despite the fact his client, Geo Vacton, lost to Kenjiro Ito. I asked Magick Man what he felt about Jolt Wrestling and his response was just five words, “Can’t wait for next week.” Couldn’t have said it better myself! For those who didn’t know, there were several members of the Detroit Red Wings in the audience for last Tuesday’s show, including Steve Yzerman, Brendan Shanahan and Chris Chelios. It was nice to see all three, since hockey fans are missing them (Damien is a huge hockey fan. If there was Hockey this year, I doubt he would have ever been able to properly plan the return of Jolt Wrestling! Interviewer Josh Boodreau is as well, as he was once a DJ in British Columbia for a Sports station, PG13. We still like to rib at Josh that the Sedin Twins are alien twins whose purpose was to eat the brains of the opposing team. Of course, if they did their job, Vancouver would have beaten Calgary. Zing!) and we would all love to see the NHL back in action. It was a pleasure to see Yzerman and Shanahan again. I can’t say the same for Chris Chelios, that ignorant punk. Taking jobs from minor leaguers is never respectable in my book. Go play hockey for a charity! A lot more buzz from the internet, this time about the erØn Interview. I received a few e-mails about his comments so I thought I would answer those now: From Jeremy Janisse, Whistler, B.C.: “What is the difference between “Eron” and “erØn”?” Answer: It was symbolism that had much to do with his career. He used to run the gimmick of a Magic: The Gathering character “Eron the Relentless”, and once he was fired from the fWo, wanted to put a new spin to himself. Dropping the nickname, the upper case E and adding a null symbol, he became erØn. He reverted back to being “Eron” once re-entering the fWo and hasn’t gone by erØn since. It was funny when jOlt Wrestling did a House Show in Puerto Rico, and the ring announcer called him “Err Null Ion!”. Ever since that day, Crude would mock him and call him Errnullion. He never liked that. Basically, its just to symbolize his career in jOlt, just like how we use “jOlt” to symbolize Jolt Wrestling in 2001 and 2002. From Jessica Morkin, Marietta, GA: “What was the Chicago Screwjob?” Answer: The Chicago Screwjob was when Erik Rave turned heel on Brandon Youngblood, costing him the jOlt World Heavyweight Title. It was a plan orchestrated by Samantha Bevins to once and for all get the belt off of Damien Lee’s “Ace” Brandon Blade. While she would have preferred the belt on her “Ace” in Graphic Violence, the job was done fine with erØn. Since this was in Chicago, and erØn holding the belt was such a disgusting and repulsive notion at the time, it was dubbed the Chicago Screwjob. From Zach Snyder, Fargo, NC: “What project is Eron talking about? Answer: I have no idea. The only projects Eron was ever involved in, to my knowledge, were the quickly aborted Independent promotion “Renaissance Wrestling” and his faction The Rogues. What either have to do with conditions for Eron to come to Jolt Wrestling is beyond me. A bizarre rumour has been floating around different Wrestling forums that Jolt Wrestling is actually Outcast Championship Wrestling in Florida. I can assure all of you, we’re not. However, I will say, it would be nice to have Kellen Kinkade on the roster. Another bizarre internet rumour I feel compelled to strike down at the neck: Donaven Winters in Jolt Wrestling? It’d take me finishing an entire bottle of dwarf spirits to even SEE that! Winters left jOlt Wrestling on negative terms, and hasn’t made too many friends he could keep from his last tenure in the Fans Wrestling Organization. Now, we’re all about cleansing your sins at the door, but come on, folks. Jolt. Donaven Winters. Wrestling. One of these things, is not like the other. Jolt Wrestling management has been in talks with Konami Studios of the possibility of Dee Dee Rodriguez appearing in commercials for the release of “Dance Dance Revolution With Mario” when it comes stateside for the Nintendo Gamecube. Konami has found DDR’s obsession with… DDR to be an entertaining and commercially pleasing aspect and hopes to have Dee Dee “Dance with Mario”. Crazy! We are sad to announce that the Soul X Mates, Corbis Synn and Ramon O’Connor are no longer a part of Jolt Wrestling. Both have quit due to personal reasons. We hope that Synn and O’Connor can get their issues straightened out and return to Jolt Wrestling, hopefully in time for the 2005-2006 Tag League. Kenjiro Ito took a vicious kick from Geo Vacton on Monday Night in Plymouth. Luckily, the black eye was easily coverable on the show and it wasn’t serious. I should note that Kenjiro Ito has a bizarre obsession with Cup Noodles. He eats around five of them before a match. I think I smell another sponsorship opportunity coming, eh Nissan? For those who need a reminder, this is how the Triple Crown Tournament looks so far: Chris Mezz, Jeff Garvin, Lusus and Kenjiro Ito advance to the Quarter Finals. Next Tuesday Night Intense will feature Jonathan Conspiracy vs. Ken Kaze, Brian Raindal vs. CJ Newfield, Back Alley Brawler vs. Brandon Youngblood and Polar vs. Carson Nash. I must say, Polar and Carson Nash have been heating up against each other and the entire locker room can feel it. As well, will Xias make another return to Jolt Wrestling after eating an M-80? I guess we’ll all have to see, now won’t we? Dear Dave O’Neill: If you ever try to sneak into the arena backstage again and steal a pair of Polar’s tights, we will have to arrest you. You got away with a pair last week, but you won’t next time. We seen the internet pictures. There’s fans and then there are extremists. You need to get some help, son! And that is all I have for you folks. I hope you enjoyed this week ends Mixx Report, because I sure enjoyed writing to ya all. Keep those letters coming! Mixx Mixx, Esq.
