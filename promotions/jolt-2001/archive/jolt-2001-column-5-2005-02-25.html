WRESTLESAULT SPOILERS!

25 February 2005

What this is A column from jOlt Wrestling’s 2005 site, posts/5, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 20 March 2005.
WRESTLESAULT SPOILERS! Posted by Mark Dawkins on 02/25/05: I knew that'd get your attention. Well, hello. Mark Dawkins here, incase you people don't remember me. Yeah, I haven't had much time to recap my show as I've been following the Jolt Wrestling crew around on tour, and it'll end at Wrestlesault. I'll be backstage with my review, though, as the show goes on. Yeah, so I've been avoiding you people to hang with the wrestlers? Doing stuff like this was my dream as a kid, so leave me be. Plus, I get PAID for this stuff. How cool is this? Jolt Wrestling really knows how to treat an employee. And speaking of being treated good, I recently spent some time with former jOlt employee, Eron. Yeah, the slashy O, Eron, but I'm too lazy to find the key for that character. I got his opinion on a couple of the matches going on at Wrestlesault, and some other stuff.. Here's a transcript of what went down: Geo Vacton vs Dawson James? "This is a battle between an annoyance and a chicken shit. ACDC sucked. They use three songs and change the lyrics a bit to repeat albums. I have a feeling Geo Vacton will be the very same. Dawson James gets clones out of himself becuase he is too afraid to step in the ring one on one, so he doesn't have my respect. Bathroom break match." THE KING OF CHARISMA, King Cobra vs. Basic in the FIFTH AND FINAL MATCH of the classic BEST OF FIVE series...kinda. "If I wanted to see two toothpick Japs squirrel around on the mat, I'd have made it two women and ordered a tape. Yawn. I guess to make a prediction, King Cobra will win due to having a better name. Next!" It's obvious Eron doesn't think so highly of the new Jolt talent. But that's okay, because my weekly TNI review say otherwise..or at least the 2 that I've done, so far. himynameis POLAR VS. MR. UNTOUCHABLE, CARSON NASH "Nash reminds me of Mike Bear. Mike would one minute be Mr. Laughmeister and the next be sticking a drill into his skull. Carson is similar, but he can't decide whether he wants to be a Fan Favourite or a big angry bad guy. Switch to decaf, you tard. Polar beats up midgets, so he gets the nod from me." Mike Bear was a former World Champion of another company, you know. It's obvious that Eron's jealous of Carson Nash. Personally, I think you put Nash and Garvin in the ring for 30 minutes, let them wrestle, and see what happens. Garvin vs. Ito is already a MATCH OF THE YEAR canidate, because Garvin can carry any man in this company to a ***** match. Especially these next four... The Match of *ALL* Matches...NINJA FREAKIN' BRIGADE vs. Team KAMI/George "To quote the great Roger Waters, "Get them up against the wall. If I had my way, I'd all have them shot." Especially the furry. NEXT!" ONELTTRBTTR vs. HIS TIME IS NOW "Ah, a match I truly have interest in. I know Jonathan Conspiracy. I watched him wrestle while I was still in jOlt. The man has talent. It'll take every inch of that talent to give Xias a tough battle, and it'll be about giving Xias a tough battle that matters, because he won't be winning. Xias has an unfair edge due to the constant interference and chair shots, but he'll get whats coming to him. Xias is talented. Xias should be in Jolt to show that talent. However, he needs to be tested, and who better to do it then Jonathan Conspiracy? One thing I hope to see is "One Letter Better" break Xias' arm so I won't have to see one more fucking clothesline from him. Take the Stan Hansen/Kenta Kobashi inspiration and shove it up your ass." *THE* TRIPLE CROWN* "Thats a tough one. First you have Chris Mezz, who I would call a "great" brawler, but nothing "great" came out of that uncleaned anus dubbed PIW. Jeff Garvin came from another anus in tSc, but at least that one was washed. I'd call him a "great" talent if he ever faced more competition then Keith "RVD thinks I'm spotty" Scott Zimmerman. Brandon Youngblood is trying to get out of a drought after allowing himself to be owned by a rookie, which I'll get to in a moment. Youngblood used to be a fine athlete until the world realized his job was nothing more then to make choads like Alias look good. Finally, Kenjiro Ito. Ah. I can't wait to see that kid get his first concussion. He'll be crying for mercy then. Too hot for his own good. The only Ice Age thats going to occur is on his own career when hes opening up pup tent Indies in Troy, Michigan. Loser. After all of that negative opinion, I have to give to Jeff Garvin. At least he has at least shown his superior dominance in his matches. Jeff Garvin for the Triple Crown." I then asked him if he had anything else to say. I was hoping for a no, but I got this instead. "Yes. Folks, this isn't Jolt Wrestling. This isn't jOlt Wrestling. This is Damien Lee taking an old trademark and slapping it onto his own puppy. It's like shooting your old dog in the back of the neck and then getting a new puppy and naming it the same. Yeah, I donated the jOlt World Heavyweight Title and am doing these appearances, but hey, the money is nice for a few jack off appearances to give this place some extra credibility. Oh, and Steve from Chatham? Try jumping me again you piece of shit. Out." Now that we've got Eron's predictions out of the way, it's time for something better... *MY* WRESTLESAULT predictions. First up, we've got YOUNGBLOOD vs. "Simply Crazy" Chris Mezz....that would be an awesome fight and all, if Mezz's moveset wasn't absoloutely horrible. Look at me, I'm Chris Mezz, I know an inside cradle, a stone cold stunner, and a brainbuster. Go learn some moves, Mezz, because everyone knows that Newfield would have tore you up if Eron didn't have an infatuation with you. Winner: YOUNGBLOOD, moves on to finals. THE KING OF WRESTLING, JEFF GARVIN vs. WRESTLING'S NEWEST PHENOMENON, FUNAKI v2...doesn't it disturb anyone else that Ito looks like a skinny version of the ENTERTAINMENT's Funaki? Jeff Garvin is the king, and there's no way he's not in the main event, not if Jolt wants an actual good match in the main event. Winner: ORIGINAL DY-NO-MITE vs. DEFUSE KIT DAWSON JAMES...Excuse me? Did someone say James had a clone? Does this remind you of Sting vs. Jarrett where like 50 Stings came out? Oh, what a cool spot that would be...Geo Vacton is underrated. DEFINITELY. Winner: GEO VACTON COBURN P. VALENCIA vs. IMPACT ROWDY BURNS...Rowdy Burns' segment last week wins this match for him. It'd be a shame if he lost. Winner: IRB LUSUS vs. NINJA K...Oh shit, when did this happen? I didn't even know Ninja K debuted yet. Really, they should have put *some* kind of hype around this. Winner: NINJA K ICANTSTANDSTUPIDASIANGAMESLIKEDDR vs. ONCE TRIED TO WRITE SHOW REVIEWS FOR ACTION! WRESTLING, NEVER DO IT AGAIN, PLEASE, PHIL ATKEN...they were horrible, really, they were. And you were a crappy replacement to replace Jeff Garvin as the champion. Winner: DDR THE KING OF ALL THAT IS GOOD, KING COBRA vs. BASIC...incase you didn't know, King Cobra simply RULES Winner: KING COBRA KEVIN NASH AIN'T GOT SHIT ON CARSON vs. POLAR...You know, this match is going to be an end of a feud that was getting pretty dull. I mean, it was pretty much the same thing over and over. Nash has a cooler finisher... Winner: UNTOUCHABLE JONATHAN CONSPIRACY vs. SOMEONE'S JEALOUS OF KENJIRO ITO...I've been hanging around JCON all week and he's a legitamite dick. So, XIAS goes over here. Winner: VORAN XIAS MITTENS T. JERICHO & NEW JACK++ vs. KAMI-KAZE...Incase you people didn't know, I hate Ken Kaze. Winner: NINJA BRIGADE MEZZ VS. ITO...this is for the Ambassador gold, it's only right that Kenjiro Ito gets it and takes it to defend in Japan, or something. Plus, I hate Mezz's moveset. Winner: THE JAPANESE FANS Do you think I'm a little racist? I don't. THE TRIPLE CROWN... Incase you can't figure out who I think is going to win, go back and read over who I've been salivating for this entire column. This is going to be a great show, I'm sure of it. See you after the show, everyone. -MD
