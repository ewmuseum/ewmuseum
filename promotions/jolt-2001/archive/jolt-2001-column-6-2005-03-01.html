WrestleSault I Review Posted by Mark Dawkins on 03/01/05: Jolt Wrestling Presents... WRESTLESAULT As recapped/reviewed by Mark Dawkins. I recorded this thing so I could watch it the first time, and let me tell you, this Pay-Per-View contained possibly the best match I've ever seen in my life. And a couple of other high quality matches. There'll be a ***** match, maybe even two in this one. And a superstar, a wrestling icon is truly born in this one. "Holy shit" seemed to be tonight's theme. And let me tell you, if you're a wrestling fan, you'll find this absoloutely nothing short of spectacular. Tonight, Jolt Wrestling brings you... WrestleSault WrestleSault I, it'll be remembered as. The beginning of everything that is right in the world of wrestling today. Ladies and gentlemen. As you read this review, keep in mind, I'm just a reviewer, but what you see from these men tonight, they're truly legends. Let's get the show underway. Very cool segment. • DAMIEN LEE and MIKE MIXX are your commentators...or so you think. As Mixx announces the matches, BAWITDABA or whatever plays. Kill whoever is coming out for using this theme song. It's WIPPIT GUUD! And luckily for us, the music changes. Wippit comes out and says he'll be the third part of commentary for tonight's show. Mixx has a heartattack. • JULIE MALONE has flowers. They're not from JEFF GARVIN though, and she gets a bit upset. However, she gets mad once they find out who they're form...Oh, KENJIRO ITO, you sly dog, you. • Triple Crown Tournament Semi Finals: BRANDON YOUNGBLOOD w/ MELISSA GARVIN vs. "SIMPLY CRAZY" CHRIS MEZZ[/b] Two fans favorites. Youngblood gets a lot of the cheers in this one. The crowd chants his name. The two scuffle for a bit, before going back and forth At one point in this match, Mezz goes outside and gets a couple of boos. Lots of closed fists from Mezz. They go back and forth and exchange two counts. I hate the two count, I miss the good ol' fashioned one count. I'm sorry, but I hate Mezz's moveset. Go back and forth again, Mezz used lots of closed fits, again, couple of two counts. Mezz quickly in and out, Youngblood dominates for a bit, two count. Really? Where has the art of the one count gone. I never see any one counts anymore. Mezz's lousy moveset almost gets him pinned as Youngblood held the dragon 'plex. Mezz's knockout punch of doom almost gets the 3. Caught Youngblood off guard. Crowd isn't feeling Mezz. I suspect a heel turn. Back body drop THROUGH the table. Good spot. Dropkick off the apron by Youngblood. Seems to me like Mezz should just be in the street fight later, cause he's not wrestling, he's brawling. Back in the ring. Youngblood tries a belly to belly, but Mezz reverses and gets a two count. Rolling Germans by Mezz to bridge, two count. Stunner by Mezz. 2.8. Fans chanting Youngblood's name, I would have preferred CJ Newfield in this match instead of Mezz. Newfield won't even be seen on this show. SUPER SPINEBUSTER from Youngblood. Gets two. Attempted rolling 'plexes by BY but Mezz rolls him up with an inside cradle for two. Mezz takes him up top. Superplex and pin for the finish @ 13:33. That sucked. Or not, ref saw Youngblood's foot at 2.9, so no finish. This is the end for Mezz, it seems. After something like that, you almost always lose. Yup, as the match is restarted, Youngblood dominates and gets a back suplex off the top. Cut throat. Gridlock on Mezz for the submission @ 15:47 ***1/2 - Youngblood looked like a star, but I can't help but dislike Mezz's moveset and the fact that the ending seemed a bit rushed. They show sportsmanship after the match, aww, how cute. • Hype for the upcoming match... • COBURN P. VALENCIA vs. "IMPACT" ROWDY BURNS[/b] Aw, yeah, no disqualification. Just like I like it, baby. Valencia beats on IRB for a bit, and when he tries to get back up, he falls. Possibly a serious injury? Broken nose? CPV tries for the pin, but didn't work. Rowdy gets a quick pin. No. Ref interferres as IRB beats on CPV. Isn't this match no DQ? IRB brutally beats on CPV for a bit before getting a two count. IRB gets mad and bitches out the referee. Tonight, a superstar was born, and his name is Brandon Youngblood, YOUR Jolt Triple Crown Champion. Overall Thoughts: Not only was this show a classic show, but Youngblood vs. Garvin is the best match I have ever witnessed in my life. Such adrenaline. Such character. As you watched the match, you could feel the intensity in the air. Kudos to Garvin on a great match, truely the best pound for pound worker that Jolt has to offer. Kudos to Youngblood as he deserved it. Polar/Nash also get a lot of credit for putting on a great show, as do Team KAMI/Kaze and the Back Alley Brawler. Kenjiro Ito gets a lot of credit too, as Jolt's 1st star created in this show. Just wished they could have paired him up with someone better than Mezz, who has seemingly done nothing up to this point. Great show. Jolt Wrestling is now on the map.
