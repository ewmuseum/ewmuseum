What this is The Tuesday Night Intense page of jOlt Wrestling’s 2005 site, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 8 February 2005, announcing the weekly Tuesday show on BroadcastTV and the Total Impact! house-show tour.
What do you get when you take one of the greatest wrestling shows in Pro Wrestling history, breathe new life into it, and take it off Sundays and onto the hottest new network on TV? Tuesday Night Intense, coming to you every Tuesday Night at 9PM EST, 6PST on BroadcastTV!
