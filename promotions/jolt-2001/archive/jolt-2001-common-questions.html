What this is The common-questions page of jOlt Wrestling’s first season, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 9 January 2002. Its closing answer names the site’s designer and gives a messaging handle; both are redacted.
:: Common Questions :: questiOn What does jOlt mean? answer jolt (jlt) v. jolt·ed, jolt·ing, jolts. v. tr. To disturb suddenly and severely jOlt Wrestling is just that. When discussing the federations name, we wanted something that sounded swift, yet proffesional. Something that stood out, but didn't take half a page to fit the name in. At jOlt we want to quickly stun the e-wrestling fan by interactivity. Visual eyecandy and stunning storylines. We want to grab your attention quickly, hense the name jOlt. questiOn Why do you spell Jolt in lower case lettering and with a capital "o"? answer Jolt is spelt jOlt simply to grab attention and originality. Some decide to capitalize the middle consanent in a three consenant name. We are similar, but use the whole word: jOlt. questiOn What makes jOlt different from other eFederations? answer We like to believe that we are different, but we're not. jOlt Wrestling is just like the million other organizations on the web today, but we try to offer more to the handler and fan. Thus, we provide more visual eyecandy and multimedia to allow something fresh all the time. questiOn When will jOlt Wrestling accept new handlers? answer jOlt Wrestling scouted its original roster well before accepting anybody. If you were active or participated in federations advertised on such sites as eWCenter, you probably were looked at, but maybe didn't fit in for the first few months of planning. jOlt Wrestling is always looking for new talent; therefore, we will offer a Waiting List very soon. New handlers are usually brought in every couple months to strengthen our Fan Base and allow every handler a chance at jOlt. To be accepted you must show relative interest in the federation and know what jOlt is about. A handler who knows jOlt Wrestling storyline wise and can point out where he could fit in, he would have more of a chance then a fan handler who couldn't name five characters on the roster. questiOn Can I use your a jOlt character for my eWrestler? answer jOlt Wrestling creates its images for members only. To get an image, apply for the Waiting List and wait for your opportunity. The graphics and 3D art that are created for jOlt are copyrighted under jOlt-wrestling.com and belong to the handler. questiOn Can j0lt design me a site? answer jOlt Wrestling don't design sites, we are an e-fed. However, the designer of the current site, [name withheld] can be emailed here or can be contacted on aim under the name of [detail withheld].
