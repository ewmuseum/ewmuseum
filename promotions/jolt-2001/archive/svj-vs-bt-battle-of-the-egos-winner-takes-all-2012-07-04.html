J The Ripper — 4 July 2012 "THE MAN THAT BRINGS HIS OWN SPOTLIGHT!" vs. "THE DEFINITION OF EGO!" EGO vs. EGO WINNER TAKES ALL WHO WILL BE THE EGO?! ::Tale of the Tape:: SVJ Multiple time champion. Including multiple top tier titles. Even his moves have the word "Star" in them. A man that only cares about gold and all the riches that comes with "being the man". Willing to go to any extreme to "Be the Man." BT A man that will go to the same exact extremes as SVJ to "Be the man." Bought an entire company just so he could hold the top title. Would kick his own grandmother down a flight of stairs and follow it with an elbow drop to be champ.
Has undermined and destroyed a company just to have the right to compete for the title again.
When these two clash (as long as there aren't any mirrors around) who will walk away as the TRUE Ego? Will SVJ's experience prove to be too much for BT or will the almost endless supply of BT's money steal him yet another title, even if it's just a moniker? You decide!
