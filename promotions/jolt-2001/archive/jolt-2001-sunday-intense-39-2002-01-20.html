Sunday iNtense 39 Troubled Emotions New Orleans, Bourbon Street A place with the debauchery is just as rampant as the alcoholism. I sit here, on a bench next to a half-blind man with a dog. He's dressed in rags, sipping Jack Daniels from a styrofoam cup. The dog sleeps lazily at his feet. We sit here as intoxicated college students regurgitate their mixture of vodka and 7.99 All You Can Eat Crawdaddy Buffet all around us. Me and the man, stuck in our own worlds. Completely oblivious to things that exist. This man, I have to wonder; does he know where he is going to sleep? Does he know where his next meal is going to come from? Does he have any relatives that care about him? I sit here and watch him as he quietly sips his drink. In the expanse of my mind, I can hear all the background noise. The music. The people. The general hustle and bustle. I keep watching him. He finishes the drink and thanks me for buying it for him. I simply nod. He stands, wrapping the tattered plastic leash around his arm and wanders off into the crowd with the Irish Wolfhound. He disappears. And now I am alone. Left to my thoughts of the things that should not be. Lighting a cigarette, I watch the crowd and think about people. About jOlt. About the Mafia... About the Don. The Don. Now there was a complete waste of human flesh. The poster boy for birth control. The Don was not evil. He was not malicious. He wasn't even threating. With his heavy Italian lisp, constantly cocked eyebrow; that smoldering cigar stuck between his chubby cream filled fingers, was the epitome of everything that was pathetic. He was processed. He was fake. He was the antitheisis of what a villain should be. If anyone should by running the Mafia, it should be me. I am tired of walking behind a man with no direction. No purpose. No depth. I flick the remaining ash from the cigarette and chuck it at the ground, staring into the concrete, looking for something that isn't there. The Don's reign was going to come to an end. An extremely abrupt halt. And I am the man that will do it. And no one can stop me. Not the Don. Not Damian Lee. Not even Brandon Blade.
