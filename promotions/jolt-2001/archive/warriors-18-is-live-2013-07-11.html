Seth — 11 July 2013 There was quite a bit of fun to be had on this most recent show, so I've decided to offer some two cents since it doesn't seem like a lot of these shows get the love they deserve.
--Tramel is a goddamned genius. I mean that in the nicest of ways. His openings for the shows are really well done and make you feel like you're watching a wrestling show. Anything that captures a little bit of that kind of realism goes a long way with me.
--The Derecho/Aran/Turnbull story has been really interesting to me. It seemed when Aran couldn't get the job done, he made some sort of backdoor deal to get the Relentless Title back. Waymoth is a GREAT character and Derecho is spot on as always. Derecho's the asshole here, but he may be getting played by other assholes and perhaps a double turn could be in the works? Aran and Turnbull both look very comfortable at the top of the card with Derecho. Color me intrigued.
--See first comment. I have always loved reading pretty much anything that Tramel puts out. Matches, segments, you name it. He is a master of prose, he knows his characters well, and the Crimson Order (as well as the Jury) had a great outing this show. I demand Heirs/Crimson Order action down the line, sir, if even just for an Eiji/Gallway cruiserweight blowout alone.
--Poor Kayden. Love that character, but a nice dominating win by Turnbull to continue his badassery.
--Having Tony on your roster is a good thing since his characters tend to be pretty awesome. Good way of getting some of the shine back to ReVolt (who are also interesting to me) and carrying the feud forward.
--From reading his MWA and this segment, there's a lot of potential to Sven Deadly that I hope to see come out. While Sylo and Ninja K are the predominan stars of the Underground, I'd definitely like to see more stars rise in that place and this could definitely be the character's calling. Nice segment and reintroduction here. The split personalities thing is cool as well and hope to see that played up.
--Kudos to Markus and Brett for the story here and the way this was pulled off was nicely done. Thomas either planned to win the belts or get leverage on the beastly tag team champions (and in that sense, the latter may be the better deal). Markus (when not being a douche) has a great knack for building his characters up and I could imagine this segment play out on TV with the guy losing his wife. Looking forward to the end game here.
--Jacobs on a Jericho-esque path to the top is nicely done and Ed pulls this off well. Good win and his feud with Mike Extreme has a lot of legs to be something fun. Would like to see Extreme get a couple of wins under his belt also, murdering a couple bitches en route to his upcoming date with SVJ.
--I've read the last couple segments with Jon Le Bon and they've been pretty funny. I'd definitely like to see more of him. SETH'S FREE BOOKING ANGLE: Jon Le Bon as the third member of Devastation Inc and freebirds that shit.
--Won't comment too much on my own stuff other than I hope people have enjoyed the Heirs/SSL feud. It was fun really building up to this match over the last couple shows. SSL are a really fun tag team that I think have a Cryme Tyme-esque vibe to them that works. Hopefully this added a little more legitimacy to them. (as legit as a modernized hippie and a fat deluded ladies man could have).
Ugh. Good show by all involved. Would've liked to have seen a little more involvement from a couple of the Thieves Honor participants, but we've still got a ways to go until then.
Good stuff, guys! Derecho — 11 July 2013 Heirs of Wrestling vs Crimson Order? A match written by both Seth and Tramel? I can't book it.. I.. I.. just can't. I'm thinking of others when I say that... if that match were to happen, the world's population won't have heads anymore because they will all explode from the sheer awesomeness of that match
Residntantihero — 13 July 2013 Hehehe....I find that match up intriguing. Looking forward to having that happen. Sooner than later
