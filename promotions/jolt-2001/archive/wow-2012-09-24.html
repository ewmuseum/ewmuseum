eron — 24 September 2012 So [name withheld] showed me this a few weeks back. I was... pretty shocked. Didn't think of saying anything until now.
E-Wrestling feels so foreign to me now. As if it was a life I never lived. I know I wasted a good portion of my young teenage life writing stories and procrastinating on writing matches but my memories of it all are pretty fragmented. It has been so long since I had any involvement in it.
Seeing the Jolt page and some familiar faces on the roster was a nice trip down memory lane. Eric seemed a little annoyed by it all but I found it awesome. I couldn't give a fuck if it was our intellectual property or anything like that. It's quite cool that Jolt Wrestling is still alive, even if I have no part in it. I was always just keeping it on life support anyway. I'll probably tell JoBo about this, he'll be floored that Chris Titan is still in existence.
You guys have probably moved on from the old Jolt history but feel free to use it as much as you like. It's nice to see a belt called the Relentless Title and that someone wasted their time in Poser to create an Eron avatar. You guys were doing whatever you wanted to anyway but keep on writing and enjoying yourselves.
Anyway, hope all are doing well. Have a good one! Eron North! — 24 September 2012 Oh. I'm sorry, I think you think the title is named after Eron.
