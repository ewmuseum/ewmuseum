A sports entertainment icon. A private investigator. A newspaper columnist. An aspiring documentary maker. All these things and so much more. If ever a man was born to be a star, it is Trey Vincent. Your new Franchise Player is here. But who is the man under the winter hat, which he wears during all seasons, even summer? The man wearing self-promoting T-shirts for his private investigation business, T.V.P.I. (1-800-999-8874, or 1-800-XXX-TVPI)? First of all, Trey Vincent should be a star right now. And he's willing to find fame in any fashion, be it through sports entertainment or some sort of television show. Trey is all about entertainment. He is a man obsessed with fame. He is obsessed with being on television. His initials, TV, scream that since birth he was meant to be a star. In the ring, Trey's only goal is to entertain and get paid. Trey Vincent was born March 23, 1976 in Minneapolis, Minnesota. All that is known about his history at this time is that for a period of years, Trey amassed a great deal of debt. All he owns is a vehicle, a Nissan Pathfinder. Besides that, he claims to have no possessions. He doesn't even know if his parents are still alive. He is also part Irish, and blames his recurring troubles with alcohol on that. At time Trey got his first break into the business, Trey was broke. He was living in his Pathfinder and struggling to make ends meet. Desperate for work, he had fallen back into his back-up job as a private investigator. He had all the training. He tracked down missing people, caught cheating couples, found criminals and anything else that came his way, as long as he got paid. But it was his goal to be on television. So he took a gamble. It had been a dream of his to become a sports entertainer. Not a wrestler, a sports entertainer. Let's get that straight first. The difference between wrestling and sports entertainment is money, fame and glory. Wrestling makes stars. But sports entertainment makes icons. Sports entertainment makes superstars. Sports entertainment takes ordinary human beings and makes them become bigger than life itself. People worship sports entertainers. Idiots bring signs to shows that say Trey Vincent Is God. And if God were a wrestler, he would be Trey Vincent. He doesn’t pretend to be a sports entertainer, he simply is one. But at this time, certain people have a monopoly on the business and sports entertainment jobs were scarce. He decided to start in the business at the bottom, paying his dues in a hardcore wrestling federation, a.k.a. bush-league. Not enough exposure for Trey. But he did it for the money. He sent out tapes to some people in the business, as well as the FOX channel, in hopes of landing either a sports entertainment spot or a reality television series based on his adventures as a private investigator. The fed liked what they saw and signed him. He took the gig. It paid. That was all he cared about at this point. After all, when you're living in your vehicle, you take what you can get. Still desperate for cash and hoping to sell his show to a network, he kept both jobs, and as luck would have it, he was able to both film interviews to hype up matches and maintain his investigations. Eventually after a meeting with FOX, he learned they had enough reality shows for the upcoming fall season. No other networks were biting and he didn't have any contacts. But that all changed when he met Taylor Thompson. Taylor is a retired model. She lives in a secluded mansion with a driveway longer than some towns. Two men hovering around the 7-foot mark guard the home. Initially, she contacted him to protect her body from a stalker at the end of her modeling career. In return, she promised to get him in contact with one of her friends in the television business. She allowed him to live in her mansion during the arrangements, and as a bonus, she would teach him how to improve his out of the ring behavior. She convinced him the way to live is to dominate the sports entertainment industry and also have a mansion, cars for every day of the week, a pool, your own employees, and whatever else his heart desired. To be rich, successful and happy. To hire people to beat up peons who beg for an autograph. The two had an instant attraction and more blossomed rather quickly and business relationship turned personal. To an extent. Trey continued to protect her body and foiled not one, but two stalkers. After breaking into the business, Trey received a phone call from an acquaintance back in Minnesota who asked him to write a weekly column for Minneapolis Today, a local newspaper. Yet another avenue had opened up to Trey Vincent. He took it and would use it to share his thoughts on events in the world, his opponents and reader questions. Meanwhile, Trey was still waiting for Taylor to live up to her end of the agreement, a meeting with a television executive. Eventually, it happened, and Damian Graziano entered the picture. When Trey and Damian met, Damian was working for HBO. But before the end of the conversation, Damian was fired and took a new job with TLC. He promised to allow Trey to create his show, called 'T.V.P.I.' And then there were tough times. The extreme fed folded, and he found himself in an even lower budget federation. It was the type of show you’d be lucky to randomly come across at 2:30 a.m. on a Spanish channel. The pay was bad, the roster was bad, and Trey now found himself alone. It has been unclear to this point what broke up Trey and Taylor, but it happened. And things haven’t been good since for Trey. He felt fame fleeting away. Trey’s goal is to force himself into every television he possibly can. Preferably a network so his greatness is not just limited to cable subscribers. He should be on your TV every week. Trey Vincent is entertainment. He will give you entertainment the likes of which you have never seen. His charisma is only matched by his ability to put on the greatest sports entertainment matches on the card. He can carry 1-star talent to 5-star matches. He is the man you build your company around. All are fakers when compared to everyone’s TV Champion. Trey will show the world who belongs on TV and who doesn’t. You wanna spike the ratings? Advertise Trey as being on the card. You want to sell out an arena? But his name up in lights. You all should bow down and worship your television screen when he comes on. With a face like his, with charisma like his, he was born a superstar, lives like a superstar and will die like a superstar. Television delivers his greatness into every home in the country. Trey is what every fan out there can never be, a winner. Every fan can live vicariously through Trey so their failed lives don't seem so bad. The guy who created television must have known that one day a man named TV would one day rule TV, and be everyone's TV champion, regardless if he has a title or not. Unless you’re on television, nobody cares who you are. You are a nobody. He is an example for others to follow. A shining example of what this sport has to offer, a person others should use as an example if they want to know what success is. Anyone who talks about him is in the company of greatness. Maybe it'll be easier on you all when you lose if you just pretend you're Trey Vincent. If you were to compare Trey Vincent to any animal, it would be a mountain lion. A solitary, strongly territorial hunting species, he doesn't need anybody to survive. The male spends a lot of time marking his large territory. Mountain lions can be very dangerous animals. Some have been known to injure or even kill people but they tend to avoid humans unless cornered and threatened or suddenly surprised. Trey is the hunter. This is his game. And whoever is across from him in the ring is his victim. This mountain lion only knows one direction, and that's up. Even his name means winner. The name Trey means three. Three is the most important number in sports entertainment? Why? Because three means victory. Thus, his first name means victory. As for the name Vincent? Well, besides the fact that it is the name of the king of sports entertainment, the name itself means conqueror or victor. Again, it means, winner. There are only two roads in this life. And if you’re listening to anyone other than Trey Vincent, the most outrageous sports entertainer today, you’re on the wrong road. Anyone disagree? Well, he’ll give you the sports entertainment lesson of a lifetime. He’ll give them their 15 minutes of fame. But 15 minutes of fame just is not enough for Trey Vincent.
