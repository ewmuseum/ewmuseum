jOlt Character Summaries

1 July 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Notorei — 1 July 2012 Jolt Character Summaries: Note: for quick reference Latest Update: Warriors 03 I. Adam Lazarus a. W01: i. Defeated by Reno Davis with a submission. ii. Attacked backstage by Bane Loneheart with a pipe, which he had done on Intense 35 to Brandon Blade.
b. I36: i. Defeated Bane Loneheart in a quick upset that eliminated Bane from the Underground tournament.
c. I37: i. Attacked in his locker room by Bane Loneheart. Damien Lee ultimately suspended him once he was seen hitting Bane with the same pipe.
ii. Defeated by Mike Extreme in third round of Underground tourney by forfeit thanks to Damien suspending him.
II. Alexander Jerusalem a. I35: i. Defeated Brandon Blade in Loser Leaves Jolt match by disqualification when Sylo speared him into the corner, causing the ring to collapse. He was then finished off by Sylo with a Systematic Shutdown.
b. I36: i. Attacked Sylo backstage with the help of Bane Loneheart. He ends up getting pulverized by Sylo.
III. American Panda a. W03: i. Defeated Manzo Tanaka via pinfall. IV. Aria Murphy a. W01: i. Defeated by G.G. Gentry by pinfall with the help of the West Texas Terrorists. V. Avispa Ultima a. W01: i. Defeated both Sanchez Cano & Cordova in a Flyweight Championship bout with a pinfall to Cano.
VI. Bane Loneheart a. I35: i. Defeated by One Eye in first round of Underground Tournament. ii. Attacked Brandon Blade backstage with a pipe prior to his match. b. W01: i. Attacked Adam Lazarus backstage with a pipe, same as he did to Brandon Blade on Intense 35.
c. I36: i. Attacked Sylo backstage with help of Alexander Jerusalem. Sylo ends up hitting Bane in the knee with his pipe, before pushing him out the door to finish up AJ.
ii. Defeated by Adam Lazarus in a quick upset, eliminating him from the Underground tourney.
d. I37: i. Attacked Adam Lazarus, with a pipe, in his locker room. Laz was able to take the pipe and fight back, only to have Damien Lee see this and suspend him.
e. W03: i. Defeated by Kenjiro Ito via submission. VII. BIG Little Italy a. W01: i. Defeated by Derecho via knockout in Underground title match. Was given a spinebuster through a chair when the match was over.
VIII. Brandon Blade a. I35: i. Attacked backstage by Bane Loneheart, with a pipe to his knee, prior to his main event match.
ii. Defeated by Alexander Jerusalem in Loser Leaves Jolt main event in a DQ when Sylo was scene spearing AJ which caused the ring to collapse. Before this though, SVJ interfered and superkicked him.
IX. Broken a. I35: i. Hit Sylo with a hummer before being beaten pretty bad. He had been sent by Jim Johnson.
X. Citizen a. W01: i. Defeated Jason Rau by knockout in the first round of Underground tourney. b. W02: i. Defeated Mike Extreme in second round of Underground tourney by knockout. XI. Clan Avispa a. W02: i. Defeated Fueled By Ignorance by pinfall. XII. Cordova a. W01: i. Defeated, along with brother Sanchez Cano, by Avispa Ultima in a triple threat match for the Flyweight title. Cano was pinned.
XIII. Damien Lee a. I36: i. Came out to fire Brandon Blade even though he didn’t want to. Mandates that nobody can attack anybody else outside of a sanctioned match. Finally books SVJ vs Sylo in Legacy title match.
a. W03:[/INDENT] i. Board assigned Rune Winters to help enforce jOlt policy. XIV. Derecho a. I35: i. He seemingly snaps mentally as he obliterates Rune Winters in the Underground Championship match. A bloody, twisted match which he pushed the envelope.
b. W01: i. Defeated BIG Little Italy by knockout in an Underground title match. Assaulted a few referees in the process and gave BLI a spinebuster through a chair once the match was over.
c. W02: i. Open invitation to any of the eliminated wrestlers in the Underground tournament. ii. Defeated Jason Rau by knockout in non-title Underground match. XV. Fueled By Ignorance a. I35: i. Defeated by Heirs of Wrestling. ii. Titan was frustrated backstage with Register for their loss. b. I36: i. Titan attacked Register backstage, but didn’t finish the job, opting instead to leave him beaten backstage.
c. W02: i. Defeated by Clan Avispa by pinfall. ii. Titan attacked Reno Davis with a chair after he told him to get his head out of his ass.
d. W03: i. Defeated by One Eye & Phoenix by pinfall. ii. Titan hit Register after the match. XVI. G.G. Gentry a. W01: i. Bumps into Aria Murphy backstage, trash talks, and winds up being challenged to a match later in the night.
ii. Defeated Aria Murphy by pinfall with the help of the West Texas Terrorists, which was Nashvillain’s plan.
XVII. Greg Vincent a. I37: i. Defeated by Ray Chavez via Disqualification when he hit him with his iconic steel chair.
ii. After the loss, laid out referee Darius Underwood with another chair shot. b. W03: i. Hit Ray Chavez with iconic chair only to be slammed head first into wall by Jason Rau after.
XVIII. Grendel a. I36: i. Defeated Ray Chavez by countout after giving him a powerbomb from ring to floor. XIX. Heirs of Wrestling a. I35: i. Defeated Fueled By Ignorance. b. I36: i. Defeated RingRats by pinfall. c. I37: i. Defeated Sweet, Sweet Lovin’ by pinfall. XX. Jason Rau a. W01: i. Defeated by Citizen via knockout in first round of Underground tourney. b. I36: i. Defeated by Khristain Keller via pinfall in Underground match. c. W02: i. Defeated by Derecho via knockout in non-title Underground match. d. W03: i. Slammed Greg Vincent head first into a wall after he tried attacking him with a steel chair.
XXI. Jim Johnson a. I35: i. Booked Brandon Blade in Loser Leaves Jolt match for championship against Alexander Jerusalem.
ii. Attacked by Sylo. A brutal beatdown ending with a powerbomb from the ring through the announce table.
XXII. Jonathan Conspiracy a. I35: i. Was attacked in-ring by the West Texas Terrorists prior to his match with Nashvillain.
ii. Defeated by Nashvillain in Relentless title match after West Texas Terrorists attacked him prior.
b. W02: i. Defeated Rush by pinfall for International championship. XXIII. Kayden Paulton a. I35: i. Attacked backstage by Mike Extreme prior to their scheduled match. ii. Defeated by Mike Extreme via forfeit after brutal attack. XXIV. Kenjiro Ito a. W02: i. Got arrested after appearing in the ring with three retired Jolt titles, trying to get a contract because he was the last triple crown champion.
b. I37: i. Defeated Phoenix by pinfall. c. W03: i. Defeated Bane Loneheart via submission. XXV. Khristain Keller a. I36: i. Defeated Jason Rau by pinfall in Underground match. b. I37: i. Defeated One Eye by pinfall. c. W03: i. Defeated Mike Extreme in Underground tourney match via knockout. XXVI. Manzo Tanaka a. I36: i. Defeated Sam Sweet by pinfall. b. W03: i. Defeated by American Panda via pinfall. XXVII. Marcus Davis a. I36: i. In ring self introduction followed by longtime rival ‘Hollywood’ Vince Matthews interruption.
XXVIII. Mike Extreme a. I35: i. Attacked Kayden Paulton backstage prior to their schedule match. ii. Defeated Kayden Paulton by forfeit because of earlier attack. b. W02: i. Defeated by Citizen in second round of Underground tourney via knockout. c. I37: i. Defeated Adam Lazarus by forfeit in the third round of the Underground tourney. d. W03: i. Defeated by Khristain Keller in Underground tourney match via knockout. XXIX. Nashvillain a. I35: i. Defeated JCON by pinfall after West Texas Terrorists attacked him prior to match. b. I37: i. Defeated by Sylo via disqualification when the ref called the match for the excessive beatdown by him and the West Texas Terrorists. SVJ also interfered, but Sylo fought them all off, with Nash leaving the ring before Sylo could get to him.
XXX. One Eye a. I35: i. Defeated Bane Loneheart in first round match of Underground Tourny. b. I36: i. Attacked backstage by mystery man. Phoenix came to his save, albeit too late. c. W02: i. Defeated by Reno Davis with a submission in second round of Underground tourney. d. I37: i. Defeated by Khristain Keller via pinfall. e. W03: i. Defeated Fueled By Ignorance with Phoenix via pinfall. XXXI. Phoenix a. I36: i. Found One Eye attacked backstage by a mystery man. He immediately suspected Derecho.
b. I37: i. Defeated by Kenjiro Ito via pinfall. c. W03: i. Defeated Fueled By Ignorance with One Eye via pinfall. XXXII. Ray Chavez a. I36: i. Returns with a crappy contract, in debt, broke, and wearing a mask to cover his scarred face. He even gets swindled into having former LoC color commentator Keith Kane as his manager.
ii. Defeated by Grendel via countout after being powerbombed from ring onto floor. b. I37: i. Defeated Greg Vincent by Disqualification when the Megastar hit him in the back of the head with his iconic steel chair causing blood to be spilt.
c. W03: i. Hit backstage by Greg Vincent with iconic chair while trying to save Jason Rau. XXXIII. Reno Davis a. W01: i. Defeated Adam Lazarus by submission in first round Underground tourney. b. W02: i. Defeated One Eye by submission in second round Underground tourney. XXXIV. RingRats a. I36: i. Defeated by Heirs of Wrestling via pinfall. XXXV. Rune Winters a. I35: i. Defeated and destroyed by Derecho in their Underground title match. He was left blood and beaten, after his opponent attacked him after the match was over.
XXXVI. Rush a. W02: i. Showed up drunk or high as Damien Lee persuaded him to get help. ii. Defeated by JCON with a pinfall, losing his International title. XXXVII. Sam Sweet a. I36: i. Defeated by Manzo Tanaka by pinfall. XXXVIII. Sanchez Cano a. W01: i. Defeated, along with Cordova, in a match against Avispa Ultima in a Flyweight title match. Cano was pinned.
XXXIX. Sweet Sweet Lovin' a. I37: i. Defeated by Heirs of Wrestling via pinfall. XL. Sylo a. I35: i. Hit with a hummer driven by Broken. He was sent by Jim Johnson, part of a plan that didn’t work. He ends up beating Broken within an inch of his life.
ii. Demolished Jim Johnson in the ring, ultimately with a powerbomb from the ring through the announce table.
b. I36: i. Kicks Natalia out of his locker room only to get attacked by Alexander Jerusalem and Bane Loneheart. He fights both men off with AJ getting the worst of it.
ii. Defeated Vince Jacobs in Legacy title match by DQ, after Natalia threw SVJ his title and he repeatedly hit Sylo with it to escape the ‘No Escape” submission.
c. I37: i. Brawled with SVJ backstage only to be separated by several wrestlers and officials.
ii. Defeated Nashvillain by disqualification after The West Texas Terrorists, Nash, and SVJ wouldn’t stop the beatdown. He ultimately fought them off and saw SVJ scared for the first time, checkmate.
XLI. Vince Jacobs a. I35: i. Interfered by giving Brandon Blade a superkick in his main event match. b. I36: i. Defeated by Sylo via DQ after hitting him several times in the head with his Legacy title to get him to release the No Escape submission.
c. I37: i. Brawled with Sylo backstage after seeing Natalia crying after speaking to him. Several wrestlers and officials had to separate them both.
ii. Interfered in Sylo v. Nashvillain, only to see Sylo fight them all off. Sylo noticed him showing fear as he backpeddled up the ramp.
XLII. Vince Matthews a. I36: i. Interrupted longtime rival Marcus Davis in the ring. XLIII. West Texas Terrorists a. I35: i. Attacked JCON & Sweet Aroma before his match with Nashvillain for the Relentless Championship.
ii. Again attacked JCON after his match, putting the boots to him. b. I37: i. Helped in the main event showing Nashvillain v. Sylo. Their excessive beating of Sylo caused the ref to call the match a dq. They were on the tail end of Sylo’s trademark and finisher.
