TNI1 Wrap-Up Posted by Mark Dawkins on 02/03/05: So, nobody e-mailed me about my Mardi Gras report. It kind of hurt my feelings. I'd like to know if you people read these things. Oh well, it got posted about a week after the show aired. This time, though, I'm taking time out of watching AMERICAN IDOL to review watch this show. Couldn't they have picked a better night for Intense? Wednsday? Nothing good is on on Wednsday. Oh well, on to the show... Jolt Wrestling: Tuesday Night Intense 1 Wrap-Up(02/01/04) • Live from the Cajun Dome in New Orleans, LA • Recap of the Mardi Gras PPV happenings between Jonathan Conspiracy and Chris Mezz. Mezz shows up, calls JCon a puss, and challenges him for next week, or, tonight's show. • Your hosts are Damian Lee and Mike Mixx. They bickered a bit, Mixx looked like he didn't really care, Lee announces that tonight JCon vs. Mezz, and American Hero III will be live in the house tonight to announce Block A of the Triple Crown Tournament. Mezz picks "The Original" Jeff Garvin to win the tournament. I don't doubt it. • Lee is interrupted by the eery music and weird promo stating that someone is coming. That's cool, I guess. I put my money on Ultimo Dragon. • Unknown Elements vs. Soul X Mates The debut of "Simply Perfect" Shawn Arrows as one half of Unknown Elements, with Carlos Lopez. I've rarely seen any of Arrows and Lopez is unknown in my book. Soul X Mates contains Ramon O'Connor and Corbis Synn, two other guys I've never heard of. Arrows got a few boos, but the crowd was pretty much dead for this match. SXM came in and rushed at UE, but UE turned it around and stomped on them. Arrows and O'Connor started. They scuffled around for a bit with a couple of lock-ups but nobody really coming off victorious. Arrows tried to take advantage, O'Connor reversed. Couple of kicks to the forearm from O'Connor who had a lock on Arrow's arm. Arrows reversed and got a lock on O'Connor's arm. They mess around a little bit more before. Couple of more back and forth manuevers, rollup, kickout. Match has bored me pretty much. They go back and forth, eventually with Arrows locking on a Tarantula. Some more back and forth action, some more boredom. Couple of people in the crowd booed and yelled. Couple of pinfall attempts. Back when I was a wrestler, I used to be a tag teamer, and this, my friends, is definitely not what you want in a tag team match. Arrows pretty much dominates the rest of the time. At this point I'm wondering what the hell Lopez and Synn are doing near the ring. This would be a decent singles match, but it's not. Ah, finally, tag into Synn. Synn immediately went uptop and connected with a frog splash as Arrows went for the tag. Pin attempt but interruption by Lopez with a nice springboard legdrop. Match is starting to pick up. Low blow from Arrows on Synn as the ref got Lopez out of the ring. Tag to Lopez. A slap and then some nice double team work by UE. Tag back to Arrows. Arrows dominated and dared O'Connor to come into the ring and he did but was quickly stopped by the referee. Lopez in. Double team. Pin attempt but broken up by O'Connor. From this point on, Arrows and Lopez use lots of nice TAG TEAM strategy and eventually get the win with a shooting star @ 18:55. 3/4* -- There was a part of the match where it really picked up but the first 10 minutes bored me, and the last 2 minutes just seemed like UE were toying around with SXM. Arrows and Lopez are just starting though, and I'm sure sooner or later they'll be working well as a team. • JCon out to announce the winner of the eBay Auction for the Jolt International Title. JCon announces someone from New Orleans won the auction, but that he ripped him off and is still the International Champion. OHH BURRRRRN. Crowd goes apeshit and JCon gets a couple of death threats. • Cuts backstage to a casino...err...the lockeroom. Mittens T. Cat walks in flipping a coin and points at the Back Alley Brawler, who is the house in the lockeroom gambling thing. "You, you're in my sweet new tag team called the Ninja Brigade, you dig?" God, I love Mittens. BAB is also pretty flippin' sweet (what? I've seen Napolean Dynamite) too. BAB says he'll beat Mittens ass, and Mittens says he'd do the same, but he doesn't want to. Mittens sees potential in BAB. He says BAB could be a sweet ninja. Mittens gets in a cute ballsack joke. BAB says he can kick ass but what can Mittens do? Purr someone to death? BEST. SEGMENT. EVER. Mittens says he knows Kenjiro Ito is gonna be around to make a bet on a certain match, and that he'll lose. BAB puts his arm around MTC and the two leave on good terms. Oh, that sneaky Mittens. Sneaky sneaky. Holy crap. • Jeff Garvin, and a chorus of boos, pounded on a door. Brandon Youngblood opened, and Garvin made a bit of fun at his PRIME tag team partner. Garvin says he wants to bond with Youngblood as they are teamsters in PRIME. Youngblood says PRIME is using him to boost Garvin to champion status instead of being a has-been that's working the mid-card. Garvin says he's held more titles than Youngblood ever will. Youngblood says Garvin's career is fading and his is picking up. Garvin says at WrestleSault, if they're competing against each other, Youngblood is gonna be looking up at the lights. Lala, does anyone know what PRIME is? Staredown of doom as the camera fades. • King Cobra vs. Basic King Charasmatic is down first, slapping hands with the crowd in a very...green...entrance. Basic comes down next, and looks very uninterested in anything. Quick staredown led to a tieup. Double pushes led to a double shoulderblock. Cobra dropkicks Basic to the outside. Baseball slide onto Cobra. The crowd absoloutely adores King Cobra. He's got a certain swagger to him. Cobra got back and went up top for an asai moonsault. Cobra gets up and greets the crowd. 6 count before Basic was back in the ring. Staredown. Basic trips up Cobra and goes for a submission before letting go. Basic picks Cobra up, whips him into the corner, trys a jumping knee attack but misses. Cobra works on the arm. Whips Basic into the corner, and hits the jumping knee attack. Cobra tries another whip, Basic reverses, strong arm clothesline. Forearm smash by Basic. Spinning DDT. Pinfall. Two Count. Basic attacks Cobra a bunch, Cobra staggering around, Basic tries his finisher, Cobra reverses. German Suplex. Moonsault. Pinfall. Kickout. Not too much else going on. Another moonsault attempt by Cobra, Basic, however, caught him and hit him with a powerbomb. Pinfall. Almost 3. Basic picked Cobra up, Ametheyst Driver. Match @ 10:05 * - Decent match, nothing special. Both had their moments. • We go back to BAB who is taking bets, and looking pretty happy. Someone comes in and puts $3000 on Kenjiro Ito to take Brandon Youngblood in their match tonight. BAB makes fun of him, and the man says he is Kenjiro Ito and he's confident. BAB pretty much says he has no chance to win. BAB says the odds of him winning are 1 to 25. Ito says he's a high roller and is gonna bet 5K$. Stud. Just a stud. • A short video clip of ex-Jolt wrestlers and we get an interview from an ex-Tag Team Champion, Persephone. She says she was nervous first entering Jolt in 2001. She says that things could have been a lot better in Jolt, but things were decided at the last minute. She says that she's been in other federations since Jolt and that one day you might see her in the ring again. • Classic: Team VIAGRA vs. Rush and Knox vs. Sepiroth De Luc and Persephone vs. Grendel and ZaC the Monkey (jOlt Tag Team Championship) - 09/01/01 I'd review this match, but it's classic, and I'de like to take the time to enjoy it. The end result though is Persephone getting the pinfall on Grendel after a reversal of a small package. Ahh, good ol' Jolt. • Back Draft vs. Polar When we come back from commercial break, we've got Back Draft waiting in the ring for Polar. Polar ran down and told the fans to fuck off. Your typical lock up, Back Draft getting the best with a kick to the gut. Back Draft sent Polar down, but Polar returned the favor with the LOW BLOW OF DOOM~! that sent Back Draft onto his knees. Polar than stuck his crotch in Back Draft's face...ahh...just kidding. Polar toyed with the crowd abit, then grabbed BD's head and slammed it agains tthe mat a couple of times. The crowd really dislikes Polar. BD got up and connected with a standing sidekick. BD then connected with a clothesline. Yada, yada. Fun. BD thinks he's got the best of Polar, but Polar hits a neck-breaker. Pinfall attempt, but at 2, Polar gets off him and says someone needs to be an example. He dominates him some more and plays possum with the crowd again, getting up after another 2. Polar goes up top and takes BD with him, Ice Cap. This time, he doesn't get up @ 6:45 -- DUD. Post match beatdown without a chair. How exciting. I wish Booderau was the victim of this beating, he annoys the shit out of me. • Polar is still in the ring, talking about how past and how he's worked with some great wrestlers and he's Jolt's best. But then "Untouchable" Carson Nash shows up and gets a fucking gigantic pop from the crowd. Nash says Polar needs to get out of the ring because it's Nash's ring time and that he's seen better ringwork from his sister. Semi-burn. Oh, but Nash doesn't have a sister. Burn. Polar says he wants a match with him next week and calls him "Joe Nobody." Nash says that Polar hasn't jobbed to anyone as good as Nash before. Nash leaves and Polar squeals like a pig. • Dawson James came out and said he debuted at the Mardi Gras show but he still doesn't think the peeps know who he is. He says he feels important tonight and wants an interviewer which brings out Kelley Andrews. She asks him why he has a match with himself tonight? Huh? He says he'll be just as suprised as everyone else. He does your typical heel wants the interviewer thing. After some more of the dating game, she asks about Geo Vacton. He says Geo was not a star and should be frowned upon. He was the greatest wrestler in zTwhat? He badmouths Geo a bit more and guess who comes out? Geo Vacton comes out and calls Dawon James gay. They argue for a little bit about zTw, and the crowd chants that James sucks. Geo runs down and knocks Dawson down with a superkick. They're seperated and Dawson swears for revenge to avenge this TRAGEDY. • Someone's yelling in a room. It's Coburn P. Valencia on the telephone. He's an old British guy and says he's been disrespected. He says he wasn't booked cause the Jolt wrestlers were scared. He says next week, we'll see him and you'll like it. • Dawson James vs. Dawson James Dawson comes out and complains about how he just got jumped. But the cool "COMING" promo plays again. I'm not even going to bother reviewing this. James paid someone to come out and get their ass kicked, dressed up as himself. DUD. • Ninja K shows up at the Jolt Wrestling Video Library. Tiiight. • Ken Kaze shows up (with George) and says he wants to play power rangers with BAB. Wow, BAB seems to be the focus of a lot of people's attention on this show. I wonder if...nah, nevermind. Kaze hurts himself playing Power Rangers with his trash can, and Geo Vacton comes in to make sure he's alright. George and Kaze argue for a bit...I can't believe I'm calling a trash can by his name. I feel incredibly stupid. Anyways, BAB comes in and talks trash. Kaze claims to be the Turqouise Ranger. • Polar comes down and talks about Nash's dead parents and horrible intro music. He says Nash might be Untouchable, but not Unbeatable. • Kenjiro Ito vs. Brandon Youngblood Youngblood gets some smooching on the outside of the ring with Melissa Garvin. Damn, all these ex-tSC'ers like hooking up with co-workers sisters and stuff. Ito shows off his power and suprises Youngblood. A couple of tieups, toss-up as to who wins. Ito with a double leg takedown, tried to convert it to a cloverleaf, but Youngblood looked exactly like Ken Kaze, going insane with his kicks. Lots of heat in Youngblood's direction. Melissa is yelling at Youngblood and he knows if he doesn't step it up, he's going to be sexually frustrated for a while. Youngblood pushed Melissa down. Yawn, days of our lives? Ito and Youngblood decided to slap each other across the chest. Ito dropkicked Youngblood to the outside and connected with a snap suplex. Ito threw him into the stairs and roughed him up a bit. Back in the ring. Youngblood got up punched Ito before hitting a snap suplex of his own. Youngblood worked on Ito's back for a while. Bow and arrow backbreaker, Ito finally got Youngblood's shoulders on the mat, B.Y. kicks out at 2. Rolling German Suplexes by Youngblood. Pinfall...almost a 3, Ito kicks out. Youngblood tries another German 'Plex, Ito reverses, tries his own, punches Youngblood in the back of the neck a couple of times and connects with the German Suplex. He trash talks to Miss Garvin and then cockily pinfalls Youngblood. Two count. Youngblood spit in Ito's face and rolled him up. Almost 3. Modified Whiplash Bash by Youngblood. Attempted spienbuster, but Ito reversed it into ITO'S ICE AGE! Ito wins via pinfall @ 12:13 -- **1/2*, good match, could have been something special, but good none the less. • Ito goes out to collect from Back Alley Brawler, and says he's feeling awesome. BAB says he won him some money too. Ito says he's gonna get some head, BAB says be careful. • Carson Nash backstage with Booderau, plays to the crowd a bit before going on about how cool he is. • American Hero III announces the matches for Block A of the Triple Crown Tournament • Jonathan Conspiracy (w/Sweet Aroma) vs. Chris Mezz Lockup, spear from Mezz. Mezz began throwing punches at conspiracy. Mezz connects with a belly to belly. Overhead belly to belly. JCON grabs the tights and throws Mezz out of the ring. The two go back at it when...you guessed it...THERE WILL BE NOTHING LEFT TO HOLD HIM BACK...and it's Voran Xias, err...Xias...whatever he goes by now. Xias clears house with a chair and says he's back in action. He says the fWo sucks and that he's the greatest free agent in the WORRRRLD~! he talks crap about JCon and Mezz and then Xias OUT. NO CONTEST. Final Thoughts: Alright show after an electric Mardi Gras Pay-Per-View. Kenjiro Ito established himself as a star. I don't like all this mentioning of other companies. Some people are obviously bitter. I didn't like the tag match as it felt more like a singles match than a tag match. Next week looks like it's going to be pretty awesome.
