02/24 - WrestleSault Press Conference

26 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The report of the WrestleSault press conference at the Bell Centre in Montreal, posted on jOlt Wrestling’s 2005 site on 26 February 2005 and captured by the Wayback Machine on 20 March 2005.
02/24 - WrestleSault Press Conference Posted by Jolt Wrestling.com on 02/26/05: For the second day in a row Jolt Wrestling has taken over the Bell Centre! Hundreds of fans gathered in the lobby to catch yet another Jolt Wrestling Press Release. Yesterday came the announcement of the finalist involved in Jolt’s Triple Crown Tournament, a tournament which will decide who will become the Champions of Jolt Wrestling. The room was packed with fans all of which standing before a small stage with a table set up where Mike Mixx, a Jolt Wrestling ring announcer, and several Jolt Wrestling Superstars sat. The likes of Dawson James, Jonathan Conspiracy, Polar, Geo Vacton, and Carson Nash were all in attendance. They were each spread apart from another in order to make sure that they didn’t get things started prior to WrestleSault! Seeing as how each man in the room had a reason to knock the lights of at least one other person, security was around pretty heavily! Mike Mixx took center stage, standing behind a large podium and welcoming the crowd to the Bell Centre. “First of all, we’d like to thank Montreal for hosting us for not only today, but for this entire week!” The crowd, like any other crowd would, cheered for itself. “Now, let’s get started…” Mike Mixx cleared his throat and glanced at a sheet of paper in front of him. “Over the last few weeks these two men have really hated each other… Dawson James announced himself as the only wrestler to come from a former federation, ZyberTech Wrestling, and experience as much success as he had. Geo Vacton begged to differ, and a rivalry ensued between these two men. Now, for the very first time ever, they will face each other in a One on One match, live and only at WrestleSault!” The crowd cheered, but soon started to boo as Dawson James went to the podium. Dawson James stood before the crowd looking dressed up as usual in his casual, yet so incredibly formal and metro sexual suit. His perfect teeth gleamed at the crowd as he smiled. “Yes, yes. It’s finally going to happen! Yours truly, an undeniably legendary wrestler in this industry is going to give that little punk over there the opportunity of a lifetime. And, at the very same time, I will be blessing all of those in attendance by allowing them to bask in the glory that is me, Dawson James. If he’s really that good, then tell me… Why isn’t he still in the tournament for the Jolt Triple Crown, huh? Oh, yeah… That’s right. He lost in the first round. Kinda funny, but mostly just plain pathetic! That’s ok though, because I’ll show him a lesson this Sunday… wait for it… at WRESTLESAULT!” The crowd booed Dawson James, which was predictable. Geo Vacton grinned and slowly made his way up to the podium. He looked over the crowd, and then stared at Dawson James before speaking. He stood before the Montreal crowd with a Koivu (Montreal Canadiens) hockey jersey on and a baseball cap of the bulldogs, showing his local spirit. “First of all, I think I speak for the entire Jolt roster when I say how damn happy we are to have our first pay-per-view right here in Montreal!” That comment was obviously well received. “Now, as for Dawson James over there…” Dawson began to laugh loudly, interrupting Geo. “Yeah, that idiot over there. That’s ok though, because I got a kiss waiting for him.” Dawson again laughed loudly, breaking over the laughs from the crowd. “Heh, the Idiot Kiss—“ Dawson broke through with laughter again. “Will you shut the hell up?” Dawson stood up and looked at Geo. “Ha, ha, ha! Are you talking to Me?” Geo shook his head. “Sit down ya big jerk with the R from Toys ‘R Us.” Dawson sat back down and Geo continued. “Now, Dawson can say all he wants… the fact is I’ve already got a win over him in our tag team match last week, and know what? I wouldn’t be here today if it weren’t for him… I’d of been on yesterday’s press release talking about going into the final round of the tournament for the Jolt Triple Crown.” Dawson was grinning. “You all better show up on Sunday for this one to see me slap that smile off the face of that jerk over there… ‘Cos I’m T-N-T, I’M DY…NO…MITE!!!” The crowd yelled Geo’s catch phrase back him as he said it, and then Geo held his arms up to the loving crowd. "You know who else this crowd thinks might be pretty DY-NO-MITE?" "ME!" The voice came from Polar, who was now stepping up to the podium. The crowd booed loudly as Geo Vacton gave a blank stare. "Not exactly you, but, you can take the microphone if you want," Geo said and returned to his seat. "I know Mixx's supposed to announce what's going on in our match this Sunday, Nash, but I don't really care because I'm going to tell these people what's REALLY going to happen. This Sunday, at Wrestlesault, me and bitchboy Carson over there are going to be in Jolt's very first Last Man Standing Chairshot match. "And well, I feel bad for Nash, he's got such a promising and already remarkable career in the wrestling business. Not only am I going to knock him out of this match, I'm going to knock him out of Jolt and all wrestling for good. Nash cost me my spot in the Triple Crown Tournament thanks to a steel chair, he's cost me matches to people that AREN'T EVEN IN MY LEAGUE! So I'm going to make sure that Nash knows payback is a bitch. You're a deadman Nash, a deadman." Polar heard the boos from the press, but it was alright, it wasn't anything Polar hadn't heard before. But the crowd LIT UP with a small "CARRRSON! CARRRSON!" chant, as Nash, who was wearing a nice looking suit, tieless, of course, and had a bottle of water in his hand approached the podium. "Now that you've heard Polar talk, let me ask you, who was REALLY listening to that? Polar, you talk to me like you think I'm scared of you. This is Wrestlesault, the only thing I'm scared of is tripping on the way to the ring, being embarrassed in front of a hundred million people. All eyes are on me, Polar, and that upsets you, doesn't it? This match is really just to watch me smash your head into bits and pieces. "You don't scare me, hell, you probably don't even scare these people in the audience. And come Wrestlesault, I am going to expose you for what you are. You're a damn mockery to the 'bad-ass.' You want to learn to be a badass? Go watch my tapes from Action! I ended 2 people's careers, and don't think I'm scared to end yours.Your fate is in my hands, Polar, and right now, I'm just toying with you. No, not the kind of toying you like to do, but I'm plotting just how to end it. It's over, Polar, give up, don't even bother showing up on Sunday." The crowd lit up as Nash capped his water, and turned back to his seat. However, an arm turned him around, and he was soon face to face with his opponent. Jolt security rushed the stage and tried to split the two men up. Luckily for you, the microphone was there just so you could hear the two men talk trash to each other. "You know I'm not scared of being fired, not if I got to beat the living shit out of you." "Bring it, baby, just bring it. You won't be able to, though, I'm UNTOUCHABLE. Hence the name, UNTOUCHABLE CARSON NASH." "The only thing untouched about you is your penis, you fag." "Haha, you're so original. We'll see, Sunday." "Yes we will." By then, security had broken up the melee. The crowd was stunned at the events that occured. Mike Mixx took the podium, and prepared to introduce the next men. “Okay, and with that we now move on to the final match we have to announce on this evening. Actually this match has yet to be signed, but a challenge was put out by Jonathan Conspiracy to Xias, who isn’t contracted by Jolt and so isn’t here today, so without further ado, Jonathan if you may.” Mixx returned to his seat, as Jonathan got to his feet and made his way to the podium. “Ah it feels so good to back in a wrestling ring. It feels even better being a World Champion in a prestigious wrestling federation. But then you get SOME PEOPLE…” Jonathan shot a glance over the crowd and shook his head. “…Some people who take it upon themselves to not only ruin ya opportunity to get closer to the goal of being World Champion, but a few weeks ago they ruin ya first match back in action. Now after that match I decided not to worry about it, because I knew I had bigger things to achieve here. I figured Chris Mezz, who has no business still being apart of the Triple Crown Tournament, would take care of things and he did a decent job, but it wasn’t enough for me. However, I felt Xias wasn’t a big enough problem to deter me from me goal. But once again Xias decided to get involved in my business, and that’s where my challenge comes in. So I’m going to sit down, because I don’t have much to say but this...” Jonathan cleared his throat. “And if he doesn’t accept this challenge, then he isn’t as great as he builds himself up to be. See you at Wrestle Sault Xias, or should I pull one of your maneuvers and just show up unexpectedly at a wrestling event during one of your matches, to get my point across? I hope I have your attention! Now don’t disappoint me!” Jonathan returned to his seat and sat down. Mike Mixx returned to the podium and looked around at the crowd. “Well, I think we can all feel the intensity building up around here surrounding Jolt’s Pay-Per-View, WrestleSault! I KNOW you’ll all be there, but if you needed any more reasons to show up… There’s three to my right, and four to my left. Ladies and Gentlemen, take care!” And so it was. WrestleSault continued to shape itself during its second day of press conferences. To sum things up, the following matches were announced: Geo Vacton VS Dawson James. A heated rivalry will come to its head at WrestleSault as these two angry men square off against one another for the sake of glory! Polar VS Carson Nash. In what is sure to be a hellacious match, Polar and Carson Nash will face each other in a Steel Chair Match! How do you win? By cracking a chair over someone’s skull and knocking them out, of course! Jonathan Conspiracy VS Xias. Since invading Jolt a few weeks ago, Xias hasn’t made too many friends in the company. One of the people he’s rubbed the wrong way is Jonathan Conspiracy and after Xias ruined Jonathan Conspiracy’s chances of wrestling for the Triple Crown, JCON has vowed to seek revenge!
