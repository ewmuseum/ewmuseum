Sunday iNtense 35 Introduction jOlt Wrestling 34 brought all too much speculation around the organization over this past week. So many questions arose and so many problems needed to be solved. Damien Lee would be out for at least an hour of tonight's show, if not all of iNtense 35 from suffering a brutal attack by half-owner The Don on the last show. The Don's recent 'Power Struggle' has cost jOlt Wrestling more than the fans could possibly see. The backstage environment under Don's 'leadership' has sunk to a new low, and the only thing that could save jOlt Wrestling now... would be a miracle. Merry Christmas iNtense opened to Damien Lee's name tag on his door. Interviewer Christmas Jameson. Christmas knocked on Damien's door and a low deep voice told her to come in. She looked into the camera and opened the door slowly to reveal The Don. His long legs lead to his big black boots that dripped water on the papers scattered on Damien's desk. The fourth season of the year was amongst us as some snow could be detected on the bottoms of his shoes. The Don smiled and pointed towards the seats in front of him. Christmas shook her head and smiled, but did move closer to The Don. The Don, being the gentlemen he was, got up from his seat and looked down at Christmas. "Um... Tony. I have just a few questions for you to answer... I'll only take a few minutes of your time." "O.K." Tony smiled looking down Christmas' shirt. Christmas went off. "Yeah Umm... What is it exactly that you are trying to prove here in jOlt. I mean, Damien Lee offers you a spot in jOlt Wrestling and all you do is try to take away his pride and joy. He works very hard for this and you just come along and wreck everything. You fought for his and you fought erOn and you fought Samantha Bevins and you only fought them, because you wanted their power. You're a lousy human being and a lousy friend. This interview is over." Christmas turned around, lifted her head and began walking out, but her hair suddenly became grasped under The Don's grip and she was pulled back into The Don's chest standing face to face with him. "Get me some coffee... Capiche?" The Don tossed Christmas onto the ground. She got up, brushed off her top and skirt and walked out the door slamming it behind her. jOlt Wrestling Newswire "Hello fans of jOlt Wrestling. My names John Roccott. John Roccott moved towards and handful of computers all on some part of the jOlt Wrestling website. John sat down and told the cameraman to zoom in. It was my pleasure and half fun at jOlt Wrestling's iNtense 35." Vigor's opposite. There is, quite possibly, only one feeling which defines a wrestler's opening night. Is confidence? Could be. Confidence is a feeling of self-assurance, and believing in yourself. Though, there is another feeling which is often felt strongly beneath a man's skin. Fatigue? Whether or not your bones and muscles are physically drained, you can still fight through it all mentally. The feeling of which we're trying to discover is often overlooked. It's the feeling you receive right before you ask that 'pretty girl over there' to join you in a few drinks. Remember that play in elementary school? Right before you enter the scene, this feeling in the pit of your stomach held you back. Nervousness. Steven Denton sat just outside the entranceway that would lead him down to the ring. In a small plastic chair, Steven hunched over, had his eyes pointed directly at the concrete floor. A few droplets struck the floor, and Steven shook his head. "I'm already sweating... this is pathetic," Steven spoke softly to himself. "How in the hell am I supposed to accomplish anything in jOlt when I can't even think accurately, due to a case of stage fright? For crying out loud..." Denton wiped a few beads of sweat off his forehead. He couldn't let his nervousness be seen. Denton sat up straight, and took a deep breath. After a moment, he created a rather relaxed look on his face, and hid his overwhelming feeling of withdrawal and lack thereof. Normally, Steven wouldn't become nearly as perturbed at he was at this moment. In Japan, he'd be in front of very large crowds (though possibly not as large as the one tonight), and hadn't felt even a pinch of nervousness. This was a large change in how he felt about jOlt, and wrestling in general. How could he perform the way he knows he can, if he's shivering with freight all the way down to ringside? As the time approached, Denton's feeling of anxiety grew rapidly. A feeling of failure was already creeping into his head. He'd fallen into a bottomless pit, and there was no one close enough to hear him scream. Singles Competition Lance Knight vs. Steven Denton In the arena, jOlt fans and followers wait rather eagerly for something exciting to occur. Steven, however, puts it all aside, and slides into the ring. He has a match to win. Slowly, "Voices" fades to nothingness, as Denton paces back and forth around the ring's interior. A much louder murmur strikes up once more. "Payback" by Flaw. This, as opposed to "Voices", gets a much more welcome reaction, as the more educated half of the audience reaches their feet. Lance Knight. Being a former Survivor, Knight receives a very welcome reaction. He let's his confidence be known, stepping quickly down the entrance ramp. Out of the corner of his eye, Lance catches a "Knightfall" sign. For a pinch of a second, a sliver of a smile crosses his face, before it's wiped off in favor of a look of determination. Knight climbs into the ring, checks his knee braces, and turns to face his first opponent in jOlt. Only a millisecond after the opening bell, Steven Denton clocks Lance Knight with a forearm between the eyes. Knight stumbles back against the ropes, and Denton is quick to Irish whip him across the ring. Denton turns to face his oncoming opponent, and bends down, flipping Knight into the air with a back body drop. However, using his agility, Knight lands on his feet. Denton's momentum from the throw causes him to take a pair of steps back. This presents an opportunity to Lance which he cannot refuse; that being to grip onto Steven's neck, and sit-out with a beautifully executed reverse neck breaker. Knight floats on top for the cover, but only draws a two count from the referee. Without another moment's hesitation, Knight brings Denton up. He lands a pair of fists on Denton's face, but when he's warned by the official, he finishes the sequence off with a stiff upper cut, stumbling Steven into the corner. Lance lands a harsh kick to Denton's left ribs, before back up a bit, and ramming into his opponent with a shoulder. He backs out quickly, and watches Denton, rather subconsciously, step forward out of the corner. The Pennsylvanian native capitalizes, snatching Denton into his grasp, and tossing him overhead with an exploder suplex. As Denton writhes in pain, Knight wipes a bead of sweat off his forehead. Steven, using the ropes, begins to climb to his feet with his back to Lance. Lance, seeing another opportunity, rockets backwards off the ropes and towards Denton. The Illinois native, however, is very aware of his surroundings, and turns to face Knight at the last possible second. Knight, not suspecting that Denton would turn, is caught by surprise as he's flattened with a mind-boggling lariat, sending him shoulder first to the mat. After a glance over the audience, Denton drops an elbow across Knight's chest, and covers. Knight, easily, throws his shoulder up at two. Steven considers his options, before bringing Knight to his feet. He tosses Lance into the ropes, and throws yet another lariat, this time having it reversed. Knight slides ducks underneath the arm and behind Denton where he wraps on a kata ha jime. Steven, immediately, feels the stab of excruciating pain, nearly causing him to instantly blackout. Denton, however, keeps his composure about him, and fights the large arms of Knight's. Because of the surprising battle Denton puts up, Knight has to stay on his feet with the move, rather than dropping down and adding the extra leverage with his legs. Time passes, nearly twenty seconds, before Denton manages to maneuver himself backwards, behind Knight, and free himself. Quickly, Denton knees Knight in the back, wraps his arm around Knight's head, and drops down into a Dragon Sleeper. However, after only five seconds in the hold, Lance flips backwards over Denton's shoulder, and lands on the apron, due to the closeness of the submission to the ropes. Knight head butts the back of Steven's skull, before leaping over the ropes and Denton. Knight, using his momentum, runs across the ring and off the opposite elastic bands, as Denton backs off them as well. They race at each other, leap frog, and rebound off the ropes once more. Like two sound barrier-breaking fighter jets, Knight and Denton collide at mid-ring. Steven, looking to add onto the sequence, looks for another leap frog... but he's caught. Knight catches Denton in a spinebuster like position, but instead throws him backwards, performing an innovative exploder suplex variation. Denton seems out like a light as Lance covers. One... two... thr-no! Denton, in all his pain, managed to shove Lance off. The crowd let out an overwhelming gasp of shock, as Knight ponders the happenings himself. However, he doesn't ponder for long, lifting Denton back to his feet. Iron City Violence sends Denton sprawling backwards against the elastic, before he's shot to the opposite side with an Irish whip. As Steven returns, Knight ducks his shoulder, catches him in an inverted crucifix position, and dumps Steven nearly headfirst to the mat. The crowd awes at the carnage, as Lance covers for the easy three count. "Payback" plays once more, drawing a cheer from the fans in attendance. Knight holds up a fist, before dropping from the ring. As he strolls towards the back, Steven Denton slowly picks up the pieces in the ring, using the ropes for leverage. He looks around himself at the curious eyes, and loyal jOlt fans. He had failed, and failed miserably... and everyone had seen. How the hell could he have any sort of future now? Denton regained consciousness and walked up the ramp dizzy as could be while Lance Knight celebrated his much deserved win.
