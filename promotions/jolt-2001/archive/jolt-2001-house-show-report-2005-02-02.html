House Show Report (02/02/05)

5 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The report of the 2 February 2005 Total Impact! house show in Baton Rouge, posted on jOlt Wrestling’s 2005 site on 5 February 2005 and captured by the Wayback Machine on 20 March 2005.
House Show Report (02/02/05) Posted by Jolt Wrestling.com on 02/05/05: Jolt Wrestling Presented "Total Impact!" in Baton Rouge, LO last Wednesday to a heated audience of over 5,000. The fans were excited to see the high impact action they had witnessed the previous night on Tuesday Night Intense in New Orleans. Fan Response: Danny Dibatista, Baton Rouge, Louisiana “I really liked the show tonight, everyone was pretty excited and it was so loud at times that I could hardly hear myself scream! I really liked DDR alot! ..but Lusus gave me the creeps. I can't forget to mention Kenjiro Ito, that guy was getting on my nerves but he was entertaining so I'll give him that much. I'd still take Brandon Youngblood anyday of the week though, he can at least speak proper english. And last but definitely not least! let's give it up for the tag teams, the best part about the show. The Ninja Brigade are definitely worth the price of admission alone! those guys were hiliarious! thank for coming to Baton Rouge, come back soon okay?!” LaRoux Gambit - Baton Rouge, LA Following the Main Event Jonathan Conspiracy and Sweet Aroma returned to the ring, and this time JCON had the jOlt International Title in tow. He got on the mic and bad mouthed Xias and Chris Mezz, until he heard "Here I am in Jolt. And you don't know who I am. But soon, you will know." over the PA system. Breaking kayfabe Jonathan said he'd give someone $7 dollars if they could guess who that "mystery woman" is. He invited a fan into the ring and they said it was Xias, at which point Jonathan gave the kid the jOlt International Title to hold while he pulled the money out. He then gave the kid the $7 dollars and took the belt back and exited the ring. The kid then picked up the mic and replied "Hey this is Canadian!" Jonathan revealed his trademark smirk and signed a couple of autographs and took some pictures, but was quickly back in character when someone snapped a photo of Aroma's ass with their camera phone. He even made a call on the phone and told the fan that he was "roaming!". All in all it was a great show, and I hope that Chris Mezz is okay, because the whole foot caught in ropes thing didn't look like a planned spot, but if it was I'd give that spot of the night. Thanks for taking feedback, make Jolt #1! Internet Website Reports: Sean Willis, reporting for www.SmarkYou.com 2/2 Jolt Wrestling House Show from Baton Rouge Louisiana The house looked to be about 5,000, but there were still lots of seats available in the upper decks. Crowd made up for it though, as they popped loud and often for most of the Jolt superstars. King Cobra vs. Fret Magic Both guys showed off a lot of their more aerial moves, so this was a good choice to fire up the crowd. Cobra takes it over Magic with his finishing Snakebite. Went on a bit too long though. The Pearson Boys vs. Unknown Elements Solid, if unspectacular tag team affair. Mark Pearson and Shawn Arrows were the two standouts of the contest, as their sections were the best of the bout. Not a lot of heat, but that can improve with time, as they've got a solid match down pat. Arrows wins it for the Elements with the Arrow-Shot DDT on Jesse Pearson. Brandon Youngblood vs. Joey Starr The crowd was noticably torn on Youngblood after his poor performance on the the first Intense, with a few smarks even chanting "ITO!" at him to try and throw him off his game. Still, the crowd was split for him, but they booed Starr strongly throughout the contest. The DQ finish only made them hate Starr more, as Joey bashed BY in the head with a steel chair. Kenjiro Ito vs. Coburn P. Valencia Both heel and face got good heat, but this is the South. Any foreigner's going to be over as a heel. Little dissapointed considering how dominant Kenjiro was on Intense, but the crowd enjoyed seeing Valencia's comeback. Most of the fans didn't like Ito's arm work, but they came back when Valencia started making his own comeback, and they popped big for Ito's finishing top rope Blizzard Suplex, one of the more spectacular moves of the night. Ken Kaze & Love!Love! KAMAKAZIE!!! Vs. The Ninja Brigade Worked as a comedy match, this was one of the highlights of the night. The kids loved LLK and Mittens T. Cat, while Ken Kaze and Back Alley Brawler provided most of the brawling. The Cat's Krunk Train is ludicrously over in Louisiana. Kaze's Binary Thesis was enough to pin the Back Alley Brawler, as all four men, even the Brawler, got an ovation post match. A little silly for my tastes, but it worked for what it was. DDR vs. Lusus Very short match, probably due to the newness of DDR. A well worked big vs. little match, with DDR slowly winning over the crowd with her Dance Dance Revolution based offense. Still, Lusus got the win, crushing DDR with his Jacob's Ladder finisher, which is an awesome sight in person. CJ Newfield vs. Brian Raindal Both guys got good cheers/boos coming out, but this went on too long with no real payoff. A 30:00 draw, it would have been better were it shorter or had a finish. A technically solid match, but the crowd (and myself) were very unhappy with the TL draw. Carson Nash vs. Polar The crowd was into both guys, who went on to work a very good sub ten minute contest. Polar wins it by the Icecap. They should have given these guys some of the thirty minutes alloted to Newfield/Raindal, match would have been great with a bit more time. Jonathan Conspiracy vs. Chris Mezz This was the contest most of the fans were waiting for, and it showed. Kept short but sweet, with both guys going at each other full bore continuing their vendetta. Ended in DQ after Jonathan Conspiracy hit Mezz with a chair at 10:33, when JCON ducked a Mezz thrust kick leading to Mezz's foot getting caught in the ropes. Things are far from over between these two. Heat for this was absolutely unreal, as many of the fans pelted Conspiracy with sodas and popcorn. Ninja K vs. Jeff Garvin. Odd choice for the main event, as J-Con vs. Mezz had more heat. Still, this was the better pure contest. Crowd DESPISED Garvin, but had difficulty getting behind Ninja K as Garvin was in control for most of the contest, but K launched a comeback. Had the crowd believing he would be victorious until Julie intervened, cutting off the Ninja before his Morning Star Press and allowing Garvin to lock in the Hammer Jammer for a tainted submission victory. Top Face Heat: 1. Chris Mezz 2. Carson Nash 3. Brian Raindel 4. Ninja K 5. Coburn P. Valencia Top Heel Heat: 1. Jonathan Conspiracy 2. Jeff Garvin 3. Joey Starr 4. CJ Newfield 5. Lusus Overall, a good show. Would have liked to see more of the faces go over, as most face victories were only by DQ. Still, a good night of wrestling in Baton Rouge, and hopefully a sign of things to come. Thank you for all who witnessed the show, and thank you for making the beginning of Jolt Wrestling an impressive affair! Thanks Baton Rouge! Joltwrestling.com
