YanSuko — 11 June 2012 This thread is the continuation of the altercations between Sylo and Marcus Davis during the UltraTitle Tournament.
Note: Marcus Davis V2 defense of the BHC Heavyweight Championship on June 5, 2012: Marcus Davis (O) v Kazuhiko Hokama (X) via Submission (Kubi Jippa) [22:15]
We open to the Marcus Davis mansion in Fussa, Tokyo, Japan once more. This time Marcus stands behind the podium with his representatives his Publicist Danielle Lira, Agent Amanda Mancini, and 1/3 of his legal team Ashley Hawkins stand behind him. Two dozen or so journalists sit in chairs in front of the podium. Marcus has the BHC Heavyweight Championship seated on the podium in front of him. His body language does not seem all that pleased.
Marcus: I would like to thank everyone for coming here this evening, I would like to make a few statements, to alert you I nor anyone else will be fielding questions after I have completed my statements…
Marcus pauses briefly. Marcus: As everyone is now aware, I was successful in my defense against Kazuhiko Hokama last week in what was an extremely stiff challenge from one of the best wrestlers I have ever been in the ring against. Everyone is also aware that Jay Edward Sylo has been silent on this the offer the Burning Honor Committee made to him and did not make a statement in the week time frame that was given to him to accept the match…
I have spent the last week convincing the committee that they should give Sylo more time to accept the match in Paris, and early this morning despite the unprofessional nature shown by Jay Edward Sylo they have extended his chance to wrestle me for the BHC Heavyweight Championship match until Saturday, June 16[SUP]th[/SUP] 11:59 PM Japanese Standard Time…
All the reporters begin to type feverishly on their electronic devices. If he does not respond or does not agree to the match, a replacement opponent will be chosen in his place. Either way, I will be defending the BHC Heavyweight Championship on July 1[SUP]st[/SUP] in Paris. I would personally encourage Sylo to be that opponent on July 1[SUP]st[/SUP] as it was he who began our issues and I would like to quickly put an end to these issues so we both can move on with our lives…
I allowed Sylo to get into my mind and cost me a match that I otherwise would have easily won, and I will not allow him to do so again. If he honestly feels as much ill will towards me as he has led the global wrestling community to believe, than he should have no problems answering this challenge…
Marcus clears his throat. In my personal opinion Sylo sees wrestling as nothing other than a paycheck that allows him to fund his Mixed Martial Arts....
Air Quotes. “Hobby.” He no longer has any respect for the sport and simply uses it to pay for the highest priced trainers who are paid to tell him he has the skills necessary to be the best Heavyweight fighter in the world. Sylo is nothing short of a fighter…
I on the other hand am a Martial Artist and have been since I was a toddler. He enjoys telling each and every one willing to listen that he has the ability to end my career and that he would do such if he ever stepped into the ring with me…
Well….July 1[SUP]st[/SUP] is his opportunity to prove he is more than just talk… And to address other rumors…yes…the NJWF has allowed me to enter contract negotiations with Jolt! But I have no other information at this time…Thank all of you for coming…
The reporters stand up and shout questions in his direction, which alert the security guards to keep them under check as Marcus and his staff walk to another room of the mansion.
