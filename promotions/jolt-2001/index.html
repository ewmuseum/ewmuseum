Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

jOlt Wrestling began in 2001 with eight wrestlers and crowned its first World Champion, Brandon Blade, at the pay-per-view a2o: Alpha To Omega on 24 June 2001. Its weekly Sunday iNtense reached its forty-first show on 3 February 2002 under its in-story owners Damien Lee and The Don, and its second season opened with Unwanted on 28 May 2002, where a thirty-man battle royale made Erik Rave the first champion of a new World Championship; the season's surviving record ends with a preview of its next iNtense, captured on 4 June 2002.

In 2005 it returned as Jolt Wrestling with a street-show introduction in New Orleans on 23 January and a weekly Tuesday Night Intense that opened at the CajunDome on 1 February, its own events page listing shows into April. In June 2012 a board on the fwrestling.com hub took the name again, folded in the roster and top belt of Legacy of Champions, and ran iNtense, Warriors and pay-per-views until what its own board calls the final show for jOlt Wrestling on 13 October 2015.
