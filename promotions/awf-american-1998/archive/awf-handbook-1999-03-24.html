American Wrestling Federation Handbook Vol. 1 ------------------------------------------------------- Contents. 1.Introduction. 2.Shows. 3.Titles 4.Staff 5.Rankings, 6.Roleplaying. 7.Brief Rules. 8.Strats. 9. Application. ------------------------------------------------------- 1. Introduction. American Wrestling Federation came to be one night when I got tired of watching good role-players get the shaft because they were not popular with the president. I have been wanting to put a fed together for a while but felt it would never work out. But I promise as President that if you Out role-play your opponent you will win the match. No matter of Angles etc. Unless you agree before hand. -------------------------------------------------------- 2.Shows. We're going to have two weekly shows. "Saturday Breakdown"- The name says it all...It's going to review some happenings inside the AWF. Matches in it will be summarized. But it will mostly be a talk-through show about weekly events. And it will have no more than 4 summarized matches. "Monday Night Mayhem". It will be a play-by-play show with 7-8 matches. Also will consist of interviews etc.. It will be the big show of the week. PPV's.-.They will be every two months so that good angles are forming in them. And you guys will get to help choose the names. ---------------------------------------------------------- 3.Titles. We will have 3 Titles. 1. World Title 2. Television Title 3. Tag Team Titles The World Title and Tag Team Titles must be defended within 20 days of winning them, and at least once every thirty days thereafter. The Television Title must be defended once a week. The holders of the World and Tag Team Titles have the option of not putting the title on the line in any match, unless the Championship Committee overrules them. If a wrestler wins a non-title match by pin or submission, he will receive a second match with the title on the line within two weeks, unless the Championship Committee decides otherwise. (NOTE: If you are worried about us depriving you of a title shot, please understand that most times we do this will be in the case that a wrestler wins with outside interference.) -------------------------------------------------------------- 4. These like in any other fed will be decided by how well you role-play, which allows you to win matches. No one will get a title shot until after two matches. But there are exceptions to this rule depending if there is a good angle going. ---------------------------------------------------------------- 6. Roleplaying. This is a booked fed. So you must role-play to win. RP's, like any where else, shouldn't be 5 sentences. Those role-plays will get you a win against an opponent who doesn't RP. But they will get you thumped by a opponent who does 40-50 liners. I know a lot feds say Quality over Quantity and that's true because 9 times out of 10 if there long they have Quality. Our shows air on HBO Sports as a new pilot. So Cursing is allowed, Some sexual content is allowed. But remember HBO doesn't show Real Sex or Martin Lawrence at times when kids are watching, And we do.. ------------------------------------------------------------------ 7.Brief List of Rules. A.No attacking opponents in your role-play Example ( Bobby nails Chris Mason over the head with a chair and says "Your just lucky the Prez wants us to wait till Monday") if you want to hit them with a chair. E-mail me and I will write it into the card. B.No Shawn "The Hitman" Pierce or anything like that. You can have a attitude or gimmick like Bret or Austin but not a dub of the same name. C. You are allowed 3 Wrestlers per e-mail address. I would prefer one singles and a tag team. But it's your choice. D. Be Bold don't be afraid to ask me about new angles. E. Once every two weeks I would like to hold a Press Conference in the AWF [detail withheld] This would consist of me and the VP talking about happenings in the AWF and news. Once a month I will ask you to write me and tell me what you would like to happen with your wrestler in the near future, So I can have an idea of what to include in the cards. You can e-mail them to me or tell me at a Press Conference. The Press Conferences are not mandatory but may help you win in a close match. Unless your opponent has a good excuse. Any info revealed at the Press Conference will be mailed to you via e-mail also for reminder. ------------------------------------------------------------------- 8. Strategy. I would prefer them to be in ASAP on your upcoming match and no later than the day before of the match. They can include anything about your match you want say for example some moves you want to use or an interference.
