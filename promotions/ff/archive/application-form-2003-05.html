Application Form Some things you need to know before you apply (IMPORTANT!): 1) Sports Entertainers not allowed. You will most certainly get hurt and unless you can show us that you're ready to combat with World Class fighters, we suggest that you step aside and let the professionals do their job. 2) We are not a hardcore wrestling sports-entertainment fed. We are like the UFC, a legit fighting company looking to display fighting in its purest form. You won't see any screwjobs here. My name is NOT Vince McMahon nor do I ever plan on emulating him. 3) The staff here at FLATLINED are interested only in world-class fighters that practice the varied principles of combat. That way, we are able to show to our audience the vast contrast of fighting styles. 4) We have two belts for two different weight divisions. The Heavyweight (215 lbs and above) and Lightweight (anything below 215 lbs). There are other accolades one can receive aside from being the Champion in one's given weight division. We will also have a FightFest (June PPV) Tournament Champion, who will have to fight people from both weight classes. Since the FightFest champion cannot lose his crown until the following year, we will award him a trophy instead of a Championship Title. Both National and International Talent are welcome to apply. 5) Since we are a States-based promotion, instead of measuring height in meters, we will measure by inches and instead of measuring weight by kilograms, we will measure weight by pounds. 6) Matches will have a 5 minute time limit and will be held within the dreaded Octagon, in which the winning fighter may seek victory through Submission, Referee's Decision, or Judge's Decision. 7) Fighters are only restricted to fighting in their weight division for title bouts, aside from that, it's openweight competition. Other important things to know about Flatlined before applying… 1) On every fightSHOW (Flatlined's weekly program aired Sundays on HBO), the owner characters sell tickets based on the fights, not the fighters per se. Therefore, your conventional eWrestling tactics are thrown out the window. No longer will you be able to taunt the crowd or hype up a match, but instead you will be writing 'off camera' segments relating to your character's storyline. 2) Applications are based on several elements: how prepared you are for Flatlined, how enthusiastic you are with Flatlined, what kind of person you are backstage, and writing talent. I will not accept applicants that show little to no effort in their applications. I need to know you're committed to the idea if you're applying. Because this is such a new concept to the world of eW, I'm gonna need to be assured of your commitment to Flatlined. There is no getting around it. 3) Handler attitude is important. If you get three strikes (on most occasions, I'll cut it even shorter, depending on the severity of the offense) because of backstage shananigans, you're out. I want to have a harmonious backstage environment conducive to quality writing and I don't see how a bad apple can help me achieve that. 4) We don't hire based on name status, so you can easily come from whereabouts unknown and still be accepted here over the bigger names if you can fill the requirements of OOC #2. In the same breath, 'big name' rpers are also welcome to submit an application, but there is no guarantee that you will be accepted. If your character blows chunks and does not mesh well with the fed, you won't get accepted. 5) The emphasis of Flatlined is character development, meaning no gimmicks. If your application seems cliche, chances are you'll blend in with the rest of the applicants and get lost in the crowd and more than likely won't be accepted. Your pushes are determined by your activity and storyline in Ff. 6) Flatlined fighting's goal is to publish a sense of character diversity under one roof while making all of our angles as realistic as possible. 7) When submitting your Flatlined app, consider the reasons why a professional company would hire your character. Take into account that a convicted felon won't have much of a chance in joining this fed, despite petty threats, etc because Jared Davidson, the owner is very concerned about the image Flatlined exudes to its viewers. Because Flatlined is a family legacy that has just been passed down to him, he doesn't want to ruin it with bad publicity. 8) Your character in Flatlined will be exclusive to Flatlined until the day you quit or are released from the roster. As a handler, you are free to handle in other feds while being on the Flatlined roster... just don't do it with your Flatlined character. Although this is a very controversial clause to the application, we feel, as roster and staff, that to portray this sense of reality, it would be unrealistic for one particular character to fed in two places at once, especially if this character is a sports entertainer trying to make his name in the dangerous world of Mixed Martial Arts. Also, because we are not a part of eWrestling in the least bit, I will look over applications that have characters that have already existed in eW. Flatlined is a part of a different realm, and we would prefer that when applying, you start from scratch like everyone else currently on the roster has.
