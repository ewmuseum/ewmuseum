03.19.03: FLATLINEDfighting released several of its fighters. Upon the culmination of fightSHOW 10, the company released a statement with the names and reasons for release of many of the fighters. Lucien Keenan, Arson Zanders, Nadev Aryeh, Geodin Deminchara, and Deacon Triage were all let go as their short contracts expired. Desheng Qi is reported to have been fired for substance abuse. In addition to this it is reported that Darius Potempa was drawn back to Brazil for more money and both Enrique De La Mora and Eric Abram will be out quite a while with serious bone breaks.
