A History Lesson Since the 1930s, Flatlined Fighting has been around, entertaining the masses with its hard-hitting fast-paced fights that would leave its audience hollering, applauding, and chanting in approval of its unmitigated display of brutality and inhumanity. Its stars would be paid hefty amounts for their efforts and America embraced them for it. It brought them to a different world, apart from theirs, where they could release and be carefree while watching their favorite fighter pummel another’s face into a misshapen mess of blood and bruises with his fist en route to a glorious victory. One man’s vision dream has become the fantasy escape for millions around the globe. Benjamin Oscar Davidson, Sr., in the wake of the Great Depression, wanted to distract the citizens of the New Jersey area of their poverty, and charged a mere five cents for admission. It had always been that way since. The Davidsons had focused their attention on making NJBO an affordable escape for their fans. That is, until Oscar Davidson III broke tradition in the mid 90’s. The New Jersey Boxing Organization became the New Jersey Fighting Organization, and as his lust for money increased over time, it evolved into a very effective, efficient, and prosperous business. It stemmed from a family legacy that has thus far lasted seven decades. And as Oscar III’s tenure abruptly ended on the tragic 9/11 terrorist attack that shocked both America and the World, Jared’s quest to fill 70 years of tradition began. It has been a long road, indeed. And tonight, Jared prayed he would be able to fill the incredibly large shoes that were laid out before him.
