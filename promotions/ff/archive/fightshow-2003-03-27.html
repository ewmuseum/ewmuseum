Press Conference The curtain breathed, and flashbulbs went off from the camera men kneeling in front of the stage that held a podium and three men walking towards it. Horendo Lorenza, Ramon Pirenzo, followed by Frank Minio in Jeans and a black Flatlined Fighting t-shirt. Ramon cleared his throat, then, the room silenced, and he smiled. 'This is our first time doing this, so I'm going to be brief. Frank Minio doesn't quite boast an amazing record, but he does show skill and definition in the octagon that cannot be overlooked. We in the Phantom Camp feel ready to push forward and take what we can earn. The Flatlined Fighting World Heavyweight Championship. ' The announcement was taken differently by each reporter. In the coming weeks, Frank Minio will challenge three heavyweights, picked by the Flatlined management, and he will beat them within the first round.' It seemed like a joke to some in the room. 'After these opponents Frank will meet Javier Garcia at the upcoming pay-per-view... Frank wants to say a few words now that we have this in the air, then we'll take questions from the members of the press, Frank?' 'Yea, John Gansen from the Associated Press, Frank Minio, are you out of your mind?' Frank stood tall and nodded with an offended smirk on his olive face. 'Have a good day.' Frank, Horendo and Ramon walked towards the curtain as chaos broke loose below, questions being hollared at them as they escaped the feeding frenzy, cell phones, cameras flashing... Meat eaters... Ready to cannibalize their own for a scoop. Horendo warned Ramon that they would recieve a question like that, and oddly, Ramon found himself quite amused by Frank's reaction. No nonsense. He was getting to like the new, the READIED Frank Minio.
