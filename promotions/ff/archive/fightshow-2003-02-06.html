Opportunity Strikes Jared Davidson had called Jamie Pierce into his office to tell him about the news. It took him careful thinking in order to choose the right person for the job, but finally he made up his mind, and now he shuffled through his desk drawers in order to find something. Jamie sat confused as to why Davidson sent him to his office. He impatiently shuffled in his chair while Davidson spent more time searching. It didn’t take long for Davidson to find what he was looking for. Then Davidson brought out what he was secretly hiding, practically shoving the magazine in Jamie’s face. It showcased Takeo Brown on the cover, underneath a headline that read, “Takeo Brown: Flatlined Fighting’s Franchise?” Their speculation ended up correct, Takeo Brown would become Flatlined’s Franchise in the future. Jamie simply stared at the magazine while Davidson continued holding it. A sheepish grin emerged from Davidson. Jamie raised an eyebrow. There seemed to be little good that could come out of this. ”That’s nice and all. But what exactly does this have to do with me?” Jamie asked, as he leaned back in the chair speculating. Davidson tossed it onto the desk. “It’s Fighting Monthly. The leading magazine that covers fighting, surely you’ve heard of it?” Jamie shook his head. “Every year, the editors at Fighting Monthly ask me to pick a fighter who will appear on the cover, and with that there is also an article written on them. This gives the fighter maximum exposure and it’s an extraordinary honor to appear on it. The fighter holds the respect of Flatlined Fighting and we hope we can trust them, because Flatlined Fighting receives as much exposure than the featured person does. This tradition has been going on for a long time and I’m going to keep it going. Takeo Brown appeared on the last issue and it helped push his career even farther. He was on the last one…and I want you on the next.” Jamie mimicked Davidson’s smiling, while peering down at the cover with praising eyes. He then looked up at Davidson, who’s face suddenly turned serious. His piercing bold eyes judged him from the opposite end of the table. ”This is a great responsibility. But I trust that you’ll know what to do with it. There’s enough magazine articles about you already, but we feel that offering a different side on your fighting career will benefit all of us.” Davidson said as he pushed the magazine closer. “So what’s your answer?” Jamie didn’t need to think twice. “When do we start shooting?” Davidson grinned. “I’ll notify you with the details. But just so you know, I won’t be booking you in many fights near the photo shoot date. But I want you to fight at least once either this week or next week. You fought last week against Takeo, so I’ll assume you want next week.” ”Next week would be better.” He shuffled through his papers, pulling out a schedule. His finger ran across the blocks until he found a name. “You’ll be fighting Enrique De La Mora. He already has a fight this week, so I’m guessing that it’ll be no problem for you after rest.” Jamie nodded and stood up to take his leave. He held out his hand for a handshake, and Davidson extended his. “Nice doing business with you boss.” Jamie said, as he started towards the door in a more pleasant mood than when he entered. He exited the room, leaving Davidson alone in his office, only to think over whether or not his decision was a wise one. Temporarily Delayed As Jacob Meyers walked down the corridor, he wasn't focused on his purpose for doing so. Instead, all his attention was on his fight tonight. Although he was trying not to take this to a personal level, Ramis had crossed the line. And though he wasn't out for vengeance, he was determined to do the same to Ramis as he did the first night. Only after repeating these thoughts for the twentieth time tonight, he realized he had passed the office. He gave a sigh and rubbed his forehead, then turned and jogged back. His fist gently rapped the door, as Jared Davidson said... 'Come in.' As Jacob closed the door behind him, he noticed Jared was on the phone. Once he heard the click of the door, he kept his back to him. Although Jared at first didn't understand, he realized soon enough and continued his phone call. After the salutations, Jacob humbly turned around to a smiling Davidson. 'I received my note to come see you, sir.' 'Ah, good. How are you feeling Jacob?' 'Fine, sir. Toned up, and in the perfect mind-set.' 'I take it your ready for your match tonight then. Give the people a good show, eh?' 'So long as it pleases many.' And grants me what is mine, he said to himself. 'Well then, sounds like things are in order. Which makes this harder.' Jacob raised an eyebrow, as Jared motioned him to sit down. 'Jacob, I received a call earlier today. And, I am as disappointed as I assume you will be, at this poor show of responsibility. But, it appears as though Chester Ramis has developed a severe case of the flu these past few days. What first was a mild case needing rest, has turned into a rather urgent matter, as his health is seriously at risk.' 'I see. He is hospitalized I take it?' 'No, but is receiving the proper medical care to assure his safety. I'm terribly sorry Jacob, but your match tonight will be cancelled. But it is re-scheduled for next week, by which time Chester is expected to be fully recovered.' 'Well, things don't always go as we plan do they?' 'Jacob, I'm terribly sorry. I know-' 'No need for apologies. These things do happen. And his health is the most important thing.' 'Again, sorry for not getting to you sooner.' 'That's quite alright. I'll look forward to next week. Hope the show goes well.' Jacob stood up politely, sharing a nod and smile with Jared. He was genuinely sorry, but there was nothing he could do. Jacob seemed to understand, and left on a good note. He just wondered if the shaking in his punching arm was too evident, as his face slowly grew red with the exit sign he passed under. Rain Down Thunder He had felt it all week, a feeling so intense that it was hard to eat, sleep or think. He had felt it most of his life, a feeling of remorse, a feeling of…a feeling of regret. However, he knew very little of how it was going to manifest itself. It felt like he was going to spew his internal organs whenever he thought of it, it was swelling up inside of him, burning, tasting even bitterer with time going by. People say time heals wounds… “Fuck no...” Umaga sat with his head in his hands trying to take on board as much fluid as possible; his dreadlocked hair covered most of his face, cascading like a frozen waterfall over his barren expressionless face. His preparation had been low key and perhaps sub par, tonight he would be running on pure adrenaline, hate and anger. This could only result in one thing… It was going to be a hellacious occasion, Umaga would need all of his strength, skill and guile to defeat Vayin, but after all, he knew… It was gonna rain down thunder… Kristofer Niedzwiedz VS Enrique De La Mora The peaceful chords of 'Sunday Sun' by Beck! struck up over the soundsystem as Kristofer Niedzwiedz stepped through the curtains and walked briskly to the Octagon. His eyes appeared calm and placid, yet at the same time his jaw was set and his steps were measured. He walked alone for the second time in as many bouts. Shona Aun, his trainer, having been told to stay back in the locker room. Kristofer ducked into the cage and turned a slow circle, examining it, taking note of every corner, not wanting to make the same mistake as last week, not wanting another loss due to bad ring positioning. 'PUNETAAA!!!!!' This signaled the entry of Enrique De La Mora, 'Orgullo' by Nonpoint started up strong as he stepped through the curtain holding an even one and one record. He too appeared calm. His stride was longer than Kristofer's and he hit the ring yet quicker, his steps carried him right up into the face of Niedzwiedz. '¿Tiran ellos un pequeño uno en mí esta semana? Sea mejor chico rápido. . . . Better be quick, boy.' And so he turned his back on the smaller fighter. He threw a few punches towards the cage for effect, his left eye darted back to the smaller Niedzwiedz with every outstretched right. And then the referee interjected. He called them both to the center for some last minute instructions before calling for the bell to begin round one of this bout, scheduled for three five minute rounds. Both men hesitated to dive into the fray for a few moments, both cautious by nature, measuring each other up. But the first move was made by De La Mora as he came in quick with an experimental right jab, which was easily ducked by Niedzwiedz. Niedzwiedz poked in two light side-kicks to the torso of De La Mora, but kicks are not his specialty and De La Mora failed to so much as flinch. However, the next attack wasn't so easy to toss off. Niedzwiedz shot on him, causing De La Mora to step back to avoid it, but it was a feint and Niedzwiedz came up hard, wrapped his arms around De La Mora, and tossed his weight sideways. The effect was that both men were sent hard to the canvas of the Octagon. Kristofer was on top, his arms inside the arms of the bigger man with a bearhug, reminiscent of his last match with Minio. This time however his arms were inside the arms of his opponent and he had his head back to avoid the headbutt. And he rolled over De La Mora to go for an armbar... unfortunately he just wasn't strong enough and was almost literally thrown away by De La Mora. Both men back to their feet, standing at a decent distance from each other. And then De La Mora pressed in with a hard series of right and left jabs, his arms a flurry of motion as the smaller Niedzwiedz just barely avoided the blows. Until a left hook caught him right in the jaw as he ducked the wrong way. He was sent reeling by the blow, and was quickly caught up in a ¾ Nelson... into a slam! The hold was held and De La Mora dug in with it causing Niedzwiedz to grunt in pain. But a reversal was in order as the former Michigan State Champion knew exactly what to do in such a situation. He reached in, hooked the opposite leg of De La Mora, and rolled forward putting De La Mora on his back. In a typical wrestling match, that would be a three point move as Niedzwiedz quickly mounted him and locked in a Hadaka Jime.. which was quickly broken up by the official as the round comes to an end. Both men walking back to their corners to sip some water and examine the other for weakness. Niedzwiedz appeared more complacent as De La Mora grinned looking at the calm face on his opponent. And the bell rang. Both men slowly stood and walked towards the center of the Octagon, both dropped into fighting stances, Kristofer was obviously the worse for wear, his left cheek bruised from the blow he had recieved from his opponent. And then De La Mora struck again, going hard with a flurry of punches that were all easily avoided by Niedzwiedz, until he was grabbed. Both hands reached out and took ahold of the head of Niedzwiedz, the knee of De La Mora came up and struck Niedzwiedz square in the forehead, the blow sent him into complete delirium. Then the Kataha-Jime choke hold was applied. Followed closely by De La Mora straddling the back of Niedzwiedz, his weight pressed the young man to the floor, his spine was bent the wrong way as he was sent into sleep. The last words heard by Niedzwiedz were; 'Dé la bienvenida al Reino chico pequeño.' Welcome to the Kingdom little boy. Winner: Enrique De La Mora via submission New Signee Press Conference The hustle and bustle around the reasonably large room seemed to drown out the requests of the small, well-dressed man standing at the front of the conference room, his arms raised as he calls for quiet. The room was buzzing, people taking seats facing the table where our smartly-dressed spokesman stood in front of. Numerous cameras’ flashbulbs were going off as he tried to speak. In the crowd, what looked to be a mix of journalists, cameramen and important persons started to quiet down as the spokesman’s announcement is finally made audible. “If you would all like to quiet down, we will begin the Signee Conference,” he said in a deep, Mid-European accent. Walking around the right of the table, he took a seat next to two other gentlemen, one dressed in a similar manner to him, clad in a silk suit, a smart bowler hat sitting atop his head, resting nicely on a full head of long, golden hair. The other man is completely different – his extravagant robes looked like something out of a fairytale, the golden stitching tying beautiful pieces of silk together, their bronze-coloured designs as flamboyant as the rest of the outfit, the fine garment sitting snugly over his massive frame. The spokesman moved forward in his seat, pulling a black microphone towards him as light feedback echoed around the room. “Excuse me...Well, thank you all for coming, and I hope that everything you hope to get out of this conference will come your way during the duration. First of all, let me introduce myself – My name is Adabi Tymier, and I am the Official Representative for the Turkish Royal Family. Today, I am here on behalf of a member of the Turkish Royal Family, the man you see sitting next to me,” stated the Tymier, motioning towards the robe-bearing monster in attendance. “This man, as some of you may know through Internet Rumors, is the latest edition to the FLATLINED fighting roster, so it is my pleasure to introduce one of Turkey’s most upstanding individuals, Prince Andreas Michel Odessa,” grinning with glee as he finished. A slight applause rose from the crowd as Andreas nodded towards the reporters, not really giving much away about himself. Adabi continues, the grin still plastered across his face, “And beside him is his personal advisor, Mr. Pierre Krieve. Now, I think it would be a good time to take some questions from some of the budding journalists out there,” he said with a chuckle. A few hands raised in the crowd, but Tymier pointed to a young man, a spectacle wearing fanzine column writer, holding a thick notepad and a bundle of sheets, “Brian Carbury, Octagon News.com...Is there any particular reason why you have came to Flatlined from Turkey, Mr Odessa, as it seems a long way to come for a career you have little experience in,” questioned Carbury. Odessa pulled a microphone towards himself as he slowly sat up in the chair, the full extent of his massive frame now prominent, “As I explained to the Ff management when I applied, I am here to further my career as a Fighter, but also to further the reputation of my homeland, Turkey, which, in recent, has suffered great losses due to terrorist affiliations, which I can assure you, the Royal Family has nothing to do with.” Carbury nodded and took a few notes before sitting down again. Adabi wasted no time in picking another buddy reporter to ask a question or two. This time, a middle-aged, grey haired journalist got his shot. “Martin LeSous, Fighting Weekly Magazine...How do you react to claims of your Royal status being a ‘meal ticket’ to your spot on the Flatlined roster,” asked LeSous, looking pleased with the way he delivered the question – not exactly with how Odessa looked in response to it. Odessa grabbed the mic and pulled it right up to his mouth as he stood up out of his chair. Pierre and Adabi looked a little surprised at their client’s actions. “How DARE you degrade my family like this! Who do you think you are, coming along to a conference of this sort, and simply bringing down the tone with your cheap remarks?! My hiring for this company was based on the fact that I am one of the biggest, and toughest, competitors you’ll find around this business...Not the fact that I was brought up as part of a Royal Family! So maybe you should think before you speak again, American!” With that done and dusted, Andreas flicked the microphone off of the table after slamming it down in anger, marching past Adabi as Pierre quickly followed him, trying to calm Odessa down as he walked off through a set of double doors, presumably leading to the Fighters area. Adabi looked around nervously before taking the microphone he used earlier from the Technical desk next to the large conference table and addressing the crowd. “I’m afraid to say this conference will not be continuing, but you can thank Mr. LeSous over there for that,” he barked, eyeballing LeSous heavily before handing the microphone back to a technician and heading off after his fellow countrymen. Anger Management Davidson remained in the usual spot where he would be found. His pen sped over the papers and it was obvious he was deeply involved in whatever he was doing. The pen shot from his fingers and soared as Takeo Brown busted through the door. A strong breeze of air shot from his direction after he slammed it shut with one easy push. Davidson rose from his desk, but Takeo didn’t stop in place, instead his massive figure increased pace towards him. A deranged glint shined in Takeo’s eyes, and his fists remained tightly gripped at his sides. “Yo’ Davidson, I want a fight signed. Me versus that punk ass!” Davidson was aggravated. ”Let me guess, by punk ass, you mean Jamie Pierce. You want a fight against Jamie Pierce? Again?” ”Ya' hearin’ right, Davidson? I said I want that punk ass bitch, Jamie Pierce! He ain’t nothin’ compared to me. Nobody makes a fool outta’ Takeo Brown, nobody!” Davidson shook his head about to lose control of his temper, but then decided to think it over. All the office needed was another hot temper for things to really end up in chaos. Takeo remained in his enraged state as Davidson considered the request. He sat back down and started strumming with a finger, remembering that Jamie Pierce wouldn’t be fighting in any of the weeks to come. Jared wondered how Takeo would handle the news, obviously like all bad news, he wouldn’t take it well at all. “Sorry, Takeo. Jamie Pierce won’t be fighting you anytime in the near future. He’s booked until-“ And then Davidson cut off completely in mid sentence, because he was dawning on a point that he didn’t want to touch on. Takeo pressed nearer until he caught a glimpse of the magazine cover. As he started to realize, he understood what was happening and his nostrils flared once the new image of the cover popped into his head. However, as Takeo look back towards Davidson, his face turned calm. It was something very unlikely to occur, unless there was a good reason. ”Can’t wait for the photo shoot.” He said, his lips changing to a not so right smile. “You won’t be at the photo shoot. I didn’t ask for you to be on the cover this year.” Takeo sneered. “If the God of Fight ain’t on the cover, then who is?” Davidson leaned back in the chair and turned deadly serious. “Jamie Pierce. The reason he can’t fight you is because he’ll be the one on the cover. Sorry to say this Takeo, but it looks like your days are through as being the top dog. Unless you save yourself, there’s no hope of you ever getting back in the spotlight. In their eyes anymore, you’re just a nobody. After losing to Liphe Gonne, and then Jamie Pierce, they could give less of a damn about you. You’ve failed in their eyes.” The words hurt Takeo but they were words already heard the past week. He was a desperate man and a desperate man would do anything. That would include pleading, but Takeo Brown was a desperate, arrogant man. “Put me on the cover. Cause’ The Franchise is makin’ a comeback.” It only took Davidson seconds to answer. ”Flatlined Fighting isn’t going down in your sinking ship, Takeo. Prove to me that you can win again, no, prove to yourself that you can win again. Not for the sake of being on the cover, because it’s too late this year after your series of losses, but for the sake of your job. Whatever you use to have…I suggest you find it.” Davidson said, turning away from Takeo after he finished. Takeo rocked the desk with a fist. His hand slammed fiercely and papers, pens, folders, scattered into the air all at once. “Ya’ fuckin’ sign me to a fight gainst’ Pierce, Davidson. Ya’ fuckin’ do it or else.” Davidson rocked the desk his self as he stood to his feet. He leaned over the desk and faced Takeo Brown face to face. ”You know, Takeo. Maybe if you actually acted your age then I might consider giving you a re-fight. But you disgust me. NOW GET THE FUCK OUT OF MY OFFICE!!” Takeo Brown scoffed at Davidson and almost nodded as he turned his back. It was as if he realized that this was bound to happen soon enough. Davidson’s face flushed with anger as Takeo mumbled on his way out. He disappeared around the corner, shaking his head and muttering obscenities under his breath. Jared took his seat allowing his anger level to lower, but then it jumped off the charts, as he picked up the magazine and threw it against the wall. Opportunity Strikes Twice Enrique De La Mora was walking backstage, when suddenly he was pulled into a room to his own confusion. A bulky hand was placed over his mouth, almost suffocating him, as he attempted to push the other person off him. The other person dragged him farther away from the hallway. All he could see was the light slipping itself farther away from him, but then it completely snapped off as the door was slammed close. ”No harm meant. I just want ta’ talk.” A voice said, as it unhanded him into the darkness. If they hadn’t said there was no harm meant, then Enrique would already be striking wildly at them. A light snapped on. And standing before Enrique was Takeo Brown, folding his arms across his chest, almost as if to appraise the fighter before him. Enrique stared confusedly at Takeo, but what he was about to be confronted with would soon be put out into the open. ”You take bribes?” Takeo asked. Enrique stayed silent for a moment but then nodded his head. Takeo nodded also and a devilish smile broke his lips, obviously happy that Enrique answered the question correctly. “We have the opportunity to help one nother’. You’re fightin’ Jamie Pierce next week, and I need to one up him. Specially’ after the fool took ma’ spot on the magazine cover. I gotta’ teach the bitch a lesson.” Enrique nodded, asking him to go on. And he went on. “I want ya’ to mess em’ up real bad. You bloody his face up and I’ll give ya’ some extra cash and bonus points if ya’ cheap shot em’. I want the punk’s face fucked, ya’ hear me? And if ya’ ain’t doin’ the job right, ya’ ain’t get the money. But if ya’ do it right, ya’ get it.” Takeo extended his hand for a handshake. For the deal to be completed. Enrique seemed hesitant, but then he realized the pay off, and they shook. ”It’s a deal then.” Takeo said, as Enrique nodded and took his leave. It didn’t take 37 seconds to finish the deal, but it was close to it. All that was needed to be said was said, leaving Takeo Brown to simply wait and then watch as Jamie Pierce’s world crumbled. An ugly face could never get on the cover, but if all goes to plan, Takeo Brown’s thrilled face will. Dénouement This was it… the week's preparations, or lack of, would all culminate here tonight in the octagon. It was perhaps the end of time in a period of Umaga’s life where he was looking forward to better days. Tonight would signal how far he had come in such a short time. It would either be testimony to his character, or a divine signal from the sacrosanct being above to change his ways… He felt good… damn he felt very good. His whole body, mind, soul had never felt so cleansed; the journey through the forest had done its good. The tranquillity of it all had made him focused on the task in hand. Hit or be hit… Crush or be crushed… Defeat the Lion, or walk no more. The ominous figure of Umaga cast a decadent shadow on the nearby wall, his flowing locks dangled lifeless in front of his eyes. Umaga stood, eyes closed, his arms hanging limply at his side. He was at peace with himself. Reihana lifted his arm slowly and tugged both of his gloves so that they fitted tightly. The sequence began… Umaga turned slowly and hit the play button on the nearby stereo system: ‘The Message’ by Nas slowly started. Umaga lightly skipped on the spot, swinging his large arms in all directions. He began to shadow box, gaining rhythm with each strike, every blow having the power of death over life. He became the Pacific Lion… … Dénouement … Life began here… Xyler Vayin VS Umaga Reihana Umaga Reihana His tangled mane dangled heavily from the short-lived downpour of avian bottled water as the assigned official, gave him the rundown of the rules and regulations. Master of the unfamiliar African martial art, Masikan, Reihana closed his eyes and sighed heavily as his musical introduction faded out of earshot. His eyes were still firmly closed, as he made his way into the eye of the public. They responded with a heartfelt applaud, as the young lion made his way to the octagon. Xyler Vayin He stood with the look of a man who greeted fear with a sword and a samurai mask, because he was The Beast From the Southeast. Vayin was not a well-known individual, but an assassin nonetheless, his aggression had been known to escalate during combat. Mudshovel – Staind… he walked with purpose… a purpose to defeat the Pacific Lion. Both men stood in the octagon, eyeball to eyeball, as the referee began to instruct the competitors. Both men turned and proceeded to walk to their respected corners… Last minute words… Umaga strolled to the middle of the octagon, he stood in his familiar loose stance, Vayin approached Reihana and smiled a cocky smile. Trigger one… Umaga swung wildly at Vayin, who parried the shot downwards, Vayin goes low and attempts a double legged tackle. Vayin slams Umaga to the mat and attempts an early submission hold, gripping Umaga’s knee in a vice like hold. Vayin attempts to add more pressure to the hold, but is kicked into the cage by the domineering Umaga. Umaga slowly stands, but is taken by surprise by a rapid assault of quick fire kicks to the head. Vayin rattles combos of kicks like a machine gunner into the side of Umaga’s skull. Umaga does well to counter; he catches Vayin’s leg and wrenches it. Vayin falls to the surface, but is quick to rise…he is looking for the kill. Smack… Vayin unleashes a plethora of stiff left hand shots to the face of Umaga, each one growing with impact. Umaga kneels on one knee, making flurried attempts to block The Beast. Vayin raises his muscular left arm for a final impact… Ouch… Umaga rises and nails a shoulder into the exposed ribcage of Vayin, before running and spearing him into the cage wall. Vayin bounces back from the cage only to be drilled by a somewhat sloppy Uranage takedown. Vayin, somehow manages to stand; Umaga turns to face him, he swings, but his huge paw is evaded by Vayin, who flips Umaga over with a rolling armbar. Vayin uses all his weight to try and hyper extend the arm of Umaga, but the Pacific Lion is just too strong…Umaga snatches his arm away from Vayin… he rolls onto his stomach as Xyler gathers his thoughts. He has to put him away… Both men stand, Umaga looking worse for wear… WHAM! Vayin hits a perfect judo side leg throw, whipping Umaga to his back, he looks to apply some sort of submission… Ding… End Round One Vayin obviously pumped from his superb early round showing did not converse with his corner man, showing a slight cocky edge to him. Umaga, on the other hand sat in his corner, alone, shut out and hurt. He had allowed the smaller man the advantage, perhaps his preparation was sub par… Umaga stood, his eyes fiery with animosity, Vayin smiled in his direction. The crowd responded with a hushed silence… Vayin stood groggily, but was immediately slammed to the ground. Umaga hit a superb Sambo slam variation. Umaga immediately sprung into life, his whole body felt renewed. Vayin stood slowly… unaware of… Crack… Crack… Crack… Umaga hit three consecutive uppercuts, each landing perfectly on the chin. This only slightly rocked Vayin, who staggered slightly, Umaga charged in and ducked a groggy forearm. Umaga locked in a vice like grip around Vayin’s waist, with his immensely powerful arms, and slammed Vayin to the ground with a horrific back slam. The bell came just in time… Ding… End Round Two Strangely enough… the fight had been a tale of very different proportions. On one hand Vayin’s epic showing in the first round, then Umaga’s power display in the second. Vayin had surprisingly not been knocked out by the back slam. It would’ve knocked a lesser man clean out… Umaga was the first to the middle of the octagon… he outstretched his gloved hand to Vayin, as a sign of respect, Vayin however did not return the gesture, and shunned the hand. The crowd jeered ever so slightly. The last round was like a game of chess, involving partial submissions, counters, interspersed with some heavy sparring and punches. It could’ve gone either way… Umaga’s powerful shots were making their marl, on the other hand Vayin’s submissions were slowly wearing down the Pacific Lion… 10… Both men break from a mutual clinch, Umaga waits leaning on the cage… 9… Vayin prepares himself for one last attack… 8… Charge! 7… Vayin hurtles towards Umaga… 6… Vayin is within striking distance… BANG… The arena seemed to move in ghastly slow motion as the time descended slowly… Umaga stepped backwards, his flowing mane flapping manically in the octagon. Vayin was close enough to smell Umaga… 5… Umaga pushes his foot onto the cage… 4… He pushes up and off into the air… 3… CRUNCH. Vayin collapses in a crumpled heap, his head dropping loosely on his shoulder… Umaga had leapt from the side of the cage and hit a hellacious rising knee uppercut to the exposed chin of Vayin… that was it… The Last Resignation… The referee immediately called for a knockout victory…Vayin’s ringside crew dived into the octagon, as both men lay crumpled and battered from the gruelling encounter. Cameramen set up their equipment while photographs snapped shots that would be worth thousands of dollars if it was hot off the presses. The setting was plain: a long table covered with blue decoration cloaks, a black, wooden pedestal was designated for the speaker as next to him sat 4 chairs, 2 on each side. The chairs closest to the stand had tall glasses of water intended for the two superstars who would attend the press conference. Everyone was at their guards, news people, photographs, cameramen, they were all ready for the oncoming commedia dell'arte. This was the media's battleground.. Press Conferences. Impatient fans whom had won tickets to attend the event sat near the middle of the rows of chairs, feeling engulfed, outsiders behind enemy lines when suddenly, the blue curtains broke their still form and the horses were off. Cameramen shot pictures at rapid fire, not knowing or caring who stepped through the curtains and reporters stirred nervously, waiting to pounce on their opportunity to yell out questions, either ignored or responded by the athletes.. This was the rush of being in the media business. To their delight, the first man to have appeared from beyond the curtains was owner of Flatlined fighting. Clad in a black three piece suit and a blue, thinly striped dress shirt with lighter shades of that same color shirt, tucked in his pants. To accentuate and finish the ensemble he wore a navy blue tie, striped in barbershop pole fashion in a lighter blue gloss. Behind him walked out a monster of a man garmented in a simple white shirt, taking the contours and ripples in his muscular frame and a pair of old-fashioned blue jeans. He strapped the title over his shoulder and walked out with a grin, facing the media directly. He sat to Davidson's right as the man in charge of the promotion stood near the plinth. Soon afterwards came out Flatlined fighting's bad boy himself, Javier Garcia. The hottest signee on the market was also the hottest head in the industry. Arrogance and lordliness lived in his step. He walked over to Jared and shook his hand before sitting to Davidson's left, all without taking a single glint towards Gonne. The wave of pictures stopped shortly after everyone was in place and the room's volume muted. 'First off, let me welcome you ladies and gentlemen to the official press conference and the official contract signing for the match between our proud champion, sitting to my right, Liphe Gonne.. and the most talked about free agent in the game, to my left.. Javier Garcia, at Flatlined fighting's first Pay-Per-View, Pride & Victory. The proceedings this evening will be simple. Firstly, a standard weigh in for each competitor to assure they are heavyweight fighters.. but just by looking at these two muscle monsters we can already assess this as a fact.' The crowd of reporters, tech-men and fans laughed at the obvious. 'Secondly, we will receive questions from the media and answer them to the best of our abilities and finally, the official signing for this bout's contract. So, I invite first the man to my right, once again, heavyweight champion, Liphe Gonne to come and weigh himself. He stripped down to his boxers to the appreciation of the females in the room. Gonne floated over to the scale, a wretched old thing, so cliche as in doctor's offices. It had a black base to stand on, and a chipped white stand holding up its main mechanism. 'Gonne'.' A few chuckles ran through the room but Liphe did not find it so funny. He glared over towards Garcia, giving him a look of death but the puerto rican fighter was not fazed. The official weight was given by the man who stood next to the scale.. 'Two Hundred and Nineteen Pounds.' Gonne headed back towards his designated seating place and sat down after giving Garcia another look, trying to intimidate him though again, The Cinder Block was not impressed. His name was called by Jared and he stood up, walking towards the scale, ignoring his opponent. He stood on the scale and yawned. 'Two Hundred and Forty Pounds.' Garcia hopped off the scale, content with the fact he had 21 pounds extra, for he knew this would serve as leverage in the bout. Both men sat at their respective seats, none looked happy to be here. After the weights had been jotted down, Davidson got back on the pedestal and continued his task as master of ceremonies.
