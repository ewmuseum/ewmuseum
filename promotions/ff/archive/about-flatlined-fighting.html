About Flatlined Fighting

May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
About Flatlined Fighting Flatlined is a new concept being brought to this circle, a concept based on real mixed martial arts (MMA) federations such as UFC and PrideFC. With Jermz at the helm, Flatlined has been a hot topic in small discussion circles for some time. For those of you not familiar with real fighting feds, here's some background information. Fighting organizations like the UFC compile a nucleus of the best fighting talents across the globe (ranging in styles and variations), and then put them in a caged octagon to settle their differences. Flatlined is no different. Winners aren't decided by pinfall or countout...they are decided via knockout, submission, or judge/referee decision... or can be decided by the fighter's trainer if he/she decides to throw in the towel for their combatant. Ken Shamrock, and his father were great products of this real-life fighting league. As many of you wrestling fans know, Ken made the switch to WWF in the late 90's. This sport isn't professional wrestling, and you won't see combatants 'selling' high risk attacks or grapples. These people do it for real. Actually, many real fighting organizations are illegal in the states. It's noted by many judiciary committees as simply 'human cock fighting'. The outcomes are often too brutal to claim responsibility for. Sometimes these organizations will hold or tape their events in foreign countries where it is perfectly legal. Another notable difference between pro-wrestling and conventional fighting feds is the fact that vignettes, or televised segments are purely non-existent in these fighting organizations. Sure, there will be segments on the Flatlined shows, but they will stress more of the real-lives of the characters off camera. Besides trying to exemplify a pure fighting experience, Flatlined is also trying to express an acute sense of realism to its viewers. This will be accomplished by the real and tangible characters working for the company. All of them have been picked and hired by Flatlined because of their great skill and personality. You won't see any pre-packaged gimmick wrestlers one often sees in eW feds. These are all real human beings, enduring a dangerous life filled with numerous scars. Moving on, Flatlined is attempting to cater to its viewing audiences with interactive fan items, as well as extensive research and historical information. There will be a betting script that allows fans to bet, and vote, by picking the winners each week/PPV. There will also be a fan art section, where aspiring artists can house their Flatlined inspired art. Next, there will be many content-loaded informational pieces scattered throughout to interest and inform its readers. With great assistance from [name withheld] (Flatlined's own MMA resource man), Flatlined will have extensive research on fighting styles found throughout the world. There will also be a decades section which will describe 19th Century decades seen through the news of fighting. Example: The civil rights movement and the uproar that was caused when boxing champion Muhammad Ali refused to enlist into the army. This affects Flatlined's approach towards boxing (as they, in the 60's were a tiny regional boxing league named the New Jersey Boxing Organization) because Muhammad Ali, at the time, and even in present day, was a boxing icon. Lastly, there will be a Dojo section, where you can read true history about specific gyms that Ff's fighters honed their skills in. As of late, members and aspiring applicants have noticed an extremely thorough application process. Also known as 'The Application from Hell,' Flatlined's complicated acquisition process has turned many prospective members away. 'It is a very complex application, I know... but I feel that if we are to portray this new environment to eW adequately, I need to know that the applicants I accept are competent in the world of MMA and know what they want their characters to be like,' commented Jermz. 'I want a full, competent roster that feels comfortable feuding with any member of that roster. To accomplish this, the application rule must be enforced. From here on out, there will be no 'free rides' in Ff unless I see a completed application, for the reasons stated earlier.' Flatlined also requests of its applicants that the characters stay exclusive to Flatlined (we're not asking for handler exclusiveness here, just character exclusiveness) because we feel, here at Flatlined Fighting, that it would be ludicrous for a Sports Entertainer to be a part of the organization, as he wouldn't last with the quality of the trained professionals that hang around in Flatlined's backstage area. So finally, we are coming to the point where the line separating reality from fiction is becoming more and more blurred by the moment. With the launching of Flatlined Fighting, it's safe to say that the line will not only break…it will shatter to the likes no one has ever seen or have dreamed about. A sound roster and even sounder backstage team round out what can only be seen as a juggernaut for the next generation of 'e-simulation'. Are you ready to be Flatlined?
