Guilty Pleasures 1 - Cutting Bread, Cutting Loose Frank Minio February 10, 2003 FTF gym, Henderson, NV Bright Sunlight. Reality seemed so distant. The sunlight hit the window, light prism showing a rainbow, an orgy of color onto the glass of orange juice sitting on the breakfast nook. The only well kept spot in the entire upstairs apartment over the Fist to Foot Gym, the eating quarters. Frank stared at the glass, dancing in color as he smeared jelly on a piece of toast. It wasn't exactly following his diet, but he had learned that always doing things right wasn't exactly putting wins under his belt. He could at least enjoy himself as he lived. His face stared idle and his hands had minds of their own, one palming the underside of the crisp browned toast, the other forming a figure eight with a butter knife and jam on the tip. Frank always was particular about the evening of condiments. Always EVEN. Always ALOT. He always made sure that the extra flavor on whatever he was consuming was dripping from it. Ketchup, Jelly, Barbecue sauce, when he was younger he even liked it when cheese slipped off his pizza. Some people are just perculiar like that. Frank broke the silence by clearing his throat, and then did the unexpected by breaking the light show being performed by two close friends of his, Sunlight and the Window, by wrapping his hands around the Orange Juice. For a moment he glared at the prisms on his knuckles, then lifted the glass to his lips and sipped with little effort. He gulped, and gulped, and gulped, eventually killing the entire content of the glass, then he looked around with a guilty grin, and lifted the Orange Juice carton that was next to him and filled his glass again till the last drop. 'Mornin' Frank.' Ramon's voice broke the previously tripped code of silence that the room around the two men held. Ramon wandered past Frank reading some print out of local bookies. Numbers that were ran about the bets being made on Flatlined Fighting. Ramon always was into the underground, he was always a fan of easy money. Not quite a betting man, but he did keep his eyes on what was being said, being done, about his fighters and their surroundings. 'Whats up?' Frank asked between crunching on his toast. Horendo walked behind Ramon and headed straight for the fridge, grabbing an apple out then walking out of the room again, almost completely unnoticed. Ramon waited a long minute or so before resting the paper down then laying his attention on Frank. 'Not much, I tried calling Lucas again today. No answer. That cock sucker. I can't believe he did that to us.' Frank corrected Ramon. 'To me.' And Ramon smirked, grabbing the Orange Juice container and an empty glass nearby. He lifted dead air. 'Both... Did you drink all of the damned Orange Juice again?' Frank let out a forced laugh as he finished his smothered toast. He moved onto the Potatoes he had prepared, shredded, home fry style. He loved his hash browns. It had been a while since he had enjoyed them, he was on a strange diet that Horendo had put him on, some power diet but Frank stayed away from any food he enjoyed, he figured if he liked it, it wasn't good for him. He used the salt shaker to completely douse the potatoes in the unhealthy snow, as a teen Frank put salt on everything. His family hated that. He loved it. 'Guilty pleasure, so sue me.' he would say to them. 'Thats alot of Salt, Frank.' 'Guilty pleasure, so sue me.' Frank grinned. He grinned in a way that only someone breaking a diet after losing all self confidence could grin. He grinned in a way that only someone who had lost a bout because a trainer with a stick up his ass tossed in the towel because he didn't like the way Frank applied a submission could. He grinned in a way that only someone who fell in love with a woman he barely knew and let her destroy his ego could. He grinned in a way that only someone with a complete lack of abandon could. Strap in folks. Its looking to be a wild ride. View Frank Minio's Biography View Frank Minio's Chapter Archive View Frank Minio's Gallery
