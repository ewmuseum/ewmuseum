Guilty Pleasures 2 - Drinking from the Carton Frank Minio February 10, 2003 FTF gym, Henderson, NV 'Horendo! You ass! Thats my milk too!' Ramon screamed at the top of his powerful lungs. A few short seconds ago he walked into the kitchen area reading his printout of bets when he saw a sight that he never pictured in his head. Horendo was standing with his back arched and his neck cocked backwards, gulping down the milk from the carton. His mouth. On milk that Ramon drank from, in a glass of course. Horendo's mouth. 'Your fucking mouth is on my milk man!' 'And I back washed too.' Horendo laughed deep. Thats how he laughed. He finished off the milk, slapping it down coldly on the counter, next to the Orange Juice. Today Ramon had a craving for OJ, and Milk, and he arrived in the kitchen twice with both items just to watch them be finished off and laughed at. He was growing angry about this. And he began formulating a plan. Frank emerged from the doorway into the kitchen, walking with his coffee mug towards the counter. He pour a cup, steaming hot still off the warmer, then poured an extra amount of sugar, and a bit of the creamer into it. He stirred, staring into space and walked back into the other room to watch some tapes of other fighters. Horendo watched Frank grace out of the room then turned to Ramon, who's mind was moving a thousand miles an hour under the surface. 'He sure is taking it rough.' 'Huh?' 'Frank... he's taking that loss rough. This is the worst he's been after any of them. He has so much potential. I can't wait till he really proves himself. Really becomes that man he wants to be.' 'Yea. He was pretty happy when he suckered Lucas a few weeks ago, maybe we should find that cock sucker and let Frank have a go on him.' Ramon's suggestion tempted Horendo, who felt extra guilty about the entire situation. He was the one who left the camp and came back only with the addition of Lucas who he knew would teach Frank submissions. Frank knew more now, but at what cost? Horendo had finally decided that it was time to do something. To get physical. He looked side to side, patted his hands on his pant pockets and nodded to himself. 'I'll be right back Ramon, I gotta... make a call.' 'Uh, alright.' He walked quickly out of the room and Ramon's ideas sparked. He turned, grabbing the creamer, then poured it down the sink. He took the apples from the fridge, taking a knife and began skinning them over the sink, the creamer still swirling down the drain. He was up to no good. And he was grinning like a child. He was grinning like an idiot. He skinned the remaining apples and laughed lowly to himself, glancing over his shoulder to make sure no one was coming.
