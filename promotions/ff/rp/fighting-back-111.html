Fighting Back. Chester Ramis February 08, 2003 St. Paul, Minnesota Chester woke up, only to hear a knock at his front door. He rubbed the sinuses above the bridge of his nose as the nausea in his head began to fade off. He had been throwing up alot in the past few days, and he had lost about five pounds with the trailing flu that plagued his body. It would be inevitable that he would move to the light-heavy ranks because of this. 'Who is it?' Chester croaked weakly. The door opened without a response. Nathan Longstreet entered and took off his coat and scarf, and hung it up next to the door. 'Just thought I'd come to check on you,' Nathan stated. Chester rolled his eyes, then his body onto his side. He sat up on the couch and rubbed his forehead. 'I'm fine.' 'Oh, that's really great to know. I just hope Ff is a little more sympathetic...' Nathan commented. 'What do you mean?' Chester asked, totally forgetting the pending answer. 'You were supposed to fight last night, but you didn't. I'm sure that Jacob Meyer's is awfully disappointed that you couldn't show up to play...' 'I don't care Nathan,' Chester responded. And he was telling the truth. He really didn't care. He felt as if the past week would have been his last, and he didn't really feel like falling victim to Nathan's chastising. 'Are you feeling any better, Chester?' 'Yeah... A little.' 'Yeah, I'm sure that stress didn't help it much.' 'Huh?' 'I got your message, kid. About deciding to teach at the university. Let me just say that you are getting into some deep shit here. I know you're worried. I knew you were weary of how I'd react. I could hear it in your voice. A voice I've heard too many times. A voice wishing to move on' 'B-But..' 'I know you're different Chester. That's the problem. I think you know full-well just what you're getting yourself into, and I admire your ambition. But the thing is, I don't think Jared can defend you much longer. I got you booked against Meyer's next week, and you better go out there like you own the place. You need to fight like a bat out of hell to finally get some respect that I know you deserve for all of this hard work.' A much needed silence seperated them, as Chester slowly digested the words. He finally nodded and looked up at Nathan like a child seeking parental guidance. 'What am I gonna do, Nathan?' Nathan clasped the front of Chester's right shoulder and replied softly: 'You're just going to have to suck it up and do it all.'
