Tangled Web Of Deceit [8] Ijinza Yebanka March 31, 2003 Richmond [March 24 2003] Ijinza looked down at his bowl of dried noddles and smiled; it was dinner time. He had worked all day long and that really whet his appetite. Finally, though, Luke had a chance to sit down and rest. He could do so while eating, which was the bonus. And the even better thing; it was his favourite dish. Dried chilli noodles with mushrooms, four fishballs, tons of fishcake, minced and a juicy hard-boiled egg. Fejona, on the other hand, was simply having fried rice with a lot of soya sauce and a small piece of sirloin steak on the side. The two cousins were enjoying dinner in the confines of their motel room in Richmond. It was almost nine o' clock and for Asians to eat that late, it's shocking. Of course, Ijinza & Fejona have been living in the United States for many years now and it was common for them to eat later than usual. Sometimes, they even went out for dinner at half past ten. 'Let's eat!' Fejona declared, as she began slicing up her sirloin steak with a knife and a fork while glancing at a newspaper on the table. Luke nodded his head and made a very quick prayer, before picking up his chopsticks. Being the older of the two, it would usually be Min who would declare when it was suitable to commence eating. Another Asian custom, that has started to erode due to the way youngsters think nowadays. For non-Oriental Asians, the rule isn't as heavily practiced but it's better than like what some Singaporean whose name starts with the letter K does; bring his food up to his room and talks to girls on the phone while eating. ...Right. Of course, it looked like the years of immersing herself in the American culture made Fejona completely abandon her Asian beliefs. She was eating steak and donned trendy clothing. One that revealed a wee bit too much skin. Of course, unknown to Luke, she was also playing the role of a slut to attain whatever she wanted. Prostituition, they say, is the oldest trade in the world. But maiming or killing one you've slept with for money does seem a tad extreme. Fejona, however, is one of few who actually fit into that mould. Luke, on the other hand, had stuck to most of the traditions he had learnt back home, for a very simple reason. His mother passed down those traditions to him. And he valued his mother very much. Gulping down a fishball and glancing at Fejona, Ijinza began to think of his day. It had been hectic from the get-go. From the second he woke up, the Cambodian's cell-phone had been ringing incessantly. It annoyed him to high-heaven but owning a chain of restaurants never promised to be an easy task. On the contrary; the logistics involved can make a grown man drop to his knees and wail like a baby. One of the problems the Cambodian faced in the morning was a supply shortage of certain spices vital to the creation of some of his restaurant's trademark soups. Without the spices, the soups would have to be taken off the menu and that meant a huge dip in revenue; the soups were one of the franchise's hottest selling item. It would also reflect badly on Ijinza and his management skills. Word would go around, and patronage would nosedive. Having obtained enough slices of her steak, Fejona pushed her hair out of her eyes and folded the newspaper to allow for easier reading, before digging in. Ijinza couldn't help but keep glancing at her. Whenever they went out for dinner, many people would mistake them for being a couple. It was weird; during the day, most of the time, the two would constantly disagree. At night, after all the work had completed, the two cousins could unwind and be very carefree. Hence the reason quite a few people thought they were a couple. Luke had to admit, as he slurped down the minced chicken, that Fejona was pretty attractive. And that he quite liked it being seen with her in public. Meant people wouldn't think he was gay or whatever. 'What?' Fejona asked as she caught her cousin staring at her oddly. 'Nothing. You just... look very fresh today.' Min blushed, as she forced a spoonful of her rice down her throat. Women always love being complimented but for her own cousin to do so? After all the arguments they had gone through over the years? Quite amazing. 'Thanks. You, on the other hand, look very tired. You do remember what is tomorrow, right?' Yebanka nodded his head as he used his chopsticks to isolate the mushrooms from the minced chicken. fightSHOW. Final of the Lightweight Title Tournament. Against the one and only Chester Ramis. Fejona Min was on the edge of anticipation as well. fightSHOW would be huge. It would decide whether Ijinza experienced joy or plunged into the depths of despair, like she wanted him to. But even she had to admit, her cousin stood a great chance of winning. He'd manage to get to the Final, albeit due to a bye the previous week. Ijinza wasn't too pleased with that and promptly focused his energies on his restaurant chain. Min was hoping for that mistake to come back and haunt Luke. The tangled web of deceit was now only beginning to be weaved, as the two cousins continued their dinner in silence. None of them knowing what awaited in the months to come.
