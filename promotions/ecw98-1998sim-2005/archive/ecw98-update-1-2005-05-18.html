I will run all characters up until we sign some members, if you decide to join as a character you will begin at his status in the fed and have to work your way up. IE: Main Eventer, Mid-Carder, etc; We will hold one card a week every Saturday called Hardcore TV and it will air on TNN. The results will all be brief and summarized except for our pay-per-view events which will be broadcast in full play by play by Joey Styles. So if you decide you would like to recreate some of the most hardcore matches and redefine history, you may join as any wrestler available that is on our roster or as any free agent in the real world as long as they're not signed to a written contract with any other federation. I will make a list of characters that are in written contracts with other promotions. After people have joined on as characters, results will be determined only for pay-per-view events by roleplaying and participation, our weekly show results will be decided upon push/status and based on simulation. When roleplaying, the rules are general. Don't break kayfabe, use cursing only to an extent, and don't use anyone else's character without their permission. When roleplaying for a pay-per-view or supercard event we will have a certain formula: Brackets [] for actions and your wrestler last name in all caps for speaking. DREAMER: This is what I mean. If you wish to join, just contact me at [detail withheld] with your desired wrestler and a sample writing. Great!
