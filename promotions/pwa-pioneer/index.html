Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Pioneer Wrestling Association ran a weekly TV show, "Wednesday Night Chaos," and its own annual calendar of named PPVs (Chaos, Rampage, Genesis, Battledome, Summer Sizzler, and others). Its own home page names an "AOWF Homepage" link under a section headed "(AOWF feds)" alongside JWA, W4F, BWA, and MW/UHWA, and its own official message board is hosted directly on the alliance's own domain (aowfmedia.org) rather than a separate board host — the fed identifies itself as part of the Alliance of Wrestling Federations on its own terms, not merely as a guest booked on the alliance's own broadcasts.

Its own served Title History page runs one of the richest dated lineages in this collection's own holdings, now fully chained here across fifty-nine recorded reigns. Tundra won the PWA World Title's own founding tournament final at "Chaos" on 30 March 1999; the ledger's own last attested change crowns Aura at "Thriller" on 14 November 2002. Between them the belt changes hands through drawn-out gauntlets, mid-match vacancies, and, three times running in the summer of 2002, a split belt shared by two co-champions before Fallen Angel unifies it outright.

"Wednesday Night Chaos," 5 January 2000, survives as a full broadcast script, now structured match by match: Nightstryker opens the show over the rookie Aries; Creed wins by disqualification over the Magician, who is run off by two masked men wearing reigning champion Stephen "the Monkey" McDonald's own symbol; Fletcher Moran beats Heavy Metal after a rock-paper-scissors toss settles who wrestles in Reese's place; and CY2K Cinergy's closing assault on Fear ends with Fear stretchered from the arena. The night's own Tag Team Title defense — the Brothers Grimm, Sirus Moran and Grifter, against Rage and Ashe's own Natural Selection — never reaches a decision: the lights cut out, and a debuting stable billed "the family" (Faith, suspended on a harness controlled by Blood) strings Rage and Ashe up from the rafters, closing on a promise to make Sirus "let go of his childhood" — a threat against his stuffed bear "Al" that pays off seventeen days later, when Faith wins half the Tag Team Title off Sirus in exchange for the bear's return.

A second address carries further record of the same fed: a 50%-co-owner's own personal GeoCities account, hosting bio cards for a family of characters billed as the Moran Clan — Sirus Moran, 'Al', Grifter, Fletcher, Samantha, Mack and the Prisoner, "there have never been any others part of the group." Sirus alone held the World Title five times in the corpus's own dated ledger, from a July 1999 win over Dr. Steven Remo to an August 2002 win over Nightstryker; one of those reigns — an April 2000 gauntlet win over Ironheart — ended when he vacated the belt in a loser-leaves-town match staged over 'Al's own return. His brother Fletcher Moran arrived at the PWA after two earlier, since-closed feds, one of them wrestled disguised as Sirus himself, and made his own dated PWA debut on "Wednesday Night Chaos" with a win over Heavy Metal.

Storylines

The held corpus carries one gesture at a running arc: 'Al', Sirus Moran's own stuffed bear, is taken and returned across three separate documents — referenced as recently absent on "Wednesday Night Chaos" (5 January 2000), traded for half the Tag Team Title seventeen days later per the fed's own Title History page, and given its own full backstory on Sirus's own bio card. A genuine beat-by-show recap needs more served shows than the one this wing holds; the motif is carried in the relevant characters' own tenure notes and the show's own notes field instead of a manufactured recap here.
