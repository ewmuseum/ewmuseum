TTWA — Roleplay

Battlezone: World Heavyweight Championship, Round One

16 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene pans across a sports arena, filled to capacity by screaming fans. Various signs are held up in the air, sporting sayings such as, "El Gringo Loco - it's what's for dinner!", "BLEED HERE", and "My parents went to Mentaur and all I got was this lousy sign." The camera then changes shots to Daniel Hayes and Curtis Wilson, who are both sitting behind the announcer's table sipping coffee, obviously oblivious to the camera.)
CW: ... so the guy looks at me and he says, "Rectum? Damn near killed
'im!" DH: (chuckling) I don't believe he said that. There's no way... hey, what's that? CW: What's what? DH: (pointing at the camera) That little red light... holy crud, we're LIVE! CW: (drops his coffee in a scramble to get his headset on) WHAT? DH: (quickly donning his headset) *ahem* good evening, folks, and welcome to the TTWA's FIRST Battlezone! I'm Daniel Hayes, and my partner here is...
CW: (squirming in his seat) ... really damn uncomfortable. That's some
HOT coffee! DH: As you know tonight we will be having the first eight matches of our
World Heavyweight Championship tournament, starting the road to the gold, and ending at "TTWA World War I", a month and a half away!
CW: For now, though, it looks like our friendly neighborhood announcer is finally in the ring, so let's get down there and get the camera off us for a while. There's got to be more interesting things to watch than me spilling scalding hot coffee on my crotch.
DH: (as the scene cuts to the ring) Interesting, yes. Humorous... no. (The announcer, a short fat guy in an obviously rented tuxedo, taps the microphone once and sends a deafening squeal of feedback throughout the arena. He shrugs helplessly as the crowd starts to boo him, and starts his announcements of the first match.)
RA: Ladies and gentlemen, this is our _first_ matchup for the TTWA World
Heavyweight Championship! Introducing first...
("Supernaut" by 1,000 Homo DJ's blares out over the loudspeaker as the
Bounty Hunter strides out of the entrance curtain.)
RA: ... hailing from Manhattan, New York, and weighing in at two-hundred and sixty seven pounds... BOUNTY HUNTER!
(Bounty Hunter rolls into the ring under the bottom rope and mounts the corner turnbuckle facing the aisle, staring intently down it as his music fades out and the announcer continues.)
RA: And his opponent, hailing from Athena, Georgia and weighing in at two hundred and fifteen pounds... THE KING OF CHAOS, CHRIS CAGE!
(Offspring's "Pretty Fly (for a White Guy)" starts blasting out over the loudspeakers now, as Bounty Hunter just watches Cage emerge from the locker rooms and stride towards the ring. The voiceover cuts back to the announcers.)
DH: Cage seems pretty confident, just staring down Bounty Hunter as he makes his way to the OH MY GOD!
(The capacity crowd stands up and turns towards the entrance ramp, where a second figure brandishing a steel folding chair has sprinted down the walkway, straight at Cage. There is a resounding *ka-WHANG* as the chair richochets off the back of Cage's skull, dropping him to the ground like a sack of potatoes.)
CW: That was the Mutilator! Jakuzi Masahito has just waffled the King of
Chaos with a steel chair! Chris Cage is OUT!
DH: And look at the Bounty Hunter! He's just standing in the ring, wondering what the heck just happened out here!
(Masahito turns to the Bounty Hunter, waves, and runs back to the locker rooms.) DH: We've got the medical crew down there now, checking out Chris Cage... oh, this looks bad. They're calling for the stretcher. CW: I _love_ this! So far, we've got more injuries than matches in the TTWA! DH: Oh, be quiet. (There is a bustle of activity in the aisle, as a stretcher is wheeled down to help the semi-conscious Chris Cage out of the arena. A bearded man in a tuxedo climbs into the ring, says a few words to both the referee and the Bounty Hunter, and takes the microphone.)
RR: Ladies and Gentlemen, I'm Robert Rossel, the Commissioner of the Top
Turnbuckle Wrestling Federation.
(Somebody in the crowd yells out, "Hey, it's Vince!", and is quickly removed by security.) RR: It seems that a situation has risen here, even before the first match has even started. According to the rules of the tournament,
NO wrestler can have a first-round bye into the second round. Therefore, this match WILL happen... although not with Mr. Cage.
(There is a slight murmur from the crowd as Rossel hands an index card to the announcer, waves to the crowd, and rolls out of the ring.)
RA: *clearing his throat* Barring any more unforseen instances like this one... his opponent, hailing from New York City, and weighing in at two hundred forty-five pounds... NICK NASDAQ!
(Nick Nasdaq, resplendent in his power business suit, strides out from the back, stops at the top of the ramp, and looks the crowd over. He glances up at Bounty Hunter who, at this point, is ranting like a madman in the ring and claiming he "earned" a first-round bye, makes his way to the ringside area and slides under the bottom rope.)
--- NICK NASDAQ vs. THE BOUNTY HUNTER --- This was an excellent match from start to finish, with Nasdaq's technical skills being offset by the sheer grit and stamina of Bounty
Hunter. The Hunter nearly scored the pinfall at several points in the match, including one after hitting his "Collection" German
Suplex, although the momentum of that landed Nasdaq's feet on the bottom rope, and broke the count. Nasdaq, as well, had several near-falls, including a two-count on a sleeper hold and a schoolboy roll- up that was kicked out of at the VERY last second (the ref's hand had actually hit the mat, but waved off the three count afterwards).
The end of the match finally came when Nasdaq missed a clothesline, and Bounty Hunter attempted a crucifix roll-up. Nasdaq threw his weight forward, however, and spun Bounty Hunter off his shoulders and into the "Black Monday" neckbreaker, notching his first victory in the TTWA as well as a second-round berth in the tournament.
WINNER: Nick Nasdaq in 13:44 with the "Black Monday" Neckbreaker. --- DH: And a shocking win for Nick Nasdaq! He wasn't even scheduled to be on the card tonight, and here he is, in the second round of the tournament!
CW: That just goes to show you, Hayes. You can... (Curtis is cut off by the announcer in the ring, starting the set-up for the next match.) CW: Damn rookies. RA: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is scheduled for one fall, with a fifteen-minute time limit. Introducing first, weighing in at two hundred and forty one pounds... DARKCHYLDE!
(Darkchylde emerges from the dressing room, almost completely covered in a black cloak. He strides slowly down to the ring, climbs between the ropes, and just stands in the center while the announcer continues.)
RA: And his opponent, hailing from the Great State of Michigan, and weighing in at three hundred and fifteen pounds... THE LUMBERJACK!
("I Need a Hero" blares out over the loudspeakers as the massive Lumberjack makes his way to the ring. He nods to the fans, steps over the top rope of the ring, and looks intently across at Darkchylde who has just now shed his cloak.)
--- THE LUMBERJACK vs. DARKCHYLDE --- Darkchylde spent the first part of the match fustrating the slower Lumberjack, outrunning him and dodging him with his superior speed and technical skills. This didn't last very long, however - Lumberjack connected with a shoulder tackle that sent Darkchylde sprawling to the mat, and the big man spent a good amount of time just keeping him there with various leg drops and body slams. Finally, the match came to a heated conclusion when an extremely frustrated
Darkchylde, after stunning Lumberjack with an eye rake, proceeded to hurl a ball of fire into the face of his opponent, in full view of the referee, earning him the disqualifiation loss and a quick retreat from the ring as a slightly charred but otherwise okay Lumberjack recovered from the initial shock and chased him off.
WINNER: The Lumberjack in 8:16 via disqualification. --- CW: Okay... chairshot - check. Fireball - check. All we need to see now is someone get tossed off the top of a steel cage, and my night is complete.
DH: Obviously, things in the TTWA are shaping up to be a bit more... violent?... than we initially figured. CW: Ummm... Dan? If you didn't want violence, you should have picked a sport OTHER THAN PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING to commentate on. Like figure skating or golf or something.
DH: (sighing) They turned me down for the figure skating job. CW: And on that pitifully depressing note, let's get to ringside for the next match. (Scene cuts to the ring announcer, who clears his throat and begins speaking.) RA: Ladies and gentlemen, it's time for the next match of the evening! Introducing first, hailing from Mexico City, Mexico, and weighing in at two hundred and seven pounds... EL GRINGO LOCO!
(Mariachi music blasts over the speakers as the curtains at the top of the ramp part. El Gringo Loco strides out, his full bodysuit professionally airbrushed so that it appears that he is wearing a bright lime-green tuxedo. He waves to the fans as he jogs to ringside, apparently oblivious to the looks of total and sheer shock on the faces of the crowd as they see what he's wearing.)
CW: (voiceover) El Gringo Loco must be Spanish for "Fashion Victim". DH: (voiceover) Oh, will you be quiet. RA: (blinks as he sees El Gringo Loco, then continues) And his opponent, weighing in at two hundred and sixty-six pounds... ERIK
LESHREK! (Leshrek strides out, visibly wincing at El Gringo Loco's outfit as he sees him in the ring. Unfortunately, that ugly-assed suit started moving really quickly as Leshrek got close to the ring, with El
Gringo Loco catapulting himself off the opposite ropes, running towards Leshrek, and hurling himself over the top rope at his opponent...
.... who caught him like a frisbee and nearly powerslammed him through the concrete. The timekeeper, astute individual that he was, rang the bell as El Gringo Loco was in mid-air, signifying the start of the match, an act that was not lost on Leshrek.)
--- EL GRINGO LOCO vs. ERIK LESHREK (already in progress) --- Leshrek, after planting the luchador into the concrete, simply left him there and rolled into the ring, sitting on the second turnbuckle as the referee completed the ten-count and waved for the bell.
WINNER: Erik Leshrek in 0:22 by count-out result. --- CW: That's GOT to be a record of some kind, for fastest match. DH: Well, the official word is that it lasted twenty-two seconds - indeed, a very quick bout.
CW: One second for each inch of floor that Leshrek put El Ojo Rojo through. DH: Hang on a second... it looks like Jack Brawn is making his way down to the ring! (Scene cuts to the ringside area, where Jack Brawn has appeared. he grabs a microphone, climbs into the middle of the ring, and waves for the crowd to quiet down.)
JB: Masahito, listen up! There are always stipulations, right? Always rules! And always a way for others to get the upper hand!
Well, I have a stipulation of my own. You can have your steel cage, but we must both wear blindfolds, that way you won't see it coming when I smash you into the side of the cage, and I won't have to deal with your blood all over my hands.
(Brawn then tosses a mask into the center of the ring, hands the microphone to the ring announcer, and head back to the dressing rooms.)
DH: It seems that Brawn has accepted the Mutilator's challenge, and added his own little twist to it!
CW: That's not the next match, though... let's get that one over with so we can watch the carnage.
RA: Ladies and gentlemen, introducing first, standing six feet, ten inches tall, and weighing in at an even three hundred pounds, I give you... SCOURGE!
("Superbeast" by Rob Zombie starts up, as Scourge makes his way to the ring. He stands in the center and throws both his arms up into the air, then waits for his opponent.)
RA: And his opponent... standing six feet, five inches tall and weighing two hundred and sixty pounds, from London, England... KEVIN
"THE CAVALIER" CHRISTIANSEN! ("Superbeast" fades into Carl Orff's "Carmina Burana: O Fortuna" as
Christiansen, geared out in a platemail shirt and shield, makes his way to the ring, shaking hands with the crowd as he goes.)
--- SCOURGE vs. KEVIN "THE CAVALIER" CHRISTIANSEN --- Christiansen put up a good fight, going blow for blow with the much bigger Scourge for longer than many would expect, but faltered when he was thrown out of the ring. While out of his element in a knock-down, drag-out brawl, Christiansen could do little to stop the onslaught of Scourge, who was much more attuned to this type of fight.
Scourge proceeded to savagely beat Christiansen while on the floor, clokeslam him through the Spanish announcer's table, and then roll the Cavalier's bruised body back into the ring for the easy cover from there.
WINNER: Scourge in 6:52 via pinfall. --- CW: I like this guy's style. That was a good, no-nonsense ass-smoking there. DH: It was a definite show of brutality, yes. Hopefully this next match will showcare the more scientific aspects of this league.
CW: This next match is the blindfold cage match. DH: (sighs) Maybe the match after that. RA: Ladies and gentlemen, this next first-round matchup has been signed under special stipulations! By consent of both athletes, and the acknowledgement of the booking committee...
CW: (voiceover) We have a booking committee? DH: (voiceover) Hush. RA: ... this next matchup will take place in a steel cage, with both participants blindfolded! As per the normal steel cage rules, the first man to escape from the steel cage and descend to the ringside floor will be named the victor! Please note: removal of the mask is grounds for immediate disqualification.
(The crowd starts cheering, with one fellow starting up a chant of "Man-
KIND! Man-KIND!" He, too, is removed from the premises by the
TTWA's professional crack Security and Crowd Morale Team, or the TTWAPCSACMT for short. The steel cage is lowered around the ring, with the one door on the side opened for entry.)
RA: Introducing first, hailing from New York City, and weighing in at two hundred and forty pounds... JACK BRAWN!
(Brawn makes his way down the entrance aisle, Chumbawumba's "Tubthumping" playing in the background. In his hand is clutched the blindfold mask, which he holds aloft as he passes the crowd. As he enters the cage, the referee assists him in donning the mask, and he is then escorted to the far corner of the cage.)
RA: And his opponent... the reason for this cage match tonight, weighing in at two hundred sixty-seven pounds, and hailing from Osaka,
Japan... THE MUTILATOR, JAKUZI MASAHITO! (Nirvana's "Paper Cuts" begins to play as Masahito emerges from the locker room. He has his mask in one hand and a steel chair in the other and, upon seeing Brawn in the ring already, picks up his pace. He quickly dons the mask as he goes to lay the chair down outside the ring... then shoves the referee off the apron! He slams the door shut just as the announcer rings the bell, pulling the chair inside with him.)
--- JACK BRAWN vs. JAKUZI "THE MUTILATOR" MASAHITO Special Stipulations: Blindfolded steel cage match! --- Brawn attempted a quick escape from the cage, turning and bolting up the wire wall as soon as the bell rang, but was nailed in the back by Msahito's chairshot, dropping him to the canvas. A few swift kicks to the ribs later, Masahito pulled Brawn up and it devolved into a wild, blind brawl from there, with both men being thrown into the wall of the cage repeatedly. Towards the end of the match, with a trickle of blood visible dripping out from under the mask of the
Mutilator, the two men (unaware of each other's location) both began to climb the same wall of the cage, facing the entrance aisle. Both had just about reached the top when the Bounty Hunter emerged from backstage, and scaled the outside of the cage. He began simultaneously peppering both men with punches (with Brawn and Masahito each thinking it was the other one who was doing it), finally knocking
Masahito back into the ring, and then turning his attention to Brawn
(who was just cresting the top of the cage at this point). Before Brawn could react, Bounty Hunter had grabbed him by the throat and thrown him off the side of the cage (amidst great yells of joy and "I love my life!" from Curtis Wilson), sending him into the third row of the crowd, his fall luckily broken by a conveniently placed pack of Cub Scouts. Strangely enough, this did satisfy the victory conditions of the match, and the semi-conscious Brawn's arm was raised in victory just as the medics arrived to carry him off.
WINNNER: Jack Brawn in 11:02 by escaping the cage. --- DH: Apparently, Bounty Hunter's still steamed from Masahito's meddling earlier tonight. CW: And Brawn was just collateral damage to the whole thing... I got to see someone thrown off a cage! (marks it off on his checklist)
DH: (sigh) RA: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is scheduled for one fall and, mercifully, is NOT a steel cage match! Introducing first, from Boulder Colorado, and weighing in at a massive four hundred and sixty two pounds... VINNY "THE MOUNTAIN" GAMBONE!
(Vinny Gambone comes out from the backstage area, almost totally blocking out the door behind him with his girth. He lumbers to the ring and climbs in, hopping around a bit and nearly taking the ring announcer off his feet with the shockwaves from it.)
RA: (steadying himself) And his opponent, hailing from Waco, Texas... TONY OUTLAW! (Tony Outlaw comes out to Metallica's "Wherever I May Roam", striding down to the ring with a smug look on his face. He jumps the top rope and looks around, then turns to face off with Gambone.)
--- TONY OUTLAW vs. VINNY "THE MOUNTAIN" GAMBONE --- Tony Outlaw spent the first half of this match either bouncing off of
Gambone, or being thrown around the ring by him. The match looked to be a squash (no pun intended) for a while, until Outlaw had the presence of mind to clip one of Gambone's legs out from under him from behind after the Mountain missed a clothesline attempt. Seeing that Gambone was barely able to support his weight on his now compromised leg, Outlaw took his time dancing around the bigger man and wearing him down, eventually tiring him out and sealing the win with a sleeper hold.
WINNER: Tony Outlaw in 7:33 with a sleeper hold. --- DH: A very impressive win for Mr. Outlaw, showing some good tactics against the much larger Gambone.
CW: I gotta give him credit, he sure whittled away at the Mountain. I guess we can start calling him Vinny "Gravel" Gambone now, hm?
DH: No, I don't suppose we can. CW: Aw, crud. RA: This next match is scheduled for one fall, with a fifteen minute time limit. Introducing first, standing five feet, six inches tall, and weighing in at one hundred and twenty five pounds... FLYING
RAPTOR! ("Welcome to the Jungle" by Guns n' Roses plays over the loudspeaker as
Flying Raptor makes his way to the ring. He flips over the top rope, landing on his feet, and looks around at the crowd warily.)
RA: (edging away from Flying Raptor) And his opponent, hailing from
Melbourne, Victoria, Australia, and weighing in at two hundred and eighty pounds... "DANGEROUS" DAVE HARDWICK!
(Foreigner's "Eye of the Tiger" comes on as Hardwick walks down to the ringside area. He pumps his arms into the air in a salute to the crowd, and steps between the ropes and into the ring.)
--- FLYING RAPTOR vs. "DANGEROUS" DAVE HARDWICK --- This was the classic matchup of David versus Goliath... the small, agile
Raptor against the larger, powerful Hardwick. Raptor initially confused Hardwick with his unorthodox style and quick movements, and Hardwick dazed Raptor with repeated hard shots to the smaller man's head and shoulders. The end finally came when, after dropping Hardwick with a top-rope dropkick, Flying Raptor went to the top rope again as his opponent staggered to his feet. Raptor's cross body block from the top was sidestepped, and Hardwick dropped a leg over the back of Raptor's head, stunning him long enough for
Hardwick to score the pinfall. Sometimes, Goliath does win. WINNER: "Dangerous" Dave Hardwick with a leg drop in 8:20. --- DH: For a small man, Raptor sure put up a big fight. CW: Well, you know what they say, Hayes. It's not the size of the dog in the fight... DH: ... it's the size of the fight in the dog. CW: Actually, I was going to say "... because any dog falls over if you hit it with a big enough stick", but yours seems to make more sense for the situation at hand.
DH: (sighs) Let's get to the last match of the evening. RA: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the final matchup of the first round! Introducing first, from Tampa, Florida, weighing in at two hundred and thirty pounds... CREMASTER!
(Metallica's "One" starts to play as Cremaster emerges from a cloud of smoke set off by the entrance ramp. He slowly walks to the ring and climbs in, then waits.)
RA: And his opponent, standing six feet, six inches tall and weighing in at one hundred and eighty pounds... CHRIS ELLIOTT!
(Fatboy Slim's "Rockafeller Skank" starts playing over the loudspeakers as Elliott appears. He makes his way to the ring and jumps the top rope, then rushes straight at Cremaster to start the match!)
--- CREMASTER vs. CHRIS ELLIOTT --- Elliott and Cremaster spent the entire match wearing each other down, alternating between scientific holds and reversals, and some more high-flying tactics. Cremaster took the advantage in the match with a vicious belly to belly suplex that threw Elliot into one of the turnbuckles, but that advantage was quickly reversed when Elliot subsequently knocked Cremaster from his perching place on the top rope to the outside of the ring, then hitting him with an Asai moonsault. The end came when Elliott, in a desperation move, reversed a whip into the turnbuckles, sending the equally tired Cremaster chest-first into it, then rolled Cremaster up for the pin as he staggered backwards away from it.
WINNER: Chris Elliott with a schoolboy roll-up in 13:20. --- DH: And with that, the eight participants in the second round of the tournament have been chosen.
CW: Indeed, they have... as has the restaurant I'm going to once we get out of there. Is there anything else, Hayes?
DH: Not at this point. Thank you, one and all, for watching the TTWA's first card - we can assure you, there's more where that came from.
CW: (pulling on Hayes' sleeve) Food. Now. Go. DH: For my nutritionally deficient friend, I'm Daniel Hayes. Good night, folks. (Camera fades out as Hayes and Wilson run off to KFC.)
