WOLF: The First Wrestling Online Game Is Here

8 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All the way from www.wolfwrestling.com Center Productions proudly presents...... W.O.L.F, The first ever Wrestling Online Game! What's a wrestling online game you ask? Well, its similar to e-wrestling, only a lot more advanced! If you like e-wrestling, then you'll love W.O.L.F! Join for free right now by going to
www.wolfwrestling.com ! If you want more information, then read on! ABOUT US: For over two years we have been involved with e-wrestling throughout the internet. Finally we just got sick and tired of the whole idea behind e-wrestling, or lack there of. Most e-feds don't base match decisions on anything more than who can type out the most role-plays in a short amount of time. I've never figured out how that is realistic to Pro Wrestling. One day we decided that it was about time somebody changed Fantasy Wrestling for the better, and actually added a bit of realism to it. That's when the idea of
W.O.L.F, The World's Only Legitimate Federation was born! After presenting our idea to Vancouver based "Extreme Canadian Championship Wrestling", we were given two thumbs up and full endorsement! You have to think that if a major independent wrestling promotion such as ECCW, member of the NWA, will endorse us then we have to be at least half decent!
GENERAL IDEA BEHIND WOLF: When we started to think about the whole concept, we decided that it would be best not to associate ourselves with e-wrestling. We wanted to get ourselves as far away from e-wrestling as we possibly could. Fantasy
Wrestling -yes, ewrestling -no. Over the course of several months we worked on a bunch of things that nobody really tried before. We looked at all the faults of e-wrestling and then tried to find solutions for them. The main idea behind WOLF was to create an environment, which was as realistic to the world or professional wrestling as possible. Through WOLF, we wanted to provide a fun filled fantasy wrestling experience that all members could enjoy. We wanted to make WOLF like playing a wrestling video game online, only against a bunch of real people. All the things that people love about wrestling would go in there -the storylines, characters, drama, competitiveness, and excitement. WOLF would be a form of expressing oneself and being creative. Every day, we wanted people to think about new things to do with their characters and to say in their interviews. After many hours of planning, and then putting the ideas into motion, I look back and cannot even imagine going back to the way it used to be! We are certain that all of you who have been frustrated with e-wrestling in the past, will agree, that
WOLF is leaps and bounds ahead of traditional e-wrestling!
HOW WE'RE DIFFERENT: Our major difference to e-wrestling is the way that we decide matches. With most e-feds, the guy who typed out the longest "role-plays", or interivews, won the match. Winning and lossing just came down to how much time you had on your hands, and how many role-plays you could type out in that time. When
I look back, I don't know how e-wrestling stayed so popular! With WOLF, a lot of things go into deciding who wins and losses the matches. For starters we use a simulator, which we programmed ourselves, to write and decide all the matches. This way, all decisions involve an element of chance. When deciding matches rests totally on the opinion of one person, lots of people are going to be left un-happy. In WOLF, the simulator asks us to decide a
"push" for each wrestler. The "push" is just a rating out of a possible 100 points that we feel the wrestler deserves for that particular week. You could even call it a grade that we hand out. Our decision is based upon:
character personality, current angle or storyline, and of course effectiveness of role-playing over the week. So we rate each wrestler and then put the number into the simulator. The higher the number you have, the better your chance will be of victory. However, that's not to say upsets won't happen... Of course that's where the fun comes in! Besides that, all wrestlers will NOT be created equal. In that I mean that a wrestler who has been around for a while will be tougher than a rookie. When you start a new character in WOLF, you get 20 character points to place any way you like over four different categories (Strength, Speed, Charisma, and Vitality).
With more victories, you gain more character points to add any way you please. This is similar to how WWF Warzone works, in that your wrestler gets stronger as he gains experience or "levels up". Other differences between us, and traditional e-wrestling feds is our personalized members page. When you join WOLF, you get a login and password to your own personal account!
From there, you basically manage your wrestler in every aspect. Another very important aspect of WOLF is our own role-play board. In e-wrestling, role-plays were sent via email, thus always filling up your email account!
You will not an email account to be a part of WOLF! Everything will be done through the web, including role-playing! One thing you'll notice right off the bat is that WOLF let's its members win prizes! Members collect "prize points" which they can later exchange for such things as Computer games,
Wrestling tapes, and PC software! The grand prize is an authentic WWF title belt from beltster, worth almost five hundred dollars!
WHY JOIN WOLF: The answer is simple.... If you're a wrestling fan or enjoy e-wrestling, then W.O.L.F is for you! It's a fun and enjoyable medium, in which you compete against many other people just like yourself. W.O.L.F is more than just a game, it's a community of online wrestling fans! We're confident that after only two weeks of trying it out, you'll want to be a part of WOLF full time! There's no obligation. If you don't like it, just quit -No questions asked! Join up today, you've got NOTHING to lose and EVERYTHING to gain!
