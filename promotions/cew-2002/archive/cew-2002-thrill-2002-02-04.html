[CEW] THRILL!

4 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cyber Extreme Wrestling's very own THRILL has hit the airwaves! On tonight's card: CEW newcomer Nemesys MAKES HIS DEBUT against CEW rookie Chainz. Buzzsaw has been a CEW Hardcore Icon for his entire career, and is respected as god-like within the federation. But he's never held the
World or North American championships. He gets his chance tonight as he takes on Jeremy Hunter for NA gold!
Billy Boltz made his return to CEW television last week, as a hired-gun, ending Chris Johnson's chances in the KODM Tournament.
Allen Watts has some old issues to settle with Boltz, and these two collide TONIGHT! Wes Slater is in the Hall of Fame. Phoenix Murdoch wants to prove to the world what a crock of shit Slater is! These two men have been testing each other for months, but have never hooked it up in the ring. Now we finally get to see it ... to the 5th power! The
Slater/Murdoch BEST OF FIVE series starts TONIGHT! Fast and Furious made their presence known at Unleashed by taking out
Chainz and handing Censored the win. Censored defeated Shank in his opening CEW match, but now both Censored and Shank bring their partners to the ring as Fast and Furious collides with the Rokken Goth
Connection!
"Showtime" Nick Alexander hooks up with CEW owner Chris Powers tonight to take on Courtney Westman's Tag Team Champions: The Presidents of
Power!
And in the main event ... Vince Darrick went into Thrill two weeks ago as the HCW Extreme Champion, and the KODM favorite. Descendent comes into Thrill this week the HCW Extreme Champion ... and the KODM winner! Darrick tried to prevent D's victory, but it was not enough.
Now these two Hardcore monsters will hook up in the ring for the VERY FIRST TIME! It's ALL TONIGHT, ON CEW THRILL! ( http://cew.theewz.com/thrill02.17.htm )
