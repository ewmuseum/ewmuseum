[CEW] Thrill (Volume II, Episode 18)

15 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THRILL Monday 02.11.02 This week … on Cyber Extreme Wrestling's THRILL!! On tonight's card: Mad Dog makes he return to CEW action, taking on CEW's resident gothic nightmare: Shank! Last week Chainz and Nemesys put an end to their singles match by agreeing to go out and get some whores and beers. This week, they've sobered up (maybe) and have teamed up to take on the Fast and the
Furious.
Last week, a Best-of-5 series between Phoenix Murdoch and Wes Slater got under way. And, against many predictions … Wes Slater DOMINATED!
Can Murdoch bounce back and tie the series up at 1 win apiece? Months ago, Mark Knight defeated Chris Johnson for the North American title. Knight's career rose from there, while Johnson has basically floundered, including being kicked out of the Time by Knight's order.
Johnson wants to prove he's better, and he'll get his chance this week! "Showtime" Nick Alexander is part of the Authority. Hussein Fatal, wants to BE the Authority in CEW … by taking everyone else OUT! The two hate each other, and they'll be in the ring … TOGETHER … AS A
TEAM. As if things could be ANY worse for Alexander … he gets to face the PoP yet again … the tag-team that is slowly becoming his
arch-nemesis. FOR THE CEW WORLD TITLE: CEW's Main Man: AC puts his title on the line against CEW's Hardcore Icon: BUZZSAW!
And could it get any better in the main event? Kris eGo has been out to prove himself for weeks, and he blames Outlaw Immortal for trying to steal his thunder. What will happen when these two meet in the ring?!?
It's ALL TONIGHT (well, actually, it was Monday Night), ON CEW THRILL! ( http://cew.theewz.com/thrill02.18.shtml )
