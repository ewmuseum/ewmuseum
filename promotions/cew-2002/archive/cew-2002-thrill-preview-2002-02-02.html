[CEW] Thrill (full preview inside)

2 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Last week Thrill was off the air as Cyber Extreme Wrestling presented CEW UNLEASHED ZERO-TWO. Last week, on Unleashed ... (this is a quick review, if you haven't, please check out http://cew.theewz.com/unleashed_02.htm .) Censored used the interference of his tag-team partner, Twist, to get a victory over fellow rookie Chainz.
First round results from the KODM: Darrick over Murdoch (even with a career performance by Murdoch) Buzzsaw over the 'Natural Born Killer' Wes Slater Hussein Fatal over "Your Hero" Chris Johnson, via paid-off interference from Billy Boltz. Descendent barely defeated Allen Watts, becoming the new HCW Extreme
Champion Courtney Westman booked her PoP into a Tag-team championship match against Jeremy Hunter and Mark Knight. Knight and Hunter would face each other later in the night, and self-destructed as a team, giving the PoP the win.
KODM Second Round resutls: Hussein Fatal with a convincing win over Vince Darrick, in a back and forth match. Descendent vs Buzzsaw. The New Guard of CEW Hardcore versus the Old GOD of HC. This match ... was classic. Words can't do it justice. CHECK IT OUT ... NOW! D gets the win in the end. Jeremy Hunter wins his first ever CEW North American Championship when he defeats Mark Knight and Da*Link*Went.
Descendent over Hussein Fatal in the finals of the KODM. This match is another near classic, with Buzzsaw coming out to present D with the
KODM trophy, essentially handing the torch.
Yet another match of the year candidate ... Outlaw Immortal defeats
SNA. This is another MUST-SEE match-up.
The main event: AC defeats eGo in one of AC's best matches in recent memory. After the match Outlaw Immortal comes out to get face to face with AC, and is promptly confronted by an understandably mad eGo.
CEW THRILL Monday, February 4, 2002 Nemesys vs. Chainz Chainz showed that he has talent in his Unleashed match against Censored. Nemesys is yet another CEW rookie with something to prove. Both of these men have proven in their interviews to have more than one screw loose ... but who will stay in control in the ring?
Hunter (CEW North American Champion) vs. Buzzsaw Buzzsaw is one of the greats in CEW, always has been. But, thanks to his concentration on Hardcore and the Hardcore championship, he doesn't have the title-filled past that lesser men have accumulated.
He wants to take the first step to remedy this and defeat Jeremy
Hunter for the North American title. Hunter is coming off a huge win over Link and Knight. Who will win in this match up of the New Guard and the Old Guard?
Boltz vs. Watts This grudge match has been formed back stage, both men having something to prove. Billy Boltz has not been seen for months, but had a huge impact on Unleashed. Watts has floundered around the bottom of
CEW for most of his career, but proved that he DOES have talent with his incredible Unleashed fight against Descendent. Both men have something to prove, but which one will be able to?
SPECIAL MATCH...BEST 3-5 SERIES... WES SLATER VS. PHOENIX MURDOCH!!!!! This match has been brewing for weeks ... and now, we'll get to see anywhere between 3 and 5 of them. Slater's cronies have been responsible for taking on Murdoch on a regular basis, while Murdoch has taken his time, honed his talents, and proven himself to the CEW fanbase. CEW's "Natural Born Killer" is a member of the Hall of Fame, while Murdoch is on a tear that will likely lead him to that point in the future. Which one of these two men will take the first step, and victory, in this fight?
Fast and Furious vs. Rocken Goth Connection Fast and Furious (also known and Censored and Twist) were responsible for Censored's victory over Chainz at Unleashed. Eddie Ace and Shank
(RGC) have been around CEW for no-one-knows-how-long. Who gets the victory in this battle of unproven tag-teams?
Nick Alexander/Chris Powers vs. Presidents Of Power (CEW Tag Champions) You're kidding me ... right? SNA will team up with CHRIS POWERS?!? to face the PoP. Alexander all but destroyed the time with his actions a few months ago at Icestorm. The PoP seem to be the only saving grace the Time may have left. The PoP is out to prove themselves against actual competition ... and this looks like the best they may get for a while.
Vince Darrick vs. Descendent (HCW Extreme Champion) Vince Darrick went into the KODM tournament at Unleashed as the favorite, with Descendent seemingly the only man that could threaten his place on the throne. Descendent came away the winner and HCW
Extreme Champion, even with Darrick's interference in the main event.
Darrick brought the HCW belt into CEW, never planning on dropping it. Descendent is the CEW's newest Hardcore King, with no plans of ever losing that title. These two huge men will meet at Thrill ... but will either one really win?
