Countdown to IWC Saturday Night

18 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene opens once again in Nate Shaft's gym. This time however "the Black Panther" Jesse Williams is with Nate and of course, "Big Badd" Steve Tadd) ST: Hey boss! Where's Lector at? NS: He's over at True Value Hardware picking out his favorite type of ladder. But Steve, we're not here to talk about you and Lector. We're here to talk about you and "Black Panther" teaming up on Saturday Night [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] ow, some people think that you two can't make a good team? ST: You're right boss, a lot of people are saying that.............a lot of STUPID people. (laughter from all three) C'mon man, we're Suspects. We work out together five days a week. Satisfaction Guaranteed, I'm guaranteeing that we're gonna give you a headache. NS: Unlike the rest of this federation, The Usual Suspects are above using the color of a man's skin to be a reason to not team up and be life long friends with a man. Tell 'em what I mean Jesse..... BP: That's right. These are two of the three white men that I consider down. The other one's out shopping right now. I've got no problem with the Taddman. Satisfaction Guaranteed, Lector and I never got our rematch after you blatantly cheated to beat us because I was black. But it looks like I get my chance for revenge when I team up with the other big man in the IWC to take you down..... NS: Beautiful, beautiful. *wipes an imaginary tear from his eye* It touches me to see such comrodary. But Stevie...... not only do you get to beat up on Satisfaction Guaranteed, but it's a double-header for you cuz you get to then beat "Big D" Devon Myers to get in the Ringmaster tourney....... ST: Well it must be my lucky day.....You know, I think I finally found out what "Big D" means chief...... NS: Please....Enlighten us..... ST: It stands for "Big Dreamer" cuz that's what Devon Myers has to be if he thinks he's even in the same league as I am. (laughter from all three again) I'm not even worried about Devon Myers. Jesse's told me all about what a ex-pro football player has to offer in the wrestling ring......Plus he played for a real team, any no talent loser could play for the Jets!
