[IWC] Countdown to the Ringmaster Tournament

1 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"At last . . . A long sought-after dream, is realised . . ." <<The room is dimly-lit. A lone, burning candle sits in its ornate brass holder, upon the well-polished oaken writing desk. The subdued, but strangely soothing glow, casts writhing, entrancing shadows in its wake. Sitting in his leather chair, behind the aforementioned desk, a pony-tailed Gabriel Whitecross stares intently at the beacon of light. He is stripped to the waist, and it appears that he wears a grey pair of baggy tracksuit bottoms over his lower body. A white towel is draped around his broad neck, and a thin mist of sweat covers his face, shoulders and chest . . . A clear indication that `The Era' has just been working out. By his heavily taped right hand, there sits the ultimate beauty of the IWC World Championship belt . . . Its polished gold surface, glimmering in the available light. In his trademark low, monotone voice, he continues to speak>>. " . . . On the Monday night just passed, I fought the honourable Rocket Launcher, in a bloody `war of attrition' for this very prize, that now lies at my side . . . <<He runs a fingertip lightly over the belt's surface, seemingly savouring its essence>> . . . Military Man, you fought with great courage and strength, and your desire to win was clear for all to see. In the end, only a fraction of a second separated the victor from the vanquished . . . I find myself fortunate to be regarded as the former . . . " <<He pauses for a moment, to wipe his brow with the towel. He looks thoughtful>>.
