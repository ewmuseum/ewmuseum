[IWC] Saturday Night Showdown, Nov. 8

10 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Frank King: Now coming to the ring, led by his manager Rob Dante, standing 6'6" and weighing in at 330 pounds, here is THE RAMBUNCTIOUS ONE!!!!!! (Rob Dante leads his behemoth of a man as "I'm Broken" by Pantera blasts through the arena. As they slowly walk to the ring Bobby Cane and Howard Cleese speak.) Bobby Cane: I'm Broken is the perfect name for a song to a Rob Dante managed man. Howard Cleese: I'm almost afraid to ask, but why is that? Bobby Cane: Cuz if Dante ever meets up with Nate Shaft again that's what he'll be......Broken. (sadistic laugh) Howard Cleese: Will you stop! (The arena goes pitch black and the opening riffs of "Enter Sandman" from Metallica start to be heard.) Frank King: And now.....making his return to the IWC....The man from Death Valley, weighing in at 225 pounds, here is.....THE DARK KNIGHT! (As his name is called out smoke fills the arena and the Dark Knight steps out from behind the curtain, he slowly walks to the ring as the lights begin to come back up.) Howard Cleese: It's good to see the first IWC Crusierweight Champion back where he belongs. Bobby Cane: Yeah, about as good as it is to see Willard Scott doing the weather every morning. Howard Cleese: You know Willard retired Bobby. Bobby Cane: Great, now if the Dark Knight would do the same everyone would be happy. Howard Cleese: Never mind Bobby. They start it off in a collar and elbow lock-up....Dark Knight is thrown to the mat by TRO. Bobby Cane: But the Knight gets back up and tries it again. Talk about dumb moves? Howard Cleese: But he turns it into a nice side headlock.....Knight sent into the ropes by TRO......tries a clothesline.......Ducked........Knight off the other ropes and hits a big Flying Forearm...... Bobby Cane: And did he hit him hard. TRO is already woozy. Follow up by Knight.....Spinning heel kick by Knight Howard Cleese: Now he throws TRO into the corner.......here's a chop, and another, and anot [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] uch of a reaction to "the prodigy", those who do don't seem to like the man. Relentless, Tickle goes up on the second rope and throws his hands in the air SCSA style trying to get a reaction. Suddenly, there is a huge reaction as "Something Wicked" by Nuclear Assault starts ringing the sides of the arena.) Howard Cleese: Here he comes!!! Bobby Cane: Whoopty-frickin-doo! Frank King: Now coming down the isle, from parts unknown, weighing in at 249 pounds,... "The Era of Defiance" Gabriel Whitecross (the crowd goes absolutely ballistic as Whitecross makes his way to the ring. The crowd actually starts chanting "Gabe, Gabe, Gabe" as he winds his way down the isle. Whitecross has a very scary smile on his face the entire walk.) Bobby Cane: Look at this loser. Tickle will wipe that smile off his face. Howard Cleese: He's no loser, Bobby. He's one of the best and brightest stars the IWC has to offer. Bobby Cane: Whatever, these two have a big history with one another don't they Howie? Howard Cleese: It's Howard. And yes, these two have one of the biggest feuds in E-wrestling history! This should be a war. I don't expect to see too many cross-face chicken wings in this one. Bobby Cane: Well, Tickle sure didn't want to wait for the silver-locked one to make it to the ring. He's all over Whitecross with rights and lefts on the outside.... Howard Cleese: I'll admit that Tickle has definitely taken the upper hand early on in this one. but look, Whitecross still has that smile on his face, it's as if he's enjoying this beating. (for the next five minutes or so the action is in the control of Tickle. The Era gets in a few shots but it's mostly all Jon Tickle in control. He keeps it on the outside, using the ringposts, a steel chair, and throwing the steps on Whitec [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] t matter though, Whitecross must have sustained serious injury during the beating he took on the outside. Howard Cleese: I don't know Bobby, look. Whitecross still has that grin on his face. He's proven time and time again that he is one of the most resilient superstars that wrestling has ever seen. And Look!!!!! Whitecross has taken the upper hand! he's landing right hand after right hand on the jaw of Tickle. Now Whitecross gives the man a bodyslam and slaps on a Cobra Clutch in an effort to break his man down. (the action begins to get slow, so once again, Bobby and Howard talk about other things. For about five minutes the action is slow and both wrestlers trade blows there are a couple of pinning attempts, but neither wrestler is able to get real close to a win) Howard Cleese: Well Bobby, Who do you think will win the Ringmaster Tournament that's starting up this Monday on IWC Monday Night? Bobby Cane: It should be a great Tourney, but when it comes down to it, I think "The Black Panther" Jesse Williams will come out on top. He's got a new attitude, he's about ready to explode in a big way, and of course, he has Nate Shaft managing him. But should he fall, I have two other favorites to take the tourney. Howard Cleese: Let me guess.........Lector or "Big Badd" Steve Tadd? Bobby Cane: (in a serious voice) How'd you know? Howard Cleese: Call it a hunch. Well, I believe that none other than The Dark Knight is gonna pull through and win this HUGE tournament. Bobby Cane: I don't think he's got what it takes, but this is the IWC, and anything can happen at anytime.........(the action in the ring picks up as Whitecross nails Tickle with a perfectly executed brainbuster) speaking of anything happening, Whitecross has started to take control of this match-up. Howard Cleese: That was a great move........now Whitecross has Tickle up......he hooks a leg while they're standing.....and gives him a fisherman's suplex into a bridge cover. Here's the count......1........2......no Tickle gets up. Bobby Cane: What guts by Tickle. I hate to admit it, but both Tickle and Whitecross have shown great courage in this one. Howard Cleese: Your right Bobby. Whitecross rolls Tickle over........Groin shot by the prodigy and Whitecross goes down.... Bobby Cane: It looks like Tickle has caught a second wind.....huge press slam by Tickle....Oh my he through him straight over the top rope......On the outside now, Tickle nails the [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] ed DDT through the table!!!!!!!! Tickle has got to be out cold.. Look at him bleed. Howard Cleese: I think "the Era" is done messing around... He throws Tickle into the middle of the ring.......and there it is! The Broken Talisman!!!!! I don't think Tickle is conscious though........he's not, the ref is calling for the bell. Frank King: YOUR WINNER, "THE ERA OF DEFIANCE" GABRIEL WHITECROSS!!!!!!! Bobby Cane: That was quite a match-up there.......but look, Whitecross will not let his version of the STF go........and here comes John Payne, and he's got a steel chair!!!!!!!! Howard Cleese: Payne gets in the ring and throws the ref out........Look at him, he's telling Whitecross to keep the Broken Talisman locked in!!!!! Bobby Cane: Tickle's leg must have a broken leg by now.....his career may be ended by these two morons. They should be fined and suspended.....Where the hell are the IWC officials when you need them?!?!?!?! Howard Cleese: Here they come now. the officials have come to put an end to this....but Payne won't let them in. He's swinging that steel chair at anyone who comes near. We're out of time, we've got to go!!!!!!!!!!!!! Bobby Cane: Why are these idiotic fans cheering for these two bafoons....... (scene fades to black)
