[IWC] Ringmaster Tournament, Hour 4

1 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<Under the harsh glare of the arena lights, they stand. Rocket Launcher, dressed in his familiar army fatigues, and wearing a focused and determined expression on his face, stands in a corner and glares at disdainfully at one `Suicide Machine' John Payne. Payne paces slowly back and forth, grinning maniacally at his counterpart as he does so. At ringside, the General is once again conspicuous by his absence. Suddenly, the lights dim, and the aisle is bathed in an eerie red glow. The fans begin to cheer loudly, as the sinister grind of Nuclear Assault's `Something Wicked' spews from the speakers. Gabriel Whitecross, with the IWC World Title worn proudly around his waist, walks towards his first demanding and potentially gruelling, Title defence>>. Howard Cleese: You know, Rocket Launcher thinks he is at a disadvantage in this match, but to tell the truth, it's Whitecross that is more hindered . . . This is, in essence, a triple threat match. That means that the Title can change hands, IRRESPECTIVE of if Gabriel gets pinned or not. Not too fair if you ask me . . . Bobby Cane: Stop whining, Cleese. Gabe signed the contract . . . He knew what he was getting himself into! <<Tiring of waiting for the Champion to get to the ring, Payne takes matters into his own hands, leaps over the top rope, and charges up the aisle, towards his `friend'. Spotting this, Whitecross unhooks the belt, and responds in kind. To the deafening roar of the partisan crowd, Whitecross and `Machine' collide head-on, exchanging strong punches [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] `MACHINE' CRASHES INTO LAUNCHER AT HIGH SPEED . . . OHH! . . . WHAT SICKENING CLASH OF HEADS! <<Through his blurred vision, Whitecross sees `Suicide' staggering towards him. Summoning up the last slither of strength in his tortured body, he executes a drop toehold upon his foe>>. Howard Cleese: THERE IT IS! . . . THE BROKEN TALISMAN! Bobby Cane: BUT I DON'T THINK THAT PAYNE IS CONSCIOUS! . . . THAT CLASH OF HEADS WAS DEVASTATING! Howard Cleese: LOOK AT GABRIEL PULL INTO THIS SUBMISSION! . . . HE'S NOT HOLDING ANYTHING BACK! . . .THE REF CHECKS THE ARM OF THE SUICIDE MACHINE! ONE! . . . TWO! . . . Bobby Cane: LAUNCHER IS COMPLETELY OUT OF IT, ON THE ARENA FLOOR! Howard Cleese: . . . THREE! . . . IT'S OVER! . . . WHITECROSS DOES IT! <<As soon as Gabriel hears the bell, he relinquishes his grip immediately, and sinks into unconsciousness>>. Frank King: Here is your winner . . . AND STILL IWC WORLD CHAMPION . . . `THE ERA OF DEFIANCE' GABRIEL WHITECROSS! <<All three men lie motionless, but thankfully still breathing, inside and around the ring area, as medical assistance races down the aisle>>. Howard Cleese: I've said it once, but I'll say it again . . . WHAT A MATCH! . . . Folks, we're out of time . . . But I hope that you have had a fantastic time watching this year's Ringmaster Tournament . . . From Bobby and me . . . SEE YOU NEXT TIME, EVERYBODY!
