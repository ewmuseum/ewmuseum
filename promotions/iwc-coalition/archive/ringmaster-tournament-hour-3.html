<The crowd begin to settle again. Suddenly ''Cosmic Girl'' by Jamiroquai booms out from the loud speakers high above. ''Sensational'' Simon Monk walks to ringside. The crowd boo, but Monk does receive some support from the female fans at ringside, some of whom are screaming. Monk walks around to the commentary position, and sits down next to Bobby Cane.> Ring announcer: ''This next match is a special elimination match for the Cruiserweight championship!!!! A wrestler is eliminated by pinfall, submission, D.Q, or countout. The last man remaining will be declared the winner. About to make his way down the aisle, from Queens, NY, weighing in at 230lbs..........MUY SUAVE!!!!'' <The crowd begin to stir again as ''Doin' It'' by LL Cool J st [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] feet, he shoulder charges into the corner, and Suave lands on his......' Howard Cleese: 'Suave's eyes are watering!! The White Phoenix climbs up, and delivers a DDT from the top turnbuckle!!' Bobby Cane: 'Phoenix with the cover.......ONE.....TWO...... THRE....NO!!!! A foot on the rope from Suave!' Howard Cleese: 'Phoenix isn't wasting any time...... he sends Suave to the corner..... WOW!!!! The Phoenix Strike from The White Phoenix!!! Phoenix makes the cover!!!! ONE.......TWO......... THREE............... YES!! WE HAVE A NEW CHAMPION!!!!!!' Bobby Cane: 'What a match!!! I can't wait to get to the bottom of what was going on with Monk!' Howard Cleese: 'I'm Sure the White Phoenix will have some harsh words for Monk! WOW...... A ball of fire from The mouth of the Phoenix!!! Where did that come from?! this guy will never cease to amaze me!' Ring announcer: 'Here is your winner, and NEW IWC Cruiserweight champion!!! THE WHITE PHOENIX!!!!!' <The White Phoenix stands in the centre of the ring holding the IWC Cruiserweight championship belt high above his head. The crowd give him a standing ovation.> Howard Cleese : We're entering Hour 4 of Ringmaster !!!! (Fade out)
