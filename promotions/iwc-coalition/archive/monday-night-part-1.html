[IWC] Monday Night, Part 1

12 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
King:The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the IWC World Tag Team Championship! Introducing first, the challengers, (Chopin's Tristesse Etude fades into the arena) King:Accompinied to the ring by Lord Robert d'Artois, weighing in at a total combined weight of 510 pounds, JUN "TYGER" KAMURA and REINER MAGNUSSON!!! (The trio come down the aisle and enter the ring) King:And their opponents, ("Outshined" by Soundgarden fades into the arena) King:Accompinied to the ring by "Sexy" Stacie Money, weighing in at a total combined weight of 569 pounds, they are the IWC World Tag Team Champions, "The Franchise" Todd Money and "The Pinnacle of Extreme" Tommy Deam, SATISFACTION GUARENTEED!!! (Stacie Money and Satisfaction come down the aisle, gold belts gleaming. They enter the ring to cheers) Cleese:We're set to go! Magnusson and Deam will start off. Magnusson charges in with a lariat, ducked by Deam! Cross-body from Deam! Magnusson gets up dropkick! Magnusson goes to the outside! Cane:Magnusson is trying to gain back his breath here, wait, here comes Deam! Cleese:Springboard plancha! Deam rolls Magnusson back in. Deam from the top, Magnusson gets up, flying clothesline! Cover, 1.....2....Kickout! Deam picks Magnusson up and tags in Money. Money with a kick to the midsection, DDT! Second rope legdrop now! 1.....2..Kickout! He climbs to the top again, misses with a moonsault! Magnusson rolls over and tags in Kamura. Kamura comes in, dropkick to Money! Dr [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] ers with his little finger. 1.....2.....3! No! Kamura kicks out! Money can't believe it, nor can Lector! Money climbs to the turnbuckle, elbow drop, no! Kamura moves out of the way! Kamura gets up, so does Money, Tyger Diver! He gets up and is yelling at Lector! Cane:He should tag! Or go for the pin. Cleese:How can he? Magnusson is brawling with Deam on the outside and Lector ticked him off. He turns around, cross-body from Money! 1.....2....Kickout! He picks Kamura up, powerbomb! Cane:Wait! Someone is attacking Lector! Cleese:Who is that? Cane:I don't know...waaaait a minute. Doesn't that guy look familiar? Cleese:I don't believe it! That's Pimp Daddy! One of the greats! He takes Lector down! He's stomping away! Money isn't paying attention to Kamura and is watching the brawl on the outside! Wait! Kamura has him rolled up! 1.....2.....3! They did it! King:Here are your winners, and NEW IWC World Tag Team Champions, JUN "TYGER" KAMURA and REINER MAGNUSSON! Cleese:Deam has thrown Magnusson into the crowd and is going after the legend! Money now is in with the brawl! Kamura is lying in the ring, regaining his strength. Kamura rises to his feet, climbs to the top, corkscrew plancha onto the outside, and on Satisfaction Guarenteed! Pimp Daddy avoided it! PD goes over to the timekeepers table, grabs the belts, and is beating away on mostly Deam, getting a couple of shots on Money as well! Magnusson is back into it, and they're all fighting! Magnusson and Kamura grab the belts and go back to the locker room with d'Artois, who was standing around watching all this! PD is brawling with Satisfaction Guarenteed! Cane:Here comes DWP! Cleese:He hits PD! Where's the security?
