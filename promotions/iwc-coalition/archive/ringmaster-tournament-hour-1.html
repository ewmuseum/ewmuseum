[IWC] Ringmaster Tournament, Hour 1

1 December 1997

<<The theme from the `Usual Suspects', pumps through the entire Arena, as Frank King moves the microphone closer to his lips . . . But as he begins to speak, the connecting curtain is almost ripped from its hanging, as a battered and slightly bloodied `Black Panther' Jesse Williams is sent careening in [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] ! Introducing first, representing the Usual Suspects, accompinied to the ring by Nate Shaft, weighing in at 390 pounds, STEVE TADD!!! (Tadd and Shaft come down to the ring, with cheers from Suspects fans. Tadd has a determined look on his face, and Shaft looks like he's loving everything.) ("American Nightmare" by White Zombie fades into the arena) King:And his opponent, representing the Extreme Team, accompinied to the ring by "Sexy" Stacie Money, weighing in at 414 pounds, DEATH WITHOUT PERMISSION!!! (The 7' monster and Stacie Money appear out of the smoke filled entrance way and start walking down the aisle. They enter the ring and the bell sounds) Cleese:We're set to go! Cane:This is going to be a power contest, I'm sure. Cleese:It probably will. Cane:Let's just hope the ring doesn't fall apart. Cleese:Big forearm to the back of DWP by Tadd. Remember, these two fought a while ago on IWC Monday Night, in a Falls Count Anywhere match, which ended in a double DQ. Another forearm by Tadd brings DWP to one knee. Another brings him down. Cane:No love loss between these two. Cleese:Stomp from Tadd. Now he runs into the ropes, and he just sat on the back of DWP! Now he's sitting there. He gets off and proceeds to work on the back of Tadd with stomps and elbowdrops. He rolls him over, cover, 1....Kickout. Now Tadd picks DWP up, belly-to-back suplex. Cover, 1.....2...Kickout. He picks DWP up, whip to the ropes, sidewalk slam! Cover, 1.....2....thre- kickout! He almost had him. Cane:Come on Tadd. Put him away! Cleese:He sets DWP up, DDT. Cover, 1.....2...Kickout. He helps DWP to his feet, whip to the turnbuckle. Tadd charges in, and DWP lifts his foot, and Tadd goes to the mat! DWP catching his breath, and Tadd is getting to his feet. Clothesline from DWP! Cover, 1....Kickout. No
