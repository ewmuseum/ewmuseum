[IWC] Monday Night, Part 2

12 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Frank King: The nest bout is for the IWC's Crusierwieght Championship!!!! On his way to the ring.....The White Phoenix!!! (A gong is sounded and a piercing eagle follows it up, the Phoenix walks confidently to the ring as the crowd cheers) Bobby Cane: Hey Howie. Do you know how your going to tell these two apart? Howard Cleese: *in a "boy, aren't you stupid" voice* By there names Bobby. Bobby Cane: No stupid, by those huge tattoos on their backs. Frank King: And now, The IWC Crusierweight Champion, The Raging Dragon. (traditional Chinese music plays. But there is no sign of the Dragon....After some time the music dies down and eventually stops.......then suddenly.....) (The Dragon throws the curtain back, stepping out onto the runway. He is stripped to the waist, wearing only his pitch black silks, in his right hand, a microphone, in his left, the coveted Cruiserweight belt. As it becomes obvious that the Dragon has no intentions of traversing the aisle, the crowd slowly quiets down. He raises the mic to his lips to speak.) Dragon: There is something I must say to all of you, (his gaze swivels to encompass all of the fans in the seats), and to you, (his gaze finally settling on his opponent, the Phoenix). I have had one of the longest undefeated streaks since the IWC first opened its doors, and that is no small task considering the level of talent and dedication held by all the athletes here. The culmination of my success was when I won this, (he raises the belt, holding it by one strap, so that all may see), the IWC Cruiserweight Championship, an honor I am most unworthy of. (He pauses as the crowd erupts into another round of applause and screaming at both the mention of the championship, and this mans past successes. As they quiet down, he continues.) Dragon: But.... that all ends, here, tonight. (Another short pause to allow for a rather loud hush from the crowd.) Dragon: This night, I am supposed to war with that man in the ring now. But for once, this war is by no means professional. Phoenix, you and I will war many times, I can already see that. But this, (raising the belt into the air once more), can have no part in it. It will become but a token to us, and that will demean its presence. (The Dragon suddenly releases the strap, letting the belt fall to the floor.) As of this moment, I am officially relinquishing this title, I have nothing to do with it. If you wish to retrieve it Phoenix by [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] nd shaking with anger. The crowd is restless and confused, a small contingent of them holding up cigarette lighters as a symbol to their hero. The Phoenix takes one step forward and stops, obviously unsure what to do next.) Cleese: A strange turn of events here. The Dragon is on his way to the locker room, and the Phoenix...OH MY GOD! (The White Phoenix suddenly points toward the locker room, where a sheet of flame erupts, blocking the entrance. The Dragon jumps backward away from the flame as the crowd erupts in confused cheers. The Phoenix motions for a microphone.) Phoenix: Now that I have your attention, Dragon, I have some things to say. You skulk about the darkness, talking of events in your past which may not have happened. You take on the name of one of the Great Beasts, the most noble beings in creation, yet you walk away from this battle. You speak of your record in the IWC, but cast aside the honors bestowed to you as if they were rotten. If you wish to wear a mask, then do it. But do not pretend that someone else has strapped it to your face. (The Dragon is obviously enraged; he points at the Phoenix and shouts something in Chinese, but it's incomprehensible over the noise of the crowd.) Phoenix: You and I will fight, Dragon, today and in the future. Leave the belt where it is. After I have destroyed you, I will take it. I've worn such belts before. Just like the black belts of martial arts masters, it is a symbol of honor and glory. You may throw it away, but the wise man would treasure it. Now, if you're not a coward, prove it. Step in the ring. We will fight, and we will see how long you withstand my fire. (The Phoenix drops the microphone, bows deeply, and steps back against the far turnbuckle, to the resounding cheers of the crowd.) (The Drago [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] : No!!!!! It was caught by Phoenix. And there's a leg sweep takedown and Dragon hits hard. Bobby Cane: Man did his head hit the mat hard. This is the greatest match I've seen in a long time. Follow up by Phoenix.......Picks up Dragon......Snap Suplex... Howard Cleese: Again the White Phoenix throws him into the corner....Follow up. Flying Back Heel Kick!!!!!!! Bobby Cane: That's the Phoenix Strike!!!!!!!! That's his finisher!!!!!!! 1!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! the winner the White Phoenix!!!!! Howard Cleese: The ref has the Crusierweight Championship belt....... but he's not giving it to anyone........ Bobby Cane: That's right! Raging Dragon did relinquish the title before the match....Was this even a title bout? What the hell's going on here Cleese? Howard Cleese: I don't know.....I really don't know.....But I know someone who does. Here comes IWC commissioner Fred Lang. He's coming out from back. It looks like he has answers. Fred Lang: I've been watching from the back, and I understand what the Controversy is all about is all about. Dragon you did officially relinquish the title before this match. (the crowd cheers and the White Phoenix is upset as they think Raging Dragon is still the champ) BUT, when you picked up the belt to make your way to the ring, you reclaimed your belt. Since no one else claimed it you became champion again. This once again was a Championship match! Bobby Cane: WE HAVE A NEW CHAMPION!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THE WHITE PHOENIX IS CHAMP!!!!!!!!!!!!! Howard Cleese: yes he is, and look at him celebrate in the middle o f the ring! Bobby Cane : Well, it was a great match, and long hail king Phoenix. ("The Nate Song" blares and recieves "The Usual Cheers and Jeers" from the capacity crowd as Lector, Steve, and Nate get in the ring.) Bobby Cane:It's Suspect time! Howard Cleese: Oh great! You know Nate is almost forty years old and he acts like he's sixteen. An now that he has Steve Tadd with him it's even worse. It's as if the IWC turns into their personal study hall. BC: But he is a riot. An the only reason Steve is around is because.... HC: Nate has ticked off so many people that they want to beat him up. BC: When you say it, it sounds bad. Nate:(pulling a mic out of his vest as the music dies down) Please sit down, we love ya really. Just some things to take care of. First Lector and Steve you guys don't have to wrestle because you did such a "bang" up job.(holds mic in front of Lector) Lector: I can't take all the credit. (holds up belt) I'd like to thank Conrad Jefferson and Black Pantha for beating on Johnny Bravo. But, most of all I'd like to thank the man who started the Suspects. My Mentor, My Friend, and My Boss...Nate Shaft.(mixed reaction) Yet, Steve you also did a "bang up" job this weekend too.(walks away so Steve may talk into the mic) HC: Like Nate needs a bigger ego. BC: Hey when Lector's right, [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] got one of my own to face in the ring in order to qualify for the Ringmaster Tourney. Marksman, you can't hit what you can't see, and, more importantly, you can't him me when you're lying face down on the mat. You got a torpedo pointed at you, buddy. What's it feel like to be the hunted instead of the hunter? Huh? That's what you are, believe me. I've got you in my sights, and, come Monday, November 15th...... (he holds up the 2 x 4, pointing it at the camera like a gun) Torpedo: Bang!! Battleship sunk, as I blow you right out of the water!! Now, Iain't got nothing against you, but the fact of the matter is that you're stading in my way. And that, believe me, is the one place you don't wanna be. But, you won't be there for long, cuz after I get through with you you'll be left in my wake just like everyone else. Plain and simple, guaranteed.
