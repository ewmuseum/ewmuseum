[IWC] Saturday Night (23 November)

23 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Frank King is heard trying to speak, but the crowd noise is deafening when the light's go out and "Enter Sandman" by Metallica is heard in the arena) (he shouts just to be heard) THIS MATCH IS FOR THE CRUSIERWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP OF THE WORLD!!!!!!!!!!! COMING TO THE RING FIRST, THE CHALLENGER........ FROM DEATH VALLEY, CALIFORNIA.....WEIGHING IN AT 225 POUNDS, THE FORMER CRUISERWIEGHT CHAMPION ...........THE DARK KNIGHT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (DK goes to the ring slowly as lighters fill the arena with a little light. he looks ready to go to business, but does gesture to the crowd to show appreciation for their great ovation, as he en [gap in the surviving capture — the intervening broadcast text was not recovered] WP clubs Money from behind! What the fuc....heck is going on! He just Choke Slammed Deam! DWP's taking off his mask! IT"S LECTOR IN DISGUISE! HE AND PAIGE ARE STOMPING THE FORMER CHAMPIONS! HERE COMES TROUBLE...DWP IS COMING OUT OF THE STANDS! HE IS COMING IN THE RING LECTOR AND PAIGE LOOK WORRIED! I CAN"T BELIEVE THIS WHERE THE HELL IS CANE!!!! DWP HELPS UP MONEY AND GIVES HIM A POWERBOMB!!!! HE TAKES OFF HIS MASK IT"S STEVE TADD!!! THIS IS BLOODY MADDNESS!!! HERE COMES ANOTHER DWP WITH BIG D!! THIS HAS TO BE THE REAL ONE THEY BOTH HAVE BATS!!!! FROM THE RAFTERS FOUR ROPES WHO THE HELL IS UP THERE......IT'S TORPEDO, NATE SHAFT, JESSE WILLIAMS...AND....BOBBY CANE!!!! THEY HOIST THE MEN TO THE RAFTERS!!!! I CAN"T BELIEVE THIS WE"RE OUTTA TIME!!!!!! (Fans cheer as Torpedo, Nate, Jesse, Bobby, Lector, Steve, Paige, and Drake wave at the Extreme Team) [name withheld] [detail withheld] IWC President Handler of WAR!
