Corporal Punishment 6'6, 320 lbs. Heel From: Quantico, Virginia Finisher: Boot Camp Finisher Description: Boot to the head Entrance Music: "Warheads" by Extreme Background: Austin Jakowski is a former marine who served in the gulf war. After the war, he received an honorable discharge. Unsure of what to do after military life, he ran into an old high school friend who was running a minor independent federation. The friend convinced Austin to join. So Austin gave it a shot and wrestled under the name of Corporal Punishment. Corporal Punishment became well known within the mid-atlantic area. It was at an MEWF show in Baltimore, Maryland, that SWF Booker Brian Frenck would see him wrestle and decide to sign him to the SWF.
Upon Corporal Punishment's arrival, he noticed that there seemed to be stregnth in numbers as all of the champions belonged to one stable or another. So he took it upon himself to start a new stable. A stable he referred to as the Third World Order (T.w.O.) Corporal Punishment decided that he did not want a bunch of rookies in his stable, so he recruited 4 wrestlers that had been with the SWF since day one. Mr. Roboto, Time Warp, Monster Mark, and Deaf Leopard. And while things have not gone as as he had hoped, the Corporal remains confident that one day the T.w.O. will be the top dogs in the SWF.
