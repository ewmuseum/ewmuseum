Hi, it's me again, continuing to update the RSPWF newsgroud with information on the Supreme Wrestling Federation. We still don't have enough wrestlers coming in, it's gonna be a lot of fun though so read and join. SWF Superme Wrestling Federation Commissioner: The Legendary Ron Yvette Newsflash Updated Daily Description Cards PPVs Rankings Titles Other Stuff Joining NEWS FLASH! (News Flash done in Mark Mode) TODAY Another wrestler signed on the dotted line today. That wrestler's name is The Vigilante, a former police officer who's had enough of the revolving door jail system and is out for revenge. There is a rumoured tie between this man and Booker [name withheld], more as this story develops, though I'm sure any relation would mean Mr. Vigilante would not be allowed to compete on SWF Slaughterhouse or for the [name withheld] controlled Tough Man Title. Another rumour has it that perhaps some former members of the Reign of Terror may be on their way to the SWF. Mr. Yvette has told us he doesn't want his fed to turn into a Underground and ROT dominated alliance and he may cap the amount of members from each alliance to make sure there is variety. Mr. America is also a member of the Underground Alliance. Commissioner Yvette has announced an infomercial for the SWF to air all over North America. It will feature SWF stars in competetion with stars from other Pro-Wrestling federations in a small hall in Baltimore. These wrestlers chose to compete and were not sanctioned to compete by their employers. The matches enclude: Phoenix vs. Shawn Michaels Doug Bacon vs. The Sandman Jagged Edge vs. The Undertaker Mr. America vs. Hollywood Hogan The Championships section has been updated Put all rumours aside, the Phoenix has been sent to join Doug Bacon representing the Underground Alliance in the SWF. Phoenix is a monster who seems to destroy the competition no matter where he goes. Can anyone defeat this man? More rumours circulating around that the estemed Professer Mike Janus may be on his way to Supreme Wrestlling Federation. At this time there are no confirmed reports. As of today this is the current SWF roster.... FRONT OFFICE Commissioner/Tuesday Booker: Ron Yvette Saturday Booker: [name withheld] Rankings/Week In Review: [name withheld] WRESTLERS Mr. America Doug Bacon Jagged Edge Phoenix MY ORIGINAL PLAN I've been in e-wrestling for a while now and never, until I came up with this plan, have I had the desire to run my own fed. However, I'm really interested to see how this one turns out and if it goes as I hope it willl then I'm sure it'll run for as long as I'm still at home or not working a lot. Mostly everything will be e-mail based as it's a pain to update homepages all the time and it's more convenient to get stuff via e-mail. The flashes will be sent out to a mailing list, and the rankings, profiles, etc. will be sent out to the mailing list by myself. On the flash topic, I don't want to restrict you, so you can say whatever you want in the flashes as long as it doesn't break one of the following rules. discriminatory slurs (you know what they are) other wrestlers' without their handler's permission current wrestling superstars (considering it would be built as a competative fed) Only one wrestlers per handler, and I won't have a wrestler. It will be based on TNM, except I don't have TNM plus so hopefully the Saturday Card writer will. I will book matches if you don't flash. Oh, and if you're one of those big ass whiners, don't bother joining. The whole idea is a fed that would be able to acceptably compete in today's wrestling markett, but still have a lot of extreme type fun. To understand this completely you'll have to read the following review of the cards. Set: TBA (card writer) Announcers: TBA (card writer) Arena: TBA (card writer) Basic Concept: This show would be on at late night so that the wrestlers could be vicious, bloodthirsty, weapon using maniacs and still be on TV :) This is where you'd set up the crazy gimmick matches, the bloody attacks, the casual curse or sexual reference, basically no holds barred live crazyness. You know what that means so I don't have to really go into detail on it. Any non-PPV title matches will most likely be on TT. THURSDAY: SWF Across the World! A house show. Just matches. One bad camera angle, crowd pops, guys running in to the ring, ring introductions and winners announced, cheap little run ins, and faces saying how much they love the town while heels disagree. That's all it is. SWF RINGSIDE REPORT There will be highlights of past cards as well as the schedule for upcoming cards. There will be an interview segment, as well as "can we talk questions." There will be a rumor segment and for those of you who want to do the anonymous guy who spreads stuff. I'll gladly take those and keep your identity a secret. There will also be ramblings of the hosts as to the latest happenings around the SWF. That should cover the basics. Any questions then contact me at [detail withheld] PPVs I will do one PPV every month and a half. I have the first three planned already. I hope to start up in May, maybe even sooner if I get the response.. June 1rst - SWF World Title Tournament Where: Camden Yards, Baltimore (yes, I know it's outdoors) Description: The first 16 wrestlers signed in the SWF will square off in a new style of tournament. The winners of the first matches go on in the tournament while the losers square off on the B-Side of the tournament to decide who is the B-Side winner. The winner of the A-Side (winners) will face the winner of the B-Side in a No DQ, Falls Count Anywhere Ladder Match for the SWF World Title. Here's the catch. The B-Side winner must pin the A-Side winner twice while the A-Side winner only has to pin the B-Side winner once. Get it? Plus any other grudge matcehs. July 13th - Super Run Of Death Description: 10 rings spread out around the arena. All of them feature a different gimmick match. These rings will be used in a 9 man run of death tournament where the winner of match 1 goes on to match 2 etc. The winner of the run of death will get a shot at the champion at the next PPV. Also championship matches and any other grudge matches. August 31rst - SupremeFest Description: The SWF's showcase card. This one will have celebrities, majorly hyped matches, all that sort of stuff. Our major event of the year. TITLES SWF World Title The main title in SWF land. This title goes to the "best" wrestler in the federation, the highest ranked get the shots. It has to be defended once every 30 days. Anyone who loses a title shot due to a DQ or Count Out will eventually get another one. If the champion walks out of a match his title defence does not count. SWF American Title Same rules apply as above. Can only be won by someone who is an American citizen. SWF International Title Same rules apply as World Title. Can only be won by someone who is a citizen of a non-american country. SWF Tag Team Titles For Tag Teams. Same defence rules apply. SWF Tough Man Title - Booker [name withheld]'s Title 1. all matches involving the Toughman Title must involve some sort of gimmick match (obviously) 2. Tough Man Title Defenses. a. Title must be defended at least once a month (that does not include PPV or title shot result of Tough Man Gauntlet) and at all PPV's b. Champion chooses the stipulations for the match unless he wants his opponent or SWF management to decide, except under the following conditions i. defending at PPV - all Tough Man title defenses will be "spin the wheel make the deal" matches with the stips being announced just before the match. ii. defending against the winner of the Tough Man Gauntlet - The 1st SWF Slaughterhouse of each month will have a special Tough Man Gauntlet match, which works like this. 2 competitors will be chosen at random (current champions will be excluded from this drawing) the 2 competitors will battle it out in a normal match. the winner of that match will then wrestle in a handicap match against 2 random competitors (champions will be included in this selection) If the wrestler is fortunate to win this match as well, he will then proceed to a handicap match against THREE random competitors (again, champs will be included in this drawing) and should the victor of that first match also be lucky enough to win this match, he will then get to wrestle the current Tough Man Champ the following SWF Slaughterhouse show, and he will get to choose the stips, unless he wants the Tough Man Champ or the SWF management to decide. Just so you know, I'm Canadian, but the fed will be based in America (lets say Baltimore, I don't know why but what the hell) so that's why the title is there. RANKINGS This is the first official SWF Rankings system. Wins Win by pin or submission +2pts Win by disqualification or count out +1pt Losses Loss by pin or submission -2pts Loss by disqualifcation or count out -1pt. Ties No points won or lost Triangle Matches If it's a one fall, then the winner will recieve points for only one win. If it's an elimination, then the winner must beat both of his opponents, he'll recieve the appropriate points for both, to get the right to two wins. If he only beats one man, then he will recieve the corresponding points. If one of the losing participants gets a win, then he will also recieve the corresponding points. (i.e. if one man pins his opponent then loses by DQ, he gets 1pt. If he wins by DQ, but loses by pin then he gets -1pt. Ect. You get the point.) This rule will follow for effect for both singles and tag teams. Of course this same rule applies to 2/3 Falls Matches. Battle Royals etc. Battle royals, royal rumbles, and survivor series matches will not effect your ranking. 3 Man Tag Matches 3-man tag matches will effect the corresponding. (i.e. a tag team and their single ally win the match, they get the corresponding number of points. If they lose, then the same applies. The tag team will have it go to the tag team, unless they so desire to have it in singles [at their descrection if they wish to put it in singles] and the singles will go into his singles column. Hardcore Matches There will be no extra points for hardcore matches winning or losing. Tie Breaking 1-Whichever wrestler/team has more wins. The verterans to get special treatment. 2-If they have the same amount of wins, then it will be decided by whoever won when the two wrestlers/teams last met. If wrestler/team A beat wrestler/team B, then wrestler/team B is ranked higher and vice versa. 3-Whichever wrestler/team has the least loses. This is the same for singles and tag teams. Other Also, so no new wrestlers jump to first place, you will have to have wrestled four matches to be ranked. Jobber matches will count against your record as well.
