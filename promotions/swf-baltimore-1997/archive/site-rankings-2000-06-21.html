The Sanitarium of Fear Rank 4. The Living Dead Rank 5. The Sugar Circus Scoring Method: +2 for winning by pinfall or submission. +1 for winning by countout or disqualification. 0 for draws. -1 for losing by countout or disqualification. -2 for losing by pinfall or submission. no points awarded for battle royals. in three way or four matches, above rules apply per fall. (i.e. in a four way match, if you pin 2 people but got pinned by the 3rd, you'd get +2 for each of the two you pinned and -2 for being pinned, totalling +2.)
Handicap matches: if the outnumbered side wins, they get 2 points, and -2 for who got pinned. No points are used if the outnumbered side loses.
No extra points for Gimmick or Title matches.
