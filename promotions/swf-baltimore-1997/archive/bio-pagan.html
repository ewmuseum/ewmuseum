Pagan 6'3, 246 lbs. Heel From: New Orleans, Louisiana Finisher: Final Judgement Finisher Description: Top rope double underhook DDT Manager: Destiny Entrance Music: "Death Wish" by Christian Death SWF Titles held: SWF American Title Background: Abandoned before birth by his father and disowned before the age of 10 by his mother, Michael Christopher spent most of his youth seeking the answers to his questions in his birthplace of New Orleans, Louisiana. Considered a social outcast, the majority of his time was spent reading everything from Shakespeare to Poe to the books on which every world religion were based. Frustrated in finding no salvation in any of these words, he tossed his past into the very back of his mind and entered the world of professional pit fighting at the age of 18.
Battered and bruised almost nightly, it was here that he was discovered by a scout from a major wrestling organization based out of Canada. Convinced that he had what it took to be a leader in a more extreme style of professional wrestling, he took the job and headed north. It was here, spending half of the night in a hotel bar after battling for nearly one hour in a battle royal taking place in Toronto, that he met a young lady by the name of Emily Campbell who would later be introduced to the wrestling world as Aissa. Never really gaining a name for himself, Christopher became frustrated with the working schedules and lack of push. Using this as a learning experience, he packed his bags and headed for a tour of organizations throughout Europe where he captured his first championship along with former tag team partner, Mark Adams. Holding onto the titles for around four months, the team received an offer that they could not refuse. They were brought to Japan for a try out and were immediately accepted into the ranks of many top stars.
Their union however was soon sent into a bitter downward spiral as Christopher discovered his true nature... that of a heel. On December 19, 1994, Pagan was born. A shroud of darkness seemed to engulf his entire presence wherever he went, and his career shot directly to the top. Bringing in Aissa as his new manager, Pagan spent close to a year switching titles back and forth with his former tag team partner in a feud that ended with a table death match, which would actually be Adams' last match in the sport. Steadily rising higher and higher up the ladder and constantly increasing his arsenal of moves along the way, Pagan became a household name throughout Japan. At the peak of his career overseas, he was contacted by an American based fed called the Supreme Wrestling Federation. Finally after weeks of negotiations, Pagan made his SWF debut on June 28, 1997. Despite a brief absense from the sport (rumoured to be mainly because of Aissa's recent pregnancy), Pagan has returned to an entirely new SWF. A league that has become even more brutal and chaotic since his departure is now forced to look into the cold eyes of the man of sin yet again.
However, not even Pagan remains the same. More determined, more blood thirsty, and maybe more unstable than ever before... he has turned the tables on the world. The answers he spent so long seeking have been found, but now he has thrown an entirely new set of questions before the wrestling world and only he knows the truth.
