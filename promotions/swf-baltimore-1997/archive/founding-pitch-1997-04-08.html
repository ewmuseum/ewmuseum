New Fed - SWF

8 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, read this or go to the homepage at http://www.geocities.com/TimesSquare/Alley/7415/swf.html SWF Superme Wrestling Federation "NO CHEAP GRAPHICS, NO SUPERSTAR PICTURES, JUST DAMN FINE E-WRESTLING AT IT'S BEST!" Commissioner: The Legendary Ron Yvette Description Cards PPVs Titles Other Stuff Joining MY ORIGINAL PLAN I've been in e-wrestling for a while now and never, until I came up with this plan, have I had the desire to run my own fed. However, I'm really interested to see how this one turns out and if it goes as I hope it willl then I'm sure it'll run for as long as I'm still at home or not working a lot. Mostly everything will be e-mail based as it's a pain to update homepages all the time and it's more convenient to get stuff via e-mail. The flashes will be sent out to a mailing list, and the rankings, profiles, etc. will be sent out to the mailing list by myself. On the flash topic, I don't want to restrict you, so you can say whatever you want in the flashes as long as it doesn't break one of the following rules. discriminatory slurs (you know what they are) other wrestlers' without their handler's permission current wrestling superstars (considering it would be built as a competative fed) Only one wrestlers per handler, and I won't have a wrestler. It will be based on TNM, except I don't have TNM plus so hopefully the Saturday Card writer will. I will book matches if you don't flash. Oh, and if you're one of those big ass whiners, don't bother joining. The whole idea is a fed that would be able to acceptably compete in today's wrestling markett, but still have a lot of extreme type fun. To understand this completely you'll have to read the following review of the cards. CARDS SATURDAY CARD (To be named by the person I get to write the card) Important Stats: Airs live on sindication from 11:30pm until 1:30am. Set: TBA (card writer) Announcers: TBA (card writer) Arena: TBA (card writer) Basic Concept: This show would be on at late night so that the wrestlers could be vicious, bloodthirsty, weapon using maniacs and still be on TV :) This is where you'd set up the crazy gimmick matches, the bloody attacks, the casual curse or sexual reference, basically no holds barred live crazyness. Not many titles will be defended on this show, with the exception of the tough man title. TRIUMPHUNT TUESDAY LIVE! Important Stats: Airs live all over the world on some big name network television chanel. No names. From 8:00pm to 10:00pm. Card Writer: Me Set: Normal arena except for a big four way screen hanging over the ring and two giant red Ts on either side of the curtains. Announcers: Robert Jones, Tom Rogers, Captain Canuck, RA: Joe Hall, Backstage: Gabe Zachery-Colbert. Arena: Rotates. Little Tidbits: There will be an interview show with Captain Canuck called "Captain's Corner" every show for intervies and attacks. I will ask enemies of anyone wrestling to send in little comments about their enemy to be played during the match as a sort of double screen thing. Jobber matches could be added if requested. For attacks if there are circumstances that call for me to use my judgement on how it would play out I won't hesitate to do this, and I'll show no bias, you have to trust me. Basic Idea: This is a RAW/Nitro type show. This is where you have to censor yourselves, don't do anything they wouldn't do on national TV from 8 to 10. You know what that means so I don't have to really go into detail on it. Any non-PPV title matches will most likely be on TT. THURSDAY: SWF Across the World! A house show. Just matches. I will try to make this the most like an actual house show possible. One bad camera angle, crowd pops, guys running in to the ring, ring introductions and winners announced, cheap little run ins, and faces saying how much they love the town while heels disagree. That's all it is. PPVs I will do one PPV every month and a half. I have the first three planned already. I hope to start up in May, maybe even sooner if I get the response.. June 1rst - SWF World Title Tournament Where: Camden Yards, Baltimore (yes, I know it's outdoors) Description: The first 16 wrestlers signed in the SWF will square off in a new style of tournament. The winners of the first matches go on in the tournament while the losers square off on the B-Side of the tournament to decide who is the B-Side winner. The winner of the A-Side (winners) will face the winner of the B-Side in a No DQ, Falls Count Anywhere Ladder Match for the SWF World Title. Here's the catch. The B-Side winner must pin the A-Side winner twice while the A-Side winner only has to pin the B-Side winner once. Get it? Plus any other grudge matcehs. July 13th - Super Run Of Death Description: 10 rings spread out around the arena. All of them feature a different gimmick match. These rings will be used in a 9 man run of death tournament where the winner of match 1 goes on to match 2 etc. The winner of the run of death will get a shot at the champion at the next PPV. Also championship matches and any other grudge matches. August 31rst - SupremeFest Description: The SWF's showcase card. This one will have celebrities, majorly hyped matches, all that sort of stuff. Our major event of the year. TITLES SWF World Title - The major title SWF American Title - Can be won only by American born wrestlers SWF International Title - Can be won only by non-Americans SWF Tag Team Titles SWF Tough Man Title - Gimmick Match Title basically Just so you know, I'm Canadian, but the fed will be based in America (lets say Baltimore, I don't know why but what the hell) so that's why the title is there. OTHER STUFF The whole point of this is of course to have fun. I look to have fun, I want you to have fun, lets make it fun. It should be interesting to add together the best of both worlds here as I combine ideas from some of the different feds I've been in. Credit to Stevie of the OWA for inspiring the "be realistic" part of the fed and to all UEA guys for the "Blood" part of the fed, because that has it's place too. I hope to see lots of roleplaying and lots of fun feuds. Also, look for the commish to get involved now and then :) JOINING One of the things I really didn't want to do was put all that crap in the TNM, so I'm not going to do it. Of course without TNM Plus this may hanper the tag team division (lack of finishers) but we'll see what we can do about that. Oh, and you'll need to send me things for the profile of your wrestler... Your Name: E-Mail: Wrestler's Name: Height: Weight: Born: Backround: Manager: So, if you want to join the way to do it is to create your wrestler in TNM, export him, and send it my way. Do it all, including managers, because I don't want to have to. I know it's lazy and all but it's the only way I'll run the fed. If you are thinking about creating a bunch of new moves forget it, I'll be scanning for that. No Bull shit please :) As soon as we're ready to start it up I'll mail you with the mailing list and we'll get started. E-Mail Me With Questions or Wrestlers...
