Mr. Shhh SWF World Champion 6'3", 240 lbs. From: New York City, New York Finisher: The Peacemaker Finisher Description: Kneebash to the face Manager: Mr. Smith & Mr. Jones Entrance Music: "Money" by Pink Floyd SWF Titles held: SWF American Title, SWF World Title – current Background: Alex Tyler, A.K.A. Mr. Shhh, was born to Scottish immigrants on October 4, 1968 in New York. His is a true story of “rags to riches”.
Alex Tyler spent his youth in a really low class, nasty area of New York City. His father, was good man who got in over his head. At age eight Alex spent his days doing odd jobs around the neighborhood just to help his family. Then his sister Jezebel was born but his family could not provide for her and had to send her away to Scotland to live with relatives. Jezebel later married Sean McKenzie and returned to the United States and under Alex’s guidance McKenzie also joined the SWF.
Seeing the need for a better environment for his family Alex’s father aided a group of a prominent N.Y. family. Later as the Tyler family was fleeing town their car was forced off the road. Both parents were shot to death and Alex was taken in by “the family”. He was educated in the best school and was trained in the art of survival. He is very good at his trade and has made billions. Although rich he longs for the challenge of the hunt and the final battle. Hence he showed up one day at the door of Brian Frenck and was hired immediately. He started out as a loner in the SWF moving up very quickly. He made an alliance with another wrestler named Pagan and was later brought into a stable known as Trinity. Later Alex had to fight Pagan for the American Title Championship. During the Ides of March PPV, in a surprise move he temporarily denounced Trinity and joined a stable known as The Gangstas in order to compete and win the World Title Championship. He left The Gangstas shortly after the win and he along with former Trinity stablemate The Sandman joined forces. Soon, after a long absence, Pagan joined the two and together the three formed The Church of Pain. At the time of this writing The Artful Dodger and The Thugz are also members of the group making them the marquee names of the SWF. Alex, or Mr. Shhh, is a powerful faction to be reckoned with and is considered “One of the deadliest men” in the SWF and possibly the world.
