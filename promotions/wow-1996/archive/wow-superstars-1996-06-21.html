{We see Havoc, again he is smashing his forearm against a concrete wall. Over and over again, until he spins around, glaring at the camera} [JH] Attention! {mock military cry} Well, he's got -my- attention. And no way some guy in a uniform is standing in my way. Chaos is in effect, Sodier-Boy, and the battle lines have been drawn. And I've got more bones to pick with some others out there as well...So don't think -any- of you are safe. {Qoth the Raven sign again, which is just his way of saying, The Chaos Effect/there are so few hand signals left, sorry./} Sonic's gonna' go....{turns to slam wall again as he shouts..} BOOM!!! {cheez openeng montage, I'm going to summarize mainly with flashes. Hopefully, later in the week Chris will have time to actually write this out. Hunchback of Notre Dame took to long} {A row of seats high up above a certain lawn tennis club in South London i.e Wimbledon. Westbrook is watching a game which can only be heard in the background; the point finished with a ripple of polite applause, and he turns to the camera} [Jamie Westbrook] Well, that was the Battle Royale, was it? My, what an interesting idea that was - not REAL wrestling of course, more like a disreputable pub brawl, but it was certainly an interesting experience. Not my cup of tea, though; I prefer proper wrestling, but I'm pleased not notice quite a few Americans gave up before I did. Got in a few good licks before going down - after all, there's only so much one Brit can do against so many Yanks. Back to normal business this week: another American, another victory, I guess. But I'm really looking forward to my next contest against some Joey Igrozza chap - sounds a bit like an Italian to me, but I'm assured he is an American. Presumably one of the poor, or huddled masses, or something. No matter. He seems to have an interesting view of history; I look forward to giving him a lesson thereof. Now, if you'll excuse me, I think I hear a strawberry cream tea calling... {back to ring, Westbrook vs. The Aerialist} Aerialist screams "I wanna win the world title", and turns around into a Westbrooke bearhug. Jamies breaks after a few seconds to punch the "yank", which he follows with a rope rake. He tosses the aerialist to the ropes and demonstrates the accuracy of his name with a backdrop. He hits a side suplex, follows it with a jawbreaker, and sends his opponent to the ropes for a powerslam and the 1..2..pick your opponent up by the hair and piss off the ref. He hits a somersault thunder flip (BTW, the second anyone wants to describe that move to me, feel free to), and picks him up for a punch combo. He whips him to the corner, follows in with a corner piston kick, climbs to the top for a moonsault, and picks him up for his finisher, the crusifix shoulderslam. A 1, 2 and 3 later and we have a winner in 1:59 {The locker room area. Larry Dawson stands next to Don Antonio and the rest of The Maffia. Lynch "The Hunter" Xavier is among the men. Larry speaks..} [LD] I am standing here in the locker room area with Don Antonio, the manager of The Maffia. Mr. Antonio, has Lynch " The Hunter" Xavier joined The Maffia? [DA] That's-a Don Antonio. Well Larry, you see, I made him an-a offer, he couldn't-a refuse. HAHAHAHAHA. [LD] And what might that be Mr. Antonio? [DA] That's DON Antonio, DON! Mr. Xavier came to us and asked us to grant-a him a request. I said we could do-a that if in-a turn, he would preform favors for such-a a request-a. He has accepted our-a deal and we will pay for his-a surgery to put a metal plate-a in-a his neck, and he will be our-a "hit man". HAHAHAHA. {Next, Lynch vs. Cavalier} Since Cav is a tag wrestler, and Lynch a singles, we shouldn't expect much out of Cav (besides, the dice hated him today). Lynch nails Cav with a short-arm chlothesline, a side suplex, a cover, and not even 2. He picks him up and hits a belly to belly, sends Cav and himself to the ropes and hits a bulldog lariat, and a northern right suplex, but again Cavalier escapes before 2. Lynch DDTs the Seeker, gives him a full nelson suplex, and the covers, gets 2, but not 3. He russian legsweeps the face, puts on an abdominal stretch which Cavalier quickaly escapes. european piledrives the 235 pounder, and covers. Once again, the "hitman" fails to get two. Picks him up, hits his finisher the cradle suplex, and finaly manages to get that third slap and the win. Lynch Xavier win in 2:27 {Ogre vs. Bill Kramer} Ogre opens up with a whip to the turnbuckle, and goes in with a tripple turnbuckle smash. He picks up Kramer and repeats the move. Rinse and repeat again, and finaly settles to leaving him in the corner and punching him twice. He continues the fists with a punch combo and a punch flurry. He avalanches the poor old man and the pin is only academic at this point. Ogre in 1:19. {Boot Hill cemetery. In the background, a funeral service is taking place, presided over by "Fats" McGwire. In the foreground is "Slim", resplendent in a black morning suit (and that is a LOT of material), with his back to us, and a Stetson (black, naturally) in his hand. He turns to the camera} [Slim] We're the World Champions. I said we would be, and we are - you know I ain't no liar. So Thunder and Lightning had better listen up, and listen good. They might as well stay home. The result will be the same, and it'll be a might less painful for them. There's no darn way we're going to lose our title to a pair of cripples fresh out of the hospital. Even fully fit, they wouldn't have a chance. We'll book them a place here - one plot will be enough, by the we've finished with them... {He replaces the hat on his head and exits, as his brother lobs a handful of earth into the grave} {AHHHH!!!! AOL COMERCIAL!!!!! AHHHHHH!!!!!!} [Victor Gold] The team of the 90's gets the title shot they've been waiting for!!! You put us in the hospital...but couldn't keep us there!! We mean business...and fans...WE WON'T disappoint!! The titles will be ours...prepare to be THUNDERSTRUCK!!! [Thunder] Its all in the past...we won..but a cheap shot put us out of action!!! [Lightning] Revenge is sweet..and you can't keep us down!! [Thunder] Power... [Lightning] And Speed... [Thunder] We will be the champs...they're our belts and we're back...slamm'in harding then ever!!! [Lightning] We're coming baby, Gold Corp style!!! {tag title match, Thunder and Lightning vs. Outlaw Brothers} Flex and slim start with a test of strength. Slim wins and stomps Flex. Flex dodges a vertical splash and SLim bites mucho dust. He tries a press Slam but can't pick up all 510 pounds. Lynch and the Maffia begin to make their way to the ring. Slim elbows Flex, the Maffia blocks the ringway, and Lynch enters the ringside area Flex press slams the big guy, and Lynch trips over the ringside steps. Both to the ropes and Flex floors Slim with a BIG chlothesline. Slim blocks the tag, kicks Flex, but Flex manages to block Slim's tag. Lynch belly to bellies Victor Gold, Flex press slams again, tag, as Lynch stomps the manager. JOhnny Dreamer runs down to ringside , but is stopped by a Bull chlothesline and a Slick Vic legdrop. Lynch dodges a Lou Thez from rick off the platform, and Thunded notices what's going on, but misses a Thunder Strike flying shoulderblock. Vic with a kick and low blow to Johnny, as Lynch tries to side suplex Ricky, but gets dropkicked and Ricky goes over to help Johnny. Bull powerbombs John, as Vic catches Rick and irish-whips him to the railing. Bull splashes John, and Lynch stomps Flex. Bull tries for a second powerbomb, but JOhn backdrops his way out of the second finisher. Ricky clips Vic's knee, so the others can finaly get by. At the same time, Cavalier emerges from the back. Lynch gets a side suplex off before Johnny gets there. Though he doesn't notice him immidiately. Johnny goes to the apron as Lynch picks up Flex again, yet receives a press slam for his efforts. Johnny hits a hollwood drop from the apron, and the bell rings signaling Thunder and Lightning got so wrapped up in Vic Gold and everything, they forgot their match. So the outlaws win in 2:57. Dreamer et. al chase off Lynch et. al. {Cut to a man standing by in an interview area. He has unkept hair which hangs over his face keeps the camera form getting a good look at his face.} [Man] You know people say that growing up can be rough.... those people don't know the half of it. From the day I walked into my high school as a lowly 7th grader, Brian Wilson, a 9th grader not to be confused with the lead singer from the Beach Boys.... took it upon himself to make my life difficult. The next 4 years for me was a living hell of being beaten up, riduculed and laughed at... and even when Brian graduated, that pain he inflicted on my just come on coming.... {He flips the hair out of the front of his face so you finally see his eyes} Because I had to live with the fact that *I* let him do that to me.... I never had the guts to stand up to him and I damned myself to a personal hell.... But now I'm ready to face him and you know what.... I can't find the guy. And it's haunted me day in and day out.... Now that I'm bigger, stronger and ready to cave in his skull... I can't find that son of a bitch! So now I head into WOW and you know what I see.... I see myself surrounded by Brain Wilsons.... But I just want you all to know, Brian..... nobody steps on Devon Glenn anymore... {Devon Glenn vs. Will Mtthewson} Devon opens up with a punch. Both wrestlers go to the ropes, and Devon hits a bodypress but doesn't go for the pin. He fist drops Will, headbutt drops him, again both to the ropes, and chlothelsine. He throws Will through the ropes, and does a springboard bodypress to the outside. He does a plancha axe handle from the top rope to follow it up, moves the pads and hits a running powerbomb. You can hear Devon mumbling "Come on Brian... don't give up on me now... the fun's just starting...". He rolls Will bakc in, sets him up on the top, and back brian kicks him off. Devon goes to the ropes, and "accidently" collides with the ref. He goes to the outside, grabs a chair, and goes up top. By this time Will is up, but the flying chair shot knocks him back down. He goes up top again, holds the chair under his legs, and jumps off for a somersault chair shot. He hits a springboard headbutt drop as the ref begins to awaken, and picks up Will for a fisherman buster (cradle suplex that stops when the opponent is picked up over head and falls to the side, ala brainbuster), and covers a near unconcious Will Matthewson for the pin. Devon Glenn wins in 1:59. {Havok is in the locker room, he has the TV title strapped around his waist....He looks angrily at the camera....} [James Havok] New T.V. Champ of WOW, baby! Vorpal, you crazy son of a clown, I'm gonna' knock your head so hard, you'll be sane for a week! I survived 15 of WOWs best, what do you think I'm going to do to you, PUNK! We're gettin' CHAOTIC this weekend! {He spreads his arms} Chaos is in EFFECT!!!!!! {camera fades in on a padded room. inside , nicolas vorpal is in full restraints and bouncing off the walls and hiting his head on the padding. he sees the camera and runs after it foaming at the mouth. the camera is quick though and dashes out the door. as the door slams behind the camera man, the camera looks back through the window of the room only to see vorpal crash into the window, cracking the glass. Dr. Gregory Helsing stands next to the room's door and starts to talk to the camera....} [GH] This trip to the WOW has really been good for you, ay Nic? {from inside the padded room you hear a gruff scream} [GH] Yes it has, hasn't it. You have seen what my Nicolas Vorpal can really do. Your champion is now in the hospital because of the power of Nicolas Vorpal. He is a rabid wolf , an amazon gorrila , a nile crocodile when you step into the ring with this man, you put not only your title on the line, Havok, you put your life and sanity on the line. When you leave this arena(he is now pointing at the camera and talking with much enthusiasim),you will be on a stretcher and you will not have that title around your waist! And now, I'd like to introduce to you, a new member of The Helsing Insane Stable, OGRE! {the camera turns to the left and sitting in the corner is the huge ogre nawing on a piece of raw meat. he sees the camera, gives a grunt and goes back to eating the dripping raw steak} [GH] I was so impressed with his performance in the battle royale, that i offered him a part in my stable. he will fit in well. Havok, I hope, I sincerely hope, you have insurance, cause you're gonna need it. {James Havok vs. Nicholas Vorpal} Ogre hits Havok with a large bone on the was to the ring. However, Havok forearms Ogre and gets the bone. He begins to beat on Ogre with the bone (which he seems right at home doing),a dn Greg Randolph finaly seems to realize there is supposed to be a match going on. On a stroke of brilliance, he says that the match will start when Havok gets to the ring. Havok sends Ogre to the ringside barrier hard, and, for some odd reason, Vorpal goes out after Havok (you'll see why its odd soon enough). Havok hits Ogre hard again with a tripple turnbuckle smash into the barrier, grabs a chair, gits Ogre with it, and Vorpal arrives. The two stare each other dow, and Havok smashes Vorpal with it. I'll spare you all the boring details, but Havok basicaly beats on them for a bit outside, using his multiple attack benefit from being a level 3 tweener, using forearms, mat slams, the railing, and a shoulder-carried camera. He the Chaos effects each, both very hard, and returns to the ring after laying out helsing with a forearm. Vorpal is really beat up, and since the match officialy started when Havok got to the ring, he just stood there, arms outstretched, as Vorpal was counted out. Havok in 50 seconds (it really takes them too long between counts), tho there was that brawl for about 2:04. [Victor Gold] WHOOO!!!! We're back...and now its time to take care of business!!! [name withheld]...you're in DREAMER's way, we got the belt and we are not going to give it up!! [HJD] Baby, can you feel it....That's right Johnny Dreamer is on a roll!!! Look at this Davis {Dreamer points to the WOW International Title}. This is what you want, to be the #1 contender.Nope, ain't gonna happen. The lights and glamour of Hollywood are back in tact and you had better believe...THE GOLD CORP is ready for some action!!! {during Jack Davis intro} [Louie] Ha Ha Ha, Baby! I'll handle this one, it might be a bit out of your league, chump. Well, look at this, people. You all saw what an awesome talent my brother is. And he had the presence of mind to duck out of the ring as the last competitor eliminated, thereby getting a free shot at the Number 1 contender's ranking, and the International belt. It couldn't have worked out better if I planned it that way! Ha Ha Ha, baby, it's gonna be like taking candy from a little baby. So without further adieu... From Flagstaff Arizona, weighing 224 lbs. Keep listening to me bro, and you'll be the World Champ in no time. And if he thinks he's beating you tonight, then he IS a dreamer! Ha Ha Ha, baby! {Johnny Dreamer vs. Jack Davis} Davis starts with a drop toehold, but gets cought in a hammerlock, and has some trouble escaping. John picks him up and bodyslams him, drops a hard elbow, but gets his eyes raked. Jack puts on a hard lever leglock, and Dreamer has some trouble escaping (he's already hurt from the run-in with the maffia), and hurts his leg.
