WOW Superstars (11/30/96)

30 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, this is short description because I don't want the first card after 3 weeks to be TOO late... Match 1: Spammer vs. Jamie Westbrook power/brawling Spammer starts off with a bearhug which Jamie's struggling makes worse before he can get out, and then Spammer IMPLANTS Jamie into the mat. He surgically removes the Duke and tosses him to the ropes, going to the opposite pair himself, for a shoulderblock. Whips Westbrook into the corner and follows him in with a corner clothesline, whips him to the ropes and backdrops him. He slaps on another bearhug for a similar duration, but Jamie goes low to take the initiative. However, he can't capitalize as Spammer once again makes an indentation in the ring using Jamie as the tool to do it (ie bodyslam hard). Westbrook again steals the momentum with a punch, whips Spammer to the ropes, and is shoulderblocked. Spammer hits a fallaway slam, whips the Duke to the ropes and hits a powerslam. A powerbomb later and Jamie seems to be out of it. Well, the Academy must be considering him for best actor as he hit a low blow on the big guy. Whips Spammer into the ropes, Spammer pass a couple of times, then uses his own 400+ pounds to shake the ring foundations as Jamie backdrops him. Jamie rakes his eyes on the rope, but stumbles forward and hits his head on the turnbuckle afterwords. Spammer is up first, and stomps Jamie pretty darned hard. A crucifix shoulderslam later and we have a 1..2..3 count and a winner in The Spammer. ------------------------------------------------------------------ (The scene: a steamy, rain-forestlike jungle setting, with various animals popping up, then disappearing back into the foliage ...a loud crashing noise can be heard, as the camera comes upon a clearing, where a large truck is parked ... two burly guys holding chainsaws, and dressed in t-shirts, jeans, and hardhats, are walking up to one of the trees ... strangely, they look sorta familiar, and not unlike a typical preliminary wrestler ...) [Thinner Guy] Hey, Bob ... you really think this clear-cutting is such a good idea? Especially behind the government's back and all ... [Pudgy Guy] I don't think we'll have much of a problem ... after all, who's gonna miss a few HUNDRED-THOUSAND trees, eh? (Bob elbows his pal, and the two share a hearty laugh ... a faint noise can be heard off in the distance ... [Noise] [Bob] tttttttttttttttttttttttttttttt Well, we'd best get to work ... iiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii help me fire up this chainsaw, OK? iiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii iiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii [TG] mmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmm OK, no problem ... say do you hear mmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmm that? bbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbbb eeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee [Bob] EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE Yeah ... don't sound like no animal. EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE Sounds like it's coming from ... EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE RRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR (both look up) RRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR RRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR [Both] RRRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! AIIIIIIIIIIIIGGHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!! {The pair's scream is cut short when somebody comes flying from above to nail the big guy with a double axhandle, knocking him senseless to the ground ... his buddy stumbles back, and bumps into a large man with short blond hair and a Fu Manchu -- who proceeds to German suplex him} [TJ] How's it feel, gentlemen? Torn from your sense of security, flung to the ground helplessly, your entire life shattered in an instant by one cold unfeeling soul? There are but two of you, wasted & miserable lives... think of the HUNDREDS of years of history you seek to obliterate within this forest -- and explain to me why Kyle and I shouldn't break those chainsaws over your heads ... {The flying nut hops up, howling} [KM] YEEEEEEEEEOOWWWW! MAN, is that ever a TRIP! Tony, you have GOT to try that sometime!!! (the guy on the ground moans, and Kyle steps over him to get to Tony's "victim") See, it's like this. We bought tickets to Pittsburgh, and our FRIENDLY neighborhood airline sends us to Peru instead. Now we COULD beat up our hard-working associates at said flyboy outlet ... OR we could beat the tar out of some no-account losers like YOU! [TJ] Patience, Kyle ... ignorance is to be corrected, not punished ... and these two aren't gonna be ignorant anymore, I think. C'mon ... we've got a plane to catch. [KM] START RUNNING NOW, WOW PUNKS! With a head start like THIS, you MIGHT get AWAY FROM US! THERE'LL BE HELL TO PAY, AND TRIPWIRE'S HEADED YOUR WAY!! {turns back to Tony as the camera fades} You think they got big trees like this in Pittsburgh? ------------------------------------------------------------------ Match 2: Tom Carr debut Tom opens up with a collar and elbow tie-up, and manages to thumb Gary Wilson in the eye. He puts on a reverse chinlock, and holds it for a bit before switching to a hamstring pull. He tosses Wilson through the ropes, into a post, and back into the ring. He inverse atomic drops Wilson before sliding outside, pulling him to the post, and slamming his leg into it. He then rolls back in and puts on a leglace for about half a minute. He then switches to the Carr Leglock (a Texas cloverleaf) and gets the submission. Please note: his finisher is a sleeper, not a cloverleaf. Anyways, he grabs the mic and... ------------------------------------------------------------------ [TC] For those of you who aren't quite sure who I am or what I'm doing here, allow me to explain: the name is Thomas Carr, though Tom is fine ... though if any of you punks come up to me outside this arena I'll slap you. I've held the GWC Heavyweight title, I've carried a couple partners through GWC Tag Title reigns, and I've had a title belt around my waist more longer than half of that dressing room has been alive. Hell, I even have two sons in the business ... damn ungrateful kids that they are, at least I can respect what they do in the ring. Most of the time when I turn on the TV to watch wrestling, I dunno if I'm watching wrestling or the circus. We have guys coming through the ring, some idiot rollerblading on the way to the ring, and a whole lot of people who like to run their mouth - heck, the only thing missing is the song and dance number, and they had that at the AWI Fan Poll Awards ... but very rarely do I see anyone actually /WRESTLING/. Now, I'm sure there are a lot of people who think I'm too old for the ring. Well, I've seen Straite in the ring, and to be fair I'll give him credit - he does ok. Now, if someone of Jerry's advanced years can still put together a reasonably fair match, then I'll have no problem. Pay attention, W.O.W, 'cause I'm here to show you that the name Carr has always been and will always be synonymous with wrestling... wrestling the way it should be! ------------------------------------------------------------------ Match 3: Blitz Brothers vs. Manticore and Wraith So the whole triangle comes down, Minotaur leaves the ring to play manager, the blitzes come down, do that whole step back line up and dive into the ring thing, then go and attack Manticore and Wraith. Carter (yeah, they're twins, but I'm not doing commentary so I'll label them correctly without having to guess anything) clotheslines Wraith, while Curtis has somewhat less luck, being shoved into the corner my Minotaur. Carter goes to the ropes, sending Wraith to the opposite ones and hits a forearm. He short-arm clotheslines Wraith, while Curtis dodges an Avalanche from Manticore and the big guy hits the buckle hard. Both Blitz brothers (or Stone brothers, either way) clothesline Manticore over the top rope, and Curtis goes to his corner finally getting the stuff settled. Carter whips Wraith into the ropes and hits a Lou Thez, but doesn't even get 1. He atomic drops him and tags in Curtis, and the two double short-arm clothesline Wraith, Carter hitting his with authority. Wraith, however, shoves Curtis to the corner with said authority, and hits a low blow to knock the wind out of an advancing Curtis. He slams him to the mat, goes for a tag, but Curtis pursues right into a DDT. Wraith covers but Curtis powers out by 3. Wraith tags out, and Manticore hits a running shoulderblock. Curtis avoids the splash and goes and hits a tag to his brother who forearms the immense Manticore. Manticore chokeslams the ex-football player, then ribcrushes him. Carter dodges his Manticore Pounce running vertical splash and tags out to Curtis, who amazingly manages to stun gun the 400+ pounder. He kneedrops the triangle member and puts on a side headlock. Manticore takes a bit of time escaping, but does escape and hits a fallaway slam. He goes for the tag, but is clotheslined from behind by Curtis. Manticore chokes the Blitz bro for 4, then tags out. Wraith slaps on an inverse leglock which takes Curtis quite a while to escape. Curtis hits a spinebuster, however, and makes the hot tag to Carter. Carter NAILS a running forearm, sends Wraith to the ropes, as he goes to the opposite set himself, and hits a BIG bulldog lariat. He elbowdrops the technical member of the triangle, and as he gets up, Wraith gets out... of the ring that is. Carter tries to take it to him with a plancha shoulderblock but Wraith dodges it sending Carter to the railing REALLY hurting his shoulder. Wraith, not trying too hard to be gentile with Carter's arm, rolls him back in and follows him up, slapping an armlock on that hurt arm. Carter manages to get out quickly enough, but he still takes a fistdrop to his shoulder, then gets put in a knee armlock. Carter, his shoulder swelling up to the size of a melon, water that is, manages to not submit to it while in the hold for over 3 minutes before he finally escapes. However, he is promptly DDTed and pinned. ------------------------------------------------------------------ {Camera cuts to a wiry-muscled Asian man wearing kelly-green lantern-pants (the slightly bell-bottomed trousers kung-fu artists wear), black armbands from wrist to elbow decorated with a winding Chinese dragon design, and a red headband. He stands in front of a graphic depicting a dragon's head facing the screen, breathing fire, with ornate gold lettering reading "Dragonfire Kien Lun"} [Kien Lun] {speaking with a noticeable Cantonese accent} Greetings to W-O-W fans everywhere! Kien Lun is here, to test his skills against any challenge I can find ... I may not be the strongest or the largest man in wrestling today, or even in this organization, but I hope to show the fans that skill an spirit can overcome any opponent. I am not one for long speeches, so I will let my performance speak for me; I am going to do my best to show all the great fans out there a little something they have never seen before in every contest I fight. I may not win every fight, but I will hold nothing back -- when you watch a match with Dragonfire, you can bet on action! ------------------------------------------------------------------ Match 3: Kien Lun debut match Kien opens up with a legsweep followed by an axe kick (basically putting foot over a prone opponent and bringing the heel down into him), picks up the Aerlialist, and hits him with a karate kick, karate punch, and roundhouse kick. Whips him into the corner, hits a handspring elbow and follows it with a moonsault (splash, not bodypress) for a 2 count. He hits a side kick, which he follows by a horseman's throw (picks up opponent, bounces off the ropes behind him, flips over him, grabbing his head at the height of his flip (coincidentally, he's upside down), and as he continues over so does his opponent... yes Fei Long does this in Super SF2). Hits a springboard moonsault (yeah, I know, usually called Asai, bear with me here) for another 2 count. He puts on a reverse toehold (aka lever leglock) for a while, to take a breather (which you as well need), but lets it go soon enough to pick him up, go behind and to his neck wrecker (ok, take a breather, this is a tough one... he stands behind, vaults up onto his opponents shoulders, spins around, falls down in front of his opponents grabbing his head for a DDT... got that?). He picks the Aerialist back up, twice since he fell down again the first time, climbs the rope, and hits his Dragonwing death drop (shooting star scissors piledriver... of course, that's about the only possible way to do it flying, but I digress...) and covers a pretty much destroyed Aerialist for the cover and the pin. BTW, if you're annoyed by all the weird moves and move names, don't blame me, its not my fault Bryant chose Kien Lun as a place to show off his knowledge of wrestling moves and other oddball moves... Anyways... ------------------------------------------------------------------ {fade to Mike Taylor, standing in front of a backdrop with his name and a stylized eagle in flight on it. Mike's got the TV title belt wrapped around his waist, and looks excited (surprise)} [MT] Oh, yesyesyes*yes*!! Fans, thanks to all of your support, I've got my *first* title belt!! This Television title already has a legacy for excitement and for the quality of people holding it, and I just hope that I can live up to that! This week, I have a title defense against "The Philly Phlash" Joey Igrozza. Joey, I already know that you're a *great* wrestler, and I know we're going to have one heck of a match! [MT] As for the guys I won this from: Prophet, man, I don't exactly know what you're all about, but if you ever want a title shot, you got one! You're a *great* competitor! {expression turns a little displeased} And as for "The Maimer", I don't know what your problem is, but if you want this title back, all you have to do is ask for a shot. I don't know *why* you have to go attacking people from behind and stuff like that... when wrestlers do that, they show they just don't have any sense of respect for this sport. You don't like losing, then you come and ask for another shot, don't hit me from behind!... But for now, the Eagle's flying high!! ------------------------------------------------------------------ Match 4: TV Title Mike Taylor vs. Joey Igrozza Joey opens up with a side headlock, which Taylor handily escapes but then gets legswept. Joey hits an elbowdrop, puts on a half-nelson which he turns into a full one, but Mike quickly escapes that as well. Joey hammerlocks Mike, which Mike has more trouble with but still manages to get out of quickly enough. Both go to the ropes, pass once, Mike leapfrogs the next time, and Joey bodypressed on try #3 for the 1..kickout. Again both to the ropes, Joey hits a HARD Lou Thez for the 1..2..3!! He got him!!! no he didn't!!! count Yeah, I know, whitespace is a cheap way to get suspense in, so sue me. Anyways, Joey DDTs Mike, which doesn't stop Mike from putting on a lever leglock first (aka reverse toehold). Joey quickly escapes, but is hit with a flying bodypress from the Eagle for a not even 1 count. before he kicks out. Both to the ropes and Mike hits a dropkick. Picks up the Philly Phlash and gets hiptossed. Joey in control misses a flying bodypress and bites muchos dust. Mike goes up top and NAILS a flying elbowdrop. He covers for nothing, picks up the challenger, and forearms him. Joey dodges a springboard dropkick, and puts on a knee armlock which lasts no time at all. He elbowdrops the champ, both go to the ropes, and Joey hits a bulldog lariat. Joey goes up top and hits a bodypress for the 1...2...powerout!!! Yeah, its even more cheap this time... shaddap!!! Taylor dropkicks Joey, goes to the apron, and hits a springboard dropkick. He picks Joey up, but Joey hits a small package for the 1..2..3!!! Its not cheap this time 8-). Joey gets really excited, handshake with Taylor thanks him for great match, gets emotional with thanking fans etc.. no, no references to God, his wife, or his mentor (thank god...) ------------------------------------------------------------------ {Open up to The Gym (tm) where Jerry Straite, Deric Alaffia James, and Kenneth "Kayo" Thompson tend to hang out. Jerry and Deric are studying a tape of Angelo Migilachio and the Maffia. Deric's World Tag Title belt lays on the floor in front of the TV. Kayo Thompson is off on the side, talking on a cordless phone, buffing his World Tag Title belt to a perfect shine.} [DAJ] Have to say, I'm pretty worried about this one. Their smallest guy is bigger than me or Kayo, and they have worked together before. All things considered, me and Kayo are a pretty recent team, less known throwing you into the mix. [JS] Yeah, I know. You 'n' me both got experience wrestling on our own, but neither of us have a lotta tag experience. 'Course, neither does Angelo. He's as much outta place as I am. [KKT] {Into the phone} Yeah, baby, I know I didn't get any of the pins, but it's still all good... I handled up on my business anyway. Plus, you know Deric's been hangin' around with Straite too long, so I gotta let him get the pins or else he'll think he's an old man... [DAJ] I agree... But I think the one we'll have to worry about is The Bruiser. Of all of the "big guys" here, he's one of the few that's kept his weight in competitive shape. Ya can't really do alot when you're 350, 400 pounds, yaknowhatI'msayin'... but Angelo's big and fairly quick, plus he has that "doctor's approved" tape wrist... [JS] Yeah, but that's easy taken care of. Break the arm at the elbow, no problem. Listen, Deric, I gotta level with you... I'm dyin' here. Doctors are tellin' me that I shouldn't get in the ring for a couple of weeks, and for once I think they're right. [DAJ] I was wondering when you were going to say something. You've been limping around ever since you stepped outta that ring. But that makes us down a man. [KKT] {Puts the towel down, and picks up a can of wax} Well, for a white boy, Straite's a-ight. I mean, he's Canadian -- he's a bit ass-backwards anyway... Awww... why it gotta be all that? How many other people you know have a WORLD title within a year a' starting wrestling... he's 7'4", 400 lbs... if he don't start getting titles early something's pretty damned wrong! [JS] Nah, nah, it's covered. See.... {looks toward the camera, then leans in and whispers something to Deric, to his obvious delight} We'll spring it on 'em right before the match. [DAJ] {smiling} Oh, MAN!! G, you are DE MAN!! After what he pulled.... oh, man, that's beautiful! [JS] {grinning} Yeah, ain't it, though? {Straite and Deric finally notice that Kayo's being less-than studious here...} [JS] Hey, Kenneth, you planning to join us here, or are you gonna come into this match blindfolded? [DAJ] Yeah, G! The honeys are gonna be there anytime... we have business here. [KKT] {Into phone} Hold on... I gotta deal with my two mothers again... {To Straite and Deric} Look, y'all... Straite handled his at D-N-D... didn't win, but he's in contention for another shot most likely... we won the World Tag Titles... and plus, how much train' do we really need for this? I mean, it's not like we have the most stellar three-man record anyway. [JS] Well, One, that's not somethin' to be proud of, and two, trust me, it's *all* gonna turn around 'fore long. [DAJ] Two words, Kenneth -- "Tilt-a-Wirl. Piledriver." [KKT] {sighs, then speaks into phone...} Hey, Jessica... I'll talk to ya later... I got work to do... [JS] {motions to the camera} Hey, you, out! We gotta stuff to take care of, donchaknow? {fade out} ------------------------------------------------------------------ Match #5: Jerry Straite, Kayo, Deric James vs. Maffia & Angelo Migalachio ------------------------------------------------------------------ {When Jerry, Kayo, and Deric are announced, the trio come out, except that Jerry's wearing street clothes, to the surprise of the Underworld group and the fans. The three of them get in the ring, and Jerry asks for the mic.} [JS] Gonna have to ask your patience for a sec, people. Don't worry, there'll be a match. {points to the opposing corner} And *Don* Antonio, keep your guys in check; Myers is looking for some spare change, and dropping a few fines on you probably wouldn't bother him none. [JS] The doctors took two looks at me after Devil's Night, and said "If you get in that ring in the next couple of weeks, you probably won't walk out." Now, I don't like listening to doctors. They are usually over concerned about stuff that don't matter. But maybe it's 'cause I'm getting older, I'm a little more worried about that kind of junk. Maybe it's 'cause I had to limp down here that I know, for once, they're right. [JS] So, I'm not wrestlin' tonight. I know you're disappointed, and a little worried about Kayo and Deric, but I'm not gonna let the boys here fight on their own. See, that'd be a bad idea, even against these three yutzes. So, I called up a guy who'd I'd been talking to, on and off, for a few weeks now. He said "sure, I'll help you kick some people around". So, ladies and gentlemen, please welcome to our loose little group of gentlemen here, a man who last week beat *three* men in under *four* minutes..... {Havok's music kicks up, and the fans who are paying attention go nuts, while the bad guys look a little concerned} [JS] "THE MACHINE" JAMES HHHHAAAAAAVVVVVVVVVVVOOOOOOOOKKKKKKKKK!!!!!!! {Yep, pop-opalooza for the man of chaos, as he joins the team to a round of handshakes.} [JS] James, glad to have you on the team, and now, Mr. Referee, *you* can start the match. {Jerry tosses the mic back out to the ring announcer, grabs a chair, and chases Lynch Xavier, who came down with the Underworld, away} ------------------------------------------------------------------ Match 5b: James Havok, Kayo, Deric James vs. Maffia&Angelo Migilachio Havok starts it out by whipping Vic to the corner, kicking him, shoving him through the ropes, and, true to form, goes to work on him with various illegal alien items. He hits him with a chair, HURLS him into the steps, and the impact of that collision seemed sufficient for him to toss Vic back into the ring. However, Vic is able to DDT James even with his likely bruised ribs and is able to tag out to Bull Maneli. Bull hits a punch combo, whips James to the buckle, and misses an avalanche. Havok forearms Bull, then moves onto Vic and Angelo before shoving bull into Vic knocking Fanelli off the apron. He tags in Deric James who, amazingly enough, manages to somehow position himself so the old school ending ends up in the middle of the ring, not the Underworld corner. However, it doesn't stop Angelo from BOLTING across the ring to break the pin before even the ref can get down to count it. However, Havok bolts in and elbows Angelo. He continues on with a kneelift, then tosses him out of the ring to the annoyance of Albert Simpson, while James gets taken down fireman's stile but dodges a splash. He tags in Kayo, who hits a super critical New Jack Funk for the pin on Bull. Erase all about Kien Lun, insert this: Match 4: Vorpal&Ogre vs. Aerialist and Lawrence Boggs Vorpal starts off with an irish-whip of Boggs into the ropes. He then avalanches him, and tosses him outside. Vorpal whips him with his chains a few times, rolls him back in, Ogre tags in Vorpal, Vorpal slapps on a headvice and Boggs quickaly submitts for a short but impressive win.
