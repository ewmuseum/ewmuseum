WOW — Weekly Broadcast

WOW Superstars (9/28/96)

28 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{GM note: For all you not in the WOW, this is coming out without commentary and without flashes because my modem died when I was 4 rounds from finishing the card, so I had deleted all the flashes already. I also need time to catch up (see the date this was scheduled for) and I injured my finger so am typing impared, so to minimise typ0s, this is without commentary} Match 1: Takashi Kimoshita vs. Dr. Sydney Pain w/ "High Rollin'" Louie Davis Takashi opens up with a hiptoss, and follows that with a running somersault fistdrop and flying chlothesline. However, Pain reverses an irish-whip and avalanches the Japaneese star. He powerchokes him until Albert Simpson gets to 3, then chokeslams him. He gives Takashi a reverse fallaway slam, but misses a splash. Takashi hits a running jumping somersault chlothesline, goes up top and hits a somersault splash for 2. Pain rakes his eyes and tosses him over the top rope, but Kimoshita grabs onto the top rope and pulls himself back in ala Steamboat/Shawn (the great name battle between BattleMasters and BODYSLAM!), and hits a springboard moonsault back brain kic before Pain turns around. However, he takes his time in picking him up, and gets chokeslammed for it. Takashi is tossed over the top rope sucessfully this time, and followed out by Pain who receives insturctions from Louie Davis. Pain then whips him into the rail near the entrance aisle, chokeslams him onto the concrete, powerbombs him onto the railing, sets a table adjacent and perpendicular to the apron, picks up Takashi, climbs up onto the apron with him, and delivers a Painbuster (brainbuster) through the table. He rolls back in at 8, and Kimoshita is counted-out. Match 2: "Bombtrack" Alex Young vs. Gary Wilson Rumor has it Bombtrack is concidering changing his nickname to something about being incoherant, but that's another story. Anyways, Bombtrack hits the ring, coming out from under it, and nails his with a running dropkick. He goes to the other rope, and hits Wilson with a running jumping clothesline. Tosses him outside, and rams his head in rapid succession into the post, railing, monitor, and apron. He rolls Wilson back in, press slam, and The Bomb for the pin. Match 3: Damien Omega vs. "Bedlam" Ben Wilder w/ "High Rollin'" Louie Davis Bedlam opens up with a bodyslam, but gets kicked in the knee by Damien. Damien then takes him down with a go-behind, and slaps on a leglack for about 20 seconds before Bedlam breaks. He then puts on a boston crab and gets the shocking early submission after 15 seconds of boston crab and about 1 minute of match. Match 4: Deric Alaffia James & Kenneth "Kayo" Thompson vs. The Aerialist and Gary Wilson Deric starts out with the world champ wanna-be Aerialist. He kneelifts the Aerialist and gives him a flying somersault bodypress as he rises. He gets two before the Aerialist kicks out and again knocks him down just as he rises with a jumping spin kick, and then whips him into the corner. The Aerialist tags out, and Wilson runs in only to be dropkicked. Deric DDTs Wilson and tags in Kayo. Thompson gives a inverted atomic drop to Gary Wilson and follows it up with his "New Jack Funk" punch. Then tags in Deric who finishes it off with a bridging double hamstring pull "Old School Ending". {GM Note: Bills just won. Steelers are now tied for #1 in the AFC!!!!!} Match 5: The Seekers vs. The Hellhounds Dane attacks early with a dropkick on Cavalier, and follows it with a hard running vertical splash which gets 2. Both rise and go to the ropes with Dane Tice hitting a lou thez press not even for 1. Dane tags in the Taskmaster and they hit a double suplex. Taskmaster picks up Cav but gets scisors piledriven. Cav tags in Paladin who hits a belly to belly suplex, and follows it up with its counterpart the belly to back suplex. He pasues for a second and gets press slammed. Taskmaster then powerbombs Pal, but Paladin manages to do his famous full-nelson suplex for 2. Whips Taskamaster to the ropes for a hard big boot kick, but Taskmaster manages to block the tag. He puts paladin in for a backbreaker rack which is short lived, but enough to allow Taskmaster to tag out. Dane flies in with a Lou Thez press, but doesn't evenget 1. Dane rakes the eyes and tags out, but Taskmaster falls prey to a spinebuster. He manages to grab the leg of Paladin to prevent a tag, but the DDT that nearly implants his head into the canvas kinda takes the will to prevent the tag away from Taskmaster. Cavalier comes in with a moonsault, but doesn't get more than 2. He picks up Taskmaster and gets chokeslammed. Taskmaster puts Cavalier up on his shoulders ala Animal, and tags in Dane, who goes up top and does a "Dane Bite" flying back mount victory roll for the 1, 2, 3. Match 6: "The Machine" James Havok vs. "The Bruiser" Angelo Migalachio Starts with a collar and elbow tie-up, and Havok shoves Angelo into the corner. He follows Angelo in, and gets punched by the taped fist champion. Angelo hits a brutal punch combo, but James retaliates with one of his own, and tosses Angelo out. He follows him outside, whips him to the post, rams his head into the rail, and tries to toss him into the steps, but Angelo ducks out in time and Havok's momentum carries him into the steps hard. Angelo rolls in and waits to see if Havok will be counted out. However, Havok rolls in at 8, and even kicks Angelo before the Bruiser can do anything. He gets up, and tries to whip Migalachio into a corner, but is reversed with authority. However, Havok manages to dodge an avalanche, hit a hard knee lift, a gutwrench suplex, DDT, and through the ropes toss. {GM note: This is how far I got last time} He goes out again, tosses Migalachio into the stairs, powerbombs him through the announcer's table, and rolls him back in. Havok rolls back in himself, and hits the "Chaos Effect" gutwrench powerbomb for the pin. {Gm note: You can see how frustrated I must have been, typing all that before the note and more (I made it more entertaining), and then to have that little left...}
