WOW Superstars (10/5/96)

5 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Dr. Sydney Pain is in his "office", with Ben Wilder seated on a couch behind him. Wilder has some flash cards in his hands, and is showing them to Wilder.} [Pain] {holding up a picture of a VCR} OK...VCR. It's a Sony. {Bedlam gets a big scowl on his face} You know, this is what they used to play Aladdin on every day in the "institution". [Pain] Easy, big guy, easy. Now here's the next one. {holding up a picture of a video camera} It's a Mitsubishi video camera. Kind of like the ones they use to tape Superstars on, but these aren't quite as cheap. {Bedlam gets an even angrier look on his face, grabs the flash card out of Pain's hand, and tears it into shreds. [Pain] OK, here's another. {holds up picture of a video game machine} It's a Nintendo. You know, one of those things you say is turning peoples brains into mush. Go ahead, Ben, smash it if you like. [Ben] All these devices are causing society to forget how to think and how to interact. Let me destroy them and save humanity from this onslaught before it is too late. [Pain] {holding up a picture of Kimoshita and Omega in action} OK, now note where all of this onslaught comes from. That's right, Japan. Now take a look at these guys, {shows him picture of Kimoshita and Omega} they're some of the ones responsible. Wilder rises, grabs the picture crumples it into a little ball and stomps on it a few times. [Pain] Good, good. But I think you've gotten a little too motivated now. Very soon, it's going to be time to stomp on these two for real. For all of the pain that Japanese technology has inflicted on Ben Wilder, is going to be felt by those two buffoons in the ring. They'll wish that they were home playing Nintendo when we get through with them [Wilder] We must destroy them in order to save them, for they do not know what it is that they're doing to our planet! {Wilder starts throwing furniture around} [Pain] Um,...yeah. OK, save it for the match. Easy. {he stands up as if to hit Bedlam, who backs down and cowers in the corner} {Pain smiles at the camera} Yes, someone's going to feel some PAIN! Sack-a-shit, I have yet to return the favor of being eliminated from the Rumble. And Omega, well you better say your prayers, 'cause when Bedlam's done with you, you're gonna wish that you had been in Hiroshima instead! ----------------------------------------------------------------------- Match 1: Dr. Sydney Pain and "Bedlam" Ben Wilder w. Louie Davis vs. Takashi Kimoshita and Damien Omega Longest match of the night folks. Anyways,Omega opens up with a big go-behind takedown of Pain and slaps on a leglace. Pain escapes semi-quickaly, and is hit with a kneelift and a Boston crab. He is in the crab for a good period of time, but rakes the eyes to escape before submitting. He then powerchokes Omega and chokeslams him after 3. Whips him into the corner, and hits an avalanche. He tags in Bedlam, who bodyslams the man who embarrassed him a week ago, but Omega kicks the back of his knee hard. He goes for the tag, but is blocked and powerchoked. Omega quickly escapes, and is as quickly thrown over the top rope. Wilder follows him out, finds a chair, and hits him with it. BTW, for reference, this is on the OTHER side of the ring (near Pain). Bedlam continues with a toss into the steps, and a head into the monitor which Omega no-sells. However, he doesn~t no-sell the chokeslam through the announcers table, to which Ripley comments: "OK, that~s it! Next week, we~re NOT at ringside!!!". Of course, he is nearly interrupted by Kimoshita streaking across the ring for a Plancha bodypress of Pain into the guard rail between Ripley and Taylor. Bedlam smashes a monitor over Kimoshita~s head, making him the last one standing, and he proceeds to roll himself and Omega back into the ring. Covers, hooking a leg, but Omega raises a shoulder after 1. Bedlam attempts a short arm clothesline, but its Omega with the clothesline as Kimoshita rises and begins to run back to the corner. However, Omega does notice Kimoshita is not there and goes for the tag, Bedlam attempt to sneak up behind him, but Omega sees him, and DDTs him. Still, Omega does not realize Takashi is not there, and goes for the tag. This time Bedlam connects with a neckbreaker. Bedlam hits a running fist drop as Kimoshita gets to the corner only seconds too late. However, as Bedlam picks up Omega, he is hit with a European piledriver, and Omega slaps on, for a very short period of time, a lever leglock. He tags in Takashi and Pain manages to stagger up. Takashi comes in with a BIG springboard Lou Thez press, and almost gets the 3, but Wilder manages to power out. Pain is in his corner now, and Kimoshita goes up top and misses a somersault splash with Ben Wilder rolling out of the way. Bedlam tags in Pain, who delivers a rib crusher to Kimoshita. Pain hits a HARD reverse fallaway slam, hooking a leg and getting to 1 before Kimoshita gets a shoulder up. Both are up and Kimoshita hits a running jumping forearm. He goes up top again, but, like his partner, Pain is able to dodge the flying somersault splash. Takashi regains the advantage with a BIG scissors piledriver, and covers. However, Louie is up on the apron to distract Albert Simpson, and Bedlam runs in, only to clothesline another rushing in Damien Omega, and stomp Takashi as Pain kicks out. Pain powerbombs Kimoshita, but as he goes for the tag, he is hit my a DEVASTATING Venom Blast hurricanara. Takashi covers, Bedlam rushes in again, and hits Pain with the running fist drop. Omega, who had entered, hits Bedlam with a AMAZING DDT, as Takashi hits a springboard somersault splash and hooks a leg for the 3 count. ----------------------------------------------------------------------- {Camera opens on a black screen with a few assorted crayons scattered over it..} [Voice Over Guy] The following announcement is paid for by the N.V.O. {Camera opens on Nick Vorpal's head with a yellowish glow illuminating his face...} [NV] Burn Burn, oh your gonna Burn! {camera pans back to see the full picture now. Nick is sitting on a log, wearing an NVO shirt, roasting a hot dog on a stick over an open camp fire in the woods...} [NV] Dr. H. was nice this time and let us get out for some fresh air tonight, YAY!!!!You know what? {he takes a bite of the hot dog, then puts the dog back in the fire} I would like to make some comments on some of the new "talent", here in the WOW. First off, I have the right to do this because I was an original in the WOW, just like Jerry was an original back in the aut 2! Alex Young....you have charisma, you've got p'nash, hey you're a crazy <BLEEP> that likes to hurt people. I like it!!!!! You got potential kid, and I hope to be working with ya in the future.But wait, what is this this little voice tells me? YOU USE YOUR POWERS FOR THE FORCES OF GOOD!!!!! {puts out his hand in the "Talk to the hand" sign}. Um.....the boxing-guy whose name escapes me right now.....you are stupid, you try to help Jerry when he's being beaten on!{sarcastically} Don't you know he can hold his own in a battle? HA!!!!! I have made a vow, that when i come back to the WOW, i will take care of business. Jerry, I am glad you want me re-instated. Please talk to Jim Myers and we can get things on. Jimmy H., you just suck, I'll have a talk with you later young man!! And Jacky the new world heavyweight champion, though you are my friend homeslice,you will all have to answer to the headvice, ain't that right Ogre? {Camera pans to Ogre sitting on a log chewing on his bone. He nods his head and goes back to chewing. Camera pans back to Nick.} [NV] Exactly! And if worse comes to worse, I'll take 'em all on at the same time if it comes down to that, and I am sure that Sonic will watch my back baby, and the big man Ogre, and the Overlords as well. When I come back, I will be a force to be reckoned with! {You see the hot dog Vorpal was roasting burst into flames and and Nicky makes a weird look on his face} [NV] Burn,Burn, Oh YES, YOU'RE ALL GONNA BURN, HA HA HA HA HA HA!!!!!!!!!!!! {looks off camera} NO, NO OGRE! NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! {Camera pans to ogre who has a mouth full of baked beans and a constipated look on his face, camera fades out, and a loud fart noise is heard in the darkness with vorpal screaming in the backround "there goes the fresh air, ya fat schlub!!!!" ...} [Voice Over Guy] The previous announcement has been paid for, by the N.V.O. ----------------------------------------------------------------------- Match 2: The Overlords vs. the Aerialist and Gary Wilson Well, the Overlords come in, and Bulwark starts off with a belly to belly suplex on the Aerialist, then drags him to the corner for his trademark 2nd rope vertical splash. He tags in Eric, who simply locks on the Cloverleaf and gets the submission. ----------------------------------------------------------------------- [Dan Holloway] Sorry, Sonic, but I told you so. For what it's worth, I thoroughly enjoyed that match. Anyway, on to other business. Angelo Migiachio. "The Bruiser". You're gonna find out soon enough what a couple of other people have already learned. I don't care who you are, who you know, or where you're from. You're just another jerk for me to pound on for a while in my eyes. Now, I'm sure you want what I've got around my waist, just realize that I ain't gonna give it up very easily. I fully expect any dirty, low down tactics that you can come up with, but you're going against me. I am the dirtiest, baddest, most hardcore you or anyone else will ever step into the ring with. Keep that in mind. After our match, you won't be able to forget it. ----------------------------------------------------------------------- Match 3: TV Title Defense Dan Holloway vs. Angelo Migalachio w/ Don Antonio Holloway opens this one up by hoisting up Migalachio and hitting him with a running powerslam. He wrist spikes the bruiser, and hits him with a INCREDIBLE short-arm clothesline (1 super crit roll and 1 crit roll if you were wondering)!!! However, Migalachio gets in a punch, punch combo, and punch flurry before his eyes are raked, and he is bodyslammed. The Maimer whips the Bruiser into the corner and after a long pause hits an avalanche. He legsweeps the boxer and then hits him with a running clothesline as he rises. Backbreaker, but Angelo manages to hit a hard belly to back suplex. He tapes up his fist and drops it, but to no avail, as he misses. However, as Dan picks him in, he does get in a hard punch combo. Yet that is not enough to prevent the whip to the corner, nor the accompanying triple turnbuckle smash. Holloway hits the bulldog, and a powerbomb. He hits his signature tilt-a-whirl backbreaker and gives the thumbs down signal for his finisher, a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. He connects with that too, and gets the pin. ----------------------------------------------------------------------- {The Spammer is seen in an old beat-up gym pumping an amazing amount of iron... he then stops pumping and looks grimly into the camera...} [Spammer] You know, there use to be a time when REAL men stepped into the squared circle. Real men who didn't attempt any pansy "finesse maneuvers" or running around the ring until their opponents got tired of watching them... These Real men lived up to the term "Might makes Right". Those were the great days when a match was mano-a-mano and strength vs. strength. None of this lousy, "Let's surprise the opponent with a really quick drop kick..." crap that we see today. You shouldn't need to surprise an opponent with an attack, that's for GIRLY-MEN! A REAL match should be nothing but POWER MANEUVERS... I challenge ANYONE to a POWER MATCH... Throw away those wimpy "agility" moves... throw away those girly "technical" moves... LET'S BRAWL! And may the REAL Man win! Or are you MAN enough? {Spammer continues his weight training...} ----------------------------------------------------------------------- Match 4: Spammer w/ The Dealer vs. Gary Wilson To make his point clear, Spammer shoulderblocks Wilson, clotheslines him, press slams him, then hits his crucifix shoulderslam for the pin. ----------------------------------------------------------------------- {screen shows Deric James and Kenneth Thompson watching their jobber match (taped) on the television. Deric's pointing out stuff and giving Kenneth a few tips} [DAJ] See, you probably should've tagged out right about then..... Hey, the camera crew's here. Hold on a sec, fellas; Kayo, you seen Jerry around? [KKT] Yeah, I think he's off pouting. You'd think he'd get over losing that belt by now, man.... it's not like it was his woman or anything... [DAJ] Take it easy on the man, he's been runnin' things in his last couple feds, so he isn't used to these kind of losses. It'll take him a few days. You wanna go get him and bring him in here? [KKT] Sure {heads off screen} [DAJ] Anyway, we asked you to stop by to talk about this 3-man match we got against Manticore, Lord Minotaur, and the Wraith..... [KKT] {off-screen} YO, STRAITE, GET YOUR SORRY BUTT UP HERE! [JS] {off-screen} AW, MAN.... [DAJ] {apparently ignoring the commotion} I felt it would be a good way to introduce ourselves, and these other new guys, to the fans here. Besides..... {Jerry and Kenneth join Deric on screen} [JS] {grumpily} Whattaya want, James? Can't you see I'm busy feeling sorry for myself? [DAJ] Yeah, and that's the problem. Come on, we got this match against the Triangle of Doom this week. [JS] "Triangle of Doom"??? They didn't really call themselves that, did they? Oh, man, days like this I actually *miss* Rick Daniels and the Hitman..... Hey, should we give ourselves a cheesy name? [KKT] "Trio of Beatdown....ers"? ... "Triumvirate of Ass-kickers"? ... "the Syndicate"? Nah, that's a wussy name... [DAJ] Nah, nah, guys. Jer, what'd you mean, you miss those guys? [JS] Well, see, in the last few months, WOW's had a *lot* of guys like these losers. Vorpal, Ogre, Pain, Ben Wilder, Enigma, and now these yahoos. I'm starting to feel like I'm the warden at the asylum, not champ of a wrestling fed. [KKT] *cough*cough*wasthechamp*cough*cough* [JS] Oh, yeah, *thanks* for reminding me. What do we know about these guys? [DAJ] Not a thing. Not that I'm stressin' that, since the three of us combine *all* the best of wrestling. I don't think we got a whole lot to worry about. Look at it like this, G -- we're about to strike a blow for sanity in WOW. [JS] Actually, yeah, that could be kind of fun, couldn't it? On the other hand, it's kind of hard to see.... me, poster child for the mentally stable. Only in wrestling.... [KKT] {chuckles} Which explains again why I went into /BOXING/... {scene fades out} ----------------------------------------------------------------------- Match 5: Jerry/Kayo/Deric vs. Triangle of Doom (Lord Minoaur/Manticore/Wraith) Well, we go through all of the Triangle's pre-match rituals, the only notable one for future reference is Wraith's fireball glove, which consists of inserting a pellet of some sort into a glove pointed into the air, which then shoots off a burst of flames. Minotaur requests Straite, who is happy to oblige, and we have our first two competitors. Minotaur starts with a bearhug, which looks like it hurts considering Minotaur is one of the strongest, if not the strongest, person in the fed. Jerry also manages to take over 20 seconds to escape, not exactly helping matters any. He is promptly hit with a low blow and brain buster to follow. Minotaur tries a DDT, but Jerry does get a small package in for 1. Straite then hits a headbutt, and whips Minotaur into the corner, but misses with a clothesline into the corner. Minotaur hits a full-nelson suplex, an AMAZING DDT, and a through the ropes toss. However, try as he might, he cannot distract Simpson, and Straite rolls back in at 4. Minotaur, quite frustrated by this, hits a gutwrench suplex, but is downed by a dropkick from Straite, who tags in Kenneth "Kayo" Thompson, who immediately hits his "New Jack Funk" Punch and covers for 2. He picks up Minotaur, hits a punch combo, whips him to the ropes, allows him to pass once before hitting a discus punch. He hooks a leg, but only gets 1. Kayo then tags in his good friend Deric Alaffia James, who DDTs Minotaur and hooks a leg for another count of 1. Both men up and go to the ropes, and James hits a sleeper. Minotaur is caught in it for about 1 15 seconds before he escapes. James whips Minotaur into the ropes, and hits a jumping roundhouse kick and covers for 1 once again. He picks up Minotaur and falls prey to a snap suplex. Minotaur tags in Manticore, who hits a hard low blow. He rib crushes James and hits a belly to belly suplex. However, James dodges a splash and tags in Kayo. Kayo hits a punch flurry with awesome force, and tags in Jerry, who runs in with a clothesline to down the staggering giant. He hooks a leg and gets a count of 1, though this draws in Wraith, which in turn draws in Kayo to prevent a pin save, though Kayo is caught with a neckbreaker. At this point, James and Lord Minotaur figure "why not?" and a wild brawl erupts. Minotaur tosses Jerry to the outside, and Manticore headbutts Deric James. Meanwhile, Kayo was floored Wraith with a hard punch. Kayo progresses to Minotaur, who a hard punch flurry also takes down, while Manticore splashes Deric James and Wraith rolls outside. Wraith gets another one of those pellets from his robe hanging on the ringpost, grabs his glove and fireballs Jerry. Minotaur bodyslams Kayo, while Deric Manages to get the upper hand in his battle with a jumping spin kick. However, Simpson now manages to regain control, as Wraith rolls a very weakened Jerry Straite in, and Manticore proceeds to hit his trademark "Manticore Pounce" running vertical splash. Kayo runs in after 1, but doesn't get there in time to prevent the third slap of the hand, declaring the Triangle the winners. After the match they find a camera and Minotaur rants about how great they are, and how Wraith and Manticore deserve a tag-title shot and how he should be in the international title tourney and he's cut off for... ----------------------------------------------------------------------- {Backstage with Larry Dawson, is Joey Igrozza} [LD] I'm here with Joey Igrozza, the "Philly Flash" who is going to be wrestling Sonic Soldier tonight in the first match of the International title tourney.Joey, how does it feel to have a shot the International title?? {Joey is full of energy and smiling. He just runs with it.} [JI] Actually, I was a bit surprised by this. I was planning on working on my tag wrestling for Devil's Night Dance, and then I heard that I was in the tourney. I figure that win at Wrestlefest catapulted me into the tourney; and believe me, I'm happy to have a shot at a WOW title. [LD] Yes, that was an impressive performance against Jamie Westbrook at Wrestlefest. You don't usually see young wrestlers get this kind of opportunity this soon. {slightly serious} [JI] I understand that and I hope to make the most of this opportunity and give the fans their money's worth. Back home in Philly, sports fans are tough. Talent is less important than working hard; if you're a blue-collar, lunch-pail kind of guy, they appreciate you more. [LD] How do you feel about drawing Sonic Soldier in the first round? He's a pretty tough opponent. [JI] EVERY wrestler in this thing is a tough opponent. I think that the triangle match at DND will be an awesome spectacle, no matter what the lineup is. You've got to respect Soldier's ability; he's got some pretty good wins under his belt. I aim not to be one of them. I'm glad that there aren't going to be any flagposts to get hit with at ringside; I hope that he'll wrestle a bit cleaner than my last opponent. (grins) Hopefully the fans will see a great match. [LD] Well, that's all the time that we have for now. Back to you, Andy. ----------------------------------------------------------------------- Match 6: Joey Igrozza vs. Sonic Soldier w/ Uncle Slick God that last line must have been cruel to anyone really missing the commentary. Anyways. Soldier with an awesome Lou Thez press, getting 2. He slaps on a chicken wing for all of about 3 seconds, and goes for a springboard somersault Lou Thez press, but Joey dodges it. However, Soldier manages to regain control with a hard backdrop. Joey slaps on a hard side headlock, which he holds for about 30 seconds, and then moves into a half-nelson, which doesn't work nearly as well. He is dropkicked by Sonic, and gets put into a BRUTAL Indian deathlock. You can almost hear Joey's leg crack from the sheer pressure of this. Sonic does recognize this, and, noticing the pain on Joey's face, decided that the leg is the way to go. Joey escapes after about 30 seonds, but as he rises, and puts weight on that leg, he collapses. He tries again, with more success, but Sonic clips that injured leg with a shoulderblock. He keeps working on the leg with a hamstring pull, and a kneebreaker drop, and at this point Matt Anderson, for the sake of Joey's leg, ends the match and awards it to Sonic Soldier. Finaly, some parting words from out comish saying that the Triangle gets their wish... Kayo/Deric vs. Seekers vs. Manticore/Wraite vs. Enigma for tag straps and Minotaur vs. Jack vs. tope two ranked losers in the International tourny for world at DND.
