WOW Superstars (6/8/96)

8 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The opening credits and stuff} [Andy Ripley] Hello wrestling fans, welcome to WOW SUPERSTARS!! Tonight we will see the first two rounds of the WOW tag-team tourniment as 8 top tag-teams go into randomly paired competition!! I'm your host, Andy Ripley and with me as always is Taylor Jones! [Taylor Jones] Yes, it is I! The one, the only, Taylor Jones. Wrestling genius extrodinare, olor comentator, and general all around knowledgeable guy. [AR] Unh hunh, sure. Anyways, tonight we will see vetrens Thunder and Lighting in action... [TJ] As well as the ever awesome Dr. Sydney Pain, with his new partner, and former patiend, "Bedlam" Ben Wilder.. [AR] And a slew of new tag-teams. First, let's heare some comments from them. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- {A dojo training center. Viper and Poison are in the ring fighting some no-name ninjas and practicing their skills as a tag team.) [Poison] {punching some guy in the face} you know Viper {kicking another guy}, this is a little too easy. [Viper] Well, Poison, {leg sweeping some ninja} their isn't too much skill in the WOW, so why should we even practice on skilled opponents. [Poison] Viper, you're right(scissor piledrives a jobber to the mat), we should just be given the tag belts. [Viper] Yes, I agree totally. We are the best skilled atheletes in the sport and we'll prove that to all of the WOW Superstars! {poison goes up to the top rope, Viper takes a jobber and oriental power bombs him onto the man who was knocked out by the scissors piledriver. then poison splashes on top of the 2 men. the camera fades out after viper raises his sword in victory and poison spews mist in to the air} ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- {The team called Enigma appears. Clock is in the backround, Jigsaw is up front. Through out Jigsaw's speach, Clock is quietly repeating the words "tick-tock-tick-tock....." over and over again} [Jigsaw] Piece by piece, moment by moment, answer by answer, we come closer. Let's just see what the WOW is made out of, Clock. Let's see if they can stand up to us. Because, Clock, their time is running(pause, smiles coldly)....out. [Clock] ...tick-tock, Bells, bells, bells. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- {Setting is completely black, erie howling echoing back and forth...The Hellhounds step in but since it's pitch-black all you can see are the neon-red-orange flames dancing around. Suddenly, lights flip on. The huge, enormous Taskmaster is standing behind with his arms folded across his chest, kinda like an enforcer. Dane Tice is almost dwarfed by the piller behind him but is still pretty buffed up and wiry looking} [Dane] {howls} The HELLHOUNDS ARE HERE!! And all you tag teams better watch out! Because we're going to go at you as if you were dripping raw steak... So STEP BACK and get outta our way before we roll through you! Because a piece of HELL is comin' your way... {lights fade...complete silence} ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- {the camera pans out into what looks like a crumbeling abandoned city. an old, white man with a long white beard and white cain is sitting in a throne among some rubble. on both sides of him stands a knight clad in silver armor each with a shield. one holds a sword, the other a mace} [man] Superstars of the WOW, the tag tournament begins now. You will have to face the Seekers. The living embodiment of courage, honor and justice. Valienty you will fight, but I'm sorry to say to you, you shall all fail. [Knight 1] Master... [man] I'm sorry, my son. I should not brag about your incredable talents. [Knight 2] You are still wise beyond your years, Avatar [Avatar] These are Paladin, and Cavalier. To all of the tag teams in the WOW. Remember these faces. They'll be the last that you see. But at leats you can die an honorable death knowing that you were defeated by the best two tag team wrestlers ever... ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- {Cut to what looks like a home video. Louie Davis and Dr. Sydney Pain are getting into a car, Pain just barely being able to squeeze in. Davis is at the wheel, as they pull away from a parking lot. Signs indicate they are headed towards the I-94 to Lansing, Michigan} [Louie] Ha Ha Ha, alright, baby, soon I'll have a REAL tag team in my stable. What did you say that guy's name is. [Pain] Ben Wilder...but we just called him Bedlam down at the Facility. I tell ya man, he's a real case. Trying to save the world from technology or something like that. You should see some of the stuff he writes, man. He's outta control! [Louie] Great! I like my guys to have an edge to them. What do you say our odds are of pulling this off. [Pain] As long as you do the talking, at first, we'll get him out. Then, I'll have to step in and give him some...special treatment! [Louie] I don't care what we gotta do, I just want a kick ass team. Not like those Lollapalooza rejects Social Deviance. [Pain] Oh, this guy's a winner for sure, if he does whatever I tell him to do, and he WILL, you'll have every title in WOW in your stable. [Louie] If this guy's half as tough and half as crazy as you say he is, I'll be callin' my bookie and putting all my cash on you! The boys in Vegas are gonna [bleep] their pants when I rake in that championship gold! Ha ha ha, baby! {Both laugh as the video is turned off. It resumes an instant later} [Pain] OK, we're here. Remember, your name is Dr. Alfred Sheimer. Here's the transfer papers I got from the Facility. Just sign it in these three places, the way I showed you, and you'll be in the clear. I gotta stay back, if they see me, it's blown. {The car stops and Louie gets out and walks slowly into a large white building. A sign by the door reads "Michigan State Mental Health Centre - Doctors' Entrance" Louie walks up to an office and knocks on the door} [Voice] Bell's office, what can I do for you? [Louie] I'm Doctor Scheimer, I'm here to transfer a Benjamin Wilder. [Voice] Ben Wilder? What the...? Step inside for a sec. {Louie enters the door, and the camera shuts off. The camera then shows him coming out, with signed and stamped papers in hand. Dr. Bell, the man in the office, is with him. They are walking through a portcullis, into an area where the mental patients watch television. There's a ruckus going on, as VERY tall and big man is standing in front of the television, and giving it a flurry of punches} [Bell] OK, I still can't understand why any doctor in his right mind would want to transfer someone like Wilder into a private care facility? I mean, just look at that! [Louie] Well, we feel we can provide the care he needs to function as an almost normal member of society. Oh, my god! Thats him? [Bell] That's Wilder all right. Best of luck to you. {Wilder continues punching the television set, the screen has been smashed into shards, and his hands are dripping with blood. He mutters, as if he were alone...} [Wilder] Destroyer of the family structure which we hold so dear...we become detatched...must stop...every new invention leaving behind a trail of social problems...television turning our minds to mush...a subtle form of manipulation and control [Bell] {as if he were speaking to a child} OK, Ben, this man is Doctor Scheimer. He's going to be taking you to a new home. Would you like that, Ben? [Wilder] {ignoring Bell} Making us dependent on it, we can't survive without our new machines!.. {Bell calmly grabs Wilder's arms, and calls over some attendants who quickly swarm in, bringing out a straitjacket which they put on Wilder. He is led back out to the door by Davis and Bell, and then Davis takes him out to the car. Suddenly, Dr. Pain opens the car door, and Wilder's face goes white. He whimpers a bit, as Pain grabs him} [Pain] OK, Bedlam, you're going to come and live with us. [Wilder] NO! NO! {He lashes out towards Pain with a kick, and tries to bite him, but Pain is too quick, and smacks Wilder with the back of his hand. In a last bout of rage, Wilder lets a kick go towards the camera, which can be seen flying through the air, before going THUD! on the ground} ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- {A whole bunch of ticking and clicking startss} [RA] The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, at a total combined weight of 580 lbs., both from Kingston, New York, CLOCK, JIGSAW, COLLECTIVELY THEY ARE ENIGMA!!! {Clock follows behind Jigsaw as the crowd gets really freaked out and annoyed by the clicking and ticking. "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC is relief enough for a pop, never mind the fact who's music it is...} [RA] And their opponents, led to the ring by Victor Gold, at a total combined weight of 524 pounds, FLEX THUNDER, RICKY LIGHTNING, THE TEAM OF THUNDER AND LIGHTNING!!! {Mega pop as the two go to the ring. They enter, and we go to commentary} [AR] Bell rings and Jigsaw and Ricky Lightning jockey for psoition. Jigsaw slaps on an armlock AND NEARLY RIPS OF RICKY'S ARM!!! Ricky seems to be in excrutiating pain!! [TJ] Yup, looks like he hurt his arm. If Enigma keeps doing this to people, they may become my favorite team! [AR] Ricky manages to escape, but right into an arm wringer! Again Lightning struggles with the hold, but he manages to escape. Jigsaw makes the tag to Clock, who irish-whips Lighting into the corner RIGHT ONTO THAT ARM!! [TJ] These guys are VICIOUS... I LOVE IT!! [AR] You would. Clock with a running shoulderblock knocking down Ricky, but Rick gains controll with a drop toehold. Lighting gets up, AND MAKES THE TAG TO THUNDER!! [TJ] So, a bumb arm tags in a bumb brain. [AR] *sigh* Flex runs in and PRESS SLAMS Clock. Clock seised the initative with a kick, however, AND FLEX HURT HIS KNEE! [TJ] Two injuries??? You guys are too generous. Maybe you could do Vic over there, and if Baby Dreamer shows up... [AR] Oh please, will you stop? Clock makes the tag to Jigsaw, who applies a toehold to the already injured leg of Flex Thunder. Flex has alot of trouble escaping, but after around 25 seconds manages to. Both men up, and Flex with a headbutt. Flex bodyslams Jigsaw, picks him up, both to the ropes, CHLOTHESLINE!! [TJ] So no-brians managed a few lucky moves, so what? Doesn't prove a thing except Engima is pre-occupied with trying to figure out how to best hurt his leg. [AR] Flex MISSES with a headbutt drop, but manages an armdrag takedown. Both men up, and Jigsaw with a karate kick to Flex's bumb leg, and he wraps it in a lever leglock! Flex quickaly out though, and hits Jigsaw, this time, with a press slam. [TJ] Yeah, but you can see that his knee is hurting him. I think Enigma will become the official hospitalisers of the WOW. [AR] Jigsaw with ANOTHER karate kick to the leg, and goes for the tag BUT IS BLOCKED!! Flex managed to grab his leg and hold him back! Flex gets up, whips Jigsaw to the ropes... POWERSLAM!! Flex gets up, and now HE makes the tag. [TJ] I thought we got rid of Dreggs and the Dreggs impersonators. [AR] Lighting with a dropkick. To the ropes, SENTON!! Picks up Jigsaw, and gets a karate kick to his bad arm for his efforts! Jigsaw going for the tag, but AGAIN its blocked!! Jigsaw slaps on a wakigatme armbar, releases it, and makes the tag! [TJ] Tick tock tick tock... [AR] What are you doing? [TJ] Counting the seconds left that Ricky Lightning will have a left arm. [AR] Clock extends Lightning's arm... SPLASH ON THE ARM by the 310 pounder! He repeatedly stomps the arm, and then slaps on a chokehold. Randolph finaly notices it and Clock releases the hold on 4. Clock goes for the tag, but Lightning blocks it!! [TJ] Dammit, why are there so many Dreggs clones? [AR] Because he was a good ta-team wrestler? [TJ] Shut up and broadcast. [AR] Lightning with a drop toehold AND TAGS IN FLEX!! Flex with a running chlohtesline, to the top... waits... THUNDERCLAP!! NO!! Jigsaw dropkciks him in mif air. Goes for the tag, BLOCKED AGAIN! PRESS SLAM!! Thunder again going up top.. THUNDERCLAP FLYING SHOULDER CONNECTS!! [TJ] Did you see how much they cheated? [AR] What are you talking about? [TJ] They distracted the referee so Jigsaw couldn't tag out so many times! [AR] I didn't see him tag. [TJ] I did! [AR] Cover.. 1.. 2... 3!!! [RA] Here are your winners, THUNDER AND LIGHTNING!! {"Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays but is interrupted as Clock runs into the ring. Thunder forearms him and Ricky begins to enter.} [TJ] YES!! I knew they'd be the catalysts of injury! [AR] This is disgraceful! Jigsaw clips out Flex's knee with a shoulderblocks as Lightning dropkicks Clock! OH NO!! CLOCK CHOKSELAMS LIGHTNING AND RICKY IS OUT COLD!!! Jigsaw slaps on a EXCRUTIATING SCORPION DEATHLOCK!!! [TJ] {sings} Good bye right leg, good bye right leg [AR] Will you be serious??/ Flex in excrutiating pain, BUT HERE COMES DREAMER!!! Enigma scatters, but the damage is done.. how will these two wrestle later tonight??? [TJ] HAHA!!! A bye! [AR] Well, the medics are coming to take them away... [TJ] They're coming to take them away, haha! [AR] This is a tragety. Hopefully, James Myers can solve this for us later tonight. But first, this comercial break. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- You know, that comercial where Jay hurt his ankle so won't fence tomorrow morning and is typing with his eyes closed he's so tired?
