WOW — Weekly Broadcast

WOW Superstars (10/18/96)

18 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GM notes: Ok, here's the deal. I thought I could catch up, I couldn't. DND is now coming out Nov. 2nd, and the card scheduled for last Saturday coming out this Saturday (hopefully... I will have to have everything run by then as I'll be fencing all day prolly). Now, I'm 1 name/name and 1 jobber short, so here's the deal. From now on, if I don't have a full card 1 week ahead of time (2 after DND), I will insert matches. My policy on the required playing has been only enforced thus far on supercards. It will now be enforced on Supercards and on matches I have to select. Though, IC-wise this doesn't make sense, OOC-wise it does since having to arrange matches is not my job, and it posses me off when my players are too lazy to send email to me and someone saying so-and-so challenges so-and-so. However, I will not be doing this on Supercards. Instead, it will keep being pushed back until I have a full card. Speaking of which, DND as it is looks like: Jack Davis vs. Jerry Straite vs. Minotaur Enigma vs. Kayo&Deric (I was about to write dayo&keric...) vs. Seekers Sonic Soldier vs. Winner Haley/Straite vs. Winner Vengeance/Acid Vengeance vs. Alex Young vs. Devon Glenn vs. Paragon Some sort of TV title match hopefully Dan Lea vs. James Havok vs. FWA rep Hellhounds vs. AWI rep vs. FWA rep Seeing as the last two are not likely to occur since Eric isn't likely to get the FWA up, running, and organized in less than a week, I need 2 more matches to fill this up. I also need a jobber match for next week or it gets pushed back. BTW, this is the last week w/out commentary. As for next week's match... you'll see 8-) Now, to the card Match #1: Mike "The Eagle" Taylor vs. Dr. Sydney Pain Taylor, who was introduced first, starts this one off before the bell with a plancha dropkick onto Pain, who lands on the concrete entranceway. Taylor tries a springboard bodypress off the railing onto pain, but is caught and hit with a fallaway slam. Pain picks up the eagle, and is hiptossed, but Pain manages to catch him again, this time in an asai moonsault. Pain runs to the post, sandwiching Taylor between it and himself, goes for a powerbomb, but takes a little to long and is backdropped. Taylor hits a DEVASTATING springboard dropkick to the outside, rolls Pain back in and rolls back in himself. Taylor continues with a neckbreaker, climbs up top, extends both his arms toward the sky in his signal for his finisher, a somersault fist drop called The Talon, and hits it for the 2 count. Did I get you? DOH! Anyways, Taylor dropkicks a rising Pain, and hits a running elbowdrop, but Pain rakes the eyes to regain control. He powerchokes the Eagle until Randolph gets to 4, then chokeslams him down. He sets for the Painbuster, but Talor hits a HARD scissors piledriver and covers for the pin. Match #2: Angelo Migalachio vs. The Aerialist Punch, punch combo, punch flurry, snap suplex, vertical suplex, taped fist, taped fist combo, taped fist flurry, taped fist drop, irish-whip to corner, taped fist corner piston punch to 10 punches, tilt-a-whirl tombstone pin. ------------------------------------------------------------------------- {Deric James, Kayo Thompson, and Jerry Straite come into the door of their shared gym... well, more specifically, Thompson bursts through the door stomping around in anger, Deric James comes behind in, shaking his head, and Jerry Straite follows, looking kind of morose. Thompson is blathering on as the door bursts open...} [KKT] ..io of MONSTERS!!! WE GOT BEAT BY A BUNCH OF GUYS THAT THINK THEY'RE MONSTERS!!! [DAJ] Kayo... you're over-reacting. Sometimes you have off days... sometimes the other guy is just, no pun intended, straight up better than... [KKT] IT'LL BE A COLD DAY IN HELL BEFORE SOMEBODY CALLING HIMSELF A MAN-WHORE... [DAJ] "Manticore"... [KKT] WHATEVER!!! THE THOUGHT OF THAT'S EMBARRASSING! [DAJ] Actually, until you lose to a white rapper calling himself "The Jammer", I don't think we can compare... [KKT] How the hell am I supposed to go to a club after this? I can imagine it now... "Hey baby... yeah, I was on TV... I was the one that got beat by the guy that makes horns out of his hair before he pins you... yeah, baby, I'd love to go home with you tonight..." THAT AIN'T HAPPENIN' DERIC!!! [DAJ] Actually, if you're starting out going "Hey baby", I can't see where you'd get a lot of play anyway... [KKT] {Points his finger at Deric... trembling in anger...} You... You... You are /NOT/ helping here, man. [DAJ] That's because you're taking this too hard, man. Tell 'im Jerry... this is a temporary setback at worst... [JS] {looking like he's a little better, and a sarcastic smirk on his face} Hell, yeah, you could've actually been the one pinned. Imagine how embarrasing *that* could've been. {looks down again, mutters} If this keeps up, maybe I *should* retire. [DAJ] Nah, nah, G., none of that. Look, we have more to worry about... Jerry, you've got the International title tourney, and we have to start getting ready for the Overlords and the International tag tourney... [JS] {grins again} Oh, yeah, I haven't told ya 'bout that yet, have I? I've got a jolly surprise for Myers 'n' all the rest of them. [DAJ] {suspiciously} You're not supposed to be keeping secrets from me, man. [JS] Don't worry, me boyo, I'll tell you *all* about it. And trust me, I *will* walk out of Devil's Night with a title. [DAJ] So, that settles it... you're gonna get your title, whatever you want, and we're gonna get ready for the Overlords and Devil's Night Dance... It's gonna be on, and we're gonna run things from here on in... {Group break, like in football... as we fade out...} [KKT] If I don't get any play at Magic City, I'm comin' back and whippin' both y'all's asses... [JS] Yeah, like you had a chance anyway..... *YIPE*!! HEY, WATCH IT!! ------------------------------------------------------------------------- Match #3: Deric James and Kayo Thompson vs. The Overlords Deric and Bulwark start out in the ring. The circle for a second, Deric ducking in to try to grab a leg, but, instead, Bulwark grabs him for a belly to belly suplex. Bullwark bounces off the ropes and Deric /BARELY/ dodges a splash. He grabs the downed giants legs, flips over into his "Old School Ending" bridging double hamstring pull, and scores a pinfall in 29 seconds. Match #4 Paragon vs. Gary Wilson Paragon hits Gary Wilson with a full nelson suplex, headbutt, vertical suplex, underhook suplex and puts on the Boston Crab. Wilson quickly submits, but Paragon holds the crab for a full half minute longer, all the while yelling stuff about Vengeance, who, as the camera pans out to him in the crowd, looks more and more irritated. Finally, Paragon releases the hold and returns to the locker room. Match #5: "Bombtrack" Alex Young vs. Devon Glenn Glenn introed first, Bombtrack introed, again he's not coming from the lockers, camera pans to him repelling off the roof. He stops about 15 feet above the ring and begins to unhook himself. Glenn sees him, but stupidly enough stands right below him. Young drops down, and his hit by a dropkick from a not-so-stupid-seeming-now Devon Glenn. He punches Bombtrack, but is hit with a jumping shoulderblock. Young bounces off the ropes, but is tossed through the opposite set by Devon Glenn. Devon catches his breath for a moment, then hits a springboard headbutt drop to the outside. He grabs a chair and nails Bombtrack with it, but Young elbows his way out of a DDT onto said chair, grabs it, and hits Devon with it in return. He grabs a monitor, and smashes Devon with it (actually, it was more like Bombtrack smashing thin air and Devon stupidly walking into said thin air, but I digress). He whips him with a cable, and both roll back in. He press slams Devon, goes for a punch combo, but Devon dodges them and "inadvertently" collides with Referee Albert Simpson. At this point, Andy Ripley is handed a memo from James Myers which he reads... [AR] ...Anyone using any of the TV equipment as a weapon ever again in a WOW sanctioned event will be subject to a severe fine. This is expensive stuff, not to mention the possible loss of satellite feed we could get... ... SO STOP IT!!! Anyways, Glenn rakes the eyes of Bombtrack, goes and gets the above mentioned chair, and hits a flying chairshot. Devon goes to the opposite corner, and hits a flying somersault chairshot preformed ala the atomic Arabian face-buster. He tosses the chair outside as Simpson begins to stir, and DDTs the Rage Against the Machine fanatic. He goes up top again for a flying fist drop, sets Alex up on the 2nd rope outside, but Alex dodges the super back brain kick, sending Devon outside instead. Alex climbs up one rope, and hits a top-rope plancha bodypress onto Devon. He rolls Devon back in, goes back in himself, and gets punched for his efforts. Devon goes for a scissors piledriver, but Alex grabs him in midair and turns it into a BIG fallaway slam. He whips Devon to the ropes and hits "The Bomb" powerslam for 2 before Devon can raise a shoulder. He picks up the madman and is scisorspiledriven. Devon sets Bombtrack on the 2nd rope again and hits the super back brain kick. He then hits a hard dropkick through the ropes, rolls Alex back in, and follows suit with himself. He picks up Young and hits the fisherman buster for 1...2...Paragon breaking the pin with a flying fistdrop HARD! (you know, those guys at SNL are friggin' geniuses. The only time I would have laughed hysterically at that Women's International League of Kick Boxing skit was at 1:00 am, but I digress). The bell rings signifying Devon's win via DQ, as Paragon, seeing Devon kinda laid out, goes to work on Bombtrack with a front-face suplex. Vengeance, still fuming from Paragon's match, runs in from the crowd, prompting Paragon to leave and Devon to clothesline him over the top rope. Devon hits a springboard plancha bodypress onto Vengeance, but before he can do anything else, Bombtrack hits a critical springboard plancha bodypress on Devon. Devon gets up and leaves, and the whole mess is sorted out. Match #6: Jerry Straite vs. Dean "The Comet" Haley Dean is introed, Jerry is introed, he comes out, and... {Jerry's announced. Once both men are in the ring, Jerry stops the ring announcer and gets the mike.} [JS] HOW YOU DOING, EH? {crowd pops again.} Dean, can I have a few minutes here? Thanks, man, you won't regret it. I wanna do an impromptu Straite Talk, right here, just for the hell of it! That all right with all of you? {points to the announcer's booth} Quiet, Jones, you don't have a vote. {some more cheers, a few laughs} You all saw, or at least heard about, my intensely humiliating loss to 'The Man' Jack Davis, I'm sure. Now, I'm not about to offer excuses for that.... except that Jack had three guys at ringside, and I only had two watching my back. And, by the way, might as well do this in public. Thank you, to Deric James and Kayo Thompson for the hand. Those guys, and guys like the one standing in the corner, tapping his foot impatiently {points to Haley}, are what make this sport great! Right? {cheercheercheer, Jerry's getting cheesy, I think} Enough of the brown-nosing. Anyway, a week after the match, I drop by Myers' office to sign the contract for the return match at Devil's Night Dance. I sign it, and then notice it's *not* for the title match, but for *this* match. So, I went and had a quite chat with Jimmy Myers..... {interrupted by a snorting sound from the ref} What's your problem, man? [Ref] Actually, that's *not* the story I heard. [JS] Fine. I kicked the door down and screamed at him for five minutes. You happy, now? {shoots a look at the ref} Good. Anyway, Jimmy tells me that the top six ranked wrestlers, minus the ALLIANCE title contender, were to wrestle for a shot at the vacant Intercontinental belt. I tell him I don't *want* the IC belt, I wanted the Davis' heads on poles. He tells me that it wasn't his concern, and that this match was mandated. For some reason, I was a *wee* bit upset with that answer. He's telling me, I *can't* wrestle for the World title. CAN'T! CANNOT! Whata you all think of that? {boobooboo} Now, I *like* Myers, and all that, but this is the *second* time he's gone and ruined my plans. This could get irritating. Anyway, what we're left with is a World title match..... with *no* legitimate challengers, since all the big guys are off playing with the IC and ALLIANCE belts. So, here's what I propose to do about the situation. {turns to Haley} Dean, mi amigo, hope you do well in the IC title match, 'cause I'm not gonna wrestle you. {few gasps as Straite shakes the startled Haley's hand} If you wanna do this match later, that'll be cool, but it'll have to be later, *after* I get my belt back. I got the contract in the back, waiting to be signed and shipped off to Myers, and if he wants to tell me I *can't* do that, he'd better tell me from the other end of a *very* long telephone line. 'Cause I'm not going to put up with this for too much longer. Ladies and gentlemen, apologies for taking what would've been the best match of the night, and turning it into a gabfest, but I got things to do, and *this* match isn't one of them. Mr. Referee, start counting, cause I'm OUTTA HERE! Later, all! {flips the mike back out to the ring announcer as he hops down to the floor. Some boos (from smarts who wanted to see a great match) some cheers (from marks who want Jerry to get the world title). As Jerry is leaving, he is given a package from someone. Jerry, with camera peering over his shoulder, opens it to reveal a NVO t-shirt and a poem, scribbled in crayon, reading: Roses r red Viluts r blu Soon i wil be coming After U! Luv Nick And we fade into Myers' office} [JM] Well, Jerry, I should have expected as much, I suppose. However, there is little I can do as that isn't specified as illegal and you could have thrown the match anyways. However, I can do the following. First, that is never, ever, ever, ever, ever, ever allowed again. Period. Second, Jerry, since some of the fans didn't seem too happy about it, and I'm sure Dean would like the chance to score a major upset, you get to wrestle that match next week. {he pauses for a second, scribbles something down on a note pad, and holds it up to the camera, reading: Luv James}
