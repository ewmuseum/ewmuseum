And on that note, lets get to the ring. {Death March in G plays to the fans delight in booing} [RA] The following contest, scheduled for one fall, is a preliminary match in the WOW International Tag-Team Tournament! Introducing first, at a total combined weight of 890 pounds, from Parts Unknown, Bulwark, Eric the Red, THE OVERLORDS!! {They come down to the ring, Bulwarks yells at the fans a bit, and enter it as the music changes to "To Dream the Impossible Dream" from Man of La Mancha} [RA] And their opponents, led to the ring by their manager, Avatar, at a total combined weight of 544 pounds, from the Crystal City of Avalon, Cavalier, Paladin, THE SEEKERS!!! {The Seekers come down to the ring in breastplates, helmets, and wielding a broadsword and battle-axe respectively. Bulwark whips the Seeker to the corner, but Cavalier dodges an Avalanche as Bulwark hits HARD! [TJ] You see, if he sticks to tossing the little guy around and cheating, he can't lose! If he tries to fly his way to victory, he... well, against the Seekers he still can't lose, but it'd take longer to win. [AR] Bulwark slow to capitalize, and is caught with a scissors piledriver! Bulwark now makes the tag to Eric, Eric slowly picking up Paladin, who darts behind for a HUGE bridging full nelson suplex for.. 1... And that's it! [TJ] You see, the move they used to win all those matches way back when has little or no effect on Eric. The Seekers lucked out last week, but the curse of the titles lives on! [TJ] Yup, Eric can do Paladin's move better than Paladin can. [AR] Eric slaps on a reverse toehold for a very short period, then goes to an inverse leglock for a bit longer, Paladin breaks out, POPS UP AND HITS A VERTICAL SUPLEX! Going for the tag, but Eric clips his knee. Eric tags out now, picks up Paladin.. belly to back suplex, BUT BULWARK TRIPS AND MISSES THE LEGDROP! Both down now, ref counting...4...5...6...7.. And Paladin gets up, and makes the tag! [TJ] In other words, he's so afraid of Bulwark he has to get out to get away. [AR] Cavalier up top... HITS THE MOONSAULT but doesn't cover. Cavalier goes up top again, tags in Paladin who picks up Bulwark.. GERMAN SUPLEX, which Cavalier quickly follows with a flying guillotine drop... [TJ] Otherwise known as a leg drop by those who don't feel like making their moves sound as amazing as they are... then again, in this case, that move should have been called the pile of shit... [AR] Simpson counting.. 1... Eric runs in.. 2.. BUT CAVALIER SCISSORS PILEDRIVES HIM!! 3!!! [RA] Here are your winners, THE SEEKERS!!! [AR] So the Seekers are going to be at BattleZone... so much for your curse, eh Taylor? [TJ] Stick to play-by-play. [AR] Anyways, up next is the man you'll see on January 11th against Dean Haley, Donnovan Dunn!! [RA] The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, weighing int at 242 pounds, "Strong Guy" Will Mathewson {Mathewson hops up onto the turnbuckle, but no-one cares enough to even notice. "Stand" by Blues Traveler keys up, the fans not knowing to cheer or boo} [RA] And his opponent, weighing in at 238 pounds, from Lansing, Michigan, DONNOVAN DUNN!! {Dunn emerges from behind the curtain, casualy struts down to the ring, removes his jacket, grabs the mic for his ritualistic pre-match rant, and...} [DD] LISTEN UP, you couch-potato Pittsburgh peons! I have a bone to pick with you ... and a PRIME example of it is sittin' in the corner over there pickin' clams out of his teeth! This guy sniffs too much surfboard wax, throws on a pair of cheap spandex pants, bobs his head up and down and says "dude" a coupla times, and thinks he's a WRESTLER ... AND YOU LET HIM! [DD] Bums like him are YOUR fault! Every time you clap for a Dr. Zahorian posterchild like "the Spammer"-- and by the way, buddy, Hormel's on line one--every time, you make me sick ... every time you cheer like Steelers fans at a parole board hearing, just because "KO" Thompson pulled another ILLEGAL punch and saved himself the trouble of actually learning to wrestle, you make me sick ... every time you ignore a quality wrestler like Jack Davis to root for some macabre cartoon-character dimwit like Capt. Zoom or the Blitz Brothers, you make me sick ... [DD] Well, my doctor got tired of prescribing me drugs, and so I'm here to show you what wrestling's like in the REAL world! And I'm going to start with Moondoggie over here! Pay CLOSE attention, so you'll realize just WHY they say ... DONOVAN DUNN IS SECOND TO NONE! {clocks Mathewson with the mic to start the match} {bell rings} [AR] And Dunn with a cheap shot to start things off. [TJ] I dunno, that early attack could get him over 1000 dollars, doesn't sound too cheap to me. [AR] Anyways, Dunn with a clothesline and hits a couple of hard stomps.. you know, they didn't look that powerful to me... [TJ] Well, Donnovan did have to reinforce his boot for medicial purposes, so it may be a bit harder than the average boot. [AR] Ok, so we're talking loaded boots here. Anyways, Dunn slaps on a head-scisors, which Mathewson struggles to escape, and Dunn just goes into a grounded front-facelock which Mathewson again gets stuck in for a while. {fans start a boring chant, to which Dunn responds with a "SHADDAP!!"} [AR] Dunn picks up Mathewson, and another low blow! Tosses him outside, and follows him out. Grabs a chair, folds it back up, picks up Mathewson... standing head scisors over the chair, hooks Gary's arms... FRONT-FACE DDT ONTO THE CHAIR!! [TJ] Well, he had to do something to make up for his completely boring opening. [AR] Dunn rolls him back in, picks up Mathewson.. BACK BRAIN KICK WITH THE LOADED BOOT!! [TJ] Yeah, he calls that the reality check. [AR] Well, it seems to be more than enough to make the pinfall a reality, as Dunn gets his first win here in the World Organization of Wrestling. {"Stand" by Blues Traveler again blasts over the loudspeaker, this time triggeirng boos} [RA] Here is your winner, Donnovan Dunn! [AR] Well, up next we would have had The Blitz Brothers against Deric and Kayo, but due to the severity of Curtis Stone's injuries, the two parties have agreed to a singles match between Carter and Kayo. [TJ] Showing the idiodicy of both! Deric and Kayo are the tag-champs, and beating up on an injured guy would be the easiest way to get momentum going into WrestleFest, while Carter is primarily a tag-wrestler, while both Deric and Kayo have had extensive singles backgrounds. [AR] Well, beofore we see that match, lets go to Tom Buchanon in the WOW controll center. {Scene to controll center, Wrestlefest logo on the monitor in the background} [TB] Thanks Andy, heya wrestling fans. As you know, in just one week, the WOW will proudly be presenting Wrestlefest, December 1996. In this card, we have 4 title matches, the most prevelant one being last year's Perot Cup runner up, Vengeance, against Jack Davis for the World Title. Now, Jack hasn't been seen much outside of his title defences since winning the title. Rumor has it that he and Louie are having some differences and are trying to stay out of the spotlight to avoid these coming out into the open. More on this as it develops. [TB] In another match for world titles, Deric James and Kayo Thompson will defend their tag-team title against Louie Davis' duo of Dr. Sydney Pain and "Bedlam" Ben Wilder. Like Jack, Pain and Wilder haven't been seen much either. Is this more evidence pointing towards friction in Louie's stable? Again, if I find anything out, I will let you know. [TB] Sonic Soldier will be defending his International title against newcomer "Bombtrack" Alex Young. Young has been on an absolute tear since he's joined the WOW, but Sonic Soldier has been nigh undefeatable, his only loss coming at the hands of Dan Holloway, and the only man to have ever defeated James Havok by pinfall or submission. This should be a good matchup. [TB] Joey Igrozza will defend his newly aquired TV title against the leader of the Triangle of Doom, Lord Minoraur. The man Joey won the belt from, "The Eagle" Mike Taylor will be facing Dan Holloway, the man he defeated for the title back in Devil's Night Dance. Holloway certainly made his agrivation at losing known to the Eagle, who spent the night after he won the title in the hospital due to Holloway's attack. [TB] But the big match at the card, the match that we've been wating to see for months upon months, Jerry Straite and Nicholas Vorpal will finaly collide, in tag-team action however as Jerry and James Havok will face Helsing's men, Vorpal and Ogre. Now, to the ring! {Sceen to ring, "From out of Nowhere" by Faith No More plays, causing a chorus of cheers} [RA] The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by his brother, Curtis, weighing in at 248 pounds, from Buffalo, New York, CARTER STONE! {Carter comes down to the ring, slapping hands, as Curtis hobbles out on crutches, with a brace on his leg, covering a HUGELY swollen knee. He really doesn't look to be in wrestling shape. He enters the ring, hands off his belt, the bell rings...} [AR] Collar-and-elbow tie-up to start, both struggle, Kayo with a BIG hiptoss, Carter looks toward his corner... [TJ] There you go, that's the problem with tag-team wrestlers wrestling singles. [AR] And Kayo with a HARD New Jack Funk. He covers.. 1.. 2... 3!! {"ATLiens" keys up causing another big pop} [RA] Here is your winner, KENNETH "KAYO" THOMPSON!! [AR] The Blitz Brothers just can't seem to get themselves started here in the WOW. [TJ] Yeah, well, it does say something about the competition. Usualy, it sucks so much that there's at least someone that converted football players can beat, but not here. [AR] Well Carter and Curtis are heading back to the locker room, as Deric and Kayo celebrate, so let's take a quick comercial break. {"America the Beautiful" plays to a big crowd pop} [RA] The following contest, scheduled for one fall, will run under power match rules. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by his manager, the Dealer, weighing in at 450 pounds, from Honolulu, Hawaii, THE SPAMMER!! {Spammer comes down to the ring, smiling slapping hands, hugging kids, etc. Music switches to "Dust Devil" by Butthole Surfers and we get a pop} [RA] And his opponent, weighing in at 275 pounds, from Croton, New York, "THE MACHINE" JAMES HAVOK!! {Havok comes out to everyone going crazy, with one arm tied behind his back. Evidently, he felt it neccisary to make the fight fair, since Spammer has that injured arm. Than again, Spammer still has the arm, and it doesn't look all that bad. Oh well.. bell rings} [AR] Spammer starts off with a bearhug, which Havok takes a bit of time to escape, both to the ropes, and Spammer hits a shoulderblock. Havok kicks Spammer but gets taken down with a forearm. [TJ] How /STUPID/ can Havok get? I mean, yeah he's an awesome wrestler, but hanging around Deric and all them must be messing with his head. [AR] Well, it does remove the possibility of him hitting his finisher, or most power moves, so Havok will be reduced to brawling. Spammer bodyslams him, and then hits a rib crusher. Picks up Havok, who NAILS him with a forearm, but it doesn't prevent a press slam. Havok elbows the big guy, but gets NAILED with an awesome vertical suplex. Spammer accents it with a punch. [TJ] Its a darn shame too. I mean, Havok had the killer instinct any and every wrestler dreams of, but Jerry, Kayo and Deric had to go and sissify him, and now look at him. Because Spammer's shoulder is still a bit stiff he's tying his arm behind his back! [AR] Spammer picks him up, and Havok with a forearm. Follows it with a knee-lift and NAILS a elbow. Havok off the ropes, and hits Spammer with a clothesline. Spammer reeling but standing, Havok off the opposite set, and FLOORS the big guy! Stomps on him.. and again! Picks him up, and Spammer interrupts his momentum with a forearm. [TJ] See what I mean, all that killer instinct he showed there, numbed by the sissification shown by his arm behind his back. He's getting beat on by the SPAMMER of all people! [AR] Spammer sets for a powerbomb, BUT HAVOK BACKDROPS HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE!! Havok follows him out, rams his head into the steps, shoves him into the railing with authority, grabs the bell and rings Spammer's head with it. [TJ] Andy.. don't every try to make a joke that bad again. [AR] Picks him up, DDT!!! Wait..., Anderson's stopping the match, ringing the bell himself... [RA] James Havok has violated the rules for the match by using a DDT, therefore, your winner is SPAMMER!! {"America the Beautiful" keys up to a pop, Havok looks pissed, but takes the loss in stride as Spammer celebrates} [TJ] Look right there, he knows he just got screwjobbed, but does he make Spammer pay for it? NO!!! [AR] *sigh* Well, let's get to the ring for our next debut, coming in from the FWA, Captain Zoom! [RA] The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at 242 pounds, Gary Wilson!! {Wilson hops up onto the turnbuckle, getting a chant of "Gary! Gary!" from mutants in their Gary Wilson "Skills? Bell rings...} [AR] Zoom offers a handshake, which Wilson cockily ignores. Zoom gets a little perturbed at this, and bodyslams Wilson. He slaps on a body scissors which Gary takes a bit of time escaping, only to follow that up with a bearhug. [TJ] First we have people named after canned-meat, then we get football players, now comic book characters??? I may have to offer my services as manager to Dunn just to make my life a bit less neausiating. [AR] Zoom dropkicks Wilson, and nails him with a forearm. Wilson quickly rolls out of the ring... wait a sec, Zoom up top... BODYPRESS TO THE FLOOR!! He rolls back in, rolls Wilson back in, climbs up top... FLYING LOU THEZ PRESS!! Joey, that was a great match, and I hope we can do it again, real soon, belt or no belt. [MT] Thing is, after I lost the belt to Joey, I was doing a little thinking. It was my first belt, and it hurt a little bit to lose it. But Joey's a great guy, and I'll know he'll defend the belt best he can. So that's okay. But I thought about the way I won it, and I remember how "The Maimer" Dan Holloway attacked me when he was eliminated from the triangle match. [MT] Thing is, Taylor Jones was right. He *was* fighting a two-on-one the entire match, and that was unfair. *But*, he also tried to cripple me and the Prophet right after he was eliminated, and that wasn't all that nice of him, either. So here's what I want to say. Holloway, I want a match with you. I'm calling you out. Two reasons. First, I want to clear up the whole two-on-one thing, because I think I can beat you one-on-one. Second, I think you're a bully, and a coward, and I want to beat you to prove it. [MT] Until then, this eagle's going to *soar*! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- [RA] The following contest is schedule for one fall.
