EEF Test Transmission [3/4]

4 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We cut straight to the ring were we find Howard Robertson) Howard: Introducing first, hailing from Gijon, Spain. Weighing 248lbs. Here he comes "El Matador" Pedro De La Vega! (Spanish Flamenco music starts playing as Pedro makes his entrance, dressed like a matador. He has his long black hair in a ponytail. He's getting a great pop as he head for the ring.) Luke: Pepe is going to go down tonight ha ha ha!!! Howard: And his opponent, hailing from Bruxelles, Belgium. Weighing 284lbs "The Eurocrat" Hans Van Der Fluyt! (He has no entrance music but is greated by massive boos from the crowd as he walks towards the ring. He stops several times shouting how he will make wrestling illegal in the European Union. He is dressed in a grey suit, white shirt and a black tie. He has short brown hair and a small brown mustache. He climbs into the ring and shouts that he hates how the Spanish farmers tries to cheat in their applications for European Union financial support.) John: Well there is the bell. Pedro charges but is taken down in an armdrag by the Eurocrat. Luke: Ha Hans gets up brushing of his suite just as if he had touched something dirty! John: Pedro gets up and another armdrag by "The Eurocrat". Luke: Big grin on Hans Van Der Fluyt's face. He picks up Pepe and throws him over the ropes to the outside! John: Listen to the crowd booing. And Hans Van Der Fluyt starts yelling at the crowd again! If we would hold a popularity contest this man would finish last! Luke: Well wrestling isn't a popularity contest! John: Pedro gets up on the apron but a European Uppercut by "The Eurocrat" sends him to the outside once again. The referee is warning "the Eurocrat"! Pedro gets up on the apron again! Luke: Ah, He's only going to get knocked down again. John: No! Pedro catches " The Eurocrat" by the tie! Big forearm to the kisser by Pedro. Followed by a headbut and another forearm shot. "The Eurocrat" is backing away! Pedro jumps over the rope with a dropkick that send the hated man from Bruxelles down! A big Olé by Pedro. And a elbowdrop! The crowd is going wild. Pedro picks "the Eurocrat" up and whips him into the ropes big backdrop by Pedro! He's on fire now! Luke: Yeah, yeah. Whatever! John: The Eurocrat tries to get up but gets caught in a kneelift by Pedro! Cover by the Spaniard! 1 2 Kickout! John: Pedro with an elbowdrop! And a new cover! 1 2 Kickout! John: Pedro picks up "The Eurocrat" he goes for a bodyslam but "the Eurocrat" counters with a small package. 1 2 Pedro reverses it! 1 2 "The Eurocrat" Rereverse it! 1 2 Kickout! John: Wow! Both men get up to their feet and a headlock by Pedro. He takes "The Eurocrat" down. Luke: I didn't know Pepe could fight this good! John: He's been great this far! Luke: Well don't count out the Eurocrat yet! John: "The Eurocrat" manages to reach the ropes and Pedro releases the hold. Pedro backs of as "The Eurocrat" gets up to his knees. Pedro moves in… Low blow by "The Eurocrat" referee get in there warn him! Luke: Hey, Pedro has had his fun! John: "The Eurocrat" rakes Pedro's face against the ropes, and then starts to choke Pedro against the ropes the referee counts, 1… 2… 3… "The Eurocrat" releases the hold. He picks up Pedro and bodyslams him. Luke: Yeah, now it's "the Eurocrat" with the offense! John: "The Eurocrat" picks Pedro up in a Firemans carry, turns it into an Aeroplane spin and drops him like a bad habit! Cover. 1 2 3 John: He pinned him! I don't believe it! Luke: Learn to live with it! "The Eurocrat" Hans Van Der Fluyt is here to stay! Howard: Winner of the match! "The Eurocrat" Hans Van Der Fluyt! Time of match 5:47 John: Let's cut to a commercial, but stay tuned, next up are our main event! **************************************************************************** ** Many people have grown tired of using expensive training machines! Machines that are complicated to use and don't keep what they promise! So we searched all over the way to find the best way to get in shape and here is what we found! Introducing "Running". Yes, Running is the most effective way to get in shape! So if you mail us 30 D-Marks we will allow you to run! Send us your money and start running! You won't regret it! **************************************************************************** *** This program is a test transmission from the European E-Wrestling Federation.
