EEF Test Transmission [1/4]

4 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The show opens to "The Montague and Capulet Families" from Romeo and Juliet by Prokofjef with clips of a European map that gets consumed by flames. Next the European E-Wrestling Federation logo apears, then we cut over to some short clips from Copenhagen, the Danish Capital. We see the little mermaid, the popular buissnes street Ströget and pictures of the pariswheel in Tivoli, before we cut into the arena - Pumpehallen. The camera pan over the small arena who is jampacked, we see a lot of "EEF" signs and a "I hate the Eurocrat!" sign before we reach the commentators both. In it we find John Fitzsimmons, a man in his fifties, dressed in a black tuxedo. Luke "The Rock" Anthony is standing on his right side. Luke, a wellbuilt man in his late thirties is dressed in a Pink Tuxedo and a large brown Cowboy hat. As they start to speak the music fades away, leaving us only with screams and cheers from the crowd.] John: Wellcome to wonderful Copenhagen! Luke: I don't think it's so wonderful, the weather is awful, the people are awful and you are awful! John: Thank's! Comming from you, that is a compliment! However, wellcome to this EEF test show. It's the first of a series of rehearsal shows before our big opening in July.And what a line up we have for you tonight! We have four fights for you tonight. First we have "The Italian Gigolo" Luigi Maldini going up against the "The Hungarian Hitman" Lazlo Bogdan. Luke: That could go either way! John: That one will be followed by Lady Jasmine who will try to give "The Fit Irishman" Tommy O'Shea a Free Makeover! Luke: He needs it! But then again so do Lady Jasmine… John: Ha! Luke: …and so do you John! John: Hrm.. Then we have the man all of us love to hate "The Eurocrat" Hans Van Der Fluyt! Luke: Oh, I thought you ment yourself! John: Going up against "El Matador" Pedro de la Vega. Luke: Well little Pepe is going to be taught a wrestling lesson by "the Eurocrat" tonight! John: Then we have the main event, "The Radiant One" - Roland Ulv against "Rabid" Randolph Richards. Luke: That could be a classic. I hope Richards beat the crap out of the Swedish Idiot! John: Well let's go to the ring (We cut over to the ring were we see ringannouncer Howard Robertson standing by, dressed in a black suite.) Howard: It's time to get in on! (The crowd starts cheering.) Howard: Our first match is a single match with a set timelimit for 10 minutes. Introducing first, hailing from Budapest, Hungary. Weighing 239 lbs. He is the "Hungarian Hitman" Lazlo Bogdan! (Bogdan comes out from the dressing room area, dressed in grey wrestling tights, kneepads and wrestling boots. Wearing a grey mask. He is well built and has a large scar on his chest. He walks straight to the ring ignoring the fans who is giving him a mixed wellcome. He enters the ring and starts jumping up and down as he awaits his opponent.) Howard: And here is his opponent. Hailing from Venice Italy, weighing 238 lbs "The Italian Gigolo" Luigi Maldini.. (O Sole Mio with José Carreras starts playing as Maldini enters the arena. He's pretty short, out of shape with a big beer belly. He has greasy short hair. Is unshaved and smokes a cigarrett. He wears a pair of blue wrestling tights with an Italian flag printed on it. He flirts with several women on his way to the ring but they only turn their heads away in disgust. As he gets closer to the ring he turns to the crowd to talk with yet another woman. "The Hungarian Hitman" sees the opportunity and jumps him from behind and starts hitting Maldini with axehandle blows to the back. The music fades away and we hear the bell.) Luke: Ha! Bogdan decided to start early! John: He turns Maldini around and slaps the cigarett out of the Gigolo's mouth! Kneelift by Bogdan sends Maldini to the floor. Luke: Wow! Look at the Hungarian stomping away on Maldini. John: The referee tries to get the men into the ring. Luke: Well there they go. Bogdan whips Maldini into the ring and gets into the ring after him. John: Maldini tries to get up but another axehandle blow by Bogdan keeps him down. Luke: By the way do you know how Bogdan got that scar on his chest? John: No, Bogdan picks up Maldini in a bodyslam! Luke: He cut himself while shaving his chest! John: Come on! Luke: It's true, those Hungarian guys are hairy! John: Elbowdrop by Bogdan but Maldini roles out of the way. Luke: That's the first offensive move by Maldini this far! John: Offensive? Both men back up on their feet, Maldini locks on a headlock on Bogdan! Luke: Well Maldini needs to catch his breath! He's a chain smoker you know! John: Bogdan tries to get out of the hold! Luke: Yep, he smokes chains! Don't know hoe he does it but he does! John: Maldini releases the hold and whips Bogdan into the ropes. Oh flying crossbodyblock by Bogdan and a cover! The referee is on the job! 1…. 2….. Shoulder up! Luke: Bogdan is complaining about a slow count! John: Maldini gets up, big punch by Maldini! He tries to whip Bogdan into the ropes but "The Hungarian Hitman" reverses it and whips Maldini into the corner. Bogdan charges into the corner but is greated by a big boot by Maldini! Luke: Ha! Look, Maldini is gyrating his hips and the crowd is booing him! John: Yep he's pretty alone in thinking he's beautiful! Luke: Yeah and that he can wrestle! John: Maldini with a cover. 1 Kickout! John: Now it's Maldini complaining about a slow count! Come on! Luke: Bogdan from behind, rolls Maldini up! 1 2 Kickout! John: That was close! Both men up! Savate kick by Bogdan send Maldini down. Bogdan is really dominating this match! Luke: So what, so would a three year old in a wheel chair! John: I don't now about that, Maldini slowly gets back up on his feet they lock up. Snap-Suplex by Maldini. Cover by Maldini look he has his legs on the ropes. The referee doesn't see it! 1 2 3 John: There is the bell I don't believe it! Maldini gets the win! What injustice! Luke: Yeah Bogdan is pretty upset to. He starts kicking the ropes in anger! Howard: Winner of the match, after 3:46 "The Italian Gigolo" Luigi Maldini! (The Godfather theme starts playing and the crowd starts booing.) John: Look what's that! Luigi is slipping some money to the referee! I hope the President looks into that! Luke: Where? I didn't see anything! John: Oh, it seems like Bogdan want another piece of Maldini! Maldini escapes the ring and head for the dressing room chased by Bogdan! Well we have to cut to a commercial, stay tuned! ****************************************************************** Are you tired of buying things from tv-shops that doesn't work or stops functioning after a couple of months? Well now you don't have to wait that long! Introducing "Rubbish" The Tv-product that's not good for anything! We break it before we send it to you! That way you don't have to break it yourself! "Rubbish" will never work! So you don't have to worry about getting into shape, bake something, or paint something! You can't get a worse deal than that!! So what are you waiting for? Send us your money we need it more than you! Check your teletext page 700 for prices and ordering information! Or call us at 1-800-SUCKER "Rubbish" The product you can live with out! ******************************************************************* This program is a test transmission from the European E-Wrestling Federation. The EEF will start operating in July. It's a member of GIWA. If you want to know more about the EEF Visit our homepage at http://home4.swipnet.se/~w-45120/Index.htm
