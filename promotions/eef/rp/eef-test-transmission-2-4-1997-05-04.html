Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
John: Well we are back! As you can hear "I wan't muscles" with Olivia Newton John is playing that means only one thing…. Howard: Making his way to the ring…Hailing from Dublin, Ireland. Weighing 234 lbs. "The Fit Irishman" Tommy O'Shea! Luke: They should have said "The potatoe farmer" Tommy O'Shea! John: Stop it! (Tommy O'Shea makes his entrance, he's well built, has short red hair and is very pale. He's dressed in a pair of green wrestling tights and green wrestling boots. He gets a warm wellcome by the fans and gives several high fives on his way to the ring.) Howard: And his opponent, hailing from Bradford, England. Weighing 378lbs ….. "The Queen of all dragqueens"! Lady Jasmine! (Vogue by Madonna starts playing and the crowd starts booing and whistling as Lady Jasmine makes his entrance dressed in a long red silk gown, he's wearing a lot of makeup and his long blond hair is flowing free in the air. He blows kisses to the fans on his way to the ring.) John: He looks ridicioulus! Luke: What are you talking about she's a fine looking lady! John: It's a man! Luke: Watch it, if she hears you she'll give you the Free Makeover! (Lady Jasmine slowly climbs into the ring, blows a kiss to Tommy O'Shea before he takes of his gown. Under it he's wearing a pair of small pink wrestling tights and a pair of white wrestling boots. It's not a pretty sight. He is F-A-T!) John: Well it looks like Lady Jasmine is ready to wrestle, there is the bell. Tommy O'Shea offers a test of strength but Lady Jasmine declines by shaking his head and his hands. The crowd goes boo! Lady Jasmine climbs out of the ring and grabbs a microphone. Lady Jasmine: Shut up you stupid people! You should be glad you get the opportunity to watch me wrestle! John: Oh, here comes Tommy O'Shea! Dropkick to the back of Lady Jasmine sends him over the guardrail! Luke:What a coward! John: Well the crowd seems to like it! Lady Jasmine gets back up on his feet Luke: Her feet! John: And get suplexed back over the guardrail! Wow what power by Tommy O'Shea Luke: Well it looks like he hurt his back when he lifted Lady Jasmine. John: Tommy O'Shea gets back into the ring and Lady Jasmine is getting back up on his feet. It looks like Tommy hurt his back. Luke: What are you? A parrot? I allready said that! John: Lady Jasmine climbs back into the ring. He's going after Tommy but gets kicked in the midsection. Followed by a European uppercut by Tommy O'Shea. He has the fat man in trouble! Luke: First of all she's a lady! Second of all she's not fat she's only big boned! John: Standing dropkick by Tommy! Luke: Oh no he hit Jasmine's beautiful face! John: Lady Jasmine gets up on his knees begging for mercy! Oh low blow by Lady Jasmine! Luke: Ha! That's smart wrestling! John: The referee warns Lady Jasmine who starts attacking Tommy O'Sheas injured back with big forearm shots! Followed by an Atomic Drop! Oh, Tommy is in trouble! Lady Jasmine picks him up in a backbreaker. Tommy is down and Lady Jasmine starts posing for the crowd. They are booing him! Luke: Those primates wouldn't recognize talent even if it jumped up and bit them in the <bleep>! John: No swearing! Lady Jasmine finally stops posing and picks up Tommy! Belly-to-Belly Suplex by Lady Jasmine. Luke: The lady can wrestle, no doubt about that! Cover by Lady Jasmine! 1 2 Kickout! John: That was close. Lady Jasmine gets up, and plants a big elbowdrop on Tommy. Wow, that's 378lbs landing on him! Luke: Sssh, you should never talk about a lady's weight! John: For the last time! Lady Jasmine is not a woman! Luke: John, I'm shocked! John: Lady Jasmine starts to climb the ropes. He reaches the top turnbuckle. I don't believe it, The Big man is going to jump! Here comes with a big splash! Wow, Tommy moves out of the way! The entire ring shock when Lady Jasmine came crashing down. Luke: Poor Jasmine! Now both men are down. John: Ha you said it! Luke: What? John: You said both men are down! Luke: Hrm.. Let's watch the match! Both men, I mean Lady Jasmine and Tommy O'Shea is getting up. Tommy with a right hand but Lady Jasmine blocks it and gets behind Tommy O'Shea! He, she slaps on the Free Makeover! This could be it! John: Yeah that sleaperhold is a difficult hold to break free from! Tommy O'Shea is sinking fast. The referee is lifting his arm. It goes down. He lifts it a second time. It goes down. He lifts the arm a third time! It goes down. There's the bell! Lady Jasmine gets the win! Howard: Winner of the match Lady Jasmine! Time of match 8:16 Luke: Look Lady Jasmine is going to work she grabbs her beauty bag. Here comes the lipstick. Ha Tommy O'Shea is getting red lips to match his red hair! John: This is disgusting! Luke: Ha, Lady Jasmine puts a black wig on Tommy! Now aint he pretty? And a big kiss form Lady Jasmine before she leaves in triumph! John: I feel sick! Let's cut to a commercial….
