EEF Test Transmission [4/4]

4 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
John: Well we are back this is what you have been waiting for. Let's listen to some comments recorded earlier. (Scene opens inside the backstage area. The Radiant One - Roland Ulv is standing by, dressed in his ringattire, a pair of wrestling tights designed as the Swedish flag, blue kneepads and blue wrestling boots. He's in great shape with a body the Greek gods would have been envious of. He has his long blond hair in a ponytail.) THE RADIANT ONE: "Rabid" Randolph Richards yur moments away from facing thi Swedish sensation that rocks thi Nation! Take a good look at my body! It's beyond perfection. I am thi Radiant One! Thi most Radiant star in wrestling today! I will outclass yu, I will outsmart yu and I will outwrestle yu! Yur going down fur sure! (The scene fades to black) John: Powerful words from Ulv let's here from Richards. (The scene opens inside a lockerroom. "Rabid" Randolph Richards is lacing his boots. He's dressed in black wrestling tights, black kneepads and black boots. He has short brown hair and a mustasch. On his left arm he has a tatoo saying "Scotland" forever! His face is scared but he looks happy.) RICHARDS: Well, well. Tonight I finally get to face the wee little prettyboy Ulv! Lad, let me tell you one thing! I may not be pretty to look at but I am the toughest man alive! I will break bones in your body that you didn't even know you had! I'm gonna punch you in your face so hard that your feet will hurt for a month! The Glasgow kiss is going to come down on you to night! It's going to be rrrRRRRabid! (Scene fades to black.) (We cut back to the ring!) Howard: It's time for our main event! This one is set for one fall and has no timelimit! Introducing first! Hailing from Glasgow, Scotland. Weighing in at 257 lbs…. "RABID" RANDOLPH RICHARDS!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The opening music from A Clockwork Orange starts playing as Richards makes his entrance.He is dressed as earlier, and walks straight to the ring without even glancing at the fans. He enters the ring and leans back on the ropes.) And his opponent, hailing from Stockholm, Sweden. Weighing in at a superb 265 lbs. "The Radiant One" Roland Ulv (The Swedish National Anthem starts playing, The Radiant One dressed as earlier walks slowly towards the ring carrying a steel bucket full of Fermented Baltic Herring. He puts down the bucket on the apron and climbs through the ropes.) John: Well here is the bell. Look at them, they don't move they just stare at each other. Luke: This is tense, every head in the arena is turned against the ring! John: They slowly walk toward each other. They are standing face to face. Richards pushes The Radiant One away. The Radiant One rushes back, they lock up and The Radiant One takes Richards down in a headlock. Luke: Richards with grabs hold of The Radiant One's right arm, he breaks free. He holds on to the arm and turns it into a wristlock. He whips the Swedish prettyboy to the ropes and drops to the mat to trip the Radiant One but the Swede manages to hold on to the ropes and comes back with an Elbowdrop on Richards. The Radiant One picks up Richards in a headlock. John: It looks like The Radiant One is trying to wear down the Scotsman with headlocks. Luke: Well I have never seen a man being beat by a headlock.. John: Oh Richards manages to grab hold of the Radiant Ones right leg. He lifts him up… Kneebreaker by Richards. Luke: Yeah keep the stupid Swede down! John: Richards drops and elbow on the Radiant Ones leg. He continues by locking on a Spinning Toe Hold. The Radiant One screams in pain! Luke: Haha! John: The Radiant One must break free from this hold if he don't want to loose this match. Luke: Said John, once again stating an obvious fact! John: The Radiant One manages to grab a handful of Richards hair and pulls him over into a small package. The referee is on the job. 1 2 Kickout. Luke: That was a hairpull! Why was the referee even counting? He should warn the Radiant One not count! John: The Radiant one gets up shaking his right leg in an attempt to get the blood flowing again. Richards get up and clips the Radiant One from behind. Cover by Richards. 1 Kickout! Luke: Damn! John: Richards with a fistdrop! He conects! The Radiant One roles out of the ring to take a breather, Richards climb the turnbuckle to recieve cheers! The Radiant One seems frustrated and picks up a chair, he's threatening to bring into the ring! But the Referee persuades him not to! The Radiant One reenters the ring without the chair. They lock up and Richards whips the Radiant One into the corner! He follows up with a big clothes line, and starts pounding away on the Radiant One. Listen to the crowd counting the blows! One…. Two… Three… Four… Luke: I love it! John: Wow the Radiant One catches Richards in an Inverted Atomic Drop, follows up with a Belly-to-Belly Suplex. Richards is down! The Radiant One starts flexing his muscles and the crowd boo him! Richards is still down and here is the cover by the Radiant One! 1 Kickout by Richards! Luke: Take him out Richards! John: Are you still upset with the Radiant One since the time he stole your girlfriend? Luke: Shut up! John: The Radiant One with a Single Leg Boston Crab but Richards is able to reach the ropes.The Radiant One lets go of the hold but grabs Richards leg and drape it onto the ropes and jumps on it. Richards scream in pain. The Radiant One grabs hold of the leg once again and drags Richards towards the corner, The Radiant One slides to the outside and slams Richards leg against the steel ring post! Luke: Come on! Get in there ref! John: The Radiant One reenters the ring, he's going for a D.D.T but Richards reverses it with a backdrop! But wait the Radiant on grabs hold of Richards hips and pulls him down, cover! 1 2 Kickout! Luke: What's wrong with Richards? John: Both men get's up The Radiant One with a sleeper but Richards counter with a Jawbreaker. Richards get up, Russian Legsweep by Richards. Elbowdrop by Richards! Richards tries to grab hold of the Radiant Ones left leg but is kicked away. The Radiant One gets back up! And dives after Richards leg but Richards moves out of the way. They lock up the Radiant one whips Richards into the ropes and is going for a bakcdrop but Richards catches him Pilledriver by Richards! Cover 1 2 Shoulder up! Luke: Yeah but that took a lot out of the Radiant One! John: Richards starts climbing the ropes he's going for his Glasgow Kiss! The flying headbut he has made famous! Luke: Yes! John: Here he comes but he misses as the Radiant One sits up! The Radiant One is strugling to get to his feet. He sees that Richards is out cold and goes for the cover! 1 2 Kickout! John: Unbelievable! Luke: That was close! John: Both men get up they are tired. Luke: Well who wouldn't be? John: They start to trade punches! A right hand by Richards, A left by The Radiant One Right by Richards, Right by The Radiant One! Richards whips the Radiant One to the ropes and runs to the other side himself, they meet in the middle of the ring. Both men are going for a clothesline! Both men go down. Look the Radiant One's right arm are covering Richards the Referee starts counting! 1 2 Shoulder up by Richards! Luke: This is one tough battle! John: Both men starts to get up. They lock up, oh Radiant-Plex by the Radiant One! The Ref counts! 1 2 3 Luke: Oh No!!!!!!!!!! John: Well the Radiant One managed to slap on his finisher and that gave him the victory. Howard: Winner of the match! The Radiant One - Roland Ulv! Time of match 15:22 John: The Radiant One rolls over to his bucket! Oh no, there's no call for that! He splashes the Fermented Baltic Herring All over Richards and starts to pound him with the steelbucket! Get security down there! Luke: Oh it looks like we are running out of time! See you in a couple of weeks for next test transmission before our official opening in July!
