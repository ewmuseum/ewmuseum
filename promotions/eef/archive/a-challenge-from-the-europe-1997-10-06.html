A Challenge from the Europe!!!

6 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi e-wrestling fans! My name is Reginald Humphries Jr. I run the EEF. I'm a demanding fed head. I try to get good handlers with wrestlers that fit into the federation. That way it's easier to create good angles and the fed gets more entertaining. I mean how many feds doesn't have 10 sexyboys 8 men from the darksides etc. In order to avoid that I have chosen a different approach. I have chosen to put up wanted adds in which I describe what kind of wrestlers the fed needs in order to retain the high standard.. Ok here is what the EEF is looking for right now. 1. A brittish snob tagteam Yes it's amazing, the EEF is a European Fed and we still haven't been able to locate a good Brittish snob tagteam. Come on I know there's a lot of Brittish handlers out there! Take the chance to create a tagteam you can roleplay well in an enviroment you know! 2. A French speaking face. Come on I know there's a lot of handlers from Quebec. Here is your chance to use those french catchphrases you allways dreamed of using! He could be from Quebec or France or any other french speaking country. The French audience really demands a hero and so does the EEF in order to earn more money in France. 3.A Japanese tagteam! To bring in some oriental action into the European E-wrestling scene. Use an english speaking manager in your flashes. 4. A mexican or spanish wrestler! There must be someone out ther who can portray a spanish(mexican wrestler! 5, A young american rookie tagteam! You know like the Rockers in the mid eighties! This could be a good way for you to start with a wrestler from scratch, work your way up and perhaps catapult on off the members into single competition. Well if you think you are up to the job contact me at [detail withheld] or go directly to the European E-Wrestling Federations homepage at http://home4.swipnet.se/~w-45120/Index.htm
