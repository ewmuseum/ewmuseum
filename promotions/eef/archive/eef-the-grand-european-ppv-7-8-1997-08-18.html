[EEF] The Grand European PPV (7/8)

18 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FRANK: Our next match is scheduled for one fall........coming down the aisle from Dublin, Ireland, weighing in at 262 lbs, SKIDMARK HARRIS!!! (Chariots of Fire plays as Skidmark comes to the ring with Ms. Goodyear, while the fans boo.) FRANK: His opponent, hailing from Nottingham, UK, weighing in at 241 lbs. SIR ALFRED CAR........TWRIIIIIIIIIGHT. JOHN: Here comes Sir Alf thundering to the ring!!! And the fans go wild!!! He's all over Skidmark Harris! LUKE: That cheater!!! JOHN: Cheater? Hey don't you remember how Harris attacked Sir Alf after their match at the SJPW Battlecade? Alfred throws Harris to the outside. Now their battling on the outside. Referee Henderson starts to count.... ONE!!! JOHN: Alf whips Harris to the guardrail!!!!! TWO!!! JOHN: Alf tries to smash Harris face against the guard rail but Harris blocks it and throws Alfred into the steel steps....... THREE!!!!!! JOHN: Harris with an Irishwhip no reversed and Harris into the guardrail! FOUR!!!! LUKE: Wow the guardrail jumped from the impact of Harris face! FIVE!!!!!! JOHN: Alfred grabs the steel steps and throws them on Harris!!!!! Well he use to say that he likes to mix it up if he has to! SIX!!!!! JOHN: Harris is counting starts in the Arena ceiling, here comes Alfred! SEVEN!!!!! JOHN: Alfred grabs the steel steps and throws them on Harris again but Harris moves out of the way! EIGHT!!!!!! JOHN: Harris with a low blow and a fist ful of Alfreds face starts to get back up! NINE!!!!! JOHN: Both men fall over the guardrail! TEN!!!!!! (DING!!!!DING!!!DING!!!) JOHN: This one will be a double countout but these guys aren't done!!!!! LUKE: This is great!!! I love this kind of action!!!!! JOHN: Ms. Goodyear nails Alfred with the crowbar but it doesn't phase him. Harris gets up and nails Alfred with a chair!! And Harris and Goodyear high tail it out of there!!! And Alfred is hot on the chase! Well I'm sorry the match ended that way I would have seen a winner! LUKE: You did! Harris beat the living daylights out of Alfred !!!!!!!!! JOHN: Well that means its time to crown the first ever EEF Tagteam Champions FRANK: The next match is scheduled for one fall with no timelimit! It's the final of the Tagteam tournament. Introducing first hailing from UK, weighing in at a combeigned weight of 438 pounds the Tagteam of the Monarchy!!!!!!! (Mozart's 5th Symphony starts playing and the Monarchy and Queen Victoria heads for the ring! Big boos from the crowd.) LUKE: There's the first EEF Tagteam Champions right there! JOHN: Well the Stallions may have something to say against that. FRANK: And their opponents hailing from Rome, Italy. Weighing in at 461 pounds, THE ITALLION STALLIONS!!!! (The Arena errupt as The Freshman by Verv Pipe starts playing and the Stallions run to the ring.) JOHN: Well it looks they have got the fans on their side! LUKE: Well that won't help them tonight their in with perhaps the finest tagteam to ever grace a wrestling ring. (DING!!DING!!!) JOHN: It looks like it will be Sia and Antonio to start things up. Antonio runs into the ropes. Here he comes back, wow big clothesline from Antonio sends Sia out of the ring. Antonio follows him to the outside. LUKE: Sure now he's going to turn it into a brawl! JOHN: They're brawling on the floor. Antonio knocks Sia into the ringpost. Antonio with a kick to Sia's midsection! Wow Antonio is on fire! He knocks Sia into the ringsteps. Sia looks like he doesn't know what hit him! Oh flying shoulderblock by Antonio! Sia is really being overpowered this far. LUKE: Well it isn't over yet! JOHN: Antonio smashes sias face into the ringsteps. Sia struggles to get back up to his feet. He is grabbed by Antonio and whipped back into the ring! LUKE: Bane help him!!!!! JOHN: Antonio goes for the pin. Henderson counts. ONE!!! JOHN: Kickout by Sia. Antonio tries to pick Sia up but gets nailed with a low blow! Sia twists Antonios arm and tags in Bane they whip Antonio into the ropes. And allmost decapitate him with a double clothesline! LUKE: Great move by the Monarchy! JOHN: Sia leaves the ring. Bane locks on a Boston Crab! LUKE: YES!!!! JOHN: Oh no! Antonios back can't be in to good shape after the earlier match with the Jury! Antonio screams in pain but won't submit! LUKE: Sia signalling for a tag. JOHN: Bane picks up Antonio and tags in Sia. They pick him up and executes a double Gorilla Press. Wow. Antonio comes crashing down hard. Cover by Sia! LUKE: We have our champions! ONE!!!! TWO!!! JOHN: Franco comes in to make the save. Sia gets up and the referee tries to make Franco leave the ring! Sia drags Antonio into the Monarchy's corner. They start to choke him! Franco tries to reenter the ring but is cut of by Henderson which gives the Monarchy even more time to choke Antonio. Henderson is back on the job an the Monarchy stops choking Antonio. LUKE: Great tagteam wrestling! John: Queen Victoria gets up on the apron. Henderson gets over to her. Oh Bane just nailed Antonio with the Royal Sceptre! Sia with the cover. Henderson is on the job. ONE!!! JOHN: Franco tries to get in but is intercepted by Bane!!!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!!! Luke: YES!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (DING!!!DING!!!DING!!!) JOHN: And we have the winners, by cheating! LUKE: By being smart!!!!!!!! FRANK: Winners of the match and the first EEF TAGTEAM CHAMPIONS…. THE MONARCHY!!!!!!!! LUKE: Yes! (Mozarts 5th Symphony starts playing but it's allmost impossible to hear it from all of the boos. Franco is attending to his brother who's still out cold while the Monarchy is handed the tagteam titles. The Monarchy leave in triumph! ) JOHN: The first EEF tagteam champions. LUKE: Wow, what a night. First the Monarchy wins the tagteam titles and now Archer is going to win the European Heavyweight Title. What a night for their stable!
