EEF Ring Warriors

20 May 1997

Camera cuts to a packed Royal Albert Hall where the fans are reelin' and rockin'. Cut to the announcer desk where Rick Fuji and "Baron" Wolfgang Von Drake are seated.) Fuji: Hello wrestling fans and welcome to European Warriors! I'm Rick Fuji and along with my broadcast partner Wolfg... Drake: Nein! You vill address me as Der Baron! Fuji: Sorry "Der Baron", anyway fans we will be bringing you some great matches tonight and to kick things off we have Josef Tadeuscz, "The Cavalier"is taking on Veteran wrestler Orson "Irish Wolfhound" Edmonds, the one-time Irish tag champion with Mike Mayall...before the latter was injured three months ago in an auto accident. Let's go down to Frank Heineman for the introdutions... (Cranberries' "Salvation" begins as the crowd boos loudly the entrance of Edmonds, dressed in camouflage with "IRA" stitched onto his armpads) Heineman: This match is scheduled for one fall...making his way into the ring first, from Binbannogh, Eire, weighing in at 248 pounds, The Irish Wolfhound...Orson Edmonds...! (Fans boo even louder as he enters the ring. He waves to the audience and removes his jacket, dropping it at ringside) (The Theme music changes quickly into Qkumba Zoo's "Child Inside", and Josef "The Cavalier" Tadeuscz is escorted to ringside by his manager, Lady Solidarity) Heineman: His opponent.... making his way down the aisle with his manager Lady Solidarity...from Lodz, Poland, weighing in at 260 pounds....Josef...the Cavalier...Tadeuscz! Fuji: Josef is getting into the ring now to the cheers of the fans...Lady Solidarity is helping him remove his jacket, and it looks like the match is ready to begin... (ding) Fuji: Looks as though both opponents are sizing each other up to start with...Orson moving quickly to his left and circling the Polish wrestler... here comes the lockup... and Edmonds puts the newcomer in an armbar... Now a reversal..... now reversed again...Edmonds tightens the lock.... and a reversal by the cavalier...Edmonds backs him into the ropes and throws him for a whip-in...off the ropes, the Wolfhound shoulderblocks the big Polish man down...as Tadeuscz moves to his feet, Edmonds bounds off the ropes to clothesline him. Tadeuscz ducks the clothesline...Edmonds off the other side and OH MY! Tadeuscz plants a dropkick right on his chin! Edmonds is up quickly but a series of arm drags take him down...As he rises again, Tadeuscz grabs him and Powerslams him to the ground...it's obvious that the veteran is hurting from that attack...Tadeuscz lifting him off the ground...And the Wolfhound has raked his eyes! Drake: Ya, dat ist the way to go. A great manuver by a veteran competitor. Now ve vill see vhat dis "Cavilier" ist made of. Fuji: Tadeuscz is standing prone as Edmonds lands a standing dropkick on his chin to drop him to the ground...Orson Edmonds grabs a hank of hair and lifts the Polish wrestler to his feet...there's an Irish Whip-in...Edmonds following him into the turnbuckle.... And he moves away! Edmonds falls to the ground! Drake: Nein! Dumkopf! Dis "Wolfhound" ist a disgrace to all Irish men! He should be vipping dis rookie, but instead he is on der floor. Fuji: The crowd is cheering wildly as the Cavalier Josef Tadeuscz once more gains his momentum! He grabs Edmonds and lifts him into another Powerslam, then a snap suplex, then into the ropes....Ooh...nice Bencao Handspring...the big guy is showing more than just power moves here...this guy could be a serious contender in no time...the wind is knocked out of the Wolfhound...The Cavalier reacts to the fans cheers as Edmonds climbs slowly to his feet... He turns and sees him...moves toward him and Edmonds kicks him in the midsection to slow the Cavalier down... Edmonds flings him to the ropes, goes for the armdrag, reversal into a full nelson...he sweeps the leg and drives Edmonds face first into the mat! He calls that move the Kosciusczko. Down goes Edmonds! Tadeuscz lifts him to his feet, tucking his head in under for the Polish Pride(Modified Fishermanbuster)....lifts him up and slams him to the mat...rolling him for thecover, the referee counts... 1......2.......3..! (ding ding ding ding) Heineman: The winner of this bout...Josef..."The Cavalier"...Tadeuscz! (Crowd cheers as Qkumba Zoo's "Child Inside" plays as Lady Solidarity enters the ring and raises Tadeuscz's arm in victory) Fuji: A great win for this young senstation over the veteran Edmonds. Drake: Bah! He ist just lucky I am retired or I vould show him that chivalry ist dead. Fuji: Whatever you say "Baron". Let's get down to Frank for our next match. Heineman: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first already in the ring....hailing from Gijon, Spain and weighing in at 247 pounds..."El Matador" Pedro Del Vega! (Crowd gives a good pop as Vega climbs to the second turnbuckle and screams out "Ole!!". The cheers turn to boos as Mozarts 5th Symphony starts up.) Heineman: His opponent...Hailing from Kent, England and weighing in at 251 pounds...."Classic" Andrew Archer!!! (Archer makes his way down and looks over the audience with utter disdain. He is wearing a custom smoking jacket and wearing a monocle. He steps in the ring and tells the referee to back Vega off and he takes off his jacket to reveal long white tunks with a seal on the side and blue boots) (Ding) Fuji: We are underway in this one as Vega looks to lock up...Archer backs off with a look of disgust and is complaining about Vega being "greasey" Drake: Ya, ya. You don't vant to be getting in there and try und vrestle a greased pig. Fuji: Vega looks to lock up agian and Archer backs off once more. Vega is getting frustrated and now Archer is yelling at the fans. Archer turns back to face Vega...Vega with an armdrag...Ole! Drake: Stop cheering for dat two bit bullfighter you little vhipper snapper! Fuji: Vega now locking on an armbar and Archer looking for a way out. Vega cranks down on the hold, but Archer manages to make it to the ropes and Vega with a clean break. Good sportsmanship from Vega. Drake: Vhich just shows how stupid he is. When you have a man on the ropes you don't back off you choke him. Ya! Fuji: Archer complaining to the referee about a hair pull, but Vega clearly didn't do it. Vega motions for Archer to lock up...Archer moves in...Collar and elbow tie up...Archer with a side headlock and now he is bragging to the fans...Vega moves him back into the ropes and shoots him to the farside..No! Archer with a handfull of hair and maintains the headlock. Referee Jonathan Smalls warning Archer who is pleading innocent. Drake: Leave him alone Smalls just count the ein, zwei, drei when it comes time. Fuji: Vega again trys to sling Archer off the ropes and this time he does...Archer with a body tackle and Vega goes down...Archer off the ropes...over the top of Vega...Vega up and hits a beautiful hiptoss!...Archer up and Vega with a dropkick!...Archer rolls to the floor and wants a time out. Drake: A very smart move here by Archer. It breaks any momentum dat Spanish fool had built up. Fuji: Archer back in and Vega rushes over...Oh! Thumb to the eye by Archer...Now a kick to the midsection followed by a vicious European uppercut that puts Vega down to the mat....Fistdrop by Archer finds its mark and Vega is hurting. Drake: Now choke the air out of him. HA-HA! Fuji: Calm down "Baron", I don't want to perform CPR on you. Drake: Hah! I am in perfect physical condition und if you doubt it I will take your in the ring for a personal demonstration. Fuji: I'll give you a raincheck on that one Baron. Archer has applied a Boston crab and he is really wrenching back with all his weight and Smalls is checking with Vega, but he says no. Drake: Give it up you twerp, you have no business even being in der ring vith someone as great as der "Classic" won. Fuji: What are you, Archer's PR man? Archer releases the crab and is now taunting the crowd...Vega to his feet and he is really favoring his back now...Archer with a kick to the midsection and sets for a suplex...OH! Snap Suplex on Vega and Archer really took him over hard...Archer covers 1...2...Archer pulled him up! Drake: Ya! Dish out more punishment. Ha-ha! Throw him out here Archer und I vill teach him a lesson. Fuji: They can just keep it in the ring as far as I am concerned and your supposed to be and impartial commentator Baron...Archer once again taunting the crowd and he sets for another suplex...Vega counters with a small package 1... Drake: Nein!!! Fuji: 2...thr...Oh! Archer just kicks out...Vega up and Archer with a right hand to the face, but it didn't even faze Vega who is getting his second wind and feeding off the fans cheers...Archer begging off now and Vega hits him with a couple of kicks to the midsection and sends him to the ropes...BIG back body drop by Vega!!! Vega waits for Archer to get to his feet...Vega off the ropes...Flying Clothesline...No! Archer catches him and spins into a spinebuster! That's the "Classic Touch"!! He covers 1...2....3!!!!! Heineman: Your winner...."Classic" Andrew Archer!!!! (Mozart's 5th Symphony plays and the crowd boos as Archer stands with his hands in the air and a look of utter contempt on his face.) Fuji: Nice effort by "El Matador", but Archer pulled it out at the end. Drake: Bah! Dat match vas never in doubt. Fuji: We have more exciting action fans so take it away Frank! Heineman: The following contest is scheduled for one fall...Introducing first already in the ring...hailing from Dublin, Ireland and weighing in at 234 pounds...The "Fight Irishman" Tommy O' Shea!!! (A smattering of cheers for O'Shea from the crowd. ) Heineman: His opponent hails from Hamburg, Germany and weighs in at 290 pounds....Max Von Winter!!! (The fans boo loud and long as Winter comes out dressed in a military garb and smooths back his handlebar mustache.) Drake: Now you vill see a real athelete. Just look at him. He is truley a superior man. Vinter vill destroy dis "Fit Irishman" und make der Fatherland proud. Fuji: He is an impressive looking athlete I'll give you that. O'Shea moves in and Winter with a kick to the midsection and a trio of stiff double axhandles that drive O'Shea down to the mat! Drake: Ya! Dominate him! Ha-ha! I love dis. Fuji: O'Shea up and Winter grabs him by the throat with both hands and lifts him for a choke! Come on referee stop this. Drake: Nein! Let him choke the life out of dat fool! Fuji: Referee Mark Henderson laying in the five count and Winter breaks by dumping O'Shea down on the mat. Winter turns to Henderson and gets in his face and Henderson backs off. You have to think Mark is more than a little intimidated by that bruiser. Drake: Just as you are intimidated by me ya? Fuji: Ahem...Winter now stomping away at O'Shea and Tommy is taking a beating here. Winter pulls O'Shea up and sends him outside to the floor right in front of us. Stay in your seat "Baron". Drake: Vhat? I vas just getting up to get a better look is all. Fuji: Right. Winter out of the ring and pulls O'Shea up...Oh! Rams O'Shea into the steel steps...Winter with a scoop and a slam on the concrete!! He yells to the "Baron" that that move was for him. Drake: I am so honored. Do it again! Ha! Fuji: Winter throws O'Shea back in and gets up on the apron. Winter now screaming out at the fans! Drake: Sit down and shut up you imbeciles und vatch a real athlete. Ah, these dumpkopf's have no respect for greatness. Fuji: Winter climbs back in...and O'Shea meets him with a right hand and another...O'Shea fighting back and now he hits a dropkick, but he didn't budge the big man. O'Shea back up and Winter is telling him to take his best shot. O'Shea spins around and is going for the St. Patricks Punch(Discus Punch)!! He comes around...Oh!! Winter caught him in the German Claw before he could land the punch! Drake: Now squeeze his temples until what little brains he has comes squirting out his ears. Ha! Fuji: Yuck. Winter has the hold secure and O'Shea is screaming in pain...Referee Mark Henderson is signalling for the bell. It's over!! (Ding,ding,ding) Heineman: Your winner....Max Von Winter!!!! (The crowd boos as Winter refuses to release the hold.) Fuji: Come on break it already. Drake: Vhy should he. Let him have his fun. Fuji: He finally releases the hold and holds up claw in the air and the fans are really laying into him. Drake: As I said, all of them have no respect for true greatness. I salute you Max Von Vinter! Fuji: Oh brother. If you are done with this gushing show of affection we will get to our next match. Heineman: The following contest is scheduled for one fall...Introducing first from Nantes, France...weighing in at 218 pounds..."The Designer" Paul Laurent!!! (The fans boo as Laurent makes his way down the aisle. He has long purple hair and is wearing designer original tights, this time with a clash of purple, red and orange. "Opium" by Jump Little Children suddenly blares over the PA and the crowd pops) Heineman: His opponent hails from Liverpool, England and weighs in at 245 pounds...William Madison Gage!!! (Fans pop as Gage comes out wearing his silver Oakley shades and blue tights. He jumps over the top rope and takes his sunglasses off.) Fuji: I'll tell you what "Baron", Gage symbolizes the true English gentleman. Here is a man who has money, but hasn't lost respect for the hard working man either unlike Andrew Archer. Drake: Dis makes me think he is any idiot. Vhy vould you want to be around dirty smelly commoners when you could be living the good life in your mansion. Archer has der right idea. Fuji: Laurent and Gage lock up in the middle of the ring...lightning fast armdrag by Gage and both men up quickly...Laurent charges...right into a Japanese armdrag!...Gage up and Laurent backs off a bit holding his arm...Laurent moves out of the corner...Collar and elbow tie-up...Laurent with a wristlock and he twists it for added pressure...Gage flips over and now kips up and reverses the wristlock and has Laurent screaming in pain and he reaches for the ropes to cause a break. Drake: Dat ist der smart way to escape a hold. Not flipping around like some kind of circus monkey. Fuji: Gage moves in and Laurent ducks through the ropes and yells at referee Jonathan Smalls to move Gage back...Laurent steps out on the apron and yells out to the fans...Gage up from behind he spins Laurent around and snapmares him back into the ring...Laurent up and Gage with an irish whip...Spinning leg lariat by Gage!! He covers 1...2...Laurent kicks out. Drake: He von't be able to vin on one move alone. Fuji: Gage up and Laurent with a rake of the face!...Laurent now rams Gage's head into the turnbuckle and hits a backhand chop! Laurent once again yells out to the fans and sends Gage into the opposite corner...Gage catches himself and leaps up...he hooks a charging Laurent around the head with his legs and spins into a flying headscissors!...Laurent heads to the floor after that great move. Drake: Bah! Just more acrobatic nonsense! Fuji: Gage isn't done either...he hits the second rope...Spring board Somersault Plancha!!! I can't believe it! Great move by Gage and now he pulls Laurent up and throws him into the ring...Gage up on the apron...Slingshot legdrop! He covers...1....2....no! Laurent managed to kick out somehow...Gage now locks on a half Boston crab. Drake: Come on you purple haired freak fight!! Fuji: Nice cheerleading there "Baron"...Laurent makes it to the ropes and Gage breaks the hold...Gage pulls Laurent up...Laurent with a thumb to the throat!...He scoops Gage up and slams him! Elbowdrop by Laurent...He covers and hooks the tights...1....2....kickout by Gage! Laurent is complaining about a slow count to Smalls and Gage rolls him up from behind...1...2....thr...Oh! that was so close! Both men up...Laurent with a clothesline...Gage ducks it and runs Laurent into the ropes for a rollup, but Laurent holds onto the ropes and Gage falls back. Drake: Smart move by der Frenchman...Now he needs to grab a chair and hit Gage in der head vith it! Fuji: Laurent tapping his head and telling all the fans just how smart he is...Gage from behind...German Suplex! Drake: Nein! He is from England! Fuji: 1....2....Laurent kicked out! Drake: That vill teach him to try und use a move invented in der Fatherland. Fuji: Gage quickly up and he is heading to the top rope! Laurent up too though and catches Gage on top...Laurent catches him with a couple of right hands and now climbs up to the second turnbuckle...We might see a superplex here...He tries to get him...No!..Gage blocks it and drops Laurent off the buckles with a front face suplex...Gage now off the top....Flying Splash....He covers 1....2....Laurent got his foot on the ropes! Drake: Great ring presence by der "Designer". Now he needs to grab a chair. Fuji: Would you just forget about chairs! Laurent with a chop and now a kick to the midsection...Laurent with and irish whip...He catches Gage in a spinning sidewalk slam!...Laurent up and he is signalling for his finisher the "Lariat Designed by Laurent"(Flying clothesline)! Gage to his feet and Laurent comes off the ropes...Gage catches him and falls back with a Hotshot!! Laurent hit throat first across the top rope and he is out...Gage pulls him up and sets for a suplex...he brings him up...he spins him...and drives him down into a tombstone piledriver!!! He hit the Steiner Screwdriver or Michinko Driver and it's a move he calls the "Deep Blue"....He covers 1....2....3!!!! (Ding, Ding, Ding) Heineman: Your winner....William Madison Gage!!!! (The crowd explodes with cheeres as Gage stands triumphant in the ring.) Fuji: What a great night of action fans. I sure hope you join us next time for more EEF action on European Warriors. For "Baron" Wolfgang Von Drake I'm Rick Fuji saying goodnight. (Fade out)
