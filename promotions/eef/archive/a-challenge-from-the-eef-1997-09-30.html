A Challenge from the EEF

30 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen is black.) SPEAKER: This November the EEF continues one of the oldest traditions in Professional wrestling… (We cut to the Munich Cup, it's two feet tall and filled with D-Marks. The camera circles around it.) SPEAKER: The most prestigious wrestling tournament in the world will return… Wrestlers from all over the world will come to the EEF locking up with the top 16 EEF superstars in a 32 man single elimination tournament. (We cut over to a title belt. A man grabs it and throws it in a trash can.) SPEAKER: This isn't about titles. This is about honor. And money! The winner recieves 500.000 D-Marks and there's more. (We cut over to a big battle royal. The action is hot and furious.) SPEAKER: A big 30 man battle Royal… And there's more… (We cut to footage of the Hangman's Jury squaring of with the Wild Beasts.) SPEAKER: The EEF Tagteams will lock up with tagteams from all over the world in a big 16 team tagteam tournament. (We cut to the Olympiahalle in Munich.) SPEAKER: It's the biggest PPV in the year. THE MUNICH CUP IS BACK BIGGER THAN EVER!!!!!! A FOUR PART PPV SPANNING OVER A WHOLE WEEK BRINGING YOU THE BEST WRESTLING TALENTS FROM ALL OVER THE WORLD!!!!!!! (We cut over to the logo of DSF Action and Sky Sports.) SPEAKER: Order it today at 1-900-Munichcup For more info contact the EEF http://home4.swipnet.se/ ^w-45120/Index.htm
