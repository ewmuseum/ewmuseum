The Referee is breaking up the holds. LUKE: Both men going for their finishers desperate to get the job done in time. 20 seconds left. JOHN: They lock up, and an armdrag by Muscatone. He lets go of the arm and a beautiful Belly-To-Belly-to-belly-suplex. No L'Ordonnateur blocks it. LUKE: Ten seconds. (DING!! DING!!! DING!! FRANK: The time for the match has expired. There for neither of them will advance to the next round. This match is a draw. (First big boos from the fans as they would have liked to see a winner of this one, then big cheers as the wrestlers head back for the dressing room.) JOHN: What a shame! But a great display of wrestling skills and stamina from both men. LUKE: Yeah for once I have to agree with you. And do you know what this means? JOHN: No! LUKE: That Archer recieves a bye for the next round to! He has reached the semifinals with out even wrestling a match. In my opinion that makes him the favorite to win the European Heavyweight Title. JOHN: Well perhaps it does. Let's wrap up the first round. In the first match we saw The Cavalier advancing after beating Dusty Rhodes. LUKE: He didnt impress me. That was a slow and boring match. I dont think he will go all the way. JOHN: In the second match we saw Jeff Wrobleski beat a Schlotzhauer after Dean put up a brave fight after recieving a nasty cut to his forehead. LUKE: Well after that cut I think loosing was the best thing that could happen to Dean. There was no way he was going to go all the way with a cut like that. This way at least he didnt get seriously hurt. JOHN: What do you think about Wroblseki? LUKE: Well its hard to tell with a man like him. He could go all the way but then again he could attack someone in crowd and get qualified. He's totally unpredictable and a nightmare to wrestle. I think he will demolish the Cavalier.
