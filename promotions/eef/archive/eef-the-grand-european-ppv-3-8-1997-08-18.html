[EEF] The Grand European PPV (3/8)

18 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The European E-Wrestling Federation http://home4.swipnet.se/~w-45120/Index.htm FRANK: The next match is the 6th match in the first round. Introducing first, hailing from Chicago, USA. Weighing in att a superb 275 pounds. THUNDER!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Thunderstruck by AC/DC starts to play and the fans start to sing along..) Fans: AH, AH, AH, AH, AH, AH, AH, AH..THUNDER!....AH, AH, AH,,, AH,, AH,, AH,, AH,..THUNDER!...THUNDER! THUNDER! (Thunder comes out from the back...He is 6"5', 275 lbs., He is wearing long black pants, with Thunderbolts going down the sides of the legs, he wears a black mask with a thunderbolt in the middle of the mask. He is a well built man with a huge upper body forming a V shape and though his wrestling tights you can tell he also has well built leg muscles....He starts high fiving the crowd makes one pass around the ring then enters the ring) JOHN: One of the brightest stars in the EEF. LUKE: Oh yeah? I heard he was pretty dumb. FRANK: And his opponent, weighing 326lb, from Sacramento, California, "Holocaust" Kevin Chambers! ("Sweet Dreams" by Marilyn Manson begins to play and thousands of boos can be heard as Kevin Chambers walks out.) John: Not a popular man this Kevin Chambers. Luke: No but a powerful man. (Ding!! Ding!!) JOHN: They lock up. Chambers with a Go-behind into a full nelson. LUKE: HA look how Chambers is toying with the rookie. JOHN: Well it he wants to toy perhaps he should have locked his fingers. Thunder breaks out of the hold and retaliates with a rock hard chop to Chambers chest. The fans love it and Thunder now with a Go-behind and a bridging back suplex by Thunder. Did you see how he picked up the big man? ONE!!! TWO!!! JOHN: Kickout by Chambers. LUKE: Well I aint going to dispute that Thunder is powerful or talented. He is. But he just makes to many rookie misstakes and Chambers will make him pay for that sooner or later, you just mark my words. JOHN: Thunders starts working the fans. And gets nailed by a forearm to the back. LUKE: See!! JOHN: Thunder whips Chambers into the ropes and Chambers burst back with a Running Forearm Smash. And a cover. ONE!!!! TWO!!! JOHN: Kickout by Thunder who gets back up. But a headbut by Chambers sends him back down. Chambers with a knee to the back of Thunder. Thunder screams in pain. LUKE: Sure thats more than 300 pounds behind that knee. JOHN: Thunder gets up Wow he catches Chambers with a spinebusterslam. ONE!!!! TWO!!! JOHN: Kickout by Chambers. Chambers catches Thunder with an inside cradle. ONE!!! JOHN: Kickout by Chambers. Both men back up Thunder with a chop. And another and the fans go wild! Another chop and Chambers is realing. He manages to grab Thunders arm and whips Thunder to the ropes. POWERSLAM BY CHAMBERS!!!!!!!!!! LUKE: Awesome! ONE!!! TWO!!! JOHN: Thunder gets his right shoulder up. Chambers throws Thunder out of the ring and follows him back out. Oh, Thunder took a bad fall, landing on his head. LUKE: Goodnight Thunder! We all know what kind of damage Chambers can do on the outside. This will be great! JOHN: Chambers picks up Thunder and starts hammering him with lefts and rights. LUKE: I dont hear those fans now! I guess Holocaust shut them up! JOHN: Thunder with a Go-behind and a Full Nelson. He has it locked on good. LUKE: What is that idiot doing? They are on the outside of the ring. JOHN: The referee is up on five now. Thunder keeps the hold and Chambers is begining to go down. SIX!!!! LUKE: Release the hold you idiot! SEVEN!!! JOHN: Well he hit his head pretty hard when he was thrown out, I'm not sure he knows where he is. He's fighting for his life. EIGHT!!!! JOHN: Chambers starting to push Thunder backwards, but still cant escape the hold. NINE!!! JOHN: Chambers makes them crash into the ringpost. But Thunder keeps the hold and Chambers is sinking fast now, Just look how he's knees is shaking. TEN!!!! (DING!!! DING!!!) JOHN: Both men are counted out. And Chambers is out cold. Thunder releases the hold. He enters the ring and the fans cheer for him. LUKE: Well they can cheer if they want that wont help him, he was counted out. That idiot costed Chambers the match. Only a rookie could do such a stupid thing. JOHN: The referee explaining for Thunder that he has been counted out and Thunder is livid. LUKE: He's livid! It was his own fault! JOHN: Well both men counted out so neither man will advance resuliting in Gage recieving a bye in the next round. A tough match, Thunder the moral winner in my opinion. LUKE: Are you crazy! Moral winner. He costed Chambers the match. JOHN: Well Thunder works the crowd again and they cheer him as he heads for the dressing room. LUKE: I hope Chambers will go after him and make him pay for this.
