The European E-Wrestling Federations FAQ The European E-Wrestling Federation http://home4.swipnet.se/~w-45120/Index.htm A proud member of the GIWA Reginald Humphries Jr. (Owner and President of the EEF and member of the GIWA board of directors.) [detail withheld] Table of Contents: 1 Introduction 2 EEF Info 2.1 Handlers 2.2 Creating a wrestler 2.3 The EEF Money System 2.4 Jobbermatches 2.5 Our Cards 2.6 Our PPV's 3.1 Match Strategies 3.2 Sneakattacks 3.3 Flashes 3.4 Injuries, Suspensions and periods of Inactivity 4 Rankings and Titles 4.1 How the titles are defended 4.2 Rankings 5.0 Contacts ____________________________________________________________________________ 1 INTRODUCTION E.E.F. is a brand new fed. It's owned and run by Reginald Humphries Jr. ([name withheld]). I created this fed because I was tired of most of the fed around. I wanted to create a different fed, and with different I don't mean a hardcore fed because god knows there's enough of them allready. So I created a fed that will allow you to develop angles and enjoy roleplaying. The federation takes place in Europe mainly because I live in Europe and it's easier for me to give a good description of Europe than America. To help me with the fed I contacted people who had experience of running feds themselves so they knew what they were getting themselves into. They all have experience of writing cards and creating angles, that means that even if I myself should have to be out of town for a week or two the fed still will have three other persons able to make sure the fed keeps running. Unlike the majority of the E-Feds around that tour the world the EEF is a regional fed located in Europe. We don't have any World Titles we have our own regional belts and recognize the GIWA world titles. To find out more about the history of the EEF read the EEF history page. 2: EEF INFO - The E.E.F. is a BOOKED league, meaning the EEF board votes on to see who wins the matches. The votes are based on four things. * The quality of the flash sent in prior to the match * The wrestlers statistics which every handler must fill out before joining. * The match strategy sent in for the match. * Other factors like if someone is going to sneak attack a wrestler involved in the match, if a wrestler has bribed the referee etc. You get the picture. We are four people who vote on the matches. The majority wins or if there isn't a majority the Prez makes the call. 2.1 HANDLERS Handlers are allowed one single wrester or one tagteam. This rule exists to promote interaction among the players. 2.2 CREATING A WRESTLER As the EEF is a European fed were looking primarily for European wrestlers but some other wrestlers will be accepted to ad some color to the federation. Each handler is allowed to have one single wrestler or one tagteam. The limits for wrestlers are: height - no taller than 7'3 , no shorter than 5'0 . No heavier than 450 pounds, no lighter than 150. The EEF is a original wrestlers only fed. No real wrestlers will be accepted. Ok if you name your wrestler Desmond "The Hitman" Harris he will be accepted. But if he starts acting and talking like Bret Hart he wont be accpeted. Each handler must sign an exclusive EEF contract for his wrestler. This only effects the wrestler, the handler is of course allowed to compete with other wrestlers in other feds. This rule exists to try and create some sort of logic in the e-wrestling world. If this was the real world a wrestler wouldn't be able to compete in a U.S based fed and a European based fed at the same time. So he won't be able to do it here either. The bottom line is that we want good original wrestlers. 2.3 THE EEF MONEY SYSTEM The EEF also have a money system it works like this. When you sign an EEF contract you recieve 40.000 D-Marks per month of the contract. You will also recieve money from your matches. A win will give you 20.000 D-Marks A draw will give you 10.000 D-Marks A loss will give you 5.000 D-Marks Jobbermatches will give you half of the normal money. Well that's how you earn it now on to how you can spend it. *You can go on a trainingcamp. That will cost you 80.000 and make your wrestler inactive for a week. But after that week you can increase one of your categories with one point or ad a new move to yur repetoir. * Rumour has it that one of the referees in the EEF is accepting bribes, I don't know if this is true but if it is perhaps he can be persuaded to take some money and look the other way while you hit your opponent with a chair. Rumour even has it that one of my VP's is on the take. I seriously doubt this but if it's true perhaps he could see to it that you become the Special guest referee in one of your enemies matches or propel you into a titlefight. But beware if you attempt to bribe a member of the EEF staff and I find out about it you will get a heavy fine! * Perhaps you would like to increase your fanstatus by giving some of the money to charity? That will probably give you more support from the fans. * Buying extra flashes. The EEF will finance one flash per wrestler per week. (And of course prematch comments.) But perhaps you want to send in an extra flash before an important match to give you an edge over your opponent. Well you can but in that case you will have to pay the camera crew yourself. An extra flash will cost you 40.000 D-Marks. Yes I know it's more than you earn from a fight but if it's for a titlefight it could be worth it. More about flashes under the flashes section. * Pay fines. If you are a rulebreaker, if you attack the fans, the referee, ambush other wrestlers, run away from a match etc. You will be fined. The size of the fine depends on the "crime". Don't get me wrong I will allow sneakattacks it's just that you will have to pay for them. Besides if your fined your wrestler will have something to complain about! * Other things, who knows what you can come up with? Well you do! Perhaps you're going to buy that car you allways dreamed of. Perhaps your going to buy favors from other wrestlers? Be creative! If you come up with a great way to spend your money and tell me about it I will probably allow it! 2.4 JOBBERMATCHES This is something every "real" fed have. It's a way to let the superstars shine! So we have them to. And you write them yourself, but don't worry it's not as hard as it sounds. Just write what happens, we will then ad the commentators and edit the match into one of our cards. This will let us get to know your wrestler better, what moves he prefers etc. You can use one of the EEF jobbers that we have listed on our Jobberspage or create a jobber yourself. Just make him original, no real wrestler. And remember you only have to write one jobbermatch per month for your wrestler, you recieve money for it and you gain ranking points from the matches. So of course if you want to improve your ranking or earn more money you can write more than one jobbermatch a month. Of course other wrestlers may sneakattack you during your jobbermatch and you may sneakattack other wrestlers during their jobbermatches we will ad that to the match if a sneakattack strategy is sent to us. All matches will be listed three days prior to the card so you will have the time to plan attacks etc. 2.5 OUR CARDS The EEF have two cards. The European Warriors, a weekly show held at the Royal Albert Hall, on Tuesdays which consists of the jobbermatches and one or two Superstar matches, and EEF's Touring Main Event, a biweekly show comming from different arenas all over Europe. Pitting the best superstars against each other. Excactly where will be listed on our homepage so you'll know where your wrestler will be. In July 97 EEF's Touring Main Event will become a weekly show. Each card features an interview segment. "The Classic Corner" with "Classic" Andrew Archer on European Warriors and "Rapping with the Rock" with Luke "The Rock" Anthony on EEF's Touring Main Event. To get interviewed there you must contact them so you can work out how the interview will be. 2.6 PPV'S The EEF will have 4 EEF PPV's a year. One is allready set for November. The Münich Cup which you can read more about on our homepage. We will also have a PPV in July to crown our champions. But that's not all. The fact that we wont have more EEF payperviews doesn't mean that we won't have more PPV's as stated earlier the EEF is a member of GIWA so we will have crossover PPV's with other GIWA feds as well as letting you take place in their PPV's. A few other feds have also contacted us about crossover PPV's but nothing is concrete as of yet. We will keep you updated. 3.1 FLASHES This is one of the most important things if you want to be succesful in the EEF. Being descriptive, being true to your carachter, developing storylines, and writing your flashes like movie clips will get you far. As in real life swearing will be edited out of the flash as will "smart" comments. With smart comments we mean using words like jobbers etc. that real wrestlers don't use. What we are looking for in the flashes is good roleplaying Each handler can send in on flash per week and one flash per card (Pre match comments etc.) The handlers send in the flashes to the Prez the flashes are then edited together and massmailed out to the rest of the fed or if prematch comments added to the card. The one flash per week rule exists to make sure that everyone gets the same opportunity. The only way to send out more than one flash per week is if you pay for it as explained in the EEF Money System section. *Important* No scenes of child abuse, sexual abuse, sexual assault, rape, or racism will be accepted. This is not a warning rule.. one time and you are OUT. 3.2 MATCH STRATEGIES Sending in a match strategy will give you an edge over your opponent. This is especially helpful if your opponent didn't send in anything. A strat can be something like this. Adrian Norton will not try to match power with "the Elk" Erik Hanson He will use his speed to out maneuver the bigger man. He will hit as many high flying moves as possible. Dropkicks, Flying Elbows, Spin Wheel Kicks, etc. He will also go for pinfalls whenever the big man is off his feet (hopefully stunning "the Elk" Erik Hanson with a Dropkick off the Top or something similar). If he gets in trouble, he will roll out of the ring, hoping for a breather. If "the Elk" Erik Hanson comes out after him, he will get back into the ring asap. They can be more detailed or less detailed. All strats must be sent to me two days prior to the car so I have the time to include them into the card 3.3 SNEAKATTACKS I also want strats for sneakattacks. If you just write that you want to run into a match and attack someone then the sneak attack will most likely fail, but if you write a detailed plan on how you're going to do it, it will probably work. But beware sneakattacks can lead to fines. If the plan involves several handlers make sure both or all parties confirm it with me. All Sneakattacks must be sent to me two days prior to the card so I have the time to include them in the card. 3.4 INJURIES, SUSPENSIONS AND PERIODS OF INACTIVITY In this game we pretend that wrestling is a real sport. So you if your unlucky you may be injured. Don't worry, we won't have any fatal injuries or tear limbs of your wrestler. But you may break an arm or sprain an ancle some injuries may be so serious that you must be out of action for a while, some may be minor injuries that will allow you to continue to wrestle while you recover. But if you step into the ring during that time the injury may effect your wrestling skills and other wrestler may take advantage of it. Injuries will mostly take place to build heat or if a handler need a break from e-wrestling. - Suspensions. Ok this sounds like a big one but it will mostly be used to develop angles. If I'm going to suspend a wrestler I will first talk with his handler and explain why the wrestler is going to be suspended and what angle I have planned to see if it's ok with him. If not he won't be suspended. During supension the wrestlers are allowed to flash but not to wrestle. Of course he won't recieve payment during his suspension. The only things that can get a handler suspended is if he is mean or rude to another handler or if he uses our mailing list to advertise without my permission. - Periods of inactivity. I know that everybody cant spend their entire life on the net. So if you're going on a holliday just drop me a note and I'll take your wrestler out of action during your absence. But if you don't drop me a not you risk loosing matches while you're away. 4.0 RANKINGS AND TITLES The E.E.F. has 2 Singles Titles and 1 Tag Team Title. The EEF European Heavyweight Title This title is the most wanted and highest ranked title in our fed. To become the European Heavyweight Champion is the greatest achievment you can make in the EEF. The EEF German Title This title is equivalent to a U.S title or an Intercontinental title. It's the second highest ranked title in the EEF. The EEF Tag Team Title The title all the tagteams are competing for. 4.1 HOW THE TITLES ARE DEFENDED Titles must be defended at least two times a month and on every PPV or Supercard. Titledefenses on PPV's and Supercards can count as one of the two monthly defenses. They must also be defended against a top contender or it doesn't count as a defense. Topcontenders for the European Heavyweight title are those ranked 1-5. Topcontenders for the German Title are those ranked 1-10. Topcontenders for the Tag Team Title are those ranked 1-4 . You can, of course, still give unranked wrestlers a shot at the title(s) but that wont count as one of the mandatory title defenses. You are also allowed to compete in non-title matches but should you loose such a match the wrestler who beats you will be named the number one contender for your title. If the German Champion defeats the European Heavyweight Champion, he must give up their original title to the Board of Direcors (who will determine a match or matches for a new champion). If a wrestler is 1/2 of the EEF Tag Team Champions, they may ALSO hold a singles title at the same time. (He would be busy though.) The EEF Champions themselves have most of the say in who they defend their title against as long as they follow the guidlines. There will undoubtably be challenges from the other handler's wrestlers, and you may accept or reject them. If there are no challenges, then throw one out yourself. I'm sure most wrestlers wouldn't mind getting a title shot. However, you will also be able to compete for the GIWA World titles. They will be decided in September 97 and the EEF will send their topcontenders both single and tagteams to compete for the titles. If a EEF Titleholder should win a GIWA Title(s) he would have to vacate his EEF Title(s) because he won't have the time to defend both titles as he will have to tour all the GIWA feds, staying two weeks at the time in each fed defending the GIWA title against the local topcontenders. However if he looses the GIWA title he will automatically recieve a title shot for his old title. 4.2 RANKINGS Rankings are computed after each wrestling card. The ranking system will give you: 1 point if you beat a jobber. 0 point if you draw or loose against a jobber. 3 points if you beat another EEF Superstar, 2 points if you draw against another EEF Superstar 1 point if you loose to another EEF Superstar 5 points if you beat someone ranked #5 or better. 3 points if you draw against someone ranked #5 or better. 1 point if you loose against someone ranked #5 or better 10 points if you beat a champion 5 points if you draw against a champion 2 points if you loose against a champion The Tagteamwrestlers are a special case in the rankings, however. Whenever a tagteamwrestler competes in single action, the outcome is based in the single rankings. If he a tagteamwrestler fares well in single competition he can be a contender in both divisions. Basically, what he does in each division is totally independent of the other. The top 5 ranked EEF wrestlers are also the top 5 EEF contenders for the GIWA World title. And the top 3 EEF Tagteams will be the top 3 EEF contenders for the GIWA Tagtitle. 5 CONTACTS: You may contact the other wrestlers in the EEF to get to know them, set up teams, stables, feuds, etc. Make sure that once you set something up, that both (or all) of the parties confirm it with me so I know. Directly if they have a e-mail address listed or via the president. If you want to contact someone via the President just write who's the message is for, then I'll see to it that it gets to the right person. If the message are in the form of a roleplay they will be treated as such. Well if you read the FAQ and liked it you might as well send in an application.
