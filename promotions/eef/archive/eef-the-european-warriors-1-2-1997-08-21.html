[EEF] The European Warriors (1/2)

21 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE EUROPEAN WARRIORS [The show opens to "The Families of Montague and Capulet" from Romeo and Juliet by Prokofjef as we see a European map. A circle of stars appears on the screen and starts spinning as the background turns blue. Then the EEF letters appears before the image changes into the European Warriors logo. Cut to an exterior shot of the Royal Albert Hall. Cut to the inside of a jampacked Royal Albert Hall. We reach the announcer desk where commentator John Fitzsimmons wearing a black tuxedo and his short grey hair neatly combed, and former wrestler now turned commentator Luke "the Rock" Anthony dressed in a brown suit white shirt and a pink bandana. and tie with his long black hair tied in a ponytail. The music fades away as the two EEF commentators start to speak.] JOHN: LIVE from the Royal Albert Hall! The EEF making it's return following the Grand European PPV. A PPV in which we saw the first EEF Tagteam Champions, the first EEF German Champion and the first EEF European Heavyweight Champion being crowned. And what an event it was. The Monarchy whom we will see in action here tonight stealing the Tagteam titles in the final against the Itallion Stallions. LUKE: The didn't steal it they outsmarted the Stallions! I loved how Queen Victoria whacked Antonio with her sceptre for the win. JOHN: Whatever. And the Cavalier Josef Tadeuscz winning the EEF European Heavyweight Championship against Archer. LUKE: Archer had the match won! Tadeuscz just got one lucky shot in. JOHN: Well enough about that let's get to the ring and announcer Frank Heineman. (We cut to the ring we're we find Frank standing by ready to announce the match.) FRANK: Ladies and gentlemen, the next match is scheduled for one fall. Currently in the ring, weighing 284 lbs, from Bruxelles, Belgium. "The Eurocrat" Hans Van Der Fluyt!! (As always Van Der Fluyt is arguing with the fans. He shouts something about the new european currency and the fans boo him. He's wearing a gray suite and a dark tie, looking perfect). FRANK: And his opponent, making his EEF debut tonight. One of the most succesful European wrestlers of all time. He comes down the aisle at a weight of 328 lbs, accompanied by his manager Steve 'Money' Martin. From Bochum, Germany: "Normal" Klaus Lehmann!!!! (With the first sound of "Bochum" by Herbert Groennemeyer the fans goes crazy. It seems like the European fans really have missed him. A few fans hold signs up in the air. One reads "The world is Normal". Normal enters the arena with a big smile on his lips waving to the fans. One step behind him we see 'Money' Martin in a deepblue Armani suit. Both men look great. As always Lehmann wears his heavy black leather jackett with the letter "N" and all european national flags printed on the back. As he enters the ring he slips out of the jackett and gets his shirt off. Then he follows his own tradition and throws the shirt into the audience). (DING!!!DING!!!DING!!!) JOHN: There's the bell. The debut of Klaus Lehman. LUKE: Both men watch each other like hungry lions. This will be a good test of just how good Normal really is. I personally never recognized his self proclaimed greatness. JOHN: Self proclaimed? He's not a bragging kind of guy. He has paid his dues all over the world and now he has come to the EEF. They lock up. Normal with an armdrag on the belgian. The Eurocrat tries to escape and Normal whips him into the ropes. And a Beautiful clothesline by Normal!!!! LUKE: Wow, I mean pretty good. JOHN: Normal picks him up and hoists him high up in the air! Vertical Suplex by Normal and the fans go bananas as Normal manhandles the Eurocrat. LUKE: Hey this one is far from over. The Eurocrat is a cagy veteran. JOHN: Normal to the ropes. Big splash by Normal and a cover. Referee Harvey Smith on the job. ONE!!!! TWO!!! JOHN: Kickout by the Eurocrat! Normal picks up the Eurocrat and whips him into the turnbuckle. Wow Normal is really fired up tonight wanting to impress the EEF crowd. LUKE: Well that can be a big misstake. I hope he don't underestimate the Eurocrat. JOHN: Normal goes for an avalanche but Van Der Fluyt moves out of the way. NORMAS MISSES AND HITS THE POST!!!!! LUKE: Ha! Van Der Fluyt now with a couple of European Uppercuts and a big right hand that sends Normal down! JOHN: He puts on his arrogant smile and turns around to the fans telling them to stick it. The fans react with boos! LUKE: Well what do you expect from those primates? JOHN: Normal gets up. Small Package by Normal!!!! ONE TWO!!! JOHN: THR.......Kickout Roundhouse right by Normal sends the Eurocrat down. He grabs the left leg of Van Der Fluyt and slaps on his NORMALIZER!!!!!! LUKE: NO! JOHN: The Eurocrat screams in pain. This submission hold is very painful. He won't be able to survive very long in this one. ......... ......... ......... JOHN: No, Van Der Fluyt gives up!!!!!! Normal Klaus Lehman wins his debut! (DING!!!DING!!!DING!!!) FRANK: Ladies and gentlemen, here's your winner, "Normal" Kla...... (Lehmann grabs the mic. 'Money' Martin enters the ring and stands left to Lehmann) NORMAL: Hi everyone.........We are back!!!!! (The fans start to cheer) NORMAL: You know......Van Der Fluyt......where I come from.......peoples don't wear silly ties......they work hard!!!!!! But.......I guess you learned your lesson. (Big reaction in the audience) NORMAL: So folks.....this victory has been.....WAY..........TOO........EASY!!!!! Mr. Humphries, I want some more!!! I wanna prove to everyone what I'm all about. I want a handicap match! Gimme two of your guys.....not next week......not tomorrow......I want 'em tonight!!! In one hour I'll step into this ring again.....and then I want two guys out here to fight with. And I really don't care who it is!!!! And now to all my fans out there: ICH BIN WIEDER DA!!!!!!!!! (To the sound of "Bochum" Normal and Martin leave the ring and head towards the backstage area. The fans give 'em an enthusiastic good-bye). JOHN: Wow how about that for a debut! LUKE: So he wants to do a handicapmatch huh? JOHN: I just got word through my headset. The president has given his ok for the match. Well I guess we will see more of Normal tonight. LUKE: He's crazy he will be creamed! JOHN: Well Normal isn't the only good German wrestler in the federation up next is the Iceman Dean Schlotzhauer. But before that one let's listen to some comments from him recorded earlier. ---------------------------------------------------------- [The scene opens inside a gym located in Bonn, Germany. "The Iceman" Dean Sclotzhauer is inside the gym working out on a tread mill. Dean is wearing gray sweat pants, a white t-shirt, and a pair of running shows. He is very sweaty. He turns off the tread mill and grabs a towel to whipe of his forhead. A forehead taped as a result from the stiches he recieved after the Grand European PPV. He then walks over to a near by table and grabs his water bottle and takes a drink. He places the bottle back down and looks at the camera putting the towel around his neck. Dean begins to speak.] Dean: Well, let me start by saying that I am glad that president Humphries is feeling better and that The EEF is up and running again. Now, I have noticed that I am slowly sliding off the ranks. At one point in this federation, I was ranked as the number one contender, but now it seems that I just keep sliding down further and further. I have decided that if I want to regain my number one contendership, I am gonna have to get some matches. So, as of now, I have a contract signed with my name on it. Anyone who wants to face me, sign on the dotted line. It is a good old fashoned one fall match. After I am through with all these matches against whoever signs my contract, I will be going to training camp for about a week and improving my skills so I can win a title. The first thing I will do when I come back from training camp, however will not be a title match, but a match that I have wanted for many months. I want to face Ray" My Despisal" Stevens in a no disqalification, no count out, pinfalls count anywhere match! Ray Stevens, every night when I lay in my bed, I am haunted by how you have insulted the fans of The EEF and how you have made my life miserable. So, Ray Stevens, you can do the manly and finish this fued once and for all, or you can turn your tail and run like so many times before. Speaking of the fans, I am sorry that I could not capture a title. I will win a title, you can mark my words, I will. Josef Tadeuscz, congratulations, you deserved that title. Archer, I don not really like you, but congratulations. I hope that you will give me a shot some time. Well, that is all the time I have. I have a plane to catch. [The scene fades black as Dean gathers his things and heads out the door.] ------------------------------------------------------------------ JOHN: Words from a disapointed Dean Schlotzhauer. He was unfortunate to recieve that cut against Wrobleski in the tournament. If it hadn't been for that he could have gone far. LUKE: Bah, he's not even worthy to shine Archer's shoes. JOHN: Your impossible! Let's go to the ring. FRANK: Currently in the ring. The Hungarian Hitman Lazlo Bogdan. And his opponent. Hailing from Bonn, Germany. Weighing in at 217 pounds. The Iceman Dean Schlotzhauer! (The fans starts to cheer as the German National Anthem starts playing Dean walks out dressed in black tights and black wrestling boot, with his short black hair slicked back. He enters the ring and gets ready for the match.) (DING!!DING!!DING!!) JOHN: There's the bell. Bogdan charges towards Dean who takes him down with an armdrag. Bogdan gets up and another armdrag by Dean. I've never seen Dean as focused as he is tonight. LUKE: Well now he has to compete for the German fans against Klaus Lehman. JOHN: Bogdan gets up and a Inverted Atomic Drop by the Iceman!!!!!!! LUKE: Ouch! JOHN: Dean turn him around and an Atomic drop sends Bogdan down again. Wow, great wrestling abilities being displayed by Dean tonight. You know what? LUKE: No. JOHN: I just thought about it. What a great tagteam he and Normal would make. LUKE: Bah they would be destroyed by teams like the Dragon Legion or the Monarchy! JOHN: Scoop slam by Dean! Dean now starting to stomp away on Bogdan. LUKE: That Bogdan is really hairy. JOHN: Stop, that's not very nice! LUKE: He could be used as a carpet for crying out loud. JOHN: Dean now whips Bogdan to the ropes and a running forearm by Dean! Wow! Is he on top of his game or what? LUKE: Well he needs to be if he goes around challenging Ray "My Idol" Stevens. JOHN: Well the two of them have quite a history between themselves. LUKE: Dean is going to be all history if he steps into the ring with Stevens again. JOHN: Well that's your opinion. Dean with a Tombstone. Wow what a maneuver. LUKE: Of course it's my opinion! I said it didn't I? Why would I voice somebody else's opinion? JOHN: And now he slaps on the German Clover Leaf on Bogdan! Bogdan submits emediatly. (DING!!DING!!!DING!!!) FRANK: And your winner! The Iceman Dean Schlotzhauer!!!! (The fans start cheering as the Iceman heads back to the dressing room area.) JOHN: Impressive win by the Iceman. I don't even think he broke a sweat. LUKE: Well, wrestling The Hungarian Hitman is one thing. Wrestling Ray Stevens is a completely different thing I grant you that. JOHN: Look! Here comes EEF President Reginald Humphries Jr. (The EEF president, dressed in a black suit with his thinning grey hair neatly combed walks to the ring and gets hold of a microphone and starts to speak.) REGINALD: I want to take the opportunity to talk to you the great fans of the EEF and the great wrestlers of the EEF! I am back in action and I have a few things to say. For a couple of months now the EEF has been a gathering place for the finest wrestlers in the world. (Big Cheers from the fans.) REGINALD: Yes we have wrestlers from all of the world. Europe, North America and even Australia! Yet there is a certain federation… A itsy bitsy pathetic little federation called the UWF that insists on badmouthing us. (Big boos from the fans.) REGINALD: They insist on badmouthing our wrestlers!!! (Even bigger boos from the crowd.) REGINALD: Well it's got to stop! They have picked the wrong federation to mess with. Because you don't mess with the European E-Wrestling Federation. The Federation with the best wrestlers in the world! (Great Cheers from the fans.) REGINALD: So I have decided to take no more of their crap. If it's a war they want it's a war they are going to get! I here by declare war on the UWF!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Great cheers from the fans.) REGINALD: This won't be a war between Europe and the U.S. It's a federation war. A war that will prove that the best wrestlers in the world no matter if they are from Europe, Australia, U.S.A or Pakhistan or what ever comes to the EEF! (The fans are now on their feet cheering for the President.) REGINALD: Now on with the show! (Reginald Humphries leaves the ring accompaigned by big cheers from the crowd in the Royal Albert Hall.) JOHN: Well it has been developing for quite some time now. And now it's finally here. LUKE: Ha! The UWF will be an easy match for the EEF. The UWF wrestlers are out of shape, overpayed and in for a real rude awakening should they dare to step into the ring with the EEF superstars. FRANK: Currently in the ring, the Albanian Assasin!!!! (Boos from the fans.) FRANK: And his opponent hailing from Seattle Washington.. Weighing in at 238 pounds! RAY!!! YOUR IDOL!!!!!!!! STEVENS!!!!!!!!!!!! (The fans start booing as Stevens heads for the ring. Dressed in black wrestling tights and a white T-Shirt saying "I'm Starrs idol!") LUKE: I love it here's my Idol! And look do you see his T-Shirt? JOHN: Well I don't think UWF wrestler Devon Starr will like that one bit. LUKE: Why? It's true! (Stevens enters the ring and charges the Albanian Assasin before the bell.) JOHN: Stevens with a kick to the midsection and a forearm to the Albanian Assasins back and down goes the Assasin. (Intense booing for the fans.) (DING!!! DING!!! DING!!!) LUKE: That's my Idol. Doesn't waist any time just jumps straight into the action. JOHN: Vicious kicks now by Stevens. LUKE: He's destroying the Assasin with those kicks. Go Stevens Go!!!! JOHN: Stevens pulls him up by the hair and whips him to the ropes. Stevens greats him with a powerful clothesline as he comes back and down goes the Assasin. STEVENS: GET UP YOU LOOSER!!!!!!! LUKE: Ha, you just gotta love the Idol! JOHN: The Assasin struggles to get up but is sent down again by another vicious kick to the head from Stevens. Stevens picks him up and whips him to the ropes again. Elbow by Stevens and down goes the Assasin. LUKE: Stevens at his best! JOHN: Stevens pulls him up and dellivers a standing dropkick!! LUKE: Beautiful dropkick by Stevens!!!! JOHN: Stevens climbs the turnbuckle and screams to the fans. STEVENS: THAT'S WHY I AM YOUR IDOL!!!!!!! JOHN: The Assasin slides out of the ring. LUKE: He's trying to escape! The coward! JOHN: Stevens leaves the ring and catches the Assasin. DDT STEVENS!!!!!!! LUKE: I love it! JOHN: The Assasin is out cold. Stevens gets back into the ring climbs the turnbuckle. Oh this spells trouble for the Assasin. (Stevens points to the commentators and screams.) STEVENS: THIS ONE'S FOR YOU LUKE!!!!!!!!! JOHN: STEVENS JUMPS OF THE TURNBUCKLE WITH HIS FALLING DOWN FLYING KNEE DROP! HE CONNECTS!!!! LUKE: I'm flattered! JOHN: Stevens really squashed the Assasin with his former finisher. Stevens now pulls the Assasin up by the hair and takes him back into the ring. LUKE: I think he's sending a message to both the EEF and the UWF right here tonight. JOHN: I think so too! Stevens starts hammering the Assasins head in the turnbuckle. One, Two, Three, Four, Five. LUKE: I love it! JOHN: Stevens now finally stops… HEARTPUNCH BY STEVENS!!!!!! And a arrogant one finger pin by Stevens as Louie Falcotta starts to count. ONE!!!! LUKE: He will never come back after that one! TWO!!! THREE!!! (DING!!! DING!!! DING!!!) FRANK: And the winner of the match. RAY YOUR IDOL STEVENS!!!!!!!!! LUKE: Impressive win by My Idol Stevens!!!!! JOHN: Let's listen to some comments recorded earlier by the new EEF European Heavyweight Champion Josef the Cavalier Tadeuscz shown on BBC 3 yesterday. LUKE: Oh no, not the polish sausage. JOHN: Stop it! ---------------------------------------------------------------- [The scene opens in a news studio. Behind a desk, two commentators sit. One is a tall black man named DAVIS, the other, a thin white woman with black hair named KIRSTEN. The news anchors for the BBC3 evening news. Behind them is a picture of Josef Tadeuscz, EEF European Champion, holding his belt high in the air at the Grand European Tournament.] DAVIS: This was the sight, as the EEF crowned it's first ever European Champion. But what's next on Josef "The Cavalier" Tadeuscz's agenda? BBC3 reporter Sandra Wills is on location in Nysa, Poland, with a report. (cut to Nysa, a college auditorium, where Josef speaks in Polish to an assembled crowd.) SANDRA- Hard to imagine a European community including the Eastern Block countries from so long ago. But with Reginald Humphrey's EEF, superstars such as the Dragon Legion and Josef Tadeuscz have been given a chance to proudly represent their countries. And in Josef's case, show them that his championship is one won for the people he loves. Here in Nysa, a collegiate style wrestling tournament is held yearly, drawing competitors from the Czech republic, Hungary, and former states of the Soviet Union such as Latvia and the Ukraine. Josef, a former competitor, has been taking time to talk to the young competitors throughout the country about winning, the EEF, and lessons in life. JOSEF (subtitled)- When you begin, it is never easy. My success now comes after months, years of failure. What matters is that you only fail truly if you give up. If you keep trying, you will not be held back long. SANDRA- Josef has stopped in several cities including his hometown of Lodz, Krakow, and Gdansk, before stopping here in Southern Poland. Skill takes you so far, but faith, and a people's faith in heroes, carries you a long way.
