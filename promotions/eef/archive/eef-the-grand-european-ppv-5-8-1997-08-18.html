[EEF] The Grand European PPV (5/8)

18 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JOHN: What an evening we've had so far, Luke. LUKE: You said it, John. Who would have expected Kegonite back in the ring at all, let alone in the tournament? JOHN: As we start the second round, you know we have quite a group, and honor roll if you will, of wrestlers from literally around the world, competing for the European Title. LUKE: No doubt about that, John. Archer and Gage from England, Tadeuscz from Poland, Ray "My Idol" Stevens from the US...and Wrobleski from "The tree". He's an odd one. JOHN: What about Kegonite? He's come back with a fury tonight. LUKE: You can quote me on this one, John. He's gone off the deep end. He's forgotten what pain is, and he might well be our first heavyweight champion....I don't know if even the Classic one, Andrew Archer, could hold him down for long. JOHN: Well, he's facing Ray "Your Idol" Stevens after this matchup. Let's go to the ring for the introductions. ("One" by Metallica begins and the fans begin to boo. Some cheer wildly as Jeff "The Rocket" Wrobleski makes his way to the ring.) LUKE: Will you listen to those cheers? JOHN: I don't get it...this guy s seriously dangerous, seriously nuts.. and yet he's getting quite a following here in the EEF. LUKE: It's the same thing that happened with George Steele almost 20 years ago. You go nuts, you get one hell of a following. FRANK: Introducing first....from "The Tree" Weighing 312 lbs. Jeff "The Rocket" Wrobleski! (Rocket enters the ring. Theme music changes to "The Child Inside" by Qkumba Zoo and the fans begin to cheer as Josef "The Cavalier" Tadeuscz makes his way to the ring with his manager, Lady Solidarity) FRANK: And his opponent. Accompanied by his manager, Lady Solidarity, from Lodz, Poland, weighing in at 260 lbs. Josef "The Cavalier" Tadeuscz! (Fans cheer as Tadeuscz enters the ring) LUKE: I just don't get this guy Tadeuscz. He's pretty plain as a wrestler, not as technically skilled as the Classic or as crazy as Kegonite, not big like Demon Mankuma or small and skilled like Iceman Schlotzhauer. How does he rate? JOHN: He's a fan's wrestler, Luke. He gives his all every time he gets in there and the fans really get behind that. (DING!!!DING!!!) JOHN: Rocket out of the corner, Josef barely gets out of the corner in time to avoid the splash, rolls him for a pin, but not even a one count. Rocket is up and drills Tadeuscz with a running lariat. LUKE: Is it me, or is Tadeuscz usually dwarfed by his opponents? Bonecrusher, Wrobleski...only person he's wrestled that's his size is the Classic One. And he couldn't get him down. JOHN: Wrobleski stomping on Tadeuscz's head as he's down, Tadeuscz is stunned by the sheer ferocity of the attack, and Wrobleski to his knees, bouncing Tadeuscz's head off the mat! LUKE: Wrobleski took down a tough competitor in Schlotzhauer, and he's not letting Josef get onto his feet to get any offense. This is good strategy for the wild man, as he lifts Tadeuscz in a front face lock. WHOA! Drops him in a front face slam, and covers him for the pin. ONE!!!!! TWO!!!! JOHN: ..kickout by Tadeuscz. Wrobleski right on him with a headbutt to the back of his head, and Josef rolling to the corner. LUKE: Wrobleski following him...looks like he's biting Tadeuscz's shoulder! Tadeuscz with a head butt to break it. JOHN: Uh-uh. You aren't going to get him with a head butt. Wrobleski irish whip and follows him in, BIG splash and Tadeuscz is really hurt. LUKE: I smell Upset here. Hey, John, could you imagine Wrobleski and Kegonite going at it next round if they both win? JOHN: Forget the ref, and just let them tear each other in half. LUKE: (laughing) You said it. Tadeuscz now lifted into a bear hug by Wrobleski, and this will wear down the Pride of Lodz even more. Lodz? Is that an Elks Lodz or a hunting Lodz? JOHN: Oh, give me a break, Luke... Lady Solidarity there cheering on her charge and leading the fans. LUKE: Now that's not fair. Wrobleski hasn't got anyone like that cheering him on. What he needs to do is carry a tree to ringside to root for him as well. JOHN: Tadeuscz fighting back, bellring clap! Wrobleski grabs his ears Tadeuscz with a standing dropkick! Another! And Wrobleski goes down! LUKE: Unbelievable strength of will by the young Tadeuscz against the big man. He's pumping to the crowd, off the ropes as Wrobleski gets up, cartwheels and hits him with the bencao! JOHN: What a comeback! Both men look hurt now, Tadeuscz just moving a bit faster to his feet, but a shot to the midsection by Wrobleski. LUKE: This is a tough, tough match for both competitors, John. I don't like either's chances for the European Titlematch. JOHN: Both William Madison Gage and Andrew Archer getting a bye this round. How about that, Luke? Archer gets to the semis without getting in the ring. LUKE: That's going to produce our next champion, I can feel it. JOHN: Wrobleski still hammering on his midesction, now to his feet, signalling he's going for the headbanger! Grabs Tadeuscz by the hair, no! Tadeuscz into the full nelson! LUKE: Wrobleski already starting to power out of this... JOHN: Kosciusko! LUKE: Bless you. JOHN: No, Tadeuscz hits him with the Kosciusko! LUKE: Then he should be disqualified. Hitting a man like Wrobleski with a sausage won't... REG: Give me a break Luke. Tadeuscz is up! The fans cheering him for the Polish Pride! He has Wrobleski Positioned. LUKE: But can he get the big man up? He's been beaten on pretty badly by the Tree man. REG: He lifts him...no...he tries again....no. He lifts him and gets him up! Slams him down! The Pin! ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!! (DING!!!DING!!!DING!!) LUKE: Unbelievable! Tadeuscz pins the Rocket after a major major beating. Let's go to the announcer for the official word. FRANK: Your winner. Josef..."The Cavalier" Tadeuscz! REG: What a matchup, Luke. LUKE: Yeah, but this matchup has my eye even more. Coming up next, Ray, "My Idol" Stevens, vs. the newest madman in the EEF...Tomas Kegonite. JOHN: Right now, let's hear what some celebrities are saying about the matchups tonight. Who do you want to see win the European heavyweight title? (Cut to montage.) DON MURACO: You know, I spent the better part of 15 years in the ring...And there ain't NOBODY I'd like to see win the title more than a man after my own heart. Ray Stevens, You are definately "My Idol" TOMMY LASORDA: Too bad Thunder can't win it. That good old American know how is just what we oughtta be seeing. I guess I have to pick William Gage, just because he knows how to treat the people. HUGH GRANT: Er, well...I..uhm...I think that...if anyone...were..that is, if anyone were going to be...the top wrestler...in all of Europe...I would say it should be Josef Tadeuscz. He's...uhm. He's really good. PRINCE ANDREW: Royal greetings from Buckingham Palace. Tonight a new champion is scheduled to be crowned in the EEF. Classic Andrew Archer, we're all rooting for you. Do your country proud. CHRIS FARLEY: Tomas Kegonite! He's nuts! He's gonna tear everyone apart in this tournament! YEAH!!!! LUKE: Who the hell invited Hugh Grant? JOHN: Some good celebrity votes of confidence for our superstars. LUKE: Who invited Hugh Grant? That's like asking Dame Edna. JOHN: Well, since you mention it, our guest Announcer for this next match... LUKE: Oh, don't tell me... DAME EDNA: HELLO! (the fans cheer.) DAME EDNA: Oh it's nice to be here. Especially with this next matchup. Introducing first... from Seattle Washington in the United States. Weighing in at 248 lbs. A lovely boy, if I do say so myself, Ray, "Your Idol" Stevens! (FANS mix cheers and boos as Stevens walk to the ring) JOHN: Fans getting more and more behind Stevens...just for this matchup, I don't think anyone wants Kegonite to win. LUKE: John, what is Dame Edna doing in the ring? DAME EDNA: And his opponent. From Berlin, Germany. Weighing 267 lbs. A real nasty person with a bad attitude, Tomas Kegonite! (Welcome to the Jungle" by GNR plays as Kegonite runs to the ring amidst the fans boos.) LUKE: Kegonite to the ring and Dame Edna getting out of there as quick as he/she can. Maybe Kegonite isn't all bad. (DING!!!DING!!!) LUKE: There's the bell, and the match is underway. JOHN: Match or brawl? Kegonite and Stevens just in the middle of the ring trading punches. Stevens with the advantage backs him into the ropes and throws him across the ring, drills him with a clothesline! LUKE: Kegonite grabs the leg of Stevens and pulls him down, biting it! Referee Harvey Smalls lays the count down and Kegonite breaks the hold. JOHN: What made Kegonite flip like that? LUKE: I dunno. He's from a tremendously successful wrestling family, maybe he just wanted to break out on his own. JOHN: Kegonite with a step over toe hold here, putting the wrench on Stevens. From his debut though, as a referee, to his matchup against Orson Edmonds, he was not the most stable of individuals. LUKE: You're right there, John. Stevens grabs Kegonite's hair and monkeyflips him over! JOHN: Good move there by a man who hates scientific wrestling. It's somehow fitting that he and Schlotzhauer made flip sides of the same coin. That's a rivalry that won't be ending soon. LUKE: Stevens with a side headlock on Kegonite into a bulldog. Stevens is just working him over now. I like Stevens. He gets the job done. No muss, no fuss, just gets the job done. I'd like to see him holding some title once the night is over. JOHN: Lifts him again and screaming! Kegonite is biting him on the side! LUKE: That's one way to get out. Tyson approach. JOHN: Lifting Stevens into a side suplex, coming down hard. Stevens is hurt. LUKE: And Kegonite gets up and waves to the crowd. He's one tough individual, there's no doubt about that. JOHN: Stevens up but hit with an iron claw hold. Stevens wanted Kegonite in the ring, as revenge. If he loses now, he loses all his chances to avenge himself the loss to Schlotzhauer months ago. LUKE: Stevens fighting back as the fans cheer...reverses the claw into an Armlock, Kegonite lets go, and Stevens shaking that armbar! JOHN: Kegonite on his knees! Stevens wants to hurt him! LUKE: Elbows to the arm twist and Kegonite is roaring! I've never seen Stevens so revved up! JOHN: Lifting Kegonite, slams him on that twisted arm! Again, lifting him into a short clothesline, and covers him for a pin! ONE!!! TWO!!! LUKE: He screamed and rolled off him! Kegonite bit him again! JOHN: Referee warning Kegonite, who just pushes him aside...you don't want to do that, big guy. LUKE: Grabbing Stevens by the throat! Slamming him into the corner over and over again! Referee Harvey Smith trying to keep order! JOHN: WHOA! Did he ever hit Smith! Smith from across the ring signalling for the bell! It's over! (DING!!DING!!) LUKE: Not for Kegonite! Stevens is going limp here, and he has to face Josef Tadeuscz in only a few moments! JOHN: Fans cheering...here comes Holocaust Kevin Chambers! Here comes Ice Cold Jake Borg! They're in the ring and getting Kegonite off Stevens! Kegonite on his way to the dressing room with Chambers and Borg in hot pursuit! LUKE: Harvey Smith has had a rough debut tonight. 3 matches, and one knockout. But there's your winner, Ray Stevens, by Disqualification. JOHN: Stevens looks out of it. Fans, When we return, Josef Tadeuscz vs. Ray "Your Idol" Stevens i the Semifinals. And then, a match that we've all been waiting for. Andrew Archer, the self proclaimed Classic One, will face William Madison Gage. Stay tuned! VO: Four federations. One Alliance. One World Champion. The Global Wrestling Council's Tournament "Battle for the Belts", coming soon. JOHN: What a tournament that will be. The five wrestlers from the EEF to enter this tournament will be announced after our final round matchup which will feature one of these four wrestlers. Josef "The Cavalier" Tadeuscz Ray "Your Idol" Stevens "Classic" Andrew Archer William Madison Gage LUKE: Yeah and now it's time for the second round of the tagteam tournament. JOHN: Yes. Second round action about to happen let's get to the ring with Frank Heineman FRANK: The next match is scheduled for ONE FALL!!!! Coming down the aisle, weighing a combined 503 lbs, THE HANGMANS JURY!!!!! ("Dangerous Tonight" by Alice Cooper plays as Judge Wilson and the Executioner slowly walk to the ring.) LUKE: These guys are awesome. They will destroy the Stallions tonight! FRANK: Their opponents, weighing in at 461 pounds, THE ITALLION STALLIONS!!!! ("The Freshman" by the Verve Pipe plays as the Vega Brothers run to the ring giving high fives while the fans cheer.) JOHN: Both these men are tired from their matches earlier and we all know that the Jury beat the Stallions on the Touring Main Event a week back. This is gonna be tough for the Itallion Stallions. LUKE: Tough? The Jury is going to massacre these youngsters! JOHN: The Stallions jump the Jury before the bell. Franco throws the Judge out of the ring and Antonio dropkicks the Executioner to the ground. Franco leaves the ring. (DING!!!DING!!!) JOHN: There's the bell so it looks like it will be Antonio Vega and the Executioner who will start it off. The Executioner nails Antonio with a backhand chop. Antonio comes right back with a right hand but the Executioner doesn't move an inch. The Executioner takes Antonio down and with a headlock takedown. He hangs on to the headlock, no, Antonio slips out of it! Now they're rolling around in the ring. This is a wild one. Executioner with a single leg takedown. LUKE: Executioner now with a leg drop across Antonios head. That will teach the rookie some respect! JOHN: The Executioner picks up Antonio, and sends him back to the ground with a rock hard kneelift. Luke: He just introduced Antonio to his knee! He's being polite JOHN: Cover by The Executioner. ONE!!!! TWO!!!! JOHN: Kickout by Antonio. Quick count by Falcotta. Executioner twists Antonio's arm and tags the Judge who comes in a lays some right hands right in Antonio's chest. SWINGING NECKBREAKER the Judge with a cover!!! ONE!!! TWO!!!! JOHN: No, Kickout by Antonio. Another fast count by Falcotta. The Judge locks on a Boston Crab and Antonio is hurting. LUKE: Now this is vintage Jury! First the Executioner softens up their opponents, overpowering them, taking them down, then a tag to the Judge who comes in with great submission holds to weaken them up even more. JOHN: Antonio finally reaches the ropes but there's no break. What is this Antonio is holding on to the ropes and Falcotta doesn't do anything!!!! LUKE: Perhaps his out of position. JOHN: Out of position!?!?! He's standing right beside the ropes. LUKE: Well perhaps his blinded by a camera flash or something. JOHN: Well the Judge finally releases the hold. He looks at Falcotta with a surprised look on his face. I wonder what is happening in that ring. LUKE: Well that hold took a lot out of Antonio. The Judge grabs Antonios leg and drags him back in the center of the ring and re-applies the hold. Franco jumps on the top rope and nails the Judge with a dropkick. What!!! He's not supposed to be in the ring!!!! JOHN: Falcotta fast on the job and tells Franco to leave the ring. Antonio is trying to make the tag. The Judge tags in Ex. Ex grabs Antonio's leg and drags him back in the center of the ring. The Ex nails Antonio with a elbowdrop across the knee. They are destroying Antonio! LUKE: and I'm loving it!!!!!! JOHN: Ex with an Irish Whip, Antonio to the ropes, he ducks the clothesline, comes of the ropes and FLYING FOREARM by Antonio!!! LUKE: No!!! JOHN: Both men down. Antonio still dazed from the beating he has taken this far. Both men crawling towards their corners. Ex is getting the tag to the Judge!!!! And Antonio tags Franco!!! Franco comes in and nails the Judge with a running lariat wow!!! Cover!!!! ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!! JOHN: NO!!! Not enough!! Franco picks up the Judge and nails him with a backbreaker and sets him up near the turnbuckle, what's he going to do? LUKE: Hopefully leave, he's ugly as sin!!! JOHN: Franco climbs the top rope!! SHOOTING STAR PRESS!!!! WHAT A MOVE!!!! ONE!!!! TWO!!! JOHN: Save by the Ex!!! He enters and pulls Franco of the Judge! LUKE: That was too close!!! JOHN: Franco gets up and nails the Ex with a dropkick that sends him over the top rope to the outside. Judge is getting back up Franco turns around and the SUPERKICK!!! Cover by Franco. ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!!! THREE!!!! (DING!!!!!DING!!!DING!!!!!) FRANK: Your winners........THE ITALLION STALLIONS!!!!!! JOHN: The Stallions with the big upset win! What a performance by the talented Franco Vega, who took matters in his own hands and beat the Hangman's Jury!!!! LUKE: Well okay, he has some talents. JOHN: He sure has, he could be a major superstar in the EEF!!! And with his more calm and collected brother he could go far tonight! They are in the finals tonight and if they win there will be big celebrations in Rome! LUKE: Well I never thought they would get a win over the Jury!!!!!!
