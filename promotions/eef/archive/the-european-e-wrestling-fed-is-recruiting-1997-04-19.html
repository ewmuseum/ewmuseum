The European E-Wrestling Fed is recruiting.

19 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, first things first. My name is [name withheld] I live in Sweden(Europe), I'm 23 years old and work as a stand-up comedian. I have been watching wrestling since 1985 and seen allmost every major Pay-Per-View since then. My e-wrestling experience: I have handled the Radiant One Roland Ulv and Rabid Randolph Richards and a tagteam called the Sons of RA in the following feds. The MWCW, The WWWA, The MWWFand The UWF. Ulv has held the following titles. MWCW Powerhouse title (Two times) The WWWA I.C title The MWWF I.C Title The MWWF European Title (Current champion.) Richards is newer to the game but has held: The MWWF U.S title. The Sons of RA is also new to the game (three matches.) But is the current MWWF Tag Team Champions. As a card writer I have been writing payperview results for the MWWF. (The long ones with a lot of description nad lots of commentating.) Well that's who I am. On to the EEF. The EEF will be a "booked" fed. I want to create a fed with only top quality handlers. I will screen the applications Each handler will be able to have one single wrestler or one tagteam. I myself won't handle any wrestlers, I will handle the EEF jobbers. But you will never loose to them unless you've bee extremly lazy. They are a part of the fed only to entertain you. Each handler must write one jobbermatch for his wrestler or tagteam per month. It's not as tough as it may seem. You will only write what moves are performed etc. I'll edit the match putting in commentaries etc. All wrestlers must sign an exclusive contact with the EEF stating that he won't be competing in another fed duirng the contract period (1-3 months.) This is an attempt to stop the absurd current EEF situation were the same wrestler competes in different feds often on the same the night. Ok, I know it's only a game, but I think it would be pretty cool if we had at least some realism. I won't have 36 ppv's a year either. As most feds that has many PPV's have lame PPV's. I think it's better to have few but sensational payperviews. Were longtime feuds finally get's settled. So if your intrested to be a part of a different fed, and want to get to know more about the EEF. Visit the European E-Wrestling Federation Homepage and check it out. But remember this is only the first draft of the page. I have 10 megs availabel so we will be able to do alot with it. Cardwriters or persons who want's to be a part of the EEF staff. Mail me and tell me about yourself, your past e-wrestling experience, and a sample of what you want to work as, A magazine, A cardsample etc, you get the picture. The EEF http://home4.swipnet.se/~w-45120/Index.htm If you have any questions mail me at this address: [detail withheld]
