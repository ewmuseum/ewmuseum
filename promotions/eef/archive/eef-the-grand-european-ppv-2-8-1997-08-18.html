[EEF] The Grand European PPV (2/8)

18 August 1997

The European E-Wrestling Federation http://home4.swipnet.se/~w-45120/Index.htm (Michael Bradford enters the ring and gets a microphone.) MICHAEL: I have an announcement to make. Recently EEF president Reginald Humphries Jr was injured at the hands of Mr. Tomas Kegonite. (Fans start booing as they hear Kegonites name.) JOHN: I hope they punish that lunatic good. MICHAEL: So in the absence of Mr. Humphries I have made a tough decission. I have come to the conclusion that Mr. Humphries was wrong when he kicked Mr. Kegonite out of the EEF. JOHN & LUKE: WHAAAT???????????? MICHAEL: So I have reinstated Mr. Kegonite and signed a new contract with him. (Big boos from the fans.) JOHN: I dont believe it! MICHAEL: I have also decided to give him a place in the Tournament. LUKE: What? I hope Mr. Humphries sees this. He's going to fire that Bradford guy for this. JOHN: I dont believe it. He ends referee Jonathan Smalls career, he gets kicked out of the EEF and he puts the EEF President out of action and now he's competing for the titles? MICHAEL: SO With no further delay, the third match in the first round. Hailing from Berlin Germany, Weighing in at 267lbs. ("Welcome to the Jungle" by Guns and Roses starts playing and the fans gets out of their seats to boo the man about to head for the ring.) MICHAEL: TOO-MAAS KEEG-OO-NIITE!!!!!!!! (Tomas Kegonite walks out, dressed in black shorts and black boots, and an arrogant smile on his face. He seems to enjoy the booing from the fans and the fact he's back in action. He looks into a camera and starts talking.) KEGONITE: I told you, that you wouldnt be able to keep me out! JOHN: Well have to see about that once the President gets back. MICHAEL: And his opponent. Hailing from Dublin Ireland Danny McGee!!!!! (The Irish National Anthem starts playing and Danny McGee heads for the ring, dressed in green tights and kneesavers.) JOHN: A concerned look on his Danny's face. LUKE: Well wouldnt you be concerned. JOHN: He enters the ring but is jumped by Kegonite. Kegonite with a roundehouse right and a bodyslam. And now he locks on a sleeper. (DING!! DING!!) LUKE: Kegonite started a little early but now the match is under way. JOHN: Look thats not a sleeper, its a chokehold. Come on ref! LUKE: Danny starting to sink pretty fast now. JOHN: Well he isn't getting any air. Come on Falcotta! But the referee isn't doing a thing. LUKE: ARE YOU BLIND FALCOTTA! CHECK THE HOLD YOU MORON!!!!!! HE'S CHOKING HIM!!!! JOHN: Falcotta is lifting McGee's arm. It drops… He lifts it again.. JOHN: I don't believe it. Kegonite is choking him and Falcotta is letting it slide. It drops a second time. LUKE: Perhaps he's afraid of Kegonite. Remember what he did to Jonathan Small. He lifts again. It drops a third time and Falcotta signals for the bell. (Ding!!! Ding!!) JOHN: Outrageous! Kegonite, a man who shouldn't even be in the tournament advances after obvious cheating. (Welcome to the Jungle starts to play again. And the camera zooms in on Kegonite.) KEGONITE: The EEF is my playground! You couldnt keep me out. Now I'm going all the way. (Then he heads back for the dressing room. Danny McGee wakes up and slowly gets back up on his feet.) JOHN: Kegonite advancing to the next round and he says he will go all the way. LUKE: HE WILL NOT GET HIS HANDS ON A TITLE! IF IT COMES TO IT I WILL TAKE HIM OUT MYSELF!!!!!! JOHN: Wow, I would like to see that. Lets get ready for the 4th first round match. FRANK: The next match is the fourth match in the first round. Set for one fall with a ten minute timelimit. Introducing first. Hailing from Seattle, USA weighing in at 238 pounds... Ray "Your Idol" Stevens. ("Mombius Hibachi" by The Melvins starts playing as Ray "Your Idol" Stevens enters and heads for the ring. Wearing black short tights, black knee savers and black boots. Well built with shoulderlength blonde hair. He wears a T-shirt with the text "Hail your Idol". The fans are booing.) JOHN: Stevens looks angry. LUKE: Well he should be, he doesn't like Kegonite better than I do. FRANK: And his opponent, hailing from Mobile, Alabama, USA. Weighing in at 324lbs The American Nightmare Terry Brody!!!!!!! (All Hell's breaking loose by KISS starts playing and Brody with long Scraggly hair and beard. Brown eyes. Not fat but not muscular. Dressed in Brown trunks, brown west and kneehigh Brown boots heads for the ring.) JOHN: Terry Brody, a huge man, who was very impressive in the royal rumble we had a couple of weeks ago. LUKE: He sure was, you know what. These two men in the ring are perhaps my two favorite wrestlers in the EEF. JOHN: Thats no surprise I think everyone knows that from your commentating the last couple of weeks. (DING!! DING!!) JOHN: They lock up and Stevens takes Brody down with a low blow. The referee warns Stevens about the lowblow. Brody gets up and gets slapped by Stevens. LUKE: This are two different styles. Brodys awesome power against the king of mind games Ray Stevens. JOHN: The slap infuriating Brody who charges in with a clothesline but Stevens ducks beneath it and hits Brody with a jab. LUKE: Stevens has to stick and move in this one. He cant match power with Brody. He must try to keep the big man off his feet. JOHN: Another clothesline by Brody. Stevens ducks again and nails him with another jab. Brody moves in and this time he catches him with a big forearm smash. LUKE: A Crushing Forearm smash by Brody. JOHN: Brody now with a bearhug. LUKE: Oh that is going to squeese alot out of Stevens. JOHN: Stevens screams in pain and struggels to get an arm between himself and Brody. LUKE: Just look at the arms on Brody! They are huge! Stevens has to get out of this hold fast or else he can kiss his titledreams goodbye. JOHN: Stevens unable to get his arm in instead a facerake by Stevens and Brody releases the hold. Brody moves back covering his face and a kneelift by Stevens. LUKE: There's no smarter wrestler than Stevens. JOHN: Brody stays on his feet. Clothesline by Stevens, Brody still wont go down. Stevens runs into the rope to gain speed and here he comes.. LUKE: And down Stevens goes after a devastating shoulderblock by Brody. JOHN: Brody now planting a big legdrop on Stevens. A cover. ONE!!! JOHN: Kickout. Brody picks up Stevens he's going for a bulldog but a lowblow by Stevens slows him down. Stevens with a Belly-to-back suplex. Both men stay down. LUKE: Well that took a lot out of both men. It's not easy to pick up a man Brody's size. JOHN: Both men trying to get back up. Stevens gets up first and nail Brody with a kick to the head that sends the big man down. Stevens now with a chokehold against the ropes. Henderson counts 1.. Stevens releases the hold. Brody tries to get up but Stevens nails him with a double-axehandle chop. LUKE: Stevens needs to work on Brodys legs. JOHN: Elbowdrop by Stevens. Kneedrop by Stevens who's really punishing the big guy. Another elbowdrop no, this times Brody rolls out of the ring. LUKE: He needs a breather. Stevens was on a roll. JOHN: Baseball slide by Stevens knocks Brody into the guardrail. Brody is down and Stevens follows him to the outside. Brody gets up. HEARTPUNCH BY STEVENS!!!!!!!!! ONE!!! LUKE: Ouch. He really connected with that one. TWO!!! JOHN: Stevens slides back into the ring. THREE!!! FOUR!!! FIVE!!! JOHN: Brody is still out cold. SIX!!! SEVEN!!! JOHN: Brody starts to get up. EIGHT!! JOHN: BRODY IS STARTING TO CRAWL AGAINST THE RING.. NINE!!! JOHN: He gets his hand on the apron and tries to get up, will he make it in time? LUKE: Yes he does. Brody reenters the ring in the last split second. Stevens picks him up and whips him into the turnbuckle and here Stevens comes charging in. Big boot by Brody! Stevens goes down. JOHN: Brody to tired to capitalize on the advantage. He slowly moves towards Stevens. There a cover by Brody. ONE!!! TWO!!! THRE!!! JOHN: NO! STEVENS RAISING HIS SHOULDER JUST IN TIME!!!!! LUKE: That was close!!!!!! JOHN: Brody picks up Stevens. Left hand by Stevens! Another left hand no Brody blocks it and a short clothesline by Brody and another cover. ONE!! TWO!!! JOHN: Kick out by Stevens. Brody complaining about a slow count. Whats the time? LUKE: Well they are past the eight minute mark. They have to finish this one soon or else neither of them will advance in the tournament. JOHN: Stomachbreaker by Brody, he turns him over and another cover. ONE!!! TWO!!! JOHN: Kickout! Brody picks up Stevens and Stevens rakes his eyes. Right hand by Stevens. Stevens goes for another right hand but Brody blocks it. And here comes a right hand from Brody, No Stevens catches him in an inside cradle!!! Henderson on the job. ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!! (DING!!DING!!) JOHN: Stevens gets the threecount! LUKE: A tough match. Both men showed a lot of hate. Brody comming back after the Heartpunch JOHN: And in the next round he will face a man he hates. Tomas Kegonite. That could be a classic! LUKE: Well a valiant effort by Brody but he came up short in the end. I feel sorry for him. I hope Stevens kicks Kegonites (Bleep.) JOHN: Luke!! FRANK: The next match is the fifth match in the first round. Introducing first hailing from Norway, weighing in at 260 pounds. He is ICE COLD JAKE BORG!!!!!!!!! (Running with the devis by Van Halen starts playing and Jake, with short black hair and a black goatee , dressed in black tights and black boots, starts heading for the ring on his way he meets Stevens. They shake hands before Jake continues to the ring.) JOHN: What was that all about? LUKE: You'll find out sooner or later. FRANK: And his opponent hailing from Liverpool England Weighing in at 245 pounds. WILLIAM MADISON GAGE!!!!!!!! (OPIUM, by Jump little children starts playing and there are big cheers as Gage, dressed in blue wrestling pants heads for the ring. He high 5's the fans on his way to the ring.) (DING!! DING!!) JOHN: Borg attacking Gage before the bell with a forearm shot. Hiptoss by Borg no Gage turns it into a backslide. Falcotta on the job. ONE!!! TWO!! JOHN: Kickout by Borg. Borg back up and nails Gage with a low blow. Faceslam by Borg! LUKE: I love it! JOHN: Borg pick up Gage and executes a kneebreaker. LUKE: Smart move, if he takes out Gages legs there will be no fancy highflying. JOHN: Borg whips Gage to the ropes, Gage comes back and nails Borg with a clothesline. Borg gets up again but Gage takes him down with a cartwheel kick. Gage goes for a DDT but Borg counters it with a backdrop. Borg turns around and is caught by a jumping side kick. LUKE: I cant believe the speed of these two. JOHN: Yes, a very fast paced start of the match. LUKE: Gage goes for a German suplex, but Borg counters it with an elbowsmash. Borg runs into the ropes. He comes back and misses with a clothesline. JOHN: Gage able to duck down in the last second. But Borg grabs his head and takes him down with a faceslam. LUKE: Ha, the more he mess up Gages face the more I love it. JOHN: Borg picks him up and executes a jawbreaker. He picks him up and a bodyslam by Borg. Borg now going for the pin. ONE!!! TWO!!! JOHN: Kickout. Great wrestling being displayed by Ice Cold Jake Borg. Borg now whips Gage into the corner and starts going to work with him with vicious chops to Gage chest. LUKE: Wow! They heard those in the back road. Gage goes down. JOHN: Borg picks up Gage and whips him into the ropes. And here comes Gage with a high cross body block, but Borg catches him. Backbreaker by Borg Unbelievable!!!! LUKE: WOW! I've never seen Borg so impressive as he is tonight. He's dominating Gage. JOHN: Borg with a leg hammerlock on Gage but Gage reaches the ropes. Borg picks up Gage again. Wow a jumping DDT by Gage. LUKE: I dont think he hit it on the spot. JOHN: No, it doesnt look like it. Borg is getting up. Sunsetflip by Gage!!!!! ONE!!! JOHN: Kickout by Borg. They get up and a STANDING DROPKICK by GAGE!!!! He moves in behind Borg and slaps on a sleeperhold. Jawbreaker by Borg.! LUKE: HA!!!! JOHN: Borg whips Gage into the ropes, flying cross body press by gage, but Borg rollsover into a pinning position. ONE!!! JOHN: Kickout by Borg. Borg back up and he nails Gage with a European uppercut. Borg goes for a facerake but Gage blocks it and shoves Borg away. Standing dropkick by Gage but Borg moves out of the way. Borg now whips Gage into the ropes Gage comes back and Borg goes for a hiptoss but Gage counters it with a backslide. ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! (DING!!DING!!!) JOHN: Gage gets the threecount and advances in the tournament. LUKE: Ah, he was so close! He dominated the entire match. This isnt fair! JOHN: Well fair or not fair. Gage advances to round two.
