ASWF (in-house storyline)

24 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It looks like the ASWF is really heating up. What will ASWF stars do to rid this fed of the VICELORDS. LG Cool, Tha Doggfather, Comatose Killa, and El Diablo have destroyed all that the ASWF was built on. The stars of ASWF are going to have to stand up against these thugs and take this fed back. Chonan, X-Factor, and Marc Mission have all stated that it is their mission too take the VICELORDS out. While Narceses Santogos and Chico El Gringo continue to rule the Tag Team Division, Aaron Carpenter and Enigma have shown that they are a top contender to the titles that the Latin Suaves (Santogos and El Gringo) have held for nearly 7 months now. How about that Man in the Box match the other night. It was between X-Factor and VICELORDS leader Stinger. To everyone surprise, it was revealed to be Chonan. For all that don't know, here is a recap. LG Cool, Tha Doggfather, and Comatose Killa entered the ASWF saying that there leader was yet to be named. It seemed obvious that it was X-Factor. I mean, he had turned on his long time friend, Marc Mission and cost them the Tag belts. In front of 25,000 fans, the VICELORDS invited X-Factor into the ring. He was followed out by Chonan and Marc Misson. Marc was in one corner and Chonan in the other, as the VICELORDS stood in the middle with X-Factor. The offered him leadership, as they stuck out there hands to skake. Chonan and Marc tried to convience him not to do it. He went to Marc, whispered something and then pushed him back. He shook their hand, but then pulled back and put the X-File on LG Cool. He had set them up. But, Chonan, ASWF World CHampion, had set the ASWF up. He was the leader all along. The VICELORFDS jumped X-Factor and Marc Mission. Chonan fell from grace when he lost his World Title, which Stinger then took over VICELORDS. Then it all leads up to Chonan helping X-Factor in his Man in the Box match against Stinger.
