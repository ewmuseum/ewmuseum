ASWF — News

Welcoming Press Conference

22 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Traffic roars by a large glass tower with a large 'ASWF' sign hanging from high above. Several late-showing fans and members of the press rush into the revolving door as security keeps back several hippie-ish looking protesters who seem angered by the "destruction of llamas" by the ASWF. Horns loudly screech as the screen fades to black.]
[The ASWF logo appears on the screen as the slogan, "Tradition Rules!" slowly seeps onto the screen as a deep voice bellows, "TRADITION RULES!"]
[Several hundred press members and fans have piled into the small but plush lobby of the ASWF Complex. A raised stage has been erected at back of the lobby on which a long table draped in a large ASWF Tradition Rules logo. In the center is a large black podium with a microphone. To the left sits J.
Clear, Paul Toth, and Rikky Bull. To the right sits Clyde Robinson and ASWF
Head of Security, Mr. Big. However, the seat nearest to the podium is left vacant. J. Clear and Rikky Bull make their way to the podium.]
Clear: Ladies and gentlemen... I'd like to introduce myself. My name is J. Clear. I have been with this company since its inception in 1991. I am proud to say that not only am I not only the best annoucer in the business...
Bull: Next to me of course, Clear... next to me... Clear: ... not only the best annoucer in the business... but also the best annoucer in the best wrestling organization in the business! [the fans cheer as the press looks on with confused looks]
Bull: Hold on, Clear... hold on, Clear... now... nothing personal to the ASWF, but we haven't had a single match for over a year and let's keep in mind that you haven't annouced a damn match since your little accident! Your wife was not too please, was she, Clear?
Clear: Will you stop! Ladies and gentlemen... I have the honor of WELCOMING
YOU BACK TO THE TRADITION! WELCOME BACK TO ASWF! [again, the crowd cheers, the press looks confused, but still snaps photos. A fan in the back holds up a
"CLEAR IS A SHMUCK" sign.] Unfortunately, the new chairman and president of the company has been unable to appear. Frankly -- we don't know who he is. We were really looking forward to finding out! Nonetheless, we'd like to thank him or her, whoever it may be, for bringing what we love back! [a few of the fans begin to clap] At this time, I'd...
[Mr. Big stands up, sighting a man making his way through the crowd, sporting an expensive black suit with slicked back hair pushing his way through the crowd. As a result of the whispers of the fans, we realize that it is
"Ironfist" James Paxton.] Clear: Mr. Paxton -- what are you doing here? Mr. Big, please? [Mr. Big delays his action as Paxton pushes his way into the spotlight.] Paxton: Why else would I be here, Clear? I am the big star of this organization. I am the reason all of these people are here. I am here for the press conference... Big... you are washed up... so please, take your seat! The
Ironfist has a statement...
[Paxton pulls open his jacket slightly and removes a piece of paper. The podium suddenly tumbles as a large man wearing khaki pants and a blue polo shirt with a small American flag on the chest climbs onto the stage. Several informed fans in the back scream, "GLORY!" The apparent Glory takes Paxton down with a double leg takedown and begins to take it to Paxton in front of the packed house. The camera flashes prove blinding as the members of the press quickly scribble their insults of professional wrestling for tommorow's story's title. Mr. Big pulls Glory off of Paxton. Glory quickly returns to form, apologizing to Paul Toth who is quietly mumbling, "Why can't we ever have
ANYTHING around here?" Paxton slowly gets up, looking as if he has been violated in some way. The room is roaring in noise as Mr. Big looks in Glory's direction. From behind him comes Paxton again who dives onto Glory, quickly delivering blows to the young superstar. Mr. Big begins to pull Paxton off but is quickly moved out of the way by ASWF Commissioner Paul Toth , who, as the key note speaker of the evening, is wearing an expensive suit. the aging commissioner yanks Paxton's neck back and drops him to the stage. A room continues its roar as security pulls Paxton out of the room. The angered Glory slowly staggers out of the room, although not after slapping the hands of several deserving fans and giving the thumbs up. The podium is quickly pulled back up.]
Clear: Ladies and gentlemen... we would like to apologize... let's try to quickly move on with the program... at this time... I would like to present to you a man I respect more than nearly anyone in the world, a man who has been a champion here in the ASWF on 4 seperate occasions, a man who has higher moral standards than any individual than any person I've met in my life, and a man who... can frankly easily take down a man twenty years his junior. Ladies and gentlemen... our esteemed commissioner... Mr. Paul Toth!
[the fans respectfully clap and cheer as cameras flash.] Toth: Good afternoon everyone! I know J. already got to do this, but it feels great so... WELCOME BACK TO ASWF! [the fans cheer.] The doors are now open.
We are signing new talent non-stop and we are excited to get the first broadcast of ASWF THRASHER under way in just a few weeks! In fact, the first
ASWF THRASHER broadcast will come to you LIVE! right here from Pittsburgh,
Pennsylvania in JUST A FEW WEEKS! It is going to be one exciting show! [crowd pops.] In addition, the All Star Wrestling Federation Championship will decided shortly LIVE! from London, England with WORLD BASH 1999! This time -- we're coming for the English! [crowd pops.] We would like to thank whomever has purchased this company... we have no idea who he or she is at this juncture... however, I would like you to read a prepared statement... [Toth pulls a piece of paper from his pocket, pulls his spectacles from his other pocket and places the spectacles on his nose.] "To my 'friends'... " That is in quotes... "... at ASWF. Welcome to my world. You MAY... " That is in capitals... "... You MAY be suprised at my identity. Perhaps not. You will find out soon." [Paul removes his glasses.] It goes on to outline some...
well... of what he or she refers to as "corporate restructuring." With all due respect to our new owner, these are outlandish demands and I will be in touch with your representative in hopes of taking care of these. These things simply will not happen on our first Thrasher.
[Crowd begins chattering. The Clear heckler holds up the other side of his sign which reads, "TRADITION SUCKS!"]
Toth: What the hell is this! Put that sign down! [Toth pushes the podium out of the way and begins to walk down to the floor. Rikky Bull charges over and attempts to hold him back.] Toth: MIND YOUR OWN DAMN BUSINESS, LARRY! JESUS! [Bull releases him and quickly walks off stage. Toth pushes through the crowd but security cuts him off at the pass before he can reach the heckler. Toth shows his dislike of the heckler as he displays several hand gestures and kicks his feet as security drags him away. The heckler is too taken away.]
Clear: Ladies and gentlemen... [Clear seem very disturbed at this point.] Thank you very much. Welcome back. We'll see you at Thrasher. [Clear nods his head in misbelief and quickly walks off stage in the direction of Paul Toth.]
[fade to black.]
