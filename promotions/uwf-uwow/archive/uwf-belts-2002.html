UWF Championship Belts (2002)

27 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UWF WORLD TITLE: The UWF World Title has been held by some of the all time greats in professional wrestling. The title was initially decided in a 16 Man Single Elimination Tournament which included men such as Shawn Michaels, Bret Hart, the Undertaker, Mankind and Goldust. Then we saw everybody battle down until only two men were left... Shawn Michaels and Bret Hart. The hottest feud in wrestling gave way to our first UWF Champion, the HEART BREAK KID SHAWN MICHAELS!! Since then, we've had the greates hold the gold like Hulk Hogan, Ric Flair, Undertaker, Bill Goldberg and of course, STONE COLD STEVE AUSTIN! UWF WORLD TAG TEAM TITLES: The World Tag Team Title has always been a title to keep your eye on. Lightning fast action constantly racing around the ring, you can barely keep an eye on it. Undoubtedly, the all time most winningest Tag Team Champions in the UWF are the Road Warriors, holding a record Five Titles before moving on to finish their careers in the bush leagues of the UWOW-ALLIANCE. A new breed is about to take over... beginning NOW! UWF WOMEN'S WORLD TITLE: The Powerful Women of Wrestling have been a side show, a perverted side show, a skillful side show and lately in the business we have seen the Women become less of a side show and more of a legitimate force. With women like Ivory, the Fabulous Moolah, Chyna and Jacqueline being on record as former UWF Women's Champions, how can you argue with the statement that the ladies of wrestling have never been more skullful and dangerous? UWF HARDCORE TITLE: The new norm in professional wrestling is becoming HARDCORE ACTION!! Be it Jeff Hardy, Rob VanDam, Eddy Guerro, Hugh Morrus or any other legendary stars like Tommy Dreamer, Cactus Jack or the Texas Legend Terry Funk, you can't deny the ability of the men in this division!! Past title holders include Bam Bam Bigelow, Big Van Vader, Cactus Jack, Terry Funk, the Sandman and Raven! Who's going to be the NEXT ICON of HARDCORE WRESTLING??? Stay tuned to more UWF action... UWF CANADIAN TITLE: The UWF is based in Canada and what better way to honor our home country than to establish a Canadian Heavyweight Title? With icons like Bret Hart, Val Venis, Edge, Christian, Lance Storm, Owen Hart, Chris Jericho, Chris Benoit, and so many others in the UWF rings, we needed to combine TWO BELTS... the now defunct European and United States TitleS! When the Canadian belt was forged at Halloween Havoc 2001 on October 31st, we determined the new champion in a European .vs. U.S. Champion match... UWF LIGHTWEIGHT TITLE: Small... tiny... high fliers. Those are words to describe the Lightweights, but you can't deny their intensity and courage. Lately, the Lightweight Title has turned away from foreigners and back to more generic stars like Dean Malenko, X-Pac, Lash LeRoux, and even Shano-Mac, Shane McMahon. You can't deny that the old school of Lightweight Action is hot on their heels though! Eddy Guerro, Rey Mysterio Jr and members of Kaientai are always tough challengers and you can't forget the young hungry stars the UWF seems to add all the time! UWF INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE: Who's Who in Professional wrestling? Read the list of former UWF Intercontinental Champions!! Starting with Hunter Hearst Helmsley, then a member of DX, and going through men like Shane Douglas, Diamond Dallas Page, Rob VanDam, Sting, Bill Goldberg and The Rock!! All former Intercontinental Champions have seen some success in other areas of the company, especially the few who have gone on to win the UWF World title afterwards!! Goldberg, Triple-H, Kurt Angle and the Rock! UWF TELEVISION TITLE: Although the UWF is internet... you can't forget the UWF TELEVISION TITLE!! We operate like a TV Show so what the heck, HERE WE GO!! The list of former TV Champions is VERY impressive!! The Canadian Crippler Chris Benoit, Total Package Lex Luger, the Loose Cannon Brian Pillman, Bret "HITMAN" Hart, "Mr. Perfect" Curt Hennig, Bam Bam Bigelow, Tazz and "Big Poppa Pump" Scott Steiner have all held the amazing belt. More recent title holders include Shawn Stasiak, Al Snow and William Regal... all stars of the future and present!! RETIRED U.W.F.
