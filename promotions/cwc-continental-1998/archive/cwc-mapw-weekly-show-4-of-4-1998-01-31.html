CWC: MAPW: Weekly Show part 4 of 4

31 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE CONTINENTAL COUNCIL Proudly Presents: ..... MID-ATLANTIC PRO WRESTLING ~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~ S H O W C A S E For the Week of: 01-24-98 ~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~^v^~ ================================================================== <<The scene cuts back in from commercial and we return to the action in the ring>> [BLAIN]: Fans, welcome back...we've just witness some tremendous action during the break. Tony Ruiz and "Iceman" David Smith have squared off, as has Commander Troyer and Johnny Utah. Meanwhile, the battle swings back and forth between that of Rex Stone and Rex Ruthless. Tully, if you had to pick a winner who would be your favorite right now... [MANTEL]: Well you know that I don't particularly like answering those kinds of questions so I think that I will pass on answerin'... Tony Ruiz and "Iceman" David Smith lock up in the corner. Ruiz spins around and nails Smith with a hard sevat kick to the chest. He executes it once more and Smith grabs hold of him. This time, he reverses positions and THROWS Ruiz into the corner. He winds up and UNLOADS with a vicious chop that is heard around the arean. And ANOTHER! Ruiz flies backwards with the second one and slumps down to the second rope. Smith lifts up his leg and plants a foot in the throat of Ruiz. Tony Ruiz however manages to escape with a low blow to Smith's groin area and the "Iceman" doubles over, moving backwards towards the center of the ring, where he bumps into Commander Troyer and Johnny Utah. They each become distracted, and Ruiz suprises all three of them by rushing out of the corner and tackling them to the ground. All four men now rolling around, kickng and punching one another. <<The crowd begins to explode with a variety of cheers>> Rex Ruthless and "The Crippler" Rex Stone are battling meanwhile in the opposite corner. Ruthless lays down a hard right hand to the jaw of Stone and "The Crippler" stumbles to the side of the ring. Ruthless goes over to the pile of wrestlers and picks up Troyer by the back of the neck. He takes Troyer and THROWS him over the top rope eliminating him... <<Commander Troyer elimated 8:06>> A hush falls over the crowd as the 6'7" 310lb monster discarded the decorated Vietnam Veteran. Johnny Utah looks around and then moves over to the ropes. He points at Troyer and begins to laugh and mock him. But Ruthless comes over and GRABS him by the back of the neck. Picking him up with one hand, he SHOVES him over the top rope and chucks him on top of Troyer outside of the ring <<Johnny Utah eliminated 8:19>> Ruthless looks out at them as Troyer begins to unload and go bezerk once more. Smith and Ruiz look at one another and then run back into the ropes. They come off and double drop kick Rex Ruthless. He staggers to one knee and they both look at one another stunned. Ruiz grabs hold of the "Iceman" and whips him into the ropes. The Iceman comes off and catapaults himself off of Ruiz, who gets on his hands and knees, with a flying elbow smash that sends him down on both his knees. They look at one another once more and this time Smith hurls Ruiz into the ropes. Ruthless back up on his feet...Ruiz comes off the ropes and catapaults himself off of Smith. He CONNECTS with a HUGE FLYING CLOTHESLINE that TAKES the big wrestler off his feet and over the ropes. But Ruthless keeps hold of Ruiz and takes him down with him to the canvas.... <<Rex Ruthless and Tony Ruiz eliminated 10:12>> [MANTEL]: Well, the final two are now in the ring. Rex Stone taking on the former Florida Heavyweight Champion. It should prove to be an interesting contest... [BLAIN]: Clearly though there is a weight advantage as Stone outweighs Smith by roughly one-hundred and seventy pounds!!! Stone and Smith meet in the middle locking up. Smith with a gouge to the eyes. Another rake to the eyes and Stone turns away. Smith begins to hammer away at the back of Rex Stone but "The Crippler" quickly recovers and turns around. Smith reaches back and smacks Stone with a shot across the face but the "Crippler" in close proximity grabs hold of Smith and picks him up off the mat. He hoists him to the side and DROPS him down for a sidewalk slam. Smith tries to get up by himself but Stone grabs a handful of hair and gets him up to his feet. Smith goes for the eyes once more and gouges Stone. He takes the burly man by the arm and whips him into the ropes. Stone comes off and Smith delivers a classic, text book dropkick. Stone staggers backwards and almost falls backwards over the ropes. Smith runs in with a clothesline, but Stone DUCKS IT and sends Smith over the top rope and down to the floor.... [PETERS]: The Winner of the Battle Royal and NEW MID ATLANTIC HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION...."THE CRIPPLER"_REX....STONE!!!!!!!! <<The crowd stands at attention, as the championship belt is handed to him. He raises it high over his head and then puts it over his shoulder. Doom and Volcano, both members of the Hearts of Darkness come out of the back to congratulate their stablemate...>> [BLAIN]: Fantastic match-up. Rex Stone is the NEW champion and it appears the the Hearts of Darkness might be re-forming. [MANTEL]: Well, if they do it would not be good for Richard Richardson...to say the least. We're out of time though...so we'll see you next week. The main event just announced as Richard Richardson battles "Iceman" David Smith for the Florida Heavyweight Title. It should be a great one...so long every-one... <<The scene fades out on them and the crowd at hand>> ================================================================== CWC - Continental Wrestling Council All Rights Reserved 1998 ==================================================================
