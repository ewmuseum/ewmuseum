Elite Championship League Wrestling — Founding Announcement

27 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings fellow e-Wrestlers ... Elite Championship Wresling League is preparing to open it's doors November 1st... A fed created by roleplayers for roleplayers. People who have 'been there' and 'done that' for more then a decade have decided that it's time to toss their hats into the ring. Run by two guys who have had a collective twelve years of experience working matches, angles, storylines and strats, two guys who have written cards for more then five years, have decided that the next step is to run their own e-fed. November 1, 2004 the ECLW opens it's doors. Weekly wrestling and roleplaying shows...bi-mothly PPV's...and the rule that this is a game and should be fun help support the foundation that the ECLW stands on. So, if you have the time to shed a few tears...to sweat and bleed for a fed that will ask nothing more of you then to give it everything that you have once a week...a fed that will work it's ass off to put the cards out not only ontime, but keep them fun and unbiased...then why not join the ECLW? After all ... what do you have to lose? If you think that the ECLW might just be the e-Fed for you, then the handbook is listed below. Join now while positions last. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= THE REAL PEOPLE BEHIND THE ECLW =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [name withheld] – I have been participating in e-wrestling since 1997 and I feel that throughout the years of not only handling several e-wrestlers, but helping out on a couple of e-fed executive committee boards that I am qualified to start my own e-fed with one of my friends. Writing is a passion of mine, period. You can contact me at any time by e-mailing [detail withheld], or by sending a message to [detail withheld] on AOL Instant Messenger. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-= TITLES =-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Heavyweight Title [Anyone 230 +] Lightweight Title [Anyone Under 230 lbs.] Tag Team Titles [Any Weight Class] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-= SHOWS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Ring Rage - The "flagship" show of the ECLW that takes place every Friday night. Ring Rage will usually host 6 matches per week. This will be the show for the backstage and in-ring promos, as it is the only lived televised show that the ECLW has to offer at this point in time. All titles will have potential of having to be defended at least once a month ... and potentially more than that. Rules for Ring Rage: 1) Any wrestler that has a match at Ring Rage has a freebie 60 seconds, which can be anything from the wrestler arriving at the arena, showing him/her walking down the hall, in their locker room, a very quick backstage interview, etc... that can air on Ring Rage without having to get the permission of the President or VP. However, you may not attack anyone unless written consent is submitted by both parties agreeing to it. 2) Wrestlers not scheduled to have a match at Ring Rage have the option of bidding for 3 interviews (2 backstage & 1 in-ring). These segments would be on a first come, first serve basis. 3) For wrestlers that are booked to wrestle at Ring Rage, there will be two open spots for a in-ring or backstage promo. Since they are scheduled, both spots are viable for either in the ring or backstage. These spots will also be on a first come, first serve basis. Slander - This will be the weekly show that airs every Monday night on the FX Network. This show will preview all of the wrestler's promos for their upcoming matches, etc... Every wrestler on the roster is permitted 2 rps at the most on this show. Slander will also display 2-3 matches, usually pre-recorded clips. Rules for Slander: 1) As listed above, wrestlers will be allowed 2 role-plays on Slander, but no more than that. If you opt to do both of your rps on Slander, then you will not be available to try for the backstage/in-ring segments at Ring Rage, but you will be allowed the 60-second freebie airtime, provided that you have a match on Ring Rage. 2) As also stated above, the matches that are previewed on Slander will not be written out in full detail. Those will be saved for Ring Rage and the bi-monthly Pay-Per-Views. In other words, the matches on Slander will be summarized. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= PAY-PER-VIEWS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The ECLW will have pay-per-view on a bi-monthly basis. This way, all of the angles and feuds have ample time to build to an intense level. The pay-per-views are listed below. January · Mind Games - This PPV is rather unique ... each year the gimmick of the PPV changes, so one can never tell what each year's Mind Games PPV holds in store for them. March · Stormbound - In the ECLW ... it isn't always sunny and bright. We have our rough days with backstage assaults and verbal abuse flying every which way ... that's where Stormbound comes into play. It allows all of those filthy mouthed son of a guns to put their money where their mouth is. This PPV isn't gimmicked. May · Master Of The Mat - This event is the equal to the WWE's Royal Rumble, except instead of 30 men, the ECLW has 25 vying for the honor of being known as that year's 'Master Of The Mat' and also earning a shot at any title. July · Animosity - This PPV will be a place to settle some heavy tension building between wrestlers inside the ECLW. This PPV will be a standard one as well, no gimmicks. September · Deadly Pairings - This is a $$C.M.B$$ sponsored PPV that is slightly modified equivalent to the old WCW PPV known as Lethal Lottery. November · Capital Chaos - This PPV is a standard PPV that includes a cage match as the main event. This event as the name slightly hints will take place in the Nation's capital. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= PERSONALITIES =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Brian Grey - Grey is the man behind the ECLW. It is through him that the ECLW functions. He is very rarely ever seen on camera. Vic Rancid - Vic does color commentary on Ring Rage (and some of the pay-per-views). He also doubles as a part-time manager in the league, especially if he takes a liking to a particular wrestler. He favors heels 99% of the time, but can always give props to a face that impresses him. Mike Douglas - Mike is the play-by-play announcer for Ring Rage (and some of the pay-per-views). He calls the match as fairly as possible, but he cannot stand the under-handed tactics of the heels in the company and will always favor their opponents in that situation. Chris Hudson - No relation to Scott. Chris hosts Slander and frequently joins the announce team on pay-per-veiws. Chris is a veteran in the wrestling business, commentating on shows for various organizations for 20+ years now. His knowledge of the sport and its history is second to none. Rebecca Young - She is the gorgeous blond bombshell that announces all of the ECLW superstars to the ring at Ring Rage and Slander. Paul Steele - Paul is a former wrestler that decided that he had accomplished all he wanted to inside the squared circle, so now ... he helps dictate what transpires out of the ring, if things get out of control. Standing at a height of 6'5" and weighing in at 279 pounds of solid muscle, Paul isn't one to take crap off of anyone ... at least not more than a couple of seconds. Clark Stevens - Clark is an experienced man in the interviewing department. He has worked in several federations throughout his years and executes his job very well. Clark is the interview for Slander, he is rarely seen on any other ECLW event, save for the pay-per-views. Cynthia Sanchez - You want Latina Heat? Then look no further than Cynthia Sanchez. This beautiful young woman is the rookie of the announce team, but she continues to impress the executives day in and day out with her abilities as an interviewer. Cynthia is the interviewer for Ring Rage. She will also be seen on the ECLW's pay-per-views as well. Cynthia hopes that all of her hard work will eventually pay off and perhaps lead her to becoming the first female announcer in the ECLW. Andrew Tucker - Andrew is the head referee for the ECLW. He takes his job very seriously and is extremely strict when it comes to enforcing the rules. The wrestlers he works with do not intimidate him while he is doing his job, an invaluable asset for a referee. Andrew is the type of referee that will push back if shoved. He is usually the referee that calls the title matches. Bobby Townsend - Bobby is a relative newcomer to the refereeing scene and can often be easily intimidated by some of the larger talent in the ECLW. He is very concerned for his safety inside the squared circle, but he can call any type of match and seems to enjoy doing his job, at least most of the time. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= APPLICATION =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Handler's Name: Handler's E-Mail: Handler's Contact Info [AIM]: ---------------------------------------- Wrestler's Name: Hometown: Height: (Max 7'6") Weight: (Max 500 lbs.)
