Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Phoenix International Wrestling opened in March 1998 as a renamed continuation of its operator's prior federation, which had run since December 1996; its own founding FAQ, posted 29 March 1998, states the switch plainly rather than treating it as a fresh start.

Its own first surviving broadcast, Wednesday Night Warfare (15 April 1998), opened with a Phoenix rising from a bonfire to spell out “PIW” in flame against the night sky, broadcasting from a newly built Phoenix Dome in Pittsburgh in front of 7,000 fans. Its commentary team argued from the opening bell over whether an untested wrestler, Roadkill, could already be called “the greatest wrestler ever in the history of PIW” before a single match had been fought.

That same Roadkill went on to headline the card against Caladan Moore, working around an arm injury Moore had spent the match targeting; Moore finished him with the Perfect Move at 13 minutes 8 seconds to steal the main event, prompting an on-air accusation from one of the announcers that he had “paid off the ref.”

No closing statement survives. Its own last attested activity under the Phoenix International name runs to 27 May 1998; what became of the promotion after that isn't known from what remains.
