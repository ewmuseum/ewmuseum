Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

MECW, Main Event Championship Wrestling, returned in the spring of 2001 from a nine-month hiatus that a fatal computer crash had forced on it, campaigning through March and April for wrestlers from a Geocities-hosted website under its president, Tony.

Its comeback card, Thursday Night House Party, aired on 2 May 2001 and crowned Corey Irons the promotion's Hardcore Champion outright, with no opponent named in the promotion's own report. The reign was short: on a later Main Event broadcast, reported that week, Johnny Eastland took the title from him in what the promotion's own report called a stunning upset, while the Misfits won the Tag Team Championship on the same card.

Irons answered on 9 May 2001, beating the Iron Predator, MECW's first Hall of Fame inductee, for the vacant Television Championship. Three weeks later, at Hell Night, the promotion's first pay-per-view, Irons beat Eastland in a tournament final to become the first MECW World Champion.

By early June the Television Championship had gone vacant again, and Johnny Eastland took it a second time, beating a rising talent named Jaguar. A rematch for the World Championship and a defense of the Tag Team Championship were both promised for the same week; neither result survives.

How the run ended is not in the surviving record.
