Has your e-wrestler fulfilled you?

18 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I Have been in E-Wrestling since January of this year.I am finally a superstar in these feds I go to.It is not easy to get that kind of fame.I didn't get much time to role-playe on the weekend and still don't,I just write longer and better flashes.My first few Wrestlers were experments that didn't work,like I had Critical Condtion-knda of a nut case bad ass,he didn't work because the role-playes i did wasn't constant.Then Raging Warriors-A tag team rip off of Road Warriors.And Wicked-Just a normal wrestler and the one that never workd at all.The all was retiered after march.Then i was thinking of some better people.I came up with these people. Death Machine/Miss Conception He was cool,he had a white albino tiger named Nimbus accampined him to the ring.He even got disqualifed once when Nimbus bite a wrestlers toe off.He was succesful in the one fed he was in.He was the First EWA TV Champion.The fed closed down he went with it.Also that was in the EWA was Smith & Wessen-Revolver & Calaber They were a buch of cow boys looking to have fun.They also was in the EWA,Tey were the first EWA Tag Team Champions. Then I decided to get better on flashing so I needed a whole diffrent group of wrestlers.A came up a pretty good tag team.They were The Bright Brothers-Jeff"The Death"Bright & Xavier"Xtreme"Bright.They had a succesful record in the TWO but never got a title shot,But became Tag Team Champions in LWWA. I also needed a great singles wrestler.I wanted people to be afraid of him,but like him.And wanted him to be a crusierweight.I thought of the phenom gimmick.So I came up with "Lightning"Levi Ash He was my most ssuccesful wrestler.I had a winning record most everwhere i went.I was the most feared wrestlers in some of these federation.One federation all the wrestlers congragulated me on winning the title.The titles i have won were EWA IC,Crusierweight,Tag Team.LWWA Crusiweight.I am the current FEW Crusierweight champion,and current AWA Cruiserweight champion So which wrestler do you like? ~Levi
