Match of the Year! ;) Quigley vs. Manning from TAEWF's February Fury!

Feb 18, 1997, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera cuts to special guest broadcast crew, former FoPEW play by play man Vic MacInnis, accompanied by an unfamiliar face, wearing quite a sneer...] VIC: Vic MacInnis here fans, with my NEW co partner, Mel Blake, on special assignment here in the TAEWF to cover what may be the biggest, most anticipated main event in TAEWF history, as we see World Champion "Quickstrike" Chris Quigley do battle with longtime rival, "The Masterpiece" Kurt Manning! This one is gonna be huge. It's so big in fact, that our own TAEWF President will be doing the ring announcing, just so he can be part of this match! Before he go down to him, Rod Bettingfill is waiting in the back with Kurt Manning! [Camera cuts to backstage, where Rod Bettingfill stands, dressed in a flashy tuxedo, microphone in hand. Next to him is a smirking, gum chewing Kurt Manning, who is wearing a white singlet, with a very realistic art design of the TAEWF World Title belt around the waist area of the suit.] ROD: Ladies and gentlemen, I'm standing at this time with the challenger tonight, Kurt Manning! Kurt, this has shaped into one of the most intriguing matches in TAEWF history, as you've given us a look into the past, at your only meeting with Quigley before, where you claim he had to use that foreign object to get by you. KURT: That's the story, Bettingfill! Quigley ruined my perfect record in the WIWF, and now I'm gonna get my payback, by ruining the thing he's been waiting for, for such a long time! All you could hear Quigley talk about was how much he wanted that World title, and now, he had it shoved in his face on a silver platter! Well Quigley, tonight, I don't care if I have to take that silver platter and whack you upside the head with it! If that's what it takes to ruin your life... I'll do it! ROD: This is clearly going to be a technical matchup... do you think you can go move for move with someone the caliber of Quigley? KURT: Hello?! Is this thing on?! What kinda stupid question is that, Bettingfill?! I told ya before, my father taught Chris Quigley everything! But if you asked me, my dad did a half assed job! Quigley looks like an idiot out there sometimes and... ROD: If I may interupt... I've never seen Chris Quigley look like an idiot in the ring... KURT: Well you're gonna tonight! "The Masterpiece" is going to give Quigley a wrestling lesson! There's gonna be wrestling experts in the audience taking notes for the new Textbook of Wrestling, dedicated to Kurt Manning! Tonight is the night where I finally do, what everyone knows I've got the ability to do! I've been held back by stupid managers, bad promoters, knee injuries, and stubborn little brothers, but now, it's all in the past! And so will Chris Quigley's title reign! This match will be MY masterpiece, Bettingfill! It's gonna be worth something someday, and you can take THAT to the bank! Show's over, Quigley! I'm coming for you and that title! [Manning walks off the set, as Bettingfill closes up...] ROD: Kurt Manning looks ready, folks! Is Chris Quigley? We'll find out! [Camera cuts back to ringside, as President Koss stands in the ring...] KOSS: Ladies and gentlemen... it is time for the MAIN EVENT!! [crowd pop!] This match is scheduled for one fall... with a 60 minute time limit... and is for The American E-Mail Wrestling Federation World Heavyweight Championship!!! [Another crowd pop!] Introducing first... the challenger! [Chariots of Fire starts up and the fans begin to boo and yell insults...] Hailing from Phoenix, Arizona... weighing 251 pounds... here is... "THE MASTERPIECE" KURT MANNING!!! [Manning emerges from behind the curtain to a chorus of boos, with both arms raised high, and an arrogant smirk on his face, as he chomps on his gum. He swats the fans hands off him, while ignoring the less than favourable remarks from them.] VIC: We could be looking at the next World Champion right here, Mel, as I understand his parents are in attendance tonight. MEL: Damn right he's the next World Champion, MacInnis! You think for one second, Weakstrike is going to outwrestle this man? I'm going to go on the record as saying Kurt Manning is the #1 technical wrestler in the world today, and he's gonna strut his stuff in front of his folks! VIC: Well that's quite a compliment, Mel. I wonder if Kurt can live up to it. MEL: You'll see... VIC: We're awaiting the champion, and we have word Bettingfill is with him backstage! [The camera cuts back to the previous scene, only this time "Quickstrike" Chris Quigley is standing next to Bettingfill, wearing his usual black wrestling pants with graphical silver lightning swarming around the legs, and black cross over straps forming an "X" on his chest. He has his black leather jacket on over it, and wire rimmed shades, which are partially covered by his long front bangs, hanging down over. He has the TAEWF World title belt slung over his shoulder, and holds it there with his hand.] ROD: I'm now with the Champ, guys! Chris, can I ask you what your strategy is going into this match? CHRIS: Strategy? There is no real strategy in a match like this, Bettingfill! You've got Kurt Manning in there, who I'll admit, is probably one of the best technical wrestlers in the world... but if he thinks for even one second, that he's better than me? He's dead wrong! This match tonight is gonna be complete spontaneous action on my half! There is no way you can tell how this is going to pan out! Manning may want to wrestle... he may want to brawl... he may want to get down and dirty outside the ring! No matter what he wants to do, I'll be ready to give it right back to him! ROD: What about Manning's claim that you can't beat him without using foreign objects? CHRIS: That's all a part of Kurt's game, Bettingfill! He likes to piss you off, play these little mind games, and get under your skin! He wants to throw my focus off, but it ain't gonna work! I've seen it all, done it all, and heard it all, and there is nothing you can do, that'll throw me off my game, Manning! Tonight, the Quickstriker is gonna be applied, and I ain't gonna let go until you are outta my hair for good! Get ready to be STRUCK DOWN! [Quigley walks off the set...] ROD: The champion is up to the task, or so it seems. Back to you guys! KOSS: And his opponent... [For Those About to Rock starts piping through the PA system and the fans erupt in cheers!] ...from Corner Brook, NF, Canada... weighing 238 pounds... he is one half of the FWLI North American Tag Team Champions... he is the 1996 TAEWF Master of the Mat... and he is the TAEWF World Heavyweight Champion... here is... "QUICKSTRIKE" CHRIS QUIGLEY!!!! [Quigley emerges from behind the curtain and the crowd manages to get even louder, as Quigley walks down the aisle, slapping a hand every few steps, while fireworks go off overhead. Quigley climbs between the top and middle ropes and walks around the ring, givng a Jimmy Snuka-esque "I Love You" handsign to the crowd. Quigley then removes his leather jacket and shades, handing them to a ringside attendant, as his music dies down. Quigley kisses the World title belt, before handing it to referee Hal Jacobs, who holds it up to a loud cheer from the crowd.] --------DING--------DING----------DING---------------- VIC: There goes the bell and this baby is underway! Manning and Quigley involved in a staredown in the center of the ring... Manning just spit his gum in Quigley's face! Quigley responds with a hard punch to the head, that sends Manning down hard on his back! Manning quickly back up and slaps Quigley hard, staggering him back! Manning follows up with a right hand that's blocked by Quigley, who fires back with one of his own that sends Manning back down again! Manning rolls away from Quigley and gets back to his feet... and now he charges at him! Hiptoss takeover by Chris Quigley and Manning slides across the ring! He's back up again and runs at Quigley once more! Quigley ducks Manning's clothesline attempt and once again floors Manning with a hard punch!! Manning drops to the mat and rolls out of the ring to collect his thoughts! MEL: That's a smart man right there, when things aren't going your way you... WAIT! Quigley launches himself over the ropes down on top of Kurt! Now Quigley is hammering away on his forehead! Quigley grabs Manning by the locks and lifts him to his feet... Quigley whips Manning hard into the steel steps, sending them flying right off their base! Quigley grabs Manning by the hair again... lifts him up for a suplex... but instead lets him drop back down stomach first across the guardrailing!! Manning flops back to the concrete floor, and Quigley grabs him again, sliding him back in the ring! What a cheater Chris Quigley is! VIC: Quigley means business here tonight, that's for sure! "Quickstrike" re-enters the ring, as Manning slides back to the corner, begging off, but Quigley will have none of that. He sends a boot hard into the chest of Manning, before grabbing him by the hair again... wait... Quigley with two handfuls of hair starts spinning around... Manning is being spun around in mid-air by his locks!! Quigley releases his grip and Kurt goes skidding across the canvas... RIGHT INTO THE STEEL POST!! Kurt Manning is taking a beating! MEL: What won't Quigley stoop to, tonight?! Quigley goes in and grabs Kurt by the wrist, lifting him back up... shortarm clothesline and Manning is down again! Quigley grabs him... Russian leg sweep! A cover by Quigley... 1... 2... and Manning got the shoulder up! He's still in this one! VIC: But he has got to be re-evaluating his decision to challenge Chris Quigley right now. This is obviously a changed man since they fought back in the WIWF! Quigley has a definite killer instinct in him now, that he always lacked in that federation! Quigley goes to grab Kurt once more, but Manning sends a hard boot right into Quigley's groin, sending the champion down to his knees in agony! Manning shakes a few cobwebs out, and he knows he has to do something big, and quick! Manning goes to the opposite corner, and runs from behind at Quigley, who is in a sitting up position... Manning leaps over the champ, while grabbing Quigley's neck in the process, hauling Quigley's whole body forward and then snapping it back hard into the mat! Beautiful jumping neck snap by Kurt Manning! Manning up to his feet and grabs Quigley by the hair... bodyslam by Manning, and now he drops down with a kneedrop! MEL: Manning grabs Quigley's greasey locks again and lifts him to his feet... reverse knife edge by Manning... and another one... and yet another! Vicious chops! You can hear them all over the arena! Quigley's chest is RED right now, as Manning grabs his arm and whips him to the opposite corner, and charges in after him with a dropkick! Quigley falls to the mat, right on his face, as Manning takes to the top rope, something he doesn't always do! Quigley gets back to his feet, slowly but surely, as Manning flies off with a flying axehandle that sends Quigley back down! Manning starts stomping away on Quigley, and now grabs him by one of his shoulder straps, dragging him to his feet. Manning is setting up for the Masterplex already... Quigley wraps both his legs around one of Manning's legs, making it impossible for Kurt to execute the move! VIC: Great counter by Chris Quigley! And OOOOH!!! Manning just countered THAT by sending a knee right up into Quigley's groin! Quigley falls to his knees, as Manning runs back against the ropes... and a spinwheel kick on the kneeling Quigley, sending him buckling backwards to the mat! Manning gets to his feet, with his arms raised, as the crowd rains down on him with booes! This is no time to work the crowd Kurt! MEL: He's a showman, McInnis. You know that, you used to work with him every week during FoPEW broadcasts. VIC: This is an entirely different thing, Mel. Manning yanks Quigley up... and executes a perfect backbreaker! Manning goes to the top rope once more... flying kneedrop finds its mark! A pin... 1... 2... and Quigley escapes the back door! Manning should have hooked the leg! Manning whips Quigley to the ropes and puts his head down for a back body drop... too soon! Quigley, quick as a cat, grabs Manning's neck and nails him with a swinging neckbreaker!! Both men are down on the mat staring at the ceiling tiles! MEL: C'mon Kurt! You've got this thing! Don't let it slip away now! VIC: This is far from over yet, I think, Mel. Although they've been going at a breakneck pace! But from these two, we could see the 60 minute time limit expire! They've both wrestled over an hour before, and they can do it again! MEL: Manning is the first to move, as he drapes an arm over Quigley! Make the count ref! One... two... thr... NO! Quigley gets the shoulder up! Don't ask me how! Manning gets to his feet, as does Quigley! Manning with a punch, blocked by Quigley! Quigley with a kick to the midsection that doubles up Manning! Quigley... PILEDRIVER by "Quickstrike"!! He can't capitilize right away, as he falls back to the mat! Manning starts to move again, as Quigley gets back to his feet. Quigley climbs to the top rope... Manning back to his feet and turns around... Quigley with the LIGHTNING STRIKE (Flying fist/bodypress)!!! The cover is made... 1... 2... and Manning KICKED OUT!! He wants it BAD!! VIC: Kurt Manning kicked out of the Lightning Strike! Not too many people can say that! MEL: Kurt Manning kicked out of the Lightning Strike! See? I just said it. VIC: No... I mean... Ah forget it! Quigley doesn't waste time being frusterated, as he just grabs Manning again... full nelson by Quigley... DRAGON SUPLEX!!! 1... 2... and HOW did Kurt Manning kick out that time?!! This time Quigley slams his hands on the mat, in frusteration! He was sure he had him beat! Quigley grabs Manning one more time... fisherman suplex! He's using Manning's own finisher against him!!! One... two... three... NO! He didn't get him!!! That was DAMN close and Quigley thinks he shoulda won it! Quigley gets in referee Hal Jacobs' face and is yelling at him! MEL: Quigley better control himself, or he'll lose by disqualification! Wait... Manning is up slowly from behind... roll up!! 1... 2... and he ALMOST won it right there, while that moron Quigley bitched at the ref!! VIC: Quigley looks more annoyed than anything, as he grabs Manning... Samoan drop! That'll knock the wind outta ya! He grabs Manning again... whips him to the ropes... flying forearm!! Quigley back up, as Manning is slowly up... Quigley with a dropkick that sends Manning back to the corner! Manning bounces back out and Quigley with a kick to the midsection... Manning catches the foot... Quigley spins around... ENZUIGIRI!!! Kurt Manning is in his own little world right now as Quigley has dismantled him ever since that roll up attempt by Manning!! Manning is using the ropes to get back to his feet, as he clutches the back of his head with one hand! Quigley is just waiting for him with his foot ready to launch... Manning is up... SUPERKICK by Quigley, and Manning goes HURTLING over the top ropes into the guardrailing with a crash!! The crowd gives Quigley a huge roar for that one, as Quigley gets to the top rope... what's he gonna do? MEL: Quigley lets himself fly to the outside with a flying splash at Manning... but Kurt quickly grabbed a chair that he was lying next to and holds it up over his body!!! Quigley comes CRASHING into the chair as well as Manning!! They're both down outside the ring, and they look like the survivors of a train wreck, McInnis! VIC: Are you sure they SURVIVED?! Quigley is holding his head, and he is busted open from that chair! Wait... Quigley grabs Manning and drapes him over the guardrail... now Quigley with a chair and YOU GOTTA BE KIDDING ME! Quigley goes to smash it down on Manning, making him the meat in a steel sandwich, but Manning rolls himself off the railing back to the floor, and Quigley just put a sizeable dent in both the railing AND the chair! MEL: Quigley drops the chair as Manning is attempting to crawl away from it all! Quigley grabs Manning by the tights, tearing the shoulder straps from the singlet as he drags him back! Quigley sets Manning up... suplex down on the concrete floor!! Quigley rolls back in the ring and sits in the corner, catching his breath, as Manning is holding his back, in serious pain! Manning hauls himself back into the ring, ending the referee's counting, and Quigley is back up and quick on the attack, nailing Manning with vicious stomps and boots! VIC: Quigley lifts Manning to his feet... SMALL PACKAGE BY MANNING!!! 1... 2... OOOH! AND HE CAME SOOO CLOSE THAT TIME!!! Quigley back up fast, and sends a kneelift to Manning's chest... and backs against the ropes... Lariat by Quigley that sends Manning into a 360 spiral!! Quigley grabs Manning by the hair and lifts him... he sits him up on the top turnbuckle... Quigley with his snap superplex!!! A cover... 1... 2... and Manning drapes his foot over the bottom rope! Kurt Manning is really showing us some incredible resiliance tonight!! Quigley grabs Manning by the hair and whips him to the ropes... Manning ducks a clothesline and nails Quigley with a fist to the midsection on the rebound! Manning sets him up... a stone cold stunner by Kurt Manning!!! The cover... 1... 2... and QUIGLEY KICKS OUT! The champion is showing his famous resiliency as well! MEL: Kurt is somehow on the offense again, as he grabs Quigley by the hair and lifts him back up... inverted atomic drop by Manning... a knife edge chop... and now Manning whips him to the ropes... grabs him on the rebound and a STUNGUN executed by Manning! Quigley flipped right over the top rope to the outside with the momentum, as Manning slides outside as well! Manning grabs a chair and stalks Quigley with it... he raises it, but Quigley nailed him with a kick to the midsection, and Manning drops the chair! Quigley grabs it... and SMASHES IT OVER MANNING'S HEAD! There is literally a DENT in that chair!!! Manning is not only bleeding, but it looks like he is OUT COLD! Quigley throws Manning back into the ring, and he re-enters himself. Quigley adjust the kneepads, before going to the 2nd rope. Flying elbow connects! Quigley back up, and he grabs Manning's legs, as the fans go NUTS!! Quigley wraps the legs around, if he hooks this in it's OVER! There is no escaping the Quickstriker! He's trying to turn it over... as Manning is actually pushing up with his arms... lifting all this tremendous pressure with his upper body! He manages to kick Quigley off! What an effort by "The Masterpiece"!!! VIC: I can't believe what these two have taken in and dished out in this match! We are at the 30 minute mark now! Both men are bleeding like faucets! We can see Kurt Manning's parents in attendace, and they've been wincing the whole time! Quigley is back up, and he is sending punches into Manning's lower back! He wants to try the Quickstriker again! He goes to grab a leg, as Manning flails them around, and his foot caught Quigley right under the chin, sending him flying back on to the mat, in a daze! Kurt Manning is struggling to his feet, he has got to be so tired he's nauseous! He's in so much pain he must be numb! And the same can be said for Quigley, but they keep fighting! Manning grabs Quigley and throws him to the ropes... FRANKENSTEINER!!! HOW THE HELL DID MANNING DO THAT THIS LATE IN THE MATCH?!! This has got to be it!! 1... 2... and QUIGLEY RAISES THE SHOULDER!! MEL: These guys are gonna have to be handed guns to finish each other off! Normal wrestling moves aren't doing the trick! Manning tirelessly to his feet, and whips Quigley to the ropes... powerslam, but Quigley somehow eluded it and fell behind Manning!! German suplex!!! 1... 2... KICKOUT by Manning!! Both men are up and now they are just trading blows back and forth in a vicious fight!! Quigley doesn't lose many of these against men his own size, and he punches Manning back to the corner! Quigley goes to the top turnbuckle behind Manning and sits on his shoulders!! Manning walks out of the corner slowly, supporting Quigley's weight! What is Quigley doing?! Quigley leans forward... and rolls Manning along with him! Quigley has both Manning's legs with that BEAUTIFUL Victory roll! One... two... thr... I CANNOT BELIEVE MANNING KICKED OUT OF THAT! VIC: Neither can Quigley, if you can read his lips at the moment! Quigley back up to his feet, and grabs Manning by the legs! Is he going for the Quickstriker again? No... Quigley with a figure four leglock!! Manning is in a world of hurt now! This may be the acid test for that knee of his! He's had a long history of knee trouble, and this will NOT help! Manning is flailing his arms around like mad, and screaming! But he won't quit! Manning is reaching for those ropes... he can't get to the ropes... he's going to have to give it up! Referee Hal Jacobs leans in close so he can catch the submission if Manning gives it... AND MANNING JUST GRABBED THE REFEREE AND THREW HIM INTO QUIGLEY BREAKING THE HOLD!!! Manning crawls over to the ropes, and uses them to regain his footing, as the referee marches over and is screaming at Kurt! Wait! Quigley is up and he grabs the ref and shoves him aside! Quigley is WAILING on Manning in the corner with lefts and rights!!! Manning's nose is broken! It's gotta be broken as Quigley stops the barrage of fists, and grabs Manning's arm, hip tossing him out of the corner! Manning is in a pile in the middle of the ring! MEL: Quigley wipes the blood and sweat out of his eyes, before going up top once again! Quigley turns around... what the hell is he thinking?! He's gonna try a moonsault NOW?!! I've never seen him do a moonsault before ANYWAY, let alone 45 minutes into a brutal match! Quigley flies off... beautiful moonsault, but MANNING ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY!! Quigley has just cost himself the title with that stupid move!! VIC: Quigley knew it'd take something spectacular to finish off Manning. He took the risk but it did NOT pay off! Manning is going over to the near unconscious Quigley... he's not going for the cover... he's going for the Masterplex! He has him set up... WAIT A MINUTE! Is that Stephanie Manning running down the aisle?! She has tears streaming down her face! Where was she all this time?! She was supposed to be in Kurt's corner, as usual, but she wasn't for some reason! Maybe we'll find out why! Manning lets Quigley drop to the mat in a heap, and he goes through the ropes, standing on the apron, yelling at Stephanie! Stephanie is pleading with him for some reason! Manning is telling Stephanie to get the hell out of there... and Quigley is actually back up!! Manning and Quigley lie, nearly motionless, amid all the wreckage, as Stephanie may have just made things worse!! Why the hell was she sticking up for Quigley in the first place?! Quigley with a wild swing, Manning ducks and he's got him hooked up for the Masterplex once again!!! Do it!! Do it!!! Manning goes to lift him, but the back gave out!! "QUICKSTRIKE" CHRIS QUIGLEY!!! [The crowd goes absolutely wild, as the referee goes over to Quigley, who is face down on the now crimson mat, and lays the belt next to him. Kurt with the chair... RIGHT OVER HIS FATHER'S HEAD!! OH MY GOD!!! MEL: [under his breath] I can't believe this... I can't believe it... VIC: Diane Manning is screaming at Kurt... Manning slides under the bottom ropes and walks back down the aisle to maybe the loudest booes I have ever heard in my broadcasting career! I can't believe what Kurt Manning did here tonight! VIC: Well folks, Mel speaks the truth. This match will go down in the wrestling legend books, as a truly titanic struggle. For all of us here at TAEWF, that's it for February Fury! We'll seeya soon!
