The WWA Online (Membership Drive)

29 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi there, I am the head booker for the WWA Online and we are on a membership drive. Straight up, we are looking for original characters with good handlers who like to Role-play. We are not looking for fly-by-nighters who haven't got what it takes to stick around. The WWA (World Wrestling Association) was born out of e-wrestling, starting with handouts given to players once a week, to a discussion group area on a BBS, to an series of e-mail leagues, to the amalgmation that we have today of one league, with one world title and 4 years of history behind the wrestlers who are there. We use the TNM 6.2Plus, and we have been referred to by [name withheld] as "...one of my favorites". We are about to have our first PPV on Sunday August 3rd, and after that we will enter into a new format, where we will have 3 cards a week, sent out as detailed summaries and then a weekly round-up show, with high-lights from the week and the 9 best matches. There are 3 bookers, and we are all comitted to this league. So what have you got to lose? Head on over to our website and check it out. And we will gladly answer any and all questions you have. Thanks for your time. Visit The WWA Online http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Loge/6523/
