Females in e-Wrestling (community reply)

24 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pretty good timing for me to see this, because just this week the UWF had the debut of it's first female wrestler, the Lady in Red. I didn't really expect to get into the whole "female division" idea, and still don't really. While that may be fine for leagues with a diverse roster of lady grapplers, the UWF doesn't really have any plans to add a full division(heavyweight, cruisers, tag team and skyweight are about all it can handle!). Even so, the LiR will get the chance to wrestle male opponents, which is clearly what the character wanted. Personally, I don't think there should be a gender gap either, afterall this is roleplaying, and everybody should get the chance to have fun the way they want to.
