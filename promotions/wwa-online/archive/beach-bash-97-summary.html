Beach Bash '97 Summary

4 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BEACH BASH '97 Dateline; Sunday August 3rd, 1997 - Muscle Beach, California ODDBALL CLAIMS WORLD TITLE In what will go down as a very controversial win, The Oakland Oddball won the vacant WWA World Heavyweight title in a tournament finals match against "Nightmare" Nick Steele. The match, plagued with attempts by both The Oakland Oddball and his manager "Maniac" Michael Gates to cheat, finally turned in Oddball's favour, when Gates smashed Steele in the head with the Odd one's surfboard. Special guest referee Don Napolitano missed the attack, due to Oddball's distraction while it happened. Moments later Oddball applied his "Wipeout" Tombstone from the third, and the rest as they say is history. Or is it? This week, as the WWA expands to three cards, one of Thursday, Friday and Saturday, Napolitano has promised to review the match and make some decisions. Also on the card, a 6 man Gauntlet was held to determine the first ever WWA Ironman champion. The six wrestlers eventually drawn were Max Van Smash, "Vicious" Terry Slater, Chaos, "Perfect" Tommy Chapman, "Precious" Percy and Rip Roberts. Van Smash was picked first, and destroyed Slater and then Chaos in record time. Then he face Chapman and was defeated by Count-out after Chapman Perfect Pressed him on the concrete floor. The next match saw Chapman and Percy face each other, and these two tag-partners gave it there all, but Chapman scored the victory to advance. Rip Roberts came to the ring with his new/old manager Freddy "The Weasel" Dixon, still Chapman held his own, and looked like he might win the title, until from out of nowhere, "Golden" Tony Roberts, Rip's brother, clubbed Chapman, allowing Rip to then DDT him for the win and the Ironman belt. The Red Dragon and Jack Morgan settled nothing in their growing feud, when they battled to a 30 minute-time-limit draw. After the match, Morgan revealed backstage that he is not done with The Red Dragon, and intends to face him "..as many times as it takes.". No one is sure where this feud is heading, but hot rumours have suggested that Mr. Ito, Dragon's manager, is looking towards Japan for assistance, and that Morgan is looking towards his own family. In tag-team action, Hot Stuff, who had signed an open contract for the Bash, were as stunned as the crowd when The Nightriders stormed the ring and systematically picked them to pieces. The bizarre team, formerly managed by Lilth were on their own, but were still as dominant as ever. "The Gyroscope" may have ended his feud with The Million Dollar kid, by defeating him in a ladder match. Both wrestlers did an excellent job, but Shimono proved to be the more able in this confrontation, climbing the ladder in the end and grabbing the WWA Beach Bash '97 towel that was suspended over the ring. Jeff Ladd has egg on his face, when Athena, who had chosen him to be her opponent for the right to have a WWA Women's title created, defeated him with an assist from Shanna, another female wrestler. In the match, Athena showed herself to be wrestling a little more aggressively than in the past and she was holding her own. Unfortunately, as many observed Ladd was just the bigger and better wrestler and was on his way to win, when Shanna emerged from the crowd and DDTd him on the outside, causing a count-out victory for Athena. Amanda Reese, Ladd's manager who used to wrestle as Amerika Freedom, attacked Athena and Shanna, who were attempting to flee to the dressing room. She was no match for the toe, but was saved by her former tag-team partner Stevie Power, who was backstage. The brawl was split up when Martin Flagg, Keith Morgan and Craig Reese, Amanda's brother, came down. Richard Press and J.D. Lee battled like titans to see who would be the number one contender to the WWA Crown. Both of these giants took their turns beating each other from pillar to post, but in the end Press nailed the big man with his patented Big Splash for the one, two, three. Lee was enraged and attacked Press, throwing him out of the ring and then Hammer Dropping him through the time-keeper's table. Lee then left Press there and walked back to the locker rooms. Medics assisted Press from the ringside area, but no injuries were reported. "Impact" Andrew Castle, "Deadeye" Dave Wilson and "Cold As Ice" Arron Stone laid a perfect trap for Chris Page. In the tag match, where Page and Castle were partners, Castle turned on Page, joining Wilson and Stone in beating the young Texan. After all was said and done, Castle then told his now former manager "Mister Money" Jarred Milton that he was with Wilson and Stone now and that if Milton did not leave the ring, he would go back to the dressing room on a stretcher, just like Page. Many were surprised by this turn of events, and Chris Page has not yet been available for comment on this matter. Results of Beach Bash '97 - The Nightriders def.
