WWA Insider Edition 1/Volume 1 - 12.08.97 WELCOME Hi and welcome to the first ever edition of the WWA Insider. You may remember me as the WWA Super-Fan. Well it seems the big wigs over at the WWA have decided if they can't beat me they might as well help me. So here I am loyal fans to bring to you the inside scoop on the WWA. The hottest rumors and news bites that the hottest league in the world has to offer. This periodical will not be a weekly or even a monthly thing. It will be produced only when there is news and rumors worth talking about. If you want gossip just head over and check out Lynn Krieg's show, cause that's where the dirt is. I don't care who hates who and who is sleeping with who. All I care about is the wrestling and the contracts and the news stories that are important to the WWA and you the fans. So sit back, strap in and enjoy the first edition of the WWA Insider. CHANGES TO BORN AGAIN Well it seems that the WWA has found itself scrambling after an unfortunate car accident has prevented Women's World Champion Stevie Power from defending her title at the upcoming Born Again PPV. After 24 hours of deliberation, the WWA Championship Committee has come to a decision, and fans of Stevie Power won't like it one bit. The announcement, released to the press only hours ago is as follows. Stevie Power will be stripped of the title and it will be awarded to her challenger Rachael "The Black Pearl" Donovan. But it doesn't end there. Another battle royal will be held on the Born Again card, this one for the right to face the Women's Champion in a match-up later on the card. 10 women will be invited and they will battle it out in an over the top rope fight. But, Donovan will also fight another match on the card, when she will face Amanda Reese, Power's partner and best friend earlier on the card for the title. who ever walks out of that match-up with the belt will face the winner of the battle royal later. As for who will be in the Battle Royal, speculation is that the entire WWA women's roster (minus Donovan and Reese) will be there, but as of press time this roster was only 5 women strong, they being Adrianna MaCintyre, Kim Li, Silver Sphinx, Vikki Vinyl and Shanna. Where will the other 5 women come from? Speculation is that there may be a couple of women left to sign with the WWA and that invitations will be sent out to various women wrestlers from both the IEWA and the GDWA. No word yet as to who they might be. POWER TO BE AT BORN AGAIN From her hospital room today Stevie Power responded to the WWA's announcement concerning the World Women's Title. In a quick statement to the WWA Insider she promised to be at the Born Again PPV to hand over the title to Rachael Donovan and to lend support to her partner and friend Amanda Reese who will be facing Donovan on the card for the belt. Doctors have confirmed that Power will not be able to wrestle for at least 6 months, due to back and leg injuries she suffered in a head-on collision. Power did promise though that she will return to the ring as soon as she is able. WWA ENTERS INTO MYSTERIOUS CONTRACT NEGOTIATIONS Now I don't know who it is. And I certainly haven't heard much more than rumors, but it has come to my attention that the WWA has entered into negotiations with a world famous superstar who is demanding big money and has gotten many WWA superstars up in arms. The name flying around the locker room is none other than Eric "The Viper" Douglas. Now as to whether or not this is true remains to be seen. But if it is true, certainly WWA World Champion Chris Page and WWA Ironman Champion "Fearless" Richard Press are going to have things to say about it. As you may remember just about 3 years ago Eric Douglas ended the career of Texan wrestling legend The Cisco Kid after refusing to release a Figure-Four leglock that wound up breaking both of the Kid's legs. Both Page and Press are protégés of The Cisco Kid and I'm sure would be none to happy to see Douglas head the WWA's way. And if Viper is signed, will he be one of the unannounced men in the battle royal? If her were to win and Page were to hold on to his World title against Castle it would mean that Viper would get the shot at Page. Now that's a match I would love to see. WWA CRUISERWEIGHT TITLE SOON TO BE GONE With Doug Christian's refusal to resign with the WWA the championship committee decided to vacate the World Cruiserweight title, promising a tournament in the new year. The insider on the other hand has seen with his own eyes a WWA Office memo that claims the Cruiserweight title may just be quietly swept under the carpet and forgotten. And who's name did I see on that memo? None other than WWA President Don Napolitano's. While the WWA has officially maintained that the title will not be dropped and that a tournament will go through in the new year, one can't help but notice the lack of cruiserweights in the WWA's current roster. Hmmmmm? WWA LOOKING TO EXPAND INTO EUROPE With the recent troubles of the ILCW and it's monopoly on European wrestling, the WWA has begun to negotiate with some of the member leagues, especially those in England, France and Germany to possibly do a tour later in '98 and maybe even begin cooperative ventures with these leagues. Remember that it has been a long time since a primarily American league has made strong ventures into European territories as the fans there are loyal and dedicated to the European sport. LPWL GONE BUT NOT FORGOTTEN Recently it was announced that the LPWL was going to be restarted and enter into partnership with a couple of other leagues. Well it is now official, female wrestling legend and owner of the LPWL Sandra Bullet has accepted a booking position with the WWA, thereby agreeing not to pursue a reopening of her league. Bullet will directly handle the WWA World Women's title and the upcoming World Women's Tag Team titles. MORTY ROBBINS TO MANAGE RED MENACE Morty Robbins has announced that he has signed on as the manager of the Red Menace. Robbins, who already manages Max VanSmash, announced this coup from his Las Vegas home late last night. Robbins feels that the Soviet legends have what it takes to become the WWA World Tag Team champions and warns that High Impact had best not grow too attached to their belts. Robbins has managed 3 different teams to 5 tag team championships, including the former NAWA World Tag Team Champions Sunset Boulevard. Will he do the same for the Red Menace? Only time will tell. BORN AGAIN ALMOST SOLD OUT Even without a completely confirmed line-up for the December 21st event, ticket sales in Phoenix have been so steady that the box-office predicts the event will sell out at least a week before it occurs. Certainly the WWA has provided a great deal of mystery to this event by not releasing all the names in the 20 man battle royal or all the names in the 10 woman battle royal. One match that has been added and confirmed is The Marquis will pick up the open contract offered by Ironman Champion "Fearless" Richard Press. The Marquis has challenged with a Iron Maiden match, which is for all intents and purposes a Casket Match with the Iron Maiden substituting for the casket. The only difference I can see is this, an Iron Maiden stand upright, while a casket lays flat. So I guess it will take quite a bit for the wrestler to be secured in the Maiden and then have it closed on them. Should be interesting. WRAP UP Well that concludes the first edition of the WWA Insider, I hope you enjoyed it and I look forward to any comments or questions you might have. And hey I can't be everywhere at all times, so if you have learned a really rocking piece of news e-mail it to me. If I use your scoop, you will be added to my list of honorary insiders. Until next time, peace. The Insider
