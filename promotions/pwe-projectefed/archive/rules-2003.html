Rules

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1. MAIN RULES 1a) The PWE is not like the WWE, we didnt want to copy the WWE name so we made our own name called the PWE, which stands for Pro Wrestling Entertainment! We have many diffrent idea being thrown around in the PWE Management...were trying to get them down! 1b) No "TOSing" PWE Members...grow up allittle bit guys! If you dont like them or got a grudge against them...go to request a match but you got to have a decent reason why the match should take place... 1c) You can only control one wrestler here in the PWE...if you want to retire him and start over on a new guy and career, thats all in good! But the wrestler may make it to the hall of fame, if he does only you can bring them back...if the wrestler doesnt....he can be used by the next person that wants him! 1d) Original Wrestlers only allowed into the PWE, because it gives out better quality role plays and shows how creative you can be instead of just copying! 1e) If you sign a contract with the PWE, he cant be used in any other inter-fed that PWE belongs too! So make sure you dont have that problem because then you will have to pick which fed you want! If you choose to leave, your character is still allowed to be used in the PWE but he will be "JOBBED"! 1f) No challenging for PWE Staff Positions! You will lose all the time, unless its part of a storyline...thats the only exception! 1g) You cant "TAKE OUT" other wrestlers and others! That means "NO KILLING", seriously Injury etc...etc... If you do use another wrestlers in your RP to attack them you cannot just beat on them and knock them out, let them at least try to fight you back...(Be realistic)! This is called "God Moding". This means that you are making your character so invincable that he is like god! THIS WILL NOT BE ALLOWED OR TOLERATED! 1h) You can't make "CONTRACTS" for matches that are or will result in a titile shot nor use it to hinder another wrestler in any way without the permission of the PWE Staff!!! (That means half has to agree to it) 1i) PWE MANAGEMENT CAN'T WRESTLE! If the PWE management want to wrestle due to a reason or a "Special" storyline, then the rest of the PWE Staff will come up with the match! 1j) PWE Management must be notified if you wish to attack or do anything with another character not under your control, even if you got the other player(s) permission, for storyline reasons! This is an attack fed but you must do this reasonably so you can only attack 2 times in a card!! As such all must respond to each attack by 24 hours of the posting! All must RP accordingly to the PWE Management will decide if and how the attack went about in the results. The results are law what happened in the results is what actually happened! As a sum up of all the events in a particular card(S)!! 1k) All decision and results are to our discrection and are final when the PWE Management makes there decision! NO BUTS ABOUT IT! SO DON'T GO CRYIN' ABOUT IT!! 1l) When you attack or are attacked we; PWE Management will use the following injury system: DAMAGE FACTOR SYSTEM: 1-3= Can wrestle without problems, just minor injuries. 4-6= Can Wrestle but injury get worse or effect outcome of the match, which will be decided from the role plays by the injured character and opponnets! (Can still win matches with no problem but the injury will play a role in the results!) 7-8= Should not wrestle (DOCTORS ORDERS!!) injuries are serious, but wrestler has the option too or not. ( If wrestler decides to wrestle he/she can win, but the greater chances are you will lose the match and get hurt even more) 9-10= CANNOT WRESTLE!! If character wrestle or has any physical confrontations he/she is risking thier career! (WILL LOSE THE MATCH AUTOMATICALLY!) ***NOTE***: Wrestlers who have serious injuries (7-10), cant be attackes unless they put themselves in that positions. (EXAMPLE: if they attack you, or they go to the ring, leaving themselves open to be attacked or if you get the permission from the PWE Management!!! Seriously injured wrestlers should stay away from the arena/ring to aviod more injury! Any attempts to attack seriously injured wrestlers will result in a penalties to you wrestle which will be decided by PWE Management! 2. Roleplay Rules: 2a) All RPs are due by midnight Eastern Standard Time! 2a1) Saturday Night Shockwave roleplay(pormos) are due Friday Night at Midnight Eastern Time! 2a2) Pay Per View roleplay(pormos) are due Saturday Night at Midnight Eastern Time! 2b) No personal business on the boards, use the OOC(Out Of Character)posts to be posted on the OOC Board! 2c) No terroristic threats unless it's done as part of your RP torwards the other "wrestler(s)" or character(s) 2d) Roleplays anytime you want as long as you want but make it relevent to the building of the storyline or your match! 2e) MOST IMPORTANTLY HAVE FUN!!!! 2f). If you got a match and dont Role Play, your wrestler will get strikes aganst them and/or suffer losses, be suspended, "JOBBED" which means you will not be shwcased on any of the big ecents and will have to "Try Out" again to get showcased, stripped od title(S), fired(5th Strike), or banned from the PWE in which case you will have to "TRY OUT" again with another character!!! If for some reason you wish to retire your wrestler, you must post it on the OOC board or as an Role Play on the RP Board announcing it! If you have a title them the title will become "vacant" effective the next card or when the PWE management sees fit! The same goes if you leave the PWE by improper means. At which time the PWE Managment will render a decison based on the title rules & by special circumstances. 3. Title Rules: 3a) All titles will exchange hands only if a match has been fought and won by pinfall, submission, or by a special stipulation in the match! Forefitures will only gain the wrestler a win or loss on there record. But no titles will be awarded! If the Champ Forefits the match, the title will become "vacant" and the PWE Mangement will decide the best time to assign a match for the title! The PWE does not hand titles, we have vaule and Pride in them and our Wrestlers should too! So we want you to earn them and you should feel the same. 3b) Most of the titles are pretty normal like those in real life wrestling except for the "Hardcore Title" *HARDCORE TITLE: 3c) It is 24/7 title which means it can be fought for at anytime and anywhere as long as you have a ref with you. Now in reality I know you cannot possible be on a PC for 24hrs a day 7 days a week so this is the PWE rule: 3c1)At random we (the PWE Management) will announce that from a certain time to a certain time or a specific time it will be open season on the Hardcore Title! You can only role play once for the 24/7 attack!!! Now its up to the current champ to go roleplay for rolepplay to defend his/her title or making his/her escape! If someone stops him/her from escaping by RPing before the time limit! The winner will be the one who out roleplays the 24/7 Hardcore Champ! The Champ has until the deadline to respond to all of the "24/7" attacks of the other wrestlers! If the champ does his OFFICAL MATCH Roleplay at he percise deadline he/she must respond to the attacks 1 hour after their posting!! No losses will be added if you fail to get the title(unless you're the champ and you lose and then in which case you will gain all the loss from the wrestler that beat you!) No wins will be added if you get the title either! Your reward is simply getting the title (unless you're the champ and you win and then in which case you will gain a win for all the wrestlers that you out roleplayed!) If in event the champ doesn't role play, he will lose his title and will suffer other Penalties determinded by us (the PWE Management) and the wrestlers who wanted to fight for the title will fight in a HARDCORE type of match by us (the PWE Management) when we see appropriate!
