This should be good. (#21)

18 April 2003

This should be good. Vikki Addison PWE Roleplay #21 Date: 4/18/2003 Location: Scene opens up in backstage in the PWE area. The locker room to be exact. It is a long white hallway, with a blue light floor with the lights reflecting down from the cieling. The hallway has white walls and blue doors, with a few tables on the side. Vikki Addison is leaning against the wall, she is in her usual in ring attire, looking at the camera. She puts a magazine down that she was reading on the table next to her, and takes a sip of coffee in a white cup she is holding. Vikki Addison: So, on Shockwave I did what I say I would do, I went in and defeated The Unknown Dragon, I give him the credit for facing me, but he just wasnt ready for me, and now is not his time. As of right now, I go in Shockwave against Scott Curtis in a Hell in a cell match, with Keshia Williams already on my back. Keshia, I heard what you have said, and believe me, now is not the time for you to be jumping on me, I got bigger things to worry about, you can sit back and relax while I take everybody out, then I will see you down the road. But right now I got something bigger to worry about, and that is Scott Curtis. Vikki Addison: Scott, listen to me, you may think I have squeaked by on Shockwave, you may think I have cheated, but Scott, anything goes on Shockwave, we can use anything, whether it is chairs or brass knuckles, if you are as tough as you say you are, you will put up a good fight and forget about who is cheating, because reality is going to smack you in the face during this match, and that reality is mine. Chris, you do not understand this, but I will do whatever it takes to win. I will wrestle, fight, bleed or sweat, if I were you, then cheating is the only way of beating me. And Chris, you are just slipping away, and I love it. Vikki pauses and takes a sip of her coffee, then place sit back down. Vikki Addison: Let me ask you something Chris, are you prepared for someone like me? I am the Dark Angel in here in PWE, and I do not take people lightly, and I believe you are the light one here. I did choose to go against the males, and I expect you to put up a good fight, because if you don't, I am just waisting my time, and the PWE title should just come to me. Scott, you tell me I chose to tangle with the big boys, and you are right, you expect this to be a walk in the park, don't you? You believe you can just step right over me and move on, right? Believe me Scott, I am not your first girlfriend who was all soft and easy on you, I will run right over you or use my fists if I have to. I'm actually looking forward to this, I have not been officially welcomed into the PWE, this will be the wake up call for me, so Scott, I am saying there is a chance that you might win, but your going to have to use both sides of the brain, I have faced other opponents like you, I have seen people just like you, and they panic. So Scott, don't tell me that cheaters never win, because you will regret it, especially in a dangerous match like this, and there is no turning back, so you better be ready, I am ready, I am in the best shape ever, I'm coming at you full speed ahead, and you're just like my other opponent, unknown. Who is on your side? Last week on Shockwave, while you and I were standing in the ring face to face, who was the crowd cheering for? Me. Who are the announcers behind? Me. I got everyone behind me back, Scott, you are just lost in the wilderness, and you're fighting skills are just as bad. Scott, you need to watch last weeks Shockwave over and over, tell me, what happened during your match? You were left on your back, bleeding. If Landen Ozz can get the job done like that, this is a merry go round for me. So Scott, you can bring your brass knuckles if you want, bring anything, I'll come barehanded, and I'll bring the punishment. You are my next step towards the title, and I thank you for that. This tournament is just getting better and better...and the more I think about it the more I like it. Vikki walks away.
