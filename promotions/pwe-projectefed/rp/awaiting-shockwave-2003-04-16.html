Awaiting Shockwave. Keshia Williams PWE Roleplay #19 Date: 4/16/03 Location: Keshia Williams' Apartment in New York City, New York. Date: Wednesday April 16, 2003 Time: 4:42 p.m Average Wednesday afternoon, average channels on T.V, average channel flipping. I'm sitting here in my white t-shirt and black sweats with dirty socks enjoying day-to-day channel flipping. Do I wanna watch MTV?....Nope. Switch. BET?....Nah. Let's try it again. Sportscenter....hey I'm up for that. Let's see if the Bucs are making anymore transactions. Awww crap caught a commerical. 'Pro Wrestling Entertainment proudly presents it's weekly action packed, Saturday Shockwave. The show has been postponed from Saturday, April 12, 2003 to April 19. Expect to see high impact action as the undisputed tourney continues with high profile matches. Brad DeriX goes up against Dante Jones and Vikki Addison gets closed in against Scott Curtis. The exictment doesn't stop there, also on that night we will see a three way match with Ulitmo Guerrera vs. The Russian Hammer vs. Keshia Williams, PWE's hottest new addition. Tickets are still on sale but are almost sold out. Check out PWE action live from the Arrowhead Pond in Anaheim, Ca.' Ha pretty good commerical hopefully we'll catch good business with that. I get up and walk out of my living room to my kitchen where my fridge is full of.........Nothin! Damn I would really like some milk for my fruity pebbles cereal. But then I gotta get up, get some shoes on. Head out to my 2003 Silver Bentley Arnage T and go to the store and get me some milk.......I'm too lazy. To hell with it. I walk back to my favorite couch and continue watching my sportscenter. A tall black man with braids is on the screen ummmmmmm....... 'Reminds me of that Russian Hammer character. The Russian Hammer is quite a looker. Maybe it's just me but he really doesn't look Russian. He looks like a grade A gang banger that be smoking on the corner of 110th street. But hey, like I said.....that's just me right?' I sigh a bit and my stomach growls in anger of hunger. Gezz....I think I can last without eating for a while. I need to take my lazy butt to the gym and get fresh for this match on Saturday. 'Yeah that Russian Hammer is quite a looker, sounds like a high school crush. It is a shame I gotta kick his butt on Saturday. I watched his little press conference. Too bad I'm from New York, not Florida. But hey I'm honored seriously. I hear good looking women come from Florida. I take that as a compliment. But nothing gets me like his multiple names. Now I understand nicknames and everything but at one point his name is Thunde...Thrnu....whatever it is. Then his name is Hunter, then we have The Russian Hammer. Oh and get this, his real name is Uzi? Like a gun? What the...? I mean hey maybe it's because I'm American I must not understand it. Maybe he's describing something. His he is hung as big as an uzi or something......ohh god what if he is? Hammer my number is [detail withheld] if that is true. You probably got big feet don't you? Just letting ya know. Holla at cha girl aight.' I smile at the idea of Hammer hung like a uzi....that sounds reall.....wait girl get a hold of yourself, focus....he's the emeny after all. 'Oh and one more thing to ya Hammer....I got the feeling that during your press conference. There was a reference made about me and whatever other than that whole Florida misunderstanding. I think you have the current mindset that I'm a male, or something of that sort. They were refering me as a male so I guess I'm correct. Well Hammer you continue with that state of mind....you know that I'm a boy and everything. Cause you'll be very surprised to see what ends up walking up toward the ring on Saturday night. And you'll be even more surprised when you end up looking up at the lights while I'm looking down at your highly beaten body. I mean hey your 6'8', and true you weigh twice as much as me. But there's something that I have that you will never be able to grasp....that something just might give me the edge I need. It just might be my line to victory, and that's a bitches mentality. While your testosterone is running high and you taunt and jive around. You may think I'm out for the count. While your in that state of mind.....I'm willing to do whatever it takes to win. Whatever it takes.....just like Vikki did with the famous chairshot heard round the world, Hammer....whatever it takes. See you Saturday sexy.' I take a satisfying breath and curl more into my couch. Another commerical god. 'Oh and Ultimo Guerrera. I also heard from the press conference that your quite the big talker......................hear that......................that's silence. Where you at big boy. I thought you was a force in here. Right now you sound like.......wait a minute. You sound like nothing!! If I'm supposed to be hearing something from you it's awfully quiet. I mean I hope no one has turned you into a deaf-mute. I'm wondering if I'm actually gonna see you show up Saturday. Hey...hey....PWE.....you smell something cause I smell something. **sniff, sniff** I smell a pussy! Rite, Rite.' A Silver Dollar will make ya Holla!
