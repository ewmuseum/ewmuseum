EWCW: The Big One, Part One

1 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here it is, EWCW THE BIG ONE, part one of three. (Opening video for EWCW's BIG ONE!!!) Vince Lawler: Welcome everyone, to the first EWCW: THE BIG ONE!!! Tonight we have every title on the line TWICE! And we have the first round of the Lightweight Tournament!! Jerry McMahon: Yeah, but the REAL big win, is seeing ME, Jerry McMahon! VL: Let's go to the ring! (Disco Inferno blasts over the loudspeaker) Howard Buffer: And now, in the opening match, for the EWCW Super Junior Title, here is DUUUUUUUUUUUUUUDE FOOOOOOOOOLLLLLEY!!!!!!! VL: And here he comes, that Disco King, Dude Foley!! JM: Oh puh-lease!! I can't stand this guy! If I didn't have a duty here, I'd go finish him now! HB: And his opponent, making his EXTREME DEBUT, led by Miss Christine, "Franchise" Shane Stud!!! JM:Yeah, now HERE is a piece of talent! We'll have to see… VL:Here we go, EWCW Super Junior Title, we are under way, and Dude slaps on a sleeperhold. Stud gets out, but gets nailed with a lariat, and the crowd goes wild!! First, Jerry, let's talk about the card tonight, in hour one, we have Pimp Daddy and The Hustler facing a combo of LFS, not yet known. JM:Well, THAT should be good, two quality teams going at it! But I am looking forward to the debut of BLOODLUST, and seeing DFR's Ghetto Boy LOSE his world title. I hate those punks! VL:Well, maybe so, and I think they feel the same. In hour two, fans, we have the first bracket of the Lightweight Tournament, and in hour three, more title matches! JM:Well, it should be interesting seeing that lightweight action, but I'm waiting for Monday Night, and seeing DFR go at it TWICE!! HAHAHA! VL:Let's go back to the match. Stud to the ropes, he misses a kick, and gets a belly-to belly suplex! Mule kick, but he is up, and takes control JM:OW! Nice neckbreaker, I think he's trying to injure Dude. He whips Foley to the ropes, and that punk hits a shoulderblock!! I can't believe it! VL:Well you better, because the Dudeness is on a roll!! He gets a two count, and nails a lariat. This match going back and forth, and Franchise hits a Dragon Suplex, two count, and a facerake. JM:YEAH! That's what I like to see! VL:What? CHEATING?? JM:Hey, it's only cheating if you get caught! VL:Anyways, Dude takes Stud down with a series of kicks, and a fallaway slam. Two count, and that is all he'll get. Franchise, the young rookie, not faring too well here. He goes for a belly-to-belly, and it is blocked. JM:Stud now, tossed out, but like a true fighter, gets back in. Foley with those cheating kicks, they look like low-blows!! VL:Will you stop?! JM:No I won't, you loser. DAMN! Another neckbreaker, and again, a low-blow by Foley! DQ him ref! VL:Well, I saw that one, and I don't condone it! SMALL PACKAGE! NEW CHAMP, no only a two. He hits the belly-to-belly, only a two again. He is pulling it all out! JM:Let's hope he DOESN'T pull it all out! Knee by Stud, and a chop. Foley reverses, and these idiots like him!! VL:They don't like him, they love him!! Sleeper, and Franchise is going out!! He can't keep his arm up! If it goes down, he's out! It drops! Wait, it landed on the rope! He gets kicked, and he retaliates with a hard right hand! JM:Chop by DUD, and two more! YES! Stud nails him! He is trying to get him on the top rope, but it's blocked. Now nailing him with kicks, belly-to-back, Dude blocks it, and Franchise is whipped to the ropes. VL:Dude off the rope, flying forearm, ONE....TWO......THREE!!! HB:Your winner, and stiiiiiiill EWCW Super Junior Champion, DUUUUUUDE FOOOOOOOOOOLEEEEY!!!! VL:Next up, yet another title match, with LFS' L.A. Nova, and The King Of Light facing Pimp Daddy and The Hustler in Tag Title Action! JM:THIS should be good! VL:And here come the champs, LFS!!!! Making their entrance, the "LIGHTER" side of DFR. And here comes Hustler and Pimp Daddy. JM:YEAH! Here we go, LFS takes it as Nova startsm he is just pounding on Hustler! Hustler comes back and NAILS a backdrop over the top! It could be over now! VL:Yes, some great tag action, Hustler now throws Nova into the railing, and the post! JM:I've gotta wonder, Vince, if Nova is gonna take the Lightweight Title too, he is in the tournament tonight! VL:He sure is, and so is a mystery on Monday night to fight in the tournament against Billy McNamara! JM:Yeah, I hope they pound their heads in!! HAHAHA! VL:Nova tags out to the King, and nails a clawhold on the forehead of Hustler! It has to be over! He breaks it, but he is hurt. Elbow by the King, he misses a headbutt, and Pimp Daddy is tagged! JM:Here we go! He hits the King with an elbow smash, in comes Nova, he's nailed too! Pimp whipped to the rope, OH, he bumped into the ref! VL:This is trouble! Pimp is coming back, and Hustler is tagged in to help! In comes Nova, and he hits Hustler with an enzuigiri! The head kick! They are outside, and Hustler goes into the rail! We have to take a break, we'll be back! VL:We're back, and during the break, they kept it oustide! They are double-teaming Nova, and the crowd is behind Hustler. JM:This is the kind of action I like to see! VL:They go back in, and it's the King and Pimp. Kneebreaker, and I fear the end for Pimp and Hustler. In comes Nova, but Daddy hits a football tackle! Cobra clutch by King, and Pimp gets to the ropes. Nova leaves, and Pimp takes control. JM:I tell ya, I have never seen tag action like this, Vince! It's awesome, from the best e-league EVER, EWCW! VL:I tend to agree with you there, and that is a bit scary.. JM:Well, it is Halloween. VL:I know that's right. Pimp nails a thunderous DDT on King, and, what's this? King just got a lamp from a fan! He nails Pimp with it! JM:Bright Idea! VL:I guess, but Hustler has another one. To make the save. Claw on Pimp, he escapes, and hits an octopus hold. Pimp comes back with a leg lariat, and an armbar. JM: There it is! THE PIMP PLEX!! Oh! Only a two count, and Nova is tagged. Great teamwork, like a well oiled machine. VL:Yessir. Double backdrop by LFS, and King leaves the ring. Doctor bomb, one……two……thr…… Wait, the bell rang! I am getting word the time limit expired! JM:Oh boy, now what? VL:The match is gonna continue! Nova hits a hiptoss, and a kick. Goes for a slam, but is escaped. JM:He just got hammered!!!! VL:Missed DDT by Pimp, and is backdropped. Nova is throwing him around like a rag doll! Suplex, and a two count only. Nova is complaining to the ref about a slow count, Pimp rolls him up, ONE…………TWO………THREE!!!!!! WE HAVE NEW CHAMPIONS!!!!!!!!!!!!!! HB:Your winners, and NEW EWCW Tag Team Champions, Pimp Daddy, and The Hustler!!!! VL:The crowd is going nuts, we have new champions!!! JM:All I can say is WOW!! VL:Yeah!! We have to take another break, but when we get back, BLOODLUST makes his debut for the TV Title! (scene unrolls to Westside working out) Westside: You know, now that the EWCW is back and running I have decided to lose some weight. I am down at 225 so I can compete with these light niggas. I want the Leight weight son of a bitch in the ring after he wins. I will claim the title and I will rule. Hey DFR I am looking for a stable to join. I want to apply to be a member. Uh oh this nigga challenged me to a match I better kick his ass. (scene fades out) VL:Well, how about THOSE comments from Westside?? JM:As far as I'm concerned, anyone that asks to join that group of pansies DFR suck! VL:Hey, Hey, Hey! Calm down a little bit. JM:What? Are you with DFR?? VL:No, now let's get back to the ring. VL:Well, here comes BLOODLUST, making his debut, a man we know NOTHING about! JM:Yeah, and here comes the favorite, Babyface Killer! VL:Bloodlust takes the early lead, and throws Killer over the top! WOW, what strength! JM:I guess. Babyface has the power too, he just powerbombed Bloodlust on the concrete floor!! VL:This is shaping up to be a good one! They are back in, and Babyface takes control, but is suplexed hard! JM:Lok at this guy, the crowd already like him! Two count, and a DDT! Another failed pin, and is just over-powering the baby here. VL:Now hang on, he is a great athlete! JM:Yeah, whatever! VL:Anyways, Babyface is being pounded, wait, Double Underhook Suplex, and the crowd is on their feet! Bloodlust comes right back, though, and they go back outside. Babyface into the railing, and they go back in. Brainbuster, and a dragon suplex by Bloodlust. This guy can do it all! JM:And then some, I like this guy! VL:That's a surprise, you never like anyone! Bloodlust continuing with the power, and that is what this crowd likes! DDT, and a kneedrop. The crowd is chanting for Bloodlust! I have never seen a response like this! Babyface to the rope, he hits a backdrop, and goes for a legdrop. Bloodlust moves, and goes for a pin, only a two, and he hits a splash. JM:BIG splash! Bloodlust looks great, maybe DFR will get this talent! VL:I shudder to think! Babyface reverses an Irish Whip, and in goes Bloodlust. He gets clotheslined, and hit by a sunset flip. One count, and Bloodlust has lifted him up with one hand!! Brain buster. He puts him on the turnbuckle, what is this? POWERBOMB FROM THE TOP!!! ONE…TWO…THREE!!!! We have another new champion!! HB:Your winner, and NEEEEEEEEEEEEW EWCW TV Champion, BLOODLUST!!!!!! JM:If this is a sign of things to come, tonight is gonna kick a- VL:Easy big fella!! VL:OK, here we have two great superstars, two different weight classes, but on style. This is gonna be a match where the end will come when one man makes a mistake. JM:Yeah, and it WON'T be the Nature Boy! VL:We'll see! Here comes Gunslinger, The US Champ, from DFR. And now the Nature Boy. Heeeeeeeeeeere we go! Gunslinger takes the early advantage, he goes for a claw, it's countered by a body slam. JM:Abdominal stretch, Billy gets out, and a kick by Nature Boy. He punches him in the head, but gets nailed with an enzuigiri! VL:Ow, that's gotta hurt! Well, folks, we will be back after this! VL:We're back, and it has been back and forth through the break. Gunslinger with a lariat, two count, he throws him into the corner, and charges. Nature Boy moves, and slaps ona headlock. JM:McNamara to the corner, and blocks an earringer attempt. Northern Lights on Nature Boy, two count, and the crowd is going wild! They are looking tot he back. VL:Wait, it's the Horsemen!! They are coming to the ring! He's going for the Six Shooter Slam, and the Horsemen are in!! They are beating the hell outta Gunslinger!! And here come the officials! They are holding DFR back, we are seeing a brawl, and they go into the crowd!! The officials are breaking it up, let's get the word, although, I think I know what it is. HB:Your winner, by DQ, and still EWCW US Champion, Gunslinger Billy McNamara!! VL:No surprise there, and as they are cleaning this up, we will break, and return for the main event of hour one!! VL:We're back, and The Ghetto Boy is ready to face Bull Dozier! JM:Yeah, maybe we'll see a new champ!! VL:Maybe so. JM:Here comes the punk from DFR, and now Bull. VL:Let's go to the ring. Ghetto nails a powerslam early, and gets a two, neckbreaker, and he puts Bull on the turnbuckle. JM:Ghetto Bomb!! Oh No!!! He got the three! My god, DFR IS A Dark Force Rising!! VL:I guess!
