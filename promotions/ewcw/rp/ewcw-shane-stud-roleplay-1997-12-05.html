EWCW Shane Stud Roleplay

5 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Franchise Shane Stud is in the dressing room. He looks really pissed.] "This SUCKS!" he screams. "I am tired of this crap! Two LOSSES IN A ROW!" [Shane smashed a bottle of water against the wall.] "Look, I am the Franchise because i am damn tough enough to be! And Aztek, I admit that you beat me, but we all know that it could have gone either way. And the fact of the matter is that, everyone knows that when you and I step back in the ring, I will prove, ONCE AGAIN, that the wrestler will beat a brawler. "You may be bigger and stronger than me, but I am the greatest wrestler in this federation. And I will get my US Title back." [Shane takes a deep breathe.] "I want this match soon, because I want to be the one that will dethrone you! Come on Aztek, do you accept the challenge? I took yours and gave you a title shot, do you have enough SACK to give me the rematch? I am waiting for your reply!"
