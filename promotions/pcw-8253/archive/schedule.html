The final two weeks depends on if it's pay-per-view time or if it's time for a Pacific Coast Highway Tour for some and the Coastal Tour for others. For example, a match between 'Hollywood' Hogan and Sting would be posted as follows: • Sting d. Hogan @ 7:30 • via the Scorpion Deathlock • *** • NOTE: Sting locks the Scorpion on Hogan as NWO-Hollywood & NWO-Wolfpack brawl outside the ring. The first line denotes who the winner of the match is and the time of the contest. The second line is how they won. The third is how good of a match (out of *****). This is a good gauge of how well the two wrestlers are performing as a whole. A 5-star match means that both handlers are doing an excellent job, a 3-star means the winner has been doing a solid job but the looser got jobbed. A 1-star match.... well, let's hope that we never have one of those :) The 'NOTE' section allots for sneak attacks and run-ins to be reported, so strats/attacks will be considered in the booking of these matches. [image: PCW Tuesday Trifecta] • Held on the third Tuesday of the rotating schedule, all PCW titles will be defended in triple-threat matches. All matches will be written in full. Weeks 5a-6a {PPV Weeks} WEEK SUN MON TUES WED THUR FRI SAT 5a Coastal Report - - PCW Primetime - - PCW Main Event 6a Coastal Report - PPV Pre-Game Show - - - PPV Spectacular The only difference between this schedule and the first four weeks is the second week. The PPV PREGAME SHOW would be filled with mostly interview spots and possibly two or three matches. The point of this card is to build heat for the upcoming ppv. Handlers should be warned than if you're scheduled on the ppv you MUST participate on the pregame show, otherwise you can expect to job at the ppv. Like any fed, the mega-card with mega-matches will happen at the ppv's. Weeks 5b-6b {Tour Weeks} WEEK SUN MON TUES WED THUR FRI SAT 5b Coastal Report - PCH Tour Day 1 PCW Primetime PCH Tour Day 2 - PCH Tour Day 3 6b Coastal Report Coastal Countdown PCH Tour Day 4 - Coastal Carnival Day 1 PCH Tour FNFT Coastal Carnival Day 2 PACIFIC COAST WRESTLING will be featuring an innovative, exciting feature that you don't see in most e-feds -- and that is the concept of wrestling tours. One tour will be the Pacific Coast Highway Tour in which eight PCW superstars will venture down the Pacific Coast Highway on cards separate from the rest of the PCW roster. The other tour will be the Coastal Tour which will be open to the rest of the wrestlers, will take them to venues outside of the Pacific Coast, and will culminate in a mega two-day event. PACIFIC COAST HIGHWAY TOUR Eight PCW superstars take off down the road on a tour that will feature: ROOMMATES: While on tour each wrestler will be paired with another to room with while on the road. At a minimum roommates will be required to wrestle as a tag team once and give one interview. But be warned, this is a big part of of the PCH Tour, and it would behoove you to take it seriously because participation, or lack there of, will come into play in the booking -- especially FNFT booking. THE UNEXPECTED: Have you ever played the game "Oregon Trail"? Well, if you have you know that unexpected things like "Tommy breaks his leg -- loose 3 days" happen periodically. While nobody will fall victim to tuberculosis on tour, unexpected things will occasionally happen. Some things bad (slight injury that could cause losses, roommate looses key & you get no sleep). Some things good (win the lottery & can afford a bigger room). But things will happen, and how you deal with them in interviews will be noted. THE LEARNING CURVE: Most of all, the PCH Tour should be viewed as a chance to learn: dealing with another handler as a roommate, dealing with unexpected occurrences, dealing with losses. Yes, dealing with losses. Nobody will go an entire tour without loosing a match. Nobody. But how you deal with these losses is what's important. If a handler does an exceptional job of dealing with three straight losses, when it comes time for FNFT booking they'd be more apt to get the win over a guy who's wrestler only lost a single match but did nothing with it. In the end, FNFT is the only card that carries any weight. Consider it the measuring stick of the tour. PCH TOUR DAY 1 -- OPENING NIGHT • The opening card for the Pacific Highway Tour. Four one-on-one matches, three written in event center format with one full match. PCH TOUR DAY 2 -- ROOMMATE RIOT • As a part of the Pacific Coast Highway Tour wrestlers will be paired with a roommate they will travel with on the road. On "Roommate Riot" tag teams consisting of roommates will wrestle each other. Two matches, both written in full. PCH TOUR DAY 3 -- ROOMIES • Day 3 of the Pacific Highway Tour will be seven matches reported in results only. With this many matches all wrestlers will wrestle several times. The main point of this card will be the 'roommate interviews', a non-optional part of being a part of the tour PCH TOUR DAY 4 -- CHALLENGE NIGHT • Inevitably on the tour somebody's going to get on your nerves. This is the card that you can call somebody out. The number of matches depend on how many challenges are made, but only one match will be written in full. PCH TOUR FNFT • By far the most important card of the tour, with first round matches written in event center format and the semi-finals and finals written in full. A tournament with rankings based on win/loss record over the course of the tour. But win's and losses aren't what's important in the booking in this card -- how well you did overall is. So winning this is an honor, and all champions will be enshrined in the PCH Tour Hall of Fame and will be invited back when it comes time for a "Champions Only" tour. COASTAL TOUR While eight PCW superstars are traveling down the Pacific Coast Highway, the rest of the PCW roster will be on a separate tour that will often times carry them away to a distant coast -- sometimes the Eastern seaboard of the United States, sometimes the coast of France, sometimes up & down Japan. The first week of the tour will consist of two normal cards on Monday (Koastal Kickoff) and Wednesday (Primetime). Everybody on the roster will be guaranteed a match on one of the two days, but only one match for each card will be written in full. The real fun of the tour begins on week two: COASTAL COUNTDOWN • Eight matches, all written in event center format. Some matches will be one-on-one. Some will be triple threat. Some will be survivor series. But eight matches with eight winners who will advance to wrestle in a tournament on Coastal Carnival, Day 1. COASTAL CARNIVAL; 1ST DAY • The winners of 'Coastal Countdown' advance to wrestle on this day in a tournament card with the first round written in event center format and the semi-finals and finals written in full. The winner of this event will face the PCW Coastal Champion on Coastal Carnival, Day 2. COASTAL CARNIVAL; 2ND DAY • The second day of the Coastal Carnival will be highlighted by the winner of day 1 versus the Coastal Champion. Also on this day all champions on the tour will defend their titles and all remaining participants will compete in a battle royal with the winner receiving a title shot at the Coastal Champion before the next ppv. Most matches will be written in full, but the main event as well as the battle royal will be for sure. ---- ©1998 PCW, a Punk Ass Production.|Mail PCW [image: setstats]
