Pacific Coast Wrestling Titles ---- PACIFIC COAST WRESTLING CHAMPIONSHIP BELTS Like any federation, PACIFIC COAST WRESTLING will recognize the best-of-the-best with championship belts. In the PCW there will be three singles titles and one tag team title. PACIFIC COAST HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION • The 'granddaddy' of the championships. The top guy at any given time will be wearing this belt. To capture the belt a wrestler has to exhibit skill in guestbook interviews, make an effort to be seen on cards in both frequent matches and on-card interviews and most importantly, be able to draw and sustain heat. To hold onto the belt the wrestler needs to prove that they are really the top guy in the fed. The belt must be defended on Tuesday Trifecta and all ppv's, but if you're only putting it on the line that often it doesn't say much for you being the top guy in the fed, now does it? Only wrestlers listed in the top-10 can challenge for the title. Champ Won From When How Hale NA August 16, 1998 Won 8 man mega tourney at "One Night In Hollywood" PACIFIC COAST CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION • To wear this belt you must be under 235 lbs., but to be the top Cruiserweight in the PCW you are going to have to be the class of the division in every way -- interviews, matches, character. Everything. The belt must be defended on Tuesday Trifecta and all ppv's, but any Cruiserweight worth the title will put it on the line nearly every week. All Cruiserweights will be able to challenge for the title. Champ Won From When How Daft Vader NA August 16, 1998 Won 8 Cruiserweight Guantlet at "One Night In Hollywood" PCW COASTAL TITLE • The Coastal Title in PACIFIC COAST WRESTLING is essentially the working man's title, as it will be defended on every Main Event, Tuesday Trifecta, Coastal tour and ppv. To capture the title a wrestler must exhibit the ability to roleplay against a wide variety of characters effectivly and be the type of character that can maintain the heavy workload. To hold onto the title the champion has to outclass every opponent he faces. All wrestlers can challenge for the Coastal Title. Champ Won From When How Colossus NA August 16, 1998 Won Coastal Title Battle Royal at "One Night In Hollywood" PCW TAG TEAM TITLES • Only one tag team will sit atop PACIFIC COAST WRESTLING, and they will be wearing these belts to prove it. To be champions a tag team must have two strong characters who can draw heat to the entire division. Essentially, the tag champions will be the center-piece of the tag division, and must prove that they are to be the champions. Tag teams ranked in the top-5 will be able to challenge for the title.
