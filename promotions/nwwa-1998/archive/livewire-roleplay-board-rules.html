NWWA Interview Page NWWA LIVEWIRE Welcome to the NWWA Livewire. Here you will post your interviews and roleplays that is so important in the sport of wrestling. The roleplays or interviews you place here is what determines if you win or not. Remember, quality not quantity decides who wins although many quality roleplays are helpful. Rules for the roleplay board are located within the roleplay and will be strictly enforced at all costs. Below for tips on better roleplaying and see what category you fit in and how you can make yourself better at roleplaying. AMATEUR: Now this guy can’t spell all that great. He also isn’t very creative and doesn’t put a whole lot into his characters. He basically challenges opponents, has a lot of big "I’m going to Kick your ass" talk and then loses. Most of these people get mad when they lose and don’t try again. MID-CARDERS: Don’t get me wrong, these guys are good. Most are even on their way to the top, they just need a little extra. These guys know basic HTML, and have started to create an image for their character. Their basic role-play lay along the lines of "Wrestler comes out to his music, Wrestler makes challenge and trash talks, Wrestler leaves". Now there is nothing wrong with that, they just need to go a couple of steps farther. GREAT ROLEPLAYER: These guys are a class above. The obviously have been at this for a while. These guys not only know HTML, but also how to keep their roleplays fresh. These guys do more than the ordinary trash talking, and have no basic roleplay. These guys create a complete character. They know how he will act, look, and think. They have a complete past, present, and future for their character. Their roleplays are almost stories. They consist of detailed (but not to long) descriptions of where wrestler is, what he’s wearing, what he’s doing, and who he is with. They look at e-wrestling as an unwritten story and they use their characters to make it more creative. RULES FOR ROLEPLAY BOARDS: DO NOT ROLEPLAY ON THE BOARD IF YOU HAVE NOT SENT IN AN APPLICATION AND HAVE NOT RECEIVED A REPLY BY THE PRESIDENT STATING WHETHER YOU HAVE BEEN ACCEPTED OR NOT. DO NOT PUT OTHER WRESTLERS, THE PRES, OR ANY OTHER EXECUTIVES OF THE NWWA IN YOUR ROLEPLAYS. YOU CAN HAVE ANNOUNCERS, INTERVIEWERS, OR CAMERA CREWS, THAT IS FINE. I WOULD APPRECIATE IT, THANK YOU. DO PUT ADVERTISE OTHER FEDS ON MY ROLEPLAY BOARD. THE OOC BOARD IS WHERE YOU CAN POST YOUR E-FED ADVERTISEMENTS. DO NOT IMPERSONATE OTHER WRESTLERS IN THE INTERVIEW ROOM. IF YOU DO AND I FIND OUT WHO YOU ARE, YOU WILL BE BANNED FROM THE NWWA 4-LIFE!!!! NWWA Livewire (Roleplay Board) NWWA Backup Board (Use only if Main Roleplay Board is Down!!!!) NWWA Test Board (Test your html codes, pics, midis, and other stuff for your roleplays here) NWWA OOC Board...DO NOT ROLEPLAY HERE!!!! [image: setstats]
