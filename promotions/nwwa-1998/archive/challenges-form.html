NWWA Challenges NWWA CHALLENGES FORM Simply fill out the form then copy, paste, and email the challenges form completely filled to the [detail withheld] and the CEO and President will look over it and decide whether it will be signed or not for the card (more than likely it will be). MATCHES WILL NOT BE SIGNED IF THE MATCH HAS NOT BEEN ACCEPTED ORRRR IF THE CARD IS ALREADY FULL. If the card is full...then more than likely your match will be signed on the next event and if a wrestler has not accepted your challenge and has been dodging you for some time...then we will sign the match. If you challenge for a title and the champ is avoiding you and dodging you....then we will sign the match but it will be a NON-TITLE MATCH. Challenge Information: Wrestler Name: Name of Wrestler you are challenging: Name of Event and Date of Event for which match will take place: Is this a gimmick match: If yes, What type of gimmick match (Gimmick = Ladder Match, Table Death Match, I Quit Match, etc.): Any added Stipulations to the match: Name of title(s) on the line (if any): HAS THE MATCH BEEN ACCEPTED (Must be accepted on the roleplay board): [image: setstats]
