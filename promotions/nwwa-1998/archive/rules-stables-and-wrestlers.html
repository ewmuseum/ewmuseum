Stables and Wrestlers Page STABLES AND WRESTERS SECTION WRESTLERS: 1. The NWWA is an "ORIGINAL" wrestler e-fed ONLY!!!! We will not except anyone who turns in a real-life wrestler application. What we mean by original is that your wrestler must be created or made up from your head....not off of TV. 2. Wrestlers must seem as real as possible. No 8 ft. 900 lbs or 3 ft. 90 lbs. wrestlers please. Keep the wrestlers as real as possible and remember to roleplay well with them. Also please select moves that your wrestler would be able to perform in the ring. For example...a 450 lb man will no likely go up to the top turnbuckle and execute a moonsault nor will a 200 lb wrestler be able to deliver a gorilla press slam. This fed is created to be realistic in every aspect possible. 3. I have set the limit to 4 wrestlers per email address. If you chose to have 4 wrestlers, then I expect you to roleplay with all 4 wrestlers not just 2 out of the 4. No one will have more than 4 wrestlers, no exceptions. 4. Wrestlers who wish to compete for the Cruiserweight Title must be 230 lbs. or less. Heavyweights cannot contend for the Cruiserweight Title but Cruiserweights can compete for heavyweight titles. STABLES: 1. Stables are allowed here in the NWWA. This is how feuds and wars get started in wrestling. 2. I have decided to set the limit to 10 wrestlers per stable and that number will stand for now. If I would set it at unlimited, things would get hectic and confusing to who was on who's team.
