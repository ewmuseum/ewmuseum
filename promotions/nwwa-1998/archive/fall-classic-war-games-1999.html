NWWA "Fall Classic: War Games"

1999-08-29
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NWWA Monthly PPV NWWA MONTHLY PPV NWWA "FALL CLASSIC: WAR GAMES" August 29, 1999...8:00 PM Eastern Live from Target Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota Only On Pay-Per-View Call Your Local Cable Company For Availability (Card Subject To Change) Commentators For Event: Matt Austin (Main Commentator) "Doctor" Kevin Micheals (Color Commentator) ***"Perfect Strangers" by Deep Purple plays throughout the arena as Shane Douglas and Francine walk out to a chorus of boos from the crowd. Douglas just laughs as both walk down to the ring*** AUSTIN: This next contest at War Games is set for one fall and it is a Hell in a Cell match....introducing first being accompanied by the Queen of Hardcore, Francine....he hails from Pittsburgh, Pennslyvania and weighs in at 238 lbs...."THE FRANCHISE" SHANE DOUGLAS!!!! ***Douglas climbs into the ring and climbs up to the top turnbuckle mocking the crowd as they boo him. The words "Hey is this on???? Yo Jimmy, hit me with some of that Triple H shi...." followed by Triple H's WWF Theme Song. The lights in the arena go out as blue, green, and yellows lights circle the arena. Triple H and Chyna walk out to some boos from the crowd as well and both ignore them as they walk to the ring*** MORRIS: Aaaaaaaand his opponent....being accompanied to the ring by the 9th wonder of the world, Chyna....he hails from Greenwich, Connecticut and weighs in at 255 lbs.....one half of the NWWA Tag Team Champions....."TRIPLE H" HUNTER HEARST HELMSLEY!!!! ***Helmsley climbs into the ring and gives Chyna a high five as the officials on the outside pull her away from the ring. Both Triple H and Douglas eye each other down as the cage slowly lowers down over the ring. As the cage reaches the bottom, both men get face to face and start the trash talking as the bell sounds to start the match*** AUSTIN: Both men are doing the trash talk here Kev. There is no way out of the cage and no way in so these two will battle to see who the real winner is. Douglas just shoved Triple H out of his face and Triple H shoves him back. Douglas nails Triple H with a slam to the face knocking Triple H to the ground. Triple H gets to his feet and Douglas nails him with right hands. Douglas forces Triple H back to the corner and drives the shoulder to the midsection. Douglas sets up Triple H and delivers a hard knife-edge chop and Triple H staggers from the corner holding his chest and falls to the mat. Douglas delivers a leg drop to the back of Triple H's head and makes the cover...1.....2...kickout. Douglas picks up Triple H and applies a headlock. Triple H whips Douglas into the ropes and Douglas nails Triple H with a shoulderblock. Douglas runs into the ropes and Triple H ducks down as Douglas leaps over him. Douglas hits the opposite ropes and Triple H grabs Douglas and tosses him over the top rope to the floor. Triple H climbs out of the ring, grabs a chair, and drills Douglas over the head with it. Triple H picks up Douglas and sends him face first into the cage. Triple H grabs Douglas in a headlock and nails him with right hands. Triple H climbs back into the ring and pulls Douglas up to the mat. Triple H hooks Douglas and suplexes him back into the ring. Triple H covers..1....2...kickout. Triple H grabs Douglas and snapmares him to the mat. Triple H walks to the corner, slowly walks out, and delivers a hard knee drop across the forehead of Douglas. Triple H walks over and slowly starts climbing up to the top turnbuckle. I think this is a big mistake by Triple H here, Kev. Douglas is to his feet and falls into the ropes knocking Triple H down onto the turnbuckle. Douglas grabs Triple H and nails him with a hard right hand that sends Triple H onto the ropes. Douglas grabs the ropes and starts shaking them up and down as Triple H is in serious pain. Douglas runs into the ropes and nails Triple H with a clothesline that sends him off the ropes and to the floor on the outside. Douglas climbs up to the top turnbuckle as Triple H gets to his feet and Douglas leaps off connecting with a crossbody block on Triple H on the outside of the ring sending both men crashing into the cage wall. Douglas gets to his feet and staggers over to the ringsteps. Douglas knocks the top ring step off and grabs Triple H. Douglas pulls Triple H up onto the steps, hooks him, and delivers a piledriver onto the ringsteps. Douglas rolls Triple H into the ring and slowly follows him in. Douglas covers Triple H....1......2....thr-foot on the ropes. Douglas picks up Triple H and whips him into the ropes. Douglas goes for a backdrop but Triple H counters by grabbing Douglas and driving him face first into his knee. MICHEALS: WHAT A MATCH WE GOT GOING HERE MATT!!!! Triple H staggers to his feet and grabs Douglas. Triple H nails Douglas with several forearm shots to the face and whips him to the ropes. Triple H nails Douglas with a leaping knee to the face. Triple H picks up Douglas and hooks him for the Pedigree but Douglas quickly counters by backdropping Triple H over the top rope to the outside. Douglas gets to his feet and walks over to the ropes but Triple H grabs him and trips him to the mat. Triple H takes Douglas's knee and slams it down onto the mat. Triple H grabs a chair and slams it against the knee of Douglas as Douglas screams in pain. Triple H slides back into the ring and pulls Douglas to his feet. Triple H whips Douglas into the ropes and goes for a clothesline but Douglas ducks, hooks Triple H, and plants him with an inverted Russian Legsweep. Douglas gets to his feet as Triple H gets up as well. Triple H runs into the ropes and Douglas catches him with a sleeper. Triple H quickly counters by moving his body in a headlock position and whips Douglas off into the ropes. Douglas comes off and Triple H puts him in his own version of the sleeper. Douglas goes down to the mat and the ref raises up his arm and drops. The ref lifts up his arm again and it drops again. The ref goes for a third time but this time Douglas's arm stays up. Douglas gets to his feet, positions his body around in a headlock position, and delivers a belly-back suplex on Triple H. Matt....look at that....that's....that's CHRIS JERICHO COMING DOWN THE AISLE!!!! I didn't think he would be here tonight but he said he would be. Both men are up to their feet in the ring and are trading right hands. Triple H goes for a clothesline but Douglas ducks it. Triple H charges again and Douglas backdrops him to the floor and Triple H rolls into the cage. Douglas falls into the ropes trying to get a 2nd wind. Wait a second....Jericho has made his way around to the side of the cage where Triple H is. Jericho just slipped Triple H a pouch through the cage. Francine has come over and shoves Jericho and then slams him in the face as the crowd erupts. Douglas reaches out and grabs Triple H but Triple H reaches into the pouch and throws some powder into the face of Douglas blinding him. Douglas falls to the mat in pain as Triple H gets in the ring. Triple H grabs Douglas, hooks him, and plants him with the Pedigree. Triple H covers....1.....2.....3. It is all over!!!! MORRIS: Here is your winner...."TRIPLE H" HUNTER HEARST HELMSLEY!!!! AUSTIN: So Triple H gets the win here thanks to the antics of one Chris Jericho. I have to admit Kev that both men gave it their all and I really thought Douglas had the match won but with Jericho helping out, Douglas chances looked mighty slim. MICHEALS: Well fans it is main event time here at the PPV and before we go to that I just want to say.....what the hell happened to the lights???? What is going on Matt???? ***The lights in the arena go out and the Y2J Millennium Clock appears on the titantron. The clock starts the countdown from 10 and when it hits zero, fireworks fire up from the entranceway as Chris Jericho's WWF Theme Song plays. The lights in the arena come back on to find Jericho standing at the entrance way with his arms straight out from him holding a mic as the crowd erupts in cheers with some boos. Jericho cracks a smile and begins to talk*** JERICHO: Since this is the biggest part of the PPV, I figured that the man who brings in all of the ratings for the NWWJ should be here. Now, before you announce the two non important wrestlers to the ring, please give the fans who ordered the PPV time to flip back to the channel to see that I'm here at the PPV. Now if you all will excuse me, I now must do what I came here for.... ***Jericho walks down to the announce table and sits with Matt Austin and Kevin Micheals. Jericho puts on a headset*** AUSTIN: Uhhhhhhh well hello Mr. Jericho, it certainly is a surprise to see you joining us this evening... JERICHO: Well it is real simple Matt, the NWWA without Chris Jericho is like having a romantic evening without the sex if you catch my drift. I have come here to the NWWA to raise the ratings and already I can feel the percentage raising just a couple of notches due to my being here.... MICHEALS: Exactly why are you here Chris???? I mean you don't have any matches and neither of the two men in the main event have anything to do with you. JERICHO: Oh contrar my good sir. You see Mc...Mc...McMicheals, I have everything to do with this match cause with all the run-ins and the attacks tonight....the NWWA needs an enforcer if you will....someone to define the word justice....someone to bring right to those who have been wronged and tonight that man is me, Chris Jericho. AUSTIN: Alrighty then, let's get to the ring for the main event..... ***"Beth" by Kiss plays throughout the arena as Kirk walks out with Miss X as he proudly wears the IC title around his waist. The crowd cheers for him as Kirk looks out at everyone and starts walking to the ring as fireworks shoot off behind him*** MORRIS: This next match is set for one fall and it is the main event of the evening....it is a Title vs Title match with the winner being crowned the World Champion and the loser crowned the Intercontinental Champion....introducing first making his way to the ring being accompanied by the lovely Miss X.....he hails from Sacramento, California and weighs in at 254 lbs.....the current NWWA Intercontinental Champion.....KIRK!!!! ***Kirk slides into the ring and looks down at Jericho, who just waves at Kirk. Kirk walks around the ring and bounces off the ropes as the crowd continues to cheer. The words "Do You Smell What The Rock is Cookin?" fills the arena followed by the Rock's WWF Theme Song fills the arena. The Rock walks out with the World Title drapped over his shoulder to the cheers of the crowd. Rock slowly walks down to the ring as fireworks shoot off behind him as well*** MORRIS: Aaaaaaaaaaand his opponent....he hails from Miami, Florida and weighs in at 275 lbs....the current NWWA World Heavyweight Champion.....THE ROCK!!!! ***Rock walks up the ringsteps, walks across the ring apron, and climbs up to the 2nd turnbuckle. Rock pulls off the World Title and holds it out for everyone to see as he looks into the crowd and gives the people's eyebrow. Rock climbs down and steps through the ropes as the bell sounds to start the match*** AUSTIN: Here we go with the big match of the night guys....Rock....Kirk....should be a good one..... JERICHO: Yeah right whatever...plucking nose hairs is more exciting then watching these two wrestler. Fat people who stuff their faces with food 24 hours, 7 days a week have got more talent then these two combined. Well if you 2 will excuse me I have to go enforce a match.... AUSTIN: Okay, before I was rudely interrupted. Rock and Kirk lock it up and quickly Kirk applies the armbar and puts the pressure on. Rock forces Kirk back to the corner and surprisingly a clean break is made. Both men lock up and Rock applies the sideheadlock. Kirk whips Rock off into the ropes and Rock nails Kirk with a shoulderblock. Rock runs into the ropes, leapfrogs over Kirk, hits the other ropes, and Kirk catches him with a hiptoss. Rock gets to his feet and gets hiptossed again. Rock gets up and is met with a clothesline from Kirk. Rock gets up once again and Kirk bodyslams him to the mat and the Rock slides to the outside of the ring holding his back. Kirk goes outside following Rock and the Rock slides back into the ring. Kirk slides in behind him but Rock stomps him down to the mat. Rock picks up Kirk and shoves him back to the corner. Rock starts delivering hard right hands to the face of Kirk. Kirk grabs the Rock and tosses him into the corner and delivers rights of his own followed by a slap to the face. Kirk whips the Rock into the far side corner but runs into the Rock's boot to the face. Kirk staggers out, turns, and is met with a Rock clothesline. Rock walks over and stomps away on Kirk viciously to the mat. Rock picks up Kirk and whips him into the ropes. Kirk comes off and is met with an elbow to the face. Rock runs into the ropes and drops a small version of the people's elbow on Kirk. Rock covers..1....2....kickout. Rock picks up Kirk and bodyslams him to the mat. Rock grabs Kirk's leg and places him in some sort of leg lock. Kirk tries to fight the pain and Rock finally releases the hold and starts dropping knees onto the Kirk's leg. Rock gets to his feet and runs over to the ropes as he yells at a fan to shut up as the fan taunts him. Kirk comes up behind Rock and rolls him up from behind...1.....kickout. Rock quickly gets to his feet and nails Kirk with hard right hands to the face. Rock picks up Kirk and delivers an atomic drop to him followed by another clothesline. Rock grabs Kirk and whips him into the ropes. Rock goes for a backdrop but Kirk hangs onto the ropes and drives an elbow to the back of Rock's head. Kirk gets to his feet and picks up Rock. Kirk forces Rock into the corner and delivers hard chops to the chest. Kirk whips Rock into the far corner and the Rock comes out only to be backdropped by Kirk to the mat. Kirk bodyslams Rock to the mat and he climbs up to the 2nd turnbuckle. Kirk measures up Rock and delivers a guillotine leg drop from the 2nd turnbuckle onto Rock. Kirk covers..1....2....kickout. Kirk applies a chinlock onto Rock trying to wear him down. Rock gets to his feet and drives the elbows to Kirk's midsection. Rock runs into the ropes and is met with a knee to the midsection by Kirk. Kirk picks up Rock and delivers some left handed jabs to the face. Kirk runs into the ropes, comes off, and the Rock quickly plants him with a Samoan Drop. Kirk gets to his feet and Rock clotheslines him over the top rope. MICHEALS: Rock goes outside and kicks Kirk in the head knocking back against the announcers table. Rock picks up Kirk and bodyslams him onto our announce table. Rock climbs up to the ring apron, leaps off, and delivers an elbow drop onto Kirk right through our table!!!! BOTH MEN APPEAR TO BE OUT COLD HERE ON THE SHATTERED TABLE. Rock and Kirk get to their feet and start to trade punches. Rock tries to swing at Kirk but Kirk ducks and backdrops Rock over the guardrail into the crowd. Kirk climbs over the guardrail into the crowd as well. Kirk continues to hammer away on Rock in the crowd. Kirk grabs some fans popcorn and throws it in the Rock's face. Kirk walks over and grabs a chair and attempts to nail the Rock with it but the Rock grabs some fans beer and slings it in the face of Kirk. Rock delivers hard right hands of his own forcing Kirk back down to the guardrail. Rock grabs Kirk and attempts to slam him face first into the guardrail but Kirk gets his foot up onto the rail to block it and slams Rock's face into it. CHRIS JERICHO IS TRYING TO INTERFERE!!!! Jericho grabs Kirk and tries to pull him off of Rock but Kirk turns and drills Jericho with a right hand knocking him to the floor as the crowd goes nuts. Kirk climbs back over the guardrail and brings the Rock back over with him and rolls him back into the ring. Kirk looks down at Jericho and climbs back in as well. Kirk picks up the Rock and attempts a bodyslam but the Rock slides over the back of Kirk, hooks him, and drops him with the Russian Legsweep. Kirk gets to his feet and turns only to walk into the Rock. Rock hooks Kirk and plants him with the Rock Bottom. Rock signals to the crowd and tosses off the elbow pad as the crowd is to their feet. Rock runs into the ropes, leaps over Kirk, runs into the other ropes and attempts the People's Elbow but Kirk rolls out of the way at the last second. Kirk gets to his feet and dropkicks Rock down to the mat. Kirk sets up the Rock for a clothesline but Rock ducks and Kirk nails the ref by accident. Kirk is checking on the ref to see if he is okay. WHAT THE HELL!!!! THAT IS TRIPLE H!!!! Triple H has just slide into the ring and attacks Kirk from behind. Triple H is beating the hell out of Kirk while the ref is down. Triple H turns and starts beating on the Rock as well. ***The sound of drums fill the arena followed by a base guitar as the crowd gets to their feet wondering who is coming out. "Crush Em" by MegaDeth plays as Goldberg runs out from the back as the crowd goes nuts. Goldberg runs straight down to the ring*** AUSTIN: IT'S GOLDBERG!!!! DA MAN IS HERE KEV!!!! Goldberg slides into ring and Triple H turns only to be met with a devastating spear football tackle as the crowd responds to the impact. Goldberg clotheslines Triple H outside of the ring and goes out after him. Kirk gets to his feet as the Rock does as well. Kirk walks over, grabs Rock, and plants him with Kirk's Own!!!! Kirk goes for the pin but there is no ref to count. Kirk goes over and shakes the ref....JERICHO HAS ENTERED THE RING!!!! Jericho comes up behind Kirk with the World Title in his hand. Kirk turns and Jericho blasts Kirk in the face with the World Title and Kirk falls the mat with an impact. Jericho pulls the Rock over ontop Kirk and Jericho shakes the ref as he slides out of the ring. The ref rolls and makes the count..1..........2...........3!!!!! The Rock has retained the World Title!!!! Goldberg and Triple H still battling it out on the outside of the ring. MORRIS: Here is your winner and STILLLL NWWA World Heavyweight Champion.....THE ROCK!!!! AUSTIN: Fans we are out of time....for everyone in the NWWA and for Kevin Micheals....thank you all for tuning in and goodnight.... ***Scene fades to black as Goldberg enters the ring and stands side by side with Rock as the show goes off the air*** 1999-2000 NWWA PRODUCTIONS [image: setstats]
