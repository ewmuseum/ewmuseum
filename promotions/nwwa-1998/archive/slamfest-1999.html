NWWA "Slamfest" (1999)

1999-05-30
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NWWA Monthly PPV NWWA MONTHLY PPV NWWA "SLAMFEST" May 30, 1999...8:00 PM Eastern Live from the Tokyo Dome in Tokyo, Japan Only On Pay-Per-View Call Your Local Cable Company For Availability (Card Subject To Change) Commentators For Event: Matt Austin (Main Commentator) "Doctor" Kevin Micheals (Color Commentator) ***Scene shifts to the back at Bagwell's lockerroom. Bagwell is on the floor as Hill stomps away on him. A dented metal chair lays next to Bagwell and Hill walks off down the hall yelling "That US title will come back around my waist Fluff". Hill exits the room and heads to the ring*** UNITED STATES TITLE MATCH Dale Hill vs Buff Bagwell (c) ***Hill took control early working on the neck of Bagwell which Hill injured with the chair. Hill piledrived Bagwell several times and went for a cover but Bagwell kicked out barely. Bagwell came back though and nailed Hill with a flying shoulderblock. Bagwell would catch Hill with a series of dropkicks and nail Hill with a clothesline knocking him to the outside of the ring. Bagwell would keep control of the match but Hill would battle back though and catch him with a low blow behind the ref's back. Hill would catch Bagwell with a running powerslam and almost get the win but Bagwell kicks out. Hill would catch Bagwell with a hotshot move off the top rope snapping Bagwell's neck back. Hill thought he had the match won but still Bagwell kicked out. Bagwell would come back and catch Hill with a swinging neckbreaker off the ropes. Bagwell would connect with a DDT to the mat. Bagwell would then point to the turnbuckle as the crowd would go nuts. Bagwell would climb up and set up Hill. Hill would get to his feet and Bagwell would attempt the Blockbuster but Hill would move and Bagwell would nail the ref as he hit the mat. Both men would lay on the mat and Hill would be the first to his feet. Hill would walk over, pick up Bagwell, hook him and deliver the Bang. Hill would cover but no ref to count. Hill would walk over and shake the ref as Bagwell slides over to the corner and grabs the US Title. Hill would walk over to Bagwell and pick him up only to be clocked upside the head with the US title. Bagwell would toss out the title and cover Hill as the ref would turn and slide over to make the count*** MORRIS: Here is your winner and STILLLL NWWA United States Heavyweight Champion.....BUFF BAGWELL!!!! i>MORRIS: Here is your winner and NEWWWW NWWA UNITED STATES HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION AUSTIN: What a win there by Bagwell thanks to an assist from the US title I might add. Hill should be the new US champ but Bagwell pulled it out of his bag of tricks to walk out still the champ. MICHEALS: A good hard fought match between both men Matt. I don't think we seen the last of these two going at it in the ring. Let's get to the ring for more great action.... ***Scene shifts to the back to find Buff Tortoise, Dale Hill, and Jax stomping away on The Rock on the ground. Carl is sitting against a forklift loader that is parked in front of the Horsemen lockerroom. Security enters the area and DX leaves the Rock laying on the ground*** INTERCONTINENTAL TITLE / STEEL CAGE MATCH ***First man to exit the cage and touch the floor is the winner*** "Nature Boy" Ric Flair vs Kirk (c) ***Perhaps the best match of the night between 2 legends of different eras. Kirk jumped on the advantage first by slamming Flair repeatedly into the cage. Kirk caught Flair with a brainbuster followed by a snap leg drop. Kirk tried to climb out of the cage but Flair was up and caught Kirk with a low blow that knocked him back down into the ring. Flair then went to work at his usual slow pace working on the legs of Kirk. Flair grabbed Kirk and started raking his eyes on the cage as the crowd booed him. Flair positioned Kirk and delivered those sharp knife-edge chops of his that seems to just peel the flesh off. Kirk came back though and hammered away on Flair. Flair staggered forward, threw up his fists like he was ready to fight, then fell flat on his face. Kirk grabbed Flair by his hair, ran him across the ring, and slammed him headfirst into the cage. Kirk would dropkick Flair and Flair would fall over the top rope between the ropes and the cage. Kirk would run into the ropes, come off, and execute a splash onto Flair sandwiching him against Kirk and the cage. Kirk would dropkick Flair against the cage and Flair would slide down to the mat. Kirk would hook Flair for a suplex but Flair would hang on and drive Kirk's head repeatedly into the cage wall. Flair would climb through the ropes and level him with another chop. Flair would set up Kirk and drop the big knee on him. Flair would make his way over and tell the ref to open the door. Flair would start his way through when Kirk would come up from behind, hook Flair, and belly-back suplex him back into the ring. Kirk would grab Flair's leg and apply the figure-four leg lock onto Flair. Flair would get out though by getting to the ropes. Both men fight hard to win this match as the tide switched back and forth. In the end, Miss X came down to ringside wearing a Horsemen t-shirt. She would open up the cage door and slide a chair into Ric Flair. Flair would take the chair and level Kirk with it. Miss X would yell at Flair to crawl out the door as she waved the IC Title at him. Flair would crawl and get to the door where Miss X has made her way up to the doorway. Flair gets to his feet only to get clocked upside the head with the IC Title. Flair would stagger back into the ring right into Kirk who would DDT him to the mat. Kirk would then crawl and pull his way over and fall through to the door to the outside of the cage and the bell would sound as Miss X would take off the Horsemen t-shirt to reveal a DX t-shirt. She would help Kirk up and hug him as both of them make their way to the back*** MORRIS: Here is your winner and STILLLLL Intercontinental Champion......KIRK!!!! AUSTIN: Kirk is still the champ after that hard fought battle and Miss X showed the world that she is DX for life. What a match and a shocking turn of events here at Slamfest and we still got the main event to go. MICHEALS: Fans let's get to the ring for the big main event of the night.... MAIN EVENT: WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE vs TITLE LADDER MATCH Buff Tortoise vs The Rock (c) ***Both men started off brawling in the aisle way as The Rock made his way down to the ring. Tortoise would slam Rock's head into the guardrail and nail him with a right hand. Tortoise would go to whip The Rock towards the ladder but Rock would reverse it sending Tortoise crashing into it. Rock would pick up Tortoise and pound away on him viciously on the outside. Rock would pull off and yell at a fan standing at ringside. Rock would pick up Tortoise and roll him into the ring. Rock would follow and continue his relentless assault on Tortoise in the corner. Rock would pick up Tortoise and whip into the opposite corner and follow him in with a clothesline. Rock would pick up Tortoise and bodyslam him to the mat. Rock would go outside and slide the ladder into the ring. Rock would lay the ladder into the corner and put Tortoise in the opposite corner. Rock attempts to whip Tortoise into the ladder but Tortoise counters with an armbar followed by a short clothesline. Tortoise picks up Rock, hooks him, and delivers a Russian legsweep. Tortoise gets to his feet and delivers repeated driving elbows to the head of Rock. Tortoise picks up Rock and whips him to the ropes and levels him with a clothesline. Tortoise goes to pick up the Rock but the Rock pokes him into the eyes. Rock walks over to Tortoise who grabs his legs and trips him to the mat. Tortoise hooks his legs and slingshots the Rock into the ladder in the corner. Tortoise would bodyslam Rock in the ring and open up the ladder. Tortoise would climb up to the middle of the ladder and deliver a diving headbutt down onto Rock. Tortoise would lay the ladder down in the center of the ring. Tortoise would pick up the Rock, stomp him several times in the stomach, and run into the ropes. Tortoise would come off and the Rock would hook him for the Rock Bottom as the crowd would get to their feet. Rock would execute the Rock Bottom to Tortoise onto the ladder. Rock would slowly stagger to his feet and grab the ladder. He would set the ladder across the face of Tortoise. Rock would pull off the elbowpad as the crowd would go nuts. Rock would run into the ropes and execute the People's Elbow onto the ladder with Tortoise under it but the Rock would hurt his arm in the process. Rock would slowly set up the ladder and start climbing it. He gets to the top but Tortoise is up and dropkicks the ladder which causes it to fall and the Rock falls throat first over the top rope snapping him back into the ring as the ladder hits the ref knocking him down. In the end, both the Rock and Tortoise would be climbing up the ladder....one on each side. Cougar would make his way down to the ring, climb in, and knock the ladder over sending both Rock and Tortoise crashing to the mat. Cougar would pick up Tortoise and deliver a chokeslam to him. Cougar grabs the Rock and delivers another chokeslam to him. Cougar would pick up both men and execute a double chokeslam in the ring and leaves both men laying there. Rock and Tortoise after a few minutes start staggering to their feet. Both men bump backs against each other, Tortoise spins and drives Rock to the mat with the Snapper as the crowd goes nuts. Tortoise slides over and sets up the ladder. He starts slowly climbing it up, rung by rung. Rock starts to stir a little. Tortoise gets to the top and tries to reach for the belt but misses. Rock is up to his feet and staggers over to the ladder. Tortoise reaches for it again but misses. Rock grabs the ladder and leans on it a second trying to catch his breath. Tortoise goes to reach it again and grabs the World Title as the Rock shoves the ladder over and Tortoise crashes down to the mat with the NWWA World title clutched in his arms as the ref calls for the bell*** MORRIS: Here is your winner and NEWWWWW NWWA World Heavyweight Champion.....BUFF TORTOISE!!!! 1999-2000 NWWA Productions [image: setstats]
