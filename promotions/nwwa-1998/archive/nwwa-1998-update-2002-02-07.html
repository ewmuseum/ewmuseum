Association.  I confess that I never thought we'd be hearing about another
NWWA broadcast outside of the usual rumors of a reunion show.  But what we have here is not a reunion show, but a full-fledged return!  The NWWA is coming back!
  There's going to be a few changes in order.  [name withheld] has decided to cut back on the schedule, at least at first.  The weekly show, Nightmare, is no more.  It has been replaced with a bi-monthly Sudden Death.  Less frequent shows will also mean that every show will be twice as intense and twice as full.  Twice a month
Sudden Death will air.  Every week Boiling Point will air, which is simply a renamed version of Recoil.  It's where the NWWA superstars will fill the air with trash talk.  And every other month in place of one of our Sudden
Deaths will air a supercard.  This will not be a pay per view.  They will be free for all of the many hundreds of thousands of NWWA fans out there.
  The big question on everyone's minds is...who'll be a part of the new NWWA?  Will we see any old faces or will the roster be completely restructured?  The answer to that is...yes and yes.  You'll see a mixture of both old and new faces.  Former NWWA
Heavyweight champion and possibly the most popular NWWA star of all time,
"Reckless" Ryan Stitt has once again signed on with the NWWA.  Current NWWA
Television champion Tommy Lionheart has signed on with the NWWA once again.  "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock has signed on with the NWWA. 
The ONLY Triple-Crown NWWA champion, The Hollywood Kid, has signed on with the
NWWA once more.  These are all names that have been verified.  Rumors are circulating about Billy Joe McCallister, Vile Vince Viper, D-Man, Sinister, and of course the current NWWA Heavyweight champion...Voron Mikhailatnov. 
Another strange rumor about former UWA Heavyweight Champion T.N.T. has surfaced.  I can say only one thing about this, and that is that I'll believe it when I see it.
  It's sure to be a great evening, Sudden Death on February 17th!  Don't miss it!   News from around the league:   ---> Rather than two flash packets between every card, there will be only one.  We will have a Boiling Point flash packet on alternating weeks between cards.  This will mean flashes and strats only once every other week, and will free the week of the card up for match writing.  I think this'll work beautifully.

Roleplaying - Flashes   A flash is the most necessary form of roleplaying that you can do in the NWWA.  If you've been in e-wrestling for very long, you know what this is.  The best flashes come from unique settings that will enhance your character.  I grade roleplays based upon different criteria.  I do believe in quality over quantity.  But this does not justify writing up a 2K flash in three minutes and expecting to win something.  I also look at the time spent on a roleplay.  If I'm comparing a mediocre 10K flash and a mediocre 4K flash, I'm most likely going to give the win to the guy that wrote 10K.  At the same time, if you write 20K of rambling just to impress me, I may very well give the win to your opponent who wrote a high quality 8K flash.  Be original.  Use humor and a supporting cast to enhance things.  Don't saturate your flash with a lot of profanity or graphic sex. 
  I'm very strict when it comes to having flashes sent in on time.  I will give a deadline every week. 
Sometimes I won't get to sending out the flash packet until it's a little late.  So I'm not going to toss out a flash just because it's past the deadline.  But basically, if I send out the flash packet before I get your flash, you're up the proverbial creek.  I can't think of any case where this would not result in your losing your match.  Punctuality is important!
:P    b. Roleplaying - Strats   I'm a little more relaxed than most fed heads on strats I think.  I'm going to assume that everyone wants to win their match, and that they want to win it using the moves and the finisher that they put on their application.  So there's no need to send me a detailed strat telling me what moves you want to do in what order and whether or not you are going to look happy when you win.  When someone sends me a 3-page detailed strat with 10 or 12 spots to use, I'm likely to just tell them to write the entire match.  Please don't do this to me and my writers.
:)   What I want every week is a ring entrance.  If you don't intend to do anything outside of trying to win and leaving the ring when you're done, you don't need to give me a gameplan. 
But at very least send me a ring entrance with the ring announcer to put before the card.  If I don't receive this ring entrance, it will subtract a few points from your chance of victory.  I will not write ring entrances either.  I will simply write your wrestler as standing in the ring waiting for his opponent ala Barry Horowitz. 
  As far as ring entrances go, please do NOT send me the lyrics to an entire song and call it your ring entrance.  I would actually almost prefer a more descriptive ring entrance with an instrumental for music.  You can send me lyrics, but don't let your entrance take up three screens.  Because who actually reads those things anyway?  I never read any of those lyrics. 
  I'm not going to create a form for strats.  In your strat just let me know if there's anything special that you want to do before, during, or after the match such as attack an opponent or eat a twinkie while pinning your opponent.  Strats also cover anything you might want to do during the card that has no bearing on your match.  If you have a backstage interview to do during the card, put it in your strat.  If you want to go destroy a hot dog stand at some random point in the card, include it in your strat. 
   c. Match Writing   I may or may not as you to write one match per month.  If I do ask you to take 30 minutes to an hour extra out of your month to write a match for the NWWA, please don't tell me that you don't have time.  If you have time to write a flash every week, you have time to write a match every month.  If you can't do it this week, I'll ask you to do it the next week.  Also don't tell me that you don't have the ability to write matches.  Once again, if you can write a decent flash, you can write a decent match. 
  It may initially be required that everyone write a match per month.  If you aren't willing to take an hour per month to do this, then you obviously don't care much about whether the NWWA is going to be around in a month or two.  I don't care if I have a roster of the top
30 RSPWF guys.  If none of them are willing to write matches, we'll sink.  Punctual match writers are the backbone of the NWWA, not roleplayers.
  I would also appreciate anyone who would want to write every week.  The more effort you put into the NWWA, the more the NWWA will invest into your wrestler.
