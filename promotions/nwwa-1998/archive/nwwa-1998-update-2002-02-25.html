In the first match, old rivals THK and Sabbath will once again meet in the ring. Who would have thought this could happen? Everyone remembers the controversial ending of the Roseman/Coals Cup, when Sabbath took the Heavyweight strap from THK. THK has a chance for revenge, but will he drop the ball? And is Sabbath in any condition for a match with THK after his violent bout with Triple-V? [ALL-STATE TITLE TOURNAMENT ROUND 1] Tommy Lionheart vs. Vile Vince Viper - The charismatic newcomer against the most infamous veteran in the sport. Tommy Lionheart has his work cut out for him in what could be the greatest test of his career against the King of Snakes, Vile Vince Viper. Flaming Bob has banned all weapons from ringside and said that he will be watching Viper very closely for any illegal doings. [ALL-STATE TITLE TOURNAMENT ROUND 1] "Wildcard" Jacen Canadi vs. Shack The Wrecker - Their match at Sudden Death ended in controversy, which Canadi pulling off a rather shady victory over the impressive Shack. A rematch is in order! This time legendary referee Jack Damon has been called out of retirement to make sure that there is a true clear winner. Shack The Wrecker has the enormous task of defeating Jacen Canadi and keeping his credibility alive in the NWWA. [ALL-STATE TITLE TOURNAMENT ROUND 1] "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock vs. Phantast - Round one concludes with former TV champion Kenneth Morlock facing the mysterious newcomer, Phantast. Morlock perhaps has the most difficult task of all, as he knows not how to prepare for his shadowy opponent. For Phantast it's a major break, as he has a chance to elevate himself to stardom early in his career. The All-State title is only 3 matches ahead. [DOUBLE DEBUT MATCH] "Lathspell" Chris Lupsa vs. Dynamo - Two upcoming superstars in the sport of professional wrestling will step into an NWWA ring for the first time. Their styles are similar but their backgrounds are much different. Sparks will fly at Sudden Death as these two NWWA rookies collide! [HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH #1] "The Dark Knight" Voron Mikhailatnov [c] vs. Unknown - At the last Sudden Death, Voron Mikhailatnov returned with the old Heavyweight title. In an attempt to show that he is unbiased, Commissioner Flaming Bob ordered both Voron and Ryan Stitt to defend their belts against unknown opponents on Sudden Death. Who has Bob chosen to be Voron Mikhailatnov's opponent? And will a title change hands? [HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH #2] "Reckless" Ryan Stitt [c] vs. Unknown - Ryan Stitt is unarguably the most popular superstar that the NWWA has ever seen. Now in his second reign as champion, Stitt will spend a week waiting on the unknown. Flaming Bob has chosen an opponent and promises that it will be one to Stitt's liking. But how much should we read into that? If Ryan Stitt wins, he'll wrestle in the Unification match at Bash III. If he loses, it's back to the drawingboard. Roleplaying - Flashes A flash is the most necessary form of roleplaying that you can do in the NWWA. If you've been in e-wrestling for very long, you know what this is. The best flashes come from unique settings that will enhance your character. I grade roleplays based upon different criteria. I do believe in quality over quantity. But this does not justify writing up a 2K flash in three minutes and expecting to win something. I also look at the time spent on a roleplay. If I'm comparing a mediocre 10K flash and a mediocre 4K flash, I'm most likely going to give the win to the guy that wrote 10K. At the same time, if you write 20K of rambling just to impress me, I may very well give the win to your opponent who wrote a high quality 8K flash. Be original. Use humor and a supporting cast to enhance things. Don't saturate your flash with a lot of profanity or graphic sex. I'm very strict when it comes to having flashes sent in on time. I will give a deadline every week. Sometimes I won't get to sending out the flash packet until it's a little late. So I'm not going to toss out a flash just because it's past the deadline. But basically, if I send out the flash packet before I get your flash, you're up the proverbial creek. I can't think of any case where this would not result in your losing your match. Punctuality is important! :P b. Roleplaying - Strats I'm a little more relaxed than most fed heads on strats I think. I'm going to assume that everyone wants to win their match, and that they want to win it using the moves and the finisher that they put on their application. So there's no need to send me a detailed strat telling me what moves you want to do in what order and whether or not you are going to look happy when you win. When someone sends me a 3-page detailed strat with 10 or 12 spots to use, I'm likely to just tell them to write the entire match. Please don't do this to me and my writers. :) What I want every week is a ring entrance. If you don't intend to do anything outside of trying to win and leaving the ring when you're done, you don't need to give me a gameplan. But at very least send me a ring entrance with the ring announcer to put before the card. If I don't receive this ring entrance, it will subtract a few points from your chance of victory. I will not write ring entrances either. I will simply write your wrestler as standing in the ring waiting for his opponent ala Barry Horowitz. As far as ring entrances go, please do NOT send me the lyrics to an entire song and call it your ring entrance. I would actually almost prefer a more descriptive ring entrance with an instrumental for music. You can send me lyrics, but don't let your entrance take up three screens. Because who actually reads those things anyway? I never read any of those lyrics. I'm not going to create a form for strats. In your strat just let me know if there's anything special that you want to do before, during, or after the match such as attack an opponent or eat a twinkie while pinning your opponent. Strats also cover anything you might want to do during the card that has no bearing on your match. If you have a backstage interview to do during the card, put it in your strat. If you want to go destroy a hot dog stand at some random point in the card, include it in your strat. c. Match Writing I may or may not as you to write one match per month. If I do ask you to take 30 minutes to an hour extra out of your month to write a match for the NWWA, please don't tell me that you don't have time. If you have time to write a flash every week, you have time to write a match every month. If you can't do it this week, I'll ask you to do it the next week. Also don't tell me that you don't have the ability to write matches. Once again, if you can write a decent flash, you can write a decent match. It may initially be required that everyone write a match per month. If you aren't willing to take an hour per month to do this, then you obviously don't care much about whether the NWWA is going to be around in a month or two. I don't care if I have a roster of the top 30 RSPWF guys. If none of them are willing to write matches, we'll sink. Punctual match writers are the backbone of the NWWA, not roleplayers. I would also appreciate anyone who would want to write every week. The more effort you put into the NWWA, the more the NWWA will invest into your wrestler.
