Attacks and Interviews Page ATTACKS AND INTERVIEWS SECTION ATTACKS: 1. This is what makes fantasy wrestling really fun, being able to attack your opponent. 2. To do an attack, write up the attack by describing where it happens, who it is against and how the attack happened and email it to me for which ever card you want it to be on. 3. Attacks are a good way to injure a wrestler or humiliate him. REMEMBER THIS: The attacks you send in can benefit you by injuring a wrestler. 4. If the attacks that are sent in outrageous or just plain sick....THEY WILL NOT BE USED!!!! No exceptions. Just keep in your mind, if they don't do it in the WCW or WWF then it will not happen here. 5. Attacks are also used for pushes for matches. Let's say that you have a match against someone on Tuesday Night Turmoil. Any card before that or during the card before the match, you can send in an attack on your opponent and come Tuesday, you will get a small push towards winning the match depending on how well the attack is. Now your opponent can also send in attacks on you as well so just remember the old saying.....WHAT GOES AROUND WILL PROBABLY COME AROUND. 6. I will no longer be writing up attacks for the wrestlers any more because I have gotten emails stating that they didn't want their wrestlers to attack so and so etc. etc. If no attacks are sent in, then may the best man win in the matches. 7. Attacks for the cards must be sent in by the day before any card at the latest. Which means if the card happens on Tuesday...attacks must be in by Monday night, same for Thursday Night Showdown...same for PPVs. INTERVIEWS: 1. Interviews are used for pushes just the same as attacks but are not as big. 2. If you would like to have an interview for the column "The Tyme Bomb!!!!", then contact Justin Tyme, NWWA reporter. 3. Send in the interviews for any card by the day before the card at the latest which means if the card is on Tuesday....they must be in by Monday night, same for Thursday Night Showdown....same for PPVs. 4. NO EXCEPTIONS!!!!! Any attacks or interviews sent in on the day of the card WILL NOT be used unless I decide to use them. [image: setstats]
