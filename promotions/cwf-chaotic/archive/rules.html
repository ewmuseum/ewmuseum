CWF (Chaotic, 2003) — Rules

Rules

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chaotic Wrestling Federation DOES NOT believe in giving more chances than necessary. If a person breaks any of the rules a first time, that person will be suspended for a week. Any titles held during the time of suspension will be stripped instantly. If another offence happens, you will be fired without hesitation. Read the rules carefully and understand them to avoid this from happening to you.
CWF Managament demands respect from ALL members, whether staff or wrestlers. If any problems arise, speak to management and we will resolve it calmly as best we can, DO NOT cause a confrontation on the forums or you will be suspended. Any disrespect shown towards CWF management will result in an automatic suspension for a period agreed upon by management. Management don't tolerate complainers, sore losers, troublemakers etc in CWF, we've had plenty already on the roster and on staff in the past and don't want any more like that. Those kinds of people annoy those who love and enjoy e-wrestling, ruining the enjoyment of this game. Anyone who fits any of those personality traits will be suspended and/or fired when trouble arises. If you admit to being one of those types of people, DO NOT fill out an application form, CWF doesn't want you.
CWF DOES NOT tolerate any form of racism, sexism or any other comments regarding religion, race, sexuality or other differences of opinion. This is a game and CWF likes to incorporate a friendly atmosphere between members so please, treat others how you expect to be treated or suffer the consequences. Punishments will include a formal warning through PM, suspension of account for a period of time and finally banning from the boards.
Upon completion of your application form, you will be notified through email within 72 hours after submitting an application on whether or not you were accepted into CWF. Please refrain from registering and posting on the boards until you have received the email letting you know you were accepted. Also allow 3 days upon joining the forums for CWF to put a graphic for your character on the website, if it won't be posted in that time, management will inform you, please be patient. Just because your picture isn't on the roster page, doesn't mean the rest of your info isn't so please, be patient.
Only use original characters, DO NOT use wrestlers already in existence in real life. Again, an original character shows creativity and originality to not only staff but also the other roster members. The owner of CWF also helped run NLCW and once had an application from a character named Latino Heat who was an exact replica of Eddie Guerrero so was sent back to the drawing board and was eventually accepted as a character named El Diablo Urbano. This is an example of what we like at CWF, original characters. If you're not original then we'll send you away until you come up with something creative.
If you're a male character then please, allow for realism in your character. Characters can't weigh more than 500 pounds or less than 150 pounds. Characters can be between 5-7 feet tall but no taller or shorter than that. Like it says, all about realism in CWF. Same goes for female characters, they can't weigh any more than 230 pounds or less than 100 pounds and their height must be between 5-6 feet.
CWF does NOT tolerate any form of racism, sexism or any other comments regarding religion, race, sexuality or other differences of opinion. This is a game and CWF likes to incorporate a friendly atmosphere between members so please, treat others how you expect to be treated or suffer the consequences. Punishments will include a formal warning through PM, suspension of account for a period of time and finally banning from the boards.
DO NOT use other wrestlers or even staff members in rps without asking for permission first. If you really have to attack someone in a post then create your own jobber character or even create a passer by in the street to take the full brunt of your assault otherwise you'll be seen as uncreative and unoriginal plus upset people for using their characters without asking first. You can use the General Manager character in rps as long as your character only talks to him with no violence shown towards the GM in any way.
CWF will NOT count stacked posting, ie roleplays that are posted near deadline. You have a week to roleplay for a match so use that week, if you really must post rps close to each other on deadline day then make sure they're posted with a 2 hour space between them. Staff check times posted and if we don't see at least 2 hours between rps we won't count the latter post. This is to allow your opponent(s) time to roleplay.
CWF cannot stress this enough, DO NOT COMPLAIN IF YOU LOSE! We've seen enough of this both in CWF and elsewhere and eventually lead to someone leaving over the issue. This is just a game and everyone has a week where they're not as good as they were a previous week. No one can maintain perfection so if you happen to lose then don't complain about it, pick yourself up and rp harder the next week to make up for the loss. One final note, all members of the upper management vote on winners each week to their own strict guidelines so if you do lose, then it's usually by a majority vote.
CWF Aggression is posted every Sunday at 11PM Eastern Standard Time at the earliest. Sometimes it goes up later, perhaps a day or two, most of the time it has been posted several hours early so please, don't send messages on the boards asking when results will be up, keep checking the website and when results are up, you'll either find out yourself that way or staff will announce it on the forums. Any questions on when results will be up WILL BE IGNORED. It states when here and on the RP Boards on the forums, if you still feel the need to ask then be prepared to have your question deleted and ignored.
Aggression and PPV roleplaying deadline is 12 midnight EST every Saturday night. This WILL NEVER CHANGE! If your rp is posted a minute after deadline ends, IT WILL NOT COUNT.
Requesting a match for Aggression or a PPV can only happen on the request board on the forums. Deadline for posting requests is Wednesday at 12 midnight EST, after that all matches will be official.
Send in all segments and matches by 12 noon EST every Saturday. This is to allow HTML to be added to the weekly shows so please, abide by this rule to allow your character/angle to be developed further.
The Rumor Mill, Segment, Angle, Interference forms MUST be submitted by 12 midnight on Fridays to allow for management and writers to add them to results. Please remember that not all ideas will be used for various reasons, don't be upset by this, just try again later.
