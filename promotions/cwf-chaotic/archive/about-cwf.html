About CWF

undated

The Chaotic Wrestling Federation (CWF) was started in January 2003 by Chaos A.D. founding member Dazz and his fellow members Violent A and Souless as not only an alternative to other wrestling promotions but also as a place they could rule with an iron fist however they liked. They had become disillusioned by the fact that the Championship Wrestling Alliance (CWA) had banned Chaos A.D. from competing there due to their dominance in other companies such as Revolution Wrestling Federation (RWF) and many more. Violent A and Dazz had even been banned from tag team competition because of their tight grip on the RWF Tag titles as well as the 6 month undefeated reign as RWF Hardcore champ and several month run as RWF World champ that Dazz had, Violent A's TV title run that lasted several months and the grip that Souless had on the US title. The anger and frustration that this brought them helped make the decision to create their own company to not only do as they liked, but also to show others how a great wrestling company is truly run.
Dazz was named as General Manager of their weekly show Monday Night Havok but banned from competing because of his new role as management. A man named Alex Fealy was given the task of running CWF as its President, Alex being perfect for the job thanks to his loyalty to the company and his honest and fair handling of CWF. Alex and Dazz introduced the Television title to CWF in the opening month then quickly followed by adding United States and World Heavyweight championships to the fold. With the addition of tag teams coming in, the Tag Team titles were soon added, followed by the Cruiserweight and finally Hardcore titles. With stars like Chaos A.D., Nemesis, Spike, Rob Lesnar and the most popular star in CWF at the time Magma, Monday Night Havok became the number one show to watch in America, Canada and the rest of the world. Dazz was known as an egomaniac in his wrestling days but as General Manager, the power quickly went to his head and he did as promised, favoring Chaos A.D. and awarding them decisions and championships, most being overturned by President Alex. He was also innovative though, the best known of his innovations being the Highway to Hell match, a demonic structure in which a 15 foot high cage with a roof is lowered around the ring, the structure covered in barbed wire with various weapons tied to the inside walls while the entire structure is surrounded by a wall of 6 foot high fire. This match was introduced at the June pay per view of the same name to determine the true World champion when a double pin vacated the title and five contenders stepped forwards claiming they had the right to hold it. A 6th man was needed and Dazz placed himself into that match, eventually going on to win the contest and become the World Heavyweight champion once again.
The very next night on Monday Night Havok, President Alex stripped Dazz of the title and declared a Fatal 4 Way contest to be held at the Death Wish event in July between the remaining uninjured contenders, Magma, Fear, Rob Lesnar and Sam Van Dam. Magma won the match and became the last World champion of Chaotic Wrestling Federation as it closed its doors in August of 2003 due to money issues.
Former wrestler and businessman Lord Phillippe DeMontfort bought the rights to the CWF name and exactly a year after the company closed its doors, declared CWF back in business. He managed to garner the very best names in the business including Christian Cage, Rage, Benny Boot and others and on September 12th 2004, the first CWF Aggression went live on Sunday nights. Instead of having a General Manager, DeMontfort decided to place a President in charge, naming retired wrestler and another Chaos A.D. founding member Tommy Chaos as the man in charge, his bodyguard being his good friend and the third man who created the legendary group Fear. A Vice President was named as Tommy's second in command, that man being James Vilhelms who was treated as Tommy's slave and did all the tough jobs. The first Aggression brought some surprises with Ryan Coleman winning the newly created CWF International title in a battle royal in the night's main event, a Chaos title being created and awarded to Jason Garret and Christian Cage receiving a greeting from an old friend by the name of The Odinist
Other stars such as Justice, The Flock and Cenotaph joined, competing in some innovative matches such as the Asylum match while Chaos A.D. waged war, this time on former friend Dazz and his fiance Faith. The Chaos title also caused some contoversy, the 24/7 rule stating that the belt could be won by anyone at any time, this statement becoming truth when NLCW star Jason Wright appeared during a match backstage to win the belt only for Jake Fury to head back to NLCW 4 days later to win it back in the middle of the ring on their show Avulsion. Wright was soon forced to leave NLCW and joined CWF, becoming a member of Chaos A.D. for only the second time in his career. In January 2005, DeMontfort did the unthinkable and sold CWF to Hollywood director Seth Payne, who fired every staff member apart from the CEO, Lady Reiko DeMontfort, and promising the talent a new era. He brought back the General Manager role, naming retired wrestler and former NLCW Commissioner Jaymz Marshall as the new man in charge of Aggression.
Under Jaymz came more talent, wrestlers such as Dark Rayne and Steele joining the ranks ready to prove to the world why they are held in the highest regard. A month after taking over CWF, Seth Payne quit and sold the company to another retired wrestler and a man who held the NLCW World Heavyweight title on several occasions and was also one half of the NLCW Tag Team champions along with Dazz: the self proclaimed "Only F'n Show" Jason Stylez. Stylez left CWF as it was but promised something huge would begin shortly, that something being the return of his old company, the uWo. A war was started between uWo and CWF with Scotty Ace, Lightning and several others showing up on Aggression but soon fizzled out, CWF killing them off and driving them away when Jake Fury defeated Scotty Ace for the Chaos title at Ultimate Chaos in an Electrified Barbwire Swimming Pool of Death match. CWF was left on its own again to head straight to the top while the uWo threat died a month later with the company shutting down again. Around this time, Faith became the first woman to ever hold a World Heavyweight championship when she defeated her fiance Dazz after taking the challenge he offered to anyone who wants the belt. She fended off tough competition from Priest and Christian Cage but eventually fell short in June when Cage reclaimed the gold in Highway to Hell.
Dark Rayne also had an unprecedented run as International champion, becoming the only undefeated champion in the new era of CWF and longest reigning champ ever. Talent Inc, a group consisting of Christian Cage, Dark Rayne, Dragon Master and Johnny Speede, became the most popular of all time and enjoyed the successes they brought upon themselves. In late summer of 2005, CWF had problems with some of the managerial staff, those people seeming hellbent on closing CWF down over jealousy issues, spreading rumors and lies about the company which almost brought CWF to its knees. Luckily, the DeMontforts are tough and more ruthless than ever, vowing to get back at those former staff members for what they did and promising to reopen Chaotic Wrestling Federation and once again become the top wrestling company in the world.
Chaotic Wrestling Federation is entertainment based and comes from the heart of New York City, CWF's home arena being the most famous of them all, Madison Square Garden. CWF has pushed for tough battles in the ring and entertainment inside and out of it too. Any man or woman in charge of CWF shows whether they are General Managers, Vice Presidents or even the CEO herself have strived to provide nothing but the very best for fans on a weekly basis live on TV and the very best for all those working for the CWF company. Anyone wishing to sign up and join one of the most popular wrestling companies of all time can also expect to be among the finest people in the business and treated equally, as is the policy within CWF. Not only does CWF favor entertainment and sometimes shock value on rare occasions, they also respect the traditions of the wrestling industry and uphold the need to give fans what they desire: pure wrestling in its many forms.
Chaotic Wrestling Federation is now owned and run by Dazz once more but the company is still in the hands of the DeMontforts, the two making a deal to show CWF programs on their brand new television channel "CHAOS TV", a channel they began to show the very best TV shows, CWF being the crown jewel of the scheduling with all CWF TV shows and pay per view events shown free of charge to all fans in exchange for their money spent on specially produced PPV programs featuring the various stars of stage, screen and CWF.
The CWF Chaos title is an anything goes championship held under a no holds barred ruling. If you're the Chaos champion, expect to have difficult and dangerous confrontations in defence of the title in matches involving weapons, barbed wire, thumbtacks and whatever else comes to mind. The toughest wrestlers in the business have held this title, from Jake Fury to Cenotaph, Faith to Violent A and Priest plus many more in between. CWF also has a Cruiserweight championship for males only that weigh 225lbs or under. This title involves all aspects of high flying action and incredible moves with the best of the smaller wrestlers putting on amazing matches all in the name of winnnig the Cruiserweight championship. Females also get to compete for the CWF Women's title, proving that they are the best in the company by holding the title. Think you have what it takes to win these and more? Anything is possible and CWF likes to cultivate stars whether they are new to the business or veterans of the ring.
Could you become a future CWF champion or Hall of Famer? Or perhaps you'd like to advertise CWF as one of our delectable Divas? Maybe even a writing job working behind the scenes is more for you?
