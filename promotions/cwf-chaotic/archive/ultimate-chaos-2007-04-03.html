CWF Ultimate Chaos - April 3rd 2007 ADD INTROS PS: Hello and welcome to CWF Ultimate Chaos right here in the Ford Field Center in Detroit, Michigan. I'm Phil sullivan alongside my broadcast partner Derrick "The Man" Grimes and what an incredible jam-packed night we have in store.
DG: Every single championship will be on the line tonight when Generation Xtreme defend the Tag Team titles against The New Two Man Power Trip and The Hit Squad in a triple threat tag team match, Skye Estado will challenge Faith for the Women's championship and X-Factor will try to retain the International title against The Punk.

PS: I hear word that Mr Wallace will be here tonight but lets take you to Lucinda Welch for our first contest.
