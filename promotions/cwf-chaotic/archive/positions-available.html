The head writer will be in charge of the team of writers who will create the CWF shows and PPVs on a weekly/monthly basis. Head writer will make sure that all writing assignments will be distributed to the writing team and ensure that matches and segments are handed in on time for shows to be posted on time
If you'd like to become CWF's Head Writer, click Writers will write the matches/shows for CWF shows such as Aggression and each monthly PPV, ensuring that they are written and finished in full and sent in on time to assure CWF results can go on the site on time. Once a writer joins, that will be their only job in CWF and unless they're going on vacation or dead will be expected to keep up their assignments on a weekly basis.

This position is open to both dedicated column writers whose only job will be to write columns on the goings on within CWF and also to members of the CWF roster who wish to use a column to give a wrestler's point of view.
If you'd like to become a CWF Column Writer, click See above but this time for the CWF magazine instead of an on site column. If you'd like to become a CWF Magazine Writer, click Talent Relations will be placed in charge of a specific division within CWF and will deal with members of that division regarding angles, promos and also recruiting more members for that division, for example, a Talent Relations representative for the Tag Team division would recruit more tag teams into CWF and also referee any comments, questions and complaints from members of the tag team division. Talent Relations will be placed in the following divisions: Heavyweight, Cruiserweight, Women's, Chaos and Tag Team.
If you'd like to become a CWF Talent Relations officer, click Heavyweights wrestlers are those who will compete in CWF against each other in singles competition as well as tag team competition. Heavyweight wrestlers are determined by weight with those weighing 226 pounds or more being placed in the Heavyweight division. Title belts a Heavyweight can compete for are the World Heavyweight, International, Chaos and Tag Team championships.
If you'd like to become a CWF Heavyweight, click Cruiserweight wrestlers are determined by weight, anyone who weighs 225 pounds or under are considered to be a CWF Cruiserweight wrestler. Cruiserweights can compete for the Cruiserweight, Chaos and Tag Team championship belts in both singles and tag team competition.
If you'd like to become a CWF Cruiserweight, click CWF Divas can compete in singles and tag team competition but aren't limited to in ring action, instead an applicant can choose to become a wrestler or a non-active character that helps promote CWF around the world via message boards, websites and also compete in special contests such as Bikini Contests etc in much the same style as WWE Diva Stacy Keibler. Those applicants who do choose to compete in the ring can compete for the Women's, Tag Team and Chaos championships.
If you'd like to become a CWF Diva, click Tag Teams are superstars who compete actively in tag team competition and have a team of at least 2 people, either male or female. The only championship eligible for a team is the Tag Team championship although members of a team can also compete in singles competition and hold singles gold, that title belt determined by the division that competitior is in, for example, Melina and Johnny Nitro may hold the Tag Team titles but Melina would only be eligible to hold the Women's title in singles competiion.
If you'd like to become a CWF Tag Team, click An advertiser's job is to advertise CWF all over the internet on message boards, websites and wherever else possible within the allowed limits. For example, if a message board requires the owner's permission to advertise on their forums then you must obtain permission before posting an ad for CWF on their boards. Banners will be passed onto advertisers to post on boards and sites where allowed.
If you'd like to become a CWF Advertiser, click A HTML Writer is someone who will add HTML to the various CWF TV shows and PPVs before they're added to the main site. This will help management since they will be busy adding HTML to other things for the website and also organising cards, adding new superstars and Divas and everything else you can imagine to help keep CWF at the very best. A HTML Writer working for CWF will only have 1 task per week, that being adding the code before the show is posted so it should be no trouble at all to do this every Saturday night/Sunday morning. Once the task is done for one week, the show coded will be emailed to management ready to be uploaded onto the correct page of the site until the next week's coding needs to be done.
