PCW (Power Championship Wrestling, 1995)

"PCW MISCHIEF NIGHT MAYHEM"

28 October 1995 (for a card dated 30 October)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On Tuesday, October 30th, PCW will be holding its first card live from the Poughkipsee Civic Center in Poughkipsee, NY.
The matches will be:
US Title Tournament
Mischief Night Mayhem Madness!!!
Rules:
This is a tournament to see who is declared the US Champ. After the first round matches, the winners names are put into a hat and selected at random for the second round. This will keep everyone off balance!!!
First Round Matchups:
Rano
vs.
"White Tiger" Scott Morton
Rocky Mountain
vs.
"Personification of Evil" Damon Bradley
The Executioner
vs.
Purple Haze
The Heartbreak Kid
vs.
Juggernaut
World Tag Team Title Battle Supreme!!!
This Tournament for the World Titles will be competed under the following rules. BOTH men are allowed in the ring at the same time. There will be
NO tags or illegal Double Teaming. All other rules apply!!!
First Round:
The Original Jersey Boys-Tony & Vinnie
vs.
Masters of Violence-Punisher & Demolisher
The Pretty Boys-Ian & Nigel
vs.
The Predators-Viper & Panther
PCW World Title Matchup
BEST 2 OUT OF 3 FALLS
Apocalypse
vs.
Johnny "Sidewinder" Missle
PLUS!!!!!!
Interviews from various Superstars who will be involved in the Brawl for the
TV Title on Friday Night Power Hour {November 3rd}!!!!
PCW MISCHIEF NIGHT MAYHEM......BE THERE!!!!!!!
