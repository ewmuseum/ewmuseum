Okay, sorry about the delay in getting back to you, but how will you be running this league? Do we need to send in strategys (ala the old IWF) or will you just be doing things on a point basis (ala most of the e-wrestling leagues) or is it just a scripting league (ala the FWF and EMWA)? Also can the "threats" you mention be broadcast to everybody in some sort of weekly report or do you just mean it to be inter-personal? At any rate, below you will find couple of "bits" from my two entries, Jack "the Ripper" and BANZAI!!! [ roll tape ] (In the studio are two men. The smaller man is short, stout, and dapperly dressed in a white linen suit, snap-brim hat, and spats. He is smoking a large cigar. Behind him is a larger man, just a little under 6' tall, in a dark pin-striped suit. He has a sallow complexion and a continuous muscular twitch on the right side of his face. He has a bad-tempered expression on his face. These are Jack "the Ripper" Morandini and his manager, Dominic "Da Boss" Altobelli) Da Boss: Well, well, well. Da first seeds in da SWL are out and it appears dat da Ripper gets ta face some nit-witted insect what call himself Da Tick. Whatcha got ta say ta dis loser, Jack? Ripper: Nothing. (the Ripper flicks open a switch-blade knife and grins at the camera) Da Boss: Well punk, looks like we're gonna see if youse can take a ripping and keep on ticking, eh? Both:HAR-DE-HAR-DE-HAR-DE-HAR!!! [ end tape ] <fade-in> <two men in ninja costumes (one all black, the other all red) stand> <flanking a third man who is dressed in WWII Japanese Army uniform> Hojo Tojomoto: Halloween, fifty-one years ago next month, the forces of Imperial Nippon smashed the forces of the decadent West at Pearl Harbor. When you step into the ring with us, we will destroy you as gloriously as our honorable anscestors did the U.S. Navy then. Ninja and Ronin will leave you crippled and begging for mercy, but there will be none, for it is our destiny to destroy all who face us. All: banzai!...Banzai!!...BANZAI!!! <fade-out>
