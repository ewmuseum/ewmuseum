Organization: Memorial University of Newfoundland Date: Wed, 4 Nov 1992 19:21:49 GMT Lines: 55 Thought that unusual subject would get your attention...Now then, on to the business at hand, namely my new wrestling federation. It will be called the Simulated Wrestling League(SWL). The catch about this one will be that the matches will be e-mailed to the participants involved. Bi-weekly results and ratings will be posted on the wrestling newsgroup, recounting the two previous weeks happenings and their effects on the wrestling scene. Using these ratings, you, acting as a manager will try and sign matchups between higher rated wrestlers. Remember the higher your wrestlers rating is, the better quality opponents you will fight, moving you closer to title shots. Anyone wishing to submit, may send their descriptions of one wrestler and one tag team(optional) to my address below. The description of your wrestlers should include: Appearance: what he looks like, what he wears, general description Entrance Music: if applicable Height: ????? Weight: ??? Style: choose from following: Brawling, Scientific, Underhanded, High-Flyer, or combination Moves: you receive punch, kick, chop, stomp, etc. you must choose 30 additional moves, corresponding to the chosen style of wrestling (ex. Brawling= Turnbuckle Smash, Knee Lift, etc.) Scientific= suplexes, karate kicks, etc.) Underhanded= foreign object, manager interference) High-Flyer= drop kicks, body tackles, etc.) Finishing Move: choose from traditional finishers or create one of your own. Name it as you see fit, just describe it. Double Team Maneuvers: choose 5 moves together with partner Double Finisher: choose as above finishers Valet: if applicable Tag Partner: if applicable And there you have it. All you need to do now is send your submissions, questions and the like to me. I am... [name withheld], the Nomad [detail withheld]@morgan.ucs.mun.ca P.S.: Only e-mail please. Thanks in advance...
