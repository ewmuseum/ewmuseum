A simulator-and-roleplay hybrid HWF-tagged fed whose own closure the newsgroup community nominated as a 1997 news story. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Hardcore Wrestling Federation announced itself on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 6 July 1997, hosted at geocities.com/area51/zone/7723, and called itself, in its own words, a fed that combined “the best of both worlds, simulator and role-playing.” Its own launch ad put a sixteen-man World Championship tournament at the center of the pitch: the first sixteen entrants to sign on went straight into the bracket. A week later its own president introduced himself to the newsgroup by first name only, disclaimed any competitive posturing — “I am not going to say We are the best or get into a competitive argument with the powers to be” — and pitched the fed as a home for “a fun bunch of guys(some women), who are in a league to have fun and get creative.”

The newsgroup's own reception was skeptical from the first reply. One regular asked how the fed could claim to be the best “when you're just starting out,” and needled it for posting the same recruitment ad five times under five different subject lines in one day. A self-appointed newsgroup watchdog made the same count independently — five ads in twenty-four hours — and told the fed that one post a day was plenty; its own surviving thread carries no reply to either complaint.

How the HWF (GeoCities) actually ran beyond its own recruitment drive isn't in the surviving record — no roster, no results, and no further shows survive under its own name. What survives instead is an outside notice of its end: the newsgroup community's own 1997 Year End Awards ballot nominated the fed's own closure, credited there to its president by name, as a News Story of the Year candidate that November, independent of anything the fed itself ever announced. Its own record gives no date for when that closure happened, only that by four months after its own launch ad, the newsgroup at large already knew it was over.
