Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Universal Wrestling Circuit, UWC on its own page, was founded in April 2005 by two veteran e-fedders who meant to continue the success of their old e-fed, the BMWF. Its front page asked readers whether they had what it takes to survive in the circuit and sent them to the rules and an application. Its menu ran through rules, application, staff, schedule, rankings, roster, title history, stables, shows and forums, with pages on wrestler creation and roleplay tips.

Seven steps were given for applying: read the rules thoroughly; make sure of the time to roleplay at least once a week; read some of the fed's shows to get the style; read the help sites provided; fill in an application; check the forums, where the application would be posted; and wait while the current UWC players voted on it. Its news item read “6/7/06: UWC Carnage Results now up! YAY!” A sidebar named Tamer, “Winner of March”, and below it Cash Flo.
