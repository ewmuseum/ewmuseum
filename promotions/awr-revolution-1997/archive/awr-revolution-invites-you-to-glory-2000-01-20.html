Time for the Revolution. # The Revolution begins now # いいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいい ~ Official AWR Website: ~ [#1 in Efed Universe's Top100] www.geocities.com/TimesSquare/Frontier/9038/mainpage.html いいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいいい I know you have received hundreds of thousands of ads. Join this fed, join that fed. You may think this is a desperate call for drawing people into some low ranked fed desperately trying to survive. Well, it is not.
This is an invitation. I have browsed the web read a lot of apps, watched a great deal of wrestlers, read numerous roleplays and I chose some adds that I though would be an addiction to the AWR.
The AWR started way back in 1997 and got a new look at the beginning of
2000. Here are some AWR characteristics you might be interested in reading before even checking us out:
- the fed is ran by email and it is a booked one - roleplays are sent out in packs - we have two weekly cards - one PPV/month - a great web page - a weekly newsletter - some of the best roleplayers on the net today. This should be the general idea. I won't get down on my knees and ask you to join. You read what the AWR is about, if you think it's interesting...then give it a look, and give it a try. If not...sorry to have disturbed you.
