What's the deal?" - AWR Card Results: "Wrath & Shocker Results" - AWR Card Previews: "3rd man in the tournament?" - AWR PPV Previews: "Revelations" - AWR Column: "The Issue" by [name withheld] - AWR Interview: "Calixto Baptista" by [name withheld] - AWR Personal Profile: [name withheld] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ -= Editorial =- I know the rules keep enforcing the roleplaying issue but I will address a few more "cool" stuff as far as roleplaying goes. Let's call them inside rules of some sort. This league has great roleplayers and that's what we encourage. We encourage good roleplaying to reach excellency not great roleplaying to reach average status (some leagues do!!!) So, here are some pointers.
1. Use detailed description in your roleplays so people can feel the atmosphere in the roleplay. See Wasp, Kash, Tiger and Keizmen for the best examples here. A good placement determines a roleplay in many ways.
2. Try to separate the roleplay in more bits, each dedicated to someone else. Just say the "Wrestler X pauses for a moment or whatever. 3. Starting after the PPV people that do not roleplay will *not* be booked for matches. That means you have to roleplay if you expect to fight and also, you have to challenge in order to fight.
4. People not roleplaying for 14 days are going by bye unless you notify me of who knows what bad event that just won't allow you to roleplay. 5. Roleplays are points. 6. Roleplaying brings angles along. 7. Roleplaying develops the character. 8. CHECK THE SPELLING OF YOUR ROLEPLAYS!!!! So, roleplay, AWR President, [name withheld] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ -= News =- - Wasp has made a great impact on the AWR although he came out just over a week ago. Wasp, an experienced and famous individual across the wrestling world, decided it was time to get between Kash and White Tiger, the two men battling over the AWR World Belt. Lots of rumours have spread lately, most of them announcing a union between Tiger and Wasp that should eventually cost Kash his title. President Lupsa is still to address this matter.

Visit the AWR official homepage for more info on how to vote: www.awr.8m.com -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -= Wednesday Wrath results [January 19, 2000] =- This Wednesday card had a car line-up that spelled ratings with a capital "R". The card kicked off heavily as the AWR fans saw another promising newcomer in the person of Messiah. Caedmon also made his re-debut giving his
low-card opponent absolutely no chance. The first match in Group#1 of the US Title tournament was up next. It featured Adam Stryker and Wolfgang. The latter, a newcomer to the AWR tried to measure up to the skills of AWR legend Stryker but he just couldn't cut it. Stryker walked away with the win after a great match.
Comic Relief was DQed in his match against Raskal as Pauly Shore threw pudding at Raskal. Comic Relief still walked out thinking he is the victorious one.
The second match in G#1 of the US Title tournament saw Demonic 2000 defeat Wolfgang. Father Time and White Tiger fought next in a great submission match. When the match was closing on the end zone, a cat fight ensued between Passion,
Tiger's manager and the Dream Girl, the manager of Father Time and Sandman. The match ended in a double DQ but these women are certainly not done with each other. A strange segment was up next as we saw AWR newcomer Wasp enter World Champion Kash's locker room and felling like pretty well in there. Kash was not exited about this and he invited Wasp outside to settle the score.
Before Kash walked out, White Tiger attacked him with a chair. Tiger also accidentally nailed the Wasp. Kash and Tiger brawled into the arena giving the fans a preview of the World Title match to follow next week. the segment ended with a shot of Wasp watching the brawl scene and enjoying himself thoroughly.
The night ended with the third match in the G#1 of the US title tournament. The match saw Adam Stryker and Demonic 2000 in astonishing form. Demonic put up a hardcore match but Stryker had the sense of emergency to turn the tides around and score the win and thus qualify for the US title match that will happen next week at the PPV.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -= Saturday Shocker results [January 22, 2000] =- * Summary by [name withheld] * - Calixto Baptista defeated Inferno by pinfall in the first round in the AWR US Title Tournament. This match was pretty intense with both gladiators pulling out move after move on his opponent. At one point Calixto nailed Former AWR World Champion
Inferno with his finishing manoeuvre, the Voice Of The Voiceless, but Inferno actually kicked out of it. Calixto capitalised to the end until he applied the Voice Of The Voiceless again and Inferno kicked out AGAIN (...)
but Calixto covered him once again for the win. Calixto is going in the third round in his group later tonight for a spot for the US Title match on
January 30 at Revelations next Sunday. - The Sundance Kid defeated Wasp by disqualification. This was Wasp's first match in the AWR, and he did quite an impression on the fans with his very brutal wrestling style. The crowd was obviously on
Wasp's side. Wasp capitalised through the entire match, pulling off a backdrop driver through a table outside the ring. Referee "Suburban Ranger"
Zane Fink was always in Wasp's way, avoiding him from destroying the Kid with various illegal objects. At one point Wasp even tried to powerbomb Ref
Zane but The Kid stroke him from behind. Later Wasp thrown many chairs in the ring and on multiple occasions tried to hit The Kid with it. When he tied the Kid in the corner and nailed him with a chair with it for a second time, the ref had enough and disqualified Wasp. He then tasted Kid's blood and said to the camera : "But your mind is not with your prey!". He then hit the ref with the chair, and wrote Kash and White Tiger's names on a chair.
- Sandman defeated Inferno by pinfall in the second round in the AWR US Title Tournament. This match was pretty balanced between the two wrestlers with nice move combinations from both of 'em. At one point Inferno was going for the Flame
Thrower but Sandman countered the move with an elbow strike on Inferno's back. Sandman then capitalised to hit the Eternal Nightmare later on for the win. Sandman is going in the third round in his group later tonight to face
Calixto Baptista for a spot for the US Title match on AWR Revelations on January 30th. - The match between Talon and Father Time ended up in a double disqualification. The match was going on normally until Dream Girl interfered and kicked Talon multiple times in the ribs. Passion then came to ringside with the commentating crew. She said that she would kick Dream Girl's butt in a swimsuit competition, calling her a "50 cent slut". She then left and clotheslined Dream Girl and whiped her on the security guard rail. White
Tiger then came out to the ring and double-bulldogged Talon and Father Time. As the girls continued brawling outside, Father's bud Sandman came out and drop-kicked Tiger over the top rope. - The camera faded back to the parking garage on the south end of Montegomery Civic Center where we could see Kash's black Mercedes Kompressor. Wasp then came into view and with the chair he wrote Kash and White Tiger's names on. He then destroyed the windshield with the chair and entered the car through it. He smashed a Don Perrier bottle on the top of the car. The screen faded to black as The Wasp continued his tirade on the car with his chair.
- Calixto Baptista defeated Sandman by pinfall for the spot in the triple threat match for the AWR United States Title next Sunday on AWR Revelations PPV. This match was really violent with tons of tables being broken and tons of illegal objects being used. Both guy ended up being busted open ; Sandman had his face busted with Calixto giving him a Fameasser on a chair and
Calixto's face being busted by an Arabian Face Buster. At some point he battle went outside. Calixto tried a frog splash from the second floor of the arena through a table but missed. Sandman executed a awesome bomb through a table and a piledriver on another one that didn't even break.
Calixto then executed a leg drop through a chair from the top and a fall-forward powerbomb through ANOTHER table for the three count.
- Scene opened with Kash getting out of his locker room. He was going for his sneakers in his car when he caught sight of his car, completely dismantled. He called out the guy who did this and The Wasp came out from behind an ice cream truck parked around there. Thyn then got into a vicious brawl, Kash bodyslamming Wasp on the car and Wasp later smashing his now-trademarked chair on Kash's head. Kash then sprayed an aerosol can in
Wasp's eyes, blinding him. White Tiger them came out attacking Kash. Kash then capitalised with many garbage can shots to the head but White Tiger and
Wasp then joined together in beating up Kash until security officials broke the group up. - In the evening's main event, Kurt "The Wolf" Keizmen defeated Adam Stryker in a enclosed steel cage match to retain the AWR Television Title. This was an AWESOME match, with both competitors giving their 110 percent in this contest. Both wrestlers pulled out many move with Stryker with a pedigree on Keizmen from the top or Keizmen with a armdrag from the top rope. At one point Keizmen was busted wide open with a catapult face first in the cage by Stryker but at one point Keizmen was holding Stryker on his back and he jumped off the top and sent Stryker's neck first between the cage and the pole. Stryker was out cold and then collapsed in the middle of the ring. Keizmen had no choice but to cover Stryker for the victory. The show ended with Keizmen helping Stryker out in a great act of sportsmanship.

Now with first two sections of the tournament complete it will be interesting as to which man will be crowned the new AWR
United States champion at the upcoming PPV. With Adam Stryker and Calixto Baptista already winning their groups they will have to wait until Wednesday Night to find out who their final opponent will be. And with the rivalry heating up between Baptista and Stryker, it really wouldn't matter who the third man in the match would be because these men will only be looking to destroy each other.
The current T.V. champion Kurt Keizmen is still undefeated, but his opponent for the PPV has still not been determined. But most of his wins have been very controversial, can he remain a champion very much longer?
The New Era is slowly but surely dying. Inferno has been trying to keep everything together, but with Angel of Death and himself being the only surviving members I don't think that they are going last very much longer, especially with Caedmon looking to get his hands on Inferno, and Angel of
Death busy fighting all of the members of the Global Alliance, these two are bound to fall apart unless they can find help.
Just when you thought that the Global Alliance was dead, their leader Talon seemed to come out of nowhere and is attempting to pick the pieces up and form a greater Alliance than there has ever been. These men are some of the best athletes that I have ever seen, and as long as they can stick together they will last a long time.
And finally things may be getting really interesting real soon. With the AWR being the #1 e-fed on the Internet, many wrestlers are looking to enter the federation. I think that this will turn everything up a notch and then we will see who deserves to be called the best.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -= Interview with Calixto =- by [name withheld] (Comic Relief has just been given a microphone.) Comic-- Hello fans of the AWR. Today I will be interviewing Calixto Baptista. Now Calixto I must say you have an impressive win streak. Can you tell me about your victories?
Baptista-- Well It's just a beginning for me in the AWR and all those victories doesn't mean very much to me. Indeed, I does show how much of a great threat I am to all the AWR wrestlers that want to get in my way but
I'm looking more in the future. A victory over the recently fired Shadowlight, the newcomer Sandman and the "living over-rated legend" Inferno is just the beginning of my quest to prove once and for all to the Americans and all other imperialist nation that the power of the people is the only way to go. Not only it's the strongest of all the powers but it is the only
FAIR way to run a nation. I am really looking forward for the US Title Tourney : even though title are pretty much meaningless to me, I think I'd like to wear this United State Championship Belt, if it's the only way to be recognise here in the United States.
Comic-- What is your favourite movie, and why? Baptista-- I never really known American cinema, since there are no diplomatic relations between Cuba and America -
America cutting the relations BEFORE the Cuban missiles crisis, unlike many of you may think, and this because America saw the threat that was the power of people for "American democracy" - I was never really familiarised with
American cinema. The way I see this and the thing I realised since I'm here in America is that cinema is one of America's best attempt to corrupt the population and spread the disease known as the "American dream" from west to east and all around the world. American patriotism at his best: Americans slaying people in Vietnam, Americans fighting the evil communists and so more. But there were some movies that were indeed good films. Movies like
"Full Metal Jacket" by Stanley Kubrick, which shows us how the Americans were trained to be killing machines and to have no emotions in the Vietnam war. It also exposed us the American hypocrisy : America, pretending to spread peace all around the world, getting involved in a civil war in
Vietnam and ignoring the population asking for peace. Comic-- What do you think about the return of Caedmon? Baptista-- I don't know very much about this guy... but I heard he is a legend here in the AWR. And I don't really give a rats ass either. The problem is now that the AWR is filled up with has-been coming back to action. We don't want this; we want to see new wrestlers and new talents!
Still, in his group for the AWR United States Championship, I think he will make it out of it and will face me and Stryker this Sunday on Revelation. So on Revelation, I will fight 2 has-beens and I will prove once and for all that the old wrestlers just can't stand the revolution going on right now in the AWR.
Comic-- This past Saturday you attacked Stryker. Is there a specific reason behind that? Baptista-- I said it many times before and I will say it again : I didn't attacked Stryker to make a reputation because I'm a new comer in the AWR : I did it because Stryker is just the kind of people that I just can't stand.
He represent all the hypocrisy going on the United States : the rich people making profits over the poor ones. Look at Los Angeles : there is a poor side, with desperate people working for nothing, and a rich side, using the poor people to work in sweat shops for nothing, laughing and humiliating them. Adam Stryker is always acting like a smart-ass here in the AWR with everyone: he has a too much high opinion of himself for what he really is. I want to prove to everyone that Stryker is nothing: just another imperialist ass hole that won't survive against the power of the people. He better prepare for this Sunday because we are always ready to fight.
Comic-- Now Calixto. It has been said that I have no taste. But I prefer Chocolate puddin popz as opposed to Vanilla. What is your favourite Flavour? Baptista-- *sigh* ... I'm lactose intolerant. Comic-- What are your plans for the PPV? Baptista-- I always didn't cared about titles or contendership :because I don't think it really represents the talent of the guy who holds it. I only fight for honour and my believes. But I think the US Title is special, because it really represents something to you Americans. If I win the US
Title this Sunday on Revelation, that will mean to you Americans that I rule in America, and that I am the best wrestler here, huh? So that is exactly what I want : recognition. And if it takes the US Title to have it, I will do anything in my power to have it and you can be sure that I will. Stryker, you will never prepare enough and you will be destroyed you like you are being destroyed now desperately trying to have the TV Title.
Comic-- Thanks Calixto. Good luck at the PPV. For the AWR I am the one the only Comic Relief.
