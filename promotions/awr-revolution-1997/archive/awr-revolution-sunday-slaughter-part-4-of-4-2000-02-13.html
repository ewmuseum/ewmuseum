[Green lights fill the arena. The entrance way is filled with smoke. Kash steps out of the smoke, and the fans begin to boo wildly. Kash, being used to this sort of thing, merely looks around at the crowd, and then makes an obscene gesture to further infuriate them. He walks cockily to the ring and makes sure to take the time to trash talk any fan who he seems worthy. Once he arrives in the ring, he stands in the middle of the ring and absorbs the hatred of all the fans in the arena.
Kash is very fit and trim. His body isn't bulky, but his muscles are very well-defined. He is built like Steve Blackman. He has a few scars on his arms and back from hardcore matches and field missions, and an odd Japanese symbol tattooed on his arm. There is some speculation among the ranks of the AWR what this symbol means exactly. Kash is a very handsome guy. Kash has brilliant white hair and a white goatee.
His eyes are a very piercing light blue. Kash wears a pair of green, baggy, ripped corduroy shorts and a grey T-shirt that says something like "Kash Hurts". He has black elbow and knee pads. He has a few pairs of black combat boots like the ones that he wore in the Army that he likes to wear in the ring.]
Lance: It’s so strange to see the World Champ being introduced first… Deion: Where do you come up with these stupidities?!?!? Mike: and now…from Havana, Cuba…standing 6'5 and weighing 265 lbs…he is the voice of his people…the AWR US Champion....CAAAALLLLIIIIXXXXTTTOOO
BBBAAAPPPTTTTIIISSSTTTAAA!!!!!!!!! ["Born of a broken man" by Rage Against The Machine starts playing as the American fans is the arena start booing like crazy. Calixto walks out with a confident and very serious look on his face. He carries a dirty and uncared for US Belt. He looks at the booing crowd chanting
"USA - USA", he just looks at them with a discouraged but amused look and he chuckles. He doesn't look offended, he is just amused by "their ignorance". Calixto has short black hair with black eyes and a short bard. He is shaped pretty much like Farooq (but he is more agile). He oftenly has an angry look (wide open eyes and grinding his teeth). He has a scar right next to his right eye. He is muscular but not a power- house though. He wears a black T-shirt with Che Guevara on it. He wears green army pants and combat boots. He has a red band around his right arm.]
Lance: That US belt looks like shit… [The camera zooms on the belt. It doesn't look in a very good condition. The gold is not shiny at all, and there is a huge red star spray-painted on the belt. There is also some leather missing on the strap.]
Deion: Jesus… Hope Kash wins it…cause this gold needs serious reconditioning!!!! [*Ding ding*] Lance: Kash now tapping referee Gilles Poison on the shoulder and smiling at him… Deion: He’s probably his lover… Spade: That’s sick… [Calixto looks bored as he charges Kash and pushes him in the ropes choking him. Calixto raises a knee and kicks Kash in the gut then whips him the other way, follows him and hits him with a powerslam as he bounces off the ropes.]
Lance: Great start from Calixto, he’s now climbing on top of Kash and punching him in the head…
Spade: And Kash throws him off…both men up…lock up…Calixto outsmarts Kash and twists his hand behind his back… Deion: Calixto with stupid wrestling here…but now he locks a head lock as well, sweeps Kash’s legs…AND DROPS KASH WITH AN UNUSUAL FACE SLAM!!!!
Lance: Great move by Calixto, he picks Kash up…and whips him hard in the corner…and follows…running clothesline!!!! Calixto is jumping on the second rope and prepares to punch Kash…
[As Calixto is getting ready to do some punching on Kash, Poison walks over and just pulls Calixto down signalling him that this is not the way to do it. Calixto is pissed and starts screaming at him. Kash comes from behind and nails Calixto with a bulldog.]
Deion: Great bulldog!!! Spade: I think this Poison dude is trying to keep things too fair and is ruining the match… Kash grabs Calixto up…whips him…no…drags him close…short lariat…and a swinging neckbreaker now!!!!
Lance: Kash is smirking at the crowds Deion: Kash with an advantage…drops a few elbows to Calixto’s knee and LOWHEAD SCISSORS ALMOST BREAKING BAPTISTA'S NECK THERE!!!!!! Kash scoops him up…headbutt...STUN GUN!!!
[Kash is now pointing to Gilles Poison seemingly saying "Watch this carefully". Kash lays down for the cover and Poison is immediately down and counting like a bullet.]
ONE.. TWO.. THRE….SHOULDER UP!!!! Lance: HEY FISH!!!! WHAT WAS THAT??? Spade: Shit… Gilles counted like a bullet…and almost handed Calixto the defeat… Hope this is the first and last time he’ll do this is this match…
Deion: Could be the stress of his first main event, you know… Lance: Yeah sure!!! [Kash is obviously pissed as he climbs to the top rope and waits for Baptista to get up. Baptista gets up and before he can even turn his attention to Gilles, Kash launches.]
Deion: Kash jumps down with a diving shoulder block knocking Baptista down again!!! Hey, two "downs" in one sentence!!!
Spade: Kash whips him in the corner hard and follows with a clot - BAPTISTA IS FLYING!!!!! He just jumped back on the top rope and Kash meets the turnbuckle. [Kash staggers out with his back turned to the turnbuckle… Calixto doesn’t hesitate for a second and launches off the top planting the
World Champion face first with a flying bulldog] Deion: Calixto going for a cover… Gilles with a count… ONE… Kickout!!! Deion: See? Fair count!!! Lance: Anyway… Calixto pulls Kash near the turnbuckle, slides out and OH MY!!!!! He just pulled Kash’s legs wide in that post!!!! Crowds: OUUUUUUUUUUCCCCHHHH!!! Deion: Got that right ya’ nutcracker!!!! Spade: And now he's wrapping Kash's leg around that turnbuckle!!!! Chic back in, spins Kash and - FIGURE FOUR APPLIED AROUND THE POST!!!! Ropes to far - Kash is kicking the steps in pain!!!!
[Strangely Kash is not trying to break the move although you can see the pain on his face. Actually he is just grinning at the ref who seems more disturbed by the move than any of the competitors.]
Lance: What the hell is wrong with Poison?????? Spade: What the hell??? [Poison gets down and unlocks the figure four while he keeps motioning to Calixto that the move is illegal or something.]
Deion [laughing]: Man…Lance…even you don’t enjoy this amount of fairness… Lance: Fairness my ass!!!! Something is fishy!!! [Calixto is up on the apron and pulls Gilles near the ropes choking him. Kash gets to his feet and charges fast. Before Calixto can react

[Kash grabs a purse from some woman in the first row and starts whiping Calixto with it. He then hands the purse back to the woman and charges Calixto. [Calixto cry can be heard trough out the arena] Calixto: NOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!! Lance: Kash finally lets go and boots Calixto in the back of the head!!! Kash is showboating to the fans, and Calixto has rolled out of the ring….
Spade: Kash follows and hits Calixto with a right left combination on the floor and Calixto goes down in a heap…. And Kash is going for a chair…. Kash has a chair, and Calixto is staggering…
*WHAMMMMMM* Lance: VICIOUS CHAIR SHOT AND CALIXTO IS BUSTED OPEN!!!!! Spade: Kash rolls Calixto back in and jumps onto the apron, and a spring board into a leg drop…AND MISSES HIM!!!!!! CALIXTO ROLLS OUT OF

Lance: WHAT THE HELL IS POISON DOING HERE????? Deion: He’s just being the people’s ref Lance! Spade: POISON STOPPED CALIXTO FROM USING THE CHAIR!!!! [Calixto turns and gets in the face of Poison again. Calixto has Poison backing up the aisle way and Kash is coming from behind with another chair, and another vicious chair shot to the back of Calixto.]
*WHAMMMMMM* Deion: Kash in control… Spade: Fuck you Deion… Calixto is being screwed!!! Deion: Yeah….Sure…Kash is now dragging Calixto by the hair back to the ring, and he rolls him in again…. Kash rolls into the ring and grabs him with a arm bar... Calixto is trying to fight to the ropes…

Calixto: Well what happened was exactly what I though. The power of the people was predominant tonight as there is no doubt that Kash didn't delivered tonight. Why ? Because not only he wasn't even able to stand up against me, but he employed some sell-out referee to get the job done. You see Kash was sure that he couldn't do ANYTHING against the voice of the people and I understand him. So he buyed some referee to make up a tap out. But does that affect me ? Not very much. First of all, Kash, good for you, you can have this wortheless belt. But you employed some referee to get the job done ? Kash, that proves how pathetic you are. You are not even able to face the revolution that is going on in the AWR. So you use the only thing you have left : money. I dare you not to say that it was a very genious idea to buy a referee because we saw that a lot of times before from people who can't beat their opponents. There is only one thing that affects me tonight. It's the fact that there is no justice or morality here in the AWR. I feel screwed and I don't like being screwed. No, not screwed by you Kash, but screwed by one man. His name's Chris Lupsa.
[Crowd reacts as Calixto Baptista mentions the president's name.] Calixto: So Chris Lupsa, if you have the GUTS to come down here, get your ass out of your presidential office NOW!

Calixto : Well, well, well Chris Lupsa. You know, I decided to enter the AWR because of its great reputation. People said that they were revolutionizing the wrestling world. You know Chris, I received TONS of offers from other wrestling organizations to join them but you know what ? I stayed with the AWR because I saw that the real talent was here. I really taught I could make a difference being in the AWR with nice competiton. You know what Chris ? You know what I just realized ?
I just realized that my work here and my revolution is useless. Know why ? Because you are making money with me Lupsa. You are using my image and my people's image to make a personnal profit with it. You are using that little "rebel" and "communist" side on me to promote you events and sell more tickets.
[Lupsa is trying to explain himself to Calixto but Calixto doesn't listen and continues talking]
Calixto : ... And I'm saying to myself : How can I make a revolution when people is making profit of this revolution for their own profit ?
My work is maybe very usefull here in the AWR, but I can't stand being manipulated by some high imperialist to make him richer when I could help people in need. And that is exactly what I am going to do Lupsa...
