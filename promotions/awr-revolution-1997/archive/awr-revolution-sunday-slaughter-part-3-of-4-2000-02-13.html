=============================== The Wasp vs. Kurt "The Wolf" Keizmen Rules: TV Title on the line, winner gets a World Title Shot at Final
Stand Referee: Sean "Timebomb" Elliot Match writer: [name withheld] =============================== [A low humming sound begins to reverberate across the sound system, rattling the chairs of the fans, and you can even see the mat on the ring trembling a bit from the 'bass' of this noise. The lights go out, and red spotlights begin to dance across the arena, giving no hint at a pattern, just randomly taking their course. The sound begins to hum slightly louder, and the cameras pick up on several small children holding their ears, trying to shield themselves from the sound.]
Mike: Ladies and gentleman, the following the match is a TV Title match, scheduled for one fall...
[Mike shakes his head looking at his scorecard, as if he can't believe what he is suppose to say... After a couple seconds, he disheartedly goes on...]
Mike: The reason you are all here... The enigma of all wrestling...The man of a thousand faces... The KING of many rings... The...
[He stops and looks toward the scorekeeper's table, as if asking 'do I
HAVE to go on??'. Finally, through the stoppage...]
[On the Jumbo-Screen, you start to see images of what looks to be a
'Wildlife America' series, with enormous swarms of bees violently taking down several large animals. Then, going through several scenes, we are shown several images of the Wasp's past in the (current fed)...
Wasp entering an arena, him standing in the ring with a first look at his trademark grin... The crowd is BLASTING him with a chorus of boo's, signifying the heat he was getting... Wasp's trademark grin from several different angles in different settings... Each one laid out so that it appears more evil than the previous one... The last one shown stays up on the screen for about three seconds, showing Wasp with his head slightly lowered looking up through the top of his eyes, and his grin nearly stretching from ear to ear...'The Kiss' from the soundtrack of the Last of the Mohicans starts to play...]
Mike: Weighing in at TWO hundred... FORTY two pounds! Six foot Three of rage...The WAAAASSSPPPPPP!!!!
[The Wasp steps out from behind the curtain, wearing black leather pants that are tucked into a shiny pair of black wrestling boots... He wears no shirt, and a very noticeable tattoo on his left shoulder cap is of a wasp with a long stinger on it, and the words, 'Sector
Domination' are scribed underneath the wasp in cursive script. His goat- tee is well trimmed, and the mustache accompanying it is wide on his face, almost like a handlebar mustache without the curls at the end. He walks to the ring, playing up to whatever reaction he gets from the crowd, working them for a bigger response. Just as he gets to the ring,
Wasp jumps up on the side apron, turns out toward the crowd and kind of leans out while holding on to the rope, and glares intently at the crowd for a few seconds...]
[He slingshots himself over the top rope into the ring, and the frightening glare he was giving the crowd slowly transforms itself into a huge grin... An EVIL grin... He takes the microphone from the announcer, and starts pacing around the ring before he stops and tilts his head slightly to the right toward the fans, as if he were a teacher waiting for his class to be quiet before he begins his lecture. Wasp chuckles and starts pounding his hand on the top of the microphone, creating a LOUD thumping sound. He does this until he is able to draw the proper boo's from the crowd, then just tosses the microphone over his shoulder and into the crowd and laughs.]
Mike: And his opponent…standing five foot eleven and weighing in at two hundred and thirty five pounds…he is the AWR’s Undefeated…Uncontested… and Undisputed TV Champion…KURT "THE WOLF" KEIZMENNNN!!
