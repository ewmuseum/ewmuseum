So what is this e-fed juggernaut UCW that's threatening to take over the FW world you ask? Well, I've been running UCW for about nine months now over on efednews.net. Due to several problems on both sides of the ball, the apparent death of my tag team partner (if anyone sees Dan, tell him I love him), and other endeavors that I'm pursuing in the e-fedding world, I've decided that a move to FW would allow for more views and a lot of expansion for UCW.
Until some issues (new jobs, old jobs with more work, personal problems, etc.) a couple months ago that slowed us a bit, we were pumping out a new card every two weeks (week and a half for RPs, half a week for results). As you might have noted, we have quite the roster of superstars as well, which leads for a great time in both match-writing and building angles. All are welcome to join in on the action, I'll be posting an application either later tonight or tomorrow evening.
Any more questions, feel free to hit me up on here or IM, [detail withheld]. -Shane Linguistic — 28 June 2006 He's in MCW so you'll be seeing a lot more of him in the near future!
