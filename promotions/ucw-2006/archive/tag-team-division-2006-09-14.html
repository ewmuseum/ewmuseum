Tag Team Division?

14 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 14 September 2006 Hey guys, I'm just looking at the build for the next PPV (which is Night of the Legends II, you know, the big show) and I've already got 6 matches in line. Looking at the rest of the roster, I have 9 wrestlers left over, which to me seems like a formidable tag division.
I realize that none of these wrestlers are geared to be tag teams, but what do you think about using this PPV cycle to maybe try and develop some teams? We could either try to figure out teams ourselves or I can do a lethal lottery.
I think this is the best idea to better the fed and really get everyone some good airtime, because I'm not going to be able to give everyone singles programs.
Personally, I would cream over teams like Kin Hiroshi and The Sergeant, but that's just my idea.
The other solution would be to have a house show for everyone not on Revolution and PPV. I'm fine with either idea, but I wanna know what you guys think. If I get enough responses tonight, I'll post a new card tonight. If not, it'll be up tomorrow. So hurry up and respond!
-Shane Hell_Fighter — 14 September 2006 Just so you know Shane... I have no objections toward the idea of Nakita Dahaka joining a tag team division, but for storyline purposes, I kind of have to wait on the MCW Finale card (the Loser Is My Soldier match) before I can know for sure on what the plan of attack is on my end. That's if I want to do something based off of the storyline I am currently in (also making sure that Rob Franklin's handler is still all set for the new UCW)
But even if something doesn't quite work out, get with me and I am sure we can come up something with Nakita if you indeed can use her.
But I am trying to do the "wait and see" approach until hopefully this weekend when the card should be finished and posted.
thegr817deuce — 15 September 2006 One reply? Is that all I can get here? I'm not wanting to force anybody into anything here. That's why I'm looking for responses. Please help me out here so I can get the card up tomorrow.
-Shane Mr_Incredible — 15 September 2006 Here's a reply. WVU Mountaineers RULE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Oh wait.. you weren't looking for just any reply.. you meant for the question. Well can't help you there, sorry dude Linguistic — 15 September 2006 I'd be interested in helping out. I just have to get back into the swing of things since I just got back from my surprise vacation.
I'll be in touch on IM... you and I both have talked about the possibilities of a tag division.
Josh irishred — 16 September 2006 screw it...sign me up with someone and my partner and I will be the first UCW Tag Team Champions.
By someone I mean Dan Ryan...we hate each other...it will be fun. Phenomenal — 16 September 2006 Or you could team up with Frankie Scott...He hates you too. MrWest — 18 September 2006 If all it takes is hating Red to be his tag team partner, heck, that should be an easier job to qualify for that bagger at Winn-Dixie
robishott — 18 September 2006 I'm all for this. There should be a lot more RPers after the merge, so why not get a tag team division in there? This way more people would be used from week to week.
Phenomenal — 20 September 2006 Shane, That Lethal Lottery idea sounds good too. I don't know a lot of guys around here and it would be good to take a chance.
In fact, I want to team with that Winn Dixie Bagger guy. thegr817deuce — 20 September 2006 Okay. Since it would seem that the majority of you believe this is a good idea, we're going to do it. How, you may ask?
Well, how about we do this. EVERYONE, even those who know they have something going on in singles, send me a PM with 2-3 people you think you might like to tag with.
Of course, this won't work in most situations, but it at least gives me an idea of what could possibly get some great work out of people members of the team.
In the end, a lethal lottery will be imposed, but with this kind of info, I can always have one of those professional wrestling lethal "lotteries".
So PM me those partners people. I wanna see what we can do here. -Shane
