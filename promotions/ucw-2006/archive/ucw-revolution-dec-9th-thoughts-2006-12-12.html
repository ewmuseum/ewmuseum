UCW Revolution (Dec. 9th) Thoughts

12 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Phenomenal — 12 December 2006 Nice Show Shane! I'm excited to see how Night of Legends II plays out. Frankie is in a good spot right now. I just feel really sorry for Sarge and Dakota. Paybacks are hellacious!! Linguistic — 12 December 2006 What are you talking about, paybacks? Poor little ol' boyscout Sargie was only trying to protect his shot at the title... and Dakota's head from Frankie. It's not Sarge's fault he can be clutsy from time to time. How do you think he stumbled into that soda machine and book case in his last match with Frankie?
TheOriginalSE — 12 December 2006 The show was great.. I'm a lurker when it comes to UCW since I don't have time to handle right now...
But I thought the flow of the show was wicked smooth. The matches were fun to read .. I especially like Esteban screaming like a little girl when Dan Ryan yanked the door open .. LOLzer.
Incredible shocker at the end ... I can't wait to see which side is chosen! Linguistic — 12 December 2006 Bro, if all of the confusion in my personal life hadn't caused me to scratch MCW as a regular fed... Shane and I could have really put some effort into a bad ass invasion angle.
That being said, Shane has done an awesome job with this! I really appreciate Jarret coming over to help put this whole thing over. We're making this one hell of an angle!
I like exclamation marks!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Oh, and I love lamp!
