thegr817deuce — 17 October 2006 As much as it pains me to do this, I'm going to ask for someone to help me put the finishing touches on this show. It's been way too long and I just can't find the time to get this last match pounded out. I really thought that things were going to work out this week, but I'm in charge of my department this week at work because my manager is on vacation, which is fine until one of my associates decides he's just gonna stop coming to work without telling anyone.
This leaves me at what is on pace to be 54 hours of work with 12 hours of school sprinkled around that. Therefore, finding time to do writing is going to be non-existant.
Bottom line, I've got one match. If someone can get me one match, I'll have the vignettes ready and we can finally get this show posted. Anyone that's interested, please reply ASAP.
Thanks guys. -Shane Mr_Incredible — 17 October 2006 Dude... I'm here for ya.. Send me what you need and I'll help out whatever I can I'll be online tonight -Mr. I irishred — 18 October 2006 Shane why didn't I get a match assignment for this card? If you need help just ask. You've got my email bro.
