Overall Thoughts Hell_Fighter — 13 September 2006 I will start this out since this is my survey and I will provide my thoughts on the ppv. First off, I huge shout out to Shane and company (all of the match writers) for putting out the card. Yeah it may of been late, but then again its better to be late and be great than be on time and mediocre. You all should be proud.
Anyway onto my thoughts on the card: Match of the Night Hands down, the Elimination chamber match and the crowning of the new UCW World Heavyweight Champion, The First. Everybody should be proud of the great job that they put in and there can still be a lot of things that could transpire from even this match.
Also lots of buildup leading up to the EC match that really made the fans leave home happy.
Segment of the Night Mr Incredible's segment was quite entertaining and very crowd pleasing. It made me cheer in my seat. He had great interaction with JJ (Jerry Jenkins) It was kind of reminessent of an old school interview like Hulk Hogan/Mean Gene type stuff. That's what I thought about it that was really cool about it.
Funniest Moment of the Night After RE-Reading the card over I saw just how FUNNY as hell the BGB segment and taking Cloverleaf to the gay bar, that was some great stuff.
Then of course it was a bit of surprise that BGB was the Midnight Rider, but then again somehow that actually makes sense.
Somehow I can picture him going, "Do you know the rodeo burger? YOU CAN'T HANDLE THE RODEO BURGER!"
Angle of the Night Bryan Storms turning on MCW and joining UCW. Good pops for the UCW and makes UCW look more face. It makes MCW look more genuinely heel. This is a great continuation in the days to come for the UCW/MCW feud.
Granted I kind of was thinking to myself when I saw how Storms was going to be in Team MCW's corner, I was reading thinking to myself "he's gonna turn on us". But I didn't know the degree of how. It was good that they won the match and they turned on MCW afterwards.
The other reason why I enjoyed it is that it really made UCW look more like faces and that MCW more as heels in the days to come. This should be very interesting how this plays out.
Impending/forshadowing future Feuds of the Night Personally the one thing stuck out in my mind and has my creative juices flowing like a bottle of Drain-O is a possible feud with Nakita Dahaka and Bryan Storms.
If this gets the greenlight and does happen, then awesome as I will do whatever I can to make this into a fun feud, but if it doesn't that's okay too. I'm here to have fun and make the most out of any feud that I am in
Another feud that I can see happening is Mr Incredible Verses Bryan Storms in some UCW US Title feud title match feud. That's another one that I think that could be really great.
Shocking Moment of the Night Goes to the way Bryan Storms attacked and turned on Team MCW sided with UCW after the match.
Face of the Night Storms: for both winning the UCW US title and also for siding with UCW and making them look like faces (as they should be)
Honorable mention: Cameron Cruise for taking two consective Humility Bombs from Dan Ryan and kicking out of the first one. That was a good mark out moment for me.
Heel of the Night Irishred, even though he lost the title had some good heel moments I think, second only to Dan Ryan for doing two consecutive HUMILITY BOMBS to Cameron Cruise in order to eliminated him.
MVP? The First earned this one as he has been given the ball and is expected to carry the UCW into the future.
Second runner up: Bryan Storms I think. Overall Thoughts A great card that was definately worth the wait. Everybody involved should be very proud and hopefully Shane and company should have lots to work with going into the coming weeks now the merger of the UCW and MCW is complete and the LVW merger is underway. Great things are coming for the UCW in the coming weeks and months man. This should be fun for everybody.
irishred — 13 September 2006 let me start by saying it was an honor to serve as the UCW Champion for as long as I did. Congrats [name withheld]. Take the ball, run with it and make UCW proud.
Match of the Night Without sounding too egotistical I have to say the Elimination chamber. Some awesome work done in the rp's led to an excellant write up.
Segment of the Night Big Gay Bruce showing up as Midnight Rider wins his match. Who the hell is the Midnight Rider then?
Funniest Moment of the Night Cloverleaf coming to in a Gay bar. Angle of the Night Storms joins UCW. Nice job by all involved. Storms is a serious up and comer. Impending/forshadowing future Feuds of the Night Red/Ryan. Three feds together. They don't like each other...period. This all started from the MBE Cup over a year ago and if I have my way this will be the fued of late 2007 and early 2008 and break the damn internet in half.
Cruise/First. Cam deserves a title shot. Storms/MCW. Actually i see Bryan moving quickly into the title picture too. But first, Who will he recruit to battle MCW?
Shocking Moment of the Night The Storms turn and BGB showing up. Face of the Night Storms: Yup...the turn cements him as a face. Honorable mention: The First for taking the title from a cocky heel champion. Heel of the Night Adam Benjamin takes out Mr. Incredible there has to be a fued between these two. BGB for taking Kenny to a gay bar.
MVP? The First, period. Overall Thoughts The Irishred era has come to an end. God I was SO honored to have the chance to be a champion. Shane and Kenny believed in me and I hope I did a good job for the company. Ya' know what though? I believe in UCW. I know this federation can be awesome under the leadership it has. With tthe influx of talent we have this should be one incredible year. The give and take of ideas sets this fed apart.
