Irishred

thegr817deuce — 13 July 2006 Handler's Name: [name withheld] Email Address: [detail withheld] Any variations of Instant Messengers: [detail withheld] on AIM Wrestler Name: Irishred Height: 5'10" Weight: 235 Hometown: Yankton, South Dakota Face/Heel: Tweener actually Appearance: Shaved head, red goatee, green eyes. Huge chest and a rounded mid section (think Perry Saturn), Huge legs (think Taz). To the ring Irishred wears Doc Marten boots, blue denim cargo shorts, and a MidWest Mafia baseball jersey with the number 13 on the back. Irishred is a thick man to say the least.
Style: Irishred is a brawler with some amatuer wrestling moves thrown in from his days of high school wrestling. All in all Irishred will use every single dirty trick in the ring he can. Go face to face with you and he'll bite the tip of your nose off or kick your knee back into itself. Irishred has learned some submission moves along the way and will use those. Irishred is also a big fan of suplexes. But not as much as he LOVES weapons.
Finishing Move: Mafioso Perfecto-fireman scoop into a fishermans suplex or Payback-Walk around chickenwing submission. I walk into the move instead of around the move as it is done in amatuer wrestling. This basically rips the tendons of the shoulder and elbow. It hurts...trust me.
Move Set (Note any set-up moves at the beginning of the list): The Shank-Knee to the kidney area off of the top rope.
Eye poke Knee to the nuts while up close so the ref can't see. Closed fists to the head. Chair shots. Ring Bell shots. Belly to belly suplex. Gore Throat shots. Biting. Eye gouge. Entrance Music: Bad Company-Bad Company. Entrance Description: As Music starts red and green pinpoint spots crisscross the arena and track Irishred as he walks down the ramp. Green cloverleaf spot on center of the ring as Irishred enters, stands with his head back and closed fists touching each other in front of his chest. When he spreads his arms wide the cloverleaf turns to red. The crowd of course goes crazy at this point.
Titles Held: MBE Extreme championship. History: Irishred is the leader and founder of the Midwest Mafia. He repeatedly competed for and battled against the original MBE.
The MwM was a revolutionary group that shook the foundations of MBE much like the 4 Horsemen had done in the NWA years before.
Irishred was involved in many inovative and original matches such as the "back alley mugging match" against JamesIrish and the "Flaming Fire Escape Match" against Proffessor T. Irishred held the MBE Extreme Belt for a time. Although his win loss record may not show it Irishred never truly lost for he has always gained a measure of revenge and pain upon those who would seek to best him or hold him down.
Just as the Midwest Mafia was gaining a huge second wind with its new members he was incarcerated for 3 years and the MwM was left in the hands of the Red Don. The MwM, like MBE, died. Upon his release from prison; Irishred sought to remedy both of those situations.
As much as he hated the powers that be of MBE Irishred loves the camraderie and competition of MBE. Although Irishred has never been as interested in belts as just fighting and the protection of the MwM and those he calls family he competed in the MBE Cup. There he faced and defeated former champions Cross, Paco and Housefly. He has made it to the finals against internet darling Dan Ryan and MBE wrestler LondonBoy. Despite his appeals MBE was not to reform so he joined A1E to continue his comeback and take his place among the wrestling elite.
Irishred recently reformed the MwM in A1E with Elim and Freakshow to aid in his battle against Anarchy Inc. Approached by the Sensational one Irishred has brought his no nonsense style to UCW to prove his prowess here. Irishred fears no man and does things his own way no matter what.
With Elim already in the fold one has to wonder if the MwM will make an appearance in UCW...only time will tell.
