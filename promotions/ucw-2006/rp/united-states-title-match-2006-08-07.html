thegr817deuce — 7 August 2006 Adam Benjamin (champion) vs. Bryan Storms Mr. Incredible will be the special guest referee for this match! The RP deadline will be Saturday, August 19th at 11:59 PM. Mr_Incredible — 7 August 2006 They say when a man faces his destiny, the destiny ends and he becomes the man that he really is...
(Mr. Incredible stands in front of a mirror tying his tie within his home located on Capitol Hill in our Nation's captial, Washington D.C.)
MR. INCREDIBLE: Those words are too powerful to only have a one dimentional meaning. On one side you have the likes of the one track mind of UCW's very own Mr. Adam Benjamin, who has this crazy theory that he deserves respect since he has in his possession a little trinket called the United States Championship belt.
And on the other side, you have Mr. Storms who believes that Mr. Benjamin is a cry baby fraud, who doesn't deserve the title, so he's come to UCW for guts and glory. Well I wont' argue with you in regards to Benjamin, because you definitely have the guts, however the glory part is still undecided.
(Incredible finishes and slowly pulls his tie tightly so that it's in a neat knot. In the background UCW's Revolution is playing. It is towards the end of Mr. Benjamin's match with Mr. Storms as Benjamin smacks his opponent with the belt)
MR I: (Shakes his head with disgust regarding Benjamin's actions on Revolution) Ironic isn't it. A man that is filled with so much rage, doing anything and everything to get what he wants.. what he believes he deserves.. the respect of being a champion.
And here is where the irony kicks in Mr. Benjamin. Even if you do hold the United States Championship, I'll never call you a champion. Because simply put, belts don't make champions. Attitude, humility, courage are just a few characteristics of a champion. And you have neither of those qualities. Am I not right? Come on Mr. Benjamin, you can say it, I won't be offended.
Because frankly you know I am right, as well as the fans of UCW. They can see it as plain as day.
You can't fool anyone with this whole "I'm a champion, where's my respect" crap campaign you have going on.
Anyone that acts out on rage, goes the cowardly route by attempting to get in a cheap shot in whenever the hell they can, and not owning up to one's own faults, will never be called a champion.
Face the facts Mr. Benjamin, you truly never were a champion to begin with. And after Scars and Stripes, you'll never be a champion ever again. (Mr. Incredible turns off the t.v. and slowly makes his way over to the door) MR I: Now Mr. Storms, don't think that just because I have my eye on Mr. Benjamin here, doesn't take you off my list of people to watch. As far as I'm concerned you're just as dirty as him. Don't think for a second that you'll be able to sneak in any help from your outside friends.
Remember Mr. Storms, this is your last chance at Mr. Benjamin. So don't mess it up, because I wouldn't think twice in disqualifying you.
Believe it. When push comes to shove Mr. Storms, I push harder. Don't test me. Do you realize the consequences of what you did in Syracuse, Benji? Really, truly, inside yourself do you understand the can of worms you just opened?
Before Revolution, this thing between you and I was about honor, about pride, about wrestling, Adam. Did we like each other? Not one bit. But for all your bravado and ego, I thought at least you had some respect in you, if not for me, at least for yourself and that title you hold. I guess I was flat wrong about that, champ.
Because when you hit me with that belt and took the easy way out, you spit on everything you claim to love about this sport. You desecrated that championship and yourself, and you pissed on the legacy you had built as one of the finest competitors in this industry. Hell, as the so-called "face" of Ultimate Championship Wrestling, you desecrated the very company you work for, representing this place as nothing but a breeding ground for cowards and cheaters.
Between you and me, Adam, this little rivalry of ours was indeed about pride and honor, about the legacies of MCW and UCW, about one champion valiantly defending against another. But, because of your actions at Revolution, because of the grave you have dug for yourself, that's all changed.
Bryan takes the MCW title belt off and lays it on the ground next to him. BS: This isn't about promotion versus promotion anymore, Adam. This isn't about the MCW World Championship, or your petty insecurities, or anything that happened at all in Major Championship Wrestling. Now, no matter how cliche it sounds, you've made this matter personal. Because you've stolen from me, Adam Benjamin. you stole my chance to put my stamp on UCW, you dishonored yourself and your championship, and that's something I don't take kindly too.
I've sacrificed too much on my way here to have it taken away by a coward, his crony, and a complete lack of respect for what the title of "champion" means. I didn't let my baser instincts defeat me, and after the way you screwed me over at Revolution, I damn sure won't let you get one over on me anymore.
Because if you indeed have gotten so fat and happy, so arrogant to believe that you can waltz out of any situation by breaking the rules, at Scars and Stripes the New Age Technician will be in for a truly rude awakening when you see everything fall apart.
They say those who live in glass houses shouldn't throw stones, Benji. At Revolution, you started tossing pebbles. You try and pull anything like that again, you disrespect me and try to take the cowardly way out one more time, those pebbles will become boulders and the glass house that is your title reign will certainly shatter.
Because this isn't about MCW and UCW anymore, Adam. This isn't about Jalen Latham, or Ken Cloverleaf, or Christian Sands, or Karl Brown, or the Sergeant, or Mr. Incredible, or anyone but you and me. For that night, I don't give a damn if an invasion succeeds or fails, I just want your head on a silver platter.
Thankfully, that's what I've been given. I've already been screwed with once by your acts of cowardice, Adam. I'd think twice if I were you about pulling any bulls**t stunts like that ever again, and certainly not on me.
Special guest referee or not, it doesn't matter to me. As long as you call it down the middle and make the three count when it's time, Mr. Incredible, you'll have no problems for me.
Because unlike the paper champion I'm facing, this World Champion is no coward, and the only way I want to win this is cleanly, 1-2-3 in the center of that ring. That's not only the real, true, honest way to win, but it's what'll embarass Mr. Benjamin the most.
To be beaten, fair and square, by the better man. A man, based on his actions at Revolution, who obviously scares him, who he perceives as a true threat. Otherwise, he wouldn't have taken the coward's way out.
At Scars and Stripes, Adam, your perceived threat will be one hundred percent real. And I just don't think you can deal with that.

Your reputation, your arrogance, and yes, most certainly, your UCW US Title, they'll all disapper as your life comes crashing down around you. Either way you try for it, clean or dirty, everything you believe about yourself is coming to an end.
Because I'm just the man to do it. You said you spit reality in my face at Warfare, Adam, but at Scars and Stripes, you'll come face to face in reality.
You'll realize that an MCW wrestler, an "inferior" like me, was the man you couldn't put away.
So try to cheat again, Adam. Be a coward one more time. I hope you do, because I will certainly not let you get away with it again.
