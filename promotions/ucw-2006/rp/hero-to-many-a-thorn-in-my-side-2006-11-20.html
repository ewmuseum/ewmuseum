Hero to many, a thorn in my side

20 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Adam_Benjamin — 20 November 2006 (Slowly the camera man approaches the Locker Room of "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin. The door is opened so the camera man enters. It is here we find Adam sitting rocking on a bench clinching the bloody American flag of Mr. Incredible.)
"You want a word from me I take it? Is that why you have walked into my locker room un invited?"
(Adam cringes as he looks downward at the blood on the towel.) "In my career I have had feuds with wrestlers of which I disliked. However, when the match was over, I always walked away leaving no hatred towards that man. For I knew that he felt my hatred throughout that match, and that itself was enough suffering for any man to go through.
But as I sit here, I honestly can say I have a hatred that will not go away. No matter what the out come is at this PPV, I will hate the man that these people consider a hero.
Mr. Incredible, for the past few months you have been a thorn in my side. You are like this punk kid that no matter how hard I beat you up in the play ground, you still will not go away.
You cost me the United States title, and I told you that I would get my revenge. However tonight, you cost me the World Title, and now revenge simply might not be good enough.
I have tasted your blood, I have heard your screams. But at the PPV I will hear your voice say "I quit". But I will not stop, no I will make sure that you are not able to ever come back to this sport.
You took my dream away from me tonight. I know personally I owned the First, regardless of what anyone else thinks. Be it the week of promo's, were I took all his bull **** theories and sent them back at him with a fast ball he could not even make contact with.
Or tonight, when we stepped inside that ring. Were I in total control, before you interjected yourself into my business. You could not wait, you had to steal my spotlight.
Well old man, its time to pull the plug on the life support machine once and for all. I have spit in your face, I have your blood on your flag, and now I am going to take your career.
(Fade to black)
