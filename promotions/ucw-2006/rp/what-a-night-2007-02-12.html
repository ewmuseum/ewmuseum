UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

What a night.

12 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MarcusWestcott — 12 February 2007 Fade into a locker room shortly following the events that transpired at Night Of The Legends II. Who's sitting there? None other than UCW's latest acquisition, Beast, decked out in jeans and a black "I Gored Jalen Latham And All I Got Was This Lousy T-Shirt" shirt. Beast leans back in a black leather chair and smiles as he sees the camera.
Beast: Well, what did you think, UCW? I really didn't know which was more fun - stringing both guys along, or seeing the look on Latham's face as I gored him to the mat, or Cloverleaf's face as he realized I'd be working for him. Or maybe, it was the smell of Latham's pants as I sent him to the mat. I dunno.
But regardless, now, there's no question about it. I AM UCW, and quite frankly, I can't wait to turn this place on its collective ear.
I'm putting everyone on notice. I don't care whether your name is Irishred, Dan Ryan, Sargeant {you're weclome, by the way, Sarge}, or The First. Hell, anyone in that locker room.
Beast is here. And he's about to show you exactly what he can do. Look out, UCW. Fade out.
