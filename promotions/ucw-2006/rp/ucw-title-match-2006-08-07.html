thegr817deuce — 7 August 2006 Irishred (champion) vs. The First vs. Dan Ryan vs. Cameron Cruise vs. Joey Melton vs. Jonathan Marx
This match will be an Elimination Chamber match! The match will start with two men in the ring, while the other four are entrapped within a plexiglass cell. Every five minutes, a new competitor will be unleashed into the fray in random order. Winning the match will consist of being the last man left who has not been pinned or submitted for the duration of the match.
