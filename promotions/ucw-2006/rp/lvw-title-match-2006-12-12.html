UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

LVW Title Match!

12 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 12 December 2006 "Cowboy" Jimmy Donovan (Champion) vs. Irishred vs. Dan Ryan This match will be fought under hardcore rules. The RP deadline will be Friday, December 22nd at 11:59 PM. irishred — 18 December 2006 Irishred gets the biggest ego in the business and a addled cowboy in a match for a worthless belt.
Whoop De freaking Doo. This match is going to be about nothing but pain, revenge, hatred and destruction. Everyone of you have seen the lengths that I will go to to make a point. All of you have seen me violent...but you ain't seen nothing yet. Not one freaking thing.
I'm going to make this the most violent match in Wrestling history just to prove once and for all that there isn't a single person on this roster that can stand toe to toe with me. I'm going to baptize Jimmy and Dan in their own blood just to prove a point. I'm going to make the little old ladies watching at home throw up when they see the skin peeled from Dan Ryan's face and the bones breaking through Jimmy's skin; all just to prove a point.
I'm going to take that little LVW belt lay it down in the middle of the ring and paint FUCW across the gold. That's right; the **** UCW belt.
I told you all from the start that this federation is mine. I warned all of you to take me seriously. I told you all that there would be repercussions if I was not taken seriously. This is the time. The continuation of the most dominant run in any federation takes another step towards legendary status as I destroy two more wrestlers in my federation.
I can't be stopped. I won't be stopped. I am UCW and UCW is mine.
