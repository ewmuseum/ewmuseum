UCW Title Match!

25 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 25 October 2006 The First (Champion) vs. Adam Benjamin The RP deadline will be Friday, November 3rd at 11:59 PM. Adam_Benjamin — 25 October 2006 (Fade into the Locker Room of "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin were he is seen sitting holding a bloody towel in his hand his face is covered in blood. His hand raises the towel up as he looks into the camera.)
"I said it to your face, and I will say it again. This towel I'm holding is proof of the blood I've made you've spilled. It's very stains upon it that I carry with me is a representation of what you've created, of the monster you've unleashed.
Now take a look at my face. This is my own personal blood. Caused by a chair shot, however exposed more by several head butts to a tapped out victim of Yours Truly.
So with this towel I combine my blood with yours blood. (Adam clears his face with the already blood stained towel. A new fresh coat of blood is seen on the towel as Adam holds it towards the camera. His face now smeared of blood.)
"We are now blood brothers Mr. Incredible. I will give you credit you made me tap out. You managed to attack me from behind and tap me out. Now I am begging you to do it again. However this time I will be standing in front of you, not with my back turned.
And it will be then that I will leave you laid out for either ten seconds or tapping out as I choke the life from your American hero body.
Until then watch your back.. (Adam gives a sadistic smile towards the camera, then quickly glues his eyes deep into the camera)
"Now lets get down to the matter at hand. Moments ago I received a contracted, one of which I have been waiting for since I debuted here in UCW.
When I first entered I wrote a letter signed "Yours Truly". This letter was addressed to a man named First. At the time he was the first ever UCW world champion. On this letter I stated that I was the New face of UCW.
Flash to the present day. The First is now settling into his second rein as our champion, and I am still the new face of UCW.
You see First I have waiting for a long time to walking to the ring with "the best". The title you hold states you are the best, however I just can't seem to come to grips with that notion.
Now this week you and I are going to step into the ring and find out who desires to be great, and who simply is great.
This federation is in desperate need of a savior. It has other federations running around in it. It has some American hero trying to ruin my life.
And most of all it has you as it's champion. (Adam smiles) "So here on the twenty-fifth day of October I stand before this company as it one and only Savior.
From this day forward I am going Save this company from the trash that has leached onto it.
I am going to bring the world title to royalty. I am going to take the world title and give it meaning.
So from now on I will be known the world around as "The new face of UCW, as well as it's Savior" "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin.
(Adam gives a insane look deep into the camera) "And First let me be the, well first to warn you. I am in a very pissed off mood. And I am aware that you are going to come back with several witty comments.
So let me save you the trouble champ. I have not lost it! Mr. I has not turned me into a psycho. The blood lose today has not turned me into a biblical figure. And last but not least save me some boorish "you are not ready to be champion lines" I have been the the top of the mountain. This is not my first rodeo. This week I am going to step into the ring with you and ground and pound you deep into the Mat.
And in doing so I will become the new UCW Champion. Then Live on PPV I will defend my title in the match that matters.
Then you and Cruise can have your little match. Take a look at me and journy into the mind of a maniac. And realise win, lose, or draw, you will suffer! Because there is no limit to what I will do to become the Champion of this federation..... No limit what so ever!!!!
(Fade to black) The Great Eye — 25 October 2006 (FADEIN: The First, wearing his Mad Hatter jacket and black karate pants. He has on coke bottle sunglasses and the UCW World Title over his shoulder. His hair is a crazy rainbow of colors.)
THE FIRST: "Oh Adam, how dramatic the blood merge and all that...I mean I am truly inspired...But really, what is blood? Just a fluid of the body...Just one of the means by which we're kept trapped in these rotting meat prison...
I think if you truly wanted to bind yourself to Mr. Incredible, I'd have gone about the process of tethering your soul to his...Oh to be soulbound...Now that is truly doing something special...That means it's forever...Or until one of you gives up and surrenders the conflict...I was soulbound but once...That was not in hate, but rather in love...It ended after a scant 300 years...Kids now-a-days, no sense of commitment...(sighs)
I think you could hold this title with honor Mr. Benjamin...I really do...I don't know why you wouldn't...But you try to cut me off from bringing this up...This seems to be a sign that you...Are lacking somehow...That you must hope that I don't degrade you...(Shakes head) Oh no...This is the path of defeat...To enter the arena merely hoping to prove your worth is the surest way to end up with your guts spilled on the sands of Rome...
Another great way to end up going beyond the veil is to worry about things other then the matter at hand...Talking about others, lamenting your loss...All of that might be acceptable when fighting for lesser stakes, against lesser opponents...But I...(Smiles)...I'm the champ...Which makes me your better...No...Your King...No your Pharaoh...Both King and God...(Shakes head)...Wow, I gotta lay off the mushrooms...Was getting a little out of synch with the time stream...I do apologize.
(Composes himself) What we will be Mr. Benjamin is warriors, fighting for pride and honor. For leather and gold...Me to begin my second reign in style...You seeking to prove yourself to the world...It shall be a great battle, of this I am sure...I am sure the fates will render a fair judgement upon both our souls as to what the result will be...I hope you are ready for it Mr. Benjamin, for it will be a great night indeed...
Adam_Benjamin — 26 October 2006 The clock on the wall reads high noon. As the sounds of the mat crashing are suddenly heard. Seen standing in the ring inside a training room in "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin.)
"Welcome fans of UCW, once again I enlighten you with my appearance. For today the new face of UCW, and it's Savior is going to talk about what it means to become a champion in this sport.
Through my years of wrestling I have sat back and watch wrestlers after wrestler stand in the challenger spot and talk about what they feel about being a champion."
(Slowly the camera zooms up close the Adam, a bandage is seen on his forehead.) "They all come from different backgrounds with several different theories of greatness. Each will spend segment after segment trying to convince both themselves and you the hard paying fans that they are capable of becoming the elite wrestling in this federation.
Some will come out and say they are "High Tower of Power" and they can not be stopped. Now most guys with flashy names are usually trying to cover up there lack of skills with a name. And in the end give the champ on that night a great challenge but fall to there ego and blow there chances of glory.
Then you have the "I am going to kill you" type of interviews. This guy tends to disregard anything the current champion has done, or possibly can do against him. Inside this man's head no one can even lace his boots.
Well these guys usually blow there big match faster than a whore in a alley way. (Adam rolls his neck in the ring as he talks) Then you have the guy who has been waiting for this first shot his entire career. He will give the match of his life, but in the end the champ will find a way to defeat him.
(Adam climbs up on the top rope and takes a seat.) "Then of course you have Yours Truly. Now I am a man who has held world titles, dined with kings and queens, and wrestled all over the world.
For every name I call myself it has justification. Take the savior moniker. In no way am I trying to be a lord or Christ himself.
No what I am doing is saving the word of wrestling from the very trash that has leached on to it like mold onto a rock.
I save it with outstanding class, pure wrestling the way it was meant to be seen, and I save it with most of all honor for my fellow man
(Adam gives a sinister look towards the camera) And I for one look at our current champion as a outstanding wrestler. He is two time champion, and I can not take anything away from what he has done thus far in his career.
(Adam claps his hands) So then that leaves a question. What outcome will Yours Truly have this week when he steps inside the ring for a chance to become the absolute best the UCW has to offer.
Well it is simple, I will continue to bring the same intensity and ability I bring week in and week out. I will leave all that I have inside that ring and give the First his biggest test to date.
And in doing so, if he can so how withstand my challenge, then so be it! For that will only showcase the very fabric in which this company has to offer these fans. Two outstanding wrestlers dueling out for the richest prize in the game.
(Adam looks deep into the camera) "However, know this First, my attention this week is all yours. I am man that has hatred and anger built deep inside. The towel I carry, covered in blood of two men now symbolizes the evil within a man.
For it is this blood (Adam hold the towel up) that but one drop of has the power to win. All the world forgiveness of its world of sin!
My mind is set on the goal at hand. I know that this might be my only shot at the one thing I desire most. And by god First prepare yourself for a night of pure wrestling.
For I know am fueled with intensity that I never have had. Thank Mr. I for that. This week I seek not to prove my worth than any man.
I only seek to become the champion... (fade to black) The Great Eye — 27 October 2006 (FADEIN: The First sitting in a graveyard in the daytime. His face is pale white from greasepaint. He has a racoon stripe across his eyes and his lips are painted black. His hair is a mess of colors scattered around his face. His Mad Hatter jacket covers up the UCW World Title belt a bit.)
THE FIRST: "You seek to be Champion...But do you know what that is? What is attention what is fame, what is the other side of the wall? You ever think of that? You go out to a show and out comes some band, like Nine Inch Nails or whoever and they get on that stage and sing...But they are really on the other side of a wall, you can see them, maybe even touch them, but truly, you live on two different worlds...Your interaction is fleeting at most...
We live on the other side of that wall when we are in the ring battling, but you and I, even when we engage in a battle, are seperated by who we are, what we've done...I am the young man who has achived, you are the young man seeking to achive...It is that simple...We are different, not just because of our appeance or attitudes...But our lives, our souls make is so...
Do you really seek this? Do you really seek fighting an opponent so desiring of this title he'd hit you with a chair while you're defenseless until he sent you to the hospital? That when you woke up, days had past? Is that truly what you want? To have the whole world gunning for you? This is a burden, but it one I carry because I must...It is the part I must play in the world...The hero to the misunderstood, the different, the outcast...They need a hero, they need a voice...I am that voice...I am their champion...
I respect you Mr. Benjamin, I truly do, you seem like an honorable soul...Rozy loves your aura...You seem like you're on the right path...Except for the fact that the path has led you to me in a situation where I must fight you, must resist you, must leave the ring champion, must cost you the chance to be the champion...I deny you your goal...
Perhaps it is best I do this, perhaps you are not truly ready for this...It would be good for you not to be champion to soon, not to burn out...I'd hate to be the bossy father with you...But given the fact that I've played the part for the better part of humanity's existance, except for those times when I was a woman...I can't help but want to do that...So no, you can't have the car tonight, and no you can't be champion...
So well Mr. Benjamin...I do hope we have a grand fight, an epic battle, that we take the fans on a journey they will talk about for years to come...
But know that you really don't know what this title it...It looks like glory and honor...It is truly...The cross I bear...
But I bear it gladly... (FADEOUT) Adam_Benjamin — 28 October 2006 We fade into a message written on a scroll. Behind ever closed door lies a fear, a reality that one must meet before he dies. And upon this meeting it is there that one realizes his fate. Soon your door will open and it is there that you will meet me...
Yours Truly The New Face of UCW (We now fade into "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin standing in front of a UCW back drop prepared to conduct a interview.)
"The message you just seen is the very message in which I delivered to our champion. The moment that message talks about is this week.
"Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin challenging the First for his world championship. This week I am standing at the door way. I am slowly knocking, and without knowing it the First is going to open the door. And like I have done before in my career, I am going to take my place inside that room as its new champion."
(Adam nods his head in approval.) "First you asked if I truly know what being a champion is all about? The Answer to that quesion is, YES!
I know everything that comes with being a champion. I know all about the extra interviews, the constant fund raisers, the feeling of the press at your every move.
And I also know the feeling of being the hunted. Knowing each day that a locker room of superstars do indeed wish they were me, and in wishing would do anything possible to turn that wish into a reality.
I have been a world champion, I know what it is like to achieve. But now my attention, and my goal is to achieve world championship status at your expense.
For as a competing member of this roster I too desire to hold the belt you have around your waist. But unlike most of the comon stars here I do not dream, for dreams sometimes never come true.
And all you need to do is look up my track record and see when I desire something, I make sure that I achieve my desire."
(Adam looks deep into the camera) "This week unlike fictional TV it seems the Hero as you have type casted yourself must fall to the hands of the evil bad guy.
You claim you must resist me this week. But honestly can you? Can you be at the top of your game enough to fend off all that I bring inside the ring this week.
I have nothing to lose, but everything to gain champ! When it counts I am going to ground and pound you into the mat and force you to look deep inside your soul and question is it really worth all this pain?
And before you answer yes, understand that I will not stop until that bell rings. I care nothing about your well being, any family, or any wife or kids.
And as the end nears you will become with one with your sences. You will slowly learn the reality of each of them one by one.
It will start with your hearing. The sound of the bell ringing ending you title run. That sound will echo within your head. Rattling, ratttling, there will be no escape from its horring volume.
Then the vision will attack you. As the sight of me standing holding your glory high above my head. Like a piercing arrow striking a warrior in battle, you will be stuck on the sight me me hovering over you.
All the feelings of greatness soon will turn numb. The touch, the sensation, the warmth of that title belt gone.
Then so will go the very smell of the leather that once dawned your body. At last but not least all that has just happened will leave you speachless. Motionless you will look for questions that have no answers.
Prepare yourself, because win, lose, or draw this will be the toughest match of your career.
The fans will in fact get there money's worth this week. And UCW will for certain be the talk of the wrestling world.
