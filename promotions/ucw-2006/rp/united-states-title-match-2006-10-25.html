UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

United States Title Match!

25 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 25 October 2006 Bryan Storms (Champion) vs. Dakota Smith The RP deadline will be Friday, November 3rd at 11:59 PM. JC — 29 October 2006 (Chants of UCW and MCW echo throughout the studio as the camera starts to focus on none other than Rico Suave as another segment of his show, the Rico Suave show is about to start, it is considered by many as one of the most controversial shows around Rico appears to be disgusted about something as he looks at his watch and shakes his head)
RICO: Well as always folks, my guest of honor is late yet again But no matter, itll give me some time to give you some insight into a few things that I know you are unaware of But thats no surprise considering your lack of intellect and common sense, guess we all cant be intellectuals now cant we!? (Laughs) MCW UCW The only thing that separates the two is one letter One nearing its downfall, the other!? Lets just say theyre about to journey into purgatory and they dont even have a freaking clue (Distorts his face) What am I talking about!? Im talking about Dakota, you dumb ass!!! Who else would I be talking about!!! Did you think I was going to say, the Sarge!? Or how about Bryan Storms!? (Laughs and shakes his head) Damn, you people really are that dumb Thats why you work for a living and people like me and Dakota entertain for a living Thats right, no matter how you slice it, it will always be entertainment I entertain you with my wits and charisma, and Dakota entertains you with his brutality, his cunning, his total dominance Dont deny it; you all love the violence he brings to the ring Hes the man that most will come to hate, not that he gives a rats ass about it You will still come to see him; youll all pay to see him play And as you can see he plays for keeps!!! Hes what 
(His phone rings, he ignores it and is about to continue, but is interrupted by a crew member who whispers in his ear)
RICO: Its about time!!! Folks Ive just been informed that Mr. Dakota has finally arrived and is at this very moment making his way here And there he is folks!!! DAKOTA!!!
(As Dakota approaches, the majority of the audience boo, he looks in their direction and sneers; he goes as far as flipping them off As he sits, Rico extends his hand, Dakota just looks at it and then at Rico as he slaps his hand away)
DAKOTA: So Rico, what BS have you been telling these dumb ignorant peons!? (Audience boos, he smirks and Rico just stares dumbfounded) Save those boos for when I take Bryan apart and walk away leaving him staring into the rafters writhing in pain and disappointment Regardless of what Bryan and these peons think, I am taking him out
RICO: Now, now Dakota lets not jump the gun here Bryan is the UCW US champ and he was up until a few days ago the MCW Heavyweight champion The man knows a thing or two, about a thing or two and lets not forget he helped you beat the Sarge He beat McMillan to win the MCW heavyweight title and believe me when I tell you; Chris McMillan is one tough hombre He then comes over to the UCW and captures the UCW US title He just beat Rob Franklin; the man is on a roll for Gods sake You havent really done much and Im not taking anything away from you Dakota, but hey man, youre my main man and I feel close enough to let you know when youre slipping You got it all man, but youre letting something cloud your mind I think I know what or better yet WHO it is that has been occupying your thoughts, distracting you from your true self You need to wake up Dakota, remember who you are and stay your course, no distractions You can beat Storms, I know you can but you have to concentrate Dakota, stay focused
(Dakota just stares at him with a cynical grin, his eyes darting out at Rico Before Rico can continue Dakota cuts him off)
DAKOTA: Yea I can see your point, and if I were just as dumb as your audience here I would believe all that crap you just spewed out Bryan, I hope youre out there watching this crap, look at me Bryan I want you to understand something; I dont want you to take this ass whipping personal Im not taking anything away from you either, Ive watched your matches over and over again and I can honestly say that I know you like a well read book So you beat Chris and became the MCW champ, well at least for a while anyway, but not to worry, youre the UCW US champ and you just beat Robby So what!!! Who have you really beaten Bryan!? Nobodies, Bryan, thats who youve beaten, NOBODIES!!! But now youre going to face me and you and I have an issue to settle, you made a big mistake crossing my path and interfering in my last match didnt help your dilemma any I want you to know that Im not underestimating you Bryan, Im just that damn sure of myself
RICO: Dakota, if I may interject, didnt Nikita interfere in that match also, in fact she came down with you I dont hear you *****ing about her interfering in your match; In fact I havent heard much on that connection But Dakota listen to me please, you have to concentrate on Storms, he knows what to expect more or less; hes not going to take you lightly Hes coming down with a mission He has a lot more to lose than you do; you know hes coming down to try to take you out I know, I know, just dont stray off, not now Dakota; you are almost there, you are almost at the top of the mountain, dont look down, and dont look back
DAKOTA: You neednt concern yourself with Nikita Its none of your damn business anyway Bryan after all the BS is said and done, itll all come down to just you and me in that ring Dont get your hopes up too high Bryan, all I want you to do, is to be focused and prepared to take me on I want to beat you at your best, I want you to be at the top of your game; beating your ass will mean more to me than taking your belt And YOU!!! (Glares at Rico who nervously looks back) You little piece of crap, take your words of wisdom and stick them up your ass!!!
(Without saying another word, he gets up and walks off Rico is about to say something but thinks again and lets him walk off He waits until Dakota is gone before he begins to again speak)
RICO: I am beside myself folks, here I tried to give Dakota some good sound advice and he almost went ballistic; talk about having a short fuse (His eyes narrow as a malicious grin crosses his face) Or was I just pulling your chain while lighting a fire under Dakotas ass!? Guess youll never know, now will you Bryan One thing is certain, Dakota is rude, arrogant and has no respect whatsoever for anyone Goes by his own rules, and those he makes up as he goes along Hes young Bryan, but dont let that thought linger too much Hes that acorn that didnt fall far from the tree, the spitting image of he who is his father, just as nasty, just as brutal and just as mean And I think it was a big mistake, you coming in and clocking the Sarge, the way you did but hey, thats just my opinion and theyre like assholes, we all have one (Shrugs his shoulders and smiles) and such is life Live and learn I always say Maybe you have a master plan, maybe not, who the hell knows Im sure youre not going to tell; but anyway I want to wish you good luck, not that youre going to need it, youre the big bad wolf of NO!!! Wait a minute!!! Thats Chris moniker Youre Bryan Storms, hmm, no its not FAME ON, thats Johnny Storms, damn I seem to have forgotten what your moniker is And in a short time, so will everyone else Good night dude, and to my lovely audience I say, the pleasure has been all yours!!!
(Audience again starts to chant UCW and MCW Rico laughs and walks away as the camera fades to block)
