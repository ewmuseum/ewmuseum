UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

Adam Benjamin vs. Chris McMillan

15 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 15 September 2006 Singles Match. The RP deadline will be Saturday, September 23rd at 11:59 PM. Adam_Benjamin — 20 September 2006 (Fade into "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin sitting in front of a UCW back drop. in his hand is a bloody towel.)
"You are looking at the new face of UCW. For the last few months I represented this company and all of it's fans, as I proudly defended the United States of Whatever Championship.
throughout my rein, and by god till its bloody conclusion one parasite continuously stuck his noise into my business."
(Adam looks in anger.) "It was his medaling that caused me to loss that very title last week live on PPV. Now personally I know that is not the last title I will hold in this great company. No soon enough the World title will be in my sights.
That will come in time, but right now all I want is redemption. I want a man to stand up and face me one on one, and until he does that I am going to make things very interesting for both him and the opponents I face.
(Adam smiles) "That starts this week against Chris McMillian. Chris I am sorry that your name is signed to face me. Personally I liked you up until I read it was you I was facing.
And what happens to you this week can be directly linked to a old man who will not come out of retirement and fight me.
The old Yours Truly would tell you that he is going to out class you, out smart you, and most of all out wrestle you this week.
Sorry to say all I am going to do this week is beat you down any way possible to end your life for three seconds.
I am hold in my hand a towel that is covered with the blood of a man who is trying to play games with me.
This week I am going to ground and pound your face into the mat and possible taste you blood as well.
I said it moments after my dignity was cowardly taken away from me, and I will repeat my message so everyone can hear it loud and clear.
The UCW is about to become on of the most violent federations in all of professional wrestling.
Hatred has lead me to the land of horor, and by god I am ready to unleash the ultra violence on all that choose to stand in my way.
I hope god prays for your safety, For Yours Truly could give a sh*t. (Fade to black)
