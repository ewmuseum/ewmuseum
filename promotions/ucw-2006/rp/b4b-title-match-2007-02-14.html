UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

B4B Title Match!

14 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 14 February 2007 Irishred (Champion) vs. Cameron Cruise The winner is the first man who can make their opponent bleed. The RP deadline will be Saturday, February 24th at 11:59 PM. irishred — 15 February 2007 Irishred sits in the locker room holding the former LVW World Heavyweight title. Three men's blood stain the belt brown. Irishred looks into the camera and picks up a can of red spray paint. Red sits the title belt on the ground and shakes the can of paint. He holds the can over the belt and spray paints "B4B" on the face of the belt. He blacks out the LVW writing and on the nameplate he paints Irishred. Irishred tosses the can over his shoulder and begins to talk.
Cameron Mother******* Cruise. How you doing loser? You don't mind if I call you that do you Cam? I mean I believe in speaking the truth and you, simply put are a loser. You got your ass handed to you by the First last week. You know...the First, the man I hospitalized and chased out of UCW. The man who is running around with my other belt. You know the guy I'm talking about right? Hell you weren't really in the ring that long so if you have forgotten it's no big deal loser.
I've seen wrestlers take longer to get to the ring then it took you to get your ass whipped Cammy. That was truly a pitiful display.
I don't know if you watched the rest of the card or not loser; but that night I won not only a match but yet another belt. I beat the hell out of two men taking the LVW title from one of them, beating them both bloody and starting a revolution here in UCW.
I don't even know if you will bother to show up this week Cammy. I don't know if I could if I was a loser like you. How do you even face yourself Cruise? For God's sake you are a disgrace to this federation and that's saying something. You're a disgrace to the whole damn profession.
You'd be nothing in this business all together if it wasn't for Melton. I don't know how you conned Cloverleaf into inviting you into this federation in the first place. But you fit in with most of the other losers that Cloverleaf has let into this freak show.
That crap is going to stop, I guarantee that. You see I'm going to start to separate the wheat from the chaff, and I'm starting with you.
You want to climb back up that ladder? You want to earn a shot to play in the main event again? Make me bleed. That's all you have to do. The stips are easy, make me bleed take my belt and prove to the whole damn world that you're not the loser I say you are.
I don't think you can do it Cruise. If fact I know you can't. This week I prove to the world what I've known all along, Cameron Cruise is a sham.
Irishred picks up his Blood for Blood title and walks off camera. TSiegel — 20 February 2007 "Has he shut up yet?" (Fadein, Cameron Cruise back home, on the back porch of his house in Jacksonville, NC. Cruise, stretched out on a couch dressed in blue jeans and a blue-hooded pullover, has a cellular phone to his ear, his face buried in the couch.)
CRUISE (to the person he's talking to on the phone): He did?? Four days ago?? Good Grief, yeah, I suppose....yeah...well you know how his promos sound nothing like non-stop *****ING sessions....yeah....see ya in couple hours.....later.
(CRUISE swings himself around on the couch from his 'nap', into a sitting position, the hood over his head hiding all but the sleeping-bags under his eyes.)
CRUISE: You ****ers from YOU-SEE-DUB, aren'tcha?? I figured you'd be around eventually. Lemme guess....you're here for the response I have for Cloverleaf signing me up against 'Red too right??
(The Cameraman 'nods' at Cruise.) Fine. Get aload of this. IRISH ****ING RED. Ya know...maybe you heard me making afew comments on the phone a couple minutes ago, maybe ya didn't...but the fact of the matter is that whom they WERE for wasn't YOU.
But you might as well as press the 'REWIND' button on your remote control to see if you got it or not, 'cause I quite frankly don't feel like going over it a second time with a DIFFERENT person.
However. You sir, have grown on me to be what merely agitates me the more I think about it: An Ignorant ****.
You ***** about not getting what other people DO deserve, and then when you're thrown a 'bone' from time to time, ***** because it isn't the biggest piece.
Here's a hint 'Red: 'neither myself nor anyone give a ****. Sure, I'll wager that I've probably had to wait longer periods of time for Melton to get out the bathroom AFTER he's had foods with a ton of laxative in it, than I had in the ring against The First a couple weeks ago for the title.
But then again....I didn't have a horribly bad night to sleep it off. After all....I wouldn't be facing YOU for a title that EMBRACES the very nature of what you do for a living: Fight.
You break or fracture bones, ligaments, and make opponents bleed Crimson. But to call me a 'loser' and 'disgrace to the Profession'?? Son, do you even KNOW how to apply a Wrestling Hold?? Because that's what I do for a living, 'Red; I WRESTLE, and in some instances....do it WELL.
But thanks to you and your 'opportunity' and to Cloverleaf and 'his'...I don't get to very much this week, and believe me 'Red when I say this.
I don't [I]INTEND to. In fact....once I make you bleed 'Red....the way I feel about this match...and the way here-on-in general....
(Cruise takes the hood off his head, revealing a Cameron Cruise with 'bloodshot' eyes, and a look of COMPLETE FOCUS.)
I just may never STOP. That...ignorant....is a REALITY CHECK...that not only you won't like....but I promise you 'Red....that you just might downright HATE IT.
(Fadeout.) irishred — 20 February 2007 Wait a minute Cammy...when did you go through puberty? See I noticed it right away when your voice was actually of a manly octave. For the first time since you began to pollute my Television screen you didn't sound like a member of the Vienna boy's choir. Good for you.
Now that the congrats are out of the way let's get to the meat of it shall we? You are going to make ME bleed. That's what you are implying correct? I mean after all the swearing, whining, complaining and posturing I think I got the gist of it. You're going to make me bleed.
I've gone on record as stating that I'm a bleeder. In fact I think I was so bold as to state that at this point in my career a pillow fight may make me bleed. Well Cammy baby, you don't hit as hard as a pillow, so I've got no worrying to do here. Although you may have finally put a little bass in your voice and started to sound like you have a set, the sad fact remains that you are not only a loser, you are a pretty boy pansy.
Boy, you wouldn't last one night with my sister without crying uncle let alone have a chance in hell of beating my ass. As the old saying goes, I beat up badder men then you on the way to a fight. Oh yes...I pulled out the cliche boy. That's how confident I am that I will beat you, I've gone straight to Clicheville without passing GO and without collecting one hundred dollars.
Do us all a favor Cammy, make sure you are completely awake the next time you open your moneymaker and start rambling. Maybe then you'll actually start to make some sense; cuz' the crap spilling out of your mouth right now is fiction that wouldn't fly in comic book form let alone real life.
So you just bring your happy ass to the ring all full of piss and vinegar and I'll give you a little lesson in what the fight is really all about. UCW is me and I am UCW and that is a reality check you will learn this week.
