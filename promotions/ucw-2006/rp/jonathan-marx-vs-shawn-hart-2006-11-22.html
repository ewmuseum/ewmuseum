UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

Jonathan Marx vs. Shawn Hart

22 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 22 November 2006 Singles Match. The RP deadline will be Saturday, December 2nd at 11:59 PM EST. PaulNJ21 — 3 December 2006 ::Marx is standing at the taylor’s shop, decked out in an new black Armani suit with the NEW World Heavyweight Title around his waist::
JONATHAN MARX: When I first started in wrestling, I had two goals. One was to win the World Heavyweight Title and lead an old school revolution. The second was to face off against the all time greats of their sport and make my mark in the annuals of history. Shawn Hart, as a ten time world heavyweight champion and the greatest WFW World Heavyweight Champion ever, it has been a dream of mine to match my skills directly against yours to prove to the world who is the better man.
You may have a streak of gray in your hair, you may be a bit slower these days with the El Codo Explosivo… but when you want to go, you can still hang with the best in this sport which you’ll have to do and then some to beat me.
Don’t be cocky about what happened the last time we met when you left me in a pool of my own blood because Pearl Harbor me by jumping me on the way to the ring to compete for the UCW World Heavyweight Title and putting me out of action for two shows…. This time, I know you are coming and I’m going be ready to extract revenge for putting me out of action and keeping me from completing the Triple Crown by holding the NEW, NFW, and UCW World Heavyweight Titles all at once.
I may be a wrestler, but after you spilled my blood, I will have no trouble spilling yours even if it means ruining this good Armani suit to do so. An eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth… this will make your two out of three fall epic with Doc Silver look like a cake walk and outcome will be the same, the legend in the making will come out over the older unbeatable legend on top.
Be prepared for the old school revolution, for the leader of the Golden Age has emerged and his name is Jonathan Marx.
FTB ShawnHartXXX — 3 December 2006 Sorry Paul... I've been looking forward to this match for weeks, but I was out of town for 4 days over Thanksgiving and dealing with Finals since I've been back I apologize for not posting and hope we get the chance at this again sometime.
