UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

The First & Benjamin vs. Cruise & Irishred

11 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 11 March 2007 Tag Match. The RP deadline will be Wednesday, March 21st at 11:59 PM EST. irishred — 19 March 2007 The only true UCW champion...the only man to completely dominate every single wrestler on the UCW roster, the man who puts asses in the seats, the man who put UCW on the map; Irishred walks onto the screen in front of a UCW backdrop. He has the blood stained B4B title draped across his shoulder. He wears his newest UCW T-Shirt that has a picture of Irishred with a crown of thorns around his head, on the back it says "Will you bleed for your sins?" Irishred adjusts the title and stares into the camera, or at least we assume he is as he is wearing a pair of mirrored sunglasses.
Is there a wrestler in this company that I haven't completely owned? Is there a man, woman or Beast that dares to stand in my way? Is there anyone worthy of actually stepping into the ring with me?
What do I have left to prove in this federation? I owned the World Title for the better part of a year. I took the LVW Championship and made it my own. I damn near crippled the man posing as the World Champion. I have embarrassed each and every person that dared to enter my UCW ring with me...what else do I have to prove?
Seriously...someone tell me. This week for some unknown reason Mr. Cloverleaf has chosen to put his number one commodity in a tag team match with the completely worthless Cameron Cruise as my partner. I understand why he did this. Cruise needs the rub and really looking across the ring at what I am facing it's not like I'll need a partner to win this match anyway.
Benjamin and the First will do most of my work for me. They don't trust each other, they don't like each other but they are forced to not only be partners but to stand across the ring from the most dangerous man in professional wrestling.
I've taken blood from the First, I've taken blood from Cruise...that leaves you as the latest sacrifice Benjamin. This will be your baptism in blood. This will be your time in the sun.
I don't care who wins this match. I don't care what happens in this match so long as I get to add your blood to this lovely mix. Irishred caresses his B4B belt as he laughs softly.
From the very beginning of this federation I stated that UCW would be mine. I have yet to be proven wrong. Nothing that happens in this match will change that fact. I am UCW and UCW is Irishred.
Irishred turns away from the camera. The last image we see is the words on the back of his T-Shirt.
Adam_Benjamin — 21 March 2007 (A UCW logo is seen as Adam Benjamin emerges from left of the camera) "First, its funny for a guy who thinks he has my number, you are the one that is seemingly stalking me. I mean costing me matches? If you are trying to play mind games, you are truly barking up the wrong tree.
Seriously you are simply pissing me off. And hey I like it. Keep it up, because soon enough I am going to have you in the ring and take your title.
This week we are set to team up, but know this, I am entering that ring to win this week. You will not get attacked, nor will I look for revenge, that will happen when I face you one on one.
Now let talk about Irish Red. Look into the camera, My Name Is "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin. I have defeated you in the past, and this week I am going to shut you up in the middle of the ring. Seriously I am not here today to give a long speech, I just want you to understand that you are a good wrestler, but you are in fact beatable.
As for Cruise? Well I guess it all depends on which Cruise shows up. It is the die hard true champion, or the mailman mailing it in. The truth is four of this company's best are going to give the fans the match they paid to see.
This company is the future of professional wrestling. I here about other feds, who cares. This is The UCW, and this is were the action is. Be a part of pure wrestling, and watch this show this week.
Cruise, Red, prepare to get out shinned, out classed, and most importantly out wrestled. (Fade to black) The Great Eye — 21 March 2007 (FADEIN: The First sitting in a graveyard, his face covered by his hair, which is dyed black.)
THE FIRST: "Still yours is it now Mr. Irish? I didn't know this...It's not my company either...It exists beyond us...We work to further it's glory...We are but parts of the machine, it is all anyone can ever be...No man is an island, no man is a company, or a country, or anything more then just being himself...We are...Ourselves Irish...That is the end...Anything else is madness...
I know it sounds like the height of hypocrasy for me, the man of a million lives, to speak in such a way...But it's the truth...My soul is me...It's all I have, it's all we take when this frail mortal coil falls away...
Oh they may speak of you as a legend of this company one day...One day you may enter the UCW Hall of Fame and have your legacy told to those who didn't know of it, and their eyes may widen and their jaws slacken in the understanding of your greatness, but no, you will still not be this company...
Of course now I'm supposed to speak about how I shall beat the madness of your claims out of you, but this is impossible, deep faith in something, no matter how misguided, is something nearly impossible to break...So I shall not try...I shall just do what I always do, what all warriors must do, and that is to prove myself the better man in battle...
We meet again Mr. Irish, not for the first time, nor for the last... Good day to you Sir. (FADEOUT) TSiegel — 21 March 2007 (Fadein on Cameron Cruise, dressed in blue jeans and a light Blue-hooded UCW pullover as Cruise slumps down on a couch in front of the camera. His eyes are still bloodshot, the bags under his eyes dark from the lack of sleep.)
CRUISE: You know 'Red, it's interesting how you paint yourself as the image of what UCW is and what it represents when really....
You're nothing short of the opposite. You'd prove well enough with those words in a debate about which is better...Chicken fights or Bum fights...it really doesn't matter because they're both illegal and irrelevant.
Congratulations 'Red...you succeeded in not only defeating me last week...but proving my point.
It's like you're a "Double-Threat", in that respect. (Takes a sarcastic deep breath.) So EXHILERATING..... 'Course then there's Adam Benjamin. Do you even know who you are anymore?? You're in a match with a man as your partner that just so happens to be the World Heavyweight Champion...and you think he's stalking you in anything else not related to this match??
(Cruise scratches his head for a second.) Gee....you know Adam this isn't just common sense knockin'...but I'd quit the B*TCHING and DO SOMETHING ABOUT IT.
Then again...what do I know Adam...you think that I'm just someone who MAILS IT IN. Embarrassment?? Delusion?? Only The First and Irishred crave these things... I've come to UCW and am coming to Revolution to do one thing and one thing only; to make an IMPACT and it's by way of what I do best.
That...is a REALITY CHECK that all three of you just...won't like. (Fadeout as Cruise gets up from the couch and exits.) irishred — 22 March 2007 Look the gangs all here. Are we really going to rehash everything that lies between all of us?
Let's look at the facts here plain and simple. Benjamin is the number one contender and doesn't trust The First at all. The First is in his classic "I play head games" with everyone mode. Cammy is mad at the world and is taking this match seriously. He's ready to make and IMPACT so really none of the rest of us stand a chance.
And me...well I'm in this for one reason and one reason only; I was booked in this cluster. I don't care for any of the other three in this match, I don't like any of the other three in this match and beating any of them yet again isn't going to do much for my career.
But...I will cement a win for my team. I will do whatever is necessary to win this match. If that means knocking out my team mate; oh well. The one and only true UCW Icon will dominate this match further cementing his place as the greatest man to ever grace this federation.
Oh and Cammy baby; that's a reality check you just have to live with. TSiegel — 23 March 2007 (Fadein, Cruise in front of a UCW Revolution backdrop. The same appearance more or less but with his "Anarchy" shades on hiding the look of irritation in his eyes, one couldn't tell that they're still bloodshot.)
CRUISE: 'Red, what you're telling me here isn't anything new, as a matter of fact, I've heard it one time or another from some ugly hack who thought he was tough sh*t, but still ended up looking up at the lights as a result of a Shipwreck or Reality Check because of it.
I've BEEN living with it. Now-a-days it's just daily ROUTINE. But you're smokin' crack if you think I'm sittin' on the wayside watching Sammy Benson look-a-likes pick their nose while you show me up.
You forget...this is an actual WRESTLING match, which means half of your offensive arsenal is down the sh*tter.
(Cruise shakes his head) 'Less you forget 'Red...this isn't the first time we've been partners on the same side, and to tell the truth...the last time we tagged, I wasn't truly halfway impressed with you then either.
But we're doin' it again, this time against a team that actually speaks relatively decent ENGLISH, so perhaps this time will be different.
But as far as I'm concerned...the only way you're getting through this alone in the spotlight WITH your hand raised is by the very method you said yourself: Knocking me out.
We both know that I can bleed, and I can careless about it. But layin' me out... It'll be a cold day in hell when that happens. Now THAT 'Red...is a REALITY CHECK that YOU'RE going to have to live with. (Fadeout.)
