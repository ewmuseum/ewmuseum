UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

United States Title Triple Threat Match!

21 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 21 November 2006 Dakota Smith (champion) vs. Jogi Fresh vs. Frankie Scott The first man to score a pinfall will walk away with the United States Title! The RP deadline will be Saturday, December 2nd at 11:59 PM EST. JC — 23 November 2006 (We fade in on Rico Suave who has Dakota’s newly won UCW US belt on one shoulder, the MCW NA belt on the other, a devious grin crossing his face, as he walks slowly towards our camera… As he stops, he extends his arms out and lets out a loud barrage of laughter…)
RS: Well folks, we have a new UCW US champion, and it’s none other than my man Dakota!!! Were there ever any doubts!? I bet you all thought that Storms was going to run roughshod over my man, but it went the other way around, now didn’t it!? Now the big question here is, just who does Dakota work for!? He’s still the MCW North American champion and now he’s the UCW US champ… Go figure… Is he working for both Latham and Cloverleaf, or he is truly that acorn that did not fall from the tree!? Can he be playing both ends against the middle!? (Laughs) Man, I just love these controversial moments, I bet by now most of you are saying, “yea, who is Dakota really affiliated with!? Is he UCW!? Is he MCW!? Or is he that snake in the grass that just thinks about himself and ONLY himself!? Dakota is a very complex man, a man who you really can’t figure out, just when you thought you had the answers, he changes the questions… I tried to warn you all about him, but I bet you thought I was just spewing out some hyped up manure smelling sounds just for the hell of it… Well reality sucks and it just bit you in your ass… You are at the gate, amigo and I am the gatekeeper!!! (Pauses momentarily looks into the camera and begins to grin…) Always wanted to say that!!! But enough of the controversial bull crap; we’ll come back to that later… Now I need to address this “triple threat match” Dakota will have against the likes of Jogi Fresh and Frankie Scott… (Shakes his head as he again grins…) Two sacrificial lambs…
(He looks into the camera, again he shakes his head and crossing his arms he motions for us to come closer…)
RS: Lamb number one, Jogi Fresh… (Smirks) Fresh meat!!! Lamb number two… Frankie Scott… (Again he smirks, which slowly turns into a devious grin and then into a cynical laughter…)
RS: What excuse will you use this time Frankie boy!? I can just picture how this will all turn out, you’ll throw Jogi to the wolves or should I say wolf or is it snake!? Screw it, whatever!!! (Laughs) You’ll get in a cheap shot here and there, looking for that golden opportunity that will never present itself… And you want to know something Frankie!? I am hoping that I am wrong about you this time, hopefully the lure of gold will give you some cojones and you will actually fight as if your life depended on it… And knowing Dakota, most likely it will… He doesn’t really like you Frankie, he’s seen right through… And do you seriously think that you or Jogi have any type of chance!? Come on Frankie, get real!!! You and Jogi are just an appetizer, something to keep him a little content until he gets his hands on Sarge… You remember him don’t ya Frankie!? Jogi, don’t know much about you, in fact don’t know s(bleep)t about you, so I won’t say much… Not much to say anyway… In fact screw you Jogi and screw you too Frankie!!!
(He is about to turn and walk away, but stops midway… As the camera focuses in, we see that it is none other than Dakota… Deadpan with eyes beaming into Rico who nervously again extends his arms and says…)
RS: The CHAMP has ARRIVED!!! I was just telling these peons, how… (Gets cut off…) DAKOTA: You never cease to amaze me, how you can mesmerize these peons with your crap is beyond me… What bull have you been shoveling down their throats!?
RS: I was just enlightening Jogi and Frankie on a few well known facts… I didn’t want them to get their hopes up…
DAKOTA: (Smirks) They don’t matter, they are insignificant… Sarge, it would seem that the ball is in your court, answer my challenge… I am giving you the opportunity to live your dream… A chance at the gold, slim as it is, it’ll be the closest you will ever get to it… So bring your mother approved, combat tested ass down and once again taste the agony of defeat…
RS: Err, Dakota, you’re fighting a triple threat match against somebody named Jogi Fresh and Frankie Scott… Your match with the Sarge is after the triple threat match… You’re jumping the gun a bit… Let’s stay focused…
DAKOTA: What are you doing walking around with my belts!? RS: Just wanted to do some showboating for the peons… (Smirks and winks…) You know, rub it in, jerk their chain a bit…
DAKOTA: No Rico, I don’t know… All I know is that I called Sarge out and I still haven’t gotten an answer… As far as this triple threat match… A prelude to “Night of the Legends II”, that’s all it is, nothing more than a prelude… That’s where I’m going to make the Sarge a legend… I am going to make him famous… Unlike Jogi and Frankie, I am going to hurt them, I don’t like Frankie much and Jogi, well he just happens to be heading to the wrong place, at the wrong time… The question is, which one will I beat up on more, who will I smash into the ring for the 3 count…
RS: I hear that Dakota, but anything can happen, all it takes is 3 seconds and it’s all over… I don’t know much about Jogi, but Frankie is a little sneak and 3 seconds may be all he needs Dakota… It’s like a 3 on 3, only each one of you will be the 3… 3 seconds, for a 3 count, 3 opponents…. Hmmm, very interesting…
DAKOTA: Whatever Rico… (He turns to walks off, leaving Rico dumbfounded momentarily…) RS: Where’re you going Dakota!? Is that IT!? End of conversation!? DAKOTA: No, make sure that you keep them real shiny (Points to his belts, he pokes them hard, making Rico grimace…) End of conversation… (Walks off…)
RS: (Staring and shaking his head, he says…) Talk about rude, obnoxious, arrogant, cocky and just being a plain old SOB, that man there, (Points in the direction Dakota walked away to…) He’s king of that freaking mountain, yep, he’s king of Mt. Son of a b(bleep)h… Frankie, Jogi good luck trying to knock Dakota off that freaking mountain of his, you’re going to need all the damn luck you can get and you’ll still come up short… But it’s all good guys, at least you’ll come out of it a little bit wiser, a whole lot of hurt and disappointment but a bit wiser none the less…
(Bursts into a cynical laughter, as he mockingly acts out polishing Dakota’s belts… Again he extends his arms, this time he spins around and starts to walk away in the opposite direction that Dakota took… Camera slowly fades to black…)
JC — 28 November 2006 (Scene opens somewhere in Atlantic City, it is nighttime, we see Dakota and Rico by the boardwalk… Deep in conversation or rather Rico is in deep conversation, Dakota is staring out into the star filled night…)
RS: You know, you’re right Dakota… These damn peons in the UCW are no better than the idiots we faced at MCW… Nothing but damn peons Dakota, and unfortunately as much as you hate doing these promos, they’re in the contract and they’re binding… But hey, we can make them work for us, just think of them as forewarnings to your opponents, never mind that these cockroaches think giving you the silent treatment will in any way take you from your game plan… Let them think that Dakota… Let them think whatever the hell they want, none of it matters once you got them in the ring, whether they decide to give you the silent treatment or come out and talk a lot of smack, it won’t mean squat… It didn’t work in the MCW and it certainly didn’t work for Bryan Storms… So screw them Dakota… Screw them all!!! They all fear you Dakota, they know that once they’re in the ring with you, all the bull ends… They don’t really want any part of you, they don’t want another loss in the lost column… With the exception of the Sarge, all I see is a bunch of ball less wanna bees and never were… They’re walking around with their asses so high up in the air I’m surprise they haven’t blacked out from their own stench…
(Looks at Dakota who is still staring into the night sky…) RS: You’re not even listening to me are you Dakota!? DAKOTA: Nope… You talk too damn much Rico… RS: Hey dude, you got a match against two individuals that might be a bit hungry; not that it will matter much… I was just trying to point out, that….
(Dakota cuts him off…) DAKOTA: All you’re doing is burning some brain cells and wasting your time… Do you really think that I give a rat’s ass about Frankie or Jogi!? The only thing those two are going to walk away with will be a lot of pain and disappointments…
RS: That goes without saying, Like I said before, they’re just two more lambs being led to slaughter… What I want to know is why you’re still wasting your time with Sarge!? Go after the big fish, better yet, just relax and enjoy being champ for a while… Do like everyone else does…
DAKOTA: I’m not like everyone else ya damn asswipe… RS: That, we all know, but why am I, an asswipe now!? (Dakota just stares at Rico and grins…) RS: You’re up to something, I know that look… I hope you are not planning to do what you did in the GLCW, you saw what that got us… A bankrupt federation and you almost banned… MCW gave you an opportunity to get back into the game as is the UCW, don’t screw s(bleep)t up Dakota… We got a real good thing going on here…
DAKOTA: And your freaking point is!? RS: My “freaking” point as you so eloquently put it; is this… Don’t blow this Dakota… You saw what Latham did to Bryan; he stripped him of the MCW world title… A title that should have been yours from jump street… And while many say that the MCW is dead, I say differently… And you at the moment amigo hold all the aces for both the MCW and the UCW… Think about it Dakota, here you are champion in both federations and the way I see it, this MCW so-called invasion was nothing more than a well planned ruse, just like its closing, nothing more but a well thought out stratagem… I’m beginning to see the whole picture now… And you are the key factor in their little game, and if we play our cards right and you listen to me, we will be on top of that mountain in no time…
(Dakota continues to stare into the night sky; a cynical grin crosses his face as Rico continues talking and making plans… Rico soon realizes that Dakota is not really paying attention to him…)
RS: Are you even listening Dakota!? I’m telling you man, the ball is in our court!!! DAKOTA: “Our court”!? I don’t see you getting in the damn ring, night after night handing them their asses… You’re just along for the ride…
RS: Hey, who got you your title matches!? ME!!! Who is about to make you a household name!? ME!!! Who got you a s(bleep)t load of money!? ME!!! That’s who!!! Who got you…
(Dakota cuts him off laughingly… Rico just stares angrily and shakes his head in disbelief…)
DAKOTA: Shut the f(bleep)k up Rico, you’re beginning to sound just like them… You’re here because of Nevada… You didn’t make me, I made myself!!!
RS: Nah, you’re wrong dude, Nevada and the NEH/UA made you… Face it amigo, you cannot deny that… Nor can you deny the fact that I played a major role also…
(Dakota continues to laugh…) RS: What’s so funny!? DAKOTA: You are… You’re a very funny guy; I see now just how you mesmerize the peons… (His facial expression changes as does his demeanor…) Like I said before, you’re just along for the damn ride…
(Without saying another word, walks off towards the casino… Rico watches as he enters the casino, before he says anything else…)
RS: Yea, that’s right walk away from the truth… You can’t handle the truth!!!
