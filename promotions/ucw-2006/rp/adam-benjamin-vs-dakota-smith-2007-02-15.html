UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

Adam Benjamin vs. Dakota Smith

15 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 15 February 2007 This is a NON-TITLE match. Singles Match. The RP deadline will be Saturday, February 24th at 11:59 PM. JC — 19 February 2007 Once upon a time in..... (Camera fades into a screen that has “Scene one” written on it and as we continue to focus in, we see Dakota as he now is standing in front of the screen… Both belts on his shoulders, a devious smirk crossing his face and standing next to him; an individual just as big as Dakota…)
DAKOTA: Well it is very obvious what happened at “Night of legends II”… Once again you crashed and burned Sarge… Once again you blinked!!! Did you seriously think that I was about to let go of this, (Points to the UCW US title) just like that!? Did it ever cross your feeble mind that you were up against someone who is even more hungrier than you!? (Shrugs his shoulders and continues to smirk) You truly believed that you had a chance, an ever so slight chance… But you blinked and that’s all it took Sarge, one freaking blink and I took it away… I have to admit though you did give me a hell of a fight, up until you blinked… (Laughs and hands both belts to his still unknown companion and slowly starts to pace…) Sarge, are you listening!? (Pauses, as if awaiting a reply…) Okay, I’ll keep it short, this way your lil peon fans can explain it or at least try to… I just wanted to let you know… S(bleep)t happens… Sure you’re pissed, but just look at it this way; you gave it your best shot, but unfortunately it just wasn’t your night, again… So tell me, how did it feel to come so damn close!? (Pauses as he rubs his hands together, his eyes narrow as he peers into the camera…) But tonight, is not really about you Sarge, tonight I focus on “Mr. Adam Benjamin” A man, just like you full of ambition, a man who has his bag of hopes and dreams, again just like you… But unlike you, he’s a veteran of the mat he’s been around the block a few times and has had his share of championship belts…. A man who is talented, and very ring savvy, knows the ring like the back of his hand and on top of that has the talent and skills to back his rep up… Interesting… How lives can parallel… Hey Adam, I know you see things from a very different perspective you see the glass either half full or half empty… Me!? I see neither all I see is a glass with some damn water!!! You say it’s your time to shine, to move upward and stand as the new face of the UCW…
(Again he pauses and again he paces, he rubs his hands together and slowly brings them to his face as if in deep thought… He stops and faces the camera and with a deadpan stare says…)
DAKOTA: You might just be that Adam… But coming into my realm is a very bad mistake!!! As you can see I am on the top of the mountain… My mountain!!! And I don’t intend on giving that up, not just yet anyway and while this is a non-title match, I’m taking it like I take any match, very seriously… You’re no different than the rest, so get that out of your head, and don’t look at me as another step upward… That you will need to seek elsewhere, all you’re going to find here is a lot of pain and disappointment… (Smirks) Now don’t get me wrong Adam, I’m not taking anything away from you, but your ego is about to get a rude awakening, your upward spiral to whatever star you’re shooting for is about to crash and burn… Other than that, there’s not much I can and want to say about you Adam, I know of you, but I don’t know you… (Smirks) But all that’s about to change… Fate and destiny are about to cross paths and all hell will break loose, I say this because you’re looking to spiral upward and I’m looking to make you spiral downward… So let’s just see who will prevail in the end Adam… Let’s just see who is hungrier, and whose night it will be…
(Without saying another word, he turns and walks away, his companion’s eyes follow Dakota as he walks away, he stands there with arms crossed, both belts dangling on his shoulders, a very sinister grin crosses his face as he shakes his head and begins to walk away also….)
Fade to black… Adam_Benjamin — 19 February 2007 (Fade into "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin standing in front of a UCW back drop.) "This week I am facing a man name Dakota Smith. He is the current United States Champion in this federation. He also carries another title from a company he works for.
He is very proud of what he has done, and will not hesitate to tell the world of his accomplishments.
He also likes to rub his hands together." (Adam smiles) "You see my hand remains still, I do not shake at the thought of facing someone. I never fear what lies head, for if I do, then what shall come to pass has already been revealed.
I could come out here today and tell everyone that Dakota is a great athlete, yet against me he will be exposed and left searching for answers.
But the truth is everyone already knows that. As for your realm? I have been there, done that, and I have moved on. You glorious Mountain is nothing more than a simple bump in my road. One of which I will jump over without any major problems.
And if you look deeply into your mountain you will see the Engraved signature of "Yours Truly" for I was the First man to stand on your wonderful mountain.
I am very glad that you are taking this match seriously. Truth is that if you don't I will hurt you very badly.
I am a professional, I am not some Johnny come lately. This week unlike your past opponents I do not blink".
(Adam shakes his head at the thought of blinking) "You claim that you are going to take down my upward spiral? The truth is that is not going to happen.
What is going to happen is I am going to raise you to stardom, by showcasing your abilities against a wrestler such as myself.
And when it is all over, you will learn from this defeat. This week I am going to test your desire, and you hunger. Rise above the glass, and lets see if you can hang at the top of a real mountain. (Fade to black) JC — 23 February 2007 Black moon rising.... (As we zoom in we see Dakota standing stoically in front of the giant screen on which we see Adam Benjamin as he raves on about himself… Dakota seems to be amused by this as he smirks and laughs throughout the entire segment… And as the segment ends, he rubs his chin and rubs his hands together momentarily and paces like some sort of caged animal…)
DAKOTA: Very interesting Adam… It’s very interesting how you have turned this and made it all about me… You may have fooled the peons, but I know you better than that; you see Adam, I know what you’re about… You’re all about nothing… And reflecting your shortcomings and what you perceive as the truth is nothing more than just another segment of your delusional daydreaming… And quite frankly Adam, you need to face facts and realize that it’s not about me, it’s about you Adam… Coming out here and trying to downgrade me, is not going to cut it with me… No, no Adam, your talk doesn’t bother nor worry me at all… The fact that you think of me as nothing more than a “simple bump in your road” is just an opinionating afterthought, a case of wishful thinking, but most importantly it’s your opinion amigo and you’re entitled to it… And come to think of it we are all entitled to our opinions Adam, they’re like assholes we all have one… But as far as “major problems” are concerned, you haven’t seen “major problems” yet amigo, so don’t make claims you will not be able to keep… You’re going to find that this “simple bump in your road” will turn out to be a freaking brick wall… And yes I did see your engraved signature, but wait!!! There was more, you forgot to mention that it read something like this… “Yours Truly” was here, but now he’s gone…
(He begins to pace in front of the screen, a cynical grin begins to cross his face…) DAKOTA: You say that I’m proud of what I have done!? That I do not hesitate to tell the world of my accomplishments!? (Smirks) And which would those accomplishments be Adam!? Other than the Sarge, all I’ve fought have been nobodies… I guess you’ll be the very first professional I will have the honor of taking down… And Adam I am going to professionally kick your ass, but don’t take it personal Adam, it’s just business… (Smirks) And then again it could be the other way around huh amigo!? It could be you who professionally kicks my rookie ass, after all you are a “professional” or so you say, but one thing I do know and that’s that you’re not any damn “Johnny come lately”… That I do know for a fact…
(Smirks and rubs his hands together only this time in a cynical manner…) DAKOTA: It’s just a little annoying habit I have, I just like to pace and rub my hands together… We all can’t be like you Adam; you’re one of a kind… Hell when God made you, he broke the mold after seeing what he had created… But what you really need to do Adam is to stop looking into that mirror whenever you address me… That is not going to work with me so reflect on someone else and concentrate more on how you might survive our encounter and believe me when I say; I am going to be more than a “simple bump in your road”!!! I am going to be that brick wall your blind ass didn’t see coming…
DAKOTA: You are right though when you say that you’re going to raise me to stardom, beating you as I showcase my abilities against a wrestler of your status can only elevated me, even if it is just a notch or two, none the less it’s an elevation… And when it’s over Adam, I will learn from this defeat… I will have learnt that defeating you was only a matter of time… Another thing I will have learnt from this defeat is that you did not take it gracefully, for whatever reason you will take this defeat the wrong way… Instead of accepting it for what it is, you will no doubt become even more bitter than what you have shown me so far… But come and test my hunger and my desire anyway Adam… Come and learn what hunger and desire is really all about… Come and watch how I rise above the glass and show you just how I hang at the top of a real mountain…
(Without saying another word, he turns and walks away… Fade to black…) Adam_Benjamin — 25 February 2007 *Fade into "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin wearing a "UCW" shirt. His mood is seemingly calm, yet his tone tells you he is all business.*
"I could come out and trade opinions with little Dakota. I could tell him I am a lion and he is my pray. Or that he is young and I am just more established.
Or I could go the other route, like others take. I could come out and be humorous and get everyone to watch my promo's for comedy purposes.
But Yours Truly does not jump though hoops, I do not make animals out of balloons nor do I tell funny jokes.
I am not going to come out here and fire off in a verbal pissing contest like some geek in a wrestling E fed."
(Adam looks into the camera) "I am a professional, my talking is done in the ring. What I come out here and say during the week is not overrated hype, it’s the simple truth of what lies ahead for my opponents.
Now as for you being a simple bump? You claimed to be on top of the mountain. Yet I have stood on the United States championship mountain already.
Hence the signature reference. The truth is that after our match I am going for another title, and it’s the World title.
This week I walk into our match and break the brick wall down, slowly grounding and pounding your entire structure.
And I will stand in the middle of the ring and showcase everything that I have to bring to the table.
Testing you both mentally and physically. Track records are great, but its what you do in the present that matters. Prepare for a match of your career, but realize that there are simple two ways out, Tap or get pinnned.
The truth is told in the ring, not behind this camera..... ***Fade To Black**** JC — 25 February 2007 The eleventh hour... (Once again we fade onto a giant screen and once again we see Adam Benjamin speaking…. This time we notice his tone has changed from his previous segment… He is more calculating, more precise; Dakota again rubs his chin and once again he grins….)
DAKOTA: And on the eleventh hour, he broke down, and showed his true colors… (Smirks) Adam, there’s a lot of things you could do, but you won’t… You’re too good for that, your pride won’t allow it; your ego won’t permit it… So you come out here and say the same old crap… Hey amigo, we all know that you’re no overrated hype… (Again he smirks) And I am sure that no one wants you to come out here and fire off in some damn pissing contest… Especially like some idiot geek from some… What was it you called it!? Oh yeah a wrestling E fed… Well anyway, I am not expecting you to jump through any hoops nor do I expect you to make animals out of balloons… No Adam, I don’t want you to do any of that crap… But I do want you to come to this match, not with a chip on your shoulders, but with the realization that your chances of winning are just that; chances… Whether I have the upper hand or not is not important, but what is important is that you get used to the idea that you gave it your best shot and you came up short… Accept the fact that in the 11th hour you will blink, and all it will take is 3 seconds… Three lousy seconds Adam; and that’s no overrated hype Adam, I am neither a damn geek nor am I a damn comedian… And I really don’t give a rat’s ass that you have stood on the mountain I presently occupy and as far you’re your quest for the world’s title, good luck… And good luck trying to break the brick wall, and if you succeed in doing that, that’s when your problems will really begin… You see Adam, it’s not the brick wall you need to concern yourself with it’s what’s behind the wall…
(He pauses as he slowly starts to laugh cynically, he starts to pace; his facial expression changes from a mocking one to a deadpan stare…)
DAKOTA: Adam, you need to understand one thing, I am no “Johnny come lately” either… I am neither underestimating you nor am I intimidated by you… I do respect your skills and your past accomplishments, but it means nothing to me once we are in that ring; all I will see is yet another obstacle, another challenge that needs to be taken down… You see Adam, I see right through you, so you come and showcase everything you have to bring to the table… I welcome you testing me physically and mentally, expect the same amigo… But most importantly Adam, prepare for the fight of your entire career, the one that just might make you or break you, either way prepare Adam, but you better realize that there are not simple ways out… No, no Adam, you forgot that you can also get the f(bleep)k knocked out also… And remember Adam I go by one rule and that’s by any means necessary… See you in the ring Adam…
(Turns and walks away… Fade to black…)
