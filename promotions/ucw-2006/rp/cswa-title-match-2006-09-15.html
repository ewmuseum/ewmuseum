CSWA Title Match!

15 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 15 September 2006 Professor Tremendous vs. "Cowboy" Jimmy Donovan vs. Mateo The RP deadline will be Saturday, September 23rd at 11:59 PM. MrWest — 18 September 2006 (A Midget stands in the center of the ring. He is covered head to toe in elephant ****.) MIDGET: Greetings scablings and scablets of Unusual Championship Wrestling (or whatever the "U" happens to stand for around here).
I am the Team T Midget. And come to you tonight adorned thusly because my employer wanted to make absolutely certain that I fit in fully with the environment around here so as not to intimidate the "talent" and scare away all challengers well before match time {as it appears we may have done on the last stop along The Good Professor's CSWA UNIFIED World Title World Tour.}
So, sewer-dwellers of Ugly Championship Wrestling, it is your turn to witness the brilliance of Professor Tremendous as he makes a mockery out of all you stand for by easily defending the grandest belt in all of wrestling with one arms tied behind his back and while eating a chicken salad sub.
Now tell me, Unprepared Championship Wrestling, who have you selected from your crapulent roster to fall before the grace of my master this week.
(The Midget waits for a bit.) MIDGET: Well, I am waiting.... (After a moment, a man with an Olympic Silver Medal climbs into the ring. He holds a sheet of paper in his hand.)
MIDGET: What are you doing here? ALLWORLD: Um...I found that booking sheet, I misplaced last night. It was tucked in between pages 76 and 77 of my "The Complete Garfield" anthology.
MIDGET: Okay, so what do we got? ALLWORLD: Looks like "Cowboy" Jimmy Donovan and some guy named Matteo. MIDGET: Never heard of them. Were they on the scouting report from last week? ALLWORLD: Um...I don't think so. Tuss said they're actually both from the recently defunct LVW promotion.
MIDGET: Incredible. Absolutely incredible. UngodlySucky Championship Wrestling here truly must be the most absolutely cowardly and scrubtastic federation on the planet if they can't even find anyone on their own roster brave enough to challenge the Professor.
(Staring right into the camera) And to think that a doused myself in elephant crap for you. (The Midget heads to the ropes and starts to climb out of the ring.) ALLWORLD: Uh...where are you going? MIDGET: Well if we don't want to scare away these two schmucks from the Vegas before The Prof arrives, I really am going to have to go find a tubercular hooker to piss on me post-haste so they can feel at home.
ALLWORD: Oh right, of course. TH — 22 September 2006 UCW backdrop. CJD: Well, lookee here, I done scored me a big one fer my first UCW match. Professor Tremendous an' Erik Mateo in a CSWA UNIFIED Championship match! I feel almost as happy as the first time I laid eyes on the Bunny Ranch. Even though I done heard rumors that the belt the Good Professor was done carrying around wasn't the real McCoy, but some lil' midget guy covered in sh*t said that none of that was true. I'll be danged!
Now, like I said at Scars 'n Stripes, I wasn't comin' round here just tryin' to monger the gold an' disgracin' the legacy of Las Vegas Rasslin', but they done gave this opportunity to wrangle more gold for myself. The more titles I done merge into this title (pats the LVW World Championship), the more it's gone mean when I finally take the MCW and UCW Titles. Plus it's all takin' my mind offa not visitin' the ladies of the night. Seriously, I keep breakin' out in cold sweats in the middle a' the night. My pee-pee's havin' withdrawals, an' not the good kind either. In fact, I almost jumped all over this girl the other day. She was dressed in fishnets, an' a short skirt an' all that, but I was good, an' I didn't proposition her. I reckon it was a good thing too, cuz I found out she wasn't a hooker, just some high school girl on her way back from school. Man, I woulda had egg on my face.
But now, I gotsta take on the Professor, who ain't even graced us wit' his presence yet, an' just sent a midget all covered in sh*t... wait a second...
Jimmy strokes his chin. CJD: Nah... he couldn'tna been the same one. Different smells. Y'know, livin' in Oklahoma fer most a my life, you get to tell the difference between dungs.
Anyway, there's the Professor and then there's a familiar face in Erik Mateo. I done tangled wit' him in LVW, an' he's one tough sonuvagun. Y'all got to be tougher than a cowboy steak to win the Las Vegas Title. But another thing 'bout tough meat is that it don't taste good. Trust me, hookers complain to me about guys with chapped dicks all the time, an' how they don't taste as good as guys like me, who use cocoa butter an' all that other fancy sh*t to keep our pee-pees nice and not stanky.
Y'gotta have taste to get you far, Erik. That's how I got to where I'm at. I gots the right combination of tough and tasty. You... well, yer done good at pourin' drinks.
An' lemme tell you, after this match, I'mma gon' need lotsa drinks to celebrate the mergin' of another title to my LVW Title.
Fade. MrWest — 23 September 2006 (CondoTremendous: Professor T and his number one lackey Tuss have just finished going over a "detailed" scouting report of this weeks foes)
PROF: You serious? This guys really a cowboy? An honest to goodness cowboy? TUSS: Yeah, it appears so. PROF: Wow! I mean...WOW! I didn't know they made real cowboys anymore. He smoke Marlboros?
TUSS: Not sure. PROF: Well, all real cowboys smoke Marlboros. TUSS: Then I guess he does. PROF: Good. {Note to self: kick the Cowboy guy in the lungs} So what else do we know about this shmuck?
TUSS: Apparently, he likes hookers. PROF: Midget hookers? TUSS: Not that I am aware. PROF: Damn. Search continues then, huh? TUSS: Search continues then. PROF: So what's the deal with that belt he's carrying? TUSS: It's the main title from some recently defunct regional promotion called Las Vegas Wrestling. Donovan was their first and only Champion.
PROF: So is his belt cooler than my belt? TUSS: Cooler? Or more legit? PROF: I believe I said cooler. TUSS: No. Yours is cooler. PROF: I knew it! I KNEW IT! This CSWA UNIFIED GOLDRUSH World Title is the coolest belt anywhere, isn't it.
TUSS: THat's only because you are wearing it, sir. PROF: Oh, you are such a good Lackey Troll. TUSS: Actually, you kicked Troll of the team about a year ago when you caught him eating your PEZ, sir. I am Tuss.
PROF: Whatever. So what can you tell me about our other foe this week? TUSS: His name is Mateo. PROF: What else? TUSS: It sounds like his first name is Eric. PROF: Okay. That's enough. This should be a piece of cake. TUSS: Yeah? PROF: Absolutely. In all my years of wrestling - from peewee on up to today - I have never once lost a match to a guy names Eric.
TUSS: Really? How many have you faced? PROF: A whole bunch I am sure. Who really keeps track of names anyway? TUSS: Me. PROF: Of course you do.
