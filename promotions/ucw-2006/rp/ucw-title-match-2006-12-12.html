UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

UCW Title Match!

12 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 12 December 2006 The First (Champion) vs. Cameron Cruise The RP deadline will be Friday, December 22nd at 11:59 PM. The Great Eye — 15 December 2006 (FADEIN: The First sitting in a graveyard in Salem Mass. His hair is a mess of colors, his face pale with black eye shadow and lipstick. He wears his mad hatter jacket and black gi pants.)
THE FIRST: "Reality Check...What is reality? I mean, what is your reality Mr. Cruise? What is my reality? Who decides what reality is? Why are we in Iraq? Who is running this country? Who's a better QB, Tom Brady or Peyton Manning? Your answers make up your reality, mine make up mine, and our realities would most likely, be different...
You see reality is truly subjective...There are few true facts that can not be challenged in this world...Men and women study science all their lives and work to discover the history of this earth...And they are told their lives are a lie, that man was made from nothing by God...Who's reality is right again...And if wrong, who would ever admit to that?
You want a reality check but you hardly know what reality is, why do you even fight Mr. Cruise, what is the reason, the drive, the desire? Do you know your reality? Do you understand your world? What happens when we die Mr. Cruise, what is your reality about the afterlife, do you think it's even possible it's the same as mine? (shakes head) I doubt that a great deal...
And this is who you are...A man telling others that he will bend them to reality's will, when he doesn't know reality is truly molded and sculpted to the will of whoever beholds it...So what you really want is for people to listen to you, to believe you, to obey you...You seek not to bring others into contact with reality...But rather to bring them into submission to your will...
You are the petty tyrant, the pitiful ruler, the blustering bully, who is truly all bark and no bite...
But there is one reality that can be defined, can be proven, can be accepted as fact, and that is wins and losses, that is what happens to us in that ring...That is a reality that MUST be accepted, no matter who wants to try not to...And it is in that ring, in front of the eyes of the world, you will face reality, you will have to accept that you know nothing, and see even less...You will be driven from the field of battle once more, without the gold, without the championship...
That is reality... (FADEOUT)
