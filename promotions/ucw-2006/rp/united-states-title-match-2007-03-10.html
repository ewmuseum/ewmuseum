UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

United States Title Match!

10 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 10 March 2007 Dakota Smith (Champion) vs. BG Bruce Singles Match. The RP deadline will be Wednesday, March 21st at 11:59 PM EST. JC — 14 March 2007 (The following is a paid non-political announcement Brought to you by the good people at casa NEH/UA The screen fades into a collage of the NEH/UA, first off we see none other than the controversial JC, from there it fades off into Kraven and Flatliner Next on the screen is the legend himself Nevada!!! The screen then fades off onto a spinning sphere a panther and eagle are also shown as they dig their claws into the sphere, out of nowhere a dagger impales itself dead center From the bottom of the sphere, blood begins to drip from the protruding dagger Fade off When we come back, standing in front of the camera is a huge massive figure who we mistake for Dakota at first, but as we focus in, we see that it is his companion as of late Fade off)
(When we fade back in, we are face to face with none other than Dakota himself, his stoic expression saying it all)
DAKOTA: Yet, another controversial win for Dakota Yet another tainted victory for the books First Sarge, then Beast and now the First The Sarge, I can understand why he did what he did, the other two!? Who the hell knows why!? It would seem that they have some ulterior motives But that is something I will need to dwell on at another time, now I have you BG Bruce (Pauses and shakes his head) I can only imagine what the BG stands for and I dont know what Cloverleafs reasoning behind this one is, but I tell you what Youre s(bleep)t out of luck Bruce, wrong place, wrong time I feel like my back is up against the wall Bruce, why do I feel this way you ask!? Well its not your life style BG, your life style is your life style Only dont expect to get treated any differently because of it and I know that you dont, I just felt like putting that out there I have no qualms about beating you down, I will have no remorse afterwards It wont really matter that you are coming in this with a victory in your pocket and neither does the fact that you actually believe you stand a chance make any difference to me I am going to take you out just the same; I am sure that you will have a totally different opinion of our match Wont matter Bruce, nothing much will matter other than your beliefs and my reality once we are face to face in that ring And just how determined you are going to be once the dance starts; no good will come out of this match BG I will sink deeper into unpopularity, not that I give a rats ass about it, but what will you get out of it Bruce!? Nothing Absolutely nothing Other than a vicious ass whipping, youre coming out of this with nothing!!! BG, I dont want you to perceive my last 3 matches as something that you can expect, come into this match focused on one thing and one thing only Survival!!! Thats all you need to concern yourself with from the moment you hit the ring
(Pauses and starts a slow methodical pace, and after a few paces continues speaking) DAKOTA: Im not underestimating you Bruce; its just the way I am; much like you, you are who you are and I am who I am Im not here to prejudge you nor will I judge you Im just going to beat your ass down BG And thats no hype amigo; you see Bruce, its like this You have your reasons and beliefs that you might just have a chance at obtaining some gold, and that is good that you think that way, otherwise all will be lost for you BG You need to come into this believing that you have a snowballs chance in hell Think defensively and forget about coming at me offensively, thatll cause you to blink and thats all its going to take amigo And above all else, think about this Bruce; there has to be a reason why you were given this title match against me, and there also has to be a reason as to why these peons keep interfering in my matches Now I can name a few off the top of my mind, but I wont Hey Bruce, do you know who the smartest man in the world is!? Give up!? Okay Ill tell you, the smartest man in the world is the one who claims he doesnt know crap And thats how Im going to play it for the moment I know why you were given this title match, I can give you about 50 thousand reasons why Think about that when you come down to the ring BG, and when we are face to face look deep into my eyes and see the rage that burns deep within my soul
(Without saying another word he walks away; we slowly fade off into what appears to be another paid non-political announcement as we are privy to see what appears to be an eagle as he devours a small animal Fade off into a brick wall with NEH/UA scrawled on it in red Fade to black)
MrWest — 18 March 2007 [(BG Bruce sits astride his Hog with his "Lifepartner" Estaban wrapped snuggly behind him.)
BRUCE: Dakota, huh? Well that's a mighty pretty name, isn't it? Almost as pretty as that United States Heavyweight Title Belt that you tote around like a Hollywood Starlet with an accessory Chihuahua.
Frankly "Dakota", I don't give a damn about your qualms or your rage or where you might plan to sink or not sink in the weeks to come. All I care about right now is getting my hands once more around the scrawny little throat of Kenneth Q Cloverlerleaf, our illustrious commissioner. And, if unstrapping that shiny belt from around that dainty, little waist of your is what it takes to get his attention again, then that is exactly what the Lavander AssAssin is going to do this week at Revolution.
You see, Sweet Cheeks, you aren't up against just another wrestler this time out. You are up against a movement over 660,000,000 strong worldwide (yes, that's right, over 10% of the planets's population) and from all walks of life from accounants to hairdresser to ministers to this great big monster of a queer who is going to rip a hole in you large enough to drve Rosie O'Donnell trough.
So you'll have to forgive me if I leave the survival obsessions to your next "victim"; because, this week, Hell has a Blizzard in it's forcast. And is is going to be a blizzard of queer big enough that even Satan himself will have to take the day off of work while I finish my "plowing".
You do like a good "plowing", don't you Dakota? I think deep down all of you over-muscled hyperthyroidic freaks probably do.
But don't worry, after a couple weeks time, I am sure you'll dig your way out of the Gayalanche. And the only scars left to show for it will be your memories of title you once held and the fact that it hurts like bejesus when you try to sit.
JC — 24 March 2007 (VO: “There was a time when virtue was essential to what a man stood for, when chivalry was the quality and manifestation of what manhood stood for… Those days are long gone now, replaced by a generation that sought an easier and shorter route to their desires… Their dreams no longer a thing of pride, their hopes perceived only by their delusions”…)
(We then fade in on Dakota, who slowly paces inside an empty arena, in the middle of the ring…)
DAKOTA: Here again I have to deal with yet another asshole, a “Lavender” one at that… Hey Bruce or should I just call you “Lavender Ass”!? Ass-in BG Bruce!? Whatever… I am however glad that you don’t give a crap about much other than getting your hands on, what was it that you called him!? Oh yeah, our illustrious commissioner, Kenneth Q Cloverlerleaf…
(Pauses and looks out into the empty arena…) DAKOTA: You have a serious problem and that’s that you obviously lack CSI… Common sense and Intelligence… You see Brucie, I don’t give a rat’s ass about your little love spat with Cloverleaf and I don’t give a rat’s ass that your kind represent about 10% of the world’s population… Am I supposed to be concerned that I am up against this great big queer monster, who thinks he’s going to rip me a new asshole!? Think again my little feathered amigo; Think this one out hard and long... Regardless how big of a percentage you people have, it’s just you… It will be just you standing face to face with me and that is where you need to concern yourself… No accountant, no hairdresser nor minister will be able to help you in the ring… Here your innuendos, your opinions will mean CRAP!!! Here you will realize that the world is not as rosey as you would like it to be, in my world, it’s all black and grey…
(Again he pauses and looks out into the empty arena, this time he extends his arms outward…)
DAKOTA: So Brucie, you come and fill the arena with all your gay accountant and hairdresser friends so they can witness first hand and don’t forget to have a few ministers there so, this way they can pray for you after I beat your ass… But not to worry Brucie, afterwards when the pain has subsided you can go on with your life and your pursuit of Cloverleaf…
(Laughs in a cynical manner as he climbs down and walks off… Fade to black…)
