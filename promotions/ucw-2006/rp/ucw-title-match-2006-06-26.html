UCW (Ultimate, fwrestling.com, 2006–2007) — Roleplay

UCW Title Match!

26 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
thegr817deuce — 26 June 2006 Irishred (Champion) vs. The First The RP deadline will be Wednesday, July 5th at 11:59 PM. thegr817deuce — 26 June 2006 Red: The more things change the more they stay the same. Isn't that right First? I mean as a believer in past lives you must more then most not only understand but truly believe this statement.
You and I for the UCW championship yet again. *sigh* Are you tired of this? Does the thought of getting your ass beat by me once again bore you or do you actually believe that this time will be different?
First I know what's going to happen here. It will be the same as it ever has been between us. You will walk out in your sad sack way and mope about hoping to garner the sympathy of the crowd and somehow draw enough strength in that to defeat me.
Things have changed though First. This is not the UCW crowd that showed up in our formative days. This is not the disenfranchised youth looking to you as an uninspired leader. No the true fans of our sport have flocked to the UCW arena to see me, a true fighter, take our sport to new levels.
They aren't interested in your mindless ramblings and fantasies. They aren't entertained by your simple entertainment. They are here for blood. They flock to the arena to see me snap bones and tear tendons. They are here to see a fight and First, no one in the business fights like Irishred. My people, my "Redheads" will show up knowing that their champion; the UCW World Heavyweight Champion will give them the fight they need.
Through me they will be able to let go of their aggressions. As they watch me dismantle you they will be able to let loose that anger they hold inside for their spouse or employer. By watching me hurt and maim you they will find a release.
The crowd will be raucous. The air will be filled with electricity. The anger and tension will be palpable. There are not enough lives in this world or any other to save you from the inevitable beating you will take in my name.
I told you I would reform UCW in my image. I told you I would be the UCW Champion. I told you I would be recognized as the face of UCW. I told you that you would fall before me.
All I have said has come to pass. Don't doubt me now First. Don't think for one second that what I say will happen will come to pass. I will walk into our match the UCW Champion and I will walk out the same.
The more things change First, the more they stay the same. thegr817deuce — 26 June 2006 (FADEIN: THE FIRST sitting on the steps on Front Street. His hair is a rainbow of colors, green, blue, red, purple taking center stage. He's wearing a black silk button up shirt and his black karate pants. His nails look like they have been professionally done and are also painted black.)
THE FIRST: "Lies...Misrepresentation...A gross need to distort...These things never change with you Red...I wonder why...Why the man who beat me for that title has to go down such a road...You talk about my assured beating at your hands...How many times have we been in the ring together Red...How many times have we fought?
The answer is four... How many times have you beaten me? The answer is one... Yet I'm promised an asskicking...Promised a beating of epic scope...Yet, that result has happened exactly twenty-five percent of the time...
You got lucky Red...One time you got lucky...And because you managed to be completely soulless, completely without mercy...Completely without respect for the well-being of another fellow human, you did send me to the hospital...There is no changing that, I could never have done anything about it...So if you want credit for that, you have it...Congrats...
But the two times before that when we fought for the UCW World Title...I won...Oh whine and ***** about the other man in the ring, but you still lost...That's the result, period...I pinned your ass to the ground for 3 seconds and won one of those matches...Maybe it's not as cool as chairing the **** out of a helpless opponent and getting him send to the ER...But it's what we do, it's our job...
Things never do change Red...Some people like you will always exist, over inflated self important assholes who think that one result changes everything, one win makes them great...
You've been a coward as a champion...You've been sitting back doing nothing, this is what, your second title defense...Pathetic...You STILL haven't had a match for that title that you've won without needing weapons to get the job done...
Just some over rated scarred up garbage wrestler who's managed to hide behind Cloverleaf's skirt...
You're a joke... This week I show you how unfunny you are. (FADEOUT) thegr817deuce — 27 June 2006 Oh look the little fantasy creation is actually showing a little bit of heart. The little goth kid stepped out of his mommies basement; put away his 12 sided dice and actually stood up and said something with some substance.
Oh how your fathers chest must swell with pride First and oh how the other little nerdlings must feel so let down.
Really it's pathetic how quickly you desert your own little dreamworld when you realize that particular little strategy doesn't work against the World Champ anymore.
What happened to your assertions that "gosh darn-it I really did live all those past lives"?
Wait I get it. You were trying to be smart by not doing what I said you would do. You think that is going to throw me off my game. You think that will rattle me. You actually believe that you will somehow gain the upper hand.
That's cute. Listen First, go back into your basement and pull your little hoodie from Hot Topic back on, put on some Cure and cut yourself. Roll your little dice and pretend to hack up some demon or something. Lay naked next to your little goth chick biting on one another and pretending to be vampires as you glow in the dark like albino freaks. Get out your spell books, paint a little pentagram on your floor and re-live your "past" lives.
Do anything but show up for our match. I damn near crippled you last time First. This time I'll kill you. I have told you time and time again you don't belong in this federation. This isn't some little crap-hole backyard wrestling federation with trampolines and camcorders.
This is UCW son. This is where real men come to fight. This is where men of determination and courage, heart and passion come to face the baddest mother****er in the business.
This is where that same bad ass holds the Gold. This is UCW...this is Irishred territory and you aren't wanted or needed.
Show up and pay in blood. Do the smart thing and walk away and you'll just be forgotten that much sooner. No matter what you choose your time here is over...that's a cold shot of the truth. The Great Eye — 1 July 2006 (FADEIN: The First sitting on the Front Street Steps again, in the same get up as before.) THE FIRST: "I'll pay in blood? What other price can I pay against you Red...What other price can you pay against me...We are warriors...We fight...We bleed...It is what we do...There can be no other way...The wages taken are those in blood, in bones, in pain, in years off our lives...You act so big and tough about this...When it's just a point of fact, it's reality...You've never much had to do with reality...
When did I ever claim I haven't lived before, countless times...When have I denied my soul? Never...Yet you make things up...(Shakes head) the lie, weapon of those who have nothing else, flows so swiftly from your lips...But you're only conning yourself Red...
You send me to the hospital because you're a prick...Plain and simple, it was no talent, no skill, no excellent tactics on the field of battle, no just an accident, an accident you decided to take avantage of...When that accident befell you...I didn't do the same...It proves nothing, means nothing...Is nothing...
I've seen your kind since humanity first started forming civilizations...The drunken braggart, the tough guy, the bully...Never are you as tough as you think you are...Never do you see your own defeat coming...Never do you understand the way of the world...
The Blind King...So alone...So clueless...So naive... He will be dethroned...The True King takes his place once more... The King Is Dead Long Live The King. (FADEOUT) irishred — 2 July 2006 You've seen my kind since the beginning of humanity? You mean men like Genghis Khan, Attila the Hun, Eric the Red, King Richard the Lionhearted, Brian Boru and Sir William Wallace. Men that are true warriors. Men that led by example. Men that took no prisoners. Men that did exactly what they said they would.
Then First you should know that you stand absolutely no chance in that ring with me. Call me a bully, call my physical domination of you an accident, say whatever you need to say to ease your mind enough so you can sleep at night...but to call me a liar. To say that I have twisted facts to my favor?
That my young delusional foe is laughable. You are the one who luck shown it's lucky light on too many occasions. Your farcical title reign was nothing more then one lucky victory after another.
A true champion, like myself has decisive victories on his resume. A true warrior like the UCW Champion sends those who would stand against him to insane asylums or hospitals. Or...in your case to his mommy's basement.
I am the one constant in your sad little life. I am the one dose of reality that you can't hide in your stories of past lives. I am the warrior that you cannot defeat. I am the UCW World Heavyweight Champion and you are nothing more then a desperate challenger begging for one last shot at immortality.
The legend and myth of the great warriors grow as time passes...bards sing their tales for all time...sacrifices are laid before their feet...children long to be like men like me. You long to be like me.
You can't though First...you don't have the heart. You don't have the physical tools. You don't have the mental toughness. You are not now and never will be a champion again.
This time First I almost pity you. I know what's going to happen in that ring and despite your gift of precognition and the experience of all those past lives you have absolutely no clue.
You rage against fate like you have a chance to change your destiny. You of all people know better.
Your time has come and gone. The Irishred era is here in UCW for now and forever.
