A promotion run on the fwrestling.com hub in 2006 and 2007 by one owner, whose weekly Revolution and three pay-per-views, an invasion by MCW, the purchase of Las Vegas Wrestling and three titles are recorded in his write-ups and the handlers' own roleplays. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Ultimate Championship Wrestling came to the fwrestling.com hub in June 2006 after about nine months on another board, its owner explained, and brought its latest write-ups with it: Civil War and two Revolutions from June. From the hub it ran a weekly Revolution and the pay-per-views Scars and Stripes and Night of the Legends II. Its handlers wrote the matches' roleplays and the owner wrote the shows, and the record keeps both, with bios and the title histories he kept.

Its story was an invasion. MCW, a neighbouring promotion, challenged it on air in July 2006; Bryan Storms turned at Scars and Stripes and took the United States Title; the commissioner bought Las Vegas Wrestling; and Irishred, who had been champion, repainted the LVW belt as B4B. On 9 April 2007 the owner put the fed on hiatus, which he said was not a closure, and nothing later is held.
