Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Troy Windham Wrestling opened on 7 September 2008 as the self-declared least competitive league in FW Central's history: a low-rent promotion of sleazy small-arena New England wrestlers, its namesake an owner who lost his own title to his commissioner in the first roleplay. Its roleplayers answered with a card of characters in cutoff shorts, perms and mullets, most written by the owner and a handful of regulars.

In its first fortnight it ran house shows in Bangor, Hermon, Portland and Newington and announced a first television taping in Barre, Vermont. The record is the forum threads of those four weeks, to 2 October 2008, with no result held for the Barre taping.
