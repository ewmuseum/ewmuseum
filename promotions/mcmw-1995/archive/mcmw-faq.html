MCMW FAQ

MCMW FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS: OK, here are a of frequently asked questions about the MCMW, I've been getting a lot of mail requesting more info so here it is. 1. WHAT IS THE MCMW? The Motor City Marauder Wrestling Federation. It is stationed in Detroit Michigan and is one of the hottest fantasy wrestling organizations around. It's real life counterpart would be a hybrid of the hardcore ECW and the great wrestling of the NWA of 1980s. The MCMW is run out of Millersville University and features over fifty wrestlers to grapple for it all in the computer labs and dorm PCs. It started out two years ago founded by myself as a freshman. It started with eight wrestlers represented by five freshman wrestling hardcorers and has expanded vastly since then. Read on for more info. 2. WHO'S IN CHARGE? I am. And who am I? Well my name is Johnny Extreme and I'm the commisioner of the MCMW, I set up all the matches, decide which angles and feuds we are going to persue and all that stuff. In real life my name is [name withheld]. We run the matches on the PC live and I record all the events that transribe. Then soon after the events, I take my notes and transform them into a report that I will post on the newsgroup, rec.sport.wrestling.fantasy. I hope you all enjoy my reports, they are great ones to write and provide great entertainment and a change from the WWF and WCW. We have dedicated the MCMW to the fans, because we are big fans, and the write ups I do are from a fans prospective. I want to sign all the matches the fans want. 3. CAN I JOIN? Currently, only students at our University can enter wrestlers because we all like to crowd around the PC and watch the match progress live. It is a great feeling and right now we want to keep it that way. Although we are considering adding some members of the internet, right now we will stick to only students entering the MCMW. 4. HOW DO YOU DECIDE THE MATCHES? We created a wrestling simulator last year in our computer labs. It is text based and has all the aspects of pro wrestling intertwined in it. We use this program and it has been successfull int he past so we will continue to use and keep updating it from year to year. 5. WHAT IS THE MCMW MISSION STATEMENT? "We here at the MCMW, from the commisioner's office, to the wrestlers, to the promoters are all devoted to putting out the greatest brand of extreme and exciting wrestling in the fantasy wrestling world today. We will sign all the top starts to compete for our titles and make appearances on our PPVs and we'll never forget our great fans, because without the fans there is no MCMW! So let's get Extreme!" 6. WHAT TITLES ARE IN THE MCMW? Here are all the titles sponsored by the MCMW! Singles: 1. MCMW Title 2. MCMW Midwest Title 3. MCMW TV Title Tag Team: 1. MCMW Tag Team Titles 7. WHAT TV SHOWS FEATURE THE MCMW? Originally the MCMW featured four great shows, Extreme Excitement, Wrestling World, MCMW Mania, and Monday Night Mayhem, but with cutbacks, all those shows have been dropped for one show which will air at 8 PM on Monday Nights leading up to Monday Night Raw and Nitro for real wrestling fans, the show is called The Danger Zone and features some exciting and extreme battles, with the MCMW TV Champ defending his title their every week! 8. WHAT PPVs DOES THE MCMW HAVE? They have a bunch and here are the descriptions: (January) Seasons Beatings! - Held on New Years Day this is one of the most popular PPVs! (March) Uncensored! - All specialty matches that take it to the extreme! (April) Hardcore Hell! - Brutal Bashes and Battles! (June) King of the Beach - A tourney decides who is truly the king of the beach! (July) MatWar - The summers hottest feuds heat up in this brawl for it all! (August) Extreme Extravaganza - Three days of pain, agony and wrestling! (September) WarZone! - Features Wargames and other extreme matches! (October) FrightNight! - Horrors and Terrors take place on this evil card! (December) Armageddon -The MCMW Champ puts the title on the line in a Royal Rumble match 9. WHO WAS THE FIRST MCMW CHAMP? Fortress. He won the title at Matwar on July 18, 1995 in a stunning turn of events. Fortress was hired as Khan's body guard to protect khan himself and his wrestlers, Kong, Bearilla and King of Beasts. When a wrestler was injured, MCMW commisioner ordered Fortress into action to face The Midnight Rocker and he won. By virtue of competing on the card, Fortress was entered into the Battle Royale to decide the champion at the end of the night. The last three men in the Rumble were Bearilla, Kong and Fortress, all charges of Khan. Khan ordered Fortress and Bearilla to leave. Bearilla left. But Fortress said no way and powerbombed Khan and went on to win the Battle Royale and an impromtui subsequent match the same night over Kong. From that point on Fortress has been one of the biggest MCMW Fan favorites because of his charisma and his awesome athletic skill for seven footer. 10. WHAT'S THE HISTORY OF THE MCMW? It started with weekly tournaments every Thursday Night on a TV program we called Thunder Thursday. Every week we had eigth wrestlers enter a tournament and for each win in the tournament a wrestler earned ten points, with a twenty point bonus for winning the tournament. The original 8 wrestlers were, The Natural, The Franchise, Arnold Krush, Master Oki, The Mighty Miguel, Blackjack, and Tex Lexington. Tex is still active, while Master Oki manages while the other ones are retired in the MCMW Hall of Fame. The MCMW never officially declared anyone the MCMW Champ in first few years, we just competed to win the points race and be declared the 1993 Champion of the World. In 1993, the first year ever, The Franchise won the points race and was declared the 1993 Champion of the World. He won twenty-two of a possible forty weekly tournaments. In the second year, 1994, in a great race that came down to the end, Tex Lexington won the points race edging out The Franchise who was viaing for a repeat. In 1995, on July 18, 1995, the MCMW held a PPV called MatWar where Fortress won a Battle Royale to be declared the first ever MCMW Champion. The first ever pay-per view though was held one year before MatWar which is generally acknowledged as the first. The first PPV of the MCMW was held on July 5, 1994 and it was called Motor City Mayhem. The Main Event featured The Natural vs. The Franchise in a classic match.
