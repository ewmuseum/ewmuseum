MCMW Monday Night Mayhem, 17 July 1995

17 July 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Joe "Six-Pack" Sullivan) Live from the MCMW Arena it's Monday Night Mayhem! And boy do we have a great show tonight, the Main Event tonight is The Great Kamikaze vs. The Rebel, pick me a winner in that one Ted. ("Terrible" Ted Burns) Well, that's a tough one to call, to tell you the truth I don't like either one of these guys, they are too busy pandering to the fans and kissing babies to get down to business and hurt somebody real bad, but if I had to pick a winner, I'd go with The Great Kamikaze because of his rulebreaker past, he may be a babyface now, but bad blood still flows between these two, they had one hell of a feud a couple years ago on the independent scene with all kinds of matches with special stipulations but the feud was never finished because The Rebel took a two year hiatus before returning to MCMW last fall. Even though they'll shake hands in the beginning, my money's on a bloody brawl by the time the final bell rings, there still is bad bloode between these two. (Ed "Six-Pack" Sullivan) And that's not all fans on our first ever Monday Night Mayhem telecast, we will see Brian Boulder in action, along with Khan's Lords of the Jungle, The Hitchiker and The Nightmare Warrior is in the house! ("Terrible" Ted Burns) Well then let's shut the hell up and get down to wrestling! FIRST MATCH-UP Brian Boulder pins Evan Rice following a Rockslide Powerslam. MATWAR PAY-PER VIEW PROMO COMMERCIAL BREAK SECOND MATCH-UP The Lords of the Jungle defeats Alberto Kane and Amman Haggee-Jackee when King of the Beasts pins Alberto Kane following a big splash off the ropes. INTERVIEW WITH THE LORDS OF THE JUNGLE JOE SULLIVAN - Well, Lords of the Jungle, you guys have a big match-up tommarrow, you guys will be taking on the Team of Pain, Axel and Anvil. BEARILLA - PAIN! PAIN! PAIN! We love Pain! But there is one pain we hate! that's the team of Pain! Axel! Anvil! Come MatWAR it's a barbed-wire baseball bat match, and we plan on hitting a home run with your shitty little heads! KHAN - Ah yes! My Terrors of the Darkside are unstoppable, why just look at my Lords of the Jungle! Who can stop these machines of terror and destruction, A combined weight of over 800 pounds! KING OF THE BEASTS - Pain! Pain! Kiss your sorry asses goodbye! (Exit Lords of the Jungle) JOE SULLIVAN - Well Ted, who do like in that great bloodfest tommarrow? TED BURNS - My prediction? My prediction? PAIN! No, no, no, sorry guys just kidding, you know Rocky III, Mr. T, how could you not pick the Lords of the Jungle, they are the best tag team I've ever seen! COMMERCIAL BREAK THIRD MATCH-UP The Hitchiker defeats Chick Carnegie by DQ when Ludvig Von Frankensteiner storms the ring and attacks The Hitchiker. A melee ensued and it took several wrestlers from the dressing room to break up the brawl. The Hitchiker swore he'd get revenge tommarrow night. MATWAR PAY-PER-VIEW PROMO COMMERCIAL BREAK FOURTH MATCH-UP The Nightmare Warrior defeats Jordie Ashe after storming the ring and clotheslining Ashe a could times, Ashe got frustrated and left the ring getting counted out. The Nightmare Warrior grabbed the mike and challenged anyone from the back to come out and fight him! Newcomer to the MCMW Hans Helmet stormed the ring and began to batter the Warrior with a steel chair. Brian Boulder then rushed the ring and through Helmet over the top rope saving The Nightmare Warrior who was none to happy with the whole course of events and clotheslined Boulder over the top rope from behind. Boulder ran back into the ring and another brawl ensued. The Warrior thought Boulder was steeling his spotlight and that he could handle Helmet himself. COMMERCIAL BREAK MAIN EVENT! THE REBEL VS. THE GREAT KAMIKAZE This match was a classic! Both men stuck to scientific wrestling for the first twenty minutes but things turned ugly when The Rebel slapped the Great Kamikaze, who followed that with a vicious eye rake and through him over the top rope. Kamikaze hopped to the top rope and moonsaulted The Rebel on the outside of the Ring. They continued to battle as the refs count reached ten and both men were counted out. But MCMW President Johnny Extreme charged the ring and ordered the match to continue because the fans payed to see a definite finish to this feud. The match was restarted, and The Rebel took control of the match until Kamikaze got out of the way of the Rebel's patented top rope leg drop. Kamikaze then took control with a wide array of high risk moves softening up The Rebel. He climbed to the top rope and hit his patented finisher moonsault and covered him for the pin, but pulled The Rebel up at two. Kamikaze went to the outside and stole the production table used by the Monday Night Mayhem crew and placed it the center of the ring with The Rebel lying prone on it. Kamikaze scaled the top rope and flew off onto the table with a moonsault but the Rebel moved. Kamikaze smashed himself into the table, and the Rebel rolled him up with a small package for the win. After all that both men shook each others hands and raised their arm in victory. The fans gave a standing ovation to both men. This match was a definite all-time classic. MATWAR PAY-PER-VIEW PROMO COMMERCIAL BREAK OFF THE AIR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!1
