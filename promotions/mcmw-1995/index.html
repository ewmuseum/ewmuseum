Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Motor City Marauder Wrestling Federation began in 1993 as a Millersville University computer-lab game, its results decided by a text-based wrestling simulator its own students built and ran live, crowded around campus PCs. Rather than crowning a champion outright, its first two years ran on a weekly points race across eight founding wrestlers, with the year's leading scorer declared “Champion of the World” each December.

That changed at its second pay-per-view, MatWar, on 18 July 1995: Fortress, hired as a bodyguard for the stable Khan called the Terrors of the Darkside, turned on his own employer mid-card, powerbombed him, and won the battle royal that followed to become the promotion's first outright MCMW Champion. The night before, its first-ever Monday Night Mayhem telecast had built toward that same pay-per-view: Khan's Lords of the Jungle, Bearilla and King of the Beasts, promised a barbed-wire baseball bat match against a rival team called Pain, and a classic main event between the Great Kamikaze and the Rebel ended in a double count-out, restarted on the order of MCMW President Johnny Extreme, and finished with Kamikaze crashing through his own moonsault attempt off a stolen production table before the Rebel rolled him up for the pin.

The federation ran four singles and tag titles — the MCMW Title, the Midwest Title, the TV Title and the Tag Team Titles — across an annual calendar of named pay-per-views running January through December, and by August 1995 had consolidated four earlier weekly shows into one broadcast, The Danger Zone, its TV Champion defending every week leading into the real-world Monday Night Raw and Nitro.

Financial cutbacks announced 1 August 1995 ended its weekly television taping in favor of house shows and a magazine, though the promotion promised its pay-per-view calendar would continue; its last surviving posts, from two weeks later, still describe a running federation. No closure notice of its own survives.
