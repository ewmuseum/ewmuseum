(please, e-mail correspondence only) Reply to: [detail withheld] Presenting, The T.A.W. Tourney of Champions Well, well, well! I bet many of you were not expecting this. And if you were...well, shuddup about it. Nobody likes a person who gloats! :P Well, what this is, is a tournament. Every two weeks a card is going to come out. Pitting four man teams up against each other, ending in the semifinals and finals on the final card. The winner gets, well, to put it bluntly, the winner gets bragging rights. This is an internet-wide tournament. The catch, one person can own two men per team at the max, and only one entry per e-mail address. It is a roleplay based tournament, and the roleplays are gonna work a bit differently than you might be used to. Instead of four roleplays, each from a different team member, you are going to have to use your heads, to colaborate if you will. There is a three roleplay per week limit, so make them special. The application is below, I ask team members to use one application for the team, and that you list the e-mail addresses of the participating handlers next to the wrestler they handle. The deadline for entries is October 1, 1997. So thank you for your time, and let the games begin.
