JWO: Power Trip 5/16

3 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Promo — 3 May 2007 JWO will be coming to the Richmond Coliseum, in Richmond, Virgina on 5/16. The Main Event of the evening will see Faye Walker, hot off of her dominating, record setting victory on the debut Power Trip!, will be taking on the hillbilly from Georgia, Hornswagglin' Huck!
Otaku, will be squaring off against the man who sent a message to everyone in the JWO last week, "The Bloodduster" AC Blood!
And in other action, we'll see the legendary Hida Yakamo in action against "Risky Business" Ricky Bernero.
We'll also get details on exactly how the JWO World Champion will be determined, so make sure that you're there!
