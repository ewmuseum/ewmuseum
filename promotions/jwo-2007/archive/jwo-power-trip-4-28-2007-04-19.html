Promo — 19 April 2007 It's inauguration time in JWO'ville and we're talking about the debut of our flagship show, Power Trip!
Our debut show will be going down in none other than Nash-vegas itself, the home of Country Music, Nashville, Tennessee at The Nashville Arena!
We've got all of the JWO Superstars lined up for action and it's going to be coming to you on April 28th.
This event is an open RP event in order to begin your character builds. We'll take the RP and build the first show out of that.
RP Deadline is 4/25 at 10:00 pm ET
