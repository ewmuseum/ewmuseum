Foxx — 19 April 2007 So I just post the standard RP under my own topic? It's not a new concept to me, but I just wanna make sure ^^;
How exactly do you mean you'll "build the show around them"? I'll start writing something up ASAP. Promo — 19 April 2007 Yes. For this show, you can create your own RP threads. We will build the show around the RP's to showcase the characters. If some characters seem to "click" and would probably work together, then you won't see them facing off on this show. If a character seems to have issues with another, then there will probably be some interaction (not necessarily a match) between them on the show.
I didn't want to go as far as to say this, but I will. There will likely be some "jobber" matches taking place on this show just to help establish the character building.
