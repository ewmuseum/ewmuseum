Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

TRIUMPH Wrestling moved from idea to open applications inside a single evening: its founding post of 19 March 2001 opens, “Well, less than four hours since the idea was conceived, TRIUMPH Wrestling is now accepting applications,” though the fed itself wouldn't open for “another couple of weeks,” hosted at a Geocities address.

Its first signing followed the same day: an advertisement announcing that Jacob “Archangel” Sawyer had signed the promotion's first contract, inviting readers to “be just like Jacob and join TRIUMPH Wrestling” under the tagline it would carry from here on, “Wrestling's heart still beats.”

Handbook v1.1, published the next day, set out TRIUMPH's own philosophy in its own words: “not about a flashy website or a '.com' domain name, it's about quality over quantity.” Roleplaying ran through a password-protected board, with only the two role-plays closest to a card's deadline counted toward the hand-written result. Only fictional wrestlers were accepted — “if your Stone Cold Steve Austin impression just can't wait, join the 'WWF'” — and while “funny” gimmicks were welcome, the handbook said outright that TRIUMPH “prefers the serious approach.”

The same handbook laid out a full schedule: a weekly Monday show, TRIUMPH: Megablast, airing 9pm live on the NNW Network opposite the Vanilla Networks' own Wildfire broadcast, with a pay-per-view planned roughly every six weeks and a first one, still unnamed, set for 3 June 2001. Two championships were confirmed at press time, the TRIUMPH Heavyweight Championship and the TRIUMPH International Championship.

Megablast's first card went up on 9 April 2001, and the post announcing it thanked the newsgroup readers “who has helped me lay the foundations for the fed,” calling the road ahead long but adding, “I think we can make it to the very top.”

That post is the last document TRIUMPH Wrestling's own record carries. Whether a second Megablast followed, whether the pay-per-view planned for 3 June 2001 was ever held, and how or when the promotion ended aren't in the surviving record.
