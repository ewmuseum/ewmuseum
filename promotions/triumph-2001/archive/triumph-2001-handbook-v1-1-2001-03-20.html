================================= TRIUMPH Wrestling "Wrestling's heart still beats" ================================= ------------------------------------------- Handbook v1.1 Created 20.3.2001 E-mail: [detail withheld] Website: www.geocities.com/triumphwrestling -------------------------------------------- Contents ######## - What is Triumph Wrestling? - The handler we are looking for - Rules - The TRIUMPH Wrestling schedule - Titles - TRIUMPH on TV - TRIUMPH on PPV - Application form What is Triumph Wrestling? ########################## TRIUMPH Wrestling is essentially an Internet/E-mail based fantasy roleplaying wrestling federation. TRIUMPH isn't looking to be a huge commercial success, it just hopes to revert back to what brought this pastime to the dance: wrestling. TRIUMPH Wrestling is not about a flashy website or a '.com' domain name, it's about quality over quantity.
Of course, we don't expect you to believe that TRIUMPH can be the ultimate e-wrestling experience, but we hope that you'll give us the chance to prove it to you. The roleplaying is done via a password protected roleplay board.
Whilst handlers can roleplay as much as they please, the two roleplays closest to the next will be the only ones taken into consideration when cards are written.
TRIUMPH cards are, of course, hand-written. Although some may complain about
TRIUMPH using the roleplay board approach, we believe that the roleplay board will help keep handlers at a semi-leisurely pace, instead of being rushed to meet deadlines. It also reduces the workload on card writers, ensuring a higher quality of card.
TRIUMPH realises what wrestling is today. It isn't two guys in generic trunks rolling around on a mat in some dark hall, it's now about gimmicks, costumes and modern day theatre. TRIUMPH understands that sometimes storylines can take priority over actual wrestling, but rest assured that when that long-awaited grudge match occurs, you'll see the perfect mix of action, story telling and wrestling.
The handler we are looking for ############################## TRIUMPH is looking for good, hard workers with experience. Realism is important to us, as we believe that realism adds an extra dimension to storylines. 'Funny' gimmicks are always welcome, of course, but TRIUMPH prefers the serious approach. We take ourselves seriously and we'll always tell you things straight.
The people behind TRIUMPH are not looking for handlers to become 'best buds' with, just handlers who can work well with others in order to create storylines and action. Of course, any friendship in TRIUMPH isn't exactly forbidden, but if you join TRIUMPH to make friends you'd be better off in a chat room.
Handlers can complain at times, you've probably done it yourself. What happens when a handler complains to TRIUMPH? Simple: the matter is considered. Under NO circumstances will a card already sent out be changed unless a genuine mistake has occurred. For more on this see 'Rules'.
Rules ##### At TRIUMPH we don't have too many rules. Most of what we ask is general common sense. When applying: * TRIUMPH only accepts fictional wrestlers. If your Stone Cold Steve Austin impression just can't wait, join the 'WWF'. * Once your application has been sent in, we will notify you as being part of the roster. Some things to remember on the roleplaying board: * Don't break character. If you want to say something out of character use the OOC board or, ideally, e-mailing those who it may concern. * Roleplay twice a week at maximum. If you want to roleplay more than this then you can do, but only two roleplays will be taken into consideration during card writing.
* We appreciate that swearing has become a regular occurence in wrestling talk these days. If you feel your character should swear, use words that wouldn't be censored on a wrestling broadcast.
Some things to remember on the OOC board: * Racism, discrimnation or any other kind of alienation will not be tolerated. * Although you can discuss anything on the board, use your common sense and keep it within reason. Cards will be sent out via e-mail. There will be one card a week to begin with (TRIUMPH: Megablast). If the schedule/workload becomes lighter then there may be more cards added. There will be a pay-per-view type show every six weeks or so. Things to remember about the cards:
* Unless a genuine mistake has been made, cards will not be altered after they have been sent out. The bookers decision is final. * TRIUMPH allows sneak attacks, but not only to advance a storyline. If you feel your wrestler really should run-in, send TRIUMPH an e-mailed request.
