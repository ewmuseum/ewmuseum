[AOPW] Brief Advertisement, 18 June 1997

18 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, I'm Robbie Greer,and I'm president of the new regional fed of the UWF,the AOPW(American Organization of Proffesional Wrestling).Now with all the Anti-SHEF stuff going round,many people advertising on this are getting a bad name.Well,I don't advertise like all the others saying......... "Well,My fed kicks ass.......and...uh...it kicks ass....and uh....did I mention it kicks ass??" No,I don't do that kinda stuff. I state things as they are,as factual information.Now,AOPW is run with TNM a bit like UWF is,but we also use a thing I call the booked push system.What this does,is it takes a roleplay you did,and it gives you added push points.Here's an example..... Super Other Guy is scheduled to face Mr.Man soon.Super Other Guy sends out this awesome flash saying how he'll beat the hoo-hah out of Mr.Man in about a 4K RP.While Mr.Man sends in a low quality flash saying he'll beat the hoo-hah out of Super Other Guy in about 3 lines.Now,It's much more likely Super Other Guy will win(cause he'd deserve it,do to his hard work).Super Other Guy's "Push Points"or likeliness to win,would be increased,and he'd have a good chance of winning over Mr.Other Guy. I use the TNM factor to organize things,and to use the same level as the other UWF regional feds and the UWF. AOPW has 2 singles belts at first,and 1 Tag team title.This is how it starts out,cause It's not fun if we have a belt for every wrestler in the fed.It makes the belts lose prestige if you think about it. AOPW has 2 shows a week,and a PPV every 6 weeks.This allows time to build up heat in feuds,and to have enough time to start feuds before a PPV,which is normally an ending point for most feuds. I just wanna say that I have had alot of expierience in E-wrestling.I was president of the WAIWA for a year,Vice president of [name withheld]'s NCWA for 2 months,wrestled in DPW and PCW(Marc Stevens),and was co-president of the WWWA(Death Dean's). I hope you all take a look at this and please don't judge me as a big mouth.I am just stating the facts about me and my fed.I hope Anti-SHEF doesn't put me on their hit list :-),and I hope some of you E-mail me at [detail withheld] for an application to join the AOPW.Thank you for reading about the AOPW ========================== AOPW President, [detail withheld] (Robbie Greer)
