Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

American Organization of Proffesional Wrestling opened on 18 June 1997, pitched by its own president as a new regional fed of the UWF and, in his own words, an alternative to every other recruitment post's empty boast: “My fed kicks ass... did I mention it kicks ass?” His own, he said, would state things “as factual information.”

That information described a hybrid house style: TNM-simulated matches adjusted by a “booked push system,” where the effort put into a wrestler's own roleplay — a detailed promo against a bare one-liner — shifted the “push points” deciding who was likelier to win. Two singles belts and one tag title opened the promotion, kept deliberately few so they wouldn't “lose prestige,” running on two cards a week and a pay-per-view every six weeks.

Its president introduced himself with a résumé of prior leadership roles across half a dozen other e-feds, careful to distance the post from a wave of anti-spam sentiment then circulating the newsgroup: he hoped, he wrote, not to be judged a big mouth for saying so.

No further post survives after 15 July 1997. Whether the promotion ran a card, crowned a champion, or held its first pay-per-view isn't in the surviving record.
