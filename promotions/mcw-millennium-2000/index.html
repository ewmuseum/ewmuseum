Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Millennium Championship Wrestling announced itself on 26 March 2000 as a joint venture of three co-presidents splitting booking duties roughly four months apiece — a solution, in the founding post's own words, to the common complaint of an e-federation president who says “I don't have enough time to do this twelve months a year.”

It described itself as a booked promotion mixing real-life wrestlers with invented ones, applications for the same real wrestler judged against each other rather than settled first-come-first-served, each handler allowed up to two wrestlers and one manager, and match outcomes decided by a booker rather than a simulator.

No roster, card, or later post survives; whether Millennium Championship Wrestling opened as its announcement promised is not part of the record it left behind.
