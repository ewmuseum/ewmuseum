Millennium Championship Wrestling: New Fed Seeks New Members

26 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I don't have enough time to do this twelve months a year." How many times have you heard an e-fed president say that, or at least something similar to it? Can you count them on one hand? I can't. Not only have I heard it, but I have said it a couple of times, as well. But I love running an e-fed. I always have. So how do you solve a dilemma like this? You go out and find two more guys who are just as good and probably even better than you, and you team up with them. To make a long story short, I did just that. I am currently teaming with [name withheld] and [name withheld] to bring you a new fed: Millenium Championship Wrestling. MCW has three presidents; however, there will only be one booker at any given time. Each of us will be handling booking and writing duties roughly four months per year, which pales in comparison to all that responsibility for twelve months. The fact that we have all three worked together before in various capacities has shown that we are all basically on the same page and willing to work to put together an outstanding fed. As for other general information: *The fed is booked; therefore, wins and losses are not decided by a simulator of any kind, but by a booker. *The fed is composed of "real life" wrestlers and "made up" wrestlers. If you've never seen or tried this style, it works quite well. *Applications will be taken for wrestlers for a two week period, at which time the three presidents will vote on applications. Applications for real wrestlers are not "first come, first serve." We will be judging between multiple applications for the same wrestler. *Handlers will be allowed to control two wrestlers and no more than one manager, in most cases, and wrestlers can be entered into singles and tag divisions, but may not be in contention for titles in both at any given time. There is more information, and there are applications, of course. If you're interested in getting your hands on more info plus an application, visit the MCW's temporary webpage at: http://home.earthlink.net/~bssimpson2000 Hopefully, you'll join MCW and have some fun with us. [name withheld] Millenium Championship Wrestling
