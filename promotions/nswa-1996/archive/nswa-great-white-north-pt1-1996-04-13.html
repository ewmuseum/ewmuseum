New South Wrestling Association — Card

NSWA "Great White North"

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GS: Good evening folks and welcome to the great white north as the NSWA envades Canada for the weekend...
JC: GOD it is cold, would someone please turn on the heat.. GS: What? It was in the 60's today, if you would wear somthing besides thos skimpy light weight suits. Well tonight the NSWA is bringing you 10 hot matches highlighted by a Extreme title defense, an honor match and the debute of another new NSWA star known as Spartan. Well lets get up to the ring and our first match....
MATCH 1: RAEKWON VS. FIRE FIGHTER ALEX RA: NOW COMING TO THE RING, STILL TRYING TO FIND HIS MANAGER WENDY IS F.F.
ALEX. HIS OPONET TONIGHT IS THE MAN WHO STOLE THIS IS RAEKWON... Down the aisle comes a wrestler in a boxers robe and hood as on the big screen you can see Wendy and Raekwon kissing and winking at the camera. FF Alex is about to exit the ring and go after the wrestler when from behind comes Raekwon and smashes a wooden chair over the head of FF Alex (chair splinters into pieces). Raekwon then takes part of the chair and rams it into the head of FF Alex drawing blood. The ref grabs the wood piece and along with the other pieces throw them out of the ring. Raekwon then picks up FF Alex and whips him off the ropes and lands a big back drop. Raekwon then delivers a suplex and covers for the 1-2 but FF Alex is able to kick out. Raekwon the picks up Alex and throws him over the top rope to the floor. He then comes off the apron only to have Alex move and land on the steel guard rail. Alex then picks up a t.v. cord and begins choking Raekwon with the cord. Alex then suplex's Raekwon down across the steel ring guard.
Alex then rolls into the ring and goes to the top rope and comes off down onto Raekwon only to catch a punch to the midsection. Raekwon then whips Alex into the steel ring guard and follows in with a splash.. Raekwon then takes Alex and goes to slam his head into the steel ring post, but Alex blocks it and slams Raekwons head into the steel. Alex then picks up a large piece of the wood chair and drives it into the throat area of Raekwon and then rolls him into the ring. Inside the ring Alex is about to go for a brain buster when you hear Wendy screaming for Alex. Alex looks at the aisle and running to the ring is Wendy. Alex goes over to the rope, but Raekwon sneaks up from behind and rolls up Alex for the 1-2-3. Winner: Raekwon. As the women gets ring side it's not Wendy, but a women made to look like Wendy. She tosses Raekwon a piece of steel which Raekwon uses to start choking Alex. Raekwon continues to choke Alex till Alex is out cold.
Alex is then choke slamed 1-2-3 times by Raekwon. But then from the back comes Louie D. with a chair and slides under the bottom rope and slams it into the back of Raekwon. But then from the back comes that man again in the robe and him and Louie D. go at it exchanging blows. Raekwon gets to his feet and slams the chair into the back of Louie D. and then choke slams him down as NSWA officals hit ring side......
GS: Who is that guy with Raekwon? JC: If I am right, and I always am I belive his name is GZA or something, he is a long time friend of Raekwon. Looks like they cleaned house tonight, and poor Alex, thought Wendy was back, but that was just Raekwons other women who came down to help...
GS: Well this Raekwon is out of control and someone is going to have to do something with him. Well we are, wait, I am getting word from the back...
Mean Jean: Gordon I am back here and was just about to interview Lobo in concerns with his match when Raekwon an that GZA broke in. They threw me to the side and begin working over Lobo. Lets show the footage..
You see Raekwon and GZA both attack Lobo and beging beating him down, GZA removes the steel toed boot of Lobo and smashes it into the head of Lobo.
Then Raekwon picks up Lobo and from standing on top of a chair power bombs Lobo down to the hard cement. GZA then with the help of Raekwon super pile drives the Extreme Champ into the floor. From there both men pick up a nearby steel locker and drop it ontop of Lobo. Raekwon then begins screaming at Lobo that this is Extreme and he is a fake champion. Raekwon then takes the Extreme belt and smashes it over the head of Lobo and both Raekwon and GZA exit laughing as Lobo lays in the middle of the locker room.
Mean Jean: I am here as they load Lobo into the ambulance, he is bleeding badly and is in some pain. I don't know what this means for his title defense tonight.
GS: Well I am here with Comishioner Dusty Rhodes, what is the call. Dusty: Well Gordon, as you can tell Lobo is in no shape to wrestle after the beating at the hands of Raekwon and GZA. I will be meeting with the board about the future of Raekwon and GZA, as for the title defense, if Lobo is able it will occur next weekend at our NIGHT OF CHAMPIONS which will be coming to you all live from Chicago. Should Lobo not be able to wrestle, he will have to forfiet the title and it will be a match between the top 2 contenders.
GS: Well if I know Lobo, he will be in that match with Vladamir next weekend. Well folks that means our 2nd match is a scratch and we move on to match 3, lets get up to the ring...
MATCH 3: WEST INDIAN KID VS. SHADOW RA: OUR NEXT MATCH FEATURES ONE OF THE MEMBERS OF THE DARK SIDE, HE IS ESCORTED TO THE RING BY HIS MANAGER D.E. KILLAMAN, THIS IS THE SHADOW. HIS OPPONET TONIGHT IS ONE OF THE NEW STARS IN THE NSWA, THIS IS THE WEST INDIAN KID.
As the two men go to lock up the lights flicker and you see what appears to be Shadow hitting West Indian Kid with something. Shadow then goes to the middle ring belt and comes off with an elbow to the head of the West Indian Kid. He covers and gets a 1-2 but the Kid is able to kickout. Shadow then picks up the Kid and delivers a european upper cut and then whips the Kid off the ropes and lands a drop kick. He covers and gets a 1-2 but again the Kid is able to kickout. Shadow then picks up the kid and whips him off the ropes again and goes for a back flip only to have the Kid drop an elbow to the back of the head of Shadow. The Kid then delivers a spinning side kick to Shadow and then a standing drop kick. The Kid covers and gets the 1-2 before the Shadow can kickout. The Kid then picks up Shadow and whips him off the ropes and leaps from over him and connects with a cross body. The Kid then leaps up on the top turnbuckle and comes off with a backward moonsault and cover for the 1-2-3. WINNER: WEST INDIAN KID JC: Wow the Kid pulled off the upset, and boy is he happy. Wait what are Razor Blade and Anarchy doing at ring side, wait, OUCH they just throw the Kid over the top rope and down onto the steel guard rail, and now D.E. is slaming a chair down onto the Kids back and now he is slapping him in the face and yelling at him. Look out now Razor Blade is setting and just delivered a brain buster to the Kid out on the mat. But lookout here comes Modeus and Mitcla down the aisle as Modues jumps onto the back of Anarchy while Mitcla attacks Razor Blade. But Shadow is up and just came off the ring apron with a double ax handle to the back of Modues. D.E. is ordering them to, wait here comes Predator and Grave Digger along with Revolutionary and D.E. and company are making a run for it....
GS: Looks like the Outlanders and friends have not forgotten what happen last week. But the West Indian Kid is hurt as he is being helped to the back by Revolutionary. Well up next is our first tag action of the night as the Super Humans make a return to the ring against the Masked Assassions, lets get up to the ring....
MATCH 4: SUPER HUMANS VS. MASKED ASSASSIONS As the Masked Assassions are climbing into the ring they are attacked by Power and Punish who were already in the ring. They throw MA #1 up and over the top rope and he lands right down on the broadcast booth of Gordon Soldie and James Crocket knocking Crocket and MA #1 out cold. Inside Power and Punish then whip the other MA off the ropes and land a double drop kick.
Power then picks up the MA and delivers a face first suplex and then comes off the ropes and drops a knee to the head of MA #2. He then tags in Punish who comes off the top rope with a clotheline after Power and whipped MA #2 off the ropes. Punish covers and gets the 1-2-3. WINNERS; SUPER HUMANS.
GS: (getting to his feet) We need a medic down here, James is drolling all over himself and is out cold. (Gordon helps up the MA) Someone help this man and his partner back to the locker room.
JC: Who, what, where am I? GS: Sit still, medics are coming. Well, who the hell won that match. (he is told) WHAT? Wasn't the Super Humans disqualified? They ruined some very valuable equipment.
JC: Ah, Gordo, I am not feeling well, you will have to go the rest of the way without me...
GS: Well, maybe it isn't all bad after all, but I think the Super Humans are trying to send a message and they used the Masked Assassions to do it. Well up next is the debut of a new......
Just then the lights go out and screams can be heard from what seems like the whole building. The lights then come back up and in the ring is D.E.
Killaman, Executioner and a large head stone with the following written on it. HERE LIES A FOOL HERE LIES AN IDIOT HERE LIES SOMEONE FOOLISH HERE LIES THE SPARTAN GS: I am telling you I am getting wait.... here comes the Spartan....
MATCH 5: SPARTAN VS. EXECUTIONER As the Spartan hits the ring Executioner drives 1-2-3-4 boots to the back of the head. The Executioner then picks up the Spartan and delivers a suplex to the Spartan. The Executioner then goes over and picks up the head stone and drops it down ontop of Spartan, only Spartan rolls out of the way and the headstone breaks in half. The Spartan picks up half of it and drives it into the midsection of Executioner then delivers a ddt to the Executioner.
He covers and gets a 1-2, but D.E. Killaman gets up on the ring apron drawing the attention of the ref. The Spartan gets up and goes over and slaps D.E. But the Executioner picks up Spartan and delivers a belly to back suplex and the Spartan lands with his head hitting a piece of the headstone. Executioner covers as D.E. gets down and the ref counts 1-2-3.
WINNER: EXECUTIONER. Then Executioner picks up the other piece of the head stone and is about to slam it down onto Spartan when from the ceiling down comes Ghost Rider (on a bungy cord) and slams a 2x4 into the head of Executioner. Ghost Rider then disappears back up into the ceiling and cat walk area. D.E. has to drag Executioner back as Spartan finally comes to....
GS: WOW, that was incredible, How in the hell did Ghost Rider ever pull that off? He laid out Executioner, but not before the Executioner got a cheap win over the Spartan.
