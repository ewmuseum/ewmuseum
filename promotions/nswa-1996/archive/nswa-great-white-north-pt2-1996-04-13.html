New South Wrestling Association — Card

NSWA "Great White North"

13 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GS: Well up next is our former Extreme champion, lets get up to the ring.... MATCH 6: JASON ANDREWS VS. LUMBERJACK HAYNES.... RA: NOW COMING TO THE RING, WITH HIS MANAGER THE PRIME MINISTER, AND LOCAL HERO CANUCK, IS LUMBERJACK HAYNES..
GS: HEY, what in the hell is Canuck doing coming down, yes this is his hometown, but... Oh god some women just kissed that ugly idiot and she passed out...
RA: HIS OPPONET TONIGHT, LOOKING FOR HIS SECOND VICTORY HERE IN THE NSWA IS JASON ANDREWS......
Canuck walks over to Jason and spits on him and then as Jason takes a swing Canuck ducks and then drives a knee into the midsection and then and elbow to the back. Canuck then rolls Jason into the ring and Lumberjack Haynes delivers 1-2-3 boots to the head and then covers for the 1-2 but Jason gets his foot on the bottom rope. Haynes then picks up Jason and delivers a belly to belly suplex. He then picks up Jason and delivers a power slam and covers for the 1-2 but Jason is able to kickout. Haynes then comes off the ropes and drops an elbow but Jason rolls out of the way. Jason gets to his feet and drives a boot to the midsection and then a ddt. Jason then goes to the top rope and is about to come off when Canuck starts shaking the rope and Jason falls to the floor outside the ring. As Prime Minister distracts the ref Canuck works over Jason with forearm smashes and such. He then whips him into the steel stairs only to have Jason reverse it throwing Canuck into the stairs. Jason then leaps up on the apron only to catch a clothesline from Lumberjack Haynes. Haynes then picks up Jason and suplex's him back into the ring and covers for the 1-2 but Jason is able to kickout.
Haynes then picks Jason up and locks on a bearhug. The ref checks and the hand of Jason goes down 1-2, but on the third time Jason keeps it up and drives 1-2-3-4 fists into the face of Haynes who drops Jason. But Haynes comes off the ropes and drops an elbow to the midsection of Jason and covers for the 1-2, but Jason is still able to kickout. Haynes picks up Jason and whips him off the ropes and goes for a back flip only Jason summersaults over Haynes rolling him up for the 1-2 but Haynes is able to kickout.
Haynes is then able to catch Jason with another clothesline as Jason was getting to his feet. The Prime Minister then again gets up on the ring apron. The ref is trying to get him down, but behind the refs back Haynes holds Jason as Canuck comes off the top rope and smashes his chesse gradder into the head of Lumberjack Haynes as Jason was able to get out of the way.
Jason then drop kicks Canuck out of the ring and covers Lumberjack and the ref counts 1-2-3. WINNER: LUMBERJACK HAYNES.... Jason rolls out of the ring and exits as Prime Minister and Canuck are in the ring. Prime Minister does not know that Canuck missed and accidently hit Lumberjack. Canuck and Prime Minister help Haynes to the back...
GS: Well Canuck may be a fan favorite here, but I am not sure if Lumberjack Haynes is to happy. But Jason was able to pull off the win, with some help from Canuck and is looking to move into the rankings down the road. Well up next is another tag match as we head to the ring...
MATCH 7: DARK FORCES VS. BLADE RUNNERS RA: NOW COMING TO THE RING, WITH THEIR MANAGER BAD BAD LEROY BROWN ARE B.J.
AND JAMES BROWN THEY ARE DARK FORCES.... THEIR OPPONETS TONIGHT ARE VIPER AND VENOM, THEY ARE THE BLADE RUNNNNNNNNNNNERS...
Black Jack and Viper start for their team and lock up in the middle of the ring. Viper is able to move to a head lock and then gets whipped off the ropes but hits BJ with a shoulder block. Viper then drops an elbow, but BJ is able to roll out of the way. BJ then picks up Viper and body slams him back down and then he drops an elbow only to have Viper roll out of the way.
Viper gets up and tags in Venom who catches BJ with a clothesline, then another and then a third and then covers for the 1-2 but BJ is able to kickout. Venom picks up BJ and delivers a double underhook suplex. He covers and again BJ is able to kickout after 2. Venom then picks up BJ and whips him off the ropes and goes for a drop kick but BJ held onto the ropes and Venom catches nothing but mat. BJ tags in James Brown who picks up Venom and delivers a shoulder breaker. James then picks up Venom and whips him off the ropes and lands a clothesline on Venom. JB then picks up Venom and hits him with a spine buster and a cover for the 1-2 but Venom is able to kickout. JB tags in Black Jack who comes off the top rope down across the leg of Venom. BJ then locks on a one legged boston crab, but Venom is finally able to crawl to the ring ropes and get a break. As Venom gets up BJ grabs the bad leg and is about to spin Venom around when Venom hits him with a sabot kick to the back of the head with the other foot. Both men are down and finally Venom is able to tag in Viper who comes off the top rope with a splash down onto BJ. He covers and gets the 1-2 but BJ is able to kickout. Viper picks up BJ and whips him into the Blade Runners corner.
Then Viper goes after James Brown. While the ref is trying to seperate Viper and JB, Venom using the ring corner rope chokes BJ down. Viper then comes over and sits BJ up on the top rope and delivers a ddt from there. He then covers and gets a 1-2, but BJ puts his foot on the rope only to have Venom push it off and the Blade Runners get a 3 count and the win. WINNERS: BLADE RUNNERS... B-B Leroy Brown jumps into the ring and begins screaming at the ref that his man had his foot on the bottom rope. The ref tells him he didn't see it and exits the ring.
GS: Well looks like the Blade Runners pulled off a cheap win and Dark Forces are not to happy, this really puts a dent in their championship plans. Well up next is a match I have been waiting for, lets get up to the ring....
MATCH 8: GRAVE DIGGER VS. D.I.G. As Grave Digger was coming to the ring D.I.G. stepped through the crowd and over the guard rail and attacked Grave Digger. He caught him with a clothesline from behind and then a pile driver on the floor. The D.I.G.
went on to the ring. In the ring he drew the attention of th ref as D.E. Killaman begin kicking and stomping on the Grave Digger, but GD was able to get to his feet and he proceded to choke slam D.E. Grave Digger then came running to the ring and slid under the bottom rope and delivered clothesline to D.I.G. who had just turned around. Grave Digger then picks up D.I.G. and delivers a choke slam and cover for the 1- but Grave Digger pulls D.I.G. up and administers another choke slam. Grave Digger then gets to his feet and exits the ring and goes underneath the ring and comes out with a large headstone with D.I.G.'s name on it and today's date. But from behind Nitemare Capone attacks Grave Digger slamming his head into the head stone cracking it. He is about to do it again when Dark Rider (Nite Mares opponet on monday) comes running down to ring side and clotheslines Nitemare. While this is happening Dark Rider's manager Dr. Insane begins slapping around D.E. Killaman. In the ring as Grave Digger gets in D.I.G. drives a forearm to the back and then sets and hits a piledriver. Outside both Dark Rider and Nitemare continue to battle as Dark Rider is thrown into the steel guard rail, but moves as Nitemare attempts a splash. Inside as D.I.G. stands after the pile driver and signals match over Grave Digger raises up. D.I.G.
turns and drives a boot to the head of Grave Digger and then administers a power bomb, but again Grave Digger sits up. D.I.G. now again drives another boot to the face and then picks up Grave Digger for a neck breaker only Grave Digger blocks it and drives a knee into the midsection. Grave Digger then hits D.I.G. with his tombstone piledriver and cover for the 1-2-3.
WINNER: GRAVE DIGGER. Outside of the ring Nite Mare has seen what has happend and slides into the ring to check on D.I.G. as Grave Digger throws the pieces of the head stone into the ring. Finally other members of the Dark Side come out but by then Grave Digger and Dark Rider (along with Dr.
Insane) have left ring side. GS: Well, looks like D.I.G met his match tonight. And what about Nite Mare and Dark Rider going at it, they will be inside the ring going at it on Monday so it should be interesting. Oh yea, and poor D.E., looks like the good Doctor made a house call so to speak.. Well up next is...(crowd noise goes wild....) Oh boy, he's here it's the hometown hero, the boy who made good, it's the Dean ....
MATCH 9: THE DEAN VS. REVOLUTIONARY As the Dean makes his way to the ring the crowd goes crazy. In fact a very beautiful women leaps over the guard rail and well, shows the Dean her assessts and then procedes to lay a long weeeeet kiss on the Dean until Sunny pulls her off and slaps her and both women start going at it. Finally the Dean seperates the two women (the one from the crowd missing a good portion of her clothing) and the Dean continues to the ring high fiving everyone. Then out comes Revolutionary to boos and cups and drinks being thrown at him.
GS: Well looks like we know who the fan favorite is tonight. Well as we all know this is a match of honor. No punches, no cheap shots and so on, this is to be a wrestling match.
Both men lock up and the Dean is able to get a head lock but is then whipped off the ropes and Revolutionary is able to take the Dean down to the match with a drop toe hold and move on to a head lock. But finally the Dean is able to get to his feet where he is able to break the head lock when he suplex's the Revolutionary. The Dean then comes off the ropes and drops an elbow on the head of Rev and covers for the 1-2 but Rev is able to kickout.
The Dean then picks up Rev and delivers a back suplex and then covers for the 1- but Rev kicks out. The Dean picks up Rev and throws him off the ropes and goes for a back drop only Rev is able to lock on a back roll and get a 1-2 before the Dean can kickout. From there the match goes back and forth with both men staying within the rules of wrestling. Even at one point as Rev fell through the ring ropes the Dean did not go out after him and even held the ring ropes apart when Rev did come back in. The match continued on and as it hit the 20 minute mark both men were wearing down.
Rev had the Dean in a standing figure four, but the Dean was finally able to get to the ring ropes. Rev then whipped the Dean off the ropes and went for a back drop but the Dean was able to flip over and land on his feet. Where the Dean the delivered a belly to back suplex with a bridge and a cover for the 1-2-3. WINNER: THE DEAN. As both men got to their feet they looked at each other and then were about to shake hands when out of no where came Ed Salazar with a trash can lid. He slammed it into the head of the Dean who fell into Revolutionary. Ed then picked up Revolutionary and procedded to pile drive him. Ed then took the trash can lid and begin slamming it into the head of the Dean. He then layed the lid on the mat and ddt the Dean into the lid and then pile drove Revolutionary onto the lid. Finnally Shock Attack and Paralizer came running down and Ed Salazar exited the ring. Both Revolutionary and the Dean had to be carried from the ring...
GS: What in the hell has happen to Ed Salazar. I know Ed is upset that Revolutionary is ranked higher then him in the World Rankings, but this attack was uncalled for. The comish may have to look into this action...Well that brings us to our final match of the night as the no. 1 contenders for the tag belt go against the Metal Milita....
MATCH 10: METAL MILITA VS. DEATH'S DEAMONS RA: NOW COMING TO THE RING WITH THEIR MANAGER D.E. KILLAMAN, IS FACE OF DEATH AND TOMBSTONE, THEY ARE DEATH'S DEAMONS... THEIR OPPONETS TONIGHT ARE LARS AND JAMES, THEY ARE METAL MILITA.....
Face of Death and Lars start for each team and right off FoD drives a knee to the mid section. He then whips Lars off the rope and drives a boot to the face of Lars. FoD then picks up Lars and choke slams him down. He then picks up Lars and whips him into his own corner so that Lars can tag in James. James comes in and catches a double ax handle to the back by FoD.
He then gets a ddt and a cover for a 1-2 but James is able to kickout. FoD tags in Tombstone who comes over and picks up James and drives a shoulder into the jaw and then whips James off the ropes and hits him with a drop kick. Tombstone then picks up James and goes for a pile driver but James is able to back flip Tombstone over. James then tags in Lars who goes over and picks up Tombstone and delivers a suplex. Lars then comes off the ropes and drops an elbow down on Tombstone and covers for the 1-2 but Tombstone is able to kickout. Lars then picks up Tombstone, tags in James and they both land a double drop kick and James covers and gets a 1-2 but FoD drives a boot to the back of the head of James. D.E. then gets up on the ring apron and grabs the ref. FoD then goes over and hits Lars with a loaded fist knocking Lars down across the steel ring guard. Inside of the ring as Tombstone picks up James FoD assists as they apply a super tombstone piledriver. Then FoD goes outside after Lars while in the ring Tombstone delivers another piledriver. Tombstone gets to his feet and is about to go for the cover when Mitcla hits the ring and slams something into the back of the head of Tombstone and then lays James on top of Tombstone and then Mitcla exits the ring. The ref turns around and counts 1-2-3. WINNER: METAL MILITA. D.E. can't believe it and goes crazy and throws the ref over the top rope and then waves. Down the aisle comes Nitemare, D.I.G., Executioner, Sandman and Hell's Angels. They are begin to work over Lars and James. But then down the aisle comes Dark Rider, Grave Digger, Ghost Rider, Predator and the Outlanders and we have 16 men going at it in and around the ring. Dark Rider and Nitemare continue to battle while Ghost Rider and Executioner are really going at it as Executioner takes a mike cord and is choking Ghost Rider. The action continues as both teams battle to the back as you see Dr. Insane chasing D.E. Killaman through the crowd...
GS: Well what a way to end a night with all hell breaking loose. Looks like D.E. is finding out that life is hell and he has his hands full. Well folks that's a rap for tonight, we will see you monday night with 8 more matches and we will have an update on Lobo....
