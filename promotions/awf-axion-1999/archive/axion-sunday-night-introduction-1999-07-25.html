AWF Sunday Night! — Introduction

25 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2734 AWF SUNDAY NIGHT! Live! Walnut Creek Park, Winston-Salem, NC! ___________________________________ [...The Scene opens up on a cracked up open road. There are buildings on the sides. Some of them smoking. All of a sudden, a shadowy figure walks towards the camera screens. Two lamp posts light up only to show President Charles. Chuck places his hands in his suit...] Chuck Gemelli- Well fans, like I said....the Axion Wrestling Federation would soon make an outstanding comeback to the world of Proffesional Wrestling. And it seems as if we have picked the perfect timing for it all. For tonight you will witness action like you've waited for, for a long time. Action that only the AWF could surprise you with. This is the truth of extreme action....this is
AWF's Sunday Night.... [...Chuck spins in the other direction with a cocky look and begins walking away as the lamp posts fade, the cameras fade back onto a booth containing
President Chris, Imortal Rob, Scott Worthington, and "Masta Thug" Theo Verman ready for the Sunday Night! Introduction...] "The Imortal" Rob- Wow! It's so great to see the Axion Wrestling Federation make a comeback. This is going to be fantastic as hell. I've been with AWF since the beginning and it's time to see things work out for the best. Right now Scotty's Lord Of The Ring is doing hot! and it's in it's final rounds I heard huh? Well anyways fans the action is going to be good tonight, not great but good. Scott Worthington- Yep fans. One of the reasons the action won't be the greatest AWF will hold tonight is because this is AWF's re-opening. Lots of contract signings have been made. ::rubs fingers together:: Chuck with the money. And alot more to soon be added. But anyways, the AWF will try to open strong! Tonight's Main Event includes two of the AWF's top superstars....why you ask? Because I know both of these individuals very well...."Suicidal" Kydd
Riggs, an awesome competitor, goes one on one against "The Epic" Lord Infamous, another punk but he's a great athlete. "Masta Thug" Theo Verman- Bah, why don't both of your clowns shut up. Nobody can fight just like Thug can. We have a couple of Main Event Spotters tonight also..."Extreme" Eric Magnus goes one on one against "The Insane Assassin" Ian
Parker in a no disqualification matchup. Maxx Justice goes up against this character.....Sumo? Heh.....Maxx all the way on this one. Scott Worthington- Yeah, the action is going to be good. Notice how I said good. Anyways lets get ready for the action! Stay tuned.... [...Camera Fades to commercials...]
