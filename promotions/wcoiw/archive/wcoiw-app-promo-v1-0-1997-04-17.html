I am the former president of the WFSW, and I've been involved in e-wrestling for about a year and a half. Some of you may know me as [detail withheld] on IRC or as the handler of the Vegas Fighters. Anyway, WFSW was _extremely_ successful when it was around (ask any former WFSW handlers), but I was forced to close it due to time restraints from school and other things. However, this time around I'm working with four other people ([name withheld], [name withheld] [name withheld] and [name withheld]) and between the four of us we know we have what it takes to run a _great_ fed. -=-=- WCOIW INFO -=-=- * THE WCOIW WAREHOUSE Most of WCOIW's cards will be held at the WCOIW Warehouse in Chicago, IL. It's an old, broken down warehouse that President Kane has purchased to hold cards at. It is a very small place, with a seated section with 100 seats in the back, and general admission in the front (all around the arena) for another 150 or so people. The Warehouse has a Crow's Nest which is actually the remains of the condemned second floor-- there is a large factory lift that is used to get from the floor to the Crow's Nest. The bigger of the two is called Monday Night at the Warehouse. It will have around 6 or 7 matches each week, and it will also feature Crow's Nest interviews. Underground in Chi-Town is our Thursday card. It will have 3-4 dark matches as well as a main event match (sometimes 2). Flashes will be sent out a few times a week, and there will be a weekly newsletter-- more on that soon. * WCOIW TALENT DIVISIONS The WCOIW has three talent divisions. The Heavyweight Division, the Skyweight Division (under 230lbs.), and the Tag Team Division. There are four titles for the time being. They are the WCOIW Heavyweight Championship Title, the WCOIW Great Lakes Heavyweight Championship Title, the WCOIW Skyweight Championship Title and the WCOIW Tag Team Championship Titles. * WCOIW SUPERCARDS Every few months, a supercard will be held at the WCOIW Warehouse (or possibly a larger venue if we make enough money). They will be big cards (with a snappy title for the card) with a load of great matches and title defenses. We will also have smaller supercards occasionally. -=-=- WCOIW STAFF -=-=- * Fedheads/Bookers/Writers: - Heavyweight Division Bookers/Writers - [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) - [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) - [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) - Skyweight Division Booker/Writer - [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) - Tag Team Division Booker/Writer (and X-Plosion writer) - [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) -=-=- WCOIW IS BOOKED -=-=- That pretty much says it all. :) However we aren't one of those strict roleplaying feds that only looks at flashes to decide match outcomes. We prefer to look at the entire picture (angles, storylines, strats, etc) to decide. Although flashes _will_ have a lot to do with who gets pushed and who doesn't.
