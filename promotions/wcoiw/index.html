A Chicago warehouse promotion whose own founding handbook laid out three divisions, four titles and a hand-drawn venue floorplan before any result ever survived to confirm a single card was held. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Windy City Organization of Independant Wrestling posted its complete founding handbook to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 17 April 1997, calling itself “a new revolution in independant wrestling federations.” Its own president introduced himself as the former president of another fed, the WFSW, working this time with four more fedheads, and staked the WCOIW to a fictional home: the WCOIW Warehouse in Chicago, Illinois, described as “an old, broken down warehouse” with a hundred seats in the back and general admission in front for “another 150 or so people,” complete with a floorplan of its ring, seating, and a Crow's Nest reached by a converted factory lift.

The handbook named six wrestlers as its roster — Johnathan Shock, Lord Thomas Imperius, Lord Foul the Despiser, Hollywood, Johnny “Cool” Coles, and Chris Deagau — across three divisions (Heavyweight, Skyweight for wrestlers under 230 pounds, and Tag Team) contesting four championships. It planned two weekly cards, Monday Night at the Warehouse and a Thursday undercard, Underground in Chi-Town, plus occasional supercards at a larger venue “if we make enough money,” and called itself booked rather than a strict roleplay-judged promotion, deciding outcomes on the whole picture of angles, storylines, and submitted strategies together.

No card, result, or second document of its own survives. By June 1997 one of its five founders was already describing himself, in a different promotion's own founding post, as a former WCOIW co-president — the only trace anywhere in the surviving record of what became of it.
