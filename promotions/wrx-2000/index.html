Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Wrestling Revolution X billed its own recurring program, Saturday Night Rage, as graphic, weapons-heavy hardcore wrestling from the opening warning card on. The surviving broadcast was staged, by its own hosts' account, in the WRX Arena on 7 April 2000, though it did not reach the newsgroup until 1 August.

On screen, co-president Mike opened the show with a promo aimed at a rival figure called Outlaw and used the night to set up an approaching card he called Extreme Showdown, on 16 April, where several of the night's wrestlers would fight for a place.

A Scaffold Death Match saw “The Fallen Angel” Azrael fall from a rigged scaffold through a barbed-wire net and a set of tables before his opponent, billed on the way to the ring as “the only veteran that matters,” finished him with his own LeD aCHe finisher. The WRX Pacific Championship changed hands in a brawl fought through a supermarket set, where a wrestler billed as “Bad-Ass” pinned an interfering Shawn Ryder rather than his scheduled opponent, Jon Talon, to take the belt. An Electrified Barbed Wire Cage match, with the winner advancing toward the WRX Presidential Championship, went to “The Impact Player” Rob Stylz, who put H2O away with a Spiral Bomb through a table.

The broadcast closed with a tag match, Legacy and Shane Martin beating Jon Talon and Jesse Madrid to earn their own place at Extreme Showdown; Mike closed the show by booking Martin and Legacy into a three-way there against James “Saint Vyolence” on 16 April.

Whether that supercard was ever held, and any later card, result, or notice of closure, does not survive in this record.
