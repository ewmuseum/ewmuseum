I'm sure this lil' fight of ours, will hit him. And I wouldn't be surprised to see his alcoholic ass back in the AAW real soon." K. Carter: "Oh god, don't jinx us! And just show each and everyone of these wannabes filling the ranks of the AAW, what it's like to be a superstar! Because I am, the last true Icon." K. Damon Jeriko, Zeek Johnson, Steve Stone, Mark Stone, "Sundevil" Chris Revelb, Draco, Shocker, Triple S, darkSide. The AAW was a young fed, striving to become a top ranked fed. Unless those guys come back and we return the original impact players. I don't think the AAW will ever be the same. But that's not a bad thing, not at all!" K.
