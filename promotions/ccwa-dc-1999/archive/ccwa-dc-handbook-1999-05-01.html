-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [ TABLE OF CONTENTS ] -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [1] - Introduction [2] - Information on the CCWA [3] - CCWA Staff [4] - Joining the CCWA [5] - Application to the CCWA [6] - Tag Team Application [7] - Closing -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [ INTRODUCTION ] -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Welcome. Welcome to the CCWA. You still don't know what that stands for, do you??? Well, It's the Capital City Wrestling
Alliance. Or CCWA for Short. Still with us??? Good, you aren't stupid...That's a good start to joining the CCWA. But why should you join the CCWA???
Well, we are aiming to have a Roster of about 24 people when it is full. I don't expect this to happen via. My Posts on The Newsgroup immediantly, so I will be making up some jobbers...to give you a taste of the Cards that will be produced. And with Any Luck...
THEY WILL BE DAMM GOOD ONES!!! Now, I await you reading the Rest of this Handbook and stepping into the ring of the CCWA...Where Tradition Rules, And the Heavyweight
Championship is ALMOST CERTIANLY...A Wrestling One!!!
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [ INFORMATION ON THE CCWA ] -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [ Appendix A - CCWA Titles ] CCWA WASHINGTON DC CHAMPIONSHIP - The Biggest Title in the CCWA...The Washington D.C. Championship. This is the biggest Prize in the CCWA...if you have this, I guess you are offically the Man...With this Title, comes the Duty of defending it once every 3 weeks...but you also get the Bragging Rights!!! Universal weight limit.
CCWA CAPTAL HILL TITLE - The Second Biggest Titles...Defended every week on "Warped In Washington". This is a stepping stone to the Washington DC Championship, but it isin't just a stepping stone...It means that you are able to say you are a CHAMPION.
CCWA SUPERFLY TITLE - Cursierweight Championship. Defended weekly, This is for the Best Wrestler who is UNDER 231lbs in the
CCWA... CCWA WASHINGTON DC TAG TEAM TITLES - The Equivlent of The Washington DC Championship for Tag teams.
[ Appendix B - CCWA Shows ] WARPED IN WASHINGTON - The Weekly Show of the CCWA. Held on Sunday Evening, and it has a slot on A Local Cable Network from 1-2am Eastern Every Monday Morning, or Sunday Night, depending on how you view it...CCWA Capital Hill Title and Cursierweight Titles on The Line, and occasionally a CCWA
Washington DC Title on the Line...You may see old Stars, Such as Marty Janetty and Rick Martel make appearences on this show.
Roleplay Deadline is at 5pm Eastern on The Friday Before The Card. [ Appendix C - The CCWA Arena ] EVERY CCWA show is held from the CCWA Arena...This is based in Washington, DC. The capicity is 2500.
[ Appendix D - Supercards ] These are held every 6-8 weeks...much as I love to see them Monthy, that's WAY too many and 6-8 weeks is about right. These are going to have about 6 matches on them, just the Top Stars of the CCWA. NOT broadcast on pay Per View. The Upcoming one should be held in Early June. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [ MEET THE STAFF ] -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [ Appendix A - Harold B. Burke ] The Owner. The Booker. All Those Nice things that involve running you own Wrestling promotion. He's about 28, and has a nice little supply of cash that lets the Federation run Smoothly...a wrestling fan out and out, and not like Ted Turner...He knows that buisness...and that's why He Runs the CCWA.
[ Appendix B - John Clayton ] The Man who checks the Running of the Federation on a Day to day Basis. If he was in a Top Federation, he would be called the Commishoner. Here, we call him the President...And at times, he's just a kineving little bastard who sets things up, and Blames it on Harold Burke...and knowing him, he usually gets away with it.
[ Appendix C - Eric Epstien ] The Main Play by play Commentator in the CCWA. He's nice, polite, and a general good guy all round. Fans like him...He's a great
Commentator, and once was called the 'Real' Eazy E because of his laid back approach to his Commentary.
[ Appendix D - 'Ice Cold' Levi Lenten ] He used to be a Wrestler...He's not adverse to getting into fights... He's a former World Champion in 3 Organisations...Hey, Did I mention he's a heel, and a Real Asshole of a Color Commentator at that???
[ Appendix E - James Walsh ] Good Sense of humor, sick as it may be...This guy is an Interviewer, a joker...and against Heel Wrestler, that's a Bad Combo. He insults people, but it's not serious...he's a runt, so when he offends somebody, a hospital Bed Awaits...

CATEGORY!!!)- Intelligence- Ring Awareness- Background- The More Detail, The More points you will get in the Category Above Gimmick- The More Detail, The More Points you will get in the Category 2

If you give your Wrestler a lot of High Flying Martial Arts Skills, and write a Strat for you Match saying you want to take down the much larger opponent with Kick to The Legs and High flying Skills, you will do better because it is all linking up.
OTOH, if you have a Big Guy, but say you want to Wrestle a Fast paced Match, the Start is not very good...The More Points, The More options are open to you and the Easier it is to do better in Strat Writing and therefore in your Carrear.
