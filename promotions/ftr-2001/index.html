Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

FTR introduced itself to the newsgroup on 2 May 2001 already claiming a “2+ year history and very strong RP'ers,” and pointed readers to its own site, ftrwrestling.com, to sign up.

It went quiet and came back on 4 October 2002 calling itself “an e-fed with a rich 3+ year history” returning from a seven-month hiatus, moved off simulator-decided results toward matches settled by a vote of at least three trusted members, and listed Brock Lesnar among the wrestlers still open for a new writer to pick up.

A card called Rampage, billed as “the FTR's first card in the Rebirth Era,” went up at its site on 30 October 2002. Nothing further survives: no result from Rampage itself, no later card, and no notice of how or when FTR closed.
