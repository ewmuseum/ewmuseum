iW — Founding FAQ

International Wrestling, seeking new blood...

16 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi guys, I'm Alan, owner of iW: International Wrestling. We're just starting out and are looking for some new people to come and join our ranks. I've been running feds since July '99 and think, with some great help from the team I've assembled thus far, that with iW we may have created the best basis for a promotion yet. Now all we need is you! Come check out the site at http://iw.shadowsoftheempire.co.uk and have a look around. Alternatively I've included the FAQ below, to answer a few of your questions... Thanks for reading, Al Frequently Asked Questions What is iW and how is it run? International Wrestling (iW) is an e-fed, a fantasy wrestling federation. It is a booked fed, with role-playing determining the push that your character gets. A new applicant will usually enter iW at the bottom of the ladder, and only quality role-plays will help that wrestler to climb the ladder, get into high profile feuds and gain title shots. The outcome of matches is determined by the owner and the bookers/graders. (see "behind the scenes" page) In addition to this, iW is a semi-themed federation, harking back to the classic days of the old WWF. We consider ourselves, in many ways, to be stuck in the 1980's and like our characters to be big, colourful and over the top! Also our storylines will not head down the hardcore route too often, cage matches being a rarity etc. How are the results determined? Each role-play is judged for Relevance, Enjoyment, Grammar and Overall Look/Style. Basically, Quality over Quantity! What shows are there? At the moment there is a weekly show, iW Tonight! The show will take place every Monday. There will also be one SuperCard Show at the end of every two month tour. (see below) When will results be posted? Tonight results should be viewable by Monday nights, as they should be posted late on Sunday's or early Monday morning. Any future PPV's will be posted some time during Sundays, most likely during the evening. (BRITISH TIME!) There may be delays in some weeks, due to work commitments or technical problems, but we will try and keep these delays to a minimum and let you know when to expect the show. What's this about Tours? Basically iW will embark on a series of global tours. Each tour lasts for 2 months and will consist of seven iW Tonight TV Tapings and one iW Supercard Show! Each tour will come from a different Country/Region, with SuperStars jetting from the UK to the US and Canada, Mexico, Australia, Japan, South Africa and Europe! How does the Contract System Work? A SuperStar will be contracted to 2, 6, 12, 18 or 24 months. In other words, 1, 3, 6, 9 or 12 tours. By filling in an application you are signing a contract, allowing us to use your character's name and likeness for the specified period of time. If you should leave before the contract is up we will continue to use your character in any way we choose until the end date. That is, of course, unless you come to us and ask nicely to leave, in which case we will happily cancel your contract. The contract starts the day you submit the App! Can I contribute to the shows? Yes, please do. In fact, the more you contribute, the more money you could earn (see below)! If you want to do an on-show interview or character spotlight then e-mail it to us at the owners address (see "behind the scenes") and we'll try and use it somewhere. But make sure that you get this into us by Saturday lunchtime at the latest, so it can be worked into the show properly. And if you have something to go on the website, like a column for iW Xtra, then please send it to us and we will use it. What Titles are there? There are currently three titles in iW! The International Championship, the Continental Championship and the Junior Championship. We're waiting to see how things go before creating a tag-team title, but one may well come in time. The same applies for a women's title. Can I join the staff of iW? If you think that you have what it takes to write exciting matches, come up with good storylines etc. then let us know, with examples of your work and we will consider making you a part of the team. Also check the "behind the scenes" page, if there is "n/a" next to a job title then we are looking for someone to fill that role, could it be you? E-mail us! Where is iW based? Well, in real life, I'm based in Southampton, England. But, as far as shows go, we like to travel the globe. You may find that we'll be in the UK for a tour, then do a quick trip around Europe before spending the summer in the US. Could be that we're in Australia, Japan, South Africa or Canada. We could even pop along to Mexico! Contracts The idea of contracts is that you can earn money, which will put you on our "Rich List". Can you become the highest paid iW SuperStar? There will also be chances to spend this money. You may want to change a stipulation of an upcoming match, pay off a booker to get a certain opponent, or title shot. Maybe buy your way into the iW ShopZone, having your own T-Shirt? This side of things is still being worked out... so may well change in time. Basic Wage Upon first entering iW a SuperStar's wage is £1000 per week. Then we adopt this scale: 5 wins = £500 per week increase 10 wins = £500 per week increase 20 wins = £750 per week increase 30 wins = £750 per week increase 50 wins = £1750 per week increase So the more you win, the more you earn. In addition to this, when a contract is re-negotiated a SuperStar is entitled to a 5% pay rise. Bonus System The following are your initial bonuses: Match Scheduled : £250 Title Match Scheduled : £350 Match Won by Pinfall/Submission : £200 Match Won by Countout / DQ : £100 Match Won by foul means : £200 fine Title Match Win : £1000 Title Match Successful Defense : £500 Interview Segment Appearance : £150 Backstage Segment Appearance : £100 Minor Injury Inflicted : £500 fine Serious Injury Inflicted : £1000 fine Staff Member Injured : 1 Weeks Basic Wage fine These bonuses are up for negotiations at the beginning of every tour (2 months) and at the point of re-negotiating a new contract. iW are willing to give more money in bonuses, as long as the SuperStar in question is willing to give up something in return, ie reduce his Basic Wage etc. How a SuperStar goes about these negotiations is entirely down to him/her! Other Clauses A SuperStar may negotiate for other items to be added to his contract, ie Title Shots or guaranteed matches against certain opponents. How a SuperStar goes about negotiating these Clauses is down to them, much like the Bonus System. But iW will not dish out Title Shots to just anyone. Deserving shots will be given as appropriate. Those who are not getting shots will have to give something up in order to get one!! General Rules Have fun: There is no greater law within the community than this. Your first, last and only goal should be to have fun. And if, for a moment you begin not to have fun, you should retire that very moment. For we can do nothing but agitate you then. Be active: This is a very important rule. Without your activity we have no community. Without a community then we have no iW. Get involved with the community on the OOC board Be reasonable: We try, we really do. But sometimes we can't accommodate everything. We know you may want a title shot, but understand that everyone can't have a shot, and there may be someone more deserving than you for whatever reason. In similar terms, if you write a strat for an event and we edit it, alter it slightly, don't get mad. We're just trying to take as much as we can from what everybody wants. Respect everything: At iW HQ we're big on respect. That means respect them when it comes to OOC disputes and handle things maturely. Respect other member feds, and don't waltz into them and post "Your Fed Sucks" posts without giving them a name and a site to fire back on. Don't mess with other guys characters unless you have their express permission. Don't use racial slurs, keep sexual and religious connotations to a minimum. Some folks may not like them. And most of all, respect the rules Don't break any criminal laws OOC: Do not threaten, harass, blackmail, intimidate or verbally abuse any other handler OOC. While perfectly acceptable in character, this tends to break rule one. Avoid "sick" crimes IC: We frown on things like that. Your wrestler smokes a joint now and then? Fine. You want to murder a character in your role-plays? Fine. Go ahead. But for God's sake don't detail a disemboweling or something similar. That's just disgusting. Same goes for rape, child molestation and a whole host of other deals. There will be no warning on this one, I will bring down punishment for a violation of anything that I have said here. If it's something borderline, I will inform you, add it to my list and expect that you won't put it up again. This breaks rule one for me. We are KAYFABE: That means we don't know that bookers determine the results of the events, we don't know that wrestling is fake, we don't believe there is any behind the scenes activities at all. You'll notice this is the last rule. There's a very good reason for this. We want you to push this as much as you can without breaking it. It's one of the traits of our community. This is one we want bent - but not too much. Application Rules Be Original! This is, and by far, the most important point you must achieve. Too many e-wrestlers look and think alike these days. There are, in my opinion, way too many Steve Austin and Undertaker look-alikes. I have nothing wrong with that, as characters can share some points with real wrestlers, but the key to be original is to find something that will make your character UNIQUE. He must have something in his way of reacting, in his gimmick, which will set him apart from the pack. This applies not only in the character, but in the way he talks, acts, dresses, etc... Try to find some cool quotes that only your guy will say. No matter if your wrestler is inspired from a real character or if he's your own idea, make sure he does have that touch of originality, something that only he/she will have, that will make them special. Be Honest! Too many people want their guys to be perfect, almost like they were invincible demigods. The problem is those people never want to admit they can have flaws. The truth is, nobody's perfect, even though everybody would want to be. This is one way to be original and unique, as well. You must look at your characters' personalities from both sides. Of course, a wrestler's weakness can be used against him, but the simple fact you do admit your weaknesses also gives you an extra push with the fed head, as that's the essence of role-playing. Don't fear to admit your character's flaws, because it will make him only more interesting and it will also highlight his strengths. Fill in all areas of the application form if you can. If you want to create a tag-team from two existing singles wrestlers then also fill in a tag-team application form. Roleplaying Rules All role-play's to be posted on the official iW forums - you can link to a web page with your rp if you wish. You have to have been accepted into iW before you can post, non-members messages will be deleted. You must register with the board, and it would make things much easier for us if your username was your character name. Or in the case of tag-teams, your team name. Role-plays can take any form you like, whether it is like an in-ring interview, a pre-recorded piece, an interview with a staff member backstage or anything along these lines. You should aim to role-play at least once a week, not role-playing will affect your push. As a general rule try to only role-play once a day top, more than this is overkill. Keep cursing to a minimum. Excessive swearing is an annoyance and doesn't add much to a role-play. Racial slurs will not be tolerated under any circumstance. Anyone found breaking this rule will be given a warning, then a final warning, and then their contract will be terminated. Make sure that CAPS LOCK IS TURNED OFF when you write your role-plays! Writing a role-play in all capitals is plain old stupid, and bloody annoying to read! Plus it will affect your score. Huge and capital letters should only be used to indicate your character screams. Don't overdo it! Put important words in bold or italic Imagine how would your wrestler actually talk, and write it like you see it. Try to put some emotion in there. Try to be innovative, and start trends. Don't jumble everything together in one big 5K paragraph. It's a little bit harder to read and doesn't look very good. Write in multiple paragraphs, it will definitely help you out. Remember what you are writing, an interview like the ones you see each week on RAW and SmackDown!. Do not make them too long, but don't make them too short either. Make them interesting, funny and make good use of the information the president gives you before your match. If you have a strategy that you want to use or an idea for an attack or storyline advancement then e-mail the president and explain what you would like to happen, with good reasoning! Then just sit back and see if your ideas are deemed acceptable to use. If you give a good enough reason, and it would further the angle you are involved in, then the events are more likely to be used.
