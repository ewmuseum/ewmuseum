RMWA Overdrive, 8 February 1998 (Part 4 of 4)

February 8, 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<Camera fades in> JS: We are ready for our MAIN event on this edition of OVERDRIVE, and it should be a dandy.
CK: I actually gotta agree with you on that statement. We got two men who do NOT like each other in Stan Silvers and Dan Travis. And The Diamondback has not been a happy camper since Stan Silvers called him trash then saved Simon Brock from Dan a few weeks back. Travis will definitely be out for blood tonight.
JS: On the opposite end of the spectrum is "The Doc" Stan Silvers. A man who vowed to clean the RMWA up of what he called "Trash" that litters the rings of this federation. Seems like Silvers has taken it upon himself to be the self appointed sheriff of the RMWA, but tonight he takes on the #1 Most Wanted Villain in the RMWA in Dan Travis. It should be a epic battle! Lets get down to Dave Ritter for the formalities.
Dave Ritter: Ladies and gentlemen, at this time we have a special guest commentator for the next match-up............let me introduce, accompanied by his valet the lovely Debra Williams........."SUPERSTAR" SIMON BROCKKKKKK!!!
(Brock and Debra burst through the curtain and he raises his fists to a chorus of boos from the fans. He seems almost oblivious to that fact that the fans hate him and he kneels in the entrance way and poses for the crowd as Debra stands behind him smiling. The couple pose for a few photos before Brock heads down to the ringside area and Debra makes her way back to the locker room)
JS: What the hell is this?!? Who invited him down here?!? CK: Uhhhh, hey the mans a star! You should be honoured! JS: (rolls his eyes) I should have known! (Brock reaches the broadcast booth and shakes hands with Chad but ignores JS. He takes his seat and puts on the headset)
Brock: Hey Chad, lets get this thing underway! (DING*DONG*THE*WITCH*IS*DEAD) CK: What the hell was that? Dave Ritter: Making his way to the ring first hailing from the Lonestar state, Houston to be exact. He stands at six feet nine inches and weighs in at 350 pounds....
"DIAMONDBACK" DAN TRAVIS! ("The Devil Went Down to Georgia" by the Charlie Daniels Band starts to crank up in the arena, after a few seconds of the song playing "Diamondback"
Dan Travis emerges from the curtains and is welcomed with a chorus of boo's while he makes his way to the ring. This monster is wearing Black and blue wrestling tights, red boots and a vest to the ring. His slow stroll to the ring is a bit menacing, but Holloway waits in the ring unimpressed. Dan has brown hair and blue eyes, with a constant scowl on his face. He has several scars on his arms and chest, from knives, bullets and various other implements of destruction that have been used on him in the past. There is one scar on his face, from the bottom of his left ear to about two thirds of the way to his chin; he got it from a broken beer bottle. He is massive and powerfully built. Dan finally makes it into the ring and is staring at the entrance way waiting for Stan Silvers to make his way to the ring.)
Dave Ritter: AAAnnnnd his opponent....hailing from Denver, Colorado tipping the scales at 250lbs and standing 6 feet 4 inches......accompanied by his manager Walk Withers......ladies and gentlemen.....
STAN "THE DOC" SILVERS! ("Another One Bites the Dust" by Queen blares over the PA and the crowd start to cheer for the hometown boy. Silvers and Withers burst through the curtains and Silvers raises his arm to even more rapturous cheers from the crowd. They make their way down the aisle with Silvers slapping a few hands but keeping his eyes trained on Travis at all times. He has a mean look on his face and looks like he really means business. He reaches the ring and rolls underneath the ropes and awaits the bell, Withers takes up his customery post on the outside.)
(DING DING DING) JS: Alright were underway! the two lock up, Travis throws Silvers into the corner, he charges in with a big elbow which rocks the head of Silvers back! Silvers comes out of the corner and Travis smashes him face first into the mat!
CK: haha good start from Silvers, hey Simon, you were up against Travis a couple of weeks ago, what was your impression of him.
Brock: Uhhh, well Travis is a tough guy, but as a wrestler he'd be outdone by a baboon. The only reason he beat me was because I had to watch for Debra as well. I cant say I ever talk to him, I prefer people who can string more than 3 words together. Silvers is a useless scrub too, I hope they beat each other into oblivion!
JS: Silvers does a kip up back to his feet! He nails Travis flush on the jaw with a big right hand! Travis's head rocks back....Silvers seizes the advantage and applies a nerve hold right to the neck of Travis.........................he's really wearing the big man down..........Travis making his way to his feet......elbow to the midsection from Travis! And another one! A third breaks the nerve hold! Travis shakes the cobwebs out......Silvers has a swing at Travis but Travis sidesteps! Iron claw from Travis.....he forces Silvers down to his knees!........................The fans are rallying behind The Doc!.........the referee checks the arm:
...............it drops once! ...............it drops twice! .................NOO!! Silvers keeps his arm up on the thrid occasion and starts to fight his way outta this clawhold!.....He's back to his feet........reverse knife edge chop! and another one! and another one! Travis releases the claw! Silvers spins his around....SLEEPERHOLD FROM SILVERS! he's trying to send Travis to the land of the fairies......NO! Travis runs backwards and clobbers Silvers against the turnbuckle.
CK: Well, both guys trying to wear down the other down early on here, its pretty obvious Silvers took the worst of that, Travis out weighs him by 100lbs!
Brock: 100lbs of fat though don't forget...... JS: Travis picks up Silvers and whips him against the ropes. Silvers come back......Travis nails him with a fist to the midsection which takes the wind out of Silvers......he hooks him up...DDT FROM TRAVIS!!.....Silvers looks like he took a pretty bad fall on his neck there...
Brock: ahh poor guy! maybe he should crawl outta the ring and go back to digging up coal in the rocky mountains!
CK: hahaha JS: Silvers never worked as a miner and you know it! Travis picks him off the matt and gives him two big elbows to the back of the neck, he hooks him in a front face-lock and tries to turn him! REVERSE NECKBREAKER FROM TRAVIS!! he's really going after the neck of Silvers. Travis picks him up again.....kick to the midsection......he runs against the ropes.....FLYING BULLDOG FROM TRAVIS!! The cover:
1........... 2.......... NO!!!! CK: Lucky begger.... JS: Silvers rolled that shoulder right at the last minute.... Travis is annoyed now, he picks Silvers up and throws him into the ropes.....he goes for a clothesline...BUT MISSES......Silvers comes back with an inverted atomic drop and Travis staggers backwards in pain! Silvers moves in for the follow up.....OHHH!!! thumb right in the eye from Travis stops Silvers dead in his tracks.
CK: That there was one of my favourite ever moves when I was in the ring! JS: Didn't The Amazing Bob block that move from you and beat you with a small package right after in your final match?
CK: Huh?!? JS: Maybe that was someone else, Travis scratches the back of Silvers with those long fingernails.....another one of your favourite moves CK, he throws Silvers against the ropes....TILT-A-WHIRL BACKBREAKER ATTEMPT!.....YES! HE GOT IT.....Silvers crashes to the mat......Travis picks him up and throws him against the ropes again, he's going for another tilt-a-whirl backbreaker......he gets him up.....NO!! Silvers spins out of it and lands on his feet! Silvers runs off the ropes, clothesline from Travis.....misses!......Silvers keeps running.......clothesline from Silvers.....misses.......OHHHH!! Silvers comes back off the ropes and he and Travis hit head to head.....both men go down!
Brock: haha what a couple of morons! This is funnier than Friends! CK: Don't you think Silvers looks a bit like Jennifer Anniston? Brock: I think you might have something there CK, I think its the hair..... JS: (rolls his eyes) I cant believe you said that!...... Both men struggle back to their feet....Travis grabs Silvers in a one handed choke!........he grabs him with the other hand and turns it into a lifting choke!...........the ref finally begins to count:
1..... 2..... 3..... 4...Travis throws Silvers to the mat and grins evily at the referee.... CK: If I were the ref I wouldn't get in Dan Travis's face... Brock: I think if I was this red I'd have ended it all by now, and I don't mean the match! Could you imagine being that ugly?!?
CK: hahaha JS doesn't have to imagine! JS: Oh my Lord, why me?!?.......Travis hooks up Silvers.............PILEDRIVER!!! PILEDRIVER!!! OH NO! THIS ONES OVER:
1.......... 2.......... NOOOO!!!!!! JS: Silvers kicks out at the last second to remain in this match! Silvers and Travis make their way back to their feet. Travis swings at him with a big roundhouse right which dazes Silvers. Travis runs into the ropes and comes back at Silvers.....goes for a clothesline but Silvers ducks! Silvers runs against the opposite ropes......Travis ducks for a back body drop but Silvers sees it coming!! WOW!! SPINNING DDT!!!! COVER HIM STAN!!
1.......... 2........... 3..NNNNNNNOOOOO!!!! JS: I don't know how he managed it but the stunned Dan Travis rolls his shoulder right at the last split second.....
Brock: Tsk, tsk, I thought you guys were supposed to be impartial, I sure as hell am, I hate them both!
CK: Just ignore him, I hear he's getting replaced soon anyway.... JS: (rolls his eyes) Both men back to their feet, Silvers nails him with a hard right hand....OHH!! Travis clobbers him with a forearm to that weakened neck and Silvers crumples to the mat. Travis picks him up and puts him on the top rope.... UH OH!! YOU KNOW WHATS COMING HERE!!
CK: Night night Doc.... JS: THERE IT IS!! THE DIAMOND DRILL!! THE TOP ROPE BRAINBUSTER, PUT IT IN THE BOOK! TRAVIS RESTS A HAND ON SILVER'S CHEST!!!
Brock: Amateur..... 1.......... 2.......... 3.....NOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!! JS: OH MY GOD!! STAN SILVERS ROLLS THE SHOULDER RIGHT AT THE LAST MINUTE!!! WHAT COURAGE, WHAT GUTS, WHAT FIGHTING SPIRIT FROM STAN SILVERS........
CK: haha maybe, but he's now practically dead! You might as well ring the bell now referee, he's finished.
JS: Dan Travis now smiling!?!.......he raises both arms and advances on Stan Silvers......OH NO! I think we know what's coming now!!
Brock: Constrictor time....you know, I never gave up to that move, the referee stopped it just as I was about to escape, the standard of officiating in the RMWA really is pathetic....
JS: TRAVIS WOULD HAVE BROKEN YOUR NECK IF THE REF HADN'T STOPPED IT AND SILVERS RUN IN TO SAVE YOU!!
CK: Call the match hothead! JS: Dan Travis moves into position for the Constrictor....he hooks the arms of Silvers and lifts him.....
Brock: I can hear him give up already.......... JS: NNNNNNNNNOOOOOOOO!!!! STAN SILVERS SPINS OVER AND OUT OF THE CONSTRICTOR AS TRAVIS LIFTED HIM UP!!!!!!!!! TRAVIS HASN'T GOT A CLUE WHAT HAPPNENED!!! SILVERS IS DAZED.......BUT HE KICKS TRAVIS IN THE MID SECTION....HE HOOKS THE ARMS............TTTTTTIIIIIGGGEEERRR DDDRRRIIIVVVEERRR!!!!!!! COME ON STAN!!!!!!
1.......... 2.......... 3......ITS OVE......NNNNNNNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! JS: FOOT ACROSS THE BOTTOM ROPE SAVES DAN TRAVIS JUST AS THE REFEREE WAS SLAPPING THE MAT FOR THE THRID TIME!!!!
Brock: Come on! When will these morons learn to hook the damn leg so that doesnt happen! Am I the only one in the RMWA with any sense?!?
JS: THE CROWD ARE GOING ABSOLUTELY BESERK!!!!! I DON'T THINK EITHER MAN KNOWS WHERE HE IS.....THERES AN ALMIGHTY CHANT OF 'SILLLLLVERS' RINGING ROUND THE ARENA......ITS DEAFENING!!! BOTH MEN ARE OUT ON THEIR FEET......TRAVIS SWINGS AND MISSES.........RAKE TO THE EYES BY SILVERS!!!!! HE HOOKS HIM IN A HALF NELSON!!!!!!!
CK: OH GOD NOOOOOOOO!!! JS: DOC-PLEX!!!! DOC-PLEX!!!! DOC-PLEX!!!! DOC-PLEX!!!! DOC-PLEX!!!! ITS GOTTA BE OVER!! WHAT AN UPSET!!!!
1.......... 2.......... 3.........YYYYYEEEE.......NNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Brock: COME ON! ONE OF YOU WIN FOR GOD'S SAKE!! JS: I DON'T BELIEVE IT, WHAT RESILIENCE FROM DAN TRAVIS!!! GIVE THE MAN HE'S DUES HE'S BEEN BATTERED NEARLY AS MUCH AS SILVERS BUT HE'S STILL ABLE TO KICK OUTTA THE DOC-PLEX!!!! FOLKS I THINK IM HAVING A HEART ATTACK......
CK: First piece of good news I've had all night.......... Brock: Lets hope this match lasts a little longer then.... JS: (panting) STAN SILVERS IS BACK UP, DAN TRAVIS IS BACK UP........SILVERS KICKS HIM IN THE STOMACH......SILVERS OFF THE ROPES.....MISSES WITH A CLOTHESLINE!!!..........SILVERS COMES BACK AND TRIES FOR ANOTHER CLOTHESLINE.....NOO!! TRAVIS CATCHES THE ARM..................OH WAIT A MINUTE!!! TRAVIS PULLS SILVERS BACK TO BACK WITH HIM!!!!! HE HOOKS THE OTHER ARM!!! NOOO!!!! TRAVIS LIFTS HIM!!!!!!
CK: HAHAHAHA AT LAST!!!!!......... JS: THE CONSTRICTOR!!!!!! TRAVIS HAS IT LOCKED ON!!!!!!!!.................................................................. OHHHHHHHHH!!!!! SILVERS IS FIGHTING!!!! HE WONT GIVE IT UP.....................................MY GOD HE'S BEEN IN THIS A LONG TIME, THE REF IS ASKING HIM BUT HE JUST WONT GIVE UP.....................................HE'S PASSED OUT!!! THE REF RAISES THE ARM!!!!
1.......... 2.......... 3.......... OOOHHHHH I CAN'T BELIEVE IT!!! ITS OVER!!!! (DING DING DING) Announcer: LLLLLLadies and gentlemen, the winner of the match............"DIAMONDBACK" DAN TRAVIS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
Brock: About time too...... (The crowd go ABSOLUTELY WILD with boos, litter hits the ring and hundreds of little kids are crying their little eyes out. "The Devil Went Down to Georgia" by the Charlie Daniels Band starts to play over the PA, but is turned off when it becomes apparent that the action isn't over.....)
JS: OH WAIT A MINUTE!!! DAN TRAVIS ISNT LETTING GO OF THE CONSTRICTOR!!!! STAN SILVERS IS OUT COLD, HE'S NOT MOVING, COME ON!!!! HE'S KILLING THE GUY!!!! GET SOME SECURITY OUT HERE!!!!!!! LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, MY VOICE IS GOING......
CK: Oh what a damn shame....... ..... ..... ..... ..... ..... Brock: Right I'm off outta here, later CK, uhhhh you know I'd help Silvers but I'm late for a dentist appointment you know.....
CK: Sure I understand.....later man.... (Brock puts down his headset and walks to the back seemingly oblivious to what is happening to the man who saved him last week, Stan Silvers at the hands of Dan Travis. The crowd boo like hell until he goes through the curtain..)
