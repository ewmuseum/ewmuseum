RMWA Avalanche (Part 4 of 10)

March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jeff Stewart: We are about to get set for the first ever tag team titles match. The winner of this match will go down forever in the books as number one. When they are dead and gone people will remember these guys as the first ever tag champions of the world.
"Chillin" Chad Killian: You are so melodramatic Jeff. But the fact remains this is for the RMWA Tag Championship. This match has a little bit of everything. Mystery, Hype, and Raggin Psychos.
Jeff Stewart: Can Micheal Halloway and his new found partner be enough to stop the mad men from the moutians, The Raving Psychos?
"Chillin" Chad Killian: Well Jeff, we will soon find out... Jeff Stewart: Lets go to Dave Ritter, for the introduction. Dave Ritter: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following bout if scheduled for one fall, fight to the finish, for the RMWA WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP!!!!! Now coming to the ring, Challangers number one.
["Trigger Happy Jack" by Poe blares over the PA. Raving Psycho Mondo and
Raving Psycho Mucho step from the back and walk to the ring.]
Dave Ritter: Accompanied by Randy Blaze. Hailing from the state metal instatution in Colorado Springs, CA. They stand at 6'5, They are.....Raving
Psycho Mondo and Raving Psycho Mucho!! MAYBE THE NEXT WORLD TAG TEAM
CHAMPIONS........ THE RAVING PSYCHOS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Mixed reaction from the crowd] Jeff Stewart: Well both these men look serious. This is all business tonight in the Raving Psychos Camp.
"Chillin" Chad Killian: Now its time for the moment that everyone has been waiting for, The unveiling of the NEW partner of Micheal Halloway. I wonder who it could be?
Jeff Stewart: I dont know but I sure am excited. "Chillin" Chad Killian: *Looks at Stewart sliding his chair over a little bit.* Dave Ritter: And the second team in this match... ["Alien Nation" by Scorpion starts.] Dave: Now being led to the ring, by Steve Edwin Borden. He stands at 6'5 and weighs in at 275 lbs.
MICHEAL HALLOWAY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Music stops then, "Sudorific" by In Strict Confidence starts.] And his partner! He stands at 6'2 and weighs in at 239 lbs. From Rochester,
New York. The mystery man..
DR. HATE!!!!!!!!!!! Jeff Stewart: This is certainly a suprise. Look at this guy. The intensity on him unbelieve able.
CK: I know I wouldn't want to meet him in a dark alley. Jeff Stewart: Well it looks like Raving Psycho Mondo and Micheal Holloway are gonna start this one.
{{Ding Ding Ding}} Jeff Stewart: Mondo has started this one and speared Holloway in the corner with a football tackle. Mondo grabs Halloway by the hair and slings him across the ring.
"Chillin" Chad Killian: This is not the way Halloway and his new partner Dr. Hate want to start this match. Jeff Stewart: Mondo is on the attack big time now, and starts to lay the boots into the chest of Halloway. Mondo raises his foot, I think that he is gonna try to put a hole right through Halloways chest! Halloway quickly rolls to safety outside of the ring.
"Chillin" Chad Killian: Smart thinking from Halloway, lucky for him and the janitors that would have had to clean that mess up.
JS: Halloway walks around to his corner and has a conference with his manager
Steve Borden. Good move from Halloway that could lead to a bitter end to
Halloways chances at winning the belt. Mondo is moved back into his corner and
Halloway re-enters the ring. Here comes Mondo again with that agressive attack. LARIAT FROM HALLOWAY! He caught Mondo right in the throat with that almost knocking him out of his boots.
CK: That sent chills up my spine. You could accually see Mondo's neck snapped back. JS: Halloway is right on the attack. Big Knee drop from Halloway across the forehead of Mondo. Halloway is a big man and that had to hurt. Halloway rises to his feet and throws his fist into the air.
Crowd: YEAHHHHH!!!!!! CK: Not smart thinking from Halloway, he had Mondo down and now its time to put him away. Instead he takes time out of the match to appease the peaons.
This could serious jeopardize his chances at winning the straps. JS: I think you're right because that has given time for Mondo to tag his partner Mucho. Halloway turns smack dap into a double clothesline from the
Raving Psychos and they are going to work. Double elbow drop down across the chest of Halloway. The ref finally regains order and shows Mondo out of the ring but the damage has already been done. Mucho lifts Halloway up by his hair and hoist the big man upon his shoulder, Running POWER SLAM from MONDO!!!!!!!!
Cover... 1......... 2.............. 3..NO!!! Dr. Hate makes the save. CK: Well, Dr. Hate must have had some formal training before he came to the
RMWA. He knew enough to risk it when his partner was in trouble.
JS: Though the pin was not made, Halloway is still in a prone position flat on his back. Mucho bounces off the ropes and drops be big leg across the throat.
Mucho lifts Halloway up to his feet and launches him to the other corner. CK: These Raving Psychos are massive. They are throwing Halloway around like a rag doll and he is 270 +.
JS: Mucho drives the elbow onto the top of Halloways forehead then reaches over and tags his partner Mondo. Mondo quickly in the ring grabs Halloway in an arm bar.
CK: Ya know this match has pretty much has been all Raving Psychos. We haven't seen much more then a knee drop from Halloway.
JS: Mondo irish whips Halloway into the ropes. Attempted leapfrog by Mondo...SPINEBUSTER!!!!!! Halloway came from out of no where with that spine buster. CK: Now is time for Halloway to get to his corner. JS: And that is exactly what he is doing. Halloway on hands and knees begins to crawl towards his corner. Mondo is still down from the spinebuster and is starting to stir a little. Halloway makes the tag and Dr. Hate is in the ring.
Mucho runs in and catches a drop kick into the mouth from Dr. Hate he's down! Dr. Hate runs to the corner. Mondo slowly to his feet...SUPERKICK!!!!! CK: You could hear that pop all the way to downtown Denver! Hate with a quick cover... 1.......... 2..................... JS: NO!!! Big kick out from Mondo. Hate rolls from the power of the kickout and is now back to his feet. Hate like a cat is up to the top turnbuckle.
Mondo slowly turns. Highcross body! BUT MONDO CATCHES HIM! What power! Backbreaker from Mondo! That quickly put an end to that stint of agression from the new comer Dr. Hate. Hate is down on the mat clutching his back. Mondo smartly tags in Mucho who locks a strong inverted choke hold around the neck of Dr. Hate.
<<2 minutes pass>> CK: Man, Dr. Hate has been in that for a while. I think that he might be out. The ref is going to check. He lifts his hand once....twice....Wait a minute? Borden is up on the ropes yelling at the apron. I wonder whats goin on there. JS: Halloway has a chair!!! HE JUST CLOCKED MUCHO IN THE HEAD WITH THAT CHAIR! Mucho seems unphased but has released the hold turning towards Halloway. CK: I dont think that Halloway so much cared for knocking him out, but he wanted to break that hold, Dr. Hate was obviously going out.
JS: This is giving Dr. Hate some much needed rest time, giving him the strength to pull himself back to his feet. Mucho has Halloway by the throat.
Dr. Hate jumps on Muchos back and starts to wail away! Mucho down to one knee, drop kick to the back of the head from Dr. Hate. Dr. Hate with a quick tag out to Halloway who drives the knee into the face of Mucho. Mondo is kind of out of it staring at bug that has crawled into the ring and isnt paying attention.
CK: It's double clubberin from Halloway and Dr. Hate. JS: Mucho is down in the corner. Halloway is in and drives the knee hard into the side of Muchos face. Halloway lifts Mucho up by the head. Halloway lifts the big man, Verticle Suplex into the cover.
1........ 2................NO! CK: You are gonna have to do alot more then a verticle suplex to beat this big man. Halloway is visably upset by this and starts to slam Muchos head hard into the mat.
JS: Halloway lifts Mucho up and whips him into the ropes. Jumping Clothesline sends Mucho to the mat. Halloway doesn't go for the cover and lifts Mucho to his feet. He once again whips him into the ropes.
CK: He whipped to close to Mondo and Mucho makes the tag. Halloway hasn't realized it and takes a swing at Mucho who simply steps out of the ring.
JS: Halloway finallly realizes what has happened only to catch a elbow into the face. Halloway is down. Mondo is going to work with those massive meathooks on the side of Halloways head. Mondo whips Halloway into the corner and runs at him full blast. Halloway moved and Mondo just hit the ring post...
CK: Ouch.. JS: Yeah poor Mondo he might have dislocated his shoulder on that one. CK: No Im talking about the ring post. JS: Halloway quickly tags out to Dr. Hate who jumps over the top rope and drops a legdrop onto the throat of Mondo. Dr. Hate lifts Mondo and locks a variation of a arm wrench on it. That has to be very painful but Mondo won't give up. Dr. Hate releases the hold and lifts Mondo to his feet.
CK: Look how Mondo is favoring that arm. I think that he is seriously hurt. JS: Dr. Hate drives a european uppercut into the throat of Mondo. Sharp kick to the stomach, reverse neckbreaker on Mondo. Dr. Hate with a tag to Halloway.
Halloway enters the ring and drives a elbow into the shoulder of Mondo. Halloway slides out of the ring grabbing muchos arm slaming it hard into the ring post. Both these teams have given their all today.
CK: I think they are gonna have to do more then that! Here comes Mucho and he is going wild on the two. And wait a minute here! Randy Blaze is upon the apron in the refs face!
JS: Mondo exits the ring and crawls under the apron???? Here he comes out again. CK: That isn't him! Thats...Raving Psycho Schizo! The Psychos have a fresh man in the ring!
JS: And he is going to work on Mr. Hate. POWERSLAM from the new man! Dr. Hate is down. Halloway comes in and catches a sidewalk slam! This new fresh man is tearing these guys apart! Schizo grabs Dr. Hate, POWERBOMB! Halloway is getting up and gets nailed with the big boot. Schizo points around the arena,
HES HULKING UP! CK: I am a real American, fight for the rights of every man. JS: LEG DROP ACROSS THE THROAT OF HALLOWAY. THE COVER.. 1................. 2...................... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!! JS: NEW CHAMPS! NEW CHAMPS! Crowd: YEAH!!! Crowd: BOOO!!! Dave Ritter: The winner of the match, and first _EVER_, RMWA World Tag Team
Champs. THE RAVING PSYCHOS!!!!! JS: Unbelievable!! The Psychos pulled the old switch-a-roo!! CK: That Randy Blaze is a *genius*, Jeffy! Just when you think you've seen all the psychos, he brings along another one! I love it!
JS: Folks, up next we've got that big Holy War match between Kendel Jones Superstar and Necros....
