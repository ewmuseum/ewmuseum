[Scene shows the RMWA ring. Big Bang Baby blast as a man appears from the curtains. It's The Fantasy Brett Young. He has on a pair of blue jeans. He has on a RMWA shirt. He flops himself into the ring. (Raven style.) He grabs the mic from the ring announcers hand.]
Young: They scheduled a super card with out there biggest star. They tried to hold me down with out giving me a match. What's a super card with out
The Fantasy? I have arrived. I will start getting match or the RMWA will taste a little taste of havok. They will taste what FEAR is! What Chaos is.
So RMWA I beat 3 of you're new comers. I proved I was your best new talent. Now giving me what I deserve matches to work my way up. No one is safe from me. Brock to Winters to Osiris. I will get whats mine.
[Young paces the ring staring down and looks up.] Young: Chambers was a waste of my time. Jmang was too. The guy named Ruiz tryed to win but after reaching out just couldn't grasp it. I made it known to the RMWA that I wasn't a joke. I showed them the skill that I worked so hard to master. Ruiz you felt the wrath of Young. You got allot to work on.
The RMWA is for men. [Young reaches in his pocket. Pulls out a wad of bills.] Young: Right here is 5 grand. I will gladly give any one here it just to beat the new guy. Me? Who here will take me up on the offer.
Chambers............JMang................and Ruiz will tell you its not the easiest thing to do. RMWA you couldn't be scared.
[Young drops the mic. Flops out of the ring grabs a RMWA is the best sign from a little kid. Rolls back in.]
Young: [holding the sign up.] IF this is true then give me some real competition NO Chambers. NO Jmang. NO Ruiz. I want to show the world that I am the man that can destroy anyones wrestling carrier at will.
[Young tears the sign up and spits on it and flops out of the ring hands it back to the kid and walks off.]
JS: Brett Young has demanded a 'challenge' from the RMWA executive committee....He doesn't want anything else to do with Anthony Chambers or Tony Ruiz, but from what I know, Tony Ruiz has been granted a re-match with Young when Overdrive returns in 2 weeks!!
CK: Brett's gonna be mad when he finds out about that, Jeffy. JS: That's for sure, Chad. Fans, this next match is one that a whole lot of people have been looking forward to for quite awhile now, including the likes of Chico Jericho, as he has to lock horns with this extremely-talented mystery man next!
CK: Jeffy, I wouldn't want to be in Jericho's boots right now, he's coming off of an ankle injury AND he doesn't even know who he's facing...
JS: Plus this new attitude of his can't be much of an advantage, I doubt he's going to be taking this man seriously!
CK: He's got Herd-itis, Jeffy, of course he isn't going to take this guy seriously, and that's going to be his downfall!
JS: Let's go to Dave Ritter for the introductions.... <Camera flips to Dave Ritter, the only ring-announcer out-lawed in 4 states (why he is, we don't know)>
DR: The following singles contest is schedule for ONE FALL....Introducing first, he hails from Pittsburgh, Pennslyvania, and weighs in at 235 pounds....CHICO JEEEEERRRIIICCCHHOOO!!!!
<"Walking on the Sun" by Smash Mouth starts up as Chico Jericho makes his way to the ring, being jeered loudly by many of his alienated fans. Chico rolls under the bottom rope and raises his fists high in the air, causing the boo's from the fans to grow louder.>
JS: Chico seems to be pretty confident, Chad. CK: I think he's just putting on a show Jeffy, there's no way he can be confident coming into this one...
DR: AND HIS OPPONENT....The.....Uhh....MYSTERY MAN!! (all of a sudden, "With my Baby Tonight" starts up. Nobody comes out, just the song plays, with the lyrics changed, apparently by the masked man, with a woman doing back up for him)
MM: Spend my day working hard on a plan Then I spend my night jumping guys in the ring I can't wait to sneak attack somebody tonight You know that the faces have the prez rapped round their little fingers
But you know that I always have a piece of plunder with me
And I can't wait to sneak attack somebody tonight. W: Knock their lights out Break their legs MM: And we'll leave all the faces lying down in blood. W: Only if they never fight again.. MM: Maybe it's true, I got a one track mind But it's hard to not want to hurt a face, tonight. W: I can't wait to sneak attack somebody tonight. (band keeps playing for a minute, allowing you to hear the booing crowd.) W: Grab the ball bat, knock them down. MM: And we'll leave all the worry of contenders behind. W: Only if I can get a few shots in.... MM: I'm the kind of guy who likes to hurt them right
So you won't find me complaining if we get fined big.
W: I can't wait to sneak attack somebody tonight Maybe what we're doing might be wrong... MM: How in the world could hurting a face be wrong?? W: Never knew injury could take so...long BOTH: Longgg....... MM: I wake up with the title And the sunlights peaking Thru the curtains on my room And there's just one thing I'm thinking W: I can't wait to sneak attack somebody tonight. MM: Don't ya know I can't wait to sneak attack somebody tonight. W: Grab the ball bat, knock them down. MM: And we'll leave all the worry of contenders behind. W: Only if I get a few shots in.... MM: I wake up with the title And the sunlights peaking Thru the curtains on my room And I swear that all I'm thinking is... W: I can't wait to sneak attack somebody tonight.... MM: Someone tonight.....someone tonight W: I can't wait to sneak attack somebody..... MM: I can't wait to sneak attack somebody tonight. (crowd boos as the band finishes the song. The crowd keeps booing. Then, the lights in the arena come back on, and standing behind Chico Jericho is none other then the man who was singing the song, sporting a CDFC t-shirt and a grin on his face, stands the mystery man)
JS: OMIGOD!! THAT'S---- CK: I DON'T BELIEVE MY EYES!!! <The anticipation getting to you yet?> <RECORD-BREAKING CROWD POP> <You probably want to know who he is, don't you...> Both: <screaming> CHRIS DEUGAU!!! JS: I DON'T BELIEVE IT!! CK: CHRIS DEUGAU IS IN THE RMWA!!! JS: CHICO JERICHO DOESN'T BELIEVE HIS EYES!! <Bell sounds> CK: HERE WE GO JEFFY!! JS: Jericho rubs his eyes so he's sure that that really is Chris Deugau....Deugau moves in, Jericho with a kick to the midsection, he grabs Deugau by the head and tosses him into the corner, and starts working away on the midsection with a series of hard right hands!
CK: Jericho may be shocked, but it certainly didn't take much time for it to wear off! JS: Irish whip by Jericho to the other side, Deugau hits the corner hard and walks out--AND JERICHO SENDS HIM BACK INTO THE CORNER WITH A NICELY PLACED DROPKICK!! Jericho charges into the corner for a monkey flip--NO! DEUGAU GOT OUT OF THE WAY--AND HE NAILS JERICHO WITH A BRIDGING GERMAN SUPLEX!!
.........................1 ................................2 ...........................KICKOUT!! JS: ALMOST! Chico Jericho is going to have to keep aware of Deugau's movements, not only can he pin you about a million differant ways, he's one of the best technical wrestlers in this sport to date!
CK: Not to mention one of the most experiance AND one of the most respected! JS: Jericho and Deugau both up, both men hit the ropes, leapfrog by Jericho, they hit the ropes again, Deugau ducks underneath the clothesline--NOO! CRUCIFIX BY CHRIS DEUGAU!
CK: Jericho slipped right through it! Lucky for him, Deugau could've had it right there! JS: Jericho is the first one to his feet, whips Deugau to the ropes, boot to the midsection by Jericho, Deugau doubles-over---DDT BY CHICO JERICHO!!
CK: And he's celebrating it as if he just won the Heavyweight Title!! Get after him Jericho!!
JS: That *was* a nicely-executed DDT by Jericho, I bet he could've had him if he would've followed through on it...Jericho picks Chris up....Scoop slam by Jericho....He's going to the TOP!
CK: This isn't a wise move so early in the match!! JS: Jericho quickly climbs the ropes--HE LEAPS--AND CONNECTS WITH AN ELBOW ACROSS THE CHEST OF CHRIS DEUGAU! THE COVER BY JERICHO--
...........................1 ................................2 KICKOUT!! CK: Chris D kicked out of that one with authority Jeffy! JS: Jericho picks Deugau up, side headlock now....Jericho backs into the corner, setting Chris D up for a running bulldog I do believe--
CK: <interrupting> He's going to show Deugau what technical wrestlings all about!! JS: Jericho starts to run--DEUGAU PUTS ON THE BRAKES! Chris D picks him up, spins around and drops him on the top turnbuckle!
CK: That *HAD* to hurt Jericho! JS: Deugau jumps onto the second turnbuckle--OMIGOD!! THIS IS HOW HE SENT VULGAR OUT OF COMMISSION--GERMAN SUPERPLEX BY CHRIS DEUGAU!! CHICO JERICHO HIT THE MAT _HARD_ JEFFY!!
CK: I think Chris D hit his head mighty hard on that one, not to mention it took one Hell of an adrenaline burst just to pull that one off!!
JS: Deugau is pulling himself up now, while Chico Jericho is starting to move very slowly....He'll be lucky if he didn't get one heck of a concussion or neck injury from that one.
CK: That's what happened to Vulgar, only a steel chair was underneath his head when he landed!!
JS: Deugau gets slowly to his feet, as does Chico Jericho....Deugau's up first, he goes low on Jericho with a single leg take-down, he holds onto the ankle and--HE ROLLS CHICO JERICHO OVER INTO A HALF-BOSTON!!
CK: JERICHO'S CLOSE TO THE ROPES! HE CAN GET OUT OF THIS ONE!! JS: Deugau's leaning back on this one as much as he can...You can see the pain across the face of Chico as he inches his way towards the ropes....
CK: C'MON CHICO! JS: He's almost there.....HE'S GOT THE ROPES!! Chris D reluctantly releases the hold as Chico tries to regain himself and get back to his feet.
CK: It's gonna be tough Jeffy, I don't know how strong that leg of his will be for the rest of this one...
JS: You're right, Chad, Chico's not going to be able to walk to well after he was in that half-Boston for a good 30 seconds....Chico takes a swing at Chris D, Deugau dodges it and nails Jericho with one of his own....Chris backs Deugau up, whip to the ropes--REVERSAL BY JERICHO AND HE CATCHES CHRIS D WITH A NICELY EXECUTED SPINEBUSTER!
CK: But AGAIN he doesnt' follow through on it!! Chico could've won this one again, but instead he decides to jaw with the fans!!
JS: Chris D. kips up and positions himself behind Chico Jericho, who has taken it upon himself to start an arguement with some of the ringside fans...
CK: They called him "Chika", Jeffy! How couldn't he get mad?? JS: Chris D. spins Jericho around, lifts him up and drops him with a fireman's carry....Deugau grabs the leg of Jericho, drops an elbow across the midsection....Deugau picks him up--TEXAS CLOVERLEAF--NO! JERICHO WITH THE THUMB TO THE EYES!
CK: THERE YOU GO CHICO!! That's how you get things done!! JS: Chris D. drops Jericho's legs and tries to regain his vision...Jericho gets up, Jericho hits the ropes--SPINNING HEEL KICK SENDS DEUGAU TUMBLING OVER THE TOP TO THE OUTSIDE!!
CK: Go _get_ him Chico!! Stay on the offensive!! JS: Chico goes to the outside, picks up Deugau up by the hair--HE'S GOING FOR THE CYBORG <shoulder-breaker> ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR!!
CK: TALK ABOUT BAD DEBUTS!! JS: Jericho picks Deugau up across his shoulder---AND CHRIS SLIDES OUT OF IT--HE LEVELS JERICHO WITH A BIG FOREARM SHOT TO THE TEMPLE! And no sooner does Jericho hit the floor does Chris D. slide back into the ring! You'd think he'd go after Jericho on the outside, Chad!
CK: Chris Deugau is _not_ at home on the outside, Jeffy...inside the ring he can beat a man a million differant ways, outside of the ring he doesn't have a clue what to do!
JS: Jericho climbs back into the ring, stomp to the back of the head by Deugau, he picks Jericho up, whips him into the the corner, Jericho stumbles out AND DEUGAU FLOORS HIM WITH A FLYING BODYPRESS!
CK: Dead-on target Jeffy! Like Deugau or not, he nailed Jericho good! JS: Deugau pulls the stunned Chico Jericho to his feet, whips him to the ropes, Jericho comes charging in full steam--HOT SHOT BY CHRIS DEUGAU!!
CK: HE COULDA KILLED CHICO ON THAT ONE!! DISQUALIFY THE MAN!! JS: Chico's head snapped WAAAY back on that one....Deugau pulls him to his feet, grabs hold--FISHERMAN'S SUPLEX INTO A BRIDGE BY DEUGAU! THE COUNT--
.........................1 ...............................2 ........KICKOUT!! JS: Chris *almost* had Chico on that one, and I do believe he thinks he did as he starts after referee Joey Stupka about a slow count....
CK: Chico's starting to get back up again Jeffy!! C'mon Chico!! Show this rah-rah what the RMWA's all about!!
JS: Deugau with his back turned to the Jericho, Chico pulls himself up in the corner....Deugau turns around AND JERICHO NAILS HIM WITH A DOUBLE AX FROM THE SECOND ROPE!!
CK: He his Deugau *hard*, but only enough to bring him down to one knee! I told Chico he should've prepped himself more for this match, but NOOOOOOOO....
JS: Jericho picks Deugau up, whips him into the corner, JERICHO CHARGES IN--AND DEUGAU SIDE STEPPED IT!!
CK: JERICHO HIT HIS SHOULDER ON THE STEEL RINGPOST!! GOOD LORD!! JS: Chris pulls Jericho out by the ankles, drops him on the mat....Chico's going to have a tough time shaking out _those_ cobwebs, Chad!
CK: He could do it if he wasn't hangin' around those Herd guys! I think they may have killed his career with this stupid attitude of theirs!
JS: Deugau pulls Chico up by the back of the head, balances him in the corner....Deugau runs to the opposite side, he charges into the corner--FLYING LEG LARIAT BY CHRIS DEUGAU CONNECTS!!
CK: RIGHT ON THE CHIN!! HE COULDA CHIPPED CHICO'S TEETH!! JS: Chico flipped up over the top rope and he's just laying on the ring apron now....Chris D. grabs his legs and starts to pull him into the ring---JERICHO SITS UP SUDDENLY AND--
CK: YEEEEEEEEEEEOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOWWW!! JS: <groaning> Chico Jericho _nailed_ Chris Deugau right in the lower...._LOWER_ abdominal region....Jericho is raising his hands in victory while Deugau curls up into a ball....
CK: He WON Jeffy! No man can fight back after a shot like that one!! JS: Jericho nails Deugau with a few kicks to the back and kidneys....Jericho picks him up, whips him to the ropes--FRANKENSTEINER BY CHICO JERICHO!!
CK: HE DROPPED THE BIG TECHNICAL WIZARD RIGHT ON HIS HEAD!! You da man, Chico!! JS: Jericho's quick to his feet, he rolls Deugau over....AND HE NOW SIGNALS FOR THE JERICHOSAULT!!
CK: This is it Jeffy! I love it!! JS: Jericho quickly makes his way up to the top--JERICHOSAULT--HE MISSED!! CK: NOOO!!!! JS: HE LANDED RIGHT SQUARE ON THAT INJURED LEG!! Looks like Chris D got up from that low-blow pretty easily, eh Chad?
CK: <grumbles> JS: Deugau quick to his feet; he grabs the leg of the fallen Chico Jericho and--FIGURE 4 LEGLOCK APPLIED BY CHRIS DEUGAU!! HE'S GOT IT LOCKED ON IN THE MIDDLE OF THE RING!!
CK: This is _wrong_ Jeffy!! Chico had this one locked up!! JS: Jericho's trying to resist the pain.....HE CAN'T TAKE IT!! CHICO JERICHO TAPS OUT!! CK: NOOO!!! <Bell sounds> DR: The winner of this match, by submission, CHRRRRRRIS DEUGAU!! CK: HEY! Someone tell that technical oof to let Chico out of the figure-four! He could end Jericho's career!!
JS: Deugau holds onto it until the 4-count....His first night here, and he's already trying to send a message out to the rest of the RMWA wrestlers....Not only will Chris Deugau be a force in the lightweight division, he's more then capable of hanging with probably anyone in the heavyweight division!
CK: He should be thankful there's no "hardcore" or "extreme" division Jeffy, because if there was, Chico'd kick some "technical ability" into Mr. Deugau!
