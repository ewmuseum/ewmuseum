RMWA Avalanche (Part 2 of 10)

March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<Minor crowd pop as the current RMWA Commissioner, Vic Rizzo, makes his way to the ring. Vic is carrying a large cardboard square with a circle drawn on one side of it. A large plastic red arrow is pinned onto the center of the circle.>
Vic Rizzo: I have here the wheel that has the 8 possible stipulations for the Blackout/Fuse match that will occur immeadiately following the match between the American Heroes and Deathward. The eight possibilities are: Steel Cage match, Table Death match, Tables and Ladders match, Chairs and Chains match, Scaffold match, Crowd Participation, Taipei Death Match, and a Texas Death match.
<<<HUGE CROWD POP>>> JS: The crowd's loving the possibility for violence here tonight, Chad! CK: So am I!! <Crowd grows silent as Rizzo spins the plastic arrow> VR: Tonights match between Blackout and Fuse will.......be.........a <Rizzo holds the cardboard square high over his head> VR: A TABLE DEATH MATCH!!!! <<<HUGE CROWD POP>>> JS: A Table Death match!! This one's gonna be a classic, Chad! Folks, for those of you who don't know what a table death match is.....The RMWA is going to supply a large number of tables around ringside for the wrestlers to use. The only way a person can win this match is by putting their opponent *through* a table, and they don't get up before the referee's 10-count. Needless to say, anything goes in this one!!
CK: Jeffy, we're gonna see plenty of blood and broken bones in this one! I love it!! JS: Okay, Chad, we have a potentially explosive tag team match ahead! The
American Heroes will try to assert themselves as title contenders, as they do battle with another great team, Deathward!
CK: Bah! The Zeroes may have all these half wits cheering for them, but those Italian stallions have the tools! Nothing makes them sicker than stupid flag-waving freaks! You just watch how Deathward gives the Zeroes the wrestling lesson their trainers should have given them!
JS: No doubt, Deathward is a fantastic tag team combination, but who will fail and who will prevail is all up in the air right now! Let's go to
Dave Ritter!
Dave: Ladies and gentlemen, this tag team contest is scheduled for one fall, with a twenty minute time limit! First, hailing from Rome, Italy and Venice,
Italy respectively...at a total combined weight of 510 pounds...Michaelangelo
D'Artielli...Lord Christian Capulet...they are...DEAAAAAAAATHWAAAAAAAARD!!!
("Infected Voice" by Sepultura plays over the loudspeakers, as the arena lights dim down to nothingness. Blue spotlights suddenly erupt from the rafters, and shine down on the members of Deathward waiting at the end of the aisle. After a few seconds, their music fades off and the lights are restored; D'Artielli and Capulet then proceed to the ring.)
JS: The crowd not showing much appreciation here, Chad! I guess Deathward's attitude rubs them the wrong way.
CK: So does soap, but you don't hear them complaining about it! (D'Artielli removes his lengthy maroon cloak to reveal his long black tights, which have "DEATHWARD" stenciled in gothic lettering down the side. His feet are adorned with maroon boots, which reach most of the way up his shins.
His handsome, but pale, features are accentuated by hazel eyes and long dark hair, kept in a ponytail down his back. He has long limbs, and his thin, yet muscular frame forms an almost perfect "V" from his shoulders to his waist.
Capulet goes through a similar ritual, although his cloak is black velvet on the outside, blue underneath. It drops to the ground to reveal a black latex singlet, which has the same insignia across the midriff. His shape is very much like his partners: thin, almost frail, angled "V" torso, with dark hair.
The difference is that Capulet's hair is cut off about two inches down the neck, and is slicked back across his head; in addition, his eyes are completely black. His face is very grim, lacking any trace of mirth or happiness.)
Dave: And their opponents...hailing from Sierra Vista, Arizona, and the backwoods of West Virginia respectively...weighing in at a total of 499 pounds...here are Casey Spahn, and "Dyamite" Danny Anderson...they are...
the AAAMEEEEEEERICAAAAAAN...HEEEEEEEEEROOOOOOOOOES! ("Where do the Children Go" by the Hooters blares from the loudspeakers, and the Heroes run down the aisle, slapping hands as they head for the ring.
Spahn is wearing a short red, white and blue jacket, fastened only at the bottom. On his head sits his red U.S. Army beret. His wrestling shorts are striped red and white, and he sports a pair of blue boots. He has short brown hair, cut in military style, and hazel eyes. Anderson's attire matches his partner's, save that he wears long, blue and white wrestling trunks, with red boots. Anderson has piercing blue eyes, shoulder length blonde hair, and sports a killer smile. His face bears a close-cropped goatee, and there is a diamond earring in his left ear.
They get to the ring and split up, running in opposite directions around the ring and slapping hand with as many people as they can reach. As they meet on the far side of the ring, they jump through the ropes and begin criss crossing the ring in opposite directions, each bouncing off a different set of ropes. They pass each other twice, then stop and slap hands. Then...)
JS: Omigosh! Deathward *nailed* the Heroes! D'Artielli clotheslined Anderson right out of the ring, and Capulet clipped Spahn's right leg out from under him! Capulet and D'Artielli are stomping the stuffing out of Spahn, and they're laying into the leg with all their heavy artillery! What a deplorable ambush by Deathward!
CK: Are you kidding?! It's brilliant! Take out half of the team before the match even starts! They're going to work on that punk Spam...heck, he hasn't even gotten his gear off yet! Aw, his schmuck of a partner got up off his duff and cleared them out!
JS: Fortunately for the Heroes, Anderson was still very much aware! Deathward have changed pace, regrouping outside the ring while Anderson helps his partner back to his feet. The ref finally has the bell rung, and it looks like
Anderson will replace a limping Casey Spahn in the ring against D'Artielli. DING!! DING!! DING!! The two larger halves of these tough teams lock up. They're pushing back and forth...and Anderson gets thrown to the mat!
CK: Hah! That'll teach ya, suckup! Only twits like these out here in the audience would fall for your act! My good pal Mike ready to lock up with the Dud...look at that! There's your true American hero, Stewart! He pulled the ponytail!
JS: You're full of it, Chad! That was clean power, just like...Mike's? Since when are *you* friends with Deathward? And what act are you talking about?
CK: Come on, Stewart! You don't really believe all that guff about loving
America, do you? Those two faced turncoats would do *anything* for heat!
They play the goody two shoes, and salute the flag when it suits them, then they blatantly *cheat*, and these dopes lap it up!
JS: Please! Anderson whips "Mike" off the ropes, and lands a beautiful back elbow smash! And he *drops* that elbow down into D'Artielli's sternum! A scoop by Anderson, and a *hard* body slam! D'Artielli is clutching at his back, and Anderson gives him a vicious kick to the lower lumbar area!
CH: Go ahead, Stewart. He won't mind if you call him Mike...I've given you clearance. Mike's in a bit of trouble from the Dud's illegally spawned attack, but he'll come back, don't you worry! Dud whips Mike off the ropes again, and Mike ducks the clothesline! Dud turns around, and Mike levels the goon with a big knee to the gut! Mike grabs him by the back of the head...
and drills him with a European uppercut! Great counter wrestling! JS: It wasn't *that* impressive, Chad. Mike, as you call him, drags Anderson over to the corner, and tags in-
CH: Chris! JS: Do you mind? CH: Not at all. JS: Gee, thanks! *Chris* tagged in, and a double whip off the ropes! Mike catches Anderson in a massive spinebuster! And Chris with a springboard...wow!
Right into a perfect somersault leg drop! A great double team move by Deathward! Chris off the ropes himself, and *drives* down with a snapping elbow on Anderson! Chris stops to give a little show for the fans, and I don't think that's very smart. That's more the providence of his tag team partner, anyway; it isn't like him to showboat, so I'm told. Anyway, Chris picks Danny Anderson up off the mat, and Irish whips him! No, Anderson reverses it! Capulet impacts with the turnbuckle, and he's down on one knee!
Anderson staggers a bit, but makes the tag! Spahn leaps into the ring, and strikes Capulet in the face with a drop kick! Spahn tosses Capulet off the ropes, and lands a heart attack clothesline!
CH: Blech! Those flag waving fruitcakes are coming back! Spam lands a couple of *closed fists!* to the side of Chris's head, then kicks him in the guts before heading for the ropes. He's off...and lands a passable swinging neckbreaker, for a talentless hack. Now he locks on a side chinlock, trying in his own pitiful way to put Chris out. And of course, ref, he's *choking* in there! Naturally, that biased turd pays me no heed, and the flagrant rulebreaking continues unabated! Luckily, Chris is far too much of a professional to fall victim to that for long! He's already looking to regain his vertical base, and is up to one knee as I speak! Spam trying everything in his unconsiderable power to stop it from happening...and catches a stiff elbow for his troubles! And Chris with an innovative escape, stomping on the toes! Spam lets him loose, and Chris bounds to the ropes...springboard into a leg lariat! Outstanding aerial move by Chris sends Spam to his back!
JS: Will you leave off with the phony cheating routine?! Capulet with a smart series of moves to escape, and heads for his corner! But Spahn cuts him off with a waistlock from behind...into a released German suplex! Quick thinking by the Hero to keep Capulet in the ring! Spahn pulls Capulet to his feet...
Russian legsweep! A cover by Spahn! 1!!! 2!!! And Capulet kicks out! Spahn rolls him over, and applies a front chinlock! He's pulling back on Capulet's neck, wrenching vertebrae and straining tendons in a possible attempt at a submission! Capulet is in great pain here as Spahn leans way back on the chinlock, and the smaller half of Deathward is in a tight spot! He's two feet from the closest set of ropes, and only four feet from the Heroes' corner! Capulet is straining...and D'Artielli runs in with a stomp to the back of Spahn's head! Where's your talk of cheating
*now*, Chad? CK: All's fair against patriotic peons, Stewart! Chris gets back to his feet, rubbing his neck a bit...and lands a big spin kick on Spam's head!
He drags Spam back to his feet, and whips him off the ropes! *Beautiful* baseball slide into Spam's right leg! The puke hits the mat crying like a girl, and Chris wastes no time in applying a standing figure four! He's yanking on Spam's knee, trying to tear it out, and rightly so after that fiasco before! I'm surprised the ref doesn't make Chris break the hold, just because he's not licking the ref's boots like those Zeroes! Chris is really pulling on the leg, and Spam needs some kind gentleman to give him a tissue! Hah!
JS: Oh, puh-leeze! Spahn is doing his best to find the ropes, but he *is* in a great deal of pain! Capulet is a consummate technician, and he has that standing figure four applied flawlessly! Finally, Spahn manages to get hold of the ropes, and the ref forces Capulet off! Capulet stomps away at the right leg of Spahn, compounding the damage done by the standing figure four! He's off the ropes, and drops a splash across the leg! Capulet flies up to the second rope, and plummets downward with a double axehandle! Spahn's leg is taking enormous punishment! Look at this! Capulet is taunting
Anderson! "Dynamite" comes rushing in, and the ref has to stop him! And in comes D'Artielli to help out! He and Capulet are stomping on the leg of Spahn, and...oooh! They made a wish at Spahn's expense! He's rolling around the ring in agony, taking shots to the leg the entire time! Anderson is livid, but he's only providing them with more of a chance to beat on his partner! He *must* control himself, or the Heroes are done for!
CK: Great, ain't it?! That's what you get for teaming with a lummox, Stewart! Sure, he's pretty strong, but strength isn't worth much when you can't tie your own shoes! Mike leaves the ring, and the ref comes back to the match.
Tag by Chris! Mike's heading for the top rope! Chris delivers a hamstring stretcher, and keeps the leg exposed! Mike off the top...*drives* a knee into the leg of Spam! He's doing some screechin' now, I tell ya! Chris leaves, and Mike lifts that twerp up one more time! Kneebreaker! He's still got ahold of Spam's leg, and he *yanks* it straight away from his pelvis!
Spam's finished, I guarantee it! It's just a matter of time now! Mike pulls
Spam up, right up into a gorilla press! He *pitches* Spam across the ring!
Oops! Right into his corner! Dud tagged in! He slingshots over the ropes, and shoulderblocks Mike to the mat!
JS: Anderson is all over himself! He's got his shot again! He scoops
D'Artielli up onto his shoulders...fallaway slam! Anderson drops an elbow across D'Artielli's face, and scoops him up again! Anderson has D'Artielli up in the front powerslam position...and jams the shoulder onto his knee!
A picture perfect shoulderbreaker by Anderson! He applies an armbar...and
D'Artielli rakes the eyes! He quickly returns to his corner and tags out to Capulet, who leaps up to the top rope and flies off...into a powerslam by Anderson! Awesome reversal by the Hero! He drags Capulet to his feet, and slings him into the ropes! Oh, Anderson down awfully quickly for that back body drop, and Capulet sees the opening! A wicked boot to the teeth by Capulet! Anderson's head snaps back...into a savate kick! "Dynamite"
is staggered, and Capulet leaps up onto his shoulders! Frankensteiner! Capulet Irish whips Anderson across corners! He backs up...handspring elbow smash! Anderson is in *big* trouble!
CK: Yes! Chris with a standing drop kick to push the Dud back into the corner! Chris whips the Dud off the ropes again, and catches him with an incredible flying headscissors! Chris is on a roll, as usual! He whips the Dud in again...sidestep into an enzuigiri! Deathward has everything under control here, just as they should! Chris whips the Dud into the ropes again...wait! The Dud got a lucky blind tag to Spam! He reverses the whip, and gets out of harm's way! Spam has cleared the ropes, Chris is coming off...caught in a monkey flip! Chris gets launched...right into the arms of the Dud! I can't believe it! He caught Chris in a side suplex!
It's that so called Final Assault of theirs! Spam covers, and the Dud tackles Mike! No, ref, don't count! There are other people in the ring!
1!!! 2!!! 3!!! What a ripoff! Dave: Your winners, at 9:21...the AMERICAN...HEEEEEEEEEROOOOOOOOOES!
