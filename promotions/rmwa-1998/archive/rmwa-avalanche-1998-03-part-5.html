RMWA Avalanche (Part 5 of 10)

March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jeff Stewart: Ok... our next match is the so-called "Crucification
Match", featuring Kendel Jones Superstar and Necros....
Charles Killen: Do they actually get to nail their opponent to the cross? Man, that would rule.
JS: No... no they don't. They have to strap their opponent to that huge cross in the center of the ring. They'll be surrounded by the fifteen foot steel cage to prevent interference, and of course the other deterrent to having people get involved is the stipulation that *anyone* who interferes in this match will be suspended indefinitely from the
RMWA. CK: The bottom line is someone... or maybe both people... involved in this match is going to the hospital tonight. There's gonna be blood, there's gonnna be violence... oh man.. this is gonna be so cool.
[Camera cuts away from the broadcast team to the ring. The ring annoucer takes the house mic and clears his throat.]
Ring Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is a Crucification Match! The rules are as follows: The winner of the match is the man who straps his opponent to the 9' cross inside the steel cage. There are NO pinfalls or submissions allowed. Anyone interferring in this match will be suspended from RMWA! There *MUST* be a winner!
["Long Hard Road out of Hell" starts up on the PA system] RA: Introducing first.... hailing from Cleveland, Ohio and weighing in at 218 pounds... accompanied to the ring by Wolfgang Von Geist....
this... is... NECROS! [Necros is about 6'2" with shoulder length black hair, black fingernails, celtic tribal arm band tattoos and a series of scars on his arms. He's wearing black deniam jeans, Doc Marten boots, black elbow pads, a t-shirt with a picture of a female vampire on the back. He climbs into the cage as the fans give him a steady chorus of boos.]
[Necros' music ends as "John the Revelator" by The Faith Choir takes its place.] RA: And his opponent... hailing from Fayetteville, Arkansas and weighing in at 235 pounds.... this is... KENDELL... JONES....
SUPERSTAR! [KJS emerges from the back and starts down the aisle. He approaches the cage, staring at Necros and Wolfgang Von Geist. One of the ring attendants hurries up the aisle and hands Jones a microphone.]
KJS: Ya know the minute that I walked into this fed I was just full of suprises. Wheather it be a sneak attack, or an all out frontal assault.
Ya just didnt know what to expect next. Well none the less keeping up with the standard, my partner for tonights main event. I couldnt just have chump out of Bummfuck, Egypt. Ohhh no, This is a
PPV. This is the big show. This is...... DRAGO!!!!!!!!!!!!!! {{KJS points to the enterance as Drago walks from the back. KJS starts to laugh wildly jumping around as Drago walks to KJS' side.}} KJS: Let's get this over with... [Drago hands a baseball bat to Jones who immediately rushes into the cage with it. The referee looks like he wants to take the bat, but realizes he can't in this matchup. Instead he shuts the door and locks the door closed with the chain.]
JS: Well this match is under way and Kendell Jones Superstar just took a wild swing at Necros with that baseball bat, but missed.
CK: Suh-wing battah battah battah! You gotta love this Jeff. No rules, locked inside the cage.... this is what wrestling is all about!
[KJS slowly moves in on Necros, using the bat to force Necros towards the corner. Kendell takes huge overhand swing at Necros, but again
Necros is just a bit faster and he manages to sidestep the blow. Necros quickly grabs hold of the other end of the bat, and he and KJS start a tug-of-war for the weapon. As Necros gives a large pull on the bat,
Jones let's go and with Necros put off-balance momentarily KJS levels him with a solid lariat.]
JS: Good move on the part of Kendell Jones Superstar as he sends Necros to the mat.... and now he's just stomping away on him! W're not likely to see much scientific wrestling in this match, that's for sure.
CK: ooh.. pearls of wisdom with Jeff Stewart. What would we do without you? JS: Funny, that's what management and I often ask about you. CK: hey..... [Jones continues to kick away at the downed Necros, but Necros puts an end to it with a solid low blow. Jones staggers back as Necros gets back to his feet, baseball bat in hand. Necros measures his man, then drives the end of the baseball bat into Jones' ribs. Jones falls back against the ring ropes and just manages to duck a wild swing by Necros.
Kendell Jones kicks at Necros, connecting with his knee and Necros steps away, holding his leg. Jones immediatley seizes on the moment and kicks the back of Necros' leg. Necros responds by jabbing backwards with the bat, but Jones grabs the bat away and places yet another kick to the knee area of Necros' leg. Necros drops to the mat and slowly drags himself away as Jones turns to face the crowd, swinging the bat like a baseball star.]
JS: Jones could be making a mistake right here. He should be pressing his advantage instead of showboating.
CK: Show's how much you know. Necros is a punk and he's gonna be spitting out splinters after Jones shoves that baseball bat up his....
JS: OH MY GOD! [In the ring, Jones is holding his right leg and screaming in pain. Necros is using the ring ropes to get back to his feet, and in one hand he's holding a metal spike, the tip of which is dripping blood. Necros has a sadistic grin on his face as he licks the blood off the spike.
Necros bounces off the ropes and drives an elbow into Jones' head, sending the "Superstar" down to the mat. Necros raises the spike up again and drops down to the mat, trying to drive the spike into Jones'
leg again, but Jones rolls aside as the spike gets stuck in the mat.] JS: If that had hit, Jones' leg would've been pinned to the mat! Necros is *sick*! CK: Of course he is... but hey, it's not like KJS is a choir boy. He's the one who brought the weapons into play in the first place. If you can't stand the heat, stay the hell out of the cage.
[Jones backhands Necros as Necros tries to pull the spike out of the mat. Both men are slow to get up and both men are favoring one leg....
KJS because of the wound from the spike, and Necros from the damage KJS managed to inflict on his knee. Necros limps towards Kendell Jones and
Jones meets him halfway. The two men lock up and Necros drives his bad knee into Jones' midsection and follows it up with a DDT. He follows it up with a legdrop and then another.]
JS: The look on Necros' face says he's feeling the effects of those legdrops, but it's not stopping him from dropping them across the chest of Kendell Jones. Necros now heading to the turnbuckle... he's going up top.... MOONSAULT! He hit the moonsault, and now he's trying to drag
Jones towards the cross in the middle of the ring.... but Jones grabs hold of Necros' shorts and pulls back... and Necros goes headfirst into the steel mesh of the cage!
CK: That's why you can't count Kendell Jones out! If this was a regular match that Moonsault may have done the trick, but there's no pinfalls here, and no way is Jones going to get crucified. Look at
Jones... he's back to his feet... he's waiting on Necros.... and there's a cross-face chicken wing! Jones has the chicken wing locked on and he can hold it on as long as he wants, because there's no submissions in this match.
[Jones laughs maniacally as Necros shouts out in pain. The more Necros cries out, the tighter Jones tries to wrench in the hold. Necros reaches back with his free arm and grabs hold of Jones' hair and drops down in a modified version of a jawbreaker. Jones rolls across the canvas, holding his jaw, as Necros drags himself to the far corner obviously still feeling the effects of the chickenwing.]
JS: It wasn't a pretty move, but it got the job done. Both men are taking some time to get their wits... such as they are given these two loopjobs.... back in order. Jones is checking on that wound he got from
Necros' spike and Necros is trying to work the kinks out of the knee
Jones put a hurting on.
CK: Hey... no time limit, no worries. [Jones slowly gets back to his feet and looks out at Vampyra, who is pacing back and forth at ringside. He laughs and yells out Necros'
name. Necros looks up at Jones, who then spits between the cage mesh, catching Vampyra right in the face. Necros looks stunned for a moment, then... inspired by Vampyra's cries of rage and anger, he charges across the ring at Jones. Jones avoids Necros' charge and Kendell forces
Necros into the corner where the two begin exchanging punches and kicks.
Necros catches Kendel with a headbutt to the bridge of the nose, and immediately blood begins pouring from Jones' busted nose.]
CK: ooooh... that's GOTTA hurt! Jones is bleeding like a stuck pig! JS: Necros has bloodied Jones. and... SPINEBUSTER! Necros just drove
Jones into the mat and Necros has regained the upper hand.... and he's dragging Jones towards the crucifix again.
[Necros pulls Jones up to his feet and lifts him up...] CK: OUCH! Spinebuster onto the crucifix! Necros just may have broken
Jones' back! JS: ...and Necros is trying to strap Kendel's feet to the cross.... but
Jones manages to push Necros back with his foot... but there wasn't much behind it. On the outside Vampyra is calling to Necros... she's telling him... I think she's telling him to give Jones another spinebuster. She really wants Necros to end Jones' career!
CK: Necros is listening too! He's pulling Jones up again... Spinebu.... NO! JS: Jones hit the Deliverance Driver! Necros had him up for the spinebuster, but Jones turned it into the jumping DDT he calls the
Deliverance Driver! Vampyra can't believe it! Jones is setting Necros up on the crucifix, and he's heading towards the turnbuckle... he's up on the top rope.... SHOOTING STAR PRESS! Incredible distance on that move, but he hit it!
CK: Hell, even *I'M* impressed by that one. JS: Well I'm sure that makes it all worthwhile for Kendel Jones... but
Jones isn't looking for your applause right now... he's stretching
Necros out on the cross.... he's got the feet secured.... the left arm now.... and the right arm! HE GOT HIM! KENDEL JONES WINS!
[Jones continues to pound away on the helpless Necros as the ring official unlocks the chain that holds the cage door closed. Wolfgang
Von Geist immediately charges towards the door, but is met by Drago who
nails him from behind with a chairshot to the back of the head. With Von Geist stunned, Drago grabs the key to the padlock and shoves the referee down. Drago slams the door shut and uses the chain to lock it in place. Von Geist begins to move back towards the cage and again
Drago bashes him with the steel chair again.]
CK: All right... the match is over, but the war isn't! Jones has unstrapped Necros... and he's got the Jones-mission locked on! He's gonna break Necros' neck... this is just so cool! Vampyra is going nuts at ringside, but she's not gonna be able to do anything unless she tries to climb the cage... and personally I'd like to see that. Maybe Jones could put the Jonesmission on her too.
JS: You are such a sick.... Hey... what's with the lights? [the arena lights go out. Fans being pulling out Bic lighters, trying to get some light back in the arena. "Angel with the Scabbed Wings" by
Marilyn Manson starts up in the darkness.]
CK: For cryin' out loud... doesn't anyone listen to anything but
Marilyn Manson these days?
[After several moments the lights come back on and standing in the ring is a massive sized man... nearly seven feet tall and obviously weighing well over 300 pounds. Kendel Jones has released the Jonesmission on
Necros and is obviously nervous, but is sizing up the mystery man.]
JS: Who in the hell is *that*? CK: Beats me... but Jones doesn't look happy to see him! Jones goes for a running lariat.... but look at that... the big man caught him around the throat! He twists him around... kind of a reverse Diamond
Cutter there.... he drove Jones to the mat on the back of his head!
JS: Yes, that's called a Reverse Neck Breaker, "Mr. Broadcast Genius."... a Rude Awakening if you will.... and Jones hit hard.... I think he caught the back of his head on the end of the crucifix! Drago is unlocking the cage door... there go the lights again!
[The lights go off again and after several moments come back up, and as mysteriously as the large man had arrived, he is gone. RMWA security, along with wrestlers from the locker room, finally arrive at the ring to prevent further altercations.]
JS: Folks---WE'VE GOT TROUBLE BACKSTAGE!!! CK: What?!? <Camera flips backstage to the lockeroom of "Razorblade" Zakk Wylder. Wylder is Ed Rogers' manager, Smelly the Sockpuppet, on his right hand. Ed Rogers is also present in the room, as he is wailing away on Wylder who's trying to cover-up in the corner. Also, Smelly is pounding away on Wylder, which is unusual being that Smelly is on *Wylder's* hand>
CK: LOOK AT THAT!! THAT BIG DOPE'S HITTING HIMSELF IN THE HEAD! HAHAHAHAHA!!! JS: So is Ed Rogers, who is trying desperately to retrive his stolen manager and confidant, Smelly the Sockpuppet.
CK: ROGERS HAS SMELLY!! JS: Rogers and Wylder are playing tug-of-war with Smelly the Sockpuppet--OMIGOD!! ROGERS LEVELS WYLDER WITH A SHORT CLOTHESLINE!! Rogers grabs the stretched-out Smelly from Wylder, puts him "back in position" and--SMELLY ENHANCED MANDIBLE CLAW ON ZAKK WYLDER!!
CK: Eddie's gone off the deep end this time Jeffy!! That's the second time Wylder's tasted that claw!!
JS: That move is *lethal* Chad....Here comes Shawn Friday to pull Rogers off Wylder--NOOOO!!!!!
CK: HAHAHAHAHAHA!!! JS: ROGERS LOCKED SHAWN FRIDAY IN THE SMELLY ENHANCED MANDIBLE CLAW!! I DON'T BELIEVE THIS!!
CK: THIS IS GREAT!! JS: Friday drops to the floor, trying desperately to get out of that mandible claw....Here comes the RMWA "Talently Challenged" corps and the referee's....
GOOD LORD!! NOT MR. GROOVY SHORTS!! CK: HAHAHAHAHAH!!! Those shorts may be groovy, but Smelly doesn't care about fashion!! JS: Frankie the Dancing Goldfish and The Amazing Bob are pounding away on Ed Rogers, who has gone *way* off the deep end--AND ROGERS RELEASES MR. GROOVY SHORTS AND LOCKS IT ON THE AMAZING BOB!!
CK: And Bob passed out instantly!! This is so funny, I think i'm gonna cry!! JS: Frankie's pounding away on the back of Rogers head--AND NOW SMELLY'S GOING AFTER FRANKIE!! This is *outrageous* Chad!!
CK: Joey Stupka is hanging off of Rogers' arm! That's not a good idea Joey!! JS: ED ROGERS GRABS JOEY STUPKA AND LOCKS *HIM* IN THE SMELLY ENHANCED MANDIBLE CLAW!! ROGERS HAS GONE BERZERK!!!
CK: This is a moment for RMWA history! Smelly and Eddie have leveled Zakk Wylder, Mr. Groovy Shorts, The Amazing Bob, Frankie the Dancing Goldfish, Shawn Friday, and Joey Stupka!!
JS: Finally, Ed and Smelly are leaving....They did enough damage, though. I think that'll teach Zakk Wylder a thing or two about kidnapping Smelly in the future!
CK: I don't, a rah-rah like Wylder doesn't learn too quickly. JS: Folks, we've gotta take a quick break to get this carnage cleaned up.... <To be continued....>
