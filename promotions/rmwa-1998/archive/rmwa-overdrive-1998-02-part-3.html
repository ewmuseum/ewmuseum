RMWA Overdrive (Part 3 of 6)

February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(camera opens up on a lonesome prarie where we see a dirty looking cowboy cooking a pot off beans over an open campfire. The night is full of stars and the whimperings of coyotes in the air.)
"Dirty" Dick Austin: Rocky Mountain Wrestling Association. I have always wanted to lay my hat in The Rocky Mountains but as you can see Texas has always been my home.
(spits) "Dirty" Dick Austin: I think it might be time for me to back my burlap back and head for dem mountains. RMWA, I am a simple man with a mean streak. I come from a long line of Cowboys. Cattle is a way of life.
Fighting is a way of life and wrestling is a way of life as well. RMWA, my boots are made for walking and believe you and me. I am going to walk right into RMWA and lay my claim.
(camera fades) <And rises....> JS: Our next match of the evening puts two undefeated men up against one another. The Great American Spaz has not been beaten here in the RMWA if you can belive that! But his incredible luck streak may come to a screeching hault here tonight, as he takes on one of the most violent men to grace the sport of professional wrestling in Kuwasi "Blackfist" Shakur. It should be a barn burner.
CK: Last week Shakur nearly killed Sean Napier. Word on "Da Sniper" is that he'll be ok in a few weeks to wrestle, just not with the RMWA. President Vic Rizzo gave Sean Napier his pink slip this week after he no showed for some events and promo shoots. I didn't like him anyways!
JS: Well, we at the RMWA wish Sean the best in his future in or out of wrestling. But lets now turn to the match at hand, Spaz against Shakur. Lets get down to ring annoucer Dave Ritter for the formalities!
Dave Ritter: Comming to the ring first, he hails from the fighting town of Philadelphia, Pa. He stands at six feet two inches and weighed in tonight at 245 pounds. Here is...
THE GREAT AMERICAN SPAZ! (You made me right by Billy Joel starts to play over the loud speakers as The Great American Spaz burst out of the lockers to the delight of this Denver crowd. He has short brown hair, medium build, and wears pants-style tights. the tights have on one leg an American flag design, but when the design crosses over to the next leg, it becomes swirly, fragmented, etc. Well proportioned man, with a fair tan, sometimes with a day-old beard.)
Dave Ritter: And his opponent, from South Bronx, New York! Standing at six feet seven inches and weighing in at 300 pounds even.
KUWASI "BLACKFIST" SHAKUR (Ah yeah by Boogie Down Productions starts to play over the loud speakers as Kuwasi Shakur makes his way to the ring greeted by boo's. Shakur is a sturdily build African American male, with muscular shoulders, arms and chest. His head in shaven and his face has no hair on it. His confidence and, in many respects, his edginess are evident in his dark brown eyes and faint scars which mark his past wars. His continual expression is a mad-dog glare punctuated by a walk and posture that arrogantly invite trouble. He just has a pair of black trunks on and has a hockey stick in his hands!)
CK: Wait a minute! The Avalanche aren't scheduled to play tonight, especially in a high school gym!
JS: I don't think The Blackfist really gives a damn, he enters the ring, but Spaz quickly lays a knee into Shakur before he could defend himself from that move. And Spaz is hitting Shakur with some heavy forearm shots to the back. Shakur drops the hockey stick after this first wave of attack from Spaz. He backs Kuwasi into the ropes and whips him into them and applies a sleeper in the middle of the ring on Shakur! This might not be the smartest move to start a match with.
CK: Certainly not, Kuwasi Shakur is not nearly worn down enough to submit to a sleeper, I doubt it's even haveing any effect on the big man.
JS: It looks that way since Shakur just snap mared the much smaller Spaz down to the mat and is begining to lay those big boots into the back of The Great American Spaz's head. Now Shakur brings Spaz to his feet and hits him with a few knife edged chops and flings Spaz off the ropes and hits him with a powerslam! He planted with that powerslam.
CK: He picked Spaz up like he was a little rag doll! JS: Shakur bounces off the ropes and plants a elbow squarely to the chest of Spaz. Now Shakur is letting Spaz stagger to his feet, Shakur goes to the outside of the ring and gets the hockey stick! He returns to the ring and smacks the staggered Spaz with the hockey stick and it breaks and half, the blade of the stick flies into the crowd.
CK: Their's a lawsuit for good old Mr. Rizzo! JS: Even though the stick was broken, its not stopping Shakur from using the thing, he's just smacking Spaz with that splinter ridden stick! Spaz is busted WIDE open! But remarkably he's still standing!
CK: Now he's not! Kuwasi Shakur just springed off the ropes and nailed
Spaz with a running stick shot and he crumples to the ground a bloody mess!
JS: Shakur isn't gonna let Spaz get a second to catch his breath as he picks The Great American Spaz up and hits a Northern Lights Bomb! The Cover!
1 NO! JS: Spaz isn't gonna say die that easily as he easily kicks out after one, though it was a sloppy cover by Shakur. Kuwasi isn't too happy, he picks Spaz up again and looks like he's gonna slam him down. NO! HE DROVE SPAZ'S FACE INTO THE TOP TURNBUCKLE!
CK: Snake Eyes! JS: It looks like he's going for it again! This will certainly end this match if he hits it, but it looks as if Spaz is wiggling out of the slam posisition and hits Shakur with a drop kick that throws the big man off balance,. He hits Shakur square in the mush with another dropkick that backs Kuwasi into the ropes.
CK: The big man is now tied up in the ropes, this is that little nugget
Spaz's chance to take the big man out.
JS: Spaz is laying in some good left and rights into Shakur, he's now going into the ropes, but referee Joey Stupka got Kuwasi Shakur lose! And he catches Spaz and backdrops him out of the ring. But Spaz landed on the apron and is going to the top rope. Shakur has no clue where Spaz is, for all he knows Spaz is laid out on the concrete.
CK: SPAZ HIT'S A JAWJACKER OFF THE TOP ROPE AND SHAKUR IS DOWN! Spaz follows up by climbing on top of Kuwasi and starts laying some heavy blows into Shakur, this isnt the Spaz we expected against the much more experienced and skilled Shakur!
JS: The Great American Spaz brings Shakur to his feet, which might not be a good idea, and whips Kuwasi into the ropes and looks like he's going for a backdrop. BUT SHAKUR STOPS SHORT AND HITS A DOUBLE UNDERHOOK POWERBOMB ON SPAZ!
Ck: That will stop the momentum Spaz had going his way. But it looks like the Blackfist is a bit dizzy himself, though he is on his feet.
JS: Now the dizzied Shakur is going up to Spaz and hits him with the 242 SPINNING CHOKE SLAM! THE COVER! 1 2 3 (DING*DING*DONG*RAMA*LAMA*DING*DONG) Dave Ritter: The Winner of this match in 8:58. Kuwasi Shakur! CK: We need to invest in a new bell. JS: Yep, any how great victory for Kuwasi Shakur, who continues his impressive preformances as of late here in the RMWA. Well for our next match Kyle Manson takes on John Proundstar, but me and Chad get this match off!
CK: Woo Hoo! JS: RMWA President Vic Rizzo is gonna call the action with Vanessa
D'Angelo for our next match! I'm gonna get a beer.
(Commercial)
