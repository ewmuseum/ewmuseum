[The prelude of screaming in Ozzie Ozborne's "Crazy Train" fills the arena. A large face pop emerges from the capacity crowd and all necks turn to witness the entrance of one, Xavier Lee Jackson.]
[A few seconds later, the athletically built African American flips the curtain aside and enters the arena area. Outfitted in faded blue jeans and a black swestshirt that reads "XJ"; this mammoth individual walks down the aisle towards the ring exchanging handslaps and handshakes with the adoring crowd.]
[XJ scoops up a micrphone from the announcer's table and slides into the ring where he his greeted by an even louder pop. A short time later, the crowd hushes enough for XJ to speak]
XJ: [In his baritone voice] Well, well, well....it seems as though the boys in the RMWA towers forgot to add Xavier Lee to the lil' ol' show tonite...
[Big pop as XJ smiles and shakes his head] XJ: That's cool, though, 'cause this gives ol' Xavier Lee a chance to chirp a bit; and chirp-chirp he will.
[A chuckle from many in the crowd] XJ: [Scratching his bald head] Two things on Xavier Lee's lil' list tonite; the first of which involves my good friends, The Herd...
"BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!" [Most in the audience "boo" at the mentioning of "The Herd". Xavier Lee nods and issues a slight smile]
XJ: You'd think by now that these s<bleep>-for-brains would learn. The last time they tried to thwack that lil' girl, Xavier Lee tossed Mistah Scott
Winters to-and-f<bleeping>-fro. And...just a few short nights ago, Mistah
Kendall Jones kinda got in Xavier Lee's way and went "boom"...
[More laughs from the crowd at the memory of Kendall Jones being easily floored by Xavier Lee Jackson]
XJ: So...here's the scoop, Herd...you go right ahead and continue your brainless behavior..
[quick nod] XJ: ...'cause Xavier Lee will keep on thwumpin' your _stupid_ asses left-and-f<bleeping>-right each and every time you try that s<bleep>.
[Huuuuuuuge pop from the crowd as Xavier Lee changes the microphone from one hand to another. The crowd buzz remains knowing that Xavier Lee is on a roll]
XJ: Mistah Scott Winter..... [More boos at the name "Scott Winter" as Xavier Lee closes his eyes and shakes his head] XJ: ....get a f<bleeping> life, man.... [The crowd bursts out in laughter at the expense of Scott Winter] XJ: Winter, my lil' friend, you're a _joke_. Not only are you a joke,
Mistah Winter, but you're also the God-damned laughing stock of the RMWA.
[More chuckles from the crowd as many stand and applaud] XJ: [Itching his nose] Now...on to the real reason why Xavier Lee's payin' y'all a visit and that would be the RMWA Rocky Mountain Title Match... [Crowd pops as XJ uses his forefinger to gesture with while he continues] XJ: It's real simple...Xavier Lee wants a piece of the winner of tonite's match. [HUGE pop at the thought of Xavier Lee Jackson battling the Rocky Mountain
Champion] XJ: [Nodding] That's right, babe, Xavier Lee's demandin' that he pay the winner of visit next week. And...Xavier Lee don't give a rat's ass if it's the tough-guy-wannabe named "Black Fists"; the man that _will_ be dreamin',
Kyle Manson; this lil' ol' "Mystery Guest".....or.....
[The crowd revs up in anticipation of the last name called...] XJ: [With a sparkle in his eye as he continues sarcastically] ....._orrrrr_..my good friend, Mistah Scotty Winter! [Another large pop at the thought of a XJ/Winter rematch] XJ: Mistah Winter, you do _not_ know how much Xavier Lee wants you to win this match tonite so your stupid _stupid_ self could be thrown around the arena one more time.
[More chuckles] XJ: But ya' know somethin', it just dont matter to Xavier Lee who wins. 'Cause no matter who it is, after they get done playin' with Xavier Lee, their gonna be missin' the toy they brought....and _that_ would be the
strap-a-leather-and-gold. [Another mammoth pop as Xavier Lee Jackson sets the microphone on the apron and slides out of the ring as he exits the arena area to a thunderous ovation.]
CK: XJ's challenging the winner, Jeffy! JS: Actually, Chad, Vic Rizzo gave him the first shot at the title, due to his performance two weeks ago in that no-count-out, no-DQ, falls count anywhere brawl with Scott Winters!
