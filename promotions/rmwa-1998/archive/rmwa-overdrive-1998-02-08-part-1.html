CK: They're not hiring a third announcer, are they? JS: No Chad.....Unfortunately, "The Hollywood Heartthrob" Dave Diamond has left the RMWA due to contractual difficulties....

CK: Oh, gee Jeffy, they tell us what they *think* is happening, but they don't even know themselves! These RMWA really make me wonder how they got their jobs. Just call the action, ok?
JS: Okay, fine. Our first match of the evening was a double debut, as "Stonewall" Ed Jackson took on Ed Rogers....Let's go to these words from Ed Rogers.
------ [Fade from black.] [A sock takes up most of the screen. Not a normal sock, mind you, but a really, really, really dirty looking sock puppet, with a magic marker face. In back of the sock, is one of those red and white swirly hypnotizing doodads.]
[The Puppet] You are getting sleepy... Very sleepy... Good... Your eyes... Their eye lids are getting heavy... Yes, oh so very heavy... Good... Slowly now, your eyes become so heavy that you can't stand it anymore... You must close your eyes, now... Good... You are in a very deep sleep, but you will listen to your new master, Smelly the Sockpuppet, and you will do as he says, but when you awake you shall only remember what I tell you to remember... Good...
[The camera pulls away from Smelly, and now we can see the man who's hand is pecariously placed up his rear end. That reminds me of a Bill
Clinton joke... No! No, I have to get a grip on myself. I have to describe this guy. I must have self discipline. Okay, the puppeteer, he's dressed in a blue and red knit sweater on top of a white turtleneck. The man is slightly heavy set, especially around the waist.
His face is fairly clean cut looking: short brown hair combed to the side, friendly if somewhat goofy grin, glazed over eyes, large black teardrop painted over his left cheek. Well, all right, I lied about the clean cut part. The man looks like he needs supervision when around sharp objects.]
[Smelly the Sockpuppet.] Now that you RMWA fans are completely under my power, I would like to introduce my friend and I...
[Oh, hold on a second, I forgot something, and it's kind of important. When the sockpuppet, Smelly, talks, the man's lips move. It's both funny and down right scary at the same time.]
[Smelly] ...I am the greatest manager in the history of professional wrestling, the innovator of violence, the man that basically defined a manager's proper position in modern wrestling. I have ravaged the ranks of the
INWI and the IEWF, now the LWC, for years, and now I have come to this wrestling organization to bring it into the twenty-first century with the same kind of managerial genus that I have used all over the world. I am the one, and the only master of disaster, the eliminator, the man, the monster, the machine, I am Smelly the Sockpuppet.
Now, your probably wondering what an international superstar like me is doing in a... [The man in the background, controlling Smelly, yanks on his, um,
Sock-body once or twice to get the puppet's attention.]
[Smelly] What do you want, stupid? [Man, speaking with an unconfident voice] Well, er... I thought that you might want to tell the television neighbors who I am... [Smelly] Now, Ed, haven't we gone over this a million times before? Who do the plebeians come to watch?
[Ed] Well, you, Smelly. [Smelly] Right, Ed. And why do they want to see me, instead of say, someone we both know by the name of Ed Rogers?
[Ed] It's because they know that you are the brains and they know that I am just a puppet in your skilled hands.
[Smelly] Right, Ed. Now, if they already know that you are insignificant, why should I even waist my time telling them who you are if they are just going to think of you as that stupid hanger on that Smelly the
Sockpuppet, greatest manager of all time, keeps around as a pet. [Ed perks up for some reason.] [Ed] WOW!! [Smelly] What the heck are you happy about? [Ed] You mean you think of _me_ as a pet? [Smelly] Well... er... [Ed] You just made this the happiest day of my life! [Smelly] I mean, maybe a gold fish or an ant. Not a serious investment like a dog or something. I certainly would never get emotionally attached to a loony like you.
[Ed] Oh. [Smelly] Well, you've wasted enough of our time already with your silly little outbursts, lets get back to the real purpose of this interview.
[Smelly looks back into the camera.] You are probably wondering, that is you would be wondering this, if you weren't in a hypnotic trance, why a huge international star like Smelly the Sockpuppet has showed up in a dinky-rink wrestling organization like this.
Well, the answer is simple: Charity. I care about the huddled masses of starving, homeless, stupid children in backwards areas of the world like Rwanda, and Somalia, and Cleveland. I care, so I have been touring all the bush leagues on the indy circuit, putting together some money to give to one of those charities with the lady walking around in the slum, talking about the stupid poor children.
No, no, you people don't have to clap, that's just how much I care. Not many people know this, but I like buying medicine to help the poor, and
I like buying food to feed the bolemic, I like to buy condos for the homeless, I like...
[Ed] To buy whiskey for getting drunk and beating me with the studded belt. [Smelly] Shuddup, Ed! [Smelly looks back in the camera.] Kids today... [chuckle.] My point is that if I am to feed the hungry, and house the poor, I am going to need more money than "jobs" like this will pay. So I need you people at home, the people that Ed calls the "television friends"...
[Ed] Neighbors. [Smelly] Whatever. All I know is that I need you guys to help me out. So, when
I snap my fingers, you will remember none of this conversation, but you will have a burning desire, no a need to sign your names to checks with out filling in that "amount" section, and then sending the checks to
"The Poor Stupid People's Fund" care of Smelly the Sockpuppet, 123,
Sesame Street, The Neighborhood, 12345. The poor stupid people's fund also accepts slightly used women's underwear. Thank you.
[Ed snaps his fingers on his free hand. Fade to black.] ------ +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ This match was even for nearly the entire time. Ed Jackson hit Ed Rogers with an amazing thrust kick-spine buster-powerbomb string, but the ever-so friendly Ed Rogers was able to kick out every time. However, Ed Rogers quickly gained the advantage thanks to the advice of his "manager", Smelly the Sock Puppet, and managed to take down Jackson and lock him in an STF, then a cross-face chicken wing, and THEN an armlock-leglock submission, all of which Jackson was able to out of thanks to the ropes. This one ended when Ed Jackson rolled to the outside to avoid a Smelly the Sockpupptet-enhanced Mandible Claw, when Ed Rogers came off the top rope with a senton backsplash. This maneuver successfully knocking out Ed Jackson, but allowing for Ed Rogers to roll into the ring around the '8' count, giving him the count-out win.
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ CK: That Ed Rogers is one nutty guy, I think I like him! JS: Yes....Needless to say, if the RMWA had some sort of a 'Hardcore' division, Ed Rogers would be a top contender....But we don't, so, tough luck Ed.
CK: Way to be blunt, Jeffy. JS: Our second match of the evening featured one "Razorblade" Zakk Wylder taking on "The Enforcer" Chris Michaels....
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Chris Michaels dominated this match early, staggering Zakk Wylder with a well-placed thrust kick to the jaw, and a perfectly executed fallway slam followed up with a powerbomb. Just as it seemed Zakk Wylder was finished, Wylder landed a desperation DDT on Michaels, and proceeded to demonstrate his boxing ability by driving Michaels into the corner with a series of rights and lefts. As soon as Wylder was able to get Michaels to cover-up, he hopped onto the second rope, and DDT'd Michaels, giving him the win.
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ [ Camera shows Fuse, dressed in full ring attire, being carried by
Blackout at one arm and three other men, all around the same height and build, carrying Fuse by the rest of his limbs. Blackout is wearing an ankle-length black leather trenchcoat, black pants, black leather boots,
and he has a pair of John Lennon style sunglasses resting at the bridge of his nose. He has long black hair that reaches the middle of his back
and a pale complexion. The man at the opposite arm wears a black knee-length leather trenchcoat similar to that of Blackout's, a black shirt underneath, black pants, black leather boots, and a pair of dark shades over his eyes. He has long black hair that reaches down to his shoulders, a black goatee, and a pale complexion. The man behind
Blackout is wearing a black t-shirt with the face of Jesus on it, black pants and black leather boots, and it is all covered with a long black trenchcoat that ends at mid-ankle. He has fine black hair that is parted in the middle and rests on his shoulders. And finally the man to
his side is wearing a plain black t-shirt, black pants and black leather boots, also with a long black trenchcoat. He has black hair that is parted in the middle and rests at his chin. A silver ankh hangs around his neck, and you realize how similiar he looks to the man next to him.
] [ All four men exit the backstage area and bring Fuse down the aisleway, the crowd immediately begins to boo upon sight of Blackout and the other four men. Some people yell out obscenities at them and others go so far as to throw things at them. None of them show any emotion toward the fans, not acknowledging them in the slightest. As they reach the ring,
Blackout and one of the men at Fuse's legs take Fuse by both ends of his
body and roll him into the ring as the two men who were carrying also carrying him climb into the ring. Blackout walks over to the commentator's table and stands in front of Killian and Stewart. Camera gets an up close shot of Blackout pointing at each of them and saying something to them that isn't picked up by the microphones. He grabs a microphone from off the table and slides it into the ring. He then grabs one of the tables from ringside and slides that into the ring as well, then joining the other three men. ]
CK: Oh boy, here we go! Blackout and these other three men, shrouded in mystery for the time being, have an unconscious Fuse in the ring and we knew he was backstage, but he isn't scheduled to wrestle tonight.
Blackout and the other three men each have a corner of this table now and they're setting it up in the middle of the ring!
JS: We need security out here, _now!_ Oh, wait, nevermind...here comes security now! Shawn Friday and the rest of security are coming out here to help Fuse, but two of these men have just grabbed chairs from ringside and they're threatening any of them that even try to come in with them!
CK: The other two men are dragging Fuse onto that table now and he's starting to move a little bit, but whatever they're going to do to him is going to put him right back into unconsciousness! What's this? One of the other men is getting up onto the top rope and he's sitting there, facing the crowd. Blackout climbs up underneath him and hoists him onto his shoulder...
JS: OH GOD! NO! DON'T DO IT! CROWD: OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!! JS: BLACKOUT JUST _POWERBOMBED_ THAT MAN RIGHT ON TOP OF FUSE AND
THROUGH THE TABLE! Fuse is hurt! He has to be! That man isn't even feeling the effects of it as he gets right back up now and Blackout has that microphone!
[ Blackout grabs the microphone off the mat and points to the security outside the ring. ] BLACKOUT: "You all just wait right out there until we're finished, because if you so much as come near entering this ring, we'll snap that runt's neck in two and you'll have been responsible for the paralysis of him because of your careless actions. Sure, it would be against the law and we would probably go to prison for the rest of our lives, but it sure as hell would be _worth_ it."
[ Blackout laughs sadistically and the entire crowd erupts with boos, showering the ring with debris. ]
JS: Security isn't even going to do anything, because as far as they know, these men could be psychotic killers without a care of who they hurt and how they do it. And from what I've seen already, that's exactly what these men are! Cold and heartless human beings.
[ Blackout looks down at Fuse and grins. ] "Now isn't this just a pretty little picture? You see, Fuse, I _knew_ it was you that attacked me last week before even hearing your pathetic, whining, crybaby little voice on Latenight. The way you tied me up...put that note on my forehead. [ Chuckles. ] I always liked the fact that you had no originality, Fuse. Luckily, wherever Blackout goes, my brothers come with me. You should know that by now."
[ Blackout looks down at the unconscious Fuse, lying now in the center of the ring with pieces of the table scattered over his body. He shakes his head and then looks back out into the crowd, smirking as he hears the boos pour in from the audience. ]
"Ah, how good it is to hear the call of hatred again. The utter disgust from which each and every one of your hearts shows toward my brothers and I. Ladies and gentlemen...for those of you who do not quite know who we are...or for those who are simply too ignorant to pay attention to the rest of the wrestling world. We are the Four Horsemen... [ Grins slightly and then it slowly fades into that of a sneer. ] ...of the
_Apocalypse._ And Fuse...when you said that eliminating me would be a small task, you were completely mistaken. You of all people should know that the 4HotA never go down without a brutal...bloody...and destructive fight. [ Grins. ] And that's exactly the way I'm going to settle matters with you, masked hero. You always wish to save the day and be the "good guy", but you know what? There's always going to be a roadblock standing in your way. I've beaten you once before for my brothers and I
_will_ defeat you again."
[ Blackout smirks and the crowd boos again before he continues. ] "Now, allow me to take a moment to introduce to you my brothers of the apocalypse. The men that shall lead the RMWA into the dark future that awaits them. Each of our goals equal...each of our attitudes the same.
Because there truly is only one purpose to our being here. And that is to wreak havoc and cause the carnage that every man fears could be possible. We shatter the hopes and careers of our peers, we destroy the wrestlers that have a legacy to live up to and reduce them to nothing more than spectators. Because that's the only thing they can do after we have crippled them and put them in a bed where they're served three meals a day through a tube. [ Snickers. ] That's right, RMWA fans...prepare to be faced against the dark challenge of the Four
Horsemen of the Apocalypse. Pestilence and Nightshade, two of my closest friends. Some of the times we have shared breaking bones and cracking skulls. [ Chuckles. ] It's a legacy of our own, but more than that...it's a _livlihood!_ And then Penance...a man with usually little to say, but so much on his mind. One of the founding fathers of the
4HotA, along with Pestilence, of course. But I digress...I really am taking up too much of the spotlight, so I believe I shall hand it over to the true _brothers_ of the group..."
[ Blackout smiles and hands the microphone over to Pestilence and steps out of the way. Pestilence and Nightshade both step forward at the same time and chuckle as they look at their unconscious adversary.
Pestilence grins evilly as he looks into the camera and begins to speak.] PESTILENCE: "Fuse, Fuse, Fuse..you _never_ learn, do you? After all the times the apocalypse left you lying in a pool of your own blood, after all the tears you cried in the privacy of the locker room, and after all the nightmares we so graciously gave to you, you still come back for more. Let this be a lesson to you, child of Britain. A pendulum that is pushed _always_ swings back, and you have pushed us far beyond our boundary, and for that, you will _suffer_."
[ Pestilence then hands the microphone over to his brother, Nightshade. He takes the microphone with a slight grin and nod towards Pestilence, and nudges Fuse in the ribs a few times with his boot before speaking. ]
NIGHTSHADE: "I can't help but feel a case of deja vu. [grins] A persistent sheep you are, 'caped crusader,' but eventually you'll run out of wool to protect your British ass, making it quite fit for the kicking. All these months, we've pounded against of the gates of the universe, waiting to be set free. Waiting for our destiny to contact us and tell us where we stand, and it has. _You_ have set us free, Fuse.
You alone. And it is _you_, friend, that will suffer the consequences." [ The crowd boos Nightshade with a vengeance, and he looks towards them with a sadistic smile on his face. He then looks at Fuse again and resumes speaking. ]
"And there were four. Four who rode the jet black steeds, and I promise you that this is _only_ the beginning, for I have seen the future. Take not for granted the air that you breathe and the heart that pumps blood through your veins, because soon, it will all come to an abrupt ending.
You will all be left seeing red, and you will drown in a sea of your own bloody feces. In the past we have demolished even the greatest of champions, and from here, it can only get better. Well, for us, anyway.
[smirks] Retribution hath come, and it _will_ consume all that choose to ignore it." [ Nightshade and Pestilence both step back and hand the microphone over to Penance, who slowly accepts it and then observes Fuse for a moment.
Penance walks over to the corner turnbuckle and climbs up on top of it, sitting as he looks down at Fuse, talking directly to him. ]
PENANCE: "What chaotic tragedy has entered your life now is the same that was haunting you almost everywhere you have gone. Where Fuse is...the apocalypse follows, because that is our nature. Eliminate the weak and the daring that wish to end what we have so skillfully begun.
Only this time, Fuse, you came to where the apocalypse was. It is not often someone follows the apocalypse, but it is the path in which you have chosen and a dark path it is indeed. You wish to rid the world of
Blackout? Then you must also try to rid the world of the apocalypse.
Something that will never happen and it is inevitable because of what we represent, Fuse. But alas, I am not going to have the opportunity face you in the near future, thy enemy. Unfortunately, there are limits to how many can be officially _in_ this place, but no matter. It's their choice to have lost a two-time Light-Heavyweight champion."
[ Penance grins and then turns himself around on the turnbuckle, standing up onto the top rope as he looks out into the vast audience surrounding him. Fans throw things, trying to hit him as he stands there, but Penance doesn't move to avoid the debris. He simply stands there and continues. ]
"It is because of our legacy that shall bring the Four Horsemen of the
Apocalypse glory once more. It will once again show the wrestling world why we are to be _feared_ by each and every man that steps into the ring with us. Unpredicatble is an understatement for what people have called
us. Labelling us as cheaters and attackers. Well, to those who put those labels on us, I say to you that you are completely..._right._ [
Laughs. ] But what we also are, is damned good at what we do! I would go so far as to say the best in the ring when it comes to skill and intellect. My brothers would agree...and once we have brought the devastation to the RMWA...there is no...turning...back!"
[ Penance snickers as he drops the microphone and then moonsaults from the top rope, landing on top of Fuse one more time. Fuse rolls over onto his stomach after the impact, clutching his midsection. Blackout walks back over to the microphone and grabs it off the ground. ]
BLACKOUT: "Heed our words, RMWA...for the apocalypse is upon you. As said by the horsemen and delivered by the apocalypse...you shall be engaged by War..." [ Blackout hands the microphone to Penance. ] PENANCE: "...overcome by Famine..." [ Penance hands the microphone to Pestilence. ] PESTILENCE: "...sentenced by Pestilence..." [ Pestilence hands the microphone to Nightshade. ] NIGHTSHADE: "...and judged by Death. So it is written...and so we shall make it come to pass." [ Nightshade drops the microphone and all four men leave the ring, sliding underneath the ropes and hopping over the guardrail, leaving through the crowd to avoid security, who all pour into the ring and help
Fuse up. ]
CK: Jeff.....I'm scared..... JS: As we all are Chad. As we all are. <Camera fades out>
