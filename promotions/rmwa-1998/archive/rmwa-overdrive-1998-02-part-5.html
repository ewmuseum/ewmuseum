<Camera fades in> Sal Preston: Good Evening RMWA Fans! (crowd cheers for the ever popular Sal Preston) We have a very special guest for you tonight ladies and gentlemen, he is one of the premiere athletes in the RMWA, and is in with a great chance of becoming the first ever RMWA cruiserweight champion! (The crowd cheer again and a start of "Raven!, Raven!, Raven!" starts up.) So without further ado, let me introduce.......The man who on
Thursday night set the fastest ever winning time in the RMWA's short history, of 3 seconds.........."Superstar" Simon Brock!!!!!
(Jesus Christ Superstar blares through the PA and Simon Brock and Debra Williams come through the curtain to a chorus of boos from the fans, but he seems pretty amused by it all. Brock is wearing his in ring attire consisting of his long black trunks and black boots. Debra is wearing a long elegant white dress and a dark red lipstick and is looking gorgeous as ever. He gives the crowd a few poses in the entrance way which really riles them up and then proceeds to the ring, grabbing and ripping up a "Brock Sucks" sign on his way. When the pair get to the ring, Debra waves to the fans and blows them a kiss which results in a huge amount of wolf whistles from the fans which seems to amuse her. Brock walks over to Sal
Preston and leans on his shoulder awaiting the first question.) Preston: Alright, Simon Brock, since this is the first time I&rsquo;ve interviewed you since the start of the RMWA, may I just start by staying you&rsquo;ve made quite an impression so far.
Brock: You know Sal, I have to agree with you. I think I kinda showed just what ability I have on Thursday night with my record breaking victory...
(the video wall (do we have one?) Shows Brock carrying the beaten down Amazing Bob to the ring and pinning him straight away.)
Preston: Uhhhhh, yeah. You have to admit it wasn&rsquo;t exactly the hardest match you'll ever wrestle in the RMWA. Bob isn&rsquo;t the greatest wrestler on his best day, and he had already wrestled in the card!
Brock: Hey! I didn&rsquo;t ask Bob to wrestle twice, its not my fault he has some kind of complex about being amazing. All I do is beat who&rsquo;s in front of me, I don&rsquo;t care if they&rsquo;ve wrestled twice in a night or not once in the last year. ....Lets get off this stupid subject anyway.
Preston: Ok, maybe you'd like to address a man I interviewed here last week, a man who issued a challenge of sorts you, that being "Big Daddy"
Leon Jackson!! (crowd pops) Brock: Oh puh-lease! Jackson, I don't know what you know about me, but I don&rsquo;t grant matches to rookies, particularly not a clueless putz like you. You think that by offering me $2000 I'd let you get in the ring with me?!? (Brock and Debra both laugh) I don&rsquo;t get out of bed for
$2000 let alone step in the ring. Make it a 6 figure number and maybe, just MAYBE ill think about it, but since that&rsquo;s more money than you'll make in your whole life, you might as well forget about it! And one more thing before we move along from Mr. Jackson, I hear the RMWA front office had a phone call from the 70's last night asking for you, they want their black v-neck sweater back. (Brock and Debra laugh, Preston gives a sort of nervous smile) Get yourself a decent dress sense, a decent hair cut, and then learn some wrestling, and then you might be worthy of being in this federation with me. In the meantime, you might as well run along home before someone embarrasses you! The Man They Call Bill maybe?
Preson: Alright! Fighting talk from Simon Brock.......I have to ask you, who do you consider to be your main competition in the cruiserweight division?
Brock: Sal, there is NO competition. I'm rubbing shoulders with the heavyweights in the main events while these losers are wrestling for peanuts in non televised matches! I think most people agree that when it comes to cruiserweights in the RMWA, there is only one man that&rsquo;s gonna be wearing that belt, and that&rsquo;s ME! (crowd boos) Boo all you like, you can cheer for your Red Raven&rsquo;s and your Leon Jackson&rsquo;s, but each and every one of them knows that when it comes to the crunch, they cant hold a candle to "Superstar" Simon Brock in the ring, or in anything else for that matter! (smiles at Debra, Debra smiles back) I don&rsquo;t know what plans the RMWA has for sorting out the cruiserweight title, but it doesn&rsquo;t matter. I'll win any tournament, or any kind of match, and ill wrestle any amount of times in an evening. IT DOESN'T
MATTER!...... Later Sal, come on Debs.
(With that, Jesus Christ Superstar comes over the PA for the second time. Brock climbs to the second rope and raises his hands ala Steve Austin with a cocky grin on his face and is met with a chorus of boos. Debra looks on and claps for her man. Brock and Debra exit the ring and Brock struts back to the dressing room with Debra close behind)
JS: Like him or not, Simon Brock is one talented man! CK: Who cares if they like him? The fact of the matter is, he's almost guarenteed to be the first lightweight champion!
JS: Fans, let's go to the ring for our first main event of the evening. <Camera flips to Dave> Dave Ritter: Introducing first, he hails from Cleveland, Ohio, and weighs in at 218 pounds....He is accompanied to the ring by Vampyra and Ghoulgirl....Here is NEEEEECCCCRRROOOOSSS!!
<"Long Hard Road out of Hell" by Marilyn Manson begins to play over the PA as the eerie trio of Necros, Vampyra, and Ghoulgirl make their way to the ring.>
CK: Cleveland, eh? I wonder if they know Drew Carey? JS: Shut up, Chad. <Camera flips back to Dave> DR: AND HIS OPPONENT....He hails from Chicago, Illinois....Weighing in at 228 pounds....Here is BLAAAAACKOOOOOUUUUT!!!!
<"Fade to Black" by Metallica begins to play as the lights go out in the arena, and many of the fans in attendance hold their lighters high over their heads....>
CK: Jesus, it's like i'm at Wrestlepalooza! JS: This is odd....Blackout's music is playing, but I don't see Blackout anywhere.... CK: Maybe that's his "thing". He calls himself Blackout, maybe you're not supposed to see him come to the ring?
JS: I highly doubt that....This doesn't look good, at all. CK: Awwwww, great. That suit Sal Preston is coming down now! <Chad stands up and proceeds to shout threats and profanities at Preston> JS: The lights have come back up now as Sal Preston is making his way to the ring....Albeit reluctantly.
CK: Reluctantly? Sally's just afraid of that babe, Vampyra! You know how he gets around women like her, Jeffy.
JS: She's a vampire, Chad! CK: I know! She can suck my.....blood anytime! JS: <ignoring Chad> Sal's got the mic, let's see what he has to say. <Camera flips to Sal> Sal Preston: Uh, Hello all....I know we had scheduled Necros to face Blackout tonight, but, uh....
<Preston pauses, scratching the back of his head> SP: We, uhh, can't find the guy. All his gear's here, but he's gone. Looks to me like he left against his will. Anyways, we've got all our people looking for him, so hopefully we'll have some sort of update later for you guys tonight.
<Crowd begins to boo and throw trash in the ring> SP: Oh, and, Necros wins by default. <Booing grows louder as Preston exits> JS: That certainly is odd.... CK: No Jeffy, Necros is the odd one! And, where'd Blackout disappear to?! <Suddenly, the lights go out> CK: AAAAAAIIIIIIIIIEEEEE!!!!!!!! JS: Damnit Killian! Get your hand off my knee!! <"Dove's Cry" by the Artist begins to play as the man known as "The KunterTaker" begins pushing a casket to the ring.>
JS: What on Earth is going on!? CK: Well Jeffy, that guy there, that's "Necros", and those two women with him, that's "Vampyra" and "Ghoulgirl"--
JS: <interupting> I didn't mean that, I meant why's that guy pushing a casket to the ring?!?
CK: You mean The KunterTaker? I dunno, why is he pushing a casket to the ring? <The lights come back on> JS: This oughta be interesting.... CK: Necros doesn't seem to like what's going on.... JS: The "KunterTaker" opens the lid and---SCOTT WINTERS!! CK: AND HE'S DRESSED UP AS A VAMPYRE! HAHAHAHAHA!!!! JS: Complete with cape and fake teeth, Scott Winters is going into the ring AND NECROS CHARGES---
<<<<<<<<<<<<<CRACK>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> CK: HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAH!!!!!!! JS: HE HAD A COCONUT HIDDEN UNDERNEATH HIS CAPE!!! CK: HE SHATTERED IT OVER NECROS HEAD!! I LOVE IT!! JS: Vampyra and Ghoulgirl quickly run to the outside....Winters has knocked Necros out COLD!
CK: Hehehehehe.... JS: Winters removes the cape....He picks up Necros and---QUICK SNAP DDT BY WINTERS!! CK: Right onto the coconut remnants! JS: The "KunterTaker" is in the ring now, rolling Necros into that casket....And Scott Winters is holding off the attacks of Vampyra!
CK: That Vampyra's one tough-as-nails chick, if you ask me. JS: The "KunterTaker" has gotten Necros into the casket, Winters has held off Vampyra's attacks, much to her dismay....And they're making their exit!
CK: OK guys, this is getting old fast....WAIT! JS: What?!? CK: Kendel Jones Superstar is up and pointing at Scott Winters!! JS: This should be interesting.....Maybe now we'll find out why Kendel Jones is in the RMWA!
CK: Denver IS pretty far away from the Jones family ranch.... JS: Kendel's standing in front of the ring......AND HE THROWS HIM A JOHN DEERE HAT!! CK: .....A John Deere hat? I mean.....A JOHN DEERE HAT!!! HOLY MOTHER OF GOD!! JS: Scott Winters sets the John Deere hat down on top of the casket as he and "The KunterTaker" push Necros out of the arena.....Kendel hops over the guardrail, and it's time for our second main event!
Jeff Stewart (standard play by play guy) and Chad Killian (smart-ass color man) Dave Ritter JS: Time for more action folks as The American Heroes and the Raving Psychos. Let's go to Dave Ritter now in the ring.
(Camera fades off of Stewart and switches over to Dave Ritter standing in the ring with a microphone in one hand and a set of index cards in the other.)
DR: Ladies and Gentlemen...(Crowd pops) Our next match up is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first...
("Where do the Children Go", by The Hooters begins to play over the PA) DR: Weighing in at a combined weight of 499 pounds...Here is Casey Spahn and "Dynamite" Danny Anderson...THE AMERICAN HEROES!!!
(Two guys run out from behind the curtain full speed slapping fans hands on the way. They get to the ring and split up slapping hands all around the ring. The finally get in the ring and start bouncing off the ropes opposite to one another. The meet in middle with a high five and make their way to their corner. One of the guys, Casey Spahn, has Short brown hair, cut in a military style. Hazel eyes and Tattoo of 82nd Airborne crest on his left arm. He has on a short red, white, and blue jacket, clasped at the bottom, but otherwise open. He also wears his red beret he received while in the US Army. The other guy. Danny Anderson, Has shoulder length blonde hair, piercing blue eyes, and a killer smile. He has a short-clipped goatee and a diamond earring in his left ear. He has on blue and white wrestling pants, with red lace up boots.)
DR: And their opponents... ("Trigger Happy Jack (Drive-by a Go-Go)" by Poe takes the place of "Where do the Children Go" on the PA)
DR: Weighing in at a combined weight of 620lbs. Here is Mucho and Mondo...THE RAVING PSYCHOS!!!!!
(Both men come bursting down to the ring with saliva hanging from their mouth. Both guys wear black wrestling boots and full length black wrestling tights with the word "RAVE" written all over them in white lettering. Their heads are shaved, and have scars all over. The hit the ring and pounce on The American Heroes.)
JS: They're at it already the Psychos are pounding away on the Heroes. This match could be over before it begins.
CK: Well I don't care who wins one team is a set of anal assassins and the other are a set of slobbering idiots. So I could care less.
JS: The ref has finally gotten these teams a part Looks as if Anderson and Mondo are starting things off. Mondo and Anderson now standing in the middle of the ring in a stare down, and I do mean down. Mondo with a significant height and weight advantage here...Anderson backs off a little, and now fires in with a right hand, and another...Now a backhand chop, and another...A third, and Mondo is just absorbing all this damage...Anderson backs off a little more, and now he hits the ropes...Shoulderblock...OH
CK: Man! That's like hitting a brickwall! Anderson goes down like a shot, and now he is back up, and he retreats to his corner and he and his little butt buddy talk about what to do against this monster.
JS: Mondo isn't waiting he charges in and grabs Anderson and sends him into the opposite corner. Anderson tries to throw some punches but Mondo just absorbs them all. Mondo now throws a few punches of his own sending Anderson back. Mondo whips him to the ropes and here comes Mondo off the ropes as well and a massive lariat by Mondo.
CK: He clobbered him with that it surprises me though that he even knows how to perform a high caliber move like that.
JS: Anyway Mondo now picking Anderson up, scoop slam by Mondo. Mondo now coming off the rope and drops the elbow. Anderson moves out of the way. Anderson now up to his feet as well as Mondo. Anderson pounding the abdominal region of Mondo. Mondo is laughing at Anderson and letting him belt him in the gut! Mondo looks like he is tired of playing and grabs Anderson by the skull and throws him to his partner!!
CK: I knew he couldn't pull two moves off now he is relying on his strength to back him up.
JS: Well when your as big as this guy you can do those kind of things. Anderson tags Spahn in and Spahn now looking over his opposition. Spahn charges in and pulls Mondo's feet out from underneath him. Spahn now pounding on the big guy and Mondo is feeling this blows to the head. Spahn picks Mondo up to his feet and backs him to the ropes. Spahn now sending Mondo to the ropes, no Mondo reverses and sends Spahn to the ropes. Mondo misses the boot, now missing the clothesline. Spahn with a cross body but is caught by Mondo.
CK: Once again he is using that brute strength. See he is carrying him around the ring. This guy is an Amazon. He shouldn't even be allowed to wrestle.

CK: something intelligent for a change I guess. JS: Mondo is outside and he has a chair. Mondo back in the ring and he clobbers Spahn with it. Mondo now has Spahn in his arms. Powerslam onto the chair! This could end it. Mondo covers but their is no ref Randy is bitching him out?

JS: Mondo picks Spahn up and tags in his partner Mucho. This guy is as big as Mondo! CK: Just as stupid I bet. JS: We'll find out in a minute. Mucho pounds on Anderson's face and now he, he's BITING IT!!!
CK: Oh boy...this guy will be fun to watch. JS: Mucho let's go and starts chasing the ref around the ring? Is he retarded or something? Mucho stops going after the ref and proceeds after Spahn. Mucho doubles Spahn over and sets him into a standing head scissors...JACKNIFE.
CK: This ain't WCW no cops are gonna arrest him for that one. JS: Mucho now tags in his partner now Mucho and Mondo are in the ring. Mondo with a spinning sweep kick and Mucho with a spin wheel kick! Spahn just got a little SHOCK TREATMENT!!!! Here is the cover by Mondo...
ONE... TWO... THREE!!!!! DR: Here are your winners at a time of 5:35...THE RAVING PSYCHOS!!!!!!!!! JS: Well folks, that's our show for tonight-- CK: <interupting> WAIT! JS: What is it now Chad? CK: You forgot about the Supercard announcement!! JS: Ohhhh.......Yeah, I did.
