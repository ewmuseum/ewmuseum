Dave Diamond hits Xavier Lee Jackson with a ring bell Stan Silvers executes the Doc Plex on Dave Diamond Xavier Lee Jackson knocks Walter Withers off the ring apron with an X-Line
"Dimebag" Doug Baxter executes Primal Concrete Sledge on Frankie
The Amazing Bob falls down Scott Daubs pummels Osiris in the corner Scott Winters applies the Testicular Claw on The Amazing Bob Osiris powerbombs Scott Daubs Johnny Johnston gives his 'insight' as guest ring announcer The Spontaneously Combusting Man catches on fire The Raving Psychos attack The Nomadic Tribe Scott Winters attacks a Cow> <Camera flips to the sold-out RMWA arena in Denver, Colorado. 8000-plus fans have jammed themselves into the arena, and are screaming at a near-deafening level. Signs like "RMWA NEEDS WRITERS", "WRITE, DAMNIT", "MARRY ME BOB", and "WILDSIDE 4 LIFE" can be seen throughout the arena. The camera then flips to the RMWA broadcast booth, where Chad Killian and Jeff Stewart sit>
JS: HELLO EVERYONE AND WELCOME TO RMWA OVERDRIVE!!! <Crowd pops as fireworks erupt in the ring> CK: I still can't get over that introduction we had earlier! That was GREAT! JS: Fans, before we run-down the matches we saw to start off tonight's show, we might as well show you the tape of tonight's "National Anthem".
CK: Get those VCR's rolling, folks, this is a classic moment in RMWA history!! <Tape rolls> [Announcer:] Ladies and Gentlemen, we are proud to introduce to you the one and only, Canada's award winning singer, Bryan Adams!!
(Crowd Pops...and then a man comes out pushing a shopping cart with a bumblebee in it. The man wears a New York Yankee jersey with Anderson and the #72 on the back of it, and blue jeans. He is sort of chubby and has short blonde hair)
[Chad Killian] Who the hell is that? [Jeff Stewart] Its Bryan Adams, I guess. (The man gets in the ring with the shopping cart and grabs the mic. There's a short pause..then he begins.)
[Man:] I'M AFRAID OF AMERICANS! I'M AFRAID OF THE WORLD! I'M AFRAID I CAN'T HELP IT! BECAUSE YOU ALL SUCK! ("I'm Afraid Of Americans" by David Bowie plays over the PA system as the man pulls out a hat from his crotch that says "Winters Is God" and puts it on)
[Man:] I'M AFRAID OF AMERICANS! ESPECIALLY THOSE IN COLORADO! WINTERS RULES! ALL BECAUSE MR BUMBLEBEE SAID SO! [Jeff Stewart] Get this guy out of here! (Some officials grab the mic off him and he grabs the shopping cart and leaves.) [Chad Killian] Obviously some deranged Scott Winters fan. (He grabs a camera and starts to talk.) [Man:] My name is Bryan Adams, it says so on my Drivers License. (He pulls out his wallet and shows the camera a drivers license that has masking tape and written in Red crayon it says "bryan Adams")
[Man:] If you want to know who I am, you'll know by the end of tonight. (He starts to laugh hysterically and psychodically and then leaves with the shopping cart.)
<Tape ends> CK: Shawn Friday really needs to clamp down on those fans getting hold of the microphones....What happened to the REAL Bryan Adams, anyway?
JS: Chad, Bryan Adams was never scheduled to come here tonight, at least, the real one wasn't.
CK: Ahhh.... JS: OK folks, we were scheduled to see two matches before the show went on the air, that being Vulgar versus Chico Jericho and Masoa Tsirato versus Red Raven, however, "Big Daddy" Leon Jackson planned otherwise....
CK: Indeed he did, and I must say, hat's off, Leon Jackson! JS: He knocked Jericho out cold, Chad! CK: Maybe so, but he saved us from the torment of actually having to watch another boring RMWA match!
JS: Fans, Vulgar gets this one by default....And speaking of Leon Jackson, let's go to these words from him.
------ (The scene opens in the lockeroom backstage at RMWA Latenight. Leon
Jackson is sitting on a bench in his black tights. He has a towell draped around his neck. He smiles for the camera, and uses the towell to whipe his forehead.)
Jackson:Whew! Did ya see that one RMWA? Damn, what a match. That one took alot outta "Big Daddy" baby. You don't even know. Tunaboy is one hell of a competetor.
(A broad smile begins to emerge on Jackson's face. We can see that he is fighting it, but to no avail. He bursts into laughter.)
Jackson:(Whiping a tear from his eye)Oh man, that's priceless. Did you see the way I dismantled that fool? Not that that in itself was any big accomplishment. But you see kids, the thing is, I did it with *style* baby. That's what makes all the difference. Any two bit schmuck could have watzed out there and handed that jerk his head in less than a minute, but
I did it with finesse, with a certain savior fare. I put on a show baby, and *that's* what seperates me from the average man baby. Whenever "Big
Daddy" steps in that ring, no matter who I'm up against, It's showtime pal. Plain and simple. It's like Slick Ric said, "I could wrestle a broom and entertain you people." Not that any of you slack-jawed yokels are worth the effort, but that's okay, "Big Daddy" is a generous man. I don't mind bearing the burden of being the lone bright spot in your pathetic little lives. But that's just cause I love you. HAHA! That and cause the
RMWA pays me a hell of a lot of money to do it, but that's a whole other story.
On to more important, well maybe not more *important*, but more pressing matters. I got a few words for the so-called "superstar" of the lightweight division, Simon Brock. I heard what you had to say last night
Simon, and for some reason I feel that some of your remarks were intended for "Big Daddy". I'm gonna save myself the trouble of havin' to listen to you bitch and moan. I'll go ahead and agree to a match right now. Now,
I know what you're sayin' right now, "But Big Daddy, I'm not worthy."
Well, you're right, you're not worthy, but it's all good, nobody is worthy of this. (Jackson flexes his bicept and kisses it.) So you just name the time and the place, and try not to piss yourself on the way to the ring. I know you're excited, I mean who wouldn't be, but just the same try and control yourself.
Last but not least, I just want to dedicate last night's matchup to a very special person. A man who has inspired me greatly...my idol, Chico
Jericho. Chico, I love you brother. You have no idea what being in the same fed with you means to me. As a matter of fact, I think I'll show you my gratitude this Sunday. See ya there baby. Peace and hairgrease.
------ CK: Peace and hairgrease....HAHAHAH!!!! JS: Leon Jackson certainly seems to want to dominate the lightweight division, however, I think he's going to have some trouble with one "Superstar" Simon Brock!
CK: I think Leon Jackson and Kyle Manson need to hook up, share women tips with each other. Hell, i'd give them some advice, but they clearly don't need it!
JS: Especially from you, Chad. Our second pre-show match featured Red Raven taking on Masoa Tsirato....
CK: Tsirato's lucky, I think the Amazing Bob and Evil Pete were looking for trouble tonight!
JS: Fans, for those of you who don't know, during Masoa Tsirato's debut interview, he had some harsh comments for the RMWA "Talently Challenged" wrestlers, and two of those "talently challenged" men came out looking for trouble tonight!
CK: Hopefully he won't have too much trouble this Thursday on Latenight when he faces both men in a handicap match!
JS: Somehow, I doubt that Tsirato will be in much danger....Anyway, back to his match with Red Raven. This one seemed to be fairly equal as it went on, with both men dominating the match at one point or another. Howvever, the end came fairly sudden, when Tsirato dropped Red Raven square on his head with a brain buster.
CK: When a man as tall as Red Raven gets dropped on his head like that, it's not something you can just rebound up from, it's no surprise to me that he got beat that way.
JS: OK folks, we've got to take a commericial break, we'll be right back! CK: WAIT A SECOND!! JS: What? CK: THERE! LOOK! LOOK! THERE! THERE! LOOK! JS: Wha........?? OMIGOD!!! CK: KENDEL JONES SUPERSTAR IS IN THE FRONT ROW!!! JS: What?!? CK: Right there, in the front row-- JS: <interrupting> OMIGOD!! IT'S KENDEL JONES, SUPERSTAR!!! HOLY SMOKES! CK: GOODNESS GRACIOUS! JS: .....We'll be right back. ++++++++++++++++++++ [The camera shows a scene of a four-lane divided highway, lined with trees, on a somewhat steep incline. The camera pans over to a man pushing a boulder almost as large as he is up the hill in the breakdown lane. Cars whiz by him like he doesn't exist. The first feature that strikes you are his eyes: pure white. His long black hair and scruffy black beard have obviously not been washed or combed in far too long. He wears a rather ordinary pair of black pants underneath a burlap shepherd's robe. He wears nothing on his very dirty and calloused feet. He is tall, about 6 1/2 feet, and muscular, but not ripped. You notice he is missing the pinky finger of his right hand as he pushes the boulder further up the hill. He is quite obviously exerting himself, breathing heavily and sweating.]
[He takes notice of the camera and turns his head towards us while continuing to push his stone]
[Shivers run down your spine when his blank "eyes" look at you. You feel like they are boring into your soul]
*HUFF* I am Sisyphus. This stone is the cross I bear for my sins. I have committed many, and I must become the greatest *PUFF* wrestler in
history before I am purged of them. I am coming to the RMWA, and I will defeat you all. Scott Winters, John Proudstar, Chris Michaels, Leon
Jackson, you will all fall at my feet. My stone has no mercy. My stone does not choose who it *GASP* destroys. My stone is a floor wax AND a dessert topping!
[On one particularly large push, the stone rolls off to the side and starts going back down the hill]
DAMN! That ALWAYS happens! [Sisyphus chases it down, barely avoiding the passing cars, stops the rock and starts pushing it up again]
Where was I? Oh yes, destroying you all. You will all fall victim to my Sisyphus sleeper, and when it's over, and you're lying helpless on the mat, begging for *PUFF* mercy, my stone will show you NONE! Hades has given me a task to complete, and complete it I must before I am granted peace. I will roll this rock to the top of the wrestling world, and when I am there, I will be reunited with my family and this will be over. Now leave me! I have pushing to do.
<Camera fades out>
