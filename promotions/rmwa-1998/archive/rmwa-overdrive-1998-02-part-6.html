RMWA Overdrive (Part 6 of 6)

<Camera fades in> Vic Rizzo: It's great to be back in the brodcast booth! Vanessa D'Angelo: Shove it Rizzo! VR: Glad to see you too again babe! What are your observations on the next match? VD: Kyle Manson is gonna kill tonto! He's a damn stud! VR: Ok then, lets get to Dave Ritter and the ring annoucements! Dave Ritter: Comming to the ring first, hailing from, uh, Damn RMWA research staff couldn't even look up where this guy lives! But he stands six feet three inches and weighes in at 245 pounds. "THE AMERICAN DREAM" KYLE MANSON! [The lights start flashing, and "Born in the USA", the revised version sung by White Zombie, blares across the loudspeakers. "The American Dream" Kyle Manson walks towards the ring, hugging and kissing all of the female fans that are clamouring to touch his perfect body. A lot of the men look a little upset, but Manson doesn't seem to give a rats ass...] Dave Ritter: The following contest is scheduled for ONE FALL...Introducing first, he hails from Phoenix, Arizona, and weighs in at 295 pounds...he's accompanied by his manager, The Shaman, here is... "THE APACHE" JOHN PROUDSTAR! <Native American Chanting begins playing throughout the arena as the
Shaman leads the 6'4", 295 pound John Proudstar to the ring. As the two come to the ring, the Denver fans roar their approval of John Proudstar>
VR: We start off here with John "The Apache" Proudstar in the ring and
"The American Dream" Kyler Manson backing off in a mockery of the fear his big opponent supposedly inspires in the hearts and minds of fans. Well, it does look like what few kids were able to sneak into the arena are now mocking John Proudstars
Native American heritage by doing little war dances and prancing around like little indians on the war path. The
Shaman signals for RMWA head of security Shawn Friday to escort the kids from the arena. VD: What kind of wrestling promotion do you run here Rizzo! I mean, they're not even supposed to be here! This RMWA security really sucks if they can't spot three snot nosed brats that snuck into the arena. Proudstar isnt to happy about that war dance that those kids put on,getting on TV for 50 minutes probably wasn't a good idea for those kids since Proudstar will know their faces if he ever see's them again. Buton the bright side, Sweet
Cheeks Kyle Manson
is getting a good laugh out of this. VR: I'm going to have to have a long talk with RMWA head of security Shawn
Friday and his staff about this embarassment. Anyways, Kyle Manson is still looking and laughing at the kids that are getting taken away from the arena and Proudstar is slowly creeping up behind Manson .
. . AND MANSON QUICKLY TURNS AROUND AND NAILS HIM WITH A THUMB TO THE
EYE!!! That really stunned the big native american, Kyle follows up with a SUPERKICK that sends John Proudstar to the outside of the ring . Manson waits for Proudstar to crawl back into the ring, but to pass time until the Apache gets into the ring. Kyle
Manson is having a good old
time flipping off the carpet sales man convention from Utah who are here tonight. What's that he called them? Inbred pillow-biters? VD: Well, have you ever been to Utah? That would probably be your assessment of their population, too. It would be a really pretty place, but it was easier to raise cattle and hordes of babies there than it was to make the place nice.
VR.: Well, I personally prefer beef to scenery. I would prefer babies to scenery, too, but the last baby I was associated with was just too annoying. Manson primps his hair and John Proudstar catches him in a belly-to-back suplex!!! Proudstar covers!!! One . . . Two . . . Kickout by Manson, who is now shaking and begging John Proudstar to stop!!! John Proudstar is starting to feed off of the crowds posistive vibes towardards him and is starting a war dance! Manson gets up and charges Proudstar, but The
Apache catches him with a double knife edged chop and looks like he's going to set up for a
DDT!, But Manson puts
an elbow where the sun don't shine!!! Kyle Manson hops up on the top rope and gets ready to give John Proudstar the same elbow a little higher. He hits a perfect elbow-drop to the throat. Delaney Henderson: That idiot Proudstar won't stay down, even though tonto is getting his hide handed to him by Kyle Manson he'll just pop up and continue to take a beating like a fool. Cedric Bichon Sr.: I don't know about that, Vanessa. I don't think anybody with the physical capacities to wrestle would get up after that one.
One . . . Two . . . Three . . . And John "The Apache" Proudstar pounces up!!! Well, you finally called something right, Vanessa, and now Kyle Manson taps Proudstar on the shoulder from behind. John Proudstar turns around, and gets introduced to Manson's thumb again!!! Proudstar is becoming good friends with that thumb it seems. VD: He seems to go a lot for the old thumb to the eye. You used to do that a lot too, Vic, but you just didn't know that many other moves. VR: I did too, in fact, I knew the move Kyle Manson's doing now, the hurri-whatever style victory roll, but I was always a bit more careful to guard for the . . . REVERSAL INTO A POWERBOMB BY JOHN
PROUDSTAR!!!! He slams Manson to the mat and covers. One . . . Two . . . Manson kicks out, quite literally, he planted a boot in the groin of John Proudstar!!! Now he nails the The Apache with a baseball slide!!! Now that Kyle Manson's got Proudstar down, he goes back up to the top rope for a fistdrop!!! Again, he catches the throat perfectly and covers. One . . . Two . . . Thr-- Another shoulder up by Proudstar, he just wont stay down! But
Legend grabs a handful of
hair and gets ready to make him take a nap. Kyle Manson locks the sleeper on with a judo scissor leg lock as well and waits. To avoid the indignity of actually having to pass out, John
Proudstar submits!!! ( Ding - dong - ding - dong ) Dave Ritter: The winner of this match in 14:43. "THE AMERICAN DREAM" KYLE MANSON! VR: Impressive win by Kyle Manson, just one of the up and comming stars of the RMWA! VD: I love that bell. VR: We'll be back after we pay some bills!
