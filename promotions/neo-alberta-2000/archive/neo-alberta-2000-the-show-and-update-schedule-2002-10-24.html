I don't normally do this... but what the hell. And the link... http://neo-wrestling.com/showevent.cfm?id=46 Thoughts and stuff can be sent to [detail withheld] Of course, there are some open spots so feel free to send me apps and tell your friends about us! ================== NEO WRESTLING INCORPORATED www.neo-wrestling.com NEWSLETTER ISSUE #5 October 23rd, 2002 CONTENTS -Notes from the desk of Wallace Storm -News and Rumors -Results from the last episode of "The Show" -Waiver Wire -Uncivil War -NEO "The Show" Upcoming Lineup (with deadlines) -"Uncivl War" Lineup ================== FROM THE DESK.... ================== Man, I am disappointed in the show. The matches and oncards were fine but it seemed so thrown together. That won't happen again. I had major computer troubles and such but they look fixed and we can get a movin' and get NEO back on top. Lots happening over the next few shows, lots of changes... it's going to be a ride kids. Out of character for a second, some VERY important notes. 1) There are no goddamn pyros or no titan tron... so backstage vignettes will NOT get pops. 2) ALL FRICKING multi part oncards must be passed by me FIRST! I will not use any that are not. Period Of course I can use writers and all that shit... the usual deal. ================== NEWS AND RUMORS ================== -Matt Saunders suffered a major concussion and neck injury. Yes, he is out indefinite... possibly forever. -Hannibal Carver has been ordered to be at the next show? Isn't he... banned? Could it get worse? -Kristoff St. Livingstone won the TV title in an impromptu appearance. Word is George Lovecraft and Vic Valiant have both hit the bricks and been given their walking papers -Also gone is Vandal Gomez. Why, no one knows. He just up and vanished. -The tag team situation is heating up! Two new teams signed this week plus increasing heat within the ranks should make for interesting weeks to come. This may be the strongest NEO's division has ever been. -On the rumor mill: Despite rumors, neither former tag champs Lights, Camera, Action nor former Skyweight Champion Ketiak are going to return -Also impressing... Cole Druggan. The former RCW standout is certainly making a name for himself in the NEO ranks. He along with Nightlife and Debonair had some eye popping performances in last show's main event. -Russ Brady and Steve Dumars may have left NEO early... more on this as it develops. ======================= "THE SHOW" RESULTS ======================= -Jayson Graham d. Sean Watts in a TSTL matchup -Dreamboys and Cow & Chicken d. Pop Stars and World O' Hurt -Vic Morrison won a Skyweight Gauntlet Match -Douglas McCray d. DDI in the first of a Best of 7 -Kristoff St. Livingstone d. Vic Valiant to win the Alberta TV Title. -Mike Waldrop was the sole survivor in a ten man elimination tag match =============== WAIVER WIRE =============== CHAMPIONS NEO Heavyweight Champion: Mike Waldrop Pacific Champion: Derek Irvin Skyweight Champion: AsH Alberta TV Champion: Kristoff St. Livingstone Tag Champs: Nightlife RANKINGS AVAILABLE ON THE NEO WEBSITE. GOING- Vandal Gomez, Vic Valiant, George Lovecraft ============== UNCIVIL WAR ============== NIGHT ONE From the Hammerstein Ballroom in New York City -The final 8 in the Sky's the Limit Tournament will come to a head in a one night tournament -See Oita Pro Wrestling take on P*WIN Wrestling in a special challenge match! NIGHT TWO From the Axis Ballroom in Boston NIGHT THREE From the NEO Arena in Edmonton, Alberta Matches know to be happening... -If it gets that far... a best 2/3 falls for the NEO Pacific Championship between Derek Irvin and Douglas McCray -plus _all_ NEO titles shall be on the line! ================================ UPCOMING "THE SHOW" SCHEDULE ================================ The Trek to Uncivil War continues and bodies are being left in it's wake. Matt Saunders is effectively done with wrestling because of his injuries at the hands of Hannibal Carver. Vandal Gomez is nowhere to be found. The hatred between Mike Waldrop and Hannibal Carver is growing... and about to reach critical. New faces... new stars... changes are on the horizon! This edition of "The Show" comes to you from the Thunder Bay Civic Auditorium in, you guessed it, Thunder Bay, Ontario! The NEO crew is on the road... and it brought some guests! It's a BEST OF SEVEN series and it's up one to nothing with "DEADLY" DEREK IRVIN falling behind. Match two continues... can "THE CORRUPTOR" DOUGLAS MCCRAY keep his lead? Talk about double duty! Not only is "DEADLY" DEREK IRVIN facing Douglas McCray but he competes in his first round match in THE SKY'S THE LIMIT taking on unknown "APOCALYPSE" ADAM WHITAKER! Will DDI survive the night? In a FIVE WAY CHALLENGE the first man to challenge newly crowned Alberta Television Champion Kristoff St. Livingstone shall be decided as ERIC STORM, "MR. EXTREME" ANDY SLAUENWHITE, the odd "CYBER PUNK" BILLY BROAD, "NO MERCY JEFF FLOYD" and the debuting SENOR CLOAK! In the second first round THE SKY'S THE LIMIT match the returning RED DRAGON will take on the well known and well travelled VD VITRONE! VIC MORRISON won the gauntlet and now he get's his prize taking on NEO SKYWEIGHT CHAMPION... ASH! Will Spikyjim... err... Kurio Tenshi make his appearance again? Word is something is big is coming... something you won't believe until you see it! The KINGS OF HARDCORE haven't competed for a while but now they do against THE EWIAK BROTHERS in an ANYTHING GOES MATCH! Plus... in a battle worthy of the Main Event _anywhere_, the Demon Lord... the Recividist... LORD ARCHIBALD DUNSCAITH will be forced to live up to his words from last show as he takes on BSCW interloper and superstar... THE SCRAYPER!! A match for the ages folks!! Plus Wallace Storm has demanded Hannibal Carver show up... in the ring!! A face to face meeting with the South Boston Strangler can't be good... and the NEO Champion can't be far behind! Mailing list to come soon. NEO Wrestling 2002
