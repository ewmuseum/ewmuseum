<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> NEO WRESTLING INCORPORATED New Era Old school wrestling. <><><><><><><><><><><><>><><><><><> http://neo.sites.cc [detail withheld] Post Show Update Dec 3, 2000 WEBSITE NEWS ************** +Obviously the big news this week is the BRAND SPANKING NEW website. If you haven't seen it yet, what the hell is wrong with you people? We have had nothing but compliments and I expect this to be a major boon in the success of NEO as a whole. With all kinds of features it will keep you usy for some time. +Also updates are going to be done a little bit different. Keep your eyes open and you will see what I mean. WAIVER WIRE ************* HIRED-Sushi Tsunami, Iceman, 'Silent Storm' Cody Townsend GONE-Bulldozer, Louie 'The Italian' Decarlo, INJURED/HIATUS-Dr. Hate, Jesse Babylon Rumors are still rampant about more talent coming in. It should be interesting. NEWS AND RUMORS ******************* +The rumors of a Japanese our are still going strong. Not only has the FEW expressed interest but rumors are about that the JPWF may also have a part in it. Only time will tell but either way it will be great for NEO. Not too many regional federations get to branch out and travel, then again most regional feds are not NEO. +A second belt on the way? Whispered hushes say so. This is a development sure to impact everyone on the roster. +The Febuary Supercard is looking bigger and bigger all the time. It is said to be a full weekend event right from the home of grunge, Seattle, Washington! +The monthly awards will start as soon as our first champion is crowned so get cracking if you want to make an impact. +On the subject of impact, NEO has the chance to send two of it's wrestlers to the RWA montly event where you could have the chance to become the RWA interfed champion. Email us for more info. +Talking about the RWA. NEO won two awards from them. We won the best website and best updates in thier monthly awards. Congratulations to [name withheld] once again for a job well done +And of course, we are always looking for writers. You know where to get ahold of us if you are interested. +If anyone is interested in doing a column or something or the sort on the website please let us know MY COMMENTS *************** +This card was simply spectacular. From top to bottom it went flawless with great participation by a large number of people. The oncards really helped out and hopefully these keep coming. +I am jakked about the website. I love the design and layout. He we won awards, how bad can it be? Be sure to let [name withheld] know how much you appreciate it.
