Update #1 !!

27 December 2001

Hola, Well, here we are with the first update of the _new_ NEO. Yes, we are new. Some have said I am reopening, I say I am restarting. A different attitude, a different feel, new faces...I think this will be one hell of a ride. I hope everyone had a wonderful Christmas and Santa treated you well. I know mine was fabulous. I spent plenty of time with my brand new nephew, saw friends I haven't seen for a year...was more then excellent. Hell, I wish I didn't have to come home so early.
Anyhow, I am ready to kick things into gear. The roster thus far is kick ass and will work out most excellent. Just one look at it shows that this is a totally new NEO. We have some returning faces, we have some WWA faces and some totally new ones. This is gonna so rule. So enough blabbing. I want to get back to playing with my Christmas presents so here's the update.
Oh and one more thing. Some of you getting this update applied, but I haven't replied back. If you're on the show or I have contacted you, obviously you are in. If I am missing anyone, let me know.
NEO WRESTLING UPDATE #1 Dec 26th, 2001 CONTENTS 1. Message from the boss 2. Notes about strats and general rules. 3. Roster News 4. Schedule 5. Roster/Contact info. 1. MESSAGE FROM "DA BOSS" -Here we are folks. Things are rolling now. This update will be the first in what is hopefully many so pay attention to it. This is some reiteration of the general way we do things here, info about the first show and the matches we're having. So pay attention, there is gonna be a test after!
2. GENERAL NOTES, RULES AND SUCH -The first rule in Fight Club is to....oh, wait, wrong show. Remember kids, no flash packets this time around. Instead everything is on the show. You still do flashes, they just get put as vignettes, backstage oncards, etc. Generally I prefer the flashes to be present time backstage oncards,
in rings, joint angle development oncards, etc, etc. You can do pre-taped stuff, let's not go nuts with it though, okay? If you have any questions, simply ask. As well, strats are very important. They convey how your character is to be portrayed in the ring, a big factor in my NEO. Much of
NEO is about the actual wrestling aspect. For those of you who have been here before, you know what I am talking about.
So... Match Outcome Determination= Flashes/oncards + Strats + Angle +
Intangibles. Intangibles...sometimes I may pick a person to win simply cause of gut feeling. Remember, this is all about having fun, this isn't some competition you are making money on. So have fun dammit...and that's an order!
3. ROSTER NEWS Coming- Everyone Going- No one Returning- Some one(s) Lame eh? We are still taking applications and YES, we are taking women. Skyweights and a few women would be appreciated as a matter of fact. Oh and sorry, at this time...not tag teams.
4. SCHEDULE NEO Wrestling is _BACK_ and back with a bang! From the brand spanking new
NEO arena, Wallace Storm brings his critically acclaimed company back to the chaotic world of professional wrestling. There is a little bit locked down for the first show and expect some surprises!
In the opener NEO will bring to the table what made it famous in the first place...skyweights! The returning COLT MONTANA will team up with Japanese newcomer JINMENJUUSHIN and WWA superstar "UP ALL NIGHT PABLO O'CONNOR to take on the team of rookie TOMMY BEDLAM, hard hitting daredevil CESAR RISLEY
and "ITALY'S SON" GORDON VENTA. But really, it isn't just a match, this one is a special one...elimination! The two teams will start off elimination style, a pinfall, submission, DQ or Countout eliminating a wrestler. The last one standing....is the new NEO SKYWEIGHT CHAMPION~!
NEO gets extreme as ACW's fan favorite, "THE WEST COAST THRILLER" WILL BRODY takes on the dangerous HANNIBAL CARVER in a hardcore match! Anything goes in this one and you better believe anything _will_ happen!
For the first time EVER in NEO there will be a woman's match as the former
WWA and WWO star HEATHER LOWRY takes on the very unpredictable, yet capable
(and voluptuous) HEATHER CAULFIELD...yes, _that_ Heather Caulfield, the manager of NEO star Ktulu! These ladies will show they can hang with the boys.
Two men, with not a full deck of cards between them meet up in what should be stiff. The vicious psycho "MOURNING STAR" CYRIOUS AERO will take on the strange, yet dangerous (and former NEO TV Champion) KTULU!
You know this isn't it though. Look for more to happen in this show, _plus_ a special announcement to open the show from the owner of this circus...Wallace Storm!

like to see on the show from all ya'll. Send EVERYTHING to [detail withheld] and/or [detail withheld] 5.
