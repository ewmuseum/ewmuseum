The Ewiak Brothers NEXT SHOW AND DEADLINES ================================= This next show will be in London, Ontario from Big Al's , a large Night club. Something different (and yes, the place is fake) :)
In a debut match "BOOM BOOM" BILLY BANE takes on former Gunryo Pro star AKIRA SHINASHI! The NEO tag team division gets hotter by the week with more sure to come. THE EWIAK BROTHERS have been trying to make an impact and they will try one more time against WORLD O'HURT!
The second round of THE SKY'S THE LIMIT TOURNAMENT continues with a pair of matches sure to wow. The first will see former EMWC Junior heavyweight
Champion JUVENIL INFIERNO take on a man who gets another chance in BSCW's SETH HARKER! The second match will see BSCW replacement BLADE JIN take on Japanese sensation "BAD GENOME" KYOHEI YAMADA! With the series at 2-1, the BEST OF SEVEN continues with "DEADLY" DEREK IRVIN in the lead! Another show and another chance for "THE CORRUPTOR" DOUGLAS MCCRAY to get back into things. Only this time things are going to be a little more different. The series has been nothing short of technical.
Now things get taken to another level... IN A STREET FIGHT! The ALBERTA TELEVISION gets another defense. After the incredible match last show, champion KRISTOFF ST. LIVINGSTONE returns to take on... A MYSTERY
OPPONENT!! Who can get enough of the skyweights? Obviously not the NEO fans as they will see a six man match that is sure to shake the foundations as
"KICHIGAIJIMITA KURIO TENSHI will team up with one of his opponents at UNCIVIL WAR in the NEO Skyweight Champion VIC MORRISON! On their side will be the masked odd man SENOR CLOAK! Together they will face the dream team of SUSHI TSUNAMI, PSICOPA and luchadore EL MARIACHI!
The MAIN EVENT should be one hell of a doozy as newcomer and member of 3sb~! REMBRANDT teams up with the man who could be the next Heavyweight Champion in HANNIBAL CARVER! Together, the pair shall take on an odd and unexpected pairing in "CREAM OF THE CROP" MIKE WALDROP and the returning yet still injured MATT SAUNDERS! Some say blood runs cold... a lot will run in this match, dress warm.
Plus you will hear from everyone involved in Uncivil War and _so_ much more!! **DEADLINES** Strats, flashes, etc are all due by Monday Dec. 2nd at Midnight.. repeat MONDAY DEC. 2ND AT MIDNIGHT. Send everything to [detail withheld] suckas. If you have any questions or concerns email me. Just get your stuff in... and help me write.
**DEADLINES** UNCIVIL WAR LINEUP! ====================== The Sky's the Limit Final 8! Already in... Tripp Shade and Spikyjim! As well Oita Pro superstars shall take on P*WIN stars in a special challenge match!
The NEO HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP is on the line as the current and long time champion "CREAM OF THE CROP" MIKE WALDROP takes on arch nemesis HANNIBAL
CARVER! This is going to be one for the ages! What a series it's been and at UNCIVIL WAR it all comes to a stop as the PACIFIC CHAMPIONSHIP is defended in either a SINGLE FALL MATCH or a 2 OUT OF 3 FALLS MATCH depending on the score come the Supercard! Currently tied at 2-1 with one match to go, "DEADLY" DEREK IRVIN will take on "THE
CORRUPTOR" DOUGLAS MCCRAY! NEO will see it's first ever TABLES, LADDERS AND CHAIRS MATCH and it will be for the NEO TAG TEAM TITLES as current champions NIGHTLIFE defend against
THE KINGS OF HARDCORE and DEBONAIR! The ALBERTA TELEVISION TITLE will be defended no matter who carries it. Involved in the match are four men with unlimited potential, all looking for their spot. Current champion KRISTOFF ST. LIVINGSTONE, knock out artist COLE
DRUGGAN, the spot machine JEFF FLOYD and next show's MYSTERY OPPONENT! It was announced and it will happen in a 4 CORNERS MATCH! VIC MORRISON will take on "KICHIGAIJIMITA" KURIO TENSHI, former champion PSICOPA and the NEO legend SUSHI TSUNAMI!
