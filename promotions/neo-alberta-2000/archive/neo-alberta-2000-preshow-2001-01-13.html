NEO (Alberta, 2000–2001) — Card Archive

Preshow 1.13.01

13 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NEO WRESTLING PRESENTS THE SATURDAY SPECIAL PRESHOW! [The scene opens up in the backstage area of NEO Wrestling from Moose Jaw,
Saskatchewan. A young man, dressed in a pair of jeans and NEO t-shirt stands beside some technical equipment. He has very short cropped blonde hair and has a youthful exuberance you can just see in his smile.]
MAN: "Hey there folks I am Jack Black and this is the Saturday Special preshow. We got some great stuff ahead for you including three matches so let's not waste anytime."
[A graphic of Mike Flame comes onto the screen, fading out to a small square in the corner of the screen, much like you see on the news.]
JB: "Word is coming into NEO that Mike Flame, a very popular wrestler here in NEO, will not be able to make it to Moose Jaw. Apparently he has come
down with quite a flu and was not able to make the flight over. This really impacts the show tonight as he was to face Wraith as part of the
Pacific title tournament. This is a big potential three points he gives up.
That will hurt him in the standings for sure." [The graphic disappears and goes to the ring where a half full arena awaits the show.] JB: "Let's go too the ring for some action. We will get to see the debut of a newcomer, and an odd one at that, Red Hot."
RED HOT vs TRACTOR TRAYLER [The two men came down to the ring, Trayler, the local product, not knowing quite what to think of the eccentric Red Hot. The match got underway with
Trayler using his brute power to control early on. A body slam, leg drop and
another bodyslam resulted in a two count. Trayler would not be daunted though, whipping Red Hot into the corner and going to work with heavy punches and kicks. A piledriver was reversed into a back body drop and Red Hot went to work hitting a pair of DDT's,. some punches, a trio of lariats and finally belly to back suplex for a two count. An elbow drop got another
two and soon the two were back to brawling back and forth. Trayler attempted to end the match with his Hail Bail Toss but Red Hot was able to reverse and his version of the Stun Gun...the Love Gun, for the three!
JD: "YOUR WINNER....REEEED HOOOOOOOT!!!!" [The camera returns back to Jack Black at Saturday Special.] JB: "Well that certainly made for quite the debut. I am sure he will have some more interesting words for us on Wednesday. Talking about Wednesday it should make for an interesting show after this Saturday Special. With four tournament match, not to mention a great light heavyweight match pitting
Dean Williams against John Adams, people are going to have a lot to say.
talking about light heavyweights let's get right to our next match and another debut, Kid Dixie against another local product, the Estevan Kid."
KID DIXIE vs THE ESTEVAN KID [Right off the bat the action started with Kid Estevan getting a drop toe hold, a pair of snap suplexes and leg drop from the top rope. With Dixie stunned he went to the top once again this time missing a senton splash. After that things were nothing but downhill from there. Dixie showed his methodical approach hitting a brainbuster, russian leg sweep, a lariat and a big splash to get a two. A top rope leg drop got two and having had enough
Kid Dixie hit it into high gear. A great snap suplex got a two and he followed up with another brainbuster, a DDT, a flapjack and several elbows to the neck area. He finally finished it up with his modified Russian leg sweep known as 'Shotgun Wedding'.
JD: "YOUR WINNER.......KIIIIIIIID DIIIIXXXXIIEEEE!!!" [Fade back to Jack Black.] JB: "With Storm really wanting o push the Skyweight division Kid Dixie certainly has a home, especially after that dominating performance. I am sure he is going to make some waves. Before we go to our final match let's go check out how the Pacific Title tournament is going so far."
[A graphic showing the Pool of Blood shows up on the screen.] POOL OF BLOOD Kenta Ogawa -3 pts Wraith-0 pts Black Knight- -- Mike Flame- -- JB V/O: "Obviously these standings will be quite different. Wraith picks up an automatic three points due to Flame not being here. Flame will have zero and Black Knight has the HUGE and hotly anticipated match against NEO champ Steve Dumars. I feel things will be very different and hotly challenged as it comes down the wire."
[Another graphic comes up, this time for the Pool of Sweat.] POOL OF SWEAT Alexander Strakoi-3pts Scorpion-0pts Matthew Reason- -- Thanos- -- JB V/O: "This is another hotly contested division but only between three people it seems. Thanos has apparently left NEO so it will be interesting to see how things play out here. It makes me very curious to see what Storm may have in mind."
[The screen changes this time to the Pool of Dreams.] POOL OF DREAMS Amuro Balsa- 3pts The Messiah- 1pt Pegasus- 1pt Eblis- 0pts JB V/O: "This is easily the closest division. Any of these four men could easily come out a winner, all being pegged as potential finalists.
Obviously the rivalry shall be more and more heated as the weeks go by. I for one will be watching!"
[The camera switches back to the arena and Jack Black. He is looking up at the camera as he sits on an empty box, papers in hand.]
JB: "Wondering where the fourth pool, the Pool of Tears is I bet. Simply put they have not had any matches of yet. However this week we will get to see Flynt and Rizer in action, a match I am sure will be very exciting.
Both are big brawlers known for their brute forth rather than their technical mastery. Throw Ray Owens and the release of Xavier and you got one heck of a mix up there. Anyone and I mean ANYONE can come out of this
one. I am sure all the experts will be keeping an close eye on it." [Jack Black gathers up his papers and starts to head off.] JB: "OH, I almost forgot, before we go we have one more debut, the big man,
Fallen Scion."
FALLEN SCION VS ROCKY MOUNTAIN JIM Although it was a bit obvious Jim was a stranger to the gym *g*, this match was a bit closer than anyone thought it could be, at first anyhow. The
Rocky Mountain man tried his best to take Scion down, getting him in the corner and hitting several splashes. Scion came back with a twisting bulldog and an Irish whip into a big boot for two. A powerbomb got another two and this is where things got messy. A couple of Jim's friends, mainly
Tractor Trayler and The Hoodoo Hillbilly hit the ring to maker the save.
Scion was all for it though, taking out Trayler with a chokeslam and Hillbilly with a massive powerbomb. RM Jim made his attack here hitting a German suplex on Scion for a two. He went up to the top rope but was crotched there by the big man(and when I mean big we are talking 6'11" and 356lbs). Scion, in an impressive show of power, scooped up RM Jim over his shoulder planting him with a Kryptonite Krunch like inverted piledriver. This spelled the end as Scion picked him up, hitting the 7th Circle Inverted
Crucifix Powerbomb picking up the win.
JD: "YOUR WINNER.....FALLLLLLEN SCIIIIIIIONNNN!!!!" [The NEO logo and credits flash onto the screen, rolling by as the show fades out.] Copyright NEO 2001
