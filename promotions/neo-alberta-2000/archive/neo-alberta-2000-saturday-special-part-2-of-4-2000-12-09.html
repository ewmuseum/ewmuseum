Saturday Special (Part 2 of 4)

9 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We cut from the standard ring-shot to a split-screen; on the right side, we see our traditional commentators, and on the right side, we see the "talking head" camera shot of Steve Dumars. He is in wrestling gear--he is in sunglasses--and he is in, surprisingly, a good mood.]
TS: "Wipe the smile of your face, Dumars." JJ: [sigh] "My apologies, Steve, for my partner." SD: "Hey, hey, that's OK. I survived ten minutes sitting next to him last week, I can survive this short little thing... plus, I don't have to deal with the stench."
[Dumars grins--Smith tries, and barely succeeds, in holding his words back.] JJ: "So Steve, would you say that you are ready for your match against
Rizer, to take place shortly?"
SD: "Ready? I happen to think that I'm always ready for a match, but in this case, I'd like to emphasize the fact that I'm ready. Underline it, italicize it, put a whole bunch of those pretty neon lights by it, do whatever you will--just know that Steve Dumars is ready for Rizer, and he's ready _now_."
TS: "So Dumars, tell me--what will it feel like to suffer your second-straight pinfall on Saturday Special?"
SD: "Teddy baby, why don't you tell me--how does it feel to know that you weigh about as much, plus or minus, as a family of four?"
[Ominous silence from Smith, with a distasteful scowl.] SD: [with a grin] "That's what I thought. Back to the matter at hand--Rizer. I'll put it simply--I'm not coming into this match with the assumption that I'm going to roll over this guy... because I'm sure that's not going to happen. Contrary to what some others think, I do believe that a lot--if not all--of the wrestlers here in the NEO are immensely talented. If they weren't, they simply wouldn't be here.
"I'm ready for this match, like I said, but I realize that this is as big of a match for me as it is for Rizer... this is basically make-or-break. I came off that pinfall against Owens a few weeks ago, and Rizer has a stake, a wonderful opportunity to catapult himself to the top of the roster...
"So we break even. It all comes down to talent, it all comes down to luck... and maybe, maybe it just comes down to fate. [shrug] Who knows?
"But speaking of Ray Owens--and trust me, I won't do a lot of it right now, that's later--I just wanted to let him know that his 'competition' he's desiring is standing before him right now and speaking. So you're '100% man'? So you plan to take all the punishment I can deliver, then throw it back in my face and laugh about it?
"Okay. Then do it. I'll be here--I'll be waiting--and I'll be ready to actually _do_ something about it. We can all talk up a storm in these interviews, but when it comes down to the in-ring stuff, let me ask you--do you have what it takes? Don't fool yourself, Owens, that was anything but decisive two weeks ago--we both know it, you just won't admit it--so the big conflict is yet to come.
"When it does? Then be prepared to eat your words. Rizer, see you in the ring." [The split-screen disappears as Dumars walks off the camera shot.] JJ: "Wow, he is fired up and those strong words will go a long way!" TS: "Strong words? Strong stench more like." JJ: "Oh shut up Teddy, let's just go to our next match." ["One step Closer" blasts over the pa system as Trinity walks out from behind the curtain and Townsend follows wearing a black robe and has a towel around his neck. The crowd boos as Townsend throws the sweaty towel at a fan. Townsend climbs into the ring and then helps Trinity into the ring.
Townsend circles the ring and then stands in the middle with his arms spread as Trinity stands on the second turn buckle flirting with the crowd.] JJ: "Cough*ego*cough." TS: "Hehehe" [Lightly the beginning of "Its On" by KoRn begins to play and as the full blast of the music hits IceMan then walks out with a medium speed bop to the ring wearing along with his wrestling attire a pair of Oakley's and walks out with a bottle of Aquafina water. He gives his shades to a lucky fan as he gets to the ring and then climbs in over the ropes and climbs the turnbuckle amping the crowd up. ]
TS: "Awww, what a nice guy....lame-o." [Ice is an African American male with the build of Ken Shamrock. Three tattoo's, IceMan in script on left bicep , on his right arm a Lion jumping at you with Fire around it, and a Firey Sun on his back, which is small just under his right shoulder blade.. Five ear rings, two in his left ear lobe, one in the left cartilage and two in his right, one in his tongue. He has a goatee and his hair is low with the goatee and sideburns connected from the jawbones. ]
JJ: "Well it is widely known that he will be G-Digga's partner against
Hell's Alliance." [He has RVD type boots with the shin protectors that are black but the color is blue in the back. Black pants with blue and white on them, zigzaging pointy shards of ice outlined in white and looking like they are cracked.
He has Nike batting gloves all black.and an elbow pad on his left arm.] JD: "Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall and is a double debut! [Big POP] JD: "First to my left, he is from Brooklyn, New York and stands 6'3". Weighing in tonight at 248lbs, here is IIICCCCEEEEMANNNNNN!!!!!" [The crowd has no idea who this guy is and just kinda clap.] JD: "And his opponent, from Atlanta, Georgia, stands 6'2" and weighs in at
256lbs...he is THE SILENT STORM....COOODYYYY TOOOWWWNNNNSSSENNNND!!!"
JJ: "He is a pretty broad man. I would be very surprised if he can do some of the moves he says he can."
DING DING DING TS: "And they're off!" JJ: "Where the heck are we, the Kentucky Derby?" TS: "Eh, close enough." JJ: "Whoa! Townsend with a sprinting charge at Iceman, but it's sidestepped... and Iceman comes right back with a dropkick to the back!
Townsend sent down to the canvas, and Iceman right on top of him with a series of big right hands."
TS: "This guy might just be smart enough to realize he's gotta make a first impression on all of us here... especially yours truly. We wouldn't want
Iceman to suffer the constant ridicule which people like Steve Dumars, Mike
Flame and the rest of the moron bunch."
JJ: "Er... nonetheless, Iceman on top of Townsend with those right hands, but the ref gets in there and breaks it up. Iceman pushed back, and
Townsend up to one knee. He regains his footing--Iceman charges--but Townsend with a drop toe hold, sending Iceman sprawling face-first to the mat." [Townsend latches on to Iceman's left ankle, and applies a modified ankle lock submission.]
JJ: "Townsend with that submission maneuver... somewhat early in the match, but it could be a useful strategy to wear down Ice. These two men are about the same size, have about the same wrestling style and are both new to the federation... so what we're looking at is a match to gauge who will be seen as the "jumpstart" here in the NEO..."
TS: "Or maybe you're just talking out of your ass again." JJ: "That may be so, indeed. Anyway, Iceman has reached the bottom rope, and the referee forces the break again. Townsend up to his feet--he brings
Iceman up with him--and that left ankle looking to be slightly hurt, as
Iceman's walking with a slight limp."
[Townsend grabs a wrist and Irish whips his opponent into the ropes. Iceman hits the strands, comes back and meets a wild, swinging clothesline attempt--but Iceman has the sense to duck it, and continue his journey to the opposite set of ropes.]
JJ: "Iceman back--Townsend with a backbody drop attempt... Iceman with a big kick to the face! Townsend stunned... Iceman with a knee... then a DDT! DDT brings Townsend to the canvas... a cover!
One! Two! Thr...NO! Big kickout from 'Silent Storm.'" [Townsend brings Iceman to his feet, then shoves him back into the corner turnbuckles. Townsend follows in, then hits a _mean_ backhand chop, and the resulting *smack* echoes throughout the arena... resulting in the standard
"WHOO!" from the fans.]
TS: "Nature Boy he ain't, but this guy's got a hell of a backhand chop." JJ: "That is indeed correct... there's another, and a third... Irish whip--no, Iceman reverses--Townsend into the opposite turnbuckles! Iceman charges... Townsend moves, and Iceman crashes into the 'buckles! He's staggered... and there's a rollup!
One! Two! Thr--NO! Townsend only got two and a half there." [Both men quickly back to their feet, and, subsequently, both men run into opposite ropes. They come back, and Iceman sprawls face-first onto the canvas, allowing Townsend to leap over. Iceman is back up, and back into the ropes... they charge towards each other...]
JJ: "Collision! Both Iceman and Townsend went for a body press... and they hit, stomach-to-stomach! Both men are down! But Iceman... Iceman has some sense, and he drapes an arm over Townsend's chest..."
[One! Two! Kickout!] JJ: "NO! Townsend had the sense to kick out there..." [Iceman slowly, steadily regains his footing... just as Townsend gets to his knees. Iceman plants a big boot to the forehead, sending Townsend back down to the mat... then follows it up with a big elbowdrop to the chest.]
JJ: "*Harsh* elbowdrop to the chest may just have caved that man's ribcage in." TS: [sardonically] "Yeah, and that big boot to the face could've crushed his skull. This guy isn't exactly Mister Universe, if you catch my drift."
JJ: "Nonetheless, outrageous claims aside these guys are pretty well built... both are about six-two, six-three... maybe two fifty, plus or minus a few pounds. What we're looking at is basically a mirror-image here."
TS: "Yeah, these guys are--" [Smith is interrupted by a complete blackout... and the crowd, after a second's worth of confusion, erupts in a _huge_ pop--the only kind of pop, really, a blackout can get from a crowd.]
JJ: [excited] "What the hell is going on here!?! The lights... the lights, our monitors, hell, even the rampway lights on the arena stairs are out!
This... this, well, this is definitely not scheduled!" TS: "Damnit, Johnson, give me back my wallet! And get your damned hand off my thigh!" JJ: "That isn't _me_, Smith... what... what the hell is that!?! Something... something's going on in the ring!" [Crashing sounds, indeed, are taking place in the ring... as Johnson described, the view is pitch-black... all we can see is the NEO logo, superimposed on the lower right hand corner... but nonetheless, the sounds of wrestlers being slammed down _hard_ on the wrestling mat are evident.]
JJ: "What the..." [The lights swiftly and suddenly come back to life--and both Iceman and
Townsend are laid out, sprawling on the mat--both men are face-down... and both men are *out cold*.]
JJ: "OH MY! ICEMAN AND TOWNSEND! LAID OUT!" TS: "YOU'RE SCREAMING IN MY EAR, JOHNSON!" JJ: "Iceman and Townsend out cold in the ring... and what the... what is that in the middle of the ring?"
[The camera zooms in to the two identical silver objects... upon closer inspection, we see that they are...]
JJ: "Gloves...? Yes... yes, they're chain gloves! A pair of gloves made out of chain mail! What... what could that mean?"
TS: "I don't know, but I have a sneaking suspicion that whoever laid these two men out used those very gloves."
JJ: "Yeah, that's obvious--but who was it?" TS: "How the hell should I know? These two were having themselves a nice little match, the lights go out, you steal my wallet, take the picture of my dog, then try to cop a feel while I'm praying to the gods I believe in that
I'll survive to see another day..."
JJ: "Er--okay, Teddy... but who the hell was that? And what does he want with Iceman and Townsend?"
TS: "I'm sure he just wanted to send a message to someone, or something... for some reason... damn, I really don't know." [The referee is in the ring, assessing the carnage... after ten seconds or so, he goes to the ring announcer and has a small chat.]
JD: "Ladies and gentlemen, the referee has deemed this match a NO CONTEST!" [The crowd boos at the result, but seem to still be confused at the whole chain-glove-ordeal.] JJ: "What the heck was that? I am as confused as the crowd." TS: "Let' just get them out of the ring and get onto some real wrestling." JJ: "Somebody needs a lesson in compassion. Let' go see some of our sponsors." ============================= Be sure to stop by the message board daily for news, fun and hilarity, not to mention the odd argument and poll. While there send your greetings to all the new guys. ============================= [Backstage Kris Casey pops open a can of cola. Cheshire breaks the silence with a meow.]
"KRIS CASEY" : You know kid - you shouldn't be hanging around in dark places. 'Cause somewhere, in some dark corridor you're hanging around in - someone's gonna take a shot at ya. And it might be in your best interest if you pray to God it's not me.
[Casey shrugs his way past Cheshire. He only gets a few feet before he stops and turns to add to his case, only to find that Cheshire is no longer around. The camera then switches back to the commentators.]
JJ: "Well that was certainly odd. Brief, but odd." TS: "It was odd. Odd like Dumars thinking he is funny. Odd like Flame thinks he has talent. Odd like.."
JJ: "For God SAKES TEDDY! Will you SHUT UP?" TS: "Sorry...geez, take a pill." (The speakers begin to emit a staticy crackling noise, and deep blue light fills the small arena.)
V/O: Prepare yourself..... ["I Disappear" by Metallica takes the place of the static and filters out through the speakers the volume at max. A small fog rolls before the curtained entryway and the deep blue lighting fades to purple. Rizer steps through out into the arena, and takes a small gaze around before striding to the ring. Occasionally he grabs one of the barred guardrails and shakes it for whatever reason, he climbs into the ring over the top rope and stands as the music quiets, and the normal lighting fades back in.]
[Rizer stands an impressive 6'9", with a massive weight of 324lbs. Although he is not pure muscle he is no where near being fat. His torso is almost a massive block of chiseled concrete, he has a well defined upper section to his body which can be noticed through his wrestling entourage. Rizer's lower torso area is thick in the calves and it is easy to tell somewhere along the line he was a track and field star. His overall effect leaves almost nothing to the imagination as you can tell immediately he would have no trouble lifting something 2 1/2 to 3 times his own weight. Rizer's hair is a deep brown and really thing and stringy, especially when tis wet. He wears a full body suit with two sleeveless arms. The suit itself makes use of the colors black, blue, and purple to great extent with gold trimming around the sleeve area. His suit has a basic set color of black. Then a bird, phoenix to be exact, type symbol is across the chest in a deep purple.
This symbol extends into a pattern of wavy lines that warps around the back of the suit and up to the neck, and down to the ankles. The blue color comes in the form of several star shaped patterns that are very sparingly found throughout the suit, maybe a total of five in all.]
TS: "The music sure is appropriate. From what I heard this guy has been missing interview after interview for Flash Central. Not good."
[As Rizer's theme music dies down, it is suddenly and swiftly replaced by the opening chords of "Thunder Kiss '65", as performed by White
Zombie--signaling the entrance of Rizer's opponent and loveable, huggable fan-favorite... that's right, Steve Dumars.] JJ: "Hey Teddy, it's your best friend." TS: *groan* [Dumars enters the arena, and is greeted by the cheers of his fans. He raises both arms high in the air, returning the admiration, and then proceeds towards the ring. Steve is dressed in his traditional wrestling attire... that being a pair of long, blue trunks with a pair of white vertical racing stripes running down each leg. His upper body is covered by his traditional t-shirt--"Steve Dumars; Who Else Is There?" (available soon in the NEO Store!)--and his right elbow is covered with an elbowpad.
Otherwise, he is strangely without his cap or sunglasses, and his hair is wet down.] [Dumars approaches the ring, but doesn't enter--instead he motions for the microphone. He waits for his music to die down, then he brings the microphone to his lips.]
SD: "Hello, Canmore." [BIG hometown pop.] SD: [motioning towards his opponent] "And also, hello Rizer. I know we may not see eye-to-eye on many things... even on the basic level, seeing as though you're taller than me--but please, please just understand that I'm not here to hurt you, just to rough you up a little bit and maybe leave this building with one more win notched on my belt."
TS(with sarcasm): "Oh, he ....is ...soooooo funny." JJ: "Quit it Teddy, let the man finish." [Dumars grins; crowd pops.] SD: "No, no, seriously, I'm more modest than that. Yeah, I think so anyway. The truth is--I'm gonna give it my all, you're going to do the same--I hope--and one of us is going to leave *this* *here* *very* *ring* [he pats the canvas with each word for emphasis] a victor! I think we're *both* winners here, Rizer... I mean, look at us, we're both in the NEO, one of the greatest and most promising wrestling federations to come around in a long, long time, and we have the chance to do the thing we love--wrestle--in front of all these great fans!"
TS: "Suckup." SD: "So, Rizer, put in that extra ten percent and I think we'll _all_ be winners here." JJ: "Interesting words from Steve. Let's go to Jack Daniels for the ring introductions." TS: "Interesting? He just sucked up." JD: "Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall!. First, to my left, he is reputedly from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Standing 6'9" and weighing in at 324 lbs, here is RIIIIIIZZZZEEEEERRR!!!" [The fans let out a mild, decent, polite pop.] TS: "Not much reaction, but can you blame them?" JD: "And his opponent..." [The fans start cheering VERY loud, interrupting poor Jack. JJ: "Someone is bitter I see." TS: "Damn rights." JJ: "The two circling again and Rizer charges in to grab Dumars. Steve sidesteps and sticks out a foot, Rizer trips but catches his balance and looks EVEN more mad now! Dumars is already getting under his skin and that is not a good thing for Rizer."
TS: "A good thing for him might be to grab Dumars head and rip it off his shoulder. Wait...no...that would be a good thing for everyone!" JJ: "Dumars initiates the lockup this time. Rizer there for it but Dumars is faking! Dumars in behind...BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX!"

JJ: "Rizer does not look pleased with his performance...WHAT THE?" [The crowd starts going nuts as the lights flicker and then suddenly go totally dark.] JJ: "What's going on here?
