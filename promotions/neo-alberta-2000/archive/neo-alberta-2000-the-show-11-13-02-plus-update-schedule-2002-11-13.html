11/13/02 "The Show" plus Update/Schedule

Here it is for your viewing pleasure... http://neo-wrestling.com/showevent.cfm?id=47 ============= NEO WRESTLING INCORPORATED www.neo-wrestling.com NEWSLETTER November 13th, 2002 CONTENTS -Notes from the desk of Wallace Storm -News and Rumors -Results from the last episode of "The Show" -Waiver Wire -Uncivil War -NEO "The Show" Upcoming Lineup (with deadlines) ================== FROM THE DESK.... ================== I think this show came off pretty damn well with some hot angles developing, some really good matches and all kinds of crazy fun. Plus it is fast paced, always important. Out of character for a second, some VERY important notes. 1) There are no goddamn pyros or no titan tron... so backstage vignettes will NOT get pops. 2) ALL FRICKING multi part oncards must be passed by me FIRST! I will not use any that are not. Period Of course I can use writers and all that shit... the usual deal. ================== NEWS AND RUMORS ================== I don't need to put them here anymore because we, ladies and gentlemen, now have a newsgroup to put them on! Simply go to http://groups.yahoo.com/group/neo_newsletter and see for yourself! ======================= "THE SHOW" RESULTS ======================= -"Deadly" Derek Irvin d. Adam Whitaker -"Deadly" Derek Irvin d. "The Corruptor" Douglas McCray -Jeff Floyd won a 5 way TV Title Contenders match -The Ewiak Brothers d. Kings of Hardcore -Vic Morrison d. Ash to become the new NEO Skyweight Champion -Lord Archibald Dunscaith d. The Scrayper -Hannibal Carver d. Mike Waldrop in a non title match =============== WAIVER WIRE =============== CHAMPIONS NEO Heavyweight Champion: Mike Waldrop Pacific Champion: Derek Irvin Skyweight Champion: Vic Morrison Alberta TV Champion: Kristoff St. Livingstone Tag Champs: Nightlife RANKINGS AVAILABLE ON THE NEO WEBSITE. GOING- Billy Broad, Andy Slauenwhite, Eric Storm COMING- Rembrandt, El Mariachi, WINGER Shimizu, Jack Jones, Sushi Tsunami ============== ROSTER ============== (If I missed anyone, please let me know) HEAVYWEIGHTS 1. Hannibal Carver 2. "Cream of the Crop" Mike Waldrop 3. "Deadly" Derek Irvin 4. "Top Gun" Ben Tuckwell 5. Lord Archibald Dunscaith 6. Psicopa 7. Vic Morrison 8. Jayson Graham 9. "The Corruptor" Douglas McCray 10. Cole Druggan 11. Jeff "No Mercy" Floyd 12. "Chaotic" Chris Childress 13. "Kichigaijimita" Kuroi Tenshi 14. El Mariachi (Cesar Cruz) 15. Kristoff St. Livingstone 16. Senor Cloak 17. Jack Jones 18. Sushi Tsunami 19. Rembrandt 20. WINGER Shimizu TAG TEAMS 1. Nightlife 2. Debonair 3. Kings of Hardcore 4. World O' Hurt 5. The Ewiak Brothers ============== UNCIVIL WAR ============== A HUGE joint BSCW/NEO extravanganza taking place in three arena's over three nights. Night one will be "The Sky's the Limit" final 8 with special attractions, night two and three showcasing the best in NEO and BSCW talent! Already signed on the NEO side... -PACIFIC TITLE MATCH "Deadly" Derek Irvin vs "The Corruptor" Douglas McCray: 2/3 falls match! -TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH Nightlife vs Kings of Hardcore vs Debonair: TLC MATCH! -plus all the other titles will be on the line and more ...by the end of next show expect almost _ALL_ NEO matches to be announced And for the TSTL tourney... OITA PRO vs P*WIN CHALLENGE! -Hiroshi Abe/Takayuki Morita/Yutaka Suzume vs "The Sensation" Jason Stevens/Brent Marvel/Marcus Daniels ================================ UPCOMING "THE SHOW" SCHEDULE ================================ It was pure chaos... _pure_ chaos as NEO brought to you another show from Thunder Bay. The return of Sushi Tsunami, the debut of Rembrandt and amazing match after match... what a night! This next edition should be no different as NEOo comes to you from Hamilton, Ontario, live from the Hamilton Legion Hall! Intimate... interactive... and of course... chaotic!! It's debut time in NEO as the wifebeatin' JACK JONES takes on the high flyin', masked luchardore EL MARIACHI and Oita Pro sensation, not to mention new NEO signee WINGER SHIMIZU! After winning last Show's Contender's match "NO MERCY" JEFF FLOYD gets his shot at the ALBERTA TELEVISION CHAMPION and it's holder, KRISTOFF ST. LIVINGSTONE! DEBONAIR shall team up with the newest member in NEO in REMBRANDT to take on the trio of NIGHTLIFE and sometime ally JAYSON GRAHAM! Will The Kings of Hardcore or Debonair be far behind? THE SKY'S THE LIMIT TOURNAMENT continues into the second round! In the first match BSCW's SPIKYJIM will take on replacement and Oita Pro star MASAHARU KAZ! The second match will see GLCW's TRIPP SHADE take on the biggest first round surprise in "FLAMING DRAGON" CHANCE BRANIGAN! LORD ARCHIBALD DUNSCAITH shall team with "CHAOTIC" CHRIS CHILDRESS to take on the duo of COLE DRUGGAN and "THE TOP GUN" BEN TUCKWELL! In the third of a best of seven with the series tied at one a piece, "DEADLY" DEREK IRVIN will once again step up again "THE CORRUPTOR" DOUGLAS MCCRAY... in your Main Event for the evening! Plus... -Hannibal Carver and crew will be in the building! What's Mike Waldrop going to say after his loss and what do they have in store for each other?! -Sushi Tsunami made his return and Wallace Storm says he has something special in store for him! -The New Skyweight Champion, Vic Morrison will be in the house which means Kurio Tenshi won't be far behind -Who was the masked man who attacked the Kings of Hardcore... and will he be back?! Mailing list to come soon. NEO Wrestling 2002
