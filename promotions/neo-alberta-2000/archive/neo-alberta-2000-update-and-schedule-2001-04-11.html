Japanese talent will be favored but anyone is welcome. They always make the show that much more exciting for the viewer. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= UPCOMING SCHEDULE =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= *Please notice all changes and additions **All matches in order on show as shown here ----------------------- APRIL 15th, 2001 LETHBRIDGE, ALBERTA, CANADA UNIVERSITY OF LETHBRIDGE ----------------------- DEBUT MATCH! Marcus "Spyder" Mann vs Psicopa -What would NEO be without a Skyweight match? Both men can take it to the air and take it there in a most amazing fashion. Mix in some great mat work and you have the makings of a classic SKYWEIGHT BATTLE! SHIIRABUKA Hidaki vs 'Roman' Thomas Lawrence -The newest member of the New Revolution takes on one of the top juniors in the world. Lawrence claims to be the best Skyweight in NEO and the world but he needs to prove it against the veteran talent Hidaki. DEBUT TAG MATCH! Monkey You vs The Josh Machines -A pair of guys who dress like monkeys versus the newcomers from India. This match will be yet another showcase of NEO's growing tag division and a build up to an eventual champion. SINGLES BATTLE! 'The Dream' Jerome Davis vs Lincoln Stone -Between the size and stylistic differences and the heat they have with other NEO-ites this could turn out to be quite eventful. Both men are spectacular talents and looking for that one breakout match or fued to propell them to super stardom. Could this be the one? NEWCOMER SIX MAN TAG MATCH! Sean Watts/Trent Black/Randall Crane vs Ben Tuckwell/Ronan Frasier/Mike Waldrop/ -The heat is obvious between Watts and Waldrop and of course Crane will be looking for some measure of revenge. Add in 3 other great talents and you have the making of one hell of a match. Look for any of these 6 to be a future breakout star or stars. MAIN EVENT GRUDGE MATCH! Matt Saunders vs Matthew Reason -The New Revolution took out Matt Saunders in a vicious beating at the March 25th show. Saunders asked for this match so he could extract a little revenge. Storm was more than willing and gave it to him without argument. This one will be HOT! ----------------------- APRIL 22nd, 2001 OKOTOKS, ALBERTA, CANADA OKOTOKS CENTRAL HIGH ----------------------- FRIENDS OR ENEMIES? Red Hot w/ Hank Shank vs Alexander Strakoi w/ Pierre -Both men are known to enjoy the 'finer' things in life not to mention having teamed up at times. However the inevitable has happened and they will face each other one on one. THREE WAY DANCE! Sean Watts vs Psicopa vs 'Top Gun' Ben Tuckwell -Once again I have to state...what would a NEO show be without the high flyers. A couple of these guys are new and wanting to really prove themselves. This could very well be the place they do it. HARDCORE MAYHEM! The Rejects vs Russ Brady/Trent Black -The tougher they are, the harder they fall. Just how tough are these men? Tough enough to do battle in a no rules match. Anything goes so expect not only a blood bath but one hell of a wild and crazy brawl. Talking about Crazy...... CHAMPIONS OF CHAMPIONS MATCH! Steve Dumars(Heavyweight champ) vs The Messiah(Pacific Champion) vs Sushi Tsunami(Skyweight Champion) -The three champions of NEO against each other in one match?! This should be one hell of a three way. A monster star in Dumars, the most underrated wrestler ANYWHERE in Messiah and perhaps the best pound for pound wrestler in the world will face off to see who the Champion of Champions really is. Not only that but Wallace Storm has declared that any wrestler who interferes....IS FIRED! [Open to matches] ----------------------- APRIL 29th, 2001 STORM WARNING SUPERCARD! CALGARY, ALBERTA, CANADA ----------------------- GRUDGE MATCH BATTLE! Russ Brady vs Alexander Strakoi PACIFIC TITLE MATCH! Matt Saunders vs The Messiah(c) [OPEN to matches] ALL titles will be on the line plus more to come!
