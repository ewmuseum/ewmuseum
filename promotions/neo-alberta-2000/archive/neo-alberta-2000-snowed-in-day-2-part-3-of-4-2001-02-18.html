[The crowd erupts as Mobb Deep's "Quiet Storm" hits the arena. A concerned looking Wallace Storm makes his way down to the ring.]
WS: "Thank you all for coming out to support NEO in it's hometown. It means a lot to me. It means a lot to the guys in the back. You are the reason that they are willing to get banged up each and every week."

NEO is about wrestling. It always has been and always will be. All that fancy stuff is a distraction both to the wrestlers and fans. It takes them away from what they are really here for."
