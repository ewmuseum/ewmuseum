The Show for June 23/02 plus Newsletter Issue #1

24 June 2002

Hey kids, _Finally_ it's out. It isn't as good as it could have been, no fault of the writers, just things seemed out of synch, especially with the lay off.
However, next show looks VERY promising with what will surely be some awesome happenings. I have decided to do one more show before the Supercard (which is announced this show. Check it out and see, I won't spoil it}
I will send out an update out after this, in a little bit of a different flavor. It is going to be more in character, a newsletter almost. The only real out of character bits will be the deadlines and such pertaining to the game itself.
So remember...it's the last show before the big Supercard so get your feuds rolling if you want on it. Yes...I know it is a bit quick and such but I shall send out an OOC email explaining why we are doing such. It'll all make sense after that.
Anyhow...here's the link. Enjoy! http://www.neo-wrestling.com/showevent.cfm?id=39 ========================================================= NEO WRESTLING INCORPORATED www.neo-wrestling.com NEWSLETTER ISSUE #1 June 23rd, 2002 CONTENTS -Notes from the desk of Wallace Storm -News and Rumors -Results from the last episode of "The Show" -Waiver Wire -NEO "The Show" Upcoming Lineup (with deadlines) -"Crimson Glory" Lineup ================== FROM THE DESK.... ================== I am happy with this show outside a few small personal and other minor details which can and shall be rectified. We have had meetings with our production personnel regarding timeliness issues as far as putting out our product goes. Expect these to be fixed soon. As well welcome the new talent coming in. This newsletter shall be an information center for the comings, goings and happenings of NEO Wrestling. Be sure to keep this handy.
And don't forget...only one more show before Crimson Glory, so let's be on the top of our games!
================== NEWS AND RUMORS ================== - Injuries continue to plague the NEO roster. Ben Tuckwell is the most notable victim. Don't expect to see him until after Crimson Glory. - Kraken apparently has left NEO without having a single match. - Also rumored to be on the way out- Eddie McIntyre, Catastrophe, Horo Atashi - Expect a new influx of talent soon. Watch for upcoming names. - The Matt Saunders/Hannibal Carver feud and upcoming match at Crimson Glory have the eyes of the wrestling world on them. Expect some huge things between these two. - NEO Wrestling will go on tour after Crimson Glory, touring Western Canada and possibly going East very soon. Another trip to Japan or possible to the
US Mid West and North East are on the table as well.
- The Sky's the Limit is said to be a lock and may possibly be held as quick as a couple months from now. Rumors state to expect talent from outside NEO and outside North America.
- Rumor: The Waldrop/Kraken/DDI stable may already be dead but sources say to expect a heel stable to form none the less. ======== RESULTS ======== -Eddie McIntyre and Vespasian Reed went to a no contest -The Panty Thievez d. The Leading Men -DDI won a 5 man cruiserweight match -Nightlife d. Ash/David Williams -Graham/Morrison/Allen d Samson/Slater/McCray -Hannibal Carver d Ben Tuckwell in a Pacific Title Match -Messiah d. DDI/Kraken =============== WAIVER WIRE =============== RANKINGS AVAILABLE ON THE NEO WEBSITE COMING ------------- COLE DRUGGAN- 6'6" 254lbs Druggan's personality is pretty clear cut and to the point: he's not to be messed with. He's a man of few words, so when he speaks, you can be sure that he's gonna get to the point in a hurry. He mostly gets his intentions across through his actions, and if you piss him off, then he's gonna make sure that you did wrong via the loss of a few teeth and possibly a few broken bones as well. In a nutshell, he's a hard-ass. Don't cross him, or you'll get your face smashed inward.
"BLACK DEATH" CARSON OLIVER- 6'2" 219lbs He's a fighter. He enjoys nothing more, but to fight and he has always, ever since his first wrestling experience, regarded the ring as his real home. He likes to use weapons, and he likes to beat people up, period. What more can you say? He's not a nice person at the best of times, so when he's angry? All hell breaks loose.
HECTOR SANTO- 5'9" 203lbs He's obsessed with putting on a show and classic matches. He thinks if the fans pay their hard earned money to see him perform, then he needs to give them their moneys worth. He thinks work ethic is one of the most important qualities in the industry. He has a little sadistic look to, but hasn't showed us anything too sick as of yet. He's always very quiet and relaxed. He likes to watch what's going on around him.
"SADISTIC" SHANE ALEXANDER- 6'4" 285lbs Shane seems like a gothic type of individual, probably because of his no nonsense personality once in the ring. But Shane is not a gothic man, he is just mistaken. Shane's gimmick to say, would probably just have to be a no nonsense wrestler, with a desire to make others bleed. Maybe a chemical imbalance? :)
GOING ------------- KRAKEN- Show by Show appearance LORD ARCHIBALD DUNSCAITH- Released TRIPP SHADE- Contract dispute, released ================================ UPCOMING "THE SHOW" SCHEDULE ================================ The NEO Arena shall be host to yet another edition of NEO Wrestling, one with a lot riding on it. Tension is in the air as we approach this, the last show before Crimson Glory. Everyone is getting their games faces on, everyone is getting ready...it should be an explosive night of action from beautiful downtown Edmonton!
ASH competed despite a broken arm, putting up a valiant effort in the process, only to be once again attacked by DEREK IRVIN. Expect both to be in the house!
"THE APOSTLE" WES COPELAND and BRIAN TAYLOR will go one on one to finally end it all at the Supercard. No doubt both will have something to say.
Then again...actions speak louder then words! NEO will see the debut of a pair of tough as nails newcomers as the street fighter COLE DRUGGAN appears in NEO for the first time taking on journeyman
"SADISTIC" SHANE ALEXANDER! The Tag Team tourney to determine new champions has come down to four teams. Those four shall compete in the semi finals to determine who will go on to
Crimson Glory in the finals. The first bracket has DEBONAIR taking on THE
PANTY THIEVEZ! The second bracket has a pair of newcomers as the favorites,
THE BRAT PACK take on NIGHTLIFE!
PSICOPA , the curreent NEO SKYWEIGHT CHAMPION, will be in action as he takes on up and comer RED DRAGON! Psicopa and David Williams look to be on a collision course and one has to think it will be a factor in this match.
They asked for it and they shall receive as THE KINGS OF HARDCORE take on new enemies in ACID EGO TRIP. With Junya injured will Frank have to go in to this alone?
Everyone likes three way dances...so why not keep them happy. Three men with three very different enemies will go one on one...on one in the NEO
Arena. JAYSON GRAHAM, Soup Bone Samson hot on his tails, shall take on
BRIAN TAYLOR and VANDAL GOMEZ! Gomez is the wild card in this one and has been on a rampage. This one could get very interesting!
In a battle of the big men, SOUP BONE SAMSON will take on the 7 foot monster "THE EXPERT" MATT ALLEN! MATT SAUNDERS will surely have words with HANNIBAL CARVER as CRIMSON GLORY looms. What will happen next in their explosive rivalry? One can only guess!
As well expect words from both MIKE WALDROP and MESSIAH as they go into their NEO HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH....a match simply called CRIMSON GLORY!
All this plus expect the full announcement of what will go down at CRIMSON
GLORY including the complete lineup and more!
**IMPORTANT** **DEADLINES**- All material, flashes, strats, etc is due on Sunday, June 30th at Midnight Pacific Time so get your stuff in. That is more then enough time. Remember...this is the last show before Crimson Glory,
therefore _very_ important to your character, feud, angle, etc. Send _ALL_ material to [detail withheld] ================ CRIMSON GLORY ================ Most of the Supercard known now as CRIMSON GLORY hasn't been announced as of yet. Expect a blow away show but as of this current moment only a few fantastic matches have been signed for the NEO Arena!
However first expect a preshow with words from all the competitors plus a special match for everyone! The NEO ALBERTA TELEVISION TITLE has been long vacant but at Crimson Glory it shall be no more. However the man who holds it must first win a ROYAL RUMBLE consisting of anyone not already scheduled for the Supercard!
"THE APOSTLE" WES COPELAND has been a hot up and comer in the NEO ranks but has been onset by problems with another hot newcomer in BRIAN TAYLOR. The two will finally meet one on one to settle it all!
The NEO TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS shall be decided! Who will make it to the finals? DEBONAIR or THE PANTY THIEVEZ? NIGHTLIFE or THE BRAT PACK? Either way, a new champion shall be crowned!
ASH had his arm broken courtesy the Irvin Arm Breaker. At Crimson Glory he will look for revenge as he takes on the man who did it... "DEADLY" DEREK
IRVIN! The catch? This match shall be competed under I QUIT rules~! On paper that makes this match a throw away...but never, _never_ count Ash out!
HANNIBAL CARVER branded Rose, the girlfriend of MATT SAUNDERS, igniting a blood feud. These two will undoubtedly meet at the Supercard in some fashion. What kind of match it is is yet undetermined...but expect it to be, like this feud, off the mother f'n charts~!
And in the main event....CRIMSON GLORY! MESSIAH holds the NEO HEAVYWEIGHT
TITLE and will, perhaps for the last time, defend it against his long time enemy..."CREAM OF THE CROP" MIKE WALDROP! With this match promising to be the bloodiest affair in NEO history will either be able to hold anything, let alone the title, again?
