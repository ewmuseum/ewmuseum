Saturday Special (Part 2 of 4)

16 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Cuts to outside the building. A young man standing 6 feet 2 inches wearing a black overcoat, long sleeve black button up shirt, black jeans, white tennis shoes and a black beret on his head approaches the entrance where the
NEO Wrestlers enter the building. Security looks up as The man approaches and they stop him where he is.)
Man: Hello? Is there a problem? Security #1: Uh..Sir where do you think you're going? Man: Going? I'm Amuro Balsa!! I've just signed with NEO! I've come to introduce myself to all the guys and become friends with everyone and share stories of the road!
(Amuro smiles pleasantly at the Security men who turn to look at each other with bewilderment then turn to look back at Amuro.)
Security #2: Well sir do you have a NEO Pass to get in? Amuro: A pass? Oh yes! They mailed me one, hold on a minute.. (Amuro begins checking his pockets.) Amuro: I think I have it with me..Just one moment... (Security begin to look at each other while rolling their eyes as Amuro fruitlessly looks for the pass.)
Amuro: Oh..I..I must have left it in my room..I'll be sure to bring it next time...Can I come in?
Security #1: I'm sorry sir you'll have to leave! Amuro: Oh..Are you sure I can't come in? If you ask Wallace Storm I'm sure he will vouch for me...
Security #2: We can't do that sir, you'll have to leave! Amuro: Well..Ok..You are right..Heh heh..Doing your job and all..Maybe next week when I bring my pass we can laugh on this and enjoy the brotherhood of the road together!
Security #1: Brotherhood of the..? Sir You need to leave! Security #2: Mr. Storm would never hire Perverts like you! (Amuro wide eyed) Amuro: Pervert? Wh-Wh-Wh-Wh... Security #1: Just get away from us Sir before we call the police! Security #2: Yeah get the Hell out of here! (Amuro turns around walking away.) Amuro: (talking to himself) Pervert? What did I say? (Amuro walks away the Two Security laugh between themselves.) Security #1: Can you believe that guy? He expects us to believe he's in NEO? Security #2: And did you see him try to hit on us? Security #1: It takes all kinds I suppose!! Security #2: You can say that again! (Cuts back to the show) [The arena lights dim as "Baba O' Riley" by The Who begins to play. The crowd erupts, as the instantly recognizable music fills their ears. Around
40 seconds of atmospheric synthesizer music plays, until the song picks up.
The beat plays loudly until the lyrics start. ] "Out here in the fields, I fight for my meals. I get my back into my living!" The crowd begins to cheer even louder as 'The Living Legend' Mike Flame steps out from behind the curtain, a bright white spotlight shining upon him.
"I don't need to fight, to prove I'm right. I don't need to be forgiven!" Flame remains in the entranceway soaking in the arena's atmosphere, whilst the song begins to slow down.
"Don't cry, don't raise your eye. It's only a teenage wasteland!" As the song begins to up the tempo once more, Mike starts to make his way to the ring, arms raised.
"So Sally take my hand, we'll travel south 'cross land. Put out the fire, and don't look past my shoulder. The exodus is here, but I'll be walked on here. Let's get together, before we get much older. We're all WASTED!!!!"
As the songs lyrics stop, but the tune continues, Flame slowly walks up the ring steps, wipes his feet on the apron and steps through the middle rope.
Once in the ring, he signals to the crowd once again, runs against the ropes, and jumps up into the air. Flame is then joined by the crowd in his familiar cry of "YYYYYYYYYOOOOOOOWWWWWWWWW!!!!!!"
(Mike is wearing yet another of his trademark suits. This particular one is charcoal gray, and underneath he wears a white poloneck shirt. As usual, his hair is left loose. Flame reaches for a microphone, as the crowd finally begin to settle...)
-MF- "Pegasus, as you know, yourself and I will be facing off in an I Quit match at the pay-per-view. Well, that's all well and good, but I'm not sure that's gonna be enough for The Living Legend. Do you think that one match can help me exact revenge for everything that you have done to me? Hell no.
So, Pegasus my boy, I'm calling your ass out right now! (Huge pop from the crowd). We can have any kinda match you like-a Hardcore match, a cage match, your fatass on a pole match-I just don't care.
(Yet another big pop). So come on big man, prove you've got the balls!" (With that, Mike drops the microphone down to the mat, and prepares himself for his rival's entrance. Just as Mike thinks Pegasus isn't about to show up...
[We see Pegasus emerge from the entrance portal, mic in hand. He's still in his street clothes, but he's sans winter jacket at this point.]
Pegasus: You want to challenge me to a match _now_, Mike? I don't know if you've lost your marbles or what. It's bad enough that you have to wrestle once already. I don't know if we've got two oxygen tanks in the back for you to suck air out of. I really don't feel like being responsible for your death when your emphysema kills you. Honestly? I've got better things to do with my time, Mike. You've got your match at the pay per view. Let's leave it at that.
[Pegasus drops the mic and disappears behind the curtain.] JJ: "It looks like Flame is just going to have to wait for next week." TS: "All the better. People should have to pay to watch someone get killed." JJ: "I wonder if Flame realizes that his match is next." TS: "He probably forgot." [Scene cuts to the back to show Rebecca Owens meeting 'Roman' Thomas Lawrence. She seems to be having a good time in her first visit to NEO with her dad. Black Knight is seen passing in the background. The reporter escort tries to stop Knight so Rebecca can meet him too, but Black Knight continues on without saying a word.]
=============C=O=M=M=E=R=C=I=A=L============== WHEN HELL FREEZES OVER 12/23/00 + 4-Way Match to crown the first ever NEO Heavyweight Champion. + Mike Flame vs. Pegasus in an "I QUIT" match + Rizer vs. Black Knight in a grudge match + High flying action of 'Gecko' Adams and Sushi Tsunami + Elijah Flynt vs. Alexander Strakoi in a London Street Fight + Plus more to come... ============================================== [Backstage locker room, Elijah Flynt, wearing the per usual, enjoys a pre-match smoke. Not much emotion is shown, in fact, he rather looks apathetic. The Impressionable Young Reporter marches into the room. Elijah looks over, glaring at the kid. ]
Flynt: Wot? [ The teen clears his throat, trying to make it sound 'deeper'. ] IYR: Umm, you have your debut coming up, how are you feeling? Any pre-match worries? Nervousness? Anxiety?
[ Elijah rolls his eyes. ] Flynt: You sound like a bleedin' commercial for th' latest medical wonder drug. IYR: Sorry... Flynt: Don't sweat it mate. [ The youth looks down, Elijah looks over at him, exhaling a thick haze of smoke. ] Flynt: To answer yer question, I don't really feel anything 'bout fighting t'night. Just another match fer me. Nothing special about it. Sure it may be my first here in NEO, but I'm not going t'let that get t'me. I'll treat it like any other match I've 'ad. Win or lose, it's all 'bout givin' th' fans their pound... er, money's worth. If I can make 'em go 'ome 'appy t'night,
I've done me job, an' that's good enough fer me.
IYR: Cool. Flynt: As for me opponents... wot can I say 'bout 'em with out bein' negative? I can't. Scorpion's a right fruit! Eklipz is some berk who thinks black lights are all th' bloody rage! Bollocks to 'em!
[ Elijah takes a long drag, savouring the taste. ] Flynt: If ye don't mind mate? [ Elijah steps past the teen reporter, and positions himself in front of the camera. ] Flynt: Scorpion, you parade yer arse around like yer some sweet smellin' shite, don't ye? Not t'say yer an uppity sort, but ye think yer some 'ot number, right? Yer nothin'. I've fought yer type for years, all mouth and no fookin' trousers. Talk the talk, but ye can't quite back up yer words. I know your type. The type that somehow manages t'be around, but not quite efficient enough to do ANYthing.
Ooooh, an' yer nice l'il catch phrase... IYR: ~YOU WILL FEEL MY STING~ [ Elijah glares over towards the kid, then can't help but chuckle. ] IYR: What? Flynt: Nothing, you just do one 'eck of an imitation lad! Heh. [ Elijah takes another drag from his cig as he turns to the camera once again. ] Flynt: I'm really curious, Scorpy, to see wot y'mean by that. Are ye gay? Nothing wrong about bein' gay mate, I've got friends who are, but are ye still trying to keep yer arse in the closet, just makin' some sexual innuendo in yer commentary? From where I'm standin, that's pretty damn strong innuendo! But no matter... wot I suggest is savin' yerself some trouble an' just out right admit it! Easier'n filling us with yer bulls**t.
Just so ye know, I don't bend that way... but I'm flattered t'think you find yer opponents 'ere in NEO attractive like. Ta!
[ Taking one last puff, Elijah puts the cig out in a cup of water. ] Flynt: An' Eklipz, well mate, I got respect for ye... yer dangerous with your shite. Crazy stuff you do it is... I look forward t' facin' ye 'ere t'night. But something's gotta be done with yer attitude... you really should get out in th' daytime a l'il bit mate. Nevermind, to each 'is own.
[ Elijah rubs his neck. ] Flynt: Anyways, the three of us will be fightin' an' such real soon like, so
I'll let th' talkin' of me talent speak for itself in th' ring, where it belongs... instead of sayin 'ow fookin'-bloody-tastic I am. That crap never did a thing for me... sayin 'ow 'ot one is.
[ Elijah goes to leave. ] Flynt: Oh, one more thing, I am using this 'ere match as a testing ground of sorts... all th' effin' way to When Hell Freezes Over... Aye! 'At's when I face the right bastard, Strakoi... an' mates, that's wot I'm gearin' me arse for! This match tonight is me warm-up... but it's not going t'be an easy match, that's fer bleedin' sure! First match I 'ave an' it's between me an'
two other blokes? Bloody 'ell! Ah well, 'tis th' way th' bones roll I s'ppose. [ Elijah scratches his left cheek. ] Flynt: Take care lad. [ Waves to the impressionable young reporter, then heads out of the room as the camera cuts back to the announcers. ]
JJ: Now Flame is up. Did he get changed quickly enough?? TS: No way! Roman is going to kill him anyway. (An unfamiliar figure appears from behind the curtain. He doesn't have any music, and comes out to near-enough no pop at all. He stands about 6ft1, and is of fairly average build. He wears long blue tights, with yellow stars sewn on to either leg. His skin is tanned, and he wears yellow boots. Most noticeable of all, however, is his mask, which is a swirled mixture of both navy blue and bright yellow. The masked newcomer makes his way to the ring, arms raised, but still does not get any kind of reaction from the crowd. He climbs through the middle ring rope, and calls for the stick...)
JJ: I guess Flame isn't next. TS: Damn Storm and all of his international talent. "I guess the question that you are all asking is 'who is this guy?' Who I am is not important. All you need to know is that I am possibly the finest luchadore that this world has ever seen. I have beat top stars from both
Japan and Mexico, and now I want to make a name for myself here in NEO. So, in order to be recognized, I am calling out any wrestler from the back, who wants to take me on. I don't care who it is. They can be 5ft tall, or 7ft tall, 100lbs or 500lbs."
(Slight pop from the crowd, but nobody accepts the challenge.) "Come on, don't be scared!" [Pegasus emerges once again from the entrance portal. This time he's decked out in his wrestling attire with a cheshire like-grin across his face. He produces a mic and starts down to the ring.]
Pegasus: An open challenge, huh? Consider it answered. [Pop from the crowd.] JJ: Looks like Pegasus has answered the challenge of this new luchadore! TS: Looks like it's going to be a short career too for this luchadore. He had the wrong man to tango with answer his challenge.
JJ: Pegasus in the ring as the luchadore looks on. [There's a pop from the crowd as Steve Dumars makes his way down the aisle.] TS: What's he doing here!? JJ: I don't know! Steve Dumars has come down the aisle! He's stopped about midway. Pegasus turns and sees him.
[Pegasus starts to spew venomous words in Dumars' direction. That's just enough time for the luchadore to make his move.]
JJ: Pegasus has his full attention on Dumars! That's not a good idea! [DING, DING, DING.] TS: Turn around Pegasus! JJ: Inside cradle from the luchadore! He's got Pegasus' shoulders on the mat! ONE! TWO! THREE!!! TS: No way! Cheap! Cheap! JJ: The luchadore wins the match! He wins the match! [Pegasus sits on his knees stunned as can be. The luchadore celebrates, but then the odd thing happens. We hear a loud
"YOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOW" come from the luchador as the crowd gives a shocked pop.]
JJ: Is that... ? [Sure enough. The lucha pulls off his mask to reveal the "Living Legend" Mike Flame. BIG POP! Flame celebrates a bit more as we see Dumars with an amused grin on his face.]
TS: Sweet mother Mary of God! Flame's a dead man! Flame's a dead man! JJ: Flame pulled the wool over everyone's eyes! He got a victory of Pegasus! [Flame turns around at one point only to find Pegasus' boot smack right into his jaw. Pegasus grabs the stunned Flame and pulls him in for his jack-knife power bomb.]
TS: Flame's about to be retired right here! JJ: Not so fast! [Who can forget about Dumars? Not going to watch anymore, Dumars slides into the ring behind Pegasus. As Pegasus goes to lift, Dumars rebounds off the ropes and nearly takes Pegs' head off with a vicious looking lariat.
Pegasus hits the mat as Dumars checks on Flame.] JJ: Dumars with the save! Dumars with the save! TS: See! He has to stick his nose in everywhere! JJ: Flame and Dumars have made it no secret they're friends! Dumars is just helping out a friend!
[Dumars and Flame get to their feet as dazed Pegasus gets to his. Flame and
Dumars look at each other and seem to be on the same page. Double
front-facelock followed up by a double DDT.] JJ: Double DDT! Double DDT _spikes_ Pegasus into the mat! TS: Get security out here! This is unfair! It's two-on-one! [Pegasus is brought back to his feet and then taken out of the ring with a double clothesline. Pegasus hits the arena floor and quickly gets to his feet and dashes for the locker room area, grabbing the guardrail for support. Both Flame and Dumars standing tall in the ring; Flame egging
Pegasus to come back for a bit more.]
