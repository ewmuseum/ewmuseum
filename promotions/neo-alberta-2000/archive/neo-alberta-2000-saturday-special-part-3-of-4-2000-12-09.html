NEO (Alberta, 2000) — Card Archive

Saturday Special (Part 3 of 4)

9 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Cut to the locker room area where we see Pegasus getting ready for his match later on with Shannon Cannon. The blond-haired big man is in his wrestling attire, but finishing lacing up his left boot. He's seated on the bench, and looks at the camera. He brushes a few strands of hair out of his face as he starts to speak.]
Pegasus: So I get Shannon Cannon this week. It's a tragedy, really. For all the hot air 'dozer spewed. It's a tragedy I didn't get to step into the ring with him and proved while he's still a pup. I guess he finally learned he wasn't going to win the world title and split to go run amuck over another pond. It doesn't matter. I'll get to you in a moment, Cannon. I want to talk to you, Dumars.
[Pegasus sits up, pushing his hair back once again.] Pegasus: I told I was threw with you weeks ago, Dumars. You decided you still wanted me on your playground. Apparently, you were tired of the toddlers currently inhabiting your playing field, but it fits. It's all too perfect for you, Steve, because you still don't get it. You remind me of that one guy who could never get it through his head. Well, shoot. You don't remind me of him; you are him.
[He nods.] Pegasus: You wanted to play the games, so I took a bite. Apparently, you didn't like that fact and seemed almost surprised. Remember who put himself into this situation. It isn't my fault. It's no one else's fault. You made your own bed. Now you get to deal with me messing it up. You wanna say you'll get even? By all means, Steve. I'm game, babycakes. [Grin.] Do you really want to find out who can sink the deepest in the depths of sick and twisted?
[He shakes his head, his hair swaying about his shoulders.] Pegasus: I really don't you want to because you haven't seen me lose my sense of humor yet. You don't. Trust me. As for you, Owens? Go crawl under whatever rock 'dozer disappeared under. You'd be doing the world a huge favor if you just disappeared.
[Pegasus pauses and smirks.] Pegasus: As for you, Flame. I didn't do anything wrong. You talk like I was out to screw you, but if I was screwing you that bad. How are you able to walk so well? [Sly grin.] I followed the official refs handbook. You tapped your shoulder twice signaling you gave up. So I called for the bell.
What's so hard to understand about that? I mean, you surely weren't upset when the same thing won you a match. Now you cry foul when it costs you won? Hypocrite. Just go ahead and retire. Save me the energy of having to make that choice for you because I won't make it pleasant.
[Pegasus nods to reaffirm his last sentence. Then he moves on.] Pegasus: As for you, Cannon. I really can't say too much about you because
I pay about as much attention to you as I do the rest of the crazies trying to run the asylum. Understand this. Much like yourself, I'm out to win this match. While you may be more than halfway decent, that's not going to be enough. I need to vent a bit. I need to take out some anger instead of keeping it all bottled up inside and letting it eat away at me. That, sweetcheeks, is where you come in. For one night -- and one night ever I might add -- you get to be yours truly's personal punching bag. What better way as a send off into the rest of your career?
[He shakes his head.] Pegasus: No, I agree. I couldn't think of any better other way to go ahead and bless the rest of your existence you're going to call a career. Oh. And
I do plan on making a statement. Too bad you just happen to be the board
I'm going to display it on.
[Cut back to ringside.] TS: "You know, I really like him. He tells it like it is. Right down the middle is how Peg calls it and he proved it when he reffed the Flame match." JJ: "I don't know about that. It was awfully suspicious." [Back to School"by The Deftones plays over the P.A. System as G-Digga runs to the ring and slides in.]
JJ: "Well that had no wasted energy." TS: "I think he is running from talent." [As the arena turns to a darkness, "Roots Bloody Roots" by Sepultura begins to play. The fans jump to their feet and a yellow light shines near the entrance. Smoke starts to float from the ground and Chris Garrison comes walking out from the back. With his normal ring attire on, Garrison raises his hands high in the air. As the crowd gives Chris a small pop, Chris starts walking to the ring. After walking around the ring, causing the fans to reach out for a high five, Garrison slides into the ring and prepares for battle. The arena then goes back to the normal setting.]
JJ: "Here is another guy that had nothing but potential. But he has been so quiet of late, no one knows what is happening."
[Lights flicker and the Limp Bizkits 'Rollin(remix)' hits over the PA system , Gecko walks out from behind the curtains , he stands and puts his head down quickly and shakes his body hard. He slides headfirst into the ring and sits on the turnbuckle waiting for his opponent.]
TS: "Notice just how their is not even enough talent in the ring to count on one finger?" ["Bad Religion" by Godsmack hits over the PA. The lights go dark and Cheshire slinks out to ringside, sliding into the ring to a big heel reaction and staying crouched as the announcer calls the introductions.]
JJ: "These are four great exciting wrestlers. This could turn into quite a match." JD: "Ladies and gentlemen, this tag match is scheduled for one fall. First, from West Frankfort, Illinois, standing 5'11" and weighing in at
215lbs here is CHESSSHHHHIRRRREEE!!!"
[HUGE HEEL POP] JD: "His partner from San Francisco, California stands 5'11" and weighs in at 190lbs. He is JOHNNN GECKO AAADAAAAMMMMSSSS!!!!!"
[The crowd lets out a small but respectable cheer.] JD: "And their opponents, first from Hell Michigan, he is a member of the next Generation superstars. Standing 6'9" and weighing in tonight at 310lbs, here is G-DIIIGGGAAAAAAA!!!!!!" [HEEL POP] TS: "Hell's Alliance is so going to kill him." JD: "And his partner is from Sun City Arizona. Standing 6'2" and weighing in at 237 lbs, HERE IS CHRIS GARRISSSON!!!"
[SMALL FACE POP] DING DING DING John Adams and G-Digga begin the match.] JJ: "G-Digga... well, G-Digga just does not look like he's in a good mood at all." TS: "Hey, if _I_ were assigned Chris Garrison as a tag team partner, I'd have one mean scowl on my face too. Well, I _still_ have a scowl on my face, because I have to watch John Adams and Chris Garrison in a match. And yes, even though G-Digga and Cheshire are in this match--which is a partial saving grace--I still can't get over how much Chris Garrison just blows ass."
JJ: "That's enough, Teddy... Digga has control after a 'questionable' start--Adams went for a tie-up, but Digga planted his kneecap deep into the abdomen of the Gecko."
TS: "What the _hell_ kind of name is 'the Gecko' for yourself?" JJ: "It's a nickname." TS: "Okay--what the hell kind of _nickname_ is 'the Gecko' for a professional wrestler? Is it supposed to instill fear in the hearts of the opponents? Am I supposed to be 'shaking in my boots' right now? Because I'm not. And I sure as hell don't think G-Digga is right now, either... I mean, just look at him."
JJ: "Indeed, just look at the man--he's stomping the proverbial mudhole into the Gecko! The referee trying to back Digga off... but it's to no avail, as he just runs right back and drops a knee onto the abdomen of Adams."
[Digga brings Adams back to his feet, then shoves him into the corner... Digga storms in after him, and once again, drives a kneecap into the midsection of his opponent, doubling him over. Digga quickly bounces off the nearest ropes, then jumps high in the air _drives_ Adams' forehead into the canvas, in what is almost an in-air legdrop.]
JJ: "There's a 'famasser' from G-Digga, and he goes for the cover..." [The referee manages to pound the canvas twice, but surprisingly, Cheshire extends his right leg through the middle and bottom ropes, and breaks the pin with a well-placed kick to the forehead. Digga is up, and fuming--he gets right in the face of Cheshire, but the ref is able to hold the man back.]
JJ: "Well, what a surprise that is--Cheshire actually breaking the pinfall attempt of G-Digga... maybe we're actually going to see some coherent teamwork between Cheshire and Gecko."
TS: "Hell, Cheshire is the guy who, last Wednesday, basically ripped on all three guys in this match. This is one clusterfrick of a match we've got here--I don't know _how_ Storm decided to book this one, I only know that he was under some sort of influence while he did it."
JJ: "That's not necessarily the case, Smith, and you know it--however, Digga is finally pushed back from the corner of Cheshire... Gecko reaches up for a tag, but I don't think Cheshire sees him--he's still bickering with Digga."
TS: "How can Cheshire not see his partner? I mean, the guy is right there, and he has green, scaly skin. That guy can be picked out of _any_ crowd."
JJ: "Uh... _right_, Teddy. Digga heads in for the 'kill' once again, and
Gecko, still reaching for a tag, has no defense against a big boot to the midsection!"
[Adams curls up in a fetal position, and Digga takes the opportunity to bring him back to his feet. One Irish whip into the ropes later, Adams is coming back--with amazing speed--but Digga stops him dead in his tracks with a shoulderblock.]
JJ: "Adams sent down to the canvas _hard_ with that move there." TS: "So tell me, ol' buddy, who exactly do _you_ think G-Digga will be picking for his partner against the Hell's Alliance at When Hell Freezes
Over?"
JJ: "Well, Teddy, I hardly think this is the time nor the place to be discussing something like that... oh, there's a big splash from Digga! A cover! One... two... NO!--but anyway, Teddy, I don't think that we should be discussing this... Digga with an elbowsmash!"
TS: "You never pay attention to me anymore, Jonny." JJ: [mystified silence] "..." [While the awkward silence passes, we see Digga reaches towards his corner for the tag. Garrison makes the tag, then steps into the ring... but at the same time, Gecko is able to roll into his corner and tag in Cheshire--who wasn't quite paying full
attention. This allows Garrison to get a slight advantage, pulling Cheshire into the ring over the top rope.]
JJ: "Back to your point, Teddy--I truly don't know who Digga is going to pick as his partner, but I _do_ know that he better make the right choice, or else we'll be less one man from our roster. Actually, he'll still be here but we'll have to scrape him off the mat with a spatula."
TS: "I wouldn't go that far, Johnson. We both know that Digga is one tough mofo, not to mention absolutely insane. Hell, he might not even _try_ to get a partner, he might just go into that match in the handicap mentality and see what he can do that way... even though that's something that _I_ wouldn't do."
JJ: "One thing you also wouldn't do is go on a diet, big man." TS: "Now that was just a low blow." [While the two commentators bicker, we see extended action in the ring--Chris Garrison has Cheshire backed into a corner, and is winding up for a big right hand--he swings, but Cheshire, with cat-like speed, side steps the punch attempt and returns with a stiff forearm shot of his own.
This staggers Garrison--Cheshire takes the advantage, and, as gracefully as ever, leaps into the air, plants both feet onto the chest of Garrison and sends the man to the canvas with a stellar dropkick."
JJ: "Excellent form on the dropkick there, and Garrison is sent two thirds of the way across the ring. Cheshire, once again with amazing speed, runs into the ropes... there's a lighting-fast legdrop, and the resulting cover--
One...
Two... Thr--no, there's a relatively easy kickout from Garrison. However, Cheshire is back up to his feet in a flash, and he brings Garrison back up in the same amount of time. Irish whip--no, it's reversed--Cheshire sent into the ropes... he comes back--he leaps--and there's a hurricarana! But no cover."
TS: "You wanna talk about guys to run away from...talk about Cheshire. After that stunt he pulled with Dumars from last week, I mean, come on... that's a damned kitten. I hate cats myself--damn things are only good for picking up chicks--but what this guy did... well, he's one sick guy. Even by my standards."
JJ: "That seems to be the general consensus around the NEO. After Steve
Dumars hit him with the accidental--"
TS: "_Supposedly_ accidental--" JJ: "Right, supposedly accidental chairshot, this man has never been the same... he's gone on a mission, I guess it could be called, against Dumars, trying to unearth something from the man's past."
TS: "I personally couldn't care less what Cheshire has to say about Dumars, as long as it ends with Dumbass tied up somewhere with duct tape over his mouth. Or maybe just beat up real good. Anything to get his nasal voice out of my headset."
[As these two bicker back and forth, Cheshire brings Garrison to his feet and applies an abdominal stretch.]
JJ: "I do believe that you are one of the only holders of that opinion, however, Teddy." TS: "No, no, there's also Pegasus. And Ray Owens. And, probably, Cheshire... and Lawrence. And the rest of the NEO roster. Not to mention a lot of the fans--"
JJ: "And I'm just going to interrupt you right there with the play by play... Cheshire has an abdominal stretch locked in _tight_, and it's not looking like he's going to be getting out of that hold anytime soon.
Cheshire just applying the pressure... and he rolls it over into a pinfall attempt! One! Two! Thre...NO!" [Garrison barely manages to raise his left shoulder before the three-count. Cheshire up to his feet, once again with cat-like speed.] JJ: "Cheshire drags Garrison to his corner, then reaches for the tag--which he gets. Gecko with a leap over the top rope... Cheshire holding Garrison's arm high over his head, leaving him vulnerable--and Adams with a quick boot to the midsection. Once again, surprising teamwork from these two men, who
I personally thought would not be able to get along, let alone co-exist as a coherent tag team."
TS: "Once again proving that you shouldn't think." [Adams brings Garrison back to his feet, scoops him up for a bodyslam--but he can't quite hold the weight. He is forced to release Garrison back down to the canvas... and that short pause allows Garrison to get just enough energy to hit a relatively weak clothesline.]
JJ: "Not necessarily an impressive clothesline there, but it got the job done... Garrison crawling back to his corner--and there's the tag to Digga.
Digga back into the ring, and once again, he looks like a man possessed... I wouldn't want to be John Adams right now... and that's why! That's why!
That was just a HUGE lariat! I'm surprised... I'm surprised John Adams is even rolling around in pain right now, that was just a _monster_ lariat. It just floored Adams."
TS: "Indeed it did. This guy is in one foul mood." [Digga casually tosses the much smaller Adams into the corner... he charges in, following Adams, then nails the Gecko with a big elbowsmash to the nose.
Adams slowly stumbles out of the corner, and Digga follows up his move with another, higher-impact maneuver--a tombstone piledriver!]
JJ: "OH MY!! OH MY!! TOMBSTONE!! AND A COVER!! ONE! TWO! THRE...NO!" [Cheshire ran into the ring, and tried to kick Digga off Adams--but Digga rolled away before the blow hit, and instead of hitting Digga, Cheshire kicked his own partner in the stomach.]
JJ: "Call me crazy, but Cheshire had the chance to stop that kick before it hit." TS: "Okay, you're crazy--and you're probably right. Cheshire doesn't like his partner, we both know that."
JJ: "But just a minute ago they were working together as a cohesive unit... and now, Cheshire is just turning his back?" TS: "Hey, he just couldn't stop the momentum. Maybe." [Cheshire slowly "slinks" out of the ring as Digga picks Adams up, then puts him right back down on the canvas with a double-leg takedown. Digga continues to hold onto the legs, almost setting up for a sharpshooter--but instead, he violently wrenches the
right leg back at an angle which it wasn't meant to be wrenched at. Or wrenched at all, for that matter.]
TS: "Hey hey--smart move on the part of Digga here. He's taking the air attack away from Gecko. The only thing this guy can do. Maybe he's one of those lizards that have those multi-colored wings. Or something."
JJ: "Right, Teddy--right. But indeed, Digga beginning to work on the leg... he brings Adams back up to his feet--standing kneebreaker! Adams right back down, and clutching his right knee! Digga, with just two moves, has possibly inflicted a somewhat serious injury on Adams... and he's going right back in for more! A kneedrop... another... and now with a series of stomps to the inside of the knee! He's just relentless! This man is relentless!"
TS: "That's what it takes to be a damned star here in the NEO, Johnson. What you're seeing right there--what you're seeing in G-Digga's brutal attack on the Gecko's knee--what you're seeing is what it's going to take to defeat Hell's Alliance... what you're seeing is a man who really doesn't _need_ a tag team partner when WHFO comes rolling around. What you're seeing is a man not to be messed with. John
Adams is learning that right as we speak."
JJ: "Some would argue that... but none would argue the severity in which
G-Digga just picked up and slammed down John Adams' knee! Let's look at that replay!"
[The split-screen comes up, and on the right we see G-Digga scowling at the fans--the realtime--and on the left, we see a slow-motion replay of G-Digga brutally picking up and slamming down the right knee of his opponent, the
Gecko. Cut back to the one shot.]
TS: "Now that was one sadistic replay, Johnson." JJ: "Indeed it was, and it's the fault of one man--G-Digga." TS: "He's just doing his damned job... he's just trying to get this match finished. He's doing a damned good job of it, too."
JJ: "Digga is now taunting Cheshire... he's taunting Cheshire to run into the ring. And Cheshire just... he just grins. Adams up to his one good knee now, but Digga turns around, and plants him back down to the canvas with a well-placed boot to the leg."
[Adams down again, clutching his knee. Digga raises his arms to the crowd, still with the scowl on his face, and the crowd responds in kind with their chorus of boos. Digga makes an obscene gesture--one we don't have to mention, or show on television, for that matter--and then, for good measure, drops yet another knee onto the inside of Adams' leg. He makes a lackluster cover, not hooking the leg...
And only gets a two count because of it.] JJ: "Questionable decision there, not hooking the leg. Digga might've had the chance to put this match away for his team, but instead he chose to go with the arrogant lack of a leg-hook, and that's why he got the two."
TS: "First of all--a win is only a win for G-Digga. Garrison has done absolutely nothing in this match, save for standing in his corner, looking stupid and gawking at that not-half-bad-looking blond sitting in the second row there."
JJ: "He's had his share of action in this match, Teddy..." TS: "Too bad it was the receiving end of some good old fashioned Cheshire-style beatdowns." JJ: "And speaking of Cheshire--here he is, coming into the ring! Cheshire with a big forearm to the forehead of Digga! The ref pushing the illegal man, Cheshire, out of the ring, but the damage is done... and here comes
Adams to his corner! Obviously, Cheshire just did that so he can tag in...
but... but what is he doing?" [Just as Adams reaches his corner, then strains to raise his hand up and request the tag from Cheshire... Cheshire pulls his hand back and drops to the arena floor. The crowd, puzzled, and somewhat angry, begins to boo
Cheshire... and in response, he scowls at the crowd. He jumps back up on the apron, and the Gecko is still reaching for the tag.]
JJ: "It looks... it looks like Cheshire doesn't want to tag in! Adams still reaching for it... and Cheshire kicks his partner! On purpose! That was definitely an intentional kick to the face of Adams! Exactly _what_ is going on with Cheshire?"
TS: "Who knows--the guy helps Adams, the guy kicks Adams... the guy is crazy, that's for damned sure. Maybe he keeps forgetting that Adams is his tag team partner."
JJ: "I highly doubt that. I think Cheshire is doing this on purpose... and
I think he's enjoying it immensely, for that matter. Just look at that sadistic grin on his face."
[Digga, apparently deciding he's finished with Adams for now, decides to tag in his partner. Garrison comes back in, and picks Adams up... but Adams, having had a small ten-second recovery, is able to kick Garrison in the stomach, then drop him to the mat with a decent DDT.]
JJ: "Adams drops Garrison with that move, and now he's once again crawling back to his corner--Cheshire has his attention directed elsewhere, at the crowd--and there's the tag! Adams tagged the back of Cheshire, without
Cheshire even seeing it!"
[This seems to have upset Cheshire, who instead of immediately coming into the ring, again tries to kick his partner. However, Adams rolls out of the way just in time, and Garrison, now back to his feet, pulls Cheshire into the ring.]
JJ: "Garrison, with a handful of hair, brings Cheshire to the 'buckles. An
Irish whip... is reversed, sending Garrison into the opposite buckles... and
Cheshire with a poke to the eye! Heinous tactic employed by Cheshire!
Garrison is disheveled... and there's a neckbreaker! And a cover! [One... Two... Thre--Digga with the save.] JJ: "The ref forces Digga out of the ring once again, and Cheshire right back down. He sits Garrison on the mat... Indian Deathlock! Indian
Deathlock applied, that nerve-pinching hold, and it's taking it's toll on
Chris Garrison right here."
TS: "Damn. Garrison is just getting _mutilated_ in this match. Hell, the punk deserves it."
JJ: "Now Teddy, come on... that nervehold just draining the energy, the life right out of Chris Garrison... it's an incredibly painful maneuver, and because of that fact, it's incredibly difficult to summon the willpower to break out of it. Which makes it an even more effective maneuver."
TS: "Thoughtful, yet obvious, comments, Johnson." JJ: "I try my hardest for you, Teddy." [G-Digga suddenly bursts into the ring and breaks up the deathlock, mercifully for Garrison. Cheshire and Digga begin to brawl, trading punches back and forth.]
JJ: "There's G-Digga! G-Digga in the ring... but Cheshire has the advantage in the punch battle... and there's a dropkick! The dropkick sends Digga to the ropes... and over them! What a dropkick _that_ was!"
[Digga falls onto the protective mats on the outside, completely stunned... Garrison is back on his feet, and Cheshire kicks him in the midsection.] JJ: "Garrison bent over... there's a double underhook... could we see a tiger driver? Can he get him up?"
[Cheshire, in an amazing feat of strength, lifts Garrison up in the air and drops him _hard_ on his back with a tiger driver!]
JJ: "Wow! He did it! A cover... One! Two! Three! That's it! That's the three!" [The bell rings.] [Cheshire's theme plays as the referee attempts to raise his hand. Cheshire quickly, and swiftly, pulls his hand away from the ref's touch... then raises them slowly, deliberately... arrogantly... to the crowd. Of course, the crowd responds with a healthy amount of heel heat.]
TS: "I knew the psycho could do it. The man's insane--this is true--but he got the job done against Garrison and Digga tonight. I feel for Digga, though, he was wrestling a damned fine match. Too bad he was saddled with a worthless partner."
JJ: "That's a matter of debate--but Garrison did seem to be 'off his game,' as it were--he just didn't seem to work in any offense at all here in this contest." [Cheshire stands, victorious, looking at his tag team partner--John 'Gecko' Adams. Adams is still hurting from the G-Digga attack.] JJ: "Cheshire looking down at his partner--WHOA! He just snapped! Cheshire is down on top of Adams, raining down right hands... Cheshire just utterly snapped... and well, he lunged at Adams and is now just destroying him with right hands!"
[The battle between Cheshire and Adams spills to the outside, just as Digga rolls back into the ring... and then confronts Garrison, who is now regaining his senses. Digga, with a look of anger greater than we've seen tonight, pushes Garrison... Garrison tries to push back--but he's taken down by a _big_ right hand from Digga!]
JJ: "We've got a pair of brawls! The two tag teams have split off into their own battles!" TS: "Well, it looks like we won't be seeing these two teams _ever_ compete for any kind of NEO tag team gold."
JJ: "I could've told you that from the beginning, Teddy... OH! Cheshire just pushed Adams face-first into the ringsteps! He hit those HARD! And in the ring, Digga... Digga just hit his finisher! The Thrilla! Gorilla press into sitdown powerbomb! WHAT
A MOVE!" [Chaos ensues as the two battles rage on. Cheshire continues to toss Adams around like a ragdoll on the outside, ramming him face-first into the guardrail, the ringsteps, the ring apron, then finally tossing him over the guardrail and leaping over himself. The two men begin to brawl all over the floor, as in the ring, Digga continues to decimate Garrison with all sorts of kicks, punches and other moves...]
JJ: "This... this is absolute chaos! Cheshire and John Adams are your winners... but right now, they're brawling throughout the crowd! I... I just cant' describe this, folks! [pause] We've... we've gotta take a break, we're running late on it anyway... we'll be back!"
