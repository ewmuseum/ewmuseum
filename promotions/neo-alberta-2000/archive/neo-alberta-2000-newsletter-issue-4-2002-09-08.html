NEO WRESTLING INCORPORATED www.neo-wrestling.com NEWSLETTER ISSUE #4 September 8th, 2002 CONTENTS -Notes from the desk of Wallace Storm -News and Rumors -Results from the last episode of "The Show" -Waiver Wire -Uncivil War -NEO "The Show" Upcoming Lineup (with deadlines) -"Crimson Glory" Lineup ================== FROM THE DESK.... ================== The shows are starting to get pretty packed now with more participation... plus the ongoing BSCW stuff. Expect to see joint stuff between BSCW and NEO on each show leading to "Uncivil War". Either way, for those who are participating... awesome. For those who aren't, well you know what happens. One other note, if you say you will match write and say you can have it in by a certain day... make sure it is in by that day. Missing a flash is one thing... not doing a match is ten times worse and ground for immediate dismissal. Out of character for a second, some VERY important notes. 1) There are no goddamn pyros or no titan tron... so backstage vignettes will NOT get pops. 2) ALL FRICKING multi part oncards must be passed by me FIRST! I will not use any that are not. Period Of course I can use writers and all that shit... the usual deal. ================== NEWS AND RUMORS ================== -The joint NEO/BSCW card is announced as "Uncivil War" and as well... locations for all three nights have been announced! The Sky's the Limit Tournament will come from a place well known: Hammerstein Ballroom! Night 2 shall originate from BSCW's home in Boston's Axis Ballroom, Night 3 from the NEO Arena in Edmonton Alberta -Congradulations to Ash for his Skyweight Title win! He has some tough challenges ahead, surely Psicopa and this mysterious Kuroi Tenshi having him in their sights. -The tag team situation is heating up! Two new teams signed this week plus increasing heat within the ranks should make for interesting weeks to come. This may be the strongest NEO's division has ever been. -On the rumor mill: Despite rumors, neither former tag champs Lights, Camera, Action nor former Skyweight Champion Ketiak are going to return -The Vandal Gomez/Ben Tuckwell stuff has been getting some rave reviews. It should be interesting to see where it goes. -Also impressing... Cole Druggan. The former RCW standout is certainly making a name for himself in the NEO ranks. Look for big things between himself. Also surprising is the feud between Kristoff St. Livingstone and George Lovecraft. It is indeed heating up nicely. ======================= "THE SHOW" RESULTS ======================= -TSTL Match: "Kid Lightning" Delvin Branigan d. Brian Taylor -TSTL Match: Sid Starr d. Lord Archibald Dunscaith =============== WAIVER WIRE =============== RANKINGS AVAILABLE ON THE NEO WEBSITE COMING ------------- SINGLES: Numbers, BRAT Dragon Mitsu TAG TEAMS: Pop Stars, World O' Hurt GOING ------------- None. ============== UNCIVIL WAR ============== NIGHT ONE From the Hammerstein Ballroom in New York City -The final 8 in the Sky's the Limit Tournament will come to a head in a one night tournament -Other matches TBA NIGHT TWO From the Axis Ballroom in Boston -Matches TBA NIGHT THREE From the NEO Arena in Edmonton, Alberta LAST MAN STANDING! -Russ Brady vs Steve Dumars -Other Matches TBA Matches Announced thus far (day pending) - LAST MAN STANDING! Russ Brady vs Steve Dumars ================================ UPCOMING "THE SHOW" SCHEDULE ================================ NEO continues it trek towards "Uncivil" War as it comes to you from the River City, Winnipeg, Manitoba. NEO does an afternoon show... at Can West Field... outdoors! That alone should make this show _very_ interesting. THE SKY'S THE LIMIT tournament brings a whole ton of Hispanic spice as two more matches are lined up. In the first match EL CORAZON NEGRO shall take on "EL AVIADOR ALTO" TEMIDO! The second bracket has NEO talent taking on a well travelled and well known veteran as JAYSON GRAHAM will do battle with UWF's "THE ICONOCLAST" SEAN WATTS! Everyone loves the Skyweight Division... everyone! With a new champion, we shall crown a number one contender in... A GUANTLET MATCH! If you lose... you're gone. If you win... you stay. The last man standing gets his shot at Ash and his newly won Skyweight Championship. The contestants are impressive. "KICHIGAIJIMITA" KURIO TENSHI has made his intentions known but has to get through newcomer BRAT DRAGON MITSU, CAMERON HAWKINS, the impressive VIC MORRISON, HECTOR SANTO, and KRISTOFF ST. LIVINGSTONE! We shall also see a pair of debuts as the monster known only as NUMBERS takes on former GYeah wrestler "THE ICEHOUSE" LANCE OZBURN! The ALBERTA TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP is to be defended each show and this week champion "VICIOUS" VIC VALIANT will take on England's own "THE SHINING" GEORGE LOVECRAFT! BSCW vs NEO heats up as two new tags teams debut in NEO in an 8 MAN TAG! NEO's own THE POP STARS and WORLD O' HURT will take on BSCW's odd couple(s) COW AND CHICKEN and the biggest fans of Ben Tuckwell's headband... THE DREAM BOYS! "DEADLY" DEREK IRVIN won the PACIFIC TITLE in impressive fashion and makes his first defense against a man who cause him more then a little trouble last week, the Hellhero himself, "THE CORRUPTOR" DOUGLAS MCCRAY! And in what should be an awesome display the main event of The Show will be a TEN MAN ELIMINATION TAG MATCH! It's a big one as MATT SAUNDERS makes his return after Crimson Glory to team up with "TOP GUN" BEN TUCKWELL, COLE DRUGGAN and the NEO Tag Champions NIGHTLIFE! Those impressive five will be lined up against NEO Heavyweight Champion "THE CREAM OF THE CROP" MIKE WALDROP as he leads VANDAL GOMEZ, "CHAOTIC" CHRIS CHILDRESS and DEBONAIR into battle. This is one you DO NOT want to miss! PLUS... -Russ Brady will be there and he says someone is getting their ass kicked. I wouldn't argue with him. And if Brady is there can Dumars be far behind? Of course not foo'! -A bunch of new debuts... who's going to impress? -Matt Saunders has issues with Mike Waldrop and vice versa... that much is known. What isn't known is whether Hannibal Carver is going to be around to cause a bit more trouble. And the big question... will Matt Saunders even be there? **IMPORTANT** **DEADLINES** _ALL_ Oncards/Roleplays/Flashes and Strats are due on Sunday, September 15th. That is almost a week to get your stuff in, plenty of time. Send EVERYTHING in to [detail withheld] Mailing list to come soon. NEO Wrestling 2002
