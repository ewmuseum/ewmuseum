Saturday Special (Part 3 of 4)

16 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JJ: Now, as you know, NEO is expanding. As our budget grows, we might even add another show. So with that in mind, Mr. Storm is going to give another team a chance to call a match.
TS: Does that mean that I am going to have to stop taking shots at Storm? JJ: Keep taking all of the shots you want Teddy. So with that in mind, we go to Larry Mullins and Carl Wild. Take it away guys.
Wild: Thanks, Mr. Johnson. We have a great match ready to get underway here, Larry. What are your thoughts?
Mullins: We have a hungry young lion in Thomas Lawrence and a cagey veteran in Mike Flame. Both are technically sound but Mike Flame is much more experienced. I'll go with experience over youth in this one, Carl.
Wild: Let's go to the ring for the introductions..... ["Big Star" by Reel Big Fish pumps over the PA system. Green smoke feels the arena and the lights go out. Green lights then fill the arena. Suddenly, "Roman" Thomas Lawrence steps out from backstage with a smile on his face. The crowd erupts in MASSIVE boos, the majority of them giving the thumbs down signal.]
Mullins: WOW is the crowd letting him have it...... [Lawrence is bottomed out in full Roman attire. Everything from an iron breastplate to a toga and an iron helmet. Along his side, he carries a pilam, a Roman short sword. The crowd continues to boo... no surprise.]
JD: Ladies and Gentlemen, now coming to the ring. From Alpharetta, Georgia....weighing in at 230 pounds...... "ROMAN"......THOOOOOMMMMMMMAAAASSSSS LAAAAAAAAWREEEEENNNNCCCCEEEEE [Lawrence stares around at the crowd a bit, taunting and teasing them. Then, he begins to walk down the aisle. No interaction whatsoever on the way to the ring. As he gets to the ring, he removes his Roman attire, and reveals a pair of cream colored wrestling trunks underneath. He slides in the ring.]
Wild: This crowd truly hates this man. Mullins: No kidding, Carl. Can you blame them after this guy's comments of late? Wild: Indeed. [The arena lights dim as "Baba O' Riley" by The Who begins to play. The crowd erupts, as the instantly recognizable music fills their ears. Around 40 seconds of atmospheric synthesizer music plays, until the song picks up. The beat plays loudly until the lyrics start.]
"Out here in the fields, I fight for my meals. I get my back into my living!" [The crowd begins to cheer even louder as 'The Living Legend' Mike Flame steps out from behind the curtain, a bright white spotlight shining upon him.] "I don't need to fight, to prove I'm right. I don't need to be forgiven!" [ Flame remains in the entranceway soaking in the arena's atmosphere, whilst the song begins to slow down.]
"Don't cry, don't raise your eye. It's only a teenage wasteland!" JD: .....and his opponent. From London, England. Weighing in at 238 pounds, "The Living Legend"......... MIKE.........
FLAAAAAAAAAAMMMMMMMMMME...... Wild: OH MY what a reaction. Mullins: You know, Carl. I've been watching Mike Flame since I was a kid, and the responses he gets when he steps into an arena still amaze me.
Wild: he truly is a living legend, the elder statesman of the sport, if you will. Mullins: "If you will"? You been watching that Schiavone guy again, Carl? Wild: hey. At least I know the difference between a woman and a yak. Mullins: Two words, Carl. Froot Booty. [Wild gives Mullins a nasty look] Mullins: What? Wild: [sighs] [As the song begins to up the tempo once more, Mike starts to make his way to the ring, arms raised. ]
"So Sally take my hand, we'll travel south 'cross land. Put out the fire, and don't look past my shoulder. The exodus is here, but I'll be walked on here. Let's get together, before we get much older. We're all WASTED!!!!"
[ As the songs lyrics stop, but the tune continues, Flame slowly walks up the ring steps, wipes his feet on the apron and steps through the middle rope. Once in the ring, he signals to the crowd once again, runs against the ropes, and jumps up into the air. Flame is then joined by the crowd in his familiar cry of
"YYYYYYYYYOOOOOOOWWWWWWWWW!!!!!!" ] Wild: And the match is on as Thomas Lawrence attacks Flame from behind with a shot to that bad left leg that brings Flame to a knee.
Lawrence continues the assault with some well-placed boots to the ribs and sternum. A vicious onslaught to begin the match. Flame appears to be in some pain.
Mullins: Ya think? he better get the hell outta there and regroup.... Wild: It appears he agrees with you. Flame rolls out of the ring for a breather but it appears that the Roman will have none of it as he springboards over the top rope and right on top of the veteran. That seems to have taken a bit out of both men but it appears Lawrence got the worst of it.
Mullins: Well yeah. Flame tossed him into the guardrail on the way to the floor. He's dangerous out there.
Wild: The referee has begun counting both men out but mike Flame has reached his feet and slid into the ring and is consorting with the ref, breaking the count.
Mullins: What an idiot! He shoulda let the ref count Lawrence out. Wild: I don't think he would want the cheap victory, Larry. [From the entrance way, a spotlight focuses on Elijah Flynt, who is heading down to the ringside area. He's smoking a cigarette and has a chair in his hand.]
Mullins: A win's a win. Hey, what is Flynt doing coming out here?? Wild: I'm sure it has to do with his beef with Mike Flame. Flynt prides himself on his British heritage. Maybe he thinks Flame gives it a bad name.
Lawrence back to his feet, trying to slip back into the ring unnoticed but
Flame will have none of it and grabs him by the hair, dragging him back into the ring. Flame with a chop....
[WHOOOOOOOO] and another...... [WHOOOOOOOOOOOO] AND ANOTHER....... [WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO] Wild: He whips Lawrence to the ropes and catches him with a beautiful floatover butterfly suplex. he covers....
ONE.... KICKOUT Mullins: It ain't gonna be that easy..... Wild: Flame gets Lawrence back up to a vertical base by the hair, attempt to hook him in what looks like a vertical suplex but Lawrence is blocking. Once again and Lawrence blocks again then follows with a
greco-roman poke to the eyes. Mullins: Ah yes, a classic grappling hold in the tradition of the Flairs, Harts, Mulligans and of course Flames..... Wild: Of course. Lawrence is all over Flame with some closed fists. The ref is giving a warning but I don't think the Roman cares. Irish whip to the ropes and Flame is caught in a sleeper hold in the center of the ring. Flame is flailing about looking for the ropes, but no dice. What's this? Lawrence lifts him back and he slams Flame down with a uranage.
Mullins: Some impressive power from Lawrence there. That had to hurt.... [The camera cuts over to show Elijah Flynt sitting in the corner shaking his head in disgust at Flame. Flynt blows out a couple of smoke rings while the scene cuts back to the ring.]
Wild: Indeed....Lawrence back to working on that bad knee with some stomps. He flips the veteran over, grabs a hold of the left leg, lifts and slams the knee into the canvas HARD. Then drops the point of the elbow onto the back of that knee. Lawrence's gameplan seems to be coming to light.....
Mullins: Well duh Carl. It's pretty common knowledge that old knee is pretty banged-up. It's gotta be killing him at this point.....
Wild: Well if it wasn't before, it certainly is now as Lawrence settles down into an ankle lock submission hold. but he's too close to the ropes. Mike Flame grabs a hold of the bottom rope and the referee gets to the count of 4 before Lawrence lets go. But he immediately starts stomping on that knee again. Looks like the Ref will have none of that though as he pushes Lawrence back, allowing
Flame a little breather in the corner.
Mullins: Not a great place to be..... Wild: You're right Larry, as Lawrence has pushed the ref aside and charges the corner......and finds nothing but a shoulderful of ringpost. Flame getting out of the way just in time, sporting a limp now but still mobile. Flame with a schoolboy....
ONE..... TWO.......... KICKOUT Wild: Now Flame with some offense with some heavy fists to the forehead of Lawrence. Back to his feet and a standing legdrop on
Lawrence. COVER ONE.... TW....KICKOUT [Elijah Flynt, still watching from the outside, pounds his fist into his leg, breaking the cigarette that he was about to light.]
Wild: Flame with some more stomps to the chest of Lawrence. He seems to be a bit out of it at the moment. Flame goes up top. Signals to the fans.....but wait. Lawrence is to his feet and takes the feet out from under Mike Flame.
Mullins: I get the feeling he won't be singing the Bass part in any musicals anytime soon. Wild: oy....Lawrence up to the top and cinches his arms around Flame's torso. Flame with some back elbows but Lawrence isn't letting go.....He hoists Flame into a release German suplex!!!!
Mullins: He's dead. He's gotta be.... Wild: Mike Flame just launched halfway across the ring. Lawrence with the cover.... ONE...... TWO.......... THR.............SHOULDER UP!!!! ANOTHER COVER!!!! ONE....... TWO!!!!!!! SHOULDER UP!!!!! Mullins: Looks like Lawrence is getting a little frustrated there. Wild: indeed, arguing with the ref, perhaps complaining about a slow count. Flame back up slowly.....backslide on Lawrence....
ONE..... TW.....KICKOUT!!!! Counter by Lawrence.... ONE..... Counter by Flame...... ONE...... TW......another counter...... ONE...... BREAK Wild: What a display of counterattacks!!!!! Both men on their feet and a very appreciative crowd giving their approval......
Mullins: Just like Florida up until a few days ago, this is TOO CLOSE TO CALL!!! Wild: You've been saving that one, haven't you? [Mullins with a sh*t-eating grin] Wild: Both men meet in the center of the ring in an old-fashioned test of strength, neither man giving an inch. Flame takes the advantage with a kick to the gut and some more Wild chops to the chest of Lawrence. Lawrence counters with some of his own. Now Flame.
Now Lawrence. Flame takes the advantage, whips Lawrence to the ropes and delivers a picture-perfect drop kick, knocking Lawrence against the ropes but Lawrence springs back and delivers a vicious-looking lariat. Lawrence with a standing Senton into a springboard moonsault.
COVER ONE...... TWO!!!!!! THRE.......NO!!!!! Wild: Roman Lawrence back to his feet, grabs Flames legs in a wishbone and delivers a stomp to Mike Flame's.......nether regions.
Mullins: *COUGH* BALLS *COUGH* Wild: You should get that cough looked at......Lawrence with some short elbows to the left knee of Mike Flame. And now locks in a
Sharpshooter. Mike Flame is in agony. The ref gives a check but Flame refuses to give. he's fairly close to the ropes.....can he make it???
Mullins: What kind of stupid question is that? OF COURSE HE CAN!!! Wild: The veteran Flame is reaching, ever closer to the ropes while Thomas Lawrence digs in, doing his level best to stop him, but it isn't enough!!! The ref calls for the break.....and gets it at the count of 4 and a half.
Mullins: It looks like Lawrence is about ready to end this.... Wild: Indeed, Thomas Lawrence is scaling the turnbuckles looking for The Conquering. He launches himself from the top.....and finds nothing but canvas. Flame on his feet but hobbling a bit. Rips off his elbow pad and delivers an elbow to the head of Lawrence. And another. And another.....and he's....he's....
Mullins[shocked]: SAY IT!!!! SAY IT!!!!!!!! Wild: ......getting funky like a monkey..... Mullins: BAHhahahahahahaha.... IF YEW WHEEEEELLLLLLL....... Wild: *sigh*......and drops Lawrence. With one more big elbow to the forehead. The crowd is simply eating this up.......Flame lifts
Lawrence into position.....and hits a piledriver but it appears that his knee may have given out......Surely this must be it.
Mullins: He sure looks like he's getting up to me.....and PUTTING ON THE STF!!!!! [Crowd pops huge] Wild: But he's awfully close to the ropes and it looks like Flame is in as much pain as Lawrence at this point. Lawrence finds the bottom rope and Flame breaks cleanly.
Mullins: MISTAKE!!! Wild: It may well be as Mike Flame is clutching his knee on the mat. Lawrence goes right back to it with a spinning toehold, wrenching the knee. And now driving it into the canvas. Lawrence drags Flame back to his feet, even though he can barely stand now. Lawrence drags
Flame to the corner and delivers a devastating Tornado DDT. He's putting him into position......goes to the top!!!
Mullins: THE CONQUERING!!!!!! RIGHT ACROSS HIS THROAT!!!! Wild: the cover..... ONE........ TWO......!!!!!!! THREE!!!!!!!!!!!!! Mullins: WHAT A FIGHT!!!!!!! JD: Your winner at 21:37, "Roman" Thomas LAAAAAAWWWWWWWWRENNNNNNCE........ [Flynt throws his hands up in disgust and glares at Mike Flame. Elijah kicks his chair into the ring apron in disgust and heads back to the back.]
Wild: What an incredible performance by both athletes. Like him or hate him, this Thomas Lawrence looks to give anyone in NEO a run for their money. That's all from here. For Larry Mullins, I'm Carl Wild. Back to Johnny and Teddy.
JJ: Good job guys. Really professional. TS: BLAH BLAH BLAH. I'm just glad that I didn't have to talk about Flame's match. JJ: PUT THE CAMERA BACK IN THE RING!!! Mike Flame is down!!! Mike Flame is down!!! TS: RAY OWENS AND LAWRENCE ARE DOING A NUMBER ON HIM. JJ: Owens slipped through the crowd as the camera cut back to us and now he and Roman are doing a number on him. Elijah Flynt is still walking down the aisle, but the music is blaring I don't think he knows.
TS: I don't think he cares. If he was a friend of Flame's, he would have helped Flame win the match.
[Crowd Pops as someone comes running down the aisle.] JJ: Who is that?? TS: If you looked in your mailbox, you would have gotten the picture and known that that was Matthew Reason.
JJ: I saw that picture. He had an eyepatch and a beard-- why you- TS: I was bored. JJ: Matthew Reason grabs the chair that Elijah Flynt brought out earlier and slides into the ring. Look at Owens and Roman scatter like cockroaches when you turn on the light.
TS: You would know all about that. JJ: Owens and Lawrence are headed back out of here. Here comes Rebecca,
Owen's daughter. She grabs Roman's and her dad's hands and raises them in victory.
TS: Do you see?? They are forming a stable. It's all over for my enemies now. Maybe I can be their manager.
JJ: No that aren't. Roman is not the team forming kind of guy. Let's go to a break and see if we can clear some of this up.
[Before we go to commercial, you see Elijah Flynt heading out of the locker room area, going out towards the ring. In the background, Pierre de la
Bergerac snickers as he slinks towards the door that Elijah Flynt just left.]
