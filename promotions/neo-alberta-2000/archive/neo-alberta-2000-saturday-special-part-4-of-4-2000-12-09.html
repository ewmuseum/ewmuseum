Saturday Special (Part 4 of 4)

9 December 2000

[My Summer by the Deftones hits to a rousing ovation.] JJ: "The fans here in NEO are sure great. They are giving the respect to
Shannon Cannon that he rightfully deserves."
[The man in question, the massive Shannon Cannon, makes his way out from the entrance curtain. He is a very muscular caucasian man with short spiked blond hair. He is wearing a pair of black wrestling shorts of a shiny marterial along with black boots and knee pads. He makes his way to the ring, climbing up on to a turnbuckle and giving the crowd the 'rock on' salute.] TS: "Bah, they know nothing. Until they cheer for the men who deserve it, Ray Owens for instance, I will not trust them." [Angus Young brings the opening strains of AC/DC's "You Shook Me All Night
Long (live)" to life to a good heel sized pop.]
TS: "Or like this man." [Pegasus emerges from the entrance portal. His hair tied back into a loose ponytail. His ring attire consists of bi-colored, long tights. The outer and lower portion of his legs are a dark pink. The The inner thigh and upper portion of the trunks are a gold color. Across his rear is a dark pink winged-horse. He's also sporting dark pink boots with the back portion of the boot being gold and the colors divided by a black line. Black brace-like elbowpads with the elbow portion being a dark pink. He strides to the ring with confidence and a smirk crosses his face. He climbs the steps to the ring apron and then steps in over the top rope. He looks out to the crowd and strikes a bicep flex.]
JJ: "Well, let's see what Jack Daniels has to say about them." JD: "Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall. First, to my left, he hails from FAR EAST WRESTLING! He weighs in tonight at 295lbs and standing 6'5" here is SHHHAAANNNNONN CANNNNNONN!!!!!"
[The crowd gives him a great cheer.] JD: "And his opponent, to my right. He hails from Memphis Tennessee...." [HEEL POP for the mention of Memphis(only the old timers will get this one!)] JD: "Standing 6'10" and weighing in at 348 lbs, here is PEEEEGGASSSUSSSSSSS!!!" [JD leaves the ring as the ref checks the two for hidden weapons. that done he calls for the bell.]
DING DING DING JJ: "Here we go. For the second match in a row Cannon is facing a man bigger than him but once again he is not showing any fear."
TS: "That's cause he is too blind to see who he is fighting. Pegasus has one of the shortest fuses in NEO. Cannon Shannon is one dead man."
JJ: "First of all that is Shannon Cannon. Second of all he can hold his own." [The two big men go to lock up but Peg stops him with a lifting knee and begins unmercilessly pounding huge forearms into Cannon's upperback. He whips him at the ropes and wipes him right out with a big boot to the face.] JJ: "WHAT IMPACT! Cannon is going to have tread marks on his forehead after that one!" TS: "And now he is dribbling his head like a soccerball. This is great!" JJ: "Pegasus is stomping away without mercy with those big boots of his. Cannon is trying to cover up and rolls to the outside. That was smart thinking by Cannon. He had to get out of that punishing beating and got out as fast as he could."
TS: "Smart thinking...Cannon....they go together? Never even thought of that before. then again it could just be a myth like the Lochness Monster and Bigfoot and geometry."
JJ: "Geometry? GEOMETRY?! How about I just ignore that and go back to calling the match." TS: "Good idea." JJ: "Pegasus reaching over the ropes and grabs Cannon by the hair, pulling him up onto the ring apron. He winds up for a big punch but Cannon blocks! He grabs Peg and pulls his head against the ropes! Peg SNAPS back, Cannon into the ring now and picking Peg right to his feet, whipping him into a corner with FORCE!"
TS: "Peg is just resting. It is not like this guy can hurt him anyhow. Trust me, Peg's best is yet to come." JJ: "Cannon whipping away with big right hands and Peg looks stunned. Another Irish whip across the ring and Peg hits so hard he stumbles out....POWERSLAM BY CANNON! He goes for the quick pin..." ONE.................... TWO................... TH...KICKOUT! [BIG POP for the slam] TS: "Lucky move by Cannon. Peg won't let that happen willingly though. He is just resting, I know he is."
JJ: "Lucky move or not Cannon has him on the run and picks him to his feet again...Belly to back suplex takes him right back down with force. Cannon off the ropes and spikes a sharp elbow into the chest. He is right up and gives hi another and goes for the pin."
ONE.................. TWO................. KICKOUT! Peg kicked out easily with that one." TS: "Cannon just cannot finish him off. He doesn't have a clue what to do with him but pick him up to his feet and whack him with a powermove."
JJ: "Shannon is doing just that now and whips him at the ropes, Peg off, clothesline ducked and Peg turns around ....LARIAT! Cannon floored and Peg stumbles off to recover from the stiff beating he just got."
TS: "Stiff? Nah, that lariat was stiff." JJ: "Stiff or not Peg is back on the offensive and attacking Cannon with kicks to the back. He is up to his feet and Peg grabs him from behind...BACKDROP DRIVER! Cannon lands right on his neck and it looks like his neck is hurt!"
TS: "all part of the master plan of course and Peg has him right up to his feet. A body slam and Cannon is right back down. Peg keeping it going though and drops a knee right into the upperback."
JJ: " Peg picking him right up again. Cannon is clutching at his neck...Peg grasping him around the waist and lifts him for a tombstone.
Cannon trying to fight out of it....SQUARE DRIVER!! Sitout tombstone and
Peg goes for the pin."
ONE.................... TWO................... THRE..KICKOUT! [BIG POP] TS: "I can't believe these fans are happy he kicked out. Don't they have any taste?" JJ: "Peg has really been focusing on the neck. It is obviously for his Spinecrusher finishing move. Peg picking him up again and whips him at the ropes. Lariat! CANNON DUCKS!! Off the opposite ropes goes Cannon ...FLYING SHOULDER BLOCK!! Peg to the ground hard! [HUGE CHEER from the crowd!] JJ: "Cannon on FIRE! He is right up to his feet and takes Pegasus right over with a spinning German suplex! He goes for another pin! ONE.................. TWO................. THRE...KICKOUT! [HEEL POP] TS: "This is getting ridiculous. This juice monkey thinks he has an actual chance! Peg is going to wipe him right out of NEO!"
JJ: "Cannon is on a roll, it might not be that easy. He has Peg up and whips him at the ropes....JUSTIFIABLE HOMICIDE LARIAT!!!! NO!!!!!!! [MONSTER POP] JJ: "PEG DUCKS! He turns.....JACKKNIFE POWERBOMB!! CANNON IS DOWN AND OUT!!" TS: "And Peg gets right on top of him with his Spinecrusher camel clutch." JJ: "The ref right there to check on Cannon.........and call for the bell!" [The crowd starts booing.] DING DING DING JD: "YOUR WINNER.........PEEEEEGAAAAASSSSSUSSSS!!!!" ['You Shook Me All night Long' hits as Peg gets up with a triumphant sneer and walks over the ropes, to the floor and out of the arena as the crow boos him.]
[HEEL POP] JJ: "Cannon had him. He had this match won but somehow Peg managed to get off a big time powerbomb and get him in the Camel Clutch. Cannon looks hurt though. He is clutching at his neck and the trainers are in taking care of him. Pegasus is the biggest guy in NEO and showed his power here." TS: "Yep and if he isn't in that four way match at WHFO there is going to be even more trouble."

JJ: Why is he coming over here? TS: Probably to give me my backstage pass for his next concert. [Farley stops by the announcer's table and leaves his guitar with Teddy
Smith to make sure nothing happens to it.]
TS: "Hey look at this Johnny, Dude has got himself a hit list....Muldoon's been hit twice, I hope I see Mike's name on this!"
[To the ring, Dude Farley wears a pair of ripped and faded jeans, a black tank-top with Mettalica written across the front in a golden color and a pair of dirty, worn-out converse sneakers.]
JJ: Let's see if "The Living Legend" Mike Flame is in just as festive of a mood. [The arena lights dim as "Baba O' Riley" by The Who begins to play. The crowd erupts, as the instantly recognizable music fills their ears. Around
40 seconds of atmospheric synthesizer music plays, until the song picks up.
The beat plays loudly until the lyrics start.] "Out here in the fields, I fight for my meals.I get my back into my living!" [The crowd begins to cheer even louder as 'The Living Legend' Mike Flame steps out from behind the curtain, a bright white spotlight shining upon him.]
"I don't need to fight, to prove I'm right.I don't need to be forgiven!" [ Flame remains in the entranceway soaking in the arena's atmosphere, whilst the song begins to slow down.]
"Don't cry, don't raise your eye.It's only a teenage wasteland!" [ As the song begins to up the tempo once more, Mike starts to make his way to the ring, arms raised.]
"So Sally take my hand, we'll travel south 'cross land.Put out the fire, and don't look past my shoulder. The exodus is here, but I'll be walked on ,here.Let's get together, before we get much older. We're all WASTED!!!!"
[ As the songs lyrics stop, but the tune continues, Flame slowly walks up the ring steps, wipes his feet on the apron and tries to enter the ring, but
Farley blocks his entrance.]
JJ: "Farley won't let Flame into the ring." [Flame, undeterred, walks down the apron and spreads the ring ropes to climb in, but Farley comes down there and blocks his entrance again.]
TS: "That's good gamesmanship by Dude. He stared Flame down all the way to the ring and now won't even let him in. Flame is beside himself."
JJ: "Flame is asking the ref to allow him passage into the ring." TS: "The ref is intervening on behalf of the only man in NEO who gets a senior citizen discount."
JJ: "He's not that old." TS: "He's not that young either." [Once in the ring, he signals to the crowd once again, runs against the ropes, and jumps up into the air. Flame is then joined by the crowd in his familiar cry of "YYYYYYYYYOOOOOOOWWWWWWWWW!!!!!!" As the crowd settle, Flame begins to warm up, stretching on the ropes and such.]
[Flame is a handsome man with a fairly thin face and a well-defined chin. Thin nose and mouth, and always clean-shaven. He has long, shoulder-length bleach-blonde, almost white hair. Flame has an average build, but at 49, age is starting to take its toll on his body. In the ring, Flame wears his world famous claret trunks, claret boots, and navy blue kneepads. Although he doesn't wear elbowpads, he always straps up with navy blue tape around his wrists. ]
JJ: "Let's go to good ole Jack Daniels and get this match underway!" JD: "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to NEO Wrestling MAIN EVENT!" [YAY CHEER!] JD: "First to my left, he hails from Lanchestor, England. Weighing tonight at 255 lbs and standing 6'6" here is DUUUUDEEEE FARRRLLLLEYYYYY!!!"
[The crowd let's out a big bad heel pop] JD: "And his opponent, standing to my right is from London, England. He weighs 238lbs and stands 6'1". He is a living legend. HE IS MIIIIIKE FFFFLAAAAMMMMEEEE!!!!!!" [HUGE POP] DING! DING! DING! JJ: I didn't think we would ever get started. Dude wouldn't let Flame into the ring. TS: Dude looks pretty intense tonight. He calls for a test of strength with Flame right off the bat.
JJ: Farley holds the advantage here. Obviously with the extra 5 inches in height. Flame asks the fans if he should do it.
[Crowd yells NO!!!] TS: Why would they care? Geez... JJ: Flame decides to do it anyway, not being one to back down from a challenge. They lock up on hand. Flame looks cautious about locking up that second one.
TS: I would be too. You never know where Farley's hands have been. JJ: Flame grabs the other hand of Farley. Kick to the gut by Farley. Flame drops to his knees. TS: You'd think in 30 years in the biz that he wouldn't fall for the second oldest trick in the book.
JJ: What's the oldest trick?? TS: Your momma. JJ: Don't get on my mom on this show. TS: I could hardly do that and maintain the PG-13 rating. JJ: That's enough! [Farley scoops Flame up and hits the ropes and lands with a big powerslam.] TS: Huge slam from Farley. JJ: He grabs a hand full of Flame's hair. Irish whip. Clothesline from
Farley! TS: HE ALMOST TOOK HIS HEAD OFF! [Heelish crowd pop for the big clothesline.] JJ: Farley... Snapmare takeover... Reverse chinlock applied. Does he know holds?? TS: There are so many brawlers here, I would almost have to agree. I guess some guys do know some simple holds. Whether or not this will payoff for
Farley though remains to be seen. He doesn't want to get into a technical battle with the Hermit.
JJ: Farley really has that thing locked on tight. And he has his left knee right between the shoulder blades of Mike Flame.
TS: Maybe he'll pull Flame's head right off his shoulders. [The crowd perks up as Mike Flame is fighting to get to his feet. They scream encouragement to him as he gets up to one knee.]
JJ: The crowd is really behind Mike Flame. TS: Of course they are. He sucks up to them just like Dumars does. Dude should just cut his losses and let go of the hold and go to his next offensive. JJ: Flame gets around to the side of Farley and slips free. Belly to back?? No!!! ATOMIC DROP!!!
TS: Farley will hit that high note next time he is in concert, I guarantee. JJ: Mike Flame whips Farley into the ropes. Side Russian leg sweep takes
Farley off of his feet. Flame with a single legged Boston crab. He's got it locked in tight.
TS: But it isn't in the center of the ring. Farley has the ropes. JJ: So close. TS: Not really. JJ: Flame picks Farley up. Eye gouge by Farley. TS: That will take the cream out of his streudel. JJ: Farley with a bulldog. Farley really is a man possessed tonight. TS: And Flame looks a little off of his game. Maybe Father Time is calling his name. JJ: Farley tosses Flame into the corner and follows him in with a knee to the gut. TS: This is a dangerous place for Flame, just where I like to see him. Farley holds a huge advantage in the corners. JJ: Farley with a right. Another right. A left. Another right. Flame takes some good shots and tries to get out of the corner but Farley cuts him off and shoves him back into the turnbuckles.
TS: Every week I saw it. Every week I hope it's true. Maybe this guy can finally end Flame's career.
JJ: And every week to this point you have been wrong. TS: But still I remain hopeful. Just like you do when you grab that last jelly donut from the dozen. You know the box is empty, but you still hope there is another one there.
JJ: Flame with a chop out of desperation. No crowd reaction. They know he is in trouble. TS: Farley's just laughing at him. Don't get too cocky. Just finish the job. You're just like the other ones.
JJ: Farley with a whip. No, reversal, no reversal again and Flame crashes back into the turnbuckles where he started out at.
TS: Flame ducks into the ropes. The referee steps in to prevent Farley from getting over there. Come on. There are no timeouts in wrestling.
JJ: Flame needs to regroup and climbs outside of the ring to do it. TS: Farley is standing in the center of the ring pointing at his head like he knows something that we don't. Is that possible??
JJ: Very doubtful. I think he just might be a few sandwiches short of a picnic, if you know what I mean.
TS: That loud music have disturbed his brain. JJ: Farley is coming to the outside after Flame. TS: Maybe that's what he wanted. Maybe Farley has more between his ears than you give him credit for.
JJ: ME? TS: Yes you. Why just yesterday I was saying how much improvement little
Teddy had shown in math since Dude started tutoring him.
JJ: No you weren't. You don't even have a kid. You can't even get a date. TS: Oh and you can Mr. Ladies Man. [While these two were bickering, Farley snuck around behind Flame and tried to clothesline him, but Flame felt him coming and threw an elbow that caught
Farley in the throat.]
JJ: Flame with a chance to do some damage outside of the ring here. He's setting up a folding table out here. We know Flame likes to deliver piledrivers through tables when the opportunity does arise.
TS: Flame should just try to regroup and get back into the ring. He is at the disadvantage out here.
JJ: Farley comes at Flame. Drop toe hold and Farley hits the table, but only knocks it over.
TS: Was Flame trying to redo that spot that Owens hit last week?? That was weak. Come up with your own highlight reel material and leave Owens' spots to him.
JJ: Flame has to do what he can. He's been overmatched, save for a few spots where he got to Farley.
TS: But Farley never comes out like this and I think it caught Flame a little off guard. JJ: Flame stays on the attack though, sending Farley tumbling into the ring steps. TS: I guess Flame knows when enough is enough. He's heading back into the ring. JJ: Farley is back to his feet!!! He must have taken some pain pills before he came out here. He has Flame by the tights.
TS: I think Flame thought that Farley thought that Flame thinks that Farley was down for the count when in reality Farley knew that Flame didn't think that you thought that he thinks that Flame thought that Farley could get up.
JJ: Huh? TS: Just as I thought. You can't hang with me. You aren't on my level. JJ: Farley is back on his feet. He grabs Flame by the back of his tights before he can climb back into the ring. Farley locks him up and Farley is going for a ride with a BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX THROUGH THE TABLE!!!!
[HUGE HEEL POP FOR FARLEY AS HE WASTES THE FAN FAVORITE!!!!] TS: That's how you destroy a table. JJ: That table is completely destroyed out here. TS: Both men are staggering from that blow. Farley hit his head in the process, but clearly, Flame took the worse shot on that exchange.
JJ: Farley is crawling from the wreckage with this creepy smile on his face. Are we seeing the darker side of Dude Farley??
TS: I would prefer to see the winning side of Dude Farley. The side that ends the self-proclaimed legends career.
JJ: Farley is climbing back into the ring. He looks like he is going to settle on the countout.
TS: No countout!!! Cripple him!!! JJ: Farley taunts the crowd inside the ring, laughing and mocking our Canadian fans. TS: Farley needs to finish the match. Here. Come get the guitar and use it. This isn't Muldoon you are fighting.
JJ: Good point Teddy. Farley has yet to be tested in the NEO ring. Maybe he is underestimating his opponent.
[Dude climbs up to the middle turnbuckle to really get into the fans when it looks like an idea hits him. Farley climbs up to the top turnbuckle and kind of jumps/falls from the top rope down onto the fallen Mike Flame.]
TS: Wholly pig's feet!!! Dude just went airborne!!!! [The crowd reacts at the move as if waiting for permission from the announcer's.] JJ: DUDE LANDS WITH A HUGE SPLASH ON FARLEY... I MEAN FLAME!!!! That caught me offgaurd. TS: Me too! Dude is pulling out all of the stops to finally do something that the others have fallen short on.
JJ: This match is just getting more and more unreal. TS: [chanting] Finish him! Finish him! Finish him! JJ: You should be more impartial. TS: And you should get a better hairpiece but I don't bust your balls about it. JJ: Farley is up to his feet. Sort of. He's pretty wobbly. He's trying to get Flame back into the ring so he can win the match.
TS: This thing is just about over. [As the ref administers the count, Farley gets Flame into the ring and immediately goes for the cover.]
ONE!!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! No!!! KICKOUT BY MIKE FLAME!!! HE'S ALIVE!!!!! [HUGE POP FROM THE CROWD.] JJ: Flame keeps the match going!!! TS: Damn you Farley. I thought you had him. JJ: Farley calls for his finisher. He gets Flame up to his feet. FLAME WITH A HEADBUTT TO THE NADS!!!! TS: A new version of a lowblow!!! Damn. I should have known Flame would pull something like that. And Tweedle Dee in the ring just lets him get away with it.
JJ: Farley is doubled over. Flame moves behind him and delivers a standing dropkick right to the posterior of Dude Farley sending Farley crashing to the outside on the floor.
TS: Flame does this every match. It's like he plays possum and then calls on his old man strength to bail him out at the end.
JJ: Flame crawls through the ropes and is waiting on the ring apron for
Farley to get to his feet. He does. Flame leaps off with a huge crossbody block!!! FARLEY CATCHES HIM AND DELIVERS THE CROWD SURF TO THE CONCRETE
FLOOR!!! [MEGAHEEL POP FOR FARLEY] TS: Look at Farley!!! He's like a madman out of control. He's totally lost it out there. JJ: He throws Flame back inside the ring. He's going to go for the cover now. Maybe Flame can get to his can of spinach and get out of this.
