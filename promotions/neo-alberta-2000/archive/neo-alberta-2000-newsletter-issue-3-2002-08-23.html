NEO WRESTLING INCORPORATED www.neo-wrestling.com NEWSLETTER ISSUE #3 August 23rd, 2002 CONTENTS -Notes from the desk of Wallace Storm -News and Rumors -Results from the last episode of "The Show" -Waiver Wire -NEO "The Show" Upcoming Lineup (with deadlines) -"Crimson Glory" Lineup ================== FROM THE DESK.... ================== A strong show, if not a bit on the large side. Anyone else notice that new feeling we have around NEO-land? Lots of new faces, new ideas... plus the good old NEO stuff. Out of character for a second, some VERY important notes.
1) There are no goddamn pyros or no titan tron... so backstage vignettes will NOT get pops. 2) ALL FRICKING multi part oncards must be passed by me FIRST! Next show I will not use any that are not. Period ================== NEWS AND RUMORS ================== -Word is Messiah has retired from the wrestling world. All the luck to him in the future. -Rumors are Steve Dumars and Russ Brady came as a package deal with both being in talks for some time. However... don't look for other returns. Storm isn't too keen on much of the 'old' talent -NEO is looking to add some talent to both it's tag and Skyweight Divisions. Look for new talent to debut over the next couple weeks. -Cole Druggan and George Lovecraft are impressing the front office more and more by the week with their professionalism and in ring skill. Look from big things from these two.
-Jayson Graham and Nightlife shall return from their trip to Japan in time for the show. -Many in the locker room are not said to be happy about the addition of the GWC/MPW talent in Slauenwhite and Floyd. All Storm says is give them time. -On the topic, look for some joint matches between the two companies both on "The Show" and BSCW's "Venom" ======================= CRIMSON GLORY RESULTS ======================= -Vic Morrison d. Seth Harker (TSTL match) -Cole Druggan d. Carson Oliver -Kuroi Tenshi d. Ray Mason -Hector Santo d. Blaise Boyer -Ewiak Brothers d. Livingstone/Lovecraft -Valiant d. Red Dragon to retain the TV title -DDI d. McCray/Gomez/Taylor to win the Pacific Title =============== WAIVER WIRE =============== RANKINGS AVAILABLE ON THE NEO WEBSITE COMING ------------- (not yet debuted) THE "ICEHOUSE" LANCE OZBURN- Cocky, arrogant, confident... the list could go on and on. He is a man who believes in himself and his abilities. He trusts himself and those abilities. He says he is confident... his opponents wish he would just shut up. Ozburn talks....alot. Smart and smooth. That's
Ozburn. "KICHIGAIJIMITA" KURIO TENSHI- Tenshi is Spikyjim's alter ego, in a completely non secret way - like Owen Hart and the Blue Blazer, or Mitsuhiro
Misawa and Tiger Mask. Whereas Spikyjim is an out and out garbage wrestling brawler, "Kichigaijimita" Kuroi Tenshi is an almost classical Japanese style wrestler. Some high flying is mixed in with a devastating technical approach to be the polar opposite of the man without the mask. However, Tenshi's personality makes the Ultimate Warrior look totally sane. He mixes in quasi mystical and religous imagery in his speeches, has an overly deliberate and scary approach in the ring, is obsessed with death.... and of course, he has his bizarre mask.
VICTOR V HILL- He is a stuck up asshole. He despises hardcore wrestling, brawling of any kind. Hill thinks that wrestling has gone to the birds. That its a bunch of hillbillies with weapons and no ability. He is tired of the violence and continues his personal vow to end it. Basically he is trying to make wrestling stale. Fans look at him like he is boring and old hat for wanting this. They dont much care for his "Im better than you" attitude either.
"CYBERPUNK" BILLY BROAD- Surly, inconsiderate, violent, selfish, anti-authority, smoker, drug abuser, alcohol abuser, liar, callous, impulsive, unreliable, charming, manipulative, NO remorse for anything he's done to anyone. Your basic garden-variety sociopath. Not without good qualities. Just none have surfaced on camera yet.
"MR. EXTREME" ANDY SLAUENWHITE- Slauenwhite is your cocky, arrogant heel, who most love to hate, but just when you think you hate him, he will do something to gain the respect of everyone around. He is dirty and underhanded, but loves the spotlight, and loves to have all the attention on him....good or bad
"NO MERCY" JEFF FLOYD- He believe he is a living legend. He is a calculating and intelligent wrestler. He is a technical threat who can brawl, and take to the air. He talks a good game, and backs it up. He shows no remorse for his opponents, and will win anyway he can.
RETURNING ---------------- Steve Dumars Russ Brady GOING ------------- Carson Oliver ================================ UPCOMING "THE SHOW" SCHEDULE ================================ Once again NEO takes to the roads, this time coming to you from Saskatchewan
Place in the lovely Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, Canada! Last show was packed and this one is no different with a huge lineup... both literally and figuratively!
THE SKY'S THE LIMIT tournament continues bringing us the first taste of some international non-NEO talent! The first bracket will see NEO's own BRIAN
TAYLOR take on Japan's "BURNING SUN" KAZUHIRO GOLD! The other bracket will see BRIAN DEEGAN of BSCW fame taking on relative unknown SID STARR!
NEO seems to be on a bit of a recruiting drive of late and six of it's newest recruits will do battle in... A SIX MAN TAG! One side will see a trio familiar to many wrestling fans... "MR. EXTREME" ANDY SLAUENWHITE will team with fellow G/M'er "NO MERCY" JEFF FLOYD and the well known VICTOR V.
HILL! These three bring some name value and star power but against them are three looking to prove themselves in "CYBER PUNK" BILLY BROAD, the man who took out Cole Druggan last week in "CHAOTIC" CHRIS CHILDRESS and newcomer and unknown 6'9", 280lber ERIC STORM!
"TOP GUN" BEN TUCKWELL returned last week, pouring his heart out to everyone watching, only to be interrupted by Vandal Gomez. This week he makes his in ring return as he takes on journeyman high flyer CAMERON HAWKINS!
The ALBERTA TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP is to be defended each show and this week champion "VICIOUS" VIC VALIANT will take on hot up and comer JAYSON
GRAHAM! The line of title matches continues as SKYWEIGHT CHAMPION PSICOPA will step into the ring against someone... well apparently the hide and seek game is over as the masked... and insane... man will take on the so called
Cruiserweight Icon... ASH! NIGHTLIFE put on a spectacular show at Crimson Glory defeating Debonair and winning the NEO TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIPS! This week they make their first defense against THE KINGS OF HARDCORE in what should be another grueling bout!
Two men were promised matches last week. One was promised a match... another made an open challenge... so why not combine them? LORD ARCHIBALD
DUNSCAITH did not make friends with NEO owner Wallace Storm last week. This week he will challenge the very man who made the open challenge, a man not exactly close to Storm... "CREAM OF THE CROP" MIKE WALDROP with the Rookie
Monster challenging for the NEO HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!
PLUS... How will Hannibal Carver and Vic Valiant react to last week's happenings? Will Carver show up and be in attendance, even if in the audience? Matt Saunders will make his return and has a lot on his mind, requesting mc time. What exactly does he have his mind on?
What, if anything, shall happen between Vandal Gomez and Ben Tuckwell this week? What will Debonair say about the Kings of Hardcore getting a match for the belts they nearly won?
And the new NEO Pacific Champion will be in the house! **IMPORTANT** **DEADLINES** _ALL_ Oncards/Roleplays/Flashes and Strats are due on Wednesday, Augsust 28th. That is almost a week to get your stuff in, plenty of time. Those of you with matches will know what they are before hand.
