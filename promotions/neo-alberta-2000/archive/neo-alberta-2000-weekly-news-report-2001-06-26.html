Weekly News Report June 26, 2001

26 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just thought we would try something different. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- NEO NEWS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The NEO/E-Pro Joint Tour has come to a premature end. Apparently, Wallace Storm was not pleased with the accomodations for NEO wrestlers and has regrouped his troops and gone back to Canada.... NEO announced that it would have its own supercard, Beach Party, on July 15 in place of the former Symphony of the Mat show.... NEO is still looking for 2 more tag teams to solidify a vastly improved division. Interested parties can visit their website: www.neo-wrestling.com =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- LAST WEEK'S RESULTS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- "Soundbyte" Sage Holden d. Boyer via Pinfall after the Cardiac Arrest Giant Arcola & Brayden d. Kids Incorporated via pinfall after the EndGame Colt Megusta d. Matt Saunders via DQ after Mike Waldrop interfered in the match. Steve Dumars d. Alexander Strakoi via Pinfall after the Dumars Drop =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- TALENT NEWS =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- CHAMPIONS Heavyweight- Steve Dumars Pacific- Matt Saunders Skyweight- Psicopa Tag Team- Monkey You WAIVER WIRE COMING- Singles- Logan Armstrong, Stephan Andrews, "Blood Lust" Xavier Feyr, "Showtime" Rick Marley GOING- Singles- Kid Diablo, Boyer, Thomas Lawrence HIATUS- Red Hot, Cracked and Crazy =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- UPCOMING SCHEDULE =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- *Please notice all changes and additions *All matches are subject to change ---------------------- July 1st, 2001 CANADA DAY! SUNDAY SPECIAL EDMONTON, ALBERTA, CANADA Northlands Agricom ---------------------- THREE WAY DANCE DEBUT! Logan Armstrong [vs] "Bloodlust" Xavier Feyr [vs] "Mr Showtime" Rick Marley -Three newcomers to NEO look to come out as the victor and in the spotlight. All are talented but the question remains--who wants it more? SKYWEIGHT TAG MATCH! Ketiak/Tah Murdah [vs] "Top Gun" Ben Tuckwell/Adam Cage -Ketiak made a stunning debut taking out Cage, Lawrence and Diablo in one night. Now Cage teams with long time Ketiak rival Ben Tuckwell to do battle against the Demi God and Allah's warrior Tah Murdah. INTENSITY! Johnny Homicide vs Matt Saunders -Homicide took out Davidson and took him out good. Saunders is showing us sides of him we never knew existed. Both men are intense and focused, stepping it up another notch. Will either be in the mind frame to do battle and what roles will Davidson and Waldrop play in this match? We will see Sunday. BIG MAN COLLISION! Johnny Spades [vs] Russ Brady -Two huge men, two huge attitudes and two huge bundles of talent. Brady finally does battle with someone his size. Their styles contrast greatly but you know this has to be good! TAG TEAM # 1 CONTENDERS MATCH! Lights, Camera, Action [vs] Victor Payne/Syn -Two tag teams on the verge of breaking through. The winner of this match does get a great reward...they get the first crack at the winner of FoC and Monkey You at Beach Party! MAIN EVENT! UP AND COMERS DO BATTLE!! "Brutal" Brandon Franklin [vs] Mike Waldrop -Waldrop is the fastest rising star in wrestling today, Franklin is on the brink of making is huge. Now for the first time, the two young stars will do battle in the main event. Who will prevail? Who else might get involved? Which star will rise and which will fall? =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- BEACH PARTY SUPERCARD =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Well with the NEO-J tour done we need our own PPV and baby we got one! Beach Party will take place on a beach right on the lovely Lake Louis in beautiful Banff Alberta. Hot weather, hot babes and hot action will make this one HELL of a show! Throw in the fact it is outside, beside a lake and there is a party going on and you have some great potential! Already signed for the show...... DUMPSTER MATCH! KIDS Incorporated [vs] Syn/Victor Payne NEO TAG TITLE MATCH! Monkey You(c) [vs] Forces of Chaos LAST MAN STANDING! Johnny Spades [vs] "Brutal" Brandon Franklin PACIFIC TITLE MATCH! Matt Saunders(c) [vs] Mike Waldrop NEO HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE WAR!! Steve Dumars(c) [vs] Messiah Of course you know there will be more to be announced in the upcoming weeks including who it will that will be facing Psicopa for the NEO Pacific Title. We are always looking for help too. If you are an experienced writer that can keep up to NEO standards let us know and get on board for what shall be an amazing show!
