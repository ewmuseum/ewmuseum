Yup...the king is back.

15 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yo, Yeah, you read that right, I decided to being an idiot and reopen NEO. However it simply is not me 'reopening' it, but rather me starting a new fed...just with the NEO name. Things are going to be very different this time around, different rules, different feel, different way of doing things. - No flash packet. Instead, ala KCW, everything will be on the show. Roleplays will still happen, but be used as oncards instead. Of course this means that they are preferably done in or around the arena...or they can be pre taped.
- Bi weekly. I can't put out a quality show on a weekly basis...almost no one can. So we'll go bi weekly or so. - On the fly, Impromptu feeling. NEO's shows schedule/line ups aren't going to be fully announced. Some will be yes, but I would like to have them set up with in the show. The bi weekly-ness will allow the handlers plenty of time to set things up. Besides, I will usually have the matches I want to see pre planned, I will simply contact handlers to get them together.
- Roster. We're keeping it smaller then before. That and not just anyone will be getting in. I am gonna be kinda picky as far as who I let in simply because I will only be looking for certain types of characters, ones that fit with what NEO needs. As well, because you were in NEO before, doesn't automatically mean you are again. Some will be for sure, the consistent ones, the ones who worked hard, the ones who cared.
Anyhow, no specific opening date is decided. I will wait to get a roster together before doing so. Here is the FAQ for all of ya'll.
**************************** NEO F.A.Q. 1. What is this thing? (In character) -NEO is the brain child of one Wallace Storm. A once time great fed, well followed all around the world, had fallen into hard times near the end of it's first run. Storm put it on 'hiatus' and we thought his once grand idea, slimming it down to the bare essentials. Back and better then ever, the former King of the Independents, is about to reclaim it's throne.
Storm purchased an old theater, converting it into the new NEO Arena. A smaller size and balconies give it an intimate feel, similar to Hammerstein
Ballroom or the Madhouse of Extreme. A show here is something special and something else. Loud fans in a small room make for some big time fun.
2. What is this thing? (out of character) -An e-fed, d'uh. Ran by me, [name withheld], for a year. I had to shut it down for real life reasons but I am reinvigorated and back with a vengeance...and stuff. Yeah, that's about it.
3. We got rules around this place? -Well of course we do, but they aren't that hard to follow. Have fun. Don't bitch and whine if you lose. Bring it up with me, I can explain things. Have fun. Work hard. The usual. I ain't gonna be too strict around here. You wanna have fun, that's why you're here...so have fun.
4. What makes NEO so different from everyone else? -Firstly and most importantly, NEO is an independent fed. None of the glitter and glamour of the big feds like RCW, EMWC and the like. No pyros, no video walls, more an edgy, true indy feel. This is about wrestling and great storylines, all the other stuff is simply icing...icing this cake
doesn't have. This is it...pretty much the bare essentials. However there are a few other things. There is no flash packet for NEO. Instead everything is ran on one show with flashes essentially being ran as oncards.
Little things like that to make NEO stand out. 5. No flash packet, only oncards? -That's right. In the fashion of KCW, oncards will be used to further angles. These will generally be backstage, sometimes even in the ring. Sometimes these will be pretaped or set outside the arena. This makes it less work for me to worry about and less for you. Plus it gives us that low budget, ECW-ish feel I so love.
6. And this show is called? Is it weekly? Are there any other shows? -The bi weekly show is called simply "The Show". It is held bi weekly, but never on the same night every week. Sometimes it is Sunday, others it is
Thursday or Monday. It all depends on when things get done. Yeah, we are taking it easy this time. On off weeks there may or may not be a review show with news and the like. This all depends on what I want to talk about, how much news there is, how much time I have, etc, etc. Supercards will be different and not held very often, only for special occasions. We don't have PPV after all. :)
7. So, what titles is NEO going to be having? The same as before? How often are they defended? -NEO will have a few titles. So let's see what they are. As far as defenses go, I am not setting any specific number, but let's just say often. 1) NEO Heavyweight Championship- The big one, the granddaddy of them all. This is _the_ top title in all the land. You have this, you be the top dog. Simple enough eh?
2) Pacific Championship- The Second tier title, kind of like ECW's Television Title. This can be held by just about anyone and often signifies star status and an ability to go to the next level.
3) Skyweight Championship- This is for the not so big boys. Restricted to under 215lbs, this is the title that gained NEO fame the first time and will provide the most exciting matches.
For now there will be no tag titles and the Television title is on the shelf. NEO will not be keeping a gigantic roster so a ton of titles are not needed.
8. So, I'm gonna bring in my big name guy, that alright? - Fine by me, although realize that this is an INDY fed. Why would your millionaire be here when he can be in his own company? Generally I would like you to have new characters or characters not as well known. However, like all indy feds, big names can be brought in from time to time to boost attendance. They may be brought in for one show, two...who knows. I am trying to capture that true indy/ECW feel, the roster should reflect that.
I don't have noithing against the Coutrades and Temples and Case's of the world but let's be realistic, they just wouldn't fit in a company this size, it wouldn't make a ton of sense....especially if they are still wrestling for the big e-feds. However, guys not in feds, coming out of retirement, etc, etc...that is cool.
9. How does a guy win around here? - There are several ways. Roleplaying of course is the big staple. Your not rping on the show for your match, odds are you're not going to win. Strats are _very_ important as well. I like to see them. You don't do strats, your opponent does, a good chance you'll lose. Consistency, angles,
heat...there are all sorts of contributing factors. You don't like that you lost? Fine, let me know and we can discuss it. I am easy to talk to. 10. Sounds good to me, but who the hell are you? - Me? I'm [name withheld], owner of the old and new NEO as well as handler of EMWC/BUWE's November and Rosco Riggins of RCW and WWA fame. You can get ahold of me at [detail withheld] or on AIM at [detail withheld] 11. So, where do I sign up? The website is at www.neo-wrestling.com .
