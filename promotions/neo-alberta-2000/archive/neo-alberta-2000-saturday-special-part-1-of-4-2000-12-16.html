Saturday Special (Part 1 of 4)

16 December 2000

(Camera goes to a shot of the parking-lot, where a very shiny stretch limo is stopped along the curb. The driver exits the vehicle, and proceeds to the back of the limo where he opens the door, letting Ray Owens exit, followed by his lovely daughter Rebecca.
Grabbing the luggage from the trunk, the driver hands it to Owens, as father and daughter make their way toward the entrance of the arena. A middle aged man rushes towards Owens and asks him for a brief interview. Owens casts an angered and somewhat flustered look at the man, who ignores the look and shoves the mic toward Owens who rips it from the man's hand.)
Interviewer: Mr. Owens, how about a few words for the curious audience out there tonight who are wondering what you have planned for tonight?!!
(Owens looks at the man and cracks a half grin.) Owens: Well buddy, it seems a lot of people are wondering the same thing. It seems a lot of people are wondering what The Hitman has in store for tonight, wondering who he's hit with the bat....my answer for that; I'm just gonna sit back in my room with my daughter and enjoy an evening watching the show.
Interviewer: Mr. Owens I find that extremely hard to believe since you're known to be one of if not the most hated and untrusted man in the NEO.
(Shivering from the brisk air, Owens prepares to answer.) Owens: Listen buddy, tonight is just a relaxing night for me. I hadn't planned on fighting, and I don't even have my gear.
Interviewer: Well then, can we get any words on Dumars? (Owens seems totally appalled that the interviewer even brought up that foul name in his presence.)
Owens: Listen man, forget about Dumars. I've got some things I'd like to say to him, but I've got my daughter here and I really don't wanna shout vulgarities right now.
Interviewer: Um, Ray.....What are your thoughts on your chances of being in the main event at When Hell
Freezes Over? Owens: I'd rather not concentrate on that at the moment. Right now all I need to worry about is giving
110 percent in my matches, and doing the best I can to win. Rebecca: Dad, who's that? (Rebecca is looking toward the direction of a young man clad in a roman style outfit. Owens immediately recognizes the man as 'Roman' Thomas Lawrence and quickly responds.)
Owens: That's some pretty boy name Thomas. Rebecca: Can I meet him? (Owens shivers. Not from the cold.......) Owens: You don't want any part of that honey. Interviewer: If I may, I could show you around Rebecca, if it's alright with Ray of course. Rebecca: Could I dad?!!! Please?!! (A bit taken back and unsure, Owens just nods towards his daughter.) Interviewer: Why don't you go right inside those doors over there Rebecca, I'll be there shortly.
Rebecca: Ok sir, thanks! Dad, I'll meet you back in the room in about 10 minutes ok? Owens: Sure honey, have fun. (Rebecca hurries indoors, as Owens grabs the interviewers shirt and pulls him close.) Owens: Anything happens to her, I'll find you man, trust me I will. Interviewer: Gotcha sir, I promise to watch her close! (As that is said, 'Roman' Thomas Lawrence walks by, slightly bumping shoulders with Ray.) Owens: You mind buddy?!! (Lawrence just keeps on walking forward until he enters the arena and disappears out of sight.)
Interviewer: I'm gonna let you go inside Ray, so have a good night and enjoy yourself. Owens: Thanks, I'll try. (Owens hurries indoors as the scene fades to black.) ['Quiet Storm' by Mobb Deep hits to a GREAT reaction from the near two hundred loyal fans in Salmon Arm, British Columbia.]
JJ: "What a way to start off the show." TS: "I knew I should have been late today." [A line of dark clad and very beefy security guards stream from the curtain, lining up down the aisle. No sooner are they done this then the owner of NEO Wrestling, Wallace Storm, walks out with a mic in hand. He pauses at the entrance with a big smile and looks around the arena before continuing to the ring. ] [Storm is dressed in dark tan colored pants with a dark blue turtleneck sweater and stylish gold rimmed glasses. Although somewhat young looking, with neatly groomed dark hair and a clean shaven face, Storm is no stranger to the business. His whole life has been spent in and around it. This is his passion, he is living his dream.] [He waits for the crowd buzz to quirt down before raising the cordless mic to his mouth.] WS: "Ladies and gentlemen, this day shall go down in the annals of NEO history as a great day. Today a foe of NEO, a plague upon NEO has been
vanquished. That is right folks....G-DIGGA IS FIRED!!!!" [MONSTER HUGE STANDING OVATION POP] {Storm motion for them to settle down and go back to their seats.] WS: "Please, take your seats and let me continue. because he a pro wrestler, he could tell me what to do. My friends, nothing is farther from the truth." [Storm rubs his hands in joy.] WS: "Did you not learn after seeing what I did to Bulldozer? He had the same piss-poor attitude that you have. Now he is going to have company in
the soup line. This should go out as warning to anyone who thinks they can tell me what to do with my company. I do not bow down to threats or intimidation. I wouldn't be where I am if I did."
JJ: "I will give him that." WS: "You see, no one....NO ONE....waltzes into my office saying they want a title shot. You have to earn it. Jakeal....he couldn't hack losing. You see he comes from companies that push those who are friends. He never won anything fairly ever in his life. I on the other hand was not willing to give that to him. I will not give that to anyone. You must earn it. You earn the respect you deserve in this here ring. I don't care where you are from. I don't care what you have done. When you come into NEO you start exactly where everyone else does and you work your way up."
[The fans cheer in approval, even the commentators are nodding in agreement.] WS: "These fans are not idiots. They are the greatest fans in the world. They make or break you...pretty damn simple ain't it?" TS: "WHAT? WHAT A RIP!"
