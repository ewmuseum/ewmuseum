Crimson Glory Lineup

16 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LINEUP FOR CRIMSON GLORY ============================= CRIMSON GLORY will begin, as many supercards do, with a special preshow. Competitors will get a chance to say some final words about their matches. As well we will get to see a match...to whet the appetite so to speak...not to mention a surprise or two!
In the first match, two men who had a confrontation on the last 'Show' will meet face to face as the high flyer HECTOR SANTO will take on another newcomer in "BLACK DEATH" CARSON OLIVER!
The actual show will have seven matches culiminating in the main event simply titled CRIMSON GLORY. Not in the earlier Rumble, but expected to appear is VANDAL GOMEZ. There isn't a chance in hell he is leaving the arena and not doing _something_!
Opening the show is what should be a solid workrate-y contest between two men with quite the hate on for one another. BRIAN TAYLOR, egotistical as they come, has had his problems with "THE APOSTLE" WES COPELAND as late.
These two talented up and comers are sure to put on a show! tthe injured KINGS OF HARDCORE will look for revenge as they take on ACID
EGO TRIP in...take a breath...A BUNKHOUSE BRAWL TORNADO LOSER LEAVES TOWN
TAG TEAM MATCH! This match was supposed to be on the pre show but with who's involved and the sheer magnitude it just had to be moved up! It'll be a ROYAL RUMBLE and it will be for the now vacant ALBERTA TELEVISION TITLE! We all now know that "VICIOUS" VIC VALIANT is the first entrant. He is going to have one hell of a tough road as he faces a myriad of talent. SOUP BONE SAMSON,
JAYSON GRAHAM, VIC MORRISON, "WILD THING" KEVIN SLATER, the man who defeated the Skyweight Champion in RED DRAGON, "THE EXPERT" MATT ALLEN, his arch rival "THE CORRUPTOR" DOUGLAS MCCRAY, newcomers COLE DRUGGAN and "THE
SHINING" GEORGE LOVECRAFT, Brat Pack members "BAD AGENT" JOHNNY SPECTOR and
"THE SMOOTH OPERATOR" JOHNNY SPECTOR, both members of the Panty Thievez in
BRIAN DELANEY and STEVE COLES, debutee KRISTOFF ST. LIVINGSTONE, and finally
"SADISTIC" SHANE ALEXANDER. Plus...word is there will be unnamed men making their debut or maybe even their returns in this very match! Valiant sure has his hands full!
This one has "show stealer" written all over it. The two have traded attacks throughout the last couple months. "DEADLY" DEREK IRVIN went as far as to break the arm of ASH! At Crimson Glory the two will meet and see who will be the one to say give up in an I QUIT MATCH!
People love work rate, people love blood but some people just love spotfests and this here, kids, promises to be one! The NEO SKYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP will be up for grabs as reigning champion PSICOPA takes on a man who has recently become quite the thorn in his side in DAVID WILLIAMS...in a LADDER
MATCH! It started with eight, now down to two and those two will meet to determine the _new_ NEO TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS as surprise NIGHTLIFE takes on the team everyone knew would make the finals in DEBONAIR! This is another show stealer in the making!
Not every title is up for grabs but sometimes the most heated of feuds isn't about championships and gold. This one proves it more then any. HANNIBAL
CARVER has tormented Rose, the girlfriend of MATT SAUNDERS, not to mention destroying him on several occasions. Doing what none would expect Carver dumped Rose off the balcony, nearly killing both her and Matt Saunders in the process. The challenge has been made and it will happen...in NEO's first ever CAGE MATCH!
They have promised it to be the bloodiest match in NEO history, no small task indeed. The show has been named CRIMSON GLORY for a reason and indeed it shall be as MESSIAH puts his NEO HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP on the line against long time nemesis "CREAM OF THE CROP" MIKE WALDROP, which should be their last match...perhaps in their career! Barbwire... explosives... light tubes... thumbtacks... nails... panes of glass... This one will not be for the faint of heart!
