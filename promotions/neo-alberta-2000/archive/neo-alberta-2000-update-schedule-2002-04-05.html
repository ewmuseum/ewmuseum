NOTES FROM THE OFFICE =========================== Rebirth, simply put, is the biggest show to date in NEO history, size and quality wise. There were a couple definite MOTY contenders and the main event has been getting some awesome reviews. For that I thank all of you.
I am still looking for feedback so be sure to visit the forums on our snazzy website and do so. Let me know what you liked, didn't, etc. You know the drill.
Of course all the usual post Supercards stuff is happening, some major roster changes, the ignition of our tag team division..a lot going on fo shizzny. Of course I can always use help writing, just let me know.
Anyhow...you know what's going on, so do I. I'm a happy camper, I hope you guys liked Rebirth as much as I!
Two more things before we get on with the important stuff. 1) EVERYONE needs to get onto the mailing list. I eventually only want to send through topica only...and eventually through a NEO server. If you ARE
NOT on the list, send a blank email to
[detail withheld] Just follow the instructions you get from there. 2) With the roster being fairly busy and transitional, not to mention a number of people wanting in, I am not going to let people be lax. You got around two weeks now till next show, around a week till deadlines. You get nothing in...you could be gone, especially those who haven't participated for a couple weeks now. No exceptions.
2. GENERAL NOTES ABOUT ONCARDS/STRATS/ETC =============================================== -The first rule in Fight Club is to....oh, wait, wrong show. Remember kids, no flash packets this time around. Instead everything is on the show. You still do flashes, they just get put as vignettes, backstage oncards, etc. Generally I prefer the flashes to be present time backstage oncards,
in rings, joint angle development oncards, etc, etc. You can do pre-taped stuff, let's not go nuts with it though, okay? If you have any questions, simply ask. As well, strats are very important. They convey how your character is to be portrayed in the ring, a big factor in _MY_ NEO. Much of
NEO is about the actual wrestling aspect. For those of you who have been here before, you know what I am talking about.
So... Match Outcome Determination= Flashes/oncards + Strats + Angle +
Intangibles. Intangibles...sometimes I may pick a person to win simply cause of gut feeling. Remember, this is all about having fun, this isn't some competition you are making money on. So have fun dammit...and that's an order!
STRATS- _NO_ freaking pyros, _NO_ freaking video walls. None...NONE! Got it!? If you include these in your entrances there will be hell to pay. Expect your entrances to be changed by me...and trust me, you don't want that. :-) I do expect entrances in your strats. If you don't there is a damn good chance you're getting the Barry Horowitz 'already in the ring'
treatment. 3. UPCOMING SCHEDULE AND DEADLINES ======================================== There are _many_ injured, hurt, sore people coming out of Rebirth, all going through tough matches. So, in lieu of that, this issue of "The Show" will be showcasing the newer, younger talent as well as the brand new tag team division...originating from the NEO Arena.
NEO has brought the tag division back and what better way to start the show! Three teams, two well knowns and one pair of unknowns will do battle as the KINGS OF HARDCORE do battle with BSCW'S HELL ON EARTH and the rookie, but impressive team of VALETTA VIOLENCE INC. in a THREE WAY DANCE!
One returns, the other comes to us as an import from the defunct NOP. Both men are known for their risk taking neck risking workrate and won't disappoint. CATASTROPHE, a former NEO Skyweight Champion, returns to face
"THE FIREFLY" HORO ATASHI! NEO has had no shortage of newcomers knocking on their doors. Those very newcomers will do battle in a SIX MAN TAG MATCH! ON one side the trio of RED
DRAGON, "THE CORRUPTOR" DOUGLAS MCCRAY and DIABLO IV! Their opponents are more known, two making a big impact on Rebirth. "QUICKSILVER" JEFF KEENAN will team with the monster MATT ALLEN and a man who went right after the big boys in VANDAL GOMEZ!
Two allies, tearing up the Skyweight Division, will do battle for a shot at one of the biggest titles in NEO...THE SKYWEIGHT TITLE! The winner will face whoever the champion may be. DAVID WILLIAMS has been an impressive rookie but will he have what it takes to defend his friend ASH?
THE BRAT PACK and DEBONAIR have been pegged as the two top teams in the new tag division. Why not have them do battle right away and see if they live up to the hype!
And in the main event of the night, Cesar Risley, fresh off a big, if not tough, win at Rebirth will defend his NEO SKYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP against A
MYSTERY OPPONENT! That isn't the only thing to make this match big time...the match will be A LADDER MATCH!
Plus all the fall out from Rebirth, words from the new champion and much, much more! **DEADLINES!! VERY IMPORTANT!** _EVERYTHING_ is due Sunday April 14th by Midnight. Strats, roleplays, etc, etc. If you are unsure as to what is happening with you, let me know. Send everything to [detail withheld] That gives you over a week to get things done...and over a week to recharge my batteries, something I could really use!
