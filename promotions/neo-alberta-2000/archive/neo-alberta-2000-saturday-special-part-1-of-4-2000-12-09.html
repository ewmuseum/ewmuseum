[ Clad in his well-worn, light-brown, leather trench-coat, baggy brown dress pants and a scruffy white dress shirt underneath, Elijah Flynt exits his
Chevette and heads his way towards the back entry-way of the building.
Ahead of him, a young man, probably late teens, jogs up with a middle-aged camera man behind him. The young man has black hair in a crewcut style, with a lower chin goatee. He looks to be in fair shape and is dressed in jeans and in a gray tee w/ red NEO logo, signifying a staff member. The camera operator is balding, somewhat out of breath, fairly average looking and wearing a plain blue sweat shirt and jeans. The young man, takes a mic offered by the camera man, and thrusts it forward, blocking Elijah's entrance, and smiles. Elijah scowls back. ]
Impressionable Young Reporter (hereafter referred to as, IYR): Mr. Flynt! Could we have a few words with you! [ Elijah just stares matter-of-factly at the teener. ] IYR: Uh, is there a problem, Mr. Flynt? Flynt: Yeah, you're in me fookin' way! IYR: Oh, right, sorry. [ The youngster genuinely seems to be apologetic and proceeds to step aside for Elijah. ] Flynt: Ta! [ Elijah keeps on walking, heading past the assortment of boxes and gear employed by the ring crew. The kid and camera man jog after him. ]
IYR: WAIT! [ Elijah sighs, rolls his eyes, as the duo stop behind him. ] IYR: Just a few words, please? I'm new at this, I don't know what I should or shouldn't be doing... I'm truly sorry if I ticked you off back there by blocking your way. I just need a few words...

IYR: Oh hey, I can understand that... school gets me in a bummer sometimes. Anyways, uh, what should I say? [ "Bugger!", thought Elijah. ] Flynt: Oh, hmm, well, you could ask me what I'm doin' wit' meself, why I'm
'ere when I don't 'ave me debut match this week... that sorta thing.

IYR: I'm here today with one of New Era Old school wrestling's signee's this past week, Elijah Flynt!
Mr. Flynt, perhaps you could shed some light on why you decided to sign with NEO? [ "Oh bugger, bugger bugger!", thought Elijah, "This lad is so damn green!"] Flynt: Well, it's simple really. I needed a bleedin' job. [ The kid smiles. ] IYR: Oh, you like to blade! [ Elijah slaps his forehead and mutters... ] Flynt: noyoustupidgit. IYR: What was that?
