Mimic hits Royce with two in a row and Royce staggers back. Mimic takes hold of Royce and whips him… It’s reversed and Mimic is sent right into the barricade before this match ever starts!! Royce takes Mimic by the head and walks him around the ring… He whips Mimic right into the ring post!!! Mimic goes down hard and he is somewhat busted open!! Royce is told by the referee to get into the ring and let the match start but Royce pushes the referee and takes Mimic by the hair. Mimic is on his knees and dazed as Mathers talks smack and lightly smacks his face a couple times… WAIT!! LOW BLOW FROM MIMIC!! Mathers holds himself and lets out a cry for help as Mimic hits him with a couple shots to the head! Mimic gets back to his feet and rolls Royce into the ring. The bell rings and the match is under way as Mimic stomps on the back of Royce’s head. The referee breaks him off and Mimic pushes the referee, going back at Royce. Mimic kicks at Royce as Royce scurries to the corner and now Mimic chokes him on the ropes with the boot to the throat! Mimic is forced to break the hold and let Royce up, but Mimic goes back at Royce… Royce pulls himself up and kicks Mimic instead! Mimic stumbles back and turns around… CLOTHESLINE FROM ROYCE!! He makes the cover….1…2… Kick out!! Mimic and Royce both get up and Mimic is whipped off the ropes… Back Body Drop by the champion!! Mimic gets himself back up as he holds his back and Royce kicks him on the back repeatedly. Mimic makes it to the ropes and pulls himself up as Royce hits him in the back. Royce whips Mimic, but Mimic reverses the whip… Mimic waits on Royce and kicks him in the gut… Suplex to the mat from Mimic to Royce!! Mimic gets up and hits a standing leg drop on Royce. The cover…1…2… Kick out before the three from Royce!! Royce sits up and Mimic now applies a sleeper hold to the champion. The crowd watches and cheers as Royce is in the pain of the hold… The referee drops his hand…1… Mimic tightens it but Royce seems to be getting some strength…. 2…. The referee lifts it up one last time…If it drops this match is over and there is a new champion…NO!! Royce raises the hand back up and He gets to his knees!! He is on one leg as he elbows at the gut of Mimic… Another shot to the gut and Royce breaks free!! Off the ropes comes Royce… FLYING CLOTHESLINE TO MIMIC!! The two of them back to their feet and Royce ducks Mimic’s attempted punch… RUSSIAN LEGSWEEP!! Royce Mathers gets back to his feet and waits as Mimic is getting up holding his head…Royce takes a hold of Mimic and picks him up for a piledriver… Mimic squirms down and from behind hits a neckbreaker on Royce Mathers!! The champion goes down and Mimic makes the cover…1…2… NO!! Close!! Royce and Mimic are back up and Mimic lays a shot into Royce… Royce hits Mimic… Mimic hits Royce… And Royce explodes with rights, lefts, and a big right that send Mimic back into the corner!! Royce gets up on the turnbuckle and pounds Mimic as the crowd counts…1…2…3…4…5…6…7… Royce stops before he gets to ten and climbs down! He picks Mimic up and sets him on the turnbuckles… BIG SUPER PLEX TO MIMIC FROM ROYCE!!! Mimic stays down and Royce doesn’t look done yet… He takes back to the top rope and positions himself on the top… Mimic slowly gets to his feet and Royce waits for him to turn around… WAIT!! MIMIC PUSHES THE REFEREE INTO THE ROPES!! ROYCE GOES NADS FIRST INTO THE POST!!! Mimic struggles to get up and Royce has a look of shock on his face that not even Joe Pesci or his sidekick could top for Home Alone! Mimic climbs up the top of the ropes and hooks both of Royce’s arms behind his back… DOUBLE ARM SUPLEX OFF THE TURNBUCKLE!!! BOTH OF THESE MEN ARE DOWN IN THE RING!!! The referee is using the ten count and these two are still down as he reaches six… Mimic starts to move and Royce is down… WAIT?!!! HERE COMES THE XCWF PRESIDENT SID!!! Sid runs down the ramp and around the ring, picking up a chair and placing it in the corner… A German Suplex to the champion from Mimic… Sid goes around and gets the referee’s attention as Mimic is locking in the Mimic Lock on Royce!!! ROYCE IS TAPPING BUT THE REFEREE IS DISTRACTED!!! Mimic breaks the hold and goes over to Sid and the referee and goes for a shot and Sid but he hits the referee!! THE REFEREE GOES DOWN!! Sid drops off the apron and Mimic swears at him… MEANWHILE… HERE COMES ROYCE MATHERS WITH THE CHAIR TO THE HEAD OF MIMIC!! MIMIC GOES DOWN AND ROYCE TOSSES THE CHAIR OUT OF THE RING!!! ROYCE COVERS AND SID ROLLS THE REFEREE OVER… 1…2…3… DAMN IT!! [Mike Dennis]- DAMN IT ROYCE AND SID ESCAPE WITH THE WIN!! Mimic is busted open in the ring and he can’t believe what just happened to him!! He’s been cheated!! [Duff]- It’s all part of the game Mike!! Mimic lost to the best, Royce!! [Mike Dennis]- Look at the look on Mimic’s face…He wants revenge… What’s this?! What’s going on?! The lights dim out and a strange, soft music beat starts up, it sounds like “Feel so Numb” by Rob Zombie… The beat picks up and the crowd all watch in shock and start to explode with cheers as they see… MARK ESTEP!!! [Mike Dennis]- MY GOD LOOK WHO IS BACK!! AFTER BEING GONE FOR ALMOST TWO MONTHS… MARK ESTEP IS BACK!!! LOOK AT THE SHOCK ON ROYCE AND SID’S FACES!!! The Former two time XCWF Champion, Intercontinental Champion, Hardcore Champion, and Tag Team Champion comes out from the back dressed to impress. He has sweat pants, black and lime boots, and a “Now, this is a Grand Slam!” shirt on as he walks out from the back…Royce Mathers and Sid look at him as they stop dead in their tracks on the ramp and Mark stares at them… Mark charges at Royce who is still weakened from the match with Mimic and he levels him!! Mark is on top of Royce beating the hell out of him with fists of fury!! Sid can’t break them up and he tries to kick Mark Estep… Mark stops and leaves Royce on the ground as he turns to Sid who kicked him a couple times…. FIST TO THE MOUTH OF THE PRESIDENT!!! Mark yells words of vulgarity as then continues to walk down the ramp and he sees Mimic in the ring getting up and looking at him. Mark waits for a second on the outside and then shakes his head and rolls into the ring… Sid and Royce are slowly walking up the ramp and disappearing into the back, leaving Mimic alone! Mark Estep goes at Mimic and stomps away on him as he tries to get away!! Mimic is in the corner now and Mark stomps a mud hole on him as he falls down helplessly at the hands of Estep!! Mimic stays in the corner on the mat and Mark tears around the ring yelling and screaming! The fans are going nuts and Mark looks back at Mimic again… He is pulling himself up and he’s dazed… Mark takes a hold of Mimic… YES!! THE ESTEPPER TO MIMIC!!! HE GOES DOWN!!! Mark Estep has laid out Mimic and he now has a microphone… [Mark Estep]- Did you miss me Mimic? By the way, I just kicked your ass one portion at a time!! [Mike Dennis]- YOU’VE HEARD IT RIGHT… MARK ESTEP IS BACK AND EVERYONE IS IN SHOCK!!! Mark walks around the ring and kicks Mimic out of it and then turns as he sees Royce and Sid fleeing to the parking garage… They are running through the back and all you hear are the screeching of tires… Next you see car headlights and a limousine heading at Royce and Sid… They dive out of the way and the limousine screeched to a stop. The limousine takes off again and screeches through the parking garage… Royce and Sid get up and dust off and Royce looks as if he sees a ghost…A man dressed in all black: Suit, shades, boots, gloves, and everything is staring at him. And here comes that limousine again! The man is standing in the middle of the garage and the limousine screeches to a stop directly behind him. The man reveals a black baseball bat and hits Royce in the stomach with it and then hits Sid with it!! The man takes Royce’s body and walks him to the back of the limousine and throws him in kicking him as he does so. The limousine takes off as the man gets into the passenger seat up front. They leave the lot and then for some reason they stop and turn backing into a dead end…And they back RIGHT into the wall!! The limousine hits the wall and makes a loud sound as it starts to become crushed from the back side. The limousine pulls forward and back into the wall again… and again. The engine is turned off and the doors open to the front two doors… Out step two mysterious men… The walk off and remove their hats, sunglasses and jackets…. IT’S ZAC MOORE AND DONOVAN SIMMONS!! They smile as they take one last look at the scene of destruction as they walk off to their car… [Mike Dennis]- SOMEONE CALL AN AMBULANCE!! DONOVAN SIMMONS AND ZAC MOORE ARE BACK AND THEY ARE PISSED OFF!! WHAT IS GOING TO HAPPEN NEXT FRIDAY ON FEAR?! The camera cuts back into the arena as a puzzled look is spread over Mark Estep’s face and he stands in the ring thinking.
