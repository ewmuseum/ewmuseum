:: LIVE!! On TNN/Unlimited Television from the Richmond Coliseum in Richmond, Virginia... XCWF Saturday Night Fear!! :: The screen opens to the New TNN Logo screen and for some of you, those with satellite and Primetime Central TV: Unlimited Television’s logo comes up. Either way, “Up Next: XCWF Fear” is on the screen. Finally, a series of drumbeats play and then the screen flickers… The camera cuts and out of no where “It’s Going Down” by the X-Ecutioners and Linkin Park cuts in and the XCWF Logo flashes up and then is stamped over with the number 2002… The XCWF Fear video plays through as the many faces of the XCWF flash on the screen: Mark Estep, Zac Moore, Al Magz, Stryder, Mimic, Duff, Menace, all among others... The video concludes and the camera cuts to the Richmond Coliseum as the fans are cheering… BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The sounds of the fireworks fill the arena as the crowd cheers and yells. The signs are held high up in the air and the fans are ecstatic. The camera circles the arena and shows the ring… Fear would start off with a short conversation between Michael Dennis and Bryan Kaminski. The two of them talked about what would be on tonight’s card and what happened last week, as well as what we will all see on Sunday at UncoNS2XionL. Just then, “Dropping Plates” by Disturbed hit and the fans broke into a chorus of boos for the XCWF Commissioner as he came down to the ring followed by the B.o.D. at arms length. Tonight, it looked like there was something on the B.o.D.’s mind and that they would express it as Mark started to talk. Mark first noted on what happened last week, and he did so with a smile. He talked about how Phil McCann destroyed Royce Mathers in that match last week. How Royce was forced to tap in the Seizure by McCann because he simply was not good enough to win the title. Royce Mathers simply “had no desire to win the XCWF Championship”, quoted Mark Estep. The fans of course booed on this and Mark smirked going on about how much Al Magz was in for it on Sunday when he stepped head to head with Phil McCann. He said Phil was more than Al would be able to handle and that Phil was a warrior and someone that would triumph over Al Magz unlike some that tried before him. Mark turned to Phil, handing the microphone to Phil McCann who would speak next. As McCann came on the microphone the boos would overshadow whatever it was he wanted to say. But as they died down, he would start to talk again. “This Sunday, I am going to make Al Magz suffer and he will lay down for me to pin.” The first words out of Phil McCann’s mouth, a very strong start as the fans were enraged. McCann went on to say that what Mark Estep said was all true. Last week, Royce Mathers lost to Phil because he didn’t want to win. There was no desire in his mind. But unlike Royce, Phil McCann had that desire and his eyes were focused on getting that XCWF Championship. He went on saying how before he returned he made himself the silent promise to get that title from whoever had it after he returned. He didn’t care who it was, but if it was Al Magz, it would only be a better thing. A final warning was given to Al Magz from Phil McCann as McCann said that Sunday, he would be waiting and tonight, he’ll be giving Al a little preview in that tag team match. The B.o.D. looked to be finished, though Menace looked to be trying to get the microphone. There went the microphone by Menace and the B.o.D. started to leave the ring. Menace shook his head following the group out of the ring. Mark Estep and Phil McCann heading the group as the others trailed. Evan and Bruce were talking and Menace was looking up at the XCWF Tron. And that would bring the XCWF into their first commercial Break. *** Commercial Break *** - Castrol GTX – Drive Hard. Drive Happy. - XCWF Fear: The Video Game! For X-Box, PS2, and PC.
