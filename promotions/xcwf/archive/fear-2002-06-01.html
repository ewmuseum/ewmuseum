XCWF Card Archive

Fear, 1 June 2002 — results

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: LIVE!! On TNN/Unlimited Television from the Joe Louis Arena in Detroit, Michigan... XCWF Saturday Night Fear!! :: The screen opens to the New TNN Logo screen and for some of you, those with satellite and Primetime Central TV the Channel 14: Unlimited Television’s logo comes up. Either way, “Up Next: XCWF Fear” is on the screen. The camera cuts and out of no where “It’s Going Down” by the X-Ecutioners and Linkin Park cuts in and the XCWF Logo flashes up and then is stamped over with the number 2002… The XCWF Fear video plays through as the many faces of the XCWF flash on the screen… Mark Estep, Zac Moore, Al Magz, Kamikazi, Mimic, Daddy-O, Menace, all among others... The video concludes and the camera cuts to the Joe Louis Arena as the fans are cheering… BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The sounds of the fireworks fill the arena as the crowd cheers and yells. The signs are held high up in the air and the fans are ecstatic. The camera circles the arena to get set for the night coming off of XCWF MayDay! Mike Dennis and Bryan Kaminski started off talking about the night at MayDay. Title changes, controversies, and more. Mike Dennis ran down a couple matches set for Fear tonight including: Shawn Boyd vs. JC Valerio for the XCWF Junior Heavyweight Title, a triple threat for the XCWF Extreme Title and the Bruce Blade and Menace taking on Jason Smith and Benny Bodacious. Bryan added that the B.o.D. was not happy and tonight would definitely not be a good night for anyone in their way. The first match for the night was scheduled between Shawn Boyd and JC Valerio. Both Boyd and Valerio were going to put on one hell of a show for the fans. Shawn Boyd had just won back the XCWF Junior Heavyweight Title from Colby Newsome at MayDay! and he wants to hold it past tonight. In the early goings of the match, JC Valerio took it to Shawn Boyd with great skill and athleticism. JC was truly showing he wanted the title and it looked like Boyd was still recovering from that match at MayDay! a mere week ago. Boyd caught a lucky break as he scored with a frog splash on Valerio, but that wouldn’t keep JC down. He kicked out after a two and the match continued. Valerio had Boyd set for the finisher but Shawn escaped, coming off the ropes and nailing Valerio with a springboard cross body! Again, Boyd was unsuccessful in a pin as JC Valerio kicked out! Getting frustrated, Shawn Boyd called for the end as he climbed the turnbuckles. He looked to be setting up JC Valerio for a Swanton Bomb or another aerial tactic… Boyd came off the top looking for the swanton… But all he got was the canvas! JC Valerio rolled out of the way and got to his feet, a great move by Valerio! Boyd got back up, favoring his right arm and side as he tried to take down JC again. JC moved and quickly countered with an inverted DDT! Boyd went down and Valerio made a cover… JC got a near fall but Boyd managed to kick out! The fans were really getting their money’s worth for this match as JC was again pounding on Boyd. This time, he put Boyd in the corner and climbed the turnbuckles… Boyd blocks the attempted Tornado DDT by JC Valerio… Counters it and hits the Boyd Bomb!! Shawn Boyd crushes JC down to the canvas…1…2…3… The champion retains his title and could be on his way back to life in the XCWF! Backstage, a silent and angered Mimic was walking into the arena… It looked as if he may be heading towards the ring. A small bandage over his right eyebrow as he made his way into the arena and turned the corner, disappearing from the sight of the camera. *** Commercial Break *** As the show returned, a clip of XCWF MayDay was shown. More or less it looked like it was from the Women’s Title match. Tessa Windsor was the official as Tasha Evans and Victoria Vengeance were battling one on one. The clip ended and then the camera showed Tasha Evans standing with Matt Stanford, she of course is not happy. Matt asked her what she thought of the loss at MayDay to Victoria and Tasha Evans said that she was upset with it. She thought she had the match won, but if it wasn’t for that bitch Tessa Windsor, she would have won. That’s why tonight she challenged Tessa to a one on one match on Fear, to show her why you don’t screw Tasha Evans. That comment was enough for Bryan Kaminski to get into some details with himself involved with Tasha. Something about the involvement of handcuffs, a bed and them two… But that’s another story. Suddenly, an unscheduled appearance was made by the new XCWF Women’s Champion, Victoria Vengeance. She made her way down the ramp with the title, and walked around to ringside. Looked as if she’d be doing commentary with the other two. Tasha Evans came out and had a couple words with Victoria Vengeance from inside the ring, still obviously not over her loss about a week ago. Tessa Windsor started off the match, unloading on the former Women’s Champion. Chops across the chest, kicks to the mid section as well as a couple closed fists. Tessa took Tasha down with a nice bulldog in the middle of the ring and rolled her up for the pin… But that wasn’t going to be enough to keep her down. Tessa came looking for a nice dropkick, but missed. Tasha Evans hoped over her and looked for a sunset flipping pin. That too, did not happen as Tessa kicked out! Tessa had escaped and she once again came off the ropes, Tasha Evans took here and flipped her down to the mat!! Tasha now looked as if she was going to ascend the top rope in the ring. Tessa Windsor had the officials attention and from the announce position came Victoria Vengeance… She pushed Tasha from the top rope down into the ring and onto the mat!! Tasha was down and hurting as Tessa Windsor crawled over for a pin fall… She scored the win over Tasha Evans. But that wouldn’t be the end, the XCWF Women’s Champion had something to say. Victoria slid into the ring behind Tessa with the title in close grasps… WHACK! Right between the eyes as Tessa turned around! A little bit of pay back for what happened at MayDay! *** Commercial Break *** Mimic was shown, walking around backstage, as he was totally pissed off. He stormed into the room marked “TJ Faith”. Searching frantically he called out TJ’s name and tossed things around the room. TJ Faith showed himself, stepping out of his bathroom and looking a little surprised. Mimic went up to him and got into his face, grabbing him by the collar of the shirt. Faith still shocked and a little bit annoyed now. “What are you doing?!” Faith shouted at Mimic. “You fucked with the wrong man, Faith.” Mimic told him as he had TJ pressed up against the wall. Faith still a little distraught tried to formulate some words. He asked what Mimic was mad over. “You know damn well, I was screwed. You were the one that came out there… And now, you’re going to have to pay!” Mimic was definitely not in a good mood, and TJ Faith is definitely not either as he demands that Mimic put him down. TJ Faith made the attempt to verbally defend himself, telling Mimic he had no intention whatsoever on distracting him. His intentions were solely to dispose of the B.o.D. at ringside. And as far as Mimic’s actions in recent weeks went, TJ was not happy. He told Mimic that he and the B.o.D. better watch what they are doing and who they want to mess with, or they could have some trouble on their hands. Mimic smirked and then got very close to TJ’s face… “Don’t worry, there is something I have ready that will change the XCWF… Forever.” Mimic stated clear as day, then going on to make a challenge for Fear, “Tonight, I want Rage in the ring. Not only were you one to cause me my title… But his ass and that other bastard Bodacious did as well. So, I want to teach his ass a lesson!” TJ Faith was reluctant to make the match, but he did. He told Mimic that if the B.o.D. decided to rub it’s noses in another match, that he’d get involved as well. *** Commercial Break *** The next contest of Fear, puts the returning Benny Bodacious in a tag team match along with Jason Smith as they are taking on two of the more prominent members of the B.o.D., Menace and Bruce Blade. The XCWF Intercontinental Champion and his stable mate, Bruce, make their way to the ring for the upcoming contest. Benny Bodacious and Jason Smith leave the ring to meet up with their opponents in the aisle. The four of them throw fists and begin this match before the bell! But the match does start as Jason Smith and Bruce Blade end up in the ring. Menace and Benny on the aprons. Bruce and Jason have little history but they have no reason to be friends, locking up into a nice collar-elbow tie up. Jason ends up taking out his opponent with a nice slam to the canvas. His assault continues, beating on Bruce Blade, that is until Bruce weasels himself away. A nice low blow that allows him to get away and tag in his partner! But Jason Smith is also able to tag in Benny. Benny and Menace go at it for a while, Menace nailing a huge spinebuster on Benny Bodacious! A pin fall is broken up by Jason Smith and he goes at it with Menace. Bruce Blade gets back into the ring and clotheslines Jason Smith over the top, thus eliminating him as a threat right now. A two on one assault pursues as the B.o.D. dismantles their opponent. Bruce and Menace tearing apart Benny Bodacious in the ring, but here comes Jason Smith back into the ring as he manages to this time take Menace down! They go brawling to the outside of the ring as inside Bruce and Benny are left! They go at it, Benny catches a break and hits a neckbreaker on Bruce Blade!! Down for a count… Yet a kickout as Benny Bodacious lacks a hook of the leg. Jason Smith takes Menace on the outside and runs the XCWF Intercontinental Champion directly into the steel ring post! Menace goes down and Jason Smith starts to climb the turnbuckles now… Benny Bodacious takes Bruce and slams him down on the mat…. DEATH WISH!! Jason Smith hits it on Bruce Blade! The fans are going nuts… But it doesn’t look good… Here is Menace now with a baseball bat!! The Intercontinental Champion comes into the ring… WHACK! He takes on Benny first! And Jason has a pin on Bruce… BAM! Right to the back! The XCWF Intercontinental Champion costs himself the match! A disqualification as this assault with the baseball bat pursues! The fans booing as Menace smirks, kicking the crap out of Jason Smith! And Bruce Blade is getting back to his feet, slowly… They both stomp on Jason Smith and then turn around taking out Benny Bodacious again. The two members of the B.o.D. stand triumphant over these two, even as they did not legitimately pick up a victory. Yet in the end, it was them who remained standing and somewhat victorious. A shot backstage shows the XCWF Tag Team Champions, Stryder and 3rd Degree Burns watching the television and now laughing. They must have been watching that last match and now comment on what happened. Poking fun of the B.o.D., Menace and Bruce Blade in particular. Stryder says he couldn’t believe he ever respected Menace, no matter how many times he lost to him. 3DB added that the B.o.D. was almost as pathetic as Duff and 66 were at MayDay in that Cage match. That very match where Big ‘n’ Bad won the XCWF Tag Team Championships. Indeed they did win the titles, and it was a hell of a match. *** Commercial Break *** Back in the arena, Menace and Bruce Blade celebrated their victory against Jason Smith and Benny Bodacious. They laughed as they walked through the hallways and met up with their leader and his right hand man, C-Man. Mimic congratulated them on a victory and then told them that things were almost ready for their being idea. He told them to be ready for next week, it would be a great step for the B.o.D. A night no one would forget. And it would most importantly teach TJ Faith, Al Magz, Rage and the rest of the XCWF not to take the B.o.D. lightly. That to which Menace added that he would personally make sure anyone that tried to ruin their plans would be stopped and beaten to a bloody pulp, like they had done Jason Smith and Benny Bodacious. Leaving his locker room backstage was the new Vice President of Breyer’s Ice Cream, Pat Dwyer, better known to most as Destroyer. He carried a gallon’s worth of Breyer’s ice cream in his hand, eating it as he walked with the XCWF Extreme Title around his waist. Down the halls of the Joe Louis Arena he made his way towards the ring. But he met up with Curt Rock… Startled to see Curt, Destroyer gave him an odd look. Curt told Destroyer he wanted to wish him luck in his match tonight with Evan Darkness and Crimson Lord. He said he knew it wasn’t easy to be defending your title against more than one person, after all he lost it to Destroyer that way. But Curt told Destroyer that when he left Detroit the champion, Curt would be waiting for him. And that he’ll get his shot again at the XCWF Extreme Champion in the future. And when that time comes, Curt will leave the champion! Destroyer stuck out his hand and thanked Curt for his support, but told him he didn’t need it. As much as a sportsman he is, he told Curt he’ll do what he has to do to keep his title. And that would include laying out Curt Rock’s ass in the process. Destroyer then walked away leaving Curt stunned. *** Commercial Break *** The XCWF Extreme Title was on the line next. Just six days before, Destroyer put it up against seven other men in a big battle royal match with a controversial finish. Evan Darkness and Crimson Lord each plummeted over 20 stairs down to the floor of the Reliant Astrodome as Destroyer knocked them down. They he made a double cover and retained his title. Tonight would be a rematch for the three of these men. One of them would leave with the XCWF Extreme Championship! Destroyer is of course at a disadvantage in this match up. Both Crimson Lord and Evan can beat on him and then take on each other. The size is definitely in their advantage as well. But Destroyer slips away from the two big men and causes them to quarrel between themselves. That also allows Destroyer the chance to get some arsenal ready for battle. Grabbing trash cans, chairs, tables, signs, and just about anything else he can find, Destroyer gets back into the ring. Evan Darkness decks Crimson Lord with a hard clothesline that takes him down! WHACK! Destroyer meets Evan with a trash can lid to the face! It doesn’t do much, all Evan does is back up. Destroyer picks up a chair and takes it back… WHACK! Right over the head of Evan Darkness and he falls down this time as Destroyer makes the pin… It’s broken up by Crimson Lord though. A bit later though more fighting, Destroyer takes a hard shot from Crimson Lord as he is picked up and hoisted through a table in the middle of the ring!! A pin is made by Lord but it’s broken up by Evan Darkness! Those two big men fight, as Destroyer needs to recuperate. Evan takes Crimson and drops him with a big sidewalk slam in the middle of the ring! A two count on a pin fall and the match continues. Evan and Crimson Lord fight around the outside of the ring and beat on each other as in the ring Destroyer is recovering from the effects of that slam through the table. Evan Darkness and Crimson Lord are fighting in the audience and through the crowd as the fans watch on in awe. Darkness takes Crimson Lord back to the ring and pins him for a near fall. By this time, Destroyer is getting back to his feet and has recovered somewhat from the bumps he’s taken in the match. Destroyer picks up a street sign that is lying on the ground and waits as Evan knocks out Crimson Lord and moves him to the outside. Evan turns around… WHACK! He walks right into the hard shot from Destroyer! Evan Darkness goes down and now Destroyer isn’t done. He picks up Evan and since the man is too big, turns him over… Drop toe hold! Right on the sign! Evan Darkness is out of it and the XCWF Extreme Champion makes the pin fall…1…2…3… Destroyer retains his title and lives as the champion for another day! Once again, Mimic is shown backstage. The still XCWF World Championship number one contender does not look happy, but he is focused. He will take on the XCWF United States Champion, Rage, when Fear returns. And it should be a hard battle for the two of them. The camera then cuts to show the XCWF United States Champion walking down the halls making his way towards the ring. *** Commercial Break *** Destroyer is walking a happy man through the back as he makes off with the XCWF Extreme Championship. He turns a corner… There’s Evan Darkness!! Somehow Darkness is alive again and he’s beating on the XCWF Extreme Champion!! The title isn’t liable to be won right now, but Evan is taking it to the Extreme Champion! Destroyer is brutally assaulted by Evan Darkness and left in a mess of objects as Evan walks off mouth discouraging words to the champion. Finally, it’s time for the main event. Mimic and Rage going one on one in a non-title match up, a match signed earlier in the night when Mimic was enraged with TJ Faith. Both men now in the ring and going at each other fiercely. Fists are flying and the tension is there as Mimic wants to pay back Rage for what he did at MayDay! Mimic takes Rage and drops him with a huge German Suplex! Rage is scooped up for an early cover… Kickout after two by Rage! Mimic takes a motion to go for the Last Mime, but it’s early in the match and Rage gets out of it. So Rage comes off the ropes and counters with a spinning wheel kick! The challenger goes down and Rage waits on him… Dropkick! Rage rolls up Mimic… Kickout after two by Mimic this time! Mimic gets back to his feet and they lock up… Mimic knocks Rage back into the corner and they are brawling. Fists and chops by Mimic like he’s trying to kill Rage, which might be the ultimate goal. Rage manages to escape and turn the tables on Mimic, whipping him across the ring. A follow up… Mimic moves and Rage hops on the turnbuckles! CROSS BODY! Rage takes down Mimic and there’s a pin fall… Near fall! A close two and a half count! Rage back up and waits on Mimic… Mimic with a drop toehold as Rage comes in! Mimic Lock!! Rage is squirming trying to get out… Mimic pulls him to the center of the ring… Rage is fighting the hold from Mimic the best he can. Rage gets to his one foot as Mimic holds the other… Rage manages to escape via the Enziguri!! Both men taking a pounding in this match and here’s Rage again coming for a move… Mimic takes him down with a knee! Mimic off the rope with a standing elbow drop! This match is really being a high paced brawl. Moves back and forth as these two athletes fight. Rage is in a little trouble and Mimic picks him up to his feet… Here could be the Kast Mime! No! Rage gets away yet he bumps into the referee inadvertently! The ref goes down and Rage turns, here it is… ARSON SLAM! Rage takes out Mimic in the middle of the ring! The pinfall made by Rage… There is no pinfall to make! Rage looks around and then doesn’t see Bruce Blade, Menace, Evan Darkness and C-Man coming until too late! The ring is over crowded now… Three men on one here! They are all beating on Rage! Hopeless lies Rage on the ground as he is stomped on! Mimic recovering from the effects of the Arson Slam some what. Bruce Blade has Menace hold Rage as he gets set…. Blade Cutter!! He decks Rage! The US Champion is down in the ring and the B.o.D. is standing over his body in victory, though this match is still going on! And hold on… TJ Faith is coming out!! The XCWF Vice President has a chair in hand and he runs down to the ring as he slides in and tries to help Rage!! The B.o.D. not wanting to get involved with TJ Faith, scatter from the ring. Faith is left alone in there with Rage. Mimic, C-Man and the rest of the B.o.D. retire up the ramp as the fans boo for them. TJ Faith checks on Rage and tries to help him up as he looks at the B.o.D. walking up the ramp. Mimic mouths the words “Just wait till next week… Just wait…” TJ Faith has no clue about what and the camera pulls back as Rage is being held up by the XCWF Vice President in the middle of the ring. Until next time…. XCWF Fear is over. *** Fade To Black *** *** LIVE! XCWF FEAR ON TNN/PTC CHANNEL 14!! Every Saturday night from 9 to 11 p.m.! What will happen this coming week?
