Fear, 28 September 2001 — results

28 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: LIVE!! On NBC from the Key Arena in Seattle, Washington... XCWF Friday Night Fear!! :: The camera opens to the NBC Logo and then shows “Next: XCWF Fear!” The camera goes to black and then fades into a chillingly soft music. The word “Fairness” appears as then the clip of Mimic using the ropes to beat Monarch Of Massacre… “Revenge” appears as Nick Mondo is shown being knocked out by Kidd X… “Payback” is shown as the camera cuts to show Al Magz, Freak and Bruce Blade… XCWF REDEMPTION! Live on Pay-Per-View September 30th 2001 from the SOLD OUT E-Center in Salt Lake City, Utah! The XCWF Fear video starts up to the music of “Chop Suey” by System of a Down. The video ends and the camera cuts to the Key Arena from the outside as the sun is shining and fans are cheering as the camera cuts inside. Fireworks are shooting off and the crowd goes nuts. The camera circles and pans through the crowd and their signs as it zooms in and out and then looks at the stage. The camera looks at the ring and then goes to the commentators’ desk as Michael Dennis and Bryan Kaminski sit. [Mike Dennis]- WE ARE JUST TWO DAYS AWAY FROM XCWF REDEMPTION!! Hello and welcome to XCWF Fear tonight as we are getting set to explode with yet another XCWF Pay-Per-View!! This Sunday we have word of a number of matches! But there is one thing we still don’t know about… Who and what will be the main event?! Will we see Psycho Kid defend his title? Or will we have something different? [Bryan]- Well, I think we’ll see some sort of announcement! Psycho Kid is expected to be here and so are a bunch of other XCWF superstars! [Mike Dennis]- Right, and what about this new XCWF President?! Everyone has been questioning this, and we know a lot of the wrestlers are upset as well! Folks if you missed it, last Friday the XCWF Owner and former President handed down the company to someone from the outside! What will this new XCWF President have to say tonight? [Bryan]- Yeah, I have a question… What the hell was Tom Mason thinking?! I mean, he just handed down the company he made! He was the founder, owner, president, and brains of the XCWF! [Mike Dennis]- Well, I think we’ll have to wait before we judge this new guy. After all, the President has good judgment so we may be in good hands. Anyway, we have two hours of action to get through before we can think about XCWF Redemption. And tonight we’ll see Nick Mondo and Damien Strife take on Kidd X and a partner of his choice. Mondo made this challenge to get his hands on Kidd before they meet for the XCWF Junior Heavyweight Title on Sunday. [Bryan] - I don’t blame Nick. This guy came in and tried to pick a fight with him. Nick and Damien lost a match with Big ‘N’ Bad as Kidd X interfered and cost them the match! So Kidd X will get a beating tonight for what he did. ”My Way” by Limp Bizkit hits and the crowd is somewhat cheering as Luminous Legend comes out from the back. He comes to the top of the ramp and stands there. Then this fiery black mist comes up behind him and a giant explosion erupts the fans are going crazy. He comes down to the ring and stands in the center. Then a white mist surrounds the ring and another explosion comes this time from the turnbuckles. Luminous Legend stands in the ring and gets ready for the match. [Mike Dennis]- Well, we are seeing Double L in competition for the first time in a couple weeks. He has been somewhat silent after losing to Al Magz. Let’s see what he can get against Stryder tonight. Standard Match Luminous Legend vs. Stryder Out of no where Stryder’s music hits and he comes out from the back. He makes his way down the ramp to cheers from the crowd and he gets into the ring. Stryder paces around the ring and gets up on a turnbuckle raising his hand up for the crowd. He climbs down and Legend begins the attack. The referee calls for the bell and the match is started as Legend pounds on Stryder. Stryder tries to fight back by Legend hits him in the face. He whips Stryder across the ring into the opposite turnbuckles and charges… No, Stryder gets his foot up and hits Legend in the jaw! Legend stumbles backwards as Stryder comes out of the corner looking for a clothesline... Drop toehold by Legend on Stryder. Stryder’s face bounces off the mat and he rolls over as Legend decides to go for a submission early… Figure Four Leg Lock on Stryder. Stryder tries to fight the hold as he reaches for the ropes. The referee checks for the submission and Stryder is holding out. Stryder stretches for the ropes… He gets them! Stryder is safe as Legend is forced to break the hold. Stryder slowly gets back up and Legend kicks him in the right leg. Stryder falls back down and Legend stomps on the leg hard! Stryder rolls out of the ring and hobbles around the outside to shake off his injury. Legend slides out of the ring after Stryder… SMACK!! Stryder clocks Legend with a hard chop! Legend reels back and Stryder hits him again!! The crowd is in awe as Stryder battles back against Legend! Legend is dazed and Stryder goes for the whip… Countered by Legend sending Stryder into the ring post!! The referee abandons the count and gets out of the ring to get the two men back in the ring. Stryder is down and Legend rolls into the ring. Stryder slowly makes his way back to his feet as he rubs his head. Stryder rolls back into the ring and Legend lifts him up in a suplex! He covers Stryder…1…2… No! Kickout just before the three by Stryder!! Legend gets back up and Stryder is getting up as well. Stryder turns to Legend as the crowd cheers for Stryder. Legend hits Stryder… Wait a second! Stryder mounts come back as he lays into Legend with a few good punches! Legend is whipped into the ropes… clothesline by Stryder!! Stryder waits as Legend gets back up… SIDEWALK SLAM BY STRYDER!! He makes the cover…1…2… No! Kick out this time by Luminous Legend! Legend gets to his knees and Stryder goes to grab him… LOW BLOW!! Luminous Legend just low blowed Stryder!! Stryder is hunched over… FRONT FLIP DROP KICK OFF THE TOP ROPE BY THE BIG MAN!! Legend locks in the Laviathin Lock and Stryder is fighting it. He reaches forward for the ropes but can’t get them… He tries frantically to get to the ropes… But he is forced to tap! [Mike Dennis]- Stryder is done for tonight after having no choice but to tap out to Luminous Legend. But folks we are only beginning this night and I’d expect a hell of a lot more! The XCWF is heading into Redemption in two days and we are without a main event. That is why we’ve sent Johnny Fame to the President about this issue. [Bryan]- Johnny Fame?! Give me a break, he can’t ask a good question if his life depended on it. Let a real man do the job. [Mike Dennis]- Well, who do you think should be the one to conduct this interview then? [Bryan]- As a matter of fact.. ME! I’ll go get the answers from the president… [Mike Dennis]- Please tell me you’re not serious… Anyway, we’ll be back with that and more when Fear returns!! The camera pulls back as Bryan gets up from his seat and starts to walk from the desk. Mike yells out to him in disbelief. Bryan smiles and keep going… *** Commercial Break *** The camera fades in backstage as Bryan is making his way through the back to the President’s office. He pushes Johnny Fame and Fame is shocked. Bryan points his finger down the hall as he takes the microphone from Fame. Fame cusses at Bryan as he leaves and Bryan knocks on the door. [Mike Dennis]- Well, I guess Bryan wasn’t kidding when he said he’d do it. Bryan again knocks on the door and it opens… The new XCWF President is standing in the door entrance and Bryan starts to talk. [Bryan]- Hi Smith, Sal… Jim? What’s your name again dude, I forgot. [President]- Right, sure you did. Anyway, it’s Sid… What do you want, Bryan? [Bryan]- Well, everyone is wondering a couple of things and they all want some answers... So first, why would Tom give you his job and company? [President]- Well, Tom was a good owner but I also have the experiencein running a rather successful company. I ran the WWCW, which lasted a good number of years and generated quite a number of successful superstars. And, if you have nothing more I have to get busy on some things for the Pay-Per-View. [Bryan]- Well, hold on a second pal. I have a question to ask you about the Pay-Per-View. [President]- Let us back up for a second… First off, I am not your pal. I don’t want to be and I never have been your pal. Second, you will speak to me the way you should speak to any higher ranked official… Call me sir. I don’t know how things ran when Tom Mason ran this place, but while I am here this place will run like a company… Not a circus. [Mike Dennis]- Circus?! I beg to differ with that line. And this guy may run the show but who the hell does he think he is? [Bryan]- Right… Anyway, why hasn’t there been a main event made for Redemption yet? The Pay-Per-View is in two days and there has not been a signing or even a thought for a main event. Can you explain that? [President]- What’s with you and all these questions. You want a main event for Redemption on Sunday? I’ll give you one… Who’s the World Champion again? [Bryan]- Psycho Kid is the XCWF Champion. [President]- Well, let’s see… Let me think of a good opponent for him at Redemption on Sunday. As the President runs his chin Mark Estep comes by and stops to say hello. [Mark Estep]- Hey, Bryan… Hi, umm… Who’s this guy? Is he a new wrestler because he looks a little scrawny and weak? [President]- I beg your pardon. For your information, I am the NEW owner and President of the XCWF. [Mark Estep]- You must mean the XCWA, the training school… Right? [President]- No, I am the NEW President of the XCWF and not to forget… you. Can’t remember? Tom Mason handed me the company last week, you’re under my control… Mark is it? [Mark Estep]- Wow, quick learner… We may have a good candidate. Yeah, it’s Mark Estep also the XCWF Continental Champion and the ONLY Grand Slam Champion. Mark holds up his Intercontinental Title and then points to his “This is what a grand slam looks like!” T-shirt. Sid doesn’t look amused. [President] - Mark, I’ll tell you what. Since you interrupted my interview, then you tried to insult me, not to mention gloat about your insane and ridiculous achievements. I am going to give you a match at Redemption. [Mark Estep]- Finally, I was beginning to think I was being skipped over. Against who? [President] - It will be against the World Champion Psycho Kid for the XCWF Championship! Now, have a good day. Looks like I will be the first two time champion afterall!! Mark Estep smiles and skips down the hallway as the President shuts his door on the face of Bryan. The camera fades out to commercial… *** Commercial Break *** [Mike Dennis] - Welcome back to Fear here tonight. Redemption is in two days and we have now been told the XCWF Redemption Main Event! Psycho Kid will defend the Championship against Mark Estep by the order of the new XCWF President. [Bryan]- But could you believe the nerve of him?! I mean he was such an ass to me, and trying to get me to call him “sir”. What the hell is his problem… I want Tom to come back! [Mike Dennis]- Well, this guy is the President and we can’t change this. Anyway, we’ve got some more action to get to right here on Fear. Jason Smith and Unfitt Survivor are going to go at it right here, and right now! Standard Match Jason Smith vs. Unfitt Survivor No Contest. [Mike Dennis]- Interesting as that was, let’s take a short break and we’ll be back with more action… The camera cuts backstage as the Vice President is in his office and then Mark Estep walks in. Mark is smiling and TJ Faith asks why. [TJ Faith] - Hey Mark… What are you so happy about? [Mark Estep]- Nothing except for the fact that I got a match for Redemption finally. [TJ Faith] - Well, all you had to do was ask me and I would have gotten you a match. Maybe an Intercontinental defense. What did you get? [Mark Estep]- Nah, I got something better… MUCH better! I got a World Title shot at Psycho Kid! [TJ Faith] - WHAT?! WHO GAVE YOU THAT MATCH?! [Mark Estep] - That new President guy. He’s a real punk but hell, I got my match! So, I’m happy… [TJ Faith] - Yeah, that’s nice but I think I’ll have a word with him. Good luck on Sunday. Mark Estep smiles and nods as he leaves. The Vice President gets up from his desk and starts to leave his office as the camera goes to commercial… *** Commercial Break *** The camera opens up backstage as TJ Faith is walking down the hall and he is approached by Monarch Of Massacre. Massacre stops TJ and starts to talk… [Massacre]- TJ, I need to speak with you for a quick moment. I know you are busy and have things to do. But I need something from you. [TJ Faith] - Okay, well make it fast I am on my way to handle some official business. [Massacre]- Sunday at Redemption… I want a rematch against Mimic. He cheated his way to victory last week and it will not happen again! I will beat him, and I am going to this Sunday! All I need is you to sign the match. [TJ Faith] - You know what? Sure, take the match. I am in the mood to hand out a match. You have your rematch on Sunday at Redemption with Mimic. Massacre leaves and TJ Faith keeps walking down the hall way. He turns a corner and suns into Kidd X. Kidd X starts to talk to him. [Kidd X]- Oh, good. I was just about to go look for you. I need to talk to you about my match with Dysfunction tonight, I have an idea. [TJ Faith]- Go to my office and wait for me. I’ll be there in a little bit. TJ Faith keeps going again and then Donovan Simmons approaches him in his wrestling attire and starts to talk. [Simmons]- TJ, I want to pay back Raven Storm tonight. I am not going to wait until Sunday at Redemption… I want him in that ring here tonight! [TJ Faith] - Okay, well if that’s what you want… Lace up your boots and head out to the ring. You’re up next. [Simmons]- Good, thanks. TJ Faith keeps walking and the camera goes out to the ring as Mike Dennis and Bryan start to talk… [Mike Dennis]- Well, I guess TJ isn’t in total agreement with Sid on his first executive decision. And how about those two matches he just okayed? Tonight, Simmons will face Raven Storm. And then on Sunday it will be Monarch of Massacre against Mimic for revenge. [Bryan]- Well, Massacre is going to lose again, that’s all I’ll say… "Grimey" by Noreaga plays, Donovan Simmons makes his way to ringside. He stands in the entrance way, admiring the crowd as they boo him. He walks down the aisle, climbs in the ring, and climbs on the ropes and looks at the crowd again. Then he climbs down and waits for his opponent. Raven Storm’s music hits and he comes out from the back with Jenteal. Standard Match Donovan Simmons vs. Raven Storm Raven climbs into the ring and gets up on the turnbuckle being booed by the crowd as he climbs down. He circles the ring and looks at Donovan. Donovan charges at Raven by Raven ducks and Simmons goes into the corner. Simmons turns and Raven starts to hit him with lefts and rights. Simmons rakes the eyes and is able to get out of the corner. The referee checks on Raven and then allows the match to continue. Simmons comes rushing at Raven and hits him with a clothesline as he turns around! Raven goes down and grabs his head and stumbles back to his feet. Raven ducks a attempted clothesline and counters with a inverted DDT! He covers Simmons…1…2… No! Kick out by Simmons after the two! Simmons rolls over and slowly gets back up. Simmons calls the referee over and starts to talk to him about an injury he may have. The referee is distracted and down from the ramp comes Zac Moore!! Raven is distracted by the referee and Simmons as Moore slides into the ring and superkicks Raven in the jaw!! Raven goes down and Moore slides out of the ring as Simmons gets back up. The referee is confused and Simmons lays over Raven…1…2… Kickout!! WAIT! From up on the ramp comes Kamikazi!! Kamikazi runs around the ring after Zac Moore and chases him out into the crowd!! Simmons and Raven Storm are going at it in the ring as Raven counters a punch from Simmons! Raven hits a fisherman’s suplex on Simmons!! He makes the cover…. 1…2… No! A kick out by Simmons! Simmons gets back to his feet and Raven kicks him in the gut… Belly to belly attempt blocked by Simmons… A second try and Raven is able to connect with a belly to belly overhead on Simmons! Raven Storm is out of energy too and he collapses on the mat. Simmons lies there as does Storm…1…2… The referee is administering his count…3…Raven is slowly trying to get to the ropes…4…5… Storm is getting back to his feet…6… Simmons is on one knee…7… Storm is up and connects with a kick to the face of Simmons. Storm goes for a figure four-leg lock on Simmons… Simmons fights the hold and tries to break free… He is struggling to reach the ropes… But Simmons manages to reach out and get the ropes! Raven is forced to break the hold and the match continues. Storm kicks at Simmons’ left leg and then performs a knee drop on it. He tangles the leg into the ropes and presses down on it as the referee counts the five… Raven breaks the hold and picks up Simmons by the hair. He goes for the whip but Simmons counters and whips Storm into the ropes… LEG CLIP BY STORM! Simmons goes down and Raven decides to capitalize with the half Boston crab!! He locks in the move and Simmons grimaces in the pain… He tries to reach for the ropes but they are in the center of the ring… Simmons taps out to the half Boston Crab! [Mike Dennis]- Could this be a sign for Sunday as Raven Storm, Donovan Simmons, and Kamikazi will all be in the ring for a Canadian Chaos match! [Bryan]- Right and did you see how Kamikazi interfered and cost this to happen in the match? The match could have been over and Donovan would have won, but Kamikazi came out! [Mike Dennis]- What are you talking about?! Kamikazi chased Zac Moore away after ZAC interfered. It was Zac who took out Raven with a superkick to the jaw and almost ended the match! [Bryan]- Right but if Kamikazi hadn’t have come out Donovan would have beaten Storm! [Mike Dennis]- Sure, we’ll be back in a couple minutes with more action! *** Commercial Break *** The camera opens up to “Moments Ago” as a limousine pulls up and out of it step Psycho Kid. Kid makes his way into the arena with his title and goes down the hall. He is approached by TJ Faith and Psycho starts to talk to him. [Psycho Kid]- So, what was this big problem I had to rush over about? [TJ Faith]- Well, I guess you could cal it a problem. You see, I talked with the new President, Sid. And he already had announced that you would be fighting Mark Estep on Sunday at Redemption for your title. Good news is, you don’t have to accept it. [Psycho Kid]- Okay, so then what’s the bad news? [TJ Faith]- The bad news is, if you don’t accept the match… Sid will fire you and you’ll forfeit the title to Mark. [Psycho Kid]- Well, the bad news isn’t so bad considering I was accpetiong this match! I can and I will beat Mark Estep on Sunday! Last week he wanted to screw with me, in the tag match. Hell if it weren’t for me, he probably would have been killed! I’ll see you later! [TJ Faith]- Okay, peace out. Psycho Kid goes down the hall and then turns the corner as the camera goes to a live feed of the arena. The crowd is cheering and then “Awake” by Clay People hits and out from the back comes Dysfunction. Damien Strife and Nick Mondo make their way down the ramp with fog all around them. They go down the ramp to boos as they climb into the ring and get on the turnbuckles. [Mike Dennis]- Well, here are Nick Mondo and Damien Strife. Nick will see Kidd X this Sunday as they fight for Nick’s Junior Heavyweight Title. Tonight is just a preview or so says Nick Mondo. So let’s see what happens… Tag Team Match Dysfunction vs. Kidd X/? Kidd’s theme music hits and he makes his way down to the ring and the crowd cheers somewhat. He slaps a few hands but Dysfunction wastes no time in rushing out of the ring and starting to work on Kidd. Kidd is hit and punched repeatedly and Nick Mondo then throws him into the stairs!! Nick kicks at Kidd and Kidd just lays limp as they beat on him! No sign of a partner for Kidd X as Nick and Damien get back into the ring. Damien starts off as Nick leaves the ring. Nick then walks over to Kidd X and kicks him yet again and then rolls him into the ring. The bell is rung as Damien kicks at Kidd. Kidd rolls over and Damien covers him…1…2… No! A kickout just before the three by Kidd X. Damien picks up Kidd X and whips him into the corner. Kidd hits hard and Damien charges in with a clothesline. Kidd X falls back over the top rope and lands outside the ring. Damien gets out of the ring and picks up Kidd X by the head and smashes his head on he steel steps!! Kidd X bounces off the steps and lays at the ground helpless. Damien is told to get back into the ring and he does and he argues with the referee. From the side comes Nick Mondo who jumps up on the steps… ASAI MOONSAULT OFF THE STEPS!!! He takes out Kidd X and hurts himself as well! The referee gets out of the ring and scolds Nick as he checks on Kidd X again. He watches as Damien leaves the ring and grabs Kidd by the hair and rolls him into the ring. Damien gets into the ring and then tags in Nick Mondo. Kidd X is getting back up and Mondo hits the Springboard front dropkick on him!! He makes the cover…1…2… [Mike Dennis]- What the hell is this?! The lights just went out here in the Key Arena!! What the hell is going on!! On the XCWF Tron comes the Anarchy symbol and then a large explosion of flames and fireworks… The lights come back on and in the ring is KAMIKAZI!! Kamikazi has a chair… CRACKED OVER NICK MONDO’S HEAD!!! Damien Strife gets back into the ring and goes after Kamikazi… CRACKED CHAIR OVER HIS HEAD AS WELL!! Damien and Nick have been laid out by half of the tag team champions!! Kamikazi picks up Nick Mondo and whips him into the corner. He charges in and crushes Nick. Nick falls down and Kamkazi picks up Damien’s body. He whips Damien into the ropes… GROUND ZERO ON DAMIEN STRIFE!!! Damien is out of it after that powerful move!! Kamikazi now picks up Nick Mondo… FADE TO BLACK ON NICK MONDO BY KAMIKAZI!! DYSFUNCTION HAS BEEN LAID OUT!! KAMIKAZI HAS KILLED THEM BOTH!! He slaps Kidd X in the face and points to Nick Mondo. Kidd X crawls on top of Mondo…1…2…3… KIDD X WINS!! He gets up and grins at Kamikazi. The crowd is cheering and then Kidd X goes to shake Kamikazi’s hand… WAIT!! PULLED IN AND KICKED… DOWN IN RUINS ON KIDD X BY KAMIKAZI!! KAMIKAZI HAS LEFT CARNAGE IN THE RING!! [Mike Dennis]- What the hell was this all about?! Kamikazi comes out and helps Kidd X get the win over Dysfunction. But then he turns around and destroys Kidd X!! What the hell is instore for the XCWF on Sunday at Redemption! [Bryan]- Kamikazi is taking off in a hurry… He looks like he’s running to get out of here before any of them get up. Kamikazi runs up the ramp and through the back curtains as the camera follows him. He runs down a hall way and passes a bunch of guys. He then cuts into the parking garage and a limousine is waiting for him. He gets into the limousine and the limousine turns around and starts pulling out of the garage. The limousine is on its way out and then there are some bright lights ahead and a big shadow. A honking of a loud horn is heard followed by the screeching of wheels and then a loud crash… The camera goes in as you see wreckage of the limousine and then in front of it a big rig truck. There is a glare on the front windshield and you can’t see the driver. But the cameraman tries to go around to the door and the door opens. All you can see is the door and a figure wearing black boots and black pants as the camera fades out…. [Mike Dennis]- OH MY GOD!! WHAT THE HELL JUST HAPPENED?! IS KAMIKAZI ALL RIGHT?! WHO THE HELL JUST HIT THAT TRUCK INTO THE CAR?! WE NEED HELP OUT HERE!! MY GOD!! The camera fades out to black and then to a XCWF Redemption promo as the video rolls by the screen. *** LIVE! On Pay-Per-View September 30th 2001 from the E-Center in Salt Lake City, Utah. XCWF Redemption is live at 8 p.m. Eastern Time until 11 p.m...
