X-Awards, 14 September 2001 — results

14 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: LIVE!! From the Brown County Expo Center in Green Bay, Wisconsin... XCWF First Ever X-Awards!! :: The Camera opens to the NBC Logo and then goes into the XCWF X-Awards logo. The camera cuts into black and then "Control" by Puddle of Mudd starts up. The camera goes into the Brown County Expo Center and to a LIVE crowd as they are cheering. The Camera circles the arena as shows a red carpet rolled down the ramp from the XCWF Tron to the ring. The camera focuses in the ring as a pedistal is in the center. A table is next to the pedistal, which hold 11 X-Award Trophies. The crowd dies down as "Blind" by Korn hits and the President comes out from the back in a suit. He makes his way down the ramp and gets into the ring. He paces around and then goes up to the pedistal. The camera shows him as he talks... [President]- Good Evening everyone and welcome to the FIRST ever XCWF X-Awards!!! We are glad you are all able to attend! But before this gets under way, a few things need to be addressed. The XCWF is not the first group to do this. The President declared today a national day of prayer. But the XCWF wants to show their support as well. We want to extend our condolences to the men, women, and childen that all died in this past Tuesday's terrorists attacks. We are shocked and very much upset that something like this has happened. But we as certain that the terrorists and anyone who believes in them will get just what they deserve!! The crowd erupts with cheers and the President fixes his suit. He looks around the arena and then continues... [President]- Now, tonight is big for the XCWF... We are here for the X-Awards. Then shortly after is Friday Fear! Right now, we are going to present 11 awards to some of the XCWF's top wrestlers. Each of these awards was voted on by the wrestlers and by their stats. Then there are two awards... The President's Award and the Viewer's Choice. Those voted on by Myself and then the fans. So, the first award is the Most Extreme Wrestler... The nominees are... The camera fades out to black and then "Voo-Doo" by Godsmack hits as Freak's music video plays. The crowd cheers and then starts to cheer and boo as "Last Resort" by Papa Roach cuts in and 'Sick' Nick Mondo's video plays. "All-Star" by Smash Mouth starts and the crowd boos as Johnny Reno's video plays. "Clint Eastwood" by Gorillaz starts and more mixed reactions arise as Bruce Blades video starts. The video stops and the crowd is silent as the camera goes back to the ring. In the ring now stands Mike Dennis. [Mike Dennis]- The winner for the Most Extreme Wrestler award... Your Extreme Champion, The Freak!! "Voo-Doo" by Godsmack hits and from the back comes the Freak to cheers from the crowd. Freak makes his way down to the ring and Mike Dennis shakes his hand in the ring. Mike hands Freak the X-Award and Freak steps up to the podium... [Freak]- I will be short... This Award is mine for one reason... Because I am the XCWF Extreme Champion! I am the best XCWF Extreme Champion, and so far the ONLY! Tonight, I will prove it as I defeat Bruce Blade on Fear! Freak steps away and the crowd cheers as he leaves the ring with the X-Award. Mike Dennis looks over his notes as Freak heads to the back and the camera goes to commercial. *** Commercial Break *** The camera goes back into the Expo Center as Mike Dennis stands in the ring. He is at the podium and starts to talk... [Mike Dennis]- The XCWF has for over eight months showcased some of the greatest wrestlers. They've brought in more and more talent over the times and some of the newest stars have been fortunate to make it big. Take people like Mark Estep, Psycho Kid, and Al Magz. This next award is presented to the XCWF's best new rookie for the month of August and into September... The camera cuts to the XCWF-Tron as the nominees start... "Outside" by Stained hits and Darkside's video hits. Then "Clint Eastwood" by Gorillaz hits and Bruce Blade's video rolls by. Again, Pap Roach's "Last Resort" goes on and Nick Mondo's video plays. Finally, Koga Kozama'z music hits and his video plays. The video ends and Mike Dennis stands at the podium as he opens the envelope... [Mike Dennis]- And the winner of the XCWF's first ever Rookie award... Nick Mondo, the XCWF Junior Heavyweight Champion! "Last Resort" by Papa Roach hits and out from the back comes Nick Mondo. He puts his hands up in the "M's" as he climbs into the ring and goes to Mike. Mike hands him his award and Nick starts to talk... [Nick Mondo]- One thing I'd like to say is that to all the other nominees... You just aren't SICK enough!! I proved my place in the XCWF and an honor it is to get this award... Thank you!! Nick raises up his award and he leaves the ring. He heads up the ramp and to the back. In the ring Mike Dennis stands as he starts to speak again... [Mike Dennis]- Now we have seen two champions get awards. We are going to take it up a notch and go to a bigger award. In the XCWF, there were four factions... The biggest was the Canadian Elite, then was IWO, Total HaVoK, and then the newest... New Era. No one will forget the epic 6 man Street Fight with the New Era and the IWO. In one match, the IWO was split apart and vanished. The New Era: Freak, Mark Estep and Out Kast defeated the IWO. Tonight, the New Era defeats the other stables to get the stable Award. To accept the award... All three members of the New Era. "Awake" by Godsmack hits and the crowd cheers as the New Era comes out from the back. Mark Estep, Freak, and Out Kast make their way down the ramp to the ring. They each get in and gather around the podium as Mark Estep speaks. [Mark Estep]- This award is a bit of a shock to the three of us. I mean, seeing how Predator and Kamikazi were in the same category and they are two of the biggest legends. But this goes to show you that the underdog can overcome and get the win!! Thank you all for voting for us!! [Freak]- Yeah, this is an honor. We hope to be up for this award next month and hopefully take it again! [Out Kast]- Thakn you to everyone... And to my cousin may he rest in peace, this one's for you! Peace out dawgs!! The crowd cheers and New Era leaves the ring. They head up the ramp and to the backstage area. The camera cuts out to commercial... *** Commercial Break *** The camera goes back out to the ring as Chris Cook stands in the ring and at the podium. He speaks as the crowd is quiet. [Chris Cook]- In life there is hatred between people and countries, like terrorists showed on Tuesday. In wrestling a similar hatred or jealousy is shown between wrestlers. Whether it is for titles, over money, or even over women. There is always a problem. We are here to recognize the best feud in the XCWF... The crowd responds with a great deal of cheers. [Chris Cook]- The nominees are: Johnny Reno and Freak over the Extreme Title, Psycho Kid and Royce Mathers over fairness and the World Title, The Canadian Elite break up. And then, Royce Mathers and the Masked Man or Monarchof Massacre... The winner is: Royce Mathers and the Monarch of Massacre! Up on the XCWF-Tron comes Royce Mathers as he sits in his wheel chair. He starts to talk... [Royce Mathers]- Thank you, Chris. I am happy to accept this award but Massacre will not be happy after I get out of this wheel chair! I am ready to repay him for his attackon the Sensational one! And then I will take back the XCWF World Title! The crowd boos and Royce Mathers gives the middle finger as he fades out. The camera fades out to commercial as the President is shown walking to the ring... *** Commercial Break *** "Blind" by Korn hits and the President walks down to the ring again. He gets into the ring and waves a little tothe crowd. He walks to the podium and clears his throat... [President]- I am here to present the award for the best wrestler in my eyes. It's a hard decision to make and I have picked all of these men to be in the XCWF because they are good. And to pick one man that really stands out is difficult. But choose I narrowed it down to 4 of the best in my mind ringht now. You have Al Magz, Phil McCann, Psycho Kid, and Royce Mathers. All of them have been XCWF Title Holders and two currently are. This decison was again tough but I feel it goes to Psycho Kid. He is the reigning XCWF World Champion and has sacrified a lot for the title. So, Psycho Kid, the President's award goes to you! "Psycho" starts and out from the back comes the XCWF Champion, Psycho Kid. He makes his way down to the ring with the crowd cheering him on as he gets in. He paces around the ring and then shakes the President's hand and takes the podium. [Pyscho Kid]- Now, Tuesday was a tradgety, and many people suffered. Tonight, is an honor and a previliage for myself to accept such an award. I want to that all the fans that are supporting me and especially the President for choosing me. Going back to the terrorists attack on the United States, as a proud citizen of the States... I can truly say that we will get back who ever did this and they will be sorry!! God bless American and thank you! The fans cheer and Psycho Kid raises the Award as he leaves the ring and heads up the ramp. He gives out some high fives and yells out "USA" as he disappears. The President backs off and Mike Dennis gets back into the ring. He stands at the podium and starts to talk. [Mike Dennis]- Tonight we will see wrestlers risk their lives in the ring, as they do every night. And over the past four days following Tuesday, we saw firefighters and police workers risk their lives. Many have died trying and the XCWF salutes their actions. Those who died truly cannot be replaced and they will be missed. On Fear, in less than a half hour we will pay more tribute to those lost. Right now, we will honor the XCWF's Best Champion. The camera cuts to black and then "Voo-Doo" by Godsmack hits and Freak's video starts up. The crowd cheers and then the Italian Anthem hits and Al Magz video plays. "Last Resort" by Papa Roach hits and 'Sick' Nick Mondo gets cheers and boos. Finally, "Psycho" hits and Psycho Kid's video plays on the XCWF-Tron. The crowd cheers and slowly dies out as Mike Dennis talks again... [Mike Dennis]- ...And the winner is... Freak! Cheers and screams come from the crowd as Freak makes his way down to the ring again. He gets in and smirks as he accepts the award from Mike Dennis... [Freak]- You'd think Michael was surprised that I won this award. And for what, I am not too shocked. Psycho Kid is the current XCWF Champion and he's one hell of a champion. As for Al Magz, he puts up a fight and had a heck of a stunt as the US Champion, and now he's the champion again. But it comes down to the best, and that's myself, the Freak. Thank you. Freak continues to smile as the lights go out... The come back on in a minute and the Freak is gone. Mike Dennis is stunned and the crowd cheers. [Mike Dennis]- Freaky stuff... No pun intended. Anyway, when we return the winner for Most Valuable Wrestler, and the Best Match Award! The camera fades out to commercial as Mike Dennis reads his notes and the camera goes outside to screamming fans who stand waiting to get in the building for Fear. *** Commercial Break *** The camera goes back to the Expo Center and to the ring as TJ Faith is in the ring. He is dressed in his formal khakias and a dark blue collared shirt. He is at the podium and he starts to talk... [TJ Faith]- In the XCWF there is no doubt that the wrestlers get hurt. Whether it's by getting dropped through a table, knocked off a ladder, smacked with a chair, or just sometimes falling off of cages. The XCWF Wrestlers sometimes look insane and stupid for doing what they do, but all is fair and love and war. Tonight we will honor the wrestlers that have been in some of the greatest XCWF Matches. Here are the nominees for the Best Match Award... "Oh My the Carnage!" is said and with that clips from matches start to play... The World Title Triple Threat Match: Psycho Kid, Aaron Jacobs, Royce Mathers. Then the United States Title Japanese Torture Match: Al Magz against the challenger Sumo. The Four Way Ladder Match for the Extreme Title at ResurreXtion: Freak, Bruce Blade, Johnny Reno, Justin Pearce. And then the last nominee: The Four Way Tag Team Title Elimination Match: Total HaVok, Dysfunction, Immature Playas, and Japanese Duo. [TJ Faith]- Those are indeed some of the high points in the XCWF. Anyway, the winner is... The Four Way Elimination Tag Team Titles Match!! The winners of the match, Total HaVok, Predator and Kamikazi will accept the award! A great roar from the crowd as the Tag Team Champions, Predator and Kamikazi come out from the back and go down to the ring. They get in and Predator gets on the top turnbuckle and throws his hands up. The crowd cheers and Kamikazi accepts the award... [Kamikazi]- We'd like to thank the other tag teams first of all, without them we wouldn't have this award. They tried, but just couldn't beat Total HaVoK! We also like to thank Thomas Mason for setting the match, then the fans for their support, the XCWF in general and our families... Thanks and you all rule!! Predator and Kamikazi slap hands and they leave the ring. As they head up the ramp Johnny Fame enters the ring and gets ready to present the next award. [Johnny Fame]- The XCWF has already presented 8 awards tonight and only three more remain. The XCWF has been in a lack or rather a collapse in the Tag Team Division. Predator and Kamikazi have defeatened almost every tag team in the company. They are the reigning Tag Team Champions and are undefeatened. That is why hands down they are also the winners on the XCWF Most Dominate Tag Team Award! Up on the XCWF-Tron Predator and Kamikazi are shown stopping. They are a little blank at first and then turn to each other... They jump in the air and yell "Whoopie" as they run down the ramp and slide into the ring. They grab the Award from Johnny and then Johnny buts in... [Johnny Fame]- Wait! Just wanted to tell you... [Predator]- Save it Johnny, we just won another award! [Kamikazi]- True, again we'd like to thank all those that voted for us and all of our fans. But then again, there was no denying Total HaVok this award... Who is better than the Tag Team Champions themselves?! There is no one!! And one more thing... WHAT'S OUR MOTHER{{Beep}} NAMES?! The crowd cheers and responds with "Total HaVok" as Predator and Kamikazi leave the ring with two awards... The camera fades out to commercials as they are cheered on... *** Commercial Break *** The camera opens up backstage as Kamikazi and Predator are walking down a hallway... From behind comes Calgatraz and they attack Predator and Kamikazi!! They get up in their faces and yell "Who's the most Dominate Tag Team now?!"... The camera goes back to the arena floor and the crowd is in shock after the attack. In the ring stands Matt Stanford... [Matt Stanford]- Well, the next award tonight is probably one of the top awards. This award will go to the person who has most shown the qualities of being a fan favorite. A fan favorite is well liked by the crowd, a great athlete in most cases, and an all around great person. Here are the nominees... The camera cuts to the XCWF-Tron and "Voo-Doo" by Godsmack hits as Freak's video plays. He gets a good amount of cheers and then his video and music dies down as "Psycho" starts and Psycho Kid's video starts. Even more cheers erupt for the World Champion and then the cheers continue as Mark Estep's video goes on the screen... Then comes "Bodies" by Drowning Pool and Total HaVok is on the screen. The crowd's cheers die down as Matt speaks... [Matt Stanford]- And the winner of the Fan Favorite Award... Total HaVok, Predator and Kamikazi!! The camera goes to the XCWF Tron and ramp as the crowd cheers. There is no sign of Predator and Kamikazi... "Grimey" by Nore starts up and out from the back comes Zac Moore and Donovan Simmons! They are booed by the crowd as they make their way down to the ring and get in smiling. Dononan rips the award out of Matt's hands and Zac talks... [Zac Moore]- We'll take it from here! Thank you Matt... [Matt Stanford]- Well, I hate to tell you but you didn't win this award! [Zac Moore]- Did we not? Well, I hate to tell you... We have the award now! What are Predator and Kamikazi going to do about that?! I mean they are backstage and being attended to by medics... Haha. [Matt Stanford]- Right, anyway... Listen to this crowd, they really dispise you! The crowd boos and Zac Moore smiles... [Zac Moore]- Are you kidding me?! Everyone knows that they want MOORE!! And Moore they will get! Zac Moore and Donovan Simmons leave the ring laughing as they are booed by the crowd. They hold the X-Award as they disappear into the back... Matt Stanford leaves the ring in shock as the camera fades back out to commercial... *** Commercial Break *** The arena is shown again as Commercials end Mike Dennis is in the ring. HE stands at the podium and then speaks... [Mike Dennis]- Tonight we will also give out a big time award to one wrestler who shows he will not back down. That he can over come all obstacles and do the impossible. There is one man in the XCWF that stands out above them all and have set records, and even broken some. He is an XCWF veteran and a veteran to all of the wrestling world. The XCWF's Most Valuable Wrestler award goes to... The crowd is silent in anticipation of the announcement... [Mike Dennis]- Mark Estep! The XCWF Intercontinental Champion who has set the record of being the first Grandslam Champion! The crowd cheers and out from the back comes Mark Estep to "Fat Lip" by Sum 41. The crowd roars with cheers and Mark is wearing a "This is Grandslam!" T-Shirt as he gets into the ring. He climbs on the turnbuckle and raises his hand and then gets down. He takes the award... [Mark Estep]- Wow!! What an honor this is! First I want to extend my condolences to the families of the Terrorist attack victims. Althought your loved ones cannot be replaced, we will bring those responsible down! And next, my friends, family, and Out Kast's cousin who was killed in the attacks. You don't know how bad this is until it hits you first hand, and Out Kast I will be there for you! Thanks!! Mark Estep takes the award and leaves the ring to cheers. He heads up the ramp and in the ring is Bryan Kaminski. He is at the podium and holds the last X-Award... [Bryan]- Well, the first ever XCWF X-Awards are drawing to a close and it is getting to be the time of the last and final award. Perhaps the most glorious award of them all... The Viewer's Choice award! The Viewer's Choice was designed for the chance to let every fan vote for the one XCWF Star that they think deserves to be recognized. There were no official nominees by the XCWF itself. But some of the names on the list were: Mark Estep, Psycho Kid, Unfitt Survivor, Damien Strife, and even our very own President, Tom Mason. The votes were all tallied up to this moment as the poll was closed. And the fans have all voted... The camera cuts out and then comes back on with the rolling of "Blind" by Korn... On the ramp appears the President and he makes his way down the ramp to the ring. He gets in and shakes the hand of Bryan Kaminski. [Bryan]- You're Viewer's Choice Award goes to the Xtreme Enterprises President... The XCWF Owner and President... The Founder of the XCWA... The man who made this all possible... THOMAS MASON!!! The crowd cheers and Tom takes the podium and then starts to speak... [President]- I will not take long, becuase Fear is in just a few minutes. Like I said when the night started, we would like to offer our condolences to those who's families are victimized by the terrorist attacks. And we hope tonight's action helps pull your minds away from the chaos that we are all subject to as of late. And without further ado... XCWF FRIDAY NIGHT FEAR IN FIVE MINUTES!!! The crowd cheers and the President and Bryan shake hands. The President takes a microphone and then the crew moves in and starts to move the items from the ring. The camera fades out to black and to the NBC Logo... *** Up Next on NBC... XCWF FEAR!! Fear is set to explode with some great matches and big superstars on hand! Join the XCWF as they pay tribute to those lost in the World Trade Center and Pentagon Plane Crashes and Building Collapses...
