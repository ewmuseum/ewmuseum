Warfare, 16 January 2002 — results

16 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: LIVE!! On TNN from the Lockhart Arena in Miami, Florida... XCWF WEDNESDAY WARFARE... THE FIRST 2002 EDITION!!! :: The Scene Opens up to a packed arena with the fans on their feet cheering the XCWF on. [Dennis]- Welcome to the return of Warfare! [Kaminski]- Brought to you by XCWF and Xtreme Enterprises! Our First match right after we come back from commercial will be the Triple Threat match. [Dennis]- Between the uprising superstars of the XCWF. Dream Master , Dark Angel and Sterling Moore. The screed fades into commercial. After a short while the camera comes back on. All of a sudden the arena lights goes out as Limp Bizkit's "Rollin" blasts throughout the arena. The Dream Master makes his way out from behind the curtain to cheers and jeers from the crowd. He walks down to ringside as the fans continue to react to him as he enters the ring and grabs a microphone. [Dream Master] - I am starting to get sick and tired of not getting any damn respect here in the XCWF. Ever since I have came to XCWF I have been disrespected by you the fans and by that low life scumbag Tom Mason. [Mike Dennis]- Scum bag? What did Tom Mason do to Dream Master... I mean, he gave him a job after all! [Bryan]- Mike, I have no idea man... Maybe this is his way of saying he's in the WWCW Elite? [Dream Master]- So Mason, tonight, the Dark Angel's and 'Stunning' Sterling Moore's blood will be on your hands. I am going to leave them lying in this ring just like I will do to any other challenger!! Now bring those jackasses out here so I can make them famous. Dream Master throws the microphone down and he paces around the ring as he waits for his two opponents to come down to the ring.... Triple Threat Match Dream Master vs Dark Angel vs Sterling Moore Dark Angel comes out from the fans with no music or anything. Dream Master doesnt expect the attack from behind. Angel stops punching and stomping on Master as he falls to the mat. Sterling Moore's music hits the P.A. System and he walks out from the back. Angel doesnt give any signs of him hearing the music and continues the beating. Moore calmly walks down to the ring and gets inside. He stands behind Angel as Angel delivers more punishment to Master. Moore taps Angel on the shoulder. Angel turns around like what the hell but only to meet a right hand to the side of the head. Moore delivers a few more right hands before finally the fourth one knocks him down to the mat. Master rolls to the outside to recover. Moore pulls Angel up by the hair and whips him into the ropes and hits a big boot. Master seems to be fully recovered now and gets ontop of the turnbuckle. Moore plays to the crowd and turns around to meet a cross body block from Master. Master starts pumbling Moore. The crowd starts getting into the match. Master gets up at the same time Angel does. Master hits a back elbow on Angel before he clotheslines him over the top rope. Angel falls to the outside shortly followed by Master that jumps over the top rope and lands on Angel. Moore is back up on his feet and is looking for blood. He runs and hits a baseball slide onto Master. Master falls clutching his face in agony. Moore picks Angel back up and rolls him into the ring and then Master. Moore gets on the top turnbuckle and jumps off to do a clothesline on the now standing Angel. Angel catchs him and rams his back into the turnbuckle. Moore bounchs off of the turnbuckles and comes back towards Angel only to meet a falling neck breaker. Master gets up with the help off the ropes and looks like he is out of it. Angel runs and hits a running ddt onto Master. Master is on the mat and is non-moving. Angel picks him up and hits a powerbomb with a cradle..The ref counts 1..2..3!! [Dennis]- Oh My God! What a stunning victory by Angel. [Bryan]- Right as I thought surely Angel was going to be out of the match he comes back with the win. Wow! What a performance. *** Commercial Break *** Singles Destroyer vs Johnny Reno [Dennis]- This looks like it is going to be a promising match up. [Bryan]- I think , personally , that the Destroyer will win this match up. "I'm a cloud" by Boy Hits Car starts playing and Destroyer comes out onto the entrance way. He comes out with a water bottle and spits the water in the air like mist. He climbs onto the apron and does a flip over the ropes. He gets in the ring and lifts his arms twirling with red and white fire works going off the four corners. Johnny Reno comes flying out from the back with no music. The match is underway early. Both men start throughing punchs. Reno starts slowing down after awhile and Destroyer is taking controll of the match. Reno finds himself against the ropes. Destroyer throws him off of the ropes and hits a clothesline sending Reno to the floor hard. Reno rolls out of the ring and acts like he is going to leave. Destroyer goes after him. The two wrestlers meet up at the entrance ramp and start brawling once again. Reno slows down once more and Destroyer takes the offence once more. The ref is trying to get both men into the ring but they just wont have it. Destroyer tosses Reno into the safety railing. Destroyer runs at Reno to clothesline him over the railing but Reno ducks and back drops him up and over the railing. Reno takes a second for a breather and looks at what he did. He then stumbles back to the ring and rolls into the ring. Destroyer finds his way to his feet and makes his way back to the ring. He climbs up to the ring and gets in. Reno comes at Destroyer but Destroyer cuddles the ropes and the ref gets Reno away from Destroyer. Reno goes back at Destroyer but Destroyer grabs the ropes again. Finally Reno gets pissed off and pushs the ref out of the way. The ref goes over the ropes and to the outside. Reno hits a double arm ddt on Destroyer making Destroyer lie motionless on the mat. Reno is still pissed so he goes to the outside and grabs a chair. With the ref still knocked out on the outside but is coming through little by little. Reno gets into the ring. Destroyer climbs to his feet with the ropes help. WHAM!! Reno clocks Destroyer right between the eyes with the chair. Reno tosses the chair to the outside and covers Destroyer as the ref sees this..1..2..3!!! [Dennis]- Cheap! Cheap victory!! [Bryan]- What the hell was that for. Reno could of went for the pin with that Double Arm DDT but no he has to go to the reserve... the CHAIR!! What a bastard. Womens Title Match Amenity Divine vs Victoria Vengeance "Alive" by P.O.D. starts and the crowd cheers for Victoria Vengeance as he makes her way out from the back. Victoria stands at the top of the entrance ramp playing to the crowd as Divine comes from behind and clocks Victoria behind the head with a powerful forearm. Victoria falls to the hard metal surface and holds her head. Divine seems to be ready for this match as she picks Victoria up. Divine pile drives Victoria on the ramp way. Victoria isnt moving to much now except for her heavy breathing pattern. Divine grabs Victoria by the hair and begins slamming her head off of the hard , cold , steal metal. The ref breaks them up and tells them to take it to the ring. Divine picks Victoria up and takes her to the ring. Divine rings Victoria into the ring. Victoria gets on to her feet as Divine tries to hit a hurricaranna but Victoria counters and dilvers a powerbomb. Both women are on the ground thriving in pain. Victoria and Divine get up at the same. Divine runs at Victoria but in a desperation move Victoria hits THE BREAKDOWN. And she connects with it. Victoria rolls onto Divine for the cover. 1..2..3!! Leslie comes running from the back and begins to attack Victoria. Punching , kicking and stumping her to the mat. The ref pulls Leslie away from Victoria and Leslie walks to the back yelling and screaming choice words we cant show on public television. [Dennis]- Hahaha! Short victory for Victoria dont ya think? [Bryan]- How could you be so sadistic?! HAaHaha! Singles Match Nick Mondo vs Blizzard [Dennis]- This is going to be a great match. [Bryan]- Agreeable. [Dennis]- Theirs a brawl in the back are cameras are their now. The cameras take you to the back in the middle of the hall way. Nick Mondo and Blizzard are brawling it out. Other wrestlers are stadning around watching whats going on like Daddy-0 and Colby. Their is a table off to the side and Mondo picks up Blizzard and rams him through to the table. Mondo gets up from the wreckage and looks around for another weapon. Aztec comes from behind with a chair and cleans Mondos clock. Mondo falls on the floor not knowing what has happened , holding his head. Daddy-0 attacks Aztec and begins brawling with him after Aztec drops the chair. Colby picks the chair up and rams the side of it into the stomach of Aztec. Aztec bends over in pain right as Daddy-0 hits The One Night Stand. Blizzard is up and grabs Mondo. He starts leading him back towards the ring. They come out from behind the black cutain. Mondo hits a belly to back suplex on the top of the entrance ramp. Blizzard rolls around in pain but manages to begin to get on his hands and knees. Mondo takes a breather and then gets up. Both men are on their feet and are brawling back and forth. Blizzard head butts Mondo sending Mondo flying backwards. Mondo lands on the far side of the Entrance ramp very close to the edge of the top of the ramp way. The drop is a good 10 feet. Blizzard see's this and runs and hits a baseball slide to Mondo. Mondo falls off of the side. Blizzard jumps down after all this and pulls Mondo up. He leads him to the ring and rolls him in. Mondo gets up with the help of the ropes but Blizzard gets on the top TB and jumps off and hits a clothesline. Blizzard covers.. 1..2..3!! [Dennis]- Wow! Blizzard actually got the pin over Mondo. [Bryan]- I knew he would the whole time. Singles Match Daddy-0 vs The Menace Absolutely Anytime hits the P.A. System and Daddy-0 comes out from the back. The crowd responds and Daddy-0 goes to the ring. Menace appears from the back and walks to the ring. The two stand face to face after the bell rings. Daddy-0 mumbles something and then grins. Menace acts like he is looking away and pops Daddy-0 in the jaw. Sending Daddy-0 to the ropes. Menace runs and clothesline Daddy-0 over the top of the ropes and sends him to the outside. Menace goes out after Daddy-0. Daddy-0 gets up and hits a big boot onto The Menace. Menace falls to the floor and is in pain. Daddy-0 grabs a fans coke and awaits Menace to get up. Menace gets up and Daddy-0 throws the soda into Menaces eyes. Menace falls back onto the appron of the ring. Daddy-0 jumps up onto the appron and pushs Menaces head down onto the appron so his throat is straight with the ring. Daddy-0 jumps and tries to hit a leg drop but Menace moves out of the way. Daddy-0 is grabbing his ass in pain. Menace puts his head underneath Daddy-0s arm and hits a spinning suplex to Daddy-0. Daddy-0 is pretty much out of it. Menace rolls Daddy-0 into the ring. Menace gets in and hits a standing elbow drop. Menace picks Daddy-0 up and throws him into the corners. He runs after him but to meet a back elbow from Daddy-0. Aztec comes running out from the back. Daddy-0 jumps off the top TB and hits The One Night Stand. The ref is not paying attention to whats in the ring but to the outside with Aztec. Daddy-0 breaks the pin and throws Menace into the ropes and hits a samoan drop. Aztec grabs a chair and jumps into the ring. He clocks Daddy-0 causing Daddy-0 to split open and bleed. He is covered in blood now. Aztec continues to smack Daddy-0 with the chair. Menace gets up and helps Aztec in the punishment of Daddy-0. The ref finally breaks this up. The ref calls for a no contest. [Dennis]- That was just totally cruel of Menace and Aztec. [Bryan]- Don't think Daddy-0 wont get some revenge for what just took place. Tables Match Al Magz vs Colby Newsome "Bad to the Bone" starts to play. Three of the four guitar riffs blast. On the fourth the lights go to black. As the drums kick in a set of small pyros go off. Right to left from the viewers perspective. The lights come back on and Hi-Tech is at the top of the ramp. He makes his way to the ring as the crowd goes wild. Once in the middle of the ring his valet, Cadence, de-robes him. She gets in front of him, sways her hips and slowly squats, until it looks as if oral sex were possible. She stands up and exits the ring. The lights turn off and everything is pitch black. Green white and red fireworks shoot up from the entrance way and Al Magz walks out. He starts down the ramp and rolls into the ring. He then jumps up on the turnbuckle and raises both arms in the air. After Magz jumps down both men stare each other in the eyes. Intill Colby spits into Magz's eyes. Magz tries to whipe his eyes but Colby spins and hits him with a punch. Then spins and hits him with a punch. Continues to do this intill Magz is against the turnbuckles. Colby begins to hit shoulders into Magz stomach. Colby stops at about 5-6 and whips Magz into the other corner. Colby runs and Magz sticks his foot in the air. Colby falls to the ground holding his face in pain. Magz rolls out to the outside and sets a table up on the outside. He rolls back into the ring to powerbomb Colby. He does this with great power. He picks Colby up and whips him into the ropes. Magz tries a clothesline but Colby ducks it and goes off of the other side ropes and comes back with a flying clothesline. Magz gets hit but is still standing. Colby runs off the ropes and comes back and hits another clothes line. Magz is still standing.. Colby whips Magz into the ropes but Magz reverses it. Colby comes flying back with a high leg clothesline. Magz finally goes down. Magz gets to his feet and Colby tries a hurriacarana but Magz hits a power bomb instead. Magz picks Colby up like a rag doll and gets him into a Gorilla Press Slam. He walks over where the table is but Colby wiggles free. He drops kicks Magz where Magz goes over the top rope and onto the appron. COlby comes running and does a suicidal dive onto Magz. But Magz moves out of the way and looses his balance. Both men fall threw the table at the same time. The ref calls the match. [Dennis]- What a match! [Bryan]- It sucks that it kind of had to end in a no contest state but a good match never the less.. [Dennis]- Warfare has lived up to its standards... We'll be back after these commmercials... *** Commercial Break *** "Timebomb" by Godsmack hits the stero and the crowd is on their fet booing for the WWCW Asian Pacific/XCWF Technical Champion as he walks out from the back and he has the title around his waist. He makes his way down the ramp and climbs into the ring, getting on the near turnbuckle and putting his hands up and then rubbing the gold around his waist. He climbs down and snatches the microphone out of the announcer's hand and he paces the ring to a chorus of boos as he has the AP Title over his shoulder... [Mimic]- Well, it's great to see the appreciation I can get from my fans in this sorry state of Florida... The very state the President's brother governs. The state inwhich they cannot count holes through paper... I suppose they have trouble counting how many [beep] it takes to fit into an [beep] too!! The fans boo and Mimic chuckles a little bit. He paces around the ring and he then looks the camera square in the lens... [Mike Dennis]- What exactly does Mimic want out here? Is he just here to annoy us and taunt the fans... As if he ever has anything that means something well to say... [Bryan]-[Mimic]- Last week, I sat in the back and I watched what happened all night. I had a great deal of time to watch the show... Being that I had most of the night free until the main event. And what I saw disgusted me more than the people that love the Miami Dolphins!! I saw Royce Mathers wearing the XCWF Championship title... And, I may not be a pro-XCWF person. I despise the XCWF and it's owner, Tom Mason. I despise any person that supports the XCWF. But, there is one person I despise... Who isn't a member of the XCWF, and he happensto be a member of the WWCW Elite, and to be the XCWF Champion... ROYCE MATHERS!! The crowd boos at the sound of Royce's name and they listen as Mimic's voice becomes harsh and more serious than ever... [Mimic]- Royce Mathers is not a champion, he is a pawn for Sid! Ever since the day Royce Mathers showed back in the XCWF last month, he has been controlled by Sid. Everything he is told to do, he does. He is not a wrestler, he is a puppet!! A fraud!! Royce Mathers' deserves the XCWF Championship as much as the Miami Dolphins deserved to win the SuperBowl this year!!! Let's face it Royce Mathers does not deserve the XCWF Championship... If anyone does... IT'S ME!! And Royce, I am challenging you for the title this Friday on Fear!! [Mike Dennis]- Give me a break! Mimic thinks he deserves the XCWF Championship. I don't agree that Royce deserves to be the champion either, Zac Moore should still be the champion in my mind! But after he was screwed at The Summit... [Bryan]- I think that will be a great match!! Mimic against Royce Mathers... WWCW Elite agianst itself!! What is better than that!! [Mike Dennis]- I'll tell you what... Seeing someone like Zac Moore, Mark Estep, Kamikazi, Al Magz or even another XCWF star, someone more worthy become the champion! Mimic isn't even a XCWF wrestler, he was never to begin with! [Mimic]- Royce Mathers, what do you say? Are you going to accept my challenge?! Or am I going to have to hunt you down and beat a response out of you right this second?! What's it going to be... PUNK!! Mimic stands in the ring as the crowd is silent... There is no movement, no music or presence of Royce Mathers... Mimic drops the mircophone and climbs out of the ring as he starts to walk up the ramp and then he stops as "Da Rockwilder" by Red and Method Man hits the speakers. Royce Mathers steps out onto the stage as the crowd boos him as well. He has a micrphone and Mimic just watches as he has the XCWF Championship over his shoulder as he starts to talk... [Royce Mathers]- Well... Let's take things a little slow for you. First, why don't you climb back into that ring and pick up that microphone... Then I want you to STAND there and wait until I ask for you to speak! Mimic's eyes light with fire and he mouths words to Royce as Royce motions to him to get into the ring. Mimic gets in the ring and picks up the microphone as he waits, watching Royce... [Mike Dennis]- I can't believe these two... We have two of the most egotistical wrestlers out here right now and they are looking in total disgust at each other... [Royce Mathers]- Now... If I heard you right... You want a shot at the XCWF Championship this Friday night against 'Sensational' Royce Mathers... The man who is responsible for leading the WWCW against the XCWF. Sid has you wrapped around his finger like a tamer trains a lion... You're not tough, you're weak... I will prove it this Friday night!! [Royce Mathers]- This Friday eh? Well...
