Fear, 1 September 2002 — results

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: LIVE!! On TNN/Unlimited Television from FirStar Center in Cincinnati, Ohio... XCWF Saturday Night Fear!! :: The XCWF sadly apologizes for the miscommunication and problems that came with not airing this week's episode of Fear. Conditions beyond our control brought upon this, so I figured that for what was put out, a highlight show was owed, so here now are the highlighted events that made the Sep. 01 Fear a show to be put on, but just never got to flourish. So in the darkness of the FirStar Center in Cincinnati, here are the live show results. The show began before the opening credits, with TJ Faith and Mark Estep in the back, conversing over the recent change of power. Mark threatened TJ, by claiming that he is goign to have a time on his hand sharing power with Mark. TJ informs Mark that Tom did not hand the company over to Mark, because Mark is still the commissioner, and not the acting president, like TJ. Mark become infuriated and began to mock TJ, claiming that he should emphasize the acting, because that's all he will be doing, because Mark is really in charge. TJ didn't find it funny, and as his first act as the acting President, decided that he would make the main event for the night. Al Magz... defending the IU title... in a Lumberjack Match.... against Mark Estep!! TJ laughed it up and walked out, while Mark says that TJ has too much on his plate to handle, and he won't be able to get out of what's coming. Opening Credits, lead into the first match, being Johnny Reno vs. Bruce Blade vs. Chris Collins. Reno and Blade get to the ring first, and await the arrival of Collins. Collins comes out to the ring, but not in his normal gear, but in street clothes and a mic. He cuts his music and begins to rant about how he went to Unleashed, beat Jesse Ramey in perhaps the bloodiest, most brutal match the XCWF has ever seen in that barbed wire match and now he's being insulted by opening the card. He goes on to say, that Reno should be disappointed as well, because he came as far as going into three matches against Shawn Kain for the NEW Championship, and he's in the same predicament, while Kain has the night off. And Bruce, well he hasn't made it any higher than Extreme Division since his arrival many months ago. Collins says, that as a chant of rebellion, he refuses to attend the ring and battle this match, and Reno and Blade should also believe in his philosophy, that the XCWF has become too political, becoming a TJ Faith vs. Mark Estep program rather than the XCWF superstars themselves. Collins then asks Reno and Blade, where do they stand... do they battle it out one-on-one? Or do they walk out with Chris? Before long, the two men thought it over and it seems the the two are getting ready for the match. The ref goes to ring the bell, and Reno and Blade circle each other.. and as they go for what seems the tie-up, they both swerve and dive at the ref. Hitting the Blade Cutter and the All-Star Smash on him, and began to exit the ring to the boos of fans. They join Chris on stage, who is snickering, and he shakes hands with Reno and Blade, the three men hold hands in the air at the top of the ramp, before a swarm of security comes from the back and tackle the three men. Mike and Bryan have no idea what's going on, and the show is immediately cut to a commerical. The show comes back with TJ Faith in the ring, he tells the fans that since they are swindled from one match, he decided to put two new guys on the card for entertainment. He introduces Rokan Dalden, and then Shawn Jackson. The two battle it out for a good while, going in and out of the ring, until Rokan tried to hit a Top-Rope Body Splash on a standing Jackson, but Jackson managed to catch him across his shoulders and hit him with his Spinning Rack Faceplant DDT variation known as the "BronzeSlaught" gaining the win, and walking away from the ring with arms raised. In the back, after the match, TJ Faith is seen in the back, with Chris Collins standing before his desk, and a crowd of security behind him. TJ asks what possesed Chris to do such a thing? And Chris admits that this show has become political and not physical. Chris wants some more respect for himself and the wrestlers in the back, and he wants it tonight. TJ thinks and he tells Chris, that he can prove how well he can take on the competition, when he is one of the lumberjacks for tonights Main Event match. If he can take care of Estep, a change might be in order. Chris smiles and nods, and takes the deal. TJ lets him go, and he opens the door, being met by Tessa, before the door closes behind him. Tessa asks Chris if the deal is done. Chris says, it will be. Following that, we hit the ring, and then, something we didn't expect to occur did...[Following written by Chris Metal:] ‘Never Die’ by Creed blasts out from the speakers, and the fans rise in anticipation for the return of the hated-by-some, respected-by-others Chris Metal. [Mike Dennis] Hey! He’s back already? TJ told me he’d be out till Christmas! Damn it! [Bryan] Hey, leave off him! It shows that he’s in great physical shape to return from that kind of shoulder injury so quickly… Chris makes his way out from backstage onto the ramp. He is wearing his customary black pants with electric gold lines down the sides, but he has a bandage around his left shoulder. His manager, the infamous ever-present Victor Thompson, is by his side, wearing his usual grey jacket and black trousers. [Mike Dennis] God, don’t you just hate that smarmy old fogey?! [Bryan] How could you EVER describe Victor Thompson as SMARMY, of all things? Chris makes his way gingerly into the ring, still feeling the effects of his injured shoulder. Victor goes around the ring to collect a microphone from the timekeeper’s table and goes into the ring next to Chris. [Mike] Oh Christ, don’t tell me they’re gonna talk to us… ratings drop! ` [Victor Thompson] Yes, as you can see, we are back. A chant of ‘PAE-DO-VIC! PAE-DO-VIC!’ goes up around the arena. [Mike] Haha! That’s it! [Bryan] Shut up Mike. [Victor Thompson] Hey, hey, that’s not fair! Hear me out, hear me out. The chants die down a little. [Victor Thompson] Now, as you all know, two weeks ago on Fear, Chris’ shoulder was brutally injured at the hands of the one that calls himself… Darkside. Boos go up around the arena. [VT] Therefore, Chris’ Junior Heavyweight title was unfairly snatched away from him… as was the chance to compete at Unleashed, one of the biggest events of the year! [Crowd] Darkside sucks! Darkside sucks! Victor hands the mic to Chris. He addresses the crowd. [Chris Metal] Hey, all of you XCWF fans here in this fine arena… The crowd cheers. [CM] Well, I know we’ve had our differences in the past, like you lot not liking it when I used some, well, interesting tactics to capture my title… [Mike] Pff! Interesting tactics… he CHEATED! [Bryan] No, he was clever… but I don’t know why he’s selling out now… [CM] The point I’m getting to is, how would you like it if just because some half-assed sumbitch ripped up your shoulder, you lost one of your prized possessions – for me the Junior Heavyweight strap – just because of that one complete dicksmack you lost the chance to do something you’d been looking forward to for god damn AGES! For me, performing at one of the BIGGEST events of the year, XCWF Unleashed. The crowd’s chant of ‘Darkside sucks’ gets louder. [MD] I must concede, he has a point. [Bry] No he doesn’t! Jeez, I’m disappointed in him, selling out to the fans like this… [CM] So that one man, that complete ASSHOLE Darkside has deprived me of doing something I love to do. And does he regret it? Does he care? Does he heck. ‘DARKSIDE SUCKS! DARKSIDE SUCKS!’ [CM] You know, there’s something similar happening in England right now, though it’s kind of more high profile at the moment… Victor, fill them in… He hands the mike to Victor. [Bry] I’m confused, what’s he getting at? [Victor] Right, have any of you people heard of… Manchester United? A small cheer goes up. [Victor] Roy Keane? A smaller cheer. [Victor] Oh well, at least a few of you have. Well, two years ago, Roy Keane made a tackle on a Manchester City player who he’d been at odds with for ages, Alf Inge Haaland. Haaland was badly injured and is still out of soccer. And in Keane’s autobiography which was released this month, he admitted he did it on purpose. Haaland is sueing Keane. [MD] What? So… so Chris Metal is SUEING Darkside?! Just ‘cos he couldn’t compete at Unleashed and he lost his title? He can’t do that! Wrestlers get injured all the time! [Bryan] Haha, I was beginning to doubt Chris but now I understand… that’s spiced it up a bit, eh? I really do like this kid… A chant of ‘SUE! SUE! SUE!’ is getting up to rival the ‘Darkside sucks’. Chris and Victor are smiling throughout it. [Chris Metal] OK, haha, that was funny, but as you know I give jack about money. Geez, that was funny. Hearing Mike Dennis: (high voice) ‘he can’t do that! No, please don’t do it Chris, please…’ The crowd are laughing along. [Chris Metal] Of course I’m not sueing Darkside for money, hell I have enough money. But Darkside is going to pay. There’s a more satisfying way of getting revenge, and Darkside, you better watch out, because every minute, of every hour, of every day, I’ll be watching you, and Darkside: you will pay so much that you’re gonna wish you’d been sued instead. Darkside… I’ll be watching you. ‘Never Die’ by Creed hits and Chris and Victor leave. [Mike] I don’t get it… so he’s NOT sueing? [Bryan] Geez Mike you need to follow better. The women's number one contenders match, takes place following the commerical break, and the match seems to be going no where, as both women seem to gain a hand on the other in a short while. Soon enough, Victoria Vengeance comes storming to the ring, and she dives into the ring, and begins beating on both ladies. The ref calls for the bell, for the draw, and Victoria is relentless in her attack. She places Angie in a Texas Cloverleaf, and then tosses Alexia up against the steel steps and across her neck on the barricade. Victoria is restrained by upcoming officals, and she is kicking and screaming towards the back. In the back, Mark is takling with Phil about his IU title shot, and Phil's contendership match. Mark explains how that Phil might be battling him once again for the IU (IC) title, and that no hard feelings, but Mark won last time, and he's bound to win again. Phil shruggs him off, and just tells him that he is ready to just go out there and make an example out of Stryder, because he doesn't care about the IU, he just wants back what's his. Back in the ring, we get set for Crimson Lord vs. Scott Amazing, of the IoV. And the match turns out to be an awesome back and forth match, with Amazing managing to gain the upperhand, and catching Lord with a roll-up after his attempt to bring a bat into the ring. After the match, the massive Lord tries to pick the bat back up again, and beat up Amazing, but Amazing manages to duck his attempts until the IoV comes out and begins to hop on the back of Lord, minus Stryder, who is preparing. Burns hops in and chop blocks Crimson, causing him to drop the bat. Amazing picks it up and 3rd Degree Burns tells Duff to get a chair and bring it into the ring, as they proceed to beat down on Crimson more and more. Amazing using the bat and taking it to Crimson's midsection. Duff slides the chair in, and 3rd Degree applies it to Lord's leg. Duff brings another chair into the ring and slides it to Amazing. Burns goes to the second rope, as Amazing gets to the top rope with the chair in hand. He braces himself, and then he looks over at Burns, Burns nods as the fans go nuts, and Amazing and Burns simultaniously fly off the ropes!! Amazing drops an elbow drop with the chair across Lord's face, and Burns steps on the chair, practically breaking Lord's foot! Lord rolls about in pain, and Amazing gets up and proceeds to assault Lord with the bat if he begins to move. Burns asks for the mic and recieves it. He begins to brag about how the IoV are going to be innovating new things, and that this is only the beginning. They have destroyed a long time nemisis to prove to the world that the IoV is not goign to be stuck being known as threats to the tag team division, but to the world. Tonight is the night that it changes, and that IoV not only means Innovators of Violence, but the Inception of Viciousness...because a new wave will sweep through, and it will come crashing down on the XCWF! Burns drops the mic as he takes IoV along with him to the back, as paramedics come out to try and help Lord, who has been broken and decimated, then commerical. The show comes back, with Crimson being carried out, and Mike practically hallucinating. The music of Darkside hits, and he comes out on the ramp, ready for his XCWF JHW Match. He comes out as Crimson is going up. He makes his way pass the stretcher, but quickly turns about and pushes away the paramedics. He begins to pounce on Crimson! He then moves to the back, and pushes off paramedics trying to restrain him! Darkside is able to break away and he pushes the stretcher....RIGHT OFF THE STAGE! MY GOD!! CRIMSON HAS CRASHED TO THE FLOOR ATTACHED TO THE STRETCHER!! HE MUST BE IN TWISTED SHAPE! The medics rush back down, as the fans flow a "Holy Shit" chant with many boos. Darkside snickers and heads down to the ring with a sadistic look on his face, with his hands held high. Talus then comes racing from the back, without music and pounces on Darkside for what he had done to his friend. The two go out at it, until Talus is able to capture the victory when Chris Metal manages to sneak into the ring and ring Darkside's bell with a steel chair and sneaks out, and Talus plops on for the pin. Talus holds his title high, and Chris reenters and tries to battle it out with Darkside. Talus stands there and tries to elave the ring, but is attacked by Scotty Amazing, the runner up in the JHW Battle Royal, and battling up and down the ramp, until they are broken up by the officals and sent to commericals. TJ is in back and he wndering the halls, and busts into the IoV locker room, asking what the hell that was out there, that he has no control over the show now, and that the IoV has just run amok and crossed the line tonight. They wiped a member clean from the roster, and he might never wrestle again. Burns steps up face to face to TJ, and begins to talk about how it was well worth it, how Crimson had no place in the XCWF anymore, anyways. He was taking up space that has been blocking the path of the IoV since the Inception.. The IoV have been stuck facing him over and over, and if he wasn't around, there was no need to be stuck in that position. TJ rebuttles and begins to tell him that he better watch his back, because he might still be stuck in a hell of a spot himself, under TJ's skin, and that isn't somewhere he wants to be. With a show, and a faction, he wouldn't want to gamble all that... and that he will give him time to get it together, or else, the IoV will be MIA...I don't want you anywhere out there in the IU Title Shot Match, you hear, and walks away. Phil McCann and Stryder head out to the ring, Stryder already there, watching TJ taunt the IoV. The two men battle it out and go back and forth, Stryder seeming to gain an upperhand, until for the umpteenth time tonight, a match is interupted, with Johnny Reno coming out to ringside and beating on both men. Reno, after walking out tonight, is leaving another mark in this match, and beats on the men until the ref calls for another draw. Reno stands in the middle of the ring, as Evan Darkness walks out on the ramp and just stands there staring at the ring. Chris Collins is in the back looking on the monitor and sees Reno's carnage and begins to talk, "The plan is in effect." He turns around to Tessa, and she nods, and heads down the hall into the locker room. She opens the door and yells out..."Plan B!"...She comes back and the camera focuses on Chris snicker as the camera fades out to commerical. The camera comes back from commerical, for the main event. The ring is surrounded by the lumberjacks, Shawn Kain, Colby Newsome, Menace, Phil McCann, Jesse Ramey, Chris Collins, Tessa Windsor, Johnny Reno, Bruce Blade, 3rd Degree Burns, Stryder, Chris Metal, Shawn Jackson, Talus, Darkside, Scotty Amazing amongst other. Al and Mark come out to the ring, and have a match that involves much throwing out and jumping of. The mix ups between some rivals occur as well. Near the closing of the match, Mark is about to hit the Estepper on Magz, but Chris Collins jumps into the ring and manages to get Estep off of Magz and gives him the Collins Crusher. Magz falls to his knees, and Chris extends his hand and helps him up....but only to pull him in and give him the Collins Crusher as well!! Menace jumps into the ring and tries to tackle Collins, but Reno and Blade run into the ring and tackle Menace and beat him down. The rest of the lumberjacks file into the ring and begin to have one massive brawl, bodies flying everywhere!! Magz manages to crawl out of the ring and rest on the side, until the ring is only containing Reno, Blade, and Collins... Collins grabs the mic, and he tells Reno and Blade to hold up Mark. Collins bashes the XCWF and its officals and berates them like eariler in the night, and begins to tell them that its time to get some justification, and some respect.. its time.....for a STRIKE!! The fans go nuts and Collins drops the mic and runs back intot he ropes...Al is there and he manages to pull down the rope, and Chris falls out of the ring! Al jumps in and hits Blade out of the ring with the IU title, and Reno runs at Al, Al swings, but Reno ducks it and into the ropes. Reno comes back and...MAGZIMUM IMPACT!! Al gets up and he begins to point down and yell at Reno, but WAIT!! ITS EVAN!! Evan jumps into the ring, and he turns Al around, grips the neck and ...ACHLUOPHOBIA!! Evan drops Al with viciousness, and leaves the ring just as quickly as he got in... Chris stumbles to his feet.. as Mark is holding himself against the ropes getting up. Evan stands on the ramp once more staring down. Mark looks at him squinted eyed, while Collins looks at both sides, at Evan and Estep. Burns pulls himself up and drags on the apron as Evan walks into the back, and Collins smirks. The shot focuses on TJ in the back, dropping things off his desk, in fury, and the final shot of TJ with a furious look on his face as the copyright shows and ends the show. XCWF Redemption! Live on Sunday September 29th, 2002 from Montreal!!
