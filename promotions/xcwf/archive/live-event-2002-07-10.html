Live Event, 10 July 2002 — results

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XCWF Live :: LIVE! Wednesday Night in front of a live crowd from the Hilton Coliseum in Ames, Iowa!! :: This week's house show kicked off from Ames, Iowa in the Hilton Coliseum. A near sellout crowd was on hand for the event as some of the XCWF's upcoming talent are booked in matches for the night. Our ring announcer started us off with the "Star Spangled Banner" and then the fans were ready and roaring to go for tonight's festivities. The first contest for the night involved Evan Latear and Jimbo Trimmins. It was a hard fought match up that ended in a no contest. The second match of the evening was quite a battle. This match involved Johnny Reno and Darkside. The match started off fairly even. Darkside then came out flying. He whipped Darkside into the ropes and on return gave him a powerful spin buster in the middle of the squared circle. He couldn't seem to keep Darkside down with that powerful move. Reno became fairly frustrated about the fact that Darkside kicked out of such a powerful move. Reno quickly went back after him. He had Darkside trapped in the corner and was rapidly giving him shoulder blocks into the abdomen area. He climbs the turnbuckle and hits Darkside with a tremendous tornado DDT into the canvas with his head driving into the ground. Reno begins to start slowing down after this combination of moves. He has went as far as he possibly can. He has become sluggish. Darkside doesn't for a second hesitate on this opportunity. He lunges after the tired Reno and begins to take control of the match. Reno charges after him and Darkside manages to pull off a DDT. Reno lays on the mat stunned. Darkside goes up onto the top turnbuckle and signals he is going high. He attempts the shooting star press but Reno rolls away just in time. The hopes of Darkside to win this match have just began to slip away because Reno seems to have regained his wind. He grabs Darkside by the hair and lifts him to his feet. Right when he thinks he's regained control Darkside starts to punch his way through Reno. He's risen to his feet now. Reno and Darkside are face to face. Darkside kicks him in the gut and gives him his finishing move! Darkside had the leg hooked for the 1...2...3! He had beaten Johnny Reno as the referee slapped the mat for the three! The third contest of the night was for the Junior Heavyweight Title. JC Valerio was put up into a title defense situation against Chris Metal. As usual this was a very entertaining match put on by the two of them. The contest started off with Chris Metal in high hopes of wining the Junior Heavyweight Title. He had just started to take control after a missing frog splash by JC Valerio. Immediately following Metal was swarming after JC. He was anxious to finish this match. He grabbed Valerio and lifted him to his feet. Metal flung himself off the ropes and came back drilling JC Valerio in the neck area with a clothesline. Metal again bounced off the ropes this time returning with a huge rolling thunder on top of the champion JC Valerio. He went for the cover and by about a split second almost one the title. That wouldn't stop Metal from taking his offense to JC however. JC charged him and Metal gave Valerio a giant back body drop to the outside of the ring! Valerio landed right on his back absorbing the fall. A minute or so later Valerio was attempting to rise to his feet. Chris Metal was on the inside of the ring. The instant that Valerio was on his feet Metal spring boarded himself off the top rope in hopes of hitting JC with a drop kick. JC however saw this in the corner of his eye and made his best effort to move out of the way. By the time he started to move he got nailed in the back of the knee by the drop kick. Both men were lying down on the concrete. Valerio is the first man to rise to his feet. He grabs Metal and rolls him into the ring. This is Valerio's opportunity to retain his title. Can he do it? JC has decided to climb onto the top turnbuckle. He launches himself off and nails Metal with a frog splash dead center in the stomach. He scores the 1...2...3 to retain his championship. Next up was The Naturals fighting up against the Nation. Jordan Law and Ian Chase were facing Crimson Lord and Mort. This was a strange bout. Jordan Law and Ian Chase are both powerhouses while Crimson Lord and Mort are giants. The way this bout went was back and forth between the two teams. The Naturals had full advantage off the start when they used the strategy of grounding Crimson Lord in their corner. Crimson was stuck in between the turnbuckle and Ian Chase. He was viciously hammering on the big man as the crowd cheered after every blow that was delivered. Law was then tagged in and did quite a number on the big man. Right when he thought he had total control over the big man and when he thought he had become helpless Crimson lord made a comeback. Law failed to give him a Gorilla Press Slam. Lord capitalized by giving him an upper cut right into the jaw. Lord managed to reach his corner and tag in Mort. Law thought he could get a tag in also but Mort grabbed him by the foot, The match had continued on and on of punching back and forth. Once Mort had knocked Law down he attempted a quick pin. Unfortunately for him Ian came in and broke the pin. Crimson Lord then came in and tossed Ian to the outside. They began brawling on the outside. Inside of the ring Mort has taken total control. Law has no offense moves put in recently. Mort has him set up for the big finish. The rigor mortis is perfected! Mort has the opportunity to put him away. He's about to cover Law when 3rd Degree Burns and Stryder enter the ring. They first hit Law so that Mort and Crimson Lord are disqualified. They then begin to beat down on Mort while Lord is stranded on the outside trying to beat down Ian. Finally Mort has taken enough of a beating and they let him be. Crimson Lord is left on the outside demolished by Ian. What’s a night with out Women’s action? Well, it’s dull if you’re a guy. So you can bet that the next contest really proved to be a winner, despite a couple low spots. Tasha Evans, the XCWF Women’s Champion taking on Victoria Vengeance, the title was not on the line in this match. It started off with a little smack talking back and forth. Vengeance looked to take control with a bulldog, by Tasha managed to hold on and drop Victoria back into a nice suplex! Victoria and Tasha would lock up and battle around with hair pull spins and a couple forearms to the face here and there. As the match continued, Tasha looked to hit a DDT… Countered and Victoria took her down to the mat! It was an all out brawl in the middle of the ring, both ladies rolling around pulling their hairs. A great site for our male audience while at the same time, it was a low spot. But the girls tried to make up for it. Victoria Vengeance had a look of vengeance in her eye (no pun intended) as she set up herself on the top rope for a hurricanranna… Off the top and she nailed it! Sadly to say for her, it was not enough to win! Tasha managed to kick out and the champion would not be outdone, at least not yet. She tried for a come back, avoiding a suplex and then scoring with a huge DDT on the challenger! Would it be enough? No! Again, Victoria would kick out. The two of them trying to give all they could to the fans. Victoria fought back and knocked down Tasha with a nice dropkick. She headed up the ropes, looking for mismanaged, but missed! She hurt herself pretty bad with that miss and now it was Tasha Evans to take advantage… Twist of Faith! The pin and the victory both scored at once for the XCWF Women’s Champion! The upcoming contest was between Vegas and Doe. They both are pretty new stars to the XCWF and certainly put on one hell of a show for the audience. The match had been lot's of back and forth punching from side to side of the ring. Doe had manage to grab onto Vegas with a few choke holds. Vegas kept getting out of them because of the referee making Doe let go so he wasn't disqualified from the match. After the choke holds Vegas was pretty winded but style continued with his cruiserweight style against John Doe's brawling style. It was a good combination for a match. Vegas had finally gotten control for a little part of the match. He went up top and hit the awaiting Doe in the face with a missile drop kick. It was quite effective because there was no movement for a good percentage of the time. Vegas then went to Doe and decided he needed to be worn down a little bit. So he gave Doe a sleeper hold that became very tiring. The audience didn't react to this hold too well and began to boo them because there was a lack of entertainment. This is where Vegas clued into pulling out the big guns. The big finish so to speak. He attempted to give Doe a hurricanranna but was power bombed on the spot rolled up into a pin. It just wasn't enough though to keep the high flier down. Doe started to hand out a beating to Vegas with clotheslines, body slams, pile drivers and all of those big moves. You name it he did it. Doe decided the match should be over so he pinned Vegas but came up short. When Doe was arguing with the referee about the pin Vegas managed to somehow give Doe a school boy and end the match with the three count. Stryder and Scott Styles were next into the ring, the in ring debut for the young HVW World Champion. He came out second as Stryder was in the ring. Styles held a microphone in hand as he stood at the foot of the ring. He started by with a little diss to the fans, saying that all Iowa was good for was corn. No one cared much for that comment. Styles went on to say that the XCWF should be bowing to him and in awe of such a man as he, the HVW World Champion, undefeated as he added. Stryder grabbed a microphone of his own and told Styles that he would give him an unpleasant welcoming to the business. With that, the match began as the fans really got into it. The two men brawled in the middle of the ring, Stryder taking the advantage over Styles in the opening minutes of the match. The XCWF veteran took it to Scott in the corner of the ring. The rights and lefts exchanged. Then he went to work with clotheslines, clobbering the upstart from corner to corner. An elbow caught Stryder off guard and Styles went to work. In moments Stryder was barely reeling and Styles took him down with a nice German suplex! A near fall with the pin but Stryder managed to free himself. Back to work against each other they went. Stryder ducked a clothesline and grabbed Scott… A neckbreaker! The pin was close, but this time it was Scott kicking out! The fans got into it as the big man, Stryder, took it to the top rope. He came off, but there was Scott to nail him with a hard dropkick to the abdomen as he came down! Both men were reeling on the mat as this great contest dragged on. As the moments went by, Stryder tried to take control. He aimed for a boot, missed it and came back off the ropes. As he returned he walked into a spinebuster from the challenger! Down for the count… Nearly but Stryder got out. Scott picked him up and got him set up for the Lights Out, avoided by Stryder! He countered into a Tiger Suplex and took down Styles! Yet another close fall and Scott stayed alive in this match up. Stryder got to his feet, looking to climb to the top and nail his frog splash. He was set, but it was a drastic move by Scott to push the referee into the ropes! Styles headed up the turnbuckles and looked to set up Stryder for a superplex… Counter and Stryder knocked him down with a face crusher! Off the top, nailing a cross body on the challenger!! Stryder looked to have the match under control. Slowly Styles rose to his feet… WHACK! A huge standing side kick by Stryder knocked him down and allowed the three count to be made as one half of Big ‘n’ Bad picked up a victory! The main event of this house show was for the InterUnited championship. Chris Collins has the chance to face the champion Menace in a regular singles bout. He had come down to the ring with Tessa Windsor by his side. Menace was alone as usual. This sure was the highlight of the night. This match is what all the fans came for. It had started off as a regular wrestling match that suddenly turned ugly when Chris Collins hit Menace in the head with a steal chair when the referee was distracted by Tessa Windsor and her good looks. Collins tried to pin Menace but this wasn't the way to win the match so Menace kicked out with ease. Menace then decided this match had become a war. He pulled out all the stops on Chris Collins. First he tossed Collins to the outside. He rolled out of the ring and began punishing Collins with right-handed punches to the face. Collins was trapped in the barricade. Menace lifted Collins onto his shoulder and slammed his back over the top of the steel. Collins landed in the crowd and tried to run away and escape. Menace grabbed him by the shirt and turned him around. He tossed Collins back over the steel barricade and made sure he paid his dues. Collins was helplessly rolled into the ring by Menace. The chair was still sitting in the middle of the ring, which the referee still hadn't noticed. Now it is time for Chris Collins to get in his offensive attempts. Menace was still out powering him when Collins caught a lucky break and gave Menace a low blow with the referee once again distracted. He tried to roll Menace up into an inside cradle and only made it to 2 and a half. Menace thought enough was enough and he grabbed the steel chair. The referee tried to stop him from using it but moved out of the way once he was in full swing. CRACK! Menace drilled Collins with the chair. The referee called for the bell and Menace retained the title by DQ! The show closed as Menace got into the face of Chris Collins, talking some smack until finally Psycho Kid came out from the back, looking to gain an upper hand on the InterUnited Champion tonight in preparation for their big match up on Fear this coming Saturday! Menace didn’t see it coming and walked into an assault from Kid. Off the ropes he sent Menace… BIG BOOT! Down went the IU Champion and then he was reeling as he got up. Psycho Kid finished him off with the Psycho Bomb in the middle of the ring, much to the fans’ approval. Together, Chris Collins and Psycho Kid stood in the ring, hands raised as the fans cheered and the show ended. XCWF UncoNS2XionL!! Live in eleven days at 8 p.m. only on PPV! What a night it should be with the XCWF World Championship, PTC Extreme Championship and more all on the line! And don't forget THREE Survivor matches!! WARNING!!!
