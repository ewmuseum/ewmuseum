:: LIVE!! On TNN/Unlimited Television from the Brown County Expo Center in Green Bay, Wisconsin... XCWF Saturday Night Fear!! :: The screen opens to the New TNN Logo screen and for some of you, those with satellite and Primetime Central TV the Channel 14: Unlimited Television’s logo comes up. Either way, “Up Next: XCWF Fear” is on the screen. The camera cuts and out of no where “It’s Going Down” by the X-Ecutioners and Linkin Park cuts in and the XCWF Logo flashes up and then is stamped over with the number 2002… The XCWF Fear video plays through as the many faces of the XCWF flash on the screen… Mark Estep, Zac Moore, Al Magz, Stryder, Mimic, Duff, Menace, all among others... The sounds of the fireworks fill the arena as the crowd cheers and yells. The signs are held high up in the air and the fans are ecstatic. The camera circles the arena and out of no where “Princes of the Universe” by Queen hits. What a way to start off Fear but with the Number one contender and suspected XCWF Owner, Mimic coming to the ring! Mimic goes down the ramp followed by Menace, Bruce Blade, C-Man and Evan Darkness. The B.o.D. stands in the ring, as Mimic would begin to address the crowd. He talks about how last week he became the XCWF Owner because Tom Mason was stupid enough to sign away his company. So, he said we’re going to change the name to something a little bit more suitable. And that name was voted on by the new XCWF Executive Board, The B.o.D. They decided that Untitled would be titled, in fact it would be called B.o.D. Hostile Takeover!! The fans didn’t like the idea and responded with harsh criticism and boos. At that point, TJ Faith had heard enough! The XCWF Vice President made his way out and started to critique Mimic’s ownership skills. He said there was no way that Untitled would be changed to “Hostile Takeover”. There had been too much money put into promoting Untitled already and they even had logos, commercials, ads, and the whole works. He owned the XCWF meaning he had the power to do what he wanted, when he wanted! Just because TJ wanted to whine, doesn’t make Mimic stop and feel sorry. At Hostile Takeover, the real B.o.D. would begin. The B.o.D. under Mimic’s reign as the Owner and World Champion of the XCWF! And that he has a little surprise for everyone at Hostile Takeover as well. But in the meantime, Mimic said tonight’s main event has been booked. TJ Faith said that it wasn’t to his knowledge. Mimic smirked telling TJ Faith not to interrupt him when he was just going to make the match! And that would be between Menace and Al Magz, both the Intercontinental Championship and World Heavyweight Championship on the line… IN A CAGE!! Mimic pointed to the ceiling of the Brown County Expo Center as a cage was suspended overhead!
