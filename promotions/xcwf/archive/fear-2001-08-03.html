He gets in and awaits his friend and opponent for tonight… XCWF World Title Tournament Anywhere Falls Match Aaron Jacobs vs. Royce Mathers "When You Fuck With The Best, You Die Like The Rest...!" appears on the XCWF-Tron in big bold white letters, with a hint of gray shadowing. The fans look at the entrance way in anticipation as "Paparazi" by Xzibit blares over the PA and throughout the arena. Pyro goes off at each corner of the ramp as "Sensational" Royce Mathers steps out onto the ramp and is met with the deafening noise of the crowd. A big smile appears over his face as he begins to make his way down the ramp. Many of the fans are chanting "Royce...Royce...Royce", as he steps onto the apron and looks over the crowd. He then enters the ring and hops onto the closest middle turnbuckle as he flashes his hands into the air. He jumps down and looks at Aaron. The referee calls for the bell and these two men lock up, Aaron comes out with Royce in a headlock. Royce quickly escapes and comes back off the ropes with a flying clothesline to his buddy. Aaron is down and Royce leaves the ring to stock up on some ammunition… He pulls out a table, a couple chairs, a ladder, and a trash can! Talk about being prepared! Royce throws some of the crap in the ring and then slides the table in. He gets in and picks up the chair. By this time Aaron is up though and he kicks Royce in the gut, Royce drops the chair. Aaron hits neckbreaker on Royce and he goes down! Aaron grabs the chair and bangs it on the mat, waiting for Royce to get up… Royce gets up and he ducks the shot and connects with a drop kick! Aaron goes down and Royce picks him up, whipping him into the corner and then grabbing a chair. He goes over to the corner and goes to hit Aaron with the chair but Aaron escapes just in time! Aaron slides out of the ring and takes the ladder as Royce comes at him. Aaron swings the ladder at Royce and Royce yells “What are you nuts?!” Aaron laughs and slides the ladder into the ring. But Royce meets him with the chair and cracks him over the head with it!! Aaron goes down and Royce picks up his fallen body. He tosses him out of the ring and goes after him. He covers Aaron for the pin…1…2…NO! Aaron kicks out! Aaron is lifted to his feet by Royce and Royce attempts to punch him.. Blocked by Aaron and Aaron delivers a punch! Now Royce goes to whip Aaron into the barricade but Aaron reverses that and sends Royce into the barricade! Royce goes down and Aaron hops the barricade and goes into the crowd! He steals some guy’s chair and lifts it up… CRACK! Across the skull of Royce Mathers!! Royce goes back down and Aaron sits down, giving Royce the chance to wake up. Royce starts to come to and Aaron lifts him up from the floor, he starts to drag him through the crowd. Then Royce hits him with a fist… Aaron hits Royce with a fist, and it goes back and forth! Then they reach the doors to the concession stands… Aaron whips Royce into the doors and he goes down. The cover by Aaron…1…2…NO!! Royce kicks out! Aaron is shocked and picks Royce back up. He opens the door to the main lobby and they are battling as fans are out there cheering and booing. Royce blocks Aaron’s attempted whip into the wall and counters it with a body slam! Aaron is down and holding his back and Royce sees a broom. He goes and grabs the broom taking it back over to Aaron and breaking it over Aaron’s head!! Aaron goes down and Royce looks for something else now… He sees a table with drinks on it… He picks up Aaron and drags him over to the table. He takes a drink out of a cup and then splashes one in Aaron’s eyes… IT’S WARM COFFEE!! Aaron yells and Royce says “Oops…” as he picks Aaron up on the table… THE FLATLINER!!! IT’S OVER FOLKS!! Royce makes the cover on his friend and then the referee counts…1…2…3… It’s over!! [Chris Cook]- Well, tough luck for Aaron Jacobs!! Royce seemed to have accidentally scorched his eyes with hot coffee! I wish someone would do the same to him..OHH WAIT!! Aaron Jacobs has come to and just splashed coffee in Royce’s eyes!!! [Predator]- They look like a bunch of two year olds! Anyway, they’re even now! And it looks like on Sunday it is Royce Mathers facing his good friend Mark Blaze! An interesting yet Canadian favored match up! [Chris Cook]- Well, that will do it for us tonight on Fear!! Stay tuned for your local news. And don’t forget to catch ResurreXtion on Sunday only on Pay-Per-View!! For Predator and the rest of the XCWF… WE’LL SEE YOU SUNDAY!!!
