Calendar Date: 06/05/2002 Time: 4:45 p.m. Venue: Peoria Civic Center City/State: Peoria, Illinois Standard Match Twista and Nikos Zion fight to a no contest. Women's Match Special Referee: Tasha Evans Tessa Windsor defeats Victoria Vengenace thanks to a cheap roll up using the ropes and a quick pin from Tasha Evans. XCWF United States Title Rage is successful in defending his title against Evan Darkness and scores a pinfall even as Bruce Blade tried to interfere. Tag Team Match Mort and Crimson Lord defeat Duff and 66. 66 and Duff have a little arguement and 66 walks off leaving Duff in bewilder. Special Challenge JC Valerio defeats Shawn Boyd with a huge moonsault off the top rope. XCWF Extreme Title Destroyer defeats Curt Rock to retain his title in a nice hardcore match. XCWF Intercontinental Championship Benny Bodacious is defeated by Menace as he attempted to capture the IC title. Menace hit the Renegade for the win. Tag Team Match Al Magz and Jason Smith defeat Mimic and Bruce Blade thanks to a disqualification as C-Man tried to help Mimic win the match up. :: Back to XCWF.tk ::
