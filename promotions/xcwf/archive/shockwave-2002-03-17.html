XCWF Shockwave :: LIVE! Sunday Night on TNN at 8 p.m. XCWF ShockWave from the St. Paul Arena in St. Paul, Minnesota!!! :: The camera starts at the TNN “Up Next: XCWF Shockwave” screen and then cuts to black. “It's Going Down” by the X-Ecutioners and Linkin Park, the official song of the XCWF starts to blare and the camera shows the inside of the St. Paul Arena as the fans are cheering and signs are waving in the air. A couple pyros shoot off the stage area and the crowd cheers as the camera pans around the commentator’s desk where Bryan Kaminski sits along side this week’s guest, Mike Dennis… [Mike Dennis]- Welcome to XCWF Shockwave! I am Mike Dennis, back on Shockwave for the first time in weeks here on TNN with Bryan Kaminski. And what a week in XCWF action we have had! Total HaVok II is in just three weeks and tomorrow marks the XCWF’s One Year anniversary! [Bryan]- Ah, yes Mike! And what a year in the XCWF it has been! We’ve witnessed more in this year than more federations have in their whole existence! [Mike Dennis]- Yes, a great year it has been Mike. And I think we’ll just see an even better second year!! Total HaVok II brings us even more than the first one did! So far we’ve heard of four matches, including a somewhat ‘double main event’ with the Circuit and the XCWF Championship and Intercontinental Titles on the line! This is without a doubt going to be one unforgettable XCWF event! [Bryan]- Without a doubt Mike! I cannot wait to see Tasha Evans, Leslie Dupnik and Victoria Vengeance in a Strip Tease Match!! My God! It’s going to be great!! [Mike Dennis]- Somehow it doesn’t surprise me you’d be interested in that. But I am interested as well, the same as I am interested in watching the rest of the Pay-Per-View… Everything from the World Championship and Intercontinental Championship match all the way to the Circuit! And speaking of the Circuit there are more entries for this huge match! Recently signed are: Benny Bodacious and Shawn Boyd, Jason Smith and more lower card superstars! And that will take us to a total of 12 entries for right now! [Bryan]- Mike, I heard rumor of a special stipulation for the Circuit besides it being 30 men over the top rope… But I cannot be sure. [Mike Dennis]- Well, this Friday night it could come to us from San Antonio, Texas! And I know that this Friday we will see some footage from a court trial that takes place this Wednesday in Florida as Tom Mason and Mimic could face charges for the assault on Zac Moore! This should be interesting! [Bryan]- I don’t like that Court thing… What will happen if Tom Mason and Mimic end up in jail?! The XCWF could be ruined!! [Mike Dennis]- Well, the XCWF would probably run better and not have as many sick happenings. Anyway, we have some action to get to here on Shockwave… "My Way by Limp Bizkit starts up. The fans seem to be baffled they then see the man they saw one before, Luminous Legend. He comes to the top of the ramp and stands there. Then this fiery black mist comes up behind him and a giant explosion erupts the fans are going crazy. He comes down to the ring and stands in the center. Then a white mist surrounds the ring and another explosion comes this time from the turnbuckle. [Bryan]- This match is going to be pretty bad Mike… I just have that feeling… [Mike Dennis]- Anything that doesn’t involve women wrestling is bad to you… Jazzie Jeff’s music hits and he makes his way out from the back and down the ramp towards the ring as the fans watch on… Standard Match Luminous Legend vs. Jazzie Jeff Luminous Legend gets nailed with a charging axhandle bodyblock from Jazzie Jeff.
