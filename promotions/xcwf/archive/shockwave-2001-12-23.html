ShockWave, 23 December 2001 — results

23 December 2001

:: LIVE! Sunday Night on TNN at 8 p.m. XCWF ShockWave from the Metrodome in Minneapolis, Minnesota!!! :: The camera starts at the TNN “Up Next: XCWF Shockwave” screen and then cuts to black. “Chop Suey” the official song of the XCWF starts to blare and the camera shows the inside of the Metrodome as the fans are cheering and signs are waving in the air. A couple pyros shoot off the stage area and the crowd cheers as the camera pans around the commentator’s desk where Bryan Kaminski sits alone… [Bryan]- Welcome to Shockwave! And although I lost my job on Fear, I have gained this position as for the absence of Rich Davies! But welcome to Shockwave and we are just one week away from XCWF: The Summit live on Pay-Per-View! Tonight, some XCWF and WWCW athletes will be in the ring, and tonight’s main event is for the Women’s Title! Victoria Vengeance defends the title against Sedusa! Let’s get right into the action! Standard Match Johnny Reno vs. Shadow Johnny Reno comes out to his music as pyros go off behind him. He steps down from the ramp and heads to the ring. He enters the ring area and walks around flexing, getting warmed up for his match. Just then, Shadows music plays as he runs down to the ring, anxious for it to start. He slides in the ring and the bell sounds as the match starts. They start in a grapple and move their way around the ring. Shadow gets an Irish whip off and catches Johnny Reno and flips him over his back. Johnny lands and goes for the Fisherman’s suplex. After two hits he lets go and continues his assault on Shadow. He starts with some punches and they make their way to the corner. Johnny holds up his hand and SLAPS Shadow over the chest which makes one loud sound. Johnny does it once more and Irish whips him into the other corner. Johnny runs and jumps on the dazed Shadow, but Shadow quickly dodges it as Johnny hits the top ring post. Shadow runs and smacks him with a good hard clothesline as Johnny hits the ground hard. He gets back up again as Shadow kicks him in the gut and gives him a Piledriver! He picks him back up once again and throws him into the ropes. Shadow runs to the other ropes and looks a if he’s going to clothesline him but Johnny reverses it and it’s a Double Clothesline! Both men lay on the ground and lay motion less. The Ref counts to 6 as they start to get up. Shadow is the first to get up and gives Johnny a goo hard elbow to the face! Johnny gives him one of his own! Shadow spins around and Johnny goes for the schoolboy! The count …1.... 2...NO! Kickout by Shadow! Reno waits as Shadow gets up and kicks him in the gut. Shadow is hunched over and Reno drops him in a big neckbreaker! Reno gets up and climbs the turnbuckles and he comes off for a big elbow drop… NO!! Shadow moves out of the way and Reno crashes into the canvas hard! Reno is slow getting up and Shadow takes him by the bad arm and yanks on it. Reno yells in the pain and Shadow now wrenches the arm… Shadow let’s go of the arm and kicks Johnny in the gut… BIG DDT BY SHADOW!! Shadow slowly makes his way for the cover… 1…2… WAIT! What’s this some woman has climbed into the ring and distracted the referee!! It looks like Marie! Marie is the ex-girlfriend to Johnny Fame, and former girlfriend and manager of Johnny Reno!! Shadow gets up and yells at the referee as Marie leaves the ring and now Johnny Reno is up! Shadow turns around… ALL STAR SMASH BY JOHNNY RENO ON SHADOW!! The cover…1…2…3… [Bryan]- That didn’t seem to be like the match I would have hoped for! No matter. We have a few more matches coming up but first here’s a commercial from our sponsors! ***Commercial Break*** The camera fades into an XCWF: The Summit promo! The picture of the blackened pyramid in the background with a glowing shine on the top as Royce Mathers and Zac Moore are on opposite sides. The XCWF Championship over Zac’s shoulder! [Bryan]- XCWF The Summit is live next Sunday on Pay-Per-View!! Seven matches signed for the event! The huge main event, perhaps the biggest in XCWF history… Royce Mathers returns and faces the XCWF Champion himself, Zac Moore! They will battle in a “Summit Match”… The rules are simple… Anything goes and the only way to win is to climb the XCWF Tron to reach the belt hanging on the top! This could end someone’s career at The Summit! Standard Match Benny Bodacious vs. Luminous Legend Machine Head" by Bush thrusts over the speakers in the arena as yellow smog fills the enterance ramp. Suddenly, a figure stands in the way. The yellow smog clears and Benjamin Bodacious is seen standing there, waving his Crystal Pepsi in the air as he runs down the ramp to the ring. The crowd is on its feet as Benny graces the ring. Benny jumps in the ring, bounces back and forth off the ropes and then awaits the match. [Bryan]- Well, Benny looks ready for this match. Just last week he and Shawn Boyd lost their chances in the Tag Team Tournament when they suffered a loss. But Benny hopes to go somewhere in singles standings, as Shawn is already successful. He is the Junior Heavyweight Champion and you can bet Benny would like a title of his own! Legend’s music hits and the Luminous Legend himself makes his way out from the back. Blue and white pyros shoot off as he makes his way down the ramp to cheers and climbs into the ring. HE circles around and gets ready for the match…The two go at it throwing punches left and right. Luminous backs him into the corner and starts kicking him in the chest. Benny tries to fight back with a elbow to the side of the head but Luminous just shrugs it off and keeps kicking. After ten, twenty kicks to the chest, Luminous throws him from the turnbuckle and goes up top! Luminous sits there waiting for him to get up and turn around. Benny gets up and stumbles over to the ropes. Luminous falls and hits himself on the top turnbuckle. Benny regains his balance and goes over to him and throws him over himself onto his back! Benny runs over and gives him a flying elbow to the chest. He grabs his legs and gives him a kicks him in the groin. Luminous grabs his groin and rolls around. Benny starts kicking him and kicking as Luminous is rolling around. Benny Picks him up by the head and throws him into the ropes and puts the sleeper hold on him. Luminous goes "out" for a second and regroups. He gives him a elbow in the stomach a few times. Benny lets go as Luminous goes for the Neck Breaker. He rolls him up. 1... 2.... No! Kickout by Benny! Benny gets back to his feet and Legend trips him up with a chop block! Legend now stomps away on Benny as he rolls out of the ring. Benny stumbles around the outside and Legend meets up and hits him in the forehead!! Benny goes down and Legend kicks him and the crowd is cheering. Legend picks up Benny and whips him right into the steel steps!! The steps dislodge and Benny goes down on the ground with a thud! Legend walks to Benny as he lays there holding his back in the pain… the referee is nearing five on his count and it keeps going… 6… Benny is dropped on the barricade by Legend and Benny collapses to floor…8… Legend kicks Benny as he lays on the ground and then he picks him up and rolls him into the ring.. But the referee signals for the bell and disqualifies both men!! Legend can’t believe it but he shakes his head as he makes his way up the ramp. In the ring Benny nurses his injuries and slowly gets back up… [Bryan]- Luminous Legend focused too much on beating on Benny that time and it cost him the match, when he probably could have easily won the match! We’ll be back with more action… Jason Smith goes one on one with Shannon York when we get back! *** Commercial Break *** The camera opens up backstage to the Owner’s room and the door opens and in walks… Johnny Reno. Reno has a look of anger on his face as he marches up to the desk of Tom Mason… [Tom Mason]- Well, Mr. Reno… Haven’t we learned to knock? I mean, you’re what 25 years old and you’ve worked for me over a year now… You should know by now. [Reno]- Tom, shut your hole for a minute! I am tired of your ass trying to push me around… I said it the other day, I won’t put up with it anymore! Last week you booked me on Fear and now this week I am on Shockwave… What the hell is with that? [Tom Mason]- John, I just thought that you’d be happy with a match. Seeing how Fear was all filled up last Friday I thought you take a spot where you could get one… [Reno]- Well… You thought wrong, Tom. [Tom Mason]- I did what? [Reno]- You heard me… You thought wrong! And now I think you owe enough to make it up to me! So, I am not requesting a match at The Summit… I am ORDERING you to give me a match at The Summit next Sunday on Pay-Per-View! The games are over and someone needs to pay for your mistake! So, who’s it going to be? [Tom Mason]- Well, you’re forcing me to give you a match at The Summit? Hmm… You’re finally deciding to become an egotistical maniac are you? Well, we need something to satisfy that ego… How about a title shot? [Reno]- A title shot? Now you’re talking my style… Against who? [Tom Mason]- How about a Junior Heavyweight Title shot against Shawn Boyd… No Disqualification sound good? [Reno]- That sounds good… I’ll show you why they call me “Five Star” Johnny Reno. Just like those days in the CWF… I was the two time Television Champion! Hahaha… Shawn Boyd…What a joke. Johnny Reno walks out of the room talking to himself and laughing. Tom Mason shakes his head and the camera cuts out to commercial… *** Commercial Break *** [Bryan]- Well, just minutes ago you saw Johnny Reno demand a match next Sunday at The Summit from Tom Mason. And strangely enough, Tom gave him a match! It seems Reno will go head to head with the Junior Heavyweight Champion… Shawn Boyd! Standard Match Shannon York vs. Jason Smith [Bryan] -Well, it looks like Shannon York is set to take on the US Champion in a non-title match up here on Shockwave… Let’s check it out… Jason Smith makes his way down to the ring as his theme music plays. The XCWF US title around his waist and the crowd cheering him on as he gets into the ring and walks around waiting for his opponent… Shannon York walks to the ring. Tim Martin is the referee for this contest. Jason Smith executes a pumphandle suplex on Shannon York. Shannon York walks around the ring. (the bell rings) Jason Smith tackles Shannon York. Shannon York is back on his feet. Shannon York hits a flying karate chop right to Jason Smith's neck. Jason Smith executes a headlock takedown. Shannon York gets hit with the shooting star press from Jason Smith. The ref starts the count. ...1 Shannon York escapes. Shannon York climbs to his feet. Jason Smith hits a kneeling headbutt to Shannon York's groin. Jason Smith grabs Shannon York and applies an arm wrench. Jason Smith executes a flying knee drop right to the gut of Shannon York. Jason Smith jumps off the turnbuckle with the flying bodypress on Shannon York. Jason Smith gets up. Shannon York gets knocked on the ground and Jason Smith flips onto him. Jason Smith gets back to his feet. Shannon York gets up. Jason Smith gets up off the ground and Shannon York hits him with a flying dropkick. Shannon York gets back to his feet. Shannon York covers Jason Smith hooking the leg. Tim Martin counts. ...1 ...2 Jason Smith escapes. Flying side kick by Shannon York takes Jason Smith off his feet. Now Jason Smith standing. Jason Smith jabs Shannon York. A flying shoulder block send Jason Smith to the mat. Shannon York stands up. Jason Smith is up again. Jason Smith jumps from the top and nails Shannon York with a flying axhandle smash. Jason Smith goes off the top and hits a flying sitdown splash on Shannon York. Jason Smith gets up. Jason Smith kicks Shannon York on the mat. Shannon York is up again. Shannon York picks up Jason Smith and delivers a tilt-a-whirl suplex. Shannon York gets back to his feet. Jason Smith is locked in an elbow submission by Shannon York. Referee Tim Martin is checking for a tap out. ... (AHHHH!) ... ... Shannon York breaks the hold. Shannon York executes a flying headbutt on Jason Smith. Shannon York sends Jason Smith to ringside. Tim Martin starts the count (.1) Shannon York hits Jason Smith with a flying senton. Shannon York is up again. Jason Smith gets back to his feet. Jason Smith gets thrown into the turnbuckle. Shannon York comes at him and drills him with the splash. Jason Smith moves back to his feet. They head back into the ring. Jason Smith holds his mouth after receiving an elbow smash to the face. Jason Smith, standing on the top rope posing for the fans, is caught by surprise. Shannon York German suplexes him!! WAIT!! HERE COMES BRUCE BLADE!! Bruce runs down the ramp and gets into the ring and pushes Shannon York away from Jason Smith! The referee calls for the bell as Bruce begins to assault the XCWF US champion!! [Bryan]- DAMN IT!! LOOK AT BRUCE BLADE! He can’t wait until Sunday! He knows that he can’t beat Jason Smith for the US Title if Jason is at one hundred percent!! Referee pour out from the back and run into the ring to get Bruce Blade off of Jason Smith. Smith is left lying on the ground and Shannon York comes back into the ring to check on him. Bruce Blade leaves and smiles and talks trash . [Bryan]- That’s right Bruce… You run to the back! This is not the Bruce Blade the XCWF knew just a couple weeks ago!! *** Commercial Break *** The camera cuts back into XCWF Shockwave as it shows what just happened to Jason Smith moments ago… The camera then shows a picture of Bruce Blade and Jason Smith on opposite sides as Jason has the US Title over his shoulder… [Bryan]- What you just saw was Bruce Blade attack Jason Smith in the middle of his match with Shannon York! Bruce just couldn’t wait until next Sunday to get into the ring with Jason, he has to start his fight tonight! And they do meet next Sunday at XCWF: The Summit on Pay-Per-View!! Also on Sunday at the Summit Kamikazi goes one on one with Jeremy Tailor from the WWCW Elite! JT stepped up and took the challenge to face him for the title at The Summit. There is no stipulation yet as to what type of match it is! But at Warfare Tuesday Night, Kamikazi and Zac Moore take on Royce Mathers and JT in a Tag team match! And right now… we have the ladies on their way to the ring! Whooo!! Purple lights flash, and Sedusa makes her way down the ramp smiling, and walks up the steps, goes under the bottom rope, and walks around the ring taunting the fans. The fans cheer as “Alive” by POD cuts in and the XCWF Women’s Champion, Victoria Vengeance, comes out from the back in her very short skirt and low cut V-Neck shirt! She gets down the ramp and climbs up the apron to the ring… XCWF Women’s Title Standard Match Sedusa vs. Victoria Vengeance She gets through the ropes slowly and the crowd cheers as she gives the title to the referee… Victoria gives a cheap shot, and the match is under away. Victoria gives mounted slaps to Sedusa, she picks her up and drives her down to the mat with a DDT, Victoria goes to the top rope, but Sedusa runs over to the top rope and cuts her off, Victoria goes down in pain, Sedusa grabs Victoria’s hair and swings her across the ring, then she goes for the pin. 1…2…kick out, Victoria kicks out with authority, then Sedusa kicks Victoria in the mid section and picks her up from the ground, and swings her into the ropes and does a bulldog. She Picks up Victoria again and does a hurricanranna, she pins 1…2..kick out again by Victoria, Sedusa tries the same move again she lifts her legs up for the hurricanranna but this time Victoria sees it coming and she reverses it into a powerbomb pin, she covers Sedusa 1…2..kick out, Victoria gives a elbow to Sedusa, and picks her up and does a suplex, and picks her up again and does a belly to back suplex, and drags her over to the ropes and does a mid air moonsault. And starts kicking away at Sedusa, she picks up Sedusa and throws her into the turnbuckle and does a diving spear into Sedusa, Sedusa thrives in pain, as she tries to regain her breathing, Victoria keeps the kicks going driving into Sedusa’s throat, she picks up Sedusa and goes for a punch, but Sedusa blocks Victoria’s punch and gives one of her own, but Victoria punches back and the two women keep exchanging blows, but Sedusa kicks Victoria in the mid section and nails her with a dangerous DDT. She goes for the pin 1…2…kick out again by Victoria Vengeance, Victoria gets up and roles out of the ring, for a breather, but a unknown person walks down to the ring, no one knows who the person is, but she just stands on the XCWF-Tron, she got a shirt on saying 100% bitch, and the name Sheena printed on. She walks down to the ring looking at Victoria Vengeance and stops she stares at her, Victoria gets back into the ring, and Sedusa drops the elbow, Victoria is out in the ring, he picks her up and does a Piledriver, but Victoria somehow gets up and spears Sedusa, she gives multiple punches. Victoria gives a leg drop to Sedusa, and kicks her ribs, she starts thumping her fists down on Sedusa… Victoria hits Sedusa with a chop across the chest… Whoo!… Goes the crowd as she does it…. WHOOO... Again, a chop by Victoria to Sedusa. Victoria whips Sedusa into the corner and starts to hammer on her.. 1…2….3…4…5… Pulled out of the corner by the referee and Sedusa recuperates. Victoria goes back to grab her and Sedusa hits her in the face, and then once more. He lifts up Victoria and drops her on the turnbuckle!!! Victoria grabs her face and lies on the mat. Sedusa covers her…1…2… Foot on the ropes by Victoria Vengeance and the count is broken! Victoria Vengeance gets up to her feet and Sedusa whips her into the ropes. Sedusa is bent over waiting for her to return… Kick to the face by Victoria Vengeance … gutwrench powerbomb by Victoria Vengeance on Sedusa!! Victoria Vengeance makes the cover on Sedusa…1…2…No! Kick out by Sedusa!! Victoria Vengeance gets up and grabs Sedusa by the hair. She drags Sedusa over to the corner and starts banging her head on the turnbuckle…1…2…3…4…5… Sedusa falls down to the floor!! Victoria Vengeance picks Sedusa back up and is calling for the break down!! She punches Victoria Vengeance in the face and then climbs the turnbuckles… Sedusa hits the ropes!! Victoria Vengeance hits herself hard as he falls on the turnbuckle the wrong way. Sedusa climbs up with all the strength she has left… WAIT!! Punch by Victoria Vengeance … with all the breath she got left! Sedusa is out of it! The cover is made…1…2… The referee stops the count cause Sheena is up on the apron!! Sedusa is waking back up and she is measuring Victoria Vengeance up for a shuffle side kick! Blocked Shuffle SideKick by Victoria Vengeance!! She spins Sedusa around and whips him into the ropes… Sedusa stays against the ropes and Victoria Vengeance charges in… Sedusa knocks Victoria Vengeance out of the ring!! Victoria Vengeance falls at ringside as Sedusa is trying to regain his strength in the ring. The referee checks on Victoria Vengeance and Sedusa and they seem all right. Victoria Vengeance gets up on the apron and Sedusa goes to grab her… The referee is now battling with Sheena who is trying to get in the ring… BREAKDOWN!! Victoria Vengeance just hit the break down! SHE COVERS SEDUSA… 1…2…3… Sheena walks back up the ramp, Victoria Vengeance looks puzzled at Sheena! Victoria has retained her title. [Bryan]- WHOOO!! Victoria retains her title her tonight! That will just about do it for XCWF Shockwave tonight… I am Bryan Kaminski, don’t forget to catch Warfare Tuesday night for X-Mas Carnage!! And be sure to order The Summit on PPV next Sunday!! *** Camera fades to black *** XCWF SUMMIT!! LIVE! Next Sunday on Pay-Per-View from the sold out Metrodome in Houston, Texas!
