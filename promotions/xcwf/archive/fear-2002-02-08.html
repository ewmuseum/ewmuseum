XCWF Card Archive

Fear, 8 February 2002 — results

8 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: LIVE!! On NBC from the Mellon Arena in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania... XCWF Friday Night Fear!! :: The screen started off with no clips from XCWF New Dawn on Sunday. The camera went to a live shot of the Mellon Arena in Pittsburgh, PA. As Mike Dennis and Bryan Kaminski were giving a little information on what happened on Sunday at New Dawn, Mark Estep interrupted them. A very unhappy, displeased Mark Estep made his way down to the ring and took a microphone as he began to speak. “For one year I have been with this company and I have done more than many men have,” Mark Estep said as he went on to list numerous accomplishments he has made. Winning the XCWF Championship twice, being the only XCWF Grand Slam champion, so on. The fans and Mike Dennis alike were still puzzled on Mark’s purpose. But then he went on and said a few words about New Dawn, saying that it was the highlight and the low point of his career. It was his best match, and it was his best match. And then, Mark Estep said that it was time for him to leave the XCWF. He announced that he was not retiring but going to the PWA, the wrestling school owned by Primetime Championship Wrestling. And with that Mark Estep dropped the microphone and walked backstage. Backstage at this time, Tom Mason was entering the building and he had a smile on his face as in one hand was a brief case and on his right, he had a lawyer. The camera then went to commercial and a promo for the next XCWF Pay-Per-View event, Reality on March 3rd 2002 from the Boston Fleet Center in Boston Massachusetts. *** Commercial Break *** The first match of the night was scheduled between Colby Newsome and his brother, Dominick “Daddy-O” Newsome. Mike and Bryan showed a clip from last Sunday as Colby cost his brother and Donovan Simmons the XCWF Tag Team Titles in the huge Tag Team Turmoil match. Colby took the fast lead in the match laying his older brother out with a series of punches and then a suplex. But a costly mistake as he whipped Daddy-O into the turnbuckle and went for a charge allowed Dominick to take control. Dominick decided to do a brawling as he threw his brother to the outside of the ring and they fought at ringside. Colby was able to drive his brother shoulder first into the steel steps and then catch a breather. He rolled Dominick back into the ring for the cover, but Dom kicked out after a two count. Colby proceeded to drill his brother down to the mat with a big DDT and following up with a leg drop off the top rope. However, Colby’s high-flying techniques did not keep Dominick down as he kicked out again before the three. Dominick got an adrenaline rush with a little help from the fans and then starts to battle back as he lays out his brother with a big tornado DDT off the turnbuckles! Both of them were down in the ring and then Daddy-O gets back to his feet and clotheslines Colby over the top rope to the outside. Daddy-O then gets a chair and proceeds to crack his brother over the head with it to pay him back for what he did to him last Sunday at New Dawn! The referee disqualifies him and awards the win to Colby Newsome. The camera went backstage as Donovan Simmons is shown walking down the halls. Matt Stanford approaches him and asks him about tonight’s tag team match for the titles. Donovan says that he and his partner are ready for their match and that tonight they will capture the Tag Team Titles. Matt asks if he can tell everyone who his partner is for the match tonight. Donovan laughs and tells Matt that they will all see soon enough! In another section of the arena, Predator and Kamikazi are walking into the arena. Kamikazi with the IC title over his shoulder and Predator with a smile on his face. The two of them run into Sid who is also entering the arena. Sid stands there and snarls at the two of them. Kamikazi and Predator laugh at Sid as he says, “I vow that the two of you will pay for what you did. Whether it be now, or later…”. The camera cuts to commercial… *** Commercial Break *** Backstage Tom Mason is sitting at a desk smiling as he is signing a couple papers and then he turns and hands them to his lawyer. He then gets up and looks like he could be on his way to the ring. The camera then cuts across the back to where Menace is walking into the building with his Extreme Title over his shoulder. He runs into Johnny Reno who tells him how he is going to win the XCWF Junior Heavyweight Title tonight when he beats Shawn Boyd! In the ring, Rusty Miller and Darkside are going to get at it. Lately, Rusty Miller has been offended by the comments from Darkside. Rusty takes a fast lead in the match over Darkside whipping him into the turnbuckles and delivering a hard clothesline. Rusty takes down Darkside with a Northern lights suplex and the pin is broken up before a two count. Rusty gets Darkside back to his feet and throws him against the ropes but misses with the clothesline and Darkside comes back with a clothesline of his own! Darkside with a dropkick on the wrestling veteran, Rusty Miller. Rusty Miller manages to escape Darkside and comes back on a return delivering repeated shots to Darkside’s face… Rusty Miller takes Darkside and delivers a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker to the canvas! Rusty climbs to the top rope and gets set to jump off as Darkside makes his way to his feet… But Darkside hits the ropes and Rusty falls between the turnbuckles. Darkside climbs up on the ropes and grabs Rusty landing a huge superplex on the ring mat! The cover and Rusty manages to kick out after two. Darkside gets Rusty down with a swinging neckbreaker and he takes it to the top rope now. Off the top rope Darkside comes with a big body press but Rusty gets his knees up crushing the ribs on Darkside! Darkside hops around the ring and Rusty gets to his feet. A clothesline over the top rope by Rusty and Darkside manages to hold on and land on his feet on the apron. Darkside pulls Rusty by the neck down on the ropes. Climbing to the top Darkside jumps off and hurricanrannas Miller down on the canvas! He waits as he starts to stir to his feet… He sets him up for The Backstabber by Miller counters it into a front facelock suplex and drops Darkside forward on his face! Rusty Miller takes advantage of the situation and applies the Miller lock!! Darkside tries to escape and reaches out for the ropes… The referee waits for the tap out but Darkside is relentless to give into Rusty’s move! Wait! Darkside manages to get to the ropes and it forces Rusty Miller to release the hold or be disqualified! Rusty stomps away on Darkside and then picks him up and drills him with a Side suplex! The cover and Darkside kicks out after a two from that. Rusty Miller is getting fed up and just wants to punish the young upstart as he throws him out of the ring and at ringside. Darkside stumbles around and Rusty clotheslines him down to the mats! Rusty now goes and gets a chair from the announcer’s area. The referee has seen enough and he steps out of the ring and stops Rusty from using the chair. Rusty turns around and Darkside takes him down to the ground and is on top of him beating on him! Darkside gets off of Rusty and picks up the chair… He drives it into the sternum of the veteran and Rusty grabs himself. The referee forces him back and Rusty slowly crawls to his knees… Kick to the gut by Darkside and Rusty goes back down! Darkside picks him back up and rolls him into the ring. The referee and Darkside both climb back in and Darkside covers Rusty… Kickout just before the three! Rusty is hurting and Darkside picks him up and whips him into the ropes where rusty ducks the clothesline and comes back with a roundhouse kick! Rusty could capitalize and win this match… Rusty staggers and Darkside gets back to his feet… MILLER TIME BY RUSTY! The cover ….1…2…3… Rusty Miller takes the win in this match! Afterwards Rusty gets up in the face of Darkside and mouths off. Darkside spits at Rusty and Rusty proceeds to kick Darkside and then he locks in the Miller Lock! Referees run down the ramp and get into the ring trying to break up this little confrontation and they manage to get Rusty out of the ring. Backstage, Tom Mason is shown once again as not only a lawyer is next to him but TJ Faith as well. The two of them make their way down the hallway as the camera cuts to commercial… *** Commercial Break *** The arena is filled with cheers as Tom Mason and TJ Faith along with a lawyer are walking down the ramp to the ring. Tom, TJ and the lawyer get into the ring and Tom Mason gets a microphone from the announcer. There is a table in the center of the ring and a couple chairs as well. The lawyer lays the briefcase Tom had earlier down on the table and opens it… Tom Mason begins to talk: “Last Sunday night I told you that we would see the end to the WWCW Elite. Tonight, right in this right, in front of all you fans and the viewers at home, we will live up to that promise!” Tom goes on to say a couple comments about the WWCW in general and then he calls out the XCWF President and the leader of the WWCW Elite, Sid. Sid then makes his way to the ring and stands around until Tom tells him to sit down. Then Tom continues as he calls out the XCWF Chairman of the Board, Kanyonaro. Kanyonaro follows the same procedure Sid did and he comes into the ring and sits down. Tom begins to explain what is going on and what they have to worry about. They start with Sid as he sits there and he looks at Tom Mason with a disgusted face. Tom tells Sid that the XCWF was and did crush the WWCW Elite, why would he even attempt to kill the XCWF? And then Mr. Mason lays out a contract on the table from the briefcase and passes it to Sid. “This contract is in fact one that states your resignation from the XCWF as President. When you sign this, you have no stock, no rights and no business at all with the XCWF. You will never show your face on XCWF television again, and I’ll be sure about that!” Sid looks Tom dead in the eyes and shakes his head as he then hands a pen to Sid. “You know what to do…” says Tom. Using the pen Sid signs his name on the contract and then pushes it up to Mr. Mason. Tom Mason turns to Kanyonaro and then says to him what he said to Sid. He hands Kanyonaro the same type of contract he handed Sid and tells him to sign his full name, Matt Orzolek. Matt signs the name and slides the papers up to Tom Mason. Both Kanyonaro and Sid do not look happy and then Tom begins to talk again: “As of this moment, the two of you are and forever will be… FIRED!! Now, get out of my ring and away from my company!! Kanyonaro and Sid both get up and start to leave… Wait!! They turn back and start to wail off on the XCWF Owner and his partner TJ Faith!! Kanyonaro stomps away on Tom Mason as Sid drops TJ with a forceful knee to the stomach followed by a DDT!! Kanyonaro and Sid look enraged as they then get up and climb out of the ring… For what could be the last time ever. In the ring, Tom Mason and TJ Faith are lying on the mat, the two of them in shock and stunned from what just happened. Backstage the camera shows Dream Master standing with a bandage over his head and he is next to Johnny Fame. Dream Master begins to comment on New Dawn and the Tag Team Turmoil match, where he mentions his partner, Gothic, hitting him over the head with a chair! Dream Master says that he cost them the match and that next Wednesday on Warfare, he would cost Gothic his life! And that Gothic would be dreaming… forever! With those words the camera cuts out to another commercial… *** Commercial Break *** The XCWF Tag Team Champions make their way down the ramp and get into the ring as they await their opponents, Donovan Simmons and his mystery partner. Shannon York and Unfitt Survivor discuss a little strategy and then look as Donovan Simmons walks out from the back and down the ramp to the ring with a microphone. He stands at the bottom of the ramp and looks into the ring as he talks… “Now, last week I was screwed over in my match for the Tag Team titles. Tom Mason only saw it fair that I got another shot here tonight on Fear. And with that he gave me the choice of any partner I wanted to have… And why shouldn’t I have chosen the XCWF Champion, Zac Moore? Well, I didn’t because he is not in good conditions to fight tonight. But I did choose the next best thing… The XCWF United States champion… JASON SMITH!! Jason Smith and Donovan Simmons together enter the ring and begin their match with the tag team champions. Jason and Shannon York start off the match and York ties up Smith as he hammers him with repeated rights and lefts. Shannon York with the whip across the ring into the turnbuckles but Smith moves as York was making the run in. Jason Smith takes York from behind and drops him with a Neckbreaker drop. The cover…1…2… York kicks out and Jason Smith takes him by the hair. Kick to the gut and Jason throws Shannon into his corner. He tags in Donovan and the two of them double-team the tag team champion… Unfitt Survivor runs in by Shannon catches him with a clothesline and he goes down. Shannon drags Survivor out of the ring and he gets back on the apron as Donovan Simmons and Shannon York go at it… York counters Don’s whip and then Don ducks the spinning wheel kick… But York catches him with a fist instead! Shannon York needs to tag but Donovan has his foot and won’t let him tag. Donovan and York are both up and York goes for the enziguri… Ducked by Donovan who retaliates with an underhook Russian Leg sweep to York! Shannon reaches out for the tag but Unfitt is too far away to get it. Donovan tags in Smith once again and the two of them stomp on Shannon York. Jason Smith now starts to climb the turnbuckles as he partner leaves the ring and gets on the apron. Jason jumps and misses with an elbow drop as York gets out of the way! York tags in Survivor and he comes charging in to the ring! Jason Smith is knocked down by Unfitt and then once again! Into the ring charges Unfitt’s brother, Donovan, who is also laid out by Survivor! DDT to Jason Smith by Unfitt Survivor and he makes the cover…1….2… Broken up by Donovan! Survivor and Donovan fight against the ropes and then York is back up and comes charging in… Unfitt ducks and Donovan goes over the top along with Shannon! In the ring Unfitt Survivor turns around and walks right into a waiting Jason Smith… SUPERKICK TO THE CHIN!! Unfitt Survivor goes down and Jason Smith makes the cover…1…2…3… We have new XCWF Tag Team Champions!! York and Simmons are still going at it on the outside and then York realizes he lost as he then slides back into the ring. Jason Smith slides out and gets the titles from the referee as he is joined by Donovan Simmons. The two of them walk up the ramp with the titles and the camera cuts backstage… Tom Mason is sitting with TJ Faith and the two of them are icing down their heads. A knock on the door and in comes XCWF Commissioner, Mack Silverstein. Mach walks up to Tom’s desk and slaps a piece of paper on his desk… Tom demands to know what it is he just put down. Mack tells him it’s his contract and that he is resigning as the Commissioner to make it easier for the company, this way Tom doesn’t have to come looking for Mack. Mack says his goodbyes and the camera cuts out to commercial… *** Commercial Break *** Mark Estep is walking backstage and he runs into Predator and Kamikazi… He tells them each thank you for the ass kickings and the memories over the past year. He then gives them each a hug and handshake as he leaves the arena for the parking lot. Kamikazi and Predator look at each other and shake their heads as they continue to go about their business. The Duff is shown in the back with his ‘Duff is the stuff’ shirt on. He is sitting in a chair in his locker room, pondering thoughts about what is going on. What to do now? Duff asks himself out loud. Duff is upset about the WWCW Elite being destroyed, he is upset about not winning the XCWF United States title and he is upset that he cannot think of ways worth continuing his career. Into his locker room walks a very disgruntled Psycho Kid with Zoron at his side. You can hear the boos from the arena fans through out the arena. Psycho Kid looks at Duff and then tells him not to be so glum, he says that Duff should not settle for losing. And that he should go out and get a big win. Duff replies to Psycho Kid and tells him that he will get a big win, tonight in the WWCW Elite Battle Royal for Immunity! Psycho Kid gets closer to him and tells him that HE is the one that will win the match tonight. Tonight they will find out who will win the match! The XCWF Junior Heavyweight Title is on the line in this next match as Shawn Boyd and Johnny Reno go at it again. Johnny Reno is a little anxious at winning the title here tonight and he goes right at Shawn trying to take him out with some big moves. Shawn is a little too good for it and he counters Johnny and takes him down in a Brainbuster suplex! The cover is made but Reno manages to kick out after the two. Shawn Boyd whips Reno into the ropes and waits on him but Reno comes back and kicks Shawn in the chest as he was bent over. Reno backs up into the ropes again and comes back with a lateral press, taking Shawn Boyd down on the canvas! The cover is made but Shawn throws Johnny off after a two. Shawn Boyd goes for a power clothesline but Reno ducks and watches Shawn turns around… Kick to the gut and a big DDT by Reno! Johnny Reno backs against the ropes and comes back with an elbow slamming down on the champion! Shawn Boyd rolls and slides out of the ring to which Reno follows him to the outside. He takes Shawn by the Hair and walks him to the steel steps attempting to smash his face on them but it’s countered and Shawn Boyd smashes Johnny Reno’s face on the steps! The Junior Heavyweight Champion goes to work on the challenger as he whips him into the barricade and rolls back into the ring leaving Reno on the outside. The referee starts to administer his ten count and Reno is slowly beginning to get to his feet. Reno is not moving fast enough for Shawn Boyd so Boyd leaves the ring and grabs Johnny, rolling him back into the ring. Shawn Boyd picks him up and sets him for a suplex… Reno gets away and lands on his feet… Clothesline to Shawn Boyd by Johnny Reno! The Junior Heavyweight Champion gets back to his feet fast but Reno ducks his attack and catches the champion with some sort of superkick maneuver! Boyd falls to the ground and Johnny Reno makes the cover… This could be the end of Shawn Boyd’s title reign…1…2…Boyd gets the shoulder up just as the referee was setting for a three count! Johnny Reno can’t believe it and he takes Boyd tossing him into the corner. Johnny Reno goes to work on Boyd with rights and lefts; he lays kicks and then applies a chokehold onto Boyd… The referee tries to get Reno off of Boyd but he doesn’t want to back off and he whips Boyd across the ring into the other corner. Johnny Reno changes in and Shawn Boyd moves out of the way! Reno’s body goes through the second turnbuckle and he goes shoulder first into the turnbuckle post! Shawn Boyd hops up on the ropes and jumps off with a missile dropkick to the head on Johnny Reno! The cover is made with the hook of the leg….1….2… Kickout from Johnny Reno who really wants this title tonight! Boyd whips Reno against the ropes and hits him with a monkey toss to the floor! He applies a tight lock on the shoulder of Johnny Reno, tightening the hold trying to get the submission… Reno kicks Shawn in the face and Boyd breaks the hold. Boyd backs against the ropes and here comes Reno with a clothesline to Boyd. But Boyd ducks and flips Reno over the top of the ropes!! Reno manages to hold on and land on the apron, he makes his way up the turnbuckles and Shawn Boyd doesn’t even see him coming… Reno jumps at Boyd but wait a minute! Boyd catches him by surprise with a punch to the face and Reno goes down from the force! Shawn Boyd sizes up the challenger as he is getting up and he whips him into the turnbuckles… Boyd climbs up on the turnbuckles hammering away on Johnny Reno who is beaten badly… THE BOYD BOMB BY THE CHAMPION!! The cover is made after the amazing move…1…2…3… The XCWF Junior Heavyweight Champion is still Shawn Boyd! Zac Moore is backstage with the XCWF Championship in his hands as the camera shows him entering the office of Tom Mason with a big smile on his face. He shuts the door and the camera can’t get anymore than that. Instead, it goes to TJ Faith walking down the halls and he approaches Sabbath and LT Smash, the man who calls himself the North American Champion. TJ Faith is polite and asks them how they are doing today. LT says he’s feeling great, he’s the XCWF North American Champion and no one will beat him. TJ says that could be right because no one will ever have to face him again, nor will they have to face Sabbath. They are a little puzzled and ask why. TJ Faith puts his hands on each of their shoulders and whispers… You’re both fired! Faith walks away and Sabbath and LT Smash are in shock as they just stand there. *** Commercial Break *** Jason Smith and Donovan Simmons are walking around backstage with the XCWF Tag Team Titles over their shoulders and smiles on their faces. The two of them walk into Benny Bodacious and Shawn Boyd, with the JHW title over his shoulder. Donovan and Jason Simmons do a little gloating saying that everyone should bow down to the XCWF Tag Team Champions, the greatest ones of all time! Shawn and Benny laugh and Jason asks them what’s so funny. Benny tells them that they could never be the best Tag Team Champions, “You are not Bodacious enough to be the Tag Team Champions!” Donovan and Jason Smith do not think it’s funny and they go ahead and leave an open challenge out on the table for Wednesday on Warfare, a Tag Team titles match. Simmons and Smith would teach them what tag team wrestling is all about. Leslie Dupnik is shown with the XCWF Women’s title over her shoulder as Tasha Evans, a new woman in the division, approaches her. Tasha introduces herself and offers her services as a friend to Leslie. Leslie smiles and tries to see if she is really after the XCWF Women’s Title. Tasha says that she isn’t worried about that title right now, she’ll get it when she’s ready. But that Leslie should have some sort of ally to help her out against other women like Victoria Vengeance, who might get a little jealous and seek out Leslie for her title. Leslie again smiles and then extends her hand, shaking with the new found friend. Tonight’s main event is a WWCW Elite battle royal for a XCWF Contract! Members of the WWCW Elite poor out of the back and enter the ring. The Duff, Dark Angel, Mike Dupnik, Sterling Moore, Psycho Kid, Project, Gothic, Jay Helms and Arson. They are all in the ring and the only way to be eliminated is to be tossed over the top rope by another competitor. The match starts off as all nine of the men are battling in the ring and each attempts to throw another over the top… No one goes over in the first few minutes as they all are battling in the ring trying to get a job. Dark Angel is nearly tossed over the top but he manages to hold on and fight off Mike Dupnik. But on the other side of the ring Psycho Kid and Arson are working together and they put Sterling Moore over the top rope!! Sterling Moore is out of any chance of picking up a XCWF Contract!! Jay Helms decides to rush in from behind on Psycho Kid and Arson and he catches a boot to the face. Psycho Kid picks him up and holds him in a gorilla press over his head, showing his strength as he tosses him over the top rope! Jay Helms has lost a job as well! Psycho Kid and Arson are cleaning house as they now go to work on Gothic over on the other side of the ring… Gothic starts to fight them each off and he manages to slip away and get to the other side of the ring. Mike Dupnik however puts his once a friend, Dark Angel over the top rope with a big fist to the fact and Dark Angel is not going to have a guaranteed job tomorrow! Mike Dupnik goes to work on Gothic and the two of them are exchanging shots. Duff and The Project are battling over the articles at the beginning of their names and Duff slams The Project down on the mat! He stomps away on Project in the corner and then turns as Arson and Psycho Kid start to go for him… Duff kicks Psycho kid and nails Arson with a punch… Psycho kid grabs Duff and attempts to throw him over but from behind Gothic, Mike Dupnik and Project attack Arson and Psycho kid! There are six men standing in just one corner of the ring and someone is going to go over the top rope! Duff ducks and Psycho Kid takes Project and tosses him over the top rope to the outside! Project has been eliminated and we have only five men left in the ring! Psycho Kid turns around and goes after Mike Dupnik as Arson is double-teamed by Gothic and Duff. Together work to eliminate Arson and we are now down to four men! Mike Dupnik is trying his hardest to eliminate Psycho Kid but he can’t seem to get him over the second rope…. Psycho Kid grabs Mike by the throat… CHOKE SLAM!! Mike Dupnik is laid out and now Duff and Gothic go at Psycho Kid… Thrust to the throat by Psycho Kid to Gothic… Clothesline to Duff! All three men are down and Psycho Kid waits as Gothic gets up to his feet… Big toss over the top rope and Gothic has been eliminated from this contest as well. Three men are left in this match: Mike Dupnik, Duff and Psycho Kid. Psycho Kid has eliminated four of the six guys that have been tossed over the top rope… Kid turns around and Duff is going at him! Duff hammers into him with repeated rights and lefts… Knee to the gut followed by a DDT from Duff!! Mike Dupnik is back up and Duff sends him against the ropes… Duff runs in and it seems that Dark Angel has Mike’s foot!! Mike can’t move and he falls back over the top rope to the outside! Mike Dupnik is eliminated and we are down to just Psycho Kid and Duff!! Duff watches as Psycho Kid gets to his feet and he sets him up for a back body drop… Countered and Psycho kid takes down Duff with a snapmare. Duff gets back to his feet and Psycho Kid catches him… Sidewalk slam to the canvas! Duff holds his back and seems to be a little over powered in this match as Psycho Kid picks him back up by the hair… Duff tries to fight back with a couple shots to the face and then he backs up hitting a clothesline on Psycho Kid, but he doesn’t go down… Another clothesline and this time Kid goes down on the mat! Duff backs against the ropes again and comes back as Psycho Kid is getting to his feet… Dropkick back and Psycho Kid falls through the ropes!! He’s not eliminated because he didn’t go over the top!! Duff climbs out and gives chase to Kid who is walking around the ring trying to gain his strength back… Duff turns him around but Kid catches him with an uppercut and then picks him up dropping him on the barricade!! Duff goes down and Psycho Kid gets a steel chair and runs at Duff, driving it into his stomach!! Duff drops once more to his knees and Psycho Kid throws the chair into the ring and then takes Duff, throwing him into the ring. Psycho Kid gets into the ring and picks up Duff, leaning him against the turnbuckles and chopping Duff across the chest… WHOOO… WHOOO… WHOOO!! Duff holds his chest and Psycho Kid picks him up for a spinebuster… Wait! Duff is fighting back as he hammers away to Psycho Kid’s head and gets away from the spine buster! Psycho Kid misses with a clothesline and Duff kicks him back against the ropes… WAIT LOOK OUT!! CHAIR TO THE HEAD!! Duff cracks Psycho Kid over the head with the weapon he brought into the ring!! Psycho kid falls back against the ropes… Duff with the clothesline and this match is over as Duff wins and has a job with the XCWF!! With those last actions, the camera pulls back and shows Duff not even celebrating in the ring as on the outside Psycho Kid is pissed off and he can’t manage to stand. The camera fades out and goes to a promo for next week’s XCWF Fear on NBC! *** The Camera fades to black *** *** LIVE! On Wednesday February 13th 2002!!
