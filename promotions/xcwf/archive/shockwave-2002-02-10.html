10 February 2002

XCWF Shockwave :: LIVE! Sunday Night on TNN at 8 p.m. XCWF ShockWave from the U.S. Air Arena in Landover, Maryland!!! :: The camera starts at the TNN “Up Next: XCWF Shockwave” screen and then cuts to black. “It's Going Down” by the Executioners and Linkin Park, the official song of the XCWF starts to blare and the camera shows the inside of the U.S. Air Arena as the fans are cheering and signs are waving in the air. A couple pyros shoot off the stage area and the crowd cheers as the camera pans around the commentator’s desk where Bryan Kaminski sits alone… [Bryan]- Welcome to XCWF Shockwave! I am Bryan Kaminski here alone hosting tonight's show! Let me tell you first that this coming Wednesday we will see a HUGE Tag Team Match: Kamikazi and Zac Moore, two men that have no mutual friendships will team up against The Duff and Psycho Kid! But here tonight we kick things off with a XCWF Women's match... Standard Match Victoria Vengeance vs. Tasha Evans Tasha Evans comes to the ring... Victoria Vengeance walks to the ring. Tim Martin is the referee for this contest. Victoria Vengeance gets nailed with a charging axhandle bodyblock from Tasha Evans. Victoria Vengeance checks out the ring. (ring, ring, ring) Tasha Evans slaps Victoria Vengeance in the face. Tasha Evans gets up. Victoria Vengeance with a high crossbody on Tasha Evans. Victoria Vengeance climbs to his feet. Tasha Evans gets hit with the shooting star press from Victoria Vengeance. Tim Martin counts the pin. ...1 ...2 Tasha Evans kicks out. Tasha Evans moves back to his feet. Tasha Evans executes the jumping sidekick on Victoria Vengeance. Victoria Vengeance places Tasha Evans over by the turnbuckle. Victoria Vengeance jumps off with a flying sommersault neckbreaker. Tasha Evans slaps Victoria Vengeance. Tasha Evans short lariats Victoria Vengeance. Tasha Evans gets caught with an elbowsmash to the face. Tasha Evans bounces Victoria Vengeance off the ropes and clotheslines him. Victoria Vengeance gets up. Victoria Vengeance uses a cradle suplex on Tasha Evans. Now Victoria Vengeance standing. Tasha Evans climbs to his feet. Tasha Evans jumps from the top and nails Victoria Vengeance with a flying axhandle smash. Tasha Evans applies an arm wrench to Victoria Vengeance. Victoria Vengeance climbs to his feet. Tasha Evans rakes his fingers across Victoria Vengeance's back. Victoria Vengeance hits Tasha Evans with a Baba chop. Victoria Vengeance bites Tasha Evans's arm out of desparation. Tasha Evans hits Victoria Vengeance with a double underhook piledrive right into the mat. Victoria Vengeance gets back to his feet. Tasha Evans uses a snap mare takeover on Victoria Vengeance. Tasha Evans chants start. Tasha Evans uses a legsweep faceslam to bring Victoria Vengeance down. Tasha Evans uses an inside cradle to pin Victoria Vengeance. The ref starts the count. ...1 ...2 ...3 ] [Bryan]- Tasha Evans has won the match! And impressive she was. When we get back, Cade Malibu will attempt to impress Dream Master in one on one action! *** Commercial Break *** The camera opens up to the US Air Arena and shows Bryan again sitting as the fans are awaiting more action... [Bryan]- Welcome back and we are now set for the second of four matches tonight on XCWF Shockwave. We thank you for tuning into the show tonight. And we expect to see you tune in again on Wednesday night for XCWF Warfare from Providence, Rhode Island! Standard Match Cade Malibu vs. Dream Master Dream Master comes to the ring... Cade Malibu walks to the ring. Tim Martin is the referee for this contest. Dream Master executes a pumphandle suplex on Cade Malibu. Cade Malibu places Dream Master on the turnbuckle and executes a front-layout suplerplex. (ding, ding, ding) Cade Malibu gets picked up and dropped with the fireman's carry by Dream Master. Dream Master jumps off the turnbuckle with the flying bodypress on Cade Malibu. Cade Malibu goes up to the top and comes down on Dream Master with an asai leg lariat. Cade Malibu drags Dream Master to the floor. Tim Martin starts the count (.1) Cade Malibu cuts Dream Master with a blade. Dream Master is bleeding as a result. Cade Malibu goes for a leg drop but Dream Master dodges the attack. They fight into the aisle. (..2) (...3) Cade Malibu knees Dream Master and rolls back to his feet. (....4) Dream Master kicks Cade Malibu in the back of the leg. Cade Malibu gets up. (.....5) Cade Malibu and Dream Master move back to ringside. Cade Malibu and Dream Master move back into the ring. Cade Malibu hits Dream Master with a rolling elbow smash to the face. Dream Master stands up. Dream Master gets thrown into the turnbuckle. Cade Malibu comes over and smashes Dream Master's head into it. Cade Malibu nails Dream Master with a double underhook suplex. Cade Malibu goes to the top and executes a flying hip attack on Dream Master. Cade Malibu is back on his feet. Dream Master is up again. A flying shoulder block send Cade Malibu to the mat. Dream Master stands up. Cade Malibu gets back to his feet. Cade Malibu hiptosses Dream Master. Dream Master gets hit with the shooting star press from Cade Malibu. The ref starts the count. ...1 ...2 Dream Master kicks out. Dream Master climbs to his feet. Dream Master kicks Cade Malibu in the head. Cade Malibu gets hit with a diving elbow smash from Dream Master. Dream Master is up again. Cade Malibu stands up. Dream Master hits a koppo kick on Cade Malibu. Cade Malibu gets hit with the shooting star press from Dream Master. Referee Tim Martin makes the count. ...1 ...2 Cade Malibu escapes. Cade Malibu gets back to his feet. Cade Malibu goes for a side belly-to-belly suplex but Dream Master dodges the attack. Dream Master is locked into the painful bow-and-arrow submission. Tim Martin is checking for a tap out. ... ... Dream Master trys to escape. ... Dream Master is fighting the hold. Cade Malibu breaks the hold. Dream Master jumps from the top rope and hits Cade Malibu with a flying armdrag. Dream Master chants start. Dream Master gets up. Dream Master locks Cade Malibu in the odd octopus hold. Referee Tim Martin is checking for a tap out. ... ... ... ... Cade Malibu escapes. Dream Master puts Cade Malibu in an arm grapevine submission. Now Cade Malibu standing. Dream Master with an illegal chokehold on Cade Malibu. Cade Malibu gets hit with the shooting star press from Dream Master. Tim Martin counts the pin. ...1 Cade Malibu escapes. Dream Master gets elbowed to his midsection by Dream Master. Dream Master short clothslines Cade Malibu. Cade Malibu stands up. Cade Malibu picks up Dream Master and front slams him on the mat. Dream Master gets hit with the shooting star press from Cade Malibu. The ref starts the count. ...1 ...2 Dream Master kicks out. Dream Master get whipped into the corner and Cade Malibu follows himin with an avalanche. Cade Malibu chants start. Cade Malibu puts Dream Master on the turnbuckle and executes a belly-to-belly superplex. Dream Master climbs to his feet. Cade Malibu executs the slingshot facebuster, slamming Dream Master's face to the mat. Cade Malibu is up again. Cade Malibu goes off the turnbuckle with a flying sommersault splash. Cade Malibu chants start. Cade Malibu gets up. Cade Malibu Choke Slams Dream Master. Dream Master looks to be out cold! Dream Master gets hit with the shooting star press from Cade Malibu. Referee Tim Martin makes the count. ...1 ...2 ...3 [Bryan]- The winner of this match, Cade Malibu!!! Cade has been impressive, he lost his first match last week but picks up a good win here on Shockwave! Stick with us.. Tag team action is next! Mike Dupnik and Dark Angel, old friend go at it! *** Commercial Break *** [Bryan]- Back on Shockwave here on the NEW TNN! Check out Shockwave every other Sunday Nights and Warfare every other Wednesday Night! Warfare is live this week, and omitted next week. Shockwave how ever will be on back to back, broadcasting again next Sunday! Tag Team Match Dark Angel and Mystic vs. Mike Dupnik and The Project Dark Angel and Mystic come to the ring. Mike Dupnik and The Project walk to the ring. Tim Martin is the referee for this match. Dark Angel places The Project on the turnbuckle and executes a front-layout suplerplex. The Project checks out the ring. (the bell rings) Dark Angel puts The Project in the achilles tendon hold. The Project gets hit with the shooting star press from Dark Angel. Referee Tim Martin makes the count. ...1 The Project escapes. The Project is up again. Dark Angel tags Mystic. Mystic sets The Project up DDTs him into the mat. Mystic monkey flips The Project onto the mat. Mystic stands up. Mystic makes the tag to Dark Angel. Dark Angel jumps off the turnbuckle with the flying bodypress on The Project. Mystic goes off the turnbuckle with a flying sommersault splash. Mystic hits The Project with an elbowdrop. The Project gets hit with a diving elbow smash from Mystic. Mystic moves back to his feet. The Project is back on his feet. Mystic double underhook faceslams The Project hard to the The Project. Dark Angel makes the tag to Mystic. The Project sets Mystic up DDTs him into the mat. Dark Angel tagged in by Mystic. The Project kicks Dark Angel in the groin. Dark Angel brings in Mystic for Dark Angel and Mystic. Mystic is back on his feet. Mystic hits The Project with an earringer. Mystic tags Dark Angel. The Project hits Dark Angel with a heart punch. Dark Angel is back on his feet. Dark Angel gets sidewalk slammed by The Project. The Project chants start. Dark Angel moves back to his feet. The Project drags Dark Angel to the floor. Tim Martin starts the count (.1) (..2) The Project pokes Dark Angel in the eyes. (...3) The Project gets caught with an elbowsmash to the face. (....4) The Project executes the jumping sidekick on Dark Angel. Dark Angel gets back to his feet. (.....5) They head back into the ring. The Project goes to the top and executes a flying hip attack on Dark Angel. Dark Angel gets hit with the shooting star press from The Project. The ref starts the count. ...1 Dark Angel escapes. Now Dark Angel standing. Dark Angel makes the tag to Mystic. The Project hits him with a back fist. The Project tags in Mike Dupnik. Mystic stomps Mike Dupnik's head. Mike Dupnik gets up. Mike Dupnik brings in The Project for Mike Dupnik and The Project. The Project jabs Mystic. The Project executes a huge gutbuster on Mystic. The Project hits a running forearm smash on Mystic's face. The Project brings in Mike Dupnik for Mike Dupnik and The Project. The Project gives Mystic a crotch chop. Mike Dupnik climbs to the top rope and nails Mystic with a reverse flying elbow drop. Mystic clotheslines Mike Dupnik. Mystic tags Dark Angel. Dark Angel with a huge fisherman buster on Mike Dupnik. Dark Angel rakes his fingers across Mike Dupnik's back. Mike Dupnik gets snap suplexed by Dark Angel. Dark Angel is back on his feet. Dark Angel climbs to the top rope and nails Mike Dupnik with a reverse flying elbow drop. Dark Angel chants start. Mike Dupnik moves back to his feet. The Project tagged in by Mike Dupnik. Mike Dupnik jumps onto Dark Angel's shoulders, twists, and DDT's him onto the mat A springboard bulldog by Mike Dupnik sends Dark Angel's head crashing into the mat. The Project connects with a low blow. Dark Angel goes down. Dark Angel hits a koppo kick on The Project. Dark Angel tags in Mystic. The Project clotheslines Mystic. The Project makes the tag to Mike Dupnik. Mystic executes a headlock takedown. Mystic tags in Dark Angel. Dark Angel hits a frog splash on Mike Dupnik. Mystic grabs Mike Dupnik and applies an arm wrench. Mike Dupnik punches Mystic repeatedly. Dark Angel makes the tag to Mystic. Mexican hiptoss executed by Mystic. Mike Dupnik gets hit with a fisherman suplex by Mystic. Dark Angel does a handspring and hits Mike Dupnik with a bodyblock, what a move! Mike Dupnik goes off the top turnbuckle and double foot stomps Dark Angel. Mike Dupnik gets back to his feet. Mystic tags Dark Angel. Dark Angel clotheslines Mike Dupnik. Mystic jumps from the top rope and hits Mike Dupnik with a flying armdrag. Mystic grabs Mike Dupnik's head and DDT's him on the mat. Flying somersault drop kick by Mike Dupnik puts him back in the match. Mike Dupnik tags The Project. The Project hits Dark Angel with an elbowdrop. Dark Angel makes the tag to Mystic. The Project rolls onto Mystic connecting with a knee. Mystic is up again. The Project puts Mystic on the turnbuckle and executes a belly-to-belly superplex. The Project gets up. The Project sends Mystic to ringside. Tim Martin starts the count (.1) The Project swings a Steel chair and hits Mystic. Mystic is bleeding as a result. Mystic is up again. (..2) Mystic chops The Project. (...3) Mystic connects with a flying knee. The Project goes down. (....4) Mystic measures The Project up and drops a closed fist. Mystic catches The Project leg, but The Project reverses it with an enzuigiri to Mystic's head. The Project climbs to the second turnbuckle and moonsaults onto Mystic. The Project is up again. They head back into the ring. The Project executes a flying knee drop right to the gut of Mystic. The Project moves back to his feet. The Project nails Mystic with a belly-to-back suplex. The Project chants start. The Project moves back to his feet. The Project uses a legsweep faceslam to bring Mystic down. Mystic gets hit with the shooting star press from The Project. The ref starts the count. ...1 ...2 ...3 [Bryan]- What a match, a long one at that! The winners of this match, Mike Dupnik and The Project!!! And we are a littl over due for a commercial! We;ll be back and cap off the night with a match between Benny Bodacious and Destroyer... Should be a good one! *** Commercial Break *** XCWF Shockwave goes back on the air as Benny Bodacious makes his way to the ring to cheers from the crowd. He gets into the ring as he waits on Destroyer and the match to start... [Bryan]- Well, Benny Bodacious and Shawn Boyd will take on the Tag Team Champions, Jason Smith and Donovan Simmons this Wednesday for the belts. Benny and Shawn made fun of the champions on Fear saying that they were not going to make good champions. Jason and Donovan were a bit offended and issued the challenge! Standard Match Destroyer vs. Benny Bodacious Destroyer walks to the ring. Tim Martin is the referee for this contest. Destroyer gets nailed with a charging axhandle bodyblock from Benny Bodacious. Destroyer tests out the ropes. (ring, ring, ring) Benny Bodacious discus punches Destroyer. Destroyer stands up. Benny Bodacious goes off the top turnbuckle and double foot stomps Destroyer. Destroyer gets hit with the shooting star press from Benny Bodacious. Tim Martin counts the pin. ...1 Destroyer escapes. Destroyer gets up. Benny Bodacious nails Destroyer with a belly-to-back suplex. Now Destroyer standing. Destroyer executes a neck scissors on Benny Bodacious. Destroyer is up again. Destroyer grabs Benny Bodacious's arm, drops down, and has him in an arm grapevine. Benny Bodacious's arm is just being TWISTED by an arm grapevine by Destroyer. Benny Bodacious stands up. Benny Bodacious gets thrown into the corner and gets boot choked by Destroyer. Destroyer pins Benny Bodacious against the ropes and chokes him with his forearm. Destroyer executes the brain buster on Benny Bodacious. Now Destroyer standing. Destroyer covers Benny Bodacious. The ref starts the count. ...1 ...2 Benny Bodacious kicks out! Benny Bodacious moves back to his feet. Benny Bodacious gets thrown into the corner and gets boot choked by Destroyer. Destroyer holds Benny Bodacious in the corner, choking him with his forearm. Benny Bodacious kicks Destroyer in the stomach. Destroyer is back on his feet. Destroyer pins Benny Bodacious against the ropes and chokes him with his forearm. Benny Bodacious gets thrown into the corner and gets boot choked by Destroyer. Destroyer executes a neck scissors on Benny Bodacious. Destroyer climbs to his feet. Benny Bodacious stands up. Destroyer pins Benny Bodacious against the ropes and chokes him with his forearm. Destroyer executes the brain buster on Benny Bodacious. Destroyer climbs to his feet. Destroyer grabs Benny Bodacious's arm, drops down, and has him in an arm grapevine. Destroyer sucks chants start in the crowd. Benny Bodacious gets back to his feet. Destroyer executes the brain buster on Benny Bodacious. Destroyer climbs to his feet. Destroyer executes a neck breaker. Destroyer goes for a pin. Tim Martin counts the pin. ...1 ...2 ...3 [Bryan]- Destroyer escapes with the win and leaves Benny shocked in the ring! Could this happeno n Wednesday to Benny against Jason Smith and Donovan Simmons?! He should hope not... For the XCWF, I am Bryan Kaminski... Good night and we'll see you on Warfare!! A stunned Benny Bodacious is on his knees in the ring as Destroyer smiles walking up the ramp with his hands raised high in the air. The crowd somewhat boos for Destroyer as the camera fades out to an XCWF Wednesday Warfare promo... *** Camera fades to black *** XCWF WARFARE!! LIVE! Every other Wednesday on TNN from 9 p.m. to 11 p.m.!! This week the XCWF will be in the Providence Civic Center in Providence, Rhode Island!!!
