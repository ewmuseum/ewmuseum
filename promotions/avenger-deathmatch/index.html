Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Avenger Deathmatch was not an e-fed with a roster or a title system — it was a weekly novelty attraction on a web-design operator's own personal site, "Avenger Unlimited." Its own premise, in its own words: find two opponents "who have something in common," run "perfectly duplicated computer generated simulations of each person into our computer(N64) using a specially designed program," and let them fight until one side won, with a new matchup posted every week. The one surviving matchup pits Luke Skywalker against Neo, billed "A Super Sci-Fi Slobberknocker."

The same site's own company history dates itself to a December 1998 conception, following two earlier, since-closed sites the same operator ran (The Smackdown Hotel, then Hardcore News), and names a separate, sibling site born the same day: The Avenger's House of Wrestling, an actual e-fed run by the same operator and not held here. The site's own front page restates the same matchup in its own words — "The Battle Of The Summer Sci-Fi Box Office Hits" against the dedicated page's own "A Super Sci-Fi Slobberknocker" — and separately names the sibling site's own address.

Luke Skywalker and Neo are billed under their own canonical names, played straight rather than as a ring-name homage, so both are minted as portrayals of the figures they name. Neither document that survives here says who won: both point their own "click here to see the match" link at a second GeoCities account never captured in this holding.
