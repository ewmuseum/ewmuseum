Now, we can do this the easy way or we can do it the hard way, it's your choice. So, what’s it going to be Jake? Are you going to give me back the belt?
[Circling the ring to the announcer’s table, Morgan drops his belts on the table before plopping his partner into Butz’ lap. Thinking it over, Jake runs a hand over his chin.]
Jake Morgan: Hmmmmm ... No, I don't think so. I think I'll keep it. [Newton kicks the bottom rope in anger.] The way I see it, Newt, your boy attacked me. He lost. He tried to prove that he could hang with a true Champion like myself and he failed. It's time you, and your little upstart promotion learned that failure carries SEVERE consequences. You want your belt back? Come take it.
You want me to apologize? Come make me. You want to Humiliate me? How? I beat your best, Skip Westervelt in twenty seconds. I pounded both you and your bodyguard, into the floor. I destroyed your Junior Heavyweight Champion. So, who's left to defend your little promotion? That fat tub of goo, Chip?
[Jake points at the beaten duo inside the ring.] Don’t think so. Been there, done that. Those mental idiots, Demented? Or maybe even the border swimmers, Mexico's Most Wanted? [Newton nods his head vigorously as Brody invites Jake to the ring.] Bring 'em on. One at a time or all at once, because if that's the best you've got, this belt will stay around my waist for a long time.
[As Jake rants, Buddy Lee Jr. approaches from behind with a chair in hand. He raises it and just before he hits Jake in the back with it, he is caught by a mule kick from Jake. Without hesitation, Jake spins and grabs the bent over
Buddy Lee, dropping him with a DDT. Back on his feet, Jake flips off Newton, before laying the boots to Buddy Lee. He stops suddenly, scoops up his belts and jumps into the crowd as the entire X roster runs out to save their champ. Jake makes good his escape with both of his titles as the program comes to an end.]
