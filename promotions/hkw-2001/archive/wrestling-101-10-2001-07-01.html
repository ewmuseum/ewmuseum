Wrestling 101 — No. 10

1 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 3 —

[The camera opens on the back door of the Hard Knocks venue as it opens and HKW newcomer Gravis Knight steps through. Inside the building, he is quickly ushered to the side behind a rope barricade by both members of Blue Light
Special.] Harry Palmer: No cutting in line! We were here first. [The confused Knight looks up and down the small line of gathered fans before turning back on the two at the front of the line.]
Gravis Knight: What?!?!? Harry Palmer: Shhhhh ... he’s going to be here in any second! [Palmer suddenly spins around as the door opens and a cheer goes up from those waiting in line, several holding handmade signs with Jason Flint’s name on it. The cheer suddenly dies when HKW intern Neal steps into the building.
With a disgusted wave, Knight turns to walk off, stepping right into the feathered Mean Green mascot.]
Mean Green: There you are. I ... errrr ... someone clogged the toilet ... [Shoving the big bird back, Knight storms off as a cheer goes up behind him when the door opens once again. Leaving them, the camera cuts to Jason Flint, dressed up in a pair of jeans and a red mechanic’s shirt, making his way through the array of personnel. With a smile on his face, he tosses his bag into a corner as he looks upon the hustle and bustle of Hard Knocks.
His smile melts away when the State Club Boyz and Ghost make a beeline for him. Leaning heavily on his crutch, Curtis looks Flint over with a big grin on his face.] Curtis: Look at what we got here, boys ... a washed up dud. What are you doing here, loser.
[Curling a smirk, Flint pushes his red lensed sunglasses onto his head and looks Curtis dead in the eye.]
Jason Flint: Guys ... good to see you, too. Glad to see life’s treating ya right. [With a big smile, Flint pats a bewildered Curtis on the shoulder before an angered Tony steps into his face.]
Tony: Maybe you didn’t hear what Curtis said? [Throwing his hands up as he takes a step backwards, Flint smiles at the trio.] Jason Flint: Whoa, there compadre, I’m not looking for any trouble. [Grabbing up his bag, Flint walks quickly off.] Gotta run, guys ... maybe we can get together later ... [The confused trio watches Flint’s “big thumbs up” before the camera breaks away, this time focussing in on a pacing Lars while Raoul gives a seated
Backyard Billy a manicure.] Lars: Oh! They are gonna be sooooooo shocked. Raoul: And overwhelmingly excited! Backyard Billy: Guys, is this manicure thing really necessary? I mean come on ... and Lars, it’s backyard not backdoor. [With a limp wristed wave, Raoul shushes Billy.] Raoul: Will you please stop squirming? And yes, this is necessary ... you’re one of “US” now.
Backyard Billy: One of “US”? Lars: I can’t hear myself think over here. I hope everything turns out perfect. The creamy bisque, the wine, the escargot, the creme brulee ...
Backyard Billy: Uhmmmm ... guys, shouldn’t we be more focussed on our tag team match against Blue Light Special?
Raoul: Larsypoo, quit worrying. Everything's gonna be awesome. You hired the best AND, you’re gonna be acting as waiter. How can anything go wrong?
Lars: You never know in this place ... [A concerned Lars fades away as “Debonaire” by Dope begins to play. Along with the pounding beat, faces flash by quickly accompanied by their names. Men and women of all sizes and shapes as well as all manner of dress.
The “wiggas” DJ Stiff and Mo of CTD - the preppy State Club Boyz, Curtis and Tony - the enigmatic Ghost - an angry Angel Rotten - identically dressed in
“Super K” smocks, the members of Blue Light Special, Harry Palmer and Dean “the Machine” McQueen - the pink clad pair known as Pretty in Pink, Lars and Backyard Billy - and finally, the HKW Texas Outlaw Champion, Jake Morgan.
As the last of the pictures fade, the logo of Hard Knocks Wrestling explodes into view before being swallowed up by inky blackness along with the ending of the noisy music.
Switching to the inside of the venue, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz sitting ringside.] Howard Butz: Good evening fans! [Taking a swing through the audience, the camera picks up the small but boisterious crowd with it’s signs, all trying to get the camera’s attention.]
Howard Butz here and we’re coming to you from the School of Hard Knocks in Lewisville, Texas ... on KDFI, Channel 27! [Butz’ voice is cut off and the camera makes another run through the crowd as a booming voice is heard over all the background sounds.]
Voiceover: You are watching KDFI, Channel 27 - Home of the Texas Rangers! [Sound is returned to normal on Butz.] Howard Butz: Big show tonight with our first opening round tag team match between Blue Light Special and Pretty in Pink. We also have a main event match that should be very entertaining as ...
[Butz stops when the fans start booing as HKW Texas Outlaw champ, Jake Morgan steps out from the back with his title belt over his shoulder. Making his way towards Butz, Morgan tosses down his belt on the table before having a seat.]
Jake .... Jake Morgan: Yeah, good to see you too, Putz ... I mean, Butz. Howard Butz: What can I do for you, Jake? Jake Morgan: Do for me? Have a seat and ask YOUR champ your stupid questions. Howard Butz: We’re about to start the first match ... Jake Morgan: On MY show, we start when I say so ... not before. Howard Butz: Uhm ... okay, Jake. As we head into our first week of the tag team tournament, do you care to elaborate on who your partner is going to be?
[With a laugh, Morgan turns with a smile when Shawn Mullins’ “Lullaby” blasts out from the PA.]
Jake Morgan: Awww ... too bad, looks like we’re ready for our first match ... [In black tights and a white t-shirt with "RAUNCHY" across the front of it, newcomer Gravis Knight steps out from the back, with mic in hand while making several lewd gestures, bringing on the boobirds before sliding into the ring.
Pacing back and forth, Knight stops, bringing the mic to his lips.] Gravis Knight: It's about time that you sleeze balls shut your damn mouths. [Knight grins as it has just the opposite effect on the small but packed crowd.] I understand here in Texas, there’s a saying about steers and q<BLEEP>s but the only thing I’ve seen so far is a whole lot of chickens ...
Oh yes, this whole town ... all 77,000 of you are little itty bitty CHICKENS! You ... in the shadow of Denton and just thirty six miles away from Dallas ... are all a bunch of chickens. You, like the people in the back are a bunch of feathery little peckers who don’t know the first thing about respect. You see someone in a REAL place, in a REAL city knows how to treat someone like me properly. Oh yes, they would, people from New York, from Atlanta and especially from my home town of Chicago would show the proper respect for a true champion.
[Knight starts pacing again when the boos turn to cheers as the Mean Green mascot in all his featheryness steps out from the back, a trail of toliet paper stuck to his foot. Climbing into the ring, the “poultry of power” swipes the mic away from Knight, bringing a cheer.]
Mean Green: You really need to fix that overflowing toilet in the back. It’s starting to smell ...
[With the fans laughing, Knight nails the mascot with a forearm to the beak, knocking him back into the ropes. With referee Orly Diaz hitting the ring, he calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Whipping his opponent across the ring, Knight knocks him down with a well-timed drop kick to the kisser before coming off the middle ropes with a knee drop.
Howard Butz: Looks like we have ourselves match, Jake. Jake Morgan: Yeah, this should be a good one. The big bird taking on the big mouth. After a back body drop, Knight hits a elbow drop foregoing a cover for a little strut around the ring.
Howard Butz: Knight has talent as we saw last week, Jake but this is his television debut. Jake Morgan: Oh, this guy wrestled last week? Howard Butz: You know he did, Jake. Knight with a single leg takedown. Working the leg over now with a spinning toehold.
Jake Morgan: You know, watching this has made me hungry. I could go for a drumstick. Driving his boots into the back of the bird’s thigh, Knight teases stomping on his privates but draws a warning from the ref. Holding the bird by the ankles, Knight drops to the mat with a legdrop that draws a groan from the male fans in attendence.
Howard Butz: That had to hurt, Jake. Jake Morgan: What? Did you say something? Sending Green across the ring, Knight catches him around the waist on the return, taking him over with a belly-to-belly suplex. With his opponent down,
Knight paces the ring taunting the booing fans, dipping his head through the ropes to get a little closer to his adoring fans but is pulled back in by the ref.
Jake Morgan: If nothing else, he was right about the chicken part. Howard Butz: I think you were part of the group he was referring to. Jake Morgan: Hate to differ with you there, Putz but I was right here with you when he was addressing the guys in the back but if he wants some, I’ll be more than happy to put him in his place and show him the pecking order.
Howard Butz: Right. Knight working the legs now. There’s a boot to the knee. He is punishing the leg. Mean Green down on one knee. KICK TO THE HEAD! Bouncing off the ropes, Knight shoots forward, hitting his opponent with a somersault leg drop. Sitting in place, Knight waits for a count.
Howard Butz: Knight with the cover. ONE! TWO! Shoulder off the mat. A little too nonchalant with the cover there, Jake.
Jake Morgan: A chicken fried steak sounds good right about now. Howard Butz: There’s a whip into the corner and Knight following with a big clothesline. A second Irish whip into the opposite corner is followed by another clothesline. Stepping out of the corner, Knight moves to the center of the ring waiting for his staggered opponent.
Howard Butz: Knight setting Mean Green up here ... Jake Morgan: Mashed potatoes, gravy, cole slaw, buttermilk biscuit ... Grabbing the bird around the head, Knight falls back to the mat, planting him into the canvas with a DDT.
Howard Butz: THE STICK! THERE’S THE STICK! Jake Morgan: Yeah, yeah ... the Stick’s been stuck and I’m outta here! Leaving the table, Morgan disappears into the back while inside the ring, Knight mounts the ropes. Leaping off the top, he spins his body through the air, crashing down on top of the flattened bird with a corkscrew splash.
Howard Butz: AND THERE’S THE STAIN! KNIGHT HOOKING A LEG FOR THE COVER! ONE! TWO! THREE! *** Ding *** Ding ***

Howard Butz: Gravis Knight with an impressive television debut victory!
[Pulling away from the ref and threatening him, Knight heads for the back after tormenting fans on his way before the camera breaks away.]
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
Taco Bell is now open later. So get your fat ass off the couch and come get fatter! Yo quiero Gordo Americanos!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [With the break over, the camera finds the Pretty in Pink team making it’s way through the back.]
Backyard Billy: So, what’s the plan for tonight’s match, Lars? [Flipping his pink boa over his shoulder, Lars shoots Billy a look.] Lars: Plan? I think you can see that my schedule is full with tonight’s exciting dinner reunion. I don’t have time to wrestle.
Backyard Billy: WHAT?!?!?! Raoul: Do be a dear and win one for the team, Billy. [The pair walk off, leaving Billy alone when the camera leaves him to find Jake Morgan outside of his dressing room looking at a letter from an envelope that was taped to his door.]
Jake Morgan: NEEEAAAALLLL!!! [Not budging, Morgan waits impatiently, arms folded, for the arrival of the HKW intern when a worried looking Neal runs into view.]
Neal: Whats up? [Morgan shoves the paper into the intern's hands.] Jake Morgan: What in the hell is this? I've got better things to do than attend some idiotic meeting during the show. Go tell that boss of yours to stick ...
Neal:It’s not corporate stationary but it must be something ... especially since Flint got one, too.
Jake Morgan: Flint? I'll be there! [Turning his back on the intern, Jake retreats into his dressing room when a voice shouts out his name. Turning, Morgan grins at Angel Rotten standing there.]
Angel Rotten: Look, I need a partner for the tag tournament. You need a tag partner for the ...
[Breaking out into laughter, Morgan shakes his head as he dips back into his dressing room, slamming the door behind him. Leaving an angry Rotten, the camera returns to ringside and Howard Butz.]
Howard Butz: Next up, we have our first match in the tag tournament ... [Suddenly the lights dim and a blue spotlight begins to flash through the venue.] Howard Butz: And here comes Blue Light Special! [Harry Palmer and Dean McQueen make their way to the ring, slapping hands with fans brfore rolling inside. After a rather warm welcome, Palmer asks Butz for a mic.]
Harry Palmer: No one should have to pay high prices, yet over the last few weeks, we’ve been attacked at every turn because Pretty in Pink is determined to sell you their overpriced products when they know full well, you can get the same thing at your nearest K-Mart store.
All of you good people, in your trailers, should not be expected to fork over your hard earned minimum wages on things that you can elsewhere at up to seventy percent cheaper.
[The fans start booing Palmer as he continues.] Oh, don’t you think for one second, I don’t feel your pain. It doesn’t take one with your high school GED’s to understand how you feel because we do and that’s why we are here for you ... all the little people!
We’ve stood up for you, the little people but Pretty in Pink continues to push us and push us ... well, why don’t we just roll the tape ...
[Taped footage begins to roll with the camera focussing in on the floor and several cream puffs. A large black boot comes slamming down, squishing the delectable treat then another and another until there is only one left.
Focussing in on the last one, an arm sweeps in and snatches it up. Moving upwards, Dean McQueen is brought into view, a big grin on his face as he eyes the cream puff, bringing the footage to an end.]
You see, we’ve reached the end of our rope here ... [With Palmer pointing at McQueen, the camera zooms in on the tight lipped wrestler. Opening his mouth wide, McQueen reveals his blue colored tongue, teeth and gums.]
Now ... it’s personal!!! [The camera moves back to Howard Butz as “It’s Raining Men” begins to play.] Howard Butz: Blue Light Special not exactly endearing itself to these fans ... wait here comes Pretty in Pink ... no, just Backyard Billy! [With the music blasting, Billy charges out from the back, sliding into the ring as the booing turns to cheers for the pink clad wrestler. Alone, Billy comes up throwing punches on both men. With the action underway, the ref calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: There’s the bell but no Lars in sight. Is he fashionably late or is Billy going this one alone?
With the ref pushing McQueen out of the ring, Billy sends Palmer across the ring, taking him down with a head scissors on the return. Rolling back to his feet, Billy hits the corner, busting McQueen in the chops before hammering
Palmer back to the mat with a clothesline. Howard Butz: Billy’s a bundle of energy, taking the early advantage on his opponents. Scoop slam and a cover. ONE! Palmer kicks out.
Leading Palmer around the ring out of an arm bar, Billy slams Palmer’s head into the far turnbuckle. Spinning the dazed man around, Billy throws him rounghly back into the corner before opening up with reverse knife hands.
Howard Butz: The fans counting each blow as they land. Here comes McQueen into the ring. Orly Diaz cutting him off.
After using a shoulder to batter Palmer’s midsection, Billy puts the man over his shoulder, falling forward into the center of the ring, slamming Palmer down.
Howard Butz: There’s a cover. ONE! TWO! THR ... NO! McQUEEN WITH THE SAVE! WIth a warning from the ref, McQueen’s boot to the back of Billy’s head does the job, allowing a dazed Palmer to make it back to his corner for a tag.
Into the ring, McQueen pulls Billy to his feet, sending him into the ropes and hitting with a double chop to the chest.
A legdrop later, McQueen makes a quick tag to his partner where they hit with a double elbow drop. With Diaz counting, they floor Billy with a double vertical suplex before McQueen makes an exit.
A knee lift and a follow-up clothesline send Billy to the outside allowing McQueen to work their opponent over while Palmer distracts the referee. Howard Butz: Having no partner is starting to tell on Billy now. There’s a running knee to the head and a whip into the ring barricade from McQuen on the outside while Palmer distracts Diaz. WAIT ... WE HAVE TO BREAK FOR A
COMMERCIAL! DON’T GO AWAY! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Back from the break finds all three men inside the ring ... Billy locked in a full nelson by McQueen while Palmer slaps his face. With the fans booing loudly, Palmer steps back as Diaz tries to restore order. Falling forward,
McQueen drives Billy’s face into the canvas. Howard Butz: THE PRICE CUT! McQUEEN WITH THE COVER! Refusing to count, the ref wants McQueen to leave the ring as his partner in the legal man. With a boot to Billy’s back, McQueen leaves the ring. Whipping
Billy into the far corner, Palmer follows him in with a corner splash before working him up the ropes. Climbing up, Palmer wraps his legs around Billy’s neck, throwing him over with a hurricanrana. Tagging in McQueen, the pair bounce off opposite sides of the ring, nailing Billy with a pair of splashes.
Howard Butz: There’s a high five and a pair of elbow drops. Billy really starting to be worked over.
Palmer out, McQueen snaps Billy over with a suplex then wrapping him up in a rear chinlock. Pulling up in Billy’s head, McQueen asks the ref for a submission. Fighting for all he’s worth, Billy works himself back to his feet, bringing a cheer from the fans.
With a back elbow, Billy works himself free. Shooting himself off the ropes, he’s set for a comeback when he runs into a knee to the back from Palmer on the ring apron.
Howard Butz: Back to the double teaming ... there’s a powerslam from McQueen and Palmer “Slashing Prices” with a leg drop off the top rope. McQueen with the cover. ONE! TWO! NO! BILLY WITH THE SHOULDER UP AT THE LAST SECOND! WHERE
IS LARS? With the fans chanting Billy’s name, he continues to be worked over, running into a double clothesline before they follow with double elbow drops off the top rope.
After barely escaping another pin attempt, Billy rolls to the outside where he is again worked over, lifted into the air and dropped on top of the barricade.
The chanting leads into a big cheer as Lars emerges from the back, rushing to Billy’s aid. Howard Butz: THERE HE IS! IT’S LARS! Rushing ringside, Lars lays into both of his opponents, taking them both out before leading Billy back to his corner with Raoul’s help. With the legal men back in the ring, Billy is once again on the recieving end of a beating as
Palmer powerbombs the young man. Howard Butz: There’s a tag. McQueen and Palmer with a double whip ... BILLY WITH THE TAG! HERE COMES LARS! Lars into the ring let’s loose, nailing both men with clotheslines. Pushing PAlmer into the corner, the stinging limp wristed knife hand chops fly, dropping Palmer to the seat of his pants. Dashing across the ring, Lars flies into the air, nailing Palmer with a bronco buster, bouncing up and down on his opponent’s face. With a glazed look, Lars is pulled off of Palmer by the ref.
Howard Butz: McQueen pulled into the ring. What is that? A face smothering? Oh, that is disgusting! Come on referee, break that up!
Whipping McQueen into the corner, Lars charges in with his backside splash, doing it several times, lingering a little too long on the final one. After booting him from the ring, Lars puts Palmer into a standing head scissors before flipping him up onto his shoulders. Holding Palmer in the air for a long time, Lars sits down into a powerbomb before hooking his legs in his pumping pin.
Howard Butz: The disgusting cover. ONE! TWO! THREE! PRETTY IN PINK ADVANCES TO THE NEXT ROUND! *** DIng *** Ding *** [With Billy’s head on Raoul’s shoulder, the Pretty in Pink team celebrates their victory before the camera breaks away for another commercial.]

— Part 3 of 3 —

*** COMMERCIAL BREAK ***
Commercials, commercials, commercials - all in the name of “free television”. We endure this crap just so we don’t have to pay for our programming but is it really free? Not really because when you think about it, every hour long block of television is spliced with twenty to nearly twenty five minutes of commercials. And to make matters worse, for our “free television”, we’re dishing out anywhere from thirty to seventy-five bucks a month to the cable companies just to have a few worthwhile channels on top of tons of crap channels that no one watches.
On a positive note ... drink Dr. Pepper! It makes the world taste better! Nothing like being hooked! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** {Returning from the commercial, the camera peers into the ring where Skip Westervelt chats with referee Orly Diaz while pointing to a small table set up in the center of the ring ... a table with two placesettings as well as a bouquet of flowers. The shrugging referee has no answer for it when Jake
Morgan, with a disgusted look on his face, in a black HKW T-shirt, blue jeans and boots makes his way towards the ring, in his hand is an envelope.]
Jake Morgan: All week long, I've been hearing about one thing. One man's name is on everyone's lips .... FLINT. For some reason everyone is excited to see the man who a little more than six months ago, said he was too good to wrestle in small promotions. A man who claimed that he was too good for the minor leagues, that he was on his way to the big time. What happened to his plans, his "big career"?
[Jake smiles.] I'll tell you what happened. He lost his first match and didn't step foot in the ring again, until now. Apparently that doesn't matter to the fans. They are just happy to have him to cheer for again. For awhile I was confused by that but I've finally figured it out. Do you have any idea why the people react to you that way, Flint?
[Once again his face splits in a grin.] It's simple really. They identify with you. You see, you're their HERO, because you are just like most of them. You're a has-been. Most of these toothless rednecks were High school athletes or cheerleaders. They all had dreams of being the best there is at their chosen sport but then life kicked them in the teeth, and they learned the truth ... they never had what it took to be "THE BEST." They didn't have the fire, the determination or the drive to be professionals, so they got a 9 to 5 job and got fat. Now, they get together every once in awhile, have a few beers and talk about the "GLORY DAYS."
[Morgan pauses as the booing grows louder.] When they look at you, they see themselves, a person who once had been a "STAR" but who gave up on it. A person that tossed his dream down the drain and joined the "real world." Now that you're back, they are praying that you succeed. They "NEED" you to succeed because if you can, then maybe there is hope for them.
[He stops smiling and gets a serious look on his face.] Too bad for them, because you have NO CHANCE to succeed. Things will get tough for you and you'll run and hide again. You know why? Because, once a quitter, always a quitter.
[He runs a hand along his belt.] Take some advice, Jason. Quit now and save yourself a whole lot of pain. At least my way you can look back on your "Glory Days" with some pleasure but if you get in my way, "Pleasure" won't exist in your life anymore.
[Holding up the envelope.] Now, I don’t know what this is all about but don’t think for a second, Flint, I’ve forgotten all about your cute little games! [The booing grows louder until Living Colour's "Cult of Personality" begins to blare and Jason Flint makes his way out to the ring, mic in hand. Outstretching his arms and spinning around, he soaks up the applause.
Jason Flint: Thank you all, for that fine ovation, its great to be here in Hard Knocks Wrestling. [Chants of "HKW" start which brings a smile to Flint.] Yeah, yeah ... poor HKW went out and got Jake Morgan and got a whole lot more than they bargained for. Jake Morgan ... the man, myth of not wrestling but of something I would like to call...baby back ribs! I heard this one time that
Morgan downed like seventy dollars worth of Chili's finest in one sitting. He just whooped the hell outta them! So for all you rib fans ... BEWARE! Jake Morgan may get them before you do! [With a seething Morgan, David Knox joins Flint and Morgan on the outside of the ring.] David Knox: It’s great to see you back in professional wrestling, Jason! Jason Flint: AH ... the man of the hour. Da Boss. I was sitting at home, maxing and relaxing ... working on my tan and everything, I got this phone call.
"Please, Mr. Flint, you are so great, please come down to Lewisville and show Jake Morgan what's what!" Truly touching, to be needed like that. And so I'm here, here to put my UNDEFEATED record against Morgan on the line.
My question to you is: Why? [Knox looks at Flint, shrugging this shoulders.] Now, while I appreciate your endorsement, you have to understand something ... and Jake, this goes to you as well. I’m here for one reason and one reason only ... and that’s to wrestle. I’m not here to teach anyone a lesson in humility and I’m certainly not here to teach anyone a lesson.
I think once everyone realizes that, we’ll all be alot better off. I’m here in Hard Knocks Wrestling to get back to where I was six months ago and as for this ... like you, Jake, I don’t have a clue.
[Reaching into his back pocket, he pulls forth an envelope similar to Jake’s when the State Club Boyz and Ghost march out from the back. Curtis, using one crutch marches to the front.]
Curtis: Jason, you’re here only because we let you be here. You see, things have changed from the old days. You’re no longer the top dog calling the shots. You step out of line ... we step ON you!
[Charging down to the ring, Ghost and Tony plow into Flint and Morgan, setting off a brawl on the outside of the ring. With Ghost at Morgan’s throat, Tony slams a shoulder into Flint, knocking him to the floor for a stomping.
After taking several blows, Morgan hits with a headbutt, momentarily staggering Ghost before spinning him around for an atomic drop. Pulled to his feet, Flint is sent into and over the barricade bringing the fans to their feet when Tony comes flying over the top with a splash.
With Morgan, hammering away on Ghost, Skip Westervelt joins the fray with a plancha, knocking all three to the floor as chants of “H-K-DUB!” start up.
First to his feet, Morgan cuffs Skip’s ears then puts him down with a short arm clothesline. A chop from Ghost cat hes him by surprise before a short arm knee to the stomach.
Lifted into the air, Flint’s eyerake break’s Tony’s hold and allows Jason to jump from the top of the barricade for a hurricanrana onto the floor.]
Howard Butz: We’ve got a battle going on here, folks and the ref hasn’t even called for the bell!
[Making his way back to ringside, Flint hammers Ghost from behind with a double axehandle. Grabbing both his Ghost and Morgan’s head, Flint slams them together before being taken to the floor by a Skip drop kick.
Grabbed from behind, Skip is tossed aside by an angry Tony who pulls Flint to his feet. With Tony holding Flint from behind, Curtis rams his crutch into
Flint’s midsection. Ready to take a second one, Flint reverses the hold, switching places with Tony. With Tony on his knees, Flint hits a super kick, putting Curtis down.
Spinning around, Flint is caught with a diving clothesline from the apron by Morgan.] Howard Butz: We’ve got five men battling it out on the outside of the ring. WAIT! HERE COMES LARS! HE’S IN A WAITER’S OUTFIT? [Putting the boots to Ghost, Westervelt is spun around and driven into the floor by a Morgan DDT. Yanked up by the hair, Morgan is repaid by Ghost with several right hands. A Flint suicide dive takes care of that.
Tony, trying to get to Flint hammers Skip from behind. Grabbing Skip, Morgan rolls him into the ring. The ref calls for the bell with someone finally entering the ring.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: WE HAVE A MATCH? LARS PLEADING WITH MORGAN NOT TO MESS UP THE TABLE! Whipping Skip across the ring, Morgan catches him up on the return, spinning around and driving him through the table with a spinebuster.
Howard Butz: Flint in trouble on the outside. There’s a thumb to the eyes of both men. On the apron ... FLYING CROSS BODY BLOCK! FLINT WIPING OUT BOTH MEN!
INSIDE THE RING, MORGAN’S GOT A COVER! ONE! TWO! THREE! *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: IT’S OVER! LARS IS CRYING! MORGAN’S GOT THE PIN! FLINT TOOK CARE OF GHOST AND TONY! YOU BETTER BELIEVE WE”VE NOT HEARD THE LAST OF THIS! WE’RE OUT OF TIME! [The credits begin to roll with the ending of the program.] *** Hard Knocks Wrestling©2001 ***
