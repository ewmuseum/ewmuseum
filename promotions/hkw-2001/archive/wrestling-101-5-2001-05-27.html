Wrestling 101 — No. 5

27 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 3 —

[The camera opens on HKW newcomer Tom Pierce walking into the wrestlers’ locker room and stopping in front of a beautifully wrapped gift basket done up in pink and baby blue. Lifting the basket up, Pierce looks at the cream puffs within before pulling off the attached card.]
Tom Pierce: “Tomas, Once again, welcome to HKW. Looking forward to talking with you tonight on Cloud 9. Remember, if you need ANYTHING, just call. Your new friends, L and R.”
[Looking around, Pierce scratches his head.] L & R? [Shaking his head, he looks momentarily startled by the tap on his shoulder from Blue Light Special member, Harry Palmer.]
Harry Palmer: Greetings. [Palmer and his partner, Dean McQueen’s eyes light up as Pierce spins around, holding the gift basket before him.]
Dean McQueen: Cream puffs? Don’t mind if I do! [Taking the basket from Pierce’s hands, McQueen steps out of the way as Palmer steps up, in his hands what appears to be several CD’s.] Harry Palmer: We’d like to welcome you to the promotion and as such, we’re here to offer a little help if we can. K-Mart had this close-out on all this hip music, so Dean and myself thought this might make your music selection just a little easier ...
[Palmer winks as he hands over the stack of CD’s to Pierce. Looking through the selection, Pierce peers up at the smiling man.]
Tom Pierce: This is a joke, right? Gloria Gaynor? Billy Ocean? The Best of the BeeGees? Harry Palmer: No need to thank us. It’s just our way of welcoming you to Hard Knocks Wrestling! [After slapping Pierce on the shoulder, Palmer leads McQueen away.] Hey! Don’t bogart the cream puffs! [An unbelieving Pierce looks from the CD’s to the two men walking off, shaking his head. Leaving the locker room, the camera focusses in on three identical impossibly thin, blonde haired men directing HKW personnel as they carry in crates through the hallway.
All three check off each box that passes by on their clipboards when Neal the intern approaches. Before uttering a word, a hand is held up in his face, stopping him in his tracks.]
Blonde #1: Young man, there must not be ANY interruptions if we are expected to get everything set up in time!
[The three leave Neal rolling his eyes as they chase off after the equipment when “Debonaire” by Dope begins to play. Along with the pounding beat, faces flash by quickly accompanied by their names. Men and women of all sizes and shapes as well as all manner of dress.
The preppy State Club Boyz, Curtis and Tony - the young baseball bat wielding Backyard Billy - an angry Angel Rotten - identically dressed in “Super K” smocks, the members of Blue Light Special, Harry Palmer and Dean “the
Machine” McQueen - the tough but husky Tom Pierce - the pink clad Lars, also known as the Lone Stranger - and finally, Jake Morgan.
As the last of the pictures fade, the logo of Hard Knocks Wrestling explodes into view before being swallowed up by inky blackness along with the ending of the noisy music.
Switching to the inside of the venue, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz sitting ringside with HKW owner David Knox and Shane standing nearby.] Howard Butz: Welcome to Hard Knocks Wrestling! I’m Howard Butz and with me is owner David Knox!
[Taking a swing through the audience, the camera picks up the boisterious crowd with it’s signs, all trying to get the camera’s attention.]
We’re coming to you from the School of Hard Knocks, here in Lewisville, Texas ... on KDFI, Channel 27! [Butz’ voice is cut off and the camera makes another run through the crowd as a booming voice is heard over all the background sounds.]
Voiceover: You are watching KDFI, Channel 27 - Home of the Texas Rangers! [Sound is returned to normal on Butz.] Howard Butz: Thank you for joining us tonight, David. David Knox: It’s my pleasure. Howard Butz: After last week’s fireworks, what do we have in store for tonight’s program? David Knox: Last week was certainly exciting, Howard, even if things didn’t turn out exactly as I had hoped ...
[“The Boys Are Back In Town” plays over the PA system announcing the arrival of Curtis and Tony of the State Club Boyz. With mics in hand, the pair approaches the table.]
Howard Butz: We’re being joined by the State Club Boyz! [With the music cutting off, both men step up closer to the table as the fans cheer loudly.]
Curtis: We’re not out here to hold things up, so we’ll get right to the point. Last week, things got a little out of control in our tag team match against Shane and Morgan ...
[The mention of Jake’s name brings an onslaught of boos.] Some things happened and we’d like to apologize to you, Shane. Both Tony and I hope you don’t hold a grudge against us and maybe we can put this all behind us ... [The fans cheers as Shane shakes hands with both State Club Boyz which suddenly changes to boos as a clapping Jake Morgan steps out from the back.
Howard Butz: Get ready for some fireworks, folks but first, we have to break for these words from our sponsors!
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** A red and yellow decorated pizza parlor with a gorilla throwing pizza dough into the air and catching it. Mounds of fresh ingredients - pepperoni, canadian bacon, green peppers, black olives and more piled high onto piles of cheese.
With crust baked to a golden brown, welcome to Monkeyman Pizza! “When you need pizza, don’t monkey around! Call Monkeyman Pizza! Free delivery or swing on by and pick it up yourself! You’ll go bananas for
Monkeyman Pizza!” *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Coming back from the commercial break, a smiling Morgan stands there while Shane holds back both Tony and Curtis as best he can.] Jake Morgan: Isn’t this nice. I’ve got all four of you all in one place, how lucky for me? Now, before I address the three of you so-called wrestlers, I’d like to say that you’re little ploy to rattle me didn’t work, Knox. You see, no matter what you do ... keep me outside, not allow me to get ready until just before my match ... whatever you do is not going to work.
Your tactics might work on these guys but it’s not going to work on a professional like myself. Now roll the damn tape!
The fans howl as a pre-recorded clip of Morgan opening the back door to the HKW arena plays. Perplexed, he stops when two men in HKW Security t-shirts step in his way.]
Jake Morgan: What? Terry: Sorry, sir ... security check. We have to go through the bags of all the wrestlers on our list.
[He flashes a clipboard at Jake, pulling it down quickly so the contents can’t be seen.] Jake Morgan: How long is this going to take? I want to get to my dressing room and relax ...
[Terry smiles as he answers.] Terry: A few minutes, sir. [Jake follows them over to a table, where they proceed to take everything out of his bag to examine it. Jake waits patiently as Harry Palmer walks by, a large bag over his shoulders. Terry waves at him to pass.]
Jake Morgan: What the <BLEEP> was that? Terry: Don't know what you mean, sir? Jake Morgan: You just let Palmer pass ... Terry: Not on the list, sir. [Angel and Ricky Starr pass but are not checked.] Jake Morgan: And them? Terry: Not on the list, sir. If you would be so kind as to wait over here until your escort arrives ...
Jake Morgan: My escort? What in the hell are you talking about? Terry: All persons on our list are to be escorted through the building, sir. Jake Morgan: Why? Terry: Your personal safety, sir. Jake Morgan: Who else is on this list? Terry: Privileged information, sir. Have a seat, sir. Your escort to Shane’s office will be here shortly.
Jake Morgan: Shane's office? Why? Terry: Don't know, sir. Just following orders, sir. Shane should be here within the hour. Jake Morgan: An hour? <Bleep> that! Why? Terry: Don't know sir. Just doing what I'm told. [Shoving Terry away, Jake snatches the clipboard out of his hand. With an angry look, Jake throws the clipboard down, scoops up his belongings and storms off.
Zooming in on the clipboard, the camera reveals the only name on it ... JAKE MORGAN. As the clip ends, the camera returns ringside where the smiling Morgan is pointing at Knox.]
Jake Morgan: It doesn’t work, Knox ... not now, not ever! Now, as for the three of you ... [Morgan turns, pointing at Shane and the State Club Boyz.] Last week, my selfish partner saw to it to deprive these fans of seeing a true athlete but not tagging me into the match ...
Howard Butz: Hold on a second, Jake ... you wouldn’t tag in! [Shooting Butz a look, Morgan cuts him off.] Jake Morgan: No one asked you, Butz! So, shut your mouth! Now, as I was saying, these fans didn’t get to see yours truly in any action ... I’m here to fix that ... tonight, inside that ring, I am challenging both of you to a handicap match ...
[The announcement brings not only a smile to the State Club Boyz’ faces but a cheer from the fans as well.]
... but not just any match. you see that just won’t do. No, what we’re going to have is a No Disqualification Ladder match.
[Chants of “H-K-DUB!” start around the small pavillion but are waved to silence by Morgan.]
And you, Knox are going to be the special referee! [Knox nods his head in agreement as Morgan rolls into the ring.] Okay, we have our match and referee issue solved but that’s just not good enough. You see, I don’t like any one of these bastards sitting out here but each time I step into the ring, they are going to see the best damned wrestler they’ve ever seen in their pathetic lives and because of that, I have one little addition for the match that is going to put it over the top.
[Strolling around the ring, Morgan stops in the center and points up to the ceiling.] Making this one for all the marbles, hanging down from the rafters, is the Hard Knocks Wrestling Texas Outlaw belt ... [The fans go nuts as Morgan smiles down at a complaining Knox who is held back by Shane.] Curtis: Morgan, you’re on! [Knox is led away from the ring by Shane and the State Club Boyz as the camera returns to Butz.]
Howard Butz: We’ve got the debut of Tom Pierce! We’ve got a handicap ladder match to crown our first champion! Do not go away!

— Part 2 of 3 —

*** COMMERCIAL BREAK ***
Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? A second commercial opens with two men standing inside a wrestling ring. Both men looking out of place in their preppy clothing. Their names - Tony and
Curtis - the State Club Boyz, scrolls across the bottom of the screen. Curtis: So, you want to be a wrestler? Then you need to call the School of Hard Knocks because to be the best, you have to be trained by the best! Call and begin your education today!
Tony: The best money can buy! A phone number flashes across the screen as scenes of potential wrestlers working out in a ring plays.
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the break, the camera focusses in on Butz and Jake Morgan who has joined him ringside.]
Howard Butz: I’ve been joined by Jake Morgan for our first match of the evening. Jake, what do you think about our two wrestlers?
Jake Morgan: Undercard special. You have wrestling school grad versus some fat guy ... Howard Butz: Tom Pierce is not fat! Jake Morgan: Oh yeah? You could have fooled me. Anyway, these people don’t give a rat’s ass about this one ...
[Morgan is cut off as Balc Sabbath’s “Iron Man” rips through the PA system.] Howard Butz: There’s Tom Pierce making is way to the ring. [Making his way around the ring, Pierce high fives fans before rolling under the ropes. After being checked by referee Orly Diaz, the bell sounds.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Circling, the two meet in the center of the ring. Pierce rolls to Starr’s back, putting him in a hammerlock before putting him down to the mat with a drop toe hold. Floating over, the bigger man pulls Starr back to his feet with a front facelock.
Pushing him back into the corner, Pierce releases his erstwhile guillotine choke, firing in a reverse knife hand before stepping away at the ref’s command.
Locking up in the center of the ring, Pierce gets control, putting his opponent in a side headlock before pushing him off for the far ropes. Shooting back,
Starr dips his shoulder, ramming it into Pierce but having no effect. Howard Butz: We’ve got a contrast of styles here with the high flyer taking on the grounded submission fighter. I understand Pierce has a background of no holds barred fighting in his native California, Jake.
Jake Morgan: I think you had it right the first time, he should be grounded. Back in the ring, Starr tries again with the shoulder butt, getting the same result as the first try.
Howard Butz: Pierce wants him to try again? Hitting the ropes again, Starr fires forward, this time right into Pierce’s arms. Lifting the smaller Starr off his feet in a bear hug, Pierce nails him with head butt before shaking the ring with a belly-to-belly suplex.
Howard Butz: Pierce in a top mounted position, firing away with lefts and rights before being pulled off by the ref.
Jake Morgan: This is exactly what the fans want to see? You don’t think they want to see real wrestlers in a ladder match?
Whipping Starr off into the ropes, Pierce drops him to the mat with a Fujiwara armbar. Looking for the submission, Pierce put him down too close to the ropes allowing for an escape.
Howard Butz: Starr grabbing the ropes and the ref calling for the break. Jake Morgan: Some professional you’ve got there. He doesn’t even know where he is inside the ring. Have no fear fans, coming up in your main event, you won’t see any of these amateur mistakes ...
Howard Butz: Give it a rest, Jake. Pierce with a whip into the ropes. Starr shooting back ... CROSS BODY BLOCK! Starr with the cover! ONE! TWO! Pierce throws him off. That was close, Jake.
Jake Morgan: What? Did you say something? Bouncing off the ropes, Starr’s drop kick to Pierce’s knees knocks him to the mat. Moving to the ring apron, Starr comes back in with a slingshot legdrop, again putting Pierce into a pinning predicament.
Howard Butz: Starr making the cover! ONE! Pierce kicks out! Starr has a little bite left in him, eh Jake?
Jake Morgan: I think that’s the first intelligent thing you’ve said this whole match. This certainly does bite.
A drop kick puts the standing Pierce back down but he rolls out of the way of Starr’s somersault senton. Pulling Starr up by the hair, Pierce clubs him with a savage forearm before scissoring an arm and dropping to the canvas for a submission.
Howard Butz: Pierce with the arm trapped. Diaz asking Starr for the submission ... Ricky Starr hooks the bottom rope with his leg. There’s the break. Jake Morgan: Yup. Howard Butz: That’s all you have to say? Yup? Jake Morgan: Yup. Whipping Starr into the corner, Pierce finds no one at home as he charges in. Starr, dropping to his knees, pulls Pierce down with an inside cradle, putting his shoulders down for the count.
Howard Butz: INSIDE CRADLE! Starr with the pin! ONE! TWO! KICK OUT BY PIERCE! A bit wobbily from hitting the corner, Pierce pulls himself up as Starr throws himself back against the ropes. Dashing forward, he launches himself into the air ... right into the awaiting Pierce’s arms. Dropping down, Pierce slams
Starr down across his knee. Howard Butz: SIDE BREAKER! Pierce slow to get back to his feet, now, pulling Starr up and whipping him across the ring. Rearing back, Pierce waits for Starr to come back. Lashing out, Pierce stops Starr in his tracks with a blow to the chest. One punch and Starr drops like a ton of bricks.
Howard Butz: HEART PUNCH! I haven’t seen one of those in a long, long time, Jake. Jake Morgan: Yawn. Making the cover, Pierce counts along with the ref as he slaps out the three count. *** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Tom Pierce with a successful debut here in Hard Knocks Wrestling , getting the nod over RIcky Starr. Do not go away ... next up is Cloud 9 ... you don’t want to miss it!
[Butz and a frowning Morgan are replaced by thise wonderful commercials that help to keep television “free”.]
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Catch the Texas Rangers and the highest overpaid player in the league who isn’t worth the money even if he hit eighty homer runs and bats over .500 for the year! Despite the addition of Alex Rodriquez for a quarter of a billion dollars and several other aging free agents, some of which may already be on their way out, the Rangers pitching staff still sucks and the team can’t win a game. One hundred and ten plus games left and the Rangers are already out of it.
On a positive note ... drink Dr. Pepper! It makes the world taste better! Nothing like being hooked! And when your teeth rot out of your mouth, head to Castle Dental! $19 a visit including x-rays. There is no telling what kind of care you’ll get but for that price, does it really matter?
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Coming back from a commercial break, the camera pans through the crowd as “Its Raining Men” begins to play over the loudspeakers. The camera focusses in on Lars seated behind an old teacher's school desk where a chalkboard, overhead projector and dry erase board complete the classroom look.
Raoul, dressed in a blue and green plaid “schoolboy outfit” consisting of overall shorts, a white cutoff collared shirt, kneehigh socks and black loafers, lays across the desk in front of Lars.
Lars stands, by placing his hand on Raoul's rear end and pushing himself out of his chair, to reveal his latest outfit, a baby blue pantsuit and horned rim glasses as the music fades out.]
Lars: Welcome to the another edition of Cloud 9, this week, sporting a preview of things to come! I hope everyone likes it.
[The crowd begins to cheer as HKW owner/promoter David Knox makes his way from the back to the Cloud 9 set.]
Ooooh! Look everyone, it’s Davey! Hi Davey. I'm so glad you could join us this week. What a pleasant surprise!
[Lars reaches under his desk and pulls out a thermos and two styrofoam cups. He pours liquid into the cup and offers it to Knox.]
Herbal tea? I insist. David Knox: Lars, what are all the crates in the back? They’re everywhere, and no one will tell me anything except that you ok’ed it all.
Lars: Thats all the decorations and fixtures. I know its a mess right now but its only temporary and its going be great once everything’s finished ... Mr.
Principal! David Knox: Listen, before the night is over, we need to sit down and go over these proposed changes. I dont want anything too drastic. Got it?
Lars: [With a look of feigned innocence] Mwa? Drastic? Never. I'm just giving the place a much needed touch up. I'm sure once you hear the triplets plans, you'll be as excited as I am about it. After the show, I'll pick us up a bottle of wine and we can sit down alone in your office, just the two of us.
David Knox: Skip the wine, it’s not necessary. Lars: Nonsense! We'll have a grand time. Seeya later Davey. [Whispering] I'm in the middle of a show. [Knox rolls his eyes and walks off to the back.] Lars: And now, I'm proud to introduce the latest addition to our happy little school ... Tomas Pierce!
[There is a big cheer as Backyard Billy and not Tom Pierce emerges from the back. The frightened Raoul hugs Lars tightly as Billy approaches.]
Are you out here to ruin my show again? Here to tear up my set or perhaps you’re here to gloat over what you did to my face?
[Raoul shrieks when Billy snatches away Lars’ microphone.] Backyard Billy: Look, I don’t know what your problem with me is but I had nothing to do with what happened to your set ... and I certainly had nothing to do with what happened to your face, though I have to say it is an improvement!
[The fans howl as Raoul tries to break free of Lars’ grip and scratch Billy’s eyes out.] You better hold him back or I might have to smack him around some, too. Now, I don’t like you anymore than you like me ... so, let’s do this. Next week, you ... me inside that ring ... and the loser leaves town! You see, Lars this place isn’t big enough for the two of us!
[The fans cheer before Lars snatches back the mic from Billy.] Lars: I don’t want you to leave Hard Knocks Wrestling, what with that tight ... muscled ... hot ... sweaty ... [Lars gets a dreamy, far off look on his face before being snapped to reality by Raoul.] Right, where was I? Oh, yes ... I don’t want you to leave but I do want you to do something with that atrocious hair of yours!
[An angered Billy lunges forward but is stopped and pulled away by the security that has come out to seperate both parties. All three men fight to break free of their captors to get at each other but are unsuccessful as they are led away.

— Part 3 of 3 —

Making it’s way from the backstage area into the outside of the building, the camera stops on HKW security man, Terry at the back of the building, trusty clipboard in hand.
Looking up from his list, he holds up a hand when approached by a fashionably dressed man in a black suit carrying a briefcase.]
Terry: Excuse me, sir. What can I do for you? [The approaching man grins widely as he stops.] Man in suit: The question isn’t “What can you do for me.” It’s “What can I do for you!” [The grinning man nods his head at the confused security guard when the camera again is on the move, this time heading back inside where wrestler Tom Pierce is walking determindly through the hallway. Passing doors, Pierce is stopped when a door opens and the large green and white clad mascot of the University of North Texas steps out, bumping into Pierce, nearly knocking him over.]
Mean Green: Hey, why don’t you watch where you’re going! [With a disgusted wave, the tall eagle mascot walks off, muttering under his breath as Pierce is restrained by both members of Blue Light Special.]
What a moron ... [Moving back to the ring, the camera focusses in on Jake Morgan emerging from the back while "Can't Get the Best of Me" by Cypress Hill plays in the background. Making his way to the ring, Morgan stops long enough to have some words with the fans before taunting both members of the State Club Boyz already inside the ring.
Able to keep the State Club Boyz back, David Knox, as referee, checks Morgan’s boots before giving both parties last second instructions and pointing at the title belt hanging from the ceiling., then calling for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Our title match is under way, folks and it promises to be a good one! With no disqualification, it was open season on Morgan as both Curtis and Tony go on the attack, driving Morgan back into the corner, stomping him down into the mat before the ref calls for a break.
Howard Butz: I can’t tell you what Morgan was thinking when he asked for this match as it’s two on one with the man climbing the ladder walking out as the
Texas Outlaw Champion! A steady diet of double teaming keeps Morgan off balance, taking a double hip toss and following double leg drops and elbow drops.
Tony’s tilt-a-whirl back breaker sets up Curtis’ top rope leg drop, spinning Morgan off of his partner’s knee.
Howard Butz: The State Club Boyz are taking it to Jake Morgan! Tony picking up Jake ... what’s he doing? Lifting Morgan into the air, Tony holds Jake up near the ropes while Curtis climbs to the top. The fans, now on theoir feet, hold their breath in anticipation of what’s to come.
Howard Butz: Tony leaping from the top ... OH MY GOD! Curits’ drop kick sends Morgan out of the ring, dumping him onto the ring apron then out onto the floor. After sharing a high five, Tony joins Morgan outside of the ring, hammering him several times before propping him up against the ring barrier in a seated position.
Howard Butz: Curtis now pushing the ladder outside of the ring. Tony taking over ... he’s propping it up under Jake’s chin. What are they doing?
With a nod to his partner inside of the ring, Tony signals his partner into action. Bouncing back against the far ropes, Curtis takes off across the ring, throwing his body through the ropes. Hitting the propped up ladder with his back, it was an easy slide, slamming his boots into Morgan’s face. There is a huge cheer as Morgan slumps to the floor.
Howard Butz: OH MY GOD! BOTH BOOTS TO MORGAN’S FACE! Chants from the fans bring a celebration from the Boyz before turning their attention back to the match. Tony’s scoop slam onto the ladder is followed by a leg drop off the ring apron from Curtis.
Howard Butz: Working over Morgan, systematically taking him apart. This is one match, I think Jake Morgan bit off more than he can chew. It’s been the State
Club Boyz from the beginning! Heading back inside the ring, both wait until Morgan pulls himself to his feet before launching into action. Grabbing Curtis by the arm, Tony spins him around, whipping him across the ring towards the now standing ... and badly bleeding Morgan.
Howard Butz: Curtis flying out of the ring ... MORGAN MOVED! JAKE MORGAN MOVED OUT OF THE WAY! There was a loud crash as Curtis slammed full force into the ring railing, falling to the floor in terrible pain as Morgan moves out of the way at the last second. There is a stunned silence as Curtis rolls up into a ball on the floor before Jake takes the boots to him.
With his partner down, Tony leaves the ring coming to his rescue. Hammering Jake from behind with an axe handle smash, Tony knocks the man from his partner. Howard Butz: Referee David Knox is calling for help ringside! Curtis is in a bad way! Using the ring barrier, Tony attempts to choke the life out of Morgan with his boot. The bloody faced Morgan struggles, unable to breathe until a low blow knocks his foe off.
Howard Butz: Morgan with a chair in Tony’s face ... RUNNING KNEE TO THE CHAIR! A loud noise echoes through the arena as the chair smashes into Tony’s face. Pulling the bleeding Tony to his feet, Jake tries to tear the gash open further, biting into his head.
With Tony trying to put some distance between himself and Morgan, Jake follows, winding up with the chair before slamming into his opponent’s back and over his head, knocking him to the floor.
Howard Butz: The paramedics are ringside, securing Curtis while Jake pushes the ladder into the ring. What is he doing now? He’s setting up the ladder near the ropes? He wouldn’t ...
Quickly setting up the ladder near the ropes, Jake climbs to the top, looking down at the struggling Tony. Climbing onto the top step, Morgan balances himself for a second before leaping off, leaving the ring and crashing down not on Tony but Curtis, strapped down on the gurney. It collapses dumping both men onto the floor and sending the EMT’s scattering.
Howard Butz: HE DID! JAKE MORGAN JUST CRUSHED A HELPLESS CURTIS! Chants of “H-K-DUB!” explode from the fans as Morgan is very slow to get to his feet while the EMT’s help Curtis. Across the ring, Tony slides inside the ring.
Howard Butz: Tony setting the ladder up in the center of the ring and ... Jake’s up on the apron! Climbing the ladder slowly, Tony gets to the top, trying to reach the belt which is just out of reach. Hitting Tony on the leg, Jake gets his attention long enough to flip him off before pushing the ladder over. Hurtling off the top of the ladder, Tony lands on the top rope before sliding to the mat in pain, bringing a pained groan from the fans.
Howard Butz: Jake leaving the ring for a chair? Back inside the ring, Morgan takes down the ladder, slamming it down on the mat, then slamming Tony on top of ladder out of the cobra clutch. Propping the chair on top of Tony’s chest, Morgan climb’s the nearest corner to the fans’ booing.
Howard Butz: Jake climbing to the top! Thumb across the throat ... leaping ... FROG SPLASH! MORGAN WITH THE FROG SPLASH! With the refin the ring checking on Tony, Morgan sets up the ladder one handed while the other holds firmly against his ribs. Slowly pulling himself up with one arm, Morgan gets to the top.
Howard Butz: Morgan reaching for the belt ... he has it ... WE HAVE A TEXAS OUTLAW CHAMPION! JAKE MORGAN WINS! *** Ding *** Ding *** [Climbing down from the ladder, Morgan listens to the boos as David Knox hoists his arm into the air. Pulling away from Knox, Morgan levels him with a clothesline.
Standing over Knox and jawing, Morgan never sees the man joining him inside the ring. Clad all in black with a single white handprint on his shirt, a black man with a white skull painted on his face waits.
Grabbing Morgan and spinning him around, the man grabs the title belt and throws it down then drives a boot into Morgan’s midsection. Doubled over,
Morgan’s head is then driven into the title belt with a face driver. With the fans wanting Morgan’s blood, the mystery man strips Morgan’s shirt off before pushing his down on Jake’s face. Holding up a bloody paw, he slams it down onto Morgan’s chest, marking him with a handprint as the credits roll, bringing the show to an end.
