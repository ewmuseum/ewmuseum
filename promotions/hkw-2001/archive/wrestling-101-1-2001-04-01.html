Wrestling 101 — No. 1

1 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on Jake Morgan stalking the hallways in the backstage area of the Hard Knocks Wrestling venue. With his long hair hanging down in his face and a black gym bag slung over his shoulder, the abrupt wrestler bumps and pushes HKW personel out of his way before stopping in front of his dressing room door.
Pushing the door open, he enters, tossing his gym bag down onto the couch inside. An angered look overcomes him as he does a doubletake on a lovely decorated basket set upon a table.]
Jake Morgan: What the ... [Approaching the basket, Morgan pulls a small envelope free, reading the card within.] Cream puffs?!?!?! [Violently throwing the basket to the floor, Morgan sticks his head outside of his dressing room with only one thing on his mind.]
NEAL!!!!! [With Morgan’s bellow echoing into the distance, the scene changes, finding Neal the intern with a confused look upon his face standing with referee Orly Diaz before the seated David Knox.] Neal: Be nice to Lars? Are you kidding? Last week it was a footbath ... this week, he wants peeled grapes then he’s asking me these weird questions.
[Neal does a limp wristed imitation of Lars, his voice suddenly taking on a lisp.] Neal, do you dream of pink clouds? [An understanding Knox nods his head in agreement.] David Knox: Yes, Neal ... I am well aware of it. He’s been asking me the same question. It has to do with something he pitched me earlier in the week.
Orly Diaz: Pink clouds? David Knox: Uh, you don’t want to know. Anyway, Neal ... I’ll have a little talk with Lars. In the meantime, it won’t hurt to get him what he wants ...
Neal: Uh uh ... I’m drawing the line at the footbath thing. David Knox: Okay, okay ... I’ll talk to him. Did you at least hand out his cream puff baskets to everyone?
[Neal is overcome by a devilish smirk.] Neal: Oh yeah, I handed out his “special” baskets. [Turning, the intern leaves the meeting.] Orly Diaz: What about Morgan, boss? David Knox: Don’t worry about him. I’ve got that all taken care of next week ... [The camera switches to the back of the refurbished roller rink as the back door opens and a person dressed in a gorilla suit with the red and yellow hat and shirt of Monkeyman Pizza steps through carrying several pizza boxes.]
Gorilla: Pizza’s here! [Stepping forward, the gorilla is suddenly knocked to the ground. With the camera pulling back to reveal the attacker, an angered Angel Rotten delivers several well placed boots to the delivery person sprawled out on the floor.]
Angel Rotten: If you think you’re going to get a match with that stupid gimmick, you better think again, buddy ... not when I’ve been waiting for a match now for two weeks!
[Trying to sit up, the pummeled gorilla tries to defend himself.] Gorilla: But I’m not here to ... [Cutting him off, Rotten gets into his face.] Angel Rotten: I know exactly why you’re here. You just think that because I’m a woman, I’m not good enough to wrestle! Gorilla: No, not at all ... {Cutting him off, she boots him in the head.] Angel Rotten: I KNEW IT!!! Alright. Let’s see if you’re brave enough to show your face next week!
[With a final stomping, she storms off.] Gorilla: Wait ... I’m just the pizza guy ... [A door opens and out steps Lars in a pink robe, matching slippers and a towel piled high upon his head. Looking around, the Lone Stranger eyes the boxes of pizza at his feet.]
Lars: Pizza? I ordered peeled grapes ... NEAL!!!!! [Dope’s “Debonaire” begins to play before they fade from view. Along with the pounding beat, faces flash by quickly accompanied by their names. Men and women of all sizes and shapes as well as all manner of dress.
A short and powerfully built “Twizted” Gary Pate - the preppy State Club Boyz, Curtis and Tony - the young baseball bat wielding Backyard Billy - an angry Angel Rotten - identically dressed in “Super K” smocks, the members of Blue Light Special, Harry Palmer and Dean “the Machine” McQueen - the pink clad Lars, also known as the Lone Stranger - and finally, Jake Morgan.
As the last of the pictures fade, the logo of Hard Knocks Wrestling explodes into view before being swallowed up by inky blackness along with the ending of the noisy music.
Switching to the inside of the venue, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz sitting ringside.] Howard Butz: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Hard Knocks Wrestling’s ... Wrestling 101! [Taking a swing through the audience, the camera picks up the boisterious crowd with it’s signs before returning to Butz.]
We have an action packed hour tonight coming to you from KDFI, Channel 27! [Butz’ voice is cut off and the camera makes another run through the crowd as a booming voice is heard over all the background sounds.]
Voiceover: You are watching KDFI, Channel 27 - Home of the Texas Rangers! [Sound is returned to normal as the camera completes it’s loop, returning to Butz inside the ring.] Howard Butz: Last week was full of twists and turns and this week is sure to be a repeat performance. We’ve got Lars taking on Gary Pate and a main event of
Jake Morgan ... [Butz’ head snaps around as the fans starting booing. The object of their disgust? One Jake Morgan stepping out from the back. With a pizza box in hand,
Morgan has a seat at the announcer’s table.] I’ve been joined by Jake Morgan ... Jake Morgan: Save it, Butz. They all know who I am, let’s just get on with it. [The opening riffs of "Iron Man" by Black Sabbath fill the arena as The Twisted One, Gary Pate makes his entrance. He has a menacing grin on his face as he passes the bloodthirsty fans, screaming for this mad man. His eyes dart from place before catching sight of the ringside camera. He bangs his head a few times near the camera, sending his brown hair wildly up and down. He spits into the crowd before heading into the ring.
Grabbing a microphone, he waits for the crowd to quiet down before speaking.] "Twizted" Gary Pate: Last week, in front of all you fans, I was ROBBED of my first victory here in Hard Knocks Wrestling.
[A smattering of cheers filters into the ring.] To make matters worse, I lost it to a queer cowboy! And all because my stupid [BLEEP] partner couldn't get the job done. [The amount of whistles and cheering slowly increases as he talks.] So tonight, I take care of the Lone Stranger and next week I can move on to bigger and better things.
[He pauses, then raises the microphone to his lips once again.] Oh yeah, and some people have been asking me about what I think of Jake Morgan telling me to keep my eyes off of his King of Hardkore Trophy ... see folks, I can't get angry at Morgan for his remarks. He suffers from a terrible disease known as Hardcore Envy. This is where those like Jake Morgan can sense those more dangerous than him and see's this as a threat. So next time Jake Morgan goes around babbling about being the King of Hardcore, just remember... it's all in his head.
[Throwing down the microphone, Pate eyes the pizza eating Morgan who seems to be ignoring him.]
Howard Butz: Some very pointed comments thrown your way from a certified hardcore competitor, Jake.
Jake Morgan: Yeah, this is some pretty good pizza, Howie ... [“YMCA” by the Village People begins to play as the pink and powder blue “cowpoke”, Lars steps out from the back and stands at the top of the short ramp holding onto a small stick horse.
“Mounting his horse”, Lars gyrates his hips in place while smacking himself on his buttocks bringing laughter as well as a few deragatory remarks as to his sexual preferences.
Skipping along, Lars “rides” around the ring before climbing inside. After handing the ref his horse, Lars yanks off his chaps revealing his pink tights, doing a little dance, showing his glistening body.
With a little hip thrusting, he pulls free his phallic waterpistol, “shooting” his “ammo” into the crowd before “taking a drink”, shooting it into his mouth. Twirling the gun on his finger, the sated wrestler reholstered. While dropping his holstered guns, Lars directs the ref to keep the ready Pate away from him.]
Howard Butz: Gary Pate is ready to mix it up! [While checking Lars’ boots, the ref has a pelvis thrust into his face. Pushing Lars back, Diaz calls for the bell.] *** Ding *** Ding *** Charging forward, Pate misses with a clubbing forearm as Lars bails from the ring. With Lars protesting to Diaz, Pate leaves the ring with a slingshot plancha, both men hitting the floor.
With both men on their feet, it becomes a foot race as Pate chases Lars around the ring. Jumping off from the ring steps, a double axe handle slows Lars’ retreat. A whip into the ring barrier brings a cry of agony from the “Pink
Cowpoke”. Howard Butz: The referee counting both men out of the ring. Eight ... nine ... and Pate slides back in to stop the count. Climbing the ropes, Pate came flying off with a leg drop that missed it’s target as Lars rolled out of the way. Pulled to his feet, Pate was sent headfirst into the barrier. As his opponent slumped against the barrier, Lars pressed him against the structure, cutting off his air with his backside.
Jake Morgan: Hope he didn’t eat Mexican for lunch. Howard Butz: Now, that’s a disgusting thought. Rolling Pate back into the ring, Lars heard an earful from the ref about fighting on the outside of the ring. A shortarm knee to the stomach put Pate down which was followed with a leg drop from the middle ropes.
Pate rolled a shoulder up off a two count. Howard Butz: That was close! Pate sent into the ropes and taken down with a flying butt bump? What would you call that one, Jake?
Jake Morgan: Did you say something, Howie? Lars was strolling around the ring to the booing of the fans. Pulling Pate to his feet and into a standing head scissors, the Lone Stranger was all smiles lifting Pate into the air and up onto his shoulders. Holding Pate in place for a little too long gave him enough time to recover and rain down blows.
Dropping from a seated position, Pate came down right on top of Lars’ knee. Sympathetic cries of pain from the fans didn’t help any as Pate bounced away in pain having been groined.
Spinning Pate around, Lars pushed him into the corner before opening up with his limp wristed knife hands that “thwacked” loudly. Climbing up onto the ropes, Lars did a little hip gyration just inches from Pate’s face.
Howard Butz: Now, that’s uncalled for! With the fans booing, Lars was about to start punching when Pate threw himself forward, pushing Lars off the ropes and sending him crashing to the mat. From his knees, Lars backpeddled pleading with the advancing Pate. Diaz was in with a warning about Pate’s closed fist. Grabbing Lars by the hair, Pate reared back when his target winked and blew a kiss at him ... just a little too much for Gary Pate. One right between the eyes laid Lars out.
On the mat in convulsions from the blow, Lars was easy pickings as Pate dropped to make the cover.
Howard Butz: Pate with the cover ... one ... two ... and Lars rolls him over. Oh my God ... he’s trying to kiss him now. Pate’s shoulders are down ... one ... two ... and a kick out. Jake Morgan: Yeah, and there’s the guy coveting my trophy? Shaking his head, Morgan waved off the action inside the ring before leaving for the back to the booing of the crowd. Inside the ring, Pate charged the ducking Lars with a clothesline. Passing harmlessly by, Pate was surprised as
Lars goosed him from behind. Spinning around, he was taken off his feet with an arm drag and pulled back up into an arm bar.
Trying to break free, Pate missed with a back elbow as Lars dropped in with a go-behind. Standing behind Pate, Lars tried to lift the struggling man off his feet, pushing his pelvis forward each time. Despite struggling, Pate was lifted off his feet and planted into the mat with a belly-to-back suplex. Lars bridged for a two count barely missing the victory.
Howard Butz: TWO! That was a two count. Pate barely got the shoulder up in time. Lars has Pate out on his feet as he climbs the ropes ... and .. TORNADO
DDT! Oh man, this one is over! Wait ... what’s he doing now? He’s discussing fashions with the fans?!?!?!
Lars gave the locals an earful about their lack of fashion sense while Gary Pate struggled to get back to his feet. The ref got involved as he and Lars had words. With his hands on his hips, the animated Lars was not going to be talked to in that tone. Not to be shone up, Diaz started with a count ... until he was taken off his feet by a Gary Pate clothesline that was meant for Lars.
The fans howled as Pate lashed out with a front kick, doubling his opponent up. Forearms to the back drove “Pinky” to his knees when Backyard Billy rushed out from the back, a steel chair in hand. Hitting the ring, Billy motioned for
Pate to hold Lars with the ref still down on the mat. Pulling Lars up from behind, Pate held the protesting Lars as Billy worked the crowd up as he slammed the chair repeatedly against the canvas.
Howard Butz: Backyard Billy looking for a little revenge after last week’s loss ... OH MY GOD!!!!!
Swinging the chair, Billy let out a cry as he slammed it down. The loud crunch was sickening as it impacted and laid out it’s target. Pulling back the chair, Billy was shocked to see Gary Pate laying flat on his back. Spinning around, he took a boot to the gut before having his face planted into the mat thanks to a facebuster from Lars.
After rolling Billy from the ring, Lars pulled Pate to his feet. Pulling an arm through his Pate’s legs, Lars dropped behind him, thrusting his pelvis into’s Pate’s backside several times before lifting him off his feet and dropping him with a pumphandle slam - doggie style.
Howard Butz: Ewwwwww ... The fans were booing as Lars sashayed around the ring before pulling Gary Pate to the center of it. Dropping down in what can only be called a “69 cover”,
Lars was on top of his opponent as Orly Diaz crawled over and slapped out a three count. *** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 2 —

Howard Butz: This one is over, thanks to Backyard Billy, a chair and one badly
missed swing! You better believe that Gary Pate is not going to be a happy camper when he comes to!
[Holding his head, Orly Diaz lifted the victorious Lars’ hand into the air. Leaving the ring, Lars retrieved his belongings before heading back in. After wrapping his feather boa around Pate’s neck, he applied some make-up and lipstick.
Howard Butz: Oh, this is just too much! [After admiring his handiwork, Lars got a mic.] Lars: Now, that’s a start but he needs alot more work before he becomes ... yummy ... {There was a twinkle in his eye as he left the ring, “saddled up” and rode his horsey into the back. Leaving ringside and the booing fans, the program faded into a commercial break.]
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** A red and yellow decorated pizza parlor with a gorilla throwing pizza dough into the air and catching it. Mounds of fresh ingredients - pepperoni, canadian bacon, green peppers, black olives and more piled high onto piles of cheese.
With crust baked to a golden brown, welcome to Monkeyman Pizza! “When you need pizza, don’t monkey around! Monkeyman Pizza! Free delivery or swing on by and pick it up yourself! You’ll go bananas for Monkeyman
Pizza!” *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [The local commercial ends and a smooth return to the program finds Jake Morgan talking to Neal the intern in the backstage area.]
Neal: I don't know how it happened? I would assume that it's because your door doesn't have a lock on it.
Jake Morgan: That's because I thought my privacy would be respected around here. Obviously, that’s not the case. So, you need to be a good intern and
"fix it." Neal: How? [With an exasperated look, Jake puts his arm around Neal's shoulder.] Jake Morgan: Start by going to Home Depot, Lowes or ACE Hardware and getting the best dead bolt that money can buy. Install it on my door, then bring me the keys.
[Jake gives Neal a " squeeze," then starts to walk off. He turns around before leaving the room.]
It'd be in your best interest to get this done today, and before you leave ... [He points to a wastebasket with a broken decorated basket in it.) Get rid of that. [Jake walks away, leaving Neal alone in his dressing room. Neal goes over to the wastebasket, picks it up and starts to head to leave when he notices something and sets it down on a table.
Taking the broken basket apart, Neal sets each item out on the table. First, a card, then a yellow, short sleeved dress shirt and finally several crushed cream puff pastries.
Putting it all back into the busted basket, a chuckling Neal reads the card in a lisping mocking tone.]
Neal: “Dear Jacob, Here are some cream puffs to "sweeten up" that attitude of yours. I hope you like them. I spent all day yesterday slaving away in the kitchen, I felt so
"domesticated" afterwards that I had to set up an emergency visit with Raoul. You can only imagine what a pain that is. Anyway, I must be going. Toodles, Lars P.S. I also got you a "little gift." Your clothing is so drab, so I thought I'd add a little color to your life. Hope it fits.
Ta Ta.” [There was a smirk on his face as he dropped the card in with the rest of the trash and walked off, muttering under his breath.]
It’d be in your best interest to get this done today ... [With a smooth fade, the camera returned to ringside and Howard Butz.] Howard Butz: Next up is our main event as Jake Morgan takes on the other half of Blue Light Special, Dean “the Machine” McQueen. Let’s just hope that this week, Dean fares better than his partner, Harry Palmer.
[Booing from the small crowd starts up as Morgan makes his way from the back. There’s a smile on his face as the fans boo, growing louder as he pumps his fists into the air while making his way into the ring.
After arguing with the ref, Morgan turns on the fans in the front row, inviting several of them into the ring if they have something to say. The booing subsided as his opponent stepped out from the back. Following Harry Palmer, the pair circled the ring slapping hands with the fans who were hoping for a better showing this week than last week.
Entering the ring, McQueen was given some last minute instructions from the ref before the start of the match.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** As McQueen slowly came out of his corner, Morgan held up a hand stopping him. Making his way towards Butz’ position, he dipped through the ropes asking for a mic. Jake Morgan: I think last week, we got off on the wrong foot. While the two of you were just being friendly and welcoming me here with open arms, I was being an ass ...
The fans were cheering loudly at Morgan’s proclamation. They agreed wholeheartedly. Jake Morgan: Maybe, we could start over? There was a smile on his face as he stuck a hand out towards McQueen. The fans were quick to tell him of a doublecross but Morgan denied it. Looking back at his corner, McQueen got all the prompting he needed as Palmer told him it was alright. Stepping slowly forward, McQueen grasped Morgan’s hand and shook it.
He smiled as Morgan had told the truth ... that is until Morgan showed his true colors, pulling back and leveling McQueen with a short arm clothesline.
Catching McQueen in the throat, Morgan stepped over his downed body as he yelled into the microphone before throwing it down.
Jake Morgan: That looked like it hurt. He was laughing as he pulled McQueen to his feet for a bodyslam, making a cover following an elbow drop.
Howard Butz: What a rat! Morgan makes the cover ... one ... two ... Oh! He just pulled McQueen up before the ref slapped the mat a third time. Rolling McQueen to his stomach ... a step over toe hold and a crossface ...
The ref was watching closely as Morgan pulled back on McQueen’s chin, stretching him out. He asked McQueen for the submission, getting a slurred reply that he took for no. Releasing the hold, Morgan pulled Dean to his feet and into a side headlock. Pushed off, Morgan was sent across the ring. Bouncing off the ropes, he returned, slamming a shoulder into McQueen and knocking him off his feet. Hitting the ropes again, Morgan shot back, dropping a knee this time onto the downed man.
Howard Butz: Morgan with the cover ... one ... two ... oh, come on! Again he pulls McQueen up. Come on ref, do something!
Morgan sent McQueen into the ropes, nailing him with a super kick on the return. Dragging McQueen into the corner, Morgan used the ropes to propel himself into the air, coming down with a splash on top of his victim. On his feet laughing, Morgan stepped between the ropes out onto the ring apron. From there he jawed with Orly Diaz before coming back in with a slingshot leg drop.
Howard Butz: A whip into the corner and Morgan follows in with a clothesline! Stepping back from McQueen who slumped in the corner, Morgan got right into the face of the cameraman with the remote, pushing his face into the lens.
Jake Morgan: You know that had to hurt! After snapping McQueen’s head back with a forearm uppercut, Morgan pulled him out of the corner. Spinning around, Morgan sent McQueen across the ring.
Howard Butz: There’s an Irish whip ... Morgan with the ... POWERSLAM! That shook the ring. The fans are not liking this.
Morgan was on his knees gloating. Morgan’s lips were moving as he returned to his feet and started pointing at McQueen’s partner on the outside of the ring. Several fans did their best Kenny Rogers impression, throwing objects into the ring as Morgan strutted his stuff.
Climbing the ropes, Morgan balanced himself on the top while waiting for McQueen to struggle to his feet. Beaten and bruised, the dazed McQueen spun around to find his attacker. Leaping off the top, Morgan passed over the top of his victim, grabbing his neck as he went, pulling him down with a flying somersault neck breaker.
Howard Butz: That is totally uncalled for. Morgan pressing a finger into Dean’s chest for the cover ... one ... two ... and again, he does not take the pin! I don’t know if this is some sort of message for everyone in the back or if Morgan is just a sadistic bastard ...
Morgan looked bored as the ref pointed an accusing finger his way. Pulling McQueen back to his feet, Morgan spun him around and locked on the Oriental Spike. Having seen enough, Orly Diaz called for the bell, this one was over. *** Ding *** Ding *** [With the match over, Morgan was not so quick to break the hold. McQueen was limp in his arms having had the blood cut off to his brain. Diaz threatened
Morgan with disqualification, starting his count.] Howard Butz: Diaz counting on Morgan ... one ... two ... three ... four ... Morgan dropped McQueen ... HE’S GOT THE REFEREE! HE’S GOT DIAZ! [Diaz slipped free as Harry Palmer rushed the ring to save his partner as well as the ref. Charging forward, he ran right into Morgan’s awaiting arms.
Palmer’s arms flailed away as he tried to escape but to no avail. The fans were booing loudly as David Knox ran out from the back and hit the ring.] David Knox: Jake, you need to let him go this instant! [Morgan smiled at the HKW owner but didn’t release the hold. Knox circled but kept a safe distance from the two wrestlers.]
You need to release him this instant ... or else! [Morgan laughed as he pulled Palmer towards the ropes. With a sudden jerk, he lifted Harry into the air.]
Howard Butz: Wait! What’s he doing? OH MY GOD!!!!! [Throwing Palmer forward, Jake sent him crashing through the announcer’s table. There was a momentary stunned silence as Butz scrambled for cover followed by the unmistaken chanting in the background, “HOLY SHIT!” that was partially drowned out for television.
Wiping his hands, the smirking Morgan looked at the shocked owner.] Jake Morgan: There ... he’s been released. [With a laugh, the cocky Morgan walked off without a glance backwards. Midway up the ramp, he stopped as Curtis and Tony of the State Club Boyz stepped out from the back.]
Curtis: The past couple of weeks, you’ve been running all over this promotion like you owned it. Well ... next week, Jake that all ends. I’m challenging you ... one-on-one inside that ring!
[With the fans howling, a smirking Morgan nodded his head before the camera broke away from the ring and made it’s way into the back where Angel Rotten was busy eating one of Lar’s delectable cream puffs. Devouring it, she licked her fingers clean of any and all remains of whipped cream that might have escaped.]
Angel Rotten: That was really good! [Zooming in as she smiled, the camera zeroed in on her blue colored teeth. Moving into the hallway, a speeding pizza delivery gorilla ran by.] Gorilla: I’m not going make it ... I’m not going make it! [Rounding a corner and nearly barreling over Neal the intern, the Gorilla stopped.] Where’s the bathroom?!?!?! Neal: Over there ... [Not waiting a second longer, the gorilla dashed off, both hands to his backside. The devilishly grinning Neal waved a hand in front of his face.]
That smell ... [Leaving the laughing intern, the camera departed the building for the outside. In the back of the building, a lone light illuminated the doorway. Moving in closer, the camera focussed in on the single person “guarding” the entrance. A single person, sitting to the side of the door. With the camera moving in ever closer, the man was dressed in jeans and a black HKW shirt and cap, his head hanging down.
Moving in closer still, the camera focussed in on the unmoving man’s shirt, more importantly, to the symbol on his chest. A single large handprint dripping in white paint.
