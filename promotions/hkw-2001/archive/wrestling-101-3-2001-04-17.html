Wrestling 101 — No. 3

17 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on a pink clad Lars along with his friend, hair stylist Raoul busily decorating a small locker room with pastel blue and pink streamers as well as painting happy-faced flowers across several metallic lockers.
Zooming in quickly, the camera focusses in on the nameplate upon the locker. In big, bold letters is the name: JAKE MORGAN. With the camera pulling back, Lars stops his exhaustive work and rolls his eyes as he looks at his partner.]
Lars: You know, Raoul ... our work is never done. First, we help Jacob out with his decorating sense ...
Raoul: Hmmmph ... lack thereof, you mean! Lars: Whatever. Then, we have the show to get ready for. This week, Cloud 9 is going to be a doozy.
Raoul: And don’t forget your match with that charming Billy ... Lars: How many times do I have to tell you, I have way too much to do than to engage in a hot, sweaty contest with Billy ... undulating ... young ... muscles
... [Lars stops himself, getting a far-off look on his face before snapping out of it, licking his lips before taking out a handkerchief and dabbing at his forehead with it.]
Is it hot in here or is it just me? Anyway, I don’t have time to engage Billy this evening, we have so much more to do ...
[As Lars speaks, Raoul’s attention is caught by something out the window. His eyes go big as a skull shaped face peers back at him before dashing off.]
Raoul: EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEKKKKKKKKK ... [Raoul’s high pitched shriek snaps Lars to attention.] Lars: What is it? Raoul: There was someone outside! [Taking a look out the window, Lars spins in place to face Raoul. Shaking his head, an indignant Lars puts his hands on his hips as he addresses his partner.]
Lars: Oh, you big silly ... scared by your own reflection. Now stop playing, we have alot to do ...
[Grabbing up his paints and materials, Lars leaves the dressing room while Raoul stares out the window before the camera fades out on them, joining the pair known as Blue Light Special in a heavy discussion.]
Harry Palmer: Dean, we have to do something ... [A nodding Dean McQueen appears to be deep in thought before snapping his fingers.] We can’t let her go through with it. This is Jake Morgan we’re taking about. She’s not ready to take him on. He’ll kill her! What? You have a plan?
[Again, McQueen snaps his fingers, opening his mouth to speak but stopping before uttering a word. Shaking his head, McQueen rubs his chin, deep in thought. With another snap of his fingers, a grinning McQueen appears ready to put forth a plan but again stops, this time scratching his head.
Leaving them, the camera finds Neal the intern making his way through the backstage area. Rounding the corner, he nearly walks smack dab into a waiting
Jake Morgan.] Neal: Sorry ... [Taking a step backwards, Neal turns to race off before a big hand clamps down on his shoulder.]
Jake Morgan: Neal! Just the man I was looking for. [Neal turns around and sheepishly looks up at the wrestler, a grimace of pain on his face.]
Neal: Hi, Jake. Ummm ... what can I do for you? [Tilting his head, Jake thinks for a second, flashing an evil looking grin.] Jake Morgan: Hmmmm ...well, I can think of many things that you can do for me. My shopping, my laundry, detail my car but ... [Neal gulps deeply as Jake throws an arm over his shoulder.] Neal: B ... b ... but? [Jake smiles as he puts the squeeze on Neal.] Jake Morgan: But that can wait. Right now, I have a little business to take care of with a certain young woman ...
[After releasing Neal, Morgan walks off, leaving Neal to slump back against the wall and breath a sigh of relief when “Debonaire” by Dope begins to play before Neal fades from view. Along with the pounding beat, faces flash by quickly accompanied by their names. Men and women of all sizes and shapes as well as all manner of dress.
The preppy State Club Boyz, Curtis and Tony - the young baseball bat wielding Backyard Billy - an angry Angel Rotten - identically dressed in “Super K” smocks, the members of Blue Light Special, Harry Palmer and Dean “the
Machine” McQueen - the pink clad Lars, also known as the Lone Stranger - and finally, Jake Morgan.
As the last of the pictures fade, the logo of Hard Knocks Wrestling explodes into view before being swallowed up by inky blackness along with the ending of the noisy music.
Switching to the inside of the venue, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz sitting ringside.] Howard Butz: Hello wrestling fans and welcome to Hard Knocks Wrestling’s ... Wrestling 101! [Taking a swing through the audience, the camera picks up the boisterious crowd ready for an evening of wrestling action. Jumping up from their seats, several fans hold up hand-made signs showing their favorite wrestlers or condeming those they don’t like. Making it’s way around the audience, the camera returns to Butz.]
We’re coming to you from the School of Hard Knocks, here in Lewisville, Texas ... on KDFI, Channel 27! [Butz’ voice is cut off and the camera makes another run through the crowd as a booming voice is heard over all the background sounds.]
Voiceover: You are watching KDFI, Channel 27 - Home of the Texas Rangers! [Sound is returned to normal on Butz.] Howard Butz: Glad you could join us this evening, fans ... we have a big evening ahead of us ...
[Butz is cut off as Cypress Hill’s “Can’t get the best of me” blares out over the PA system. A hardy round of booing starts as Jake Morgan steps out from the back.
Dressed in his regular clothes, Morgan slowly strolls out to the ring with a smirk on his face, stopping long enough to have a few words with fans in the front row as he passes. Making his way into the ring, he motions for Butz to bring him a mic.
After tearing the mic from Butz’ hands, Morgan shoves him away.] Jake Morgan: Angel, not only did you make a fool of yourself by claiming to be the Queen of Hardcore but you continued this foolishness by aligning yourself with Neal, in a plot to get revenge on me. Did you think I wouldn't find out?
Did you think I wouldn't care? Did you actually think that I would not have thought of it first?
[Jake shakes his head.] Remember Angel, you're the rookie, not me. Anything you think of, I've already thought it. Anything you try, I've already tried. Anything you do, I've already done it. You're not original. You're not inventive and you are not the
Queen of Hardcore. [He taps himself on the chest.] You're just a <BLEEP> pretender to the throne. There is only room for one monarch in HKW and you're looking at him. In a few minutes, you can try to stage your coup, you can try to lay claim to my crown but when you fail ... and you will fail, you will get down on your knees and kneel before the true King of Hardkore.
[That smart ass smirk appears on his face one more time.] It's good to be the King! [There was a cheer as Angel Rotten steps out from the back. Walking out to ringside, she crosses her arms over her chest while shaking her head.]
Why don’t you make this easy on yourself? Turn around and walk your ass into the back and we’ll forget this ever happened ...
[Laughing, she pulls a mic from her back pocket.] Angel Rotten: You know, Jake ... the only thing that rings true here ... you are the true Queen of Hardcore in these parts!
[Morgan’s face became a mask of rage as the audience laughed at the man inside the ring.] In fact, seems to me that you’re the biggest bitch in this promotion! [With Angel climbing up onto the ring apron, Morgan came forward with a pointing finger in the face of his opponent. Dipping her shoulder, she drove it into his midsection. Doubling him over, she used the ropes to propel herself over the top with a sunset flip, pulling Morgan over as she went into a pinning predicament.
Referee Orly Diaz slid into the ring after racing out from the back, hitting a one count after calling for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Diaz with a one count on the very surprised Morgan! Morgan’s eyes narrowed as he watched Angel motioning for him to bring it on. She was fired up and looking for a fight. Rising to his feet, he caught her with a forearm before spinning around and sending her across the ring. On the return, she ducked under his outstretched arm, continuing on for the ropes.
Bouncing back, she took to the air and nailed the turning Morgan with a flying cross body block, taking him to the mat.
Howard Butz: Rotten with the cover! ONE! TWO! And Morgan powers out, throwing her off! Morgan was up and complaining about his pants being grabbed. Diaz shook his head. With the ref not seeing it, Morgan looked for some help from the fans and of course, no one saw it either.
Turning his back on Diaz and Rotten, Morgan was again jawing with the fans. With a rude gesture, he was pulled down from behind, rolled up with a schoolboy for a two count.
Howard Butz: Angel again with a cover! Morgan leaving the ring for a breather ... Oh, what now? Come on! Morgan complaining his pants were being held! Diaz hit eight on his count before Morgan rolled back into the ring, taking several stomps to the head. Pulling Morgan to his feet, Angel spun around for an Irish whip only to have it reversed.
Howard Butz: Morgan with a counter! SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE! He practically took off her head with the move. And now he’s gloating ...
Morgan strutted around the ring as the ref checked on the pained Rotten. Pushing Diaz out of the way, Morgan pulled her to her feet. Shielding her with his body, Morgan blocked Diaz’ view as he pulled her roughly to the mat by her hair. With the fans howling, Morgan acted innocent as Diaz warned him.
Howard Butz: Come on! He pulled her down by the hair! How low will this guy go? A knee drop added to Angel’s pain. Pulling her up by the hair, Morgan spun her through the air with a tilt-a-whirl bodyslam, shaking the ring as she landed.
Howard Butz: Morgan throwing himself back against the ropes ... LEG DROP! He has the cover! ONE! TWO! OH! He let her up ...
Morgan continued to smirk as he took a little stroll around the ring. Diaz checked on the fallen Angel before warning Morgan. Waving him off, Morgan left the ring to retrieve a chair.
Howard Butz: Morgan throwing a chair into the ring ... Diaz was over quickly with a warning as Morgan opened the chair up, setting it up in the center of the ring. An adamant Diaz was ready to throw the match out but backed off as Morgan assured him of his intentions.
Grabbing the chair, Morgan set it down over Angel’s throat before taking a seat himself. Howard Butz: COME ON, REF! He’s choking her! The boos were out in force as Morgan leaned his weight into her throat. Diaz was in Morgan’s face with a count.
Howard Butz: The referee counting ... ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR ... and Morgan’s up off of the chair. Oh, come on! Throw this one out!
Morgan sat right back down which started another count from the ref as Angel’s legs flailed away. There was a cheer as both members of Blue Light Special charged out from the back. Hitting the ring, Orly Diaz had no recourse but to call for the bell.
*** Ding *** Ding *** [Folding up the chair, Morgan swung it around, clobbering first Harry Palmer then smashing it over the head of Dean McQueen. Both men were flat on their backs as Morgan threw the chair down.
Stepping back, he waited for the two dazed men to stand before charging forward, nailing them with a double clothesline. Pulling Palmer up, Morgan sent him across the ring, flipping him high into the air with a back body drop.
Palmer was next to be sent across the ring but was caught as he returned. Lifting the hapless Palmer into the air, Morgan spun around and planted the man with a spinebuster slam. Jumping back to his feet, Morgan had some choice words for both men until the ref was in his face with a stern warning.
Brushing Diaz off, Morgan pulled the pair to their feet and knocked their heads together. With them stumbling off, Morgan turned to Angel, pulling her up and lifting her into the air and holding her vertically before falling back with a suplex.
Climbing the ropes, he was off with a leg drop, sitting there with a smile on his face as chants of “Morgan sucks!” filtered into the ring, starting off low but gaining in volume quickly. Morgan’s reply was simple but blurred for television.
Howard Butz: The fans are telling it like it is! Spun around by the ref, Morgan retaliated with a right hand that caught Diaz between the eyes. Taken by surprise, Diaz hit the mat like a ton of bricks. The chanting grew louder as Morgan egged them on before turning his attention back to the Blue Light Special boys.
Palmer was the recipient of a dragon suplex while McQueen took a DDT before both were hurled from the ring. While they were struggling to pull themselves off of the floor, Morgan taunted fans from the ring apron, going so far as to hold the ropes open with an invitation to get a little bit of what he was passing out. Of course, this went over well with the fans who showed their appreciation of Morgan with insults both verbal and visual.]
Howard Butz: He’s taken out the referee and Blue Light Special ... what else does this guy want? Come on! Get out of the ring!
[Palmer and McQueen were on their feet when Morgan hit the middle ropes and flung his body back, hitting the two with an asai moonsault onto the hard floor. Jake Morgan was the only one of the trio to rise to his feet after the hit.]
Howard Butz: Oh, my god! Morgan with a moonsault onto the floor and he’s the only one getting back up!
[Reaching under the ring, Morgan pulled out a table, pushing it into the ring and setting it up.]
Howard Butz: What’s he doing now? {With the table set up, the evil grinning Morgan pointed down at Angel Rotten before pulling her to her feet. Pushing her up onto the ropes, he quickly joined her.]
Howard Butz: He couldn’t ... [Balancing himself, he pulled her up to the top turnbuckle with him.] Howard Butz: He wouldn’t ... Jake Morgan: It’s good to be king! [Falling backwards, he pulled Angel over with him, superplexing her through the table, splintering it as the fans jumped to their feet, watching in stunned silence for long seconds.]
Howard Butz: HE DID! Oh, my god ... he did! [Slow to get back to his feet, Morgan was holding his head as Orly Diaz called for the bell wanting some help from the back.]
*** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 2 —

Howard Butz: Diaz calling for a little help from the back!
*** Ding *** Ding *** *** Ding *** Ding *** [Shane, wearing an HKW Security t-shirt rushed out from the back bringing a chant of his name from the fans as he neared. Following him were several more men wearing identical shirts.
Outnumbered and looking worse for wear, Morgan left the ring without incident as the fans were snapped out of their shock. Holding his head, Morgan disappeared into the back as the program broke for a commercial.]
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Comedy. Black entertainers - D.L. Hughley, Cedric the Entertainer, Eddie Griffith amongst others as well as the occassional token white comedian thrown in for good measure. The absolute Best of Def Comedy Jam! Over one million sold! You can’t go wrong with this one and all for only $9.99! Rock the house, order now!
A second commercial opens with two men standing inside a wrestling ring. Both men looking out of place in their preppy clothing. Their names - Tony and
Curtis - the State Club Boyz, scrolls across the bottom of the screen. Curtis: So, you want to be a wrestler? Then you need to call the School of Hard Knocks because to be the best, you have to be trained by the best! Call and begin your education today!
Tony: The best money can buy! A phone number flashes across the screen as scenes of potential wrestlers working out in a ring plays.
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the break, the camera returns to an obviously upset Howard Butz sitting ringside.]
Howard Butz: Welcome back. If you’re just tuning in, you missed the carnage devastated upon Angel Rotten and the members of Blue Light Special by Jake
Morgan ... [Scenes of Morgan’s asai moonsault onto Harry Palmer and Dean McQueen are followed by his superplex of Angel Rotten through a table inside the ring flash by.]
A despicable by a despicable person ... what now!?!?! [The lights dim briefly as a pink strobe light flares to life. With a hissing sound, smoke billows out from the back as “It’s Raining Men” begins to play over the loudspeaker. Lars pops out from the back and begins to frolic around the ring. “The Lone Stranger” is not dressed in his normal mockery of the Lone Ranger, instead, he wears a short silver toga that his rear end hangs out of, pink angel’s wings and a silver halo.
Skipping around the ring and waving his magic wand at the fans for several seconds, Lars “flutters” over to his set. While blowing kisses at the fans, pink and white balloons fall from the ceiling.
Finishing his rounds of the ring, he approaches the silver stage background with small pink “clouds” floating on the backdrop where a pair of lavender love seats and a glass coffee table await his arrival.]
Lars: Once again, welcome to Cloud 9, the show where yours truly will talk about the important issues in Hard Knocks Wrestling that all of you are interested in.
Joining me, of course, my friend and one of the best stylists in the DFW area! Everyone put your hands together for ... Raoul! [“It’s Raining Men” begins to play again as Raoul steps out from the back. The stylist comes out with a big grin, clapping his hands and jumping up and down. Wearing a light pink shirt with ruffles and large billowy sleeves and tight, tight leather pants, Raoul saunters out to the set where the two kiss the air between them, their lips only inches apart.]
He’s sleek and oh so chic ... [Raoul spins on his heels, showing off his form.] Today, we also have a very special friend joining us ... if you will, please join us in welcoming Jaques!
[A tall, well muscled blonde haired man in white pants and shirt makes his way out from the back, waving at the booing fans followed by Neal the intern carrying a tray and a small ice bucket.
With Lars, Raoul and Jaques all saying their hellos, Neal sets up the items on the table. With the tray, glasses and ice bucket in place, Neal makes his exit.]
Oh ... Nealllllll ... you can sit right here next to me ... [Neal turns to see the smiling Lars patting the love seat and the 4 inches of space remaining for a third person. After making eyes at Neal, Lars’ answer is a look of disgust from the intern who then dashes off.]
Humph ... that boy is so uptight ... Thank you for joining us here today, Jaques, on Cloud 9. Jaques: The pleasure is all mine ... Lars: Oh, you dear boy. [Lars and Raoul share a giggle.] While we pour some wine to wash down our cheese ... I’d like to welcome our ... “subject” for today’s discussion ... Backyard Billy! [There is a cheer for Billy as Raoul wrinkles his nose.] Raoul: Someone in definite need of a make-over ... Lars: Exactly, you big silly ... that’s why he’s today’s topic ... [A cheer ripped through the arena as Billy steps out from the back. With a determined look on his face, he approaches the three seated men.]
Raoul: And as you can plainly see, “early American caveman” just won’t do. Lars: But he can be helped! Isn’t that right, Raoul? [Looking Billy over, Raoul nods his head.] Raoul: Uhm hmm ... [Kicking the table and sending cheese and wine glasses flying, Billy points at Lars. The sudden action brings a screech and terrified look from Raoul.] Backyard Billy: I don’t know what your problem is but you have five seconds to get your ass off that couch ...
Lars: Love seat ... Backyard Billy: You have five seconds to get into that ring for an asswhipping! [An approving audience cheers loudly for the very serious Billy. Indignant, Lars raises to his full height, looking Billy square in the eye.] Lars: As I’ve already said, I, sir, am too busy to squander my precious time for any more brutalization that will only end in my victory ...
[With chants of “Kick his ass!” in the background, an irate Billy steps closer.] Backyard Billy: If you won’t get into the ring, then I’ll just have to kick ... HIS ass! [Turning from Lars, Billy points directly at a horrified Jaques. Leaping over the table, Billy grabs the man much to the pleasure of those in attendence, pulling him along to the ring. After hammering away with several forearms,
Billy rolls him into the ring, getting the starting bell from a shrugging Orly Diaz.] *** Ding *** Ding *** Whipped into the ropes, Jaques is put down with a drop kick. Kipping to his feet, Billy is a blur of motion, using the ropes to build momentum. Bouncing this way then that, the young grappler shots forward with a drop kick to Jaques face.
A somersault senton is followed by a two count on a cover. Billy works the crowd while Jaques desperately tries to crawl out of the ring.
Howard Butz: Jaques trying to escape and Billy with an elbow drop! Climbing the ropes now ... Billy on the top ... FLYING ELBOW DROP! There’s a cover! ONE!
TWO! THR ... NO! Jaques just barely escaping! Moving quickly, Billy pulls Jaques to the center of the ring, flipping him to his back. Grabbing his legs and falling backwards, Jaques is shot into the turnbuckle.
Howard Butz: A slingshot into a face full of turnbuckle. Billy going to the top ... MOONSAULT! Billy hit the moonsault! Hooking a leg. ONE! TWO! THR ... NO! Shoulder up! Somehow, Jaques escaped! Pulling Jaques to his feet, Billy sent him into the ropes, sending him through the air with a hip toss. Bouncing off the ropes, he then landed a leg drop.
Holding onto the ropes, Billy with a severe boot stomping that had Lars complaining from ringside.
Howard Butz: Referee Orly Diaz wanting a break in the action and we’ve got to break, too! Don’t go away, we’ll be right back!
[The camera breaks away from the action for a commercial.] *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Clothing by the tons! Pants, shirts, shorts, socks, undies and much, much more - all on sale in the big SPRING BARGAIN HUNT! Fifty to seventy percent off on all the name brands at ROSS! Factory seconds, discontinued lines, close-outs and much, much more at ROSS!
Lose weight easy and naturally with Dexatrim Natural! Yes, you too, can lose all that extra baggage you carry around and look just like the beautiful woman in the commercial - simple, easy - just a few little pills each day can get you where you want to be! No drugs! All natural! All caffeine!
Injured in an accident? Come to K-Clinic! Get medical help right now. No fuss, no muss - we’ll even do the insurance paperwork for you. No hassles just healing - hell, we’ll even tutor you in what to tell the insurance adjusters in order for you to get a bigger settlement - because at K-Clinic, we care!
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Back to the action finds Backyard Billy chasing Lars on the outside of the ring while Jaques lies flat on his back inside. With a dash of speed, Billy catches the toga clad Lars much to the pleasure of the fans. With a smirk on his face,
Billy lays into Lars with a series of right hands that has him reeling. Howard Butz: Billy working over Lars with the right hand! Orly Diaz on the outside, counting Billy out! Billy rolling back into the ring ... and right back out! Lars on his knees pleading and Billy’s having none of that!
Pulling Lars to his feet with a handful of hair, Billy nails him between the eyes before whipping him into the barricade around the ring. Charging forward,
Billy flips his body, hitting Lars backfirst bringing a cheer from the fans. WIth some words from the ref, Billy heads back into the ring. Using the ropes, Billy propels himself inside, landing a slingshot leg drop. Howard Butz: Raoul helping Lars to his feet and Billy’s dragging Jaques to the near ropes. Jaques hanging across the ropes ... and Billy with a running start ... BRONCO BUSTER! Ride him cowboy!
Bouncing up and down on top of Jaques, Billy drives him down into the ropes. The fans were screaming for Billy when he pulled Jaques off the ropes at the insistence of the ref.
Howard Butz: Billy propping Jaques up in the corner ... SUPER KICK! SUPER KICK! With Jaques falling forward, Billy caught him with an arm around the head. Charging forward, the young wrestler leapt up onto the ropes while spinning his body. Howard Butz: Billy on the ropes ... TORNADO DDT! Jaques has been planted! This one is over folks!
With Billy hooking a leg, the ref drops to the mat and slaps out a three count. *** Ding *** Ding *** [Lars looks faint as Billy jumps up and glares at him from inside the ring with the fans loudly cheering for the victorious wrestler before the camera moves away from the ring for the backstage area where both members of the State Club
Boyz, Curtis and Tony stand in front of the Hard Knocks Wrestling banner.] Curtis: You might think it’s over because last week, you turned out to be the better man but mark my words, Jake ... we’re just getting started. You see, things are building now ... the pressure is rising and before you know it, it’s all going to boil over and the State Club Boyz will be standing victorious ...
[A smiling Tony steps forward wringing his hands.] Tony: That’s right, Jake and next week, why don’t the two of us kick it up a notch and see what kind of damage the State Club Boyz can produce ...
[A smiling Curtis slaps Tony on the shoulder as he steps forward.] Curtis: The absolute best ... money can buy! [Leaving the State Club Boyz, the camera found the object of their challenge, Jake Morgan strolling through the backstage area. Stopping in front of a door, he pulls out a set of keys, opening the door.]
Jake Morgan: Holy <BLEEP>! [Following him inside, the camera pans the newly decorated room, the one with Lars’ pink and blue “stamp”. Again on the move, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz making his way through the back of the arena.
Rounding a corner, Butz hurriedly passes several doors when one opens and an arm grabs him, dragging him inside.]
Howard Butz: Urrrrrkkkkkkkkk ... [From within, there is a loud noise then all is quiet. Several seconds pass as the camera remains on the door until it opens and a hooded head pops out, looking both ways down the hall. Satisfied, the figure disappears back inside as the credits begin to roll.]
