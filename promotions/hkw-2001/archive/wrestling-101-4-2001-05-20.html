Wrestling 101 — No. 4

20 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on a seated School of Hard Knocks owner David Knox in his office wearing khaki's and a black HKW golf shirt. Also in the room are orly
Diaz, dressed in his referee garb and HKW announcer Howard Butz, decked out in "business casual" attire as always. The men are discussing the matches and interviews scheduled for tonight's show when a knock at the door interupts them.)
David Knox: Come in. [The door opens and in steps Raoul, friend and companion of Lars, “The Lone Stranger”. Raoul is adorned in cutoff, very short blue jean shorts, a pastel pink half tank top, sandals and the same dark sunglasses he was wearing earlier in the week.]
Raoul: Hi Davey! [Raoul winks at Knox, who grimaces.] Hi Butzy! Helllllllloooooooo Orly! [Raoul stares at the HKW ref for an uncomfortably long amount of time. Knox breaks the awkward silence.]
David Knox: What can we do for you, Raoul? Raoul: Lars sent me to find out two things really. First, he saw that championship belt and we have to say that he wouldn’t be comfortable wearing something so gaudy. It would clash with both his ring attire and normal fashionable outfits ... and we cant have that.
[Silence fills the room as everyone just stares at Raoul questioningly.] And secondly, We must know the exact date of the taped show so that he can guarantee our people will be done with the set makeover. It's really important!
David Knox: Raoul, about the changes ... Raoul: (interupting) Don’t you worry about a thing, Davey, We’ve got everything under control. It'll be perfect.
[Raoul leaves the office after blowing kisses to the stunned men.] Howard Butz: I'll never understand why you hired them. Orly Diaz: Me neither! He's a real pain in the ass. David Knox: I know but despite his faults, he's talented in his own way. Not to mention, the controversy surrounding him brings publicity and we need all the publicity we can get.
[Diaz and Butz shake their heads as the camera fades to the outside of the building to find a lone security guard holding a clipboard and looking it over while another man, not too tall and a bit on the stocky side wearing an HKW shirt and sweats with a duffle bag over his shoulder looks on.]
Security: I’m sorry, I don’t see it. [The man with the duffle bag mutters under his breath as he shakes his head.] Man: Pierce ... Tom Pierce ... Security: I’m sorry, Mr. Pierce, I don’t see your name on the list. I can’t let you in. Tom Pierce: Look, I’m supposed to be on tonight’s show ... Security: Sorry man, not on the list, I can’t let you in ... [Both heads turn as a voice from off camera booms in.] Voice: This mark giving you trouble, Terry? [Swinging in the direction of the voice, the camera picks up both members of the State Club Boyz. Both men step up to Pierce, looking him over.]
Tony: Why don’t you step aside and let some real wrestlers pass? [Tony and Curtis share a laugh as they push past Pierce and head into the building just before the camera smoothly fades back into the building on the back of a man wearing a puce colored shirt sitting in front of a mirror.
Looking at himself, the man gasps, revealing his identity by the high pitched squeal ... Lars.]
Lars: That’s it ... I’m going home! [The door opens and closes in the background before Lars is joined by Raoul.] Raoul: Message delivered! Lars: Pack up, we’re going home! Raoul: Home?!?! You have a match tonight against An-Hell ... Lars: That’s Angel and no, I’m not going out there looking like this! Raoul: You’re overreacting. It’s nothing, I’m sure. The swelling has gone down since last week ...
Lars: This is nothing? [Turning his head towards Raoul, still keeping his face out of view. Seeing it, Raoul grimaces.] Raoul: Ugh!!! That is horrid! Not as horrid as that ugly little An-Hell ... but bad nonetheless.
[Pursing his lips, Raoul smiles at Lars.] I’ll tell you what ... you leave that face to me and I’ll leave you with the task of beating, then spanking that horrendously ugly little man.
[Lars and Raoul giggle as the scene fades away as “Debonaire” by Dope begins to play. Along with the pounding beat, faces flash by quickly accompanied by their names. Men and women of all sizes and shapes as well as all manner of dress.
The preppy State Club Boyz, Curtis and Tony - the young baseball bat wielding Backyard Billy - an angry Angel Rotten - identically dressed in “Super K” smocks, the members of Blue Light Special, Harry Palmer and Dean “the
Machine” McQueen - the pink clad Lars, also known as the Lone Stranger - and finally, Jake Morgan.
As the last of the pictures fade, the logo of Hard Knocks Wrestling explodes into view before being swallowed up by inky blackness along with the ending of the noisy music.
Switching to the inside of the venue, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz sitting ringside.] Howard Butz: Glad to see ya’ll could make it to north Texas’ best in independent wrestling ... Hard Knocks Wrestling!
[Taking a swing through the audience, the camera picks up the boisterious crowd with it’s signs, all trying to get the camera’s attention.]
We’re coming to you from the School of Hard Knocks, here in Lewisville, Texas ... on KDFI, Channel 27! [Butz’ voice is cut off and the camera makes another run through the crowd as a booming voice is heard over all the background sounds.]
Voiceover: You are watching KDFI, Channel 27 - Home of the Texas Rangers! [Sound is returned to normal on Butz.] Howard Butz: Some big things are beginning to shape up. We have a challenge match with the State Club Boyz calling out Jake Morgan and a partner of his choice in tonight’s main event tag team bout!
Also on tonight’s program, we have Angel Rotten and Lars mixing it up again ... and where there’s Lars, Backyard Billy is sure to be close by ... but before we head to the ring for the first of our matches, it is my great pleasure to introduce to you a newcomer to Hard Knocks Wrestling! Coming to us all the way from California, here is Tom Pierce!
[Butz motions to the back when Union Underground’s “South Texas Deathride” blares out over the PA and Angel Rotten steps out from the back. Carrying a Singapore cane in hand, she storms out to meet Butz ringside.] Angel Rotten: Let’s get this started! Howard Butz: Angel, I’ve got an interview with HKW newcomer Tom ... Angel Rotten: Butz, why don’t you do us all a favor and shut it? These people aren’t here to listen to you and some other guy flap their gums! What they want to see is me pound that “Pink Petunia”!
[Angel smiles at the agreeable cheer from the fans beofe shoving Butz out of the way and entering the ring. Shrugging his shoulders, Butz can only shake his head.]
Howard Butz: We’ll be right back with our first match of the night! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Catch the Texas Rangers and the highest overpaid player in the league who isn’t worth the money even if he hit eighty homer runs and bats over .500 for the year! Despite the addition of Alex Rodriquez and several other aging free agents, the Rangers pitching staff still sucks and the team can’t win a game.
Minus manager Johnny Oates and with one hundred and twenty or so games left, the Rangers have dropped into the cellar. It’s going to be a long summer.
On a positive note ... drink Dr. Pepper! It makes the world taste better! Nothing like being hooked! It might not help the Rangers but it sure will start your day off with a kick!
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Returning from the commercial, the camera focusses in on the entranceway from the back when “It’s Raining Men” starts playing loudly throughout the small arena, bringing on the boobirds as Lars steps out from the back.
Not just any old Lars, mind you ... but for this week, a “new” Lars. Stepping out from the back in his usual pink tights and matching boots, Lars has added a few new items to his ensemble. A short pink cape and a pink mask with baby blue highlights mark out this week’s “Luchadore Lars” look.
Holding out his cape, he gallops in place for a few short seconds before slapping himself on his backside and taking off for the ring.
Climbing up onto the ring apron and turning his back to the ring, he leans back against the ropes before flipping himself backwards, up and over the ropes.
Rolling over the ropes, he lands smoothly on one knee facing the sea of “adoring fans”, again holding his cape out. Seeing her target not paying attention, Angel is quick to shoot forward to deliver a beating but is intercepted by referee Orly Diaz.
With Angel pacing like a caged animal, Diaz checks out her opponent before calling for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Moving to the center of the ring, the two have a few words which leads into a slap to the face from Angel. Hard enough to nearly spin Lars around, he turns back towards her, his hands on his hips before striking back with one of his own, a hard slap that snaps her head around.
Howard Butz: OUCH! Lars with a retaliatory slap of his own! Angered, Angel fired one right back drawing one in return from Lars which brought the fans to their feet, howling. In the center of the ring, the two traded slaps ... loud slaps to each other’s faces. First Angel, then Lars before the ref steps in to break it up. Stepping between the two is the wrong thing to do which Diaz quickly finds out as both Lars and Angel slap him on the face.
Howard Butz: Referee Orly Diaz stepping in and getting slapped now! This looks more like the Three Stooges ...
Surprised, Diaz fires back with one of his own to Angel’s face before waving a finger at her with a warning.
Howard Butz: Angel Rotten getting a warning from the ref. Come on, ref ... she wasn’t the only one!
With a little smirk on his face and an indignant nose raised in the air, Lars looks down at Angel while she gets reprimanded. Turning, Diaz let Lars have it across the face before giving him a warning also. Looking more shocked than hurt, Lars looks at the ref while the fans howl for more of the same.
Howard Butz: Lars and Diaz now going nose to nose ... Pushing Diaz back, Angel lashes out with a front kick, doubling Lars over. Grabbing his cape and pulling it forward over his head, she jumps into the air. Howard Butz: Front kick into ... FACEBUSTER! Angel just planted Lars with a facebuster. Rolling him over, there’s a cover ... ONE! TWO! NO!
The audience roars it’s disapproval as Lars rolls a shoulder off the mat. Pulling him to his feet and knocking him back into the ropes with a back elbow, Angel opens up with several chops before sending him across the ring. After hitting the corner, Lars slumps down before Angel follows up by dashing across the ring.
Howard Butz: Angel charging across the corner ... landing on Lars ... MONKEY FLIP! Thrown onto his back, Lars shot up in pain begging the ref for time but getting no help from Diaz. Pulled to his feet, Lars gets spun around but gains control with a counter. Ready to send her off across the ring, Angel counters him and drops him on his face with a drop toe hold.
Howard Butz: Angel climbing the ropes! Waiting on the middle turnbuckle, she waits for the staggered Lars to get back to his feet before launching herself into the air. Landing on his shoulders, she pushes herself forward before falling backwards.
Howard Butz: HURRICANRANA! NO! Lars with a POWERBOMB! Pushing her away, Lars regains his feet. Leaning heavily against the ropes, he waves a hand in front of his face as if to drive away a bad smell.
Howard Butz: What is he doing? He’s complaining to the referee about something ... he’s holding his nose?
After adjusting his mask, Lars climbs the ropes. Balancing himself on the top and posing with his cape held out, “Luchadore Lars” springs into action, leaping from the top turnbuckle and spinning himself into a senton, crushing
Angel beneath himself and rolling back to his feet as graceful as a cat. Posing from his knee brought out the boobirds from the fans drawing some taunts from Lars for unappreciative “rabble”.
Howard Butz: This mask thing is going to his head! Waving off the fans, he pulls Angel back to her feet, spinning her up onto his shoulders in a fireman’s carry. Parading around the ring with her across his shoulders draws more boos.
Howard Butz: Now he’s parading her around like a sack of potatoes across his shoulders! Orly Diaz urging Lars into action. Lars is twirling ... what’s he up to? Spinning her off his shoulder ... onto his ... GUTBUSTER DROP! He just dropped her across his knee!
From a kneeling position, Lars holds Angel across his knee. Holding her in place, he again poses before pointing down at her backside.
Howard Butz: He wouldn’t! Holding up a finger, then two and finally three, Lars lets her have one across her butt. Howard Butz: He did!

— Part 2 of 2 —

Several more follow as he holds her in place across his knee. The booing changes to cheers as the two combatants are soon to become three. Charging from the back, good to his word, is Backyard Billy. Sliding into the ring, a boot to
Lars’ back drops him to the canvas with no more thoughts of spanking Angel. Howard Butz: IT’S BACKYARD BILLY! Billy putting the boots to Lars! With Diaz pulling Billy off, Lars gets precious seconds to escape the ring for the safety of the outside. With Billy and Diaz arguing, Lars reaches into the ring, pulling Billy off his feet and slamming his face into the canvas.
Howard Butz: Billy biting a face full of the mat! Lars pulls him to the outside! The two start hammering away at each other on the outside pushing the fans into a frenzy as the two send the other into the ring barrier with neither man able to gain control.
Howard Butz: Billy punching away! Lars with a whip into the ring barrier! SPINNING LEG LARIAT! Billy rolls out of the way! They’re fighting around the ring followed by the referee trying to break things up!
Diaz is having trouble pulling the two apart and is pushed out of the way before they are joined by Angel Rotten ... the hard way.
Howard Butz: Angel on the ring apron ... APRON DIVE! APRON DIVE! Oh my God, Angel just got back into the match with an apron dive, knocking both men to the floor and the fans are going crazy!
The small crowd rips into an “H-K-DUB!” chant, rising to their feet for a better look at the three wrestlers on the floor.
Howard Butz: Diaz pulling everyone apart and Angel just liberated a chair and tossed it into the ring! Rotten and Billy pushing Lars into the ring!
Kicking the chair to the middle of the ring, Billy and Angel whip Lars across the ring, meeting him in the middle on the return with a pair of front kicks to the midsection. Doubling him over, they both grab him by the head, jumping into the air and sitting down into a double face driver, smashing his masked head into the chair.
Howard Butz: FACE DRIVER! They just laid Lars out with a double face driver! Referee Orly Diaz calling for the bell! *** Ding *** Ding *** [The fans are booing loudly because of the ref throwing the match out when Billy drops to his knees, pulling the laces free on the back of Lars’ mask. In the background, chants of “MASK! MASK! MASK!” growing in volume by the second as Billy fights to remove the mask.]
Howard Butz: Add insult to injury, Billy’s trying to unmask Lars! Tearing the mask free, Billy stands, waving at the fans while Lars sees his chance to escape, covering his reddened face and dashing from the ring.]
Howard Butz: There goes Lars, covering his still swollen face from last week’s bee stings! We have to break for a commercial ... we’ll be right back!
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Comedy. Black entertainers - D.L. Hughley, Cedric the Entertainer, Eddie Griffith amongst others as well as the occassional token white comedian thrown in for good measure. The absolute Best of Def Comedy Jam! Over one million sold! You can’t go wrong with this one and all for only $9.99! Rock the house, order now!
A second commercial opens with two men standing inside a wrestling ring. Both men looking out of place in their K-mart smocks. Their names - Harry Palmer and
Dean “the Machine” McQueen - Blue Light Special, scrolls across the bottom of the screen. Harry Palmer: So, you want to be a wrestler? Maybe you could be the next big thing in the world of sports entertainment!
Dean McQueen: Then you need to call the School of Hard Knocks because to be the best, you have to be trained by the best! Call and begin your education today!
A phone number flashes across the screen as scenes of potential wrestlers working out in a ring plays.
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Returning from the break, the camera returns to the back of the building near the wrestlers’ entrance where three security guards “entertain” the lone
Tom Pierce. As before, the one man with the clipboard continues to look up and dpwn his list while the other two, who look amazingly identical, do their best to calm the angered wrestler.]
Terry: I’m sorry, I still don’t see you on the list ... [All heads turn as a voice comes in from off-camera.] Voice: Hey, Terry! [Stepping into view is Blue Light Special member Harry Palmer, dressed and ready for action in his Super K smock.]
Harry Palmer: Hey, Ted! Hey, Tod! Ted: I’m Ted. That’s Tod. Harry Palmer: Sorry, Ted. Sorry, Tod. [Looking at the angry man standing with them, Palmer smiles before making introductions.] Hey, Tom. Do you know Terry, Ted and Tod? Tom ... Terry, Tod and Ted. Ted, Tod ... Tod: I’m Tod. That’s Ted. Harry Palmer: Sorry, Ted. Sorry, Tod. Tom ... Terry, Tod and Ted. Tod, Ted, Terry ... Tom. [Shaking his head, Pierce grabs his bag and pushes past the security, making his way into the building. The confused Palmer can only scratch his head.]
Hmmm ... I wonder what got into him? [Leaving the four men, the camera returns to the inside of the venue and the cheering fans as "Can't Get the Best of Me" by Cypress Hill plays over the speakers when Jake Morgan makes his way to the ring alone. His opponents, the
State Club Boyz, already in the ring, give each other a high five as Jake steps through the ropes without a partner. Jake goes through the motions of warming up for the match. Orly Diaz checks out all three men, and is about to start the match when "Debonaire" by Dope plays over the speakers. David Knox walks through the curtain followed closely by Shane. Shaking his head, Knox makes his way to the ring. When he gets there, he slowly climbs in near the State Club
Boyz. They look at him quizzically. Knox sees this and holds up a hand to forestall any questions on there part. He then walks over to Orly and putting a microphone to his lips, he asks Diaz a question.)
David Knox: Orly, you do know that this is supposed to be a Tag Team Match? [Nodding, Orly replies.] Orly Diaz: Yes sir but Jake didn't bring a partner with him. So, I asked him if he wanted to forfeit. He told me "No" and that he would fight them both by himself. The Boyz didn't seem to mind, so I was going to make it a
Handicap match. [Knox nods and rubs his chin. He looks over at the Boyz.] David Knox: So, you don't have a problem making this a Handicap match? [They both smile and shake their heads "NO." Knox then turns to Jake.] And you don't have a problem with this either? [Looking bored, Jake shakes his head. Knox steps back into a corner, and looks at all four men.]
Well, I DO have a problem with making this a Handicap Match. A Tag Team Match was scheduled, advertised and damn well expected by these fans to occur here tonight. So, Jake you will pick a partner and you will do it NOW!
[Jake walks toward David before being intercepted by Shane. Jake rolls his eyes and pushes past him to stand in front of Knox. Reaching down he takes the microphone from David, looking down on the boss.]
Jake Morgan: I had a feeling you'd say something like that Knox, so, I have a partner in mind.
David Knox: Who? [A predatory smile forms on Jake's face as he strolls the ring.] Jake Morgan: My first choice for a partner was a certain skull-faced little toe-rag who likes to show himself from time to time but since we don’t even know if he’s in the building, I’ll go with my second choice.
[Stopping his strolling, he steps right up to the boss, bumping him with his chest.] My second choice is ... YOU! [The choice catches everyone offguard, eliciting a surprised response from the crowd. The smiling Morgan frowns when Shane tears the mic out of Morgan’s hand.]
Shane: Hold on a second. I’m not going to allow you to pull him into a match. No way, Morgan. When he said anyone from the back ... he meant ... [Shane’s cut off as Morgan tears the back from him.] Jake Morgan: He meant ANYONE from the back! But ... I’ll tell you what. If our illustrious boss is too much of a pussy to step into the ring, you’ll do
Shane because it won’t take much to beat those two pansies across the ring! [Stepping forward, Morgan shoves Shane back into the oncoming State Club Boyz before hightailing it out of the ring. With David Knox exiting the ring, the ref calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Caught by surprise, Shane gets tossed around by Tony, the bigger of the two State Club Boyz after getting whipped into the ropes and returning for a scoop power slam. Howard Butz: On the recieving end, Shane getting all the support from these fans while the smug Jake Morgan grins from his corner! Shane pulled back to his feet and whipped across the corner! Bouncing off the ropes ... CLOTHESLINE!
Knocked off his feet, Shane takes a slingshot legdrop from the tagged in Curtis. In the far corner, Morgan reaches into the ring for a tag and looking terribly bored.
Even with chants of Shane’s name by the fans, it isn’t enough as he gets smacked around by the overly efficient State Club Boyz. Keeping things to a move or two each, they tag in and out of the match.
Howard Butz: Curtis with a vertical suplex and a cover! ONE! TWO! NO! Shane got the shoulder up! Stay down, Shane, stay down! Oh, what’s this? Morgan running into the ring and being cutoff by the referee. He’s complaining about something ... and the ref will have none of it.
With Morgan fighting being led from the ring, the State Club Boyz stop and both beckon for him to get into the match, leaving Shane alone in the center of the ring on the canvas. Morgan’s name being chanted by the fans gets the State
Club Boyz involved as they pound the turnbuckles, leading the cheering. Howard Butz: Morgan with his hands over his ears does not like what he’s hearing. Get in the ring, Morgan!
Complaining the ref again, Morgan points out individuals in the crowd which brings a significant amount of booing. Trying to walk away, Morgan grabs the ref and pulls him back to the corner to complain.
Howard Butz: It’s getting loud in here! Everyone wants Morgan in the ring ... and they’re going to get it with Shane heading back to his corner to make the tag! Pulling himself to his feet using the ropes, Shane slowly returns to his corner before reaching out to make a tag when Morgan leaves the corner, dropping from the apron to argue with fans in the front row.
Howard Butz: Shane to make the tag and Morgan leaves the corner!?!?! What’s going on!?!?! With Curtis pulling Shane away from the corner to the center of the ring, Morgan returns to his corner while his partner takes a body slam. With Shane laying flat on his back. Curtis makes a tag with Tony who comes into the ring with a slingshot splash.
Howard Butz: Tony with the cover ... ONE! TWO! NO! Shane kicks out again! Pulled to his feet, Shane is whipped across the ring, smacking into an unsuspecting Morgan, who seems more interested in arguing with the fans, from behind.
Howard Butz: The ref calling for Morgan to enter the ring ... he’s counting that as a tag! Listen to these fans go wild!
Morgan, from the apron pleads his case, not wanting to enter the ring but is cut off as Tony and Curtis pull him in with a double hip toss. Crisscrossing the ring, they drop a pair of elbows before Tony is led from the ring.
Howard Butz: Now, we’re getting to see what we’ve wanted all along! Morgan pulled to his feet ... STANDING DROP KICK! Curtis bouncing off the ropes ...
LEG DROP! There’s a cover ... ONE! AND A KICKOUT! With a tag to his partner, both men pull Morgan to his feet, whipping him across the ring. Rather than boucing off the ropes and returning, Morgan dives over them, slapping Shane on the way out, making a tag.
Howard Butz: He dove out of the ring and made a tag rather than getting what he had coming to him! What a coward ... and the fans aren’t afraid to let him know what they think of him!
Back in the ring, after a quick tie-up, Shane is treated to more rough treatment with a Belly-to-Belly suplex from Tony and a follow-up top rope splash from the tagged in Curtis.
With Curtis making the cover, a disgusted Morgan leaves the ring for the back, followed by a heavy dose of booing.
Howard Butz: Curtis with the cover! There’s ONE! TWO! THREE! Thankfully, this one is over! *** Ding *** Ding *** [With Morgan disappearing into the back, both State Club Boyz argue with referee Orly Diaz pointing towards where Morgan had just disappeared into the back.
Chants of “H-K-DUB!” start up from the fans when a lone figure with dark hair steps out from the back. Making his way down the small ramp towards the ring, he stops and points at the State Club Boyz inside the ring as the credits begin to roll bringing the show to an end.]
