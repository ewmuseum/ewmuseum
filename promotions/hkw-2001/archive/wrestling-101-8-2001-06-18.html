HKW — Card Archive

Wrestling 101 — No. 8

18 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 3 —

[The camera opens on HKW Texas Outlaw champ, Jake Morgan strolling through the backstage halls of the Lewisville venue with a gym bag over one shoulder and his title belt over the other. As the scowling Morgan proceeds, he abruptly stops as laughter breaks out from view. Turning with Morgan, the camera finds
Shane, the head of security, leaning against a nearby wall.] Shane: Good luck in your tag team match with Pierce ... [Shooting Shane a look, Morgan walks off shaking his head. Zooming in on Shane, the camera catches him muttering under his breath.]
You're going to need it ... [Leaving the smirking Shane, the camera is on the move, turning up in the front foyer of the building, passing through the throng of fans making their way into the building, passing concessions and a table of HKW t-shirts and paraphenalia.
Set up near it is another table, smaller in size, manned by the "Lone Stranger", Lars. Moving in for a better look at his wares, the camera pans the various skin care products before pulling back.
Walking past the tables, everyone's favorite intern, Neal has a few words with the HKW personnel when he sees the waving Lars. Putting his head down, Neal quickly moves past the small table, stopping only when Lars loudly clears his throat.
With a look of surrender, the intern grudgingly turns to the "Pink Avenger", standing there with his hands on his hips.]
Lars: Didn't you see me trying to flag you down? Neal: No, Lars ... I didn't. Lars: Humph ... young people today. Anywho ... I have a little errand for you my trusty personal assistant ...
[Rolling his eyes, Neal sighs.] Neal: Yes, Lars. [Lars eyes light up as he puts a hand to his chest.] Lars: My, my ... do I like this change in attitude. M'kay ... with the Hard Knocks new signee ... that big ... lucious ... muscular ... make-up wearing ... hunk ... [Lars' eyes get that far-off glassy look for a brief second, stopping long enough to lick his lips before snapping back to reality by Neal's voice.]
Neal: Lars? LARS!!! Lars: What?!?!? Neal: You mean Nate Hatred? Lars: Oh my! Yes, Nate ... I want you to go to my dressing room. You'll find a gift basket made especially for him ... the one with the cream puffs. Oh, how he'll remember those delightfully cream filled morsels ...
What a welcome to the promotion. You be sure to tell him that I personally filled those with cream just for him! Now, scoot ... scamper! And if you see
Davey ... tell him, I'll be coming in a few! [As the frowning Neal runs off, the table is approached by both members of Blue Light Special. As Harry Palmer and Dean McQueen look at the prices then back to each other, the camera leaves them, focussing in on a muscular man in dark clothing. The man swings his head with long hair around as he's approached.
Standing in front of him, the self appointed "Star Maker" Dillon Diamond extends a hand when the large man snaps his head back, revealing the black paint around his eyes, growling. With a gulp, Diamond hesitates for a second before speaking.]
Dillon Diamond: The question you should be asking isn't ... [Thrusting his arm forward, the man wraps his hand over Diamond's face, stopping him in mid-sentence and shoving him roughly to the floor as
“Debonaire” by Dope begins to play. Along with the pounding beat, faces flash by quickly accompanied by their names. Men and women of all sizes and shapes as well as all manner of dress.
The preppy State Club Boyz, Curtis and Tony - the young baseball bat wielding Backyard Billy - an angry Angel Rotten - identically dressed in “Super K” smocks, the members of Blue Light Special, Harry Palmer and Dean “the
Machine” McQueen - the pink clad Lars, also known as the Lone Stranger - and finally, the HKW Texas Outlaw Champion, Jake Morgan.
As the last of the pictures fade, the logo of Hard Knocks Wrestling explodes into view before being swallowed up by inky blackness along with the ending of the noisy music.
Switching to the inside of the venue, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz sitting ringside.] Howard Butz: Good evening wrestling fans! [Taking a swing through the audience, the camera picks up the boisterious crowd with it’s signs, all trying to get the camera’s attention.]
Howard Butz, and we’re coming to you from the School of Hard Knocks, here in Lewisville, Texas ... on KDFI, Channel 27! [Butz’ voice is cut off and the camera makes another run through the crowd as a booming voice is heard over all the background sounds.]
Voiceover: You are watching KDFI, Channel 27 - Home of the Texas Rangers! [Sound is returned to normal on Butz.] Howard Butz: We’ve got an action packed program as this week Jake Morgan teams up with Tom Pierce to take on arch nemesis Tony and Ghost in tag team action ... but up first, we’ve got the debut of Nate Hatred stepping in against Ricky Starr!
I don’t know much about Hatred or what he’ll bring to ... [Butz stops as “It’s Raining Men” blares through the PA and Raoul, dressed in his schoolboy outfit marches out from the back.]
We’ve got to take a break ... DO NOT GO AWAY!!! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Commercials, commercials, commercials - all in the name of “free television”. We endure this crap just so we don’t have to pay for our programming but is it really free? Not really because when you think about it, every hour long block of television is spliced with twenty to nearly twenty five minutes of commercials. And to make matters worse, for our “free television”, we’re dishing out anywhere from thirty to seventy-five bucks a month to the cable companies just to have a few worthwhile channels on top of tons of crap channels that no one watches.
On a positive note ... drink Dr. Pepper! It makes the world taste better! Nothing like being hooked! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the break finds Raoul inside the ring with Butz. Prancing around the ring, Raoul points out at heckling fans, making claw motions and stepping between the ropes, stopping only as Butz pulls him back into the ring.]
Howard Butz: Hold on there, wildcat ... you came out here to say something? [Composing himself, Raoul plasters his hair down before demurely turning to Butz.] Raoul: You know, Butzie ... you’re cuter close up ... with a little work, you’d be simply ravishing!
[Butz takes a big step back as the fans howl out in laughter.] Yes, you big silly ... I have something to say. I just wanted to come out here and remind everyone to pick up their skin care products out in the main concourse ... and seeing some of them, I can say they really need it! Look at that big fat woman right there!
[Butz pulls Raoul back as the boos rain in from the crowd. That changes fast as both members of Blue Light Special walk out from the back carrying signs ...
Palmer with one bearing a big yellow smiley face while McQueen’s sign says “Turn back the clock on prices”. Stepping into the ring sets Raoul off, who jumps on Palmer, flailing away while both McQueen and Butz try to pull him off. Try as he might, Palmer can’t shake Raoul off.
With the riotous action inside the ring, the fans scream out as Backyard Billy, dressed in a t-shirt and sweats ... and of course, his head covered in a bandana, charges out from the back, diving into the ring with a Singapore cane in hand.
After helping to pull Raoul off, Billy, as the voice of reason, steps between both parties as Raoul nearly falls over himself trying to get at Palmer or
McQueen. With both parties pointing fingers at each other, things soon break down as Palmer and McQueen get in Billy’s face. Remaining calm, Billy turns to Raoul urging him from the ring. Spinning back around, Billy lashes out with his weapon, putting both men down with quick shots to the head, the snap of the cane ringing out and bringing everyone to their feet.
Throwing the splintered cane down, Billy pulls Palmer to his feet, whipping him across the ring and spinning him up into the air and dropping him flat on his back with a back body drop in the center of the ring.
Pulling McQueen up, a second Irish whip has Dean vaulting over Palmer on the return, right into a super kick. His jaw meeting Billy’s foot, McQueen staggers back before falling on top of his partner.
With both men down, Billy takes off into a full run, jumping up onto the ropes and spinning his body back through the air and landing on the pair with a high arcing moonsault, bringing the fans to their feet.
Jumping back to his feet, Billy climbs the ropes as a chant of his name breaks out. Tearing the bandana off his head and revealing his new hair style has the women screaming. Jumping down from the corner, Billy points at Raoul before stripping off his shirt and in one swift motion pulling his sweats off his legs, revealing pink colored tights underneath.
Throwing an arm to his head, Raoul faints dead away before the camera returns to a laughing Howard Butz.]
Howard Butz: Billy in pink? Joining forces with Lars and Raoul? I’ve seen it all now! We’ll be right back with Ricky Starr and Nate Hatred!
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Listen to the MIX 102.9 with Jeff and Anna in the morning! What kind of crap do they play? Who knows ... I’m not up that early in the morning, anyway.
A second commercial plays with a handsome young man standing inside a wrestling ring. The well built and shirtless man smiles as his name scrolls across the bottom of the screen.
Backyard Billy: So, you want to be a wrestler? You want to be just like the guys you see right here every week? You want to become the next State Club Boy? Maybe you want to be like me or perhaps you want to be like the champion, Jake Morgan?
Then you need to call the School of Hard Knocks because to be the best, you have to be trained by the best! Call and begin your education today!
A phone number flashes across the screen as scenes of potential wrestlers working out in a ring plays.
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK ***

— Part 2 of 3 —

[With the commercial over, the camera returns to the ring as the house lights
dim, followed by swirling red lights swirl around the arena. Smoke slowly billows forth from the entrance way as Nate Hatred emerges from behind the curtains and strides down to ringside. With the red lights blinking on and off,
Hatred seems to be gliding to the ring. With the house lights exploding back on, he circles the ring, his eyes never leaving his prey before he slides under the bottom rope and comes up charging the turnbuckle.
With referee Orly Diaz checking this big man’s boots, he calls for the bell, stepping out of the way as Ricky Starr comes crashing in with an elbow.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: Ricky Starr wasting no time in going after the bigger Hatred! Grabbing Hatred’s arm, Starr spins him around, whipping him off towards the ropes, nailing him on the return with a drop kick.
Howard Butz: Hatred looks solidly built ... not the tallest guy but I’d guess he’s at least in the two fifty range if not heavier.
Knocked back against the ropes, Hatred doesn’t fall, catching himself. A charging elbow is followed by several reverse knife hands,
Howard Butz: Starr stepping back after an onslaught. Hatred throwing his hair out of his face ... he’s smiling?!?!
A clubbing forearm rocks Starr before he is lifted into the air and thrown backfirst into the corner. A running knee to the stomach lifts Starr off his feet before crumpling toi his knees. Yanking Starr to his feet, Hatred viciously whips Starr across the ring into the opposite corner. Slamming backfirst into the turnbuckles, Starr staggers out of the corner.
Howard Butz: Hatred with a full head of steam. Clothesline ... NO!!! SPEAR! SPEAR! HATRED WITH A SPEAR! On his knees near the downed Starr, Hatred whips his head back, smiling out at the fans, a twisted grin on his face. Rising slowly to his feet, he pushes the ref out of the way, pulling his opponent to his feet.
Howard Butz: Whip into the ropes. BOOT TO THE FACE AND STARR IS DOWN! Dropping a leg across Starr’s throat, Hatred sits with the pin as Diaz drops and counts. Howard Butz: Leg drop. Hatred with the cover. ONE! TWO! NO! Hatred rolling back to his feet. HE’S NOT FINISHED YET!
After a short stroll around the ring, Hatred pulls Starr back to his feet for another whip into the ropes. Starr rebounds right into Hatred’s arms. With ease, Hatred presses Starr into the air, holding him aloft before sending him crashing to the mat, finishing the gorilla press slam.
Throwing himself back against the ropes, the big man shoots forward, jumping into the air and splashing his prone opponent. After dragging Starr to the center of the ring, Hatred rips him back to his feet, setting him up with a standing head scissors.
Howard Butz: Hatred setting Starr up in the center of the ring. There’s the thumb across the throat ... he’s going to finish it!
Looking out at the crowd, Hatred releases his grip on Starr before turning towards the corner and pointing at it. Half dragging Starr across the ring,
Hatred puts him up the ropes before joining him at the top. Howard Butz: Hatred setting him on the top ropes. He’s got the footing. Hatred spining Ricky Starr into the air! HE WOULDN’T! Pushing Starr high into the air, the face painted Hatred leaps from the ropes, sitting down into a powerbomb, shaking the ring under both men’s weight.
After both bounce back to the mat, Hatred sits holding Starr by the legs with his back firmly on the canvas.
Howard Butz: HE DID! HE DID! NATE HATRED WITH THE TOP ROPE POWERBOMB! THERE”S THE COUNT! ONE! TWO! THREE! HATRED WINS! HATRED WINS! *** DIng *** Ding *** [With Starr down, Diaz punches Hatred’s arm into the air but is shoved out of the way as the solidly built man returns to Starr, putting a boot across his throat, choking him. After recieving a warning, Hatred chases the ref before before putting the boots to Starr and drawing a heavy dose of boos.
Finished, Hatred walks off to the back as the camera breaks away to focus in on a door nameplate that reads "David Knox: HKW President". Swiveling around, the camera pulls back in time to catch the approach of Lars in pink leather chaps, a baby blue thong decorated with a star on front and a fuzzy baby blue holster.
Reaching the door, lars knocks twice before entering the room. Knox, busy with some paperwork, looks up and rolls his eyes.]
Lars: Hi Davey! David Knox: Hello Lars. [Lars fluffs the cushions on one of the chairs, exposing his thonged rear end to the HKW owner before sitting down with a playful smile on his face. Knox grimaces at the sight, speaking after several seconds of silence.]
David Knox: So, what can I do for you, Lars? Lars I'm glad you asked. I want to enter the tag team tournament. David Knox: Okay. Who's your partner? Lars: I'm not sure now since Tomas vanished. There are just so many choices! David Knox: Morgan's looking for a partner. Lars: As if! There is NO way I'm teaming with someone utterly lacking in style and flair. Jacob takes absolutely no pride in his attire and his wrestling thingies ...
David Knox: Those are called holds or moves. Lars: Whatever. They lack any originality. David Knox: What about Angel? [Gagging himself with his finger] Lars: I refuse to team with the ugliest man here. What would we be called? Beauty and the Beast? David Knox: Ummm ... Lars? Angel is a ... Lars: Hideous creature? I know! If I looked like him, I would never step foot outside my house. You might have to get rid of him because he may be beyond even Raoul's help.
David Knox: Uh-huh. Anyway, just let me know when you figure out who's gonna be your partner.
[Blowing a kiss at Knox, Lars leaves, talking to himself as he walks down the hall.] Lars: Raoul's too busy ... Neal hits like a girl ... [Leaving Lars to ponder on a partner, the camera finds Curtis of the State Club Boyz, leaning heavily on his crutch, talking to Ghost.] Curtis: Man, this is going to be good. You and Tony in a tag match against Morgan and Pierce ... only Pierce isn’t even in the building. [Jabbing a finger into Curtis’ chest, Ghost gets in his face.] Ghost: I don’t care who’s with him ... I WANT MORGAN’S ASS! Curtis: Chill ... Tony will be ready in a minute, then it’s ass whipping time. [Ghost nods his head in agreement before being knocked to the ground with a loud cracking sound. Standing over Ghost and eyeing Curtis is a grinning Jake
Morgan weilding a badly dented chair.] Jake Morgan: Guess that evens things up, now doesn’t it? Maybe you want some, too? [Throwing down the chair, Morgan dashes off as Tony comes flying out into the hallway ready to fight. With Curtis shaking his head in disbelief, the camera breaks away to find Nate Hatred making his way through the hallway, given a wide berth by the HKW production crew. He stops as Neal the intern, carrying a wrapped basket, blocks his path.]
Neal: Lars would like to welcome you to Hard Knocks Wrestling. [His face hidden by the long locks hanging down into his face, Hatred watches Neal intently, studying his face.] He said he creamed ... URKKKKKKKK ... [Grabbing Neal by the throat, Hatred pulls him close, tearing the basket from his hands then shoving him to the floor, grasping for breath. Looking at the basket, he slams it down on the floor, sending the cream puffs scattering.
Stomping on them, Nate Hatred makes little cream splatters before stormimg off. Zooming in on the one remaining cream puff, an arm comes into view, snatching it up.] Voice: Yummmmm ... cream puff! [As the hands pulls the tasty treat off the floor, the scene fades into yet another damnable commercial.]
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? *** COMMERCIAL BREAK ***

— Part 3 of 3 —

[With his music blaring, Jake Morgan, his head and arm still heavily bandaged,
walks out from the back to the usual sound of boos from the fans. Pelted with several items, Jake threatens them, even going so far as to grab one fat, obnoxious fan but is stopped by security.
In an even more sour mood than usual, he lays his belt at his feet and pulls a mic from his back jeans pocket in the center of the ring.]
Jake Morgan: Well David, at the house show, you asked me a question. Did I think I was tough? When I told you what I have proved to you and each of these morans week in and week out, you DEMANDED proof.
[He points down at the belt.] Is the fact that I still possess this proof enough? Is the fact that your hired gun couldn't beat me, when I was one handed proof enough?
No? If not, then I’m ready for your next challenge. In fact, I'm more than ready for it. You see ... I may be beaten ... I may be bruised and I might be just a little battered but even with all of that, I'm still ten times the match for anyone back there. So come on Davey, I'm tired of waiting.
["Debonaire" by Dope begins to play with David Knox and Shane walking out from the back.] David Knox: Jake, you proved me wrong. You ARE one tough S ... O ... B. You are one tough man to stand inside this very ring with one arm tied behind your back and not only wrestle a very competant man in Gary Pate ... and going one step further ... you won.
You beat Gary Pate and you beat him at his own game, a hardcore match. You’re a tough guy. There is no doubt about it and to be quite honest, I never doubted it for a second but you see Jake, as in life, there are rules here. You can’t bully the people who work for me and you can’t bully me!
Now, tonight ... I had you scheduled for a tag team match with Tom Pierce against Tony and Ghost but Pierce has turned up missing ... so, since you seem to like being challenged ... you’ll be facing your opponents by yourself ...
and to be fair ... if you win tonight, I’ll put your name down in the tag tournament! [Knox stops as Morgan chuckles before the State Club Boyz march out from the back.] Jake Morgan: Hate to break it to you, Knoxie but I already took care of Casper! Looks like it’s just me and Tony! [The place erupts into cheers as Tony hits the ring and the two men start trading punches while on the outside, Curtis shouts encouragement. Withering under the exchange, Morgan is sent into the far ropes, returning only to be taken off his feet as the ref hits the ring and calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Dropping to the mat, Tony unloads with rights and lefts until Morgan rolls him over, dropping his own bombs. With the tide turned, Curtis gets the fans involved by pounding on the apron.
Howard Butz: Morgan with some words for Curtis outside the ring. There is no love lost between Morgan and either of the State Club Boyz.
Yanking Tony to his feet, Morgan whips him to the canvas with a snap suplex, then another. Rolling back to his feet, Morgan kicks out at Curtis but misses.
Howard Butz: Oh come on! It’s bad enough that Morgan’s responsible for Curtis’ injury ... now you want to kick a man on crutches? There’s something to be said for a man who kicks someone when he’s down ... and Jake
Morgan seems to be just that sort of man! Hovering over Tony, Morgan drops several knees to his opponent’s head. With a smile on his face, Morgan seems to be enjoying this evening’s work.
Howard Butz: Morgan jawing with the fans in the front row. Curtis encouraging Tony ... Holding the ropes open, Morgan invites one of several fans into the ring if they’d like to show just how tough they are. With a disgusted wave, he returns to the match.
Settling in behind a standing Tony, Morgan locks in the cobra clutch. Trying to escape, Tony fights to get to the ropes for a break but to no avail as Morgan cinchs in the hold.
Howard Butz: Tony trying to escape but once Jake has you locked in it ... there’s no escape! The referee asking Tony for the submission. Lifting the arm ... Morgan going for a throw! Tony hooks the leg and blocks it!
A big cheer breaks out as Morgan shakes Tony trying to end his struggles but it’s not meant for the Texas Outlaw champ ... it’s for one “Ghost Faced
Killa”. Sliding into the ring, Ghost gets in behind Morgan, locking in a sleeper hold forcing the surprised Morgan to drop Tony.
Howard Butz: IT’S GHOST! GHOST HAS MADE IT TO THE RING AND HE’S GOT MORGAN IN THE CENTER OF THE RING WITH A SLEEPER! With Tony on the ground, crawling away, the ref steps in and starts a count on Ghost. Howard Butz: Diaz counting. ONE! TWO! THREE! GHOST NOT LETTING GO! FOUR! Releasing his grip on Morgan, Ghost and the ref have a few words before he locks the hold back on, this time shaking Morgan just as he did to his partner seconds ago. Again, Diaz counts on Ghost before the mysterious wrestler drops him and heads to his corner.
Howard Butz: Ghost leaving the ring and Tony with a few precious seconds to get his head back into the match is back in business. Running shoulder block puts
Morgan down. Elbow drop and a cover! ONE! SHOULDER UP! Morgan kicked out! Hitting Morgan with forearm uppercuts, Tony sends his rattled opponent in the opposite corner ... his own corner where the waiting boot of Ghost sits on the top turnbuckle. Crashing into the boot, Morgan staggers back out of the corner, right into the awaiting arms of Tony before he is flattened with a back suplex.
Howard Butz: Tony with a tag and Ghost putting the boots to the champion. Elbow drop to the back of his head and Ghost with the cover. ONE! TWO! THR ... Morgan got the shoulder up just in time says referee Orly Diaz.
Pushing Morgan away, a sharp pull on the wrist draws him closer, right into Ghost’s abdominal stretch. With the ref in his face asking for the submission, Morgan does his best to fight it off despite the pain.
Howard Butz: Morgan doing his best to fight it and Ghost trying to snap some tendons and ligaments.
Falling forward, Ghost drives Morgan’s face into the canvas. Rolling him over, Ghost comes up arguing over a slow count. Forcing Morgan back into his corner, Ghost hammers away with several chops to the chest that pop loudly before a tag is made.
Howard Butz: Ghost distracting the ref as Tony and Curtis both beat on Morgan. With Tony holding Morgan’s arms, Curtis slams the end of his crutch into Morgan’s stomach, doubling him up. With both men leaving the ring, Ghost hammers him in the back with a forearm, driving him to his knees. A boot to the head puts Morgan on his back before Ghost drops a leg and covers, this time for a two count.
With a tag to Tony, Ghost allows himself to be whipped across the ring, leaving his feet for some major air before smacking into Morgan with a corner splash.
Stepping away, Ghost is pushed across the ring and out by the ref as Tony is the legal man in.
Howard Butz: Morgan using the ropes to pull himself up. With Morgan in the corner, Tony spins him around, driving a boot to his back before pushing him up the ropes. After tagging him with right hands, Tony tags in his partner.
Howard Butz: With no partner in sight, things are starting to look bad for Jake Morgan. He is starting to take a beating. Pushing Morgan up the ropes despite the ref’s protests, Ghost pushes him off, right into the waiting Tony’s arms. Grabbing Morgan by the neck, Tony sits down into a stunner.
Howard Butz: How much punishment can Morgan take? Tony off the ropes. There’s a leg drop. Ghost back in. Tony and Ghost working surprisingly well together to destroy the champion. Last week, they were fighting each other but this week a common enemy finds them together!
With a whip into the ropes, Morgan is sent flipping through the air thanks to Ghost’s spinning heel kick, hitting the champ in the head. Using the ropes to get back to his feet, Morgan is grabbed and spun around. Driving a boot into
Morgan’s midsection, Ghost grabs him by hair and jumps into the air before sitting down. Howard Butz: FACEBUSTER! FACEBUSTER! GHOST JUST DROVE MORGAN”S FACE INTO THE CANVAS! THERE’S A COVER AND A COUNT! ONE! TWO! THREE! NO! NO! NO! The ref jumps to his feet waving two fingers in the air for all the booing fans to see signalling the count. A disgusted Ghost gets into Diaz’ face slapping his own hand showing a three count. With Diaz waving two fingers in his face,
Ghost leaves the ring, tagging in Tony. Barely able to stand, Morgan is sent into the ropes, coming back to Tony’s drop kick. Flat on his back while Tony leaves the ring, Jake Morgan sits back up. He takes a licking but keeps on ticking.
Howard Butz: Tony on the ring apron. Using the ropes back into the ring ... SLINGSHOT SPLASH! Tony hooking the leg. Morgan up with a one count. There’s a tag! Ghost into the ring, the two men double team Morgan, hammering him back into the ropes then sending him off with a double Irish whip. Returning, Morgan ducks under a pair of back elbows and making it to the other side of the ring where his partner, Tom Pierce is waiting with an outstretched arm for the tag.
Howard Butz: IT’S PIERCE! PIERCE HAS MADE IT TO THE RING! Charging in, Pierce levels Tony with a clothesline and the intent of doing the same to Ghost only running into his boot. Doubled over, Pierce has his head grabbed and soon finds himself up close and personal with the canvas.
Howard Butz: FACEBUSTER! THERE’S A FACEBUSTER! Down and dazed, Tom Pierce offers no resistance as Ghost rolls him over and rolls him up as Diaz slaps out the three count.
*** Ding *** Ding *** [The beaten and brutalized Morgan staggers away from the ring with his eyes on his taunting and victorious opponents as the credits quickly roll before everything fades away into a commercial.]
