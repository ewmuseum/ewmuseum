Wrestling 101 — No. 2

— Part 1 of 3 —

[The camera opens in the backstage area on Jake Morgan, ready for action in his black wrestling shorts, boots, a black Morgan "Myth" shirt with the sleeves and neck cut out and his hands taped up in black athletic tape. With his wet hair hanging down into his face, an imposing Morgan rubs his hands together as he speaks.]
Jake Morgan: The best money can buy! That's what you and your boyfriend, like to say isn't it Curtis? You believe that money is the answer to all of your problems. It can fix everything right?
[Closing his eyes under the tangle of hair, he shakes his head.] Wrong. Money bought you the best ring attire. It bought you the best training. It bought you the best entrance music but it couldn't buy you the one thing you need, and that I have in spades ... TALENT.
No matter how much money you pay to train, you will never be close to me in ability. It's something that you just can't buy. That's why I'm the main draw in HKW and you are just a roster filler.
[With a quick flick of his head, Morgan snaps the hair out of his eyes.] Maybe you're thinking that your money can buy you a win against me? Maybe you think that if you offer me enough money, I'll lie down for you? Maybe you think that your money will buy you the Texas Outlaw Title?
[This time he shakes his finger at the camera.] Wrong again. Just like I said earlier, I'm not for sale and that belt WILL be mine. Anyone who gets in my way, WILL be destroyed. Your mouth wrote a check, that your ass can't possibly cash. Now, it's time to repay that outstanding debt.
[With a sudden jerk, he gets right up into the camera.] Try to beat me. Try to stop me. Try to Survive me! [The grinning Morgan fades away to another part of the backstage area where Lars, the Lone Stranger stands with Raoul and an obviously bothered Neal the intern.] Lars: Neal, you’re sure everything is ready for tonight? [Rolling his eyes, Neal shoots back with an icy answer.] Neal: Yes ... [Throwing a wrist to his forehead, Lars falls backwards into Raoul’s arms.] Lars: Oh Raoul, I feel faint ... should anything go wrong with tonight’s debut ... I’ll just ... I’ll just die!
[Whipping a folded fan from his pocket, the deft fingered Raoul snaps it out and furiously fans the faint Lars while Neal seems even more annoyed.]
Raoul: Oh, you big silly ... everything will be just fine, They’re going to love you. You’ll be the smash of the show ...
[Standing under his own power, Lars snaps out of it, a big smile on his face.] Lars: Do you really think so? [The two men start chirping away, passing compliments back and forth, stopping abruptly as two young men carrying their gear nod at Neal.]
Young man #1: Yo, Neal ... Neal: Hey, guys. [As the two men walk off, Lars eyes go wide while Raoul nearly salivates at the pair.] Lars: And just who are THOSE two young hunks of man flesh? [Shaking his head in disgust, Neal shoots Lars a look.] Neal: A couple of new students ... Raoul: Yummy ... [Rolling his eyes, Neal walks off as “Debonaire” by Dope begins to play before they fade from view. Along with the pounding beat, faces flash by quickly accompanied by their names. Men and women of all sizes and shapes as well as all manner of dress.
The preppy State Club Boyz, Curtis and Tony - the young baseball bat wielding Backyard Billy - an angry Angel Rotten - identically dressed in “Super K” smocks, the members of Blue Light Special, Harry Palmer and Dean “the
Machine” McQueen - the pink clad Lars, also known as the Lone Stranger - and finally, Jake Morgan.
As the last of the pictures fade, the logo of Hard Knocks Wrestling explodes into view before being swallowed up by inky blackness along with the ending of the noisy music.
Switching to the inside of the venue, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz sitting ringside.] Howard Butz: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Hard Knocks Wrestling’s ... Wrestling 101! [Taking a swing through the audience, the camera picks up the boisterious crowd with it’s signs. Jumping up from their seats in the front rows, a group of rowdy fraternity guys make themselves heard as the camera swings by. The loudest of the group is the green and white clad University of North Texas mascot, Mean Green, in custom dressed as a big eagle. The big bird flips off the camera, which is partially blurred, before returning to Butz.]
We’re coming to you from the School of Hard Knocks, here in Lewisville, Texas ... on KDFI, Channel 27! [Butz’ voice is cut off and the camera makes another run through the crowd as a booming voice is heard over all the background sounds.]
Voiceover: You are watching KDFI, Channel 27 - Home of the Texas Rangers! [Sound is returned to normal on Butz.] Howard Butz: We’ve got a packed house tonight ready for some big action ... and action is exactly what they’re going to get! We’ve got the debut of Cloud 9 from Lars ... whatever that might be as well as a main event pitting the big bully, Jake Morgan taking on Curtis of the State Club Boyz ...
I have it under good authority that owner David Knox has a little surprise in store for Mister Morgan should he get out of hand! What it might be remains to be seen ... but before we go to the first match ... we’ve got a few bills to pay!
Don’t go away! We’ll be right back! [Butz and the arena fades away into a commercial.] *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Clothing by the tons! Injured in an accident? Come to K-Clinic! Get medical help right now. No fuss, no muss - we’ll even do the insurance paperwork for you. No hassles just healing - hell, we’ll even tutor you in what to tell the insurance adjusters in order for you to get a bigger settlement - because at K-Clinic, we care!
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [With the commercial over, the camera returned to the ring where Angel Rotten was in the process of pacing. As she moved back and forth, the fans were split over whether to boo or cheer for the self proclaimed “Queen of Hardcore”.
Motioning for Howard Butz to join her in the ring, Angel stopped the pacing as the announcer entered the ring.]
Angel Rotten: I’m pissed! I’ve been just informed that my opponent for tonight isn’t able to make it for our match. I’ve been told that he’s in no condition to wrestle tonight ...
Howard Butz: I’m afraid that’s the case, Angel ... [Angel shook her head as the boobirds rose.] It seems that Gorilla Boy had a little run-in with someone in the back ... with Jake Morgan. [Snapping her head around, she glared at Butz.] Angel Rotten: Butz ... I don’t give a damn why he’s not here, all I care about is getting a match and kicking someone’s ass! I don’t care if it’s you or him ...
[Pointing into the crowd brought on a good mix of boos and cheers before she had some words with the loud rowdies in the front row.]
This isn’t some T&A show ... I’m serious about kicking some ass, so you or someone else better get me someone and you better do it right now!
[Chants of T&A seemed to anger Angel enough for her to leave the ring for the apron in order to shout at fans. The UNT mascot whipping everyone into a frenzy raised the noise level greatly as Angel shrugged off the referee to leave the ring.
With his back turned, Angel struck the costumed mascot in the back before pulling him across the ring barrier. Holding the big head, she slammed her knee repeatedly into her hapless foe. Ignoring referee Orly Diaz, she rolled the costumed cretin into the ring.
Following an argument with Diaz, Angel got her wish as the ref called for the bell.] *** Ding *** Ding *** Holding onto the ropes, Angel worked out her leg as she drove it into the bird’s beak over and over. Diaz’ counting finally made her step away.
Climbing the ropes, she worked the crowd while the ref helped Mean Green to it’s feet. Pushing Diaz out of the way drew a sharp warning which seemed to not bother her in the least, sending her opponent into the ropes and taking him off his feet with a well placed knee to it’s midsection on the return.
Throwing her arms out, Rotten sat down into a leg drop across the mascot’s throat. She was laughing as she stood and dropped down several more times.
Pulling the bird back to his feet, she sent him into the ropes only to take him down with an arm drag before sending him through the ropes with a drop kick following a second Irish whip.
Howard Butz: She’s got the leg drop down ... and a drop kick sends the big bird to the outside! Angel’s following ... APRON DIVE! Oh my god!
An apron dive had both wrestlers down but only momentarily as Angel used the ring steps as a drum, using it to pound out a tune with Mean Green’s head.
With a body slam, she put the poultry down for a leg drop off the ring apron which brought a momentary halt to Diaz’ count out. The bird was reeling as he was rolled back into the ring.
Whipped into the ropes, he shot back right into Angel’s clothesline and spun through the air. Lifted up, the beaten bird was hooked in the corner upside down in a tree of woe. Climbing the ropes, she had the entire audience groaning along in pain as she stepped on the bird’s genetalia. Using the ropes to bounce up and down made it worse before a count from the ref brought her down.
Howard Butz: Now, that has got to hurt. Mean Green is totally at Angel’s mercy and right now, she is no Angel of Mercy! Referee Orly Diaz trying to free the bird but having little luck. Diaz now with a warning for Angel and she’s ignoring him totally. BASEBALL SLIDE!
Stepping back across the ring, she shrugged off Diaz before charging forward and nailing a baseball slide, foot to beak. With the bird knocked loose, Angel climbed the ropes and waited for Mean Green to rise.
Howard Butz: Angel waiting for Mean Green. Turning ... Angel jumps! HURRICANRANA! NO!!! VICTORY ROLL! ONE! TWO! THREE! It’s over and Angel Rotten has the win! *** Ding *** Ding ***

“the Lone Stranger” is not dressed in his normal mockery of the Lone Ranger, instead, he wears a short silver toga that his rear end hangs out of, pink angel’s wings and a silver halo.

Man: Long time no see, Jake. David Knox: I think you and Shane are familiar with each other from your PAW days? [Jake shook his head as he laughed. It was quiet in the arena as the fans waited to see the fireworks. Again, stroking his chin, Morgan nodded his head.
Lunging forward, Morgan wrapped his arms around Shane’s waist. Lifting him off his feet, Morgan spun his body before dropping to the mat, planting his man with a spinebuster bomb. Slowly rising to his feet, Jake had a sly grin on his face as he motioned for the wide-eyed Knox to come forward and take his
“medicine”. Cutting away from the ring, the camera found it’s way into the back, focussing in on a locker with the nametag of JAKE MORGAN across it. Panning the room, the camera settled in on the closed window where a fresh white handprint dripped paint as the credits began to roll.]
