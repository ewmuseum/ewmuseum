HKW — Card Archive

Wrestling 101 — No. 7

9 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 3 —

[The camera opens on Lars wearing only a pair of cutoff jean shorts, sitting on the couch in his lockerroom talking on a pink maribou feathered phone. With his feet kicked up on the couch and a fruit smoothie within reach on the coffee table, the “Rainbow Warrior” sits surrounded by a ton of papers everywhere.]
Lars: No, no ... it’s just that I've changed my mind. [His head bobs up and down as he listens to a voice on the other end.] No ... I loooooooove the work the three of you did. You guys really knocked yourselves out this time, but Raoul and I talked it over and we feel that everyone would be more comfortable with our original idea.
[Again, he listens to the person on the other end of the line.] Uh-huh ... thats right, “The Love Shack!” [He stops and listens.] Money is no object. Kiss, kiss! Ciao! [Lars starts to hang up the phone but quickly puts it back to his ear.] One more thing! Can you guys make sure that everything's out of here before tonight? [He smiles as he hears an answer.] Oh great! You guys are the best. Give everyone my love. Toodles. [Leaving Lars as he hangs up the phone, the camera moves to outside of the Hard Knocks venue, focussing in on the line of people waiting to get in. Flashing past the line, the camera focusses in on one person, his head covered in a bandana, walking past. The person stops and swings his head around at the scream of a young woman’s voice.]
Girl #1: IT’S HIM! IT’S HIM! [Stopping to look around, Backyard Billy is suddenly surrounded by several girls and guys. He smiles as several hands thrust forward with pens and paper for autographs.]
Girl #2: He’s cuter in person! Girl #1: Billy, show us the hair! [Shaking his head, Billy ignores them as he continues signing away. All the girls surrounding break out into a chant, wanting to see his hair. With a sigh, he raises a hand to his head as the camera swings around behind him. Pulling the bandana from his head, the girls scream in unison.]
Girls: It’s beautiful! [With a laugh, Billy finishes the autograph then picks up his bag before heading into the building. Returning to the girls, they watch him leave, one with her hand over her heart “showing” the palputations. Moving back inside the building, the camera slips into the office of HKW owner David Knox along with Shane and two of his “wrestlers”, El Macho Sasquatcho and the Mean
Green mascot.] David Knox: I called you into my office to talk about your match today with Tom Pierce ... [At the mention of the name, El Mach shoots a hand to his neck as if in pain.] El Mach: I don’t know, boss ... this busitis ... David Knox: Right ... anyway, Mr. Pierce isn’t in the building, so I’m pulling your match ...
El Mach: Yessssss ... [He pumps his fist in jubilation when “Debonaire” by Dope begins to play. Along with the pounding beat, faces flash by quickly accompanied by their names. Men and women of all sizes and shapes as well as all manner of dress.
The preppy State Club Boyz, Curtis and Tony - the young baseball bat wielding Backyard Billy - an angry Angel Rotten - identically dressed in “Super K” smocks, the members of Blue Light Special, Harry Palmer and Dean “the
Machine” McQueen - the tough but husky Tom Pierce - the pink clad Lars, also known as the Lone Stranger - and finally, the newly crowned HKW Texas Outlaw
Champion, Jake Morgan. As the last of the pictures fade, the logo of Hard Knocks Wrestling explodes into view before being swallowed up by inky blackness along with the ending of the noisy music.
Switching to the inside of the venue, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz sitting ringside.] Howard Butz: Welcome to Hard Knocks Wrestling! [Taking a swing through the audience, the camera picks up the boisterious crowd with it’s signs, all trying to get the camera’s attention.]
Howard Butz, here and we’re coming to you from the School of Hard Knocks, here in Lewisville, Texas ... on KDFI, Channel 27!
[Butz’ voice is cut off and the camera makes another run through the crowd as a booming voice is heard over all the background sounds.]
Voiceover: You are watching KDFI, Channel 27 - Home of the Texas Rangers! [Sound is returned to normal on Butz.] Howard Butz: Last Week at our house show, David Knox announced a Tag Team Title Tournament. He said that it should begin in the ... ["You Can't Get the Best of Me" by Cypress Hill plays over the PA system and as the fans jump to their feet Booing, Texas Outlaw Champion, Jake Morgan walks out from the back with his title belt around his waist.
Several fans throw trash at him as he walks to the ring. A beer hits him square in the face, causing the Champion to rush the barricade, only to be held back from the offending fan by the quick reacting security guards. Wiping his face off, Jake continues to the ring. He slowly climbs in and brings a mic to his lips.]
Jake Morgan: Well, it seems everyone wants something from me. Pierce wants to watch my back. Lars wants to wash it.
[He cringes and shivers, as if a chill just ran up his spine.] Tony wants my belt. Ghost wants to attack me in the dark and the fans want to throw trash at me. On top of all of that this so called “Star Maker” wants to do ...
[He shrugs.] something for me? I really have no idea, what it was he was talking about. Everyone wants something. I'm no different. I want something and I want it now. Howard Butz: Who cares? [Jake runs over to the side of the ring where Butz sits and leans over the ropes, pointing at him.]
Jake Morgan: I don't know what you just said Howie but if you're smart, you'll keep that fat mouth shut! You interrupt my time again and I'll put your Fat ass through a table.
[Butz glares back at the champ, but keeps his mouth shut.] As I was saying, I want something. I want a PARTNER for the tag tournament and I want him NOW! I don't care who it is, as long as they want to win the belts. [Jake waits for a minute or so but no one comes out.] I will not allow the tag tournament to go on without me, so somebody had better step up to the plate back there, because I'm not leaving the ring until
I get a partner. [Morgan starts to pace back and forth when someone emerges from the back. Not one of the wrestlers as asked for the self-proclaimed “Star Maker”. Nearing the ring, he takes a mic away from Butz before heading into the ring, keeping his distance from the champ.]
the Star Maker: Jake, I’m here for one thing only and that’s to see you ... OR someone in the back, move up to the next level ... to take their game from just star and become a superstar.
It’s not a question of everyone wanting something from you ... this is a question of you wanting something from me. How can YOU blossom under my tutelage ... whether we’re talking about a tag team partner, being a champion, money, women, fast cars or Hollywood deals ...
You or someone in the back is going to have the answer to the question and when you do, let me be the first to say, the sky’s the limit.
[With as smile, he leaves the ring, handing the mic back to Butz who signals for a commercial.]
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Listen to the MIX 102.9 with Jeff and Anna in the morning! What kind of crap do they play? Who knows ... I’m not up that early in the morning, anyway.
A second commercial plays with a handsome young man standing inside a wrestling ring. The well built and shirtless man smiles as his name scrolls across the bottom of the screen.
Backyard Billy: So, you want to be a wrestler? You want to be just like the guys you see right here every week? You want to become the next State Club Boy? Maybe you want to be like me or perhaps you want to be like the champion, Jake Morgan?
Then you need to call the School of Hard Knocks because to be the best, you have to be trained by the best! Call and begin your education today!
A phone number flashes across the screen as scenes of potential wrestlers working out in a ring plays.
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the break, the camera shifts to a scene backstage in Lars’ locker room. Lars is dressed in his “Lone Stranger” outfit, sitting on the couch, obviously in a huff. Raoul enters the room wearing a flowery pair of bellbottoms and a tied up tiedyed shrt.]
Raoul: Lars? [Lars doesnt answer.] Larsy? Whats wrong? You’ve got Cloud 9 coming up in a matter of minutes. You’d better put a smile on that gorgeous face. Lars: No. I have absolutely nothing to smile about. I was just told that Tomas failed to show up this evening, so now, I don’t have a guest to makeover. I have a zit! And to top it all off, I have no set because those mover idiots took it when they removed the rest of the triplets’ decorations. Which means, that I would have to do it in the ring. No thanks.
Raoul: I wonder what happened to Tomas? I'm sort of worried. Lars: Me too! I'll betcha that Nacho Splatcho did something to him. I just don’t trust anyone who dresses up in strange costumes.
Raoul: Me neither. [Lars stands up off the couch and storms out the door. The camera follows him as he makes his way to the ring. Raoul rushes along behind to keep up. Entering the ring, he signals to Howard Butz.]
Lars: Butzy, get in here. [Shaking his head, Butz enters the ring.] Howard Butz: What can I do for you, Lars? Lars: I'm so mad that I can just spit! Not that I would. Howard Butz: Of course not. Lars: First, those Macfrugals boys interfered in my makeover of Billy last week even though it was a stipulation of the match. And, I'd like to add that Billy has yet to schedule an appointment with Raoul to carryout his part of our agreement before the match ...
Howard Butz: Well, Lars to be fair, don’t you think that Raoul’s haircut carried out the stipulations of the match?
[The fans begin to boo loudly as Lars shoots Butz a look.] Lars: I don’t remember asking you a question, Butzy. Anyway ... to make matters worse, that dirty little monkey may be responsible for the disappearance of my guest and future tagteam partner, Tomas Pierce. I want nacho out here right NOW! I swear, if he had anything to do with Tomas’ disappearance he's in for one heck of a spanking!
[The fans cheer as El Mach steps out from the back, heading to the ring. Climbing up onto the ring apron, the springboarding simian bails out when Raoul shoots forward trying to scratch out his eyes before being pulled back by Lars.
Climbing back onto the apron, El Macho cautiously enters the ring. Inside the ring, the simian points at Lars to hold Raoul back.]
Oh, don’t you worry about him scratching your eyes out ... worry about me doing it! [Throwing down the mic, Lars releases his grip on Raoul, throwing himself forward at the trapped El Mach, slamming into him and knocking him back against the ropes. With Raoul and Howard Butz vacating the ring, Lars dances around the fallen body until referee Orly Diaz hits the ring, calling for the bell.]

— Part 2 of 3 —

*** Ding *** DIng ***
Howard Butz: Looks like we have ourselves a little impromptu match, folks!
Pulling the hairy howler to his feet, Lars sends him flying back into the corner with a knee lift. With El Mach pinned in the corner, Lars quickly mounts the ropes for a little hip gyration much to the displeasure of all ...
especially that of El Macho Sasquatcho! Howard Butz: Oh, that is just disgusting! After blowing kisses to his “adoring fans” Lars opens a can of “Whoopass”, a big dose, pummeling the grubby gorilla with right hands. Jumping off of the ropes, Lars wraps his legs around the bigfoot face, sitting there with a smile on his face, grinding his hips before throwing his body back and taking El Mach over with a hurricanrana.
Howard Butz: Head scissors into a hurricanrana ... and now, he’s straddling his face ... ref, stop this!
Leaning forward with a smile on his face, Lars has his foe pinned to the mat. Dropping down, Diaz hits the mat twice before the furry fury lifts a shoulder off the mat. Howard Butz: Sasquatcho with an escape! Lars pulling him to his feet. A whip into the ropes. Big clothesline. There’s an elbow drop.
Scrambling over his downed opponent, Lars covers him, burying his face in El Mach’s crotch. Diaz slaps out a count while the man on the bottom’s arms flail away looking to escape.
Howard Butz: Lars with the pin, if you want to call it that. ONE! TWO! THREE! NO!!! The luchadore escaped! Getting away from Lars, El Mach staggers around the ring, wiping at his face before being grabbed and sent across the ring. Hitting the ropes and bouncing back, El Mach rolls over Lars back as the latter drops for a back body drop.
Howard Butz: Setting up for a back body drop. El Mach rolling over his back ... waistlock. There’s a back elbow from Lars! Staggered by the blow, El Mach ducks under the second blow then lifts Lars up before dropping him down across his knee for a back breaker. Holding his jaw after sliding Lars to the mat, El Mach makes a mad dash across the ring into a handspring, hitting the ropes and splashing downed Lars.
Howard Butz: Handspring splash! There’s a cover! ONE! TWO! NO! Barely escaping the pin, Lars uses the ropes to pull himself to his feet while Raoul looks on in horror. After nailing Lars with a drop kick into the corner, El Mach pulls him out, ducking behind into a waistlock. Howard Butz: El Mach lifting Lars off his feet ... Struggling to not be thrown, Lars lands back on his feet, kicking back, catching El Mach in the family jewels.
Howard Butz: OUCH! With the missing link on the mat, Lars slides to the outside and the awaiting arms of a concerned Raoul. Hugging his friend, Lars draws a rather loud round of jeering. Rolling back into the ring, Lars flips El Mach upside down, hanging him out on the ropes in a tree of woe.
Backing away, Lars takes a running start, hitting the mat, sliding his groin into El Mach’s face rather than slamming in with both boots to the face. A big smile melts from his face as the blow dislodges his opponent. WIth El Mach dropping from the ropes, he lands on top of Lars, covering his face with his crotch and vice versa.
Howard Butz: That is just disgusting! Dropping to make a count of the “cover”, Diaz calls for a break, noting Lars’ legs under the ring ropes. Getting back to his feet, Lars argues with Diaz for not making a count. Howard Butz: He’s arguing with the ref? He wanted to be pinned? What is he doing? After watching Lars slap out a count slowly in his hands, Diaz can only watch confused. That is until Lars shoves him. Regaining his balance, Diaz shoves
Lars right back, knock him to the mat much to the delight of the fans. On his knees, Lars pleads with the ref for a timeout but of course, gets nothing but a warning.
Getting back into the match, Lars turns his attention back to El Macho Sasquatcho, using the ropes trying to pull himself up. Grabbing both ankles, Lars pulls El Mach off his feet, holding him in the air while his opponent holds onto the ropes for dear life.
Standing behind El Mach, Lars licks his lips before going into a little bump and grind motion, pistoning his hips back and forth.
Howard Butz: Break that up ref, they’re in the ropes and we have children watching! With Diaz counting on Lars, the “Pink Power Ranger” sits down into a facefirst powerbomb, pulling El Mach free of the ropes. Rolling back to his feet, Lars wraps his legs around El Mach’s body, rolling to the mat and putting his back down.
Howard Butz: Body scissors. Lars with the pinning predicament! ONE! TWO! THREE! *** Ding *** DIng *** [With Raoul rushing into the ring an wrapping Lars into an embrace to share the victory, both men start a stomping party on the downed El Mach.]
Howard Butz: This is uncalled for. Lars with the pin and now, he’s trying to make El Macho Sasquatcho into a stain on the canvas! Let’s have a little help ringside!
[With the fans booing as Lars and Roaul continue the beating while ignoring Orly Diaz’ warnings, Backyard Billy answers the call. Stepping out from the back, the young wrestler stops at the top of the ramp momentarily before taking off for the ring at a dead run. Sliding into the ring, he comes up in a fluid motion, stopping in front of Lars.
Seeing Billy, both Lars and Raoul stop their pummeling. With a bandana covering his hair, Billy shakes his head as he holds up a hand. Looking down at El Macho
Sasquatcho, then back to Billy, Lars collects Raoul and leaves the ring without a word. With the fans chanting Billy’s name, the camera fades into a commercial break.]
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Commercials, commercials, commercials - all in the name of “free television”. We endure this crap just so we don’t have to pay for our programming but is it really free? Not really because when you think about it, every hour long block of television is spliced with twenty to nearly twenty five minutes of commercials. And to make matters worse, for our “free television”, we’re dishing out anywhere from thirty to seventy-five bucks a month to the cable companies just to have a few worthwhile channels on top of tons of crap channels that no one watches.
On a positive note ... drink Dr. Pepper! It makes the world taste better! Nothing like being hooked! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [In the back, the camera returns to find Texas Outlaw Champion, Jake Morgan ready for war. With his title belt over his shoulder, the champ makes his way through the hallway when he rounds the corner and nearly runs into Curtis and
Tony of the State Club Boyz. Stopping, Jake smiles as Tony is held back by partner Curtis.]
Jake Morgan: Oh, that’s good. {Balancing on one crutch, the injured Curtis does his best when the smirking Morgan walks off, shaking his head.] Curtis: Save it for the ring. [Regaining his composure, Tony watches Morgan walk off when a voice behind them and out of camera view chimes in.]
Voice: He needs to be taken down a peg. [Turning, they find the head of security, Shane standing there with a scowl on his face.] Shane: If you need a partner for the tag tournament, count me in! [The three touch fists when the camera breaks away to find Angel Rotten having cornered Neal the intern in the men’s bathroom. Up against the wall, he has a nervous look on his face as his eyes dart from the Singapore cane leveled at his chest then up to the holder’s narrowed eyes.]
Angel Rotten: What about Billy? Neal: I don’t know, Angel. David hasn’t said a thing about the teams or the seeds yet. Angel Rotten: What about Morgan? Neal: As far as I know both he and Lars are banking on Pierce but I really don’t know. [Both stop when the door opens and they are joined by the Mean Green mascot. With a little laugh, the big bird with the newspaper under his arm shakes his head as he heads into a stall.]
Mean Green: You kids continue with your lovefest and don’t mind me. [With the growl, Rotten hammers him from behind with her weapon as Neal makes a run for it. With a smooth fade, the camera emerges from the back to find Jake
Morgan standing on the ramp taking in the booing of the fans while one of his opponents, Tony waits inside the ring.
Walking down, Morgan is met halfway by the charging Tony, setting off a fusillade of fisticuffs. Withering under the blows, Morgan is rolled into the ring when the lights start to flash. Blinking on and off, they suddenly are doused as an eerie laughter spews forth from the PA. With the lights suddenly back on, Morgan is in the process of being stomped by both Tony and Ghost as the ref calls for the bell.]

— Part 3 of 3 —

*** Ding *** Ding ***
After being pummeled, Morgan is pulled to his feet and whipped into the ropes by both men. Rebounding back, he is hammered to the mat with a stiff double clothesline. Standing over the body, the two men go nose-to-nose eyeballing each other.
Howard Butz: We’ve got a staredown going on here. After this week’s meeting between the two at a house show, there is no love lost inside that ring!
With neither man willing to make the first move, they both look down at Morgan before nodding to each other.
Howard Butz: DOUBLE ELBOW DROP! Looks like they’ve put aside their differences for the time being and are going to take it out on Jake instead.
Back to his feet, both of Morgan’s arms are twisted out of a pair of wrist locks. Pushing an arm high into the air, both Tony and Ghost pull sharply down on the arm. Adding to the pain, Ghost hammers away at Morgan’s bandaged arm bringing a howl of pain from the champ.
Moving from wristlocks to arm bars, they shift their weight in order to lift Morgan into the air before falling back into a double suplex. Pinning him to the mat, both men hold him down as the ref hits the canvas.
Howard Butz: THERE’S A PIN! ONE! TWO! NO! Morgan able to power out. While Tony argues with Orly Diaz about a slow count, Ghost slams Morgan into a corner, opening up with solid reverse knife hands to the chest. Reddening the champ’s chest, Ghost takes him out of the corner with a high arcing hip toss before coming down with a diving headbutt.
Howard Butz: Ghost with a cover! Diaz with a one count and Morgan has a shoulder up. It’s going to take alot more than that to put Morgan away.
There’s a double whip into the corner ... now Tony whipping Ghost across the ring. Charging across the ring, Ghost leaps high into the air, slamming into Morgan with a corner splash that hits some major air. Stepping back, Ghost watches
Morgan stagger out of the corner before falling flat on his face. Pulling Morgan to his feet, Tony sends him into the ropes, catching him on the return for a belly-to-belly suplex. Again the two eye each other suspiciously with the champ down.
Taking turns, the two men slam Morgan into the mat ... Ghost with a Samoan drop while Tony pounds him into the canvas with a sidewinder suplex. With Tony making the cover, Diaz hits a one count until Ghost grabs a leg and pulls him off of Morgan.
Howard Butz: Ghost breaking up the pin attempt! Going eye-to-eye, the two have words when Tony shoves Ghost back. Getting up into Tony’s face, Ghost returns the shove. After a few more words, Ghost drops down on top of Morgan, making the cover.
Howard Butz: Ghost with the cover now ... and Tony pulls him off! Back to the staredown, they start shoving each other once again before breaking out into a whirlwind of punches.
Howard Butz: We’ve got a fight on our hands! Tony backing Ghost into the corner! The fans count out each blow as it hits it’s target. Blocking one, Ghost spins Tony into the corner before opening up with his own right hand, connecting with Tony’s head repeatedly.
Howard Butz: Ghost now in control of Tony. What started out as a double team on Morgan has quickly broken down into a brawl! Grabbed by the shoulder, Ghost is spun around to look into the smiling face of Jake Morgan. Reaching out, Morgan jabs a thumb into both men’s eyes before looping an arm around both mens’ necks. Falling back, the champ drives them headfirst into the mat.
Howard Butz: DDT! JAKE MORGAN JUST NAILED THEM WITH A DOUBLE DDT! MORGAN WITH THE COVER ON GHOST! ONE! TWO! THREE! NO! GHOST UP AT THE LAST SECOND! With Morgan arguing with the ref on the count, Tony pulls Morgan down with a school boy, pinning him to the mat until Ghost drops a forearm to break it up.
Back on their feet, the three men eye each other until meeting in the center of the ring, fists flying. Blocking blows from both men, Ghost hits each with a staggering head butt before taking them down with a pair of clotheslines.
Knocking Tony to the outside, Ghost picks up on Morgan, putting him down with a scoop slam and a leg drop before making the cover.
Howard Butz: Leg drop and a cover. ONE! Tony with the save, pulling Ghost to the outside! Outside the ring, the two once again go at it, hitting each other with some big bombs, bringing the fans to their feet. Left alone in the ring, Morgan has enough time to shake the cobwebs in order to return to the battle, using the ropes to propel himself to the outside with a plancha, knocking his two opponents down.
Howard Butz: All three men are down and referee Orly Diaz starting a count. Diaz at six ... Morgan is first to his feet, picking up Tony and hoisting him into the air only to bring him down across his knee.
Howard Butz: There’s an atomic drop ... SUPER KICK SENDS TONY INTO THE BARRICADE! Pulling Ghost to his feet, Morgan unloads on him, slamming the man into the barricade before putting him down with a powerbomb onto the hard floor. Rolling
Ghost into the ring, Morgan continues with the beating, sending him into the ropes and flattening him with a spinebuster. Not quite finished, Morgan heads to the ropes to come flying off with a big leg drop.
Howard Butz: You had to expect this, folks. These are two men who do not like each other! Ghost pulled to his feet ... SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE! How much more can he take?
Back on his feet, Morgan throws one of his adversary’s arms around his neck, cinching him up for a vertical suplex. Lifting Ghost into the air, Morgan drops him back to his feet when Tony nails him from behind with a low blow.
Howard Butz: Morgan with the vertical suplex ... TONY WITH THE LOW BLOW! Helping the pained Morgan to his feet, Ghost doubles him over with a boot to the midsection. Grabbing Morgan’s long hair, Ghost drives him facefirst into the canvas with a facebuster.
Howard Butz: Tony with a hammerlock on the downed Morgan and Ghost with the boots to the injured arm! Morgan’s always been able to take some punishment but how much can he take? If they can’t pin him then they’re going to put him out of action!
With Diaz asking for the submission, Morgan can only answer with a shaking head. The answer to Butz’ question didn’t take long to come about as Tom
Pierce raced to the rescue. Howard Butz: IT’S TOM PIERCE! PIERCE WITH A FOREARM TO GHOST! THERE’S A BOOT FOR TONY! TOM PIERCE TO THE RESCUE! As the dressed Pierce hits both of Morgan’s opponents, referee Orly Diaz calls for the bell despite the boos from the fans.
*** Ding *** Ding *** [Rolling to the outside, Morgan collects his belt before heading to the back, leaving Pierce in the ring. There is a smirk on his face when Ghost and Tony pound Pierce into the mat with a double suplex, catching him from behind as the credits begin to roll.
