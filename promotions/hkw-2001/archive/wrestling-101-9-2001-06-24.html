Wrestling 101 — No. 9

24 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 3 —

[The camera opens on a Ford Bronco as it pulls up in front of the Hard Knocks venue. Focussing in on the passenger side door, it opens and a leg swings out followed by the rest of a young man with a blonde receding hairline and matching colored goatee.
Dressed in jean shorts and a dark t-shirt with the picture of Lee Jean's icon, Buddy Lee, the man turns back to slam the door shut, stopping as the unseen driver says something to him.]
Man: Yes, mom ... I will be careful. Yes, mom ... I'll call you as soon as I am finished. Don't worry, mom, I'm a big boy now. Yes, I love you, too.
[Slamming the door, the man turns to find himself surrounded by the State Club Boyz and Ghost. With all three men grinning at him, the man gulps deeply before they drop their gear at his feet.]
Curtis: It's about time someone got here to carry our things. Tony: Yeah, we were beginning to think we'd have to carry our own bags. [Looking from one to the other, the man turns to find the grinning Ghost punching one hand into the other.]
Man: But guys, I'm not here to ... Curtis: Save it. Man: But I’m ... Tony: Yeah, save it. [The two men share a high-five before walking off, Curtis, still limping away on a crutch followed by the mysterious Ghost. Ready to protest but with them already gone, the man shrugs his shoulders before picking up their bags and following off after their owners.
Heading into the building, the camera finds the intern, Neal, whistling to himself as he makes his way through the back hallway when he is suddenly grabbed and spun around, only to come face-to-face with Angel Rotten.]
Angel Rotten: I'm still waiting on that tag team info! [The surprised Neal tries to back away but in Angel's firm grasp, has nowhere to go.] Neal: Right ... uhm ... I was just looking for you. Five teams are in with only three open spots. Mr. Knox keeps that info to himself. I'm guessing two of the spots ars open to teams from The X and the last one for ...
Angel Rotten: What about Morgan? Neal: He says he's in but without a partner, Knox says no way. Angel Rotten: Good, then I still have a chance ... [Releasing Neal, Rotten storms off with the intern muttering under his breath.] Neal: Yeah, fat chance of that happening. [With Neal walking off shaking his head, the camera is once again on the move, this time finding Lars and Raoul in a close huddle, speaking to each other in hushed whispers.]
Raoul: shhhhhhhhh ... Lars, he'll hear you. Lars: I don't care. I want to know why he destroyed the Cloud 9 set. After all we've done for him, I think I deserve to know.
Raoul: Larsipoo, I thought we already went over that. We were going to forgive and forget. After the makeover, Billy's turned over a new leaf. We're seeing a side of him, no one else has seen ...
[Listening to his lispy friend, Lars gets a glassy-eyed faroff look as he ponders what's been said, licking his lips in a lustful manner before being snapped out of it.]
Look at him, isn't he just the cutest? [Following Lars' and Raoul's gaze, the camera turns to find a pink clad Billy, complete with his new hairdo, signing autographs for a bevy of teenage girls as
“Debonaire” by Dope begins to play. Along with the pounding beat, faces flash by quickly accompanied by their names. Men and women of all sizes and shapes as well as all manner of dress.
The “wiggas” DJ Stiff and Mo of CTD - the preppy State Club Boyz, Curtis and Tony - the enigmatic Ghost - an angry Angel Rotten - identically dressed in
“Super K” smocks, the members of Blue Light Special, Harry Palmer and Dean “the Machine” McQueen - the pink clad pair known as Pretty in Pink, Lars and Backyard Billy - and finally, the HKW Texas Outlaw Champion, Jake Morgan.
As the last of the pictures fade, the logo of Hard Knocks Wrestling explodes into view before being swallowed up by inky blackness along with the ending of the noisy music.
Switching to the inside of the venue, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz sitting ringside.] Howard Butz: Hello wrestling fans! [Taking a swing through the audience, the camera picks up the small but boisterious crowd with it’s signs, all trying to get the camera’s attention.]
I’m Howard Butz, and we’re coming to you from the School of Hard Knocks, here in Lewisville, Texas ... on KDFI, Channel 27!
[Butz’ voice is cut off and the camera makes another run through the crowd as a booming voice is heard over all the background sounds.]
Voiceover: You are watching KDFI, Channel 27 - Home of the Texas Rangers! [Sound is returned to normal on Butz.] Howard Butz: If you’re tuning in to Hard Knocks Wrestling for the first time, you’ve picked a good time to do it as tonight, we bring the Hard Knocks debut of the “Texas Bad Boy” ... “Loaded Gun” Dustin Neal.
Throw in an announcement by HKW owner slash president David Knox and you have a great show ... but fans, Hard Knocks Wrestling does not stop there! No siree.
Tonight, on Wrestling 101, we bring to you the return of one of this area’s most popular wrestlers. Gone from the ring for the better part of six months, he makes a return right here ... TONIGHT!
Now ... LET’S GO TO THE RING! [Leaving Butz, the camera makes it’s way to the ring where referee Orly Diaz checks the boots of CTD’s DJ Stiff while his partner, Mo, on the apron offers up some last second advice when The Union Underground’s “South Texas
Deathride” blares through the PA and the shoulder length blone haired Neal steps out from the back.
Making his way to the ring, the muscular young man holds out his hand to slap high-fives but pulls it back out of reach at the last second, drawing some boos.
Sliding in under the ropes, Neal pops up to his feet, ready for a fight, waving for Stiff to come and get some. Both members of CTD move forward but are cut off by the ref. After restoring order, Diaz calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Circling, the two lock up in the center of the ring. Out of a collar and elbow tie-up, neither gains control until pushing the other back.
Howard Butz: Circling again. Collar and elbow ... Neal with control. Go behind into a hammerlock. There’s a drop toe hold. Neal back to his feet.
After a little strut around the ring, Neal turns back to the action, again tying up with Stiff. With a go behind, Neal gains control with a chicken wing.
Howard Butz: Neal in control. Stiff with a back elbow, Two. Three. Stiff with a reversal. Working the arm. Diaz watching closely.
Neal’s back elbows miss there mark, allowing Stiff to wrench on the arm bringing a cry of pain from the “Loaded Gun”.
Howard Butz: Irish whip into a back elbow. Stiff with a leg drop and a cover. ONE! Too early. Back on his feet, Neal complains about his hair being pulled before being spun around and taking a sharp blow to the chest.
Howard Butz: That sounded painful. Stiff pushing Neal into the corner for a series of right hands. Diaz with a count and Stiff backing away.
Turning away from the ref, Stiff charges into the corner, driving a knee into Neal’s midsection. Hooking an arm, Stiff throws Neal over with a hip toss. Howard Butz: DJ Stiff with a hip toss and ... Dustin Neal lands on his feet! I may not know what got Neal barred from the Lonestar Wrestling Council but I’m seeing the athleticism that got him there.
Reaching back, Neal catches Stiff by surprise, snapmaring him over onto the seat of his pants. Dropping down, he lands an elbow to the back of Stiff’s head, bringing Stiff’s partner, Mo onto the ring apron.
Howard Butz: Diaz trying to keep Mo out of the ring. Neal running forward. NECK SNAP! That had to hurt. Rolling to his feet, Neal struts around the ring looking for some adulation from the fans before turning his attention to the corner where Diaz struggles with Mo.
Howard Butz: Neal with a running forearm for Mo! Come on ref, that is totally uncalled for. Diaz with a warning for the newcomer.
Howard Butz: We have to break for a commercial! DO NOT GO AWAY! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Listen to the MIX 102.9 with Jeff and Anna in the morning! What kind of crap do they play? Who knows ... I’m not up that early in the morning, anyway.
A second commercial plays with a handsome young man standing inside a wrestling ring. The well built and shirtless man smiles as his name scrolls across the bottom of the screen.
Backyard Billy: So, you want to be a wrestler? You want to be just like the guys you see right here every week? You want to become the next State Club Boy? Maybe you want to be like me or perhaps you want to be like the champion, Jake Morgan?
Then you need to call the School of Hard Knocks because to be the best, you have to be trained by the best! Call and begin your education today!
A phone number flashes across the screen as scenes of potential wrestlers working out in a ring plays.
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Howard Butz: There’s a cover. ONE! TWO! WHAT?!?!?! Neal just pulled him up! With a smug look on his face, Neal shakes his head at the booing fans while pulling Stiff back to his feet. After pushing him up against the ropes and working him over with lefts and rights, Neal sends Stiff across the ring, taking him down on the return with a knee to the gut, spinning him through the air and dumping Stiff facefirst into the mat.
Howard Butz: Come on, ref. He’s stepping on the back of his neck! Mo’s seen enough! HERE HE COMES!
The other CTD member is met by Orly Diaz and led from the ring. Following a leg drop across the back of Stiff’s neck, Neal’s rear chinlock puts pressure on the downed man’s neck. A couple of cross forearms to the head don’t help the situation either.
With Mo out of the ring, the ref returns to the action, asking Stiff if he wants to submit. Watching closely, Diaz calls for the break when Stiff struggles to the ropes.
With Stiff returning to his feet, Neal misses with a punch only to turn into a boot to the stomach. Grabbed by the hair, he is rammed facefirst into the raised boot of Stiff’s partner, propped up on the top turnbuckle.
Howard Butz: Diaz with a warning for Mo, sending him back to the floor. Stiff taking over, driving Neal’s head into the turnbuckle repeatedly.
Dazed, Neal is pounded into the canvas with a snap suplex and a cover, somehow, getting a shoulder up on a two and three-quarter count.
Howard Butz: That was close. DJ Stiff almost walked out of here victorious! Dustin Neal now on the recieving end of a beating. Stiff laying into him with the boots. Stiff climbing the ropes ...
Climbing to the top and trying to steady himself, Stiff loses his balance, dropping to the mat, drawing laughter and a certain chant from the fans in the background.
Howard Butz: Can they say that on television? It’s a good thing we’re on late! Pulled to his feet, Neal, with time to recover, lands a forearm uppercut before whipping Stiff across the ring. On the return, he drives a boot into Stiff’s midesction, doubling him up. Grabbing him by the neck, Neal spins his body, sitting down.
Howard Butz: SPINNING NECK BREAKER! DJ Stiff’s neck has gotten quite a workout. Back on his feet, Neal waits across the ring for his wobbled opponent to stand. Using the ropes to pull himself up, Stiff is sent flying over the ropes, out onto the apron.
Dazed and slow to stand, Stiff is an easy target for the charging Dustin Neal. Diving over the top, Neal grabs Stiff’s head, bringing him along for the ride with a somersault neck breaker, dumping both men onto the floor below.
Howard Butz: OH MY GOD! OH MY GOD! THE REF LEAVING THE RING TO CHECK ON STIFF! THIS DOES NOT LOOK GOOD! Getting back to his feet slowly, Neal pushes Diaz out of the way, pulling Stiff to his feet and rolling him into the ring. Grabbing Stiff from behind, Neal latches on a cobra clutch, dropping to the mat and applying a body scissors.
With the amount of punishment to Stiff’s neck, it’s over as Diaz calls for the bell. *** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 3 —

[Neal pulls his arm away from the ref, marching right up into the camera,
motioning with his fingers as if they are six guns.] Dustin Neal: BANG! BANG! YOU’RE DEAD! [With a laugh he shoves the camera back before kicking Stiff several times then high tailing it out of the ring as Stiff’s partner comes after him.
With Neal gone and Mo leading DJ Stiff to the back, the camera zooms in on Butz. Howard Butz: An auspicious debut for one “Loaded Gun” Dustin Neal! What now?!?! [Butz looks around as “It’s Raining Men” blasts out from the loudspeakers and Raoul, dressed in a tight black t-shirt, choker and purple jeans, enthusiastically skips down to the ring and hops into the seat next to a rather annoyed announcer.]
Howard Butz: I've been joined here at ringside by Raoul. What can I do for you, Raoul? Raoul: Lars and I decided that I should be the one to lead the show into a break before Cloud 9 because frankly, you dont announce it with the enthusiasm and pizzaz the show deserves.
Howard Butz: Wasn’t there a Cloud 9 just last week? I thought David and Lars decided to make it a bi-weekly show?
Raoul: I think that the last one is just stuck in your head Butzy! And now ... everyone get ready because when we return from these commercials, we'll go straight into the glorious extravaganza that is Cloud 9 hosted, as always, by the sexy, stylish and stud muffinest wrestler in HKW ... LARS!!!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
Taco Bell is now open later. So get your fat ass off the couch and come get fatter! Yo quiero Gordo Americanos!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning from the commercial finds the house lights dimmed and the crowd quietly gathering its energy for the oncoming boo-fest. Suddenly, a spotlight drops on the area that holds the Cloud 9 set as "Love Shack" by the
B-52s begins to play while the set rotates toward the audience. The set, decorated with lava lamps, incense burners and candles has a massively oversized bean bag couch with Lars sprawled on top of it in all his glory.
Raoul, dressed as before, dances spastically to the music in front of the couch. Lars, wearing tiedyed bellbottoms with a matching scarf and platform sandals, pulls a microphone from under himself. Turning over, he propshis head in his hands, kicking his legs in the air.]
Lars: Big kisses all around! Welcome to CLOUD 9!!!!! [Lars pauses to soak up the applause but is met by laughter and booing.] I had a fantastic show all about the use of makeup as an enhancer planned tonight but my scheduled cohost and canvas for the evening failed to show up.
Shame on you Nathaniel! [He pauses.] So instead, I've decided to talk about the upcoming tag team tournament. It’s going to be sooooo exciting! First off, let me just say there are a number of good teams that have already entered.
Raoul and I even wasted a couple of hours looking at the teams from that X group and let me say that with a little work from us, their teams could look fabulous. Just remember, even though we'll "technically" be opponents, we're always ready to help each and every one of you to look your best, because if you dont look good ... I wont want to roll around the ring with you.
Next up on my list of teams is CTD. I haven’t met these guys yet and I'm not sure what the T stands for but those outfits have nothing whatsoever to do with Cross Dressing. I'm suuure Raoul can find something in your sizes.
Raoul: Without a doubt! They make clothes for any size, even guys with horrendous bodies like theirs.
Lars: Raoul ... its not our place to pick on the stylishly handicapped ... Oh who am I kidding?!? What were they thinking?
Raoul: Maybe they have a women's prison garb fetish? [Both men burst into laughter as the crowd jeers them.] Lars: Now, you stop that! Next up are those K-mart boys. Yuck!! Raoul: Make that a double yuck! Lars: Next week, Raoul and I will be going on location and braving the local K-mart to show everyone how bad it really is. I swear, the things I go through to help people.
Raoul: I'm NOT touching ANYTHING! Lars: I'll be sporting a pair of gloves, so everything should be alright. Getting back to the tournament, neither Jacob nor Angel have found a partner yet. Raoul: (Sarcastically) Hmmm? Wonder why? Lars: We'll skip them. Raoul: Goooood idea. Lars: Then, we come to the newest, most amazing ... Raoul: And sexy! Lars: Team to join the tournament ... "Pretty in Pink". A combination of the high flying Backdoor Billy and myself. Is there really any doubt as to who will win it all?
[The crowd begins to boo loudly.] Lars: That was a rhetorical question THANK YOU. [“Pretty Fly for a White Guy” by the Offspring plays as Backyard Billy steps out from the back. Dressed in a huge t-shirt and baggy sweats with his head covered by a bandana, he walks out with Singapore cane in hand with a big fuzzy pink bangle hanging from the tip of it.
With Billy stepping up, Lars smiles as the young wrestler hands his weapon to Raoul.] DO IT FOR ME, BILLY! Raoul: Uh huh, uh huh! [With a wave of the hand, Billy tears off the bandana from his head, revealing his stylish cut.
Lars: DO IT FOR ME, BILLY! Raoul: Uh huh, uh huh! [Grabbing the torn collar of his oversized shirt, Billy tears it in half from his body, revealing the oiled chest underneath. With his gaze fixed, Raoul licks his lips as his hand strokes up and down on the cane.]
Lars: DO IT FOR ME, BILLY! Raoul: Uh huh, uh huh! [With a single yank, Billy pulls away the sweats, showing the tiny pink shorts underneath, bringing a squeal from the small female portion of the audience while both Lars and Raoul hungerily eye him.
The cheers die away when Harry Palmer and Dean McQueen of Blue Light Special make their way out from the back.]
Harry Palmer: CUT THE MUSIC! You come out here each week, preaching about skin care or peddling your overpriced cosmetics and beauty products and now ... you’ve done this to one of the promotion’s very best ...
[Palmer points at Billy, sending Raoul over the top who is quickly restrained by Lars and Billy.]
On this show of yours, there’s no mention of Blue Light Always or talk of the best tag team here ... us. So, prance around all you want because one way or another, we’re going to beat you in the tag tournament ... and when that happens, we’re shutting you down!
[With smiles on their faces, the Blue Light Special boys are nailed with the Singapore cane held by both Lars and Billy. HKW security fills the ring to pull both parties apart.
With Raoul clawing at the air to scratch some eyes out, David Knox walks out from the back with Shane. Entering the ring, crowd roars it's approval bringing a smile from Knox. Putting a hand up, he quiets the crowd so he can continue.]
David Knox: I’d like to thank you for coming out to watch another exciting night of HKW action and would like to take this opportunity to discuss the upcoming Tag Tournament. In the next week, the random pairings of the eight teams will be made to kick off the battle for the tag belts but seeing as we have some animousity here inside the ring, I am going to kick things off a little earlier than announced.
Next week, you gentlemen ... Pretty in Pink and Blue Light Special will be the first match to open things up for us which leaves us with six more teams to determine.
CTD, Tony and Ghost as well as the team of Ricky Starr and Cody Sanchez bring us to five. Two of the other three teams will be represented by the X, who have graciously agreed to be part of this tournament.
At this time, I'd like to introduce the owner of X ... Newton. [ZZTop's "Heard it on the X" plays over the PA system and a young man dressed in a black "X" T-shirt, khaki pants and a red hat, worn backwards, walks to the ring, followed by a much larger man wearing all black, with the exception of a red bandana.]
Newton: ’Sup, Dave? David Knox: Welcome to HKW, Newton. We have five teams ... and two spots open for The X. [The X faithful start an "X" chant, causing the HKW fans to respond in kind., bringing both owners to smile at the level of fan participation.]
Newton: Well, first off, Skip Weste ... Voice: SHUT THE HELL UP! [Newton looks over at Knox, who's shaking his head, while he rubs his forehead. He starts to apologize to Newton, as the owner of the voice steps out from the back ... Jake Morgan, with a microphone in hand and his title belt wrapped around his waist. Fans rain down boos but he ignores them all with his attention focused on the ring and the men standing in it, especially David
Knox.] Jake Morgan: Correct me if I’m wrong but I don’t remember hearing my name mentioned for the tournament. There will be no tournament until I am ...
[Newton steps forward, cutting Morgan off.] Newton: Yo, no one asked you ... [Jake turns his attention from Knox to the young owner.] Jake: Listen up you reject from a Limp Biskit video, children should be seen, not heard. Now shut up or I'll come up there, spank you and throw you and your little nanny out on your collective asses.
[Newton's bodyguard and Shane walk up to the ropes holding them open for Jake, beckoning him to enter the ring. He ignores them and continues on his rant.] David Knox: Jake, you’re not a stupid man, so why do you keep insisting that you should be involved in the tag tournament, when you have no partner?
No partner, no tournament. Give it a rest already. [Jake looks at Knox like he just shot his dog. Shaking his head, Jake pulls the microphone to his lips once more.]
Jake Morgan: You want five teams to compete with two more teams from this bozo’s halfbaked promotion for the HKW tag team titles and you want me to give it a rest? Seven teams I can beat by myself and you want to deny your
Texas Outlaw champion and the fans the opportunity to see why I AM THE BEST THIS PLACE HAS TO OFFER? How can I give it a rest, when you've been preparing me to compete in this tournament for weeks now? I've been fighting handicap matches for weeks. I had a partner for most of those matches but he was too much of a <bleep> to show up, then when he did get the balls to show, he got beat in two seconds. I've proven time and time again, that I can beat two men all by myself. I don't need a partner but I’ll play the game your way ...
[Knox looks at the two men protecting him and Newton in the ring, then smiles.] David Knox: My way? Jake Morgan: That’s right, Knox ... I have a partner. [With a smirk, Morgan drops his mic and heads to the back as the show breaks for a commercial.]

— Part 3 of 3 —

*** COMMERCIAL BREAK ***
Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the commercial, the camera finds Jake Morgan inside the ring with his opponent for the main event, the same man from the beginning of the program, carrying the bags of the State Club Boyz.
With the bell, the camera moves to the ringside table with Howard Butz who has been joined by the Star Maker Dillon Diamond and a handsome young man.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: We’re underway! With the action going on in the ring, the camera remains with Butz and his companions at the table.
Howard Butz: Joining me for the main event is “the Star Maker” Dillon Diamond and on my right, Jason Flint, former Public Access Wrestling Texas and Tag Team Champion. Gentlemen, thank you for joining us. Pulling back, the camera takes in Morgan whipping his opponent into the ropes and putting him down with a boot to the face. Following an elbow drop, it returns to Butz.
Howard Butz: After the long layoff, what brings you back to the sport and more importantly, Hard Knocks Wrestling, Jason.
Jason Flint: To be honest, Howard over the last six months while resting and letting some nagging minor injuries heal, I’ve been following things here in
HKW. From the has-beens, never-weres and never-will-bes ... Howard Butz: You’re referring to Gary Pate, Tom Pierce and Nate Hatred? The three men share a laugh while inside the ring, Morgan hammers his opponent with a body slam then a knee drop off the middle ropes.
Jason Flint: Right. Seriously though, there are some talented people here as well as some old familiar faces. I’ve been sitting back and watching from afar.
Howard Butz: One of those familiar faces being the HKW Texas Outlaw champion, Jake Morgan in our main event, defending his title against The X’s Buddy Lee Jr.! Morgan, slipping in behind the smaller man, tries to wrap him up in the cobra clutch but instead finds himself dropped with a stunner. Scrambling for the cover, Lee hooks a leg.
Howard Butz: There’s a cover. ONE! TWO! KICK OUT BY THE CHAMPION! WE’VE GOT OURSELVES A GOOD ONE HERE, FOLKS! Jason Flint: This Buddy Lee Jr. looks pretty good. Howard Butz: By being here, does this mean we’re going to be seeing a return to the squared circle, Jason?
Jason Flint: I think it’s safe to say that is a very strong possibility. With the rest and time away from the ring, I am one hundred percent healthy and being here today, watching from this close makes the juices flow. I can hear the ring calling me back into action.
WIth a short arm clothesline, Morgan puts Lee down to the canvas. Throwing himself back against the ropes, he shoots forward, leaping into the air and landing a splash on top of Lee, putting the challenger in a pinning predicament.
Jason Flint: Wow ... Jake learned a new move ... a belly flop. I’m impressed. I guess you can teach an old dog new tricks. Howard Butz: There’s a cover! ONE! TWO! THR ... NO! After arguing with the ref over the slowness of his count, Morgan pulls Lee to his feet and slams him back into the corner tunrbuckles before opening up with reverse knife hands that pop loudly of Buddy Lee Jr.’s chest.
Jason Flint: I take that back, some things never change. Howard Butz: Morgan opening up now on his oppponent! Jason Flint: I’ve seen this one. Here it comes ... punch, punch, kick. Howard Butz: There’s a couple of right hands and a boot to the midsection. Buddy Lee Jr. now in all kinds of trouble. Also with us today is the self-proclaimed “Star Maker”, Dillon Diamond ... Dillon, chime in anytime.
Dillon Diamond: Thank you Howard, I’m just happy to be here. With a title defense going on and just a few seats down from someone who has wowed the crowds time and time again with his acrobatic wrestling style, it’s all my pleasure. It’s a great time for wrestling and more importantly, Hard Knocks
Wrestling. It kind of leaves me speechless but I’ll do my best to be a professional and less of a fan.
Howard Butz: Dillon, with Jason possibly returning to the ring, does this put him into your equation as well?
Dillon Diamond: Well, I should say so. I am always on the lookout for the best talent and that man ... or woman, who turns out to be the complete package ...
the looks, the ability inside and outside of the ring, the physique ... is who I’m after. That’s the person, I’m ready and willing to take all the way to the top! Maybe after the match, I can sit down with Jason and find out what
I can do for him. Throwing him into the mix just makes my job that much harder but that’s what I do and have been doing for a number of years now. This company is filled with talented people ... Backyard Billy, Tony and Curtis,
Lars, Angel Rotten, the champion, Jake Morgan ... Howard Butz: BUDDY LEE WITH THE REVERSE ... BRAINBUSTER! Sorry, Dillon ... Jason Flint: Morgan going for the vertical suplex and Buddy Lee wraps his leg around Morgan’s, blocking him before countering his move and dropping the champ on his head. You know, the longer this match goes on, I am more and more impressed with this Buddy Lee Jr.
Howard Butz: Morgan rolling to the outside. He’s got Buddy Lee by the leg ... pulling him out of the ring. There’s a whip into the barricade. Morgan laying into him with the boots ... HERE COMES MORGAN!
With the ref following him and warning Morgan to take the match back into the ring, the champ grabs a chair vacated by the scrambling Butz and Diamond. With the chair in hand, Morgan locks eyes with the now standing Flint. With Diaz getting between the two men, Morgan breaks away and rolls into the ring, breaking up the count before heading back to the outside. Throwing the chair down to the ground, he pulls Buddy to his feet.
Wrapping an arm around Buddy’s head, Morgan slaps him on the back before driving his head into the chair with a DDT before pushing Buddy back into the ring.
Howard Butz: There’s an Irish whip! SPINEBUSTER! Jason, Dillon, it looks like Morgan is ready to put this one away. Flint’s reply is simple yet effective as he covers his mouth to stifle a yawn. After a short arm knee to the stomach, Morgan puts Buddy down with a reverse Russina leg sweep but rather than cover him for the win, he approaches the ropes near the announers. Holding the ropes open, he motions to the smiling
Jason Flint. Howard Butz: I think that’s an open invitation, Jason. Chants from the fans bring the growling Morgan into a shouting match before he is steered back to the bout by the ref. After hammering on a lethargic Buddy
Lee, Morgan ends the match following a suplex out of the cobra clutch, nonchalantly pressing both hands onto his opponent’s chest.
** Ding *** Ding *** [With Morgan grabbing up his belt and making off to the back after pointing at Flint, the camera returns to Butz and company.] Howard Butz: We’re out of time. We’ll see you next week!! *** Hard Knocks Wrestling©2001 ***
