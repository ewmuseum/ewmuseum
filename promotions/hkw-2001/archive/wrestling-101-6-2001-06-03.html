Wrestling 101 — No. 6

3 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 3 —

[The camera opens on Howard Butz standing in front of a HKW banner along with newly crowned champion, Jake Morgan. Morgan, ever ready for battle in his wrestling gear, stands with the adornments of his most recent encounter ...
slung over his shoulder hangs the Texas Outlaw strap while his arm is heavily taped. The smiling Butz turns to the camera and raises the microphone to his lips.]
Howard Butz: Hello fans, I'm here with our new Texas Outlaw Champion, Jake Morgan. So Jake, how does it feel to be the top man in HKW now? [Jake looks at Butz as if he's crazy.] Jake Morgan: NOW? What do you mean now? From the moment I signed a contract I've been the TOP MAN. This belt doesn't make it so, I MAKE IT SO! Where the hell have you been, Howie? Who else has packed the house like I have? Who else has dominated their matches? Who has come even close to beating me?
Howard Butz: Well, Curtis has ... [Jake turns on Butz quickly, screaming at the top of his lungs.] Jake Morgan: Curtis is in the Hospital thanks to me. He may never walk again. So, don't ask me about Curtis. [Turning if off just as quickly, Morgan runs a hand through his hair, he smiles as he answers the question in a oily fashion.]
He's history. Howard Butz: Okay then, what about Ghost? [Smiling, Jake slowly nods his head.] Jake Morgan: I go through the most brutal match in HKW history on my way to becoming this promotion’s first champion ... and you ask me about a person who has yet to wrestle a single match? You ask me about someone who has attacked me from behind to get a little attention?
What about him? [Before another word is said, both men plummet into sudden darkness. Moving elsewhere, the camera focusses in on two rather bizarre individuals sitting at a table playing cards and having a couple of beers. Decked out like a big eagle, the UNT mascot sips his Miller Light through a straw while the monkey suited, El Macho Sasquatcho deals out the cards.]
Mean Green: So, I looked him square in the eye and asked him if he had a problem. EMS: Yeah? What did he say? Mean Green: What do expect from some fat roadie? He said, “no sir, no problem”. So, I let him off the hook.
[EMS stops with the cards and points behind the UNT mascot while shaking his head.] Fat guys ... they’re all alike. [A hand suddenly grabs the mascot by the beak, snapping the head around. Looking into the eyes of the seated “walking bag of feathers” is none other than HKW wrestler, Tom Pierce. Dressed in a Guns ’n Roses t-shirt over his wrestling togs, Pierce kicks over the table, sending bottles, cards and El Mach flying.]
Tom Pierce: Don’t call me fat, ass<BLEEP>! [Pulling the eagle out of his chair, Pierce looks back at the scrambling gorilla.] Maybe you should find yourself a seat, we got some good matches coming up. [Smiling, Pierce drags the Mean Green mascot out of the room. moving to the outside of the building, the camera focusses in on HKW security guard Terry standing with his back to the backstage entrance, doing his best to look tough and important. The camera pans around to show the approach of Raoul, Lars’ best friend and confidant. Wearing the same schoolboy outfit that he wore on
Cloud 9 the previous week, Raoul saunters up to Terry, giving him the once over.] Terry: Yes? Raoul: I was wondering if you might be interested in making a little extra cash tonight? Terry: (Obviously a little uncomfortable) Ummmm ... uhhh ... that would depend on what I have to do.
Raoul: Nothing much, just a little manual labor. Maybe 2 minutes ofwork total will earn you and three of your friends $100.
Terry: A hundred bucks? Whata we gotta do? [Raoul leans over and whispers into the security guard’s ear.) Terry: I dont know. You make it twenty dollars each ... Raoul: M’kayyyyyyyyy ... Terry: DEAL! [Terry rushes off, leaving his station to find three other guards. Licking his finger, Raoul “makes a mark” in the air when “Debonaire” by Dope begins to play. Along with the pounding beat, faces flash by quickly accompanied by their names. Men and women of all sizes and shapes as well as all manner of dress.
The preppy State Club Boyz, Curtis and Tony - the young baseball bat wielding Backyard Billy - an angry Angel Rotten - identically dressed in “Super K” smocks, the members of Blue Light Special, Harry Palmer and Dean “the
Machine” McQueen - the tough but husky Tom Pierce - the pink clad Lars, also known as the Lone Stranger - and finally, the newly crowned HKW Texas Outlaw
Champion, Jake Morgan. As the last of the pictures fade, the logo of Hard Knocks Wrestling explodes into view before being swallowed up by inky blackness along with the ending of the noisy music.
Switching to the inside of the venue, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz sitting ringside.] Howard Butz: Welcome to Hard Knocks Wrestling! [Taking a swing through the audience, the camera picks up the boisterious crowd with it’s signs, all trying to get the camera’s attention.]
Howard Butz, here and we’re coming to you from the School of Hard Knocks, here in Lewisville, Texas ... on KDFI, Channel 27!
[Butz’ voice is cut off and the camera makes another run through the crowd as a booming voice is heard over all the background sounds.]
Voiceover: You are watching KDFI, Channel 27 - Home of the Texas Rangers! [Sound is returned to normal on Butz.] Howard Butz: Last week, we crowned a champion and this week promises to be just as exciting but before we get to our first match and the rest of the action, we have this from Backyard Billy on his Loser Leaves Town Match against Lars in our main event!
[Butz disappears as the taped interview runs. Standing in front of the HKW banner is Butz with Billy.]
Howard Butz: ... feud has gone on for weeks now ... Backyard Billy: Howard, tonight, it’s all over. Lars is going out on a stretcher and that will be that ...
Howard Butz: Now, Lars has already said he doesn’t want you to have to leave, should you lose ...
[Whipping his head around, an angry Billy yanks the mic away from Butz.] Backyard Billy: Look ... there is no losing tonight. You can consider one eyesore finished ... 1 ... 2 ... 3!
[The clip ends with Billy storming off. The smiling Butz returns in live action ringside.] Howard Butz: As the show opened, we saw part of my interview with Jake Morgan, when the lights went. Production accident or sabotage? We don’t ... WHOA!!!
[A cheer rises from the crowd as Tom Pierce dragging the Mean Green mascot by his beak both emerge from the back. Pulling the protesting fowl to the ring,
Pierce tosses him inside. With referee Orly Diaz running down the ring, a argument between he and Pierce ensues before he reluctantly calls for the bell and a match is suddenly underway, much to the fans’ delight.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Shoving back the ref, Pierce pulls the bird off the mat, pushing his large head down before unloading a series of uppercuts into his beak. After knocking his adversaey back into the corner, Pierce sends him flying across the ring, whipping him into the far ringpost.
Howard Butz: Pierce is fighting mad and he’s unloading on his opponent. I’m not sure what set him off but I for one would not want to be on the recieiving of what he’s handing out right now! We’ve got to break for a commercial ...
DO NOT GO AWAY! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** All-star Michael Finley, Dirk Nowitski and Steve Nash of the Dallas Mavericks ... the hottest ticket in Dallas ... oh, wait, basketball season is over leaving us with the Texas Rangers and the highest overpaid player in the league, Alex Rodriquez! A quarter of a billion dollars later, the Rangers find themselves already over 20 games out of first place. A team on a one way ride to nowhere!
On a positive note ... drink Dr. Pepper! It makes the world taste better! Nothing like being hooked! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Returning from the break finds more of the same with Pierce handing out a beating. Following a scoop slam, Pierce’s boot finds a home on the bird’s beak. With an elbow drop bringing down to the mat, Pierce hooks a leg for the pin, pulling up the feathered headgear on a two count. With the ref’s admonishment, Pierce answers back with a devious smile.
Howard Butz: That is one battered bird and it looks like he’s going to get more because Tom Pierce does not look ready to end things just yet.
Lifting the bird into the air by his legs, Pierce leans back before whipping his body forward, slamming his opponent on his back. Still holding on to the chicken legs, Pierce flips his body forward, landing on top of the lifeless bird and putting him into a pinning predicament again.
Howard Butz: There’s a cover. ONE! TWO! Flipping his body back, Pierce rolls off his opponent back to his feet where he, in one fluid motion rips Mean Green off the mat and into the air again.
With the same fluid motion, Pierce puts him down again, flips on top of him before rolling back to his feet and repeating the process a third time.
Howard Butz: THERE IT IS AGAIN! Three slams onto the mat and each time, Pierce refuses to go for the cover!
After dragging his hapless foe back to the center of the ring, Tom heads for the ropes. From the top and a chant of “H-K-DUB!” in the background, Pierce leaps off, landing a ring rattling elbow drop.
Howard Butz: THE EAGLE HAS LANDED!!! Nearly bouncing back to his feet, Pierce circles the ring, whipping the fans into a frenzy before stopping and pointing down at the Mean Green bird. With his thumb, Pierce drags it across his throat, signalling the beginning of the end.
Whipping the fowl forward, Pierce waits for the return with a raised fist. On the return, the eagle stops dead in his tracks, wanting no part of Pierce’s
Heart Punch. Howard Butz: There’s a kick to the midsection. Pierce now wrapping up Mean Green’s head ... DDT!!! Pierce planting his opponent’s head into the canvas. That has to be the end of it!
Pulling the bird to his feet, Tom Pierce is not quite done. Howard Butz: Pierce pushing the bird forward into the ropes. Falling back with a roll-up. Pierce following through, he rolls to his feet. HE HAS THE LEG! HE
HAS THE LEG! Locked in a half Boston crab and in the middle of the ring, the bird was plucked and grounded. Going nowhere fast, there was nothing to do but tap the mat ... and tap he did.
*** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 3 —

Howard Butz: Tom Pierce finds himself victorious once again in Hard Knocks
Wrestling where you never know who ... or what is going to show up for a match! [Butz shakes his head before the camera follows Pierce to the back amid the shouting fans. Moving to the back, the camera focusses in on a whistling Raoul in his little schoolboy outfit swinging an empty wicker basket. Blowing a kiss at the camera, he heads into a room and is followed.]
Raoul: Yoohoo ... Lars ... [Stopping with a smile on his face, he waves his basket at the yet unseen Lars.] Guess what I just found. This. [He rolls his eyes as he holds the basket up.] Tomas apparently does not like cream puffs. YOUR cream puffs. He gave them to those Blue Light Special boys.
[Sticking a finger in his mouth, Raoul imitates vomiting.] Maybe we should send him somthing else instead. We both know not to send him a fruit basket since he doesn’t like fruit. I ask you, what kind of man does not like a couple of fruits?
Anyway, I was thinking about putting together a nice skincare basket for him. An emoliant, maybe some facial scrub ... [Raoul stops when there is a knock at the door.] Lars: Cooooommmmeeeee Iiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiin! [David Knox steps in but jumps back and quickly covers the camera lens with his hand before the interior of the room can be seen.]
David Knox: Lars! Put some clothes on! Lars: Oh, sorry Davey. [Both Lars and Raoul giggle for several seconds with only Knox's hand visible before he uncovers the lens and enters the room. Covered by a short, pink robe,
Lars steps into view.] David Knox: I just wanted to let you know that I've granted one of Billy’s requests for your match.
Lars: Oh? David Knox: Yeah, I've agreed to allow the match to be a hardcore match. Lars: MMMMMMM! Hardcore! David Knox: You do know what a hardcore match entails don’t you, Lars? [Lars has a silly grin on his face and appears to be daydreaming.] Lars: I have a pretty good idea, but I didnt know we could show that on TV. Thanks for the warning Davey, I'll do my best to make it exciting. [Lars ushers Knox to the door and shuts it.] David Knox: It’s not too late to ... [Knox looks surprised as the hits him on the backside before the camera fades into another crappy commercial.]
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** Scenes of poor children in some third world country flash by as the Christian Children’s Fund asks you to make a difference in their world for as little as 89 cents a day! Food, medicine, education all possible on 89 cents a day! Won’t you help make a difference? A second commercial opens with two men standing inside a wrestling ring. Both men looking out of place in their preppy clothing. Their names - Tony and
Curtis - the State Club Boyz, scrolls across the bottom of the screen. Curtis: So, you want to be a wrestler? Then you need to call the School of Hard Knocks because to be the best, you have to be trained by the best! Call and begin your education today!
Tony: The best money can buy! A phone number flashes across the screen as scenes of potential wrestlers working out in a ring plays.
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Back from the commercial, an impatient Backyard Billy leads the fans in their “BRING IT ON!” chant, working them into a frenzy. Pacing back and forth like a caged animal, Billy is calmed by referee Orly Diaz when suddenly Raoul bursts from the back and skips down to the ring. Grabbing a microphone from
Howard Butz, he climbs into the ring and taunts Billy by sticking his tongue out at him. Diaz restrains the irate Billy. Before speaking, Raoul looks around the ring at the number of weapons and objects, looking a little puzzled by it all. Shrugging his shoulders, he speaks.]
Raoul: And now, the moment you've all been waiting for, Backdoor Productions proudly presents the one ...
THE ONLY ... LAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRRRRSSSSSSSSSSS!!!!!!! [The lights dim and a pink spotlight shines down on the entranceway to the ring. From the loudspeakers, “I know What Boys Like” begins to play.
Stepping from the back are the four security guards, dressed as Egyptian slaves, carrying their “master” atop a giant pillow. Lars, dressed in a skimpy version of a pharoah costume, is fanning himself and eating grapes.
Inside the ring, Raoul jumps up and down with excitement. Trailing along behind the entourage is an angry looking Neal, carrying a box that he drops in
Lars’ corner The camera focusses in on the contents of the box which includes feathers, fuzzy handcuffs, a large pillow and scented oils and lotions.
Lifted up to the ring apron, Lars gestures to his “adoring fans” when Billy charges across the ring, hitting him with forearms before pullinb him over the top rope into the ring. With Raoul diving for cover, the ref calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Tearing Lars’ headgear and extras from his body and kicking them out of the ring, Billy sends the man for a ride into the ropes, throwing him over with a couple of deep arm drags.
Pulled back to his feet in an arm bar, a dazed Lars is sent across the ring again, this time sent flying through the air with a big back body drop much to the fans’ delight.
Howard Butz: This has been brewing for weeks, folks and this is one chance Billy is not going to allow an easy escape! Yanked off the mat, Lars counters Billy, sending him across the ring into the far ropes. Ducking his head, Lars sets up for a back body drop of his own but is caught with a kick from a very ready Billy. Jumping up onto the ropes, Billy comes off with a twisting leg drop.
Howard Butz: Billy taking it to Lars. He’s got a chair ... Bouncing off the ropes, Billy shoots forward, diving at the last second, slamming the chair into a seated Lars’ head.
Howard Butz: OUCH! This is getting brutal fast! Pulling Lars up by the hair, Billy rears back with a fist, stopping when a begging Lars throws up his hands pleading for mercy. Shaking his head, Billy fires away with punches with the fans counting as each blow lands.
Howard Butz: Punch after punch landing! Still pleading for mercy, Lars slams his head forward into Billy’s crotch, knocking him to the canvas. Pulling himself up using the ropes then the referee who got too close, Lars staggers back to his corner, grabbing some items from his box in his corner.
Howard Butz: What’s he doing? Staggering back to Billy, Lars dumps the contents of a bottle before dropping to his knees and rubbing it into Billy’s skin.
Howard Butz: HE’S RUBBING OIL ON BILLY!!! HE’S RUBBING OIL ON BILLY!!! With the ref pushing him off of Billy, Lars becomes embroiled in a shoving match with Orly Diaz. Threats from Diaz are cut short with a chairshot from
Billy to Lars’ back. With Lars down, Billy tries to wipe the oil from his glistening chest. Screaming at Raoul on the outside of the ring, Billy props a garbage can between the ropes in the corner.
Howard Butz: There’s an Irish whip into corner and a follow-up corner splash! Stepping back, Billy allows Lars to stagger out of the corner before falling flat on his face in the center of the ring. Grabbing the partially crushed garbage can, Billy lays it in the center of ring before pulling Lars up the ropes.
Howard Butz: Billy pushing Lars up the ropes. Cinching him up ... there’s ... a ... SUPERPLEX ON THE GARBAGE CAN! Billy with the cover. ONE! TW ... Diaz pulled away by Raoul!
Pulled from the ring, Diaz lets Raoul have an earful but the damage is done, breaking the count. Sliding to the outside of the ring, Billy slams into the ref who is pushed in front of him by Raoul.
Howard Butz: Billy with a baseball slide ... right into the ref! Raoul pushed Diaz right into the oncoming Billy! With the fans howling for blood, Billy chases Raoul around the ring until they are joined by Lars to the rescue.
Howard Butz: Lars and Billy going at it! There’s Raoul with a chair shot! The fans laugh at what passes for a chair shot from Raoul, if the weak swipe can be called that. With the threat of Billy now passed, Raoul and Lars share a hug outside the ring while the fans boo at the unmercifully.
Howard Butz: Better pay attention to your opponent, Lars! Billy with a slingshot plancha! Saving Raoul, Lars pushes his companion out of the way just in time to take the full brunt of the blow. Both men hit the deck, bringing the fans to their feet while the shreiking Raoul pleads with Lars to get up.
Howard Butz: Billy to his feet first and chasing off Raoul. If you’re just joining us, this is a Loser Leaves Town match that has been brewing over the last few weeks. Billy with an elbow to the back of Lars’ head.
After whipping Lars into the ring apron, Billy sends him into the ring barrier, following behind and crushing him with a spinning leg lariat.
Howard Butz: Billy pulling a table out from under the ring and setting up outside between the ring and the barrier. He’s dumping Lars on top of it. The fans are eating this up as Billy climbs the ropes ... what’s he ... SENTON!
OH MY GOD! BILLY WITH A SOMERSAULT SENTON OFF THE TOP ROPE! The weight of both men on the table splintered it bringing a chant of “H-K-DUB!” from the fans. After pushing Lars back into the ring, Billy tosses a second garbage can in with his adversary before coming in himself with his trusty Singapore cane in hand.
Pulling Lars to his feet, Billy drops the can over his unsteady adversary. Nodding his head at the fans, Billy winds up with his weapon before unloading on the can and “ringing Lars’ bell”.
Howard Butz: HE’S SPLINTERED THE SINGAPORE CANE! With the weapon in fragments, Lars, with a dented garbage can over his head, drops to his knees.
Howard Butz: DROP KICK! DROP KICK TO THE FACE! With Lars down, Billy heads to the corner where the box of “unusual weapons” is. Dumping it’s contents, Billy smashes the box before turning back to Lars. After dragging him to the center of the ring, Billy was up the ropes once again, motioning for the finish.
Howard Butz: Billy ready to end it with a shooting star splash ... wait, Raoul from behind hitting him with a pillow!?!?!?!
Hit over the head from behind, Billy falls forward like a ton of bricks, crashing facefirst into the canvas. On the outside of the ring, Raoul did his best to hide the brick he pulls out of the pillow before climbing into the ring and pulling Lars’ arm across Billy’s prone body.
Howard Butz: NO!!! Raoul pushing a half conscious Diaz into the ring! There’s the count! ONE! TWO! THREE! NO! LARS WINS! LARS WINS!
*** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 3 of 3 —

[A gleeful Raoul dances around the ring like a little girl before helping the
victorious Lars to his feet. Out of it, Lars leans heavily upon Raoul then the ropes as Orly Diaz in similar shape, lofts the victor’s arm into the air to a chorus of boos.]
Howard Butz: Backyard Billy is banished from Hard Knocks Wrestling with the loss! Wait, Lars has something to say ...
[Handed a mic from Raoul, Lars waves off the booing fans bringing some quiet before speaking.]
Lars: As I said before, I don’t want Billy to leave ... [Lars stops as the cheering fans erupt.] ... but ... that hair has to go! Billy NEEDS a make-over! [The smiling Raoul pulls out a pair of scissors, cutting at the air then dropping to his knees.]
Howard Butz: He’s cutting Billy’s hair! Snip, snip, snip goes Raoul with his shears, sending hair in all directions to the boos when Harry Palmer and Dean McQueen hit the ring to make the save, sending both Lars and Raoul fleeing.
With the fans booing and throwing things at Raoul and Lars, the pair flee to the back while inside the ring, both memebers of Blue Light Special help Billy to his feet, covering his newly shorn head , leading him from the ring. The camera fades as they disappear into the back.]
*** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** A red and yellow decorated pizza parlor with a gorilla throwing pizza dough into the air and catching it. Mounds of fresh ingredients - pepperoni, canadian bacon, green peppers, black olives and more piled high onto piles of cheese.
With crust baked to a golden brown, welcome to Monkeyman Pizza! “When you need pizza, don’t monkey around! Call Monkeyman Pizza! Free delivery or swing on by and pick it up yourself! You’ll go bananas for
Monkeyman Pizza!” *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** [Returning from the break, the camera finds Angel Rotten talking with Neal, making her point with emphatic jabs of her finger in his chest. She stops as they are approached by a man in a suit, carrying a briefcase, the same man as the previous week seen entering the building.]
Angel Rotten: Can I help you? [The approaching man grins widely as he stops.] Man in suit: The question isn’t “If you can help me.” It’s “Can I help you?” [The grinning man nods his head at the confused Angel and Neal when the camera again is on the move, this time returning to the ring where “Can't Get the
Best of Me” by Cypress Hill is followed by champion Jake Morgan stepping out from the back.
To a chorus of boos, he makes his way to the ring, rolling inside. With the belt firmly over his shoulder, the heavily taped up Morgan, bandages on his forehead and his arm, strolls around the ring. After calling for a mic from
Howard Butz, he has to stop several times because of the loud booing and taunts.] Jake Morgan: Shut your mouths! Let YOUR champion speak! [Strolling the ring until the fans quiet, Morgan speaks.] Earlier tonight, my interview with Butz here was cut short ... now whether it’s David Knox trying to get back at me for winning this ...
[Morgan smiles as he strokes his belt as if it were a lover.] ... or that idiot, Mr. Scary Skullface ... I don’t care. The champion has something to say and now I’m going to speak my piece ... whether to want to hear it or not!
Since I stepped into HKW, everyone has wanted a piece of me. First, David Knox hired me to be the focal point of his new promotion, hoping to bring fans in with my name alone.
Guess what ... it worked. [Morgan walks around the ring before turning back to the fans and continuing.] Then Blue Light Special wanted to ride my coattails to stardom. After I taught them the futility of that, it was the State Club Boyz trying to make a name off of me.
[Stopping, Morgan grins at the fans.] Obviously that didn't work. Next up, Lars tried to cement his place in HKW by trying to make me over in “his image.” Guess what ... that didn't happen either. [He pauses for a breath.] Ghost, who hasn't even had a match yet, thinks that breaking into my locker room, peeping through my window and attacking me from behind will make him an instant hero. He's wrong. He doesn't realize it yet, but he'll learn the hard way ... just like the rest, that he’s just another bump in the road to me.
Now, we have the newcomer Tom Pierce. He wants to tag with me and make a run for the tag belts. Of course, all you idiots out there might think that’s a good idea, thinking that Ol’Jake doesn't exactly have a lot of friends.
[Jake levels an icy stare at the crowd.] I don't need friends! The State Club Boyz were friends, did it help them win the title? No ... You see, friends are a liability. One that I don't need but if Pierce wants to be my partner, then he needs to understand a few things.
First, I'm not looking for friends. Second, I'm not just a bad guy. I'm the worst guy you've ever met. I have no problem sacrificing anyone to save myself. If you get in my way, if you try and take what is mine, I'll put you in your grave ...
[Morgan stops with the rising cheer for State Club Boy, Curtis stepping out from the back, leaning heavily on one crutch, making his way to the ring, stopping on the outside.]
Curtis: That’s a lovely little speech there, Jake but I don’t ... these fans don’t want to hear it. What they want to know is when you’re going to put the strap on the line.
And ... we already know you’re too much of a pus<BLEEP> to put the title up against the number one contender, Tony ...
[The smiling Jake waves Curtis off.] Jake Morgan: Tony had his chance last week and look what happened. I broke him just like I broke you ...
Curtis: That’s right, Jake ... you broke me and you won the belt. Our champion is too afraid to put his title up against someone who he’s already
“broken”.
[Chants of “COWARD!” set Morgan off, bringing him to slam his fists down onto the ropes and pointing at the smiling Curtis.]
Jake Morgan: He wants a shot at my belt? Bring him and I’ll break him just like I broke you!
[The smiling Curits can only watch the livid Morgan point down at him from the ring when the lights go out, batheing the arena in total darkness. Long seconds pass before a voice is heard echoing out of the blackness.]
Voice: Let’s have some fun ... let’s dance! [There is a loud cracking sound before the lights flicker to life. The sight greeting the fans is the enigmatic Ghost holding a chair, standing over the downed body of Jake Morgan as the credits roll, bringing the show to a close.
