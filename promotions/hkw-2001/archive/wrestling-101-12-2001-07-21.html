Wrestling 101 — No. 12

21 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

[The camera opens on Skip Westervelt talking with his obese partner Chip, who is happily gnawing on a candy bar when they are approached by the pink clad
Lars and Raoul. Both men, ready for a rigorous workout in their leotards and matching legwarmers, eye the pair before them.]
Lars: Just the men we were looking for. Raoul: Oh, yes ... they'll do just fine, Larsy. Lars: Since this month is "Shape up or ship out" month, Raoul and I would like to extend our services to work Chip back into a svelte fashion of his former youth ...
Raoul: I like that "shape or ship out" navy motif, Lars ... [Staring off into space, Raoul gets a far-off look of days gone bye before snapping back to attention.]
Lars: We'll mold you into a lean, mean, fighting machine! [Starting to march off, Lars holds up a hand, stopping his partner then leaning in closer towards Skip.]
Looks like you could use some work, too. Two for one special. Come along, Raoul! [Skipping past, Raoul pats Westervelt on the tummy while Chip can only shrug his shoulders. Moving on, the camera focusses in on another part of the backstage area. With a worried look on his face, Neal the intern hurries down the hallway, peering over his shoulder from time to time to see if he is being followed. With his focus on what's going on behind him, Neal runs headlong into Jason Flint.
Grabbing Neal by the shoulders, a smiling Flint steadies the intern before letting him go.]
Jason Flint: Better slow down there before you hurt someone. Neal: Sorry man. If you see Lars ... you haven't seen me! [Neal rushes off as the smiling Flint gives him a thumbs up before turning around into the grinning Curtis.]
Curtis: Well, look who we have here. You know, Jason ... you're either the bravest guy in the world ... or the dumbest one.
Jason Flint: What? Curtis: In the old days, you'd surround yourself with an entire army before calling people out. My guess is that one too many big bumps has made you stupid.
[Shaking his head at the grinning Curtis leaning heavily on a crutch, Flint is taken down from behind by partners Tony and Ghost. Knocked to the floor, Flint is stomped into submission before they are pulled off by Curtis.]
Yup ... stupid. [As the trio walks off, Tony turns back on Flint.] Tony: All the way to the top. [After zooming in on Flint, the camera is once again on the move. Following on the heels of Texas Outlaw champion, Jake Morgan, his tag partner, Angel Rotten rolls her eyes as he reads her the riot act.]
Jake Morgan: I don't need a partner to win the belts. Angel Rotten: Yeah, yeah. I know. You've only told me a hundred times already. Jake Morgan: And another thing. You just stand back and let me work. Angel Rotten: Whatever. I thought you said you were doing the opening of the show? [Coming to their destination, Jake pushes open the door to his dressing room, stopping in surprise as he peers in.]
WHAT THE!?!?!? JAKE MORGAN WEIGHT LOSS CENTER!?!?!? [Looking into the room, the camera zooms in on a pink ribboned basket with video tapes, a leotard and pink leg warmers along with a big card as
“Debonaire” by Dope begins to play before they fade from view. Along with the pounding beat, faces flash by quickly accompanied by their names. Men and women of all sizes and shapes as well as all manner of dress.
Skip Westervelt and the obese Chip - the preppy State Club Boyz, Curtis and Tony - the enigmatic Ghost - an angry Angel Rotten - identically dressed in “Super K” smocks, the members of Blue Light Special, Harry Palmer and Dean “the Machine” McQueen - a returning Jason Flint - the pink clad pair known as Pretty in Pink, Lars and Backyard Billy - and finally, the HKW Texas Outlaw
Champion, Jake Morgan. As the last of the pictures fade, the logo of Hard Knocks Wrestling explodes into view before being swallowed up by inky blackness along with the ending of the noisy music.
Switching to the inside of the venue, the camera focusses in on announcer Howard Butz sitting ringside.] Howard Butz: Hello Texas ... [Taking a swing through the audience, the camera picks up the small but boisterious crowd with it’s signs, all trying to get the camera’s attention.]
... and welcome to Wrestling 101 coming to you from the School of Hard Knocks in Lewisville, Texas ... on KDFI, Channel 27!
[Butz’ voice is cut off and the camera makes another run through the crowd as a booming voice is heard over all the background sounds.]
Voiceover: You are watching KDFI, Channel 27 - Home of the Texas Rangers! [Sound is returned to normal on Butz.] Howard Butz: Big evening ... [An annoyed Butz is cut off as Morgan and Angel Rotten step out from the back. Composing himself, Butz shakes his head as the pair as they approach to a chorus of boos.] Welcome Jake ... [His words are cut short as Jake yanks the microphone out of his hands and Angel “shoos” him away.] Jake Morgan: You can sit down now Butz, your services are no longer required. [The fans boo as Jake laughs at the disgusted announcer.] “The best money can buy” that's the State Club Boyz slogan, isn't it? [Angel nods her head vigorously when Jake looks over at her.] The problem with that slogan is that money can't always buy the best. Do frills and ruffles make something the best? All the bells and whistles, don't either. If you want the best Mexican food in DFW, where should you go? Abuelo’s Mexican Embassy, On the Border, Chipolte’s or Don Pablo’s?
No, while those places are good, they are also a little expensive. No, if you want the best Mexican food, you go to a place like Herreras. It has excellent food and costs less. For Italian, do you go to the IL Sorrento
Restaurant, the Itallian Villa or Lombardi's Cafe? No, once again you go to a place like Bari's or the Cafe Paparazzi.
[He rubs a hand over his stomach and looks off into the rafters for a second then shakes his head as he turns to Angel. Covering the microphone, he whisper's something to her where she nods and heads for the back. Jake smiles and once again turns back to the crowd.]
The most expensive is not always the best, much like the State Club Boyz. Yeah, they have money. Yeah, they are spoiling that ghetto trash, Ghost with it but does that make them the best? Hell no, it doesn’t!
[He holds up his X Jr. Heavyweight Championship and runs the hand with the microphone over his Texas Outlaw title, before pulling it back to his mouth.]
These signify who the best is. Money didn't buy these. Hard work did. I busted my ass, bruised my body, twisted my ligaments, pulled my muscles and bled gallons of blood to get these. Now, the State Club Boyz and their buddy Ghost think they can walk in and stop me from claiming my other belts? That's not just dumb, it’s suicidal. Boyz, later on tonight Angel and I will be waiting right here in that ringt for you to come and get your asses kicked again. You had better bring some of your precious money with you, too. You'll need it to pay the Doctors.
[Taking a seat, Morgan smiles at the groaning Butz when the PiP’s Lars and Raoul, still in their workout clothing, skip out to the ring. After blowing kisses to the fans, Lars thows himself flat on his back and Raoul helps to pump his legs. By Raoul’s pelvic gyrations, he seems to be enjoying it just a little too much when Skip Westervelt and Chip march out from the back.
Grabbing a mic from Butz, Skip enters the ring.] Skip Westervelt: Lars, I have only one thing to say to you ... I AM NOT FAT! [Getting off the mat, Lars saunters up to the angry Skip.] Lars: ARE TOO! Skip Westervelt: AM NOT! Lars: ARE TOO! Skip Westervelt: AM NOT! [Using the mic, he bounces it off of Lars’ head before referee Orly Diaz calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Grabbing the reeling Lars, Skip lays in with several slaps to the chest befire knocking Lars down with a boot to the stomach then the head as he slides down the ringpost.
Howard Butz: I don’t know what just went on, Jake. Jake Morgan: We have a match you idiot. Pulling Lars to his feet, Skip hammers away with rights to the head out of a side headlock until the ref puts an end to that because of a closed fist. After cranking Lars’ neck, Skip is thrown off for the far ropes only to return with another side headlock. Again cranking down on his opponent’s neck, Skip has it all under control until Lars lifts him off his feet and falls backwards with a suplex.
Howard Butz: Lars with a suplex. That’s one way to work yourself out of a predicament, eh Jake?
The hard stare sends Butz back to the match with Lars whipping Skip into the corner before dancing in and slapping away at Skip’s stomach bringing the fans to laugh.
Angel returns with three white Jack-in-the-Box bags and a large Drink which she drops in front of Jake. Smiling, Jake rubs his hands together before he begins to pull food out of the bags ... first, a rice bowl with two egg rolls, followed by two tacos, stuffed jalapenos, an Ultimate Cheeseburger and a Super scoop of curly fries.
The last item he pulls out is a slice of Cheesecake which he sets off to the side. Without asking Butz if he would like any, Jake dives into the food.
Leaving Skip in the corner, Lars moves to the center of the ring where he starts doing jumping jacks then drops to the mat, doing obscene push-ups that have Raoul in a tizzy on the ring apron.
Howard Butz: What is he doing now? This is a wrestling match, not a workout video! Jake Morgan: Mmmmmmmmmphhhhhh ... Butz turns in disgust to his broadcast partner with a stuffed mouth, food everywhere. Howard Butz: Angel, your thoughts on tonight’s match against Tony and Ghost? Angel Rotten: It’s going to be a tough one for sure but I can guarantee, no one is going to beat us in this tournament. Not them and certainly not Pretty in Pink.
Back on his feet, Lars runs in place as Chip argues with the ref over the antics when Skip pulls himself to his feet. Back to back with Lars, Skip gets control, trying for a backslide. Fighting it, Lars rubs his backside against
Skip’s breaking the hold. Spinning around, Skip drives a boot into Lars’ midsection, doubling him over.
Howard Butz: Westervelt back against the ropes. ROLLING OVER THE TOP OF LARS! BACKSLIDE! NO! BACK TO BACK POWERBOMB! SKIP JUST PLANTED LARS INTO THE MAT! THERE’S A COVER! ONE! TWO! KICK OUT! Angel Rotten: That was a nice move. Jake Morgan: Mmmmmmmmmphhhhhh ... With Lars down, Skip gives him an earful about his weight before a boot stomping. Pulling Lars to his knees, Skip lays in with a fist then skillfully blocks Lars’ attempt at a low blow. Wagging a finger in Lars’ face, Skip pulls him to his feet with a smile on his face but it melts away as Lars shoots out his other hand, latching on with a testicular claw.
Howard Butz: Now, that looks painful and Lars of all people ... Leading the pained Skip around the ring, Lars blows out his cheeks and walks around like a fat man, mocking Skip and Chip.
Reaching across the table, Butz grabs at a french fry only to have his hand slapped away by the champion.
Howard Butz: OUCH! Pushing Skip into the corner, Lars again slaps away at his chest then moves lower to his stomach, leaving red welts with each blow. Charging into the ring,
Chip comes flying in. WIth a warning from Raoul, Lars dips out of the way at the last second. Skip Westervelt isn’t so lucky as his obese partner slams into him.
Realizing his mistake, Chip is led from the ring as Skip staggers out of the corner before falling flat on his face. Pulling Skip to his feet and bending him over, Lars steps behind him, thrusting his pelvis at his opponent’s backside before lifting him into the air and slamming him down with a pump handle slam that he does D-O-G-G-Y STYLE.
Blowing a kiss to the fans, Lars climbs the ropes before leaping off and landing a headbutt to Skip’s crotch. Hearing the impact, the ref turns to find Lars facedown, buried in Skip’s lap.
Howard Butz: THERE’S A COVER! ORLY DIAZ WITH THE COUNT! ONE! TWO! THREE! *** Ding *** Ding ***

— Part 2 of 2 —

[Rolling into the ring, Raoul pulls a dazed Lars off, leading him from the ring
with a victory and the fans cheering loudly as the camera breaks away for a commercial.] ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** M&M’s. You know which commercial ... the green naked M&M caught without her shell on. It’s been a long time since they pushed the green M&M as an aphrodisiac angle. Five bucks says they won’t push the cancer causing red dye
M&M’s anytime soon. BEER. It’s good. In fact, you should be drinking one right now. If you were, you might be able to get fine chicks like those in our commercials instead of sitting on your ass watching wrestling at one in the morning on a Saturday. So drink beer, because if you drink enough, anyone or anything can look really good. BEER. Remember, its cheaper than crack!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning from the break finds Backyard Billy hustling through the backstage area. Navigating through the busy hallway, he stops.]
Voice: BILLY? BILLY! [Spinning around, he smiles as he sees the owner of the voice before two shake hands and share a laugh.]
Backyard Billy: Trashman! How are you? Man, it’s been ages. Trashman: How the hell are you? It’s been a long time since our trampoline wrestling days. Backyard Billy: Gotta run but let’s get together after the show and I’ll introduce you to the guys!
Trashman: Deal. [After shaking, Billy runs off as does the camera, finding the owner of the X along with his bodyguard Brody and his former Junior Heavyweight champion,
Buddy Lee Jr. inside and office.] Newton: This is going to be even better than I thought. Brody: What’s that, boss? Newton: Morgan’s not going to make weight and in thirty days, my belt comes back to me. Brody: Ya think? Newton: I know. Buddy Lee Jr.: But Newton ... Newton: I just have to make sure he doesn’t. Buddy Lee Jr.: But Newton ... Newton: Why don’t you go round up a few of the boys, I have a plan ... Buddy Lee Jr.: But Newton ... Newton: WHAT?!?!? Buddy Lee Jr.: Don’t you want him to make weight so I can win the belt back fair and square?
[The camera fades on the head shaking Newton as it again runs into a commercial.] ***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** HKW Texas Outlaw Champion Jake Morgan is standing in the middle of the wrestling ring yelling and generally looking very unhappy. The champ is wearing blue jeans and boots without a shirt. Morgan's words have been left out so that an announcer can talk about the product.
Voice: Are you unhappy with the shape you’re in? Do you miss the days when you could see your toes? Are you no longer in fighting shape? If so, you need
Body Solutions. Burn inches while you sleep because we all know that you’re too damn lazy to get off your butt and work out. Body Solutions, THE diet plan of “heavyweight” champions!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** [Returning to the ring, the camera follows Jason Flint out from the back in his civvies. A loud cheer is waved off as Flint climbs into the ring with something to say.]
Jason Flint: Stunts ... ugly wrestling ... no shows ... people not happy with the return of this area’s most electrifying wrestler ...
In just a couple of weeks, we’ve seen nothing but distractions. Perhaps it’s time that we turn this place rightside up!
[Climbing up the ropes, Flint leads the fans in a cheer as Tony and Ghost hit the ring followed by Curtis. Pulling Flint off the ropes, he and Tony get into a shouting match which is quickly joined by Ghost.
Wanting no trouble, Flint takes a step back before winding up and laying Tony out with a big right hand. After blocking Flint, Ghost lashes out with his leg only to be countered and taken to the mat with a leg drag.
Bouncing off the ropes, Flint throws himself forward, hopping over Tony and landing with a somersault senton on top of the face-painted man. Rolling back to his feet, Flint is quickly to the top before coming off and landing a leg drop on Tony.
With Curtis on the ring apron, Flint points at him before grabbing him be the head and dropping to the canvas and pulling his target throatfirst down across the top rope. With his head snapping back, Curtis falls to the apron before rolling off to the floor.
Throwing himself back against the ropes, Flint was again on the move, this time slamming his feet into Tony’s face with a drop kick as the State Clubber tries to get back to his feet.
Dragging him across the ring and dropping him across the bottom ropes, Flint uses the ropes to springboard to the outside of the ring, dropping a guillotine legdrop across Tony’s throat.
Hooking the ropes with his arms, Flint rolls back into the ring over the top with little effort. Spinning around, he catches a leg to the midsection from
Ghost. Grabbing Flint by the hair, Ghost buries his face into the mat with a sitdown facebuster.
Barking in Flint’s face, Ghost doesn’t get a chance to do more as Jake Morgan hits the ring with a vengence. Clearing Ghost from the ring with a severe boot stomping, Morgan turns on Flint.
With a grin on his face, the champ shows a gentler side as he helps Flint to his feet. Patting him on the shoulder, Morgan sends him off on his merry way
... nah, just had you going there for a minute. The bully scoops Flint up onto his shoulder out of a fireman’s carry then depositing him on the canvas with a fallaway slam. Now, that’s more like it.
Having cleared the ring, Morgan basks in the fusillade of boos from the audience until being attacked from behind by Tony. With Morgan having his head rammed repeatedly into the top turnbuckle, the ref calls for the bell.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Pulling Morgan from the corner and snap maring him over, Tony was open to slam a knee into the back of his foe’s head. A tag brought in Ghost who went right to work with a drop kick to the seated champ’s head.
After a double elbow drop, Tony was led from the ring by the ref. A scoop bodyslam later, Tony was flying off the middle ropes with a knee drop.
Howard Butz: Ghost and Tony taking it to the champion from the opening bell. There’s a cover! ONE! TWO! NO! MORGAN WITH A SHOULDER UP! Sending Morgan into the far ropes, Ghost blasts him with spinning jump kick before making a tag to Tony.
Howard Butz: Tony now in with a slingshot leg drop. A cover. ONE! Jake with a kick out. Watching Tony’s rear chinlock closely, the ref asks Morgan to submit, getting only a shaking head instead. Powering to his feet, the champion struck with a couple of back elbows, working himself free then hitting the ropes.
Howard Butz: Morgan with the clothesline. Tony ducking under. There’s a waistlock. BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX! TONY WITH THE COVER! ONE! TWO! THR ... NO!
MORGAN ESCAPING! Wobbling badly, Morgan is whipped across the ring. Getting to the far side, his partner reaches out and slaps him on the shoulder for the tag. After Morgan takes a big spill on the return with a boot to the face, Angel Rotten launches herself into action with a missile drop kick.
Howard Butz: Angel now in and there’s a forearm to Ghost on the outside. Tony on his feet. Irish whip, into a drop toe hold.
With Tony holding his face in pain, Angel puts the boots to him then drops an elbow before making a cover for a two count. After arguing with the ref, Angel cinches Tony up for a suplex but is countered and snapped over. Making a tag to
Ghost, the pair roll her over, grabbing an ankle and lift it into the air before slamming it down into the mat.
Howard Butz: Angel lifted into the air ... what are they doing? DOUBLE FRONT SUPLEX! With an admonishing from the ref, Flint leaves the ring and Angel Rotten to Ghost. Pushing her up against the ropes, Ghost sends her sprawling to the floor with a hammering clothesline. With his opponent crumpled on the floor below,
Ghost taunts an angry Jake Morgan. Howard Butz: Ghost heading to the outside to punish Angel Rotten! A knee lift slams her back against the ring barrier when Orly Diaz warns Ghost back, allowing Morgan to move in to help his partner. A bit of a stand-off occurs between Ghost and Morgan until Tony blindsides the champ with a double axe handle from behind.
With both Morgan and Rotten down, Ghost and Tony stomp them before the ref’s count forces Ghost to bring it back into the ring.
Howard Butz: Rolling Angel back into the ring. This match as been all Tony and Ghost from the start. No love lost here with the prize not only being a win but the right to move on to the tag team tournament finals against the Pretty in
Pink boys. It goes from bad to worse with a piledriver, leaving Angel writhing on the mat. Making a tag to his partner, Ghost is knocked to the canvas by a charging Morgan. Howard Butz: MORGAN’S SEEN ENOUGH! A big clothesline knocks Tony from the apron, bringing the ref in with a warning. Shoving Diaz back, Morgan sends Ghost across the ring, taking him down on the return with a boot to the face. After knocking him down, Morgan is pulled from the ring by Tony on the outside.
Howard Butz: FOLKS, WE HAVE TO BREAK FOR A COMMERCIAL! DO NOT GO AWAY! *** COMMERCIAL BREAK *** A handsome young man standing inside a wrestling ring. The well built and shirtless man smiles as his name scrolls across the bottom of the screen.
Backyard Billy: So, you want to be a wrestler? You want to be just like the guys you see right here every week? You want to become the next State Club Boy? Maybe you want to be like me or perhaps you want to be like the champion, Jake Morgan?
Then you need to call the School of Hard Knocks because to be the best, you have to be trained by the best! Call and begin your education today!
A phone number flashes across the screen as scenes of potential wrestlers working out in a ring plays.
Taco Bell is now open later. So, get your fat ass off the couch and come get fatter! Yo quiero Gordo Americanos!
***COMMERCIAL BREAK*** On the outside of the ring, Morgan is entangled with both Tony and Curtis of the State Club Boyz. Being held, Morgan takes the butt of the crutch to the stomach repeatedly until knocked to his knees. At Curtis’ direction, Tony slams Morgan into the ring barrier before picking him up and dropping him down across it.
After slumping to the ground, he takes a couple of stomps and a tongue wagging while inside the ring, Angel is dealt more punishment, taking an over the top
belly-to-belly suplex. Howard Butz: Orly Diaz with his hands full now as Ghost joins the Jake bashing party. Pulled to his feet, Morgan is sent directly at a waiting Ghost who puts him down with a diving shoulder butt. Morgan takes several punches to the head beofre striking out and low blowing the attacking Ghost. A lunging Morgan takes
Tony down as well with a clothesline, leaving only Curtis to fend for himself. Turning to run off, Curtis is confronted by a grimacing Angel Rotten. Ready to flee, a hopping Curtis takes a punch from Angel, spinning around into a spear from Morgan.
Howard Butz: Morgan’s on the ring apron ... waiting ... waiting ... SUICIDE DIVE! SUICIDE DIVE! MORGAN HAS TAKEN OUT TONY AND GHOST WITH ONE SHOT! After arguing with the ref, Morgan and Angel return to business, taking the war to their adversaries. With Angel using her boots on Tony, Morgan whips Ghost hard into the ring barrier then planting him into the hard floor.
Howard Butz: MORGAN WITH THE DDT! DID YOU HEAR THE IMPACT?!?!?! Together, they lift Tony into the air, swinging him around and dropping him backfirst onto the floor. Grabbing Curtis and half dragging him along, Morgan heads directly for Butz’ table.
Howard Butz: WAIT! WHAT’S GOING ON? NO, NOT THE ... Diving for cover, Butz heads for safer ground as Morgan’s chokeslam splinters the table, leaving Curtis in a heap. With Ghost trying to return to his feet,
Morgan and Rotten come back looking for more. Morgan’s snap suplex is followed by Angel’s leg drop when the bell is rung.
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: WAS THAT THE BELL?!?!? WHAT’S GOING ON? [Ignoring the bell, Morgan and Rotten continue to assault Tony on the outside of the ring as the bell sounds again.]
*** Ding *** Ding *** Howard Butz: THERE IT IS AGAIN! REFEREE ORLY DIAZ HAS CALLED FOR THE BELL! IT’S ... IT’S A DOUBLE COUNT OUT! WE’VE GOT NEW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS! WE’VE GOT NEW CHAMPIONS! [The credits roll as Morgan shoves the ref to the ground.] *** Hard Knocks Wrestling©2001 ***
