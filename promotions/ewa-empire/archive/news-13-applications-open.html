If you're new to the site and are looking for a fed to join then join the site, click your activation e-mail, go to the forums and sign up. Plenty of oppertunities for new blood starting out, and with EWA you get more than a fed, the active members make up a small, but thriving community. The chat is nearly always active, the OOC boards are there for your enjoyment and our backstage forum is there to RP just for the hell of it. We have alot to offer with the INternational Championship, World Heavyweight Championship, Tag Team Belts and the innovative UnderGround title, our 24/7 title which carries special OOC rules all of its own. If all you're looking for is a good story and more control over your character then we offer that too. Play a part in your match writeup, write and send in segments for the very detailed results and be gaurenteed that your role play will be read and write ups catered around them.
