CB Upset?

27 July 2011

Crimson Blaze

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following the EWA World Title Match, sources from within Crimson Blaze's camp has been telling EWA officials that Crimson is rather unhappy with the circumstances regarding the EWA World Title Match. He has issued this statement: "I've been given shot after shot for World Titles, and no matter how hard I strive to be the better man, I can't get the job done, and for that matter, where in the hell was El Pablo at?!" After that statement, Crimson has reassured EWA officials that he'll still wrestle regardless as he takes on "The Pope" Marcellus Payne with EWA World Champion Justin Marsham at ringside.
