SCW (Southwestern, 2019) — Rampage 12 Schedule

Rampage 12 Schedule

11 February 2020
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

From The Lloyd Noble Center in Oklahoma City, OK

February 11, 2020

Turner & Hooch -VS- Thunder & Lightening

NOTES: In a tag team debut, Michael Turner and Isaac "Hooch" Johnson make their entrance into SCW's tag team division with a battle against Thunder & Lightening! Can the journeyman duo turn enough heads to show they belong in the deepest tag division in the wrestling world?

[TV Title Shot on the line]

El Principe' -VS- "The Law" John Henry Jones

NOTES: John Henry Jones impressed everybody with his debut victory last Rampage and he gets a step up in competition with the technically-gifted El Principe! Principe has been angry at being relegated to dark matches and nothing of substance and this show he gets his wish! On top of that, the winner will be named as the first challenger for whomever wins the TV Title in the main event on Rampage 12! This one will be interesting!

The Rich Family -VS- The Law

NOTES: The Texas legacy rolls on as the Rich Family will represent the lonestar state and take to the ring against Brady and Strauss, The Law! These two large men have been throwing their weight around for weeks, but can Texas guile end the insanity?

[#1 contender on the line]

Son of Malta/Gordon Gaines -VS- American Revival

NOTES: For the American Revival, this is a step they have been calling for ove rthe past month or two. They have felt like the deserved it and even called SCW out for not giving a shot to them. On the flip side is SOn of Malta and Gordon Gaines. Malta is outspoken that people who demand things are not to be emulated, while Gaines has also been outspoken as desiring a shot. It happens here as one of these teams will get the Bloody Sunday Lonestar Tag Title shot and the champions are scheduled to be at ringside for commentary!

Alex Reyn -VS- Gemini

NOTES: Alex Reyn's contract status may be a mystery to most, but he is listed for a match here on Rampage against Gemini. Reyn's disdain may cause him to be angry and destructive, but can Gemini find the answer where others could not?

Masked Bros -VS- Health Fanatics

NOTES: The Masked Bros continue their trek back up the tag team rankings as they get a match against Somner and Matthews, the Health Fanatics! Can the Bros get the victory and will Sweaty Bling be around to cause them any distractions? It will take full focus to get a victory over the fittest men in tag team wrestling.

[MAIN EVENT!]

[6-MAN GAUNTLET!]

[SCW TELEVISION TITLE MATCH!!!]

CK Beretta -VS- Windham Brody -VS- Flanagan -VS- Kid Chameleon -VS- "Big Kid" Chris Smith -VS- MYSTERY OPPONENT

NOTES: The SCW finally gets a second singles title as the TV Title will be crowned in a 6-pack challenge! Nobody knows the entrance order before the match and two men start off and go at it. Winner stays and the next guy comes in until the fifth and final match crowns our first TV Champion. On top of that one of the spots will be filled by a debuting mystery wrestler! This one will be fun to watch!

PLUS! The new champ, Caleb Foley, holds court. And we hope to hear more about the last minute meeting between Chris Hopper and the unknown party as it relates to the upcoming showdown at Bloody Sunday. And we will put the new injunction on SAGAWA to the test as both The Kochi Cannibal amd Johnny Psychotic will be in action.

*Card subject to change
