A Dallas-set federation, its own origin story built on the closure of the real Sportatorium, that ran a biweekly televised Rampage from June 2019 and settled its Lonestar Heavyweight and Lonestar Tag Team titles across two tournaments, its holdings running to a February 2020 card with no further update after. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Southwestern Championship Wrestling opened its own account of itself with the closure of a real building: its About page grounds the federation in the hole left by the Sportatorium's demolition, and gives it a founder, “little Mikey” Rankin, whose two childhood friends grew up to wrestle as “Filthy” Frank Rich and Terry “Stinkin” Rich while he went to SMU and ran the family oil business instead.

Its own booking committee described the federation, in its own FAQ, as a deliberate hybrid between roleplay federations and angle federations — a biweekly televised Rampage written in present-tense play-by-play from member submissions, with staff-booked angles running alongside it and dedicated supercards, Showdown and Bloody Sunday, outside the regular schedule.

The federation opened its Lonestar Tag Team Title on its first show, 18 June 2019, on Las Preguntas, and its Lonestar Heavyweight Title by tournament at its first supercard, Showdown I, on 8 December 2019, won by Pat Gordon, Jr. Both belts changed hands once more — the tag titles the same night at Showdown I, the heavyweight title on the following Rampage, 28 January 2020, when “Celtic Crippler” Caleb Foley won a three-way match for it.

A December 2019 news post announced a first national television deal and a Television Title on the way; a schedule survives for a Rampage card on 11 February 2020, meant to crown that title. No later content survives anywhere in the held corpus, and no closure statement of its own does either.
