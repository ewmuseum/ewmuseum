We all get into it initially for a little fun, to flex that creative muscle in an easy, safe, non-exerting, cozy easy chair environment. We get a few kicks, working our way through a fed or two, honing our style and making some friends, who knows, maybe even winning a match or two. Then somewhere along the line your e-wrestling career grows teeth and hair, things get a little too serious, winning becomes a matter of life and death, you neglect sleep, work, social activities and any semblance of grooming, looking for a little more time to roleplay, you start suspecting conspiracies and couruption around every corner, insults are exchanged, feelings are hurt, flame wars and shoots, personal attacks, punches thrown, the cops are called, the riot gear is distributed, tear gas, rubber bullets, a violent coup, economic devestation and the next thing you know you end up in a Bulgarian prison rooming with a half a dozen militant Ahmish raspberry farmers with head lice and the plague... Atleast that's how I remember my time in the "serious" world of e-wrestling, I'm assuming that's pretty standard. So where do you turn then? Parody e-Wrestling! That's how MCW originally came into existance in the fall of '96. The world of "serious" e-wrestling had gotten too full of itself, too competative and no longer about having FUN, what we got into this crazy mixed up bag for in the first place. Parody e-Wrestling, where just about anything goes as long as it's funny and doesn't hurt anyone directly involved with the federation itself. But even Parody e-Wrestling has it's limits, sure, MCW accieved some level of success. We made people laugh, we made people cry, we made people wet themselves to the delight of those who derive joy from the suffering of others, but after a while even parody e-wrestling becomes old hat. So what next then? Wild, unsettling, un-natural experiments in concept and gimmickry. And that's where we stand now. A new idea. Something different. Will it be good? Hell if I know, that's up to us, you the potential participant and we the alleged "folks in charge". All I know for sure is that it's going to be different, and that's all I can promiss. Below we'll try to explain the fed's concept and introduce you to how we'll be doing things. We hope it sounds as intriguing to you as it did to us :) =============================== Concept Back Story/Introduction =============================== The year is 2030, Mentally Challenged Wrestling has managed to survive for the better part of the last 34 years, passing from one ownership group to the next, some more successfull than others, but more or less wallowing in sub-obscurity, that is, untill six years ago, when Bill Gates bought the Unites States Constitution from the American government and wiped his ass with it live on National TV to the largest television audience in the history of the medium. After which, among other things, the CEO of the United States issued new cranial ratings tracking implants to every man, woman, and child across the country to more accurately track television ratings. The next week to the shock and supprise of a nation, the new ratings report showed that MCW's 3 am Tuesday morning program was being watched by aproximately 47 million people! A show due to be canceled the next week, with a two month ratings average of 0.0 in the old rating ssystem was apparently viewed by legions of fans who the Neilsen people had never even spoken to... The day after the release of the ratings a bidding war began between Monstro Collositech and Globo Mecka Co Unlimited for the show's rights. The bidding wars ended abruptly though when shadowy professional corporate raider Gregory St. Vague realized he owned both companies and merged them to become Omnicorp Industries... Since then MCW has dominated the entertainment industry, consistantly filling stadiums and playing to television audiences of millions. Omnicorp still maintains control of the promotion through a confusing twisted incomprehendable tangle of subsidiaries and an out of touch board of directors. The several million executives of Omnicorp, never being sure which of them actually outrank the others, call each other sir... =================== How to get involved =================== Parody e-Wrestling's nothing new, heck, we ourselves started back in '96 and along the way I've seen nearly a dozen other parody feds come and go. The concept for parody e-wrestling is fairly simple, while in "serious" feds they go for dark or edgy or hardcore, we go for funny. Our one reason for being is to have a little fun and make each other laugh, check your ego at the door and grab a clown hammer. MCW's not too hard to understand really. Just think of it as the Harlem Globetrotters meet the original cast of SNL meet the Simpsons, Space Ghost, and Joel Hodgson in a small town at midnight with the intention of being the last one standing inside the town line. A total careening Mitsubishi station wagon full of the screaming nuns of humor. As for the kind of characters we're looking for, well, we don't exactly look for the kind of superstars that fill the rosters of those other feds, we instead look for the people that are just too, well, odd to get a chance there. Basically, we're looking for the most unique, original and or entertaining freaks you can imagine. Everyone's got ideas along these lines, wrestlers that just are too entertaining for the mainstream feds, well, this is the place for 'em. The addition to this is our new concept. Seeing as this fed takes place in the future, 30 years in the future, it opens up a whole new genre of characters, over the hill current characters, or a next generation of parody creations! And this isn't limited to just old MCW handlers, but handlers from all corners of the parody universe and elsewhere. So, a competely new character idea, an old character aged to perfection or the decendant of an old star, anything goes, be original, be creative and most importantly have fun with it. The more fun you have, the more fun the rest of us'll have. Some of you though may need a little help with what exactly constitutes a funny character? Basically a character that is a) novel, b) has a light- hearted intent, and c) is semi-coherent works. Men, women, kids, dogs, goats, inanimate objects it's all been done. An example of a good one and a bad one is below. Good character: "Bass Master" Bubba Leon Lockhart (One of my original characters, basically a drinking, womanizing, scarily Austin-ish (even though he was created when Austin was still the Ringmaster) professional bass fisherman from the strange town of Parts Unknown, Oklahoma.) Novelty: Check, a wrestling fisherman is quite original, especially as a champion in the fast-paced world of pro bass fishing, which is full of opportunities for parody, as well as the whole "redneck Casanova" thing, which although not original, can work very well. Light-hearted intent: He doesn't attempt to set some kind of message, he's just there getting into funny situations, basically. Coherence: Well, he has a solid personality, that of egotistical (somewhat) superstar, which stays true. Doesn't try to make fun of too much at once. Bad character: (I did this one quite well, but she was still flawed from the start. ) Olga Piddledonna (One of my characters from 1997, who got so impossible to keep that I deliberately screwed her up to get rid of her, honestly) Novelty: She's an attractive, eligible, odd female . . . She's common as muck, that's it . . . She's basically a parody of '97 WWF slut-de-jour Sunny (Bound to get dated quickly) . . . Snorts the same magic dust Hulk Hogan used in that horrid Uncensored cage match (Also dated, and junkies aren't really funny) Light-hearted intention: She was created as a dis on someone's actual drug problem, for crying out loud, and the character got somewhat mean-spirited later on. Coherence: She changed so many times it wasn't funny, from librarian, to the nice chick who helps the blind guy, to junkie, and to slut-junkie. One-note gags can actually be better than really broad stuff here, as long as they're not too repetitive. There are some fairly obvious characters out there that just don't work more often than not and I'm sure you know what should be attempted and what shouldn't. Though while some things are somewhat taboo, if you think you can pull them off and make them funny without alienating or offending too many people, feel free to give it a try. Just about anything and everything else is fair game, just like the Boca Rio Hunting Preserve on Endangered Species Carnage Night. ============================== Rules, Shows, Titles and Staff ============================== Roleplays - I come from the good old LOOP, and I'm still kind of partial to doing things the old fashion way, meaning there's really really no roleplay limit to speak of. I allways thought RP limits were really, well, limiting. It's hard to get the kind of entertaining exchanges worked up that are neccessary for a place like this if you limit someone to 1 roleplay a week. That being said, there's really no minimum either. I'd like to see something somewhat weekly from everyone, but I know that can't be done most of the time and I understand that, I'm just hoping to get as much as I can from everyone. Matches - You want a match? Challenge someone. Ask for an open contract. Accept a challenge. Fill an open contract. Those are the easiest ways to not only get matches, but fueds and storys going. Again, this is a fairly old school approach to doing things, but I've allways thought that this is more interesting than just setting up matches myself. Though, if challenges aren't made I guess I'll have to fill the cards myself. I like to stick to my old ways of doing things, but I've also discovered that you can't just stick to one way of thinking and expect everyone to do it that way or else. I'd rather not assign too many matches (Other than say PPV matches or something special.) but I will if I have to. Strategies - Strats for short. If you want something special to happen in a match that you don't think we'll already come up with you can send us a strat detailing what you'd like to see happen in any given match, preferably one you have atleast something to do with... Be as descriptive or as vague as you'd like. There will be a deadline for these, more than likely set for no later than two or three days before the cards. Shows - Our main card, Tuesday Night SKREE!!!, part of OmniCorp Television's Mandatory Viewing Tuesday will often be held in OmniCorp's new Omni Plaza Huge-normous-giganti-don-grotesque-a Dome. What is a Skree you ask? Well, the elders say it is the sound one makes at the sight of an oncoming stampeed of elephant riding pandas. But the elders are old and feeble, we pushed them down and mocked thier foolishness. "Ha, HA!" we said. The secondary card, MCW Funtastical Excitainment will mainly feature the squashes of the week, where the funniest get to pad their records with victories over our jabrones. And yes, we probably will be late on occassion, but likely not that much. Titles - Every promotion needs shiney objects to fight over, and we're no exception! MCW Inter-Global Multi-Dimensional Champion of all Existance The main title, the championship all the top drawer talent will be fighting for. Can you reach the prize? Can you win the right to be called the Inter-Global Multi-Dimensional Champion of all Existance!? MCW East Carolina Championship The secondary title, it may seem like a substantial step down from the champion of all existance, but do not under estimate the importance of being the champion of all of East Carolina! The Omnicorp Vague Award for Outstanding Excellence in Teamwork Our tag team trophy. A shiney plaque bearing the visage of the all powerfull, all filthily rich Gregory St. Vague proclaiming you fine examples of employees and acknowledging your outstanding excellence in teamwork. Staff - At the moment the only staff member important enough to warrent a place in this fine document, our fearless leader... Omnicorp President and CEO Gregory St. Vague - The head of the beast. Obscenely wealthy ruler of the Omnicorp kingdom Gregory St. Vague. Worldly, cultured and refined he wields an accent so vaguely foriegn it's impossible to pin down. But rest assured, you'll be introduced to the rest of the colorfull cast of characters soon enough.
