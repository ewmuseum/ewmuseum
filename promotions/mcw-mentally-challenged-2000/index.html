Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A November 2000 FAQ announced a ninth version of a long-running comedic promotion, describing itself as returning to the fold after its original run began in the fall of 1996, and resetting its own concept thirty years into an imagined future.

In that future, the promotion is the property of a media conglomerate run by a single self-satisfied chief executive, broadcasting a flagship program from a domed arena built inside a repurposed national monument, and defending an oversized championship, a regional second title, and a tag team trophy named for its own owner.

The revival's own first broadcast paired a philanthropist commentator with a screeching owl at the announce desk, introduced a broken-English second-generation wrestler avenging his father's own unfulfilled championship chase, and cut to a transatlantic vignette in which a visiting tag team's valet suffered a nosebleed dramatic enough to summon a costumed emergency service.

The broadcast closed by recruiting two fantasy-camp instructors into the promotion's own ranks as a fresh tag team, sending them off to the federation's studio to begin a run under new ring names.
