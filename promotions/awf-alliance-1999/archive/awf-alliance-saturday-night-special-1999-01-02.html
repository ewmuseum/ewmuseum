AWF (Alliance, 1999) — News

AWF “Saturday Night Special!”

1 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The ALLIANCE WRESTLING FEDERATION rings in the New Year with Saturday Night Special, January 2nd from Arco Arena in Sacramento, California! The fur is gonna fly when World Unified champion "MR. ARMAGEDDON" CHRIS
STEELE defends his title against TOM "DA BOMB" DOMI in a battle between countrymen! Domi has climbed to the top of the ladder in the lightweight ranks, but can he pull off the upset of the year and take heavyweight gold?
Tune in to find out! The AWF will push the envelope of decency once again, with a little help from GIGGLY AND YUMMY! The World Tag Team champions have accepted a challenge from former champions THE HARDCORE CANADIANS for a "Striptease"
match! If the Canadians can regain the titles, CANDY ASS, G.A.Y.'s valet, must strip nude in front of the crowd! But if G.A.Y. gets the victory, THE
CRAZY CANUCK has to shake his money maker in front of 20,000 wrestling fans!
This match will, rest assured, stick in the mind of fans for years to come! "RUTHLESS" RAY WASHINGTON has sobered up, and is looking to make it back to the top of the AWF! Standing in his way first is the extremely dangerous
"CRIPPLER" SEAN DAVIS! Can Washington come out on top, or will Davis'
ever-present Louisville Slugger claim yet another victim? We'll find out this Saturday night!
COMMISSIONER LOLLIE has sworn that he holds no grudges against SHANE PHILLIPS, but can our "honorable" leader be trusted? And will that make any difference as to CHRIS GORDON's quest to end Phillips' career? Questions will be answered when Phillips takes on the 400 pound BUBBA THE LUV SPONGE, and Gordon goes one-on-one with the seven-foot tall "BIG GRUDGE" DAN KARMA!
New World Television champion MATTHEW BULLOCK not only has DOOM breathing down his neck, but he also has three hungry superstars looking to snatch away his gold! QUADRO, THUNDERSTRIKE KID, and "GOLDEN BEAR" DAVE WALKER meet in a Three Way Dance, with the winner getting a shot at Bullock's belt that night!
"SUICIDE BLONDE" SEAN CHRISTOPHER has been both lucky and good when it comes to the World Light Heavyweight title so far, but the champ meets his toughest challenge to date when he defends the belt against EVAN C.
MICHAELS! Also, JIM ROOSTER is back in the AWF, and he's made it clear that he wants the Lightweight belt, by any means necessary! Can ECM become the champ? Will Rooster spoil the show? We'll find out at SNS!
SILVERLUSH has the entire world hanging on his (?) every word. What antics does wrestling's original cross-dresser have planned for "MR. GASWORKS"
MARVIN HARRIS this week? the Lusher and the Gasman face-off yet again at SNS! THE ENFORCER and THE EXECUTIONER will try to earn some respect when they team with newcomer JAMES ALEXANDER to meet THE MASKED INTERNS and SEAN FOX.
Alexander has, so far, been the only wrestler to express any interest in joining The Enforcement. Will the trio go on to victory together, or is a double-cross in the works? Do Enforcer and Executioner even want James in their stable? Tune in to find out!
TEMPORARY INSANITY, ADAM PITT, and more are also on deck this Saturday night! Lock the doors and hide the good china, it's SATURDAY NIGHT SPECIAL!
