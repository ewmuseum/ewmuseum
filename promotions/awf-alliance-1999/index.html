A GeoCities-hosted federation whose New Year's card built its title picture around a Unified World championship defense and a stakes-raising tag team match. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

By its own account, the Alliance Wrestling Federation returned in late January 1999 after “nearly four months away,” placing its original opening sometime in 1998, run out of http://www.geocities.com/~brainbuster-x/awf.

Its surviving card preview, Saturday Night Special (2 January 1999, Arco Arena, Sacramento), built its night around a Unified World Championship defense by “Mr. Armageddon” Chris Steele against fellow countryman Tom “Da Bomb” Domi, and a stakes-raising tag match pitting World Tag Team Champions Giggly and Yummy against former champions the Hardcore Canadians, each side wagering a valet or member on a striptease before the crowd depending on the result.

The rest of the card carried a Three Way Dance between Quadro, Thunderstrike Kid, and “Golden Bear” Dave Walker to earn a same-night Television Championship shot against Matthew Bullock, a World Light Heavyweight defense by “Suicide Blonde” Sean Christopher against Evan C. Michaels, and a newly returned Jim Rooster declaring for the Lightweight belt “by any means necessary.”

By its own account, a further update that January found it still active; nothing dated later survives.
