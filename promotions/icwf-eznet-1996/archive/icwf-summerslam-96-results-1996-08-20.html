"ICWF Summerslam '96 Results"

20 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Results of ICWF SummerSlam '96: Falls-Count-Anywhere Match: Mr. Perfect pinned Sid after hitting him with a hammer in 0:08:27. Special Six-Man-Tag Match: The Midnight Express and Nikita Koloff beat The Hollywood Blondes and Arn Anderson when Lane pinned Pillman after the Midnight Drop in 0:13:24. The Hollywood Blondes beat Demolition when Pillman made Ax submit to an abdominal stretch in 0:07:48. Demolition Ax suffered an elbow injury. He will be out for approximately 2 cards. Dr. Death pinned Steve Austin with the Oklahoma Stampede in 0:07:12. Jake Roberts pinned Brian Pillman with the DDT after interference from Roddy Piper in 0:03:29. Roddy Piper made Sting submit to the Sleeper in 0:13:37. The Steiner Brothers beat The Dream Team when R. Steiner defeated Valentine by disqualification after interference by Ted Dibiase in 0:04:44. Loser-Leaves-Town Match: Crush pinned Hulk Hogan after an inside cradle in 0:14:07. Virgil pinned Michael Hayes with the Russian Legsweep in 0:07:24. The British Bulldogs beat Dr. Death and Diesel when D.B. Smith pinned Death after a sunset flip in 0:23:10. The Undertaker pinned The Ultimate Warrior after a waistlock suplex in 0:14:27. Vader pinned Gary Albright with the Power Bomb in 0:13:56. The Midnight Express beat Harlem Heat when Lane pinned Booker T after the Midnight Drop in 0:06:37. (The Midnight Express retained the ICWF US Tag Titles) Dan Severn and Dean Malenko beat The Hart Brothers when Severn defeated O. Hart when the referee stopped the match after a submission in 0:14:30. (Dan Severn and Dean Malenko retained the ICWF World Tag Titles) Bret Hart pinned Sabu after an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle in 0:06:01. (Bret Hart won the ICWF US Title) Diesel defeated Dan Severn by disqualification in 0:02:25. (Dan Severn retained the ICWF IC Title) I-Quit-Match: Dean Malenko defeated Terry Gordy when the referee stopped the match after a submission from the Texas Colverleaf in 0:22:07. (Dean Malenko retained the ICWF World Title) Special Six-Man-Tag Match: Demolition and Ahmed Johnson beat The Midnight Express and Nikita Koloff when Ax pinned Lane after the Demolition Decapitation with help from Oleg Taktarov after he beat up Nikita Koloff in 0:14:59. __________________ 1.
