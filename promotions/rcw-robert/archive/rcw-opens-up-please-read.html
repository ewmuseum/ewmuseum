RCW Opens Up, Please Read!

13 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello Email wrestling fans and superstars this is [name withheld] of The RCW. It is a fast growing Federation. I have been running the old RWF (Robert Wrestling Federation) for a year in September and it wasn't all that good. So I shut it down and since July has re-opened with a new name, RCW. I have about 30 wrestlers and I am looking for more. Now the RCW is a simple roleplay based league. Your roleplays have to be 20 lines or more, but they have to make some sense. Also we have have 2 shows a week, we have a live card show Saturday Night War and a talk show with special guests on Wensday and we have Pay-Per-Views every Month. Now that, that is out of the way lets get to bussiness. Now i know your probably wondering "Hey [name withheld] I've heard it before, this league is probably the same as the fifty I am in", well my friends your all wrong. This Federation is totally different, for one thing we have NO simulators, we keep track by the quality and style of your roleplaying. And I bet your also saying right now "This federation hasn't proved to be different", well I have already beaten you to it, I have some old cards, so if you want a sample match simply IM me (I am always online) or email me (if I am not) at [detail withheld] . I am working day by day with the RCW officials trying to increase attention and compacity. Now the rules are simple, in order to win you have to roleplay. And we at RCW take no REAL wrestlers name. No Ahmed Johnsons, or Stings. We have made up names,so if you want a to wrestle in the RCW you have to be willing to have a made up name (For example: Eric Cooper, JB Dangerous), do not use thoughs names they are just examples. Oh and if you don't know what a gimmick is, this isn't the league for you. Below is the application to join, it is very simple and not hard. It will give you help on how to build your wrestler.
