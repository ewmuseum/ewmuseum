I'm too lazy to post the card in 232324 thousand segments :) So, here't the link. _ALL_ feedback, is welcomed. http://www.efeduniverse.com/avw/bgb01.txt Bluegrass Bash 2001 Memorial Colliseum in Lexington, KY March 18, 2001 Commentators: Larry Morton, Christy Starks, Steve Warner _MAIN EVENT TWO_ _BLUEGRASS TITLE MATCH: RAGE IN THA' CAGE_ "New Age Icon" Chris Collins vs "Mr. Flawless" Jake Davids - These two guys fought in a blood soaked, viscious match in the finals of the AVW Bluegrass Title tourney, only for the ending to be decided by a Debacle 5-Star Chokeslam. AVW Co-President Sean White, came to the ring and stripped the title from David's, and announced the match was to be voided from the record books, and that these two men will fight in a Cage Match, and that NO ONE will interfere. Davids and Debacle got pissed, and left Collins a beaten, bloody mess. This one won't be for the squeamish. _MAIN EVENT ONE_ _SWEET REDEMPTION_ Max Pain vs Revelations - Revelations came out of nowhere, and put Max Pain not only out of the Bluegrass Title Finals, but also, out of the AVW. Pain however, fighting fire with fire, used a little blackmail scheme of his own to get his job back. Now that he's officially back with the company, his prime goal, is to extract revenge on Revelations. A long time friend for years, who betrayed him. Revelations, seems to have one goal, and that is to take out everyone and anyone to get what he wants. What does he want? That is as yet, unclear. _TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH_ Board of Education (c) vs Southern Tradition - Board of Education is riding a wave of success. To quote a source "They're the hottest thing to hit wrestling in sometime". However, we'll see if they can continue that hot streak, as they defend their titles against two men who are defending their heritage, and their roots. BOE has constantly attacked Diamonds and Dunn's heritage, and have squeaked out victories and stayed out of harms way for far too long in the fans opinion. When March 18, 2001 is here.. we'll finally see the showdown we've been waiting for. _APPALACHIAN TITLE #1 CONTENDERS MATCH_ Seth "The Gambit" Alexander vs "Pimpaliscious" Leevon Parks - Seth "The Gambit" Alexander, who many critics had pegged as a lame duck in AVW momentarily, came to life at Appalachian Extreme on Feb. 26, 2001 when he showed a mean streak we haven't saw in him since his arrival on the business. Tired of waiting, and hell bent on taking, Alexander left the announcer booth, and just reaked pure carnage in the Parks/Debacle match. Word has it, Parks is none to pleased after the 22 stitches he had placed in his scalp after that viscious crowbar shot, so this should be a match to watch!!! _SPECIAL TAG TEAM MATCH_ Mike Gillis/Laramee vs "Hardcore Soul" Devon Freemont/Dan Debacle - Hardcore Legends versus a Fraidy Cat and a huge downer of the hardcore way of wrestling. To quote Mr. Gillis, it's a "pox on the wrestling industry." Gillis and Laramee are the very unlikely combo, however, a protector/protectee relationship has brought the two together. However, one could question Gillis on this one cause..putting Laramee in the ring with these two monsters, both over 6'6" and both over 300lbs is almost, throwing Laramee into harms way. One thing is for sure, Freemont has a score to settle with these two guys, and Debacle does as well. Gillis & Laramee have been rather active as of late, in the "run-in" business, and it may comeback and bite them in the ass here. _SEAN WHITES REVENGE_ Dave Risk vs "The Power" Afwa Kilmanjaro - Dave Risk, who has been a bit of a slacker as of late, trying to do the bare minimum to get paid, ticked the boss off at this past Extreme, when he promised he'd give him some quality air time on the show, and never produced. Word has it, that White was contemplating firing Risk, however, decided against it. After seeing Kilmanjaro dismantle wrestling talent, Mitch Gatlin in the ring on the 26th, White decided to book the match. Let's see if Risk can weasel his way out of this. _OPENING MATCH_ "Holocaust" Sean Willey vs "Crackhead" Darren Mobius - Two newcomers to AVW, who haven't been given alot of ring time yet, but...this could be their big break. You only get once chance to make first impressions, and being under the lights of the biggest AVW event of the year is the best place to make that impression.
