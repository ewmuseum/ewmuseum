RSPWF, AVW is accepting Heavyweight and Tag wrestlers. I'm not truly all that picky on tag gimmicks at the moment...however, I'm looking for the following heavyweight gimmicks: - A dark gimmick. Like an Undertaker/Kane. - A Cassanova/Smoothe Operator. No, not your typical HBK schtick. I'm talking about a wrestler, of the old Rick Rude makings...or a semi-Val Venis, who makes it his main point to degrade the men, and please the women. - Different Nationalities. Russian, German, Middle East, Mexican, Ireland, and English. Those aren't the _ONLY_ gimmicks I'll accept, however...those are the ones I'm looking for, so they'll take precedence over any other apps.
