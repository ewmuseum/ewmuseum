Sure, may not seem that way from the names, but most of them are handlers of superstars, trying out something a little new, since, having their major characters in a "regional" fed, wouldn't exactly go well with the character. Now, [name withheld] and I have scheduled the FIRST card for this Monday. The deadlines will be extended more than they normally would, as we will give EVERYONE until Friday at midnight to get their stuff in. That's the deadlines on everything, roleplays, in-rings, strats. Normally, and weeks to follow, the deadlines will be a little shorter, to give [name withheld] and I more time to get the cards and matches done. However, with the uneventful run of AAWC (which was neither [name withheld] or my fault, I _WILL_ add}, we wanted to get you guys a card ASAP. I know you all have been idle for far too long waiting on unfulfilled promises of the huge AAWC....[name withheld] and I were _WRONG_ to merge XWW. We freely admit that. We had a great thing going, and we ruined it with a merger. Now, that's all behind us. Time to look forward to the AVW!!!!!! You guys will have a blast, I guarantee it!!! Few basic rules. -ONE RP A WEEK. We're doing a show format of a roleplay packet, sorta like WWF Mania. Todd Pettengill (sp?), I guess he still does it, did little recaps of the week and let you see wrestler promos etc....all we can fit in, is one roleplay per wrestler. Now, you can do TWO rp's, but the other ones will go into an "extra" packet, not included in the Show Packet. -No actual IN-RINGS. What I mean by this, is that, you can do all the in-cards and on-cards you want. But, we want them taking place backstage, with a reporter/interviewer. Like what RAW does. When a storyline calls for an in-ring, YES, we'll do them and we'll allow them, but, for basic "talk" before your match, just do backstage things (in your lockroom, interview station, hallway, etc...) As for the first card, the way it's working. We're having 3 matches, and then a Royal Rumble. The Rumble will decide the seedings in the Bluegrass Title Tourney. The last guy standing gets #1 seed, 2nd guy standing gets #2 and so on. AAWC WORK!!! Important. Did a roleplay for AAWC? A vignette? Backstage piece? We still have them, and YES, we will count them. What you need to do if you choose this option is send us an e-mail, we will send you the work you want, all you need to do is change the names of your opponent(s) in it and send it back. [name withheld] and I are sorry as hell about AAWC. We admit it was a bust, and a bad decision on our part merging, but, it's all said and done now. We're trying to make things right, and we don't want the work you guys did, to be a waste of your time. Be our guest, use em again. A little tweaking, and you're done. The order will be determined on the night of the Rumble, no one will know the order. Not the wrestlers, and even Sampson & White won't know. So, that right there gives you some oncard possibilites :) The way this thing works, is the last 8 men in the Rumble, decide the tourney brackets. As soon as we have 8 men left, we start the tally. The first guy eliminated from the Rumble after we're down to 8, will draw the 8th seed, next guy will get the 7th, next will get the 6th...and so on. This will determine the WHOLE tourney, so it's VERY important you rp and bust ass to get a chance for the title. HOUR ONE _MAIN EVENT_ Mike Gillis w/ Laramee vs "Hardcore Soul" Devon Freemont "The Judge" Scott Justice vs Stan "The Man" Riley Michael Haynor vs "Rebel" Tony Dunn DEADLINE - Friday at Midnight for EVERYTHING.
