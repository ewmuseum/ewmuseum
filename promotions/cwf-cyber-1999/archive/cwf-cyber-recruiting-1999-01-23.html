Cyber Wrestling Federation Recruiting

23 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, I am the President of the Cyber Wresting Federation. Right now we are looking for talent and lots of it. Here at the CWF we don't believe that jobbers who don't post should be in with the big guys so that is why we have developed the CWF PowerZone! Basically it is a place to train and hone your skills and make sure that when you enter the CWF you are fully prepared!
The CWF can hold 35 participants and the PowerZone can hold 20. Right now they are both at about half capacity.
If you have over TWO years experience and have been in several e-feds before. Please sign up with the CWF. If you are new or don't have that kind of experience...sign up to the PowerZone where you will learn and train as you fight.
The CWF has been opened for 8 months and doesn't plan on going anywhere. We are always looking for NEW and GREAT talent and the doors are always open at the CWF!
Thanks, President [name withheld]
