FED HEADS! PLEASE READ!

30 April 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fugginVOSS — 30 April 2012 Hey guys, I just wanted to post something directly to you, as a fed head, no matter which fed you come from or what. The point of this Championship is to create an open styled championship belt able to be defended across a wide platform of wrestling federations.
Just to point out, there is no expectation on your behalf for your federation to recognise this championship. I understand that some feds operate inside their own "universes" and what happens here mightn't mean a damn there or you're just not interested in entertaining this at all.
Alternatively, you CAN recognise this championship within your fed. You can hype it or throw your guys at it to advertise your fed. You can recognise this championship and use it push other storylines. Hell, if your World Champ appears on your show as the Intergalactic Champion calling out his number one contender to win it off him for a shot at the World belt then go for it. This place is to be used as whatever platform you need it to be. Use it to your advantage and show the world what you've got or complete shun it... the ball is in your court.
If you do happen to recognise the Intergalactic Championship as a title in your federation's "universe" then please let me know as I would like to maintain a listing of promotions that see this as a legitimate title.
If you have any questions, you are free to shout out. Thanks. MaX-Files — 30 April 2012 INFINITE Wrestling recognizes the legitimacy of the Intergalactic Championship. DEF_Vagabond — 30 April 2012 Though my fed is still in the works, Right On! Wrestling will honor the Intergalactic Championship.

The Minstrel — 30 April 2012 I had made a post about this about a month and a half ago, about the idea of uniting in an umbrella fashion under the FW banner with one "World" Title to control them all, which was not well-received and shot down.
Although this is not quite the "umbrella concept" - a title recognized by all the feds would seem to be, at minimum building along those lines(although I am uncertain as to whether this title will be recognized as greater than the others), which is generally being received well by the other fedheads at least in terms of a title being recognized on an interfed basis.
Kudos nonetheless. Perhaps the idea is not as far as off as people thought - still think it would be an excellent way to generate interfed activity - if there was a way too structure such a thing.
fugginVOSS — 30 April 2012 My intention for this is not for it to be more worthy than any fed's top tier title. That is not the sentiment at all. Nor is there anything enforcing any fed to participate or recognise this (which is why I'm asking for people's buy-in as fed heads).
There will be plenty of structure and there are plenty of ideas flowing. As far as community involvement, I welcome anybody to present me ideas for this, albeit I will have finally say as to whether IGC will utilise those ideas I welcome you to share them.
