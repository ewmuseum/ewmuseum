Yeah - this adds to the work but I'm not promising nothing, okay? I'd even be happy for you to make and submit your own for use in IGC if you've got the time or the image.
So this thread, basically, is for anyone who is going to participate in IGC to post a description of their guy in and I'll make a Micro.
HOWEVER... here is the rule on edits: If your description if completely lacking and I'm told something like "use your imagination" or you don't provide me anything to work with you get zero (0) edits on your Micro and live with how I make it.
If you're giving me something to work with and I've ignored or missed something crucial to your guy's appearance then I will try to redeem myself with your image.
So, Intergalactic Participants, please post a description of what your guy should look like.
