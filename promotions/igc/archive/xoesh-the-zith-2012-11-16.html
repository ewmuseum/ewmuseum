Xoesh the Zith

Mad Dog — 16 November 2012 (Disclaimer: This character is a work in progress. So some things may be added or deleted as time goes on. If this is unacceptable, please feel free to let me know. Hit me up on AIM or thru PM.)
XOESH THE ZITH Billed From: A galaxy far far away…the planet of Saedomoe. Height: 6’2” Weight: 250 lbs. Alignment: Heel Theme Music: Theme to Close Encounters of the Third Kind Ring Entrance: Multi-colored flashing lights that are synchronized to his entrance music. Wears a black cloak to the ring.
Finisher & Description: Does your wrestler have any special set-up for their finisher? Theatrics or a set-up move? Special Moves: These are your awesome trademarks that are yours alone. Xoesh copies what his opponent does in the ring. He reviews their matches and uses their moves against them.
Every Match Spots: Most wrestlers have spots they pull off in the duration of 99.9999% of their matches - what's your guys?
Xoesh will hold his opponent’s head in his hands, as if he is doing a momentary brain scan. Other than that, I can’t think of anything else.
Backstory: The origins of Xoesh are unclear. He claims to come from another planet and has invaded Earth to take it over; choosing IGC specifically due to the championship that implies the holder of the title is the Intergalactic Champion. Xoesh states that his planet received the Voyager space probe and returned to Earth because of it. He has an affinity for Chuck Berry, as a gold record was in the space probe that was received and his people listen to it frequently. He also says that he was on Earth thousands of years ago and ruled over mankind; managing the building of the pyramids for example.
Some people believe that none of this is true. That Xoesh is a human that either has deep psychological problems or was hit in the head and suffers from amnesia. Perhaps as his story unfolds, we will come to know more about this mysterious individual. He is hell-bent on capturing the IGC title and ruling over Earth in the name of his leader, Oxuzi.
