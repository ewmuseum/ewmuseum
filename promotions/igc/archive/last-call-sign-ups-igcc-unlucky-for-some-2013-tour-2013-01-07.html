fugginVOSS — 7 January 2013 As I posted last year, and there was some reasonable interest, I figured I'd go ahead and post a sign-ups thread for this.
Essentially, you just need to sign up with your character name to this and you will automatically be booked for shows to qualify to become the championship.
The current Intergalactic Champion, at the point of the tour beginning, is automatically a part of any shows.
PLEASE NOTE: That all shows will have sign up threads posted so as not to exclude the rest of the community from the opportunity to win the Championship. As for those who sign up for this they will be involved in each of the cards.
So, like I said, post the following information: Character Name By responding to this you will automatically become a part of the tour. There are no further credentials required to be a part of this.
