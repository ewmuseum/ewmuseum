Todd Killings — 26 February 2013 Wrestler Name: Todd Killings Nicknames: The Killer, The Todd Killings Billed From: New Jersey Height: 6 feet Weight: 240 pounds Alignment: Good guy Theme Music: "The Crusher" by the Ramones Ring Entrance: He walks out from the back like he doesn't even know what he's doing there. He gets in the ring and stands there walks around a bit.
Finisher & Description: DDT Does your wrestler have any special set-up for their finisher? Most of the time no. Sometimes he'll throw a person into the ropes and do it, or kick them in the stomach before he does it.
Special Moves: He does clotheslines a lot. And legdrops. He thinks legdrops are awesome. If he does a top rope legdrop that's something crazy for him.
Every Match Spots: Pretty much clotheslines and legdrops. You know how wrestlers when you watch it on TV they punch each other a lot? He does that you know, he punches the guy. He doesn't really kick, he's not a high flyer and stuff. He does a neckbreaker and a powerbomb too. Those are his favorite moves. Sometimes he grabs people by their long hair and hits their head on the corner turnbuckle like 3 or 4 times.
10-15 Moves: Clothesline, legdrop, neckbreaker, powerbomb, arm drag, headlock, elbow smash, bulldog, backdrop suplex, elbow drop, top rope legdrop.
Backstory: Todd Killings started wrestling when he saw a poster at a grocery store. Championship History: none brusch — 26 February 2013 This is the greatest bio ever.
