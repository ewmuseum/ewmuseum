Johnny Niles — 14 June 2012 Wrestler Name: Michael Raves Billed From: Albury, Australia Height: 6' Weight: 224 lbs Alignment: Face Theme Music: "New Divide" by Linkin Park Ring Entrance: Michael walks out to "New Divide", basking in the adulation from the crowd. He runs down the ramp and jumps through the middle and lower rope, stands and raises his fist to the crowd, before climbing the nearest turnbuckle and waving to the crowd. After that he performs a backflip to the mat and awaits his opponent.
Finishers & Descriptions: Adrenaline Rush (Shooting Star Press) Crash & Burn (Somersault Cutter) Does your wrestler have any special set-up for their finisher?: Only for the Adrenaline Rush: After delivering a Brainbuster to the opponent Michael climbs the turnbuckle, inhaling deeply before jumping off to deliver the Adrenaline Rush.
Special Moves: Rave Review (Jumps to the middle rope, rebounds back with a Calf Kick) Aussie Salute (Shiranui) Every Match Spots: - After being lifted up by the opponent (for a powerbomb, Fireman's Carry, etc) twists and counters into a hurricanrana or headscissors
- Lifts the opponent and delivers a brainbuster (used as set-up for the Adrenaline Rush) 10-15 Moves: Backhand Chop Running Enzuigiri Missle Dropkick Snap DDT Japanese Arm Drag Double Underhook Suplex Exploder Suplex Russian Legsweep Pendulum Kick Single-leg Facebuster Hurricanrana Spin Kick Swinging Neckbreaker Springboard Moonsault Texas Cloverleaf Bio: Michael is a huge wrestling fan, having watched guys like Stone Cold and The Rock become stars of the buisness. However, his favorite part of wrestling is the high-flying action displayed by cruiserweights. He believes that size is nothing compared to the skill and heart that one possesses. He speaks with an Australian-English dialect.
After honing his skills in his native Australia, Michael moved to the U.S. to seek competition. Taking great pride in his nationality, Michael strives to become the greatest Australian wreslter in history, as well as the greatest high-flyer. He's usually a good sportsman, but if his opponent uses dirty tactics against him, then he'll make use of the saying "two can play at that game".
During the match, he'll usually take the high-risk road to victory instead of the safe, no-injury way. He prefers using high-risk moves even when they aren't necessary. He is not a complete bone-headed daredevil, though, as he always prepares for his opponents' counter-attack.
