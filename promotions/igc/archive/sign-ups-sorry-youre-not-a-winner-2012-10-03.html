Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fugginVOSS — 3 October 2012 Gangsters, Looking to gauge interest in challengers for the Intergalactic Championship's THIRD event, Sorry You're Not a Winner.
Not limiting numbers, just testing waters. This is open to everyone. If you would like to challenge for the Intergalactic Championship you need to reply to this roleplay thread: http://www.fwrestling.com/showthread.php?24149-Sorry-You-re-Not-a-Winner-Entries , in character, (500 word limit - 1 reply per person) about why you want to be the Intergalactic Champion. Sign ups end on October 18th, 2012.
I will choose the people, from the responses in this thread, as to who will go into which of the first two matches on the card.
At Sorry You're Not a Winner there will be THREE matches. Match 1: Intergalactic Championship Qualification match for V for Victoralicious If you win this match you will go on to face the Intergalactic Champion at the next event, V for Victoralicious.
Match 2: Intergalactic Championship Qualification match for the Main Event. If you win this match you will face Phil Atken, the CURRENT Intergalactic Champion, in the Main Event of Sorry You're Not a Winner.
Match 3: Main Event Intergalactic Championship Match! Phil Atken versus the Winner of Match 2 NOTE: This will be a blind RP contest for the Intergalactic Champion. Atken will not know who his competitor is outside of the fact it will be one of the participants in Match 2.
