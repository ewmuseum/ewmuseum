IGCC Staff Management On-screen Characters Officials Backstage Crew fugginVOSS — Feb 11, 2013 at 10:54 PM Management Place holder for management details fugginVOSS — Feb 11, 2013 at 10:54 PM On-screen Characters Ring Announcer - "Pepper" Pete Peppins About: Now in his sixties, Pete was a superstar of yesteryear. His thick Texan drawl and love of the industry makes him one of the best announcers in the business. He once wrestled in a tag team with commentator "Playboy" Harry Balkin called the Big Sneezy, who sometimes reunite for good times' sakes.
Commentator - "Playboy" Harry Balkin About: Former wrestler who never really cared for titles but more for the women, Harry is a colourful play-by-play babyface announcer. He often makes inappropriate sexual innuendo about his own sexual experiences of pop culture references which he tries to mask behind a hearty "WOOOOOOOOOO!" Harry has a personal vendetta with Magnus Destructo for breaking his nose at V for Victoralicious.
NOTES ON COMMENTARY TEAM: Harry Balkin is the regular announcer for tour shows and most of the bigger events. Where he is not in the driver's seat it will be Morton Murphy. On the larger events, Harry will be joined by any number of the roster members.
Commentator (Tour Shows) - "the Raging Fear" August Monday About: August Monday is a retired wrestling legend who was known for being extraordinarily violent. When adding colour commentary, August delivers technical insight into moves along with personal opinion on character reference. His daughter, "Daddy's Little Demon" April Monday, is still and active wrestler and he is still over protective of her. He often makes references to his only grandchild, Adam Shepherd, and doesn't take kindly to mentions of him being a eunuch.
Backstage Interviewer - Sandra Dee About: Sandra Dee has been Morton Murphy's personal assistant for years but has the look and brains to be on the programming. Think of a stunning, blond with voluptuous over-the-top curves with the brains of a wrestling equivalent Larry King. Is not susceptible to come ons.
 fugginVOSS — Feb 11, 2013 at 10:54 PM Officials Head Referee - Jarvis Jolt About: Jarvis Jolt is a former wrestler who suffered a back injury that he has mostly recovered from, however, meeting his wife's demands has not gone back to ring activity as a wrestler and has taken a lead referee role with the IGCC. Formerly a very promising main event prospect, Jarvis understands how the matches should work but he won't take any shit. Push him? He pushes back. He can be a bit of a loose cannon. He performed mainly as a babyface and does not tolerate heel tactics where he has the power to intervene. He will pull wrestlers off at the end of a five count and snatch chairs out of hands.
Referee - Doug Keith About: Doug is a relatively new referee to the industry but has a lot of promise. He's not easily intimidated, despite his wiry frame, and does his best to call it down the middle without any bias. He's lenient to five-counts and will rarely call a DQ for someone not abiding by the rules. He likes to see a clear winner where possible.
Referee - Jane Russell About: Jane came into the industry as a valet but found herself wanting to get closer to the action. Some say she's here to find herself a husband. She is susceptible to being woo'ed by suitable courters and is mostly fair unless this has occurred. Don't get on her bad side though... Hell hath no fury like a woman scorned. (See also; quick counts, quick DQs)
Referee - Referee Mask I About: Little is known about this flamboyant referee aside from he is melodramatic and zips about the ring like a house fly. He can often be accused of stealing the show but tries his best to concentrate and just call it down the middle.
Timekeeper - Moses Monk About: Little is known about the giant time keeper. It is said he has a relationship with Morton Murphy but nobody can tie the two together other than this business arrangement. He is a bit oversized for ringing a bell but everybody's gotta make a living. Moses stands at 7 feet and looks to weigh in around three bucks fifty.
