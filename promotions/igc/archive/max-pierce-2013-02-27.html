MAX PIERCE Name: Maxime Jonathan Pierce Nicknames: "Mad", Height: 6'5 Weight: 255 lbs. Handedness: Right Looks: Pierce is a tall, strikingly handsome Caucasian man with short, jet-black hair, dark brown eyes, and is always clean shaven. His build is impressive - muscular, cut, and lean. He doesn't have any noticeable body hair, tattoos, or scars.
Hailing From: Manhattan, New York Disposition: Heel Gimmick: Pierce is a narcissistic, self-absorbed man from a wealthy and powerful Manhattan family. He's manipulative, masterfully so, and, using his stature, will force others into doing what he wants. He's arrogant, well-learned, and truly diabolical.
Ring Attire: Pierce does not wear a shirt to the ring. He wears a small, black elbow pad on both elbows. He wears a pair of black wristbands. He wears full-length, black tights with a small "MP" written in gold, Old English font on both hips. He wears a black knee brace, nothing too gaudy or gigantic, on his right knee. He wears a pair of black wrestling boots.
Theme Music: Sergei Prokofiev's "Montagues and Capulets, Dance of the Knights" Ring Entrance: Pierce's entrance takes longer than most due to the cues from his theme. Roughly thirty-five to forty seconds play before Pierce finally steps through the curtain. He uses it as a method to unnerve his opposition (listen to his theme). His gait is very methodical and even-paced. He will sometimes address the crowd, never talks to them, but will sneer or stare at them. He will not slide into the ring on his initial entrance as he prefers climbing the steps and then cutting through the ropes. He will stalk around the ring at times before entering. Once he's inside the ring, he will sometimes circle an opponent or at the very least move around close to them.
Tactics/Style: Pierce is classically trained. His style is best described as precision brawling with a mix of strong technical wrestling. Pierce uses a limited amount of submission wrestling - his Guillotine Choke is his only constant. He's not an aerial artist by any means and will rarely attack from above the second turnbuckle. He's very methodical and always seems to be a step ahead of his opposition. He has a wealth of knowledge as far as moves, holds, and counters go, but tends to keep a concise moveset. He's not a regular cheater, but, if the opportunity arises, he will use it as a means to an end.
