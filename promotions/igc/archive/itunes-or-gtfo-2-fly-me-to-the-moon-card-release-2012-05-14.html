iTunes or GTFO! #2: Fly Me to the Moon Card Release

14 May 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fugginVOSS — 14 May 2012 http://intergalactic.awesome-and-win.com/images/itgtfo/iTgtfo2.png [FADE IN: on Morton Murphy standing before that backdrop, looking proud as punch with a grin from ear-to-ear. Hands on hips, eagerly anticipating the call to action he comes to life as he gets a signal off shot.]
MORTON MURPHY: Wrestling fans, am I glad to see you! Or wait... thats the other way around. Whatever it is it doesnt really matter how that goes because tonight I am announcing the FIRST card for Fly Me to the Moon and I can tell you folks it is one which should be EARGERLY anticipated.
Im excited just thinking about it! [He bounces from foot-to-foot as he continues on.] MORTON MURPHY: Our card will feature many matches but its REALLY the second half of the card you guys should all be getting excited about. The first of three matches featured in the second half of our card is singles competition to find OUR FIRST qualifier for the Intergalactic Championship match at the ULTRATITLE finale.
[SMASH CUT-TO: A graphic on the screen featuring the images of the first two competitors and the match stipulations.]
Intergalactic Championship Qualifiers Match Set for one fall (RO!W) Sabrevs. Dragon Jones (DEFIANCE) http://intergalactic.awesome-and-win.com/images/micros/heads/sabre.pnghttp://intergalactic.awesome-and-win.com/images/micros/heads/dragon.png
MORTON MURPHY: (voice over style) Coming from DEFIANCE Wrestling is the first of our competitors and that is Dragon Jones. Hes going to be facing off against Right On! Wrestlings Sabre and thats SURE to be some sort of battle thatll go down in the history books. Matter of fact, theyre going to determine who will be our first contenders for the Intergalactic Championship so theres no doubt at all itll hit the ledger.
[SMASH CUT-TO: Murphy before that beautiful Intergalactic Championship backdrop once more. The image appears to his right in the top corner (our left from a viewers perspective) like it might do on the news.]
MORTON MURPHY: These are both some relatively young guys looking to get their name out there and if I dont mind saying so myself... I cant think of a BETTER way than becoming the FIRST Intergalactic Champion.
Can you? This is just the beginning of what should be a fantastic second hour of our card, folks. Not to mention the IGC Touring Roster will be filling out the first half of the card with August Monday versus Normal John Johnson in our first hour main event.
The next of our TWO Intergalactic Championship match qualifiers is a special promotion versus promotion affair with ACW heading off against INFINITE Wrestling.
[SMASH CUT-TO: The next image on display naming the participants of the next match. Intergalactic Championship Qualifiers Match Set for two falls First Fall All-Star Championship Wrestling vs. INFINITE Wrestling (ACW) Jack Harris & Kenjiro Ito vs. Max Hopper & Unfortunate Phil Atken (INFINITE) http://intergalactic.awesome-and-win.com/images/micros/heads/harris.pnghttp://intergalactic.awesome-and-win.com/images/micros/heads/ito.png http://intergalactic.awesome-and-win.com/images/micros/heads/hopper.pnghttp://intergalactic.awesome-and-win.com/images/micros/heads/atken.png
MORTON MURPHY: (voice over) A collision of one of the stalwart wrestling promotions from North America who has been around for a damn long time in All-Star Championship Wrestling facing two men off, in Jack Harris and Kenjiro Ito, against upstart promotion INFINITE wrestling. Max Hopper and Phil Atken REPRESENT in what should be some sort of crazy tag team turmoil.
[SMASH CUT-TO: Morton again with the image back in that corner as Murphy continues talking up the match.]
MORTON MURPHY: That is the first of two falls. ACW versus INFINITE. The second fall varies as we eliminate TWO competitors. Thats right. The first fall is regular tag team rules and from the winning team our second fall comes.
Are you still with me? If ACW wins then Kenjiro Ito and Jack Harris will turn on each other to become the qualifier at the ULTRATITLE finale. If INFINITE manage to defeat ACW then Hopper and Atken will square off for that opportunity.
Pretty simple, right? Im telling you now, thats going to be some match and the fans will be restless in their seats waiting to find out just WHO carries on to the ULTRATITLE finale to have the opportunity to become the FIRST Intergalactic Champion.
I know I cant wait. But I also cant wait for our MAIN EVENT OF THE EVENING! [SMASH CUT-TO: A full body picture of Hornet on the screen as Murphy carries on talking over the image.]
http://intergalactic.awesome-and-win.com/images/micros/hornet.png MORTON MURPHY: (voice over style) We have CSWA Legend HORNET who has raised an OPEN CHALLENGE to anybody out there willing to take on one of the brightest lights in this industry. Hornet has won more belts than Ive had hot dinners and Fly Me to the Moon will be yet another feast.
Of course, what is an open challenge without a reward, right? [SMASH CUT-TO: Morton standing there and Hornets image being in the corner.] MORTON MURPHY: We here at the Intergalactic Championship Commission have put on offer to the lucky challenger AND Hornet that the WINNER of that match WILL GET an Intergalactic Championship shot at a later date AT A TIME of THEIR choosing.
Thats right. An Intergalactic Championship shot in the bank to be used whenever they see fit and against whomever they see fit wherever they see fit.
Thats some prize and some deal to have sitting up your sleeve. WHO WILL HORNETS CHALLENGER BE? Well we here at the IGCC are awaiting confirmation from HORNET himself. Vizier te Seti has come out of the wood work to challenge the veteran. Vizier is the CURRENT IWF Emerald City Champion and is making a name for himself in Seattle but WILL Hornet accept his challenge?
Were all awaiting to find out just WHAT Hornet has to say about that challenge and WHO his opponent will be.
