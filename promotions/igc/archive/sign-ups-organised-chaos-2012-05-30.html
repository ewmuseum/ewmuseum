fugginVOSS — 30 May 2012 The Intergalactic Championship is now taking sign ups for the SECOND event to be held: Organised Chaos.
I was going to WAIT until after the results for Round 2 went out but I'll leave this open until after I post Fly Me to the Moon, so you can start signing up now if you're interested in being a part of our second event.
I will not stipulate how many people I am looking for, regardless there will be two (2) people going on to qualify for the grand finale at ULTRATITLE to contest for the currently vacant Intergalactic Championship.
If you are interested in being oinvolved you simply need to respond to this thread advising you're interested and with which character.
It's that simple. Unfortunately, I won't be able to take on all comers. YOUR CHARACTER IS INELIGIBLE if they are still active participants in the ULTRATITLE tournament.
So... reply to this if you're interested in becoming the first Intergalactic Champion. An announcement at a later date will advise who has been selected to participate. Thank you. INTERESTED PARTIES Eddie Whisky (IWF) Leyenda de Ocho (IWF) Christopher Ryan Eagles (TWOstars) Axion Jackson (IWF) "The Jersey Devil" Troy Matthews Lane Cash WHAT ARE WE DOING FOR THE MAIN EVENT? That is still undecided however if someone is interested in doing some sort of DREAM or GRUDGE match to main event this card please get at me with your thoughts, ideas and whatever else you might have.
All suggestions are welcome!
