Please post your feedback here! Special thanks to Colin for writing Raucous v. Xoesh the Zith J The Ripper — 11 February 2013 I'd be a liar if I said I wasn't disappointed but hey, that's how it goes sometimes. As always awesome job of putting EVERYONE over in their match ups. I was pulling for Ocho (No offense Colin!) but Colin seems to be unstoppable at this point. It'll be interesting to see who, if anyone, dethrones him.
brusch — 12 February 2013 ATKEEEEEEEEN I was hoping for the W here, but Colin's a rockstar. Well-earned. Voss, as always, makes these matches into epic stories that make everyone involved look like a million bucks. Ocho has probably gotten over more in losing two IGC title matches than in all the other matches he's had put together. A really entertaining read and a unique way to navigate the three stipulations that I surely tortured Voss by choosing.
Magnus/Sylo could have gone either way, I think - two extremely different styles between the characters. Loved the nose-breaking moment with the Playboy - all the time you see commentators get out of dodge in matches, but even when they get in the crossfire, you rarely see something this visceral, and it was pretty cool in a Monsters Ball Match.
Kudos to Xoesh as well! Looking forward to facing Mad Dog in the tour, and I hope Raucous sticks around - he's an interesting character to have around.
EYE GEE SEE! EYE GEE SEE! fugginVOSS — 12 February 2013 Thanks for the feedback, guys. Keep it coming.!
