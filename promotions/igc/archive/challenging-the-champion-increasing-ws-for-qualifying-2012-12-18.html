Challenging the Champion: Increasing W's for qualifying

18 December 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
fugginVOSS — 18 December 2012 Challenging the Champion So essentially, the challenging the Champion thing will remain as a qualifier. I do have plans for the future to perhaps increase the qualifying limit to two CONSECUTIVE W's before you can face off, just to build more to the matches and perhaps build some heat and storylines. Currently, it's pretty much bang-bang and the only hype that can occur is during promos.
I am yet to decide if this will be the case but am strongly leaning towards it. Feel free to voice your opinion.
brusch — 18 December 2012 A quick question for clarification: Does this mean a competitor must win two matches consecutively, or two matches period? Like if someone were to win a match, then go on a 4 match losing streak before picking up #2, are they eligible to challenge for the title?
fugginVOSS — 18 December 2012 Good question. It would be consecutive wins. brusch — 18 December 2012 Two consecutive I like, it allows for a constant stream of mini-tournaments where 1 win vs 1 win guys can face off for a shot at the title. Plus it's poopy if someone gets a shot if their record is like 2-8.
fugginVOSS — 18 December 2012 Agreed, Ben. However it's still possible for someone to garner a shot at 2-8 if their last two matches were Dubyas.
Colin — 18 December 2012 And then they'd be on a HOT STREAK anyway.
