A forum-hosted commission on the FW Central hub that crowned its first Intergalactic Champion in a four-way dream match drawn from across the wider community, then opened its own Unlucky For Some 2013 tour by ending that champion's 146-day reign in a bloodied main event. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Intergalactic Championship Commission announced itself 30 April 2012 at a press conference in Greensboro, North Carolina, billed in its own release as “launching from the home of CSWA.” Its own commissioner, Morton Murphy, framed the whole project around a lifelong “what if” — wondering, as he put it, why wrestlers from different promotions couldn't settle their own dream matchups against each other.

Its first Intergalactic Champion, Phil Atken, was crowned in a four-way match at its debut special, Survivalism, 3 October 2012, over Sabre, Leyenda de Ocho and Troy Matthews.

Atken's own reign ran through two further defenses of record: a blind title match whose challenger was decided by a separate triangle match, and a three-stage title match of Ocho's own design — a cage match, a ladder match into overhead scaffolding, and a last-man-on-the-scaffold match — which Atken won by biting Ocho's wrist to force him down. His reign ended at the debut show of a touring stretch, Unlucky For Some 2013, in Louisville on 28 February 2013, when Magnus Destructo finished him with the Destructo Bomb.

The same Louisville show crowned a hardcore titleholder of its own: Ikan Jobtayoo won the WREX Hardcore Championship from BookFace with a Rolling Thunder onto a steel chair.

The tour's own next two stops, in Hot Springs and Baton Rouge, were announced with full lineups, but no result survives for either, and the Louisville show's own recap was not even posted until 3 March 2015, two years after the event it describes. Nothing further is on record.
