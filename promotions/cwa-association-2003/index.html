A fantasy federation online from 2003, whose own story dates it to 1984, with weekly Assault and Chaos shows and monthly pay-per-views written by its owner and played by a roster of members' characters, until its tenant directory's cards are billed as the CWO in July 2004, after what that company's own cards call a buyout of the CWA. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The CWA's own pages say it began in 1984 as audio tapes, written results and matches in its owner's best friend's basement, and was put online as the Championship Wrestling Association in the early 2000s; the earliest capture here, from August 2003, carries its first Assault results from May of that year, a World title crowned on 21 June and a Women's title added in August. By its own news posts it ran two shows, Assault and Chaos, and a monthly pay-per-view, among them Maximum Impact, Animosity, Bloodshed, Dead By Dawn and Backdraft, and its title history lists World, Superior, Hardcore, tag, Aerial and Women's champions through January 2004.

Its record in the mirror ends in the summer of 2004. The cards of a new company, the CWO, begin on the same tenant directory on 12 July 2004, and the CWO's own owner says on its card of 2 August that he bought the CWA out and cut it to a regional company; the CWO's belts are new ones, and the CWA's are called titles nobody there recognises. The same address later carries Anarchy Wrestling and, from 2007, a returned CWA, each with a wing of its own.

Affiliations

Global Wrestling Organization (GWO, Global, 2004)

Successor to CWA (Association, 2003)
The CWO's owner says on its card of 2 August 2004 that he bought the CWA out and cut it to a regional company; every title on its cards was handed out afresh that night and the CWA's belts are called titles nobody there recognises. Roster and owner carry over; the titles do not.

Championship Wrestling Association (CWA, Revival, 2007)

Shared continuity with CWA (Association, 2003)
The 2008 CWA's front page says it was born in 1984, has been through many restarts and has returned; it carries the name and the owner of the 2003 and 2004 CWA but none of its titles.

CWA Originals (Originals, 2006)

Shared continuity with CWA (Association, 2003)
The CWA Originals tell the same company's story from 1984 under the same name and owner's persona, but its World title history names different champions from the 2003 and 2004 CWA's.
