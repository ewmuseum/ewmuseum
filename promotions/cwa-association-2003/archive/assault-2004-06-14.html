CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 14 June 2004

14 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
San Francisco is loud as the camera starts rolling and our show begins. Backstage we see Vesuvius with Joe Davidson.
Joe Davidson - V-Man, you were seen on Chaos. What were you doing there? Vesuvius - *smiles* Is Scavenger booked for Chaos this week? Joe Davidson - No, he isn't. Vesuvius - I made sure he wouldn't be. Don't mess with the man named after the mountain. Vesuvius walks away laughing as Joe backs off. We then go through the opening credits and see images of Lethal Weapon, Flock, Wish, Gerard Angelo and Humanity Crisis before heading to ringside where Henry Lesher sits next to Ray Dees once again.
Wish vs TJ Mercer "Imaginary" by Evanescence plays as TJ Mercer makes his way to the ring. He gets a warm reception from the crowd, but he is attacked half way down the ramp by Wish. Wish apparently is not in the mood to wait for this match to start and he lands a series of double axe handle smashes to the back of TJ before picking him up and body slamming him on the steel. Wish then drags TJ down the ramp and rolls him into the ring. Wish then steps onto the apron and through the ropes and the bell sounds to get the match started.
Henry Lesher - Must they throw these people I don't care about in the ring every week against each other? Haven't I been punished enough?
Ray Dees - Apparently I am here which means I have not been punished enough either. Henry Lesher - You punished others by making them watch you wrestle. Wish leg drops TJ to the back of the neck, then places him in a cross face chicken wing. TJ reaches for the ropes as the ref checks for a submission. The crowd starts a TJ chant as we see Vesuvius and Sandman coming out from the back. Sandman gets a few chants, but some ringside fans let Vesuvius have it for his continuing battles with fan favorites such as Scavenger and Shannon Skanes. TJ manages to snag the rope to force the break and Wish reluctantly lets him up. Wish then turns and sees Sandman and Vesuvius there and has a few words for them. Wish then turns back to TJ and takes a shoulder block from him. TJ then gets to his feet, whips Wish off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Vesuvius nods his head at ringside, apparently pleased.
Ray Dees - What is Vesuvius doing out here? Henry Lesher - Looks like he is checking up on his newest member of Anarchy. TJ stands Wish up, whips him into the corner buckle and then bulldogs him out of it. She then rolls him over for the pin. 1...2.... kick out by Wish. TJ stands Wish up, whips him into the buckle and charges straight into a back kick to the chest from Wish. Wish then jumps to the second rope, turns around and lands a clothesline to the mat on TJ from there. Vesuvius looks less pleased at ringside with this development. Wish then picks TJ up and gives him a running powerslam in the corner. The crowd boos as Wish looks at them and sneers. He tells Vesuvius his pupil's time is up. He picks TJ up and delivers the Wishful Thinking on him as the crowd boos and then he goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Wish gets the win and rolls out of the ring, anxious to get away from Vesuvius and Sandman.
Henry Lesher - That was almost quick and painless. Ray Dees - TJ is in what they used to call the Dees' Position. I spent a lot of time counting lights from the mat.
Henry Lesher - You must have seen double a lot. Ray Dees - And how! Vesuvius slides into the ring as TJ gets to his feet. Vesuvius then backs TJ into the corner and tells him that there is no room for error in Anarchy. Vesuvius then grabs the microphone from the ring announcer.
Vesuvius - I regret that Kathy Smith and Kelly Buchanan chose to leave. In their place, we have fine people like TJ here. I am also afraid we have lost Noah Skanes who has chosen to go to Chaos and pursue other avenues there by the permission of Gloria and Shelia Lavin. In his place, I bring you Lori!
"Cold" by Static X plays and out comes Lori, the ex manager for Myst. She heads to the ring to the cheers of the crowd, but she has no reaction to the fans at all. She steps onto the apron and inside the ring, then grabs the microphone from Vesuvius.
Lori - I am here. I am in Anarchy Inc. It's time for me to show I am more than Myst's sidekick. Maybe someday I'll show Myst she's not all that either. Time for me to grab the spotlight!
Lori throws down the microphone and heads out of the ring with Vesuvius, Sandman and TJ Mercer close behind. We then look backstage and see Lethal Weapon entering the arena. His walk quickens when he spots Angela and Ash in the hallway. He drops his gear and goes for the golden bullet spear on Ash and nails it. He then tries for one on Angela, but she sidesteps it. Officials race in to split them up before Angela and Weapon can really get into it. Weapon walks away with a smile on his face as they start attending to Ash who looks like he was broken in half.
Pyro vs Tommy Air "When Darkness Falls" by Killswitch Engage plays as Angel Peters walks Pyro to the ring. The crowd has a rather mixed response to him as he wears a new Dark Horizons T-shirt on his back and is talking to Angel all the way to the ring. Pyro steps up onto the apron and then into the ring as Angel takes her place at ringside. We then hear "Smells Like Teen Spirit" by Nirvana and Tommy Air comes out from the back. He steps into the ring and raises a hand to keep Pyro back as he takes the microphone from ring announcer, Lacy Brown.
Tommy Air - Now as all of you know, it’s going to be me verses Halloween Jackass, in a Hell in the Cell at this year’s CWA Sun Stroke in twelve day’s time.
The crowd cheer loudly. Tommy Air - Now a lot of people have been saying things recently about me, they’ve been saying I’m to young to be in the CWA.
The crowd cheer loudly again. Henry Lesher: I'm telling you right now, five minutes alone with Tommy, and my shock therapy kit, and I could make him a real man.
Tommy Air - They’ve been saying I’m not good enough to be in the CWA. The crowd cheer even louder this time. Henry Lesher: And it's the one time they're right, oh wait it's me that's been saying that. And I'm always right, so there.
Tommy Air - Still, I’m only the CWA Superior champion, and let me remind you I was the very first CWA Common Wealth champion, which is now being held by the very lovely Myst.
The crowd cheer even louder than before. Henry Lesher: Blah, blah blah. More pointless hyperbole from a pointless person. Tommy Air - But not only have I won gold, I’ve beaten some of the best, but still these people say terrible things about me. They say that I’m a fluke, that my whole career has been just pure luck.
Henry Lesher: Oh yeah, one more time they've been right... Oh, right me again. Tommy Air - Shut your mouth, you think I can’t hear you from there. Remember what happened last time dumb ass, but this time it won’t be Paul throwing your ass to the floor. In fact, this is the very same man who said those things about me, he said in his mind I was the luckiest CWA wrestler in 2004. Well I might be the luckiest wrestler but you’re the luckiest man, for not dieing from a heart attack when Paul choke slammed your ass onto the steel chair in this very ring. I’ll prove to you Lesher that I haven’t got a drop of luck in me, because I’m pure talent old man.
Tommy Air drops the mic and rubs his championship belt around his waist, whilst his smiling at Henry Lesher, he takes of his belt and starts to warm up for his match. Pyro takes advantage of his pre-occupation with an attack and the bell sounds. Pyro whips Tommy into the corner and lands a series of kicks to the chest which knock him off his feet. Pyro then stands Tommy up and snap mares him to the mat and places him in a front chinlock. The crowd boos and start a chant for the very popular Tommy Air.
Henry Lesher - Again, too young and lousy for the CWA. Luck is running out for him. Ray Dees - Easy there, Doc, match just started. Tommy battles to his feet and starts laying elbows to the gut of Pyro, then whips him hard into the corner and gives him an atomic drop back into it head first. Tommy then gives Pyro a German suplex and goes for the pin out of it. 1...2... kick out by Pyro to keep the match going. Tommy stands Pyro up, whips him off the ropes and ducks his head for a backdrop only to have Pyro kick him in the face. Pyro then gives Tommy a spinebuster to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by the Superior champ. Pyro stands Tommy up now, whips him off the ropes and tries for a dropkick, but Tommy holds onto the ropes and Pyro hits the mat hard on the back of his head and neck. Tommy then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. Pyro gets to his feet, turns and takes a flying cross body from Tommy to the mat and Tommy tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Pyro.
Henry Lesher - The so called champ is having trouble disptaching the head of the Job Squad.
Ray Dees - I used to head that group and quite proudly I might add. Henry Lesher - You have no pride. Tommy stands Pyro up, then whips him into the corner. The two men then clothesline each other to the mat near the corner. The ref starts counting them both down. Tommy grabs onto the rope at the three count. Pyro grabs onto the ropes at the four count. Tommy gets to his feet at six and starts putting the boots to Pyro, forcing him into the corner. Tommy then monkey flips Pyro out of the corner to the mat. Tommy then does a lionsault onto Pyro for the pin. 1...2...3! Tommy Air pulls off the win over Pyro this week. Tommy then gets to his feet and points a finger at Lesher. From the back we see BasketKase racing down the ramp and he hits the ring, attacking Tommy from behind.
Henry Lesher - Make him eat that finger, BasketKase! Ray Dees - This unbiased announcing is brought to you by the fine people at The Gap. BasketKase throws Tommy over the top rope, grabs his title belt and dives over the top, crowning him with it. Security then rushes out from the back to break this melee up as we head to a commercial.
commercial break* Corina vs Giovanni Mabius "Thing About It" by Sweatshop Union plays as Corina makes her way to the ring to the cheers of the crowd. Corina steps up onto the apron and then through the ropes into the ring. She waves to the crowd as they snap pictures of her. We then hear "Money Will Roll Right In" by Nirvana and Giovanni Mabius makes his way to the ring to the boos of the fans. Corina takes a few deep breaths inside the ring as Giovanni looks inside the ring and smiles. He slides into the ring, then quickly gets to his feet and the bell sounds. The two circle each other. Giovanni then moves in, kneeing her in the gut and placing her right into an abdominal stretch. Corina screams in pain, but refuses to submit when the ref asks her.
Ray Dees - Looks like this babe might not last long. Henry Lesher - She is a Glamour Girl. None of them last long. Corina reaches for the ropes. The crowd cheers her on as Giovanni tells the crowd to shut up. Of course, this gets chants directed at Giovanni. Corina then somehow finds the energy to wriggle free and grab the ropes. The ref pushes Giovanni back, but Giovanni kicks Corina in the back, sending her over the top rope to the floor. Giovanni smiles as he makes his way to the top rope. Corina turns and sees Mabius flying off the top and side steps it and clotheslines him to the floor which brings all the fans out of their seats. Corina then rolls herself into the ring as Mabius slowly gets to his feet. He rolls himself inside the ring at a six count. Corina then starts putting the boots to him. Corina stands Mabius up, whips him to the corner and then she does a cartwheel into a back elbow to the chest before snap maring him to the mat. Corina then comes off the ropes and lands a rolling thunder on Mabius and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Giovanni Mabius.
Ray Dees - Looks like we underestimated her. Henry Lesher - She is against the Job Squad. Corina goes for the pin. 1...2... Mabius manages to kick out. Corina then stands Mabius up, whips him off the ropes and lariats him to the mat and again tries for the pin. 1...2... Mabius rolls it over into a small package and hooks the tights. 1...2...3! Mabius wins with a bit of underhanded dealing here!
Ray Dees - Win for GQ as he calls himself. Henry Lesher - Yay, let's move on. Backstage we see Lethal Weapon getting ready for his match tonight. He is stretching in the locker room when we see Ash passing by. Seeing his opening, Ash attacks Weapon from behind, ramming his head into the locker door. Weapon responds by shoving Ash backwards over the bench and then dives on him, landing punches to the head on him repeatedly. Security is called in to break things up. Shelia Lavin then appears and doesn't look happy.
Shelia Lavin - You two are obviously not being productive tonight. Therefore, you are suspended from action tonight, both of you. Next week you will meet in a steel cage match for the world title. However, you will be barred from the building that night until your match is set to begin, not a moment before. Once you're in the cage, beat the hell out of each other. Good night for tonight, gentlemen.
Gerard Angelo vs Angela "Hell Song" by Sum 41 plays as Gerard Angelo makes his way out from the back. He hears it from the crowd, but not like usual. After all, he is fighting the hated Angela in a match this week and that at least makes him somewhat popular. Angelo makes it to ringside and then takes a clothesline from Angela as she comes in through the crowd. She climbs onto the guard railing and splashes Angelo from there and then stands him up and rolls him into the ring while holding her ribs.
Ray Dees - Angela looking worse for wear from that golden bullet spear earlier. Henry Lesher - Because she simply isn't very good. Angela enters the ring and the bell sounds to make this match official. Angelo gets to his feet, sees Angela holding her ribs and starts putting the boots to her abdomen. Gerard then whips her hard into the turnbuckle and knees her in the back as she staggers out of it. From there, Angelo gives Angela a reverse neckbreaker to the mat. He then goes for the pin. 1...2... Angela manages to kick out. Angelo then stands her up, whips her off the ropes and she ducks a clothesline, then gives Gerard a side Russian legsweep to the mat before going for a quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Gerard Angelo.
Ray Dees - Angela was reaching on that attempt. Henry Lesher - Angelo is a member of the almost Job Squad. Angela then goes for a figure four leglock on Gerard. Gerard kicks her in the back as she turns to lock it in. She hits the corner buckle hard and Angelo rolls her up as she comes out of the corner. 1...2... kick out by Angela. Gerard then gets to his feet, grabs Angela by the hair and takes a shoulder block from her. She then backdrops him to the mat before she grabs her ribs and uses the ropes to hold her up while she winces. Gerard gets to his hands and knees and she starts putting the boots to him. Angela then stands Gerard up and then clotheslines him to the mat.
Ray Dees - Angela might have cracked ribs. Henry Lesher - Who cares? She could be without a head and still beat this guy. Ray Dees - Do you respect anyone? Henry Lesher - I like myself a lot. Ray Dees - Someone has to. Angela waits in the corner for Gerard to get to his feet. He does, turns and takes a kick to the jaw courtesy of Angela's Ashes and he goes down like a rock. Angela then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Angela gets the win and rolls herself out of the ring again holding her ribs. She staggers her way to the back, not taking anytime to celebrate this win at all.
Ray Dees - Hard fought win there. Henry Lesher - Whatever. Backstage, Killswitch and Gothic Angel open the doors to the arena, the walk inside and they are stopped by Joe Davidson.
Joe Davidson - Killswitch, why have you returned? Killswitch - Because no one inspires the terror that the monster does. Joe Davidson - And what's the deal with you and Gothic Angel? Killswitch - She still loves Pyro, and I have work to do. Gothic Angel vs Chastity "Naked Cousin" by PJ Harvey plays as Chastity makes her way to the ring. She high fives the fans on her way in and then slides into the ring. She steps up to the second rope in the corner and poses for the many cameras going off. She blows the crowd a kiss and then we hear "Headstrong" by Trapt and Gothic Angel makes her way to the ring to the boos of the crowd. She shakes her head when she sees Chastity in the ring and tells her that she is not in her league. Chastity motions for her to get into the ring, even holding the ropes open for her. Gothic stands at ringside, her hands on her hips and demands that Chastity back off.
Ray Dees - Chastity showing some nerve here. Henry Lesher - How the hell did this match get so high on the card? Shouldn't this have been shown before we went on the air?
Gothic Angel steps onto the apron and then slowly steps through the ropes into the ring. The bell sounds and Chastity charges in with a series of knife edge chops to the throat of Gothic before whipping her into the corner buckle. Chastity then hip tosses Angel to the middle of the ring before dropkicking her in the back of the neck. Chastity then tries wrapping her up for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gothic Angel. Chastity then stands Gothic Angel up, whips her off the ropes and misses as Gothic Angel ducks a clothesline. Gothic then gives Chastity a DDT to the mat and goes for the pin, hooking the leg. 1...2... kick out by Chastity. Gothic Angel starts having words with the ref about his counting ability as she pulls Chastity to her feet. Gothic whips Chastity shoulder first into the turnbuckle, then gives her a pump handle slam to the mat. Gothic goes for the pin, placing her feet on the ropes for extra leverage. The ref spots it and refuses to count it. Gothic then gets in the ref's face.
Ray Dees - She couldn't have expected the ref to miss that. Henry Lesher - The refs miss far worse than that every day and you know it. Chastity uses the ropes to pull herself up as Gothic Angel continues to argue with the ref. Angel then turns her attention back to Chastity and takes a knee in the gut, then a snap suplex to the mat from Chastity. Chastity then gets to her feet and lands a knee to the forehead of Gothic Angel. Chastity then heads to the ropes and makes her way to the top. Gothic Angel stands, turns and takes a missile dropkick to the chin from Chastity. Chastity then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gothic Angel. Chastity then stands Gothic Angel up and whips her off the ropes. Gothic ducks a clothesline, comes off the ropes and takes a roundhouse kick to the head from Chastity.
Ray Dees - Nice roundhouse. Henry Lesher - If you say so. Looked sloppy to me. Chastity again goes for the pin. 1...2... reversal into a small package by Gothic Angel. 1...2... kick out by Chastity. Gothic then two handed chokes Chastity on the mat. Chastity gasps and then rakes the eyes of Gothic Angel. Chastity uses the ropes to quickly get to her feet and start putting the boots to Gothic again, driving her into the corner buckle. Chastity then whips her to the opposite corner and then gives her a backdrop to the mat. She then does a lionsault onto Gothic and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gothic Angel. Chastity then pulls Gothic Angel to her feet, whips her off the ropes and lifts her onto her shoulder and delivers an F-U! As she goes for the pin, Halloween Jack appears from the back and grabs Chastity by the ankle, pulling her out of the ring to the floor where he clotheslines her down. The bell sounds and Chastity wins by DQ, but Jack is putting the boots to her. Ophelia and Corina come out of the back and Jack backs off, laughing at the girls that have come out to make the save on Chastity.
Ray Dees - I wonder if I was attacked by Halloween Jack if they would come out to save me. Henry Lesher - They might come out to kick your ass since you're an overgrown puss--- Ray Dees - Anyways, moving on... Backstage we see Shelia Lavin shaking her head, Micheal Leary at her side as they watch the monitor.
Shelia Lavin - We'll see how big of a man Jack is when he faces Chastity next week. Micheal Leary - His match is next, too. Shelia Lavin - Yeah, we'll see what happens with that. I had better keep Humanity Crisis under control.
Micheal Leary - Good luck Halloween Jack vs Notorious BLC Halloween Jack rolls into the ring and tells the ref he is set to go. He is still out here from his interference in Chastity's match moments ago. She is out of the area now, but we don't know if she will be back. As Jack tests the ropes and stretches a bit, we hear "Wedge" by Hollow and Notorious BLC makes his way to the ring to the cheers of the capacity crowd. BLC shakes his head as he looks upon Jack inside the ring. BLC steps up onto the apron and then through the ropes and the bell sounds as Jack attacks BLC in the corner. Jack's attack doesn't last long as BLC fights back with a series of elbows to the chin of Halloween Jack, driving him to the center of the ring.
Ray Dees - Main event time, thanks to the wash out of the world title match tonight. Henry Lesher - I hear fans were wanting their money back on account of that until they remembered Ash was the world champ, so it was no big loss.
BLC whips Jack to the corner, then gives him an atomic drop back to the center of the ring. Notorious BLC then picks Halloween Jack up and body slams him to the mat, then delivers an elbow to the throat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Halloween Jack. Notiorious BLC then stands Jack up, whips him off the ropes and kicks him in the gut, then follows that with a snap suplex to the mat. BLC again goes for the pin. 1...2... Jack again kicks out. BLC stands Jack up, then takes a jawbreaker from Halloween Jack which drops him down to the mat. Jack then places BLC in a side headlock and smiles as the crowd boos him. BLC battles back to his feet and shoves Jack into the ropes which forces the break. Jack backs off a foot or two, then delivers a swift kick to the left knee of BLC. He then grabs BLC's left leg, pulls him down to the mat and places him in a figure four leglock. BLC screams in pain, but refuses to give up when the ref asks him. The fans start a chant for BLC.
Ray Dees - BLC in trouble here. Henry Lesher - He was in trouble the moment this match started. He simply isn't good enough to defeat Jack.
BLC tries to turn the hold over as Jack battles to hold it steady. BLC then reaches back for the ropes. His shoulders touch the mat and the ref counts. 1...2... BLC rolls a shoulder up while still reaching with the other hand. BLC then sits up, grits his teeth and starts turning the hold over. Once the pressure is reversed, Jack grabs onto the bottom rope to force the break, the advantage of being closer to the ropes than BLC was. Both men retreat to opposite corners and get to their feet slowly. They then charge at each other. Jack fakes he is going for a shoulder block and instead dives for the knees of BLC, taking him down hard. Jack then slaps on an ankle lock submission. BLC reaches for the ropes, screaming in pain. BLC rolls sideways, then back again. He is then forces to tap out or have his ankle snap and the bell sounds. No sooner has the bell sounded than Frank Merritt jumps over the guard railing at ringside.
Ray Dees - Win for Jack. Here comes Frank. Halloween Jack sees Frank at ringside and smiles. Frank rolls into the ring and starts putting the boots to BLC. He then turns and, for no reason, lands a kick to the jaw on Halloween Jack and gives him the Blunt Wrap in the middle of the ring as BLC rolls himself out of the ring. BLC starts to stagger to the back as Angela and SinmaN race into the ring to try and convince Frank to release Halloween Jack, that they are not against Humanity Crisis. Frank refuses to release the hold, stating he will release it when he is reinstated. "Crescendolls" by Daft Punk plays and we see Shelia Lavin coming out with Micheal Leary at her side.
Shelia Lavin - You want back in, Frank? I can't think of a better punishment than letting you back in, but not next week. No, I have plans for you on this one. At Sunstroke in two weeks, you will face Vesuvius as Scavenger has been deemed unfit to compete at Sunstroke. Have fun against the Mountain, Frank!
"Links 2-3-4" by Rammstein plays as Vesuvius comes out from the back and shoves Shelia and Micheal aside. He takes the microphone from her and talks to Frank on his way into the ring.
Vesuvius - Frank, welcome back. Scavenger will be at Sunstroke. I will beat him. Then I will beat you. It's time to take out the trash.
Vesuvius slides into the ring and he and Frank start going at it toe to toe. SinmaN steps in and takes a boot to the jaw from Vesuvius. Vesuvius clotheslines Angela down as Harmony Fallacia and TJ Mercer race in to take care of Anarchy business. Soon after, security makes their way into the ring area to try and break things up.
Ray Dees - What a finish! That ends our show for tonight! See you next week at our next edition of Assault!
