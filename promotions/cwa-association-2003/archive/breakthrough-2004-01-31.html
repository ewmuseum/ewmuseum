CWA Breakthrough, 31 January 2004

31 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Baltimore, Maryland is jumping as we begin the latest edition of Breakthrough, complete with pyrotechnics and loud music as always. The opening credits this week feature the MobScene, the Minors, Myst, Glamour Girls and Nacho Mauma. We then head to ringside where Romeo Salvador has a new ally, Baby Calaway, as fellow announcer. Inside the ring stands new ring announcer, Donny Rave, set to announce the contestants in our first match here tonight.
TJ Mercer vs Damien "Imaginary" by Evanescence plays as TJ Mercer makes his way to the ring. He receives a warm ovation as he steps up to the apron and waves to the capacity crowd. He steps through the ropes into the ring as the arena darknes and we hear "Alone in the Dark" by Testament playing. Damien makes his way to the ring to the usual booing from the crowd. He appears unaffected by it as he slowly makes his way to the ring, never taking his eyes off the man inside it.
Romeo Salvador - Welcome to the latest edition of Breakthrough as I am joined by my new colleague, Baby Calaway.
Baby Calaway - Yeah, it's awesome being out here, even if it is next to the man who tried to kill me.
Romeo Salvador - I didn't try to kill you. I was trying to date you. Baby Calaway - Demona says you make people get itches when you hug them. Romeo Salvador - What? Baby Calaway - Shhh, the match is starting. The bell sounds and the two men lock up. Damien breaks the lock up and places TJ into an arm bar. He then turns that into a hammerlock and drops onto his back, holding TJ above him for the submission attempt while Damien is careful not to allow his own shoulders to fall to the mat and allow TJ a possible cheap pinfall here. TJ refuses to submit, managing to grab the bottom rope to force the break. TJ retreats to the outside of the ring, holding his sore shoulder as Damien heads out after him. Damien attemptrs to whop TJ into the steel ring steps, but TJ reverses it and Damien's shoulder hits the steel steps hard. TJ then rolls Damien into the ring.
Baby Calaway - The little evil guy's shoulder's gotta hurt right now. Romeo Salvador - I imagine it would. TJ rolls Damien over for the pin. 1...2... From beneath TJ, Damien rolls to the side, plants both feet on TJ's arm and pulls as hard as he can. The ref then changes from counting Damien down to asking TJ if he submits. TJ refuses and quickly gets into the ropes to force the break as the ravenous Damien refuses to let go until the ref informs him of how close he is to being disqualified. Damien gets to his feet, stands TJ up and whips him into the corner. He charges then straight into a boot from the chin by TJ. TJ goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Damien. TJ stands Damien up, whips him off the ropes and Damien ducks a clothesline. He then DDTs TJ to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2.... TJ kicks out.
Baby Calaway - TJ kicks out. Damien kicks out. No winner yet. Romeo Salvador - You're so sugary sweet. Odd after months next to Gloria who is nice and all, but very different.
Baby Calaway - You kissed that Glory Lavin. Don't get ideas of kissing me. Romeo Salvador - You kidding me? Last time I thought about that you ended up in the hospital.
Damien stands TJ up, whips him into the corner and TJ turns and shoulder blocks a charging Damien to the mat. TJ then looks to the crowd and smiles. He pulls Damien to his feet, looks set to power bomb him to the mat only to deliver the Ultimate Sacrifice and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! TJ Mercer with the win here this week. TJ rolls out of the ring as we see a familiar face coming out from the back.
Baby Calaway - That Chase girl is here. Romeo Salvador - We haven't seen Amy Chase since that odd disappearance during her match with Angela some time ago.
Amy looks like she's been in a mud wrestling match as she enters the ring, grabs Damien by the arms and drags him out of the ring and then up the ramp as the crowd cheers her on. We still have no clue what happened to her or what Damien had to do with it.
Romeo Salvador - This is bizarre. Backstage we see Kathy Smith entering the arena. She is approached by new interviewer Ray Dees.
Ray Dees - Kathy, a moment of your time? Kathy Smith - Well look what the probation officer dragged in. Ray Dees - Hey, if you're talking about what happened in '87, I was bird watching and that naked girl's window got in the way. Now, tonight you are here to see Myst against Kelly Buchanan. Your thoughts?
Kathy Smith - To me, it's odd. The new wife and the woman who helped break up my marriage to Shannon. I find this humorous as it is drmamatic.
Ray Dees - You cheering for anyone in the match itself? Kathy Smith - If I was, I don't believe I would tell you. Ray Dees - *winks* Fair enough, gorgeous. Beef Jerky vs Masked Man "The Mexican Hat Dance" plays as Beef Jerky makes his way to the ring to the boos of the crowd. Before the show he came out to tell everyone he might be the new guy, but he knew when people sucked and this crowd sucks. On his way to the ring, he spots a kid in a Rey Mysterio mask. He points and declares this kid is what's wrong with wrestling fans today. He looks to take a swipe at the kid's mask, but security block him. The ref comes out of the ring to warn Beef about these tactics, especially for his first match in the CWA. Beef shouts a warning to the fans in that area before resuming his walk to the ring and then stepping onto the apron. As he enters the ring, hr is attacked from behind by The Masked Man who just emerged from the crowd. The bell sounds and Masked Man picks Jerky up and body slams him to the mat which is impressive enough. He then stands him up, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Masked Man then tries for a camel clutch, but Beef Jerky quickly gets to his feet with Masked Man on his back and then runs backwards into the corner to squash him.
Romeo Salvador - Two men inside the ring here, neither of whom have my vote as nice guy of 2004.
Baby Calway - Come on. Beefy and Masky are new. Maybe they get nervous. I remember one time I got nervous and almost throwed up, but I didn't and then went to a movie and it was scary, but I liked it.
Romeo Salvador - Wow... Beef Jerky stands Masked Man up and gives him a knife edge chop to the chest. He then whips Masked Man off the ropes and places him in a bear hug. Beef holds him up there as the fans' mixed reaction conyinues as it has from the moment the new masked wrestler attacked the man named after a snack treat. Masked Man starts battling out of the bear hug with a series of elbows to the side of the head of Beef Jerky. Masked Man then eye rakes Beef Jerky before elbowing him in the shoulder and the hold is broken. Masked Man then delivers a series of headbutts which drive Beef Jerky to the ropes and then whips him to the opposite corner buckle. Beef comes out of the corner and both men clothesline each other to the mat. The ref starts counting them both down.
Baby Calaway - These two boys just knocked each other silly. Romeo Salvador - You should be on Sesame Street. Baby Calaway - *frowns* They said I was too perky. Elmo was jealous. Romeo Salvador - Wow again. The ref reaches a count of five before The Masked Man shows signs of getting up. He uses the ropes to pull himself up at seven, the same count where Beef Jerky starts getting to his feet as well. Masked Man is up at eight and the count stops. Masked Man then puts the boots to Beef Jerky. Beef then grabs a leg of Masked Man and pulls him to the mat. Beef Jerky then says something about Masked Man looking like a luchadore and he tries to pull off the mask. Masked Man manages to monkey flip Beef Jerky to get him off of him. Masked Man then quickly gets to his feet as we see Angel Peters coming out from the back to the cheers of the crowd.
Romeo Salvador - This should be interesting after we saw the Masked Man there leering at her in a promo this week.
Baby Calaway - That makes him off my Christmas list. Masked Man sees Angel at ringside. He goes to the ropes. Beef Jerky gets to his feet and attacks Masked Man from behind with a double axe handle to the back. Beef Jerky then gives Masked Man a pump handle slam to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Masked Man. Beef then stands Masked Man up, whips him off the ropes and ducks his head and takes a kick to the face from Masked Man instead of landing the backdrop. Masked Man then spits something in the face of Beef which causes him to scream. Masked Man then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Masked Man wins here this week!
Romeo Salvador - The ref saw that. How can he let that slide? Baby Calway - Not very nice, spitting like that. He might give Beefy a cold. At ringside, Angel shakes her head and turns to leave as Masked Man celebrates in the ring. He then screams Pyro's name before we head backstage. There we see Lunacy Muse trying to get into the building. Security is blocking her.
Lunacy Muse - Shannon apologized. I still work here, Drama is over. Guard One - Sorry, miss, we have orders to keep you out of the building. Lunacy Muse - You know I could drive a spiked heel through your head. Guard Two - And you'd be on death row for it. Come on, Miss Muse, you need to go. At this moment, Myst walks by to enter the building. Lunacy calls to her. Lunacy Muse - Hey, Myst, you think your hubby is an angel? You think he's the dutiful hubby? You'll be where I am soon enough!
Myst turns, gives Lunacy a dirty look and then continues towards the door. Lunacy Muse - Tell him I said hi and he was wonderful at the awards show that night! Myst flies into a rage, charging at Lunacy. More security guards keep the two apart as we hear the crowd cheering what they are seeing on the Titantron from the arena.
Scavenger/Nacho Mauma vs Northern Exposure The crowd cheers as "Sanitarium" by Metallica plays and Scavenger walks out with the chair in hand and Lauren leading the way. As the crowd cheers, Spikey Tiger and 6Pac Dudley emerge from the back and knock Scavenger down with a double clothesline from behind. Then then drag Scavenger to ringside and 6Pac rolls him inside the ring. 6Pac holds Scavenger steady as Spikey lands kicks to the chest of Scavenger. "Anything Goes" by Guns 'N' Roses plays as Scavenger takes a beating. Nacho Mauma races down to the ring and slides unde rthe bottom rope. He body slams Spikey. He clotheslines 6Pac. He then superkicks Spikey over the top rope to the floor. He whips 6Pac off the ropes, picks him up and gorilla press slams him to the floor as the bell sounds to get this match underway. 6Pac and Spikey regroup outside the ring while Nacho checks on his partner and Scavenger says he's fine. Scavenger steps out of the ring as Spikey takes his pace in their corner.
Romeo Salvador - Some intense action here. Myst fighting Lunacy outside and this brawl happening inside as these tag teams go at it.
Baby Calaway - Nasty Muse with her nasty words. I hope Myst shows her. 6Pac and Nacho lock up in the middle of the ring. Nacho shoves 6Pac to the mat hard. 6Pac gets to his feet and kicks Nacho in the knee which drops him off his feet. 6Pac then tries for a figure four leglock. He turns around with Nacho's leg in his hand and Nacho uses his free leg to kick 6Pac into the corner. He then rolls him up for the pin when he comes out of it. 1...2... kick out by 6Pac Dudley. Nacho tags in Scavenger as he holds 6Pac by the hair. Nacho holds him open for a kick to the ribs from Scavenger. Scavenger then gives 6Pac a fallaway slam and goes for the pin. 1...2... 6Pac kicks out. Scavenger stands 6Pac up, whips him off the ropes and then backdrops him to the mat. Scavenger then sets in the corner to spear 6Pac. Spikey charges from his corner and manages to grab Scavenger by the hair and pull him to the mat as 6Pac gets to his feet. 6Pac tags in Spikey. Spikey then charges at Scavenger and knees him in the face before he can get to his feet. He then whips him into the opposite corner and gives him a German suplex out of it that he holds for an attempted pin. 1...2... kick out by Scavenger.
Baby Calaway - You maple leaf boys sure do cheat a lot. Romeo Salvador - No we don't. Baby Calaway - Attacking Scavy before the match and then Tigger attacks Scavy from the apron. Shame on you boys.
Romeo Salvador - Tigger? Tiger stands Scavenger up, whips him into the opposite corner and charges straight into a spear from Scavenger. Scavenger goes for the pin. 1...2... 6Pac breaks it up. The fans groan, certain that was going to be a three count. Scavenger stands Spikey up and tags in Nacho Mauma. Nacho then enters the ring, places Spikey in a headlock and then DDTs him to the mat. Spikey's head hits the mat hard and then Nacho rolls him over for the pin. 1...2...3! Scavenger and Nacho Mauma are this week's winners, but Spikey Tiger is not moving. 6Pac enters the ring and motions to the ref they need medics for the fallen member of Northern Exposure.
Romeo Salvador - Tiger is hurt. That DDT hit him bad onto that mat. Baby Calaway - Tigger is not moving. Poor Tigger. Backstage we see Shannon Skanes shaking his head as he storms down the hall. He is headed off by Ray Dees.
Ray Dees - Hey boss, where you headed so fast? Shannon Skanes - Ray, great to see you enjoying your new job, but I have people to deal with.
Ray Dees - Like who? Shannon Skanes - Tony Parizi is trying to hire Lunacy back in another capacity and I need to know what the hell he has planned and if Q is in on it.
Ray Dees - Weren't Lunacy and Tony talking about cutting Q and you out of it? Shannon Skanes - Yeah, like that matters. Ray Dees - So you still don't trust Q? Shannon Skanes - Would you? Myst vs Kelly Buchanan "Dirrty" by Christina Aguilera plays as Kelly Buchanan makes her way to the ring to the cheers from the crowd. It's not as loud as it usually is for her entrance, though. She steps into the ring and tests the ropes as we hear "A Place In My Head" by Linkin Park play. Myst comes out to a huge ovation from the crowd. She smiles and high fives a few fans on her way to the ring. She even stops to autograph an 8X10 a kid holds out for her from the crowd. She then looks into the ring, sees Kelly and she nods. She says something to herself and then slides into the ring. The bell sounds as Myst sits in the corner accessing the situation as if this is the point of no return.
Romeo Salvador - The debut of Myst is here. It's been talked about for quite some time and now the moment is here.
Baby Calaway - Misty is really gonna be somethin'. I know it! Myst gets to her feet and the two girls lock up in the middle of the ring. Kelly shoves Myst into the corner. Myst frowns, comes out of the corner and slaps Kelly across the face. Kelly slaps Myst. Myst lands a solid right hook to the jaw of Kelly which drives her to the corner. She then monkey flips Kelly to the center of the ring as the crowd cheers her on. Myst gets to her feet, waits for Kelly to stand and delivers a shoulder tackle on her. She then goes for the pin. 1...2... Kelly Buchanan manages to kick out. Myst then pulls Kelly to her feet by the hair. She whips her off the ropes and backdrops her to the mat. Myst heads to the ropes and climbs to the second rope. She lands an elbow to the back of Kelly from there.
Romeo Salvador - No pent up anger in Myst at all. People in the back might want to take notice.
Baby Calaway - Her nose is all scrunchy. That's how I know she's mad. Myst then stands Kelly up, whips her off the ropes and gives her a dropkick to the mat. Myst then stands Kelly up and takes a thumb to the eye. Kelly then gives Myst a snap suplex to the mat. Kelly follows this up with a leg drop and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Myst. Kelly then stands Myst up, whips her into the corner buckle and then gives her a spinning power slam out of it. Kelly again tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Myst yet again as the fans cheer. Kelly then rolls Myst out of the ring as this match looks to be getting ugly.
Romeo Salvador - Kelly's getting angry here. Baby Calaway - I don't think she 'spected a fight out of Misty like this. Kelly stands Myst up, whips her towards the ring steps. Myst manages to halt her forward momentum, jumps onto the steps and turns. She then launches herself from there and clotheslines Kelly to the floor. As Myst rolls herself into the ring, we see Kathy Smith appear on stage. The crowd cheers this as well. Cheerful crowd, indeed. Kelly gets to her feet and rolls herself into the ring slowly. She is placed in the STF by Myst!
Romeo Salvador - The Mystifier is locked in! Kelly reaches for the ropes, but she is unable to grab them as Myst leans back further to put more pressure on the neck and back of Kelly. Kelly s forced to tap out and Myst is the winner!
Baby Calaway - Way to go, Misty! Myst rolls herself out of the ring with her arms raised. Kathy applauds from the stage and starts to leave. Myst catches sight of Kathy and shakes her head. However, she still finds time to shake hands with and high five fans on the way to the back. Backstage we then see Dante and the crowd boos as Charley MacAdam stands next to him to interview him.
Dante - Nice to be here. Charley MacAdam - You retired. Why are you here? Dante - Shannon called. Charley MacAdam - Shannon called? Dante - You deaf and dumb? Yes, he called. Now, be gone. He waves Charley off and then makes his way to Shannon's office down the hall. MobScene vs The Minors Alexander Wesson and Eryk Smith are led to the ring by Minx as "Natural Born Killas" by Dr. Dre and Ice Cube begins to play. The crowd appears to be happy to see them as they have been to see many of the stars here tonight. They make their way down the ramp and step up onto the apron and then inside the ring. They don't have much time to enjoy their time inside the ring as The Minors come out without any music or fanfare whatsoever. They simply mean business and Benny and Mike mean this match will be a tough one for the MobScene to win indeed. Benny steps through the ropes as Smith leave Wesson in the ring to start the proceedings. The bell sounds and the two teams lock up in the center of the ring as the crowd cheers.
Romeo Salvador - Enthusiastic crowd here to say the least. The main event has been reached.
Baby Calaway - Don't tell me that. I don't want my first show to be over. It's fun even if you are the guy who hurt me and stuff.
Wesson places Benny in an arm bar. Benny eye rakes Wesson, then reverses the arm bar and kicks Wesson in the chest three times. He then arm drags Wesson to the mat and turns the hold into a hammerlock. The crowd cheers as Benny applies the hold deeper and the ref asks for a submission. However, Wesson refuses to submit and instead grabs the middle rope with his free hand. The ref forces the break and Benny allows Wesson to his feet. Benny then gives Wesson a monkey flip into his corner where Mike is waiting and clocks Wesson with a right hook. Benny rolls Wesson up for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Alexander Wesson.
Romeo Salvador - Nice teamwork by The Minors there, showing what brothers can do. Baby Calaway - I have a sister and we tag and we win a lot and it's cool. Romeo Salvador - I think we all knew that. Baby Calaway - Yeah, it's the best. Benny tags in Mike. They whip Wesson off the ropes and try for a double clothesline which Wesson ducks. Wesson then lariats both of the Minors to the mat. He reaches for the tag as Eryk Smith leans in as far as he can to try for the tag. Wesson leans, but Mike Minor has him by the ankl;e as Mike gets to his feet. Wesson stands on one leg, but he does a spinning heel kick with the other leg to break Mike's hold on him. Wesson then dives to his corner and tags Smith in. Smith enters the ring and clotheslines Benny, then backdrops Mike before throwing Benny out of the ring over the top rope to the floor. Smith then whips Mike into the corner buckle and then gives him a dropkick to the back as he stumbles in the corner. He then tries to roll Mike up. 1...2... kick out by Mike Minor! Smith stands Mike up, whips him off the ropes and tries a dropkick, but Mike Minor sidesteps it.
Romeo Salvador - Nice ebb and flow thus far to this match up this week. Baby Calaway - That sounds good. Ebb and flow. We should make that a tag team. They'd win a lot.
Mike stands Smith up and whips him into the corner. He then gives him a overhead suplex to the mat. Mike then tags Benny in. They stand Smith up and give him a double boot to the face. Smith hits the mat hard and Benny tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Eryk Smith. Benny stands Smith up, whips him off the ropes. Smith ducks a big boot from Benny and then gives him a reverse neckbreaker. He tries for the pin. 1...2... Mike Minor breaks things up. Smith tags in Wesson. Smith holds Benny Minor up for a Doomsday Device, revisiting the Legion of Doom days. Mike Minor charges into the ring and knocks Benny off Smith's shoulders to prevent the move. Wesson dives from the top rope, landing a clothesline on Benny Minor from there. Mike and Smith get into a lock up and fall through the ropes. Wesson sets Benny up on the middle ropes. He then races across the ring, comes back and lands a 6-1-9! He then lands a lion sault on Benny and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Smith and Wesson have done it again!
Romeo Salvador - Don't look now, Dark Ice Symphony. There's a new tag team force in the CWA.
Baby Calaway - This is what they call upset tummys. The Minors have upset tummys and so do their fans.
Romeo Salvador - That's a way of putting it. Well, we are done this week. See you next week for another edition of Breakthrough.
Baby Calaway - And hug your teddy bears and have lots of lollies. Red ones if you've got them!
Romeo Salvador - Uh, yeah... good night.
