CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 29 December 2003

29 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
St. Paul, Minnesota plays host to tonight's Assault. We see a few New Year's wishes from the corwd, most of which involve divas from the CWA roster. We then head to ringside where we are ready to get the action underway.
Angela vs Amy Chase "Sweet Leaf" by Black Sabbath plays and the crowd gives Angela a huge ovation as she makes her way to the ring. The crowd appears quite enthralled with her as she steps through the ropes and waves to the audience. We then hear "Pass That Dutch" by Missy Elliott and Amy Chase makes her way to the ring, looking a bit nervous.
Henry Lesher - Amy looks like a deer caught in headlights. Let's shoot her. Ricky Simms - On that note, we welcome you to Assault. We have five action packed matches for you this week.
Henry Lesher - How do you know they are action packed? Watched them already? Amy steps through the ropes and is attacked by Angela from behind. The bell sounds and Angela whips Amy Chase off the ropes and lands a sidewalk slam on her. Angela then comes off the ropes and cartwheels onto her. Angela goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Amy Chase. Angela stands Amy up, whips her off the ropes and Amy ducks an elbow. Amy then dropkicks Angela to the mat and quickly places her in a headlock.
Ricky Simms - Amy looks to be establishing herself here. Henry Lesher - A headlock is not a sign she has established anything. Amy turns the headlock into a sleeper. Angela immediately grabs the ropes and forces the break. The lights then go out and the crowd murmurs about what is going on. When the lights come back on, a fire begins burning inside the ring where Amy Chase was. Angela backs away in horror. The ref calls for the bell, awarding the decision to Angela as she leaves the ring with a look of fear in her eyes.
Ricky Simms - What the hell just happened? Henry Lesher - Something cooler than cinder blocks, for sure. Ash rushes out from the back to accompany Angela to the back. The crowd is hushed at what they just witnessed, not sure what to make of it. We then head to the back where Jon Shocker is bouncing off the walls.
Jon Shocker - She knew someone was stalking her! She knew something was gonna happen! That proves it! She never cut a damned promo all week out of fear! We need to find her!
The camera pans over and Night Dweller looks calm, pulling his shades down slightly. Night Dweller - She's around. I'll find her. He then gets up and casually makes his way out of the room with Shocker throwing chairs up against the wall.
Romeo Salvador vs Giovanni Mabius "PIMP" by 50 Cent plays as Romeo Salvador makes his way to the ring. He receives an ovation as he high fives fans and makes his way to the ring. He steps through the ropes and looks to the camera and has a few words for Pyro. He points to his waist and states he is the next Aerial champ as we hear "California Love" by Tupac play and Giovanni Mabius makes his way to the ring. Mabius gets a less warm reception as he enters the ring and then attacks Romeo from behind while Romeo has a few words with the ref. Mabius then whips Romeo off the ropes and catches him with a clothesline. Mabius struts around the ring and poses for the crowd. He then stands Romeo up and nails him in the throat with three knife edge chops.
Ricky Simms - Romeo starting to feel the effects here of the punishment from Mabius. Henry Lesher - Romeo is a walking joke. Pyro will kill him. Ricky Simms - Gotta love the unbiased reporting around here. Mabius whips Romeo off the ropes. Romeo ducks a clothesline and then Romeo lands a series of kicks to the front and back of Mabius. Romeo then knees Mabius in the gut and gives him a double axe handle to the back, knocking him to the mat. Romeo then picks Mabius up and body slams him before landing a knee drop to his throat and going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Mabius. Romeo then stands Mabius up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Romeo heads for the ropes and climbs to the top. Mabius then gets to his feet, grabs Romeo while he is on top and delivers a body slam from the top rope on him. Mabius then heads to the ropes and starts to climb to the top himself. Romeo gets to his feet, staggers into the ropes and knocks Mabius off the top rope to the floor awkwardly.
Ricky Simms - That looked a lot like the shot he landed on Baby Calaway by accident a month ago.
Henry Lesher - Proves it wasn't an accident then. The ref heads outside the ring and takes one look at Mabius' leg and then calls for the bell. Romeo wins by ref's decision.
Henry Lesher - Cheap wins abound for Romeo, but a win is a win. Ricky Simms - He doesn't look happy, but at least he is on the winning track heading into Breakthrough's match up with Pyro. That should prove to be an interesting one indeed.
The crowd applauds the effort of Romeo as he heads to the back and we head backstage. We see Night Dweller going door to door when he happens upon a storage closet where Amy Chase's shirt hangs when he opens the door. This catches the usually calm and collected Dweller off guard. He takes a look at the shirt. There's blood on it.
Night Dweller - This is bad. Ash/Max Cameron vs PB FUBU/Spikey Tiger "The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson plays as Ash comes out to the ring with Max Cameron close behind, and regarding Ash with suspicion. The two step onto the apron, try to step through the ropes at the same time and collide. They then start having words, step away from each other and enter the ring at opposite ends of the ropes. We then hear "Eye of the Tiger" by Survivor and Spikey Tiger makes his way to the ring with PB FUBU close behind waving a Canadian flag. Spikey slides into the ring and is attacked by Max Cameron as Ash pulls him off and declares that he's supposed to attack first. Max shoves Ash into the ropes and Ash lays Max out with a lariat. Ash then stands Spikey up and whips him off the ropes as Max rolls himself out of the ring. Ash levels Spikey with a shoulder block and then has a few words for Max outside the ring. Max takes a step towards the back as if meaning to leave. Instead, he heads to his corner and takes his place there.
Ricky Simms - Ash taking the bull by the horns here as he and Max make an unlikely tag team.
Henry Lesher - I have learned that if I don't care about guys here long enough that they will align with each other.
Ash stands Spikey up, looks to make the tag to Max and then drops his hand and gives Max the finger instead of making the tag. Max steps partway into the ring and the ref blocks him off. Ash applies a two handed choke on Spikey while the ref is distracted. Ash releases the choke when the ref turns his attention back to the match at hand. Ash then whips Spikey into the corner and charges into a big boot to the face. Ash falls to the mat hard as Spikey tags in FUBU. FUBU charges into the ring and lands a series of kicks to the chest of Ash as Ash struggles to get up. FUBU then whips Ash into the ropes and levels him with a dropkick.
Ricky Simms - Northern Exposure is definitely working better as a team thus far. Henry Lesher - That may not matter. FUBU stands Ash up, then whips him into his own corner. Max elbows Ash in the back of the head and tells the ref that's a tag. Max then enters the ring and attacks FUBU with a double axe handle to the chest. Max then picks FUBU up and launches him across the ring with a huge slam. Max then waits till FUBU gets up and lands a kick to the jaw on him before picking him up and giving him a spinning powerslam and going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by FUBU. Max stands FUBU up and takes a jawbreaker from FUBU. Max falls back and takes a kick to the side of the head from Ash which he claims is a tag. Ash enters the ring and Max rolls out cursing under his breath. Ash locks up with FUBU who then turns Ash around and backs him into their corner where he tags in Spikey Tiger. Spikey comes in and lands kicks to the chest of Ash in their corner which drops him off his feet. Spikey then boot chokes Ash against the ropes. He does this, tags in FUBU and then whips Ash off the ropes as FUBU jumps to the top rope. FUBU lands a flying side kick to the head of Ash as he comes off the ropes. Ash drops onto his back and crawls towards the corner. Max kicks Ash in the face to make the tag.
Ricky Simms - Nice tags made here so far. Henry Lesher - I am actually enjoying this. Max enters the ring and dropkicks FUBU to the mat. Max then picks FUBU up and lands a choke slam on him. Max then pulls FUBU to the corner and signals to the crowd that this match is over. He lands a MP3 on FUBU and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Ash and Max Cameron win an improbable match up!
Ricky Simms - I cannot believe they won that. Henry Lesher - I can. I just don't care. Max and Ash exit opposite sides of the ring. They don't even acknowledge each other as they head to the back. Backstage we see Damien in a dark corner. Joe Davidson approaches him.
Joe Davidson - Damien, we haven't seen much of you lately. Damien - You'll see a lot of me soon. Joe Davidson - You;re here on the same night as Amy Chase went missing. Questions are being asked.
Damien - Cheap pyrotechnics and drama, not the way of Damien. Joe Davidson - So you don't know what is happening? Damien - Oh I never disavowed any knowledge of what's happening. Perhaps Dark Forces has bit off more than they can chew.
Joe Davidson - I guess that's the final word on that. Damien - It is. Now leave me be. Reality vs Eternal Nightmares "Harder Better Faster Stronger" by Daft Punk plays and Q and Shelia make their way to the ring. They look like they are still put out that Q isn't champion and Q grabs the microphone from the ring announcer when he steps through the ropes.
Q - Dante! Tonight you will pay for taking my title as the CWA will pay for handing it to you! I have just made a deal with the Mafia!
"Come With Me" by Puff Daddy plays and Tony Parizi comes out to a chorus of boos with a microphone in his hand.
Tony Parizi - You little douchebags little up! The Mob has watched the crap here long enough. Q will be the owner and that's because that ungrateful Shannon Skanes screwed us out of his company! That will not happen again!
Tony heads to the back as "Know" by System of a Down plays and Dante comes out from the back with Fallout at his side and Adam Lewis walking close behind him. Dante has a few words with Q as he makes his way to the ring. He holds the world title high and garners more negative reaction than even Q did and that's saying something.
Ricky Simms - Major tag battle here. Henry Lesher - My dreams continue to come true as these two great united fronts come together.
Ricky Simms - More unbiased announcing. Henry Lesher - You wish you were me. Ricky Simms - I will not justify that with a response. Dante steps into the ring with Shelia as Q and Fallout take their places in their respective corners and Adam Lewis lurks at ringside. The bell sounds and Shelia and Dante lock up. Dante shoves Shelia into the corner and smiles. Shelia comes out with a superkick to the jaw and an attempt at a quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Dante. Shelia stands the stunned Dante up, whips him off the ropes and takes a shoulder block which knocks her to the ground. Dante tags in Fallout, stands Shelia up and whips her off the ropes and level her with a double dropkick. Fallout stands Shelia up and whips her into the corner and then lands a shoulder block on her while she's there. Shelia responds with a thumb to the eye of Fallout and then rakes his eyes along the top rope. Shelia then pulls Fallout to her corner and tags in Q. Q steps over the top rope and slams an elbow to the back of Fallout's neck. He then tosses Fallout into the corner and demands for Dante to make the tag. Dante slaps the shoulder of Fallout and enters the ring. He enters the ring and starts going at it, toe to toe, with Q. Dante starts gaining an advantage and drives Q into the corner. Q then wheels Dante through the air into the corner and lands a series of body shots before giving him a bionic elbow to the forehead.
Ricky Simms - This feud is just going to grow from this match. Henry Lesher - If there's a God it will, yes. Q then whips Dante to the opposite corner before charging in and landing an avalanche on him. Dante falls face down to the mat and Q rolls him over for the pin. 1...2... Dante gets a foot on the bottom rope. At ringside, Adam Lewis cheers wildly and Q flashes a dirty look at him. Q then pulls Dante to his feet, whips him to the ropes and readies to clothesline him. Instead, Dante holds onto the rope and pulls himself out of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Looks like Dante wants a breather. Henry Lesher - Hope he's not outside for too long that he gets counted out. That would be a shame.
Q doesn't stay inside the ring and wait for Dante. He slips under the bottom rope and starts pursuing him around the ring. Adam Lewis steps into Q's way and is quickly shoved aside. However, Fallout then dives onto Q from his corner and starts laying a beating on him before he has a chance to react. Dante then stops his retreat and comes back to help his partner. As they start double teaming Q, Shelia comes in from behind Dante and kicks him in the back, then turns him around and DDTs him to the floor. She then takes a kick to the head from Fallout when she tries to turn her attention to him. Q then spears Fallout to the floor. The ref reaches a count of ten and calls for the bell. This match is a double count out.
Henry Lesher - The feud grows. This is a thing of beauty. Ricky Simms - Yeah, it's wonderful. Backstage we see Shannon Skanes with a smile on his face. He is headed off by Charley MacAdam.
Charley MacAdam - Shannon, have you heard about the return of Tony Parizi to the ownership picture?
Shannon Skanes - Yep. Charley MacAdam - Any reaction to that? Shannon Skanes - I just came from the doctor's and I am medically cleared to wrestle. Let them do whatever the hell they want.
Charley MacAadm - Sounds great. Shannon Skanes - So tell Parizi I'll be coming for him soon enough. The Moose vs Jon Shocker "If You Want Blood" by AC/DC plays and The Moose comes out to a huge ovation from the crowd. He smiles and high fives a few fans on his way down the ramp and to the ring. As he steps into the ring and checks the ropes, we hear "Phenomenon" by LL Cool J and we see Jon Shocker making his way to the ring with his bright blue leather jacket on and his diamond encrusted shades as he struts to the ring and the fans cheer. Shocker steps through the ropes and takes off the shades. He then hands the shades to the ref and tells him he needs them before he nods at Moose. Moose looks ready to pound on him as the bell rings. Shocker starts taking his jacket off and then kicks Moose in the gut. Shocker drops the jacket and then whips Moose off the ropes and lands a spinebuster on him before looking to the lights and letting out a scream.
Henry Lesher - What a freak. Ricky Simms - He is unorthodox, for sure. Shocker stands Moose up and whips him off the ropes and then lands a backdrop on him. Moose is then stood up by Shocker and whipped into the corner. Shocker charges into the corner and headlong into the knee of Moose. Moose then pulls Shocker to the mat by his hair and then lays the boots to him. Moose then stands Shocker up and gives him an inverted atomic drop followed by a headbutt which knocks him back down to the mat. Moose tries for the cover. 1...2... kick out by Jon Shocker. Moose stands Shocker up, whips him off the ropes and lands a clothesline on him which causes him to flip in mid air and land face down on the mat. Shocker is then pulled to his feet by Moose who lands a series of rights and lefts on him which drive him into the corner buckle. Moose then monkey flips Shocker high in the air and then he drops hard to the mat. Moose then gets to his feet and places Shocker into a figure four leglock.
Ricky Simms - Nice move there by Moose. Henry Lesher - How can this be the main event? Were the Shmelkys busy? The ref asks Shocker if he gives up and Shocker says no as the fans cheer him on. Shocker reaches for the ropes, but is mere inches too far away from them. Shocker then clenches his fists and finds the energy to lunge and grabs the ropes to force the break. Shocker rolls himself out of the ring and Moose follows him out. Moose throws Shocker into the guard railing, then gives him a DDT on the floor as the ref counts them both out. Moose rolls into the ring to break up the count and then rolls back out. Moose stands Shocker up and tries to whip him into the steel steps only to have Shocker reverse it and instead whip Moose into the steps. Shocker then picks Moose up and slams him on the floor before rolling into the ring to beat the count. Shocker then rolls back out of the ring which starts the count over again as the ref starts to scream at them to return to the ring. Shocker stands Moose up with the intention of whipping him into the steel corner post. Instead, the move is reversed and Shocker hits the steel post hard. Moose then rolls Shocker into the ring before entering the ring himself.
Ricky Simms - Moose really starting to look like he is in control of this one. Henry Lesher - It's only Jon Shocker. It's not like he's beating Dante or something. Moose stands Shocker up, then looks set to nail him with the Time Bomb. Shocker must sense this, because he starts punching furiously at the abdomen of Moose and then rakes his eyes before landing a stunner on him. Shocker goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Moose. Shocker stands Moose up, whips him hard into the corner buckle and then shoulder blocks him while he's there. Shocker then gives Moose an inverted atomic drop and headbutts him to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... Moose reverses the pin and rolls Shocker into a small package. 1...2... kick out by Shocker. Shocker then gets to his feet quickly, waits for Moose to get to his and then clotheslines him to the mat. He then heads to the ropes and climbs to the top. Moose gets to his feet and turns, sees Shocker diving and moves out of the way. Shocker lands a superkick from the top rope to the chin of the referee.
Ricky Simms - Dear God! That ref is out! Henry Lesher - Like they do anything anyways. Shocker and Moose lock up. From outside the ring, we see Dante enter. He smashes Moose in the back of the head with a chair. Shocker has a few words for Dante, but Dante manages to swing a chair shot to his head as well. He then lays Moose's arm across the chest of Shocker and waves to the booing crowd as he leaves the ring. The ref comes to somewhat, sees Moose's arm across Shocker and makes the count. 1...........2.............3! The Moose with a Dante aided win.
Ricky Simms - What a great world champ he is. Henry Lesher - I was just going to say that without the sarcasm. Ricky Simms - With that, we are out of time. See you next week for another edition of Assault!
