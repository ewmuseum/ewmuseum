CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 15 March 2004

15 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Louisville, Kentucky is lively as usual as we begin our latest edition of the CWA show known as Assault. This being two weeks before Terror in the Aisles makes it all even more tense than it might be on another night. The opening credits roll and we see Flock, EOS, Q, Anarchy Inc. and Demona Calaway before we head to ringside where the action is ready to commence.
Ash vs Scavenger "Sanitarium" by Metallica plays as Scavenger makes his way to the ring with the full support of the crowd behind him. Scavenger steps through the ropes into the ring and waves to his fans as Ophelia takes her place at ringside. Calvin Kool is also there, not too far from Ophelia. "The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson starts to play and we see Ash making his way to the ring which draws the ire of everyone present. Angela leads him to the ring and tells the camera she will be holding up a title of her own here tonight. Ash then races into the ring and he and Scavenger start battling toe to toe as the bell sounds to get this underway.
Ricky Simms - Big match here tonight between these two to start things off as we decide the number one contender heading into Terror in the Aisles.
Henry Lesher - Yeah, that makes this major. Change the channel. Maybe there's something else on.
Ash whips Scavenger hard into the corner buckle. Ash then avalanches Scavenger in that corner and lands an atomic drop on a staggering Scavenger right after that. Ash then clotheslines him to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Scavenger. Ash then stands Scavenger up, whips him off the ropes and then backdrops him to the mat. Ash follows this up by heading to the ropes, heading to the top rope and giving the fans a finger before diving off and attempting a five star frog splash. Scavenger rolls out of the way and Ash crashes to the mat hard. Scavenger then tries rolling him up for a quick pin. 1...2... Ash kicks out. Scavenger then gets to his feet and Ash gets to his. The two start trading knife edge chops. Scavenger starts to overpower Ash, driving him to the ropes. Ash responds with a thumb to the eye of Scavenger. He then whips him hard into a nearby buckle and lands a series of kicks to the chest of Scavenger, leaving him fallen in the corner and looking winded. Ash then heads to the middle of the ring, gives Scavenger the finger and races in and lands a bronco buster on Scav.
Ricky Simms - Ash taking over here. Henry Lesher - How does Scavenger qualify as a contender to anyone? I mean, really, the guy is a loser.
Ash drags Scavenger out of the corner. He then sets him up for and delivers a DDT on him and then tries for the pin. 1...2... Scavenger just rolls a shoulder out to prevent the three count. Ash then stands Scavenger up, whips him hard into the corner and attempts a clothesline which Scavenger ducks. Scavenger then picks Ash up and gives him a power slam and tries for the pin. 1...2... Ash kicks out. Ash then rolls himself out of the ring with Scavenger close behind. Scavenger grabs Ash by the hair. Ash turns and gives Scavenger a jawbreaker. He then starts pulling the mats back from the concrete flooring. Scavenger gets to his feet and takes a headbutt from Ash. Ash then sets him up as if he is about to DDT him onto the exposed concretre. Instead, Scavenger shoulder blocks Ash into the guard railing. Scavenger then rolls himself into the ring with Ash close behind and they both manage to beat the count, despite the pain they are in. Ash pulls a length of chain from his tights which he hides from the ref. The fans see it and let Ash know they don't approve. Ash turns and tells them all to shut up while concealing the chain. He then turns back to Scavenger.
Ricky Simms - Dirty tactics here by Ash. Henry Lesher - Whatever it takes to end this thing. Scavenger eye rakes Ash. Ash drops the chain. Scavenger spots it, picks it up and drills Ash in the head with it in plain sight of the ref who then calls for the bell. Scavenger has been disqualified. While Scavenger argues over this, Dragon and SinmaN appear from the back and attacks Scavenger inside the ring. Ophelia tries to enter the fray only to take a chair shot from Angela. Dragon and SinmaN level Scavenger with a series of kicks and punches while a helpless Calvin Kool pulls his hair out at ringside. Dragon and SinmaN then smile as Ash grabs a spray paint can from the corner and sprays "420 Flock" on Scavenger's reddened back. They then all put the boots to him one more time before leaving together. The crowd boos them loudly.
Ricky Simms - Haven't seen them do that in awhile. Henry Lesher - Yeah, it's been done. Backstage we see Noah Skanes entering the building with the CWA Warriors Title over his shoulder. He is approached by Hank Roberts.
Hank Roberts - Noah, a lot has been said towards youir family this week by Flock. Now you're becoming vocal on the matter. Why now?
Noah Skanes - Hank, I am a champion and, as such, I feel it is my job to defend my family and my wrestling home when I need to. It just so happens that I need to.
Hank Roberts - Anything to say to Wren tonight? Noah Skanes - No, I prefer to do my talking inside the ring, thank you. Max Cameron vs Kenji "Lightning Rod" by Offspring plays as Kenji makes his way to the ring. He has a very solemn look on his face as he enters the ring. He even paces as he awaits his opponent, Max Cameron. "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC plays to signal the arrival of Max Cameron and Kenji stops pacing when Max emerges on stage through a green curtain of smoke. Max then quickly makes his way to the ring and has a few words to the camera about Flock dominating tonight's show. Max slides into the ring and the bell sounds as Kenji starts putting the boots to him. Kenji then stands Max up, attempts to whip him off the ropes only to have Max reverse it. Max then clotheslines Kenji to the mat. Max leg drops Kenji and tries for the quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Kenji.
Ricky Simms - Another big match for Flock here. Henry Lesher - How do you figure? Is Kenji a major star or something, because if he is I missed it.
Max stands Kenji up, hip tosses him into the corner. Kenji lands on his feet, turns and gives a round house kick to a surprised Max Cameron's jaw. Max falls and Kenji goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Max Cameron. Kenji then stands Max up, whips him into the corner and then gives him a headscissors takes down to the mat. Kenji then heads to the ropes and quickly leaps to the top. He attempts a moonsault, but he lands on the raised knees of Max Cameron. Max then rolls Kenji into a small package for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kenji.
Ricky Simms - A couple of near pinfalls there. Henry Lesher - If only you got an electric shock for every time you announce near pins as if they matter...
Max gets to his feet first, headlocks Kenji and then gives him a snap suplex. He follows this up by standing Kenji up and giving him a gutwrench suplex and then goes for the pin again. 1...2... Kenji manages to kick out. Max then stands Kenji up, whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him to the mat. Max then turns to the crowd and poses for them. As he does, Kenji kips up to his feet. Max turns his attention back to Kenji and takes a series of headbutts. Kenji then monkey flips Max into the corner. Kenji then places Max into a sleeperhold. Max struggles to reach for the ropes, but Kenji simply pulls him away from them. Kenji locks the hold in and Max appears to be starting to fade. From the back we see Dragon coming towards the ring. The crowd again boos him.
Ricky Simms - This guy doesn't know how to stay in the back. Henry Lesher - True enough. However, he hasn't shown an ability to win since he came back either.
The ref checks Max Cameron and Max's arm drops once. The ref checks and it drops a second time. Dragon jumps up onto the apron. The ref goes to get him down off of there. SinmaN then slides into the ring out of the ref's sight and clubs Kenji over the head with a chair. He then rolls the almost out cold Max onto the fallen Kenji before sliding back out of the ring. Dragon drops off the apron. He and Sin then head to the back as the ref turns his attention back to the match and counts the pin. 1...2...3! Max Cameron picks up the win here as Flock does it again.
Henry Lesher - Two matches and two Flock wins. So far, so goos for these guys. Ricky Simms - Not wins the way we like to see them, but they are wins nonetheless. Backstage we see Calvin Kool with Scavenger in the parking lot. Calvin Kool - Come on, Scav. You have to reconsider. Scavenger - There is nothing to reconsider. I made up my mind. I am gone. This place can kiss my ass.
Scavenger gets in his car and drives off with Ophelia next to him. Deep Freeze then enters the picture shaking his head.
Freeze - Shame about Scavenger leaving like that. There goes your meal ticket, huh Calvin? Calvin Kool - I have back up plans. You just wait and see. Calvin walks off and we see Jon Shocker waiting for him while Freeze stands there and shakes his head.
Demona Calaway vs Angela "Stained" by Android Lust plays as the Women's champion makes her way to the ring. Demona Calaway gets more cheers than usual tonight which is probably based on the fact she is fighting a member of the hated Flock. Demona enters the ring and holds her belt high. As she does, we hear "LOVEFURYPASSIONENERGY (Fuck your Rules Man)" by Boy Hits Car and the challenger emerges from the back. Angela is booed louder than usual. It seems her days as being seen as the lesser of two evils between herself and Ash are over. She has a few words with one unruly fan at ringside wearing a Demona T-shirt. She then makes her way up the ring steps and into the ring. The bell sounds and the two begin to circle each other.
Ricky Simms - This match for the women's title. Henry Lesher - Also known as the Whogivesadamn title. Demona and Angela lock up. Angela starts pushing the defending champ into the ropes. The ref asks for a break. It's almost a clean break, but Angela's knee lift to the gut of Demona ends all chance of that. Demona then eye gouges Angela in response. Angela then shoulder tackles Demona through the ropes and to the floor. The ref has already lost control of this one. Angela stands Demona up and tries to whip her into the steel corner post. Demona stops, reverses and Angela's head bounces off the steel corner post. Demona rolls herself into the ring to break up the count and then rolls right back out. She then picks Angela up and body slams her on the floor before landing a series of punches to the now bloodied forehead of Angela.
Henry Lesher - Blood makes bad matches better. Ricky Simms - You always know the right thing to say. Henry Lesher - Well, yeah I do. Demona stands Angela up and attempts to smash her head into the ring steps. Angela blocks it, then elbows Demona in the gut before ramming her face into the ring steps. Angela then rolls Demona into the ring. Angela pulls herself into the ring and then places Demona in a Boston Crab. Demona reaches for the ropes, but is a few feet too far away to have any hopes of grabbing them. Demona luncges, but it's no use, so she starts pushing herself up off the apron. Angela tries to drop her weight on Demona to force her back down, but Demona continues to fight as the fans cheer her on. Demona then walks on her hands to the ropes and grabs the bottom rope to force the break. Angela reluctantly releases the hold only when threatened to be disqualified. Angela then turns to Demona and takes a kick to the front of the right knee from Demona. Demona senses weakness and lunges for that leg and slaps on an ankle lock.
Ricky Simms - This match has been more vicious than standard women's matches. Henry Lesher - Wait till Wren and Myst fight later tonight. Then you are going to see vicious.
Angela scrambles in the ankle lock to find the ropes and she does. From the back we again see Dragon coming with SinmaN at his side. The fans are really hating this now. Dragon and SinmaN don't get far. Leech and Moose attack them on the ramp and the four men start going at it. Angela gets free of the ankle lock, but looks up on the apron and is distracted momentarily by the action going on there. Demona then elbows Angela in the jaw three times before landing the Darkness Surrounding on her and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Demona wins and retains her title! Demona rolls herself out of the ring, grabs her belt from the timekeeper and holds it high for the fans to see as they cheer her on. Demona then heads to the back while Angela gets to her feet and looks shocked at what just happened.
Ricky Simms - Is this when we quote Meat Loaf and say two out of three ain't bad? Henry Lesher - I won't, but you're stupid enough to do it. Backstage we see Rated R with Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - Ryan, next week begins the quest for the Commonwealth title. Your thoughts as you face Beef Jerky in round one?
Rated R - Well, obviously it's going to take a lot of work for whoever gets that belt. There are fifteen other wrestlers in this, but I am confident in my ability to get ahead.
Joe Davidson - And how is life within Anarchy treating you? Rated R - It was the best decision I could make. Tonight we add another member and get even stronger.
Joe Davidson - Any hints as to who the new member will be? Rated R - Not from me there aren't. Myst vs Wren "Move" by Ludacris plays as Wren SilverPhoenix makes her way to the ring to the boos of the crowd. She had a lot to say this week and it would appear that the crowd didn't like it. She then steps into the ring and poses on the second rope as cameras go off. She turns and has a few words for guesr ref Kenji as well. We then hear "A Place For My Head" by Linkin Park and Myst appears from the back on a dead run for the ring. She slides into the ring and the brawl is on as the bell sounds! Myst starts smothering Wren with her title belt as Kenji struggles to gain control. The two ladies then roll out of the ring and start choking each other on the floor.
Henry Lesher - As I said. Ricky Simms - Wren made this match intensely personal and even drew the attention of the owner's son here tonight.
Henry Lesher - Wren got under Shannon's skin numerous times and now she appears content to do the same to his wife.
Wren rolls over on top of Myst and starts raining down punches on her. Myst raises her hips and spins sideways to throw Wren off. Myst then gets to her feet and pulls herself into the ring. Wren slowly gets to her feet and then enters the ring as well. Myst gets to her feet inside the ring first and lands a series of kicks to the gut of Wren. Kenji has a smile on his face as if this is great fun for him to watch. She then picks her up and body slams her to the mat. Myst then boot chokes her, using the ropes for added leverage. She breaks that choke before the ref calls for a DQ. Myst then stands Wren up and snap mares her to the middle of the ring. Myst comes off the ropes and dropkicks a sitting up Wren in the face. Myst then goes for the pin. 1...2... Wren manages to kick out. Myst then stands Wren up, whips her into the corner. Wren moves out of the way of a charging Myst and watches as she nails her shoulder against the steel corner post. Wren then applies an abdominal stretch on the defending Aerial champ and goes for the submission.
Ricky Simms - Big momentum swing there into the challenger's favor. Henry Lesher - Wren has held that title before. She intends to hold it again and if it pisses Shannon off in the process, she considers that a bonus.
Myst starts to power her way out of the abdominal stretch even as Kenji checks for a submission. Wren struggles to hold on and grabs the ropes for added leverage. Kenji forces a break of that by kicking Wren's hand off the rope and Wren has to allow Myst free. Wren then headlocks Myst and DDTs her to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... Myst manages to kick out. Wren stands Myst up, whips her into the corner. She then cartwheels and tries a back elbow only to find nobody home in the corner. Myst thumbs Wren in the eye. Myst then gives Wren a vertical suplex to the mat. Myst tries for the pin. 1...2... Wren manages to get free of it. Myst then stands Wren up, picks her up and delivers a Canuck Drop which lands in a cradle pin! 1...2... Wren kicks out at the last possible second!
Ricky Simms - Myst delivering her husband's finisher on Wren, but Wren managed to kick out.
Henry Lesher - Myst delivered it better than Shannon does, as well. Wren gets to her feet as Myst appears to be stalking her. Myst lands a side Russian leg sweep on Wren and then slaps on the Mystifyer! Wren reaches for the ropes. The crowd cheers. Wren reaches... reaches... and then starts to tap out as the pain is too much for her. Myst retains the title as the bell sounds! Kenji raises her arm in victory and shakes his head as he looks down at the fallen Wren.
Ricky Simms - Wren taps in the Mystifyer! Henry Lesher - That won't go over well with Wren. As Myst celebrates, the arena's sound system comes to life and begins to blare "I Am Hell" by Rob Zombie. Whipping her head towards the curtains, Myst looks for KillSwitch. Taking advantage of the situation, Wren shoves Myst hard into the ropes and then clubs her in the back with a forearm sending her crashing to the mat. Dropping down on top of Myst, Wren quickly locks in a STF. Looking down at the two women, Kenji just smiles and shrugs his shoulders. Suddenly from out of the crowd Max Cameron appears and hurdles the security railing with a wooden folding chair and slides into the ring. On the mat, Wren quits yelling at Kenji, smiles and points behind him. As Kenji turns to look with a look of confusion, Max swings the chair and it shatters against Kenji's face. Kenji drops like a bag of rocks with blood streaming down his face. Wren smiles as she keeps Myst locked in her own hold, the fans booing her and starting to throw objects at her inside the ring. Wren finally releases the hold and poses for the cameras. Looking at Wren, Max smiles and holds out his hand and the two shake like long lost friends. Now cocking his head, Max waits as Wren smiles and slowly pulls off her shirt revealing what is underneath...A FLOCK T-SHIRT! Sliding out of the ring together, the pair head back and straight into the fury of a charging Noah and Shannon Skanes!
Ricky Simms - Oh boy, the cavalry is here! Henry Lesher - This will turn out bad. Shannon picks Max up and drops him on audio equipment next to the ramp. Noah tosses Wren over the guard railing into the crowd. The two then enters the ring to attend to Myst, wife and mother, who won but took a beating after the win. This fight is not over, not by a long shot.
Ricky Simms - Flock has got quite the feud going with the entire family now. Henry Lesher - What will Noah and Shannon do now? I mean, Shannon last called on Q and Dante. Maybe he'll be smart and call on Q again or maybe talk Dante into coming back.
Backstage we see Vigilante with Ray Dees. Ray Dees - Vig, what is your reaction to what we just witnessed, Wren going Flock? Vigilante - You know, I hate it. I hate what I am seeing out there and tonight these people are making me mad enough to do something about it. Tonight I beat Vesuvius and next week I face and defeat KillSwitch. At Terror in the Aisles, I then get Ash again and I will kick his ass. However, I am going to raise the bar once again while all of this is happening.
Ray Dees - How? Vigilante - Ray, you're a freak and not a good freak like Samantha Johnson. No, I am not going to tell you anything.
Dragon vs Harmony Fallacia "El Cu Cuy" by Coal Chamber begins to play as Dragon makes his way to the ring. He is booed louder every time he heads to the ring. He slides into the ring and gives the crowd the finger as he steps up in the corner onto the second rope. He then climbs to the top, somersaults off and lands on his feet in the center of the ring. As he stands ready, we hear "Heads Explode" by MOnster Magnet and Harmony Fallacia makes her way to the ring wearing an Anarchy shirt and carrying a cream pie in her right hand. She hands it to a little kid in the crowd and then races into the ring as the bell sounds. The two go at it toe to toe with Dragon grabbing Harmony by the hair and tossing her through the ropes and to the floor. The crowd boos as Dragon ascends to the top rope. Dragon leaps off, landing a scissor kick to the back of Harmony's neck and driving her face first to the floor.
Ricky Simms - A lot of heat between these two. Henry Lesher - A lot was said towards the end of the week and these two are simply going to tear each other to pieces in there.
Dragon stands Harmony up. He then tries to whip her into the guard railing. Harmony elbows Dragon in the gut and then whips him into the guard railing instead. Harmony then rolls herself into the ring. Dragon gets to his feet and follows moments later and one moment before he would have been counted out. Harmony delivers a standing side kick to the jaw of Dragon, then stands him up and gives him a snap suplex to the mat. Harmony then comes off the ropes and lands a knee drop to Dragon's forehead and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Dragon. Harmony then stands Dragon up, whips him off the ropes and delivers a lariat on him in the middle of the ring. Harmony again tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Dragon yet again. Harmony then stands Dragon up. Dragon picks Harmony up for a side to side suplex. He then jumps to his feet, heads to the corner and makes his way to the top rope. He dives off for a flyign knee only to find Harmony rolling out of the way before he gets there. Harmony then uses the ropes to pull herself to her feet.

Harmony eye rakes him right back. Harmony whips Dragon off the ropes, then drops her head to try for a backdrop. Dragon kicks Harmony in the face, then picks her up and throws her over the top rope and to the floor. Dragon then climbs to the top rope. Harmony then gets to her feet and stands over Dragon. She then makes her way to the Spanish announce table and starts to clear the monitors and papers off of it. Dragon gets to his feet slowly. Harmony grabs him by the hair, brings him over to the Spanish announce table and delivers a series of elbows to the head of Dragon. He then rakes her eyes. She headbutts him and rams his head into the table. Harmony picks Dragon up and slams him on top of the table. She then climbs onto the apron and then onto the second rope from outside. Harmony lands a leg drop which drives Dragon through the table as the ref reaches a ten count and count both wrestlers out.

Gloria Lavin - I have seen enough. Harmony, you have all that energy to spend? Next week you apend it against Angela inside that ring. Dragon, you have done nothing but stir up shit backstage and in the ring all night. I have seen enough. You're suspended until the week after Terror in the Aisles. Have a nice vacation.

Backstage we then see Ash throwing stuff around the locker room over that announcement. Apparently he is not taking kindly to the leader of Assault suspending one of his stablemates. Another portion of backstage then shows Kenji sitting with an icepack against his forehead when Vesuvius enters the picture.
Vesuvius - Rough night out there, Kenji? Kenji - *nods* I've had better. Vesuvius - You think over my offer? Kenji - *pauses* You know what? I have. Anarchy has a new member. The two shake hands. Vesuvius - Good choice, my friend. The group no one pays attention to grows yet again. Rated R vs MLSQ42 "I Hate Everything About You" by Three Days Grace plays as Rated R makes his way to the ring. He receives a strong reception as he holds 4 fingers high, signifying that his group just gained another member.

Rated R makes his way into the ring and the crowd cheers as he poses in the corner. He then removes his T-shirt as we hear Tomoyasu Hotei's "Battle without Honor or Humanity" and Q makes his way to the ring to the cheers of the fans. It's still baffling to hear this guy being cheered when he has really changed nothing about himself or softened his stance on anything. Rated R then does a springboard moonsault onto Q from the ropes and again tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Q. Rated R then stands Q up. Q punches Rated R hard in the gut. Q then headbutts Rated R into the ropes, then whips him off the ropes and delivers a thundering boot to the chest as he comes back to the middle of the ring. Rated R follows. Q punches Rated R in the forehead and then picks Rated R up and drops him on the railing. Rated R's throat slingshots off the guard railing as fans in that area wince. Q goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Q grabs the win here.

Moose then whips Frank off the ropes and spears him to the mat. Moose then heads to the ropes, climbs to the second rope and then drills a knee into the chest of Frank Merritt before going for the pin. 1...2... Frank kicks out.
