:: CWA Rules and Regulations CWA Rules and Regulations Read the rules and obey the rules and that will mean more fun for everyone here short term and long term.
1. Respect others. No attacking opponents in roleplays is tolerated. Please also refrain from negative talk on the boards towards any one person. I will allow arguing to a point, but there is a time to just shut up and play the game. If we wanted a fight, we'd go to a chat room.
2. Role play at least once a week to prove you want to be booked. Not roleplaying will result in you not being booked. I will also take you off the boards as a member due to prolonged inactivity. This is about a three week period. If you haven't posted anything at all for three weeks, no point in keeping you on the mass e-mail lists.
3. Original wrestlers only. No real wrestler names. Ralph Van Dam does not qualify as an original wrestler name.
4. Losing happens. If you do not agree with a result, take it up with people on the staff here. Don't whine OOC or make a big stink that causes divisions amongst us. I have been through a lot of this. A loss does not mean your life ends. It means you get another shot next week. I went months in a fed once never winning a match and then was rewarded for my effort with a title run. It does happen that way sometimes. Prolonged arguing and whining will result in your firing and I hate firing people.
5. Racist remarks are not tolerated. Various other slanders will probably gain you a lot of losses, so try to make your role plays intelligent arguments rather than slam fests riddled with foul language. There's no word filters on the boards, but there's no "Best Swearing" awards given out here either. This carries over to the OOC boards as well. Calling someone a "fag" whether be it a member or a WWE star or whatever is not going to go over here. Have some tact please. Threas get locked or pulled because of this.
6. World title shots are not given to people who whine a lot for them. Work hard and your shots will come soon enough. Nothing makes me see someone as less of a champion than constant begging for a title shot or a better feud. This all goes to the whole whining thing. I don't reward complainers and whiners. I reward those who have come here to play a game. My champions defend nearly every week, so I don't hide the belts either.
7. Make your role plays longer than 10 lines at the very least or welcome to jobber land. If you need any help in creating longer RPs, let me or some of the more experienced talent here know and we'll be happy to lend a helping hand. I accept people new to the fed world and am willing to help any way that I can.
8. Role play limits are 3 per week for Assault and Breakthrough and 5 per pay per view. Now, recently there has been talks about people posting half a role play and then the other half much later. If you are posting and RP in halves, please post the halves within a half hour of each other. Otherwise, it makes it look like you tried to make 2 role plays into one massive RP. Save it to word pad if you're not finished it and then post it as one RP once you finish it later. It saves some confusion for voters and saves the thread from being held up when your opponent(s) might be wanting to post.
9. Enjoy yourself. And, if for any reason you are not enjoying yourself, let someone know and we can work with you. It's very frustrating to have someone up and quit or blow up and not tell anyone why.
10. This is a game. Please see it that way. This is meant to be fun. If you're here to have fun and see what you can do against members of this roster, you'll do well.
