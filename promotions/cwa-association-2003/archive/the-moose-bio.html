The Moose (bio)

The Moose 6’6” 265 Hometown: Baltimore, Maryland Music: If You Want Blood(You Got It)by AC/DC History: The Moose grew up in the suburbs of the Baltimore - Washington D.C. area. He has no idea how he got the nickname but now it's simply the name he goes by. He was discovered fighting in toughman contests in sleezy inner harbor bars while still in college. Moose was noticed by respected Japanese wrestler Taki Yokomitzu. Yokomitzu took Moose under his wing and trained him in mulitple wrestling styles adding to his power based style. He then tagged with another promising young wrestler and won many tag titles on the independent scene. He was then hired by
Clash of The Champions Wrestling, made an immediate impact by gaining the hardcore and world championships on his 2nd and 3rd weeks with the company. He later combined the titles after taking over the company.
The Moose bounced around different feds all over the globe until staying with IWC for an extended period of time capturing every title as a member of The Main
Events. Growing tired of having the other members talk for him despite being the world champion he left the group. The ensuring war between the members left entire arenas ruined. He became disillusioned with the business after this. It was around this time that he retired to Baltimore and opened a small tavern near the harbor.
He resurfaced in the bingo halls and auction barns of Nevada gaining the world title while drunk on St. Patrick's Day in Sierra Championship Wrestling. His old friends brought him back as he joined the ranks of
UCW during a war between owners, as a joke he was awarded the People's Title, a belt made out of cardboard, but it became a coveted title until UCW merged with XDZ where The Moose wrestled his way to the top then had the world title screwed away from him by the owners. After being banned from the ppv Moose jumped the guardrail and went about destroying the new champion.
He was fired and went to Japan. It was here where he sustained a devastating knee injury during a tag team death match which took him two years to rehab from,
Almost three years from his injury he signed with CWA and stepped into the ring on Carnage. The Moose has made a name for himself in the CWA through brutal matches with Leech and by winning the CWA Hardcore
Title at Sunstroke. Titles Held: COTCW: 2x Hardcore, World ECW: Tag w/ Blue Bomb & Blue Nova SCW: World WWWC: 2x Hardcore, Tag w/ The Wolf(As Magnum Force) IWC: IC, Tag w/ Rabies, World UCW: Peoples, Hardcore XDZ: Hardcore, World MWF: World, Tag w/ The Wolf Various Independent Tag Titles w/ The Wolf(As Smith & Wesson) 5 Time King of The Deathmatch CWA: Hardcore Injuries: Cracked Ribs Blown out Knee Broken Fingers Various Burns Barbed Wire Cuts Broken Foot Multiple Concussions Finisher: The Moosedriver(Reverse Death Valley Driver)
