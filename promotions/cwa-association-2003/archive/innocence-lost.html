Innocence Lost

undated (captured 2 August 2003)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This group of ladies is the first all female stable in the CWA and has a diversity going for it others may not have. Kathy Smith and Samantha Johnson have been fighting each other for years. Kristen Burren and Robyn Connor have been fighting the forces of darkness together for years. What began as a tag team between Kristen and Robyn has become a strong stable now, one with a bright future.
Height: 5'6" Weight: 125 Hometown: Minneapolis, MN Alignment: Face Finisher: Twist of Fate Titles held: None Music: "My Favorite Game" by Cardigans History: Samantha was a rookie of the year years back, but she always felt she got the short end of the stick in the results column and soon began to work as an announcer. She was treated as the standard T+A vehicle instead of as a wrestling talent and her time in the CWA as an announcer was more humbling than her wrestling experiences had been. She had briefly been a women's champion, but that was all forgotten until the day she saw what her reputation was and knew she had to return to the ring to reclaim her reputation and make the gains she never could before. Now she's free to go as high as her talent will take her. Time will tell how far that is.
Height: 5'7" Weight: 130 Hometown: Edmonton, AB Alignment: Face Finisher: Bombshell - Kathy's version of the Bubba Bomb Titles held: None Music: "Twilight Zone" by Golden Earring History: Kathy was the women's champ for nearly a decade through numerous title reigns and feuds. She also became the wife of the owner, Shannon Skanes. This led to numerous problems as Kathy's title reigns were sometimes seen as nepotism. However, no one could argue her talent then and few would argue it now. She's now teaming with three girls and leading them, she hopes, to the promised land of gold. Gold belts, that is. She is also chomping at the bit for the women's title to make a comeback.
Kristen Burren Height: 5'7" Weight: 135 Hometown: Powell River, BC Alignment: Face Finisher: Walker Breaker - top rope spinning power bomb Robyn Connor Height: 5'5" Weight: 125 Hometown: Vancouver, BC Alignment: Face Finisher: Robyn has not come up with a finisher yet as she is still learning the craft. History - These two have been together for years. Their years together have not been fun and games. Kristen has an ability to sense things around her as does Robyn. This is more acute with Kristen, though. She knows when danger is coming and senses things from people. She is psychic, but gets impressions of the future, not so much a clear picture of things about to happen. Kristen came to the CWA to escape the demons of the past. Robyn followed her here. The demons from the past also followed and made an appearance at an actual CWA event! Now those events appear to finally be passed, but there's no telling if they really are. These two are the original Innocence Lost, which have now grown to four.
