SinmaN and Viper are both pointing to SinmaN's shoulder and complaining to the camera about the Superior title belonging there and their need to get that back as soon as possible. SinmaN then races into the ring and slides under the bottom rope and straight into a brawl with Lone Star as the bell sounds to get this going.
Ray Dees - Finally a good meaty match here between a very good wrestler who BLPW shafted and a very good wrestler the CWA and BLPW have shafted. It is amazing how often wrestling companies treat people like crap.
Henry Lesher - Yes, but many people in the business are crap. Lone Star and SinmaN exchange knife edge chops to the chest. SinmaN rakes the eyes of Lone Star. Lone Star lands a hard right hand to the jaw of SinmaN which staggers him and backs him into the ropes. SinmaN comes back with a kick to the gut of Lone Star. Lone Star gives SinmaN a forearm smash to the chest. Lone Star then headlocks SinmaN and tries for a bulldog, but SinmaN pushes him off and into the corner buckle hard. Lone Star stumbles out of the corner and SinmaN rolls him up from behind for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Lone Star. SinmaN then stands Lone Star up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him over the top rope to the floor. He then kicks Lone Star through the ropes and the ref warns SinmaN against this, pushing him back to the center of the ring. SinmaN argues against this, and while he does that, we see Viper sneaking around the ring to where Lone Star is. Lone Star sees Viper coming and lands a short arm clothesline on him to the floor. Lone Star then rolls himself back into the ring. SinmaN takes a shoulder tackle from Lobe Star and Lone Star goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by SinmaN.
Ray Dees - One gets the feeling that Lone Star really hates SinmaN and Flock in this match.
Henry Lesher - Flock is an easy target. However, they are the closest to a faction I can support that this show has.
Lone Star stands SinmaN up, whips him into the corner buckle and then lands a DDT on him when he comes out of it. He then signals that he is ready to finish this match. He heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. SinmaN stands and turns to face the missile dropkick from Lone Star. Lone Star kips up to his feet. He then jumps to the top rope and lands the Falling Star on SinmaN and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Lone Star pulls off a win here over SinmaN.
Ray Dees - Is that an upset? Henry Lesher - If you're SinmaN, you're damned upset yeah. Backstage we see a very upset Frank pacing. He looks at Wren and nods at her. They have seen enough of this.
Ray Dees - What the hell are those two up to? Henry Lesher - What I want to know is why the hell Wren is always on Assault when she is supposed to be on the Chaos roster.
Ray Dees - Maybe Baby Calaway was traded for her. Henry Lesher - Nice thought. Ash vs Tommy Air "The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson plays and Ash, the new World champ, makes his way to the ring. He receives the usual chorus of boos from the crowd. He also appears to be glad to be back in the US, commenting on it to the camera on his way to the ring. Again, he is a Flock member travelling alone and that has had mixed results here tonight. Ash makes his way up the ring steps onto the apron, looks out into the crowd and shakes his head at their comments fired towards him. As he steps through the ropes into the ring, we hear "Smells Like Teen Spirit" by Nirvana and out comes Tommy Air to a huge ovation. Ash rolls his eyes inside the ring as Tommy makes his way down, high fiving the fans all the way to the ring. Ash then tells one ringside fan to just shut up. Tommy slides under the bottom rope into the ring, then gets to his feet as the bell sounds and this match is underway between the top two guys on Assault.
Ray Dees - This should be interesting. Henry Lesher - Maybe it will be, but I sort of doubt it. The two lock up. Ash breaks that with a knee to the chest of Tommy. He then headlocks Tommy and bulldogs him to the mat. Ash then lands a series of rabbit punches to the back of Tommy Air's neck. Ash then stands Tommy up, whips him off the ropes and then hip tosses him high into the air and over the top rope to the floor. Ash then steps through the ropes and stands on the apron. Tommy gets to his feet only to take a kick to the jaw from Ash on the apron. Ash then scissor kicks Tommy to the back of the neck from there. Ash again rabbit punches Tommy to the back of the neck on the floor. Ash then stands Tommy up and rolls him into the ring. Ash pulls himself up onto the apron and then steps through the ropes. Ash puts the boot to Tommy, then pulls him to his feet and whips him into the corner. Ash then charges into a straight right kick from Tommy Air. Tommy quickly headlocks Ash and gives him a springboard bulldog to the mat. Tommy tries for a quick pin here. 1...2... kick out by the champ. Tommy then comes off the ropes and lands a rolling thunder on Ash and again tries for the pin. 1...2... Ash again kicks out to keep this match going.
Ray Dees - Tommy with a strong comeback here. Henry Lesher - Which is worth nothing to me. We need something to happen to shake this up. From the back we see Chastity coming to the ring. The crowd cheers her on as she leads the rest of the Glamour Girls to ringside, all of them dressed in elegant evening gowns, many sequin covered.
Henry Lesher - Not what I had in mind. Ray Dees - I always have that in mind. Tommy stands Ash up, whips him off the ropes and Ash ducks a clothesline. Ash then gives Tommy an elbow to the ribs follows by a vertical suplex to the mat. Ash then looks at the girls around the ring and starts to laugh. He tells Chastity she isn't worthy of a title shot and her win at Blind Rage meant nothing to anyone as he stands Tommy up and whips him hard into the corner buckle. Ash charges in for a shoulder block, but Tommy moves out of the way and Ash lands hard in the corner, nailing his shoulder to the corner post. Chastity and the girls applaud from ringside. Tommy appears to be a bit perplexed by this, looking around at the girl at ringside. The crowd boos when they see Wren SilverPhoenix coming down the ramp from the back. Shelia Lavin is following almost immediately behind her and appears ready to dish out some punishment on Wren this week for the beating Flock gave her a couple of weeks ago. Before she can get far, Frank Merritt attacks Shelia from behind, then gives her a power bomb on the ramp before he and Wren continue to ringside and evening gown clad girls scatter with the exceptions of Ophelia and Chastity. Inside the ring, Tommy is still watching this so Ash waits for him to turn and face him and plants him with the Pipe Cashing. He goes for the pin on Tommy. 1...2...3! Ash gets the win here over a distracted Tommy.
Henry Lesher - Those strippers are a menace. Ray Dees - But a hot one. Henry Lesher - It's time for them to pay. Outside the ring, Chastity and Ophelia show no sign of backing down from Frank and Wren, desopite a size disadvantage and the fact they aren't exactly dressed for wrestling. Ash slides out of the ring behind them with a smile on his face. He drills Chastity in the back of the head and Wren clotheslines Ophelia down to the floor. Frank then stomps on both girls as The Flock deals with things in its own little family way. Shelia Lavin is motionless still on the ramp as EMTs come out to attend to her. They'll soon be needed by Chastity and Ophelia. Frank power bombs Ophelia on the floor first, then power bombs Chastity second and stands over them both smiling. Wren high fives Frank and Ash. Backstage we see Charley MacAdam in the office with Lunacy Muse.
Charley MacAdam - Lunacy, we just witnessed a horrible attack on the Glamour Girls and your co-GM.
Lunacy Muse - Yes, it pains me to see this stuff happening. However, I cannot ban all of Flock. That will hurt business. Instead, Frank and Wren are suspended from ringside until further notice. Ash claims that Chastity doesn't belong in the ring against him. Next week he proves that when he defends the world title against her. That should about do it.
Charley MacAdam - I guess that about does. We head back to ringside where Flock has now left and EMTs are attending to the three fallen women in the area.
Ray Dees - That was sick. Henry Lesher - That almost made up for the rest of the show. Ray Dees - That does it for us. See you all next week.
