The Salt Lake City crowd cheers loudly as they see the show it about to begin. Another huge pay per view is about to come to pass and we are anxious to see who will be the winners and losers this week in these epic battles for supremecy in the CWA. We then see the lights dim and we hear "Into the Void" by Nine Inch Nails plays. Kathy Smith leads Kristen Burren, Samantha Johnson and Robyn Connor to the ring. All four are dressed in black and all look very elegant. Kathy leads the way into the ring and the crowd catcalls them as they steps through the ropes. kathy then grabs a mic.
Kathy Smith - What am I doing, you ask? In the past week I have been seen with my ex husband talking about managing him and been asking Vesuvius about what his future will be and now here I am with these ladies? What gives? Allow me to enlighten the CWA faithful. You'll notice that not one of us were booked this week and this marks the second pay per view in the last three that Samantha Johnson wasn't booked for and in the other one she fought me. What gives?
Kathy hands the microphone to Samantha. Samantha Johnson - I was robbed of matches here many times over and robbed of a place in the pay per views around here. So who has it in for me? Apparently the execeutives in the back don't think I have it in me to be one of the elite around here. Allow me to say this is a load of crap and the four of us deserve a place here as much as anyone else does. Next month is Animosity. By then we expect to be shown as the top contenders that we are. I expect another shot at that Cruiserweight belt and I expect to see any combination of us getting a shot at the tag team belts. Believe me, we have the drive and the talent and we are set to make things happen.
Samantha then hands the microphone to Robyn Connor who gets a huge ovation. Robyn Connor - I was gone for a few weeks, all tied up in things beyond my control. That is over and Innocence Lost grew during my time away. This is a major day, people, marking the rise of an all female stable in the CWA and I am proud to be a part of it. Starting next week, Innocence Lost is heading straight to the very top!
The fans give the four a huge ovation as they make their way out of the ring. The four of them join hands at ringside and raise them in mock victory since they aren't booked here tonight.
Henry Lesher - My, what a way to kill the beginning of another pay per view. This is very troubling indeed.
Ricky Simms - Don't they have a point? Henry Lesher - It's not their point that the fans were drooling over and the point to me is that I do not care.
Pyro shoots from every direction of the stage area as we hear "(I Hate) Everything About You" by Three Days Grace playing to mark the official beginning of Maximum Impact! We see the Flock stepping out of a green limo together and giving the one fingered salute to the camera as they go by. Flock is in the house and ready to fight apparently. As the song continues to play we see that there is someone in the rafters, but we cannot see who. The cameraman tries to zoom in and get a better shot, but we fail to make out who that person might be. That is open to speculation.
