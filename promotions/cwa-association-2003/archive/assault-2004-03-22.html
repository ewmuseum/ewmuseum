CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 22 March 2004

22 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's another full house as we begin our Orlando, Florida show of Assault. We see Shannon entering the building with the Entourage close behind him. The crowd cheers and Joe Davidson approaches the CWA owner.
Joe Davidson - Shannon, there is lots of specualtion of something big going down tonight. Shannon Skanes - I am becoming the hardcore champ, if that's what you mean. If it isn't, I don't care.
Joe Davidson - But about EOS... Shannon Skanes - Right, a group founded on the ideal of getting rid of me in name, I believe? Next time you come to me with a rumor, make it believable.
Rated R vs Beef Jerky The Mexican Hat Dance plays as Beef Jerky makes his way to the ring with his arms raised above his head. He has a few of his standard run ins with fans on his way to the ring. He then resumes making his way down to the ring and steps through the ropes and frowns as "I Hate Everything About You" by Three Days Grace plays and Rated R starts making his way to the ring to the cheers of everyone here in Florida.
Ricky Simms - Rated R gaining fan support now that he is a member of the Anarchy group. Henry Lesher - Why do wrestlers care about the fans? It's not like they can hop into the ring and help and, if they did, they'd make the wrestler look even more pathetic no matter the outcome.
Rated R steps through the ropes and then ducks a clothesline from a charging Beef. The bell sounds to start the match as Rated R arm drags Beef to the center of the ring. Rated R then stands Beef Jerky up, whips him into the corner and Beef lands a big boot on Rated R from there. Beef then spears Rated R to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Rated R. Beef stands Rated R up, whips him into the corner buckle and then avalanches him while he's in there and smiles as Rated R staggers out of the corner before face planting in the middle of the ring. Beef then rolls Rated R over and again tries for the pin. 1...2... Rated R again manages to kick out.
Ricky Simms - Beef taking full advantage of the size advantage here. Henry Lesher - Well, when you have as little talent as Beef, you have to use whatever you can find.
Beef stands Rated R up. He whips him off the ropes. Rated R ducks a clothesline, then headlocks Beef and DDTs him to the mat. Rated R then stands Beef up, headlocks him and delivers a strong vertical suplex on the much larger Beef Jerky. The crowd cheers in appreciation, but Rated R grabs at his back as if he pulled a muscle in that effort. Rated R then comes off the ropes and lands an elbowsmash to the throat of Beef and tries for the pin. 1...2... Beef manages to kick out. Rated R then stands Beef up. Beef kicks Rated R in the back of the leg that Rated R was just favoring. Beef then grabs that same leg, pulling Rated R off his feet. Beef tries for an ankle lock. Rated R kicks his way free. Rated R then somehow manages to quickly get to his feet. Beef charges straight into a clothesline from Rated R.
Ricky Simms - Rated R appears to have pulled his hamstring there. Henry Lesher - Beef's too stupid to take advantage of it. Rated R heads to the ropes and climbs to the top. Beef slowly gets to his feet, turns and takes a flying shoulder block from Rated R. Rated R goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Rated R wins.
Ricky Simms - Rated R moves on to the quarter finals for the Commonwealth title next week! Henry Lesher - Wow, top eight for a secondary title. That's amazing. As Rated R celebrates, we see Vesuvius backstage applauding with Kenji, Noah and Harmony around him.
Vesuvius - This is how we build champions. We build as a group and we achieve alone.... unless Flock is involved. *turns to Kenji* You can take Ash down, right?
Kenji - I shall take Ash down. Vesuvius - And lovely Harmony has Angela all tied up, right? Harmony Fallacia - Silly V, but of course. Vesuvius - And Noah? Noah Skanes - Wren is no one, a mere speck to me. Flock shall not beat any of us this night.
Charley Moore vs SinmaN A man with a bongo drum comes out and starts playing and this means that the plumber is on his way out. Charley Moore starts making his way to the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd, a mix of cheering and sheer revulsion at his appearance. Those in the front row were happy to be so close to the action until they got downwind of this guy. Some cover their noses as he passes by. As Charley makes his way into the ring, "Sinner" by Drowning Pool plays as SinmaN makes his way to the ring. He shakes his head as he looks at Moore inside the ring and asks whether or not they could have found him a real opponent as he looks directly into the camera. Sin then steps up onto the apron and then into the ring as the bell sounds to get things underway.
Ricky Simms - Another match in the Commonwealth tournament here, the last before Dizz and Nathan Cristion decide the final position in what is being termed as a pretty strong grudge match.
Henry Lesher - Dizz being back could destabilize the whole battle for power in the CWA. I mean, the main event tonight is huge in that way and I never care about matches here. However, if Vigilante wins and pushes KillSwitch out for a month, that hurts Flock a lot. If it goes the other way, it could cripple EOS.
SinmaN charges at Moore when the bell sounds and tackles him to the mat and then lands a series of punches on the fallen plumber. SinmaN then stands Moore up, whips him into the corner and then backdrops Moore as he stumbles out of it. SinmaN shouts something to the crowd who was already booing him loudly. SinmaN then stands Moore up. Moore rakes the eyes of SinmaN. Moore than whips SinmaN off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Moore tries for a quick pin. 1...2... SinmaN kicks out. SinmaN then rolls himself out of the ring with Moore close behind. Moore rams the head of SinmaN into the railing. SinmaN then lands a knife edge chop to the chest of Moore. He then picks Moore up and drops him over the guard railing. SinmaN then rolls himself back into the ring, arms raised in victory as the ref starts the count again.
Ricky Simms - SinmaN confident of his win here for the Flock. Henry Lesher - Flock is in almost every damned match tonight. It harkens back to the NWO days when WCW started going down the crapper.
Moore gets to his feet and barely beats the ten count back inside the ring. Moore lands in a headlock from SinmaN. SinmaN delivers a snap suplex on Moore. SinmaN then places Moore into a sharpshooter. Moore reaches for the ropes as SinmaN leans further back to increase the pressure. Moore screams and then taps out, which gives SinmaN entry into the second round for the Commonwealth title.
Ricky Simms - That was a great win for SinmaN, clean and quick. Henry Lesher - How clean could it be against Charley Moore? Oh well, at least it's over. Ricky Simms - SinmaN moves on to the round of eight in the running for the Commonwealth title next week at Terror in the Aisles.
Henry Lesher - I try to muster up the energy to care, but I don't care. Backstage we see Vigilante punching the heavy bag when Ray Dees enters the picture. Ray Dees - Vig, champ, a moment of your time if you will? Vigilante - Yeah, what do you want? I have a match to get ready for. Ray Dees - Vig, if you lose tonight you are gone for a month. What plans have you made? Vigilante - Ask KillSwitch. He's the one who is going to be packing his bags, not me. Ray Dees - If you do find a way past KillSwitch, you face Ash next week at Terror in the Aisles.
Vigilante - Okay, correction, WHEN I find a way past KillSwitch, I move on to defeat Ash again at Terror in the Aisles. I'll admit they have improved, but I am perfection and nothing can beat that.
Vig then returns to his work out and Ray leaves the room nodding his head. Kenji vs Ash "Lightning Rod" by Offspring plays as Kenji makes his way to the ring. He is accompanied by Skinny and the two talk to each other on their way down the ramp, Anarchy T-shirts on them both. Kenji gets a pat on the back from Skinny and then makes his way into the ring. "The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson plays and Ash, the CWA's number one contender, makes his way to the ring. He hears it from the crowd and shouts back at a few of them. He then says something to the camera about SinmaN being the man tonight and then steps onto the apron and into the ring. Kenji launches an uncharacteristic attack on Ash before the bell sounds. Kenji delivers a fisherman's suplex on Ash and then a leg drop before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Ash. Kenji then stands Ash up, whips him off the ropes and lands a spinning heel kick to the chin of Ash. He then kicks him in the gut and then gives him a Pedigree and again goes for the pin. 1...2... Ash again kicks out.
Ricky Simms - Very unlikely attack here by Kenji. Henry Lesher - He's in Anarchy now. It appears to have helped his attack. Kenji stands Ash up and then whips him through the ropes to the floor. Kenji then climbs to the top rope, waits for Ash to reach his feet. Kenji launches himself for a flying cross body. Instead, Ash catches Kenji and then gives him a spinning power slam to the floor. Ash gets to his feet with a smile on his face as the crowd boos him once more. Ash then rolls Kenji into the ring and pulls himself in with the aid of the ropes. Ash stands Kenji up, whips him hard into the buckle and then bulldogs him out of it before trying for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kenji. Ash then leg drops Kenji and heads to the ropes. Ash climbs to the top rope and lands a flying headbutt on Kenji from there. Again, he goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out again by Kenji.
Ricky Simms - Kenji proving to be tough to beat this week. Henry Lesher - Shame he is still going to lose. Dante said the other members of Eternal Nightmares suck donkey balls and he has been right thus far.
Ricky Simms - I'm not sure you can say that on the air. Henry Lesher - Well I just did. Ash stands Kenji up. Kenji thumbs Ash in the eye, headbutts him and then gives him a series of karate style chops to the chest, driving Ash into the corner. Kenji then hip tosses Ash to the middle of the ring. Kenji dropkicks Ash in the back of the neck. Kenji then tries for the pin on Ash. 1...2... kick out by Ash. Kenji stands Ash up, whips Ash into the corner. Ash comes out of the corner and spears Kenji to the mat. He goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kenji. Ash then stands Kenji up, turns to the crowd and shouts at the and then delivers the Pipe Cashing and goes for the pin again. 1...2...3! Ash gets the hard fought win over Kenji.
Ricky Simms - Big win by Ash. Henry Lesher - Because Kenji sucks donkey balls. Ricky Simms - Stop saying that. Backstage we see Dizz looking at a Cosmopolitan magazine. Samantha Johnson enters the picture.
Samantha Johnson - Dizz, big return tonight against Nathan. What's with the magazine. Dizz - Getting in touch with my feminine side, Sam, and just in time for tonight's match. It's gonna be simply awesome.
Samantha Johnson - Are you okay? Dizz - Sam, I am.... suuuppppeeerr. Samantha backs away from Dizz who appears to be purring. Angela vs Harmony Fallacia "LOVEFURYPASSIONENERGY (Fuck your Rules Man)" by Boy Hits Car echoes over the PA and the crowd boos as Angela makes her way to the ring. She is accompanied by the already victorious once tonight Ash. Angela then points to Ash and decalres he is the top contender for the belt that KillSwitch is going for later tonight. Angela slides into the ring as "Heads Explode" by Monster Magnet plays and Harmony Fallacia emerges from the back, Mr. Binky in hand. She does a somersault on her way to the ring. The crowd cheers her on as she places Mr. Binky at ringside and then jumps onto the apron. She leaps onto the top rope and then lands a dropkick to the chin of Angela from there as the bell sounds to get this match underway.
Ricky Simms - Harmony wasting no time here. Henry Lesher - Anarchy just took one loss and she is trying to prevent it becoming two. Harmony applies a headscissors on Angela. Angela quickly turns that over and gets to her feet, breaking the hold. Angela then kicks Harmony in the gut three times before giving her a gutwrench suplex. Angela tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Harmony. Angela stands Harmony up, whips her off the ropes. Harmony cartwheels around a clothesline. Harmony then gives a springboard bulldog to Angela and goes for the pin. 1...2... Angela gets a foot on the bottom rope while Ash applauds from ringside. Harmony then stands Angela up. Harmony whips Angela off the ropes and lariats her to the mat. Harmony then comes off the ropes and goes for the rolling thunder, but Angela gets her knees up and Harmony lands on them. Angela then gets to her feet and stands Harmony up, then gives her a vertical suplex.
Ricky Simms - Back and forth battle here which could go either way. Henry Lesher - Yeah it could go either way, but Harmony's jumping around like that is making me dizzy.
Angela then picks Harmony up and body slams her. Angela then takes her place in the corner. Harmony slowly gets to her feet, turns and takes Angela's Ashes. Angela goes for the pin. 1...2... Harmony gets her foot on the bottom rope. Angela argues with the ref and we see Kenji coming out from the back. Ash spots him coming and charges out to meet him on the ramp. The two start fighting. Angela notices this and goes to the ropes. Harmony plays the opportunist and rolls Angel up for the pin. 1...2... Angela reverses it and has a handful of tights as she holds Harmony down. 1...2...3! Angela pulls off the win. Angela then rolls out of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Kind of a cheap win for Angela, but a win nonetheless. Henry Lesher - That's right. She won. Thank God that match is over. Angela heads up the ramp where Kenji and Ash are still fighting to get involved. Harmony rolls out of the ring and grabs Mr. Binky from ringside. She charges up the ramp and clubs Angela with it. She then nails Ash in the face with it and Ash falls off the ramp. Kenji then gives Angela a choke slam on the rampway before he and Harmony head to the back.
Henry Lesher - There will be hell to pay for that. Ricky Simms - I tend to agree with that statement. Backstage we see Chain Smoke and Tiffany in the locker room talking strategy with the tag belts on a bench behind them. Charley MacAdam enters the picture.
Charley MacAdam - Tonight you two defend the tag titles against Jon Shocker and Black Panther. Your thoughts?
Tiffany - They had their time. This is our time. Chain Smoke - I am not going to fail tonight. Dark Forces will be getting their lights turned out fast tonight. I then take my belt home for another week. The tag titles stay right here.
Charley MacAdam - Any thoughts on Flock being three wins for three matches so far tonight. Tiffany - *shrugs* Sounds like business as usual to us. Noah Skanes vs Wren The crowd boos as "This Means War" by Busta Rhymes and Ozzy plays and Wren SilverPhoenix starts on her way to the ring. She has a Flock T-shirt on and a defaced picture of Myst in her hands which declares Myst to be a paper champion and scared of Wren. Wren blows a kiss to the camera before tearing the picture to pieces and then she enters the ring. We then head "Just Like You" by Three Days Grace and Noah Skanes starts on his way to the ring with a very determined look in his eyes. Noah looks into the ring, spots Wren, and an evil grin crosses his face. He then steps onto the apron and then into the ring. The bell sounds.
Ricky Simms - Wren's battle with this family continues. Henry Lesher - The kid comes over here for what some figure is a one shot deal from Warriors, but time will tell.
Noah and Wren lock up. Wren kicks Noah in the gut, then whips him off the ropes. Noah shoulder blocks Wren to the mat. He then places her in an arm bar. She reverses it. Noah then lands a forearm smash with his free arm to the jaw of Wren. Noah then hip tosses Wren into the corner. He then walks right into a mule kick from Wren. Wren then shoots a leg out from underneath Noah. As the two fight, we see Pyro making his way out from the back to the shock of everyone here.
Ricky Simms - What is Pyro doing here? Henry Lesher - Coming out to watch a match. At least, that's what it looks likee to me. Pyro makes his way down the ramp as Wren places Noah into a Boston Crab. Noah quickly reaches for and grabs the bottom rope as Wren takes notice of Pyro down here. Wren has a few words for him as she stands Noah up. Noah eye rakes Wren, then headbutts her down to the mat. Noah then picks Wren up and power slams her and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Wren. Noah stands Wren up, completely ignoring Pyro's presence here. Noah then whips Wren off the ropes. Wren ducks a clothesline and then delivers a headscissors takedown on Noah. Wren then lands a leg drop on Noah and goes for the pin. 1...2... Noah kicks out. Wren has a few words for Pyro again as she pulls Noah up by the hair. She then shouts in Noah's face only to have Noah drop down and give her a jawbreaker. Noah then quickly gets to his feet and starts stomping the fallen Wren.
Ricky Simms - Noah fighting with a lot of heart tonight. Henry Lesher - Well, Wren did say last week that he should never have been born. That will give anyone a bit of fight.
Noah then stands Wren up, whips her to the corner and then gives her a superkick to the chin as she staggers out of it. Wren falls face down and then Noah places her in the sharpshooter. The crowd cheers as Wren reaches for the ropes. Pyro paces at ringside as Wren finally concedes she is too far from the ropes and she taps out. The bell sounds and Pyro slides into the ring. Pyro then locks in a crippler crossface on Wren. Noah sees this, laughs and leaves the ring.
Ricky Simms - Why is Pyro attacking Wren? Henry Lesher - Who cares? Great return for Pyro here. Pyro holds onto the crossface until he sees Angela and Ash charging towards the ring, now recovered from the Anarchy attack they suffered. Pyro slips out into the crowd with Ash in close pursuit as Angela attends to Wren in the middle of the ring.
Henry Lesher - Flock loses one here and Anarchy close the night out with two wins and two losses.
Backstage we see Myst looking at her title belt and frowning. Harmony Fallacia enters the picture behind Myst.
Harmony Fallacia - I thought you weren't going to show up here tonight. Myst - *caught off guard* Oh, it's the pie throwing woman. No, I made it. Shame about your loss.
Harmony Fallacia - You ready to defend that title next week? Myst - *laughs* What? Of course I am but you could hardly challenge me for it. You've had enough trouble here lately.
Harmony Fallacia - See you in Canada next Monday. Myst chuckles to herself as Harmony leaves, Mr. Binky in hand. The Moose vs Demona Calaway "If You Want Blood" by AC/DC plays and The Moose emerges with hsi belt over his shoulder as pyro shoots off from the staging area. Moose's ovation gets louder as he approaches the ring, EOS as popular as ever. Moose slides into the ring and holds his belt high as he poses on the second rope in the corner buckle. As he does so, we hear "Stained" by Android Lust and Demona Calaway comes out with her Women's title around her waist. She receives a mixed reaction as she makes her way down the ramp and then slides into the ring. The bell sounds and she stands nose to nose with Moose. Moose shoves Demona. Demona responds with a dropkick on Moose. Demona then stands Moose up and takes a left hook to the chest follows be a headbutt from Moose. Moose then whips Demona hard into the turnbuckle and gives her an atomic drop as she comes out of that same corner. Moose tries for the early pin, hooking the leg. 1...2... kick out by Demona. Moose stands Demona up, whips her off the ropes and Demona holds onto the ropes to avoid a dropkick from Moose. Demona then lionsaults onto Moose and she goes for the pin. 1...2... Moose kicks out and then rolls himself out of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Rare clash between two belt holders here. Henry Lesher - At least Moose is being made to prove he deserves that belt around his waist.
Demona waits while Moose paces outside the ring. The ref reaches a five count before Moose grabs the ropes and pulls himself back into the ring. Demona eye gouges Moose and then whips him into the corner before monkey flipping him back to the center of the ring. Demona then places Moose into a sleeperhold. The ref checks for a submission. Moose shakes his head and tries to break free of the sleeper. He snap mares Demona to the mat and then places her in a headlock. Demona squirms free of that and slides herself out of the ring to regroup.
Ricky Simms - Each champion battling for whatever ground they can gain on the other. Henry Lesher - I would wager on it if I cared. Demona climbs the ring steps and re-enters the ring before the ref can reach the ten count. Demona and Moose lock up in the middle of the ring. Moose breaks it and headlocks Demons. Demona backs Moose into the ropes, whips him off the ropes, leap frogs Moose and then hip tosses him when he comes back to the center of the ring. Demona applies a full Nelson on Moose, but doesn't have the strength to hold it. Moose powers out of it, elbowing Demona in the ribs on both sides. He then turns, headbutts her and picks her up for and delivers the Moosedriver. Moose goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Moose retains the Superior title.
Ricky Simms - Good showing here for Moose against a woman who has dominated most matches she has been in.
Henry Lesher - Maybe he will earn that title of his yet then. Backstage we see KillSwitch pacing in the locker room. Hank Roberts enters to interview him. KillSwitch grabs Hank and power bombs him on the floor of the locker room. He then leaves as officials and security stream into the room to attend to the fallen interviewer.
Dizz vs Nathan Cristion "Stillborn" by Black Label Society plays as Nathan Cristion makes his way to the ring to the boos of the crowd in this, the last of the first round matches for the Commonwealth title tournament. Nathan steps up onto the apron and then through the ropes into the ring. He gets booed as he poses in the corner on the top rope before jumping off into the middle of the ring. We then hear "Toxic" by Britney Spears and Dizz comes out in pink tights with a blonde wig on, dancing as he heads to the ring, tossing flowers to the crowd.
Henry Lesher - Even I find this disturbing. Ricky Simms - I find it disturbing how comfortable Dizz looks as a woman. I guess the comments Nathan made this week were taken to heart.
Henry Lesher - I think some guys in the crowd are ready to hand him a phone number. Dizz dances up the steps and then enters the ring, blowing a kiss at a shocked and confused looking Nathan. The bell sounds as Dizz removes the wing and then he charges at Nathan and starts landing rights and lefts to the jaw of Nathan Cristion before arm dragging him to the center of the ring. Dizz then stands Nathan up and then hip tosses him over the top rope and to the floor. Dizz jumps to the top rope and tries for a flying dropkick from there, but Nathan sidesteps it and then watches Dizz fall hard to the floor where Nathan really puts the boots to him. Nathan stands Dizz up and whips him, head first into the ring steps. Nathan then rolls himself into the ring and waits in the middle of it for Dizz. Dizz is slow to get up, his forehead bleeding from the shot into the steps. Dizz then grabs the apron and rolls himself into the ring just before the ten count to keep this match going. Nathan then rolls Dizz over and places him in a camel clutch.
Ricky Simms - The three time world champ is down here. Henry Lesher - Yeah, he has held the world title three times and more than a month maybe once?
Dizz struggles against the hold as Nathan leans back. Dizz cannot reach for the ropes, so he instead uses his legs to push himself up off the mat and to lessen the pressure. The crowd starts a chant for Dizz as he gets to his hands and knees and Nathan's hold looks less and less effective with each passing moment. Nathan releases the hold and stands Dizz up. He lands a vicious knife edge chop to the chest of Dizz. Dizz responds with a headbutt on Nathan, then a kick to the front of the knee. Dizz then picks Nathan up and gives him a body slam before plaving him in an ankle lock submission. Nathan grabs for the ropes and reaches them right away to force the break. Dizz stands Nathan up. Hathan eye gouges Dizz. Dizz headbutts Nathan. Nathan tosses Dizz over the top rope. Dizz grabs the top rope and manages to hold himself on the apron, landing on his feet. He turns Nathan around, throat over the top rope and then drops off the apron, slingshotting Nathan's throat off the top rope.
Ricky Simms - Nice dexterity there by Dizz. Henry Lesher - Yeah, so what? Dizz rolls himself into the ring as Nathan chokes for breath. Dizz then places Nathan in a sleeper. Nathan quickly elbows Dizz in the gut to break free of it. Nathan whips Dizz off the ropes. Dizz ducks a clothesline. Dizz then whips Nathan into the corner and lands the Reckless Abandon. He goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Dizz pulls off the win seemingly from nowhere.
Ricky Simms - Great win for Dizz to move on to round two. Henry Lesher - As I struggle to care. Backstage we see Hank Roberts being attended to and Shannon Skanes standing there shaking his head. Tony Parizi is next to him.
Shannon Skanes - Hank was my first manager. There's no reason for anyone to do this here tonight.
Tony Parizi - You know what you have to do. Shannon Skanes - I am perfectly aware of what I have to do. Dark Ice Symphony vs Dark Forces The crowd cheers as they hear "Fight Music" by D12 and they see Black Panther and Jon Shocker making their way to the ring, led by Chastity. Shocker enters the ring and poses for the crowd in each corner while Panther's music plays and the lights darken with every other beat before coming up again. Panther and Shocker then converse about who starts this match as "Soldier" by Eminem plays and Chain Smoke and Tiffany are led from the back by Jennifer. Tiffany starts sparring with Shocker while Chain Smoke locks up with Panther in a choke battle, each man with their hands around the other's throat. They fall over the top rope still in a nasty grip with each other. Shocker whips Tiffany off the ropes. Shocker tries for a big boot which Tiffany baseball slides under. Tiffany then quickly gets to her feet and then kicks Shocker in the jaw. Tiffany then dropkicks Shocker to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Shocker baited the champs into the ring. Henry Lesher - Yeah and it has worked out really well for him so far. Look at him go. Outside the ring, Chain Smoke choke slams Panther to the floor. Inside the ring, Tiffany stands Shocker up, attempts to whip him to the corner only to have Shocker reverse it and drive her hard into the corner. Shocker then dropkicks her in the back before trying to roll her up for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tiffany. Shocker stands Toffany up, whips her to the corner again and then Tiffany brings her knees up and knees the chest of a charging Shocker as Chain Smoke finally takes his place in their corner. Toffany shoulder rolls to their corner and tags in Chain Smoke. Chain Smoke steps over the top rope and then grabs Shocker by the hair and starts shouting in his face. Shocker spits at Chain Smoke. Chain Smoke wipes the spit off his face, smiles and then headbutts Shocker down. Panther gets to his feet at ringside, grabbing at both his throat and his back.

Chain Smoke lands a leg drop on Shocker and goes for the pin. 1...2... Shocker manages to roll a shoulder out. Chain Smoke then comes off the ropes and measures Shocker for an elbow. Chain Smoke drops the elbow and finds no one home as Shocker sits up. Shocker then lunges for the tag, but Chain Smoke grabs an ankle and holds Shocker in the ring. The fans start chanting for Shocker as he pumps his fists in an attempt to find the energy to roll over, despite his sore leg.

Henry Lesher - Yes and they would be losers. Shocker summons all of his strength and starts to turn the hold over. Seeing this, Chain Smoke reaches a foot in through the ropes and kicks Shocker in the back of the head. The ref sees this and demands a break of the hold. Tiffany turns to Chain Smoke about this and Shocker crawls over quickly to his corner and tag in Black Panther. Panther clotheslines Tiffany. Panther then backdrops Chain Smoke as he enters the ring. Panther picks Tiffany up and power slams her to the mat. Panther then whips Chain Smoke off the ropes. Shocker pulls the top rope down and Chain Smoke tumbles out over top of it. Panther then waits until Tiffany gets to her feet and lands a Rock Bottom on her and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tiffany.
Ricky Simms - I thought for sure that would be it. Henry Lesher - Finally Shocker did something smart there. Panther tags in Shocker and then holds Tiffany up in a bear hug. Shocker comes off the ropes and clothesline her down out of the bear hug not unlike what the Hart Foundation once did back in the day. Shocker goes for the pin. 1...2... Panther elbows Chain Smoke off the apron.... 3! We have new tag team champions! Shocker jumps to his feet and shouts to the roof as Panther nods his head as if he knew all along this was meant to be. The ref hands the tag belts to them as a dazed Tiffany rolls herself out of the ring.
Ricky Simms - We have new champions! Henry Lesher - Good eyes there, Rick. Nothing gets past you, does it? Night Dweller and Chastity enter the ring and the crowd erupts when they see Scavenger in plain clothes coming in through the crowd and into the ring to congratulate the new tag team champions. Chain Smoke is shouting that this is not over, but he is drowned out by "Phenomenon" as Shocker dances in the ring with his belt on.
Ricky Simms - The crowd is into it. Henry Lesher - The crowd is sheep. Backstage we see Max Cameron leaving the dressing room. He is met with a chair to the face from Leech.
Leech - Did you miss me, Max? See you in the ring, asshole! Leech walks away as we go to another backstage area where Hank Roberts is being attended to and Vigilante enters the screen. He laughs.
Vigilante - So, KillSwitch decided to attack an announcer. Man, I know what that's like when I clocked Joe... Hank, you seem like a decent guy. I am going to avenge that tonight and then Shannon doesn't have to deal with this. I am going to put KillSwitch out for a month.
Leech vs Max Cameron vs Shannon Skanes "Deep" by Nine Inch Nails plays as Shannon Skanes makes his way to the ring. He is attacked from behind by Leech who has the same chair he just clocked Max with. Leech then tosses Shannon off the top of the stage and into a dumpster next to it. Leech then lands a flying elbow on Shannon inside the dumpster. Leech gets to his feet and tosses Shannon out of the dumpster and to the floor. Leech climbs out of the dumpster, landing with both feet on Shannon's back. Leech then grabs Shannon by the hair and pulls him alongside the ramp. He sraps an audio cable around Shannon's neck and starts to choke him out with it. Shannon grabs the cable, then starts whipping Leech across the back with it while struggling for breath.
Ricky Simms - And welcome to the hardcore title match. Henry Lesher - The style of match Leech runs people over in. Now I am into this action. Shannon breaks free of the cable choke with repeated whippings across Leech's back. He then rams Leech's head into the guard railing. Shannon gets to his feet just in time to take a flying clothesline from Max Cameron as he enters the fray, leaping off the ramp and recklessly to the floor. Max lands a series of rights and lefts on Shannon, but has taken his eyes off Leech. Leech stands behind Max with a sadistic smile on his face as he picks up a nearby chair. He smashes Max in the side of the head with the chair. Max falls face down and the bell sounds meaning this hardcore match is now in the books having officially started. Leech rolls Max over and goes for the pin as the ref rushes to that area. 1...2... kick out by Max Cameron. Leech shakes his head as he pulls Max by the hair to ringside. He starts pulling the mats away from the concrete floor. Max starts to get to his feet, blood flowing from the back of his head. Shannon gets to his feet and spears Leech to the floor. He then turns and takes a round house kick from Max. Max then sets Shannon up over the exposed concrete and appears to be readying for a piledriver. Shannon stands up and backdrops Max to the floor.
Ricky Simms - All three men taking a beating in this unholy contest of blood and guts. Henry Lesher - This is what wrestling should be all about right here. Shannon stands Max up and whips him head first into the steel corner post. Leech gets to his feet and shoves Shannon from behind, sending him shoulder first into the steel steps. Leech then stands Shannon up and rolls him into the ring. Leech then raises the apron and looks underneath for weapons. He starts tossing what he finds into the ring. He tosses a kendo stick, a trash can and its lid and a chair into the ring. Max Cameron is seen pulling himself into the ring where he grabs the kendo stick. Max smiles as Shannon staggers to his feet. Max clubs Shannon with the kendo stick. His smile dies when Leech reaches into the ring and pulls Max out by the feet and Max faceplants on the mat on his way out. Leech grabs the kendo stick from Max's hand and starts whipping him across the back with it. Leech then places the stick on the throat of Max and starts to choke him with it. He then rams Max's head into the steel corner post.
Ricky Simms - This is brutal. Henry Lesher - This is my Christmas. Shut up and stop spoiling the mood. Shannon rolls out of the ring with a chair in hand. He clocks Leech across the head with it and then clocks Max with it. He then screams at Max to insult his wife now, then rolls him into the ring, casting the chair aside. Shannon enters the ring and tries for the pin on Max. 1...2... Max gets a foot on the bottom rope. Shannon then stands Max up, whips him off the ropes and then backdrops him to the mat. Shannon then picks up the trash can and slams it on the head of Max. He then leg drops the trash can onto his face and goes for the pin. Leech has climbs to the top rope with the kendo stick in hand. He dives from there. Shannon moves out of the way and Leech's kendo shot strikes Max on the chest. Shannon then slams the trash can lid off of Leech's face and tosses him over the top rope. Shannon goes for the pin on Max. 1...2...3! Shannon wins the hardcore title! Shannon rolls out of the ring and comes face to face with Leech. Leech wipes blood from his lip and hauls off as if he is going to punch Shannon. Instead, he extends his hand and shakes hands with him.
Ricky Simms - Great show of sportsmanship here by Leech. Henry Lesher - Since when is he sportsmanlike? Something smells here. Shannon holds the belt high and heads towards the back as Leech looks inside the ring and laughs at the fallen Max. At least Leech isn't that guy. Medics come to the ring and place Max onto a stretcher and Shannon is seen heading into the back where he finds Moose right around the corner.
Moose - Congrats on the win, champ. You think you can just take a title from EOS? Shannon Skanes - I just did, but I'll stick around to watch this heavily hyped main event match.
Moose - You do that. *taps the hardcore belt* We'll talk about that after. Shannon gives Moose a dirty look as he walks away. Tony Parizi comes over, glancing at Moose.
Tony Parizi - Want me to watch him? Shannon Skanes - Nah, it's cool. They are all in for a shock tonight. KillSwitch vs Vigilante "I Am Hell" by Rob Zombie plays as KillSwitch makes his way to the ring. He is led by Crossbones tonight and has no reaction to the crowd at all, looking to be very focussed on his goal tonight.
Ricky Simms - An now for the main event. Henry Lesher - No, the main event was already had a few moments ago. That was great. KillSwitch steps into the ring and then we hear "Walk All Over You" by AC/DC and Vigilante makes his way out from the back with the world title over his shoulder. The crowd cheers him on as he makes his way to the ring. Vig says something to the camera about the hardcore belt leaving EOS and that not being right. He then makes his way up the steps and into the ring. He hands the belt to the ref as the bell sounds to get the world title match started. KillSwitch eye gouges Vigilante in the middle of the ring and then tosses him over the top rope to the floor. KillSwitch then climbs to the top rope and lands a leg drop to the back of Vig's head which drives him face first to the floor. KillSwitch then stands Vig up and whips him head first into the ring steps before rolling him back into the ring. KillSwitch smiles as he climbs onto the apron and then steps into the ring. KillSwitch then goes for the pin. 1...2... Vigilante kicks out. KillSwitch stands Vigilante up, whips him off the ropes. Vigilante ducks a clothesline and then lands a spinning neckbreaker on KillSwitch before he tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by KillSwitch.
Ricky Simms - Good back and forth action thus far. Henry Lesher - Yeah, we need more of it outside the ring. Inside the ring bores me so. Vigilante stands KillSwitch up. He whips KillSwitch into the corner buckle. The two then clothesline each other to the mat. Vigilante shows signs of movement first and drapes an arm over KillSwitch. 1...2... KillSwitch kicks out to keep the match going. Vigilante uses the ropes to get to his feet. Vigilante then adds a few kicks to the head on KillSwitch. The crowd cheers. He then whips KillSwitch off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Vigilante then stands KillSwitch up and whips him into the corner. He then lands the Catch 22 on him as he comes out and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Vigilante retains the title as he rolls himself out of the ring!
Ricky Simms - KillSwitch is gone for a month! Henry Lesher - He sure is. Vigilante keeps the damned belt for one more week at least. Backstage we see Ash with Ray Dees on the Titantron as Vigilante watches. Ash - Next week I was to face you, Vigilante. However, due to the screw job I watched Max Cameron get, I grant my title shot to him.
We hear "Deep" by Nine Inch Nails and new hardcore champ Shannon Skanes makes his way out of the back with the belt over his shoulder and already in a suit.
Shannon Skanes - Nice match there Vig. Now, I am done with listening to this crap, but Max getting a world title shot amuses me. I will allow the man who was carted out of here to the hospital his shot at Terror in the Aisles.
"If You Want Blood" by AC/DC plays and we see Moose coming out and standing behind Shannon, not looking inpressed.
Shannon Skanes - Hey, Moose, nice to see you. *not looking back at Moose* You don't look happy. I'll make you happy. At Terror in the Aisles, you get to defend your title against Nathan Cristion, Leech and Ash. Four corners match, last man standing is the winner.
Before Ash can answer, the Titantron turns to snow and Shannon pulls a T-shirt out of his jacket. He holds it up before him, a black shirt. He turns it around and it reads EOS on the front.
Shannon Skanes - How the hell did that get in there? Shannon turns and shakes hands with Moose. Leech comes out from the back with Dizz and Vigilante makes his way to the four of them. Vigilante grabs the microphone.
Vigilante - Max, you got screwed tonight. Welcome the newest member of EOS! Shannon Skanes passed the test tonight be standing toe to toe with the most hardcore bastard we have and now he is one of us. If you don't like it, go to hell!
"Walk All Over You" by AC/DC plays as the crowd explodes, seeing Shannon putting on the EOS shirt.
Ricky Simms - I don't believe it. Henry Lesher - Does this make Shannon an enemy of himself? He is the state, right? Ricky Simms - I don't..... know.... We fade to black, ready now for Terror in the Aisles.
