CWA News (2003 to 2004)

July 2003 to 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
News - July 28, 2003 Maximum Impact was a great success! The results are accessible through the July PPV link on this very page. Thanks to Moose and Xtasy for writing a bit for me to keep me from being late with the event.
We have a great line up again this week as more new players sign up. Please get to know people here and enjoy your stay. We're pretty laid back here and don't take ourselves too seriously. Those who try their best get rewarded here. Those who chellenge the owner to a fight get their wish. I believe Wren SilverPhoenix put me on the hit list next. There's always openings for match writing and general board spamming. Fed recruiting isn't cool though, so if you sign up here and try to recruit through us, that won't be cool. Please don't do it.
If you're passing through, sign up and take a shot. Thanks to all for coming. August 1, 2003 Week one on the new boards went well and we are getting rave reviews on our site and, for that, I thank the fed. The voting council is now public and this is going to change things up a bit. I have seen a lot of arguing about votes and results and now people can see who's voting and if anyone questions a loss, I trust that my voters will be more than happy to clear up votes if need be. They don't appear to be the shy type anyways.
I am pondering new titles as well since we have so many newbies and so many guys here deserving that maybe finally bringing in the women's title and maybe a PPV only title would be good for shaking things up a little more. A hearty welcome to the boards to Cross and Kenn, even if it's just to talk OOC and read what a Freak Rob truly is. That's all for now. Enjoy your stay.
August 19, 2003 The card for Animosity looks great and many thanks to those of you who have been putting in a great effort every week. Congrats to Wren SilverPhoenix on becoming the new Aerial champ. I wish everyone luck in the King of the PPV match up and this is going to be a good match up. I am very happy that our little fed here is devoid of infighting and everyone seems content to work together towards the larger goal.
October 4, 2003 We are readying for Dead By Dawn now and it's encouraging to watch veterans return to the fold after some time off to pick up where they left off. There are excellent feuds continuing in the endless quest to see who truly is the best around here. I'm proud to run a place that is full of so many talented RPers from all over. It's also a good thing to have so many get along so well. Thanks for working with me and for being hard workers. It makes my job easier and my dedication to eberyone here stronger with each passing week. For those passing through, this fed is as I have said before always hiring. If you think you have what it takes to become part of a great fed experience, apply and we'll get you involved as soon as possible. Thanks for your time.
October 30, 2003 With a stellar Dead By Dawn now behind us and with membership at all all time high, we are glad to welcome so many new stars here like Twisted, Wraith and others. Congrats and mad props go out to Dizz, the Triple Crown champion! You're the best man and now the world knows it! I look forward to the next one when we start it all over again. Good work is being done by all, so I have no complaints. Keep it up, guys and gals. A note to anyone passing through. The CWA is always hiring new talent.
As an add on to the above post, since it has become a topic of controversy, I geniunely thought either Law or Stallion were going to win the Triple Crown. I was blown away when I read Dizz won it. I feel he deserves a mention on our site the same as RPers of the week get on the left hand side there. I honestly didn't know that BLPW guys came here to read my home page. That surprised me. You guys are welcome to peruse and poke at my site. If my voting caused any issues or hard feelings, no offense meant. I am the most unbiased that I can be. If Law came here, the guy would probably win the world title. Fact is, I have not voted on a CWA event in 6 months. I voted there because Leech couldn't and Demona forgot and hadn't been following it. Shit happens and life goes on. For you guys here, rest assured. I have done my best to make everyone here feel welcome and treated equally. Thanks for your support guys.
November 3, 2003 Another huge event and some great title changes. Dizz becomes World champ with a win over a concussed Shannon Skanes. Great triple threat match for the hardcore title. As of this writing, I have yet to award the Superior title. Soon enough it will be written, guys and gals. I have managed to get past all of last week's nasty BS and let's get on with it! We have 3 weeks until Backdraft, the November Pay Per View. I'm sure it will be another great one. Watch for Warriors to be a weekly show now as Scavenger, Vesuvius, Shannon Skanes, Noah Skanes and others will battle each other. RPing just isn't where my heart is at. My heart is with match writing right now. Anyhow, enough rambling. Don't forget Remembrance Day next week. I'll make mention of it on Assault.
November 12, 2003 Well, this week was the usual strong event. Max Cameron was very close to becoming the new champ. He'll get more shots. We are very close now to Backdraft. There are enough feuds and twists and turns to keep us guessing when that card goes up next week. Now that we are three weeks removed from the Triple Crown event, I can safely say I could never see myself agreeing to an interfed event again. I like running my fed and writing my results. Worrying about three feds worth of egos is going to remove years from my life and drain the fun out of it for me. I am 100% decided on that. I'll just come up with some cool PPV ideas in the next few months and go from there. That's all for today, boys and girls.
January 17, 2004 Well, we sit at the edge of Battle of the Warriors 2004 and it's been some great RPing overall. It's nice to see this site doing as well as it is, now over 4000 hits. I am very appreciative of what I have here as well as what I have at home. This fed and my family combine to form a life I actually enjoy quite a bit. It's great to see so many title changes and champions being made which shows just how even a playing field the CWA truly is, no matter what anyone else might think or say on the subject. While the steady stream of new wrestlers has slowed recently, the handlers we have here as among the very best and I love the talent base we have here. 2004 is going to be another great year as we reach the one year mark since I took over UWN and transformed it into the CWA you see before you today. Thanks for all the support and friendship I have been shown. It has been appreciated.
March 5, 2004 Here we are right after a 19 match pay per view which kept me on my toes and wore me out. I am amazed at how large the roster has grown in size, but we always seem to have the need for new members, so it'll keep being that way for now. I will admit I have thought about closing down apps for awhile, but I am holding back on that. I look forward to the next pay per view which is Terror in the Aisles. We will recall that was the first pay per view this fed put on after taking over from UWN a year ago. I greatly enjoyed writing it, even though we lost a few main eventers as a result of that. There have been OOC grumblings. Cooler heads should prevail, though.... right? RIGHT????!!!! Tune in for more updates on that. Oh, and my wife is due to kick my ass in RPs again soon, so tune in for that as well. Any webcast of my wife physically kicking my ass will be on pay per view and not on UPN as was rumored.
