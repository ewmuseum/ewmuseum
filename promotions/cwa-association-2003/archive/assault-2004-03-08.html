CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 8 March 2004

8 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Little Rock is the host for this week's CWA Assault. The fans cheer loudly as they see pyro shoot off in every direction and this, of course, means that the cameras have started to roll and the CWA is on the air. We then see the opening credits roll with The Flock, EOS, Demona, Myst, Calvin's Army, and Vesuvius with Rated R before we head to ringside where Henry Lesher and Ricky Simms are ready to call the first match of the night.
Beef Jerky vs The Moose The Mexican Hat Dance begins to play and Beef Jerky comes out to the boos of most in the crowd. He shouts at the camera man to get out of his way as he heads for the ring. He gets into a heated exchange with a few fans at ringside before he finally steps up onto the apron and then enters the ring. We then hear "If You Want Blood" by AC/DC and The Moose makes his way to the ring. Moose high fives fans on his way to the ring. He has the Superior title over his shoulder. He then drops the belt at ringside and steps onto the apron. Beef makes a move towards him, but backs off when he sees Moose doesn't flinch. Moose steps through the ropes, not once taking his eyes off of Beef. The bell sounds and the two lock up.
Ricky Simms - Welcome to the latest edition of Assault. This week we have some great matches, to the point where Moose is our opening match of the night.
Henry Lesher - Just get me to the Q match and then I can leave. Moose shoves Beef back intop the ropes. Beef turns it around and shoves Moose into the corner. The ref demands a clean break. Beef backs off partly, then gives a forearm smash to the chin of Moose followed by a knee to the gut and then a hip toss to the center of the ring. Beef then stands Moose up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Beef then starts getting into another verbal exchange with a fan at ringside. Moose gets to his feet. Beef turns and takes a series of rights and lefts from Moose followed by an eye gouge. Moose then whips Beef into the corner and then knee lifts him to the gut on his way back out, sending Beef hard to the mat. Moose then stands Beef up and DDTs him to the mat and rolls him over for the pin. 1...2... Beef gets a foot on the bottom rope.
Ricky Simms - No quit in Beef tonight. Henry Lesher - You say that like it matters or something. Moose stands Beef up. Moose whips Beef into the corner. Moose then gives Beef an atomic drop. Beef falls back to the mat. Moose climbs to the second rope, then delivers a well placed knee to the chest of Beef. The crowd voices their support of Moose as he again goes for the pin. 1...2... Beef kicks out. Moose then stands Beef up, bends him forward and then delivers a Moosedriver! Moose goes for the pin again. 1...2...3! This time, Beef didn't kick out. Moose is your winner!
Ricky Simms - Decisive win for Moose here this week. Henry Lesher - Yeah he beat down that Hamburger person. Moose heads outside the ring, grabs his title and holds it high for the fans nearby. His celebration is cut short when Frank Merritt emerges from the back and attacks Moose from behind. Merritt whips Moose into the steel corner post. Frank then grabs Moose's title belt and smiles as he waits for the Superior champ to turn around. Frank crowns Moose with his own belt and then throws it down on him as he signals to the crowd that Flock won another one. The fans don't sound impressed.
Ricky Simms - I wonder if Frank could do that in a fair fight. Henry Lesher - There you go again thinking fight have to be fair. Backstage we see Wren entering the building and she is headed off by Charley MacAdam. Charley MacAdam - Wren, a lot was said between yourself and Kenji this week and somehow Myst became involved in it. Can you explain this?
Wren - I can explain this. I am the true Aerial champ. I came back for my title. I am going to take it from Myst and I'll start by beating the poser who couldn't beat her last week.
Charley MacAdam - You have also taken several verbal shots at Shannon Skanes. That feud isn't dead for you?
Wren - No feud ever dies. For me, as long as Shannon is walking around this place, he will be my target.
Max Cameron vs Pyro "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC plays as Max Cameron makes his way to the ring. He hears it from the crowd as usual. He is attacked half way down the ramp by Pyro. Pyro picks Max up and slams him on the ramp. Pyro then puts the boots to Max and then drags him down to ringside. Pyro rolls Max into the ring. Pyro then heads to the corner, jumps onto the apron and makes his way to the top rope. He leaps off to land a five star, but Max moves out of the way as the bell sounds to start this match officially. Max then uses the ropes to get to his feet as Pyro tries to catch his breath. Max and Pyro meet in the middle of the ring and start trading punches. Max starts to overpower Pyro and Pyro rakes the eyes of Max Cameron. Pyro then clotheslines Max to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Max Cameron. Pyro stands Max up and whips him into the corner buckle hard. Pyro then DDTs Max to the canvas. Pyro tries for the pin. 1...2... reversal into a small package pin attempt by Max. 1...2... Pyro kicks out.
Ricky Simms - These two no starngers to each other at all. Henry Lesher - Doesn't mean I will like the match. From what I understand, it will be the last match for Pyro. Too bad, so sad, let's see Q.
Both men get to their feet quickle. Pyro tries a clothesline on Max. Max ducks that and then lands a reverse neckbreaker on Pyro. Max tries for the pin. 1...2.. Pyro kicks out. Max then pulls Pyro to his feet, whips him hard into the corner. Max places Pyro over his shoulder and then gives him a fisherman's suplex. Max heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. The crowd boos as he gives them the finger and then lands a flying elbow smash from there. Max then stands Pyro up, whips him into the corner and then delivers a sidewalk slam on him as he comes back out of it. Max goes for the pin. 1...2... Pyro kicks out. Max then stands Pyro up, whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him to the mat. Max then stands Pyro up, whips him off the ropes again and dropkicks him to the mat. Max then turns to the crowd and tells them to shut up. Of course, that just encourages them to get louder.
Ricky Simms - Flock sure are hated by the CWA fans. Henry Lesher - Yeah, they have that effect on people. Max again stands Pyro up. He whips Pyro into the corner and then gives him a gutwrench suplex out of it. He goes for the pin. 1...2... Pyro kicks out. Max stands Pyro up. Pyro gouges the eyes of Max Cameron. Pyro then whips Max off the ropes and plcaes him in a sleeperhold. The sleeper is doomed as Max elbows Pyro in the gut three times, then delivers a stunner on him. Max looks to the booing crowd and smiles. He pulls Pyro to the corner. climbs to the second rope and delivers the MP3! Max goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Max wins the match over his former stablemate.
Ricky Simms - Max manages to come up with the win here this week. Henry Lesher - Goody for him. Inside the ring, Max stands Pyro up. The two look at each other and Max shakes hands with him. He then leaves Pyro in the ring shaking his head. Max makes his way to the back, having a few words with a couple of loud fans. Backstage we then see Lunacy Muse entering the building and the crowd boos when they see her on camera. Gloria Lavin heads her off.
Gloria Lavin - Look, I don't want you coming in yet. Lunacy Muse - What? I thought after what you said on the phone that we had done the deal. Gloria Lavin - Lunacy, I will admit you won. Q will have to deal with you on Breakthrough and give you some position there. My calling you was separate and I didn't want you to come in yet.
Lunacy Muse - You didn't make a verbal deal with me to keep this show on the air, did you? A deal you never intended to honor?
Gloria Lavin - No, I did no such thing. As far as I am concerned, you are legal counsel in the CWA. Anything that goes on the air goes through you first. I understand the deal we made and I will honor it. I just wanted to prevent...
Shannon Skanes - *entering the picture* Well look what the cat dragged in. Gloria Lavin - ...Shannon seeing you yet. Shannon Skanes - We have some unfinished business, Miss Muse. You tried to end my career some months ago.
Lunacy Muse - You're mad at me because I failed? I could try it again. Gloria steps in between the two of them. Gloria Lavin - This is what I was worried about. Muse, I will talk to you later. Shannon, you have a big match to get ready for. Back to your respective corners.
Lunacy and Shannon step back and then turn away from each other. Gloria sighs and then looks up as if for divine guidance right now.
MLSQ42 vs Frank Merritt Tomoyasu Hotei's "Battle without Honor or Humanity" begins to play and Q starts for the ring as the crowd appears to be... cheering him on? This is odd to say the least as Tina leads Q out. She is used to shouting at fans to shut up. Now, she allows them to cheer and it appears to confuse her. After all, Q hasn't changed the way he does things. Maybe the crowd just sees him as the lesser of two evils. Q steps over the top rope into the ring and shakes his head as he listens to the cheers. He knows he could change those cheers to boos quickly. "Seizure of Power" by Marilyn Manson plays as Frank Merritt makes his way to the ring.
Henry Lesher - Let me address a clear problem with CWA booking right now. Q is a monster, a cold and unfeeling monster. He is being cheered by these idiots right now because he is matches up against someone who is clearly too big of an idiot for any fan to cheer for. Q must be hated and feared. I saw a fan tonight dressed as Q. How sick is that? You don't love him! He hasn't changed a damned thing about himself!
Ricky Simms - You act like a kid having to share a toy. Henry Lesher - He's mine, I tell you! I blame Shannon for this! He made the fans like Q! He'll pay for that!
Ricky Simms - It's possible that the fans think for themselves. Henry Lesher - Nope. Fans think whatever the guys in the ring want them to. That simple. The bell sounds to start the match as the two men circle each other. Q lands a punch to the temple of Frank which knocks him back. Q then lands a series of knife edge chops to the chest of Frank Merritt before hip tossing him into the corner. Q then gives Frank a side Russian leg sweep to the mat. Q then goes for the early pin. 1...2.. Frank kicks out. Q then stands Frank up and attempts a whip into the corner, but Frank blocks it and then reverses it whipping Q hard into the corner before delivering a clothesline from behind on Q, sending him face first to the mat. Frank then places a knee over the back of Q's neck and holds him down while the fans boo.
Ricky Simms - Frank taking over here. Henry Lesher - It's cause those idiots are cheering Q on. Q, spit on the fans! Tell them you hate them! Tina, land a rock bottom on a kid at ringside!
Ricky Simms - That's sick. Henry Lesher - Exactly. Frank releases Q from the stranglehold and stands him up. He then whips him into the corner. Q raises both knees to the chest of Frank as he charges into the corner. Q then grabs Frank around the throat and two hand choke slams him to the mat and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Frank Merritt. Q then stands Frank up, whips him off the ropes. Frank grabs the ropes and pulls himself out of the ring. Q then drops to the mat and follows Frank out. Q grabs Frank and smashes his head onto the announce table. He then picks him up and slams him on the announce table before measuring him for an elbow to the throat.
Ricky Simms - The fight is right in our laps now. Henry Lesher - I wish. Q pulls Frank off the table and rolls him into the ring. He then rolls himself into the ring and stands Frank up and then lands the Q Jumper on him! Q goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Q gets the win here over Frank Merritt.
Ricky Simms - Q gets the win here. Henry Lesher - That comes despite the support of these idiot fans. Maybe if he smacked a few babies or something.
Backstage we see Gloria Lavin shaking her head as she walks Q get out of the ring. She turns and comes face to face with Scavenger.
Gloria Lavin - Hi Scav. Scavenger - I want a match next week. Gloria Lavin - No problem. Scavenger - I want a big match next week. Gloria Lavin - You got it. Scavenger - Or I will go to BLPW. Gloria Lavin - Yeah, I'll get you a match next week then. Scavenger - Good, I want a big match. Kenji vs Wren The crowd cheers as Kenji starts making his way to the ring, "Lightning Rod" by The Offspring playing and Skinny leading him to the ring. As Kenji makes his way down the ramp, we see Wren appear from the back and walk one step behind Kenji the whole way to the ring. Kenji makes his way up the steps. Skinny turns, spots Wren and warns Kenji. Wren punches Skinny in the face. Kenji turns and dives on Wren, landing a cross body to the floor and then raining down punches on her. Kenji then stands her up and rolls her into the ring. Skinny has a few words for him before he follows Wren in and this match can really get started. Kenji goes on the attack right away. Kenji stands Wren up, whips her off the ropes. Kenji backdrops Wren to the mat and then heads to the ropes and jumps to the top. Kenji launches himself onto Wren with a flying cross body and tries for the pin. 1...2... Wren kicks out.
Ricky Simms - Wren was quite vocal all week and made no secret of her dislike for the Aerial champ.
Henry Lesher - Well, she was simply calling things as they really are. She knows Kenji and Myst are both overrated.
Kenji stands Wren up, whips her off the ropes and then Wren ducks a clothesline and then somersaults and headscissors Kenji to the mat. Wren then quickly gets to her feet and boot chokes Kenji against the ropes. Wren breaks the choke before the ref DQs her. Wren stands Kenji up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a tilt a whirl backbreaker. Wren then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kenji. Wren then has a few words for the ref about counting to three. Wren stands Kenji up, then snap mares him to the mat. Wren comes off the ropes and dropkicks the sitting up Kenji in the face. Wren again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kenji again. Wren then stands Kenji up and whips him through the ropes. Kenji crashes hard into the guard railing and Wren makes her way to the top rope. Kenji slowly gets to his feet, then turns and takes a double axe handle to the forehead from Wren. Wren then rolls Kenji inside the ring and heads to the ropes again, climbing to the top and turning backwards. She tries for a moonsault only to have Kenji rolls out of the way.
Ricky Simms - Wren a little too confident there. Henry Lesher - You can never be too confident. Kenji tries for a pin on Wren. 1...2... Wren kicks out. Kwnji then rolls himself out of the ring. Skinny goes to him to see that he's okay. Kenji states that he's fine and then sees Wren getting to her feet. Kenji fakes that he's stumbling and Wren tries for a missile dropkick through the ropes. Kenji sidesteps it and watches Wren crash into the guard railing. Kenji then rolls himself back into the ring. Skinny stands near Wren, clearly wanting to put the boots to her. However, he backs off as she gets to her feet. Wren enters the ring slowly as the ref reaches an eight count. Kenji drop a scissor kick to the back of Wren's neck before she gets all the way to her feet inside the ring. Kenji then rolls Wren over for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Wren. We then see Myst coming out from the back with the Aerial title over her shoulder. The crowd cheers as Kenji brings Wren to her feet. Kenji attempts to whip Wren to the corner. Wren reverses it and whips Kenji to the corner. Wren then gives Kenji a spinning neckbreaker. Wren spots Myst on the ramp and motions her into the ring.
Ricky Simms - This could explode here. Henry Lesher - I sure hope so. I am dying for some excitement. Wren goes to the ropes and holds them open for Myst. Myst shakes her head and blows a kiss at Wren. Seeing this, Kenji rolls Wren up from behind. 1...2...3! Kenji rolls out of the ring the winner! Kenji then retreats as Wren quickly gets to her feet and charges out of the ring after him. Skinny raises a hand now, no longer worried about getting Kenji disqualified. Myst turns and heads to the back with her belt still over her shoulder.
Henry Lesher - Well, Myst sure screwed that match up. Ricky Simms - Wren will be livid. If she didn't want her in the ring bad before then, she does now.
Backstage we see Ash with Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - Ash, tonight is your big night. Ash - Wrong, it's Vigilante's big night. It's the night he learns that he isn't superman. It's the night he learns that there is a new top dog and that the Flock is the top stable here, not his band of misfits.
Joe Davidson - That sounds very confident. Ash - What else would you expect? It's time you guys stop fearing the mighty champion and see him as being human. Tonight he goes down for the count. His ass gets cashed.
Demona Calaway vs Ophelia "Gold Dust Woman" by Hole plays as Ophelia makes her way to the ring, led by Scavenger. She gets a strong ovation from the fans as she enters the ring and Scavenger takes his usual place at ringside. "Everything Destroys Itself" by Kinesis plays as Demona makes her way to the ring. She receives a warm ovation as well, so it will be interesting to see which person they support at the end of this match up.
Ricky Simms - Big match for Ophelia here, her biggest yet as she faces the Women's champion.

Demona breaks the lock up, placing Ophelia in an arm bar. Ophelia reverses it. Demona does a somersault and then knife edge chops Ophelia to the throat. Demona whips Ophelia off the ropes and dropkicks her to the mat. Scavenger cheers his woman on from outside the ring. Demona stands Ophelia up and whips her into the corner buckle. Demona then picks Ophelia up and lands a perfect vertical suplex on her. Demona tries for the pin. 1...2... Ophelia kicks out.

Demona stands Ophelia up, cinches her for another suplex. Ophelia blocks it. Ophelia then gives Demona a vertical suplex that is every bit as impressive as the one she just took. Ophelia then heads to the ropes and climbs to the second rope. She lands a leg drop to the chest of Demona and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Demona Calaway. Ophelia then stands Demona up, whips her to the ropes. Demona ducks a clothesline from Ophelia. Demona then picks up Ophelia and body slams her to the mat. Ophelia brings her feet up and kicks Demona in the chest, driving her to the corner. Ophelia then gets to her feet, then cartwheels into the corner. Demona moves out of the way and then gives Ophelia a spinning neckbreaker. Demona then stands over Ophelia as Scavenger stands at ringside shaking his head.
Ricky Simms - Looks like Demona is thinking about ending it. Henry Lesher - Then end it already. Demona stands Ophelia up, then delivers the darkness surrounding. Demona covers Ophelia for the pin. 1...2...3! Demona Calway wins another one.
Ricky Simms - And on that point Demona ends it. Henry Lesher - Good. I couldn't take much more of that tripe. Backstage we see a group entering the arena. We spot Annalyse Nicoletti, but someone knocks the camera over before we can spot anyone else. We then see a different portion of the backstage area and we see Vigilante doing push ups when Samantha Johnson enters the picture.
Samantha Johnson - Vig, looking good for tonight's match up. Vigilante - I should be. I am the champ, after all. Samantha Johnson - True enough. Any words for Ash? Vigilante - Yeah, a shame his push ends tonight. Dude has talent, but he doesn't have championship sized testicles. I do. Vince McMahon envies me.
We then look to another portion of the backstage area and Myst is leaving her locker room. Wren appears out of nowhere to chair shot her to the face, but Shannon steps into the way of the shot and he drops like a rock. Myst then starts brawling with Wren as the security starts to pour into the area. Kenji appears from the locker room and looks down at Shannon, then points at Wren.
Kenji - Next week, I am the ref when you face Myst! An aerial competitor should judge an aerial match!
Anarchy vs The Flock "Links 2-3-4" by Rammstein plays as Vesuvius and Rated R make their way to the ring clad in red and black of Anarchy. Dragon then pulls Rated R to his corner and tags in SinmaN. SinmaN heads to the top rope while Dragon stands Rated R up and holds him open. Vesuvius gets a foot on the bottom rope.

He grabs her shirt at the neck and tears. Sure enough, there's an Anarchy Inc. shirt under there! The crowd cheers as Dragon steps into the ring, still pulling cream filling out of his eyes as SinmaN slowly gets to his feet off kilter. Rated R and Vesuvius accompany the third member of Anarchy to the back. There will be hell to pay for this.
Henry Lesher - Maybe Vesuvius is onto something here. Backstage we see Ash turning things over in the dressing room at seeing what just happened to his fellow Flock mates. Charley MacAdam thinks about going in for an interview, but he stops himself and then beacks out again. Apparently it isn't worth the risk to life and limb. We then head back to the ring.

This is going to be a messy match, resembling a lumberjack match.

KillSwitch is then seen coming out of the staging area and he decks a couple of stage hands on his way to the back as Angela tags along behind. They pass Ash who looks very intense as he readies for the fight of his lifetime next!
Vigilante vs Ash "Walk All Over You" by AC/DC plays as Vigilante makes his way to the ring. He passes by the Entourage and Shannon and Myst, appearing to slip Myst his phone number. He then high fives fans on his way to the ring with the world title belt around his waist. Vigilante then tells the camera that the belt is coming home with him tonight. Vigilante then unlatches the belt and hands it to the timekeeper before making his way into the ring as the crowd cheers him on. The cheers quiet down as we hear "The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson play and Ash makes his way out from the back, emerging through green smoke and declaring to anyone who will hear him that he is the next world champ. Angela then appears behind him, already changed and looking refreshed and ready for more here tonight. Ash then runs the rest of the way into the ring. The bell sounds and the brawl is on. Ash bowls Vigilante over and right out of the ring. Vigilante then rolls on top of Ash and the two start choking each other. Ash gouges at the eyes of Vigilante and then monkey flips him into the guard railing.
Ricky Simms - Welcome to the main event, a match for the world title! Henry Lesher - Great match up here for both men and finally I see light at the end of that dark tunnel. Tonight is going to have an end.
Ash then stands up, grabs Vigilante by the hair and whips him head first into the steel ring steps. Ash then rolls himself into the ring to break up the count before rolling himself back out. He stands Vigilante up again and whips him inti the guard railing shoulder first. He then sets him up for and executes a pump handle slam. Ash rolls himself into the ring again to break up the count before rolling himself back out. He again has to stand the world champ up. He attempts to whip him into the steel corner post. Vigilante stops his forward momentum and instead whips Ash into the steel corner post. The ref is upset about not having any control of this match, but there is little he can do. Vigilante rolls himself into the ring and then rolls himself back out. Vigilante starts pulling up the mats at ringside. Ash slowly gets to his feet and both men are bleeding at this point. Ash charges at Vigilante and Vig ducks and gives him a spinebuster where the mats used to be. Ash lands with a sickening thud on the concrete floor as Vigilante rolls himself into the ring again to break up the count and then rolls back out.
Ricky Simms - No intention shown by either man to take this inside the ring. Henry Lesher - No, there shouldn't be. I mean, this is a title match between two men who hate each other more every week. There is no limit on what they might do here tonight!
Vigilante stands Ash up and gives him a knife edge chop to the chest. Ash rakes the eyes of Vigilante and then bounces his forehead off the guard railing. Ash then picks Vigilante up and slams him on the floor. Ash then rolls into the ring to break up the count and then rolls himself right back out. He finally rolls Vigilante into the ring.
Ricky Simms - This match is finally going inside the ring. Ash must figure he has Vigilante done and finished.
Henry Lesher - Those two things are just so different. Ash stands Vigilante up, whips him into the corner buckle and then delivers the Pipe Cashing and goes for the pin. 1...2... Vigilante gets a foot on the bottom rope! Ash then stands Vigilante up and goes for it again only to have Vig rake his eyes before he can properly set up for it. Vigilante then kicks Ash in the front of the knee, then kicks the other. He then shoots a leg out from under Ash and goes for an ankle lock submission hold. Ash reaches for the ropes. Seeing he is too far away, he rolls over and kicks Vigilante off of him. Ash then quickly gets to his feet only to be chop blocked by the champ. Vigilante goes for the pin. 1...2... Ash kicks out. Vigilante then stands Ash up and whips him into the corner buckle. He gives him a German suplex, then holds him in it and lands another one. He then keeps his hands around Ash's waist and lands a third on the challenger.
Ricky Simms - Shades of Chris Benoit there. Henry Lesher - Don't let Vigilante know you said that. Vigilante stands Ash up. From the back we see Scavenger making his way to the ring. He has an EOS shirt on one arm and a Flock shirt on the other. He is taking his turns blowing his nose on both. Scavenger then tosses them to the crowd and turns his attention to the action inside the ring. He announces to the camera he is getting a big match next week. He is going to be noticed.
Ricky Simms - This can't be good. Henry Lesher - Nothing that chair minded freak does is good. Scavenger steps right up on the apron and the ref heads him off. Scavenger asks if he may enter the ring. The ref directs him to the back. Behind the ref's back, Leech enters the ring. He grabs Ash and bulldogs him to the mat. As Leech inflicts punishment on Ash, SinmaN enters the ring and grabs Leech into a headlock. They start to grapple as the ref turns and sees all that's going on inside the ring. The ref doesn't know who hit who first, so he calls the match a double DQ. The crowd boos and then we hear "Not Gonna Get Us" by TATU. Gloria Lavin appears on stage.
Gloria Lavin - No, no, no! This is not the way this feud ends! Scavenger wants his big match! He gets Ash! No count out or DQ! I have seen enough of that tonight! Vigilante gets to sit at ringside with KillSwitch as they get ready for their match the week after! The winner between Ash and Scavenger gets a World title shot at Terror in the Aisles! You know why? Because Gloria runs this show.
Gloria turns and leaves everyone very confused inside the ring. Ricky Simms - March 22nd, KillSwitch gets his shot at Vigilante. The winner will face next week's winner between Ash and Scavenger! That should be great!
Henry Lesher - Yeah right. It should be... Ricky Simms - Next week will be a great one then!
