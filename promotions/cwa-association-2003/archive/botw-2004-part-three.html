Shengee Shmelky vs Spikey Tiger "Eye of the Tiger" by Survivor plays and we see Spikey Tiger heading out from the back, dragging Shengee Shmelky behind him. He says something to the camera on the way down the ramp about always hating boy bands before he rolls Shengee into the ring. Half the fans love him for clocking the one half of Shmelk and the rest hate him, as often is the case for Spikey. Spikey enters the ring and we hear the bell sound.
Ricky Simms - It would appear Spikey knocked Shengee out in the back before even having a chance to get the match going here.
Henry Lesher - He must have had to endure listening to him rap. Spikey climbs to the top rope and dives off, landing an elbow to the chest of Shengee Shmelky. He then covers him. 1...2... he pulls Shengee's shoulders up from the mat and shakes his head. The ref has a few words for him about that. Spikey stands Shengee up, looks to the crowd which is still divided in its loyalties and lands the Eye of the Tiger move on Shengee and again goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Spikey wins the match with ease against a defenseless Shmelky.
Ricky Simms - Big man that Spikey. Henry Lesher - At least we didn't have to sit through fifteen minutes of a pathetic match to reach that conclusion. I like it.
Spikey Tiger grabs a microphone from the ring announcer. Spikey Tiger - Okay, so first I job to Dante tonight and then they throw me this piece of shit for afterwards? I am so sick to death of either being screwed in a match or winning in a squash. I want someone to come out of the back and accept a challenge from me for next week! I want a fight!
No sooner has Spikey said that when we hear "I Like Goils" by Type O Negative and we see Warriors champion Caleb Knox standing at the top of the ramp. The crowd explodes. Luckily for us all, he also has a microphone in hand.
Caleb Knox - Wow, such anger there Spikey! Now, I normally stay in the shadows and say little while I battle my way through the Warriors roster which is kept away from you guys, but I simply cannot resist a challenge. You see, I grow tired of the attention you guys get on a nightly basis while my roster is ignored.
Spikey Tiger - Your roster and title are ignored because you're nobodies. Caleb smiles, nods and readies himself to say something. However, he instead drops the mic and races into the ring where he gets into a fight with Spikey Tiger as security streams into the ring to split the two of them up.
Ricky Simms - That was unexpected. Henry Lesher - I must say that is most unexpected indeed. My hat is off to Caleb for shutting the guy up. Watching Tiger wrestle is bad enough.
As security breaks the two of them up, we look to the back where Dante is looking at his title belt. Samantha Johnson enters the frame.
Samantha Johnson - Dante, this match between yourself and Vigilante appears to be quite hotly contested right now as you cost Vigilante a possibility for a shot at that million dollars earlier tonight.

Samantha Johnson - Your modesty is stunning. Dante - Yes I know it is. Tonight I keep this belt. Vigilante hasn't got a hope. Samantha Johnson - You sound pretty confident.
