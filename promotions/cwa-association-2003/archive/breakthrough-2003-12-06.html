CWA Breakthrough, 6 December 2003

6 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Peoria, Illinois is the home to another edition of Breakthrough, this one coming right on the heels of what is being called the best CWA Pay Per View ever, Backdraft. The show begins with opening credits featuring Pyro, Gothic Angel, Cleo Styles and Scavenger before we head to ringside where Romeo Sa;vador is sitting with Gloria Lavin. Gloria is a few feet further away from Romeo than is the usual.
Gloria Lavin - I am proud to announce that I am here despite the threat that Romeo may pose to me, no matter how accidental that may be.
Romeo Salvador - I have been hearing this all week.... Scotty Ryan vs Casanova "Clown" by Korn plays and Scotty Ryan starts down the ramp towards the ring. As he reaches the bottom of the ramp, he is attacked by Casanova who throws him hard into the apron, shoulder first. Casanova then body slams Scotty Ryan at ringside and follows this up with a series of kicks to the ribs of the fallen new CWA star. Casanova then stands Ryan up and rolls him into the ring. As Ryan himself enters the ring, the bell sounds to get things officially underway here. Casanova places Scotty Ryan into a sleeper, apparently anxious to get this match finished quickly.
Romeo Salvador - Good start for Casanova here. Gloria Lavin - Both of these youngsters have shown enthusiasm in their short time here, though we never saw a rpomo from Casanova all week.
Scotty Ryan battle his way to his hands and knee as Casanova attempts to keep his grasp on the sleeper. Scotty elbows Casanova in the gut, then again, then another one which finally breaks the hold. Scotty then whips Casanova off the ropes and shoulder blocks him to the mat. He follows this up with a leg drop to the chest of Casanova. Scotty tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Casanova. Scotty Ryan then stands Casanova up and whips him into the corner buckle. Ryan clotheslines Casanova as he comes out of the corner. Ryan then places Casanova into the Sharpshooter and leans back. Casanova reaches for the ropes. The ref asks Casanova if he gives up.
Gloria Lavin - I seem to remember another Calgary wrestler using that move to some level of success.
Romeo Salvador - It's a bit early to make those comparisons, Gloria. Now let's stop this you being afraid of me crap. I slipped and what happened to Baby Calaway was an accident.
Gloria Lavin - Just keep it over there, stumble boy. Casanova reaches the bottom rope and the hold is broken. However, Scotty stands Casanova up, headbutts him a few times and then backs him into the corner where he lands a series of punches and kicks which knock him off of his feet. Scotty looks to the crowd who cheer. He then picks Casanova up and delivers the Styleizer. He covers Casanova for the pin. 1...2...3! Scotty Ryan with a win in his first CWA match!
Romeo Salvador - Nice win here. It will be interesting to see how things go in the coming weeks for this kid.
Backstage we see Ingee Shmelky practicing a few dance moves. He has very big, billowy purple plants on and shiny gold necklaces which reach past his waist. Q enters the picture.
Q - Boy band, huh? Ingee Shmelky - Flava is gonna be huge. You signing up? Q - Ummmm, no. However, I will be there to witness the train wreck this week. Ingee Shmelky - *frowns* No wrecks here, Mister Q462. It's boy band tryouts. Q - Sure it is. Frank Merritt vs Damien "Solitaire Unravelling" by Mushroomhead plays as Frank Merritt appears at the top of the ramp which is lit green. Frank makes his way to the ring, high fiving a few fans who are carrying 420 Flock signs of wearing their T-shirts. Frank slides into the ring, removes his jacket and readies himself for his opponent. "Alone In the Dark" by Testament plays as Damien makes his way to the ring. Damien hears a few cheers, but only very few. As he slides into the ring, he is attacked with a series of kicks from Frank Merritt. The bell sounds as Merritt stands Damien up, whips him into the corner and then charges in and gives him a stiff shoulder block before monkey flipping him to the center of the ring.
Gloria Lavin - Strong starts for Merritt here. Romeo Salvador - He has had a strong level of success here in the CWA thus far. Hooking up with one of the top stables in the CWA sure didn't hurt him either.
Gloria Lavin - Indeed. Merritt whips Damien off the ropes. Damien ducks a clothesline and then lands a reverse neackbreaker on Merritt and goes for a quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Merritt. Damien stands Merritt up and whips him through the ropes to the outside of the ring. Damien then follows him out and picks up a chair. He wields it to hit Merritt on the floor, but Frank rolls out of the way and the chair bounces off of the floor. Merritt then mule kicks Damien in the chest, causing him to drop the chair. Merritt then quickly gets to his feet and whips Damien into the steel corner post. Merritt then rolls Damien into the ring and rolls himself in to barely beat the referee's ten count right after. Merritt picks Damien up and gorilla press slams him to the mat. Merritt then gives Damien an elbow smash to the throat. Merritt then heads for the ropes and makes his way to the top. The crowd cheers as he lands a flying elbow smash to the chest of Damien and tries for the pin. 1...2...3! Frank Merritt with the win this week over Damien!
Gloria Lavin - And the wins continue for Frank here in the CWA. Romeo Salvador - Yeah, he is quickly putting together a strong CWA resume to be sure. However, the higher you go in this company, the more targets you'll have on your back.
Gloria Lavin - Is that why you stay out of the ring and lose when you do go into it, unless you're injuring little girls?
Romeo Salvador - Baby is a full grown woman. Gloria Lavin - I get the feeling it's not the first time the manwhore has said that. Backstage we see Night Dweller entering the arena where he is quickly headed off by Dante. Night Dweller - What the hell are you doing here? I thought you ran Assault. Dante - I am everywhere, night person. I just want you to know I have your back in there tonight.
Night Dweller - Aw, that's sweet, but I don't need your help. I'll be just fine. Dante - Oh, I insist. Have a good night, Mister Dweller. Night Dweller gives Dante a dirty look as Dante leaves to deal with some other piece of pressing business. He is up to something.
Cleo Styles vs Angel "Trouble" by Pink plays as Angel makes her way to the ring to the cheers of the fans. As she steps into the ring, "Here To Stay" by Korn plays and out comes Cleo Styles to a far more negative reaction than she got in her home land Australia. She shows nothing but contempt for those booing her on the way to the ring. She orders the ref to hold Angel back as she makes her way into the ring. Cleo steps through the ropes and then charges at Angel and lands a spine buster on her. She starts landing rights and lefts to Angel who simply tries to cover up and survive here. Cleo rams her head into the mat five times before the ref forces her to stop these tactics or risk a DQ here.
Romeo Salvador - Well, Cleo sure is in quite the mood here tonight. Gloria Lavin - She is taking it to Angel and Angel doesn't have an answer for the attack thus far.
Cleo stands Angel up and whips her into the corner. She charges in for a shoulder block, but Angel leaps onto the top rope, than rolls behind Cleo and rolls her up for a quick pin. 1...2... Cleo manages to kick out. Cleo then rolls out of the ring and looks quite upset that Angel had any offence against her at all. Angel slowly gets to her feet as Cleo gets onto the apron. Angel charges and knocks Cleo off the apron with a pretty decent shoulder block. Cleo lands hard against the guard railing and grabs at her back and winces as she gets back to her feet. Angel climbs to the top rope as Cleo staggers around. Angel then lands a missile dropkick on Cleo at ringside to the delight of the capacity Peoria crowd. Angel then stands Cleo up and rolls her into the ring. Angel heads for the ropes again.
Romeo Salvador - This usually is a bad idea the second time. She could be making that one too many trips to the well.
Gloria Lavin - I am inclined to agree with you there. Angel leaps from the top and lands a five star frog splash on Cleo. Both Gloria and Romeo were wrong. Angel tries for the pin. 1...2... Cleo gets a foot onto the bottom rope. Angel pulls Cleo to the middle of the ring and tries for the pin again. 1...2... Cleo kicks out. Angel then stands Cleo up and tries to whip her into the corner buckle only to have Cleo reverse it, throwing her hard into the corner. Cleo then headbutts Angel to the mat. Cleo follows this up by heading onto the apron and lionsaulting onto Angel. Cleo goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Angel. Cleo then picks Angel up over her shoulder and lands an F5 on Angel! Cleo goes for the pin again. 1...2...3! Cleo Styles with another win.
Gloria Lavin - It is getting tough to ignore the record Cleo is putting together here. Romeo Salvador - She has a lot of talent which is equalled by her unquenchable desire to get to the top. She'll be getting a title shot for the Women's gold soon is my guess.
Cleo is booed as she leaves the ring. We head backstage and see Shengee Shmelky in front of a mirror posing as Shawn Michaels once did. He is the heartbreak albino and tonight he makes his big step towards becoming an absolutely huge star. Adam West enters the picture.
Adam West - Shengee, you should know that I will be managing Flava. Shengee Shmelky - No one can manage Flava! We are out of control! Adam West - Yes, so I hear. Shengee, tonight you'll see that rules are needed for this band to succeed.
Shengee Shmelky - We're gonna get chicks. Adam West - Yes..... that is what I hear. Adam mumbles something about Catwoman and leaves the picture as Shengee sings Shawn Michaels' theme song to himself and starts posing again. His 115 pounds have never looked so stringy and stretched...
Ebony vs Gothic Angel "Right Round" by Dope plays as Gothic Angel makes her way to the ring. The fans cheer her on as she steps through the ropes. As she tests the ropes, "Down With the Sickness" by Disturbed plays and Ebony starts making her way to the ring to a reaction almost as strong as that of Gothic Angel. Ebony slides into the ring and the two ladies stand nose to nose in the middle of the ring and have a few words with each other. The bell sounds. Gothic Angel shoves Ebony. Ebony shoves Gothic Angel. Gothic Angel then whips Ebony off of the ropes and drops her with a backdrop. Gothic Angel then stands Ebony up then gives her a snap suplex to the mat.
Gloria Lavin - Gothic Angel is really taking the fight to Ebony here. Romeo Salvador - Gothic Angel always puts a strong effort into her matches. Gloria Lavin - That's not why you're looking at her like that. Romeo Salvador - True enough. Gloria Lavin - Quick, Romes, go injure her and then ask her out. Romeo Salvador - Shouldn't you be posing naked somewhere or marrying another woman right now?
Gothic Angel stands Ebony up and places her in an abdominal stretch. The ref asks Ebony if she quits and Ebony shakes her head. Ebony reaches for the ropes which are mere inches away. Gothic Angel struggles to hold Ebony where she is. Ebony manages to lunge for and grab the ropes to force the break. Ebony then turns and roundhouse kicks Gothic Angel in the back of the head. Ebony follows this up with a DDT and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gothic Angel. Ebony then stands Gothic Angel up, whips her into the corner post and lands a dropkick on her as she comes out of it. Ebony then stands Gothic Angel up, whips her off the ropes and gives her a headscissors to the mat. Ebony tries for the pin. 1...2... Gothic Angel reverses the pin. 1...2... kick out by Ebony. Gothic Angel stands Ebony up, whips her into the corner buckle and then lands a spine buster on her. Gothic Angel in control now picks Ebony up and delivers a brain buster on her. She then stands EWbony up, whips her off the ropes and superkicks her in the chin and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Gothic Angel gets the win as Ebony kicks out just a moment too late!
Gloria Lavin - The win goes to the gothic one this week. Romeo Salvador - She is quite talented. Gloria Lavin - Stop leering. Boy Band Time!! As Gothic Angel leaves the ring and Ebony rolls herself out the other side, the crowd cheers. There is a a large sign proclaiming that the coming of Flava is next. Gothic Angel makes her way to the back without incident. Ebony heads alongside the ramp to the back, also without incident. We then see Ingee Shmelky emerge at the top of the ramp as "Numb" by Linkin Park plays. He smiles and waves to the crowd, his bling bling down to his waist and a Flava T-shirt in his right hand. He tosses it into the crowd and announces to that section he signed it personally. Ingee blows kisses to fans. He enters the ring and grabs a microphone.
Ingee Shmelky - There comes a time in every boy's life when he needs to be a man. I am going to be a man this week as I name my band members for the greatest boy band ever! *loud pop* Bring on Shengee and Bass, the newest members of Flava!
Shawn Michaels' "Sexy Boy" plays as Shengee poses at the top of the ramp and heads to the ring with "Bass" close behind. Bass is wearing a large hat and what appears to be a mustache as "he" steps into the ring. Ingee looks at him in an odd way and asks Shengee a question as Shengee kisses his bicep.
Ingee Shmelky - So you and Bass here ready to kick it up to the next level? Shengee Shmelky - Yo yo yo! Word to your mother! Peace home boyyyyyyyyyyyyssss!!! Ingerella here be right homes! The heartbreak albino, little Black Bass here and the Ingerella shall rock dis damned town tonight! You ready to rock, Peoria?! *another loud pop*
From the crowd emerges Frank Merritt. He climbs into the ring and smiles. Ingee Shmelky - Mister Merritt, sir, you gonna join Flava? Frank Merritt - I am gonna make sure the booking around here changes. Whoever decided this segment deserved to be ahead of me caused this.
Ingee Shmelky - Caused what? Frank Merritt rams Shengee and Ingee's heads together. He then kicks Ingee in the back, picks Shengee up and delivers a FU, dropping him onto Ingee hard. He then menaces towards Bass who drops to "his" chest and rolls out of the ring. Frank then heads to the ropes, climbs to the top and lands a shooting star press on the fallen Shmelky Brothers, ending tonight's hopes for a boy band concert.
Romeo Salvador - Frank just pulled a pretty low stunt here. Gloria Lavin - Agreed. Sickening. Frank poses in the ring, mocking Shengee. He then puts the boots to the fallen Shmelkys again. He heads out of the ring and grabs a chair. He then brings the chair into the ring and clubs Shengee in the back with it fice times. The crowd cheers as Scavenger heads out from the back. He slides into the ring, avoids a chair shot from Frank Merritt and gives him a stunner. Frank drops the chair and rolls out of the ring. Scavenger picks the chair up and swings over the top rope at Frank, narrowly missing him. Scavenger then attends to the fallen Shmelkys.
Gloria Lavin - Frank with some nasty intentions here tonight. Romeo Salvador - He got Scav's attention and I'm not sure if that's what he wanted. Gloria Lavin - But I imagine Flava will continue on despite this. Backstage we see Lightning talking to someone in the locker room. That person is just out of sight. As the camera pulls in for a closer look, Lightning spots the camera and shuts the door.
Gloria Lavin - What the hell is he up to? Romeo Salvador - He wouldn't be a CWA star without a little mystery. I am sure it's nothing.
Night Dweller vs Pyro "Mother" by Danzig plays as the crowd cheers the arrival of Dark Forces leader Night Dweller. He makes his way to the ring and high fives fans on his way down. He steps onto the apron and salutes the crowd once before stepping through the ropes. He then waits for Pyro. Pyro enters the ring behind him before Night Dweller's music is even done playing. Pyro attacks Night Dweller with a clothesline to the back of the head and then places him in a Crippler Crossface as the bell sounds to actually start the match. The ref is already asking Night Dweller if he gives. Night Dweller reaches for and manages to grab the bottom rope and force the break of the hold. Pyro then gets up and starts putting the boots to Night Dweller until Dweller is kicked right out of the ring. Night Dweller gets up holding his ribs and takes a la plancha through the ropes by Pyro. Pyro then lands a series of rights and lefts to the head of Night Dweller.
Romeo Salvador - The leader of Dark Forces doesn't look very dark or scary here tonight. Pyro is really taking it to him.
Gloria Lavin - Pyro with an attack before the bell here. Night Dweller paying the price for not being on his toes in his first Breakthrough match up.
Pyro stands Night Dweller up and whips him into the steel ring post head first. He then rolls Night Dweller into the ring and climbs onto the apron. He smiles as the crowd boos him. Pyro then steps through the ropes and grabs Night dweller by the hair. Night Dweller is bleeding badly as Pyro whips Night Dweller shoulder first into the turnbuckle. Pyro then starts pulling the pritective padding off of the rope in the corner as Night Dweller struggles to get to his feet. Pyeo rakes the eyes of Dweller and then rams his head into the exposed steel in the corner. He then whips Night Dweller to the opposite corner and drops him with a clothesline as he comes back out of it. Pyro goes for the pin. 1...2... Night Dweller gets a foot on the bottom rope to keep the match alive.
Romeo Salvador - Dweller is bleeding pretty bad here. Gloria Lavin - The ref will have to at least think about stopping this due to blood loss. Pyro stands Night Dweller up, whips him into the corner where the buckle is exposed and charges in. Night Dweller drops to his knees and watches as Pyro slams head first into the steel corner he exposed. Night Dweller then gets to his feet and elbows Pyro on the top of the head. Pyro again rakes the eyes of Night Dweller. Night Dweller shakes his head, grabs at his eyes with one hand and delivers a thunderous chop to Pyro's chest with the other. Pyro flies across the ring and falls on his back, now also bleeding from the corner buckle shot he just suffered. Night Dweller shakes off the effects of Pyro's attacks and stands him up and delivers a gutwrench suplex on Pyro as he tries to gain control in this match up. Night Dweller then delivers a knee to the chest followed by a two handed choke to the throat which the ref quickly forces a break of.
Gloria Lavin - Well, Night Dweller is taking control here, but he is losing a lot of blood even now.
Romeo Salvador - Night Dweller has the crimson mask on. He's gonna get weak quick with that amount of blood being spilled.
Night Dweller stands Pyro up and gives him a tilt a whirl backbreaker and goes for the pin. 1...2... Pyro kicks out. With that, we see Max Cameron making his way to the ring which gets Night Dweller's attention immediately. He heads outside the ring and meets Max Cameron on the ramp. Pyro slides out of the ring and attacks Night Dweller from behind with a double axe handle to the back. He then rolls Night Dweller into the ring and takes something from Max Cameron. Pyro drills Night Dweller in the throat and then tosses the object out of the ring.
Gloria Lavin - Max brought a piece of metal out here for Pyro to nail Night Dweller with! Romeo Salvador - Those idiots in Youthful Aggression never learn. Pyro goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Pyro then starts putting the boots to Night Dweller as Max enters the ring to join in. From the back comes Cossack and Black Panther and Pyro and Max clear out of the ring and escape through the crowd. Cossack and Panther attend to their fallen leader.
Romeo Salvador - Good, another group can fight with them for awhile. I am sick of seeing my homeboys fighting with them all the damned time.
Gloria Lavin - Pyro gets the win here, but this feud has just started. Backstage we see Rated R and the crowd goes wild. He is with Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - Rated R! It is great to see you back in the CWA! Why did you leave? Rated R - That's none of your business. Joe Davidson - Where have you been? Rated R - Also none of your business. Joe Davidson - Why did you choose now to make a comeback? Rated R - You're not listening. Rated R leaves Joe standing alone. Joe looks at the camera and shrugs. Scavenger vs Fallout "Know" by System of a Down plays and Dante leads Fallout to the ring. He applauds as Fallout enters the ring and demands that the crowd do the same. Of course, they answer with a loud chorus of boos. He gives them the finger as "My Girlfriend's Girfriend" by Type O Negative starts to play and Scavenger starts out from the back. He salutes the fans from the ramp and makes his way down with the same chair in hand he wrested away from Frank Merritt. He heads to ringside and has a few words with Dante. He then places the chair next to the apron and enters the ring to the cheers of the crowd.
Romeo Salvador - Has Scavenger adopted that chair he hit Merritt with? Gloria Lavin - Addictions die hard, Romes.
