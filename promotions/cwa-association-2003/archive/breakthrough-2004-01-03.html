CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Breakthrough, 3 January 2004

3 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
St. Louis, Missouri is the setting for this week's Breakthrough, Welcome to 2004 signs are everywhere as the fans cheer wildly. The first show of the new year appears to be very well attended and looks like it will be a very good one. We immediately head to ringside where Romeo Salvador and Gloria Lavin are ready to get things underway.
Kris vs Executioner "Dirge" by Death In Vegas plays as Executioner makes his way to the ring and catches the derision of the crowd. He is led to the ring by Tina as usual and slides under the bottom rope and then poses in the corner for the fans who don't like him any more this week than they have any previous week to date. He then has a few words with the ref as "Bad Religion" by Godsmack begind to play and Kris makes his way to the ring to a much stronger reaction. He races into the ring and he and Executioner start trading shots as the bell sounds to officially get things underway.
Gloria Lavin - And there's no wasting time with introductions this week as Kris hits the ring and goes straight to the attack.
Romeo Salvador - Glad to see it. Tonight my fans get to watch as I become the Aerial champion heading into Battle of the Warriors.
Gloria Lavin - Well, at least you're confident about it. Executioner whips Kris into the corner and clotheslines him as he comes out of it. He then stands Kris up, whips him to the opposite corner, but this time misses with the clothesline and Kris kicks Executioner in the gut as he turns to face him. Kris then lands a series of forearms to the head of Executioner and then whips him into the corner and dropkicks him in the chest as he comes out. Kris then tries for an early pin. 1...2... kick out by Executioner. Kris stands Executioner up, then snap mares him to the mat and places him in a front face lock. Executioner appears to be in pain from the hold, but quickly rolls out of it and away from Kris. Executioner then waits till Kris is on his feet before sweeping the legs out from under him and placing him in an ankle lock. Kris lunges for and grabs the bottom rope and the ref forces a break. Executioner is to his feet first and pulls Kris to his feet, then picks him up for a power slam. He nails it and tries to pin Kris. 1...2.... Kris gets a foot on the bottom rope.
Gloria Lavin - Smart use of the ropes there by Kris. Romeo Salvador - Executioner may have won this match right there if he had pulled him to the middle of the ring.
Executioner stands Kris up, then whips him off the ropes and lands a roundhouse kick to the chest of Kris. Kris staggers backwards and falls through the ropes to the floor. Executioner follows him out while Tina cheers from ringside. Executioner ramsd Kris head first into the guard railing. He then stands Kris up and take an eye rake, then Kris bounces Eceutioner's head off the top of the guard railing. Kris then rolls into the ring and awaits Executioner with a smile on his face. Executioner enters the ring on his hands and knees and Kris gives him a leg drop to the back of the neck which bounces his face off the mat. Kris then rolls him over and hooks the leg for the pin. 1...2...3! Kris wins! Kris rolls out of the ring as Tina enters it in protest and trying to tell the ref Kris had grabbed tights that he really hadn't. The ref isn't hearing any of that and leaves Tina there compalining.
Gloria Lavin - Nice win for Kris as he starts out his CWA career on a good note. Romeo Salvadro - For sure, he has to be proud of this win. Backstage we see Frank Merritt in the locker room getting ready for the match tonight. Gerard Angelo enters the frame and smiles.
Gerard Angelo - There is no harm in admitting you cannot get past us, you know. Frank Merritt - Go blow yourself. Gerard Angelo - You are fighting myself and Carnage this week. This is going to be horrible for you.
Frank Merritt - Why are you still here? I'll kick your ass in a minute. Gerard laughs to himself as he leaves the room and we head back to ringside for our next match up.
Pandora vs TJ Mercer "Imaginary" by Evanescence plays as TJ Mercer makes his way to the ring for this week's debut match. He gets a warm reception and apparently has done well in dark matches leading to this debut here tonight. As he steps into the ring, "Black and Blue" by Rancid plays and the crowd cheers as Pandora makes her way to the ring. She has little to say to the fans as she makes her way down the ramp, yet another female in the growing list of female stars in the CWA. She steps onto the apron and is allowed entry to the ring by TJ as the bell sounds. The two circle each other and then lock up. TJ then shoves Pandora into the ropes, and gives her a fisherman's suplex as she comes back to the middle of the ring. Pandora then rolls herself out of the ring to collect herself.
Gloria Lavin - A pair of debuts here this week. Romeo Salvador - I know which pair I like better. Gloria Lavin - How did I know you'd say that? Romeo Salvador - Because I am male. TJ stands Pandora up, then delivers a body slam on her. He heads to the ropes, climbs to second rope and dives off to land a knee to the forehead of Pandora before trying for the pin. 1...2... Pandora kicks out. TJ stands Pandora up, whips her off the ropes and lowers his head to try for a backdrop only to have Pandora turn and land a scissor kick on TJ before rolling him up for the quick pin attempt. 1...2... kick out by TJ Mercer. Pandora then stands TJ up, and then snap mares him to the mat before placing him in a grapevine submission. Problem is, she doesn't have the arm strength to hold TJ there and he battles his way out of it and kicks Pandora in the gut and then DDTs her to the mat. He then heads for the ropes and begins his climb to the top. Pandora slowly gets to her feet, turns, see a diving TJ and dropkcisk him in mid air and the collision knocks both of them to the mat hard.
Gloria Lavin - Nice defensive move by Pandora. Romeo Salvador - Shame she appears to have knocks herself out. Speaking of knockers... Gloria Lavin - Don't say it, Romes. TJ gets to his feet first as the ref almost had them both counted down for ten. He then grabs Pandora by the hair and pulls her to her feet. He whips Pandora off the ropes and lands a dropkick to the chest on her. He then jumps to the second rope and moonsaults onto her and goes for the pin. 1...2...3!
Gloria Lavin - That move is called the End Game and it lived up to its name as it ended this one after a spirited effort by Pandora.
Romeo Salvador - This game is done, but there are six more games to come tonight, including me winning the Aerial title against Pyro. I then head to Battle of the Warriors as the favorite for the million bucks and the women.
Backstage we see Sierra making her way to the locker room when she is headed off by Menace.
Sierra - Out of my way. Menace - So, after the show is done tonight and Scavenger is done, where are we headed? Sierra - You're heading home alone and I am heading home with my man. Menace - Your man isn't headed home. He is headed to the hospital. Sierra pushes past Menace and he appears ready to strike her, but he only smiles and shakes his head instead.
6Pac Dudley vs Tater Evans "I Will Survive" by Gloria Gaynor plays as Tater Evans makes his way to the ring. He receives a pretty standard lukewarm response as the fans still try to figure out what the deal with this guy really is. He steps into the ring and poses in the corner as "Southtown" by POD plays and 6Pac Dudley makes his way to the ring. He gets an ovation as he carries the Canadian flag to the ring. He hands the flag to a stage hand and then enters the ring. Tater comes over and grinds against the leg of 6Pac, causing Dudley to shove him to the mat as the match begins.
Romeo Salvador - Now that was creepy. Gloria Lavin - Mr Sweetness excels at creepy. Evans gets to his feet and smiles as 6Pac circles him, trying to guage how to fight this guy. The two lock up and Evans drops and monkey flips 6Pac through the second and top rope to the floor. Evans then gets to his feet and smiles as he leaps through those same ropes to land a la plancha on 6Pac. Evans then stands 6Pac up and attempts to whip him into the steel steps only to have 6Pac reverse it and send Evans shoulder first into them. 6Pac then rolls himself back into the ring. Evans gets to his feet holding his shoulder before rolling himself back into the ring and beating the ref's count. 6Pac then grabs the sore arm of Evans and places him in an arm bar. He then places the arm over the top rope and tangles it there as the ref demands a break. 6Pac breaks the hold and gouges the eyes of Evans before whipping him off the ropes and then hip tossing him to the mat. He then pins him face down in a hammerlock and the ref starts asking Evans if he gives up.
Gloria Lavin - 6Pac really focussing on the damaged shoulder here. Romeo Salvador - 6Pac is the master of this kind of attack. This is resembling hard core tactics and he loves those.
Evans reaches for the grabs the rope with his free hand to forces the break. Evans then rolls himself out of the ring where the damage was done the first time. 6Pac waits till Evans gets up, then launches himself over the top rope for a Senton only to have Evans sidestep it and watch 6Pac land painfully on the floor. Evans then gets onto the apron and lands a roundhouse kick to the head of 6Pac as he stands up. Evans then steps through the ropes into the ring as the ref keeps counting. 6Pac grabs the bottom rope as he hears the ref reach a count of 6 and pulls himself into the ring. Evans pulls 6Pac to his feet by the hair and lands a haymaker on him, then gyrates before landing another which knocks him to the mat. Evans goes for the pin. 1..2... 6Pac reverses the pin. 1...2... Evans kicks out. 6Pac then gets to his feet first, stands Evans up and whips him into the corner before delivering the Bottle Smasher on him as he stumbles back out of the corner. 6Pac goes for the pin. 1...2...3! 6Pac Dudley wins the match.
Romeo Salvador - For me, there was never any doubt my bro there was gonna win this one. Gloria Lavin - Touching indeed. Backstage we see Tony Parizi with Q. They don't know that the camera is on them. Tony Parizi - I keep hearing rumblings backstage that you're not certain of aligning with me, Mister Q.
Q - I'm not and you knew that. I simply seeing this as a means to an end. Tony Parizi - This is not about ownership and you know that. Sally and me have work to do. Shannon tried screwing us out of what is rightfully ours. That was a big mistake. He will pay for that.
Q - Yes, yes, you get Shannon and I get the CWA. Tony Parizi - I paid a lot of money to get him medically cleared to wrestle Sally, you know.
Q - Fine. We can talk later about all of this. They part and we head back to the ring. Frank Merritt vs Gerard Angelo/Carnage "Solitaire Unravelling" by Mushroomhead plays as Frank Merritt appears at the top of the ramp in green light wearing his standard bomber jacket. He unzips it and starts to the ring when Carnage and Gerard Angelo attack him from behind on the ramp. They toss him off the ramp and onto the guard railing. Angelo doves off and lands a double axe handle to the back of Merritt and then Carnage does the same from the ramp. They then pull Merritt off of the railing and drag him to ringside where they ram him head first into the ring steps.
Romeo Salvador - Well, Frank is outnumbered and appears to be beaten before this match even has begun.
Gloria Lavin - He really should have seen this coming as he employs the same tactic himself on occasion.
Angelo rolls Merritt into the ring and the bell sounds as Angelo enters the ring and Carnage takes his place in the corner waiting for the tag. Angelo stands Merritt up and gives him a series of knife edge chops which send him reeling to the ropes. Angelo then chokes Merritt over the top rope until the ref forces the break. Angelo then pulls Merritt to his corner and tags in Carnage. He holds Merritt open for a kick to the ribs from Carnage before leaving the ring. Carnage then places Merritt into an abdominal stretch in an attempt to make the Flock member submit. Instead, Frank starts to power his way out and manages to hip toss Carnage to the mat. Angelo jumps to the top rope behind Merritt, calls to him and then lands a flying body press on him from the top rope.
Gloria Lavin - Merritt being overwhelmed by the fact he has to fight two men here. Romeo Salvador - It's a tall task when a wrestler is good and trying this. Merritt isn't even good.
Gloria Lavin - This is a matter of opinion. Romeo Salvador - Yep, you can quote me on that. Merritt gets to his feet and takes a right hook from Carnage as Angelo is forced out of the ring. Carnage then tries a thumb to the eye which Merritt blocks. Merritt then clotheslines Carnage to the mat and starts stomping him to the mat. Merritt then stands Carnage up and whips him into the corner buckle, then chop blocks him as he comes out and places him in an ankle lock submission hold. The crowd appears to be getting behind underdog Frank here as Carnage reaches for the ropes and screams that he won't submit. Carnage lunges for the ropes, fails to reach them, but he manages to roll onto his side enough that he kicks Merritt away from him and into his own corner. Carnage charges as Angelo holds Frank there. Frank breaks free and Carnage spears Angelo off the apron by accident as Frank Merritt rolls Carnage up for the pin. 1...2...3! Frank Merritt wins the handicap match!
Romeo Salvador - Nice win for Merritt as Carnage and Gerard Angelo have a mishap there. Gloria Lavin - Merritt will take the win and get out while the getting is good. Backstage we see Ingee Shmelky with a chart in his right hand as Charley MacAdam stands to his left.
Charley MacAdam - I understand this chart is going to be filled with names for the Best Wrestler in the World Tournament at Battle of the Warriors.
Ingee Shmelky - It sure is, Charley. Monday night we announce the match ups for all sixteen participants at Battle of the Warriors. Flava will be selling T-shirts there, too.
Charley MacAdam - Any truth to the Pamela Anderson- Shengee rumors? Ingee Shmelky - They are just friends. Tommy and Kid Rock are intimidated by his manliness according to my brother.
Charley MacAdam - All is well between the two of you? Ingee Shmelky - Yes, it is. He still calls me named and hits me a lot, but we're cool. Charley MacAdam - That doesn't sound well at all. Ingee Shmelky - You're right. It doesn't. Ingee hangs his head as we return to the ring. Andon vs Abe "God of the Mind" by Disturbed plays as Abe makes his way out to a huge ovation. He makes his way down the ramp only to be attacked by Andon who emerges from the back with a belt in his hand which he chokes Abe with. The ref leaves the ring and heads to the ramp where he warns Andon that he is ready to throw this match out if he doesn't stop. Andon releases the choke with the belt and smacks Abe across the back with it, the black leather leaving a welt. He then tosses the belt aside and pulls Abe down to ringside. He then rolls Abe into the ring and steps onto the apron and poses for the booing masses. Andon steps through the ropes and smiles.
Gloria Lavin - This man is sick. Romeo Salvador - His obsession with Gothic Angel has led him into dark territory and I hear there may be charges coming out of it against him. This is a twisted tale indeed.
Andon stands Abe up and gives him a punch to the throat. Andon then uses the top rope to rake the eyes of Abe. Andon follows this up by whipping Abe off the ropes. He tries for a backdrop. Instead Abe shoulder rolls across the back of Andon, lands on his feet and places him in a sleeperhold in the middle of the ring to the delight of the crowd. The ref checks on Andon as he appears to be going out. Andon flails about to reach for the ropes, but they are too far away. The ref checks on Andon as his arms drop. He raises the arm and it drops once. He raises the arm and it drops a second time. He then raises it a third time, but Andon man ages to keep his arm up, then kicks Abe in the shin hard to break the hold and grabs the ropes and tries to catch his breath.
Gloria Lavin - Andon barely escaped that one as Abe takes control. Romeo Salvador - This match is very emotionally charged. Abe grabs Andon by the hair and pulls him to the middle of the ring again. Andon then lands a jawbreaker on Abe. Andon then starts two hand choking Abe. The crowd then cheers as we see Gothic Angel coming out of the back. She has a hatred in her eyes that we haven't seen before and Andon immediately takes notice of her. He release his choke on Abe. He slides out of the ring and stands at the foot of the ramp as Gothic Angel stands half way down looking at him with a combination of fear and hatred.
Gloria Lavin - Brave girl. Romeo Salvador - Or a stupid girl. Andon takes a step towards her as he professes his love to her and she takes a step back. Andon then hears the ref counting and rolls into the ring to break up the count before rolling out again. Abe catches his breath and slowly gets to his feet. He spots Andon outside the ring pleading for Gothic Angel's love as she shakes her head and a tear rolls down her cheek. Abe climbs to the top rope and dives on Andon, dropkicking him in the back of the head. Andon falls forward and Gothic Angel pumps her fist and tells Abe to kill him. Abe stands Andon up and whips him head first into the steel corner post. Abe then kicks him in the gut and DDTs him on the floor. Abe then rolls Andon into the ring, steps onto the apron and somersaults over the top rope and leg drops Andon to the back of the head as the blood pours from his brow. Abe rolls Andon over for the pin. 1...2...3! Abe picks up the win.
Romeo Salvador - Well, score a win over the sick bastard Andon. Gloria Lavin - This isn't over between him and Gothic Angel. I can feel it. Backstage we see Pyro pacing. Joe Davidson enters the frame. Joe Davidson - Pyro, any reaction to the fall of Andon moments ago? Pyro - Joe, I have a match in a bit here. Why would I necessarily care about that? I have had a bad week.
Joe Davidson - Thoughts on Romeo Salvador? Pyro - The guy is a joke and he knows it. Joe Davidson - So you're confident in your abilities here tonight. Pyro - Joe, I already said I wasn't in the mood once. Get the **** out of here. Joe retreats and we head back to the ring. Menace vs Scavenger "Stunt 101" by G Unit plays as Menace makes his way to the ring to the boos of the crowd. Menace enters the ring and seems to soak in the negative energy from the crowd as if it was fuel for tonight's match up. We then hear "Sanitarium" by Metallica and Scavenger comes out to a strong reaction. He is wearing black tights and a black T-shirt with "Got chair?" written on the back and a picture of the many chairs of Scavenger on the front. He is led by Sierra who has a black leather skirt on and a red sweater. She takes her place at ringside in her spiked heels as the meaner looking Scavenger has a few words with Menace from ringside. He has the chair in one hand and appears to be ready to use it. Scavenger steps into the ring and Menace takes a step towards him when he realizes Scavenger has yet to drop the chair.
Romeo Salvador - This is not the usual Scavenger. New music and a new look. Gloria Lavin - He appears to have gone all Tommy Dreamer on us because of comments that Menace made this week regarding his heart. I can't help but notice he has a black armband on with his father's name on it.
Romeo Salvador - Anyone who knows Scavenger in the back knows he has a lot of heart. That bothered him.
The two lock up as the bell sounds. Scavenger throws Menace into the corner. Menace laughs and asks if Scavenger can do better than that. Scavenger answers with a spear. The crowd erupts. Scavenger goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Menace. Scavenger stands Menace up, whips him off the ropes and tries for a clothesline only to have Menace duck it and then give Scavenger a side Russian leg sweep. Menace then lands an elbow smash to the throat of Scavenger. Menace follows this up by standing Scavenger up, whipping him off the ropes and backdropping him hard to the mat. Menace then stands Scavenger up and picks him up for a power bomb. He lands it and the crowd is silenced as he goes for the pin. 1...2... Scavenger kicks out with enough power to launch Menace across the ring!
Romeo Salvador - Scavenger knows it is Battle of the Warriors time. He always looks good at this time of year is what I am told.
Scavenger does a kip up to his feet and a dazed Menace stumbles into a series of right and lefts from the now boxing Scavenger. Scavenger then measures him for a bionic elbow before delivering a bulldog on Menace and going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Menace. Scavenger stands Menace up and tosses him through the ropes to the floor. Scavenger then follows him out and grabs an electrical cable and whips Menace with it. Menace turns, wincing in pain and thumbs Scavenger in the eye. He then throws Scavenger into the guard railing and starts putting the boots to him as Scavenger tries to get to his feet. Sierra is then spotted by Menace and he smiles and takes a step towards her. Again the male weakness for the fairer sex shows up. Scavenger gets to his feet and charges at Menace only to have Menace step to the side, trip Scavenger up and place him in an STF. The ref keeps the count going, reaches ten and this match is over, a double count out.
Gloria Lavin - No winner here between these two. Romeo Salvador - They both came out here with something to prove and I think that they both proved it.
Menace releases the STF and heads for Sierra again and she starts to run. She stands on the opposite side of the Spanish announce table as Menace grabs for her. Scavenger gets to his feet, then charges at Menace and spears him through the table! Wood flies everywhere as both men lie amonth the splinters and monitors that were a table mere moments earlier and the "HOLY SHIT" chants start. Both men are cut by the metal they just went through. Scavenger spits blood as he rolls over. He then stands Menace up and the two start going at it toe to toe. Officials stream out of the back to split them up.
Romeo Salvador - This will add to the hatred between these two. Gloria Lavin - No doubt about that. Menace and Scavenger start punching the officials as they are incoming. They go as far as to pick up the monitors and start using them as weapons against each other. They then clock each other with montiors and both men go down where they are mobbed by the men in striped shirts and suits. We hear them screaming at each other from beneath the rubble and bodies. Sierra retreats to the ramp and to the back, knowing this is a good time to get out. Menace and Scavenger are dragged around oppositew sides of the ring as we head to the back where Vigilante is standing with Ingee Shmelky.
Vigilante - Look, this is pretty simple, Ingee. I want to know who I am fighting in the tournament now.
Ingee Shmelky - I haven't got the brackets yet. Vigilante - Look, I am still pissed you didn't book the Moose match I demanded on my show. I wanted that match. Gerard Angelo and a partner against Moose.... steel cage. Where is it?
Ingee Shmelky - My secretary said no. Vigilante - Lunacy Muse? You're taking orders from that? Dizz enters the frame to a huge pop from the crowd. Dizz - Yo, Ingee, who am I facing in this tournament thing? Ingee Shmelky - I have not got the brackets done yet. Dizz - Look, albino, there is a a million dollars on the line and I am winning it. It's that simple.
Ingee Shmelky - Monday, you guys all find out Monday. Ingee leaves in a hurry as Vigilante and Dizz look at each other. They both scowl. Vigilante - What? Dizz - Nothing. What yourself? Vigilante - Also nothing. Dizz - Good then. We then head back to the ring. Gothic Angel vs Tiffany We hear "Soldier" by Eminem as Tiffany heads to the ring with Chain Smoke close behind her and Kill Switch appears close behind him. It's an odd trio to be sure. Tiffany jumps up onto the apron and the crowd cheers her as pyrotechnics go off and she enters the ring. We then hear "Spin Me Round" by Dope and Gothic Angel starts on her way to the ring. As she makes her way to the ring, we see Andon, blood running down his face, on the Titantron.
Andon - My love, I forgive you. I forgive you for what I am going to do. Someday soon, my love.
He fades away from the screen and Toffany has a few words with Chain Smoke and KillSwitch about Andon. Gothic Angel shudders as she finishes making her way to the ring. She steps onto the apron, enters the ring and the bell sounds. The two ladies lock up and Tiffany takes the early advantage, placing Gothic Angel into a headlock. Gothic Angel quickly breaks that and whips Tiffany off the ropes and then gives her a spinning heel kick which knocks her to the mat. Gothic Angel looks to the ramp, paranoid that Andon might be coming for her. Gothic Angel stands Tiffany up, whips her into the corner, then races into the corner, cartwheels and does a handstand. She places Tiffany in a headscissors and then flips her out of the corner to the middle of the ring. Gothic Angel tries for the pin. 1...2... Tiffany kicks out.
Romeo Salvador - Nothing creepy about the start to this one, the bloodied Andon declaring his love for this woman?
Gloria Lavin - I always say I have seen everything in my time here and then something new comes along. He's like ther Terminator with sex drive.
Romeo Salvador - The Sperminator? Gloria Lavin - That sounds very wrong. Gothic Angel stands Tiffany up and whips her into the corner. She then charges headlong into an elbow from Tiffany. Tiffany then picks Gothic Angel up for a vertical suplex. Tiffany stands Gothic Angel up and gives her a snap suplex. Tiffany heads to the ropes. She climbs to the top rope as Gothic Angel gets to her feet. Tiffany tries for a flying body press, but Gothic Angel catches her in mid air and lands a spinning power slam on Tiffany and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tiffany. Gothic Angel gets to her feet and grabs Tiffany by the hair. Tiffany hip tosses Gothic Angel through the ropes to the floor. Tiffany climbs to the top rope and prepares to dive on her when we hear Andon on the microphone from the top of the ramp.
Andon - My love must pay. Andon charges to ringside as KillSwitch and Chain Smoke look at each other and shrug. Andon charges straight into a 3-D on the floor! Tiffany lands a flying cross body on the distracted Gothic Angel before rolling her into the ring. Tiffany then stands Gothic Angel up and lands the Heinous Situation and goes for the pin. 1...2...#! Tiffany with the win here over Gothic Angel!
Romeo Salvador - One good distraction deserves another. Gloria Lavin - Gothic Angel is not getting rid of this guy. He's risking life and limb to get to her.
Romeo Salvador - Been fun. I am up next. Main event Romeo time. Gloria Lavin - Good luck. Romeo Salvador - Thanks, babe. The crowd cheers as Romeo plants one on Gloria and she responds positively, running her hands down his back. Romeo pulls away, looks to the roof and screams, then steps into the ring. Andon is being cleared away by EMTs for the second time tonight. We then hear "PIMP" by 50 Cent playing as Romeo stands in the ring and the fans cheer him on.
Pyro vs Romeo Salvador As Romeo stands in the ring ready, the fans starting to chant his name, we hear "PIMP" fade out and "Immortal" by Adema plays as Pyro makes his way out of the back with the Aerial title around his waist. He looks cautious as he slowly makes his way to the ring.
Gloria Lavin - Pyro looks uncertain right now as he heads into this title defense. Henry Lesher grabs Romeo's headset and sits down. Henry Lesher - That is because Pyro is a putz. Gloria Lavin - Where the hell did you come from? Henry Lesher - I am the unholy spawn of you kissing Romeo Salvador. Shut up and call the match.
Pyro steps into the ring and points to the belt and tells Romeo he's not getting it. Romeo beckons him on. Pyro charges with the intention of belt shotting Romeo in the head. Instead, Romeo ducks it and elbows Pyro in the back as the bell sounds. He then gives him a reverse neckbreaker and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Pyro. Romeo then stands Pyro up, kicks the belt out of the ring and headbutts him three times which sends him into the corner. Romeo then snap mares Pyro to the middle of the ring and goes for a quick pin. 1...2... Pyro kicks out and then rolls himself out of the ring.
Gloria Lavin - The action getting a bit too hot for Pyro. Henry Lesher - Battling it out over a title so few care about. Gloria Lavin - Do you ever have anything nice to say? Henry Lesher - No, but I usually smile when I insult people. Gloria Lavin - Wonderful. Inside the ring, Romeo stands Pyro up and takes an eye gouge. Pyro then whips Romeo off the ropes and lands a dropkick on him. Pyro then picks Romeo up and gives him a sidewalk slam. Pyro then heads to the ropes, climbs to the top and lands a five star frog splash on Romeo and goes for the pin. 1...2... Romeo gets a foot on the bottom rope.
Henry Lesher - And the idiot behind door number one who doesn't know how to hook the leg is...
Pyro stands Romeo up, then whips him into the corner and gives him an atomic drop which sends him face first to the buckle. Romeo falls back out of the buckle and Pyro clotheslines him to the mat. Pyro then heads back to the ropes and climbs to the top. As he reaches the top, Romeo kips up to his feet. Romeo then grabs Pyro off the top and slams him to the mat. Romeo then stands and looks to the crowd who immediately get to their feet. Pyro gets up and Romeo nails him with the Invertabreaker! Romeo rolls him over for the pin. 1...2...3! Romeo wins and is the new CWA Aerial champion!
Gloria Lavin - He won! He won! Henry Lesher - I thought you preferred shrubs over trees. Gloria Lavin - I beg your pardon. Henry Lesher - Just go get a room with him and get it over with. Good night everybody. We fade to black with Northern Exposure members crashing the ring to lift the new Aerial champion up. Romeo is king for a day. Tune in next week for more exciting CWA action!!
