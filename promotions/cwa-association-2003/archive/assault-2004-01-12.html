CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 12 January 2004

12 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The crowd cheers as the Titantron displays their smiling faces as we head to Cincinnati, Ohio and another edition of Assault. There are eight action packed matches on the way.
Gerard Angelo vs Pandora "Tear Away" by Drowning Pool plays as Gerard Angelo makes his way to the ring. As he does, we see Pandora sneaking out behind him. He steps onto the apron, still unaware that she is right behind him. He steps through the ropes, turns and takes a clothesline from Pandora, relying on the element of surprise.
Ricky Simms - With that we begin another edition of Assault. Henry Lesher - A show marred by the absence of Dante, Q, Damien and anyone else I give a damn about. It surprises me that I actually showed up today.
Ricky Simms - And we are oh so grateful that you did. Inside the ring, Pandora stands Angelo up, whips him off the ropes and levels him with a roundhouse kick to the jaw. Pandora then stands Angelo up, whips him off the ropes and tries for another only to have Angelo duck the kick. Angelo then gives Pandora a spinebuster on the mat and tries for a quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Pandora. Angelo then stands her up, whips her into the corner face first and then gives her a side Russian leg sweep to the mat. Angelo then rolls her up for another attempted pin. 1...2... another kick out by Pandora.
Ricky Simms - A couple of near falls for Gerard Angelo there. Henry Lesher - We know how much I care about almost win,s Rick. Angelo stands Pandora up, then snap mares her to the mat and places her in a sleeper. Pandora uses her foot to snag the nearby bottom rope and the ref forces the break. Angelo gets to his feet and lays the boots to Pandora, eventually kicking her right out of the ring as the fans boo his every move. Angelo then climbs to the top rope and waits for Pandora to get up. She turns to face him. He dives and misses as she sidesteps him and watches his head bounce off the guard railing. Pandora then rolls herself into the ring. Angelo gets to his feet slowly, turns and takes a la plancha from Pandora to the delight of the crowd.
Ricky Simms - Nice move there by Pandora. Henry Lesher - Not caring, but nice move yes. Pandora then stands Angelo up and whips him into the steel corner post. She charges only to be grabbed by Angelo and given a power bomb on the floor. Angelo smiles, then rolls Pandora into the ring. Angelo then rolls himself into the ring and goes for the pin. 1...2... Pandora gets a foot on the bottom rope. Angelo climbs to the top rope, waits for Pandora to get to her feet and then delivers a DDT on her from there and tries for the pin. 1...2...3! Win for Gerard Angelo.
Ricky Simms - The man who calls himself the future grabs a win here. Henry Lesher - Future of what? Maybe of the fast food service industry and that's being generous.
Backstage we see Q standing next to Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - Q, this coming week you defend the tag titles and you get a shot at becoming the winner of a million dollars and a trophy as the best in wrestling today. What strategy might you have heading into this event?
Q - You expect me to tell you? Well, you're in luck. It is really very simple. I am going to keep the tag belts, win the million dollars and then buy up enough stock that I will be the ultimate owner of the CWA and I will make it stick, Joe.
Joe Davidson - Any words for Jakob, your first round opponent? Q - Yes, my condolences to his family on their upcoming loss. Kelly Buchanan vs Gothic Angel "Dirrty" by Christina Aguilera plays as Kelly Buchanan makes her way to the ring and flashbulbs go off and the crowd cheers. Kelly slides under the bottom rope into the ring and poses in the corner for the crowd before blowing them a kiss. We then hear "Spin Me 'Round" by Dope and Gothic Angel makes her way to the ring as Kelly jumps back and forth in the ring, anxious to get things underway. Gothic Angel steps onto the apron and laughs at Kelly. Kelly motions her inside and the bell sounds. The two circle each other, then lock up. Kelly throws Gothic Angel to the mat. Angel quickly shoulder rolls out of it and back to her feet. Kelly smiles and motions Gothic Angel to her. Gothic Angel charges into a drop toe hold from Buchanan. She then places Gothic Angel in a cross face chicken wing. Angel immediately grabs the rope to force the break.
Ricky Simms - Good start for Kelly here. Henry Lesher - Better than I figured she would have, but she still sucks. Gothic Angel gets to her feet. Kelly then comes off the ropes and attempts a dropkick which Gothic Angel manages to sidestep. Angel then takes Kelly into a headlock and gives her a bulldog to the mat before trying for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kelly Buchanan. Gothic Angel stands Kelly up, gives her a vicious knife edge chop into the corner, then does a hand stand and headlocks Kelly with her legs before rolling her out of the corner. Angel tries for the pin again. 1...2... kick out by Kelly Buchanan. Angel then heads to the ropes, climbs to the second rope and lands a double axe handle to the back of the neck of Buchanan. Angel then clothesline Kelly to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kelly Buchanan.
Ricky Simms - Well contested match here between these two ladies. Henry Lesher - These two what? This is Kelly Buchanan we are talking about right? Ricky Simms - Kelly Buchanan was a CWA Women's champion. Henry Lesher - That was before there were actual women here. Angel stands Kelly up, whips her into the corner and then gives her a belly to back suplex to the mat. Angel then gets to her feet and boot chokes Kelly using the bottom rope for leverage. The ref forces the break and Gothic Angel hears a few boos from the crowd. She then rolls Kelly out of the ring and heads for the ropes. She makes her way to the top rope as Kelly staggers. Kelly then takes a flying body press on the floor from Gothic Angel. Both women lie on the floor winded for a few moments before Gothic Angel gets to her feet first. She then headbutts Kelly to the floor before standing her up and rolling her into the ring.
Ricky Simms - Gothic Angel really in control of this one right now. Henry Lesher - I have already slammed Kelly twice now. Don't make me slam her again with such an easy opening.
As Angel readies to finish Kelly off, we see Andon emerge through the crowd with a chair in hand. He enters the ring and drifts Angel with it. Angel falls face down to the mat and Andon eyes her up with a creepy smile crossing his face as the ref asks for the bell. Abe charges into the ring with a chair and swings only to miss the retreating Andon. Abe then attends to Gothic Angel while Andon proclaims his love from ringside. The ring announcer declares Gothic Angel the winner by DQ as Kelly gets to her feet and tries to take in what's going on.
Ricky Simms - That sick and twisted Andon attacks again. Henry Lesher - He is a twisted one and not in the way I usually like them. However, he has promise and that's more than I can say for half the roster around here.
Backstage we see Charley MacAdam with Ash from the 420 Flock. Charley MacAdam - Next week you take aim at Pyro in round one of the BOTW tournament, but you will also be facing Scavenger later that night. Why did you call him out?
Ash - Simple reason, really. I have never been a Scavenger fan with that stupid chair and all that kind of crap. The guy is a loser and he had the nerve to mess with Frank Merritt. That puts him on my hit list.
Charley MacAdam - Scavenger fights alone and you have Leech and Frank backing you up, as well as Angela. Does this give you an unfair advantage?
Ash - No, my talent does. Scavenger is about to learn when you mess with the Flock, you get smoked.
Charley MacAdam - Well put. Scavenger vs Kris "Bad Religion" by Godsmack plays as Kris makes his way out of the back to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He makes his way to the ring amid pyrotechnics and then poses outside the ring before stepping inside. The lights go out for a few moments and the crowd pauses. We then hear the opening baseline of "Sanitarium" by Metallica and Scavenger appears with Sierra leading the way to the ring. Scavenger gets a huge ovation which just gets louder when he raises the chair high above his head. Scavenger points inside the ring, says something to the chair and places it against the apron before stepping onto the ring steps and motioning Kris to get back or else.
Ricky Simms - Scavenger here tonight for another exciting appearance. Henry Lesher - Yes, it's great he is willing to leave his planet to visit us like this. We are truly blessed.
Scavenger steps into the ring and smiles as the bell sounds. He asks Kris if he heard the bell and Kris nods. The two lock up. Scavenger quickly knees Kris in the gut and then whips him off the ropes and lands a backdrop on him. Scavenger says something to Sierra and points to the lights. He then picks Kris up and gives him a gorilla press slam to the mat. The crowd is impressed with that show of raw power from Scavenger. Scavenger then places Kris in a surfboard submission maneuver as we see Ash at the top of the ramp. Ash is booed by the pro Scavenger fans. The ref, meanwhile, asks Kris if he submits and Kris shakes his head. He reaches for the rope, but his hands are too far away and bound by Scavenger's to reach far.
Ricky Simms - Kris in trouble early here. Henry Lesher - Yeah he came out flat and let Scavenger walk over him at the opening bell. This doesn't bode well for his future.
Scavenger releases the hold, seeing that he isn't going to get a submission out of it. Scavenger locks in a headlock and Kris hurriedly gets to his feet and whips Scavenger off the ropes. Kris shoulder blocks Scavenger to the mat and then lands a leg drop to the throat of Scavenger. Kris then goes for the pin as Ash applauds from the ramp. 1...2... kick out by Scavenger.
Ricky Simms - What the hell is Ash doing out here anyway? Henry Lesher - Like you deserve to know. Kris stands Scavenger up, whips him into the corner and charges headlong into an elbow to the chin from Scavenger followed by a spear. Scavenger goes for the pin. 1...2... Kris gets a foot on the bottom rope. Scavenger then stands Kris up, whips him into the corner, then DDTs him to the mat. Scavenger rolls him over again for the pin and hooks the leg. 1...2....3! Scavenger picks up the win. On the ramp, Ash shakes his head and then makes his way out back as the crowd cheers Scavenger on. Scavenger rolls out of the ring and asks Sierra about Ash and she simply shrugs as she hands the chair to Scavenger.
Ricky Simms - Scavenger wins and Ash didn't attack him. Henry Lesher - Yet, the night is still young. Backstage we see Tina making her way into Q's locker room. When the camera tries to follow, the door is slammed in the cameraman's face. The camera then turns to the right and we see Kelly Buchanan with an ice pack against her forehead. She is approached by Ophelia and Chastity who were better known as the Glamour Girls.
Ophelia - Looks like Kelly here is having a hard time. Kelly Buchanan - Well, look what the whore dragged in. Chastity - Kelly, Kelly, Kelly..... we know you don't like us much, but we have a proposal. Your career has stalled and our careers have been stalled. We work together.
Kelly Buchanan - Together for what exactly? Ophelia - To get the women's title for one of us and the tag belts for the other two. Kelly Buchanan - *sighs* I'll think about it. The Glamour Girls smile and nod at each other and then leave. We hear some whistles from the crowd watching them on the Titantron.
TJ Mercer vs 6Pac Dudley "Southtown" by POD plays and the crowd cheers as 6Pac Dudley makes his way to the ring, led by Spikey Tiger waving the Canadian flag. 6Pac makes his way to ringside and then removes his T-shirt and hands it to a kid in the front row. 6Pac then steps onto the apron and through the ropes where he will wait for TJ Mercer. "Imaginary" by Evanescence marks the entrance of TJ as he makes his way out from the back to a mixed reaction. The girls appear to like the guy quite a bit as he makes his way down the ramp, never taking his eyes off of 6Pac Dudley. TJ slides into the ring and 6Pac starts putting the boots to him as the bell sounds to get things underway. 6Pac stands TJ up, lands a few knife edge chops to the chest of TJ and drives him into the corner with a kick straight in the gut. 6Pac then snap mares TJ to the middle of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Strong start for the leader of Northern Exposure. Henry Lesher - The guy's a bum. What more is there to say on the matter? Ricky Simms - And once again the petty hatred that Henry Lesher feels towards 6Pac must be dragged out into the public eye again. I am so proud of this.
Henry Lesher - Yeah whatever. 6Pac stands TJ upo, but takes a jawbreaker from Mercer. Mercer then slaps on a quick figure four leglock, relying on the element of speed to try and grab a quick win. 6Pac manages to grab the bottom rope before any real damage can be done by the hold and forces the break. 6Pac gets to his feet and TJ starts in on him with a series of rights and lefts to the face. 6Pac responds with a stiff dropkick which sends TJ out of the ring. 6Pac then follows TJ out, stands him up and attempts to whip him into the steel steps only to have TJ reverse it and send 6Pac into the steps. 6Pac grabs his knee and screams in pain as TJ stands him up and rolls him into the ring. TJ enters the ring and places 6Pac into a Boston Crab and leans back in an attempt to make Dudley submit. Dudley reaches for the ropes as Spikey starts a 6Pac chant from ringside. 6Pac reaches for the ropes, but finds he is just a little too far away to grab them. However, he refuses to submit and give Mercer the win here.
Henry Lesher - This is the kind of position 6Pac is used to being in. Ricky Simms - I wouldn't count him out yet. Henry Lesher - Thank God I am not you, then. Ricky Simms - I am thankful for that as well. 6Pac lunges for the ropes twice. Mercer struggles to hold him in the center of the ring. However, 6Pac starts to win that battle and pulls Mercer to the ropes. He then grabs the bottom rope and the ref forces the break of the hold. 6Pac again grabs his knee, obviously in great pain right now. Mercer stands 6Pac up and whips him off the ropes. 6Pac's knee gives way as he comes back off the ropes. TJ tries for the pin, hooking the good leg. 1...2... 6Pac manages to grab the bottom rope with his hand from his back. Mercer gets to his feet and starts putting the boots to the injured knee. The crowd cheers 6Pac on as he struggles to stand even under this barrage of punishment. 6Pac rakes the eyes of Mercer, then gives him a clothesline to the mat. 6Pac then heads to the ropes. He starts to climb. Mercer gets to his feet and turns and takes a double axe handle to the chest. 6Pac tries for the pin. 1...2... Mercer kicks out. 6Pac then slaps on an STF and holds Mercer in the middle of the ring. Mercer struggles to make his way to the ropes.
Ricky Simms - Nice submission hold by 6Pac. Henry Lesher - Yeah, like he could get anyone to submit. Mercer reaches for the ropes, tries to pull himself over, but instead gives up and taps out and the crowd erupts. 6Pac gets to his feet, still favoring that sore knee as Spikey enters the ring to help his stablemate celebrate a big win.
Ricky Simms - I believe 6Pac just made someone submit. Henry Lesher - Shut up. Backstage we see Abe looking on as his opponent for Battle of the Warriors celebrates. Gothic Angel enters the shot.
Gothic Angel - Go out there and make us two for two tonight. Abe - *nods* I'll try. Andon vs Rayn "Poem" by Taproot plays as Rayn makes his way out from the back to a strong positive reaction from the crowd. As he high fives fans on his way to the ring, he is attacked from behind by Andon who has grown tired of waiting.
Ricky Simms - This matches pits newbie Rayn against the newest psychopath in the CWA, Andon.
Henry Lesher - If he could channel his illness, he could become a top contender. Andon picks Rayn up and body slams him on the steel mesh of the ramp. Andon then puts the boots to him before two handed choking him. Andon smiles as the crowd boos. He tells Rayn that it's all her fault and he must pay for what she has done. Andon then stands the now bloodied Rayn up and walks him to ringside. The ref is now warning Andon to stop these tactics or risk DQ. Andon pouts as he rolls Rayn into the ring. He then heads for the ropes and starts his climb to the top. Rayn gets to his feet, not appearing certain of where he is or what day this is. Andon dives and lands a moonsault on Rayn, knocking him back to the mat where Andon goes for the pin moments after the bell sounds to officially start this match up. 1...2... kick out by Rayn. Andon shakes his head and informs the ref that was a three count to no avail.
Ricky Simms - Welcome to the CWA Rayn. This young man is taking quite the punishment. Henry Lesher - And it is all Gothic Angel's fault. She has been completely careless and heartless to him.
Ricky Simms - You're kidding. The man is sick! He kidnapped her! He has tried to force his will onto her and force his way into her life.
Henry Lesher - Look at the man! Andon is built like a Greek God. I would be.... I mean, any woman would be lucky to have him.
Ricky Simms - Excuse me while I puke somewhere. Andon stands Rayn up and whips him into the corner buckle. Andon then gives Rayn a fireman's carry into a power slam and again goes for the pin. 1...2... Rayn kicks out. Andon again argues that was a three count. Andon then stands Rayn up, whips him into the corner and gives him a side Russian leg sweep out of it and pins the bloodied man again. 1...2... kick out again by Rayn as the crowd cheers Andon's opponent on. Andon stands Rayn up, whips him into the corner again. This times, Rayn turns and thumbs Andon in the eye, then headbutts him to the mat. Rayn follows this up with a leg drop across Andon's throat. Rayn goes for the pin. 1...2.. Andon kicks out. Rayn then stands Andon up, whips him off the ropes and spears him to the mat before trying again for the pin. 1...2... Andon gets a foot on the bottom rope. Rayn then rolls Andon out of the ring, and then immediately follows him out. Rayn stands Andon up then shoulders him into the guard railing. Rayn then picks Andon up and drops him on the railing. He then pulls him off of it before whipping him into the steel corner post. Andon is busted open now as Rayn rolls him back into the ring.
Ricky Simms - Severe punishment dished out by both men here. Henry Lesher - Oh it's just a little bit of blood. Big deal. Rayn goes for the pin on Andon. 1...2... kick out by Andon. Rayn then elbow smashes the fallen Andon, steps out of the ring and then lands a shocking springboard shooting star press on Andon. The Rebirth has been executed to perfection and the crowd goes wild as the ref counts it. 1...2...3! Rayn with a win here.
Ricky Simms - Great finishing move there by Rayn! Henry Lesher - Not bad. Ricky Simms - Okay then.... Backstage we see Leech pacing. He is approached by Samantha Johnson. Samantha Johnson - Leech, this week you get a chance to wrestle in the Battle of the Warriors tournament. What are your thoughts as you head into what should be a wild week?
Leech - You wanna know my thoughts, Sam? The Flock is going to take no prisoners and the Flock is going to rule that ring! Max Cameron is going to lose twice next Monday and maybe he'll be lucky enough to lose both times to me!
Samantha Johnson - Anyone in this thing you're concerned with facing? Leech - Hey, they have me against the Mob in round one, Sam. Once I get past that has been, it's smooth sailing from there.
Samantha Johnson - Well, good luck to you. Leech - I don't need it. Samantha retreats and Leech continues to pace. Rated R vs Fallout "I Hate Everything About You" by Three Days Grace plays as Rated R makes his way to the ring. The crowd cheers him on, but that cheering is tempered when Fallout emerges from the crowd and launches himself off of the railing and clotheslines Rated R to the floor before landing a series of right and lefts to the head of Rated R as the crowd boos.
Henry Lesher - Wonderful attack there by Fallout. Ricky Simms - Great, like you're not drooling enough from Andon earlier. Henry Lesher - I beg your pardon? Ricky Simms - Just watch the match and try not to stare. Rated R is stood up by Fallout and then whipped into the apron where he nails his spine hard against the unforgiving surface. Rated R is then rolled into the ring as Fallout bows before the crowd. Fallout then rolls himself into the ring and places Rated R in a sleeperhold. Rated R struggles to his feet as the crowd cheers him on. Despite the time he has spent out of the ring, Rated R is still quite popular with the fans and many of them have T-shirts on that have his name and face on them. Rated R grabs the ropes to force the break of the hold. Rated R then elbows Fallout in the gut a few times before gouging the eyes. Rated R then whips Fallout off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Rated R then measures Fallout and lands an elbow smash and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Fallout.
Henry Lesher - No way he is getting pinned that easy. Ricky Simms - I must agree there, some wishful thinking by Rated R. Rated R stands Fallout up, then whips him off the ropes and lands a dropkick on him. Fallout rolls out of the ring to regroup while Rated R gets to his feet. Rated R motions Fallout into the ring. Fallout pulls himself onto the apron and then orders Rated R to get back. He tells the ref to watch Rated R as he steps through the ropes and back into the ring. Fallout and Rated R then circle each other. They lock up and Rated R places Fallout into an arm bar. Fallout reverses the hold. Fallout then hip tosses Rated R to the mat and pats himself on the back. Rated R kips up to his feet, ducks a right hook from Fallout and then picks Fallout up and body slams him to the mat. Rated R then heads to the ropes and heads to the second rope and lands a scissor kick to the back of Fallout's neck from there and rolls him over for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Fallout. Rated R then stands Fallout up and whips him into the corner. Rated R charges into Fallout with an avalanche. Fallout falls to his knees in the corner and is boot chokes over the second rope by Rated R as the ref counts off and almost disqualifies Rated R. Rated R then stands Fallout up, attempts a whip to the opposite corner only to have Fallout stop and reverse the move, sending Rated R hard into the corner.
Henry Lesher - Never count a member of the Eternal Nightmares out of the match. Ricky Simms - Yes yes, the admiration society continues over here. Fallout picks Rated R up and power slams him to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Rated R. Fallout then stands Rated R up, whips him off the ropes and misses with a dropkick as Rated R stops in his tracks. Rated R then lands the Restricted out of nowhere and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Rated R celebrates the win as he rolls out of the ring and high fives the fans. They cheer as we see Fallout slowly getting to his feet, not wanting to face what just happened here.
Henry Lesher - Lucky win for Rated R and Fallout will be back. Ricky Simms - A win is a win as you always say. Backstage we see Night Dweller standing with Joe Davidson. Dweller is wearing a suit and tie tonight, all black jacket, pants and tie with a red shirt.
Joe Davidson - Night Dweller, next week you step into the ring in a tag match against Total Mayhem. Are you in the least bit concerned with what could happen?
Night Dweller - Not at all concerned, no. I have been in this business for a long time. Total Mayhem is going down and going down hard.
Joe Davidson - Who do you like to win the tournament this coming week? Night Dweller - I could see Dante pulling it off, but never count out Dizz or Q or even Moose. Anyone who has held the world title bere has to be considered a strong contender.
Joe Davidson - Thanks for your time. Oh and we wonder about the search for Amy Chase. Night Dweller - *narrows his eyes* Damien knows where she is and he is going to answer for that.
Joe Davidson - I see. Shelia Lavin vs Abe "Harder Better Faster Stronger" by Daft Punk plays as Shelia Lavin makes her way to the ring. She receives the usual cold response from the fans who dislike her as much as ever. She makes her way inside the ring and smiles as the fans boo her. "God of the Mind" by Disturbed plays as Abe makes his way to the ring to a much more positive reaction. He has a T-shirt on which proclaims his hatred for Andon. The crowd appears to like that as well.
Henry Lesher - Look, Abe made a nifty T-shirt. Shelia can strangle him with it. Ricky Simms - Remember when she kicked your ass? That was funny. Henry Lesher - That's all water under the bridge. Ricky Simms - I know. You two march in the same parades so it's all good. Abe steps through the ropes and the bell sounds. He and Shelia then circle each other before locking up in the middle of the ring. Shelia tries for an arm bar on Abe only to end up taking a clothesline from him. Abe then knees Shelia in the throat twice and tries for a pin as she chokes for breath. 1...2.... Shelia rolls out from under the pin. Abe then stands Shelia up and hip tosses her back to the mat. He then stands her up and whips her off the ropes and Rock Bottoms her to the mat and tries again for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Shelia.
Ricky Simms - I don't see her choking him with the T-shirt yet. Henry Lesher - I need to get some more go to hell greeting cards, I see. Abe stands Shelia up, whips her into the corner and then charges straight into sweet chin music. Shelia tries for the pin, using the ropes for leverage. 1....2.... the ref spots the feet on the ropes and refuses to count the three. Shelia stands Abe up, whips him off the ropes and misses with a clothesline. Abe lands a spinning neckbreaker on Shelia. Abe again tries for the pin. 1...2.. small package reversal by Shelia. 1...2... Abe manages to kick out. The two get to their feet and lock up again. Shelia kicks Abe on the knee cap, then headbutts him twice to drive him into the corner. Shelia follows this up with a monkey flip out of the corner. She goes for the pin on Abe. 1...2... Abe kicks out. Shelia stands Abe up, whips him to the corner and tries for sweet chin music as he stumbles back out of the corner. Abe ducks the kick and then gives Shelia a spine buster on the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... Shelia kicks out. Shelia then rolls out of the ring to collect herself. From the back we see Executioner making his way down the ramp. The ref warns him off, but Executioner keeps coming. The ref leans out through the ropes to continue berating Executioner and then Q emerges through the crowd and power bombs Abe on the floor which enrages the fans.
Ricky Simms - Typical Reality tactics. Henry Lesher - That's why they are winners. Q rolls Abe into the ring and then crouches down at ringisde as Shelia lands a rolling thunder on Abe and Executioner retreats to the back. The ref counts the pin. 1...2...3! Shelia with a typical win.
Henry Lesher - Just try and stop them. You can't. You cannot defeat Reality. Q enters the ring and holds the tag belts high. They go into Battle of the Warriors on a winning note as the show fades to black.
Ricky Simms - See you all next week in Washington DC!
