CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 22 December 2003

22 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seattle Washington and another edition of CWA's Assault. It's a roaring crowd as the show begins and "Halls of Fear" by Nino Rota plays as Sal Clemenzo makes his way to the ring with a Santa hat on. He is led to the ring by Big Sal and steps through the ropes and blows kisses to the booing fans.
Sal Clemenzo - Good evening Seattle! Nice to see you love me as much as I love you. I wanted you people to know that your truly has heard about this best wrestler in the world deal and I hear the winner also receives one million dollars. This means I get to fund the Mafia whom you people simply love and get to beat down four of the pretenders in the CWA that same night! I like my odds and soon you people will too. Time for Sal to hit the ring and take out the trash.
Sal throws the microphone down on the mat and leaves the ring as we head into the opening credits. We see images of Q, Dante, Vigilante, Max Cameron and Pyro before we head to ringisde where our event is set to begin.
Dark Forces vs Total Darkness "Fight Music" by D12 plays and we see Black Panther methodically making his way to the ring while Jon Shocker dances like a boxer shadow boxing and claiming to be the greatest of all time, Muhammad Ali style. The crowd cheers as they hold their tag titles aloft and pose. As they pose, they are attacked by Puppet Master and Black Lightning from behind. The challengers put the boots to them on the ramp before dragging them down to ringside. Puppet Master stands Black Panther up and then tosses him head first into the steel ring post. Puppet Master then rolls Panther into the ring and steps onto the apron with an evil smile on his face. The opening bell rings and the match is on as Black Lightning stomps Jon Shocker a few more times before taking his place in his corner. Puppet Master stands Black Panther up, whips him into the corner, then gives him a fisherman's suplex and lands a leg drop. Puppet Master goes for the pin. 1...2.... kick out by Black Panther. Puppet Master tags in Black Lightning, stands Panther up and holds him open for a series of kicks to the ribs of Panther. Black Lightning then places Panther into an abdomenal stretch.
Ricky Simms - We start off the Christmas edition of Assault with this tag title match up. What about this shocking announcement by our former world champ?
Henry Lesher - Sal is the greatest world champ we have ever had... along with Q. If Dante wins tonight, he makes it a tough trio.
Ricky Simms - I knew you would be happy. Henry Lesher - That's all that really matters. Black Lightning tightens his grip on Panther, but Panther starts to power out of the hold. Shocker gets to his feet and starts screaming at Black Lightning inside the ring. He jumps onto the apron and challenges him to allow Panther to tag so they can settle this face to face. Black Lightning laughs, releases Panther and Panther tags in Shocker. Shocker looks to the ceiling and starts to laugh. Shocker steps through the ropes, starts shadow boxing and then beckons Lightning on. Black Lightning charges and takes a boot to the chin from Jon Shocker. Shocker then picks Lightning up by the throat and choke slams him to the mat. Shocker then starts jawing at Puppet Master as he stands Black Lightning up. Shocker whips Black Lightning into the corner and lands a kick to the jaw of Black Lightning. Shocker then tags in Black Panther. They whip Lightning off the ropes and then gives him a double clothesline. Panther tries for the pin. 1...2.. Puppet Master breaks it up.
Ricky Simms - Strong showing by the champs right now. Henry Lesher - Two dark teams and neither of them have any real toughness. Sad to see. Panther stands Lightning up, whips him into the corner and takes a dropkick from Black Lightning. Lightning tags in Puppet Master and holds Panther open as Puppet Master drills a shoulder into the same side they already abused earlier. Puppet Master then places Black Panther into a flying hammerlock. Panther appears ready to give up, but refuses when the ref asks and then Jon Shocker storms into the ring and gives Puppet Master a chop block.
Ricky Simms - Puppet Master goes down with that chop and the weight of Panther landing on him. That could snap his leg!
Henry Lesher - Nice move by Shocker. Panther rolls Puppet Master over for an ankle lock submission and locks it in. Black Lightning charges to break it up only to take a lariat from Shocker. 1...2....3! Dark Forces retain the belts and the knee of Puppet Master looks to be in pretty rough shape.
Henry Lesher - That is the killer instinct one needs to win a match. Great tag champs. Ricky Simms - It was clean somewhat, but Dark Forces showed they aren't the standard baby face tag champs right there.
Henry Lesher - A win is a win. Backstage we see Shannon Skanes with Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - What would the owner of the CWA like for Christmas? Shannon Skanes - I would like to get back into the ring again. Joe Davidson - Any word yet on when that will be? Shannon Skanes - Not a single word yet. When I get the green light, some guys and gonna pay.
Joe Davidson - Have yourself a Merry Christmas. Shannon Skanes - You too Joe. Ash vs Max Cameron "The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson plays as Ash makes his way to the ring. He gets a mixed reception and is led by Angela all the way to ringside. Ash steps into the ring as we hear "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC play and hardcore champ Max Cameron makes his way to the ring. Max slides into the ring and shows the belt to Ash. Ash responds with a dropkick to the chest of the champ as the bell sounds to get the title match underway.
Ricky Simms - 4 matches and 4 titles on the line tonight. Henry Lesher - Yeah, about time we trimmed the fat around here. Max is stood up by Ash, whipped off the ropes and given a backdrop. Max is then stood up by Ash and given a series of knife edge chops to the chest which drive him to the corner buckle. Ash then snap mares Max to the mat. Ash then stands Max up and takes a thumb to the eye. Max then whips Ash into the corner and delivers a stiff clothesline on him and then goes for a quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Ash. Max then rolls out of the ring, reaches underneath it and pulls out a broom handle. He rolls himself into the ring, gets to his feet and smashes Ash with it across the back. Max then does it a second time which breaks it. He tosses the pieces out of the ring where Angela picks them up and throws them back at Max and hits him in the face with one of the pieces. Max then rolls out of the ring and charges at Angela. Angela ducks a clothesline and then she kicks him in the back which sends him over the railing. Ash rolls out of the ring and makes sure Angela is okay before heading for the railing. Max gives Ash a jawbreaker on the guard railing and then jumps over and goes for the pin on the floor. 1...2... kick out by Ash.
Ricky Simms - Max is fighting a numbers game here. Henry Lesher - Who died and made you Michael Cole? Numbers game? Is this the FBI? No, it's Flock. It's lower than FBI.
Ricky Simms - Get into the spirit, Doc. Henry Lesher - I am in the spirit. I have told you to go to hell twice already. What else am I supposed to do?
Max stands Ash up at ringside and tries to slam him into the ringsteps only to have Ash reverse it. Max slams into the ring steps which Ash then picks up and slams across Max's back. Ash then rolls Max over for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Max. Ash stands Max up and whips him into the steel corner post head first. Max falls like a rock as Ash senses he is about to become the new hardcore champ. He goes to pin Max when Pyro appears through the crowd and lands an elbow smash across the back of Ash to break up the count before it even begins. Pyro stands Ash up and headbutts him down to the floor. He smiles as he reaches under the ring and pulls out a chair. Pyro hauls off to swing the chair at Ash and is blindsided by a charging Leech who launches himself off the railing from just out of Pyro's line of sight. Leech then starts pounding rights and lefts to the head of Pyro.
Henry Lesher - We need more matches like this. Ricky Simms - We have tons of outside interference here. I figured that would please you. Leech stands Ash up and makes sure he is okay as Pyro lies in a bloody heap on the floor. Max gets to his feet and takes a stiff kick to the chin from Leech. Leech then rolls Max into the ring and heads for the ropes as Ash rolls himself into the ring. Leech dives from the top and lands a five star frog splash on Max before Ash does a rolling thunder on Max as well and goes for the pin. 1...2... Marcus Cameron hits the ring and clubs Ash with a chair to break up the count! Marcus then takes a dropkick which drives the chair into the chest of Marcus. Adin Vick enters the ring, tries a chair shot on Ash who moves out of the way. Instead, the chair nails Max right in the head. Leech kicks Marcus Cameron out of the ring. Ash goes for the pin. 1...2.... kick out by Max who reverses the pin. 1...2...3! Leech makes the save a moment too late. Max somehow retains the title as marcus and Adin shake their heads and make their way to the back.
Max rolls himself out of the ring and makes his way up the ramp and has a few words with Adin and Marcus. They push and shove each other and max orders them out. Marcus shakes his head and chuckles to himself as he and Adiv make their way to the back.
Ricky Simms - Is this the end of Youthful Aggression? Henry Lesher - I dare not hope. Max Cameron vs Pyro vs Romeo Salvador "PIMP" by 50 Cent plays as Romeo Salvador makes his way to the ring where Pyro and Max Cameron are still in rough shape. Mac has retained his hardcore belt, but has taken quite the beating along the way as has Pyro, his stablemate.
Ricky Simms - This is going to be ugly. Henry Lesher - Judging by the appearance of the opposition, it should be a quick night for Romeo here.
Ricky Simms - I am inclined to agree. Romeo steps into the ring as he is announced as the third competitor in the Aerial title match up. The crowd cheers Romeo on. Romeo has been enjoying a following an announcer gets as time goes by, much like that enjoyed by Jerry Lawler. Romeo then motions Max and Pyro into the ring, well aware this is a handicap match for him.
Ricky Simms - Bad shape or not, he is facing two men at once. Henry Lesher - Yes it is going to be great to watch. Romeo lands a right hand on Max, but takes a knee lift to the gut from Pyro. Pyro then headbutts Romeo to the mat. Pyro stands Romeo up. He and Max whip Romeo off the ropes and double elbow him in the throat to take the early advantage. Max goes for the pin. 1...2... Romeo rolls out. Pyro then pulls Max to his feet and starts jawing at him about the title and it being his to win. Max shoves him hard. Pyro shoves him right back.
Ricky Simms - More trouble in paradise. Henry Lesher - No surprise there. Max stands Romeo up and whips him into the corner. He turns to say something to Pyro and Pyro clotheslines him down to the mat. Pyro lands a series of rights and lefts to the head of Max Cameron. Romeo looks somewhat taken aback by this, but steps outside the ring onto the apron. He then climbs to the top rope and lands a spinning kick to the head of Pyro from there. Romeo then tries for the pin on Pyro. 1...2... kick out by Pyro. Max gets to his feet, bleeding profusely from the forehead now. Max puts the boots to Romeo as Romeo gets to his feet with the use of the ropes. Max whips Romeo off the ropes. Romeo leap frogs Max. Romeo then comes off the ropes and lariats him to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... Pyro breaks up the count. Pyro stands Romeo up and lands a knife edge chop on him. Romeo answers with a dropkick.
Ricky Simms - Romeo fighting back here. Henry Lesher - Well it helps that Pyro and Max have decided to be stupid and fight each other before really beating the crap out of him.
Pyro whips Romeo off the ropes. Max trips Romeo down, then cradles him for a pin. 1...2.... Pyro breaks it up, then rolls Max for the pin. 1...2...3! Pyro wins and is the new Aerial champion!
Henry Lesher - Smart move by Pyro. Pin the guy who least suspects it! Max Cameron gets to his feet quickly and has a few words with Pyro. Pyro laughs and leaves the ring. Romeo then gets to his feet and dropkicks Max in the back. Romeo takes the hardcore belt from his hands, looks at himself in it and then smacks Max across the back with it before leaving the ring with his arms raised to the delight of the crowd.
Henry Lesher - Yes yes, big man beats up guy who wasn't facing him. Ricky Simms - That's never bothered you before. Henry Lesher - It wasn't Romeo before. Backstage we see Joe Davidson with The Moose. Joe Davidson - What would The Moose like for Christmas? The Moose - World title. Joe Davidson - Anything else? The Moose - A chance to become the best wrestler in the world. Joe Davidson - Okay, and that's in a few weeks. The Moose - And I win it when it happens. You can take that to the bank. Joe Davidson - Anything to say to the fans? The Moose - *nods* Thanks for the support. I look forward to being the champion of the people again real soon.
Dante vs mlsq42 "Know" by System of a Down plays as Dante makes his way to the ring with a cinder block above his head. It has a world title belt scribbled in black marker all the way around it. Behind him Adam Lewis is holding up a sign that reads "Your new World Champ: Piss Off." Dante steps into the ring after leaving the cinder block at ringside. Adam Lewis takes a seat at ringside with the sign still held high. We then see Dante grab the microphone from the ring announcer.
Dante - You people love me! *loud boos* Stop it. You're embarrassing me. Now, if I win this match I lose my position as the Commish and I have decided that Lunacy Muse has done a fine job as secretary and therefore she stays there! The new Commissioner should I win will be Ingee Shmelky! Now, I have one thing to order for next week and from now on. Ash and Max really don't appear to like each other. Max Cameron has been close to being world champ as has Ash as well. I find it a shame that they hate each other so and, being the big hearted guy that I am, I order Max and Ash to tag together next week! *loud boos* Oh yeah, you people love me!
Henry Lesher - These people should shut up and listen to a genius. Ricky Simms - Oh please... Q's music starts which interrupts Dante as he makes his way to the ring. Q is shaking his head and is led to the ring by Executioner who is carrying a cinder block of his own.
Ricky Simms - You must be torn as to who you are cheering for. Henry Lesher - I am cheering for Dante and then Q to win the rematch. Then I want Damien to get involved and I can truly be happy seeing true champions emerge among the Moose's and Ash's of this world.
Ricky Simms - Moose's? Henry Lesher - Do not correct my grammar, little man. Q steps over the top rope and Dante appears ready to charge, but thinks twice about it. The bell sounds and Q delivers a spear on Dante before landing a series of rights and lefts to the gut of Dante. Q then stands him up, throws him into the corner and lands rights and lefts to the gut of Dante again. Dante then responds with an eye rake. Dante then gives Q and inverted atomic drop. At ringside we see Adam Lewis and Executioner start jawing at each other. Dante picks Q up inside the ring and gives him a power slam before trying for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Q. Dante then stands Q up and whips him into the corner. Dante then takes a thumb to the eye from the defending champ follows by a solid right hook which launches Dante across the ring and through the air. Q then picks Dante up by the hair and airplane spins him twice before letting him go right through the ropes to the outside of the ring. Q then steps over the top rope, jumps down from the apron and gives Dante a double axe handle to the back. Q then whips Dante head first into the guard railing before rolling him into the ring. Q jumps onto the apron and steps over the top rope. Dante gets to his feet slowly and finds himself immediately placed in a sleeper hold. Dante lunges and grabs the ropes. The ref forces the break.
Ricky Simms - You sure are quiet. Henry Lesher - I have never been so torn in a match. Dante elbows Q in the throat as he approaches, then kicks him straight on the knee cap which sends the bigger man down to the mat. Dante then lands a knee drop to the back of the head of the fallen and face down Q. Dante rolls him over for the pin. 1...2.... kick out by Q. Dante then stands Q up and whips him over the top rope to the floor. Dante then climbs to the top rope quickly. He spots Executioner out of the corner of his eye and lands a dropkick on him from there. He then goes to Q and takes a kick to the gut from the champ. Q then rolls Dante into the ring and curses under his breath when he sees Executioner is down. Q steps into the ring and Adam Lewis jumps onto the apron. The ref goes to Adam Lewis. As he does, Plague slides into the ring to attack Q and instead takes a stiff boot to the head before Q sends him right back through the ropes. As he does, Adam Lewis jumps off the apron and Kenji appears from the crowd and jumps onto the apron. Again the ref is forced to turn his back. As he does, The Reaper enters the ring and he takes a spine buster from Q before being rolled out of the ring. Q then turns and takes a shot with brass knuckles to the chin from Dante who then places the brass knuckles back into his trunks before going for the pin. 1...2...3! Dante has done it!!!
Henry Lesher - There is a God and He likes Dante!!! Ricky Simms - I cannot believe that. Dante called on virtually every member of the Eternal Nightmares to gain his first CWA title! By hook and by crook he has become the top dog in the CWA!
Henry Lesher - Dante may not be Commish. He has now been promoted to CWA God! Ricky Simms - That wraps up tonight's shortened edition of Assault. Tune in tomorrow for the CWA 2003 Awards show!
Henry Lesher - Ah hell, Dante's champ! Merry Christmas everybody!!!
