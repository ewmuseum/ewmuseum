CWA Breakthrough, 27 December 2003

27 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tampa, Florida is the setting for another solid edition of Breakthrough. The credits start to roll and we see images of Scavenger, Executioner, Pyro, Gothic Angel, and Flava. We then head to ringside where an already insanely loud crowd greets our announcers for this week.
The King vs Andon "Numb" by Linkin Park plays as Andon makes his way to the ring. Behind him he drags the out cold man called The King. He then rolls him into the ring as he smiles for the camera. He steps through the ropes and demands that the ref ring the bell to get this match started, for what it's worth.
Gloria Lavin - Looks like Andon is starting this week by sending a message. Romeo Salvador - You think so? Andon looks around the ring and poses for a few female fans who are all too anxious to get his picture. He then lays one foot on The King and demands for the ref to make the count. 1...2....3! Andon with a win without even a single move done. All the punishment was inflicted elsewhere.
Andon - *yanking microphone from the ring announcer* I have been nice about this, but now I will be a little meaner and to the point about it. I want Gothic Angel. She is mine! Tonight, Angel, come to me!
"God of the World" by Disturbed plays and Andon shakes his head as Abe appears at the top of the ramp. He has a microphone as well.
Abe - Big talk, Andon. You don't get your angel tonight. Instead, you get me. Hope you can win a match when you actually face someone head on.
Abe throws the microphone to the floor and charges into the ring. He battles through a series of punches and kicks from Andon, then whips Andon off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Andon rolls out of the ring, slapos the apron and begins his retreat as fans at ringside taunt him. He starts telling Abe that next week will be his turn to feel what King felt. Abe motions him back to the ring, going as far as to hold the ropes open for him. Instead, Andon continues his retreat.
Gloria Lavin - Looks like the new guys are battling over a female. That's never been done before.
Romeo Salvador - You've been cranky since the awards show when Shelia refused to go for a quickie.
Gloria Lavin - I did no such thing. You asked Shelia to take me to a hotel room for you to videotape.
Romeo Salvador - Yeah, it was a great idea. Backstage we see Q with four trophies and Executioner at his side as Joe Davidson enters the picture.
Joe Davidson - Congratulations Q on your awards this past week. Any New Year's resolutions?
Q - Of course there are. But I don't see why I should tell you. Joe Davidson - We thought it would be fun to hear the New Year's reolutions of the CWA wrestlers.
Q - I am not fun. Find someone else. Joe Davidson - You got it. Joe leaves Q to discuss things with Executioner and we head back to ringside. Angel Peters vs Frank Merritt "Trouble" by Pink plays and Angel Peters makes her way to the ring with the fans cheering her on. She steps through the ring, casts aside her robe and blows a kiss to a kid in the front row. She then waves to the rest of the crowd as "Solitaire Unravelling" by Mushroomhead begins to play and Frank Merritt makes his way to the ring. He drops his jacket on the ramp half way down, then races into the ring and attacks Angel from behind as she stands on the second rope and waves to the crowd. Frank pulls Angel to the center of the ring as the bell sounds. He then picks her up for a power bomb, but instead she gives him a hirakirana and drops him to the mat. Angel Peters then gets to her feet holding her back and stands Merritt up, then whips him off the ropes and lands a dropkick on him which sends him down to the mat.
Gloria Lavin - Unlike other Flock members, Frank doesn't seem to care to play for the fans at all.
Romeo Salvador - And why should he? I mean, I play it up for the fans when they cheer, but could care less when they don't. That works well for some wrestlers.
Angel waits for Frank to re-enter the ring, never taking her eyes off of him. He steps onto the apron as the ref reaches a five count. He almost takes a swing at the ref for counting before he steps back into the ring. He then locks up with Angel Peters. Frank then powers Angel back into the corner. The ref asks for a clean break. Instead, Frank delivers a shoulder block on Angel before retreating to the middle of the ring. Angel then comes at Frank only to take a forearm smash to the chin. Frank follows this up by whipping Angel off the ropes and giving her a tilt a whirl backbreaker. He goes for the pin. 1...2... Angel kicks out. Frank then rolls Angel over on her stomach and places her in the Blunt Wrap. The pain is intense and Angel taps out immediately. Frank hears the bell ring to declare the match over, but still holds her there for a few moments longer.
Gloria Lavin - Nice submission hold there by Frank Merritt. Romeo Salvador - He has impressed me thus far in his CWA career. Backstage we see Dante with his new World title belt. He is making his way down the hall when Lunacy Muse heads him off.
Lunacy Muse - You have taken me from hell and sent me further down the spiral and I will make you pay.
Dante - What seems to be the secretary's problem now? Lunacy Muse - The sec.... *smiles* Asshole, you know what the problem is. Instead of handing me back Assault, you have handed it to Ingee Shmelky. He is already talking about changing the show's name to Flava and having everyone lip synch to their music on the way to the ring. I hated you for making me your slave. This makes it that much worse.
Dante - Aren't you going to congratulate me? Lunacy Muse - No I am not. Dante - I congratulate you on being the gopher for a moron. Lunacy Muse - Fabulous. Menace vs Executioner "Dirge" by Death In Vegas plays as Executioner makes his way to the ring. Behind him walks Tina, as always. It's odd that she is wearing a T-shirt that is asking people to buy a Flava CD. Executioner steps through the ropes as his music fades and the crowd boos him more and more. We then hear "Stunt 101" by G Unit and Menace starts to the ring. He is smiling all the way down to ringside, but smiling at Tina. He starts advancing on her and Executioner slides out of the ring and stands nose to nose with Menace. The two starts throwing rights and lefts at each other as the crowd goes wild. Executioner rams Menace's head into the ring steps, then whips him into the guards railing.
Gloria Lavin - Pleasant way to start the match. Romeo Salvador - It's a member of Reality against a very angry man. I didn't expect Hart verses Michaels here.
Executioner rolls Menace into the ring and then rolls in himself. Executioner quickly gets to his feet, stands Menace up and whips him off the ropes before backdropping him to the mat. Executioner then lands a knee drop to the throat on Menace before applying a two handed choke on Menace that the ref demands a break from, but doesn't get uintil he counts to four, one less than needed to call this match for DQ. Executioner than stands Menace up and sets him for a choke slam only to take an elbow to the side of the head follows by a kick in the ribs from Menace. Menace then whips Executioner into the corner and charges in for a shoulder block only to find no one home. Executioner then rolls Menace up for a quick pin. 1...2... Menace rolls out of it. Executioner then stands Menace up, whips him off the ropes and Menace ducks a clothesline. Menace then kicks Executioner in the back of the thigh. Executioner drops to the mat in pain.
Romeo Salvador - That's a charley horse. Gloria Lavin - That'll be hurting Executioner for days. Menace then pulls Executioner to the center of the ring and applies a figure four leglock. The ref asks Executioner if he submits and Executioner refuses to. Even as Menace tries to increase the pressure and deepen the hold, Executioner is reaching for the ropes. Menace struggles to hold Executioner in the middle of the ring as he feels himself being pulled towards the ropes. Executioner then tries to turn the hold over which would reverse the pressure. The crowd appears to be torn as to which wrestler they hate more and want to see lose. Executioner gets the hold half way reversed. Menace struggles to right the hold and Executioner reaches for and grabs the rope to force the break. Menace is angry as the hold is broken, certain that would be his winning move. Menace stands Executioner up. Menace lands a series of knife edge chops on Executioner which drives him into the corner buckle. Menace then attempts a whip to the other corner only to have Executioner reverse it and whip Menace hard into the corner buckle. Executioner steps towards Menace, but Menace manages to do a shoulder roll and forearms the sore leg of Executioner. Executioner stumbles, grabs the leg and Menace delivers the Drive By on him and goes for the pin. 1....2....3! Menace with the win over Executioner!
Romeo Salvador - Menace wounded the animal and then went for the kill. This guy has some real talent.
Gloria Lavin - He won't be off Assault for long. Menace raises his arms in victory and then turns and sees Tina attending to that leg of Executioner's. Menace smiles. He is about to take a step towards Tina when we hear "Hardest Button To Button" and Scavenger comes out of the back, a microphone in hand.
Scavenger - So this is the much talked about Menace. I have heard you have tore quite a swath of destruction through the CWA. Next week you face me and if you take another step towards the girl there, you face me right here now!
Menace takes a step towards Tina and Scavenger drops the microphone and races into the ring where he almost beheads Menace with a clothesline before rolling him out of the ring. Scavenger picks up an audio cable and starts strangling Menace with it as security comes out of the back.
Romeo Salvador - Why does Scavenger have to play hero for the weak around here? Gloria Lavin - It appears to make him feel good and I don't see that it does any harm. Security guards break up the fight between Menace and Scavenger. Inside the ring, Executioner gets to his feet and makes his way out of the ring with Tina's help. Backstage we then see Charley MacAdam with Ingee Shmelky. It would appear Ingee is teaching Charley a few dance moves. It would appear to be the robot... They then notice the camera.
Charley MacAdam - Ingee, this week you become the new GM of Assault. What plans do you have for the show?
Ingee Shmelky - Well, I was thinking Assault sounds pretty harsh. We can call it CWA Flava. Then we all dance at the start of every show and the roster dances together at the end, probably to some music from Gloria Gaynor.
Charley MacAdam - And you think the roster will go for this idea? Ingee Shmelky - It's a cool idea. They'll like it. Charley MacAdam - Good luck with that. Gothic Angel vs Gerard Angelo "Right 'Round" by Dope plays as Gothic Angel comes out to a huge ovation from the crowd. She steps through the ropes and talks a bit to the ref as we hear "Tear Away" by Drowning Pool and Gerard Angelo makes his way to the ring. He gets a very cold reception by comparison and lets the fans hear it right back from him as he steps up onto the apron. "The Future" steps into the ring and offers his hand to Gothic Angel and tells her she is clearly overmatched. Angel holds her hand out, but at the last moment knees Gerard Angelo in the gut, then snap mares the big man to the center of the ring as the bell sounds to get this one started.
Romeo Salvador - Nice to see Angelo making more friends here. Gloria Lavin - He should have known better than that. Gothic Angel dropkicks Gerard Angelo in the back of the neck before he has a chance to get up, then goes for a quick pin. 1...2... Angelo kicks out. Gothic then heads to the ropes, climbs to the second rope and jumps off to execute a leg drop from there only to find Angelo had rolled out of the way and Gothic hits the ring hard with her tailbone. Gerard Angelo then gets to his feet, grabs Gothic Angel by the hair and gives her an atomic drop. He then tries for an early pin himself. 1...2... kick out by Gothic Angel.
Gloria Lavin - A couple of near pins early here. Romeo Salvador - Both trying to gain a quick and easy advantage here. I must admit I am pulling for Gothic Angel here.
Gloria Lavin - Gee, I wonder why. Gerard Angelo stands Gothic up and whips her into the corner, then gives her a spear as she comes out of the corner before he again goes for the pin. 1...2.. Gothic Angel gets a foot on the bottom rope. Gerard Angelo then stands her up again and tosses her into the corner buckle and starts landing body shots with rights and lefts followed by an elbow to the forehead. Angelo picks Gothic up and gives her a gorilla press slam to the mat. He again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gothic Angel. Gerard Angelo then stands her up while questioning the ref about his ability to count to three. The ref holds up two fingers and shrugs as Gerard Angelo whips Gothic off the ropes. Gothic ducks a clothesline, then kicks Gerard in the gut and gives him a bulldog to the mat. Gothic Angel then gets to her feet and starts delivering kicks to the back of "The Future's" neck. Gothic Angel then stands him up, whips him into the corner and dropkicks him in the back. Gerard Angelo stumbles, turns and lands a killer right hook to the jaw of Gothic Angel. Gerard Angelo then rolls himself out of the ring as Gothic Angel sits in the ring dazed and confused. Gerard Angelo grabs a steel chair and brings it into the ring. The ref warns him not to use it, so he clubs the ref with it which causes an immediate DQ. He hauls off to nail Gothic Angel with it only to have the chair grabbed away by Pyro as he enters the ring. Pyro clubs Gerard Angelo with the chair to the delight of the crowd.
Romeo Salvador - What the hell? Gloria Lavin - Looks like Pyro is wasting no time in building new alliances. Gerard Angelo is kicked out of the ring by Pyro who then attends to Gothic Angel. On the back of Pyro's shirt it reads Acolytes of Sin. As he takes her out of the ring, Abe appears from the back and the two men make sure she is okay as the crowd cheers.
Gloria Lavin - This is an odd turn of events. Romeo Salvador - I am very surprised to see this alliance starting up, almost as surprised as I am to hear fans cheering Pyro on. I don't see that lasting long.
Backstage we see Carnage heading towards the stage area we assume in order to help his ally Gerard Angelo out. Instead, he takes a shot to the head with a two by four from Frank Merritt. Frank laughs and tosses the two by four onto Carnage before walking away.
Gloria Lavin - That was senseless. Romeo Salvador - That has kind of become Frank's MO around here. Scavenger vs 6Pac Dudley "Hardest Button To Button" by The White Stripes plays as Scavenger makes his way to the ring with a chair in one hand and Sierra holding the other. She takes her place at ringside as Scavenger steps onto the apron and holds the chair high for the crowd to see. They erupt into chants for the chair and people snap pictures of the chair. Scavenger then places the chair against the ring apron and steps inside the ring. As he has a few words with Sierra, "Southtown" by POD plays and 6Pac is walked to the ring by that lovely blonde we saw in his promo this week and she's carrying a Canadian flag. Inside the ring, Scavenger shakes his head and outside the ring Sierra looks pretty ticked. 6Pac slides into the ring, gets to his feet and the bell sounds.
Romeo Salvador - 6Pac is a genius. The cat fight will distract Scav and 6Pac gets the win. I see it now.
Gloria Lavin - Kind of a cheap win, isn't it? Romeo Salvador - Not if you're the winner it isn't. The two lock up in the center of the ring and Scavenger powers 6Pac into the corner. The ref asks for and gets a clean break. The two then lock up again in the middle of the ring and 6Pac knees Scavenger in the abdomen. 6Pac then delivers a series of forearm smashes to the face of Scavenger before headbutting him down to the mat. 6Pac then lands a well timed elbow smash to the throat of Scavenger. 6Pac then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Scavenger. 6Pac then comes off the ropes and lands a rolling thunder on Scavenger and again tries for the pin. 1...2.... Scavenger kicks out. Outside the ring we see Sierra taking a few steps towards the girl 6Pac brought to the ring. The girl takes one look at Sierra's eyes, drops the flag on the floor and runs from the ring as fast as she can. Sierra laughs, shakes her head and returns to her side of the ring. 6Pac stands Scavenger up, whips him off the ropes and both men clothesline each other to the mat.
Gloria Lavin - Both men are down. Romeo Salvador - 6Pac will get up. Scavenger is nothing. Scavenger uses the ropes to pull himself to his feet as 6Pac uses his own power to get up. 6Pac is to his feet first, takes a step towards Scavenger and takes a kick to the abdomen. Scavenger then lands a kick to the side of 6Pac and then DDTs him to the mat and tries for a quick pin. 1...2... kick out by 6Pac Dudley. Scavenger then stands 6Pac up and whips him off the ropes and places him in a sleeperhold when he returns to the center of the ring. 6Pac immediately lunges for and grabs the ropes to force the break. 6Pac then turns and thumbs Scavenger in the eye. As the battle inside the ring continues, we see Menace heading out from the back with flowers in hand.
Gloria Lavin - This doesn't look good. Romeo Salvador - The man might want to apologize for his earlier advances on Tina. Maybe the flowers are for Scavenger.
Gloria Lavin - I doubt that. Menace heads around the ring and is spotted by Scavenger. Scav leans through the ropes and Menace clubs Scavenger with the bouquet. Scavenger drops like a rock as the bell sounds and Menace drops the flowers away to reveal a baseball bat inside them. He then smiles at Sierra and starts after her. 6Pac looks at the action and nods to Romeo. Romeo gets to his feet, takes off his headset and waits for Menace's next pass as he closes in on Sierra. Romeo lands a superkick to the chin of Menace which knocks him cold in his tracks. Romeo then puts his headset back on and sits down.
Gloria Lavin - Why did you do that? Romeo Salvador - Because chasing a woman with a baseball bat is not cool. Gloria Lavin - You're still trying to make up for what you did to Baby Calaway, aren't you?
Romeo Salvador - Like I would tell you if I was. Gloria Lavin - Well, Scavenger wins this one by DQ as the heat between him and Menace only grows.
Backstage we see Gerard Angelo and Carnage in the locker room and not looking impressed. Charley MacAdam decides this would be a good time for an interview.
Charley MacAdam - We saw Frank Merritt nail Carnage here with a two by four. What do you two have to say to him?
Gerard Angelo - Frank Merritt just signed his death warrant. Next week he gets to face myself and Carnage in a handicapped match. The revolution begins with you, Frank.
Charley MacAdam - That will be quite the match up. Carnage - Frank Merritt will wish he was dead. Tater Evans vs Pyro "Immortal" by Adema plays and the newest Aerial champion makes his way to the ring with the belt raised above his head. This week, Pyro has a title and even has a few fans behind him after that run in earlier tonight to save Gothic Angel. Pyro steps into the ring, kisses his belt and holds it high as the fans take pictures. We then hear "I Will Survive" and "Mr. Sweetness" makes his way to the ring shaking his head.
Gloria Lavin - Okay, now this guy is bizarre. I have no idea what to expect here. He has been heard complaining that this is simply bad booking and not wanting to be here against a man like Pyro.
Romeo Salvador - He'll learn really quick that he cannot call the shots. The bell sounds as Tater Evans enters the ring and swings his hips towards the crowd before blowing them a kiss. Pyro doesn't know how to take this and actually backs off for a moment before charging for the attack. Evans sidesteps it and drops Pyro's throat onto the second rope. Evans then chokes him with that second rope for a few seconds before the ref forces the break. Tater Evans again grinds his hips before standing Pyro up, whipping him off the ropes and giving him a spine buster.
Gloria Lavin - This match was signed before Pyro became the Aerial champ, so that title is not on the line.
Romeo Salvador - Judging by the early returns in this match, that might be a good thing for Pyro. Evans is showing some toughness and some moves that creep me out.
Tater stands Pyro up and whips him into the corner. Pyro lands hard and falls off his feet. Tater then gives Pyro a bronco buster. Tater pulls Pyro out of the corner and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Pyro. Tater then stands Pyro up, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks Pyro to the mat. Tater then heads to the ropes and tries to hit a lionsault only to find Pyro not there when he lands. Pyro gets to his feet, takes a look at Tater Evans and rolls himself out of the ring.
Romeo Salvador - Pyro might be thinking about escaping here before captain creepy has a chance to get up.
Gloria Lavin - He appears a bit baffled by the man's appearance and behavior. Pyro climbs onto the apron as Tater Evans gets to his feet. Pyro steps through the ropes on a six count and then locks up with Evans. He overpowers Evans and throws him to the mat hard. He then starts putting the boots to him. Pyro stands Evans up in one corner and whips him to the opposite one. He then gives him a bulldog to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tater Evans. Pyro stands Evans up again and whips him into another corner buckle. He then gives him a sidewalk slam to the mat. He again tries for the pin. 1...2... Evans refuses to stay down for three.
Gloria Lavin - Quite the main event match we have going this week. Romeo Salvador - A firestarter and a flamer. Gloria Lavin - I cannot believe you just said that. Pyro stands Evans up, whips him into the corner and attempts a shoulder block which Evans gets out of the way of. Evans then rolls back and rolls Pyro up for the pin. 1...2...3! The crowd is confused as it appears both men's shoulders were down.
Romeo Salvador - Who won? The ring announcer than makes the unpopular announcement that both men were counted down and this match is a draw.
Gloria Lavin - Evans can call this an upset then. He went into the ring for his first ever CWA match against a title holder and he got a draw.
Romeo Salvador - That proves he is no pushover for sure. Gloria Lavin - That wraps up another week of Breakthrough as Evans and Pyro both argue their case to the ref.
