CWA Assault, 12 April 2004

12 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It is a packed Madison Square Garden cheering as CWA Assault comes to town. The opening credits roll with images of Reality, the Flock and EOS before one last shot of Anarchy and then we head to ringside where Henry Lesher and Ricky Simms are ready to call the action here again this week.
Pyro vs Gerard Angelo "Dammit" by Blink 182 plays as Gerard Angelo makes his way to the ring. The crowd doesn't look or sound impressed to see him today as he has a few words with some unruly fans in the front row. He steps through the ropes and poses in the corner and gives the finger to one fan he doesn't like for some unknown reason. He then starts pacing inside the ring as he awaits his opponent. "Firestarter" by Prodigy plays as Pyro makes his way towards the ring. The fans have a more mixed reaction to him. He races into the ring and the bell sounds as the two start battling with a series of lefts and rights.
Ricky Simms - Welcome to another edition of Assault, one week closer to the roster split. Henry Lesher - Yes and the exciting opening match of the night is jobber one and jobber two in a virtual deathlock to decide who sucks more than the other. I am on the edge of my seat.
Ricky Simms - Captain sarcasm here will, of course, be managing the return of BK, for one week only.
Henry Lesher - A true wrestler in the ring with a kid. Pyro whips Gerard off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. The crowd appears to like that idea. Pyro then starts raining down punches on Angelo. Pyro then stands Angelo up and whips him off the ropes and attempts a clothesline. Angelo avoids the clothesline with a last moment duck of the head and then gives Pyro a kick in the gut and then a spinning neckbreaker. Gerard goes for the pin. 1...2... Pyro manages to get a foot on the ropes to prevent the pin. Angelo then stands Pyro up, whips him hard into the corner buckle and charges in shoulder first. Pyro moves out of the way and Angelo's shoulder hits the corner buckle hard before Pyro rolls him up for an attempted pin. 1...2... kick out by Gerard. Pyro then stands Gerard up and delivers a series of forearm smashes to the chin and then picks him up and gives Angelo a fisherman's suplex.
Ricky Simms - Well executed. Henry Lesher - Yes, they should be executed. Pyro again stands Angelo up, whips him hard into the corner and then gives him an atomic drop. Pyro then stands Angelo up once more and whips him off the ropes. Angelo grabs the top rope and stops himself. Pyro charges at Angelo. Angelo delivers a thundering clothesline on Pyro. Gerard then heads to the ropes and climbs to the top. He lands a flying headbutt on Pyro from there which impresses a few fans, but not many. He goes for the pin. 1...2... Pyro kicks out. Angelo then stands Pyro up, looks to the crowd and gives a thumbs down. He delivers a Past Tense on Pyro and goes for the pin again. 1...2...3! Gerard Angelo pulls off the win this week.
Ricky Simms - That win is huge for Gerard Angelo. Henry Lesher - Is it really? Angelo rolls out of the ring with a smile on his face, quite proud at having won this match here this week. As he makes his way out, we see Shelia and Gloria Lavin backstage watching on the monitor.
Shelia Lavin - Well if I have to take one of them, I take the winner Gerard Angelo. It's only fair.
Gloria Lavin - Okay, and Pyro heads to Chaos while Angelo stays on Assault. That's fine with me.
Ricky Simms - Pyro and Gerard Angelo were just drafted! Henry Lesher - If only it were the military draft to save me from seeing them for at least six months.

Henry Lesher – Hold on Ricky, it’s getting better again. Ricky Simms – Oh trust this upstart to ruin Vigilante’s last moment in the CWA! Max heads to the ring with a smile on his face. Vigilante motions for the others to stand back and let him in. Max gets in and laughs at Moose, Leech and Dizz. He then goes toe-to-toe with Vigilante. He stretched out a hand, looking for Vigilante to shake it. Vigilante, slightly confused, smiles and shakes Max’s hand in a sign of respect. Max rubs the World Title as if telling Vig it’s in good hands. Vigilante nods and smiles with thanks as Max Cameron takes his hand back and turns away.
Henry Lesher – Max is being nice? But he doesn’t get far as Vigilante, in his farewell moment, turns Max back around and nails the CATCH 22!!!! The crowd erupt as Vigilante talks smack to Max who is sprawled out on the mat. Vigilante jumps to each turnbuckle for the crowd as the Enemies party on in the ring.
Ricky Simms – NOW THAT’S VIGILANTE! WHAT A WAY TO LEAVE THE CWA! Henry Lesher – Farwell and good riddance I say! Ricky Simms – Oh he’ll be back Henry, he’ll be back! HAVE FUN IN HOLLYWOOD VIG! Henry Lesher – He’ll be back one day but we’ll be back next Monday for MORE Assault so tune in folks.
Ricky Simms – Good night. Vigilante waves at the crowd and yells. “I LOVE YOU GUYS.” We then fade to black, ready to get going towards next week's event.
