CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 17 May 2004

17 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tokyo watched Chaos two days ago and now they watch the final Assault before next week's Russian pay per view, Blind Rage. The crowd is enthusiastic as pyro shoots off from every place on the stage area and then "Breathe" by Swollen Members featuring Nelly Furtado plays and the opening credits of Assault begin to roll. We see images of Flock, Anarchy, Tommy Air, Shelia Lavin with Micheal Leary and BLC before we head to ringside and our show begins.
Frank Merritt vs SinmaN "For Whom the Bell Tolls" by Metallica plays as SinmaN makes his way to the ring with the Superior title over his shoulder. He has a few things to say to the camera on his way to the ring. He steps inside the ring, hands his belt to the ref and explains to the ref nothing had better happen to it or there will be hell to pay. "Hate Me Now" by Nas begins to play and Frank Merritt emerges onstage with Wren SilverPhoenix at his side. They make their way to the ring and we suddenly take note of the fact Viper isn't out here. SinmaN shakes his head and has a few words with Frank as Frank enters the ring. He states they agreed to a one on one fight, no managers. Frank says that Wren is a wrestler, not a manager. As they debate things, Viper does appear from the back and makes his way quickly to ringside.
Henry Lesher - Trouble in paradise as we begin Assault here this week. Baby Calaway - Tonight you will be talking to Mister Snuggledybear, *holds a white teddy bear out to Lesher* I will take to Mister Snuggledybear and so will you. I will no longer speak to you.
Henry Lesher - Were you dropped on your head as a child? Baby ignores that question as the bell sounds and these two Flock members start trading punches in the center of the ring. The fans appear to be decided they want to see both men beat the living hell out of each other. SinmaN whips Frank into the corner buckle and then both men clothesline each other to the mat. Frank starts to get to his feet first and lands a leg drop to the throat of SinmaN. Frank then stands SinmaN up and takesa a thumb to the eye. SinmaN then elbows Frank in the gut four times, driving him back to the corner. SinmaN snap mares Frank to the center of the ring and places him in a front facelock. Again, the crowd seems to just want to watch these guys hurt each other. Outside the ring, Wren and Viper jaw at each other. They aren't going at it physically yet, but they are close to it. SinmaN rolls out of the facelock and drills a forearm into the gut of Frank and then lands a solid forearm to Frank's face. SinmaN then stands all the way up, stands Frank up, whips him hard into the corner and gives him a sidewalk slam when he comes out of it. SinmaN then looks to the ropes. He heads to the top. Frank gets to his feet and takes a missile dropkick to the chest from the top rope. SinmaN goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Frank Merritt.
Baby Calaway - Mister Snuggledybear says that looked like a big owie. Henry Lesher - I have a bottle of cyanide with Mister Snuggledybear's name on it. SinmaN pulls Frank over to the ropes, drapes his neck on the middle rope and chokes him with it for a few moments. He then comes off the ropes and jumps onto the back of Frank's neck and then pulls Frank down to the mat and again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Frank Merritt. SinmaN then stands Frank up and whips him through the ropes to the floor. Wren goes over to attend to Frank. Frank nods and says he's fine as Viper shouts some advice to SinmaN inside the ring. Frank rolls himself into the ring. He uses the ropes to get to his feet, then locks up with SinmaN. SinmaN knees Frank in the gut to break the lock up. Frank then headbutts SinmaN while blinded before clotheslining him to the mat. Frank then has a few things to say to Viper. Viper jumps onto the apron and the ref heads over to get him out. Frank then tells Sin it's nothing personal, but gold is on the line. He then whips SinmaN into a corner buckle where Wren drills Sin in the head with a chair. Frank then rolls Sin up for the pin. 1...2...3! Frank Merritt with an underhanded win this week to advance to the finals!
Baby Calaway - Mister Snuggledybear is sad. He hates seeing people cheat. Henry Lesher - I love it. Give me more. Inside the ring, Frank waits for SinmaN to get up, a trickle of blood running down his face. Sin turns and sees Frank with his hand extended. Sin tells Frank the rematch will go differently and then shakes hands with him before they leave in opposite directions. We then head backstage where Ray Dees is with Tommy Air.
Ray Dees - Tommy, what are your goals heading into Blind Rage? Tommy Air - I got screwed out of the world title here on Assault. I got screwed out of my Commonwealth title. I have unfinished business with Flock and, since SinmaN appears to be free for a match at Blind Rage, I am throwing a challenge out there to him.
Ray Dees - It's the Superior title you want then? Tommy Air - That and respect. Having a belt earns you a lot of respect and I intend to have a title belt again before long.
Charley Moore vs Gerard Angelo A single drum beat is heard as Charley Moore appears on stage to the joy and revulsion of the crowd. Japan has never seen anyone like this guy as the plumber makes his way to the ring with a twisted smile on his face. They give him a mixed reaction as the front rows plug their noses as he passes them by. Charley then steps through the ropes into the ring and we hear "Dammit" by Blink 182 and Gerard Angelo comes out from the back to a more universal negative response than Charley got. Angelo rolls himself into the ring and comes face to face with Charley. Angelo slaps Charley across the face. Charley eye gouges Angelo as the bell sounds to get things officially underway. Charley whips Angelo off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Charley then knee drops Angelo to the head, follows this up with a two handed choke. The ref counts off, but Charley breaks the choke before the ref reaches the five count and DQ. Charley then drags Angelo into the corner, climbs to the second rope and lands an elbow to the forehead of Gerard before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gerard Angelo.
Henry Lesher - A match almost as sickening as my job situation. Baby Calaway - Mister Snuggledybear thinks the stinky guy stinks. Henry Lesher - Better insight than you give. Baby Calaway - Mister Snuggledybear thinks you stink, too. Charley stands Angelo up, tries to whip him off the ropes only to have Gerard reverse it. Gerard then knees Cjarley in the gut as he comes out of the corner, sending him somersaulting to the mat. Angelo then places Charley into a grapevine submission and holds him there as the ref checks for a tap out by Charley. Charley shakes his head, refusing to give up here. The crowd boos as Angelo shouts at them. Charley manages to snag the bottom rope with one foot and that forces the break of the hold. Angelo then throws Moorte through the ropes to the floor and raises his arms in victory in the center of the ring. Charley gets to his feet and shakes his head before looking inside the ring and seeing Angelo. Moore grabs the bottom rope and pulls himself inside the ring. Angelo lands a series of kicks to the face of Charley, but Charley fights through them and then spears Angelo into the corner buckle. Charley lands a series of shoulder blocks in the corner. Charley then gives Gerard Angelo an inverted atomic drop.
Henry Lesher - The plumber showing some spirit here this week. Baby Calaway - Mister Snuggledybear says he smells like you. Baby sticks her tongue out at Lesher and then we look back into the ring. Charley picks Angelo up and body slams him to the mat, then lays an elbow into his throat before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gerard Angelo. Charley then stands Angelo up, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Charley then sets Angelo up and delivers a tombstone piledriver on him and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! The win goes to Charley Moore!
Henry Lesher - I guess shit does float. Baby Calaway - That's not nice. I am gonna talk to the people in that truck in the front and that'll be it.
Henry Lesher - The stupid bear is addressing me tonight, not you. Baby Calaway - Mister Snuggledybear covered his furry ears when you said that bad word. As Moore heads to the back, we take a look backstage and see Ash in the Flock dressing room with Joe Davidson.
Joe Davidson - Ash, any concern over what we saw in the ring between Frank and SinmaN? Ash - Flock is a family. Sometimes there are arguments and such. Nothing we can't handle though.
Joe Davidson - If you win tonight, you face Frank next week at Blind Rage for the world title. Any thoughts on that?
Ash - Frank is a warrior. It would be nice if we had a real champion here instead of some EOS handpicked type at the top.
Joe Davidson - Always an unbiased opinion from Flock members. Vesuvius vs Micheal Leary "Schism" by Tool plays as Vesuvius makes his way to the ring, the lights darkening for his entrance. The crowd cheers him on as Noah Skanes leads the way to the ring. Noah makes his way up the steps and enters the ring first, Vesuvius close behind. Noah and Vesuvius talk things over as we hear "Lose Yourself" by Eminem and Micheal Leary starts making his way to the ring,led by Shelia Lavin. Micheal steps into the ring as Noah steps out on the other side. Noah and Shelia exchange a few words outside the ring. Inside the ring, Vesuvius and Leary opt for a test of strength. Vesuvius shows signs of gaining the advantage right away, but Micheal kicks Vesuvius in the gut, then gives him a side Russian leg sweep to the mat. Micheal then lands a series of punches to the head of Vesuvius before pulling him up to his feet. Micheal arm drags Vesuvius into the corner, then arm drags him back to the middle of the ring. Micheal then stands Vesuvius up, whips him off the ropes and Vesuvius ducks a clothesline and then Vesuvius lands a series of rights and lefts to the jaw of Leary which drives him into the corner.
Henry Lesher - There was a time when I pulled for Vesuvius. Baby Calaway - Mister Snuggledybear says you're a gloomy Gus. Henry Lesher - I have a microwave at my hotel room with his name on it. Vesuvius picks Micheal Leary up and power slams him to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Leary. Vesuvius then picks Micheal up again and lands another power slam. He once again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Leary. Vesuvius stands Leary up and we see Ash and Angela on stage as the crowd starts to boo. Noah and Shelia both take exception to their presence here and they head off and start having words. The ref turns his attention to this and SinmaN slides into the ring and slams a chair into the head of Vesuvius. The ref hears the impact, sees SinmaN there and calls for the bell. SinmaN then turns and takes a clothesline frim Micheal Leary. Leary is then attacked by Wren SilverPhoenix. The crowd is enraged as Frank and Wren lay a beating on Leary and Vesuvius as Shelia and Noah enter the ring.
Henry Lesher - Vesuvius wins by DQ. I wonder if either of them feel like winners. Nice to see Flock taking care of business.
Baby Calaway - Meanie. Backstage we see Tommy Air shaking his head as he watches what has just been happening inside the ring. He then leaves the room and appears to have a sense of purpose here. *The Notorious one is shown walking backstage when stopped by Ray Dee’s. Notorious is in his ring gear and has a towel slung around his neck.
Ray Dees - BLC! Can I get a quick word from ya? BLC - Sure, go ahead. My fans want to see me anyways…. Ray Dees - Frank wanted me to ask you something…. BLC raises a questioning eyebrow. BLC - Oh? And….? Ray Dees - How does this feel? BLC - How does what feel? And on cue, Frank attacks from behind, driving his axe handle into the left shoulder of BLC, sending him sprawling forward and onto the floor. Ray scrambles out of the way as Frank tosses the axe handle aside. Frank pulls BLC to his feet by the hair and Russian leg sweeps him into a wall. BLC staggers forward and Frank sizes him up. BLC turns around and is floored with a right hook from Frank.
Frank Merritt - HOW DOES THAT FEEL!! You want some of Frank Merritt?! You got me, bitch!! Frank spits on BLC as Ash and Angela enter the picture and get Frank out of there before security arrives. BLC slowly begins to stir and get up as we switch to the ring again for our next match up.
Marcus Cameron vs Wish "Nothing Left To Lose" by Puddle of Mudd plays as Wish makes his way out to the ring to a mixed reaction from the crowd. He enters the ring and stands ready and poised as "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays and Marcus Cameron makes his way to the ring. He gets a warm response from the crowd. He steps through the ropes and the bell sounds as Wish attacks him and the match is begun. Wish whips Marcus into the corner buckle, then gives him a gutwrench suplex out of it. Wish goes for an early pin here. 1...2... kick out by Marcus. Wish stands Marcus up, whips him hard into the corner and charges in with a shoulder block that misses when Marcus sidesteps it. Marcus then gives Wish a pump handle slam to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Wish.
Henry Lesher - The guy's name is Wish? Wish I wasn't here. Wish this match was over. Wish I was on Chaos.
Baby Calaway - Mister Snuggledybear is sticking his tongue out at you. Henry Lesher - I am cringing in fear. Marcus Cameron stands Wish up, whips him off the ropes and then gives him a boot to the chest which knocks Wish down. Marcus goes for the pin. 1...2... Wish manages to kick out. Marcus stands Wish up, whips him into the corner, then kicks Wish in the gut and gives him a snap suplex to the middle of the ring. Marcus then stands Wish up, takes a knife edge chop to the throat and then gives Marcus a snap suplex to the mat. Wish then heads to the ropes, springs to the top and lionsaults onto the fallen Marcus Cameron. Wish then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Marcus Cameron.
Henry Lesher - Marcus is as impressive here as his brother Max. Baby Calaway - Mister Snuggledybear says he likes Max and you should be nice. Wish stands Marcus up, whips him hard into the corner buckle and charges in shoulder first. Marcus catches Wish and gives him a pump handle slam to the mat. Marcus then pulls Wish to the center of the ring and places him in a sharpshooter. Marcus leans back as Wish tries to reach for the ropes. Wish reaches, then tries to pull himself to the ropes. Marcus stands fast with the hold on in the center of the ring. Wish then taps out and the ref calls for the bell.
Baby Calaway - Yay Marcus! He's a winner, Mister Sunggledybear! Henry Lesher - My IQ drops with every passing moment. Backstage we see SinmaN rounding the corner in the hallway when he is attacked by Vesuvius and the two of them start going at it. We then see Tommy Air appear in the scene with a chair in hand and the three of them go at it tooth and nail. Security then races in to break things up before we head to the ring for our next match up.
Kelly Buchanan vs Notorious BLC "Not Ready To Go" by The Trews plays as Kelly Buchanan makes her way to the ring. She is led by Kathy Smith and the crowd cheers the two of them on. Kelly is sporting new Suicide Blonde tights. They're red with black writing and the Anarchy logo on the back. Kelly makes her way inside the ring and then we hear "Wedge" by Hollow and a noticeably limping BLC makes his way out towards the ring. Kelly has a few words with Kathy about BLC's ability to compete here tonight and Kelly heads to the ropes to see BLC into the ring. She is heard offering to postpone this match.
Henry Lesher - What a wuss. Baby Calaway - Mister Snuggledybear thinks that's very nice of her. BLC steps inside the ring and tells Kelly he is fine. The bell sounds and the two circle each other. Kelly and BLC lock up. Kelly kicks BLC in the ribs to break the lock only to take a clothesline from BLC. BLC then leg drops Kelly and holds his leg after as he goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Kelly Buchanan. BLC stands Kelly up, whips her into the corner. Kelly jumps onto the top rope, turns and lands a hurricarana off the top rope on BLC and brings him down hard to the mat. Kelly then quickly gets to her feet and somersaults onto BLC's chest and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by BLC. Kelly places BLC into a sleeper as Kathy cheers her on from ringside.
Henry Lesher - How did this match even make it onto the card? Two people no one cares about.
Baby Calaway - Don't listen to him, Mister Snuggledybear. Kelly applies the sleeper and BLC fights to reach for the ropes. BLC rolls away from the hold and manages to grab the ropes to force the break, showing strength superior to any that Kelly can muster here. BLC then gets to his feet and puts the boots to Kelly. Outside the ring, we see Kathy pacing and cheering Kelly on for a comeback here. BLC picks Kelly up and body slams her, then gives her a knee drop to the head and goes for the pin. 1...2... Kelly manages to roll a shoulder out. BLC then stands Kelly up, whips her off the ropes and Kelly ducks a clothesline and dropkicks BLC to the mat. She then places BLC into a crossface chicken wing. BLC fights to reach for the ropes. Kelly holds on as BLC starts to fadem clearly still not at full strength since the Flock attack on him before this match took place. BLC then fades and the ref lifts the arm, drops it once. He drops the arm a second time. The arm drops a third time and the ref calls for the bell. Kelly is the winner by knockout.
Henry Lesher - That was pathetic. Baby Calaway - Yes, Mister Bear, you're right. BLC was brave like the brave little toaster. He is my hero, too. Kelly is nifty in those tights. Lesher is a stinkyhead.
Lesher is heard groaning as we head backstage and see Frank Merritt with Charley MacAdam. Charley MacAdam - BLC suffers a loss there thanks to your softening him up. Frank Merritt - No, he lost to a woman fair and square. Kelly couldn't beat women up, but she can take out BLC. The guy is pathetic and I have no fear of him at all.
Charley MacAdam - So if I told you that two weeks from tonight Shelia has scheduled a match inside a cage between you and BLC, it wouldn't bother you?
Frank Merritt - She can't do that! I never signed a contract! That's garbage! Frank turns a table over and heads out screaming. Angela vs Harmony Fallacia vs Tommy Air "Heads Explode" by Monster Magnet plays and Harmony Fallacia makes her way to the ring to the cheers of the crowd. She is attacked on the rampway by Angela who lands a series of double axe handle smashes to the back of Harmony before throwing her off the ramp. We see Tommy Air come out from the back and he attacks Angela from the back and pulls her down the ramp the rest of the way to the ring. Tommy rolls Angela into the ring and we see Harmony get to her feet and start resuming her trek to the ring. We then see Kathy Smith coming out from the back with Mr. Binky in hand. Tommy stands Angela up, whips her off the ropes and both wrestlers dropkick each other in the center of the ring. Harmony then slides into the ring with Kathy at ringside with Mr. Binky, looking ready to deal some damage on the Assault Women's champion.
Henry Lesher - Kathy has no need to be out here. Much like she is with her ex husband, she manages to have her nose everywhere it doesn't belong.
Baby Calaway - You know, Mister Snuggledybear, you're a nice bear. Kathy is a nice girl. Some people aren't nice and are really stinky bad doctors.
Harmony grabs Angela by the hair and takes a jawbreaker from Angela. Tommy then dropkicks Angela down before she has a chance to get ready for the shot. Angela tumbles outside the ring and Kathy gives her an evil look. Kathy stalks around to her and looks ready to drive Mr. Binky into her forehead. Angela smiles as we see Tiffany emerging through the crowd. She heads over the guard railing and attacks Kathy with a chair. Angela laughs and enters the ring to a rude reception. Harmony kicks Angela in the head repeatedly. Tommy stands back and watches at first, then grabs them both by the hair and rams their heads together. Tommy then whips Angela into the turnbuckle and then whips Harmony into the same corner. He gives Harmony a German suplex, gets to his feet and takes a superkick to the chin from Angela. Angela goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tommy Air. Harmony gets to her feet, then leg drops Angela to the back of the neck before she has a chance to get up. Harmony then picks Angela up and slams her onto Tommy's chest. She then comes off the ropes and does a rolling thunder onto both of the fallen foes. She pulls Angela off Tommy and goes for the pin on him. 1...2... kick out by Tommy Air.
Henry Lesher - This match is awful. Baby Calaway - Mister Snuggledybear likes it, says it's exciting. Henry Lesher - No one cares. Harmony stands Tommy up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Harmony then gets rolled up from behind by Angela. 1...2... Harmony manages to kick out in time to keep the match going. Angela then stands Harmony up, whips her off the ropes and Harmony ducks a clothesline attempt by Angela. Tommy lands a drop toe hold on Harmony from the mat and then tries for an STF only to have Angela drop a knee to the back of Tommy's neck. Angela then stands Tommy up, whips him off the ropes and lands the Angela's Ashes and goes for the pin. 1...2... Tommy manages to get a foot on the bottom rope. Angela then stands Tommy up, whips him into the corner and gives him a dropkick in the corner. Tiffany jumps onto the apron. Angela pulls brass knuckles from her tights and drills Tommy Air in the chin with them and then delivers the Angela's Ashes again. She then turns and clubs Harmony in the chin with the brass knuckles, tosses the knucks out of the ring and goes for the pin as Tiffany drops off the apron. 1...2...3! Angela pulls off the win! Angela rolls out of the ring, sees Kathy Smith getting to her feet and starts putting the boots to her. She grabs Mr. Binky off the floor and slams it over the back of Kathy. She and Tiffany then raise each other's arms and head to the back.
Henry Lesher - Angela taking pride in her work tonight. Backstage we see Shelia Lavin in her office not looking impressed with what she just saw. Shelia Lavin - Flock is biting off more than it can chew. Kathy gets a shot at the Women's title at Blind Rage then next Monday. See how Angela does against her one on one.
Lone Star vs Ash "Lay Low Remix" plays and we expect Lone Star to come out. Instead, we find Lone Star and Ash already exchanging rights and lefts on stage as they emerge through the curtain. Lone Star kicks Ash in the gut. Ash kicks Lone Star in the gut. Lone Star eye gouges Ash. Ash eye gouges Lone Star. The crowd goes wild as they see Ash and Lone Star brawling and fall right off the corner of the stage area and all the way to the floor far below.
Henry Lesher - There is no controlling this. Baby Calaway - Nasty people, huh Snuggledybear? Lone Star gets to his feet first, but takes a shoulder block from Ash into the side of the scaffolding holding up the stage area. Lone Star then knee lifts Ash to the face. Both men are spitting blood now as Lone Star sets Ash up for a DDT. Ash shoulder blocks Lone Star into the steel scaffolding again. Ash then places Lone Star in a headlock and looks ready to give him a suplex on the floor. Lone Star knees Ash in the gut to break it and then slams Ash's head into the steel scaffolding. Lone Star picks Ash up over his shoulder and rams him head first into the steel. Ash then falls off Lone Star's shoulder, now bleeding from the forehead.
Henry Lesher - This is a fight! Baby Calaway - Snuggledybear, maybe you should watch the lights. This is kinda tense and stuff.
She points the face of the teddy bear up towards the lights and appears not to be watching the violence herself. We then head back to the carnage and find Lone Star grabbing an audio cord and whipping Ash with it. Ash responds by grabbing a nearby chair and clubbing Lone Star across the knee with it. Lone Star falls and Ash crowns him in the forehead with the chair. Ash then lands a series of punches to the head of Lone Star before he stands up and starts dragging him down to the ringside area. Just when we believe this match might finally hit the ring, Lone Star finds the strength to hip toss Ash over the guard railing and into the crowd. Lone Star then heads over the railing and DDTs Ash to the floor. He rolls Ash over, slaps him across the face and shouts some unmentionable things at him. Lone Star stands Ash up, and then pulls him by the hair towards the back. He rams his head into a folding chair, then pulls him to the doorway heading into the concessions area. He rams Ash's head into the door. Ash responds with a desperation sweep of the legs out from underneath Lone Star.
Henry Lesher - Doesn't get any better than this. Baby calaway - Don't listen to his, Snuggledybear. Look at the lights. It's not over yet. Ash plants an elbow in the chest of Lone Star. Ash then lands a series of punches to the head of Lone Star before headbutting him. Ash then slowly gets to his feet, spitting blood on the concrete floor. Ash opens the door into the concessions area and pulls Lone Star behind him. There they are met by security and a man who appears to be Headhunter from BLPW?! Ash is taken aback, then takes a spear from the Headhunter look-alike. That look alike then reveals he is actually... Scavenger! Scavenger then smiles, but takes a series of rights and lefts from Lone Star! That ends it, the match now being declared a no contest. Scavenger and Lone Star battle while Ash gets to his feet and the three of them continue their brawl while security tries in vain to separate them. Backstage we see Frank Merritt looking quite satisfied with what he is seeing here.
Henry Lesher - Looks like we have a triple threat match for the world title next week! Frank gets to deal with the scraps known as Ash and Lone Star. That will be a war.
Baby Calaway - And I am gonna be there in Russia with Russia people doing Russia things. It could be neat. There's a big curtain there?
Henry Lesher - IQ dropping more with every passing moment. See you next week. Baby Calaway - Night! Don't let the bedbugs bite! We fade to black with Scavenger, Ash and Lone Star still embroiled in a major fight here.
