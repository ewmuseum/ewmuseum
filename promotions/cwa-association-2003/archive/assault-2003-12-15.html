CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 15 December 2003

15 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The crowd cheers as the opening credits roll. We see images of all of the cinder block attacks that have taken place over the past few weeks. We then see Shannon telling Dante to find out who is doing this or it's his job on the line. We then head to ringside where Henry Lesher and Ricky Simms are ready to get the show on the road.
Noah Skanes vs Dante "Just Like You" by Three Days Grace plays as Noah Skanes makes his way to the ring. The crowd loves the kid as he steps into the ring and salutes the fans. We then hear "Know" by System of a Down and Dante makes his way to the ring withbout nearly as warm a reception. He has a T-shirt on that proclaims him as Assault's official investigator. He has a few words with fans at ringside as he makes his way into the ring. He then turns his attention to Noah and threatens him which is met by taunts from the kid. The bell sounds and the two lock up. Dante immediately cheap shots Noah with a knee to the midsection. Dante then whips Noah off the ropes and attempts a clothesline which Noah ducks. Noah delivers a spinning neckbreaker on Dante and hooks the leg for a quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Dante.
Ricky Simms - Welcome to the show, boys and girls. Things are starting fast here. Henry Lesher - It's the holidays. All the idiots want to run home to their families. Ricky Simms - What are your Christmas plans? Henry Lesher - I want to kill some reindeer, maybe send a few go to hell greeting cards and then drink till I forget who I am.
Ricky Simms - That is truly touching. Noah stands Dante up, whips him to the ropes and Dante uses the ropes to stop his progress and then drops and rolls out of the ring to collect himself against this barrage. The crowd cheers as Noah heads to the ropes and climbs to the top, but he thinks better of it and jumps back down to the mat as Dante gets onto the apron. Dante steps through the ropes and didges a roundhouse kick from Noah. Dante then gives Noah an atomic drop to the mat followed by a clothesline. Dante then puts the boots to the son of the owner before laying him over the bottom rope, then coming off the ropes and straddling him, choking him across the bottom rope. Dante then rakes the eyes of Noah before kicking him in the back a few times as the ref tries to restrain him.
Ricky Simms - Dante with a real vicious streak here this week. Henry Lesher - The man's job is at stake. Of course he's mean. Dante pulls Noah to his feet and delivers a series of knife edge chops to the chest. Dante then whips Noah into the corner buckle and charges in only to receive a forearm smash to the shoulder from Noah followed by a knee lift to the jaw. Noah then delivers a spinebuster on Dante and goes for the pin. 1...2... Dante gets a foot on the bottom rope. Noah then stands Dante up, whips him off the ropes and lands a backdrop on Dante. Noah then stands Dante up and lands a vertical suplex on him as the crowd starts sensing that Noah is getting into a groove here. He stands Dante up and lands a snap suplex on him as we see Fallout coming out of the back to the boos of the crowd.
Ricky Simms - This is never a good thing. Henry Lesher - Oh, it's always a good thing when there's more nightmares out here than less.
Noah pulls Dante to his feet and then headbutts him back down. He spots Fallout coming and alerts the ref to Fallout's presence. The ref heads over to warn off Fallout and Adam Lewis enters the ring and delivers a chair shot to the head of Noah Skanes before racing right back out of the ring. Dante makes the cover as Fallout retreats and the ref makes the count. 1...2...3! Dante pulls off a cheap win here.
Ricky Simms - Another despicable win for our commissioner. Henry Lesher - Sorry, I didn't hear anything after you said win. Backstage we see Vigilante entering the building to a huge ovation. He shines the titles belt over his shoulder as he is approached by Q who receives a loud chorus of boos.
Q - Enjoy the belt, Vigilante. Once we get past the holidays, it's mine. Vigilante - Haven't you heard that I rock? Outta my way, nutboy. Q laughs as Vigilante steps past him and continues on his way. Shelia Lavin enters the picture shaking her head.
Shelia Lavin - Someone needs to deal with him. Q - Couldn't agree more. 6Pac Dudley vs Black Panther The crowd cheers as "Southtown" by POD plays and FUBU, Romeo Salvador and Spikey Tiger lead 6Pac Dudley to the ring. Spikey is carrying the Canadian flag. 6Pac enters the ring and tests the ropes as "Fight Music" by D12 plays and Black Panther makes his way to the ring led by The Cossack, Jon Shocker and Night Dweller. They get an ovation and it's clear these two groups don't much appreciate each other's presence here. Panther steps into the ring as the two factions start to have words.
Ricky Simms - Looks like war is about to erupt at ringside. Henry Lesher - Big deal. We get to see Northern Exposure get their red and white asses kicked enough.
The bell sounds and 6Pac attacks Black Panther with a vicious kick to the knee cap of Panther before slapping on an ankle lock submission trying for a surprise win here early. Panther scrambles to the ropes and grabs the bottom rope to force the break. 6Pac then lays the boots to the knee of Panther, clearly feeling that the best way to keep the big man down was to blow one of his tires out. 6Pac starts measuring the knee for repeated elbow drops while his group cheers him on from ringside. Panther manages to roll out of the ring to collect himself. 6Pac climbs to the second rope and poses for the fans. There are a few scattered boos from the Dark Forces supporters. Panther is helped to his feet by Shocker who starts jawing at 6Pac.
Ricky Simms - This situation is going to explode. Henry Lesher - If we're lucky, yes. Panther rolls into the ring and 6Pac tries immediately for a figure four on him. Panther kicks 6Pac in the back with his good leg and gets to his feet with the use of the ropes as 6Pac collides with the corner buckle. Panther gives 6Pac a DDT and goes for a pin, hooking the leg. 1...2... 6Pac kicks out. Panther then rains down a series of rights and lefts to the head of 6Pac. The crowd cheers as Panther screams and then lands a series of elbows right between the eyes of 6Pac. Panther then stands 6Pac up, whips him off the ropes and tries for a clothesline which 6Pac ducks. 6Pac then chop blocks the knees out from under Black Panther. 6Pac then tries for and executes a Boston Crab on Panther. Panther reaches for the ropes, but he is too far away. 6Pac tries leaning back further, but Panther manages to push himself away from the mat and then inches his way over to and grabs the ropes.
Ricky Simms - 6Pac with a number of close calls here this far, but he can't quite finish off Black Panther.
Henry Lesher - I doubt he can. It is 6Pac, after all. Panther gets to his feet. 6Pac grabs the sore leg of Black Panther, lifts it intending to pull Panther off of his feet. Panther instead uses the ropes to bring both feet up and kicks 6Pac square in the chest. 6Pac comes off of the ropes and takes a spear from Panther. Panther goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by 6Pac Dudley. Panther then stands 6Pac up, looks to the crowd and they cheer. Panther then grabs 6Pac by the throat and delivers the Black Death with 6Pac landing hard, face first on the mat. Panther then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Black Panther picks up the win and the ring fills as FUBU, Tiger and Romeo enter the ring only to be joined by Dweller, Shocker and Cossack. The six intruders battle each other as Panther pulls 6Pac to his feet and tries for another Black Death as people appear from the back to try and separate these two siperpowers.
Ricky Simms - Northern Exposure not taking the loss weel. Henry Lesher - That's odd. You would think that they would be used to it by now. Backstage we see Shannon Skanes chasing down Dante. He grabs him by the arm and turns him around to face him.
Shannon Skanes - Nice little attack on my kid! Dante - Glad you liked it. I have bigger fish to fry. Shannon Skanes - Maybe you and me need to hit the ring again. Dante - You're already one step away from ending your career. Please let me push you over. Shannon Skanes - You had better name the culprit tonight or you'll be out of a job. Dante - Oh I have your culprit. Trust me. Shannon Skanes - I'll never trust you. Dante - Smart man. Dante leaves Shannon there as he swears to himself. MLSQ42 vs Dex Cooper "Shit Hits the Fan" by Obie Trice plays as Dex Cooper starts on his way to the ring for what is touted as a hardcore match up. He is pushing a shopping cart full of weapons down the ramp when he is attacked from behind by Q who nails him with a boot to the back of the head. Q then rams Dex's face into the cart, tosses him off the ramp to the floor and then dumps the contents of the cart onto him before he has a chance to get up.
Henry Lesher - Q is here. Ricky Simms - We noticed. Q pushes the cart to the ring and then heads down the side of the ramp where a crumpled Dex Cooper lays bleeding on the floor. Q stands Dex up and rams his head into the guard railing. He then pulls him to ringside and whips him into the steel ring steps. Q then stands Dex up and lands a few knife edge chops on him. He then rolls Dex into the ring. Q makes his way down the side of the ramp and picks up a stop sign and carries it to the ring. Dex stumbles to his feet, the blood running down his face. Q steps onto the apron. Dex punches the stop sign right into the face of Dex Cooper.
Henry Lesher - Good start to this match. We have weapons, blood and Q. What more could anyone want in a match?
Ricky Simms - Notice that the title is noton the line. Henry Lesher - Shouldn't be on the line either. Dex has earned nothing. Dex grabs Q by the hair, pulls him onto the apron and then headbutts him off. Q stumbles and falls back, nailing his spine against the shopping cart. Dex then steps through the ropes and jumps off the apron landing a double axe handle to the chest of Q. Dex then picks Q up and body slams him on the floor. Dex then makes his way alongside the ramp where the weapons lay and picks up a trash can. Q gets to his feet and turns just in time to take a shot to the head from the trash can. Q falls back to the floor. Dex then places the trash can across the chest of Q and leg drops the trash can onto Q. Dex follows this up by rolling Q back into the ring. Dex then steps onto the apron and tosses the trash can into the ring.
Ricky Simms - Dex taking control here. Henry Lesher - Not for long. Q is the champ for a reason. Dex steps into the ring and picks up the trash can. He then takes a low blow from Q. Q grabs the trash can and drills Dex in the forehead with it. Q heads to the corner and starts pulling the turnbuckle pad off the turnbuckle. Dex gets to his feet and charges at Q, ramming his head into the half off turnbuckle pad. Dex then picks Q up for a belly to back suplex to the mat. Dex follows that up with a knee drop to the throat of Q before rolling out of the ring. He grabs a table from under the ring and slides it inside. Dex starts setting up the table as Q gets to his feet. Dex finishes setting up the table, turns and takes a kick to the face from Q. Q sets Dex for a Pedigree only to have Dex backdrop him through the table to the delight of the crowd.
Ricky Simms - Q tasting the wood there. Henry Lesher - He'll get back up. Dex goes for the pin. 1...2... Q gets a foot on the bottom rope. Dex then pulls Q into the middle of the ring, delivers a leg drop and again tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Q. Dex stands Q up, whips him off the ropes and knees him in the abdomen which sends him somersaulting through the air and to the mat. That's saying something for a man of Q's size.
Ricky Simms - Dex in full control. Henry Lesher - You only wish. Dex stands Q up, takes a thumb in the eye and Q delivers a Q Jumper on Dex and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Q picks up the win seemingly out of nowhere. He rolls out of the ring and raises his arms in victory as he snatches his belt away from the timekeeper.
Henry Lesher - Look who won. Ricky Simms - Yeah yeah... bully for Q. The crowd cheers as they see Moose in the back with Charley MacAdam. Charley MacAdam - Moose, you're taking a week off this week. Any thoughts on the action thus far?
Moose - It's as good as any show can be without the main eventers. I mean, EOS is the top group and we merely taking some time to enjoy the holidays as only we can.
Charley MacAdam - Who is next on your hit list? Moose - Well, wouldn't you like to know? The fact is we are going to get to the very top and anyone in our way is going to pay the ultimate price.
Shelia Lavin vs Giovanni Mabius "Better Harder Faster Stronger" by Daft Punk plays as Shelia Lavin makes her way to the ring. She receives as warm a welcome as Q did moments earlier and returns the sentiment with a few rude hand gestures. She steps into the ring as "Calfornia Love" by Tupac plays and Giovanni Mabius makes his way to the ring with his usual Youthful Aggression T-shirt. The crowd doesn't appear to like him either. He steps into the ring and is attacked by Shelia before the bell even has a chance to ring. Shelia whips Mabius off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. She then applies a head scissors on Mabius and attempts to strangle him out.
Henry Lesher - Nice beginning for the match for Lavin here. Ricky Simms - Typical tactics, but they do work for Reality so I can't say I am surprised by what they do anymore.
Mabius battles to his hands and knees before Shelia releases the hold, aware he is not going to give up now. Shelia places Mabius in an arm bar, but takes an eye gouge from Mabius followed by a stiff right hand to the jaw which knocks her down. Mabius smiles as he puts his boot on her throat and chokes her until the ref demands he release her. Mabius then stands Shelia up and whips her through the ropes to the outside of the ring. He follows this up by climbing to the top rope. Shelia turns, sees Mabius coming and sidesteps the coming elbow smash. Mabius hits the floor hard and then slams into the guard railing. Shelia then grabs Mabius by the hair and whips him into the steel ring steps before she rolls herself back into the ring.
Ricky Simms - Shelia back in control here. Henry Lesher - She is very well versed in going outside the ring. Mabius gets to his feet and then rolls himself into the ring before the ref has a chance to reach a ten count. Mabius is then stood up and whips into the opposite turnbuckle by Shelia Lavin. Shelia and Mabius then clothesline each other to the mat to the cheers of all. The ref starts counting them both out while the crowd counts along with him. Mabius reaches the ropes and uses them to get to his feet moments before Shelia Lavin has a chance to do the same. Mabius kicks Shelia in the gut, then gives her a rake to the eyes followed by a knee lift to the face as he then sets her up for a power bomb. He gets her into the air for it, but she starts landing punches to the head of Giovanni and then headscissors him to the mat. She then goes for a quick pin. 1...2... Mabius kick out.
Ricky Simms - She can't have expected a three count there. Henry Lesher - Why not? It's not like she's facing a champion like Q or Dante. Shelia stands Mabius up, whips him off the ropes and lariats him to the mat. She then goes for the pin. 1...2... again Mabius kicks out. Shelia then sets herself and waits for Mabius to get up. She then delivers sweet chin music on Mabius and he falls like a rock. She goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Shelia Lavin is the winner!
Henry Lesher - Two for two tonight. Ricky Simms - Executioner would have made it three for three if he had been here. Backstage we see Leech pacing back and forth and not appearing to be very impressed. He appears annoyed with Samantha Johnson as she enters the picture to interview him.
Leech - What do you want? Samantha Johnson - Leech, there has been a lot of talk lately about your continued part time duty here and its effect on the Flock. What do you have to say on the matter?
Leech - **** off. That's what I have to say. Samantha Johnson - The fans want to know... Leech - The fans want to know? Really? The fans can mind their own business. If Leech is fighting his own personal demons, then that is his business. Now get out of here while I am being nice because of who your sister happens to be.
Samantha retreats out of the shot as Leech continues pacing. Ash makes his way past shaking his head on his way to the ring.
Ash vs Max Cameron vs Spikey Tiger "Eye of the Tiger" by Survivor plays as Spikey Tiger makes his way to the ring waving the Canadian flag. The crowd cheers as he makes his way down the ramp and into the ring. He hands his flag off to the time keeper and then has a few words with the ref. As this happens, we hear "Hell's Bells" and hardcore champ Max Cameron makes his way to the ring. He receives a nasty response from the crowd and he and Spikey are getting into it verbally while he is still only half way down the ramp. Max stops at the bottom of the ramp and continues his argument with Spikey. As they spat, we hear "The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson and out comes Ash on a dead run. He attacks Max from behind and throws him head first into the railing before sliding into the ring where he and Spikey start going at it as the bell sounds to get things underway.
Ricky Simms - Max was too busy arguing with Spikey to actually get into the ring there. Henry Lesher - Max needs to smarten up. Oh well, what can I expect? Ash whips Spikey off the ropes and gives him a sidewalk slam. Ash follows this up with an elbow smash to the throat and then places Spikey in a sleeper as we see Max collecting himself at ringside. Max slides into the ring behind Ash and places him in a sleeper as well. The crowd cheers as Ash drops Spikey and then snap mares Max to the mat. Ash then dropkicks Max in the back and the crowd goes wild.
Ricky Simms - They sure like Ash compared to his opponents here tonight. That might be the loudest cheer I have heard him get.
Henry Lesher - Interesting that the Flock gets such a huge response. Maybe the audience is even dumber than I thought.
Ash stands Spikey up and attempts a whip into the corner only to have Tiger reverse it. Tiger charges in but Ash is not home. Spikey's shoulder hits the corner. Ash gives Spikey Tiger a German suplex. Max then grabs Ash and headbuuts him and stands him up with Spikey and rams their heads together. Max delivers a knife edge chop to each man twice before Ash kicks Max in the gut. Ash then rams Spikey and Max's heads together. Ash whips Max into the corner and then attempts to whip Spikey into that same corner only to have it reversed and nearly gets his head taken off by Max Cameron as he comes out of the corner. Spikey then superkicks Max in the jaw and goes for the pin. 1...2... Max kicks out. Spikey stands Max up and then tosses him over the top rope to the outside. Spikey climbs out onto the apron and lands a double axe handle to the back of Max Cameron. Inside the ring, Ash gets to his feet and watches Max and Spikey battle outside the ring. Ash charges and la planchas both of them through the ropes to the delight of the crowd.
Ricky Simms - Ash showing well here. Henry Lesher - Yeah, an opportunist usually wins a match like this one. Ash rolls Spikey into the ring and then rolls himself inside. Ash then delivers the Cashed on Spikey and goes for the pin. 1...2.. Max makes the save by pulling Ash put of the ring. max then lands a series of rights and lefts to the head of Ash. He tries to whip Ash into the steel post, but Ash reverses it. Ash then rolls Max into the ring and headsfor the ropes as Spikey Tiger gets to his feet. Ash reaches the top. Spikey nudges the top rope and knocks Ash off balance and causes him to fall to the mat. Spikey stands Max up, picks him up and body slams him onto Ash. He then rolls Max away and tries for the pin on Ash. 1...2... kick out by Ash.
Ricky Simms - Ash was nearly done there. Henry Lesher - I would love to announce who I am cheering for, but they can all go to hell.
Ricky Simms - It is touching to see you in the spirit of the season. Spikey stands Ash up, whips him into the buckle and then takes a clothesline from behind by Max Cameron. Max then lands a dropkick on Ash. Max stands Ash up, whips him into the buckle and gives him a sidewalk slam to the mat. He goes for the pin. 1...2... Spikey breaks it up, pulling Max off and landing rights and lefts to the head of Max Cameron. Spikey then whips Max off the ropes. Max clotheslines Spikey down as Ash kips up to his feet and delivers the Cashed on Max Cameron before going for the pin. 1...2... Spikey breaks it up!
Ricky Simms - Another Cashed and another near fall that is broken up at the last moment. Henry Lesher - Ash has no finish. It's that simple. This match could go on all night. Spikey whips Ash off the ropes and takes a dropkick from Ash. Max gets to his feet, goes for a lock up with Ash and instead takes a DDT from him. Ash tries for the quick pin, hooking the leg. 1...2...3! Spikey interferes, but is a moment too late and the winner is Ash. Ash rolls out of the ring with his arms raised in victory. Leech appears at the top of the ramp and applauds his efforts as Ash makes his way back, high fiving fans along the way.
Ricky Simms - Impressive win by Ash here. Henry Lesher - Yes, it has been quite the night... blah blah blah... Backstage we see Scavenger with Lauren and Night Dweller. Night Dweller - Scav, it is nice to see you have life figured out now. You have a good woman and a good chair.
Scavenger - Yeah, it's the good life for sure. The crowd cheers as they see Sierra, Scavenger's ex, enter the frame and shake her head. Scavenger - Where the hell did you come from? Sierra - Scav, we need to talk. *looks Lauren up and down* Yeah, we really need to talk. PB FUBU vs Black Lightning The large Cell is lowered around the ring and it is now time for the Hell in the Cell match. "SharpShooter [Best of the Best]" by the Rascalz featuring Bret "Hitman" Hart, blares over the speakers. There is a huge pop from the crowd as Northern Exposure founder PB FUBU appears on the stage waving his Canadian flag. He walks down the aisle, high fiving the fans at ring side. He grabs a mic from the ring announcer and enters the Cell. He then raises the mic up and starts to talk ......
PB FUBU - Yo Lightning, I caught your promo yesterday, and ya didn’t prove a damn thing other than that you are a demented gothic, freak who shoulda stayed in that fucking mental hospital. There you coulda talked about your imaginary Creatures, and people would actually believe you. Well dawg, you don’t scare me one fucking bit and either does this Cell or the barbed wire. In fact, adding barb wire to the cell, just makes tha match that much more hardcore, which is right up NE’s dark alley. So get your gothic, blood licking ass out here and lets get this party started.
Ricky Simms - That will get us off the air in 21 states. Henry Lesher - Lucky states. PB FUBU hands his flag and the mic to a member of the ring crew as "Last Resort" by Papa Roach blasts over the speakers. All of the house lights go off as a spot light hits the aisle. Black Lightning walks down the aisle slowly. He is wearing his long black cloak and is not looking at anyone in the crowd. He stops at the Cell’s door, and bows his head as if he is praying. He then pulls off his cloak and enters the Cell. The door is then locked and the match is officially started. Before Black Lightning can do anything, FUBU sling shots himself over the top rope and nails him with a flying cross body block. They both fall backwards and Black Lightning’s back crashes into the cell.
Ricky Simms - This is going to be a nasty fight. Henry Lesher - All tag chamos should face each other after they pass their peak. It's good for them.
FUBU grabs him around the waist and pulls him off of the barb wire. FUBU throws Black Lightning over his head with a belly-to-belly suplex. Black Lightning lands hard on the floor as the fans cheer FUBU on. FUBU then reaches out to grab Black Lightning again, but he is met with a low blow. Black Lightning then grabs FUBU’s head and drills him to the floor with a DDT. Black Lightning then grabs FUBU’s long hair and uses it pick him off the ground and toss him face first into the cell.
Ricky Simms - Tremendous punishment is suffered in these matches that usually end careers. Henry Lesher - If only we could be so lucky. FUBU’s face is deeply cut upon impact. Black Lightning jumps on this opportunity and begins to rub FUBU’s face across the wire, busting it wide open in the process. Black Lightning then pulls FUBU off the wire and flashes that evil grin at the fact that FUBU’s face is now a bloody mess. Black Lightning throws FUBU into the ring who lands on his back near the ropes. Black Lightning then jumps onto the apron, sling shots himself over the top rope and crashes down on FUBU with a leg drop. He goes for the pin but only gets a one count as FUBU gets his foot on the bottom rope. Black Lightning swears as he pulls FUBU away from the ropes. Black Lightning then bounces off of the middle rope and goes for a Lionsault, but lands hard on FUBU’s knees. Black Lightning grabs his gut and appears to be in great discomfort after that impact.
Ricky Simms - These guys are both going to be in rough shape after this is done. FUBU slowly gets up and sees that Black Lightning his now on his knees. FUBU runs towards him, jumps up and nails him with a Shining Wizard to the back of the head. Black Lightning falls back down to the mat, and FUBU quickly jumps up to the top rope. He flies off with a moonsault and lands right on Black Lightning’s torso. The ref makes a two count before Black Lightning kicks out. FUBU, doesn’t let this frustrate him as he goes right back to Black Lightning. FUBU picks Black Lightning up and throws him into the turnbuckle. FUBU then follows him in for a shoulder block, but Black Lightning moves. FUBU’s shoulder hits the post hard and Black Lightning rolls him up in a school boy. The ref counts to two before FUBU kicks out. Black Lightning then picks FUBU off of the mat and drops him with a body slam. He waits for FUBU to get up before running towards the corner, running up the ropes, jumping off and nailing FUBU with the Lightning Kick to the head. FUBU falls to the mat and appears to be out cold. However, instead of going for the pin, Black Lightning just spits on FUBU and then rolls out of the ring. He looks under the ring and pulls out a table. He then tries to push it into the ring. However, before he can get it all the way into the ring, it comes flying back and nails Black Lightning in the face, as FUBU hit it with a baseball slide. Black Lightning falls to the floor and FUBU slides under the bottom rope and out of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Hard to believe these two were teammates mere weeks ago. Henry Lesher - Proves there is a thin line between love and hate. As Black Lightning gets up, FUBU kicks him in the head. FUBU then looks under the ring and pulls out a chair. He swings it at Black Lightning’s head and connects. The chair bends over Black Lightning’s head and blood starts to pour down and into his mouth. FUBU drops the bent chair on the floor and stands over Black Lightning laughing. FUBU then reaches down to grab Black Lightning. However, as he does so, Black Lightning spits some of his blood right into FUBU’s eyes. Black Lightning then grabs FUBU’s arm and whips him hard into the cell.
Ricky Simms - I am amazed at the change in Lightning. Henry Lesher - He claims to be a member of the darkness now. Black Lightning pulls out another chair from under the ring and smashes it across FUBU’s back. He does this a few times, and each blow drives the wire deeper into FUBU’s chest. Black Lightning then throws this chair down beside the other one and stares at FUBU who is stuck to the wire. Black Lightning then turns towards the table and slides it into the ring. He sets it up in the middle of the ring and then notices that FUBU has managed to pull himself off of the wire. Black Lightning quickly runs to the corner jumps up the top rope, flies off and nails FUBU with a missile drop kick. Black Lightning then picks FUBU up and tries to roll him into the ring. However, FUBU reaches into his jeans pocket and pulls out a set of brass knucks. He slides them onto his hand and then smashes them right into Black Lightning’s temple. Black Lightning falls down and appears to be out cold. FUBU throws Black Lightning into the ring and lays him out on the table. FUBU then climbs to the top rope and signals for the CN TOWER BOMB. He flies off but at the last second, Black Lightning rolls off the table and onto the mat. FUBU then crashes hard through the table and is now surrounded by wood. Now its Black Lightning’s turn to climb to the top rope. He does so and signals for the ShockWave. He flies off and nails FUBU with it. This looks like the end for FUBU. However, Black Lightning does not go for the pin. Instead he looks straight up towards the rafters and waves his hand in a down ward motion. After a few moments, a tall figure can be seen descending from the rafters on a long rope.
Ricky Simms - What the hell.... Henry Lesher - Hell is a good guess. The bottom of this rope is tied in the shape of a noose. As the figure gets lowered, the fans see who it is and they are shocked to see that it is PUPPETMASTER.. He is lowered all the way down to the top of the cell. Puppetmaster unfastens his harness and stands on the roof. He then pulls a pair of wire cutters out of his cloak and cuts a hole in the cell’s roof. He then waves his hand and the rope continues to lower through the roof and all the way down to the mat. Black Lightning then grabs the rope and places the noose over FUBU’s neck. The rope is then raised, and FUBU is lifted off the mat and is now being hung. The lights go off and the entire cell bursts into the flames. When the lights come back on Black Lightning and Puppetmaster are now where in sight. The ref tries to pull FUBU down but he is not strong enough. The outside ref quickly opens the door and enters the cell to help. Just then Spikey Tiger runs down the aisle with a large knife in his hand. He hits the ring and quickly cuts his friend down.
