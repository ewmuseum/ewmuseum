CWA (Association, 2003) — History

CWA History: a brief history and time capsule

undated (captured 2 August 2003)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: Brief History The following is a brief history and time capsule of the CWA May 3rd - Leech and The Moose work together inside a cage against 6Pac Dudley in the main event and a tag team is formed. CellBlock is fast counted by ref Troy Strong who is burned later that week in a CellBlock promo.
May 10 - Sinners Inc, Vesuvius/Twilight, Eternal Nightmares and The Mosee/Leech move on in tournament to decide the tag team champs of the CWA. Romeo Salvador wins Hardcore Hell and becomes the hardcore champion.
May 17 - Double Trouble makes a one time appearance and gets the win over Spikey Tiger. Mach arrives in the CWA. In a Cruiserweight Rumble, 6Pac Dudley wins the belt by hook and by crook. The Eternal Nightmares win the tag belts in a four team single elimination match to become the champions. In that match, Leech turned on The Moose starting a major feud.
Overload May 25 - Leech takes the hardcore title in a glass chamber match with Romeo Salvador with Mick Foley as special guest referee. Damien wins a four way match to get a shot at the cruiserweight title. Mach and Diablo battle to a draw in a tower of doom match.
May 31 - Mach, mlsq42, Dizz, Leech, The Moose, Celia, Romeo Salvador and Cerberus move on to round two in world title tournament. Damien defeats 6Pac Dudley to become the new Cruiserweight champ.
June 7 - Dizz gets the bye into the third round. Cerberus, Mach and Leech earn huge wins to join Dizz. Leech and The Moose appear to have put issues behind them, but Leech also attacked his own wife Amy. NADP earns a shot at the tag belts. CellBlock had his hands in a few matches and stuck Scavenger in a body bag at the end of their match up. Lethal Weapon earns a hard fought first win over The Freak in a debut match for both that was memorable.
June 14 - Mach and Dizz win their matches and move to the finals. Freak wins over Asmodeus and then takes 3 Mach 5s before falling to Mach that same night! Freak is on his way up, way up! Lethal Weapon starts his war with MSK by knocking down 6Pac for the count and declaring his intention of going after CellBlock. The Unstable reunites and ruins the World Tag Team match between NADP and the Eternal Nightmares with the return to action of Full House. Katt Wylde and Scarlet Bitch win their debut matches as do Cyrus and Canadian Hero. Diablo is fired by Sinners Inc., ending his reign of terror in the CWA! Neil Kelly appears on CWA TV and the rarely heard from VP Dave is not impressed.
June 21 - Neil Kelly does not come out this week, but his one appearance seems to signal something for VP Dave as he calls the shots for the tag team title match at Sunstroke, calling for a scaffold match! The CWA crowns a World champion in Dizz, but it is not without controversy. Freak and Q make a statement about their mutual dislike for Mach and ensure he does win the title. However, Mach is named for a rematch next week in a triple threat match that will also involve Q. BOD are crowned as new tag champs this week inside a cage when Havok and Asmodeus get involved and all hell breaks loose. Celia wins the Cruiserweight title from Damien thanks to a momentary distraction by Lunacy Muse. The Warriors roster spills over into the Assault show and a rumble is announced that will involve the CWA owner himself tagging withn Bruce Miller for the first time in a very long time. CellBlock wins a bloody war with Lethal Weapon with a little help from Romeo Salvador. Scavenger appears to join Sinners Inc. as he aligns himself against Vesuvius!
June 29 - Dizz retains the title at Sunstroke with a win over both mlsq42 and Mach. Q then proceeded to steal the belts away and Mach declared this to be his last CWA match. The Moose won a huge double cage match for the hardcore title. Lumberjacks and electricution risks didn't stop him from dethroning Leech as the champ. Full House won a scaffold match against BOD and the Eternal Nightmares. Scavenger dumped tag partner Deep Freeze for a bucket of dry ice which never sublimates. Cerberus won a body bag match with CellBlock and Murderdolls enjoyed a successful debut as a team in the CWA. Kristen Burren appears to have been stolen from the ring during their tag match against the New Word Followers. She is still missing long after the show is over.
July 5 - Dizz loses by count out to Q, so Q is given the Inter Continental belt Mach just vacated. The big news is the return of the Flock. Leech, Xtasy and Freak align to form the group and rumor has it a female will also be joining their ranks. Stable wars have begun between the Unstable, Sinners Inc and the Flock.
