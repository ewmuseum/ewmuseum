CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 8 December 2003

8 December 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
San Diego, California is the scene where we set this week's Assault. The show begins with the standard pyro light show to get the crowd fired up and then the opening credits with images of Vigilante, Dizz, Moose, Demona Calaway, mlsq42, Northern Exposure and Dark Forces before we head to ringside where Ricky Simms and Henry Lesher are in their usual spots.
Northern Exposure vs Arctic Front "Sonne" by Rammstein plays as Cossack and Noah Skanes are walked to the ring by Merlin, the grey haired man with a cane in one hand it is clear he doesn't need as he points it at certain fans on the way to ringside. He takes up his perch at ringside and his team steps into the ring and chat as much as one can with the quiet Russian. We then hear "T.O. Party Anthem" by Maestro and FUBU and Lightning make their way to the ring. Lightning is lagging behind and appears to be mumbling to himself. They step up onto the apron and FUBU has a few words with Lightning who simply nods in response.
Ricky Simms - Lightning not appearing to be quite himself this week. Henry Lesher - Perhaps it is starting to sink in how much his stable sucks. The bell sounds and Noah starts this match off against PB FUBU. The two lock up and Noah quickly goes behind him and gives him a German suplex. He releases and FUBU and Noah stand nose to nose again. They start to lock up and FUBU knees Noah in the gut, then rakes his eyes and whips him off the ropes. Noah takes a hip toss in the center of the ring. FUBU follows this up with a dropkick to the chest of FUBU which drives him through the ropes and to the floor. Noah heads out after FUBU. Lightning is nearby, so he drops a double axe handle to the back of Noah and then heads back to his corner as FUBU gets to his feet and lands a clothesline on Noah before rolling him into the ring. FUBU then starts climbing to the top rope. He dives and lands a flying elbow smash to the chest of Noah and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Noah Skanes.
Ricky Simms - That was close. Henry Lesher - FUBU should have hooked the leg. Oh well, can't expect smart wrestling from these two.
FUBU stands Noah up and whips him off the ropes. Noah ducks a clothesline, tags in Cossack and gives FUBU a spinebuster. Cossack then enters the ring, picks up FUBU and gives him a gorilla press slam. Cossack then stands FUBU up, whips him off the ropes and knees him in the gut, sending him somersaulting into the air and to the mat. He then stands FUBU up and whipsa him into the corner buckle. Cossack charges shoulder first into the corner, but FUBU dodges it and heads to tag his partner as Cossack lands shoulder first in the corner. Lightning looks ready to tag, but waits till FUBU is within reach, then drops off the apron, slingshotting his own partner's throat off the top rope and sending him flying. Cossack shakes off the effects of the shoulder crash into the corner and gives FUBU a pump handle slam as Lightning leaves ringside once again mumbling to himself. Cossack goes for the pin. 1...2....3! Lightning cost Northern Exposure the match!
Ricky Simms - It would appear we have problems within Northern Exposure. Henry Lesher - Within? It's clear Lightning made the smart move and left the team. Ricky Simms - But why? Henry Lesher - Why not. The group is led by 6Pac Dudley. Isn't that reason enough to leave?
Noah and Cossack celebrate in the ring even as they realize something happened to their opponents here this week which they hadn't fathomed before the match had started. Backstage we then see Q with his world title belt and the San Diego crowd even shows him a few cheers as he slings the belt over his shoulder. Shelia Lavin enters the frame.
Shelia Lavin - Big night tonight for both of us. Q - All is going according to plan. Tonight Reality continues its take over of the CWA. The two high five each other as we head back to the ring for our next match up. Spikey Tiger vs Executioner "Eye of the Tiger" by Survivor plays as Spikey Tiger waves a Canadian flag and makes his way to the ring. He high fives a few fans along the way before handing the flag off to the time keeper and then stepping into the ring. "Dirge" by Death In Vegas plays as Tina leads Executioner to the ring. The crowd has a less enthusiastic response to him than they did to Spikey Tiger. Executioner steps onto the apron only to be clotheslined off of it by Spikey Tiger. Spikey then climbs to the top rope and waits for Executioner to get to his feet. Spikey lands a flying body press onto Executioner on the floor. Spikey rolls Executioner and the bell sounds to get this underway.
Henry Lesher - At least Spikey is showing some jump here to start off this match. He'll need it against this guy.
Spikey puts the boots to Executioner and then stands him up, delivers three knife edge chops to the chest and then whips Executioner to the opposite corner. Spikey arm drags Executioner to the middle of the ring and places him in an arm bar. Executioner thumbs Spikey Tiger in the eye, whips him off the ropes and shoulder blocks him to the mat. Executioner then elbow smashes Spikey in the chest. Executioner picks Spikey up and gives him a power slam to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... Spikey Tiger rolls out to prevent the loss. Executioner then stands Spikey up and elbows him in the gut, then gives him a DDT and again tries for the pin. 1...2... Spikey Tiger again kicks out.
Ricky Simms - Executioner and Spikey both showing a lot of jump this week. Henry Lesher - Executioner is an ally of the world champ. Of course he has a lot of jump. If he doesn't, Q comes out here and kicks the crap out of him.
Executioner stands Spikey up and whips him outside the ring. Tina takes a few steps towards Spikey as the ref holds Executioner back. Tina kicks Spikey in the head twice before retreating to the other side of the ring. Spikey gets to his feet and starts pursuing Tina only to take a kick in the face through the ropes from Executioner. Executioner than heads out of the ring despite the ref's protests and efforts to keep this inside the ring. Executioner picks Spikey up and drops him over the guard railing. Executioner then pulls him off of there and attempts a whip into the steps only to have Spikey reverse it. Executioner's shoulder bounces off of the ring steps. Spikey then rolls himself into the ring, shaking off the effects of the attack outside the ring. Executioner rolls himself into the ring moments later as we hear Tina cheering him on.
Ricky Simms - Tina very vocal here. Henry Lesher - She is also very distracting to Spikey Tiger which is key here. Executioner and Spikey get to their feet and then they lock up. Executioner gets behind Spikey and gives him a belly to back suplex. He follows this up with a gutwrench suplex to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Spikey Tiger. Executioner stands Spikey up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Executioner then heads for the ropes. Tina jumps onto the apron to cheer Executioner on. The ref goes to her and tells her to get down. From the crowd comes Shelia Lavin. Shelia tosses the ring bell to Executioner. Executioner leaps from the top rope, drilling the ring bell into the chest of Spikey Tiger before tossing it out of the ring and going for the pin. 1...2...3! Executioner with a highly suspicious win.
Ricky Simms - Wow, that was low. Henry Lesher - The song of the loser, Rick. Executioner wins and that is all that matters here. Chalk up another case of Reality outsmarting their opposition.
Executioner rolls out of the ring, his arms raised in victory. Spikey is choking for breath inside the ring as the ref checks to make sure he's breathing. Tina embraces Executioner and they head out with the crowd booing them louder now than they did before the match began. Backstage we see Black Panther and Jon Shocker with the tag belts over their shoulders. Next to them stands Charley MacAdam.
Charley MacAdam - With me stand the world tag team champions, Jon Shocker and Black Panther. Guys, your thoughts on tonight's match up with The Flock?
Black Panther - It is not the first time I have been here with this belt over my shoulder. Tonight, The Flock is here to take us on and tonight we will retain these titles with a win. We will prove we are not one hit wonders and that we have the strength and will to become the very best and stay there. Winning the titles is easy. Holding them is the real challenge.
Charley MacAdam - Now, that is true. Still, we have... The lights go out and we hear a few solid thuds. Charley MacAdam says nothing and then the lights come back on and broken cinder blocks litter the floor where Black Panther and Jon Shocker are laying. Charley MacAdam looks both ways and then shouts...
Charley MacAdam - We need medics here! Ricky Simms - Is this the work of the Eternal Nightmares? Henry Lesher - I don't know, but anyone who ends a backpatting promo like that has my vote. To me it still seems like someone else must be behind this. Dante likes to be right there to take credit when he pulls this stuff.
Ricky Simms - Good point. Dex Cooper vs Giovanni Mabius "Shit His the Fan" by Obie Trice plays as Dex Cooper makes hias way to the ring. He looks pissed off as he comes down while they are wheeling Spikey Tiger to the back on a stretcher. It appears that Assault will be needing quite a few of these tonight. Dex steps over the top rope and the crowd cheers as he steps up to the second rope and poses for the audience. We then hear "California Love" by Tupac and Giovanni Mabius makes his way down the aisle. He receives the standard boos that Youthful Aggression has become accustomed to. Giovanni has a few words with Dex before he even steps onto the apron to get into the ring. The ref pushes Dex back to allow Giovanni entry to the ring. As Giovanni steps through the ropes, the bell sounds to get the festivities underway.
Ricky Simms - Northern Exposure and Youthful Aggression meet again. Henry Lesher - Yes, the young meet the stupid yet again and I find myself caring less with every match.
The crowd cheers as the two lock up in the center of the ring. Dex muscles Giovanni into the corner. The ref orders the break. Just as it appears to be a clean break, Dex knee lifts Giovanni and then shoulder blocks him twice in the corner. Dex follows this up with an inverted atomic drop. Dex then picks Giovanni up and gives him a running power slam and goes for the pin. 1...2... Mabius kicks out. Mabius is pulled to his feet by Dex who whips him to the corner. Dex then charges in shoulder first only to find no one home. Mabius rolls Dex up for a quick pin. 1...2... Dex kicks out and Mabius starts landing forearm smashes to the back of Cooper's neck. Mabius then stands Cooper up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Mabius then heads to the ropes, climbs to the second rope and then lands a well times elbow to the forehead of Dex Cooper from there before trying for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Cooper. Mabius then rolls Cooper out of the ring and follows him outside. Mabius starts pulling back the protective mats to expose the concrete as the fans in that area cheer the apparent coming violence. Mabius sets Cooper up for a piledriver. Cooper stands straight up, backdropping Mabius to the floor. Cooper then turns and smiles. He rolls into the ring and back out to break up the count. He then picks Mabius up and power bombs him on the concrete floor.
Ricky Simms - That was sick! Henry Lesher - That was more like it. Ricky Simms - Mabius' neck just bounced off the concrete. Henry Lesher - Like I said... Dex Cooper stands Mabius up and then rolls him into the ring. Cooper rolls himself into the ring right after and smiles as the crowd cheers. Dex grabs Mabius by the hair, rabbit punches him in the back of the head a few times, then picks him up and drops him with Tha Rabies Bite! He goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Dex Cooper with the win for Northern Exposure here this week.
Ricky Simms - Mabius could not recover from that power bomb on the floor. Henry Lesher - That is three matches for Northern Exposure tonight and they all appear to be getting meaner. They need that.
Backstage we see Lightning in the locker room by himself. Joe Davidson enters the picture and has to duck right away as Lightning throws a chair at him. Joe raises his hands in surrender.
Joe Davidson - Lightning! I simply want to know why you attacked FUBU out there tonight! Lightning - People want to know why? They think they can understand? Tell FUBU I will see him next week in Hell in the Cell!!!
Joe nods and retreats as Lightning picks up another chair. 6Pac Dudley vs The Moose The crowd cheers as "If You Want Blood" by AC/DC plays and The Moose makes his way to the ring. He's wearing an Enemies of State T-shirt and high fives fans wearing that same shirt as he hits ringside. He then rolls into the ring and waits for his opponent. "Southtown" by POD plays as 6Pac Dudley emerges at the top of the ramp and makes his way to the ring with a smile on his face. He makes his way up the steps and enters the ring without any interference from Moose. The bell sounds and the two lock up. 6Pac monkey flips Moose into the corner and then both men stand face to face again. Moose charges at 6Pac and 6Pac executes a drop toe hold on Moose. 6Pac turns this into the Crippler Crossface, but cannot hold tight and Moose reaches the ropes to force the break.
Ricky Simms - Big match here for 6Pac as he tries to move up the ladder. Henry Lesher - Hope the ladder falls on him. Ricky Simms - What a bucket of joy you are. Henry Lesher - They are paying me to be Lesher, not to be happy. Moose gets to his feet and takes a dropkicks from 6Pac. Moose nearly falls out of the ring, falling over the top rope. He holds onto the top rope with both hands and pulls himself back in. He turns, ducks a spinning heel kick from 6Pac and then spears him to the mat. Moose follows this up with a series of rights and lefts to the face of 6Pac and then hooks the leg for a pin. 1...2... 6Pac kicks out. Moose pulls 6Pac to his feet, whips him off the ropes and then picks him up and press slams him to the mat. Moose then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top as 6Pac struggles to his feet. 6Pac turns, sees Moose diving from the top. Moose misses with a double axe handle, instead getting caught in mid air and being Rock Bottomed to the mat. 6Pac goes for the pin. 1...2... Moose gets a foot on the bottom rope. 6Pac then stands Moose up, whips him off the ropes and misses with a clothesline. Moose gives 6Pac a roundhouse kick to the back and then gives him a reverse neckbreaker to the mat. Moose then stands 6Pac up and appears ready to deliver the Moosedriver. 6Pac lands an elbow to the abdomen on Moose to end those thoughts and then rakes Moose across the eyes. 6Pac delivers a standing dropkick which knocks Moose down and causes him to roll out of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Good instincts there by 6Pac to stay alive here. Henry Lesher - I hate his instincts. Moose gets to his feet on the floor as 6Pac slides out of the ring. 6Pac attemots to ram Moose's head into the steel corner post. Moose gets two hands up to block it. Moose then elbows 6Pac in the gut and then knife edge chops him to the chest. Moose follows this up with a series of headbutts before rolling 6Pac into the ring and then himself. Moose stands 6Pac up and goes for the Moosedriver. He lands it! 1...2... 6Pac kicks out! Moose cannot believe it and shakes his head, in denial that even happened. Moose stands 6Pac up and goes for another Moosedriver and lands it again! 1...2... 6Pac kicks out. Moose shouts at the ref who holds up two fingers and shrugs. Moose then stands 6Pac up one more time and delivers another Moosedriver! 1...2...3! Moose finally gets the win!
Ricky Simms - 6Pac kept his hopes alive through two Moosedrivers, but the third proved to be too much for him.
Henry Lesher - 6Pac needs to find new work. Backstage we see Night Dweller in a panic as Charley MacAdam enters the picture. Charley MacAdam - Night Dweller, tonight we saw a cowardly attack against the tag team champions. What are your thoughts on the matter?
Night Dweller - I am going to make certain that someone pays! Charley MacAadam - Any leads on who would have done this? Night Dweller - Nothing yet, but I swear when we know who is up to this, they'll pay! Night Dweller throws a chair which narrowly misses Charley's head as he ducks. Our interviewers are in the line of fire tonight.
Angela vs Dante "Know" by System of a Down plays as Dante makes his way to the ring to a chorus of boos from the masses. Dante is accompanied to the ring by Adam Lewis, the same Adam Lewis who clocked Scavenger on Breakthrough this week. Dante steps into the ring and Adam Lewis smiles as Dante pretends he cannot hear the boos and asks the crowd to grow louder. We then hear "The Sweet Leaf" by Black Sabbath and Ash leads Angela to the ring. Ash has a few words for Adam Lewis as he reaches ringside and the two start pushing and shoving as Angela enters the ring. Dante attacks Angela with a knee to the chest, then snap mares her by the hair to the middle of the ring. Dante then stands Angela up, whips her off the ropes and backdrops her to the mat as things get more physical between Ash and Adam at ringside.
Ricky Simms - The Eternal Nightmares are making more appearances here on Assault. Adam is apparently getting around.
Henry Lesher - Dante keeps quite the circle of friends. I am impressed as always. Dante gives Angela a backbreaker in the middle of the ring and goes for a quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Angela. Dante stands Angela up and whips her through the ropes where she collides with Ash. Dante then slides under the bottom rope and grabs Angela by the hair and whips her into the guard rail. Adam Lewis gets onto the guard rail and leaps off, landing a body press on Ash from there which bowls him over onto the floor. Dante then rolls Angela back into the ring as the ref warns Dante to watch his tactics or risk a DQ loss. Dante stands Angela up, whips her into the corner and attempts a superkick which Angela shoulder rolls away from. Angela then forearm smashes Dante in the face three times, whips him off the ropes and lands a roundhouse kick to the jaw of Dante. Angela then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Dante. At ringside, Ash reverses a whip by Adam Lewis and sends him head first into the steel corner post. Ash then picks Lewis up and gives him a German suplex on the floor. Ash gets to his feet and spits on Adam Lewis and applauds Angela's efforts inside the ring. Angela whips Dante into the corner. She then cartwheels and backflips an elbow into the chest of Dante before hip tossing him back to the center of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Great athletic ability shown here by Angela. Henry Lesher - Well, of course. However, she is no match for a man like Dante. Angela stands Dante up, whips him off the ropes and they double clothesline each other to the mat. Dante immediately shows signs of getting up as we hear Ash cheering Angela on. Angela rolls to the ropes and uses them to pull herself up as Dante gets to his feet. Dante charges. Angela steps aside and Dante charges right through the middle and top rope and lands hard against the guard railing. Adam Lewis staggers around the ring to attend to Dante as Ash follows Lewis. Lewis hears Ash coming and turns to deliver a haymaker only to have Ash duck it. Ash shoves Lewis and he staggers and falls over Dante. Angela heads outside the ring and DDTs Dante on the floor. She then rolls him into the ring and heads inside and to the second rope. Dante gets up, turns and takes a lariat from the second rope from Angela. Angela goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Dante. At ringside, Ash whips Adam Lewis into the steel ringsteps. Ash then stands him up. Lewis low blows Ash and then punches him in the throat. Lewis grabs a nearby chair and slams it across the head of Ash. Inside the ring, Angela stands Dante up, whips him into the corner and gives him a shoulder block there. Lewis gets up in the opposite corner and holds the chair in the corner buckle. Angela attempts a whip to the opposite corner. Dante reverses it. Angela manages to hit the corner and duck the chair all at once. Dante charges, Angela side steps and Dante's head hits the chair full force. Angela rolls Dante up for the pin. 1...2...3!
Henry Lesher - What kind of crap is that? Ricky Simms - Looks like Adam Lewis cost Dante the match there. Henry Lesher - This is a miscarriage of justice! Ash rolls into the ring and embraces Angela to celebrate the win. Adam Lewis curses at ringside, throwing the chair away. Ash then leads Angela out and the two head up the ramp while the Eternal Nightmares pick up the pieces inside the ring. Backstage we see Q with Jow Davidson.
Joe Davidson - Q, last week you won the world title. Your thoughts on the win? Q - Little man, it was a long time coming. Next up, Shelia steps into the ring and wins the Women's title. After that, we head for the tag titles. This roll is not going to stop.
Joe Davidson - No one has seen Dizz since he lost the title. Your thoughts on that? Q - I don't care. Dizz is nothing to me. He lost to me before and managed to keep the title because he's a coward. If he wants to play coward now and send others to do his dirty work, that's up to him.
Joe Davidson - What do you think of Max Cameron. Q - If this is the top contender I have to face, things are getting worse around here, not better.
Joe Davidson - He did fight in four matches last week. Q - And he won two and lost two. Tonight he fights one and loses won. A .500 record is good in some sports, but this isn't one of them. People don't beat me. That's why I am the World champ and he has some nothing belt.
Joe shrugs as Q leaves with that said and we head back to the ring. Shelia Lavin vs Demona Calaway vs Tasha Calaway "Harder Better Faster Stronger" by Daft Punk plays as Shelia Lavin makes her way to the ring. As she hits ringside, we see Tasha and Demona catfighting in the locker room. Shelia steps into the ring and appears to sigh as she shakes her head watching the battle going on before the fight even actually begins. Tasha drags Demona kicking and biting out of the locker room and drags her to the steps leading to the stage. Demona picks up a chair along the way and clubs Tasha with it.
Ricky Simms - This is highly unusual, seeing these two fight. Henry Lesher - Makes this match better for Shelia, though. I am sure she was worried about having to fight the two of them as a united force. Nice for her to see fighting between them.
Tasha and Demona punch and kick at each other until they reach ringside and then they roll into the ring and the bell sounds to get things underway. Shelia kicks Demona in the gut, but takes an eye gouge from Tasha. Tasha then picks Shelia up and gives her a tilt a whirl backbreaker. Tasha then grabs Shelia by the hair and takes a kick to the chest from Demona. Demona then whips Tasha off the ropes, leap frogs her and then monkey flips her as she comes back to the center of the ring. Demona waits for Tasha to get up and takes a chop block from behind by Shelia Lavin. Shelia then applies an ankle lock submission on Demona. Demona reaches for the ropes. Tasha then applies a sleeperhold on Shelia which Shelia immediately gets to her feet in and picks Tasha up and gives her a belly to back suplex.
Ricky Simms - Great action thus far between these ladies. Henry Lesher - It's been okay. It's not like Dante or Q or Damien are in there or anything.
Shelia attempts an ankle lock on Tasha, but Demona takes Shelia into a headlock and gives her a DDT and goes for the pin. 1...2... Tasha breaks it up by raking her nails down Demona's back. Tasha then attempts a DDT on Demona only to have Demona give her an Angle slam to the mat. Demona goes for the pin. 1...2... Shelia pulls Demona off Tasha. Tasha rolls onto her back and plants her feet in the abdomen of Shelia Lavin, driving her to the corner. Demona kips up to her feet, then does a hand stand and head scissors Shelia over and to the mat. Tasha gets to her feet, grabs ahold of Demona and places her in a headlock. Demona picks Tasha up and gives her an Angle slam to the mat and tries for the pin on Tasha. 1...2... Shelia pulls Demona off Tasha. Shelia picks Demona up and gives her a power bomb to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... Demona gets a foot on the bottom rope.
Ricky Simms - Lots of near falls here between these ladies. Henry Lesher - Near falls, whatever. Let's see some finish. Shelia pulls Demona to her feet, whips her off the ropes and then backdrops her to the mat. Tasha gets to her feet and spears Shelia to the mat. Tasha then picks Demona up and slams her onto Shelia. She then comes off the ropes and lands a rolling thunder on both fallen women. She pulls Demona off and goes for the pin on Shelia. 1...2... Shelia reverses it into a small package. 1...2... Tasha break it up! Tasha then picks Shelia up and gives her a power slam to the mat. 1...2... kick out by Shelia Lavin. Demona then trips Tasha up and rolls her up for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tasha as Shelia Lavin rolls out of the ring. Shelia picks up a steel chair and slides into the ring. She then slams it into the head of Tasha and the bell sounds. She slams it into the head of Demona and then slides out of the ring shaking her head. As Shelia makes her way up the ramp, Baby Calaway appears from under it and places Shelia Lavin in the Huggle as officials stream out of the back to break things up. Inside the ring, Demona and Tasha get to their feet and start battling each other again.
Ricky Simms - This is a mess. Henry Lesher - A mess that Shelia apparently became tired of and decided to cause the no contest.
Ricky Simms - That surprises no one, of course. Henry Lesher - It's Shelia's prerogative. Backstage we see Dante entering his office with an icepack against his head. His office is filled with cinder blocks. He turns and is face to face with Shannon Skanes.
Shannon Skanes - What the hell is going on, Dante? Can't you control the show? Dante - Shannon, I have no clue what the hell is going on or how these got here! Shannon Skanes - Then you have until the New Year to find out. If you don't know by then, someone else gets your job.
Dante - Then I need next week off from the ring. Shannon Skanes - Deal. I want this cinder block crap to stop. It has screwed up every event we have had for weeks now.
MLSQ42 vs Max Cameron "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC plays as Max Cameron makes his way to the ring and hears the boos of the crowd. He has a sign in his hand which proclaims him as the sole survivor of the stable match last week. The fans don't appreciate the sign any more than they appreciate him. He steps into the ring and tosses the sign into the crowd where it is quickly torn up. We then hear Tomoyasu Hotei's "Battle without Honor or Humanity" begin to play and the new world champion Q makes his way to the ring with a smile on his face and the world title over his shoulder.
Henry Lesher - Bravo, Q! You are the champion! Ricky Simms - Doc, sit down. Q steps over the top rope and shows the belt to Max Cameron. Q appears to be getting a larger face pop than Max Cameron right now which surprised many. The bell sounds and Q tosses the belt aside and starts going at it with Max Cameron toe to toe. Q whips Max off the ropes. Max shoulder blocks Q and neither man budges. Max shoulder blocks Q again and neither man budges. Max runs at Q again and is picked up for a fisherman's suplex to the mat. Q then elbows them in the head repeatedly and smiles as the blood runs down Max's face. Q then stands Max up and whips him over the top rope to the floor. Q then climbs to the top rope and waits for Max to get up. He leaps off the top rope and lands a knee to the face of Max. Max collapses at ringside and Q then stands over Max and declares that this match is over.
Henry Lesher - The man is a killer. He is a machine. Now that he has the world title, no one will stop him.
Ricky Simms - I am getting sick listening to this. Q stands Max up and whips him into the guard railing. Q then stands max up and kinfe edge chops him across the chest before rolling him into the ring. Q climbs onto the apron and then steps over the ropes and into the ring. Q stands Max up, whips him into the corner and charges right into a knee lift from max. Max then starts landing hard rights and lefts to the head of Q. Max kicks Q hard in the ribs and then gives him a DDT and rolls him over for the pin. 1...2... Q kicks out and thumbs Max in the throat. Q then quickly gets to his feet, grabs Max by the hair and stands him up. He then attempts to whip Max into the corner only to have Max reverse it and Q hits the corner hard. Max then gives Q a snap suplex.
Henry Lesher - Max is getting confident here. Poor kid. Ricky Simms - We have seen Max Cameron pull off some huge wins in the past few weeks, so there is no counting out the hardcore champion here.
Max stands Q up, whips him off the ropes and both men clothesline each other down. Max uses the ropes to pull himself to his feet first and then starts putting the boots to Q. Max then rolls Q out of the ring and heads out to where the mats were pulled back earlier revealing the concrete floor. Max sets Q up for a DDT only to have Q elbow Max in the side of the head. Q then picks Max up, spins him around and gives him a spinebuster onto the exposed concrete.
Henry Lesher - That would be the confidence flying right out the window. Ricky Simms - I must agree there. That was a nasty looking shot. Q stands Max up, then rolls him into the ring. Q again climbs onto the apron and then steps into the ring. Q looks to the crowd and raises his arm in the air. Max stumbles to his feet. Q grabs him for a choke slam. Max rakes the eyes of Q. Q kicks Max in the gut. Q then lands the Q Jumper and goes for the pin. 1...2... Max kicks out! Q looks shocked. Q stands Max up, whips him off the ropes and Max ducks a clothesline. Max then kicks Q in the back of the knees. Max then lands a Rock Bottom on Q and goes for the pin. 1...2... Q manages to kick out! Max stands Q up, whips him into the corner and charges in shoulder first only to find Q not there when he gets there. Q then lands another Q Jumper on Max and goes for the pin! 1...2...3! Q retains the belt!
Henry Lesher - Never a doubt in my mind. Ricky Simms - Yeah whatever. Max kicked out of Q's finisher and started fighting back and you were scared. I get the feeling Max Cameron is going to be a world champion someday.
Henry Lesher - If he ever does, I quit. Ricky Simms - Ref! Restart the match! Restart it now! Oh well, thus ends another edition of Assault. See you again next week!
