Choosing a Fed

Choosing a Fed There's nothing more frustrating as a player than bouncing around between a dozen feds trying to find your niche. There are a few that get it right on their first try, so here's ways to decide before applying where you might belong.
Take a good look at their roleplay boards. Some are pop up riddled and look awful. Others are clean and sharp. Take a look at how long the average roleplay is in the fed, what they seem to look for in champions and then decide whether or not you fit that mould.
Check out the OOC. Is there a truckload of fighting going on? Are they friendly to newcomers? Are the boards busy? Some feds have great roleplayers and a horrible out of character atmosphere that just ruins it.
Some feds allow roleplay attacks while most don't. If you want a more confrontational style fed which allows use of other people's characters, then check their rules. Rules are really quite important in both their content and quality. Some feds have only 507 simple rules while others have 20+.
Once you're registered for the boards, get to know the fed owner and some people on the roster. It's always a big help to you in being booked and getting angles across if people know who you are. Besides, feds are more fun when you're a part of the happenings.
Don't be on the hunt for main event status in week one. If you find a small fed where you're main event right away, be aware most of the feds close within the first month. Don't be discouraged. Sign up with a large fed and work your way up. You'll feel more pride when you get somewhere that way.
Decide what is more important, winning or wtoryline or both. There are feds that vote on results and those that run based on angles, so choose between them and decide what means more to you.
