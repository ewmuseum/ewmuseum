CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 7 June 2004

7 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempe, Arizona is the site of tonight's Assault. The crowd is anxious to see tonight's event. Backstage we see Halloween Jack entering the building and the crowd has a warm response to seeing him. He runs into Joe Davidson there.
Joe Davidson - Jack, welcome to Assault. Halloween Jack - Thanks, Joe, good to be here. Joe Davidson - Any plans for tonight? Halloween Jack - I have had a few offers, but I am keeping my intentions close to the vest. Just keep your eyes open.
Jack walks away and we head to ringside where the action is about to begin. Gothic Angel vs Micheal Leary "Lose Yourself" by Eminem plays as Shelia Lavin walks Micheal Leary to the ring. He doesn't appear to be in the best of moods tonight after the results he has been getting on recent Assaults. He steps through the ropes and looks around at the crowd briefly. We then hear "Headstrong" by Trapt play and Gothic Angel comes to the ring with KillSwitch close behind. Humanity Crisis is on the scene and the crowd don't sound very happy about it. Angel steps onto the apron and smiles at the booing crowd. She then steps through the ropes and the bell sounds to get this match started.
Henry Lesher - Great, KillSwitch is back. Ray Dees - I would figure he would be right up your alley. Henry Lesher - He would be, but he left. People I like leave. I have thus decided I no longer like anyone.
Leary and Angel lock up in the center of the ring. Micheal backs Gothic into the corner. The ref asks for a clean break. Micheal backs off. Gothic then knees Micheal in the gut followed by slamming his head into the corner buckle. Gothic then gives Micheal a side Russian leg sweep to the mat and goes for an early pin. 1...2... Micheal kicks out to keep the match going. Angel stands Micheal up, whips him off the ropes. Micheal then shoulder blocks Angel to the mat. Micheal elbows Angel on the mat. Micheal then lands another elbow on Angel and then places her in a side headlock. Angel battles to the ropes to force a break of the headlock. Micheal backs off and Angel lands a dropkick on him. Micheal then takes a snap mare from Gothic Angel.
Ray Dees - Going both ways here. Henry Lesher - Yeah I am alternating between bored and asleep myself. Angel places Micheal into a sleeperhold. Micheal responds with an elbow to the gut of Angel, then gives her a sidewalk slam to the mat. Micheal goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gothic Angel. Micheal then stands Angel up and whips her off the ropes, then clotheslines her to the mat. KillSwitch starts to move towards Shelia. Shelia has a few words for KillSwitch and Micheal moves to the ropes and has a few words for KillSwitch himself. Gothic Angel rolls Micheal up, hooking the tights. 1...2...3! Gothic Angel pulls off the win over Micheal Leary!
Ray Dees - That's a big win for Angel. Henry Lesher - And a big win for people trying to sleep. Backstage we see Chastity with Corina and Ophelia in the locker room. Charley MacAdam enters the shot.
Charley MacAdam - No one gave you a chance, but we have seen your work this week and the laughing appears to have stopped for the most part.
Chastity - I have said all along we were more than pretty faces and now we are proving that.
Charley MacAdam - Tonight you have a chance to prove it against none other than the world champ.
Chastity - I am going to make certain the rest of the CWA takes us serious. Charley MacAdam - Good luck. Chastity - Hopefully I don't need it. Gerard Angelo vs Marcus Cameron "Dammit" by Blink 182 plays as Gerard Angelo makes his way to the ring. He receives a reaction from the crowd that tells him they don't much appreciate his work. He has a few words for the fans on his way down the ramp and then steps through the ropes into the ring and awaits Marcus Cameron. His wait doesn't last long as "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC plays and Marcus makes his way to the ring to the cheers of the crowd. Marcus slides into the ring and he and Gerard start exchanging rights and lefts as the bell sounds to get the match underway. Angelo backs Marcus into the corner. Angelo then shoulder blocks Marcus, but Marcus responds with a quick knee lift to the face of Angelo. Marcus then clotheslines Gerard to the mat, climbs to the second rope and then misses with a knee drop from there as Gerard moves out of the way.
Henry Lesher - This is two straight matches I haven't cared about and I hear a stripper is getting a world title shot tonight. The world is coming to an end.
Ray Dees - Keep a happy thought there, Doc. Gerard uses the ropes to get to his feet. He then puts the boots to Marcus before he has a chance to get back up. Gerard then stands Marcus up, whips him into the corner and then gives him a fireman's carry out and then drops him to the mat. Gerard two handed chokes Marcus on the mat. Gerard waits until the full four count before releasing the choke as the ref warns him against that. Angelo then rolls Marcus out of the ring. Angelo follows Marcus out, then grabs Marcus by the hair and rams him head first into the guard railing as the fans in the area cheer Marcus on. Gerard then rolls Marcus into the ring, then Angelo heads to the ropes and heads to the top. Marcus gets to his feet as Angelo hits the top rope. Angelo lands a flying body press on Marcus and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Marcus Cameron.
Henry Lesher - Why must these matches drag on like this? Ray Dees - You're melodramatic. Angelo starts to stand Marcus up. Marcus lands a jawbreaker on Gerard Angelo. Marcus then picks Angelo up and power slams him to the mat. Marcus follows this up with a leg drop to the throat of Angelo. Marcus then tries for the pin. 1...2... reversal into a small package by Angelo. 1...2... kick out by Marcus. Marcus rolls himself out of the ring. Angelo quickly follows Marcus out. Angelo takes an eye rake from Marcus Cameron. Angelo then headbutts Marcus Cameron. Gerard then picks Marcus up and gives him a body slam on the floor before Gerard rolls himself into the ring. The ref continues to count as Marcus gets to his feet. The ref reaches the full ten count and Gerard raises his hands in victory as the fans boo this decision.
Ray Dees - Angelo picks up the win. Henry Lesher - And I gather more indifference from it. Backstage we see Ash entering the building with Angela close behind. They are headed off by Samantha Johnson.
Samantha Johnson - You two appear to be determined for something. Ash - We have business to deal with. Samantha Johnson - With? Ash - You will see. Samantha backs off as Ash and Angela blow past her. It must be bad if Samantha is backing off from her own sister.
Brother Bubble vs Notorious BLC "Hymn of Joy" plays as Brother Bubble makes his way to the ring with the crowd booing him the whole way in. Brother Bubble seems oblivious to this as he spouts off to the camera about commandments and things of this nature. He then steps onto the apron and through the ropes. We hear "Wedge" by Hollow and Notorious BLC makes his way to the ring. He gets a strong positive reaction from the crowd. BLC enters the ring on a dead run and the bell sounds as the two start brawling in the middle of the ring.
Ray Dees - Another matches starting fast here. Henry Lesher - These wrestlers are as anxious to end their matches as I am to see them end.
BLC stands landing a series of knee lifts to the gut of Bubble, then whips him hard into the corner and then hip tosses him out of it. BLC then places Bubble into an arm bar. Bubble reverses it, then lands a knife edge chop on BLC. Bubble then dropkicks BLC to the mat. Bubble heads for the ropes, climbs to the second rope and lands an axe handle smash to the back of BLC's neck. Bubble stands BLC up, whips him off the ropes and BLC ducks a clothesline and lands a reverse neckbreaker on Bubble and then goes for the pin. 1...2... Bubble kicks out. BLC stands Bubble up, whips him into the corner and then places a boot to the throat of Bubble. We then see The Dawg coming out from the back and the crowd has a strong reaction to him. They apparently are cheering on BLC more than they are cheering him on here.
Henry Lesher - Great, now the crap of Chaos is spilling over onto the crap of Assault. Ray Dees - It's craptacular! BLC breaks the boot choke and takes a thumb to the eye from Bubble. Bubble then chokes BLC across the top rope as the ref warns that he is about to DQ the next person who cheats. Bubble then releases BLC from the choke and gives him a punch in the throat, then clotheslines him to the mat. Bubble then sets BLC up for a figure four leglock. BLC kicks Bubble in the back, sending him into the corner where he hits his shoulder. BLC kips up to his feet, then sees the Dawg at ringside. BLC slides out of the ring under the bottom rope. He starts having wirds with The Dawg. Dawg's verbal match against BLC doesn't get far as Rayn appears from the back and attacks Dawg with a chair as Brother Bubble rolls himself out of the ring and starts trading punches with BLC.
Henry Lesher - An orgy of crappy fighters before us. It's a crappgy. Ray Dees - I like that term. I had better write that down. As the four battle outside the ring, the ref continues to count and reaches a ten count and both BLC and Brother Bubble are counted out as security makes their way out from the back.
Ray Dees - Sunstroke stands only a couple of weeks off and these guys are getting ready early.
Henry Lesher - Like people will pay to watch them. They're filler, washroom break material.
Backstage we see TJ Mercer entering the building and the crowd cheers. He is headed off by Vesuvius.
Vesuvius - TJ, we talked and you have come to a decision, right? TJ Mercer - I have come to a decision, yeah. Vesuvius - And? TJ Mercer - It's on. TJ moves on and Vesuvius smiles. We then head back to the ring. Wish vs Pyro "Nothing Left To Lose" by Puddle of Mudd plays as Wish makes his way to the ring. He receives a chorus of boos as he makes his way down the ramp and lights flash to signal his entrance. Wish slips inside the ring and poses for the crowd, then gives one fan the finger in the front row. We then hear "When Darkness Falls" by KillSwitch Engage as Pyro makes his way to the ring to the cheers of the crowd. Pyro high fives fans on his way down to the ring, then has a few words for KillSwitch towards the camera before he climbs the steps and enters the ring. The bell sounds and Pyro locks up with Wish.
Henry Lesher - It doesn't get better. I keep wanting it to. Ray Dees - Yeah, but hotness in the forms of Angela and Chastity are waiting for us. Henry Lesher - That doesn't increase my will to live either. Pyro backs Wish into the ropes, whips him to the corner and then shoulder blocks him in the back. Pyro then gives Wish an atomic drop before tripping him down to the mat. Pyro climbs in the corner to the top rope. Pyro waits for Wish to face him. Wish instead dives for the ropes and Pyro lands hard on the top rope in the splits position. Wish then snap mares Pyro off the top rope to the mat. Wish then climbs to the second rope and delivers a scissor kick to the back of Pyro's neck from there. Wish then rolls Pyro up for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Pyro. Wish then pulls Pyro to his feet, whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him to the mat. Wish poses for the crowd and Pyro kips up to his feet. Pyro then round house kicks Wish in the back of the head, knocking him through the ropes to the floor.
Ray Dees - Wish got overconfident. Henry Lesher - Showing up for the match makes him overconfident. Pyro heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. He waits for Wishg to turn and this time lands a missile dropkick to the chin of Wish. Pyro then lands a series of punches to the head of Wish before standing him up. He starts to throw Wish head first into the ring steps. Wish instead plants his feet and tosses Pyro shoulder first into the ring steps. Wish then rolls Pyro into the ring before entering the ring himself, avoiding this match becoming the latest of tonight's count out parade. Wish places Pyro into a sleeperhold. Pyro battles to free himself from it as the ref checks for a submission. He lifts Pyro's arm. It drops once. He lifts Pyro's arm again. Pyro keeps the arm up and manages to elbow Wish in the gut. Pyro lands another elbvow as he gets to his feet. He then lands a third elbow and then hip tosses Wish to the mat.
Ray Dees - Pyro fighting back here. Henry Lesher - Prolonging my agony. I hope they're happy. Pyro picks Wish up, then gives him a backbreaker. Pyro comes off the ropes and lands a rolling thunder on Wish and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Wish. Pyro shakes his head as he stands Wish up, then whips him into the corner buckle. Wish stumbles out and takes The Burnout from Pyro who then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Pyro gets the win here over Wish! The lights then go out. The crowd is silent as we hear a thud inside the ring. The lights then come back up and KillSwitch is smiling as he stands over Pyro. KillSwitch then leaves the ring as the crowd boos him.
Ray Dees - KillSwitch making his presence felt here. Henry Lesher - That was something better than what we have seen thus far tonight. Backstage we see Harmony Fallacia and Vesuvius in the locker room. Harmony has her arm around the neck of Vesuvius when TJ enters the picture with a bag slung over his shoulder.
Harmony Fallacia - Cool, so you're coming out? TJ Mercer - I am getting nowhere. Perhaps a tripto the Compound could help me. Vesuvius- Welcome aboard, TJ. *shakes hands with Mercer* We'll get you on the map yet. TJ Mercer - We're going now, then? Harmony Fallacia - Yeah, my opponent didn't show. Off we go to the nearby compound. Vesuvius - Wish they held events in Arizona more often. Lone Star vs Tommy Air "Lay Low Remix" by Snoop Dogg plays as Lone Star makes his way out to a huge reaction from the crowd. He takes his time, high fiving fans on the way to the ring. He then steps up onto the apron and through the ropes. We then hear "Smells Like Teen Spirit" by Nirvana and Tommy Air comes out to just as huge of an ovation. Tommy holds his Superior Title high and tells the camera that he is the champ now and no one is taking this away from him. Tommy slides into the ring and Lone Star is putting the boots to him right away. Lone Star kicks the belt aside, pulls Tommy to his feet and whips him hard into the corner buckle.
Ray Dees - Superior title on the line here. Henry Lesher - But what are these two superior to? Lone Star places Tommy into an abdomenal stretch. Lone Star holds on as the ref asks Tommy if he submits. Tommy shakes his head and flat out refuses to submit here. Tommy reaches for the ropes as Lone Star tries to strengthen the hold here. The fans appear to be torn between the two here. Tommy finds the energy to start powering out of it, but Lone Star simply adjusts and the hold is as strong as ever. However, Tommy lunges and manages to grab the rope which forces the break. Lone Star appears to be upset as the ref pulls him off of Tommy and Lone Star gives the ref a shove. The ref threatens Lone Star with a possible disqualification. Lone Star just laughs and turns his attention back to Tommy Air. Tommy dropkicks Lone Star to the mat. Tommy then springs back up and monkey flips Lone Star into the corner.
Ray Dees - Tommy looked good there. Henry Lesher - Tommy Air is, in my mind, the luckiest CWA wrestler of 2004 thus far. He is either that or paying people handsomely.
Tommy headlocks Lone Star and then delivers a springboard bulldog on Lone Star and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Lone Star. Tommy then stands Lone Star up and whips him off the ropes. Tommy leap frogs Lone Star. Lone Star stops in his tracks, turns and rabbit punches Tommy in the back of the head. Lone Star then gives Tommy a German suplex to the mat. Lone Star then holds Tommy there for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Tommy Air to keep his title. We then see Ash coming out from the back with a chair in hand and the fans boo him. Lone Star stands Tommy up, whips him into the corner and gives him a round house kick as he comes out of the corner. Lone Star then notices Ash and has a few words for him and how he is next.
Ray Dees - Wonder what Ash is out here for? Henry Lesher - Probably to wish them both well. Tommy gets to his feet behind Lone Star. Lone Star turns and takes a shoulder tackle to the mat from Tommy. Lone Star then takes a series of punches to the head from Tommy on the mat. Lone Star reverses it, rolling on top of Tommy and landing a series of punches on the defending champ. Ash takes a seat at ringside, sitting on the steel folding chair he brought with him. As Ash sits down, Shannon Skanes appears on stage and the crowd goes wild. Lone Star stands Tommy up, whips him into the corner and tries for a shoulder block. Tommy sidesteps it and Lone Star hits the corner hard. Tommy rolls Lone Star up for the pin. 1...2...3! Tommy Air with the pin gains the victory! Tommy rolls out of the ring and the ref hands the belt to Tommy. As Tommy makes his way out, Ash slides into the ring on one side and Shannon slides in on the other.
Ray Dees - All hell is about to break loose. Henry Lesher - Yeah, this should be fun. Ash and Shannon look at each other. This is the first time these two have been in the ring together since the entire mess with the State and EOS. Ash has the chair in hand. Ash rears back and Lone Star manages to block it. Lone Star then kicks Ash in the gut. Shannon then chop blocks Lone Star down and Ash crowns him with the chair! Ash then picks Lone Star up and gives him a tombstone piledriver onto the chair as Shannon stands in the corner and nods. Shannon grabs the microphone.
Shannon Skanes - Lone Star, there comes a time in every man's life when they need to re-evaluate their career. That time for you is now. You;re fired!
Shannon leaves the ring as Ash puts the boots to the out cold Lone Star. Ash then signals to the crowd that Flock is forever and he is now getting a mixed reaction.
Ray Dees - What the hell was he fired for? Henry Lesher - For being Lone Star? Who cares? The guy pissed me off anyways. Backstage we see Greg Anderson and Steve Lang with Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - The so called perfect team here tonight and we wonder why. Steve Lang - You know, the Sensational Seven are ready to take over the show. This week, we are watching. I can tell you, though, that this is a big night what with the firing of Lone Star there and the title match for a stripper. Big night.
Joe Davidson - Anyone in particular you guys want in the ring? Greg Anderson - No, we'll beat everybody. Angela vs Lethal Weapon "I Stand Alone" by Godsmack plays as Lethal Weapon comes out to a huge ovation. Weapon steps onto the apron as the pyro shoots off in every direction. Weapon then steps through the ropes and steps up onto the second rope and raises his arms to the crowd. "Sweet Leaf" by Black Sabbath plays as Angela comes out. She is holding up a sign that expresses her unhappiness with the suspensions of Frank and Wren. Angela then drops the sign at ringside and steps up onto the apron, regarding Weapon with caution as she looks inside the ring. She tells him this fight is for Flock. Lethal tells her to get the flock into the ring.
Ray Dees - Angela taking the Flock on her back here. Henry Lesher - They say they run this show. You know, if I was on this show, I wouldn't tell people that.
Angela enters the ring and the bell sounds to get this match started. They lock up and the crowd cheers as Lethal throws her hard into the corner. Lethal then steps straight into a thumb to the eye from Angela. Angela then rakes her nails down the back of Weapon, then gives him a reverse neckbreaker. Angela goes for an early pin. 1...2... kick out by Lethal Weapon. Angela stands Weapon up and tries to whip him off the ropes, but Weapon reverses it. Weapon then clotheslines Angela to the mat. Weapon then knee drops Angela to the forehead and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Angela to stay alive in this match. Weapon stands Angela up and whips her into the corner. He then gives her an inverted atomic drop and a knife edge chop after that which knocks her back into the corner and she slumps down there.
Ray Dees - Lethal Weapon really taking over here. Henry Lesher - Well, he is a lot stronger than her. Lethal Weapon grabs Angela by the throat and pulls her to her feet. Angela then rakes the eyes of Lethal Weapon with her nails and follows that up by drilling a kick into the kneecap of Lethal. Angela drills another kick to the other knee cap and Lethal Weapon falls. Angela then kicks Lethal in the face, following that up with an attempt at a Boston Crab which she cinches in nicely as the crowd starts a chant for Weapon here. Weapon reaches for the ropes, but his knees are clearly in agonizing pain. He resists the urge to tap out as the ref checks for that. Lethal then finds the strength to start pushing himself up from the mat. He pushes off, weakening the hold Angela has on him. Sensing she is losing grip, Angela releases Weapon entirely and starts putting the boots to him.
Ray Dees - She just couldn't hold him. Henry Lesher - He is tough, I will give him that... well, against women. Angela stands Weapon up, whips him off the ropes and Weapon clotheslines her to the mat for her trouble. Weapon then stands her up, whips her into the corner. He charges into the corner for a spear which Angela dodges, then rolls Weapon up for the pin. 1...2...3! The bell sounds.
Ray Dees - Angela won. Henry Lesher - Weapon won. The replay shows that both sets of shoulders were pinned to the mat and it is announced we have ourselves a draw. Angela escapes the ring and Lethal Weapon looks quite unimpressed, grabbing the microphone from the ring announcer.
Lethal Weapon - I am tired of fighting little girls. I came to Assault to fight one man and he knows who he is. Ash, if you have any guts at all you'll face me next week. Then, we'll see just how tough you are. Angela, enjoy the fact you stepped into the ring with me and lived to tell about it. You may not be so lucky next time.
Lethal drops the microphone and heads out of the ring, "I Stand Alone" blaring throughout the arena.
Ray Dees - There's an ultimatum. Henry Lesher - At least he is as tired of fighting girls as I am of watching him fight them.
Camera goes backstage. KillSwitch and Ash are in the Flock locker room. KillSwitch: So, as I was saying, you brought me to CWA. Ash: Yes, I did. KillSwitch: Now that I am back, I want things square between us, being that we will be Flock's new competition and I will one day be taking that very title, I figure I should give you one last wish.
Ash: Wish, how about favor? KillSwitch: Indeed. Ash: You want my title? Ash gets in KillSwitch's face. KillSwitch: Relax, I have quite the journey to it, we won't be enemies but I do have my own plans holding that title. And Gothic Angel will be wanting Angela's title, I just don't want to be like everyone that's ever dealt with you and betray you.
Ash: I have a favor in mind. KillSwitch: Excellent. We then see Halloween Jack making his way from the stage to the ramp and walks down to the ring, and grabs a mic, the same one Lehtla Weapon just dropped.
Henry Lesher - God, not another speech. “Well here it is Jack’s new home Assault and I guess I should thank Gloria for sending me here, for sending me to the illustrious show, the show where opportunity lies around every corner.”
Jack nods to the crowd briefly before his face twists into an expression of rage, “Yeah thanks, Ms Lavin, thank you for making me the joke of the CWA, all week I as labelled the clown of Chaos, the outsider who had no right to be in that ring with two greats such as Vigilante and Myst…”
Jack pauses to spit at the utterance of those names. “Only to be deigned by an interference from Leech and now, now everyone is laughing at me, do you think I can’t hear them? Jack couldn’t handle Chaos what chance does he have here. You’ve made me a laughing stock Lavin.”
Pacing the ring Jack looks at the crowd before speaking quietly into the mic. “I am no joke, I am Halloween Jack. You all laugh now but I will play with the hand fate has dealt me,”
I Am Hell by White Zombie hits the speakers and out steps Gothic Angel to KillSwitch's music.
Ray Dees: Could this be Ash's favor, beating Halloween Jack? Gothic Angel stops atop the ramp.
Gothic Angel: So you are here on Assault, I bet you're wondering who specially requested you.
Halloween: I was. Gothic Angel: It was this man. KillSwitch enters through the curtain dragging Lone Star by his left foot. They walk down the ramp, KillSwitch still dragging Lone Star. KillSwitch lifts Lone Star and throws him into the ring. Gothic Angel steps into the ring, followed by KillSwitch. All four in the ring now.
KillSwitch: You have been selected to join a faction, the faction of darkness, will you rejoice and bask in the dark night air. Think of it this way, this man turned down the Flock and he ran into the wall...13 times. Do you understand?
Halloween Jack doesn't budge. KillSwitch gets frustrated further, Gothic Angel and KillSwitch lift Lone Star draping his arms over the ropes in the corner. Gothic Angel slips out and grabs the ring bell and slides back in. Gothic Angel runs from one side of the ring to Lone Star's corner running the chair into his ribs. A trickle of blood flows from his mouth. KillSwitch takes the chair and smashes at his knees.
KillSwitch: Would you like to be next? Before Halloween Jack enters, backstage we see Ash laughing at the sight of Lone Star's current situation. Halloween Jack looks at Lone Star, and then to KillSwitch with a look of disgust. Halloween Jack pushes KillSwitch, he drops the chair, Halloween Jack picks it up and lunges to KillSwitch, Gothic Angel stands in his way. Lone Star tries to lift up but he gets crowned by the chair swung by Halloween Jack. Lone Star collapes, bleeding his crimson mask. They stand united in, standing in Lone Star's blood. They lights go out. When they come back, Humanity Crisis is written in Lone Star's blood, next to his head. Gothic Angel, KillSwitch, and Halloween Jack are gone.
Ray Dees - This is way over the top. That's just wrong. Backstage we see Shelia Lavin watching this with Shannon Skanes in the background. Shannon Skanes - I fired the guy and that's it. Humanity Crisis just took things a step too far.
Shelia Lavin - They appear to excel at that. Shannon Skanes - Get them under control. Shelia Lavin - Thanks for picking me for that job. Chastity vs Ash "Naked Cousin" by PJ Harvey plays as Chastity makes her way to the ring with her arms raised and to the cheers of the crowd. Chastity signs an autograph for a fan and continues on her way to the ring. She steps up onto the apron and bows to the cheering fans before stepping inside the ring.
Ray Dees - This babe has made a believer out of me. She has been working out really hard. Henry Lesher - Oh, God, save me. Look, you have no shot, had no shot and neither does she in this match. Wake up.
"The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson plays and Ash comes out with the world title high above his head and tells the fans that his people have taken over the show and then heads down the ramp to the ring where Chastity doesn't look like she is backing down in the slightest. Ash jumps up onto the apron, shows her the belt and says she is not getting it. Chastity responds by dropkicking Ash off the apron to the floor and the crowd explodes. Ash drops the belt on the floor and is in a rage. He slides into the ring and the bell sounds to get the match started. Ash starts landing rights and lefts, but Chastity blocks a series of them and thumbs Ash in the eye, then lands a series of knee lifts on the stunned world champion. She tries for a hip toss, but Ash kicks her in the back and then gives her a side Russian leg sweep.
Henry Lesher - And the Powerpuff girl has lost her steam. Ash picks Chastity up and body slams her to the mat. Ash then pulls her over to the corner and climbs to the second rope, then lands a knee to the chest of Chastity and goes for the pin. 1...2... Chastity manages to kick out. The crowd starts a chant for her and we see Corina and Izarra coming out from the back to cheer Chastity on here. They are half way down the ramp when we see Gothic Angel attack them from behind and she drills Izarra with a clothesline from behind, then bulldogs Corina to the steel rampway. Inside the ring, Ash stands Chastity up and smiles at her. Chastity responds by spitting in Ash's face. Ash attempts to slap her across the face. Chastity ducks that, grabs the arm of Ash and tripes him to the mat, applying a Crippler Crossafec on him.
Ray Dees - Go girl go. Henry Lesher - This is sad. At least those other twits were taken care of. Chastity strengthens her hold on Ash. Ash reaches for the ropes as Gothic Angel takes her place at ringside. Ash lunges to the ropes and manages to grab the bottom rope to force the break. Chastity lets Ash up. Ash spears Chastity to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Chastity. Ash then stands Chastity up, whips her off the ropes and Chastity manages to hang on to the top rope and Ash clotheslines nothing. Ash smiles, nods and clotheslines Chastity over the top rope to the floor. He then backs off as the ref warns him about his actions. Gothic Angel headlocks Chastity and DDTs her on the floor before rolling her back inside the ring. Ash then stands Chastity up and gives her a Pipe Cashing to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Ash retains the title.
Henry Lesher - Flawless victory over cheese by Flock. Ray Dees - I like cheese. Henry Lesher - You should. You're a rat. Ray Dees - Well, that does it for us. Thanks for joining us for another stellar edition of Assault.
Henry Lesher - I disagree. Ash is seen celebrating over the out cold Chastity as Gothic Angel steps through the ropes and tells the fans that is what happens to the other women in the CWA when she is around. We fade to black off of that.
