CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 5 April 2004

5 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Halifax, Nova Scotia is the site of another packed house for the CWA as we move away from Terror in the Aisles and towards Outrage. The crowd cheers as the opening credits roll and we see images of EOS, The State and then of Dark Forces. We then head to ringside where the action is all set to get underway.
Gerard Angelo vs Shengee Shmelky "Step Up" by Drowning Pool plays as Gerard Angelo makes his way to the ring. The crowd boos him in his Assault debut as he battles to prove his worth on this show even while rumors abound backstage. Angelo steps into the ring shouting at the fans and then at the ref. His music fades and "Tiny Rubberband" by The Butthole Surfers plays as Shengee Shmelky emerges from the back, led by Adam West. On his way to the ring, Shengee declares his love for Baby Calaway.
Henry Lesher - Dear God, make this quick or I will be forced to open fire. Ricky Simms - I think it's nice Shengee is pursuing the girl. Henry Lesher - That's because you aren't a man. Shengee steps into the ring and the bell sounds to get this match underway. Shengee charges straight into a clothesline from Angelo. Angelo then leg drops Shengee and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Gerard Angelo has won a match in record time on Assault! He shakes his head. He grabs the microphone from ring announcer, Lacy Brown.
Gerard Angelo - I thought I was here to fight a wrestler! This is garbage! Next week I had better get a challenge in this ring!
Gerard then throws the microphone down on the head of Shengee before making his way out of the ring as the fans continue to boo him. We then head backstage where Vigilante is on a cell phone with Joe Davidson there.
Vigilante - Rock? Rock? No, the Rock can't act. Walking Tall? He would have done better with a remake of Gigli. Yeah... yeah, I expect Warner Brothers to get back to me today. K... talk to you later. *hangs up the phone*
Joe Davidson - Vig, if we could have a moment of your time. Vigilante - You know, being a future Hollywood star isn't easy with people up in my business all the time.
Joe Davidson - You're a future Hollywood star? Vigilante - Joe, were you not just listening to me? Hollywood has taken Hulk Hogan, The Rock and even Bill Goldberg on, but now they are taking a true star in me.
Joe Davidson - Anything to say about your match tonight? Vigilante - Yeah, Flock by any other name is still Flock. Vig then leaves Joe standing there. Joe shrugs with a nervous smile on his face before we return to ringside.
Nacho Mauma vs Pyro "Anything Goes" by Guns N Roses plays as Nacho Mauma makes his way to the ring from the back. The fans cheer him on even as he has a few things to say to the camera about Vigilante and then about Max Cameron. Nacho then jumps up onto the apron and poses for the crowd before stepping through the ropes into the ring. Nacho then tests the ropes as we hear "Immortal" by Adema and Pyro makes his way out from the back to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Pyro hits the ring on a dead run and the two start battling tooth and nail as the bell sounds to officially get this match started. Nacho gets the early advantage, whipping Pyro off the ropes and then backdropping him to the mat.
Ricky Simms - These two anxious to get things underway here. Henry Lesher - Yeah I am glad, because I am anxious to see this match overwith. Ricky Simms - Nacho is telling anyone who will listen about his win over Vigilante at Terror in the Aisles last week when he pinned him in that huge King of the Pay Per View match.
Henry Lesher - It's called a fluke win. Has no one explained it to him or does that mask not let the oxygen in?
Nacho stands Pyro up and whips him threough the ropes to the floor. Nacho then lands a la plancha through the ropes on Pyro when he does get back to his feet. Nacho then stands Pyro up and attempts to whip him into the steel ringsteps only to have Pyro reverse it. Pyro whips Nacho hard into the ring steps and the crowd in that area loves it as Nacho's shoulder crashes into the steps hard. Pyro then rams Nacho's head into the steps before rolling into the ring to break up the count. Pyro rolls back out with a sadistic smile on his face. Nacho slowly gets to his feet and then takes a series of kicks to the chest from Pyro. Pyro tries to whip Nacho into the steel corner post of the ring, but Nacho reverses it and whips Pyro into that same steel post. Nacho then rolls himself into the ring to break up the count before he rolls himself back out. Nacho then picks Pyro up and drops him over the guard railing.
Ricky Simms - Brutal action outside the ring here. Henry Lesher - About time we got something happening. Nacho rolls Pyro into the ring before rolling himself inside. Nacho then stands Pyro up and takes a thumb in the eye. Pyro then whips Nacho off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. He then stands Nacho up. Nacho elbows Pyro in the head. Nacho then delivers the You'd Rather Be Dead and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Nacho Mauma pulls off the win here this week over Pyro!
Ricky Simms - Big win for Nacho here. Henry Lesher - It's debatable how big a win it is. Backstage we see Shannon Skanes pacing in his dressing room. The crowd cheers, but those cheers change to boos when they see Myst enter the picture.
Myst - How's your head? Shannon Skanes - It's better now. Coming back to do more damage? Myst - Loser. She leaves and Shannon shakes his head, at a loss for words towards his wife at that moment.
"Lay Low Remix" starts playing on the PA system. The lights go off for a moment as the crowd goes wild.
Ricky Simms - What is going on in here? Henry Lesher - It looks like we lost the power in the arena. Good, let's go home. A couple of seconds later, the lights turn back on. Lone Star is standing on the stage. He is wearing silver baggy pants and light blue BLPW logo t-shirt. Crowd starts giving him a mixed reaction.
Ricky Simms - It's Lone Star! Henry Lesher - But why is he wearing BLPW t-shirt? This is CWA! Maybe he came to save me from this rabble!
Lone Star starts rising his hands up to show a ghetto-bird sign to the crowd, but then stops. He points at the t-shirt and takes it off. He is wearing a black t-shirt underneath it.. "Lone Star is CWA" is written in the red bold letters on the front, and a ghetto bird signature picture is drawn on the back with ghetto, Inc written underneath it. The crowd starts cheering wildly.
Henry Lesher - That's it. There's no hope for me ever getting away from you. Ricky Simms - Could this mean ... Lone Star takes the BLPW t-shirt and spits in it. Then, he starts wiping his shoes with the t-shirt before throwing it away. He rises his arms up and salutes the crowd with the ghetto bird sign before walking down the entrance ramp and entering the ring. One of the ringside workers tosses the mic to Lone Star. Crowd quiets down for a moment.
Lone Star - C ... W ... A, let me holler at ya! Crowd cheers again. Lone Star - By this point, most of you, my loyal fans, you're probably wondering what the hell is the most entertaining man in the badlands doing in CWA.
Henry Lesher - Yeah, tell them and shut them up! Lone Star - It is very simple. A man like me, he goes where the success is. He follows the people. And people, they have 3 choices on their hands these days. First choice is WWE. And hell, nobody wants to watch that. These days, Vince McMahon had turned WWE into a god damn soap opera. On the other hand, there is BLPW. And I used to work for them for quite a while. But you know, lately, Badlands Pro-Wrestling turned into a second league promotion. There is always something that is keeping it down. It might be that asshole, JC Michaels. It might be a lack of talent ... Who knows. But the bottom line is, BLPW can't measure up. That is why people had turned to the third, and the best choice, CWA. CWA is a place where all the superstars are treated equally. CWA is a place where superstars get the opportunity according to how good they are, not who they know.
Ricky Simms - He's got that right. Lone Star pauses for a moment. He takes a couple of steps around as the crowd still cheers.
Henry Lesher - ... He still didn't tell us why is here. Lone Star - You know ... About 10 days ago, while doctors were trying to put my body back together after that brutal match I went through with Headhunter, I received a phone call from none other than Mr. Shannon Skanes. He said that he felt bad about how BLPW had mistreated me. He said that if I decide to give him a chance, there is a CWA contract waiting for me. So I was left with 2 option. Option number 1: Staying in BLPW and facing more injustice, or Option number 2: Coming to CWA, and showing everybody what being the most entertaining man is all about. So hell, I took a little trip to CWA executive office, and the rest is history. Now, the only thing that matters is, starting tonight, LONE STAR IS CWA!
Suddenly, Hardcore State of Mind starts playing on the PA system as Ash, Angela, Sinman, Wren and Frank Merritt walk out to the stage. The crowd starts booing. The flock start moving down towards the ring in the group.
Henry Lesher - What the hell is flock doing here? Ricky Simms - Beats me. Flock enters the ring. They start slowly surrounding Lone Star. Ash asks for a mic and gets one from the ringside.
Ash - Lone Star, buddy ... How's it going? I see that you finally came to CWA. Lone Star nods his head. Lone Star - Yeah, I did finally come to CWA. So you and your flock buddies came out here ... why?
Ash points at Lone Star and then laughs for a moment. Ash - Common man, why are you so impatient. We came out here to welcome you into the CWA, and also, we have a very interesting proposition for you. I know how good you are. You are one of the most impressive young athletes in wrestling today. But you're not a real superstar. Every time you tried to reach the top, you choked. And you know why's that? It's because you had nobody backing you up. You had nobody you could depend on. If you join the flock, you will have somebody who can hold your back. You will have friends at the powerful places, and you will be respected because CWA fears The Flock. So, what do you say?
Ricky Sims - Flock is trying to recruit Lone Star. Henry Lesher - I can't believe this. Silence in the ring goes around for a couple of moments as boos are heard from the crowd. Then, Lone Star starts talking.
Lone Star - So you want me to join the flock. Ash nods his head. Lone Star - You want me to become one of you? Ash nods his head again. Lone Star - You want me to turn my back on all of my fans just because you say so? You know what? Screw you, and screw the flock!
Crowd starts cheering wildly as Sinman, Wren and Frank Merritt start closing in on Lone Star. Ash stops them from attacking Lone Star.
Ash - Don't touch him! Back away! The rest of the flock backs away. Ash turns back towards Lone Star. Ash - So, you say screw the flock? Usually, I would kick your ass for saying that, but this time, I will let you go. I will give you another chance to rethink your decision. And take some time. I am sure that at the end you will realize that you can't make it in CWA on your own. And when you realize that, The Flock will be waiting for you...
Flock's theme music plays on the PA system. Ash starts laughing as the rest of the flock joins him. They slowly exit the ring as Lone Star is left behind noticeably not in a good mood. Flock backs away towards the backstage.
Henry Lesher - I don't like the way this is going. Ricky Sims - Lone Star seems to be out of himself right now. Henry Lesher - It looks like the flock intimidated him. Backstage we see Gloria Lavin not looking impressed as she watches things going on. Lone SZtar was her big shock of the night and Flock just had to ruin it. She'll have to deal with them right away.
Leech vs Harmony Fallacia "Enemies of Reality" by Nevermore begins to play and Leech comes out from the back, his arms raised and the crowd cheering him on. Leech rolls into the ring and has a few words with the ref about how he wants this match to be called. We then hear "Heads Explode" by Monster Magnet and Harmony Fallacia comes out with Mr. Binky in hand and a twinkle in her eye as she makes her way to the ring. She skips to the ring almost gleefully as she then finishes things off with a cartwheel at the bottom of the ramp.
Henry Lesher - Great, the cheerleader is here. Ricky Simms - Does anything make you happy? Henry Lesher - When Q squashes EOS, I am happy. I swear I will be happy again before this night is out.
Harmony slides into the ring and blows a kiss to a fan in the crowd and then the bell sounds and we are ready to get things underway. The two lock up. Leech quickly turns it into a headlock and then Harmony backs Leech into the ropes before whipping him off the ropes. Leech shoulder blocks Harmony down to the mat, then comes off the ropes and knee drops her in the chest before trying for an early pin. 1...2... kick out by Harmony. Leech stands Harmony up, whips her hard into the corner and then backdrops her to the mat. He follows this up with a leg drop and again tries for the pin. 1...2... Harmony again is able to escape the three count. Leech then stands Harmony up, whips her off the ropes and attempts a clothesline which Harmony ducks. She then does a handspring off the ropes and then gives Leech a headscissors takedown to the mat. She then uses her legs to try and choke Leech out while the crowd cheers.
Ricky Simms - The fans appear to be a bit torn here. Henry Lesher - The fans are useless. Did you hear the way they cheered that Lone Star person? I mean, sure it's all nice that he's here, but they don't cheer that way for me and I have been here for years.
Ricky Simms - There are reasons for that, you know. Leech rolls to the ropes and forces the break of the choke. He then gets to his feet and dropkicks Harmony before she can get her bearings. Leech then picks Harmony up and body slams her to the mat. Leech then heads to the ropes, climbs to the second rope and lands an axe handle smash to the head of Harmony Fallacia. Leech then tries for the pin. 1...2... Harmony reverses it into a small package. 1...2... kick out by Leech. From the back we see Frank Merritt on his way to the ring with a coy smile on his face. He says something to the camera about Leech and about EOS.
Ricky Simms - What the hell is he doing out here? Henry Lesher - He's Flock. He probably figures it's time for him to wrestle or something. Leech and Harmony get to their feet and lock up again. Leech then whips Harmony off the ropes. Frank reaches for Harmony, but misses her foot by an inch. The ref has a few words for Frank who simply laughs the ref off. Leech gives Harmony a swinging neckbreaker and then notices Frank outside the ring. He leans through the ropes with a few words for Frank. Frank responds with a punch to the face of Leech with brass knuckles. The ref calls for the bell and Frank rolls himself into the ring to beat Leech down with the knucks. Harmony then grabs Frank from behind and places him in a sleeper. Leech is announced the winner by DQ thanks to Frank's interference. Frank gets to his feet and starts to fight his way out of the sleeper, eventually snap maring Harmony to the mat before clubbing her with the knuckles. Frank then high tails it out of the ring when he sees Vesuvius and Shannon Skanes racing into the ring to save their stable mates. They then stand nose to nose and have a few words.
Ricky Simms - This could turn ugly. Henry Lesher - I sure as hell hope so. Vesuvius throws the first punch. The two then start trading punches in the middle of the ring with neither man giving an inch. Shannon whips Vesuvius off the ropes and clotheslines him down. Vesuvius rolls himself out of the ring, pulling Harmony out with him and not taking his eyes off of Shannon. Vesuvius points a finger at Shannon and says he will see him soon. Shannon stands over Leech and motions him back to the ring. Vesuvius thinks better of it, heading to the back.
Ricky Simms - There is a lot of tension in the air tonight. Something big is going down. Henry Lesher - I sure hope there is, because this is ridiculous. Backstage we see Gloria Lavin looking at a few things on her desk when Dave Morbid enters the picture.
Gloria Lavin - What the hell are you doing here? Dave Morbid - What's that? Oh, don't mind me. I am simply in the mood for a little chaos. Know where I can find any?
Gloria Lavin - Go to the Flock's dressing room. There's always some chaos going on down there.
Dave Morbid - Don't you mean The State? Gloria Lavin - I know what I mean. You're not amusing me. Dave Morbid - *looking around her office* My opffice will be bigger. Gloria Lavin - What the hell are you talking about? Dave Morbid - Tsk tsk, Miss Lavin. I am not one to ruin a surprise. Where is the chaos in that?
Halloween Jack vs Noah Skanes "Bodies" by Drowning Pool plays as Halloween Jack makes his way to the ring, the fans booing him after what they saw Jack do to Jennifer earlier this week. Jack appears not to care in the slightest and is ready for another week on Assault. As he slides into the ring, we hear "Just Like You" by Three Days Grace and Noah Skanes makes his way to the ring to the cheers of everyone in the crowd. Noah makes his way to the ring, hands his sunglasses to a little fan in the crowd and then tells the cameraman he always wanted to do that and winks before stepping up onto the apron. He then steps through the ropes and the bell sounds. Jack charges into the corner, but an attempted clothesline misses as Noah ducks it. Noah smiles as he stands in the middle of the ring and Jack gives him a glare.
Ricky Simms - Something has snapped in Jack this past week. Henry Lesher - Maybe it's the fact his season is over? Jack and Noah lock up. Jack knees Noah in the gut. Jack then whips Noah off the ropes and gives him a tilt a whirl backbreaker. Jack then goes for an early pin. 1...2... Noah kicks out. Jack then stands Noah up, whips him off the ropes and tries for a clothesline which Noah ducks. He then boots Jack in the gut and drops him with a stunner. Noah then stands Jack up and whips him hard into the corner buckle. Noah then lands a series of knife edge chops on Jack. Jack reverses it, throwing Noah into the corner and lands a series of knife edge chops on Noah. Jack then hip tosses Noah to the middle of the ring. Jack then stands Noah up and whips him through the ropes to the floor. Jack then climbs to the top rope and attempts a flying elbow to Noah's back from there. Noah dodges it and Jack hits the floor hard. Noah then drags Jack over to the announce table, picks him up and slams him on top of it. Noah then rolls himself back into the ring.
Ricky Simms - Almost have Halloween Jack in out laps here. Henry Lesher - He's not really my type, Rick. Jack gets up off the table and uses the bottom rope to pull himself inside the ring before the ref can reach the ten count. Noah then grabs Jack by the hair, pulls him to his feet and headbutts him twice, sending him into the ropes. Noah then whips Jack off the ropes and spears him down. Noah then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. Jack gets to his feet, turns and takes a flying body press from Noah. Noah goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Jack. Noah stands Jack up. Noah whips Jack into the corner. He charges and Jack clotheslines Noah to the mat. Jack the heads to the ropes, makes his way to the top and tries for a flying elbowsmash. Noah rolls out of the way and holds Jack's shoulders down for the pin. 1...2...3! Noah Skanes pulls out the win!
Ricky Simms - Roll up win there for Noah. Henry Lesher - Another fluke for the kid. His luck will run out and we all know it. Halloween Jack quickly gets to his feet and has a few words for the ref. The ref tries to defend his three count and Jack responds with a kick to the gut and a stunner on the ref before he leaves, shaking his head. Backstage we then see Vigilante pacing and Moose enters the picture.
Moose - I wanted to wish you good luck this week. Vigilante - That means a lot to me. Moose - What's with the whole movie and Hollywood thing, Vig? Vigilante - What do you mean? Moose - Well, I mean, we have a lot going on and all and EOS needs to be united here. Vigilante - You don't need to worry about me, man. You know that. Moose - Yeah, just there seems to be something going down tonight. Tommy Air vs Frank Merritt "Seizure of Power" by Marilyn Manson plays as Frank Merritt makes his way to the ring to the boos of all in the crowd. He gives the finger to one fan wearing an EOS shirt at ringside. Frank then makes his way up the steps into the ring. We then hear "Enemies of Reality" by Nevermore plays and Leech makes his way to the ring which confounds the fans and Frank Merritt inside the ring. Leech makes his way around the ring and takes a seat at ringside next to Henry Lesher. Lucky for him there's a spare headset.
Ricky Simms - To what do we owe the honor? Leech - Since jackass was kind enough to stick his nose into my match, I figured I would monitor his hard core rules match here with Tommy this week.
Henry Lesher - This at least holds the promise of violence. Leech - Oh there will be violence. "Smells Like Teen Spirit" by Nirvana plays and Tommy Air comes out with Jennifer. Jennifer has a chair in her hand. Tommy races into the ring and from the back we see Halloween Jack making his way to the ring with a very pissed off look on his face. Jack is within arm's reach of Jennifer when a hand grabs his arm from over the railing. A giant of a man steps over the guard railing and Halloween Jack's eyes go wide as the man picks him up and body slams him on the railing before standing between Jennifer and Jack as a shield. Jack gets to his feet. He then heads to the back telling Tommy to watch his back. Both Tommy and Frank have been distracted by this scene. Frank snaps himself out of it first, chop blocking Tommy to the mat before he starts raining down closed fists to Tommy's head as the bell sounds to get things underway.
Ricky Simms - This is going to be a mess. Henry Lesher - It had better be. Frank stands Tommy up and tosses him over the top rope. He then heads to the top rope and leaps off, landing a scissor kick to the back of Tommy's head. He rolls him over on the floor for the pin. 1...2... Tommy manages to kick out. Frank stands Tommy up, whips him into the guard railing next to the time keeper's table. Frank then stands Tommy up and measures him for a right hook only to take a thumb in the eye. Leech gets up from his spot at the announcer's table with a grin on his face. He reaches under the apron and pulls out a trash can lid as Tommy lands a series of forearm smashes on Frank Merritt. Leech tosses the trash can lid to Tommy and Tommy crowns Frank with it. Leech then rolls himself into the ring and motions Tommy to roll Frank into the ring. As Leech sets Frank up, we see SinmaN coming in through the crowd. SinmaN slides into the ring, a chair in hand and nails Leech across the back with it. Frank then DDTs Leech to the mat.
Ricky Simms - This keeps getting more confusing. Henry Lesher - Yes it is entertaining. Tommy rolls into the ring, gets to his feet and dropkicks Frank in the back. SinmaN tries for a spinning heel kick on Tommy, but Tommy shoulder rolls away from it. The giant who rescued Jennifer earlier steps up onto the apron. SinmaN charges and dropkicks him off the apron. Leech then kips up to his feet, spins SinmaN around and plants him with a DDT. Leech then rolls SinmaN out of the ring. Tommy stands Frank up, whips him to the ropes and dropkicks him back to the mat. Tommy then lands a spinning neckbreaker on Frank, dropping the back of his neck onto the chair that SinmaN just brought to the ring. Tommy goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Tommy Air wins and advances to the finals in the Commonwealth title tournament!
Ricky Simms - The kid moves on to face the winner of Dizz versus SinmaN later tonight! Henry Lesher - I am sick of this kid thinking he can steamroll his way to the top and fluking out the whole damned way along.
Lesher gets up from his post and takes off the headset. He then makes his way into the ring and starts talking to Tommy about tonight's match. Leech and SinmaN brawl outside the ring. Tommy's bodyguard steps over the top rope and stands behind Tommy, arms folded across his chest. Lesher doesn't give the giant a second look. He starts poking Tommy in the chest with his finger as he makes his points. Tommy looks back at the body guard and the body guard picks Lesher up and choke slams him onto the chair which still lies in the ring. As he does, we see BasketKase emerge from the back on a dead run screaming. He slides into the ring, dropkicks the body guard over the top rope and then picks a confused Tommy Air up and gives him a shock treatment in the corner. BasketKase screams to the crowd who are quite excited to see the beast back inside a CWA ring. BasketKase then attends to the fallen Dr. Lesher.
Ricky Simms - Lesher treats the oaf like crap and still BK shows him loyalty. I just don't get it.
Backstage we see Ash turning things over in the dressing room at the sight of Frank losing. Charley MacAdam enters the room cautiously.
Charley MacAdam - Ash, can we have a moment of your time? Ash - For what? To ask me about Frank's joke of a loss or about what the hell Leech was doing out there? That match was a farce. Frank gets Leech next week or I am going to break every skull in the other dressing rooms!
Charley MavcAdam - You're busy. We'll talk later. Charley leaves as Ash throws a chair at the mirror and shatters it. Angela/Myst vs Demona/Annalyse Boy Hits Car begins to play and Angela starts on her way to the ring with the crowd solidly opposed to her. She shakes her head about that as she makes her way down the ramp. She steps through the ropes and smiles for the booing fans, some of whom are snapping her picture. We then hear “A Place For My Head” by Linkin Park and Myst comes out to a very mixed reaction. She steps through the ropes and does a standing somersault. She then high fives Angela as we hear “Stained” by Android Lust and Demona leads Annalyse to the ring. They slide into the ring and all four ladies battle in the middle of the ring as the bell sounds and the ref is left to try and sort things out. Annalyse and Angela fall over the top rope and leaves Myst and Demona in the ring as the legal participants.
Ricky Simms - This match gets underway after that carnage. From what I understand, Dr. Lesher is done for the night, so I am now joined by Kelly Buchanan for the rest of the night. Nice to have you here, Kelly.
Kelly Buchanan - I'll try not to blonde this show up. Ricky Simms - *laughs* You're a lot easier on the eyes that the Doc, so just be yourself. Demona whips Myst off the ropes. Myst ducks a clothesline and lands a reverse neckbreaker on Demona. She goes for the early pin. 1...2... Demona rolls out with ease. Myst stands Demona up, whips her to the ropes. Demona drops down and slides out of the ring. She then superkicks Angela as Angela was just preparing to power bomb Annalyse on the floor. Myst then slides under the bottom rope, missile kicking Demona straight into the guard railing. Myst then stands Demona up and rolls her into the ring as Angela heads to their corner to ready for the tag in. Myst stands Demona up. Demona chops Myst in the throat, then monkey flips her to the corner. Outside the ring, Annalyse gets to her feet and pulls herself onto the apron ready to be tagged in. Demona then stands Myst up, whips her into the opposite corner and then gives her a bulldog into her own corner before tagging in Annalyse. Annalyse stands Myst up, whips her off the ropes and dropkicks her to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Good back and forth action here. Kelly Buchanan - It's like a nightmare to me. All those women who beat me in the past all together in one tag team match up.
Annalyse stands Myst up. Myst rakes the eyes of Annalyse and then quickly pulls her to the corner and tags in Angels. Angels leaps over the top rope and lands an elbow to the back of Annalyse's neck. Demona shakes her head on the apron as Angela tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Annalyse. Angela then comes off the ropes and lands a rolling thunder on Annalyse and again tries for the pin. 1...2... Annalyse gets a foot on the bottom rope to prevent the loss here. Angela looks frustrated as she stands Annalyse up, whips her off the ropes and Annalyse responds with a lariat that takes Angela down. Annalyse reaches for the tag to her partner as Demona leans in, arm outstretched. Angela rolls to her corner and tags Myst in. Myst races into the ring, dropkicks Demona off the apron to the floor and then locks Annalyse into the Mystifyer. Annalyse struggles against it.
Kelly Buchanan - That hurts like hell. Ricky Simms - Looks like it. Annalyse finally realizes she is not going to reach the ropes and she taps out. The bell sounds and Myst and Angela are the winners. The crowd has a mixed reaction to the win as Angela and Myst raise each other's hands in the middle of the ring. The State claims their first joint win here tonight.
Ricky Simms - Good teamwork shown by Myst and Angela here. Kelly Buchanan - Don't let that fool you. The mood in the back is poison right now, one of the reasons I jumped at the idea of being out here rather than back there with them.
As Kelly says that, we head backstage and find Vesuvius and Shannon Skanes having words in the dressing room when Noah Skanes steps in between with a smile on his face.
Noah Skanes - You know, as much as I love the little tiff between my father and my compadre, I am not digging this anymore. You two had better work this out before the roster split.
Noah walks away as Shannon and Vesuvius give Noah a rather shocked look. Wren vs Rated R "This Means War" by Busta Rhymes and Ozzy plays and Wren SilverPhoenix comes out from the back to the boos of the crowd. She gives the finger to the camera on her way down the ramp as pyro shoots off and she points to her State T-shirt and points out they are number one. She then makes her way up the ring steps onto the apron before suggestively stepping through the ropes.
