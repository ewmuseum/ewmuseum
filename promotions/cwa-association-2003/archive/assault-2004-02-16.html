CWA Assault, 16 February 2004

16 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kelowna BC is rocking and rolling as we get the latest edition of Assault from Canada. Some are waving Canadian flags with Northern Exposure written on them. Some are holding up signs congratulating hometown hero Myst on her win at Breakthrough. Others are holding up signs for EOS and even for the hated Flock. Backstage we see Shannon Skanes entering the arena to a huge cheer from the crowd. He is headed off by Samantha Johnson.
Samantha Johnson - Shannon, tonight you have to name two partners. Are you prepared to announce them?
Shannon Skanes - They are calling the event Malicious Intent for good reason. I have been at this for a very long time and I have never been put into this position. I guess I could thank Dante for forcing me to expand my horizons. Nathan Cristion, Ash and Max Cameron are going to be seeing me and my partners at Malicious Intent, though they will wish that they hadn't.
Samantha Johnson - So you have picked your partners? Shannon Skanes - I never said that. Samantha then watches as Shannon walks away. She shrugs for the camera and we head to ringside for our first match of the night.
Dizz vs Frank Merritt "Nobody's Listening" by Linkin Park plays as Dizz makes his way to the ring to a huge ovation. The three time champion has been having a tougher than usual time in the CWA and this is a match that could get him back on the winning track. Dizz steps onto the apron as his pyro continues to go off on stage. He steps through the ropes and gives a thumbs up to a fan in the front row wearing his T-shirt. "Seizure of Power" by Marilyn Manson begins to play as Frank Merritt makes his way to the ring. He gets the usual hatred from the fans and, as usual, he enjoys it the whole way. He slides into the ring and smiles at Dizz. The two circle each other as the bell sounds and then they lock up.
Ricky Simms - Welcome to another edition of Assault. Henry Lesher - Yes, for those of you expecting hours of tedious boredom and overacting, we present Assault in its entirety instead.
Dizz places Frank in a headlock. Frank backs Dizz into the ropes, then picks him up for a side to side suplex. Frank then quickly gets to his feet, grabs Dizz by the hair and stands him up. He then headbutts him three times and then whips him to the opposite corner of the ring. Frank clotheslines Dizz as he comes out of the corner and then goes for the cover. 1...2.. kick out by Dizz. Frank then rolls Dizz out of the ring. He follows him out and pulls a mat away from the concrete floor even as the ref is warning him not to. He has a few words for the ref, sets Dizz up for a piledriver and then Dizz straightens up, backdropping Frank to the floor. Dizz then leg drops the throat of Merritt and then goes for the pin. 1...2.... kick out by Frank. Dizz then heads to the ropes and quickly jumps to the top. He tries for a five star frog splash, but misses as Merritt rolls out of the way. Frank then quickly gets to his feet and starts putting the boots to Dizz. As he does this, we see Nathan Cristion appear at the top of the stage.
Ricky Simms - What the hell is Nathan Cristion doing here? He's going to be in the big six man match at Malicious Intent.
Henry Lesher - You mean the three on one squash? Yeah he will and that makes him my hero. Maybe he wants to make it four on one and is scouting Frank for it.
Nathan takes a step towards the ring as Frank whips Dizz off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Frank then notices Nathan on the ramp and looks puzzled. Nathan applauds Frank and appears to be egging him on. Frank turns, grabs Dizz by the hair and picks him up for a power bomb. Dizz punches Frank repeatedly in the face. Frank then falls backwards to the mat with Dizz on top of him. Dizz elbows Frank in the forehead and then goes for the pin. 1...2... Frank kicks out. Nathan Cristion takes a few more steps down the ramp which generates more booing from the crowd. Dizz stands Frank up, whips him into the corner and then charges in. Frank then backdrops Dizz over the top rope and to the floor. Frank is held back by the ref when he wants to charge out. Nathan Crisiton sees his opening and goes for it. He goes to ringside, picks Dizz up and slams his back into the steel corner post. He then stands him up and DDTs him on the floor before rolling him back into the ring. Nathan then makes his way back towards the dressing room area as Frank looks surprised at the state Dizz is in now. Frank sees Nathan disappearing through the curtain and smiles.
Ricky Simms - Like Frank needs that kind of help! Henry Lesher - Nathan was just helping the match along. That's all. There's nothing wrong with that, is there?
Frank then slams Dizz to the mat with the Blunt Trauma and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Frank wins the match with an assist given to Nathan Cristion.
Ricky Simms - Why would he help a member of the Flock? What is in it for him? Henry Lesher - Fame, fortune, woman, drugs? Pick one or all of the above. Backstage we see Shannon in his office when Q arrives at the door. Shannon Skanes - Just the man I didn't want to see. Q - The feeling is more than mutual. I heard you called for me, so here I am. Shannon Skanes - Take a seat. We need to straighten a few things out around here. Q takes a seat and then Dante shows up at the door. Dante - What's up now? *spots Q* Are we performing an exorcism? Q - Learn to spell! Dante - Buy a vowel! Q raises up, but Shannon gets between them. Shannon Skanes - Guys! I didn't make these calls so we could get into childish name calling here. Look, we each have concerns and it's time we address them. Dante lost his job on Assault. Q wants to run the company.
Q - I am not working for him. If he becomes Assault GM again, I'll set the building on fire.
Dante - Just make sure you're in it when it burns. Shannon Skanes - That's enough! Now, I have an idea that will benefit everyone. Sit down and behave and I think you'll all see the light.
The three sit down and then we return to ringside for our next match up. Ash vs Gothic Angel vs Rated R "Right Round" by Dope plays as Gothic Angel makes her way to the ring. She gets a warm reaction from all as she steps onto the apron and then slowly steps into the ring. As she does that, we hear "I Hate Everything About You" and Rated R makes his way out to a huge ovation as Canadian crowds always cheer on their own. "The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson plays as Ash makes his way to the ring. He is led by Angela who is without a match this week and says something to the camera about the roster fearing her. Ash then slides into the ring and the bell sounds as both Gothic Angel and Rated R put the boots to Ash. They then stand him up, whip him off the ropes and give him a double clothesline. Ash rolls out of the ring to where Angela is waiting for him. Rated R and Gothic Angel try to wave Ash back into the ring. Ash starts to leave. Rated R heads out of the ring and grabs Ash by the hair, turns him around and headbutts him to the floor. Rated R then picks Ash up and throws him back into the ring where Gothic Angel applies a sleeperhold.
Ricky Simms - Looks like this triple threat match is playing out as a handicap match. Henry Lesher - Ash has few friends and is a target of pretty near everybody. However, odds are slim that Rated R or Gothic Angel would allow each other to win this match up.
Rated R enters the ring and sees that Ash is fading in the sleeper. Rated R appears to bide his time waiting. He then pulls Gothic Angel up by the hair and delivers a reverse neckbreaker on her. He then tries for the pin on Ash. 1...2... Ash gets a foot on the bottom rope. Gothic Angel gets to her feet as Rated R applies a sleeper on Ash now. Gothic Angel kicks Rated R in the side of the head. She then stands Ash up, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Rated R kips up to his feet and plants a standing side kick to the jaw of Gothic Angel. Rated R then picks Ash up and body slams him to the mat. He picks Gothic Angel up and tosses her over the top rope to the floor and then starts up the ropes. Rated R dives off and misses with a flying elbowsmash as Ash moves in time. The fact Angela called out to warn him helps. Ash gets to his feet, groggy from the double punishment he has taken. He spots Gothic Angel stepping onto the apron and dropkicks her off of it. He then turns his attention to Rate R who is holding the elbow which just crashed into the mat. Ash places him in an abdomenal stretch.
Ricky Simms - Ash succeeding here despite the numbers being stacked against him. Henry Lesher - Once Rated R and Gothic Angel started fighting each other, the door was wide open for him.
Gothic Angel gets to her feet on the floor. She then rolls herself into the ring behind Rated R. She dropkicks him in the back and he falls forward with Ash underneath him. Gothic Angel then comes off the ropes and gives a rolling thunder to both of the fallen men at the same time. She rolls Rated R over for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Rated R. Ash gets to his feet and puts the boots to Gothic Angel before she has a chance to get back up. Ash then picks her up and throws her over the top rope to the floor. Rated R charges at Ash. Ash throws him through the ropes to the floor. Rated R crashes on top of Gothic Angel. She assumes it was a purposeful attack on her. The two of them grapple on the floor as the ref counts. Ash turns to Angela and winks at her. Rated R stands Gothic Angel up and takes a thumb in the throat. Gothic Angel then whips Rated R towards the steel corner post. Rated R puts his hands out to prevent the impact and then turns and lands a kick to the chin of Gothic Angel. The ref continues to count as Ash actually retreats from where the battle is going on. The ref reaches a ten count and both Rated R and Gothic Angel are done for this week. The bell sounds and Ash is the winner.
Ricky Simms - I hate to say it, but the Doc called it. Ash waits till his opponents turned on each other and then won this match by count out.
Henry Lesher - You don't get to be called doctor if you're stupid. Inside the ring, Ash grabs the microphone from the ring announcer Donny Rave and begind to speak.
Ash - I have pretty much had enough of being ducked. Next week, I want a shot at the winner of the Superior Title match here tonight! I am going to get what I want! 420 forever!
Ash throws the mic back to the announcer and then makes his way out where Angela hugs him and then leads him to the back. Backstage we then see Dante with Joe Davidson.
Joe Davidson - Dante, we saw Q and yourself in Shannon's office. What is going on here tonight?
Dante - We talked about weather and sports and even some talk about how incompetent our reporters are.
Joe Davidson - Does Shannon really have a plan to get the three of you onto the same page? Dante - If you read your program for tonight's show, dingus, you'll see we are on the same page. Page three. Now, leave me alone.
Dante takes off and Q approaches. Joe Davidson - Can I have a minute of your time, Q? Q - Not now, I have to set the building on fire. We then head back to ringside. Vigilante vs Max Cameron "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC plays as Max Cameron makes his way to the ring. He doesn't look happy to be here tonight at all. Of course, he is facing a man who hasn't lost a match in months. This is Max's first big shot since a world title shot against Dizz that nearly saw him walk away as champion. As Max enters the ring, "Back In Black" by AC/DC plays and Vigilante comes out with a smile on his face and his belts high in the air. He has a few words for Max as he heads down the ramp about not quite being a top tier talent in the CWA. Max motions him inside the ring. Vigilante drops his belts to the floor, slides into the ring and the two start going at it toe to toe as the bell sounds and the crowd goes wild.
Ricky Simms - The double champion out here to face one of his own tonight. Henry Lesher - Let the paint dry watching commence! You know, I must hand it to Shannon on this one though. If he has Q and Dante on the same page, that would be quite the feat.

Max lands one right back on Vigilante, then eye gouges him and snap mares him to the center of the ring. Vigilante gets to his feet, takes an arm drag from Max followed by another which is followed by a third. Max then picks Vigilante u0p and body slams him to the mat, then stands him up and delivers a snap suplex on him. Max tries for a pin, hooking the leg. 1...2... kick out by Vigilante. Max stands Vigilante up, whips him off the ropes. Vigilante ducks a clothesline, then dives and chop blocks the knees out from underneath of Vig. Vigilante then applies an ankle lock on Max Cameron. The crowd doesn't know what to make of this after the accusations by Vigilante and the denials by Max all week.
Ricky Simms - What is this, interference night? Henry Lesher - Anything to put this match out of its misery. Max manages to grab the rope to force the break as Chaz and Gothic take their seats next to the timekeeper. Max gets to his feet and Vigilante lands another resounding knife edge chop to his chest. Max reels against the ropes. Vigilante whips him into the corner buckle, then lands a series of choulder blocks on him for good measure. Vigilante then picks Max up and military press slams him to the center of the ring. Vigilante winks at a female fan in the front row, then headlocks Max and tries for a DDT. Max then runs Vigilante hard into the corner buckle. Max then gives Vigilante and inverted atomic drop back into the buckle and lands a series of shoulder blocks on him the same as Vig just laid on him. Max then gets up in the corner on the second rope and starts raining down punches to the face of Vig as the fans count the shots. They reach eight before Vig wraps up Max and gives him an inverted atomic drop followed by a headbutt which knocks Max to the mat. At ringside, we see Chaz and Gothic shaking their head. Vigilante then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. He hits a moonsault on Max from there. He then goes for the pin. 1...2... Max manages to get a shoulder up. Vigilante gives the ref a dirty look and questions his ability to count past two.
Ricky Simms - Barely stayed alive there. Henry Lesher - I am barely staying awake. When are Chaz and Gothic going to start clubbing people with chairs?
Vigilante stands Max up and whips him off the ropes. They both clothesline each other to the mat. Their arms cover each other as well and the ref counts the pin. 1...2... both roll a shoulder up at the last possible moment. Vigilante gets to his feet first, finally spots Chaz and Angel at ringside and has a few words for them. He turns his attention back to Max, setting him up for a vertical suplex which Max blocks. Max then delivers a vertical suplex on Vigilante. Max follows this up by applying a figure four leglock on the defending champ. Vigilante reaches for the ropes as the ref asks him if he sumits. Vigilante refuses to give up, lunging for and grabbing the bottom rope. The ref forces Max to break the hold. Vigilante rolls himself out of the ring, holding a sore leg from the well applied figure four. Chaz and Gothic Angel get to their feet and loo at each other. Max rolls himself out of the ring and stands nose to nose with Chaz. Vigilante watches on. Max then turns and plants a boot to the chin of Vigilante and Chaz starts putting the boots to the champ. The ref calls for the bell, DQing Max. Max rolls himself into the ring with Chaz and Gothic Angel. The two interfering members take off their shirts to reveal Youthful Aggression shirts underneath as Max poses for the man flashbulbs going off.
Henry Lesher - Now that is interesting. Ricky Simms - Youthful Aggression is back! Max Cameron defects from EOS and starts his group back up with Chaz and Gothic Angel in there with him!
Henry Lesher - Ask him if he cares that he lost the match. As they pose, Vigilante gets to his feet and Moose is seen coming in from the back with a chair in hand. Max, Chaz and Angel scatter as Vigilante and Moose enter the ring. Moose has a few nasty things to say to Max and Max gives him the finger in response. This won't make Max popular with the fans. Max, Chaz and Angel take off running up the ramp and into the back. We then head backstage where Shannon is standing with Charley MacAdam.
Charley MacAdam - We understand that you're ready to announce your partners for Malicious Intent.
Shannon Skanes - *nods* I am on my way to the ring to make that announcement now. The crowd cheers as "Deep" by Nine Inch Nails booms throughout and Shannon Skanes makes his way down the ramp. He nods towards Moose and Vigilante as they pass each other by. He high fives a few fans, then rolls into the ring and has a microphone thrown to him inside the ring.
Shannon Skanes - So here I stand! Now, I have been through hell and at Battle of the Warriors I was put through it again by Sal Clemenzo. However, we are a month removed from that and I feel fine! I took some shots at Dead By Dawn, the night I won the world title! I then dropped the title two weeks later to Dizz and I have been hearing about my career being over since then. I have heard it and heard it and heard it. Everywhere I go, people ask me when I am going to retire. Those people can blow me! I beat Q! I beat Damien! I beat Moose! I won in the octagon! I am still the same wrestler I was on that fateful October night! I am still capable of winning inside this ring! Now, Dante made this damned match and I have Ash, Max Cameron and that new jerkoff Nathan Cristion to deal with! I also have the board breathing down my neck to wrestle or retire! Well, I am not going into this match alone! I am going into this match with a simple philsophy! Keep your friends close and your enemies closer!
"Know" by System of a Down plays and Dante starts on his way to the ring to the shock of everyone. Dante steps into the ring and has a few nasty words for a fan in the front row.
Shannon Skanes - Dante here was forced out of the game, but I made sure he got a special allowance for a one match appearance!
Dante - *grabbing the microphone* I simply had to do this! The fans demanded it! I have been besieged with requests to return to action and so here I am, tagging with a man who can't control his own company.
Shannon Skanes - *grabbing the mic back* And our tag partner is on his way in as well. Tomoyasu Hotei's "Battle without Honor or Humanity" begins to play and shock deepens as Q makes his way to the ring.
