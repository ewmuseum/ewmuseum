CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 21 June 2004

21 June 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Boston is the home for tonight's Assault and the crowd is quite loud as we get ready to begin the show, the last event before Sunstroke and the crowds have been quite anxious to get to that event. Backstage we see Ash entering the building, ready for the steel cage tonight. He hears the boos of the crowd, but they don't appear to affect him any more tonight than they do at any other time. The rest of the Flock is awaiting him in the back and they then make their way into the locker room where the door is shut for them to dicuss Flock business. The credits then roll and we see images of The Flock, Anarchy, Glamour Girls, Micheal Leary and Humanity Crisis before we head to ringside where Henry Lesher and Ray Dees are set to announce the action tonight.
Tommy Air vs BasketKase "Smells Like Teen Spirit" by Nirvana plays and we see Tommy Air on his way to the ring with the Superior title over his shoulder. He takes in the crowd's cheers and adulations so much he doesn't hear BasketKase coming behind him. BasketKase tosses Tommy off the ramp to the floor below. BasketKase then jumps down, landing a double axe handle to the back of Tommy.
Henry Lesher - This is when BasketKase teaches that child Tommy some respect. Ray Dees - Yeah, lot of guts it takes tro attack someone from behind. Of course, I might have won a match once in awhile if I had tried it myself.
BasketKase drags Tommy to ringside and rolls him into the ring. BasketKase then grabs the Superior title belt and looks upon it with a curious eye. He tosses it over the top rope into the ring before rolling himself into the ring as the bell sounds to get things underway. BasketKase whips Tommy off the ropes, then gives him a drop toe face first onto his own belt. BasketKase then yells "MEAT!!" and the crowd echoes it back to him. Despite his heelish behavior tonight, they are still cheering him on. BasketKase stands Tommy up, whips him off the ropes and spears him to the mat. BasketKase then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by BasketKase. BasketKase looks to Lesher at the announce table. Lesher nods, but says nothing. BasketKase then starts choking Tommy on the mat while screaming in his face.
Ray Dees - BasketKase is a monster this week. Henry Lesher - He has always been a monster. BasketKase stands Tommy up, whips him into the corner and then gives him a sidewalk slam in the middle of the ring. BasketKase then gets a twisted grin on his face. He points at the corner which means his dreaded finisher is coming. He stands Tommy up. Tommy senses this is the time for a comeback and starts landing rights and lefts to the head of BasketKase. Tommy then whips BasketKase off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Tommy leg drops BasketKase across the throat and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by BasketKase. Tommy then picks BasketKase up and power slams him to the mat, shocking considering his slight frame. Tommy again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out again by BasketKase. Tommy stands BasketKase up, whips him into the corner and then charges in for a shoulder block. BasketKase sidesteps the shoulder block, picks Tommy up and gives him the Shock Treatment in the corner and then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! BasketKase with a huge win! We then see Vesuvius on the titan tron applauding.
Vesuvius - About time someone shut that kid up. BasketKase, you want meat? You like meat? You did quite a job as the janitor in the compound. We would be glad to have you back as a full fledged member of the group, inside the ring and out. Lesher, you would get full rights to your labs, no questions asked. Think about it.
BasketKase rolls out of the ring, running to the back screaming for meat. The mountain man promised meat. Anarchy Inc. will give BasketKase meat!
Ray Dees - Any comment on that offer? Henry Lesher - It's no secret Anarchy needs something to put them over the top. I'll have to think about this further.
Backstage we see Lethal Weapon entering the arena and he is approached by Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - Weapon, tonight you are facing off against Ash inside a steel cage. What is your mindset going into this battle?
Lethal Weapon - I intend to destroy him. I intend to make him hurt and hurt bad. Flock no longer rules Assault. The show will be mine.
Weapon shoves Joe out of the way and continues to his locker room. Wish vs Corina "Nothing Left To Lose" by Puddle of Mudd plays as Wish makes his way to the ring and the crowd gives him some nasty words for his appearance in Boston tonight. Wish has a few words of his own for one fan in the front row before he pulls himself up on the apron, then steps through the ropes. He then starts arguing with the ref, though the match has yet to start. Word has it Wish has been high strung since finding out he was scheduled to wrestle in the Summer Sanitarium match next week. We then hear "Haunted" by Evanescence and Corina makes her way to the ring to the cheers of the crowd and catcalls from most of the men. She blows a kiss to one fan who has a sign proposing marriage to her, then she steps up onto the apron where Wish dropkicks her to the floor. Wish then rolls out of the ring and lands a series of forearms to the back of Corina as the crowd boos his action.
Ray Dees - Nasty tactics here by Wish to get things started. Henry Lesher - Good for him. I am sick of these little girls and their T&A crap. Wish runs Corina head first into the steel ring steps, then rolls her into the ring. He smiles as the crowd boos louder. He rolls himself into the ring and the bell sounds. He goes right for the pin. 1...2... Corina gets a foot on the bottom rope. Wish then pulls Corina to the middle of the ring and again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Corina. Wish then stands Corina up, whips her off the ropes and knees her in the gut, sending her head over heels through the air and then to the mat. Wish then picks Corina up and body slams her to the mat before delivering an elbow drop to the throat. He then goes for the pin again. 1...2... kick out by Corina. Wish shakes his head, believing that should have been a three count to end this match. Wish stands Corina up and whips her into the buckle. Corina then jumps to the top rope and lariats a shocked Wish from there. She goes for the pin. 1...2... Wish kicks out. Corina rolls herself out of the ring to collect herself. Wish rolls out right after her. He starts running after her. She rolls herself into the ring. Wish rolls himself into the ring and straight into a dropkick to the face from Corina who then goes for another pin. 1...2... Wish manages to kick out.
Ray Dees - That was close. Henry Lesher - Shame I am not close to caring. Corina stands Wish up, whips him into the corner buckle and then monkey flips him out of it. She then waits for him to stand and then lands a superkick to the jaw of Wish. He staggers and then falls face first to the mat. Corina then stands Wish up, heads to the ropes, headlocks him and gives him the FF Look and goes to the mat. She rolls him over for the pin. 1...2...3! Corina scores her first win here over Wish!
Ray Dees - Nice move. Wonder what the FF stands for. Henry Lesher - She says it stands for "furious finisher." Who knows what the hell it really stands for and who cares?
Backstage we see Frank Merritt with a smile on his face as he sits in the locker room. Charley MacAdam enters the picture.
Charley MacAdam - Frank, your suspension has been lifted. Is that the reason for the smile?
Frank Merritt - No, the reason for the smile is because Vesuvius is about to end his career by facing me.
Charley MacAdam - You are also both slated to appear in the Summer Sanitarium match. Frank Merritt - He isn't going to make it. Halloween Jack vs Chastity "One Step Closer" by Linkin Park begins to play and Halloween Jack makes his way to the ring and he hears it from the crowd as he does so. Jack wastes no time in making his way to the ring, not even pausing for a moment to enjoy the music or pyrotechnics which trumpet his entrance. Jack slides into the ring and then looks back to the entrance with great disdain as "Naked Cousin" by PJ Harvey plays and Chastity makes her way to the ring. The crowd has a similar reaction to her as they did to Corina earlier. She steps up onto the apron and orders Jack to step away from her. The ref pushes Jack away and Jack laughs as if this is all greatly amusing to him.
Ray Dees - Second Glamour Girl of three tonight. Henry Lesher - Every one of them less talented than the last. Chastity steps into the ring and the bell sounds. She and Jack circle each other and then lock up. Jack breaks the lock up and rakes the eyes of Jack and then gives her a headbutt into the ropes. Jack then whips Chastity into the corner and then gives her a fisherman's suplex and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Chastity. Jack then stands Chastity up, whips her into the corner buckle and she nails it hard with her back, then falls face down to the mat. Jack then places her in a crossface chicken wing in an attempt to make her tap out. Chastity reaches around and grabs the bottom rope to force the break right away. Jack gets to his feet and doesn't appear to be happy with himself right now. Jack then stands Chastity up, whips her off the ropes and misses with a clothesline. Chastity kicks Jack in the ribs, then gives him a spinning neckbreaker to the mat. Chastity tries for the pin. 1...2... Jack manages to kick out. Chastity then leg drops Jack before heading to the ropes and making her way to the top. Jack gets to his feet, turns and takes a missile dropkicks from the top rope. Jack crashes to the mat and Chastity once again goes for the pin. 1...2... Jack manages to kick out.
Ray Dees - That was close. Henry Lesher - Doesn't make her talented, makes Jack pathetic. Ray Dees - You're a very negative person. Henry Lesher - Who brings in the ratings. That is why I am going to Sunstroke to commentate, not you.
Chastity stands Jack up in the corner and starts landing knee lifts on him. Jack responds with a vicious clothesline to the mat and then leans over her, landing numerous punches to the head on her. Jack then uses the bottom rope to choke her as he pulls her head forward. The ref counts nearly to the five count before Jack re;eases the choke. He then starts the choke again. The ref again counts to four before she chokes for air. The ref then threatens to DQ Jack. Jack starts having words with the ref as Chastity uses the ropes to get to her feet. Jack turns and takes a kick to the ribs from Chastity. Chastity then lands a sunset flip on Jack, pulling him down for the pin. 1...2... Halloween Jack manages to kick out. Chastity stands Jack up, whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him back to the mat. Chastity then heads to the ropes, jumps to the top and lands a shooting star press on Jack and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Chastity grabs the win over Halloween Jack. Chastity then rolls herself out of the ring where she is attacked by Gothic Angel. Chastity turns and starts trading rights and lefts with Angel. She then shoves Angel over the guard railing into the crowd. Chastity then retreats to the back before KillSwitch has a chance to come out here.
Ray Dees - Nice win for Chastity. Henry Lesher - The Jack-Tommy match just became a battle of the losers in a hell in the cell.
Backstage we see Gloria Lavin entering the office where Shelia Lavin sits and looks over contracts and ratings and things of that nature.
Shelia Lavin - Yes, Gloria, may I help you? Gloria Lavin - This Summer Sanitarium match we have set up is sweet, but it could cause problems.
Shelia Lavin - Enlighten me on how. Gloria Lavin - When a Chaos roster member wins an Assault title or vice cersa, we have a problem.
Shelia Lavin - Then we trade. If we have to trade a few, then that is what we do. Gothic Angel vs Notorious BLC vs Harmony Fallacia "Wedge" by Hollow plays as BLC makes his way to the ring to the cheers of the crowd. He high fives some of the fans as he makes his way to ringside. He then steps up onto the apron, salutes one of the fans carrying an American flag and then enters the ring. No sooner is he inside the ring than we hear "Heads Explode" by Monster Magnet and Harmony Fallacia comes out to a huge ovation from the crowd. She steps through the ropes and waves to the crowd, then turns to BLC and taps him on the head with Mr. Binky. BLC slaps it away and gets in her face. She smiply stands there smiling as we hear "Headstrong" by Trapt and Gothic Angel slides into the ring, still nearby from her brawl with Chastity. The three start brawling as the bell sounds to get the match started. BLC headbutts Angel to the mat, then headbutts Harmony to the mat. He lands an elbow drop to the throat of Angel, then tries the same on Harmony which misses as she rolls out of the way.
Ray Dees - Triple threat rules here. Henry Lesher - In this week's collection of three people no one cares about, an Anarchy member, a Humanity Crisis member and a nobody.
Harmony gets to her feet, plants a foot in the forehead of BLC, then snap mares him to the corner buckle. She rams BLC's head into the buckle a few times, then is grabbed from behind by Gothic Angel who slams Harmony's head into BLC's. Gothic then hip tosses Harmony to the middle of the ring. As she stands, Gothic tries to whip BLC into Harmony, but BLC stops his forward motion and whips Gothic into Harmony instead. BLC then scissor kicks Gothic to the back of the neck and tries for the pin. 1...2... Gothic Angel manages to kick out. BLC stands Gothic up and whips her into the corner. He then takes a dropkick to the chest from Harmony and BLC falls through the ropes to the floor. Harmony then turns her attention to Gothic a moment too late as Gothic spears her to the mat. Gothic then goes for the pin on Harmony. 1...2... Harmony kicks out. Gothic stands Harmony up, then whips her off the ropes and backdrops her to the mat. Gothic then tries for a missile kick underneath the bottom rope on BLC, but he moves out of the way and she crashes hard to the floor. BLC then rolls himself into the ring. He knees Harmony in the gut repeatedly. He then snap mares her to the middle of the ring, then bulldogs her to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... Harmony manages to kick out as Gothic Angel rolls herself into the ring. Gothic sweeps the legs out from under BLC as he gets to his feet, then eye gouges him on the mat. Gothic then goes for the pin. 1...2... BLC kicks out.
Ray Dees - It's cosfusing. Henry Lesher - Yes and boring all at once. Gothic stands Harmony up. She whips her off the ropes. Harmony ducks a clothesline, then comes off and both women clothesline each other to the mat. BLC looks at both and opts to pin Harmony. 1...2... Harmony kicks out and reverses it into a small package. 1...2... BLC kicks out. Gothic Angel uses the ropes to get up as Harmony lands a series of punches on BLC. BLC then throws Harmony off and takes a leg drop from Gothic before he has a chance to get out of the way. Harmony then gives Gothic a roundhouse kick which sends her over the top rope. Harmony then lands a rolling thunder on BLC and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by BLC as the crown groans, believing that was going to be it. Harmony stands BLC up, whips him to the ropes where Gothic trips him down to the mat and then Harmony leg drops BLC to the back of the head and rolls him over for the pin. 1...2...3! Harmony wins the match as Gothic is one second too late to prevent the three count.
Ray Dees - Huge win for Harmony here this week. Henry Lesher - Yeah, those jobbers have no idea what hit them. NEXT!!! Backstage we see Vesuvius with a cart full of raw meat. BasketKase is nearby with a smile on his face. Vesuvius beckons him over.
Vesuvius - BasketKase, this is a free offering. There is more meat in it for you if you join us.
BasketKase - *charging at the cart and knocking it over, meat everywhere* MEAT!!!! *consumes meat off the floor*
Vesuvius smiles as eh leaves BasketKase to wallow in his meat fantasy. TJ Mercer vs Gerard Angelo "Hell Song" by Sum 41 plays as Gerard Angelo makes his way to the ring with the support of very few in the crowd. He says something to the camera about being the next TV champion. He then makes his way to ringside as his pyro goes off, then jumps onto the apron. He steps through the ropes and tells the cameraman that he is the next champ. We then hear "Imaginary" by Evanescanece and TJ Mercer makes his way to the ring with Vesuvius close behind. Vesuvius is still evaluating this new property of his group. TJ rolls himself into the ring and Gerard puts the boots to him as the bell sounds to get this underway. Gerard then stands TJ up, whips him into the corner buckle and then lands a series of body punches on TJ. The ref warns Gerard about the closed fists, so Gerard starts kicking away at the chest of TJ, causing him to slump in the corner as Vesuvius is shown outside the ring shaking his head.
Ray Dees - Vesuvius out here already after feeding BasketKase. He doesn't look impressed with TJ thus far.
Henry Lesher - Would you be? If he wants Anarchy to be taken seriously, he has to be more choosy about who he puts in it.
Gerard stands TJ up and grabs him for a choke slam? TJ nails Gerard in the head with a series of elbows before Gerard has a chance to try and execute a move usually reserved for wrestlers larger than he is. TJ then headbutts Gerard, following that up by eye raking his eyes along the top rope. TJ then punches Angelo in the throat which drops him to his knees. TJ then knee lifts Angelo in the face, dropping him to the mat where TJ tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gerard Angelo. Vesuvius paces outside the ring, clearly impressed with this turn but not completely comfortable in this match yet. TJ stands Gerard Angelo up, whips him hard into the corner and then clotheslines him to the mat. TJ heads to the ropes, climbs to the second rope and lands a double axe handle to the back of Gerard. TJ then picks Gerard up for a power bomb, but Gerard lands a series of punches to the head of TJ and TJ drops him. Gerard then lands a series of knife edge chops to Gerard's chest, driving him into the corner. TJ then pulls Gerard out of the corner to the center of the ring and slaps on a sleeperhold. Gerard reaches for the ropes, but quickly drops to his knees.
Ray Dees - TJ looking to make Gerard quit or fall asleep here. Henry Lesher - I am fighting the same battle here. Gerard's arm drops once, but does not drop the second time. He then elbows TJ in the ribs to break the hold and then Angelo snap mares TJ to the mat. Angelo then picks TJ up and body slams him to the mat. Angelo then tries for a leg drop, but TJ moves out of the way and then quickly slaps on a figure four on Angelo from the mat. Angelo screams in pain, reaching for the ropes as Vesuvius applauds from ringside. Angelo manages to reach the ropes, grabbing the bottom rope to force the break. TJ breaks that and then heads to the ropes. He makes his way to the top rope, then lands a missile dropkick to the chin of Gerard and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! TJ Mercer pulls off the win. TJ rolls out of the ring to Vesuvius who pats him on the back.
Ray Dees - TJ impressed Anarchy's founder there. Henry Lesher - Look what he was up against though. Backstage we see Chastity with Corina and the two are drinking champagne. Chastity - Two down, one to go. It is all up to Kelly from here. Corina - And if she loses? Chastity - We love her anyway. Corina - Hey, I am just glad to have my win to get that monkey off my back. Kelly Buchanan vs Micheal Leary "Not Ready To Go" by The Trews plays as Kelly Buchanan makes her way to the ring. The crowd cheers her on as she steps through the ropes and poses on the second rope, cameras taking her picture from all over the packed arena. She then gets down to the mat as we hear "Lose Yourself" by Eminem and Micheal Leary makes his way to the ring, led by Shelia Lavin. Shelia has a few words of instruction for Micheal as they make their way down the ramp. Micheal then steps onto the ramp and then through the ropes. The bell sounds and the two lock up in the middle of the ring. Micheal shoves Kelly into the corner, then gives her a gutwrench suplex out of it. Micheal then picks Kelly up and gives her a sidewalk slam before going for an early pin. 1...2... kick out by Kelly.
Ray Dees - Micheal looking focussed here on the goal this week. Henry Lesher - He had better. His career is going nowhere fast. He is facing a Glamour Girl.
Micheal stands Kelly up, whips her off the ropes and then backdrops her to the mat. Micheal heads to the ropes, climbs to the second rope and then lands an elbow smash to the back of Kelly Buchanan before she has a chance to get up. Micheal then gives Kelly a pump handle slam to the mat and again goes for the pin. 1...2... Kelly gets a foot on the bottom rope to prevent the three count. Micheal then stands Kelly up. Kelly drops to her knees, giving Micheal a jawbreaker. She then shoves Micheal down to the mat and then lands a knee drop to the forehead on Leary before she tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Micheal Leary. Kelly stands Micheal up, whips him hard into the corner buckle and then gives him a snap suplex to the middle of the ring. Kelly then gets to her feet and says a few things to Shelia. Shelia beckons Kelly out to say those things to her face.
Ray Dees - Kelly showing some fire here this week. Henry Lesher - Makes sense. If her record gets any worse, she's fired. Kelly slides out of the ring and starts having words with Shelia. Micheal slips out of the ring after her and then turns Kelly around and they start trading ounches. Micheal tries to whip Kelly into the ring steps. Instead, she jumps onto the ring steps, turns and clotheslines Micheal off of them. Micheal grabs Kelly from the floor, rolls over and starts landing punches on her as the ref counts. The ref's count reaches ten and the bell sounds, both competitiors being counted out. Micheal gets off of Kelly and looks back at Shelia who is shaking her head. Kelly gets to her feet, short of breath. She and Micheal look at each other. The stand nose to nose. Kelly then backs off and offers her hand to Micheal. Micheal looks down at it, then at the crowd. He shakes hands with Kelly and the two go in their separate directions.
Ray Dees - That's rare. Henry Lesher - That's pathetic, too. Backstage we see Chastity and Corina high fiving each other, then leaving their champagne behind. Moments later, Ash and Angela enter the picture laughing. Ash sips from one still half full glass and Angela takes the other and smashes it against the locker door. Ash laughs to himself.
KillSwitch vs Pyro "I Am Hell" by Rob Zombie plays as KillSwitch comes out to the boos of the crowd. He makes his way down led by Starr and Jennifer. Starr holds the ropes open for KillSwitch to step through as he enters the ring. "When Darkness Falls" by KillSwitch Engage plays as Pyro comes out to a mixed reaction. Rumors of his demise appear to have been overstated. Pyro slides into the ring and KillSwitch starts putting the boots to him before he has a chance to get to his feet. KillSwitch then drops a series of forearm smashes across the back of Pyro's neck. KillSwitch then pulls Pyro by the hair, airplane spins him around the ring a few times and then lets him go, sending him flying into the corner. KillSwitch then picks Pyro up and power slams him to the mat and then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Pyro.
Ray Dees - Pyro showing some fight here. Henry Lesher - That wasn't fight. Those were death throws. KillSwitch stands Pyro up and whips him hard into the turnbuckle. KillSwitch then charges in shoulder first, but Pyro moves out of the way and KillSwitch's shoulder hits the corne rpost hard. Pyro then kicks KillSwitch in the ribs a few times before wrapping his arm around the top rope and then dropping to the mat, almost pulling it out of the socket. KillSwitch screams in agony as Pyro gets up, smile on his face. He starts landing a series of upper cuts on KillSwitch in the center of the ring. He then places KillSwitch into a hammerlock. Pyro picks KillSwitch up off the mat in the hammerlock. KillSwitch screams in pain, but does not submit. KillSwitch then falls forward and gives Pyro a snap mare to break the hold. KillSwitch then places Pyro into a sleeperhold. Pyro punches KillSwitch in the ribs to break it and then gives him a German suplex to the mat. KillSwitch then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Pyro. KillSwitch stands Pyro up, whips him off the ropes and then backdrops him to the mat. KillSwitch then heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top. He lands a moonsault on Pyro from there and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! KillSwitch pulls off the win over Pyro!
Ray Dees - All we have left now is the main event! Henry Lesher - I can hardly wait for that. Backstage we see Shelia Lavin with Gloria Lavin and they are on their way to the ring?! We hear "Crescendolls" by Daft Punk and Shelia Lavin leads Gloria Lavin to the ring. She slides into the ring and the crowd is hushed. The cage still hangs above the ring. Shelia has a microphone in hand.
Shelia Lavin - I have some good news and bad news. The good news is that Sunstroke is next week! *loud pop* The bad news is that it appears we have a problem in the main event tonight. Lethal Weapon and Ash again got into an altercation backstage and this match is again put off, screwing you fans out of your main event. *loud boos* Gloria and I are not here to announce the end of the roster split. We are here to announce there will be.... a new draft the night of Sunstroke! Yes fans! It is time to shake this place up! I don't give a damn what stable you are in or who you think you are! It is time for change, boys and girls!
Gloria Lavin - *grabbing the mic from Shelia* It is the only way to prevent what we saw here tonight from happening again. It is clear we need a shake up. We need to take control of our shows!
The two leave together and the crowd is left to boo the fact that cage will not beused tonight.
Ray Dees - What the hell happens now? Henry Lesher - I go to Chaos to commenate with Ricky, thankfully. Oh God, did I just say I want to commentate with him? I am ill, greatly ill!
Our show fades to black as we look up at the unused cage, now awaiting Sunstroke.
