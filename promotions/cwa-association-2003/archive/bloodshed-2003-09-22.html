CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Bloodshed, 22 September 2003

22 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The crowd in Edmonton stands and cheers as they see themselves on the Titan Tron. It's another full house for another huge CWA pay per view. Tonight there will be thirteen matches put on to amuse and entertain the CWA faithful.
------------------- Spikey Tiger vs Demetra ------------------- The crowd cheers as a local boy, Spikey Tiger makes his way out as "Eye of the Tiger" by Survivor plays. Spikey isn't wearing the standard Youthful Aggression T-shirt and doesn't have a smile on his face. Tonight is all business and it appears that things are once again changing for this young CWA star. "One Step Closer" by Linkin Park plays as Demetra makes her way to the ring with Pyro close behind her. Pyro, of course, left Youthful Aggression to join forces with Total Darkness last week. As Demetra steps through the ropes, Spikey and Pyro have words with each other.
Ricky Simms - We are wasting no time in getting this one underway. Tonight is going to be another incredible night for wrestling.
Henry Lesher - I don't know if I fully agree with that, but there are some interesting things happening behind the scenes as the 420 Flock appears to be regrouping.
The bell rings and Spikey locks up with Demetra. Spikey then picks her up, and gives her a fallaway slam and tries for a quick pin. 1...2... Demetra rolls a shoulder out ro break up the count. Spikey then stands her up, whips her to the ropes and lands a backdrop on her. Spikey follows this up with a leg drop across the throat and then tries again for the pin. 1...2... Demetra gets a foot on the bottom rope. Spikey then stands her up and, sets her up and gives her a belly to back suplex. He follows this up with a dropkick as she gets back to her feet, sending her through the ropes and the crowd appears to be enjoying this burst of energy from Spikey Tiger.
Ricky Simms - Demetra needs to get herself together here. Henry Lesher - This is not a pretty sight thus far for any fans of Demetra. Pyro helps Demetra to her feet, then she steps onto the apron. Pyro shouts something nasty towards Spikey and Tiger dives through the ropes, giving Pyro a la plancha into the guard railing. The crowd cheers, but their cheers fade when Demetra comes off of the apron and land a scissor kick to the back of the neck of Spikey. She then whips him into the steel steps head first. Demetra follows this up by rolling him into the ring and then climbing the ropes to the very top. Spikey gets to his feet and turns and catches a flying body press from Demetra which she then tries to turn into a pin. 1...2... Spikey kicks out. Demetra then stands Spikey up and whips him off of the ropes. Spikey ducks a clothesline and then picks Demetra up and gives her a power bomb and goes for the pin. 1...2....3! Spikey wins! No sooner has the ref's hand counted three than Pyro is inside the ring and the fight is on. The two trade punches and kicks as Demetra rolls out of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Pyro and Spikey are picking up where they left off last week. Henry Lesher - Spikey is in way over his head here. Total darkness is going to whip him into the ground.
Spikey and Pyro battles through the ropes and to the outside of the ring. Demetra then picks up a chair and clubs Spikey over the back of the head with it and then the two of them lay a beating on him at ringside as officials pour out of the back to try and break things up. Pyro and Demetra then leave Spikey in a heap and laugh to themselves as they look at the bloodied and fallen Spikey on the Titan Tron.
Henry Lesher - Bloodshed is living up to its name here. Ricky Simms - Pyro has a major match later tonight. I wonder if he's taking his eyes off of that just a bit by getting into it with Spikey here.
Backstage we see Sal Clemenzo talking with a few figues in the shadows and we see Q nearby. Tony Parizi's face then appears before the camera lens and Tony waves, then punches the camera and the shot turns to snow. There are things going on here tonight we're not supposed to know about.
Ricky Simms - Was Q meeting with Sal back there? Henry Lesher - Why don't you go down and ask them? Ricky Simms - No thanks. ---------------------- Luke Criston vs Caveman ---------------------- The Indiana Jones theme plays as Luscious Luke Criston makes his way down the ramp. The fans boo him while others laugh at the entrance music. As Luke reaches the ringside area, he is attacked from behind by Caveman. Caveman tosses him into the ringsteps and then rolls him into the ring, Caveman climbs to the top rope and then lands a five star frog splash on Luke as the match officially begins. The ref counts the pin. 1...2...3! Caveman wins!
Ricky Simms - What the hell? Henry Lesher - If only all filler matches were this short. I think the Indiana Jones music made him suck. That's my theory. Caveman picks up another win here.
Caveman grabs the microphone from the ring announcer as she announces him the winner. Caveman - Who gives a damn if I beat Indiana Jobber here? I made a challenge this week and I hear someone had the nerve to step up to the plate so let's go, Romeo! Show me what a GM can do against a real star like me!
"P.I.M.P." by 50 Cent blasts as Romeo Salvador walks down the ramp and laughs as he steps into the ring, decked out in a fine black suit and with his hair slicked back. He takes the mic from Caveman.
Romeo Salvador - So you think you have what it takes to beat me because you beat this little bitch down? You think you have what it takes to hang with me puta? I know you've been on a good run lately, but you call me out and that ends puta.
Caveman - *yanks the mic away from Romeo* You talk the talk but do you... Caveman never has a chance to finish that setence as Romeo starts throwing rights and lefts to the head and chest of Caveman, then sends him to the ropes and dropkicks him to the mat. Romeo gets to his feet as Caveman slides out of the ring. Romeo motions him inside, but Caveman shakes his head and says no. Romeo picks up the mic again.
Romeo Salvador - Looking a little yellow there, homes! Now, next week on Breakthrough, you and me are going to fight and it's going to get nasty! Hard core rules, falls count anywhere puta! You'll wish you never called me out!
Romeo throws the mic down as caveman retreats up the ramp. Romeo helps Luke Criston to his feet, then gives him a Rock Bottom to the delight of the capacity crowd. Romeo then points at the fallen Luke and tells Caveman that'll be him next week.
Ricky Simms - Romeo accepted the challenge with a major exclamation point here tonight! Henry Lesher - Caveman and Romeo, that might be a good fight. I may actually tune in to see that.
We see backstage that Vesuvius is talking to someone. We cannot make out who it is and as the cameraman moves to get into a better position to observe the meeting, the door is shut in his face. He's not having a good night for digging up the dirt.
------------------- Charley Moore vs Mini Sal ------------------- As we hear tommyguns fire and the crowd expects to see Mini Sal making his way to the ring, we instead see backstage as Charley Moore and Mini Sal brawl in the locker room. Charley picks the midget up and gives him a swirlie in the nearby toilet. Mini Sal then low blows Charley and then slams a locker door into the forehead of Charley Moore. Sal then picks up a shovel which Charley apparently brought with him tonight and clubs him in the face with it. Mini Sal then breaks it across the back of Charley Moore. Mini Sal then drags Charley out of the locker room into the hallway and slams him into the wall. Charley then is stood up by Sal. Charley headbutts Sal down, then starts kicking away at the midget with all the power. Charley picks Mini Sal up and body slams him on the floor. Charley follows this up by dragging Mini Sal up the stairs and onto the stage where the crowd cheers when they see the two combatants.
Ricky Simms - The fans are apparently eager to see this one. Henry Lesher - Which is more than I can handle. Charley is a sicko and Sal is... well... weird.
Charley picks Mini Sal up and runs at the wall with him over his shoulder. Mini Sal slides off and then rams Charley into the wall head first. Mini Sal then climbs onto a nearby speaker and lands a nice diving DDT on Charley from there. Mini Sal then makes his way to the ramp and starts making his way down to the ring to the cheers of the fans. Charley stumbles as he gets to his feet. He then makes his way towards the ring as Sal rolls into it and beckons Charley to enter. Charley steps onto the apron and Mini Sal races at him and shoulder blocks his knees through the rope. Charley falls off of the apron and his face bounces off of it as he hits the floor. Charley lands against the guard railing.
Ricky Simms - Charley appears to be having some difficulty in getting into the ring. Henry Lesher - Judging by the smell from here, Charley may also have been having problems finding deodorant.
Ricky Simms - I wish you were kidding about that, but the smell is enough to curl your hair.
Mini Sal climbs to the top rope and dives off, landing a dropkick to the chin of Charley Moore. Mini Sal then stands Charley up and rolls him back into the ring. Mini Sal then signals that he is heading up the ropes once again. Charley gets to his feet slowly, turns and sees Sal jumping. Charley catches Sal in mid air and lands a spinning power slam on Mini Sal from there. He goes for the pin. 1...2... Mini Sal rolls out. Charley then gets to his feet, picks Mini Sal up and gives him an airplane spin for three circles and then tosses him to the opposite corner. Charley then signals that this might be the end of Mini Sal here. Mini Sal gets to his feet and takes a spear by Charley Moore. Charley attempts the pin. 1...2... Mini Sal gets a foot on the bottom rope.
Ricky Simms - Mini Sal's streak nearly ended there. Henry Lesher - No mention of the word streak when Charley Moore is inside the ring, please. No one wants to see that or suggest it to the guy.
Charley Moore stands Mini Sal up, then hip tosses him to the middle of the ring. He then hip tosses him again before placing him in an arm bar which is quickly reversed by Mini Sal. Mini Sal then heads to the ropes and climbs to the second rope. He dives off, attemtping a shoulder block from there. Charley moved out of the way and Mini Sal lands face down on the mat. Charley then places Mini Sal in a Camel Clutch, though he would call it the Camel Flush. Mini Sal reaches for the ropes, screaming in pain. Finally, his back appears to be able to take no more and he is forced to tap out. Charley gets to his feet and raises his arms in victory while the ref gently raises the arm and then takes off from the smell.
Henry Lesher - Great, stinky smurf is now the guy on the unbeaten run. Ricky Simms - Impressive victory here for Charley Moore over a very game Mini Sal. Henry Lesher - Oh, it's gamey all right... Backstage, we see Lunacy Muse making her way around the backstage area when she is approached by Joe Davidson.
Joe Davidson - Lunacy, what kind of plans do you and Dave Morbid have in mind for Shannon tonight?
Lunacy Muse - *through a forced smile* Now, Joe, what kind of person would I be if I gave all of my ideas up to a CWA yokel with a microphone? Everyone is just going to have to wait and see what I have in mind here tonight.
She walks away and leaves Joe there with a disappointed look on his face. We're not sure why he would expect Lunacy to open up to him, but he was clearly expecting that.
--------------------------------- Outkast vs Giovanni Mabius --------------------------------- The CWA faithful get to their feet when they hear "Wake Up Hate" by Korn and they see Outkast making his way to the ring for tonight's debut match up. He steps through the ropes and the fans have a luke warm reaction to him. "Here Comes the Money" starts to play as Giovanni Mabius makes his way down the ramp with a sly smile on his face. He steps onto the apron and Outkast looks ready to take a run at him. Mabius drops off the stairs to the floor, but otherwise appears to be unconcerned.
Ricky Simms - Quite the look to this match up. Another new CWA star inside the ring against a very new star as well.
Henry Lesher - You build up these pointless matches so well, Ricky. I am impressed. Mabius steps through the ropes and the two men lock up as the bell sounds. We are underway and Mabius starts by placing Outkast in an arm bar. Outkast reverses it only to take an elbow to the head from Mabius. Mabius then whips OutKast off of the ropes and levels him with a lariat. Mabius then picks Outkast up and gives him a body slam. Mabius comes off the ropes and tries for a rolling thunder, but Outkast moves aside and quickly slaps Mabius into a head scissors sleeper. Mabius then rolls onto his head and flips over, landing on Outkast for an awkward pin, but it's a pin nonetheless. 1...2... kick out by Outkast. Giovanni Mabius stands Outkast up, whips him off of the ropes and Outkast leap frogs Mabius. Outkast then turns and gives Mabius a Twist of Fate. Outkast tries for the quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Mabius.
Ricky Simms - That was nearly a win for Outkast right there. Henry Lesher - Don't bother a man when he's trying to sleep, Rick. Outkast picks Mabius up and gives him a power slam. Outkast then goes to the ropes and starts to climb. He reaches the top and makes an attempt at a shooting star press. Instead, he lands face down on the mat as Mabius rolls away. Mabius then kips up to his feet and waits for Outkast to get up. Mabius then plants him with a spear. Mabius then picks Outkast up over his shoulders and delivers The Hit. He goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Giovanni Mabius is the winner this week!
Ricky Simms - Nice win here for Mabius as he continues to push himself forward in the CWA. Henry Lesher - Yeah, the kid's the next world champ. Whatever. Bring on the next match, please.
Backstage we see The Legend with Charley MacAdam as they discuss tonight's King of the Pay Oer View match.
Charley MacAdam - Tonight you make a one match only return to the CWA. Why now Legend? Legend - Why not now? 420 is coming back strong here in the CWA. Ash is going to embarrass that ass clown, Q. Fact is, this is the best time for me to step into that ring, grab a trophy and head back home.
Charley MacAdam - So there's no truth to the rumor you're close to signing here> Legend - Sign here for what? I am a BLPW God now. I don't need the scraps that the CWA gives me. I came here to take something from people and then leave the same way I came in. It's actually pretty simple when you think about it logically.
Charley MacAdam - What if you're left alone in the ring with Angela, a girlfriend of a 420 Flock member?
Legend - Then she'll see why she should have stayed out of my match tonight. Legend leaves the frame and Charley is left to shrug as we return to ringside for our next match up.
------------------ Puppet Master vs Shelia Lavin ------------------ The crowd boos when they see Puppet Master on his way to the ring in dimmed light. Their reaction does not change as Shelia Lavin races out of the back and starts drilling forearm shots to the back of the neck of the much taller Puppet Master. Puppet Master turns, picks Shelia Lavin up and gives her a body slam on the steel ramp. Puppet Master then drags Shelia by the hair towards the ring and drops her next to the guard railing before boot choking her there. Puppet Master then stands Shelia up and whips her into the ring steps. The crowd's booing hasn't gotten any less heartfelt thus far. Puppet Master rolls Shelia into the ring, then steps onto the apron and finally over the top rope as the bell sounds and we are officially underway here.
Ricky Simms - Big match here for these two. Damien and BasketKase pulled no shows tonight. Henry Lesher - Typical fare for BasketKase lately. He hasn't been focussed ever since he started with the drugs and crap. I have never been less impressed.
Inside the ring, Puppet Master whips Shelia Lavin into the corner buckle. He then charges into the buckle and shoulder blocks her there. Puppet Master follows this up with a hip toss that sends Shelia to the other side of the ring. Puppet Master then whips Shelia off of the ropes and drops her with a backdrop to the mat. Puppet Master then places Shelia into a Boston Crab. The ref asks Shelia if she submits and she refuses to. Shelia then squirms and claws her way to the ropes and grabs the bottom rope. The ref forces the break as Shelia clings to the bottom rope. Puppet Master releases the hold and then stands Shelia up. He takes a gouge of the eyes from Shelia followed by another and then a headbutt which sets Puppet Master on his heels. Puppet Master then gets whipped off the ropes and Shelia cartwheels into a head scissors which takes Puppet Master down. Puppet Master then takes a leg drop from Shelia. Shelia then gets to her feet and smiles. She places Puppet Master in the Lavin lock to the delight of the very few fans that she does have. Puppet Master reaches for the ropes, but fails to reach them and the ref calls for the bell.
Ricky Simms - Impressive come back for the win by Shelia here. Henry Lesher - The sloppy girl wins again. As Shelia stands in the ring and raises her arms in victory, "Bitch" by Meredith Brooks begins to play and we then see Scarlet with the Women's title over her shoulder and a microphone in her hand.
Scarlet Bitch - Hey Shelia, nice win. Next week we'll see what you really have to offer when you and me square off for this belt. Enjoy tonight, because when the truth is revealed tonight, you'll see why you haven't got a hope in hell next week.
Shelia laughs when she hears this, even as Scarlet heads to the back again. Shelia turns and Puppet Master has gotten back to his feet and he gives Shelia a spine buster to the mat which shakes the ring. Puppet Master then rolls out of the ring and raises his own arms in mock victory. The crowd doesn't appear to be any more impressed with him than they are with the always hated Shelia Lavin.
Ricky Simms - I look forward to that Women'd title match next week. Henry Lesher - It won't be a pretty match. That's for sure. We hear "If You Want Blood, You Got It" and we see a montage of Moose's greatest moments in a CWA ring. There is then flaming text before the screen which states "returning soon...." and then there is an explosion of pyro within Skyreach Centre and the capacity crowd cheers both that display and the fact that the former CWA champ is returning soon.
Ricky Simms - It's going to be great seeing Moose inside the ring again. Henry Lesher - If you say so. -------------------- Hitoni Kiashi vs Demona Calaway -------------------- The fans cheer as "C'Mon" by Regurgitator plays and Hitoni Kiashi makes her way to the ring. She steps through the ropes and poses for the crowd in the corner. As she goes to the opposite corner for more posing, "I Want To Be A Writer" by Idlewind begins to play and the crowd also has a strong reaction to Demona Calaway as she makes her way to the ring. Demona slides into the ring and then gets to her feet quickly and stands nose to chest with Hitoni Kiashi. The bell rings and Hitoni throws a karate chop to the chest of Demona that sends her flying to the corner. Hitoni then gives Demona a monkey flip out of the corner and follows this with an attempt at a bulldog. Demona shoves Hitoni into the corner and then gives her a German suplex as she comes out of it.
Ricky Simms - Both girls doing well early on here. Henry Lesher - There is an abundance of women on this roster right now. It's quite upsetting.
Demona places Hitoni in a full Nelson and holds on for dear life, hoping for a submission by the huge Asian female. Instead, Hitoni begins to power out of it and hip tosses Demona to the middle of the ring. Hitoni then levels Demona with a dropkick to the chin while she's down. Hitoni tries for the pin. 1...2... Demona kicks out! Hitoni brings Demona to her feet, whips her off of the ropes and shoulder blocks her down. Hitoni then stands Demona up again, comes off the ropes and lands a scissor kick on Demona and again tries for the pin. 1...2... again Demona manages to kick out. Hitoni picks Demona up and attempts to place her in position for a Tombstone piledriver. Demona rolls out of it, somersaults onto her feet and then dropkicks Hitoni through the ropes. Demona then climbs to the top rope, leaps off and lands a body press on Hitoni. Hitoni then stops herself from falling down and power slams Demona through the Spanish announce table, using her own momentum against her. The HOLY SHIT chants begin there.
Ricky Simms - I think she might have broken Demona in half! Henry Lesher - That was fun to watch. Hitoni rolls into the ring and then walks around the ring as if to assume she has it won and demona has no hope of getting up from that fall. However, Demona fights to her feet and staggers to the ropes and drags herself into the ring. Hitoni is shocked, but starts putting the boots to Demona. Hitoni then stands her up and readies for a power bomb. Demona manages to backdrop Hitoni Kiashi instead and then places her in the Demonic Possession. Hitoni reaches for the ropes as the crowd cheers Demona on.
Ricky Simms - Where on Earth did Demona find the energy for this? Hitoni manages to grab the bottom rope and the ref forces a break of the hold. Demona then stands Hitoni up, whips her off of the ropes and both ladies clothesline each other down to the mat. Neither of them show much sign of movement as the ref starts counting them both down. He reaches a four count before Hitoni rolls over first onto Demona for the pin. 1...2... Demona reverses into a small package. 1...2... Hitoni kicks out. As she does, we see Shelia Lavin coming out of the back. She has a sign that reads "I am a winner. Face Reality" written on it. Hitoni gets to her feet and the ref goes to Shelia about being out here again. As Shelia has a few words with the ref, Q slides into the ring and nails Hitoni in the chin with a set of brass knuckles. He then turns, shrugs and nails Demona with them as well. He places Demona on top of Hitoni and rolls out of the ring as Shelia finally agrees to leave the ringside area.
Ricky Simms - The two snakes strike again. Henry Lesher - They are winners. Even you have to admit that. The ref counts the pin. 1...2...3! Demona wins, with a heavy assist and concussion thanks to Q.
Ricky Simms - Shelia will pay for that. Backstage we see Lunacy Muse with Joe Davidson. Lunacy has that sadistic smile that's never good for anyone.
Lunacy Muse - You know, that was a heinous attack. I think we have a triple threat match next week for the women's title now. Demona will face Shelia and Scarlet and the three will tear each other to shreds with my blessing.
Joe Davidson - We will be looking forward to that. Let's head back to the ring. ---------------------- 6Pac Dudley vs Venom ---------------------- "Poison" by Alice Cooper plays as Venom makes his way to the ring to a loud negative reaction. He slides into the ring and salutes himself as the fans continue to boo his presence inside the ring. We then hear "Southtown" by POD and we see 6Pac Dudley on his way to the ring with a Canadian flag over his shoulder and a gleam in his eye. Edmonton starts a 6Pac chant before he's even all the way down the ramp. He hands his flag to an audio hand at ringside and then steps into the ring and the bell sounds. Venom and 6Pac circle each other and then lock up.
Ricky Simms - Big grudge match here between these two. Henry Lesher - Which I hear is no DQ. Ricky Simms - And that just breaks your black heart, doesn't it? 6Pac tosses Venom over the top rope to the outside of the ring. Venom crashes into the guard railing as 6Pac climbs the ropes. 6Pac dives off, landing a shotting star press on Venom outside the ring. 6Pac then rolls Venom back into the ring and climbs the ropes yet again. The crowd cheers as 6Pac tries for a second shooting star only to have Venom move aside. Venom then gets to his feet and lands axe handle smashes 6Pac repeatedly. Venom then picks 6Pac up and gives him a gutwrench suplex. Venom follows this up by giving 6Pac a DDT to the mat and trying for the pin. 1...2... kick out by 6Pac much to the delight of the biased Canadian crowd. Venom whips 6Pac off of the ropes. 6Pac ducks a clothesline and round house kicks Venom to the head. 6Pac then gives Venom the Razor's Edge and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Venom!
Ricky Simms - Great action here as these two fight it out. 6Pac slides out of the ring and grabs a chair. He folds it, slides it under the bottom rope and steps through the ropes. 6Pac takes the chair and swings it, connecting with the skull of Venom. Venom drops like a ton of bricks and 6Pac makes the pin. 1...2... Venom gets a foot on the bottom rope. 6Pac stands Venom up, then gives him a tilt a whirl backbreaker and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Venom. 6Pac then stands Venom up, whips him to the ropes and takes a dropkick from Venom for his efforts. Venom tries for a pin, placing his feet on the second rope. The ref doesn't notice. 1...2... 6Pac kicks out! We then see Max Cameron racing into the ring. He grabs Venom by the shoulder and turns him around. The two shove each other as 6Pac gets to his feet. 6Pac looks confused and then takes a double roundhouse kick to the chin from both Max and Venom!
Henry Lesher - This is when refs get really mad because there are no rules. There isn't a damned thing they can do about it.
Ricky Simms - There is nothing the ref can do about this situation for sure. Max and Venom whip 6Pac off of the ropes and pick him up high above their heads and then toss him onto the guard railing at ringside. Max and venom then go to opposing corners of the ring and start climbing the ropes. As they do, PB FUBU and Lightning emerge from the back. FUBU tosses Venom off the top as Lightning enters the ring and pummels away at Max Cameron. Lightning then climbs the ropes, and gives Max a belly to back suplex from the top rope to the mat. FUBU is outside the ring, picks up Venom and drops him face first to the ramp. He then rolls Venom into the ring and heads to the ropes. As Lightning rolls Max outside the ring to continue the punishment, FUBU lands a flying elbow smash to the chest of Venom. 6 Pac then rolls into the ring, high fives FUBU and gives Venom the Bottle Smasher and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! 6Pac wins!
Henry Lesher - Every match should have that quality to it. Ricky Simms - That was an electrifying match up. This feud has expanded and isn't over/ Max cameron has some sort of affiliation with Venom.
Northern Exposure all wave the Canadian flag as they head to the back and Max and Venom try to regroup. The crowd understandably eats this up. We then shift to backstage where Vesuvius stands with a poster before him. He grabs a young CWA staffer by the neck and shows him the poster while not showing the camera what's on the front of it.
Vesuvius - What do you think? Staffer - Wow, you're adding them to the group? Anarchy is back! Vesuvius - Thanks. Now, shut up and leave me alone, kid. You bother me. Vesuvius shoves the staffer out of the shot and looks at the poster again and smiles. ---------------------- Katt vs Annalyse vs Ophelia ---------------------- "Closer" by Nine Inch Nails plays as Ophelia is led to the ring by Cindy Knox. She gets a warm reception as usual and blows a kiss or two at crowd members who hold up signs proclaiming their love for one half of the Glamour Girls. As they step into the ring, we hear "Lost" by Suzi play and Annalyse makes her way to the ring to a less enthused crowd response. Annalyse is half way to the ring when she is attacked from behind by Katt. Ophelia then races out of the ring and all three girls tangle on the ramp.
Ricky Simms - Katt never seems to be one concerned with entering the ring in the usual manner.
Henry Lesher - That's a good thing. I think good things are coming for the Murderdolls. Katt tosses Ophelia off the ramp and Ophelia lands in the front row. Katt then drags Annalyse by the hair to ringside where Annalyse grabs Katt's hair and gives her a reverse neckbreaker. Annalyse then rolls into the ring as Ophelia climbs onto the guard railing. Ophelia dives onto Katt and cross bodies her to the floor. Ophelia then climbs the steps and gets into the ring herself. Her and Annalyse start throwing rights and lefts and then Annalyse whips Ophelia off of the ropes and levels her with a backdrop to the mat. Ophelia then grabs Annalyse by the feet and then pulls her down into a roll up for a pin. 1...2... kick out by Annalyse. Ophelia then gets to her feet, pulls Annalyse to the corner and then climbs to bulldog her from the second rope. Katt grabs the feet of Ophelia and she crumples in the corner and Annalyse starts putting the boots to her.
Ricky Simms - Katt showing some intelligence right there. Henry Lesher - She's one of the smarter wrestlers we have in the CWA. Katt enters the ring and starts sparring with Annalyse. Katt quickly takes over with a series of chops to the chest of Annalyse. She then whips Annalyse off the ropes and levels her with a clothesline follows by a leg drop and an attempted pin. 1...2... kick out by Annalyse. Katt gets to her feet and takes a dropkick to the back of the head from Ophelia. Ophelia then picks Katt up and gives her a German suplex. She then tries for a second, but Katt blocks it, elbows Ophelia in the head and then gives her a snap mare to the middle of the ring. Katt then takes a Russian leg sweep from Annalyse. Annalyse then slaps on a scorpion deathlock. The crowd cheers as Katt appears close to tapping out. Ophelia then takes Annalyse into a headlock and DDTs her. Ophelia tries for a pin on Annalyse. 1...2... Katt breaks it up, rolls Ophelia up with a handful of tights and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Katt wins and rolls out of the ring with her arms raised in celebration. Ophelia gets to her feet right away and argues her case to the ref who simply shrugs and shakes his head when she asks for a restart.
