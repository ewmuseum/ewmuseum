Frank pulls Dizz up, and holds him while Wren hits the Reckless Abandon on Dizz! Sinman pulls Leech up, and The Blood Drain onto him! Max grabs Moose, and hits the Moosedriver 1 onto him! And Ash pulls Vigilante up, and nails the Catch 22 on him. Q hands the mic to Myst, who looks down at Shannon.
Myst - This is for your own good Shannon. I hope, one day, you’ll see that. Until then, watch your back. I'm done playing second fiddle to these idiots.
Myst looks up at the booing fans, and shakes her head at their boos. "Black Number One" by Type O Negative plays and she hands the microphone to Ash, who looks down at Vigilante. Myst's music dies down and she stops half way up the ramp to look back into the ring at Ash's parting words.
Ash - I told you Vigilante. A new era has arrived. Ash then drops the microphone onto Vigilante as The State leaves to “The Thing I Hate” by Stabbing Westward. We fade out on the 5 men, laid out in the ring and we are all wondering what the hell is going on.
