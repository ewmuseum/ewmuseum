CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 2 February 2004

2 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Portland, Oregon is the home of Assault this week. The crowd cheers as they see themselves on the big screen. They know this means that the show is about to begin. This signals the beginning of the opening credits and we see images of Vigilante, Dizz, Moose, The Flock and Q before we head to ringside to get the action underway.
Gothic Angel vs Chastity "Naked Cousin" by PJ Harvey plays as Chastity makes her way to the ring. Before she's half way down the ramp, we see her attacked by Jakob of Total Mayhem. Jakob picks Chastity up and throws her to the floor from the ramp. He then pulls Chastity by the hair to ringside and then slams her head into the ring steps before rolling her into the ring. Jakob then escapes by leaving over the ring railing. The crowd boos. We then head "Right Round" by Dope and we see Gothic Angel running to the ring.
Ricky Simms - What the hell did Jakob do that for? Henry Lesher - Cause he felt like it, maybe? Gothic Angel steps into the ring and the bell sounds. She goes over to Chastity, picks up her arm and watches it drop. She looks at the ref and he calls for the bell. Chastity is not fit to continue and could be really badly hurt here. Gothic Angel is declared the winner by superior decision. Her music starts to play and she doesn't look pleased with the net result here. We then see Gloria Lavin appear at the top of the ramp with a microphone in hand. The crowd cheers her.
Gloria Lavin - Man, what the hell was that? Jakob and his little buddies in Total Mayhem are suspended for that stunt. Now you are left here wondering what to do. Well, odds are you would have beat Chastity anyways, so next week you face Angela inside that ring for a chance to become the number one contender for the ladies' title. Have yourself a great night.
Gothic Angel looks pleased with that announcement. Backstage, the vulture and sleazeball Ray Dees is with Angela.
Ray Dees - Looks like you get a shot at being number one contender next week. Angela - And my opponent for this week is suspended. The CWA really needs to get its act together here. Next week, I'll show Gothic Angel why I am the next women's champion in the CWA.
Ray Dees - Any words for her? Angela - Yeah, what I just said. Weren't you listening? Ray Dees - Partly I was. The other part of me was staring at your breasts. Angela slaps Ray in the face and walks off as he holds his cheek and appears to be near the point of crying.
Shelia Lavin vs Harmony Fallacia "Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger" by Daft Punk plays as Shelia Lavin makes her way to the ring. She hears the boos of the crowd which, as usual, don't appear to bother her in the least. She steps onto the apron and looks into the ring at the ref and has a few words for him before she even gets into the ring. Once inside the ring, "Heads Expode" by Monster Magnet begins to play and Harmony Fallacia begins to make her way to the ring. She receives a negative response much the same as Shelia did. Harmony then steps into the ring and the bell sounds to get this match, basically our first of three this week to have a real start, underway. The two lock up in the center of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Word has it that Chastity sustained a concussion from the attack by Jakob. Henry Lesher - That's a crying shame for her. Whatever. Next week I have to watch Gothic Angel and Angela in the ring together to decide who gets the nest Women's title shot? This truly is sad.
Shelia overpowers Harmony and flings her into the corner buckle. She then lands a series of body shots on her before eye gouging her and then hip tossing her to the middle of the ring. Shelia then places Harmony into a sleeperhold as she tries to wear the 420 Flock member down. The ref checks on Harmony as she fades. He picks her arm up once and it falls. He picks it up a second time and Harmony manages to hold it up to prevent the loss early here. Harmony then battles to get back to her feet as the fans cannot decide who to cheer for... or was that who they hate more right now? Harmony albows Shelia in the ribs to loosen the hold before giving her a snap mare to the mat. Harmony then stands Shelia up, whips her off the ropes and dropkicks her to the mat. Harmony then heads to the ropes and climbs to the second rope. She dives off, landing a flying elbow smash to the chest and going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Shelia Lavin.
Ricky Simms - Near fall there for Harmony Fallacia. Good match up thus far. Henry Lesher - Yeah, Angela wins by forfeit and Gothic Angel wins by the attack of the forfeiter. Now we have Harmony against Shelia. Who's booking this crap?
Ricky Simms - Nice to see the positive Lesher attitude this fine Monday night. Harmony stands Shelia up, then whips her off the ropes. Shelia ducks a clothesline and turns and lands a reverse neckbreaker on Harmony and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Harmony Fallacia. The crowd appears to be leaning towards Harmony right now. Shelia picks Harmony up and body slams her to the mat. She then gives Harmony a leg drop and goes for the pin again. 1...2... Harmony gets a foot on the bottom rope. Shelia then rolls Harmony out of the ring. She climbs to the top rope as Harmony gets to her feet. Shelia lands a flying cross body on Harmony on the floor as fans in that area cheer. Shelia then stands Harmony up and rams her, head first, into the guard railing. She then rolls Harmony back into the ring before entering the ring herself. Harmony slowly gets to her feet, turns and takes sweet chin music from Shelia. Shelia goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Our winner is Shelia Lavin.
Ricky Simms - Typical no nonsense win for Shelia Lavin this week. Henry Lesher - Maybe next week she can fight a match that really matters. Gloria Lavin appears at the top of the stage again. She's having a busy week as she is in charge of the show.
Gloria Lavin - Well if it isn't my ex-wife. Now, next week is going to be a big week for you, Shelia. You see you, get your shot at the world title. Now I couldn't allow it to be a simple match because I know how much this title shot means to you. Therefore, the title shot is going to be within a steel cage with no DQ! *loud pop from the crowd* So get ready for fun because Moose is going to be holding the key to the cage door. He gets to face the winner of the match at Malicious Intent March 1st! *another loud cheer*
Ricky Simms - Wow, what a great match that will be! Henry Lesher - Seems to me Gloria wants to watch her ex get hurt. This is wrong. Reality will never get a fair shake with Gloria helping to run things.
Backstage we see Frank Merritt with Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - Tonight the Flock goes in there against EOS. Can I have your thoughts on this match and how you feel things have been going for the Flock?
Frank Merritt - Look, this has been a long week as I have been waiting to show those jokers their place at the top is over. I am going to walk with Ash and Leech into that ring and show EOS a thing or two.
Joe Davidson - Which member of the group do you think has the best chance to pull Vigilante down as world champ?
Frank Merritt - Any of us could do it. Once he stops ducking us and faces us with the gold on the line, we'll show him a thing or two.
MLSQ42 vs Menace "Stunt 101" by G Unit plays as Menace comes out from the back to resounding boos from the crowd. Portland appears to know who they don't like as much as Baltimore recently appeared to love darned near everybody. As Menace walks down the ramp, he is clubbes with a clothesline to the back of the neck from an attacking Q who appears to be in a bad mood tonight as he puts the boots to Menace. He then stands Menace up and whips him hard into the side of the ring. Menace's ribs bounce off the ring and he then turns and takes a series of forearms to the chin from Q who then rams his head into the apron before rolling him into the ring. Q pulls himself onto the apron and then steps over the top rope into the ring.
Henry Lesher - Q, as always, a wrestler after my own heart. Who needs entrances? Not him! Ricky Simms - At least with Dante gone, I only have to hear this once every now and then. The bell now sounded and this match begun, Q whips Menace hard into the corner. He then gives him an atomic drop after that. Menace lands hard on his back in the ring, not able to regroup at all yet. Q then picks Menace up and power slams him to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... Menace manages to kick out. Q then stands Menace up, whips him off the ropes and both men clothesline each other to the mat. Menace then rolls himself out of the ring to try and gather his strength to fight back here against Q. Q rolls himself out of the ring just moments late and chrges straight into a shoulder block by Menace which launches Q into the steel corner post, nailing his spine against the unforgiving surface. Menace then clotheslines Q to the floor. He follows this up by ramming Q's head into the ring steps before rolling him back inside the ring. Menace grabs Q by the hair, pulls him part way to his feet and then connects with a haymaker to the jaw. He goes for the pin. 1...2... Q manages to roll a shoulder out before the count can reach three.
Ricky Simms - Great match thus far. Henry Lesher - Q only has good matches. This is going to be the highlight of the night for me.
Menace stands Q up, whips him off the ropes and attempts a clothesline which Q ducks. Q then lands a choke slam on Menace which shakes the ring to its very foundation. The crowd seems impressed by this as Q grabs Menace around the throat, picks him up and choke slams him again. Q then stands Menace up, gives the crowd a thumbs down and lands the Q Jumper on Menace and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Q wins the match here this week!
Henry Lesher - And Q silences yet another opponent here this week. Nice work. Ricky Simms - I must say Q does a good job here this week. He wins clean and the fans appear to be enjoying watching him do it.
Henry Lesher - Did it hurt to admit that? Do you need a nap? Ricky Simms - Oh please now... Backstage we see Vigilante with Ray Dees. Ray Dees - Vig, tonight you have the six man tag and next week you have Shelia Lavin inside a steel cage with Moose holding the key. How do you feel about that?
Vigilante - I feel great. Now, about you having a show in the CWA, that's kind of my job and I am tired of people ripping my idea off.
Ray Dees - I see. Well my show will be... Vigilante - Crap, I know. You know Vig rules just like the rest know and you all wish you were Vig. I am the CWA champ and an award winning CWA superstar. Deal with that.
Ray Dees - You going to settle the score with the Flock here tonight, once and for all? Vigilante - As I have learned over the past year, there is no once and for all in the CWA. There are temporary cease fires. That's it. I don't see the Flock and EOS being done with each other for a long time yet. Oh, and I rock.
Rated R vs Dizz The crowd cheers as "Nobody's Listening" by Linkin Park plays and they see three time world champion Dizz on his way to the ring. Dizz jumps onto the apron and then steps through the ropes and into the ring. As Dizz has a few word with the ref, we hear "I Hate Everything About You" by Three Days Grace and Rated R makes his way to the ring to a strong reaction as well. Rated R high fives a few fans on his way to the ring. He then steps through the ropes and the bell sounds to get the match started. The fans cheer as the two lock up. Dizz drops down and monkey flips Rated R into the corner. Dizz then quickly gets to his feet and then arm drags Rated R across the ring and into an arm bar.
Ricky Simms - Dizz in the ring building a strong record. It won't be long till we hear about him going for the world title yet again.
Henry Lesher - Why? Just so he can lose it a week or two later? If we wanted a champ like that, we'd pull someone from the Warriors roster.
Rated R breaks the arm bar and then rakes the eyes of Dizz and dropkicks him. Rated R then body slams Dizz to the mat, climbs to the top rope and lands a moonsault on Dizz and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Dizz. Rated R then pulls Dizz to his feet, whips him into the corner and gives him a German suplex out of it. He then tries again for the pin1 1....2... Dizz again manages to kick out.
Ricky Simms - Dizz refusing to allow the pin here. Henry Lesher - Wouldn't be much of a wrestler if he allowed himself to be pinned, idiot. Rated R stands Dizz up and whips him into the corner. Dizz and Rated R then clothesline each other to the mat. Dizz uses the ropes to get to his feet first. Dizz places Rated R in a headlock and punches him in the throat. Rated R then gives Dizz a side suplex to the mat to break the headlock. Rated R then picks Dizz up for a power slam. Dizz slides down the back of Rated R's shoulder and then shoves him hard into the corner buckle. He elbows Rated R in the back twice, turns him around and gives him a snap suplex to the mat. Dizz then gives Rated R a gutwrench suplex and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Rated R. Dizz then stands Rated R up and whips him off the ropes and lands a backdrop on him. Dizz then stands Rated R up and gives him the Reckless Abandon. He goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Rated R. Dizz looks shocked at Rated R kicking out of that move. Dizz looks at the ref who insists it was simply a two count.
Ricky Simms - Rated R kicked out of that? Henry Lesher - Yeah, so what? Dizz stands Rated R up and whips him off the ropes. Rated R ducks a clothesline and then gives Dizz a spinebuster. As they continue to battle, we see Leech emerge from the back. He has a chair in hand and walks down the aisle to the ring, shaking his head. He steps up onto the apron as Dizz comes over to tell Leech to get the hell away from the ring. Leech smiles and clocks Dizz with the chair which causes the ref to ask for the bell. The winner by DQ is Dizz and Rated R is not happy about it. Rated R slips out of the ring, though, backing off while keeping his eyes on Leech the entire time. Rated R pulls up a chair at ringside. Leech steps into the ring and appears not to notice Rated R didn't leave. Leech grabs the microphone from ring announcer, Donny Rave.
Leech - Now we can get to the real wrestling match as the Flock cleans out EOS! Ricky Simms - Leech doesn't see Rated R sitting there. Henry Lesher - Might be a large oversight there. Ricky Simms - I'd say so. Leech, Ash, Frank Merritt vs Vigilante, Max Cameron, Moose "The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson plays as Ash and Frank Merritt appear at the top of the ramp with Angela in tow behind them. The two pat each other on the back for their respective greatness as the crowd boos them mercilessly. It's going to be another big match here in the main event. They step into the ring. They pose for the crowd, to each other. Leech high fives them both and then tosses the chair he nailed Dizz with out of the ring. Dizz also has not left the area. He has actually taken a spot in the crowd, sitting in the front row. As the Flock stands, bathe in a green light inside the ring, we hear "Back In Black" by AC/DC and Vigilante leads Moose and Max Cameron to the ring. Maxine trails close behind, making her first appearance at an event in some time. She has words with Frank Merritt as Max holds her back.
Ricky Simms - Things between Max and Frank have gotten pretty hot. Henry Lesher - Well, of course they have. Frank has been getting under the skin of anyone he has faced here. He is the driving force behind the turn of the Flock. He is actually starting to win me over, though their lifestyle is pointless and worthless.
Ricky Simms - Sounds like your kind of people. Henry Lesher - That reminds me. Hallmark made me a new set of go to hell greeting cards. They have become very popular and will be leading members of the eat shit and die series from Hallmark.
Ricky Simms - You can't say that on network TV. Henry Lesher - Well, that's what they're called. The bell sounds as Max steos through the ropes to square off against Frank Merritt to start this match off. Leech shouts something to Max about the hardcore belt which distracts Max long enough for Frank to kick him in the gut. Frank then picks Max up and gives him a German suplex to the mat. Frank then drags Max to his corner and tags in Ash. Ash and Frank whips Max off the ropes and gives him a double clothesline to the mat. Ash then picks Max up and power slams him to the mat and goes for the pin. 1...2... Max manages to kick out. Ash stands Max up, whips him off the ropes and lands a knee to the gut which sends Max spiralling through the air before he lands hard on the mat. Ash then stands Max up, whips him to the corner. Max comes out of the corner, ducks a big boot from Ash and levels Ash with a hip toss over the ropes to the floor. Max then stumbles to his corner and tags in Moose. Moose doesn't even step into the ring. Instead, he drops off the apron and heads around the ring to confront Ash while mumbling to himself under his breath.
Ricky Simms - There are some serious hate issues going on here. Henry Lesher - That's why you need to shut up and watch these guys fight and beat the hell out of each other.
Moose pulls back the mats covering the conrete next to Ash. He then stands Ash up, Ash thumbs Moose in the eye and DDTs him onto the exposed concrete. He then rolls Moose into the ring while Angela stands nearby applauding his efforts. Angela then looks at the camera and states that she is the next women's champion. Ash tries for the pin on Moose. 1...2... Moose gets a foot on the bottom rope. Ash then tags in Leech. Leech arrogantly steps through the ropes. He slaps Moose in the face and asks is he's missed him. He then rabbit punches Moose five times before standing him up and whipping him into the corner buckle. Leech follows it up with a dropkick to the back of Moose. Leech then rolls Moose up for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Moose. Leech then stands Moose up, whips him off the ropes. Moose stops his momentum at the ropes. He glares at Leech and wags a finger at him as if to say that the beating is over. Leech charges at Moose which doesn't prove to be a good idea. Moose hip tosses Leech to the floor. He then tags in Vigilante. Vigilante drops off the apron, heads around the ring and rams Leech's head into the guard railing before rolling him back into the ring.
Ricky Simms - EOS taking over on Flock here. Henry Lesher - For now. I mean, there are rumors that things within EOS are not all perfect and Flock are a unified force.
Vigilante enters the ring, picks Leech up and body slams him to the mat. He then comes off the ropes and lands a knee drop to the throat of Leech. He goes for the pin. 1...2... Leech kicks out. The camera shows Rated R at ringside still showing no emotion. Another camera shows Dizz doing much the same thing in the front row. Vigilante stands Leech up and pulls him to the corner and starts ramming his head into the turnbuckle while the crowd counts the shots. He reaches ten and then Leech gives Vigilante a series of knife edge chops to the throat. Leech then rakes the eyes of the CWA champ and gives him a snap mare to the mat. Leech then pulls Vigilante to his corner and tags in Frank Merritt. He holds Vigilante open and Frank then kicks Vigilante hard in the ribs. He then gives him an abdomenal stretch. The ref checks for a submission, but isn't likely to get one. Vigilante struggles to free himself as Frank tries to hook the hold in deeper. Out of view of the ref, Ash reaches into the ring to grab Frank's arm for extra leverage. The crowd boos, but the ref sees nothing. When the ref does look, Ash releases the arm of Frank. As soon as the ref asks Vig again, Ash grabs the arm of Frank Merritt. The ref looks and this time he catches it. He forces a break of the hold.
Ricky Simms - Double team tactics here from the 420 Flock. Typical fare from them, I guess.
Henry Lesher - Whatever wins you the match. The champ is hurting right now and there's nothing Max or Moose can do from where they stand right now.
Frank stands Vigilante up, whips him into the corner. Vigilante gets out of the way of a charging Frank and Frank's shoulder strikes the corner post hard. Vigilante tags in Max Cameron. Max charges into the ring, picks Frank up and gives him a shoulderbreaker across the knee. Max then goes for the pin. 1...2... Ash breaks up the pin. Max then pulls Frank to his feet and whips him into the corner and demands for Leech to tag himself in. Leech chuckles and tags Frank's hand. He steps through the ropes, then locks up with Max Cameron. Max kicks Leech in the gut, then drops him with a Stunner. Max goes for the pin. 1...2... Leech kicks out. Max then stands Leech up, whips him off the ropes. Leech leap frogs over Max, then gives him a sunset flip into a pinfall. 1...2... kick out by Max Cameron.
Ricky Simms - Lots of near pinfalls in this one here. Henry Lesher - It's been a good one for sure. I never say that. In fact, I withdraw that statement. This match sucks.
Leech stands Max up and whips him off the ropes. Max ducks a clothesline, lunges and tags in Moose. Moose smiles as he steps into the ring with Leech. They stand face to face and Moose slaps Leech across the cheek. Leech hauls off for a right hand which Moose blocks. Moose then lands a series of punches to the jaw of Leech, then hauls off and drills him with a haymaker and tries for the pin. 1...2... Ash breaks it up with a kick to the head of Moose. Vigilante then enters the ring and starts going at it with Ash. This brings Frank and Max into the ring and they start brawling as the ref tries to make heads or tails of this match up. Rated R gets up from his chair as Leech is thrown from the ring by Moose. Rated R clocks Leech with the chair. Dizz gets up from the front row. He jumps over the guard rail and grabs Frank's foot under the bottom rope as Harmony Fallacia appears from the back and she nails Dizz in the back of the head with a trash can lid. The ref finally gives up and wipes the match out. Rated R lands another chair shot on Leech and then throws the chair down. He makes his way out as the remaining Flock and EOS get into it. Even Angela hops up on the apron and chokes Moose from outside the ring. It's a mess.
Ricky Simms - No payoff here as feuds deepen and rivalries get stronger. Henry Lesher - Just how things should be, dammit. What a great way to end the show, a huge brawl.
Ricky Simms - Don't miss next week's Assault. Vigilante defends the world title in a steel cage against Sehlia Lavin!
