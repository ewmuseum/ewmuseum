CWA Breakthrough, 7 February 2004

7 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dallas is hopping as we get ready for another action packed edition of Breakthrough. The opening credits roll and we see images of Northern Exposure, Baby Calaway, Demona Calaway, Hitoni Kiashi and Beef Jerky before we head to ringside where Romeo Salvador and Baby Calaway are ready to call the action.
Romeo Salvador - Before the action gets underway, I have something for my co-host here. "PIMP" by 50 Cent plays and Romeo stands. Men in tuxedos stream out from the back area and they each have a dozen roses in their hands. They make their way over to Baby Calaway and leave the flowers on the table in front of her. There winds up being six bouquets, two red, two yellow and two white ones. Baby sits there with her jaw dropped, not certain of what to say as Romeo takes a seat now with a smile on as his music fades.
Romeo Salvador - Sorry for hurting you in our match. I only wanted your attention. Baby Calaway - This is nice. I don't know what to say. Just be nice. You really hurt Baby's neck. That was bad Romeo.
Romeo Salvador - I know. Baby Calaway - Demona tells me sometimes boys wanna get over my pants or something. You trying to get over my pants?
Romeo Salvador - No, of course not. Dex Cooper vs The Masked Man "Shit Hits the Fan" by Obie Trice plays as Dex Cooper makes his way to the ring. The crowd has a positive response to him as he holds up a picture of Spikey Tiger and tells the camera that he's going to be back soon, not to worry. Dex steps over the top rope into the ring and poses once for some fans in the front row with caneras. As he does, the arena darknes briefly. When the lights come back up, The Masked Man is inside the ring behind Dex. The bell sounds as The Masked Man attacks Dex with a series of forearms to the back of the neck which knock him down to his knees. Masked Man then picks Dex up and body slams him before landing an elbow smash to his throat and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Dex.
Baby Calaway - That masked avenger sure likes attacking people. He was playing with the lights, too.
Romeo Salvador - Nice observation. Masked Man grabs the feet of Dex and starts putting him into a sharp shooter. Dex instead liftsa himself up to power out of it and kicks Masked Man right through the ropes to the floor. Dex then rolls himself out of the ring, not looking impressed at being attacked before the match even started. Dex makes his way over to Masked Man, stands him upo and punches him between the eyes. Dex then picks him up and drops him on the railing. Dex elbows Masked Man in the back while he lays prone over the railing. Dex then pulls him off of the railing and rolls him into the ring before entering the ring himself.
Romeo Salvador - Nice moves by my fellow Northern Exposure ally there. Baby Calaway - There sure are a lot of Canadians in the CWA. Romeo Salvador - We're born fighters. Dex stands The Masked Man up, whips him off the ropes and then levels him with a clothesline. Dex follows this up with a leg drop across the throat and then an attempted pin. 1...2... kick out by The Masked Man. Dex then stands The Masked Man up, whips him into the corner and both men clothesline each other to the mat as he comes back out. Their shoulders are down and their arms are across each other, so the ref makes the count. 1...2... both men roll out of it to keep this match going. Dex and Masked Man then both get to their knees and start trading right hands to the jaw. Dex starts getting the advantage with two quick shots and then elbows Masked Man in the foregead. Dex then gets to his feet, kicks Masked Man hard in the chest and then goes for the pin. 1...2... Masked Man manages to kick out. Dex then gets to his feet, pulls Masked Man to him and then delivers the Deadly Rabies Bite. The crowd cheers as Dex then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! The winner of this match is Dex Cooper!
Romeo Salvador - Never a doubt. The big man wins yet again. Baby Calaway - Dex sure is a big guy. He's way taller than Demona and me. Backstage we see Ray Dees, hair slicked back, being interviewed by Samantha Johnson. Samantha Johnson - Ray, your show debuts this week. Who are your guests going to be? Ray Dees - Well, sexy Sam, glad you asked. My guests will be none other than the lovely Myst, the surly Q and the legend Dante!
Samantha Johnson - Quite the line up for week one. I was... The interview is abrultpy halted when we see Truxx enter from the right of the screen and Vigilante enter from the left. They both shake their heads.
Truxx - No one promotes the Truxx stop. Vigilante - No one promotes the Vigilante Report, the show you little boys want to emulate.
Truxx - Maybe we'll be on little Deesa show here. Ray Dees - Hey, the more the merrirer, right? Vigilante - Oh, we'll be merry. I cannot believe after a year here that my show is given so little air time on the shows and here's Mister Dees before show one getting promoted.
Truxx - The Truxx Stop's ratings have been through the roof. This is simply wrong. It will be dealt with.
Truxx and Vigilante leave a very uncomfortable Dees with a very speechless Samantha Johsnon.
Ray Dees - So.... yeah.... we have quite the show with... ummm... Flava as the studio band and Frank Merritt on trombone for us.
Samantha Johnson - Trombone? Ray Dees - Yeah, first pop act to use one. It should be interesting to see Frank with Flava since he tried to kill them a couple of times.
Samantha Johnson - The talk show circuit is heating up indeed around here. Andon vs Chaz Jacobs "Immortal" by Adema plays as Chaz Jacobs makes his way to the ring. The crowd appears to appreciate him as he high fives them along the way. Chaz then slides into the ring and says a few things to the ref. "Numb" by Linkin Park then plays and we see Andon making his way to the ring to the boos of the crowd. He never takes his eyes off Chaz in the ring.
Romeo Salvador - And now from the creepy section comes Andon. Baby Calaway - He was bad. He doesn't treat that Gothic girl nice at all, but says he loved her. He's crazy. Demona says he's obsensced with her or something.
Romeo Salvador - Obsessed? Baby Calaway - That's the word. Andon steps into the ring and flies into a rage as he and Chaz exchange a series of punches and kicks on each other, the bell sounding to officially get this match underway. Andon whips Chaz off the ropes. Chaz ducks a clothesline. Chaz then picks Andon up and gives him a tilt a whirl slam to the mat. Chaz goes for an early pin. 1...2... Andon manages to kick out. Chaz then picks Andon up and gives him a sidewalk slam. He then heads for the ropes, climbs to the second ropes, turns and attempts a knee to the throat of Andon which misses when Andon rolls out of the way. Andon quickly gets to his feet and places Chaz in a sleeperhold. Andon says something into Chaz's ear about Gothic Angel and Chaz struggles to get ffree even as it becomes obvious that he is fading badly here. Andon cinches the hold in deeper as the ref starts to check on Chaz. The arm drops once. The arm is raised a second times, starts to drop, but Chaz finds the energy to keep the arm raised and then snap mares Andon to release himself from the hold. Chaz then places Andon in a front facelock.
Baby Calaway - Jazz with some nice moves. Romeo Salvador - Could go either way here, though I would like to see that sicko Andon drop for the three count.
Andon battles his way back to his feet, then whips Chaz off the ropes. Andon lands a dropkick on Chaz before heading to the ropes. He climbs all the way to the top and leaps off for a moonsault which misses when Chaz rolls out of the way. Chaz then quickly gets to his feet, climbs the ropes and lands a moonsault which connects on Andon before going for the pin. 1...2... Andon gets a foot on the bottom rope. Chaz then stands Andon up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him hard to the mat. Chaz then applies a sleeperhold on Andon. Andon gets right to his feet, sensing this hold could put him out. He then runs backwards into the corner buckle to break the hold before elbowing Chaz in the ribs repeatedly. Andon then turns to face him, gouges his eyes and kicks him in the gut before hip tossing him to the center of the ring.
Romeo Salvador - I think surivial instincts have kicked in for both men thus far in this match. You can never underestimate that.
Baby Calaway - Nope cause undresstimating is a bad idea. Andon then picks Chaz up and lands a power slam on him and goes for the pin. 1...2... Chaz kicks out. Andon picks Chaz up, whips him off the ropes. Chaz ducks a clothesline and then lands a spinebuster on Andon. He then heads to the ropes, climbs to the top and lands a five star frog splash from there and again goes for the pin. 1...2...3! The winner of this hotly contested match is Chaz Jacobs!
Romeo Salvador - Nice win for Chaz here. Backstage we see Gloria Lavin with Charley MacAdam. Charley MacAdam - Gloria, you have obviously been given a lot of power in the CWA. How do you feel about the card you put together for your first week as a booker for Assault?
Gloria Lavin - Shelia gets what she wants as she always does. I simply made an alteration to the rules is all. I think this week's Assault will be one of our best watched yet.
Charley MacAdam - Is itawkward booking her matches? Gloria Lavin - No, we dealt with any awkwardness a long time ago. It's all done now. Demona Calaway vs Hitoni Kiashi "C'Mon" by Regurgitator plays as Hitoni Kiashi makes her way to the ring. The crowd cheers her as she steps over the top rope and smiles pleasantly for the capacity crowd. We then hear Demona's music and the fans seem to be excited about the prospect of seeing the CWA Women's champion inside the ring for the first time in a long time. Demona appears to be unfazed by the rabid fans' attention as she makes her way into the ring, hands her ladies' belt to the ref and the bell sounds to get this match underway. The two cricle each other and lock up. Hitoni powers Demona to the mat with one hard shove. Demona gets to her feet, nods and locks up with her again. Once again, Hitoni shoves her to the mat. Demona retreats to the corner as if sizing up the large Asian monster and then nods again as she gets to her feet. She dropkicks the knees of Hitoni to bring her down. Demona then applies an ankle lock submission.
Baby Calaway - Demona always says that the bigger they come, you gotta break a few eggs of something like that.
Romeo Salvador - I am sure she does. Hitoni reaches for the ropes and grabs the bottom rope to force the break. Demona breaks the hold, but pulls Hitoni to the center of the ring and delivers a series of leg drops to the back of the knees of Hitoni as she works over the much taller foe. Demona then applies a Boston Crab and the ref checks for a submission only to find Hitoni refusing to give up this early in the match up. Hitoni uses her hands to push her shest off the mat and then kicks Demona into the corner buckle hard. Hitoni grabs at her right knee as she tries to get to her feet. Demona quickly gathers her balance and attacks the knee of Hitoni with an elbow straight onto it. Before she can get back up, Hitoni grabs Demona by the hair and slams her head into the mat. Hitoni then chokes Demona with both hands as the ref counts for the break before calling for a DQ. Hitoni breaks the choke on a four count and then gets to her feet as Demona coughs in an effort to catch her breath.
Baby Calaway - That's not nice, choking Demona. Hitty Kishy had better stop that. Hitoni stands Demona up and gives her a vertical suplex, though the pressure kind of buckles the knee as she executes it. Hitoni is wincing again as she goes for the pin. 1...2... Demona kicks out. Hitoni then stands Demona up and goes for another suplex. Demona blocks it. Demona then givea Hitoni a snap suplex to the mat. Demona follows this up by getting to her feet, pulling Hitoni to the corner and placing her leg over the bottom rope so she can land kicks to the already hurting knee. Demona then goes to the second rope and lands a leg drop to the throat of Hitoni from there. She then pulls Hitoni to the center of the ring. She goes for a sharpshooter on Hitoni, but Hitoni fights her off, knocks her down and places her in a Boston Crab. The crowd cheers as the ref asks Demona if she submits. Demona refuses to give up and the match continues to ebb and flow adn Demona battles her way up from the mat and kicks Hitoni off of her. Demona then gets to her feet and puts the boots to Hitoni in an effort to keep her on the mat. Demona then places Hitoni in a figure four leglock.
Romeo Salvador - Fine moves here by the champ as she wears Hitoni down. Baby Calaway - She's good at that. One time when she was little a paper boy threw the paper and accidentally hit her in the head. She put him in a figure four too.
Hitoni reaches for the ropes as the fans have started to cheer her on for the upset. Hitoni does, in fact, reach the ropes and the crowd cheers as Demona is forced to release the hold. However, Demona stands in wait as Hitoni gets to her feet very slowly. Demona then sets her up, exectures a side Russian leg sweep and then lands a rolling thunder coming off the ropes before going for the pin. 1...2...3! Demona wins the match!
Baby Calaway - That was fun! I like it when Demona wins. Romeo Salvador - It shows. It really does. Backstage we see Myst with Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - I am joined by new CWA female wrestler and wife of CWA owner, Shannon Skanes, Myst. Myst, how do you feel tonight?
Myst - I think it sucks that I had a match tonight and it was cancelled. I understand next week will be a different story.
Joe Davidson - Rumor has it you'll be facing Adin Vick. Myst - I think I saw him once. Big guy. I don't know what this match will prove, but I am going to become a top star in the CWA.
Joe Davidson - We just saw the Women's champ win a match there. Is that your ultimate goal?
Myst - Demona? I respect her. I guess I'll have to work my way up to a match with her. We'll see what happens, I guess.
Nacho Mauma vs TJ Mercer The crowd cheers as "Imaginary" by Evanescence plays and TJ Mercer makes his way to the ring. He poses at the top of the stage as pyro shoots off in every direction. He's picking up new fans every week. TJ makes his way down the ramp and steps inside the ring and smiles and waves to the capacity crowd. As TJ tests the ropes, we hear "Anything Goes" by Guns 'N' Roses and the masked Nacho Mauma makes his way down the aisle and he also receives a warm reception. He then steps through the ropes and has a few words for TJ. The two stand nose to nose as the bell sounds. Nacho shoves TJ. TJ shoves Nacho right back. The two stand nose to nose again.
Romeo Salvador - These two had some heated words for each other in promos this week. It appears those hard feelings are boiling over inside the ring now.
TJ smiles and lands a knife edge chop on Nacho. Nacho then lands a knife edge chop on TJ. Nacho then picks TJ up and gives him a body slam to the mat. He follows this up by standing TJ up, then giving him a vertical suplex to the mat. Nacho then poses once for the crowd before standing TJ up, whipping him into the corner and giving him a fallaway slam as he stumbles out of it. He then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by TJ Mercer. Nacho then stands TJ up and goes for a piledriver only to have TJ stand up and backdrop him to the mat. TJ knees Nacho in the forehead. TJ then stands Nacho up and whips him into the corner buckle. He follows that up with a belly to back suplex. He then syands Nacho up, whips him off the ropes, and leap frogs him before giving him a sunset flip and rolling him up for the pin. 1...2... Nacho kicks out at the last possible moment. TJ looks upset that wasn't the three count to win the match right there.
Baby Calaway - Mercy gettin' mad cause he thought that was the end. Romeo Salvador - Gonna take more than that to take Nacho down. TJ stands Nacho up, whips him off the ropes and Nacho ducks a clothesline attempt. Nacho then monkey flips TJ into the corner buckle. TJ quickly gets to his feet and the two men lock uo in the middle of the ring. Nacho headbutts TJ while in the lock up. Nacho headbutts him again. Nacho then whips TJ off the ropes and gives him a tilt a whirl slam. He then heads to the ropes and climbs to the top. TJ gets to his feet, turns and sees Nacho coming. He catches Nacho in mid air and choke slams him to the mat. He then goes for the pin. 1...2... Nacho gets a foot on the bottom rope. Nacho then is pulled to his feet. Nacho gives TJ a jawbreaker. Nacho then gets to his feet, shakes his head a few times and then grabs TJ by the hair. He picks TJ up and power slams him to the mat. He then looks to the crowd as if ready to fonish this one off. He stands TJ up, picks him up over his shoulders and delivers the F-U! Nacho then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Nacho Mauma has won the match!
Romeo Salvador - I must say that was a very impressive win for Nacho there. Baby Calaway - That move is a swear word. I don't know why someone would do that. Inside the ring, TJ gets to his feet. He sees Nacho celebrating and shakes his head. He then grabs the microphone.
TJ Mercer - You won round one, Nacho. I'll see you in the ring again and next time I'll be the winner.
Nacho shakes his head as he watches TJ leave the ring. Nacho then continues the celebration of the win.
Baby Calaway - Mercy wants a rematch. Romeo Salvador - I imagine he'll get it, too. Backstage we see Dante with Samantha Johnson. Samantha Johnson - Dante, why is it we see more of you now than we did when you were world champion?
Dante - Because the CWA knows what brings ratings. I do. You see, I have been assigned the job as the new GM of Breakthrough. Now, I haven't agreed to do it every week. In fact, I'll only do it when I feel like it which might be rare, might not. I will decide because I am the legend.
Samantha Johnson - You claim you're the best UK export since the Beatles, I hear? Dante - No, I am a much better drummer than Ringo, so that makes me better. I am the best of the best.
Samantha Johnson - So I should be thankful for a moment of your time then. Dante - You already are. I see it in your eyes. Samantha Johnson - *rolls her eyes* Yeah, that's what you see. Beef Jerky vs Nathan Cristion The Mexican Hat Dance plays as Beef Jerky makes his way to the ring. He spots a fan in the crowd with a sign that praises his opponent and grabs it from them and tears it to pieces before throwing the pieces back into the crowd. He has words with a few more fans on his way to the ring as they boo him loudly. He steps into the ring and has a few words with the ref. We're not sure what the ref's problem is, but he doesn't appear to be too impressed with Beef here this week. "Snap Your Finger, Snap Your Neck" by Grinspoon plays as Nathan Cristion makes his way to the ring to a alightly more positive reaction than Beef Jerky got. Nathan reaches ringside and appears not to know what to make of Beef at this point. Beef starts shouting at Nathan and Nathan smiles. Nathan makes his way up the ringsteps and then steps through the ropes.
Romeo Salvador - This won't be pretty. Baby Calaway - Beefy is a bad man. Nathy is here for his first match tonight. Romeo Salvador - A former star in BLPW who had a brief run there, Nathan is debuting and this match is the main event tonight. That has to say something for the efforts both of these men made here this week. To be in the main event this soon in their careers bodes well for both.
The bell sounds and Beef attacks Nathan with an eye gouge. Nathan reponds with an elbow to the throat which connects even while Nathan is blinded. Nathan then places Beef in a headlock. Beef picks Nathan up and gives him a suplex to get free. Beef then gets to his feet first and kicks Nathan in the ribs repeatedly while teaking pleasure in his giving pain to someone else. Nathan spots a weakness and shoots a knee out with a chop block. Beef crumples in a heap and Nathan starts in on the knee. Beef turns over and mule kicks Nathan into the corner buckle. Beef then gets to his feet just in time to take a clothesline from Nathan. Nathan then pulls Beef to the ropes and boot chokes him there. The ref counts to four before he releases the boot choke. He then starts it again. The ref threatens to DQ Nathan, so Nathan releases the boot choke and rolls Beef outside the ring.
Romeo Salvador - This match is about to get reallt ugly out there. Baby Calaway - They can't win out there. Silly boys. Nathan whips Beef into the guard railing. He then stands him up and takes a kick to the gut from Beef. Beef then reaches into his boots and pulls something metal out. The ref doesn't see as he gouges at the forehead of Nathan Cristion. Nathan screams in pain as his forehead begins to bleed and Beef smiles. He then places that metal object back into his boot before rolling Nathan back inside the ring.
Romeo Salvador - Beef appears to bring weapons with him to the ring. Baby Weapons - I said Beefy was bad. Beef punches away at the forehead of Nathan as Nathan tries to get his bearing. Beef then stands Nathan up, whips him into the corner and charges in for a shoulder block which misses as Nathan sidesteps it. Nathan then gouges the eyes of Beef Jerky on the top rope before headbutting him into the corner. Nathan then appears to bite Beef's forehead as the ref again threatens to call this match a DQ. Beef is bleeding too now as Nathan whips him into the opposite corner. Nathan then gives Beef a sidewalk slam and goes for the pin. 1...2... Beef kicks out. Nathan then stands Beef up and takes a kick to the gut again. Beef then picks Nathan up and power bombs him to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... Nathan manages to just get a shoulder up. Beef then pulls Nathan to his feet, whips him into the corner and takes a boot to the chest from Nathan. Nathan then spears Beef to the mat. He again goes for the pin. 1...2... Beef manages to kick out of that one as well.
Romeo Salvador - Lots of high impact moves and neither man is staying down for the count. Baby Calaway - Bad Beef. Bad bitey Nathy. These two are making me mad, cheating and stuff. Romeo Salvador - These two don't bill themselves as nice guys. Nathan pulls Beef to the middle of the ring and applies a sharpshooter. Beef screams in pain, but refuses to give. He instead pulls himself up from the mat and grabs at the feet of Nathan. He then manages to sits himself up and pull Nathan down which reverses the hold. Nathan immediately starts pulling himself up from the mat. Before he can reverse it again, Beef releases the hold. Both men have lost a lot of blood here. Beef stands Nathan up. Nathan rakes the eyes of Beef. Beef headbuuts Nathan. Beef then elbows Nathan in the forehead and then whips him off the ropes. Nathan holds onto the ropes as Beef readies to deliver a big boot to his chin. Nathan wags a middle finger at Beef. Beef charges and Nathan ducks, pulling down the top rope which causes the monster Beef to fall over the top rope and to the floor. Nathan climbs to the top rope. The ref watches as Beef gets to his feet and pulls something from his boot. The ref sees the glimmer of metal scissors and shouts at Beef. Nathan launches himself, unaware of the weapon awaiting him and Beef nails Nathan in the forehead as he dives onto him. The ref then calls for the bell, DQing Beef.
Romeo Salvador - My God! He almost skinned Nathan alive! Beef grabs the tongue of Nathan as he places the scissors agianst his throat. Romeo senses this is big trouble, jumps up from his seat and attacks Beef, climbing onto the table and diving off with a leg drop to the beck of Beef's neck. Romeo then grabs a folding chair and clubs Beef over the back of the head with it as Nathan struggles to get to his feet and is announced thew winner by DQ. Nathan sees that Romeo saved him from further injury and thanks him with a spinning power slam on the floor. Nathan then spits blood on Beef and leaves ringside. Neef starts getting up saying Nathan's name to the camera.
Baby Calaway - Poor Romey got hurt. Bad Nathy. Thbat's it for Breakthrough this week. See you next week. Remember, there's nothing that makes you so mad that a hug from a teddy bear can't fix.
We see another shot of the monster Beef Jerky holding the bloodied scissors high as he heads to the back in serahc of Nathan Cristion or anyone else who dares to get in his way.
