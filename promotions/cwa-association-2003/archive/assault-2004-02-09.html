CWA Assault, 9 February 2004

9 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Vancouver, BC is the host for this week's Assault as the crowd cheers loudly and we see pyro shoot off from the stage as they see themselves on the big screen. We see the opening credits roll as images of Dante, Q, Vigilante, Dark Ice Symphony and The Flock before we head to ringside where Ricky Simms and Henry Lesher are ready to call the action.
Dizz vs Q "Nobody's Listening" by Linkin Park plays and Dizz makes his way to the ring as the crowd goes wild. They don't get much more popular than the three time CWA champion, though he has been quiet recently. Dizz high fives fans on his way down to the ring and then steps onto the apron. He slips inside the ring and then gets on the second rope and raises one arm to the fans as pictures are snapped by the hundreds. Then, Tomoyasu Hotei's "Battle without Honor or Humanity" begins to play, and the arena goes black. After a few seconds, as the horns kick in, there is a single large burst of blue pyro from every conceivable place in the entranceway, and Q walks out, slowly making his way to the ring. He receives a less warm welcome from the Vancouver crowd who know this man's hatred for the family running this place and this happens to be their hometown.
Henry Lesher - Fans once again showing ignorance to poor Q here in Canada. Ricky Simms - You didn't just say poor Q, right? I mean, the guy's not some sort of mercy mission.
Henry Lesher - Look, Rick, it's bad enough they booked the guy to wrestle first here tonight. It's quite another for the fans not to show him the respect he is due as the true legend now that Dante has retired and will forever be missed.
Ricky Simms - Missed? The guy is always here! Q steps through the ropes and then the bell sounds. He and Dizz, two former CWA champions, circle each other inside the ring. They then lock up and the crowd cheers as Dizz kicks Q repeatedly in the gut, then whips him off the ropes and dropkicks him down. Dizz follows this up by pulling Q to his feet, whipping him into the corner and then monkey flipping him out of it. Dizz goes for the pin on the dazed Q. 1...2... kick out by Q. Dizz then pulls Q to his feet. Q gouges the eyes of Dizz, then headbutts him three times to send him reeling to the ropes. Q then picks Dizz up and gives him a gorilla press slam to the mat. Q follows this up with a boot choke in the ropes against the now reeling Dizz.
Henry Lesher - Q showing his dominance here. Ricky Simms - Dizz has a way of battling his way out of these things. Q stands Dizz up, whips him hard into the corner buckle. Dizz responds by dropkicking the knees of Q out from under him. The shorter Dizz knows he needs to cut down the taller Q to have a chance here at victory. Dizz then applies a figure four leglock on Q as the fans cheer him on. Q doesn't show any panic, but still reaches for the ropes to force the break. He finds himself mere inches away from the ropes. He lunges, pulling Dizz across the mat and does reach the ropes on his next attempt to do so. Dizz releases Q and Q pulls himself out of the ring. Dizz then follows Q out. Q turns, grabs Dizz by the hair and slams him face first into the steel ring steps. He then picks Dizz up and runs him, shoulder first, into the steel corner post. The crowd boos these tactics, but Q continues unabated. Q picks Dizz up and runs his spine into the steel corner post before Q rolls himself back into the ring.
Henry Lesher - You simply do not mess with this man. It's a very bad idea to do so. Ricky Simms - I never said that taunting Q was a good plan. I simply said that Dizz has been faced with this man and men bigger and stronger than him before and managed to survive.
Dizz slowly gets to his feet, holding his back and wincing when he moves the arm which had the shoulder rammed into the corner post. Dizz then grabs the ropes with his good arm and pulls himself into the ring. The crowd starts a chant for Dizz as Q picks him up. Dizz slides off the shoulders of Q and runs him into the turnbuckle hard. Dizz then gives Q a reverse neckbreaker and goes for the pin. 1...2... Q manages to get a shoulder up off the mat. Dizz then starts landing knee to the throat of Q. He follows this up by standing Q up and whipping him into the corner buckle. He charges straight into a big boot to the jaw from Q who then gives Dizz a pump handle slam to the mat. Q then stands Dizz up and lands the Q Jumper on him. He goes for the pin, using his feet on the ropes for added leverage and out of sight of the ref. 1...2...3! Q pulls off a win here to start this Assault against Dizz.
Ricky Simms - Cheap win for Q here. Henry Lesher - Q is the man. Cheap win or not cheap win, he is the man. What a great moment to be a CWA fan as Q dispatches Dizz.
Ricky Simms - With his feet on the ropes. Henry Lesher - Go cry somewhere else, Ricky. This is Q's grand moment. Backstage we see Vigilante with Ray Dees. Ray Dees - I am now joined by the CWA's World champion. Vig, we just saw Q manage to get past your stablemate Dizz. Your reaction and thoughts on tonight?
Vigilante - My thoughts on tonight are simply, Ray. I am going into that cage and I am going to beat Shelia Lavin. Whatever Q did out there was simply his way of getting ahead and I have no time for that now. EOS will take care of him later.
Ray Dees - Rumors fly about the state of EOS and whether or not membership within the group is stable.
Vigilante - Look, I rock. As a result, people stay with me. EOS is solid as a rock and might even consider expansion someday if anyone measures up.
Ray Dees - So I might have a chance to get in? Vigilante - You have a better chance of getting in Britney Spears. Now, I have work to do. Vig pushes Ray out of the way and leaves as we head back to the ring. Gothic Angel vs Angela We expect one thing and get another as we hear "Soldier" by Eminem and we see Tiffany making her way to the ring to a mixed reaction. She makes her way casually around the ring and then takes a seat next to the timekeeper.

It sounds like she is picking up new fans as each week passes by. She steps into the ring and then heads over to the side where Tiffany is sitting and has a few words for her. Tiffany acts as if she doesn't hear Gothic at all, so Gothic takes a step through the ropes. We then hear "The Sweet Leaf" by Black Sabbath and Angela starts making her way to the ring. Gothic then steps back inside the ring, but shouts a warning to Tiffany not to interfere here. Gothic Angel then stands Angela up, whips her into the corner and then charges into a knee lift to the chest from Angela. Angela then clotheslines Gothic to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gothic Angel. Tiffany gets to her feet for a moment, thinking that might have been the win. When she sees that it was a two count, she returns to her seat. Angela then stands Gothic Angel up and whips her hard into the corner buckle. Angela gives her a gutwrench suplex to the mat. She again goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Gothic Angel. Angela then picks Gothic Angel up, drops her across the ropes so the top rope slingshots her head back and to the mat. Angela again goes for the pin. 1...2... Gothic Angel gets a foot on the bottom rope.
Ricky Simms - Angela is giving it everything she has as she tries for the win here against Gothic Angel.

Angela stands Gothic Angel up, then gives her a snap suplex to the mat. She again tries for the pin. 1...2... reversal into a small package by Gothic Angel. 1...2... Angela kicks out. Angela rolls herself out of the ring and shares a glance with sister Tiffany Johnson. Nothing is said. Gothic Angel then rolls herself out of the ring. She takes a stiff chin kick from Angela. Gothic Angel falls back to the floor and takes a leg drop there from Angela. Angela then rolls Gothic into the ring and heads for the ropes. She climbs to the top as Gothic slowly gets to her feet. Angela dives off for a flying cross body only to be caught in mid air by Gothic who turns it into a spinning power slam to the mat. Gothic then tries for the pin. 1...2... Angela gets a foot on the bottom rope. Gothic then pulls Angela to her feet and gives her a few knife edge chops to the chest. Gothic then whips Angela off the ropes. Angela ducks a clothesline, comes off the ropes and gives Gothic a hurracarana. She then gets to her feet and sets herself as Gothic slowly gets to her feet again. Angela lands Angela's Ashes on Gothic and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Angela wins the match.
Ricky Simms - Big win for Angela. Henry Lesher - If you say so. Tiffany gets to her feet and shakes her head as she leaves ringside and Angela watches her every step of the way. The crowd cheers Angela on inside the ring, but many wonder what Tiffany will do now that she and her sister are both seen as the number one contender for the Women's title in the CWA. We then head backstage and we see Tina with a smile on her face and standing next to Joe Davidson.
Joe Davidson - Tina, you look quite pleased tonight. Tina - Why not? The only true man in the CWA has won already and we are just moments away from Reality having the world title back where it belongs.
Joe Davidson - We have yet to see Shelia tonight. What is her mindset like right now? Tina - Pretty simple answer to that. She is taking a champion's approach to this match. Joe Davidson - What does that mean? Tina - It means she is going to win and her mindset is set towards that goal. Joe Davidson - I see.... Tina - No, you don't, but you will soon enough. When that cage drops from the roof, all bets are off.
The Moose vs Ash "If You Want Blood (You Got It)" by AC/DC plays as The Moose makes his way to the ring, the third former world champ we have seen already tonight. He receives a huge ovation as he gears himself up for his impending date at Malicious Intent with the world title. Moose steps into the ring and the crowd cheers as he raises his arms skyward. Their reaction changes when they hear "The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson and Ash starts making his way to the ring. Ash receives 90% negative response in comparison with his girlfriend who usually walks him to the ring. Tonight it's even worse as he faces fan favorite Moose. Ash steps onto the apron and has a few words with Moose before even setting foot inside the ring. He then steps in and the bell sounds to get this match going. The two circle each other. They lock up and Ash places Moose in an arm bar. Moose reverses it and then takes a kick to the chest from Ash. Ash then rakes the eyes of Moose on the top rope before kicking him in the gut.
Ricky Simms - Ash using any tactic he can in order to gain an advantage here. He and Moose know each other very well.

Ash tosses Moose through the ropes to the floor. He then heads out after him, stepping onto the apron and landing an axe handle smash to the back of Moose before he can fully get his bearings about him. Ash then picks Moose up and throws him over the guard railing to the floor on the other side. Ash rolls into the ring to break up the count before rolling back out. He steps over the guard railing and starts choking Moose against the railing with a boot to his throat. The ref shouts from the ring to cease this tactic or risk DQ. Ash then picks Moose up and drops him on top of the railing. Ash then gets onto the railing and jumps down, leg dropping the back of Moose's neck. Ash then rolls himself into the ring and smiles, aware of the damage he has inflicted on Moose.
Ricky Simms - Moose has already accepted a challenge for next week when he'll be stepping into the ring for what he and Leech call one last match.
Henry Lesher - Leech has proven himself to be the better man every time they have faced each other. Next week will be no different.
Moose struggles to get to his feet and, when he does, he takes a missile dropkick under the ropes from an attacking Ash. Ash then picks Moose up and body slams him to the floor. He then pulls him over to the announce table and slams his head against it twice before rolling Moose onto the table. Ash rolls into the ring to break up the count yet again and then heads to the ropes.
Ricky Simms - He wouldn't. Henry Lesher - Sure he would. Ash dives off. Moose rolls off the table and Ash's attempted elbow smash from the top lands him on and through the table. Ash isn't moving as Moose slowly gets to his feet, grabbing the apron for balance. Moose then rolls himself into the ring and the crowd cheers. Ash still isn't moving as the ref counts. Moose rolls himself out of the ring which breaks up the count. The ref is clearly becoming frustrated with the lawlessness both wrestlers have shown so far in this match. Moose picks Ash up and body slams him on the remains of the table. Moose then grabs Ash by the hair and pulls him to the apron and slams his head against it before rolling him inside the ring. Moose then looks to the fans who cheer him on as he starts to climb the ropes. Moose reaches the top, dives off and misses with a knee drop from there as Ash rolls out of the way. Ash's knee lands sickeningly hard against the mat and he screams as he rolls around and Moose gets to his feet. The match has turned and it appears that Moose knows it. Moose grabs Ash by the hair again. He pulls him to the corner and rams his head into the turnbuckle repeatedly as the fans count off ten impacts. Moose then hip tosses the dazed Ash to the mat. He then looks to the crowd as if he's setting up to finish this match here. He pulls Ash to him and then delivers a Moosedriver and goes for the pin. 1...2... Ash gets a foot on the bottom rope! Moose is shocked and curses himself for not trying the pin in the center of the ring.
Ricky Simms - Ash manages to avoid the pin after that Moosedriver! Henry Lesher - Moose simply cannot put Ash away here. It's as I figured it would be. Moose stands Ash up, whips him into the corner and then backdrops him to the mat. He pulls Ash to the center of the ring, then pulls him to his feet and goes for another Moosedriver. He exectues it and again goes for the pin. 1...2... Ash kicks out! Moose simply cannot believe it. We then see Leech coming out from the back which is greeted by a chorus of boos from the crowd. Leech gives a few fans the finger.
Henry Lesher - Here comes an unbiased party. Ricky Simms - How can you say that and not get struck with lightning? Moose spots Leech and heads to the ropes. The two have a heated verbal exchange as the ref tries to get Moose's mind back on the fallen Ash inside the ring. Ash tries a roll up for the pin on Moose. 1...2... Moose manages to kick out as Leech takes up a spot at ringside. Ash stands Moose up, whips him off the ropes. Leech takes a swipe at the foot of Moose, but misses. Ash then monkey flips Moose and Leech looks ready to interfere yet again. Moose ducks a clothesline and whips Ash off the ropes. Leech trips Ash face first to the mat. Leech then jumps onto the apron and distracts the ref. Ash is pinned by Moose. Leech drops off the apron. The ref goes to count the pin. 1...2... Ash again manages to kick out. Leech grabs a chair at ringside, slides into the ring and crowns Moose with the chair! The ref calls for the bell, DQing Ash here.
Ricky Simms - Leech just cost Ash the match. Henry Lesher - Leech just saved Ash from further injury. He is a true hero. Ricky Simms - I know the lightning is coming. I just need to know when. Moose rolls out of the ring grabbing his head from the chair shot while Leech helps Ash up. Ash has a few words with Leech which results in a shoving match between them. Ash claims he had the match in hand and that he was going to win while Leech claims he was just trying to help. Moose is announced the winner by DQ and his music plays, so the fans cheer that. They also appear to enjoy the appearance of cracks in the Flock's alliance.
Ricky Simms -m Trouble brewing here? Henry Lesher - Of course not. You people and your negative outlook on things. Ricky Simms - That's right. Between the two of us, I am the one with the negative outlook. Backstage we see Tiffany standing next to Charley MacAdam. Charley MacAdam - I am joined by Tiffany here who watched Angela take her spot as number one contender, a spot you earned mere weeks ago.
Tiffany - It doesn't mean squat to me. Honestly, the champ keeps hiding with her title anyways. She's not booked to fight this week and made an appearance on Breakthrough for a non title bout with that Asian monster? I am disgusted with the state of the women's division.
Charley MacAdam - We have also taken notice of you and Chain Smoke and how few teams there are to challenge you guys.
Tiffany - That changes next week when we face Reality. Gloria Lavin enters the picture and interrupts. Gloria Lavin - Sorry to butt in here. Yes, next week Reality gets their shot at the tag belts. You guys have to defend those shiny pieces of gold and this match will be an interesting one. In the spirit of Valentine's, it will be a no DQ match up.
