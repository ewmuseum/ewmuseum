CWA Blind Rage, 24 May 2004

24 May 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Moscow is jumping as the pyro shoots off and we know that Blind Rage has arrived. At ringside sit Henry Lesher and Ray Dees, Baby Calaway getting the night off to deal with her upcoming match against Halloween Jack. Rumor has it that she is looking for a Halloween costume that is perfect for the match. We head backstage where we see Frank Merritt with Joe Davidson.
Joe Davidson - Frank, tonight you get your shot at becoming the world champ. What kind of preparations have you gone through to get here?
Frank Merritt - Joe, I have been... Before Frank can get much more than that out, Notorious BLC enters the shot with a chair and starts wailing away on Frank with it. The crowd can be heard cheering as BLC leaves Frank there, hardly moving. This feud is just getting started.
Sandman/Kelly vs Acolytes of Sin "Not Ready To Go" by The Trews plays as Kelly Buchanan and Sandman make their way to the ring, led by Everest who is visiting from the Anarchy compound to make sure everything goes as planned tonight. There are chants for Sandman as he makes his way to the ring, the Russian crowd hoping to inspire the rounded Anarchy member into having a great match here tonight. Sandman enters the ring and starts pacing around it, trying to find the courage here to be tough enough to secure the win against The Acolytes of Sin. As Sandman paces, we hear "Headstrong" by Trapt playing and Gothic Angel and Pyro make their way to the ring to a very mixed reaction, fans never certain what to expect from these two. Apparently, even Russia is aware of what these two are all about.
Ray Dees - Sure is nice to be at ringside. Henry Lesher - Get out of line and I'll sick the KGB on you. Ray Dees - Lesh, give it a rest man. I am as ratty as you are. Henry Lesher - I don't want you to compare us to each other at all. Ray Dees - Denial, my friend. You;re up to your ears in it. The bell rings and Kelly Buchanan is inside the ring with Gothic Angel. The two circle each other, then lock up. Gothic shoves Kelly into the corner buckle and then gives her a fireman's carry into a body slam out of it. Gothic then uses the ropes for leverage as she boot chokes the fallen Kelly Buchanan. Gothic then makes a remark Sandman's way. Sandman paces on the apron, appearing to be talking to himself. Everest tries to shout instructions to him from outside the ring to no avail. Gothic stands Kelly up, pulls her to their corner and tags in Pyro. Gothic holds Kelly in an arm bar while Pyro climbs to the top rope. Pyro leaps off, landing a double axe handle to the shoulder of Kelly Buchanan. Pyro then picks Kelly up and gives her a shoulderbreaker to the mat. Pyro then stands Kelly up, whips her off the ropes and Kelly ducks a clothesline attempt by Pyro. Kelly then dropkicks Pyro in the chest. Kelly does a shoulder roll to her corner and tags in Sandman. Sandman looks at the crowd, apparently uncertain he wants to get in there. They cheer him on. During his hesitation, Pyro attacks Kelly with a vicious elbow to the back of the neck. She falls through the ropes to the floor and Sandman's eyes glaze over with rage.
Ray Dees - Someone just poked the lion in the cage. Henry Lesher - Lion? More like a hippo. Ray Dees - No joke there, Doc. Those bastards get really mad when you poke them through the fence. I blamed a little kid for it though, so he got kicked out instead.
Henry Lesher - How cruel and spineless.... interesting idea. Sandman steps through the ropes and Pyro attacks him with a series of forearms to the back and neck. Sandman responds with a choke slam in the middle of the ring. Sandman then has a few words for Pyro about hitting women. He grabs Pyro by the throat, stands him up and sets him for another choke slam. Pyro eye gouges Sandman, then headbutts him back into a neutral corner. Pyro lands a series of knife edge chops on Sandman. Sandman then responds with a vicious headbutt that sends Pyro flying through the air before he lands in the center of the ring, almost comatose. Sandman grabs Pyro by the hair, pulls him to his feet and lands a right hook to the jaw of Pyro. Sandman then goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Pyro. Sandman slaps the mat and screams at the ref to learn to count to three or prepare to have his arms ripped off. Sandman stands Pyro up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a sidewalk slam. Sandman then comes off the ropes and splashes Pyro and again goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Sandman with a dominant performance over Pyro grabs the win for Anarchy to begin the night!
Ray Dees - Score one for the hungry hungry hippo and the Playboy bunny. Henry Lesher - Yes, glad it is over. Get Sandman some burgers and he'll be fine. As Sandman and Kelly leave the ring, we head to the backstage area where Ash stands with Charley MacAdam.
Charley MacAdam - Ash, tonight is your shot at becoming the world champ. What are your thoughts heading into this match?
Ash - My thoughts are that Lone Star will finally get what he has coming to him and then Frank and I will decide things from there.
Charley MacAdam - Any fears you will fail again? Ash - I don't fail. I am simply delayed. Gerard Angelo vs Micheal Leary "Dammit" by Blink 182 plays as Gerard Angelo makes his way to the ring to the boos of the crowd. Angelo rolls into the ring, then stands in the corner and poses which doesn't sound like it impresses anyone either.
Henry Lesher - How can the entire world cheer for the same people? Are people that easily led?
Ray Dees - George W Bush is president. I don't think he can drive standard. Henry Lesher - Point taken. Gerard paces in the ring as we hear "Lose Yourself" by Eminem play and Micheal Leary makes his way to the ring. He gets a warm reception from the crowd and high fives some fans on his way to the ring. He slides into the ring and his music fades away as Gerard starts putting the boots to him before he has a chance to get up and the bell sounds to officially start the match off. Angelo stands Micheal up, whips him off the ropes and gives him a side kick to the chest. Micheal responds with an eye gouge on Gerard even while off balance and short of breath. Micheal then gives Gerard a side Russian leg sweep to the mat. Micheal gets to his feet, shaking off the effects of Gerard's attack. Micheal then picks Gerard up and body slams him to the mat. Micheal then two handed chokes the fallen Angelo as the crowd cheers him on.
Ray Dees - Hey, while Shelia's boytoy is in the ring, we all wonder how you feel about Q leaving and Max and Myst keeping the Reality name.
Henry Lesher - Utter crap. Shannon will pay for that. When Dante left, he was all I had left. Now.... now I have nothing and no one. I am alone.
Ray Dees - Geez Doc, you need to get out more. Micheal stands Angelo up and whips him off the ropes. Both men clothesline each other to the mat. The ref starts counting both men down as they stir on the mat. Gerard shows signs of life first and gets to the ropes by the four count. Angelo gets to his feet at six as Micheal starts pulling himself up with the use of the ropes. Angelo charges at Micheal. Micheal drops the ropes down and Angelo sails right over the top to the floor. The crowd cheers as Angelo tries to get to his feet. Micheal dives through the ropes and clotheslines Angelo to the floor. Micheal then stands Angelo up and tries to whip him into the steel corner post. Gerard instead reverses it and sends Micheal head first into the steel. Angelo then rolls Micheal into the ring before rolling himself inside as well. Angelo goes for the pin on Micheal. 1...2... kick out by Micheal Leary. Angelo then stands Micheal up and whips him out of the ring. The ref warns Angelo not to keep this up or he'll throw the match out. Angelo backs off as the ref starts counting Micheal out. Angelo pulls something out of his pocket which gets the fans going.
Ray Dees - Nothing wrong with a shortcut. I had to take my share when I wrestled. Henry Lesher - I hear it did little for your win-loss record. Ray Dees - That was because of the suits in the back. They wanted people who were handsome ot strong or agile and stuff like that. They didn't see my gift.
Micheal Leary rolls himself into the ring. Gerard stands him up and whips him into the ref. The ref falls, Micheal turns and takes an eyeful of salt from Gerard Angelo. Angelo then gives Leary a gutwrench backbreaker and stirs the ref before going for the pin. A groggy ref counts it. 1...2...3! Gerard Angelo with the win this week over Micheal Leary!
Ray Dees - There ya go, Gerard. Henry Lesher - Don't encourage him. It'll just cause him to keep wrestling every week. Backstage we see Kelly Buchanan with Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - Kelly, you guys are on somewhat of a roll with that win. What's up next week for you?
Kelly Buchanan - Well we have... Before Kelly can finish her response, she is attacked by Angel and Tiffany and they throw her head first into the wall and then give her a double clothesline to the floor before leaving her lying there.
Ray Dees - Joe can't finish an interview tonight. Henry Lesher - Which is a blessing in and of itself. Harmony Fallacia vs Marcus Cameron "Heads Explode" by Monster Magnet plays as Harmony Fallacia makes her way to the ring. She gets a mixed reception as she carries Mr. Binky high and in her other hand carries masks which convey a variety of emotions. Harmony then climbs up the steps and steps into the ring. As she does so, we hear "Thunderstruck" by AC/DC and Marcus Cameron makes his way to the ring, led by Maxine. Marcus high fives a few fans who are cheering him on before he slides into the ring and the bell sounds to get things started.
Henry Lesher - People are paying to watch these matches? Ray Dees - Hey, at least they are getting pros behind the microphones here. Henry Lesher - Well one of them, yeah. Harmony and Marcus lock up in the center of the ring. Harmony then whips Marcus off the ropes and Marcus shoulder blocks her down to the mat. Marcus lands a knee drop to the shoulder of Harmony and goes for the early pin. 1...2... kick out by Harmony Fallacia. Marcus then stands her up, whips her off the ropes and gives her a dropkick which sends her down to the mat. Marcus Cameron then headd to the ropes and makes his way to the top as the crowd cheers him on. Marcus lands a flying leg drop to the back of the head of Harmony before she has a chance to get up to her feet. Marcus then rolls her over for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Harmony Fallacia again.
Ray Dees - I heard she was better at staying down on her back than that. Henry Lesher - Personal experience? Ray Dees - Pal, I frickin' wish, okay? Marcus stands Harmony up, whips her off the ropes and misses with a clothesline. Harmony then does a cartwheel into the ropes, comes back out in another cartwheel and headscissors Marcus to the mat. Harmony then holds Marcus in the headscissors, using her legs to apply a modified sleeperhold on him. This thrills half the crowd and gets the other half to start a chant for Marcus Cameron. Marcus fights his way to turn the hold over and then rolls and forces his way free. Marcus then rolls himself out of the ring to catch his breath. Harmony follows him out of the ring. Harmony grabs Marcus by the hair and slams his head into the ringside announcer's table. Marcus then elbows Harmony in the gut and rams her head into the announce table in response. Marcus then picks Harmony up and body slams her on the table, breaking it. Marcus rolls into the ring to break up the count before he rolls back out.
Ray Dees - So much for my coffee. Henry Lesher - Why do they make such flimsy tables for ringside? Marcus stands Harmony up, whips her into the ring steps shoulder first and then rolls her into the ring. Marcus slides into the ring and rolls Harmony over for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Harmony. Marcus sighs and looks at Maxine, asking her what he has to do to defeat this girl. Maxine yells back some advice. Marcus stands Harmony up, whips her off the ropes and misses with a dropkick. Harmony keeps hold of the ropes and watches Marcus fall. Harmony then leaps to the top rope and dives off, landing a five star frog splash on Marcus. Harmony goes for the pin. 1...2...3!
Ray Dees - Sometimes it is better to be lucky than good. Henry Lesher - These two are neither. Backstage we see Notorious BLC in the locker room, getting ready for his big King of the Pay Per View match. Joe Davidson enters the frame to interview him, but Frank Merritt charges in and the two of them are brawling as Joe shakes his head and mumbles something about needing a new job.
Wish vs Lethal Weapon "I Stand Alone" by Godsmack plays as Lethal Weapon comes out to a huge ovation from the Russian crowd. Weapon high fives fans on the way down and then climbsup onto the apron as pyro on stage spell out "HE'S BACK" in bright flames. The flames die down and Weapon enters the ring and has a few words for the ref on how things should go down here tonight. "Nothing Left To Lose" by Puddle of Mudd plays as Wish makes his way to the ring to a mixed reaction. He has a few words for Weapon on his way to the ring about how impressive he isn't. Wish then slides into the ring and Weapon starts laying the boots to him as the bell sounds to get this match started. Weapon stands Wish up, whips him off the ropes and spears him to the mat before going for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Wish. Weapon then stands Wish up, sets him and gives him a vertical suplex to the mat. Weapon then stands Wish up and gives him a gutwrench suplex before going for the pin again. 1...2... kick out by Wish. Weapon stands Wish up again. Wish battles back with a series of punches to the gut of Lethal Weapon before whipping him off the ropes. Wish drives a knee to the gut of Weapon, sending him somersaulting to the mat. Weapon lands hard on his back and Wish places him in a sleeperhold.
Ray Dees - Well these two are energetic. Henry Lesher - Big return for this guy I never missed and he's fighting that guy no one cares about so it's a great match up. Who the hell is paying for this and not hanging themselves?
Wish holds Weapon down as the ref checks on the sleeper. Weapon's arm drops once. The crowd starts a chant for Lethal Weapon. Weapon's arm is raised a second time, but does not drop. Instead, Weapon clenches his fists and starts elbowing Wish in the side to break free of the hold as he gets to his feet. He gives Weapon a side suplex to the mat. Weapon then gets to his feet and grabs Wish by the hair. He snap mares Wish to the mat and places him in a sleeper of his own. Weapon then knees Wish in the back while holding him in the sleeper tightly.
Ray Dees - Starting to get heated between these two. Henry Lesher - Yeah, heated.... I need something to keep me awake. Maybe if I slap a few fans around.
Ray Dees - Missing Baby Calaway yet? Henry Lesher - As much as I would miss a boil on my ass. Weapon locks the sleeper in tight and the ref checks the arm of Wish. The arm drops once. The arm drops a second time. The ref raises the arm a third time and Wish manages to avoid it dropping then. Wish starts to get to his feet. Lethal Weapon releases the sleeper, rakes the eyes of Wish on the top rope, then whips him off the ropes and clotheslines him to the mat. Weapon poses for the crowd before standing Wish up and whipping him hard into the corner. Weapon then gives Wish a backdrop to the mat. Weapon follows this up by standing Wish up, whipping him off the ropes and giving him another spear to the canvas. Before he can go for the pin, Wish rolls himself out of the ring. Weapon rolls out after him. Wish heads over the guard railing and into the aisle again with Lethal Weapon right behind him. The two start trading rights and lefts in the aisle. Lethal Weapon eye gouges Wish. Wish headbutts Weapon. They then both clothesline each other to the floor as the nearby fans cheer them on. Inside the ring, the ref keeps the count going and counts them both out, calling for the bell.
Henry Lesher - What a waste of time! Ray Dees - At least they hurt each other. Weapon and Wish get to their feet, brawling again even after the match as security tries to split the two of them up. We then head backstage where we see Joe Davidson on a couch and Hank Roberts taking notes on a notepad next to him.
Joe Davdison - Everywhere I go, I see people... nasty people and they're everywhere. Only thing is they don't know they're nasty.
Hank Roberts - What do they do? Joe Davidson - They beat each other up, sneak attacks. I can't do my job. I... Joe cannot finish that thought as Vesuvius is launched through the wall. We see SinmaN smiling through the gaping hole.
SinmaN - Oops, didn't see you there mountain man. Hank Roberts - This is a problem. Kathy Smith vs Angela "Music" by Out of Your Mouth plays as Kathy Smith comes out from the back and stands on stage. As she stands there, the lights go out. The crowd is hushed as commotion is heard. After about twenty seconds, the lights come back on and we see Kathy is down and Angela is standing inside the ring. Angela bows for the crowd who boo her. She is too far away to have done the damage herself, but someone did it.
Ray Dees - Angela is into magic tonight. Henry Lesher - I'll be impressed if she makes herself disappear. Kathy stirs on stage. She looks up and her face is covered in blood. She staggers to her feet as Angela taunts her from the ring. Angela is telling her this is what she gets for messing with the group that owns Assault, the group that runs the show. Kathy reaches ringside and Angela missile dropkicks her through the ropes. Angela then rolls Kathy into the ring and blows a kiss to one screaming fan at ringside before rolling herself into the ring. The bell sounds and Angela stands ready in the middle of the ring. She is shouting at Kathy to get up.
Ray Dees - This is graphic. Henry Lesher - Yeah, almost entertaining. Kathy gets to her feet, turns and takes Angela's Ashes and falls in the middle of the ring. Angela goes for the pin. 1...2... Angela pulls Kathy up from the mat. The crowd boos. Angela then stands Kathy up, whips her off the ropes and gives her another Angela's Ashes. Angela then goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Angela gets the win here and Kathy isn't moving.
Ray Dees - Well it was quick. Henry Lesher - Kathy isn't so pretty now. Angela heads out of the ring as EMTs head into the ring to get Kathy out on a stretcher. Backstage we see Shannon Skanes shaking his head, wearing a red and black leather jacket with a yellow 7 stitched to the back of it. Two men walk behind him towards the ring.
King of the Pay Per View The crowd cheers as they see BLC and Chastity making their way to the ring and we are ready to crown the latest King of the Pay Per View with only these two having the want to get into this match this week. They step into the ring and nod towards each other. The camera then pans around ringside and we see the other Glamour Girls sitting in the front row to watch this match. Ophelia smiles and waves to the camera while the others don't take their eyes off of the ring. The crowd cheers as the bell sounds and the two lock up in the middle of the ring. BLC breaks the lock and whips Chastity into the corner. BLC then gives her a power slam in the middle of the ring and goes for a quick pin. 1...2... Chastity rolls a shoulder out.
Ray Dees - How is this a king of anything match? Henry Lesher - It isn't. It is a sham and everyone knows it, thus the total lack of interest.
BLC stands Chastity up, whips her off the ropes and gives her a lariat and then goes for the pin again. 1...2... Chastity kicks out. BLC then stands her up again and whips her hard into the corner. BLC then lands a series of shoulder blocks on Chastity before giving her an atomic drop. BLC then power slams Chastity to the mat and goes for the pin again. 1...2... Chastity kicks out. BLC stands her up, becoming frustrated at the lack of finish to this match up. BLC whips Chastity off the ropes. Chastity ducks a clothesline attempt and then gives BLC a spinning neckbreaker. Chastity then lands an elbow smash on BLC and heads for the ropes. At ringside, the Glamour Girls get to their feet and start cheering Chastity on. Chastity then dives from the top and misses with an elbow from there as BLC rolls out of the way.
Ray Dees - If only either of them could get control and end this, though that Chastity is sexy. It would be nice if she had a wardrobe problem like Dawn Marie did a few weeks back.

Instead, Chastity punches BLC in the gut, breaks free and kicks BLC in the chest to drive him to the corner before she gives BLC a monkey flip to the center of the ring. Chastity then stands BLC up and smiles as she whips him off the ropes. Chastity lands a knee to the gut of BLC and then lands a stunner on him. Chastity looks to her girls at ringside and they cheer her on. Chastity stands BLC up, then gives him the Stripped Bare and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Chastity gets the win. She gets to her feet and the other girls stream into the ring to congratulate her. Grace and Izarra place her on their shoulders and carry her around the ring as "Naked Cousin" by PJ Harvey plays and Chastity is announced as the winner.
Ray Dees - Wow, six strippers or wanna be strippers in the ring at once! Russia never had it this good!
Henry Lesher - We'll see much better than this pukefest. Ray Dees - You're not like me in that way, are you? Henry Lesher - You have a great attention to detail. Vesuvius then stands SinmaN up, locks him in and gives him a vertical suplex that he holds for a few seconds to let SinmaN think about what's coming. Vesuvius then goes for the pin on SinmaN. 1...2... kick out by SinmaN. Vesuvius stands SinmaN up, whips him off the ropes and both men try to shoulder block each other, but neither move. SinmaN thumbs Vesuvius in the eye, then clotheslines him to the mat. Tommy rolls SinmaN up from behind. 1...2... SinmaN manages to kick out and retain his title for at least a little bit longer.
Ray Dees - This match is lacking chicks. Henry Lesher - I admire it for that and that alone. Tommy stands SinmaN up, whips him off the ropes and backdrops him to the mat. Tommy then has his legs swept out from under him by Vesuvius from the mat. Vesuvius then applies an ankle lock submission on Tommy. Before Tommy has a chance to submit, SinmaN grabs Vesuvius around the neck and slams his head off the mat. SinmaN then two handed chokes Vesuvius down as Tommy gets to his feet against the ropes. Tommy looks down and sees Vesuvius and SinmaN locked in a mortal battle here and sees his chance. Tommy heads to the ropes and makes his way to the top rope. He then salutes the crowd as he dives, moonsaulting down onto Vesuvcius and SinmaN. He goes for the pin on Vesuvius. 1...2...3! Tommy wins the Superior title! Tommy Air rolls out of the ring, grabs his new belt from the timekeeper and high tails it out of there before Flock has a chance to attack him.
Henry Lesher - True champs run like hell when they win. Ray Dees - He's in Russia. Where's he running to? Tibet? Backstage we see Halloween Jack on his way out of the building. Joe Davidson enters to interview him, but Jack doesn't feel like being interviewed about what Tommy and Jennifer just put over on him, so he spears Joe into the wall instead before he leaves. Jack has made an impression this week after all.
Ash vs Frank Merritt vs Lone Star "Lay Low Remix" plays as Lone Star comes out to a huge ovation from the crowd. He high fives fans on his way to the ring, then tells the camera he is ready for this match and it's his time to become the champion. Lone Star steps through the ropes, climbs to the second rope and poses for the crowd as we get set for the main event. We then hear "Hate Me Now" by Nas and Frank Merritt comes out with Wren at his side and Ash walking a few paces behind him, Flock showing a united front here tonight in an attempt to throw Lone Star off.
Ray Dees - A handicap match. Henry Lesher - Most matches you fought must have felt like handicap matches, what with your handicapped talent and all.
Ray Dees - You know, I suddenly understand why people who sit next to you wind up having the urge to stick their foot up your ass.
Ash and Frank enter the ring side by side and inform Lone Star that he is in for a long night. The lights then fade and we hear "No More Tears" by Ozzy Osbourne and Scavenger appears in the apotlight on stage a few moments later with his chair in hand and Ophelia at his side! Scavenger is wearing a referee's shirt with black jeans and combat boots as he is ready to be as physical of a ref as he has to be. Scavenger races down the ramp to ringside and paces around the ring as the lights come back up and Wren avoids being in the same place as Scavenger. Scav hands his chair to Ophelia, kisses her on the forehead and then rolls into the ring. He gets to his feet and stands in the middle of the ring between Frank, Ash and Lone Star. Scavenger calls for the bell after telling Frank and Ash that Flock are officially being watched and warned before this match starts. The three men start going at it, but Frank and Ash whip Lone Star off the ropes and double backdrop him to the mat.
Ray Dees - Who the hell does Scavenger think he is, walking into the main event as ref and stealing the show?
Henry Lesher - He has no talent and should have stayed gone. Frank then picks Ash up and slams him onto the chest of Lone Star. Ash then goes for the pin. 1...2... Frank pulls Ash off. Frank then has a few words with Ash about how this is supposed to go, that it is supposed to end with them finding out between the two of them who is the best, not by pinning Lone Star. Ash plays it off as if he forgot, then pulls Lone Star to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckle. Ash winds up to charge into the corner, but Frank says that is his job and Frank charges into the corner and straight into a raised knee from Lone Star. Ash then charges into the corner and Lone Star ducks him and backdrops him over the top rope and to the floor. Lone Star then lands a series of punches to the head of Frank before boot choking him in the ropes. Scavenger tells Lone Star to break the choke, but doesn't count towards a DQ. Scavenger then asks nicely if Lone Star will please break the choke as Frank gasps for air. Lone Star continues to choke Frank. Ash rolls himself into the ring behind Lone Star and rolls him up for aht epin. 1...2... Lone Star kicks out. Ash accuses Scavenger of a slow count. Scavenger won't hear anything of it, defending his ability to count to three.
Henry Lesher - Scavenger has an agenda in this match, to not allow Flock to make it to the title.
Ray Dees - No one in that ring is without an agenda. That's what the main event is all about.
Ash stands Lone Star up. Lone Star knees Ash in the gut. Frank then grabs Lone Star and picks him up for a power bomb. Scavenger starts telling Frank a story about his childhood which causes Frank to pause. Lone Star then lands a series of punches on Frank to break free of the hold. Frank takes a blind swing at Scavenger who ducks out of the way. The punch catches Ash square on the jaw. Ash looks down and finds blood trickling from the corner of his mouth. He then lands a series of knife edge chops on Frank, whips him off the ropes and gives him a spinebuster to the mat. The fans cheer as they hope this is the beginning of the end of Flock as we know it. Lone Star grabs Ash by the hair, turns him around and gives him a reverse neackbreaker. He then turns his attention to Frank, dropping him with a stunner. Lone Star then grabs Ash, cinches him in and gives him a snap suplex to the mat. Lone Star then climbs to the top rope and waits for the first member of Flock to turn and face him. Lone Star lands a missile dropkick to the chin of Frank Merritt and then goes for the pin. 1...2... Frank manages to kick out. Lone Star then pulls Frank to his feet, sets him and delivers a tornado DDT and again goes for the pin. 1...2... Ash breaks it up, kicking Lone Star in the head. Ash then whips Lone Star through the ropes to the floor.
Henry Lesher - Well at least he's out of there. Ray Dees - He has done better than I thought he would. Ash stands Frank up, then delivers the Pipe Cashing on him and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Ash wins the world title! Outside the ring, Lone Star flies into a rage, grabbing a chair and sliding into the ring. He takes a swing at Ash, but Ash slips out of the ring and the chair strikes the top rope instead. Scavenger calls to Ophelia at ringside and she tosses Scavenger's chair into the ring to him. Lone Star rears back to drill Frank in the head, but his chair shot is blocked by Scavenger. Ash then reaches into the ring and pulls Scavenger out. Lone Star swings the chair again and strikes Frank in the head with it. Ash starts sparring with Scavenger at ringside as security streams out of the back and Ash is announced as the new world champ. The crowd starts some chants which are both in English and not very nice to the new champion.
Ray Dees - That's not a very nice thing to call the champ. Henry Lesher - I don't see a champ here, just an event people will demand their money back for buying.
Ray Dees - That does it for Blind Rage! Next month it's Sunstroke! See you then! Ash leaves the crowd of security with his belt raised. Flock comes out to help him celebrate. Frank rolls out of the ring and goes over to Ash. The two look ready to fight again. Instead, Frank shakes hands with the new champ and pats the belt, saying the rematch will come soon enough. Flock then head out, on top of the world tonight.
