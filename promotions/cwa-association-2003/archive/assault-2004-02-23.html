CWA Assault, 23 February 2004

23 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Boise, Idaho makes a lot of noise when they see that the show is set to begin. It's another packed house for tonight's Assault, the one that will set up the last few matches for the pay per view, the one which will lead us into Malicious Intent. Backstage we see Dante with Ray Dees.
Ray Dees - Tonight is the last event before Malicious Intent. What are your thoughts as we get set for a major pay per view to start the month of March, one that sees the return to the ring for a one shot deal with Dante teaming with Shannon and Q?
Dante - My thoughts are simple. I run the show. I am the main event. I will see to it that Ash, Max and Nathan get nowhere at Malicious Intent, that my team gets the win even if my partners are a pair who also aren't worthy of my time. Since they are also former world champs, I'll be nice about it this one time.
Ray Dees - Truer words have never been spoken. Dante - Where the hell has your show been the past two weeks? Ray Dees - Turns out that rash was something important. However, it's all cleared up now so I can bring my show back on Sunday.
Dante - You're a freak. Gothic Angel vs Frank Merritt "Right Round" by Dope plays as Gothic Angel makes her way to the ring, led by Chaz Jakobs aka Pyro. They get a mixed reaction as they make their way down the ramp to open this edition of Assault. Angel steps into the ring and smiles as the fans cheer in some areas. Other areas appear to really hate the return of Youthful Aggression, not that Angel or Chaz would care. "Seizure of Power" by Marilyn Manson begins to play as Frank Merritt comes out to a chorus of boos. He laughs all the way to the ring as he has a few choice words for fans who have similar words for him in return. Frank then gives a warning to Chaz at ringside before he enters the ring. The bell sounds and he attacks Angel with a series of knife edge chope. He then whips her into the opposite corner and gives her a German suplex out of it that he holds for the pin. 1...2... Angel manages to roll a shoulder out.
Ricky Simms - Welcome to Assault. This match starts things off this week as we head into Malicious Intent next Monday where a lot is on the line already and not all matches have been announced.
Henry Lesher - Third week we have almost a match to start things off with a girl against a monster and the Artist Formerly Known As Pyro at ringside.
Frank picks Angel up and body slams her to the mat. He then turns his attention to Chaz, who has jumped up on the apron. Frank dropkicks Chaz off the apron and Angel kips up to her feet. Angel then DDTs Frank to the mat before he has a chance to figure out what's going on. Angel goes for a quick pin. 1...2... kick out by Frank. Angel then sits Frank up and places him in a sleeperhold. Frank quickly gets to his feet, elbows Angel in the ribs to break the hold and then gives her a fallaway slam. Angel rolls herself out of the ring to take a breather, only to be followed by Frank. Chaz makes his way around the ring and confronts Frank, getting between him and Angel. Frank looks both ways as if shocked by this bold intrusion, then grabs Chaz and tosses him head first into the steel corner post. Angel then lands a spinning heel kick to the jaw of Merritt. Angel rolls herself into the ring and raises his arms in victory, convinced Merritt won't be getting up in time to beat the ten count. Merritt uses the apron to pullhimself to his feet and then rolls himself into the ring just before the ten count. Angel starts putting the boots to him, but her rolls over, grabs the foot of Angel and spins her to the mat.
Ricky Simms - Eeffective attacks by both, but Frank always seems to be in control even when he takes shots from Gothic Angel.
Henry Lesher - Frank beat and injured Dizz last week. What does that tell you? The guy is a monster. He is not going to be stopped tonight.
Frank gets to his feet and grabs Angel by the hair. He then headbuuts her back down to the mat and looks to the crowd. Chaz slides into the ring with a chair in hand. Frank ducks a wild swing with the chair and wags a finger at Chaz. He then gives Chaz a stunner. He rolls Chaz out of the ring and Angel gets to her feet. Angel tries for another spinning heel kick, but Frank ducks it, then picks her up and delivers an atomic drop on her. He then looks to the crowd and motions that this match is over. Chaz again enters the ring with a chair in hand. Frank grabs the chair from him, tosses it out of the ring, then delivers a Blunt Trauma on Chaz. He turns, takes a kick to the face from Angel and she whips him into the corner buckle. Frank comes out, ducks a clothesline attempt and then gives her a Blunt Trauma and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Frank Merritt pulls off the win here over Gothic Angel!
Ricky Simms - Impressive display there from Frank Merritt as if in a handicap match. Henry Lesher - He really is one strong Flock member. Frank Merritt grabs a microphone from the ring announcer and begins to speak. Frank Merritt - I beat Dizz. I beat Angel and Chaz down here. I have been on a major roll. I deserve a match at Malicious Intent! I NEED a match at Malicious Intent! I need...
Gloria Lavin comes out at the top of the stage as "Not Gonna Get Us" by TATU plays. She is smiling as she responds to Frank.
Gloria Lavin - Frank, Frank, Frank, did you think I left you out? Did you think you had no match? Well, I have some news for you. At Malicious Intent, you have the joy and pleasure of facing.... Vigilante in YOUR shot at glory, your chance to become the world heavyweight champion!
Frank looks stunned in the ring as the crowd cheers. Gloria Lavin - I have been listening to you shoot your mouth off, Frank. I have heard a lot from the Flock lately. I want to see what you guys can do when it really matters. Next Monday is the most important day of your life, Merritt. Don't let it slip by.
"Not Gonna Get Us" plays as Frank starts smiling inside the ring. Ricky Simms - Frank gets a major shot at Malicious Intent. None of us saw that coming. Henry Lesher - It stands to reason. Most of the top guys are in that six man cage match. It's an opening for some of the mid card talent ad we'll see if they have what it takes to become top dogs in the CWA.
Backstage we see Max Cameron shaking his head as he stands next to Joe Davidson. Joe Davidson - Max, you are appearing in this huge six man tag match main event this coming Monday. Your thoughts?
Max Cameron - My thoughts are simple. I have been in the CWA for some time now and this match is an opportunity. I can either help my partners defeat the powerful or I can help the powerful by turning on my partners. Once that cage door is closed, all bets are off.
Joe Davidson - And I guess you won't tip your hand here. Max Cameron - You guess right. Ricky Simms - Dear God, is he going to cover him with that thing? Henry Lesher - It is all legal. I commend Ash for that original thinking. As Ash nears the fallen Leech with the trash bin, Leech finds the strength to get to his feet. He dropkicks Ash in the back. Ash's head bounces off of the trash bin and Leech then rolls Ash up for the pin. 1...2...3! Leech retains the title! The ref retrieves the belt and hands it to the bloodied Leech. From inside the trash bin two figures emerge and the crowd is silenced. SinMan and Dragon turn Leech around and give him a 3-D on the floor! They then pick him up and toss him into the trash bin. SinMan then locks it shut as Dragon kicks away at it while he laughs. Ash starts to get up with a smile on his face. SinMan shoves the trash bin all the way to the wall and then poses with Dragon. They then take Ash away for medical attention as we see Moose and Vigilante jump down from the stage to try and free Leech from the trash. Moose and Vig start fishing through and pull out a bunch of 420 Flock T-shirts that are on top of Leech. They have Leech's name on them, simply crossed out in red while the other names are written in green. Dragon, Ash, SinMan, Frank Merritt, Harmony Fallacia. It says something on the front about taking out the trash.
Ricky Simms - What the...? I am speechless! Henry Lesher - I am too.... they... they.... umm.. Ricky Simms - I guess that wraps up a shocking edition of Assault. This changes everything...
The fans are silent as Leech is pulled from the trash bin and EOS looks both concerned and just plain pissed off. The Flock is all back now. The rules just changed all over again.
