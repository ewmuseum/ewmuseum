CWA (Association, 2003) — Card

CWA Assault, 5 January 2004

5 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's a packed house as we begin another edition of CWA's Assault. We forego the opening credits this week. We forego the blustering of the masses and instead we see Ingee Shmelky with Lunacy Muse standing next to a board which has the 16 slots open for the CWA Tournament set to begin at Battle of the Warriors.
Ingee Shmelky - Welcome to the revealing of Bracket A for the Best Wrestler in the World Tournament. Here we will discover who faces who in round one of a tournament that will test the wills and the strength of half of this roster. Lunacy...
Lunacy Muse - Ingee here as Commish has picked sixteen men and women for this tournament with the hope of crowning a fair and just winner. He...
Ingee Shmelky - *tapping Lunacy on the shoulder* Actually, you picked them and... Lunacy Muse - Let's get to the names, shall we? In Bracket A we have Dante facing Spikey Tiger! We also have Ash taking his Flocking reputation into a match against the always enraged Pyro. That seals Bracket A.
We head straight to ringside where we see Henry Lesher and Ricky Simms reacting to the early reveals for the tournament.
Henry Lesher - Great first four for this event. Dante and three other guys. I am looking forward to this now with great anticipation.
Ricky Simms - There are some great names in there already. Ash is capable of an upset or two in this tournament and Pyro has had some very good matches, too. Spikey Tiger may jump into this and pull off a first round upset over Dante if the former Commish gets too cocky.
Henry Lesher - Please, Spikey beating Dante? Not going to happen Night Dweller vs Jakob "Mother" by Danzig plays as Night dweller is set to come out. Instead of coming out alone, he comes out already going at it tooth and nail with Jakob. The two battle at the top of the ramp and Jakob attempts a choke slam only to take an elbow to the side of the head from Night dweller. Jakob then shoves Night Dweller right off the top of the stage and Night Dweller crashes twenty feet to mats on the floor. Jakob then launches himself off the ramp and lands a flying elbow smash to the throat of Night Dweller.
Ricky Simms - Explosive start to things here. Henry Lesher - I love this. No big entrances, just fighting. Jakob stands Night Dweller up, whips him into the nearby audio equipment and then grabs him by the hair and pulls him to his feet again. Jakob winds up for a knock out punch and takes a low blow from Night Dweller. Jakob crumbles to the floor and Night Dweller starts putting the boots to him. Night Dweller grabs a small speaker and throws it at Jakob, striking him in the chest. Night Dweller then picks Jakob up and power slams him to the floor. As Night Dweller appears to be picking up steam, from above comes Ezra with a double axe handle to the back of Night Dweller. Ezra helps Jakob up and the two start putting the boots to Night Dweller.
Ricky Simms - This match is not happening tonight. Henry Lesher - But revel in the violence, Ricky. This is great violence. As Ezra and Jakob continue to beat on Night Dweller, The Cossack emerges from the back with a chair in hand and smashes Jakob over the head first, then Ezra. He then strikes them a second time over the head and drags the battered Night Dweller away from the scene of carnage. The match is declared a no contest and the bell never even rang to get it underway as BOD find a new feud to start.
Ricky Simms - What a way for BOD to let the CWA know that they are back. Henry Lesher - Things should prove to be very interesting when those two forces collide. We then head back to Ingee and Lunacy who show the names populating Bracket A in the tournament. We know now that Dante, Ash, Pyro and Spikey Tiger will battle for a space in the final four. Now we are about to see the next four names.
Lunacy Muse - Now we move on to Bracket B. Four more names of wrestlers vying for the title as the Best Wrestler in the World, a million dollars and a title shot if they are not already world champion. Let's announce these names. Dizz has a first round date with Romeo Salvador. The Moose has a first round date with Nacho Mauma, the enigma who has been lurking about in a mask for a long time now. One of these four will emerge as a Final Four competitor. Of course, I wish the best of luck to them all.
We head back to ringside where Henry Lesher is shaking his head. Henry Lesher - I must admit that Bracket B looks pretty tough for anyone to emerge from unscathed, so it looks like Dante has an easy road to the final.
Ricky Simms - If you say so. I mean, Dizz has been a three time champion. He did win the only Triple Crown.
Henry Lesher - He's merely a man. Dante is a God. Ricky Simms - I am gonna go throw up now. Vigilante vs Max Cameron "Hell's Bells" plays as Max Cameron makes his way to the ring. The crowd has the usual negative response with a few sporadic cheers. Max steps onto the apron and through the ropes and holds his hardcore title high. That's the same hardcore title Vigilante laid claim to not very long ago. As Max hands the belt to the ref, we hear "Back In Black" by AC/DC and we see Vigilante on his way to the ring with the Superior title over his shoulder as always. The crowd cheers him and he swallows it all up. He points in the ring at Max and shakes a finger at him and states he is the true number one contender. Max responds by waving Vigilante into the ring. Vigilante nods and steps up onto the apron and then slowly steps through the ropes. The two then stand nose to nose and Max offers Vig his hand. Vig looks down, then looks to the crowd. They warn him not to do it, but Vig nods and shakes hands with Max as the bell sounds. There is no cheap shot by Max which surprises many watching the match.
Henry Lesher - Tell me Max isn't playing Mister Nice Guy here. Ricky Simms - Apparently he is. Vig and Max lock up. Max places Vig in an arm bar. Max somersaults out of the arm bar and clotheslines Vig to the mat. Max then stands Vig up, whips him to the corner and lands an avalanche on him while he's in there. Max then places Vig in a headlock and DDTs him to the mat and tries for the pin in front of a shocked crowd. 1...2... kick out by the Top Face of 2003 against the Most Improved Wrestler of 2003, Max Cameron. Max stands Vigilante up, gives him a couple of knife edge chops to the chest and then Vig comes to and responds with a series of knife edge chops of his own. Vigilante then hip tosses max to the mat and places him in a full Nelson. The ref asks Max if he submits, but Max refuses to give up this early in the match. Max inches his way over towards the ropes. The crowd cheers Vig on to hold him there. However, Max does reach the ropes with one foot and the hold is broken by order of the referee.
Ricky Simms - These two going at it for the right to be declared the number one contender. Henry Lesher - Which means the winner faces Dante at Battle of the Warriors. Vigilante places Max into a headlock. Max then shoves Vig off to the ropes and shoulder blocks him to the mat when he returns to the center of the ring. Max then lands a leg drop on Vigilante. Max goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Vigilante. Max then stands Vigilante up and whips him into the corner. He then charges into a superkick to the jaw from Vigilante. Max falls like a ton of bricks and Vig goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Max Cameron. Vigilante then sits Max up and applies a sleeperhold on him. Max immediately senses that he is in trouble and gets to his feet, then picks Vig up and gives him a fallaway slam to break the hold.
Ricky Simms - Nice counter by the hardcore champion. He really has come a long way in the CWA.
Henry Lesher - He got one flukey win over Dante, but he's still not on that championship level. Hardcore champs fade away quickly and rarely, if ever, move up.
Max gets to his feet and pulls Vigilante to his. The two start trading blows in the middle of the ring. Vig starts forcing Max back to the ropes and then rakes his eyes before headbutting him and then whipping him off the ropes. Vigilante gives Max a Rock Bottom to the mat. Vigilante then looks to the crowd who cheers. Vigilante gets to his feet and pulls Max to him. He then sets Max up and delivers a tombstone piledriver on him and tries for the pin. 1...2...3! Vigilante pulls off the win and is the number one contender for the world title!
Ricky Simms - Huge win here for Vigilante as he now gets to set his sights on Dante at Battle of the Warriors on January 19th!
Henry Lesher - For sure, this has to feel good for him, but it won't feel good for long. Once Dante gets ahold of him inside that ring, all bets are off and that smile is dead.
Ricky Simms - I like how you hide who you cheer for. We now head back to Ingee and Lunacy who are ready to reveal the participants in the next bracket of the tournament.
Lunacy Muse - Bracket C is to be revealed now as we have just witnessed Vigilante earning a spot in the main event to fight Dante after this tournament has wrapped up. The CWA is fully aware that two people may face each other more than once in the same night during this event. That is what Battle of the Warriors is all about. Now, without further adieu, Bracket C. Brackets A and B will face off in the Semi Finals as will bracket C and D. Bracket C's matches are Q vs. Jakob from BOD and Sal Clemenzo in a return match against Leech. That concludes Bracket C and we'll reveal D after the next match up.
We again head to ringside where Ricky and Henry will comment. Henry Lesher - Sal has to be the favored son there. I mean, he has the real talent. Leech hasn't got a hope. Oh, but Q is in there too, so...
Ricky Simms - That is quite the predicament you have found yourself in, isn't it? There is a potential for Sal to face Q.
Henry Lesher - I don't like it. The Moose vs Ash "The New Shit" by Marilyn Manson plays as Ash makes his way to the ring led by Angela. The crowd has a mixed reaction to him as he steps onto the apron and then into the ring. He is in the ring for a few moments when we hear "If You Want Blood" by AC/DC blaring over the PA system. The Moose comes out to his usual huge ovation. Moose steps into the ring and is attacked by Ash from behind. Ash chop blocks Moose and then starts wailing away with elbows to the back of the knee he just chopped out from under Moose. Ash then attempts an ankle lock submission and Moose grabs the bottom rope and pulls himself out of the ring. Moose tries to get feeling back into that leg as Ash follows him out and chop blocks the knee out from under him again. Ash slaps on an ankle lock and the ref shouts at him to return to the ring. Ash gives the ref the finger, but releases Moose. He then picks Moose up and drops him, knee first on the steps.
Ricky Simms - Vicious attack here by Ash. Henry Lesher - I like it. Maybe the Flock is revisiting that killer instinct of old.... wait, they never had one. Never mind.
Ash rolls Moose into the ring and just beats the count when he rolls himself inside. Ash stands Moose up, takes a thumb to the eye and then Moose kicks Ash in the gut with his good leg. Moose then delivers a bulldog and tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Ash. Moose stands Ash up, whips him into the corner buckle and then kicks him in the back before delivering a reverse neckbreaker and again trying for the pin. 1...2... kick out again by Ash. Moose then stands Ash up. Ash grabs the sore leg of Moose, picks it up and twists it. Moose then roundhouse kicks Ash with his good leg, knocking both men to the mat. Moose grabs at his knee as he tries to get to his feet. Ash sweeps the legs out from under Moose. Moose lands wqith his throat over the bottom rope. Ash gets to his feet, comes off the ropes and jumps onto the back of Moose, choking him on the rope. Ash then shoves Moose to the mat and boot chokes him. Angela cheers Ash on from ringside.
Ricky Simms - Ash not giving up the advantage thus far here. Henry Lesher - True, but Moose is one of those lucky wrestlers when it comes to matches like this.
Ash stands Moose up in the corner and takes some body shots at him. Moose suddenly finds the energy to knee Ash in the gut, then forearm smashes him in the head. Moose then clotheslines Ash to the mat and tries for the pin. 1...2... Ash manages to kick out. Moose stands Ash up, then snap mares him to the mat. Moose then stands Ash up, picks him up for a vertical suplex and his knee buckles, causing him to drop Ash for an unexpected brainbuster. Moose then rolls over for an attempted pin. 1...2... Ash gets a foot on the bottom rope. The crowd has to sit down now, certain that was going to be a three count to no avail.
Ricky Simms - Miracle that Ash got out of that. Henry Lesher - Moose never hooked the leg. Whatever happened to the days when you always hooked the leg?
Moose stands Ash up with Moose still favoring that leg. He whips Ash off the ropes and ducks his head to apply a backdrop when Ash somersaults over Moose and leg sweeps him down. Ash then applies a Boston Crab on Moose. Moose reaches for the ropes as the fans start a chant for him. Moose lies inches too far away from the ropes and slaps the mat to spur the fans on to cheer for him. He lunges once, still too far from the ropes...... he lunges a second time, this time managing to grab the bottom rope to force the break. Ash has a few words for the ref as the break is made. Ash then drags Moose to the middle of the ring to attempt another Boston Crab. Moose kicks Ash in the chest before he can turn it over, then quickly gets to his feet and lands a series of rights and lefts on Ash. Moose then whips Ash into the corner, picks him up and delivers a Moose Driver on Ash and goes for the pin. 1...2...3! Moose gets the win out of nowhere. The crowd erupts, but their tone changes as Leech and Frank Merritt hit the ring and start pummelling Moose into the ground.
Henry Lesher - Wonder if Moose feels like a winner now. Ricky Simms - Sick attack here by the Flock. Leech renews his pleasantries with Moose here.
As the attack ensues, Vigilante races into the ring to make the save. He lands a series of shots on Leech, but Merritt and Ash begin to overwhelm him and he starts to go down to the mat. We then see Max Cameron coming down the ramp slowly towards the ring. The crowd has a mixed reaction as we don't know which way he is going to go here.
Ricky Simms - Here comes Max. This should be interesting. Henry Lesher - He fought Vigilante earlier tonight and shook the man's hand, but he was Ash's partner last week.
Max folds and picks up a steel chair at ringside and looks to the crowd. He then slides into the ring and tells Ash to pick Vigilante up. Max hauls off with the chair and a smile on his face and nails Ash in the head instead of Vigilante! Max then clocks Leech and chases Frank Merritt from the ring. The crowd cheers as Max stands in the ring protecting Moose and Vigilante.
Henry Lesher - Shows Ash who he can trust. I guess that makes it his own fault. Ricky Simms - Does this put Max into EOS? Henry Lesher - Do I care? We then head for the reveal of the final four spots for the tournament. Doubtless that there are some backstage waiting with bated breath to see the last four and how this whole tournament looks. Lunacy is now alone. We are getting the feeling Ingee isn't the strongest of Commissioners around.
Lunacy Muse - Bracket D's winner will face the winner of C, as I stated earlier. Now, while Ingee is out trying to find a song for his boy band, let's talk about the final matches. We have Vigilante facing new star KillSwitch from Us Against the World and we have Max Cameron against Frank Merritt which will make him happy after the mess we just watched inside the ring. Enjoy your tournament, people. That's your sixteen stars.
We head back to ringside where Henry and Ricky are prepared to give feedback. Henry Lesher - Clearly a weak four, it doesn't matter who walks out of that. They get rolled by either Q or Sally.
Ricky Simms - I take issue with that when two title holders are in there as well as two very strong newer contenders in the CWA.
Henry Lesher - I know you take issue with it. I would expect nothing less. Reality vs Dark Forces vs Eternal Nightmares "Better Harder Faster Stronger" by Daft Punk plays as Shelia Lavin and Q make their way to the ring. The crowd boos them as Q steps into the ring. He then points to the titantron and we see Ingee Shmelky in his office, so we assume this is after he left Lunacy Muse.
The name plate reads "Assualt GM- Ingee Shemlky". After a second a NKOTB track begins to play and we see Ingee dance into shot, busting out some phat Nutbush type dance moves, if by "busting out" you meant "screwing up" and by "phat" you meant "simple". But after a moment, a loud knock is heard. Ingee turns off the boom box he's using.
Ingee Shmelky - Come in. After a moment, we hear a door open and in walks Q, with the crowd showing some mixed emotions, but mostly boos. Q looks slightly flustered.
Ingee Shmelky - What do you want Mr W? Q - That's Q. Look, this is going to be hard regardless, so let's just get this over with. Look, my niece-
Ingee Shmelky - You have a niece? Q - Yeah, she's my sister's daughter, look... Ingee Shmelky - How old is she? Q - Who, my sister or my neice? Ingee Shmelky - Both. Q - 7 and 27. Ingee Shmelky - You neice is 27? Q looks at Ingee, then raises his hand as if to slap Ingee. Ingee, surprisingly, doesn't flinch.
Q - No. She's 7, my older sister is 27. Look, point is, for some unknown reason, my neice, the 7 year old, is a fan of Flava.
Ingee Shmelky - Cool. You want tickets to our next show? Q - NO, no, no, I mean, what use would they be? My neice is in Australia, and my sister's in Iraq.
Ingee Shmelky - She in the army? Q - Australian Air Force, logisitics manager. This gets a cheer from the crowd. Ingee Shmelky - So what do you want? Q - Well, it's her birthday, and I was hoping... Q pulls out a slightly larger than 8 by 10 glossy photo of Ingee, doing a "Yo Word" pose. Ingee Shmelky - Oh, fine. Ingee gets a pen, and goes to sign across the picture. Q pulls it away from him. Q - NO! I mean, not across you, it'll be hard to see the signature. Just here, across the bottom.
Ingee Shmelky - Ok, ok. Ingee signs the picture. Ingee Shmelky - Now, if you'll excuse me, I have to- The phone rings. Ingee picks it up. Ingee Shmelky - Hello? What? What? I'll be right there! Tell Lunacy to put the midget down!
Ingee runs out of the room, and Q calls after him. Q - That's cool, not a problem Ingee. Q then pulls the picture apart, revealing a dense, typed document under the photo, with Ingee's signiture at the bottom. Q scrunches the picture up.
Q - Not a problem at all. We don't know what that document was as the footage ends. Q - Now, here I stand inside the ring and what I crumpled up was a contract which stated Ingee stays on for six months as Commissioner. Once Battle of the Warriors is done and my hand is played, little boy band boy is gonna be out of work. I will run the show.
Q casts the microphone aside as we hear "Know" by System of a Down and Dante and Fallout make their way to the ring. They receive louder boos than even Reality did entering the ring. Dante steps onto the apron and holds up his world title for Q to see. Dante then hands the belt to the ref and steps through the ropes. Dante and Fallout stand in one corner while Q and Shelia stand in the center of the ring. We then hear "Phenomenon" by LL Cool J and Jon Shocker and Black Panther make their way to the ring. The crowd cheers as Jon jumps onto the apron where he takes a clothesline right back off from Dante. Q then attacks Dante as Shelia charges at Fallout. Black Panther helps Shocker up and then both slide into the ring and join the fray as the bell sounds to get this match underway. Panther, Dante and Q are left as the three inside the ring as Shelia and Fallout brawl right over the top rope. Jon Shocker simply takes his place in his team's corner as they start their title defense here.
Ricky Simms - What the hell is going on around here? Q crumpling up official CWA documents?
Henry Lesher - He owns you, Rick. Deal with it. Dante is thrown out of the ring by Q which leaves Q battling with Black Panther. The two exchange punches and kicks, but Q starts to take over and drives Panther to a neutral corner in a lock with Panther. The ref demands a clean break, but doesn't get it as Q lands a cheap shot to the jaw of Panther. Q then hip tosses Panther to the middle of the ring. Dante and Fallout take their place in a corner and opt to send Dante into the action here. Dante grabs Q and headbutts him to the mat. Dante then picks up Black Panther and body slams him to the mat. Dante then grabs Q into a headlock only to take a belly to back suplex from the larger and stronger Q. Q then gets to his feet and DDTs Panther to the mat as he gets to his feet and goes for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Black Panther as Reality were inches from winning the titles.
Henry Lesher - That was a three count. Ricky Simms - That's right. Maintain your cool, Doc. Q stands Panther up. Dante gets to his feet and slams Panther and Q's heads together. Q and Panther look at each other, turn to Dante and both slug him with a right hook and he is launched into the air. The crowd cheers, but that changes as Q tags in Shelia and she attacks Panther from behind. Shelia stands Panther up and takes a headbutt from Panther who then lunges to tag in Shocker. Shocker leaps over the top rope and lands a clothesline on Shelia. Dante tags in Fallout who races into the ring, grabs Shocker from behind and delivers a reverse neckbreaker on him. Fallout tries for the pin. 1...2... kick out by Jon Shocker. Fallout then stands Shocker up, whips him off of the ropes and gives him a tilt a whirl backbreaker before trying for the pin again. 1...2... Shelia lands a superkick to the jaw of Fallout out of nowhere and goes for the pin with a handful of tights. 1...2...3! Reality wins as Dante and Black Panther are too slow to make the save!
Henry Lesher - God likes me! God likes me! Ricky Simms - Funny, I was about to say He hates me... Q and Shelia leave the ring as Dark Forces get into it with the Eternal Nightmares inside the ring. Q and Shelia hold the belts high, the new tag team champions of the CWA!
Henry Lesher - Q has held nearly every belt in the CWA and he and Shelia make fine champions indeed.
Ricky Simms - Impressive resume for Q, but Battle of the Warriors looms in the distance and...
The ending of the show is interrupted as we see Mike Minor on the titantron. Mike Minor - Hey guys! Just wanted to let you know that the former champs are here and ready to lay claim to those belts. See you at Battle of the Warriors.
Mike laughs as Q and Shelia stand dumbfounded, uncertain what to make of this. A brief image flashes on the Titantron of Los Especialistos and then the screen goes black. We then end out show.
