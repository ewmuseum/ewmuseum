Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Violence Authority Wrestling ran through an eGroups mailing list, and its surviving application packet, posted 9 November 1999, was introduced by its own in-fiction vice president, Antonio Thorne, ahead of a stated 15 November opening. VAW pitched itself around “the harder, more extreme parts of wrestling,” hence its own name, Violence Authority.

Five championships were named at the outset: the VAW World Heavyweight and Hardcore Heavyweight titles, a Tag Team Championship, a Death Match Champion contested in a weekly deathmatch, and a Television Championship defended weekly on air. Two shows were planned each week, “Monday Massacre” and a Friday flagship called “The Core,” with pay-per-views held monthly.

No card, roster reveal, or result from this VAW survives; the application packet is all that survives, and no closure notice survives either. Whether the promotion opened on schedule isn't known.
