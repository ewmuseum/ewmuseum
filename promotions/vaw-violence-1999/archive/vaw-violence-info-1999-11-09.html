Championships ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ -- The VAW has various Championships on its venue. There very diverse to always be aware of the title situations because some require gimmick matches. They are as follows in order of importance:
VAW World Heavyweight Championship - The most prestigious title in VAW. The big kahuna. You have to be the best at what you do (or cheating!) to get this title. The best of the best hold this title, and it is to be held with prestige. -
VAW Hardcore Heavyweight Championship - Can you get extreme!? Then get this title! This belt is for the extreme, crazy whack wrestlers in the VAW, and you have to take pain and be willing to BLEED on live television for this title. Only hardcore maniacs can snag this peice of gold!
VAW Tag Team Championships - Are you a people person? Then get yourself a partner and into the ring! The ebst two men, as a team, are the only eligable for this title.. No 1 person
Tag Team Champ, hence the name Team. Get a friend, get some skill, and your in!
VAW Death Match Champion Reserved for only the most Hardcore, this division is not for the weak of heart. Will feature a weekly deathmatch.
VAW Television Champion This belt will be defended on TV once a week. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ -- Shows & PPVs: VAW has two shows per week, one on Friday and one on Monday.
