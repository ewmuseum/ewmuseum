Application /\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\ 1. Explanations of GCWA 1.a. Real Explanation GCWA stands for Gulf Coast Wrestling Alignment. GCWA was founded in December, 1999 by Monica Zago. Currently, we are still a young federation that is buidling on talent and trying to gain exposure. Our main goal here is something that you hear alot of in this sport we call e-wrestling, but hardly ever see. . . _FUN!_ Yes, many feds say they will give you fun, but many don't. GCWA will though~! We plan to bring the ultimate environment for all of our members along with our fans. 1.b. Fantasy Explanation In the early 1980's, a indy fed in the state of Texas called TRW, opened up. It gained alot of exposure, mainly in the south. People from all over the area begin to come to shows. Wrestlers from all over the country that were looking for their big break headed to TRW. The owner of the TRW, Mr. Foder, wasn't expecting such a large amount of interest. Getting big-headed and greedy, Mr. Foder highered the prices on almost everything TRW had to do with. The sudden inflation of prices drove many people away and drove TRW to declare bankruptcy. Soon after, a young, promising business major named Monica Zago saw a shine of life in the dead federation. Ms. Zago got a bank loan and everything else she would need and opened up the GCWA. . . the branch of TRW. 2. Who Runs GCWA The GCWA is ran by Monica Zago. She has hand picked some of the best people she could find to help her out with the daily operations of the GCWA along with some great match writters. 3. What Type of Federationg is GCWA? GCWA is a small regionally-based organization, who, when it gains enough prestige and such, will tour America and possibly more. But for now, they tour over small sections of Texas and Arizona, but not much more. The organization itself wants to provide an enjoyable environment for everyone, much like the WWF or WCW. But, with not near as many resources. We're small, much like the ECW, but not quite as "hardcore." As for matches and reports, we will use ABS (Action Brackets System) where action is put in brakets with commentating in the middle. 4. Where Is GCWA Based? The GCWA is based in Austin, Texas. . . and will mainly tour Texas and Arizona has stated above. 5. How Are Matches Decided? GCWA is a booked fed, meaning all matches are decided by the human brain. :-) Basically, there are four factors that will decide if you win or lose: 1) Flashes 2) Angles 3) Strategies 4) Participation 6. How Do I Succeed In GCWA? 6.a. Flashes The first being flashes. This is a must for a handler. It's the only thing that the federation is going to see from you, and gets the idea's of your wrestler(s) across to the rest of the federation, and is a HUGE part in deciding winners, and instrumental in success. The person with a better flash usually wins, but sometimes, even though you have a better flash than your opponent, it doesn't always mean you're going to win. 6.b. Strats The second factor is strategies, or strats. This is more of a "tiebreaker" item, but it is _greatly_ appreciated by the writers and front office of GCWA. A strategy will explain to us what exactly you want your wrestler to do during their match and other parts of the show, as well as suggesting spots and such. It makes the writers' jobs a _great_ deal easier while writing your match. An ideal GCWA strategy will include the following: Ring Entrance (How you want your character to come to the ring): Match Strategy (What you want your character to do during their match): Suggested Spots (Some ideas for moves and such during the match): Winning Situation (What they'd do if they won): Losing Situation: (What they'd do if they lost): Other (Other things you want your wrestler to do during the show, match, etc.): 6.c. Angles Ideas The third may be the biggest factor. That being angles. Even though your roleplay was slightly better than your opponent, if the bookers have an angle planned for your opponent, and it can be furthered somehow during that match, they may go with that rather than totally choosing a winner based on roleplaying. d. Misc. Participation is also a big factor, because depending on how you participate each week will depend on how successful you will be during that week. One thing everyone should know beforehand, you will NOT go undefeated forever. This is not the WCW, and you are not Goldberg. EVERYONE will lose matches here and there during the beginning of their tenure, because we want to make sure you're in this bad boy for the long run or whether you're going to run out after a loss. If you stay in and accept the losses you take (And you WILL take some), then you should become one with the league as you work and work.. and work some more! 7. What Are The Shows Held By GCWA? 7.a. Sunday Night Rebels GCWA's Main Show, this is a full, play by play, color commentary card with long, and oh-so-exciting matches on it. You'll see most of the "big" names of the organization on this card, all title matches will be held on this card, as well as in rings and backstage interviews. Hosted by Greg Rothwell and Faith Nilson. 7.b. GCWA Flashback This show is a recap show, with short recaps of certain matches between some of the roster. There will be no title matches on this card, and you will rarely see champions on it. Hosted by GCWA's bombshell herself. . . Monica Zago! 7.c. GCWA War of Words As you can tell, this is GCWA's Roleplay/Flash show. It comes out every Wednesday and Saturday with all the flashes. 7.d. Pay Per Views These are the biggest events in wrestling, and this is what the GCWA prides itself on. The biggest matches, the biggest stars, the biggest everything on GCWA Pay Per View. GCWA holds a pay per view every two to three months, depending on how much heat is being drawn, etc. 8. What Titles Does GCWA Have? 8.a. GCWA Gulf Coast Champion The biggest prize in the federation, this title is also one of the most coveted in the sport. It's what every wrestler has his eye on when they enter the organization, is held by the best, and is defended AT LEAST once a month. 8.b. GCWA Texas State Champion A good title to hold indeed. It may not be as good as the Gulf Coast title, but whoever may hold this is clearly on their way to being the best in the GCWA. 8.c. GCWA Gulf Coast Tag Champions These titles are set for two men to win, and identify the owners as the best tag team in the land. The titles must be defended atleast once a month. 9. Policies of the GCWA 9.a. Flash Policy a) You _must_ provide _one_ flash for each week your wrestler(s) has a match against an opponent. We'd like this fed to be fun, and not so much that everyone is obsessed with winning, so we ask that you only provide us with _one_ roleplay per flash packet, so that it's easier to choose a winner, on a one-to-one scale. In rings as well a roleplay for the week are acceptable, but most likely, the in ring may not do much to the result, and remember that each show will not have more in-rings than matches. b) Keep the swearing/profanity/peep shows/whatever else obscene you guys can think of, to a minimum. Or atleast to a believable level that you could imagine seeing on television. c) In-rings are fine with us, but make sure you clear them with the executive committee beforehand. d) The deadlines for your roleplays to get on the reports are as follows: Wednesday Edition of War of Words: TUESDAY NIGHT, 11:59 PM EST. Saturday Edition ofWar of Words: FRIDAY NIGHT, 11:59 PM EST. In-Rings: SATURDAY NIGHT, 11:59 PM EST. Now, things may change due to some strange circumstances that I'd like not to think of (lateness), but unless otherwise stated, those are the deadlines. 9.b. Strat Policy a) As stated above, the basis of what a GCWA strat should look like is as follows: Ring Entrance (How you want your character to come to the ring): Match Strategy (What you want your character to do during their match): Suggested Spots (Some ideas for moves and such during the match): Winning Situation (What they'd do if they won): Losing Situation: (What they'd do if they lost): Other (Other things you want your wrestler to do during the show, match, etc.): Now, you can always add anything else you'd like to, but we kindly request that you atleast have this stuff in there somewhere. b) As far as how long and detailed the strats must be, we'd like to stress that "my wrestler comes in and wins after a chair shot" isn't exactly helpful at all, but a epic on what he'd do isn't helpful either. So, find a middle point and work with it as you will. 9.c. Angle Policy Angles are what every e-fed runs on. So, obviously, they're an integral part of this one as well. Please remember a few things when coming up with an angle to use... a) Keep it at a believable basis. If your wrestler wants to become a 2,000 year old troll, I *really* doubt that angle is going to be run. It's got to be believable to the people watching on television.. or reading, in our case. If you saw Steve Austin turn into a My Little Pony character one day on the WWF, would you say "Huh!?!" I imagine you would. b) If it involves (an)other wrestler(s), please contact them first and work something out between you, before you approach the executive committee to clear the angle. 9.d. Booking Policy As stated before, there is something everyone must understand. a)*********YOU WILL LOSE!!*********** If you want to come in with a Goldberg character, who never loses,I only have one thing to say... ...Fat chance. :) If you can't handle the idea of losing a match, _do not_ fill out an application. I _do not_ want an Excedrin headache because someone lost a match. This is the part about being a fedhead that I hate the most. Can't we all just get along? Just think of the federation as a big soap opera. The people who have the best storyline going on get the most airtime on the show. Those who don't, don't get very much time. So, the people who have the best angles, and roleplays, will get the airtime, in other words, win. So, write like (Add favorite author here), and you should be alright! 10.
