What is fantasy wrestling?] Fantasy Wrestling is the next best thing to lacing up your leather boots, and stepping into the ring. It's where the most pain you'll go through, is your fingers cramping from typing at the keyboard for too long.
Fantasy Wrestling, is ran over electronic mail, where "promoters" round up a roster of "wrestlers", and put them in his fantasy promotion, and he runs events, or "cards". Over the time, races for the gold and feuds develop, and things start to heat up.
[B. What are roleplays/flashes?] Role-plays otherwise known as flashes are simulated interviews with your wrestler being the person being interviewed. They can take place in locker rooms, on the street in a house, anywhere...You decided! That is the fun of
e-wrestling [C. What are cards?] Cards are basically wrestling shows. Raw, Heat, Shotgun, and SmackDown are WWF's cards and Nitro, Thunder, Saturday Night and Worldwide are WCW's cards. The GCWA has two weekly cards, along with Supercards. The Supercards take place every two months, although, we may have a Mini Supercard every once and awhile. Wednesday Night Splashdown takes place on Wednesday, and
Saturday Night House Party on Saturday! [D. How do the matches work?] Various feds use various methods of how the matches are figured out. The winner, like in real wrestling federations, are predetermined. And you do
NOT get to pick your moves play by play, that is up to the writer to select what moves you do. If you want to, you can tell the writer something like
"If the opponent is off his guard, I will tackle him down and beat on his head," days before the card is sent out. Basically, the winner is determined by who roleplayed better, who had the better strategy, who is on a better angle or push, and things like that.
[E. Who runs this thing?] [name withheld] and [name withheld] started the GCWA in June of 1999. [name withheld], [name withheld], and [name withheld] run the GCWA in the Present
Day. [F. What is the GCWA?} The GCWA, is the Gulf Coast Wrestling Alliance, a regional federation based out of Flordia, which plans on moving around the southern coast states all the way to Texas, and the slight budget they have. The GCWA was started on inheritance money from a family death on [name withheld] side, and
[name withheld] came in to help Beans get this off the ground. Now that Brian has left us, [name withheld] takes over. Billy has to take Emergency leave, so then [name withheld] took over. And that's who runs things today.
====================================================================== (2) Events ====================================================================== [A. Wednesday Night Splashdown] A weekly show, that is broadcast on local television across the south-eastern coast of the United States. Each show will hold interviews, and up to six matches, including title matches, and gimmick matches. This is the place to make yourself, this is the place to be.
[B. GCWA House Party] Weekly house shows held throughout the South-Eastern coast, that is broadcast on local television across the south-eastern coast of the United
States. Each show will hold interviews, and up to six matches, including title matches, and gimmick matches. This is the place to make yourself, this is the place to be.
[C. GCWA Powerslam] The Powerslam, is a show that happens almost every day, where each wrestler on the roster gets a chance to express his thoughts and feelings towards his fellow employees. This is the place for your interviews/roleplays/flashes, for your upcoming matches.
[D. Inside the GCWA] Inside the GCWA, is the newsletter that is sent out twice a week. This newsletter contains news, rumors, rankings, schedules, past results, and is the main source of all the information you'll ever need to know in the GCWA.
[E. Supercards] GCWA Supercards, are mega events that will be held once every two months. Here, is where a major angles and feuds play out, and many titles can change hands. If you have something planned, this would be the place to execute.
[F. Booking] The three owners/presidents of the GCWA, [name withheld], [name withheld] and [name withheld] schedule, and book each match in the GCWA. If you have any match requests, or questions on booking, email them.
[G. How do I win my matches?] To win your match, all you have to do is out roleplay your opponent, while having a good angle going, and a good streak, plus a strategy sheet of your strategy for the match would help too.
[H. How many matches can I have?] If you really want, you can have a match every show, but the maximum amount of matches per card is ONE, unless other circumstances say so.
====================================================================== (3) Wrestlers ====================================================================== [A. How many wrestlers can you have?] You may have no more than two wrestlers. [B. Can you have a tag team?] Yes, you can have a tag team. However there will not be tag team titles to begin with. That may change in the future if there is enough interest in the tag team division.
[C. Can you form tag teams with other handlers?] Yes, you can form a tag team with another member of the GCWA. The presidents from time to time will also make tag team combinations for he purposes of one match and angle development.
====================================================================== (4) Titles ====================================================================== [A. GCWA Coastal Championship] This is the grand, ultimate prize of the GCWA. You want to rule the leauge? Win this. You want the rookies to kiss your butt? Win this. If you want to make a name for yourself....you got it...win this! This title must be defended, at least once a month, with no exceptions. And, it is expected to be defended at every SUPERCARD that is held through out the year.
[B. GCWA Florida Championship] The next best thing to the GCWA Coastal Championship. This baby is right up there, giving the prized owner the number one contender spot, and pretty much the distinguished championship of Flordia! This title must be defended twice a month, and also is expected to be defended at every SUPERCARD that is held throughout the year.
[C. GCWA Hardcore Championship] The next best thing to those of you that think your Hardcore! Are you the King of Stipulation Matches? Can you go all out, after getting hit in the head with a chair? Do you want someone to feel the PAIN? If so, then this is the Title for you! This Title must be defended Three times a month.
[D. How do I win a title?] First, you must work your way up through the ranks. Then, if the Board sees that you are fit to get yourself a title shot, then so be it, you will be scheduled to do so. Another way, is to be highly ranked, and have the champion challenge you, or the champion to except your challenge. Then, if you beat your opponent by pinfall, submission, or otherwise, depending on stipulations, you may wear the gold!
[D. How are the rankings determined?] The GCWA rankings are determined, by participation, your win/loss record, your roleplaying ability, your gimmick, and you current angles. A point system may be brought in to determine rankings as well, if so, you will be notified.
====================================================================== (5) Flashing/Roleplaying ====================================================================== [A. How do I roleplay?] Ah, the foundation of e-wrestling. To roleplay, is very simple. First, access your email program, or website, for you HotMail users. Then, in the
TO box, please send your roleplays to [name withheld] . In the subject box, please type "[GCWA] Wrestlers Name Roleplay". Then, in the body of your email, type up the roleplay, which suits your character best for your upcoming match.
[B. What is the "closed system?"] The "Closed Flashing System", is a way that e-wrestling federations control the leauges email. The closed system, has members send all there mail to the president, or presidents, and they then send out the flashes in packets, or in GCWA's case, in the GCWA Powerslam Show!
[C. What is a "Quality" flash?] A Quality flash, is a roleplay that meets the point of the roleplay. Somone could have a four hundred line flash, the other, could have a fifty line roleplay, and the fifty-liner may make a point, and steal a win.
Things dont work by how many kilobytes your roleplay is around here. It's all about getting to the point, talking the details, and not wasting everyone's time.
[D. How many flashes can I do?] Since everyone needs there own mic time, we are only allowing =TWO= flashes per match, and or packet. The only time that you are allowed more then TWO flashes, is for a SUPERCARD matchup, where you are allowed
=FIVE= flashes per match. ====================================================================== (6) Newsletters ====================================================================== [A. Inside the GCWA] The Weekly newsletter of the GCWA! This bad boy comes hot out on the press, every Wednesday, and Saturday, and it features the current news and rumors, the current rosters, current stables and rankings, and of course the upcoming schedules of GCWA EVENTS! Be sure not to miss these, as they are the most important peice of mail you may recieve from the GCWA offices!
====================================================================== (7) Stables ====================================================================== [A. What are stables?] Stables, are like groups, or gangs of fellow wrestler employee's, that team up, to start a dynasty within a promotion. For example,
Degeneration X, the New World Order, even the newly founded NO LIMIT SOLJAZ, are all stables. Interested? Read on! [B. How do I make a stable?] Well, all you have to do, is have each member send their consent to both presidents, give us the name of the stable, and create an angle, or event to bring the group into the federation. Please, no more then five members per stable!
====================================================================== (8) Sneak Attacks ====================================================================== [A. What are Sneak Attacks?] A Sneak Attack, is either interfering in a match against a hated rival, or attacking somone inside their lockerooms, or in the hotel, or something along those lines. Have a good friend in a title match? Want to help them out? Just send a SNEAK ATTACK against that friends opponent, and we'll see what we can do! ***NOTE*** Sneak Attacks are not promised to occur 100% of the time ***NOTE***
[B. How do I do a Sneak Attack?] So now you want to play dirty? Just send an email to both presidents, with the SUBJECT BOX reading "[GCWA] Sneak Attack for Wrestlers Name", and in the body, let us know who, where, when, and how you'd like to do it, and wait for the card results to come out for the outcome!

Be sure to check this bad boy out! ====================================================================== (10) Commissioner and Card Writers ====================================================================== [A. Commissioner] The commissioner of the GCWA will be held responsible for writing matches for the Splashdown and House Party shows. The commissioner will also help in the decision making process on booking and angle development. The commissioner will also be considered a card writer.
[B. Card Writers] The card writers of the GCWA will be held responsible for writing one match for the weekly GCWA Wednesday Splashdown. There will be four card writers including the commissioner. Also, the presidents will write matches for the
Splashdown and House Party shows. [C. How do you become the Commissioner or Card Writer?] To become the commissioner or card writer just contact the presidents and tell them you are interested in one of the positions. We will then get back to you on what you need to do to apply.
