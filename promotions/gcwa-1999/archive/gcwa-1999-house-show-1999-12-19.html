GCWA House Show Austin Central High School Time - Recorded ============================= [Monica Zago]: "Hello! And welcome to the first show of the Gulf Coast Wrestling Alignment! Tonight we will have a few summarized matches that happened earlier with our annoucing team, Greg Rothwell and Faith Nilson. Then, soon enough, we will have our first live match in GCWA history between "The Missing N'Sync Member" Aaron Myles and "The Unforgiven" David Pietka. As for now, let's go to Greg Rothwell and Faith Nilson for some matches that happend earlier. [Greg Rothwell]: "Thank you Ms. Zago! I'm Greg Rothwell along with my partner Faith Nilson. We hope to bring you all the entertainment that we can bring you. [Faith Nilson]: "Thank You, Greg! That is right. Tonight, we will tell you what happened in a few matches and also bring you a live matche, as Ms. Zago stated. So, without any farther ado. . . let's get to our first match. #################### Trent Musashi v. The Mysterious One ################### [GR]: "This match was a pretty fast paced one when Trent Musashi was in control. Trent used his hardcore style to take control of The Mysterious One who was trying his hardest to gain control using a submission style of wrestling. [FN]: "The first big event in the match happened when The Mysterious One has Musashi in a Canadian Crossface Hold. Musashi punched TMO in the gut, breaking the hold. Musashi leaped up, lifted TMO up and nailed him with a DDT that come out of nowhere. Making a mistake, Musashi didn't go for the pin. . . but went to the top rope. Trent Musashi leaped off, hoping to hit the "Rocky Mountain High," but The Mysterious One put his knee right into Trent Musashi's face. [GR]: "Face being in extreme pain, Trent Musashi rolled out of the ring. The Mysterious One stayed in the ring for a few moments to catch his breath. . . a mistake. Trent Musashi snuck around to the other side of the ring and climbed back in. . . with his snowboard! Trent Musashi tapped The Mysterious One with the snowboard, but before he could swing the ref grabbed the snowboard. Trent Musashi's face grew with anger and looked right into the ref's eyes. [FN]: "With this distraction, The Mysterious One came up behind Trent Musashi and rolled him up into a small package. TMO got a two count before Musashi got enough power to kick out. The Mysterious One rolled onto his knees to try to catch his breath once again. Trent Musashi took advantage of this and nailed TMO with a forearm smash on the back of his head. Apparently knocked out cold, Trent Musashi climbed to the top rope again and waited. [GR]: "After a few moments, The Mysterious One climbed back to his feet. Trent Musashi leaped off the top rope and nailed TMO with the "Frostbite" right on the jaw. Trent Musashi climbed onto The Mysterious One and gained the three count. ################### Winner- Trent Musashi ################## [FN]: "As you can tell, we had an excellent opening match. The Mysterious One and Trent Musashi both gave it their all. But, only one guy can win, and Trent Musashi was that guy. [GR]: "Dang, I was excited when I saw Musashi bring the snowboard in too! I was waiting to see The Mysterious One's face get smashed in. . . but the stupid ref had to break it up. Oh Well, let's get to our next match. ################################ Obsessive Compulsive Disorder ( OCD ) v. Rio D. Giniro ################################ [FN]: "A pretty good match. We had a Hardcore wrestler in OCD and a Brawler in Giniro. So, as you can expect, alot of this match happened outside of the ring. The referee was generous enough to let it stay outside too. . . even a few weapons were involved. [GR]: "This match was actually hardcore, which is something that you won't see in GCWA too often. The match started out with Giniro punching OCD all over the ring. This didn't last long though. OCD grabbed Giniro by the hair and threw him out of the ring. The referee didn't stop OCD from going outside though. This is where it got good! [FN]: "OCD grabbed Giniro by the head and slammed it into the ground repeatly. Giniro fought back though by flipping OCD underneath him and putting a Full Nelson on him. OCD reached back and eye gauged Giniro. Giniro released the hold and grabbed his eyes in pain. This gave OCD enough time to grab a chair and nail Giniro over the back with it. Giniro fought his way up though and started to trade punches with OCD. Giniro and OCD tied up. Giniro won it and nailed OCD with a powerslam onto the steel chair! [GR]: "By now, I was screaming my head off! I love seeing people getting hurt like that! Anyways. Giniro looked around and picked the time keeper's bell up. He set it down on the ground and picked OCD up. He inverted DDT'ed OCD right onto the time keepers bell. OCD was moving. But, before the referee could call the match, OCD tripped Giniro into the steel post. God only knows how OCD moved after that. Giniro climbed back into the ring to rest his aching body. OCD slowly climbed into the ring and was met by a waiting Giniro. Giniro nailed OCD with a sideslam, and sat him up in the middle of the ring. Giniro then went for the "Sunset" but got tripped up in the ropes when he was running into them and couldn't pull it off. [FN]: "OCD jumped to his feet and took advantage. He lifted Giniro up and nailed him with a Doctor Bomb. OCD didn't give Giniro a seconds breath before he nailed him with a Chokeslam. Giniro, barley able to stand, was near losing. Knowing this, Giniro nailed OCD with a low blow, using the last bit of energy that he could. OCD kneeled down, allowing Giniro to hit the "SunSet" and gain the pin. ################## Winner - Rio D. Giniro ################# [FN]: "I don't know why, but I don't like to see matches get has physical as that. Maybe it is just because I have a weak stomach. Way too much to tell you what all happened. The match started out with Mike Waldrop making fun of Hellbent. Hellbent got frustrated and nailed Mike Waldrop in the chest with an elbow thrust. Having the wind knocked out of him for a few seconds, Waldrop dropped down to one knee allowing Hellbent to apply an Octopus Hold. [GR]: "Mike Waldrop tried to fight it with all the power he could, but we couldn't break the hold. The referee picked Mike Waldrop's hand up and dropped it once, twice, but it stay up the third time. Waldrop began to elbow Hellbent in the gut, breaking the hold. Mike Waldrop jumped up to his feet, seemingly very energized. He whipped Hellbent into the ropes and hit him with a spinning heel kick. Mike Waldrop then climbed to the top rope. Waldrop leaped off, hoping to hit a frog splash but mis-caculated the landing area and hit nothing but the mat. [FN]: "Hellbent took advantage of this. Hellbent lifted Mike Waldrop up and nailed him with a Cobra Clutch Suplex. Hellbent went for the pin, but was only able to register a 2. Waldrop pushed Hellbent off him and ran the ropes, hitting Hellbent with a running elbow drop right in the chest. Mike Waldrop picked Hellbent up and hit him with an Spike Piledriver, apparently injuring Hellbent. Hellbent lay motionless on the mat. Mike Waldrop leand over Hellbent to see if he was okay. . . and Hellbent attacked! Hellbent tricked Waldrop into thinking he was hurt, and then nailed him with a head vise. [GR]: "Now, that is what you call cheap! Hellbent rolled Waldrop over, but only got a fist in the face for it. Waldrop jumped up and went to the top rope again. . . this time he perfectly landed the frog splash. Waldrop picked Hellbent up and whipped him into the corner, and followed up with a splash. This left Hellbent dazed and stumbling to the center of the ring. Waldrop then executed the "DA Waldropper" and gain the victory! ################################## Winner- "Cream of the Crop" Mike Waldrop ################################# [GR]: "I have a feeling that if those guys put in their all, they are going to go a long way in the GCWA.
