This league will stay open...This league there will be no ass kissing allowed...and this league will just be the best...but when need YOUR help. TOGETHER we can make the best league on the internet. ~*~*~*~RP Rules~*~*~*~ 1)No TOSING in you have a problem with the league then quit 2) If you do not RP for a match your wrestler won't show up and you won't get money (details on money later) 3)No crappy RPs with no point at all. 4) RPs must be over 20 lines long 5) No RP fights leave personal problems out of the league 6) Keep cussing to a minimum cuz when you cuss alot you sound like a 9 year old. 7) If you impersonate another wrestler put a disclaimer and your wrestlers name so I know who you are. 8) Last and most importantly have FUN ~*~*~*~GOOD GOLD~*~*~*~ Ultimate title: The big cheese only the very best in UVWA can even compete for it Internet title: The second title rank in UWVA Viscious title: This title is for the master of special matches every match has a special stip of mine and Trauma's choice. Is your guy good enough to be Mr Moneybags of UVWA? You need the money to get more guys, new clothes, or any other stuff. If you quit and your guy is still under contract he may be bought by someone else. Money can be used for show time like a Heart Break Hotel, an advertisement or anything of that sort. If you want to do a card godbless you. You will have one of the weekly cards. All your wrestlers have to be in te card. ~*~*~*~ APPLICATION~*~*~*~ You have made it. You have read all the other crap now time to get down to the nity-gritty. You can have up to 4 wrestlers. If you have 4 make one lightweight al least. If you want more guys you gotta buy them.
