A geocities-era e-fed founded 22 May 1997 that ran an active 1998 slate (a 15-part Birthday Brawl card in May, a 15-part Stable Wars pay-per-view in August-September) while its self-declared owner/president pursued a Top-100-webring profile and a multi-fed "council" alliance under the MSCW banner. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Mid South Championship e-Wrestling was founded 22 May 1997. By September 1998 it billed itself an established Top-100 federation pursuing a council-style alliance among independent feds, offering Real Audio shows and cross-promotion to any smaller or larger federation willing to join under its banner.

Its own site went dark and returned under a new domain by February 1999, with a note from its operator that new signings would now be screened for talent rather than accepted on request.

A week-in-review post from May 1999 recaps a promotion in the middle of two live angles. The stable Devastation Inc., Iceman Johnson and Tony Morris, spent three weeks recruiting the tag team of Shuan Garvin and Antonio Ruiz to reform an old alliance; Ruiz refused, but Garvin threw their own title match to Devastation Inc. outright, handing over the tag team championships and ending a two-year partnership with a Brain Buster to Ruiz's face.

The same week, Tommy Nyce upset the undefeated Brimstone to win the United States Championship, and Brimstone answered by powerbombing him through two tables; a rematch was booked for the promotion's upcoming Rage in the Cage pay-per-view, 30 May 1999. On the same card, president Phillips held up the Challenger title after referee John Taylor awarded Mike Dagger a pinfall over Justin Sain that the broadcast itself calls tainted by a foreign object, setting a no-disqualification rematch for the vacant belt at the same event.

No document dated after that week-in-review post survives in the record, and no closing notice of any kind is on file.
