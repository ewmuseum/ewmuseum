MSCW Week in Review (26 May 1999)

28 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What a week it has been in the MSCW!! Highlighted by the formation of the new "Extreme Horsemen Alliance" and their new leader Shuan Garvin , who on Saturday Night turned against his friend and tag team partner ,
Antonio Ruiz.
For the past 3 weeks , Iceman Johnson and Tony Morris a.k.a Devastation
Inc. have repeatedly tried to recruit Shuan Garvin and Antonio Ruiz to reform the old stable , Ruiz however , clearly wanted no part in it whatsoever while Garvin seemed to be seriously considering the proposition. Then this past Saturday Night as Garvin and Ruiz were doing battle with Devastation Inc. , Garvin threw the match and handed the win ,plus the tag team championship titles over to Devastation Inc.
When Ruiz stepped up to ask him what the hell he was doing , Garvin executed his version of the Brain Buster , ending the two year partnership with Ruiz. Expect these two to meet in the ring once again , but this time as enemies!
Also this past week , we saw a new United States champion crowned as the youngster , Tommy Nyce did the seemingly impossible and beat the undefeated Brimstone to capture the gold! After the match however ,
Brimstone none to happy with the outcome ,would get his revenge on Nyce by sending him through not one , but two tables with a vicious
Powerbomb! A return match has been scheduled for the upcoming PPV , Rage in the Cage on May 30th.
In other news , the Challenger title has been held up by president
Phillips , due to the controversial decision made by referee John Taylor , in the match-up between Mike Dagger and Justin Sain. Dagger was given the pinfall despite the very obvious use of a foreign object by which was used to knock Sain out cold and give Dagger the three count. These two will meet once again at the upcoming PPV , in an anything goes
