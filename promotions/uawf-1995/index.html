A hand-scored federation founded in August 1995 on a WWF-style eight-title system with a women's division, opening with a founding announcement and a full television taping, and continuing into further shows through early 1996. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The United American Wrestling Federation announced itself 16 August 1995, cross-posted to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy and rec.games.pbm: “a WWF system based league (like the AWI), 8 titles, women's division, and other nifty stuff,” matches hand-run and diced rather than simulated, pitched at “a level of family entertainment.”

Its first full TV taping, Sunday Night UAWF, aired 1 October 1995, called by announcers Andy Ripley and Taylor Jones. “Mr. Wrestling” Matt Grappler retained the Television Championship over the Hitman with a hold he called Wrestling Personified, and the broadcast closed on a challenge: the Boojum Snark, having just been eliminated from the Television Title battle royal alongside his partner, demanded a Cage Championship match against Neandrithal as payback.

By Slamfest II, on 29 January 1996, the Hitman had earned a title shot of his own against Hollywood Johnny Dreamer. The promotion's own First Annual Perot Cup, broadcast 22 March 1996 under the byline “Perot Sport Productions,” closed on a ladder match for the vacant USA Championship between longtime rivals Vengeance and Dean “The Comet” Haley: Haley grabbed the cup to win both the tournament and the title, and the two men, exhausted, shook hands and embraced as the show went off the air.

No explicit UAWF closure notice survives, but a later post from the same promotion's own operator, recruiting for a new league that May, suggests the UAWF had ended or was winding down by then.
