UAWF — Show

"Sunday Night UAWF" — the fed's first full TV taping

1 October 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All is in darkness. Then a torch is lit, and the Boojum Snark can be seen standing in the middle of a cave.

"Once again the jealous mortals have cheated me of my prize!
First, my partner Frank Abrams and I were robbed of a USA tag team title shot. Now, we have both been eliminated from the TV title battle royal! Neandrithal! You cowardly dog! You deserve nothing but a sound thrashing, and you will get it! If you are not so afraid as to give me the chance to take that precious Cage title which I truly deserve. Consider it a recompense for your crime against
Frank and myself! I will mash your ugly face against the bars of that cage, deliver the Fireball to you, and then walk proudly out the door as the NEW Cage champion! Accept my challenge, if you dare! ARRRROOOO!"

Then all is darkness...
