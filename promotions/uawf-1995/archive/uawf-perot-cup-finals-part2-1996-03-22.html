"UAWF Perot Cup Finals 2/2"

22 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[AR] Ok, folks, in a few moments we will have the culmination of tonight's extravagansa with the first ever ladder match in the UAWF, which will decide just who is the best all around wrestler in the UAWF and who is the USA champion! But first, we have some comments from all wrestlers involved.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------[ Vengeance] Dean Haley, we've been thru a lot together throughout the Perot tournament. Some would say that we were lucky, others would say that we deserve to get this far... all I have to say is, win or lose, I have the utmost respect for you. Let's put on a match the fans will never forget!
May the best wrestler win (Vengeance then smiles at the camera and jokingly points to himself, whispering out the word, "me")!
------------------------------------------------------------------------------[ Larry Dawson] Well, here he is, a man who nobody thought would get here, and of all the luck, you're facing the guy that was your partner and friend for most of this tournament, Vengeance. So, does the friendship end here?
[Dean Haley] Let's just say the friendship's gonna be on hold for the next hour or so. Vengeance, you and I have been through a lot over the past month.
I just have to say no matter what happens in this match tonight, if you
EVER need a partner who'll back you up 100 percent, look no further than the Comet.
[LD] But how intense are you going to be going against a guy like Vengeance? Do you have anything left after your emotional cage-match win last
Sunday? [DH] Larry, I don't NEED any outside motivation to get me ready for this match. The Comet is just one step away from going down in history as the best all-around wrestler in the UAWF. This match is my destiny right here. Ever since I was an eight year old boy, watching my dad on
TV, practicing the moves on my sister and brother, I knwe I was destined to follow in his footsteps. And if there was a Perot Cup when Dad was still around, I'm sure he would have won it.
[LD] I'm sure ol' Dave is very proud of his boy right now, wherever he may be. [DH] Thanks, but I haven't done what I've set out to do. I said right from the start that if I could get into this thing that I could win it. And here I am in the finals. And in, of all things, a ladder match, one of the most exciting and dangerous matches in wrestling. Vengeance, I'm sorry, but if I have to go through you to win that Cup and that championship belt, then I'm gonna do it. The Comet ain't holding anything back tonight. I guarantee that this WILL BE the most exciting match you have EVER seen here in the UAWF!!!.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [RA] Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is the final match in the 1996 Perot Cup Tourniment!!! {Crowd pops with excitement} [RA] This match is a ladder match, in which the rules are simple, there are none. The only way to win is to move the ladder from the enterence, there {points to ladder at aisle begining}, move it to the center of the ring, climb it, and retrieve the Perot Cup tropy currently suspended in the air. Now, for the participants. First, the man who in the preliminary rounds he went on undefeated at a tag-team with Dean Haley, defeating Hitman and Dr. Sydney Pain and Wermacht and Louis de Pont du
Lake. In single competition he defeated Adam Fredricson but lost to the Boojum Snark. In the singles tourniment he lost in the first round to Rick Daniels, he came in second place in the tag-team tourniment, loosing to the Juggernauts after defeating the team of Jerry Striate and
Hollywood Johnny Dreamer. He also placed second in the random tag-team tourniment, advancing with Jerry Strait over Rick Daniels and Frank the
Tank Abrams, and with Adam Fredricson over Jerry Straite and Dean Haley, finaly loosing to Adam Fredricson. In the battle royale he placed fourth, after helping to eliminate Frank Abrams, getting thrown out by
Rick Daniels. And, finaly, in the semi-finals he defeated Rick Daniels in a cage. Here is, finalist number one, hailing from Oakland,
California, weighing in at 250 pounds, VEEEEENGEEEEAAAAANNNNNCCCCEEEE!! {Vengeance goes down to ring flashy as ever, to his music of "Smoke on the Water", getting a MAJOR pop (who wouldn't after an intro like that?)}
[RA]
And, his partner, and his opponent. In the preliminatry rounds, he went undefeated in a team against the teams of Wermacht and Louis de Pont du
Lake, and Dr. Sydney Pain and Hitman. He lost both of his singles matches, however, loosing to both Frank Abrams and Rick Daniels. He advanced on to the tourniment itself, coming in with a tie for third in the singles tourniment by defeating Frank Abrams before loosing to Jerry
Straite, and came in second in the tag-team tourniment. In the random tag-team tourniment he came in in a tie for third, prevailing over the team of the Boojum Snark and Hollywood Johnny Dreamer while in a team with his arch-nemisis Adam Fredricson, before loosing to that same man, who was teamed with Vengeance, while teamed with Jerry Striate. He came in third in the battle royale, collaborating to eliminate Frank Abrams, before being eliminated by Adam Fredricson. He defeated Adam Fredricson in a semi-final matchup, in a cage, finaly purging that ghost from his past. He is the ONLY man to get points in EVERY tourniment match, hailing from Denver, Colorado, weighing in at 212 pounds, here is, the son of the late great David Haley, here is, the former UAWF TV Champion,
DEAN "THE COMET" HAAAAALEEEEEEEYYYYYYY!!! {"Ballroom Blitz" by Sweet causes another mass pop as Haley runs to the ring, slapping hands and the like}
[AR] Both men getting their instructions from referee Albert Simpson, and they shake hands as they go back to their corners, each knowing that once the bell rings, all the teamwork they have developed, all the victories that they have shared, will be meaningless as they compete for the ultimate goal, the Perot Cup.
[TJ] Geez Andy, do you have to be so dramatic about it? We're not on a soap or anything. [AR] The bell rings, and the two converge in the middle of the ring. Dean haley starts it off with a bodyslam, but gets locked in a lever leglock by his former parter Vengance. Haley struggles a little but finds his way out. Both men up, and Vengeance tosses Haley to the ropes while he visits the oposite ones himself. He goes for a dropkick, but Haley ducks!! And Haley slaps on a lever leglock.
[TJ] And while I'm on the topic of overdone broadcasting, don't you think that Albertson went a little overboard with those intros. I mean, yeah,
I guess its special to get here, even with the luck these two had, but what's up with giving their whole life story?
[AR] Vengeance escapes, but is pormptly put into a headscisors. He escapes that also, both get up, and Haley hits a Karate kick. Both go to the ropes, and Haley off with a BIG bodypress. Haley gets up, picking up
Vengeance along with him, tosses veng to the ropes while going to the oposite side himself, and he gets hit with a dropkick! Vengeance drops an elbow before slapping on another lever leglock.
[TJ] What's with all the restholds here? Sure, the rest of their moves are mildly entertaining, but are these two so pathetic they need to resthold everything they do? And when is the ladder going to get involved?
[AR] Vengeance gets out up, and hits an armdrag on a rising Dean Haley. He picks Dean back up for a press slam, both again go to the ropes, and its
Vengeance this time with the bodypress. Both men get up, and Vengeance hits a dropkick flooring both again! Again they get up, AND HALEY WITH
AN INCREDIABLY HARD THROUGH THE ROPES TOSS!! He bounces off the ropes as
Vengeance staggers up... BODYPRESS OVER THE TOP!!! Dean gets up and starts to go to the ladder.
[TJ] Not the most intellegent chap, is he? He managed to hurt Vengance BAD, not like that's anything hard to do, but he did it on the wrong side of the ring. And, what's worse, he did it on the WORST side of the ring, the side oposite to the enterence. So now he's got more distance to travel and more time for Vengeance to get up.
[AR] A keen observation as Vengance manages to get up just after Haley reaches the ladder. Vengeance rolls into the ring, goes to the apron nearest Haley, who is near the ring yethas his back turned to it...
[TJ] Bad move, as he will soon learn... [AR] AND VENGEANCE WITH A BULLDOG OFF THE APRON ONTO THE LADDER!! [TJ] Never, ever, EVER, turn your back to your opponent. Especialy when you are in a position where he can do THAT to you.
[AR] Vengeance picks up the ladder, slides it into the ring, and sets it up in the middle... he's begining to climb it!! Its a long 10 steps, at 1.5 feet a step, up. Vengance is making progress though. He's at the fourth step, but Haley's up! The fifth.. Haley in the ring.. the Sixth... AND
HALEY DROPKICKS DOWN THE LADDER!! [TJ] OOOOHHH!! You know Vengeance felt that!! The ladder fell right onto the ropes, smacing back up into his face!
[AR] Evidently Haley noticed the effect too, as he goes out to the apron. [TJ] Don't tell me he's going to check on his hurwt widdwe fwend, iv he? [AR] Evidently not, as he flips the ladder back over on top of Vengeance!! Evenidentaly, anything will go in this match! [TJ] They're fighting for the biggest prize in wrestling, Andy, and a title to boot. Plus, I doubt the extra 15 grand the winner gets over the looser hurts.
[AR] Haley picks up the ladde, and stands it up in the corner. He goes over to Vengeance... SLINGSHOT INTO THE LADDER!!! He goes to the outside...
climbs the ropes... CLIMBS THE LADDER.. SPLASH!!! NO!!! VENGEANCE GOT HIS KNEES UP!!! [TJ] Live by the sword, die by the sword. [AR] Vengeance picks up Haley for a press slam, picks him up again, and irish whips him into the ladder!
[TJ] I guess the ment what they said about their friendship being on temporary hiatus for this match.
[AR] AND HE FOLLOWS HALEY IN WITH AN AVALANCHE!!! He climbs up the ladder, and hits a moonsault from the top!!
[TJ] You know, this match is already THE bast match I have seen here in the UAWF... [AR] I'd have to concur... [TJ] So just IMAGINE what it'd be like with two quality wrestlers like Neandrithal and Rick Daniels. [AR] Venageance moves the ladder to the middle of the ring, and begins to climb. And he's getting pritty damed far!! He's on the eighth step, but
Haley is up!!! This is going to be close folks... Vengeance on the second to last step.. BUT HALEY KNOCKS IT DOWN WITH A BODYPRESS, AND
VENGEANCE FALLS TO THE OUTSIDE!!! [TJ] Haley has to be almost as hurt by that as Vengeance is. [AR] Well, they both are on the ground in pain.. [TJ] But, you do what you can to win the big ones, and this one is definatly BIG! You've heard of all the great inter-federational tourniments, but this one is bigger than them all! Even the SWA with their Road Warrior or Slic Ric tourniments can't capture the excitement, drive, and emotion that has been brought about by this tourniment. The ultimate goal in a wrestlers career can be realized here, the goal of being proclaimed the best wrestler in the world. But this tourniment carries more prestige than just some belt or a singles tourniment, this carries the title of the best all-around wreslter in the world, which mean you may not be the best at any one thing, but, dag nabbit, you're just about the best jack of all trades there is in this sport!
[AR] Well put, as Haley manages to groggily get up. He climbs to the top rope, as Vengance staggers up, and hits a BRUTAL IMPACT TO THE FLOOR!!!!
[TJ] You know, I've seen haley do alot of Impacts in his career. And I thought that one against Frericson was hard, but that one on just a convas would porbibly be the best he's delivered. The fact that its to the florr has gotta be hurting Vengeance right now.
[AR] But Haley's hurt too. He manages to get up , and roll back into the ring. He stand the ladder back up in the middle of the ring and starts climbing... he's on the 7th step but Vengeance is up.. he's on the
9th... Vengeance is in the ring.. Vengeance won't make it... Dean at the top... AND VENGEANCE JUST BARELY MANAGES TO SHAKE HIM OFF!!!
[TJ] I'm getting to old for that kind of excitement. [AR] Vengeance picks up Dean, whips his to the ropes.. TILT-A-WHIRL BACKBREAKER!!! He's climbing the ladder. [TJ] Well, Vengeance has the distinct advantage of being right there, but, then again, so does Dean...
[AR] Vengeance on top, but Dean is up, and Vengeance is.. WHAT IN GOD'S NAME... HE JUST CAME OFF THE LADDER WITH A BODYPRESS ONTO HALEY!!! [TJ] You know, there are only so many wisecracks you can pull in one match, and I'm more than out!
[AR] How very true, as we are nearing the one hour mark on this match!! Vengeance and Haley are both hurt from that, but Vengeance gets up first. He tosses Haley into the ropes and hits... NO!!! HE MISSES A
SCISORS PILEDRIVER AND HITS HIS HEAD ON THE BOTTOM STEP!!! HALEY GRABBED
THE ROPES!!! Haley runs up to the ladder and starts climbing.. he's at the 4th... the 6th... he 8th... the 9th.. He's on top... Vengeance starts to get up AND HALEY GRABS THE CUP!!!!!
[RA] THE WINNER OF THE MATCH, NEW UNITED AMERICAN WRESTLING FEDERATION USA CHAMPION, AND WINNER OF THE FIRST ANUAL UNITED AMERICAN WRESTLING FEDERATION PEROT CUP, DEAN "THE COMET" HALEY!!! [AR] Dean jumps down with the cup, tears in his eyes. His father would be proud of him, but, wait a sec! What's going on?? Vengeance in the middle of the ring having a stare-down with Haley!!
[TJ] YEAH!! FIGHT! FIGHT! {The two stare at each other for a few, suspensful, moments, and then, as you all prolly predicted, Vengeance extends his hand, and as Haley grasps it the fans go crasy! They hug, Vengeance raises Dean's hand, etc.}
[AR] And that is what its all about, ladies and gentlemen. Neither of these men could have gotten here without the other, and, in the end, their rivalry in this match came to a halt and their friendship renews! And that's all we have for you tonight, ladies and gentlemen. So, for my fellow brodcaster, Taylor Jones, Robert Albertson, the ring announcer, and our backstage interviewer Larry Dawson, this is Andy Ripley saying good night, everybody!!
