I took the liberty of updating the info below, to make it a bit more accurate. http://wwo-efed.tripod.com/ [detail withheld] The WWO is a new e-fed just started a few days ago, and yet has posted no less than 15 million times on this newsgroup..so we're in need of someone to shut [name withheld] up...or at least slow him down. Here's some info on the fed. -We have 2 cards a week, on Wed. and Fri., 1 PPV every month, and 13 posts to RSPWF every 6 seconds. -We use the Zeus sim. to write the card results, but winners are decided by quality of role plays, as well as whoever can post the most times to RSPWF the fastest. However we do write in interferences, ridiculous amounts of RSPWF posts, interviews, other speacial happenings, such as [name withheld] pissing most everyone on RSPWF off. -Each person is allowed to have 2 wrestlers, but is allowed to have as many posts to RSPWF as they'd like. -The WWO is an original wrestlers only e-fed. We don't allow real wrestlers, or wrestlers with the names of celebrities...but we do allow our fedhead to make a total nuisance of himself. On a positive note, if he has time to post to RSPWF this much, he must have time to run a fed. -We have 5 titles: World, IC, HC, LW, Tag Team, with that many titles, you see why I keep posting here in a desperate attempt to fill out every division. Just because the WWO is new, doesn't mean it's gonna be fly by night, shut down in a week e-fed. In fact, it will be a fly by night, shut down in a week e-fed because I'm alienating many people who might have been interested by posting more times in one day than probably anyone on the newsgroup throughout their entire tenure. I've been in e-wrestling for about 5 years...and while you'd think in order to get the amount of posts I've had on RSPWF that all of that time was spent posting here, but no, in fact, I posted all of those times in about three days. Three of those years I ran a very successful e-fed. I will run the WWO the same way I ran it, and with the help of good e-wrestlers and roleplayers, the WWO will be as successful, of course I won't have any of those because I keep pissing people off. And people fear that if they join my fed, I'll e-mail them as often as I post to RSPWF. Please Mr. [name withheld]...get the message. Advertising is one thing, spamming is another. And now I present to you a Moment of Arrogance I am a man who has vowed to have more women than [name withheld] posts for his New Year's Resolution A man who will start the next sexual revolution A man who is the highest level of male evolution... It's me, it's me, a man who's had more Bush than the US Presidency... Your hero, your idol, a veritible e-wrestling legend, the greatest ever to lace up the keyboard. [name withheld].
