FWA — World Pro Wrestling

World Pro Wrestling, Part One

27 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
>{The following program is brought to you in New Courier font:size 10} O============================[Part One]============================O [Black screen. The roar of a cheering crowd can be heard thundering. Fade from black into video footage of Victor Bludd executing a side- walk slam on a bloodied Orion Darke. The footage is subtitled "Last Week on WPW". As the referee lays unconscious, Bludd exits the ring and grabs a chair from ringside. He climbs back in the ring and then stands over the fallen National Hwt champion.] Roger Allen: What's he doing now?! What's he going to do with that chair?! He should just pin the man and he'd become the National champion! Why is he hesitating?! Steve Findlay: He wants to end Darke's career! He's gonna make sure that _this_ time he finishes the job! I told ya Bludd [Orion slowly rises to his hands and knees as Bludd just stares down at him with indignation.The German Tank slowly raises the chair above his head as the crowd pops big. With the chair poised over his head an evil smile crosses his face.] RA: He's going to severely injure the "Black Knight" and put him out of wre... [The crowd's pop now seems to now become deafening as Steve interrupts Roger in mid-sentence.] SF: Who the hell is THAT?! A fan's jumped on the apron! [ As Bludd stands with the chair aloft, a figure clad in a red, white and blue hooded jogging suit appears from out of the crowd and leaps to the ring apron.He grabs the chair yanking it from the Tank's grasp. Bludd with a shocked expression on his face, spins around and is greeted with a chairshot to the skull. He crumples to the mat as if he'd been shot.] RA: A fan has just knocked out Victor Bludd with a chair!! Oh my stars and garters! The fan still has the chair... and he's stepping into the ring! We've got to get some security in there right now! SF: Who _is_ this guy?! [The fan moves to the fallen Bludd, who's lying face down on the mat. He kicks the German giant's limp body with his foot then walks to the dazed Orion Darke, now propped up in a ring corner. The fan stands in front of him as Darke tries to clear the cobwebs.He glances up at the fan's face.A look of shock appears on the Black Knight's face.The fan slowly reaches up and pulls his hood down revealing him to be...] RA: Buddy Brogan!! It's Buddy Brogan!! America's Hero has returned to the FWA and in a BIG way!!! He's back to settle the score!!! SF: Aw cripes...Captain Nice Guy is back. I thought he was ban- ned from the FWA?! [Cut to a slow motion closeup shot of Buddy Brogan, a wide grin on his face. Cut back to the shocked expression of Orion Darke.The shot slows to a stop and the color drains out of it as the words of Roger Allen repeatedly echoing throughout....] RA: Buddy Brogan's back!! Buddy Brogan's back!! In the middle section of the large three sectioned desk sits the host Roger Allen,the silver-haired voice of the FWA for the past twenty seven years.He's dressed in a gray suit and red tie,his trademark wire- rimmed round bifocles affixed firmly in place. Seated beside him at the righthand section of the desk sits Steve Findlay, the three-time FWA World champion and Hall of Famer. He's dressed in a brown suit with a red and green tie. The camera swoops down from a bird's eye view to a straight on shot of Roger,shuffling a few loose papers about. His deep, gravelly voice begins to speak...] RA: Hello wrestling fans. What a show we've got for you today. As you saw at the top of the show "America's Hero" Buddy Brogan has indeed returned to the FWA and he did so last week in shocking fashion when he attacked Victor Bludd in his match against Brogan's longtime nemesis, Orion Darke, for the FWA National Heavyweight title. Well FWA President James Norcross has issued a statement concerning the entire situation and well..let's roll that piece of footage right now if we could. [Cut to footage of President James Norcross, dressed in suit and tie, seated behind a large wooden desk. A gold nameplate adorns the desk affirming his title. The slightly balding official leans forward, his hands clasped together, fingers interlocked. He clears his throat and as he begins to speak.] JN: Last week,on World Pro Wrestling,Buddy Brogan interrupted an FWA sanctioned National Hwt title match and struck the championship challenger, Victor Bludd, with a metal chair. This occurred after Bludd had already assaulted FWA referee Danny Jones,knocking him unconscious and unable to control the match. This marks the third time in as many weeks that an FWA official was assaulted in the path of performing his duties. _This_ has got to stop. And it stops now. Effective immediately,the FWA will install severe penalties for any action taken against an official,while that official is performing his assigned duty. Beginning with a fine of no less than ten thousand dollar and subsequent higher fines... right up to and including indefinite suspension,without pay. These heinous acts of selectively injuring an FWA official will no longer be tolerated. Which brings me back to last week's incident with Buddy Brogan. Buddy Brogan has indeed signed a contract with the FWA and is now a member of it's talent roster. However,by his actions last week, Mr Brogan asked for and will now receive a match with Victor Bludd. This match will take place in two weeks on WPW,and will be contested under Chain Match rules. It will be to determine the number one contender for the FWA National Hwt title,currently held by Orion Darke. That match will be tele- vised the following week on WPW.Knowing the history between Mr Brogan,Mr Bludd and Mr Darke, I feel this is the most reason- able way to settle all accounts. I beleive the fans will feel the same way. Thank you and good day. [Cut back to Roger in the studio.] RA: Well there you have it fans. Two weeks from today, right here,Buddy Brogan,"America's Hero", will face one-on-one, the German Tank, Victor Bludd. The winner will then face the National Hwt. champion, Orion Darke or the current holder at that time, for the championship the following week..and that's good news for Buddy Brogan,eh? SF: And a black day for the FWA. I mean check this out...that goof Brogan returns after a three month, basically exile after losing that brutal Loser-Leaves-Town match to Orion Darke at Grapple for the Gold III.He hides out all of the required ninety days, never doing so much as an interview, commercial,nothing..then shows up and attacks "The Black Knight" Orion Darke's opponent, Victor Bludd. This then catapults into a contenders match for the title immediate- ly?!I'm sure Victor Bludd feels that President Norcross's decision is a real fair one,huh? He's being railroaded by pompous phony Brogan and Norcross is allowing it! It's a travesty,Roger...a travesty. RA: Well...I'm not one for conspiracy theories. Coming up this hour is a one fall match between "The Tiger"Atul Rajan,the great Indian veteran and the mysterious masked man known only as Diablo. That should be a real donnybrook. SF: Maybe..maybe not. Diablo's one tough nut and I mean _nut_. He's run over and through every opponent he's faced so far in the FWA and I don't think that fossil Rajan is gonna be change that. RA: That match's coming up a bit later on in the hour as we said. Also this week we'll have a few words with the FWA tag team champions,Race Benton Jr & Dack Powers. Two young superstars who've really set the wrestling world on fire as of late. SF: Whoop-de-damn-doo. The punks are back from Japan and now all the little pimply-faced teenyboppers are gonna be so thrilled. These two geeks think they're champions because some chrome they stole over in Japan.Well boys..welcome back to the _real_ warzone. Hey..how about the match between Chief Running Scared and the newcomer "Exotic" Timothy Grant? I've heard alot of good things from my friends 'across the pond' as they say. He could be a player here in the FWA. RA: That's Chief_Bobby_Blackhawk_ and indeed it should an exciting bout. Both men looking to become a contender for the gold here in the FWA. And our main event this week and what a main event it is...as former World champion Mr Olympia takes on the reign- ing FWA World Hwt champion "Mr Terrific" Terry Sloane for the World championship. Olympia,of course, is coming off the upset non-title victory over Terry Sloane just two weeks ago right here on this very program. And tonight he'll get his shot at the belt. I'm really looking forward to that one,Steve. BC: Yeah,yeah..Old Olympia got lucky two weeks ago and caught the champ offgaurd. This week the entire Kingpin team is here and I'm gonna go on record right here, right now..and predict yet another stellar win for the Terrific One. There ain't no way he can lose with his buds behind him this week. RA: Well we will indeed see if the masked superstar,Mr Olympia,can make it two in a row over the champion, Terry Sloane. Looks to be a great hour. SF: And who knows...maybe the Fabulous Sheila might be here this week to grace us with her...uh..presence. Ain't that enough to make ya hang around? RA: Yes..it,ah,certainly is,Steve..but when we come back we'll hear from the FWA TV champion,"Awesome"Orlando Kade, so stay tuned.
