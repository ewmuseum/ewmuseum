FROM THE EDITOR O======================O I apologize to everyone for being late with the e-mag. Hopefully it was just a one time thing. Lotsa news going on with new arrivals coming in and some suuprises too. Don't forget to get your RPs in for the GNS'01 press conferance due in by midnite 7/3/01. Flash packets will be out 5PM on 7/5. Thanks to everyone who helped put together the e-mag this week(esp. Jean and Bill--I owe ya guys!). Later fiends!! Tony B. O======================O II. ROAD RAGE REPORT O======================O * GEORGIA * ATLANTA - FWA at The Omni: Diablo pinned Jean Carpentier ... Manuel Soto defeated Kanto Kanakura in a grudge match ... Jett Blacque pin- ned Moses "Big Thump" Magnum in a Dog Collar match ... The Mongols defeated Brock Kincaid & "Mr Wrestling" Ted Weaver in a Texas Tornado tag match ... "Awesome" Orlando Kade retained his FWA TV title as he pinned Buddy Brogan 2-out-of-3 falls ... FWA Lt Hwt champion Bobby Gold defeated challenger "The Handsome Halfbreed" Dino Morales ... "Rough" Red McKenzie pinned Tatterdemallion ... The FWA World Tagteam champions Race Benton Jr & Dack Powers went to a no contest with The Felony Squad ... Main Event: FWA World champion "Magnificent Bastard" Terry Sloane pinned challenger FWA National Hwt champion Victor Bludd in a cage match(Nat'l title not on the line). (6/9/01 - Att. 13,241) * INDIANA * EVANSVILLE - FWA MIDWEST at Roberts Stadium: Jerrod Blood pinned Otto von Heller ... Shotocabe defeated "Handsome" Van Williams in a Indian Strap match ... "Exotic" Timothy Grant pinned Colt McKane ... FWA Midwest Champion "Golden Boy" Hondo Howell retained his title against challenger Charlie Wolfe ... FWA Midwest tag champs Big Willie Johnson & Porkchop Nash drew with Masked Grappler & Mick the Mauler ... "Stylin'" Stan Street defeated Larry Heinimi ... Main Event: Orion Darke & Nick Fortune won via DQ over FWA National Tag champions Brute Force. (6/10/01 - att. 5,821) * FLORIDA * St. PETERSBURG - FWA Florida at The WrestlePlex: "Babyface" Boyington defeated "Silent But Deadly" Sam Stone ... Baron von Hammer pinned The Masked Pro ... Dirty Don Carson defeated Willy B. Hert in a hair-vs-hair match ... Ken Mantel pinned Armand Hussein ... "Peacemaker" Lars Graham defeated "Easy" E. Embry ... FWA Florida State tag champs The Fargo Bros pinned Law & Order in a Street Fight match ... El Halcon defeated El Santo Negro ... Main Event: FWA World Champion "Magnificent Bastard" Terry Sloane pinned challenger FWA Florida State Hwt champion Outlaw Tank Dillon in a Taped fist match. (6/15/01 - att. 8,705) * MISSOURI * St. LOUIS - FWA MID-SOUTH at The Chase: Chief Bobby Blackhawk & Billy Silverwolf defeated The Highway Patrol ... Ted Oates pinned Terrence Garvin ... Gene & Lars Anderson pinned The Olympians in a St Louis Brawl ... The Black Demon defeated Dos Caras in a mask-vs-mask match ... Brett Wayne pinned Bob Roop ... "Sensational" Steven Owens defeated I.M. Cutter in a Loser-Leaves-Wrestling match ... FWA Mid-South champ Dan "The Rebel" McGavin defeated challenger Bugsy McGraw in a $10,000 Bounty Hunter match ... Main Event: FWA National Hwt champion Victor Bludd won via countout over challenger FWA Mid-South TV champion Kardiak. (6/16/01 - att. 7,942) * TEXAS * FRISCO - FWA Southwest at the Frisco National Guard Armory: Red River Jack defeated Jeep Swenson in a chain match ... "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder pinned Buddy Brogan ... The Ghoul defeats El Santo Negro ... Tim Brooks pinned Cosmic Cowboy I ... Ali Bey defeats Cosmic Cowboy II ... Lucas McKane pinned champion Chuck Conners for the Texas State championship ... The Mongols win the FWA National Tagteam titles after defeating former champs Brute Force in a cage match ... Cowboy Red Roberts defeated "Bruiser" Bob Kannon to win the FWA Brass Knuckles championship in the tourney finals ... FWA World champ "Magnificent Bastard" Terry Sloane pinned FWA Southwest Hwt champion "Lethal" Larry Cameron in a 2-out-of-3 falls match. (6/22/01 - att. 7,523) * GEORGIA * ATLANTA - FWA at The Omni: FWA Cruiserwt champion Mondo Khan defeated Masked Lightning ... Buddy Brogan pinned Otto von Heller ... "The Tiger" Atul Rajan drew with Shotocabe ... "Handsome" Van Williams & Nick Fortune defeated "Rough" Red McKenzie & Billy Silverwolf ... "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder pinned Lars Graham in a barbed wire match ... Jerrod Blood pinned Manuel Soto ... Diablo defeated Kanto Kanakura ... FWA TV champ "Awesome" Orlando Kade pinned "Mr Wrestling" Ted Weaver in a strap match ... The Felony Squad(Killer Ron Black & "Dr Death" Ox Banyon) defeated Race Benton Jr & Dack Powers for the FWA World Tag titles in a Bunkhouse Brawl match ... FWA World Champion "Magnificent Bastard" Terry Sloane won via DQ over Orion Darke. (6/23/01 - att. 14,012) O========================O III. FWA Rumorline O=================O - There are rumors floating around that a wellknown legend and Texas toughguy may be close to signing a deal with the FWA. Rumor has it the only sticky point is he wants a guaranteed match with FWA World Hwt champ Terry Sloane. Negotiations continue... - Our lockerroom mole reports that the former FWa tag champs, Dack Powers & Race benton Jr were arguing over who cost them their world tag belts to the Felony Squad. Could a split be coming from the popular tandem? - Word through the grapevine has it a certain masked "superstar" from the Sunshine State may be entering the FWA soon. Rumor has it he has his eyes set on a certain FWA Nat'l Hwt champion... - And finally, there's whispers coming from FWA head- quarters that FWA President James Norcross is con- templating instituting stiffer fines and suspensions for wrestlers abusing FWA officials during matches. Scuttlebutt from the main office says there'll be an announcement made before the GNS'01 kicks off. Stay tuned... ------------------------------ V. FWA HALL OF FAME BALLOTING ------------------------------ Well so far the turnout for the balloting for the 2nd Annual FWA HoF voting has been great, but with a few days left I'd like to get the entire roster to get their votes in.
