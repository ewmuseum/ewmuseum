Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Federal Wrestling Alliance ran its own weekly broadcast, World Pro Wrestling, hosted by Roger Allen, billed as the show's own voice for the past twenty-seven years, alongside colour commentator and three-time former promotion World Champion Steve Findlay. The surviving opening segment (27 August 1998, the first of nine parts) opens on the previous week's angle: Buddy Brogan, “America's Hero,” returning from a suspension to attack Victor Bludd mid-match and rescue National Heavyweight Champion Orion Darke.

Promotion President James Norcross followed with a taped statement fining and threatening suspension for any further assault on an official, then booked Brogan against Bludd in a Chain Match two weeks later to determine the next challenger for Darke's title.

The broadcast previewed the rest of its own card: tag champions Race Benton Jr. and Dack Powers, billed as having won their titles in Japan; a newcomer, Chief Bobby Blackhawk, facing “Exotic” Timothy Grant; a masked wrestler, Diablo, against veteran Atul Rajan; and a main event pitting the masked Mr. Olympia against reigning World Heavyweight Champion Terry Sloane, coming off a non-title upset over Sloane two weeks earlier.

Only this opening segment of the nine-part broadcast survives; none of the matches it previewed, nor any further part of World Pro Wrestling, is held past it. Nothing establishes when the Federal Wrestling Alliance was founded or how its own record continued beyond this one card.
