APWA Saturday Night Slammasters

14 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
American Pro Wrestling Alliance "Saturday Night Slammasters" February 13, 1999 The Disney MGM Stuidos in Orlando, Florida Brett Burman: "Hello wrestling fans and welcome to the APWA! We are coming to you live from Orlando, Florida at the Disney MGM Studios as we kick off the great action here in the APWA! The APWA is proud to present
"Saturday Night Slammasters"! We have four big events scheduled for you tonight. Our first match will feature a man only known as C when does battle with Tom Strong. Also, The Decapitator will collide with
America's hero, The Patriot and Robert Payne will go one on one with
"The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels. In our main event all the wrestlers will come back and compete in a battle royal to decide who will be our first television champion. Well it's time to get started and....wait a minute....where in the hell is that music coming from?"
 ["More Human than Human" by White Zombie begins to play. Robert Payne slowly steps through the curtain and walks slowly down the aisle as boos come from the crowd. He's wearing blue jeans, a blacksweatshirt with
"LTC" written boldly on the front and leather sandals on his feet. Payne climbs into the ring and hangs over the ropes, yelling and pointing at some of the fans near ringside while raising his title. Then grabbing a mic from the ring announcer, Payne singals to cut the music, in which the music slowly fades.]
 PAYNE:"Okay everyone, I have a few words to say, so can everyone just simmer down please??"
 [The boos continues as Robert Payne waits patiently for a few moments before almost screaming to the fans.]
 PAYNE:"I SAID SHUT THE HELL UP!!!!"  [Boos can be heard even louder as Payne paces around in the ring with a hand going through his medium-length brown hair while the other clutches his Crusierweight title. Robert Payne stops walking and stands in the middle of the ring momentarily before speaking once again.]
 PAYNE:"Well, it's all the same as I remember it. You see I hope everyone understands that when I get into the ring against every opponent I face, it's going to be business as usual. You see, not only do I get to beat on that old cripple Shawn Michaels, I'll get to beat everyone else in the battle royal for the Television title! And when the tag titles come around to being up for grabs, then the Bullet and my good friend C are going to lay a hurt like no one's been hurt before!!
Got it?! Good!!"  (The crowd explodes again with boos. But Payne wasting no time begins speaking again just as quick as he paused.)
 PAYNE:"I'm not finished!!! As for anyone who tries to take the Bullet's spotlight. I hope you're listening close, because if it ever happens, your head will roll courtesy of me! Got it! Good!!"
 [Litter is starting to be thrown into the ring as Payne stands with a smile on his face taking his time to speak.]
 "And now it's time for the Bullet to go. I know you'll all miss me, but all that you have to do is remember that I'll be back to kick
Shawny's buttocks and to win a television title! So I'll see you all later you losers!"  ["More Human than Human" begins to play as Robert Payne drops the microphone, climbs to the outside of the ring and begins to make his way backstage.]
Brett Burman: "Well Robert Payne has addressed the capacity crowd here in Orlando and I am going to go on the record and state that President
Womack does not recognize the title that Robert Payne has as an official
APWA Championship. If Mr. Payne wants the light heavyweight title, he is going to have to earn it!" Let's go to the ring for our first match!"
C -vs- Tom Strong Brett Burman: "There's the bell and our first match is underway. C applies a hammerlock on Tom Strong and then connect with a wild haymaker. C goes for the cover 1...2...and no Strong is able to kick out at two. Strong comes back with a leg dive takedown and C responds with a well placed boot to the midsection. Strong now ramming C into the turnbuckles and C comes out of the corner with an elbow. C now going to work on Tom Strong with a tremendous amount of righthands and then a forearm smash and Strong is rocked. Strong is now fighting back with an elbowdrop and follows up with a clothesline. Ooh, what an elbow by
Strong as he levels C! C is now resorting to dirty tactis with haymaker and is now choking Strong, hey ref get in there! Strong comes back and plants C with a well executed belly to belly suplex and goes for the cover, the ref counts 1...2...3!!!!   Tom Strong pins C in 9:45 to win the match!!!! What a great fight to kick of the show and now we have The
Decapitator standing by somewhere!"
(A tall solidly built figure is looking at a group of flags from different countries . His hooded shadow is superimposed in the background. The light from his fireplace illuminates only his perpetually boney smile on his flesh ridden face.His eyes give off an ethereal red glow) "Hmmmm let me see this one?"
(he throws a french flag in the fire it ignites) "No it must ..be... this one! and these as well(chuckle)" (He throws in a japanese,Brazilian and a Canada flag and the fire burns brighter )
"Wait it is this putrid little catalyst for misguided nationalistic pride and a foolish sense of duty!"
(He rips up an American flag,spits on it, throws it in the fire and laughs loudly.) "Listen to me "Dudly Dipshit"(the patriot) when you face the Infernal one get ready to burn baby!!!! I will break your bones , grind them to powder and sell them   to kids as dope!
Hahahahahahahahah oh boy will I have fun ripping up a flag waving yahoo like you Hahahahahahahah!"
(He takes the flags of the rest of the countries of the world and dumps them out of a big box into the fire)
The camera fades! Breet Burman: "The Decapitator is telling The Patriot what he plans to do to him and that is our next match, so let's get to it!"
The Decapitator -vs- The Patriot Brett Burman: "The bell has rang and The Patriot is quickly taking control of this match with a series of quick jabs and now ramming
Decapitator into the turnbuckles. Oh man, Tha Patriot has just collided with The Decapitator and now both men are down! The Patriot gets to his feet first and rocks Decapitator with a clothesline and Decapitator responds by planting The Patriot into the mat with a scoop slam. Tha
Patriot now with a sneak punch and The Decapitator comes back with a chop and executed a perfect suplex on The Patriot. The Decapitator now rakes the back of The Patriot and arm whips him into the steel ringpost.
The Patriot might have injured his arm! The Patriot somehow managed to get the strength and bodyslam Decapitator. The Decapitator just took off
The Patriot's head with that flying clothesline and now follows up with legdrop. The Decapitator just leveled The Patriot with that clothesline and now he is going into his tights! He just pulled out brass knuckles and clobbered The Patriot with them! How could the ref not see that! Tha
Patriot is back to his feet and staggers to the top rope, Decapitaotr does not see him and ooh, the patriot missle and the ref counts
1....2....3!!!! The Patriot has just pinned The Decapitator in 14:47 to win the match!!!!! Now let's go to our next match!"
"The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels -vs- Robert Payne Brett Burman: "The match is underway and Robert Payne begins the match with a fireman's carry takedown. Shawn Michaels now applied a hammer lock and Robert Payne responds with a leg takedown. The match is scientific from the start so far. Robert Payne then executes and well timed kneedrop and follows up with a side russian legsweep. Michaels comes charging across the ring with a flying cross body block and takes down Payne with a side headlock. Michaels now slamming Payne's face into the canvas. Payne is now resorting to cheap shots by kicking Michaels when he is down from a chicken wing. Payne buries a boot to the midsection and Michaels comes back with small package, but Payne kicks out at two. Paynes responds with a clothesline sending Michaels into the corner and Payne follows with a knee into the back and rolls up Michaels
1...2...3!! Robert Payne has pinned Shawn Michaels in 8:22!!! Before we get to the main event for the Television Title, we are going to take you to the office of the first and newest female grappler in the APWA,
Jessica Starbird!!!"
[The scene opens in a place most wrestling fans are familiar with; the office of healer and world famous wrestler Jessica Starbird. Several shelves and bookcases line the walls of the office, and in the middle of the room is a desk complete with computer and fax machine. Jessica is sitting at her desk looking at through some papers while talking on the telephone. She's wearing her regular long blue dress and white crystals on her necklace in the shape of the peace symbol.]
Jessica: Yes, it's true, the IEWA has shut down...maybe it was a long time coming, but...yes, they had problems...maybe they're still blaming me, I don't know...I haven't heard from them in weeks until I got that fax...heck of a way to find out that you're out of a job...good riddance? Hey, I was their champion, or have you forgotten?...Okay, it didn't last long, but I made it to the top, and now everybody knows who
I am...the belt? Yeah, it's at home. I don't know what I'll do with the thing...yes I'm going to keep it. After all I've been through with the company, I deserve that reward...yeah, they're talking about a farewell tribute of some sort...it doesn't matter to me either way. I'm moving on with my career...yes, I'm still a wrestler...you though I quit because you haven't seen me in a while..I've been here, seeing clients like I usually do; as a matter of fact, I'm expecting one in a few minutes...nobody has contacted me until now...let's see...(she shuffles some of the papers)...it's called the American Pro Wrestling
Alliance...they're brand new, so...yes, it's a big risk, but this way,
I'll be able to distance myself from everybody even remotely associates with the IEWA...we all need challenges, and my challenge is to start over somewhere else...well, the men start Saturday night, and I'll have to find the show on my satellite dish...I don't know; they haven't found an opponent for me yet...believe me, you'll be the first one to know, after all, you guys miss lambasting me in the press, don't you?...I thought you accepted my wrestling career...whatever...fine, I'll see you guys at spring training...bye.
[She hangs up the phone and starts talking to herself whle shuffling the papers once more.]
Jessica: Conference calls; sheesh. I go from press conferences with the IEWA to announcing my signing with the APWA over the phone. So much for fame, huh?
[Cut to the interior of a small waiting room in the clinic where Jessica's office is located. Several people are quietly reading magazines when the door opens and a slender, muscular man with short curly hair walks into the room. He walks over to the receptionist's counter and leans on it, smiling at the receptionist.]
Receptionist: Hello, can I help you? Man: Yes, my name is Michael Keening. I believe I have an appointment with Jessica Starbird?
Receptionist: Oh yes, Mister Keening. Just one moment, I'll let her know you're here. [Michael turns and leans back against the counter, checking out the other patients waiting their turn to be seen. Behind him, the receptionist talks quietly into a phone and then hangs up.]
Receptionist: She's ready to see you, Mister Keening. Third door down the hall, on your left.
Michael: Thank you very much. [He walks down the corridor as indicated and stops in front of a nondescript door. A small brass plaque reads "J. STARBIRD" and he knocks gently, opening the door after a muffled response comes from behind it.
Inside the room, Jessica Starbird looks up from her desk where she has been taking notes.] Jessica: Mister Keening? [Michael walks into the room, his hand outstretched.] Michael: Yes, although you might know me by another name... [She looks a little puzzled as she shakes his hand.] Michael: ...we used to have an employer in common, actually. I wrestled for the IEWA at the same time you did although I'm usually wearing this... [He reaches into a pocket and pulls out a green-and-white wrestling mask.]
Jessica: Oh, you're... The Banshee, right? Michael: The one and only. I've heard great things about you, by the way. Jessica: Oh really? Considering the way I've been treated in the press lately, that comes as a surprise. Have a seat.
[Michael sits down on a chair directly in front of Jessica's desk. Jessica gathers her notes together and sets them aside.] Jessica: Your family... wasn't it involved with the CIWF somehow?  Michael: That's right, my sister Geri sat on the CIWF Executive Board. She told me about your exhibition match against Nina Miyagi and was very impressed. Jessica: Apparently not impressed enough. I wound up shutting down the company. [Jessica shrugs her shoulders while Michael chuckles.] Michael: Oh, I wouldn't say that! There were a lot of factors which led to the demise of the CIWF, but it ended on a good note, and your match was one of the reasons why.
Jessica: So, what can I help you with today? Michael: I'm actually here to see you about an injury that's been nagging me for some time. As you know, the squared circle can give you more than your fair share of bruises and bumps. And lately, I've been experiencing this nasty pain in my right shoulder. I've been to see a sports medicine specialist but he wasn't able to help me very much. Geri suggested I come see you so... here I am.
Jessica: Right shoulder, huh? Let me take a look. Michael: You want me to take off my shirt? Jessica: No, not yet. If you can, just roll up your sleeve... [Jessica gets up to attend to Michael, who rolls up the right sleeve to expose his shoulder. Jessica moves her hands around the joint and
Michael winces at a few points during her examination.] Jessica: You say you've been having trouble with this shoulder for a while. Has it been a few months or longer?
Michael: To be honest, I couldn't tell you exactly when it started, although I don't think it's been going on for more than three weeks. I just wanted to get it checked out as quickly as possible.
Jessica: Is it a constant pain; something that's always there, or does it hurt when you move it, like this?
[Jessica slowly extends his right arm out to the side, moves it upwards, then in a clockwise motion. He winces slightly at one point during the rotation of his shoulder.]
Michael: No, it's not constant, although it flares up from time to time. And it does seem to be in a particular position. I'm a bit concerned as it's hindering me somewhat.
Jessica: Hmmm... can you remember how you sustained this injury? [He frowns for a moment as he thinks but then shakes his head in resignation.] Michael: I'm sure it must have been during a match but I can't remember which one. You know how it is, all the bumps and scrapes you accumulate in wrestling?
[Jessica goes over to lean up against her desk while Michael rolls up his sleeve.] Jessica: I don't know how active a wrestler you are, but I suspect your continued participation in matches is delaying a complete healing for your shoulder. No matter what I do, or any other doctor does, there's no substitute for resting your shoulder.
[He nods his head in agreement, a look of apprehension on his face.] Michael: I was afraid you were going to say that. Jessica: Let's be honest, though, we're both wrestlers; we know that it's next to impossible to take time off to tend to these kinds of injuries. The promoters don't want to hear about muscle pulls, bruises, and cuts. If you can walk to the ring, even with a limp, you're expected to make your next match.
Michael: True, and I love competing. It would drive me crazy to miss out on some matches. Jessica: Yes, I'm beginning to understand. I've had plenty of time off to heal and to recover, and although I enjoy my work here, to be honest,
I miss wrestling and all the emotions that go along with it. Anyway, have you heard of acupressure, Mister Keening?
Michael: Michael, please. Is that like acupuncture, with all those needles? Jessica: In a way. Acupuncture is the more precise of the two, but let's face it, who really likes to be stuck with all those needles, huh? The anxiety about it would negate the benefits derived from that treatment.
Acupressure is done with the fingers at certain points throughout the body. I combine that with massage to control whatever pain you're experiencing, at least to the point where it's tolerable.
Michael: That sounds pretty good to me. Jessica: Most likely, this will take more than one session, and as we go along, I'll see what else I can do to help rebuild the muscle tendons in the shoulder. If you have time, I'd like to get started on the massage right now.
Michael: You don't have anybody else to see today? What about all those people I saw in the lobby?
Jessica: Oh, the millennium people. They're tring to convince us that the world will end come January first. Geez, I have all year to put up with this nonsense. The apocalypse, Y2K...
Michael: I take that to mean you don't believe in that kind of stuff? Jessica: I try to keep an open mind about these things, but this has gone way beyond a curious fancy. There are a lot of people virtually counting on the end of the world occuring soon. I'll explain later. Oh, you may want to go ahead and take off that shirt. I'll be using some essential oils, and they're hard to wash out of clothes.
[Michael takes off his shirt while Jessica walks over to some shelves and pulls out several bottles. She points towards an opening in her office, next to those shelves.]
Jessica: I've got this new entranceway to our garden put in recently. I'm going to have massages in the garden to see if natural settings can improve the healing of my clients. Guess what? You're the first person to give this a try.
[Michael smiles at that prospect.] Jessica: There's a massage chair waiting out there, so go make yourself comfortable, and I'll be out there in a minute.
Michael: Okay. Thanks a lot, Jessica. I really appreciate this. [Michael places his shirt on the chair and walks out to the garden. Jessica continues gathering bottles together before following him out. The scene dissolves to black as "Lush" by OTTMAR LIEBERT begins playing in the background for a moment before fading away.]
Brett Burman: "I personally cannot wait until the ladies division opens here in the APWA! Those are gonna be some great matches, but the only things is we need more female wrestlers. Now let's get to the ring for our main event battle royal for the television title!"
Eight Man Battle Royal APWA Television Title 1. Robert Payne is eliminated by Tom Strong at 2:03. 2. Ricky Morton is eliminated by The Patriot at 6:58. 3. Robert Gibson is eliminated by The Patriot at 8:39. 4. Shawn Michaels is eliminated by The Patriot at 23:00. 5. The Decapitator is eliminated by Tom Strong at 30:47. 6. The Patriot is eliminated by C at 31:36. 7. Tom Strong is eliminated by C at 32:49. C becomes the first APWA Television Champion!!!!! LET ME KNOW WHAT EVERBODY THOUGHT ABOUT THE FIRST CARD!!!
