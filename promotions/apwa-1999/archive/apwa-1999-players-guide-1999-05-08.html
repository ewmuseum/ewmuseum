Introduction Hello everyone and welcome to the American Pro Wrestling Alliance. My name is [name withheld] and I am the proud owner and president of the APWA. In order to make and run a federation, there needs to be two major factors in order to be successful. The first one is tradition, what would wrestling be without some classic wrestling, so we threw that in to the pot. The next one is hardcore, also wrestling needs to have the extreme and violent side, so we threw that into the pot also. Add these factors up and you have the APWA, a very well rounded and organized fantasy wrestling e-fed. The APWA is based in Illinois, but we will hold cards all around the world.
II. Roleplaying Roleplaying in the APWA is what makes this organization exciting and interesting as well as the best fantasy wrestling e-fed on the internet. There are three main ways to roleplay in the APWA. They are by giving interviews, flashes, or by using the message board on the website. Interviews and flashes will be used on cards and will not be seen by anyone except the author and myself until the card is released. Interviews will always take plays somewhere in the arena using one of the APWA personalities. Flashes he other hand will take place outside the arena in any setting that you wish to have. If you also want to make a standard roleplay during the week before the card is announced or released, then you can log on to the APWA website and use the APWA Roleplay
Message Board which is provided by Inside The Web. This is a good way to make challenges to other wrestlers or talk to the president concerning issues or just totally trash talk someone before the card is announced or relaeased.
Angles are what you have building up your wrestlers. Things that can build up angles are sneak attacks, special challenges, personality, roleplaying, or any other outside thing that your wrestler has going on. If you have an idea for angle that you would like to include on your match or any other match, then please email me and explain the angle that you want to have. Be sure to include "Angle" in the subject heading.
III. Promoting and Booking Matches The matches in the APWA are signed and booked by the owner/president and the vice president. Recipiants of title shots are also determined by the owner/president. You can also request special challenges, but the owner/president will take the matter into consideration before approving it.
IV. Cards The APWA currently has two weekly television cards. They are: 1. Sunday Night Slammasters - This is the big show of the week, which is similar to the WCW Monday Nitro and WWF Raw Is War telecasts. There will normally be between 5-8 matches on this card with interviews and flashes also included.
2. Wednesday Night Warzone - This is undercard show of the week, similar to the WCW Thursday Thunder and WWF Sunday Night Heat Telecasts. There will normally be 4-7 matches on this card along with interviews and flashes also included.
3. Pay Per View Events - The APWA currently has five seasonal pay per view events each year. The APWA will also be involved with other supercards with the UWP and other organizations. The APWA also has a special tournament that is on the pay per view schedule that will premier next year. They are:
[name withheld] Memorial Cup Tournament (April) BattleBrawl - June Beach Blast - August Fall Fever - October Arctic Anarchy - December V. Championships Here in the American Pro Wrestling Alliance, there are four major championships to be won. In order of importance they are:
1. World Heavyweight Championship - The most important championship in professional wrestling today. To hold this title, is to say that you are the single greatest wrestler in the industry today. This title is mostly defended on pay per views and occasionally on Sunday Night Slammasters. The World
Heavyweight Champion also gets a shot at the UWP Unified Grand Championship of the World! 2. World Light Heavyweight Championship - For anyone weighing 235 pounds or less, this is the most prestigious title in the APWA. The holder of this title is labeled as the best high flyer/technical light heavyweight in professional wrestling. This title is usually defended at pay per views as well as Sunday
Night Slammasters and Wednesday Night Warzone. 3. World Television Championship - Not quite as prestigious as the World Heavyweight Championship, but the APWA World Television Championship definitely gets more air time. This title is defended on every broadcast of Sunday Night
Slammasters and every other broadcast of Wednesday Night Warzone. This title is also defended on pay per views.
4. World Tag Team Championship - The most important championship in professional wrestling today for tag teams. To hold this title, is to say that you are the best tag team combination in the sport today. This title is mostly defended at per views and occasionally on Sunday Night Slammasters.

Alliance what it is. Young, but knows the wrestling business very well, if not better than anyone else out there. He makes sure that every event put out by the American Pro Wrestling Alliance is of the highest quality possible. If there is a problem too complex for Vice President [name withheld] to take care of, Owner and President [name withheld] will step in.
Vice President, [name withheld] - This is the man who makes most of the decisions concerning vacated championships, when and where "gimmick"matches will take place, as well as anything else concerning the wrestling talent in the APWA.
