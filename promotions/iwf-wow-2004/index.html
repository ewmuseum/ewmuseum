A Usenet e-fed publishing its own dirt-sheet magazine and staff bulletins across a 2003 tag-title recruitment drive, a January 2004 crossover event, and a 2006 relaunch, without a surviving match-by-match card of its own. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

IWF/WOW published its own dirt-sheet magazine, Slash Journalism; its fourth issue, from September 2003, runs a ten-question interview with its own World Heavyweight Champion, Matthew Reason, tracing his career through the promotion's ranks and his rivalries with Billy Rock, Justin Arcola, and Gunnar Gaines on the way to the title.

The same issue recaps the promotion's own dominant faction, the Trifecta, from its formation — Eric Travers, Elijah Flynt, and a guest referee, the Prophet, turning on the reigning champion together at a four-way title match — through several line-up changes to the version running “since Summer Explosion 2002,” when Travers, Nate McMannis, and Maverick each held a world title of their own on the same night.

A December 2003 post seeks a Vice President to run the promotion's own tag division, and a one-night tournament, Relentless, is announced for 4 January 2004 with its own results hosted off-site and unreachable from here.

An update dated 28 June 2006 announces “the RETURN OF THE FREAKIN' SLASH,” rehiring most of the promotion's former staff and vacating every title — World Heavyweight, North American, and Tag Team all listed as “TBD” — ahead of a new authority figure's reveal. No later document survives to say who that was, or how the relaunch went.
