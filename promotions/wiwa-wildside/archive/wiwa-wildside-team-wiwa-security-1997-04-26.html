Question (Team WIWA security stable)

26 April 1997

> > One more thing: Extreme Wrestling. Why? What happened to skilled > > wrestlers who could....oh, I don't know...WRESTLE? It seems to win a > > match today all you need is to talk loudly and swing a mean chair. > > Extreme wrestling is the rave right now. I'm in an extreme fed (RFHW), so > I satisfy that need. However, in NAEC, I wrestle my guys fairly straight > (3 are japanese, and one american). What bothers me is the fact that in a > 'normal' fed, there is alot of extreme stuff going on, and I've also > noticed that sneak attacks go way over the top. is there any feds where > this constant attacks/interference is not tolerated? In the Wildside Internet Wrestling Association (WIWA), I've recently gotten fed up with the huge number of sneak attacks that have been going on. This is especially true in regards to during title matches. For a while now I've had a group I call Team WIWA. They're basically WIWA security, and starting with the latest card (just sent out today) Team WIWA stays at ringside to make sure there is no interference during these matches. Mostly I did this because a lot of the sneak attacks that were coming in for title matches had nothing to do with any angles going on, and I felt I had to do something for a few reasons. 1. If I were a WIWA fan at home watching WIWA TV, I'd become fed up with all the screwjob endings and would eventually stop watching. As Bantam Bedlam, WIWA President, I want HIGHER ratings, not lower ones. 2. I specifically state on the WIWA homepage that the WIWA is NOT going to be another ECW knockoff, so constant run-ins and interferance are really not needed. Now if two wrestlers have been feuding for weeks, then a sneak attack or two makes sense. However to have one wrestler interfere in matches week in and week out with no reason is just not needed. 3. A number of handlers are getting frustrated that every time theyget a shot at a title they get ripped off. I agree. So during title matches, sneak attacks are going to be very limited from this point on, unless (as I said before) it would advance an angle. For example, The NHL Pugilists and the Extreme Team went to a time limit draw. The next week The Extreme Team interferred in the Pugilists match to help the Pugilists keep the titles so Extreme Team could beat THEM for the titles and prove they were better (incidentally, Extreme Team did win the rematch). FAVES: 1. The Eliminators 2. Taz 3. Rey Mysterio Jr. 4. Stevie Richards (the guy is just hilarious) 5. Steve Austin 6. the original Tiger Mask 7. The Sandman 8. Cactus Jack (not so much Mankind though) 9. Chris Benoit 10. Dean Malenko
