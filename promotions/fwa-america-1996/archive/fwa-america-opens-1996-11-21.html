Fantasy Wrestling America Opens

21 November 1996

I have started a new E-Wrestling fed that used the latest plus version of TNM. It is also the official e-fed of the TNM Newsletter.I have posted the first eddition of the rulebook below. If you are interested in joining then save this post and read the rulebook offline! Thanks, Matt Cole ___________________________________________________________________ The Fantasy Wrestling America Rule Book Vol.1 -------------------------------------------------------------- 1.1-How to join 1.2-Information Needed to Join 1.3-Changing Your Information 2.1-Basic Matches 2.2-Challenges 2.3-Titles and Title Matches 2.4-Special Matches 2.5-Supercards (Pay Per Views) 1.1:How to join? You join the FWA by E-Mailing President Matt Cole at [detail withheld] and send him the information needed (see 1.2) 1.2:Information needed to join.If you would like to join the FWA just send the information listed below to [detail withheld] -Your Wrestlers Name -His hometown -His ring attire (what he wears to the ring) -His Style (eg.Brawler,High Flyer,etc..) -His Finisher (is it a submission move and what is a move similar to it) -How good of a worker is he and how much stamina does he have (you have 150 points to divide between the 2) -What foreign object if any does he use -What is his weight -Is he a face\or heel -Will he sit up after a move -Is he cocky -Will he give the sign for his finisher -Will he juice (bleed) -Does he work the crowd -Will he submit -Does he have a special move (eg.blows mist into opponents face) -Is he hard to pin -Does he have a Hulk-Up -Will he do his finisher outside -Is he hardcore -Will he do moves through tables -Will he do his finisher through tables -Will he fall to his knees and beg -Will he walk out on a match -Will he no sell moves time to time -If he is in a tag team what is his partners name and what is the name of the team. Each person may have up to 5 wrestlers. I know its alot of information but bear with me :) 1.3:Changing Your Information If at any time you see that your wrestler isnt doing so good or could us a face lift just E-Mail the President and let him know what changes need to be made (eg.Change from a brawler to a technical wrestler) 2.1:Basic Matches All matches are contested using TNM v6.2 plus and I have nothing to do with the outcome.Basic matches are usually matches on the undercard and they can be made without the participants concent. They are usually used for a wrestler to "climb the ladder" so to speak. 2.2:Challenges Challenges are matches that are fought when one participant challenges another and they accept.Challenges are NOT made by the booking committee and the participants concent is needed for the match to take place.Although the matches are not made by the booking committee the head bookers concent is needed for the match to take place. 2.3:Titles and Title Matches There are 4 titles in the FWA.They are listed in order of importance. 1.FWA World Title 2.FWA North American Title 3.FWA Cruiserweight Title (under 245 lbs.) 4.FWA World Tag Team Titles Title matches can be either a basic match or a chalange.The champion CAN NOT challenge the challenger.The champion has the right to decline a challenge.Title matches can be specialty matches (see 2.4) or regular basic matches.It there are no challenges or the booking committee sees fit they can assign a title match to the #1 contender to the belt. The President has the right to strip the champion of the title and if he is injuerd for 7 or more cards he will be striped of the title.If he doesnt defend the title within 30 days he will be striped of the title. 2.4:Specail Matches Special matches are matches out of the ordinary. They can be anything from a Steel Cage match to a loser eats dog food match.They must be made via a challenge. The booking committee does NOT have the power to sign special matches. They can suggest the match to both participants but they must agree. 2.5:Supercards (Pay Per View) There will be 1 Supercard each month. There will also be a weekly on Saturday and there will be other cards ran as the President sees fit. 3.1:Conclusion In conclusion if you have any questions E-Mail [detail withheld] and future editions and\or changes of this rulebook will be given to all FWA members. FWA President, Matt Cole
