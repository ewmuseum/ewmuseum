FWA Face To Face

28 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FWA logo comes crashing on the screen and we are faded into the FWA Studio in Charlotte,NC with Lance Sharpe standing looking into the camera) Lance Sharpe- Hello everyone and welcome to our first edition of FWA "Face To Face". On this show you can call,e-mail,or fax your favorite FWA superstar and talk to him face to face. Today my guest will be the one and only "Magnetic" Morgan Starkley. Mr.Starkley isnt in the studio yet but on "Face To Face" we will also be recapping the previous week in the FWA. We have some big news on Winter War Games and a special match to announce along with having Morgan Starkley in the studio today but first I want to show you this fantastic video on "Magnetic" Morgan Starkley...... -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- (A dark room appears on the screen. Suddenly the lights in the room go on. The floor is covered with wrestling mats; from the wall hangs pictures of various high school wrestlers. Many of the pictures are faded. The camera pans from the wall over toward the center circle of the main mat. There stands Morgan Starkley, sporting a Yankees jacket, ruffled black, short hair, and his orange wrestling tights.) Starkley: This is where it all began. The wrestling room. In my high school days this is where I spent most of my afternoons, going through grueling practices, hellish conditioning, and countless hours of drills, all to get that extra edge, that something special which put me over my opponent. (Home video plays, showing Starkley pinning an outmatched opponent in a merciful 8 seconds. Next, various photos and clips of Starkley's high school achievements flash on the screen. Finally, a clip of Starkley's victory in the finals of the 1992 New York state wrestling championship is shown, with a jubilant Starkley nearly in tears after his narrow victory). Starkley: After high school, a friend of mine suggested I consider going into pro wrestling. I was skeptical, but he hooked me up with a friend of his, a man by the name of Guy Wallace. Guy ran a pro wrestling training school, and for a year and a half I trained under him. I started wrestling for some independent wrestling promotions in New York. (Grainy film of Starkley hitting a spin kick, a snap suplex and an impressive swinging DDT on various wrestlers from the independent circuit is then shown). Starkley: As luck would have it, one of Guy's associates from Japan was making a trip to the United States, and Guy asked him to come see one of my matches. His associate was Koji Hasani, who is the main promoter for Super Japan Pro Wrestling, based out of Osaka, Japan. Hasani (In somewhat broken English): When I first saw Morgan wrestle, I was amazed. Surely this man has not been wrestling as a professional for only six months, I thought. I said to Guy I must get this man to come wrestle for me. (A tape of some highlights of Starkley's Super Japan matches are shown) Announcer: Morgan Starkley debuted in Super Japan Pro Wrestling on July 22, 1994, defeating Shoichi Yatsu with a spin kick. Starkley held his own against much of Super Japan's top talent, including having exceptional matches with Shinya Misae and The Golden Griffin in the fall of 1994. Starkley formed a tag team with "Powerhouse" Jason Thompson (at this point a shot of Thompson, a 6'3" 300 monster of a man with a better than average workrate, is shown) soon after, and they challenged the Benzene Brothers (Yuji and Taka) for the Super Japan World tag team titles in December of 1994, a match that went to an exciting 30 minute time limit draw. It was after forming the team with Thompson that Starkley earned the nickname "Magnetic", for his charasmatic personality, and debuted his new finisher, the Tiger Suplex. Starkley and Johnson seemed destined to win the Super Japan World tag team titles, until Johnson suffered an torn anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee in a match with the Angry Bisons in Fukushima, Japan on March 28, 1995. Starkley once again became a singles wrestler. Morgan Starkley found success on his own, including finding himself embroiled in a memorable feud with Shinya Misae. Perhaps his best match of all time came on June 2, 1995, in a thrilling last second loss to then Super Japan World Television champion Hiro Nissan (Nissan is shown reversing a Starkley inside cradle for the pin). Later that month Misae beat Nissan for that World TV title, which led up to the greatest moment of Morgan Starkley's career. On August 11, 1995, Starkley faced his archrival Shinya Misae for the Super Japan World Television Title. After taking a severe amount of punishment, including kicking out of Misae's Northern Lights Bomb,Starkley hit Misae with his Tiger Suplex for the victory. Starkley held the Super Japan World Television Title for seven months,defending the title in many exciting, technically superior matches. Now,Starkley returns to his home country, and the 6'2", 245 pound youngster -- he's only 22 -- now hopes to make his mark in the FWA. (Cuts back to Starkley in the wrestling room) Starkley: FWA, I hope you're ready, 'cause I sure as hell am. Whether it's Bret "Hitman" Smith, Great Tsunami, Mad Dog Mortimer or anyone else, I'm ready to kick some ass. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- (Screen fades into commercial and when we return we see Morgan Starkley dressed in a T-shirt and jeans standing next to Lance Sharpe) LS- Well as you can see Mr.Starkley made it here OK and the phone lines are heating up. Lets take our first call. Caller- When and where did you get started Morgan Starkley- I started training in 1992 to be a pro wrestler in Guy Wallace's wrestling school in Buffalo, NY. My first pro match was on January 11, 1994 at a New York All- Star Wrestling show in Buffalo, beating The Antagonist with a spin kick. So I guess you could say that I got my start in the independent leagues and worked my way up from there. LS- Well I have an interesting fax here it asks "could you tell us a little bit about your history in Super Japan Pro Wrestling"? MS-Well the head promoter of Super Japan Pro, Koji Hasani, saw one of my matches and offered me a contract. I owe a lot to Mr. Hasani, he gave me my first real break. Over there in Japan things are so different...you either learn and work hard and improve or you're outta there. I was lucky because I was paired up with Jason Thompson. They call him the Powerhouse, and he deserves that name because God is that man ever strong. But he taught me a lot about the physchology of the sport and about little things you can do. When I was teaming with him they started calling me "Magnetic", and that's when I started doing the Tiger Suplex. I think he and I could have really done something special as a tag team, but he blew out his knee and it changed things. It did give me a chance to prove myself as a singles wrestler, so I guess I made the best out of a difficult situation. LS- Well before we go to commercial Id like to show all of you what went down this past week on FWA Superstars Of Wrestling. Here is some home video footage from this past week on FWA Superstars -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- -BRET "THE HITMAN" SMITH vs. MAD DOG MORTIMER (highlights)- Mad Dog Mortimer takes a swing at Bret "Hitman" Smith with the bottle, but he gets out of the way. They jump into the ring and Luca is taking on both Smash and Crash with The Godfather single handedly destroying both Watsons with The Don just standing back and laughing. VM- Luca tosses Smash and Crash over the top rope and now The Family is beating The Watsons to a pulp. Here comes Mad Dog Mortimer,bandaged head and all,. LS- He is no help as they just rip the bandage off his head and start beating him. What is this The Don pulls a pistol out of his pocket and they are beating Mad Dog Mortimer with the pistol. VM- We have The Godfather destroying Mad Dog with Luca and Scarace holding the lifeless bodys of The Watsons up making them watch all of this and slapping them in the face. Well I guess well uh see you next week folks I am really sorry you had to see this. THIS WEEK ON SUPERSTARS: Triangle Of Terror Qualifying Match: The Godfather vs. The Death Warrior Triangle Of Terror Qualifying Match: Merlin vs. Bret "The Hitman" Smith PLUS: The Great Tsunami vs. Grandmaster G AND Morgan Starkley will be in the building -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- (We return from commercial with Lance and Morgan looking into the camera) LS- Well it said youll be at Superstars next week Morgan, I can bet you have a few choice words for the Grandmaster G, and speaking on the Grandmaster I have a caller with a question. Caller- How do you feel about the Grandmaster G MS- I think that Grandmaster G is a great athlete. I mean I have no problem with the guy, I think his behavior is strange at best but I haven't had any problems with him. Now he's been talking some trash about me, but I think once I face him in the ring I'll earn some respect from him. LS- Yah hes Deffinetly a character. We have another caller...... Caller- How do you feel about your match next Saturday with Northern Grizzly MS- Well this will be my first match in the United States in over two years so I'm really looking forward to it. Now I don't know much about the Northern Grizzly. I do know that he's a 300 pounder and that he's from Canada, which will change the way I approach the match. I've wrestled 300 pounders before and beaten them; when you don't have the weight advantage you have to gain an edge through others means. LS- Speaking of the Grizzly we caught up to him and he had this to say: -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- (A cold wintry night. A dark form looms out of the flying snow. A tall, muscular man, wearing a fur cape and boots, faces the camera.) Northern Grizzly: "And thus, a new dawn comes for the FWA...The Northern Grizzly has arrived, and shall soon meet his destiny..." (camera zooms to show just his face) NG: "Magnetic Morgan Starkley...though you seem to be a true warrior, you have been selected to be the first to suffer the fate...the fate of the Northern Claw...for Winter War Games is coming...and The Grizzly will rule the Winter...and so his destiny will be fulfilled..." (fade in a white-out) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- LS- WOW! Well I have a pretty cool e-mail here she asks "Who do you think,besides yourself,is the best wrestler in the FWA"? MS- Well I've been impressed with Bret "Hitman" Smith. I'd really like to wrestle him; I feel that we'd have one hell of a match. Also I think that the Great Tsunami is a solid wrestler; he used to wrestle for a rival promotion in Japan to Super Japan Pro so I never had an opportunity to wrestle him. LS- One more question before we go to break MS- Shoot...... Caller- Do you think you will ever be FWA World Champion MS-It depends if I can adapt to the FWA style of wrestling. I believe I can outwrestle anyone in the world, and you have to have that mindset going into any match. But if I get hit between the eyes with a glass bottle I'd be just as vulnerable as any man to beating defeated. Enjoy. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- (Scene opens on a gray parking lot outside a gym at night, there is torrential rain.
