Fantasy Wrestling America — Show

FWA Superstars Of Wrestling

24 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FWA logo comes flying into the screen and then explodes and fades to the crowd at the sold out FWA Arena going nuts. We see Lance Sharpe and Vince McDuffey standing at the announcers table) Lance Sharpe-Hello everyone Im Lance Sharpe along with my partner in crime Vince McDuffey and we would like to welcome you to FWA Superstars Of Wrestling. Vince McDuffey- Thanks Lance and what a show we have tonight. In our main event we will see Crash from the tag team Crash And Smash go one on one with Rick Watson from the Watsons. LS- Yah' and thats no ordinary match. The winner of that match gets to take his partner to our Winter War Games Pay Per View to take on The Goodfellas with the winners becoming the first ever FWA World Tag Team Champions. VM- And I can tell you first hand that I know for a fact that not only The Goodfellas but the entire "Family" is here. LS- That could get real interesting. I also have the pleasure (rolls his eyes) to interview the one and only Grandmaster G. VM- Hes defiantly one of a kind. Anyway we've done enough talking so lets get set for our first match. LS- We have Bret "The Hitman" Smith taking on the high flying Mad Dog Mortimer. Earlier we had a chance to talk to Bret and here is what he had to say. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- Hitman: Mad Dog you look to be a great challenge for the Hitman. But your only the first rung on the ladder that I'm climbing. That ladder leads straight the FWA world title. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- VM- Short and sweet. The Hitman knows what he wants and he is not going to let anyone stop him. LS- Not even Mad Dog. VM- I don't think so Lance. Bret is out for blood. LS- Well enough talking lets send it down to the ring. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- BRET "THE HITMAN" SMITH vs. MAD DOG MORTIMER 1 Fall with a 15 minute time limit -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- They lock up. Bret "Hitman" Smith takes him down with an enzuigiri. Bret "Hitman" Smith goes for a dropkick, but Mad Dog Mortimer counters it with a side step. Mad Dog Mortimer takes him down with a piledriver. Mad Dog Mortimer takes him down with a Boston crab. Bret "Hitman" Smith reaches the ropes after being trapped for 9 seconds. Mad Dog Mortimer does karate moves. Mad Dog Mortimer whips Bret "Hitman" Smith into the ropes. Bret "Hitman" Smith hits Mad Dog Mortimer with a kick. Bret "Hitman" Smith takes him down with an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle. Danny Davis counts: One, two, shoulder up. Bret "Hitman" Smith throws Mad Dog Mortimer out of the ring. Bret "Hitman" Smith goes through the ropes. Bret "Hitman" Smith nails him with a snap mare. Bret "Hitman" Smith is handed a bottle. LS- Uh oh this could get violent. Bret "Hitman" Smith takes a swing at Mad Dog Mortimer with the bottle, but he gets out of the way. Mad Dog Mortimer snatches the bottle from him. Mad Dog Mortimer takes a swing at Bret "Hitman" Smith with the bottle, but he gets out of the way. Bret "Hitman" Smith snatches the bottle from him. Bret "Hitman" Smith hits him with the bottle. Bret "Hitman" Smith hits him with the bottle. Mad Dog Mortimer is busted wide open. VM- This could spell the end for Mad Dog LS- Mad Dog really looks hurt,that cut had to obstruct his vision. Bret "Hitman" Smith throws Mad Dog Mortimer back into the ring. Bret "Hitman" Smith takes him down with a reverse cradle. Danny Davis counts: One, two, kickout. Bret "Hitman" Smith goes for a dropkick to the knee, but Mad Dog Mortimer counters it with a side step. Bret "Hitman" Smith nails him with a spinning leg lariat. Bret "Hitman" Smith takes him down with a snap suplex. Bret "Hitman" Smith throws Mad Dog Mortimer out of the ring. Bret "Hitman" Smith goes through the ropes. Bret "Hitman" Smith knocks Mad Dog Mortimer into the ringsteps. LS- Mad Dog looks really hurt Im beginning to think that the ref should look into stopping this match, Mad Dog cant defend himself. VM- Dont count him out so soon. A little blood never killed anyone. Bret "Hitman" Smith reenters the ring. Mad Dog Mortimer follows him back in. Bret "Hitman" Smith takes him down with a dropkick. Bret "Hitman" Smith nails him with a moonsault. Danny Davis counts: One, two, three. The winner is Bret "Hitman" Smith. Time of match: 0:02:13 VM- Looks like I spoke to soon. Bret made pretty short work of the Mad Dog. LS- I wouldnt judge Mad Dog on this one match. He got real unlucky and lost so much blood Im suprised hes still alive. VM- Oh wait here come The Watsons. Looks like they are coming to the aid of their friend Mad Dog Mortimer. What a display of sportsmanship by The Watsons as they help Mad Dog back to the dressing room and the crowd appreciates his efforts. LS- We have to pay some bills but well be back right after this. (Screen fades into commercial) VM- Were back folks and I hope you all know that Saturday December 7,1996 marks the first ever FWA Pay Per View. It is properly titled Winter War Games and will come to you LIVE from the LVJM Coliseum in Winston Salem,NC. But Ive said enough let me send you to our partner Tood Adams in the Winter War Games Control Center...... (A big tank with the Winter War Games logo on it pulls up to the screen and shoots it and it fades into Tood sitting at a table with many big monitors behind him) Tood Adams- Hello folks and welcome to your first ever Winter War Games Control Center. Mark your calendars because December 7,1996 history will be made when we crown the first ever FWA World Champion in the Triangle Of Terror match. What is a Triangle Of Terror match you ask? Simple! 3 Men 1 Ring NO Rules!!!!!! There is only one way to get into this match,Fight For It!!!!!! In the upcoming weeks here on Superstars we will have 3 Qualifying Matches,they are: "Magnetic" Morgan Starkley vs. Northern Grizzly Merlin vs. Bret "The Hitman" Smith The Godfather vs. The Death Warrior The winners of those 3 matches will go to Winter War Games to participate in the Triangle Of Terror match. There will be 2 men in the ring in a time. They can tag out to the man not in the ring but if you get pinned your gone and the other 2 are left to battle for the strap. There will be so MUCH more action and I will keep you up to date here in the Winter War Games Control Center and Ill talk to ya' next week. (Music plays as a promo comes up telling people where to call to order the PPV etc. then we fade to commercial) (When we return from the break we see Lance standing in the ring) Lance Sharpe: Ladies and gentleman. I would like to introduce a man who claims the only thing that is 'funkier' than his music is the way he gets down on his opponents. (Lights dim. Sparkle effects begin. The background to the Sugar Hill Gangs rappers delight starts up. A tone-def voice begins to rap). Voice- It's da FWA, from Charlotte, NC. All ya'all rednecks best look out for me. . . (Through the curtians appears a well-built, atheletic man walks through the curtian. While his physique is impressive, his most distinctivce feature is his immense afro. Quite frankly the man has kept the makers of Gerry Curl in business all these years). LS- (Over the harmonic lyrics of the Grandmaster) Folks, I give you Grandmaster G!! LS- (Sarcastic) That's some very interesting music you gave to us. What exactly is it? GMG- Well, it a little ditty that I call rap. I know you folks probally haven't heard 'bout it, tho. Its a bit new. My producers down in Motown caught me in the ghetto strutting my new vibe, and cold signed me up. They say I have a chance to be the hottest thing since the Jackson 5. LS- (mockery) Jackson 5, heh? GMG- Sure, bro. It may not be hip now, but give us a few monthes and even you white folks will be boppin' to my vibe? LS- You know rap is hardly new. Even the great folks of North Carolina (crowd goes nuts because he mentioned their town) listen to rap. MC Hammer, Run DMC, Vanilla Ice. . . We love 'em all. GMG- I don't know what'cha saying? (Apparently MR. G is even more outdated than the Announcer). LS- (Changing the subject). Let's get to the reason your here. The FWA has many great wrestlers. Just what is your plan to rise to top? GMG- Look, all of the other cats are going to have to understand one thing. I'm hotter than Soul Train. Just like my music will flow up the charts, so will I. Any other wrestlers who think they can stop me from jiving to all of my people, best think again. LS- Well, I can tell you that their are some wrestlers who won't think twice about stealing your flow. GMG- (amazed) Who? All the wreslters dig my grooves. I am the man they-a-call Grandmaster G. LS- Well, Morgan Starkley for one! (Crowd pops) GMG- What? Nah. M-dogg and me are down. He's hip to my groove. Infact, he's wanted to here some more of my stuff. (Lance rolls his eyes). Yep, once you look past the fact that he can't sing like me, he's still down.... Sure, he dresses like a square, and let's face it, his hair will never be as smoothe as mine. . . (The insults continue as the screen fades to comercial) VM- Man do I feel sorry for you Lance. LS- Hey its a tough job but somebody's gotta do it. VM- Well I know Morgan Starkley personally and I know he wont stand for this. LS- I know, what does "the Grandmaster" have against Starkley? He didnt do anything to him. VM- And you know the nut actually thinks the fans like him. What a loser. LS- Tell me about it. Well unfortunately Mr.Starkley isnt in Charlotte tonight so he cant defend himself but I know he has something to say. VM- Well now its time for the big one. Lets send it back to the ring for our Main Event...... -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- CRASH w\Ms.Jessica vs. RICK WATSON 1 Fall no time limit The winners team gets to take on The Goodfellas at Winter War Games for the FWA World Tag Team Titles -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------- They lock up. Crash whips Rick Watson into the ropes. Crash executes a kick to the thigh. Crash whips Rick Watson into the ropes. Rick Watson and Crash get hit with a double clothesline. Rick Watson nails him with a back suplex. Rick Watson flexes. Rick Watson whips Crash into the ropes. Rick Watson takes him down with a high cross body. James Beard counts: One, two, kickout. Crash catches him in a dragon suplex. James Beard counts: One, two, kickout. Crash works the crowd. Rick Watson begs off. Crash takes him down with a chop. Crash is going for the pin. James Beard counts: One, two, kickout. LS- So far a back and forth match. VM- Both men seem to be testing each other. Crash nails him with a chop. Crash goes for an avalanche, but Rick Watson counters it with a kick to the head In turn, Crash counters it with a duck-down move. Crash goes for a double underhook backbreaker, but Rick Watson blocks it. Rick Watson whips Crash into the ropes, but Crash reverses it. Crash goes for a spinning leg lariat, but Rick Watson counters it with a duck-down move. Rick Watson takes him down with a fist to the midsection. Crash nails him with a fallaway slam. Crash works the crowd. Crash whips Rick Watson into the turnbuckle. Rick Watson comes back and rocks Crash with a kick to the midsection. Rick Watson executes a European uppercut. Scarface comes to ringside. LS- Uh oh. Here he is. I knew one of them would be here tonight. VM- Deffinetly here to just observe the match. LS- Yah I bet...... Rick Watson whips Crash into the ropes, but Crash reverses it. Rick Watson executes an armbar submission. Crash reaches the ropes after being trapped for 18 seconds. Rick Watson executes an enzuilariato. Rick Watson goes for a backslide, but Crash reverses it. James Beard counts: One, kickout. Crash nails him with a lariat. Crash works the crowd. Crash runs into the ropes. Crash misses with a shoulderblock. Rick Watson nails him with a high cross body. James Beard counts: One, kickout. Rick Watson nails him with an elbowdrop from the second turnbuckle. Rick Watson places Crash on the turnbuckle. Rick Watson executes the Watson Drop and goes for the pin. James Beard counts: One, two, Scarface breaks the pin. James Beard disqualifies Crash. The winner is Rick Watson. Time of match: 0:03:24 LS- COME ON REF!!!!!! Give me a break, Scarface is still beating on Watson,but wait here comes Derrick and Smash is with him. VM- All 4 men have Scarface cornered. Theyre moving in for the kill...... LS- Here comes Luca and The Godfather with The Don behind them storming out of the back. They are literally saving the life of Scarface. They jump into the ring and Luca is taking on both Smash and Crash with The Godfather single handedly destroying both Watsons with The Don just standing back and laughing. VM- Luca tosses Smash and Crash over the top rope and now The Family is beating The Watsons to a pulp. Here comes Mad Dog Mortimer,bandaged head and all,. LS- He is no help as they just rip the bandage off his head and start beating him. What is this The Don pulls a pistol out of his pocket and they are beating Mad Dog Mortimer with the pistol. VM- We have The Godfather destroying Mad Dog with Luca and Scarace holding the lifeless bodys of The Watsons up making them watch all of this and slapping them in the face. LS- How humiliating for all 3 men. Give me a break. I can tolerate alot of things but this is not one of them. I dont know what to say...... Well I guess well uh see you next week folks I am really sorry you had to see this. (Screen fades away as we see the lifeless bodys of The Watsons and the bloody mess of Mad Dog Mortimer laying in the ring and The Family walks out laughing hysterically) NEXT WEEK: Triangle Of Terror Qualifying Match: The Godfather vs. The Death Warrior Triangle Of Terror Qualifying Match: Merlin vs. Bret "The Hitman" Smith PLUS: The Great Tsunami vs. Grandmaster G AND Morgan Starkley will be in the building So make sure to join us next week for FWA "Superstars Of Wrestling"
