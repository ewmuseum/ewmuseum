An August 1997 e-fed borrowing the real Memphis USWA's name, renaming itself "The Real United States Wrestling Association" (RUSWA) within three days and running its own title tournament that same month. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A founding post on 9 August 1997 introduced “the newly opened USWA,” its own president billing it as “a simple fed with no strange gimmicks” open to roleplayers and non-roleplayers alike.

Three days later it renamed itself The Real United States Wrestling Association (RUSWA), moving to a new GeoCities address and admitting it was “in desprete need of wrestlers…as of now we only have 1 wrestler so everyone has a 99% chance of getting accepted.” It described itself as “a booked fed” using “Real or Created wrestlers”; a separate newsgroup post independently pointed readers to the renamed address the same way.

Its own president then announced a parallel play-by-mail companion federation run out of the same GeoCities address, charging 50 cents a match against “the usual 1 or 2 dollars” for a monthly booklet of roster, rankings, champions, interviews and match results, and sanctioning nine titles across its singles and tag divisions.

RUSWA announced a tournament for the following Sunday “to give away all title[s],” still short of a full roster. No result of that tournament, or of any other card, survives, and no closing notice does either.
