A reunion promotion run by two former 1990s fedheads that crowned its first World Champion through a September 2002 tournament. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Top Turnbuckle Wrestling opened in August 2002, run by two handlers who had each led a federation in the promotion's own telling of e-wrestling's 'glory years', reuniting to build a classic, less extreme style of show.

A one-month tournament for its vacant World Championship ran across four September broadcasts under the banner Evolution, its brackets narrowing city by city until a two-out-of-three-falls final crowned the wrestler John Messier as champion.

October's cards built a new number-one contender's match and a six-man tag qualifier for a second title, Impact, alongside a debut match and a launch issue of the promotion's own magazine, Pillar 2 Post, the last event this record holds.
