Conclusion =================================================== INTRODUCTION: Welcome to the official handbook for Vintage American Wrestling, an e-wrestling federation dedicated to bringing the classic style of wrestling right to your front door. Wrestling has changed over the years... From the old NWA, to the newer WWF, all the way to ECW. But our goal is to give credit to where it all began... VAW brings you back to the old-school.
Though VAW is basied in America, we encourage wrestlers of all decent. It would not be realistic to have all American wrestlers, yet it is important to know that _almost_ all cards will be held in the United States, so American wrestlers are welcomed and somewhat encouraged.
Like the classic wrestling federations of yesteryear, Vintage American Wrestling mixes a blend of different genres - lucha libre, aerial wrestling, hardcore wrestling and of course the wrestling-first philosophy of old-school organisations. Wrestling comes before run-ins, screw-job endings and so forth in VAW, but we're confident that the fed will cater for all wrestling tastes.
The driving forces behind this e-fed are [name withheld] and Johnny . Ben ran the highly successful TAW (Totally Awesome Wrestling) for 9 months way back in 1996, before he decided to turn the reigns over to a friend. Since then he has tried fed-heading a couple times, but none were as successful. Johnny ran
RDW (Red Dice Wrestling). It was also quite proserous in its six month existence.
The in-character president, is female. Her name is Jen Hoffman. Please don't harrass her : )
Read on to find out all about VAW and what is required and expected of handlers in this fed.
=================================================== TITLES: VAW has thre singles titles at the moment. More may be added as the roster grows, and if we feel there is need for another. The tag team division, which is currently un-open will open and have a tag titles as tag teams become active...
VAW World Championship (Universal Weight) - This is the grandaddy of 'em all. If you have this strapped 'round your waist, you can pretty much consider yourself... lucky : )
VAW National Championship (Universal Weight) - This belt ain't to shabby either. A main part of the old-school wrestling scheme was regional championships, and Vintage American Wrestling offers this prize as it's secondary title.
VAW Featherweight Championship (230 lbs. or less) - This division is made up of the little men. And while use of weapons and tables is strictly prohibited in the heavyweight division unless specified in a speacial rules contest, you might find these matches a little more hardcore.
====================================================== PARTICIPATION: The name of the game is participation folks. The more you get involved, the better off your wrestler will do. Participation is done by writing flashes
(interviews), in-ring interviews, promo clips, and strategies, and if you do these, your chances of doing well in VAW is pretty high.
When you write your interviews, make sure you use one of Vintage American
Wrestling's official interviewers......
Joel Howard - This man knows it all. From different wrestling techniques to statistics that can't be found anywhere, Joel can lay down the facts. He is also the face commentator for VAW cards.
Ted Archibald - To be honest this man has very little knowledge of wrestling at all, and it shows. He prefers the hype of wrestling, and his interviewing techniques are "questionable". In other words, he goes for the dirt and isn't afraid to tell wrestlers what he thinks of them. Ted is VAW's heel commentator.
Greg Gunnar - A former wrestling great, now in his 50s. He usually does the backstage interviews, and stays behind the scenes most of the time. He'll occasionally make his way out the ring to pay respects to his old loyal fans.
As for match strategies, sending them in before the deadline will greatly improve your wrestler's chances of winning a match. Strats show that you're putting effort into your wrestler, and also help the writers do their job.
Strats should include - any special entrance, overall strategy, any special moves, and anything that will continue or start an angle.
======================================================= RULES: The rules are fiercely enforced. Breaking them leads to suspension and then firing. They are...
1) No attacking officials (including announcers) 2) No attacking wrestlers (This one has SOME leeway, but very little) at ringside or on the way to the ring, unless based completely by an angle. 3) No attacking Vintage American Wrestling fans 4) No use of foreign objects (apart from in the Featherweight division) or unprofessional conduct. Big gang beat-downs and run-ins are strongly advised against, but are not prohibited. Once again, an angle can allow for some extra creativity.
======================================================== HOW TO WIN: VAW is a booked league, which means no simulator is used whatsoever. A booking committee of three/four people votes on the matches to decide outcomes, except where an angle dictates winning and losing. In deciding matches, the bookers use participation as their measuring stick.
========================================================= CARDS - SHOWS: There is only one main wrestling card in VAW each week, called "Ring Riots". It is comprised of 5-7 live matches, made up of matches, and comes out on Saturday night.
Also, the week's RPs are bundled together and put out as "Double-Talk" on Mondays and Thursdays. This means there's only two times for interviews to be screened, so quality is put ahead of quantity. You can however also include promo clips and live interviews (in ring or backstage) for Ring Riots.
Each Monday a newsletter will be sent out with rankings, news, injuries/roster changes etc, plus the confirmed line-up for the coming week's Ring Riots and provisional line-ups for the coming weeks.
So, flashes in response to what happened on Saturday's card will be used on the Monday Double-Talk, while flashes about the coming Saturday's action will be used on the Thursday show. Deadlines are midday Monday and Thursday.
Match strats need to be in by at least midday Wednesday, to give our writers time to put Ring Riots together.
===================================================== PAY-PER-VIEWS: VAW PPVs at HUGE. Usually, there are a few stipulations matches, and this is where the big feuds come to a conclusion. It's also where new feuds start brewing.
VAW has PPVs once every two months, so as not to make them too run of the mill, and keep that excitement about them.
======================================================= CHARACTER CREATION: The best thing to do is read right through this handbook and get to know the information listed here. Then, as the fed becomes more populated check out the roster, and try to create a gimmick that is different that previously existing ones. However, if you have a character or style you like best, feel free to go ahead and use it.
As we've already said, original American wrestlers will make up the bulk of the VAW roster. The roster itself will be kept lower than some "big feds", at around 30 wrestlers, which is done so that wrestlers don't get "lost in the shuffle". Of the 30 wrestlers, about 20-25 will be American, and the remainder foreign. The roster is split into the heavyweight and cruiserweight divisions, however, cruiserweights are welcome to test their luck with the big boys.
