Wave Cap, black wrestling boots, and a black pair of Oakley sunglasses. Dante is a medium build african american male. He has a trimmed body, no fat whatsoever. He has a few scars across his pecs. He has a shaven near-bald head, along with a nicely shaven beard.]
[In the asile he slowly walks to the ring, no emotion is shown on his face whatsoever he looks like he's ready to go to prison. As he walks up the ring steps, and is on the apron, he springs over the top rope, landing on the second rope, with his arms lifted in the air as the crowd boos the Ghetto Adonis even more. ]

Walking to the ring, with a microphone in hand is Ted Archibald as he busts through the ring, startling Grayson Jones as he shoves the microphone in his face>>
[TED ARCHIBALD] Grayson Jones, tell me... how does it feel to finally become a pro wrestler? ["THE NATURAL" GRAYSON JONES] Now's not the time, Archibald.... [TED ARCHIBALD] Now Natural, I just witnessed you get torn apart by the hands of the "Ghetto Adonis" Dante Evans. Just what was your game plan tonight? ["THE NATURAL" GRAYSON JONES] What the hell kind of question is that? There's no doubt in anyone's mind that I got my butt handed to me in a silver platter... but c'mon! There's just some episodes in life that don't go the way that they're supposed to be planned. I know I can beat Dante Evans... and I know full well that if that big, black, piece of dog excrement wants to try and whup my perfectly molded derierre... again... then he can accept the challenge that I'm laying out for him.
[TED ARCHIBALD] You can't be serious... Dante Evans just tore apart any sort of credibility you might've had tonight.
["THE NATURAL" GRAYSON JONES] Ted, if you know what's best for you, you'd think about a wiser selection of words in the near future. Credibility means jack right now, I know I'd be the underdog, once again if I were to step inside the ring with Evans...
but you know what? I could give a damn. Because this Ghetto Adonis represents everything that I personally can't stand about society... he's the classic sociopath that only wants what's best for him, and could give a damn about anyone else, especially Vintage American Wrestling.
[TED ARCHIBALD] Strong words from a man who showed the only thing he's good for is acting like a lifesize wrestling dummy.
["THE NATURAL" GRAYSON JONES] What? [TED ARCHIBALD] I said.... "Strong words from a man who showed the only thing he's good for is acting like a lifesize wrestling dummy."
<<Infuriated that he heard it right, Grayson Jones starts to get a little edgy. Suddenly, Grayson Jones grabs at the collar of Ted Archibald as he yells directly into his face>>
["THE NATURAL" GRAYSON JONES] Listen to me, Ted Archibald! I am "The Natural" Grayson Jones!! I am NOT just some jabronie who happened to walk in off the street! I've spent the last 2 years of my life being turned into a pretzel more ways than one!
And for you to come out here... and call me a "lifesize wrestling dummy"... ain't exactly the type of treatment I'd expect from Vintage American Wrestling! <<Looking out into the crowd with a crazed look in his eyes... Grayson Jones yanks Ted Archibald into the middle of the ring, kicks him in the gut and sets him up for a form of a suplex as he fishermens suplexes him out of his boots. Archibald remains motionless on the mat as he goes into shock as Grayson Jones picks up the microphone and continues to berate him more.>>
["THE NATURAL" GRAYSON JONES]
How'd ya like that, Archie!!! Lifesize wrestling dummy, my ASS! <<Suddenly Cheif of Security Mark Wallace runs into the ring and tackles "The Natural" Grayson Jones, knocking him clear out of his socks as the microphone goes flying outside the ring.... at that same time, Ted

<<Getting right back up on his feet, "The Natural" Grayson Jones goes face to face with Cheif of Security Mark Wallace... a stare down ensues as the big, jacked, bald Cheif of Security, and the irate youngster are deadlocked onto each other.>>
<<Entering the ring, with a microphone in hand is President Jen Hoffman.>>
[PRESIDENT JEN HOFFMAN]
Hold it!! Hold it now! "The Natural" Grayson Jones... you are hereby officially SUSPENDED from Vintage American Wrestling.... you're attack on a man who cannot defend himself is an outright violation of our rulebook.
