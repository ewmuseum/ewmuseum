The GAPW exists to provide wrestling fans with a creative and enjoyable gaming format. Ever dream of putting on tights and walking down that long aisle into the squared circle? Well the GAPW is a fed were you can create your own wrestling character and guide him to the World Title!!! GAPW was created by [name withheld], and [name withheld]. Mr. [name withheld], has ran a fed before called the WFWL (World Fantasy Wrestling League). The WFWL was great but folded to lack of help. Mr [name withheld] has also roleplayed in fed's such as, ERWI, ICE, PTWL, EXTREME, HOW, WAeW, IeWF, and NOW. Mr. [name withheld] has also wrote matches for various leagues beside his own. So it's safe to say Mr. [name withheld] has a trememdous amount of experience in the front office. Mr. [name withheld], ran ECWA which was a great fed but folded to lack of help. Len has roleplayed in such fed's as ERWI, ICE, WAeW, IeWF, and NOW so he also has a tremendous amount of experience too. So it's safe to say the co-president's are well experienced! II. -=-GAPW Rules-=- The GAPW will not allow any "Out of the World" gimmicks. Meaning, I will not tolerate wrestlers with gimmicks that are too far-fetched. For example I don't want any bionic plumbers or clowns in my league. NO comic book gimmicks, just keep it plain and simple. As a general rule of thumb, applicants whose wrestlers are similar characters the league already established in GAPW, are not admitted, even if they are top roleplayers. I mean, would you want to have five undertakers running around? ALL wrestlers must be original and not taken off any 'real' wrestler. (i.e. no heartbreaker, stone colds, or nature boys) You can only submit ONE singles wrestler or ONE tag team , but not both. Players tend to enter four or five wrestlers in one league, and only pays attention to one of them. The other three or four wrestlers he owns just sit there, eat up space, and do nothing. Also this allows you to focus on one primary character to guide. In order to ensure each player recieves sizable activity and participation in GAPW, the roster must be kept relatively small. GAPW allows 48 singles wrestlers and 12 tag teams. This allows each wrestler a match once a week and the attention the player deserves in a league. Upon being admitted to GAPW, the President will ask for a sample roleplay. Here is an idea of what is expected of you in your sample roleplay. III. -=-GAPW Particpation-=- Participation in GAPW is essential. It is the key to success. Roleplaying (see Roleplaying Regulations below) takes up much of that participation. You are required to submit a minimum of two flashes per week. One of those flashes is your major flash, meaning it's your big time flash, meaning it is your all-around big time flash that developes your character. The other flash is a shorter, but by any means equal quality to that of your major one. Read Roleplay Regulations below for full understanding of your roleplaying standards. Other ways of participation is sending in strats, sneak attacks, and getting involved in your current angles and storylines. I will get into it in more detail as the league progresses. IV. --GAPW Programs-- GAPW currently promotes two live weekly cards: -GAPW Monday Night Fright -GAPW Thursday Night Trashing In addition, there are three features that come out during the week: -GAPW Timelock -GAPW Interview Packet -GAPW Etc. Pay-Per-View's occur every three months. V. -=-GAPW Roleplay Regulations-=- GAPW emphasis on, good writing=good push. A long roleplay doesn't neccessarily mean that is a good roleplay. A well written quailty 6K roleplay can beat out a long, dull, boring and dragging 12K roleplay any day of the week. Though, the longer the roleplay the better. Remember the three most important things when creating a roleplay. Content, Length and Detail. Your flashes will be sent out in the daily feature GAPW Interview Packet. Each day, I will send out any roleplays received, unless I recieve none. But keep in mind, your are still to roleplay AT LEAST two times a week. In Interview Packet, there will be table of contents. Your all around big time flash, your major one, no shorter then 3k, will be labeled in ('s) "Major Flash". Your minor flash shorter bu equal quality will be labeled in ('s) "Minor Flash". Failure to produce roleplays of any sort, be they interviews, strats, or whatever, in a short period of time will result a cautionary warning from the President, and if the inactivity continues, your wrestler may be suspeneded or fired by the GAPW altogether. VI. -=-The Ground Rules-=- Nobody is stopping you from having fun, and that is the main reason you come to GAPW, is to have fun. But like all good things, there must be rules that each player must abide by, in consideration of the GAPW Council and your fellow players. Please follow these regulations: DON'T use another player's wrestler, unless there has been an OK by the other party and by the President. Furthermore, don't ask for your wrestler to hosptialize another wrestler unless there has been consent from the other party and the President. DON'T attack announcers or any of the GAPW staff, unless there has been express permission by the President. You can attack as many no name jobbers as you want. DON'T use excessive profanity. Often when you go into realization of your character, obscene language is all part of the game when it is hot and heavy that your wrestler is going through. However, dirty and very vulgar words will be edited out in your flashes. Words such as "ass" and "damn" will be allowed, though stronger words than it will not. You have to keep in mind that such language is not acceptable in television programmings. It is very possible to express your anger and extreme emotions without using a string of four letter words. VII. -=-How does the GAPW operate?-=- GAPW is a ''booked'' league. Meaning the match results are predetermined by the GAPW council. Results are not made up of, with that single match in mind. Generally, it accounts for the bigger picture, meaning the angles, storylines, and so forth. And your basis of roleplaying helps a tremendous way in helping the match results. However, for instance, two good roleplayers have a match against each other, both have been flashing all week, and now it comes to conclusion that the Co-President's will take it upon themselves to determine results with a coherent storyline in mind. VIII. -=-Titles-=- Global Action Packed Wrestling currently sanctions three singles titles: GAPW World Heavyweight Championship (Universal Weight) GAPW Trans-Continental Championship (Universal Weight) GAPW Skyweight Championship (Weight limit-245lbs) GAPW also sanctions one tag team title: GAPW World Tag Team Championship (Universal Weight Limit) The addition of a National Tag Team Championship is currently being considered by the GAPW council. Titles can only change hands by pinfall or submission, unless specified before the match. IX. -=-Rankings-=- GAPW uses a point system for its rankings. Once getting admitted to GAPW, the wrestler starts off with zero points and works his way up by getting victories. A wrestler is not deducted any points if he loses. Point System are as follows: +20 Points for match if win by pinfall or submission +10 Points for match if won by DQ or countout +5 Points for match if a Draw X. -=-GAPW Fictional Faces and Places-=- [name withheld]: GAPW Co-President [name withheld]: GAPW Co-President Chicago, Illinois: Action-Packed Towers (GAPW Headquarters) Action-Packed Palace: GAPW's very own arena located in Chicago, Illinois That Airs PTWF Programs GAPW's Main Sponsor: HAUS's Health & Fitness Doug Jones: GAPW's Main Color Commentator Rex "Stud" Thompson: PTWF's Co-Color Commentator Tim "Stylin" White: PTWF's Main Correspondent XI. -=-Application-=- -=-Send this to [detail withheld] ,and [detail withheld]-=- -Personal Information- (For a Tag Team send in a application for each wrestler) First Name: (Or whatever you want me to call you!)
