I Can't Believe It's Not Real ICBINR Card Summaries On The Road (February 3, 1998) National Guard Armory, Oklahoma City, OK ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ --Jack Emerald d. Antony Foster by submission with the Five Star Stretch. --Poe d. Commander Cody by submission with the Nevermore. --Kai Lewis d. El Bandito by pinfall with the Big Apple. --Jive Turkey v. Blackstreet ends in double countout. --Angry Chef d. Dane Thompson by DQ (Thompson caught using Brass Knuckles). Small Package (February 28, 1998) GPWA Arena, Oklahoma City, OK ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ --Alonzo Hodge d. Master Tao by pinfall with the Wildside Drop. --4-way match for the GPWA Tag Team Championship: The Cutting Edge (Terry & Bobby Jones), Ron Baxter & Hiroki Date, The Princes of Darkness (Belliel & Crowley), Thunder & Lightning. ++ Baxter pins T. Jones with the Magnetic Pull. ++ Belliel pins Date with a Powerbomb. ++ Thunder pins Belliel with the Rainmaker. ** Thunder & Lightning crowned GPWA Tag Team Champions. On The Road (March 19, 1978) National Guard Armory, Wichita, KS ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ GPWA Heavyweight Championship Tournament, quarter- and semi-finals: --Jack Emerald d. Kai Lewis with a reverse Indian Deathlock (Ref ends match after Lewis passes out from the pain.) ** Emerald advances to semi-finals. --Angry Chef d. Randal Prophet by countout (Prophet voluntarily leaves the ring after losing a Rock-Paper-Scissors contest). ** Chef advances to semi-finals. --Poe d. Jive Turkey by submission with the Nevermore. ** Poe advances to semi-finals. --Dane Thompson v. Violent Librarian ends in double ten-count (neither wrestler could stand). ** Because there was no winner, Jack Emerald receives a bye in the semis and advances to final round. --Angry Chef d. Poe by pinfall with the Scrambler. ** Chef advances to final round. Small Package (April 27, 1998) GPWA Arena, Oklahoma City, OK ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ --Theo Wilson d. Bandito II by pinfall with Thunderstruck. --Alonzo Hodge d. Blackstreet by pinfall with the Wildside Drop. --Hiroki Date & Ron Baxter d. The Princes of Darkness by pinfall (Baxter pins Crowley) ** As the winners, Date & Baxter receive a tag-title match against Thunder & Lightning. Slamfest (May 17, 1998) New Braunfels High School Gymnasium-New Braunfels,TX ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ --Jordan Stone d. Commander Cody by pinfall with Jamaica Me Crazy. --Jack Robinson d. Master Tao by pinfall with a top rope DDT. --Mr. Black & Mr. Orange d. the Banditos by submission (Bandito #2 submits to the Blackout). --Violent Librarian d. Jack Emerald by pinfall with the Iron Fist. On The Road (May 24, 1998) Garden City Community College Gymnasium, Garden City, KS ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ --Commander Cody d. Cashmere Harris by pinfall with the Third Stage. --Jive Turkey d. Mr. Brown by DQ after Mr. Black & Mr. Orange interfere (Man, Myth & Legend then cut Turkey's Dreadlocks). --Kai Lewis v. Randal Prophet ends in a time limit draw. --Dane Thompson d. Jack Emerald & Violent Librarian in a Triple Threat Match when Emerald submits to a top wristlock. ** The submission was apparently part of a set-up. Thompson & Emerald double teamed Librarian the entire match, finally knocking him unconscious, and Emerald submitted immediately. ** Following the match, Picken's Army attacks both men; Kai Lewis comes from the dressing room and attacks Emerald. --Thunder & Lightning d. Hiroki Date & Ron Baxter by pinfall (Thunder pins Date with a Powerslam).
