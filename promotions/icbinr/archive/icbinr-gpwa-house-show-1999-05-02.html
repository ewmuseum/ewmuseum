GPWA House Show Results

2 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GPWA House Show McClain County Expo Center - Purcell, Oklahoma 05/02/99 - Paid Attendance - 466 ~**~**~**~**~ Time Stands Still vs Pressure Point This match saw the Mark Stitar Memorial Cup #6 Seed, Pressure Point and the #7 Seed, Time Stands Still, take each other to their limits. The Drake brothers opened up with a punishing assault on Sal DeFazio of Pressure
Point. Using quick tags and well executed double teams. Sal was able to fight his way out of the corner with a burst of adrenaline and make the tag to his partner Gregg Matthews. Matthews turned the tide of the match momentarily until a Damon Drake knee to the back as he came off the ropes slowed him down. Damien and Gregg then proceeded to punish each other mercilessly until both men tagged out simultaneously. Sal DeFazio had the match under control and had rolled up Damon Drake for the pin but as both
Matthews and Damien Drake charged into the ring. Referee Dick Richard turned to put Gregg Matthews back in his corner. Allowing Damien to attack DeFazio and switch places with his twin brother. The stunned DeFazio walked right into a swinging DDT and was pinned as Damon Drake interrupted Gregg Matthews attempt to break the pin.
~**~**~**~**~ Jive Turkey vs Poe Poe walked out and bad mouthed Jive Turkey. He said he is gonna derail the grand funk railroad and cause a bigger train wreck than when
Casey Jones derailed his train so long ago. He bad mouthed "Violence" Jack
Robinson and Randal Prophet and sent out a warning to both.
Jive Turkey was the solid fan favorite in this match as the crowd erupted when "Give Up Da Funk" started over the P.A.. Jive started the match quick with a series of rights followed by a funky dance that really got the crowd going but Poe quickly quieted the arena with a thumb to the eye and a kick to the crotch. After a few minutes in the ring. Poe tossed Jive to the floor and then busted him wide open with a DDT on the concrete floor. Things were really looking bad for Jive as Patrick Davis finally got the match back into the ring but as the crowd intensity picked up. So did the intensity of Jive
Turkey and he was able to turn the match back around beaten and bloody as he was. Poe attempted a kick to the stomach to turn the momentum back his way again but it was caught and turned into an awesome Enzuiguiri by Jive. The
Funk Drop (Inverted DDT) put finally put Poe away for the 1-2-3.
~**~**~**~**~ Jack Emerald vs Violent Librarian w/Larry Pickens Violent Librarian and Larry Pickens come to the ring and start out with a few crowd insults, mostly directed at the "Detmer" section of fans. From there Larry will welcomed back Jack Emerald by telling him he wishes
Parkhurst had finished the job, but he forgave Parkhurst. Violent Librarian rushed across the ring as Larry finished talking and nearly took Jack Emerald out of his boots. Emerald was able to weather the initial onslaught by the Librarian and turn the tide in his favor. It looked like the match might end early as Emerald was about to apply an ankle lock submission but the Librarian was able to power his way to the ropes and out to the floor. Emerald took all of the insults he could take from Larry
Pickens before he finally had enough and took chase. As Pickens rounded a corner of the ring. Violent Librarian leveled Emerald with a chair to the head and tossed him back to the ring. There were some anxious moments as
Emerald took the loss after the Dewey Devestation System (Top Rope
Piledriver). Emerald left the ring under his own power but was holding his neck. ~**~**~**~**~ Hawk Hunter Hawk Hunter comes out to a loud chorus of boos. Hawk smiles and pretends that they are cheers. Hawk jumps in the ring and poses for the fans. He first starts out by saying that he is glad to inform the entire crowd that he just had a talk with Tad Detmer back stage, and the two just worked out an agreement that Hawk Hunter is the rightful and most deserving GPWA champion, and Tad Detmer handed over the GPWA title to him. The crowd again sent a round of boos and some other chants. Hawk said that it was basically a done deal and later on that night, Tad was formally going to hand over the belt to Hawk in the ring. Hawk said that Tad just did not want to face Hawk nor get put in the Talon Grip. Hawk said that Tad was a trooper and said that he gave him an affectionate noogie for a choice well made. Hawk finished saying that he was going to proudly dedicate the rest of his GPWA career to the Detmer, "The Little Guy", and said that he would be back out to greet the fans as the NEW GPWA champion later. Hawk then exited the ring to another round of boos.
~**~**~**~**~ Southern Flava vs The Man & The Myth The champs started out the match showing why they are the champs as they kept Cooley isolated in the ring in the opening minutes of this match. Mr.
Orange put on an aerial display and Mr. Black used power moves in their attempt to dismantle Cooley. A blind tag by Cooley allowed Southern Flava to get some offense in. As Slim took Mr. Orange to the mat with a clothesline as he turned around after Cooley leap frogged over his back body drop. Slim was able to keep Mr. Orange in the ring and wear him down long enough for
Cooley to recover and a series of quick tags and double teams. The most impressive being when Slim gorilla pressed Cooley and dropped him across the prone body of Mr. Orange. Mr. Black finally entered the ring and the match broke down into a brawl. Cooley was tossed out hard to the floor and Mr.
Black secured the pinfall victory after the Total Eclipse (Belly to Belly/Russian Sickle Combo) on Slim. ~**~**~**~**~ Hawk Hunter vs "Sideshow" Tad Detmer (Non-Title) Hawk Hunter was all smiles as he stood in the middle of the ring as Tad
Detmer made his way down with the Great Plains Heavyweight Title. Tad took the belt off and just as it looked as if he would hand the belt over. He wiped the grin off of Hawk's face as he pasted him upside the head with it.
Hawk Hunter then tried every trick in the book to avoid Tad Detmer. He hid behind the ref, ducked under the ropes but when he vaulted over the ropes to avoid Detmer. Detmer caught him with a no touch plancha. Hawk staggered back to his feet and waved his hands at Detmer as if he was finished with him and began walking out of the arena. Detmer gave chase and grabbed Hunter by both ears and drug him back to the ring like a child. Hawk began to beg off once again in the ring. As Detmer was looking at the fans. Hunter caught him with a lightning fast standing drop kick and then nailed him with a DDT. Hawk hammered the champ with an aerial assault briefly before tossing him out of the ring. The crowd went wild as Hawk attempted a Handspring Moonsault as
Detmer was getting to his feet on the floor but Hawk was slightly off target and both men crashed hard into the railing. Hunter was first to regain his feet and he tossed Detmer back into the ring. Both men scored near pinfalls at this point as a small package lead through a series of reversals into pinning predicaments. As both men fought back to there feet. Hawk was sent flying into the corner by Detmer and crashed hard into Referee R.U. Sirrius.
Knocking Sirrius unconcious. Tad monkey flipped Hunter out of the corner and then attempted to revive the ref as Hunter crawled to the opposite corner.
At this point, Hawk pounded on the mat 4 times and a hand mysteriously appeared from under the ring and handed him an elbow pad. As Detmer turned back around he was leveled with a running forearm smash with an obviously loaded elbow pad. Hawk then pulled Sirrius over and secured the pinfall victory, with his feet on the ropes for leverage, over Detmer.
