IOWA Smackdown: Main Event

18 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera at ringside.] Brown: I can't believe that. Meikai is an intricate part of Fire & Ice. Gleason: And if they are blaming Death Warrant....and it's true.... Death Warrant may have an angry team to deal with. And that may not be good. Brown: So you're saying that if it is Death Warrant up to no good, the plan may backfire? Gleason: Maybe. But both teams are so good....both are undefeated... and this is gonna be one for the books.
Brown: Well let's not wait any further! Take it away Michael! Michael McDonald: Ladies and Gentlemen....it is time for the MAIN EVENT OF THE EVENING!!!! *** Huge Crowd Pop *** Michael Michael: It is for the GREAT LAKES TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP!!!!! *** Crowd Pop *** Michael McDonald: Inroducing first....the challengers.....Coming to the ring first, hailing from Dallas, Texas, standing 6'2" at a weight of 247 pounds,
"The Sniper" Jamison Wilks! His partner, from Charlotte, North Carolina, 6'5" weighing 270 pounds, "The Poison Arrow" Jesse Blanchard! They are....DEATH WARRANT!!!! WARRANT!!!!
*** Mixed Crowd Pop *** [Lights dim except for a spotlight over the center of the ring. A large banner that reads "FIRE and ICE" drops from the ceiling and a scope appears on it. The sound of two quick shots shoot over the speakers and two holes appears in the banner. "Mission Accomplished" plays over the P.A. A faster series of rifle shots are heard as Death Warrant enters through the curtain, clad in a long black trenchcoats and large cowboy hats. They walk very calmly to the ring, very professional.]
Gleason: Well, if they got Meikai....she ain't with them! [Once at ringside, that stand in the ring with their heads down facing the entrance way.] Michael McDonald: And their opponents...THE GREAT LAKES TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS!! at a combined weight of 495 pounds...K-Solo!!! All$tar!!! FIRE AND ICE!!! [The arena darkens and smoke begins to fill the entrance way and a white strobe light flashes in the background to simulate lightning. "Bring The
Pain" by Method Man plays over the PA system and All$tar and K-Solo come through the smoke.
All$tar is an African-American, medium brown skin, his hair is in a very short natural with the sides faded slightly. He has a trimmed mustache and brown eyes. K-Solo is also African-American, has brown eyes and wears his hair in a short afro with a goatee.
They wear their usual wrestling outfit with a black leather vest. Solo's vest has SOLO written on the back in flames and All$tar has
ALL$TAR written on the back as if frozen in ice. They walk slowly with serious looks on their face as they stare at their opponent.)
Brown: You can feel the electricity here! This is gonna be a helluva collision!! [Death Warrant turns and slides out of the ring, meeting F&I head on. It seems like they aren't fighting particularly offensive, but rather are simply trying to tie F&I up and occupy them, not really throwing many punches except in self-defense.]
*** Mixed Crowd Pop *** Brown: Death Warrant has attacked Fire & Ice outside the ring and here we go. These two teams aren't even gonna bother to wait for the bell.
Gleason: We gotta a hell of a fight goin on and the lights aren't up yet. You can barely see these four men through the smoke. Brown: Death Warrant doesn't look they're doing much, Coach. They looked like the agressors at first but now its as if they are on the defensive.
Fire & Ice is taking it to them! [A crackling is heard over the P.A.] Wilks: Boys, boys. Surely we would do better than that. *** Mixed Crowd Pop *** Brown: What the hell? Gleason: I don't like...well...actually I DO like the looks of this. [All$tar and Solo stop fighting and begin looking around, realizing they are not fighting Wilks and Blanchard.]
Wilks: You see, the problem when you're a target is that you have to have eyes in the back of your head.
[Two more men jump over the guardrail in the aisle and rush Fire & Ice as the other two men back off. But once again, these two men aren't throwing many punches, but rather are just tying up Solo's and All$tar's arms so they can't do anything.]
Brown: I don't get this. Death Warrant tyring to play mind games. The lights still are not on and Fire & Ice being ambushed AGAIN!
*** Crowd Ooos and Pop *** Gleason: Even in the dark this crowd saw that as Solo just deliverd a kick to the family jewels of some poor guy!
Brown: All$tar has gotten the upper hand as well, tackling his man to the ground. Firing mounted punches at the head!
[A voice over the PA again.] Wilks: Now look at this. There you are, barely holding your own against a couple of overly exuberant fans. That's pretty damn pathetic. It's hard when you don't know where your hunter is...almost ...unFAIR wouldn't you say?
[Two more people aproach the guardrail, dressed the same way as the lights come up a bit. One is quite smaller than the other, and holds a chair, but seems to be looking for a way out of the encounter. The bigger one walks behind. The other two back away from Solo and All$Star a little bit, freeing them up.]
Brown: This is ridiculous! Quite possible the WORST sneak attack I've ever seen. They've sent 4 people down here now who have barely thrown a punch.
We've got two more people coming over the railing, and hell, one of them doesn't even seem to WANT to be here.
Gleason: Ya gotta wonder where Death Warrant found these clowns. And the bigger one just grabbed the smaller one and THREW him over the railing.
Brown: The small guy just tried to put the chair down, but the bigger one pushed it back at him. That guy REALLY deosn't want to be down here.
** WHACK and BIG POP ** Gleason: Oh MAN, that had to hurt. Gleason: Oh MAN, that had to hurt. Brown: What the HELL? AllStar turned around as the smallest person out there was just PUSHED right into a SUPERKICK that drove that chair right into the
FACE! All$star's Suprekick is lethal! ** Crowd Boos and BOR-ING chants ** Brown: You'd think Death Warrant would hire professionals... or at least do it right themselves. Not that I'm condoning this, but let's face it.... Fire
& Ice can beat up on nobodies all night! [Another man crawls out from under the ring, in the same trenchcoat and hat, also carrying a shotgun. He holds a microphone as well.]
Gleason: Will somebody stop this, this is pathetic. Brown: Wait coach, this one has the mic... that HAS to be one of them! Gleason: I think it's Wilks! Wilks: Thing with a good sniper is...you never know where the next SHOT... [The man with the mic nails Solo with a *WHACK* across the back of the head with the gun] *** Mixed Ooos and Groans, with a sudden LOUD POP *** Wilks: ...Is coming from! Brown: Good lord, somebody better get out here. Solo just got wasted by that shot. Gleason: That's solid steel right there. I KNEW they had a plan. Brown: You just said... Gleason: Quiet Brown! *** WHACK *** Gleason: My God, All$tar just got cracked with the other gun right across the back as he turned to react to that last shot. This is GREAT!
Brown: There's another guy coming from under the ring...it's BLANCHARD!! *** WHACK *** Gleason: Solo and All$tar are almost out! Payback's a BITCH!!! [The arena lights come back on at full blast and the two men with the shotguns take off their masks, revealing Wilks and Blanchard]
*** Very loud, Very Mixed POP *** [Wilks drops the mic as they roll Solo and All$tar into the ring.] Brown: Wilks is all over All$tar as he wraps the shotgun around his throat. And Blanchard is putting the boots to Solo now, who is trying to get towards the ropes to help himself to his feet.
*** Crowd POP *** Brown: Wilks just pulled out 2 sets of handcuffs. Gleason: OH YEAH! Brown: Oh man, this is gonna get ugly in a HURRY!! Gleason: Blanchard takes the one set and All$tar is barely conscious. Wilks cuffs him to the ropes, and Blanchard has cuffed Solo across the ring.
*** Loud Mixed Reaction again, with Oooos, Booos, Cheers all mixed in *** Brown: We need security down here immediately. And Death Warrant are gonna be suspended I have a feeling.
Gleason: No way! Brown: WHAT?!? Gleason: You can't DQ a team when they never had a match! Hell, the ref has bailed already!
Brown: Yeah but... Gleason: BUT MY ASS.... Death Warrant obviously found a loophole and thought payback is better than winning the straps.
Brown: Or maybe they couldn't beat Fire & Ice so they planned this out! Gleason: That remains to be seen. Brown: They've been working over the ribs with the butt-ends of those guns. Solo is on his feet but is doubled over... and All$tar... well, he's seen better days. *** Crowd Ooos *** Brown: Solo is cut in the back of the head Coach. Gleason: That may be the least of his worries after all this is over with. Brown: Fire and Ice have essentially been mugged here. They took out one outside, but there were too many.
Gleason: Yeah..but the others didn't really do anything. They seemed to just be a distraction, but you can see the carnage of the one that $tar hit with that superkick.
Brown: It looked like Blanchard pushed him right into AllStar. Gleason: Wilks and Blanchard don't care, I guarantee that. Brown: Those other four guys are stationed in the aisle with chairs. If security is gonna break THIS up, they better come down armed or something.
Gleason: Solo & AllStar got interrupted when they did this to Death Warrant. Let's see how far THESE guys will go. [Wilks stands in the center of the ring, as Blanchard walks back and forth between Solo and AllStar, kicking almost teasingly at them.]
Wilks: Now...what it seems we have here is a failure to communicate. You see...we TOLD you to be careful...we TOLD you WE were the hunters... and YOU were the hunted.
*** Mixed reaction *** Wilks: And you thought that after some lame chair attack those roles were suddenly going to be REVERSED!!! I don't THINK so!
[Blanchard rolls out of the ring and grabs a chair from the timekeeper's table before jumping onto the apron by K-Solo]
*** WHACK and another mixed reaction *** Brown: A CHAIR SHOT to the back of Solo's head. *** WHACK *** Brown: And another!!! Gleason: Solo is bleeding badly now. And now Wilks is giving Blanchard the mic. Brown: Wilks has put Solo's right leg over the second rope, just hanging there. Blanchard: You wanted me to SING little man...I have a BETTER idea. Why don't you DANCE, BITCH!!!
*** WHACK *** *** WHACK *** Brown: A pair of SHOTS to Solo's knee. He's hopping on one leg now, you can see the pain on his face.
Gleason: I think I saw this dance on the Flintstones once. Blanchard rearing back again....
*** WHACK and groans from the crowd *** Brown: Oh man, Solo just collapsed down after that shot. That leg is useless now, he may have some real knee damage.
Gleason: Professional hitmen picking their spots Brown. But they gotta realize that Solo has bounced back from so much! You better finish the job!
Brown: But enough is enough, they've made their point. Gleason: They don't seem to agree. Wilks: Well, you're rhythm is ok Solo, but frankly, your range SUCKS! *** Crowd Groans, with some boos *** Wilks: MUCH better! Brown: Oh GOD, a kick SQUARE in the..well.. Gleason: Right in the jewel sack. Maybe Solo can sing AND dance now... HAHAHA!! Brown: Wilks is moving over towards All$tar as Solo is in AGONY on the mat, hanging from the ropes. And no sign of security yet.
Gleason: Would you want to get in there with these nuts and tell them to stop? Brown: There has to be SOME order, Coach. And speak of the devil... HERE THEY COME!!!!!! *** Mixed Crowd Pop *** Gleason: Those four men are holding them off pretty good, but I think the security is just gonna out number them!
Wilks: Now boys, I seem to remember a little something about PISTOL WHIPPING! *** LOUD POP **** Brown: NO, they WON'T. Wilks: Question is...if we use guns THIS size. [Wilks holds up his shotgun] Wilks: Does it still count as PISTOL whipping? Brown: All$tar just SPIT IN WILKS' Face! Gleason: Christ, $tar, that wasn't bri....OH GOD!!! *** LOUD Crowds OOOOO's *** [Wilks rears back with the gun, baseball bat style, but stops mid-swing] Wilks: Just so you chumps know... [Wilks pulls out a long, plastic shotgun from his trenchcoat] Wilks: We're just PLAYING with for now. *** WHHHHHHHHHHHHACK and BOOS. Some Pop from the sickos *** Brown: STOP IT! STOP IT NOW!! Wilks just swung that gun like a BAT and nailed All$Star in the head. That was a toy gun, granted, but solid plastic that just shattered, sending pieces all the way into the crowd. Get someone down here NOW.
Gleason: You're getting your wish Brown, here comes more security, and they've got batons with them.
Brown: It's about time. I know Fire & Ice have their detractors, but this is too MUCH! Security is at ring side and some are up on the apron!
Wilks: Take your SQUARE BADGED, MALL SECURITY ASSES to the back. Cuz you don't know if these guns are LOADED!!!
Brown: Who the hell SIGNED these guys? Gleason: I LIKE IT! Brown: They have their right to revenge, but enough's enough. All$tar almost saw his future go down the toilet.
Gleason: But Wilks did his job security has paused in their tracks as Blanchard goes outside. Security is too afraid to rush him.... they don't know how psycho these guys are. Blanchard just went and picked up that fallen assailant just threw that unconscious person over his shoulder and is coming back into the ring.
Brown: Yeah, nice gratitude. He just dumped him in the middle of the ring like a sack of potatoes. Whoever it is, they are starting to come to.
Gleason: Wilks now standing over AllStar, uncuffing him. He's kneeling down, grabbing him by the hair. Blanchard has that person in the middle of the ring, and he STILL isn't moving.
Wilks: You could never have been a good sniper $tar. Because you disobey the first rule...always be sure you're firing at the INTENDED target. Because even in our line of work, it's a shame when innoncent bystanders...
Gleason: O.K., they lost me. What the F[BLLEP]k are they talking about! [Blanchard pulls the mask off of the semi-conscious person.] ** VERY LOUD MIXED REACTION ** Wilks: GET HURT!! Gleason: Oh [BLEEP] Brown: MEIKAI...it's MEIKAI...those BASTARDS!!! THEY KIDNAPPED HER AND PUT HER IN THE MIDDLE OF THIS S[BLEEP]T!!!! Gleason: She's been gagged and everything, but that rag is stained with blood. And ALL$TAR's the one who did it. Even I don't like this one!
Brown: He didn't know. This is HORRIBLE!! Gleason: All$tar is crawling over towards Meikai, but he has NOTHING left. And this crowd...they don't know WHAT to think. Wilks: Understand this right now you worthless pieces of [BLEEP}. Your talk about what a SNEAK ATTACK means...it means ANYTHING you do...WE DO BETTER!!!
Gleason: That may be right. Look at the look on All$tar's face.... I.. I...can't imagine what he's thinking. Brown: I can't believe this. And you probably approve! Gleason: I didn't say I approved exactly. But Fire & Ice sent a message...this is the respo......NO! I can't stoop that low! Women and children belong out of this sport. But it proves NOTHING...NOTHING... to put a woman in the middle of this.
Wilks: Your little not caring attitude, this turn of yours. It all comes NATURALLY to us. Hurting, seeking, hunting, taking...it's who we ARE...what we DO. And we've only BEGUN...
[Wilks and Blanchard kick Solo and All$tar one last time] Wilks: Doing it to you. Let 'em through boys. Brown: Those guys have moved as those SICKOS finally roll out of the ring. And you're right coach...there IS no DQ, but these teams are on a collision course I don't think this sport may have EVER seen.
[All Along The Watchtower blares over the PA as security hits the ring. Wilks and Blanchard walk out of the arena emotionless] Gleason: We got some stretchers coming down here! Brown: All$tar is kneeling over Meikai and apparently saying something to her. Medics are in the ring now. We're gonna go to commercial break folks while this mess is cleaned up!
=== Announcer: Tristar pictures invites you to witness a story about a small town... [Cut to a shot of a sign just outside of a quaint country town, reading: Dancer, Texas. Population 81.] Announcer: ... and a group of friends that live there. [Shot of a group of five young men sitting on the porch of a house. They look like they're five minutes out of high school and just shooting the breeze.]
Boy: Remember we always said that we'd take the first bus out of Dancer right after graduation? [Montage of clips featuring a cast of no-name, redneck, white people trying to make ordinary, boring, household scenes seem dramatic. Mom: But you can't leave! Boy: That's all I've ever wanted to do! Announcer: Little did they all know... they should have listened! [Screen goes black. The sound of a chainsaw cranking up pierces the darkness. The picture comes back with a chain-saw wielding, dead skin
mask-wearing slasher charging the camera!] Announcer: Leatherface is back! [Leatherface lands the chainsaw against an unseen victim off camera! The chainsaw struggles as blood splatters all over the screen. Shots of people running away. Shot of an old pick-up truck that won't start

[Chainsaw buzzes loudly] Announcer: Population 81 -- but not for long! === Brown: The show is over as far as wrestling action but I felt it necessary to take a break from that aweful sight.
Gleason: I myself don't know what to say. Women don't belong in wrestling... and sometimes when you play with fire, you get burned. I think that's a lesson that Fire & Ice found that out tonight. They have to do what they do best and not turn this into......we can do what you do, but even better.
Brown: And what about retaliation? Gleason: Well Solo and All$tar got embarassed and beat up tonight. Meikai is hurt....which I don't necessarily agree, but I'm not gonna make anymore of a fuss about it. She knows what danger she is in when she signs to be there manager for every match. But with that lesson that Fire & Ice has learned....you can't help but believe that a lesson is in store for Death
Warrant! Brown: But where does it end? Murrain must take control of this because somebody's career may get ended....if it hasn't happen tonight. But people we have to go....they're filing out of the Dearborn Civic Center. We have to go as our credits roll we will show you what's going on back stage. For
Coach Dan Gleason....this is Rashaun Brown signing off from another STELLAR, and SHOCKING IOWA SMACKDOWN!
[The camera shows ambulances and medics in the back. Meikai has been stabilized in a stretcher and All$tar is demanding to ride in the same ambulance as Solo gets his head gash and knee looked at! Medics load Meikai in and All$tar is helped into the back....still in obvious pain. He is being worked on as the anger on his face cannot be put in words! Solo is the same.....They load him into an ambulance and the doors shut as the camera fades to black and the credits roll.]
