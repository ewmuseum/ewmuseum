Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

TOWL opened on 26 October 1996 as a booked, roleplay-driven league whose own full name was deliberately never given out in its own recruitment copy — “everything you need to know about TOWL (except what it stands for... you can only find that out if you join) is on there” — and never surfaces anywhere else in its surviving record either. Its president ran it largely alone, recruiting wrestlers and writers by turns as the roster grew and cards lengthened past his own original plan.

Its first pay-per-view was set by a live on-air draw on 20 November 1996: thirty-four wrestlers each pulled a numbered ball from a container, sorting thirty-two of them into eight four-man teams while two went unplaced, for a bracket contesting all five TOWL championships, called live by Will Morris and “Hotshot” Hal Rogers bantering their way through every pick.

Its recurring roster carried acts like the Prophets of Rage, the Armed Forces, Billy Sexton and the W and W Express through the run that followed. Its own last pay-per-view, Torture Three, aired live from Niagara Falls, Canada, with all five titles defended under no-disqualification, no-countout rules across a card that also carried a War Games match and a triangle bout pitting newcomer Lochnar against fan favorites Flame and Sinister, who were also each other's opponents.

The promotion's own closure announcement is dated 8 June 1997, posted the same day as that final pay-per-view, and drew a community obituary thread of well-wishes from other fedheads and former competitors. One reply broke from that tone to call the promotion's president a disgrace for abandoning his roleplayers; another answered on his behalf that he had had no choice but to leave.

How it ended isn't stated beyond that. Its surviving documents run from its October 1996 opening through the closure thread that followed its final card.
