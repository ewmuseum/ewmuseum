O'Macky didn't seem too phased by the fact that his last match in TOWL had resulted in a pinfall defeat at the hands of Perfect and started things off in dominating mood. The Glitter Boy didn't know what to make of it and after being powerbombed after just four minutes, it looked like O'Macky's new mean streak was helping his wrestling. However, Glitter somehow kicked out and began to wear O'Macky down with submission holds similar to those of Billy Sexton and even attempted the armbar submission which has won Sexton so many matches, but it didn't have the same effect. Every time Glitter went for his Glitz Blitz figure four leglock, O'Macky was able to either grab the ropes or kick him off and he even turned it into a small package on one occasion. The match changed direction though, when someone jumped from the crowd, wearing a baseball cap with a Cleveland logo on it, dark sunglasses, a baggy shirt, a pair of black leggings and sneakers. Just who it was nobody knows, but it was obviously a female, possible a jealous Glitter Boy admirer. Anyway, she jumped out of the crowd and started attacking the Glitter Girl with punches, slaps and kicks and made her legs bleed and also brought tears to her eyes. The Boy was so distracted by this that O'Macky was able to perform a big 360 powerslam and nearly got the three. The Mist dragged the rabid fan off the Girl and held her so that The Enforcers were able to escort her out of the arena. The ref counted the powerslam, but it was only a two. Sexton banged the mat for O'Macky to finish it off, but as he did, Wyldcard emerged through the curtain, apparently on behalf of Bishop Hell who was in too bad a state to actually come out and was resting in his locker room with Tolwyn Dane. Wyldcard stood in the middle of the aisle to a good face pop and it obviously put the Glitter Boy off, as Wyld was supposed to be wrestling him tonight, rather than Glitter facing O'Macky. O'Macky took advantage and DDTd Glitter. Glitter kicked out at two point nine-nine. The crowd urged Wyld to go and interfere to try and lose Glitter the match, but he knew that interfering would win Glitter that match, not lose it, so he just stood and watched, hoping to put Glitter off. Unfortunately for him, the tactic didn't work, as when O'Macky lifted Glitter to his feet, Glitter managed to get an elbow to the midsection, then performed a spinning DDT of his own. He immediately moved in on O'Macky and locked on his Glitz Blitz figure four and O'Macky submitted immediately. Sexton got into the ring to break things up and immediately started pounding on Glitter. Wyldcard left at this point, but The Mist got in and helped out his mate. O'Macky was in enough pain not to be a threat and The Mist was easily able to drag Glitter out, leaving Sexton standing in the ring calling them both back, then tending to O'Macky. |+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+| Winner: THE GLITTER BOY Match Time: 17:00 |+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+|+| WM: So, fans, The Glitter Boy marches on and meets Billy Sexton in a match for the World Title next week.
