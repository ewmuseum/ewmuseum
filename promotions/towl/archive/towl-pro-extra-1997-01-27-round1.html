TOWL Card Archive

TOWL Pro EXTRA!, Round One

27 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A jazzed up version of TOWL Pro LIVE!'s theme music plays as the screen shows some of the action from the previous three nights' round robin tournament: Bishop Hell backdropping Curmudgeon, The Undead Evil Super Zombie Warrior being escorted out of the ring area, 'Painbringer' Billy Sexton locking Wildthang in an abdominal stretch, Lycanth clotheslining The Agony Uncle to the mat, 'The Irish Giant' Jacob O'Macky draping The Extremist over his shoulders, Tiger and Shane Matthews having a staredown, Anarchy headbutting Nuke, The Players Club and The Eire Alliance battling each other simultaneously, Derek Rage powerbombing Jim Owen. Then the screen shows the famos Las Vegas Strip and the many hotels there. It moves up, slowly to The Excalibur and finally to the MGM Grand and the huge lion at the entrance. Then cut down to Will Morris who is standing right next to it, wearing a pair of shorts, a white TOWL t-shirt and a white baseball cap.) WM: Tonight, fans, you will see what happened here in the desert of Nevada, more precisely this building right here, the MGM Grand Las Vegas. What occurred inside this arena last night, and on the two previous nights, is guaranteed to have the fans talking for the weeks and months to come. Every TOWL wrestler was involved, but unlike the opening pay-per-view, the wrestlers chose their partners for the tag team matches. Here's a quick rundown of the rules: (TOWL Pro EXTRA!'s theme music plays over the top of Chris Allen's voice.) CA: 1. Thirty TOWL singles wrestlers were split into six groups of five, with all men wrestling each other in a round-robin style tournament. 2. Twelve TOWL tag teams wrestled in three groups of four, also in a round-robin tournament. 3. Win is by pinfall, submission, countout or disqualification as usual. Each match in the first round had a fifteen minute time limit, with that rule being abolished for the finals. 4. The winners of each singles group would then square off in another round-robin tournament. 4a: The winners of the groups and ultimately the whole tournament were decided by how many points they gained with three for a victory, one for a draw and zero for a defeat. 5. The three group winning tag teams then proceeded into the final along with the two highest scoring second placed teams and wrestled in a round-robin tournament also. 5a: Points system as with 4a. 6. The singles winner was handed $200000 and an MGM Grand Round-Robin Trophy, valued at $50000. 7. Both members of the winning tag team were given $100000 and a $50000 trophy each. 8. The competition is to be held bi-monthly. 9. Any excess money from ticket sales revenue or merchandising on the night was to be given to a local charity to raise money for children on the streets of The Gambling Capital of The World. (Cut back to Will Morris outside the Grand.) WM: Well fans, let's just refresh our memories about who was in which singles group: GROUP ONE: Steve Swanson, Bishop Hell, Curmudgeon, Dyllon Dean, Killspree The Terrible GROUP TWO: The Glitter Boy, The Undead Evil Super Zombie Warrior, Wyldcard, Dagger, Spartan GROUP THREE: The American Patriot, The Prizefighter, Wildthang, Cro-Mag, 'Painbringer' Billy Sexton GROUP FOUR: 'Heavy Weapons' Max Doom, Jason Knight, The Agony Uncle, The Genius, Lycanth GROUP FIVE: Perfect, 'The Irish Giant' Jacob O'Macky, War, Seth Blitzspear, The Extremist GROUP SIX: Tiger, 'Sudden Death' Shane Matthews, Jax, Death (subbing for Legion), The Tower of London WM: And who was in who's group in the tag tourney: GROUP ONE: The Armed Forces, Destruction, Anarch-X, The Total Package GROUP TWO: The Players Club, The Army of The Damned, The Eire Alliance, The ThUGs GROUP THREE: The Owen Brothers, God's Gift to Women, The Prophets Of Rage, HardCore (Cut to Will Morris inside the arena) WM: OK, fans, you've seen the groupings, now you'll want to know what happened in all the matches. There were thirty nine on night one, lasting an incredible eight hours in total. On night two came the second thirty nine which completed the first round. On night three came the tournament finals, twenty matches in total. Every night was full of surprises, so here's the run down of the matches in the order they took place: (Numbers in brackets state the group.) Friday 24th January 1997: S1) 'Ice Cold' Steve Swanson vs Bishop Hell The first match of the evening certainly set the tone for the rest of the tournament. It contained everything a fan would want...near pins, cheating, great moves, and also the arrival of another TOWL superstar, Judge Death. Death stood by the ring for the entire match, but did not interfere. He just laughed when Swanson performed some high impact moves and by the actions taken by the audience, it appears that he smelled pretty vile. Bishop Hell was clearly put off, as was Swanson himself at times, by his presence though and nearly allowed Swanson to put on the Dry Ice, but managed to block it. It ended in a time limit draw. Swanson 1pt Hell 1pt 2) The Glitter Boy vs The Undead Evil Super Zombie Warrior The Warrior came out with one thing on his mind apparently: getting disqualified. He ran to the ring with DevilWoman and grabbed the TV belt off Glitter. He nailed him with it and then nailed the ref. Although the bell hadn't rung, he was still DQd. Glitter 3pts TUESZW 0pts 3) The American Patriot vs The Prizefighter The Patriot started this one very strongly. He outpowered his smaller opponent and was on top for most of the match. When it spilled outside however, The Prizefighter gained the advantage, but neither man noticed that the ref had counted to ten and then rang for the bell. Patriot 1pt Prizefighter 1pt 4) 'Heavy Weapons' Max Doom vs Jason Knight This was a real 'good vs evil' enocunter and for the most part it appeared that evil would overcome good. However, Knight managed to kick out of many of Doom's pinning attempts to the surprise of the crowd. It then looked more and more like youngster Knight was going to pull off a huge upset. The longer it went, the more the crowd fancied him as Doom lacked stamina. However, he hung on for the draw at the fifteen minute time limit mark. Doom 1pt Knight 1pt 5)'Perfect' Dani Jarvier vs 'The Irish Giant' Jacob O'Macky This was only O'Macky's second match in TOWL and he seemed unaware of just how good Jarvier is. Jarvier started off keeping the big man on the mat using his technical skills but O'Macky seemed to cotton on after about four minutes. He powered out of an abdominal stretch and then took control, getting a couple of two counts, but Perfect managed to finish him off in the eighth minute with a figure four leglock. Perfect 3pts O'Macky 0pts 6) Tiger vs 'Sudden Death' Shane Matthews Many of the fans were upset to hear this was the first match of this group, but the two small men did not disappoint. The World Champ looked good for the opening few minutes, but Tiger came back into things after that and his high-flying stuff really impressed the crowd. However, when a flying dropkick went wrong, Matthews was there to pick Tiger up, execute a hotshot on him and then nail him with a reverse neckbreaker, getting the three count. Matthews 3pts Tiger 0pts T1) The Armed Forces vs Destruction The Forces were in dominant form in this contest. They were obviously still on a high after becoming the tag champs, even though they still don't have the belts. NavCom began the match and gained the advantage for his team, then he sent DefCon in to do some serious Damage. He got in a powerbomb on Torpedo and as he did, Nav came off with his leg drop for the Atomic Bomb. They got the easy three. Forces 3pts Destruction 0pts 2) The Players Club vs The Army of The Damned Before the Army were introduced, some monks, apparently of Bishop Hell's monastery appeared and started chanting, overriding their theme music. The Army came out and walked past the monks, apparently somewhat confused by what they were doing. They got into the ring however, but every time Judge Death was on the apron, the monks stared at him and pointed their staffs at him. When they felt he was sufficiently put off, about six minutes in, they simply vanished from ringside. The Onslaught took care of things in the ring and eventually tagged The Judge in. Wyldcard came down to ringside when he got in and waited on the outside, cheering The Club on. The Club began getting the advantage and Wyldcard urged them to get the victory, however, when the ref was distracted by Reyna who had tagged in but the ref didn't see it, The Army started double teaming Danny Dynamite. Wyldcard got onto the ring apron and pulled Judge Death out of the ring as the ref turned around. He immediately rang for the bell: The Club had been disqualified due to outside interference. The Army taunted Wyldcard as they left in victory. Wyld apolgised to The Club and they appeared to accept it, but that decision was soon to be reviewed... The Army 3pts The Club 0pts 3) The Owen Brothers vs God's Gift to Women This was the second meeting of the two sides, and the outcome was the same this time as it was the last. The former tag champs were pretty convincing in this match and won it with the 'Hey Baby' where Thruster performed his MachoMove belly-to-belly and Holdah came off the top with a sunset flip. After the match, though, The Armed Forces came out and demanded their tag belts. The Gifts tried reasoning with them, but The Forces got pretty heated and attacked the Gifts with golf clubs then leaving. God's Gift 3pts The Owens 0pts S1) Curmudgeon vs 'The Pretty Boy' Dyllon Dean This match was heaped in controversy, mainly because it didn't actually happen. Dean phoned the Grand earlier that day and told them he wouldn't be appearing. They didn't believe it was actually Dean on the phone, thinking it was a hoax, but as it turned out it was Dean. TOWL Officials contacted him on his mobile and confirmed he would not be wrestling in the tournament. No reasons have been given as yet, but an announcement is to be ade during next week. Curmudgeon was given the points. Curmudgeon 3pts Dean 0pts 2) Wyldcard vs Dagger This match was one of the best of the first round. Dagger was accompanied by his mysterious new tag partner, the huge Dark Giant. He obviously put Wyldcard off, but to his credit did not interfere. Wyldcard looked like he could have got the win on a couple of occasions, but wasn't able to get the three. The match went to it's fifteen minute duration. Wyldcard 1pt Dagger 1pt 3) Wildthang vs Cro-Mag Wildthang, his tag partner Spartan, and stablemate Lycanth, were injured in an accident whilst they were training together recently and none of the three men were anywhere near match fitness. However, they all agreed to fulfil their contractual obligations and turned up for all four of their matches. Cro-Mag heard that Wildthang's shoulder had been seriously injured and worked over it for the entire match. After a couple of shoulderbreakers and short arm scissors, Cro wrapped it up with a full nelson after nine minutes. Cro-Mag 3pts Wildthang 0pts 4) The Agony Uncle vs The Genius The Uncle came into this match with a perfect loss-win record in TOWL and he continued this dismal run of form against The Genius, who did seem a little tired due to the large amount of matches he's been wrestling recently. The Genius used a number of foreign objects on The Uncle without the referee noticing and after nailing him with the bell, hit his Superbomb powerbomb from the top rope. The Genius 3pts The Agony Uncle 0pts 5) War vs Seth Blitzspear This match was power vs power quite simply. Many were surprised when Seth actually got the three count as well. War, the 389lb monster dominated for only brief spells while Seth kept on him and never gave up. He managed to pick War up and bodyslam him, apparently the first time that's ever happened to the big man, and after he nailed him with a top rope leg drop he got the pinfall win in eight minutes. Seth 3pts War 0pts 6) Death vs Jax Jax, like The Genius, came into this match on the back end of a defeat by the US Champ Perfect but that match seemed to have focused his mind and he dealt with the weird Death with relative ease. He used a few illegal holds on him which Detah countered, but when it went outside to the floor, Jax rammed his head into the ringpost and got back in before the count of ten, unlike his sorry foe. Jax 3pts Death 0pts 1) Bishop Hell vs Killspree The Terrible Again, Judge Death came down to ringside for this match and again he didn't interfere, like the monks earlier on. He just laughed when Killspree gained the advantage. The Bishop was in his second match of the evening and Killspree was in his first, but that didn't stop the large Austrian from overcoming the odds and pulling off what many considered an upset victory. Tolwyn Dane was the man that changed the course of the match after he nailed Killspree with his staff. The Bishop didn't make the pin as he apparently disagreed with the interference. He let the ref count Killspree out, which he did. Bishop Hell 3pts Killspree 0pts 2) The Undead Evil Super Zombie Warrior vs Spartan Spartan, who as we've already discussed, was only about 50% fit came to this match and this decreased to about 10% after this match was over. Again, before the bell, The Warrior nailed his opponent, this time with a chair and then DevilWoman fired a few fireballs at Spartan. The ref DQd The Warrior and TOWL Security had to escort both him and his valet away from the ring. He was banned from the tournament and fined $500000. He refused to pay the fine and was immediately banned from TOWL. Seth Blitzspear tried to make the Officials change their mind as he wanted to settle the score with him, but he was not able to. The ban stays. Spartan was deemed unfit to wrestle in the tourney again by a TOWL doctor. Rumor has it he'll be out for approximately another three weeks. Spartan 3pts TUESZW 0pts 3) The Prizefighter vs 'Painbringer' Billy Sexton This match had been eagerly anticipated by the crowd and they were not disappointed. In their first major matches in TOWL, both men were at their best and both dominated for approximately half the match. The crowd got behind The Prizefighter, as he hails from Vegas, but he couldn't put Sexton away. Sexton used his submission tactics, but the Prizefighter refused to give up. The match ended at the time limit, but the fans seemed shocked, thinking it hadn't been going more than five minutes they were so enthralled. The Prizefighter 1pt 'Painbringer' Billy Sexton 1pt 4) Jason Knight vs Lycanth Jason Knight, visibly exhausted after his first round match with Max Doom, was the underdog in this match according to resident bookmaker 'Big Duke' but he, like so many this evening, got an upset victory on paper, but it was mainly due to Lycanth, who realised that he could not win the tournament. He let Knight get the victory, which got a huge crowd pop, by refusing to get into the ring. The ref counted him out. This infuriated Max Doom who came out and nailed Lycanth from behind. A small brawl ensued, with a number of TOWL superstars, including The American Patriot and Jason Knight himself, managing to pull Doom away. Knight 3pts Lycanth 0pts 5) 'The Irish Giant' Jacob O'Macky vs The Extremist After his early defeat, O'Macky came out like a man possessed much to the enjoyment of the crowd. He finished the ever dwindling Extremist with his Human Torture Rack after just two minutes. O'Macky 3pts Extremist 0pts 6) 'Sudden Death' Shane Matthews vs The Tower of London Ninety nine percent of the crowd were fully behind the Englishman in this one, but Matthews shwed why he is World Champ, and has been since TOWL began back at the start of December last year, by demolishing The Tower. The Tower was completely out of his league and Matthews dominated the match, finishing it off with a top rope frankensteiner after six minutes. The Tower received a bad reception as he left. Matthews 3pts Tower 0pts T1) Anarch-X vs The Total Package This was the second match in TOWL for the Tower's fellow countrymen, Anarch-X. The former World Tag Champs seemed surprised at how good the Finsbury Park natives were. They outwrestled The Package for the most part and when the Package gained the advantage after about eight minutes, the brothers' sister, Tia, who is also their valet, was on hand with a VCR of all things that Anarchy had brought to the ring with him. Hellfire distracted the ref while Anarchy nailed Mr Arrogant with it and then he took care of The Perfect One whil Anarchy got the three count. Mr Destruction tried telling the ref, like the Godfather does, but the ref didn't listen to him. Anarch-X 3pts The Total Package 0pts 2) The Eire Alliance vs The ThUGs The Alliance, who were apparently very disappointed with their performances in TOWL so far, certainly made up for them in this match with newcomers The ThUGs. Without The Erotic One at ringside, Icer and DirtRat seemed lost and The Alliance finished them off following Sinn Fein's Texas Clover Leaf on Icer while Master Shamrock prevented the obese DirtRat from stopping the count. The Eire Alliance 3pts The ThUGs 0pts 3) The Prophets of Rage vs HardCore This was probably the best match of the opening night. The Prophets went in as slight favorites, with HardCore being outsiders due to them being the unknown quantity, having only wrestled once against Mikey Pea and Jim Howard previously. The Rages, who were defeated for the first time in TOWL on Monday by The Players Club, looked back to their brutal best and took control early on. Weird and Mayhem were very intense throughout the match though and kicked out of pinfall attempts with ease. They got a few twos of their own, but didn't have enough on the night to put either Derek or Shadoe away. The match ended in a time limit draw. Prophets 1pt HardCore 1pt S1) 'Ice Cold' Steve Swanson vs Curmudgeon Swanson started off very strong in this contest and kept Curmudgeon at bay for the opening five minutes. The match swung into Curmudgeon's favor though when The Glitter Boy made an unexpected arrival at ringside. He stood and watched, noticeably putting Swanson off, taunting him with his TV belt. When Curmudgeon sent Swanson for the ride, Glitter grabbed his leg and pulled him out while Curmudgeon complained to the ref. He pulled a piano string out and started choking Swanson with it. He then put him on the ring apron and Curmudgeon dragged him to his feet through the ropes and got him in position to suplex him in. However, Swanson held onto the top rope and landed on top of Curmudgeon, and as Glitter pulled on Curmudgeon's leg to get him out, Swanson got the three count as Curmudgeon was unable to kick out. He got out of the ring and chased Glitter back to the locker room area while Swanson celebrated in the ring. Swanson 3pts Curmudgeon 0pts 2) The Glitter Boy vs Wyldcard Glitter obviously managed to calm Curmudgeon down somehow, apparently by offering him a shot at his title, and came out for the next match apparently unharmed. Wyldcard however, came out to Devil's Night from the Crow theme track. Wyldcard seemed a little bemused by this, but The Erotic One told him it was her idea and would spur him on. He looked a little confused but eventually accepted it. When the match got underway, it was a very dirty battle with both men using foreign objects, and on the outside, The Erotic One attacked The Glitter Girl, telling her she lacked spine and shouldn't be Glitter's 'toy.' She used a number of wrestling manoeuvres, including a vicious gut wrench powerbomb which put the much smaller Glitter Girl out. The Glitter Boy was obviously distracted and when The Erotic One took the belt and stood on the ring apron with it, he got his weighted money clip out of his boot and nailed Wyld with it just as The Erotic One hit him. Both men went down simultaneously and the ref had no option but to call for the double disqualification. Glitter 1pt Wyldcard 1pt 3) The American Patriot vs Wildthang After his defeat at the hands of Cro-Mag, Wildthang was never likely to win this match with the 285lb giant of a man. The Patriot went easy on Wildthang though and it was a good tempered match, with both men shaking hands at the beginning and end. Nine minutes in, The Patriot nailed him with a piledriver and Wildthang loked like he could have kicked out, but didn't really want to carry on. Patriot got the three. Patriot 3pts Wildthang 0pts 4) 'Heavy Weapons' Max Doom vs The Agony Uncle After his earlier appearances, Doom came out and before the Uncle could even get into the ring, he was pounding on him. The Uncle looked beweildered and was defeated after just three minutes following Dooom's patented Doomsday Device powerslam finisher. Doom 3pts Uncle 0pts 5) 'Perfect' Dani Jarvier vs War The US Champ kept up his 100% record in this tournament, defeating War after just five minutes with his PerfectEnd DDT finisher. War started the match well, attacking Jarvier before the bell, but Perfect lived up to his name, nailing a couple of perfect dropkicks and a near perfect sleeperhold which nearly put the big man out. Jarvier 3pts War 0pts 6) Tiger vs Death Tiger went into, and came out of this match funnily enough, with a near two hundred pound weight disadvantage and with Tiger only weighing 200 himself, Death is virtually twice his weight. Tiger put up a great fight however and made up for his earlier loss to World Champ Shane Matthews with a big upset victory. He didn't let Death near him and kept using his high-flying moves to keep him on the mat. Some of the high points included this inverted moonsault from the top and this flying dropkick which nearly knocked Death's head off. A victory roll set Tiger up perfectly for his Tiger Flash finisher whereby he nailed Death with a conventional Frankensteiner after twelve minutes. The win was very popular amongst the crowd. Tiger 3pts Death 0pts T1) Destruction vs Anarch-X Destruction, after a promising start in TOWL, looked in bad form in this match. The newcomers from England notched up another victory in the tournament, which at this stage looked as if it would secure them a place in the finals, but with a final match against newly crowned World Tag Champs The Armed Forces, no-one wanted to predict anything. Nuke and Torpedo didn't know what hit them when the punks got in and hit them with everything they had. With their sister Tia egging them on from the outside, Anarch-X finished their opponents off with The Finsbury Crackdown in six minutes following some blatant disregard for the rules. Anarchy nailed Torpedo with a hubcap which he'd brought to the ring with him and then nailed Nuke with it who was on the apron. He threw it back to Tia and then put Torpedo in a backbreaker. Hellfire came off the top rope and dropped an elbow on his throat and the ref slowly counted to three. Anarch-X 3pts Destruction 0pts 2) The Army of The Damned vs The Eire Alliance Both teams went into this match with a victory 'under their belts' from their first matches and both teams were fancied by the fans, about half and half. As The Army came out though, the monks were there again with their staffs and again their chanting was much louder than the PA system. The Alliance came out looking pretty confident and when Wyldcard appeared after ten minutes of furious action, they gained the advantage. Before this, the 644lb newcomers had been on top, outpowering their much smaller opponents. When Judge Death was tagged in after The Onslaught had taken a hammering at the hands of the Martial Art expert Master Shamrock, Bishop Hell appeared at ringside out of nowhere. He stood with Wyldcard and had a staredown with Judge Death. He then moved a thumbs across his throat. Shamrock rolled him up from behind but only got a two. After this, the match lost most of it's momentum and ended at the fifteen minute time limit. The Army 1pt The Eire Alliance 1pt 3) God's Gift to Women vs The Prophets of Rage The former tag champs, still reeling from the brutal attack at the hands of The Armed Forces, came out to a moderate heel pop and the Rages, along with Medusa and Pizzazz got a massive heel pop. It was obvious who the fans favored, but this did not do the Gifts much good as the brothers took control of the match early on. They seemed to focus more on Dick Thruster, the veteran from The Costa Del Sol and worked on him for most of the match. Derek used his familiar high impact power moves, such as a chokeslam and an airplane spin, both getting two counts and a save from Holdah. It was Holdah that lost the match for the Gifts though. He was the one who was pinned by Derek's Hammer of God face claw slam. Thruster was lying on the mat groaning as Derek made the pin so Shadoe didn't even have to stop him making the save. The Prophets nailed the Gifts after the match, allowing the 'ladies' to get involved. The Players Club came to the ring though and defused matters. Prophets 3pts Gifts 0pts S1) 'The Pretty Boy' Dyllon Dean vs Killspree The Terrible Match awarded to Killspree due to Dean's absence. Killspree 3pts Dean 0pts 2) Dagger vs Spartan Due to the attack on him by The Undead Evil Super Zombie Warrior, Spartan was unable to compete in this contest. Dagger 3pts Spartan 0pts 3) Cro-Mag vs 'Painbringer' Billy Sexton This was a rough, tough match between two of the federation's submission orientated wrestlers. There weren't too many power or 'pretty' moves, mainly armbars, leglocks and bearhugs. Sexton came up trumps though and defeated Cro-Mag following a shoulderbreaker and then an armbar submission move. Cro-Mag howled in pain and submitted straight away. Cro-Mag showed his appreciation of Sexton by nodding his head when he got out of the ring. The crowd seemed to appreciate this from the Alaska native. Sexton 3pts Cro-Mag 0pts 4) The Genius vs Lycanth Lycanth, who was already injured and suffered more at the hands of Max Doom in an outside the ring attack, came out to a good crowd pop. He gave his all in the ring, but The Genius was too smart for him. He notched up his second win of the night after he used The Perfect One's Singapore Cane which Mr Destruction brought to ringside. Lycanth was knocked out long enough for The Genius to get the three. He then got out of the ring with great haste. The Genius 3pts Lycanth 0pts 5) Seth Blitzspear vs the Extremist In the penultimate match of the evening, Seth Blitzspear met The Extremist. For any Extremist fans out there, he did not even put up a fight and Blitzspear completely dominated. He used countless bodyslams and clotheslines and finished the match after eight mintues following his Flying Time Backdrop. The Extremist was counted out after being backdropped into the crowd and Seth got a popular win. Seth 3pts The Extremist 0pts 6) Jax vs The Tower of London Jax, accompanied to the ring by his Dark Monuments stablemates Dagger and the Dark Giant, was in good form in this match in the early hours of Saturday. The Tower looked half asleep and many fans shouted abuse at him. Jax toyed with him and could have finished it as early as two minutes in when he hit his Renegade Strike brainbuster superplex, but Dagger told him to carry on the punishment, so he did. He waited until Charlie said there was one minute left to cover him for the three count. He used every move in his repertoire to no offense by The Tower...suplexes, piledrivers, and neckbreakers. He used the Renegade Strike for the second time to win it. The crowd were relieved when he did. Many of them had left, or fallen asleep in their seats. Jax 3pts The Tower 0pts This concluded Friday's first round matches and most of the superstars went straight to their rooms, but a couple of them, namely The Prizefighter who apparently broke the bank at The Stardust and 'Perfect' Dani Jarvier who was thrown out of Circus Circus, The Mirage and Treasure Island during the night, for winning too much. Shane Matthews went to a seedy pool hall by all accounts and was hustling all the natives out of their hard earned cash. When they refused to pay up, he beat them all up and took the money straight out of their wallets. He and Terror apparently went back to their rooms at 7am, with Matthews not being alone but with an unidentified blonde, and got up at 5pm, apparently completely refreshed for the second night's action, which started at 7pm rather than 8 due to the duration of the previous evening. Saturday 25th January 1997: S1) Bishop Hell vs Curmudgeon The Bishop was involved in the first match of the evening for the second time in this encounter with fellow newcomer Curmudgeon. The Bishop was very focused for this match, knowing that victory would assure him of qualification through to the finals as he would be awarded the three points for his match with Dyllon Dean, who did not turn up on this evening either. The Bishop again relied on Tolwyn Dane to help him but Judge Death, who again came to ringside, attempted to stop Dane from cheating. Dane instead nailed Death with the staff and Death slumped to the concrete floor. The Onslaught came out to help his fallen partner, but then from nowhere, Wyldcard appeared and started pounding on The Onslaught. Bishop Hell got out of the ring to help The Onslaught leaving Curmudgeon in there on his own. Not one to be left out, he jumped out and joined in the fun, targetting Bishop Hell. However, Tolwyn Dane managed to nail him with his staff as well and while Wyldcard and The Onslaught battled each other, Hell rolled back into the ring and earned the countout victory. Wyldcard then left the arena, closely followed by Dane and Hell. Bishop Hell 3pts Curmudgeon 0pts 2) The Undead Evil Super Zombie Warrior vs Wyldcard With the Warrior's expulsion from TOWL, Wyldcard didn't even need to come out to gain himself three valuable points. Wyldcard 3pts TUESZW 0pts 3) The Prizefighter vs Wildthang Wildthang was still not at one hundred percent fitness whereas The Prizefighter, who had been up all night gambling, seemed on some kind of high- apparently due to the lack of sleep and not any drugs as was first suspected. Maverick was at ringside as ever shouting throughout the match which seemed to irritate the fans, but that didn't stop him one bit. In the ring however, The Prizefighter used his boxing footwork to keep out of Wildthang's way and stunned him early on with European Uppercuts. After ten minutes, he nailed him with a bulldog which nearly put him out, but stood him up and nailed him with his KO spinning mule kick. The crowd seemed to appreciate his tactics as he did not break the rules once. Prizefighter 3pts Wildthang 0pts 4) Jason Knight vs The Agony Uncle Jason Knight, who had only had to wrestle once the previous night came into this match as the favorite for the first time and he proved the bookies right. The Agony Uncle was chasing shadows throughout the match and Knight used many of his high flying moves to keep the big Tulsa native at bay. Knight finished the match with his Knightmare Drop brainbuster superplex which surprised the crowd as the Uncle is such a big man. Knight quickly hooked the legs and got the three to the delight of the fans. Knight 3pts The Uncle 0pts 5) 'The Irish Giant' Jacob O'Macky vs War O'Macky continued his comeback from his opening defeat at the hands of Perfect by demolishing War in under four minutes. Obviously War was too big for him to throw around the ring but he did manage to nail him with a backbreaker and also a big bearhug which took the wind out of him. He tried getting him on his shoulders for the horizontal backbreaker finisher, but he instead opted for a DDT followed by a legdrop to get the pinfall. The crowd were extremely pleased at the big man's performance, but War was livid backstage and rumors are the four Riders may not be around for much longer. O'Macky 3pts War 0pts 6) 'Sudden Death' Shane Matthews vs Death Matthews came into this match realising that a victory would virtually guarantee him qualification through to the finals with only Jax left to face. He did all he could though to try and lose it for himself but the ref let his blatant cheating go amiss. He choked the big man and when on the outside banged his head against the ringpost and whipped him into the guardrails but the ref let the match continue. When Matthews got back in and started complaining to the ref, Johnny Terror was on hand outside to nail him with his blackjack. Death got back in at the count of nine, but Matthews simply rolled him up, placed his own feet on the top rope and got the three count. Matthews 3pts Death 0pts T1) The Armed Forces vs The Total Package The new champs met the former champs in a match that the Forces had been looking forward to for a while now. They expected to meet them for the titles on Monday, but God's Gift to Women defeated the Package unexpectedly and The Forces faced them instead. The Package had also been wanting a shot at their six man belts with The Genius as their partner, but those belts have since been taken out of circulation by TOWL HQ. The match was more one-sided than many expected though. The Package, who had already lost to newcomers Anarch-X were clearly not on top form as they slumped to their second successive defeat in this tournament putting pay to any aspirations of winning it. The Forces came out as strong as ever, with NavCom leading the way and then letting DefCon do the real damage. Arrogant and The Perfect One tried cheating when they got the chance, but Aaron The Caddy was up to their tricks and early on in the match took the Singapore Cane off Mr Destruction, then nailed him with his putter. In the end, NavCom took Aaron's advice and used the three wood to take care of The Perfect One while the ref tried to separate DefCon and Mr Arrogant. NavCom rolled him up and got the three as Def threw Arrogant over the top. Forces 3pts Package 0pts 2) The Players Club vs the ThUGs Both DirtRat and Icer, and Danny Dynamite and Michael Reyna came into this match with one defeat to their credit. The Club dug really deep for something extra knowing that two wins from their remaining two matches was their only hope of qualifying for the next round. Wyldcard, a member of The Erotic One's stable like The ThUGs cost them their first match, but only now did they realise that perhaps he lost them the match on purpose after earlier forgiving him. The Club wrestled at their best though and nothing The ThUGs could throw at them worked. They were quicker and technically more sound and Dynamite finished Icer off with his Regulator superkick after about eight minutes. Reyna kept DirtRat at bay and the ref counted to three. The Club 3pts The ThUGs 0pts 3) The Owen Brothers vs HardCore The Owens, who are rapidly becoming known by their fellow wrestlers as The Owen Jobbers, and The Jobben Brothers, both names incidentally made up by my broadcasting colleague 'Hotshot' Hal Rogers, were in dismal form in this one. Weird and Mayhem were in complete control throughout and the dangerous newcomers finished The Brothers off in less than four minutes following their Painful finisher whereby Mayhem jack-knife powerbombed Jim Owen while Weird came off the top with a legdrop. Jerry Owen was clearly intimidated by the aptly named Weird and didn't get in to make the save. He just put his hands in the air while Mayhem covered Jim. HardCore 3pts The Owen Brothers 0pts S1) 'Ice Cold' Steve Swanson vs 'The Pretty Boy' Dyllon Dean Swanson was really annoyed when he heard Dean hadn't turned up for this match as he wanted to face the former TV Champ to judge his standing here in TOWL. He put out a challenge to Dean for when he returns, but Dean as yet has not replied. Many fans are hoping he comes in and gives Swanson the wrestling lesson of a lifetime, as he has beaten everyone he's faced so far with the exception of Bishop Hell, with relative ease. Swanson 3pts Dean 0pts 2) The Glitter Boy vs Dagger Many people expected great things from this bout, especially considering Dagger's appearance with the Dark Giant at Glitter's match on Thursday night. Many people thought Dagger would beat the TV Champ, but the champ had other ideas. For the first few minutes only, the match was clean, but when Dagger was thrown his dagger by Skull, and started threatening Glitter with it, the ref called for the bell. Glitter pulled his money clip out of his boot, but this didn't put Dagger off. The Dark Giant got in and Glitter soon got out and carried The Glitter Girl, who was having trouble walking thanks to the attack on her by The Erotic One on Friday night, back to the locker room with him. The Glitter Boy 3pts Dagger 0pts 3) The American Patriot vs Cro-Mag This match was eagerly anticipated as it matched two wrestlers with completely different styles together. Neither man had excelled in either of his previous matches, but in this one, both men wrestled to the best of their ability. The Patriot knew he had to win if he were to fulfil his dream of winning this tournament, and Cro-Mag knew a win was vital as well. However, neither man was able to put the other away and the match reached it's fifteen minute duration after a number of two counts but no threes unfortunately. Patriot 1pt Cro-Mag 1pt 4) 'Heavy Weapons' Max Doom vs The Genius The Genius, with two wins in the tournament already fancied his chances against the big newcomer Max Doom. Doom, on the other hand knew he had to win to keep up with Jason Knight who had forged a three point gap between the two of them. The match started at a very high speed with Doom gaining the advantage and stunning The Genius with his power moves. However, the Genius was able to get out of the ring and pulled Doom out with him. He gained the advantage on the outside, nearly breaking his arm on the guardrail, but Doom came back and blocked the Genius' attempt to ram his head into the ringpost and rammed The Geniu's head five times. He realised the ref was up to nine and tried to get back into the ring, but The Genius managed to grab his leg and prevented him getting in and securing the win. The ref counted both men out much to the annoyance of Doom who went ballistic on The Genius and Mr Destruction. He bodyslammed Destruction on the outside and then rolled the Genius into the ring. He performed his Doomsday Device three times and then left the arena, shouting obscenities at the fans. He obviously didn't think about how his actions would affect his chances in the tournament as Knight had only The Genius to wrestle and victory would guarantee him qualification. Doom 1pt The Genius 1pt 5) 'Perfect' Dani Jarvier vs Seth Blitzspear This match was one the fans really wanted to see. Perfect had openly asked Seth for a match for his own US Title and Seth accepted the challenge. However, due to this tournament and contractual obligations, neither man has had the time to fit the match in. Both men were completely focused and the match was a real classic. Perfect started off using his large array of techincal moves, but Seth got some extra power out of nowhere apparently and went wild in the rin while the fans went wild in the crowd. He threw Perfect around the ring and kept him on the mat, not due to his technical expertise, but with clotheslines and bodyslams, as well as piledrivers a powerbomb and a couple of chokeslams. It looked like the match was over though when Perfect nailed his PerfectEnd DDT but the ref only got to two when the bell rung for the time limit. Perfect thought he'd won it and paraded in the ring, but the ref stopped him and said he hadn't. He argued with the ref and kicked Seth a couple of times, complaining he'd obviously got the win and the bell should not be able to save him. It was to no avail and the result of a draw stood. Perfect 1pt Seth 1pt 6) Tiger vs Jax Tiger came into this match six points behind Shane Matthews and knew that he could not go through due to his earlier loss to Matthews so even if the two were level on points at the end, Matthews would qualify. This didn't stop him though and he outwrestled Jax completely. Jax, who had lost to Perfect earlier in the week in a match for the US Title seemed more intense on injuring Tiger in this match and he even had Dagger and the Dark Giant to help him out. However, Tiger pulled off an upset victory after nailing Jax with his Tiger Flash frankensteiner and then Dagger and the Dark Giant came in to make the illegal save. The ref DQd Jax and awarded the match to Tiger, who was now being triple teamed. Help was on hand though in the unlikely form of 'Ice Cold' Steve Swanson who came in and clotheslined Dagger and Jax over the top rope. He then had the Giant to cope with but after realising he couldn't floor him, got out and made his way back to the locker room. Tiger slid out of the ring and Seth Blitzspear, who mistook Dagger for an ally earlier on in the week, stormed to the ring and carried him back to the locker rooms. Tiger 3pts Jax 0pts 1) Curmudgeon vs Killspree The Terrible Killspree, who had lost to Bishop Hell and defeated Dyllon Dean by default came into this match pretty fresh while Curmudgeon had already wrestled once this evening and had taken a blow to the head at the hands of Bishop Hell. Killspree, who outweighs Curmudgeon by approximately 130lbs came out and took advantage of the plucky youngster's situation. He immediately locked on his Peersecution reinforced armbar scissorhold and Curmudgeon submitted immediately. However, he got to his feet and nailed Killspree with some vicious right hands and clotheslines and even nailed him with a reverse neckbreaker. Killspree was a little taken aback by this barrage and tried to stage a comeback, but Curmudgeon nailed a croth bulldog and left the ring to a huge heel pop. Killspree 3pts Curmudgeon 0pts 2) Wyldcard vs Spartan Wyldcard was handed the match due to Spartan's injury which he suffered at the hands of The Undead Evil Super Zombie Warrior. Wyldcard 3pts Spartan 0pts 3) Wildthang vs 'Painbringer' Billy Sexton Wildthang, whose fitness was rapidly deteriorating came into this match with TOWL's newest 'danger man', as the fans refer to him, Billy Sexton with an impossible task. Sexton told him not to worry as he'd make it quick and he wouldn't feel a thing. One out of two wasn't bad. He immediately locked on the armbar submission, but virtually wrenched Wildthang's arm out of it's socket. To his credit he left the moment 'thang submitted, but was still greeted with a heel pop unlike the Prizefighter earlier. Wildthang then pulled out of the tourney and returned home with Spartan and Pebbles. Sexton 3pts Wildthang 0pts 4) The Agony Uncle vs Lycanth Lycanth was looking to improve on his earlier performances and did so in style. He obliterated the Uncle and could have won the match as early as the second minute. He carried on though until the ref deemed the Uncle unfit to continue after using a variety of power moves and submission holds. He finished it with his Death Claw after thirteen minutes. Lycanth 3pts Uncle 0pts 5) War vs The Extremist War came into this match with one intention: to injure the Extremist, and he succeeded to a greater extent than even he expected apparently. He worked over the Extremist from the opening bell to the end when he was disqualified for using a whole host of foreign objects ranging from a chair to the timekeeper's bell, with a table and also the ringsteps in between. War had to be restrained from further damage by Death and his Envoys and rumors are rife that Extremist's career has been cut short by this attack. Extremist 3pts War 0pts 6) Death vs The Tower of London Death came into this match with the same intentions as War, but he didn't cheat once, until War came along and interfered on his behalf that is. The Tower was dominated for three minutes before War, who accompanied Death out decided to get in the ring and help Death double team the jovial Englishman. Again, he used a table and drew blood with the bell, apparently scarring the Tower's face for life. The doctor has stated that the injuries are so bad that the Tower will have to wear a mask for the rest of his career, or quit wrestling. The smart money's on him quitting. The TOL 3pts Death 0pts T1) Destruction vs The Total Package Former tag champs The Total Package were out to make up for their earlier losses in the tourney and did just that against the team of Destruction who, after a fine start to their TOWL careers are now just making up the numbers. The Package didn't need the Singapore Cane which they found outside their locker room just a few minutes earlier after Aaron the Caddy stole it. They just wrestled Nuke and Torpedo into the canvas and finished it after Mr Arrogant's 'Flyin Piledriver' on Torpedo. The Perfect One took care of Nuke while Arrogant got the vital three. The Package 3pts Destruction 0pts 2) The Army of the Damned vs The ThUGs The ThUGs, who had lost both of their opening matches came into this one desperate for victory just for their pride. It was not to be though, as The Army of The Damned were just too strong for them. Wyldcard came down to the ring again and cheered on his stablemates. It was no good though, as The Onslaught and Judge Death just had too much 'in the tank'. The Army got the pin on Icer after nine minutes following a powerbomb. Wyld got into the ring and complained to the ref about the speed of the count but got nailed from behind by The Onslaught. Judge Death joined in and made it two on one but The ThUGs soon came to Wyld's aid as did Bishop Hell. A four on two ensued with the four gaining the slight advantage, but The Army managed to get out of the ring and went back swiftly to the locker rooms with Lady Apocalypse. The win virtually guaranteed The Army a place in the finals as they finished with seven points out of nine. The Army 3pts The ThUGs 0pts 3) God's Gift to Women vs HardCore The Gifts, having lost one and won one during this tourney came in knowing they needed a win to stand a chance of qualifying. HardCore had other ideas though and made the Gifts look ordinary and proved that their brief title win was something of a fluke. Weird and Mayhem got on with the job in hand and the seven footer (Mayhem) dominated both Holdah and Thruster with his power moves. Thruster and Holdah, as a sign of their frustration, brought their conventional trouser belts in and started whipping HardCore with them. The ref immediately asked for the bell. The Gifts continued dishing out the punishment, but the two big guys got out eventually, slightly bruised with their kidney areas bleeding slightly. HardCore 3pts God's Gift 0pts S1) Bishop Hell vs 'The Pretty Boy' Dyllon Dean Hell was handed the match due to Dean's no-show. Hell 3pts Dean 0pts 2) The Undead Evil Super Zombie Warrior vs Dagger Due to the Warrior's expulsion from TOWL, Dagger was handed the match. Dagger 3pts TUESZW 0pts 3) The Prizefighter vs Cro-Mag Afetr a disappointing tournament by his standards, Cro-Mag came into this match desperate for a win for his pride. The Prizefighter was not in that kind of mood though and carried on his fine form by defeating Cro-Mag after eleven minutes following a right hook. In my mind, the pin should not have counted as boxing punches are not legal in this sport, but the ref counted to three none the less and the Prizefighter won after knocking Cro out like a light. Cro-Mag had had the advantage all the way up to there, but that one punch more than made up for that on the Prizefighter's part. He had to hope that The American Patriot would beat Billy Sexton and he would win the coin toss which would ensue as they drew when they wrestled each other. He finished the first round unbeaten on eight points. Cro finished with a lowly four. Prizefighter 3pts Cro-Mag 0pts 4) Jason Knight vs The Genius Knight continued his impressive form and defeated the dazed Genius in less than two minutes. Max Doom had indeed made a huge mistake by attacking the swaggering Fremont native as Knight proceeded in the tourney with his ten points. the most Doom could get now was eight. Knight finished the Genius off with his Knightmare Drop. Mr Destruction could only watch in awe. Knight 3pts Genius 0pts 5) 'The Irish Giant' Jacob O'Macky vs Seth Blitzspear In this battle of allies, neither man was too interested in inflicting _too_ much damage on the other. They both had their share of pinning predicaments but neither had the killer instinct to finish their opponent off. The high point was when O'Macky reversed a bearhug and turned it into a belly-to-belly suplex. This only got a two count though. Both men shook hands before and after the match. O'Macky 1pt Seth 1pt 6) 'Sudden Death' Shane Matthews vs Jax Jax came into this match knowing that not even a win was good enough to get him through. Matthews came in knowing that if he won he'd be the only wrestler in the whole tournament to qualify with a one hundred percent record. Jax came out with only his manager Major Morris and Matthews as ever was accompanied by Johnny Terror. Both men bent the rules somewhat but Matthews proved that whatever Jarvier could do he could do better and finished Jax off with a small package after six minutes of furious action. Jax did not seem interested in attacking Matthews after the bell and is rumored to have said off the record that he wasn't too upset that Matthews won as he didn't want Tiger going through even though he wouldn't have done anyway. Matthews 3pts Jax 0pts T1) The Armed Forces vs Anarch-X This match was the best tag match of the evening up to here without doubt. Both teams had won their previous two encounters and both wanted to go through with maximum points obviously. However, neither team was to do so as the Englishmen kept the tag champs at bay for the full fifteen minutes. The match contained all the right ingredients to be remembered in the fans' memories...high flying and technical all coming from NavCom, with the braling power moves coming from his partner DefCon and their opponents Anarchy and Hellfire. Both teams had guaranteed three counts on their foes, but the vital saves were made. There was a fair amount of rulebreaking, but the ref kept it under control and there was no real problem after the match. Both teams just left with their managers. Forces 1pt Anarch-X 1pt 2) The Players Club vs The Eire Alliance Having said the former was the best tag match of the evening up to that point, this match was by far the best match of the night. Both teams knew they had to win to stand a chance of qualification knowing that the Army of The Damned were on seven points. The Alliance could match them if they won and The Club could only get to six. The match went to it's time limit as well though. The match had the fans on the edges of their seats for the final few minutes with a pinning situation occurring every few seconds, with small packages, roll ups and backslides. Neither team got in any moves that could have injured their opponents however. Neither team was happy when the bell rang and both asked for the match to be extended but the ref stood firm. The Club were definitely eliminated which was the major shock of the tournament and the Alliance had to wait at most fifteen minutes to see if they were through or not in second place with five points. It all lay on the shoulders of the Owen Brothers.... The Club 1pt The Alliance 1pt 3) The Prophets of Rage vs The Owen Brothers There was never any doubting the winners of this match after Pizzazz and Medusa managed to get on top of the Owens before they even got to the ring. They attacked them with their heels and chairs and rolled them into the ring for the Prophets to double team. Shadoe didn't even bother getting in; he just let Derek take care of the two hopeless brothers. One Hammer of God after another on both men finished the match. Derek pinned them both with one hand and got a very unpopular victory. The Propehts 3pts The Owens 0pts S1) 'Ice Cold' Steve Swanson vs Killspree The Terrible Swanson came into this one knowing he needed a victory to stand any chance of getting through to the finals. However Killspree was not in the mood to let him do that. The three hundred and eighty pounder kept Swanson at bay and managed to steer clear of the Dry Ice which he dreaded most. Killspree kept at Swanson and dominated towards the end. Swanson was clearly tiring after such a tough schedule and was lucky in the end to hold on for the one point at the time limit. Both men were out of the competition and Bishop Hell was guaranteed a place in the finals. Swanson 1pt Killspree 1pt 2) The Glitter Boy vs Spartan Glitter progressed to the finals also with this default win over Spartan who was by now at home. Glitter 3pts Spartan 0pts 3) The American Patriot vs 'Painbringer' Billy Sexton Sexton knew that a victory would leapfrog him over The Prizefighter and into the finals, whereas The Patriot knew that he had to win in order to go into the coin-toss with The Prizefighter to see if he would go through. It was Sexton though who got his wish, thanks in part to Max Doom, who came down to the ring and clearly distracted The Patriot. Sexton realised his only chance, at the thirteenth minute mark, would be to throw Patriot to the outside and hope Doom would do him the favor while he distracted the ref. Fortunately for him, Doom was more than happy to comply with his wishes and drilled the Patriot into the guardrails and then banged his head on the ring apron. Sexton pulled him in by the hair and covered, getting the three while Doom held The Patriot's legs. Sexton progressed and then left the ring area while Doom and Patriot brawled in the ring, then it spilled out and they continued all the way back to the locker rooms. It was broken up by other wrestlers. Sexton 3pts Patriot 0pts 4) 'Heavy Weapons' Max Doom vs Lycanth Lycanth, on the back of a victory over The Agony Uncle in thirteen gruelling minutes, came into this one completely worn out. Doom, however, was in the mood for a real fight and took it straight to Lycanth. Doom was unable to powerslam the big seven footer but managed to finish him off after just five minutes following a big splash from the second turnbuckle. Doom 3pts Lycanth 0pts 5) 'Perfect' Dani Jarvier vs The Extremist Jarvier made a mockery of this match and defeated The Extremist with his PerfectClutch after only six minutes. The Extremist started well but after a couple of minutes, Perfect outwitted his opponent and used some of his many technical manoeuvres to keep him on the mat including a figure four leglock and also a surfboard which he has practiced since the last time he tried it back on the first of December last year. The Clutch came as no surprise and The Extremist submitted immediately. Perfect 3pts The Extremist 0pts 6) Tiger vs The Tower of London In the final match of the first round,Tiger, who had already been eliminated due to Shane Matthews' perfect record, came out looking rather jaded, but still managed to defeat the big Tower in five minutes flat. The two men shook hands before and after but apparently Madame Two-Swords was not happy with her man's performance.
