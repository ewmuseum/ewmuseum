[TOWL] Round Robin Round One

May 11, 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Cut to Will Morris standing outside The Mirage hotel in Las Vegas.) WM: Welcome to Las Vegas, Nevada, fans, for this, the second ever TOWL Round Robin tournament. In the next two hours, you'll get to see all the main action from the singles and tag matches which took place over the last three nights. Twenty four singles wrestlers, twenty four tag wrestlers. Who won the respective competitions? You'll find out tonight, in TOWL Pro Extra! (Cue the opening credits. Cut to Will Morris and Hal Rogers in the studio.) WM: Welcome to TPE fans. Alongside me this time, to describe the action for you, is 'Hotshot' Hal Rogers. Welcome, Hal. HR: Thanks alot Morris and what a show we've got for you this week, fans. The action was hot, hot, hot and there were surprises galore. Including the mystery tag team and the mystery tag wrestlers who weren't so mysterious at all... WM: Noooo. They weren't really were they...? Let's show you the official line-ups: Singles: GROUP ONE: Billy Sexton The Shark Wyldcard Dead Cold GROUP TWO: Shane Matthews The Tower of London The Patriot Jax GROUP THREE: The Glitter Boy Stud Cro-Mag Jacob O'Macky GROUP FOUR: Taylor Paine Flame Nik Nyce Tiger GROUP FIVE: Sinister Seth Blitzspear Bishop Hell Caine GROUP SIX: Steve Swanson The Vindicator The Mist After Shock Tag: GROUP ONE: The Prophets of Rage The ThunderDogs Dark Crimson The W & W Express GROUP TWO: Anarch-X The Bouncers Team Justice The Whiskey Rebellion GROUP THREE: The Armed Forces The ThUGs The Army of the Damned HardCore WM: Okay, fans, let's hear from the newcoming tag team, the ThunderDogs: ["The Drifter" Dustin Jo James & "Original CowBoy" Stan Badrock stand on the crest of a hill. The sun is setting, bathing the area in a sultry red light...] Badrock: There it is. [ Badrock points to a building in the far distance. Drifter looks for a while before responding] Drifter: There`s what? Badrock: TOWL HQ. Our next stop on the way to conquering the tag-team scene throughout the world. [Drifter pauses for a while then speaks] Drifter: Hmmmm. You know what Stan, I think this could be a bit too easy for us here. Badrock: I know - just look at the teams - Armed Forces, Prophets of Rage, Dark Crimson, Players Club, Army of the Damned, Anarch-X...none of them have even done enough to earn to be in the same ring as us, yet still they continue with their misconceptions that they are halfway decent. I guess we`ll just have to beat it into them. ThunderDog style. [Badrock puffs on his cigar while Drifter thinks about it] Drifter: You know...I might just enjoy that. [Drifter smiles. Badrock releases a smoke-ring and looks at his partner] Badrock: Ready to break some necks? Drifter: You bet. [Fade as the two men head towards the building, Badrock with a horse-saddle in one hand, while Drifter strides with his shotgun.] WM: Well, fans, instead of going to Hal, who'd no doubt tell you who won. (Hal can be heard saying 'Hey' in the background, then Will says 'ouch, stop it.') Don't forget, this time each match has a fifteen minute time limit. Scoring is three points for a win, one for a draw and zero for a loss. Let's go to the summaries. Group 1 Singles (G1s) Billy Sexton vs Wyldcard Wyldcard is probably the most under-rated wrestler in TOWL right now, and many wondered, after his showing tonight, why he hadn't had a shot at the World Champion before now. Obviously the belt wasn't on the line in this match, and even if it had been, with the fifteen minute time limit, Sexton wouldn't have lost it. Had the match been a little longer, who knows? The match did indeed go to the full fifteen minute limit and the crowd saw that as two points lost by Wyld rather than one won. Sexton seemed annoyed with picking up only one point as he was looking for maximum points, a feat previously accomplished by only Shane Matthews. Sexton 1pt Wyldcard 1pt G2s Shane Matthews vs The Patriot Another well anticipated match pitted former World Champ against his greatest enemy at the start of his reign. Matthews has been granted a World Title shot by Billy Sexton and on this showing, he's worth every penny. The two had never had a conclusive winner in their previous matches, but Matthews showed why he's always one of the men to watch as he defeated the Patriot by pinfall in seven and a half minutes following a flying headscissors. Matthews 3pts Patriot 0pts G3s The Glitter Boy vs Cro-Mag The TV Champion was accompanied by Taylor Paine and the Glitter Girl, but they didn't help him get the win in this match. Cro-Mag, who last time complained about the heat of Las Vegas was back to his awesome best, and even had the crowd rallying behind him, especially when Paine actually interfered when he was thrown to the outside. The crowd managed to alert the ref to the situation and when Cro-Mag got back in, it looked like he was going to win the match, but unfortunately for him, the time limit expired and Glitter was saved. Cro-Mag left the ring to a massive pop. Glitter 1pt Cro 1pt G4s Taylor Paine vs Nik Nyce More big news about the events of the evening, TOWL HQ were so disgusted by Nyce's attitude of late and refusing to accept their more than generous offer regarding the interfed event planned for next month that they refused to renew his initial eight week contract. He was obliged to carry on in this tournament, and to see how far he could get in the US Tourney, but after that he was to be thrown out. His heart was not in this match, and Taylor proved to be worth his number four seeding and walked straight through him in six minutes. His dinner habits appal me quite frankly, but his wrestling style is, to be honest, nothing short of brilliant. The pinfall came following a devastating inverted Tombstone piledriver, the DOA. Paine 3pts Nyce 0pts G5s Sinister vs Bishop Hell The big shock of the night was the good Bishop not turning up for his matches during the tournament. The reason? Pure and simple he had obligations in his homeland of Austria and after touring for the past month or so on and off, he decided to stay over there realising how much he missed the place. An awesome newcomer called 'Dead Air' Brian Lochnar came down to ringside offering to take Hell's place, but TOWL Officials refused due to contractual complications. Lochnar wasn't pleased though. He stepped into the ring and had a long staredown with Sinister who didn't know what to do. The Onslaught and Judge Death stepped in to get between them, but it looks like Sinister's annoyed Lochnar somehow. More on him to come during the break though. Sinister 3pts Hell 0pts G6s Steve Swanson vs The Mist This match was controlled all the way by Swanson. A variety of technical and brawling moves, as well as the occasional bending of the rules which weren't picked up by the referee, gave Swanson the win via submission after working over the young Mist's shoulder. Shoulder-breaker after shoulder-breaker and a couple of powerbombs whereby the Mist was dumped down shoulder-first. The victory came eight minutes in when Swanson applied a short-arm scissors and just continued yanking until the Mist gave up. Big win for Swanson in the eyes of many. Swanson 3pts The Mist 0pts G1t The Prophets of Rage vs The W & W Express This was an explosive tag match to start with, as the Express had been shooting their mouths off for the past two weeks since being unbeaten, that they could easily defeat the Armed Forces, who were the champs when they joined, and now the Prophets of Rage, the new tag champs, without any problem, and take the belts away. Unfortunately for them, in this match, they did not manage to beat the Prophets, in fact they didn't even manage a draw. The Halifax natives just proved too strong for Wayne and Watson, who suffered their first defeat this evening, by pinfall in the twelfth minute. Derek, who had controlled most of the match due to his 7'2" frame, powerbombed Wayne inside the ring for a three count, while on the outside, Shadoe and the ladies took care of Watson by various, dubious means. A good start for the Prophets, defending the title they won last time. Prophets 3pts Express 0pts G2t Anarch-X vs The Whiskey Rebellion The TOWL Tag Towel holders had been losing many matches by DQ recently, knowing they'd hang onto their titles by doing so. In this tournament, it seemed they were intent on going for the victory rather than, as they say themselves 'sodding the consequences.' The Rebellion, who've been somewhat quiet recently, were no match for the two powerful British Brawlers and they didn't even need to cheat to get the win, even though they did! Hellfire cleverly concealed a hubcap after Anarchy passed it to him and when he clocked Jack Daniels with it, the crowd knew it was all over. He threw it out to Tia, made the cover and let Anarchy take care of Jim Beam. He got the three at the nine minute mark. Anarch-X 3pts Rebellion 0pts G3t The Armed Forces vs HardCore When these two teams met last time, the Forces were newly crowned tag champs and HardCore were new to TOWL. Three months on, the Forces had lost the gold and were now beginning to gain the support of the fans, although about eighty percent still weren't sure as to their sincerity and HardCore, who hadn't wrestled a match in months until recently, are having a dreadful run. Last time it was a draw, this time it was an easy win for the Forces. NavCom was as quick and nifty as ever, while DefCon was allowed to run Weird and Mayhem ragged when he got in for his two minute power surges. A spike piledriver won it for the Forces in ten minutes. Forces 3pts HardCore 0pts WM: Okay, fans, there's the first third of the first round matches out of the way. We'll be back after this time out. Let's hear from 'Dead Air' Brian Lochnar, but first, here's Flame! [Cut to Flame in his locker room.] Nyce, Paine, Tiger, I hope you`re in a mood to kill. And I do actually mean kill. I`m going all the way to the top. I`m going for the gold. And when I beat you three, one after the other, that`s going to put me in the best position ever. HR: He's a git, isn't he....? WM: Erm, no. He's our employer! HR: Oh yeah. You're right. WM: Nothing..take it away. (Hal summarises the next set of matches.) G1s The Shark vs Dead Cold The 400lb plus Shark got off to a bad start in his first televised appearance since joining TOWL by losing to the under-rated Dead Cold. Many call him a rip-off, but he showed tonight he's his own man by defeating The Shark in under three minutes with a reverse DDT out of nowhere. Great win for Cold. Cold 3pts Shark 0pts G2s The Tower of London vs Jax The Tower of London returned to TOWL with a new attitude. An attitude I'm particularly partial to, actually. He punched a fan on the way to ringside for a start. Something I used to like doing myself. Jax came down with his Dark Monumental buddies and The Dark Giant and Dagger were enough to put the TOL off. Jax defeated the 6'7" 300lb Londoner in about nine and a half minutes following an impressive superplex which came about as the Tower was attemtping to perform atop rop bulldog on Jax which went horribly wrong! Jax rolled the Tower up and got the vital three. Jax 3pts TOL 0pts G3s Stud vs Jacob O'Macky What a farce this match turned out to be. Stud, the 255lb fan favorite came to ringside first but before he could even make it to the ring, the awesome Jacob O'Macky sent a blatant message out to his foe the Vindicator, who meet next week on the Pro one-on-one. Stud and The Vindicator, it has been intimated, are close pals, and O'Macky treated Stud like the Vindicator, even calling him that name while he was taking out his aggression on him. The bell didn't even ring to start the match. It rang about fifteen times to signal the end, for some reason. The match was actually declared a no-contest and both men were handed a point each, but Stud came out of it far worse than O'Macky, who, as his nickname suggests, is more interested in crushing bones, than actually winning wrestling matches. O'Macky 1pt Stud 1pt G4s Flame vs Tiger This rematch from Thunder a couple of weeks back pitted two of the hottest youngsters in TOWL against each other. Last time, Flame came out victorious over my least favorite wrestler Tiger. Gladly, Flame got the win this time as well. His hard-hitting comments made during the break, as well as those he made a few days back about his career being on the up are certainly ringing true right now. The two high-flyers flew high for most of the match, which seemed to excite the morons at ringside, but bored me solid. When Tiger went for a dropkick following a whip, Flame got right on him, sensing his neck was in pain and executed a swinging neckbreaker, allowing him to get the three count and the win, after just five minutes. Flame 3pts Tiger 0pts G5s Seth Blitzspear vs Caine Caine, still looking for someone apparently, but God knows who, came out on top against Blitzspear, one half of the first ever tag champs here in TOWL, although you'd never know it on recent form. Caine looked good money for his victory and won it after three minutes following a chokeslam atomic drop combination he calls the Revenge. A good victory none-the-less. Caine 3pts Blitzspear 0pts G6s The Vindicator vs After Shock The Vindicator, one of the few fan favorites I have any time for, and even that small amount is decreasing daily, came out on top against After Shock with little trouble tonight. He dominated the whole match and looked in a hurry to get it over with for some reason. The crowd went nuts when he finally won it with a flying dropkick off the top rope after just four minutes twenty seconds. After Shock was poor. Vindicator 3pts After Shock 0pts G1t ThunderDogs vs Dark Crimson This was always going to be a difficult match for the 'Dogs and although TOWL usually make sure that wrestlers win on their debuts, tonight the debutants had to settle for a draw. James and Badrock looked pretty good actually and managed to go the distance, fifteen minutes, with Dark Crimson who looked awesome but just couldn't put the newcomers away. The Dark Giant is fast becoming one of my favorites here but I think Badrock has got a lot of class. A great match with very few wrestling moves. A treat for me, but the crowd seemed to hate it. Every man to his own. Dogs 1pt Crimson 1pt G2t The Bouncers vs Team Justice The Bouncers, with their new name and new attitude, could easily cut it as bad guys, but they still seem too namby pamby to me. Team Justice, as much as I disagree with their attitude, looked in very good form and were totally in control throughout. Janet James has done a good job with them. A heel turn is on the cards to maybe get them some gold. They won it after just two minutes following a double jack-knife powerbomb - Harsh Justice. Justice 3pts bouncers 0pts G3t The ThUGs vs The Army of The Damned The unconventional ThUGs were still reeling from their recent match with Anarch-X apparently. Judge Death and Onslaught, who work so well as a team, came out on top in this one, with The Onslaught winning it for his team with adevastating 720 powerslam! The match lasted all of five minutes and was quite good to watch, but the constant fan cheering let it down somewhat. The Army were particularly impressive - possible the best team on show so far. Two Enforcers accompanied Lady Apocalypse and the Army down to ringside. They protected her Ladyship throughout the match. Army 3pts ThUGs 0pts WM: Thanks alot, Hal. HR: Pleasure. Back to you I believe, Morris. WM: Thanks. HR: Stop thanking me Morris. Please. WM: Sorry. Onto the second phase of first round matches. Don't forget, only one goes through in the singles and two go through in the tag tournament. G1s Billy Sexton vs Dead Cold Cold, after his win over the Shark just an hour earlier, was not in any kind of form to beat Sexton tonight. The World Champion was on top of his game after a few hours off and came out hoping to get up to four points, and that he did, in just nine minutes. He worked over Cold's arm for the duration and eventually took him out with the armbar submission, for which he's gained his awesome reputation, and is one of the reasons he's TOWL's World Champion. Dead Cold was obviously still tired from his earlier match, and hardly got in any offense. Sexton looked good for the finals at this stage. Sexton 3pts Cold 0pts G2s Shane Matthews vs Jax Former World Champ Matthews wasin good form as he continued his amazing record in the first round of this tournament. He's yet to be beaten in five first round matches, with Jax going down like all the rest, to the underhand tactics and incredible array of high risk manoeuvres. Jax was a sitting duck for Matthews who just continued to pick him off with dropkicks and shoulderblocks until he got the win in the tenth minute with a flying sunset flip off the top rope. The fans, although they hate him, certainly still hold him in high regard, and believe if he meets Sexton again, this time he'll come out on top despite Sexton never having lost to him. Matthews 3pts Jax 0pts G3s The Glitter Boy vs Jacob O'Macky Glitter recently defeated O'Macky in order to get to Billy Sexton, but O'Macky was sure the same wasn't going to happen again this time and he and his boss attacked Elite Inc as they came through the curtain. All four did eventually get to the ring and started brawling, but as they were in the singles competition and not the tag, the ref again had to rule the match a no-contest, which pleased O'Macky a great deal. Glitter was livid after picking up only two points from two matches, especially with Stud and Cro-Mag in the same group. Glitter 1pt O'Macky 1pt G4s Taylor Paine vs Tiger Paine, devastating in his previous match, was devastating in this match as well. Tiger, still shocked after the upset at the hands of Flame, came into this match far too eager for a quick win and the 6'6" 273 pounder just watched, laughed, then wrapped the match up. Many feel Tiger's constant coming and going from the fed has disrupted his concentration, but he's assured me, and all the fans, that he's here to stay now. This didn't bother Paine though, who finished him off inside six minutes with an incredible tnine move combination without any reply. It started with a simple Irish Whip which culminated in a double underhook powerbomb which gained him an easy three count. Paine 3pts Tiger 0pts G5s Sinister vs Caine Sinister kept up his impressive form of late, and, may I add, a very long unbeated run, by defeating Caine by pinfall in just under ten minutes. He's number one contender for the US Title, and on this form, even Taylor Paine's got to watch out. Sinister, accompanied by his stablemates, was never in doubt of the victory. He played by the rules as ever and dominated from the outset. A number of power moves ensued and it was a brainbuster suplex which won it for him. Sinister had full points up to here. A win over Seth Blitzspear would be an incredible feat. Could he do it? We'd find out tomorrow night, when that match was scheduled to take place. Sinister 3pts Caine 0pts G6s Steve Swanson vs After Shock After his demolition of the Mist, and After Shock's abhorrent form as of late, especially his match with the Vindicator, this would apparently be a walk in the park for Swanson. It was. Swanson started off by wearing Afetr Shock out, with armbars and abdominal stretches, and more than an average number of knee lifts. It soon became apparent that Swanson was focussing on the stomach in this match, in comparison to the shoulder which he concentrated on against the Mist. However, after he'd worked on the stomach, he didn't stop there. Immediately after a gutbreaker, he started using the many variations of the powerbomb and a few backbreakers just to even things out again. Shock put up no resistance and left Swanson to win the match, and move to six points, with his Dry Ice - top rope powerbomb followed by a scorpion deathlock. Swanson 3pts After Shock 0pts WM: Well, folks, that was four hours of action we just rushed you through, a total of twenty four bouts, with the exception of the Bishop Hell no-show. There was one major talking point at the end of the evening though. It was regarding the ThUGs and was leaked by one of TOWL's very own Enforcers. Apparently, and nobody knows where he got this information from, the ThUGs had taken a random drugs test earlier in the night, and when the results came through just a few minutes after their match with the Army of the Damned, it is rumored that they tested positive for a banned substance. The day after, the rumors were confirmed by another TOWL Official, and the ThUGs were barred from the rest of the tournament, pending an enquiry. It was well documented that the ThUGs had been using some kind of stimulant, and were even caught on camera doing so. Whether this is what caused the positive reaction we don't know. We expect more news early next week, by which time the ThUGs will hopefully have explained themselves. The rest of their scheduled matches were forfeited and maximum points were given to their opponents. (Cut to commercials and back to the studio.) WM: A disgraceful, erm, disgrace I guess, Hal...? HR: Heh. Not sure there is such a thing, but if there is, you're right. The ThUGs have been wrestling better than ever recently but maybe this is the reason for it. If it is found that the drug they were using is actually illegal in the sport of wrestling, they'll be out for good. If not, they should be back in after this tournament, right? WM: Right. Let's hope it is legal, for their sake. HR: What do you mean? I want 'em kicked out for Christ's sake. They're overweight bums. WM: Not at all. They've lost weight recently. HR: Not by natural causes. They really are a disgrace to TOWL. WM: Fair enough. Take the next phase of competition away, Hal.... G1s The Shark vs Wyldcard The 400lb oaf The Shark went down to his second loss in succession against the ever-more irritating Wyldcard. Wyld was always in control of the overweight newcomer and finished him off in about three minutes, although I fell asleep so it may have been more, or less for that matter. Whatever, though, Wyldcard, ably assisted by the gorgeous, yet misguided Erotic One, got the three count following a leaping DDT. Apparently that's a move he's been trying to perfect for the last few months and it finally paid off. Don't know what all the fuss is abut. It's a damned easy move to do anyway. And no, I'm not being sarcastic. Wyldcard 3pts Shark 0pts G2s The Tower of London vs The Patriot The American Patriot chalked up his first points of the tournament against The TOL who was making a very unimpressive comeback. The match was over in twenty seconds following his Patriot Missile. Little more to say about it to be honest. Crap match. Patriot 3pts TOL 0pts G3s Stud vs Cro-Mag These two, after only one match each last night, were both refreshed and ready for a good victory which would propel them up their group, knowing that Glitter and Jacob were both stranded on just two points after two matches. The crowd were marginally behind Stud, but they needn't have bothered. They didn't put him off unfortunately, and, unfortunately for them, they didn't spur him to victory either. The match lasted fifteen minutes exactly and both men earned one point each. Cro-Mag had the advantage throughout in my opinion, but apparently Stud used more moves. I don't believe these computers anyway. It was a good match, but was just pretty boring towards the end. Stud looks like he could go far with the right attitude, that is, opposite to the one he has right now. Stud 1pt Cro-Mag 1pt G4s Flame vs Nik Nyce Nyce was in dreadful form, knowing he had to leave TOWL within the week and made it as easy for the ever irritating, yet strangely amiable, Flame, to win the match. Flame was literally a ball of fire, as when he came down the aisle at the start, he was encased inside a large ball covered in fire and was pushed along by a couple of his fans. A remarkable sight, but it didn't make his wrestling any better. Yeah, he did the occasional moonsault and plancha, but who doesn't? He defeated the previously seemingly unbeatable Nik Nyce at the six minute mark following a monkey climb into a reverse roll-up combination. The crowd liked it anyway. Flame 3pts Nyce 0pts G5s Seth Blitzspear vs Bishop Hell Again, Hell didn't turn up, but Brian Lochnar did and before anyone could stop him, he was all over Blitzspear with martial art kicks and chops, and after a couple of minutes looked like he was about to Irish Whip Seth, but instead kicked him square in the throat with a spin kick. Blitzspear went down hard. I gather from Morris that particular combo is his finisher which he calls the Indefinite Cancellation. He then left the ring to a massive heel pop, probably the biggest ever in TOWL history. Although I'll guarantee it's nothing to the one that'll happen if I hear is in the pipeline for a few months time actually happens. Christ. That'll blow your socks off! Seth 3pts Hell 0pts G6s The Vindicator vs The Mist The Vindicator extended his winning streak here in TOWL well into double figures by defeating the Mist in this vital match-up. This means that the Mist is eliminated from the tournament. It wasn't all down to the Vindicator, though. The match was wrestled in good spirit, but turned sour when the Glitter Boy came down to ringside and apologised to the Mist for turning on him. As he offered the stupid Mist his hand, he threw some salt in his eyes. The Vindicator apparently didn't want to cover, although I could swear I heard him chuckle when The Boy started laughing at what he'd done, but maybe that's just me. Anyway, he rolled him up and got the easy three count. Vindicator 3pts Mist 0pts G1t ThunderDogs vs W & W Express Both teams knew they needed a win, the Express especially after their defeat against the Prophets, but both were trying so hard they cancelled each other out and were both counted out at the two minute mark. They lost track of all time whilst brawling outside the ring and although it looked like the Express wanted back in, Badrock and James were more than happy to keep on outside. The Express obviously couldn't get back in and referee G.V Wilkins counted them out. An intriguing match for the time it lasted. The Dogs looked even more amusing than the team they replaced, GGTW. Dogs 1pt Express 1pt G2t Bouncers vs Whiskey Rebellion The Rebellion completely took the Bouncers apart in this one in fine style. Daniels and Beam were always on top and got the win just three minutes in following a crucifix poewrbomb through a table by Jim Beam on the Equalizer. He rolled him in and Daniels covered for the three. Iceman had already been knocked out inadvertantly following a jack-knife through a table! Rebellion 3pts Bouncers 0pts G3t ThUGs vs HardCore HardCore won it by default. HardCore 3pts ThUGs 0pts HR: I'm carrying on Morris. I'm having too much fun. G1s Dead Cold vs Wyldcard Dead Cold's dismal tournament came to a crashing end at the hands of Wyldcard who notched up his seventh point of the tourney, which took him into first place for the time being. Cold did not follow the Rebellion's fine example just moments earlier and came unstuck in the seventh minute following an impressive reverse neckbreaker by Wyldcard followed by a slingshot suplex which got him an easy three count. Wyldcard 3pts Dead Cold 0pts G2s Jax vs The Patriot Jax finally got his hands on the Patriot in this fourteen minute bloodbath of a match. Within five minutes, both men were cut and conventional wrestling went out the window. This was always going to be a matter of endurance, and it was Jax who came out on top with just twenty five seconds to go. After a close match with little to choose between them, he suddenly plucked a hotshot out of nowhere then rolled the Patriot up with his feet on the ropes and got the quick three. Big win for Jax, although talks of a rematch are rife. Jax was level on points with Shane Matthews at this point with Shane having one match left against the TOL. Jax 3pts Patriot 0pts G3s Jacob O'Macky vs Cro-Mag These two both have similar attitudes in the ring - they just wanna hurt people. Unfortunately when both men think like that, the match can be very boring and this one indeed was. Neither man wanted to make a mistake and both seemed to respect each other, although I know the Mag was recently not best pleased with O'Macky and the feeling was mutual. They wrestled a realitively clean match and ironically, both men were so injured after ten minutes, they were both counted out whilst flat out in the center of the ring. This left them on three points each, with only Stud against Glitter Boy to go. O'Macky 1pt Cro 1pt G4s Tiger vs Nik Nyce Nyce's second match in the space of an hour went even worse than the first. Tiger literally ripped him apart. Well, almost. He was like a man possessed in this one, keen to make up for his previous crap form. Nyce was just not interested and Tiger won it with his Tiger Flash frankensteiner at the four minute mark. Nyce was booed out of the arena while Tiger got a big pop on his exit from the group. It was now between Paine and Flame, both of whom were on maximum points. Tiger 3pts Nyce 0pts G5s Caine vs Bishop Hell Caine won via default. Lochnar was restrained from going out to attack him, although he doubtless would have done! Top man. I can't wait for him to debut.... Caine 3pts Hell 0pts G6s After Shock vs The Mist The Mist bounced back from two successive defeats to beat After Shock with ease. Shock was never in it and the Irish turncoat outwrestled him to get the win after just six minutes. His high-arch cradle suplex, or the Mist-a-fier as he calls it, won it for him with Shock being totally unable to kick out. After the match, he looked out for Elite Inc to attack him, but they didn't bother. Not worth their effort no doubt. The Vindicator or Steve Swanson would go through to the next round. Mist 3pts Shock 0pts G1t The Prophets of Rage vs Dark Crimson The awesome Prophets continued their long unbeaten run against Crimson in this one, with Derek getting the three count on Dagger. The Prophets made sure that the Dark Giant, who started it off for Crimson, was not in for more than about two minutes and virtually forced him to tag out to Dagger, who the Prophets took advantage of to good effect. At the thirteen minute mark, the Prophets won it following a big splash by Derek on Dagger. The Giant couldn't get in in time to make the save. Many were surprised to see Crimson pinned after such a long time, but they realise, I think, that the Prophets are the hottest tag team ever in wrestling. Awesome. Prophets 3pts Crimson 0pts G2t Anarch-X vs Team Justice This rematch from before Easter was as highly anticipated as any match of this evening. It was a true classic, pitting X's blatant illegal tactics against Team Justice's too-good-to-be-true style. Obviously, this has all the ingredients of an ideal match, and unfortunately, the referee caught Hellfire nailing Johnny Justice with a payphone and DQd Anarch-X at the eleven minute mark. It had been even up to that point, with maybe Justice using mre of their repertoire than Anarch-X. The crowd enjoyed it and seemed more irritated that the match had ended with the payphone incident, rather than the incident itself. Justice were virtually guaranteed a place in the finals after that win. They'd have to slip up against the Whiskey Rebellion not to go through. Justice 3pts Anarch-X 0pts G3t The Armed Forces vs Army of the Damned These two teams have only suffered one defeat between them in the past two or three months. This match always had 'draw' written all over it, and a draw indeed it was. Fifteen minutes of solid action culminated in a draw for these, two of the best teams in TOWL at the moment. The Army came closest to winning it, but both knew a draw would virtually guarantee them a place in the finals, what with the ThUGs being disqualified and HardCore being in such bad form. The Forces _were_ through, it was up to the Army to beat HardCore a little later... Armed Forces 1pt Army 1pt HR: Phew. Will, over to you. That's too much for me! G1s Billy Sexton vs The Shark This win for Billy Sexton meant that he and Wyldcard were tied on points at the top of their group on seven. This problem did not occur last time, and no-one seemed to know what to do about it. A simple coin toss was suggested by Bill Green, but Billy Sexton seemed to think cutting a pack of cards would be more apt. Green liked it, Wyld didn't. After the match, which ended after his armbar submission, Sexton met Wyld backstage to cut a pack of cards. O'Macky shuffled them and then gave them to Green. Green offered them to Wyld, who picked the Jack of Clubs. Everyone thought he'd won until Sexton just lifted on card off the top to reveal the Ace of Spades. Wyldcard attempted to tell Green that the pack was rigged but Green trusted his World Champion. Sexton was the first name in the finals. Sexton 3pts Shark 0pts G2s Shane Matthews vs The Tower of London Matthews dominated this match from the start and got the win over the Tower in just under four minutes. The Tower soon announced his departure from wrestling just three matches after the comeback. Matthews played with him for two of those four minutes - he could have put him away much sooner. A spinning leg lariat off the top rope won it for Matthews, who, for the second time running, went through on maximum points. Matthews 3pts TOL 0pts G3s The Glitter Boy vs Stud For this match to have ended in a draw like all the others in the group was unthinkable, but end in a draw it did. All four men were left on just three points, such is the closeness of the group. The match looked like it was going to be handed to Stud when Taylor Paine actually got into the ring and bodyslammed Stud but when Glitter flashed over a thousand dollars in cash at him, the referee soon changed his mind and ruled the match a draw for no reason. Glitter, after the match, then tried bribing Bill Green himself but Green immediately told Glitter he was disqualified. Glitter couldn't believe it, but Taylor told him he'd win the money for the stable so he shouldn't worry. Instead of cutting the cards, they decided to draw straws. The short straw would go through. Cro-Mag picked the shortest and went through to the finals, much to the disgust of O'Macky, but then again, he didn't want to wrestle Billy Sexton either.... Glitter Boy 1pt Stud 1pt G4s Taylor Paine vs Flame Both men had to win or face having to draw lots, or a coin toss. Both men were up for it, but it was Taylor Paine, needless to say, who win it. He didn't have to kill Flame, like Flame said he would, but he did have to knock hi unconscious with Glitter's weighted money clip. Flame ran at Taylor while he was in the corner but Paine just clocked him with the clip and rolled him up while Glitter held his legs. He got the three and progressed to the finals on maximum points. The people who said he was another, larger, Shane Matthews, would appear to be right. Paine 3pts Flame 0pts G5s Sinister vs Seth Blitzspear Sinister had to win to be assured of going through to the next round, and win he did, in style. Lady Apocalypse and the Army of the Damned accompanied him to ringside but they weren't needed. The match was wrestled in good spirit, but Sinister was the wrong man for Seth to start his comeback. His form has been dire recently and Sinister highlighted all the bad points about his ring style. Sinister knew Seth was stronger, and countered everything Seth tried, to great effect. He knew he had to keep Seth away from him, so used a number of submission moves, such as sleepers and a camel clutch, which was the most memorable. He won it via a small package at the seven minute mark. Sinister 3pts Seth 0pts G6s Steve Swanson vs The Vindicator This was the most bizarre occurrence of the entire three day card. The Vindicator, for no apparent reason, didn't turn up for his match with Swanson, which is even more strange when you consider that he was tipped to win the entire tournament. Swanson seemed a a little relieved, mainly because it was one fewer match for him to wrestle, but also because he knew what a threat the Vindicator was, although he'd never admit that. Swanson was handed the points and went through to the finals on maximum points. Swanson 3pts Vindicator 0pts FINALISTS: Billy Sexton Shane Matthews Cro-Mag Taylor Paine Sinister Steve Swanson WM: An intriguing line-up I'm sure you agree fans. Could you pick a winner out of that lot? I doubt it....many think Matthews, whereas Paine is fancied by a few and Sinister has also had some support. The favorite is the World Champ Billy Sexton though, obviously. Let's see who got through to the tag finals.... G1t The W & W Express vs Dark Crimson This match never got underway. The Express didn't fancy their chances of beating Crimson due to their ridiculous size and attacked them before the bell. They had to win to go through to the finals, but they didn't manage it as the referee disqualified both teams for some reason. Crimson had good reason to feel hard done by. Express 1pt Crimson 1pt G2t Team Justice vs Whiskey Rebellion The Rebellion had to win to have any chance of going through, but Team Justice, possibly the team of the moment aside from the Prophets of Rage, were just too strong, especially Justice Jackson, who seems to have come back stronger from his draw with Judge Death last week. The match finished following a double Russian legsweep from the Justices on Jim Beam with Johnny Justice making sure Daniels couldn't stop the count as Jackson made the pin. Justice went through on maximum points. Team Justice 3pts Whiskey Rebellion 0pts G3t The Army of the Damned vs HardCore The Army proved too strong for HardCore in this match-up. Weird and Mayhem haven't been themselves recently and are even blaming themselves for Sinister Saturday never happening. Quite why, I've got no idea, but rumors are they were rushed to psychiatric hospital after this match. Judge Death took center stage in this match and it was he who got the win for his team which took them to the final stages. He pinned Weird following a reverse DDT. Army 3pts HardCore 0pts G1t Prophets of Rage vs ThunderDogs The Prophets defeated the plucky newcomers by disqualification at the nine minute mark. The Dogs realised they weren't going to pin the Prophets and Badrock decided to stub out on of his cigars on Shadoe Rage. Rage complained to the ref, who decided Shadoe was right and DQd the wild wes boys. They had a good tournament none-the-less, at least they weren't pinned. With three teams on two points, a play off had to be arranged, and they all agreed that an arm-wrestling contest was fairest - well, Bill Green did, as it was a quick way of deciding the best team. Everyone expected the Dark Giant to win it, but Doug Wayne proved it's all to do with technique, not arm size, when he nearly broke both Stan Badrock's and the Giant's arms. The W & W Express went through. Prophets 3pts Dogs 0pts G2t Anarch-X vs The Bouncers This was the biggest squash of the night. Just over a minute was all it took for the Cockney Crackpots to defeat the Bouncing Jobbers as they've been called recently. All four men were in the ring for the entire bout which began when the Xs got in and just started pounding away on their hapless opponents. Two gut wrench suplexes, one by each, on each of their opponents won it for them, with both Bouncers being pinned. Anarch-X go through to the finals. Aarch-X 3pts Bouncers 0pts G3t Armed Forces vs ThUGs The Forces won this one by default and went through to the finals on seven points. Forces 3pts ThUGs 0pts FINALISTS: Prophets of Rage Armed Forces Army of the Damned Team Justice Anarch-X W & W Express WM: Well, folks, we're out of time. Hope you enjoyed this part of the show. The summaries of the finals will be out later in the week, and don't forget to join us for TOWL Pro next week, as well as TOWL Thunder over the weekend. So long everybody.
