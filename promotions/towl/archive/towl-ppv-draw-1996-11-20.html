WM: Welcome, fans, to this special LIVE broadcast concerning the upcoming pay-per-view. You may pick the balls once we mix them around a bit. (The President flicks a switch and the balls move around.) WM: Just while we're doing this, fans, I'll tell you some of the numbers given to the wrestlers. Ball number one is newcomer Spartan, and the favorite, Seth Blitzspear is number twenty seven. Earlier on in the evening though, every wrestler picked a ball and as there are thirty four wrestlers in the federation, two men were going to be unlucky. Let's go back to the action now. (Every TOWL wrestler is in the TOWL HQ. Various wrestlers have picked their balls and are looking pleased with themselves. It then comes to Treat out of Halloween, who's partner Trick had drawn ball 13. Treat places his hand in the container and draws out ball number thirty four. He looks dejected, but Trick consoles him. Danny Dynamite is next to pick, and he draws ball four, then Michael Reyna draws and he gets ball 32. He shows some other wrestlers and they all smile and joke with him that he nearly missed it. Then [name withheld] picks and he gets ball nineteen. After that, it's the wrestler known only as The Tower of London. Unfortunately for him, he picks ball 33 and he realises he won't be in the ppv. He punches the container and then walks out.) (Cut back to Will Morris) WM: Yes, fans, Treat and The Tower of London will not, I repeat not be appearing in the pay-per-view event scheduled for next Friday night, November 29th. Be sure to contact your local cable operator for details. (The President has a word in his ear, and he talks to the camera again.) WM: Right, fans. This is it. Hal and myself will draw alternately, with the first four out of the bowl being in team one, the second four in team two and so on. Ready Hal? HR: Of course. WM: Well, let's get to it then. (Morris puts his hand in the container and picks out ball number nine. The President calls out the wrestler's name. It's Perfect.) HR: Yes! Come on, let's get him some decent partners, not that he needs them of course. He could win this on his own.... (Rogers puts his hand in and draws ball number four.) HR: Oh no. Tell me I haven't... (The President calls out Danny Dynamite. Rogers is irate. Morris then picks out ball number thirty. That's The American Patriot.) HR: Come on, Morris. You've got to do better than that for Perfect. WM: Umm...sorry, Hal. (Rogers then picks out ball number seven, which, it turns out, is Photon.) HR: That could have been worse. I just wish he'd make up his mind whether he wants the fan support or not. (Morris then picks out ball number eleven, which is Death. Rogers picks out 23, which is Machine. Rogers looks happy with himself. Morris then picks out ball 3, which is Jim Owen. Rogers pretends to hit Morris with his right hook. Rogers then picks out ball nineteen, which is [name withheld].) HR: That team would be unstoppable if it wasn't for that goon, Jim Owen. WM: Just leave him alone, Hal. He's a good man. I've told you that before. HR: Yeah, yeah, yeah.... (Will Morris then picks out ball number twenty, Willy Holdah. Rogers then pulls out ball 16, which is NavCom. Morris pulls out number 32, which is Michael Reyna and Rogers just glares at Morris. Rogers then picks out ball 31, which is Cro-Mag.) WM: Well, Hal, that's going to be very interesting. HR: Yes, you could say that, Morris. (Morris then picks out ball 15, The Super Evil Undead Zombie Warrior.Rogers then picks out ball five, which is Tiger. Morris then picks number 26, which is War. Rogers then picks out ball 10, which is The Genius.) WM: The controversial newcomer The Genius hasn't been in action here yet, Hal. Any thoughts on him? HR: Yeah, I like him. He's got a lot of class, and could make a VERY good tag team with Perfect. Obviously they won't be winning the Tag Titles next week, but maybe sometime in the future. They'd be literally the perfect team in my opinion. WM: On to team five. (Morris puts his hand in and picks out ball number fourteen, which is Famine. Rogers then chooses number 28, The Agony Uncle. Morris then picks ball number 22, Jerry Owen. The final member of the team, picked by Hal Rogers is number 29, Dick Thruster. Who do you fancy? HR: I'm partial to DevilWoman myself... WM: I was talking about that match, Hal. HR: Oh, right. I think the first team will win. How can the Agony Uncle possibly be on the losing team? WM: Good point. (The final match is then decided. Morris picks out number eight, Rebel. Rogers then picks out ball 25, which is DefCon of the Armed Forces. Morris then picks ball 13, Trick of Halloween, and Rogers finally picks ball 27, which is Seth Blitzspear.) WM: Woah. We've got the favorite in there and also two of the biggest men in the fed in Rebel and DefCon. Of course we know who they'll be facing as there are only four men left, but we'll make sure there's been no cheating and pick the numbers never-the-less. (Morris picks out ball number one, which is Spartan. Rogers picks out number 17, which is Pestilence. Morris then picks ball number 18, which is the Phantom Knight and finally Rogers puts his hand in.) HR: I think this'll be number two, and will be, hmmm, let's think...The Perfect One. (He pulls the ball out and it is, in fact ball 2, which is The Perfect One.) HR: Thank-you. WM: Yes, very good, Hal. HR: Thanks, Morris. WM: Watch it, Hal, people may start thinking you're becoming a soft touch. HR: Never. Especially not towards you, Morris. WM: Let's finally take a look at the teams. They come up on the screen: PERFECT, DANNY DYNAMITE, AMERICAN PATRIOT and PHOTON V DEATH, MACHINE, JIM OWEN and [name withheld]. WM: Comments, Hal? HR: I reckon that'll be a draw actually.
