[TOWL] Torture Three Pre-Show (ish) PART ONE (fwd)

Jun 19, 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[This is TOWL's last ever card.....] [Cut to a chorus of trumpets playing TOWL's theme music. The wrestlers involved in the pay-per-view appear on the screen apparently looking their opponents down although obviously the pictures have been montaged together. Eventually fireworks exploding and massive cheers can be heard as the camera pans over the Niagara Falls and then to some of the highbuildings on the Canadian side and then eventually down to the end of the promenade and to a massive stadium. The TTT logo crashes onto the screen... Inside the stadium stand two rings and 'Hotshot' Hal [name withheld]s and Will Morris stand next to them, by their broadcasting table. Morris wears a dark blue suit, white shirt and blue bow-tie while [name withheld]s wears a white shirt, bright pink slacks and black braces along with a bright red tie.] WM: Welcome everybody to TOWL Torture Threeeeeee [Massive pop.] here at Niagara Falls in Canadaaaaaa! [Bigger pop.] HR: And what a night it is, Morris. I mean, the weather's just perfect and the Falls are looking so spectacular. I cannot wait for the action to commence and I cannot wait to find out who these three mystery guys are that we've been waiting to see in action for so long. We've got the two debuts and then Glitter defending against Billy Sexton's Triumverate stablemate.... WM: We sure do. We've got all five titles on the line and don't forget, every match out there tonight will be no-DQ, no countout so it promises to be a crazy evening's action. HR: Oh yeah, Morris. Let's get things rolling. WM: Hold on, Hal. We've also got a special War Games match with a few mystery competitors as well as a special triangle match pitting the devastating martial artist newcomer taking on fan favorites Flame and Sinister but they're against each other as well!! HR: Yeah I'm looking forward to seeing Lochnar kick those two's butts. But the match I'm really looking forward to is the Spin The Wheel Make the Deal match for the US Title. No-one knows what kind of match it'll be... WM: No, but we'll be finding out real soon, right after the debuts in a few moments. And don't forget we'll be seeing Breathless in a match against anyone brave enough to answer his challenges. HR: God knows who'd do such a thing but obviously someone has. I daren't think about who.... WM: That's all coming up in the next three hours fans, so sit back, unhook the telephone and don't answer the door. HR: Why not? WM: They may miss some of the exciting action, Hal. HR: Oh yeah. Don't answer that door, fans, even if it's Miss Postelthwaite from next door wanting to..... WM: Shhhhh, Hal. Let's go to Charlie West who's standing by in the ring. Shhhh. Listen. This is the first match tonight! [Cut to the first ring. Charlie West stands inside wearing a black suit with black bow-tie and white shirt. He holds several cue cards in front of him. The Horseshoe makes his way down to ringside from underneath one of the stands.] CW: Laaaaaaaadies and Gentlemennnnnnnnnnn, prepare for TOWWWWWWL Tortuuuuuuuuuuure Threeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee! [Massive pop.] This special pay-per-view extravaganza is being brought to you _live_ from Niagara Falls, Canada and this, the first match, is the debut of one of TOWL's three latest signings. [The PA starts up with a familiar piece of music to some of the fans, although many don't seem to know who'll be coming down the aisle. Everyone looks to the entrance to the aisle in anticipation.] CW: Ladies and Gentlemen, soon to be making his way to ringside, he stands six feet two inches and weighs two hundred and seventy five pounds, he hails from...........Dunedin, New Zealannnnnnnnnnd, here is Theeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee Southernnnnn Maaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaan! [Massive heel pop as the Southern Man, wearing a 'Southern Man coat' and hat makes his way down to ringside, swaggering as he goes. His jaw-line is covered in stubble and wears grey wrestling tights.] HR: Heh heh. I love it, Morris. This is great! How on earth did we manage to sign this guy? Heh heh. Must be some genius somewhere knows what he's doing! WM: I have no idea, Hal, but just listen to those fans. They are _not_ keen on this man. Not one bit. And I wouldn't call him a genius either. HR: I don't know why not.....he's a class act. WM: Hmmmmmm. HR: What? WM: Nothing. Let's just watch the action.... [The Southern Man slowly gets into the first ring and takes off his coat and hat and hands them to some geek who takes them away.] CW: And his opponent, he hails from Niagara Falls, Canada, weighing two hundred and thirty six pounds, here is The Horseshoe. [Big crowd pop for the local guy who appears a little unsure of the situation.] HR: Who? WM: The Horseshoe. HR: What the hell kind of name is that? WM: Heh heh. Don't ask me....! [The Horseshoe wears black trunks with a silver horseshoe on the seat. He looks at the Southern Man.] HR: Just look at the eyes of the Horsehair, Morris. He's got tears running down his cheeks. Heh. WM: Obviously not looking forward to this one then, Hal! HR: Obviously not. Uck. And I see why not. DING! [The Southern Man makes his way over to The Horseshoe and goes to lock up but instead rams a big knee into the midsection then snap mares him over by the hair. Massive heel pop as The Man looks around the arena with a grin on his face. The Horseshoe tries to get to his feet but The Southern Man just drills big axehandles into his back then stomps on his head as he lies motionless on the canvas. The Southern Man kicks The Horseshoe, telling him to get him, rolling him onto his back in the process. The Horseshoe eventually makes it up and The Southern Man picks him up and slams him down to the mat with force. He kicks the Horseshoe around some more. The Horseshoe eventually gets to his feet and the Southern Man sends him for the ride, lifts him off the floor then drops him kidney first onto his knee. He stretches the Horseshoe's body by pushing down on his head and legs. He laughs as he does so.] HR: This is great action to start the show, Morris. The Southern Man's making an example out of the Horseshoe. I wouldn't want to be in his way when he actually starts wrestling some of the other superstars shall we say. WM: Yes, Hal. It's all well and good beating up these guys like Paul Hogan and The Horseshoe, but put him in with The Patriot, Tiger or even potential world champ the Vindicator and we'll see a different story. Then we'll see how good he really is. [The Man just dumps The Horseshoe down to the mat and puts a foot on his throat. The referee counts to four and forces the break. The Southern Man lifts The Horseshoe to his feet by the hair then sends him for the ride once again, catching him in a bearhug on his return. He then deposits The Horseshoe down to the mat with a version of a spinebuster but doesn't go down with him. He puts a foot on The Horseshoe's chest and the referee counts. At two the Man just takes his foot off the chest. The referee tells him off but he just brushes him off. He brings The Horseshoe to his feet, lifts him up, in position for a running powerslam.] HR: Hey Morris, he's setting him up for what he calls The Southern Slam. WM: How do you know all this? Did you know we'd signed him? HR: Did I know we'd signed him? Heh. Of course I did. It was me who brought him in for Christ's sake. WM: Why didn't you say anything? HR: None of your business. WM: But we're broadcasting partners. HR: So what? WM: Ever heard of loyalty? HR: Only to my friends....oh yeah, he just hit that Southern Slam. That's all she wrote fans. [The Southern Man lands on top of The Horseshoe and the referee counts: 1........... 2........... The Southern Man picks him up by the hair!] HR: Heh heh. Yeah. I _love_ it! WM: It's despicable. HR: Heh! He's going for another. [The Southern Man lifts The Horseshoe up, puts him in position and then performs another Southern Slam.] HR: Now I promise you, that _is_ all she wrote. [The referee counts: 1................ 2................ 3!] DING DING DING DING DING DING DING DING DING! CW: Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of the match as a result of a pinfall, the Southernnnnnnnnn Mannnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnn! [Massive heel pop as the Southern Man drops out of the ring to Charlie after throwing The Horseshoe out over the top rope. He grabs the mic and and crawls back into the ring. As he stands towering in the middle of the ring the crowd erupts even more in a huge heel pop and start throwing rubbish at him. Southern Man laughs at them, kicking some of the rubbish away, even catching a soda cup that comes close to him and firing it back at the crowd.] [Southern Man]: Shut up you pathetic hoard of inbred puke eaters, it's about time this federation saw some real talent. And I'm it. Answer me this - what have you got here that can stop the Southern man - what? I'll tell you since the mental exercise has obviously exploded your tiny excuses for brains...you ain't got squat. You see the Southern Man is UNDEFEATED in 1997. UNDEFEATED in nearly six months of professional wrestling, and since I got rid of that little money grubbing maggot of a clingon manager Max Clifford, I've been on the roll of a lifetime. And guess what...that roll is just gonna keep on going because there's no talent in this universe that can stop it. Now I wanna talk to you idiots who come along week after week and watch the great matches that the TOWL puts on. I wanna tell you I'm really sorry. I'm sorry because there WILL BE NO MORE GREAT MATCHES. You see, the Southern Man is in town, and when the Southern Man rolls into a federation two things happen. The first thing that happens is all the scrubs who have been running tough over the federation see a superior and they duck for cover, shaking and cowering in whichever corner they can fit their mangy hides into. The second thing that happens is that some little wannabe with visions of glory will come and face the Southern Man, and I will destroy him in the ring. You are looking at the man who is going to destroy every single pathetic attempt at a wrestler that steps into the ring with me. I fight for the hell of it, and when I get into that ring there can only be one winner. I don't care if you're a low life scrub, a world champion or a living giant - when you get in the ring with the Southern Man - YOU ARE NOTHING - NOTHING BUT AN IRRITATION TO BE SWEPT ASIDE AS ALL THE OTHERS HAVE! [The Southern Man pauses, making gestures at the crowd that probably shouldn't be on prime time TV.] [Southern Man]: Now for those of you who have been lucky enough not to come across me before, here's how I work. I offer an open contract. Anyone who wants to get in the ring is most welcome to come and get nine colors of shit kicked out of them. If that doesn't get me matches I set myself a target and I pummel him into the canvass at every opportunity until he will climb into the ring against me of his own will. If people are still running scared I play on the universal feature I have found in Americans. Greed. You see, I don't care much for your titles, but if it's the only way to get a match then I'm quite happy to come and take them offa the champions. And guess what morons - there's NOT A DAMN THING ANYONE CAN DO ABOUT IT! THE SOUTHERN MAN IS HERE - I'M ON A ROLL - AND I'M GONNA ROLL RIGHT THROUGH THE RANKS OF THE TOWL!!!!! [Southern Man spits on the Microphone, sending wicked feedback around the arena then as "I'm a Southern Man" plays up over the PA he strides from the ring, stopping to grab an American flag off a fan and rip it in half.] WM: Wow. HR: Wow indeed. Now that was fun. WM: Fun? Look at The Horseshoe. He can hardly move. [The Horseshoe tries to get up but can't manage it. Eventually some paramedics comes down with a gurney and wheel him back to the locker rooms.] HR: Okay. Wanna know who's next? WM: Yeah. HR: The Owens. WM: Huh? HR: The Owens are up next. WM: They're not newcomers. HR: I know that....ohhhh, you wanna know who they're wrestling? WM: Hold on. I thought it was a singles wrestler...? HR: It is. This is a handicap match. But the guy weighs more than both the Owens put together! WM: Huh? Who is it? HR: Let's go down to the locker rooms and find out. WM: Hey, I'm meant to be the premier here, you're just color. _I_ do the links. HR: That's what you think. [Cut to the locker rooms. A cameraman knocks on one of the doors and it's opened by someone who stands behind it, making it appear to open on it's own. The camera pans around as the door slams shut behind him. He turns round and is greeted by two familiar individuals: the 565lb Tonnage and his manager and tag partner Little Louie.] [T] Well suck my ass and call me Susie! Look who's come to dominate another fed! Only the greatest thing to hit the ring since our fine sport began! And of course, I brought my best friend, and the finest technical genius on record, to help me out! [L] Always a pleasure, big man! It does my heart good to see you kick the living hell out of every snot nosed droplet of maggot drool that oozes his way to the ring. But not nearly so much good as doing the deed personally, which is why I'm so happy we've come here! It's been way too long since I've had a crack at some of the flotsam you've been stomping, and I'm itching to give those twits a lesson that they don't teach you in wrestling school...unfortunately for them! [T] There's nothing like seeing a true professional in the ring, and I'm anxiously awaiting your triumphant return, Lou. Now, for those of you who don't know me, get a goddamn clue. If you've been in this business for any length of time and still don't know the Beast, you've obviously been spending way too much of it in the bush leagues. Those of you who are familiar with my work will be glad to inform the ignorant of the punishment that's in store, I'm sure. I see the Patriot is here, which is good and bad at the same time. We've held tag titles in the past, but I'm not particularly interested in tag teaming this time around. I was always a singles man at heart. And who should I see, but Seth Blitzspear! Man, and I thought there was supposed to be some competition in this fed! Lucky thing I've come to raise the average IQ, not to mention the talent level! I also see Swanson is here...not exactly my favorite person on Earth, but certainly capable of drubbing most of the scrubs into submission. That's most of those I know, but it's nice to see a couple of boys from good ol' Nova Scotia in the league; been a while since I've been there, but the best fans an the planet, let me tell you. [L] You said it, T man! Nobody knows talent like Nova Scotians, and if there are two boys from Halifax, it's no surprise that they're way up there in the rankings! Tag team champs, unless I miss my guess! But I should get on to other things. It's been a while since I've had the pleasure of making some prelim bum suffer the Lambaster, or any other move for that matter. So when the Beast decided that the TOWL was ready for him to take over, I figured now was as good a time as any to make my way back into full time competition. There's a few titles out there that I may compete for; my only regret is that there aren't any light- heavyweight belts. I always enjoyed the competition far more for that type of title match than in any other. But, beggars can't be choosers. Not that I have to beg for belts, mind you; oh, I'm very much capable of taking out anyone whom the TOWL is unkind enough to sign against me. I haven't chosen which champ is going to lose his belt to me yet, so I'll just humiliate some of the local non-talent until I do. [T] I know that the smart people will be cheering for you just as loudly as I will, Lou. And naturally, you'll succeed, because that's what happens when hard work, dedication, incredible natural ability, and a keen intellect combine in the same person. And I should know, if anyone does. I guess that brings me to the obvious question...what are my own goals in the TOWL? Well, the first is the same one that almost everyone else has, to win the World championship. Apparently, the current champ is someone who goes by the nickname of Painbringer. Well, if he should ask anyone who knows the Beast, he'll quickly discover that his moniker is far more suited to myself than to him or anyone else in the TOWL. Luckily for him, I'm not looking for a title match right away. See, unlike the typical scrubs who proliferate this business, I don't try to get by on reputation or attitude alone. I'm a professional; I don't just waltz into a fed and lay claim on a belt. I'm way too good for that. No, what I'm going to do is what any true competitor would do...that is, get some fool to put his name on a contract, put him through the wringer, and repeat until I can rightfully demand a title shot based on my ranking. And once that happens, the rest is mere formality. That process brings me to the other reason I'm here. I've never been a man to simply get into the ring, wrestle a match, and leave. The only time I really have fun is when I get to see my opponents squirm. The act of inflicting agony on whatever miserable slob has the misfortune of facing me is my job, my pasttime and my hobby all at once, and something I truly enjoy. Watching some pathetic hosebag writhe on the mat as I twist his puny body into a double helix is the kind of spectacle that got me into this sport in the first place, and is the most influential reason why I choose to remain a competitor. Sure, titles are great, but nothing is so gratifying as to hear the buttcrack I've been telling off for a week or more screaming for mercy that'll never come. Getting the World belt, as I undoubtedly will when the time comes, will demonstrate that I'm easily the most awesome force in wrestling, to those too stupid to see it otherwise; crushing every scrub in my path to that belt is a task which is its own reward. It's one I'll be engaging in frequently as time goes on in the TOWL, at least once a week, I should hope. And woe to those cursed by the management to have to take me on. [L] Y'know, big man, plenty of people in the TOWL will have something to say about all of this. They'll probably spout off about how neither of us could ever beat them, and how we won't last a week here, and stuff like that. [T] Probably, Lou. But if that happens, it'll simply add to the already immense pile of evidence proving that most people in this sport have something wrong with their heads. All one has to do to see how mistaken that opinion is, is take a look at the Beast. Five hundred sixty five pounds of career ending devastation, and the largest man in the TOWL I might add, is a force to be reckoned with, not ignored, and if those who are already here make the mistake of counting me out, it's their funeral. Now, meaning no disrespect, Lou, I could see why they might underestimate you; you aren't exactly the most imposing figure in wrestling. However, that would be making an even bigger mistake; your ability to destroy the opposition is just as impressive as mine, but in a much more insidious and subtle way. [L] Well, thank you very much, big man. No offense taken, of course. What the Beast has said is, as always, absolutely true. I'm not the biggest or the strongest man in the world, and have never claimed to be much more than average in either field. Some would say below average as far as size in concerned. But that's not how I make my money, as everyone will soon see. I take people apart, one piece at a time, and anyone getting into the ring with me who doesn't respect what I can do will quickly find themselves wishing they'd done their homework more thoroughly. I may not be as dominating as the T man, but I am just as deadly, and anyone who doubts that is more than welcome to test his hypothesis in the squared circle...whereupon I will dissect him at my leisure. [T] Heh. A warning well worth listening to, don't doubt it. So, this brings us to just about the end of our little speech. The only question left to answer then, is who we'll be looking for as our first decent opponents. The answer to that is, I don't know, or care. Lou and I are willing to pummel anyone who wants it, so whomever happens to be free at a given card is as good a victim as any. We've both dealt with every style of wrestler there is on this planet, so we don't particularly give a hoot which one is standing on the other side of the ring when the bell goes. [L] You can be sure of one thing, though...whatever style that person excels in, and wherever he comes from, won't make any difference. When either of us gets into the ring with anyone, that person will be checking the ceiling for leaks when the night's over, that you can count on. I think we've made our point, big man. [T] I agree, Lou. So, to whoever it is that I or Lou be crushing next, there's just one thing that's left to be said...make sure your chiropractor has a table waiting for you. TOWL, it's time to be dominated, Armageddon style! We're outta here! [The leave the locker room and make their way down to the ring. Cut to Charlie West. Tonnage's music plays over the PA.] CW: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest, it is a special handicap match set for one fall and marks the debut of another of TOWL's recent signings. Introducing first, he hails from Albany, New York, and weighs five hundred and sixty five pounds, accompanied by Little Louie, here is Tonnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnaggggggggggggggggggggggggggggggggge!! WM: Oh my goodness. Just look at the size of this guy! HR: He's got a sixty-_five_ inch chest, Morris. _Thirty_ inch arms! That's bigger than your waist! WM: It is not.... [Tonnage and Louie walk down to the ring to a mixed heel pop - most of his fans are obviously just terrified of him. He wears a mask which covers the top half of his head. Both he and Louie hold their arms out for the fans who want to touch them but to those who don't they just brush them off. Louie gets into the ring, followed by Tonnage who gets in over the top rope. The mustic changes to 'Follow the Yellow Brick Road' as from the film The Wizard of Oz. Massive face pop which does include a reaction from the fans of Tonnage calling the brothers 'faggots' and the like. They emerge from under the stand and make their way, hesitantly, down the aisle with Tom Watson Esq.] CW: And his opponents, they hail from Kansas City, Kansas, and are accompanied to the ring by Tom Watson Esquire, Jim, Jerry, the Owennnnnnnnnnnnnnnn Brotherrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrs! [The Brothers hop into the ring and look over at Tonnage. Tom Watson gives them some last minute advice before stepping out of the ring. Louie drops to the outside as well.] DING! WM: Okay, fans, we're underway in this one. Looks like it's Jim Owen to start against the big Albany native. HR: I wonder why he seemed fascinated by the Prophets hailing from Nova Scotia...? WM: No idea. Maybe that's where he originates from. HR: They do build 'em pretty big in New York though, huh, Morris? WM: Heh. You could say that but of course I never would! HR: Heh. WM: For a start it's not that far from here AND that man in the ring says he's from there, so _I_'m not going to upset him! [Jim walks up to Tonnage and offers him a test of strength. Tonnage looks at him, smirks, and then tosses Jim down to the ground with one arm. Jim rubs the back of his head and then gets up to his feet, bounces off the ropes and runs at Tonnage with a shoulderblock. Tonnage just stands there and brushes Jim off. He falls flat on his back. He shuffles back to his corner and looks at Jerry. Jerry just backs away.] HR: That's brotherly love for you, Morris. It makes me sick. He's just letting him battle this behemoth on his own! Get in and help him, Owen, you yellow-bellied idiot. [Jim gets up and runs at Tonnage again, with another shoulderblock but just drops down to the mat on impact. He tries one more time, this time launching himself at the big man with a flying cross body block. Tonnage catches him in mid-air, turns around 360 degrees, looking at the thirty-five thousand fans then moves with surprising haste into the corner, ramming Jim's body against the turnbuckles. He then runs to the opposite corner, ramming his back against that. He hiptosses him out of the corner into the center of the ring but as he goes for a big splash, Jerry decides to get in to help his unfortunate brother and attempts a dropkick on Tonnage. He simply brushes it off with both his massive hands. He then picks Jerry up and presses him above his head then pumps him up and down until the crowd's chant reaches ten. He then dumps him down on top of his brother, bounces against the ropes and splashes the two of them.] HR: I guess you could call that a delayed Gigaton Crush on Jim Owen, Morris. He just got caught up with Jerry then finished his combo off. Doesn't look like he's done yet though..... [Tonnage boucnes against the ropes again and performs another big splash on the two brothers. Tom Watson complains on the outside but Little Louie just nails him with a lariat. He then calls for Tonnage to go up top. The crowd quieten down in anticipation of seeing the big man jump off the top turnbuckle on top of _both_ Owen Brothers. He climbs over the top rope then ascends from the outside. He can hardly balance himself on top and simply dives straight off in a sleek motion and lands plum on top of Jerry who squashes Jim in the process. The referee looks at him but can't force him to make the pin. Tonnage looks to the outside and asks Louie to get in to make the cover. Louie hops up and puts his foot on top of Jerry's back, effectively pinning Jim. The referee seems to want to call for the bell.] HR <to the referee>: It's no DQ you idiot. Just make the count. [The referee looks down at Hal then drops to the mat. He slaps it: 1.......... 2.......... 3!] DING DING DING DING DING DING DING DING DING! CW: Ladies and Gentlemen, here is your winner, Tonnnnnnnnnnagggggggggge! [Tonnage and Louie take an Owen each and hurl him over the top rope. They then parade for a while in the ring while Tonnage's music blasts over the PA. Big heel pop with a core of supporters sitting in the East Stand who all make worshipping motions at them.] WM: What a debut. HR: I'd say 'what two debuts'! Actually, 'what three debuts'!!! WM: Huh? <pause> Ohhhh. Very clever, Hal. HR: I try my best! WM: Well, apparently we're not going to get rid of these two for a few moments, so let's go down to Chris Allen backstage who's waiting with President Green and the two competitors in the 'Spin the Wheel Make the Deal' match. Chris? [Cut to backstage.] CA: Thanks alot, Will. I'm here with President Bill Green and also 'Real Deal' Taylor Paine and 'Ice Cold' Steve Swanson who will be squaring off against one another a little later in the evening in this special stipulation match for the TOWL United States Championship. Mr Green will firstly toss a coin and ask Taylor to call and then whoever wins the toss spins the wheel. So, President Green? [Green takes out a dime and tosses it high in the air then catches it and turns it over onto the back of his hand. He looks at Taylor to call. Taylor shouts 'Tails.' Green looks at the coin and shows it to the camera and it does indeed show 'Tails.'] CA: So, Taylor won the toss. If you'd care to spin the wheel please, Taylor. Thanks alot.... [A massive colored wheel, similar to that on the wheel of fortune but instead of cash amounts on the various segments, this has special stipulation matches: Russian Chain; Dog Collar; I Quit; Barbed Wire; Cage; Lumberjack; Lumberjack (Belts); Texas Death; First Blood; Bullrope; Prince of Darkness; Coal Miner's Glove; Falls Count Anywhere; Spinners Choice. Taylor grabs it and spins it round about twelve revolutions.] CA: Is it ever going to stop?! [Silence. The wheel starts slowing down.] CA: Any preferences Gentlemen? [Silence again. The wheel stops on 'Bullrope' but then has a final burst of energy and continues to 'Prince of Darkness.'] CA: Woah. The Prince of Darkness match. Both men will be blindfolded with black hoods. Thoughts Gents? [Both men walk off to their respective locker rooms without uttering a word. Both Sarah and the rest of the Elite Inc mob can be heard asking what it landed on in the background.] CA: Thanks alot, President Green. Back to you at ringside, guys. WM: Thanks alot, Chris. HR: Yeah, thanks Allen. Great job. WM: Don't be so mean. HR: I wasn't....so you don't think he did a good job then? Huh? Huh? WM: Yes, he was marvellous. <speeds up> Let's go down to some comments from Breathless who'll be up a little bit later against a so far unnamed opponent: [Camera shows nothing but a dark floor with rats scurrying across it, the camera slowly moves up showing Breathless standing there in tattered clothes with a black, torn up mask on, covering his face. The room itself is nothing but smooth cement, a small window with bars on it is the only thing providing any light. The room is obviously a dungeon.] Breathless: Slowly the rats climbed on me, gnawing my clothes, tearing my flesh... at first I wanted to get away but then... but then I enjoyed the pain. Ever since, I have been in here, waiting my match with whoever has the guts enough to accept my challenge. [Breathless picks up a rat and begins to pet it.] Breathless: See, that's how a cancer like me operates... it starts out small, not harming anything at all... virtually unnoticeable... [Breathless then squeezes the rat's neck so hard it breaks and tosses it to the floor.] Breathless: Then it strikes... killing you... [The camera fades away with Breathless's laughing in the background. Cut back to ringside.] Continued in Part Two!
