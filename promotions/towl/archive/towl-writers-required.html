[TOWL] Writer(s) Required

Jan 13, 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello All, This mail is asking quite simply what the header states. TOWL was started up by me in November and after a rather dodgy opening month or so, is now recruiting wrestlers every few days, as well as disposing of the players who did not roleplay, and therefore the number of matches I am required to is steadily increasing. When I started the fed I promised one card per week with occasional house cards and this has been the case so far (with the exception of a Christmas break) and will continue to be the case. I am finding that the cards are gradually getting longer and longer though, possibly due to my enthusiasm and it wouldn't be fair on the players to decrease the cards in size now as they are used to the 'higher quality.' Having read this far, you might want to have a look at the cards yourself and see if you think you can match or better the current standard (far be it from me to say that every match on every card was well written). The homepage address is: http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/1592/towl.html And you can follow the links from there to get to the cards. I am looking for no more than three writers, although I won't turn anyone away who wants the job and is capable. :) If you need any more information on the fed or the job required, please feel free to contact me at: [detail withheld] Thanks for your time, [name withheld]
