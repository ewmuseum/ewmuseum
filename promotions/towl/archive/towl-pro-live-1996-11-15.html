TOWL Pro LIVE

15 November 1996

(Cut to images of every TOWL wrestler who is in the fed signing their contracts with the voice of Will Morris giving information on each of them. ‘TOWL Pro Live' theme music plays in the background, then gets louder as the TOWL logo cuts into the screen. Then it pans the arena, filled with over fifteen thousand fans, then down to Will Morris, a man of about sixty years, sitting next to another man, aged about forty five. They are both wearing headsets.) WM: Welcome, fans, to the first ever edition of TOWL Pro Live, here in the great State of Texas. With me tonight is my broadcasting colleague, ‘Hotshot' Hal Rogers. HR: That's right, Morris, and don't you forget it. I'm more of a spectacle than any of those guys out there tonight. Especially the guy we're starting with... WM: Yeah, OK, Hal. Let's go to the rundown: (Morris talks whilst the action plays on the screen: Coming up in the next hour, we'll be seeing The American Patriot in action, also the Agony Uncle will be here, as will the latest arrival, Seth Blitzspear. In tag team action, we'll be seeing the Armed Forces and The Players Club and in the main event, the arrogant Perfect will be facing veteran of the ring wars, Ged McIntyre.) HR: We should be seeing some great action here tonight, Morris. Let's go down to the ring for the first contest. WM: Over to Charlie West. ----------------------------------------------------------------------- THE AMERICAN PATRIOT vs GODZILLA McGRADY ----------------------------------------------------------------------- (The lights go down and West announces the event:) CW: The following contest, it is set for one fall. Introducing first, hailing from San Diego, California, weighing two hundred and eighty five pounds, The Americannnnnn Paaaaaatriottttt... (The crowd go nuts as ‘Stars and Stripes Forever' plays over the PA. Patriot touches the hands of every fan on his way down to ringside., then climbs into the ring over the top rope.) WM: The fans sure love this guy, Hal. HR: Yeah, but I don't see why. He's a joke. WM: Well, we'll soon find out. Let's go down to the introduction of his opponent. CW: And his opponent, already in the ring, from Bowling Green, Kentucky. Weighing 276 pounds, this is Godzilla McGrady. (McGrady puts his hands in the air and the crowd boo loudly. He then spits at the crowd and they go even more wild. McGrady then goes over to Patriot and double axehandles him from behind.) HR: This ain't gonna be easy for Patriot here. McGrady's a big man, but Patriot's got the leverage advantage. What is he? 6'9 or so? WM: Six foot eleven, 285 pounds. HR: I'd still take him out. (McGrady still pummels on Patriot in the corner. He rams his head into the turnbuckle twice, then rakes him down the back. The crowd get behind Patriot and he begins to respond.) WM: I think McGrady went a bit too far there, Hal. Patriot's livid. HR: Yeah, but I still think Godzilla'll beat him. (The Patriot, turns to McGrady and McGrady backs off. Patriot pushes him out of the corner and he falls to the floor. He then picks him up and slams him down to the canvas.) WM: Ouch. (Patriot then drops a leg across McGrady's throat and asks the crowd what they think. They start screaming for the Patriot Missile. He picks McGrady up by the hair and elevates him high up with a massive suplex. He holds him there for a good half minute.) HR: I gotta give it to him. That takes a lot of strength holding a guy over 270 up there like that. WM: I don't think anyone would deny he's one of the strongest guys in TOWL. HR: I think Machine or Rebel of the Dark Side may have a few words to say about that, Morris. You do come out with some trash. (The crowd are really enjoying the action and are screaming for the Patriot. He takes McGrady down with an arm drag and turns it into an arm bar.) HR: You see, he's taking a breather here. He just ain't got the stamina. WM: Well after a move like that, I'm really not surprised. HR: To be the best out there, you gotta have it all. Patriot's just proving he ain't the best. (Patriot moves from the arm and gets McGrady into a headlock on the mat. McGrady begins to get up and musters up the strength to get up, fall back on the ropes and push Patriot off into the opposite ropes. Patriot comes off the ropes and meets McGrady with a clothesline which knocks him right back down to the mat.) WM: The fans were shouting for the Missile again there Hal. Patriot seems to be enjoying himself out there though. HR: Yeah, but if he leaves it too long, McGrady could cause an upset. (McGrady staggers to his feet and Patriot pushes him between his legs.) WM: This could be painful for McGrady. Oh my! (Patriot turned it into a powerbomb which seemed to shudder the arena.) WM: McGrady appears to be out. HR: No, he's just acting. That didn't hurt one bit. (The Patriot again picks McGrady up and whips him into the ropes, pushes himself back into the ropes behind him, then, as McGrady comes back, hits him with the Patriot Missile.) HR: Thank God for that. Just cover him. Get it over with. (Patriot covers McGrady without hooking the leg and the ref slaps the canvas three times.) Ding Ding Ding! CW: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, The Americannnnnn Paaaaaatriottttttt. (‘Stars and Stripes Forever' plays over the PA and the ref elevates Patriots hand into the air. He climbs out of the ring over the top rope and makes his way back down the aisle, touching the hands of the fans.) HR: It makes you sick to be an American, Morris. WM: What are you talking about? This guy portrays all that is good about Americans, unlike others I could mention. HR: Meaning? WM: Forget it. We'll be back with more action after this time out fans. Don't go away. (A few commercials play and then the camera cuts to Morris and Rogers sitting by the ring, as Godzilla McGrady is stretchered out of the ring area.) WM: So much for him not being hurt. We were meant to come back from the commercials into the action. While you were away fans, a doctor had to be called to the ring to examine McGrady. It seems that he back with us, but we've been informed he's being taken to hospital for a check up. Let's hope he recovers in time for next week. HR: This is not good, Morris. WM: I know. Patriot certainly inflicted some damage on him. HR: I'm not talking about that. Why did they put this guy in there. It's obvious he's not up to it. If someone like the Patriot can do that to him, he's got no hope against anyone else. WM: I can't believe you sometimes. HR: Hey, I call it how I see it. If you don't like it, I can handle this on my own. WM: Let's go back to the ring for more action. ------------------------------------------------- THE AGONY UNCLE vs JIMMY ENDO ------------------------------------------------- CW: Ladies and gentlemen. The following contest, it is set for one fall. Making his way to the ring, from Tulsa, Oklahoma, weighing in at two hundred and ninety two pounds. Here is the Agony Uncle. (The music from the ‘Psycho' shower scene plays and the Uncle ambles down to the ring with his wife, the Agony Aunt. The fans boo him, but still try and touch his body. He shrugs out of the way and eventually gets down to ringside. He then climbs through the second rope and sits on it to let his wife into the ring. She goes over to his opponent and says something to him, then quickly gets out of the ring as the Uncle goes straight to work on him. Charlie West makes a quick exit also.) HR: This is gonna be quick. I'll tell you that much, Morris. WM: I think you may be right. HR: As always. Who's this geek he's fighting anyway? WM: His name's Jimmy Endo. HR: Oh dear. I think this could be the ‘End O Jimmy' tonight, Morris. (Morris sniggers at the comment and then calls the action. The Uncle places a knee into Endo's back and his head hits the top turnbuckle. He yelps in pain. He then turns him around and Irish whips him into the opposite turnbuckle. The ring shakes. He then whips him back into the other corner. He then runs straight into Endo and he falls to the floor.) HR: Endo went down like a ton of bricks there, Morris. Uncle could finish it right now if he wanted. WM: Somehow, I don't think he will! (The Uncle then picks him up and drapes him upside down, legs first on the top turnbuckle, and just rams him with his knee. He then, puts his foot on Endo's throat and the ref starts to count.) WM: The ref should stop this now. He could choke him to death here. HR: I think that's the intention somehow. (The ref (Andy Phillips) reaches four then makes the Uncle let go. Endo drops head first to the canvas. The Uncle pulls him by the hair to the center of the ring and goes to see his wife, while the ref checks Endo. The Agony Aunt gives the Uncle a kiss on his cheek and the crowd boo them both. She turns round and tells them to shut up.) HR: What's wrong with these morons. Why can't she show her approval of her husband. WM: But there's no need for it. It's the height of arrogance, Hal. Turning his back on his opponent, then kissing his wife...? HR: Well, I always found it a help. WM: Yeah, and how many times did you kiss a lady at ringside during a match? HR: Oh my. Look what the Uncle's doing now... (The Uncle is lying on the floor with his feet on Endo's shoulders, stretching his arms and making him scream.) HR: Such a pleasant sound, don't you think? WM: Not really, no. I wish the Uncle would just finish him off actually. HR: Good things come to those who wait, Morris. (The Uncle releases the hold and picks Endo up by the throat. He then stands him upright in the center of the ring and runs against the ropes. He comes back and goes straight through Endo, knocking him to the floor.) WM: That's what he calls ‘The Dear John.' I think it's over. (The Uncle places his foot across Endo's throat and the ref counts to get him to break the hold. He reaches four and he won't break it. The ref tries to pick his foot off Endo, but gets his hair pulled and then gets thrown into the corner, knocking him out.) WM: That's dreadful. What does he think he's doing. He had this match won. Now he goes and attacks a referee. HR: I love it. Keep on him, Uncle. (The Agony Aunt gets into the ring and begins kicking Endo. Three referees come to the ring to get him off, but they don't succeed. Then another figure comes down to the ring.) WM: Thank goodness for that. That's Jackhammer J.P Steele. HR: What's that oversized idiot doing down here? WM: Hopefully, he's going to clear the ring of the Uncle and his so-called ‘lady' wife. HR: She's one of the nicest ladies you'd ever care to meet, actually. WM: How did I know you'd say that? (J.P. Steele, begins to pound on the Agony Uncle. He tries fighting back, but gets clotheslined over the top rope. He then picks up the Aunt who is trying to claw his eyes out and places her on the ring apron. The crowd erupt. Steele follows them to the outside and they fight all the way back to the locker rooms.) HR: Why did Steele get involved in the Uncle's match? I just don't understand it, Morris. WM: I bet if it were the other way round you'd have something else to say about it, though...? HR: Rubbish. I don't believe in interfering in other people's matches. WM: But it was over. HR: Well maybe I'm going deaf, but I didn't hear the bell. WM: Let's go down to Charlie for the official word. CW: Ladies and gentlemen, senior referee, Bob Senz has informed me that the Agony Uncle has been disqualified (crowd cheer) The winner of the match: Jimmy Endo. WM: Don't go away, we'll be right back. (More commercials play, then cut back to Morris and Rogers) WM: Welcome back fans. Next up is probably the most popular wrestler here in TOWL right now. HR: I can't see why. He's got no personality at all. WM: Admittedly, he doesn't convey it through what he says, as we all know he is mute, but there's just something about him, which makes the fans love him. He's also got one of the best conditioned bodies I've ever seen. HR: Yeah, that's right. He's 336 pounds of muscle, but it ain't gonna win him no wrestling matches. If you don't got the talent, you'll get nowhere in this sport. WM: We'll see... -------------------------------------------------------------- SETH BLITZSPEAR vs ‘SEXY' SHAWN SMALL -------------------------------------------------------------- (Cut to Charlie West) CW: The following contest, it is set for one fall. Making his way to the ring, weighing in at 336lbs, from parts unknown accompanied by Pat Mills. Here is Sethhhhhhh Blitzspeeeearrrrrrrr. (Seth's music blasts over the PA. He storms to the ring, touching the hands of the fans. He steps into the ring and takes off his robe.) CW: And his opponent, weighing 226lbs, this is ‘Sexy' Shawn Small. (Small takes off his robe with a smirk on his face. He shows off his body to the fans. One female fan faints and there is a small rumble in the crowd. Most of the fans boo him. Blitzspear just looks at him. The bell rings.) HR: This is mismatch, but I think Small has enough savvy to pull out an upset here. Blitzspear doesn't seem to be taking him seriously. (Small moves to the center of the ring and raises his arms in the air, asking Blitzspear for a test of strength. Blitzspear looks to the fans and they cheer him. He accepts the challenge and Small is on his knees straight away. The crowd pop. Small asks to be let go to try again and Blitzspear duly lets go.) HR: He's just so stupid. I could tell you now what's gonna happen. WM: Go on... HR: Small's gonna kick him in the stomach. You watch. (They again lock up their hands, and Small kicks Blitzspear in the stomach, then again.) HR: What I tell ya? WM: Blitzspear is still on his feet though. Look at his eyes. He's real mad now. (Blitzspear pulls his hands away from Small's and executes a short clothesline. The crowd cheer. Blitzspear gets straight back on him as he gets up.) WM: Another clothesline! Blitzspear's really up for this one, Hal. HR: Yeah, he's really got his hands full with more than a hundred pound weight advantage. How did you get this job, Morris? (Morris ignores him as Blitzspear comes off the ropes and bulldogs Small. The crowd get off their feet and Blitzspear acknowledges them. He waits in the corner for Small to get up. Small stays down though. After a couple of seconds, Blitzspear goes over to check him. Small turns over and puts a thumb in his eye.) HR: That's very clever. Small knows he can't beat this guy by power, so he's using his head. Smart guy. WM: Yes, but that's illegal. You can't do that in wrestling. HR: The ref didn't DQ him, did he? WM: No. HR: Well, then. What's the problem? (Small then rakes Blitzspear's eyes again. Then he head butts him, but he hurts himself more than Seth and drops to the canvas. Morris chuckles along with the crowd. Blitzspear then regains the advantage and body slams Small, then again, and a third. Small holds the arch of his back and tells the crowd to shut up. They just make even more noise. Seth then whips him into the ropes and elevates him over ten feet into the air and straight over the top rope.) WM: That's the Flying Time Backdrop, Hal. Pretty impressive, don't you think? HR: I hope the ref DQs him for that. He deliberately chucked him over the top rope. WM: How do you know? HR: Well, that's the nature of the move. This is ridiculous. (Blitzspear goes to the outside, picks up the apparently unconscious Small and rolls him up for the three count.) Ding Ding Ding! (Crowd cheer) CW: Here is your winner, Setttttttttth Blitzspeeeeearrrrrrrrrrr. (Seth's music plays again and he drops to the outside, posing for a photo with a fan and signing a few autographs. Small gets up and staggers back to the locker rooms ignoring Blitzspear completely.) WM: Well, Hal, I think Blitzspear proved he's one of the top contenders for the World Title out there. HR: Yeah, but at the PPV, anyone could win it, and I mean anyone. Maybe even one of those ridiculous Owen Brothers! WM: Hey, Hal, they're two of the nicest guys you're ever likely to meet. Let's go to some pre-recorded comments with them right now. (The scene is the TOWL interview room. Three men are standing there. Two are dressed in fancy dress, one is like the tinman out of the Wizard of Oz, his brother is dressed like the scarecrow and the other is dressed smartly.) Man 1: Hi! I'm Jim Owen and this is my brother Jerry. We're real pleased to be here in TOWL and are relishing the opportunity of winning some belts in this massive PPV event. Jerry: That's right, Jim. With Tom by our sides, there's no way we can lose. Tom: Why thanks boys. Let me introduce _my_self. I'm Mr Tom Watson Esq.. and I'm gonna lead my boys to the World Tag Titles, and no-one will stand in their way. Their young, keen and intelligent, as well as being pretty strong and two of the greatest pound for pound wrestlers in this sport. Whoever they tag with in the opening matches, their teams will win, of that there is no doubt. Let's do it boys! (Jim and Jerry place a hand on top of Watson's and they then raise them into the air) All three together: Yeah! (Commercials play and then we return to Morris and Rogers.) WM: Just before the break we saw the Owen Brothers. They seem pretty focused Hal. HR: They may be focused, but who's gonna take two guys dressed up like characters from the Wizard of Oz seriously? Please. WM: Well, earlier on this evening before we came on air, they were in action against the two power men, The Masked Warriors. Let's take you back to the closing stages: (All four men are in the ring. The Owen Brothers are in trouble, but out of nowhere Jerry executes a 360 powerslam. He then works on the other Warrior while Jim goes to the top rope and lands on Gorilla One. The ref counts to three and the match ends, but before the winners can be announced, Rebel and Machine have already made their way down to ringside and get into the ring. They hurl the Masked Warriors over the top rope and begin pounding on the Owen Brothers. Both men are over seven feet tall and have no problem tossing the youngsters around the ring as they please. They both powerbomb each brother and then hoist Jerry up to the top rope and perform what's known as the Double Extermination. They then perform the same manoeuvre on Jim. Watson tries to stop them, but can't. He just gets a mouthful of fist and drops to the outside.) (Cut back to Morris and Rogers, who is laughing inanely.) WM: Haven't you stopped yet? That's one of the most dreadful attacks I've ever seen. HR: I agree. Great wasn't it...? Those Dark Side guys are certainly gonna get somewhere if they carry on like that. At least we know now why those earlier comments were pre-recorded and not live. What a joke. (He bursts into laughter again.) WM: Well, folks, I can tell you that both brothers are OK, but are still a bit shocked. I'm sure they'll be back to seek revenge on the two big men. HR: I'm sure they'll welcome the challenge. WM: Let's wait and see. Now, though it's time to take a look back at some of the other action from earlier tonight. (Cut to shots of various competitors raising their arms in victory.) WM: In the first match of the evening, the rookie Tiger took only twenty five seconds to dispose of The French Frog. He ran to the ring, jumped up to the top rope, acknowledged the fans and was jumped from behind by the crafty Frog. He turned round and kicked him to the floor and performed a version of the moonsault with pike. HR: With what? WM: I'm not sure what the term means, but I've been assured that that's what happened. I think that's what Greg Luganis did wrong when he hit his head that time. HR: Oh.....right. OK. WM: From there, he picked up the Frog and whipped him into the ropes, then executed the Tiger Flash. The crowd were really in awe of the promising light heavyweight and didn't seem too bothered that the match was over so quickly. In the second match up, the controversial tag team, God's Gift to Women were in action. In what turned out to be a very one sided affair, the Gifts dominated against the unfamiliar combination of Marty Monroe and T.P Soil. Rumor has it, though, that those two are considering becoming a regular tag team and may be looking out for each other in future events. However, the main talking point of the match was Naked Jim who, although he did not interfere, appeared to put the two underdogs off somewhat. He accompanied the Gifts to the ring wearing only a thong. It really was a vulgar sight and many of the women in the arena were rather embarrassed and apparently so were some of the men. However, once the initial reaction died down, the match got underway and after precisely 7 minutes and 39 seconds, Holdah executed a remarkable sunset flip from the top rope, known as Take That and Party, on Monroe, while Thruster took care of Soil, who was on the ring apron, by executing a vicious DDT / hotshot combination, snapping Soil's neck on the ring ropes. HR: That really was a great move. I thought he was looking a bit out of shape, but I think he proved to everyone out there tonight, that he's still got what it takes. WM: Also in action tonight in an unscheduled six man event, young rookie Extremis tagged with the Phantom Knight and Cro-Mag against Mike Williams, Andy Raymond and Paul Hogan. Cro-Mag and Extremis didn't even get into the ring until the match was over. The Knight just walked over to his opponents, planted a boot to the face to each of them in succession, then picked up the Twins and threw them through the second rope to the outside. He then put Hogan into the powerbomb position, but grabbed him by the throat as he was on his shoulders and chokeslammed him to the floor. He then asked his partners if they wanted a piece of the action, but they shook their heads and let the big man get the easy pin. HR: That Knight is certainly one mean specimen, and for his height, he's got one of the best conditioned bodies I've ever seen. WM: In the next match-up, Photon came to the ring to a mixed reaction accompanied with his barmy manager, Tables Mike. He was up against the three hundred plus pound mauler from Germany, ‘Kaiser' Schultz. HR: And what a match that was.... WM: Well, the match itself was one of the more entertaining of the evening, but it was what happened once it had ended that was more interesting. Photon, as we see here, defeated Schultz after completely out wrestling him and performing some great high-flying moves. The fans seemed to be on Photon's side, as Schultz was constantly taunting the fans and had a slanging match with one guy before the bell rang. HR: Yeah, that was pretty funny actually. I thought Schultz was gonna nail him right on the kisser. WM: Yes, Hal, but had he done that, he'd have landed himself in some pretty hot water. The TOWL directors would have taken severe action against him, possibly fining him any bonuses he may receive and the fan could easily have claimed that he was assaulted. HR: That may be true, but it never stopped me... WM: No, I guess it wouldn't have. As I was saying before I was rudely interrupted... HR: I never _rudely_ interrupt you, Morris. I may interrupt you, but it's never rude. WM: Whatever. Come on, I'm trying to tell the fans what happened out there. HR: Well get on with it. WM: Thankyou. Nine minutes into the match, Photon leaped over the top rope after being tossed to the concrete floor by Schultz and got a two count after a cross body block. He then quickly leapt to the top turnbuckle before Schultz could get back to his feet. He waited for Schultz to get up, then nailed him with a drop kick. Schultz was stunned and the fans loved it. He then went for the pin and got the three. He tried pulling Schultz up by the hair, but the ref counted quickly as Schultz's nose was bleeding. The ref signalled for the bell, but Photon wasn't finished yet. He picked up Schultz and slung him outside to ‘Tables' Mike who was waiting for him with a table he had taken from the timekeeper. He managed to get Schultz onto the table and slapped his face a few times to check if he was out. Meanwhile, Photon had climbed to the top rope and executed the Photon Press whereby he landed on top of Schultz and broke the table. Schultz apparently didn't know what happened and the broken table cut his body pretty badly. He had to be stretchered away. The crowd reacted negatively to these actions although part of the crowd seemed to enjoy it. They weren't too sure whether to cheer or boo and it appears that Photon himself hasn't yet made up his mind whether to go with the fans or turn his back on them. We'll try and get more on this in the weeks to come. HR: I don't know why he doesn't just do his own thing. Break a few rules here and there and forget the fans. They count for nothing in this sport let me tell you, Morris. WM: But without fans there would be no sport. HR: Crap. These guys would still wrestle whether there were a hundred thousand fans or fifty. WM: I don't quite get what you're saying, but what the heck... HR: Shut it, Morris. You're out of your depth here. But one guy who showed that he could have what it takes to go all the way to the top tonight was ‘The Pretty Boy' [name withheld]. This guy oozes class and that guy The Godfather is pretty spooky, even to me. WM: Yeah, Dean certainly showed us it was worth the hassle we had with him over his contract. It was touch and go whether he joined the fed, but in the end he chose to join, and even though I disagree with some of his tactics, he is certainly one of the biggest superstars here. (Images of the match are shown on the screen when Dean himself and the Godfather come to the ring area. The crowd boo him passionately but the Godfather appears to calm them down just by a glance. Dean shakes Hal Rogers' hand and Rogers bows to the Godfather, who just nods his head in apparent approval. Dean goes through the action giving his arrogant opinions and saying how great every move he performed was. He looks particularly pleased with the way he powerbombed Terry Benson just two minutes into the match. After that he blitzed him with an array of technical, high-flying and even power moves. After the action finishes, he and the Godfather walk back to the locker rooms, after again shaking Rogers' hand and Rogers again bowing to the Godfather.) HR: Wasn't that just great, Morris? That Godfather guy's OK after all. WM: His name's God_father_, Hal, not just God. He's no different to anyone else. HR: But he just commands respect though, Morris, so I gave it to him. WM: He didn't even speak to you... HR: Morris, we got on perfectly well thankyou very much. Now, what other action occurred this evening? WM: Well, the Tower of London looked particularly impressive against The Highwayman, remember? HR: Yeah, but I think that guy's attitude's wrong. WM: What do you mean? He seems a perfectly nice guy to me... HR: Exactly. He's got a lot of potential. He just needs to direct it in the right areas. WM: Namely rulebreaking by any chance? HR: How did you guess? Maybe I should try and get a word with him... WM: No, Hal. Leave him alone. I'm sure if he wants your advice, he'll ask you for it. I'm sure he'll make a name for himself the way he's going right now..... HR: Yes, but I could make him a superstar.... WM: Finally this evening, the peculiar, for want of a better word, team Halloween took care of The Masked Warriors in little under ten minutes. The match was marred by the appearance of the Armed Forces after the match had ended. Words were said, but no blows were exchanged. Talking of the Armed Forces, though, they're in action after this time out. (An advertisement for the Pay Per View appears on the screen. The run down of the sixteen different matches is given, as are some of the comments by the wrestlers themselves.) WM: If you haven't already ordered it, you can subscribe now for only $19.95 from your local cable operator or dial 1-800-TOWL-PPV. It's sure to be a big event and well worth the twenty bucks you'd pay for it. There are still tickets left to come and see it live, and you can either call into the MGM Grand box office or ring them on that hotline number. That's 1-800-TOWL-PPV. HR: How much is it to get in then? WM: You're commentating on it, Hal. It's free for you. HR: Oh.... -------------------------------------------------------- THE ARMED FORCES v THE MAY TWINS -------------------------------------------------------- (Military drumming music comes over the PA. The lights go down and the crowd begin to boo as the team of the Armed Forces make their way to ringside with the spotlight firmly set on them.) CW: Ladies and gentlemen, the following tag team event, is set for one fall. Introducing first, making their way to the ringside, accompanied by Aaron the Caddy, from Omaha, Nebraska, weighing in at a combined weight of six hundred and forty three pounds, NavCom, DefCon, The Armmmmmed Forcessss. (Big heel pop. Aaron the Caddy threatens a few fans with his golf club. This incenses them even more and they begin shouting obscenities at him. DefCon makes his way over and the fans shout in his face. NavCom calms him down and they climb up the steps onto the ring apron then get into the ring to a sea of boos.) WM: They look pretty focused don't they, Hal. Just look at the sweat on DefCon. He looks well prepared. HR: You're right, Morris. For once. DefCon's obviously been working out in the back there and even though they're only facing these two idiots, they're taking it very seriously. WM: Back to Charlie for the announcement of their opponents. CW: And their opponents, combined weight four hundred seventy seven pounds, The May Twins. (The twins acknowledge the crowd and many of them cheer and applaud.) WM: They look very intimidated, Hal. HR: Well wouldn't you if you knew you had to wrestle these guys? Mind you, Evander Holyfield had that same look on his face, but he still beat Tyson. Somehow, though, I don't think these twins will pull off the same result. WM: Well, we'll soon find out. (NavCom starts it off for the Forces and Mark starts for the Mays. They lock up in a collar and elbow tie up and Nav pushes him over to his corner. DefCon gets in a cheap right forearm to the neck and Martin gets into the ring. The ref pushes him back outside while Nav and Def take the opportunity to pound on Mark with punches from Def and a barrage of European Uppercuts from Nav. Mark tries to punch his way out of trouble, but gets nowhere. The crowd try and alert the ref, but he's still busy with Martin.) WM: These guys know every trick in the book, Hal. HR: No, there's only one guy who knows _every_ trick, and you have the honor of sitting next to him. (Nav then pulls Mark to the center of the ring and drops a huge elbow on his throat.
