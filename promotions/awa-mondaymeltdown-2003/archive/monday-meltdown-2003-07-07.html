AWA Presents: Monday Meltdown

7 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AWA Presents: Monday Meltdown Monday July 7nd, 2003 Live from Seattle, WA. The Show Begins AWA logo flashes as the intro video to Meltdown runs as fans start to scream and fireworks go off everywhere... I Can't Remember Anything Can't Tell If this Is True or Dream Deep down Inside I Feel to Scream this Terrible Silence Stops Me Now That the War Is Through with Me I'm Waking up I Can Not See That There Is Not Much Left of Me Nothing Is Real but Pain Now Hold My Breath as I Wish for Death Oh Please God,wake Me Back in the Womb its Much Too Real in Pumps Life That I must Feel but Can't Look Forward to Reveal Look to the Time When I'll Live Fed Through the Tube That Sticks in Me Just like a Wartime Novelty [The curtain opens from the backstage area and Neil Berkley along with Tommy O'Reily step out and head to the ring.] Neil Berkley: "Woe Woe Woe, what in the hell is that shit? Was the Metallica I heard playing at an AWA show? NO, I don't go for that! Who's idea was that? Chris Angel? He is gone! Gone! Gone! That is all there is to it, forget about him. Now, lets get this show underway right now, Rick come on out here and bring that little punk Tim with you and lets get it kick started right away! Now, up first we have Speedster against Michael Saxson." Tommy O'Reily: "I think that is Michael Sexton sir." Neil Berkley: "I don't care what his name is. OK, lets make this short and sweet, I don't give two pennies about these fools so lets say this is a 'whoever makes it to the ring first, doesn't get fired' match! Sounds good, timekeeper, ring the damned bell." Speedster vs. Michael Sexton [Speedster and Sexton both run from the back, towards the ring. As they reach the ring apron, Sexton rolls under the ropes, but as Speedster is trying the same manuever, Tommy O'Reily slides under the ropes and places both feet into the chest of Speedster.] Neil Berkley: "Congratulations son, you just won. So, Speedster, you are FIRED! Pack up your shit and get the hell off my show!" Tim Kemoren: "WELL, welcome to another addition of AWA's Monday Night Meltdown. I am your host Tim Kemoren, along side me as usual is Rick Ringlord. Rick, that was a heck of a way to start off a show wouldn't you say?" Rick Ringlord: "I would say so Tim! And you can say hell, it is allowed. But, who cares about that cause up next we have the Hardcore Title match between Jestin Mounts and Rae Lyyn! Jestin is coming off a great win and a career making Pay Per View by becoming the first AWA Hardcore Champion while his opponent Rae Lyyn sounds like a woman and wrestles like one." Tim Kemoren: "I don't agree with this Hardcore image that Neil Berkley is forcing onto us here. It is high time that wrestling cleaned up its act and went back to the solid family entertainment!" Rick Ringlord: "Shut up Tim, this hardcore action is just what the doctor ordered. Lets get with it." Jestin Mounts vs. Rae Lyyn Dennis Ryder: "Introducing from Evansville, Indiana...the AWA Hardcore Champion, Jestin Mounts!" ["Times Like These" by Foo Fighters blasts over the PA system and the arena lights black out for the first 8 measures... On the 8th measure the music speed picks up and the lights start flashing orange and red and the Jestin Mounts is seen at the top of the ramp. He has on sunglasses and an orange leather coat. He puts his arms out and fireworks blast from the sides of the stage. The audience boos . Jestin begins walking down the ramp. and looks around at the audience. about 3/4 of a way down the ramp he spins in a circle and runs towards the ring. He runs up the steps and jumps onto the turnbuckle. Jestin jumps off the turnbuckle into the ring. He runs over to the opposite turnbuckle and jumps onto it and gives the hang loose sign tot he audience. He walks top the side of the ring and bends over it to grab a mic from the announcer. He takes it and puts it up to his mouth and his music cuts off.] Dennis Ryder: "And his opponent, weighing in at 225 pounds, Rae Lyyn." [Halo hits over the PA and the curtains draw back, but no Rae Lyyn to be found.] Dennis Ryder: "Rae Lyyn!" [No Lyyn, no movement at all from backstage. Suddenly, Rae Lyyn comes flying out from behind the curtain, hitting the steel rampway with a huge thud. From behind him, out steps Tommy O'Reily.] Tim Kemoren: "What in the hell is going on here? Tommy O'Riley has Rae Lyyn all beaten and bloody before this match is even underway. This isn't fair, this is just no damned fair!" Rick Ringlord: "Look at Jestin, he is circling around the ring like a waiting predator. He can smell the blood in the air and it is driving him mad! He bolts from the ring and up the rampway." Tim Kemoren: "He is all over Rae Lyyn, with boots to the face and gut, My god, he just stomped on Lyyn's neck. The head of security, Tommy O'Riley is right there, step in there Tommy! I don't care if you had a fight with Lyyn, you have to stop this carnage!" Rick Ringlord: "Tommy is headed to the back. Lyyn is out! Jestin has his hands raised in victory, as he should! What in the hell? What was that? Someone just nailed Jestin in the head with a pipe. Its Jenna Cyde! Oh man, things are heating up now! Jake Jarvis is out there now, he is working over Jestin." Tim Kemoren: "There is Ender! He has Jarvis from behind, and nails him with a belly to back suplex off the rampway and through the tables below! Jenna nails Ender right in the back of the knee with that pipe. What the hell is going on here?" Rick Ringlord: "I will tell you what is going on Tim, madness. Pure madness. And I love every second of it. Jestin is back over to Lyyn, who is spitting up blood from the pounding he has taken. Jestin is on top of him. 1....2....3.... That is it, it is all over. Call the winners circle, cause Jestin is on his way. And while you are at it how about you call the morgue cause I think they have incoming!" Tim Kemoren: "That isn't funny Rick. Rae is really hurt, he needs some help out there. Now, what is that? Who in the hell is that guy? He is walking right past everyone and down to the front row! With that hood on I can't see a thing!" Rick Ringlord: "That, oh, who cares who that is. Take a look at that rampway! Jarvis is down and out, Enderis down and out and Rae Lyyn is really down and out! This is great!" COMMERCIAL BREAK Hells Angel vs. Dominic Richards Dennis Ryder: "Introducing first, making his way to th..." [The AWA music blares over the PA system and Neil Berkley along with Tommy O'Riley walk to the ring.] Neil Berkley: "So, you people think that just because this is the AWA that it is JUST the AWA. Well, I got news for you! It isn't! This is the new and improved AWA. Out with the old and in with the new. Hells Angel, what a compeditor, don't you think Tommy? And what about Dominic Richards? Destined for greatness! But I can't think of a reason why they are booked here tonight. I understand that without them, the show is just that much shorter, but you know, that was another Chris Angel decision and his decisions sucked! Richards, Angel get your asses out here. [HA and Dominic walk to the ring.] Neil Berkley: "Do either of you boys want to wrestle here tonight?" Dominic Richards: "Not really, but I will if I have to!" Neil Berkley: "Honesty, I like that! how about you HA?" Hells Angel: "Not really, no." Neil Berkley: "More honesty. I love these guys. You see, there is just one problem. Dominic is ready to go, but you HA, you don't seem so sure. SO, being the nice guy I am, I will give you a chance to prove me wrong. you are both out here, both in your gear, so ring the bell and lets have a match!" [The bell rings and just as they lockup Tommy hist HA from behind with a set of brass knuckles.] Tim Kemoren: "Now, don't tell me the ref didn't see that! He did, he is calling for the bell." Neil Berkley: "I am the owner of this damned place, restart this match, NOW!" [The bell rings restarting the match.] Tim Kemoren: "1....2....3.... My god, how cheap. I feel ripped off! Dominic Richards just beat Hells Angel without ever laying a hand on him." Neil Berkley: "Now, I will be in the back if anyone needs me! HAHAHAHAHAHA!" Jake Jarvis & Alan Fiscus vs. Ender & Craig Cane Dennis Ryder: "This match is a one fall tag-team match. Introducing first, the team of Jak..." Tim Kemoren: "Here they come, already fighting. Alan Fiscus and Jake Jarvis are at it with Ender and Craig Cane from the get-go. They aren't even waiting for the introductions! Fiscus has Ender up and suplexes him onto the ramp. Ender is definatly favoring that leg." Rick Ringlord: "Cane and Jarvis are going at it, fighting tooth and nail. Cane pushes Jarvis back, and he almost tumbles off the rampway for a second time tonight, but he fights it off. Cane runs and they BOTH tumble over the side down, through the tables! WOW." Tim Kemoren: "This is what AWA is all about, great matches! Fiscus has Ender back on his feet and they are fighting down towards the ring. They roll under the bottom rope and the ref calls for the opening bell. Fiscus is right back on top of Ender, nailing him with rights and lefts. My god, this is amazing, Ender is back on his feet and right back into things. They are going at it with all they have. Fiscus tries to nail him with a haymaker, but Ender ducks it and catches Alan in the gut with a knee. Pump handle suplex." Rick Ringlord: "Damn, now ref, get in there! Ender was pulling the hair! That was a cheap shot! Jesus Christ, can't these refs see anything?" Tim Kemoren: "Ender has Fiscus back up and whips him into the corner. He follows him in, but Fiscus moves. NO, Ender stops himself and turns around. Fiscus is smiling, knowing he just got one over on Ender. Ender dashes from the corner and nails Fiscus with a clothesline." Rick Ringlord: "There is some movement up near the ramp. Oh hell, its Craig Cane. Come on Jake, get up...you can't let this become a two on one! Cane is limping down to the ring." Tim Kemoren: "I can't believe that Cane is up after that splash he and Jarvis made going through that table. Here he comes, he rolls under the bottom rope and crawls into the corner. Ender whips Fiscus into the opposite corner. He runs at him, but Fiscus gets a boot up and nails him right in the face. Fiscus jumps over Ender and runs across the ring, nailing Cane in the chest with a low drop kick." Rick Ringlord: "There you go Alan! Take control, you are the World Champion after all. Jarvis is still down, he has been through hell tonight! Here come Jenna to check on him, it is about time she got her fine ass out here. It looks like Jarvis is OK, he is sitting up talking to Jenna. Here she comes to the ring, look at her fly down that aisle. She smacks Cane in the back with that steel pipe! And Cane is OUT OF THERE!" Tim Kemoren: "Dammit, why can't we get someone out here to control her? She needs to stay in the back from now on! It looks like Alan Fiscus is going to walk away with this one. He has Ender up and nail him with The Overthrow! That has to be it. Alan has him down. 1....2....NO, Fiscus pulls Ender up off the mat! What a cocky son of a bitch!" Rick Ringlord: "What you call cocky, some of us call confidence Tim. You see, Alan knows he has the upper hand. He knows that he is better than Ender ever thought of being. He has Ender back up again, and another Overthrow. Fiscus has this one in the bag now! Ender is done, put a fork in him!" Tim Kemoren: "Fiscus is just looking down at Ender who is on the mat, damn near knocked out. Here comes Jarvis to the ring, finally. Jarvis is in the ring, talking with Fiscus. The two of them are laughing now...what are they cooking up?" Rick Ringlord: "It doesn't matter what they have cooked up, this match is over. Fiscus is out of the ring. He grabs a table and shoves it into the ring. Now Jarvis is out of the ring and grabs a mic. They are both back in!" Jake Jarvis: "Ender, you piece of crap, we are gonna end this right here, right now!" Tim Kemoren: "Oh lord, what are they planning? Jarvis is climbing the ropes and sits down on the top. Fiscus has Ender up and places him in the arms of Jarvis. MY GOD, not that! Fiscus sets up a table right below them. Jarvis is gonna piledrive Ender through that table." Rick Ringlord: "Yes! I can't believe it, Jarvis and Fiscus truely ARE the Main Events! This is great! Ender's career is done, finished. Fiscus is calling for it!" Tim Kemoren: "NO, someone just jumped over the barricade and nails Fiscus with a chair! Jarvis sees him and pushes Ender down onto the table and jumps from the middle turnbuckle but is also met with the chair. This guy is just in time! He shoves Ender off the table and sets Jarvis up on the middle turnbuckle. He grabs him under the arms and drives him straight through the table! That is the third time tonight Jarvis has taken a trip through the tables! OH MY GOD!!!!!!!!!!! It's SAM HAIN! Sam Hain is back!" Rick Ringlord: "NO, HELL NO! What in the hell is he doing here?" Tim Kemoren: "Hain is rolling Ender on top of Jarvis. 1......2......3.....YES, Ender has done it, he has beaten the Suicide Kings!" COMMERCIAL BREAK Marshall Gates vs. Adrian Slayer Dennis Ryder: "This is the main event of the evening, one fall to a 60-minute time limit. Introducing first ("Rollout" by Ludacris starts to play as pyro blasts fire off from both sides of the ramp), at 6-feet-4, 253 pounds, from Houston Texas – he is the AWA Intercontinental champion, Marshall Gates." Dennis Ryder: "And his opponent (the lights of the arena dim as "The Final Countdown" by Europe plays. Pyro blasts go off on both sides of the ramp as a spotlight finds Slayer at the top of the ramp) at 6-feet-10, 355 pounds, from Seattle Washington – "The Extreme" Adrian Slayer." Tim Kemoren: "This should be a great battle between two solid talents. As we know, slayer is no stranger to being a champion, and Marshall Gates is the current AWA Intercontinental champion." Rick Ringlord: "Well quite frankly, this main event would have been a hell of a lot better with The Suicide Kings in here, either one of them. And didn't Slayer promise to deliver some magical fourth member to his sorry stable tonight – so where the hell is he?" Tim Kemoren: "Well, we might find out more as this match goes on Rick – there's the bell and here we go." Rick Ringlord: "One thing is for certain – Marshall Gates is know to have some power from time to time, but I don't think he can match it with Slayer. Although frankly, neither of these guys could keep up with Alan Fiscus in any kind of match." Tim Kemoren: "But I think Marshall is going to try it right now – the two men lock up and Gates backs Slayer into the corner. Referee calls for the break but Gates, big chop to the chest and Slayer shakes it off. Gates back in and Slayer quickly spins the IC champ into the corner – remember, this match is non-title, so Gates will leave the IC champion." Rick Ringlord: "WOW – I haven't seen that in 15 years. Slayer grabs Gates's face with his massive hand in an old-fashioned Claw Hold. Now Slayer grabs Gates by the back of the head – he's got the challenger's head Between his hands and LIFTS Gates OFF THE MAT!! Slayer throws Gates to the mat and sends him rolling into a corner – what an incredible show of power by Slayer." Tim Kemoren: "Gates shaking off the effects of the double-claw maneuver by Slayer, and look at his face – Gates looks absolutely stunned by this. This kid did a lot of talking this week, but I don't think he took into consideration that he is not going to Be able to go power for power with the Extreme One." Rick Ringlord: "God, don't call him that – I haven't seen that much "extreme" about Slayer. Like Fiscus said, he's all talk and ego." Tim Kemoren: "Gates, dashes past Slayer, leaps up and flies off the second rope – Slayer grabs Gates is mid air and flips the challenger back over his head, and Gates rolls into the corner. Well, so far Gates has tried two moves and neither has worked. Slayer gets Gates up by the trunks and slams him back to the canvas with a belly-to-back suplex." Rick Ringlord: "And Slayer just about folded Gates in half with that move. I will admit, Slayer's power does impress me, but we haven't seen many wrestling moves from this so-called great champion" Tim Kemoren: "Slayer steps over the Gates and OOOOO – Gates with a low blow on the Slayer, and yeah that left a mark. Slayer doubles over, and Gates a kick to Slayer's face while laying flat on his back. Gates scrambles to his feet and drop kicks the champ – Slayer rolls back slams his head into the turnbuckle." Rick Ringlord: "I think Gates wants to draw blood from Slayer if he can, which would prove Gates may finally be doing his job right. The IC champ comes off the second rope and plants an elbow in Slayer's chest. Gates, again off the rope, again a elbow to the chest, and Gates leaps to his feet and is posing! What the hell is that – he doesn't want to play to the crowd, he needs to stay on Slayer if he plans to win this match." Tim Kemoren: "Well, Gates is gaining some confidence – the challenger with a boot to Slayer's face, now up to the second turnbuckle and plants a flying close-fist punch to the forehead. Gates, again playing to the crowd, then turns back with 3-4-5-6 kicks to the Extreme One. Gates gets Slayer to his feet, whip off the ropes...Slayer ducks the clothesline, off the ropes and Slayer – WHOA, catches Gates with a spear and drives his back into the turnbuckle." Rick Ringlord: "That move snapped Gates's head back, and with the force Slayer hit it, Gates is lucky it didn't snap his back as well. Slayer still a little groggy, but he's to his feet – Gates looking like he's been run over by a truck, just sitting in the corner. Slayer puts a boot on Gates's throat and drives the hold down tight, choking the challenger with the heal of his shoe." Tim Kemoren: "Well Gates needed to neutralize Slayer's size advantage, but it hasn't worked so far. Gates, up to a knee, catches Slayer with a right hand, then another, another – but Slayer, grabs Gates's head in both hands and slams it 2-3-4-5 times against the turnbuckle, and Gates slumps over to the mat." Rick Ringlord: "Slayer gets Gates to his feet and is laying in the chops in the corner – yeah, here comes Fiscus with a steel chair, into the ring. Slayer doesn't see him and WOW! Fiscus blasts Slayer to the back of the head and that staggers Slayer back into the opposite corner. Good, now here come a very slow but still mobile Jake Jarvis and Jenna Cyde to join the attack." Tim Kemoren: "Well, I hate to say it, but the Extreme One started this with by bad mouthing Fiscus and the Suicide Kings all week – Fiscus and Jarvis have Slayer in the corner and they're kicking the hell out of the Extreme One – Now they get him up and pin him in the corner. Jenna slides into the ring with that damn lead pipe." Rick Ringlord: "Now what is this – Gates has grabbed the pipe from Jenna, and is looking at Slayer. HE WANTS TO DO THE HONORS? Fiscus and Jarvis, urging Gates to do it, to nail Slay...WHAT THE HELL IS THIS??" Tim Kemoren: "Marshall Gates blasts Jake Jarvis between the eyes with that pipe. Fiscus tries to go after Gates, but Slayer whips Fiscus across the ring into the turnbuckle, then follows it in with a running clothesline. Fiscus slumps to the mat and rolls out – Jarvis is in the ring still, and is bleeding like a shot moose, and now Gates is down on Jarvis, pounding away at that cut on Jarvis' head." Rick Ringlord: "In the mean time, Jenna has the pipe and is coming up from behind Slayer - NO DAMN IT. Slayer turns, and Jenna, there's no escape. Slayer, that asshole, don't you hurt that girl." Tim Kemoren: "Slayer grabs Jenna by the throat and lifts her – one handed, then plants a big kiss on her!! And now, CHOKESLAM, and that might have busted Jenna Cyde in two. Gates is off of Jarvis, so Fiscus is dragging his bloodied partner out of the ring. Gates and Slayer, standing over Jenna, daring Fiscus to come into the ring to get her – she's out cold." Rick Ringlord: "Well, I don't know what these two assholes Slayer and Gates has cooked up here, but that was absolutely unnecessary of Adrian Slayer to chokeslam that helpless girl...." Tim Kemoren: "Helpless girl – not hardly. Gates rolls Jenna to the edge of the ring and ease back, so Fiscus grabs here. EMTs are out here now, checking both Jarvis and Jenna – wait, Slayer has a microphone." Rick Ringlord: "What makes that guy think anyone wants to hear what he has to say?" Adrian Slayer: "Folks, I had a little announcement to make tonight, but by now I figure you've already figured it out. Extreme Impact is about to grow – it started with the Extreme One, then we added the Dominator, then the Jam Master, and now – our newest member is the AWA Intercontinental champion, my personal Texas Ranger, Marshall Gates!" Tim Kemoren: "HOLY CRAP – Marshall Gates, one of the men who created The New Generation, has jumped ship and joined Adrian Slayer's Extreme Impact. Slayer's stable now has the Hardcore champion Jestin Mounts and the Intercontinental champion Marshall Gates – and the Extreme One himself has his sites set on Alan Fiscus and the AWA world title. Now, where the hell are you going Rick? Well, it looks like Rick Ringlord has abandoned his post in disgust!" [The AWA theme plays over the PA system as Neil Berkley and Tommy O'Riley come out from the back, past Fiscus and down to the ring.] Neil Berkley: "Well, congratulations Adrian, you certainly pulled one over on all of us, great job! Now, I have a suprise for you. YOU ARE FIRED! Get your ass off my show you piece of trash!" Tim Kemoren: "What in the hell is that? Adrisn Slayer is fired? NO! He can't do that. Adrian is staring down the Owner of AWA. Oh my god, Tommy O'Riley just nailed Slayer in the side, but Slayer took the shot. He has O'Riley by the throat! CHOKESLAM! That isn't going to help his case at all!" Adrian Slayer: "You think you can fire me Berkley, you got another thing coming!" Tim Kemoren: "This isn't good, Adrian don't do it. He has ahold of the Owner, he looks out at the audience who are begging him to hit Neil. Rick Ringlord, Rick Ringlord just hit the ring, he grabs Adrian and whips him into the ringropes, he nails him with the Ringlord Five-arm! Adrian is busted open!" Neil Berkley: "You don't mess with the Board of Directors Adrian, EVER! You think you aren't fired, go on, think it boy! But you are in for a world of pain as the Board of Directors and their World Champion 'The One Man Riot' Alan Fiscus run over you like a frieght train!" Tim Kemoren: "What in the hell does that mean? Our time is up, join us next week...and we will try to sort this out!"
