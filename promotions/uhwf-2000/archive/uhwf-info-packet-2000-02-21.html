Saturday Night Disaster 4c. Pay-Per-Views 5. UHWF TITLES 6. ANGLES / FEUDS 7. MISCELLANEOUS AN INTRODUCTION The Ultimate Hardcore Wrestling Federation is an electronic wrestling federation or, eFed. Our objective is to have a large group of dedicated eWrestlers and to have a great deal of fun. We are also here to allow newcomers to the eWrestling industry an easier start so as to not be terrible roleplayers when they first start. We also want to help out any eFeds out there get better. With the resources we have and provide, we hope for it to be a succesful mission.
hWf/UHWF History - In early July of 1999, the Hardcore Wrestling Federation(hWf) was born. It lasted for about 4 months until I couldn't handle it anymore. A few months after that, I really started to miss the action of eWrestling so, the first Ultimate Hardcore Wrestling Federation(UHWF) was born. It didn't really get off the ground any and died right away. I kept trying to start it back up again, but I didn't have the resources I needed.
That's when I bought the domain name " ultimatehwf.net ". I also started to learn how to introduce CGI into my site. I was a little more experienced in designing web pages and had a little more knowledge of
HTML. I made a few more contacts and about a month and a half after buying the domain name the eFed was launched, and that's where we are now.

Angle Creator - Michael Lauxford - Creates angles and feuds in the eFed. RULES Rule #1: Roleplays (RPs) are determined by quality, not quantity. While the Board can and will put length and amount into factor when deciding a winner, the judgement will be based on who the Board has felt did a better job of RPing. Winners will then be sent out to writers, then attacks, interferances, and other such additions placed into the finalized match product. Any complaints about winners can be sent to the
Board. If a complaint about a particular winner is recieved, a rematch will be rescheduled. Continous complaints about losses will result in termination.
Rule #2: RPs must not contain any anti-semetic, racial, or other such unneccessary comment. Sexual comments are allowed on a very strignent basis (you are not allowed to continually make sexual referances). Any comment that personally insults a person's race, color, or religon will be deemed null and void and the person who wrote will immediately be terminated from the eFed. Likewise, searching an opponent's RP to find error such as these too frequently will also result in penalty and/or termination.
Rule #3: You are limited to the use of your e-wrestler and him alone, unless given permission directly from either the President or the Owner.
Creation of "temporary" characters (such as your e-wrestler's wife, a friend, etc.) is allowed. Use of managers/valets in RPs is also acceptable. Use of another's characters, a real person/wrestler, or another fed other than the feds belonging to the UltimateHWF.net Network will result in a penalty as well as voiding of the RP.
Rule #4: No RP lengtheners are allowed. An RP lengthener is defined as anything meant to make your RP look longer, such as a copyright statement, triple spacing, or anything else deemed such by the Board.
Failure to comply will result in voiding of roleplay. Rule #5: Curses are banned from subject headers. Do not make mention of them at all. Curses, however, are allowed in your roleplays. We encourage that you censor the e-mail (**** out the words), but will not punish those who do not.
Rule #6: Asking the Board for an opinion on something is prohibited. The Board is required to give out no opinions on anything at any time. Asking, for example, if your RP was good or not, will result in a punishment of some sort. Rule #7: You must send an application in for review in order to be considered for acceptance. Not everyone is accepted, so do not get your hopes up. Fill out the application as best you can, making sure to include a sample roleplay, even if you have never done one before.
Rule #8: No unoriginal wrestler names are permitted in the UHWF. Unoriginal wrestler names are defined as any celebrity or wrestler that is somewhat of a household name. You must create your own original character in order to be reviewed. Do not choose a name that is even somewhat similar to a real wrestler or celebrity.
UHWF EVENTS Tuesday Clash - This is the main event for the UHWF. All of the major feuds and matches will be during this show.
Saturday Night Disaster - This is the secondary show, like Smackdown or Thunder. There will rarely be any title changes during this show, if there even are any title matches. Feuds will be affected during this show, but not as much so as during Tuesday Clash.
Pay-Per-Views - There will be a Pay Per View(PPV) every month. This will have the most exciting action of the month at it. Almost every title will be defended at the PPV's. The new feuds will start to unfold and old ones will be completed at PPV's. We will (hopefully) have flash promos for each PPV.
UHWF TITLES Titles Available - There are currently 6 titles in the UHWF. Here is a list of them. They are in no particular order.
UHWF World Heavyweight Title UHWF Tag Team Titles UHWF Women's Title UHWF Cruiserweight Title UHWF Hardcore Title UHWF Hardcore Tag Team Titles ANGLES/FEUDS We value angles and feuds highly in the UHWF. If you have a suggestion for a feud or angle, make sure to email me and the other handler in question about it. We'll go over it and if it sounds good, I'll incorporate it into the cards. Be original with your feuds and angles.
Don't rip off other feds ideas. And, if you're in a feud or angle with somebody, and they win. But you roleplayed better, don't complain about it. Your loss furthered the angle and you'll usually get payback somewhere in the future against that same person.
