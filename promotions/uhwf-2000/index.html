Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Ultimate Hardcore Wrestling Federation opened 14 February 2000 as the official e-fed of the UltimateHWF.net network, and its own information pamphlet gives an unusually candid account of the false starts behind it: the Hardcore Wrestling Federation launched in July 1999 and lasted about four months before its owner “couldn't handle it anymore”; a first attempt to revive it as the Ultimate Hardcore Wrestling Federation “didn't really get off the ground any and died right away” for lack of resources. Only after buying the domain name ultimatehwf.net and teaching himself CGI did the owner launch the fed that survives on the record, about a month and a half later.

Its own staff table names a President, Chris Coniglio, running the league day to day, and a card-writing and angle-creating desk under Michael Lauxford. It offered six championships — World Heavyweight, Tag Team, Women's, Cruiserweight, Hardcore and Hardcore Tag Team — across two weekly shows, Tuesday Clash for its major feuds and Saturday Night Disaster as its lesser show, plus a monthly pay-per-view. Its rules barred unoriginal wrestler names outright: “Do not choose a name that is even somewhat similar to a real wrestler or celebrity.”

No card, roster or result document survives past the opening pamphlet. Whether the promised Tuesday Clash and Saturday Night Disaster shows ever ran, and how or when this run of the UHWF ended, are not known.
