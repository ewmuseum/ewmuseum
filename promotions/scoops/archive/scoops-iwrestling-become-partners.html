Title: Scoops/iwrestling become partners Al_scoops For Immediate Release [headline] Wrestling Gets Bigger [text] New York, NY March 31, 1999 Extreme Interactive Media Inc (EIM). has acquired pro wrestling site www.iwrestling.com. EIM which also owns the worlds largest independent pro wrestling site www.ScoopsCentral.com will keep the sites separate of each other and allow for independent reporting and content. "We are please to be working in partnership with iwrestling because of their strong fan base and talented staff." Says Scoops COO [name withheld]. The two sites will have a combined audience that apporaches 15 million page views per month. EIM will allow both sites to share resources in order to deliver a high quality online experince to the fan base of each site. [name withheld] the President of EIM commented, "By the end of the year, we expect to be doing close to 1 million page views per day. We have reached critical mass and are now seeing expontial growth." As pro wrestling continues its metoric rise, the fan base on the internet is bigger than ever. This exciting entertainment form has taken the TV-viewing world by storm. It's fun and it's unpredictable. It gives guys (and some gals too) something to discuss and speculate about between those gut-wrenching bouts. It's the latest rage among at-home spectator sports, attracting 40 million fans who watch it on cable, mostly males in the 16-34 age range. "I am excited to be working on the same team with Scoops Central. Scoops is the standard for wrestling content on the net and they are the biggest for a reason." says iwrestling president [name withheld].
