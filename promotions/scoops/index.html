The Monday-Night-Wars-era wrestling news portal that hosted the CyberSlam house e-wrestling game as one of its own sections, run off a shared content engine with its own reader board. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Scoops Wrestling Networks was a news operation before it was anything else, and its own surviving pages still carry that order plainly: a “SITE” menu linking the Scoops Home Page, CyberSlam, Contact Us, Reader's Mail and Yesterday's News as one integrated address, with the house e-wrestling game held as a single entry among them, not a second site of its own. Every one of its pages, news copy and game paperwork alike, came off the same shared engine — a CGI script serving templates built in Adobe GoLive, switched between sections by nothing more than a directory= parameter in the URL.

Its reader board, News/TIS/The Board, gated itself plainly at the front door: a reporter with a password could post, a fan could enter and read but never post, and the posting form beneath asked for a name without ever requiring a real one. The one release the board itself preserved records the portal's own business history rather than a wrestling news tip — Extreme Interactive Media's own announcement, dated 31 March 1999, that Scoops and iwrestling.com had become partners under one parent company, each site continuing to run separately.

How Scoops itself ended is recorded on its own game section's board, not its own newsdesk — the portal's root-site pages held here stop being captured in October 2000, before the closing was announced. CyberSlam's own board carries it: on 2 February 2001, an editorial posted there says plainly that “snowball.com is shutting down Scoops, merging the news section with their's and obliterating that which we have come to know as Cyberslam” — the game section's own death notice, naming the portal it belonged to as what was actually being closed.

Affiliations

CyberSlam Wrestling Fantasy League

Owned by Scoops (1996)
CyberSlam was a section of the Scoops Wrestling Networks portal, not a site of its own, and its own paperwork makes the dependency structural rather than decorative: a member had to register as a "Scoops Trooper" before creating a Slammer at all, the monthly champion's prize in the 1998 FAQ is "a Scoops Tee Shirt", and payments were processed by the portal's own parent company. When the portal was sold and shut down in February 2001, the game went with it.
