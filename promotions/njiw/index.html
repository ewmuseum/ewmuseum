Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

New Japan Internet Wrestling announced itself as a real-wrestler promotion, self-describing as “not currently an active promotion” in a 29 July 1997 promo for its own planned debut: Hell in Hiroshima, a 16 August 1997 battle royal staged, on the page, at the Bachuran Temple in East Hiroshima, Japan. The main event was a ten-man Heavyweight Invitational Brawl inside a cage, the first two competitors to touch a title suspended above the ring advancing to a one-on-one final to crown the fed's first ICON Champion. The promo doubled as an open call to other federations' own presidents to submit a representative for the brawl.

A cross-promotion affiliate, Philadelphia E-Wrestling, is named in NJIW's own surviving corpus without disclosing more than its own two founders' involvement. NJIW's last surviving document, dated 3 August 1997, is its own wrestler application form, still recruiting for the roster the Hiroshima card would need.

No recap, result, or reign survives to say whether Hell in Hiroshima was ever staged, or who if anyone became NJIW's first ICON Champion.
