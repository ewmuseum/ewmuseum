Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The International Wrestling Organization dated its own founding to a specific moment: “officially founded at 8:00 p.m. pacific on tuesday,” its founder wrote the next day, 9 September 1998, meaning Tuesday the 8th. It ran its recruiting drive from a personal JPS.net homepage rather than a federation site of its own.

Its pitch was narrow and specific: it wanted “serious roleplayers who will rp 1 time a week,” called itself booked, and claimed “an excellent match writer.” The same notice promised recruits it would “never disapoint” because it catered to its wrestlers.

The same call went out eight times over the next two weeks with no new content added, and one of the later posts named a first card for “next Monday” — 21 September 1998.

No result or further post survives to say whether that card happened. Its own last surviving document, from 20 September, is still an open recruiting call, and the record stops there.
