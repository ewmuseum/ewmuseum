KBH Tim McMichaels vs The Cop -The Cop was in control, going for his patented top rope belly to belly suplex, but KBH landed on his feet and then nailed The Cop with the KBH Kick and got the 1, 2, 3! Yaz vs Porn Star -A seesaw of a match that saw Yaz beat Porn Star after the Red Warrior came down and took out Yaz's leg. Porn Star and Red Warrior continued to work on it, Porn Star put Yaz in a Figure Four Leglock for at least 5 minutes. Officials finally broke it off. Razor vs Crock Master -Crock Master was in total control of this match. He had just hit a power bomb and was going for the Crock Bomb. He faked the landing, landed on his feet, but Razor caught him with a small package for the 1, 2, 3! Wildcat Jimmy J vs Cage -This was an easy match for Cage as he just beat the crap out of Wildcat throughout the match, Cage finally got Wildcat into the Cage Cutter and got the 1, 2, 3! Thursday at the Terrordome Schedule Semi-Finals.. KBH vs Yaz Razor vs Cage Also..
