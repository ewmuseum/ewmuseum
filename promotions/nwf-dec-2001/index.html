Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Network Wrestling Federation was born on a December 2001 Showdown, its ownership consortium's CEO unveiled on air and its announcers, Dennis Cook and Thomas N. Tyler, calling the first match of a promotion with no champions yet.

By February 2002 Showdown was on the road, from Pauley Pavilion at UCLA to Pittsburgh and to the Rose Garden in Portland, building three tournaments at once toward its first pay-per-view, Clean Slate: the World Heavyweight title, the World Tag Team titles and the Women's title.

Its owner by then was Stacy Ka$h, and her feud with the Blood Brothers ran through every show. She had Ryan Rankin break Kevin Richards' leg, forced Jonathan Jacobs back into his Jammer rapper gimmick, and watched Jacobs answer as The Vagabond, spray-painting Rankin's back and reaching the World title semifinals as the Jammer anyway.

The last surviving Showdown, of 17 February 2002, filled the semifinals a week before Clean Slate. No result of the pay-per-view is held.
