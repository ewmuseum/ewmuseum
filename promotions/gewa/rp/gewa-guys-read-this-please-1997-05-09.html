GEWA — Roleplay

Guys, Read This PLEASE

9 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please help me improve my flashing. read this and send me a critique at < [detail withheld] > Thanks, Jay ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~`` (Red Raven is leaning against the wall of the mansion. He spies Retribution going back to the Compound. Raven is wearing blue jeans with black hiking shoes. His shirt is a blue t-shirt from Niagara Falls. It's got a cool indian saying on it with a cool picture (I can't do it justice by describing it). As he watches the white hummer pull out of the driveway, spewing rocks and dust in the air, he sighs) RR: Another day has gone buy with the same fools as before. Such is one called G-Money, or as I like to call him, G-String. We've met before. Then you had the help of a mercenary who is now reduced to nothing. Do you remember the hospital, G-Money? Was it nice there? Do you want to go there again? I'm sure with your 'tremendous amount of money' that you can secure yourself a wing and get it named after yourself. You see, now that you became 'big time' you have reduced dramatically. Money, we will meet. You better hope it's not on my terms. (he smiles and walks around the corner of the building to the front. He opens the door and walks in, holding the door open for the camerawoman (I am not chauvinistic sp?). When the camerawoman gets inside, she takes a wide view of the magnificent mansion. The camera finally settles down on a television that Red Raven has turned on. It shows the Retribution flash from the baseball game. the part on it shows that Retribution asking/telling Raven that they shocked the GEWA) RR: Shocked, a very interesting term of words. Not that it matters. It seems that it worked it's effects. We have Mr. Belsito challenging moi'. Belsito, I've accepted your challenges many times before and I've challenged you already. It seems you ignore that and hope that I DON'T accept the next time. Feel like a drink? (The camera shakes no, but follows Red Raven into the kitchen, which seems like an awfully long walk. He opens the fridge and sees it filled with Mellow Yellow and Mountain Dew. Then he opens a cupboard and sees it filled with Vodka.) RR: Well, I guess that they don't have any of my stuff. Hmm. I guess I'll go for traditional water. (he takes out a sports cap water bottle and takes off the cap. Then he goes to the sink and fills the bottle with water from the tap. He puts the cap back on and turns it to make sure it's nice and tight. He then pulls the sports cap open and takes a long sip) RR: Not as good as OJ, but still good. You have to watch these people in the GEWA now. You have people calling me a has-been, but it seems I am on a winning streak lately, as hard as it is to believe. The one who seems washed up lately, is the true weak link of the Defeated Alliance, Pyro. Belsito, I know you believe that you can win. Good for you. More power to you. But as I see it, the Elite has no chance in hell of winning with Pyro in it. (VOTE FOR ME :) You lost 3 very powerful members in myself, Bordeau, and FF. Belsito, I have nothing to prove to you. That's why I was invited into the Elite. That's why that when EVERY single alliance formed, I was invited into everyone except Collection inc. People can say all they want in flashes, but I know the truth. However, Antonio, you have much to prove. To get your struggling singles career off the ground. To make up for almost losing your GEWA career to my friend Bordeau. You see, I don't have to beat you more than you really have to beat me. Especially after your 'pal' Pyro has lost to me 3 times (he takes another sip of water after speaking that long tirade) RR: I feel like moving to 'Vegas' don't you? I heard that it's nice this time of year. Plenty of gold. Cage, you said I don't respect you. Is that all you want from me? My respect? Put the gold on the line on MMM. You'll get respect. I'll give you my share of the winner's purse when I take the title home. If I recall right. a certain newcomer said that I wouldn't beat him and it would earn him the respect when he beat me. Just travel back around 2 1/2 almost 3 weeks ago when I beat Ivan on MMM. I'll give you some credit. You were nominated for Best Newcomer (Vote for me :). That does mean something, I'm sure. Cage, we will meet. I think I'll be nice though. Whatever stips you want, you got it. This way we are playing on equal terms. Craft veteran vs. eager newcomer. (he sips the water and puts down the sports cap. He opens the fridge again, shifts around some M.Y. and Mountain Dew and places the water in there) RR: Just a couple more rambling thoughts. Organ Grinder. I am washed-up has-been. A couple weeks ago, you might have been right. But all of a sudden, I am voted Most Improved. I've been working out. I might not talk as much as you, but that's ok. I will be watching in interest on Saturday Night. Major Unknown, should I be scared too? You're right. You haven't created a lot of heat, but you have caught my interest, whether you know it or not. It seems to me you are going too a lot of my associates in Mobster, Tank, Bordeau. That's what interests me. Are your motives all out in the open? (Red Raven walks through a long hallway) RR: Sorry to leave the flash hanging, kind of. But I got places to go. I have to buy my mother a gift for Mom's day. So, that is kind of more important that this flash. Au Revior!
