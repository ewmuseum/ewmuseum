GEWA: Yes, That GEWA. It's Back.

29 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Back in the mid-90s, when WCW was a viable wrestling promotion and Doink the Clown amused WWF fans with his wacky antics, a humble Canadian outfit rose to the top of the e-wrestling world. During its two-year run between 1996 and 1998, the Global E-Wrestling Alliance drew acclaim from members and the Internet community alike for its wit, innovation and humour.
Now, after a two-year-hiatus, the GEWA rides again. In many respects, this is the same GEWA that made "fist to the nads" and "boot to the head" common household sayings. It's still run by owner [name withheld] and writer [name withheld], the two guys responsible for the GEWA of the past.
But in many respects, it's a whole new GEWA. After two years in mothballs, this ain't a Global E-Wrestling Federation anymore. There are no more filled arenas and baseball stadiums.
No, the GEWA of today is the Guelph E-Wrestling Alliance, an upstart regional fed based in Ontario, Canada's agriculture belt which aims to recapture its past glory.
Think you've got what it takes to make your mark in the GEWA? Think you've got the grapefruits to earn the GEWA Heavyweight Title?
Climb aboard the combine -- it's going to be a hell of a ride the second time around. http://www.v-sports.net/gewa [detail withheld]
