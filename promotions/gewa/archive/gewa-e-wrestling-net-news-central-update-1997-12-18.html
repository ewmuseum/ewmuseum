E-Wrestling Net News Central Update

18 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
E-Wrestling Net News Central, your weekly-updated site on the WWW for e-wrestling news, rumours, ratings and information, is pleased to announce that the site has expanded to cover 11 of the internet's top e-wrestling feds: the GEWA, CIWF, DSWF, GFWA, IPWF, NAWC, NeWC, UCW, WIWF, WOWA and last, but not least, WSCW. Those interested in e-wrestling and especially those persons looking to join quality e-wrestling feds are invited to visit E-Wrestling Net News Central at http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Track/5820 to see what the aforementioned e-feds have to offer. Is five minutes of time a week too much an expense to expose your e-fed to the members of 11 other e-feds and prospective e-wrestling gamers? If not, then you too can post your e-fed's news, rumours and ratings for free on E-Wrestling Net News Central. All you have to do is visit EWNNC, click on the "join" link, and fill out the online application form. It's just that simple. Space is limited, so act now n form at http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Track/5820/applic.html to ensure your e-fed's spot on E-Wrestling Net News Central. Wishing everyone the best of the holiday season, [name withheld] Co-Webmaster, EWNNC Premium Section Columnist, 1wrestling.com
