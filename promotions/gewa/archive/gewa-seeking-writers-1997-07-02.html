GEWA Seeking Writers

2 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The GEWA has been existence for just over a year, and in that time has built itself into one of the top TNM feds on the Internet. Now, I'm sure at this point most of you are derisively saying "simmed fed" and turning away in disgust. If you are, fine. If you're not, continue reading. The GEWA is transforming itself into a fully booked fed, as we are gradually moving away from writing matches off of the TNM logfile. Accordingly, realizing the heavier writing load that this entails, we are seeking quality writers who can contribute on a weekly basis. A bit about the GEWA -- it started up as a one-man operation on June 28, 1996. The premise has always been that roleplaying quality equals a strong push, something that a lot of simmed feds don't worry about. So if you're wondering about the roleplaying quality in the league, believe me when I say there are some good ones. Some of the best I've ever seen, in fact. If you're interested in a position as a writer, contact me at [detail withheld] , check out the webpage at http://www.geocities.com/Track/Colosseum/5725 or drop by DALnet #GEWA if you use IRC. Experience is an asset, but not a requirement. We're looking for quality, not an established "name". Thanks for your time. GEWA President [name withheld]
