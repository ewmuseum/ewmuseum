The two men started the match by exchanging some amateur takedowns, as well as some drop toeholds and leapfrogs. Both men were applauded for their speed and agility. Southward and Metal shook hands, and Southward chopped Metal down. Southward whipped Metal into the ropes, but Metal ducked a clothesline and dropkicked Southward down. Southward kicked up and hit Metal with a flying forearm, then executed a quick snap suplex for a two-count. Metal kicked up as well, blocked a right by Southward, and began chopping Southward down every time he got back up. On the last attempt, Southward ducked and grabbed Metal's leg, taking him down in a dragon screw. Southward whipped Metal into the corner, but got nothing but a boot to the face, and was hit with a swinging DDT by Metal, then locked in a figure-four. Southward was able to make his way to the ropes, and the referee forced Metal to break the move. Metal picked Southward back up and began working on his leg, then whipped Southward into the ropes. However, Metal made the mistake of putting his head down, and Southward executed a swinging neckbreaker. Southward hit another two snap suplexes on Metal, and then a beautiful German suplex for a very close two-count. Southward whipped Metal into the ropes and tried his spinning leg lariat, but Metal grabbed the ropes, and rolled up Southward for two. As Southward got up, Metal dropkicked him down, then locked on a Scorpion deathlock. Southward tried pushing up out of it, but couldn't, but was able to inch his way to the ropes, with Metal breaking just before the five count. Metal tried to execute the Mash Pit on Southward, but Southward punched Metal in the gut, flipped out of the move, and turned Metal over and hit a Michinoku Driver #2 for another two count. An exploder suplex later, and another two count for Southward. Jayla was getting restless on the floor, but Rude looked on as Southward tried whipping Metal into the corner, but was reversed, and Metal went into the corner and was rolled up for another two. As Southward complained to the referee, Metal jumped up and executed a Victory Roll for two, and then a small package for another two. Southward tried another snap suplex, but Metal flipped behind and German suplexed Southward for two again. Metal tried the Mash Pit again, and was able to hit the move, but Southward was in the ropes. As Metal complained to the ref, Southward jumped him from behind and locked in the Southmission, but Metal got to the ropes. Both men got back up, and Metal tried another Mash Pit, but Southward got a foot on the turnbuckles, kicked off, and rolled up Metal in an odd cradle for the three-count.
