PCCW Sunday Night for December 7, Part 3

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LAMPLEY: We're ready for our main event, it'll be for the PCCW World television title. Ajax Shiva, the champion, taking on Paul-Dog. Let's get some comments from the challenger. -- {THE FOLLOWING ANNOUNCEMENT HAS BEEN PAID FOR BY THE fWo, 'CAUSE SAL IS A CHEAP BASTARD. [Paul-Dog is standing in the locker room wearing a flannel shirt, a black fWo t-shirt under the flannel shirt, and a pair of khaki pants. Of course, he is accessorized by typical Paul-Dog sunglasses. He's trying to flash gang signs as the cameraman gives him the high sign.] Paul-Dog: Hey, gringo...Ajax...I see we getting a match for the TV Belt, mang...we gonna get it on, vato...and I'm gonna give you a fWo whooping. I ain't the guy with the experience, gringo, but I'm still the guy with the (chops at his crotch) STUFF. The fWo, mang, we are raza...you mang, you just a sink cleaner. After I wipe the ring with you, I gonna wipe the sink with you, the toilet with you, and I even gonna sell you in a can. Vato, your time has expired, 'cause you're looking at the new breed....and the fWo is (trying and miserably failing to flash a gang sign) the new breed in this town. See, gringo, we much smarter than those guys in WCW...that think that everything is 'for life'....we much smarter than that, puto...we know that this sport is a business, mang...so we know that you're fWo....you're fWo 'till you get a better offer, gringo!!! So I gonna go out there with my best sunglasses on...I gonna pimp on your girl...and I gonna take the PCCW World TV Title. And I'm gonna dedicate this match to one man...and no, it's not "Hollywood Bombed" Uni....it's the one..and only...Jobbo!!!!!!! You see, gringos, Jobbo is the mang that you've gotta look up to...'cause you see, he's a lot like the fWo. He fights for what is right...just like us. His name is on TV a lot....you can almost hear our names on TV a lot...you see? The fWo and Jobbo have a lot in common....and because Jobbo is our inspiration, Ajax, gringo, sink cleaner, I gonna take your title and your woman. Gringo, you gonna wish you never heard of me. -- <The fWo theme plays, and the crowd boos.> DOUGLAS: This contest is set for one fall, and it is for the PCCW World television championship! Introducing first..representing the Face World Order..from Flint, Michigan..weighing 242 pounds..PAUL-DOG! LAMPLEY: Paul-Dog coming in..I believe he's saying something to you, Gambler. GAMBLER: Sounds like a mixture of Spanish and English..BAD Spanish, too. LAMPLEY: What's he saying? GAMBLER: NO idea. I doubt he even knows. LAMPLEY: I think he just called himself a donkey. <"Ain't My Bitch" by Metallica plays, and the crowd cheers like mad.> DOUGLAS: And his opponent..accompanied by Angel Lust..weighing 340 pounds..the PCCW World television champion..AJAX SHIVA! Shiva went right in and pounded on the overmatched Paul-Dog, punching him down and hitting him with rights and lefts. When Paul-Dog started to fight back, Shiva tossed him outside and distracted Tirantes as Angel came in and bodyslammed Paul-Dog, then tossed him in. Shiva became to systematically take Paul-Dog apart, working on his knee, then on his back. Paul-Dog, however, started to fight back, hitting a reverse neckbreaker, then locking in a sleeper. Shiva got out of the hold, but was suplexed for two, then piledriven. Shiva kicked out with authority, got right up and chokeslammed Paul-Dog, covering for two. Paul-Dog went to the floor and went after Angel Lust, giving Shiva an opening to plancha Paul-Dog. LAMPLEY: Oh crud.. GAMBLER: Here comes the Marauder. Tirantes is out with him, Shiva in the ring with Paul-Dog..DEADLIEST SIN ON PAUL-DOG! NO REFEREE! LAMPLEY: Shiva has him beat! Tirantes finally in...ONE! TWO! NO! GAMBLER: Paul-Dog just got lucky! LAMPLEY: ANOTHER DEADLIEST SIN BY SHIVA! Paul-Dog is nearly unconscious...Shiva pointing at Marauder and saying that this one is for him! GAMBLER: Shiva is setting him up for a THIRD Deadliest Sin! C'mon! LAMPLEY: Shiva going for the Deadliest Sin..MARAUDER TRIPS HIM! NO! HE'S HOLDING HIM! PAUL-DOG ON TOP! ONE! TWO! THREE!!! GAMBLER: WHAT THE?! LAMPLEY: PAUL-DOG HAS WON THE TITLE! MARAUDER JUST ROBBED SHIVA OF THE TITLE! DOUGLAS: The winner of this bout...and *NEEEEEW* PCCW WORLD TELEVISION CHAMPION...PAUL-DOG! LAMPLEY: Shiva is angry, Paul-Dog is out, and we have a new World television champion! GAMBLER: The fWo is coming out to help Paul-Dog to his feet..Marauder's out of here, Shiva's going after him! LAMPLEY: Shiva's gonna try and kill the Marauder..fans, we're outta time! For the Gambler, Fredo Capone, and Liz Oya, I'm Vince Lampley, see you next time! © 1997 Pro-Continental Championship Wrestling
