PCCW Sunday Night for December 7, Part 1

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<The remix of "More Human Than Human" by White Zombie plays, with the letters P, C, C, and W showing on screen in time, then the PCCW Sunday Night logo. A montage of clips of PCCW superstars plays along with the song, then we go to the Pro-Continental Championship Wrestling logo, then PCCW Arena with Vince Lampley and The Gambler.> VINCE LAMPLEY: Welcome to PCCW Sunday Night! I'm Vince Lampley along with the Gambler, and tonight, the PCCW World television championship will be on the line, as the champion, Ajax Shiva, defends his title against the fWo's Paul-Dog! THE GAMBLER: Paul-Dog better bring all his raza to the show tonight, cause he'll need them to put away Shiva! LAMPLEY: Also, the team that's had a turnaround as far as fan support goes, Los Gringos Locos, will step into the ring with none other than the Steiner Brothers! And we know for a fact that Law & Order are in the house.. GAMBLER: They're in Springfield for In Your House?! WHY?! LAMPLEY: They're HERE in PCCW Arena, Gambler! GAMBLER: Oh..whoops. LAMPLEY: Also, WCW World television champion Perry Saturn will square off with Jack Cole, and John Kronus has been spotted in Las Vegas. However, ECW head honcho Paul E. Dangerously has put out a restraining order on Kronus, stating that if he shows up here, he will be arrested! GAMBLER: Aw, c'mon, let 'em go! LAMPLEY: All that and much, much more..right now, let's go to the ring for our first bout here tonight on PCCW Sunday Night! <"Voodoo People" by Prodigy plays, and the crowd boos.> MICK DOUGLAS: This first contest on PCCW Sunday Night is set for one fall! Introducing first..weighing 287 pounds..MR. VOODOO! LAMPLEY: Mr. Voodoo back on PCCW Sunday Night after a lengthy absence..he's here to take on another newcomer, Daddy. Gambler, what do you know about Daddy? GAMBLER: Daddy's a drunk! I mean, I go home, and he's sleeping in my bed with some different chick every night that I don't even know and he drinks all my beer and eats all my pudding and.. LAMPLEY: Gambler, what the HELL are you talking about?! GAMBLER: Uh..I don't know much about him, that's all that I'm saying.. LAMPLEY: Well, perhaps we can find out a bit about him. Let's go to Daddy, who had these prerecorded comments. -- DADDY: Mr. Voodoo...... the big bad spellbinding creature. Well, let me tell you something, "Mr. Voodoo" setting my feet on fire, or making some black shit run down my face doesn't scare me. what scares me is the fact that you actually think you can beat me. No, that doesn't scare me actually, to tell you the truth, it infuriates me to think that you actually have the SACK to step in the ring with Daddy.....That just plain pisses me off. wanna know why? Because you're a lousy piece of shit and you don't belong within a mile, nay, within the fucking area code of me. So, you can just get your little fat ass voodoo bullshit in the ring, shake around for a little bit and try to wrestle me, but in the end, Daddy will be on top. -- <The PCCW Daddy theme plays, and the crowd boos.> DOUGLAS: And his opponent..weighing 289 pounds..DADDY! Daddy ran in and went right to work on Mr. Voodoo, who was still in his prematch ritual. Voodoo tried hitting Daddy with the skull, but Daddy caught it and tossed it out to a lucky fan. Voodoo punched away at Daddy, who just laughed at him and belly-to-belly suplexed him. Voodoo got up and was met with two clotheslines. Voodoo was rocked with European uppercuts, staggering into the corner, where he was put up top and superplexed for two. Daddy went up top for an elbowdrop, but missed, and Voodoo started to fight back, hitting with kicks and punches, finally legdropping Daddy. Voodoo began his ritual and set up Daddy for the shoulderbreaker, but Daddy slipped out and behind him, executing a neckbreaker, then finishing off Voodoo with the DaddyBomb, a running Ligerbomb. DOUGLAS: Here is your winner..DADDY! LAMPLEY: Daddy picks up a very impressive win over Mr. Voodoo... GAMBLER: Hold it. LAMPLEY: What happened to the lights. GAMBLER: WHOA! LAMPLEY: Fire in the ring! Fire in the ring! The lights are back on..there's a fire where Mr. Voodoo was, he's gone! And now the Dragon Squad is in to put out the fire. GAMBLER: Where'd Mr. Voodoo go? LAMPLEY: No idea.. GAMBLER: Boy, you're a big help. LAMPLEY: The fire is out..but the mystery has begun. Let's go up to Liz Oya in the ring. [Oya] After another exciting show in New Jersey, the PCCW is hotter then ever!!! And in the middle of the chaos one man stepped forward claiming he is the *one* champion in this great federation...so with that being said please welcome.... ["Time For Some Action" by RedMan starts to play as a JUSTICE chants get's drowned out by the BOOOOOOOS!!!] [Oya] ..the PCCW Extreme Champion...the Aerial Marauder!!!! The Marauder steps in the ring. [Oya] You sucessfully defended the title at Jersey Jam when you defeated Raven, but you did it with some unexpected help from World Champ Tony Marconi. He fooled all of us...including YOU... into thinking that he had joined JUSTICE...then he attacked you in a move that had to be painful...and a little embarassing...any comments? [The Marauder stares grimly at the camera for a long time...then he suddenly starts to laugh!!!] [Marauder] HAHAHAHAHA...that Tony sure did pull a fast one on me didn't he?? Yeah I must admit that I was tricked...and I paid the cost. But I can see why he did it. I mean think about it, every time Marconi has seen me in the ring, seconds later he has ended up flat on his back courtesy of the Aerial Express!! I'm sure our "Champion" had to save face some how. But then, after pulling that brilliant move...what does he do next? [Oya] I have no idea. [Marauder] He wrestles Ajax Shiva in what was probably the WORST championship match in PCCW history!!! And these are people that this federation wants to put in the same class as ME??? *These* are the people that the PCCW wants to call champions?? I have said it time and time again...in this fed there is only one champ...and that's me!! And at Jersey Jam, Marconi and Shiva proved my point with me having to do anything!!! [Oya] Those are very bold words... [Marauder] You ain't heard nothing yet Liz...try this one for size!!! Tony Marconi, I want you to face me one on one...title versus title. And when I lay you out AGAIN...I can walk out with the PCCW World Title!! So Sal Capone, I want to take a brief break from your pasta dinner...take that pudgy little hand of yours...and make the contract. And then Marconi, I want you to stop your hand from shaking long enough to actually SIGN IT! Once you do, your title reign is as good as done...AND JUSTICE WILL BE SERVED!!!! [The Marauder leaves the ring.] LAMPLEY: Looks like the war between the Marauder and Tony Marconi has yet to be settled..we'll be hearing later on from the PCCW World heavyweight champion..but right now, let's go back to the ring! <No music plays, and the crowd boos.> DOUGLAS: This contest is set for one fall. Introducing first..from Tonga..weighing 330 pounds..HAKU THE GIANT! LAMPLEY: Wow. Another man in the line of great stars such as Meng.. GAMBLER: How can you say that with a straight face, Vin? LAMPLEY: I just did. GAMBLER: Sigh.. <"Natural Born Killaz" by Dr. Dre and Ice Cube plays, and the boos only grow.> DOUGLAS: And his opponent..from California..weighing 295 p-WHOA! Killa almost ran over Douglas, swinging the cane at him to get out of the way, and went right for Haku. The two hit each other with flurries of kicks and punches... LAMPLEY: Killa is yelling at Jobbo as they're taking him out of here.. GAMBLER: He better look behind him! LAMPLEY: He turns around..RIGHT INTO A SPINEBUSTER! Haku the Giant with a spinebuster..into the Giant Swing! GAMBLER: Around and around she goes, when she'll stop, nobody..OW. LAMPLEY: Killa knows..he gets flung right into the turnbuckle! Cover..1...2...3! Haku the Giant wins! DOUGLAS: Here is your winner..HAKU THE GIANT! LAMPLEY: Haku the Giant gets the win..but the story here has to be with Damien Legend. What's going on with him? GAMBLER: Why're you asking me? Get Liz back there and ask him, sheesh. LAMPLEY: Liz isn't in the back, but Joey Yerdon is, and he's standing by. -- (Cut to "Mr. Brutality" Joey Yerdon in the back of a locker room.) Mr. B: Forchman, I told you that once you steped in that ring you were in for an ass kicking and what can I say? I was right! The match didn't last as nearly as long as I'd hoped it would, but Sal said to make it quick. Anyway, Forchman I'm through with you. You thought you could take me and the only thing you took was a beating at my hands. Frehley, on the other hand, I'm just starting with you. It seems that PCCW's own "fighting fairy" wants a piece of the pie, so to speak. So to you I say, bring it on! You want a fight you've got one! Anytime, anyplace! Weapons, tables, ladders, doesn't matter to me. Career, respect, hell I'll put my own mother on the line just to get a shot at you! Or no stipulations at all, makes no damn difference to me. Just sign on the dotted line. IWL, I maybe new here, but I know that this is undoubtedly the greatest wrestling federation on earth and I'm willing to fight for it. I'll put my body on the line just to make sure that the PCCW legacy lives on into the 21st Century. (Yerdon turns away, then comes back.) Mr. B: Oh, and Kit, I had a wonderful time today. You know my hotel room, I'll see you around nine? I'll be ordering some bubbily for us, so please hurry. (Turns and leaves) -- LAMPLEY: Some very poignant comments from Joey Yerdon.. GAMBLER: Hey, what's going on with the IWL thing, anyway? LAMPLEY: I don't know..the Terminator's supposed to be here later to answer any response they may have, but apparently he has yet to arrive..we'll have to see. For now, let's go back up to Mick Douglas! <"Danger Zone" by Kenny Loggins plays, and the crowd boos.> DOUGLAS: This tag team contest is set for one fall! Introducing first..representing the Danger Zone Alliance..and accompanied by Chameleon..at a combined weight of 523 pounds..Michael Jeffires..Andrew Saunders..ER! LAMPLEY: ER making their return to PCCW since the attack by Perfection's Stunade...you can see the bandage on the arm of Michael Jeffries. GAMBLER: Sheesh, he's milking that injury more than Iron Mike Sharpe. LAMPLEY: He is not...let's hear from their opponents. -- <Screen shows Johnny Razorblade flying head first out of a hotel room then the screen pans back to reveal Elite Force watching it and laughing uncontrolably> Holliday: Hey John Boy, hows that head ache?!?!? <More laughter from the group> Malone: But now on to bigger and better things. This week on PCCWSN, the return of team that fears no one and surrenders to no one, Elite Force. Beck: And who better to face than Johnny's buds, ER. Emlee: You know, Anthony Edwards has done anything good since Top Gun.... Beck: Wrong ER, dear... Malone: Same principle though....We are going to literally do surgery on a couple of henchmen. Holliday: PCCW, get the stetchers ready....My men are on a mission to hurt someone. And win, lose, or draw, the are going to hurt ER. That you can count on. -- <"Paradise City" by Guns & Roses plays, and the crowd boos.> DOUGLAS: And their opponents..representing Elite Force..and accompanied by Dave Holliday and Emlee..at a combined weight of 520 pounds..Mark Malone..Andrew Beck..ELITE FORCE! Malone and Beck started out slowly with ER, with Beck starting with Jeffries, who was favoring his arm. Beck worked on the injured arm, applying several armbreakers and finally a Fujiwara armbar. Jeffries made it to the ropes, and Beck broke on four. A lockup and a slam by Jeffires had him yelling due to the pain, and he tagged out. Saunders came in and began working over Beck, slumping him in the corner with kicks to the midsection, then whipping him acros the ring for a clothesline. Saunders did it twice more, then went off the ropes, but Emlee grabbed Saunders' ankle. Chameleon went after Emlee, touching off a brief catfight, while Beck clotheslined Saunders to the floor. As Beck distracted the ref, Malone attacked Saunders on the floor, DDTing him on a chair. Malone tossed him in, and Beck came off the top with an elbowdrop for two before Jeffries saved him. A brawl began with Malone and Jeffries, and Emlee came in and smacked Saunders with a chair. Chameleon dragged Emlee to the floor, and Jeffries tried separating them. In the meantime, Beck and Malone executed their finisher, the Death Blow, and Beck covered for the threecount. DOUGLAS: Here are your winners..ELITE FORCE! LAMPLEY: Elite Force with the win, and Emlee and Chameleon are still on the floor! GAMBLER: Hold my headset, I'll be right back! LAMPLEY: You stay right here! GAMBLER: Killjoy. LAMPLEY: Elite Force and ER are separating them...alright...as we get everything settled here, let's hear from one guy with attitude.. GAMBLER: Diesel and Shawn? LAMPLEY: NO. Blade Dryden. -- [SCENE: Backstage after PCCW Jersey Jam. Blade Dryden deposits a coin into a Pepsi machine, and unfortunatly, the drink doesn't come out.] DRYDEN: What the... [Dryden smacks the machine. Still no drink. So Blade Dryden responds the only way he knows how. He kicks the Pepsi machine in the "midsection" and then lifts it up in the air and slams it to the ground with a sloppy Attitude Adjustment. The machine busts open, with money and Pepsi flowing on the floor. Dryden takes his Pepsi, takes his coin back, and then turns to face the camera.] DRYDEN: 7 foot discount, people. Look into it. But enough about my personal life. Jersey Jam was unforgetable. First of all, everyone saw "Bad Attitude" Blade Dryden display his Shamrock-esque submission skills. Betcha didn't think I knew how to do an armbar, eh Damien? I don't know what you were talkin' about, with me making a choice or something, but I get the feeling I made the right one. [Dryden takes a swig of Pepsi, which is also The Choice of a New Generation.] DRYDEN: The PCCW is a really bitchin' place lately. Ya got "Lone Wolf" Rob Hoffman swaggering around talking about how he's the greatest, etc... etc... etc... I got news for ya, Robbie. [Dryden opens his leather jacket, proudly displaying a black t-shirt which reads "The OFFICIAL Greatest of All-Time." in white letters.] DRYDEN: And this just ain't a stupid t-shirt... it's friggin' official! Anytime you wanna step up for a quick dose of Powerbomb Fever, you just say the word, Robbie. [Dryden looks stuck for something to say, then slaps his forehead.] DRYDEN: Oh sh** I almost forgot! Ajax Shiva... you BLEW it! I've never seen anything so funny in my entire life. You've got this stupid idiot who pretends he's _not_ Japanese, but we all know the truth, who begs for a title shot, gets it, and then loses. I mean, c'mon, can anyone else see the humour in that situation? It was almost as funny as the time... [Dryden breaks into laughter for the first time ever in the PCCW.] DRYDEN: ...the time... I... I.... [laughter]... powerbombed Jack Cole after he thought I saved him! [Dryden is now almost in tears from laughter, as the cameraman gets nervous and backs up a little bit.] DRYDEN: That was Must See TV right there. As for you, Cole... the day is coming when we met again, and after we get reaquainted and take a long walk through the park... we _fight_. The Old School is closed due to stormy weather, Cole, and you can just call me Hurricane Blade. [Dryden raises his middle finger to the camera, and then knocks the camera man over as the scene drops to static.] -- <"Electric Head Pt. 2" by White Zombie plays, and the crowd cheers!> DOUGLAS: This tag team contest is set for one fall! Introducing first..at a combined weight of 488 pounds..Mosh..Thrasher..THE HEADBANGERS! LAMPLEY: Another returning team, the Headbangers return to PCCW Sunday Night to square off against Perfection. <BONK> GAMBLER: Ow. LAMPLEY: What did you just do, Gambler? GAMBLER: I tried headbanging and I hit my head on the monitor! Owie! LAMPLEY: Idiot...let's go to Liz Oya in the back, standing by with Perfection. -- Liz Oya: Liz Oya here with Perfection, before the big match with the Headbangers. Gentlemen, what do you have to say, just mere moments before you step out to the ring and wrestle the Headbangers. Lake: Let me tell you something, sugarbra. What you're about to witness in five minutes is the greatest display of tag team wrestling you've ever seen. It'll be a display that'll make you sit up and take notice. It'll make you drop your American Beer that tastes like Dustin's piss, and you'll say "My God, I was blessed". You are about to witness Perfection in Action. Oya: It's been a tale well told, that Perfection have never worked hard in the big match. Lake: I'll have you know in the XYZWF World Tag Title match, we wrestled the match of our lives. Problem was so did our opponents. Thing is, we wrestle damned fine, but we just don't have the breaks. [from offcamera]: OWWW OW OW! Lake: What in the Wide Load of Ricki Lake is that? [Dustin Fong and Greg Ellis walk into the interview area, holding their foreheads.] Lake: What the hell is wrong with you two? Ellis: You know how we're wrestling the headbangers tonight? Lake: Yeah. Ellis: Well, Dustin and I decided we'd try being like them and we banged our heads together. Lake: Greg? Ellis: Yeah? Lake: Don't think too much again. You'll wind up killing yourself or watching Beavis and Buttwipe and burn the damned place down. Fong: Dine on Danger, Snack on Death. Lake: Wrong WWF tag team, Dustin. Fong: You Gotta Purdy Mouth? Lake: Strike Two. Fong: We're exciting? Lake: Strike three. You're out. Fong: Damn. Lake: And have you seen those guys? Wearing dresses? You'll never see me look like that. Sherman Lake and Perfection have more pride than that. [Lake prances around in his banana yellow sport coat, red shirt, and white pants.] Lake: Listen, Oya. You can come out here and tell Mick Douglas and the Gambler that Perfection are here, and no one is going to stop us. Not the Headbangers, not the DZA. And most certainly not that goombah paisan Capone. Oya: Speaking of Mafia HIts...where's Mario Stunade? Lake: I asked the Gambinos about that this week. They said he won't be back for a while. Something about getting fit for shoes. I'll tell ya what. they do things right in the mob! Oya: Good luck in your match tonight Lake: We're perfect. We don't need luck. -- <"Simply The Best" by Tina Turner plays, and the crowd boos like mad.> DOUGLAS: And their opponents..accompanied by Sherman Lake and Mario Stunade..at a combined weight of 499 pounds..Dustin Fong..Greg Ellis..PERFECTION! This did NOT last long at all. Perfection distracted the Headbangers and Sherman Lake distracted the ref as Stunade came in and double chokeslammed the Headbangers. Stunade punched down both men and went to the floor as the ref turned around, seeing nothing. Perfection calmly walked into the ring, Ellis covering Mosh and Fong covering Thrasher, and scored the double pin. DOUGLAS: Here are your winners..PERFECTION! LAMPLEY: Perfection gets the win thanks to that monster Stunade! How cheap! GAMBLER: Uh, Vince, he's right there. You better not let him hear you say that. LAMPLEY: Oops.. GAMBLER: Just wait till he walks away..OK..he's gone now. LAMPLEY: Alright. Waitasec..is that.. GAMBLER: IT IS! LAMPLEY: ROB VAN DAM IS IN THE RING! He has the mic! GAMBLER: He has a PCCW shirt on! Gimme a BREAK! ROB VAN DAM: Hey guys, I don't understand why the Terminator would have to make a challenge to the IWL. First of all, the IWL is too caught up in ripping off their fans and screwing their REAL talent to get involved with a top-notch organization like PCCW! Second of all, you have the chance to get the IWL's top star, Rob Van Dam, but I can't get in contact with you! You KNOW I'd leave that armpit Macungie in a heartbeat for a chance to compete among the best in the PCCW, but guys, you gotta return Rob Van Dam's calls! GAMBLER: Where's the Terminator? LAMPLEY: I have no idea.. <CRASH! "EL JOB-BO."> LAMPLEY: IT'S JOBBO! EL JOBBO GRANDE IS COMING TO THE RING! EL JOBBO GRANDE <w/ a mic>: Rob Van Dam, you come out here and you wear a PCCW shirt that belongs on a real wrestler instead of a jackass like you, and you say you want PCCW to return your phone calls? Well, son, about the only thing we're gonna return to you is a big can of whoop-ass! <crowd pop> LAMPLEY: The crowd definitely behind Jobbo. JOBBO: Now I already got my ass kicked once tonight, son.. <Jobbo picks up a chair as he enters the ring..> JOBBO: ..and I ain't making it two in a night, so if you've got the guts, son, let's get it on right now! LAMPLEY: Jobbo tosses the mic to Van Dam..he just turned around and smiled.. GAMBLER: AND JOBBO RUNS IN.. LAMPLEY: Van Dam sidesteps, Jobbo hits the corner with the chair..VANDAMINATOR BY VAN DAM! Van Dam's dragging Jobbo to the corner..SPLIT-LEGGED MOONSAULT! <The crowd suddenly goes nuts.> GAMBLER: Ohhhhhh no... LAMPLEY: IT'S THE TERMINATOR! THE TERMINATOR'S COMING IN THROUGH THE CROWD, AND VAN DAM DOESN'T SEE HIM! GAMBLER: Shhh! Do we WANT him to?! LAMPLEY: No..Terminator sneaking up behind Van Dam..Van Dam going for another split-legged moonsault, he doesn't see The Terminator..TERMINATOR CATCHES HIM IN MID-AIR! GAMBLER: MY GOD! How did he DO that! LAMPLEY: DEATH DROP BY THE TERMINATOR ON ROB VAN DAM! AND HE'S GOING FOR TWO! TERMINATOR WITH ANOTHER DEATH DROP! GAMBLER: He just laid Rob Van Dam out! LAMPLEY: And Terminator has the mic! What an ovation! THE TERMINATOR: Now...this is the BEST the IWL has? <The crowd pops.> TERMINATOR: Well...since the IWL can't find it in their hearts to give us a REAL challenge...as far as I'm concerned this little spat is OVER. And Rob Van Dam.. <Terminator kicks him again, sending him to the floor..> TERMINATOR: ..GET YOUR F*CKING ASS OUT OF MY ARENA! <The crowd goes nuts at this point.> TERMINATOR: Now...I suppose we know which fed has the stuff...and which one doesn't have the stuff down THERE. CROWD, LAMPLEY & GAMBLER: DOWN WHERE? TERMINATOR: You know wh.. <Terminator gets a smile on his face.> TERMINATOR: Ah, what the hell. Down HERE. <Terminator does a crotch chop.> TERMINATOR: See you in January! <Terminator steps over the top rope and leaves to a HUGE ovation.> LAMPLEY: Well, I guess this is over. GAMBLER: Yeah, just like Van Dam's career..they're just NOW taking him out.. LAMPLEY: Too bad, eh? GAMBLER: Ayup.
