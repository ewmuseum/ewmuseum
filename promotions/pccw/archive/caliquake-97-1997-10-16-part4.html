Lampley: Heavy Metal slow to move in...Ohtani looking for an opening here, backing Metal off with some martial arts kicks. Metal trapped in the corner, and ducks out to get the ref to force Ohtani back. Metal back in, and Ohtani levels him with a kick. Ohtani now working on the legs of Metal, trying to set him up for that Indian deathlock. Metal with a kick to the face, and now a variety of punches on a dazed Ohtani. Gambler: Heavy Metal may be one of the best against the lucha libre style and the American style, but Ohtani has him confused and desperate early. Lampley: Metal whips Ohtani into the ropes, and clotheslines him down. Cover, 1....2.......No. Metal whips Ohtani into the ropes again, reversed by Ohtani, and a powerslam! 1, 2, No. Ohtani almost had the win there. Ohtani up...MICHINOKU DRIVER OUTTA NOWHERE! 1, 2, 3! What a quick win for Ohtani! Douglas: The winner of this contest, and still the J*CROWN champion, Shinjiro Ohtani! Lampley: Heavy Metal is stunned...he can't believe it! Gambler: So am I!
