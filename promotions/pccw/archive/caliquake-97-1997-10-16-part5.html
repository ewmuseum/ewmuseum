CaliQuake '97, Part 5

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LAMPLEY: Well, the time has come to this. Spin The Wheel, Make The Deal. The Terminator vs. The Undertaker, a Texas Death Match. GAMBLER: Wel- LAMPLEY: Let's go to Michael Buffer. GAMBLER: You never let me talk! BUFFER: LLLLLladies and gentlemen, this match is sanctioned by Pro-Continental Championship Wrestling, and the California State Athletic Commission. Spin The Wheel, Make The Deal...a Texas death match. No-disqualification! Falls count anywhere! You must get up before a ten-count. Ladies and gentlemen...from CaliQuake '97 in Los Angeles, California..LLLLLLLLLLLLET'S GET READY TO RUMBLLLLLLLLLLLE! <"I, Zombie (Europe In The Raw Mix)" by White Zombie plays, and the crowd goes absolutely nuts as the Terminator comes down the aisle. He highfives a few of his hometown fans here in Los Angeles, then steps over the ropes as the fireworks go off.> BUFFER: Introducing first...wearing black and silver, trimmed in gold...weighing in at 347 and one-half pounds..FROM LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA! He is one of the best big men in the sport today..a former VWA World's champion..a former World television champion..a former NWA champion..a former tag team champion with his partner Mike Sweetser in the Two Dudes With Attitudes..a former Extreme champion..and the former PCCW heavyweight champion of the worrrrlllldddd....THEEEEEEEEE TERRRRRRMINNNNAAAATORRRRRRR! LAMPLEY: You can hardly hear yourself think here in Los Angeles! They love their own here! The Terminator in the ring, he's just itching to get ahold of the Undertaker! GAMBLER: He sure as hell doesn't look afraid of the Undertaker, does he? LAMPLEY: Certianly not...what's taking the Undertaker so long? GAMBLER: Simple. He's playing mind games with him. He's making him sweat! LAMPLEY: It doesn't look like it's having much of an effect on the Terminator. <The funeral music suddenly starts, and the lights go low as the Undertaker strolls to the ring. Amazingly, the 'Taker gets BOOED!> BUFFER: And now...wearing black and purple...weighing 328 pounds...from Death Valley...this is the man that cost the Terminator the World's title on Thursday night...he is the former two-time heavyweight champion of the world...THEE UNNNNNNDERRRRTAAAAAKERRRRRRRR! LAMPLEY: The Undertaker coming down the aisle n-CHANGE THAT! A PLANCHA BY THE TERMINATOR AND WE'RE ON! GAMBLER: Did you see that?! The Terminator just LAUNCHED himself over the top rope at the Undertaker! LAMPLEY: The Terminator punching away at the Undertaker..TAker with chops, driving the Terminator back! Both of them going at it..Termiantor gets thrown into the guardrail and up and over! AND THE UNDERTAKER WITH A FLYING CLOTHESLINE! GAMBLER: This has got to be breaking the laws of physics! These big guys can't DO stuff like that! LAMPLEY: The two big men of the world of wrestling going at it in the crowd..Terminator with a chair to the head of the Undertaker! Undertaker doesn't even flinch! Another one, NOTHING! A third one and the Undertaker is over the guardrail to ringside! GAMBLER: Termiantor's making like La Parka with that chair! Y'think he can strut? LAMPLEY: I doubt it, Gambler! Terminator tosses the Undertaker into the ringpost! A bodyslam on the floor! The first cover! One, AND A BIG KICKOUT! Terminator's head went into the post on that kickout! GAMBLER: Undertaker is taking a lot of punishment early, and he's gonna take a lot more before this is over! LAMPLEY: I'll agree..Undertaker has Termiantor set up for the Tombstone! ON THE FLOOR! NO, TERMINATOR SHIFTS HIS WEIGHT! And a running powerslam on the floor by the Terminator! GAMBLER: That was a great counter..when you're seven feet tall, you can do stuff like that! LAMPLEY: Terminator's gonna take Undertaker into the steps...Irish whip..reversal, Terminator into the teps and over! What a shot! And Undertaker picks up the Terminator..PRESS SLAM! AND HE DROPS HIM ONTO THE STEPS! GAMBLER: He could've broken the Terminator's skull with that one! LAMPLEY: Terminator slumped against the steps..the Undertaker has a chair! He's gonna take Terminator's head off! No, Terminator moves! GAMBLER: That was lucky! LAMPLEY: Terminator back up, and he kicks the chair into Undertaker's face! They're getting REALLY close to us.. GAMBLER: Oh, NO! LAMPLEY, MOVE! LAMPLEY: Why? GAMBLER: MOVE! LAMPLEY: Terminator is setting the Undertaker up for a powerbomb! NO, HE TURNS IT ONTO OUR TABLE! <CRASH> LAMPLEY: Our table's been destroyed, Undertaker just got powerbombed through the table! COVER! ONE! TWO! THREE! TERMINATOR WINS THE FALL! GAMBLER: He's got ten seconds to get up! LAMPLEY: Terminator going into the ring and counting with the referee! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! LAMPLEY: The Undertaker is up! He's bleeding now as well! Terminator wiping the blood from his eyes as well! Undertaker going into the ring, TERMINATOR HITS HIM OVER THE HEAD WITH THE CHAIR! MY GOD! GAMBLER: You could hear that echo throughout the arena! He nearly bent that chair in half! LAMPLEY: The Undertaker is back up! Terminator tries the same thing..Undertaker moves, Terminator drops the chair! Terminator punches down the Undertaker...on the apron..double axehandle, NO! Undertaker catches the Terminator and slams him into the ringpost! GAMBLER: Now what-NO! LAMPLEY: THE UNDERTAKER HAS THE TERMINATOR UP! HE'S GOING FOR IT! GAMBLER: NO! NOT ON THE FLOOR! LAMPLEY: TOMBSTONE ON THE FLOOR! ONE! TWO! THREE! Undertaker gets the fall! GAMBLER: And Terminator might have gotten a broken neck! LAMPLEY: Undertaker is going to the entrance...he's setting up a table! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! LAMPLEY: He has a table set up near the corner on the floor...Terminator is stirring.. FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! EIGHT! NINE! LAMPLEY: And the Terminator BARELY gets to his feet! That was close! Undertaker back over..clot-NO! BACKDROP BY THE TERMINATOR! GAMBLER: Hey! Gimme that! LAMPLEY: Terminator just took the Gambler's monitor..and he tosses it at the Undertaker! GAMBLER: Undertaker caught it..what happened? My monitor went out! LAMPLEY: It's over there in the hands of the Undertaker! Undertaker tosses it at the Terminator..he ducks, AND THE UNDERTAKER WITH A DDT ON THE FLOOR! COVER! ONE! TWO! TH-KICKOUT! GAMBLER: That was a brilliant move by the Undertaker! He made the Terminator either take a DDT or a TV to the head! LAMPLEY: Two bad choices...The Undertaker is dragging the Terminator to that table..he's set him up on the table! GAMBLER: Now what! LAMPLEY: He's got another table! He's setting up a table ON TOP! What in the WORLD is he doing! GAMBLER: He just gave the thumb across the throat...oh my god...the Undertaker's going to the top rope! He's going to put the Terminator through the tables, like a sandwich! LAMPLEY: Waitasec! From the crowd! It's Sweetser! Mike Sweetser grabbed the Undertaker's ankle..Taker kicks him away.. GAMBLER: Terminator's off the tables! He's up there with him! LAMPLEY: The Terminator and the Undertaker are on the turnbuckles, punching away..Terminator with a groin shot! He has him up...OHHHHHHHHH MYYYYY GODDDDDDDDDD! <CRASHCRASH!> GAMBLER: JESUS CHRIST! LAMPLEY: THE TERMINATOR JUST SUPERPLEXED THE UNDERTAKER TO THE FLOOR THROUGH THE TABLES! MY GOD! GAMBLER: I think he just killed both of them! LAMPLEY: They are NOT moving here! They may both be unconscious! Wait...Terminator's moving..he drapes an arm on the Undertaker! One, two, three, you could count to a hundred. GAMBLER: Now, do BOTH of them have to get up? LAMPLEY: No, just the Undertaker! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! EI- LAMPLEY: THE UNDERTAKER SITS UP! The Terminator is up as well! This is amazing! GAMBLER: It's a wonder that they're not dead here! LAMPLEY: Terminator throws the Undertaker inside the ring! Whip into the corner by the Terminator! Another one! Another one...AND HE HAS HIM! CHOKESLAM BY THE TERMINATOR! GAMBLER: One chokeslam now, and you know what's next! LAMPLEY: Here we go! Terminator is going for the Death Drop! HE HITS THE DEATH DROP! GAMBLER: HE DID IT! THE TERMINATOR PLANTED THE UNDERTAKER! FINALLY, THE UNDERTAKER'S BEEN TERMINATED! LAMPLEY: He's giving him last rites! The Terminator goes for the cover...one...two...NO! THE UNDERTAKER SITS UP! GAMBLER: DAMMIT! LAMPLEY: Terminator punches him, Undertaker with chops, he's back up! Both men going at it now! Irish whip, duckunder, again, FLYING CLOTHESLINE BY THE UNDERTAKER! GAMBLER: NO! The Undertaker's gonna plant him! He's gonna bury him! LAMPLEY: Here we go! Tombstone coming u-no, Terminator slips behind him..kick to the stomach..he's going for a Tiger Bomb! He hit it! ONE...TWO..Undertaker kicks him in the head, both men down! ONE! TWO! THREE! GAMBLER: HUH? LAMPLEY: Looks now! Both men were covering each other with their feet! Both men have to get to their feet before the ten-count! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! LAMPLEY: The Undertaker's on his feet, Terminator isn't! SIX! SEV-WHAT THE HELL?! GAMBLER: Something's got him! LAMPLEY: THERE'S A HOLE IN THE RING, SOMEONE'S PULLING THE UNDERTAKER UNDER! WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON?! EIGHT! NINE! LAMPLEY: The Terminator's up, and TEN! THE TERMINATOR WINS IT! I DON'T BELIEVE IT! GAMBLER: What the hell happened?! BUFFER: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner of this Texas Death Match..THEEEE TERRRRRRRMINNNNNAAAAAAATORRRRRRR! LAMPLEY: What the hell is going on! The Terminator has won this match..but what's going on?! GAMBLER: Termiantor's over at that hole in the ring..HE'S REACHING DOWN! IS HE AN IDIOT?! LAMPLEY: He's pulling someone out...OH, GIMME A BREAK! GAMBLER: AHAHAHAHAHAHA! LAMPLEY: IT'S SWEETSER! SWEETSER PULLED THE UNDERTAKER UNDER THE RING! Can we get a damn replay?! GAMBLER: Yeah, let's! LAMPLEY: Alright...here we are from-never mind! In the ring...THE UNDERTAKER IS CLIMBING OUT! THE DUDES DON'T SEE IT! He has both of them! Double noggin knocker! Terminator's out of here! CHOKESLAM ON SWEETSER, HE'S OUT OF HERE! GAMBLER: What a brilliant plan! I LOVE IT! LAMPLEY: The Terminator has won this match..but the Undertaker was ROBBED! GAMBLER: Hey, he used the Undertaker's own idea on him! Talk about mind games! AHAHAHAHAHA! LAMPLEY: The Dudes are out of here...let's see if we can get a replay of what happened! OK...there we have it...Undertaker on the top rope, he kicks Sweetser away...go to camera #4! GAMBLER: Why? LAMPLEY: THERE! Sweetser climbs under the ring there...now to the end...the Undertaker makes it up first..and there's something poking through the mat, it's cutting through the mat..and there goes the Taker under the ring. Terminator gets up by ten, he wins it! GAMBLER: Well, too bad! LAMPLEY: Ladies and gentlemen, thanks for tuning in!
