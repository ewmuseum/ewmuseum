As expected, the Marauder took more of a technical stance of going after Raven, but Raven would have none of it, punching away at the Marauder and headbutting him. Marauder fought back with chops and kicks to the legs of Raven, then whipped Raven in and kneelifted him in the gut. Marauder charged Raven, but was backdropped to the floor, and Raven flung himself over the top rope at the Marauder. On the floor, Raven was at home, throwing the Marauder into the rail and clotheslining him over. Raven grabbed a chair and followed the Marauder into the crowd, nailing him with the chair. A second chairshot was blocked, and Marauder took the chair and nailed Raven with it. Marauder threw Raven out into the hallway, where he was smashed into a Coke machine and bloodied. Marauder took some money from a kid, put it in the machine, grabbed a Diet Coke and sprayed Raven in the face with it. As Raven was blinded, the Marauder suplexed him on the floor, then took him back into the arena. Raven kicked the Marauder and took him down the aisle, throwing him over the rail and onto the timekeeper's table, which was broken when Raven flung himself over the rail onto the table and the Marauder. Raven tried for a pin, but only got two. After sending the Marauder into the post, Raven tossed him back in and covered for another two. Raven went for the DDT, but Marauder backdropped him, and as Raven got back up, gave him a swinging neckbreaker. Raven got back up and was dropkicked out of the ring, then taken down with a tope suicida. On the floor, Raven was slammed and tossed back in, then powerbombed for two. The Marauder tossed Raven into the ropes, but ducked his head down and was piledriven for a VERY close two-count.
