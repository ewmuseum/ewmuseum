Frustrated, Sweetser began punching away at Rey's head till the ref pulled him off. Sweetser got up just as Rey came off the ropes with a flying chop, and was covered for two. Rey went off again and tried a jump into a victory roll, but Sweetser pushed Rey forward, then lifted him up and flattened him with a reverse powerbomb.
