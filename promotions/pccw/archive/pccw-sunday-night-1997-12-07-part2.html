The second Hoffmann got in the ring, Machine drilled him in the noggin with his six-man belt. The ref hadn't called for the bell yet, so it was legal, and Machine began punching away at Hoffmann. Machine whipped Hoffmann into the corner and clotheslined him, but Hoffmann fought back, driving Machine into the corner and taking him down with a slingshot. Machine remained in control, working on Hoffmann's reconstructed knee with a figure-four leglock. Hoffmann struggled and was able to reverse it, and Machine broke the hold, but continued working on the knee. Machine dragged Hoffmann to the corner and rammed his leg into the post, but Hoffmann kicked him away on the second try, sending the Machine into the guardrail. Hoffmann went to the floor and blocked a chairshot by Machine, taking the chair and denting it over Machine's head. Machinec rawled back in and Hoffmann was right after him. Hoffmann went for the Last Word, but it was blocked and Machine rolled him up for two. Hoffmann tried a legdrop, but Machine moved and Hoffmann landed on his injured knee. Machine locked in a Texas cloverleaf, and Hoffmann couldn't get to the ropes. Hoffmann refused to tap out, but the referee called for the bell to prevent any more damage. It slowed down a bit in the ring, with Southward and Scott going to their corners. Sweetser continued to work on Rick's knee, with Rick having none of it, and slamming, then suplexing Sweetser for two. Rick DDTed Sweetser, and applied a sleeperhold, countered into a back suplex. Sweetser threw Rick to the floor, where Southward threw him into the post and back in. Sweetser whipped Rick into the ropes, but unbeknownst to Sweetser, he tagged Scott, and after Sweetser missed a clothesline, Scott nearly took his head off with one, for a two-count. Sweetser rolled to the corner and tagged in Southward, who whipped Scott in and took him down with a satellite headscissors, then threw him into the corner and took him down with numerous kicks. Southward went up top for a cross bodyblock, but Scott caught him and belly-to-belly suplexed him not once, but twice. Scott hit a sidewalk slam, then whipped Southward in, but put his head down and was hit with a swinging neckbreaker. Southward tried to suplex Scott, but it was reversed and Southward went to the floor. Rick went after him and tossed him back in, and the Steiners doubleteamed the Promo Aztecans. Rick slammed Southward in the corner, and Sweetser came in to save him. Sweetser took down Rick with a dragon screw, then locked on a Scorpion deathlock, which Scott broke up with a clothesline. Southward came in and dropkicked Scott, and Sweetser whipped Rick in and took him down with, of all things, a Frankensteiner. Sweetser went up top and was about to elbowdrop Rick, but he noticed La Parka in the front row, holding up his chair..with "GRINGOS" spraypainted on it. Sweetser, instead of going to Rick, went to the floor, and planchaed Parka and Misterio in the front row. As he brawled with the luchadores, the Steiners went after Sean, executing the top rope bulldog. Jayla distracted the referee as Sweetser grabbed Parka's chair and smacked Rick in the head with it. Southward covered Rick, but Scott grabbed the chair from Sweetser as the ref turned around, and hit both Gringos with it. The referee called for the bell.
