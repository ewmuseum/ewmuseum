War of Words (7/25/00)

25 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All-star Wrestling Alliance War of Words July 25th, 2000 Featuring: - "Intensifier" Alex Fury -"Deliquent" Kevin Fontaine ^v^v^v^v^v^v^^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ "Intensifier" Alex Fury ^v^v^v^v^v^v^^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ [We see "Intensifier" Alex Fury poolside at his house (presumably) flexing for the bevy of beauties surrounding him. His cut tanned physique draws the whispers and giggles from his admirers, as he launches himself off the diving board in a picture perfect jacknife dive. He then gets out of the pool and towels himself off, grabbing his Oakley sunglasses and a cold beer for his troubles. The unhampered sunlight glints off his glasses as he gives a big toothy grin to the camera.]
AF: You know Riggs, I expected more out of you. Having been in this federation far longer than I have, I was expecting at least some sort of a fight from you, even if you are a no name loser. I mean, come on, there's a certain point where bad becomes unacceptable, and I'm sorry, but Riggs, you are unacceptable. Standing there, in your home town, insulting everyone around you. I mean honestly, how do you expect to get anywhere in this business? Ahhh, how little you know. Not that it matters, however. You're just another notch in the win column for the Intensifier, the Pinnacle of
Pro-Wrestling. Oooh, and a rematch. [He cracks a grin.] AF: I don't think so. [He takes a sip from his beer and then begins talking again.] AF: Now, concerning this upcoming match, against whats his name... Vincent
St-Pierre. What kind of crazy homosexual name is that anyways? I mean, come on, St. Pierre? What are you, a winery? What do you plan to do
"Vincent," beat me with your fine Merlot? Keep in my, whatever you try to due to rebuke me is gonna be met with some of the best smacktalk in the industry. To back that up, I'm going to introduce you to Mr. Smack.
[Fury raises his empty hand, leaving it open, pointing the back of it towards the camera.] AF: He is applied in a downward motion. Hence, the "Smackdown." So Mr. Vincent St. Pierre, lord of the dance, expect to have quite the tango on this upcoming Meltdown. You don't have the skills to slamdance with the
Intensifier. In fact, if you could slamdance, you'd be winning more often, wouldn't you. Look at your record bucko! I see a notch in the loss column for you. Being all high and mighty from Canadia isn't going to help you when you meet your southern cousin. Wait, that's disgusting, I don't want to be your southern cousin. In fact, why did I say that? Pardon.
[Fury spits and takes another sip of his beer, then looks back at the camera.] AF: Now where was I? Oh, ya know what, who cares. I need some sex. [Fade out.] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ "Deliquent" Kevin Fontaine ^v^v^v^v^v^v^^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ [scene opens outside the arena after AWA Sunday Night Meltdown. The camera seems to be in the parking area as if waiting for someone to leave the building a head to their car. After a few seconds the door opens and out walks "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine. His hair is wet and hanging down in his eyes, and his face is very tense and angry. As the camera approaches
Fontaine begins to speak, his voice is very angst ridden, and his eyes are burning with intensity.]
KF: WHAT THE F<BLEEP>!!! Craven, I don't know what sort of bulls<bleep>t mind games you are trying to play, but I'll be damned if you are gonna get away with this crap. I don't know what sort of union you seem to think that whole ordeal was going to bring about Craven, but you can forget it. I don't like you, and I never will. Fact is Craven, as of right now, you have made me begin to hate you, and that, I can guarantee is the last thing you want.
[Fontaine throw his head back in one swift motion and get some of his hair out of his face, then continues, his face and voice both seeming to be significantly more controlled]
KF: What ever your little game, it won't change the outcome of this Sunday night. I can't say I have ever been in a federation where the main event match was a surprise involving six men, but I can say I will be the one who comes out the new AWA World Heavyweight Champion. Sure, there are many sort of matches, or tournaments that could turn out of Sunday night, but I have been through them all before, and won each and every one of them. Battle
Royal, bring it on. Tourney, been there won that. Elimination Match, road that one to victory. Almost anything you can think of, I have won. I have one those matches against far more talented wrestler then the group I must step foot in the ring with this Sunday.
KF: Craven, at some point Sunday Night your blood is gonna spill, and when does you can bet I am only gonna come at you that much harder. Rome, I still haven't repaid you for your interference in my first match with Craven, but you will get your also. Essex, I am not quite sure what to say, I haven't had anything to do with you thus far, and if you stay out of my way you may not get hurt too bad. Ah, yes, "Outlaw" Brody Bryant... self proclaimed worst man in wrestling, ender of careers. Well, Bryant, you must not be the most notable, cause up until you came out and attacked an old man two
Sundays ago I had never heard of you. Cutter, well, you're just that, a washed-up has-been. This Sunday Night I will win the AWA Heavyweight Title, and I don't give two flying halves of a rats ass what kind of a match it is... Craven, be prepared, because you will take a very long ride on the
"Personal Downfall" before the night is over.......
[Fontaine shoves his way past the camera without another word] FADE OUT
