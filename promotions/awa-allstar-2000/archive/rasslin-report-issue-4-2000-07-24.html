We will begin including the offical rankings soon. *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* *^v^v^v[AWA Underground]v^v^v^* *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* - It was pure chaos this past Sunday night at the Memphis Sports Complex. The scheduled triple threat match between Craven, Rome and Fontaine turned into an impromptu backstage brawl between the three men but even more perplexing then that is the fact that Craven had the match won but instead gave the victory to Fontaine. Is there some sort of alliance forming between these two? - The much anticipated Main Event match-up between Essex and Bryant did not take place as originally scheduled. Following the conclusion of the triple threat match, Brody Bryant attacked Craven which in return opened him up for a blindsided attack from Essex and Cutter who viciously beat him with a steel chair. Security was able to finally break things up but not until the damaged had already been done. - President Richards addressed the media following this past Sunday nights event and stated that due to what had occured he would have a special announcement at next weeks Meltdown and hinted that it may involve the AWA Wolrd title! *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* *^v^v^v^v[Offical Roster]v^v^v^v^* *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* *Singles* Bryan Riggs (1-2-0) Chris Magnum (0-2-0) Tony Rome (0-2-0) "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine (3-0-0) Derek Craven (1-1-0) Lord Bradbury (1-2-0) Jason "The Dream Machine" Essex(1-0-0) Vincent St-Pierre (2-1-0) "Intensifier" Alex Fury (1-0-0) "Outlaw" Brody Bryant (0-0-0) *Tag Teams* Stunning French Candians (0-0-0) Essex/Cutter (1-0-0) Rome/Bryant (0-1-0) *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* *^v^v^v^v[Upcoming Shows]v^v^v^v^* *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* AWA SUNDAY NIGHT! *LIVE* from the Peoria Civic Center - Peoria, Illinois Monday - July 31st, 2000 - Bell Time: 9:00 PM EST **Special Announcement from President Tommy Richards** Riggs/Magnum vs. Stunning French Canadians "Intensifier" Alex Fury vs. Vincent St. Pierre {{{Match for the AWA World Title}}} Essex/Cutter/Rome/Bryant/Craven/Fontaine will all be involved so be prepared for anything and any opponent!! *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* *^v^v^v^v[Past Results]v^v^v^v^* *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* AWA SUNDAY NIGHT! *LIVE* from the Memphis Sports Complex - Memphis, Tennessee July 23rd, 2000 - Bell Time: 9:00 PM EST Vincent St. Pierre def.
