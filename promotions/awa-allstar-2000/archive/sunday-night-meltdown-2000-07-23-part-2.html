Sunday Night Meltdown (Part Two)

23 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CT: Welcome back to hour number two of Meltdown. It has been a great night of action here so far as we have seen two outstanding matches and things only look to get better here in the second half.
SH: Well the next two match-ups have been on the top of everyone's mind since they were announced last week. I know that I have been looking forward to this next match-up for a while now.
^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Tony Rome vs. Craven vs. Kevin Fontaine Written by: [name withheld] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ CT: Without further delay, let's go to the ring . Wait a second! What's this?! I am being told that there is something going on backstage. I believe we have a camera in position, so let's see if we can get in on the action!
(The scene switches to an area backstage. Derek Craven and Kevin Fontaine have teamed up and are doing a number on Tony Rome.)
CT: Derek Craven and Kevin Fontaine are putting the boots to Tony Rome backstage and here comes referee, Paul Adams and it looks like this match is going to start right here and now! Rome in a world of hurt as his two opponents take turns punishing him.
SH: Don't look now but it looks like that alliance was short lived! Craven just blindsided Fontaine with a stiff right hook to the jaw!
CT: Fontaine staggers back and Craven scoops him up and oh! He slams him down and right through the refreshment table! Gatorade and water going everywhere. Craven turning his attention back towards Rome and he brings him to his feet. He grabs Rome by the arm and slingshots him into a stack of crates that line the wall!
SH: Those crates may be made out of plastic but believe me they hurt when you get thrown into them like that! I know because I have been there, done that!
CT: Craven is like a man possessed as he rips Rome back up from the floor but there's Fontaine and he hammers Craven across the back with one of those large water jugs! Craven staggers forward and he is tattooed to the concrete floor with a bulldog!
SH: Don't look now but Rome is back on his feet! CT: Fontaine turns and he is leveled with a clothesline by Rome! Tony grabs hold of a steel chair and drives it down hard across the back of Fontaine.
Craven slowly rising to his feet but it quickly sent back to the floor as he catches a chairshot to the forehead!
SH: Craven has been busted open! CT: Blood running down the face of Craven now as Rome brings him to his feet. Rome clears a table of a veggie try and places Craven onto the table.
Now over to Fontaine, Rome looks to bring him to his feet as well. Rome scoops Fontaine into the air and SLAMS him down on top of Craven sending both men crashing through the table!
SH: This could be all she wrote! Rome with the cover on Fontaine . ONE! TWO!! NO!! SH: Fontaine manages to kickout before the referee could count three. Rome can't believe it.
CT: Rome with a handful of hair brings Fontaine to his feet but Fontaine with a little fight left in him delivers a big right hand into the breadbasket and he follows it up with another! Rome is staggered. Fontaine grabbing a handful of hair and slams Rome face first into the concrete wall!
SH: This is total chaos! CT: Craven is back to his feet and lunges in towards Fontaine but Fontaine sees it coming and turns it into a vicious power slam!
SH: Did you hear the sound that Craven's back made when it hit the concrete?! He may have broken something!
CT: Craven isn't moving and Fontaine goes for the cover . ONE! TWO!! THR . NO!!! CT: Rome breaks up the pinfall attempt! He brings Fontaine to his feet, sets him up and DDT!! Rome DDT'ed Fontaine onto the concrete! Fontaine is out cold!
SH: Somebody needs to put a stop to this match or else these guys may not have a career tomorrow!
CT: Craven has managed to get back up to one knee but he is still feeling the effects of that power slam. Rome over his way and Craven connects with an unexpected low blow and Rome is stunned! Craven is up to his feet, grabs a hold of Rome and sets him up .
SH: HELLRAISER!!! Craven just executed the HELLRAISER!!! CT: All Craven has to do is make the cover and he'll have this match won. Wait a second what's this?! Craven has just draped the arm of Fontaine over the chest of Rome?! He's giving the match to Fontaine!!
ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! SH: What the hell was that all about?! Craven had this match won . why did he give the match to Fontaine?!
CT: An unbelievable turn of events here in this match-up. After all that
Craven gives it away.
(Just then as Craven stands there overlooking the beaten and bloodied bodies of Fontaine and Rome . )
CT: OH MY!!! "Outlaw" Brody Bryant out of nowhere just blindsided Derek
Craven!! He came out of nowhere and hammered Craven to the concrete!
SH: Don't look now but Essex and Cutter have arrived!! CT: Bryant gets nailed with a chairshot to the back as Essex and Cutter arrive on the scene. The bigman is down and is unmercilessly being pummeled by Essex and Cutter. Essex driving the butt end of that steel chair into the forehead of Bryant who has been busted wide open.
SH: Security has arrived on scene and are attempting to break this mealy up. CT: It's pure pandemonium here in the backstage area and fans I hate to say it but we are all out of time!! Be sure you tune in next week where I'm sure all six of these men will have unfinished business to settle with one another! So for Steve Houston, this is Chris Thompson saying . GOODNIGHT!!!
