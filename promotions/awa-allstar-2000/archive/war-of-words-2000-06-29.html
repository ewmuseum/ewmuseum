War of Words (6/29/00)

29 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All-star Wrestling Alliance War of Words - 6/29/00 Featuring: "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine Jason "The Dream Machine" Essex ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ [Scene opens to a small diner. The diner itself is like any other diner, a middle-aged, frizzy haired waitress storms around serving the needs of the customers. The place isn't the cleanest place in the entire world, the windows covered with Vientiane blinds which look as if they haven't been dusted in 10 years, giving the place a sort of half-lighted effect. AWA's
Chris Michaels walks into the shop, the camera man (and camera) following shortly behind. Chris walks up to the sign that say "Please wait to be seated" before turning to the camera as he begins to speak]
Michaels: We're here today for an interview with one of the inaugural members of AWA, who will wrestle his first match this in just four days, at
AWA SUNDAY NIGHT... [The waitress comes up behind him and tapes him on the shoulder, as Chris turns to see her. The woman's voice is rather cold, seemingly as if she felt she had much better things to do than cater to some guy with a camera following him]
Waitress: MAY I help you?? Michaels: Umm, yeah, I was supposed to meet someone here. I think that may be him back in the corner.
[Michaels points to the rear corner, which seems darker than the rest of the diner. A man sits there, his long black hair covering his eyes, as his head is bent over the table. He appears to be writing, as his head rises every once in a while to take a drag from his cigarette or to take a swig of his coffee. The waitress's eyes follow Michael's finger, grabs a menu from the little stand to her left, and the leads him back that way without so much as another word. Michaels turns his head to the camera and gives a sort of distasteful look before rushing to catch up with the waitress. As the waitress arrives at the table, she tosses the menu down in front of the man who Michaels is apparently to meet and turns to him quickly]
Waitress: Drinks?? Michaels: Umm, nothing for me. Camera man (voice slightly muffled): I'll take a Coke, and would it be too much of a problem if I sat at this table across from them?
[The waitress makes a quick swipe of her hand as if to signal ok, followed by what could only be described as a grunt. The camera kinda falls into a steady spot as Chris Michaels takes up the seat across from the man at the table. The camera kinda focuses in on the man, who has yet to look up from the paper which his hand moves so quickly across. The man's hair is long and black, three tiny braids adorning each side of the front of his face. The man has a black T-shirt on, although the words across the front cannot be made out from the camera angle. After a minute Michaels kinda stirs impatiently, but the man still doesn't even acknowledge his presence.
Michaels gives the camera a puzzled look as he raise his shoulder, but just as he does that the man tosses his pen down across the table and takes a quick swig of coffee before raising his head. The man makes a quick sweep with his arm to get his hair out of his face, his eyes suddenly totally focused on Chris Michaels. Michaels kinda stutters for a moment as if taken aback by the whole turn of events, then seems to regain his composure and begins to speak]
Michaels: Mr. Fontaine, I am Chris Michaels of the AWA, and I am here to do the interview that you requested.
[Fontaine's mouth sort of twitches in a half smile then he begins to speak, his voice almost inaudible, but somehow still carrying a sense of power]
Fontaine: Call me Kevin. And I know who you are, or else I probably would have asked you to get up from my table long before this.
Michaels: Ummmmmm.... ok, Kevin, Sunday night you, as well as everyone else, has their first match on AWA. What are your thoughts on this match? Any predictions?
Fontaine: Yeah, I predict I am going to make Chris Magnum look like a third string quarterback trying to play at the Pro Bowl.
Michaels: That's a rather cocky prediction. Fontaine: No, Chris, that is the truth. You see, you can tell a lot about a person in the first chance you have to encounter them. I sat and watched this past "War of Words", and much to my surprise, my opponent for this
Sunday night was a no-show. I just form that I could tell that Chris Magnum was nothing more than a weak conformist, looking to ride the pant leg of someone else to fame and fortune. I mean, he showed no devotion to the federation, no respect for the fans, and no real discipline. Same with the other four wrestler's on this roster that apparently thought they were to good to give their time to the fans of AWA.
Michaels: I see your point Kevin, but it say nowhere in the contracts to my knowledge that sending a tape for "War of Words" is required.
Fontaine: I am aware of that also. But, you can normally judge people really well simply by what they do or don't do, almost better than if you have to judge them on what they are saying.
Michaels: Ok, so there were to others who sent in tapes for "War on Words"... What were your thoughts on what they had to say?? Fontaine: My thoughts?!? My thoughts are this: Essex seems like he is a rather unique individual, uncaring enough to be dangerous, but disciplined enough to be a threat. Tony Rome on the other hand comes off like an over-confident prick, which only shows deep down that he is nothing more than a scared little man trying to convince himself that he is going to be "ok".
Michaels: You seems like you are rather cocky. How do you explain this? Fontaine: I'm not cocky... I have lost. I'll be the first person to admit it. But anytime someone feels like they need to question my attitude, all they have to do is ask and I will meet them in the ring and show the my credentials.
Michaels: Alright, I was reading in my little briefing for Sunday Night that you do have previous wrestling experience... care to elaborate?
Fontaine: I've wrestled before, I still do wrestle one other fed. Michaels: And?..... [Fontaine pauses to light a cigarette, taking a long drag before returning to the conversation]
Fontaine: AND I still wrestle there. Listen, you have the little briefing, you tell the fans what I have achieved. I didn't come to AWA to brag, I came to AWA to prove myself to a greater number of people, take down some people
I haven't come across before, and generally just cause I needed something to do.
Michaels: Alright, sorry. Anyway, I need to get going so I can catch my plane... any final words?
Fontaine: Sure... [Fontaine takes a long drag off the cigarette before turning to the camera and blowing the smoke right at the lens, thus screwing up the only real shot of his face that could be seen the whole time]
Fontaine: AWA... Chris Magnum... prepare to get... ON YOUR KNEES!!!! FADE OUT ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Jason "Dream Machine" Essex ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ ( The scene opens on Jason" The Dream Machine" Essex sitting at the counter of a small New York deli. He is dressed in jeans,a black t- shirt,and combat boots his long black and blue hair is tied back under a black X-MEN cap. To his left dressed much the same way is a unusually quite Jonathan"Cutter"Chapel dressed in a rumpled black
Armani suit. Essex and Chapel are eating large cold cut sandwiches and big ass chocolate shakes. Chapel is more just looking at it. Essex looks up to see the camera and starts speaking)
Essex: Well good to see that old has-been Tony Rome was able to get out of the nursing home to join us. Tony my career is just fine thanks for asking. My opponent his name slips my mind at the moment will get to find out just how good I am Sunday.
(Essex looks like he is about to say something more when Chapel suddenly looks up and in a quite voice speaks)
Chapel: Rome..You are right..the great Cutter multiple champion..Now a manager. Yea this has what I've become Tony a former shadow of myself. You see the Cutter persona was more than a character he was like a whole different person. It got to the point I was committed just so I could remember my real bloody name. After a while Cutter just faded away but like a fool I tried to go on but it resulted in retirement. Recently the tug of the ring grew stronger and with it
Cutter came back and he wants out. You Tony can take pride in knowing that one of the most sadistic bastards to ever curse a ring is coming back and Tony it was all your doing.
(Essex looks up shocked) Essex: Jon? Are you nuts? The doctors said that your body can't take the punishment it used to.
Chapel: To hell with the doctors..I'm going out my way!! To hell with being careful...To hell with being a damn leech on your glory. It's time I remembered what it's lie to live again.
(With that Cutter walks out of the deli. Essex sits there and stares as the scene fades out)
