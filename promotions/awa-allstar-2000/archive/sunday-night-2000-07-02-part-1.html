AWA Sunday Night (Part One)

2 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to a jam-packed arena. The pounding riff of "Walk" by
Pantera rocks the arena and has the crowd on their feet, cheering as an array of lights shoot through the arena. The ring has gray matting with the
AWA logo in the center of the ring, to the side are it's tense black ropes, and to the right of that the red turnbuckle pads. On the outside of the ring, thin black matting rests against the ground, to provide some sort of protection against the cold hard concrete. The camera pans through the sea of screaming fans before finally coming to rest at the announcer's table.]
Chris Thompson: Hello everyone and welcome in!! I am Chris Thompson alongside Steve Houston and we are live from the Greensboro Coliseum here in lovely Greensboro, North Carolina for the debut of AWA Sunday Night. The
Coliseum is packed to the hilt with thousands of anxious fans waiting in anticipation for this show to begin.
Steve Houston: Mark my words when I say that tonight will revolutionize the way we look at sports entertainment. Tonight a new era begins in the sport of professional wrestling!!
Chris Thompson: I couldn't agree with you more Steve. You and I have been in this business for quite sometime now and I don't think it would be stretch to say that this is one of the most anticipated debuts ever.
Steve Houston: Only one other comes to mind, that being the debut of the now defunct NCWA. [Suddenly!! "Symphony of Destruction" by Megadeath begins to rock the Arena] Chris Thompson: What's this?! ## You take a mortal man . and put him in control! ## ## Watch him become a God. ## ##Watch people's heads a roll! ## [A huge explosion of ice white pyro erupts at the entrance ramp. A silhouette of a man comes into focus through the cloud of lingering smoke.
As it settles the silhouette takes it's form. It's none other then Tony
Rome! Rome is wearing only a pair of jeans and brown boots.]
##Just like the pied piper, lead rats through the streets. ## #Dance like the marionettes. Swing to the symphony . of destruction!! ## [Rome makes his way to the ring, making no effort to acknowledge the fans along the way. Once ringside, he makes his way over to ring announcer, Matt
Wetterson and grabs the microphone. He proceeds into the ring to address the crowd.]
Rome: You people need to sit down, shut up and listen to what I got to say!! [A huge showering of boo's is brought about by this statement.] Rome: Now I don't know who is booking this crap here tonight but they seem to think that you people want to watch Tony Rome whoop up on some second rate, no talent that goes by the name Lord Bradbury.
[Some fat guy in the front row yells out "You Suck Rome!"] Rome: Shut your mouth tubby or I'll drag your hostess cupcake ass in here and knock off about twenty pounds. Now as I was saying . Tony Rome is a Main
Eventer. Let's face the facts, for years Tony Rome has carried the sport of professional wrestling on his shoulder and if it wasn't for me you people wouldn't even be here tonight!
["You suck", "You suck" chants begin to become more and more audible.] Rome: So here's the deal Richards . you put me in the main event against
Essex because whether I'm invited or not I will be in the Main Event here tonight!!
[With that said, "Walk" by Pantera echo throughout the arena. Strobe lights fill the entrance ramp as from the back steps the man himself, AWA President
Tommy Richards!! Richards dressed for success in a two-piece, black Armani suit. With microphone in hand he responds.]
Richards: You know Rome your right, these people deserve a memorable Main
Event. I mean after all this is our debut into the world of professional wrestling!!
Rome: Glad you see it my way. Maybe you aren't as stupid as you look. President Richards: Well coming from you Rome, I will take that as a compliment. So Rome you got your wish, tonight you will face Essex in the
Main Event!
[The fans erupt with the announcement of a Rome vs. Essex match-up.] Chris Thompson: WOW! Can you believe this?! Steve Houston: Rome vs. Essex! Now you're talking! President Richards: Oh by the way did I mention that there will be a special guest referee for that match?
[Rome with a look of concern on his face looks on.] President Richards: That's right. A special guest referee . none other then
Jonathan "Cutter" Chapel!!
[The crowd grows even louder and Rome's jaw just drops in disbelief. The music fires back up and Richards makes his way backstage.]
Chris Thompson: Oh my!! Not two days ago Rome was ripping on Cutter for retiring and now he will be officiating Rome's match later tonight!!
Steve Houston: Well Tony look on the bright side, at least you got the match you wanted. Chris Thompson: That he did! Well fans as Rome makes his way backstage the ring is cleared and we are set for our first match-up of the night as Derek
Craven returns to the ring to square of against Bryan Riggs.
Steve Houston: Craven is back, how cool is that! Maybe the return of the New
Breed will be soon to follow.
Chris Thompson: Only time will tell. Let's go to the ring for the introductions! ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Derek Craven vs. Bryan Riggs Written by: [name withheld] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Matt Wetterson: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first weighing in at 250lbs. He hails from Memphis , Tennessee . here is
Bryan Riggs!!
[A hard rock instrumental begins to play as the fans come to their feet. From behind the curtain emerges a young , well built Riggs wearing a navy and white singlet with boots to match. He makes his way ringside taking time to acknowledge the fans along the way.]
Matt Wetterson: And his opponent! Weighing in at 251lbs. He hails from the
"City of Brotherly Love", Philadelphia, PA. Here is Derek Craven!!!
[Two shots of flames shoot up on either side of the entrance ramp as "Cult of Personality" by Living Colour begins to play.]
##Look in my eyes .## ##What do you see?## ##The Cult of Personality!## ##I know your anger , I know your dreams!## ##I've been everything you want to be.## ##I'm the Cult of Personality!## [Out from the back wearing black, leather tights, a sleeveless t-shirt and a pair of silver wire shades perched on the bridge of his nose, steps none other then the "Devil" Derek Craven. He is well toned with a body built for strength and speed. His honey-brown hair hangs loosely behind him, just brushing the tops of his shoulder blades. There is a tattoo of a devil holding a pitchfork with the initials "D.C." underneath, on his right arm.
Craven makes his way to the ring focused on the match ahead. There is a sly smirk across his face.]
Chris Thompson: I got to tell you what , Craven looks to be in peak physical condition. He has really put an effort in this return to the ring.
Steve Houston: Like him or hate him, you have to respect this guy as an athlete. Craven knows what it takes to get the job done.
Chris Thompson: Well both men are in the ring and as the referee calls for the bell this match-up is officially underway! Craven and Riggs locking it up in the center of the ring and quickly Craven looks to take control with a standing side headlock. Riggs breaks loose by sending Craven into the ropes.
On the rebound Craven levels Riggs with a stiff , flying forearm smash! Steve Houston: Riggs is a bit disoriented after that shot. Not a good thing to be this early on in the match.
Chris Thompson: Craven brings Riggs back to his feet and sets him up and over with a nicely executed belly-to-belly suplex! Craven hops up to the second turnbuckle and drives and elbow down across the forehead of Bryan
Riggs.
Steve Houston: Craven has taken control of this match-up but Riggs is a top competitor so one can never count him out.
Chris Thompson: Craven going for a quick cover .. ONE!!!! TWO!!!! KICKOUT!! Chris Thompson: Riggs gets a shoulder up and the match continues. Both men to the feet and Craven grabs a hold of the head and drops Riggs to the canvas with a nicely executed reverse neck breaker! Craven back to a knee and begins unloading with some big right hands to the face of Riggs. Referee
John Odom warning Craven about the closed fists and look here! Craven shoves
Odom back!
Steve Houston: Craven's lucky he Odom doesn't disqualify him for that! Chris Thompson: Craven turns back to his opponent but catches a fist to the mid-section! Riggs on his feet and rocks Craven back with a series of right hands. Grabs the arm and Craven is sent into the ropes! On the rebound and a huge dropkick sends Craven crashing to the canvas and he quickly rolls to the outside to regain his composer.
Steve Houston: Riggs is doing the right thing here and follows Craven to the outside. You got the man reeling, so don't let up now!
Chris Thompson: Riggs grabs a handful of hair and force-feeds Craven the steel steps! Riggs scoops him up and slams Craven down to the hard floor below! Riggs hops up to the ring apron as Craven struggles to get back to his feet. Riggs leaps and connects with a solid double axe handle to the lower back and Craven is down once again.
Steve Houston: The referee is close to counting these men out but Riggs wisely rolls in and back out of the ring to reset the count.
Chris Thompson: Riggs brings Craven back to his feet and looks to send him crashing into that steel guardrail but Craven turns the tables and Riggs is sent crashing hard into the steel! Craven sending his opponent back into the ring and follows. Both men on their feet and Craven sends Riggs into the ropes. On the rebound and OH!! Devastating tilt-a-whirl backbreaker by
Craven!!
Steve Houston: Did you see that impact?! Damn near broke Riggs in two! Chris Thompson: Here's a cover .. ONE!!!! TWO!!! KICKOUT!!! Chris Thompson: That was a close one but Riggs isn't quite finished yet. Craven can't believe it and complains about a slow count but referee John
Odom won't hear it and the match continues.
Riggs is lifted back to his feet and Craven applies the cobra clutch. No wait! He reaches around, lifts and slams Riggs to the canvas!
Steve Houston: Awesome modified cobra clutch slam by Craven! Riggs probably doesn't even know what day it is after that one.
Chris Thompson: Craven back to his feet , measures up Riggs and drops down across the throat with a leg drop. This could be all she wrote!!
ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!! NO WAIT!!! Chris Thompson: I don't know how but Riggs manages to get the right shoulder up just in the nick of time and Craven is furious.
Steve Houston: I don't know how Riggs has managed to hang on this long. This kids got heart!
Chris Thompson: Craven cinches back with a reverse chin-lock and he is really wrenching back on that neck. The referee checking the hold and everything appears legal. Riggs is in some serious pain but yet he refuses to give in. Craven realizing that the hold isn't working releases. With a handful of hair Riggs is brought back to his feet. Craven sets the man up and oh! Nicely executed Northern lights suplex!!
Steve Houston: Craven has pulled out all the stops here in this match. I got to say I am impressed with what I have seen.
Chris Thompson: Craven back to his feet and this could be it as he gives the old cutthroat. Craven with a double under-hook of the arms and drops down, driving the face of Riggs hard into the canvas!!
Steve Houston: Chris, I believe he calls that move the "Hell Raiser". Chris Thompson: Craven hooks the legs . ONE!!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!! Chris Thompson: It's all over with! Craven is victorious in his AWA debut. Steve Houston: Very impressive victory and what a way to return to the ring!! <END PART ONE>
