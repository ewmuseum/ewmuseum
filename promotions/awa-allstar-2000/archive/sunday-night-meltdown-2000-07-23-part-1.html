Sunday Night Meltdown (Part One)

23 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade into "Intensifier" Alex Fury, standing outside a prominent establishment filled with scantily clad members of the female gender (read strip club). The weather this night is calm, with a gentle breeze rolling in from the south. A fine cuban cigar is held in one hand, and a longneck budweiser in his other. An extremely beautiful woman shrugged up against his shoulder. It's obvious she has just walked out of the bar, as implied by the tiny red g-string, and bikini top. Her assets are only covered by the long fur coat which is draped around her body. Fury is dressed in a white baseball cap, his sunglasses propped up on his forehead. A red
"Millencolin" t-shirt is worn on his upper torso, with long jean shorts and skater shoes around his lower body. He takes a quick puff of his cigar and then nods at the camera.]
AF: You might be wondering what the Fury is doing with such a fine lady? [The camera shakes, as if the camera man was shaking "no."] AF: Good answer my friend. Good answer. Now, I am very aware that I have a particular match-up with a particular wrestler, who in my personal opinion, is far below what I have to offer the AWA. I personally could care less what this federation thinks of me. I just feel that is my duty to let Mr.
Riggs know that any attempt on is part will result in one thing..... .... .... .... can you guess what it is? FAILURE!! [Fury nods and then procedes to walk down the street, the only illumination being the bright neon lights coming from the strip club.
AF: Now listen here Riggs. You are going down. My Intensidriver is gonna be laid on you like white on rice. This is gonna be a hardcore match-up, just like it is going to be tonight with this fine young lady.
[The 20-something year old chick giggles.] AF: ... So I'll have plenty of practice before I get to you. Expect to have your dreams rejected, your hopes denied, your body slammed, and your mouth stuffed with my fist. I don't have time for you, and I have much more important things to take care. So take your ego and shove it.
[Fury pushes the camera out of the way, just as the AWA logos replace the promo.] [The scene opens to a jam-packed arena. The pounding riff of "Walk" by
Pantera rocks the arena and has the crowd on their feet, cheering as an array of lights shoot through the arena. The ring has gray matting with the
AWA logo in the center of the ring, to the side are it's tense black ropes, and to the right of that the red turnbuckle pads. On the outside of the ring, thin black matting rests against the ground, to provide some sort of protection against the cold hard concrete. The camera pans through the sea of screaming fans before finally coming to rest at the announcer's table.]
CT: Welcome in everyone to AWA Wrestling! I am Chris Thompson, alongside
Steve Houston and we are live from the Memphis Sports Complex here in
Memphis, Tennessee! SH: We have one hell of a show lined up for everyone tonight with tonight events to be topped off with a no disqualifaction match between the "Outlaw"
Brody Bryant and Jason Essex. Sort of a grudge match after what went down last week! CT: No doubt about it as everyone has been talking about last week's main event when we witnessed Jason Essex being brutally powerbombed through a flaming table. Question is will the same thing happen tonight or does Essex have an Ace up his sleeve!
SH: And we can't forget about tonight's triple threat match which will pit
Tony Rome, Derek Craven and Kevin Fontaine up against one another!
CT: There is defiantly no love loss between any of those men and it should be one to remember! Well fans it's about time to get this show underway! Let
's go to the ring where Matt Wetterson is standing by for the introductions to tonights first match-up!
^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Lord Bradbury vs. Vincent St. Pierre Written by: [name withheld] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Matt Wetterson: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, weighing in at 264lbs. and hailing from Sussex, Great Britain! Here is Lord Bradbury!!!
["God Save the Queen", Britain's National Anthem begins to play and once again boo's begin to rain down. As the instrumental hits a high point from the back steps none other then Lord Bradbury dressed in knee high black leather hunting boots, brown hounds tooth trousers, a white Victorian shirt and brown hounds tooth vest and cloak he slowly makes as the boo's rain down from the stands.]
CT: Lord Bradbury has been on a bit of a decline these past few weeks. Tonight is his night to turn all that around! Matt Wetterson: And his opponent! Weighing in at 245lbs. and hailing from
Montreal, Quebec, Canada! Here is Vincent St. Pierre!!!
[As the Canadian National Anthem begins piping throughout the arena the boo' s grow to an awesome level. From the back steps Vincent St. Pierre wearing his traditional red and white tights with a maple leaf on backside and holding the Canadian flag high into the air, waving it left to right as he makes his way to the ring.]
CT: This match will pit two good brawlers up against one another. It's going to come down to who wants it more.
SH: This should be a good match Chris. St.Pierre is coming off a big win from last Sunday over Chris Magnum and Bradbury is sort of in a must win situation after his loss.
CT: St. Pierre also put Magnum on the shelf for a while and that must be in the back of Bradburys mind. Both men in the ring and there's the bell! They quickly lock up and Vincent takes control with a knee lift followed up by a hiptoss. As Bradbury hits the mat hard St. Pierre then drops an elbow down on to Bradbury's sternum. He tries for another but Bradbury moves just in time to avoid it!
Both men are back up on their feet now squaring off in the center of the ring. SH: St. Pierre took the early advantage with a nice series of moves but
Bradbury wasn't about to just ly there and take it.
CT: Bradbury rushes St. Pierre and spears him to the mat, clapping on a painful cross armbar submission. The ref asks St. Pierre if he's had enough but Vincent shakes his head no. St. Pierre is able to get to the ropes and break the hold. Bradbury lifts him up for a vertical suplex but St. Pierre shifts his weight and drops behind Bradbury and hits an inverted ddt!
SH: Damn what a move!! Vincent had great presence of mind there! That ddt bounced Bradbury's head off the mat hard!
CT: St. Pierre lands a vicious knee drop to Bradbury's shoulder he then lifts him up and whips him into the ropes. Bradbury rebounds only to be hit with a belly-to-belly suplex followed up by a standing moonsault! Here's a cover .
ONE! TWO!! THRRR . NO!! CT: St. Pierre only gets a two after that spectacular series!! SH: What is it gonna take to put Bradbury out? CT: St. Pierre then lifts up Bradbury and drops him with a bodyslam followed up by a powerdrive elbow. St. Pierre lifts him up and delivers a butterfly suplex that sends Bradbury crashing to the mat Vincent then scales the turnbuckle and hits with a guillotine legdrop and goes for the cover!!
ONE! TWO!! NO!!! CT: Only a two count again!!! Bradbury is digging deep down to stay in this match. SH: I was sure that leg drop would finish him off!! CT: Vincent pulls Bradbury to his feet only to receive a few shots to the gut. Bradbury then drops him with a double leg take down and keeping St.
Pierre's legs hooked catapults him into the turnbuckle. Bradbury picks him up and grabs St. Pierre in a headlock, St. Pierre throws Bradbury off into the ropes and as Bradbury comes back St. Pierre hits him with a running clothesline sending Bradbury sprawling to the mat. Vincent stands up and drops an elbow across Bradbury's throat. Vincent gets up, pulls Bradbury to his feet and sets him up for ddt. He drops backwards driving him into the mat!!
SH: St. Pierre is dominating his larger opponent. I'm not sure if Bradbury can come back from this!!
CT: I'm not sure if he's even conscious!! St. Pierre helps him to his feet and slings him off the ropes. As he comes off the ropes St. Pierre hits him with a flying lariat! Bradbury falls to the mat and St. Pierre climbs up to the top rope, leaps off but Bradbury gets his foot up and hits him in the face. Vincent stumbles back into the ropes, Bradbury gets up and hammers him with a running clothesline that almost takes St. Pierre's head off!!
SH: Well Bradbury proved me wrong. He is still in this match. He may be able to pull out a win here!!
CT: Bradbury lifts up his hapless opponent and drives him to the mat with a brutal jackhammer and goes for the pin .
ONE! TWO!! KICKOUT!!! CT: St. Pierre kicks out just in time. A frustrated Bradbury sets him up for a powerbomb but as Bradbury lifts him up, St. Pierre is able to kick off and deliver a stunning ddt!!
SH: St. Pierre just planted his opponent with a devastating Samurai Driver!! CT: I've never seen that move pulled off with so much force!! St. Pierre staggers to his feet and ascends the tope rope. He signals to the crowd and leaps off with a beautiful superfly dive which finds it's mark perfectly!!
St. Pierre hooks the leg and the ref makes the count . ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! SH: St. Pierre continues his winning ways here in the AWA!! CT: Bradbury gave it all he had but fell just short. There's always next week. Well fans as St. Pierre grabs his flag and makes his way backstage the stage is set for our second big match-up of the evening and the debut of
"Intensifier" Alex Fury whom you heard comments from at the top pf tonight's programming.
SH: From what I hear this cat's got major skills! ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ "Intensifier" Alex Fury vs. Bryan Riggs Written by: [name withheld] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Matt Wetterson: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first weighing in at 250lbs. He hails from right here in Memphis,
Tennessee . here is Bryan Riggs!! [A hard rock instrumental begins to play as the fans come to their feet to cheer their hometown hero. From behind the curtain emerges a young, well built Riggs wearing a navy and white singlet with boots to match. He makes his way ringside taking time to acknowledge the fans along the way.]
CT: Listen to these fans go nuts for Riggs! SH: There is nothing like a homecoming for a wrestler, it's kind of like having home court advantage!
Matt Wetterson: And his opponent! Weighing in at 242lbs. and hailing from
Seattle, Washington! Here is "Intensifier" Alex Fury!!
[The lights dim in the arena, bathing it in a curtain of blackness. On the big screen above the entrance the word "INTENSIFIER" in stylized blood red block lettering, appears one letter at a time, as the first words of "Last
Resort" by Papa Roach gain volume and bring the crowd into a frenzy.]
# Cut my life into pieces # I've reached my last resort # Suffocation, no breathing # Don't give a f*ck if I cut my arm bleeding [The intoxicating guitar solo resounds perfectly with the flashing strobe lights and fast paced, multicolored lasers which illuminate a man's body.
This man is none other then "Intensifier" Alex Fury. He takes two steps forward, and looks over the crowd as a huge face pop erupts throughout the arena. He thrusts both hands into the air to the applause of the arena. He then precedes down the entryway, his body in full view.]
[Fury's face is painted with determination, his deeply set, dark eyes give his face a dark chisled look. His short hair is messed up and uncombed, hanging lazily infront of his forehead. His well conditioned physique is covered by an oversized dull green t-shirt with NOFX emblazoned on the front and back. He cracks his neck a few times before continuing down the entrance ramp. Baggy navy blue jeans are slung low and tied around his waist by a belt, which cascade down to his gray and white skater shoes.]
SH: Fury is a newcomer here in the AWA and it'll be interesting to see what he brings to the plate. It looks as if Fury isn't wasting time!! He just took Riggs legs out from under him!!
CT: Fury circles Riggs as he slowly climbs to his knees. Just as it looks like Riggs will reach his feet, Fury grabs a hand full of his hair. However,
Riggs is able to send a clenched fist into the gut of
Fury. Fury stumbles backwards, and then receives second and a third! SH: Riggs finally reaches a stance. After throwing another shot into a stunned Furys head, Riggs whips him into the ropes. On his way back,
Fury feels Riggs's body lift up beneath him, sending him to the mat via a back body drop! CT: Riggs follows up with an elbow to the chest. Riggs getting up and begins to parade around the
ring, playing to the crowd but from behind, Fury delivers a quick neck breaker that puts Riggs on his ass. Fury then helps Riggs up the hard way and whips him into the ropes. Riggs, however, is able to slide out of the ring before hitting the ropes. Fury runs at the ropes and hits a suicide dive, He then picks up Riggs and rolls him into the ring. Fury immediately attacks with a few shots to the side. Riggs still manages to make it to his feet. Fury coninues his assault with a couple forearms to the chin, which backs Riggs into a corner. Fury then wraps his arms around Riggs's neck, and performs a tornado ddt, from the turnbuckle!
SH: What action!! These two are really going at it!! Fury is showing us a lot in the opening minutes of this match!!
CT: Fury has Riggs up once again. This time he sends him into the corner. Riggs hits the turnbuckle with a heavy impact! As if that wasn't enough,
Fury comes charging in with a mean clothesline, which sends Riggs down once again. Fury drags Riggs into the center of the ring, and quickly applies a boston crab. Riggs writhes in pain for a few seconds .
SH: The ref is asking Riggs if he wants to give up but Riggs is screaming no!!! CT: Riggs has taken a lot of punishment. He needs to break this hold soon and does as he is able to turn the crab over. Fury then lifts up Riggs and tries to roll up him for the pin but can't! REVERSAL! SUNSET FLIP! NO!
ANOTHER REVERSAL! HALF MOON CRADLE! NO! ANOTHER REVERSAL! BACKSLIDE PIN! NO a KICKOUT! Fury and Riggs stand up and stare each other down. Riggs is the first to react by reaching down and grabbing Fury's leg for a Dragon
Screw he then picks up Fury over his shoulder and delivers a devastating
TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER!! SH: My God! Fury's head made a sickening thud when it hit the mat he looks hurt!! CT: Riggs applies a triangle choke on the groggy Alex Fury but Fury is able to reach the ropes and break the hold. Both men are able to make it to their feet and square off once again. Fury drops low and surprises Riggs with a leg sweep that sends hit crashing to the mat. Fury quickly picks up Riggs and hits a hangmans neckbreaker!
SH: Shades of the late great Rick Rude there!! CT: Fury then climbs up on the second rope and does a flying leap connecting with his elbow across Riggs's chest. Fury then goes for the cover only to get a one count! Rggs is still in this match after that elbow drop.
SH: It' going to take more than that to put Riggs out! CT: Fury stands up, kicking and stomping on Riggs several times before climbing to the top rope and leaping off! But Riggs rolls out of the way causing Fury to land face first on the mat. While Fury is lying face down on the mat, Riggs sits on the back and applies a full nelson. He pulls back on the shoulders and down on the head!
SH: That is a nasty looking hold Chris! CT: The ref is asking Fury if he wants to end this but Fury is hanging in there! Fury moans as Riggs lifts him up, and looks at the crowd. Slowly, the fans realize what is going to happen. A mixed reaction fills the arena.
Riggs lifts up Fury for the last time, and puts him on his shoulders in a
Death Valley Driver position but Fury shifts his weight and brings him over in a crucifix to get a close two count!
SH: Fury almost had him that time!! CT: Both men slowly get to their feet and Riggs rushes Fury but Fury ducks and hits him with a back body drop that knocks the wind out of Riggs. Fury then jumps and delivers a corkscrew fist drop that makes Riggs head bounce a little off the mat!!
SH: That move was just nasty. Fury has a bad mean streak it appears. He is picking Riggs up
again!! CT: Fury has Riggs up, sending him to the ropes. Fury waits for him, using the momentum to whip Riggs around in a powerslam that rocks the ring. . Fury keeps the pressure up, sending Riggs to the ropes again this time he hits
Riggs with a textbook spinebuster. Fury picks up Riggs once again and locks him in position for a northern lights suplex but Riggs has the presence of mind to block it and fall back into a ddt!!
SH: Great reversal by Riggs!! Both men are showing some incredible stamina here Chris!! CT: Riggs tries to send Fury to the rope, but Fury locks his hand over
Riggs's wrist, pulling Riggs to him. Before Riggs can react, Fury grabs the top of his head, dropping down to his knees. Riggs reels back as his jaw hits the top of Fury's head, sending him crashing to the mat. Fury rubs his head, trying to collect himself. After getting his bearings back Fury lifts
Riggs up for a vertical suplex then dives forward executing a sweet gourdbuster. He then picks Riggs up again for what looks to be another suplex but drops Riggs sideways turning it into a vicious neckbreaker!!
SH: I've never seen a move like that before and apparently neither has the crowd as they are going nuts over it!!
CT: Fury has Riggs up, sending him to the ropes Fury connects with a nasty lariat that rolls Bryans eyes up into his head. Fury looks to the crowd as he picks up the seemingly dazed Riggs only to get a low blow for his troubles.
SH: The ref is letting the match go on. CT: Riggs hoists Fury up and underhooks his arms looking for a tiger driver but Fury fights out and backdrops Riggs over. He then does a baseball like slide into Riggs side. Fury has Riggs in a half- nelson Fury locks his free arm around Riggs waist and suplexes him over onto his neck!
That may just do it!! SH: The impact was all on Riggs head it may have knocked him out!! CT: Fury lifts up Riggs and lands a cradle piledriver that bounces the ring with its force. Fury then raises his arms and looks to the crowd. Riggs is somehow trying to stand but Fury having none of this leg drops him across the back of the neck!
SH: Fury is in complete control!! That move may have broke Riggs nose!! CT: All he has to do know is bring it home! Fury lifts up the prone Riggs and locks him in position for an inverted ddt instead of dropping back he lifts him up and goes over into an inverted jackhammer. He rolls Riggs over and makes the cover .
ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! CT: What a move and Fury has made an impressive debut with that win!! SH: Both men showed a lot in this match but Fury had some moves that Riggs couldn't deal with.
