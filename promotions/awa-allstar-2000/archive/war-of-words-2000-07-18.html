War of Words (7/18/00)

18 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All-star Wrestling Alliance War of Words July 18th, 2000 Featuring: - Jason Essex - Derek Craven - "Intensifier" Alex Fury - "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine ^v^v^v^v^v^v^^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Jason "Dream Machine" Essex ^v^v^v^v^v^v^^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ - Scenes of Sunday's card flash across a TV screen illuminating the face of Jason Essex and he looks far less than happy. He stands and goes to turn on the lights and we can see the mass of bandages covering most of his back. As the lights come on they illuminate a scene straight out of a Nightmare on Elm Street film. The room is
Cutter's " Workshop" Barbed wire is hung about the room; vises with bats half wrapped in wire sit on various tables a length of chain lays coiled in a corner various other bizarre implements are
scattered about. Essex looks up at the camera with a cold stare and begins- ESSEX: I came here to find just the right present to give The Outlaw And Rome. You put my partner and teacher on the self and injure my wife. All in all I'd say you boys are f*cked . Outlaw you son get the brunt of it just because I have a no dq match with your monkey ass.
You are going to bleed boy I feel the need to punish you slowly. Will it be with a barbed wire bat? Maybe a burning table?
- Essex gets an odd look in his eyes as he looks slowly about the room. He smiles a disturbing smile before going on- ESSEX: Yes.a burning table. A fitting bit of revenge wouldn't you say? To smell your flesh burn just like mine did? To hear your cries of pain would be music to my ears boy. First you then Rome..or maybe
Rome then you ( Shakes his head) It's off no matter both of you will pay and pay in pain boys. This has gone beyond a sport this is very very personal. And damnit I hate that I really do.
- Essex scratches his head and paces some more before continuing his rant- ESSEX: Rome, Outlaw I'm a f*cking wrestler not one of those hardcore jackasses. I could put either of you out without using a weapon but damnit that wouldn't be any damn fun now would it? Whatever the outcome win or lose both of you will be left on your backs praying to an uncaring god that you both will wise up and avoid me because if not the next time I see either of you pukes I'll put your asses out permanently!
- Essex walks over to a table and picks up a barb wire wrapped bat and turns back to the camera with that odd smile and promptly smashes it into the camera- ^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Derek Craven ^v^v^v^v^v^v^ [The scene fades in to a hallway in the back stage area shortly following
Sunday Night Meltdown...The camera begins to focus on a door that is at the end of the hallway. As the camera moves closer and closer you can read a sign on the door that says "Locker Room". The door slowly opens to find a seemingly vacant AWA locker room, as all of the wrestlers have already left the arena. The lights are off except for a little flickering light dancing off of the walls at a distance. The camera begins to move throughout the locker room, finally discovering where the source of light is coming from.
As the scene comes more in to focus you see a dark silhouette of a man standing back in the corner over a circle of candles. He carefully moves around the candles to make himself visible, revealing that it's none other than Derek Craven. Blood still pouring from his forehead, sweating profusely, Craven lets out a devilish cackle as he looks deep in to the camera and begins speaking in a soft yet stern tone.]
Craven: "Rome...I can't hold anything against you for what you did tonight. I have to admit, I asked for it. No doubt about it. However, I hope that you are in your hotel room, kicking back and relaxing, and completely absorbing what exactly it is that you have done..."
[Craven looks away from the camera for a moment, shaking his head in disgust, and then turns his back to the camera and begins speaking again.]
"Rome, I have to hand it to you. Not too many men would have the balls to come out and do what you did to a complete mad man like myself, but that says a lot about you, now doesn't it?"
"It tells me that you are just as sick and twisted as the Devil, that was quite evident from the stunt you and Bryant pulled in the main event against
Cutter and Essex." One thing that you definitely have now, my friend, is the Devil's attention."
" OH YES, You definitely do...I just hope that while your relaxing and taking in the nights events, that you are ready for a complete and totally devastating reaction, not only from myself, but the rest of those that you have chosen to cross the line with."
"At this point, it looks like you're a wanted man, Rome, and that's your plan, isn't it, Tony? I could see it from the beginning when you came out on the first ever AWA card and started making demands of everyone. You knew what you were doing, and I must say, it wasn't a bad idea at all...That is, if you are willing to face the circumstances and repercussions of your actions like a man..."
"Oddly enough, I have word that I, the Devil himself is in quite the position next Sunday Night. It's Me, You and that piece of trash that calls himself the Delinquent, Kevin Fontaine."
"As I think of the possibilities of the sheer horror that could come from such an explosive situation, I can barely contain my excitement."
[Craven turns and faces towards the camera once again, his face completely covered in blood, slightly raising his voice as he begins to speak again.]
"Rome, YOU SEE THIS!?!?! [Craven points to his bloody face and begins to laugh like a psychopath, he moves towards a row of lockers and begins to repeatedly ram his head in to them time and again, causing the horrible gash to open wider and more blood to spill, then, looking back towards the camera, nearly blinded by the flowing blood.]
"Rome, I can guarantee you one thing about next Sunday night. The bloodshed in that match will make Friday the Thirteenth look like a day in Mr. Rogers
Neighborhood..."
"I can only hope that you are willing to dance with the Devil, look me dead in the eye and go face to face with me and see what the Devil is capable of.
I just want you to understand one thing. You can do whatever you want, run me over with a damn Semi, I don't care, but you can bank on the fact that
Derek Craven will just keep coming, AND COMING until you have completely beaten me within an inch of my life, or worse....At the same time, Rome, I hope you are prepared for the fight of your life on Sunday Night. I
WILL_NOT_QUIT on Meltdown until I am covered in YOUR BLOOD, TONY ROME!" [Craven moves and sits down on one of the benches, he motions for the camera to come closer.]
"As for you, Kevin Fontaine...I can't say that if Rome would not have interfered in our match that I would have defeated you, because I have no idea what the outcome would have been....BUT, regardless the outcome and how it happened, you have gained the respect of yours truly."
"It's Obvious, Fontaine, that while you do have a big mouth and tend to stick your foot in it from time to time, you do have what it takes to go straight to the top of the AWA, there's no denying that....I'm just glad to see that you and I have another date next week, because in my mind, I've got a lot to prove with you."
"I just want you to understand one thing...All of that crying and whining that you and Rome did about the silence of Derek Craven has only gotten you this...Next Sunday Night, you're going to get so damned much of Derek
Craven, you won't even know what to do with yourself, because by the time the night is over, Fontaine...The Devil will have you right where I want you."
"What you got from me tonight is one thing, what you get from Derek Craven next week, will make what happened tonight seem like a walk in the friggen park...Whether you are proud of how you gained your victory over me tonight or not, that makes no difference, simply because it's going to take Tony
Rome, and a whole lot more to put the Devil on his back on Meltdown...That's a promise....It's been a long time since I've had someone drive me in this sport like you do, Fontaine...I can't really explain why, it might have to do with your complete cocky ignorance, or it may be the fact that you have somehow gotten it in to that messed up head of yours that attaining your goal and achieving supreme success here in this federation by capturing the
AWA championship is going to be an easy task....Well, Fontaine, as time goes by, and we get to know each other a little better, you will come to realize that when Derek Craven is involved, especially when I have made it a top priority of mine, NOTHING_IS_EASY, and Fontaine, I promise you that if you, somehow, some way, find yourself as the King of the AWA, You will have gone to hell and back to get there."
"I have no problems with your goals and aspirations, Mr. Fontaine...just realize one thing....The Devil has those EXACT_SAME goals and aspirations, and is willing to do WHATEVER_IT_TAKES to ensure that no matter who stands in my way, whether it be You, Rome, Bryant Or Essex....They will all fall down as I inevitably rise to the top of the All-Star Wrestling Alliance!"
[Craven looks down at the floor and then raises his head, looking deep in to the camera, his blue-green eyes glistening like crystals as the blood continues to poor from his face.]
"Fontaine and Rome, the first two weeks of the AWA are going to seem like a trip to Disney Land after we have our battle next Sunday Night...As I have promised before...The AWA will become the Devil's playground, and, next
Sunday Night is just the beginning."
"I warn the both of you....HELL_IS_'ROUND_THE_CORNER!!!!!!!!" [With that, Craven begins his psychotic cackle once again as the camera begins to move away...He stands up, and, again begins to repeatedly pound his head against the locker doors as the scene fades away....]
^v^v^v^v^v^v^^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ "Intensifier" Alex Fury ^v^v^v^v^v^v^^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ [Fade into the parking lot behind the arena, with darkness having set in fully. A high pitched whine electrifies the air, followed by the quick growl of a motorcycle engine. A man pulls around the corner, into the parking lot, and speeds towards the camera. He clamps on the brakes, and cuts the engine. He steps off the motorcycle in haste and removes his helmet. He quickly unzips out of his racing suit to reveal his street clothing. A black shirt with the words "The Offspring" stenciled on the front. Bright silver shorts, obviously too long, hang down from his waist.
White and gray skater shoes and white socks cover his feet. His dark, almost navy blue eyes radiate determination. His fair complexion seems to fit his light brown, uncombed hair. He's of medium build, standing about six feet tall. His lean figure seems to bellie his strength and confidence, his chin held in the air. Suddenly, from off camera, two security guards move in, as if to apprehend this man.]
SC1: Excuse me sir, I'm gonna have to ask you to leave. MAN: I have full permission to be on AWA premisses tonight gentlemen. Observe. [He pulls out his guest AWA pass.] SC2: Sorry sir. Move along... Mr. "Intensifier" Alex Fury... [after glancing at the pass] [Fury moves inside the building, with the camera man walking ahead of him.] AF: Why are you following me? CM: I'm supposed to. AF: Why? CM: That's what they told me to do. You're part of the AWA's new talent, right? AF: So? Get away from me. CM: We're supposed to have an interview with you. AF: For AWA? [... he sighs audibly.] Fine. [Fury looks around to find a place to sit down. He grabs a chair and spins it around, sitting on it facing the camera-man. He seems relaxed and at ease as he brushes the hair out of his eyes.
CM: So, you've had quite a history. Why don't you tell the fans how you began your career.
AF: Well, I started wrestling not too long ago. Must have been 1994. I was
17 back then. My Dad was in the military, and he was stationed in Japan for a number of years. My Mom was just a housewife and traveled around with us.
Japan's kinda boring when you don't understand the language ya know? I mean, I picked up some, but for the first few years there, I was clueless.
Anyways, my "American" friends got me into puroresu, which I loved. I mean,
I was growing up watching the World Collegiate Championship Wrestling and
World Wide Wrestling, those were the two big feds, and I always loved that stuff. But it just didn't compare to what I saw in Japan. Anyways, when I saw that, I knew I wanted to do it. I had a mixed martial arts background, having taken Taekwondo since I was nine, and Jujitsu since I was 16. I heard a lot of the guys in the federations were experienced in that, so I decided to try and get into one of the puroresu feds. Unfortunately, I had no clue how to wrestle.
CM: So what did you do? AF: I found a Puroresu dojo. This guy named Akira Musashi, aka The Iron
Tiger, had just gotten back from his tour of Mexico. He opened up his own wrestling school. I was his only other student besides Ishiro Kawasaki. He tried me well. Anyways, I eventually made it away from his school in once piece and signed a contract with the Neo Japan Pro Wrestling Association.
It worked out pretty well, but I was a rookie, and a gaijin, which is a bad combination. I basically got fed up with it, and came to America. I hopped on the AWMC, but I suffered some pretty nasty injuries in combination for the extremist title. They decided to let me go, so here I am, ready to kick some ass in the AWA>
CM: Sounds cool. Any last words? AF: Yeah. Watch out. The Intensifier is coming through. [Fade out.] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine ^v^v^v^v^v^v^^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ [scene opens back stage after AWA Sunday Night. The camera seems to be roaming the halls, looking for someone to get some words from. The camera moves down a long hallway, and as it turns to follow the hallway to the right, the camera suddenly is severely jolted, as the mic picks up a dull
*thud* sound. The camera after much commotion comes to rest apparently hitting the ground. A fat leg, which is assumed to belong to the camera man is all that can bee seen aside from the dirty tile floor. A voice can be heard at this point, it's tone rather upset, but not totally angry.]
Voice: Dammit, why don't you watch where you are going... [The fat leg moves out of view now, and two feet can be seen shuffling around in front of the floored camera. The camera man apparently picks up the camera as this point, as the is a whole lot of jostling before it comes to rest looking right into the face of Kevin Fontaine. His long black hair is hanging in his eyes, wet as though he probably just got out of the shower. His face is seemingly set in stone, and the expression he wears seems to be one of anger mixed with frustration. When he speaks, his voice seems to be very measured, as if he doesn't want to let his true emotions be know.]
Fontaine: So, the mighty Derek Craven has finally spoken. [Fontaine begins to chuckle, although his face never changes expression] Fontaine: You know, Craven, you were right, you must have a sense of humor. You run around referring to yourself as the Devil?!? And you talk like I'm some no name wrestler just looking to make it big off of YOUR name?!?!
Craven, up until you hit me from behind with a chair, during a match that you weren't even involved in, I had NEVER ever HEARD of you. Yet you talk like everyone is trying to ride your name to fame. Fact of the matter is
Derek, I don't hate you, and I certainly don't loath you. But most of all I absolutely do NOT respect you. Respect is earned. You have done absolutely nothing to warrant receiving my respect. I faced off against far worse men then you, men far stronger, far more intelligent, and far more deserving of respect. Most of those men I have faced off with and beat. Just like I will beat you on Sunday, when me and you step foot in the ring once more.
[Fontaine swipes his hair out of his face with his hand, then continues to speak] Fontaine: The thing is, it's not just me and you on Sunday. Oh, no, the higher up figured it wouldn't be right to have the "mighty" Derek Craven beat out-right so early in the fed's history, so they added on one Tony
Rome.
Fontaine: Which is more than alright by me. Rome, I have a debt to settle with you. You had no right to interrupt my match with Craven... you sure as hell were not welcome. So come Sunday you too will get to feel my wraith.
Rome, thus far you, too, have done absolutely nothing to warrant respect. Sure as death is the high cost of living, come Sunday, Rome, the debt will be settled. Fontaine: Sunday, Craven, Rome, and the whole AWA will learn why I am going to be the first AWA World Champion...
Fontaine: Now get the hell out of my way, I have a plane to catch. [Fontaine walks out of the picture as the scene fades to black}
