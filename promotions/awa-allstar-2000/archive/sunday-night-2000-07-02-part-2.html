AWA Sunday Night (Part Two)

2 July 2000

[Scene cuts to the backstage area where we see Cutter dressed in jeans and a referee's shirt. He is asking Chanel how he looks. She replies, "Like a million dollars." Cutter responds with, "That's all?"]
[Scene switches and we see Craven walking through the curtains into the backstage area. He is wiping the sweat from his forehead with a towel, seemingly not paying much attention to where he is going and ends up bumping into "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine. A short stare down follows before Craven continues on his way.]
CT: Well fans we are about ready for the introductions for our next match-up of the evening, which will pit Lord Bradbury of Great Britain against
Vincent St. Pierre of Canada. Let's go to the ring for the introductions. ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Lord Bradbury vs. Vincent St. Pierre Written by: [name withheld] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Matt Wetterson: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at 245lbs. and hailing from Montreal, Quebec, Canada!
Here is Vincent St. Pierre!!! [As the Canadian National Anthem begins piping throughout the arena the boo' s grow to an awesome level. From the back steps Vincent St. Pierre wearing his traditional red and white tights with a maple leaf on backside and holding the Canadian flag high into the air, waving it left to right as he makes his way to the ring.]
Matt Wetterson: And his opponent!! Weighing in at 264lbs. and hailing from
Sussex, Great Britain! Lord Bradbury!!!
["God Save the Queen", Britain's National Anthem begins to play and once again boo's begin to rain down. As the instrumental hits a high point to big burst of blue pyro shoot off at the entrance ramp and from the back steps none other then Lord Bradbury dressed in knee high black leather hunting boots, brown hounds tooth trousers, a white Victorian shirt and brown hounds tooth vest and cloak he slowly makes his way to the ring ignoring the peasant fans along the way.]
SH: Talk about arrogance! Look at this guy, he thinks he's the cream of the crop. CT: No doubt about it we have two of the most arrogant individuals in that ring right now but on the bright side, all that arrogance should prove for a great match-up! Both men in the ring now and Bradbury removes his cloak, carefully folding it and placing it in the corner of the ring.
SH: Come on, this is wrestling not Martha Stewart. CT: Whoa! Bradbury doesn't waste any more time as he attacks St-Pierre before the timekeeper can even ring the bell! Lord Bradbury staggering his opponent with a series of massive left and right combos to the face that sends St-Pierre stumbling back into the corner. Bradbury driving a knee into the mid-section , not once but twice and now sends St-Pierre cross corner with an Irish whip.
SH: St. Pierre hit that corner with some serious impact and look here! Bradbury with a full head of steam, sandwiches St. Pierre against the turnbuckles with a big splash!
CT: Vincent St-Pierre staggers out of the corner and does a face plant down to the mat. Lord Bradbury going for a the win!
ONE! TWO!! NO!!! CT: St-Pierre manages to kick out on two and a half. Bradbury brings St-Pierre to his feet and sends him into the ropes. On the rebound Bradbury looking to nail his opponent with a clothesline but St-Pierre avoids it with a duck under move. Vincent stopping , turning and rocks Lord Bradbury back with a pinpoint dropkick to the chest. Bradbury still on his feet but is finally brought down after a nicely executed legsweep from St-Pierre.
SH: St. Pierre is giving up about twenty pounds in this match, it may be within his best interest to try and keep Bradbury down on the mat as much as possible.
CT: St-Pierre measuring up Bradbury and drops down across the throat with a legdrop. St-Pierre now heading for the top rope. St-Pierre wasting valuable time now as he plays onto the fans, inadvertently allowing Lord Bradbury to get to his feet. Lord Bradbury over to the ropes and sends St-Pierre flying all the way back down to the mat with a huge press slam!!
SH: Bradbury should be going for a pin right here but he is allowing himself to become distracted by the fans and this allows St. Pierre to recompose himself.
CT: Bradbury looks to bring St-Pierre back to his feet but he is stunned with a cheap shot to the groin! St-Pierre to his feet and off the ropes, nailing Bradbury with a clothesline that sends him up and over the top rope and to the arena floor. St-Pierre waits for Bradbury to stagger back to his feet and then launches himself over the top rope and finds his mark perfectly as he comes crashing down on top of Lord Bradbury!
SH: Did you see that! St. Pierre hurdling himself over that top rope without any concern for his own safety. Lucky for him he hit his target.
CT: St-Pierre on his feet and lifts Bradbury to his as well and slams him head first into the steel guardrail. St-Pierre relentless in his attack , goes under the ring and brings out a table, which he sets up using the ring and the guardrail as a prop. St-Pierre brings Bradbury back to his feet and after catching him with a couple big right hands to the jaw, sets him onto the table and begins to make his way to the top turnbuckle!!
SH: If St. Pierre hits this then this one is all over with! CT: The fans are erupting as Vincent St-Pierre climbs the turnbuckles and after patting himself on the back, leaps but Bradbury rolls from harms way and St. Pierre crashing hard onto the table, snapping it in two. Bradbury rolls the injured St. Pierre back into the ring and goes for the cover.
ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! CT: It's all over and Lord Bradbury is victorious. SH: I think St. Pierre may have seriously injured himself in that fall through the table. The referee is calling the medical team into the ring now and they are bringing the Stretcher. That's never a good sight to see.
CT: Bradbury is standing over the fallen St. Pierre and is ridiculing him. The referee finally pushing him back, ordering him out of the ring but the damage is already done.
[Bradbury is showered with boo's as he makes his way to the back.] [Scene cuts backstage where Chris Michaels has caught up with Tony Rome for a few words about his upcoming match.]
Michaels: Rome, how are you feeling about your upcoming match with Jason
Essex now that Tommy Richards has announced that the special guest referee will be none other then Cutter.
Rome: How do I feel?! How do you think I feel?! It's obvious that there is some sort of conspiracy going on here against yours truly.
Michaels: But you were in fact the one that demanded the match with Essex. Rome: Yeah, I demanded the match with Essex but I don't remember making any requests for a special guest referee, especially that no talent Cutter.
Michaels: Some would say that you made your own bed and now you have to lay in it. How do you respond to those people.
Rome: All I got to say is that Cutter better call this match down the middle because the last person he would ever want to cross paths with is Tony Rome.
Now little man why don't you go play in the street. [Rome walks away, leaving Michaels standing there holding the microphone. The scene slowly fades back to the announcer's table.] CT: Sounds like Rome is starting to feel the pressure as the main event draws closer. He obviously would have to be concerned about Cutter being the special guest referee.
SH: No doubt it. I mean just a couple days ago he was ridiculing Cutter on national television and now that same man is going to officiate a match between Rome and his best friend. The odds are against Rome in the one and in a major way!
CT: There is no question about that! Well fans let's go to the ring for the introductions to our next match.
^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine vs. Chris Magnum Written by: [name withheld] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Matt Wetterson: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, weighing in at 242lbs. He hails from San Jose, California! Here is
Chris Magnum!!
[AC/DC's "Thunderstruck" begins to play as the crowd comes to their feet. Out from the back steps the "Blonde Bombshell" Chris Magnum dressed in his green and white trunks. He plays to the crowd as he makes his way to the ring drawing on all the cheers.]
Matt Wetterson: And his opponent! Weighing in at 220lbs. and hailing from
Seattle Washington! Here is "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine!!!
["Break the System Down" by My Own Victim blasts through the arena. The entrance ramp fills with a thick cloud of smoke as the lights in the arena dim. There is a series of explosions of red pyro and out from the back steps
Fontaine. Wearing a long, baggy pair of jean shorts and a black t-shirt with the word "Stenchwhore" across the front, Fontaine makes his way to the ring.
Slapping a few hands before reaching the ring. He climbs to the apron and catapults himself over the top rope.]
CT: Fontaine looks to be the perfect example of intensity. Just take a look at those eyes as the pierce right through Magnum.
SH: Both these guys are very talented and have spent some time in Japan, so you know they have got the determination. This out to be one hell of a match.
CT: The referee goes over his final instructions and there is the bell! It doesn't look like Fontaine is going to waste anytime as Magnum gets nailed with a charging axe-handle bodyblock!! Magnum quickly back to his feet but that is short lived as Fontaine hits a spinning leg lariat to send him right back to the canvas!
SH: Fontaine is taking it straight to Magnum , he isn't even allowing him room to breath! CT: Fontaine climbs the turnbuckles and sets up high atop. Magnum getting back to his feet and is nailed with a flying body press!! Fontaine is in total control as he brings Magnum back to his feet but OH!! Magnum pulls an
Ace out of his sleeve and rakes the eyes of Fontaine. Magnum scoops him up and executes the airplane spin!! Magnum rolls across the back of Fontaine with a rolling knee and here is a cover!
ONE! TWO!! NO!!! CT: Fontaine gets a shoulder up and the match continues. SH: The momentum is shifting! CT: Magnum hits Fontaine with an elbow from the second turnbuckle and Fontaine is in some trouble now. Magnum brings him to his feet and measures him up for a single arm DDT that tattoo's Fontaine to canvas! Magnum quickly back to his feet and goes off the turnbuckle with a flying summersault splash!! What a move!!!
SH: Magnum is quite possibly one of the best athletes in the business and it's moves like that one that gets you there!
CT: Magnum with another cover attempt! ONE! TWO!! NO!!! CT: Fontaine refusing to give in. Magnum bringing Fontaine back to his feet and sending him into the ropes. On the rebound and Fontaine ducks under a clothesline attempt , bouncing back and nails Magnum with a flying lariat!!
Magnum quickly back to his feet only to be met with yet another lariat!! Fontaine has gotten a second wind as he scoops Magnum back up off the canvas and TIGER BOMB!!! That could be it!!
ONE! TWO!! THR . NO!!! CT: That was a close one there but Magnum just does get a shoulder up. Fontaine to the top rope now as Magnum staggers back to his feet. Fontaine leaps and nails Magnum with a stiff Missile dropkick that sends Magnum crashing back down to the mat. Fontaine applies an ankle lock submission and the pain is apparent across the face of Magnum but he refuses to give in. He fights his way to the ropes and the referee calls for the break. Both men will get to their feet but it is Fontaine who plants a boot to the midsection of Magnum. Sets him up and a nicely executed reverse neckbreaker
Fontaine and a cover!
ONE! TWO!! NO! SH: Magnum is taking some serious punishment but refuses to quit. CT: Fontaine with a knee to the gut and Magnum is sent stumbling into the corner. Fontaine follows him in with a big clothesline! Fontaine now begins to unload with some massive knife-edge chops and look at the chest of
Magnum! SH: He may have broken a few blood vessels!! CT: Fontaine grabbing Magnum around the neck and there's a vicious tornado
DDT!!! This could be it as Fontaine signals to the crowd. Magnum is brought to his feet and look here!!! Derek Craven has hit the ring with a steel chair!!
[WHACK!!] SH: Craven plants it right across the back of Fontaine. The referee calls for the bell and Fontaine will get a victory by disqualification thanks to
Craven's interference but nonetheless he took one hell of a chair shot!!
CT: Security hits the ring to hold Craven back and this is unbelievable! I don't know Craven's motivation for this attack but apparently there is some bad blood between these two men.
