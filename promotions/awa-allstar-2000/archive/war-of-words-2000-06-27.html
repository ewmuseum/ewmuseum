War of Words (6/27/00)

27 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"War of Words" - 6/27/00 Contents: "Commissioner" Tony Rome "Deliquent" Kevin Fontaine Jason "The Dream Machine" Essex ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ "Commissioner" Tony Rome ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ (Scene opens and we find ourselves outside of the front doors of the AWA headquarters. A black limo has just pulled up curbside. The driver steps out , walks to the back and opens the door. Out steps a tanned, well built individual. He is dressed casually in a pair of jeans and a black silk shirt which hangs freely and is unbuttoned showing off his chest and abs.
He removes the sunglasses from his face, looks up at the building and then into the camera.)
Tony Rome: Well , well , well!! The "Commissioner of Wrestling" Tony Rome has arrived to the AWA and from what I hear they have that World title all polished up and ready to sit around my waist. Believe me after looking down this roster of (*cough* *cough*) talent, that shouldn't be to hard to accomplish. There was one name however, that stuck out and struck a cord with me and that name was Derek Craven.
Craven, I thought you wised up and retired from this sport?! Haven't you realized by now that you don't have what it takes?! It looks like I am going to have to teach you a lesson Craven. I am going to have to prove to you and the rest of the world that you don't belong in the sport of wrestling. Don't take it personal Craven, but you just don't have it and you never will. You are nothing without the rest of the New Breed ... hell you were nothing with them. You are a cheater, a liar and a backstabber Craven, nothing more.
Limo driver: But sir .... Rome: I know , I know but at least when I do it , I make it look classy! (Rome proceeds through the large revolving doors and into the lobby. On the wall hangs the pictures of several of wrestlings brightest stars. Rome takes a quick look and then points at on picture inparticular.)
Speaking of guys that just don't make the grade. Jason "Dream Machine" Essex and his "buddy" Cutter have also signed contracts with the AWA. Surely they must be devolpmental contracts as they two guys couldn't even fight their way out of a wet paper sack. I mean look at Cutter, here is a guy that once held numerous titles but where is he now?! A ring valet!!
Come on Cutter what happened? Things get a little to rough for you in the ring? Jason "Dream Machine" Essex, I now understand why they call you the "Dream
Machine". You had to be dreaming when you decided to choose pro wrestling for a career. I mean talk about a career that has gone nowhere!
(Rome takes a drink of his spring water and continues. Now this sunday night I will be taking on none other then Lord Bradbury. Well Bradbury I hope you are up to the challenge of stepping into that ring with the likes of Tony Rome. I know you have been around for a while now and from what I understand you're not half bad, but Bradbury I can promise you that you have never stepped into the ring with anyone that even comes close to matching the likes of myself.
(Rome flexes his right bicep for the camera.) See Bradbury I have faced the best that this sport had to offer and one by one I have defeated everyone sent in my direction. I have dominated the competition worldwide and you Bradbury, well you aren't even worth the sweat on my brow. I'm not just going to defeat this Sunday night , I am going to humilate you! You will be made an example of Lord Bradbury. No hard feelings though. Afterall it's my job , I just happen to love what I do. So get ready becuase the "Commissioner of Wrestling" is on his way to lay down the law!!
^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ "Deliquent" Kevin Fontaine ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ [Scene opens to total darkness. The screen is entirely black, and there is no real sounds being heard. A very light breathing can be heard faintly, the only thing that makes everyone realize that this isn't some sort of technical error. It stay this way for a moment, and then the is a rustle sounds heard, shortly followed be a abrupt dull "thud". The rustling sounds resumes briefly, then the silence and light breathing resumes again. A few second pass before there is a very distinct sounds, loud compared to everything thus far, almost a sort of *Twang*. The silence resumes for a very brief moment before a loud *PPPPHHFFFTTT* sounds as the scene suddenly erupts into light. The flame on a silver metallic Zippo is seen to be the source of the light, as it is moved to the cigarette hanging from the mouth of the man who produced said light. The man's face, which the flame seems to only half illuminate, is seen to be fairly thin, his skin seemingly stretched taunt over his thing face, as if he has been half way to hell and back, and never had an easy time of it. The man's hair is long and black as far as can be told, hanging partially in his eyes. Three tiny little blond braids adorn each side of the front of his face, the light from the flame playing weirdly with the shadows that seem to hide everything of his appearance, leaving an aura of mystery around him. He brings the Zippo to the cigarette that hangs from his mouth, taking a few quick drags to ignite the cigarette, and exhaling a almost blue twinge of smoke that seems to linger in front of his face, making the shadows and high lights of his face distort. Just as quickly as it began, the man extinguishes the flame of the
Zippo, and the screen once more turns to black, the only thing different from the first time being the small orange cherry of the cigarette which almost seems haunting in it loneliness. The cherry burns bright orange for a second as the man takes a long drag from it, just barely illuminating the edges of his face, before it return once more to a dim color. The man can be heard giving a long drawn out exhale, before he begins to speak, his voice seemingly measured, controlled.]
MAN: So it begins... the life of a new federation, a entirely new battle ground, all comes to head this Sunday night in Greensboro, North Carolina. I will be there, I will be prepared, and once again, like so many times before, on so many different battle grounds, I WILL be victorious. The thing is......
[The man takes a long drag off the cigarette as the cherry once more grows in brightness only to return to it's dull tone. The man can be heard once more giving out a forceful exhale before he continues where he left off]
MAN: ... this isn't about victory, it's not about the glory, or the fame, or the woman, the money, or the nice cars. This is entirely about perseverance, about being the top dog. It's about challenging one's self, about making a statement about what is right. All these wrestlers running around now a days don't seem to understand. It's not about conforming to some lame idea, or running around under some false identity cause you think it will get cheers.
This is about being who you are, fighting for something you believe in. [The man takes another drag from his cigarette, his voice seemingly a little louder when he begins to speak once more]
MAN: And I believe in on thing, and one thing only....... ME! I believe in the power of individualism, I believe that success lies in being true to yourself. I know that I am right to, as this philosophy has done me amazingly well in my previous excursions into the squared circle. I have carried around the belts, and faced off against the biggest names in the industry and came out on top. I've come to AWA, well, for something new, something different. Some new fodder, if you will.
[Another pause as the cherry brightens once more, it's movement seemingly in time lapse on the screen.]
MAN: Chris Magnum, I know nothing of you, nothing of your past. While I am in no way a seer of the future, I do know enough about yours. I know that
AWA Sunday Night, you get to start your career in this new federation with a big, fat, disappointing **L**. That's right Chris, come Sunday night, you get to lose. Don't feel to bad, none of you should when you lose to me. It doesn't mean you're a loser, it just means you're not as perseverant as me.
You see, I have been through hell, and I haven't been back long enough to forget what it is that I hold true to. All the little conformists out there need only look at where I have gotten myself, to realize that they are indeed WEAKER! I fight for all those out there who believe in themselves, who fight for society, who refuse to be another barcode.
[Another pause as the man drags his cigarette, his face hauntingly outlined in the weird illuminations given by the bright cherry]
MAN: AWA, I am here for one purpose, to further myself, and I will beat each and every wrestler here before it is said and done. Bottom line, I'm gonna be on top, and no one, not the president, not my fellow wrestler, not even society, can hold me down...
MAN: Oh...... and allow me to introduce myself.... I am the "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine [Fontaine drags long and hard on the cigarette before he crushes it out against the wall behind him in an explosion of sparks. The screen goes right back to the total darkness of before as the sparks die out the silence returning for a second. He gives another long audible exhale, and when he speaks once more his voice is light, seemingly bordering a laugh]
FONTAINE: Trust me...... you'll remember it..... FADE OUT ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Jason "The Dream Machine" Essex ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ - The scene opens on the spacious Manhattan apartment of Chanel And Jason Essex. Essex looks slightly peturbed as he flips through channels only to find nothing on. He is dressed in a pair of jean shorts,black high tops,and a Punisher t-shirt. He stops on a rerun of Simon & Simon lets out a sigh and almost slams the remote onto the glass top coffe table. He stops just before it hits and looks around and sees only the camera.-
Essex: Chanel would have killed me if I broke that table`.(Shakes his head) Let's talk about career fustration. My short six month stint in this grand sport has been semi-fruitful.One minute I'm main-eventing the next on low cards and in multiman tag matches. All in all it bugs the piss outta me.
What does that mean here? It really means nothing I just want the AWA to know where I'm coming from I intend to be a major player here no matter what cardboard cutout the suits decide to put in my way.
-Essex leans back in his recliner and lets out a breath while running a hand through his long blue and black hair. He turns his deep blue eyes back to the camera and continues.-
Essex: The first guy I get to vent on is Vincent St-Pierre. A poor soul whom has no clue what I'm capable of. I've left a trail of broken bodies through four federations Vinny. Ok really three I did a favor for a guy in a fed one match then I high tailed it outta that piss hole. I'm not coming in here looking for allies or any type of ass kissers hell the fans I'm not to keen on either but they pay the bills. Vinny you are in for a very bad night my friend.
-The door opens and in walks Chanel dressed in jeans,a green midrift t-shirt and combat boots. Her long brownish blond hair is tied back and she has one small bag of groceries.-
Essex:I thought you were going shopping? Chanel:(Giggles) I did the rest will be up in a few babes: -Essex archs his eyebrow at the camera.Soon we can hear the sound of some poor over burdened man coming out of the elevator. After a minute a bunch of heavy bags walks in the door and a strained voice calls from behind them.-
Voice: Whatch ya heads!!!! - The bags walk into the kitchen and the amused Essex follows. Once in the kitchen the bags collapse revealing the other half of Jason's management team Jonathan"Cutter" Chapel dressed in a black Armani suit. He turns and sees Essex and starts ranting.-
Chapel: There I was minding my own biz in a small corner market and to what appears to my eyes? Your lovely wife asks if I can help get her bags up here..I looked in the back of your jeep and see two bags..Being the type of chap I am I agree...Then she opened the trunk.
-Chapel shakes his head in horror at the remembered site.- Chapel:Bags upon bloody bags!! -Essex chuckles and shakes his head before finishing up.- Essex: I have a feeling before it's all over the AWA will regret signing us. Between me and ol Chapel here we can cause quite a ruckus. Chapel: Damn right...I may come out of my semi-forced retirement... -Essex looks up alarmed at the cheshire cat smile on Chapel's face as the scene fades out. Your screen goes black for a second before it suddenly snaps back to Essex's kitchen.-
Essex: I wasn't finished you ninny. Anyway I'll see you Sunday Vinny and after we're done you will never wanna see me again.
Chapel: Imagine that! Cutting to black before the man is done! -Chapel gives a slow cut sign as the scene fades to a small circle around Chapel's head.- Chapel:Thhh That's all folks.
