This opened the door for Rome, Richards and Bryant to send Essex through a flaming table yet once again for the victory. - Nothing has been settled between Craven and Fontaine. This past Sunday saw the two meet face to face in the ring but the battle would end after the two men took a spill off the top rope and ended up being counted out.
