All-star Wrestling Alliance - http://majestic.home.mindspring.com/main.html Players Guide v1.0 Introduction: So just what is the AWA?! Well AWA stands for All-star Wrestling Alliance and we are one of the many e-federations on the net today. I am
[name withheld], owner and president of the AWA. I was introduced to the world of e-wrestling in the Spring of 1997 by an online buddy of mine and as most I began my career as a handler of such characters as Lone Wolf and "High
Flyin' Jeremy Bryan. It wasn't long after my induction that I decided to try my luck at an executive role and I took the position as head card writer for the USWA. After the demise of the USWA I took a job as Vice President of the
MSCW. I would remain there in that role until December of 1999. After taking a few months off I decided to return to e wrestling but this time as the CEO of my own e-federation, thus the AWA was born.
How things work: The AWA is a booked e-federation which simply put means that ones success depends on his/her level of role-playing and overall participation. We are heavily dependant on storylines here in the AWA, which means we needs handlers that will do their part in promoting their angles, this is done through role-playing and on card appearances such as interviews, sneak attacks and such. The more you participate the more intense and meaningful your angles will be ... however, winning and losing usually always boils down to who did a better job role-playing. If you have any further questions regarding this subject, feel free to email us at
[detail withheld] The staff of the AWA: Executives- [name withheld] - Owner/CEO of All-star Wrestling Alliance. This is the guy that signs your paychecks, so you better be nice to him.
Vacant - Vice President [name withheld] - "Tony" is the Commissioner of the All-star Wrestling
Alliance. He is famous for "screwing" people over so one may want to try and stay on his good side.
Broadcasters - [name withheld] - "The Voice of the AWA" has been with us since the start. Not biased in matches, just likes to watch good wrestling and have a good time. Chris has been around the business for quite some time, broadcasting for the now defunct NCWA and FWO.
Steve Houston - After a career ending injury, Steve took to broadcasting as a way to stay in a business that he loved dearly. He can be biased at times but for the most part keeps a level head.
Interviewers- Chris Michaels - Chris is currently the only interviewer in AWA. Not only is he in charge of in-ring interviews, but he also has to spend the rest of his time backstage looking for a juicy interview. He loves his job, but sometimes will go to far to get a story.
Ring Announcer- Matt Wetterson - Wetterson has been in the business for quite some time now. He is the most respected and well-known RA in all of wrestling, and considering that ring announcers don't get much fame that is quite an accomplishment.
AWA Championship titles: AWA World title - This is the granddaddy of them all. Only the best and brightest stars will carry this title around their waist. Hard work and dedication is what the World title is all about.
AWA United States title - A prestigious title in it's own right. This title is a great stepping-stone to the World title.
AWA Television title - This is a title for the young and upcoming stars of the AWA and/or those still polishing their skills in the ring.
AWA World Tag Team Titles - This is the granddaddy of the tag teams division. Teamwork and dedication is what it will take if you want to wear these titles around your waists.
