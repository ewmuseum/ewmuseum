Pantera rocks the arena and has the crowd on their feet, cheering as an array of lights shoot through the arena. The ring has gray matting with the
AWA logo in the center of the ring, to the side are it's tense black ropes, and to the right of that the red turnbuckle pads. On the outside of the ring, thin black matting rests against the ground, to provide some sort of protection against the cold hard concrete. The camera pans through the sea of screaming fans before finally coming to rest at the announcer's table.]
Chris Thompson: Hello everyone and welcome in!! I am Chris Thompson alongside Steve Houston and we are live from the Nashville Civic Arena here in Music City USA, Nashville, Tennessee! The Arena is packed to the rafters with thousands of AWA's finest wrestling fans waiting in anticipation for this show to begin.
Steve Houston: If this weeks show is anything like last weeks show then it will defiantly be worth the price of admission.
Chris Thompson: The big question this week is just who will be Tony Rome's mystery opponent for tonight's main event.
Steve Houston: Rome is no slouch and the guy has plenty of friends in low places, so your guess is as good as mine!
Chris Thompson: Well fans, let's go to the ring for our first match-up of the evening!! ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Lord Bradbury vs. Bryan Riggs Written by: [name withheld] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Matt Wetterson: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Bryan Riggs!!

CT: Bradbury isn't making to many fans here in the AWA but the guy has what it takes to get the job done. He proved that to us last week.
SH: Well, whether the fans love him or hate him the guy is here to do a job and collect a paycheck. They can boo all they want, but the fact of the matter is they paid to get in here and he's getting paid to be here.
CT: Well both men are in the ring and this one is set to get underway. There
's the bell and Riggs isn't wasting any time as he goes straight to the attack!! Bradbury gets nailed with a charging axhandle bodyblock from
Riggs!! Riggs puts the boots to Bradbury now before dropping down with a knee to the forehead!!
SH: That's gotta hurt! CT: Bradbury gets thrown into the corner and Riggs choking the man with his boot!! The referee trying to break thing up and faintly does, but as
Bradbury staggers out of the corner he is met with a short-arm clothesline!! SH: Looks like Riggs isn't too happy with the officiating as he is in the face of the referee sharing some words of kindness.
CT: I'm sure they're real kind words! Bradbury has gotten back to his feet and as Riggs turns , Bradbury tries for a neck scissor takedown but Riggs avoids it! Riggs with a dropkick and now using the ropes as a springboard to execute a twisting moonsault on to Bradbury!! Riggs climbs to the top turnbuckle and awaits Bradbury to get to his feet and as he does Riggs jumps from the top rope and hits Bradbury with a flying armdrag!!
SH: I gotta say , I'm impressed by the ring skills of the Riggs. I mean this guy may be a little green to the sport but he can sure wrestle!!
CT: Riggs with an Aztecan suplex on Bradbury sends him back to the mat and once again he heads to the top. Riggs leaps off the turnbuckle with the flying bodypress and Bradbury is out of it! All Riggs needs to do is make the cover but instead he looks to punish Bradbury even more. Riggs sends
Bradbury to the corner of the ring and follows him in with a vicious clothesline!!
SH: Riggs is making a point to everyone in the back and the point is that
Riggs is to be taken seriously and with caution!!
CT: Riggs with a jumping DDT on Bradbury and this has got to be over with now! Riggs lifts Bradbury to his feet and he plants him down with the front face stunner and that is all she wrote ...
ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! CT: Riggs scores an impressive and quick win here tonight over Lord Bradbury. SH: Bradbury didn't seem focused at all and was completely dominated! He needs to regroup and comeback next week more focused on winning.

Chris Thompson: It's Craven! The silent assassin is about grace us with his presence! Steve Houston: FINALLY! Craven has been stirring up a lot of trouble as of late and it's about time he explains his actions here in the AWA. Number one, what he did to Kevin Fontaine last Sunday Night? The "Delinquent" has had a lot of things to say about Craven since that brutal attack during a match that Fontaine had well in hand.

Devil's point of view...
[Just as Thompson finishes, Craven positions himself in the center of the ring and pulls a microphone out of his long black trench coat. The lights still very dim, Craven begins to speak, pacing about the ring.]
Craven: Without pussy footin' around, let's get right down to business! First of all, Tony Rome. You humor me boy, you really and truly do. You come out here and claim that the Devil is done and might as well retire, and stop wasting everyone's time.
[Craven laughs] Well Mr. Rome, you and I both know that as soon as the crowd starts to flood in to the arena, EVERYONE's on the Devil's time. You and I have had somewhat of a history between us. That's no secret. The funny thing is, is that a no talent bum like you is still trying to get a cheap rub off of a much bigger name than yourself.
Take our esteemed president for instance. Who the hell isn't going to stir the pot when you come out here and make demands on the big cheese. It makes you seem a little bigger, badder, maybe "better" than you actually are. You and I both know, Rome, that you have played second fiddle to yours truly for so long you can't even stand it!
Well, you're gonna get your chance, once and for all to back up those big, bold words that are coming out of your mouth like runny s*#t!
It's just about your time to dance with the Devil, Rome...You've got two choices, BOY! either get every piece of trash in that locker room to watch your back like a hawk, or grow eyes in the back of your head, because I'm coming to prove you wrong. DEAD_WRONG!
Now, as for my opponent later on this evening. Delinquent payment, Kevin
Poontang. Kevina, your words humor me, more than anyone else. That is one thing that you have going for you, I do have a sense of humor. I hope you realize that it's never as easy as you are hoping. If you're thinking that the rest of the AWA roster is just going to lie down and roll out the red carpet for your no name ass, you've got another thing coming. If you are under the impression that later on tonight, our match is going to be a walk in the park. You're in for a rude awakening.
[Craven runs his hand through his hair and looks deep in to the camera.] Fontaine, by the way you speak, it's obvious that you are scared stiff at the thought of stepping in to the ring with a man that you know nothing about. You have no idea what I'm capable of, and you have no idea what I just might do to make you eat each and every derogatory word that has come from that big mouth of yours.
If you haven't noticed, I'm the type of guy that isn't afraid to go out and
TAKE what he wants. The only thing I want from you, Kevie, is for you to be face down and ass up at the end of our match tonight, just the second rung in the ladder that the Devil will inevitably climb and conquer.
NO_DOUBT_ABOUT_IT! Beginning tonight, the AWA becomes the Devil's Playground. I'm going to do what I want, when I want, and I DARE, ANY, two bit, junk bitch from the back to just try and stand toe to toe with the Devil and live to tell about it!!!
[Craven begins to look out towards the crowd as an evil scowl comes across his face.] Ever since the first day that I decided to enter this business, I've heard nothing but doubters, telling me that I'm not big enough, or strong enough to accomplish anything in this sport that I have loved ever since I was a knee high, snot nosed brat. I've proved them all wrong, regardless of what
ANYONE has said, the Devil has prevailed time and again. The AWA will be No different.
[Craven begins to motion towards the fans.] All of you people are witnessing the re-incarnation of the Devil himself. Fontaine, if you have any sack down there between those weak knees of yours, bring your ass on down to the ring tonight and you will know what it's like,
FIRST HAND to go toe to toe with the sickest, most sadistic human being walking the face of the planet.
Kevin Fontaine, TONIGHT, I'm out for blood, and there's not anything that's going to stand in my way to ensure that when its' all said and done later on tonight, it will be you, looking up at the Devil in complete and total admiration. In complete awe, regretting every idiotic word that has come from that mouth of yours. When it's all said and done my friend, you might not like me. As a matter of fact, you might down right loathe Derek Craven.
But there is one thing that you can bet your bottom dollar on, Pal, and that is YOU_WILL_RESPECT_ME!
