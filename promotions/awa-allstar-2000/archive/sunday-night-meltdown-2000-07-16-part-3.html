Sunday Night Meltdown (Part Three)

16 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Tony Rome / ??? vs. Essex / Cutter Written by: [name withheld] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Matt Wetterson: The following tag team contest is scheduled for one fall and carries no time limit. Introducing first at a total combined weight of 507 pounds! They are led to the ring by the lovely Chanel! Here is the team of
Jason "the Dream Machine" Essex and Cutter!!!
["Freak On a Leash" by Korn fires up and a few bolts of lighting shoot down from the rafters, exploding at the entrance ramp. Out from the back steps
Chanel adorned in a tight fitting , red gown. She is followed by Essex who is dressed in a pair of black pants with red dragons down the side of each leg and Cutter who wears ripped jeans, black and green tiger striped wrestling boots, and a Green Hornet t-shirt. They make their way to the ring acknowledging a few of the fans along the way.]
CT: The fans definitely seem to be behind these two here tonight, which could ultimately work in their favor.
SH: I don't know if I'd get to close to either of these guys. I mean on one side you have a loose cannon and on the other, you have a guy not playing with a full deck if you catch my drift.
Matt Wetterson: And their opponents! At a total combined weight of . [Wetterson is abruptly cutoff as "Symphony of Destruction" by Megadeath fills the arena.] [A huge explosion of ice white pyro erupts at the entrance ramp. A silhouette of a man comes into focus through the cloud of lingering smoke.
As it settles the silhouette takes it's form. It's none other then Tony
Rome! Rome is wearing only a pair of jeans and brown boots.]
Rome: Cut the music!! Ladies and gentlemen it gives me great pleasure in introducing to you the man that will help me stomp the life out of those two cockroaches in the ring. Ladies and Gentlemen, I give you the most dangerous man in professional wrestling . "Outlaw" Brody Bryant!!!!
[The music fires back up and through the curtain steps the man they call
"Outlaw"! A snarl across his face is barely visible through the long black hair that hangs down in front of his face. His ring attire consisting of a pair of black jeans and faded brown cowboy boots. He and Rome begin their way to the ring.]
SH: I wouldn't believe if I didn't see it with my own eyes! The "Outlaw" has returned to the sport of professional wrestling in the good ol' US of A!
CT: Fans may not remember the story behind the name, but as it went Bryant was banned from just about every wrestling organization imaginable in the
U.S. because of the situation between him and his former employer.
SH: Well when you snap your bosses spine by powerbombing him off of a balcony and through a flaming table the job offers tend to stop coming in until now that is!
CT: Don't look now but we have ourselves a situation inside the ring as a brawl has broken out! All four men going at it and the referee has no choice but to call for the bell and start this match. Rights and lefts are being exchanged in the center of the ring.
SH: These guys have just thrown all the rules right out of the door and are just simply beating the hell out of one another!
CT: Rome just caught a hard right to the jaw and down he goes. Cutter working him over now with the boots. Stomping away at the chest but here comes Bryant and Cutter is sent up and over the top rope with a stiff clothesline! Bryant turns and catches a boot to the mid-section by Essex.
Doubled over and Bryant is sent crashing to the mat with a nicely executed double underhook suplex!
SH: Looks like we have regained some control in the ring as Cutter and Rome are both at their neutral corners. I'm sure the referee is thankful for that.
CT: Essex measuring up his opponent and drops down across the chest with an elbow. Back to his feet and with a handful of hair he brings Bryant back to his as well. Essex to his corner and makes the tag to Cutter. Cutter in and ties Bryant up with a standing wristlock. Reversal by Bryant and Cutter is sent into the ropes. On the rebound and he ducks under a clothesline attempt. Rebounds once again and this time Bryant connects with a solid flying axe handle to the forehead!
SH: Did you see the momentum in that impact! Cutter's neck snapped back and damn near off his shoulders!
CT: Essex charges in but he is caught and slammed hard to the canvas! Rome in as well and we have a double team effort on Cutter. Rome climbs to the top turnbuckle as Bryant sets Cutter up for a piledriver. Rome leaps and uses gravity to add impact to the maneuver!
SH: Cutter's neck has to be Jell-O. First the flying axehandle and then a double team effort on the piledriver!
CT: Referee John Mitchell sending Rome back to his corner and the match continues on. Bryant lifts Cutter up into the air and side slams him back to the canvas!
SH: Some serious damage could be happening to that neck and the more punishment it takes the more serious the consequences could be.
CT: Bryant tagging in Rome who comes in planting a boot right across the forehead of Cutter. Rome brings Cutter back to his feet and sets him up for a vertical suplex but it's blocked! Rome tries to execute the maneuver once again but Cutter once again manages to block it and reverses it sending Rome over and down to the canvas!
SH: Cutter had to dig deep for that one. Now he needs to desperately make the tag to Essex.
CT: Cutter slowly crawling towards the outreached hand of Essex but just as he is about to make the tag he is cutoff by Rome! Essex tries to enter the ring regardless but he is quickly cutoff by referee John Mitchell and this opens the door for some double-teaming by Rome and Bryant.
SH: Rome sends Cutter into their corner and now look here! Bryant is using some lacing from the turnbuckle padding to choke Cutter and Rome goes over to keep the referee distracted.
CT: Finally the referee regains his focus and Bryant is forced to back off. Rome brings Cutter back to his feet and sets him up for and executes a belly to back suplex into a pinfall combo!
ONE! TWO!! THR . NO!!! SH: I can't believe it! Somehow, someway Cutter was able to kick out. CT: Rome can't believe it himself and has a few words with the referee. It appears that he may be complaining of a slow count but that argument won't hold much water and the match continues. Rome looks to bring Cutter back to his feet but Cutter cheap shot to the groin and Rome goes down!
SH: Cutter needs to make a tag right here and Essex is more then ready to get in there. CT: Cutter slowly making his way towards the outreached hand of Jason Essex. He better hurry though because Rome is getting back to his feet. THERE'S THE
TAG!!! In comes Essex he makes quick work of Rome, putting him on his back with a clothesline. Bryant enters the ring but he catches a boot to the midsection followed by a reverse neckbreaker and Essex is on a rampage!
SH: The "Dream Machine" entered the ring and made quick work of Rome and
Bryant but he better not get himself to worked up because he still has a match to win!
CT: Rome to his feet and he is sent into the ropes by Essex. On the rebound and Rome is met with a super kick to the jaw! Essex hooks the leg.
ONE! TWO!! NO!! CT: Rome just does get a shoulder up. Essex brings him back to his feet, scoops him up and slams him back to the canvas. Essex heading to the top turnbuckle. High in the air and he leaps coming down and finding his target with a big splash!
SH: Essex should be going for the pin right here but instead he tags in
Cutter. Probably not the best thing to do considering the punishment Cutter took early on in the match.
CT: The tag is made and Cutter enters with payback on his mind I'm sure. Cutter grabs the legs and turns Rome over into a Boston crab! The pain is evident across the face of Rome but he refuses to give in. Cutter applying more pressure to the lower back but still Rome refusing to give up.
SH: Rome reaching out for the ropes and he is just inches away. If he could just get a little more slack. He's got it!
CT: Rome finally does reach the ropes and the referee calls for a break. Cutter back to his feet and brings Rome back to his as well. Rome favoring that lower back of his as he is sent into the ropes. On the rebound and he is caught and planted to the canvas with a sidewalk slam! Cutter in control of things now.
SH: Cutter throwing a few taunts Bryant's way and the big has had enough and tries to enter the ring but the referee is there to quickly cut him off.
CT: A tag by Cutter and in comes Essex. Double team effort by the duo as
Rome is brought to his feet and sent into the ropes. On the rebound and he is met with a double clothesline and down he goes. Essex bringing Rome back to his feet and sets him up on the top turnbuckle. Climbing up himself he latches on . BELLY TO BELLY FROM THE TOP!!! Here's a cover .
ONE! TWO!! NO!!! CT: Bryant in to make the save! Here comes Cutter and once again all four men are in the ring. Cutter nails Bryant with a forearm shot to the head and the "Outlaw" is sent staggering into the corner. Essex charges in from across the ring but Bryant pulls the referee into the path and he is sandwiched in between Essex and Bryant!
SH: Quick thinking by Bryant top pull the referee into the line of fire but now there's no referee!
CT: I have a feeling that's what Bryant wanted as Rome gets to his feet and nails Cutter from behind with some sort of foreign object. Possibly a pair of brass knuckles. That leaves Essex in there to fight off the attack of two men. Bryant hammers Essex with a big right hand and then plants him to the canvas with short arm lariat!
SH: Don't looking now but Rome has just went to the outside of the ring and is going underneath. He pulls out a table and slides it into the ring. He also has some sort of plastic bottle in his hand and is handing it to
Bryant. CT: I could be wrong but I think that was lighter fluid he handed him! WHOA!! Chanel has just leaped onto the back of Rome desperately trying to help out her men and oh my God! Rome grabbing her by the hair, flings her over his back and slams her down!
SH: There is padding on the outside floor but it is barely an inch thick. Defiantly not enough to absorb the impact. Chanel looks to be hurt. CT: Rome without thinking twice heads back into the ring. Bryant has the table set up and is now soaking it with the fluid inside that plastic bottle. Reaching into his pocket and Bryant tosses Rome what appears to be a lighter. Rome holding it into the air and now sets the table a blaze!!
Bryant bringing Essex to his feet. He sets him up and POWERBOMBS HIM THROUGH THE FLAMING TABLE!!! SH: Can you believe this?! Essex has just been powerbombed through a table of fire! CT: Essex is quickly pulled from the ring by outside attendants and somebody needs to get in that ring quickly and put a stop to these two men's actions.
Bryant pointing to Cutter who is still lying motionless but thanks goodness that security is there to pull him out of the ring.
SH: Well no doubt Essex and Cutter will get the win by disqualification but the damage has already been done.
[Suddenly everyone's attention is turned towards the ramp. Standing there observing the chaos in the ring is none other then "Delinquent" Kevin
Fontaine.] SH: I don't know what this is about but "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine has just appeared at the entrance ramp. He doesn't appear to be making any motions toward the ring, so one must assume that he is just here to observe.
CT: Fans, I hate to say it but we are all out of time here tonight!! So for
Steve Houston, I am Chris Thompson saying GOODNIGHT!!!
