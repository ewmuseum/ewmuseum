Sunday Night Meltdown (Part Two)

16 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine vs. Derek Craven Written by: [name withheld] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Matt Wetterson: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Falls will count anywhere! Introducing first, weighing in at 251lbs. He hails from the
"City of Brotherly Love", Philadelphia, PA. Here is Derek Craven!!!
[Two shots of flames shoot up on either side of the entrance ramp as "Trip
Like I Do" by Crystal Method begins to play.]
[Out from the back wearing black, leather tights, a sleeveless t-shirt and a pair of silver wire shades perched on the bridge of his nose, steps none other then the "Devil" Derek Craven. He is well toned with a body built for strength and speed. His honey-brown hair hangs loosely behind him, just brushing the tops of his shoulder blades. There is a tattoo of a devil holding a pitchfork with the initials "D.C." underneath, on his right arm.
Craven makes his way to the ring focused on the match ahead. There is a sly smirk across his face.]
CT: Craven coming to the ring with the look of complete intensity across his face. This outta be one heck of a match.
SH: There is defiantly no love loss between Craven and Fontaine. Considering that they barely know who each other are they won't have any remorse when it comes down to tearing each other apart!
Matt Wetterson: And his opponent! Weighing in at 220lbs. and hailing from
Seattle Washington! Here is "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine!!!
["Break the System Down" by My Own Victim blasts through the arena. The entrance ramp fills with a thick cloud of smoke as the lights in the arena dim. There is a series of explosions of red pyro and out from the back steps
Fontaine. Wearing a long, baggy pair of jean shorts and a black t-shirt with the word "Stenchwhore" across the front, Fontaine makes his way to the ring.
Slapping a few hands before reaching the ring. He climbs to the apron and catapults himself over the top rope.]
CT: The referee quickly getting between these two men as it appeared Craven was ready to get this one underway. Both men taking a few steps back and the referee calls for the bell and they lock it up in the center of the ring.
Standing side headlock is applied by Fontaine and Craven struggles to get free and finally does sending Fontaine into the ropes. On the rebound and
Craven drops to the mat and Fontaine hits the cross side of the ropes and is then leveled by a swift spinning leg lariat. Craven quickly jumps to the second rope , springboards into a backflip moonsault but nobody is home as
Fontaine rolls from harms way.
SH: Quick start to this match-up as both men seem to be fighting with true warrior-like spirit. CT: Well these guys are professionals and in this sport you gotta give it your all. Fontaine brings Craven to his feet and sends him into the ropes , on the rebound Craven is sent to the mat with a vicious lariat. Fontaine hops up to the top turnbuckle, leaps and crashes down across the chest of
Craven with a guillotine legdrop. He goes for the cover ...
ONE! TWO!! NO!!! CT: Craven just does kickout before the count of three. Fontaine to his feet and begins to head to the top turnbuckle once again. Fontaine may be taking to much time here and Craven is able to get to his feet. Looks like Fontaine may be in a little trouble!! Fontaine turns to set up for his maneuver but is instead met by a fist to the gut and is promptly shoved off of the turnbuckle and is sent crashing to the canvas! Craven points to the turnbuckles and he could be looking to finish Fontaine off here.
SH: Craven isn't usually known as much of a high flier but the guy is multifaceted to anything is possible.
CT: He climbs to the second turnbuckle and sets up. He leaps and CONNECTS with a knee to forehead and here's a cover .
ONE! TWO!! NO!!! CT: Fontaine got his foot on the rope and the pin is broken up! SH: That's what you call ring smarts. Knowing where you are in that ring at all times , is a must if you plan on making it in this sport.
CT: Craven gets to his feet and is now in the face of the referee! He has grabbed the referee by the collar of the shirt and cocks back his fist but from behind Fontaine rolls him up ...
ONE! TWO!! THR . NO!!! SH: Craven just does manage to kick out of that one! CT: Craven rolls to the outside of the ring to try and regain himself but
Fontaine goes leaping over the top rope and connects with a huge Suicide dive!! That took a lot out of both men as they struggle to get to their feet.
SH: Falls count anywhere in the match-up so neither man is safe on the outside. CT: Fontaine getting back to his feet and he brings Craven to his as well. He sets him up and send him for a ride into the cold steel of the guardrail! Listen to these fans go nuts as the fight comes directly to them. Fontaine grabs a steel chair and approaches Craven but Craven reacts with a shot to the gut and Fontaine drops the chair.
SH: If there is anyone that you don't want to give any advantage on the outside of the ring, that's Derek Craven.
CT: Craven with the steel chair now and he drives it down into the small of
Fontaine's back. Craven now placing the chair on the floor and grabs a hold of Fontaine and brings him to his feet. Setting him up and DDT right onto that steel chair!
SH: Fontaine is in some serious trouble now as Craven has taken control of this match-up. CT: Craven now placing the arm of Fontaine in between the seat and backrest of that steel chair and this doesn't look good for Fontaine. The referee trying to put a stop to this but Craven just shoves him out of the way and
OH NO!! The referee smacks right into the steel turnpost and down he goes!
SH: No referee! Craven now has a free pass to inflict any amount of pain that he wishes too!
CT: Craven climbs up to the ring apron and it appears that he may be looking to break the arm of Fontaine! He leaps but Fontaine is able to roll out of harms way! Craven can't believe it and as he turns he is met with a kick to the gut. Fontaine grabs the hair and slams Craven face first into the steel steps!
SH: Don't look now Chris but Tony Rome is making his way towards the ring! CT: Rome rushing ringside. The action is right here in front of us folks! Rome telling Fontaine to back away because Craven is his but Fontaine isn't too happy with Rome's intrusion and shoves his back. Rome fires back with a stiff right hand to the jaw of Fontaine. Rome grabs a hold of Fontaine and slams him head first into the guardrailing. Rome now places the head of
Fonatine between his legs, sets him up and POWERBOMBS him right through our commentary position!!
SH: This is unbelievable! Talk about interactive! CT: Rome turns and now focuses his attention toward Craven. Rome grabbing a steel chair and SLAMS it right into the forehead of Craven and he has been busted wide open!! Rome now pulls Fontaine out of the rubble that used to be out table and drapes him over the chest of Craven. He now shakes the referee and tells him to count .
ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! SH: Fontaine gets the win but not until being powerbombed through a table! CT: Fans we need to take a quick break while we get the ring crew out here to piece back together this mess! Don't go anywhere, the main event is next!!
