AWA Sunday Night (Part Three)

2 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CT: As security clears the ring, the stage is set for tonight's Main Event! SH: The pressure has to be really getting to Rome by now. In just a few minutes he has to step into the ring knowing the odds are not favoring him.
CT: Let's go to Matt Wetterson for the introductions! ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Tony Rome vs. Jason Essex Special guest referee: Cutter Written by: [name withheld] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Matt Wetterson: The following contest is scheduled for one! Introducing at this time, tonight's special guest referee! Jonathan "Cutter" Chapel!!
[Cutter steps from the back to a ovation of cheers.] Matt Wetterson: Introducing first! Weighing in at 280lbs. and hailing from
Hell's Kitchen, New York. He is accompanied to the ring by the lovely
Chanel! Here is Jason "The Dream Machine" Essex !!! ["Brackish" by Kittie fires up and a few bolts of lighting shoot down from the rafters, exploding at the entrance ramp. Out from the back steps Chanel adorned in a tight fitting , red gown. She is followed by Essex who is dressed in a pair of black pants with red dragons down the side of each leg.
They make their way to the ring acknowledging a few of the fans along the way.] Matt Wetterson: And his opponent! Weighing in at 252lbs. He hails from
Minneapolis, Minnesota! Here is Tony Rome!!!
"Symphony of Destruction" by Megadeath begins to rock the Arena] Chris Thompson: What's this?! ## You take a mortal man . and put him in control! ## ## Watch him become a God. ## ##Watch people's heads a roll! ## [A huge explosion of ice white pyro erupts at the entrance ramp. A silhouette of a man comes into focus through the cloud of lingering smoke.
As it settles the silhouette takes it's form. It's none other then Tony
Rome! Rome is wearing only a pair of jeans and brown boots.]
##Just like the pied piper, lead rats through the streets. ## #Dance like the marionettes. Swing to the symphony . of destruction!! ## [Rome makes his way to the ring, making no effort to acknowledge the fans along the way. The boo's are heavy.]
CT: A look of concern across the face of Rome as he steps into the ring with not one but two men that don't particularly like him.
SH: Rome is going to have to literally fight two men tonight. Essex is the psyical sense and Cutter in the mental sense.
CT: Well both are ready and Cutter calls for the bell! Rome and Essex tie up. Rome with a headlock but Essex able to escape and sends him into the ropes. On the rebound and Rome with a leapfrog, but Essex catches him in mid air and brings him down with a huge powerslam!
SH: Good thing Rome is in such great shape. That slam would of broke the average man in two!
CT: Essex is now standing over Rome, inviting him to get up. Rome peeling himself off the canvas glaring at Essex and Cutter. They tie up once again.
Rome with an arm wringer. He twists the arm of Essex really tightening down. Rome is climbing up the ropes with that wristlock and combination armbar on Essex. Rome is on the top rope but Essex uses his free arm to hiptoss Rome off the ropes! Rome quickly back up but Essex takes him immediately down with a clothesline!
SH: Everytime Rome thinks he has Essex in a pinch, Jason turns the tables. Rome has got to focus. CT: Rome back to his feet and Rome may have underestimated his challenger. Rome and Essex tie up. Rome sends Essex into the ropes and he nails Essex with a vicious right hand to the gut. Rome sends Essex into the corner and runs in with a knee right to the mid-section! Essex's face writing in pain. SH: Rome is taking to much time. He needs to stay on the attack! CT: Essex grabs his leg, and he grapevines the leg right through into a rollover, and he holds onto the leg. Rome is now screaming in pain. Essex with a submission move that is sending pain right through the body of Rome.
SH: Essex has that move cinched in. Rome is trying to reach for the ropes. CT: Rome is sliding towards the ropes. Essex is doing everything he can. I doubt he'll get the submission right here, but you never know. And at worst, it'll wear down Rome. Rome is about six inches from the ropes. The crowd is going wild..
SH: Rome reaches the ropes. The referee commands him to break. He starts the count to five. Essex breaks the hold at four.
CT: Rome gingerly back to his feet but Essex runs and clips the legs! Rome nearly flipped over and landed on his head! Essex immediately picks up Rome, and now he is starting to pound the head of Rome. We have seen Essex diversify his style here so far. Essex throws Rome to the ropes. Essex hits the ropes and tries to hit a high knee! Rome blocks it! He knocks Essex down, he grabs that knee.
SH: This is his chance to reverse things. CT: Rome takes the leg, and he grapevines it and turns it over to a half
Boston crab! That's one of Rome's signature moves! Essex is in some serious pain! The fans are on their feet here as Essex has all of Rome's weight falling back on that leg. Essex is trying to crawl into the ropes. Rome uses his strong base to move Essex back into the middle of the ring, but
Essex squares a
direct kick right into the backside of Rome. Essex trying to get to his feet but Rome with a shoulder block sends him back down.
SH: It would appear that Rome has gotten his head into the match. Thus far
Cutter has not been a factor.
CT: Rome hits the ropes, Essex stays down, Rome drops over him, he comes back, in the criss cross, Rome drops down. Essex drops and kicks Rome right in the head! He picks up Rome and hits him with a swinging neck breaker!
Essex is trying to get his breath and this gives Rome a chance to regain some of his wind as well.
SH: Surprisingly there has not been a pinfall attempt made yet. CT: Both men giving it their all. Essex catches Rome with a fist to the stomach and goes for a DDT but Essex gets kneed right in the abdomen. Rome grabs Essex around the neck. He's got him up in the air! He's holding Essex high in the air, letting the blood rush to his brain, and nails him with a jackhammer!
SH: Rome is showing us why he has been so successful in this sport. CT: Rome immediately back up to his feet, and he is holding onto the ropes and he is now viciously stomping a mudhole right into Essex! Essex is having his chest blundgeoned into by the boots of Rome. Essex gets to the ropes and Cutter forces Rome to a neutral corner allowing Essex time to get to his feet.
Rome shoves his way past Cutter and catches Jason Essex with a hiptoss. Essex quickly back up but Rome nails him with a dropkick. Rome takes Essex down and locks an armbar submission on Essex! SH: Look here!! Cutter is forcing Rome to release the hold. Rome refusing but Cutter threatens him with disqualifaction and reluctantly Rome releases.
Now we see Cutter becoming a factor. CT: Rome picks up Essex and throws him into the ropes. Rome puts his head down attempting a back body drop but Essex flips over top of him! Great agility by Essex. Essex turns Rome around. He hits a sky high sitout powerbomb!!! Here's the cover!
ONE! TWO!! KICKOUT!! CT: Rome just barely getting a shoulder up! Essex picks up Rome and sends him into the ropes. Essex catches him right into a bulldog!
SH: Rome just had his head driven with all that momentum right into the mat! That will scramble some eggs. CT: Rome could get into an Execederin commercial after that move! Essex is now heading to the top rope. Rome is slowly getting up. Essex measures Rome and nails him with a vicious clothesline! Rome nearly had his head taken off with that move! Essex bringing Rome back to his feet and throws him into the ropes. He catches him with a release belly to belly suplex!
SH: Essex had a game plan before he came out here and he is sure executing perfectly. CT: Essex hits the ropes and he drives a knee right into the head of Rome. Rome might have a concussion after that shot. Essex approaches the fallen
Rome and Rome rolls Essex into a small package but Cutter pulls Rome off breaking things up!
SH: Cutter wasn't sent out here to be a referee, he was sent out to be a thorn in the side of Rome without a doubt!!
CT: Rome is furious and gets in the face of Cutter. OH!! Cutter gets nailed with a vicious right hand and Rome has him up in the air and down with a slam!!! Rome has had enough but here comes Essex who has been handed a steel chair by Chanel and he plants it right across the forehead of Rome! Essex with the nonchalant cover and Cutter makes the count!
ONE! TWO!!! THREE!!! CT: Essex has defeated Tony Rome thanks in part to Cutter. SH: Tony Rome was made to be an example here tonight. Tommy Richards was sending a message to everyone. You just don't cross management!!
CT: Fans we are all out of time! Until next week this is Chris Thompson and
Steve Houston saying GOODNIGHT!!!
