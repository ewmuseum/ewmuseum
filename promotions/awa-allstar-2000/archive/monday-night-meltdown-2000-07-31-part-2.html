Monday Night Meltdown (Part Two)

31 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CT: It is time for our main event match-up! A six-man gauntlet to determine just who will walk out of here as the first ever AWA World champion!
SH: For nearly a month now, these six men have been at each others throats and tonight it will all come to a head as they will face off against one another for the coveted World title.
CT: Earlier in the evening, President Tommy Richards came out to announce the big match-up and he also announced the selection order that these four men will enter the ring. For those not familiar with how a gauntlet match works, two men will enter the ring to start things off and every three minutes another competitor will enter. Elimination can occur by pinfall or submission.
SH: In case you are late tuning in, the order in which the competitors will be entering will be . Derek Craven, Tony Rome, Cutter, "Delinquent" Kevin
Fontaine, Essex, and finally "Outlaw" Brody Bryant. Obviously Bryant and
Essex would have to be the favorites in this match due to the fact that they will enter the ring last.
CT: All six of these men are very talented individuals, so really it is anyone's match. Let's go to the ring as Matt Wetterson introduces the first two competitors.
^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Six Man Gauntlet for the AWA World title Written by [name withheld] ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ Matt Wetterson: Ladies and gentlemen are you ready? Tonight's main event will be a six-man gauntlet for the AWA World title!!
(Large pop from the crowd.) Matt Wetterson: Introducing first, weighing in at 251lbs. He hails from the
"City of Brotherly Love", Philadelphia, PA. Here is Derek Craven!!!
[The crowd comes to their feet and the boo's begin to pour down from the stands as "Trip Like I Do" by Crystal Method begins to play.]
[Out from the back wearing black, leather tights, a sleeveless t-shirt and a pair of silver wire shades perched on the bridge of his nose, steps none other then the "Devil" Derek Craven. He is well toned with a body built for strength and speed. His honey-brown hair hangs loosely behind him, just brushing the tops of his shoulder blades. There is a tattoo of a devil holding a pitchfork with the initials "D.C." underneath, on his right arm.
Craven makes his way to the ring focused on the match ahead. There is a sly smirk across his face.]
Matt Wetterson: And his opponent! Weighing in at 252lbs. He hails from
Minneapolis, Minnesota! Here is Tony Rome!!!
"Symphony of Destruction" by Megadeath begins to rock the Arena] ## You take a mortal man . and put him in control! ## ## Watch him become a God. ## ##Watch people's heads a roll! ## [A silhouette of a man comes into focus through the cloud of lingering smoke. As it settles the silhouette takes it's form. It's none other then
Tony Rome! Rome is wearing a pair of black tights with stripes down the side of each leg and black and white boots.]
##Just like the pied piper, lead rats through the streets. ## #Dance like the marionettes. Swing to the symphony . of destruction!! ## [Rome makes his way to the ring, taunting the fans along the way. The boo's are heavy.] CT: The first two competitors have made their way to the ring and believe me when I say there is no love loss between these two men.
SH: There is a long and bitter history between these two and it's just continuing here in the AWA.
CT: Referee John Odom going over his final instructions and this match is underway as he calls for the bell! Rome and Craven circling around the ring and then locking things up. Standing side headlock is applied by Rome who is sent into the ropes by Craven. On the rebound and Rome manages to duck under a clothesline attempt by Craven and as he rebounds from the ropes once again, he delivers a nice double axehandle smash to the forehead and down goes Craven to the canvas!
SH: Things are starting off quickly between these two men as one would expect in a match-up between two high caliber athletes.
CT: Craven slow to get back to his feet after that move. Rome over and brings Craven back to his feet. He sets him up and executes a pump handle slam. Once again Craven finds himself on the canvas. Rome now heads for the turnbuckles and climbs up to the second one. Rome leaps and connects with a knee to the forehead!
SH: Less then one minute before the next competitor will be sent in! CT: Rome looking to eliminate Craven as he goes for an early pinfall! ONE! TWO!! NO! CT: Craven manages to kickout on two and avoids elimination! Rome bringing
Craven back to his feet and sends him into the ropes. On the rebound, Rome looking to execute a back body drop but Craven is able to turn the tables on
Rome by delivering a swift kneelift to the face. Rome staggers back as
Craven grabs a handful of hair, jumps into the air and slams Rome face first into the canvas!
(A buzzer sounds and down the isle charges Cutter.) CT: Cutter has hit the ring and it looks like it's going to be two against one as he goes right after Rome! Cutter viciously putting the boots to Rome before bringing him to his feet. Cutter sends Rome into the ropes. On the rebound Cutter catches Rome around the neck and nicely executes a neckbreaker!
SH: Craven has let Cutter take over while he catches a breath but it looks as if that will change right here.
CT: You're right! Craven approaches Cutter from behind, turns him around and hammers Cutter with a big right hand. Cutter staggers back and Craven scoops him up and slams him hard to the canvas! Craven turning his attention back towards Rome who has slowly made it back to his feet. Craven grabs hold and sends Rome back to the mat with a big belly-to-belly suplex! Here's a cover by Craven!
ONE!! TWO!! NO!!! CT: Rome manages to kickout before the three count. Don't look now but
Cutter is back to his feet and he has just planted Craven to the mat with a bulldog maneuver! Cutter now brings Rome back to his feet and sends him into the ropes. Rome rebounds back and is nailed with a dropkick! Cutter quickly to the top turnbuckle. He sets up and leaps, connecting with an awesome 450 splash! The cover!
ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! CT: Rome has been eliminated!! Cutter with that awesome 450 splash has just eliminated Rome.
SH: Rome is helped from the ring by the referee and just in time for the next participant! (The buzzer sounds and "Delinquent" Kevin Fontaine comes flying down the isle. He gets a nice pop from the fans as he does.)
CT: Fontaine has hit the ring and he just leveled Cutter with a short arm lariat. Fontaine turning his attention towards Craven and takes the fight right to him as he unloads with a series of right hands. Craven is staggered into the corner where Fontaine will grab the arm and send him crashing into the cross corner turnbuckles. Fontaine with a full head of steam charges in but Craven moves out of the way and Fontaine crashes hard into the turnbuckles! As Fontaine staggers backwards, Craven grabs him around his head and neck using the turnbuckles for leverage he plants Fontaine to the mat with a tornado DDT!
SH: Great maneuver by Craven but there is no rest for the weary as Cutter is back to his feet.
CT: Craven and Cutter locking it up. Cutter applies the standing arm wringer. Craven fighting to get free but Cutter has that hold locked in tight. Wait a second Craven manages to reverse the hold and now Cutter is the one that finds himself in some trouble. Craven yanks down hard on that arm trying to dislocate it from its socket. He does it once again and the pain is evident on the face of Cutter.
SH: Craven best not concern himself too much with Cutter because Fontaine is starting to come around.
CT: Fontaine is slow to get to his feet but he finally does get there and delivers a shot to the kidney of Craven who was caught by surprise with that one. Craven drops to one knee as he holds his left side trying to recapture his breath. Cutter a bit relieved that his arm is finally free but that will be short lived as Fontaine delivers a boot to the gut and then with a handful of hair he pancakes him to the canvas!
SH: Looks like Fontaine is going to the top! CT: Fontaine taking a risk here as he sets up on the top turnbuckle. Craven back to his feet and turns only to be met with a nicely executed top rope hurricarana compliments of the "Delinquent"!
Here's the cover by Fontaine! ONE! TWO!! NO!!! CT: Craven manages to kickout and the match continues. Fontaine scoops
Craven up and slams him back to the canvas with authority!
SH: For a small guy, Fontaine packs a punch! CT: No doubt about that. Cutter getting back to his feet and is looking for a little payback on Fontaine as he grabs hold of the arm and sends him into the ropes. Fontaine rebounds off the ropes and is leveled with a vicious clothesline.
(The buzzer sounds indicating yet another participant is to enter. Jason
Essex emerges from the back to a large pop from the crowd. He charges towards the ring and slides in under the bottom rope.)
CT: Essex has hit the ring!! Essex goes right after Derek Craven and Cutter continues his assault on Fontaine. Cutter heads up to the top turnbuckle, sets up and leaps connecting with a huge Moonsault!! Here's the cover .
ONE! TWO!! NO!!! SH: Unbelievable!! Fontaine actually kicked out after that! CT: Cutter is in a bit of disbelief himself but what can you do. Across the ring Essex has Craven locked into STF submission hold. Craven doing everything in his power to get to the ropes but Essex has the leverage.
Craven trying to fight off the pain but it may be getting to be just too much. SH: In all my years in the sport, I don't think I have ever seen Craven submit, but he may not have a choice in this one.
CT: I can't believe the pain threshold of Craven, he just refuses to submit and Essex has no choice but to release the hold. Craven lying there in what has to be some serious pain. Essex brings him to his feet, locks hold around the waist and drives him to the canvas with a belly-to-belly suplex. Here's the cover by Essex!
ONE! TWO!! NO!!! SH: Craven kicked out!! I can't believe that after all that he still kicked out! CT: Cutter has just been eliminated!!! Fontaine getting the pinfall on him after an amazing Swanton bomb off the top rope. We are down to three remaining inside the ring for about 45 seconds more, when our final competitor will enter.
SH: With Cutter out of the way Fontaine is going to focus in on Craven. CT: Craven still feeling the effects of that STF will now have to fight off two men. Essex sends him into the ropes and on the rebound he is met with a double back body drop compliments Essex and Fontaine.
(Buzzer sounds for the last time and from the back emerges the "Outlaw" Brody Bryant. Boo's pour down from the stands as he casually makes his way towards the ring.)
SH: Here comes the "Outlaw"!! CT: This crowd has been waiting in anticipation to see Bryant and Essex finally lock horns and it looks like they will get their wish. Essex sees the Outlaw and he isn't going to waste anytime and meets him ringside. The two men exchanging rights and lefts to face! Essex has Bryant staggered!
Essex grabs hold of the arm and sends Bryant crashing hard into the steel ring steps!! SH: Essex has revenge on his mind here tonight. Bryant is in for one hell of a fight! Ct: Inside the ring Craven has taken control of things. Fontaine in some trouble after being put through a tilt a whirl backbreaker but it doesn't stop there. Craven brings him back to his feet and sets him up and executes what he calls the "Devil Driver", a maneuver much like the Pearl River
Plunge. Craven goes for the cover!
ONE! TWO!! KICKOUT!!! CT: Fontaine still has some fight left in him and manages to kickout after that vicious maneuver. Craven brings Fontaine back to his feet and sends him into the corner. Craven setting Fontaine up on the top turnbuckle and now climbs up there himself. Craven appears to be setting up for a superplex or something to that effect. He has Fontaine hooked but Fontaine fights back!!
Fontaine with a couple shots to the mid-section and Craven is stunned. SH: Craven starts to fall backwards but he holds onto Fontaine and both men come crashing down off the top turnbuckle!
CT: Both men landing wrong after that fall and both are down. The referee has no choice but to begin counting both men out .
ONE! TWO!! THREE!! FOUR!!!! FIVE!!!!! SIX!!!!!! SEVEN!!!!!!! EIGHT!!!!!!!! NINE!!!!!!!!! TEN!!!!!!!!!! SH: Can you believe this? Craven and Fontaine have in a sense, eliminated each other!! Only Essex and Bryant remain!!
CT: Still on the outside of the ring, Bryant has been beaten to a bloody mess. Essex has been relentless in his assault and has hit Bryant with just about everything possible. Essex sends Bryant back into the ring now and follows him in but not before grabbing a steel chair. Essex brings Bryant to his feet and sends him into the ropes.
SH: Essex is going to nail Bryant with that steel chair! NO!!! Bryant ducks and Essex inadvertently nails the referee!!!
CT: The referee is out cold and Essex can't believe it! Essex with that chair still in hand turns but Bryant is there to meet him with a chokeslam!!! Essex is down and now Bryant is signaling to the back.
SH: Don't look now but here comes Tony Rome and WHAT THE HELL!!! CT: President Richards is coming out alongside Tony Rome!! What is the meaning of this?! Rome going underneath the ring and pulls out a table and sends it into the ring.
SH: Rome also has pulled out a bottle of lighter fluid and we all know what this means!! CT: I can't believe this. I can't believe that President Richards is a part of this. The table has been set up inside the ring and drenched in that lighter fluid. Bryant goes into the pocket and pulls out a lighter and look here he passes it to Richards, who will do the honors of setting the table ablaze.
(A large pop from the crowd is heard and Cutter is seen rushing to the ring.) CT: What's this?! Cutter has hit the ring. A big right hand sends Rome down to the canvas, Cutter turns and fires another right hand into the face of
President Richards but it will all stop there as Bryant just leveled Cutter with that steel chair. With Cutter laid out, Bryant turns to Essex. He brings him to his and sets him up. More fuel is added to the table and it is once again set ablaze and Essex is POWERBOMBED through that table!
SH: And if that wasn't enough Richards is going to make the count that will crown Bryant the new AWA World champion!
ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! CT: "Outlaw" Brody Bryant has just become the AWA World champion thanks in part to the underhanded corruption of the AWA President, Tommy Richards.
Fans that is all the time we have, so until next week . this is Chris Thompson for Steve Houston, saying Goodnight!!! (Bryant raises the belt high into the air as Meltdown fades off the air .)
