PRESS CONFERNCE ' 99 Live from: Maple Leaf Gardens in Toronto, Ont, Canada July 15th, 1999 -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [The scene opens we see a black screen a light flashes on the screen and we see the - "Northren Lights Wrestling Logo" on the black screen now underneath the logo appears two more logos - "CTV SportsNet and HBO
Sports". The light flashes again and the scene fades out to a jammed packed - "Maple Leaf Gardens Press Conference Room". The camera zooms in on the podium at the front of the room to the right and left of the podium are large tables with three chairs on each side. An announcer over the PA system begins to speak.]
Annnouncer - "At this time please welcome - "President of CTV SportsNet - Eric Cain" and also please welcome - "President of HBO Sports - Rodney Wallace." [The two men walk in wearing black suits that have there company's logo on it. Eric Cain is slighty larger then Rodney Wallace. Rodney Wallace sits down as Eric Cain goes to the microphone at the podium and begins to speak.]
E.Cain - "Welcome wrestling fans, wrestling insiders and reporters." [The wrestling fans, wrestling insiders and reporters all clap there hands. They quite down as Eric Cain beings to speak again.]
E.Cain - "At this time I would like you to meet the owners of - "Northren Lights Wrestling (NLW)." Please welcome - "Mr.Marko Ceko" and - "JT Westwood." [Out walks Marko Ceko and JT Westwood both dressed in black suits that have - "Notheren Lights Wrestling Logo" on the back of there jackets.
Eric Cain sits down as both Marko Ceko and JT Westwood take the podium.] M.Ceko - "Hello Canadians watching on CTV SportsNet" JT Westwood - "Hello Americans watching on HBO Sports." M.Ceko - "Thank-you Mr.Cain for the wonderful introduction. NOW lets get down to business. Well as all of you we are here today to announce that
I Marko Ceko and my good buddy here JT Westwood have decide to open up a new wrestling company that is called - "Northren Lights Wrestling
(NLW)." Well what you don't know is why we choose to have this press confernce at this historic building right here in Toronto."
JT Westwood - "And why the hell is CTV SportsNet and HBO Sports HERE???? Well don't fear we are here to answer all your questions plus those two but I would like to answer my question right now!!! So Mr.Wallace and
Mr.Cain get up here and answer those questions for us as me and my buddy here have a seat."
[The jammed packed roomed claps as Marko Ceko and JT Westwood sit down and Eric Cain and Rodney Wallace head up to the podium and Eric Cain speaks first.]
E.Cain - "Well as all of you know I am President of CTV SportsNet that only airs here in Canada and wrestling on CTV Sportsnet has always been the World Wrestling Federation but last month the WWF decided to move all there shows over to our compition TSN. I was then approched by
Mr.Marko Ceko and Mr.JT Westwood and they asked me if there was anyway they could run a wrestling show on CTV SportsNet. I took then took there idea to the board of directors at CTV SportsNet and they would only agree to run - "Northren Lights Wrestling (NLW)" on CTV SportsNet if they could find an American based TV company to also air there wrestling show so that - "Northren Lights Wrestling (NLW)" could go NORTH AMERICAN wide. That is where HBO Sports and Rodeny Wallace come into play I will let him explain that."
[Eric Cain sits down as Rodney Wallace beigns to speak.] R.Wallace - "Hello, I am the President of HBO Sports. For a long time
HBO Sports has been looking for a wrestling program for the past 6 months. We at HBO wanted to go with Extreme Championship Wrestling but they made a deal with another network before we were ready to make an offer. After about two months we were still looking for a wrestling program to add to our line-up. At this time Mr.Marko Ceko and Mr.JT
Westwood had already made a deal with CTV SportsNet here in Canada but they still neeed one more thing an American based TV Company to also air there wrestling show. I found out about this, went to Mr.Marko Ceko.
Mr.JT Westwood, found out about there plans for - "Northren Lights
Wrestling (NLW)" and why HBO Sports should air there wrestling program.
After the meeting I took this to the board of directors at HBO Sports and they all agreed that we should add there wrestling program to our line-up. We reached a deal with both - "Northren Lights Wrestling (NLW)"
and CTV SportsNet. I very pleased to announce for both CTV SportsNet,
HBO Sports will air - "Northren Lights Wrestling (NLW)" program once
Mr.Marko Ceko and Mr.JT Westwood are ready. Thank-you very much."
[The jammed packed press conference begins to clap as Rodney Wallace sits down, Marko Ceko and JT Westwood re-take the podium.]
M.Ceko - "Well you heared it HBO Sports and CTV SportsNet will air -
"Northren Lights Wrestling (NLW)" programing to there line-ups. Well we are pleased to announce the names of our shows, what night and time they will be playing. Our actual wrestling show will be held on Sunday Nights it will be called - "Sunday Night - Lights Out". Lights-Out will be a sold two hours of wrestling. Now I want to know the answer to my question why are we doing the press-conference here in Toronto at Maple
Leaf Gardens."
JT Westwood - "Well I want to answer that one because 4 nights ago I reached an agreement with the ownership of Maple Leaf Gardens and I am pleased to announce that - "Sunday Night - Lights Out" will take place at the Maple Leaf Gardens most of the time. Not to worry American fans because all the other times we will be down south. I am also pleased to announce our first Pay-Per View will take place at the brand new Staples
Center in Los Angels, California at this Pay-Per View we will crown our champions. The Pay-Per Views is being called - "The Summer of Champions
#1". M.Ceko - "Wow now that is great news. Now at this point in time we don't have a time-slot set for Sunday Night - "Lights Out" but for our other show that will air Monday to Saturday at mid-night it is caled - "Late
Night" it will be interviews form the stars of - "Northren Lights
Wrestling (NLW)." Now please meet the color-commentator of Sunday Night - "Lights Out" - "The Source" Don Blair." [JT Westwood and Marko Ceko take a seat as - "The Source" Don Blair comes out, takes the podium and begins to speak.]
D.Blair - "Well folks "The Source" is back on TV once again and this time he is here to stay. Each and every week you will see my mug on your
TV screen every Sunday Night. I am done speaking NOW!!!!
[The fans clap as "The Source" Don Blair takes a seat and Marko Ceko and
JT Westwood re-take the podiuam and speak.]
M.Ceko - "Well that brings us to the end of this great day here in
Toronto and we look foward to seeing you each and every week watching -
"Northren Lights Wrestling (NLW.)"
JT Westwood - "We out!!!!" [The lights flash again and the screen is black it flashes one more time and we see the - "Northren Lights Wrestling Logo (NLW)." with the CTV
SportsNet and HBO Sports logo under the NLW Logo. At the bottom of the screen we see...........]
