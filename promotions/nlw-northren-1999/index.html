Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Northren Lights Wrestling introduced itself on 15 July 1999 with an in-fiction press conference staged at Maple Leaf Gardens in Toronto, its own owners Marko Ceko and JT Westwood announcing broadcast deals with a fictional CTV SportsNet and HBO Sports and a flagship show, Sunday Night — Lights Out, alongside a nightly interview program, Late Night. A first pay-per-view, Summer of Champions #1, was named on the same broadcast to crown three titles at once: the World Heavyweight, in a tournament climaxing there; the World Tag Team, similarly; and the North American, in an over-the-top battle royal open to the whole roster except the World title finalists.

Following the press-conference roleplay, Ceko's own recruitment letter describes a small real front office of two bookers and two further writers behind the scenes, and warns that the promotion's previous attempt at a one-man operation “does not work very well.”

A second, later notice, dated 30 August 1999, is signed by Ceko alone as “Co-Owner\President” and simply asks free agents to apply ahead of a first card set for 4 September 1999.

Whether that first card, or the Summer of Champions supercard the press conference promised, ever aired isn't in the surviving record; no card or title result survives under this promotion's own name.
