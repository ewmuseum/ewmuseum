IPWA stands for International Professionnal Wrestling Association and it is one of the many imaginary wrestling federations on the internet. Why do these ewrestling (we call this hobby that way) exist? To help wrestling fans, which are frustrated at the current real life angles, use their imagination and create their own wrestlers, their own gimmicks, their own angles. IPWA counts on the handlers, YOU, to be highly involved: it's up to you to create angles, feuds, to ask for matches, interviews. You are indirectly able to control your wrestler's destiny by your degree of involvement as well as your talent. The IPWA is directed by an executive board lead by [name withheld] (the Executive Board Chairman) and by many associates. Our objective at the IPWA is not only to make this enjoyable for you but also to recreate the highest degree possible of realism. In fact, the IPWA has its own set of employees with each of them, their own role and characteristics. The IPWA is NOT a simmed fed. It is a first class booked ewrestling federation where the best roleplayers are rewarded. All rulings are taken by the whole Executive Board which leads into impartial decisions (not always the same person making the decision). ___________ _______ | || __ |\ \ /\ / /| _ |................................................ | || ___| \ v v / | | How do I get into the IPWA? |_||_| \_/\_/ |_|_| WRITER: [name withheld] ........................................................................ Firstly, read this document thoroughly and attentively. You need to full understand how this federation operates before getting into it. Secondly, take a look at the wrestlers on the roster and read attentively their profile (including their biography and sample roleplay) to be able to see what type of wrestlers are accepted in the IPWA. Make sure that you don't have the same personna- gimmick or name as someone else on the roster because if you do, you are almost guaranteed not to be accepted in the federation. We require a sample roleplay in your application form. If you don't write one, you might as well not write the whole application form since a form with no sample roleplay will be automatically discarded. For an idea of what kind of material will be expected of you, here is an example flash, submitted by one of our wrestlers (in my opinion, this is a good roleplay; we do not expect from you, roleplays of that length. This can be considered like a long roleplay but the quality and style of writing is there...): [*SCENE: A pro football stadium in Miami, Florida. Sitting in the bleachers, fourth row from the back, sits a man, still clapping. He continues this for about ten more seconds, and then stops, he is reminded of great games of old. He gets up from his seat and begins to walk down the aisle to the floor. He grabs a steel chair and carries it with him to the center of the middle ring. The cameraman meets him there, still a little unsure about what is to happen. The man quickly pulls the chair over his head, and swings the chair in an overhead motion towards the cameraman. The cameraman pulls away, but the man stops the swing a few inches from the mans skull. He laughs for a second then the laughter is gone. His smirk is adorning his face, which has been the case recently. He unfolds the chair, and puts it down dead center in the middle of the ring. He sits down, and holds his arms out to his side, as if to invite the cameraman towards him. The cameraman cautiously does so, and begins filming, the static appearing ever so often..*] [*The man has long blonde hair, tied back in a ponytail. . He's an attractive young man. He looks about 6' and looks very atheletic. As he lights a cigarette, the camera sees his tanned hand, with gold rings on every finger. The man looks to the camera with a slight grin on his face..We then realize - this is Jade "The Blade".*] Jade: As we sit here, in a place of amusment and fun, one must think about what makes such a place so entertaining. Is it the atheletes that compete here or perhaps the thrills and chills of the of the cheerleaders. The answer in any case is the coaches, for most are freaks. People who are looked upon as different. People come to see those who are not normal, and we are amused by their strange acts. Such is the life a wrestler. But as years have passed, we see that wrestling has taken on the same look. Average people paying money to see men beat each other to death, performing feats that push the human body and makes us question the performers will to live. Why do we do what we do? Why do we pummel each other within an each of our lives? For the money? For the power? For the titles? Some of these appeal to some, but for me, it is neither. I wrestle for the respect. Each drop of blood that trickles out of my body is another payment made for my success. [*Jade pauses for a second, to think of what to say.. or more correctly, how to word it..*] Jade: How many deposits have you made, Ian Walsh? In any case, prepare to make a very large donation. Our match, along with all others upcoming, will surely be filled with violent acts, broken bodies, and open flesh. Any event where you and I will be facing each other is certainly not meant for the weak in heart or the squemish of stomach. This will be a night for warriors, to test each others metal, and see what were made of. The pain that will be experianced will be great, and I am well prepard to accept my share of it. However, I doubt your willingness to put all you hold dear on the line in the pursuit of respect among your peers. [*Jade pauses, and takes a drag of the cigarette..*] Jade: In the big two federations, all those associated with it are diluted of their success. Yes, they can handle the competition when the action stays in the ring. They can wrestle, but the true question is, can they fight. Highflying manuevers and headlocks will only get you so far in the IPWA. If you don't know how to fight, you will soon find out that a steel chair doesn't care if you have the ability of an olympic wrestler or an ordinary bum. It'll knock you down just the same. A true mark of a fighter is if he can get back up again. Will he go against all reason and get back up, and risk getting hit again. Yes, perhaps it is not the most logical action, but if you refuse to get up, how will you now your true limits. Life is a situation where you are place in a posistion and taught to stay within certain boundries. Myself, and several others, have shunned these commands, and dared face a brave new world. The going is hard, as any traveler will tell you, but the experiances that occur along the way make the pain and suffering worthwhile. Have you forgone those borders, Ian? [*Jade pauses, as he stands up, and begins pacing around..*] Jade: Ian 'Human Torture Rack' Walsh, what have you done?.. You've been in your cage all your life, and accidentally, someone left the cage door open. You flapped your little wings, and flew right into the jungle. But something you may not have known, the jungle is home to beasts of prey, such as myself, that see those that don't belong, and we quickly disperse of them for daring to step into our world. Ian, will you prove that you a hawk, or answer my suspicions that you are merely a small, scared canary, unsure of where he has wound up. Soon, I will show you where you are, and I will watch your eyes fill up with horror at your realization. A place for freaks, a place for beasts, a place for us all. Why do they come to watch you Ian? For your highflying abilities? For the image you betray? No. It is something far less personal than that. They come to see a feeding. You are the meal, I am the beast. They come to see the utter destruction of a human being at the hand of another. That is why people come to see you. And I am not one to dissapoint. The people have paid for a spectacle. Bird versus Snake. The lines are drawn, the war has begun. Will you stand and fight, and stare your destinay square in the face? Or will you run and hide, attempting to escape the inevitable? [*Jade laughs, as he takes a seat..*] Jade: I'll tell you now so you can save your breath from running. Whether you face me or run, in the end, I will reign victorious in the most violent of fashions. If that means chasing you through the streets of the city, down alleys of remorse and refuge, so be it. The fact is that I am your destiny, and destiny can not be escaped or altered. Once you see your very life is in danger, I have no doubt that you will force yourself to fight, and I look foward to that. A hunt just isn't as fun if the prey doesn't try to evade, attempt to succeed, to outwit the hunter. Do you have the intelligence to out think this serpent? I assure you, it isn't impossible, because nothing is. It is, however improbable, an improbablity that you will never know the joy of achieving. Just a little time away from a most vicious battle, the first of many for one of us. Just as every man in this league, I feel that I am the man who will succeed. We will both try, but only one can be victorious. I look foward to meeting my opponents, whether it be Ian Walsh, or whoever. The venom has laid dormant too long. It is time for it to resurface, and the IPWA will be the first to feel it's toxic bite. Ian Walsh, prepare to die. The IPWA sanctions 4 singles titles and one tag team title. Every title has his own "signification" and "clientele". Well, here they are: - IPWA World Heavyweight Title * Reserved for the main eventers and superstars only. This title is defended at least one time a month but the title holder MUST be really active in the interviews since he doesn't wrestle too often. - IPWA International Title * Reserved to any foreigner (from any popularity status). Foreigner are wrestlers born or representing any country besided the United States of America. The title is defended at least twice by month and is of equal value compared to the US Title. If the IPWA is on an extra-US tour, the title must be defended once a week. - IPWA United States Title * Reserved to the Americans only. The title must be defended at least twice a month. Has equal value compared to the International Title. - IPWA Television Title * Reserved to hard workers, mid carders or even, some times, low carders. This title must be defended at least once a week. It is a good step stone to go up in the popularity rankings. - IPWA Tag Team Titles * Reserved to any tag team. Must be defended at least once by month. The titles can only change hands by pinfall or submission unless if specified. ___________ _______ | || __ |\ \ /\ / /| _ |................................................ | || ___| \ v v / | | What are the IPWA wrestling Events? |_||_| \_/\_/ |_|_| WRITER: [name withheld] ........................................................................ The IPWA holds two weekly "wrestling" cards as well as many other "special" shows. - IPWA Wednesday Night War And Havoc * This is our less important show. The card contains several dark matches (matches where the action is summarized accompanied by post-match comments) and maybe one or two live matches (matches fully commentated). This card will be mostly directed to the less popular wrestlers offering them the chance to showcase their talent... Equivalent of Saturday Night Superstars in the Big Two. - IPWA Saturday Night Mayhem * This is the main card. It will contain around 6 matches (not lesser than 5 and not bigger than 8). All the matches are LIVE (fully commentated). This card is more reserved for the main eventers. It is the equivalent of the Monday Night shows in the Big Two. - IPWA Fantasy Island * This is the IPWA version of Livewire with recaps of the past week in the IPWA as well as some new hot and juicy news and rumors, interviews with IPWA stars, reactions to the shows... Almost anything can take place during the show. Takes place on Monday Nights... - IPWA Special Events * An innovative concept that the IPWA added are the Special Events. All special events have a special theme: "wrestlers visiting a children hospital", "press conference", "autograph signing session", "wrestlers training at a local gym"... Handlers will be invited to send in flashes related to the theme for that event. Those shows take place about two times by month. The rate though can be higher or lower depending on the circumstances. - IPWA Pay-Per-Views * IPWA Pay-Per-Views occur roughly every two months. They are the summums of most feuds and angles and the birth of others. They are the most important events in the IPWA. Many ppvs are booked and always take place around the same dates. -> February: Grand Massacre (Battle Royale Type PPV) -> April: Alarma (Hardcore Type PPV: All matches are extreme) -> June: International Incident (PPV that takes place anywhere besides the US: international flavor) -> August: Summer Splash (Normal PPV; Often set on a beach) -> October: Lethal Lottery (Spin The Wheel and Wrestle Gimmick Match Type PPV) -> December: For The Power And The Glory (King Of The Ring Type PPV) - IPWA Real Video Show * The IPWA will also have a weekly real video show (more like a slideshow with sound). The day for that is still unannounced though. The show will feature news, rumors, phone calls, interviews... ___________ _______ | || __ |\ \ /\ / /| _ |................................................ | || ___| \ v v / | | How Does The IPWA Work? |_||_| \_/\_/ |_|_| WRITER: [name withheld] ........................................................................ Technically speaking, the IPWA is a roleplaying based federation. But, as we get more into details, you can notice that the IPWA requires from its wrestlers, in average, two pieces of work by week. Now, a piece of work can be a roleplay (average length of around 4K), a match (average length of around 8K) or a commercial (average length of 4K). So, every week, you have to send at least two pieces of work, wether it is a roleplay (of any form), a match written by you or a commercial written by you... One aspect where the IPWA is innovative is in the way the flashes work. As you probably know, most feds use email or a guestbook for the roleplays. The IPWA has decided to use another technique: "event flashing". All flashes MUST take place at events or shows... On cards (Saturday Night Mayhem and Wednesday Night War And Havoc), you can roleplay using several possibilities: 1- In Ring Interview (your wrestler is in the ring or around the ring and starts running his mouth; they can be shootish or normal) 2- Commentating Type of Interview (During a match, your wrestler does the commentating (kind of like when you have guest commentators during matches)) 3- Locker Room Interview (You know what it is) 4- Around the Arena Interview (for example: Mankind and his several interviews in the Broiler Room) 5- Publicity Stunt (publicities for your wrestler (ex: the NWO; Bob Backlund when he wanted to become the US President)) 6- Promos (ex: The Edge and Val Venis promos before they entered the WWF) 7- Remote Location Interviews (ex: dX assaults WCW, Undertaker talks from the cemetery, Kane and Paul Bearer go to take the ADN test) During special events, you can roleplay for your wrestler but you MUST follow that special theme... ex: Let's say that the IPWA is at an hospital to visit young sick kids; a face wrestler could send out a flash where he is really kind with the kid and sort of promisses him a win versus the big bad heel wrestler... You can also flash for you wrestler on our weekly shows (Fantasy Island and our real audio show). The wrestler must remain in his character during the interview. He can not turn kayfabe. For the real audio show, you must record the flash. The flash could be anything: an interview, a promo, a commercial, a locker room reaction following the last card...). The final place where you can send interviews is in the Face-2-Face interviews. It's the only place where your wrestler can turn kayfabe (they are kind of like the Jim Ross interviews with Goldust and Mick Foley..). The interviews take place on Saturday Night Mayhem. Does it change your life wether you are popular or not? No! But it will change the life of your wrestler. In fact, depending on his popularity, a wrestler will be able to do different things... In fact, main eventers will, as their name says, wrestle in the bigger cards, in the final matches of the cards. They will be aloud to send in in-ring interviews... Basically, depending on your popularity, you'll be able to do stuff and not do other stuff: * Superstars * They wrestle at least once by month. They often get in-ring interviews. Basically, they "talk" a lot and wrestle less. * Main Eventers * About the same possibilities as the superstars but they wrestle at least twice a month. They can get interviews, but normally, only on WNWAH. * Mid Carders * They wrestle once a week (normally), can get some in-ring interviews during WNWAH but, usually, only locker room interviews. * Low Carders * They are not aloud to make in-ring interviews unless if asked so by the EB staff. They wrestle once a week (normally) and will often use locker room interviews to get their points across. * Jobbers * They are not aloud to send in interviews besides promos and commercials (for the cards). They can though send in face-2-face interviews or flashes for Fantasy Island. They wrestle once a week. Basically, any wrestler can make a face-2-face interview or an interview for Fantasy Island but the two cards are reserved to the popular wrestlers. The special events are reserved to the top wrestlers (Mid Carders and up unless if you are invited by staff). Don't forget that the borders between the different popularity statuses are not that clear and that a low carder might be able some times to do stuff that he would normally not be aloud to do. Suggestions must be given to the MRC or EB. - If you want to use another wrestler in your roleplays, ask his permission first... ___________ _______ | || __ |\ \ /\ / /| _ |................................................ | || ___| \ v v / | | Tips For Creating A New Wrestler |_||_| \_/\_/ |_|_| WRITER: [name withheld] ........................................................................ Creating your wrestler is the most crucial point of your IPWA career. What you create in your initial application will affect how the Executive Board uses your character. Follow these to make sure you make an interesting character who will be fun to roleplay for, and therefore, who will be successful and enjoyable in the IPWA. * Try to make your wrestler similar to yourself, at least in some ways. It's easier to "get to know" your character, and therefore, it's easier to portray that character if you share a few personality traits. * Don't make a character you can't roleplay for. What good is a wrestler with a nuclear physist gimmick if you don't know anything about the subject? What good is a big bad German if you can't write in a faked German accent to save your life? Stick to what you know. * Make sure you design a character you can have fun roleplaying for! If you're not having fun with your character, there's no chance that anyone else will, and the chances are good that you'll flounder. * Make sure your character isn't offending people on a moral level. It's okay to have a bad-ass character. Everyone wants to. But it's not okay to build cheap heat using racism, sexism, or religious hatred as a crutch. Doing this will find you quickly out of the IPWA, if you get in at all. * Make your wrestler realistic. All applications from 9-foot-tall 800 pound bullet-proof giants will be summarily deposited in the trash can. Or, even worse, maybe we'll let you in and make you lose every match because we're in a particularly nasty mood. Just don't do it. It's no fun, and the rule of thumb in the IPWA is that everyone's going to lose their fair share of matches. * Try not to rip off the real wrestlers you see in the WWF or WCW or wherever too badly. Many of our characters do have similarities to the wrestlers you see every weekend on TV, but most people find that if they want to last, they have to find their own niche. Do yourself a favor and save yourself the evolution. Be original from the start. Any applications from wrestlers who are too close to real wrestlers will be rejected. Guaranteed. * If you're already an established E-wrestler, make sure you have time to roleplay in the IPWA as well as your other feds. Spreading yourself too thin will simply make you ineffective in a bunch of federations. It's often wiser to stick to two or three federations, and roleplay a lot in them. That way, you'll have a winner, and quality will make up for the quantity you're missing. (Even if this makes you decide not to join the IPWA, feel free to get on the mailing list!) * Ask for the Executive Board's help in creating your character. Ask for our input and suggestions. We're here to help. Honest. Follow most, or all, of these guidelines, and you're off to a flying start in creating a wrestler who will soon be known as a legend throughout the IPWA! ___________ _______ | || __ |\ \ /\ / /| _ |................................................ | || ___| \ v v / | | I read all of this, now, I want to join... |_||_| \_/\_/ |_|_| WRITER: [name withheld] ........................................................................
