WFW New Blood For The New Year

7 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the recent departure of several members, WFW (World Fantasy Wrestling) is on the search for brand new talent to help kickstart a new generation of
E-Wrestlers and catapult WFW to the position of one of the, if not THE, greatest Geocities-based E-Fed in 2002.
We're looking for free agents that fit these requirements: 1) Dedication. WFW has had too many 'One Hit Wonders' in 2001 and we refuse to have them in 2002. If you join WFW, you must remain committed to WFW. All we ask is that you roleplay for your matches and that your roleplays are above our 20 Line minimum. It's not that much to ask.
2) Enthusiasm. WFW is not interested in signing on anyone who doesn't have the creative flair/passion for E-Wrestling. If you're not interested, then we're not interested.
3) Determination. The whole point of WFW going on a talent search is to find those who want to make a name for themselves and their Fed. Set your sights sky-high, rokkies! If you're good enough, you can make it big and WFW is a large stepping stone to the big-time! Already established yourself as an
E-Wrestling legend? Perfect! More name value for WFW and it allows you many opportunities in a whole new environment against whole new opponents. The possibilities, as they say, are endless.
Our Staff has much experience, they've played this game before and won many times over. But the Staff alone cannot make a great E-Fed as you know.
That's up to the roster of E-Wrestlers. Interested? Log on to www.geocities.com/wfw_web and sign up for the WFW. We await your applications! -[name withheld].
