[name withheld] excells at writing skills, winning a National Writing Contest in 1997 and 1999. Megan will put those skills into writing the cards and information for CIW.
b. CIW Flashback The update show of Canada International Wrestling. This show will happen every Wednesday, and will be hosted by the brown haired beauty herself, Ms. [name withheld]. ======================================== Titles ======================================== a. CIW International Champion The big one! Not only does this wrestler represent Canada, but all of the international countrys that CIW will travel to. Whoever hold this title will be known as the best in CIW.
b. CIW North American Champion The second best, but not to be taken lightly. Whoever hold this title represents all of Mexico, America, and Canada...also known as North America. Go figure.
c. CIW International Tag Team Champions Same as "a" above, but envolving tag teams. ======================================== The Rules/Guidelines of CIW ======================================== a. Roleplaying Guide Roleplaying is a major part of CIW, and will help you gain heat majorly. Even though the CIW keeps a focus on evolving your character, this cannot be achieved without RP'ing. There is a limit of 5 RP's on any show your wreslter appears on. Now, these RP's don't have to be a work of art. Keep it simple and to the point. CIW looks at quality over quanity.
b. Strategy Guidelines A strat basically includes what you plan to do in the match. Your entrance, offensive and defensive moves. Also include counter moves you would do, only a few though. This is just so I can make the match as entertaining as I can. It also serves the purpose of breaking a tie if one happens.
c. Angles Guidelines One of the biggest factors in CIW are angles. Without them, the CIW would be nothing. I put alot of the work on myself to make the angles, but you have to help me out and carry them out. If you have an idea for an angle, contact me and we will work it out. Angles are the things that get my attention...so if you want a title shot, or want to be succesful in the CIW...GET IN AN ANGLE!
