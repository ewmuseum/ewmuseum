A pre-launch Usenet e-fed pitched as a Canada-based international touring promotion behind a three-title ladder, announced twice in the same week under the same handbook. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Canadian International Wrestling introduced itself in a Handbook, Version 1.0, posted 1 December 1999, describing itself as a booked promotion built on what it called the “Open Flash System” — roleplays mailed to a list it nicknamed the “Flash List,” capped at five per wrestler per show — and framing its own reach in its own words: “We are a international federation, as the name shows. We will travel around Canada, including frequent stops in the United States and Mexico. Every three months, we will make a world tour.”

The handbook set out two regular broadcasts, Sunday Night Maple Leaf as its main card and CIW Flashback as a Wednesday update show, and three championships: the CIW International Champion, billed to represent Canada and every country the promotion planned to tour; the CIW North American Champion, representing Mexico, the United States and Canada together; and the CIW International Tag Team Champions. None is recorded with a champion. Booking ran on angles ahead of anything else, by the handbook's own account: “Angles are the things that get my attention...so if you want a title shot, or want to be succesful in the CIW...GET IN AN ANGLE!”

No further document survives. The handbook describes the promotion entirely in the future tense — shows that will run, a world tour that will follow every three months, titles that will be won — and no roster, card, or champion is recorded to show any of it actually took place.
