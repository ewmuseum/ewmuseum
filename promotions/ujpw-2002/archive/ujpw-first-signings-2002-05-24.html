UJPW: First Signings

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
United Japan Pro Wrestling announce the first group of what is sure to be many talented Japanese wrestlers throwing in their lots to the new company. First and foremost, former Neo Japan superstar, and former owner of the Japan Puroresu Association GENJI YAMATO has come out of his year's seclusion following the closure of JPA, to come aboard UJPW. Yamato has been one of the top names in puroresu over the last decade or so: he was a top contender, and eventual holder, for Neo Japan's J1 Rikidozan/Neo Japan Pro Wrestling Unified Heavyweight Titles, or the DOUBLE TITLES for short. In an unprecedented move, upon opening JPA, Yamato PURCHASED the disused titles, and reactivated them in JPA. Now, however, they comfortably sit in his trophy case, reminders of what has been. Yamato also has held championships abroad, being a former WWO Television Champion under Erin McCoy's version of the promotion. Yamato also won a high-profile shoot fight against Tetsuo Morishita at the inaugural TATAMI MMA/NHB event in 2000. His CHO'ONINGEN alter-ego is still one of the most remembered personalities in wrestling history, and we are happy to have him sign on. You may remember him both under his given name, or his gimmick name of SUPER TENGU. Either way, TETSUO MATSUMOTO has also decided to return to the ring, under the auspices of UJPW. Matsumoto is a Living Legend, simply dominating the sorely-missed Super Japan Pro Wrestling. He has hinted at retirement for quite some time, however his Fighting Spirit has lead him to UJPW. We are honored by his presence. We are also honored to announce the arrival into UJPW of juniorheavyweight master TASOGARE NO PRINCE. The Twilight Prince, having been a major junior star in SJPW, Neo Japan, JPA, and the American UWF promotion, returns to the ring for United Japan! He is but the first of what promises to be the premiere juniorheavyweight division in all of wrestling for the next millenium. UJPW has also received a letter of intent from former Tokyo Organization of Puroresu junior talent BRAT Dragon Mitsu. Nothing has been confirmed as yet, however. If you would like to be a part of UJPW, please send an EMail with a generic application (swipe one from another fed :-) ) to: [detail withheld] For consideration. Reminder: we are ONLY accepting JAPANESE WRESTLERS at present. Thank you. United Japan Pro Wrestling [detail withheld] http://ujpw.hypermart.net/
