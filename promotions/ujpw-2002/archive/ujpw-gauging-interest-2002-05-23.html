UJPW: New E-Puroresu League Gauging Interest

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings! We are writing to you from United Japan Pro Wrestling, a new fantasy wrestling league based in a fantasy Japan, emulating the style of All Japan, New Japan, NOAH, etc. We are aware that a similar league had shut down recently, and we are trying to gague interest in a new e-puro fed. We hope to get up and running soon, however with our experiences over the years with SJPW, Neo Japan, JPA, TOP, G-Pro...we are trying not to put the cart before the horse, and instead find out if we have a roster before we have the first show :-) UNITED JAPAN PRO WRESTLING will be a KING'S ROAD STYLE promotion, with wrestlers working to their physical limits, without so much of the "sports entertainment" aspect of the business (however, angles have always been there, so some slight SE may be inevitable). We will run with 2 divisions, Heavyweight and Juniorheavyweight, and it will be traditional style wrestling (no real gimmick matches, hardcore, garbage, etc.) As soon as we are stable, we shall announce the title tournaments, however, we can tell you at present that there will be 3: Heavyweight, Junior, and Tag Team. The fedheads are experienced in both fed-heading and handling; [name withheld] was an assistant to Neo Japan Pro Wrestling, as well as fedhead for JPA (Japan Puroresu Association). He has also handled various characters in the National Wrestling Council for the past 3 years. [name withheld] was VP of JPA for a time, adn has handled a former NeWA World Champion. It should be noted in the interests of good faith and fairness, however, that both [name withheld] and [name withheld] do indeed plan to handle characters in UJPW, but there will be no greased palms here. We respect the handlers first. It should also be noted that the first few months, there will be NO GAIJIN CHARACTERS (gaijin = foreigner). It has long been a fallacy of other e-puro feds that take place in Japan, yet have a majority of American characters. So, FOR NOW, the promotion is closed to foreign characters, JAPANESE ONLY. Once we get more settled, and have established ourselves, we will allow gaijin into the group. If you wish to give a quick, generic application as a letter of intent, or have any other questions, please feel free to EMail UJPW at: [detail withheld] We also have a preliminary website set up at http://ujpw.hypermart.net/ We look forward to hearing from you, and that you join us in keeping e-puroresu alive! United Japan Pro Wrestling [name withheld] & [name withheld] [detail withheld] http://ujpw.hypermart.net/
