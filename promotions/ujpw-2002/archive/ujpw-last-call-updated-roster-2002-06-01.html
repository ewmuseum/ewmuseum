UJPW: Last Call, Updated Roster

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[NOTE: SUNDAY is the deadline to be booked on the first show, TBA! WE NEED NATIVE JAPANESE HEAVYWEIGHTS – WE ARE FILLED WITH JUNIORS NOW! THANKS!] United Japan Pro Wrestling have announced a number of new signees to the new puroresu organization. Following the announcement that IRON CHEF KEIJI KOBE would be working with UJPW as the trainer for the juniorheavyweight division, several junior talents, both veteran and rookie, have thrown in their lots with United Japan. First and foremost, a former champion in G-Pro, and featured in the EMWC, "BLACK DOG" YOSHITO KATSUMURA will be wrestling in United Japan rings. Also, former MILL and Oita Pro junior KAZUYA ASAKINO will be joining us. Asakino is a cousin of the legendary TETSUO MATSUMOTO, and was last seen working for TOP. Young juniorheavyweight lions WINGER SHIZUMI and MITSUYA TENZAKI also join us, making UJPW one of the most well-rounded junior divisions anywhere in wrestling today. Not to say that all UJPW does is juniors, heavyweight talent JINSHI TOGAWA has also agreed to a deal with United Japan. Togawa is a former JPA talent, and hopes to add some "Explosive" excitement to the fans. UJPW are currently in negotiations with other fine puroresu talents as well. UJPW expect to make an announcement about their debut show, INFINITY 2002 GONG, late next week. Our total current signings to date are: Genji Yamato Tetsuo Matsumoto Tasogare no Prince Iron Chef Keiji Kobe (trainer) Kiyoshi Sakai Jin'ei Tamago BRAT Dragon Mitsu Yuji Yamasaki RYOUGA Kenzan Akira Tanabe Shigeru Nakamori Jinshi Togawa Yoshito Katsumura Kazuya Asakino WINGER Shizumi Mitsuya Tenzaki If you would like to be a part of UJPW, please send an EMail with a generic application (swipe one from another fed :-) ) to: [detail withheld] For consideration. Reminder: we are ONLY accepting JAPANESE WRESTLERS at present. WE NEED HEAVYWEIGHTS!! Thank you. United Japan Pro Wrestling [detail withheld] http://ujpw.hypermart.net/
