Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

HCW introduced itself on 7 July 2000 with a roster update announcing that its first card would decide championships, urging quick sign-ups for an early title shot, at a site it hosted on Hypermart. Its own two surviving posts give the promotion's name two ways — Hollywood Championship Wrestling in the first, Hollywood City Wrestling in the second, the latter signed by its president.

A second post, six days later, announced that roleplaying was beginning that Saturday and added a battle royal for a title shot to the card, still taking applications up to the night before.

No further document survives. Whether that first Saturday card was ever staged, or which name the promotion settled on, is not recorded.
