Well here is the list of wrestlers that are confirmed for NEW Weekly War on Monday, although as you will see the pickings are slim. Singles Face Heel Mace (Part) Grandmaster C (Full) Mean Streak (Part) Dethlok the Asassin (Part) Nature Boy Todd Newkirk (Full) Blind Justice (Part) Big Daddy Vicelord (Part) Phantom Striker (Part) The Folk Curt Fredrick (Part) Mad Dog Eddy Ramos (Full) The Ace Alex Porteuax (Full Tag Team Face Heel Ghetto Boys (Vicelord & Fredrick) Damage Inc (Ramos & Porteuax) Gov't Killers (Dethlok & Justice As you can see, WE NEED MORE WRESTLERS!!!!! Everyone PLEASE spread the word about this federation, especially those of you with connections in other feds. Tell them that this federation is a CAPITAL BLOODLETTER! You will see this after monday.
As for Weekly War, the match list will go up Friday. Any direct challenges must be made between the two parties involeved and then sent to me for a match to occur, otherwise the matches for this week will be selected at random. (Any interference sparking a fued is HIGHLY encouraged!)
GET READY FOR BLOOD ON MONDAY!!!!
