****** Jeff Jarrett vs Brian Pillman ****** BV: In a fantastic contest both men put on a pure wrestling show for the fans. Jeff Jarrett sinched in a figure 4 on Pillman but was caught using the ropes and that forced a break. As JJ argued with the ref Pillman mounted the ropes and as JJ turned around he was met with a flying clothsline and a cover as Pillman scored his biggest win in some time.
****** Salvatore Sincer and Alex Wright vs New Foundation ****** TP: There was many tag teams in the IWWF and the New Foundation proved that they are one to worry about. They took it to Salvatore and Wright and won the match when Owen hit a rocket launcher on Wright for the win in
11:10. ****** Savio Vega vs Yokozuna ****** BV: A great contest as Yokozuna made his return with his new manager, The Taskmaster and Savio returned with a new stable! Savio had things well in hand till he went for a spin kick in the corner,
Yoko grabbed his foot and tossed him to the floor. As Yoko argued with the referee Mr. Perfect came out and attacked Savio and nailed him with a Perfectplex on the floor. The ref turned around and counted
Savio out and Yoko got a questionable win.
****** Nasty Boys vs New Team FMW ****** TP: This was the debut of the Nasties and and the debut of the new Team FMW. It looked like the Nasties were going to win when Sags was going to go for his top rope elbowdrop but before he could Paul E.
shoved him off the top and the ref didn't see it. Bret then locked on the Sharpshooter so the Taskmaster came in hit Bret with a chair and the ref saw that and dq'ed the Nasties.

Shango won with a spinebuster in 15:19. ****** Fortunate Lives vs Foriegn Exchange ****** (TV Title shot on WW) BV: We go from two boring matches to what could be considered a classic as all four men put forth a fantastic match. Konnan was almost pinned by Horowitz at about 9 minutes when Barry used a beautiful bridging back suplex but Liger made the save. Liger caught Warlord with a sensational moonsault at 16:41 for the win.
****** Mystery Team vs Gangstas ****** TP: The mystery team turned out to be the Kongs, Awesome Kong and King Kong! Gangstas didn't care and they took it to the Kongs anyway. At the 17 minute mark, Cornette hit Mustafa with a chair and King Kong slapped on a sleeper for the win. After the match, Cornette shocked the IWWF fans that his new team is the Kongs!
****** Rick Martel vs Hunter Hemsley ****** (IC) BV: Once again Hunter defended his title against a stablemate and the fans were not impressed. Hunter scored a pin after a very weak looking pedigree at 12:50. After the match as they hugged out come a very ticked off Public Enemy, Chris Benoit, Eddy Guerrero Raven and
Stevie Richards. As they circled the ring security made a effort to stop them but to no avail as PE got a hold of HHH and Martel made like a chicken running. PE grabbed HHH in a DDT and then Eddy nailed him with a frog splash. HHH was in trouble when out came the Diamond Mine.
Chaos was ready to erupt but finally IWWF security pulled the stables apart. ****** Blu Brothers vs Public Enemy ****** (Street) TP: This was an extreme match! They used everything they could get their hands on and I think I even saw a kitchen sink! It took TPE
3 tables to win 3-0 in the street fight. After the match, the Nasties showed up and challenged TPE to a street fight at Wrestlefest, then all 4 wrestlers fought to the back and they are still probably fighting right now.
****** El Hijo Del Santo vs Sabu ****** BV: Once again not much of a match as Buellah got her man disqualified early with a chair to the back of Santo. After the bell sounded the entire Million Dollar Corporation stormed the ring and beat the snot out of Sabu, by the time Sandman, Dreamer and
Headhunters arrived Sabu was a bloody mess and had to be carried out. ****** Davey Boy Smith vs Raven ****** TP: DBS was looking for a measure of revenge against Raven and he got it. Raven DDT'ed DBS and just stood there staring at Smith. When
Raven finally went for the cover DBS rolled him up into a small package for the win. After the match DBS got more revenge as he gorilla pressed Raven through a table.
****** Crush vs Terry Funk ****** (Bullrope) BV: The keyword in this contest was violence. It was short but not sweet as Funk was a bloody mess by the end and Crush was not looking to good either. Crush caught a surprised Terry Funk with a Heart Punch and then hit all 4 buckles for the win at 8:50. As Crush celebrated out came Vader who blindsided Crush and nailed him with a series of
Vaderbombs before security pulled the Monster from The Rockies off him.
****** LOD vs Badstreet ****** (TV) TP: This match was short and sweet. Both teams almost won at different times of the match. Koloff almost had the win after he hit
Animal with the sickle but Hawk made the save. In the end, Hawk won with a cross body on Kerry.
****** Cactus Jack vs Lex Luger ****** BV: Lex Luger was primed and ready to regain his status as the top rated wrestler in the league. Cactus was not going to let this happen as he pummeled Lex for 10 minutes. Finally Lex got the advantage and put Cactus in the Torture Rack and it looked like it was all over from their, but Lex made one error his mouth was open and Cactus took this oppertunity to lock on the mandible claw and Lex crumpled to the mat a couple seconds later Lex was out cold and Cactus retianed his gold.
That does it for TNT, we will be back tomarrow with Ratings Report #5 and a statement from President Sammartino about the HHH/Malenko situation.
