****** The Dragons vs Gold Records ****** BV: In what looked like a great match up, Ricky Steamboat and Hakushi humiliated Gold Records and an incensed Jeff Jarrett nailed
Hakushi with his guitar at the 2:48 mark for the dq. Dragons take an easy victory but got pummeled by the Gold Records. Out to save The
Dragons came none other that Roddy Piper, Bam Bam Bigelow and Rey
Misterio Jr. This prompted Gold Records to hit the high road.
****** Chris Benoit vs Adam Bomb ****** TP: This was Bomb's debut with the Diamond Mine and what better way to make an impression then by beating the former Jr. champ, Chris Benoit. That's exactly what he did. Benoit put up a fight but Bomb won with a powerslam in 12:29. ****** Mr. Perfect vs Savio Vega ****** BV: A great match up between the Carribean Legend and the former AWA World Champion. Savio Vega had the match well in hand when he nailed Mr. P with the Carribean Kick. As he went for the cover Marlena climbed on the apron and Savio went after her, Savio made the mistake of chasing Marlena down the aisle and as Mr. P distracted the ref Owen
Hart nailed Savio in the ribs with his Slammy and rolled him in the ring. Mr. Perfect than locked on a Abdominal Stretch and Savio had no choice but to submit at 20:46. Let's go to the Million Dollar
Destruction Squad.

HAVE A NICE NIGHT!! ****** Lex Luger vs Mabel ****** TP: This was sort of a rematch from the battle royal on WW. This time was different though as both men were fresh. Luger showed his strength in bodyslamming the big man but Luger showed how smart he was when he tried to rack Mabel! Mabel laughed at him and belly-to-belly suplexed his way to victory.
****** Classless Society vs Harlem Heat ****** BV: This was another excellent and very long match as the Harlem Heat used their power to subdue Salvatore and Martel. Harlem Heat had the advantage until Hunter and Jeff Jarrett showed up at ringside, as
Booker turned his attention Salvatore nailed him with a beautiful dropkick and covered him for the win. After the match all 4 of Diamond
Mine attacked Harlem Heat but were quickly stopped as the Blu Brothers and Barbarian and Isaac came to the rescue and once again the DM ran from the ring.
****** Rocko Rock vs New Jack ****** (Baseball Bat) TP: Rock wanted to make his presence felt as a singles wrestler as he beat Sabu on the WW card and now he was trying to win his second singles match. On the other hand, New Jack showed that he has more home run power as he put Rock away in 15:57.
****** Bam Bam Bigelow and Rey Misterio vs Ultimate Team ****** BV: What can I say about this match except, mis-match. Bam Bam and Rey challenged the former champs and saw why they are one of the best duos in our sport. Shamrock put away Bam Bam with a sleeper at 13:59 in one of the most uneven contests in IWWF history.
****** Goldust vs Mark Mero ****** TP: Mero wanted a rematch with Goldust and he got it. He didn't get the stipulations he wanted but none-the-less he got his revenge as he put Goldie away with a Hurricanrana in 12:55. ****** Jeff Jarrett vs Arn Anderson ****** BV: Double A was marking his return and targeting one Double J. Both men showed that they are at the top of their game as they used armbars, leg locks and other submission type moves on each other. It wound up being a little luck taht helped in the end as JJ missed a clothsline and spun around only to be caught in a Gordbuster for the
1-2-3 at 13:57. ****** Johnny Grunge vs Rocko Rock ****** (Baseball Bat) TP: Grunge must have been thinking "Rock has a baseball bat match so I want one too." And he got it. He got the something Rocko did, a loss. Mustafa didn't have an easy time but he put Grunge away in 14:15 with a claw. ****** Shawn Michaels vs Ahmed Johnson ****** BV: Two of the most popular men in wrestling faced off for the first time in a great match that took both men to the limit. Shawn scored the dq win at 16:10 when he nailed Ahmed with Sweet Chin Music and went for the cover when he was nailed by Mr. Wonderful with a chair. As Ahmed and Orndorff double teamed the HBK out came Diesel to the rescue. He grabbed Ahmed and jacknifed him and went for Orndorff but he fled the scene.
****** Eddy Guerrero vs 1-2-3 Kid ****** (Jr.) TP: Eddy was the greatest battle royal champ now can he be the greatest Jr. champ? He showed in this match that he has the potential to be as he defeated the Kid in 6:23 with a swinging DDT.
****** 911 vs Mr. Hughes vs Nailz ****** (Triangle) BV: In what was billed the battle of the big men these three went toe to toe to toe for a good 20 minutes before Mr. Hughes chokeslammed
Nailz for the first elimination. 2 minutes lated Mr. Hughes went for the ride and as 911 went for the cover he was blindsided by The Kongs.
911 was unfazed by the attack but the dq was rendered as 911 grabbed both Kongs and chokeslammed the hell outta them. Hughes and Cornette wisely left the ring before experiencing the ride of a lifetime. 911 will get his revenge in 2 weeks!!
****** Rick Rude vs Stevie Richards ****** TP: Richards wanted to show Rude how sexy he was but he must have brought the wrong body! The veteran Rude ducked the Stevie kick 3 times before beating Richards with a backslide in 12:57.
****** Legion Of Doom vs Fortunate Lives ****** (TV Tag) BV: Fortunate Lives were looking to prove that what happened on WW was not a fluke as they took it the LOD early on. Warlord press-slammed Hawk right over the top rope and then threw Animal right on top at one point. Barry Horowitz took a nasty beating when Harley
Race hit him with a cane but Barry still fought back and as Hawk and
Barry fought on the floor Warlord came from behind and clocked him with a double axe handle. Barry rolled back in and Fortunate Lives scored a second count-out victory over the LOD but not the gold. A rematch is signed for the last time in a no-dq, no countout, no timelimit, street fight with the winners taking home the gold!
****** Cactus Jack vs Raven ****** (TV) TP: What more could you ask for? 2 of the most extreme athletes going at it. This was a great match that unfortunately had a screw job ending. Jack had Raven in the Mandible claw but Alphonso was disctracting the ref. Richards climbed inside the ring and nailed Jack with a steviekick to the back of the head. Raven dropped on elbow on Jack and got the pin to win some gold. BV: As Raven celebrated his victory Randy Savage, 2 Cold and El Hijo Del Santo came down the aisle and attacked with chairs. As the ref tried to restore order he took a hit from the Harley Race. Chaos has erupted as here comes the PE to help their friends. Once again controvesy surrounds the TV title, as seems to happen with Cactus
Jack. This time Raven used a little help from Stevie for the win, this really sets us up for some great moments at Wrestlefest as Stevie and
Raven must face Cactus and The Macho Man. Well that is all the time we have but we will be back tomarrow with The Ratings Report!
