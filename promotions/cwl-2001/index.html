Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Championship Wrestling League, the CWL, was a website federation owned by Ed Wilson. It opened on 1 January 2001 and told its story in numbered chapters, calling both its members' role-plays and its own shows by that name, so that any piece of the story could be found by its number.

In its own story the league was more than a century old, descended from a travelling wrestling and boxing show of 1902, and was owned by Mr. Tort of Tort Enterprises, with Terry Nodar, Rudo Insano in his fWo days, as its chief official. It aired Thursday Night Titans and returned to pay-per-view with Revolution on 31 March 2001 in Albany, New York, where Vince Jacobs beat the Mountain for the World Heavyweight Championship and Danny Culligan took the running of the league from Terry Nodar.

Its roster gathered wrestlers who had worked the fWo, the CSWA and Tornado Wrestling among other federations, and its site carried a chat center, a fan board, a store of imagined merchandise and banner advertising whose revenue it pledged to the Boys Hope Girls Hope charity. It stood on good terms with the fWo, where its owner's own characters had begun, and in March 2001 the two leagues crossed over onto each other's shows.

Ed Wilson died in the first week of May 2001, while the Thursday Night Titans of 3 May 2001 was in production, and the league ended with him. That show was left unfinished, and in the days that followed the league's members posted chapters in his memory.

Crossover booking with CWL (2001) (2001-03)
The two leagues staged a crossover in March 2001: two fWo wrestlers on the CWL's Thursday Night Titans, which the fWo's own commentary calls the first CWL/fWo crossover, and CWL wrestlers on the fWo's Sunday Slaughter of 18 and 25 March 2001.
