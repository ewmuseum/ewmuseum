What this is The CWL's own front page, from its main news item to its standing champions, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 14 April 2001. The site's navigation menus and the rotating quote at its head are not reproduced.
Arc Two Begins As the CWL celebrated a return of the big events with Revolution changes were afoot to turn all of his efforts upside down. As Vince Jacobs defeated the Mountain and captured the CWL World Heavyweight Championship, Danny Culligan used his political clout with Mr. Tort to insert himself as the new acting manager of the CWL, replacing Terry Nodar and bringing the promotion firmly into his grasp. With a tour of the North Eastern area of the United States complete, the CWL heads west toward uncertain waters and more dangerous territory than they can possibly imagine...
