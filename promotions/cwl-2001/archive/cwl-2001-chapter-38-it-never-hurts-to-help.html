What this is Chapter 38 of the CWL's second book, posted with no byline on 14 April 2001, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 22 April 2001. The league called its role-plays chapters and numbered them with its shows.
It never hurts to help Chapter 38 Book 2 CWL Roster Chapters 10:53:10 U.S. CST April 14, 2001 NOTE: The following events took place during CWL Titans. "It must be the beer. It's got to be the beer." The protesters sat outside the arena, held at bay by a cheap, paper like link chain fence. They show was more then two thirds of the way done, and the numbers had not diminished at all. They held strong, hundreds of hate-filled onlookers, pelting the building, and any who dared show there face, with anything they could get there hands on. The Don was being forced into the back on a San Jose Police car, his hands behind his back, bound together by the stainless steel cuffs. A crowd of various wrestlers had gathered already. The Total Eclipse looked on from a distance, hamburger in hand, not really sure what to make of he entire situation. His former Gunman partner, Kid cool, stood a few feet away, seemingly still lost in space, his eyes fixed on whatever it was that he was looking at high above everyone else. And Little Italy peered over the back of the forklift, the one which he and the Lepcrachan had taken refuge when this crazy fiasco had begun. He watched in horror as Legz made his way away from the scene, and he ducked back behind the forklift and turned to the equally stunned Leprechaun. Little Italy hissed. He almost had to force the words out, he couldn't believe what he had just witnessed. "Did I just see, what I think I saw?" Lep responded in a somber tone "I'm afraid so little man ….. that car had only one light working." "No idiot , I'm talking about that big man talking to Legz just then. Something wasn't right there , but I don't know what ." Little Italy spoke under his breath trying to keep their position unknown to the onlookers. The Leprechaun peered round the edge of the forklift and watched as the Police Car sped off into the distance. The crowd of gathered wrestlers began to dissolve back into the arena without a single look back to see what had just taken place. "The Don being arrested for murder? He gave me a ride into the building past the .... midget hating crowd. This guy's saint, he couldn't have done it." Leprechaun shrugged. "Maybe he did." Italy interjected quickly. "He did my ass!" Leprechaun swung his head in disgust. "Listen here Malta be it eleven beers be it twenty six beers , I don't wanna know about your sordid social life" "No , now you know that's not what I meant! He could never have killed that guy. Under that rough Mafia boss type exterior , he's truly a good guy." The Leprechaun began to pace up and down behind the big solid figure of the forklift , mentally picturing a white line along the floor as he did. "You know Eejit , I think your onto something here , sure you cant drink me under the table, but that doesn't mean you're a total gersha." Italy smiled. "Yeah, and your not half bad yourself, lucky." The Leprechaun chuckled, as Italy continued."You know what we've got to do." Lep looked perplexed, and shrugged at the small Italian. "We've got to help him out, man. It's the right thing to do!" Italy placed a hand on each hip, and thrusted his chest out toward the sky. A large, Italian flag appeared behind in, waving in the wind. Italy narrowed his eye's, and began speaking once again. "We have to right this wrong my small green friend. We have to stand up against the system, and lend a helping hand to one of our own, one of our fellow Italians." Leprechaun burped loudly. "I'm Irish." Italy paused, but quickly picked up where he left off, speaking in a deep proud tone. "Detail's, detail's. Anyway, we've got to do this!" Leprechaun questioned Italy, half drunk and falling over while Italy rambled on. "And why are we doin' this again?" "It's our civil duty!" Lep burped again. "It's the beer." Italy shook his head. "Yeah, that too. But we owe it to ourselves!" Lep smiled. " I don't know about you, but the only thing I owe to myself is to finish that crate" Italy grinned, and agreed. "Then lets get to it!" The two looked both way's, and darted out from behind the forklift and eased down the long, empty hallway. Italy put one hand on Lep's shoulder, and belched before speaking. "So what do we do after this crate is finished? Go to the police? " I dunno about you but I'm thinking a game of Strip Poker... You know I'm sure we can rope that Penny in" Italy shrugged. "Yeah, I suppose, but she's so damn pale." The scene faded.
