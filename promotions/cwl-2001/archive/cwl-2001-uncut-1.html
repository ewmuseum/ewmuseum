CWL (2001) — Card

CWL Uncut

scenes dated in its story from 11 January to 22 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The CWL's first deleted-scenes, bloopers and outtakes show, gathering material from its first story arc, with the fWo's side of the March 2001 crossover between the two leagues, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 19 July 2001.
Introduction Welcome to the very first CWL Deleted Scenes, Bloopers and Outtakes show. This show takes rare or never before seen material from the first arc of the CWL. The following segments and shows will provide you with a rare inside look into the Championship Wrestling League and the Thursday Night Titans show. While the performers often have to perform in front of live audiences and thus can do it in only one take, taped sections rarely go right the first time. Tonight you will get a chance to look at some of these candid moments that until now only the camera men and producers have seen. In addition, sometimes scenes have to be cut from the show for time. While these segments are ultimately deemed unimportant to the overall flow of the show, some of these scenes can provide rare additional insight into the characters. So enjoy this rare look at the CWL from days past, and we thank you for watching CWL programming every week. Because of you these moments are ultimately possible. Outtake January 11, 2001 Backstage, tires screeched and the smell of burning rubber wafted toward some of the stage hands. It was biter cold outside and most of the hired help were just hoping for the show to be over soon so they could all go home. Those that had been around the week before rolled their eyes. The sound of music thumping as the car neared signaled the arrival of Kaid Mann... late for the second week in a row. Mann's car pulled up to the edge of the loading bin, parking directly on top of some light cards used by the filming crew. A stage tech approached the car and wearily felt like he was reliving last week all over again. "You can't... hey! You can't park here!" Kaid Mann looked out at the man before him and sneered. He pulled a six pack of beer from behind the driver's seat and pulled one free from the plastic. Popping it, the beer sprayed out into Kaid's face, the foam bubbling over on his hand and coating his face. Surprised, Kaid dropped the six pack and it struck the steering wheel. The pressure caused several cans to explode and beer sprayed up and everywhere. Cursing as the filming crew laughed, Kaid Mann swung at the six pack to get it out of the car. His fist struck the steering wheel and the cap bent off, inflating the air bag. The giant white pillow quickly filled the car and Rove burst out laughing. "Aw hell..." Kaid grinned and laughed. Techs arrived to clean out the car get the shot ready for a second take. "I've got to back and do this part again, right? Right... okay." Commentary The following scene was originally scheduled to be another milestone in the growing dislike between the Flying Frenchie and Little Italy. In the end it conflicted with other segments on the show and was ultimately not used. The general nature of the segment remained the same in other segments that went out on Titans shows, and the growing dislike between the two came about in other ways. Deleted Scene January 18, 2001 The Frenchie veered down one hallway and wiped his brow "De toutes les promotions elle doit venir et travailler pour celui-ci..." He marched down the hallway with purpose, looking directly ahead of himself as he attempted to put as much distance as possible between himself and Ashley. Suddenly he bumped into something, stumbled, and went sprawling into the wall. He turned around and glared fiercely at what he had tripped over. "Oh man... watch it! You okay?" What he had tripped over appeared to be speaking to him. The Frenchie blinked and shook his head to clear it from the stars that were circling overhead. The masked man who looked back at him looked suspicious. The Frenchie blinked again. "Quel est ce petit homme que j'ai trébuché plus de? Ah! Little Iguana!" Little Italy frowned deeply. "That's Italy. Big Little Italy." The Frenchie snorted. "Big? Zis... big? Non... zere is not'ing big about zis iguana before me." The Frenchie stood, brushed himself off, and started to leave. Little Italy scowled at the rudeness of the French wrestler. He had been in promotions for several years now... a world renowned veteran of the ring. How could he be such a complete ass? Surely he would have had to learn to work with other people by now. Little Italy shrugged. Perhaps better to just let things be. He got up himself and dusted himself off, preparing to walk in the opposite direction. "Turn the other cheek... that's what I should do. Yes, it's always better to..." From down the hallway the distant voice of the Frenchie could be heard yelling at someone. "Zere is an iguana down zis hallway, beware! Ze t'ing is so tiny you cannot see it until it is too late to avoid stomping it." Little Italy's hands turned to fists and he growled to himself. Outtake January 25, 2001 "This is a world that has lost touch with reality." Fallen kicked at a rock and sent it bouncing down the hallway. Concrete walls made echoes as the rock spun and finally stilled. It sat there, casting a long and wavering shadow from the light bulb hanging from the ceiling. "I'm tired of turning on the television and seeing a group of attractive young people making exhaustive salaries complaining about how they just don't have enough private time. Then it's off to commercials where we are all told about the newest and brightest things that we are supposed to buy to make our lives better. Of course, these products are just designed to drive us into bankruptcy while keeping us tied to the establishment. The system has forced us into the bowels of humanity. But it doesn't matter anymore... you are born and society rams a giant tube up your ass and starts pumping in the most addictive crap ever to come down the pipe. You know why the authority figures are so against pot and other drugs? Because they don't want their monopoly on the addiction being threatened by chemicals produced in some third-world backwater. God forbid people start thinking for themselves for a change. Because if they ever do, what good is the authority anymore? What good is the government? What good is society when you have your own thoughts and ideas? I live for the day when commercials for luxury cars don't give a boner to every man sitting in his lazy boy. I live for that day... the day when people are just satisfied with whatever car they already have. The day people don't need fancy clothes with designer labels because they are happy with whatever they have. They don't need to spend the money they buy with pieces of their lives on useless garbage designed only to perpetuate their slavery to the system." "What system?" Douglas had walked quietly up behind Fallen and sat down. Fallen glanced over and him and then let his head drop back down. "Doesn't matter. What do you want?" Douglas raised his eyebrows. "What do I want? Well... have you thought over my idea? The two of us... nobody could stop us. We could crush anyone in our path... this promotion would be ours. Ours for the taking. Now what do you say?" Fallen looked up at Douglas. Douglas had a gleam in his eye and for a moment Fallen nearly believed in it all. And that was when he was aware of what he had to do. "No." Fallen slowly stood. "The answer is no. I have my own path to travel... and there is only enough room on it for one." Douglas' eyes narrowed and his jaw tightened. He stared at Fallen and felt his muscles tense. Then he relaxed. "Well... I wanted an answer and you have given me one. Fine... that is fine. I want you to just be... comfortable. If you don't want to join forces... well, I can't make you can I? No... I can't. You've got to do what you have to do... and I guess I have to do what I have to do." Douglas grinned a shark grin and picked up Fallen's bag. "Here... this is yours, right? Hey... what do you have in there? Stinks like a mother..." Fallen grabbed it from him. "It's mine. It's none of your concern." Fallen grabbed the bag away but it suddenly broke open, spilling sand out onto the floor that had been used for weight in the bag. Surprised, Fallen yelped in shock. Fallen and Douglas looked down at it and started laughing. "Cut, cut..." Fallen slapped Douglas on the back. "Okay, whose bright idea was it to use sand for the weight in the bag? Man, I've got prop all over my shoes..." Douglas looked over to the filming crew. "Are we going to have to shoot this again? I can't keep my face straight during the Lazy Boy part of Skyler's speech here." Fallen thumbed through his script. "Bowels of humanity? Who talks like that? Can't we get better writers than this? And can we please get rid of that pipe up the ass comment? I mean... come on, kids watch this show..." Commentary The following scene was originally was planned to show the growing hatred of the Don for Kodiak Vic Creed. In this segment the Don crept into Kodiak Vic Creed's dressing room to slip some drugs into his water. The segment in the end was run with less visablity into what the Don was doing, thus providing more of a surprise to the viewered when the Don collapsed from the drugged drink. Alternate Scene February 15, 2001 The hallway was empty for a while, then the Don quietly slunk into view. His eyes were narrowed and he looked rapidly from side to side, as if worried about being seen. The Don walked up to a locker room door and listened carefully to the door. Smiling, he grasped the handle and turned. It didn't move. The Don looked crossly down at the locked door and thumbed a padlock that had been slipped over the catch on the outside. Grumbling in Italian, the Don pulled a small tool set out of his pocket and removed two small metal rods. He inserted them into the padlock and manipulated the catches inside. Working quickly, the Don darted his eyes up and down the hallway looking for passersby. Finally a small click came, the Don pushed open the door. He placed the lock pick tools back into his pocket and taking one last look down the hallway he slipped inside, removing as he went a small bottle of water from his pocket. The door swung closed. On it was a simple paper that had been taped to the door. It read simply, "Creed". Commentary When the previous scene was cut it made sense to cut the next scene was well. Here Kodiak Vic Creed is speaking with Dawn, and the viewer is treated to Creed taking the drugged water and foretelling what was about to come in the match between the two. This scene provided some good insight into Dawn and Creed's relationship but was ultimately rewritten. Alternate Scene February 15, 2001 "World champ, Creed... tonight is where it all begins." Creed nodded to Dawn. "It began the moment I stepped into this federation. I was born to wear that belt and it will take a lot more than this miserable bunch of losers to take it from me." Dawn slipped her key into the padlock and snapped it open, then pushed open the door to their dressing room. They walked into the room and Dawn tossed her coat into a corner of the ring. She turned back to Creed and smiled. "What about Mountain... he seems to think he's the big boy of the sandlot." "Mountain?" Creed smirked. "You've got to be kidding me. That goofball runs around with a tattoo of a big hand on his gut. He runs into me... and he will in the finals... and I'll take my hand and shove it right up his ass." Dawn laughed. "I didn't realize you were training to be a proctologist..." Creed grunted. "You know what I mean." Dawn smiled. "Of course. Anyway, speaking of medical things you better take some of these. Anti-inflammatory... should help with the arm." "Ugh." Creed groaned. "Pills. Disgusting." Creed looked around for something to wash them down with and spotted a bottle of water that was standing alone on a counter. Popping it open he reached out for the pills and Dawn spilled a small pile of blue colored capsules into his hand. Grimacing, Creed tossed them into his mouth and drank the water, downing the whole bottle in several quick gulps. Creed looked briefly down at the bottle, puzzled as he smacked his lips. "Huh... aftertaste." Dawn took the bottle from Creed and tossed it into the garbage. "Enough talking about the future... look forward to tonight." Creed smiled widely and his grin resembled a white shark. "The Don. I'm going to paste his mafia ass so hard he's not just going to be swimming with the fishes... but be fed to them in tiny little flaky pieces." Creed punched the wall, then marched from the room with Dawn closely behind. Commentary The next scene was designed to give more insight into the growing mystery between Kid Cool and his one-sided romantic interest Nightshade. At the time Kid Cool was promoting his BONI championship idea, and ultimately this was confusing to the viewers who had too much to keep track of with Kid Cool, a character who is designed to be fairly one-dimensional. This scene is likely to be run in a slightly modified way in the future when more importance is given to the Kid Cool/Nightshade relationship. Deleted Scene February 22, 2001 "Okay... 1... 800... 632... 8230." Kid Cool scratched his head and waited for the phone to connect him to the number he had just dialed. Nothing seemed to be happening, and Kid Cool began to wonder if this was another one of those fake phones. Any second now it would start to yell at him in the voice of Bert or Ernie and he would... "Yes, hello mon?" Kid Cool blinked. "Cleo?" The voice that came back over the receiver sounded like heavy Jamaican and Kid Cool smiled. He had gotten it right. "That's correct mon... and you must be that little red topped wrestler boy. What do you need Miss Cleo for mon, when you have de whole world in de palm of your hand?" Kid Cool scratched his head. He had forgotten what he was going to ask Miss Cleo about, and had a brief moment of panic. Then he remembered that he had written instructions to himself on his hand. He quickly switched the hand holding the phone and looked at his palm, reading what he had written into the phone. "I am an elf, call me Zandar." This didn't sound quite right to Kid Cool. He looked more closely at his hand, rubbed his eyes, and tried again. "May I inject your face with this sausage." This didn't sound quite right either. Kid Cool was about to take another stab at it when Miss Cleo interrupted him. "Listen mon, Miss Cleo knows what de problems are. Miss Cleo can see it in de Tarot cards, and for you mon, she had drawn de Hangman card. But fear not little wrestler mon, for de card has been drawn upside down. Dis means that love is around de corner for you mon... but it is de love of a very dark woman who is not to be trifled with." Kid Cool's heart sunk. He hung up the phone numbly... dimly aware of his surroundings. Miss Cleo had confirmed his worst fears. Rosie O'Donnell was in love with him. Kid Cool gulped ominously. Things were going to get a lot worse before they got better. Commentary The following two segments and match originally ran on the fWo [Fans Wrestling Organization] Sunday Slaughter program. This was the fWo's side of the crossover that appeared. The crossover ran on CWL Titans and the fWo Sunday Slaughter program as a way to show support before both promotions and to give the readers a surprise moment. fWo Crossover Material March 18, 2001 The backstage of the arena was relatively quiet. The vast majority of wrestlers and other members of the talent had already arrived and by and large were about their work. Because of this simple fact, security was light at the gated entrance. There were a few fans hanging around outside and hoping to catch a glimpse of their favorite fWo superstar, but the vast majority of them were inside watching the show. The sounds from the arena could not be heard outside, and the guard was happy with this. Wrestling by and large annoyed him. It seemed as though the wrestlers came to town every three to four months, and they usually brought the sort of drunken chaos afterward that only the most extreme heavy metal bands produced. He yawned. For the next hour or so, things would be bliss. Just the night, the noise of the nearby freeway and... A car was coming closer. He saw the headlights draw nearer in the night and narrowed his eyes. A late arrival? Perhaps... but it seemed odd all the same. From his understanding they were broadcasting that wrestling stuff on television... a late arrival would mess up the plans. But the rental car was drawing nearer to his small entrance gate and there was no doubt the driver wanted in. Sighing, he let his hand drift lazily out of the box window. "ID and name." He was reaching for the clipboard which carried the approved visitor list when he was suddenly jerked forward into the glass. Gurgling, he looked with wide eyes out of the window at the driver of the car. An angry looking man with a goatee and sharply shaven blonde hair leered at him. He wore a dirty shirt with a strange blue logo on it. Three letters made up the logo... CWL. "Well god damn, if it ain't the little watch dog son of a bitch of the fWo. Saddle up cowboy, because all hell is gonna break loose... so says me. So says Neo." fWo Crossover Material March 18, 2001 MUD walked backstage nodding at wrestlers as he went. This whole environment seemed strange to him... and even stranger was that nobody seemed to notice anything strange about him walking around with all of the other wrestlers. These were strange people to him... yet nobody seemed alarmed. He reached over, picking up a cup of coffee and nodding to Mike Bear as he walked by. Bear... yet another person he didn't really know, but who seemed to know and understand him. MUD spotted the Boston Strangler coming out of a dressing room and nodded to him. "Thanks for showing up Thursday." The Strangler nodded without commitment in his gesture. "You paid well enough. And we enjoyed teaching that cocky little bastard a message. We would be surprised if he even shows up to work anymore." Suddenly the Strangler was pushed forward, stumbling a step as a high pitched southern laugh filled the room. "Wrong again chump... maybe Tort is right about Boston after all. You people really are as dumb as you look." The Strangler, who had only stumbled forward a step from Neo's shove turned sharply and growled. A fist clenched, but MUD's hand fell upon his shoulder. "You've done your part Strangler... I know I can handle this mutt." MUD stepped forward and Neo grinned wide. "You know wrong, bitch." Neo swung and caught MUD in the face, knocking him backward. He fell back, through a doorway, and found himself bouncing down a steel ramp of some kind. A sudden roar of a crowd brought instant vertigo to his senses, and MUD looked around wildly. He was in the arena, out on the ramp. Coming through the entrance way was Neo, still smiling and now removing his shirt. This was going to be a fight, and MUD's jaw tightened with determination. Well then, he thought, I guess we just settle this the hard way. fWo Crossover Material March 18, 2001 MUD vs Neo MUD drug himself up to his feet, rubbing his jaw where Neo had sucker punched him. Neo was walking toward him, and MUD retreated backward to get his bearings on the situation before wading into a fight. He was backing toward the ring, and the roar of the crowd was still dizzying to his senses. Neo, on the other hand, was smiling wider than ever. "Thought you could rabbit, did you sucker? Thought you could just drag your little red haired friend from the outside to the CWL and come jump me did you? Then you would just run off to safer ground. Huh... safer ground I already had my way with." MUD dropped to his hands and knees and shot out a boot, catching Neo hard in the knee. Neo squawked with surprise and pain and fell backward, landing with a thud on the steel. MUD spun to his feet, grabbed Neo by the neck and quickly carted him toward the ring. "Idiot... way I heard it they... fired your... ass." On his last word MUD shoved Neo into the ring and dove in behind him. Walking quickly over to the outside he shouted down at the timekeeper. "Ring that bell... this little feud has gone on long enough." More out of surprise than anything else the timekeeper obliged, the sound of the bell echoing through the arena. The crowd was still roaring with approval, mainly just surprised at the sudden arrival of two outsiders. Well, one outsider anyway... one sort of... well, MUD wasn't sure exactly. But the moment of thought cost him as Neo grabbed his ankles and pulled his legs out from under him. MUD landed backward on his back and the air rushed out of him with a thud. Neo stood, then dropped an elbow into MUD's throat. MUD gagged as Neo leaned in and grinned. "Wanted to fight boy? Biggest mistake you done ever made." No referee had arrived to call the match, but this didn't seem to deter either man. Neo had pulled MUD to his feet and tossed him into the ropes with an Irish whip. MUD came off with a bounce and met the sole of Neo's boot in his face. Before MUD could fall backward, Neo grabbed his hair and powered him down to the mat with a face slam. With MUD still facing the mat Neo slammed his head again and again into the canvas, then seemed to recognize the fact that no referee was here to observe the match. Neo quickly removed his CWL shirt and wrapped it around MUD's neck, pulling back sharply to choke out his enemy. MUD struggled, but his face was already starting to paint itself a dark crimson. He grabbed the ropes, but with no referee to insist that Neo stop it was meaningless. MUD's eyes bulged, then narrowed with a sudden idea. He slumped forward, letting his body go slack. Neo's eyebrows raised, then he stood and grinned. He pulled the shirt out from around MUD's neck and tossed it to the outside. "Huh. Pathet..." Neo's words were cut off by MUD, who was wide awake and only feigning his submission, hitting a mule kick into Neo's gut. Neo doubled over in pain, and MUD quickly stood and grasped his waist with both hands. He flipped him back and over with a body to back suplex, Neo crashing into the mat and MUD rolling free. MUD rubbed his swollen neck and glared down at the temporarily prone body of his enemy. Walking slowly to the corner turnbuckle, MUD started to climb. On the first rope he seemed to have another idea and hopped down to the arena floor instead. Shoving a tech manager out of his seat he tossed the steel folding chair into the ring and quickly followed in behind it. Neo was starting to get to his feet now, but his eyes were distant and hazy. MUD grabbed the chair and swung it over his head, bending the steel of the seat over Neo's thick skull. Neo tottered for a moment, then fell backward and handed on his back. MUD tossed the chair to one side and walked around the ring, looking out for a referee. None was coming and MUD supposed this was not a surprise. This wasn't a sanctioned fight by any means, and he supposed that the only reason it was being allowed to take place is because the fWo didn't want the negative publicity of ending a match that the crowd seemed to be enjoying. That, or they enjoyed having one of their ex-members beaten to a pulp. MUD looked down at Neo who still appeared out cold and shrugged. Then he headed for the edge of the ring and was just sliding under the ropes when he saw a man in a referee's shirt emerge from the entrance way. All eyes of the crowd looked up at the new arrival, and cheered when they recognized who it was. Little Italy was running down the ramp, a referee's shirt worn across his chest. He slid into the ring, and hiked a thumb toward Neo. "Just in the back talking to some people and getting some tips on how to handle the Frenchman I've been having problems with. Figured I'd come out here and make this one official. Now get over there... pin this guy and let's go." MUD looked back at Neo, then eyed Little Italy with suspicion. All the same, he had a referee's shirt and MUD wasn't going to complain. MUD walked back over to Neo and covered, and Little Italy quickly dropped for the count. His hand fell twice, but on the third count Neo shot a shoulder up, then raked MUD in the eyes. MUD stumbled back as Neo got to his feet and sneered at the referee. "Jesus H Christ on a popsicle stick... the refs here have shrunk and look stupider." Little Italy glared at Neo as he walked back over to MUD and slipped on a headlock. Neo was using the hold to choke MUD out, and while Little Italy eyed it with suspicion he had all but committed himself to calling this as a hardcore style bout, given the fact that both men had already cheated the rules throughout. MUD was struggling however, and Neo strained to keep on the hold. But it was obvious that MUD was going to break free, so Neo quickly loosened the hold and dropped into an inverted DDT. MUD fell flat, and lay still. Neo stood, laughed, and shook his head in disgust. Not going for the pin, Neo went over to the chair that was still laying in the ring and brought it to MUD. He placed it over MUD's leg, then went to the corner and began to climb. Little Italy struggled with what he should do, but felt determined to simply call it down the line. Neo was on top of the corner post now, and raised his arms to either side. He had an evil grin of triumph on his face, and raised his head to the rafters and let out a laugh devoid of all good humor. He leapt from the top rope with a full body splash, aiming for MUD's legs and the chair. MUD saw Neo coming and acted quickly, thrusting his legs up and kicking the chair off himself and into the air. It hurled toward Neo, whose face in a spilt second transformed from a smile of triumph to horror. The chair struck him in the face as he hurtled toward the ground, and the thud of steel on skin echoed throughout the entire arena. The crowd in unison groaned in pain as the chair and Neo's flight path changed at once and they both dropped straight down to the canvas. The chair landed first and then Neo on top of it which brought out a fresh cry of pain as he crumpled. MUD wasted no time, he rolled Neo over, hooked the leg and made the cover. Little Italy dove down and quickly made the three count and signaled for the bell. It rang, and MUD rolled off victorious. Little Italy patted him on the back, but MUD looked vaguely annoyed at the gesture and shrugged away. He raised his fists to the crowd, then exited the ring. Little Italy looked down at Neo, and shook his head. "Shouldn't have called me stupid... schmuck." Little Italy walked forward, over Neo, and exited the ring. Already fWo ring crew was headed to the ring to drag Neo out. The crowd cheered, now even more anxious to see what would come next.
