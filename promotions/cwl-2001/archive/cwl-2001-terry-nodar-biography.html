Terry Nodar CWL Character Biographies Terry Nodar Also known as: Rudo Born: May 29, 1973 Hometown: Victoria B.C., Canada Height: 5' 8" Weight: 216 lbs Blood type: A Positive Eye color: Brown Trainer: Stu Hart Theme song: Dizz-Knee-Land, by Dada Wrestling Information Wrestling style: Technical Signature spots: Back Suplexes Swinging Neckbreaker DDT Finisher: Frog Splash Finisher name: Toad Splash Historical Information Other/Previous promotions: fWo, NJPW Other/Previous teams: Lone Gunmen Other/Previous managers: Penny Non-CWL titles won: fWo World Tag Team Championship (2), fWo Crusierweight Championship Biography The man known as Rudo Insano began his career by training in the Dungeon of Stu Hart, but quickly grew uninterested by the lackluster style and antique methods of the Hart training. Traveling to Japan, Korea, and Bangkok, Terry adopted his new name after a death-defying leap from a top level balcony. This style, while dangerous to his health and safety, ensured work for the young wrestler. When he traveled back to the states Rudo was hired by the small startup wrestling promotion the FWO, and wrestled there for several years. During his stint he befriended Machina, and the two had a long and partially successful tag team career. As part of the Lone Gunmen, Rudo's popularity was moderate, but his enjoyment of the sport was high. In March of 2000 after a disasterous accident in the sea, Rudo suffered a great personal blow when Machina appeared to have perished. He attempted to continue his wrestling career for a few more months, but ultimately left the fWo in search of a different lifestyle. But leaving the wrestling industry was not meant to be for Rudo. Rudo was contacted by representatives for Mr. Tort and was asked to work as the chief offical for the CWL wrestling organization. Reluctantly he agreed, not knowing what lay in store for him...
