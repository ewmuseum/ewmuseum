Poison Ivy: Rebuttal

24 April 2001

What this is A column from the fan area of the CWL's own site, posted under Poison Ivy on 24 April 2001 and written in the league's story, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 14 July 2001.
Rebuttal Posted by Poison Ivy April 24, 2001 11:00:12 U.S. CST Been a while, hasn't it? I've missed doing this. Maybe it was the fact that I was getting used to working for two different promotions. Maybe it was the reality setting in that we buried my father a little more than a month ago. But whatever the reason, I completely lost interest in writing this for the CWL. Thanks to Trevor Bryce, I've got it back again. For those of you who are unaware, Mr. Bryce hosts CWL Live, a new webcast program that gives an audio update into the CWL every week or so. He's not a wrestling fan. He's not even someone who will simply report what's happening, like he's being paid to, and move on his business. What was the quote, again? I remember. "Wrestling is not a sport. It is not even dignified entertainment. I've agreed to do this show because I'm being paid to do it but if those clowns in the CWL believe I'm going to softball my feelings on this sick entertainment they have another guess coming." Were those cheapshots really necessary? I understand, Mr. Bryce, that you're a fairly limited person with limited talent and ability to do such a simple job as read things from a piece of paper that someone else wrote for you, but does this give you a right to disrespect our very livelihoods? And if you're going to do so.... the least you could do is be a little bit more fucking creative about it.
