What this is The CWL's own page on its chapter and match archives, presented by Fallen, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 22 April 2001.
CWL wrestler Fallen explains the purpose of archives. What are archives anyway? As you might have noticed by now, a big part of the CWL is the history of the promotion. The history of the CWL is key to the future, and with that idea in mind it made sense to have a lot of archived material. In the CWL archives take on many forms. They are chapters, they are matches, and they are many other things all designed to give readers the opportunity to involve themselves deeply in the story. The biggest and most significant archive in the CWL is of course the chapter archives. The chapter archives are mainly for the benefit of newer fans of the CWL, people who came along later and didn't see the way things began. For these people it is easy to get up to speed, because everything has been saved for you. No chapters have been discarded, nor will anything ever be discarded in the CWL. It's always going to be here for you to read. Other key archives in the CWL include the match archive, which allows you to get a quick list of every match a wrestler has been in. You can also look up worker records, interview records, win loss records... tons of archives. It's all history, and it's all available for you to read whenever you need it.
