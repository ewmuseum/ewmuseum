Help: Why Banner Ads?

as captured 22 April 2001

What this is The CWL's own page on the banner advertising it carried, written in the voice of its owner in the story, Mr. Tort, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 22 April 2001.
CWL owner Mr. Tort explains his views on banner advertising. Why Banner Ads? I hate banner advertising. They are annoying, they sell products I have no intention of ever... EVER... buying, and they slow down the page. Most people in eWrestling feel the same way; they hate banner ads. Most feds don't have them. So why does the CWL? The decision to put ads on the site was not one I made easily. Over the course of several months of getting the CWL ready and together I went back and forth on the issue. In the end one thing settled the issue; I thought that I could possibly do some good with the traffic that this site would generate. I've had a lot of success with my life, and giving something back to the world around me seemed like something I needed to do. Therefore, I've decided to commit all funds generated from the CWL will go to The Boys Hope Girls Hope foundation. The Boys Hope Girls Hope foundation provides support to children in order to help them in preparing for a college education. From their own page, "The program provides at-risk, yet academically capable, children across the country with a stable home, positive parenting, high quality education, and support needed to reach their full potential." What does all that mean? It means that kids with strong potential for success but without parents or living in destructive homes can have the same opportunities at success. I've chosen the Boys Hope Girls Hope for a few reasons. First, I've seen it in action. One of my closest friends was helped with the Boys Hope Girls Hope organization... and it worked for him. Second, the organization is non-denominational. Unlike many charities, there is no religious restrictions or agenda pushed on the young. Third, Boys Hope Girls Hope is funded exclusively through private contributions. It doesn't take funds from the Government, nor is it relying on the questionable United Way organization. All funds given to Boys Hope Girls Hope are used by the organization... there isn't any kickbacks or skimming off the top. Finally, Boys Hope Girls Hope has proven success. 100% of their high school graduates go on to college. The vast majority maintains a B average or better. With our education system in shambles, I believe the work they are doing is valuable and critical. Since you are providing the traffic to this site, I will publish the money amount being donated to Boys Hope Girls Hope every month in the Fans area. Check there for the latest report on how your traffic is impacting this charity. All revenue will be disclosed there for you. If you want to see more information about the charity the CWL is supporting, you can click here for more info: http://www.boyshopegirlshope.org.
