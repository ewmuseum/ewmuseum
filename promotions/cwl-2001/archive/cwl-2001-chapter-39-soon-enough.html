What this is Chapter 39 of the CWL's second book, posted under Andy Knight on 14 April 2001, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 23 April 2001. The league called its role-plays chapters and numbered them with its shows.
Soon Enough Chapter 39 Book 2 CWL Roster Chapters Andy Knight 11:59:57 U.S. CST April 14, 2001 "Just sign on the line, Andy." Andy Knight pressed the paper on his knee then wrote his barely legible signature on the dotted line. The two were travelling in a 1960 cherry red convertible. Wind blowing in Penny's and... well... Penny's hair at least. "Ok Andy, we picked up your clothes from the hotel you were staying in, now is there anything else we need to do before heading back to San Diego?" Andy looked in the back seat and began rummaging threw his suitcase. "It seems we have just about everything. But wait, what am I going to wrestle in?" Penny turned to Andy with that witty looking grin again. Andy knew she had something up her sleeve. "Look in the glove compartment." Andy twisted his head toward the glove box and gradually crooked the knob, unsure of the contents within. The door popped down and out fell a pair of black wrestling tights. Andy beamed as he held them up high. The tights even had his signature 'yellow star' on the backside. "Wow, I can't believe this is actually happening to me. I mean, a day ago I was nothing more the 'Andy the Drive through guy'." Andy smiled. "Thank you, Penny." She grinned back and told Andy to take out a package of papers from the side section of the car. "Andy, read those agenda's. They explain what your physical will be like before you get back in the ring. It's just a standard training thing." Andy flipped through a few pages then replyed to Penny. "One question, Penny. What’s my gimmick going to be? I hope not another one of those Viking individuals." Penny handed Andy back his contract he had just signed. "In here it states you, Andy Knight, have full creative control of your character." This time, an even bigger smile crossed Andy's face. "I still can't believe this is happening." "Well Andy, believe it!" Andy then sat up and lifted his arms high in the air. He then proceeded to bark various comments about his return to pro wrestling into the smoggy air. Yes, they had just entered Los Angeles. "Soon enough San Diego… Soon enough.”
