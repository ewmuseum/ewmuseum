I wish. Chapter 103 Book 2 CWL Roster Chapters Devastation Inc. 15:42:21 U.S. CST May 03, 2001 In the memory of Ed Wilson, a man with a passion to entertain, and what a entertainer he was. Fade up. [v/o]"You don't get to choose how you're going to die or when. You can only decide how you're going decide how your going to live." The sun beam down over the black limo, as he cruised along what only can be called the west coast. The mood was somber, and quiet. No a word was said up until this point, nothing at all. But only because there was nothing to say. Little Italy sat, his head resting on his right hand which rested on the door of the black limo, staring out the tinted window. He slowly blinked, and for a split second, he could almost visualize him in his mind. Ed. He let out a deep sigh, as looked over the edge of the cliffs which lined the road, located on the coast of California. Across from his sat Leprechaun, his head resting in his hands, a blank look etched across his face, his hair in his eye's. The door of the limo opened, and out stepped Little Italy, followed by his atg team partner, The Leprechaun. The two slowly walked to the railing, placing there hands long it. Italy looked out over the ocean, and he felt helpless, as a tear ran over the front of his mask, and crashed into the ground below. He turned to the Leprechaun, who stood, much like him, and spoke. "Do you think he's in a better place." The wind picked up, and blew Leprechaun's hair in all directions. He turned to Italy, and slowly responded, a hint of pain could be heard in his voice. 'If there is such a place, he's there, Luigi .... he's there." The two stood, and shared a moment of silence in Ed's honor, and the scene soon faded.
