Thursday Night Titans, 3 May 2001 (unfinished)

3 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The Thursday Night Titans of 3 May 2001 as the CWL's site carried it after Ed Wilson's death: the pieces found in a working file, posted with a note from the league's artist explaining that the show was in production before his accident and that the order of its segments is uncertain. As the Wayback Machine captured it on 19 July 2001.
Note: The following show was in production before Ed's accident. These pieces were included in a temporary text file. Because I did the art for the CWL I know that several new characters were scheduled to debut. Griff Truxell, Kenny Rock, Natural Born Killas, Overdose and Pete Borst were the characters I was told I needed to have art done for by Thursday. The segments for the show have been left in the order they were in when the file was found, with one piece added late from Griff that he had written beforehand. The order of the show may not be accurate but there is no way to know for certain. The Neo/OVerdose match also looked incomplete but it is here as it was left. One other segment that I was sent to review involved the Machina character. But reading over it there was a comment about the Forth of July which led me to believe that it was not scheduled to go in until a later time. Thank you. Previously... The mistakes of the past haunt our present. Violence erupted last week on CWL Titans as an enraged and ever more angry crowd exploded in a full scale riot. What was an angle set in motion years ago by the former CWL writers finally came to a bloody conclusion as fans attacked wrestlers and wrestlers gave in to temptation and fought back with devastating results. Now, one week later finds much of the CWL roster incarcerated… trapped behind steel bars. With a last minute change of venue for the show to take the CWL blissfully out of California, nothing is certain and the CWL faces a night of untried wrestlers, new faces… and deadly surprises. • Terry Nodar walked into the arena and looked around, the familiar smell coming back to him in a wave. It was strange and magical at the same time… but it filled him with a pang of uncertainty about what he was here to do. Terry looked behind him and considered just leaving… walking out the door and being done with it. Then a voice came from behind him and his jaw tightened. "Well, well… look who pulled himself out of the gutter and decided to come back." Terry turned to face Culligan. Culligan was smirking, hands on hips. Terry hadn't faced him since that night weeks ago when Culligan was put in charge. Now, the old feelings came back in a wave. Hopelessness… disgust. "Danny." Culligan waited for him to say more, and frowned when it became obvious that Terry wasn't going to. Sniffing in distain, Culligan paced back and forth before him. "I should fire you. You didn't show up for work… all over your… well, your demotion. If you can't understand how the business world works Terry, then perhaps you shouldn't be here. We all have a chain of command, and I am in charge now. That means you obey me. That means you show up to work and you do what I ask of you. Is that clear?" Terry nodded. Culligan smiled widely. "Then get to work. Now." Culligan turned on his heel and walked away, an expression of superiority on his face. Terry watched him leave with a thin smile. He pulled Flissner's business card from his pocket and looked at it. He had made a deal with the devil… but if anyone deserved to be on the receiving end of things it was Culligan. May 3, 2001 Broadcasting from Tacoma, Washington Thursdays at 8/7 p.m. CT on the WB Check local times and listings for details • Griff Truxell looked around blankly from his seated position, attempting to fight off the ever present boredom. For the second week in a row he was backstage and for the second week in a row his wrestling gear laid untouched. It was still early in the show, early enough for a obvious mistake to be fixed. Yet, as the minutes pressed on it became apparent that the mistake was to be left a gaping hole in the talent pool, and that Greatness would once again not be respected. Griff leaned his head back and rubbed his temples, just thinking of such things gave him a headache. As his hand withdrew from his face, a glimpse flashed across his eyes. A glimpse long enough for Griff to recognize who it was. Kenny Rock. Griff rose quickly and headed in the direction he had seen the man go. Griff followed, heading through shutting doors and weaving his way through people, always a second behind. Finally, he pushed through a solid oak door, and stopped, he had no where else to go. Daniel Culligan sat comfortably behind his desk. "Welcome gentlemen, I'm glad you could make it. Please, take a seat," Culligan spoke with an air of sarcasm. Griff mouthed the word "Gentlemen?" before he looked to his left. Griff's mouth snapped shut as he glared at the face of Kenny Rock, sitting almost as smugly as Culligan was. Griff looked back up to Culligan and then took a seat, not even glancing a second time at Kenny. The three sat there in an erie moment of silence, before Culligan spoke up again. "As you know, some of our roster is out due to...well...legal constraints. Now I, being Commissioner, have a few changes to make in the show..." "And?" Griff cut him off sharply. Culligan's lipped trembled as he tried to put out the anger that was quickly boiling over. Suddenly, he slammed both hands on the desk. "Listen to me, and understand this well... I don't care who you are, or what you do, tonight, you're facing the Masimata Brothers. Both of you, together. I don't care whether you win, or you lose, or whatever. Just go out there, and do your job. Understood?" Griff's jaw would have dropped if he wasn't clenching his teeth so hard. His eyes shot to Kenny, who shook his head and mumbled under his breath. "No way, no way in hell." Griff's eyes shot back to Culligan, who glared right back. The two locked eyes before Griff sank back in his chair a bit and sucked his teeth. "Fine," Griff shot another glance at Kenny. Culligan took his hands off of the desk. "Good. Get away from me." Overdose vs Neo Music spiraled over the rafters as Overdose pushed the curtains to the side and walked out onto the entrance ramp. He raised his arms for the crowd, pumped a fist, then strode to the ring, weaving in and out of camera men as he came. Overdose slowly broke into a trot, then a full run as he hit the ring and slid under the ropes to the center of the ring. Overdose rose, ripped free his shirt, and swiveled on one leg to face the ramp. Arms outstretched and palms toward the rafters, Overdose motioned his opponent to bring it on. The audience rose to their feet, murmuring restlessly as the match was about to proceed. Loud discordant music washed over the crowd as Neo came out onto the ramp, stopped, lifted both fingers in the air and began to scream obscenities to the fans in attendance. As the fans in the arena shouted Neo shouted back, louder and louder until little could be heard over the noise of the fans. Neo approached one fan near the barrier and leaned into the crowd to shout, encouraging the man to take a swing at him. Annoyed with this, a frustrated Overdose slid from the ring and headed up the ramp toward Neo. Neo saw him coming and broke out into a run while still cursing, an arm outstretched for a clothesline. Overdose ducked, came up from behind and punched with a right hand into a disoriented Neo. Overdose punched again and again, backing Neo up to the ring apron and then into the ring. The bell rang as Overdose hurried in after him, but Neo was already on his feet. Neo struck with a knife edge chop that toppled Overdose back into the ropes. Neo went for a second but Overdose ducked and hit a standing lariat on Neo as he turned. Overdose backed up, measured, picked Neo up and hit a swinging neck breaker into a hot shot. Neo wobbled and Overdose struck with a quick standing super kick. Neo fell back but Overdose reached over and grabbed him by the hair, steadying him. Overdose struck with another knife edge chop, then a third that had Neo backed into the corner. Overdose grabbed him by the waist and lifted him up again, bringing him down hard groin first into the top turnbuckle. Neo clutched at his groin as Overdose continued the offensive. Grabbing Overdose by the arm he whipped him into the opposite corner. Neo crashed into the corner as Overdose charged forward, but caught a boot to the face from Neo who had lifted his leg at the last second. Overdose staggered back as Neo moved forward, grabbed Overdose by the waist and dropped him down to the canvas in a half power slam. Overdose lying prone on the mat, Neo ripped off his shirt and posed for the crowd who gave him a lukewarm reaction at best. Grumbling Neo went for a cover, but before the referee had gotten in position Overdose powered out and shoved Neo to one side. Overdose and Neo got to their feet, Overdose ducking a running clothesline and grabbing Neo's neck from behind. Overdose dropped with a reverse jawbreaker and Neo rolled to the outside for safety. Overdose ran to the opposite end of the ring as Neo stood with his hands on his knees trying to clear the cobwebs and focus on the match. Neo looked back up toward the ring just in time to see Overdose sailing from between the second and third ropes, catching Neo hard with the tope. Overdose stood and shoved Neo up against the ring apron, then snapped Neo down with a DDT that caused the back of Neo's head to collide hard with the edge of the ring. Neo crumpled down and Overdose shoved him up and rolled him back into the ring. Before Overdose could enter the ring himself a female fan at ringside jumped the barrier and grabbed Overdose's back. Overdose turned to strike the person who had interfered, then saw it was a young woman and scowled in disgust. He recognized her as the girl that had helped Neo weeks ago at Revolution, and hiked a thumb up toward the ring at Neo. "What are you doing helping him? He's filth... a worthless pile of..." The woman slapped him at his words, cutting him off in mid sentence. Overdose's eyes flashed dangerously, and he grabbed her head and pulled her closer to him. He was about to strike when he noticed the bruised eye and fearful look in her eyes... a fearful look that didn't quite disguise an odd expression of pleasure. Overdose released her and spat, disgusted with what he saw. He shook his head and turned from the woman, turning his attention back toward the ring. Neo stumbled to his feet and kicked down at Overdose as he slid into the ring, stomping his head and hands. Walking shakily, Neo made his way to the turnbuckle and began to climb, going to the top rope and then turning to face Overdose. Overdose was getting to his feet and had a distracted look on his face, but as Neo launched himself off the top rope Overdose snapped into action and quickly stepped forward and caught Neo in midair, then twisted him over and hit a hard Tiger driver that planted Neo on the mat. Neo bounced hard, and Overdose quickly covered and hooked the leg. The referee made the count easily, and Overdose slid off with the quick and decisive victory. Basking in the cheers from the crowd, Overdose made his way swiftly back to the back with only the slightest parting glance at the woman at ringside. She looked timid and scared, then glanced up reproachfully at Neo inside the ring. Neo had rose to his knees, and glared at Overdose as he left. Then he glared down at the woman and his scowl deepened. He slid from the ring and stood looking at her for a moment, then grabbed her hair and yanked her up the ramp behind him. She howled in pain, but obliged him as she trailed along behind... Winner: Overdose by Pinfall • Over 1 million people watch CWL Titans each week. Over 1 million wrestling fans. But for every wrestling fan, there's a wrestling hater. They all have their reasons, whether it's being a concerned parent, or someone that thinks the idea of a soap opera with violence is stupid, but no-one does anything apart from bad mouth the sport. Sure, there are people in the crowd thinking they're hurting CWL with signs saying they'd rather be at Sunday Slaughter, and Tort sucks McMahon's… ahem. No-one takes any offence to those signs, they laugh. But to one man, the entire wrestling industry is no joke. There's a sudden gathering of fans crowding into a small area, and a lot of noise filling the arena. The crowd of people seem to be moving towards the ringside area, and eventually a figure climbs over the security railing, with a baseball bat in hand. Pete Borst. The arena is filled with noise, no cheering, no booing, just confused chatter. There's a sudden loud bang as Borst hits the steel steps with the baseball bat as hard as he can, denting them and knocking them over. The crowd are suddenly silent and Pete climbs up onto the ring apron and pauses for a moment, taking a deep breath before stepping between the ropes and into the ring. He throws down his baseball bat, or as he prefers to call it, his shiteball stick and he wanders over to the corner and asks the ring announcer for a mic. The announcer doesn't know what to do, and to avoid any trouble just hands Borst a mic. "This is like being a kid again. I always wanted to go the fucking circus." Pete keeps a hold of the mic and wanders towards the turnbuckles in one corner of the ring and begins to pull at the top pad viciously, ripping it clean off and pulling it to pieces, leaving foam all over the mat in the corner, much like you'd see after George 'the Animal' Steele would tear the pads to pieces with his teeth. "Foam. To protect their tender little heads." says Pete as he climbs out of the ring. He wanders around the ring for a while and then throws the mic into the ring, before tearing down the ring apron on each side of the ring. He wanders over to the announce tables and winks at the announcers before stamping hard on the table, causing it to collapse. Pete grabs the monitors and throws them on the floor hard before knocking over the other set of steel steps and climbing into the ring again. Pete has a big grin on his face as wanders over to the turnbuckle with no pad, and begins to pull hard at the bolt and rope, eventually managing to pull the top rope off of the ring post. The fans are booing loudly by now, as Pete paces around the ring looking at the mess. The ring aprons are pulled off, the steel steps dented and turned over, announce table broken, monitors smashed and the top rope ripped from one corner of the ring. Pete reaches into his pocket and pulls out a small Swiss Army knife, flicks the Overdose out and drags it about two meters along the mat. He flicks the knife closed and almost throws it into the crowd before stopping and shaking his head and muttering, "Yanks and knives, bad combo." Boos echo throughout the arena. Pete Borst has invaded a CWL show and completely destroyed the set. Well almost destroyed. Pete looks over to the video wall at the top of the aisle, seeing himself in the near destroyed ring. There's a slight gleam in his eye, and he smiles. For the first time in years, Borst is showing some emotion. He seems happy. Even more so when the fans begin to throw litter in ring. "And you wonder why Yankland is such a shit hole... although this contraption could be mistaken for a dustbin quite easily. The amount of rubbish that passes through it night in night out..." Pete grabs his shiteball stick and climbs out of the ring, catching his leg on the drooping top rope and almost falling out of the ring, much to the delight of the CWL fans. Pete just gives them the two finger salute and an evil glare before marching up the aisle. He stands about five meters from the video wall and takes a step back, getting a good run up. Suddenly several CWL official flood out of the entrance way and surround Borst. Pete spits at one of them and pushes another out of the way, before throwing the metal shiteball stick at the video wall. There are some large sparks followed the video wall going black. Moments later security are on the scene and they surround Pete Borst, cuffing him and dragging him away. As they take Borst away, there's still that slight gleam in Pete's eye, and a slight smile. It seems that finally one of the millions of wrestling haters decided to do more than verbally insult the business. Pete Borst has near ruined the entire set of the top promotion in the business, and by the looks of things, finally got rid of some of his pent up aggression that's been building for the past five years. Although Pete's not an ordinary wrestling hater. In his eyes, he has more than enough reasons to hate pro-wrestling. He came, he saw, he destroyed... and chances are he'll never be allowed near another wrestling show again in his life. Although judging by the look on his face, it wouldn't bother him one bit. Drug up and away, Pete leaned in toward the a nearby cameraman, his face inches away from the lens. There is still that faint smile of satisfaction on his face, then he rams his head forward and the camera shatters with a loud pop of feedback and electricity. At home millions of viewers watched the CWL turn to static… then explode to darkness. Credits Previously Ed Terry Returns Ed Neo vs Overdose Ed Borst Steve Griff Griff
