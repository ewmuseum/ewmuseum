Kaid Mann CWL Character Biographies Kaid Mann "Asshole" Also known as: Bongo The Clown Born: 12th of June 1980 Hometown: Melbourne, Australia Height: 6'2 Weight: 211 lbs Blood type: O Positive Eye color: Green Theme song: 'Rip it up' by 28 days Wrestling Information Wrestling style: Hardcore Brawler / High Flyer Signature spots: Punch to Stomache followed by Knee to face Slap to face, AKA 'Bitch-slap' Impact DDT Running Bulldog Frequent Bleeding Extremely Bad Language Alcohol Abuse Finisher: Top Rope Brain Buster Finisher name: Mad Hangover Historical Information Other/Previous promotions: Tornado Wrestling Non-CWL titles won: TW Cruiserweight Title ( X2 ) Non-CWL awards won: TW - First Era 'Best Match' ( vs. Tyke ) Biography From going up in Australia as a young boy, Kaid always wanted to be a pro wrestler. He used to watch the exploits of 'Hulk' Hogan and the like on TV every weekend when it was televised in Australia, and knew that he wanted to become a wrestling star some day. His first official entry into the world of wrestling was when he started a federation at School, of which consisted of himself and a few friends doing moves on each other and most of the time doing it wrong and hurting themselves. The other children would all crowd around to watch, and Kaid loved every single minute of the attention. The fights were usually short, but they always entertained. However, it was after a few weeks of doing this schoolyard wrestling that Kaid became bored. After hearing about something new in America called ECW, he decided to try and make these schoolyard matches a little more entertaining. However when he jumped from the roof of one of the classrooms and gave his friend a missile drop kick (which happen to break the poor kid’s arm), the school was quick to act, putting an end to the lunchtime schoolyard wrestling. Finding himself wanting to do more of the kind of crazy things he had just started doing at school, he quickly found a very small Australian wrestling school and began to train on the weekends. However being a young male in Australia, he began to start drinking alcohol, which could have either been a bad thing or the best thing that has ever happened to him. After a huge drinking session one night Kaid turned up to his training ‘a bit under the weather’ so to speak. In other words, he was still blind drunk. As you would imagine, his trainer wasn't very pleased, And to prove a point he made Kaid jump through a table from the top rope. Kaid tried it, smashed the table, and got straight up. He was then made to pay $70 for the table, and began to learn some hardcore wrestling techniques, using alcohol a lot more to ease the pain, and because being drunk is more fun than being sober. Soon, he was ready for his first ever fight. It was to be a Hardcore match for a small Australian independent federation called AXW, and his opponent was to be the local hero called 'The Extreme Dream' Chucky. The fight itself was amazing from a extremist point of view. Highlights included Chucky wrapping barb-wire around his midsection and doing a standing Moon Sault onto Kaid (Who at the time had adopted the gimmick of a crazy clown called Bongo the freak to try and get over with the rough Australian crowds). Kaid being hip-tossed through a glass coffee table. And finally Kaid was put into Chucky's extreme finisher, which was a 450 Centon (like a shooting star press with a little bit extra ‘flip’) trough a table. After the match, Kaid was ravaged with cuts, bruises, concussion and probably some serious internal damage, but the crowd were amazed for the entire match, even though it was completely one sided and Kaid was almost broken in two. The crowd stood and clapped for both of the young men, who had just poured their heart and soul into entertaining them. But did Kaid care? Hell no. He wanted his damn money. He’s busted his ass in this show and he wanted what was coming to him. And what did he get? $100. Was he upset about seemingly being ‘Ripped off’? Damn straight he was. From that point on he decided to find himself a real Wrestling Promotion and set sail to America, the so-called ‘Land of opportunity’ or something. No more of this busting ass for nothing, He was going to get paid, and get paid by playing the game his way, and by his rules. After touring a few independent federations in America, Kaid amassed a fair amount of money, and through some stroke of luck became an American Citizen. It was time to hit the big leagues he thought, and marched into none other than Vince McMahon’s office and demanded the WWF hire him. He was promptly removed by Security. But things were eventually going to go his way, when he was offered an extremely lucrative deal with a new Promotion call ‘Tornado Wrestling’. Kaid was quick to make a name for himself, upsetting some of the top talent and causing general mischief. The First Televised event showed Kaid in his truest form, Driving to the arena blind drunk, a cheap looking woman by his side, a bottle of Jim Beam in his hand, and one hell of a nasty attitude. He promptly had a small run in with Shawn Simon that night, and from the start you could tell that the young Kaid Mann, was trouble. Soon he was to be in the middle of one of the most controversial events to take place in TW. He single handily masterminded the break up of Tyke and his wife, and then when he couldn’t keep his hands off of her and she ran back to Tyke, Kaid Kidnapped their son Robbie. Somehow Kaid managed to adopt Robby and make him his own son, and demanded that in a final match, Tyke would pit his cruiserweight title against the adoption papers for his beloved son. Although Kaid lost the match, he was still regarded as the most hated man in the federation. Kaid enjoyed some success in Tornado Wrestling’s first era before it was closed, and fans of the federation sure enjoyed some of his exploits. So instead of waiting for his next meal ticket to come along, Kaid jumped ship into a small tournament known as the ‘Bitch with Mitch Invitational Tournament’. Admittedly, Kaid wasn’t in his best form during the Tournament, and in the second round he was eliminated, as well as injuring one of his ankles. It was a while before Kaid was seen in the wrestling world again, and some had thought that he might have left it completely. But this was not the case, and Kaid signed a contract to compete in the re-opening CWL. Opun coming into the CWL, Kaid had some vicious hardcore matches, some that might be considered the most brutal of CWL's new era. Kaid was on a roll. However life works in funny ways, and as Kaid was trying to make a name for himself in his new federation, Kaid's best friend Rove, was caught in a downward spiral, and he eventually ended his own life. This left Kaid a broken man, a shattered mirror of his former self. Until finally Kaid snapped himself out of it, and has set on his goals into a new direction. Kaid wants the fans to respect him. He wants the young children to look up to him. He wants to be the hero...
