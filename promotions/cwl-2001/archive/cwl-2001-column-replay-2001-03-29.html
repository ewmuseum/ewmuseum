CWL RePlay: March to Revolution

29 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A column from the fan area of the CWL's own site, posted under Eugene Marder on 29 March 2001 and written in the league's story, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 14 July 2001.
March to Revolution Posted by Eugene March 29, 2001 19:26:00 U.S. CST Folks, Eugene Marder here for CWL RePlay, and boy have I got a greatone foryou. We have The Don live, we have a preview of the next Titans, andbestof all, we have some juicy rumors regarding the CWL. But first, let'sget toa recap of last week's thrilling CWL Titans. First, we witnessed an epic battle between Neo and MUD. The fight wentbackand forth and back again, and Neo barely got the pinfall after aswingingneckbreaker. But let me assure you, fans, this fued is far from over.CWLRevolution will present the two of them in a ring again, and undersomewhatmore intense circumstances. Immediatly after, The Don met Leprechaunin arepeat match, but this time the gluttonous Total Eclipse caused Lep toloseby count out after an interesting round of action. And then we came toZeroagainst Havoc, which is a favorite matchup of the fans. And what amatch itwas. The action was excellent and the efforts were brutal, but it camedownto a draw after the two men almost killed each other. All we can donow iswait for Revolution to see this one again. What followed that amazingboutwas a match of epic, but unequal, proportions when Little Italy took onTheFlying Frenchie, his nemesis. From the beginning, foul play wasevidentwhen the referee's glasses were switched to ones that impaired hisvision-by TFF, of course. What followed was a mockery, with Frenchie pickingup atotally unfair win after a weak bodyslam. Of course, the CWL WorldChampionship Tournament semi-finals took place pitting Vince JacobsagainstXXXstacy. The match was monumental, showcasing talents from bothcompetitors, but Jacobs ultimately picked up a win after thehard-foughtmatch. After that, the audience was shocked when Victoria, wife ofVince,declared her love for XXXstacy and all but divorced her husband. Now,I'llgo straight into a ground-breaking interview with The Don, TonyPatziani. ------------------------------------------------------------------ Eugene Marder: Welcome, Tony. How the devil are you? The Don: Well, it's all goin' pretty nicely, sure my brotha' is deadan' I'min financial trouble, but I feel aight. I got my health I guess... Eugene Marder: Let's talk about that. You are now under investigationforthe murder of your own brother by two law enforcement agencies. I'msure youhave words about that. The Don: I ain't neva' been afraid o' no cops an' I ain't gonna' benow. Iain't got nuttin' to hide ya' see. I may be Da' Don, but dat' don'tmake meno criminal. Plus they ain't made no arrest yet anyway... Eugene Marder: Well, who do you think killed Ricky? The Don: Well, I figa' it was Creed, ya see, he tryin' to throw me offfo'our match. Eugene Marder: Wait, now. KVC may sometimes be a bit overzealous, buthe'snot a killer. The Don: Hey, he's gonna' try an' kill me at da' Pay-Per-View, luckilyI gotmo' experience in da' murderin' depart-... Wait, I mean.... I ain'tkillednobody Eugene Marder: Let's move to a different line of questions, shall we?Manypeople say that your partner, Legz, is carrying much of your weight foryou.What do you have to say to that? The Don: *The Don laughs loudly* If you mean by load, my bags, I guessyouright. Legz is an employee, he jus' helps out his employa' anyway hecan,da' kid deserves a raise. All da' employees acroos da' country shouldbetakin' note o' Legz's work ethic, maybe they wouldn't be stuck in da'deadend jobs dat' they got. Dead...*The Don laughs again* Eugene Marder: *Ahem* I see the shock of your brother's death hasn'tgottento you. The Don: Don't get me wrong, I loved Ricky, but we was neva' realclose. Hestill my brotha' though, an' fo' mamma's sake, I'm sad. I ain't gonna'showit though, o' Vic might prey on dat' weakness. Eugene Marder: Let's talk about Vic. It seems that there's no end totheanimosity- do you see one? The Don: I ain't gonna' stop hatin' him til' my car is back to roadreadycondition. My fenda' is real messed up, I called da' guy workin' on itinChi town an' he said he's havin a lot o' trouble. Damn Creed, he costme alot o' money... Eugene Marder: So the PPV won't be the end? The Don: Let's put it dis' way, da' end will be when Creed is lyin'flat onhis back, blood drippin' from his forehead, paramedics takin' him awayon aova'sized gerny after I kicked his ass an' extracted da' money from hishide. Eugene Marder: Thank you very much. Anything else to say? The Don: Well, all I gots to say is dat' much like da' gangsta's befo'me,Snoop Doggy Dogg, Al Capone, I'm innocent. I might kill somebody else,butRicky was my own blood an' I wouldn't kill him. Also, Vic, you broke myheart. See you, at da' Pay Pa' View... ------------------------------------------------------------------- There have indeed been some interesting rumors surronding CWL. Somehave todo with talent. Danny Culligan, who was fired last week, was seenbackstageand apparently does not want to leave. That could be a problem formanagement and other wrestlers. The Grunge Warrior, also a formerwrestlerin CWL, is not wanting to leave and Terry Nodar would not like a breachofcontract suit following him. Grunge Warrior is signed for a matchtomorrownight. Finally in rumors, Vince McMahon has been reportedly contacting TortIndustries. What about, we do not know, but he may be wanting toactuallybuy the promotion. More on this as it develops. The next Titans should be excellent, as should Revolution, and makesure tocheck out the special Revolution edition of RePlay later on this week! -Eugene
