The Prisoner (part one) Chapter 41 Book 2 CWL Roster Chapters Neo 13:54:50 U.S. CST April 14, 2001 Outside it was mildly sunny. This is to say, the sun was out but accompanied by a good collection of clouds. It hadn't started to rain yet, but it was only a matter of time. The street downtown was busy, with pedestrians passing to and fro on their way to Starbucks, the Gap, Nike Town, or any number of corporate stores. Neo glanced around himself with an expression he believed was a sneer. To people nearby it looked more like fearful confusion, but none said anything about it. Neo was someone who people generally did their best to avoid. He wasn't an overwhelming physical monster, but he was unpredictable and in this business that often seemed far worse. People who did things with little rhyme or reason were troublesome. Neo was one of these, and thus people kept their opinions to themselves as he passed them by. "Yep... everyone here is Neo's bitch." Eyes rolled, but people were careful to do it when they weren't looking at him. Neo was walking with a swagger that seemed to be more expressive than usual. Neo hadn't been on Titans on Monday, and several members of the CWL had hoped that he was going to jump ship again and find another promotion to infect. But toward the end of the show he had called to get the tickets sent to him for the next show the promotion was going to run. Sighing, the bookers had written Neo's name onto the card for the next show, selecting Neo to wrestle in a hardcore style match. Perhaps, they figured, the fool would break his neck. Neo came to a slow stop a few feet away from a Red beetle. What he thought was a sneer faltered for a moment, then formed what could only be described as an uneasy grin. Neo was attempting to be charming, a mood he wasn't very familiar with. He opened the passenger door of the car and bent down to get a good look at the driver. "Hello, Lucy." The girl glanced at Neo, and nodded. She was obviously nervous and her eyes darted down to the deep red welts on her arms. But Neo looked to be in a good mood for the moment... and she could only hope that this would last. Neo slid into the car and rubbed her hair with one dry hand. The gesture was more painful than pleasurable, and it had been many days since the two were linked for them. "Drive." She shook out her thoughts and pulled the car away from the curb, one last fleeting glance at the people walking up and down the sidewalk. Just faces, they were of no aid to her even if she wanted them to be. He was in a good mood for now... but this would change.
