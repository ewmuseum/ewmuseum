Can details 'bout my criminal life be revealed? Nah. Dat' would be shootin' myself in the foot, so to speak. Da' district attorneys would fry my ass befo' my ring talent could be even showcased. I'd be locked up fo' eight lifetimes but to my delight and fo' your pleasure, there are parts o' my life I can speak about wit'out gettin' taken' away in cuffs. I was born in Rome but only lived there fo' a couple o' years. My parents brought me and my sista' here to America, "The Promise Land". Promise land my ass. We lived in a tiny rat invested apartment in Brooklyn. My fatha' worked dead end job after dead end job just to make ends meat. My mother slaved over a stove day an' night tryin' to feed us wit' what she could. The miserable Brooklyn childhood went on until the age of 16 when my savia' in a black suit, Tommy Gun in hand, appeared. Dis' is where my period of criminal prosperity began but can I discuss it here? Nah. I'm sure there are a few fat FBI agents jus' waitin' for me to give away da' secrets of da' worlds most successful and influential criminal syndicate but it's ain't gonna' happen so boys, ya' can put down yo' notpads and donuts... You ain't gettin' shit outta' me. All I can mention about the mafia would be dis'... Da'mafia is a riddle, wrapped in an enigma buried in da' middle o' da' hot dry desert where nobody can find you.... Now movin' on, afta' many years o' mafia activity I became da' head honcho, da' boss... The Don, hence the name. I decided to try my hand at somethin' new. The money was of course rollin' in and I could only use so many cigars so I decided to finance a wrestlin' career. I hired a trainer. I had only one logical choice and that was the greatest wrestla' in tha' history o' da' sport. I called Big Vito immediately and he came down ta' my trainin' center. I got some o' my stooges to get in da' ring and tryout for the spot as the mafia's own wrestlin' superstar. After carefully viewin' all the applicants Vito decided none of em' were any good and told me to lace up the loafa's myself. Yes, loafa's not boots. I showed him my skills and he picked me. I was da' greatest wrestla' we had and only got betta' with time. It proved to me dat' it's true, ya' can't find good help these days. Bein' a big man, I quickly cruised through the unda'ground rankin's until one day, I got my break. I tried out fo' this little fWo Surviva' gig and very not surprisinly, I got in. I was doin' great in the contest. I had friends and all until they, like stinkin' cowards, took knives and stuck em' in my back. Oh well, I'm on to bigga' and betta' things now! When this CWL ting' gets goin' you can expect one man to rise above all else and dat' man, shit do I even ask? It'sa me! Oh yeah and if ya' disagree... You can expect to end up sleepin' wit' da' fishies, capiche? ...Gratzi... Handler Information Handler: Bob S.
