Kodiak Vic Creed CWL Character Biographies Vic Creed "Creedzilla, Alaskan Juggernaut" Also known as: The Main Man, KVC Born: 3/3/71 Hometown: Anchorage, Alaska Height: 6' 6 Weight: 348 lbs Blood type: AB negative Eye color: Blue/Green Manager: Dawn Van Dammage Trainer: Slade Wilson Theme song: 'Walk' by Pantera Wrestling Information Wrestling style: Power, with a capitol P Signature spots: Powerbomb (Creedbomb) Reverse DVD (Alaskan Avalanche) Suplex into a Piledriver (Kodiak Krippler) T-Bone Suplex Northern Lights Release Suplex Gorilla Press (for repetitions) Tiger Suplex Hangman's DDT Torture Rack into a backbreaker (Bodybreaker) Finisher: Military Press into a Powerslam Finisher name: Powerdriver Historical Information Other/Previous promotions: IIWA, SSW, EWA, ICWF, MJPW, WCA, IWA, RAW, XCW, TW, among others Non-CWL titles won: Singles Titles - IIWA Television Champion, SSW World Television Champion, EWA United States Heavyweight Champion, ICWF Universal Champion (2x), ICWF Heavyweight Champion, ICWF World Heavyweight Champion, MJPW Super Champion, WCA Heavyweight Champion, IWA World Title (2x), EWA World Title, RAW World Title, XCW World Title, current fWo Hardcore Champion Non-CWL awards won: Singles - SSW 1998 Master of the Ring Biography Growing up in Anchorage, Vic's father had big plans for his eldest son. Unfortunately, Vic had some ideas of his own. He fell in love with wrestling almost immediately, and even found a wrestling school in Atlanta that he wanted to train at. Upon graduation, Vic found he had only enough money for a one-way ticket to Atlanta. He knew that the House of Pain Wrestling Academy was holding try-outs, and that if wrestlers performed well, payment plans could be worked out. But what if he wasn't accepted? What if he blew the try-out? His doubt plagued him... for about ten seconds. No. He'd make it. He had to. Sure enough, out of the twenty three that started the try-out, only two were selected. Vic was one of them. And with that, Creedzilla started his colorful career. Starting in a tag team known as Bodycount, Vic (using the name Doomsday) tagged with fellow HOP alumni Death Warrior and won the UWA and TAEWF World Tag Titles. Deciding to go his own way, Vic hit the independant scene. After several frustrating months of entering so-called 'greatest feds around', only to have them shut down three weeks later, Creed finally got a shot in a respected fed: the IIWA. Within a few weeks, Vic won the IIWA World Television Title. After that, Vic's momentum built. He picked up the PCWF tag title, and several other singles belts. His critics became more and more impressed with the young powerhouse. His promos were better, and he was rounding out his arsenal of attacks. Hollywood also took an interest in the young man. He began getting bit parts on TV shows, and even received roles in some big-budget movies. Unfortunately, many of these roles were cut out of the final production due to Vic's unwillingness to help the movie's stars 'get over'. But it wasn't enough for the Main Man of Wrestling. He wanted more. Focusing on his wrestling, he pushed his game to a whole new level. The level (he claims) only a select few can reach. Now he's ready to show the entire World why he's called The Main Man! Handler Information Handler: Don Chat handle: [detail withheld]
