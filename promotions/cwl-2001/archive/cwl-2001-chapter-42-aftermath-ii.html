What this is Chapter 42 of the CWL's second book, posted under Ivy and Triple X on 14 April 2001, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 19 April 2001. The league called its role-plays chapters and numbered them with its shows.
Aftermath II Chapter 42 Book 2 CWL Roster Chapters Ivy and Triple X 18:11:24 U.S. CST April 14, 2001 "Honestly, Ivy.... why did you come out there at the end of the match?" "I dunno.... doing the right thing for once, maybe? It's been a while since we were on the same side.... I kinda missed it." Triple X and Poison Ivy are, in an incredible stroke of coincidence, staying on the same floor of the same hotel in San Jose. Eli Flair got off in the elevator two floors down.... it's just the two of them. DRAMATIS PERSONAE: Poison Ivy Triple X Exposition "Yeah.... been a while. Perfect Circle." "I just didn't like the way you were being played with out there. It's not right, y'know. Not the way to do business." "Yeah, not like some of the guys in Superior, right?" "Holy shit.... did you just crack a joke?" They both chuckle, and ride for a few seconds in an awkward silence. "It was good to see you back in a main event program, though. You belong there, despite what a few of the sheets have been saying." "Thanks." "Listen, I--" "I wanted to--" More silence. "You first." "Sorry, you go." They stop talking for another moment, but Ivy deigns to break the silence. "I couldn't help thinking about how close we almost were in Superior.... and how closely mirrored to real life your program with Victoria was. But I did want to apologize for blowing you off the week before Revolution. I wasn't.... exactly myself." "I know. Eli told me a little about what was going on with him. It's cool." The elevator stops, and the doors open. Trip and Ivy exit, and take half a step in opposite directions, before Ivy spins around. "So where's that leave us?" "As.... friends.... I hope?" Ivy seems to ponder this for about half a second, then reaches out and gives Trip a big hug. "I can definitely live with that. See ya later, Sean." Triple X watches Ivy disappear down the hall for a few seconds, then heads toward his own room. They've both got some travelling to do in the morning.
