What this is Chapter 107 of the CWL's second book, posted under M.U.D on 14 May 2001, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 18 July 2001. The league called its role-plays chapters and numbered them with its shows.
Not in the least a conclusion, but somewhat of an end. Chapter 107 Book 2 CWL Roster Chapters M.U.D 01:37:13 U.S. CST May 14, 2001 [Mud sat in his living room on the couch staring around. He had been here for quite some time, had some fun. The money coming in from the CWL was good, and I mean real good compared to that of TW. I mean, now that he was thinking about it everything was perfect. Or was it. Mud was missing something. There was always that certain unknown aspect of walking the road of life down the highway that gave Mud pleasure in being a nomad. Staying in one place made him feel almost sheltered...caged. Mud fidgeted in his seat, what was he still doing here? Was he finally "settling" down, had he found his home? Nah...fuck that! Mud's home was the wide open world, the adventurous world, the one where a new day meant a new task...never the same old boring schedule that he seemed to be getting used to. Things had to change. They had to change now. Mud stood from the couch and moved over to the hallway, he turned to his left and knocked on the first door. A loud, "WHAT?" came from behind the door and Mud hollared back.] Mud: Hey Toadster, grab what you want to keep and lets go. Were on the road again buddy. [A few minutes passed when the door opened, Toad had a bag over his shoulder filled with clothes and a pornographic magazine in his hand, he looked at Mud and smiled.] Toad: Bout fuckin' time. -Fifteen minutes later, Tort Enterprises 34th Floor- [Mud stood in front of Lara Summers' office. He took a deep breath and then looked back at Toad for a moment of encouragement, except the crazy little bastard was flirting with the secretary, fucking Toad, always thinking with his (BLEEP - No really, we normally put all the corse language in Mud promos but this was just too much, you should have seen the censor ladies face when she heard she almost shat her pants. Nonetheless this part is cut but the rest isn't, sorry) and not his damn little head. Oh well, its game time. Mud opened Lara's door, just like he always did and he entered the office. She looked up from her desk and smiled, she ran to him and embraced him with a long hug. He held her tight before she released her grip and looked into his eyes] Lara: So good to see you Mort. [Wham bam thank you ma'am Morty got a kiss next, they sat down on the couch in her office and Mud quickly took control of the conversation] Mud: So you wanna do it on this couch? [Lara nodded -Sorry yet again we can't show this, also this one scene was suppose to be left out of the promo but since the writer protested with all his heart we put it in, lets just say that Mud and Lara held each other and sang music for a half an hour..thats all..and if you don't get the picture as to what really happened your an idiot. Glad thats over, lets get back to it- Mud rubbed the sweat from his forehead and then began talking as they both enjoyed a tasty cigarette] Mud: So anyway babe, I did mean to actually talk with you about something. [She looked over and smiled, gawd (spelled like that on purpose) did he love that smile] Lara: Go ahead Mort. Mud: Well, ya see, I feel like everyday is too much routine. I need to get out, to explore more, life's too short to sit around and not venture into the world to see all that you can see. Toad and I have talked, were hitting the road again, leaving "le CWL" and so we wanted to know if you'd come along too. [Lara stood up half naked and shot back at him almost in a defensive tone] Lara: Are you crazy? Is this a joke? Mud: Not a joke, its for real. Lara: Wha...I..but...I..wha...but Mort! Mud: Hey Lara, this is that happy part, where you get all happy..then we do it again. So..hop to it, smile time. [Lara glared at him and walked back to her desk. She leaned against it and almost seemed to be crying, Mud just looked confused, finally she turned back to him] Lara: Mort..I can't leave my job, this is my world. Mud: But I thought I was your world. Lara: You are..but- Mud: So you'll come with us? Lara: Sorry Mort but I can't. I worked my ass of to get where I am, and I can't just leave.. Mud: But Lara..your not just leaving, your coming with...me. [She said nothing and Mud stood now, staring into her eyes.] Mud: Oh I get it...the job's more important. Lara: Why can't you just stay? Mud: Because this isn't my place Lara? This isn't where I belong. Lara: Well this *is* where I belong. So if you don't belong here, then I guess we don't belong to each other. Mud: Guess not. Goodbye Lara, just so you know, I'll always love you. Lara: And I you. [Mud turned and headed to the door, as he opened it and was about to leave Lara yelled back at him-] Lara: Wait! [She ran to him and kissed him once more] Lara: One last thing, the sex was the best I ever had. [Mud turned and winked at the camera] Mud: Oh thats right. [The door to the office closed and Mud saw that Toad had been waiting] Toad: What took so long? Wheres Lara? Mud: I took a long time to say goodbye. Toad: Ah. [The two headed to the elevator and entered it. The door closed the elevator began its journey down, Toad was the first to speak up] Toad: So you broke up? Mud: Guess so. Toad: But did you tap some ass? Mud: Shit yeah! Toad: Good boy. Mud: How bout the secretary? Toad: Nah man... Mud: Shitty. Toad: Yeah...fucking lesbiens.
