Help: How to join the CWL

as captured 22 April 2001

What this is The CWL's own page on joining its roster, presented by Kid Cool, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 22 April 2001.
CWL wrestler Kid Cool attempts to explain how to join the CWL. How to join the CWL Hello! If you're on this page you are interested in joining the CWL roster for yourself. We've tried to make this site as interactive as possible for both the members and the visitors that come to the CWL in the hopes of making it fun for everyone. However, I understand that as much fun as the fan areas might be the experience just isn't quite as enjoyable if you're not in the thick of things with a character of your own. But the CWL is very structured in terms of storylines and angles, so it's tough to bring new wrestlers into the fold. So what do we do? As you may have read elsewhere, the CWL is run with loose story arcs. These arcs take the form of three month periods of time, always ending with a special event, or "Pay Per View". At the end of every story arch the CWL is committed to bringing in new members as part of the recruitment period. The amount of new members that are included will be determined based upon the needs of the promotion at the time, usually meaning that between 2-10 members will be recruited. At times the CWL will be looking for more tag teams than singles wrestlers, or cruiserweights over power wrestlers, and so on depending on what needs the promotion has at the end of the arc. These needs will be clearly stated in several areas on the CWL site as the story arc draws to a close so nobody misses the announcement and everyone gets a fair shake at joining. The CWL doesn't use a waiting list, but rather will be asking for applications at the end of every arch. Every application will be read, and every application will get a response unless the application is of such a low quality that no fed would accept it. If you want to join the CWL, please wait until the announcement of the recruitment period to send in your application. That way you can properly target your application to the needs of the CWL at the time, and ensure that your application doesn't get lost in the process. Our hope is to make sure everyone has a fair shake in getting into the CWL, and ensure that the backstage environment remains a positive one. The biggest concern is to keep the story writing at a high quality and ensure that a real team atmosphere exists backstage. If you believe that you would be a good fit and enjoy working with others for the common good of the story, then I look forward to hearing from you. Best luck!
