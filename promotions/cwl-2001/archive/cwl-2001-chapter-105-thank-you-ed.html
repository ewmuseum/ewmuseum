Thank you Ed. Chapter 105 Book 2 CWL Roster Chapters Steve 11:38:37 U.S. CST May 04, 2001 I've never been that good with words in a serious situation, and I tend to keep myself to myself a lot of the time, so I just didn't feel comfortable posting anything along the lines of everyone else on the fan board. Besides, I think everything has been said about Ed that anyone could possibly say. In short, he was a great guy and will be sorely missed. I'm basically posting here to thank Ed for the opportunity he gave me to join CWL, and in a strange way, I'd feel kind of bad if I didn't use the posting area and scripts he took so much time to hide from everyone and to set up. My comments will be pretty much about the ewrestling side of things, because... well, that's the world in which just about all of us knew Ed in. It takes a brave guy to let a maniac called Pete Borst to come near your fed, let alone into it, especially when he intends to destroy it. Not many fed owners would have welcomed such an idea for an angle, but Ed was willing to listen to my ideas with an open mind and managed to add the angle in and play off certain aspects so that they complement other ongoing angles. In the past, I've talked to fed owners about angles, and the moment they hear one bit they don't like, they'd just say no and not let you finish explaining. Ed was always willing to listen to the ideas of others, and he'd listen to what others say and offer constructive criticism rather than just shooting the idea down without any thought. He showed that by intending to reply to every application sent in, because he understood how disheartening it is to get no response from an application, and to be wondering for ages why exactly you didn't get accepted. Ed just seemed to put that little bit of extra thought into everything, and it definitely paid off and helped out others a lot. I think we could all do a lot worse than to adopt the attitude Ed had to how we act in ewrestling, and also in the real world. Thank you for the opportunity to join the CWL, and thank you for the months of entertainment and fun you've given us all.
