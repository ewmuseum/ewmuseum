CWL Wrapped: Seventh Edition

3 April 2001

What this is A column from the fan area of the CWL's own site, posted under Murray Manning on 3 April 2001 and written in the league's story, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 14 July 2001.
Seventh Edition Posted by Murray Manning April 03, 2001 16:55:28 U.S. CST Welcome to the seventh edition of “CWL Wrapped up” fresh from the first pay-per-view of the year, Revolution. Well it’s finally happened, the CWL has got a new world champion and if you hadn’t heard already it’s the competitor who I predicted to enter the final of the competition, Vince Jacobs. Vince surprisingly beat the gigantic Mountain and left him in a very bad condition indeed but Mountain did gain a little dignity as he was awarded the CWL United States championship which although isn’t as prestigious as the CWL world title is still an amazing accomplishment, which was well deserved by Mountain for his performance even though he was defeated. The most noticed factor in this match was the fact that Victoria was not present because she has been said to have left Vince and has aligned herself with XXXStasy. I personally don’t buy this because I’ve known Victoria in the past and she can be a very manipulative, cunning young lady which leaves me to the conclusion that she will stay with XXXStasy until he has a match with Vince for the world title (Which is highly likely considering their feud) and then she’ll screw XXXStasy and run off with Vince, now this might not happen but remember that I predicted it first if it does. No matter how you want to put it Kid Cool is the first and longest reigning CWL champion of this month even though he has lost championship matches to a few of the CWL wrestlers but I don’t think that his reign of the title is on the top of Kid Cool’s priorities because if had watched Revolution then you would have seen that Kid Cool was sent flowers. You might not think that Kid Cool being sent flowers is interesting but there’s more to it because the flowers which he was given black and dead-looking which left us and Kid Cool to believe that they were from the object of his affection, Nightshade. Although the evidence was extremely convincing Kid Cool was not 100% certain it was Nightshade but once the card that had come from it was read to him “Dearest... I've been fickle in the past but I've come to understand how great you are. I'll see you in a couple of weeks... hope you like these” he was certain that it was from nightshade. Now I have my doubts about this because Nightshade is definitely not the sort of person to show any emotion (especially love!) except for hatred but the all evidence points to Nightshade and there is no one who I can think of that can fit that description. Now we’ve come to the section where I answer one of the questions from a the fans of the CWL, this weeks letter comes from Brad McKinley from Hollywood, LA. “Yo Murray, Hey I just started to watch the CWL on TV and man it’s cool as hell, it was pretty cool when Little Italy kicked that stupid Frenchie’s ass! My question for you is, who is this Kent guy who I saw on Revolution? CWL Rules!, Brad McKinley” Kent is a former wrestler from the CWL along with Paladin and Dayrunner who returned to Revolution because they are said to have been on the verge of signing a contract with the CWL but nothing is definite.
