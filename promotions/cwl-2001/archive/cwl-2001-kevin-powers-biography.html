'Good God' Kevin Powers CWL Character Biographies Kevin Powers "'The Emperor of Hardcore'" Born: March 5, 1971 in Chicago, Illonis Hometown: Chicago, Illonis Height: 6'10 Weight: 312 lbs. Blood type: Type O+ Eye color: Brown Team: The Dark Carnival Manager: 'Risqué' Roseanne Fairhurst Trainer: Apocalypse Theme song: 'Let's Go All The Way' - Insane Clown Posse Wrestling Information Wrestling style: Power/Hardcore/Submissive Signature spots: Chokeslam series of punches Boot to face when opponent is off of ropes series of elbows bodyslam sidewalk slam Jumping Legdrop PowerZone (X-Factor) Jacknife Powerbomb Lariat Clothesline Finisher: 1) Powers picks up the oppoent in a jacknife powerbomb, but first drops him on the ropes. He then takes the momentum, turns around, and drives him into the canvas for the finish and win. 2) Just like the X-Factor except he sets his opponent on the top turnbuckle, climbs to the second, jumps back and hits the move. Finisher name: 1) Kiss the Canvas (Slingshot Powerbomb), 2) Power Extreme Historical Information Other/Previous promotions: SWWA, TCW, CSWA, MWC / EWI, HEW, WWL, NFW, NWA, NthWA Other/Previous teams: The UnHoly, The Family, jWo, The Hardcore Mafia, PLR Other/Previous managers: Susan and Gina Non-CWL titles won: TCW TV Title, MWC (Now EWI) IC Title, CSWA US Title, CSWA Tag Title (First with Eddy Love then with Apocalypse), HEW Extreme Title, NthWA World Title, NthWA 1999 Owen Hart Memorial Cup Non-CWL awards won: Best Match (Eli Flair vs. Kevin Powers) - 1999 CSWA Awards Show Biography Hailing from Chicago, Illinois, 'Good God' Kevin Powers has been dubbed 'The Emperor of Hardcore' for good reason. Much is not said about Powers past. There is a rumor that he was a prior military service member and got out of the service in South Korea where he was down on his luck. This cannot be confirmed. Since his 'return' to the states he met up with Gina and Susan and he got his act together and decided to enter a wrestling school where he was noticed quickly. He first started his career in the now defunct SWWA where he was introduced into the league by Apocalypse. After the league closed down he soon found himself in Total Championship Wrestling where he won the Television Title, Multinational Wrestling Corporation where he won the first ever Intercontinental Title, and ChampionShip Wrestling Alliance where he captured the United States Title in a tournament final. The most memorable of the three belts is the reputation he gained when he battled 'Total Elimination' Eli Flair, thus earning the respect of the Wrestling World. After dropping the belts he soon tagged up with former friend 'Hurricane' Eddy Love and captured the CSWA Unified Tag Team, MWC Intercontinental Title, CSWA US Title, CSWA Tag Team Titles, TCW Television Title, HEW Hardcore Title in a tournament final. Also, during that time, he found his way to Hardcore Extreme Wrestling where he captured the Hardcore Title there as well only to drop it later on down the road. As of now Kevin Powers is still the Tag Team Champion of the CSWA, but now with a new Apocalypse in the form of .... Apocalypse. Powers manager, 'Risqué' Roseanne Fairhurst, is a former Mile High Wrestling Ladies Champion. She came to Powers side when Gina and Susan, Powers' old valets, left when times got tough. So now it's Fairhurst that makes sure that nothing happens to her man. Her normal attire consist of torn blue jeans with matching jean jacket and a red halter top. She also has blonde hair that comes down the the upper part of her back and also brown eyes. When his music starts up Fairhurst comes out and stands right in front of Powers and that's when the pyro effects go off from behind. After the pyro, which is a white fountain like effect, is done he takes a cool stride towards the ring where he lets Fairhurst get in first and then he follows there after. His ring attire consist of Black leather pants with a leather tank top. It's all in black except on one side of the leg says GOOD and on the other side of the leg it says GOD, both in red.
