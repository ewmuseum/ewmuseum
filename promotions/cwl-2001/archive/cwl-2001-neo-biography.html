Neo CWL Character Biographies Henry Clay "The fighter" Also known as: Pit, End Born: June 18, 1968 Hometown: Chicago, IL Height: 6'6 Weight: 268 Blood type: A Postive Eye color: Brown Theme song: Stigmata, by Ministry Wrestling Information Wrestling style: Brawler, Boxing Signature spots: Reverse Jawbreaker Armdrag Swinging DDT Lou Thesz Press Uppercut Body Punches Finisher: Dangerous Brainbuster Finisher name: Endblow Historical Information Other/Previous promotions: fWo, IWO, NCWA, WWO, RWL Other/Previous managers: Tiffany Non-CWL titles won: NCWA World Heavyweight Title, NCWA World Tag Titles, NCWA Hardcore TItle, WWO World Heavyweight Title, WWO Hardcore Title, RWL World Heavyweight Title, RWL Interpromotional Title, fWo Hardcore Championship Non-CWL awards won: NCWA Wrestler of the Year, RWL Newcomer of the Year Biography My name is Neo. Yeah, I know. That's not the name I was born with. But to you it's Neo. Is that a problem? And don't think it's based on some lame ass film with Keanu Reeves 'cause it ain't. I've been Neo for ten years now and I could give a damn about Hollywood and all the hangers on. Leeches, the whole bunch of them. Some people enter the wrestling industry with something to prove. I say that's all well and good when you're someone in fancy shirts with predicatable catch phrases or that drunk ass schmuck with the bald head. They should be trying to prove something because both of them are about as worthless as they come. Not to pick on either of those two mind you, because the wrestling industry is filled with people that are constantly trying to crawl their way up out of the hole of garbage that they exist in so they can get up and finally stand shoulder to shoulder with those people who were born with nothing to prove because they are just that damn good from the start. That's not egotism, that's Neo. I never wrestled the indy leagues because doing so would have been a big waste of my time. I start at the top and I'll move the promotion and everyone around me up from there. It may surprise you to learn that the wrestling industry is largely regarded as a pile of monkey shit by the general public. This doesn't come as a surprise to Neo however. After all, look around you. You've got promoters who are too busy trying to milk every last ounce of cash they can out of wrestlers too clumsy, washed up or just plain untalented to tie their own shoes let alone wrestle in front of thousands of people who paid some small fee to watch two men get their asses kicked. That's right, I said men. You have all these chicks in wrestling and that's just like as wrong as it gets. I don't give a damn how god damn many titles that broad with the silly chin and chipmunk voice has won. She's still just a worthless broad and it really says more about how pathetic the rest of the league is for getting beat by her than it says anything about the so-called skill that this skank has managed to dig up somewhere in the gutter where she goes to sleep at night. The wrestling industry is a sick, vomiting puppy and I guess that's where I really come in. I'm here to save you all. Save you from the pathetic, the dreary, the misplaced fools that prance about in their little tights. I'm here to raise not just myself, because face it pal, I'm already up there miles that you or anyone you know will ever be. I'm here not just to raise myself, but to raise up an entire promotion so that when you come to a show and watch the wrestlers you can actually go home thinking that you saw for once in your dismal little life a man who is infinitely better than you ever thought possible. Perhaps your spirit will be raised by knowing that even though your life is on a one-way track to nowhere there is someone who has proven that perfection is possible. Me. You can thank me later, if you live that long. If you don't, it's no sweat of my ass. Dig? Handler Information Handler: Kel
