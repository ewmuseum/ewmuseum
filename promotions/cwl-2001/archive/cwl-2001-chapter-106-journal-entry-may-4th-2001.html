Journal Entry: May 4th, 2001 Chapter 106 Book 2 CWL Roster Chapters Zero 22:04:37 U.S. CST May 04, 2001 Dedicated to Ed Wilson, the man who allowed me to enter this place. He was the man who made it fun to be here. I held, and hold, the greatest of respect for him. Ed, I thank you. May 4th, 2001 January 29th was the date. It has been just slightly over three months since Matt passed away. No one knew what would happen after that. It was a terrible time. And here I sit, three months later, looking back on the situation and what has happened since then. It has been a tough battle. Throughout all of this, I have had to hold strong and somehow be positive. I’ve had to because of Erica. She almost didn’t make it through the news of Matt. Following her revelation of the pregnancy, it was the last thing she needed. But I give her credit. She has held up well and is moving forward in her life. That child is going to be a strong one, indeed. Staying positive. I’d like to think that I helped her make it to that point, but I know that she couldn’t have done it if she hadn’t of wanted to. But staying positive throughout the adversity was the key. She has settled into the apartments quite well. I’m happy for her. Her dad had to do what he had to do. I’m glad that she was able to make her own decision in the matter. It was one for her own good. She’s finding herself again. She’s decorating the apartment and is even preparing the child’s room. I’ve helped her when I could. It should end up pretty nice. She’s hoping for a boy. In the child’s room, she’s working on painting and decorating it blue, so I hope it will be. Even though it’s Carenthia’s old apartment, it has a pleasant feel to it. The walls still hold Carenthia’s once hidden, but finally revealed strong will. It is already starting to rub off onto Erica, and will make her even stronger in the process. She might need the extra boost in the near future. As I write this, the future is very uncertain. I have yet to go into great detail with Erica about it, but I am now without a job. None of the management in the CWL are certain if it will ever rise again. Not even the media have made much mention of it yet. The CWL was kicked down once. None are sure if it can rise again. Personally, I don’t see how. Because of that, I find myself unemployed… and with a small fear that continues to creep closer up my neck. I was there for Erica, her child... and Matt. It was our future. That future now hangs in the balance as I decide where to go from here. Where will I go as the carpet is pulled out from under me? I haven’t the faintest idea, but I am not sure if it is as bad as how it first appeared. I’ll survive. Erica and I, we’ll both survive. She has trust in me. I can feel that trust as it weighs down on my shoulders, but it isn’t burdening. This will only make us stronger. It only creates a stronger sense of hope. Hope will be with me and help guide whatever it is that I do from here on out. It will work out and everything will be ok. Until then, goodbye… Lint "Zero" Douglas
