What this is Chapter 37 of the CWL's second book, posted under Andy Knight w/ Penny Purehart, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 22 April 2001. The league called its role-plays chapters and numbered them with its shows.
Meat n' Go Chapter 37 Book 2 CWL Roster Chapters Andy Knight w/ Penny Purehart "Welcome to Wally's Meat n' Go, how may I place your order?" "Andy, is that you?" A head poked out from the window ahead. The older man, in his late 30's, squinted his eyes to adjust to the bright sunlight. The bill of his "Meat n' Go" sun visor clearly gave no help to the situation. "How did you know my name, miss?" The car pulled ahead to window and out came CWL’s own, Penny Purehart. She wore a casual looking sundress and a pair of nifty sunglasses. "So your Andy Knight? Gerald said I'd find you here." The older gentlemen stuck out his hand and shook that of Penny. His pearly white teeth shimmered as he smiled into the camera. "I'm sorry, but do I know you, miss?" Penny laughed as she pulled a pen from her right pocket and clicked it once. "I apologize. Let me introduce myself. I'm Penny..." "Oh yeah… Penny Purehart." Andy interrupted. Penny smirked. "Yeah, but how did you know?" "I remember seeing you on Thursday Titan's. You know, that Mr. Culligan fellow, sure doesn't know how to treat a lady." Penny smiled. "So you're familiar with the CWL then?" "Familiar with it, heck, I used to wrestle for the company way back when. I used to be great in the ring. I used to be great at allot of things." Penny gave a witty looking smirk as she looked into Andy's eyes. "How'd you like to feel great again?" Andy smiled, but a look of vagueness fell across his face. "I'm not following you." Penny pulled out a small package of papers and laid them down on the counter top. "I'm offering you to be apart of the Championship Wrestling League and retain that... greatness." Andy let out a deep breath and looked around his current working environment. His boss started to yell at him as cars now lined up behind Penny. However, none of that seemed to matter. Everything else began to fade away, except for that moment when Andy was in the ring; when he was great. When he was a true warrio... BANG! The eighteen year old, pimple faced boss slammed down a bucket of soapy water with mop next to Andy. "Some kid just hurled all over table three. Go clean it up old Andy." Mr. Knight slowly turned to see the gloomy mess, which was his job. He then turned back around to see Penny with the CWL contract in hand, and the nice sunny surroundings. Then back again to the murky profession of Wally's Meat n' Go. It was an easy decision. Andy turned to the camera grinned. He took off his headset, hat, and slide through the window. Penny, already in the driver side of the car, signalled Andy to hop in. Penny then thrust her foot upon the accelerator and off they went. Andy was coming back. Andy was coming home.
