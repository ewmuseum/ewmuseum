Objects DDT (and variations) SideStep-Neckbreaker Finisher: Spinning Powerbomb into a Pin Finisher name: The Havoc-Bomb Historical Information Other/Previous promotions: eWf, dbW, HEW, SCW, CSWA Other/Previous teams: The Natural Born Killers (eWf, dbW, HEW)-- The eQualizers (HEW)-- The Forsaken (CSWA) Other/Previous managers: Yukon Jack (eWf) Non-CWL titles won: --- Non-CWL awards won: --- Biography To be truthful, there isn't much publicly known about the wrester known as Havoc, prior to his entrance into the business... other than the knowledge that he was born in Canada, raised in New York, and that he came from a good family. Up until very recently, few wrestlers active in the sport knew his real name, Mason Xavier. It wasn't that he was trying to keep it secret. Its just that.. nobody asked him what it was. Entering the sport in the middle of 1996, Havoc teamed with BloodHound under the guidance of Yukon Jack. They called themselves the Natural Born Killers. In the beginning, their goal was simple. To cause as much trouble and to hurt as many people as they could. After becoming embroiled in a war for the eWF Tag Team Titles, winning them on two occassions, the two men eventually came to a split, when BloodHound showed his aleigance to the Second Dynasty, costing them the tag belts and beginning a major transition in Havoc's career. ...as well as his life. Off camera... Mason's wife of 7 months, Rebecca, leaves him due to the stresses of road life. Mason is understanding... and resolves to focus solely on wrestling. He would never tell a soul how, in the first few months following, the lonliness almost ruled him completely. With the 'tag team strings' cut, Havoc cast his manager aside and decided to focus on what was right for him. And that was a singles career. No manager looking for a cut. Just one man, looking out for himself in a land full of vultures. After a year long struggle, Havoc reached the top of the eWf, trading it's World Title with men like the UnHoly Father, and the Dirty Hippie until that league met it's untimely demise. From the ashes of the eWf, arose the dbW. At the federations first event, the match had been signed... Havoc -vs- the UnHoly Father: CAREER Match. The hatred between them was so strong, that they felt they HAD to settle things before either of them delved too deeply in the dbW. After interference from Red Week, the Father came out victorious, leaving Havoc out of a job. So, he came up with a plan. Wearing a black mask and calling himself 'Doomsday', Havoc re-entered the dbW and hounded the UnHoly Father, and after several weeks, finally goaded him into a Title Match. The match went down, and 'Doomsday' emerged victorious by pinfall. The UnHoly Family stormed the ring and unmasked 'Doomsday', revealing the truth to the world. The Title was held up, Havoc was re-instated, and a re-match was ordered with the Title on the line once more. At SuperSlaughter '97, in what proved to be the bloodiest match in dbW history, the UnHoly Father walked away with the Gold after stabbing Havoc in the stomach with a shard of glass. Havoc rallied a group of wrestlers together to combat the UnHoly Family, calling them the Natural Born Killers. Recruiting Vulture, Dark Patriot, and 'MadDog' Ray Manson... the Natural Born Killers met heavy resistance from the Family. At a massive pay-per-view, a stable match was held... in the end, it came down to Havoc, and the UnHoly Father's dark apprentice, Red Week.
