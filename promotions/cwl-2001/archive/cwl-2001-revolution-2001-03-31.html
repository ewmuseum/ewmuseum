March 31, 2001 Broadcasting from Albany, New York In 1985 the Championship Wrestling League premiered Revolution, a Pay Per View title that replaced the traditional "Circus of Stars" that had always been run in the past. During that time the CWL was run by Robert Hailer the third, a man who by all accounts was nothing like his father or his father's father. The third Hailer was well respected... almost beloved in the industry. If he had one sin it was allowing his workers to get away with too much... and during the 80's the CWL was dominated by a select few names who insisted that they be positioned at the top of the cards. Most notable was Angus MacGregor, a multi-time CWL World Heavyweight Champion who in the very first Revolution event wrestled Killer Karnage. It was a match that MacGregor won easily, and the fans at the time ate it up. The CWL was popular then... at the top of the game with only a blonde hailed wrestler named Hulk Hogan who wrestled for the World Wrestling Federation threatening their dominance. Times change. In 2001 the era of the hero is gone, replaced by darker characters. Gone is the black and white reality of wrestling... replaced by a dim gray. Right and wrong are no longer factors... instead it is every man for himself, a philosophy nowhere as evident as in the CWL. For it will be in one arena where fates are decided and lives are forever changed. It is a revolution that began over a hundred years ago... and rages still. • The crowd was excited.
