Douglas CWL Character Biographies Steve Douglas Born: 06/19/66 Hometown: Toronto Ontario, Canada Height: 6'6 Weight: 269 Blood type: Unknown Eye color: Blue Team: None Manager: None Trainer: None Theme song: Under Dog by Testeagles Wrestling Information Wrestling style: Brawler, Pure power Signature spots: Headbutt Powerslam Powerbomb Facebuster Belly to Belly Suplex D-Day (Chokeslam except Douglas slams opp. into his knee) Finisher: Dangerous brainbuster Finisher name: Douglas Driver Historical Information Other/Previous promotions: BWO - Brutal Wrestling Organization, TCW - Toronto Championship Wrestling Other/Previous teams: Soldiers of Darkness, Brain Trust (Original Member) Other/Previous managers: Levite Prod - Bodyguard Non-CWL titles won: BWO US Championship, BWO IC Championship][TCW Commonwealth Championship Non-CWL awards won: None Biography Growing up in the suburbs of Toronto, Steve was always a well disciplined boy. He did well in school, participated in many sports activities and actually excelling in wrestling. He was your all around good kid. A student many looked up to. Steve, graduating from high school deceided to not go to University instead he jumped into life by joining construction. It was there that he built his body from everyday strenious work. But Steve always wanted more, he felt he was missing something. One night while in the local pub he found an add for wrestling try outs for a up and coming promotion. He went to the try out and performing quite qell for the companies scouts he was signed and started his career in wrestling in the federation BWO (Brutal Wrestling Organization). Dawning the name Neophyte and playing the role of a gothic slave Steve obeyed the word of his "master" the Reaper. After unsuccessful wins and feeling as though he was going nowhere Steve "broke free" of the Reaper's control. Here was Steve's chance to shine in the spotlight and Steve went into singles competition. Achieving many BWO titles and becomming and original member of the Brain Trust Steve went on to make a name for himself. At it's (BWO) closing Steve was at a prime age of twenty five and left with no job. Turning back to construction Steve found a way to support himself and just make enough to put diner on the table. Years later, after an evening at the bar Steve was scouted by Greg Roberts after he saved the man from a brutal beating. An agent for Toronto Championship Wrestling Greg begged Steve to come back to wrestling and Steve agreed. Getting back into the ring Steve would go on to be a top face in the federation and win the Commonwealth Championship. Soon after that TCW went on a hiatus and Steve returned back to construction. When TCW returned contract problems arose and Steve left TCW on bad terms. Looking for a new cause Steve found CWL, finally here he was considered one of the greats. Wrestling through a decade of CWL history Steve Douglas was known to all as a hero to the federation, bringing down many opposing forces. It was here that Steve had finally found a place to shine. Many said it was Steve that made the promotion what it was. Steve was in the spotlight and he loved every minute of it. Until... Last year however dawned a new age for Steven Douglas. Steve began to lose. Match after match and loss after loss. Suddenly the fans had a new perspective on their once great hero. Suddenly the fans stopped cheering his name. His mind filled with hate after consecutive losses and embarrasment Steve slowly began to lose his sanity. No longer was he a hero in the public's eye but a loser. A nobody. Something within Steve sparked and he turned on his fans at the last PPV. No longer referring to himself by his first name and only calling himself Douglas, Douglas has set out to cause a new wave of destruction upon the federation that once gave him much pride. Douglas want's to destroy everything and anything he can get his hands on, even if he has to take it to extreme levels. Douglas want's chaos and that is surely what he will bring. However...ratings dropped. Douglas wasn't bringing in any profit and was fired from the company. A year later he was hired back when Tort bought the company and Nodar was in charge. They wanted some old faces when they re-opened. Douglas was different now, he was bitter at the spot light being taken away from him. So hell bent on becomming famous once again he seeked out John Skyler (Fallen) to become a tag team with him. Fallen refused and Douglas began numerous attacks on him. He blamed John for stealing away any chance he ever had at becomming the star he once was. Douglas went to extreme and kidnapped Fallen. He brutally beat and tortured him. Finally after escaping Fallen went to the authorities and at the first returning PPV Revolution Douglas was taken into psychiatric care after being beaten in a match by Fallen. Douglas was held for weeks in the Insane Asylum before tricking the management to let him out. Now he prowls the streets looking for any chance at getting back into the CWL. At any means necessary.
