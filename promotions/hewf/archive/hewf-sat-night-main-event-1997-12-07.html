HEWF SAT NIGHT MAIN EVENT

7 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JOIN THE HEWF GO TO http://members.aol.com/HEWFvp/index.html HERE IS A MATCH FROM THE SAT NIGHT CARD MAIN EVENT HEWF HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH THREE WAY DANCE BRET HART (CHAMPION) VS THE FRANCHISE SHANE DOUGLAS VS TAZ McMahon: This is it ladies and gentlemen the Main Event! Lawler: Ring the bell ring the bell. I can't wait any longer this is what I've been waiting for this the whole night! McMahon: There is the bell and here we go......all three men in the ring. Lawler: The Franchise really wants this. McMahon: All three men want this. This is for the most sought after title in wrestling, the HEWF Heavyweight Title! Lawler: Shut up McMahon and call the match........... McMahon: Taz with a belly to back suplex to Bret Hart. Followed by an elbow drop by The Franchise. Looks like both men are ganging up on the champ Bret Hart. Lawler: Ha Ha McMahon Bret is finally geting the lumps he deserves. McMahon: Oh my Taz with a chop to The Franchise! Lawler: There goes the ganging up idea out the window. This is every man for himself! McMahon: The Franchise fires back with a clothesline to Taz. Bret is up and Bret executes a kick to the knee of The Franchise. Lawler: He's going for the legs to weaken him up for the Sharpshooter! McMahon: Taz with a Tazplex to The Franchise. Now it looks like both men are ganging up on The Franchise. Lawler: McMahon how many times do I have to tell you this is everyman for himself. There are no friends here. McMahon: Bret Hart with a scoop and a powerslam to The Franchise. Taz drops the elbow on The Franchise. Hart with a piledriver to Taz......your right King, there are no friends here!! Lawler: I TOLD YOU THERE ARE NO FRIENDS HERE NOW SHUT UP McMahon AND CALL THE MATCH!!! McMahon: Taz is down and Hart drops knee on the throat of Taz. Franchise is up and The Franchise with a rake to the face of Bret followed by a suplex over the top rope which sends Bret onto the concrete floor (no padded floors in the HEWF like some of the other sissy feds)! Lawler: McMahon you just couldn't go a night without that old padded sissy floor joke huh? McMahon: Your right King thats cause this is the HOTTEST E-MAIL WRESTLING FEDERATION! Where men are men . Lawler: Franchise is climing up to the top turnbuckle.....looking to come down on Bret. Taz is up and delivers a crushing forearm to the balls of The Franchise. The Franchise is hung on the ropes. Taz comes off the ropes with a clothesline that sends The Franchise to the outside. Both Hart and The Francise on the outside. McMahon: OH MY ITS STONE COLD STEVE AUSTIN! Austin running down to ringside and a kick to the midsection of The Franchise followed by THE STONE COLD STUNNER ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR!!! Lawler: UNBELEIVABLE !!! It's Hunter Hurst Helmsley running down to ringside. HHH looks right at Stone Cold Steve Austin then delivers the PEDIGREE to Bret Hart on the (you guessed it) concrete floor! McMahon: Stone Cold rolls The Franchise into the ring. HHH rolls Bret Hart into the ring. Lawler: Taz with the cover on The Franchise......1......2.....3....... McMahon: Taz with the cover on Bret Hart.........1........2..............3.....AND THE WINNER AND NEW HEWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION TAZ!!! Lawler: The Franchise is furious and so is Bret Hart they both take Taz on the outside of the ring and while Bret has the sharpshooter on him The Franchise is flying off the top turnbuckle on Taz with a steel chair. McMahon: OH my Taz is busted wide open and bleeding profusly from the cranium. How can Taz defend the belt he is gonna surely be injured after they get thru with him. Lawler: Whats left of him that is.......... McMahon: Good night and get ready for Mon Night Massacre here are the matches already signed!!! Triple Main Event The following matches are in a cage with no chance of outside interference!! Stone Cold vs HHH The winner will face the winner of The Franchise vs Bret Hart That man will face Taz for the HEWF Heavyweight Title on the same night!!! McMahon: And there you have it folks a triple main event in a cage with no outside interference . There must be a winner! Stone Cold vs HHH and The Franchise vs Bret Hart the winners of these two matches will face Taz in the cage for the HEWF Heavyweight title!!! Send your rollplays to [detail withheld] !!!
