Im running a new project known as FIRE & ICE, what we are are 2 feds that basically operate like one efed. FIRE is a women's league, and
ICE is a men's league.
I wanna debut the fed in 3 weeks, but the roster just isnt their, as well as a few extra match-writers we're gonna need.
Matches are fully written out, unless the event falls on a weekend when one fed is holding a pay per view. I expect good rps, which means more then just the 10-30 garbage, but DOES not mean you have to post one that rivals 'War and Peace' in legnth. Just build your character and set up your angles and feuds and you will go far.
Not only is their a minimum of one rp in F&I, but a max on 2 as well. You CANNOT rp more then twice in the week or so leading up to your match, some people just dont have the time to keep up to some folks who can rp twice a day. This should give everyone a fair shake.
Your limited to 2 wrestlers, and that means FIRE and ICE, so you can have one in each fed, or 2 in one of them
Ill include the league FAQ after the application if you need more information. Here is our website.

The rules of FIRE and ICE. *FIRE and ICE are not considered entry-level feds, so if you dont have prior e-wrestling experience, please dont bother to apply. We are only accepting experienced handlers.
*note* as it applies to this rulebook, FIRE & ICE will be considered one fed unless otherwise noted, in character terms, we are two organizations under one owner, but technically, it's one fed.
1. Schedule. Televised - F&I hold two shows a week. FIRE's Inferno on sunday nights, and ICE's Chill Factor on monday nights. These are where the majority of angles and feuds come together.
House - Once a week or whenever we feel it's needed, F&I will hold an
"interfed" house show. This is basically to give matches to anyone who missed out, and to debut new talent. They will be simulator generated and not hold any real implications towards TV and PPV events. Despite the two feds sharing the show, only FIRE wrestlers will wrestler other
FIRE wrestlers, ditto for ICE. Pay Per View - FIRE and ICE alternate pay per view shows, with the exception of the 3 interpromotional shows. Pay Per Views are every 6 weeks, and during the week of a PPV, the hosting fed's show is cancelled, and the opposite fed's show will be summarized rather then written out. The listing with examples.
(NOTE - F&I's first event should take place on December 9th and 10th) DECEMBER 16 - Battle of the Sexes 2001 (FIRE vs ICE yearly supercard) 12/16 Inferno and 12/17 C.F. cancelled (ALL DATES BELOW ARE GONNA BE CHANGED, DUE TO FED DELAYS) JANUARY 20 - HeartBurn (FIRE) 1/20 Inferno cancelled, 1/21 C.F. summarized MARCH 3 - Point of No Return (ICE) 3/3 Inferno summarized, 3/4 C.F. cancelled APRIL 14 - Abuse of Power (FIRE) MAY 26 - On Frozen Ground (ICE) JULY 7 - Hell Freezes Over (FIRE and ICE combined show, our marquee event) AUGUST 18 - Cruel Summer (FIRE and ICE combined show, usually rematches of HFO) SEPTEMBER 29 - Badd Girlz (FIRE) NOVEMBER 10 - Nowhere to Run (ICE) DECEMBER 22 - Battle of the Sexes 2002 (FIRE vs ICE) *A quick note on interfed events* The three yearly interpromotional battles will pit in some cases men vs women. While perhaps not the most realistic matches, they will be more about the entertainment aspect, as well as a chance for the handlers of each "fed" to battle for bragging rights. Thus, these matches will be, in most situations, contested as equal contests. Hopefully our readers can suspend their realities just a bit and enjoy these events.
2. Matches Weekly shows will generally house 4 - 6 matches. House shows will vary. Pay Pew Views will be 4 - 8 for single fed shows,
6 - 10 for interfed shows. With the exception of shows falling on the same week of a pay per view in the OTHER fed (as read above). Matches will usually be fully written out.
Summarized results will be produced when one fed is opposite the other's PPV event. FIRE & ICE matches fall under the standard rules of wrestling. 20 second countouts, DQs for varies infractions, cant win titles unless you pin or make your opponent submit, yada yada yada.
FIRE & ICE will have opportunities for gimmick or special matches. The feds will book match schedules, but challenges can be made, and outside input from handlers is welcomed, especially in situations such as special angles and feuds.
3. Roleplaying, Strating, obtaining victories TWO roleplays, AND ONLY TWO roleplays maximum are to be submitted in the one week period before the RP deadline of your upcoming match. You must do atleast one tho.
Deadlines for rps will be 72 hours in advance of a show. Thursday at
8PM for Inferno, Friday at 8PM for Chill Factor.
At that same time, any strategy forms must be submitted. STRATS ARE OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED!!!!! VERY RECOMMENDED, but we dont dock if you dont do em.
Strats can be submitted one week, and used repeatedly with no changes made to them, since they dont pertain to specific match situations. So basically you can submit one for your first match and if you want, we'd keep it on file and use it weekly until you send in an updated one. With that in mind, try to atleast send us a strat once, it helps us write your character correctly.
FIRE and ICE bookers determine a winner based on a number of factors, most often it will be whoever's RP was most successful in building their character and setting the stage for the upcoming match. If the match is intertwined into a storyline, sometimes a bit of a push will be given to a certain wrestler if his victory would be more important to the interest factor of the angle. Sometimes, in the case of feuds and angles, handlers may voluntarily accept a loss to forward the story.
4. Membership in FIRE & ICE Each fed's roster will be limited to 24 spots. A minimum of 12 spots per fed must be filled for that fed to hold matches.
If at any time the roster of one fed dips below 12, that fed will be suspended till the roster is increased.
Each potential handler has the right to create and keep active 2 characters. ACTIVE being the key word. F&I RESERVE THE
RIGHT TO MAKE INACTIVE ANY WRESTLER BELONGING TO SOMEONE WHO HANDLES TWO WRESTLERS TO ALLOW A NEW HANDLER TO COME IN AND FILL A ROSTER SPOT! THIS WOULD ONLY BE IMPLEMENTED IF DEMAND TO JOIN BECOMES INTENSE. F&I can remove any handler from the league at any time, but will only do so if that person is inactive, dissruptive to the fed, or has a foul attitude towards fellow handlers without sufficient reason. Everyone get along and play nice! And no slackers!
In the event of rosters being loaded, we also have two feds that work with us that you could sort of call our
"developemental" regions, even tho their credible feds in their own right. SEWF is a men's fed, and VWV a women's fed.
Feel free to check both out. 5. Writers FIRE and ICE requires a 5 person group of WEEKLY writers to remain afloat, that includes any staff and ownership. The number of writers beyond 5 can be unlimited.
By weekly, that means their have to be 5 writers available EACH WEEK! So that would mean 10 bi-weekly writers, and so-on and so-forth. We prefer if you could write for either side, but if your preferences are to one fed over another, thats cool... each fed should have atleast 3 writers to cover it per week.
6. Other Titles. TEWN Television Championship (Interfed belt, only defended at the interfed shows) FIRE World Title FIRE Blaze of Glory Title (Secondary level belt, akin to the IC title of the WWF) FIRE Skyweight Title (115 lbs and under) ICE World Title ICE Frozen Glory Title (like the Blaze of Glory belt in FIRE) ICE Cruiserweight Title (225 lbs and under) WHAT? NO TAG TEAM TITLES? Yup... we only will have tag belts if their is interest in the tag division.
