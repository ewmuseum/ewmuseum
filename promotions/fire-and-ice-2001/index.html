Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

FIRE & ICE announced itself on 8 November 2001 as two federations staged as one: FIRE, the Female International Rasslin' Empire, for women, and ICE, Independent Combat Entertainment, for men, both run by a single owner and treated as “one fed unless otherwise noted” even while its own rulebook insisted the two organizations stayed distinct in character. Each league ran its own weekly televised card — FIRE's Inferno on Sundays, ICE's Chill Factor on Mondays — with an occasional simulator-generated interfed house show to catch up wrestlers who had missed television time.

Its rulebook laid out a full year of pay-per-views alternating between the two leagues and meeting three times for interpromotional supercards: Battle of the Sexes to open and close the calendar, HeartBurn, Point of No Return, Abuse of Power, On Frozen Ground, Hell Freezes Over, Cruel Summer, Badd Girlz, and Nowhere to Run. Handlers were capped at two roleplays a week by rule, a deliberate choice against the newsgroup's own quantity habit — “matches are judged on quality of rp and not quantity” — and wrestlers could hold spots in both leagues at once, one apiece or two in either.

Its opening events were set for the weekend of 9 December 2001, and a 14 November follow-up still held that date; by its 24 November call for applicants the first supercard, Battle of the Sexes, had slipped two weeks to 16 December, with roster spots still open in both leagues. No later document survives to say whether that debut card, or any of the year's planned pay-per-views, actually ran.
