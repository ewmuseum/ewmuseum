Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RAW opened on 4 March 1996 as an explicit spin-off and feeder promotion for WAR, posted by its own parent federation's founder, Jacob “Jake” Kane, using that same fed's own match-determination system and pitched at newcomers and players trying out new creations.

Its record ran largely through joint broadcasts with its own parent promotion. A 5 May 1996 Sunday Night Main Event, live from a sold-out Winnipeg Arena, closed its opening angle in chaos when a faction called the Brotherhood stormed the ring against the parent fed's own reigning World Champion, Tonnage, who then cut a profane promo warning off a wrestler he accused of spreading rumors about his partner. On the same card, Hellspawn's Hades and Abyss used a hidden pair of brass knuckles to steal the RAW North American Tag Team titles from the reigning champions, Red, White and Blue, and the two promotions' own joint interfederation champion, the American Patriot, was ruled out of his scheduled title defense after spraining an ankle in an in-ring collision earlier the same night.

A week later, RAW advertised itself as a “farm league for the bigger one,” running four cards a week, a monthly pay-per-view, and five championships of its own, and floated talks of a three-way interfederation pay-per-view with a fed called the EWA.

RAW went quiet for the summer and reopened on 3 September 1996 under a new president, Jon Robinson, whose own follow-up post let slip, as a wink to regulars, that Robinson had been quietly funneling money out of the ailing parent promotion to bankroll RAW's own return, with commentator Red Robertson staying on as its silent vice president and the wrestler Lord Bryon signed on as color commentator and the host of his own daily magazine show.

By its last surviving broadcast, 2 November 1996, the reopened promotion had renamed itself Maximum Championship Wrestling, running its own Sunday Night RAW live from the Cotton Bowl in Dallas under a new commentary team and a villain, Stone Cold Derek, feuding with the Angel of Death after harassing another wrestler's own girlfriend on camera. No later document, and no closure notice, survives under this record; the promotion's own record ends there, under its third name in eight months.
