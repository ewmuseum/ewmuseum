Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A federation returns to a name it once gave up for dead. Five years after its own abrupt closure, the Internet World Wrestling Council reopens under the same banner, the same world-wide ambition, and a roster built from scratch.

Its own founding FAQ carries the shape of a promotion that has learned from its first life: a flexible schedule instead of a forced weekly grind, a censorship policy stated in plain terms, and a full slate of six championships waiting for challengers before a single card has run.

A storyline president stands at its head, a controlling, image-conscious executive with no known history in the promotion's first incarnation — a new face put in front of an old name, the clearest sign that the second IWWC intends to be its own thing even while it claims its first one's history.
