Having taken over from Mr. [name withheld] as IWO President, would you PLEASE send me your trash talk, etc.
Also, who do you want? I have controllers for: Singles: Diesel (Internet champ) Roddy Piper Shawn Michaels Hakushi Virgil Jerry 'The King' Lawler Honky Tonk Man Tag: Royal Family (aka Beauty & The Beast) (Rick Rude & Bastion Booger) Bushwhackers Women's: Sunny Donna Mimi Medusa Manager's: Ted Dibiase Hurly Burly Mrs Philips I would also appreciate it if someone would e-me a list of champions. Also, please note a change to the original limits: 3 singles, 1 tag & 2 women's. A description of finisher if non-WWF would also be appreciated, and please note that the same person can be used, under a different name
(i.e. Diesel = Vinnie Vegas, Medusa = Alundra Blayze, etc.) Closing = Friday 9th June @ 21.00, or any previous ownership will be ignored. In an attempt at security, so no-one can double-claim wrestlers, answer this:
What were the stipulations when Diesel fought Chris Benoit & who interfered? [name withheld] < [detail withheld] > Sometimes, just sometimes, I wish I wasn't as great as I am.
