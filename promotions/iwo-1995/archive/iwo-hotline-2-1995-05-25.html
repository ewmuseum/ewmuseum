"IWO Hotline #02"

25 May 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TENA: Thank you for calling the IWO Hotline! We have a big report today. I'll talk with new IWO Woman's champ Magnificent Mimi, Internet champ
Diesel, Alundra Blayze, Sid Vicious, and Dan Spivey! Friday is prime
Time Wrestling and a big match is already signed, IWO Tag champs Beuty and the Beast will meet Public Enemy for the tag titles! Speaking of tag titles, I understand that Sid Vicious has a mystery partner. Sid who is he?
SID: My partner is none other then the Dangerous one.....Dan Spivey! TENA: Hello Danny. SPIVEY: Hello. SID: We are reforming the Skyscrapers. And we are challenging the winners of the Beuty and the Beast.Public Enemy match on Prime Time.
SPIVEY: And don't forget our mystery manager! SID: That's right, when we get into the ring against the champs we'll announce who it is! TENA: I can't wait! I wonder who the Skyscrapers manager is? Now I'm going to talk to Alundra Blayze about Sister Sherrie's challenge on
Tuesday on the Ross Report! Hello Ms. Blayze. (Blayze is wearing black spandex bike shorts with a blue stripe down the outside and a tight white top)
TENA: I love my job! BLAYZE: Sherrie you can try to rewrite history but the fact remains that
YOUR shoulders were on the mat when time ran out.....I want a shot at
Mimi's title and if the path to her is through you then that's fine with me. I agree that our next match should not end in a draw so I propse no time limit at all. And just to make sure there is no doubt as to who the winner is I want an Italian death match. In case you arn't familiar with such a fight it's really very simple. There are np pin falls or submissions, only knockouts or passouts. The looser is simply the one who can not regain conciousness after 1 minute. This is how real women fight in my country. So what do you say.....Sister?
TENA: Alundra Blayze is ready for Sherrie. But will Sherrie accept. I guess we'll find out tomorrow on the Hotline! What a minute I just got a note that says Sherrie will wrestle Blayze in any match she wants! This came before Sherrie heard Blayze's interview! Wow what a match it will be this Friday when Alundra Blayze meets Sister Sherrie in an Itallion
Death match and the
winner gets Mimi! I guess we can see what Mimi think because joining me now in the very beutiful IWO Woman's champ Magnificent Mimi! Congradulations on the title victory! (Mimi is wearing a fire engine red sports bra with black shorts) TENA: I really love my job! MIMI: It feels wonderful to the IWO title, but it also feels incomplete. TENA: Incomplete? MIMI: Yes. I wasted the bimbo with a bimbo driver but didn't get a chance to strutt my stuff against a real opponent.
TENA: But Missy kept you going for well over a half hour! MIMI: Correction Mike, I kept her around to get the practice. But that's in the past. Now that I have the IWO title I will defend it against all comers, anytime in any type of match. This will be the woman's title to have. In fact I wish that other former champions like Wendi, Misty Blue,
Candie Devine, and Rockin Robin would come on over and then we'd see who the best woman
in wrestling really is. TENA: You sound as confident as you are beautiful. MIMI: (suggestively rubs her index fingure under Tena's chin) Why thank you Mike, but I doubt even you know how true that is. You see I've always been noted for my looks, stamina, and piledriver. But now I have completed working on something phrase, the total package of woman's wrestling.
TENAl What do you mean? MIMI: All I'll say for now is that I haven't had a solid submission hold in my arsenal. For the last few months I've been working with someone who is a master of a certain hold that I have now become quite good at.
TENA: Mimi tell us who this is and what hold are you talking about. MIMI: In time my dear. All I'll say is that I live the lphilosophy of my new mentor....To be the best you have to beat the best....and I am not only capable of fulfilling your every fantasy, I will use the ring to show that I am the best.
TENA: (speaking almost to himself) I wonder waht she has in store.... (30 seconds later) TENA: Now the Internet champ Diesel! DIESEL: Benoit, you little fart, I'm giving YOU a title shot this Friday.. In a casket steel cage match. To win, you've got to drag your opponent out of the ring and stuff him into the casket and close the lid.
TENA: Wow. Friday night it's IWO Prime Time Wrestling. Tag champs Beuty and the Beast meet Public Enemy, Alundra Blayze vs. Sister Sherrie in a
Itallion death match, and Internet champ Diesel vs. Chris Benoit in a casket, steel cage match! See you tomorrow!
