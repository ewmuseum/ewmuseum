Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A federation calling itself the International Wrestling Association went on the air in Canada in December 1999, its cards taped at Brebeuf College School and carried on Rogers Cable and Shaw Communications.

Its January 2000 broadcast preview looked back on five weeks of weekly tapings and forward to Only The Strong Survive, an eight-man tournament for the promotion's first World Heavyweight Championship, run by an on-screen president drawing names from a barrel: SmirtDogg against "187" Oscar Lopez, "Brash" Bobby Braxton against "Pretty Boy" Ramon Marteen, Johnathan Monarch against Lunatikk Crippler, and Haywood Jublome against Corey "The Cooker" Cookson.

The same preview carried a filmed vignette staging the top-ranked contender's mock takeover of a government office, one of several angles built to sell the coming tournament alongside a broadcast desk's own week-by-week analysis of the bracket.

The bare acronym IWA is shared with nineteen other holdings recorded elsewhere; none of their own hosts, rosters, or titles overlap this one's.

No further activity survives. The one preview held here is this promotion's earliest and only surviving document.
