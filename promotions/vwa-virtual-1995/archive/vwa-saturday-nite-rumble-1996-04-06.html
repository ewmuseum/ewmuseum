[Steeves] We'll also have the debut as a tag team of Mike Sweetser and The Terminator, plus the debut of a new superstar in the VWA, "Lightning" John Williams. [Summers] But let's go to the ring for the return of one of the biggest star of the VWA... [Bendix] This first match is schedule for one fall with a 20 minutes time limit. Introducing first, managed by Slick Rick, from the Bronx Zoo at 260 pounds: METHOD MAN !!! And his opponent: ["Don't Tread On Me" by Metallica starts up and the crowd pops big!!] representing the Dangerous Alliance, and accompanied to the ring by his manager, Paul E. Dangerously, from Albany, New York, weighing in at 239 pounds : DEREK HOLMES!!! [Holmes and Dangerously come down the aisle looking ready for business, and the crowd goes nuts as Holmes slaps hands on his way down.] Right from bell time, it showed that Holmes had learned a lot in ECW, but maybe not all the best things... He jumped on Method Man like a crazy maniac. He nailed him with a bulldog and followed it with a spin kick and a suplex. He whipped MM in the corner and rammed him shoulder first. He then executed a sling shot that sent MM flying outside the ring and then nailed him with a plancha. He then picked him up and suplex him into the crowd. He jumped over the railguard, stood up on a chair and leg dropped him from the chair. He brought him back to the ring and MM managed to make some sort of comeback. He nailed Holmes with a short clothesline and then executed an avalanche for the two counts. He picked him up and nailed him with a faceslam and a jawbreaker. MM then got Holmes in the Boston crab for about 30 seconds, but Holmes wouldn't submit. He finally grabbed the ropes and the ref had to break the hold. Method Man climbed on the top rope and went for a flying elbow, but Holmes got out of the way. Holmes got him in the Nightmares and after a few seconds MM had no choice but to submit, the ref rang the bell but Holmes wouldn't let go. Slick Rick jumped in the ring and started kicking at Holmes, but the kick didn't seem to have any effect on him. Finally after nearly two minutes, the ref rang the bell again and decided to reverse the decision, giving the match to Method Man by DQ. Holmes broke the hold and nailed Slick Rick with a clothesline before living the ring. [Steeves] I never thought I'd say this, but he's crazier than before. [Summers] I can't believe what we just saw, he had the match won, but kept on the hold so long that the ref had to disqualify him. [Steeves] Well, maybe I'm gonna start liking this guy. [Summers] Let's go right back to the ring for the debut of a new wrestler here in the VWA, but before, here's some pre-recorded comments ================================================================= [We are in the locker rooms before his match, Williams is taping up his ankles] "Well, my first match here in the VWA. Man, let me tell you I'm excited. I can't wait to get out there, in front of all those screaming fans, man, what a rush! Well, it's time for me to get out of here...see ya later, and remember, watch out for the Lightning." =================================================================== [Bendix after introducing Art Anemic] And his opponent: [Disarm from Smashing Pumpkins starts playing] from San Diego, CA at 253 pounds: "Lightning" JOHN WILLIAMS !!! [Williams came to the ring with black thighs with white lightning streaks down the lefthand side.] Williams ran down to the ring as the opening chords to his theme music blared over the speakers. He slapped hands with fans on his way to ringside, but once he got in the ring, you could tell his mind was on the task at hand. Art was really no match for John's quickness and strength in his legs as he quickly battered his opponent down. In the end, John reversed an Irish whip and nailed his opponent with a belly-to-belly suplex. And after John nailed Art with the Thunderstruck [Splash off the top rope], it was all over but the shouting, as the match ended at 0:05:45. [Summers] So, what do you think Chris ? [Steeves] Good debut, but Art Anemic is no Muta or Arn Anderson. I'll need to see more of this kid before I can make an honest judgment. [Summers] Why don't you say so now, if he beats Arn or Muta you're gonna hate him and if he loses... you're gonna hate him anyway. [Steeves] Right, you're beginning to know me. [Summer] Let's go back to Alan Bendix for our next match. [Bendix] This next match is set for one fall with a 20 minutes time limit. Introducing first: from Vancouver, British, Columbia, Canada, weighing in at 239 pounds : representing the Four Horsemen: CHRIS BENOIT!! [Benoit comes down the aisle, with a smug, yet serious look on his face.] And his opponent: from his family's private Island at 243 pounds: JAMIE CUTLASS !!! Cutlass nearly got the win two seconds into the match after a great fisherman suplex, but Benoit kicked out a two. He tried to continue his attack but Benoit got him in a German suplex followed by an elbowdrop and a headbutt. He continued with a dropkick to the jaw followed with a sleeperhold. Cutlass managed to reach to ropes after a few seconds. Benoit sent Cutlass in the ropes and went for a clothesline but Cutlass ducked and nailed him with a clothesline of his own. Cutlass then nailed Benoit with a back suplex followed by a slingshot bodyblock. He went for the pin but Benoit kicked out a two. After a few more minutes of fast pace action, Cutlass went for a Frankensteiner from the top ropes but Benoit blocked it and Cutlass fell on his head. Benoit then nailed him with a legdrop from the top for the pin at 12:05. Benoit left the ring, but it wasn't over for Cutlass as Tidal Wave came to the ring and nailed Cutlass with several "Storm Warning". Cutlass had to be carried away on a stretcher. [Summers] I can't believe Slick Rick's guys, they keep getting involve in everyone's match. President Hills's gotta to do something about this. [Steeves] Well, I think it's too late for Cutlass, I don't think we'll see him in a wrestling ring anytime soon. But let's not forget Benoit's win. [Summers] Yes, very impressive as usual, I wonder if he's back to single or if he's gonna continue to tag with Owen Hart ? [Steeves] Anyway, I think he won't be gold-less for much longer. [Summers] It's now time to go to Tony Marx with the Survival of the Fittest report. =========================================================== [Marx] Thanks a lot Tom. First I'd like to mention that the date of the PPV has been changed. It's now schedule for April 20, still at the American West Arena in Phoenix, Arizona. I now have the complete card for you: * Iron Man Match: Marty Jannetty vs. Kendo Godai for the VWA world title. This match has the potential to be even better than Shawn-Bret match at Wrestlemania. They did some damage on Ax but Smash finally managed to get in and both teams brawled for a while. Saturn was brawling in the ring with Ax and Kronos and Smash went at it outside the ring. Smash sent Kronos flying in the guardrail and after that bodyslammed him in the steps. In the ring, Saturn nailed Ax with a bulldog and followed it with an atomic drop. He nearly got the pin, but Smash came and kicked him in the back. The end finally came when Fuji gave his cane to Ax but Saturn grabbed it and nailed Ax with it. The ref called for the DQ at 10:06, but the action didn't stop there as both teams brawled all the way to the dressing rooms. [Steeves] That was fun, nobody told me it was Nuts-night here tonight, I would have brought a straight jacket for you Summers. [Summers] Very funny, let's go to our next match. After that, he sent WH flying outside the ring and nailed him with an ax-handle from the top rope to the floor. The match went back into the ring and BH nailed WH with some very surprising aerial maneuvers. BH went for the pin after a flying bodyblock but WH kicked out a two. BH went for an irish whip but WH reversed it and nailed BH with a superkick for the near fall. WH stayed on top for a few more minutes but BH finally got the win after he nailed WH with "The Reward" at 13:01 [Summers] It had been a long time since we last saw Hunter but he's back stronger then ever, [Steeves] He's not a Mounty, but he always gets his man, that song was really cool. Introducing first: ["Welcome to The Jungle" by GNR starts playing and the crowd starts cheering] from Detroit Michigan at 255 pounds: SCOTT STEINER !!! And his opponent: [Starseed by Our Lady Peace starts playing] from Vancouver, Canada at 317 pounds: RAINE TURNER !!! [Turner walked to the ring, with a very small dog wearing a headgear.] Turner attacked Steiner before the bell and whipped him in the turnbuckle and nailed him with a shoulderblock. He followed it with a faceslam and nearly got the pin after a side suplex. He went for a clawhold, but Steiner blocked it and nailed Turner with a fireman's carry. Steiner then nailed him with a dragon suplex and followed it with a snap mare. He went for the pin but Turner kicked out. Steiner kept the upper hand for the next couple minutes and nearly got the win after a Frankensteiner, but Turner managed to put his foot in the ropes. Scott thought he won the match and Turner snuck in from behind and nailed him with a Birdman, but before he had time to go for the pin, Method Man came to the ring and nailed him with brass knuckles. The ref had no choice but to DQ Steiner. Method Man and Raine Turner brawled for a while and finally Scott Steiner cleared the ring with a chair, nailing both Turner and Method Man. [Summers] Again, one of Slick Rick's man interfered and cost Scott the match. [Steeves] What do you mean ? Turner nailed him with a Birdman, he wouldn't have kicked out. [Summers] I think he would have, but we'll never know, anyway, President Hills's gotta do something about all those run-ins. And what about that dog, last week it was a rat and now a dog, does he own a pet shop ? [Steeves] It would be great for you, you'd have somewhere to sleep. [The annoying french song starts playing.] [Steeves] Shut up now Summers. [Parizo] Hi everybody and welcome to this week's Parizo's Chat. Now let's introduce my guest, he's gonna face the Aerial Marauder at Survival of the Fittest in a spin the wheel make the deal match: SAM HOLMES !!! [Parizo] Welcome here Sam, I'm very happy to have you here today. [Holmes] Yeah, whatever, boss. What do you have me here for? [Parizo] I want to know what you have to say about your match at SOTF. Aerial Marauder...you signed you own death warrant when you signed that contract, and I guess it's become my job to be the executioner. I hope you are really prpared for what you got yourself into, punk...[suddenly the crowd pops big as the Marauder walks down the aisle. He enters the ring and just stands there looking at Holmes with a face of stone.] [Parizo] Now what in the hell do you think- [The Marauder shoots a look at Parizo that shuts him right up. Parizo slowly puts the mic over to the Marauder, hand visibly shaking.] [Marauder, with a look on his face the crowd has never seen on his face before, a deathly calm] Well, well Sammy, the last time we were here, you, the Pilsbury Dough Boy over there, and that little girl Mario Ducon tried to end my career for the first time. Damn shame...didn't work then..or any of the other times you and Parzio's clan have tried to take me out. But know you have made the biggest mistake of your life fool because once that wheel is spun, your fate is sealed, and your career will end at my hands. [Holmes] [gets closer to Marauder. Let's remember one thing...*I*... am the one that asked for this match.....I *begged* Hills to have this one, and to make sure you got in. And last, but most certainly not least....*I* am the one that set up those precious matches for the wheel. [talks even softer, almost to the point of a whisper] Buddy boy.....you just got yourself in a whole mess of hurtin'. [Marauder] That's where your wrong Sammy, because your are looking at the man who has dealt you the most pain that you have ever felt, but that is NOTHING compared to the agony you will go through! [Holmes] Spare me....you talk as though I should be shaking in my boots, because the biig baaaad Aerial Marauder is all pissed off at me and is havin' delusions that he can kick my ass.
