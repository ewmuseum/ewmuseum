VWA Satruday Nite Rumble (9/14/96)

14 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Announcer] Before we begin the broadcast, we have a word from VWA executive Adam Thurman involving this show. [Thurman] Thank you. On behalf of the VWA we would like to to convey our deepest regrets about the world wide blackout of last week's Saturday Nite Rumble. We experienced a number of technical problems that would not let us get the broadcast out on time. But fear not, the appropiate amount of yelling, cursing, ruthless firing of employees, and slaughtering of totally innocent people has been done! So here is last weeks edition The Rumble...Enjoy. [Cut to the massive (Two Rumbles for the price of one...ain't life great :) VWA Arena. Your announcing crew of champions is ready to go, so let's get started shall we?] [Summers] WELCOME TO SATURDAY NIGHT RUMBLE!! And do we ever have a show for you tonight!! Barry Williams will begin the path that may lead him to the VWA World title as he faces Chris Benoit.... [Steeves] Only thing that Chris Benoit is going to lead Williams to is the ER! [Summers] We also have some great tag team action coming up as The Masters of Destruction face Demolition in a non-title match! [Hennig] Demolition had better be careful tonight, the Glamour Boyz are in the house tonight...and I believe they will do *anything* to get those North American titles back! Beating on Demolition before they even make it to Fall Fling could be a good start! [Summers] Hey, anything could happen in the VWA! [Steeves] Tell me you didn't just say that! Don't you think Vince has enough problems with copyright infringement? [Summers] Good point. Well, let's get some comments from the teams involved in our first match! It's Too Many Cooks versus the Glamour Boyz! ============================================================================= [Rick D] Glamour Boys, here we go again. I think things are even between us now. [Danny G] We beat you in the Survivor Series match a while ago but you beat us in our last match. [Rick D] So I guess this one will settle it once and for all. [Danny G] Wouldn't be bad for you to lose just before your big title match against your friends Demolition... [Rick D] Well we'll do our best to spoil your party, we'll bring you to Decadence and your chance of winning the NA titles are gonna be Turning to Stone and who knows it may well turn into a Food Fight !!! [Danny G] Any way, we're ready for you and it's gonna Rock !!! ------------------- <scene opens on the Glamour Boys in front of the VWA backdrop [Stone] Well, well, well, looks like we got those Too Many Cooks again this week on Rumble. [Martin] Boys, don't you ever give up? Did we not beat you bad enough last time that you still want more? [Duety] I think they just want beauty tips, Rols. I mean look at their ugly mugs, they definately need them!! [Martin] Well, they should at least be an easy tuneup for Demolition at Fall Fling when we take our belts back. [Duety] You know, at least the Demos have enough sense to hide their mugs behind all the paint, and that's part of the reason we like them so much. [Martin] There's a saying Demos about their being a code of honor among thieves. Well, maybe there is, but when you're as pretty as the Glamour Boys are, you have to abide by no codes!! [Duety] Admitted it is not going to be easy cause Demolition are tough customers. But, we're prettier and we're better and those belts were ours to begin with. [Stone] Don't my boys look so good? =========================================================================== [Bendix] This tag team contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first.... ("Satisfaction" by the Rolling Stones starts up, and the crowd starts cheering!)....from the streets of New York City....at a total combined weight of 533 pounds.....Rick D.......Danny G.......TOO MANY COOKS!!!!!! Their opponents....("Glamour Boys" by Living Colour starts up, and the crowd starts booing loudly, as well as some hardcore cheers, and cat calls for Moira Stone :)......accompanied to the ring by Moira Stone.......at a total combined weight of 497 pounds.....Ronnie Martin....Steven Duety...... THE GLAMOUR BOYS!!!!! Too Many Cooks went right after the Glamour Boys right when the bell rang. Rick sent Duety to the outside, and the two nailed Martin with a double front-layout suplex, but Martin managed to kick out in time. TMC showed a great amount of double-teaming and frequent tags on Martin. Martin managed to break the momentum with a rake to the eyes and a butterfly suplex on Danny, but couldn't make it over to his partner in time. Martin took the brunt of the attack for a good three minutes before he finally made the tag to Duety. Duety reversed an Irish whip into the corner, and followed up with a nasty dropkick right to the face of Danny G! Danny was in visible pain, as he clutched his face, and Duety unloaded on him with a plethera of moves. Danny was in serious trouble when he was nailed with a fishermanbuster from the top rope, but Rick saved him from getting pinned with a quick kick to Duety's head. Martin began to recover, and the Glamour Boys started to show their tag team abilities, by using a combination of double-team moves, quick tags, and performing illegal moves behind the ref's back. Rick managed to get tagged back in, but with his partner effectively out of it, there was no one to back him up, and he was nailed with the Glamour Press in 10:12. [Steeves] And the Glamour Boyz prove beyond a shadow of doubt that pretty beats ugly every single time! [Hennig] I hope your right because they don't come much uglier then Demolition! If the Glamour Boyz aren't careful the champs could make them as disgusting looking as that thing that tried to pick up on you last night! [Steeves] HEY! She....I mean he....I mean IT...couldn't help itself! It knew true beauty when it saw it! [Summers] Gentlemen *please*! This is a family show! [Steeves] Since when? [Summers] Ok, maybe your right. But let's not worry about that, let's talk about our next match as the Terminator faces Sam Holmes of the Four Horseman! [Hennig] Sam has gotten that vicious edge back that he had when he first joined Parzio's Clan. He has been on a serious roll as of late, and is turning out to be a perfect Horseman! [Steeves] Yeah, but the Terminator is still going to kill him! [Bendix] This contest is scheduled for one fall!! Introducing first....("Grind" by Alice in Chains" starts up...and the crowd starts booing loudly, while others throw up the four fingers).....representing the Four Horsemen.... from Albany, New York....weighing in at 269 pounds......."Demolition Man" SAM HOLMES!!!!! His opponent.......(Diesel's old theme song starts up..and the crowd starts booing even louder).....accompanied to the ring by Harley Race.......from Los Angeles, California....weighing in at 343 pounds........THE TERMINATOR!!!! These two men certainly do not like to waste time in the ring, and they made no exception against each other, as they went all out on one another from the get go. The two traded several axe-like blows before Terminator gained the upper hand with a sidewalk slam. He followed with a big leg drop, but Sam kicked out at two. Terminator then whipped the Horseman hard into the corner, following with a nasty clothesline! He repeated the move, but this time Sam rolled out of the way, and nailed the big man with a series of forearm shots right to the temple. He whipped Terminator out of the corner and into a short-arm clothesline, but Terminator managed to kick out at two. Holmes dumped Terminator to the outside and threw up the four fingers, which drew some great heat from the crowd. Holmes went to the outside to grab Terminator, but Terminator caught him by suprise by grabbing him and sending him face first into the guardrail. Holmes looked to be out of it, as Terminator rolled him back inside, but Holmes did manage to kick out at the last second. The next few minutes were all Terminator, as he did what few people have managed to do...dominate over Sam Holmes. Holmes was run through a gauntlet of power moves left and right, but he still managed to kick out every time. Terminator whipped Holmes into the ropes, but Holmes caught him by suprise with a kick to the gut and a *nasty* powerbomb! Holmes collapsed beside his opponent, as the ref began the ten count. Holmes managed to get on his feet first, grabbed the Terminator, and did what no one could have thought possible: he nailed Terminator with the C-4 powerbomb and got the three count at 17:27! A few minutes later, Terminator regained conciousness, and the two had a big staredown. The two exchanged words, and then turned around and walked off without another word to each other. [Summers] What a upset!! Sam Holmes has pinned the Terminator!! [Hennig] I wouldn't call it that. Sam Holmes is a former North American champ. Plus he is in one of the greatest wrestling groups in the world in the Horsemen! But what I wonder, is just what were those two talking about? [Steeves] They were probably trading ideas on how to put the Marauder back in the hospital! HAHAHAHAHA! [Summers] Very cute Steeves. Let's head back to the ring so we can see Alex indham make his singles debut against Rey Misterio Jr.! [Bendix] This contest is scheduled for one fall! ("More Human Than Human" starts up).. Introducing first.....from Denver, Colorado.....weighing in at 239 pounds.... ALEX WINDHAM!!!! His opponent.....from Tiajuana, Mexico...("Higher Ground" by Red Hot Chili Peppers starts up, and the crowd goes nuts!).....weighing in at 190 pounds... he is the WCW Cruiserweight champion.....REY MISTERIO, JUNIOR!!!!!! This was a very fast-paced match, as one would expect. Rey dominated for the first few minutes by catching the former World Tag Team champ with a flurry of high-risk moves. Windham was subject to several of Rey's trademark Hurracan ranas, but managed to kick out after each pin attempt. He managed to ground the Cruiserweight champ with a beautifully timed dropkick after Rey went for a top-rope splash! He quickly grabbed Rey and delivered a nasty backbreaker, but Rey kicked out at one. Rey was sent to the outside, and Windham caught him with a beautiful ropeflip senton. He whipped Rey to the guardrail, but Rey leapt up and moonsaulted off the guardrail right onto Windham! Both men rolled back in, and Rey quickly rolled him up, but got only a two count. Windham revrsed an Irish whip and caught Rey with a belly to belly suplex that would make Shane Douglas proud! He followed up with a Frog Splash, but again Rey kicked out in time. He set Rey on the top turnbuckle, and went for a belly to belly superplex, but Rey managed to push Windham off, and he nailed Windham with his Frankensteiner-into a pin move that got the three count at 7:45. [Steeves] Maybe Windham should go back to tag team wrestling! That way he wouldn't get his ass kicked alone! [Hennig] Hey, nobody has beaten Rey since he came to the federation...Alex Windham just found out why! [Summers] We have a *very* interesting match coming up next as Horseman Chris Benoit faces the man who will battle for the VWA World title in just a few weeks...Barry Williams! [Bendix] This contest is scheduled for one fall! ("Until It Sleeps" by Metallica starts up, and a hardcore cheer starts up) Introducing first...representing the Four Horsemen......from Edmonton, Alberta, Canada....weighing in at 239 pounds...."The Canadian Crippler" CHRIS BENOIT!!!!!!!! His opponent (The jazzed up version of the olympic theme starts up, and the crowd starts cheering loudly!).....from Kansas City, Missouri....weighing in at 265 pounds....."Whiplash" BARRY WILLIAMS!!!!!! Williams didn't look extremely focused for this match for some reason, and Benoit tried to take advantage of that with a clothesline and then whipped Williams into the ropes for a elbowsmash! Benoit threw up the four fingers then started to stomp away at Williams as he was slumped in the corner! Those kicks seemed to clear the head of Williams as he reversed a irish whip into a back body drop. Then put Benoit a nice German Suplex! Williams landed a boot to the midsection, and then followed that up with a knee lift right to the face of the Crippler. Williams went to the top rope for a splash, but Benoit got the knees up, and then caught the slumped over Williams with a powerbomb! Benoit climbed to the top and nailed a flying headbutt but Williams was just barely able to kick out! Benoit looked frustrated and tried to end it all by setting Williams on the top rope for a superplex! But as Benoit climbed to the second rope Williams began to punch away like mad on Benoit's face, then he grabbed Benoit and leaped off the ropes in a move that could only be called a top rope *spinebuster*! Benoit started clutching his back, and then Williams decided to make the situation even worse by landed a twirl-a-whirl backbreaker! Williams then grabbed Benoit's head and landed a beautiful dropkick right to the skull! Williams then put Benoit in his patent tornado piledriver for the win at 17:56! [Bendix] This contest is scheduled for one fall!! Introducing first....("You Could be Mine" by Guns-n-Roses starts up, and the crowd starts booing loudly)....from parts unknown....weighing in at 253 pounds......THE BOUNTY HUNTER!!!!! His opponent....("The Unforgiven" by Metallica starts up, and the crowd jumps to their feet!).....from the great state of Minnesota....weighing in at 260 pounds....."The Enforcer" ARN ANDERSON!!!!! Bounty Hunter tried to start the party off by jumping Anderson before the bell, but Anderson caught him coming in with a right hand, and then a headbutt. Anderson came in after the Bounty Hunter but BH was able to grab him by his tights and throw him outside the ring! BH threw Anderson into the steps *hard* and then picked up the entire staircase and dropped it on Anderson! BH tried to irish whip Anderson into the railing...but Anderson reversed it...then BH reversed it again and dropped Anderson with a short clothesline! BH moved Anderson back in the ring and rammed him chest first into the turnbuckle. BH then started to slap Anderson while he was in the corner, but all this did was make Arn made, and he responded by moving BH in the corner and slapping him. BH charged out the corner at Anderson, but Anderson quickly dropped to his knee and landed a fist in the midsection...which made The Bounty Hunter a prime target for a DDT and the win at 11:44! [Steeves] DAMN! The Bounty Hunter lost, does this mean he doesn't get paid? [Hennig] Well he said he doesn't get paid by the win, just by the number of times he hits people with his brass knuckles! And judging by the way he's sneaking up on Arn right now, I would say it's pay day! *WHAM* [Summers] WHAT A SHOT BY THE BOUNTY HUNTER!! Anderson is knocked out!! And now the Bounty Hunter is headed to the back! [Steeves] Probably to pick up his check! HAHAHAHA! [Summers] Well, while we get help for Anderson, let's get some comments teams in our next match, Raine Turner and Ugly Kid Joe versus Rock Shock! ============================================================================= [UKJ] The returning Rockshock... It's been a while, where have you been ? [Turner] Hiding [UKJ] Well you didn't choose the right guys to come out of hiding to fight... We're on a roll and it's not you guys that are gonna stop us. [Turner] By the way, Cool, Gundy, you have a problem with me ? Why don't you team up and meet me and Joe. [UKJ] Yeah, the more the merrier... ---------------------- The camera cuts to Rockshock standing in front of a black background with the red ROCKSHOCK logo scripted across it. Ron is a large, blonde man, standing around 6'7" tall with muscular frame, although he is not so bulked up as to seriously inhibit his agility. Jack is somewhat smaller, standing approximately 6'5" tall, and has a quicker and more agile build than his partner. They are wearing their usual ring-attire. Black leather jackets with ROCKSHOCK in red on the back, red tights and black wrestling boots. The Rockshock experience is about to hit town once again! Who are we goin' up against tonight, Ron? [Ron] Ugly Kid Joe and Raine Turner. [Jack] Aaah, yeah. That's right. Well, guys, you're about to find out what Rockshock is all about! We're gonna rock ya and shock ya! And when all is said and done we're gonna drop ya in yer tracks! [Ron] Let me put this in simple terms you two can understand: You play nice, and so will we. If you _don't_ play nice... well, then all bets are _off_ and you two better reserve a couple of beds in the nearest hospital. [Jack] No more Mr. nice guy in other words. Piss us off and pay the price. You've recieved fair warning. We do not intend to stand here and take any shit from _any_ team in the VWA this time around. Mess with us and we'll _mess_ you up! [Ron] We're back! We're better than ever! And we're hungry for gold! You two are the first step on the ladder leading to that gold! Prepare to be stepped on, cause tonight there's gonna be some rockin'! [Jack] There's gonna be some rollin'! [Ron] Some high flyin'! [Jack] And definitely some _hard_ slammin'! [Ron] And in the end, Raine and Joe, in the end Rockshock is gonna get some... [Jack & Ron] ....SATISFACTION!! The camera cuts back to the broadcast table. ========================================================================== [Bendix] This tag team contest is scheduled for one fall!!! ("Satisfaction" by The Rolling Stones starts up, and the crowd starts cheering!) Introducing first.. from Chicago, Illinois....at a total combined weight of 535 pounds...."Jumping" Jack Flash....."Rolling" Ron Stone....ROCKSHOCK!!!!! Their opponents.....("Our Lady Peace" by Starseed starts up and the crowd starts booing loudly).....at a total combined weight of 586 pounds....the team of RAINE TURNER and UGLY KID JOE!!!!! Raine Turner began the match versus Jack Flash. Flash jumped on the offensive path with a hiptoss, then Turner ran in only to be caught by a leg sweep by Flash. Flash then tagged in Stone and together they rocked Turner with a double dropkick! Stone tried to continue on the attack but Raine was able to get a eye rack in, and then hit Stone with a inverted atomic drop. Turner tagged in Joe, and then held Stone while UKJ went to the second rope and landed a fist right to the eye of Stone! Joe planted a legdrop on Stone, and then went for a vertical suplex...but Stone was able to block and land with a suplex of his own!! Stone made the hot tag to Flash who came in pounding away at Joe, and then irish whipped him into the ropes and dropkick him straight out of the ring. Flash leaped over the top rope with a plancha...but what he didn't see was Raine Turner sneaking up on him and blasting him in the head with a chair! Turner and Joe began to double team Flash outside the ring until Stone came over with a chair of his own and started handing out shots!! The ref was finally able to get the two legal men (Flash and Joe) back into the ring, and it was obvious that RockShock was real pissed...Flash Stun Gunned Joe, and then caught him with a brutal belly to back suplex. Flash tagged in Stone, who came in but was caught by a low blow by UKJ. The tag was made to Raine Turner, but Stone was waiting for him with a but to the midsection followed by a swinging neckbreaker. Stone bounced against the ropes, but as he did Joe nailed him with a forearm to the back of his neck. Stone was stunned just long enough to catch Raine's BirdMan finisher for the win at 25:11! [Steeves] Looks like RockShock get's no satsifaction tonight!! But I'm beating that Ron Stone is getting a hell of a headache right now!! [Summers] Well in spite of that it was a great tag match. Both these teams will be looking to get their hands on either the world champs The Midnight Express or the North American champs...Demolition! And speaking of the Demos, they are up next in a non-title match against the Masters of Destruction!! Let's get some words from both teams! ============================================================================= (Scene opens to Raekwon and Method Man in their dressing room) Raekwon: Demoliton, will be demolished this week when we crush them in our little tag match, tell em Method Man! Method Man: That's right punks, you two know we will destroy you that's why you guys were chicken to put the damn titles on the line! Well, after we beat you, you'll know who the men are and that's us! (Fade To Black) ----------------------- (Cut to Torch, Burn, and Ax in front of the VWA backdrop.) [Ax] It's about goddamn *time* that we got ourselves a match on Saturday Night Rumble! I'm starting to think that President Hils..he doesn't *want* my boys as the North Am tag champs....so he's making sure that my boys are so rusty that they'll be easy pickings. Well, I'll tell ya what... thes guys here aren't as soft as you'd hope! [Torch] See, it's real simple. We haven't be wrestling, sure. But we've been training. We've been going at it for the past month to make sure that these belts stay *right* where they are! It doesn't matter who we will be facing... even you, Glamour Boys. Business is business....and as far as I'm concerned, right now...we ain't friends. We're bitter rivals...you're the threat to the titles, and we're the exterminators. [Burn] Before *that* match we got the so-called "Masters of Destruction"! Well, lemme tell you something, kiddies....you are looking at the *originators*... the *real* destruction crew!! So, you bring all you want...but when you step in the ring with us...you'll know first-hand what a demolition derby is *really* like! ============================================================================= [Bendix] This non-title tag team match is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first... accompanied to the ring by their manager, Slick Rick.....at a total combined weight of 645 pounds....Raekwon....Method Man.....THE MASTERS OF DESTRUCTION!!!! Their opponents.....("Tonight We Murder" by Ministry starts up, and the crowd starts booing loudly..).....accompanied to the ring by their manager, Ax...from parts unknown...at a total combined weight of 597 pounds.....they are the VWA North American Tag Team champions.....Torch...Burn......DEMOLITION!!!!! Torch started the match off versus Method Man. It became obvious early that this wasn't going to be a very pretty match as the two started the match by exchanging right hands in the center of the ring. Method Man took the advantage when the ducked on of the punches and and caught Torch with a chop to the throat. Method then showed some strength as he got Torch in a shoulder breaker. Torch reversed a whip into the ropes and caught Method in a gorilla press! Torch made the tag to Burn and together they started pounding away on the back of Method Man. Then they whipped Method into the ropes and went for a double backdrop, but Method saw it coming and bashed their heads together! Method made the tag to Raekwon, and he put Burn in a bearhug! Burn used a eye rake to break the hold then went against the ropes and kneelifted Raekwon. Burn whipped Method to the ropes and went for a cross body block, but Raekwon caught him and executed a fall away slam! Raekwon picked Burn up and went for a KO punch, but Burn blocked it and caught Raekwon with a forearm smash. Burn made the tag to Torch who caught Raekwon with a shoulder block. Then we went for a clothesline but Raekwon ducked and caught Burn with a chokeslam! Raekwon tagged in Method Man who climbed up to the top and hit Burn with a leg drop. Method then put on his Ticallion Stallion finisher...but Torch ran in to make the save! Raekwon came in to face Torch and all four men started brawling in the ring. Burn threw Method Man outside the ring, and then Torch caught Raekwon in a running powerslam!! Torch and Burn started to set up for the Decapitation, but as Burn went to the top Slick Rick jumped on the apron to distract the ref. Burn hit Raekwon with the Decapitation but the ref was too busy arguing with Slick Rick to count the three!! This gave time for Method Man to sneak back in the ring and hit Burn with the ring bell and lay Raekwon on top of him! The ref then turned around to count, but Ax leaped in the ring and hit Raekwon!! Too bad for them the ref saw this, and DQ Demolition, giving the Masters of Destruction the win at 19:06. [Summmers] I can't believe that!! Slick Rick did all that interfering for his team, but when Demolition's manager Ax tries to help, all of a sudden they are DQ'ed! [Hennig] Guess that ref must go to the Nick Patrick School of Officiating! [Steeves] Hennig, I got two words for you and trying to ref a match...Lex Luger! Now it's not Slick Rick's fault his team is better! And speaking of Slick Rick, we get to see even *more* of him as his TV champ Tidal Wave goes up against "Four Horsemen whoppin' boy" Sandman Jr.! Let's hear from the champ! ============================================================================= (Scene opens up to Tidal Wave and Slick Rick; Tidal Wave has the VWA title wrapped around his shoulder) Tidal Wave: This week I got Sandman Jr. This should be another easy victory for me. I have went through so many people since I got here to the VWA. For about 2 or 3 months now, I've been VWA TV Champ with no end in sight. Sandman, you obviously don't know who you are dealing with punk, I am the elite one here in the VWA and no one will take that away from me and my title for that matter! Slick Rick: My man will destroy you Sandman then take your remains and destroy it too! You wanted a shot at the TV title, well now you got it punk! Don't go crying to anyone when you get crippled because it's your fault for thinking you can handle my Tidal Wave. And when he beats you like the dog that you are, he'll just be another step closer to being the greatest TV Champ in VWA history! But since Tidal Wave is 200 pounds bigger then Sandman, needless to say his hurt a bit more. Tidal Wave moved Sandman into the corner and started ramming the shoulder into him. Sandman went for the pin but Tidal Wave foot was on the ropes! Sandman put Tidal Wave in the corner and started kicking him *hard* in the midsection!! Then Sandman executed a beautiful handspring elbow!!! The crowd started going nuts as Sandman went for a bulldog. But Tidal Wave was able to throw Sandman off him, and into the turnbuckle. Sandman staggered out the corner and was caught with a sidewalk slam by Tidal Wave for the win at 11:32! [Summers] WHAT A MATCH!! But once again Tidal Wave retains his title! [Steeves] Hey, maybe Sandman would have had a change if he back wasn't still stinging from the caning he got from the Horseman! HAHAHAHAHA! [Summers] Very funny...village idiot! [Hennig] Hey!
