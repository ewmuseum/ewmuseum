What a *Clash*!!! [Steeves] Aaah.....shaddup. [Summers] Don't mind him..he's just upset that we have a new World champion. [Steeves] Hell..we have new champions all over! [Summers] True enough. We will be seeing a couple of the participants Saturday night here tonight! Plus we will have on Parizo's Chat...provided he's still *alive* after what happened at the Clash.. [Steeves] Shut up. [Summers] ..interview all four of the Horsemen..two of them being the new VWA North American tag champs: Owen Hart and Chris Benoit! [Steeves] And they couldn't have made a better choice, than the "King of Harts," in my opinion! [Summers] Plus the TV title is once again up for grabs, as the champion, Jamie Cutlass, faces the master of the BirdMan..Raine Turner. [Steeves] *That* will not be a pretty match. [Bendix] This tag team contest is scheduled for one fall! ["Frayed Ends of Sanity" starts playing.] Introducing first...from parts unknown...at a total combined weight of 1004 pounds....Wacko...Berserk...THE PSYCHO TWINS!!!!!! [Steeves] 1004 *POUNDS*????!!!! [Summers] Yep. Big boys, aren't they? [Steeves] They could eat up half the delis in New York City! [Summers] Yeah..probably. [Steeves] No...*Literally*! I think they could eat up half of the delis that are in New York! [Bendix] Their opponents...from the streets of New York City ["Bastard" by Strip Mind starts up.] at a total combined weight of 743 pounds...Brian Knobbs....Jerry Sags.....THE NASTY BOYS!!!!! [Steeves] Pew! For God's sake..when was the last time they *washed*?? As usual, the Twins were very ready for the match and they both jumped on the Nasty Boys before the bell rang. All 4 guys brawled for a couple minute before the ref could take control and actually start the match. The advantage switched side many times and the Nasty Boys did a lot of damage on Wacko, preventing him from tagging with Berserk. They nearly got the pin after a double body slam but Berserk came in and stopped the count. Finally after almost 10 minutes in the ring, Wacko managed to tag Berserk and he cleaned house, taking on both Knobbs and Sags. The Twins won the match at 24:23 after Wacko pinned Sags following a Straight Jacket. [Steeves] Wow...they were definately psycho in *this* match! I've never seen the Nasty Boys manhandled like they were! [Summers] I'd say! I can see some title shots coming their way soon enough! Up next..we have the return of one of the biggest superstars in professional wrestling today... [Steeves] Aw no...not him! [Summers] Yep. 'Ol HB is back with a vengeance!! We saw *that* at the Royal Rumble when he won the Rumble for the second time in a row! [Steeves] Uh-huh....but the brain damage is still there, as he is putting that WWF title shot on the line just to face Owen Hart! [Summers] He is definately gonna have his hands full with the newest Horseman to be sure..no matter *which* fed he faces him at! [Bendix] This next contest is scheduled for one fall....[The Mortal Kmobat theme starts up] Introducing first....from parts unknown..weighing in at 270 pounds...PRIMETIME PUNISHER!!! [Steeves] Prime-time.....prime-time...... [Summers] Hah! As much as I hate Deion...*he* at least has a winning record. [Steeves] Yeah..but I think this is Punisher's time..I have one of those feelings. [Bendix] His opponent ["Addicted to Love" by Robert Palmer starts up and the crowd pops big!!]...from San Antonio, Texas...weighing in at 246 pounds... SHAWN MICHAELS!!!!!!! [Summers] He's baaaaaaack. [Steeves] Anyone else here hope he gets jumped again? [Summers] No. Punisher tried to attack Michaels before the bell, but HBK saw it coming and nailed him with a clothesline. Punisher managed to keep his balance but Michaels had other plans for his evening and didn't want to waste time with PP. So he went for some chin music and got the pin at 0:56 following his Superkick. [Summers] So...ya have one of those feelings, eh McMahon? [Steeves] Shut up. [Summers] Anyway...the former North American Tag Champs..the Glamour Boys..aren't wasting too much time getting back into action as they face a team that has definately been racking up the wins as of late here..the Devastators! [Bendix] This tag team match is scheduled for one fall...Introducing first.. accompanied to the ring by their valet..."Magnificent" Moira Stone.. ["The Glamour Boys" by Living Colour starts up.] at a total combined weight of 497 pounds...from Indianapolis, Indiana.."Studly" Steven Duety... Ronnie "Rolls Royce" Martin...THE GLAMOUR BOYS!!! [Summers] Oooh..they don't look happy at all. [Steeves] They lost the North American tag belts to the Horsemen in front of a national audience...wouldn't *you* be slightly peeved? [Bendix] Their opponents.... from Denver, Colorado....at a total combined weight of 520 pounds.. Alex Windham..Scott Romero....THE DEVASTATORS!!! [Summers] They look ready...they should be getting their chance at some tag gold soon! [Steeves] Right. Not in *this* millenia. The Glamour boys are not in a good mood. Those Rocky Mountaineers aren't gonna last the night. The Boys were very upset about their title loss and decided to make the Devastators pay for it. Martin started the match against Windham and with a little help by Duety, got the upper hand in the early match. When Romero made his way to the ring, the pace shifted and the Devastators got a couple near falls. At this point, the Boys started bumping each other. It was obvious that they didn't think straight and they made a fatal mistake when Martin held Windham so Stone could hit him with her purse, Windham got out of the way and Stone nailed Martin with the purse. It was an easy pin for Windham and the Devastators got the victory at 12:39. [Summers] You were saying? [Steeves] *sigh*..and the last show was so much better. What happened to all the beautiful women? Not that Ms. Stone isn't beautiful, of course. [Summers] Well..talking about tag teams...it looks like we have some bad news concerning the former World Tag team champs, the Travis Brothers...let's take you to some words from Sam Travis before he faces Sid Vicious next. ------------------------------------ [Cut to a backdrop with the VWA logo on it. Sam Travis comes into shot and does not look pleased.] [Travis] I'll tell you one thing right off de bat, mes amis...I am pissed off royally. *Not* only..did me and my bro get *ripped* off...but them brass knucks that Steven Clark used on my bro gave him a nasty concussion..so he's gonna be out for a month or two. Right now.. belts don' mean much to me...gettting even wit' the guys that took my bro out of action is. So..no matter what happens next between me and Vicious..I comin' fer ya, Connection. ------------------------------------ [Bendix] This contest is scheduled for one fall...["Sabotage" by the Beastie Boys starts playing]...Introducing first...from Memphis, Tennessee.. weighing in at 320 pounds...BIG SID VICIOUS!!!!!! [Steeves] Aw..so Tony couldn't take the pressure, huh? [Summers] Right now..from what I understand..he can't even remember his name. So, I don't think his brother is gonna be in a good mood for a while. His opponent...[AC/DC's "Dirty Deeds Done Dirt Cheap" starts up and the crowds starts cheering.]...from New Orleans, Louisiana...weighing in at 289 pounds....SAM TRAVIS!!!! [Steeves] You're right, Tommy boy...Sam looks pissed. [Summers] I think he wants the Connection with a passion right now..Hopefully he won't overlook Vicious in this match though. Travis is more used to tag team and it showed in that match. Still, he got an early advantage using his speed and brawling skill to get Big Sid of his feet. He nailed him with some flying forearm, bodyslam and some big suplex. Vicious then reversed an irish whip and nearly took Sam head off. He went for the powerbomb later on but Sam reversed it onto a backdrop and then went for the rope and tried a flying bodypress but Sid blocked it and turned it into a power slam for the pin at 8:41. [Steeves] Yep. He is so wrapped up with getting the World Tag champs..he's overlooking the fact that he is not in tag team action right now. You overlook..you get beat. Pretty simple, really. [Bendix] This next contest is scheduled for one fall...["Gangsta's Paradise" by Coolio starts up]..from the valley of the shadow of death....weighing in at 317 pounds..."Gangsta Power" SABIN FIGARO!!! [Steeves] "The valley of the shadow of death?" Where the *hell* is that?? [Summers] Got me. Doesn't exactly sound like a garden spot to me though. [Bendix] His opponent...from London, England...[harpsichord music starts playing.] weighing in at 375 pounds....RANO!!!! [Summers] Jeez....how many freaking bluebloods do we *have* here?? [Steeves] What's wrong with that? They're bringing a bit of culture back to this profession, is all. [Summers] Class? In professional *wrestling*??? It was a match between two very tall men, Rano at 6'5'' and Figaro at 6'6''. Both guys tried to get an early advantage and impose their style to the other. Figaro showed his vast knowledge in martial arts with some great moves. He nearly got the pin after an enziguri. Rano took the advantage later on using his strength and weight advantage to wear Figaro down. He managed to get Figaro in a scorpion deathlock for about a minute before Figaro grabbed the rope. Figaro tried a comeback and has he was about to start his Bam Rush Punching Fit, Rano went outside for a breather. Figaro followed him down and was slammed in the guardrail for his trouble. Has they both went back in the ring, Rano got the pin following a jack-knife power bomb at 15:15. [Steeves] Funky. Rano can beat you in so many ways! Y'know..Rano has only one loss so far..to Barry Williams. [Summers] That's true..he's been so dominating in his other matches..you have to wonder when he will try to get his revenge on Williams. [Steeves] Dunno. Hope it's soon. I hate that guy. 'Course..Rano's gonna have to wait his turn, cause Arn Anderson wants a shot at Williams again, from what I hear. [Summers] We'll find out soon enough, when the Horsemen appear on Parizo's Chat later. Up next though...we're gonna see the return of another superstar in the VWA..The Aerial Marauder!! [Steeves] Whoopee...I know *I* can hardly contain myself...at least he's gonna have his hands full when he faces Raekwon! [Bendix] This contest is scheduled for one fall....[The theme to "Psycho" starts up.] Introducing first.....accompanied to the ring by his manager, Slick Rick.. from parts unknown...weighing in at 350 pounds...RAEKWON!!!!! [Steeves] I love this guy! He just has this bad-ass attitude about him..like he could kick the ass of every single guy here in the VWA! [Summers] Well..his win-loss record could be better, but he just arrived not that long ago..we'll see how well he can face a guy the caliber of the Marauder! [Steeves] On the other hand..you gotta think that Mr. Caffiene can't be at 100% capacity yet..he's gotta still have some ring rust in him. [Bendix] His opponent..["Time for some Action" by Redman starts up, and the crowd gets to its feet!] from parts unknown..weighing in at 244 pounds..THE AERIAL MARAUDER!!!!! [Marauder runs down the aisle and slides into the ring to attack Raekwon.] Marauder was in great shape and nobody would have guess the he had just spent nearly a month in the hospital. He started the match with a devastating attack, flying forearm, flying clothesline, dropkicks. Raekwon went outside for a breather but Marauder jumped on him from inside the ring with a plancha. He then went on to attack Slick Rick who tried to sneak on him from behind. A couple minute went by in which the Aerial Marauder stayed in control and at one point Raekwon managed to throw Marauder outside the ring and while Raekwon was distracting the ref, out of nowhere came Mario Ducon and he nailed him right in the face with steel chair. Ducon left as fast as he came and the ref finished the 10 count, giving the victory to Raekwon by count-out. [Steeves] HA! I love it!!! You were saying something about Raekwon's win-loss record? [Summers] Marauder's not gonna be happy when he comes to. He's already up and he running backstage!! Oh wait...I being told we have a tape to run.. here it is.. -------------------------------- [A tall man with a green hood over his head, so we can't see his face, stands alone in some kind of jungle...] Ah... The jungle... It's the only place where I really feel like home. Here with all my friends, those animals... only them really understand me. HAHAHA !!! I hear the VWA is beginning to look like a Jungle... A lot of people are getting involved in other peoples' business... I like that !!! HAHAHA !!! I like to mess with other peoples' life, messing with their heads... HAHAHA !!! Simple minded, that's what you are VWA wrestlers. I might have a lot of fun with you all... Yeah, I'll have a lot of fun... HAHAHA !!! --------------------------------------- [Steeves] *What* the *hell* was that?? [Summers] Dunno....weird guy. I wonder when *he's* gonna show? [Steeves] Soon, I hope..I wanna see what he looks like! [The annoying french song starts playing.... and Parizo walks in with a neckbrace.] [Summers] Heh heh...It's that time of the day, kids. Heh..with that thing on his neck..maybe we should start calling *Parizo* "Whiplash". :) [Steeves] Oh, and I bet you like to trip old ladies in the middle of the street too, don't you? [Parizo] Welcome everybody to this week Parizo's Chat. I am so mad, I don't even know where to start. [Summers] So quit while your ahead. [Parizo] First, I contacted my lawyers and President Hills, Aerial Marauder, Derek Holmes, Marty Jannetty, Tom Summers and Tony Marx should hear from them very soon. IT'S A CONSPIRACY!!! [Steeves] HO HO!!! That's rich!! [Summers] Give me a *break!* [Parizo] Everybody's against us. First about Hills, he had no right to let Holmes be in Jannetty's corner and should have reverse the decision and give the title back to us after the intervention of the Marauder. [Summers] Marauder was nowhere near the ring..he was busy giving Sam Holmes a blood-letting. [Steeves] Yeah..but he was by the ring in spirit. That's why he lost. [Parizo] Look at my neck !!! How can I interf... help Ducon with such a sour neck. Now Holmes, you will pay, both in and out of the ring, same for you Marauder. You should have stayed in the hospital, it was a lot safer for you. And you Jannetty, don't get to attach to this belt, you're gonna soon kiss it goodbye. And for Tony and Tom, well I just don't like your faces. I'm gonna sue you for being too ugly and have too big of a mouth. [Summers] Uh-huh..who exactly is calling the kettle black here? [Steeves] Oh leave him alone..he's been through a lot. [Parizo] Now, let's get to something a little more on the happy side, lets say happy times four. Like in Four Horseman: Ric Flair, Arn Anderson, Chris Benoit and the latest: Owen Hart. [The Four Horsemen slowly come down the aisle looking as smug as ever as the theme to "2001" is playing. Most are booing, while the hardcore fans throw the four fingers up, and the Horsemen respond in kind, causing a hardore pop.] [Parizo] Gentlemen..let me be the first to congratulate you on winning the North Am Tag gold..and for making probably the finest chioce for a Horseman with Owen Hart! [Flair] *Y'know* something Jacques Parizo!! This is just the *beginning* for the Horsemen!! If *any* of you people have been tuning in to WCW..you *know* we are *Gods* there!! [Summers] Funny..they looked more like the British Royal Family to me. [Flair] Doug Hills *knew* that..so he *tried* to limit the power of the Horsemen in the VWA by *not* signing Brian Pillman... Well we *didn't* let that stop us!! If we couldn't have Brian here..then we would just have to choose someone else WORTHY ENOUGH to be a Horseman. And who *better* to fill that position..who *better* to be a Horseman..than Royalty. Owen Hart...you *definately* proved yourself to be a Horseman! [Hart] Thank you Nature Boy. I told *everyone* here..in the World Wrestling Federation *and* the Virtual Wrestling Alliance..that I *am* the best there is, the best there was, and the best there ever will be! And to be a *Horseman* of all things just proved it!! I am here with the best that the sport of professional wrestling has to offer, and *together*...*we* will rule this sorry excuse for a federation..starting with the VWA North American Tag Belts! You know, Ric..I can relate with you on one point. Mein Furher Hills was too scared of the fact that if both me and my tag team partner Yokozuna were here, we'd own all the belts in the Virtual Wrestling Alliance..so Yokozuna isn't here. [Summers] Actually I heard that Yoko just couldn't fit through the front door of the Arena. [Hart] So I did what I could...just like you, I improvised. And I got me a person that I basically *grew up* with..the person that knew everything I know.. the brother I never had..Chris Benoit! [Steeves] Like he *needs* another brother? [Benoit] You realize, people..that you have even *seen* my partner in the presence of the Horsemen for the last couple of *months*! And did you even make the connection? *pfuh*..Right. I guess this shows what kind of fans you guys are. Maybe you should try hanging out with those ECW bums..at least the intelligence quotient should rise a bit between all of you. [The fans start booing.] [Summers] Think he's still bitter about being chokeslammed by 911 a while back? [Steeves] Hard to tell..why do you ask? [Parizo] That's great, guys! Now Arn, you did something that I normally don't see you doing..you actually told Flair and Benoit to back off from attacking "Whiplash" Barry Williams after you lost to him. Now, I like you and all, but..uh..why the hell did you do that? [Anderson] You wanna know why? Simple. I do have some form of an honor system.. [Summers] Doesn't it go sorta like: "Win if you can..lose if you must..but always cheat?" [Anderson] if someone actually manages to beat me clean, hell that's fine. Williams.. you did that, and not too many of these yahoos can brag about that. So, if you wanna brag about how you beat The Enforcer..go ahead. But, if you have any sense of desency or sportsmanship in that skull of yours..you *will* give me a rematch next week or the week after right here on Havoc. And if you do accept, Williams...you better be ready, cause I'm not gonna be so generous this next time. [Flair] A *new* age has fallen upon the VWA, my friends...the age of the Horsemen!! And we dare *anyone* to try and knock us off this mountain! Anyone!! [Hart] Hell, we'll put the titles up for grabs next week to any comers! [Benoit] We wanna find out who in this fed actually have the balls to face the Horsemen! [All of a sudden, "Iron Man" by Black Sabbath starts up, and the crowd goes ballistic!! The crowd becomes deafening as soon as the Road Warriors appear!] [Parizo] Now what in the name of God do *you* two want?? [Hawk and Animal get into the ring..stare at the Horsemen and point at Benoit and Hart.] [Hawk + Animal] THEM. [Steeves] *shiver* Is it just me..or did they sound like Hannibal Lecter when they said that? [The crowd pops huge!! The Horsemen start backing down and then leave the ring area, leaving Parizo alone with the Road Warriors. Parizo is visibly scared.] [Animal] Ladies..you made the biggest mistake of your lives with that challenge! You want challengers?? You want a open contract?? You got it!! Tell 'em Hawk!! [Hawk] Y'know Animal..I used to think that we were pretty insane. Heh..you talk to half the people we faced and I think they'd agree with that idea! But Owen Hart and Chris Benoit..you *must* be insane..or have some kind of death wish..if you put those titles up for grabs for *anyone* to challenge, when you *know* that a team like us would take you up on that?? Naw...you must be just stupid! But hey..I think they're doing us a big favor here! [Animal] Yeah...ya see..it's been a while..and we have been getting *very*..anxious.. D'you ever see what happens to people when they become anxious for a lengthy period of time? They start to go just *that* bit more looney then they already are! [Hawk and Animal start to come closer to Parizo] So..we need some ass to kick!! Thanks for volunteering, Horsemen!! [Hawk] [Starts to say something, but looks down.] Uh...Jack? If you're gonna do *that* all the time? [points down.] Stop wearing white and get some Depends or something. Sheesh. [Hawk and Animal leave the arena, and the crowd pops *big*!!] [Summers] What a suprise!! I knew they were back in WCW, but I didn't think that they were coming to the VWA as well! [Steeves] Yeah..we'll see..these guys haven't been a team in over three years. I betcha the coordination they once had is shot to hell by now. [Bendix] This contest is scheduled for one fall...["The Order of the General" starsts up.] Introducing first...from Hong Kong...weighing in at 209 pounds....."The Lonesome Lion" MOO KAME!!!!! [Steeves] Lemme guess...Aaron and Mico set this one up, right? [Summers] Hey..that's why they were in Japan for the last couple of weeks..to scout for Japanese talent. They found some good ones here! [Steeves] One small problem with your logic there..Kame's from Hong Kong...*Big* difference there. His opponent...accompanied to the ring by his manager, Master Fugiama.... from Nagasaki, Japan....weighing in at 238 pounds....THE SOUL TAKER!!!! [Steeves] *He's* from Japan..so the Kabuki twins might have found him. Seaking of Japan..you know who I'd love to see here? [Summers] Who? [Steeves] Tatsumi Fujinami...now *there* was a wrestler! [Summers] Still is. [Steeves] I dunno..he's getting old. I don't think he has it anymore..but back in the eighties? Whoo. It was a great match between two of the best martial artist of the VWA. Taker was very aggressive early in the match. He went for some great combination of kick, a karate kick, a mule kick and a devastating savate kick to the head. He went for the early pin following a snap suplex but Kame kicked out at 2. Taker then went for another suplex but Kame reversed it and took control of the match. He stunned Taker with a monkey flip followed by a flying clothesline and he nearly got the pin after a Frankensteiner. Taker went outside to seek comfort from his manager Master Fugiama and he got much more than that: a hand full of salt. While Fugiama was distracting the ref, the salt made his way to the eyes of Kame. But the ref saw Kame holding his face and the salt of the mat, so he disqualified Soul Taker. Taker and Fugiama argued that a fan had thrown the salt but the referee refused to change his mind. [Steeves] Man..the *one* time the ref decides to grow a brain... [Summers] Well..despite the ending, that was one hell of a match. It should be intersting to see if we get more talent like that soon. Up next is our main event!! [Bendix] This contest is scheduled for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit...and is for the VWA WORLD TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP!!! ["Our Lady Peace" by Starseed starts up.] Introducing the challenger...from Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada...weighing in at 297 pounds....RAINE TURNER!!! [Steeves] Well..Turner finally got his shot at some gold...As much as I like Jamie Cutlass and his style..I'm thinking that this may be Turner's day. [Summers] What I'm curious about is if either Mike Sweetser or Raekwon will try to cost Turner the match. [Bendix] His opponent ["Mien Herze schwimmt im Blut (My Heart is Swimming in Blood)" by Johann Sebastian Bach starts up.]...from his family's private island....weighing in at 243 pounds the VWA World Television Champion....JAMIE CUTLASS!!!!!! [Summers] Cute song. He definately has the arrogance of a champion. We'll find out if he has the *skill* to back that and help him to keep that belt. Turner was very eager to start the match but Cutlass, giving him nearly 100 pounds, decided to set the pace of the match. He stayed outside the ring for a while and each time he came back in the ring to stop the 10 count, he was back out as soon as Turner approached him. Finally, Turner grabbed him by the hair and pulled him in the ring. The next couple minutes were probably the longest in Cutlass life as Turner was all over him. Turner got a couple of near fall following a powerslam, a powerbomb and a DDT, but Cutlass showed a lot of endurance and determination as he managed to kick out each time. After about ten minutes, Cutlass managed to throw Turner outside the ring after reversing an irish whip. Turner nearly was counted out as he came back in at the count of nine. Cutlass, having the clock on his side, went for the sleeper hold and when Turner was nearly asleep, he went for the figure four. After being trapped for nearly 2 minutes, Turner grabbed the rope to break to hold. Cutlass then went for a suplex but Turner reversed it and got the pin following his Birdman at 14:10. New TV champ: Raine Turner. [Summers] Wow. Good call, Chris.. [Steeves] Thank you..thank you...what can I say? I'm just that good. [Summers] Don't get carried away, now..you didn't win a title..you just won a bet. Well...that's it for now..wait......*sigh* Here's something from Jacques Parizo. ----------------------------------- [Cut to Parizo in front of the VWA Backdrop.] [Parizo] I spoke to my lawyers and since I don't have a cas... Since it would be too much trouble to sue, I have another idea. A little challenge. Six man tag: Derek Holmes, Jannetty and Marauder against Ducon, Sam Holmes and a surprise partner... Also, I have a little stipulation for this match, if we win, the guy that gets the pin for our team gets a title shot against Jannetty, if we lose, the guy that gets pin for our team will never ever receive a title show against Jannetty. It would be a great way for you Jannetty to get rid of a contender, what do you think ? Do you have enough guts to accept the challenge ? ----------------------------------- [Summers] That should be ineresting! I'm sure we'll find out next wekk if Holmes and the World champ, Marty Jannetty will accept that, as well as Barry Williams' decision as to if he will rematch with Arn Anderson! Plus we should have our first WRESTLEWAR report next week! So until then...for Chris Steeves.. [Steeves] Summers!! Is that who I *think* that is?? [Summers] It's Paul E. Dangerously!!!
