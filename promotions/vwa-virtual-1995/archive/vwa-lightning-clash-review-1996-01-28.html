VWA — Fan Coverage

VWA Lightning Clash Review

28 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
By [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) I knew it was going to be one hell of a night when I first walked into Knickerbocker Arena. As myself and my friend [name withheld] handed over our tickets I was struck with the painful flash back of the last time I was here. It was when Micheal Jordan was making his return to the Garden to play the Knicks and I, being a proud Bulls fan, wore my starter jacket. In hindsight this may not have been a very wise move because as Jordan was scoring 55 I was being taken to the hospital with the knowledge that massive amounts of alcohol, a Bulls Jacket, and screaming "Knicks Suck" in the middle of thousands of fans, just don't mix! However I managed to put this horrible event behind me for this night only. Why, you may ask? Because this was special, this was violence, this was the VWA! (And also because security finally agreed to let me back in the Arena!) Now when you go to a wrestling card there are two very important things you should go to. One is your seat, and the other is the vendor stands! So with credit card in hand I was off to grab some stuff. [name withheld] picked up a poster of Wildheart and Jean Claude and a VWA title belt. I grabbed a poster of Thunderhawk and Lisa Lightning (actually it was a poster of Lisa, I think Thunderhawk was somewhere in the back) and a (Aerial) "Marauder Madness" T-Shirt! Yeah, Yeah, I know the guy is in the hospital but he is still my favorite wrestler and anybody that don't like it can see my after the review! :) Since we were now loaded down with stuff we went to find our seats, and when we found them it was obvious that security still remember me because they put my seats right next to this little twelve year old brat henceforth known as "the kid". He was clearly one of those kids whose parents did not beat nearly as often as they should, as you will see as we go along. But we tried not to let that get to us because we were about to see some action and the VWA provided by starting the card (and this review) off with a bang with.... Arn Anderson versus Barry "Whiplash" Williams YES!! The Four Horseman ride! We threw up the four fingers as Arn walked pass us down the aisle, of course the kid quickly threw down the four fingers, which made me immediately want to smack him! As Williams came down the aisle [name withheld] and I placed a bet on wheter Williams would win...and get jumped by the Horseman. Or lose...and get jumped by the Horseman! However, I must admit I was very impressed by Williams and he gave Arn a hell of a fight. But I just knew that Arn would win, I mean he was a HORSEMAN, he had to win right? Well as Eric Bischoff and the WCW has shown us, being a Horseman doesn't mean what it use to. And when Williams got Arn in his "Tornado Piledriver" I knew his nickname was truly earned as he got the win. But we just knew that this wasn't over yet and we were proven right when Flair and Benoit came down the aisle. YEAH!!! Now it's time for a good old-fashioned Horseman Style beating, like the ones they gave to Dusty Rhodes and Magnum T.A....and HEY..what the HELL is this? Arn is telling the Horseman to back off, I guess that Tornado piledriver must have done more damage to Arn's brain then I thought. Rating: 3 stars (Out of 5) Great match, but the Horseman let me down. After that, it was time for [name withheld] and I to do our second wager of the night (since no one won the first!). It was time to guess on who the 4th Horseman would be. I put my money on Vader, while [name withheld] put his money on Sam Holmes. At this point the kid decided to speak up and tell us that Owen Hart would be the 4th Horseman. When [name withheld] and I stopped laughing..twenty minutes later..we were fine. I mean OWEN HART? There is no way in hell the 4th Horseman could be.... VWA North American Tag Team Title Champs: The Glamour Boys versus Chris Benoit and....Owen Hart DAMN!!! I hate it when kids are right. They never let you live it down! So during the whole match I had to deal with the kid going "I told you so, I told you so!" Luckly I was able to tune the punk out so I could watch the best tag team match of the night. I mean this is what people pay to see, great athletes giving there all for the gold (now only if the WCW would realize that!). I was surprised at how well Benoit and Hart worked as a team because I really thought the Glamour Boys would be too much for them. I mean you just gotta like the Glamour Boys, two cocky men who know they look good and get all the women..just like me!! But sometimes getting all the woman just aint enough as Martin had to learn when he got caught in Benoit's Dragon Suplex for the win, and the championship. Rating: 4 stars ( The Horsemen with the gold..it all seems so appropiate don't it?) As [name withheld] and I discussed how the new Horseman would work as a group a chant of ECW! ECW! started. And when we saw a ambulance pull up in back we knew it must be time for... Sabu versus Sid Vicious Now what sick genius thought this match up? I guess the local blood bank was running low and they needed these two guys to see how much plasma they could sqeeze out eachother! This match was just a blur of tables, chokeslams, and screams for more blood. The match met it's inevitable end when Sabu moonsaulted Vicious then, since Sid was still capable of breathing, when to the top and gave him another one for the win. Rating: 3 pints of blood (What the hell was Vicious thinking any damn way, wrestling Sabu in a extreme match, but then again ANY match with Sabu is extreme wheter it was planned that way of not.) Sting versus Rano I gotta get something off my chest before I do this review. I hate it when people totally steal there ideas from other wrestlers, so as Rano came down the aisle I wondered if Steve Regal, Hunter Hurst Helmsley, Jerry Lawler, etc. were getting some royalities off the "snobby royalty" act? I was really pissed off by this and I would have given Rano a piece of my mind if he would not have beat the hell out of Sting like he did. I guess Sting had to learn the hard way that in the VWA you have to wrestle people under 50 years old every now and then and Sting was not ready to fight someone that doen't have to remove his false teeth after every match. Rating: 2 stars (See what the WCW does to you Sting?) As Sting was picking his sorry ass off the mat, I noticed that the kid was crying. [name withheld] asked him why and he said that Sting was his favorite wrestler and he was sad because he lost. Well I felt that the kid needed my support so I gently told the kid to "quit his bitching. Just because that little pussy Sting lost is no reason for you to cry like a little girl!" I guess that didn't help as much as I thought it would because all the kid could manage to say is "Who is your favorite wrestler then?" At this point I looked him straight in the eye and said that my favorite wrestler is "Gooooollllllddddduuuussssttttt!" That shut his ass up for a while! :) Noth American Championship Match Champ: Chris Sullivan versus Kendo Godai For my next wager with [name withheld] I put 20 bucks down for Chris to remain champion! And because of that he is now my least favorite wrestler! :) Come on Chris!! I put money on you for God's Sake! But hey, it was Godai's night as he showed why he is one of the best the VWA has to offer. But I got just one question for you Godai? Just how many brothers do you have man? It seems like every damn week you bring like 3 more relatives to the fed. The last time I saw that many wrestlers in the same place is during the Survivor Series when all 27 memebers of the Hart Family wrestled! :) Rating: 2.5 stars (Great Match!..But I loss 20 Bucks!!) Thunderhawk w/ Lisa Lightning versus Derek Holmes I wish I could tell you what happened during this match but I was busy with trying to see what color panties Lisa Lightning had on (I think they were red) And when I asked [name withheld] what happened he said he didn't know because he was trying to slip Lisa his phone number and security keep stopping him! Oh and I think Derek Holmes won..but don't quote me on that! Rating: 4 stars (Lisa Lightning..nuff said) "Ringmaster" Steve Austin versus Wildheart with Jean Claude Van Damme First Point: Steven Segal could kick Jean Claude's ass! Second Point: That Million Dollar Belt looks a little too heavy for Austin to be carrying around. Third Point: If that belt really is worth a million bucks and I ever see Austin around town with it..his ass is mine! Last point: That buzz cut look just aint right for Steve, and the only place a ringmaster belongs is in a circus. Thankfully Wildheart proved me right by knocking out Austin with the Dragon Punch. Rating: 3 stars "Shooter" Dean Malenko versus Mike Sweetser I love watching Dean Malenko, he is truly "the man of 1000 holds" and he must have slapped about 368 of them on Sweetser for the win and he showed absolutely no fear of Mike. But then again, would YOU fear a person with the word "Sweet" in his name? I thought not! :) Rating: 1000 moves! VWA World Tag Team Championship Champs: Travis Brothers versus the Ohio Connection w/ "Buckeye" Bill Thomas With Thomas and the Connection, you have the first three people ever to be proud of being from Ohio! So as they came down the aisle we started screaming "Baltimore Browns! Baltimore Browns!" Which drew a real hateful look from Shawntell Speed but we really didn't care. We were not afraid of them!! Besides we were right by security so they couldn't get to us!!! Anyway, the math itself was what you come from the VWA, great action, excellent moves, and no American Males! (Is it just me, or do they have the most annoying theme music in history?) In fact the only problem I have with this match is the way it ended. I mean with all this talent in the ring the match ends because a man named Buckeye interferes on behalf of the Connection!! And just what the hell is a Buckeye anyway? I got no idea. But I do know two things..that the Connection are the new champs, and that saying Baltimore Browns just don't feel right Rating 3.5 stars The crowd was really starting to buzz as our two main events approached. even the kid came out of his daze long enough to notice my "Marauder Madness" T-Shirt. So the kid, trying to be a smart ass, pointed out that the Marauder was put out of action by the men in the next match, Sam Holmes and the Champ, Mario Ducon. Well first i had to let the kid know that NOBODY could get rid of the Marauder and that I had heard on the VWA Hotline (1-900-CALL-VWA. 1.95 a minute, kids ask you parents permission before calling) that the Marauder was in the building and that he would have a surprise for everybody, which leads me to the review of.... VWA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE Champ: Mario Ducon w/ Jacque Parzio versus Marty Jannety with Derek Holmes As Mario came down the aisle I noticed he had a hard core following of fans. Some true idiots in the crowd leaped to there feet with French Berets on there heads with the words "French Legionaries" on them. I made sure that I got a good look at them so I could slash there tires later on. I also noticed there was no Sam Holmes with Ducon..I KNEW IT..the bitch knew that the Marauder was here and he ran like a little girl! Finally I was amazed at the HUGE pop Marty Jannety and Derek Holmes got when they came out..I guess being a Rocker does have its advantages :) This was easy the most surprising match of the night because I did not honestly think that Jannety would put up that much fight against the Champion. I mean I knew Jannety had heart, but this was the best in the business he was facing right now and I just thought Jannety could not handle him. I was wrong. Jannety showed the same ability that made him a champ in the WWF and by the time Ducon had regained control of the match I had turned into a total mark..cheering wildly for Jannety. But like I said, Ducon seemed to be to much as he was setting up Jannety for the skill. And to top it all off..here comes Sam Holmes! I guess he was too busy mugging some old lady to make the match on time! "Man, I said to [name withheld] as Holmes came down the aisle, Look at the way Sam is so casual soming down the aisle..he seems so unaware..this would be a perfect time for...YES!!!!..The Marauder just nailed Holmes with a chair! The crowd went beserk as the Marauder Stun-Gunned Holmes on the railing right in front of us, and I even think he noticed us before he went back to him business of trying to kill Sam Holmes! DAMN!! There was not this much blood on the ground during the Sabu-Vicious match. And the best part of it all was that there was nothing Mario Ducon could do about it and Parzio had already been piledrived and knocked out by Derek Holmes. The tide of the match had totally shifted to Jannety and he took advantage of it when he won the VWA title with the Flying Fist Drop. You know, its amazing that in a world with all types of submission holds, and suplexes, a title can still be won by a simple fist to the head. :) Rating: 5 stars (Sam Holmes gets beaten half to death, Parzio gets piledrived, and Ducon loses his title...GOD I LOVE WRESTLING!) Final Match Bret Hart versus the Terimator in a Cage Match The crowd was still going nuts after what just happened..I mean every title up for grabs tonight had changed hands! And now we had the GRUDGE MATCH between Hart and the Terminator. It seems like the crowd was evenly divided over this match..some thinking that Hart would win, and others thinking that the "Terminator Terror Tour" was too much to be stopped. I personally thought that the Terminator would win, but I had no idea it would be that brutal. The Terimator was just plain savage as he dropped FOUR Death Drops on the WWF champion for the win. And I can honestly say that I have never seen Bret Hart beaten so badly by one man. And to top that, as the Terminator was leaving, WildHeart jumped out to attack the Terminator! I love it! But WildHeart better be careful, this man just took out "The Excellence of Execution" no telling what he will do to you! Rating: 4.5 stars MAN!!! WHAT A NIGHT! New champions crowned..more blood then a episode of ER and the return of the Marauder! I can't wait to check out the next Havok! But I had one more thing I had to do before I left..I walked up to that little brat who had annoyed me all night..I was going to see if [name withheld] and I could slap the "Ohio Ouch" on him. Just then his older brothers stood up..all 6 foot 8, 415 pds a piece of them. Oh shit! I hope they still got my hospital bed reserved!! [name withheld] ( [detail withheld] )
