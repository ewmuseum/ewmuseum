He tried to get him in another DDT, but Derek Holmes and Marty Jannetty cleared the ring. ================================================== Kendo Godai vs. Chris Sullivan (non-title match) This match was almost as good as the first meeting, between the Japanese superstar, and one of the best technicians in the VWA today. For the first five minutes, the two basically felt each other out, looking for any flaws in each other's gameplan. Sullivan finally got the momentum on his side, by perfectly executing suplexes that would make Taz jealous, and basically tried to keep Godai on the mat as long as he could. Godai retaliated with a barrage of acrobatic and martial arts moves. This was one of the few matches in which both men managed to keep the action inside the ring, and they worked their asses off, but it wasn't Sullivan's night as Godai countered a flying cross-body press with a powerslam and got the pin at 25:21. Sullivan went to shake Godai's hand, and as they did, Sullivan *nailed* Godai with a clothesline, hit him with a T-Bone suplex, and applied the Sullivan Special for 3 minutes, before officials finally got Sullivan off of Godai!! Sullivan left the arena looking smug and soaking in all the boos from the crowd. ================================================= Parizo's Chat: [Parizo] Guess what time it is? Malenko went after I.C. with beautiful technical moves, but I.C. would not let him get any submission moves applied. I.C. Kept at Malekno move for move, and it was hard to tell who was going to get the advantage in the opening minutes. Malenko finally started to gain momentum, and managed to get some submission moves in, the most devastating being a figure four leglock in the middle of the ring. This was applied for almost a minute thirty seconds, but I.C. would not submit and eventually reversed it into a gravevine. Kool and Malenko went back and forth again for another 7 minutes. Malenko made a mistake one would not expect, as he ducked his head too early for a backdrop, and Kool nailed him with a faceslam. Kool used this to his advantage and leter hit Malenko with Acrophobia and got the win at 15:55. ======================================= Lex Luger vs. "Whiplash" Barry Williams [After Williams is introduced, Parizo grabs the mic.] [Parizo] Well, Well, Well Mr. Williams, you're not sure if you want in or not, you want to beat each one of my guys before you decide. When the match finally started, it seemed like Luger didn't gave it his best. Luger went for a couple punches, some bodyslams but not much more. No real offensive. Williams seemed not to be sure what to make of this. He decided to go for the kill and after a few minutes of offensive, he went for the Tornado Pildriver for the easy pin at 4:05. Parizo jumped in the ring and shook Williams hands, congratulating him for his victory. Luger did the same and he left with Parizo.
