[Summers] Hi everybody and welcome to Wednesday Nite Havoc, it's been a while. Mico and Aaron are in Japan scouting new talent for the VWA, but I have a feeling we haven't seen the last of them. I'd like to take this opportunity to thank them for the wonderful job they did during our absence. [Steeves] Easy for you to say, even a 2 Years old would seem to have done a good job compare to you. [Summers] Very funny. We have a great card for you today with a TV title match between Mike Sweetser and a newcomer Raekwon. Also we have a lot of tag team action this week with the non title match between the Glamour Boys and the Psycho Twins. [Steeves] We also have the much awaited match between Derek Holmes and Marty Jannetty vs Mario Ducon and his mystery partners. I know who he is. [Summers] Who ? [Steeves] Can't tell you !!! [Summers] Why am I not surprise... [Steeves] Enough about that, let's go to the ring for the first match. [Summers] Rano versus Frank Knight. [Alan Bendix] Introducing first parts unknown at 375 pounds: RANO !!! [Rano walks slowly to the ring on the music that HH Helmsley use in the WWF] And his opponent from Chicago, Illinois at 268 pounds: FANTASTIC FRANK KNIGHT !!! Knight knew better than try to match strength with the big Rano and decided to work on the legs. He used a wide array of take-down move to keep Rano on the mat where he could do the most damage. But Rano didn't saw it that way and used is weight advantage and his scientific skills to keep control of the match. But Knight did his best to set the pace of the match and every time Rano was trying to start some sort of offensive, Knight left the ring or backed-off. Rano was getting really upset and impatient and that nearly cost him the match after Knight caught him in a small package. But at 17:23, Rano got the pin after the Pedigree. Knight stayed in the ring a while after the match with a frown on his face. He looked very annoyed and seemed to be thinking hard about something. [Summers] This ain't the face of an happy man. [Steeves] He just lost, why should he be happy ? Maybe he just forgot to buy some milk for home. [Summers] That must be it... Let's go back to Benidx with our next match... [Bendix] This next tag team match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, [Don't tread on me starts playing and the crowd gets on their feet] from Albany, NY at 244 pounds: DEREK HOLMES !!! And his partner: from San Antonio, Texas, weighing 240 pounds MARTY JANNETTY !!! [Both guys run to the ring, slapping hands with the fans.] And their opponent: [The annoying french song starts playing] Introducing first the manager: Jacques Parizo ! From Montreal, Quebec, Canada, at 244 pounds: THE VWA WORLD CHAMPION, MARIO DUCON !!! And his partner:... Sorry, but I don't know. [Steeves] What a professional. [Summers] Oh my, here goes Parizo... [Parizo takes the mic] Ladies and Gentlemen’s, First I'd like to apologize, but because of my in ring obligation, there won't be a Parizo's Chat this week. But don't worry, I'll be back next week. Now, Holmes and Jannetty, I'm sure you're wondering, who's gonna be Mario's partner. Well, I think you're gonna like him. Especially you, Derek, I think you already know him...["Grind" for AIC starts playing] He is THE DEMOLITION MAN !! SAM HOLMES !!! [Summers] WHAT ??? [Steeves] I knew it !!! Derek seemed very upset to see his brother and wanted to start in the ring with him. But it was Jannety and Ducon who started the match. It was a very violent match that saw a lot of double teaming from both sides. At one point, Jannetty and Derek Holmes had Ducon in the ring for almost ten minutes and the champion got his butt kick really hard. When he tagged out with Sam Holmes, they tried to double team him but Parizo jumped on the ring apron and started yelling at Holmes. He started running after Parizo. That was all the time, Sam needed, he sneaks attacked Jannetty and while the ref was trying to stop Derek from hitting Parizo, Ducon and Sam slammed Jannetty outside the ring and Sam did his C-4 powerbomb to Jannetty on the floor. He then thrown him back in the ring and Ducon got the easy pin at 22:39. [Steeves] YES !!! What a great match. That Ducon is such a great wrestler. [Summers] Yeah, he's going for the longest streak of match winning by cheating. [Steeves] A win's a win. Cheating is legal, getting caught ain't. [Summers] That's where you got that journalism diploma. Let's go with our next match. [Bendix] This next match is set for one fall. Introducing first from Sarasota, Florida at 270 pounds: [The theme from Mortal Combat starts playing] THE PRIMETIME PUNISHER !!! And his opponent from Kansas City, MO at 265 pounds: "WHIPLASH" BARRY WILLIAMS !! [Williams walks to the ring to a Jazzed-up/Rock and Roll version of the Olympic theme.] Punisher tried to sneak attack Williams before the bell rang but Williams was ready. He caught Punisher with a fireman's carry and a drop toe hold. Punisher was ready for this match and did some power move on Williams. He had Williams in a torture rack for almost two minutes, but he refused to give up. Williams finally got the pin at 11:46 following a DDT. [Summers] This guy is very impressive, he's still undefeated here in the VWA. I see a belt for him in a near future. [Steeves] Yeah, in 5 minutes in the dressing room after he put his pants on. The Punisher must be getting very upset, he's still looking for his first victory. [Summers] He's working hard to say the least. It's time for some tag team action. [Bendix] Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by their manager Bill Thomas. From Ohio at a combined weight of 495 pounds: STEVEN CLARK, SHAWNTELL SPEED, THE OHIO CONNECTION !!! And their opponent from Miami, Florida at a combined weight of 464 pounds: "MR. EXCITEMENT" DROPKICK AND "THE FANTASY" BRETT YOUNG: THE AMERICAN DREAMS !!! [They run to the ring to the music of "Eye of the Tiger"] The Dreams being newcomers, the Ohio didn't know what to expect and it showed. The dreams took control early in the match and used a wide array of aerial move to keep the Ohio off balance. But the Ohio went outside, regrouping and decided to start bending the rule a little. They did some double teaming on Brett Young. Dropkick managed to tag in but finally at 24:50 Clark got the pin on Dropkick after the Ohio Ouch. After the match, Raekwon came from nowhere and started attacking Dropkick but he was chased away by Brett Young. [Summers] What was he trying to prove ? He's got a TV title match later on. [Steeves] I don't know, maybe they know each other from before. I must admit, attacking a guy when his partner's around ain't really bright. [Summers] Hey Steeves, you seem to know everything, who were the two guys that showed up last week during Owen Hart and Chris Benoit's match ? [Steeves] How should I know, I wasn't here. Why don't you call Mico and Aaron in Japan ? [Summers] I don't need to, because I did my homework and if you did yours too, you would have recognize Chris Erickson and his brother Vic. [Steeves] Chris Erickson... that name rings a bell. [Summers] It should, he was in the VWA for a couple week and came very close to winning the battle royal at November Nightmare. Nobody knew why he left, but he's back with two brothers. [Steeves] Three Ericksons !?! Man, we have three Godais and now three Ericksons, at least we only have one Summers and one Marx. [Summers] Speaking on Marx, he's back too and here he is with the clash report. [Tony Marx] Hi everybody !!! I know it's been a while but finally I'm here with a Lightning Crash report. We are only 10 day away as the Clash will take place January the 20th. Here's the complete card: We have a World Title Match with the champion Mario Ducon (w/ Jacques Parizo) defending his belt against Marty Jannetty (w/Derek Holmes). We all saw what happened in the tag match earlier. One must wonder if Sam Holmes will have anything to do in the outcome of this match... We also have a North American Title Match with the champion Chris Sullivan taking on the Dragon Kendo Godai. Godai is one some kind of losing streak since he lost the TV title to Sweetser. Will he be able to turn things around at the clash ? We also have a World Tag Team Title Match: The Travis Brothers defending the belt against The Ohio Connection (w/ "Buckeye" Bill Thomas). The Travis took things a little easy, not wrestling much since Nightmare and the Connection on the opposite saw a lot of action. It'll be interesting to see what come out of this one. And for our fourth title match, we have the North American Tag Team champion: The Glamour Boys(w/ Moira Stone) facing Chris Benoit and... the 4th Horseman that will debut at the Clash. We all thought it would be Owen Hart, but once again it seemed we were fooled. I wonder where that leaves Owen Hart. Also at the Lightning Clash: Mike Sweetser vs. Dean Malenko. On a special note, Doug Hills decided that if Sweetser was still TV champion at the Clash, the title will be on the line. So we could have 5 title match !!! Now the match that we're all waiting for: Bret Hart vs. The Terminator in the Steel Cage Match. Those two guys really hate each other and I think that in this match, the main goal of both guys won't be victory, but hurting the other. We'll also have Sting vs. Rano, Steve Austin vs. Wildheart (w/Jean Claude Van Damme), Shane Douglas vs. Derek Holmes (w/Marty Jannetty), Sid Vicious vs. Sabu and "Whiplash" Barry Williams vs. Arn Anderson. It should be a great evening and maybe the best VWA card yet. Don't miss it !!! Next week, we'll try to have some comments from some of the participant. See ya next week. [Summers] Thanks Tony. Now before we get back to the ring, We have a little something with the Glamour Boys that was recorded earlier. Camera opens on "Magnificent" Moira Stone in front of a colorful striped building. She is dressed in a tight purple ad blue bodysuit with her auburn hair down over her shoulders. [Stone] Now some of you losers out there have been talking and my Glamour Boys have been listening. You say that since we won the titles, we've hauled ass and hid with them. Not true. They just discovered this new place and I can't get them to leave. Camera rolls upward to see a giant "HOUSE OF MIRRORS" sign on the front of the building and follows Stone into the building. Once in the building Stone is surrounded by mirrors. Half bare the body of Ronnie 'Rols Royce' Martin posing with one of the North American Tag Team Championship belts. The other half portrays that of his partner, 'Studly" Steven Duety posing with hid half of the belts. [Stone] Don't you just love it! [Martin] Look at these shiny gold belts around our waists. They complement my wonderful blonde hair so beautifully. (Strokes his long flowing blonde locks) [Duety] And yet, they give us a sense of perfection, tat we are head and shoulders above the rest. Well, we already knew that, but now everyone knows. And tonight, the Psychotic Kids find out what that's all about. To tell the truth, those two morons are too ugly for me even to touch. [Martin] Calm down, there big guy. The fed heads say we have to fight them, so if we must be in the same ring with ugly people, we must. At least it's not the Nasty Boys, right. Anyway, Psychotic nuts, we've got a surprise for you tonight. [Duety] Hopefully it'll mean I don't have to look at your ugly faces to long--that could ruin my complexion. [Martin] Just think how pretty we'll be posing in the ring after this victory, Studly. But until then, we have all these lovely mirrors to worships ourselves. Glamour Boys by Living Colour starts playing and the Boys pose harder than ever into the mirrors with their belts. The camera focuses back onto Stone who is smiling largely. [Stone] My Boys look so good in gold! [Steeves] They do look good, but look won't mean anything in the next match. [Bendix] This next bout is a tag team , non title match. Introducing first from Atlanta, Georgia at a combined weight of 1004 pounds: WACKO, BERSERK, THE POWER TWINS !!! And their opponent, introducing their manager Moira Stone, at a combined weight of 597 pounds: THE VWA NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPION: RONNIE MARTIN' STEVEN DUETY, THE GLAMOUR BOYS !!! [The Glamour Boys walk down the aisle with Ronnie carrying a strait jacket and Duety handling large metal sign that reads "CENTRAL STATE MENTAL HOSPITAL".] Before the bell rang, the Boys attacked the Twins. Duety smashed is sign on Wacko's head, knocking him out. Than they tried to put Bersek in the strait jacket. But even 2 against one, Berserk, being the big man that he is, managed to escape the jacket. Berserk stayed in control of the match for a while, but with Wacko still out, the Boys kept on double teaming him. They nearly got the pin at 15:32 after a 2 on 1 suplex, but Berserk kicked out. At this point, Wacko was back on his feet and managed to tag in. Very upset, he started to throw the Boys around. At 24:21 he applied his Wacko Driver on Duety. Martin jumped in and hit him with Stone's purse, earning his team a lost by DQ. [Steeves] What a match !!! I think it's not the last we've seen of those two teams. I love those twins. [Summers] Yeah, you should hang around with them for a while It would be good for you to speak with people on the same intellectual level as you. [Steeves] When do you get your own talk show ? Ah, Raine Turner is coming up, now that's a guy I like. [Bendix] Introducing first, from nowhere land, at 235 pounds: IWANNA JOB And his opponent from Vancouver, Canada at 337 pounds: RAINE TURNER !!! [He walks slowly to the ring.] As Turner is getting ready to fight, Job turns to the crowd to find some support, Turner then attacks him from behind and does the Birdman, dropping him outside the ring at the same time. He then grabs the mic. [Turner] Don't EVER turn your back to the next VWA TV champion !!! Raine then goes outside the ring and does another Birdman to the guy outside the ring. He then thrown him back and the ring and gets the easy pin. He then pushed a ring attendant and sat on his chair at ringside to watch the TV title match. [Summers] What is he doing ? He has no right to do that. [Steeves] Go tell him. Hey Raine !!! Tom wants you to leave !!! [Summers] Shut up !!! Well I guess we have no choice, it's time for the TV title match. [Alan Bendix] This match is for the VWA TV title and with a 15 minutes time limit. Introducing first the challenger, with his manager Slick Rick, at 350 pounds from parts unknown: RAEKWON !!! [Raekwon walks slowly toward the ring with his manager. He has an insane look on his face. And his opponent: [U Know starts playing and the crowd goes crazy] from Spokane, WA at 237 pounds: MIKE SWEETSER !!! Being the maniac that he is, Raekwon tried every dirty trick in the book the win the match. He almost was disqualified many times and his manager Slick Rick had to smooth talk the referee so he would let the match continue. For the entire match Raine Turner kept on yelling a both wrestlers, challenging them and making fun of their moves. Mike tried to stay technical with the big men, but after Slick Rick got involved while the ref had his back turn, he saw red and decided to start some brawling of his own. Raekwon was in for a big surprise, he obviously didn't think that such a small guy would have that much fight in him. Mike got the pin at 11:29 following his Sweetserplex. As soon as the bell rang, Turner jumped in the ring and did a Birdman to Sweetser. As Raekwon got back on his feet, he saw Turner and went to shake his hand, Turner catches him with a big boot and did the Birdman on him. He then grabbed the belt and left the ring. [Steeves] I think he wants a title shot. [Summers] No kidding !!! There's better way for him to do that. There's about 2 name on the waiting list. [Steeves] Maybe he doesn't like to wait. [Summers] Time for the main event... [Bendix] It's now time for the main event, a tag team match set for one fall. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by Chris Benoit. First from Charlotte, North Carolina, weighing in at 245 pounds: RIC FLAIR !!! And his partners THE ENFORCER ARN ANDERSON !!! And their opponent: From Calgary, Alberta, Canada at 237 pounds: THE HITMAN BRET HART !!! And his partner, from Venice Beach, California, weighing in at 278 pounds: STING !!! Flair and Anderson had more experience as a tag team and it showed. Also, with Benoit outside the ring, Sting and Hart were clearly at disadvantage. Anderson and Hart started the match and Hart had the upper hand. But the Horsemen managed to keep him in the ring and did a lot of fast tag. At 9:34, Hart kicked out of a DDT by Anderson at 2 and a half. After that, he was in Flair's figure four for almost 5 minutes and when he reversed it in a sharpshooter, Anderson jumped in the ring but was caught by a clothesline by Sting. While the referee was trying to get Sting out of the ring, Anderson finally kick Bret in the back to break the hold. He then rolled Flair outside and began pounding of Hart. But a couple minute later as Flair was holding Bret so Anderson could kick him, he got out of the way and Arn caught Ric. Hart tagged to Sting who attacked Arn, Ric and even Chris Benoit. Finally at 24:25 Anderson submitted to Hart's sharpshooter. [Summers] YES !!! What a match !!! [Steeves] No fair, dammit. I hope the fourth Horsemen will help Flair and the gang get back on top. [Summers] Don't you already know who he is ? [Steeves] Of course I do. [Summers] Who ? [Steeves] OK, this time I'll tell you. He is... What ? Oh, sorry we're out of time. See ya next week !!!
