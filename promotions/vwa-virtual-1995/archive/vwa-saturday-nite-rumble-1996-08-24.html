Cornette ["Sweet Dreams are Made of This" by the Eurythmics starts up.] At a total combined weight of 479 pounds: "Irresistible" CHRIS SULLIVAN, "Sex Machine" CHRIS HAYNES: THE VWA WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS: THE MIDNIGHT EXPRESS!!!! Chris Sullivan started the match against Johnny Grunge. Sullivan took him down with a kneelift and then followed with a kick to the midsection. Sullivan tagged in Haynes and before he left, he nailed Grunge with a belly-to-back suplex. Haynes nailed Grunge with a flying dropkick. Haynes continued with a backbreaker and then went on the top ropes. Grunge got up and then Haynes nailed him with a dropkick from the top. He then yelled to the crowd: "This is nothing!". Haynes then nailed Grunge with an atomic drop and went for the pin but Grunge kicked out at two. Haynes went for a hiptoss, but Grunge countered with a backslide but could only get a one count. Grunge nailed Haynes with a roundhouse right and Haynes begged him to stop. Grunge went to tag Rock, but Haynes got back up and nailed Rock on the outside before Grunge could tag. Haynes then tagged him Sullivan. Sullivan nailed Grunge with a piledriver and then tagged Haynes back in. Haynes gave the sign for the Dragon Suplex and then nailed Grunge with it. He went for the pin and got the three count at 6:05. [Summers] An impressive win for the Midnight Express. [Steeves] You can say that again, Rocco Rock didn't even make it in the ring.
