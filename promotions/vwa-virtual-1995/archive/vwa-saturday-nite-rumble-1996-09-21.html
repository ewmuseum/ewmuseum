Luger quickly went for an elbowdrop, but Holmes rolled out of the way and quickly locked in an armlock. He kept at the former VWA World champ with several power moves that were definitely showing their effectiveness on the face of Derek Holmes. Holmes rolled Luger back in the ring and went for the cover, but he quickly stood up when he saw Douglas with Francine! He leapt over the top rope and ran after the Franchise.
