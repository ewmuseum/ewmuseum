VWA Wednesday Nite Havoc (1/3/96)

3 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aaron Gibson and Mico Peleks are arguing about something, but we don't know what. Aaron: Gomen Nasai, Welcome to WEDNESDAY NIGHT HAVOC!!!! Mico: Konbonwa, We have a, eh, interesting card for you tonight!! Aaron: First up, ugh, a squash match... [Owen Hart/Chris Benoit vs two jobbers] Hart and Benoit were playing around with the poor guys. Making fast tags, the used all their aerial skills to dazzle to two guys. But after a couple minute, two other guys started to make their way to the ring. They stayed away from the ring, but still close enough to have a good look. They talked together for a while, observing the match and when one of the jobber submitted to Owen Hart's sharpshooter, they left. Mico: Please tell me that was the last one tonight Aaron: Appears so Mico: Thank you, what's next Aaron: Jamie Cutlass vs Thunderhawk Mico: To the Ring!! Jamie Cutlass vs Thunderhawk [Bendix as "Mien Herze Schwimmt im Blut" from Johann Sebastian Bach starts playing] The next match is set for one fall. Introducing first from his family's private Island at 243 pounds: JAMIE CUTLASS !!! [He makes his way to the ring wearing green tights with Cutlass written down each leg in script-style writing. He walks with his nose in the air without looking to any fans] And his opponent form parts unknown, accompanied to the ring by his manager Lisa Lightning at 315 pounds:[AC/DC's Thunderstruck starts playing] THUNDERHAWK !!! Thunderhawk was ready to fight but Cutlass kept stalling the match refusing to get near Thunderhawk. After a couple minute, the match finally started and Cutlass showed Thunderhawk that he really knew how to wrestle. He applied a lot of technical move and almost made Thunderhawk submit to a scorpion leglock. But in the end, Thunderhawk showed that he was no rookie either and won the match at 10:51 with a Frankensteiner. ================================================= Aaron: Okay, I'm in a hurry, Moo Kane, Liger Mico: TO THE RING!!! Moo Kame vs Jushin Liger [Bendix] Introducing first from Hong Kong at 209 pounds: MOO KAME !!! And his opponent "Higher Ground" by the Chili Peppers starts playing and the crowd gets on its feet.]...from Tokyo, Japan...weighing 233 pounds...JUSHIN "THUNDER" LIGER !!! The two martial art artists tried to prove the world that they were better than the other. Liger took the early advantage with a combination of chop and a savate kick. But Kame fought back. He did his handspring series and tried to work on Liger's leg. Liger managed to avoid any injury to the leg and at 13:12 he blocked an attempt for a belly to belly suplex from Kame and won the match with a double underhook superplex. ================================================== Aaron: This Kane is beggining to grow on me, I C Kool vs Soul Taker Mico: TO THE RING!!! I.C. Kool vs Soul Taker [Bendix] The next match is set for one fall or 20 minutes. Introducing first from Nagasaki, Japan at 238 pounds, led to the ring by his manager: MASTER FUJIAMA: SOUL TAKER !!! And his opponent from Montreal, Quebec, Canada at 286 pounds: I.C. KOOL !!! Kool comes running to the ring and his very eager to fight, but Soul Taker takes all his time doing a little ceremony with his manager. As Kool turns his back to play the crowd, Taker attacks him from behind and goes for the quick cover after a martial arts combo. 1...2...kick out !!! Taker kept the advantage for nearly 10 minutes but at one point Soul Taker missed a clothesline and Kool knock him with one of his own. He then put on an offensive of his and overpowered Taker with some bodyslam, and a gorilla press slam. Taker to control again after Fujiama tripped Kool as he was about to do a flying avalanche. But at 17:32 I.C. Kool won with a power bomb. ================================================ Aaron: Alright, lets see, oh right words from the Aerial Marraduer Backdrop: A local hospital, the Marauder is in his room badly bruised and it is obvious he is in great pain. He is watching a tape of the beating he took at the hands of Parzio, Ducon, and Holmes [Marauder] Looks like you boys did not finish the job huh? You jumped me at Parzio's Chat, that was your first mistake. You jumped me during the Holmes match, that was your second mistake. And then you left me in the ring while I was still capable of breathing, and that is the mistake you will regret for the rest of your days! You know all the doctors around here are saying that is will be months before I wrestle again..that getting in the ring any sooner would be suicide. But I promise you this boy, I will be back..when you least expect it! I am kinda going to miss this hospital..it's a nice one..but I promised all the good people here that I would be sending them 3 new customers very soon. Look... [The camera pans back to show three hospital beds with the names "Sam Holmes", "Mario Ducon", and "Jacques Parzio" on them] [Marauder] See the doctors here are waiting for you guys..and I promise they will not have to wait long. [Fade to black] =============================================== Aaron: Okay, What's next, ah, a few words from some of the particapants at the clash! [Cut to a backdrop with the VWA logo on it and Bret Hart in front.] [Hart] Well Terminator..you finally got what you wanted..you went all over *this* fed *and* the IEWF on your Terror Tour to get my attention. Well now you've got it. January 20th..Knickerbocker Arena..we finish what we started in a Steel Cage match at the Lightning Clash! And don't *think* for one *second* that your little temper tantrum has scared me or will scare me come January 20th! I've been around the ring and the steel cage *too* long to be intimidated by another rookie that *thinks* that because he's big and powerful, that he is indestructible. Well Terminator, you go talk to Kevin Nash if you still believe that being over seven feet is some type of advantage! At the Lightning Clash...Terminator..consider yourself *executed*!! [Same backdrop with Marty Jannetty in front.] [Jannetty] Hey Ducon..you getting that bad taste in your mouth yet? You know what that is? It's *fear* my friend..cuz you're *just* starting to realize that your reign as VWA World Champion may just be coming to a close! Let's remember that the *only* way you managed to beat three of the top superstars in the VWA was through *cheating* and *interference*..in order to get that pretty belt around your waist. In the coming days..you're gonna start realizing that you *might* have to *actually* rely on *skill* to keep that gold! I know what you're thinking.."but I have my manager with me!" Well..you can fool me once..but not twice. I'm bringing my lucky charm with me.. [Derek Holmes pops his head into view.] [Holmes] Hi chuckles! See...I bet you're wondering why I'd be here. Well first..I've *always* been a fan of his [points to Jannetty]. Second, we both realize that we have the same thorn up our asses...namely you and your manager Luigi..er.. Jack..er..whatever. Third..we have some unfinished business to attend to. See, I don't like losing on account of some cheap-ass move on your part. So, I figure that if *somebody* has to knock you off your high horse..Marty is *definately* the person that can do it! I'll keep tubby company..you know..reminisce about old times and stuff..while Marty gets the job done and gets the title that he deserves. [Jannetty] These next few weeks biys..you'd think that Derek and me were joined at the hip, cuz we are gonna watch out for each other. Hell... you go find yourself a partner, and well face you in tag team action before the Clash.. [Holmes] ..'less, of course you've run out of clean undies to change into after the bout. [Jannetty] Just keep in mind boys..and this goes for all of you out there..you want to get to Derek..you come through me. [Holmes] And if you want a piece of the next world champ..you get by me. [Jannetty and Holmes come closer and closer to the camera.] [Jannetty] Plain and simple Parizo and Ducon..once we hit Albany, New York.. [Holmes] We are gonna rock.. [Jannetty] ..your.. [Both] *world*..... ================================================== Aaron: Okay Back to the Action Mico: Godai, Sandman Aaron: Good Luck Godai!! Mico: Ne! TO THE RING!! The Sandman vs Kendo Godai, no time limit, no DQ, no count-out [Bendix] Ladies and Gentlemans, this next bout is a special match. No time limit, no count-out and no disqualification. Anything goes !!! Introducing first from Part Unknown at 265 pounds: [Enter Sandman starts playing] With his manager Miss Valerie: THE SANDMAN !!! [Sandman walks to ring with... only his manager...] And his opponent from San Francisco, California at 230 pounds: THE DRAGON KENDO GODAI !!! Godai knew that the rules were in favor of Sandman so he tried to set the pace of the match by attacking early with some good martial art move. Sandman was overwhelmed by the furious attack and left the ring for nearly five minutes. Godai was getting impatient and asked the ref many times to get Sandman back in the ring, but because of the rules, he couldn't. When he finally got back in the ring, Godai started to dazzle Sandman again with some aerial move. At this point two wrestlers started to make their way to the ring. They were dressed exactly like Sandman with the same mask and all. When Sandman saw them he jumped outside of the ring and started to mix with the two other so nobody knew who the real Sandman was. So one of the three got in the ring, looking hurt. He was faking and as Godai was going to pick him up he thrown a fireball in Godai's face. The ref, realizing that he was obviously not Sandman, sent him outside the ring and asked one of the other two to get in. At this time, the Godai brother came to ringside too. Godai did pretty well, but each time Sandman got in trouble, he went outside and switched. Nobody knew who the real Sandman was. So at 32:21 Godai, exhausted from fighting three guys, finally submitted to a sleeper hold. The three masked wrestlers tried to do some more damage but were chased away by Kendo's brothers. =================================================== Aaron: Ya know, I really hate this sandman-chan Mico: now, now, whats next [The annoying french song starts playing...] Aaron: You had to ask... Mico: Tubby's chat all [Parizo] Hi everybody and welcome once again to the brightest spot of Havoc: The Parizo's Chat !!! Unfortunately, this week, the chat may not be as bright as usual because of my guest, but I was getting so tired of him begging me the be invited, that I finally cave in. My guest this week: "WHIPLASH" BARRY WILLIAMS !!! [Williams make is way to the stage under the crowd applause] [Parizo] I heard that you're not a rookie in e-wrestling. Tell us where you're coming from. [Williams] Well, I'm a member of the now-defunct DWA, where I was the Intercontinental Champion for some time (and still am). I'm also in the NcWF. [Parizo] Intercontinental champ of a defunct federation, that's interesting. Do you know that I'm still class president of my 3rd grade class ? [Williams] Considering you spent 6 years in 3rd grade, it's not such a surprise... [Parizo] Really funny... Tell me Jay Leno, what titles are you gonna go after, here in the VWA ? [Williams] The North American and/or the TV Champ straps look quite enticing to me. [Parizo] You may not be as stupid as you seem, you stay away from the world title and Mario Ducon, that's a wise choice. Now tell me what you think about the excellent wrestler that is Mario Ducon ? [Williams] Frankly, I'm a bit underwhelmed by him. Still, he's the champ, and that says something. I expect people to not take me lightly when they're in the ring with me. Likewise, I don't take Ducon lightly either. I respect him, but if we ever face off in the ring, I feel I'd have a good chance of beating him. [Parizo] We'll see about that. You did some amateur wrestling, you even won a medal ? Why did you come to the pro ? [Williams] "Some amateur wrestling" is putting it lightly. I led my high school to a CIF division championship 2 years in a row, went on to become an NCAA and AAU champ in college, and yes... I won a medal. I took the Silver medal in the 220lbs weight class in the 1992 Olympics. I decided to become pro because even though I learned to valuable skills in college, wrestling was still my life; and I'll continue to wrestle until it becomes un-fun for me. I love the competition. [Parizo] Amateur Wrestling... I don't even know why it's considered a sport. The biggest offensive of an amateur wrestler is the "Zit Popping". It's not that I'm getting bored but we have to wrap-up this thing. One last question, with whom would you like to compete in the near future ? [Williams] Well to be honest, I'm quite new to this fed, and am still in the process of "looking around" for good competition. I'll take on just about all-comers. Still, when I know I'm going to face an opponent, I study tapes of him so I have an idea of what to expect and can formulate a game plan. I'm pretty open to challenges... [Parizo] You know what ??? I was wrong, I take back what I said... I AM bored... I'm outta here, one last thing you'd like to say ? [Williams] Yeah, watch out, I think that the Aerial Marauder is coming this way... [Parizo leaves the set running while Williams is laughing his guts out] ============================================== Aaron: Back to the action please, Mico: TO THE RING!! Too Many Cooks vs Demolition [Bendix] This next match is a tag team match set for one fall or 30 minutes. Introducing first accompanied to the ring by their manager: MR FUJI !!! At a combined weight of 562 pounds: AX, SMASH, DEMOLITION !!! And their opponent:[Satisfaction by the Rolling Stones starts playing] >From New York at a combined weight of 533 pounds: RICK D, DANNY G: TOO MANY COOKS !!! TMC started the match very well with Rick using his wide array of aerial move to get Demolition off their feet. After a near three count over Ax following a flying bodypress, he tagged Danny and Ax tagged Smash. Powerhouse vs powerhouse, we say some real test of strength with Smash getting the upper hands. But as Danny was getting bodyslammed, he tagged Rick who dropped the leg over Smash's head from the top rope. That wasn't enough to knock out the big Smasher and at 22:12 Demolition won the match with the Guillotine on Rick. ================================================ Aaron: First Title Match Mico: TO THE RING!! Mike Sweetser vs Chris Haynes for TV title A flash from Haynes: [Haynes] My my my..It's about *time* that the powers that be *finally gave me what I rightfully deserve a *title* shot! Mike Sweetser..You better polish up that belt nice-like for me..Cuz I wanna be able to see my reflection in it just before I put it around my waist! And keep in mind boys and girls..once I get that Television title..I highly doubt that I will part with it anytime soon! [Bendix] This next match is for the VWA TV title and is set for one fall or a 15 minutes time limit. Introducing first the challenger from Orlando, Florida["What a man" from Salt-n-Pepa starts playing] at 237 pounds: "THE SEX MACHINE" CHRIS HAYNES !!! And his opponent ["U Know" by Boyz II Men starts playing and the crowd goes crazy] from Spokane,WA at 237 pounds: THE VWA TV CHAMPION: MIKE SWEETSER !!! Haynes was very confident coming in this match and really wanted the belt. He knew that Sweetser could be a very good brawler and decided to try to out wrestle him. That made a very good match, with both guys sticking to wrestling. It saw a lot of near falls with Haynes coming very close to winning the match after a spinning headscissors into a cradle. Sweetser than came back with great moves on his own getting a two and a half count following a cannonball neckbreaker. Finally after 13:56 of pure wrestling, Sweetser got the win with a flying bodypress. ================================================= Aaron: Next Title Match Mico: TO THE RING!!! Chris Sullivan vs Raven for North-Am title [Bendix] This match is for the VWA North American title. Introducing first from Short Hills, NJ at 235 pounds: RAVEN !!! And his opponent from Cleveland, OH at 242 pounds: THE VWA NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION: CHRIS SULLIVAN !!! Raven tried to make a brawl out of this fight but Sullivan had other plans. Early in the match he applied all sort of submission move to weaken Raven. Raven tried to use his dirty trick and almost got the pin at 12:31 after putting his feet on the rope for leverage, unfortunately the ref caught him and stopped the count. Finally at 17:48, Sullivan's effort paid off and Raven submitted to the Sullivan Special. ================================================== Aaron: That's it for this week Both: SO LONG EVERYBODY!!!
