Collar and elbow tie-up, and Hart pushed Anderson to the corner, and kneed the Enforcer hard in the gut several times, before he hiptossed Anderson to the middle of the ring. He followed up with a legdrop and went for the cover, but only got a two count. He kept at Anderson for the first few minutes with a series of well-executed moves. Anderson gained the advantage with a reversal of an Irish whip and a perfect spinebuster slam. The crowd suddenly started to boo as Shane Douglas and Francine started to come down to ringside to watch the match. The two of them set up chairs and sat down as they watched Anderson nail Hart with a nasty looking armbreaker. He cinched in an abdominal stretch, and Hart looked almost ready to give up, until he reversed the hold into a pinning move that nearly gave Hart the victory. Hart dropped the elbow, but there was no one home, and Anderson rolled up Hart for a two count, but that was all. Douglas got up and folded up his chair, as Hart caught Anderson with an inverted atomic drop, followed by a nasty clothesline. However, as Hart set up Anderson, and bounced off the ropes, Douglas nailed Hart in the back with the chair. Hart staggered towards Anderson, who then just planted Hart with the DDT for the win at 10:20. Douglas rolled into the ring and started to hammer away at Hart with the chair. Anderson tried to stop him, but got a Belly to Belly Suplex by Douglas for his efforts. Suddenly the crowd popped big as Derek Holmes ran down to ringside and went after the Franchise. He caught Douglas with a HardWay suplex and went all out against him. Francine came up from behind, and as Holmes turned around, he got salt thrown into his eyes. Douglas grabbed the blinded Holmes and nailed the Belly to Belly Suplex right onto the chair. He then took this time to grab the house mic and said a few choice words, while resting his foot on Bret Hart. [Douglas] Ladies and gentlemen of the VWA.....what you have just witnessed is *nothing* compared to what Bret Hart and Derek Holmes will be experiencing Tuesday night in Anaheim. (chuckle) No...the ass kicking that I will give these two will make tonight look like a f**king luau! So you all just sit back and relax, and if you don't like it..you can *all* kiss my ass!
