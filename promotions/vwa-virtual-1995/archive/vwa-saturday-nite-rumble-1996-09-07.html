He nearly got the mask off, when #1 thumbed Sullivan in the eye, and caught him with a small package, but didn't get the three. #1 bounced off the ropes, but Sullivan was ready, and he caught #1 with a knee to the gut. He quickly tagged in Haynes, who nailed a second-rope kneedrop right on the head of #1. Haynes then ripped off the mask, revealing "Whiplash" Barry Williams!! When #2 saw that his partner's mask was off, he removed his as well, revealing Arn Anderson!! When the Express saw this, they went through the roof!! Cornette was going nuts on the outside, and the Express decided that the best way to take care of this would be to beat the living piss out of Williams. Unfortunately, Williams managed to tag in Anderson during all the confusion, and Anderson started to clean house. At this time, Parizo and Luger walk down the aisle, stopping halfway to monitor the match, and they watched as Anderson caught Haynes, and then Sullivan with inverted atomic drops. Anderson finally caught Haynes in the DDT, and went for the pin, but Sullivan pulled Haynes to the outside, and the three walked out, grabbing the belts en route. They walked past Luger and Parizo, as the ref counted to ten, and awarded the match to Williams and Anderson by countout in 23:26. Luger and Parizo started conferring, and then just turned around and left to go backstage. [Summers] What a match! Williams and Anderson *had* them, but the champs decided instead to run. [Steeves] Run, hide...do what they want..but they're still the champs. [Hennig] What I'm curious about is why Parizo and Luger were at ringside. [Summers] That's a questin we'll have to ask at another time...cause we are out of it! For Curt Hennig, Chris Steeves, and Tony Marx, I'm Tom Summers, saying see you next week!
