VWA Wednesday Nite Havoc (1/17/96)

17 January 1996

Introducing first from parts unknown at 253 pounds: THE BOUNTY HUNTER !!! [Hunter walks to the ring to "You could be mine" from GNR.] And his opponent: from Denver, Colorado at 450 pounds: BIG VAN VADER !!! [The crowd goes crazy as fireworks start exploding.] [Summers] Hello everyone!! We are just a couple of days away from the Lightning Clash!! [Steeves] That's right, Tommy Boy. It's put up or shut up time for a lot of people here! Just like this next match..it's time for The Bounty Hunter to see if he can beat someone with the satute of Vader..and I just hope Vader finally just shuts up. [Summers] Remind me to get Vader after this match, so you can mention that to him. [Steeves] Heh..go ahead..I'm gonna blame you for it. He likes me. Hunter attacked Vader before the bell and he decided the throw the rule book away as he used all kinds of choke on Vader. He then tried a clothesline from the top rope but he couldn't take Vader off his feet. He then went for a bodyslam... big mistake. Vader gave him a big forearm smash on the back driving Hunter to the mat. Vader then sent him slamming hard in the corner and after a big powerslam, it was VADERTIME !!! Vader grabbed Hunter and did his famous powerbomb for the pin at 3:22. [Summers] That guy is unbelievable. It should be interesting to see how he is in the World Wrestling Federation. [Steeves] Hell, I'm suprised they let him keep his name and not give him something stupid like "Ringmaster" Steve Austin. Vader musta done some major scaring or something to keep the name. [Summers] Right..they were afraid of the person who got the snot kicked out of him by Paul Orndorff. [Steeves] You wanna go tell *that* to his face? [Summers] I think not. Well, The Erickson's are coming up next to face the Powers of Pain, but I wanna take this time to thank Mico and Aaron again for their outstanding job during the month of December here on Havoc! [Steeves] *pfuh!* Ha! *Those* rejects from a Bruce Lee movie? You've gotta be kidding me!! Balsa wood has more of a personality than those two! All they did was bitch about how there's no Japanese talent here...you know why there isn't? [Summers] Why? [Steeves] Because they're busy wrestling in *JAPAN!!!!* Christ! [Summers] *sigh* Here's some words from the Ericksons. ------------------------------------ [Chris] Well Vic here we are in the Big Leagues and everybody will see us rule. [Vic] You bet they will so Powers of Pain I hope you enjoy pain [Big Bad Erickson walks in] Well guys here we go. ------------------------------------ [Bendix] This next bout is a tag team match set for one fall with a 30 minutes time limit. Introducing first: ["Black Hole Sun" by Soundgarden starts playing.]..at a total combined weight of 591 pounds: THE WARLORD, THE BARBARIAN: THE POWER OF PAIN !!! [Warlord and Barbarian come down the aisle completely ignoring the crowd.] [Steeves] God they're big. [Summers] Yeah..and still a good team..even though they're with Rob Parker. [Steeves] They're with Parker? [Summers] Yeah..as the Super Assassins. [Steeves] *They're* the Assassins?? [Summers] Yeah. [Steeves] God, they're stupid. [Bendix] And their opponent: accompanied to the ring by their managers: The Knockout Twins [two very good looking women] and their brother: BIG BAD ERICKSON. At a combined weight of 467 pounds from Canada: VICIOUS VIC AND CRIPPLER CHRIS: THE ERICKSONS !!! [Steeves] Hominahominahominahomina.... [Summers] Want a napkin? [Steeves] Huh? No... [Summers] Want a bucket to catch all the drool? [Steeves] Shut up. POP tried to use their weight advantage to intimidate the brothers early in the match. At one point, Vic were caught in the POP corner and while the ref was trying to stop Chris from interfering, Warlord and Barbarian did a lot of damage on the poor guy. Warlord then went for his powerslam but Chris jumped in the ring and stopped the count at 2. That brought Barbarian in the ring as well and it turned into a four guys brawl. The brothers, realizing they couldn't match the POP power in a brawl then started doing what they do best: wrestle. And at 28:22. Vic Erickson pinned Barbarian following his Just Plain Vicious ( a DDT). [Steeves] Wow. Nice. [Summers] Yeah..the Ericksons were very impressive this evening. [Looks at Steeves] Oh will you stop looking at the *twins??!!!* [Steeves] Aw..why? [Summers] We have another great match coming up, as Alex Windham and Scott Romero, the Devastators..will be facing the relatively new team of the Nightmares, with their manager, Miss Valerie. [Steeves] YES!!!! [Summers] Why are you so excited? [Steeves looks at him] Oh for God's sake..have you been *that* unlucky with women?? [Bendix] This next bout is a tag team match scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by their manager Miss Valerie, from parts unknown at a combined weight of 600 pounds: THE NIGHTMARES !!! [They walk to the ring, dressed exactly alike with black mask, to the theme of Halloween]. [Steeves] Where the hell did *these* guys come from?? [Summers] They were acquired while we were on vacation. [Steeves] Sorta remind me of those Super Assassins...aw no..you don't think?? [Summers] Doubt it. They can't change clothes *that* fast. [Steeves] Too bad. She's much better looking than Parker. [Bendix] Their opponents...["More Human Than Human" by Black Zombie starts playing]..From Denver, Colorado at a combined weight of 526 pounds: ALEX WINDHAM and SCOTT ROMERO: THE DEVASTATORS !!! [Steeves] Oh whee...Mutt and Jeff are still here. [Summers] Who? [Steeves] Mutt and Jeff? An old comic strip from the thirties? You *can't* be *that* ignorant. Nightmare #2, the bigger of the two started the match against Romero. They did a test of strength and it was clear that #2 was stronger. He took control of the match hitting Romero with a big slam and an elbow drop. When Windham got in the ring, the pace shifted and the Devastators took control. He nearly got the pin over #1 at 14:38 after a slingshot bodyblock but #2 interfered and stopped the count. The Devastators won the match at 21:29 when Scott executed The Cruise Missile ( a flying forearm smash from the top rope) as the ref was counting, Miss Valerie pulled him out of the ring and the Nightmares were disqualified for it. The two teams continued to brawl for several minutes after the match. [Steeves] Oh, I like her. She's got a style sorta like Woman's. [Summers] Great. God made *two* of them. [Steeves] Somehow I think you have the wrong deity here, Tommy boy. [Summers] Right. Next up should be a good one. We have "Boy Toy" Mike Baldwin facing a man who's definately been on a tear as of late here, Thunderhawk. [Steeves] Ok..I'll forgive the occasional McMahon or Monsoon sayings...but if you ever imitate Bischoff...I kill you. [Bendix] This match is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minutes time limit. Introducing first: ["What A Man" by Salt & Peppa starts playing] accompanied to the ring by his manager Slick Rick, from Hollywood California at 255 pounds: "BOY TOY" MIKE BALDWIN !!! [Summers] My my..doesn't he remind you of someone? [Steeves] Rick Rude? [Summers] Nope. [Steeves] Chris Haynes? [Summers] Nope..though I'm sure he's gonna bitch about Baldwin stealing his music. [Steeves] Who then? [Summers] No. If you're gonna be that blind..you don't *deserve* to know. [Steeves] Aw...c'mon..pleeze?? [Bendix] And his opponent: [Thunderstruck by AC/DC starts playing] accompanied to the ring by his manager Lisa Lightning... [Steeves] Thank you lord!!! I *love* tonight!! [Bendix] from Parts Unknown weighing in at 315 pounds....THUNDERHAWK !!! [Summers] Impressive enterance, wouldn't you say? [Steeves] Enchanting. [Summers] Don't like him much, do you? The Boy Toy made the mistake of trying to seduce Miss Lightning and that made Thunderhawk very mad. He attacked Baldwin with a punch and a kick combo and then held him while Lisa nailed him in the face with her purse. Slick Rick tried to get involved but he was received with a dropkick. Baldwin took the opportunity to sneak from behind and nailed Thunderhawk with a knee in the back. He then went for a clothesline and a double axe handle from the second rope. But it wasn't enough and Thunderhawk got the win at 12:31 following a back brain kick. [Summers] Not bad..Thunderhawk's definately showing why he's one of the top superstars here. [Steeves] Whooptee sh*t. [Summers] Well..you should like this..he's coming back later for Parizo's chat. [Steeves] HAH! *That* I have to see! [Bendix] This next match is set for one fall. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by his manager: Master Fugiama. At 238 pounds, from Nagasaki, Japan...SOUL TAKER !!! [Summers] This is going to be a wild match, I think. With the martial arts skills we are about to see in the next couple of matches...we are definately show the e-wrestling world that we are a well-rounded fed. And his opponent: [ Gangsta's Paridise from Coolio starts playing] accompanied to the ring by his manager Miles Duncan, from the Valley of the Shadow of Death at 317 pounds: "GANGSTA POWER" SABIN FIGARO !!! [Sabin walks to the ring with his 62 years old manager and does his little ceremony with the roses as Soul Taker is looking respectfully] [Steeves] Yeah. Probably. But there's one other good thing about these martial arts matches. [Summers] Yeah? Whassat? [Steeves] Aaron and Mico can finally shut their mouths and stop bitching. [Summers] Oh, you just *love* making new friends, don't you? It was a very good match as Figaro showed everybody that his martial arts knowledge was up to par with Soul Taker's. Figaro started the match strongly, having a big weight advantage and surprised Taker with an Enziguri kick and a japanese armlock. At 6:32, Soul Taker reversed an irish whip and introduced Figaro to the turnbuckle. He then used his speed to get the big guy off his feet with a jumping spin kick and a martial art combo. Soul Taker finally won the match at 9:45 following a belly-to-belly suplex. [Summers] I'm not used to the speed of some of these types of matches, but that was great! This really could have gone either way! [Steeves] Never been to Japan? [Summers] Nope. I started in this biz in October. [Steeves] I hung out in Japan for a few years..saw a couple of JCWF matches..blow the roof off this place with some of the matches I saw there... [Bendix] This match is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minutes time limit. Introducing first from Tokyo, Japan [Oriental music starts playing.], weighing in at 234 pounds: THE GREAT MUTA !!! [Steeves] ...but if you thought *that* match was great...wait'll you see *this* one. And his opponent from Tokyo, Japan, weighing 233 pounds: JUSHIN LIGER !!! [Steeves] These guys have done crap that would make Shawn Michaels or Bret Hart look like paperweights. Sting? No contest... Hogan? Heh...heheh.... heheheh....BWAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!! This was the perfect example of a great wrestling match. Both guys worked their asses off. Liger took some great bumps, even attempting a splash from the top rope to the outside of the ring. He nearly got the pin a belly to back suplex from the top rope but Muta managed to kick out. As Liger went for a moonsault, Muta lifted his legs and caught him with a knee in the stomach. Muta capitalized on that and after many near pins after various suplex, he got the pin with a moonsault of his own at 18:03. [Summers] Wow. [Steeves] Toldja....crazy stuff. And Aaron and Mico expect guys like them to *voluntarily* come to the States?? *That's* even funnier than mentioning Hogan...heh...HAHAHAH...*chortle* [Summers] Ok..ok..we get the point now. [Bendix] This match is set for one fall with a 20 minute time limit. Introducing first: ["Starseed" from OLP starts playing] from Vancouver, Canada at 327 pounds: RAINE TURNER !!! [Steeves] He is one mean SOB...have you seen some of the stuff he's done in the last few weeks? [Summers] Yeah. I have a feeling that this match isn't gonna be like those Japanese matches we saw earlier. [Steeves] Ever the discoverer of obviousness, aren't you? [Bendix] And his opponent: accompanied to the ring by his manager: Slick Rick and his friend Tidal Wave: from Parts Unknown at 350 pounds: RAEKWON !!! [Steeves] Oy! He's big! [Summers] And pissed. He wants to be the TV champ with a passion. [Steeves] Now, I like both these guys..so to be honest I'm just gonna sit back and watch the spleens fly. Turner didn't seem too worried about Tidal Wave and Slick Rick outside the ring. He concentrated on Raekwon and attacked him before the bell with a big clothesline. He followed it with a big powerslam and a standing suplex, showing his great strength. As he went running into the rope, Slick Rick tripped him and Raekwon used this opportunity to drop a big elbow on Turner's back. He then body press him over the top rope onto the concrete and while the ref was holding him back, Tidal Wave started kicking Turner and he thrown him back in the ring. Raekwon then went for a big power bomb and Turner kicked out at two and a half. Turner then reversed an irish whip into the rope and nailed Raekwon with a flying clothesline. Finally at 9:45 Turner went for the Birdman but Tidal Wave jumped in the ring and kicked Turner in the back, earning his friend a DQ. They then went at it three against one on Turner. They left the ring laughing, walking over the out cold, but still victorious Raine Turner. [Summers] Amazing..they aren't even fair to their own kind. [Steeves] I thought this was the land of neutrality in the VWA anyway, so what the hell are *you* complaining about? [The annoying french song starts playing...] [Summers] Aw....crap. That. [Parizo] Welcome everybody to this week Parizo's Chat. First I'd like to say thank you to all the people that phoned in or wrote to congratulate me and Mario for our victory last week in the tag match. [Summers] HA! Yeah....all three. Reno Riggins, Steve Lombardi, and Iron Mike Sharpe. [Parizo] Let's just say, it was an easy victory, like all our victory and like our victory in three days at the Lightning Clash against Marty Jannetty. Now my guest this week, I guess we have to congratulate him too, cos he won earlier today. He is: THUNDERMOUTH !!! Euh HAWK !!! Sorry. [Thunderhawk walks in with Lisa Lightning] Welcome Mr. Thunder to the greatest show in town: The Parizo's Chat. First, let me ask you, where did you meet that lovely girl Lisa Lightning ? [TH] It was a long time ago...on an empty street in Canada during a thunderstorm. [Steeves] Uh-huh...oookaaayyy. She musta gotten hit with lightning in that storm. It's the only logical reason her brain would be *that* scrambled as to actually hook up with this guy. [Parizo] Oh, I should have known by the way you dress... You're homeless, I'm very sorry... Maybe Doug Hills could let you sleep in the VWA arena when there's no match. You should ask him. Now, back to wrestling you have a fair win-loss record, you even beat Ric Flair, what is your goal here in the VWA ? [TH] Fair win record ??? What kind of match have you been watching ? I'm 4-1 in single competition. [Parizo] Yeah, but you do have a 1 in the loss column, unlike let's say... Mario Ducon, who is still undefeated in singles competition. [Summers] That doesn't even deserve a response. [Parizo] But you didn't answer my question, what's your goal here in the VWA, do you need me to speak slower or something. [TH] Don't have a heart attack, my goal is to hold EVERY title in the VWA at least ONCE!. [Steeves] *pfuh!* Yeah right! I'll go reserve that room in Bellvue for you, Mr. Thunder. [Parizo] HAHAHA !!! What color is the sky in your world ? You'll never get the WORLD title, at least not until Mario decide to retire. Is it your first experience in the world of e-wrestling ? Did you ever wrestle in another federation ? [TH] This is my first E-Wrestling league, and I plan to make a BIG impact. [Parizo] You know something, too many of those little pills could get you very sick. What would you consider to be your strong point as a wrestler ? [TH] My size and strength..If I don't intimidate you I WILL hurt you instead! And quite it with your little comments, you're getting on my nerve. [Parizo] Your face sure looks intimidating to me. What would be you flaws ? [TH] I HAVE no flaw's little man! [Steeves] Uh-huh...aren't we forgetting your *loss*, Sparky? [Parizo] Yeah right, what do you think of the competition in the VWA? [TH] There are some tough opponents..but I can handle them. [Parizo] Some of them I'm sure... All of them, I doubt it. Along with single wrestling. Do you have any plans to team up with anyone in the future? [TH] I will only tell you this...Me and Lisa have some plans in the works that will Shake up the whole VWA!!. We'll let you be the first to know though. [Parizo] What is it ? What is it ? I wanna know NOW !!! Please, please, pleaaaaaaaase. I won't tell anyone. [Summers] You ever see a guy act this stupid? [Steeves] Yeah..you at your New Year's party. [TH] HAHAHA !!! You're pathetic. [Parizo] Well Mr.Hawk you sound very confident in yourself...But what makes you think you can beat the AMAZING Mario Ducon!!!? You may have beaten Ric Flair. But you are nothing compared to Mario Ducon!!!? [ThunderHawk grabs Parizo by the neck and lift's him off the ground, while taking the mike in hand] LISTEN HERE LITTLE MAN!!! I've heard enough of your rambling. I can beat Mario Ducon ANYTIME, ANYPLACE!! Him and YOU..you little WEASEL...have been dodging me!! [Summers] Hey! That name's copyrighted! [TH] He may have put the Marauder out of action, but Thunderhawk is different!!! NO ONE PUT'S THUNDERHAWK out of action!! YOU GOT THAT LITTLE MAN??!!!. [Mario Ducon comes running down to the interview area. Coming up behind Thunderhawk. Thunderhawk drops Jacques Parizo and turns around. Mario Ducon and Thunderhawk look at each other for a few second] [Parizo] Well, Well, Well Mr.Hawk, you're not talking now !!! [TH] Action speak louder than words, come to the ring Ducon and you'll see what I mean. [Parizo] You know what, go ahead, we'll be right behind you. [As Thunderhawk turns to walk to the ring, Ducon grabs him from behind and gets him in the Referendum.] [Parizo] Never turn you back to the VWA world champion, HAHAHA !!! [Ducon] Au Revoir Monsieur Hawk!! Fait de beaux reves... [Mario Ducon and Mr.Parizo walk off with the annoying french song playing] [Summers] Can't Ducon do *anything* clean? [Steeves] Hey..when you're the VWA World Champion..you can do anything..and if you doubt me...ask Flair. [Summers] Needless to say..Thunderhawk's not gonna be too happy when he comes to. [Steeves] Prob'ly not. Then again, what do I care? [Summers] We have our main event coming up next! World Television Champion Mike Sweetser will see if he can keep his current winning streak going as he faces the blueblood Jamie Cutlass! [Bendix] This next match is for the VWA TV title with a 15 minutes time limit. Introducing first the challenger: from his family's private island at 243 pounds: JAMIE CUTLASS !!! [Steeves] Sorta has a Hunter Helmsley quality about him. [Summers] What? The *arrogance* or just the annoying way he walks down that aisle with his nose up. You can see the nosehairs! Jeez! He should meet up with Rano or something..at least he can have someone to look up with. And his opponent: ["U Know" by Boyz II Men starts playing and the crowd goes crazy] from Spokane, WA at 237 pounds: THE VWA TV CHAMPION: MIKE SWEETSER !!! [Summers] Sweetser is definately one of the more popular stars in the VWA today! [Steeves] Lemme guess what you're gonna say next.."The most energetic..the most flamboyant..the most charis--" Hey!! As Mike was walking toward the ring, he was attacked from behind by Raekwon. A brawl started with both guys trading hard punch. A bunch of officials and some referee tried to stop the brawl but Raine Turner decided to join the party and it became a three-way brawl as both guys were punching the two others. Finally, Sweetser managed to make his way to the ring as the two others brawled all the way to the dressing room. As soon as Mike entered the ring, Cutlass was all over him. Cutlass, seeing his opponent already very tired, applied a lot of submission moves to wear Sweetser down even more. Sweetser managed to draw some sort of offensive after reversing a suplex but the damage was done and he finally submitted at 12:10 to a Crossbow Submission. [Steeves] Well..Cutlass definately has potential as a champion. Once you have a chance at winning..no matter what..take advantage of it! We definately saw that! [Summers] Unfortunately, you're right..what we also saw was definately not the end of whatever's going on between Sweetser, Raekwon, and Turner. I feel that Sweetser's been robbed, but we'll see what the VWA head says about this soon enough. Well, we've gotta run! We'll see you in three days in Albany, New York, for the VWA LIGHTNING CLASH!!!! Copyright 1996 Reeeeeeeaaaaaaallllyyy Big Sports, Ink. Marc ( [detail withheld] )
