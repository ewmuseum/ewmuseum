VWA Re-post (Sort of) About Opinions of the Fed

20 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi again, The pervious post I made last night got removed this morning, so I'm gonna try again :) Anyway, I'm the head booker of the Virtual Wrestling Alliance, an e-fed that I'm happy to say has lasted over nine months. Now, like an infant in its mother's womb, it must eventually open itself to the outside world and grow (see the nine months analogy? :) To do that, two things are going to happen. First, odds are Myself and my partners are going to become a little more vocal by posting more than just the shows and PPVs. Offhand, I don't know *what*, but I figure to give you the early warning :) Second (and the main reason for last night's post), I would like some opinions from those of you that may read some of the VWA's shows or PPVs. I already know the opinions of those already in the fed, but I'm curious what people *outside* the fed may think of the shows and PPVs (that is, if any of you *do* read the posts :). I am a person that is very open to opinions and suggestions, and any that you would like to give would be greatly appreciated. You don't necessarily have to reply to me personally to this post. In fact, I'd prefer it if you would respond to the post publicly (sorry, being new to news servers, I'm not familiar with the term :), so that everyone could read your opinions, suggestions, flames, whatever. Thanks for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you all. [name withheld] President/Head Booker/Writer - PPVs Virtual Wrestling Alliance
