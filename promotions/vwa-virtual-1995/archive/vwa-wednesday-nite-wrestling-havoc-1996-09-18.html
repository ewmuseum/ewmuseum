[Ventura] Jesse I do, Jacques Parizo has agreed to give me an exclusive interview to discuss the statue of the Clan. [Marx] Let's go to the ring right now for our first match. [Bendix] (After announcing Steve Stap)...and his opponent...(Metallica's "For Whom The Bell Tolls" starts up)....from Heavenly, Colorado..weighing in at 315 pounds....here is....GABRIEL!!!! The lights dim down and are replaced by fluorescent blue, red, purple lights. A billow of mist pours out of the entrance ramp. Gabriel walks methodically down to the ring, never taking his eyes off the opponent in the ring. Once the bell rings Gabriel methodically destroys his opponent. Throwing his opponent around like a rag doll, he moves on to various suplexes, slams and throws and other various maneuvers ranging from necksnaps, drop kicks, and balancing on the top rope walk (Ala Undertaker), Gabriel then throws his opponent into the ropes and performs a flying clothesline. Then he performs a devastating backbreaker leaving his opponent writhing in pain. Then Gabriel puts his opponent into his patented submission finisher, the Crucifier. The ref doesn't even have to ask, and the match is over. Before the announcer can proclaim Gabriel as the victor he has already left the ring and is calmly leaving. the ref with no ones hand to raise simply stands in the ring, looking confused then goes to attend to the beaten wrestler, still lying where he was discarded. [Ventura] Cool debut for this new VWA star, Gabriel. Stap didn't even get to do one move. [Marx] Impressive, but he seems to have a very high opinion of himself, some wrestlers here won't like that very much. [Ventura] From what I just saw, I think he can handle it. [Marx] Only time will tell. It's now time for our next match as Alex Windham makes his debut as a single wrestler to face Owen Hart. I wonder if his Horsemen friends will be there. [Ventura] I'm sure they'll be watching... [Bendix] This bout is schedule for one fall!! ["Bulls On Parade" by Rage Against the Machine starts up] Introducing first...representing the Four Horsemen...from Calgary, Alberta, Canada....weighing in at 234 pounds..... here is OWEN HART!!!!!! And his opponent: from Denver, CO at 239 pounds: ALEX WINDHAM !!! [Windham walked to the ring to the music of "More Human Than Human" by White Zombie] Owen Hart got the upper hand early in the match and nailed Windham with an a bodyslam and an elbowdrop. He then went for the fast pin, holding the tights, but Windham kicked out at two. Owen then followed with a crucifix and again went for the pin, this time using the ropes for leverage. Again Windham kicked out at two. Hart kept the upper hand for a while longer but he missed with a sunset flip and Alex finally got some offenses in. He nailed Owen with a clothesline and followed with a double underhook suplex and then went for an armbar submission, but Owen was able to reach the ropes. At this point, Gabriel came out of the dressing room and came to ringside to watch the fight. Both Alex and Owen noticed him but didn't let him take their concentration away. Alex kept the upper hand for a few more minutes but when he tried to jump on Owen from the top rope, Owen raised his knees and nailed Windham in the stomach. Owen then kept the upper hand for a while and the end came when Owen jumped from the top rope and nailed Alex with a flying body press. He went for the pin, again holding the tights, but this time Alex Windham was able to reverse it at the count of two and he got the three count at 16:14. Owen was still down and Gabriel jumped into the ring and attacked Owen. He nailed him with stomps and kicks, then performed a belly to belly suplex. Then he performed a back breaker, and then slapped on his finisher the Crucifier. He kept it on and wouldn't let go. Then Pillman, Benoit and Sam Holmes came running to the ring and Gabriel left the other way before they could catch him. As they were helping Owen up, Saxon appeared on the big screen with Gabriel, from the locker room. [Saxon] Gabriel, why did you do that for ??? [Gabriel] This Is just an EXAMPLE of what I plan to do. Gabriel his here to pave the way for the Apocalypse, but first he must weed out the WEAK, and PATHETIC, like, the weak fool in the ring right now. [Saxon] Owen Hart is weak ??? He's one of the best wrestlers here ? Do you have the slightest idea of what'll happen to you ??? You don't mess with the Four Horseman this way !!! [Gabriel] It doesn't matter WHO THAT WAS. He was weak, and that was all that mattered. If he is lucky enough to walk away from this alive , and is fool enough to come looking for me............well then ........ I will be glad to finish what I have started.........Come for me Owen Hart, but be warned. Don't come looking for me unless you are prepared to make the... ultimate sacrifice............... Don't come looking for me unless You Are ready to be DESTROYED!!!!!!! [The screen then fade to black] [Marx] This guy is MAD !!! I don't think he knows what he just got himself in. [Ventura] This fed's full of psycho, but I don't think that Gabriel's one of them. If you want to make a name for yourself here, you don't go after guys like Iron Mike Sharp or Steve Stap. You go after the big guns and there ain't not bigger guns than the Horseman. [Marx] Well I guess you got that right, but still, I wouldn't want to be in his shoes... Right now we have some comments from Bob Hansen concerning his match last week against Marty Jannetty. =================================================================== [Hansen] MJ, ya gave me one hellova match, I came up short. Well, logic says nothing is perfect, no offense Mr. Hennig. Maybe we'll try it again, Marty. I now.... I have the biggest challenge of my career. The Bounty Hunter. He was hired by the Illumaniti, and soon, vengeance is embodied in me, and all the power will be unleashed on you. May the Lord have mercy on your soul. ===================================================================== [Marx] This should be a very good match. [Ventura] The entire PPV will be good, this is VWA, don't forget it. [Marx] Of course, let's now go to the ring for our next match... [Bendix] [After introducing Jerry "Job's to the stars" Andrews] His opponent: [The theme to "Psycho" starts up.] accompanied to the ring by his manager, Slick Rick, from parts unknown...weighing in at 350 pounds... RAEKWON!!!!! Raekwon was totally dominating in this match. The crowd was in awe throughout the whole match as Raekwon just destroyed Jerry having no mercy on him. Raekwon did some bone crushing maneuvers like DDT's, Piledrivers, atomic drops, neck breakers, you name it. When Raekwon finally decided enough was enough with the punishment he was dishing out, he pinned Andrews 1-2-3 after the Top Rope Power Bomb. Winner: Raekwon (6:31) [Ventura] And a great win for Raekwon. [Marx] Seem like he's back in single competition, I wonder what are his plans now ? At this point, his challenger is so dazed, the Beast sets his man up onto the ropes and finishes him with the Frankensteiner to win his first match over his opponent. [Ventura] So, Williams apart, are you looking for new members for the Clan ? [Parizo] Not really, I like it the way it is now. Sure I'd like to have Williams, but that's pretty much it. [Parizo] It's true that there was some kind of misunderstanding last week between Luger and Mario, but we all talked about it and everything is fine now. Now if you'll excuse me, I gotta go. THE TERMINATOR!!!! And his opponent: from parts unknown....weighing in at 236 pounds.... "Mr. Excitement" DROPKICK!!!! He then grabbed Dropkick by the arm and just ripped the handcuffs from the guardrail. He then dragged him all the way to the ring and started beating him up. He nailed him with a lariat and then followed with a devastating swinging neckbreaker. He then picked him back up and took him back down with a Gorilla Press. He continued to destroy him for a little longer and then nailed him with the Death Drop for the pin at 7:04 [Terminator grabs the mic] You know something..you'd think that after ALL THIS TIME...people would get the point. You don't f*ck with The Terminator. You don't f*ck with the NWA World champion! <booes and assorted stuff> Raekwon, I had a nice asskicking to dish out tonight to this punk Dropkick. Where's this guy's partner, Hiptoss? But boy, you had to stick your nose in where it doesn't belong! If you wanted to pound on this punk, do it before or after my match. But when I step in this ring, son, you stay OUT of my way! And Aerial Marauder.." <cheers at Marauder's name> You haven't learned that lesson yet, boy. But you will. TRUST ME, Marauder..you will. *NOBODY* SLAPS THE TERMINATOR AND LIVES, BOY! I'll catch you..and when I do..I'll have TWO championships. [Marx] I can't believe this, he destroyed Dropkick and man handled Raekwon like a doll... [Ventura] He wants the World Title he wants it bad, but what's worst is that when he gets in the ring with the Marauder, he won't think about titles, he'll only think about destroying him. [Marx] That's scary. We saw the debut of HardCore last week and now a little earlier this week, Jack Saxon had a chance to talk with them. Let's hear it. ========================================================== [It's raining hard on a dark, dreary night] [Saxon] Hello, VWA fans, Jack Saxon here again, I'm out here, soaked, waiting for a new tag team, HardCore, and their manager, Zendl. [Mayhem] Thunderhawk. . .you watched my power against the Mexican Connection. . . now I invite you to feel it up close. . .WELCOME TO MAYHEM !!!! ============================================================= [Ventura] Strong words from strong wrestlers, but I believe Mill Guest saw that interview and has some things to say to them... ============================================================== [RabitHead and Mill are sitting in their locker room in the VWA Arena. [RabitHead] Ya'll are gonna get the beatin' of your life Grendel and whichever one of your boys is dumb enough to wanna rassle little Mill. Time to eat your Wheaties, Mill. . . ===================================================================== [Marx] It seems we have a feud going... [Ventura] Those new comers sure don't waste time here in the VWA... [Marx] Let's go back to the ring for another match. [Bendix] [After introducing Steve Winslow] And his oppoenent: managed by Slick Rick, from the Bronx Zoo at 260 pounds: METHOD MAN !!!
