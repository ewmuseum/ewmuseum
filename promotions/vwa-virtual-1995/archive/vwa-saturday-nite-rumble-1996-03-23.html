Williams dropkicked Roberts out of the ring and nailed Ducon with a Tornado Pildriver for the win at 14:23. As Parizo was helping Ducon out of the ring, Roberts and Williams had a long stare down and finally Roberts left the ring and grabbed the mic: [Roberts] Williams, you beat Luger, you beat Holmes and now you got a lucky win against Ducon. [Bendix] This next match is set for one fall with a 15 minutes time limit and is for the VWA TELEVISON TITLE: Introducing first the challenger, accompanied to the ring by his manager Bill Thomas, from Toledo, Ohio at 250 pounds, one half of the VWA Tag Team champions: SHAWNTELL SPEED !!! And his opponent: accompanied to the ring by his manager Slick Rick, at 350 pounds from parts unknown: THE VWA TV CHAMPION: RAEKWON !!! Raekwon didn't seem to affected by the brawl he had earlier with Vader and got the upper hand early in the match, nailing Speed with an armdrag takedown followed by a knee to the back. Raekwon kept the upper hand for a few more minutes, before Speed blocked a fireman's carry and nailed Raekwon with a enzuigiri followed by a high dropkick. Speed then kept Raekwon in an abdominal stretch for about 30 seconds but he refused to submit. Speed nearly got the win, a little later, when nailed Raekwon with a Soviet Suplex followed by a belly-to-belly suplex for the two count. Speed tried to whip Raekwon into the ropes, but he reversed it and nailed Speed with a back heel kick followed by neck-scissors. As he was about to go for the pin, Bill Thomas jumped on the ring apron and started yelling at the referee. Vader then made his way to ring side and dragged Raekwon out of the ring and power bombed him on the concrete. He then clotheslined Slick Rick and left. The referee turned and counted Raekwon out. After Speed left the ring with Thomas, Frank Knight walked to the ringside and he standed over Raekwon knocked-out body. He had that nasty grin on his face. He did nothing, he just stared at the TV champ. As he left a few of the fans started chanting "Crazy Frank, Crazy Frank, Crazy Frank" over and over, which brought a snarl to Frank's face as he glared at them and continued back to the locker area. [Steeves] What is he doing here ? [Summers] He's facing Raekwon next week for the title, maybe he's looking for the psychological advantage.
