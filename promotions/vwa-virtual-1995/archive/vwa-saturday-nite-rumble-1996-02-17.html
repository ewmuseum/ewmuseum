He did a bodyslam and then he got him in a boston crab. Sting was in it for about a minute but he managed to grab the rope. Baldwin tried a suplex but Sting reversed it, but it looks like he injured his back even more. Baldwin was the first guy up and he went on the top rope for a big elbow drop but Sting rolled out of the way. Sting then got the upper hand for the next couple minutes. He nailed Baldwin with big clothesline and a big powerslam. He nearly got the pin after a flying bodypress. He then went for the Scorpion Deathlock but Baldwin kicked him in the butt and he rammed into the referee, knocking him off in the process. Slick Rick tried to interfere but Sting nailed him with a big right hand. Sting then managed to get Baldwin in the Scorpion Deathlock but the ref was still out. Slick Rick got back on his feet and grabbed a chair, has he was about to climb into the ring, Raine Turner came running and nailed Rick with a fire extinguisher. He then did a Birdman to Slick Rick right onto the floor. Turner then climbed in to the ring and to everybody's surprise, nailed Sting in the back with the extinguisher. He then grabbed the almost passed out Baldwin and putted him on Sting. He then woke up the referee and left.
