Rick fell on the mat but when Torch tried to pick him up, he got him in a small package but Torch kicked out at two. Torch tagged in Burn and they started double teaming Rick. Scott came running to help, but the ref stopped him, which gave more than enough time to the Demolition to do their damage. They stayed on top of Rick for a while but couldn't get him out. Finally, Rick ducked a clothesline attempt by Burn and nailed him with a clothesline of his own. He tried to tag in Scott but as he was about to do it, Torch came in and distracted the ref. Scott complained but the ref wouldn't let him in. And Demolition did even more damage during this time. They went for a spike piledriver, but Rick countered with a backdrop and Scott nailed Torch, who was jumping from the top, with a big clothesline. Rick finally tagged in Scott and the Steiners got the Demolition in quite ride. They stunned Torch with a double dropkick that sent him flying outside the ring and Scott nailed Burn with a devastating Frankensteiner. Scott jumped on Torch on the outside with a plancha and Rick nailed Burn with a Steinerline. Ax jumped on the apron and was nailed by a punch form Rick. At this time, the Glamour Boys came running to ringside and while the ref was busy with Rick and Burn, they nailed Scott from behind and got him in a piledriver on the floor. The ref got Rick outside the ring and the Glamour Boys sent Scott, who was the legal man, in the ring. Burn covered Scott for the pin. Rick tried to stop the count but the Glamour boys held to his leg to stop him. Finally, the ref counted three and the Demolition became the new North-American champions at 22:40.
