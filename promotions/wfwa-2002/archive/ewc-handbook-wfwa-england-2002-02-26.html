[EWC] Handbook [WFWA: England]

English Wrestling Championship http://englishwrestling.cjb.net    EWC: There's an art to ewrestling! Contents 1. History of the fed 2. What sort of fed is it? 3. Who runs EWC 4. Shows 5. Results 6. Roleplaying 7. Titles 8. Web Site 9. Alliance 10. Closing Note History of the Fed   English Wrestling Championship was opened to the public on the 10th of July 1999. At first it was very small and only had a very simple web site. All of its shows were wrote by hand but were very simple indeed. The site was updated with the roster and some small news but that was about it. Then after a month of emailing the results EWC started to put shows on the web site, they were no longer wrote in the same style but still they were not in very long detail and did not involve announcers or complicated angles.   Then EWC started to use announcers this play by play style developed into what is seen today. A show that is extremelly detailed and won lots of claim and applause from everyone that has read them. Many people say that EWC has had matches longer than other feds shows.   As each show goes by EWC tries to top the last making them bigger and better. This steady progression has resulted in some of the most sophisticated and exciting wrestling and storylines. What sort of fed is it? EWC like many others only accepts original wrestlers (not real wrestlers) it has a great roleplaying past and since it opened nearly 3 years ago it has had many talented writers pass through its doors. So as you can guess the promotion chooses winners by the standard of their roleplays. It has a mixture of wrestling and storylines. Who runs EWC English Wrestling Championship has always been run by one person. It does not have late shows and the standard is second to none. The on screen owners of EWC is Vortex Industries who brought the company from Bungle (X8oD, TCW and DWF). Shows EWC holds two weekly shows and a monthly pay per view. The first show is on either Mondays or Tuesdays and is called Commercially Crazy. This premier show has action from all the EWC superstars and can have up to 7 matches on, and sometimes will have dark matches. Commercially Crazy is presented by Dougie Wright and Perry Haggar. The second EWC show is called Anguish and is a 'taped' show that is on wednesdays. It is a magazine style show that has highlights of Commercially Crazy, interviews, chat and a preview of the upcoming show. Anguish is presented by Ted Mathews and Dougie Wright. Results The results are wrote using play by play. They are posted on the web site and look very stylish. To see an example of a EWC show go to:   http://www.angelfire.com/ks/fantasywrestling/5102.htm Roleplaying We use a password protected mesage board that enables HTML. In the English Wrestling Championship matches are determined by roleplays, making them very important in the career of your wrestler, making the difference between jobbing and becoming a champion. Roleplays are graded on the quality not quantity. But you would usually find there is more quality in a 300-word roleplay than a 5-line roleplay. I judge roleplays on the following: ·        Creativity - you can only have a certain amount of in ring interviews before it gets a little boring. Try and find new locations make it different every time. ·        Effort- how much effort has been put in with the writing of the roleplay, this can be adding colours to the roleplay or the length. ·        Originality - has this roleplay been done before? Have I already seen it on Raw or Nitro, I hope not?  Don't copy catch phrases or gimmicks. ·        Interesting - is the roleplay boring, saying the same thing over and over and over. Or does it have that special ingredient that makes you want to carry on reading and look forward to the next roleplay. ·        Grammar/Spelling - as you can tell most of the time in the shows that I write, I sometimes make mistakes, mainly because I have to write so much in such a short time, making it hard to keep re-reading the card. But with roleplays you should always proof read it. Silly mistakes can make you look like an 11 year old not a seasoned professional Titles Here in EWC we have the following titles English Heavyweight Championship - This is the big one. Only the best will fight for this and only the great will win it. The gold belt is one of the highes accolades in the buisness. English Trilogy Championship - History has show us that winners of this title will go on to bigger and better things with the main gold. English Tag Team Championships - Unlike most efeds we take tag team seriously and love them to bits. Any two wrestlers can wear them weather or not they are a tag team. Web Site The web site is updated most days with news. It also houses a roster that has Poser images of all wrestlers that will be made when a wrestler joins. For each pay per view a Flash movie is made as a 'Pay Per View Promo'. It can be found at   http://englishwrestilng.cjb.net   Alliance   EWC is a member of WFWA. This is a world wide alliance that has its own World Champions, these champions then defend their titles over the regions, EWC is the English region. This is very similar to the real life NWA. The WFWA also has monthly pay per views in which its 8 regions come together.   http://www.geocities.com/wfwa_wrestling Closing Note Well you've read all of this, now check out the web site and sign up. We promise that you will have fun and compete with some very good roleplayers. Please feel free to email me any questions. I put a great deal of work into EWC and its always great to see some new faces on the roster.   Thanks C.
