My first fed: Red Dice Wrestling!

8 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yo, yo, yo. Some of you people reading this right now know, me, and some of you don't. I'm not a legend in E-Wrestling, but I've had my shots. I've gotten around in leagues, won titles, been jobbed, the works. And I have _NEVER_ started a E-Fed...until now. I am offering you the oppertunity to join: RED DICE WRESTLING© This fed (if all goes well) will be better then the self proclaimed "shit feds" that are out there. In a way, I am sorry for sending you this because I know you must be sick of them. But *JOIN TODAY* and you will not regret it. I'm pretty damn sure I know what the "90's" wrestling fan likes...CARDS, ANGLES, CARDS, PUSHES, and CARDS. Hell, if I'm wrong sue me. Red Dice Wrestling© will be based on RPs. Meaning it is *NOT* going to be simulated. I know there are a lot of feds who play favorites, and this will not be one of them! Remember, this fed will only be as good as you let it be. ADDITIONAL INFO: Cards, Rules, Etc. CARDS »»» Let's face it, cards always get pushed back one way or the other. So, RDW will start out with 1(one) card a week(every 7<seven>days). If things go smoothly then there could be more cards and House Shows(mini cards with a summary of matches). If you have a match on a card it is *MANDITORY* to RP. If you do not Role Play, then you will be *JOBBED* for that match. Simple? Oui, I think so. RULES »»» Well, there is the TOS on AOL that can fuck up some people. I just don't care, but, try to keep the language at a limit. **SPELING: Speeling must B all correcct. If u feel you mis-spilled a word, LOK IT UP!!(I really hope you got the sarcasim in that. Spelling don't mean shit in the fed.) ETC »»» To start out, there will only be a singles and tag devision. If all goes well, both devisions will stay. If lack of teams or support, that one will go. Small possibilty that a Web Page might be created. Hum...I'm sure that's all. If you have any questions about anything feel free to E-mail me. Just E-mail [detail withheld]. I'm not used to begging for anything, so I just ask you as a friend: Please join. APPLICATION~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ E-Mail Address: Name: Wrestlers Name: Gimmick(discription is good): Alignment(face, heel, etc): Style(be creative): Finisher: Describe Finisher: Manager/Valet/Bodyguard/etc: Past History(titles, allies, enimies): Intro Music: Fav. Moves(put down the moves your wrestler will do): Looks: Ring Attire(what wrestler wears): Other Info(any stuff I might have forgot): ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ TAG First off, fill out 2 single apps then this: Tag Team Name: Tag Team Finisher: Tag Team Gimmick: Other stuff(anything they'll do different than in singles?) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Well that's all. I really hope you'll join. Thank you and g'nite. - Johnny Lee (Fed Head) <Fed Pres's, please FORWORD to your members> Red Dice Wrestling© from the man who brought you: "the raVen" Eric Davenport The Ruiner American Nightmare "Really Rad" Reggie Rock
