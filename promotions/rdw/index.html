Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Red Dice Wrestling opened on 8 August 1997 with its founder's own admission: “I have _NEVER_ started a E-Fed…until now.” It ran on roleplay alone, no simulator, one card a week, and a plain rule for anyone scheduled to wrestle: roleplay or be jobbed.

Its first surviving card, “Livin Live!!,” aired 19 August 1997 with President Johnny Lee interviewing a returning veteran, Remy Wincott, into the broadcast booth as his new commentary partner. The Ruiner won a debuting match over Crazy Adam, Submarine beat Vulture, and ReX Watson escaped a guitar shot from Heavy Metal Joe to beat “Suicidal” Brian Young with a Rex Plex. Mid-broadcast, “Kold Blood” Kevin Kage arrived unannounced, berated Lee on the air, and dropped him with the Kage Kutter before walking out; the surviving capture of the card breaks off mid-main-event, Eric Cooper against Arsenal, with no result recorded.

Its only other surviving card, “Monday Mat Attack,” numbered part three — earlier parts do not survive — ran 25 August 1997. ReX Watson and Heavy Metal Joe wrestled to a ruled draw after Wincott and “Too Sweet” Stevie Esquire jumped the rail and brawled the finish into chaos, and the main event had Submarine beat Eric Cooper with the Sonar Splash at 20:36.

Its last two surviving posts, both signed by its founder, ask first for tag teams (20 August) and then for other writers, admitting “I am the only writer” (24 August), the day before that final card. No document survives past 25 August 1997, seventeen days after it opened.
