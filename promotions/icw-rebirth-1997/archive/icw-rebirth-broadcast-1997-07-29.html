ICW: The Rebirth — Broadcast

ICW: The Rebirth

29 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ICW. You thought we were done? Finished? Dead? Think again. ------------------------------------------------------------ (scenes from the old ICW are shown, with lots of clips coming from Heat. European United becoming the first tag team champions, The Falcon and Eagle winning the 12-man Survivor Series style match, the cage falling off in the anything goes cage match between Steve Winters and Wolf, Prodigys and Thud's falls count anywhere match, Krypt and Big Red brawling outside the ring, and clips from Spazzz winning the World Title in the 4-way match, ending with him raising the belt high above his head. We then are shown a layout similar to many news brodcasts. There are three men behind the desk. The one in the middle is dressed much like any other brodcaster would be. Your basic suit and tie kind of deal. The man to his left (our right) is a little toned down, with a dress shirt, and the other guy is decked out in a WILD outfit, consisting of colors that would appear to glow in the dark! The man in the middle is first to speak.) Man: That was the past. WELCOME TO THE REBIRTH OF... I... C.... W!!!! This is the ICW President, [name withheld], along with *points to the man in dress shirt* Shane Dewar, and *reluctantly motions towards the wildly dressed man* Buddy Wilson! Today on the specia-- Buddy: Shut up, okay? It's my turn to speak, alright? What we're gunna do is this. We're gunna announce all of the wrestlers who will be drawn for the tournament. Then we're gunna draw the seedings, then we're gunna announce the schedule of things to come. Got it? Good. Shane: Okay, without further ado, let's announce the 16 men that will be battling for the prestigious ULTRA Title! In case you are just joining us here in the ICW, here is how the belt situation will work. The Ultra title is equivilant to other feds "World" title. The winner of the tourney will get the Ultra title. Simple as that. The Elite Title could be compared to other peoples' "Intercontinental" belts. How this will work is the 3 semi-finalists in the Tourney who do not win the Ultra title will battle in a triangle match for the title. And the final belt, the Super title, which may be considered as a "TV" Title, will be decided in the upcoming weeks or months in a very special, unique battle royal. Buddy: What happened to this "without further ado" BS?? Let's go. Here are the men who will be in the tournament. We've got Knightrous, The Falcon, The Crow, Sharpshooter, Brad Abbot, Bloody Boy Mike, The Descendent, Thunderbolt, Fire Mexica, Stunner Mike Titan, Hawk, Eagle, Opera Man, Thunder Gums, Frank "The Flipper" Flannigan, and Dave "Rulebreaker" Hortz. Mike: Wow! The level of competition here in the ICW is amazing! I wonder who will end up winning! Personally, I think Fire Mexica's got a shot! He's quick, and he's got a lot of experience! Buddy: That little weasel? No way! Guys like Thunder Gums, Opera Man, and Dave Hortz will tear him apart! Shane: Well personally, I like the well rounded attack of Knightous.. Heck, it's ANYONES game here! Enough with speculation, let's get the names drawn! (With that, a couple of ICW Production Crew come out with a bin filled with, we would assume, 16 names. Mike turns spins it around a bit, and opens the lid. He reaches in and pulls two names out.) Mike: The first match annou-- Buddy: Cut the crap, alright? Mike: ... Thunderbolt will face... The Descendent! (Shane reaches in the bin and pulls two more names) Shane: The winner of THAT match will face the winner of THIS match... The Crow against... Thunder Gums! Buddy: Wow. My turn. (Buddy grabs 2 more names from the bin.) Buddy: Fire Mexica against "The Flipster" Frank Flannigan. Oooooh.. I'll be sure to watch that one! Mike: You WILL be watching that one.. You'll be doing commentary with us two on it, remember?! Buddy: Oh great... (Mike draws another pairing.) Mike: "Stunner" Mike Titan will be facing... Eagle! That should be a good one, eh Shane? (Buddy shakes his head in disgust and draws two more names.) Shane: Yes it should with their similar fighting sty-- Buddy: Brad Abbot up against... Opera Man! I know Opera Man is big and all, but come on.. OPERA MAN?! Puh-lease! Mike&Shane: Buddy! We are trying to analyze the match-ups here!! Buddy: Why not do that when we're ready to call the matches? Mike&Shane: ... Buddy: Thought so. (Shane draws the next seeding in the tournament.) Shane: The Falcon's opponent will be... Sharpshooter! Mike: There are only 4 people remaining! Knightrous, Bloody Boy Mike, Hawk, and Dave Hortz! After this draw, we will know the placings!! (Mike reaches into the bin one last time.) Mike: The final two matches are.... Knightrous versus Hawk, and Bloody Boy Mike against "Rulebreaker"!! The tournament brackets should soon be on the homepage! Now.. The schedule of things... First of all, I am announcing a NEW rasslin' show in the ICW, it will take place Tuesdays, and will be entitled ICW Invasion! Now.. I'm sure these wrestlers want to trash their opponents, devise strategies, and whatnot.. So here is what I have planned!! Tomorrow, there will be the FIRST ICW Magazine, which will talk about a LOT of things. Thursday we will hear from some wrestlers on ICW Blast-Off! Saturday will be an off day, as I want to give these wrestlers time to let us know what the have to say, and to plan their attacks.. Monday will be Guess Who? and then.. on the first ever ICW Invasion, we will have the first matches!! Thunderbolt against The Descendent... The Crow will take on Thunder Gums.. Fire Mexica will square off against "The Flipster" and Eagle will battle Stunner Mike Titan! The rest of the schedule will be posted in the magazine tomorrow!! ..(NOTE FROM THE "REAL" MIKE).. (Basically what that means is I'm givin' you guys a week to get yer roleplays going, and your strategies in. You all know the line-up for the first card which will take place in a week plus a day. I feel that is sufficient time to get a roleplay or two in, and a strategy. Remember, don't write the match, just give me the basics of what your wrestler is going to do, and why you feel it will work. The full schedule will be announced tomorrow in the magazine..) Buddy: Shut up already! For all you wrestlers out there watching, to get your match-ups, we have just gotten word that.. The VP will be none other than.... VP ROB... Now just because he is in another federation, do not assume the two are linked, okay? He's just in it for the money. Anyhow, SEND ALL YOUR COMMENTS ON OTHER WRESTLERS.. Also known as flashes, or roleplays.. TO [detail withheld] alright? Capo is busy running the fed, the last thing he needs is you guys sending him your stuff, right Mike? Mike: Well.. To be blunt, yeah. It's not like I don't ENJOY listening to them.. *shrug* I dunno.. Well, remember to send your.. umm.. flashes did you say? Buddy: Yeah. That's what the so-called "smarts" call them.. *shrug* Mike: Okay.. Send your.. flashes to [detail withheld] , and everything else to.. Umm.. Me. ( [detail withheld] ) For Buddy Wilson, and Shane Dewar.. This is [name withheld], your ICW President saying SO LONG! and we'll see you in a week for Invasion! Ciao! ------------------------------------------------------------ ---------- Peace... Fusion (of Da Fundamentlz) "Bitches be like flies.. They be attracted to the best shit..." - Ras Kass "How come when black men hit the field, They were throwin' bottles, Now they throwin' million dollar deals.." - Natural Resource "I'm not sayin' I'm number one, um.. sorry I lied, I'm number one, two, three, four, and five." - KRS-ONE "There's a thousand MCs lined up against the wall.. TIMBERRRRR!! They're all gunna fall..." - King Just [detail withheld] ----------
