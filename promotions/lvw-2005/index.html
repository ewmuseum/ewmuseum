A Las Vegas-themed forum fed whose two operators crowned a World Heavyweight Champion in a 60-minute Ironman match at their debut supercard, returned from a multi-year hiatus to crown a new champion in a 30-man battle royal, then tried and failed to relaunch a third time. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Las Vegas Wrestling opened with a recruitment call posted 15 November 2005, promising a Las Vegas-themed promotion built around new invented characters and run by two founding operators who had worked together in an earlier league. Its Grand Opening card followed on 6 December 2005, broadcasting live from the House of Blues at Mandalay Bay behind broadcasters Jeff Mayes and Sir Simon Smith.

Its own first two championships were both crowned on the same night, at “Supershow On The Strip I” (announced 21 February 2006, completed 11 April 2006): the LVW World Heavyweight Championship, decided in a four-way, sixty-minute Ironman match that went to sudden-death overtime and was won by “Cowboy” James Donovan, and the Las Vegas Championship, a secondary title settled by a seven-man Final Table Match in which the winner had to put an opponent through a poker table.

By its own account, the promotion changed hands more than once in the years that followed: a founding owner's drug addiction saw it sold on to operators who couldn't pay the roster or book casino venues, before Henry Goldale bought it back into shows and casinos — at the cost of pushing his own son, Gary Goldale, to nine largely unearned reigns as champion. LVW returned to the House of Blues in March 2009 after that hiatus, and crowned a new champion, J1D, in an April 2009 thirty-man battle royal after Gary Goldale's own title was vacated following a drunk-driving arrest.

A further attempt to relaunch the promotion was announced in May 2013, again from the same two founding operators; no card or roster document from that attempt survives, and the promotion's own last dated activity in the surviving record is a bio-card thread posted 19 May 2013, the same week as that announcement.
