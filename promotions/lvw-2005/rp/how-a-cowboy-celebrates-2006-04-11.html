How a Cowboy Celebrates

11 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TH — 11 April 2006 Fade into the entrance to the VIP lounge of Mandalay Bay. The Gentleman of Leisure is standing by with a microphone and a slightly inebriated, newly crowned LVW World Champion known as the Cowboy, Jimmy Donovan, cowboy hat on head, bottle of champagne in one hand, what we can only assume is a hooker in the other arm, and LVW Championship slung over his shoulder. Women all around too. And some guys, but mostly women.
GOL: Alright, I am leisurely standing by with the new Las Vegas Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion, James Donovan. Jimmy, how do you feel after becoming the first ever World Champ?
JD: Yeeeee-haw! I feel finer than a fox in a henhouse, or at least a rich teenager in a whorehouse! I'm darn-tootin' on cloud nine! Whoooo!
GOL: I can see that. So what about Fabiola and El-Magick-O? JD: That can wait 'til I'm done celebratin'! 'Sides, why should I worry about some yeller bellied coward when I gots this pats the title to celebrate wit'?
GOL: So what are you going to do to celebrate? JD: I'm gonna do what erryone does when they'sa win somethin' good... I'M GETTIN' DRUNK OFF MY ASS... YEEEEEEEE-HAAAA!
GOL: Sounds like a plan! I'm in! GOL drops the microphone and follows Jimmy. JD: Hey everybody, we're all gettin' laid! The crowd cheers wildly as they head into to VIP lounge to celebrate. Fade to black.
