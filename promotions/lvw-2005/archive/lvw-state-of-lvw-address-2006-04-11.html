"State of LVW Address"

11 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Koby — 04-11-06, 06:10 AM Alright, let's get down to what's what.

Again, sorry for the massive delay in SotS I... basically, the structure Mike and I have/had been running was that he wrote the majority of the matches, while I wrote 1 match, rarely 2, and put together all the little bits of the show, did all the Jamarian intros and crashes to break, and edited/formatted the whole thing from top to bottom before slamming it onto the board.

What happened here is pretty simple... I got a job.

And with it the 30-40 hours a week of time that I spent floating around doing random ****, and being The Gentleman of Leisure, got completely taken up by the more important things in life, like getting paid on a consistent basis, and taking care of a bunch of other matters I've been holding off that needed taking care of.

In the process, the card became the proverbial gorilla on the back... it was hard getting around to it with any energy at all, and eventually in the end here, I handed it off completely for Mike to post onto the board, pretty much untouched from whatever he wrote/did, except for whatever small bits I did do before I got employed (like the Main Event intros), and whatever was sent in. Some differences may be noticed from prior cards, some might not be.

Anyway, that brings us to now going into the future.

Along with me getting work, and getting back into being at the poker tables on a bit more consistent basis, Mike's been pretty much getting his full 30-40 hours a week as well since he finally got a schedule to his liking, so we've decided to change things up a bit as to how things get done regarding the cards and stuff.

We decided we needed more RPer participation in the league besides the 1 or 2 RPs, and then having you guys wait for the card. Also, we needed to avoid personal burnout (at least I do).

So starting with the next card we'll be going the NFW route of having RPers write the majority of the matches. It's pretty simple, really. It allows you guys to get more involved, and it allows Mike and I to get this league back on and maintain a tighter schedule.

We're doing a twist with the match-writing, though, and that's that we want people that want to write matches to write matches for other characters and not their own. Why? Because it'll allow you to get into the other characters in the league. Also, I like to keep the element of surprise and suspense for wanting to read the card to find out what happened to YOUR guy in a match.

This will only apply to matches... outside-of-the-ring and backstage bits, as always, done by the RPer of the character.

We want to see how this works out for the next few cards, and any feedback you guys might have to it. The idea in my mind is to have everyone get in on all the "TV" cards, making them go quicker, but more importantly, saving the energy between Mike and I to rip out a very worthwhile Supershow that has 100% of both our energies going into it. I can personally tell you there was stuff I wanted to put into this Supershow up on the board now that I never got around to putting into it, which sucks, but we just had to get it up as is.

That's that.

We'll have the lineup up for the next card on Wednesday afternoon/night, give you guys time to read the card.
