"LVW The Story So Far"

10 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Great Eye — 03-10-09, 05:04 PM After the early success of LVW, there were problems, well really, just one problem, and it was a bad one, the owner of LVW had a crippling drug addiction and it ended up having the company get sold to some shiftless ne'er-do-wells who didn't pay the talent, couldn't get shows staged in casinos, it was a dark time.

Then came Henry Goldale. Henry's inability to keep any league alive more then a couple months made him a clear cut favorite to buy LVW, which he did, but then something shocking happened, he started coughing up the 250 dollars a night to the workers. He got the place back in casinos, he turned LVW around a good bit.

Unfortunately, the price LVW paid for all of this was in the form of Gary Goldale, Henry's son, who would horrify and annoy LVW crowds with his horrible inability to wrestle and his unrelenting push. Under Henry's ownership, Gary was a 9 time LVW Champion, with most of his losses for the title being avenged by instant re-matches there-after.

Gary may rule LVW via his father's wallet, he doesn't however rule the roads of Las Vegas, and he was pinched in yet another DUI a week ago, he's expected to do 60 days in lock up.

So the LVW Championship has been vacated, and at LVW All-In the whole LVW Roster will be engaged in combat in a 30 person battle royal for the title. Henry Goldale has even made the bold decision to pay anyone who is in the match 500 dollars, in the hopes of attracting 30 people to show up for it.


INFO

Joining LVW

If you want to give LVW a whirl, you basically need to have a new character, since this place pays like crap, has a max crowd size at around 300-400 for it's normal shows, this is not where Felix Red, Craig Miles, Joe The Plumber and their ilk ply their craft. This is for green indy wrestlers and perhaps even greener guys off the street who think they can do that 'wrasslin' stuff. About the only person on FW.com currently that would be allowed into LVW would be Olvir, because he's from here, and any excuse I'd have to write a "Welcome Back!" chant is a good one.

If you have no idea about what to do for a gimmick or character concept, then send me a message, I got a bunch of LVW gimmicks already worked out in my head and just need people to use them.
