The Great Eye — 14 May 2009 OK it's been over a week and we're about 48 hours to deadline and I've gotten 1 Rp for the whole show. I know there was a situation with one wrestler that's got to be worked out but beyond that, my main event is a no Rp, the elimination series has 1 out of three Rp'ing and my other debut that isn't an issue is a no Rp...
EPW/TEAM/NFW don't have any Rp threads up so I really don't know what has people's attention distracted this time. Last show's lack of Rp (Main event not-withstanding) I kind of got cause it was an active Rp period board wide. This kind of has me frustrated cause there's no real board activity that should be holding anyone back from doing an Rp in LVW.
If people are busy, real life is crushing you, whatever is going on, just touch base with me, let me know the good word. I'm not trying to be bossy here, I'm just trying to figure out what to do.
RStrawsma — 15 May 2009 My update: A1E had RP threads up last week (I barely made the deadline there), and I've been trying to devote other time to writing a few matches for the EPW card. Add to that the real life events that have been popping up, finding the time to sit down and go into Olvir's world has been tricky.
I apologize for the poor showing, and I'll try to get something up tonight before deadline.
