The Great Eye — 16 April 2009 (FADEIN: JEFF MAYES in a grey suit jacket and pants with white shirt and white/blue tie standing in front of an LVW banner.)
MAYES: Fans LVW's just crowned it's new LVW Champion, but the action doesn't stop as we head to LVW Showdown on The Strip! The next three weeks will see events unfold that will shape that HUGE EVENT...And this week's already shaping up to be a barn burner.
(Mayes turns towards camera two) MAYES: Already we've had word from LVW President The Floor that the new LVW Champion J1D will be in action in a non-title match with the leader of The Empire...The Captain David Richter...
Also we've learned that Born Again will be facing The Spaniard in a match that's supposed to be contested under "The Lord's Rules"...Whatever that means...
But the *big* news is that apparently Born Again has secured the services of Ben Lerner, and that Mr. Lerner has a *huge* statement to make kicking off this week's action, what will he say? You'll have to tune in!
I'll give everyone not on the card already until Sunday 11:59 PM PDT to lemme know if they are in for the card or not, then I'll post up the full card and Rp threads.
