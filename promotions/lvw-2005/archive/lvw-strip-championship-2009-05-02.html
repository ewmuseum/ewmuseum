LVW Strip Championship

2 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Great Eye — 2 May 2009 (FADEIN: Jeff Mayes standing in front of a LVW Banner. He's in a tan suit jacket and pants, light blue shirt, tan tie. In front of him is a small table with a large white gold bracelet reading "Strip Championship" across the plate.)
MAYES: Well fans the battle did continue last week, and this week LVW is bringing the excitement with a NEW TITLE that may throw a big shock into the system here at LVW. The Floor has decreed we will have a new title and a new champion crowned at LVW Snake Eyes. The championship is The Strip Championship, this beautiful bracelet is both a title, and a ticket to the top.
The Floor has ruled that this title isn't just a nice title, but it's also a ticket to the top of LVW. Anyone who successfully defends the Strip title 3 times...Gets a shot at the LVW Champion on the next show if they want it...But if that person decides to press their luck and they get 5 title defenses...Then they'll get not only a shot at the LVW Champion, but they can name the stipulation for the match as well.
Of course if you have 3 wins and press your luck and lose, then you're out of luck, you get nothing. So think carefully before you make any decisions.
