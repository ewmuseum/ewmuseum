Alright everybody, we are coming back, and are here to stay!

Here are the facts of how the show is going to go.

- This is gonna be lead by yours truly (Koby) and [name withheld] (The Great Eye).

- This place, as in the past, is gonna be about sheer goofiness, gimmicks, and FUN. We will invite back LVW Legends from the past to join. Moreso, we are looking for fresh faces, fresh ideas, fresh gimmicks, fresh fun! FRESH!

If you want to join, try something new, and can’t think of anything, don’t worry! We have a goody bag chockful of new gimmicks we’d love to unleash, all it takes is someone to take the character and make it their own! We have stored up about 10 gimmicks/characters we’d love to come to life, so if you want us to play Vince McMahon and come up with a character that you’ll love and enjoy, step up the the plate and we’ll get you going!

So buy your ticket and grab a seat for LVW!

If you have a character of your own, shoot us the bio with the pertinents - Name, Height/Weight/Looks, Hometown, Entrance Music, FIVE MOVES OF DOOM, Finisher.

If you want in and want to reach into the character goody bag, shoot me (Koby) or [name withheld] (The Great Eye) a PM and we’ll go over the options!
