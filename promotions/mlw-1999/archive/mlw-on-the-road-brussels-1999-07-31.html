MLW: On The Road (Brussels)

31 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's the match, folks. -[name withheld] "I ate Lisa's "Cookies." Handler of: "Rodeo" Nevada Harris [SCW, MLW, ACW] MLW True North Champion Uncle Istvan [MCW] [Fade in] [Camera opens up in a locker room. It is looking on for a minute when suddenly, The door is kicked open and a bullrope is thrown on the table. Nevada Harris, fatigued and covered in sweat, walks in. Nevada takes off his hat and places it on the table.] NH: "Mah gawd . . . Ah told that basterd an' he didn't listen. Well, Ah guess Ah am provin' more than Ah said. It's not jus' Canadians who can't wrestle worth uh shit, it's Russians too." [Nevada smiles. He grabs a bottle of water, opens it and takes a sip.] NH: "Nah that Ah beat that big ol' Russian Bear, Ol' Nevada has his shot at the True North Tahtle. Robbie, you an' Ah are no stranguhs to each otha. We fought twice. My win was uh legit pinfall. Yer win was an ova-the- top-rope fluke. This tahme, Ah'ma get the one-up an' there ain't no stoppin' me." [Nevada takes another sip of water, pulls out a pack of cigarettes and starts to pack them.] NH: "See, hombre, they tuh neva gamble with a man whose name is uh state. Come this Friday, Ah'ma show you why that saying is true. And that, ol' buddy, is a gar-rone-tee . . ." [Nevada opens the pack of cigaretts and pops one between his lips. He lights it, takes a puff, and continues.] NH: "Ah'm runnin' this rodeo, Robbie. You is jus' anotha cowboy who is tryin' tuh make a name. Well, Robbie, the bull is too damn strong fer ya an' ya can't even go eight seconds let alone four. So, Robbie . . . jus' remember, you is just anotha cowboy. Cowboys can be replaced . . . lives can't. An' Robbie, that's a gawddam fact." [He puffs on the cigarette again as he grabs his hat and places it on his head.] NH: "See ya Friday, ol' buddy." [Fade] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [The thunder rolls and the lightening strikes through the heavens as the scene opens with a the camera spiraling down a surreal highway road surrounded by abstract landscape as Black Sabbath's "Hard Road" begins to rock surrealistic country side. In the clouds, various scenes begin to super-impose themselves against the blue-to-graying sky.] #Old men crying, young men dying# #World still turns as Father Time looks on# #Children playing, dreamers praying;# #Laughter turns to tears; love has gone# #Has it gone?# [Murquot M-Bombs "Sadistic" Shane Alexander through the roof of a newstand under the bright lights of Time Square.] #Oh it's a hard road# #Oh it's a hard road# #Oh it's a hard road# #Carry your own load# [Triple M does a can-can number with the world famous Rockettes inside the ring.] #Poets yearning, lovers learning# #On this path of life we pass along, is it wrong?# #Widows weeping, babies sleeping# #Life becomes the singer and the song. Sing along.# ["Superstar" Devon Starr breaks a golden sack over the head of Ivan Tamarak, raining a few thousand dollars in silver casino chips down upon the canvas in the MGM Grand Arena.] #Oh it's a hard road# #Oh it's a hard road# #Oh it's a hard road# #Carry your own load# [Several NYPD Helicopters chase Helter "Skelter" Skinner in a hijacked MLW Camera Blimp over New York City's skyline.] #Why make the hard road?# #Why can't we be friends?# #No need to hurry# #We'll meet in the end# [The observation deck of the World Trade Center explodes, leaving Zombie Rogers and "Tyrannius" Rex Robaire dangling an inch from their demise 107 stories above the ground below.] #Why make the hard road?# #Why can't we be friends?# #No need to worry# #Let's sing it again# [The Glitter Boy stands at the top of the entrance ramp in the World Famous Hollywood Bowl holding up his newly won Hollywood Tribute Cup as fireworks explode around him.] #Brother's sharing, mother's caring# #Night time falling victim to the dawn, shadow's mourn# #Days are crawling, time is calling;# #To the Earth another life is born, love line drawn# [Zombie Rogers unveils his deadite-emblazoned MLW Heavyweight Championship belt, holding it up high before a frenzied Sin City crowd in the heart of Las Vegas, Nevada.] #Oh it's a hard road# #Oh it's a hard road# #Oh it's a hard road# #Carry your own load# [The shot is suddenly propelled into the darkness of a tunnel at the end of the highway road and narrowly avoids several collisions with an oncoming Mack truck, a dirtbike racer, and low-flying helicopter before coming head-to-head with a giant road sign reading boldly, "MLW: ON THE ROAD" on its face, obstructing the end of the tunnel. The camera pausing for only a moment, before racing towards the sign at lightspeed, shattering it into a million crystal shards, and filling the screen with a blinding white light for a split-second before the scene becomes an elegant booth in a small Brussels cafe. The Doors "Love Her Madly" fills the air as Tonya Wallace, wearing one of several new MLW T-shirts, sits before a platter of Brussels world famous mussel dishes.] TW: 'ello all yew MLW Maniacs, an' welcoome to MLW: On th' Road froom Brussels, Belgoom. These weren't th' only muscles in Brussels this week, as th' MLW's top Superstars invaded the The Theatre Royal de la Monnaie for a night o' unbelieveable wrasslin' action. Tonight we'll 'ave th' full report on th' MLW's card at Monnaie, as well as a twenty-man Battle Royale. Bu oop first, th' man we 'eard froom et th' top o' th' show, Nevada 'arris, takin' on Rob Magnum fer th' MLW True North title.... =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Fade into a shot of a jam-packed Theatre Royal de la Monnaie as the sound of the crowd fills the scene.] RA: Alrighty Lar, we're here in Brussels, Belgium for this big event, and coming up next we have two men who have quickly developed a bitter rivalry. LF: I hate brussel sprouts, Andj. RA: Huh? LF: Isn't this the place that supplies the worlds' brussel sprouts? RA: No, I don't think so Larry. LF: Oh.... well, I still hate them. RA: Rob Magnum and Nevada Harris... how do you see this match? LF: Through beer coloured glasses I hope! RA: Oh brother... let's head up to the ring for the introductions! AJ: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the MLW TRUE NORTH TITLE!!!! ["Uneasy Rider" by The Charlie Daniels Band plays over the loud speakers.] AJ: He hails from Montgomery, Alabama. He stands at 6 feet, 6 inches tall and weighs in at 294 pounds. Here is . . . . . . Nevada Harris!!! [Nevada Harris emerges from the entrance way. He walks out to a mixed reaction of cheers and boos. Nevada does not seem to be too happy, but seems to be excited that he is here. Nevada has mid-length, black hair that is slicked back. He has dark brown eyes. He is a white man with a dark mustache. He has a long scar on his left cheek. He is husky, but not fat. He has a thick neck and thick arms. He wears a black cowboy hat, and a brown, suede, cowboy vest that is open, showing his massive chest. He also wears blue-jeans that are regular fit. He also wears white and brown, leather cowboy boots and is carrying his trademark bullrope.] [Nevada strolls down the aisle and slides into the ring. He walks over to Augie Jones and grabs the mic. "Uneasy Rider" fades out.] NH: "No Worries" . . . The first tahme we set foot in the MLDubbya ring was tuh face one anuhtha. Ah took the win from ya and you was all types uh pissed, yeah. Howeva, you was able tuh get yer revenge when ya gone an' outsmarted Ol' Nevada at Maplemania. Nah Ah dun did mah best in orda tuh get that strap that you is wearin' an' Ah ain't stoppin' mah tyrade 'til Ah get it. [The fans boo. Nevada tips his hat a little. He looks at his bullrope, smiles, and continues.] NH: Robbie . . . this li'l ol' bullrope heah is gon' end up wrapped 'round yer big ol' fat neck and Ol' Nevada is gon' go an' drag yer dumb ass all ova this ring. An' in case there is some sumbitch out there who dun agree with Ol' Nevada, Ah'ma go an' make this a gar-rone-tee. An' when Ol' Nevada makes uh gar-rone-tee, he damn sure lives up tuh it. So, Roberto, get yer ass out heah so Ah can lynch ya an' take that gawd -dam tahtle. [Nevada throws the microphone back at Augie.] ["Halls of Illusions" by the Insane Clown Posse shatters the silence that had filled the arena.] AJ: Coming down the aisle..... [The crowd erupts into a earth-shattering mixed reaction. Some love him, some hate him, and the man himself could care less either way.] ...from Detroit, Michigan. Standing at the GIANT height of 7'1 and weighing in at 368 pounds....the man who went a year unbeaten, the innovator of the "No Worries Express"...the MAN WITH NO WORRIES..........AND THE MLW TRUE NORTH CHAMPION........... ___"NO WORRIES"___!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! _______ROB________!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! _____MAGNUM______!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Through the enterance curtains bursts Rob Magnum. He stops immediately and looks around, raising his arms and soaking in the mixed reaction, a grin on his face the whole time. He nonchalantly strolls down to ringside, walks up the ring stairs, and steps over the top rope. He meanders to the middle of the ring where he again raises his arms high above his head for the world to see and looks around smirking at the crowds reaction to him. He's in his ring attire which is a pair of black leather pants that mesh into a black spandex top reading "No Worries" across it in gold. He removes his black black and gold leather vest and hucks it out into the audience before making a "cut-throat" gesture indicating his wish for the music to stop. He then removes the True North belt from around his waist and hands it to the referee.] RA: This should be a great match, Lar. LF: Rob Magnum is one big sumbitch, Andj! RA: Um, yes he is, although I wouldn't have quite put it that way. The ref holds up the title belt and hands it down to the time keeper at ringside. The official signals for the bell.... <<DINGDINGDING>> RA: ...and this True North title match is underway! NEVADA HARRIS IMMEDIATELY CLOTHESLINES MAGNUM WITH THAT BULLROPE, AND BOTH MEN TUMBLE OVER THE TOP ROPE AND HIT THE FLOOR!!!! LF: That's what I call a fast start, Andj! RA: Nevada grabs Magnum by the hair and pulls him up.... nasty right hands by Nevada Harris! LF: The good ol' boys know how to throw the best punches! RA: He's still got that bullrope on him...... HE SLIPS IT AROUND MAGNUM'S NECK AND STARTS CHOKING THE CHAMP OUT!!!! LF: Damn ref! Leave them alone! RA: The official has stopped his 10 count and is outside of the ring now, trying to stop Nevada Harris from choking Rob Magnum with that bullrope. He lays the 5 count on Harris.... and finally Harris lets go of the rope, but not before threatening the official with a little bodily violence. LF: And so he should! Interfering busybody. RA: As Magnum goes down on one knee, trying to get some air back into his lungs, Harris heads over to the security barrier and begins arguing with some of our fans in the front row. LF: Can you blame him? I mean, these Belgicans have got to be the ugliest fans I've ever seen. RA: That's "Belgians" Andj. LF: What difference does it make... they're ugly. RA: {sigh} Harris turns back to Rob Magnum.... MAGNUM CATCHES HIM WITH A HEADBUTT TO THE MIDSECTION!!! Harris is stunned, and Magnum regains his feet. The champ grabs Nevada Harris by the arm and... <<CRASH!!>> RA: WHIPS HIM INTO THE RING STEPS!!!! LF: There's that size and strength advantage, Andj. RA: Magnum stalks over and picks Harris up from the wreckage of the ring steps and tosses him in the ring. The official was being extremely lenient with his 10 count there... LF: I don't that ref knows how to count to 10. RA: Stop it, Lar. The champ picks Harris up off the mat and whips him into the ropes..... MAGNUM WITH THE BACK BODY DROP ATTEMPT, BUT HARRIS STOPS DEAD IN HIS TRACKS, BUTTERFLIES THE ARMS OF THE CHAMP AND..... RODEO DDT BY NEVADA HARRIS!!!!! LF: That was sweet, Andj! RA: Nice move by the challenger! Harris picks Magnum up.... scoop and a slam!!! Harris covers! 1....... 2....... RA: Kickout by the champ! LF: Slow count by the official if you ask me. RA: And I didn't. Harris picks up the champ again and.... SMALL PACKAGE BY MAGNUM!!!! 1....... 2....... RA: AND HARRIS KICKS OUT!!!! Another close call... now it's the champion picking Harris up off the mat..... a whip into the ropes..... POWERSLAM BY MAGNUM!!!! ANOTHER COVER!!!! 1....... 2....... RA: THREE!!!! LF: NO!!!! JUST TWO! JUST TWO! RA: Yes, the official is signalling "two" although that looked a lot like a three count to me. The champ picks the challenger up, whips him across the ring into the ropes once more.... SUNSET FLIP BY HARRIS AND A COVER!!!!! 1....... 2...... RA: AND AGAIN THE CHAMP KICKS OUT IN THE NICK OF TIME!!! This one's been extremely close, Larry! LF: What's with this Belgican beer, Andj? It tastes funny! RA: Stick to the match, Larry. Harris stands up and picks Magnum up by the hair..... and throws him through the ropes and out to the floor. LF: Maybe the damn ref will leave them alone this time. RA: Harris heads over to the timekeeper's table... and pushes our timekeeper Smedley Bumchuckle out of his chair! LF: Smedley was mouthing off to him, serves him right. RA: Harris grabs the chair and folds it up, heading back towards the champion Rob Magnum.... <<SMACK!!>> RA: He blindsided Magnum with that chair to the side of the head! LF: Magnum's history! RA: Harris drops the chair and pushes the champ up onto the ring apron. The ref is chastising Harris about that chair shot, but apparently this match will continue. LF: The first thing the ref has done right! Let them fight it out! RA: Harris climbs into the ring and picks Magnum up on the apron, pulling him through the ropes and into the ring. Harris signals to the crowd and whips Magnum into the ropes while running into the opposite himself for momentum..... VICIOUS LARIAT TURNS MAGNUM INSIDE OUT!!!! LF: We saw him use that last week!! What a move! RA: It certainly is a devestating manouevre!!!! Harris covers! 1...... 2....... LF: NEW CHAMP!!! 3!!!!!!!!!!!! <<DINGDINGDING>> RA: Nevada Harris has just won the True North Championship! LF: And who better to represent the people of Canada, Andj! RA: He doesn't even like Canadians! LF: Quiet Andj... look! [Nevada snatches the True North Title away from the referee. Nevada then snatches a microphone. As he places the title belt over his left shoulder, Nevada takes a deep breath. He begins to speak.] NH: Ah told y'all jus' what was gon' happen heah tuhnight. Ah told y'all an' y'all didn't wanna believe me. Well believe it nah, folks. Ol' Nevada has beaten Robbie again. Ah am the champeen an' Ah am callin' the shots. Nah lahk all Canadians, an' we all know that Robbie is one in min' an' ability, Rob Magnum has fallen victim tuh "The Bullrope." It's uh friend to the end an' nobody can avoid it. Nah Ah am the champeen an' there ain't no stupid sumbitch who is gon' go an' take mah gawddam tahtle. An' jus' tuh show y'all that Ah ain't jokin', Ah am sendin' out an open challenge. That's right. Tuh anyone in the MLDubbya, Ah am talkin' tuh ya an' y'all betta listen up an' y'all betta listen good. Ah challenge anyone an' everyone an' will even go through uh gauntlet if need be. An' sure enough, Ah'ma run that gauntlet an' come out of it with the True North Tahtle. That's a gar-rone-tee. [Nevada throws down the microphone and exits the ring to a round of boos from the crowd.] RA: Well, like him or hate him, "Rodeo" Nevada Harris is our new True North Champion. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Cut back to Tonya at the cafe.] TW: Wha' a way ta kick-off our show, a NEW champion! We'll be right back with ma darlin' "Scoop" William's On the Road Report. Stay with us! [Fade to commercial.]
