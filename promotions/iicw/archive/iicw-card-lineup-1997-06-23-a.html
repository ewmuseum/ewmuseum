IICW: Tonight's Card Line-Up

23 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene cuts to a IICW logo with the following text:] Voice: Monday Night, June 23rd, The IICW brings you it's first edition of Monday Night Showdown.. coming LIVE from the Cow Palace in San Jose, California.... One is known for his past accomplishments, the other is trying to show the veteran that a new generation of stars are taking over... - "Nature Boy" Todd Newkirk vs. Cherokee Chief One is a movie star, focused on becoming a star in the IICW.. The other is an ex-boxer.. A few shots to the head to many.. One will stand, the other shall fall... - "The Hollywood Heartthrob" Dave Diamond vs. "Dr. Doom" Jerome Krager A challenge has been set by a rookie from Japan, will he be electrocuted for sticking his nose in his opponents face? - "The Shocker" Brian Anthony vs. Chris DuBois A team formed across several time zones unites to go for IICW gold, while the other team wants to be world's apart and be on top of the tag team division... - World Warriors vs. Fantastic Ones One calls himself Desirable, he's had a Dynamite past and wants to blast his way threw IICW competitors.. the other was missing for several months, no one could find him.. Alas he has returned to the IICW for vengeance.. They will both explode into combat this monday... - "Desirable" Danny Dynamite vs. Richard Richardson Both hate each other with all their might. Both have talked the talk. Both have walked the walk. Now it's fight the fight, with no rules, and refs. One will win the match, both might never walk of talk again.. - Xavier Lee Jackson vs. The Perfect Pugilist in a parking lot match! All this and much more tonight on Monday Night Showdown!! http://iicw.home.ml.org [name withheld]
