Huge pop from the twenty thousand plus fans crowding the floor seating and filling the mezzanine, lining the entire arena. Over these scenes comes the voice of veteran announcer, Tim Cross]
TC: Welcome back everyone to the Coliseum! The IICW is back from it's hiatus and better than ever! Tonight we'll be crowing our Heavyweight and
Intercontinental champions, all in one night in a battle royal! JL: The IICW isn't waisting any time in getting down to business! IICW owner [name withheld] isn't taking things likely.
TC: That's for sure. I'm Tim Cross and next to me as usual is Jerry "The Feen" Leelen. It's good to be back! JL: This place stinks worst than before. TC: We got nine IICW superstars in this battle royal, the top talent in the world of wrestling today. The last man in the ring will become the World IICW
Heavyweight champion, while the runner up becomes the Intercontinental champ. JL: Cross, my money is on The Bad Boy. Bagwell has been around forever and he knows how to work a battle royal to perfection.
TC: He's got a great chance, as do "Rudedog" Alex Reed, Le Rebel, "Super" Scott
Sexton, Shawn Masters.
JL: I still say the Bad Boy has the best chance, but don't count out "Idolizer" Troy Pierce. With Demona by his side, he's alost a sure in for the title. TC: We won't delay this battle of the ages any longer, let's go to David Pelter for the introductions. [Cut to the ring where Pelter is standing in the middle.] DP: Welcome back everyone!! [Huge crowd pop!] DP: This match is a battle royal for BOTH the IICW Intercontinental title AND the IICW Heavyweight belt!!
[Another pop from the crowd] DP: Introdusing first.. From Boston... Here is "R U D E D O G" A L E X R E E D!! ["Thunder by AC/DC begins to play as Alex Reed walks down the aisle.] DP: Next.. Hailing from St-Paul, Minnesota... Accompagnied to the ring by the Super Brain.. Here is MR. FLAWLESS!!!
[As "Theme to Chariots of Fire" begin to play the hometown fans give Mr. Flawless a nice ovation, although he doesn't seem to care very much for it. Mr. Flawless has a medium Build, wears a black and red wrestling singlet. He also wears black wrestling boots with a "Mr.F" on the sides.
He has long curly/wavy dirty blonde hair. He's carrying a white towel with him to the ring, and as he enters, he flicks out his gum and throws his towel behind his back to the Super Brain. The Brain is wearing black pants and a black jacket with "Super Brain" written on the back in silver and red. He's got short light gray hair.]
DP: And hailing from Toronto, Canada... Here is T H E B A D B O Y!!!! [The fans begin to boo like crazy, except for a few cheers as the "Theme from Bad Boys" by Mark Mancina begins to play over the PA system, the Bad Boy comes out from the locker room with a cocky smile on his face. He'll walk down to the ring with a smirk. Stopping about half way in the aisle, he'll briefly walk slower to bask in the few cheers and loud boos from the crowd. Once in the ring, he'll put his right foot on the bottom rope and while holing on to the top rope with his right hand, he'll raise his left arm to once again bask in the huge crowd heat till his opponent makes his entrance.]
[The Bad Boy has short dirty blonde hair and sports a goatee. He is of a medium build and wears long black wrestling tights with several "badboy"
logo's in white all over the tights. On the buttocks, the word "Bad Boy" is written in white with a bigger version of the logo printed behind it. He's got white wrestling boots and wears a sleeveless black leather vest to the ring which shows his tribal tattoo that he has on his upper left arm. He also wears a pair of silver Oakley ray-bans to the ring.]
JL: There's the man to beat tonight Cross! TC: He looks ready for this... But these are IICW superstars.. It's not going to be easy.. Even for a legend like him.
DP: Next.. Hailing from Hollywood, California.. Accompanied to the ring by his tag team partner Legend.. Here is "S U P E R" S C O T T
S E X T O N!!! [As "Breathe" by Prodigy begins to play, the "anti-Christ" of wrestling, "Super" Scott Sexton, appears in the aisleway. Triple S stops, and looks over the vast arena... The crowd begins to boo louder and louder.
"Super" Scott Sexton is actually smiling, a sadistic smile, as he walks slowly to the ring...
After every few steps, Triple S stops and looks over the audience. Triple S is simply wearing a black Triple S t-shirt with light blue jeans and black
Timberland boots. After a couple minutes, the Most Hated Man in wrestling makes his way into the ring.]
JL: He's normally a tag team wrestler, but tonight he can become the singles champion! TC: The fans don't seem to like him this time around either, but he's got great talent and with Legend there, who knows what can happen.
DP: Next.. Hailing from Toronto, Canada.. At 6' 2" and 243 pounds.. Here is J. T. W I L D!!
[Crowd pops mildly for Wild.] JL: He's the imposter Canadian.. There's only ONE real Canadian in the IICW and that's Le Rebel!
TC: Speaking of which... DP: Hailing from Montreal, Quebec, Canada.. At 6 foot tall and 234 pounds.. Here is L E R E B E L!! [As "Sexy Boy" sung by himself (in french and english) starts to play, the arena goes dark while white and blue lights start flashing everywhere. A blue light shines on the floor with white fleur de lys which leads the path to the ring, Le Rebel walks through the curtain holding his arms high, chewing his gum and adjusting his shades. As he goes up the stairs to the ring, he climbs to the top rope and again raises his hands up high and spits out his gum and flicks it with his right hand. He then jumps into the ring, walks to the center of the ring and does a split style pose to the crowd while blue fireworks go off behind him.]
[Le Rebel is a male Caucasian with long dirty blonde hair, tanned and very cut. Wears long black tights with blue fleur de lys with a white border everywhere along the tights, inside the fleur de lys are black stripes. On the buttocks, there's a big fleur de lys in white with "Le Rebel" written in blue and black. He wears a pair of shades that are in a shape of the fleur de lys tinted in blue and has white boots with "Le Rebel" written in black on the out side sides. He's got blue and white wrist bands that go about half way to his forearms.]
JL: Now that's an entrance! TC: No doubt, but this next one might just out it! DP: Next.. Accompanied to the ring by "Dangerous" Demona.. Hailing from Toronto, Canada.. At 6' feet tall and weighing in at 220 pounds.. He is the ICON.. He is the GOD of wrestling.. He is the
"I D O L I Z E R" T R O Y P I E R C E!!! ["Kryptanite" by 3 Doors Down begins to play and the arena is indulged by a corus of boos from the crowd. Troy Pierce comes out from the back with "Dangerous" Demona following right behind. Troy Pierce has short dirty blonde hair with medium length sideburns and sports a goatee. Pierce is of a medium build and wears regular (slightly tight) light blue jeans and black timberland boots to the ring with a black half shirt (ala
Hogan/Hall) with "ITP" written on it in white and a blue and black bandanna on his head.] ["Dangerous" Demona has long red wavy hair usually in a pony tail. She wears black leotards with a red workout top and black boots. She's pretty well built, defined and tanned, and has "Double D" written on here boots and workout top.]
JL: You can tell there's already a lot of tension in that ring. TC: It's about to explode any second. But there's still two more wrestlers left! DP: Next.. Hailing from Osaka, Japan.. At 6 foot tall and weighing in at 225 pounds.. Here is A K I R A H A S H I M O T O!!!
[Shakuhachi Flute/Koto Drumming music begins to play as Hashimoto walks to the ring with his translator Sinji. Hashimoto is wearing a complete white kung-fu outfit with a black belt. Sinji is caring the Japanese flag, waving it high and proud.]
JL: Crap, I almost forgot about Hashimoto. This guy is the most respected wrestler here, why are the fans booing him?!
TC: The fans seem to agree with you there, they're booing his ass off like mad. DP: And finally.. Hailing from Memphis, Tennessee.. At 6' 2" and weighing in at 240 pounds.. Here is "MASTERMIND" SHAWN MASTERS!!
["This is what I say" begins to blast over the PA system as Shawn Masters comes out to a mixed reaction from the crowd. Masters makes his way to the ring with a determined look on him face.]
[He's got a normal cut body for someone his size. He has short dirty blonde hair with the top being a bit longer than the sides, and he sports a short goatee. He wears long black tights with a red mesh in the inner part of the tights which ends near the knees. He also wears black boots and a black vest to the ring.]
JL: And they're not wasting any time!!! Here we go! Pierce wasting to time at all going after Hashimoto, these have had their differences in the past, and Pierce wants his revenge.

Le Rebel from the choke hold. TC: Now Le Rebel and Sexton are double teaming the Bad Boy in the corner, but in comes Alex Reed and grabs Le Rebel's golden hair and pulls him away from Bagwell.

JL: WOW! Wild just slapped Hashimoto! TC: It must have got Hashimoto angry because he runs into the ropes and takes
Wild down with a huge savate kick! Now Hashimoto is choping away at Wild!
JL: Hashimoto picks Wild up,.. and hip toss's him right back down! Hashimoto again runs into the ropes as Wild is getting up..
TC: Wild with a scoop slam on Hashimoto. J. T. Wild begins to choke Hashimoto but Sexton for the save!
JL: Pierce and Le Rebel are ducking it out, and Pierce hits Le Rebel with a russian leg sweep! He picks up Le Rebel and throws him into the ropes! TC: Pierce tried to hit him with a clothesline but misses! Le Rebel continues to the other side.
JL: Bad Boy hits Flawless with a punch to the face! Bagwell is going for his finisher.. the Bad Plex, but Pierce in there with a kick to the midsection.
TC: Masters and Scott Sexton have yet to fight anyone else but themselves here! Masters throws Sexton into the ropes, but Sexton hits Shawn Masters with a short lariat, but neither will go down! Both begin to brawl along the ropes! What a fist fight!
JL: Pierce and Reed are double teaming J.T. Wild, they nail him with a double suplex and Wild hits hard! They both pick up Wild and both charge with a double short lariat and that sends Wild right into no mans land!
TC: Both Reed and Pierce continue to double team J.T. Wild and they're trying to get him over the top rope.. He's holding on but can't out match the strength of both men and he's gone!
JL: J.T. WILD IS GONE! And just as fast, Pierce elbows Reed in the gut and is trying to get him out as well, but can't seem to get him over. TC: The Bad Boy and Flawless and going at it.. Bagwell is choking Flawless, he's almost blue over there! Flawless with a thumb to the eye which breaks the choke hold.
JL: Flawless now catching his breath in the corner as Alex Reed gets Bad Boy into a head lock. Bad Boy now sends Reed into the ropes and hits him with a sidewalk slam!
TC: Sexton and Masters _still_ fighting, with Masters giving Sexton a huge swinging punch to the head! Snap mare by Masters! Masters going over to Sexton but Hashimoto kicks Masters's knee!
JL: Masters doesn't have the best knee's in the world, and boy did they just give way their! Hashimoto with an elbow drop to the head, and another one!
TC: Sexton is up and grabs Hashimoto by the head and simply pushes him out of the way and now Sexton is hitting several quick leg drops on Masters!
JL: Le Rebel and Pierce are going at it on the other side, Le Rebel whips Pierce into the ropes.. but Bad Boy was in the path and Bagwell executed a belly to belly suplex on Pierce!
TC: Alex Reed just missed an elbow drop on Le Rebel who is on the mat right now. He picks up Le Rebel and executes a fine tiger suplex on Le Rebel who rolls out of the ring.
JL: He didn't go over the top rope, so he's still in it! TC: The referee's on the outside are telling Le Rebel to get back in. Meanwhile Scott Sexton and Shawn Masters and still brawling along side the ropes on the far end!
JL: These two _really_ hate each other! Masters with an ugly groin shot on
Sexton! That just hurts watching it!
TC: Masters trying to get Sexton over.. He does it! SCOTT SEXTON HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JL: Masters turning and walling away.. No wait! Scott Sexton in on the ring apron and grabs Masters's hair! He's pulling Masters back to the ropes.. And he flips Shawn Masters out!!! "MASTERMIND" SHAWN MASTERS IS GONE!!
TC: They maybe eliminated from the battle royal, but neither seem to care at all, since they are brawling on the outside.. Sexton is going for a chair.. AND HE HITS MASTERS ON THE HEAD!! SHAWN MASTERS is busted wide open!
JL: Sexton continues to stomp away at Masters but the referee come in and are forcing Sexton to return to the locker room. Some dudes in suits are helping Masters up as well..
TC: And Masters charges at Sexton with his back turned! Sexton with the momentum just struck the guard rail!! Now he's bleeding as well!
JL: There's action in the ring and outside as well! Both Masters, Legend AND
Sexton _continue_ to brawl as all the referee's are trying to split them apart!
TC: Wait a minute!!!! "Idolizer" Troy Pierce has just been eliminated by Alex
Reed!! JL: Ya but there are no ref's on the outside! There _all_ in the aisle on the opposite end trying to get Sexton and Masters separated, that no one saw
Troy Pierce get thrown out!
TC: And just like that, Troy Pierce is back in the ring! If he manages to win this thing, you bet there are going to be a lot of protests about this!
JL: _IF_ he should win this.. Flawless is trying to get Hashimoto over the top rope here, but can't seem to get the guy out!
TC: Well the referee's are all coming back now as Masters and Sexton are back in the locker room area.
JL: Did you just see that?!?! Hashimoto did some sort of spring board move using the top rope and now _he's_ got Flawless almost over the top rope!
TC: The Bad Boy just hit a back suplex on a dazed Alex Reed, who was arguing with two referee's on the outside about Pierce still being in the ring.
Bagwell with a short lariat and follows that up with a scoop slam. JL: We got Pierce over there battling with Le Rebel.. Le Rebel has Pierce in a full nelson and Troy can't seem to get out of it. Le Rebel finally let's go when Hashimoto comes in.
TC: Hashimoto and Le Rebel are at it.. running bulldog by Le Rebel!! Hashimoto down and The Bad Boy comes up from behind Le Rebel and delivers a nice reverse double under hook suplex!
JL: I didn't know Bagwell even knew those type of moves! Pierce going after
Alex Reed.. trying to get him in an arm bar submission type move, but
Reed is fighting back. He's going to have to eliminate Pierce _again_, this time to make it official.
TC: Doesn't look like Pierce will allow himself to get out again, as he's holding on to the bottom rope with both legs. Reed with a elbow to
Pierce's back.. and another.. but still he won't let go of the ropes! JL: Pierce is trying to look behind him... WHAT A GROIN KICK BY PIERCE!! Alex Reed is in huge pain! That's the second groin kick we saw tonight.. TC: Pierce almost has Reed out... And The Bad Boy helps Pierce out and Reed is out! JL: "RUDEDOG" ALEX REED IS OUT!! TC: I just said that Feen. And look at Reed, he's livid about what just happened! He's going nuts on the outside trying to explain that he got thrown out by someone that's not even supposed to be in the ring!
JL: Tough! Speaking about Pierce, he just got hit by a Hashimoto flying axe handle. Hashimoto going for a tiger driver, but Pierce blocks it nicely!
TC: Hashimoto run into the ropes and is heading towards Pierce.. BACK BODY DROP BY PIERCE!! JL: But Hashimoto somehow managed to land right on the ring apron on the other side.. and Pierce thinks he's eliminated! Turn around!!
TC: Alex D with a SPRING BOARD BODY BLOCK ON PIERCE from the outside! Unbelievable! Hashimoto going for that tiger driver again.. and hits it! JL: Pierce is in loads of trouble with Hashimoto, as is Bagwell with Flawless.. Flawless with a nice jumping elbow thrust on Bad Boy, but the Bad Boy is laughing it off? Flawless now continues to work on Bagwell but to no affect.
Where is Le Rebel? JL: Right there in the far ring post. He's taking a breather while the rest of them fight. All he's got to do is take them all out when they're tired.
TC: That's not a fighting way to win this. JL: Who cares _how_ you win, the important part is to _win_ it! TC: Flawless is taking several punches and shots at Bagwell. JL: That's one way to get him down. TC: WOW! LE REBEL, the opportunist that he is JUST CLIPPED Flawless FROM BEHIND! Flawless is down as Le Rebel works on his hurt knee. Flawless trying to get up.. JL: So would I! He's between Le Rebel and The Bad Boy right now! Flawless using the ropes as leverage to get up.. He kicks Le Rebel in the knee as well, but it didn't do much damage to him.
TC: Le Rebel trying to flip Mr. Flawless over.. The Bad Boy now getting up and is helping Rebel to flip out Flawless!
JL: MR. FLAWLESS HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!! TC: What is Bagwell yelling now? JL: More stuff at Super Brain I think. Only four men remaining. Hashimoto and
