Introduction What is The World Wrestling Council? ^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v WWC is a coalition of tight-knit wrestling federations that compete with and against each other for dominance in this (imaginary) world of wrestling. WWC is a council of wrestling federations, which is comprised of the federation presidents and staff.
These federations compete for championships, ratings, and respect. Upon applying for WWC you will be offered a choice to join one of three federations. Each federation has it's own set of shows, feuds and world champions. However, there is only one WWC World Heavyweight Champion, the grand daddy of them all. This title is defended on a monthly basis, either at one of the federations PPVs or at one of the all-WWC supercards held every two months.
What else can you find in WWC? Unlike other e-wrestling organizations, you're truly becoming apart of an e-wrestling "world". What you do, and how you perform in one federation can affect and influence wrestlers in another federation. You can truly make a name for yourself as well as having a unique e-wrestling experience. But most of all, we hope you have fun. That's what we're all here for, afterall.
