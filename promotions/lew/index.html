A two-night, four-production-house interfed supercard staged in August 1997 at a fictional Ericsson Stadium, explicitly framed as the successor to the 1995 Blue Heaven Open. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Legends of E-Wrestling supercard staged itself, in its own broadcast's opening voiceover, as the direct successor to the 1995 Blue Heaven Open, recapping that earlier show's own famous matches before declaring “only the few and the proud can be called… LEGENDS.” Presented by Sweet Daddy Productions alongside Hailstorm/NewJack Productions, RiffRaff Productions and Jammerwolf Productions, the two-night card drew its talent from AAPW, NCW and WWW and staged itself live from a fictional Ericsson Stadium in Charlotte, North Carolina, on 16 and 17 August 1997, in front of a broadcast crowd of 70,000.

Night One's card ran a lucha libre trios match (Fallen Angel, El Magnifico and Scorpio against The Crow and Los Panteras de Guerrera), a tag title challenge from the former WWW Tag Team Champions the Tokyo Blades against the reigning NCW Tag Team Champions Bad Company, and a thirty-minute Ironman match between two multiple-time champions, Fatal Attraction and the Player's Club.

The night closed on its own main event, the Ralph Nazzaro Invitational Women's $100,000 Bunkhouse Battle Royale, won by “Terminator” Tara Collins over a field that included Lady Rose, Dixie Hart, Jeandra, Eve, Evie and Lupa. The broadcast closed the night on a comic coda: the match's own sponsor tried to claim the winner's kiss along with the trophy presentation and was decked for it.

Night Two was advertised in the same announcement — a ten-man rumble for $50,000 and a full card of its own rivalries settled — but no Night Two broadcast survives in the record, and nothing documents whether it aired as planned.
