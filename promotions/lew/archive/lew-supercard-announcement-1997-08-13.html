The Legends of E-Wrestling Supercard

13 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*ATTENTION* Sweet Daddy Productions in conjuction with Hailstorm/NewJack Productions, RiffRaff Productions, and Jammerwolf Productions present: "The Legends of E-Wrestling" Supercard, scheduld for August 16th and 17th at Ericcson Stadium in Charlotte, North Carolina.. This two night card will include high-powered action from over 50 top stars in the world... The Card has been finalized and a special Legend's of E-Wrestling Contraband Preview show is in production for Thursday night.. If you haven't already ordered it The ONLY way to see Legends of E-Wrestling is to wathc it closed circuit on RSPWF television... The card is as scheduled Night 1: August 16, 1997 - Fallen Angel, El Magnifico & Scorpio take on The Crow & Los Pantera de Guererra in an exciting Lucha Libre match.. - Former WWW Tag Champions, The Tokyo Blades challenge current NCW Tag Champions, Bad Company - Adam of The First Family and The A-Man go at it in a No Disqualification war.. - The Crazy Stancil Bros with a Mystery Partner vs. The Triad in a Bangkok Streetfight.. - Careers are on the line when Lady Rose of Thornefield Manor challenges the woman she stole the NCW Women's title from 1 year ago Dixie Hart - In their last match ever against each other: Gemini of The Suicidal Society w/Somerset vs. "The Amazing Love Machine" Dane Phipps w/Evie in a grudge double strap match - What happens when multiple time champions Fatal Attraction and The Player's Club grapple in a 30 minute Ironman match And for the Main Event - The Ralph Nazzaro Invitational Women's $100K Bunkhouse Match with members including, Jeandra, Eve, Evie, Lupa, and "Terminator" Tara Collins Night II: August 17, 1996 - The SuperPowers ("The Russian Superman" Petor Andresku and The American Enforcer) and The Domingez Brothers of Cuba clash - Hashimoto Tengen w/mystery partner and Dragon-San w/mystery partner in an international grudge match - Super-Heavyweights explode when from NCWF World Champions Big Body Brown goes against KIA in a No DQ, No Countout match - The Crazy Stancil Brothers strike against and this time former AAPW Tag Champions The Punishers will provide the resistance in a Texas Tornado match - Former friends turned bitter enemies when Evie takes on her mentor Eve in a FIRST BLOOD match - A 10 Man Rumble for $50,000! - Rivals renew their bitter war when "Original Gangsta" Gary Trent and "Dashing" Derek Lankford get it on in an I Quit -- Cage match) - And multiple champions The Stylistics w/Rob Spare take on The Suicide Blondes of The Suicidal Society in a Spin The Wheel match And in the explosive Main Event - War Games match: Team NCW and Team AAPW fight for supremacy Legends of E-Wrestling Supercard coming August 16th and 17th..
