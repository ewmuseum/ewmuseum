Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Wichzini Wrestling Project introduced itself on 1 April 2004 as “an exciting and new venture into the realm of women's wrestling,” explicitly staged as a women's offshoot running under Blood, Sweat & Chairs Wrestling's own banner, based out of BSCW's Axis Ballroom in Boston. Its own founding FAQ made the pitch for another women's promotion directly: “Quality women's federations and divisions are lacking — there's only a handful, so why not add one more to that number?”

In-fiction, the promotion was run by Talia Wichzini, billed as a veteran of women's wrestling who brought her own “twisted view of the wrestling industry” to the front office, with baby-I, also known as Naomi Ishikawa, a five-year veteran of joshi puroresu, serving as her vice president and conscience, watching out for the roster's safety even as Wichzini found her “very annoying, but very helpful.”

Two championships launched with the promotion: the BSCW Women's Heavyweight Title, passed to Talia herself as a gift after her own reign as its prior champion, and the WWP World Tag Team Titles. Both were built around a single weekly show, Girls on Film, three matches an episode, held at the Axis Ballroom whenever the promotion wasn't on tour.

No further document survives. Whether Girls on Film ever aired isn't in the surviving record.
