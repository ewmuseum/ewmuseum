Wichzini Wrestling Project FAQ Table of Contents: 1) Introduction 2) Who's Running This Joint? (Both IC & OOC) 3) Titles 4) Shows 5) A Word About Strats and RPs 6) In Closing... 7) Application [ --- ] 1) Introduction Legend has it that it was written by the Dark Ones- Necromicon ex
Mortis, The Book of the- whoops, wrong introduction.
WWP stands for Wichzini Wrestling Project, and is an exciting and new venture into the realm of women's wrestling. Based out of BSCW's infamous Axis Ballroom in Boston, WWP looks to elevate women's wrestling to a whole new level.
In layman's terms- welcome, this be a new women's fed under the mighty banner of BSCW, run by some crazy guy out of Boston named Rich.
Thanks to him for letting us do this, by the way. Concerning WWP, We Who Are In Charge (more on those folks later) are looking to provide you with quality women's e-w- nothing more, nothing less. Why not run a men's fed, you ask? Because anyone can do that. Quality women's federations and divisions are lacking- there's only a handful, so why not add one more to that number?
In WWP, we're looking for solid gimmicks with depth- a run-of-the-mill
"beautiful woman who happens to throw down with the best of them"
gimmick might be fun to run, but unless you bring something new to the table it won't get you very far in WWP.
So, if you're interested, do us a favor. Read on. [ --- ] 2) Who's Running This Joint? In character, the Wichzini Wrestling Project is run by Talia Wichzini, with baby-I backing her up as her "trusted" VP. Wichzini is a veteran of the women's wrestling world, and has decided to use her twisted view of the wrestling industry in a front office position, so she'll definitely make her charges bleed, sweat, and cry for everything they earn in WWP.
As for baby-I, well... she's the total antithesis of Wichzini. Otherwise known as Naomi Ishikawa, baby-I is a five-year veteran of the strict world of joshi puroresu. However, the masked pixie acts as
Wichzini's heart, trying to look out for the safety of the wrestlers.
Wichzini finds her very annoying, but very helpful, thanks to her energetic spirit. Out of character, WWP is run by [name withheld] and [name withheld]. Griff's handled a whole cadre of characters in e-w such as The Gremlin, Jake Shaw, and Johnny Pleasence, while Justin's run too many characters to list here- Shane Destiny, Michelle Bailey, Jeremy Rhodes, and Nightlife are just the tip of the iceberg. When it comes down to it, these two just want to have fun, and they expect you to as well.
[ --- ] 3) Titles At the start, there will be two titles in WWP: the BSCW Women's Heavyweight Title, and the WWP World Tag Team Titles. The BSCW Women's Heavyweight Title is a gift from [name withheld] to Talia after her run as the prior champion, so only the best of the best will compete and win that particular belt- with Talia involved, that's pretty much guarantee. The same thing goes for the tag titles as well, naturally.
As with any organization, more titles will be introduced if necessary. [ --- ] 4) Shows Held religiously at the Axis Ballroom, Girls on Film is WWP's regular show featuring three matches as well as comments from WWP's competitive roster. Should WWP go on tour, the venue will change, but regardless of where WWP is any particular week, Girls on Film is guaranteed to provide all the action an organization could want... and then some.
[ --- ] 5) A Word About Strats And RPs As far as roleplays are concerned, Justin and Griff are looking for quality, not quantity; in other words, a 2K RP is more than enough as far as the Dynamic Front Office Duo is concerned. While long roleplays are sometimes necessary, gratuitously lengthy RPs that talk about nothing will be frowned upon... so remember, when in doubt- brevity is good.
To keep things short and simple, the same thing goes for strats. [ --- ] 6) In Closing Ultimately, Justin and Griff want everyone involved with WWP to have fun. They also want to have fun running WWP. If everyone has fun, then there will be good times for all.
Good? Good. Now, fill out the application!
