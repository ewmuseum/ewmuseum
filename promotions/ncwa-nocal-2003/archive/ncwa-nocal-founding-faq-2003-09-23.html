=========================================================== NCWA Northern California Wrestling Alliance FAQ =========================================================== Table of Contents I. What is NCWA? II. Who runs it? III. Who runs it?(IRL) VI. History V. Cards VI. Guidelines VII. Championships VIII. Application =========================================================== I. What is NCWA? The NCWA, or Northern California Wrestling Alliance, is an Independent E-Wrestling federation based in, you guessed it, Northern California. We will be having events all over Northern California in small arenas and centers, due to our small budget. Cards will be located in or around: Galt, Lodi, Stockton, Sacramento, Redding, Yuba City, Truckee, Lake Tahoe, Fort Bragg, Jackson, Etc. =========================================================== II. Who runs it? The NCWA is Co-chaired by Scott Addison, former Indy star who fell into a small amount of cash, and decided to live his life long dream of owning a federation. The federation has a few local sponsors, and have a weekly televised show on the local cable-access channels for the town we're in. The other Co-Owner, Harrison Lloyd, Grew up on a farm outside of Clements California and was just as into wrestling as Addison. At age 17 he began his very own Backyard Wrestling Federation, Due to the fact he had no time to attend a pro school, because he had priorities on the farm. ========================================================== III. Who runs it?(IRL) [name withheld], myself, I am a 24 Year Old graduate of the University of the Pacific where I majored in Technology. I will put out good cards, On Time, and add exiting angles to make this fed fun, because that's what it's all about. My Co-Owner kicks ass at Writing angles and cards, so I think together we can make NCWA a successful federation. =========================================================== VI. History I first thought of the NCWA 3 Years ago around February of 00'.We got about 32 Guys on the roster, had some good times, but College got to be too much so I had to shut it down(Now that i've graduated I have more time). =========================================================== V. Cards We have one show per week called Saturday Night Synergy, and as I've said it will be held at a different location in Northern California each week. =========================================================== VI. Guidelines First of all...I only want original guys on my Roster, I don't care how good you are, Names like: "Ice Cold" Steve Austin, The Soultaker, and Bret Heart won't be accepted. No...We have no Line minimum...because we want Quality Role Play's, I don't care how long they are, if it's a good read, it's a good read. NO USING OTHER CHARACTERS without permission from the Handler and the staff. No promo's done in the ring...We're a low budget federation, we can't afford a house show full of "Screaming Fans" every night, keep it original We use a BCC system, you send all your RPs,Flashes,Etc. To the Co-Owners, when we get 5 we send them out in a block mail to the rest of the fed. No Complaining about a loss...maybe it's just a storyline your involved in, if that's the case you'll get a rematch. =========================================================== VII. Championships The NCWA Heavyweight Championship is the most coveted prize in all of Northern California. The best Role Player in the fed holds this title, and it is only defended at certain shows. The NCWA All-star title is for the Mid-Card division, holding this means your are on your way to a heavyweight shot, this title is usually defended Once every other card The NCWA Northern Tag Championships are for the best tag team in Northern California, These titles are defended at all Major Cards and sometimes on Synergy. If you hold a title you will be a main player in all of our Storylines and it should be considered an honor. =========================================================== VIII.
