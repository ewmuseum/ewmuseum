SEWF is Hiring Singles and Tags in 2002

23 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SEWF can be found here... http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling3/scw0/ SEWF, a small southern indy wrestling fed, is hiring both singles and tag teams for the 2002 season. We're looking for handlers interested in having fun...bottom line. No whiners, no moaners, no illegitimate children of
Scott Hall.
Only original characters please. No WWF, ECW, WCW knock-offs...though god knows they are out there some where in a fed near you. Please just not our fed.
Requirements? A pulse and the ability to have fun when things are at their worst. Also, one rp a week would be nice. You can do more, but we'll most likely grade out your best rather than let someone get in a pissing match over number of rp's. We all have lives...let's live them and not spend every minute one-upping each other on a message board. Fill out an app and we'll go from there.
Why SEWF? Why the hell not! We're based on the idea of 'keep it simple stupid'. Our cards aren't full length novellas describing hold for hold what goes on in the match. We have a little of that, but we're not going overboard. The most important aspect is angling. We ask that handlers send in short strats for angle purposes only.
Southern Extreme Wrestling Federation http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling3/scw0/
