Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This Global Network Wrestling opened cold on 30 March 1998: “Friday Night X-Plosion is on the air!” its own president and lead announcer, Brandon Kronk, declared, “welcome to the first ever telecast for GNW.” He called the show alongside Kurt Castle and Mike Collinsworth, with a studio host, Casey Stanfield, filing recaps of matches that had already happened before the broadcast began.

Those recapped dark matches included Velvet Taco beating Akira Tenno with a move he called “The Whole Enchilada,” only for both men to be attacked afterward by a chair-wielding Frankie Furious; Scott Thomas powerbombing “The Hardcore Psycho” Christian Alicea onto a chair for the win; and “Iron” Mike Austin finishing the Assassin with his own version of a cutter he called the Electric Chair.

The live broadcast itself opened with Sterling Mitchell submitting Chris Brock, building momentum, in the announcers' own words, toward an upcoming World Title Tournament; the promotion's main event, the wrestler billed as the Anchor of GNW against Johnny Riot, was still to come when the surviving text breaks off.

Only the first of the broadcast's own three parts survives; the main event and everything the promotion did afterward, including whether that tournament was ever held, is not in the record. No document under this full name is attested past early May 1998, and no closure notice of its own survives.
