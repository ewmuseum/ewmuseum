(Scene begins outside of an arena. It is daytime and everything is in hyperspeed. The showing of the arene being built is in focus. Then the scene goes black. It reopens back to the same arena that is again built. The scene fades in on a logo above the front entrance way that reads "GNW" . Under it it reads "Global Network Wresting". The scene goes black. Once again it reopens. This time inside of the arena. Fans are screaming as pyro is shooting everywhere. The words "Friday Night X-Plosion" scroll across the screen as in the top left hand corner "TV-14" pops up. The "TV-14" disapears as the scene continues to panaround the audience. It then shoots over to an announcing booth where Brandon Kronk , Kurt Castle, and Mike Collinsworth are sitting. Brandon and Mike are wearing matching black suits with white ties. And in the middle of the two is the 6'7" 305 pound Kurt Castle. He is wearing a GNW ball cap and it can be seen that he is also wearing a "Kurt Castle World Tour '98" t-shirt. The scene continues to pan in and out as they speak) Brandon Kronk: FRIDAY NIGHT X-PLOSION IS ON THE AIR!!!!!!! Hello everyone, and welcome to the first ever telecast for GNW.....I am president of GNW Brandon Kronk, and to my left are my two colligues Kurt Castle and Mike Collinsworth. We will be your hosts for X-Plosion!!! Kurt Castle: Tonight we have some big things in store for the fans......besides me that is. We have the Masters of Hardcore taking on the Made Niggaz, Patch C. Bandit and Assassin and more!!! Mike Collinsworth: And in the main event we have the "Anchor" of GNW, [name withheld] going one on one against Johnny Riot. This night is really heating up!!! Brandon: The fans here in the GNW arena are on fire. For, if you are a fan that isn't here live, you just missed THREE high impact , high flying, and all around great matches that happened before we were on the air. To get an update, let's go to our very own Casey Stanfield in the GNW studios for the report! (The scene goes black. It then reopens in the studios of GNW. Casey Stanfield is sitting at a desk. Behins him are monitors which have various TV shows viewing on them at the moment. The scene shoots in on him and stops) Casey Stanfield: Hello everyone, and welcome to the Dark Match reports here on Friday Night X-Plosion. We just witnessed a great 45 or 50 minutes of action that you could only be a part of if you were here in the arena. So lets get to our first match! (The words "Akira Tenno VS. Velvet Taco" scroll across the screen in bold white letters) Casey Stanfield: This match was a high flyer's dream. These men put on a show for us as the fans were just piling into the arena. Things went back and forth for a while until Akira Tenno hit a missle dropkick from the top rope onto Velvet Taco on the FLOOR! (Shots of Akira Tenno nailing Velvet Taco with a missle dropkick are shown) Casey Stanfield: And from there, Akira Tenno stayed on Velvet Taco for a good 3 or 4 minutes until he hit a frankensteiner on Taco and got the pin. (Shots of Tenno hitting a frankensteiner into a pin are shown) Casey Stanfield: But as you notice , Velvet Taco did get his foot on the rope at the two count. The ref realized the mistake and had to tell Tenno about it! (Shots of Akira Tenno celebrating and the ref. coming over to tell him that he hadn't won are shown) Casey Stanfield: From there it seemed as if Velvet Taco was on the rebound. Every move that Tenno attempted , it seemed as if Taco had a reverse for it. And the end came as Velvet Taco connected with his version of the head scissor take down into a cover which he calls "The Whole Enchilada" and got the win! (Shots of Velvet Taco hitting various moves on Akira Tenno are shown. Then the shot of Taco hitting "The Whole Enchilada" and getting the win are shown) Casey Stanfield: But this incident was far from over. As Velvet Taco celebrated his win, Frankie Furious came storming tot he ring with a chair and brutally attacked both Velvet Taco and Akira Tenno. Furious quickly dropped the chair and stormed out after the attack! (Shots of Frankie Furios hitting the ring and taking out Akira Tenno and Velvet Taco with a chair are shown) Casey Stanfield: And we can rest assure that the fued between those three aren't over. I do have word now that on Tuesday In Review there will be some kind of interview/press confrence by Frankie Furious. Anyway on to the next match! (The words "Scott Thomas VS. "The Hardcore Psycho" Christian Alicea" scroll across the screen) Casey Stanfield: This without a doubt was the most physical 10 mintues or so that anyone could imagine. The ref pretty much let this match go as it wanted much to the fans pleasure. As the match started it didn't take long for someone to take it to the outside (Shots of Alicea and Thomas brawling on the outside of the ring are shown) Casey Stanfield: But finally "The Harcore Psycho" got the advantage at his own game as he got a chair into the ring and began to brutally beat on Scott Thomas with it!!! (Shots of Alicea nailing Thomas with the chair are shown) Casey Stanfield: But that would not deture Scott Thomas for a minute. One mistake by Christian Alicea and Scott Thomas hit a devastating powerbomb on Alicea on the CHAIR!! (Shot of Scott Thomas hitting a powerbomb on the chair are shown) Casey Stanfield: And from there it was sheer elementary as Scott Thomas finished it off with a hurricanrana off the top for the win! (Shot of Scott Thomas getting his hand raised is shown) Casey Stanfield: Next up was the "Main Event of the Dark Matches" as "Iron" Mike Austin went one on one with the Assassin. These men brawled and they wrestled. But it didn't take long for Mike Austin to hit his verision of the Diamond Cutter from the top rope which he calls the "Electric Chair" and got the win! (Shot of Mike Austin hitting the Electric Chair on Assassin is shown) Casey Stanfield: And I'll be back next week with yet another Dark Match report. But now lets take it back to the GNW Arena and LIVE ACTION! (Scene goes black and reopens in the GNW ARENA) Brandon Kronk: Well those were all great matches, but now, it is time for the LIVE matches! Kurt Castle: And we are going to start off with a man I know quite now. He is the a close firend to one of MY close friends Dan Maddux. We are talking about Sterling Mitchell! Mike Collinsworth: And he will be going one on one with one of my personal favorites. THE ROCK!!! Chris Brock! Brandon Kronk: Well what are we waiting for?!?!? Let's get down to Robert Moyer with the introductions!!! Robert Moyer: The following contest here on GNW Friday Night X-Plosion is set for one fall. Introducing first, "Semi Charmed Life", by Third Eye Blind begins to blare over the P.A system, he hails from Houston, Texas and weighs in at 245 pounds, THE ROCK CHRIS BRO-O-O-O-O-O-O-O-CK!!!!!!! (Chris Brock brakes out into the scene to a chorus of boos. He has a mean smirk on his face as he trots to the ring. He hops into the ring) Robert Moyer: And his opponent, "Yes…And it Counts" by Black Happy begins to kick up over the PA system, he hails from South Boston, Massachusetts and tops the scales at 271 pounds, STERLING MI-I-I-ITCHEL-L-L-L-L!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Sterling Mitchell walks out and down the ramp slowly with his head down, to a favorable crowd reaction. He rolls under the ropes and into the ring, where he leans against the ropes waiting for the start of the match) DING, DING, DING BK: Well here's the start of the match. Both men are circling each other trying to fins the right moment to strike. They both approach each other and hook up collar and elbow, neither man willing to give an inch. Mitchell now quickly turns to the side and hooks on a reverse waist lock. Mitchell is trying to feel Brock out, but Chris Brock quickly gets to the rope as the ref calls for the break. KC: Mitchell looks around the croud trying to gain his thoughts. Chris Brock sees it and now he moves in for the kill. He quickly hits a double ax handle to the back of the distracted Mitchell. Brock now starts to repeatedly fire those ax handles to the back of Sterling Mitchell. Mitchell now, is trying to keep moving and stay away from Brock, but Brock gets Mitchell to the corner. Chris Brock now starts to repeatedly nail Mitchell with powerfull European Uppercuts that staggers Mitchell! MC: Chris Brock lured Mitchell right into his trap and it worked. I LOVE THIS CHRIS BROCK.....Brock now lets up on Mitchell for just a second. Brock now grabs the arm of Mitchell and fires him into the opposite corner. Mitchell hits the turnbuckle hard and staggers forward and is met by a DOUBLE ARM LARIET that sends Mitchell crashing to the mat! BK: Sterling Mitchell is in trouble early on here in the match. Chris Brock wastes no time as he drops to the mat and sinches on a side headlock. He is really applying the pressure on the 6 foot 6 figure of Sterling Mitchell. Mitchell is spread out in the middle of the mat trying to get a foot to the rope, but Chris Brock is keeping all his weight to the middle of the ring. Mitchell is now laying motionless on the mat! KC: That's a smart thing to do. The more you struggle to get out fo the hold the more energy it will eat up. Chris Brock is holding onto the hold as tight as he can, and the ref is now on the mat checking out Mitchell. Mitchell sees the ref checking on him and begins to squirm as the crowd gets behind him. Mitchell now is up on one knee and now to his feet. Brock tries to keep the hold on but Mitchell nails him with a back elbow, and another, and a third one for good measures! Brock releases the hold and Mitchell bounces off the ropes and connect with a CROSS BODY BLOCK! 1...............2..........3!!!!! MC: NO!!! He got out of it. Sterling Mitchell is on the attack now as he gets Brock to his feet. He whips Brock intot the ropes and connects with a dropkick. The fans are realy getting behind this nut. Mitchell backs up to the ropes and waits for Brock to get to his feet. As he does Mitchell charges at Brock, but Brock drops to the mat and Mitchell FLIES OVER THE TOP ROPE and hits the mat hard. BK: That's going to take it's toll on Mitchell. Brock now jumps to his feet and holds on to the top rope with both hands waiting for Mitchell to get to his feet. As he does , Brock springs himself over the top rope and connects with a falling body splash......BUT MITCHELL CAUGHT HIM! The fans erupt as Mitchell caries Brock around the outside of the ring. Mitchell now starts running and........... (Mitchell runs at the ring post like a freight train while holding Chris Brock in front of him) **SMACK** ALL 3: DAMN!!!!!!!!!!!! KC: OH MY GOD!!! That could have broke the back of Chris Brock there folks. The fans are in aww as Brock tries to get to his meet. Mitchell helps him up and rolls him into the ring. Mitchell gets in the ring himself and quickly pops off the ropes and connect with a running elbow drop. Mitchell covers 1...............2...............NO SIR!!! Brock got a shoulder up in the nick of time. That was close MC: Brock knew what he was doing the whole time. Don't worry about it. Mitchell now hops to his feet and then scoops Brock off the mat. Mitchell hooks the far arm and connects with a vicious SNAP SUPLEX. But no cover!!! Mitchell instead, now gets Chris Brock up to his feet again. Mitchell now scoops up Brock for a scoops slam but Brock reverses it and rolls Mitchell up!!!! 1.............2................3!!! YES YES YES!!!! BK: NO NO NO!!! He kicked out. Sterling Mitchell quickly gets to his feet and rushes over to Brock and connects with a knee drop to the forehead of Brock. Mitchell once again does not go for the cover, instead he now lifts Chris Brock up in a position for a reverse atomic drop. Mitchell carries Brock over to the corner and sets him on the top rope. Mitchell now climbs the ropes and meets Brock. Mitchell now tries to hook Brock with a double underhook but can't. Instead Brock connects with a despiration head butt that sends Mitchell to the mat! KC: Now Brock is getting to his feet while on the top rope. The fans are rising to their feet as Chris Brock is perched up on the top rope. With one gasp of energy Brock lunges and nails Mitchell with a SHOOTING STAR PRESS!!! Brock is out too as he lays on top of Mitchell for the cover 1................2..................NO!!! GOD DAMN!! What does it take to put Sterling Mitchell away! MC: Don't know. But I'm sure Chris Brock wants to find out. Brock has rolled off of Mitchell and both are on their backs in the ring. The ref is starting to administer the ten count!!! REF: ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!! THREE!!!!! (Neither man is moving) REF: FOUR!!!!! FIVE!!!!! SIX!!!!! (Sterling Mitchell is up on all fours as Chris Brock is attempting to get to his feet as well) REF: SEVEN!!!! EIGHT!!!!! MC: And both men have simultaniously made it to their feet. Mitchell and Brock now stair down each other for a moment and now Brock connects on Mitchell with a right hand. And Mitchell now connects with a right. Now both men are brawling it out in the center of the ring fist for fist. Mitchell now connects with a hard LEFT CROSS that drops Brock to one knee!!! BK: Now Mitchell has his fist in the air looking around at the crowd as they are getting into it now. And now Chris Brock sees Mitchell's attention turned to the crowd and sneaks in a low blow upper cut. The fans are booing like crazy now as the ref goes over and warns Brock about the maneuver. Brock shrugs the ref off and grabs Mitchell who doubled over. Brock pulls Mitchell to the center of the ring and looks around the crowd to a terrible reaction. Brock now quickly changes his focus back onto Mitchell and connects with an EVEN FLOW DDT!!!!!!!! KC: This could very well end it!! Srerling Mitchell is out completely cold. Chris Brock rushes in for the laxy cover 1..............2............and Mitchell is able to easily get an arm up. Brock pops to his feet and immeadeatly goes over to the ref to argue about the count. Now Brock turns around to see Mitchell crawling to his feet. Brock is very confident about his chances now . He pulls Mitchell up and drags him over to the ropes. He gets Mitchell back down to his knees and lays Mitchell neck first over the middle rope. (ala Ray Traylor) MC: Brock now quickly bounces off the opposite ropes and comes flying back at Mitchell and connects with a BASEBALL SLIDE DROPKICK that hits Mitchell in the small of the back. Brock now grabs the legs of Mitchell and tosses his through the middle ropes and to the floor. Chris Brock now runs over to the near turnbuckle and climbs it quickly. With Mitchell still lying on the floor on the outside Brock leaps and attempts an ELBOW DROP but Mitchell moved and Brock hit the concrete floor very hard!!!! BK: HAHA!!!! Sterling Mitchell PLAYED POSSUM!!!! Mitchell pops to his feet on the mat and gets a hurt Chris Brock to his feet. He lifts Brock up into a gorrila press position and then drops Brock over the guard rail on his hurt shoulder!! (You can hear Chris Brock screaming loudly) BK: Sterling Mitchell now runs over to the ring announcer and grabs the chair out from under him. The fans erupt as Mitchell runs back around the ring twards the fallen Chris Brock. Brock is still lying on the cement floor. Mitchell now sets the chair flat across the shoulder of Brock. Mitchell now goes over and climbs the stairs to the aprin. Mitchell now is on the aprin and is walking over to where Brock is fallen................ (All of a sudden you see Sterling Mitchell jump off of the aprin and hit a double foot stomp on the chair which is on the hurt arm of Chris Brock) ALL 3: HOLY SHIT!!!!!!!!!!!! KC: I think the fans sucked all the air out of the arena when they gasped after that deadly move. Mitchell now grabs the chair and WHACKS it over the shoulder of Brock. Mitchell now tosses the chair into the center of the ring and nearly hits the ref with it. He now returns to the fallen Chris Brock. Mitchell once again GORRILA PRESSES Brock. This time he tosses him through the middle ropes and back into the ring MC: This doesn't look good for Chris Brock he is terribly wounded in the arm. Mitchell slowing and confidently waltzes back into the ring where Brock lies. Mitchell bounces off the ropes and connects with a vicious LEG DROP!!! Mitchell covers and the ref is in place 1.........................2................and Brock is able to kick out on time!!!! BK: Neither man is willing to lose here. They know how important a win here on X-Plosion is. Mitchell now is able to scoop Brock back to his feet. Mitchell whips Brock off the ropes, NO, Brock reverses ad Mitchell goes flying off the ropes. On the way back now Brock catcehs him and has him set up for a TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER!!!!!!! But wait, Mitchell is kicking his legs and the hold is reveersed and now Mitchell has him set up for a reverse DDT!!!!! KC: But instead, Mitchell now hoists Brock in the air for a REVERSE VERTICLE SUPLEX!! Mitchell is really holding him up there now getting all the blood to the head of Brock. Now Mitchell drops the reverse Suplex down into a BACK BREAKER!!!!! Mitchell still holds on to Brock and has him in some version of a DRAGON SLEEPER!!!!!! MC: THIS WHOLE SETUP IS CALLED THE SOUTH BOSTON SLEEPER!!!!!!! AND BROCK IS TAPPING OUT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! <DING, DING, DING> Brandon Kronk: What a match we have just witnessed to open up X-PLOSION!!!! Sterling Mitchell has gained some momentum heading into the upcoming World Title Tournament!!! Let's get the official word!!! Michael Moyer: Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of this contest is STERLING MITCHEL-L-L-L-L-L-L-L-L-L-L-L-L-L-L!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
