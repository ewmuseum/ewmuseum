News and Views Volume 4

Hey everyone, I dcided to send today's newletter a little earlier than usual because I am very excited about it's contents! I am happy to bring you today's newsletter because it includes the best news that we have had to report in our three week history! In a section directly following, you will see the press conference that announces our big news! I want to welcome all those from the AeWF that will be reading tonight's newsletter. I am glad all of you have this chance to share in this great event! Well the following has the table of contents. Part 1--> Press Conference Part 2--> Other news and announcements Part 3--> Current Roster and injuries Part 4--> Card Lineups for 9/14 and 9/18 Part 5--> NaWF Championship title announcements Part 6--> NaWF Application Well on with the newsletter. See you in the ring. [name withheld] NaWF/AeWF President =================================================================== Part 1--> Press Conference =================================================================== [As the picture fades in, we see a large room with a table in front. In the backdrop is a large picture that show the words "NaWF" and "AeWF". Around each word is the picture of a hand. The hands are coming together as if they are shaking hands. The table has a podium in the middle with several microphones on the top level. Across the table there are four microphones in front of four chairs. Reporters have gathered and filled this room to capacity just waiting for something to be said. Finally a door opens as we see four men step into the room. Camaras begin to flash rapidly and reporters are bustling around trying to get the best angle for their recording devices. We see NaWF President Chris Hopper and Vice President Smelly D sit on the right side. On the left side, we see AeWF President Karol Zielinski and Vice President Jay Anstiss taking their seats. NaWF President Hopper stands and goes to the podium to begin the press conference.] NaWF President Hopper: I want to thank everyone for coming out. This is a proud day for two federations. One that is fairly new, but growing daily, and another that is having problems and sees the light at the end of their tunnel. I am happy to announce in a deal that was worked out over many hours of negotiations, the New Alliance Wrestling Federation is going to merge with Anarchy e-mail Wrestling Federation. This is a mutual decision that we feel will benifit both of our interests. I believe that AeWF President Zielinski has a prepared statement he would like to read. (Hopper sits down as Zielnski stands and walks over to the podium. He has a piece of paper that he unfolds and looks at as he begins his speech.) AeWF President Zielinski: First of all, I would like to say that I'm really exciting about this merger. Since a couple of weeks, I had big problems with running the federation, due to time and money problems. Chris became a good friend of mine some weeks ago and I saw the ultimate chance for the federation: merge with one of the fastest growing wrestling federations on Earth - the NaWF. I know Chris well and I believe that we won't have much problems with taking important decisions together. (Hopper leans into his microphone and makes a comment.) NaWF President Hopper: This is a new day for two leagues. All of us are very excited about this merger. We will not be taking questions from the press corps and other media. (The reporters seem to lunge forward trying to gain the sight of Hopper and Zielinski.) NaWF President Hopper: Go ahead, Bill. Bill Apter: Yes, Bill Apter from Pro Wrestling Illustrated. This seems like an odd match considering that the NaWF is widely considered one of the top new feds out there, and the AeWF is a veteran fed that seems to be in trouble. President Hopper, are you sure that by siging into this merger you are not siging the death certificate for your own league? (Hopper gets quizzical look on his face as he looks over across the podium at Zielinski. He smiles and begins his answer.) NaWF President Hopper: Well I really resent your question Bill. I consider myself to be a wrestling enthusiast. The AeWF was a top league for a long time and it just ran into problems that really were difficult to solve on their own. We have stated these problems already. I saw that a good friend of mine was running a league that was in trouble and I figured that a merger with the NaWF would be what the doctor ordered. (Hopper seems a little upset by the question, but still recognizes another of the would-be questioners.) Stuart Scott: Stuart Scott of ESPN, there are reports that the NaWF is really suffering for talent. That you were about to close because wrestlers worth anything were already under contract in other leagues. Is this just an attempt to stay afloat or was it an act of pure survival? Also, Mr. Zielinski will there be any problems with a structure of power in this merger? Many times this will lead to squabbling for the top spot. How have you managed to stay away from that? and do you want to be out front again? (Hopper leans forward and answers.) NaWF President Hopper: I would say that your reports are truly just rumors. In fact I have signed three new talents the day before I even met with Mr. Zielinski. It is true that we will experience a large increase in our roster, but that will be up to the wrestlers themselves. I woudl agree that it is a matter of league survival because both of us needed each other or both of us may have died eventually. It is mutually beneficial. Karol? (Zielinski leans into his microphone to address his portion of that question.) AeWF President Zielinski: I don't think that there will be any problems about who should decide what. First of all because we're good friends and second because I don't have as much time as I had before for the federation, and the merge will help me to do a better work behind the scenes of the federation. (Zielinski recognizes another reporter.) Gene Okerlund: Gene Okurland here representing the WCW hotline. What are the thoughts of the Vice Presidents of these leagues? This decision was made by the Presidents, and I was wondering if they are in agreement with the decision and why or why not? (Jay Anstiss looks over and gets a nod from Smelly D to be the first one to answer thsi question.) AeWF Vice President Anstiss: Well, Gene, speaking for myself, I have to say that I'm fully behind this merger. It's fair to say that it's been a tough decision, but it's one for the better. The AeWF has had a hard time of late, but it has survived all obstacles. And by combining both an already established league like the AeWF with a relatively new one like the NaWF can only be beneficial to them both. The NaWF has some great talent, and I'm sure that by combining them with the wrestlers of the AeWF can only result in one thing: a better program of entertainment for the fans, and that's what we're aiming for here. (Smelly D leans forward and answers.) NaWF Vice President Smelly D: What all Mr. Anstiss has said is true. And I would also like to say a few more things. Since President Hopper and I got together and came up with the idea that we needed to begin the league that is known as NaWF, we have always looked for opportunites to add to the sport of wrestling and more specificly, the New Alliance Wrestling Federation. Now, with the merger with the AeWF, our league, the NaWF, looks to grow with leaps and bounds. All you have to do is compare the rosters of the two leagues and everyone will see that beyond a shadow of a doubt, these federations are where the real men come to play. It will be exciting to see the possiblities of what could come from this merger. Of course, we presently do not know which AeWF talents will decide to move there contracts to the NaWF, but there are MANY exciting possibilities for the competitive nature of all the men that will be here. Not only in the singles realm, but tag teams as well. So honestly, I can't wait to see what is to come here in the NaWF's future! (Hopper and Zielinski look at each other and motion that this would be the last question.) Lance Russell: Lance Russell, I am a syndicated columnist and pro wrestling announcer. Mr. Zielinski, one of the questions I would like to ask you is: how will the merger affect the "anarchy" side of the federation? And for the record, what exactly is the structure of the league's upper office now and how will it effect the NaWF's current format as in will there be a major event to unify these two leagues? (Zielinski leans forward to answer.) AeWF President Zielinski: Well, in the last couple of months, after over one year of experience, I understood that the fans don't want to see extreme madness anymore and I tried to change our style. Now, with the merger, it will give us the possibility to change our style to a mix of high-flying, tag team and extreme action! (Hopper stands up and walks to the podium to answer the question.) NaWF President Hopper: Staff structure is this. I am the President of the NaWF/AeWF. Mr. Zielinski, Smelly D, and Mr. Anstiss will all be given the status of Executive Vice President. That is how it has been decided during our talks. Now as far as how it will effect our events and such, it will not effect them at all. The wrestlers will be added and start from scratch with records of zero and zero. They will fight for titles that will bear the combined name and so on. There willbe no major event to unify them because as of right now, our two leagues are unified forever. Well again, I appreciate everyone turning out and I am looking forward to a long lasting partnership between the NaWF and the AeWF. Thank you and good bye. (All four men get out of their seats as teh reporters continue to shout questions at the men. They leave and the picture fades to black.) =================================================================== Part 2--> Other news and announcements =================================================================== --> In case you hadn't figured it out yet...We merged with the AeWF!
