Hot Copy #12

1 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey everyone, I am happy to announce the signing of a top talent. Sengir will be coming into the NaWF and wrestling a dark match this Friday. I am happy to have him here and he should make a great addition to the NaWF. If you haven't checked the web site, we got a facelift. The address is: http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Pressbox/3678 Check it out. Well that is it. On with the flashes! [name withheld] NaWF President ===================================================================== "Malicious Mike Hrankiwskyj ===================================================================== (The screen opens up as we see "Malicious" Mike Hrankiwskyj driving in his brand new 98' Durango. He is cruising down I-98 in New York City listening to the radio, flipping through the stations trying to find the right song for him. As the camera shows a close up of the back seat, we see an assortment of weapons. There are shovels, chairs, brass knuckles, and of course, one lead pipe. The pipe has a few dents in it, but that is understandably so, considering it was used much by Mike earlier on in the night. Bobby Durango and the Doctor became "close" to the pipe tonight, being the victims of Mike's heavy swings.) "Bobby, Kent Powers, Crazed Animals, no difference to me. Just a few more names and faces for me to break. Durango, I still got something to settle with you that goes far beyond tonight. Twice we have battled Durango. Twice I have beaten you within an inch of your life. Twice something saved you. The first time the bell rang as I had the cover. The second time there was a mayhem in the ring and the ref had to call for the bell. That's two times that I should have beaten you Durango. You pose no threat to me Durango. It doesn't matter when or where we fight again, but rest assure that we will meet again. Your luck has to run out sooner or later. There is no way that your luck will last much longer as long as I am around here, watching your every move. Durango, you may have been something big somewhere else, but you're in "Malicious" Mike's jungle baby, and you're gonna die." (Mike continues scanning through the radio, but still has no luck finding anything to his liking. Mike finally turns off the radio and there is a few moments of a thunderous silence in the car.) "Speaking of the Animals, I had nothing against you boys. I respected you guys. You helped me out alittle, and I was very appreciative of it. You came out and helped me beat on Kent Powers a bit, and it was all fun. Any wrestlerwould be. But that's where our respect ends. Yeah, I did cheap shot you before. I'll admit to it. I'm a man of honor, and I know what I did. I realized it, and it felt good. It wasn't anything personal. Doc, you were just in the wrong place at the wrong time. It's as simple as that. If you weren't there, and it was someone else, they would have been hit too. It isn't a personal thing. It isn't for me anyways, but for you guys I assume it will be different. Doc, I did cost you the match to Durango. It wasn't planned that way, it just happened. If you got something to say about it, then we can talk some things out and if necessary we can beat it out. I got no problem with that. As a matter of fact, I am more than willing to do that. I got a match with Sammy Parker this Friday. After that, I'm free, so if you two want to come down and settle things, then do so. I just made the offer, are you willing to accept it?" (The screen fades as Mike cruises away, out of sight from the camera.) ===================================================================== Kabuki Master ===================================================================== [The scene is the backstage locker room area after NAWF Battlezone. The camera is rather wobbly as the cameraman walks through the halls. Finally he turns left into a room. On a bench, is Kabuki Master, his face a bloody mess for the second time in the last week. He is mumbling to himself in Japanese as the camera enters the room. When he notices the camera, he stops and stands up.] KM [Smiling psychotically]: Joe Williams, what the *hell* were you thinking!? You draw blood, not once, but *twice* in one week. Williams, like I've said before, you're no different than all the other pretty boys I've seen in my career. A good athlete? Sure. Good physical conditioning? Definitely. But how much punishment can you take? What kind of heart do you have? Are you willing to risk your pretty face to put on a good show or to dish out punishment on your enemy? [Kabuki looks soberly into the camera and shakes his head.] KM: No...no, I don't think you do. You see, *I* have been there. When I first broke into this sport, I joined a so-called "hardcore" promotion, the Extreme Wrestling Association. There were cages, barbed wire, and blood galore. Not only did I survive...but I *thrived*. Now, here I am a year later...I'm going back to where I started from...back to my roots. I'm going back to my roots, Joe Williams, to teach you a lesson. You call yourself "Bad Attitude." Psh...you'd get eaten alive where I came from. Williams, when you step in the cage with me, you're stepping in the ring with a man who just doesn't care. I *don't* care...I'll do whatever it takes to hurt you. Mark my words, you will pay tenfold for your sins. Sayonara, sucker! [Fade out.] ===================================================================== "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams ===================================================================== [The scene opens up with a shot of the New York skyline....then the voice of famous "Tonight Show" announcer, Ed McMahon rings through the serenity... Ed McMahon: LIVE FROM NEW YORK CITY....the ONE...the ONLY...the IMCOMPARABLE...HEEEEEEERE'S JOE!!! [The scene changes to an ordinary sound studio where "Bad Attitude" Joe Williams and his manager Steve Jackson stand in front of a huge poster of Williams in the act of beating Kabuki Master with a lead pipe. The two are looking devilishly happy as Ed McMahon walks in to get his paycheck.] Jackson: You want your money you senile old coot? Here! [Jackson takes out his checkbook and writes a sum on it. Then he has the audicity to cram the check into the mouth of McMahon, stunning the elderly former late-night sidekick] McMahon: This is HUMILIATING!!! No amount of money is worth this!! Williams: Well I only know two McMahons....and they both are jack@$$es!! [Williams and Jackson laugh hysterically as Ed McMahon leaves angrily] Williams: Ok...now that that idiot left..we can get down to business! First, THE MAN'S Cheers and Jeers for Monday's show... Jackson: That's right NaWF...prepare to face the wrath of the two greatest geniuses to ever walk into the wrestling business as we take on the recent events in the NaWF.. Williams: <smirks> First...we give great big cheers... [Both Williams and Jackson give a thumbs up sign] Williams: To the Psycotic Animals for taking out the Powers brothers..something I should have done a long time ago. In fact, I would've done it if you two hadn't gotten in my way...should I make this a jeer?!? Jackson: Nah...better to not tick 'em off... Williams: Well anyway...I'll kick you sorry butts if you ever step in the ring with me, a virtual god of wrestling, but for the moment..good job guys! Jackson: And we'd like to issue a message to the ailing SuperPowers... Williams: But we can't say it here!!! Ok....now for the biggest Cheer of all... Jackson: The winner of the week, as I like to say... Williams: The one..the only.... Williams: "BAD ATTITUDE" JOE WILLIAMS! [Jackson shakes his protege's hand] Jackson: Good job son... [Williams fakes a breakdown...] Williams: This is..the greatest moment of my career....hell...my life... ["Bad Attitude" snaps back to reality] I mean seriously...not only did I win my match against that no-talent bum....who was he again? [Jackson shrugs his shoulders as if he can't remember the namer] Williams: Anyway..not only was I victorious in that regard...but I also beat the crap out of the Kabuki Master...AGAIN! And man was it well-deserved...but what can you expect when I'm just that damn good!! [Williams stops the self-promotion after a bit of posing and then goes on to the second part of his speech...the Jeers] Williams: And the winner of Jeer number one..Steve? Do the dishonors? Jackson: <smiles> Gladly.. [Williams again smiles..his eyes are probably dancing with delight behind his designer sunglasses] Jackson: This one goes out to Jack Strange...you think you're a better trainer than me?? ME??? Steve Jackson?!? We just proved who's the best..don't you think? Williams: Indeed... Jackson: My man Joe took your man out with a punch to the head...CLEANLY! What do you think about that?!? [Williams smiles at the obvious lie] Williams: Wait Steve...even though we all hate Strange..we have to save some time for the biggest loser of them all.. Jackson: Of course... Williams: Kabuki Master..not only did I have to put up with your lame sense of humor, but then you get blood over my new $100 dollar shirt as well? Of course..what do you know about that? You probably can't even count to 100... Anyway...when we step into that cage this Friday..I'm not only gonna beat you up for fun...and for that stupid skit..but for the memory of my favorite shirt... [Jackson gives a fake sniffle at the "sentimental speech"] Williams: So until next time..remember "WHO'S THE MAN?!?" I AM!!! [The camera focuses on the face on the poster of Williams as the camera fades to black.] ===================================================================== (c)1998 NaWF Productions
