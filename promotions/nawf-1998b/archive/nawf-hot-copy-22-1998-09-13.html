NaWF/AeWF Newsletter

Hot Copy #22

13 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
===================================================================== "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme ===================================================================== Alex Extreme: All right, All right things have got to be said and said now... Alex Extreme: So even though I'm busy with a major title chance somewhere else, I'll take some time to address the fans of the NaWF and only the NaWF. Why? Because the fans of the AeWF already know who I am. I'm the man they loved to hate. I'm the man who took over there federation this summer and tried to actually make money with it. Now do you see why I walked away? Now do you see why I gave up trying to make your fed worth while? I walked away and after a couple of shows--you folded up. Alex Extreme: Now I told Chris not to even consider picking up this federation and merging. I told him it would be a terrible horrible mistake. But he did it anyway. Stupid man. Didn't you learn from that whole NECW/AeWF debacle a while back? But here we are back with AeWFland which begs the question: will the AeWF "wrestlers" finally WORK or will they continue to ride the name recognition I got them while I was there? I've already heard McQuacker and Ruined taking smack but let me give you boys a hint: without me you all don't exist. Without me people start saying "who?" Alex Extreme: To the NaWF talent: you've inflitrated by a bunch of no-talent hacks living off names I got for them. None of them are truly worth the time and effort and should be considered automatic wins. To lose to one of them should make you wish to gack, throw up, and leave the sport forever. Their "champion" this summer, who interestingly enough just joined the NaWF, left the fed while he was still champ and refused to job to anyone. Alex Extreme: By the way, for those of you who don't know me--my name is Alex Extreme but you can call me Mr. Excitement because that's what I am 24-7. I built that lousy AeWF to what ever little credit they got. I'm also one of the best wrestlers in the sport today. In the last year, I've held numerous titles so to beat me means something. Remember that NaWFers...remember that now fade to black. (Fade to Black) ===================================================================== Jinsei Shakanuzi (The Flying Opinion) ===================================================================== [SCENE: A wrestling ring. In the ring, there is a large desk and about six or seven chairs around it. In the corner, there is a bigger chair, made of hard steel, with japanese signs on it. The camera zooms out and shows the whole arena: four spotlights are showing the aisle way, one the entrance and about nine are giving light to the ring. Suddenly, the lights go out and for a moment, nothing happens. Suddenly, fireworks erupt from the aisle way, from the turnbuckles and all around the ring. However, there are no cheers, because there is no one in the arena. The opening melodies of "Them" by King Daimond starts playing over the arena as "The Flyer" Jinsei Shakanuzi appears on the aisle and makes his way to the ring. He is wearing his trademark black coat, along with black pants and a black t-shirt. His face, as always, is painted in black and white, forming the "Ying & Yang" sign. It's hard to say if he's smiling or not, but he seems to be in a good mood. He enters the ring, takes off the coat, throws it out of the ring, and sits down on the bigger chair in the corner of the ring. He is holding a microphone in his hand.] FJS - "Welcome everybody. You are probably wondering what I'm doing here and why there aren't any fans here. I'm here to debut my personal show - "The Flying Opinion"." [Jinsei Shakanuzi makes a bizare grimace to the camera, something between a smile and an evil look.] FJS - "Again, you might be wondering what the hell this show should be. I'll tell you. In the next couple of months, at irregular basis, I'll held these "shows". This is an uncesored late night show, so if you're either a child or you don't like violent or pornographic programms, you should change the channel. Now, for those among you who didn't change the channel yet, I'll explain you about what I'll be talking in these "shows" and what will happen when I'm on air." "First of all, I'll talk about business, in other words - wrestling. I'm currently under contract with Gunryu Pro Wrestling, NaWF/AeWF and Ultimate E-Wrestling federations and there are many things happening in this business about which I would like to talk without that a fucking cameraman censores anything. In the next weeks, I'll not only advertise some of my own-made t-shirts, caps, wrestling tapes and other Jinsei Shakanuzi merchandise, but I'll also invite guests, like my buddies "Wildcat" Jim Lewis Jr., Simon "ThunderStryke" McQuaid or Scorpio, but also some of the men I do not like too much, like Vinny Vitrone, Jason Storm, Mexx, Kabuki Kid and others. But before I continue, let's go to a quick commercial! Don't go away, I'll be right back!" [The camera cuts to commercial:] [Scene opens to a picture of Jinsei Shakanuzi, leaving the NECW ring with the Lightweight title around his waiste.] Voice - "Since day one as champion, Jinsei Shakanuzi was a man who had only one goal - to defend his title as much as possible." [Clip shows Jinsei Shakanuzi in various matches, all ending with a win for Jinsei Shakanuzi. People like Jim Lewis Jr., Vampyro, Shane Larson, were all pinned by the Flyer.] Voice - "Sometimes, he got the job done..." [Clip shows Jinsei Shakanuzi losing his NECW Lightweight belt to Kain, losing his AeWF King of the Air title to Wardancer and losing his IeWC Lightweight title to Mad Goat.] Voice - "Sometimes, he didn't..." [Clip shows Jinsei on the recent Night of Glory show, where he jumps on the third turnbuckle and hits two opponents outside of the ring with a plancha.] Voice - "However, he always flew..." [Clip shows Jinsei Shakanuzi performing a frankensteiner from top rope on an opponent in the NECW.] Voice - "...and flew..." [Clip shows "The Flyer" executing the Capoeira Dance on Vampyro.] Voice - "...and flew..." [Clip shows Shakanuzi executing a springboard moonsault on the outside, with the opponent moving in the last moment.] Voice - "...until he landed...badly. However..." [Clip shows Jinsei Shakanuzi with blood on his face, standing on the aisle, looking back to the ring and making his way back to the squared circle.] Voice - "...he always came back, and he'll never stop flying, landing and coming back!" [The camera fades to black and it comes back to Jinsei Shakanuzi's late nite show after a couple of seconds.] FJS - "Welcome back everybody! I'm your host, Jinsei Shakanuzi, and if you'll not change the channel now, I'll be still your host for this night! As you might probably already know, I had a 12 men Trios Tag Team match today, in the G-Pro federation. Unfortunately, I was the first being eliminated and my buddies, McQuaid and Lewis Jr. didn't manage to win the titles, even if both performed a great match! Congratulations guys! Well, I'm a little pissed about what happened, but I can't change it anymore. However, I still have a challenge for Frazer Fury. Remember when I asked you for a shoot-style match? I never got the answer. Now, I ask you again, but with one little extra stipulation: if I'll win, the International Brotherhood will get a title shot against your team, Zokugun Sangai. How about this?" "However, McQuaid and Lewis Jr. aren't the only partners I have to team up with. At one of the biggest wrestling weekends, the Jay Ralston' Memorial Card, I'll have to team up with Vinny Vitrone, an ex-ennemy from G-Pro Wrestling, Spider Green, a man I don't know much about and Wardancer, one my best friends in this business. We'll take on my G-Pro Brother "Wildcat" Jim Lewis Jr., the most talented young man I've ever seen, Andrew Tucker, a man I respect as much as I respect anybody else who proves to be one of the top crusierweights in this business day by day, Mr. Excellent, a man I hate more than anybody..." [Jinsei Shakanuzi suddenly stops, smiles and continues.] FJS - "Sorry, I forgott about my fourth opponent. Mr. Excellent comes at number two at my most hated ennemies list, as he is the man who betrayed the whole AeWF and joined up with the NECW gang. My last opponent is an old ennemy of mine. His name is Vampyro - the Master of Moonsaults. Vampyro, it has been a long time that we faced each other. But if I remember right, I won. Not only that I won our last match, I won each of our matches. It will be a pleasure for me to enter the ring to fight you. Vampyro, all four men of your team will be on my hit list...YES, even my friend Jim, but you'll be the man I'll be hunting after!" "The first Interview show of the JRMC was shown today, and there was an interview with my good friend Jim Lewis Jr., who explained why he takes part in this event and Spider Green, who showed his history in interesting clips. He comes from the jungle, and he has a great ability. He debuted at APW's Anarchy in the UK III Pay-Per-View where he fought and defeated a man called Menendez...never heard of him. He presented some of his other accomplishements, like winning the APW Lightweight title four times, pinning lightweight legends like myself, Ivan Ramius and winning the IeWC Lightweight title." "Spider Green, if you really think that you are impressing me or anybody else signed in this match with what you've showed us, you should better rent some old NECW tapes as see how people like myself or the Wildcat fight!" "By the way fans, if you would like to find out more about me, the "Flyer" Jinsei Shakanuzi, I have something what will make you happy: On November, 1st, the 1st Volume of "Jinsei Shakanuzi's Way from Rookie to Flyer" tape will be released, available at [detail withheld] - FREE!" "After this great announcement, I would like to talk about a federation I just joined, or a federation with which I'm under contract which just merged. I'm speaking about the NaWF/AeWF. The AeWF was always something like a second home for me and Karol was always a good friend of mine, especially after Extreme Chaos II..." [Jinsei makes a grimace, and seems thinking about what happened at AeWF's latest Pay-Per-View.] FJS - "Anyways. I just signed with new president Hopper, a man I've faced in the ring at "A Night to Remember" and everything looks fine. However, there is one thing, or person to be detailed who I have a problem with. This man is a wrestler who cost me my GNW Lightweight title. This man names himself "Bad Joke" Bobby Durango...wait...it's "Bad Apple" Bobby Durango. Hell, what kind of shitty name is that? Hell, did he had a problem with an apple in his childhood? Did his father give him only apples to eat? Doesn't he like apples? What does an apple have to do with wrestling? This guy certainely has a mind-problem. Well, "Apple", you interfered in my title match and though that I wouldn't make you pay for this as the GNW shut down some days after this coward attack. However, something, luck or destiny, put us in the same federation. Durango, I want you to come to my show next week. Without weapoms, without friends, with wrestling in mind. Just be here and we'll "talk", if you know what I mean." "Next topic: Ultimate E-Wrestling. A federation I'm part of since a couple of months, but did not have the time to study my opponents or do interviews. With some other problems out of way, I'm sure that I'll be able to concentrate myself a little more on this great federation. You see, because of my silence, president Jay tried to kick me out. However, he discovered a small clause in my contract: I can only leave the federation if I'll chose to do so or if I'll lose a loser-leaves-town match. So he put me in a roster place match against some...should I say it? No, I won't. Against some guy who loses all the time. His name? Mr. Johnny Bod. Hell, I came down the aisle, and finished this guy off with my Capoeira Dance. So I kept my roster place. Now, I always though that there's no much competition in this federation, but as I was in my plane from Japan to the States, I watched some of the recent shows and interview-segments! Hell, this federation is a great one! All these great athletes! I think I'll have to sign some contracts for some great matches there! Guys like Ramius, Setzer Van Strife, Alex Extreme, Keening, Vinny Vitrone, Nick Leshenn...Nick Leshenn. I believe I know him from somewhere! Oh yeah! He was in the I-Crown durng that I was preparing myself for my match against Hopper! He was saying a lot of bad things about me, like that I can't win the match because of my size, that I'm an idiot, and other bad things...well, even if I lost the match, it certainely wasn't because of my size, but because of the talent my opponent had! Leshenn, if you're watching this, let me say this: I'm challenging you to a match at the next available match. However, let's include something special into it: I chose a stipulation, and you chose another one, OK?" "Well, guys, unfortunately, I don't have much time left, as I'm sheduled to fight in some independant federation, but you can bet that I'll come back next week with a bigger show!" [Camera fades to black.] ===================================================================== © 1998 NaWF/AeWF Productions
