Monday Night Battlezone (part 5 of 5)

31 August 1998

[Commercials end and we fade back to our announcers in the broadcast booth.] DF: Welcome back fans. It is now time for our big main event. These two men have ties to each other that go back years, guys. TW: Well Parker's mentor, Jack Strange was a major player in the old NECW and he had a few matches against Parker's foe tonight. LW: And I will gauran-damn-tee ya that everything Strange tells that rookie will be enough to get his butt beaten in the ring by Joe Williams. TW: Joe Williams is a former Light Heavyweight and National Champion in teh NECW as well as other titles in other leagues. Strange and WIlliams have history, but how will it play out with a different player in the mix? DF: Only one way to find out. Let's head to the ring! ________________________________________________________________ |----------------------------------------------------------------| | MAIN EVENT!!! | |________________________________________________________________| | | | | |Monday | "Bashful" "Bad Attitude" | August | |Night | Sammy --VS-- Joe | 31, | |Battlezone| Parker Williams | 1998 | |__________|__________________________________________|__________| ("One Week" by the BareNaked Ladies comes through the PA. Out walks "Bashful" Sammy Parker wearing Aqua Trunks, And A Strange Company Training Facility T-Shirt in Black And Aqua. Parker walks down the aisle with his head down with his manager, Jack Strange at his side. Strange is wearing a black armani suit, carrying a black cane. The crowd gives a small pop. Parker continues to the ring without even looking at the crowd at all.) DF: The fans seem to like Parker, but his manager may be a different story. TW: Well Jack Strange is notorious for being a rule breaker and it is understandable why wrestling fans all over the world doubt his current alignment with Parker as a fan favorite. DF: Jack is a wild one that is for sure. [As the crowd starts to die down from Parker's entrance, Williams' entrance is started by a sexy female voice] Voice: Who's the man? [William's voice is next] Williams: I'M THE MAN!!!!!!!! [The word "Attitude" appears through lights on the entryway as two blue spotlights focus on Steve Jackson, who dressed in an Armani suit kicks things off, talking on his cellular phone and leading the way down to ringside. "Mr. Attitude" Joe Williams follows his mentor's path, as he comes down the aisle. He's dressed in his blue wrestling trunks and vest, with designer sunglasses on. He's accompanied to the ring by two beautiful women, one blond, one brunette. They slowly make their way to ringside ignoring all the fans. Williams and the women enter the ring where each women pecks Joe on the cheek before departing as Williams begins his pose-fest.] LW: He is exactly what he says he is. The man. TW: He is also self-deluded and immature. LW: You wouldn't know talent if it slapped you upside your ugly face. DF: Gentlemen, please. Williams is one of the top pound for pound wrestlers in any league. He is top hand in any weight class and knows enough submission moves to put many UFC'ers to shame. Like him or hate him, Williams is a top athelete. LW: See someone else appreciates him like I do. I am surprised by that Danny-boy! DF: Oh I appreciate his talent, but I hate him nontheless. LW: Moron. DF: Well it looks like these two are about ready to lock it up or are they? (The bell sounds and they begin to circle one another. Parker looks like he is willing to stay away from Williams.) DF: Parker and Wlliams are circling and seem as though they do not want to make contact. LW: I don't think it is both of them. I believe it is the "Bashful" freak that doesn't want to lock up with Williams. (They circle a bit until Joe turns his back and then manages to trick Parker into a false sense of security. Then he swings his leg and drops Parker with a legsweep.) DF: Williams tricks Parker and then takes him down. LW: Five points! TW: Uh Larry, it is not that kind of match. LW: Well that may bethe only action the way this little daisy boy prances around avoiding contact! I have to enjoy what I can or I will get bored stiff! TW: I guess I see the point, but maybe Parker is just biding his time. LW: I doubt it. DF: Well Williams manages to grab that leg and take him down again. Here is a knee to the chest by Williams. He is attacking Parker while he lays on the mat with knees like that! (Williams takes Parker off of the mat and whips him into the corner. You can see Jack Strange yelling "slide!" as Parker drops down and slides under the ropes.) TW: Great move to get out of a bad situation by Parker. DF: In all fairness it was Strange's direction. LW: (chuckling) Like he could think to do that by himself. DF: Well Parker is trying to get his gameplan together and look at this. Joe Williams is telling him to go ahead and get in the ring. TW: Watch out. (Parker tries to get in the ring and Williams pounces on him wiht a knee to the head. Parker grabs him and suplexes him back into the ring.) DF: What a way to enter the ring! Williams suplexes Parker back into the ring and begins to work on the neck area of Parker. TW: Wow! Joe reacts so quick that Parker really thought he was a safe distance away, but Joe made up the distance and pounced in a big way! LW: Well Joe is a smaller man than Parker, but Parker does not always have that killer instinct that you need to be a champion. DF: That is true, Larry because Parker seems to be hesitant to lock up, and then there are times that he snaps and becomes very dangerous. The problem with him is that you never know which one you will get. LW: Yeah kind of like Forrest Gump and his box of chocolates. (All three announcers chuckle) DF: I guess so. Williams is still working over the head and neck areas. Here is a jumping neck snap! Williams picks Parker up and body slams him! Williams is fighting like a man possessed! LW: And Parker is running like a girl that is frightened. What a loser. (Williams grabbs him and whips him hard into the corner and lands a terrific dropkick as Parker bounces out of the corner.) DF: Look at the dropkick by Williams! What shoudl we be more impressed about: The move itself, or the placement of the dropkick nailing the head and neck area. Joe Williams is a surgeoun and he is taking Parker apart. (Williams grabs on to Parker's head and puts it in a monster headlock.) LW: This may be the final stages of wear down on Parker. This is the headlock from Hell. DF: Parker tries to drop to his knee and get out of the lock, but he can't break it. Goodness this is an amazing hold that Joe has on Sammy. (Williams is holding onto Parker's head and will not let go. Sammy tries to whip him into the corner and can't get it done because of the grip that Williams has on that headlock.) DF: I really do not think a simple Irish Whip will get him out of this one. TW: Right Dan. It may take a little more effort than that. LW: It is going to take a miracle. (Parker manages to get his hands around Williams' waist and picks him up for a side suplex.) DF: There is your miracle! Parker gets out of the jam and begins to recover by landing a few kicks to the lower back of Joe Williams. Parker may have snapped! (Jack Strange gets up on the ring apron to distract referee Herman Gehl as Parker tosses Willaims out of the ring.) TW: It looks like that! Here he goes to the outside to go after Joe Williams! He has a chair and wham! He just pegged Joe upside the head! DF: Steve Jackson has come over to try and help, but he just gets leveled by a shot of that chair! Oh My Gosh! (Joe manages to make it to his feet and grabs a chair of his own. He then swings it and whacks Parker on the back with the chair. Parker's eyes went wide and he turns and begins swinging uncontrollably at Joe.) DF: He has truly snapped! They are swinging at each other and the chairs are just hitting one another. Parker is swinging again and again and it is too often for Williams to continue to block them. TW: Parker lands a shot! He is pounding Williams down to the ground with the chair! (Parker grabs Williams and throws him back in the ring. Williams looks dazed and out of it as Parker begins to land kick after kick to his fallen opponent.) DF: He has snapped and finally goes for a pin! 1 . . 2 . . thre... LW: Williams got out of the pin! What determination by the veteran! DF: Parker screams and begins to whip him into the corner. He catches him on the rebound off teh corner with a lariat. He nearly took his head off with that one. TW: Parker with a Powerbomb! Williams looks like he is hurt badly! Here comes another powerbomb! Oh My Gosh! How is Williams still conscious? His head bounced up about two feet after he hit the mat! DF: Williams is taking a beating that is for sure. Parker is looking at Strange, and Jack tells him to finish him. Parker goes in for the kill and WIlliams grabs Parker's tights and yanks him out o fthe ring and onto the floor! Jackson is coming over and WIlliams is grabbign the referee. TW: Jackson has a martial Arts background, so that may come into play. (Jackson begins to land a series of kicks that is followed by a shot to the head with his cane. Williams finally goes outside and begins to work on Parker by whipping him right into the steel ring post.) DF: Williams is finally doing his own work now. He rolls Parker back into the ring and lays on him for a cover! This could be it! 1 . . 2 . . thre... TW: Parker kicked out! Sammy Parker made it out of the pin and this is just amazing! Parker can't hardly stand, but he manages to get out of the way as Williams charges! (Williams hits the corner and gets caught in a DDT by Parker.) DF: DDT by WIlliams and that could do it! Parker has made it! He has taken him down and what is Jackson doing now? LW: He is protecting his protoge! DF: Jackson is keeping the referee from counting the pin that Parker has on Williams. Williams is laid out cold! Parker is angry and he walks over. TW: Big mistake. LW: You got that right. DF: Parker walks over ot get the referee's attention and doesn't realize that Williams has gotten up and is standing behind him with brass knuckles on his right hand. Turn around! (Parker turns around and gets plastered by those brass knuckles. Parker falls straight down to the mat and Williams tosses the knuckles as he runs over to Parker to finish it off.) LW: Parker is done now. Count this one up as a win for Williams. DF: Williams is trying to put on the Deep Freeze, his submission finisher. He has it and turns it over! The referee is asking Parker. He can't talk because he wa sknocked unconscious by those knucks. The referee has no choice but to call the match vefore Parker ends up injured. (Referee makes a move towards the booth and the bell sounds, ending the match. Joe will not let go of Parker's legs.) TW: Parker is in trouble because he can't defend himself and Joe obviously is not satisfied with just beating him, he wants to hurt him. DF: Williams finally lets go and he pushes the referee out of the ring as Parker gets pulled out of the ring by Strange. That referee is up and he is powerbombed! LW: This is so funny! (begins laughing) DF: We need help out here for the referee as WIlliams has him in the Deep Freeze. What is going on? (The crowd erupts with cheers as Kabuki Master comes out and has a chair.) DF: It's Kabuki Master! He is back to settle the score! We are out of time fans! We will see you this Friday at "X-Tremely Dangerous! For Trey Williams and Larry "The Crusher" Watters, I am Dan Firestone saying good night from Indianapolis! (Screen fades to black as we see Kabuki enter teh ring and swing the chair. The screen's picture is gone before we see whether or not he connected.
