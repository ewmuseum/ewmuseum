The Legendary E-Fed Returns

10 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New Alliance Wrestling Federation Thats right. Your favorite president has rose again! I, [name withheld], am back, like I promised, to bring you the hottest E-Fed to burn up the summer! I will again bring NAWF back! Now, you all are reading this, and remember all the failed attempts that people had when they opened up NAWF. Well, think back again compadres. Think back at what NAWF was when [name withheld] owned it. It was the hottest thing going on the internet! And now I am back to bring you the same quality, and more. As I have stated, NAWF is now a Summer Federation.
We open in the middle of June, and shut down in the middle of September. The three months when EVERYBODY has the time on their hands. And then we open again in the next summer. Easy as that. The NAWF roster will be composed of past NAWF stars plus new talent. To everyone, Welcome back to one hell of a summer, NAWF is back on top!
NAWF Rules 1. The NAWF will be an open role playing federation. Which means we will have RP Lists sent daily. I am choosing to do this because thats how NAWF has always been run, and I feel that is the best way to do things around here. If at any point in time I feel that I must make the fed a "BCC" mailing system, then so be it.
2. Role Playing Length? Listen, I am not going to put any limit to how long or how short your role plays must be. If your role play is short, and you get your point out, then thats how the chips will fall, and you will get credit for your role play. If you have to write some long role play, that is just nonsense, you right there piss me off. So dont think that writing something huge is going to impress me. If your role play is really to long, I will take away some credit, because there is no need for that.
3. Spamming...well, dont do it. I can understand if you are trying to get a quick RP out and you finish something short right before the deadlines...but if I get something that bombs your oponent away at the deadline, I will laugh at your efforts and disregard that role play. That is unless you both sent something out at the deadline, then your role plays will be counted for. If you do send something out five minutes past the deadline because you were somewhere else, or you have a descent excuse, I will let it pass...just as long as the role play is at honest length.
4. You have a problem with another E-Wrestler, or me, then you take it out on me. I dont want any nonsense sent to the mailing list, because thats just wrong. All it does it flood mail boxes, which goes back to the first rule. If things like that get out of hand, our mailing list will be closed to just me.
But as I was saying, if you have a problem with a loss, you IM me, and you say what you must. I know when some people lose they get heated and say things they dont mean. So if you are pissed, go ahead and take it out on me, I will not remove you from the list unless its upon your request. But, let me warn you, sending your grievances this way will not, "and the Mike means WILL NOT"
change the outcome of your matches. 5. Stables may include no more then seven members. And you must have atleast 2 members to have an official stable. 6. And let me now make this for the record, to those of you that are new the NAWF, we are unlike any other federation. We dont bore you with long, nonsense of cards. We only produce the most quality, action packed matches. There is no need for 10 mail cards, our cards at the max reach 3, and our PPV's at the max reach 5. So if thats your bag, NAWF isnt for you. We are the true tradition of e-wrestling. We are what e-wrestling is. "We were e-wrestling before e-wrestling was cool".
7. If you want a match here in the NAWF, and you are new, you must send at least an intro RP this way the fed knows who you are, and this way you let me know that I can depend on you to role play. If you do not send at least an intro RP, you will not get a match. Its not very hard, just basically tell who you are, what you came to accomplish here inthe NAWF, and what your past was...etc. You know the deal.
8. Thats basically it, anything else is commom sense. So have fun out there, and good luck to you all.
