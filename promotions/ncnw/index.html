Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

New Championship Network Wrestling billed itself, in its own words, as a “network” from the moment it opened: “The Birth Of NCNW is at HAND!!!!” announced its own launch broadcast on 1 December 1997. Its own FAQ named four titles in order of prestige — a World Heavyweight Title open only to the top eight wrestlers, World Tag Team Titles open to the top five teams, a North American Title, and a Network Title defended weekly, alternating between its two cards — plus a quarterly tournament it called the WEC.

Its two weekly shows were Wednesday's Fight Night, run out of Madison Square Garden, and the bigger Saturday Turbulence. The surviving Saturday Turbulence broadcast, in two parts, opened its own Title Tournament: E. Bola Loco defeated the Yukon Kid by small package after a brawl at ringside, and Dare Devil outlasted the underworld figure Hades, reversing a signature hold called the Armageddon into his own finishing Devils Death Drop to advance. A third bout pit “The Original Playa” Sean Donnovan against Devon Stevens, ending by disqualification when a run-in from Bryan Johnson broke up Stevens's pin, until the stable Bloodlust ran in and cleared the ring.

NCNW's own record breaks off there, still recruiting the six to eight match writers its Saturday Turbulence broadcast asked for by name. Whether the Title Tournament's own later rounds, or the quarterly WEC it advertised, ever ran isn't in the surviving record.
