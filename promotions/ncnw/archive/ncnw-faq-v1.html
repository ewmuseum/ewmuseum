NCNW — Rules & Structure

NCNW FAQ, Version 1.0

17 November 1997

NCNW Faq version 1.0 Q: What does NCNW stand for? A: NCNW Stands for New Championship Network Wrestling. There was no "Old" Championship Network Wrestling, but to be original I added the "New" to the name. Q: Who Runs NCNW? A: I am the owner and operator of NCNW, my name is [name withheld]. I also run the quarterly wrestling tournament known as WEC, or World E-Wrestling Championships. I am also looking for a VP, Booker and 6-8 Match writers as of now. Q: What cards are there in NCNW? A: The NCNW will have two cards a week held on Wednesday and Saturday. Wednesday will be known as Fight Night here in NCNW. NCNW Fight Night will be held in Madison Square Garden and will feature 4-5 Live matches and 5-6 Summaries. Saturdays will be our "bigger" show called NCNW Saturday Turbulance. We will also hold a Pay Per View Supercard 4 Times a Year in February, May, August and November. We will also hold smaller "Supercards" inbetween months, but they won't be PPV. Q: What Titles Are there in NCNW? A: There are 4 Titles to start out with in NCNW. Here is a rundown of them, in order of prestige. 1. NCNW World Heavyweight Title Top Title in NCNW. To Challenge for this title, you must be ranked in the top 8. It MUST be defended once a month, probably more, and at every Supercard. 2. NCNW World Tag Team Titles Same as #1, but you have to be in the top 5 tag teams. 3. NCNW North American Title Secondary Singles Title. To challenge for this title, you must be ranked in the top 13 Wrestlers. Must be defended every other week and at every Supercard. 4. NCNW Network Title Hardest title to hold on to in NCNW. You can be any rank, singles or tag or whatever to challenge for. It must be defended every week at Turbulence and Fight Night alternating, that is, one week it will be defended at Fight Night, the next it must be defended at Turbulence. Q: How many wrestlers do I get in NCNW? A: One singles, or one tag team. Q: How are winners determined in NCNW? A: NCNW is a booked fed. Winners are determined by Roleplay, Strat and Angle quality. Meaning in order to win, and stay in NCNW, you must ROLEPLAY at least once a week, you will be given one warning, then you're gone. If you are sick, or away, simply tell me and you will be excused.
