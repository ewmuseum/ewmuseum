NCNW Saturday Turbulence, Part 2

1 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Hades sets Dare Devil in a sitting position and gets behind him. He grabs Dare Devil and locks in the Armageddon. The Ref Checks the Arm) One, Two, Thr- NO! (His arm says up. Dare Devil gets to his feet while still in the hold. Dare Devil elbows Hades in the stomach twice and he lets go. Dare Devil bounces off the far ropes and dropkicks Hades. He picks Hades up, and puts him between his legs. He lifts him up, and Drops him into the Devils Death Drop!) [Deulist] It's over now! [Davis] NEVER! One, Two, THREE!!!!!!!! DING DING DING [Nyle] Here is your winner and advancing the Network Round of the Title Tournament, DDDDDDDDDAAAAAAAARRRRRRREEEEEEE DDDDDEEEEEEVVVVVIIIILLLLL ("Ready or Not" by the Fugees starts up again, Dare Devil walks back to the dressing room shaking hands as he goes. The ref wakes Hades up and escorts him to the dressing room.) -------------------- "The Original Playa" Sean Donnovan vs Devon Stevens -------------------- [Nyle] Our next match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Harlem, New York, Here is "The Original Playa" Sean Donnovan!!!! ("Been Around The World" By Puff Daddy and the Family starts as Blue Lasers make an X over the entrance. Donnovan runs through the lasers full speed shaking hands all the way as the fans explode. He is wearing White Full Legged Tights with Black Knee Pads and Black Speedo tights over them. He is alos wearing a white jacket that says Playa on the back. He takes off the jacket.) [Nyle] And his opponent, from San Jose, California, here is the self proclaimed greatest wrestler on the face of the earth, DDDDEEEVVVOOONN SSSSTTTEEEEVVVVEEENNNNSSSS!!! ("Year of the Boomerang" By Rage Against the machine starts. The lights go off and a spotlight shines at the entrance. Stevens walks out and the spotlight follows him all the way to the ring. He gets in and the lights come back on.) [Duelist] Whats with all the songs by Rage Against the Machine? [Davis] Beats me. They lock up. Knee lift by Donnovan. He whips Stevens into the ropes, clothesline. [Duelist] Legdrop by Donnovan, cover! One, Kickout. [Davis] You can't beat the greatest wrestler alive like that! What was he thinking? [Duelist] That he can win. He goes to Stevens legs, He crosses them, Texas Cloverleaf. [Davis] Don't give up Stevens, show him who's the man. [Duelist] He won't give up and Donnovan lets go. He lifts Stevens to his feet. Standing dropkick. He bounces off the far ropes... [Davis] Elbow drop. [Duelist] He's stomping a hole in Stevens. He picks him up. Clothesline. [Davis] Donnovan goes to the top ropes. [Duelist] He's taking too long. He goes for a splash... [Davis] NO!!! Donnovan _Did_ take too long. And now he is paying the price. Get up Stevens! [Duelist] Stevens is the first to his feet. He waits for Donnovan. Right hand by Stevens. He bounces off the ropes, FOREARM, Cover. One, Two, KICKOUT. [Davis] Come on ref, it didn't take me that long to count to my grandmothers age of 102! [Duelist] The ref counts fairly for all. Stevens lifts Donnovan to his feet. DDT!! That's the sign-up move for the Stevens Death Drop! [Davis] If it hits, this one is over. [Duelist] He gets him in the firemans carry. He climbs to the top ropes. [Davis] WHOS THIS? [Duelist] Its Bryan Johnson! He is distracting the ref. BUT Stevens hits the Stevens Death Drop! This one is over! [Davis] Stevens goes to get the ref so he can cover. DONNOVAN GETS TO HIS FEET, Oh my God. [Duelist] He grabs Stevens from behind. SHORT CLOTHESLINE. That's the signup for the Apollo DDT. [Davis] He puts Stevens in Harlem World. He's taking him to the Apollo, I can't watch Randy. [Duelist] THE APOLLO DDT. Cover! One, Two, THR-- NO BRYAN JOHNSON BREAKS THE PIN! DING DING DING. (Bryan Johnson and Devon Stevens Stomp Sean Donnovan.) DING DING DING (Fans Scream) [Duelist] Here comes somebody, WHO Is it? (Bloodlust runs to the ring) [Davis] It's those dumb graveyard shifters or something like that. [Duelist] Thats Bloodlust, and they are clearing the ring. Myst chokeslams Johnson over the top rope!! Oh no, Stevens is Left! Phantom grabs Stevens by the Throat, he lifts him up, MYST WITH A DROPKICK FROM THE TOP ROPE to Stevens Face! THE OPEN WOUND. ("Been Around The World" By Puff Daddy starts back up.) [Nyle] Here is your winner, as a result of a Disqualification, "The Original Playa" Sean Donnovan. (Bloodlust and Donnovan walk back to the dressing room.) (Fade) My Wrestlers Loved it, But Id Like Some Response From You. Im Looking For Writers, When I get them, cards will be even better
