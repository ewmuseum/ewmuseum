Introduction A. History [name withheld], lifetime wrestling fan. Wrestled amateur in high school and college, and won National Championships in the 189 pound division. He wanted more than just a desk job, so he decided to pursue his dream and become a pro wrestler. In a great career, he injured his knee, and rather than risk permanent injury, he decided it was best to retire. But he wanted to stay within the buisness. With all the winnings he got over his career, he came to the conclision to open his own organization. New Championship Network Wrestling was born. It opened in March of 1996, and had a strong run in the buisness. But in this day and age, everything is run by computers. After two years at the top, a virus in NCNW headquarters caused a complete shutdown. All records and everything was lost. NCNW entered a dark age, nothing was known, nothing. The buisness went under, and [name withheld] went bankrupt. After it's sophmore year,
NCNW was dead.
[name withheld], the biggest fan of NCNW. He was really angry when NCNW folded. He had wanted to have some part of his favorite organization, but never got the chance. One night, half asleep, clutching a lottery ticket in his hand, the numbers were drawn. He had won. All with dates in NCNW histroy. 3, 5, 11, 22,
26 and 30. 3/26, first card ever held in NCNW. 5/22, The date the first ever NCNW champion was crowned, and 11/30, the day NCNW folded. He was the only winner, and had won 45 Million dollars, after taxes. Rather than buy a new home, or new car, he wanted to do something better, and make his dream come true. He wanted to give [name withheld] the money to re-open NCNW, and better than ever. On one condition though, that he would become Co-President of NCNW. Under the new Co-Presidents, NCNW should be better than ever.

Roleplaying - New Championship Network Wrestling is a federation run by the HJS simulator. HJS stands for Human Judgement System. Therefore, I will be deciding the winners of the matches. You should all understand that we are all humans and make mistakes, so if I make a decision you don't like, E-Mail me and let me know how ya feel. I will re-evaluate my decision, and it could possibly lead to something more of your liking. But don't worry, we'll all get a turn in the lime light. Effort is another big part of roleplays. We can't all pump out 100 line roleplays. But that doesn't mean you don't put as much effort in. If you consistently do a good job on roleplaying, you'll get your push.
B. Strats - Short for strategy. Its what you want to have happen during the match. Say you're a little guy wrestling a bigger guy, you'll want to take out the legs, etc. This is also where you'd put whether you'd like someone to interfear, etc.
C. Roleplaying Rules - Just don't attack another wrestler in a roleplay. No racial slurs or anything of that like either. If anything comes up in a roleplay, I might have to make a decision on it later. You are required to roleplay once a week.
D. How it breaks down: 60% Roleplaying 15% Strats 25% Angles III. Cards and PPVs A. NCNW Presents "Friday Turbulence" Held on Friday Nights from 8-10 EST, on the ESPN network.
