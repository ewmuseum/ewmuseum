NCNW Saturday Turbulence, Part 1

1 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
....We Could Not Wait Forever.... .... So, we Decided now Was The Time... The Birth Of NCNW is at HAND!!!! S A T U R D A Y T U R B L E N C E {Fireworks explode inside Market Square Arena. The camera pans down to Randy Duelist and Eddie Davis. They are both wearing dark blue suits with NCNW Embroidered on the pocket in Gold. The fans are Screaming.} [Randy Duelist] Hello everyone, my name is Randy Deulist. I along with Eddie Davis will be bringing you all the action for tonights card. Without any further adieu, lets get to Walter Nyle for the intros of the first match. ------------- E. Bola Loco w/ Kid Nikki vs The Yukon Kid ------------- [Walter Nyle] This match is scheduled for one fall. And it is a Preliminary Round match in the NCNW Title Tournament. Introducing first, he hails from The Yukon Territory, and weighs in at 224, here is TTTTTHHHHHHHEEEEEE YYYYYYUUUUUKKKKKKOOOONNNNNNN KKKKKKKKIIIIIIDDDDDD!!!!! ("Kill in the name of" By Rage Against the Machine starts up. Yukon Kid comes walking down to ringside. The fans boo solidly. He is a white male with chin length brown hair. He is wearing black tights with a yellow stripe. He gets in the ring and waits on his oppponent.) [Nyle] And his opponent, coming from Jaumave, Mexico, weighing in at 230. Here is E. BOLA LOCOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! ("The People of the Sun" by Rage Against the Machine Starts up. Kid Nikki walks out, and he is followed by E. Bola Loco. He is a mexican man with a goatee. He is pretty mucualar, he is wearing a DFL t-shirt with Red Tights, White Boots and Bandages around his biceps. He steps on the apron, climbs through the middle rope and gets immediatly attacked by the Yukon Kid.) [Davis] COME ON!!! Go Yukon, pound that foreigner into the ground, I'm lovin it Randy!! [Duelist] Just call the damn match. Loco is down to one knee, Yukon Kid bounces off the far ropes... [Davis] Flying Dropkick! He cought Loco right in the jaw, and there he goes, Loco went through the ropes and he is on the floor. [Duelist] And Yukon follows him to the floor... Oh no, this could be brutal. He just slammed E. Bola Loco's head into the steel guard rail!! and I love it! [Davis] Loco is down and hurt, and I love it! [Duelist] You hate anyone the fans do. [Davis] So??? And your point? [Duelist] Forget it. Focus now Mike. [Davis] Whatever, shutup. Yukon throws E. Bola Loco into the ring. Slingshot Somersault Legdrop, cover. One, Two, KICKOUT! [Duelist] Yukon brings Loco to his feet. He puts Loco between his legs. [Davis] Maybe a powerbomb? Wait, Loco drops down. Yukon puts him back on his feet... AND LOCO ROLLS HIM UP IN A SMALL PACKAGE!!! One Two THREE!!!! DING DING DING [Nyle] Here is your winner, and advancing to the Network Round of the Title Tournament, E. BOLA LOCO!!! (People of the Sun starts up as Loco leaves the ring and Yukon sits wondering what happened.) [Davis] WHAT JUST HAPPENED HERE?Our next match is another first round World Title Tournament match, it features Dare Devil and Hades. Lets get down to Walter Nyle for the introductions. ---------- Hades vs Dare Devil ---------- [Nyle] This match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first from Parts Unknown, here is, the God of the Land of the Dead, here is HHHHHHHHAAAAAAADDDDDDDDEEEEEESSSSS!!!! (The sound of burning comes over the loudspeakers. The fans immediately reply with loud Boos. Hades apears at the curtain. He is wearing all black cloths. His face is painted red and he has no hair. He walks slowly to the ring. He climbs the stairs, walks along the apron, and steps over the top rope.) [Nyle]: And his opponent, from Devil's Island, here is DDDDDAAAAARRRRREEEEE DDDDDEEEEEVVVVVIIIILLLLL!!! ("Ready or Not" by The Fugees replaces the burning bodies sound. Dare Devil comes flying down the ramp shaking every hand possible while the fans are greeting him with plenty of Applause. He flies up the steps and slingshots over the top rope. Hades clears the ring until "Ready or Not" dies down.) [Davis] The fans are welcoming this NOBODY? And booing Hades? What is wrong with these Idiots? Hades is a master wrestler... [Deulist] Oh shut up. The NCNW fans can boo and cheer for whoever they want, and obviously they like this guy Dare Devil, personally I hope he kicks Hades butt. (They lock up. Hades pushes Dare Devil back into the ropes. Irish whip, Hades attempts a clothesline, but Dare Devil ducks under. Dare Devil comes off the far ropes and hits Hades with a clothesline. Hades is still on his feet! Dare Devil bounces off the ropes and hits Hades with a shoulder tackle. Hades does NOT go down.) [Deulist] What will it take for Dare Devil to knock Hades off his feet. [Davis] Everything he's got, plus the kitchen sink. Hades is just too big for this little nobody to handle. (Dare Devil bounces off the ropes one more time and hits Hades with a Scissor kick. This time Hades goes down. Dare Devil climbs up to the top ropes and waits for Hades to get up. Corkscrew Plancha. Dare Devil Covers.) One, Two, Thre, NO! [Deulist] A Near pinfall by Dare Devil. He's really taking it to Hades. [Davis] Just you wait and see! Hades will prevail. (Dare Devil picks up Hades by the hair, and whips him into the corner, he follows it up with a clothesline, NO! Hades lifts his leg. Hades hits him with a Belly to Back Suplex.) [Deulist] Oh no, this is bad, that is the sign up move for Hades signature manuver the Armageddon! [Davis] See, I told ya he will win. Continued In Part 2
