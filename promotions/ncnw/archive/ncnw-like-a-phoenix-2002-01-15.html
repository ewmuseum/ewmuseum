NCNW: Like a Phoenix Rising from the Ashes

15 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello RSPWF... Some of you may remember me, some may not. My name is [name withheld] and I haven't been here in a LONG time. I used to run a fed called NCNW (New Championship Network Wrestling. We had a long run as a top fed on the net. Now I'm bringing it back. I've had a long absence from e-wrestling as I've been working on my pro-wrestling career. Now I have time to balance everything. I'm not gonna name-drop of wrestlers we had like some people do, but we've had our share of big stars and big name staff members. So without anything else here is the player's guide. Introduction A. History [name withheld], lifetime wrestling fan. Wrestled amateur in high school and college, and won National Championships in the 189 pound division. He wanted more than just a desk job, so he decided to pursue his dream and become a pro wrestler. In a great career, he injured his knee, and rather than risk permanent injury, he decided it was best to retire. But he wanted to stay within the buisness. With all the winnings he got over his career, he came to the conclision to open his own organization. New Championship Network Wrestling was born. It opened in March of 1996, and had a strong run in the buisness. But in this day and age, everything is run by computers. After two years at the top, a virus in NCNW headquarters caused a complete shutdown. All records and everything was lost. NCNW entered a dark age, nothing was known, nothing. The buisness went under, and [name withheld] went bankrupt. After it's sophmore year, NCNW was dead. Karl Garrett, the biggest fan of NCNW. He was really angry when NCNW folded. He had wanted to have some part of his favorite organization, but never got the chance. One night, half asleep, clutching a lottery ticket in his hand, the numbers were drawn. He had won. All with dates in NCNW histroy. 3, 5, 11, 22, 26 and 30. 3/26, first card ever held in NCNW. 5/22, The date the first ever NCNW champion was crowned, and 11/30, the day NCNW folded. He was the only winner, and had won 45 Million dollars, after taxes. Rather than buy a new home, or new car, he wanted to do something better, and make his dream come true. He wanted to give [name withheld] the money to re-open NCNW, and better than ever. On one condition though, that he would become Co-President of NCNW. Under the new Co-Presidents, NCNW should be better than ever. B. Characters (On Screen Staff) [name withheld] - Original owner and operator of NCNW. He went bankrupt when NCNW folded. Mark Bivins' tag partner as well. Head of NCNW security. C. Real Staff The real staff is the behind the scenes action that makes sure Turbulence comes out on time every week. PPV Writer - OPEN! PPV Writer - OPEN! PPV Writer - OPEN! II. Rules A. Roleplaying - New Championship Network Wrestling is a federation run by the HJS simulator. HJS stands for Human Judgement System. Therefore, I (and/or any staff I hire) will be deciding the winners of the matches. You should all understand that we are all humans and make mistakes, so if I make a decision you don't like, E-Mail me let me know about it. I will re-evaluate my decision, and it could possibly lead to something more of your liking. But don't worry, we'll all get a turn in the lime light. Effort is another big part of roleplays. We can't all pump out 100 line roleplays. But that doesn't mean you don't put as much effort in. If you consistently do a good job on roleplaying, you'll get your push. B. Strats - Short for strategy. Its what you want to have happen during the match. Such as a run in, an attack focused on an area of the body, a certain finish you'd like to see, etc. Not all wrestlers are talkers. If you handle a wrestler who is more toward letting his actions do the talking, send in detailed strats and that would take the place of your RPs. An example of a real life wrestler who would fit this mold is Chris Benoit or Dean Malenko. C. Roleplaying Rules - Just don't attack another wrestler in a roleplay. No racial slurs or anything of that like either. If anything comes up in a roleplay, I might have to make a decision on it later. You are required to roleplay once a week. D. How it breaks down: 40% Roleplaying 40% Effort (This includes strategies) 20% Angles III. Cards and PPVs A. NCNW Presents "Friday Turbulence" Held on Friday Nights from 8-10 EST, on the ESPN network. Will hold between 6-10 matches, written out in Play by Play format. More often than not, the NCNW Network Title will be on the line. B. PPVs Held every other month. Usually, all titles will be on the line. PPVs already named are: January - Nuclear Winter (Year) Straight PPV March - March Madness (Year) Includes the March Madness Tournament. The winner of the tournament faces the Champion at WrestleCade May - WrestleCade (Number. This time around it will be WrestleCade III) The SuperShow of the year. Main event is the champion vs the winner of the March Madness tournament. July - Brain Damage Straight PPV September - Public Outrage (Year) Straight PPV (Usually holds more Gimmick matches) November - Trial By Fire (Number. This year will be TbF II) IV. Title Overview A. NCNW World Heavyweight Title - The big gun holds this one. Defended usually once a month, and at every PPV. B. NCNW North American Title - Secondary title. Great wrestlers have held this strap. Defended twice a month and at every PPV. Holders of this title are very likely to hold the NCNW World Heavyweight Title. C. NCNW World Tag Team Titles - Beast duo of NCNW holds these. Defended once a month, and at every PPV. D. NCNW Network Title - Work horses hold this one. Defended just about every week at Turbulence, and at every PPV. V.
