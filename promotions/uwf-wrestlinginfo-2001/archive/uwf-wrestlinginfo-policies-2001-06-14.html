UWF: Our Policies, June 2001

14 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Our Policies The United Wrestling Federation is part of a network of wrestling site joined together to make one huge megasite called WrestlingInfo.com. This fed is based on angles and roleplays. EZBoards is our method of roleplaying, which is linked to the site. This fed accepts all decent original wrestlers, male or female. We are still accepting at this point. We accept no more than thirty wrestlers at a time. There is a waiting list to be used in the event that the roster becomes too full to accept any more wrestlers. It is the applicants choice as to whether or not they wished to be placed on the the list.   History The United Wrestling Federation is intended as a carry over from the late World Wrestling Alliance (WWA) run by our CEO, [name withheld]. It was officially reopened in April 2001.   Broadcasts The UWF has three shows, only two of which take place on a weekly basis. Tuesday Riot is the primary weekly show while Friday Free 4 All is the secondary show. However, Tuesday Riot has not yet took place and will not take place until more wrestlers and staff members join the UWF. There is also one monthly pay per view that takes place each month. However, there will not be a pay per view until the fed is on a decent roll. There is a waiting list in the event that the roster is too full to accept any more wrestlers. Neither of these shows have taken place yet, but the clock is quickly counting down.   Titles There are six titles in the UWF. There is one set of Tag Team Titles and four singles titles. The order of the singles titles from most important to least important is as follows: World Title, Intercontinental Title, Hardcore Title, Internet Title & Tag-Team Titles.   Matches Many types of matches take place in the UWF, but there are two that are strictly used by the UWF. They are the Wrapped Steel Chain and the Japanese Death Match UWF Extreme. The Wrapped Steel Chain Match is basically a match where both of the opponent's hands are wrapped in steel chains and they battle it out until there is a winner. This match was created by UWF wrestler, AJ Springer when he and our CEO battled in the International Wrestling Federation over the IWF Internet Championship. Our CEO has decided to make this a gimmick match that will follow him wherever he goes. In order to win this match, a competitor must subdue his opponent long enough to get the pinfall, or battle it out until the referee deems that the opponent is injured intirely too much to continue, in which case the win would be from the decision of the referee. The Japanese Death UWF Extreme, on the other hand, is totally different. This match replaces the ring ropes with razor sharp barbed wire, the turnbuckles and outside ring mats with boards and minor explosives, and the whole thing is surrounded by an energized cage. In order to win this match you must throw your opponent into all sides of the energized cage, before you are allowed a pinfall. You are allowed to be thrown into the turnbuckles a total of two times without them exploding. The third time someone is thrown into them, they explode, causing major pain to the person subjected to the blow. The only way to win this match is by pinfall, which is only given after the opponent has been thrown into all sides of the energized cage, as said before. This match was created by The Maniac, a former IWF wrestler, also in a fued with CEO [name withheld] for the IWF Internet Championship. This match is used as the only alternative to solving a long time ongoing fued, and is, for the most part, avoided. What We Need We really need tag-teams & more women wrestlers to beef up our women's division.   URL http://www.geocities.com/wi_efed/     Thank You.... CEO [name withheld]
