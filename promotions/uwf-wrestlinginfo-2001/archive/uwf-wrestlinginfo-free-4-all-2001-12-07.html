UWF Free-4-All, 7 December 2001

7 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Start Transmission =================== Fade in.... Into a wrestling ring.... An empty ring.... until 3 letters are imprinted onto the canvas. The camera pans until it is directly overhead.... U W F. Fade To Black... The Start Of A New Era ======================= And then, a jam-packed wrestling arena is shown. Pyrotechnics go off, and the fans go wild, as "My Way" plays in the background. The focus shifts to the ring, where CEO Chris Scarbrough is standing in the middle of it, with a microphone in hand. As the crowd's cheers dies down, Scarbrough puts the mic to his mouth... "Welcome, everybody... to the UWF; this time, nothing is gonna stop us. We have had problems in the past... but no more; this is make or break. This is the start... of a new era. Let the games begin.", he says confidently. And the crowd erupts... as they know the UWF can provide top-notch entertainment... and they have faith that this incarnation will be the best yet. The scene then shifts backstage... <u>I Forgot To Do What?</u> "What?", a voice says. As the scene shifts backstage, Vice-President Kamlesh can be seen standing outside to his office, talking to road agent Hozario Suarez. "I'm telling you; the venue for tonight's show was not included in the schedule you handed out a week ago.", Hozario explains, visibly flustered. "And you're telling me this just now?", Kamlesh says, seemingly incredulous. "Y-Yes.", Hozario stammers. Silence. "Cool.", Kamlesh replies. The look of confusion is evident on Hozario's face, and Kamlesh sighs. "I took the guys to Chinatown last night; included clues of where we are now in their fortune cookies. I mean, clues where the answer is Albany, NY are quite limited; and I believe everybody deciphered them.", Kamlesh explains. Hozario nods in approval, as he finally understands, and excuses himself while Kamlesh fishes out his mobile-phone from his pants pocket. F4A then cuts to commercials. AJ Springer -VS- Edge Crusher -VS- Striker ============================================== The commercials come to an end, just as AJ Springer walks down the ramp to the tune of "Dead Cell", receiving huge cheers from the crowd, who remember him from the IW days. The second AJ rolls into the ring, an unfamiliar song is blasted over the PA... as Striker runs down to the ring, and quickly starts brawling AJ. The referee rings the bell, without realising Edge Crusher isn't out yet. Striker manages to get the better of AJ, but as the former extends his arm for a lariat, AJ ducks it in the nick of time and answering with a flying heel kick to Striker! Both men go down, but get up quickly, as AJ elbows Striker in the face and scores with a snap suplex. Edge Crusher then runs down the ramp, but stops as he gets onto the apron. He smiles and ascends to the top rope... waiting. Meanwhile, Striker lays prone on the mat and AJ looks to take advantage of that, as he jumps onto the other turnbuckle and jumps off it in a variation asai moonsault. Striker rolls out of the way, and AJ crashes into the canvas. Striker is knocked back down again, for as he stands up, Edge Crusher leaps off the top rope and connects with a tremendous flying clothesline!! EC then gets up and uses to feet to roll Striker out of the ring, before he pulls AJ up and.... snap piledriver!! Edge Crusher quickly makes the cover, as the referee counts... One..... Two.... but Striker recovers and pulls EC off AJ... pulling Edge Crusher out of the ring in the process. As EC turns to face Striker, the latter scores with a hard right-hook, before taking EC out of the equation for a while by whipping him into the steel-steps!! Striker rolls back into the ring, and pulls AJ up. He connects with a hard forearm smash, before he whips AJ into the ropes. AJ manages to hold on to the ropes as he bounces into them, though.... forcing Striker to charge at him. Springer kicks Striker in the gut before the latter can do anything and lifts him into a powerbomb position.... before drilling Striker into the canvas with tremendous impact!! As Springer makes the cover, Edge Crusher climbs to the top-rope... One..... Two..... The-BUT A FLYING LEGDROP FROM EDGE CRUSHER BREAKS UP THE COUNT!! All 3 men are down and out... but EC manages to get his back over Springer's chest, as the referee makes the count.... One..... Two.... Thr-Springer gets the left shoulder up!! Crusher was furious at himself, for not putting enough weight on Springer. He pulls AJ up and tosses him out of the ring, before he walks towards Striker. As EC grabs Striker by the neck, the latter responds with a low-blow!! Crusher hops around in pain, and Striker takes full advantage of that, as he gets up, whips himself against the ropes and executes a picture-perfect bulldog. With EC out cold... Striker makes his way to the top of the turnbuckle and signals for a frog-splash. The crowd gets hyped up... and erupt when AJ pops up and clubs Striker, causing the latter to be stradled!! AJ then gets onto the turnbuckle with Striker, hooks him and..... SUPERPLEX ONTO EDGE CRUSHER!!!! All 3 men are out of it once again, and the crowd go ballistic at what just happened. Springer starts to twitch a little... and manages to cover Striker... One..... Two.... Thre....... HOW DOES STRIKER KICK OUT?!? Springer cannot believe that Striker kicks out, and gets up, taunting Striker to come get him. As Striker gets to his feet, AJ charges at him.... and that proves to be a mistake, as Striker counters into a back-body drop. Striker turns and wobbles towards AJ, in an attempt to follow up his offense... but Edge Crusher manages to recover and repays Striker for the low-blow he executed moments ago with a low-blow of his own! Striker goes down, and EC seizes the opportunity to roll Striker up.... One.... Two... Striker rolls out of it!! Striker & EC quickly get to their feet, with Striker immediately going for a clothesline. EC ducks, and counters with a front russian leg-sweep!! With both his adversaries knocked out, Edge Crusher climbs to the top turnbuckle, and quickly leaps off with a frog-splash of his own...... but Springer gets up and executes a devastating bicycle kick, catching EC square in the face!!!!! Springer then rolls Edge Crusher over, and hooks the legs.... One............. Two........ Three!!! AJ Springer wins the war in dramatic fashion!! As AJ leaves the ring celebrating, Edge Crusher & Striker recover and start shoving each other. They suddenly stop.... before they slide out of the ring, and give chase to AJ. Springer sees this and turns around.... only to get floored by both men!! The crowd boo wildly, as Striker stomps Springer a bit, before F4A cuts to commercials. SeVeN -VS- Derek Cage ===================== SeVeN is seen sauntering down the ramp, as F4A returns from commercials. As he pulls himself into the ring, "Left Behind" by Slipknot stops playing... and the lights go out. The crowd cheer, expecting something spectacular to happen next. When the lights come on, they jeer.. for Derek Cage is in the ring, standing behind SeVeN. As SeVeN turns around, Cage goes for a clothesline. SeVeN ducks and answers with a BIG right hand as the referee starts the match. SeVeN builds on his offense with more vicious rights and lefts, battering Cage back into the far corner. A measured right haymaker sends Derek sprawling into the corner, before SeVeN scores with a series of swift kicks to Cage's gut, sending the latter down. SeVeN doesn't let up, and keeps stomping. The ref finally intercedes, peeling SeVeN off Cage. SeVeN only glares at the ref, who shrinks away in fear, before he turns his attention back to Cage..... who connects with a low blow, before flipping him through the ropes to the floor. As SeVeN struggles to stand up on the outside, Cage scales to the top rope. SeVeN staggers around a bit and when he looks up, Cageleaps off with a flying double axehandle smash! SeVeN takes it right in the top of the head and goes down in a heap. Derek recovers & stands over his opponent, raising his arms in triumph, only to get booed heavily. Laughing wildly, Cage pulls SeVeN up and throws him headfirst into the ring apron, before shoving him into the ring. Cage takes his own sweet time to get into the ring, and as he does, he wrangles SeVeN to his feet and whips him into the corner. Derek continues his assault as he walks over to SeVeN and unloads with a reverse knife-edge chop. He follows with an overhand chop, before he knees SeVeN in the gut and scores with a DDT! Cage hooks the legs, as the referee counts... One..... Two.... and SeVeN gets his left shoulder up!! Agitated, Derek pulls SeVeN up and sends him into the ropes. As SeVeN returns, Cage charges at him, attempting a clothesline. SeVeN ducks and as Cage turns around, SeVeN connects with a devastating thrust kick to Cage's chest!! As Derek falls to the mat, SeVeN quickly jumps on top of him, as the referee counts... One.... Two.... Th-NO! Derek JUST manages to kick out!! The crowd groans, thinking that SeVeN had Cage beat. SeVeN curses loudly, pulls Cage up, and hooks him in a suplex. He hoists Cage into the air for a brainbuster, but the latter wriggles free and drops behind SeVeN. He then locks SeVeN in a cross-face chicken-wing, before wrapping his legs around SeVeN's torso in an unique bodyscissors. SeVeN thrashes about wildly, but manages to get his foot on the bottom rope. The referee demands Cage release the hold, but Derek locks it in even tighter. The referee counts to 4, before Cage releases the hold, much to his chagrin. He confronts the referee, and starts arguing with him... berating him about being "unfair". This gives SeVeN time to catch his breath and sneak up behind Cage..... SCHOOLBOY PIN!!! The referee quickly hits the deck, as SeVeN has Cage's shoulders pinned to the mat.... One.... Two.... Thre-SO CLOSE!! Cage somehow gets out of the predicament and wisely rolls out of the ring, drawing huge heat from the crowd. SeVeN slaps his hand against the mat in frustation, before joining Cage on the outside. The wily Derek apparently planned it that way, though, as he produces a kendo stick from under the ring and rammed it into SeVeN's gut! The referee slides out of the ring and starts to admonish Cage for the use of the kendo stick.... but Cage simply cracks the kendo stick over the referee's head!! The members of the crowd are stunned, and Cage laughs at the fallen referee.... before SeVeN recovers and low-blows Cage!! The two men, moments later, get to their feet and start slugging each other all the way up the ramp, as physicans run down to the ring and attend to the referee. Cage & SeVeN continue brawling all the way to the back, and the replacement referee decides to call the match off... much to the dismay of the capacity crowd. New Guys Beckon... And Old Friends Meet ======================================== The scene shifts to CEO Scarbrough's office, who looks peeved at the no-contest. A knock on his door is heard, and Scar simply grunts. The door swings open and in walks VP Kamlesh. "Hey Scar, we have two new guys ready to jump on the UWF bandwagon.", Kamlesh says cheerily, while looking quite tired. "Uh huh.... tell you what; have the two new guys fight Cage & SeVeN next week.", Scar says, his eyes clued on the replay of the ending of the match. "Okay, whatever. I was thinking of putting the both of them together... but having them face off in a tag-team match sounds good.", Kamlesh replies, before he leaves the office. Scarbrough grunts, and starts to rummage through some documents, as the scene switches to the locker-room, where AJ Springer is being taped up. Commissioner [name withheld] then walks in, and stares at Springer. Springer stares back at the Commish... before the two shake hands. "Long time no see, AJ.", Commissioner [name withheld] remarks. "Yeah... been a long time since the IW closed shop. You doing good?", AJ asks. "Yeah, fine as can be. Congrats on your victory... make sure you get taped up well so that you can kick more ass, okay?", Commissioner Justin says, obviously happy to see AJ again. AJ nods, and the two men give high-fives to each other, as F4A cuts to commercials. Bret Stevenston -VS- Derek Craven ==================================== As F4A comes back from commercials, Bret is seen in the ring, stretching his muscles. "Bad Influence" by Eminem is piped through the PA and the crowd boo a little, as Derek Craven comes out of the back and swaggers down the ramp. Bret doesn't look too impressed as Craven jumps onto the apron and mounts the turnbuckle, taunting the crowd. Having enough of this revelry, Bret came in from behind and pulled XXXstasy off off the turnbuckle. The bell rang, as Bret quickly spun Craven around and drove his knee into Craven's back. Derek stumbled into the ropes clutching his gut, and walked right into a Northern Lights suplex!! The ref hit the deck to count the bridge-cover.... One.... Two.... Craven kicks out with authority! The two get back to their feet, and Craven went for the quick clothesline. Bret ducks, grabs his opponent from again and hits a German suplex, and before getting up and hitting another in sequential order. Craven was in la-la land, as Bret rolled him over and hooked the legs... One... Two..... and Craven kicks out again!! As the two men get up again, Bret goes for a crescent kick.. but Craven ducks. As Bret comes the full round, Craven kicks him in the gut and whips him into the ropes with an irish-whip. Bret managed to reverse the irish-whip, but Derek bounces off the ropes with a flying forearm! Bret stumbles backwards and falls through the ropes and onto the ground. Bret quickly gets to his feet, but doesn't get much repreive as Craven dives through the ropes and connects with a forearm smash into Bret's head. The two men both lay on the outside of the ring, breathing hard. Craven is the first one to his feet, and grabs Bret's head, before ramming his head into the steel-steps, drawing heat from the crowd. Craven doesn't bother, though, as he rolls Bret into the ring. Derek then climbs to the top turnbuckle, pumps a fist to the crowd, and connects with a dropkick on Bret, who had pulled himself up with the assistance of the ropes. With Bret knocked out, Derek drapes his arm over Bret's shoulder, as the ref makes the count.... One..... Two..... Th-NO!! Bret manages to get his left shoulder up, pissing Craven off. Craven pulls Bret up, and hooks him in the position for a double underhook, before executing a tight Butterfly suplex. Both lay still for a moment on the mat, the exhaustion catching up with each of them. Craven manages to get his arm over Bret's body, as the referee hits the deck... One.... Two.... Thr-BRET GETS HIS RIGHT FOOT ON THE ROPES!! Craven cannot believe it, and goes to argue with the referee. The referee tries to explain to Craven, but Derek refuses to listen and pushes the ref. The ref actually pushes Derek back... and into the path of a roll-up from Bret!!! The referee quickly hits the deck.... One.... Two.... Thre-BUT DEREK MANAGES TO ROLL OUT OF IT!! Both men get back to their feet, as Bret attempts a charging clothesline. Craven manages to duck it and... executes a Double Chicken-Wing Driver; THE HELL RAISER!! Craven rolls Bret over and hooks the legs, as the referee starts counting.... One..... Two.... Three!!! Craven picks up the victory!! Planning Ahead =================== Vice-President Kamlesh & Commissioner [name withheld] are in Kamlesh's office, drinking coffee, watching the finish of the previous contest. "That was short.", Justin remarks. "Yeah. By the way, I think it's time we plan next week's show. Scar has already booked one match... you have anything in particular?", Kamlesh asks. "Hmm... why not the winner of this next match face Derek Craven in one of two qualifiers for the North-American Title? Bret can then face the loser of the next match in the other qualifier.", Justin suggests. "Sounds dandy to me... oh yeah, I'm thinking of scraping the European Title. Instead, we make it the Submission Title. You can only win by submission.... what do you say?", Kamlesh probes, his face lighting up. "I say my coffee needs more sugar.... and yes to your idea. Sounds groovy as hell.", Commissioner [name withheld] replies, looking frustrated that his coffee has no sugar. Kamlesh smiles at Justin's answer.... and he smiles devilishly. Wombat Guerrera -VS- Tainted Soul ==================================== As the scene switches back to the arena, Wombat Guerrera & Tainted Soul are in the ring, being briefed by the referee. Both men nod their understanding to the ref, who walks away from both men and signals for the match to start. Wombat & Tainted stare at each other, before they go for the tie-up. Tainted sucker-punches Wombat, though and tries to whip WG to the ropes. Wombat manages to reverse it and connects with a high flipping dropkick to the chin of Tainted as the latter returns. Wombat quickly pulls Tainted up and whips him to the corner, as Womabt charges from behind. TS sees his opportunity and slides beneath the ropes, causing WG to smash his own face into the turnbuckle. Wombat staggers backwards, clutching his face, as Soul leaps up to the top rope. Wombat turns around slowly... just in time to be floored by a super clothesline from the top rope from Tainted. TS quickly pulls Wombat back up and locks in a headlock. Guerrera looks to whip Soul off the ropes, but TS managesd to hold on. Wombat wriggles around for a bit, before he releases the pressure off his neck with a backdrop neckdrop on Tainted! Wombat then makes the cover, as the referee counts.... One.... Two... KICK-OUT BY TAINTED!! Wombat swiftly gets to his feet and locks in a headlock of his own. Tainted manages to get himself up to the vertical base and backs into the corner turnbuckle, crushing Wombat as well!! As WG releases the hold, Tainted falls to his kness and gasps for air, while Wombat falls face down on his face. Tainted, seeing Wombat prone, ascends to the the top rope.. and scores with a shooting star press, sending the crowd wild!! Tainted quickly hooks the legs, as the ref counts the cover.... One.... Two... and Wombat gets the shoulder up!! Tainted gets up and wipes the sweat off his forehead. He rams his forearm into the back of Wombat's head, before he goes back to the top turnbuckle. He waits for Wombat to get up and turn around... and leaps off looking for a hurricanrana.... but Wombat catches Tainted and scores with a devastating powerbomb!! WG then rolls Tainted up, as the referee counts... One... Two.... Th-BUT TAINTED GETS HIS LEFT SHOUDER UP IN THE NICK OF TIME!! Wombat looks very frustated, as he gets up and shifts into a fighter's stance. Tainted gets up woozily and turns around. Wombat lifts his left leg and goes for a crescent kick.. but Tainted catches it and executes a dragon-screw leg-whip!! The crowd then roars, as Tainted tries to lock it an elevated boston-crab. Wombat struggles to get out of it... and does so by gouging Tainted's eyes, sending him sprawling. Wombat then gets to his feet, waits for Tainted to get up and.... hurricaranna!!! Wombat drives Tainted's head into the canvas, knocking him out. WG then makes the cover as the referee counts.... One.... Two.... Three!! Wombat Guerrera has defeated Tainted Soul, but it could have gone either way, Wombat must admit. F4A then cuts to the last commercial break.... as the main-event beckons. Cobalt -VS- Johnny Rage -VS- Confusion ====================================== When F4A returns, Cobalt is already in the ring, and Johnny Rage is standing on the outside. The main-event is about to start, but Confusion is nowhere to be found. The referee look at the announcers, who attempt to communicate with VP Kamlesh. Cobalt decides he has waited long enough, and stops Johnny Rage from shouting obscenities by diving through the ropes and connection with a suicide dive! The crowd gasp in shock, as Cobalt unloads on Rage with hard rights and left.... before he pulls Rage up and tosses him into the ring. Cobalt himself rolls under the bottom rope and into the ring, where Rage attempts to avenge the early attack by Cobalt by swinging his left arm. Cobalt ducks and executes a short irish-whip, bringing JR back to him without letting go of his wrist. Cobalt pivots his hip into Rage and flips him over with a fireman's carry takedown. Cobalt then rolls the defenseless Rage onto his face and begins alternately delivering knee strikes into the back of his head. Cobalt then pulls Rage to his feet and jumps onto his back, locking in a headlock! Rage wriggles around for a bit, before he delivers a sharp elbow to The Goblin's midsection and sends him to mat with a vicious jawbreaker. Both men lay on the mat, breathless after the opening exchange. They manage to get back up to their feet quickly, though; both looking to gain the advantage to put them ahead the other. Cobalt gets up first. He launches a stiff right at Rage, who ducks the punch and counters with a crushing full-nelson bomb. He quickly rolls Cobalt over and hooks the legs.... One...... Two.... but Cobalt kicks out easily. "The Natural" Johnny Rage pulls Cobalt up to his feet and irish-whips him to the ropes. The Goblin smartly uses his own momentum to nail a powerful flying lariat as he comes off the ropes. Rage goes down, but is back up. Cobalt bounces himself off the ropes again, but this time, Rage catches him and scores big time with a spinebuster slam, before Rage shoots himself into the ropes and drops his right leg across The Goblin's throat. Feeling cocky, Rage makes the lax cover as the referee starts counting.... One.... Two... but Cobalt forces his shoulder off the mat! Pissed, Rage slaps his hand against the canvas, before pulling Cobalt up. The Goblin sneaks in a hard punch to the abdomen, but Johnny answers back with a shot of his own, before whipping him into the ropes. As Cobalt returns, Rage goes for a clothesline but The Goblin ducks..... and uses his nimble body to pull off a stinging falling reverse DDT out of nowhere. Cobalt quickly ascends to the top-rope, measures Rage and connects with a senton leg-drop!! The crowd goes wild at the high-risk move, as Cobalt makes the cover.... One..... Two.... Th-BUT RAGE SAVES HIMSELF AND KICKS OUT!! Cobalt is none too happy about that.... but gets really pissed when he sees Confusion coming out of the back!! Confusion is wearing a "Goblin Hunting" t-shirt, and has what seems to be nail-clippers in his possession!! Cobalt shows his wrath, as he pulls Rage up, kicks him in the gut and drills Rage's head into the canvas with a DDT!! Then, The Goblin faces Confusion again.... and dares him to come to the ring!! And Confusion accepts the dare. Cobalt stands his ground, and as Confusion steps into the ring, goes for a flying clothesline off the ropes. Confusion catches The Goblin in mid-air and slams him down into the canvas with a huge powerslam, before making the cover..... One.... Two.... Thre-NOOO!! Rage drives his elbow into the back of Confusion's head, to break up the count!! Rage then pulls Confusion up, and locks him in a front facelock, before bringing him up in the air and looking for a reverse fallaway slam! Confusion follows through, though, and lands behind Rage. He pushes Rage against the ropes and as Johnny returns, Confusion kicks him in the gut and scores with a front-face neckbreaker!! With Rage out of it, Confusion rolls the former out of the ring, and goes to focus his attention on Cobalt.... who recovers and spears Confusion all the way to hell!! The referee counts as Cobalt lands on top of Confusion... One...... Two...... Thre-CONFUSION KICKS OUT JUST IN TIME!! The crowd cannot believe that Cobalt didn't get the job done. As both men pull themselves up, Cobalt swings at Confusion. The latter ducks and brings The Goblin down in an armbar takedown.... before switching to an elevated ankle lock!!! Cobalt screams out in pain, with the capacity crowd all chanting for Cobalt to tap!! The Goblin tries to reach the rope, but Confusion's hold is too strong. Cobalt looks to tap.... before he uses his free leg to good effect, knocking Confusion senselss with it!! Cobalt slowly pulls himself up via the help of the ropes, but as he turns around, Confusion kicks him in the gut and..... THE DAZED & THE CONFUSED!! IT IS ALL OVER!! But the exchange takes a lot out of Confusion too, as he lays on the mat, breathing heavily. Johnny Rage, on the outside, finally gets his act together. Seeing his opponents vulnerable, he hurries to the top-rope, and signals for a 450 splash. Just then, Confusion gets up. Seeing Rage on the top rope, he quickly regains his vertical base and catches Rage as the latter leaps off the top rope.... AND DRILLS JOHNNY WITH A STUPENDOUS POWERBOMB!! The move once again takes a lot out of Confusion, but he manages to drape his arm over Rage's right shoulder..... but The Goblin too crawls towards Rage and hooks his left leg!! The referee, confused, just starts counting.... One........... Two........ Three!!! Both Confusion & Cobalt are credited with the victory!! End Transmission =================== Confusion gets up, pissed that Cobalt did what he did. He steps out of the ring and grabs the nail clippers he brought out, before he goes back into the ring. He grins devilishy.... AND TRIES TO CLIP COBALT'S FINGERNAILS! A shadowy figure with a mask suddenly jumps over the railing, with a chair in hand. He goes into the ring and.... cracks it over Confusion's head!! Cobalt gets up woozily... but the shadowy figure knocks him out too!! With the crowd stunned, the shadowy figure puts the chair down... and starts to remove his mask. His mask is almost off... but transmission ends abruptly. F4A Edition 1 ends, with the mystery of who the shadowy figure is still at large..... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- http://uwf4L.vze.com
