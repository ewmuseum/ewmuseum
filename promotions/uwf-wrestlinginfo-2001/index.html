Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The United Wrestling Federation called itself the carry-over of an earlier World Wrestling Alliance, officially reopened in April 2001 as one fed inside WrestlingInfo.com, a network of wrestling sites joined into a single megasite. It ran on angles and roleplays, with an EZBoard as its roleplaying floor, and it took original wrestlers only, men and women alike, thirty at a time with a waiting list behind them.

Its policies promised Tuesday Riot as the main weekly show and Friday Free 4 All as the second, a pay-per-view every month once the fed was on a roll, and a title ladder running from the World Title down through the Intercontinental, Hardcore and Internet belts to the tag-team titles. It asked most of all for tag teams and for more women.

Two match types were its own. In the Wrapped Steel Chain, both men's hands were bound in chain; it credited the idea to AJ Springer, from a feud over the IWF Internet Championship with the man who became its chief executive. In the Japanese Death Match UWF Extreme, credited to The Maniac from a feud over the same belt, barbed wire replaced the ropes, the turnbuckles exploded on a third impact, and no pin counted until a man had been thrown into every side of an energized cage.

In November 2001 its vice-president announced the site finished and applications open, promising an old-fashioned fed that dared to be different. The first Free-4-All followed in December as the start of a new era: AJ Springer won the opening triple threat, a kendo stick turned SeVeN against Derek Cage into a no-contest, Derek Craven and Wombat Guerrera won their singles matches, and Confusion and Cobalt were both credited with the main event over Johnny Rage.

The card closed on a masked man with a chair laying out both winners and beginning to pull off his mask as the broadcast cut away. Nothing later survives.
