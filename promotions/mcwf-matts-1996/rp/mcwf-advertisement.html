MCWF Commercial: -- You see three guys sitting in the MCWF Wrestling Ring at Rockerfeller Center, New York, New York. They're names, the President [name withheld], former wrestler Wild Will Sloan, and Canadian Wrestling Hall of Fame announcer Justin Hunter. MP: Wow, I cannot wait till the MCWF is off the ground. WWS: Yeah, it should be cool. JH: The competition is going to be mighty stiff here in the MCWF, so you better be ready for it. MP: Sure is, and this federation is the BEST, no doubt, and we are looking for you, yes YOU to be in the MCWF. WWS: What the Pitt man means is that if you or someone you know is a wrestler, or you manage a wrestler etcetera, and you are looking for a wrestling league, this is the one you should choose. JH: The MCWF will start off by having a Tournament for both the World Tag Team Belts and the World Championship on Monday Night Masacre LIVE. Thats right, all Monday Night Masacre shows are LIVE. MP: Our inaugural Monday Night Masacre show and all the shows after that will be here in the newly deccorated Penthouse of the Rockerfeller center. The 2,000 seat arena made just for the MCWF. And the only way to be there is to have a wrestler in the MCWF. WWS: Monday Night Masacre will be shown every Monday on NBC. There will also be a Monthly Pay Per View extravaganza. And also to go along with the Masacre shows will be the Saturday Night Extreme show right after Saturday Night Live. And of course, as the Masacre show is LIVE, so is the Extreme show. JH: And your three broadcasters for all the shows are myself, Justin Hunter, President [name withheld] and Wild Will Sloan. Also be sure to check out our incredible website located at http://www.inforamp.net/~[name withheld]/mcwf/ for more information and the wrestler form and if you have any other questions email [detail withheld] MP: So be there. WWS: Monday and Saturday Nights. JH: As the MCWF starts. MP: Get those forms in today to become part of the newest and BEST federation, Matt's Championship Wrestling Federation. Commercial Ends Showing The MCWF Logo. -- Here is the form again: (No more than 4 wrestlers) Your Real Name: Your E-Mail Address: Your E-Wrestlers Name: Height: (MAX HEIGHT 7'5") Weight: Face/Heel/Nuetral Give us 10 to 15 of your wrestlers favorite moves: Finisher Name: Breif Description of finisher: Is it a submission move: Give us the introduction that will appear when your wrester(s) are introduced(Including your theme song, looks. etc... ): -- Manager Info:(Optional) Manager Name/Sex: Manager Description: Will He/She Cheat: If so with what: -- Tag Team Info(Optional) Tag Team Name: Who Is In It: Tag Team Finisher Name: Description of Finisher: -- Your Stable Name: And send all of this info to [detail withheld] -- Thanks alot [name withheld] MCWF President [detail withheld] MCWF Homepage - http://www.inforamp.net/~[name withheld]/mcwf/
