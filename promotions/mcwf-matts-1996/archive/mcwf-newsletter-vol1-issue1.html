*----------------------------------------* *Matt's Championship Wrestling Federation* *----------------------------------------* * NEWSLETTER VOLUME 1 ISSUE 1 * *----------------------------------------* * LOOKING FOR NEW WRESTLERS * *----------------------------------------* Thats right folks, the MCWF is looking for you, the newest Wrestler in the MCWF. This is a role playing league, and do not worry, I do not show favourtism to any wrestlers. We will have roughly 2 cards a week, to be named at a later date, and a PPV every month, those also to be named at a later date. Right now, what you have to do, is just send me the following: *----------------------------------------* Your Real Name: Your E-Mail Address: Your E-Wrestlers Name: Height: (MAX HEIGHT 7'5") Weight: Face/Heel/Nuetral Give us 10 to 15 of your wrestlers favorite moves: Finisher Name: Breif Description of finisher: Is it a submission move: Give us the introduction that will appear when your wrester(s) are introduced(Including your theme song, looks. etc... ): -- Manager Info:(Optional) Manager Name/Sex: Manager Description: Will He/She Cheat: If so with what: -- Tag Team Info(Optional) Tag Team Name: Who Is In It: Tag Team Finisher Name: Description of Finisher: *----------------------------------------* So please send all of that info to [detail withheld] and I will be in touch with you asap. A homepage should also be up shortly. *----------------------------------------* This has been a MCWF Promotion. Copyright 1996 MCWF *----------------------------------------*
