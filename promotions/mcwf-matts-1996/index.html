Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Matt's Championship Wrestling Federation announced itself on 20 October 1996 with a commercial script staged in its own newly decorated penthouse at Rockefeller Center — a 2,000-seat arena, it said, built just for the promotion. The script puts its own president on camera alongside a self-billed “former wrestler” and a self-billed Canadian Wrestling Hall of Fame announcer, the three of them narrating the federation into being: a Monday Night Masacre broadcast live on NBC every week, a Saturday Night Extreme show taped right after Saturday Night Live, and a monthly pay-per-view, all still to be named.

Its opening card was pitched as a double coronation — a tournament for the World Tag Team Belts and the World Championship at once, on Monday Night Masacre's first broadcast. No such card, or any other, survives. What survives instead is the recruitment drive meant to fill it: a newsletter soliciting wrestlers by form (name, weight, moves, a manager and tag-team info, “no more than 4 wrestlers”), reissued word for word a week later with the intake trimmed to two wrestlers and a tag team.

The federation's last surviving document, dated 27 October 1996, is a further reissue of that same call under the heading “Still Looking For Owners.” No roster, card, or match result of any kind survives past it, and no closure was ever posted; the promotion's own record ends mid-recruitment, one week after it opened.
