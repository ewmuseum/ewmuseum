SEEW — Card

SEEW's last...

12 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The last card of a league known as Simple Easy E-Wrestling... ***Scene opens with the SEEW logo fading across a black screen. The scene switches to a bridge in a rural town. On this bridge, three small kids jump. Scene switches to SEEW Arena, where Tritt and Fank are standing*** Tritt: "Welcome to Simple Easy E-Wrestling's Jump Off A Bridge Sunday!!!" ***Crowd goes wild*** Tritt: "I am Tritt Scoon and this is Fank Oogle, we're your broadcasters for this night of excitement!" Fank: "This is the 'Welcome Back' edition!" Tritt: "That's right, the last time we joined you all for JOABS was way back on March 16." Fank: "A special shout out to all my homebros in prison!" Tritt: "Huh?" Fank: "Ernie, Clyde, Stanley, Eugene, Francis...all you muthas keep it real!" Tritt: "Anyway, we've got some action for you tonight!" Fank: "Dang crooked! We've got Mike 'the Mechanic' Malone facing Joshner the Barbarian in a non-title bout and Television Champion Polecat squares off with Killer Kenny of the Black Widows with the title on the proverbial line!" Tritt: "But up first we have SEEW veteran Big Bully Brandon taking on the newcomer, Relentless!" Fank: "Down to SEEW Ring Announcer Smurt!" ---Big Bully Brandon vs. Relentless--- Smurt: "Ladies and gents, welcome to Jump Off A Bridge Sunday!" ***More cheering*** Smurt: "Introducing first, from parts unknown, weighing 244 pounds, here is RELENTLESS!" ***Relentless comes out wearing a pair of Calvin Klein boxer shorts and a Grandma Stagg shirt. The crowd cheers*** Tritt: "These folk love Relentless!" Smurt: "His opponent, from Big Cliftky, Kentucky, weighing 285 pounds, here is BIG BULLY BRANDON!" ***Crowd boos wildly in disgust of Brandon, he doesn't seem to care*** Fank: "The Bully doesn't seem to be a crowd favorite." Tritt: "Nope. Time for the match!" ***Ding, ding*** They lock up, Brandon shoves Relentless away. Relentless lets out a mighty scream! Fank: "Relentless is pumped up for the match!" Tritt: "That or the fact that he probably hasn't slept in four days and the coffee is going to his brain." Fank: "Good point." Brandon walks over and punches Relentless right in the mouth. Tritt: "Good way to shut him up!" Relentless falls to the mat. Brandon legdrops him. Cover: 1- Fank: "Brandon going for the quick win." Relentless jumps to his feet and starts nailing Brandon with rights and lefts. Brandon catches his fist and throws Relentless to the canvas and applies an STF. Tritt: "The Bully is looking technical early." Relentless is feeling the pain, but Brandon decides to break the hold. He picks up the dazed Relentless and bounces off the ropes, catching Relentless with a dropkick. Fank: "Displaying aerial ability, too, Brandon seems to be well rounded!" Tritt: "Must have trained hard during his time away from SEEW." Relentless falls to the mat. Cover: 1-2-kickout! Relentless rolls away to the outside of the ring. Tritt: "Relentless needs to regroup right now!" Fank: "Either that or buy a 1987 model sub-shop." ***Tritt gives Fank a weird look*** Brandon hurries to the outside. He grabs Relentless and slams his face right into the ring post! Fank: "Ooooooh!" Relentless falls to the ground. Brandon climbs back into the ring, breaking the ref's count. Then he climbs to the top turnbuckle on the post nearest the fallen Relentless. As Relentless staggers to his feet, Malone moonsaults onto him! Tritt: "Unbelievable! I've never seen Brandon do a move like that!" Brandon throws the almost unconscious Relentless back into the ring. He climbs back in and covers him: 1-2-Relentless's foot is on the ropes! Fank: "Relentless had enough sense to put that leg up, or this match would be over right now!" Brandon scoops Relentless up and puts him in a gorilla-press position. He quickly drops him and walks to another corner with a disgusted look on his face. Tritt: "Huh?" Relentless turns to the camera: "I haven't showered in three weeks!" Fank: "Oh...." Brandon has recovered now and he walks over to Relentless who has made it to his feet. Just as he gets there, Relentless backdrops him to the outside. Tritt: "Relentless was playing opossum!" Relentless then climbs up on the top turnbuckle and dives off onto Brandon. Fank: "I believe that was the 'No Mercy Dive'." Relentless throws Brandon back in the ring and climbs back on the top rope. He jumps off going for a frog splash, but Brandon puts a big foot up, catching Relentless in the stomach! Tritt: "That's gotta hurt!" Fank: "So does the electric chair." Relentless holds his stomach in pain. Brandon gets to his feet and staggers over to Relentless. He picks him up and places him in a powerbomb position. Tritt: "Looks like it's 'Bully Bomb' time!" Brandon scoops up Relentless and executes the Bully Bomb, sending Relentless crashing into the canvas. Fank: "It's over!" Brandon scoops him up again and executes another Bully Bomb, then another, then another. Tritt: "This is totally uncalled for! The fans are starting to throw trash in the ring!" Brandon executes four more Bully Bombs, at this point, Relentless is almost lifeless. Fank: "I haven't seen this many 'Bully Bombs' since Brandon squared off with Roy Cahoots in that career match!" Tritt: "That's a whole other story." Brandon taunts the crowd, then places one finger on Relentless's chest. 1-2-3! Fank: "Well, he got him." Tritt: "It was a well fought match, too bad Brandon had to crap it up at the end." Smurt: "The winner of this bout....BIG BULLY BRANDON!" ***Crowd boos wildly as more trash is thrown in the ring*** Fank: "Fans didn't like that doozy too much." Tritt: "Maybe the next match will be a little better." Fank: "Yeah, it's or the TV Title, Champion Polecat defends against the Black Widows' own, Killer Kenny!" Tritt: "Take it away, Smurt!" ---Polecat vs. Killer Kenny, TV Title bout--- Smurt: "This following contest is for the Television Title! Introducing first, the challenger, from Houston, Texas, weighing 248 pounds, here is KILLER KENNY!" ***Mixed reaction from fans*** Smurt: "His opponent, the Television Champion, from Tampa Florida, weighing 280 pounds, here is POLECAT!" ***Very enthusiastic cheers from fans*** Tritt: "Well, both men are in the ring and eying each other up, let's do this thing!" ***Ding, ding*** The men lock up, Polecat puts Killer Kenny in a headlock. Kenny elbows his way out and sends Polecat into the ropes. Frankensteiner by Kenny! Fank: "Kenny surprises Polecat early!" Cover by Kenny: 1-2-kickout! Kenny pulls Polecat to his feet, and begins a series of karate kicks to his chest area. Tritt: "Kenny knows his martial arts!" Polecat falls to the mat, Kenny goes over and climbs to the top ropes and jumps. Polecat gathers himself in time to avoid a dropkick from Kenny. Kenny goes crashing to the canvas. Fank: "Down goes Mr. Killer." Tritt: "That's why they're called 'high-risk maneuvers'!" Polecat goes over to Kenny and drops an elbow on his back. He then applies a step-over toe hold. Tritt: "Polecat looking technically sound tonight!" Kenny fights his way out, but Polecat just starts pounding away with lefts and rights to Kenny's face. Polecat then executes a DDT on his opponent. Cover: 1-2-kickout! Fank: "It'll take more than that to finish the Killer one!" Kenny starts to get a second wind, he rakes Polecat's eyes, then bounces off the ropes and catches Polecat with flying body press. He picks up Polecat and sends him into the ropes, Polecat ducks a clothesline, but Kenny catches him on the way back and nails him with a heartpunch. Pin by Kenny: 1-2-almost! Tritt: "No! Polecat kicked out!" Kenny picks up Polecat and scoops him. Powerslam! Another pin attempt: 1-2-NO! Fank: "No quit in our TV Champ tonight!" Tritt: "These men are giving it their all!" Kenny picks up the dazed Polecat and spins around catches him with a Killer Kick! Kenny poses for the crowd. Tritt: "Pin him ,idiot!" Fank: "Some love him, some hate him." Tritt: "But nobody loves those guys!" Tritt was speaking of the Mystic Beast Kings, who run down to the ring. Bloody Tiger nails Killer Kenny while Lavender Dragon goes after Polecat. Fank: "Of course...." Tiger kicks away at Kenny in the center of the ring, while Dragon moonsaults onto Polecat on the outside of the ring. Tritt: "Whoa, did you see that!?" Fank: "Unbelievable risks these men take!" Down comes Mike Malone and Lady Mai Tai to aid the attack on Polecat. Dragon goes inside the ring and starts attacking Kenny, too. They execute the "Deep Doomsday" on Kenny four times. Outside, Malone is repeatedly piledriving Polecat into the concrete floor. Tritt: "Somebody stop these guys!!!" Fank: "He's gonna kill Polecat!" Malone stops piledriving and pulls out some white paint and paints a white streak down Polecat's back. One fan tries to climb the rail to stop this attack, but he is restrained by security. Tritt: "Get that kid out of here!" Tran Lee and Johnny Armentor run down to the ring to save Kenny and the Beast Kings, Malone, and Mai Tai jump in the crowd and disappear to the back. The Black Widows stand over the fallen Kenny and Polecat and help them to their feet. Fank: "What's the decision on this match?" Smurt: "Ladies and gents, the referee has declared this match a DOUBLE-DISQUALIFICATION!" ***The crowd boos*** Tritt: "What a sorry way to end a good match." Fank: "You said it." Tritt: "Well, Polecat retains his title." Fank: "If Kenny just hadn't stopped to pose for the crowd, he could have been the champ right now." Tritt: "But instead we have this horrible scene." Fank: "Yeah, the Black Widows are practically dragging Kenny and Polecat back to the dressing rooms." Tritt: "There could be some serious damage done to them tonight at the hands of the Dojo." Fank: "Malone has to come right back out here and wrestle Joshner the Barbarian, too. If I was him, I'd watch my back." Tritt: "I'm sure he'll bring his whole clan." Fank: "As always." Tritt: "It's now time for that match, here we go, non-title affair between Joshner and Malone." Fank: "As you know, Malone and his goons were the ones that cost Joshner his North American Title at FMOTW, so he can't be happy with them." Tritt: "Back down to Smurt!" ---Mike "the Mechanic" Malone vs. Joshner the Barbarian, non-title--- Smurt: "The following contest is the main event!" ***Good pop from crowd*** Smurt: "Introducing first, in this non-title bout, from parts unknown, weighing 285, here is JOSHNER THE BARBARIAN! ***Some cheers from fans, mostly boos*** Smurt: "His opponent, accompanied to the ring by Lady Mai Tai, from Brooklyn, New York, weighing 235, here is MIKE "THE MECHANIC" MALONE!!! ***Crowd is going crazy with boos, as Malone (who somehow made it back to the back) emerges*** Tritt: "There's the snake now." Fank: "Not many in this arena rooting for Mr. Malone." Tritt: "Nope, not after all the crap he's pulled since he's been here." Fank: "Here we go, Malone vs. Joshner!" ***Ding, ding*** Tritt: "Joshner is not quite recovered from the attack at the hands of the Dojo, so he's still kind of limping, we'll see how it affects him." Malone charges at Joshner's knee and shoulder tackles it. Joshner is wobbling, when Malone tackles it again, sending Joshner to the mat. Fank: "We know what Malone is after!" Malone grabs Joshner's leg and applies a figure-four. Joshner screams out in pain. Tritt: "The Barbaric one's feeling the pain." Malone slowly edges over to the ropes, and grabs onto them, adding leverage to the leglock. After about five seconds, the ref notices this and calls for a break. Fank: "Cheating ol' punk, Mike is." Malone drags Joshner to his feet and immediately starts kicking away at the injured knee. Two more shoulder tackles and Joshner is back down. Malone drags his leg over to the ropes and begins jumping up and down on the injured knee. The ref calls for a break. Tritt: "Malone's dominating so far." Joshner is picked up by Malone and slammed back down. Malone walks over to the turnbuckles and climbs to the top. He jumps off and drops a fist right on Joshner's knee. He picks him up and throws him to the outside. Malone turns to the ref and begins telling him about a baseball team he once played for. Fank: "This might possibly be trouble, you know....maybe...." Lady Mai Tai takes the bokkan she is holding and smashes it over Joshner's knee, he lets a mighty scream! Tritt: "Ain't that fresh?" Malone stops talking to the ref and goes to the outside to get Joshner. Fank: "Life's a bummer." Tritt: "What does that have to do with the match?" Fank: "I dunno." Malone throws Joshner back in the ring and applies an STF to the big man. Tritt: "Joshner's knee has taken a real pounding tonight, how much longer can he hold out?" Fank: "August." Joshner writhes in pain, Malone locks the hold even tighter. Tritt: "People may not like Malone, but you have to hand it to him, he's one heck of an athlete!" Fank: "And a shoe repairman." Tritt: "Did you get into Ambien again?" ***No response from Fank*** Joshner finally breaks the hold. Malone drags him to his feet and starts biting him! Tritt: "'The Mechanic' using all kinds of methods tonight." The ref breaks them up, Malone sends Joshner into the ropes, he ducks a clothesline and comes back with clothesline of his own. Malone goes down. Joshner drops a leg right on Malone's throat. He picks him up and executes a gourd buster right on Malone. Fank: "Joshner also showing some range!" Cover by Joshner: 1-kickout! Joshner pulls Malone up and places him in a chinlock. Malone quickly breaks free with a series of elbows. Malone bounces off the ropes and catches Joshner with a flying forearm smash. Cover by Malone: 1-2-kickout! Tritt: "No!" Joshner jumps up and starts unloading on Malone. Left, right, left, right, he shoves Malone down! Fank: "All those fists and all it took was a little push!" Malone becomes furious, he gets right back up and nails Joshner with a few rights and lefts of his own. He whips Joshner into the corner and charges after him, Joshner places a big boot in Malone's way, catching him right in the face! Tritt: "Joshner should capitalize!" The Barbarian climbs the ropes, he jumps off and nails the fallen Malone with a big splash! ***The crowd goes wild*** Fank: "Malone has never been cleanly pinned, we might see it here tonight!" Joshner rolls over onto Malone and hooks a leg: 1-2-almost!!! Tritt: "Malone somehow kicked out!" Fank: "I'm surprised we haven't seen the Mystic Beast Kings or the Black Widows yet." Tritt: "Who knows..." Malone staggers to his feet, Joshner comes over and puts Malone in powerbomb position. He shouts "It's over!" to the crowd. Fank: "This could be it for 'the Mechanic'! But Malone flips Joshner backwards and the big man lands flat on his back. Malone then applies the 'Crazy Eight Texas Cloverleaf'! Tritt: "I've never seen anyone escape this hold!" Joshner struggles, after about ten seconds he submits! Fank: "You won't see Joshner escape, he just gave up!" Smurt: "The winner of this bout, via submission.....MIKE 'THE MECHANIC' MALONE!!!" ***The crowd boos, but not as loudly because it was a clean victory*** Tritt: "Joshner gave it his all, but came up just short." Fank: "That's two losses for the Barbarian, in just one week of competition, too!" Tritt: "Both times he came very close to victory, however." ***Polecat, sporting an icepack on his head, emerges from the dressing rooms while Malone is still in the ring. He has a mic.*** Polecat: "Malone,I am the best wrestler YOU HAVE NEVER FOUGHT! You come in here, attack me, and run. YOU'RE A [BLEEP] [BLEEP]!!!" ***Crowd cheers wildly*** Polecat: "So the question I have is why are you hiding behind that title of yours? Are you afraid to defend it? You have not defended that title ONCE since you won it! So I'm calling you out! Stop hiding! Don't be a [BLEEP] as we already know you are, admit that you are afraid that I CAN WHIP YOUR ASS ALL OVER THE [BLEEP] ARENA!!!!" ***Crowd goes nuts!!!*** Polecat: "Tonight, begins the PATH OF RAGE of the Polecat! I have already proven myself in winning this Television Title..." ***Looks down at belt*** Polecat: "And now,...I WILL VACATE IT! My sole purpose in this league now is YOU MALONE, and whoever comes in the way of my path, I WILL DESTROY! Crusher, BRING IT ON BOY! It's time to KICK SOME ASS! WHHHOOO! FEEL THE WRATH, 97! ***Crowd goes wild again as Polecat raises his hands in the air! "Perfect Strangers" by Deep Purple then blasts over the speakers and the crowd is still going nuts! He then jumps out of the ring and hands the T.V. title to Smeeto, who has come to ringside. He then points to the west Side of the crowd and goes over there, climbs over the railing, and leaves the arena through the crowd*** Fank: "I think we have seen the comeback of the original POLECAT!" ***Malone and Mai Tai stand in the ring screaming "Come back, Skunk Boy!" before they exit the ring*** Tritt: "I'm gonna go get a word with Smeeto real quick!" ***Tritt leaves the broadcast booth, grabs a mic., and goes over to Smeeto, who is standing with the TV Title*** Tritt: "Hello Mr. Smeeto, sir." Smeeto: "Hi Tritt." Tritt: "So what's going down with the TV belt?" Smeeto: "Well, I'm not sure yet, I'll have a decision in by tomorrow. But one thing that I do know is that I have just signed the main event for Constant Competition With That High School Halfback. It will be an SEEW Heavyweight Title bout between Mike Malone and Polecat!" ***Fans cheer loudly*** Smeeto: "There will also be some special stipulations for the match, too!" Tritt: "Like what?" Smeeto: "Oh, you'll see!" ***Smeeto leaves with the belt as Tritt just stands there puzzled, scene fades***
