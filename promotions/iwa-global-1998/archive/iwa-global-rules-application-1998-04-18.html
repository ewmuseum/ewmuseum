IWA Rules and Application Packet

18 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
International Wrestling Alliance ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Are you looking for a fed where you can make a name for yourself in E-wrestling? The IWA is the place. We are recruiting the best! If you don't make the cut, your gone! You roleplay to win. Here, names are taken and titles are won! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The IWA will be run by EZRIDER225 and myself. Only fake wrestlers will be accepted. So, no Jerichos, Giants, Flairs, Austins, Helmsleys, Hogans, or any other wrestler for that matter. Originality is wanted and is the only thing accepted. That's just about the only rule besides having roleplay 2 times a week and 30 lines per roleplay w/ scenes. If you are not roleplaying up to standards, EZRIDER or I will boot you out. Good luck, because your gonna need it! So, onto the app. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ You may have a maximum of 2 wrestlers and you must form a Tag team with another wrestler. You may also only have one female wrestler. The Featherweights have to be under 230 pounds. You may only hold one title at a time. Once your done with the app, please send to EZRIDER225 and myself. Thank you. Screen Name- Wrestlers Name- Height- Weight- Date of Birth- Alignment (Face/Heel/Neutral(If neutral, please explain)- Home Town- Finisher (If you use a special name, please list the name you use and explain the manuever)- Setup Move (If you use a special name, please list the name you use and explain the manuever)- Five Favorite Moves- 1. 2. 3. 4. 5. Wrestling Style (Please Mark One) __1. Amateur Wrestler __2. All Around Wrestler __3. Brawler __4. High Flier __5. Mat Technician __6. Submission Wrestler Describe Wrestler's Ring Attire- Describe the Wrestler's Features- Write the Wrestler's Ring Entrance (Like what happens and so forth)- Theme Song (Must be a real song)- Artist- Which Title Would You Like To Go After First- Anything and Everything Else that You Can Think Of- ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ These will be the titles that will be used in the IWA. IWA Global Champion (Defended every card) IWA Superstar Champion (Defended every other card) IWA North American Champion (Defended ever week) IWA Featherweight Champion (Defended every other card) IWA Global Tag Team Champions (Defended every card) IWA Superstar Tag Team Champions (This may be a title, depending on how many tag teams we get) (Defended every other card) IWA Women's Champion (Defended every other card) All of these are true unless stated otherwise. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Well, that's all I can think of, so fill out the app and send them in so you can be in the tournaments and the battle royals for the belts. -Greg
