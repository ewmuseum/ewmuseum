Tuesday's tragic events.  I know that by now, it has become increasingly horrific and you yourself said that some of our people were directly affected by this.  You don't have to use this, but I just want to get it out....and thank you because of the touching tribute on the site.
Thanks.)       Hey guys, this is Glen, handler of Kyren Knight.  Instead of the usual promo, I thought I'd reflect upon Tuesday's very terrible and atrocious acts. Tuesday's pictures will forever be burned into the memories of
Americans and people around the world everywhere.  The horror I felt as I watched them play the same sickening scene over and over.  I remember it because it just didn't feel like a normal day.  I had been working night shifts (I work with a special police department divison.)  It had been a particularly slow night.  Before shift change I said "Must be something big gonna happen today, I can feel it."  Little did I know how terribly right
I was.  I was awoken by the sounds of my family screaming.  When I went to see what was wrong, my girlfriend told me "Glen, the World Trade Center is gone.
    The Pentagon has been attacked, my god we are under attack."  The thought that this could happen here was so very scary and unnerving.  I, for the first time in a long time cried.  I had never felt angry, hurt, and uncertain like I did Tuesday.  But, I watched as brave firefighters and policeman gave there lives as well as bystanders to save the man next to them and I thought to myself, My god these people are showing the world what America is all about.  No matter what happens, we are going to get back up and stand tall and proud. These people died doing their best.To those deeply affected by this tragedy whether they lost a family  member or they were there, I can do nothing but offer my deepest condolences and my thoughts an prayers are with you and your families. I know it is small and not worth much but my heart is truly with you.  To those that are helping right now.....GOD BLESS YOU.  You are what America is about and true heroes. Thank you for listening.  If you guys wanna contact me to talk or anything, my e-mail is
