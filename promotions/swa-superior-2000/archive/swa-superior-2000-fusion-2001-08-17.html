(Viper turns around slowly as the tall man looks fiercely at the back of his head.  As Viper turns around he is met by the large man's right hand.  He proceeds to chokeslam the World Champion in the middle of the ring.  He looks down at Viper, spits on him, then stands back up.)
Viper, consider yourself the number one target of my wrath.  You have just been reacquainted with none other than the Five Star Pyscho, Dan Debacle.   Have a great day. ** The scene fades out on the ring with Viper unconscious and Dan Debacle exiting the ring.  Fusion goes to black.**
