SWA (Superior, 2000–2001) — Card

SCW Magazine, 18 August 2001

18 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCW Magazine   {The scene opens up to the interview area of Fusion.  Mike Johnson is standing with a microphone in hand.}
  SCW Fusion: Back to School 2001 is just about set.  As we all know that Main Event will pit, SCW Heavyweight
Champion Viper, against the man who won the right to get a shot at the title just a few weeks back, Mike Powers.  Viper had a special guest show up on Fusion last night, the Five Star Psycho, Dan Debacle, lets show you what happened when the guest showed up.   ((The crowd begins to turn away from the Jumbotron.  A man standing about 7' and well over 300 is standing in the ring, behind Viper.  Viper continues to watch the Jumbotron as Maverick smiles.) Viper, the man that has arrived in SCW is no stranger to gold.  In fact, in just a few short weeks back in the day, he became the World Champion of a fed called BDCW.  You are well familiar with it.  In fact, all you have to do is look over your shoulder right now, and your memory should be refreshed.
(Viper turns around slowly as the tall man looks fiercely at the back of his head.  As Viper turns around he is met by the large man's right hand.  He proceeds to chokeslam the
World Champion in the middle of the ring.  He looks down at Viper, spits on him, then stands back up.)
Viper, consider yourself the number one target of my wrath.  You have just been reacquainted with none other than the Five Star Pyscho, Dan Debacle.  
Have a great day. )) What does this man have in store for Viper in the future, and what does Maverick have in store for Viper and "Heavy Metal" Van Hammer.  Maverick had these words for the two men last night.   (First things first.  Hammer wants to threaten me with the authorities, fine.   But bottom line is this.  I was voluntarily admitted and voluntarily discharged.  You can hire Private Investigators and Detectives.  Go out and find your own Sherlock Holmes, but you will never find me.  I do my bidding these days behind closed doors.  And right now Viper, your career is on a
Five Star Tailspin. You thought you had me beat.  You thought you would never have to deal with me again.  But you were wrong.
 Dr. Hamlin did help me out.  He helped me figure out who I really am.  I am a guy that goes out and beats people up whether people like it or not.  And right now, you are on the top of my list.
 You wont see me in your face.  You wont even feel my fist.  But there is one man that wants a piece of the action.  He is a man that is very familiar with you and your ways.  This guy is the man that I knew I could count on to get me out of the Loony Bin.)  
What a night Fusion: Back to School 2001 will be.  Now, unlike a Pay Per View this show will be on regular stations that hold Fusion, just on a different night.  It will be held
Saturday, September 1, 2001 in the Dean Dome, on the Campus of The University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.   Now, SCW Fusion, next week, will take place from Cole Field House, on the Campus of the University of Maryland, in College Park, Maryland.  The Main Event of the Evening will be a Cell Cage Tag Match with the participants of the King of Hardcore Tournament. "Heavy Metal" Van Hammer and Kyren Knight will take on Charles Purnell and Mike Powers. That means, not only will Powers get his shot at the Heavyweight Title, but he will also be in the Kind of Hardcore Tournament. In other action, a non title contest between the SCW Heavyweight Champion, Viper and "The Star Killer" Kevin Candido  Will Debacle show up?  Will TPO be present in the match. Those two will have a match up of their own.  Two Psychos doing battle.  What a fight that will be.  Here is a run down of the entire card and the Up to date card of SCW Fusion: Back to School 2001.
See you Friday night for Fusion.   SCW Fusion Friday, August 24, 2001 The Cole Field House University of Maryland College Park, Maryland   Main Event Cell Caged Tag Match "Heavy Metal" Van Hammer and Kyren Knight vs Charles Purnell and Mike Powers   Non Title Match Viper vs "The Star Killer" Kevin Candido   Psycho Battle "The Five Star Psycho" Dan Debacle vs The Psychotic One   "Iron Man" Jordan Jones vs "Dazzling" Dave Tuck   There will be an update on the condition of SCW Superstar, Michael Blaze, after his apparent drug overdose, as well as an interview with his wife, Elizabeth McGinnis.   There will also be some pretaped comments from Dan Debacle and Maverick as well as Viper and The Psychotic One.   SCW Fusion: Back to School 2001 Saturday, September 1, 2001 The Dean Dome University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill Chapel Hill, North Carolina   Main Event SCW Heavyweight Title Match Viper (c) vs Mike Powers   King of Hardcore Tournament Three Tiered Cage One Briefcase with Ten Thousand Dollars and a Five Thousand Dollar Diamond Ring Participants: "Heavy Metal" Van Hammer Charles Purnell Kyren Knight Mike Powers   Television Title Charles Purnell (c) vs "Dazzling" Dave Tuck vs Kevin Candido   "Iron Man" Jordan Jones vs "The Five Star Psycho" Dan Debacle
