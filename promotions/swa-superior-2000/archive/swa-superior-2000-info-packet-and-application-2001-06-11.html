SCW Info Packet and Application

Superior Championship Wrestling   This is Superior Championship Wrestling.  We are an established organization with a great executive staff and a great group of established wrestlers.  We are currently accepting applications for all types of wrestlers.  This information sheet will give you all the info you need on our fed.
    Titles:   We currently have three titles.  They are listed below with the current champions.   SCW World Heavyweight Champion - Maverick SCW North American Champion - "Heavy Metal" Van Hammer SCW World Television Champion - "Iron Man" Jordan Jones   Shows:   Fusion     SCW Magazine   We have two shows.  Fusion and SCW Magazine.  Fusion is the card of the week.  We try to get all the wrestlers on the show in matches.  SCW Magazine is our card line up show.  It also has interviews and recaps from the previous shows.  The hosts also throw out THIER opinions on the goings on in the
SCW.   On Air Personalities:   We have 5 on air personalities.  We prefer you dont use the Pres, VP, or CEO in your
RP's.  That whole angle is over.  The on air personalities are.
  Mike Jackson - SCW Senior Reporter and host of SCW Magazine Brock Kaufman - SCW Ring Announcer and co-host of SCW Magazine John Melcher - SCW Fusion Face Commentator Spike McNeil - SCW Fusion Heel Commentator Ted Ratigliano - SCW Reporter All these men can be used for interviews or you can use your own reporter.  On ABSOLUTLY no occasion can they be attacked, unless we do it in a card.
  PPV's   There is one PPV a month.  Names are determined for them as the month comes along.  All titles will be defended at the PPV's unless a certain feud prohibits it.
  Executive Committee   Shawn - CEO - [detail withheld]   Paul - President - [detail withheld]   Scooter - V.P. - [detail withheld]   Rules   1. Only created wrestlers are allowed here in SCW.  I want to encourage creativity, and I prefer created wrestlers, so do you best to make one up.  Just don't call him
"Y2K" Jeff Jericho.  That just shows a lack of imagination.
2.   This is an email fed, that will be run like any other, and this site will be where you can come to voice concerns or interact with other e-fed members.
3. There will be one show a week until the angles start to pick up.  4. There will be a pay per view event every 2 months.  This is because I would rather build angles up, and not just have matches for the sake of having them.
5. Foul language is accepted, though not encouraged.  Treat this fed as though it were the real thing.  Learn to censor yourself, and be realistic.
6. Too many spelling errors will ruin a roleplay, please proof read them. 7 . The President, Vice President, and Commissioner and Executive Board are the only voting members in the fed. 7a.  The results of matches are determined by voting on roleplay quality, unless there is an arrangement between handlers and the President, this is the only way to win or lose.  If you don't roleplay, you will lose!
8. RPs are judged on quality, not quantity. 9. The Executive Committee may have wrestlers in the fed, but to ensure fairness, they will not be voting on thier respective matches.
  10. No attacking other wrestlers in rps.  That goes for announcers as well.  If you need to attack anyone, beat on homeless people in your rp.
11. Send all interference to the President.  He will get it out to the right people. 12.
