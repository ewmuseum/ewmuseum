JM:  Welcome back to Fusion.  We have a few words from the SCW's newest member.   <I> (Scene opens on a black screen. After a moment of pure darkness, a small pin point of light appears on the screen. The light slowly expands until it forms a large circle. Inside the circle is the face of a young man, judging by his looks his seems to be in his early twenties. His long, stringy hair, is a strange neon green color, and his hanging over his face, casting his eyes in shadow. His complexion is pale, and his face is expressionless. After a moment, he slowly raises his hand into the light, and pulls the hair out of his eyes, while at the same time raising his head to look into the camera. No one here is safe from the lessons of life. No one is above world, and its my goal to teach each and everyone one of you that simple fact. For too long you've lived in your ivory towers, content to watch the world go by, believing yourselves safe. Alas my friends, your assumed safety has come to an end. Your teacher is here, and his name is "Dark Star" Lance Sullivan.
I've watched you all, I know you all. I know the dirty things you do, when you think no one is looking. I know your deepest, darkest desires, your most base of passions. I know your fears, your weakness, your strengths, everything that makes each and every one of you, who you claim to be. I know the truth.
<I>(Sullivan slowly cranes his neck to the left, letting his hair fall to the right side. Slowly he lowers himself to the ground, and sits with his knees pulled tightly to his chest. He looks into the camera again, after once again pulling his hair back off his face.)</I>
"Dark Star" Lance Sullivan: Those that claim to be most righteous, those that claim to be pure and virtuous, you will be first to feel the burning fury of the darkest star that hangs in the heavens. You will be cleansed of your lies, cleansed mind, body, and spirit. I will teach you, I will cleanse you. Most importantly, I will break you down, until you see the truth.
See that you are no better than any other man. You are NOT above the world, above the harsh lessons of life. You are just like the rest of us, just like me.
I hear you now, laughing. You find my words amusing. You don't believe I can make good on my promises. I assure you my friends, you are wrong. For I AM
"Dark Star" Lance Sullivan! I AM the evil in your hearts. I AM the man that will show you, what you really are, dirty, vile, loathsome creatures. Then and ONLY then, will you understand the lessons of life. When you have been broken at my hands, you will see my light, the light of the Dark Star, and you will revel in it.
<I>(Sullivan slowly lowers his head, as the circle of light begins to close, until finally once again the screen is black.)</I>
