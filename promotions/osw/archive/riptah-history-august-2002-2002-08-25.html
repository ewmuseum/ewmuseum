Riptah — history (August 2002)

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History The 6’4” 290lb monster known as Riptah is as bad-assed as they come. With a mix of power and agility, he is the balanced member of Da Westsidaz. A clean, mostly shaven head and wrap-around sunglasses are a staple look for Riptah that he is most oftenly shown in. Along with his black jeans and Nike Air Estivals, Riptah is usually either wearing a Westsidaz Football Jersey or a white beater. Although he us able to take to the air on occasion, he is better used as the powerhouse of the team, whether it be attacking with his vicious rights and lefts, powerslaming an opponent or bashing someone over the head with a steel chair. Riptah grew up in a pretty poor section of Los Angeles and was born Andrew Aleksander Washington. His parents had decent jobs in a local bar, but six kids is a little much to raise on that kind of salary. Riptah is one of five boys and one girl in the family. His two older brothers are T.J. Washington and Sean Washington. He has a twin brother named Jesse and two younger brothers, Mike and Jordan. His youngest brother Mike is 14 and his oldest brother T.J. is 30. Riptah’s little sister, Cita, is 16. The family was mostly distant, hanging out with their own crowds at school, except for one thing. They all loved wrestling. Sean was always the strongest, followed by Andrew, who was always gunning for his superior brother. Cita spent, and still does, most of her time beating on Mike. Andrew’s school years weren’t anything out of the ordinary until High School, where he really excelled in Football until an untimely suspension after he put his own Quarterback through a table of Gatorade. Afterwards Riptah just DJed or served as a Bouncer until he decided to get into a wrestling school. He got signed by the PWN, met SC and met up with Short Circuit. What more can be elaborated on that? During there time with PWN, however, Riptah met a young reporter there. She worked for a newspaper, writing results of the matches in her daily weekly column. The two hit it off because the woman, Chantal Holden, was also from California, so they a lot of local things. Soon the two started going out, Riptah always referring to her as his ‘little daisy’. After Riptah was comfortable in PCW, him and Daisy become much more close. Riptah finally had a secure job and it was time to celebrate. He takes good care of his mother, sending her gifts weekly and visiting her often. But Riptah was also very busy in his time in PCW. After he took some time off due to nagging back injuries, he bought his own nightclub, the EDGE, which became a very successful hotspot and even was over enough to start a chain of them. Riptah is now back in OSW and not only does he have a lust for Tag Gold, but he likes the idea of starting fresh with 0-0, forgetting about his past single record of no wins and SIX losses.
