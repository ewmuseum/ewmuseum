Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak vs. Doug Troy and Mystery Opponent

2002-03-09
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak vs. Doug Troy and Mystery Opponent Written: March 9, 2002 Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall and is a tag team matchup! Introducing first, representing the Red Army, weighing in at a combined weight of 775 lbs, Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak!!! The Soviet National Anthem as sung by the Russian Red Army Choir plays around the arena as the lights dim down to their usual deep, dark red, occasionally flashing a bright yellow around the room as well. The hulking duo of Stanislav and Walczak emerge from the backstage area with Alexei Ruslan and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang behind them and walk down to the ring to a chorus of boos. Robbie: My heroes, always the fan favorites! Gotta love ‘em! The two Communists climb into the ring and stand facing the entryway, awaiting their mystery opponent and occasionally mentioning something to the other. Henrich: And their opponents, introducing first, weighing in at 230 lbs, DOUG TROY!!! “Brainless” by The Urge plays as Doug Troy walks out onto the entryway and waves to the crowd. He walks down to the ring and stands outside with his arms crossed, waiting for his opponent. Stanislav and Walczak point and yell at Troy, telling him to get in the ring, but Troy pays them no attention as he waits. Nate: Here we go folks, who’s coming down to counteract the communists…… Earl: And his partner, making her OSW debut…. Nate: Uh oh…. Earl: ….weighing in at 133.3 lbs, TEMPEST!!!! Nate: GOOD GAWD!!!! “Battleflag” by Lo-Fidelity All Stars plays over the PA system as the crowd pops hugely for Tempest! Tempest runs out from the backstage area and heads down the entryway. Stanislav and Walczak erupt in disgust….. Nate: TEMPEST IS DOUG TROY’S MYSTERY PARTNER!!! SHE HAS COME TO FIGHT IVAN STANISLAV!! Robbie: What’s the big deal? She’ll hop in the ring and Ivan’ll level her! Not that hard. Earl gets out of the ring as Speedy Riggs slides in… Steven: Gee, I couldn’t see that one coming… Ivan walks over to Riggs and talks with him, giving him orders about the match. Tempest and Troy walk around the ring to their corner while Ivan continues to argue. Walczak heads to his corner and waits. Nate: It appears that Ivan will be starting this match out for The Red Army. Riggs nods in agreement with Ivan and tells the other team to choose who will start out. Troy climbs in the ring and grins at Stanislav. Ivan roars and points at him, roaring obscenities in Russian. The bell rings…. Nate: Here we go folks!! Doug chuckles and reaches back, tagging in Tempest in front of Ivan. Tempest hops over the top rope and lands, waving to Ivan and then blowing him a kiss…. Robbie: LOOK AT DOUG TROY!!! WHAT A FRIGGIN’ COWARD!!! Steven: Boy, she seems to be having a lot of fun…. Ivan roars at her and tells her to leave, but she doesn’t pay attention to him and eggs him on to fight. Ivan shrugs and reaches back, slapping Tom’s hand hard and getting out of the ring. Nate: Ivan isn’t’ going to fight Tempest? Robbie: It’s strategy. Let Tom fight Tempest so that Ivan can save his strength for Doug Troy. It’s simple really. Nate: Yeah, whatever. Tom climbs into the ring and instantly rushes towards Tempest and throws a wild clothesline. Tempest rushes forward at him at the same time and slides between his legs. She pops back to her feet and hits Tom in the back with a spinning heel kick, staggering the Polish Punisher into the corner. Tempest runs and kicks off of Tom’s back, flipping and landing deftly on her feet. Nate: Is it just me, or is she having too much fun in this match!?! Steven: She’s in the ring with two men that hospitalize people and she’s busy having fun! Robbie: Chun Li is going to get her ass kicked, just like she would if I was using A. Honda against her. Nate: What in the hell are you talking about!!! Tom spins and growls at her, going for a lockup. Tempest ducks to the side and hops on the back of Walczak with a sleeper hold. She hangs on his massive back and wrenches the hold. Speedy Riggs goes to check… Nate: C’mon! That’s a legal hold!! Riggs claims that it is a choke and yells for Tempest to break the hold. Ivan claps, Doug protests, and Tempest yells down at Riggs. 1….2…..3…..4….. Reluctantly, Tempest hops off of the back of Tom and turns to yell at Riggs. Walczak uses this to his advantage and grabs Tempest by the back of the neck and the legs, lifting her easily up in the air and tossing her across the ring. Tempest lands hard on her rear end and rolls into the corner. Tom laughs and dusts his hands off. Steven: That’s right! Beat her ass! Show her the man-breasts are better!! Robbie: What the hell are you talking about? Nate: You don’t wanna know. Tom methodically walks over to Tempest and picks her up, setting her up in the corner. He winds back for a knife edge chop and swings, but Tempest ducks at the last moment. Walczak misses her and smacks the back of his hand off of the steel post with a loud “CLANG.” The Crowd: Oooooooooooooooooo!!! Walczak howls in pain. Nate: Youch! That’s gonna hurt! Tempest rolls across the ring and tags in Troy. Tom turns and tags in Stanislav who is now more than happy to get in the ring. The Russian Bear surges into the ring and stomps over to Troy with no concern that he is giving Troy loads of time to prepare. Ivan swings with a hard right hand, missing Troy by inches. Troy ducks to the side and punches Ivan in the stomach which does nothing. Ivan grabs Troy by the neck slides him across the ring. Ivan points and roars at him. Steven: I like when Ivan is angry, it’s exciting. Robbie: Entertaining exciting, or sexual exciting? Steven: A little bit of column A, a LOT of column B! Robbie: Why do I even ask…. Nate: Someone has been watching a little too much Simpsons. Troy hops to his feet and size up The Russian Bear. Ivan brings his arms high in the air and rushes forward in a rage. Troy does the same. Ivan reaches down to grab Troy but Doug leaps vertically, clearing Ivan’s arms! He knees Ivan in his jaw and knocks him backwards! Troy follows up with a running knee lift to the chest and a kick to the gut of Stanislav and locks him in a DDT position! Nate: Doug Troy with a head full of steam. Steven: Does… Nate and Robbie: (simultaneously, interrupting) SHUT THE HELL UP!!! Doug goes for the DDT, but Ivan doesn’t go down. He tries again, but Ivan stays standing. Ivan wraps both his massive arms around Doug’s waist and squeezes, turning Troy into a limp mass. Ivan laughs and picks Doug high in the air with one hand, military pressing him! Robbie: What an amazing physical specimen that Ivan Stanislav is! Ivan presses Doug ten times and dumps him to the mat. Ruslan, Wang, and Riggs clap in admiration. Ivan points to Tempest and then points at the fallen Troy, trying to make a point. Robbie: That’s right!!! Tell that ho who’s boss!!! Nate: She’s not a ho!!! Robbie: $20 says she’s over here blowing me under the table. $50 says I get a half and half. $100 bucks says I get straight sex. Nate: Even is she was a whore, she wouldn’t be charging Atlantic City streetwalker prices. Robbie: How would you know how much streetwalkers in Atlantic City cost? Nate: Your mom told me. Stanislav turns to Troy and runs, kicking him hard in the side and knocking him to the corner. Ivan lifts Doug and picks him up by the neck with two hand. He roars and tosses Doug across the entire ring, letting him land by Tempest. Doug reaches for the tag and it is made. Tempest climbs in the ring but Speedy shakes his head, indicating he did not see a tag. Ivan nods and tells Tempest to get out of the ring! Nate: What the hell? She tagged! We all saw it! We all heard it!! Robbie: Dumas, Speedy has been a referee much longer than us, how are we to know what it is like inside that ring? Nate: I wrestled there!! Robbie: That is not a valid excuse…. Ivan grins and switches with Walczak, not even trying to tag while Doug and Tempest argue with Riggs. Tom grabs Doug from behind and lifts him into the air, bringing him down in a wicked backbreaker! Steven: This is unacceptable! Robbie: And having sex with Boda del Inferno is?? Nate: Point=Decker. Riggs turns and asks Ruslan at ringside if a tag was made. Alexei nods and verifies it, so Riggs accepts the tag, stifling a chuckle. Tempest yells to Speedy but he ignores her. Tom lifts Doug and sends him into the ropes, Doug rebounds into a huge sidewalk slam! Pin by Tom! 1… 2… …kickout at the last moment by Troy! Nate: Hmm…that wasn’t a bit of a quick count…. Tom lifts Doug up by the hair. Doug grabs the Polish Punisher by the side of the head and hits him with a jawbreaker. Robbie: Nice move there by Walczak. DT tries to rally his strength, punching Tom hard in the side of the head and lifting him slowly, Doug barely gets the Punisher up and scoop slams him, falling to the mat himself! Doug clutches his back and rolls over to Tempest and tags her in. She hops in but Riggs shakes his head, not verifying the tag…. Nate: I CANT BELIEVE THIS! HE WILL NOT RECOGNIZE TEMPEST AS THE LEGAL MAN…er….woma…er…WRESTLER!!!! Tom shakes the cobwebs free while Riggs protests. Without an active member in the ring, Troy grabs Riggs and yells at him, instantly getting a tag out of Riggs then. Tempest gets in the ring in a huff, sticking her tongue out at Riggs. Robbie: Unsportsmanlike conduct! DQ!! DQ her!! Nate: I think it was kinda hot!!! Robbie: (singing the Jungle Fever tune) Nate’s got yellow fever…yellow fever…Nate’s got yellow fever…. Tempest lifts Tom up and sends him into the ropes, Tom rebounds into a monkey flip! Amazingly, Tempest is able to heft Tom through the air with her feet, right into Stanislav! Ivan falls from the apron to the ground with a thud. Wang and Ruslan run over to help him whilst Tom bounces off the ropes and lands on the back of his head. Tempest stands there and giggles. Nate: Tempest is in complete control!!! Robbie: Ohhh..she’s gonna get it now! Tempest lifts Tom again and puts him in a headlock, she goes for a bulldog but Tom blocks the attempt and shoves her forward. He follows up with a clothesline, hitting the much smaller Tempest in the back of the head and flattening her! The Crowd: Ooooooooo!!! Nate: That’s gotta hurt the lithe Tempest!! Robbie: Well if she can’t take the heat, she should get her MSG lovin’ ass back in the kitchen!!! Nate: (sighing) Send your complaints directly to Robbie Decker, 1467 North Hillshire… Robbie: (sighing) WHOA!!!!!! Stanislav climbs back on the apron in a huff, extremely angry now. He yells for Tom to tag him, which Tom obliges. Tempest slowly rises to her feet as a shadow falls over her and Ivan Stanislav looms behind her. Robbie: This is gonna be GREAT!! KILL!!!! Nate: LOOOOOOK OUUUUUT!!!! Tempest turns and sees Ivan, waving up at him. Stanislav looks down coldly. In a flash, Tempest slides between Ivan’s legs and jumps up behind him! She leaps on his back and plants her feet on his back and holds his suspender straps to stay on, perched on his massive back!! Nate: AAHAAAHAA!!! Robbie: Not like she hasn’t been in this position, except for from the…. Nate: (interrupting) FAMILY SHOW!!! The crowd is in a frenzy. Ivan roars and whirls around in an attempt to dislodge Tempest from his back. He swings and tries to pull her off, but only manages to pull the front end of his pants up. He roars again and eyes the turnbuckle. Like a locomotive, Stanislav backs up to the turnbuckle to crush Tempest, but as he nears she climbs to his shoulders and leaps off. The Russian Bear SLAMS into the turnbuckle, shaking the entire ring, but not hitting Tempest. Nate: She’s just too quick for him!!!! Steven: I heard he’s just too quick for her, wink wink, nudge nudge… Robbie: CAPITALIST PROPAGANDA!!! ALL OF IT!!! Ivan growls as Tempest runs towards him and leaps, he easily catches her in the air and tosses her across the ring. Tempest lands on her side hard, winding her. Nate: IVAN SHOULD BE ASHAMED OF HIMSELF!!! He stomps over and picks her up, sending her into the ropes and catching her, tossing her vertically into the air and letting her land hard on her stomach! Steven: Beat her up Ivan! Teach her a lesson!!! Nate: OH COME ON!!! THIS IS AN OUTRAGE!!! Robbie: SHE WANTED TO BE OUT THERE, SO LET HER GET BEATEN DOWN!!!! Ivan chuckles to himself and watches Tempest tag in Troy. Ivan roars loudly and absorbs two punches from Troy and clubs him over the head, instantly sending him stomach first to the mat. Ivan hefts Troy up onto the turnbuckle and faces him towards the crowd. He wheels back and BLASTS Troy in the back of the head, knocking him into the guard rail from the top turnbuckle!! The Crowd: Ooooooooooooooo!!! Nate: DOUG TROY IS DEAD!! Robbie: IF WE’RE LUCKY!!!! Stanislav nods to Walczak and starts to get out of the ring. The Polish Punisher charges into the ring straight at Tempest, who stands wide eyed on the apron. Tempest leaps to the top rope and hops over Tom. She lands on her feet just as Tom rebounds off the topes and ducks a clothesline. She runs to the opposite side of the ring and springs into a back elbow off the middle rope, connecting with Tom in the gut and doubling him over. Steven: Shouldn’t Speedy Riggs have thrown out the match? Speedy Riggs covers his eyes, seemingly blinded by something in the middle of the ring…. Nate: I think that answers your question…. Tempest tries to DDT Tom, but he easily stays standing. Tom wraps his arms around the waist of Tempest and tosses her high over his shoulders, sending her landing hard onto her back! The crowd erupts into a frenzy of boos. Tom turns and kicks her hard in the side, knocking her over, and leaps into the air for a legdrop…. Nate: Alright…she’s dead…. …BUT no one is home! Tempest rolls to the side just as Tom connects, rear first with the mat! The crowd explodes. Nate: TEMPEST IS LIKE GREASED LIGHTNING IN THERE!!!! Steven My…. Nate and Robbie: (simultaneously) SHUT THE HELL UP!!!! Tempest hops to the top turnbuckle and leaps, knee dropping and rolling off of Tom and standing with a smirk on her face. Nate: TEMPEST WITH THE HUGE MOVE THERE!!! Outside the ring, Stanislav sends Doug into the ring steps, crushing his knee against them and scattering them. Doug groggily stands and leans against the steel post. Ivan roars and punches at Doug, missing and crunching his massive hand against the steel with a loud “DONG!!!” The Crowd: OOOOOOOoooooooooooooo!!! Ivan roars as the steel post buckles slightly…. Robbie: The STRENGTH of Stanislav!! Nate: Yeah, and look at him now!! Ivan grabs his hand and howls loudly, blood gushing from his knuckles. Doug drives a high knee into the face of Ivan, staggering him backwards. Doug grabs Ivan in a suplex position…. Nate: Doug is going for a suplex!!! Doug grabs a hold of Ivan’s suspenders and pulls hard, straining his back. Ivan’s feet leave the floor and then thunder back to the ground… Inside the ring, Tempest sits atop the shoulders of Walczak, slapping him on the forehead. Steven: You know, Tom Walczak is not the kind of guy to joke around with…. Tom stops and falls backwards, trying to crush her, but Tempest easily hops off of him causing Tom to crack his head on the mat! Amidst all this, Speedy Riggs continues to palm at his eyes. Troy tries to lift Ivan again, screaming loudly when suddenly, Alexei Ruslan and Communist Party Member Wang charge from either side and drive their shoulders into the sides of Doug, collapsing him. Ivan stands and nods to his friends and climbs into the ring. Ruslan and Wang put the boots to Doug…. Nate: Tempest is alone with Stanislav and Walczak!! Steven: She is living a dream of mine!!! Robbie: I think I’m gonna be sick!!! Ivan gets in the ring and roars to Speedy, who blinks and gets his bearings. He starts to count for Doug… 1..2..3..4.. Nate: Isn’t that kinda fast? Robbie: Nah. 5..6..7.. Doug rolls over and trips Wang, spooking Alexei and making him give up the attack… 8..9.. Doug quickly slides into the ring just in time and hops on the back of Ivan. Ivan staggers forward into Tempest and sandwiches her in the corner!! The Crowd: Ooooooooooo!!!! Nate: The combined weight of Ivan Stanislav and Doug Troy: 630 lbs JUST CRUSHED TEMPEST!! Tempest falls out of the corner while Doug punches Ivan in the back of the head. Tom grabs Doug by the waist of his pants and peels him off of Stanislav. Suddenly the crowd explodes into a thunderous pop. Steven: UH OH!! LOOK!!! From the backstage area, Meanstreak charges down the ring in a hurry… Nate: MEANSTREAK IS HERE! HE’S NOT WAITING FOR SUNDAY!!!! HE WANTS STANISLAV AND DT, RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!!! Alexei and Wang scream to Tom and Ivan, who turn and notice Meanstreak. Ivan, Tom, and Speedy, exchange glances and bail out of the ring, making themselves scarce…. Nate: LOOK ‘EM RUN!!! WHAT A BUNCH OF SISSIES!!!! Robbie: He who fights and runs away…. Nate: SHUT UP DECKER!!!! Meanstreak slides into the ring and lifts Troy with ease. He spins Troy around and glares into DT’s eyes before throwing him into the ropes and nailing him with Chaotic Peace!! Nate: CHAOTIC PEACE ON DOUG TROY!!! MEANSTREAK IS OUT FOR BLOOD!!! In the background, the entire Red Army can be seen running up the entryway at a lightning pace. Back in the ring, Tempest slowly rises, not sure what has happened as Meanstreak looms over her. Nate: NOOOOOO!!! HE WOULDN’T!!!!! Robbie: I CAN NOT EVEN CONDONE THIS!!!! Tempest blinks and look up at Meanstreak. The crowd is in a state of frenzied state of anticipation. Nate: DON’T DO IT STREAK!!! SHE’S AN INNOCENT BYSTANDER IN ALL OF…. Nate’s words trail off as Meanstreak grabs Tempest by her throat and squeezes hard. She flails weakly, trying to break the hold… The crowd immediately explodes into a thunderous rage. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! DON’T DO IT!!! Robbie: I CAN NOT EVEN ABIDE THIS!!!! Nate: DOUG TROY IS OUT COLD!!!! NO ONE IS OUT HERE TO SAVE HERE!!!! In the aisle, the crowd’s sudden outburst of fury causes Ivan Stanislav to stop at the entryway to see what happened. Nate: WHAT? STANISLAV HAS STOPPED!!! The rest of the army retreat farther before they realize The Russian Bear is not there. Ivan roars to Meanstreak…. Nate: FOR GOT SAKES MAN, FOR ONCE IN YOUR BLACK HEARTED LIFE DO THE RIGHT THING!!!! Meanstreak glares out from the ring, down the aisle towards Stanislav, all the while holding the helplessly flailing Tempest up high in the sky by her neck. In the aisle Ivan begins to move towards the ring to stop Meanstreak, but Alexei, Wang, Riggs, and Tom grab him and stop him. Robbie: IVAN WANTS TO FIGHT, BUT THE RED ARMY IS HOLDING HIM BACK!!!! Nate: CAN ANYONE REALLY HOLD BACK STANISLAV THOUGH?!?! HOW REAL IS HIS INTENT TO HELP!??! Ivan roars and points at Meanstreak who still holds Tempest in his hands. Nate: IVAN IS TRYING TO HELP HER, BUT THEY WON’T LET HIM!!! Alexei grabs Ivan by the jaw and talks to him. Ivan nods slowly and looks up at Meanstreak for a moment. Stanislav then begins backing up the ramp slowly, towards the back, with his eyes never leaving the ring. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Robbie: MORE SNATCH WHERE SHE CAME FROM!!! GOOD SHOW COMRADE!!! Nate: GOD DAMN IT, THAT FRICKING DIRTBAG HAD A SHOT TO SHOW HE WAS HUMAN BUT CHOSE HIS OWN SAFETY OVER TEMPESTS!!! HE HAS TO BE THE BIGGEST DIRTBAG EVER TO GRACE A WRESTLING RING!!! In the ring, Meanstreak roars out at Ivan to come get him, but Ivan continues to backup the ramp. The crowd is enraged. Debris is not only flying into the ring, but at the Red Army as they back up the ramp. In the ring, Meanstreak uses his off hand to make a throat cutting motion, then the Monster puts Tempest up across his shoulders, with her face and bossom facing the ceiling. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Robbie: MEANSTREAK HAS TEMPEST SET UP FOR AT DEATH’S DOOR!!!! Nate: SOMEONE HAS TO SAVE HER!!! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOOSE OUR FEED!!!! Ivan starts to walk towards the ring again, but Walczak grabs him, while Alexei slaps him across his chiseled jaw and tries to talk sense to him. In the ring, however, it’s a different story, as The Monster They Call Meanstreak roars, then quickly and powerfully rotates his shoulders and wait, planting Tempest onto the canvas, face and torso first via the deadly reverse Death Valley Driver he calls At Death’s Door. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Tempest bounces sickeningly off the mat, her neck twisting violently, and lays, not moving. Streak turns and roars to the crowd as Doug Troy and Tempest lay crushed at his feet. Nate: TEMPEST IS HURT!!! SOMEONE GET THE PARAMEDICS OUT HERE!!! Robbie: SHE SHOULD STAYED IN THE KITCHEN!!! Nate: MEANSTREAK HAS GONE TOO (BLEEPING) FAR!!! HE HAS LOST ALL CONTROL!!!! A fury filled rage rises from the crowd, as a hailstorm of trash and other objects fly into the ring. Extra security rushes out to beat back fans who have jumped the guardrail in an attempt to get to the ring!!! Nate: MEANSTREAK HAS CROSSED THE LINE!! STANISLAV IS AN UTTER COWARD AND LADIES AND GENTLEMAN, QUITE FRANKLY, THIS ENTIRE MAIN EVENT HAS CULMINATED INTO PERHAPS THE MOST DISGUSTING SERIES OF EVENTS I HAVE EVER HAD THE DISPLEASURE OF WITNESSING!!!! Robbie: GET THE HELL OUT OF HERE!!! IT (BLEEPING) RULED!!! Nate: I HOPE TO GOD DOUG TROY IS NOT HURT SO HE CAN LAY A HURTING ON BOTH STANISLAV AND MEANSTREAK TOMORROW NIGHT!!! WE ARE OUT OF TIME!!! WE WILL SEE YOU TOMORROW NIGHT AT CROWNING GLORY!!! GET ME THE HELL OUT OF HERE!!! The sound of Nate throwing down his headset in disgust is heard, as the camera heads back up to the ring, where a wild eyed Meanstreak stands over the fallen Tempest, batting away debris and keeping paramedics at bay….as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears on the lower, central portion of the screen…..as the scene fades to black.
