Heresian Bloodlust & Caleb vs. The Red Army & Loyal Communist Party Member Wang

2002-06-04
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Heresian Bloodlust & Caleb vs. The Red Army & Loyal Communist Party Member Wang Written: 06/04/2002 Nate: (sighing) I doubt that…but we will find out soon enough the identity of said individual, but first we have to head to the ring for our next match of the evening, which promises to be something! Robbie: Something alright! Tonight we are going to see Cain and Hessian whoop up on those damn sell outs Krieg and Walczak! Steven: Don’t forget about Wang! We all know I never do. Tee Hee! There is dead air for 1…2…3…4…5… Nate: That’s right! Tonight, Krieg and Walczak will be accompanied to the ring by the Loyal Communist Party Member Wang and they will be taking on OSW new Tag Team Champions, Heresian Bloodlust with their manager, Caleb! If Walczak and Krieg win tonight they are going to get the chance for the belts at our next Pay per View next month, Head on Collision!!! Robbie: No way in hell! Those damn sell outs are going to choke! There isn’t Tag Team in this fed that has the power and pent up anger that Hessian and Azrael Cain have! Nate: Well, let’s take it to Earl who is waiting inside the ring for the introductions! The camera shot changes to Earl who is waiting in the ring with the mic to make the teams introductions. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, boys and girls, children of all ages, this next match is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring first, Representing the Red Army… Backstage Voice: Hold up! Heh-Heh. Who the hell do you think you are? Heh-Heh! Those communists pieces of crap don’t deserve to be announced first! Heh-Heh! Nate: That voice sounds awfully familiar in a creepy kind of way! Robbie: That sounds like… Suddenly, Caleb appears from the backstage area with a microphone in his hand. Caleb: Shut the hell up Henrich! Heh. There is no way that we are going to let you have those damn soviet sons of bitches come to the ring before the best warriors in all of the OSW! Heh-Heh. So shut up and let me take this one! Heh-Heh-Heh. Nate: Persistent little bigger ain’t he? Robbie: Ha! Steven: He usually gives me the creeps, but he just shut Earl up, so why am I complaining? Caleb: All right all of monosyllabic Neanderthals out there! Are you ready to witness the best tag team combination to ever grace this realm? Heh-Heh-Heh. The fans begin to boo wildly! Caleb: Well, even though you aren’t worthy enough to witness the carnage that they are going to deliver tonight, they’re coming out anyway to get some revenge! Heh-Heh-Heh. Nate: Man does that little guy have a mouth on him. “Carmina Burana” begins to play on the PA system as the Hessian and Azrael Cain enter from the area holding their belts high in the air for everyone to see. Caleb: Here they are folks! Heh! Azrael Cain and Hessian! Your Tag Team Champions! Heresian Bloodlust!!!! All three of them make their way down the ring as the fans boo wildly at the Tag Team champs. Hessian and Cain climb the apron and enter the ring over the top ropes. Caleb climbs through the ropes. Caleb walks over to Earl and chases him out of the ring. Steven: Look at Earl run from that hobo! BOCA~!!! Caleb: Alright, now let’s get our next victims out here. Heh-Heh-Heh. Bring out Tom Watchamacallit, Michel Krog and their little Wang! Heh-Heh-Heh. Oh…and don’t play their annoying little song! Who wants to hear the music from a dying country and an already dead government! Heh-Heh-Heh! Nate: That’s Walczak, Krieg, and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang you little gremlin! Robbie: HA!! HA!! That was great!!! HA!! HA!!! Steven: WANG~!!!! Michel Krieg, Tom Walczak, and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang appear on the entryway, but “Carmina Burana” continues to play as the make their way down to the ring to the mixed cheers of the fans. Robbie: Listen to those wretched people cheer for these sellouts! The people are so Fickle! Walczak, Krieg and Wang climb to the apron and enter the ring and begin to stair down Cain, Hessian, and Caleb. Nate: It seems that the Red Army Members didn’t think that entrance was as funny as you did Decker! Robbie: Serves them right! Damn sellouts! Led by their damn sellout leader, Ivan Selloutislav!!! HA!!!! The Goth, Hessian remains in the ring as Cain and Caleb climb to the outside apron. While on the other side of the ring The German War Machine, Michel Krieg stands waiting for the bell to start the match. Walczak and Wang are on the outside of the ropes in their corner. Nate: Well, here we go folks!! Krieg and Hessian charge at one another and clothesline each other. Neither man budges. They stare at one another and bounce off the ropes again and go for another clothesline, but again, nothing! Both men stare at each other for a long time and bounce off of the ropes again. Hessian goes high for a clothesline, Krieg ducks low under him and turns quickly. He hits Hessian with a forearm and staggers him. Krieg rushes forward with a low knee, followed by a quick suplex, sending the massive Hessian crashing to the mat! Nate: Tremendous move by Michel Krieg!! Krieg gets to his feet just as Hessian does. Both men lock up in the center of the ring again. Hessian suplex’s Krieg this time and stands quickly. Krieg sits up, only to get kicked in the side of the head and knocked back down to the mat. Hessian bends down and lifts Krieg. He sends The German War Machine into the ropes. Krieg bounces back. Hessian back body drops Krieg to the mat again and tags in Cain! Steven: That’s a lotta meat in the ring…mmm. Robbie: Shut the hell up, Steven. Cain climbs into the ring and lifts Krieg in a waistlock. He pulls back and slams his forearm into the back of Krieg, knocking him to a knee. He pulls Krieg up and hits his lower back again and lifts him, and for a third time, nails his lower back. Krieg falls to his hands and knees and clutches at his back. Nate: Azrael Cain being very methodical about his attacks. Cain bounces off the ropes and leaps into the air, dropping both knees into the back of Krieg and knocking him into the mat. Krieg roars in pain while Azrael lifts Krieg up and hits an atomic drop! Krieg staggers forward and reaches out for Walczak, but Cain drops him with a hard clothesline to the back of the head. Steven: If Cain keeps that up, Krieg’ll be knocked out of commission for the rest of this match! Robbie: I don’t like it when you analyze stuff, Steven. Nate: Why, you don’t like it when a flaming, openly gay homosexual shows you up? Robbie: Shut up, Dumas. Cain yells at Walczak and then points at Krieg, who clambers back to his feet. Cain and Krieg lock up again. Cain goes for a suplex, but Krieg blocks it. Cain tries for another, but again, Krieg blocks it. Krieg lets out a roar and lifts Cain into a vertical suplex and drops him into a brainbuster! Krieg grabs his back and falls forward, just barely tagging Walczak! Cain gets to his feet slowly. Walczak rushes into the ring and spears Cain hard in the back, knocking him to the mat! Walczak continues on his course and SLAMS into Caleb, knocking him off the apron into the guard rail! Nate: Walczak just plowed through Caleb! Hessian grabs at Tom, but Tom bats his hand away and slams his head into the turnbuckle. This gives Cain just enough time to spin Tom around, kick him in the gut, and DDT him into the mat with authority! Robbie: Heh, well, there goes the momentum! Cain lifts Walczak up cockily. Tom grabs Cain by the side of the head and puts his head under Cain’s jaw and drops to his knee, hitting him with a perfect jawbreaker! Cain staggers backwards and grabs at his jaw. Krieg shoves his knee between the middle and top rope, hitting Cain in the back. Walczak grabs Cain and sends him sternum first into the turnbuckle. Cain hits hard and stumbles backwards into the arms of Walczak. Walczak puts him in a full nelson and lifts him into the air and drops him in a huge full nelson slam! Walczak goes for a pin. 1… 2… kickout by Cain! Nate: It’s going to take more than that to keep the Marked Beast down. Robbie: The Red Army should be renamed the Pink Army, because Tempest has thoroughly denutted them all. Walczak lifts Cain and knocks him back to the mat with a short arm clothesline. Walczak dusts his hands off and walks back to his corner. He rears back to tag in Krieg, but Wang slaps him on the back, more for moral support. However the referee considers it a tag!! Walczak slaps Krieg’s hand, but it isn’t recognized! Nate: Wang accidentally tagged himself in!! Wang climbs into the ring and looks around as Cain reaches forward and grabs him and sends him into the turnbuckle! Wang hits the turnbuckle and puffs his cheeks out as the massive Azrael Cain rushes after him for a body avalanche! Robbie: HA HA!! KILL HIM!! Wang screams and rolls out of the way at the last possible moment. Cain hits the turnbuckle with tremendous force and staggers out of the corner. Wang rolls up Cain!! Nate/Robbie/Steven: WHOA!! 1… 2… Cain kicks out, causing Wang to roll backwards into the center of the ring! Nate: What power!!! Caleb reaches under the top rope for a tag, begging Cain to tag him in. Wang looks to Walczak and Krieg, but they tell him to keep fighting. Wang nods… Cain tags in the hobo, who scurries into the ring. Caleb points and laughs at Wang. Wang’s eyes widen in anger! Nate: Check that out! Wang is angry!! Caleb rushes towards Wang wildly. Wang lowers himself into a martial arts position and easily sidesteps out of the way, sending Caleb crashing into the turnbuckle. Wang kicks forward suddenly and nails Caleb in the back of the head, sending Caleb hitting the top turnbuckle with his forehead! Wang lets out some sort of loud scream and wraps his arm around Caleb and takes him down with authority!! Robbie: I can’t believe it! Wang can fight?! Nate: Well, he is from the military! Caleb rolls over, already winded. Wang lift him up, only to get a low blow for his trouble. Wang drops to his knees as his slanted eyes cross. Caleb laughs and punches Wang in the side of the head and knocks him over. Caleb grabs Wang’s legs and drops a knee to the groin. Walczak lets out a roar and climbs into the ring…. Steven: Here it goes! Hessian sees Walczak get in the ring and gets in himself. He charges at Walczak and spears him, sending both men flying between the top and middle ropes to the floor!! Nate: OH MY GOD!!! Steven: Yeah, they’re dead. Walczak and Hessian beat on one another instead! Inside the ring, Krieg and Cain follow suit and get into the ring and rush at one another. Caleb and Wang dive in opposite directions, not wanting to be caught in the center of the collision. Krieg hits Cain with a forearm to the nose, sending blood spurting from Cain’s nose! Krieg backs Cain into the ropes and flips him over the top rope and climbs out onto the apron. Krieg leaps from the apron and double axehandle’s Cain over the head! Nate: It’s pandemonium folks!! Caleb and Wang, the two legal men who spent the entire time of the above events hiding, get to their feet. The two men lock up in a conventional manner. Caleb stomps on Wang’s foot and swings wide. Wang ducks and thrusts his palm into the sternum of Caleb, winding him! Nate: Wang using a bit of Chinese kung fu there!!! Robbie: Wake me up when its over. On the entryway, Hessian grabs Walczak in a chokeslam and slams him into the steel grating. By the announce position, Krieg sends Cain headfirst into the steel steps! And inside the ring, Wang bounces off the ropes, leaps, and delivers a wicked spinkick to the side of Caleb’s jaw! Teeth spew from the hobo’s mouth!! Robbie: Don’t tell me…is that the crowd?? Crowd: WANG!! WANG!! WANG!! Robbie: The entire crowd is full of fags!! Steven: I kind of like it!! Caleb spits another tooth out and stumbles around the ring. Wang kicks him hard behind the knee, pinning his leg to the ground, and stomps over the back of his head! Nate: Jesus Christ! Wang’s wicked!! Hessian, meanwhile, powerbombs a now semi-unconscious Walczak into the entryway. Cain gains the upper hand and tosses Krieg over the guardrail into the crowd and follows him in. Wang kicks Caleb over, who doesn’t move. He puts a foot on Caleb’s chest and salutes the crowd, who salutes the Chinese Communist back! 1… 2… 3!!! Winners: Tom Walczak/Michel Krieg/Loyal Communist Party Member Wang Nate: The Red Army has done it!!! I can’t believe WANG got the pin!! Robbie: NOOOOOO!! Now we must suffer through the Red Army vs Heresian Bloodlust at Head On Collision!! Nate: Folks, while security gets this situation in order, lets head to a commercial!!! Hessian continues to boot Walczak while Krieg hits Cain with a folding chair around the fans. Security pours out everywhere. All the while, Wang showboats in the ring, flexing, and generally making a fool out of himself.
