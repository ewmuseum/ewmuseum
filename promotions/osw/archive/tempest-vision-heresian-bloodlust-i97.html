Tempest-Vision: Heresian Bloodlust

2002-02-03
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tempest-Vision: Heresian Bloodlust Written: 02/03/2002 The scene reopens to reveal a camera seems to be bobbing its way down the hall in the back of the arena as a voice narrates dramatically... Narrator- Ladies and Gentlemen for the very first time, you will be witness to the secret rituals of OSW's own Satanic cult. These rituals have been guarded jealously by their practitioners, hidden from the eyes of the public due to the belief that they grant the practitioners great power. The camera suddenly turns and an awkward view of Tempest's face comes into focus. She grins and speaks. Tempest- So let us delve into the forbidden realms of... Heresian Bloodlust. Tempest stares at the camera with a wide eyed gaze, moving it toward her and away in a zoom-type gesture. Then the view switches to the door of a locker room. Almost silently, the door opens to reveal a view of Hessian that not many have seen. With one hand down the front of his pants and one down the back, he squirms, apparently adjusted something. A snap is heard and he relaxes as if much relieved. Minion imitates the move, but apparently is adjusting a pink thong inside his appropriately gothic tights. Tempest- Ahem... oh Hessy-poo! Hessian's eyes widen to the size of dinner plates and his head cocks to the side. As he turns around Minion the Imp screams like a banshee and tries to hide his pink fuzzy thong from the view of the camera. Minion- Who...what.........why.........FECK OFF!!!' shouts Minion, as he bursts off past the camera into hiding. Hessian meanwhile fidgets with whatever it is he's doing, and turns around smoothly to Tempest. Hessian- So.........Ivan's woman is a dab hand with the old camera eh? Filmed anything bigger than me in his company or is it all Imp-sized in the boudoir? Tempest- Well, I dunno, looks to me like that was a prop adjustment going on there, and here I thought I was gonna get some juicy Satanic ritual stuff. Don't you drink blood, or raise demons or something interesting? Hessian- Only on the weekends, m'dear. And having the world's most satanic jock strap is nothing to find funny... Tempest- Ok maybe, but still... Why bother being evil Satanists if you can't even sacrifice a few virgins on a Saturday night? A sigh emanates from behind the camera and it shakes from side to side a bit. Tempest- This is not gonna be a big ratings getter. Hessian releases a deep guttural sigh and turns to an old, grey locker beside him. With a feeble THUMP! the locker becomes ajar, and as it opens fully, the mangled, twisted corpse of a young female appears, looking blankly at the camera, whilst Hessian further fixes his crotch. Hessian- Ratings.........an Imp in a pink fuzzy thong and this *points to the corpse* won't get you ratings? Then only me beating Meanstreak will.........oh wait......... *Dr. Evil pinky & smirk* Tempest nods, the camera moving up and down a bit. Tempest- Ok, ok, not bad. Although I suspect Imp and his thong has sent the entire female audience into convulsions. Meanstreak huh? Ok, I'll go interview him. The camera swings wildly out of focus and jostles as if she's skipping as she exits…
