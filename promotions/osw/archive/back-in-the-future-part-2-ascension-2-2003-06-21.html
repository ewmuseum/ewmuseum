Back In The Future (Part 2) (Ascension #2)

by 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst

What this is The OSE site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#76) by 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst, dated 21 June 2003, written from Washington D.C, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
OOC - Excuse any bad grammar or spelling. My computer messed up beyond repair this week and this is being written from a PC without Microsoft Office. -----=====|=====----- Revenge is sweet. But this revenge will cancel the action in the first place making me the greatest vengence-making guy in the Universe! Six Finger Cisco, making a name for himself by picking up cheap victories against the greatest technical wrestler in the Universe, will soon find his whole world upside-down. For all the cheap victories he gained will be wiped out, erased from history thanks to the fabulous, magnificent, exceptional brain inside my head. You are nothing Cisco, and your extra digit will not save you from this vengence, for I have travelled back to before you were even aware of our conflict and defeated you before it even happened! Ascension will be mine for the taking, no other imbecile in the tournament has anything on me. I am Exceptional Dave Hurst, the greatest technical wrestler in the Universe! -----=====|=====----- In the last chapter, I had come across a genie, disguised as a software pirate, who could grant me any wish I pleased. He just had to download them from the internet. So i asked for a time machine and that's exactly what I got, only it wasn't a time travelling machine, but a grandfather clock, which could only tell the time. So I asked for a time travelling machine, and I got one, disguised as a red telephone box, this machine was a tardis time travelling machine. Although, I'm still not sure what a tardis is. Anyway, after whacking a load of buttons and pressing the enter key to go. The machine had a slight pause before violently shaking and sending me through time. I arrived at my destination, only when I arrived I found myself standing at exactly the same place, except for a slight breeze. When I asked the genie why it didn't work, he explained to me that I only travelled through time by about a month and a bit, and that nothing much in the scenery would have changed in that time. So to check I decided to ask people of the date. After people telling me to 'Fuck off!' to 'Get lost, you freak!' and a small girl running for her dear life, I realised that it was not, in fact, a light breeze, but my clothes had vanished! The genie explained that I travelled and not my clothes, so I ran for cover in the form of a small alleyway, which is where I am now. -----=====|=====----- 'I don't fucking believe this!' I murmer to myself as I shuffle through side alleys wearing nothing but the sheets of newspaper that I hold over my genitalia with one hand and over my rear end with the other. Where can I get some clothes? I rush through the alley and turn a right corner. Without realising it, I run straight into something and end up on my arse. Looking upwards at the huge object I have just run into, I realise the sheets of paper have flown from my private parts and are blown away. In front of me is the biggest arsed bastard I've ever seen in my life, since that Azala Zameer anyway. 'Erm... sorry?' I say in an innocent please-don't-hurt-me kind of voice. As I take a good enough look at the guy, he appears to have a black number tatooed down his arm. He is wearing a torn up strait jacket so that his arms can be freed. You know what that means don't you...? ... ... HE'S A FUCKING ESCAPED CONVICT! A devious smile beams across the face of the convict as he slowly walks towards me, I try and crawl backwards away from him then find my back against a wall. He approaches me with his arms held out ready to grab me. Using my exceptional cunning, I leap to my feet and hit him with a swift kick to the gonads before rolling past him and running for my life. Running down the alley, the convict is chasing after me, slowly though, but getting there. I see a light at the end of the alley and decide to run for it. The light envelops my surrounding as I run out of the alley and back into the public eye. I am safe now. That convict can't touch me while I'm out here. Especially with the police car that is up the street with two police officers on their radios standing outside them. I stop and start to walk slowly. There are a group of nuns walking past me, I give them a slow wave. As I do so they all turn and see me. 'HEATHEN~!' they scream at the tops of their voices as they all make a run for me. Oh shit, I forgot! I'm naked! This is the point where I run for my life. Running past the alley that I was in earlier, the convict notices me (as you tend to do when a naked person runs past) and chases after me too. So I'm being chased down the street by a convict who wants a bit of arse, closely followed behind by a bunch of angry nuns, armed with bibles, who want to castrate me. I can hear the feint sound of a nun screaming 'Eric warned us of people like you!' as I pass the police car. They see the convict and immediately get into action. They finish their donuts and get in the car and chase after us. This is not good. I'm in the middle of Washington D.C, completely naked, being chased by a convict, a group of nuns and a police car, complete with 2 fat-arsed bastards. I run past a bunch of shops, Tim Horton's slows the police down for a moment as they dribble. As does the Starbucks. The 5th Baptist church of Washington slows down the nuns for a moment, but the convict is closing on me. I pass Big Mouth's Burger Bar and as I do the door flies open, smacking me in the head. Ooh! Everything is blurry, the convict is getting closer, some fat guy steps out of the burger bar with a tray of burgers stacked high. The convict runs into the burgers and they fly out in all directions. Who is that fat man? What did he want with about 200 burgers? That's when I realise that it is, in fact, ex-PCW superstar The Overweighter, that had just stepped out. This explains everything as the guy is the largest superstar in wrestling. Unfortunately, the collision with the door has done a lot more damage than was once thought, the blurred vision gets worst as I slowly lose consciousness. I can just see Overweighter pummeling on the convict as everything fades away... -----=====|=====----- Expecting to be in a hospital bed, being looked after by lovely nurses, imagine my surprise as I turn and find my back lying on a rock hard matress and a face that is certainly not a nurse. It's the convict from earlier! I'm sure he has the same aspirations in here that I hoped the nurses did, but that's not what I have in mind with this guy. I'm not a poof ya know! The drool that eminates from the convicts mouth is sickening, not to mention shit-scary. I'm sure he's just waiting for a good moment to pounce. He leans forward as if that moment is about to arrive. 'Sir, there's some people here to meet you' a police officer says. 'YES! Let them in! Let them in!' I scream in desperation. Thank God for that! I thought the convict was going to grab me then! Who is it, I wonder? 'HEATHEN~!' Oh no, it's the bloody nuns from earlier! The nuns flood into the jail room and line up next to the bars. The convict still hasn't taken his eyes off me and is still drooling at my naked body. 'How dare you defile the ladies of christ?!' the nuns scream. 'You... you... you sick freak!' The nuns start humming and getting out crosses and waving them at me. This looks more like a voodoo curse than the usual goings on of innocent nuns! They're EVIL! Looking to my right, I see the convict is moving ever-closer to me, the nuns are doing their spells. As this happens the police officer comes back into the room, the nuns hide their crosses behind their backs and pretend to pray and the convict wipes the drool from his mouth. The police officer gives a probing look into the room that no malpractice is taking place before saying, 'Mr. Hurst, there is another visitor for you.' 'YES! Let them in!' I scream at the top of my voice 'Let everyone in! Please!' Stepping into the room is The Overweighter. He takes one look into the room, points at me and shouts 'YOU MADE ME DROP MY BURGERS~!' 'It was him I swear!' I scream, pointing at the convict. The convict is getting closer, the nuns are cursing me again and The Overweighter is yanking the bars back and forth trying to get in. All this is a bit much for me as my vision blurs once again and I fall to the floor. -----=====|=====----- 'Leave me alone!' I scream at the top of my voice as I am awaken by someone. Looking around me I realise I'm in a hospital bed and am awaken by a very hot-looking nurse. 'YES! IT WAS ALL A FUCKING DREAM!' I scream in delight as the nurse taps my shoulder trying to interrupt me. 'Not exactly, Mr. Hurst' she says 'you were in the cell room, but you feinted as the police officer came back in the room, apparently the convict had a visitor. The police officer saw you'd feinted and you were brought to the local hospital.' 'But I'm okay!' I say, as I look to the side of me and see who's in the opposite bed. 'SHIT!' It's the convict! I get out of my bed and run for my life, out of the room and away. TO BE CONTINUED...
