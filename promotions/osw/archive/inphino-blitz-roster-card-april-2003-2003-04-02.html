Inphino Blitz — roster card (April 2003)

2 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Inphino Blitz Division: Wrestling Status: Active Real Name: Classified Birthdate: Classified Hometown: Sado Island, Japan Height: 6'3 Weight: 230 lbs. Nicknames: Miyabi (Elegant, referring to himself as elegant because of his elegant way of life), Sorrow Allignment: Heel Entrance Music: 'No pains No gains' (N-Type) by Malice Mizer 1The lights lower, flashing dimly with black lights. A heavy drum begins to boom through the arena, seconds later the sound of an ethereal harpsichord climbing to a peak in the background. As soon as it peaks, “N.p.s N.g.s (N-Type)” by Malice Mizer roars through the speakers. Inphino steps out from backstage, stopping on stage for a moment to stare out at the masses. He cuts two fingers across his throat palm facing out before walking down towards the ring. When he enters, he turns around in the ring, his arms out at his sides with his head tilted back and eyes closed. Then he stops in the center of the ring to bow to the audience deeply…before throwing his head back and letting out a eerie screech, his hands clutching the sides of his head. -=- 2A raspy whisper cuts through the arena: “No pains…No gains…” DIE GAME!! “N.p.s N.g.s (N-Type) by Malice Mizer blazes over the speakers as Inphino comes into the darkened arena with blood-chilling scream emptying from his lungs. Clutching his head, he drops down to his knees before his head lowers to the ground. He stops screaming, looking up with a sadistic smile on his face. Blitz crawls a few feet down the ramp before hopping up and walking to the ring. Information Combat Style: Inphino’s main style is called the Zero Technique/ Mu Shiki, which is no form, no style. Unorthodox, unpredictable methods of martial arts combat unable to be read because of the unstable mind frame Inphino has relapsed into at this time. Kicks are thrown out with full force and speed, and hand strikes and slicing and swinging, slinging his hands in a harsh swatting motion at the opponent in a reckless assault. Inphino now adapts to his opponents’ styles with the following mixtures of martial arts he has devised through his relentless training, the three main sub-forms of the Zero Technique (there is no aerial style because Inphino uses aerial moves for the situation no matter what style; same with counters and multiple target situations). Forms are interchangeable during a bout: Shogeki Style: this is Inphino’s main offensive style. While in this style, he obtains a psychological mind, sadistically torturing the opponent with relentless martial arts strikes. Without mercy in his attacks, he will not hold back on any kicks or punches, going for optimum impact and damage with each blow. Fighting stance shows Inphino in a slightly hunched martial arts position, his guard raised open handed palms facing towards him. DNA Style (Dramatic Nightmare Assault): the technical style of Inphino, using grapples and submissions for the anatomical destruction of his opponent. He analyzes the size and physique of the opponent and attacks in regard with the various technical maneuvers to break them down scientifically. This Fighting stance has Inphino standing straight, his feet grounded and past shoulder width and his guard raised with the right hand at the midsection and the left near the face. Onryu Style: The anarchist within is unleashed, and Inphino is chaotic with a random barrage of all his techniques; grapple, strikes, submissions, etc. This is the darkness from within taking over, nothing on his mind besides bringing complete destruction of whoever’s in front of him. The Fighting stance is Inphino hunched over, his shoulders slumped and his arms dropped down in front of him, looking limp. His head lowers, looking up past his black hair with glazed eyes and a sadistic smirk. Set Up Move: Countdown (A jumping corkscrew roundhouse kick, full rotation applies as Inphino twists back around and hammers his foot into the face of the opponent, knocking them back onto the mat while landing in a crouched position. Stronger impact is made when the kick lands on top of the head and knocks the opponent face down into the mat, where Inphino adds a heel grind into the back of the head for extra punishment. ) Finisher: 1 -- Inphinite (pronounced Infinite) 2 -- Inphinity (pronounced Infinity) (1 -- A stiff standing snap roundhouse kick to the face for the knockout. If the opponent ducks, the Inphinite can be reversed (called the Inphinite Reverb) into a reverse snap roundhouse kick. 2 -- Inphino catches the opponent in an inverted reverse full nelson hold in a standing position, dropping back to drive the opponent face first into the mat with a face driver. Then he proceeds to make the opponent submit in a face down inverted reverse full nelson submission hold. ) Title History: none Award History: none Other Federations: PCW, PURE, HCW Other Federation Awards: PCW/PURE/HCW Tag Team, PURE Legend (hall of fame), HCW World, HCW Continental Handler Information Handler Name: Marquette AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Quotes "Hakai!
