Ivan Stanislav/Tom Walczak/Michel Krieg vs. Reno Davis/Doug Troy/El Drago

2002-06-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivan Stanislav/Tom Walczak/Michel Krieg vs. Reno Davis/Doug Troy/El Drago Written: June 23, 2002 Nate: Ladies and Gentlemen, we are back on Saturday Slam and we’re ready for our Main Event! Three major feuds collide between six of the most talented men in the OSW! Robbie: Yeah, but they all mesh together with the great and powerful Red Army! Not only do they hate their enemies, but they’re also the best of friends together! They obviously have the advantage! Steven: That might be true..but can The Red Army actually fight as a team tonight? It seems that this collective commune are more indiscriminate individuals! Nate: Nice play on words Steven! Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest is a six-man tag match! Introducing first, weighing in at a combined weight of 1,122 lbs, they are the Red Army…Ivan Stanislav, Tom Walczak, and Michel Krieg!!! The Soviet National Anthem by the Russian Red Army Choir plays over the arena speakers as the lights dim to a deep and dark red. The three massive Communists, with Stanislav in the lead, emerge from the backstage area and slowly walk towards the ring. The crowd shows their disapproval of the three colossal men, but Stanislav, Walczak, and Krieg pay them no attention. Nate: Those men combined weigh over half a ton! Troy, Davis, and Drago are going to have to pull out all the stops if they want to overcome these colossal communists! Robbie: Enough with the alliteration already! Steven: Uh…Alliteration Already?? That’s actually… Robbie: SHUT UP! The Red Army enters the ring and heads to their corner. Stanislav talks to Walczak and Krieg, seemingly directing traffic. Earl: And their opponents, weighing in at a combined weight of 676 lbs, they are the OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Doug Troy, Reno Davis, and El Drago!!! “Momma Said Knock You Out” by LL Cool J plays over the loudspeaker as the crowd stands tall and cheers wildly. Doug Troy, Reno Davis, and El Drago emerge from the backstage area and raise their arms together before slowly making their way to the ring. Stanislav, Krieg, and Walczak roar and point at them as they near the ring, each yelling at their respective enemy. Troy, Davis, and Drago climb up into the ring and into their corner as Stanislav points and tells Krieg and Walczak to go to their corner. Stanislav turns to see El Drago standing in the ring with him! Nate: Drago surely hasn’t forgotten what Stanislav did to him last week on Slam! He wants his match with Ivan now! Ivan points at Drago and roars, telling him to get out of the ring. Drago shakes his head slowly. Ivan growls and turns, tagging in Krieg! Krieg climbs into the ring just as Drago turns and tags in Troy! Robbie: He’s scared! Drago tagged out! Steven: What about Ivan?! Robbie: That’s different! Troy points and Krieg and motions him to come forward. Krieg growls and turns, tagging in Walczak! Walczak thunders into the ring just as Troy tags in Davis! Ivan roars as Davis enters the ring and tags himself in. Stanislav thunders towards Davis, who tags in Drago!! Robbie: Ever have déjà vu? Ivan roars and points at Drago to tag out again. Drago shakes his head as Ivan turns and tags in Walczak! Walczak doesn’t hesitate and runs at Drago before he can tag out again! Drago slides to the side just in time as Walczak swings with a wild clothesline. Drago backs up into the center of the ring, Walczak in front of him, Ivan and Krieg beyond the ropes behind him! Nate: Not a good position to be in for El Drago! Drago and Walczak lock up, but Walczak is too dominant. Tom shoves Drago backwards into the corner. Krieg stands on one side, Ivan on the other, and whip Drago back towards Tom! Tom grabs Drago in a double chokehold and chokeslams him to the mat! Robbie: AAHAAHAA!!! What the Dragon get squashed like a Dragonfly!! Ivan yells from the corner and Walczak walks over tagging in Ivan. Ivan steps through the ropes and goes over to Drago pulling him up to his feet by his mask. Nate: And now Ivan wants Drago after Tom took him down. Ivan: You wanted Ivan! Ivan connects with a clubbing right hand dropping Drago to a knee. Ivan: You cannot handle Ivan! Again another clubbing blow this time dropping Drago to the mat face first. Nate: Not after your crony just took him out. Robbie: Now Nate, That’s the number one contender for the World Title you are talking about, he is no croon. Ivan stomps on the head of El Drago as the crowd boos and he walks over to the corner tagging Krieg in. Michel starts to climb the turnbuckle instead of entering the ring and he starts to measure up Drago. Nate: That is a long way for him to make it. That’s nearly three quarters of the ring. Robbie: No problem. Steven: He sure is taking his time. The enormous German starts to wobble on the top about the same time Drago starts to move. He leaps off but Drago easily rolls to the side out of the way. Nate: NOBODY HOME!!! The crowd starts to cheer on Drago as he pulls himself to his feet with the ropes. Krieg is crawling towards his corner and is close to a tag. Steven: El Drago better tag out in a hurry. Instead Drago charges into the enemy Corner with a Suicide Cross Body taking both Walczak and Stanislav by surprise and all three men go crashing in a heap to the outside as the crowd explodes. Nate: THAT WAS UNBELIEVABLE!!!!!! Robbie: You leave the caps lock on again Nate? Nate: Sorry. Krieg reaches up for the tag to find no one in the corner. He starts to look around and is met with a dropkick in the face from Reno Davis. Doug Troy meanwhile is charging over to the pile of giants and a Mexican. Steven: Looks like Gaston’s going to let Reno take over for Drago. Davis pulls Krieg up and slaps him across the chest with a knife-edge chop. The crowd oooos after he blow and he starts laying the chops in nicely one after another leaving Krieg’s chest a rosy pink. Nate: That’s worse then a sunburn. Robbie: How? Sunburns are a bitch. Troy has reached the pile and is pulling a battered Drago off the two monsters. He holds him up and starts to bring him back to the corner but turns and gives Walczak a kick to the head for good measure. Robbie: Big mistake. Walczak roars back to life and stares at Doug. Troy quickly rolls Drago into the ring and inadvertently right behind Davis. Reno takes a step back and hits El Drago and goes toppling over him through the ropes and into Doug which sends him staggering into the guardrail. Walczak meanwhile charges into him with a forearm and sends Doug skidding across the top of the guardrail on his ass and then falling off near the announce table. Nate: That was interesting. Robbie: Guys, I think we best be moving. Walczak follows up the attack stomping on the world Champion a couple times and then he starts to dismantle the Old School Announce table, which is made from wood. Nate: This isn’t looking good for the World Champ. These tables don’t collapse like other ones do. Robbie: It’s to discourage the wrestlers from destroying them and disturbing us every match. Steven: It doesn’t seem to be working now. As Walczak sets up the table to his liking Krieg and Drago are trading blows in the ring and Davis has gotten back to his feet as Ivan does. The two stare each other down and then Reno charges at Russian Bear only to be back body dropped into the first row. Nate: Look out! Tom rolls Doug Troy on top of the announce table and starts to climb up on top to join him. El Drago ducks a clothesline attempt from Krieg in the ring and nails Michel in the back of the head with a drop kick sending him face first into the corner. Robbie: There is action everywhere. Drago follows up with a Dragon Splash on Krieg in the corner and on the outside Ivan steps over the guardrail into the crowd looking for Davis. Walczak lifts Doug up to his feet and tries to set him up but Doug fights back with a couple of rights staggering the big Polish man. Nate: Get him off the table Doug!!! Robbie: Put him through the table Tom!!! Doug knees Tom in the midsection and with extreme quickness ddt’s him head first through the table and a loud cracking sound can be heard as his head goes into the wood table but the table is still standing. Nate: After the soda machine I knew he had a hard head, but that hard of a head I didn’t. Ivan is pursuing Davis through the crowd along the guardrail the massive Russian swatting and tossing him as they get closer towards the back. Drago pulls Krieg out of the corner by the back of his head. The crazy Mexican leaps over the top rope, head still in hand and uses the rope to impact his neck and Krieg. Robbie: Ivan is going to destroy Davis. Nate: And Drago is destroying Krieg. Troy rolls off of the announce table leaving Walczak looking like an ostrich with it’s head stuck in the sand. He rolls into the ring and charges at Krieg hitting him with a baseball slide and sending him out through the bottom rope right at Drago’s feet. Steven: Is this still a match or just an all out brawl? Robbie: Have you heard the ring bell? Ivan tosses Davis over the guardrail near the entrance way and starts to drag him by his red hair up the ramp to the back. Nate: Where is Ivan going with Davis? Drago bends down to pick Michel up but the German drives him back with all his might cracking him into the unforgiving guardrail. Robbie: Don’t you see, it’s divide and conquer! Doug turns his attention back towards the announce table. Walczak is trying desperately to pull his head free from the table but he can’t get free. Troy starts to climb up the turnbuckle as the crowd cheers in anticipation. Doug leaps off with an elbow the moment Tom stands up however the top of the able is still stuck around his head and the World Champion goes sailing elbow first right through the remains of the table and crashes into the cold concrete. Robbie: I don’t care who you are, that’s gotta hurt. Walczak’s yells as he pulls down on the pieces of wood around his neck. Blood starts to gush through a cut in his forehead and actually squirts about a foot and a half as he snaps the board in two with a roar and frees himself. Steven: I’ve seen some squirts in my day… *SMACK* Ivan disappears backstage with Davis and Krieg lifts El Drago up with a press slam and tosses him over the guardrail into the aisle way. He wastes little time hoping onto the guardrail and leaping off with an elbow this time finding it’s mark. Nate: We have to stay with the action in the ring but we will bring you anything important with Davis and Ivan if it occurs. Hopefully we can get a cameraman out there. Walczak’s face is soaked in blood as is a good portion of his upper body as he starts to clear the wreckage of the announce table and pull Doug back out of it. Krieg has Drago back on his feet and is moving him up the aisle with rights and lefts, staggering the Dragon backwards. Nate: I don’t like this at all. Robbie: Divide and Conquer. Walczak pulls Troy out of the wreckage and walks him over to the ring post where he bashes his head against it. He grabs Doug again and rolls him into the ring but holds onto the elbow Doug smashed into the concrete that is covered in blood although with the way Tom is bleeding it could very well be his. Steven: That elbow looks in bad shape. Walczak uses the rope as he wraps Troy’s arm around it and pulls down putting pressure on the elbow. The World Champ screams in pain as he thrashes about on the mat and manages to free himself as he catches Tom in the eyes with an index finger. Robbie: That is gruesome. Doug squirms away from the ropes holding his elbow as Tom claws at his eyes trying to regain his normal vision. Krieg and Drago are now all the way up near an exit to the main lobby of the arena and a big boot to Drago’s face sends him rolling down the ramp and out of site. Michel follows after him. Nate: We will stay with the action in the ring and get a camera to follow Krieg and Drago and report anything important that happens!!! Tom has his vision back and his target is locked as he rolls through the ropes and goes after Doug. Doug springs to his feet and stares into the eyes of the Polish Punisher waiting for him. Walczak stops and stares back into the eyes of the World Champion. Neither man moves a muscle and the crowd is going insane. Nate: In just eight days these two men will go one on one for the World Title, but right now they are going one on one to win this match for their teammates that have disappeared from the ring area!!! At once both men let the fists fly and Doug gains the momentum driving Tom back into the ropes. He starts to lay in with a couple of body shots and then backs up, dancing with quick feet and going for the knock out punch only to have Tom duck slightly and his hand nails the top of his head. Nate: He was looking like Lewis there but now he’s more like Tyson. Robbie: Top of the head Nate, hardest place in the body, and from what we’ve seen from Walczak he’s got one of the hardest in the world. Doug staggers back holding his hand and Tom charges at him with a clothesline sending the champ down. Steven: Could this be a preview of what happens in just eight days? Doug gets back to his feet slowly as Tom stalks his prey and knocks him back down to a knee with a right hand. Nate: Doug looks out of it. Doug tries to get back to his feet again but Walczak clubs him down with a two handed blow and Doug falls on his hands and knees before him. He grabs the back of his neck and jams his head into position and hoists him up delivering the Polish Powerbomb. Robbie: It’s over!!!! Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Walczak falls on top for the cover and Pierre counts. 1... 2... 3!!! Winners: The Red Army As Tom raises his hands in victory a blur can be seen in the corner of the screen followed by a loud crash. Nate: THE RED ARMY WINS IT BUT THE WAR IS STILL GOING ON!!! Up in the second layer of the arena Michel Krieg stands a crazed look in his eyes. Nate: WHAT THE HELL ARE THEY DOING THERE!!! Robbie: It’s Krieg!!! Steven: Does that mean Drago… SPANDEX!!!!! Nate: A camera must have picked that up. The camera cuts back to show Drago flying mid air from about twenty feet up falling through several speakers situated near the entrance way. Walczak meanwhile has gotten the World Title belt and is standing over an unconscious Doug Troy pointing at the title and blood from his open cut drips onto Doug’s face. Robbie: The Red Army has destroyed their victims just like they will in eight days. Now what happened to Ivan? Nate: HE IS STILL BATTLING WITH DAVIS!!! The scene switches to the Oakland Arena parking lot. The camera focuses on a large steel door just outside of the building as a low rumble is heard and the door flies open! Reno Davis flies through the door headfirst and lands hard on the asphalt as Ivan Stanislav emerges, growling at Davis. Reno rolls over slowly as Ivan grabs the steel door and rips it from the wall and raises it over his head!! Nate: GOOD GAWD!!! DAVIS AND IVAN ARE OUTSIDE THE ARENA!! THE ARENA CAN NOT HOLD THIS FEUD!!!! Robbie: KILL!!! KILL!!! KILL!!! Davis stands slowly as Ivan swings the door. It slams into Davis' side with a CLANG, sending The Unwanted Angel spinning, his back to Ivan. Ivan brings the door up over his head and roars again, slamming it into the back of Davis' head!! Reno staggers into the hood of a what appears to be a Dodge Intrepid and rolls out of the way just as Ivan swings the door down and impales it in the hood of the car! The car alarm goes off and the lights flash as Ivan grabs Davis! Nate: CHAOS HAS BROKEN OUT IN THE PARKING LOT!!! Steven: CHAOS IS GOING TO BREAK OUT IN MY HOTEL ROOM IN ABOUT 20 MINUTES!!! Davis wraps his arms around Ivan's huge trunk of a waist and pushes Ivan against the car, rocking it as the alarm echoes louder. Ivan growls and wraps his huge hands around Davis' waist and lifts him effortlessly up over his head and chucks him over a nearby Plymouth Horizon, into the bed of a pickup truck! Ivan: COME ON LITTLE MAN!!! COME FIGHT ME!!! Ivan laughs loudly and walks around the Horizon to the truck just as Davis' arm shoots up with a tire iron and clocks Stanislav in the side of the head! Ivan roars and falls backwards into the Horizon, cracking the passenger side window with his elbow. Davis leaps from the bed of the truck and dropkicks Ivan in the face! Stanislav rocks back, flattening the roof of the car!! Nate: SOMEONE CALL SECURITY!!! Davis stands as Ivan shakes the cobwebs free. Ivan swings and misses Davis, knocking the side view mirror from the truck and sending it careening into the shadows of the arena. Davis gut punches Ivan, but his wrist buckles! Ivan grabs Davis by the face and slams the back of his head against the window of the truck, cracking it. Stanislav pulls Davis back and sends his head through the driver’s side window completely in a shower of glass! Robbie: CUT HIS THROAT!!! MAKE HIS NECK MATCH HIS RED HAIR!!! Ivan pulls back Davis from the glass and lifts him by the neck with one hand…. Ivan: Get used to the pain, Unwanted Angel… Blood sputters from Reno's mouth as he kicks weakly. Ivan: I will break you, just like I broke your primate friend! Ivan laughs angrily and throws Davis hard onto the asphalt, skinning his legs and knees as he falls in front of the Horizon. Ivan walks over and lowers his boot over the back of Davis' neck. Nate: He's gonna choke him out! His boot is bigger than Davis' head!! Ivan: You have attacked Ivan where he was unprepared….Reno Davis…and you have taken away Tempest….. Nate: IVAN IS STILL RAMBLING ABOUT TEMPEST!!! Robbie: THE YOKO ONO OF THE RED ARMY!!! Ivan: You have hurt me in ways I never thought imaginable…but Ivan can do the same… Ivan growls and stomps down hard on the back of Reno's neck. Reno squirms weakly and coughs, blood coming from several wounds on his forehead and his mouth. Stanislav walks to the rear end of the Horizon and grabs it's bumper. Ivan: Das vidanya, in Russian, mean's goodbye, Reno Davis!! Ivan growls and tilts the car forward slowly until the entire vehicle rests up on its front end so that only Ivan's hands are seen on either side of the car. Davis struggles to get up!! Nate: NOOOOOO!!! HE HAS THAT CAR UP OVER DAVIS!!!! Robbie: THIS IS GREAT!! Ivan: Das vidanya, Angel…now you can learn for sure if you are wanted in heaven! Stanislav effortlessly pushes forward as a low groan comes from the car and it falls forward, slamming it's roof over Reno Davis with a crash!! Nate: OH MY GOD!! Robbie: HE'S DEAD!! IVAN KILLED HIM!! AAAHAAAHAAA!!! Steven: BOCAAAAAAAAA!!!!!!!! Nate: SOMEONE CALL THE PARAMEDICS!!! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Stanislav dusts off his hands, a sadistic grin over his face as he walks beside the car. Reno Davis' legs lay limply from under the wreckage, the rest of his body hidden beneath over a ton of steel. The Russian Bear then grits his teeth and looks down at Davis. Ivan: You killed a part of me…now..I kill a part of you…DYAAHAAHAA!!! Ivan laughs and raises his arms over his head. Ivan: DYAAAHAAAHAAA!!! Ivan booming laughter continues, as he spats down on the car in spiteful hatred for his arch-enemy, Reno Davis…as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears on the lower central portion of the screen as the scene fades to black.
