Damnedest Move of 2001 Award - (Eternal Damnation through a HIAC) Singles Record: PCW:- 4-1-1 Description: In Ring Attire: Hessian is a huge man, with only 4% body fat, and a body full of bulging muscles. His sheer mass suits his gothic look, as in the ring Hessian wears a pair of large black boots, over black denim pants. He has a steel belt with small spiky studs in it. Hessian wears a relatively tight black sleeveless shirt that shows off his hairy, scarred arms and 42inch biceps, as are the rest of his upper body muscles through the shirt. Attached to the collar of the shirt by a golden chain is a huge removable black cape. Over his hands and lower arms he wears large, silver steel gauntlets, that make his huge, menacing hands appear even more deadly. A silver steel mask, complete with an inhumane skull motif, reminiscent of the Predator from the movie of the same name covers his entire face. The only visible things through his mask are his dark, cold blue eyes, and his shoulder-length, slicked back black hair, which hangs loosely down. Hessian has a text-book square jaw, moulded by his mask, and he is completely covered in scars and wounds from the accident that turned him into what he is today…pure evil. Hessian has a deep, commanding voice that booms and sends shivers down even the bravest man’s spine. Out of the ring Hessian wears the same as in ring. Ring Entrance: The lights in the arena die down to black, and as “I Am Hated” by Slipknot booms through the PA, accompanied by white strobe lighting. Hessian appears through a cloud of mist on the stage as the chorus quickly kicks in, and walks slowly down to the ring, passing evenly spaced brown poles, topped with flaming skulls, that after he passes, blare up in a quick inferno, then die down. Hessian enters the ring and removes his cape, and waits for his challenger. History: The facts about Hessian are not very well documented, for he is a very secluded individual who is keeping secrets from everyone, but they are not all entirely his…however his manager, Minion the Imp, has enlightened us on a few facts. Hessian’s manager told firstly that although many may think his height as unrealistic, Hessian actually suffers from a mild case of Acromegaly, a disease that causes the growth hormones to spin out of control, hence turning Hessian into the 7’2” Giant he is today, and he will keep growing. Hessian’s manager also told of just how Hessian came to be; back on 24th October 1996 during a brisk evening jog, the man who would soon become Hessian was taking a brisk evening jog through the quiet Detroit streets. A stereotypical feature of Detroit is the violence, and it is no surprise that from out of nowhere Hessian was jumped by a gang of 15 thugs. In the blink of an eye Hessian had been reduced from the specimen of good health to a crumpled heap in the middle of the street. From the shadows of a nearby alleyway, looked on a mysterious figure, eyeing up the fallen giant. Hessian’s once handsome looks had been taken from him, as he was covered from head to foot in ugly scars, and was unable to get up or call for help. The mysterious figure finally emerged from the shadows and approached Hessian. He studied Hessian for some time, and finally got him medical help. After 4 long months in rehabilitation, spending countless hours stretching healing, torn ligaments, waiting as stab wounds and welts from the knuckleduster, baseball and steel pipe beatings healed. The months ended and Hessian was back to 100%. Strangely though, the Samaritan who had saved him 4 months earlier showed up a couple of days before Hessian was released from the hospital, and escorted him back to his family’s home. Unbeknownst to them, as they travelled back through Detroit to the family home, the very same gang who attacked Hessian 4 months previously caught sight of him, and so followed them back to the house…to finish Hessian off. As Hessian and his mentor got out of their cab and went to enter the house, one of the thugs threw a Molotov cocktail, meant to kill Hessian, in order to prevent him suing or coming after the gang, which actually missed and was hurled through an upper floor bedroom window, immediately sparking off a horrific inferno. Hessian was helpless to do anything, and watched as his home and memories burned away to cinders, and Hessian listened in terror as the shrill screams of his family echoed throughout the neighbourhood. By the time the blaze died down, there was nothing left but the back wall and some cloth. Soon after, Hessian became enraged, and thoughts of revenge ran through his head. Fortunately his mysterious saviour approached him and offered to provide for him, take care of him, even adopt him and…train him. Hessian wondered what this man meant by training and soon found out, as the two embarked on a five-year quest…to convert Hessian to the dark side. The years slowly passed and Hessian was given said name, and a whole new attitude. His mentor had warped Hessian’s mind and now wanted to test his skills in the wrestling world. Hessian questioned the reason for him being told to wrestle, but his mentor insisted that from his background, Hessian would love it. To this day Hessian wrestles in the OSW, after 2 weeks in the PWA without a match, a 5 month stint in the NTICW, which went very successfully for the Satanic Monster, then Hessian wrestled for a couple of months in the PCW and now in OSW. As an OSW wrestler Hessian is fighting for recognition, fighting for respect…fighting just for the Hell of it. Hessian also discovered that his mentor of those five long years was actually…Vampir Nosferatu. ----------------------- Valet/Manager: - Name: Minion The Imp Hometown: Detroit, MI Height: 3’11” Weight: 79lbs Self Defence Moves:- The Jaws of Satan - Bite to the Nads The Jab of Satan - Punch to the Nads The Kick of Satan - Kick to the Shins Impin’ Ain’t Easy - Minion grabs his attacker’s Nads and squeezes as hard as he can. Appearance: Minion The Imp wears a full brown hooded cloak, but he will usually leave the hood down. Minion has a botched face, beady black eyes and a bottom lip that sticks out slightly. He has a little moustache and goatee, and he has long, stringy black hair on the back and side of his head only. Minion’s key features that add to his “Imp-ness” are his long, hooked nose and his large ears, which jut out from the side of his head. Minion also has yellow, dirty teeth because he never brushes, and has a voice that is almost exact to British comedian Joe Pasquale, in other words he sounds like he is high on helium, which he is not. Finally, Minion’s skin is a slight grey colour, and his hair and blue eyes are highlighted from the grey skin. Hessian
