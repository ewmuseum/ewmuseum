Causing a Ruckus

2002-06-08
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Causing a Ruckus Written: 6-8-02 Nate: Welcome back to Slam!!! Well, we’re just about to get to our next match of the… “Ante Up Remix” by MOP & Funkmaster Flex hits the PA system, interrupting Nate in mid-sentence. A thunderous pop erupts as the OSW Tag Title Contenders, Da Westsidaz, make their way out from the back carrying baseball bats. The two of them make their way quickly to the ring with microphones and slide inside, with serious looks on their faces. Short Circuit paces around the ring with a concerned look on his face while Riptah gains composure. Decker: Good God, wake me when it’s over. There’s not even eye-candy out here to make me happy. Smith: Huh? SC’s right in the ring… Nate: Daisy is probably at the hotel because Da Westsidaz probably didn’t want her out here in harms way. You’ve seen what has been happening for the last month, haven’t you? Decker: Nope, but I got a lot of zzzzz’s. Riptah looks at the baseball bat with a disgusted look on his face, then turns to the crowd, who still seem to be thrilled about the Hall of Famers’ presence. Riptah paces for a moment, then addresses them. Riptah: My friends, it has come to my attention that OSW is in trouble. We are losing money quickly and steadily, but the reason hasn’t been found out. Nate: I haven’t heard this rumor, in fact, I’m pretty sure that OSW is doing great… Decker: DAMMIT NATE! Don’t take everything so seriously, Da Westsidaz are just ranting aimlessly like always. Riptah: The reason lies on the shoulders of our OSW Tag Team Champions. The crowd erupts in boos for the Goliaths. Riptah quiets them down and continues steadily. Riptah: Heresian Bloodlust has been a cancer to OSW. They’ve done nothing but destroy and damage the credibility of this company with their antics. Let’s start with Azrael Cain, shall we? This man is slow, unimaginative and dumb. He doesn’t pull his own weight around this place and is carried by his lack-luster partner. This man isn’t hated for being satanic, he’s hated for taking up airtime! Decker: Hmm…sounds like what’s happening right now. Riptah: …and Hessian? Hessian drags around a perverted, disgusting, gross-out, purple midget who makes me sick to my stomach with hatred. This leprechaun does nothing but annoy and disgust people with his foul, putrid appearance and behavior. Surprisingly an audible “Minion” chant breaks out, just loud enough for Riptah to hear. He’s surprised at first, but then just shakes it off. He walks into the corner, and relaxes, as his long-time partner/friend takes center-stage. Short Circuit: But good people, there is hope yet for this company. Oh yes there is. You see, the reason these two air-time hogs are on the show is the little, glittering thing they hold on their shoulders. The OSW Tag Team Titles are the reason these freaks are on SLAM! every week and the OSW Tag Team Titles are the reason why these foul, disgusting monsters pollute the airwaves. But y’know what? We can fix that, June 23rd, at OSW Ascension. The crowd pops a little for the statement, hoping that someone can end Heresian Bloodlust’s title reign. Short Circuit takes off his giant orange glasses, wiping sweat from his forehead. Short Circuit: At Ascension, we’re going to end this joke of a title reign Heresian Bloodlust has had. At Ascension, we’re going to start a new era in OSW. At Ascension, we’re going to win the OSW Tag Team Titles and at Ascension, we’re going to show why DA WESTSIDE… Crowd: IS THE BESTSIDE!!! Short Circuit: …is da bestside. Now hit our music. “Bounce” by System of a Down hits the speakers as the first-ever OSW Tag Team Champions, Civil Disobedience walk from the backstage holding microphones another large pop for a fan-favorite tag team. Nate: Hawke and Masters!!! Decker: Guess they’re tired of being ignored by Da Westsidaz for the last five minutes… The Kings of Suburbia have huge grins on their face, seemingly enjoying Da Westsidaz promo. In the ring is a different story, as Da Westsidaz are obviously upset about being interrupted like that. Seth: Whoa, whoa, whoa, WHOA! Did I just hear correctly? Did you just completely forget the REAL opponents in this match? Did you just forget CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE?! Wow, you guys sure are forgetful aren’t you? I mean, really, we have been kicking your asses week in and week out for about the last month! Short Circuit: Hey, wait just a--- Trevor: Shut it, dicksmack. You’re on our time now. I mean, holy shit. When they told us this show's ratings needed to be saved, I didn't think things could have been this bad. Seth: I'm totally there with you, Trev. Da Westsidaz on the mic?! You might as well show re-runs of Rosanne. Either way, people are going to change the channel as fast as possible. The two in the ring pace in anger, gripping the baseball bats. Short Circuit points the bat at Civil Disobedience, using pretty colorful language to describe the two. Riptah takes a different approach… Riptah: You better watch who you’re talkin’ to--- But is interrupted like he isn’t even there. Trevor: I can't believe backstage security actually let you guys get to the ring. Don't they have a responsibility to protect the people in attendance? Every time you dicksmacks open you’re mouths, you’re killing the brain cells of everyone watching. Seth: Yeah, both through the utter stupidity of what you’re saying and also with their stank-ass breath. Trevor: Oh, of course. You can't forget to mention your stank-ass breath. Where do you think that comes from, anyway? Seth: Well if I was eating out that slut Daisy, I'm sure my breath would stink like sardines, too. There's a lot of cheese on that taco, if you catch my drift. Riptah stares in utter disbelief as the two just keep on talking, verbally ripping Da Westsidaz a new asshole. Short Circuit whispers something into Riptah’s ear and he nods in agreeance. Trevor: I catch it, all right. Just like these two dicksmacks in the ring are going to catch a suburban-style beat down come June 23rd. Not only are we going to win the titles off of the big-ass lazy biznitches, we’re going to prove that it’s the Mid-Westside that’s the true bestside! As “Bounce” lights up the speakers yet again, Da Westsidaz slide out of the ring, charging after Civil Disobedience with the bats in hand. Riptah roars something at them, but the duo disappear backstage with Da Westsidaz in hot pursuit. Nate: Well, Da Westsidaz sure didn’t like Civil Disobedience’s take on things. This tag division seems to be really heating up. Decker: *snore* Huh? What? Oh yeah, commercial time. Commercial: OSW is sponsored in part by KY Jelly. KY Jelly: Kid tested, mother approved. The scene opens to the popular duo of Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters panting for breath, obviously still trying to hide from Da Westsidaz. They turn the corner and look to each other. Seth: I don’t think Riptah took the sardine comment to well. I guess I should’ve noticed that they had baseball bats and all… Trevor looks down the hall and doesn’t see Da Westsidaz coming. An ethnic voice rips down the hallway though, telling the two that they’re coming. Trevor looks at a Women’s Bathroom and gets an idea. Trevor: Seth, we gotta hide in there. Da Westsidaz will never think to look in there! Seth: But that’s the… Trevor: Do you wanna be embarrassed or let Da Westsidaz’ go Al Capone on our asses? I say we hide in the chick room till the show goes off the air. That way they’ll never find us! Seth: Fine, let’s go already. The longer we wait to decide, the longer it is before Da Westsidaz find us. Trevor: No Seth, the SHORTER it is till Da Westsidaz find us. Seth: Whatever. Civil Disobedience quickly disappear into the Girl’s Washroom. They look around inside and the beautifully cleaned, perfect room. It has it’s own radio, television and everything. It seems completely perfect, like the Washroom From Heaven or something. Trevor: Good lord! The Men’s Bathroom had a dying animal on the floor and this place is perfect?! Talk about sexism. I’m going to have to file a complaint. Seth: I know, really! Look at this place! It even has this neat little envelope dispenser! Seth plays back and forth, taking out multitudes of little, pink envelopes. Trevor: Uh, Seth, I don’t think that’s a… All of a sudden a loud sneeze breaks out in the washroom. Hawke and Masters get on the defensive and look around, but no one is anywhere to be found. The two scan the area and notice two glowing eyes in the vent. Seth kicks the vent real hard and out rolling comes Minion the Imp, holding a tube of KY Jelly in his hands. Seth: AH! Trev, it’s a gremlin!! Trevor: No you idiot, it’s that little puke Minion! What the hell are you doing here?! The little creature snickers evilly at the two faces. Minion: Same thing you’re here for, the POON! And I woulda seen it too if you two clowns didn’t bust in here. Trevor: Poon? No, were not here for that. We’re hiding in here! We were just… A voice is heard and the bathroom door starts to open slowly, so all three of the idiots dive for hiding places. Minion jumps head-first into the toilet and Trevor hides in a stall, leaving Seth to fend for himself. He quickly hides behind the garbage can, but is in clear view as AZRAEL CAIN WALKS IN?? Azrael: Minion? Are you in here? Hessian is going to kill… Both Men and Imp appear from their hiding places and look puzzled at Azrael. Azrael stands in the middle of the room with a sledgehammer, as confused as they are. Civil Disobedience & Minion: You want poon too? Azrael: No you idiots, I’m here to find Minion. It’s obvious where he would go and…WAIT A SECOND! WHAT THE HELL ARE CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE DOING HERE?! Azrael swings his hammer at Trevor, who ducks, so the hammer crashes into a huge center mirror sending glass everywhere. Trevor and Seth boot it out of the washroom and sprint down the hallway and slams into Hessian at full speed. The look up at the masked monster who is holding a large chainsaw wrapped in barbwire. Seth: AH! WHAT THE HELL IS THAT??! Trevor: Let’s not stick around and find out!! The two of them get to their feet and get ready to run only to see Da Westsidaz holding their baseball bats at the end of the hallway. Riptah: You two whiteboys are dead! You hear me?! DEAD! Trevor and Seth look at each other with sad faces as Da Westsidaz charge. In the confusion, Riptah starts to unload on Hessian with the baseball bat. Seth receives a Sledgehammer into the ribs, dropping him like a brick. Trevor starts to give Short Circuit a barrage of rights, staggering him, until a flood of security officials pour into the hallway, dragging the respective parties away from each other. Each member of Civil Disobedience fold up a chair for protection and start yelling across the room at the OSW Tag Champions, while Da Westsidaz try and fight through all the uniforms. Moose is barking orders at the three teams, trying to keep them separated until the Commissioner shows up. Howard Porter: Hey! Knock it off you guys! I book your match and this is how I am compensated? Fine. I’ll give you guys something to chew on for awhile. I’m banning every one of you from the arena tonight, so pack your bags. As for your match, here’s a stipulation for you! “Weapon of Choice” match will suffice I assume? Considering you guys like to disrupt everything using your idiotic antics with weapons and almost destroying my main event a few weeks ago, I think this will be perfect. But, if any of you guys try and pull something stupid like this again, I’ll suspend you all indefinitely! That means no match, no title shot. Da Westsidaz and Civil Disobedience yell in disagreement at Porter, but security holds them back. Hessian and Cain have a smug look on their face, their minds already spinning with plans. Howard Porter: I don’t know what you guys think is funny either because if you mess up, I’ll vacate those titles! I work too hard around here for six people who think they’re hotshots to run around unchecked. You three teams better act accordingly or you’ll be work elsewhere!!! All three teams shake their head in anger, but don’t act out. They all take their leave one by one, as the scene goes to commercial.
