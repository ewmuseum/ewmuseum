The Feed Written: February 16, 2002 The scene fades into the back as we see the figure known as Corg laughing to himself. He isn’t alone long however as OSW’s commissioner Ed walks up behind him and taps him on the shoulder startling the gothic freak. Corg looks around quickly and breaths a sigh of relief when he realizes it’s just Ed. “Oh man did you give me a scare Ed.” Corg says. Ed responds with a goofy smile as he pats Corg on the shoulder as he replies, “Who were you expecting? Ritchie?” Corg’s smile fades as Ed continues talking, “So what were you laughing at anyways? I’m always in for a good joke.” Corg starts to chuckle again as he remembers what he was laughing at and between chuckles he replies, “Oh I was just laughing at how, hehe, how I cost Ritchie that match against Blaze.” Ed’s smile never fades as he starts to laugh a little bit himself, “That was pretty funny but you know what else would be funny?” Corg’s smile fades once again and is replaced with a quizzical look as he waits for the answer. Ed rests his hand on Corg’s shoulder as he tells him, “You vs Matt Blaze next Saturday, LIVE on SLAM!” The sounds of the crowd cheering in the arena can be heard as Ed gives Corg a good pat on the shoulder before leaving the stunned goth to contemplate what just happened. The scene fades back to the ringside announce position, of the OSW announce team. Nate: WOAH!!! Welcome back! Corg’s interference has earned him a match with Matt Blaze for next Saturday night! Robbie: Corg should be welcoming a chance to show what a real wrestler can do in the ring against a all talk and no skill worker like Blaze. Nate: Corg didn’t look happy about…Wait a minute!! It looks like our own Shanda Russell is in the right place at the right time and is standing by backstage with Corg, to get his reaction to what just happened. Shanda?!? The camera shifts backstage and is focused in on the lovely Shanda Russell and Corg. Shanda: Thanks guys! I am indeed here with Corg, whose appearance in the Ritchie Syxx/Matt Blaze earlier this evening cost Corg’s arch-rival, Ritchie Syxx the match. Now, Corg, it would seem, Commissioner Ed was not pleased with your activities, and has ordered you to face Blaze next Saturday night on Slam! Any thoughts. Corg’s red eyes stare directly into the camera, while the piercings which have skewered his pale flesh glisten in the spotlight. Corg: I would like to thank Ed for feeding me Matt Blaze next week. I plan to make even more of an example of Blaze next week, then I do of Doug Troy here tonight. Shanda nods. Shanda: So you are predicting a win for over Doug Troy here tonight? Corg: I am predicting pain and suffering for Doug Troy and the same for you, unless you leave me be. With that, the Silent Assassin Corg, turns and skulks off into the darkness of the backstage area. Shanda: Thanks for your time Corg and good luck in your match tonight against Doug Troy. Just as Corg exits the shot, Shanda begins to address all of the OSW fans again, but she is rudely interrupted by a massive dark figure that steps into the shot. The camera pans out to see that it is Azrael Cain looking for his partner, Hessian. Cain stops walking and stares at Ms. Russell.
