Corg vs. Rhett Locke, to decide the stipulation at Head on Collision

2002-03-30
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Corg vs. Rhett Locke, to decide the stipulation at Head on Collision Written: March 30, 2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam. We are set to head to the ring, where Corg will battle Rhett Locke in a non-title match, with the winner of this match getting to choose the stipulation for their title showdown at Head On Collision. Robbie: Corg is going to destroy Locke here, then destroy him again at Head On Collision. Locke would be best served just to make this the title match, so he can avoid the agony of a second meeting with “The Silent Assassin” Corg. Nate: Locke is too much of a competitor to EVER lay down for anyone, as you will witness in a few seconds. Let’s send it up to ring announcer Earl Henrich who will formally introduce the principles. 'Chemical' by No One begins blasting over the speakers, and the fans rise to their feet. As soon as they see the Silent-Killer, Corg, step out from behind the curtain, a shower of jeers rain down on him. Amidst the storm, however, Corg only smiles and gives them all the middle finger. Earl: The following match has been scheduled for one fall, and is to determine who will decide the match stipulation at Head on Collision! Introducing first, from Detroit Michigan, weighing in a 6', 172 lbs. He is the Anti God, the Silent Killer....COOOOOOOOOOOORG! Nate: Corg will have his work cut out for him tonight! Robbie: Bah! Corg will destroy Rhett Locke, name a stip, and take his title at the pay-per-view! Nate: Big words coming from a man with such a small mind. Steven: Big words coming from a man with such a sma-- Robbie: If you say it, I'll kill you. Corg leans in a corner of the ring, and anxiously awaits the arrival of his opponent. After a few moments for the crowd to die down, 'Aerials' by System of a Down hits the P.A. system. Rhett Locke chuckles for just a moment as the fans cheer their International Champion. He slowly stalks to the ring, and Corg begins pacing. Earl: And his opponent, from Portland Oregon, standing at 6' 1, 218 lbs! The OSW INTERNATIONAL CHAMPIOOOOOON! RHETT LOOOOOOOCKEEEEEEEE! Rhett slides under the ropes, and immeadiately Corg takes advantage. Corg is stomping at Locke's ribs mercilessly! Robbie: Look at him go! Nate: Yeah yeah... Rhett holds his ribs, trying to protect them from further harm as Corg lifts him up by his hair. Corg gives a sadistic smile and irish whips Locke into the ropes. Corg ducks for a back-body drop, but doesn't connect as Rhett lifts a knee to Corg's face. Corg yells, and soon finds his legs sweeped out from under him by Locke! Robbie: Damnit! Nate: Alright! Steven: BOCAAAAAAA~! Corg quickly gets to his feet, and runs right into a series of punches from Rhett Locke! Left, right, left, right, left! Rhett soon follows up with a kick to Corg's midsection, and a DDT! Rhett screams, releasing some of the pent up adrenaline, much to the fans' delight! Locke, perhaps prematurely, climbs to the top! Nate: This might be a bad move on Rhett's behalf... Indeed, it is. Rhett jumps, and attempts a guillotine legdrop, but fails as Corg rolls out of the way. Corg slowly climbs to his feet, and grabs Rhett by the hair once more. Locke tries to fight free, but only recieves an elbow to the back of the head. Robbie and Steven: Ha! Nate: Screw you both! Robbie and Steven: Bah! Corg simply manhandles Rhett, twirling him around and bending him back, and gives Locke the Blood for Blue! Corg wisely goes for the cover! Betty: Like, umm 1! Betty: 2...and...stuff! But alas, she is unable to make the pinfall, thus she leaves Corg and Decker in quite an angry state! Robbie: STUPID BITCH! Nate: For once the slut's good for something! Rhett slowly gets to all fours, but is kicked in the gut and sprawls to the ground. Corg smiles, and lifts up Rhett by the hair once again. Corg simply pushes him into the corner, and charges the International Champion! ... ... But Rhett is able to roll out of the way, and Corg slams right into the turnbuckle! In a burst of energy, he grabs the back of Corg's head and slams his face into the turnbuckle over and over! 1! 2! 3! 4! 5! Corg rolls onto the ropes, and Locke continues his burst of adrenaline. He springs onto one side of the ropes and spears Corg, they both go flying over the ropes, and Betty sighs. She pulls out an old yackback, and presses 'play' Betty: 1! 2! 3! 4! No movement from either indeviduals, as both of them crashed hard into the guard rail. Robbie: Get up! Get up Corg! Nate: I'm mesmorized...Betty actually had a good idea. Betty: 5! 6! 7! 8! 9! 10! Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Nate: NOOOOOOOOOO! Steven: BOCAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA~!! After a bit, both of them rise to their feet. Corg is exasperated, and he sends a kick to Rhett's gut, and slides him into the ring! Corg digs in his pants pocket, until he reveals a pair of handcuffs! Robbie: woooooo! Nate: Oh god, this doesn't look good... Corg slides in the ring, and flashes a sadistic smile. All eyes are upon him...and soon after the two seconds of silence... A hail mary of boos occur as Corg begins to whip Rhett Locke with the handcuffs! Nate: This is bullshit! Steven: mmmm, bondage! Rhett is soon handcuffed to the bottom rope, and Corg demands a mic. Corg paces around the ring for a bit, until he is tossed the microphone he desired. Corg: RHETT! TELL THE WORLD YOU'LL GRANT THE STIPULATION I WANT! TELL THEM! Nate: OH C'MON! THIS IS CRAP! Robbie: THIS IS GREAT! Corg shoves the mic into Rhett's face, but Rhett doesn't say anything. Corg frowns, and sends a stiff kick to Rhett's gut. Rhett falls now, instead of slumped and sitting he is now sprawled out and laying down. Locke can barely lift himself up. Corg: Now...let's try this again. Tell them! Corg shoves the microphone into Rhett's face. Rhett: ...Fuck...you... Corg: BASTARD! Corg puts the hurt to Rhett, he kicks punches, scratches...and Rhett is mutilated, mauled, and nearly destroyed. Rhett enters a state of unconciousness, and the crowd can only boo. Nate: LOCKE REFUSED TO AGREE!!! HE REFUSED TO LET CORG NAME THE STIPULATION FOR HEAD ON COLLISION!!! Robbie: Because he passed out like a pussy. Pooo-saaaay I say!!! Nate: (sighing) Leave it to Robbie Decker to belittle an absolutely courageous and gutty performance. We need to head to a commercial. When we return, we will head to the ring for our main event of the evening. The crowd continues to boo loudly. They litter the ring with trash, which seems to be a norm for OSW as of late, and Corg gives a sadistic smile as he llooks down upon the helpless and unconscious Rhett Locke. Corg spits upon Locke,, then slides out of the the ring.….as the scene fades to commercial.
