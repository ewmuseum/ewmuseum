Corg vs. Lillith, Round One of the Cruiserweight Title Tournament

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Corg vs. Lillith, Round One of the Cruiserweight Title Tournament Written: May 11, 2002 Nate: Could be worse. Could be two guys betting over whether or not someone can lift a car… Robbie: …I have no response. You are absolutely right. Nate: Thanks for being big enough to admit it. Folks, I have received word we are just about set to head to our first match of the evening, which is a first round Cruiserweight Title Tourney match, featuring Corg taking on the Queen of Extreme, Lillith. Steven: BOOOO!!! Lillith go home!!! Robbie: This match should be banned, because Goth on Goth violence is holding Goth’s back as a people. Nate: (disgusted) Thanks for that bit of indepth commentary. Let’s send it up to Earl Henrich who is in the ring and ready to introduce the principles. Earl: Introducing first, hailing from Twilight, Pennsylvania! She weighed in at 5'5', 115 lbs! She is the Queen of Extreme...LILLIIIIIIIITH! Steven: Boooo! Booooo! Queen of Putretience!!! Nate: No movie quoting please…. 'I Stand Alone' by Godsmack suddenly blasts over the P.A. system, and to the cheering fans, the crowned Queen of Extreme steps out from the back. She holds a single rose to her chest, with a slight sneer trailblazing across her face. She slowly moved down the entrance ramp and entered the ring. Earl: And now, introducing her opponent, from Detroit Michigan! He weighs in at 6'0', 172 lbs! The Silent-Killer, The Anti God...he is COOOOORG! However, Nate is interupted just as 'Chemical' by No One begins playing. The lights dim, and pyrotechnics blast all the way down the ramp. Much to the fans' distaste, Corg stands triumphantly on the ramp as the pyros die down. He too makes his way a bit slow to the ring, however, once he enters, the bell rings immeadiately. The opening match is underway. Corg charges toward Lillith with the ferociousness of a lion, however, Lillith easily side-steps him. The Silent-Killer quickly stopped and turned on his heel, to end up kicked in the stomach and DDT'd on behalf of the Queen of Extreme. The resilient Corg is able to jump to his feet with great agility, and send a vicious sidekick toward Lillith. Lillith ducks under it, and Corg takes initiative, clotheslining her hard to the mat. Nate: That nearly took her head off! Robbie: Serves her right, the damn wench! Nate: Shut up Robbie, you bastard. Steven: Boys, boys...please... Corg soon finds himself feeling extreme pain from his genitalia as he lifts her up by her hair. The Anti-God looks down, and in horror finds his testicules being squeezed by Lillith, until she sands up, spits in his face, and gives a hard judo chop to Corg's throat. Robbie: That is SO NOT COOL! Nate: I hate to agree with a pedaphile, but I guess I'll have to. Robbie: Why I oughta... Corg hits the ground hard, and she jumps to the top turnbuckle. Whilst he grips his throat, she waits. Corg slowly gets to his feet, and looks up just in time to see her in midflight, attempting a dropkick. Corg jumps back, and pushes downward on her feet, simply slamming her to the ground as she reached him. Corg siezes the day, and begins stomping away at her upper body. Lillith can't help but to yelp slightly at every stomp. However, Corg soon finds himself laying down after she caught his boot and slinged him over her to the mat. Steven: I'll be damned, a woman knows what she is doing...that's a first in wrestling history. Nate: I'll be damned, a faggot can't keep his mouth shut. That's the first time in...oh, wait, no it isn't. Robbie: hehehehe. Lillith stands up, and sends a stiff kick directly to Corg's face. She vaults herself into the ropes, and as it springboarded her back, she jumps on his cranium! And the blood begins to flow. Robbie: Well, this sucks... Lillith continues to lay it into Corg, her feet kicking his cranium with stiff shots. She lifts Corg up by his once spiked red hair and irish-whips him, and with a bull's strength he burrows through her, only to launch off the ropes once more, jump on to the second rope, and connect with an Asai Moonsault. Betty Lovejoy stands there for five seconds, before leaving her day dream and getting down to count! 1! 2! ...No! Robbie: God damnit! Corg had it won! Corg had it won! Lillith stands up, only to find Corg charging toward her once again, but she ducks, and catches him in a full-nelson just in time. She hooks one of his legs with hers, and falls forward... In the name of pain. However, an unlikely failure suddenly emerges sending the crowd into a chorus of boos. The camera shifts to reveal noneother than… Graphic Violence. Nate: It's Graphic Violence! He's coming down to the ring! Robbie: Excellent observation, bitch. GV charges the ring when suddenly, the crowd pops. Nate: LOOOOOK!!! The camera shifts to the aisle, to reveal another figure is following GV from behind… Rhett Locke, with a chair. WHACK! Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOO! Nate: YEEEEEEEESSSS!!!! Steven: BOOOOOOCA!!!! The steel chair connects with GV's cranium, and GV hits the floor like a sack of potatoes. Nate: RHETT LOCKE IS TAKING GRAPHIC VIOLENCE TO TASK!!! RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!! GV finds himself getting pummeled with left and right's to his face by Rhett Locke, and with sheer force and adrenaline, he throws Rhett Locke nearly four feet away and off of him. The two brawl like maniacs to the back, while Lillith starts screaming at GV! Lillith: DIE!!! DIE!!! DIE!!! Nate: LILLITH LOOK OUT!!! Nate’s warning is too late, however, as Corg rolls her up from behind, and Betty once again counts, extremely quick, but it couldn't be purposely. Betty isn't smart enough to cheat, after all! 1! 2! 3! It's over! Winner: Corg Nate: Thanks to Graphic Violence, Corg has won the first round of the cruiserweight title tournament!! Robbie: Yes! Yes! OooOh Yes! Steven: Heh, he orgasms bigger than BOCA~! himself... View Corg 's Biography
