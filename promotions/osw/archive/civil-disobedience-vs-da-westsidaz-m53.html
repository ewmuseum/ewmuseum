Civil Disobedience vs. Da Westsidaz

2002-05-02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil Disobedience vs. Da Westsidaz Written: 5-2-02 Up on the jumbotron, the OSW logo fades to black and the scene from Head on Collision last week appears where Heresian Bloodlust is attempting to kidnap Shanda Russell into the backstage area when suddenly they are laid out by two chair shots by none other than Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters. The fans are cheering wildly until both Masters and Hawke are then laid out by two more chair shots by Da Westsidaz who remain at the entryway holding the chairs high in the air over top the bodies of Heresian Bloodlust and Civil Disobedience. Nate: That was the scene last week after Cain and Hessian successfully defended the tag belts. And now tonight Civil Disobedience are going to square off! Robbie: I think that it is safe to assume that Cain and Hessian are going to make an appearance tonight as well! Steven: And most likely those two little creepy things that they call managers will be following them closely behind. Nate: Well, let's take it to Earl for the introductions of what will most definitely be a very interesting match. The camera shot changes to Earl Henrich standing in the ring with a mic in his waiting to make the introductions. Earl brings the mic up to his face in order to begin. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, Boys and Girls, Children of all ages. The next match is scheduled for one fall. The referee for this match is Pierre Gaston. Coming to the ring first... Steven: Hey Earl! Earl! What the hell do you think you're doing? Earl gets a confused look on his face before he responds to the annoying homosexual. Earl: I'm making the introductions for the match. Steven: What are you doing? Earl: Making the match's introductions! Steven: What? Earl: Introducing the Match!!! Steven: Huh? Steven: INTRODUCING THE DAMN MATCH!!! I AM INTRODUCING THE NEXT MATCH! IS THAT OKAY WITH YOU? Steven: Oh, introducing the match. I get yeah. Yeah Earl, go right ahead, man. Steven begins to chuckle to himself as Earl glares at the flamboyant announcer. Nate and Robbie roll their eyes at Steven and after a second they both just lean over and simultaneously lean over and flip Steven's chair toppling him to the hard concrete floor. Earl: Coming to the ring first, from California and at a combined weight of 460 pounds! Accompanied to the ring by their manager Daisy! 'Ante Up Remix!' by Mop and Funkmaster Flex begins to blast in the arena as the fans cheer at the top of their lungs. Earl: They are Riptah and Short Circuit!!! Da Westsidaz!!! Riptah, Short Circuit, and their gorgeous manager Daisy jump out of the backstage area together and begin working the crowd over for more pops as they start to work their way down to the ring. Nate: And here they are, the PCW hall of famers! The five time PCW tag team champions! Robbie: And not to mention that manager of there’s. Oh yeah baby! Look at that Ass! Steven: I am! I am! Robbie: I mean look at Daisy's ass, not Riptah's! Jesus! Earl: And their opponents. coming from Aurora, Illinois! Weighing in at 460 pounds! 'Bounce' by System of a Down replaces the 'Ante Up Remix' on the arena's sound system and the fans continue to cheer just as loud as before. Earl: Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke!!! Civil Disobedience!!! Masters and Hawke explode from the backstage area to fans delight. Both of them charge to the ring after a moment of working the crowd over at the entryway. Nate: Well, there is definitely no decisive favorite here tonight in this match! Robbie: That's right, they both suck! Masters dives under the rope and hits Short Circuit with a spear. Both wrestlers crash to the mat. Hawke dives under the ropes second, but when he tries to spear Riptah he is caught by the much larger wrestler who hits Hawke with a sidewalk slam. Nate: And here we go! Referee Gaston signals for the bell right away in order to start the match. Riptah leans over and pulls the much shorter Hawke up to his feet. Riptah picks Hawke up into the air into a gorilla press slam. Riptah pushes Hawke into the air, but on the way down, hits Hawke with a stunner out of mid air. Nate: Punk-out! Riptah just hit Hawke with the Punk-out! Robbie: Kill him! Kill him! Masters is on the other side of the ring laying his fists into Short Circuit's face until Daisy comes over and nails from behind with her purse. The blow from the purse knocks Masters to the mat. The purse tears open from the impact to expose the fact that their was a brick hidden in Daisy's purse. Nate: Oh my God! Daisy just hit Masters with a brick loaded purse! Robbie: Kill Him! Kill Him! Daisy and Short Circuit roll Masters out of the ring. The limp body of Masters hits the concrete floor hard. Short Circuit climbs up onto the top rope and starts to call over to Riptah who has the stunned Hawke up on his feet. Immediately, Hawke is lifted up onto Riptah's shoulder into an electric chair position. Short Circuit leaps off the top rope and grabs hold of Hawke by the waste and hits him with a sit down powerbomb! Nate: And there is the Bestside Bomb! Da Westsidaz are here to take care of business this week. Robbie: Kill Him! Kill Him! Riptah slaps Short Circuit's hand. Riptah and Daisy exit to the apron of the ring. SC is holding onto the sit-down position after the powerbomb. Gaston slides into position to make the count. 1... Nate: This could be it! 2... Steven: BOCA~! NO! Hawke manages to get his shoulder up off the mat. Short Circuit slams the mat hard with his hand only to get back to his feet in order to continue to the attack. SC is overtop of the stunned suburban. Circuit leans over and grabs Hawke by the T-shirt, but instead of pulling him to his feet, SC is hit with a low blow by Hawke that topple Short circuit to the mat. Nate: Hawke is up! Robbie: Kill Him! Kill Him!!! Steven: Whose side are you on here? Robbie: Neither! I just want to see both of these tag teams kill each other! Hawke gets back up to his feet and starts to kick away at Short Circuit who is down on the mat, while he is still holding his back in pain from the assault that he has already received. Nate: Hawke is fighting back here, but he needs to get a tag soon. Steven: Who is he going to tag? Masters is out cold! Hawke takes off across the ring at a dead sprint only to bounce off of the ropes. Hawke comes back and leaps through the air only to come crashing down onto the Short Circuit’s neck with a vicious leg drop. SC is rolling on the mat now clutching his throat in pain. Hawke gets back onto his feet and pulls Circuit up to his feet and hoists him up into the air only to hit him with a Swinging fisherman suplex that crumples Short Circuit onto the mat. Nate: And there are some impressive moves from the fighting back Hawke! Hawke crashes onto the mat and makes the a quick cover. Gaston slides into position. 1... Riptah climbs onto the top of the turnbuckle. 2... Riptah leaps off of the turnbuckle and comes crashing down trying to hit a leg drop... The pin is broken up by Riptah crashing down onto Short Circuit!!! Nate: Hawke rolled out of the way! Riptah just nailed his own tag partner with a flying leg drop! Robbie: Kill Him! Kill Him! Steven: They're on the same team, Decker! There is a pause. Robbie: Kill Him Anyway!! Hawke gets up to his feet quickly only to hit the shocker Riptah with a spinning heel kick that drops Riptah to the mat on top of his partner. Riptah is getting back up onto his feet quickly though only to get sent back to the mat with a clothesline from Hawke. Nate: Hawke seems to be in the driver's seat right now! Hawke lifts his arms up into the air and the fans receive him more than warmly as the arena's roof almost blows off with cheers from the fans. Hawke turns around though and he is caught with a stiff bitch slap from Daisy! Nate: Bitch Slap! Robbie: Kill Him! Kill Him! Steven: Fucking Skirts! Hawke looks stunned for a second while he holds his cheek trying to soothe the stinging sensation. He starts to stalk towards Daisy who is backing up nervously. Nate: This doesn't look good for Daisy! Steven: Kill Her! Kill Her! Robbie: Save her! Save Her! Hawke is about to get his hands on Daisy, but instead he is thrown to the mat by a spear delivered by Daisy's boyfriend Riptah. Riptah quickly gets to his feet and pulls Hawke up onto his feet only to hoist him up and hits him with a spinebuster, but once on the ground, Riptah rolls Hawke over a lays a sharpshooter on. Short Circuit slides across the ring and slaps a crippler crossface onto Hawke as well. Nate: Westside Stretch! Robbie: Kill Him! Kill Him! It looks as if Hawke is going to tap out soon. Steven: Hey fellas, who is that on the turnbuckle. Out of no where a flying figure comes crashing onto Riptah and Short Circuit with a double clothesline that breaks up the hold. Nate: Its Masters! Masters is back! Steven: BOCA~! Robbie: Kill Him! Kill Him! Steven: Will you stop that! Masters crawls up to his feet still noticeably shaken. He pulls Short Circuit up to his feet only to send him back to the mat with a half-turn neckbreaker! Masters is pumped up right now and starts to work the crowd over. Hawke is suddenly hoisted into an Olympic slam by Riptah! Nate: This is an all out melee! Robbie: Kill Him! Steven: This is great! Riptah is up on his feet again and starts to work the crowd over! Nate: This has turned into a fight for the fans affection! Robbie: Screw the crowd! Someone better kill someone quick! Steven: I have a feeling that is going to happen real soon! Look! The two tiny managers of Heresian Bloodlust, Caleb and Minion, crawl over the security barrier and immediately make a dash for the ring. Instead of crawling under the ropes though they both dive under the apron. Nate: What the hell are they doing? Steven: I'm not sure, but it always seems that we're asking that very week about those two! Robbie: Screw that! When those two appear that usually means Cain and Hessian are nearby! Someone's going to DIE!!! Riptah hasn't noticed that the two managers are here though and he continues his assault on Masters by starting to lay the boots to the suburban's ribcage! Nate: Where are those two little things? Riptah leans over and pulls Masters up to his feet, but Masters delivers a quick to the big man's abdomen and hits him a Russian leg sweep. Riptah hits the mat hard. Nate: Masters is in control! Robbie: Will those two idiots do something already? Steven: Here they come! Caleb and Minion appear from underneath the mat on opposite sides of the ring. Each of them have a rope in their hands that are tied to something underneath the ring. Nate: Why do they have those ropes? Steven: Screw that! What are those ropes tied to? Inside the ring, Masters is down on top of Riptah now and beating down Da Westsida! Meanwhile Hawke's head is being slammed into the turnbuckle by Short Circuit! Caleb and Minion tug on the ropes sharply. Nothing happens though. Caleb and Minion look frustrated. Minion gets an idea though and whistles to someone on the other side of the barrier. Nate: Who is he signaling to? The Goliath, Hessian appears from behind the barrier though and crawls over top only to take the rope from Minion's hand. Cain appears from backstage area sprinting down the ramp. He gets to Caleb and steals the rope from the small hobo. Cain and Hessian look at one another and tug on the ropes with great force. Nate: What the hell?! There is a large snap sound heard underneath the ring though! Steven: Oh God! Suddenly, the center of the ring collapses! Civil Disobedience and Da Westsidaz are launched into the air and crash down onto the now slanted mat only to slide to the center of the ring. Nate: What the hell? Steven: Can someone please tell me what just happened? Robbie: HA! Cain and Hessian release their grips on the ropes and approach the ring. Caleb pulls Cain's sledgehammer out from behind the barrier and hands it to the beast. Minion hands Hessian a steal chair! Cain and Hessian climb up into the collapsed ring. Nate: Heresian Bloodlust is here to get some revenge for last week! Robbie: Suddenly, this match just got very interesting! Steven: This is going to be ugly! Short Circuit is climbing to his feet, but he is quickly slammed back down to the mat as a result of a chair shot from Hessian. Riptah lunges at Hessian, but his efforts are quickly thwarted by a shot to the back from Cain and his sledgehammer. Cain and Hessian look onto Masters and Hawke! Robbie: Someone is going to die! Cain pulls the sledgehammer back and brings it down onto the gut of Masters that causes him to spit blood out of his mouth. Hawke rolls over only for his eyes to grow very wide as he sees Hessian standing over him with the steel chair pulled back over his head. Hessian brings the chair crashing down onto Hawke's head repeatedly rendering him unconscious. The fans are booing wildly at the top of their lungs! Nate: There is no doubt about who got the biggest response from the crowd this week! Robbie: They just decimated Civil Disobedience and Da Westsidaz!! Steven: BOCA~! Daisy rushes to the ring, but she is quickly stopped by Caleb and Minion. Caleb pulls a mic out of his pocket and tosses it to Hessian. Hessian catches the mic in order to address the crowd. Hessian: That's right all of you damn peon! It's your Tag Team Champions! We just thought that we would come out and greet all of fans! The boos are echoing throughout the arena. Hessian: Last week, Cain and I fulfilled our promise of a Dark Sabbath for the Red Army! Nate: Yeah, but it didn't hurt that that little hobo was the ref. Robbie: Show some respect to the champs, Nate! Hessian: We laid waste to Civil Disobedience in order to win these belts, we our titles against Propaganda and the Global Champion Vampir Nosferatu, and we ruined Krieg and Walczak last week in order to retain our belts! And then our victory is cut short by the likes of these four men! Tonight, we have shown everyone that there is no one that can stop us! Hessian tosses the mic to Cain. Cain: Week in and week out, we have promised Carnage! And week in and week out we have delivered on our promises! If the acting president Ed wants to bring the five time PCW champions to OSW in an attempt to remove these titles from our wastes so be it! That is just more fuel for the fire! There is no team in the OSW that can stop our reign of terror! Robbie: HA! Cain: We have just show you all what happens to those that interfere in our affairs! Last week these punks decided to get themselves some attention by jumping us after our victory. Cain lays a fierce kick into Masters head and then continues. Cain: This is only the beginning of our reign of terror. If you want these titles boys, you are going to need more than steel chairs and your skills to stop us! When you step into the ring with Heresian Bloodlust, you better come prepared for a war! Because a war is what you are going to get! Cain drops the mic on top of the bodies of the fallen tag teams. 'Et Fortuna' from Carmina Burana begins to blast the sound system of the arena as the fans boos are still echoing. Cain and Hessian exit the ring. As the pass over their victims, Cain and Hessian make sure to lay one more kick in for good measure and spit on the tag teams. Nate: Well, it seems that Civil Disobedience and Da Westsidaz hit a nerve last week! Robbie: Yeah and seems that Cain and Hessian just cut that nerve out! HA! Steven: Uh, what about Daisy? Hessian and Cain stop where Minion and Caleb are holding onto Daisy who is fighting with all of her strength to get free, but to no avail. Cain raises his massive hand up and starts to caress the young ladies face with it. She is still trying to fight to get free. Nate: Oh come on! Haven't they already proved their point? Robbie: Nope! Steven: Kill the Skirt! A slow smile stretches across Cain's face. Cain pulls his hand back only to bring it crashing back into Daisy's face with fierce bitch slap that sends her collapsing to the concrete floor crying hysterically. Nate: Good God! Robbie: HA! Steven: Fucking Skirts! Hessian and Azrael Cain begin to walk up the ramp with Minion and Cable following closely behind them. Before they disappear into the backstage area though they stop and the entryway and remove their belts. Cain and Hessian hold their titles up high into the air in order to egg the crowd's anger on further. After a moment, all four of the demonic figures walk back into the backstage area. Nate: Well, Heresian Bloodlust certainly made their presence felt! Robbie: Their presence? They also made a steel chair, sledgehammer and a fierce bitch slap felt tonight! Steven: That was great! Nate: Well, let's get to a commercial while the crew tries to get the ring ready for the next match! Robbie and Steven's laughter can be heard as they the scenes fades to black
