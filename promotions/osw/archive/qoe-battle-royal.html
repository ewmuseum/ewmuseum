Queen of Extreme: Battle Royal

14 Apr 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Queen of Extreme: Battle Royal Written: April 14, 2002 Earl: The following is an over-the-top battle royal! Participants will be disqualified from the match if they are thrown or willingly exit the ring over the top rope, and one or both of their feet touch the ground outside the ring. I want a good clean fight; no hair pulling, no wedgies, and no exposing yourselves or others -- save it for the Pay Per View. Ring the bell! The bell does just that, and immediately, the lump of ladies crash towards eachother, forcing Earl to scurry backwards and nearly fall out of the ring. Bethany Yamato makes the first big move of the match, cracking Lillith in the back of the neck with a knife-edged chop, then spinning and nearly taking Anna Keyson's head off with a kick. The purple-skinned femme ducks under the blow, however, and replies with a hard punch to the inside of Bethany's thigh, nearly dislocating her leg. Steven: Pfft. Now she'll be even looser! Amanda, Kid Wonder's valet, seems to be making a more cautious approach. Sticking to the outside of the battle, she circles around the mob, finally finding a target: Faith Paris. With an evil grin, she grabs the Virginian beauty's hair and pulls backwards, putting the suprised Paris off balance. With surgical precision, Amanda knifes a punch right into Faith's throat, dropping her to the mat like a sack of curly-haired potatoes. Robbie: Whoah, these chicks are fighting DIRTY! Which is, of course, just the way I like it. Primetime Laura Wilson, no longer facing Faith Paris, takes advantage of Amanda's momentary distraction, whipping a high kick straight into her shoulder. Before she call follow through, however, she's taken from behind by the former Queen of PCW, Brandi! The crowd cheers as Wilson pushes forward, trying to toss Daniella over the top rope, and nearly succeeding. Nate: We could have our first eliminatee soon! Robbie: And whoever goes first says goodbye to any chance of an OSW contract! Using every ounce of strength in her body, Brandi pushes against the struggling Laura Wilson, and seems just about to finish the job when Anna Keyson smashes into her from behind! Already off balance, Brandi flips right over her intended victim, losing her grip and landing hard outside the ropes, but lands on the ring apron. Keyson wastes little time in ripping both Laura and Brandi with a double clothesline, sending them both to the floor below. Robbie: Who landed first?!? Earl Heinrich, outside the ring again, lifts his mic, then points to Laura Wilson!!! LAURA WILSON HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!! BRANDI HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Nate: PRIMETIME LAURA WILSON HIT THE FLOOR JUST BEFORE BRANDI, SO SHE IS THE FIRST ONE TO GO AND SHE IS INDEED OUT OF THIS COMPETITION!!! Robbie: Whoohoo! G'day, mate! Nate: Don't laugh yet, Decker! Triumphant, Keyson turns around with a broad smile on her lavender face...a smile that turns to a look of shock as a smiling Faith Paris gives a quick wave, then suddenly drops, grabs Anna's legs, and flips her right over the ropes! ANNA KEYSON HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Steven: They're dropping like flies! Like big, purple flies with ugly bulges on their chests! Nate: They're called 'breasts.' Steven: I don't care what they're called. If my Boca doesn't have them, I don't want them. Faith flashes a dazzling grin at the crowd...and then ducks as Lillith's foot whistles harmlessly over her head. The Virginian beauty returns the move with a hard punch to the gut, and Lillith coughs and stumbles backwards for a moment before suddenly snapping off a series of knife-edged chops in the general direction of Faith's head. Slowly but surely, Paris gives ground, and finally ends up teetering for balance, pinned in a corner. Nate: This looks bad for everyone's favorite southern belle! Robbie: I don't see any southern belles. I see a goth chick beating up a some blond named after France, though. Nate: I thought Steven was supposed to stereotype women. Robbie: ...Low blow. Finally, one of Lillith's Judo chops slashes through Faith's desperate defenses, taking her on the temple and dropping her into the turnbuckle. Smiling slightly, Lillith steps back to ready the coup de grace...and is suddenly grabbed by the hair and whipped right over the shoulder and out of the ring by Bethany Yamato! Steven: Oooh, she's going to have a bald spot after that! Earl: LILLITH HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Nate: A...modified judo flip by Bethany Yamato! Cackling slightly, Yamato opens her hand, letting a thick clump of Lillith's raven hair drift down to the mat below. Her victory is short-lived, however, as Amanda suddenly plows into her with a running clothesline, draping the unsuspecting Yamato across the top rope! Robbie: No! Don't let Kid Wonder's little sex toy beat you! The struggle continues for a moment, with Yamato holding on for dear life while Amanda tries desperately to shoulder her over. Behind them, Faith Hill stands back up from the temple shot, takes stock of the scene before her, and smiles. Nate: Faith could win this right now! With a mighty battle cry, Paris charges...just as Bethany manages to drop to the apron and roll in, and Amanda is thrown to the side! Realizing her mistake too late, Faith dives right over the top rope, and is helped along by a quick shove from Amanda. Nate: Damn! Earl: FAITH PARIS HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Yamato quickly rolls back to her feet, and snaps off a quick kick to Amanda's shin, hobbling her. Before she can come around with a roundhouse, however, Amanda ducks under and simply tackles the bigger woman to the ground. She manages to lay in a few punches, but Bethany quickly regains her senses, and kicks out with both feet, launching Amanda away from her. Robbie: Come on, Bethany! Steven: Is this going to go on much longer? A murmer begins to rise from down the ramp, and the camera follows to the source of the noise: a massive, obese Japanese man, standing six feet fall and over five hundred pounds, plods down the ring at a liesurely pace. Nate: Who is that?! Robbie: I don't know, but he looks like so kind of sumo wrestler... Steven: I am less attracted to him then I am to Brandi. There is dead air for 1….2….3….4….5 seconds. The sumo lumbers calmly down the ramp, ignoring the fast-paced fight going on in the ring, and slowly climbs the steel steps to the apron. Nate: WOULD YOU LOOK AT THE SIZE OF HIM?!?! HE’S GOTTA BE 500 LBS!!! The ropes barely seem able to stretch far enough to allow his massive girth, and referee Earl Heinrich quickly slides in, yelling at the big man. Nate: COME ON EARL!! GET THAT FAT PIECE OF CRAP OUT OF THERE!!! Robbie: When you're a five hundred pound sumo wrestler, you don't need to explain things to ANYBODY. Earl steps up to get in the big man's face, but the sumo merely brushes past him with one arm, a gentle gesture that nonetheless almost sends Earl sprawling out of the ring. Then, with a slow determination, he somehow manages to climb up to the top rope, and perches there patiently. Robbie: He'd better move his fat ass, because I don't think the turnbuckle can take it! Steven: Ugh, normally I would make some suggestive comment, but he’s just….disgusting. Argh!!! Back at the fight, Yamato finally seems to be gaining a definite advantage. She chops left, then right, and then finally snaps a quick jab up between Amanda's breasts. Robbie: Awe yeah…right there baby!!! Nate: Shhhhhhhhhh!!! Then, before the blonde can recover, Bethany spins around with a huge roundhouse kick, catching Amanda in the cheek and sending her sprawling...directly under the huge sumo. Only now does Yamato seem to notice the big man, and she screams for him to stop -- too late. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Steven: Oh, no...I can't watch! With a couple of bounces for leverage, the sumo suddenly takes off through the air, landing a second later with his entire body weight on Amanda's prone form. Nate: MY GOD!!! AMANDA…..AMANDA’S GOTTA BE DEAD!!! The crowd erupts into a chorus of boos. Then, with the same slow, deliberate movements that marked his entrance, he stands, picks the nearly-crushed woman up, and tosses her directly over the top rope, further causing the crowd to erupt into a chorus of boos. AMANDA HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! Winner: BETHANY YAMATO View Lillith 's Biography
