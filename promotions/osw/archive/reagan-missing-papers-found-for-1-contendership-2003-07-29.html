Reagan Missing; Papers Found for #1 Contendership

29 July 2003

by Shanda Russell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own news post (#112) by Shanda Russell, dated 29 July 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Shanda Russell confirmed suspicions with four words in a Tuesday press conference. 'Jane Reagan is missing.' The CEO of Old School Entertainment, Reagan was witnessed falling under some sort of peril at the conclusion of Sunday Night Slam. Since then, no one has seen Reagan or has any knowledge of her wherabouts. But while Reagan might be gone, her handiwork remains. Shanda said that, during a search of Reagan's office, paperwork was found setting up a series of matches to determine the new No. 1 contender for the OSW World Heavyweight Title. Three qualifying contests will take place on the coming episode of Overtime, to air July 31. They are: Rhett Locke vs. Clark Carlisle (rescheduled from Slam) Scott Taylor vs. Silvio Rochelli Trashcan Man vs. Matt Register The inclusion of Rochelli is perhaps the most perplexing. A relative unknown, the millionaire worked his way into the good graces of Reagan prior to her disappearance--earning her praise on the most recent episode of Slam. Still Rochelli likes his chances. 'With my inability to do things wrong, my legions of fans will definitely not be disappointed with the result of my match with Scott Taylor,' he said. Compounded by the recent disappearance of OSW Commissioner Steven Smith and abduction of OSW President Trashcan Man, it's been a rough week for people in power in OSW. Although Trashy is back, Smith remains missing. Shanda denied rumors that authorities had presumed Smith dead, considering the flamboyant executive had not been seen since shortly after the Ascension pay-per-view. Shanda said that OSE is offering a $50,000 reward for anyone that can provide information as to the whereabouts of Reagan or Smith.
