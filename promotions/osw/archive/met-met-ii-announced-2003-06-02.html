MET II Announced

2 June 2003

by Staff

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own news post (#3) by Staff, dated 2 June 2003, from the MET II site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
This official press release was announced from OSE Personnel. After the recent announcement that the Queen of Extreme II contest would be a no go, Jane Reagan announced the following statement... 'After my partners decided to cancel the Queen of Extreme tournament, we have decided to move on and announce that there will be a second Most Extreme Team Tournament this year. 'We are accepting applications as of this moment. The applications will close on July 6. '32 teams will be accepted into the event and the first 16 will be announced on the July 13th Slam and the second group of 16 will be announced on the July 15th Assault. 'The contest will then begin starting on the 20th and run until the 30th of September with events running on both weekly shows.
