Corg vs. Aimz, Round Two of the Cruiserweight Title Tournament

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Corg vs. Aimz, Round Two of the Cruiserweight Title Tournament Written: May 25, 2002 Nate: Now folks, it would appear that we are finally ready to head to the ring for our first match of the evening, and it will be of the cruiserweight variety, as Amy Campbell will head to the ring to take on Corg. Robbie: Corg is going to gut her like a pig, so we can see the bodily fluids of the six guys she blew in the parking lot before the show pour out of her belly. There is dead air for 1…2…3….4….5 seconds. Nate: (still somewhat stunned by Robbie’s comments) On that note, lets head to the ring for our first match of the evening. 'Chemical' by No One erupts over the speakers, the loud powercords and fast-paced melody setting the stage for Corg and Shyme to step out from the back. As the fans send a downpoor of jeers to the ring whilst Corg slides in, Shyme screams 'GO TO HELL!', instantly sending the fans into another verbal assault. Robbie: COOOOOOORG~! COOOOOOOORG~! Nate: Hey Robbie, shut up before I shut you up. Steven: I'll be forced to shove my penis-in your mouth- if you do not shu-- Robbie: I know you didn't just say that. Steven: ...*gulp*... However, the fans chorus of boos went straight into ecstatic cheers when 'Only the Strong' by FLAW blasts over the P.A. Several of the males in attendance starts an 'AIMZ IS HOT' chant as Amy Campbell steps out, with a slight grin on her face. She surges toward the ring, charging at top speed. As she slides in the ring, the referee calls for the bell, and the match is underway. Robbie: YOWZA! She's one hot momma...but no match for...COOO-- Nate: If you say it, I WILL destroy you. Quickly Corg sends a stiff punch to Aimz's throat, and before she's able to clamp her throat with her hands he irish whips Amy into the ropes. As she vaults back, she finds herself caught by a big back-body drop, and she hits the mat hard. The impact leaves Campbell reaching behind her back and screaming in pain, however it only motivates Corg more as he jumps onto the third turnbuckle, spins, and does a huge guillotine leg drop from the top. Nate: C'Mon Aimz...Get up... Robbie: Sorry y'bastard, no getting up from that! Steven: Yeah, y'bastard! Nate: Damnit, the homo is always on your team! The fans are in sheer rage, spewing insults and cuss-words at Corg. The Silent Killer only laughs them off and lifts Amy up. He whips her into the turnbuckle. The impact is hard, however when Corg comes charging in, she dives out of the way and his shoulder smacks right into the post. Amy recovers, and as Corg lays motionless by the turnbuckle, she jumps to the third, and flips back into a huge moonsault! Aimz hooks the leg! One! Two! Thr-- no! Corg narrowly escapes defeat as he lifts the shoulder up! Corg reaches all fours, but he doesn't notice Amy bouncing off the ropes. As Corg reaches his knees, she dropkicks the side of his cranium to where he nearly drops right there. Robbie: ...How the HELL does she do it? Steven: Nooooobody knoooooows... Nate: Freegin' idiots. Amy steps outside and onto the apron to climb the ropes. The fans cheer as she signals to them all with her arms in the air. However, Corg jumps to his feet as Aimz prepares for lift off, and she finds herself shoved to the outside of the ring. The fans find themselves jeering Corg once again as he springboards off the ropes. But as Amy recovers and attempts to reach her feet, Corg finishes his move as he jumps OVER the top rope and corkscrew plauncha's onto the once standing, now fallen, Aimz. Nate and Robbie: JESUS CHRIST! Steven: HOLY BUJEEZUZ! The referee begins to count as the two lay there. Both recover at just about the same time, but Aimz is kicked in the stomach and rolled in the ring. Corg soon follows his opponent. But she reaches her feet first, and as Corg stands, she sends a side kick his way. The Silent Killer catches her foot, and in an almost poetic motion she flips upward, while with her free foot she kicks him in the face and afterward she lands safely upon one knee. Corg literally drops like a ton of bricks, his nose busted open. Nate: Yes! Yes! Steven: Do I make ye horneh babeh, do I? Do I make yeh friskeh!?! Robbie: hehehe...I don't care who you are, that was funny. The fans cheer in a state of sheer exstacy as Corg gets up and licks the drooling blood from his face. Aimz smiles and runs into the ropes. Corg catches her by the throat as she springboards off however. With the smile of a sadist that seems to fit his features so well, he knees her in the gut, spins her backwards, bends her back and does a wicked looking Inverted DDT... Blood for Blue. Robbie: YES! WOO! Steven: Do I make yeh-- Robbie: Shut up! Nate: hehehehe... Both seem to have given it there all, but alas the fans still boo the standing Anti God, Corg. With a sickening display, he spits on her face and kicks her cranium with a big boot. The fans jeer even louder after that, if it's possible. Corg gives the fans a middle finger, and lifts Aimz up by her hair. Corg whips her into the closest set of ropes, and with a miraculous display by Amy she jumps to the second rope, springboards off, and jumps onto Corg's shoulders. With her legs tightly around his neck, she spins and flips, sending Corg flying across the ring with that flying headscissors. Nate: Go Amy! Robbie: Bah! Go backstage and give everybody a job like you should be! Steven: I think that job would be quite handy for me, because I can do miraculous wonders with this mouth o' mine, of yes I can. Nate: ... ... ... ... Amy's about to win! The fans shout at the top of their lungs once again, however as Aimz jumps to the top turnbuckles, and signals for 'Dead Aim'... Robbie: LOOOOOK! The camera shifts to the aisle way to reveal Queen of Extreme runner up, Bethany Yamato, heading down the ramp. Yamato charges the ring, and verbally berates Aimz as she stands on the top turnbuckle. Aimz steps off and leans over the ropes, returning the insults with some of her own. And almost a repeat of Corg's last match, he's given the rest he's needed between the two's bickering. Corg stands and sneaks up cunningly behind Amy, until he locks in Death Blooms, a fatal tazzmission. Nate: No damn it! This isn't right! This isn't right! Corg: KILL!!! KILL!!! KILL!!! Amy Campbell soon finds herself running out of breath, and taps out in less then five seconds (which felt like years to her). Corg lets the hold go as the referee calls for the bell once more, and lifts The Silent Killer's hand in the air, declaring him the victor. Winner and Moving on to the OSW Cruiserweight Title Match at Ascension: Corg Robbie: And as Corg won, the sun rose, and once again...all was right in the worl-- Nate: Shut your trap, ass! Steven: Temper temper, babeh! Nate: Call me 'babeh' again, and watch me kick your face in! 'Chemical' by No One blasts over the P.A. again while Corg and Shyme exit, Corg being a step closer to Cruiserweight gold. Bethany Yamato soon enters the ring, and assaults Amy with a huge punch kick combo. Nate: WHERE THE HELL IS SECURITY!!! Robbie: WHO CARES!!! WHERE GOTS US A CAT FIGHT!!! Yamato continues her assault on aims…Left jab, side kick, left jab, spinning roundhouse, european uppercut, jumping front ki-- Robbie: Damn it! Amy ducks the jumping front kick then proceeds to lay Bethany Yamato with a flury of punches of her own. Left, right, left, right! Nate: AIMZ IS ON FIRE!!! Bethany Yamato stumbles back and into the ropes, only for Amy to send a HUGE left uppercut to Bethany's chin, and send her flying out of the ring! The crowd explodes. Nate: AMY CAMPBELL JUST DROVE OFF BETHANY YAMATO WITH A FLURRY OF PUNCHES!!! Robbie: Ask Aimz about where her cruiserweight title shot went… Nate: (sighing) We need to head to a commercial. We will return, right after these messages!!! Aimz stands on top of turnbuckle, pointing down and trashtalking Bethany as the scene fades to commercial. View Corg 's Biography
