Mr. Smith

2002-07-13
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mr. Smith Written: July 13, 2002 Suddenly, ‘Jane Says’ by Jane’s Addiction fades into the speakers, interrupting Nate in mid-sentence. The camera swings to the entryway, as OSW owner Jane Reagan surges out of the backstage area, dressed in an impressive looking dark gray business suit/skirt ensemble. Nate: Sheesh…Here comes OSW’s disease ridden whore of an owner… Robbie: Bitter? Nate: I’m not into S & M. Robbie: What? Nate: Nevermind. Steven: Penis. Nate: What? Earl Heinrich tosses Jane a mic, over the ring, and across the ramp, nearly smacking her in the forehead. You can read Jane’s lips as she lips “Bastard!” to Earl. The crowd gives a mixed reaction to Jane while she enters the ring and attempts to calm them down. Jane: *sigh* Thank you, thank you. You’re all too kind, really. Robbie: I know, we really are. Nate: Shut up!!! Jane stalks around a bit more, then continues. Jane: But I have to get straight down to business, immediately. I’ve put this issue off for two weeks now, but the time has come… Nate: What’s she talking about? Steven: Penis? Nate: *ignoring Steven* Robbie, what’s she talking about? Robbie: Penis? Nate sighs. Jane: …Corg. The crowd begins booing their heads off as they hear his name being said by Jane. Jane: Corg, our current Cruiserweight champion. Corg, a man, or a kid, I should say… a kid, whom has shown me his true colors over the past few weeks. Nate: Yeah, we’ve seen the colors of… Steven: *interrupting Nate* …his penis. Nate: … Robbie: Oh come on, Dumas. Steven: Don’t be such a party-pooper. Jane: He has attempted to rape, kill, and assault Aimz on numerous occasions. Now, maybe this type of behavior is acceptable in other promotions, but not in this one! I am sick and tired of Corg performing such perverted and violent actions on MY show. Nate: I hate to agree, but, go Jane, go! Jane: Corg is a sick, perverted, and insane bastard! He is a disgrace to the roster, and more importantly, he is a disgrace to this business! A quiet jolt of cheers emerges from the crowd, as they begin to accept Jane’s truth. Jane: He may have problems of his own, but he doesn’t need to take them out on a nice, kind, and lovely girl like Aimz. It’s just too bad that it had to end up this way, for Corg’s sake, because as of right now, Corg is no longer the Cruiserweight champion! Robbie: What?! Nate: And he damned-well shouldn’t be! That scumbag, Corg, deserves it! Steven: I think Jane’s a lesbian. Nate: I think you’re a lesbian. Steven: Yeah, something like that. Jane: That’s right, I am stripping Corg of his Cruiserweight- ahem, *the* Cruiserweight title. The fans previous cheers become overridden by a chorus of louder cheers, as the crowd shows that they agree strongly with Jane’s decision. Jane: Corg has said multiple times before that he always gets away with whatever he does. Well, not this time. No, no… you see I don’t think that simply taking away Corg’s title is enough. Nate: Where’s she going with this? Jane: Going to prison for a few days just isn’t enough for Corg… I think he deserves far better, oh yes. Steven: I don’t like the sound of this. Jane: Corg… ‘Machines’ by Crossbreed begins playing over the arena speakers, interrupting Jane. Corg soon emerges on the stage, as he stares down at the owner of OSW. Nate: Uh oh, I smell trouble. Steven: I smell… erm, no. Nate: Hopefully he stabs her… Robbie: Er… Nate: I am allowed personal commentaries. Jane: Corg, what a coincidence, we were just talking about you! Corg’s theme music dies down, and slowly fades away, allowing Corg to speak. Corg: Wow, I care. Jane shakes her head in disgust. Jane: Well, you know Corg, you should care. You should care about some things in life, like for instance… your job! Because as of this moment… Silence, a long pause flows through Jane’s mouth. Everything seems as if it’s going in slow motion. Silence. … Jane: You’re fired! A large gasp is heard throughout the crowd, then followed by cheers. Corg just ignores the crowd, and basically ignores Jane as he looks as cool as a cucumber. Nate: Oh my god! The Whore just fired Corg! Steven: Nooooooooo! Nate: Finally, justice has been served! Robbie: Bah. Corg: Funny you would say that, love, because I knew you’d attempt something foolish like that. Thus, I came prepared… Nate: Knife? Robbie: Condom? Steven: Beaded strand? Behind Corg, another gothic figure appears. He is a bit shorter than Corg, with the same skin tone, black hair, and… a business suit. Robbie: What the hell? Since when do Goths wear suits? Steven: I don’t know, but they turn me on. Corg: Janie, allow me to introduce you to my friend… Mr., uh… The gothic business man whispers something into Corg’s ear. Corg: …oh, yes, Donald Shits… erm, Donald Smith, my lawyer. In the ring, Jane rolls her eyes at the sight of Donald, as his appearance doesn’t look too impressive. Corg: Mr. Smith here has reasons to believe that within my contract, there is a clause that states I cannot be fired until the date of January 1, 2003. Nate: What?! What the hell is he talking about? Donald begins to make his way down the ramp, and approaches the ring. Jane: Yeah, I’m sure… ‘Mr. Smith’ enters the ring, and with the snap of his fingers, a briefcase appears in front of him. Robbie: How’d he do that? Steven: Magic, Robbie. Magic. Donald opens the briefcase. He scrimmages around for a while, but alas, he pulls out a stack of papers. He fumbles through the papers, his skin looking like camouflage next to the white on the stack of paper. Nate: This is a bunch of crap. Donald then hands the papers to Jane. Jane intently looks at them, then her eyes grow wide. Jane: What?!? And injunction?!?!? Donald merely laughs. Donald: That’s right Lady Jane, a judge has ruled that pending the outcome of the criminal charges pending against my client, he can not be fired. Signed sealed and delivered. Considered yourself served!!! Jane’s face drops. Nate: WHAT?!?! CORG CAN’T BE FIRED!!! Robbie: YES~! Jane shakes her head in disgust, then throws the papers back in Donald’s face. Jane: (seething) We’ll see about that. With that, Jane turns and storms towards the back as a sadistic smile crosses Corg’s face. Nate: THIS IS A TRAVESTY!!! HOW IN THE HELL COULD THIS HAVE HAPPENED?!?! Robbie: If the glove doesn’t fit… Nate: (interrupting) THAT’S QUITE ENOUGH!!! WE HAVE TO HEAD TO A COMMERCIAL!!! WE WILL BE RIGHT BACK AFTER THESE MESSAGES!!! Corg and Donald share a grim laugh as they watch an seething Jane Reagan head into the backstage area….as the scene fades to commercial. . . . . . .
