Alan Kriegman — history (August 2002)

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History They say that all people are good hearted, just with evil tendencies. Obviously, whoever said this has never met Alan Kriegman. A ruthless but impeccably groomed Evil Supergenius, Alan Kriegman seems to be more at home playing the role of a villian in a spy movie than in the wrestling world. However, Alan is not playing any roles, nor is he pretending to be what he says he is. A few years ago, Alan Kriegman was a globally recognized evil supergenius, bent on world domination and the destruction of anything that stood in his way. Though his plans were always thwarted, he never ceased in his attempts. During his career in the villian business, Alan met Barbara Bush...no, not the first lady. She was a stunningly gorgeous, shapely young woman who shared Alan's thirst for power and cravings for destruction. He quickly seduced her and before long they were married. Alan Kriegman and Barbara Bush set out to achieve together what they couldn't apart. You think I'm talking about those two teaming up to form some invincible coalition of evil and taking over the world, right? Sigh...I'm afraid you've got it all wrong. You see, Alan and Barbara KNEW that together, there was nothing they could not accomplish. Unlimited power, the glory of global conquest, and the elimination of all things good were just within their reach. All they had to do was take it. But was it really what they wanted? After all those years as an Evil Supergenius, Alan Kriegman realized that there was something even more important than the power he sought. For what good is power if one is lonely and unhappy? Ruling the world would sounds lucrative, but is it really? To never know the joys of love, of spending your life with your soulmate, of living the true dream? Alan Kriegman realized that he had been fooling himself in his missions to take the world by force. He thought that was what would make him happy, but the evil supergenius soon saw how wrong he was. Alan realized that he had found what he had really been looking for his whole life, he had succeeded. What he really wanted was to be able to live in peace, in the company of one who loves him. Barbara realized the same thing, and together, Alan Kriegman and Barbara Bush-Kriegman retired from the villiany business. But our story does not end there, for while he may have been retired, the world had hardly heard the last of Alan Kriegman. Upon retiring, Alan pondered where the best place to spend his life would be. It was during this search that he stumbled upon a small, virtually perfect suburban community in the heart of New Hampshire, preserved in all its quaintless. The name was perfect. Pleasantville, New Hampshire. Alan Kriegman and Barbara bought a house in the suburbs, complete with white picket fencing and a paperboy, and retired. Retirement in the 'burbs brought all the peace and happiness that Alan and Barbara were looking for...but it still felt as if something was missing. Sure, they were young, in love, and perfectly fine with the idea of spending their lives together in Pleasantville, but at the sae time, they both missed the excitement of their former days. They didn't want to go back to the world domination business, but something had to be done to add a little excitement in their lives. Then, one day, Alan Kriegman discovered professional wrestling. Oh, what fun it looked to be! His thirst for destruction would be satisfied, as would his need to at least conquer SOMETHING. A lethal martial artist and a veteran in the art of cheating, Alan Kriegman would fare very well in this industry. Alan Kriegman, with Barbara at his side, set out to take over the world...of professional wrestling. Things went incredibly easy for Alan at first. He'd find a random federation, plow through the roster like an unstoppable plague, and conquer the company. Before long, the federation would wither away and die after Kriegman had sucked the life out of it. Alan did not purposely mean to take over and kill all these federations, it just sort of happened. No federation could survive long with him on their roster, and as Alan's collection of world championship belts grew, the number of federations out there diminished. Then, Alan Kriegman found Primetime Championship Wrestling. It was a new company, less than three weeks old, and Alan figured it would be easy pickings. Oh how wrong he was. Alan spend more time in PCW than he did in all the other companies combined, spendig almost two years in the place. The evil supergenius became an icon in PCW. Along the way to glory in PCW, Alan Kriegman made few friends and numerous enemies. His most infamout ally, perhaps, was an overweight superhero by the name of Dillan Meyers. Smelling an opportunity to use the dim-witted champion of good to his benefit, Alan formed an alliance with Dillan. Good and Evil worked together, forming a tag team known as 'Goovil'. Together, Alan and Dillan became the first-ever PCW tag team champions, and later went on to win the rights to call themselves the OFFICIAL Greatest Tag Team in PCW History. Dillan Meyers would play an important role in Alan's retirement from PCW, but that will come later. Before long, Dillan Meyers had 'expended his usefulness'. Dillan would expend his usefullness multiple times to Alan their tenure in PCW, but whenever Alan found a new use for Dillan, the superhero would always be tricked into resuming their friendship. The first time Alan tossed Dillan to the curb, was was because he found a new group of friends. Three fellow geniuses, by the name of 'The Scholar' Howard Porter, Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman, and Nick Taylor. This foursome of brainiacs formed a nearly unstoppable alliance, and The Think Tank was born in PCW. The Think Tank's reign of terror in PCW faced much opposition, but it was not until the kidnapping of PHD, the crippling of Nick Taylor, and the dissappearance of Howard Porter that its fall from grace was complete. Alan Kriegman, the last remaining member of The Think Tank, went on in PCW. As time went by, he met his long lost twin brother and fellow PCW legend Frisco, won the PCW World Championship, reformed The Think Tank with Porter and PHD, broke up again, and achieved numerous other accomplishments that would earn Alan Kriegman the status of PCW icon. His career was long and storied, but all good things must come to an end. It was the first show of 2002. Alan Kriegman announced his retirement...but not before wrestling one last match. The one match he wanted more than anything else. Alan Kriegman betrayed his old friend Dillan Meyers one last time, and announced that the two would finally meet against eachother...in a Pleasantville Death Match. The Pleasantville Death Match was perhaps the single most destructive match in PCW history. The two battled for what seemed like hours. During the battle, Alan Kriegman's own house was destroyed, as were many other structures in the small community. Little did Dillan know that he was playing right into the hands of the evil supergenius, when Alan introduced his ultimate weapon... Metal Gear STREAK, a massive robotic re-creation of PCW/OSW legend Meanstreak, which Alan piloted. Kriegman used the gargantuan 'mech' in his attempts to destroy Dillan Meyers, but things did not go exactly as planned...or did they? The final confrontation was on a grassy hill on the outskirts of Pleasantville, when Dillan Meyers, all on his own, took on Alan Kriegman in Metal Gear STREAK. After a vicious struggle, Dillan has actually managed to remove Alan from the mechanical beast, and the two did battle on the ground...with Alan coming out on top. After decieving Dillan one last time with a false truce, Alan Kriegman won the match with a simple pinfall. 1-2-3, evil defeated good. But this was not the end. Alan didn't just want to win the match...he wanted to prove how superior he was by literally destroying Dillan Meyers. STREAK was just what he needed to do this. Alan Kriegman and Barbara Bush, together, climbed back into the cockpit of Metal Gear STREAK so they could eliminate Dillan once and for all...but all that they found was their own destruction. After launching an entire salvo of missiles at the pudgy champion of justice, Dillan Meyers, for the first time, showed the world that he really WAS a super hero. Using some unexplained power, he not only deflected the missiles, he set them on a new course. Right at Metal Gear STREAK. Stunned into disbelief, Alan and Barbara did little more than scream in horror. A few seconds later, Metal Gear STREAK was destroyed by the missiles, taking Alan Kriegman and Barbara with it. Or so we were all led to believe. The majority of the world had been convinced that Alan Kriegman truly was dead. There was no way he could have escaped being blown up along with STREAK. However, there were a few, who believed differently...these were the ones who knew the truth, that Alan Kriegman was simply too evil to die. Fast forward to the birth of Old School Wrestling, when a mysterious figure stroking an I-Cybie made his debut. This enigmatic man became known as The Vax. This is where our story ends. Alan Kriegman, along with his two old friends 'The Scholar' Howard Porter and Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman has revealed themselves as the evil masterminds behind 'The Vax'. Where will Alan Kriegman go from here? What does he have in store for Old School Wrestling? Can anyone stop him? As the old cliche goes, only time will tell...
