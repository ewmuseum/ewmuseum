Jesus St. Crux — history (August 2002)

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Jesus grew up in the town just across the border from El Paso, Texas - home of the legendary Guerreros. Jesus grew up wanting to be just like his heroes, the Guerreros, but his parents, being devout Christians, would not permit their only son to be a wrestler. Instead, they wanted him to become a priest. Jesus was too light hearted to settle into such a serious profession - and well... the celibacy thing kind of sucked. He did agree to do some missionary work though - and went to Samoa for his tour of duties. There he excelled at learning from the art of wrestling from some of the greats, but his small size led him to develop a style more similar to the luchadores than the power style of many Samoans. When Jesus returned home, he found that his parents had died in a car accident. Jesus was crushed, and left wrestling for a bit. Eventually, Jesus decided that the best way to honor his parents was to use his God-given gift at wrestling. So, Jesus floated from indy fed to indy fed, until finally, one of the big federations signed him, hoping to re-envigorate their Cruiserweight division. Despite his phenomenal success there, the fed eventually closed, and Jesus St Crux was left without a home. Always the optimist though, he toiled again through the indy feds until some old wrestling friends pointed him towards a federation where it seemed as though skill and determination always won out over those who who cheat their way to the top. Wanting some competition that viewed wrestling as he did, and being only twenty-six and still at his prime, Jesus decided to check out Old School Wrestling.
