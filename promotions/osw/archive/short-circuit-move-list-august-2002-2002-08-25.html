Short Circuit — move list (August 2002)

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Move List Diffused: (Shooting Star Press) Short Circuit knocks his opponent down and goes to the top-rope. He then jumps off the top-rope and back flips in mid-air, landing stomach-first onto his opponent. Educated Feet: (Kicking Frenzy) Short Circuit starts with a kick to the mid-section, then kicks out his opponent’s knees, making him collapse in a praying position. Short Circuit then roundhouses his opponent, knocking him to the floor. Short Changed: (Flipping Neckbreaker) Short Circuit goes to the top-rope while his opponent is facing him. He jumps off the top-rope and flips over his opponent, hooking the back of his head on the down, hitting a neck breaker. Simply Stunning: (Flipping Stunner) Short Circuit goes to the top-rope while his opponent has his back to him. He jumps off the top-rope and flips over his opponent, hooking his head on the way down, driving his opponent’s chin into his shoulder. DJ Deathlock: (Tarantula) Short Circuit has his back to his opponent while they are both in the corner. He then pushes himself off the ground using the ropes, hooking his own legs around his opponent’s arms. He then crawls over the top-rope and uses his arms to hook his opponent’s legs and stretches his opponent. Bling Bling: (Reverse Frankensteiner) The opponent goes to the top, but Short Circuit kicks one of his legs out. The opponent lands in a sitting position, facing the outside of the ring. Short Circuit jumps onto his opponent’s shoulders and leans back, throwing his opponent off the top, onto the canvas moonsault-like. Last Best West: (Frog Splash) Short Circuit knocks his opponent down and goes to the top-rope. He jumps off the top-rope and makes a frog-taunt by putting his hands between his legs and pulling them out quickly. He lands stomach-first onto his opponent. Caution Tape: (Tazzmission) Short Circuit hooks one of his opponent’s arms and his opponent’s neck with his arms. He starts to choke the opponent, falls backwards and hooks his legs around the opponent’s midsection, choking him. The opponent usually taps out or passes out during the move. Short Fuse: (450) Short Circuit knocks his opponent down and to the top-rope. He jumps off the top-rope and front-flips in the air, landing stomach-first onto his opponent. Afro-Kick: (Standing Side Kick) While standing, Short Circuit kicks horizontally at his opponent, catching him in the chin.
