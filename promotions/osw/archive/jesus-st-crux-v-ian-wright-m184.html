Jesus St Crux v Ian Wright

2002-05-25
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jesus St Crux v Ian Wright Written: May 25, 2002 The scene shifts elsewhere backstage to find Jesus St Crux and Caridad Mariposa walking around backstage. The Mexican has a look of utter determination on his face as he steps up on the ramp from behind. Outside in the arena the first chords of 'Fever Dog' play. With each drum riff, pyros explode to the side of the entrance ramp, and when the first words are spoken, Jesus and Caridad step out from backstage. Jesus stands at the top of the ramp, looks up at the ceiling, makes the sign of the cross, and then chops his hand downward. Jesus and Caridad run down and slide into the ring as pyros go off at the corners, and then they stand on opposite turnbuckles, and raise their arms as the crowd chants 'Jesus'. Nate: What a match-up we have coming up now! Jesus St Crux and Ian Wright, two men who have quite a history between them, including events last Saturday on Slam when each interfered in the match of the other! Steven: So long as they're in there getting all sweaty, I - Nate: Steven, don't complete that thought. Earl: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is an unsanctioned match for the UWA World Heavyweight Championship! Introducing first, being accompanied by Caridad Mariposa, standing 5'8' and weighing 220lbs, from Juarez, Mexico: Jesus St. Crux! 'Hate To Say I Told You So' plays across the arena soundsystem as Jesus dismounts the turnbuckle and Caridad exits the ring. Ian Wright starts walking down the ramp. He stares intently at the man in the ring - one of his greatest opponents - as he slowly climbs the ring steps. Earl: And his opponent, from Clane, Ireland, standing 6'3' and weighing 285 pounds: Ian Wright! Robbie: The man who didn't get a fair shake last week. St Crux was only going for the cruiserweight title, but Ian Wright was in a number one contender match for the International Title! And look! Now he's reduced to battling for a title from a defunct federation! Nate: These two men want to prove to themselves and to everyone else that - Steven: Umm... Where's the referee? Nate: Don't interrupt me, Steven. Steven: But... where's the ref? Nate: Hmm... Speedy Riggs was supposed to call this match - A burst of static explodes across the speakers, then 'Freak on a Leash' blares out. Three men run down the ramp - a Mexican dress in green, a man dressed in red and black, and the third wearing a lumberjack's outfit. Jesus St Crux and Ian Wright look at each slightly confused, then turn to face the newcomers. Robbie: More UWA guys, I take? Steven (singing): I'm a lumberjack and I'm okay! Nate: I think that's Iguana, Echo, and Jean Paul Pierre something something something and something else French Leblanc! Ian Wright's old running buddies in the Doctor's Den! Steven (singing): I wish I were a girlie! Robbie: Steven, if you finish that... Steven (singing): Just like my dear... CRASH! Steven moans as he hits the floor after having his chair kicked out from underneath him. Leblanc immediately slides into the ring while Iguana quickly mounts a turnbuckle. Caridad springs up underneath Iguana and knocks his leg aside, causing him to slip and crotch himself on the top turnbuckle. Leblanc tries for Le Big Boot on Wright, but the Irishman easily ducks. Wright pivots and catches Leblanc on the rebound and sends the big Quebecois through the air and smashing into the mat with a head and arm suplex. Echo tries to circle the ring, but St Crux charges and springs over the top rope, hammering the man outside with a suicide plancha. Caridad jumps and drags Iguana down from the toprope with a hurricarana; his head bounces off the mat and then he rolls out of the ring. Caridad stands near Wright and backs off out of the ring when the Irishman grins evilly at her. Nate: And out come Moose Malone and his security, along with a new referee. I don't know what happen to Speedy Riggs though. Robbie: What are these UWA guys doing out hear anyways? We just finished beating off CCW, do we have to beat off the UWA now too? Leblanc gets a little punchy with Moose Malone, but the big security guard pushes the Quebecois down while Jim Axtell drags Echo and Iguana to their feet. Ian Wright and St Crux ready themselves in the ring as Referee Gaston calls for the bell to start the match. Nate: Well, after a bit a turbulence, we're underway! Wright starts a bit faster than St Crux, immediately grabbing the smaller man and whipping him into the ropes. Wright holds on and reigns him back in for a powerful short-arm clothesline, knocking St Crux to the mat. A sharp elbow to St Crux's sternum follows it up. Wright drags St Crux to his feet and scoops him into the air; he pauses for a second, then powerslams St Crux back into the mat. The Irishman hooks a leg and covers. Un... Deux... St Crux kicks out as Gaston raises his hand for the trois. Wright places his forearm across St Crux's head and grinds it into the mat until Gaston tells him to stop. Wright immediately gets in Gaston's face, but turns in time to clothesline St Crux as the Mexican stands. St Crux continues his tumble and slides out of the ring; as Wright approaches, St Crux grabs his feet and pulls the Irishman off balance. St Crux turns Wright around so his head is hanging off the side of the mat, then St Crux jumps up to the apron, and drops a hangman's legdrop across Wright's neck. Wright crumples a bit, and falls out of the ring where St Crux pulls him up and whips him into the steel ring post. The Mexican charges and springboards off the crowd restraint and lands on the fallen Wright with a moonsault. Jesus regains his feet and rolls the hurting Wright back into the ring. Nate: St Crux is turning this match around. Steven: Mmm... latinos... St Crux sends Wright to the ropes and spins with his heel racing for Wright's head. The Irishman ducks and bounces off the ropes again, then catches St Crux with another attempted spinning heelkick, and plants St Crux into the mat with a modified spinebuster. Wright staggers to his feet after hitting the off-balance move, but St Crux somehow manages to grab a hold of Wright's arm and drag him back down to the mat. Weakly St Crux scrambles across Wright's back and starts locking in the Crucifix Crossface, but Wright gets to his knees and flips St Crux over, pinning him. Un... Deux... St Crux releases the hold and rolls Wright off of him. Nate: It looks like Wright's research is paying off. St Crux runs to the ropes and comes charging back as Wright stands. St Crux jumps and curls around the Irishman's back, floating all the way around for a Crucifix DDT. St Crux rolls Wright onto his back for a pin, but Wright raises and elbow that catches St Crux on his chin and staggers him back. Wright rises to his knee just enough to grab St Crux's leg and send him spinning with a Dragon Screw Legwhip. Wright exits to the ring apron and climbs to the top turnbuckle, and then lines up for a jump. As he sails through the air for a kneedrop, St Crux rolls aside and Wright smashes his knee into the mat. Wright limps to his feet in pain but is tripped up as St Crux lines up his knee with a quick dropkick. Jesus bounces off the ropes as Wright sprawls across the mat and jumps into a somerault, dropping another leg across the Irishman's neck. St Crux stays in the sitting position and pulls one of Wright's legs up as Gaston slides in for the count. Un... Deux... A thumb to St Crux's eye from Wright's free hand disorients the Mexican and he slides off Wright. Robbie: Ahh... sometimes it's nice to see a classic. Wright regains his footing just before St Crux, so he slams a shoulder into the Mexican to knock the smaller man into the corner. The Irishman whips St Crux across the ring and charges after him, but St Crux vaults the ropes in the opposite corner and Wright crashes into the turnbuckles only to stumble out. St Crux springs up to the top turnbuckle and leaps with a missile dropkick that sends Wright sprawling to the mat. Wright continues his roll and stands up, but he looks around trying to find St Crux, apparently having lost him. As Wright turns towards him St Crux launches a kick into Wright's gut, doubling the man over. St Crux hooks both of Wright's arms and twists him around, then snaps Wright's head into the ground with the Last Rites. St Crux quickly scrambles for a lateral press while hooking Wright's leg and Gaston moves into position. Un... Deux... Trois! Winner: Jesus St Crux Nate: Jesus St Crux quickly rolls out of the ring to the waiting arms of Caridad! Robbie: Ian Wright isn't too pleased with Gaston or St Crux. He's shifting his gaze between the two. Steven: What a lousy pin... I couldn't see my latino sweet's ass... Dead air... 1... 2... 3... CRASH! Steven sprawls on the floor as again his chair is knocked out from beneath him. Robbie: Thank you, Nate. Nate: Anytime... Jesus St Crux and Caridad make their way up the ramp, UWA Title in hand, as Ian Wright fumes in the ring. Nate: I think we'll give Wright a bit of time to himself and cut to a commercial until he exits the ring. Ian Wright lets out a scream of frustration as he stares out at the Mexican born-St. Crux clutching the UWA Title belt in hand, as he heads into the back as the scene fades to commercial.
