The Breakup

2002-04-27
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Breakup Written: April 27, 2002 The camera shifts backstage, where Tempest is seen, having slowed to a jog in the back of the arena. The camera turns to face her and focuses on her tear streaked face. She stops entirely and put her hands over her eyes sobbing. Heavy footsteps are heard as Ivan Stanislav appears from almost nowhere in front of Tempest. Ivan: TEMPEST!! He rushes towards her with his arms outstretched. Tempest looks up and backpedals away from him. Tempest: Leave me alone! You lied to me! I thought everyone was just trying to be mean saying those things about you, but they were true! Ivan shakes his head and slows down, not wanting to appear threatening. He keeps his hands out. Ivan: Nyet! Tempest, I can explain! Do not cry, I do not wish for you to cry..... Tempest backs away snuffling. Tempest: You should have thought of that before you lied to me! How am I supposed to trust you now? Ivan reaches out, his hand shaking, and grabs Tempest by the shoulder, dwarfing her. Ivan: It was different then! Things were different! I did not know...I did not wish to end what we had and tell you truth...I.... Tempest squirms, trying to get free of him. Tempest: But you could have told me the truth in the beginning! I know we were fighting then, I would have looked past that. but now.... let me go! Ivan roars loudly. Ivan: NO!! He grabs her with his other arm and struggles with her as a flash of urgent rage shoots through his face. Ivan: You will stand here and LISTEN TO ME!! Tempest cowers and struggles to free herself. Tempest: No! I've heard enough of your lies! What else are you lying about? Maybe something important, like even caring about me! Ivan grits his teeth. Ivan: I DO care for you Tempest!! Will you listen to yourself!! I am NOT a liar....it is not my fault!! I would not have done that had I not cared for what he have! Tempest: What we have is based on that lie! It's all a lie! She sobs and glares at him through the tears as she struggles. Tempest: LET GO OF ME! Ivan growls as Tempest manages to get free. She tries to rush past him but he whirls around and grabs her arm, almost jerking her off her feet. The crowd in the arena boos loudly. Ivan: I will NOT let you leave! What we have is based on something...something great Tempest!! Something bigger than both of us!! Tempest looks panicked as she struggles in Ivan's iron grip. Tempest: The only thing bigger than me is you an your lies. Maybe those cruel military guys know you better than you think. Ivan blinks as if slapped hard in the face. He freezes and stands straight. He lets go of her hand roughly. Ivan: Is that it? Is that what you think?! You started this and you wish to attack me?!!? Ivan roars and slams his foot into the concrete, actually cracking it. Ivan: YOU WISH TO NOT GIVE IVAN TIME TO EXPLAIN?! THEN LEAVE! Ivan points over Tempests' shoulder, standing suddenly very defiant. Tempest sniffles backing away. Tempest: I will... you've already explained plenty. Your lie started this... and now it's the end of it. Nate: OH MY GOD!!! IS THIS IT FOR IVAN AND TEMPEST?!?!? Robbie: HOPEFULLY, YES!!! Steven: Maybe I can catch him on the rebound!!! Nate: HEARTLESS BASTARDS!!! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!!! Robbie: NOT NOW!!!! Nate: I AM AFRAID SO!!! FANS, IF OUR FEED SUDDENLY CUTS, WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT ON SLAM!!!! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!!! Back in the backstage area, Tempest turns, sobs once then takes off toward the door. Ivan: Tempest!!!! Ivan blinks as she exits out the door he does not move....Ivan mutters to himself. Ivan: What have I done?? TEMPEST!!! Ivan rushes through the door after her into a car lot where Tempest is seen springing toward a black limousine. She turns at the sound of the door that Ivan slams open and screams at him. Tempest: LEAVE ME ALONE!!! I NEVER WANT TO SEE YOU AGAIN!!! Nate: (solemnly) Oh man… With her eyes puffy and tear streaked, she jumps in the back of the limo which screeches and peels away. Ivan rushes after the car, which is far too fast for him to catch. He then falls to his knees and lets out a mighty roar. NYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEETTTTTTTTTTTTTTT!!!!! As the smoke clears from the tires squealing, Ivan is left alone….with nothing but his howl of anguish…..as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling Logo appears in the lower, central portion of the screen….and the scene fades to black.
