Solution Comment On Debut

26 May 2003

by The Solution

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own news post (#55) by The Solution, dated 26 May 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
After making their arrival on Slam, under the name “The Soltuion”, many fans were left confused and outraged by the actions of former arch enemies Cassius Deamont and Chris Matthews when they ran in and ruined the proposed feud ending bout between Enforcer and Shocker and The Maximators. It was the opinion of some people, both fans and those within the company, that Deamont and Matthews still harboured ill will towards each other, and had merely teamed together to gain leverage in contract negotiations. The two men denied these reports, with Deamont’s exact words being that “The Solution are no longer willing to stand for the kind of political bullshit that played us off against each other in the first place.” “It’s like we said in the ring… We (The Solution) achieve our greatest success when we work together, and we’ll be proving that here.” When asked about how he thinks Enforcer/Shocker and the Maximators will react to their interferance in the match, Deamont had this to say… “Right now, we don’t give a shit who we piss off. I’m willing to go on record as saying there isn’t a single team in OSW right now who can hurt us, and certainly not either of those two bottom of the barrel duo’s. If either of them wants to try to get revenge on us, or anything like that, for what we did, they’re more than welcome to try.” Chris Matthews was also asked to comment, but declined, merely stating that Cassius had already said all there was to say.
