Ivan Stanislav vs. Frankie Bones and Drew Hostyle

2002-04-27
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivan Stanislav vs. Frankie Bones and Drew Hostyle Written: April 27, 2002 Who We Be' by DMX pumps across the arena, and the fans immediately erupt into boos. A moment later, two familiar figures step out from the back, ready for battle: Drew Hostyle and Frankie Bones, two-thirds of 3B. Enjoying the heat, the duo wave mockingly to the fans as they make their way down the ramp, to the ring. Earl: First, from Brooklyn, New York, they are the mindless groupies of our OSW World Champion...DREW HOSTYLE AND FRANKIE BONES, THREE-BEEEEEEEEE!! Just as the two black men climb up on the apron, they're cut off my another familiar tune: the Russian National Anthem. The jeers turn to deafening cheers as Ivan Stanislav, grimly determined and with veins bulging, stomps down the ramp with fists clenched into miniature boulders. 3B barely has time to reach their corner before the Russian Bear storms in. He stops in mid-ring, an odd expression on his face...and then, with initial hesitation, he turns to the fans and raises his fist high. The explosion of approval shakes the ring ropes. Nate: LISTEN TO THAT APPLAUSE! IVAN STANISLAV, THE RUSSIAN BEAR, IS FIRMLY ON THE SIDE OF THE FANS!! Robbie: THAT WON'T HELP HIM AT HEAD ON COLLISION! OUR CHAMPION DOESN'T NEED CHEERS! HE'S THE MASTER MANIPULATOR! Grinning happily, Ivan turns around...putting his back in perfect placement for a double spear from 3B! The big man grunts in suprise and goes down hard, while the fans boo loudly. Nate: Cheap shot! Where the hell is the referee?! We cut to the commentary table, where Steven Smith is hunched forward, speaking to the large-breasted woman beside him: Betty Lovejoy. Steven: ...So, if you test it, you'll find there really IS a difference between lubricants-- Nate: AHEM! Steven: 'Ahem' yourself, Natey. Oh, the match is on? You'd better get in there, Betty. Betty: Like, okay! Steven: Call me later, girlfriend! We'll go shopping! Waving, Betty slides into the ring, with the camera following her progress. Finally, we're able to see what's become of Ivan Stanislav; the Russian Bear is slumped against the turnbuckle, with Drew Hostyle and Frankie Bones taking turns delivering kicks and stomps. Realizing something's amiss, Betty walks over to the two men, and begins pulling angrily at Bones' shoulder. Frankie snarls something at her, and a new expression comes across the referee's face: determination. Nate: I don't think Frankie's going to get away with ignoring the ref! Robbie: What's she complaining about?! It's not like having both partners in the ring at once while pinning their opponent in the corner and repeatedly kicking him in face is against the rules! Lovejoy's sharpened nails poke into Bones' flesh, and the woman manages to turn him around, albeit with great effort. Enraged, FB again snarls something, but Betty isn't intimidated. As best she can, she explains why Frankie's not supposed to be doing whatever he's doing. Meanwhile, Ivan continues to be pummelled by Drew Hostyle. Nate: Someone make that idiot woman look behind her! With an angry shrug, Frankie shoves past the referee and returns to his corner. Satisfied, Betty turns back to idly watch Hostyle attempt to tear Ivan's nose off with his foot. Steven: Um, Betty, that's against the rules... Robbie: Truly a fair referee! Thanks, toots! Blood begins to flow from a slight cut above the Russian Bear's eye, and the wound apparently revives him; as Drew steps back to wipe his brow (stomping mudholes is hard work, after all), Ivan suddenly leaps up, SMASHING Hostyle's face with a HUGE rising clothesline! Nate: YES! THE BEAR IS BACK! With a furious roar, Ivan lifts Drew high overhead with a single hand, and slowly pulls his arm backwards... The crowd cheers, anticipating the blow... And Ivan throws his arm forward, SMASHING Hostyle with an IRON CURTAIN!! ...Well, at least he tries to, as Frankie Bones suddenly runs in and NAILS THE RUSSIAN BEAR WITH A RUNNING DROPKICK! Nate: NO! DAMN! SO CLOSE!! Robbie: YOU FORGOT TO WATCH YOUR BACK, COMRADE!! Steven: And Betty doesn't even realize anything's wrong!! Indeed, while Lovejoy quickly orders Frankie back to his corner, she doesn't seem to realize that he did anything wrong. Gasping for air, Drew Hostyle crawls over as fast as he can, and tags his grinning partner. Nate: And here comes Frankie! Smiling like a shark, FB sidesteps around the Russian Bear, positioning himself for a coupe de grace blow. When he finally realizes that no such opportunity is going to present itself, however, Bones merely shrugs and charges forward, SMASHING Ivan's little Russians with a kick to the groin!! Steven: Ooooh...poor Ivy... Groaning like a collapsing house, Stanislav rolls over, bringing his knees up quickly enough to knock Frankie off balance. Quickly enough, the big man shakes off the pain, and pushes himself to his feet. Nate: What a fighter! Ivan may have just lost his testes-- Robbie: He didn't have any to begin with. Steven: *at the same time as Rob* Now I'll never know true love. Nate: He's back up! Grinning confidently, Frankie sets up and suddenly snaps off a quick kick to Ivan's skull...which the big man abruptly grabs by the ankle, twists, and sends Bones face-first to the canvas! Quickly, the black man pulls himself back up, only to be met with a HUGE chop to the back of the neck! Frankie collapses like a sack of potatoes, his knees buckling from the force of the blow! Nate: WHAT A HIT! Robbie: THAT'S ILLEGAL! WHERE'S BETTY WHEN YOU NEED HER?! With a roar, Drew Hostyle suddenly leaps over the ropes, charging straight for the Russian Bear. Ivan grins slightly at the sight, and suddenly snaps a fist into Drew's stomach, doubling the smaller man over, and uses Hostyle's momentum to lift him straight overhead for a RED SCARE!! Nate: THIS ONE COULD BE OVER! AGAINST ALL ODDS-- Growling like a feral animal, Frankie throws himself to his feet and begins a charge...WHICH IS BROUGHT SHORT AS IVAN SUDDENLY BRINGS DREW HOSTYLE DOWN, USING HIM AS A GIANT CLUB TO BAT BONES AWAY!!! Nate: OH MY GOD!! Robbie: NOO!! THAT'S DEFINITELY ILLEGAL!! Despite the blood running from his cut eyebrow, Ivan smiles victoriously and raises his arm to the sky...AND IS SUDDENLY SCOOPED UP ONTO TWO VERY LARGE, TATTOOED SHOULDERS... Steven: OH, NO! IT'S-- ...AND PLANTED HEAD-FIRST INTO THE MAT WITH AN AT DEATH'S DOOR REVERSE DVD!!! Nate: NOO!! DAMN THEM!! DAMN SWITCHBLADE AND HIS LACKIES!! Robbie: NO POINT IN DAMNING A MAN WHO'S ALREADY GOING TO HELL, AND WHO'S GOING TO TAKE EVERYONE WITH HIM!! Triumphant, the Monster They Call Meanstreak spreads his massive arms, making his massive torso appear even larger, and bellows out a mighty 'ROAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAUNF!' Nate: Wha--? The crowd explodes as another familiar face joins the fray: Ed dives in, comes up, sprints forward, and drives his feet right into Meanstreak's spine with a running dropkick! The roar is cut short as the Beast flips right over the top ropes, landing in a heap outside. Ed follows without hesitation, using the ropes as leverage as he leaps over and lands right on Streak's head with a painful-looking flip! The fans roar in approval as the Hardcore Legend begins raining punches down on the disoriented Monster's face, twisting Meanstreak's skull back and forth. Nate: AND NOW ED HAS JOINED THE FRAY! THIS MATCH HAS COMPLETELY DEGENERATED! Robbie: THE FUN'S NOT OVER YET! HERE COMES ANOTHER TALENTLESS JOBBER! Recovering from being hit by his own tag team partner, Frankie Bones stumbles to his feet and immediately spots the unconcious Russian Bear. Grinning oddly, he slowly advances towards him -- in the background, Ed runs by with a small table monitor in hand -- and prepares to drop and make a cover. Robbie: LOOK BEHIND YOU, FRANKIE!! Steven: I LOVE THIS GUY!! A hand suddenly clamps down on the black man's shoulder, and Bones turns around...RIGHT INTO A DAMER SUPERKICK TO THE CHIN!! Nate: MICHEAL DAME!! MICHEAL DAME HAS JOINED THE MELEE, AND HE'S TAKING IT TO FRANKIE BONES!! Dame begins throwing punches, almost wildly, but with deadly accuracy. Frankie stumbles back, gasping for air, and suddenly finds himself pinned up against the ropes. He struggles desperately to shake the stars out of his skull, but runs out of time, as Micheal PLANTS ANOTHER DAMER KICK TO THE FACE, FLIPPING BONES RIGHT OVER THE TOP TO SPLATTER ON THE OUTSIDE MATS! Dame takes a moment to taunt, and then slides under the bottom rope, continuing his attack. Nate: THIS IS OUT OF CONTROL!! IVAN STANISLAV AND DREW HOSTYLE ARE UNCONCIOUS IN THE RING!! MICHEAL DAME IS BEATING THE TAR OUT OF FRANKIE BONES!! ED IS TAKING IT TO MEANSTREAK!! Robbie: Might want to double check on that last one. We cut to the side of the ring, where Ed has grabbed a steel chair and holds it high over an apparently dazed Monster. He holds the pose for a moment, then suddenly brings the chair down with a solid smack...ON STREAK'S FIST!! The now-dented chair flies right out of Ed's grasp, sailing high into the air before landing with a dull clatter in the ring. Ed, now disarmed, backs away a step as Meanstreak pulls himself up, and turns to regard the Commissioner with a sickening grin. Meanstreak: *picked up on a nearby camera* Ya find my 'weakness' yet, boyo? Ed: *also picked up* I'm working on it. Meanstreak: Try all ya want...ya ain't gonna find one. Before Ed can reply, Streak suddenly surges forward, throwing a massive fist straight at Ed's head. The smaller man sees it coming, however, and quickly ducks underneath with a clean dodge. However, Meanstreak is a beast with two hands, and the second one comes up to drill the Hardcore Legend right in the diaphragm, launching him straight into the air!! Nate: OH MY GOD!! WHAT A PUNCH!! Robbie: DIE!! SURVIVE THIS, ED!! It takes several seconds, but Ed finally comes down with a solid smack on the thin padding. Grinning, Streak slowly, methodically cracks each of his knuckles, and then starts forward with a slow, menacing pace. Then, abruptly, he's gone -- knocked aside as Micheal Dame is tossed into him, and Frankie Bones follows close on his heels! Nate: LET US NOT FORGET ABOUT DAME AND BONES! TWO SEPERATE BATTLES ARE RAGING AT ONCE!! Steven: MAKE THAT THREE! DREW HOSTYLE IS STANDING AGAIN, AND IVAN'S WORKING ON DOING JUST THAT! Rubbing his head, the Russian Bear slowly rises to a kneeling position, while Hostyle leans against the ropes and shakes his head to clear the sparkles. After a moment, he blinks, trying to take in the scene...and his eyes focus on the dented steel chair previously wielded by Ed. Nate: NO!! BETTY, TAKE THE DAMNED CHAIR!! Betty, however, seems preoccupied with her bra, which is somewhat visible through her twist-tie referee shirt. Unfortunately, we quickly leave her to her business, as Drew stumbles over to the chair, lifts it high...AND SMASHES IT DOWN ON IVAN'S FACE!! Robbie: YES!! Nate: NO!! Steven: BOCA!! Ivan wobbles on his knee, stunned...and is hit again, and again, and again. Finally, after a half dozen chair shots, the Russian Bear falls to the side, defeated, bloody, and unconcious. Drew crawls over to make the cover, just as Betty finished adjusting her bra, and she makes the count... 1... 2... 3! Earl: The winners of this matchup...FRANKIE BONES AND DREEEEEEW HOOOOSTYYYYYLE!! Nate: NOOOO!! DAMN!! THAT CHEATING BASTARD HAD TO USE A CHAIR TO TAKE IVAN DOWN!! Robbie: FALL, YOU OVERGROWN RUSSIAN RETARD!! FALL!! From the back, Moose Malone, at the head of a small mob of security men, race down the ramp and to the melee that still rages outside the ring. While Dame and Bones are seperated easily enough, it takes three security men and a stun gun to tear the Monster they call Meanstreak from the battered Ed. A few moments after Moose and the gang run out, two more familiar faces head down the ramp: Micheal Krieg and Tom Walczak. Nate: A disappointing turn of events, folks, but the Red Army sticks together. Robbie: Bah, the EMTs just can't carry Ivan's fat ass up the ramp. Stoic and serious, Krieg and Walczak advance forward...AND ARE CUT OFF FROM BEHIND BY THE HERESIAN BLOODLUST!! Nate: NO!! LET THEM AT LEAST GET THEIR LEADER?! Steven: THESE BATTLES WON'T STOP!! Robbie: LET 'EM JOIN THEIR BOSS IN THE HOSPITAL!! The security men who aren't busy subduing people immediately turn and race up the ramp, trying to break apart the four massive warriors battling it out halfway down. Just before Moose's boys can reach them, however, Micheal Krieg takes a sudden plunge off of the ramp, slamming with wet thud on the cold, hard cement below. Walczak battles on alone as Hessian and Azrael press the attack, but soon enough, the three remaining fighters are broken apart. Nate: MY GOD!! THIS MATCH HAS BEEN NOTHING BUT A GIGANTIC, BLOODY-- Robbie: Clusterfuck. Nate: EXACTLY!! WE'LL BE BACK RIGHT AFTER THIS BREAK, FOLKS, SO STAY TUNED!!
