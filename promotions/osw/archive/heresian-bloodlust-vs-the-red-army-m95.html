Heresian Bloodlust vs. The Red Army

2002-04-28
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Heresian Bloodlust vs. The Red Army Written: 28/04/2002 Nate: Welcome back to Head On Collision! And here we are! Our first title match of the evening! These massive men have been at each others throats for weeks now and now tonight, all of that will come to a crushing climax. That’s right folks; coming up next Krieg and Walczak will be taking on Cain and Hessian. Up on the jumbotron, a montage of scenes from the past few weeks begin to roll as Nate continues to talk. Wang pinning Caleb is one of the first highlights that are shown in fool. Nate: It was only a couple of weeks ago that Heresian Bloodlust one the titles from Civil Disobedience, but since then Krieg and Walczak have been in the shadows waiting for their opportunity to fight for the belts. And it was shortly after that when Communist Party Leader Wang pinned the disgusting manager of Cain, Caleb. The scenes continue in montage fashion until this clip reaches climax of Caleb leaping up on the turnbuckle ripping open his trench coat in Superman style to reveal a referee shirt hidden underneath. Nate: Then the Red Army was thrown a curveball when it was announced that Caleb would be the referee for all of Heresian Bloodlust’s title defending matches. The scenes continue to roll across the screen. The vicious attacks and high impact moves scroll across the screen. It begins to slow down and finishes with a wicked sledgehammer shot to someone by Cain. Nate: And coming up next, four of the most destructive wrestlers in OSW will face off with the Tag Titles on the line. Robbie: Krieg and Walczak are finally going to discover what happens to people who follow Ivan Selloutislav!! Steven: Four powerful men and only one prize! Nate: That’s right! Coming up next, the Tag Team Title match! The camera shifts to Earl Henrich standing in the ring waiting to make the match’s introductions. Earl slowly brings the mic up to his lips as the fans are cheering already from impatient anticipation. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, Boys and Girls, Children of all ages! This next match is for the OSW Tag Team Titles and is scheduled for one fall! The referee for this match is none other than the fowl little hobo himself, Caleb! Caleb emerges from the backstage area with his referee stripes on and starts to make his way down to the ring as the fans boo wildly in disgust of this vile little old man. Nate: Well, Caleb seems ready for tonight! Caleb climbs into the ring and starts to waddle around the ring as the fans’ boos have not let up one bit. Steven: You think he took a bathed for this? Nate: Doubtful! Very doubtful! Earl: And now, representing the Red Army and weighing in at a combined weight of 722 pounds! And coming to the ring first, from Berlin, Germany! Weighing in at a heft 347 pounds and sanding at seven feet tall… “Private Eye” by Alkaline Trio begins to play to the fans delight! Earl: He is The German War Machine, Michel Krieg!!! Krieg appears on the entryway and starts to flex his muscles as the fans cheer. Earl: And his partner, coming from Gdansk, Poland. Weighing in at 375 pounds and standing at six feet ten inches… “Bounce” By System of a Down begins to blast through the PA system and the pops from the crowd get even larger. Earl: He is the Polish Punisher, Tom Walczak!!! Walczak enters the ring area next to Krieg. Both of them look at each other for a second and exchange a nod. Both of them take off down to the ring with their eyes fixed on Caleb. Nate: This doesn’t look good for the little hobo. Caleb gets an “Oh Christ” look of panic on his face as a result of the charging communists. Walczak and Krieg and enter the ring and immediately start to chase the referee around the ring. Robbie: Hessian and Cain better get out here quick. Nate: You know what a sad day it is when our fans have no one to cheer for except for the Red Army! Robbie: Damn sell outs! Earl continues with the introductions. Earl: And their opponents. They are the OSW Tag Team Champions! They come from parts unknown! They weigh in at a combined weight of 725 pounds! The arena goes dark and the fans begin to boo wildly. “Carmina Burana” begins to sing out through the speakers. Krieg and Walczak are still trying to get their hands on the wiry little hobo who has managed to completely elude them. The strobe light begins to flash as the bloody pentagram emerges on the jumbotron. A huge wall flame explodes from the entryway, but Hessian and Cain do not appear from behind the wall of flames! Nate: What the hell? Robbie: Oh this is never good. Steven: Where ever they are, they better get out here quick. Look! Walczak and Krieg have Caleb cornered outside the ring now against the barrier. Both of the communist slowly approach the fear struck hobo. Just as Krieg and Walczak are about to get their hands on the hobo, two massive figures fly into the camera shot from over the barrier and strike both Red Army members in the head with some foreign object. Krieg and Walczak drop to the floor. Nate: Someone get the lights on! Robbie: No! Let those sell outs get what is coming to them. Steven: Get em! The lights in the arena come on again and everyone is shocked to see Krieg and Walczak already on the ground holding their bleeding heads. Nate: Well, this one is starting out with a bang! Robbie: You mean a whack! The camera shot changes though and Cain and Hessian are standing over the bodies of the communists with their belts in their hands. The servants of Satan drop their belts and start to pummel away at the already injured challengers. Robbie: That’s what they get for trying to hurt a defenseless old man. Hessian and Cain stop their beatings so that they climb into the ring, so the match can officially start. Caleb slides into the ring and signals for the bell. The bell rings and the hobo starts to count out Krieg and Walczak. 1… 2… 3… Krieg and Walczak are starting to move. 4… 5… Both of them start to crawl towards the ring. 6… Both are at the apron now. 7… Krieg stumbles and falls back to the floor. 8… Walczak manages to get himself into the ring. Nate: Well, we almost had out first ever count out at a Pay per View! Walczak is getting to his feet, but before he can he is met with a clothesline from both Hessian and Cain. Robbie: He’s all alone! Ha! Get him guys! Rip the sell out apart! Cain and Hessian pick up the stunned Walczak both of them slap a hand around his throat and hoist him into the air only to bring him crashing back down to the mat from a double Chokeslam! Steven: That was some impact! Robbie: You like that don’t you? Nate: Hey man, back off! He promised to be good for this! Hessian leans over to pull Walczak up again. Before he gets the Polack up though he is hit the back with a falling leg drop from the top rope delivered from Krieg! Nate: And Krieg is back in this one! Robbie: …joy. Cain lunges at Krieg and stars to hit him in the face repeatedly with a combination of lefts and rights. Both Walczak and Hessian are starting to stir. Nate: A true battle of the Behemoths! Walczak is the first one to his feet and pulls Cain off of his partner by the beast’s ponytail. Cain falls to his back hard on the mat. Walczak starts to lay the boots into Cain. Krieg is getting up to his feet. The German stops for a second to wipe some of the blood off of his face. Krieg starts to head towards his partner to help with Cain, but before he takes two full steps, he is hit with a running spear from Hessian that sends both of them tumbling through the ropes! Nate: This is all out chaos! Robbie: (In true Mr. Burns fashion) Excellent! Nate: Well, it seems that Cain and Walczak will be the first to face off here in the ring tonight. Walczak is still punching away at Cain until Caleb grabs hold of one of the Polack’s hands and orders him to stop. Walczak gets to his feet only to push the five foot ref over. Caleb rolls across the ring. Walczak turns around only to be met with a quick kick to the gut by a now standing Cain. Walczak is doubled over until Cain grabs hold of him and manages to pull up into the air for a second before sending him back to the mat with a Powerbomb! Nate: Look at these two guys. They have the same physical stats as one another. Steven: And Hessian only has three pounds and two inches more than Krieg! Nate: This is one hell of a match up! Cain follows up with a leg drop but he is met with a boot to the tailbone from Walczak. Outside the ring, Krieg has managed to get control. HE irishwhips Hessian into the turnbuckle face first. There is a loud Clang noise that resonates throughout the arena as Hessian’s face plate dings off of the metal post. Krieg looks up to see Walczak crawling towards the corner looking for a tag. Nate: Hessian and Cain are down! This is the Red Army’s chance! Robbie: Get up Cain! Come on Hessian! Walczak lunges at the corner and manages to make the tag to Krieg. Krieg hurtles himself over the ropes and rushes at Cain. Krieg starts to lay into the beast with kicks to the ribcage. Cain is rolling across the mat trying to escape the barrage. Krieg leans over and pulls Cain up to his feet. The German war machine manages to get his hand around Cain’s throat and hits him with a stiff chokeslam! Nate: Cain is being viciously assaulted and Hessian is just beginning to stir. Krieg goes for the pin. Caleb slides into position. Robbie: What the hell is that lunatic doing? Nate: This could be it. Caleb slams his hand down hard on the mat twice real fast. 1… 2… And… No! Caleb just extends his middle finger no his right hand and starts to laugh at the angered German. Nate: Oh, that is so not a smart thing to do right now! Krieg slams his hand down hard on the mat and charges at Caleb, releasing his pin on Cain. Caleb slides under the ropes to escape. Krieg is at the ropes yelling at the little hobo, but when he turns around though, he is greeted with a lunging clothesline from Cain that sends the German toppling over the top rope. Krieg falls to the concrete floor below, but he lands on Caleb! Nate: I just think Krieg crushed the hobo! Robbie: NO! Steven: What the hell was Cain thinking? Cain falls to his knees and starts to head towards his partner Hessian is up on his feet leaning for a tag. They quickly slap hands. Hessian enters the ring and starts to taunt Krieg who is barely stirring. Suddenly Hessian is flipped backwards into a suplex from Walczak! Walczak starts to yell at Hessian. He continues to kick away at the sides of Hessian. Nate: Cain is hurting, Krieg and Caleb are out, and Hessian is being assaulted by Walczak! This is total chaos. Steven: Wahoo! Walczak continues his barrage of kicks to the midsection of Hessian. Robbie: Wait a minute! What the hell is coming out from under the ring? The apron is stirring underneath the ring. Suddenly Minion the Imp appears from under the ring with Caleb’s sledgehammer in his hands. Robbie: I was wondering where that little guy was! Steven: There is no way he can use that thing! It’s too big! Minion crawls into the ring under the ropes and slowly starts to stalk towards Walczak all the while laughing to himself. Steven: There is no way… Minion pulls the hammer into the air and starts to wobble from the weight. Steven: …in hell! Minion brings the sledgehammer down and hit cracks Walczak across the back, but because of the weight of the hammer, the head comes flying back and hits Minion in the face rendering him unconscious! Steven: Told you so! Nate: Yeah, but he did his damage! Walczak is on his hand in knees in pain. Hessian battles to his feet and lunges down at Walczak with his elbow extended. He hits the Polack in the face with and elbow drop! Nate: This is getting vicious! Walzcak’s nose starts to bleed. This just pisses off the Polack though as he gets to his feet and spears Hessian to the mat. Walczak is on top of Hessian now and pounding away at Hessian’s already scratched mask. Blood from Walczak’s knuckles remains on the Goliath’s mask. Outside the ring, Cain is standing over top the now stirring Krieg. Cain reaches down and pulls him off of Caleb. Cain hoists Krieg into the air and powerbombs him into the security barrier! Nate: Good God! Steven: BOCA~! Robbie: HA! Cain crawls into the ring under the ropes and grabs his sledgehammer from the mat. Cain pulls the hammer back. Nate: This is going to hurt! Cain brings the sledgehammer back… Steven: And messy! Cain is eyeing up Walczak! Robbie: Unleash the Fury!!!! Cain slams the hammer into the small of Walczak’s back. The Pollack topple over to off of Hessian in pain. Hessian gets to his feet. Cain holds the hammer high into the air and is about to bring it back down onto the chest of Walczak, but someone snatches the hammer out of Cain’s hand. Robbie: What the hell… Nate: It’s Krieg! Cain turns around only to be greeted with the head of his hammer being jabbed into his face. Cain topples over to the mat. Krieg now focuses on Hessian, but before he can strike again, Hessian hits him with a big boot that causes the German to drop the hammer down to the mat. Robbie: Take that Sell out! Hessian leaps up onto the turnbuckle and leaps off hitting Krieg with a leg drop! Krieg is flopping around holding his neck. Cain is back to his feet now, but there is blood all over his face and his nose is crooked. Nate: Cain’s nose has to be broken! Cain grabs hold of his nose and with a sharp movement he snaps it back into its original position! Blood spurts forth onto the mat! Steven: EEEWWW! Nate: GOOO! Robbie: Black! Crowd: OOOO! Nate: The beast seems a little pissed off right now! Robbie: You would be too, if someone just broke your nose with your own hammer! Cain hoists Krieg up and only to put him into a powerbomb position. Cain turns though and powerbombs Krieg into the turnbuckle! Nate: The Ninth Gate! Cain just hit Krieg with his single finisher! Robbie: Ha! Cain goes for the pin, but Caleb is still outside of the ring not moving. Hessian gets outside of the ring and tries to get Caleb up but to no avail! Nate: There is no ref. Walczak is up on his feet and grabs hold of the sledgehammer. He pulls it back and brings it down with a great force onto Cain’s back. There is an instant welt where the hammer hit Cain’s flesh! Hessian sees what has occurred and quickly under the rope, but he is met with another sledgehammer shot to the face thus denting his mask. Blood pours forth from underneath the mask. Nate: Ladies and Gentlemen, you might want to remove your kids from the TV now! Robbie: No Way! Tough Love baby! Walczak gets Hessian up and into a powerbomb. He spins around with the massive Goliath up in the air only to bring Hessian down onto the mat. Walczak is in the sit down position trying to pin. Nate: There is the Polish Powerbomb! Steven: This should be it! Robbie: Yeah…if there was a ref you jackasses! Walczak gets up in frustration. He is the only one on his feet right now. Walczak leans over to grab the sledgehammer. Nate: This is definitely going to be bad. Steven: Very, very bad! Robbie: Who the hell is that coming out the crowd? Walczak is oblivious to the man coming out of the crowd; he is grasping the sledge with a crazed look in his eyes. The intruder is on the apron now and jumps up onto the turnbuckle. Robbie: Hey that’s… The intruder leaps from the turnbuckle and hits Walczak in the back of the neck with a double leg drop. Robbie: …Reno Davis! Steven: Reno Davis! Nate: Reno Davis!!! Robbie: Is there an echo in here? Davis looks down at the unconscious Walczak and Krieg and exits the ring in order to get a folding a metal chair. Davis slides into the ring with the chair and proceeds to beat Walczak in the face with it! Nate: Davis is the only one that is upright in the ring! Steven: And he is beating The Red Army down! Robbie: Ha! Davis brings the chair down one final time onto the Polacks’ face, but this time he leaves the chair there. Davis springs up onto the turnbuckle again only to leap of and deliver a leg drop on top of the chair that rests on Walczak’s face. Nate: AH! Steve: Ouch! Robbie: HA! Crowd! OOOoooOOOO!!!! Davis rolls out of the ring and starts up the steel ramp screaming at the unconscious Red Army members. Nate: He said that he was going to make his presence felt this week! Steven: And he made his weight felt on Walczak’s face! Inside the ring, there is no one moving right now except for the imp known only as Minion. The little imp is trying to get to his feet despite the fact that his face was busted wide open by the glancing hammer to the face. Nate: Minion is up! That little annoying bastard is moving! In the corner though, Krieg is starting to stir, while Minion is trying to shake some sense into Hessian. Hessian is beginning to stir slightly as Krieg is trying to get to his feet. Hessian is now on all fours in the middle of the ring. Krieg is leaning on the turnbuckle. Minion rushes over to Cain and is attempting to shake some sense into him now. Nate: If Minion gets Cain up as well, this could be trouble for the Red Army. Robbie: Excellent! Cain is stirring as well now. Minion rushes to the outside of the ring to try and get Caleb to awaken. Krieg dive forward and tries to pin Cain, but Caleb is still outside the ring! Steven: I don’t think any of these guys know what is going on right now! Nate: That is probably due to the fact of the extreme blood lost by everyone in this match! Hessian lunges at Krieg and manages to have the German release his grip on Cain. Krieg rolls out onto the apron under the ropes. The dazed Hessian gets to his feet and crosses to his the unconscious Walczak only to him to his feet. Once the unconscious Walczak is up, Hessian hits him with the SIX CUBE!!!! Nate: Dear God in Heaven! Steven: Or Hell! Robbie: Six Cube!!! Nate: This one is over! Hessian goes for the pin only to realize that there is no ref! Hessian slams the mat hard with his hand, but he is caught from behind by a diving elbow from Krieg! Nate: Maybe not… Hessian releases the pin and lies on his back. Krieg reaches down to pull Hessian to his feet, but a black gloved hand grabs hold of the German’s wrist only to irishwhip him across the ring. Nate: Cain is up! Cain is up!!! Steven: Boca~!!! Krieg bounces off of the ropes and comes back at Cain who bends over for a back body drop. Krieg is sent over the beast’s back. Out of shear exhaustion Cain topples over backwards onto his back, but the damage has been done. Krieg is caught out of the air by an upright Hessian who hits him with a Death Valley Driver! Nate: Heretic’s Wrath! Heretic’s Wrath! Hessian is on his back on top of Krieg who is on his back on the mat. Nate: I guess that could be considered a pin. Steven: Look over there! Minion is throwing a Caleb into the ring in order to make the count. Caleb is crawling over to the pile of bodies in the middle of the ring and begins count. 1… 2… 3… Winners of the Match and still OSW Tag Team Champs: Heresian Bloodlust! Nate: Heresian Bloodlust wins! They have retained the belts! Robbie: Death to all sell outs! Steven: BOCA~!!!! Minion and Caleb are helping the totally drained Cain and Hessian to their feet. The managers are celebrating the victory. Minion rushes over to the belts that are still outside the ring. He brings them back inside the ring. Caleb and Minion start to put the belts back on Cain and Hessian! Both battered warriors are being helped out of the ring by their managers. Nate: What a match! Steven: That was everything that we’ve promising! Robbie: HA!!! Nate: Shanda Russell is outside the ring right now waiting to hear from the champs! Russell runs into the shot and starts to ask Cain and Hessian questions. Shanda: You both have been boasting of a victory and tonight you got it! You get to keep the belts. How do you feel? Cain: How do you think we feel wench!!! Shanda recoils out of fear Cain’s temper. Hessian: I think I am going to celebrate like a true champion tonight. Minion, bring this harlot with us! She will satisfy me greatly. Nate: Did he just call Shanda a harlot? Steven: What does he mean satisfy? Robbie: Even better! Minion grabs hold of Shanda and starts to drag her up the ramp towards the backstage area. Shanda is trying to get herself free, but to no avail. Nate: Someone do something! Hessian wants to sodomize poor Shanda! Steven: Can I watch! They are all at the entryway at the top of the ramp, but before they can disappear into the backstage area, both Cain and Hessian are struck from behind with steel chairs. Nate: What the… Steven: Who the… Robbie: Where in the… Then the chairs are swung back again and brought down on top of Caleb and Minion’s head thus crumpling the two managers to the ramp. Shanda falls to the ramp as well, but she was not hurt from the chair shot to the little imp’s head. Nate: Someone came to Shanda’s rescue!! Steven: its Hawke and Masters! Civil Disobedience! Robbie: NO! Weren’t we punished enough earlier this evening when we had to watch their match? Masters and Hawke hold the chairs high over their heads as the fans cheer at the top of their lungs in delight of the Suburban Bad Asses! Nate: I can’t believe it! Steven: BOCA~!!! Robbie: Kill em! Kill em all!!! Masters and Hawke celebrations are cut short though as they both are struck down from behind by two more chair shots from unknown figures standing behind them. Nate: GOOOD GAWD!!! The fans are cheering wildly at the sight of the wrestlers that are standing behind the fallen bodies of Hawke and Masters! Nate: THAT’S RIPTAH AND SHORT CIRCUIT!!! THE FIVE TIME PCW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS, DA WESTSIDAZ ARE HERE!!!! Steven: Riptah can rip me anytime!!! Robbie: ARGGGGH!!! Riptah and Short Circuit stand over the fallen bodies of both tag teams holding their chairs high in the air getting pops from the crowd. Nate: DA WESTSIDAZ ARE HERE IN OSW!!! WE NEED TO HEAD TO A COMMERCIAL!! WE WILL BE BACK AFTER THESE MESSAGES!!! The shot starts to fade to black on Riptah and Short Circuit standing in the middle of the entryway still working the crowd over the limp bodies of Heresian Bloodlust and Civil Disobedience! The cheers of the fans are still as loud as they ever were though as the scene finally fades to black!
