Clay Vendetta/Jim Axtell vs. Griff Truxell/Kory Storm (6/1/02 - Slam!) Written: 6/1/02 Clay Vendetta/Jim Axtell vs. Griff Truxell/Kory Storm Match Result: Loss Via: Pinfall (Truxell uses brass knuckles on Vendetta) Stipulations: None. Notes: This marks the first match between Vendetta and Truxell. Nate: Don't even start me, Smith...Up next folks, we have will have tag team action featuring Griff Truxell and Kory Storm taking on two OSW favorites in Clay Vendetta and Jim Axtell!!! This match is a toss up guys. Robbie: Toss up?? You've gotta be kidding me! One, they've got two of the most impressive resumes this sport has... two, they're facing team Rent-a-Cop and Vanilla Midget... and three, they're up against a guy who's not even a wrestler! Nate: Hey! Jim Axtell's tough as nails and will never quit. Robbie: Spare me the cliches, Dumbass. Axtell's going down faster than Stewart Hix on a drag queen. Steven: Awwww, snap! As the two enter the ring and head to their corner, the music dies down... and is replaced by 'Carry On My Wayward Son' to a HUGE pop! Nate: Vendetta and Axtell are here and the crowd is 100% behind them! The two are no sooner out of the curtains then they are rushing the ring, sliding through the ropes, and meeting their respective enemies half-way, slugging it out in the center of the ring! Nate: WHOA! These two teams aren't even going to wait for the bell, they want it right now! Trying to maintain a semblance of order, Referee Gaston slides into the middle but is quickly forced to duck away as the combatants keep fighting! Vicious in the middle are Clay Vendetta and Griff Truxell, and Storm and Axtell are duking it out in one of the corners! After about fifteen seconds, Gaston calls for help, and out rush Speedy Riggs and Pimply-Faced Intern Ref, FINALLY managing to separate the snarling wrestlers and return them to their corners. Nate: Well, it took a pack of officials, but it looks like we're going to be able to get this match underway... *DING DING DING* The bell finally rings, and it looks like Axtell and Storm are to be the first two (legally!) in the ring. Laughing at the security guard, Storm taunts away, but Axtell is taking his time and scouting out Storm. Motioning for Axtell to 'come on', Storm laughs... and is met with a hard right to the nose! Stringing together a group of slow but powerful punches, Axtell knocks Storm back into the ropes and swings again... but Storm ducks around the punch and turns Axtell back towards the ropes, where he lets loose a barrage of knife-edge chops on the security guard! Nate: Axtell's going to have to look out for moves like that against a cagey team like Storm and Truxell... without much wrestling experience, those two are going to be able to exploit the weaknesses of the security guard-turned-grappler. Robbie: Allow me to demonstrate why I'm a world-renowned columnist and you're a peon, Dumas... all of that verbal diarrhea can be summed up in two words: 'Axtell sucks.' Steven: Mmmmmmm... dreamy... I LOVE a man in uniform... yowza!*WHACK* Whipping Axtell across to the opposite ropes, Storm takes a step forward and places a well-executed dropkick right into Axtell's face! Picking Axtell up, he grabs him by the arm and puts him down to the mat with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker! Taking the bruised Axtell by the hair, he drags him over to the friendly corner, he tags in Griff Truxell, who immediately heads to work with a trifecta of kneelifts to Axtell's gut. Picking the security guard up, he's pressed by Truxell and dumped to the canvas, where Truxell goes for a quick cover... ONE! TWO! ... but Axtell kicks out. Picking him back up, Truxell whips him back into the friendly corner and traps him there, and it's clear that Storm is getting in a few shots too! Coming out of his corner to assist is an angry Clay Vendetta... but Referee Pierre Gaston holds him back, and the two continue to pound away on the helpless Axtell! Finally backing to his corner and still protesting, Vendetta relents, and Gaston heads back to the action, where Truxell makes a showy tag to Storm. The two enter the ring, picking up Axtell and FLATTENING him with a huge double-suplex! Truxell returns to the corner to beat the five-count, and Storm proceeds to stomp away on Axtell and go for the pin... ONE! TWO! ... but Axtell kicks out again, and Storm punishes him by pounding away on the mat. Picking up Axtell, Storm sets him up for a BIG piledriver and implants Axtell, covering again... ONE! TWO! THR... no, Axtell's shoulder is up again! Storm protests a slow count to Gaston but is met by a clear show of two fingers and an audible 'deux!' from the Frenchie ref. Robbie: Slow count! Nate: The hell it was! Axtell is taking a pounding here, though, against the two ring veterans. He's got to get out of the ring and make a tag soon. Picking up Axtell, Storm drags him back to his corner, tagging Truxell. The man they call 'Wrestling Itself' whips Axtell into the ropes and takes him down, face-first, in a drop toe-hold! Sliding to the mat, Truxell hooks the leg and face and wraps Axtell up in an STF! Robbie: Yes! Break him! Brutality on the police, I love it! Nate: You're horrible.Robbie: 'Incorrigible', Nate. 'Incorrigible'. Steven: Could I have it in a sentence? Nate: Hey, I didn't know you watch the National Spelling Bee. Steven: All those little boys...*WHACK* Nate: It's always a new low with you, Steven. Axtell looks to be in trouble... but he's got a free arm and proximity to the ropes, and with a small struggle, grasps the bottom ropes! Nate: Axtell is on the ropes! He might not have the wrestling know-how to match with these two, but he's got at least twice the heart! Robbie: Heart, schmart. Gaston forces an angry Truxell to release the hold... and he does, dragging a battered Axtell to his feet and whipping him into ropes, going for a BIG sweeping clothesline... but Axtell ducks! Stunned by the miss, Truxell is only able to turn around and get an arm out before Axtell comes back off the ropes with a HUGE flying clothesline, sending both men sprawling to the mat! Nate: WHAT A MOVE! A last burst of effort from the exhausted Axtell sends both men to the canvas! Gaston starts the count as both men lie on the canvas...ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Crawling to his corner is Axtell as Truxell shakes off the cobwebs... Nate: AXTELL NEEDS A TAG HERE, AND VENDETTA IS STRAINING TO MAKE IT! Robbie: Yeesh, volume. FIVE! SIX! Up on one knee is Truxell, breaking the count... but with a final lunge Axtell tags Vendetta's outstretched hand! Racing into the ring Vendetta goes RIGHT after Truxell! Nate: Truxell and Vendetta have wanted at each other ever since Truxell cost Vendetta a match two weeks ago on Slam! and this feud has been boiling ever since... now we finally get to see the two of them go at it! Robbie: Oh, for Christ's sake, Dumas, it's frickin' Clay Vendetta... who cares? Apparently, the crowd doesn't agree with Robbie, as the crowd erupts watching Vendetta lay a set of big left hands into Truxell's gut! Reeling, Truxell is backed against the ropes, and Vendetta whips him out... but Truxell manages to dive out of the way of a drop-kick, tagging in Kory Storm instead to a shower of boos. Nate: Truxell wanted no piece of Clay Vendetta! Robbie: Get it straight, Nate... it was a smart tag to get out of there and get a fresh man in. Storm has to face the fiery Vendetta, though, who greets him with a quick boot to the midsection and an armdrag to the canvas. Stomping away on Storm, Vendetta brings him back up... and RIGHT back down with a a snap powerbomb! Dragging the dazed Storm to his feet, Vendetta irish-whips him into the friendly corner... and tags in a refreshed Jim Axtell, who uses the opportunity to go right to town on Kory Storm! Nate: And now Axtell's got HIS chance to get a little revenge... wait a minute, what... what's going on on the ramp?? The crowd breaks into heavy boos, and a quick cut of the camera shows the reason: stepping out from behind the curtains and moving purposefully down the aisle is Kanaida, a sneer on his face. Nate: What the HELL is Kanaida doing out here? He's got NO part in this match! Robbie: Hey... hm. Wait, give me a sec to justify this... thinking... thinking... ah, here's one! 'Because it pisses you off, Nate.' That's good enough for me! Nate: Watch it, Decker, or I'll bust your OTHER kneecap... the one you don't use as an excuse to pussy out of everything anyway. Robbie: Point taken. Meanwhile, within the friendly confines of the squared circle, Axtell is laying into Kory Storm with big, roundhouse punches, backing Storm into the ropes. Grabbing Storm's arm, the security guard whips Storm across the ring, gets a running start, and meets him halfway with a VICIOUS shortarm clothesline! Storm is down and Axtell, who has turned, can now see Kanaida, who's now right at the end of the aisle, facing the side of the ring. Locking eyes, Kanaida is shouting... Nate: Kanaida is flinging insults from ringside and it looks like he's gotten Axtell's attention! And indeed he has, and it's enough to get Axtell to come to the ropes and jaw back. What he doesn't realize is that behind him, Kory Storm is slowly but surely getting to his feet. As the two exchange unpleasantries, Storm bounces off the back ropes... but at the front of the ring, Axtell has had enough of Kanaida, who is taunting Axtell by putting himself just barely out of reach and motioning for Axtell to bring it on. Ducking down and lunging underneath the bottom rope, Axtell grabs for Kanaida and misses... but in ducking, he unknowingly avoids a flying clothesline by Storm, who accidentally throws himself over Axtell, OVER the ropes, and, with a shocked look on his face, RIGHT onto Kanaida!! Robbie: Incoming!! Nate: Storm was shooting for Axtell but he hit Kanaida... and from the looks of the pile on the outside, Kanaida is NOT a happy camper! Steven: I like camping. Did I tell you about my time in the Boy Scouts? Robbie: Alright, even I'm stunned by the awfulness of that comment. Kory Storm rolls off Kanaida, winded and hurting... but Kanaida, apart from the momentary stunning, is okay, and LIVID. Turning to Storm, he immediately starts laying down vicious stomps on the prone Storm... until a cadre of security storms the ringside area. Robbie: Yeesh, it's a rent-a-bacon get-together... who's gonna tell them that we're all out of donuts? Nate: Shush! Kanaida's already interfered in this match too much. Dragging Kanaida back towards the curtain as he struggles and yells, the security guard pulls back; but in the ring, the confused Axtell turns around... to meet a swift kick to the stomach by Griff Truxell, who's entered the ring in Kory Storm's absence. Axtell doubles over and Truxell grabs his head... and PLANTS him with a PISTON DDT! Robbie: Awwwwww YEAH! SECURE THAT, BITCH! Nate: Piston DDT! Truxell could have this one in hand... but what's going on?? Pierre Gaston is refusing to count!! With a foot planted on Axtell's chest, Griff Truxell has a cocky cover on... but Gaston grabs him by the wrist and tries to drag him towards his corner as Truxell protests! Nate: Griff Truxell is not the legal man, Kory Storm is! As Truxell heads back to his corner, Axtell crawls across the ring... and with a last burst of energy, tags in Clay Vendetta! Vendetta heads across the ring, gives consideration to going after Truxell (who's still being held back arguing with Gaston) but instead steps out of the ring and pulls in the legal Kory Storm, who's still groggy from his collision on the outside. In the middle of the ring, Vendetta hoists Storm and hits him with THE THROWBACK! Covering Storm, Vendetta waits for a count, but there's a commotion... Nate: What the hell? Kanaida's broken through the security and is on his way back to the ring! Kanaida has rushed the ring and hopped up to the apron to try to get through to Axtell, but referee Gaston meets him there and is shouting for him to leave the ring! With Gaston distracted, Vendetta gets up to come over and try to get the ref's attention... but he's tapped from behind, turns around... and is BLASTED by a punch to the face from Griff Truxell, who slips something metallic off his hand and back into his tights! Nate: THIS IS BULLSHIT! GRIFF TRUXELL JUST TOOK VENDETTA OUT WITH A SET OF KNUCKS! Robbie: Woo-HOO! Now THAT's Old School Heelery, let me tell you! Vendetta looks to be out and busted open on the mat... and as security catches up to Kanaida again on the outside, Truxell drapes Storm's arm over Vendetta! Gaston turns around, sees the cover, and counts... one... two... THREE! WINNERS: Kory Storm and Griff Truxell The crowd immediately erupts into boos... and more than a few pieces of garbage and refuse make their way towards the ring, where Truxell props himself up on the top turnbuckle, sitting and grinning as he soaks in the abuse of the fans. Catching a flying soda cup, he wings it back into the audience, laughing away as trash starts to fill the ring. Nate: This is a crock of bullshit! Robbie: Oh, STOP hating on Truxell. He just showed he's willing to make any sacrifice to win a match! Nate: HE CHEATED! Robbie: 'Cheated' is such an ugly word, Nate. How about 'advantaged himself'? Steven: Ooooooooh, sounds naughty! Nate: You're both idiots. Truxell wins in a match he did NOT deserve to win, folks, and we've got to head to a commercial... which ought to give our ring crew a little time to sweep up the cups and trash that Griff Truxell is being pelted with. Back in a few, enjoy the ads...
