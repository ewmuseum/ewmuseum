Armour v. Sebastian Toomes

2002-07-07
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Armour v. Sebastian Toomes Written: July 7th, 2002 Howard Porter, hobbles out once more on his crutches. He shifts his weight onto his good side for support and holds up a microphone. Robbie: What a trooper! The man can barely walk! Nate: What the hell? Wasn’t he hobbling on the other side last time! Robbie: How old are you now Nate? Nate: 28…why? Robbie: Because your sight must be going. Porter: Toomes! Sebastian Toomes, out here with your lackeys, Civil Disobedience and…and our beloved wench of an owner, Jane Reagan. Porter shakes his head. Porter: Tsk tsk tsk. I understand you are used to people buying pay-per-view events to see that kind of stuff from you, but really. The crowd screams its disapproval. Porter: Quiet while your betters are talking, Tulsa! I have more I.Q. points in my toenail clippings than you have in all of your state schools and universities combined! But I didn’t come down here to run down Tulsa. Oh no, besides this third appearance from me managing to once again spike ratings on this high school media-club level production, I’m here to defend myself against your slanderous remarks against me on national television! But you see, a good Commissioner cares for the state of his wrestlers, and I, Sebastian, am a good Commissioner. That is why in my debilitated condition… Nate: That’s just bullshit! Porter is afraid to get in the ring! The crowd boos as well. Porter: I said, in my weakened and injured condition, I can not allow myself to enter the ring and risk possible injury, I simply wouldn’t be fulfilling my duties. Nate: Porter is using his power to get out of a Toomes ass kicking! Robbie: Hey! He’s a good Commish! Would you rather it still be Ed? I could not possibly be the stalwart and heroic opponent to your treachery and your seemingly ever-present need to cheat to win… So, seeing as I am a genius and have already looked 20 steps ahead of you… Toomes takes a step back and crosses his arms intently. Porter: Make that 21…I have provided you with an opponent. Someone… Someone who has the raw ability and power to go toe to toe with THE STAR, as you like to call yourself. Someone that I have picked to give you… a fair fight. I mean, after all, isn’t that what you want? A fair fight? Toomes interrupts. Toomes: No, I’d be happy just going up there and kicking your ass right now. How’s that sound, genius? Porter smiles. Porter: No, I don’t quite see that happening… You see, I’m very well protected… Your opponent tonight, you might say he’s my…armor… against little bugs like yourself… Toomes: Yeah, right, Porter- Toomes is cut off as a huge man, 6’7” and about 370lbs, grabs him from the rear and hits a MASSIVE German suplex on Toomes! Robbie: WHOA! Nate: PORTER’S HIRED GOON IS OUT HERE AND TAKING IT TO SEBASTIAN TOOMES!!! Seth and Trevor rush the huge man but Seth gets clotheslined hard to the mat while the big man grabs Trevor’s throat and hits a devastating Spiral Chokeslam! Trevor is slammed down to the mat, utterly decimated! The Crowd: OOOOOOOOoooooooooo!!! The camera shifts to Jane, an accomplished in ring performer despite her ownership role, as she runs off the ropes and attempts a hurricanrana, but the big man easily powerbombs Jane on top of Trevor! Nate: KILL!!! KILL!!! Robbie: That’s my line… Porter smiles as his stand-in destroys The Elite. Porter: If I may steal Earl’s thunder… Sebastian Toomes’ opponent, weighing in at 367lbs… Hailing from Chicago, Illinois… CALL HIM…ARMOUR! Nate: THIS IS ARMOUR?!?! I’VE ONLY HEARD THE NAME IN WHISPERS IN THE PAST!!! HE HAS BEEN BANNED FROM EVEN NO HOLDS BARRED COMPETITIONS!!! The camera shifts back to Porter. Porter: Enjoy Sebastian. Ring the bell! DING! DING! DING! Nate: MY GOD! TOOMES AND THE ELITE WERE UTTERLY DESTROYED! AND NOW PORTER WANTS TOOMES TO FIGHT THIS MONSTER? Robbie: This guy… HE’S FUCKING HUGE! Toomes gets into a crouching position and leaps at Armour, trying to pull off The Future Shock, but Armour grabs Toomes’ hair as he tries to spin around Armour’s body! Flinging Toomes roughly to the mat, he sinks his elbow into Toomes’ spine. Toomes grunts in pain as he rolls away, but immediately Armour is on him, spearing him through the ring ropes! Nate: This guy is a monster! Toomes is being eaten alive out here! Toomes hits the concrete and rolls into a ball as Armour runs off the opposite side of the ring and flies outside the ring with a suicide tope, smashing into Toomes and the announce table! CRASH! There is a hiss of feedback as the announce table is flattened on top of the three announcers. After a moment of frantic activity by Dumas, Decker, Smith and Heinrich, Armour stands, brushing himself off. Toomes isn’t moving. Armour picks up a monitor, hefting it with one hand, testing its weight. Toomes groggily gets to his feet and Armour tosses the monitor at Toomes’ face. Then, a split second later Armour DROPKICKS the monitor into Toomes’ face as the hapless former OSW World Champion catches the monitor in his hands. Toomes’ head rocks back, a spray of blood coming from his now broken nose! Nate: Shit! Is this on? Can anyone hear me? Robbie: I think I’m on… The little green light is showing! Nate: Steven, you on? Steven: Yeah… This guy just stiffed the shit out of SeaBass! Truer words were never spoken. Toomes lies on the concrete, clutching his broken nose as Armour stands over him, his eyes on the fallen ex-champion writhing in pain. Armour leans down and grabs Toomes by the hair, lifting him to his feet. Toomes lashes out with a right hook, smashing his fist into Armour’s face, but Armour shrugs it off. He grabs Toomes by the throat and leads him over to the ringpost. Hefting him up, Armour CHOKESLAMS Toomes into the ringpost! Toomes exhales sharply as the wind is knocked out of him, and he crumples to the concrete. Nate: Ok, we’re back, folks… This Armour character has decimated the Elite, and now he’s utterly destroying their leader! Robbie: THIS GUY IS FREAKING HUGE! Steven: Christ… They’re calling in the EMTs, I just heard it… Armour picks Toomes up again, getting him into a powerbomb position… He picks Toomes up and SLAMS TOOMES’ SPINE ONTO THE BARRICADE! Crowd: HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! Toomes spasms as he hits the concrete, a river of blood coming from his broken nose. Blood is everywhere as the former OSW Champion tries to get away from this newcomer who has single-handedly destroyed the Elite. Armour isn’t having any of that. He picks Toomes up by the hair and Irish Whips him into the steel ring steps. Toomes thunders into the steps with a loud crash, and he lies still. Nate: Someone needs to stop this match! Toomes is being destroyed by this beast! Robbie: THIS GUY IS FRICKIN HUGE! Steven: GET OVER IT! Nate: I’ve never seen Sebastian Toomes this wrecked before! Armour grabs one of the steel ringsteps and slams it down on Sebastian’s head, the dull sound of metal hitting skull resounding through the arena. The crowd is seething, part of them cheering for this monster, part of them screaming for Porter’s head, part of them in sympathy for Toomes. Nate: TOOMES IS DESTROYED! STOP THE DAMN MATCH! Again, the steel ringsteps slam down on Toomes’ head. Again. Again… Until Armour tosses the ringsteps away and picks up Toomes, who is now covered head to toe with his own blood. Nate: SOMEONE CALL THE PARAMEDICS!!! The crowd is outraged. He lifts Toomes to his feet and drives his massive fist into Toomes’ throat with a nasty throat chop. Toomes falls back to the concrete, choking. Armour continues the assault with kicks to Toomes’ ribs, driving Toomes towards the ramp. Nate: Damnit, this wasn’t a match! This was a set-up! Robbie: Jesus, I’ve never seen Toomes get so fucked up… Toomes is picked up again, in a powerbomb position as referees and security officials come pouring out from the back… Armour SLAMS Toomes down on the steel ramp, smashing Toomes’ head and shoulders against the steel. The referees swarm him as Armour powerbombs Toomes AGAIN on the steel ramp! Nate: JESUS!!! FINALLY SECURITY IS TRYING TO STOP THIS CARNAGE!!! Robbie: I have lost all respect for Toomes!!! Armour is the man!!! SLAM! Toomes is powerbombed once more before the officials can tear Armour away, who swats at the officials like flies. An EMT team immediately comes down the ramp with a backboard. Toomes is covered in blood, unconscious, and helpless on the steel ramp. ‘Don’t Go Off Wandering” by Limp Bizkit hits as Armour walks up the ramp, chuckling to himself. Porter is wheeled out again and the big man takes Porter’s wheelchair. Porter: Good job, Armour… I know you can’t hear me now, Toomes, but take heart… You never knew what hit you. You never had a chance to resist the power that IS Armour! And if you have any unbroken bones in your body, and you wish to keep them in that state, I highly suggest you not interfere in my plans anymore… Armour? Let the professionals clean up this trash. Nate: THIS IS A TRAVESTY!!! HOWARD PORTER HAS GONE TOO FAR!!! Robbie: Too far to entertain us?!?! There is no such place too far!!! Nate: WE NEED TO HEAD TO A COMMERCIAL!!! WE WILL BE RIGHT BACK AFTER THESE MESSAGES!!! Armour wheels Porter away from the broken form that is former OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian Toomes, as ‘Genius’ hits and we fade to commericial.
