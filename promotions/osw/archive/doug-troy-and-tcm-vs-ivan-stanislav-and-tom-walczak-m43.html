Doug Troy and TCM vs. Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak

2002-02-09
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Doug Troy and TCM vs. Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak Written: Feb. 9, 2002 Nate: Welcome back folks! It’s time for the Saturday SLAM! Main Event! Robbie: The Red Army against Trashcan Man and Doug Troy! This is going to be funny! Steven: Why do you say it’s going to be funny? Robbie: Because Ivan and Tom are pissed. That can’t be a good thing! Nate: Yeah, but are they ever not pissed? Robbie: When Ivan got the World Title he was pretty….no..no, he was still pissed…. Earl Henrich stands in the ring to the roar of the fans. He pulls out his note cards and gets the nod from the announce position…. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, it is now time for the OSW Saturday SLAM! Main Event!! Introducing first, weighing in at a combined weight of 525 lbs! Doug Troy and Trashcan Man!!!! “Brainless” by The Urge plays over the loudspeaker as Doug Troy and Trashcan Man come out to an enormous ovation! TCM carries a large metal trashcan with him as the two make their way down to the ring. Nate: Listen to this capacity crowd!!! Robbie: Listen to this theme song! It fits perfectly!!! “Brainless” slowly dies down as the lights dim to a deep and dark red….. Earl: And their opponents, weighing in at a combined weight of 775 lbs, representing The Red Army, “The Russian Bear” Ivan Stanislav and “The Polish Punisher” Tom Walczak!!!! “The Soviet National Anthem” by the Russian Red Army Choir blares over the loudspeaker as the crowd goes sour right away. From the backstage area, Speedy Riggs, wearing his yellow and red referee stripes, makes his way down to the ring! Steven: Looks like Speedy Riggs is the referee for the match! Nate: How did that happen? Riggs slides into the ring and eyes TCM and Doug Troy. Riggs goes over to tell TCM to get rid of the trashcan, but TCM yells at him. Riggs quickly recoils, deciding to wait instead. From the backstage area again, Ivan Stanislav, Tom Walczak, Alexei Ruslan, and Communist Party Member Wang head down to the ring!! Steven: Hmmm…I count four of them! Robbie: Very deductive, Steven…. Nate: Why are there four of them? Robbie: I’m guessing that Alexei is watching out for Ivan, and Wang is watching out for Walczak. It makes perfect sense in my mind! Nate considers arguing, but decides to forget about Decker altogether. Stanislav and Walczak climb into the ring in unison, causing the entire squared circle to shake. Riggs points to TCM and yells to Ivan, trying to get him to make TCM discard the can. Ivan shrugs and laughs at TCM and tells Riggs to let him use it! Henrich bails out of the ring as Ivan nods to Walczak. Ivan gets on the apron as does Doug Troy. Nate: Looks like Walczak and Trashcan Man are going to lock up first! Riggs calls for the bell and steps out of the way. Walczak cracks his knuckles and glares across the ring at Trashy. Trashcan Man slaps the bottom of his trashcan and takes a few steps towards Tom. Tom takes off at a charge, seemingly going for a spear. TCM tosses the trashcan, bottom first, straight at his head, SLAMMING it into his skull! The rimmed end hits Walczak’s head and opens up a gash! Nate: Y’know, you’d think that bald idiot’d be a bit smarter than that…. Robbie: He’s from which country again? Walczak stumbles backwards and shakes the cobwebs from his head. TCM charges forward, not letting Walczak get too much time to recover. TCM locks up with Walczak. However, Walczak easily gets the upper hand. He lifts TCM into an inverted atomic drop and drops TCM onto his knee. Trashy yells and grabs his crotch, only to be leveled by a shortarm clothesline! Nate: That was a viscious clothesline!!! Walczak rubs the top of his head, seeing that he is indeed bleeding. He picks TCM up by the hair and sends him into the ropes. TCM bounces off the ropes and comes back at Walczak. Walczak with a hard right hand but TCM ducks and rebounds again. TCM leaps into the air and slugs Walczak in the back of his head, staggering the massive Polack forward. TCM puts Walczak in a full nelson and yells loudly, lifting Walczak into the air and dropping him in a full nelson slam! TCM slowly rises to his feet but favors his back slightly. Nate: Looks like that last move might have done more damage to Trashcan Man than it did good! TCM lifts Walczak up by the head, but the Polish Punisher jams his shoulder into TCM and back body drops him to the mat. Walczak reaches out and tags in Ivan. Stanislav climbs into the ring and roars at TCM who rises slowly. Ivan points to troy and slaps his palm. Nate: Looks like Ivan wants to fight Doug Troy!! Robbie: Give him what he wants! I wanna see Doug get murdered on national television! TCM nods to Doug and tags him in. As Doug gets into the ring the crowd begins to cheer louder and louder!! Crowd: DT!! DT!!! DT!! DT!!!! DT!!! Ivan roars over the crowd and slams his hands on his chest. He stomps his foot on the mat three thunderous times and roars again. Troy smirks at the crowd and brushes his nose with his thumb. Stanislav bends at the waist and yells a Doug. His Russian voice cuts through the crowd’s sound…. Ivan: Come on Doug Troy! Try and hurt Russian Bear!! DYAAAHAA!! Ivan laughs loudly as the crowd gets louder!! Steven: My God! I can barely hear my voice!!! Stanislav stands up straight and laughs. Troy yells and charges at Ivan. Just as Troy gets close to Ivan, The Soviet Machine reaches out and palms Troy’s head and shoves him away. Troy shakes his head and charges again, undaunted…. Nate: I know if I was there it would be a different story, but for Troy, I don’t think a frontal assault is the most intelligent thing to do against Ivan. Ivan absorbs a forearm from Troy and looks down at him, being that Ivan is a foot taller than him. Ivan laughs and points to his chin, daring Troy to hit him. The crowd roars louder and stomps their feet. Troy winds back far, preparing to nail Stanislav. He swings, and just as he does he changes it up, CRACKING Ivan in the right knee with a hard kick! Nate/Steven/Robbie: WHOA! Ivan roars and bends over, clutching his knee. Troy punches Ivan hard in the side of the head, causing the Bear to stand straight again. Ivan blinks for a moment as Troy punches Ivan in the gut….but it doesn’t do anything. He wails again and again at the iron stomach of Stanislav. Ivan grits his teeth and grabs the back of Troy’s head, whipping him backwards to the mat with a WHOOM! Nate: Tremendous attempt by Doug Troy but if you try to club a man of that size, he’s only going to get angry. Troy gathers himself up and composes himself. He stares back at Ivan who roars, points, and laughs. Troy backs into the corner and smirks. Ivan blinks, suddenly confused. Troy looks around at the crowd, laughs, and flicks Ivan the bird! Nate: HA HA HA!! Ivan roars and charges in a blind rage towards Troy with his arms high over his head. Troy ducks to the side and hits a drop toe hold. Ivan slips and falls headfirst into the top turnbuckle! Ivan roars and staggers backwards! Nate: Troy has Ivan reeling!! Ivan staggers. Troy sees his opening and dropkicks Ivan in the chest. Ivan staggers back and wheels his arms around, trying to keep his balance. Troy leaps and tries to execute a hurricanrana, but Ivan catches him an powerbombs him to the mat! Troy bounces off the mat and lays prone on the ground. Ivan stomps around the fallen figure of Doug. He reaches down and grabs Troy by the throat and lifts him in the air with ease. Nate: What about the ref?! Count!! Riggs palms at his eyes…. Steven: Looks like he has something in his eye! Ivan laughs as Troy begins to fade away. Riggs continues to fool with his eyes, seeming to be in….obvious….pain. Ivan laughs, looking at Riggs. He holds Troy with one hand and reaches out with the other and tags in Walczak. Riggs sees the tag! Nate: Wait a minute! He saw that!!! Ivan chokeslams Troy hard to the mat and laughs, climbing out of the ring to the apron. Riggs finally appears to clean out his eyes and begins to call the match again. Nate: Oh, how convenient! Walczak circles Troy who blinks up at the lights. Tom kicks Troy hard in the side, forcing him over on his side. Troy starts to stand but gets a helping hand from The Polish Punisher. Walczak sends Troy into the turnbuckle and follows him. He spears him hard in the corner and drives his shoulder into Troy two more times. Tom picks Troy up again and sends him into the other corner. Troy with a reversal, sending Tom into the corner instead! Nate: This could be a key moment for Doug Troy…. Troy charges and slams his knee into the head of Tom. He pulls Tom out of the corner and snap suplexes him to the mat. He goes for the quick cover…. ……… 1………KICKOUT by Walczak! Steven: Speedy Riggs…punctual as ever! TCM yells at Riggs and puts a foot through the ropes. Ruslan, who’s been biding his time outside, grabs TCM’s other foot to prevent him from entering the ring! TCM turns and yells at Ruslan, who holds onto one leg with both arms. Nate: Things are not looking good….. Ruslan continues to hold on to TCM’s leg while Communist Party Member Wang slides into the ring. Riggs stares directly at Wang and then turns his back nervously. Wang charges straight at Troy, who now stands. Troy catches Wang and hiptossess him across the ring into Riggs! Riggs and Wang fall in a heap of arms and legs in the corner! Robbie: And now watch my friends as the house of cards comes to the ground! Stanislav roars as he sees his two Communists get hurt and barrels into the ring! TCM kicks himself free of Ruslan and climbs into the ring as well. Troy stands beside TCM as they square off against the two foreigners. Nate: Here we go folks! Start your engines!! Ruslan sneaks his way around the ring and hops on the apron behind Troy. He grabs Troy by the back of his jeans! TCM and Walczak charge and brawl with one another. Stanislav rushes forward and winds up for a HUGE clothesline. Troy runs and is held by Ruslan! Stanislav swings, Troy ducks, and Ruslan is CRUSHED in the face and knocked to the guardrail! Steven: MY GOD! IVAN JUST LEVELLED RUSLAN! Robbie: ACCIDENTAL!!! Ivan hangs over the top rope and yells to Ruslan to see if he is alright. Troy grabs the waist of Ivan’s pants and hoists him over the top rope to the floor! Ivan falls and hits skull first into the hard floor! Amazingly, Ivan shakes his head and rises to his feet, rubbing his forehead. Troy blinks dumbly, amazed at the endurance of Stanislav! The Two Round Faced Women bolt down to the ring in order to help their fallen Major Wang….. Robbie: The match is still legal! Riggs never called for the bell!! Walczak lifts TCM in the air into a military press and dumps him to the mat and proceeds to stomp holes into him. The Two Round Faced Women slide into the ring and gather up Riggs and Wang and clean them off. Stanislav helps Ruslan up and climbs back in to the ring, roaring at Doug Troy. Robbie: KILL!! KILL!! KIL!! Troy and Ivan charge and clash in the middle of the ring. Actually, Ivan charges INTO Doug who goes for a clothesline. His arm snaps across Ivan’s chest and Doug twirls in the air and falls to the mat. Ivan stands and grins. Slowly, Communist Party Member Wang, The Two Round Faced Women, Alexei Ruslan, Tom Walczak, and Ivan Stanislav all stand over their fallen opponents, occasionally stomping them when they try to stand. Ivan nods to Wang, Ruslan, and The Women and they bail out of the ring. Ivan picks up Troy, Walczak picks up TCM. The two communists look at one another and then at Riggs and nod. Nate: I don’t like this…I don’t like this at all! Stanislav is a psychopath!! Ivan whips Troy into the Red Scare and tosses him across the ring. Walczak nails TCM with the Polish Powerbomb and both men pin their opponents at the same time! 1… 2…. …3!!! Winners: Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak of The Red Army!! Ivan and Tom celebrate in the ring as the commies pile into the ring with them! The crowd is booing like there is no tomorrow. Nate: THIS IS AN OUTRAGE!!! THERE WAS NO ORDER IN THE RING!!! Robbie: I SAW NOTHING!!! Nate: WE ARE OUT OF TIME!!! WE WILL SEE YOU…. Suddenly the lights in the arena completely go black, interrupting Dave, mid-sentence. Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON?!!? Suddenly, the flickering illumination of the Jumbotron provides a faint light for the arena area. The camera focus shifts to the Jumbotron screen and pins in until it encompasses the entire shot. The tron fades from static to a shot of the backstage area of the arena, this time into a cramped, messy little office that looks to have (until recently) served as a large broom closet. It does have a window, however, or rather a hole knocked through the wall with transparent tarp over it. The only thing that makes it identifiable is a familiar looking steel-barbed wire-nail chair, resting on the rickety old desk that takes up half the space. Nate: THAT’S ED’S CHAIR, WHICH HE CALLS BOB!!! The shot remains focused on the nail studded, barbed-wire chair for a moment, then zoom out as Ed enters the room, a defeated man, failing in his attempt to find an opponent for Ivan Stanislav next week on Slam! The shot shifts ever so briefly to the ring, where the Ivan and the rest of the Red Army are intently watching the Jumbotron. Ed looks affectionately at his chair, and lets out a deep sigh. Ed: (apparently unaware he is being filmed) Well, great. No one will step up and take down that Russian retard. How am I going to explain this to Jane? I'll be the shortest reigning commish of all time... Suddenly, a crappy MIDI version of Wagner's 'Flight of the Valkyries' begins to chirp from somewhere nearby. The camera shifts to a large, gray cell phone velcroed to the wall, with a 'Hi, Im... Fred' badge stuck to it. Ed's hand appears, wrenches it free, and pulls it to his ear. Ed: Heylo? Heavy breathing serves as Ed’s only response. Ed: I love you too, sweetheart, but atleast talk dirty for… Suddenly, a deep, gruff, rage-filled voice interrupts Ed mid-sentence. Voice: I heard yer lookin' fer someone to fight Ivan... There is a brief shot of the ring again, where Ivan is shown glued to the happenings on the Jumbotron. Ed squints his eyes in thought. Ed: Wha-- Who is this? Voice: Check yer caller ID... The Hardcore Legend pulls the phone away from his ear, stares at the dimly glowing screen, and gapes. Nate: WHO THE HELL IS THAT?!?! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOOSE OUR FEED!!! Ed’s eyes widen in terror, and we zoom in upon the phone, its screen turned away from out view, and on the faint green glow cast by it….as the © 2002 Primetime Championship Wrestling logo appears on the lower, center portion of the screen....as the show fades to black.
