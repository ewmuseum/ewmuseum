Corg vs. Aimz, Cruiserweight Title Tournament Final

23 Jun 2002

At Ascension — deciding OSW's own first-ever Cruiserweight Champion.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Corg vs. Aimz, to mark the first ever Cruiserweight Champion Written: June 23, 2002 Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen the following contest is scheduled for one fall… and is for the FIRST EVER OSW CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!!! 'Machines' by Crossbreed hits the sound system, and the crowd begins to boo heavily. The camera briefly cuts to a small group of Goths in the audience who are holding up a sign reading 'KILL CORG KILL', then back to the entryway. Earl: First… Making his way to the ring at this time… From Detroit, Michigan… Weighing in at 172 lbs… CORG!!! The booing remains constant as the freaky-lookin guy called Corg walks out from under the Jumbotron and approaches the ring. Earl: And his opponent… 'Here to Stay' by Korn graces the arena, and while there is some cheering, it is mostly drowned out by boo's. Earl: From New Minas, Nova Scotia, Canada… Weighing in at 143 lbs… She is a member of The Elite… AMY CAMPBELL!!! The 90% heel heat mixed with 10% feminist face heat continues as the hot Canadian chick known as Aimz cockily walks down the ramp. Nate: It was only a few weeks ago when Amy Campbell, along with Civil Disobedience and Sebastian Toomes formed the revolutionist faction known as The Elite! One has to wonder how long it'll be before those scumbags involve themselves in this match. Robbie: Oh Nate you're so negative. Aimz doesn't need help to take out that little freakshow in the ring. Aimz is about to enter the ring when Corg suddenly slingshots himself over the top rope and flips in mid-air, knocking her to the ground with a senton! Steven: DAY-UM! Robbie: FREAK!!! The match hasn't even started yet! The crowd cheers for the bold move by Corg, who has completely laid-out Aimz and is now getting to his feet. Nate: The match has just started and we've already got some high flying action going on! Grinning sadistically, Corg bends down to grab Campbell by the hair, and pulls the pretty young heel-ette to her feet. Aimz cringes and claws at Corg's hand in an attempt to free herself, but to no avail, and is basically dragged to the ring by her hair. Corg rolls her under the bottom rope then hops up to the ring apron and climbs to the top of the nearest turnbuckle rather than simply enter the ring. There he waits…and waits… until finally, Aimz gets to her feet. Robbie: WATCH OUT AMY!!! Aimz looks up and sees a 170lb goth flying right at her. Corg hooks his legs around Campbell's neck and hits a PERFECT hurricanrana! Nate: Nice rana by Corg! Amy is sent sprawling, rolls forward in a soumersault, and actually manages to spring to her feet, staggering into a turnbuckle. There she leans for a moment before turning around and comes face to face with Corg. The freakish OSW superstar has Aimz trapped in the corner, and is looking down at her with a look of sadistic pleasure. Steven: Hmmm… Someone's got a cruuuush! Corg clasps his right hand around her throat and pushes his horrid face against hers, and suddenly stops…when Aimz kicks him square between the legs. Crowd: OOoooohhhhhhh!! Corg doubles over and releases his grip on Aimz, who then puts him in a side headlock and climbs to the middle turnbuckle, following with a TORNADO DDT! Robbie: YEAH! Now that's what I'm talkin' bout! Amy gets up with Corg lying there flat on his face, runs to the ropes, the springs off the middle and executes an asai moonsault! The crowd boo's as Campbell begins to pull Corg to his feed. The dazed goth throws a punch as the pulls at him, but Aimz easily moves aside and knees him right in the face. As Corg gets up, Aimz runs toward the ropes, bounces off, and rolls under a clothesline from him. She pops up behind Corg, low-blows him again, and follows up with a bulldog, sending him face-first to the mat! Nate: Campbell is fighting like a cheating little bitch! Robbie: Watch your tounge! Corg is just getting whoomped by a girl. Aimz turns Corg over and drags him over to the nearest turnbuckle, where she climbs to the top and motions to the crowd, receiving a fair amount of hatred in return. In a picture-perfect display of cruiserweight prowess, Amy leaps off and does a PERFECT 360 IN MID-AIR… Steven: Holy Shit! …and slams hard against the mat after Corg rolls out of the way. Robbie and Nate: HOLY SHIT! Steven: He was playing possum! Corg quickly rolls Aimz over and makes the cover! Betty Lovejoy hits the mat and starts counting. 1… 2… NO! Betty points out that Amy's foot is in the ropes, prompting a curse from Corg. The gothic warrior picks up Aimz, who is clearly out-of-it, and sits her down on the top of the turnbuckle. With that, Corg climbs up to the middle rope, and hooks Aimz in a headlock. Nate: Corg's going for a superplex! Robbie: DAMN HIM! Corg drapes Aimz' arm over his neck, then grabs the waist of her pants and attempts to pick her up for a superplex…but she fights it. He attempts again, but Aimz locks her feet in the ropes to insure she ain't going anywhere. Corg makes a third attempt, AND GETS HER UP! Nate: Here it comes! Aimz kicks and fights while being held upside-down, struggling with all her might. In the end, she succeeds. Corg loses control of her and drops her back down to the turnbuckle…AND AIMZ REVERSES IT! Robbie: HOLY SHIT!! LOOKIT THAT! AIMZ IS HOLDING CORG UP FOR A VERTICAL SUPLEX WHILE STANDING ON THE MIDDLE ROPE…AND SUPERPLEXES HIM RIGHT OVER THE TOP ROPE, OUT OF THE RING, SENDING HIM DOWN TO THE FLOOR BELOW!!!!! Nate: OH MY GOD!! Crowd: Holy Shit! Holy Shit! Holy Shit! The crowd cannot help but cheer with sheer energy as Cord crash-lands in a heap on the floor before the mat. The cheering becomes even louder when Aimz climbs to the top turnbuckle and faces Corg. Nate: Oh Man! What is she gonna do?! Robbie: She is woman, watch her kick ass! With Corg laying below, AIMZ LEAPS OFF THE TOP TURNBUCKLE, HITTING A SHOOTING STAR PRESS ALL THE WAY DOWN TO THE FLOOR, MAKING PERFECT CONTACT ON CORG!!! Crowd: OOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!! Nate: AIMZ JUST EXECUTED A SHOOTING STAR PRESS FROM THE RING TO THE FLOOR…AND IS ACTUALLY GETTING UP!!! Surely enough, though very dazed after what she just pulled off, Aimz is pulling an unresponsive Corg toward the ring. Corg, however, is such dead weight that Amy can't get him into the ring herself. Robbie: What the… Hey Lovejoy! Help her get Corg in the ring so she can finish his ass off! Aimz continues to struggle with the out-cold Corg, but with no success. It is then that the crowd suddenly erupts into boo's, when none other than Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes and CEO Jane Reagan trot down to the ring. Nate: What the hell are THEY doing here?! Amy's stablemates in the Elite are coming down here! Amidst the hateful response of the crowd, 'Seabass' walks over to Aimz, who looks relieved to see him. Meanwhile, referee Jane Reagan is up on the ring apron, distracting the..well…easily distractable Betty Lovejoy by feigning interest in a conversation with her. With Lovejoy distracted, Sebastian hoists Corg up onto his shoulders and dumps him in the ring, giving a cordial nod to Aimz afterwards. Campbell immediately gets in the ring and makes a cover on Corg, and Jane Reagan points it out to Betty. Lovejoy makes the count! 1… 2… NO!!!! CORG KICKS OUT! Robbie: WHAT?! Nate: CORG JUST KICKED OUT AFTER BEING SUPERPLEXED OUT OF THE RING AND HIT WITH A SHOOTING STAR PRESS!!! Steven: I LOVE a man with endurance! Toomes and Reagan look on with disbelief, as does Amy. Corg appears to be getting up under his own free will. Aimz, who is still shocked, lays in punch after punch to Corg, but the gothic warrior's second wind allows him to swat aside these blows and stand upright, once again glaring at Aimz with a look of pure hatred. Robbie: Oh no… Someone do something! As if following Decker's command, Jane once again hops up on the ring apron and distracts Lovejoy, and Seabass has grabbed a steel chair from ringside and enters the ring, sneaking up behind Corg. Sebastian is so busy concentrating on Corg that he does not notice the sudden explosion of cheers from the fans, nor does he notice… Nate: IT'S RHETT LOCKE!!! …A heavily-bandaged Rhett Locke slide under the bottom rope into the ring with a chair of his own. Robbie: SEABASS WATCH OUT!!! Rhett Locke springs to his feet and dives right at Toomes, nailing him right over the head with his chair! Switchblade is knocked to the mat and rolls out of the ring in surprise, scowling up at Rhett from the floor. Locke then sees Reagan on the ring apron and goes to take a swing at her, but the crafty CEO quickly hops down from the apron, where the two regroup for a moment before taking flight after Rhett exits the ring and chases after them with his chair! Nate: CORG AND AIMZ ARE ALONE AGAIN!!! RHETT LOCKE HAS JUST CHASED TOOMES AND THAT WHORE REAGAN FROM RINGSIDE!!! Once Reagan and Sebastian have disappeared up the ramp, Rhett Locke returns to ringside, remaining on the outside as if standing guard. Suddenly, we cut to the back, where a VERY angry-looking Graphic Violence is glaring at a monitor, his face twisted into a look of rage. Graphic Violence: WHAT…THE FUCK…ARE YOU DOING DOWN THERE!! YOU AREN'T SUPPOSED TO EVEN BE FUCKING *MOVING* AFTER WHAT I JUST DID TO YOU… In a fit of malice, GV grabs the monitor and HURLS it against the wall, smashing it to bits. Graphic Violence storms down the hall, emerging into what looks like to be the catering area, where Chico and Gunmetal, better known as Twisted Metal, are conversing over a tray of buffalo wings. Gunmetal: Go on… Chico: So yeah, The Vax said tha- GV: GET THE FUCK OUTTA MY WAY!! The two bots turn towards GV as he stomps toward them and flee the scene. Moments later, an entire table full of food is overturned and destroyed as Graphic Violence continues to vent his rage. We cut back to the ring. Nate: What was THAT all about?! Robbie: GV KILLED Rhett Locke last match, he shouldn't be out here right now! Nate: Looks like GV didn't get the job done! Steven: Hey guys, if you were paying attention to the match and not what was going on backstage, you might be interested in what you're missing… In the ring, Corg has just knocked Aimz to her knees with a sweeping kick to shins, and follows up by hitting her with his finisher, a spinning superkick to the back of the head… DEATH BLOOMS!!! Nate: CORG'S GOT AIMZ!!! Robbie: NNNOOOOOOO!!!! Amy hits the mat like a rock, and Corg makes the cover!!!! 1… 2… 3!!!! Nate: YYYEEEESSSSSSSS!!!!! WINNER, AND NEW OSW CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION….CORG!!!
