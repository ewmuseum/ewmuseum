Twisted Metal Entrance Music: 'Jesus built my Hotrod.' by Ministry. Status: Heel Manager(s): Humanoid Robot Model XA512 - 'Cobol' Tag Finisher: Self Destruct (More of a last resort than a finisher, this is just what it sounds like. The bot in question blowis itself to hell and wreaks havoc upon anything nearby. (The personality 'Black Box' is recovered later.)) Entrance Video Information Humanoid Robot Model A512a - 'Gunmetal' Hometown: Atlantic City, NJ, USA Height: 5'0' Weight: 400 lbs. Humanoid Robot Model A512b - 'Chico' Hometown: Atlantic City, NJ, USA Height: 5'0' Weight: 400 lbs. Signature Moves: 1.) Pneumatic Ram: Air driven pistons in the forearms deliver rapid fire punches!5.) Loose Parts: Being as damaged prone as he is, things [arms, legs, head, whatever] tend to... fall off in battle. They make great weapons!Overload: A Gunmetal specialty. With his Windows operating system being more buggy than most roach motels, Gunmetal experiences periodic electrical surges that ground in anything he is touching. While powerful, they also cause his systems to crash, necessitating a full re-boot sequence which lasts for over 10 minutes.Lowrider: A Chico specialty. He had some special hydraulics installed. Y'ever see them cars bounce up and down? Imagine that on your chest Set Up Move: Desperate Straits (Used in cases of severe damage and desperate straits, though the way the writers use it... pretty much at any time. ) OSW Title History: Nada OSW Award History: Zilch Other Federations: PCW Other Federation Awards: Nope Handler Information Handler Name: Michael K. AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: N/A Bio Gunmetal, along with his 'brother' Chico, are the second generation footsoldiers of the C.L.O.D. computer system. The elder of the Sec-Gen troops, Gunmetal recieved the single available copy of Windows A.C. for his operating system. Sadly, this has resulted in an obsession with 'better' upgrades and an almost manic fetish for hardware. (*Nudge-nudge, wink-wink*) The younger of the Sec-Gen troops, Chico recieved a freebie Linux system for his operating system. But, during the hurried downloading phase of his Evil programming, (The library was closing.) a copy of ICQ was accidentally added instead of vital .evl files. The constant download of computer viruses and a perpetual exposure to banal chatrooms has altered his formerly pure mission from 'Kill Humans' to 'Humans?' Physically, both Chico and Gunmetal basically identical to each other and their parent Cobol (in his pre-upgraded state). However, Gunmetal tends to have a profusion of (largely unessesary) attachments mounted all over, while Chico has the odd habit of dressing in human clothing. History: It's long so I'll condense. 1.) THE Multiple Man's supercomputer C.L.O.D. , in the fashion of all great A.I.'s, hates humankind and plots it's destruction. 2.) Through a lucky turn of events, Valerie Dietrich salvaged Paul Arovnik's automaton 'Sunshine' from a CCW dumpster. 3.) Val begins creating her own machine humanoid. 4.) During the final software download, CLOD inserts evil files (extension .evl) into the mix. The robot (Cobol) escapes. 5.) Cobol commits a long string of electronics thefts and builds a mechanical army. 6.) The army scatters throughout the United States, loging itself undetectably in the infrastructure. Now: The time is right for revolution! Mecha army membership has swelled into the millions, and the signal is given! But there is a problem. As is, CLOD does not have the computational capacity to fully command even a small portion of his troops. (Wooden semiconductors'll do that to ya.) Knowing this, CLOD directed it's lead follower Cobol to obtain the legendary Pentium X chip. But it is expensive. Seeing this, Cobol worked tirelessly worked as an accountant until it had resources enough to create two near-perfect duplicates of itself. The remaining funds went towards massive physical and computational upgrades. They entered the wrestling racket for the BIG money, and the Tag Division because it seemed the shortest route to that end. Hope that they never fulfill their goal. Pray for it.
