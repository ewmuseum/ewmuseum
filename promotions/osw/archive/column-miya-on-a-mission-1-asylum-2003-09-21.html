Miya On A Mission 1: Asylum

21 September 2003

by Miyagawa

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own column (#10) by Miyagawa, dated 21 September 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Greetings Peeps, It has come to my attention that certain fan members that we have around here, weren’t around when the bulk of the roster were fighting together in a completely different federation, Primetime Championship Wrestling. Now as the roster member of PCW that had the most matches (somewhere in the range of 130 matches in total) and a new member of Old School Wrestling, I feel somewhat that it is my duty to teach and educate those of you about the historical facts surrounding certain members of the roster. Such members of the roster as the OSW World Champion Chris “Boda” Sloboda, Clyde, Six Finger Cisco, Darkstar, James Edwards, Asa Fountain, Gaz Green, Xavier Kannon, Mike Marvellous, Nerezza, William S. Powell, Ivan Stanislav, Tessa Windsor, and of course my dear old self. (Authors note: If my memory is unclear, I will likely guess. I’m not Byron Calver, so forgive me for any mistakes) To start with however, I will choose a subject that is dear to my heart. Instead of doing a single person, I will instead tell you the history of one of OSW’s foremost stables, The Asylum. Think back youngsters, think back. We’re going all the way back to October 2000. PCW has been open for best part of a month and people are thinking towards the radical PPV coming up known simply as “Alliances”. A singles match takes place, pitting a man simply known as “The Joker” against OSW Favourite, The Trashcan Man. The two fight, and TCM is ultimately victorious. However, the two become friends and start tagging as a tag team known as “Asylum” against the then Tag Team Champions of Ryan Knox and Derrick (my memory is a little foggy about his first name to be honest) Diamond. Somehow Diamond would never show up, leaving Knox to take on Asylum. Always though, Stewart Hix and Jake Keeton would get involved, causing Knox to retain the tag titles. Anyway, Alliances approached and TCM and Joker wanted an Asylum foursome to take part. They inducted a new PCW signee by the name of Miyagawa. The fourth member was kept under wraps until the actual night, and was revealed to be none other than the monster known as Meanstreak. The other three were eliminated early, I remember being eliminated by Kris Kanton. Anyway after being run over by the PCW Yellow Hummer driven by Johnny Raindance, Meanstreak was pinned and the stable known as sHs (Consisting of Knox, Revenant, Kanton and The Preacher). The Joker left shortly after Alliances, only to be replaced by Bang Davenport. This was just before Christmas when [name withheld] was famously offered a spot in Asylum only to turn heel and return as “The Mack” [name withheld]. A memorable three on three match took place pitting TCM, Miyagawa and Bang against [name withheld], The Chef and one of [name withheld]’s friends that I can’t remember 100%. I’ll probably remember it tomorrow and kick myself. Asylum drifted along not really doing much. Miya won the Cruiserweight Title, while TCM had a couple of Extreme Title reigns. Then Media happened. Media itself will be covered better in another issue, but suffice to say that it turned PCW on it’s head at Shanghaied 1. A bigger turn for a fed is yet to be done. Miya and TCM both lost their titles shortly afterwards and TCM vanished. Slowly but surely Asylum drifted apart. Bang went off on his own, and Miya joined up with the Blood Brothers, Jon Kano and some others in a stable called “The Outcasts”. It was around this time that Mr Smiley Face made his first appearance in a PCW ring. M-S-F and The Outcasts stayed around for a few months. Eventually M-S-F was revealed as TCM, and Miya and Bang went back to join him, along with a new member in the form of Jeremy “JJ” Johnson and one of Miyagawa’s protégé’s in the shape of Phoenix. Shortly after however, PCW closed it’s doors for a month off, re-evaluating the roster while it did so. Bang Davenport would be cut from the roster, never to return or be heard of again (until he was hired as a PWC Commentator, anyway). Miyagawa formed a Japanese based stable for a few weeks called the Elements. A few month’s prior it was “cool” to be a Russian when the Red Army arrived, now because of the Elements, it was “cool” to be a Jap. TCM won the world title around this time. Miyagawa went on to join a stable called “Old School” and they had a PPV named after them… “Back To Old School”. It was Miyagawa’s work backstage that brought that stable together. A month past and it was decided that it was time for the return of Asylum, prior to Alliances. So together, Jeremy Howard, Alexander Miyagawa, Timo Bolomba and Dan Hollywood. After Asylum was formed a year prior to win the event, this year they actually won it, with Jeremy Howard winning it overall. The stable itself stayed together all the way through to March Madness the following year. It went up against the former PCW2 stables “Wild Deuces” and Jason Snow’s motley crew. Timo never had really joined, and in fact tried to take Howard’s title shot off him, but failed. JJ left, to be replaced by Dan Hollywood, who with Jeremy Howard became the only tag team with the name “Asylum” to win the Tag Team Titles, if only for a week. Xavier Kannon and Darkstar joined, and Bobby Stormbringer was about to be brought in. At March Madness, Boda won the PCW World Title. Three days after, he lost it. And in the process, almost every stable in PCW at the time imploded against each other. Asylum reappeared later that year when Miyagawa returned from injury, along with the P.W.A. and the blue haired Anarchy. Anarchy was in store from the new Asylum, but the group destroyed itself when Nerezza of the P.W.A. felt that Miyagawa wasn’t holding up his end of the bargain and crushed his skull against a wall, leading Miyagawa to wear that infamous metal skull cap. And that was the last time that Asylum was in PCW. In overall, the following members were Asylum members (if only for one night): The Trashcan Man The Joker Alexander Miyagawa Meanstreak Bang Davenport Phoenix Jeremy “JJ” Johnson Jeremy “Krayzie” Howard Dan Hollywood Timo Bolambo Xavier Kannon Darkstar Bobby Stormbringer. And so ends this little trip through memory lane. If you noticed any errors in this, then keep them to yourselves, this is more a case of providing a basis for knowledge, then the knowledge itself. For accurate knowledge, seek out the lore master that is Byron Calver, PCW’s #1 Fan. This October, Asylum is three years old. Happy Birthday Asylum. Yours sincerely, Alexander Miyagawa
