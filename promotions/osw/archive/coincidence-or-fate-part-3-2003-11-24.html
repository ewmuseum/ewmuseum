Coincidence Or Fate? Part 3

24 November 2003

by Angelus Jones

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#226) by Angelus Jones, dated 24 November 2003, written from El Paso, Texas, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Everything was a blur. He was too tired from driving all night in the blood soaked car. He was exhausted as he lifted rock after rock after rock into the vehicle weighting it down. Though he was relieved as he let the handbrake off and watched the car roll quietly off of the edge of the embankment and slip quietly into the murky depths below. He could tell he wasn’t at the seashore from the lack of salt in the air or the sound of the waves, but he knew from the looks of the place wherever he was nobody would come looking for any bodies here any time soon. Angelus watched through tired eyes as bubbles floated to the surface along with some weeds and scuzz, and after a few minutes walked away knowing that at the bottom of the watery grave lay the bodies of four men that by fate or coincidence had been handpicked and returned to Angelus to finally pay for their ways. Leaving behind the last of his troubles, Angelus kept walking along the side of the dirt road until finally he collapsed into the little dry ditch by the roadside. Falling into a deep sleep Angelus could finally rest in peace, and dreamed of the moment his troubles came to an end. The car was quiet inside as they sped away from El Paso to home for the night for Angelus. Talk of the rodeo was over quickly and from then conversation between them was sparse, save the odd joke or two. The four Mexicans had foolishly believed Angelus had no idea of who they were and went on as though they were all buddies. It wasn’t until everything finally went quiet and civilization sank into the horizon that Angelus took his revenge. To his left, the eldest of the four, Julio had fallen asleep. Quickly pulling a switchblade from his pocket, Angelus leaned over slightly and pushed the blade into the back of Julio. Pulling it out and hiding it back in his pocket, Angelus waited for a few moments hoping he had struck at the right point and sighed with relief as Julio continued to sleep away, unaware he was slowly bleeding to death. In the front seats were Carlos and Jose, who didn’t notice until the last moment that their other brother Cesar was slumped back in his seat with blood flowing from the knife wound in his heart turning his clothes a dark crimson. Before Carlos could turn around, the blade had dug into the back of his neck, severing the spinal cord before being twisted and pulled out. Jose slammed on the brakes and before the car had even stopped had opened the door and rolled into the ditch. Getting to his feet he ran as fast as he could away from Angelus. Thinking he had made a successful escape, Jose turned back only to scream out in horror as the 7’0” of Angelus loomed over him, and with a violent kick took Jose’s legs out from under him sending him sprawling face-first into the road. Jose began whimpering as Angelus, without a word, dragged him by the legs back to the car. Opening the trunk, Angelus held Jose by the throat and without a moments pause slammed it down and revelled in the sickening crunch that followed. Jose’s body slumped to the ground only to be quickly slung into the trunk by Angelus. Pulling Cesar’s body from the back seat, Angelus pushed it into the trunk next to Jose’s body pushing it in violently until both finally fitted. Slamming it shut, Angelus pulled Carlos from the passenger seat, throwing him into the back and closing all doors before taking his place in the driver’s seat. Angelus sighed and quickly took off as the silhouette of another vehicle appeared on the horizon. As quickly as it had happened it was over, but Angelus was smiling. Angelus wanted to believe with everything in his soul that what had happened to Jack Ainsworth wasn’t of his doing, but he couldn’t take back what he had done. A moment of insanity had taken one boy’s life, and now another had finally rid Angelus of what had destroyed his life. While nothing could be taken back or done about what had happened, Angelus knew now he could live his life peacefully now that everything in his past could be buried forever. Tomorrow a new life would begin, a new life as Angelus Jones…the Wanted Man.
