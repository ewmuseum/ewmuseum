Getting Reno on Board

2002-06-01
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Getting Reno on Board Written: June 1, 2002 The scene fades back up backstage once again as Ivan Stanislav, Alexei Ruslan, and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang are seen walking down the hallway, already in a heated conversation. Ivan- Little runt is going to do what I say..and not get in the way..or else!! Wang: Pwapowschik, do not wowwy!! He will do what you say, and he will wike it! Weno Davis is too stupid to do anything against you to your face! Alexei: And maybe..just maybe…we’ll be eating monkey brains…Hee hee haa!!! Wang walks over to a door and brings his finger up to his lips. Wang: SShhhh…we awe hear!! Ivan brushes Wang away and looks at the door and reads the nameplate, which reads “Reno Davis.” He grits his teeth and takes a step back. Ivan brings his massive leg back and kicks squarely into the doorknob, knocking the entire door from the hinges and sending it flying into a corner of the room. Reno Davis is seen changing for his upcoming matchup, and is dressed in his boxers and his right knee brace. He spins around and stares at the three Red Army members. Reno: Hey guys, I'm flattered but can you come back? I'm not feeling too sexy right now. Stanislav barges into the room, flanked by Wang and Alexei. Alexei takes notice of ZaC, who sits in a sink and plays with a banana. Ivan squints at the knee brace for a moment, and then looks at Reno. Ivan: Reno Davis, just the dog I am looking for!! Reno smirks and replies. Reno: Well Ivan, I'm sure everyone's looking for my dog, but whether you get it or not is entirely up to me. Reno winks at Ivan. Ivan blinks for a moment, not understanding a word that he just said. He growls, knowing that somewhere in his phrase was an insult. He takes a step closer to Reno, not at all happy. Ivan: Quit your joking, whelp. You and I, whether we wish it or not, must fight together...tonight! Reno: Yup. Reno nods, suddenly feeling enlightened on his purpose in life thanks to Ivan. Ivan grits his teeth and balls his hand into a fist. Ivan: THAT being said...I want to make it clear that you do what I say in that ring and NOT get in the way Reno: Hmm? Ivan grins as Wang eyes ZaC now and then whispers to Alexei. Ivan: You see, Reno Davis, you are small and feeble. In a match such as this, you will do nothing more than get hurt and then you would cost us match...and I do not wish to forfeit the chance to personally end your life on national television. Therefore, you let Ivan stay in the ring, and you make sure that no one gets a pin while I am busy destroying our opponents, and there will not be problem, understand, boy? Reno: Where do you get those nifty suspenders? Reno flicks one of Ivan's suspenders and then makes eye contact with the massive man. Ivan shoves Reno back with one hand. Ivan: Don't you dare touch me, capitalist! Davis flips backwards onto his hands, springs back up onto his feet and then pulls out his toy lightsaber. Reno: Bring it on. As Reno pulls out the lightsaber, Ruslan quickly pulls out a revolver on instinct. Ivan brings a hand back to Alexei. Ivan: Nyet, do not kill him just yet Alexei, he is of use to us...and besides, I will take great pride in smashing his weak..tiny body. Ivan looks over at ZaC for a moment. Ivan: Well, Reno, I suggest that we do discuss our tactics....if you do not mind going to my own locker room..I have information on all of our enemies..... The Unwanted Angel waves his right hand in front of him and uses an old Jedi Mind Trick. Reno: You will invite me to your locker room to discuss tactics. Ivan growls and rolls his eyes. Ivan: You only prove your immaturity the longer you breath. Get dressed, and let us go! Reno again waves his hand and spoke. Reno: Go away, I'll see you at your room. Reno again waves his hand. Reno: Go away, I'll see you at your room. Ivan points a finger at Davis. Ivan: Watch your tongue boy.....remember..I am only doing this because I wish to kill you for what you did to me and...and..... Ivan growls loudly.... Ivan: Just remember..... He turns and heads out of the room. Alexei slides his gun into his pocket and smiles arrogantly at Davis as he and Wang turn and follow The Russian Bear out of the room. Reno: Jedi mind tricks? Wow, I'm so not witty tonight... Davis then realized he was only dressed in his boxers. Reno: Where'd this lightsaber come from? Reno scratches his red mop of hair as the scene shifts to the ringside position of the OSW announce team.
