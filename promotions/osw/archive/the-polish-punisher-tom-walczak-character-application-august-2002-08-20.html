'The Polish Punisher' Tom Walczak — character application (August 2002)

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'The Polish Punisher' Tom Walczak Real Name: Tom Walczak Nick Name: The Polish Punisher Catchphase: "Jescze Polska Nie Zginela..." Character Handler: Tom Walczak MSN Account: None AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Kashmir" Led Zeppelin Age:30 From: Gdansk, Poland Height: 6'10" Weight: 375lbs Wrestling Style: Powerhouse Signature Moves: 1. Powerbomb 2. Polish Hammer 3. Clothesline 4. Big Boot 5. Doublehanded Chokelift Powerbomb 6. Chokeslam 7. Presslam 8. Spear Finisher Move: Polish Powerbomb: Poerbomb done in many variations but preferably from the top rope into a sitout position. Set-Up Move: Polish Plant: Throws opponent into rope and then delivers a pancake type move, but as his opponent comes towards the mat he catches him and delivers a spear. Current Team: None Past Teams & Groups: The Red Army, Euro Union, Thugs 4 Hire Title History: Rising Star Champion Extreme Champion Awards History: Description: Walczak has a very defined chest area. He has massive arms and legs. His head is shaved completely bald, and he has a brown goatee with some gray specks in it. He has dark-brown, almost black eyes. He wears red and white colored boxing shorts in the ring. He also has black cutoff grappling gloves and red boots. He has two tatoos both say Polska one in Red one in white they are on on his arms. He also has a huge scar that goes diagonally across his chest. History: Walczak never met his bio-parents seeing as how he was left on the steps of an orphanage before he reached a year. While at the orphanage Walczak was always in constant trouble, and he was never really accepted by the sisters or many of the children. He did have his group of friends, but they, like him were troublemakers. He stayed at the orphanage until he turned 14. The only ones that were saddened by the departure of Walczak were his friends, and they too soon left to pursue a life of organized crime. The nuns were so elated by the departure of Walczak that they paid for his trip entirely, and Walczak’s friends have told him on various occasions that a huge party was thrown after he departed. His destination in the states was New York. His whole life he had heard wonderful stories of the city, but when he arrived he painfully realized that this was not the case. Walczak had no friends or relatives that he knew of that lived in New York, and he had to stay in the doorways of abandoned buildings or churches in order to get some sleep. He wrote to the orphanage many times saying he wished to come back, but each time they wrote back saying that it was now impossible for him to return. Walczak needed a way to make some money he needed a way to make money, and his chance came when he stumbled into an underground fight club. He intended just to sleep there for the night, but what he got himself into was much more. Walczak knew none of the English language, and he was tricked into getting into the ring. Walczak was no small challenge however. At 14, he stood six feet five inches, and weighed around two hundred and seventy five pounds. He was looking at the man across from him when all of a sudden the man threw a haymaker punch. Walczak ducked out of the way and quickly countered with a kick to the kidney. The man fell to the ground in pain, and Walczak was pronounced the winner. The room erupted, and a man came into the ring and handed Walczak a hefty sum of five hundred dollars. This made Walczak’s face light up. Walczak continued fighting, and had a vicious streak of thirty straight knockouts the longest in the clubs history. The owner of the club saw this and ordered a match between the undefeated champion, and Walczak. The match lasted all of fifteen seconds, as Walczak won with a quick counter punch breaking the man’s nose and knocking him out. The man that he won the championship crown from was an impatient man and a sore loser. When he regained consciousness he came after Walczak. Him and a bunch of his thugs cornered Walczak in an ally and after a valiant fight they slit his chest open and that is why he has a scar there still visible to this day. Walczak did not know however that this was to become the luckiest day of his life. On the way back from a Broadway show, a man heard Walczak moaning. He went to check on him, and saw him in a pool of his own blood. He told his wife to call the ambulance and Walczak was rushed to the hospital. Then came the defining moment in Walczak's life. As he came out of the emergency room, the doctor revealed to the man that Walczak lived in the streets. Upon hearing this the man stated that he would adopt him, and the reason being that the man was a Polish immigrant. Walczak moved in with the man, known to him as Jozef Walczak, you see Walczak had never known his real last name so he decided to take on the mans last name. These people were the nicest people Walczak has yet to encounter. They are the two people in this world that he loves and trusts. Other than them he trusts nobody. He moved in with them to their Copiague home, and has lived there on and off ever since. At age 25, due in part to being wanted by many underground fighters, he left for Poland. He trained there for about five years, and then just before he turned thirty, he received a letter from PCW. They happened to be at one of his fights and thought that he had potential. They said he would start out in the PWA, and could then work his way up from there. About a month after joining PWA he was one of the first to graduate. Walczak made an immediate impact, by winning the Rising Star Championship. He has not lived up to his potential yet and is waiting to showcase his talent in the new OSW.
