Introducing the new hero of the OSW

2002-03-30
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introducing the new hero of the OSW Written: March 30, 2002 The crowd roars its approval as Jane stamps her foot and screeches into the mic. Reagan: Oh yeah?!? You and what army?! At that moment a wash of red light flares over the arena and the Soviet National Anthem blares over the loudspeaker as Stanislav, Ruslan, Wang, The Two Round Faced Women, Riggs, Walczak, and Krieg all head out onto the entryway. The crowd jumps to its feet as Tempest grins with great amusement. Nate: That army! Looks like Ivan Stanislav has something to talk about!! Robbie: All hail Ivan Stanislav!! He is the MAN!! Earl: And her partner…. Stanislav stands beside Tempest, produces a microphone, and roars into it, drowning out all other noise…. Ivan: Just one moment, WENCH!! Earl throws the microphone over his shoulder and gives up. Nate: WHOA!! Robbie: Stanislav isn’t screwing around!! Steven: YEAH! YELL AT HER IVAN!!! Reagan blinks and points at herself as if in complete surprise. Ivan roars again. Ivan: That is right, I am talking to you you piece of capitalistic trash! I am tired of hearing you spout off what you believe is ‘final word’ in OSW! Because we of Red Army no longer will listen to your words that come from your trash mouth!! The members of The Red Army stand behind Ivan and nod in unison with their leader. Ivan grins to himself. Reagan begins to get angry and yells at Ivan. Switchblade nods with her. Reagan: You big ogre! I can snap my fingers and make you disappear!! Nate: I can’t believe this! Listen!! There is a quiet, very quiet chant going through the crowd…. Crowd: ivan…ivan…ivan….. Robbie claps in happiness. Ivan cocks his head to the side, extremely surprised at hearing a positive reaction! Tempest looks up at Ivan and smiles. Ivan grins and grinds his teeth together. Ivan: If I wish to hear words from you, I will open up toilet bowl in Arkhangelsk mess hall and tape record it!! DYAAHAA!! Ivan takes a step closer to the ring, the Red Army stands poised to attack behind him and Tempest watches bemused. Ivan: I have come from PCW under your request in order to combat the federation that was led by Tyler Nelson! Tyler Nelson, a capitalist pig…. Ivan pauses just as Reagan begins to speak…. Reagan: Hey Ivan…. Ivan: But at least he wasn’t capitalist WHORE!!! Toomes tries to get out of the ring, but Reagan stops him. Ivan roars and points at the two in the ring…. Ivan: At least Tyler Nelson got the job done in office! Not on office desk!! Ruslan laughs and claps beside Ivan. Nate: Some strong words by Ivan Stanislav!! Ivan: Since I have been in PCW, you have always been telling people not to trust Ivan Stanislav! That Ivan Stanislav is backstabber! That Ivan Stanislav is man without cause, a man that is only out to fulfill his own wishes…. Reagan falls silent, not liking how Ivan is spinning this… Ivan: And now, YOU have come into OSW, taken the trust of THESE fans, and spit in their faces!! The crowd, amazingly, pops for Ivan! Ivan: I have always been man of people, and it is people like you that have tried to stop what I have fought to preserve! And now, the true colors of Jane Reagan, the ‘bulwark of good’, is shown as nothing more than tramp!! Reagan looses her cool and screeches at Ivan. Reagan: Stanislav, you can kiss my ass!! Ivan: It appears that that position is already taken, Reagan!! Toomes yells and climbs out to the apron. Instantly, the other eight members of The Red Army prepare to rush, stopping Toomes from taking another step forward. Tempest locks eyes with Reagan a moment as Jane appears to be suffering a bout of disbelief as to what's happening. Ivan: It is this in which I, Ivan Stanislav, have tried to destroy! Ivan turns to the crowd and proclaims to them, his voice booming and echoing around the arena. Ivan: People of OSW, I hope you now see who is true hero in OSW, and who is true adversary!! And do not say that Ed is this very thing, because he has unleashed something against the OSW that he himself cannot control: Meanstreak!! A chorus of boo’s erupts as Meanstreak’s name is mentioned and Tempest nods, rubbing her head slightly. Ivan grins to himself as he realizes he has the people’s total attention. Reagan tries to interject again, but Ivan’s voice booms over hers easily… Reagan: Listen you…. Ivan: There is only one thing to do! There is only one group within OSW that has power to bring OSW back to where it should be: The Red Army! The President is a slave to flesh, the Commissioner is nothing more than pawn of President, and us, The Red Army, are true fighters for proletariat! For working class!! It is us that will deliver the injustices of OSW to mat of OSW ring, and we will create a true, perfect, classless federation!! Nate: I can’t believe this!! Crowd: IVAN!!! IVAN!!! IVAN!!! Ivan grins widely as the crowd changes gears and rallies behind him! Reagan yells over the crowd at Ivan… Reagan: This is coming from the man that has pulled more games on the crowd than anyone else in the business? This is coming from the oppressive tyrant of the PCW? You are full of shit, Stanislav, and no one believes you!! Suddenly Tempest, whose mic has never actually been turned off, pipes up. Tempest: I believe him. All eyes turn to the diminutive form, nearly lost in the hulking shadows of the Red Army. Tempest: You've talked a good game, but it was all lies Jane. You were supposed to be my friend. But guess who turned out to actually be on my side? Ivan... So just maybe I'll be appearing in red, considering the alternative. She nods abruptly, to a huge pop from the fans. Even Stanislav, who stands behind her, drops his jaw, seemingly inspired by this sudden change of heart. Reagan, however, takes this moment to fire back at Ivan. Reagan: Hey Stanislav, you can have her, she’s not good for anything anyway!! And besides, just because some stupid tart like Tempest likes you, doesn’t mean you have the trust of ANYONE else! Ivan growls and walks past Tempest, pointing directly at Reagan. Ivan: Trust? This is coming from woman that is having affair? Hmm? What would Phantom Booker say if he were here, slut? What would he say? American women have no honor! They are bound to money and pleasure, and nothing more! Well Jane Reagan, your little game is over, because an Iron Curtain has blocked this vision of deceit from which you thought you had, and now, we are going to destroy you! Ivan Stanislav has done nothing against the fans unless it was to further create what you are against: Equality and peace!! Nate: Ivan Stanislav laying the law down to the President of the OSW!! Robbie: All hail Ivan Stanislav! A TRUE HERO!! Reagan: Stanislav! You can say whatever you want, and that little tramp too, but everyone knows that it is Jane Reagan that the people will follow, not you!!!! Reagan gives her famous thumbs up signal…. Nate: HA HA!! Crowd: IVAN!! IVAN!!! IVAN!!! Tempest and the rest of The Red Army give her a thumbs down signal, to a burst of cheers from the people! Ivan points at Reagan and Toomes…. Ivan: You two are on the top of Red Army’s wanted list. You two disgust me, and will fall now that the TRUTH has been shown about Jane Reagan! We of Red Army will bury you, Jane Reagan, and you’re little castle made of cards as well! Ivan laughs and begins to walk backwards with his Army and Tempest. Ivan: Reagan, you will wind up on your back once again, I am sure….but it will not be because of the spineless whelp beside you..it will be because of Ivan Stanislav’s mighty fist, shattering your worthless spine….DYAAAHAAAHAA!! Toomes yells out, unable to hold it in any longer. Toomes: Alright Stanislav! C’mon!! We’re waiting for you!! Put your money where your mouth is!! Ivan looks over at Tempest and nods. He turns to The Red Army and tells them to leave, which they do. Nate: Ivan Stanislav is going to fight this one without another communist at ringside?? Ivan turns and grins wickedly. Ivan: Sebastian Toomes!! Jane Reagan!! You are both in line to be buried! I have acquired my shot at World Title for Head on Collision! There is nothing that you can do to stop me! But why should we wait for Pay Per View when we can fight NOW!! Ivan roars and throws the microphone away.
