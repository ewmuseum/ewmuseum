Sneaky Little Bastards!

2002-04-13
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sneaky Little Bastards! Written: 13/04/2002 Nate: I'm glad to see that those Hooked on Phonics classes are working out for you, but seriously...Wait a minute folks! I'm being told that there is some action in the backstage area. It seems that Caleb and Minion are up to something. Let's see if we can get some answers. Steven: Action in the backstage area! Alright! Nate and Robbie sigh as the camera shot changes to a poorly lit hallway in the backstage area. Several footsteps can be heard running towards the cameraman. Suddenly, Caleb and Minion turn a corner and a sprinting down the hallway as if they are running form something. Nate: What the hell are those two up to now? Caleb and Minion duck into an alcove in the hallway. As Caleb and Minion disappear out of sight about a dozen security gaurds led by Moose Malone turn the same corner that the two managers just turned. Nate: It's Moose! The fans begin to cheer wildly. The security gaurds run past the alcove where Caleband Minion are hiding. After a moment, Caleb and Minion peak their heads out around the corner and step into the hallway. There is something in the hobo's hand. The cameraman zooms in on the paper. Nate: That's Azrael Cain's contract! How did those two get that Steven: Forget about that! What the hell did they do to piss Moose off so bad? Calen and Minion are breathing heavily with their hands on thier knees. Minion: I...think...that...we...are...in...the clear. Caleb: Good idea! Heh-Heh. I didn't think that it would work, but sure enough, we got the contract. Heh-Heh-Heh. Minion: Once in a while I do come up with one. But we need to get this back to the guys before anything else happens. Moose: Hey! There they are! Get em! Caleb and Minion: AHHHHHH!!! The two little managers bolt out of the camera shot and run down the hallway. Moose and dozen gaurds pass quickly through the camerashot. Nate: What the hell are those two up to? Robbie: Who cares! THey're giving old Moose a work out though aren't they! HA! Steven: Those guys give me the creeps. Robbie: Your shadow gives you the creeps.
