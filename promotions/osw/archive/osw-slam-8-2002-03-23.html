Slam! 8, 23 March 2002

23 March 2002

The scene fades up backstage in Ivan Stanislav’s locker room. Stanislav stands and faces a wall just to the left of a television with a VCR. Behind him sits a small table. Stanislav hangs a large Soviet Flag beside the television. Suddenly, the door behind him opens and Speedy Riggs rushes into the room, clad in his red and yellow referee stripes. He carries a tape under his arm. Speedy: Ivan! Alexei finished with the tape you ordered! Ivan turns from the flag and grins as Speedy hands him the tape that he is carrying. Ivan: I know she will come and ask, and I will convince her otherwise with this tape….DYAAHAAHAA! Speedy: Comrade, what is on the tape? Ivan grins to himself as the camera focuses on the tape entitled, “Communism at Work.” Ivan: The ‘truth’ about what happened between herself, Meanstreak, and myself! I’ll make her watch the entire thing, and then she’ll understand everything! Watch! Ivan laughs and puts the tape in the VCR and hits play. Instantly the television turns on to play the doctored version of that fateful Saturday Slam in which Ivan abandoned Tempest. But instead, it opens up to Ivan Stanislav flattening Doug Troy on the entryway and running down to the ring where Meanstreak stands. Since we know that this is not really what happened, Alexei must have gotten some stock footage of old matches, since we notice the camera’s cut around, probably switching matches of Ivan’s. Ivan: You see? Just like Soviet times! She will never know! Speedy: Ingenious! Good luck Comrade, I have to go! Speedy turns and leaves. Ivan grins and ejects the tape and puts it on the table, at that moment the flag behind him falls. He grunts and turns, fixing the flag. Behind him, oh so silently, a gloved hand grabs the tape and slides it away, replacing it with another, almost IDENTICAL tape! Ivan growls to himself and fixes the flag and tosses the duffel bag away, not even noticing that anything is wrong. He turns and admires his handiwork with his back to the camera. Suddenly a knock sounds at the door. Ivan does not turn around but merely bellows at the wall. Ivan- Da...come in, what is it?? The door opens a little and Tempest peeps in smiling. Tempest: Hi Ivan. I was hoping I'd be able to catch up with you tonight. Ivan turns, his right eyebrow raised. Ivan: Ah, Little Storm. Might Ivan help you with something? Tempest chuckles as she walks in and folds her arms. Tempest: Well, it's sorta what I don't want you to help me with that I wanted to talk to you about. Ivan scratches his forehead, feigning confusion. Ivan: What ever do you mean? Tempest: Oh come on Ivan... you even made a press release about how you and the Red Army are gonna storm my match with Meanstreak. Ivan sighs and grumbles, his brow lowers and his ears raise. Ivan: Ah......that Tempest nods with a little smirk. Tempest: Yes that. Ivan I'd really appreciate it if you didn't. Ivan shakes his head slowly and puts his hands in his pockets, he looks at the floor. Ivan: Tempest, I have heard what you said to Alexei and I have thought about it. But this is something that I must do, you must understand that. I cannot...and I will not allow Jane Reagan to push my men around and I will not allow her to push you around either! Tempest sighs. Tempest: This isn't entirely Jane's doing. She may think it'll be the end of me, but I intend to prove otherwise. I asked for this match after all. Ivan: For all I know, Meanstreak could be in league with them too! Who is to say he did not attack you because he WANTED you to attack him, giving Jane Reagan the opportunity to eliminate you? Ivan shrugs and takes a step closer to Tempest. Ivan: Do you understand? Tempest shakes her head. Tempest: I think you're giving him way to much credit. I don't think he plans anything or thinks anything through. In fact I'm counting on it. How else would I have a prayer in this match if I couldn't outsmart him? Ivan: Outsmarting him is not the problem, Tempest. It is damaging him that is. Other than myself, few men have ever kept Meanstreak down. I do not underestimate your abilities, but you may not be physically capable of defeating him! Ivan stands beside Tempest, looking down at her. She shrugs and looks up at him hopefully. Tempest: Maybe. But I have to try. Just to look myself in the face, you know? Ivan says something in Russian and shrugs. He then pauses and crosses his arms over his chest. Ivan- Alright, I want you to give me one good, reasonable reason why I, as Commander of Red Army AND as Praporschik Ivan Stanislav, should not interfere. Keep in mind regarding the loss of ground we risk due to this action. Tempest: You mean besides the fact that it isn't your match? Ivan: Every match in the OSW involves me, because I want to control OSW itself. Tempest rolls her eyes. Tempest: And that means controlling me and what I do too? Ivan shakes his head and shrugs. Ivan: I am not controlling you, per say. You are fighting in your match, are you not? Ivan pauses and smirks. Ivan: You cannot think of reason, can you? Tempest pouts a little. Tempest: I can think of dozens, but I don't know if they mean anything to you. I don't understand why a simple challenge, that I want to see through, isn't something you get. Why don't you see that this is the right thing to do? If it were you or anyone else, you'd understand my reasons. I have to face Meanstreak to really be finished with him and what happened. Ivan sighs and puts his hands on his hips. He thinks for a long while and looks at Tempest. He grinds his jaw and blinks, and then nods. Ivan: Okay...okay....I will not attack Meanstreak... Tempest smiles. Tempest: Thank you. And hey, if it'll make you feel better, I really do just want a match with him. If he tries to beat me up after it's over or something, then I don't mind the help, okay? Ivan nods. Ivan: I will most certainly take you up on that offer! But here, if I must allow you to go fight alone, you must make up for it with communist learning!! Ivan grins wide, proud of something, and grabs his "Communism at Work" tape and runs over to the VCR. Tempest snickers and shakes her head, but follows. Tempest: Ok, what have you got there, hmm? Ivan laughs and bounds back over to Tempest, shaking the ground as he does. He produces a remote control. Ivan: It is called "Communism at Work" Tempest!! I thought perhaps you should see what had happened to Meanstreak when he tried to hurt you more on Slam weeks ago! I hope you are ready!! She smiles and nods patiently. Tempest: Sure, roll it. Let's see this great work of unbiased reporting. He smiles and hits the remote as the VCR whirs to life. The TV suddenly plays footage of Ivan and Tempests' tag match from weeks ago, but it’s what REALLY happened! Meanstreak just starts to run down the entryway as Ivan's jaw drops. Tempest: Yeah I never really knew what happened here... Ivan yelps and slams his thumb on the remote control, sending his thumb punching through the remote. He tosses it over his shoulder and turns to Tempest.... Ivan: Tempest.... He looks at the television, and then at Tempest and grabs her suddenly by the shoulders, turns her to him, and kisses her directly on the lips, making sure to spin so her back is to the television. He continues to kiss her as the video plays in the background. Tempest makes a little startled noise as her eyes widen a moment. Then she seems to relax and wraps her arms around his neck. Ivan keeps an eye open and watches the television as Tempest wraps her arms around his massive neck. He bends precariously far over to kiss her. He puts his huge hands on her hips and stands, letting her literally hang off of his huge neck by her arms. The kiss lasts for the duration of the video, which is about three minute. When it ends he slowly pulls away takes a step back, his eyes as wide as saucers. Ivan: Uh..I..er.... Tempest smiles with a touch of color in her cheeks. Tempest: What was that for? Ivan swallows hard and tries desperately to regain his composure. Ivan: That...Tempest...that was...that was for good luck. Ivan nods and smiles somewhat confused, yet warm at the same time. Tempest smiles warmly and wraps her arms around his enormous waist and hugs him. Ivan pats her on the head, his eyes still very confused. Tempest: Thanks. I'll need it. Ivan blinks and hugs her back gingerly, not wanting to crush her in his arms. He lets go and smiles as she heads out the door. Ivan turns quickly, grabs her arm and spins her around. Ivan: Do be careful.... She pats his hand and chuckles. Tempest: Don't worry I'll be fine. You'll see. Ivan swallows and nods and lets her go, letting her flit out the door. He swallows again and stares at the wall, stunned, and then rubs the back of his head. He turns his back to the door just as Alexei Ruslan BURSTS into the room. Alexei: PRAPORSCHIK! I saw on television!! The tape! I ran here instantly! What happened?? Ivan turns and blinks at Alexei, not realizing that Tempest’s red lipstick is smeared on his lips. Alexei blinks… Alexei: C….comrade? Comrade??? Ivan: WHAT? Alexei points to his lips. Ivan turns to a mirror and gasps. At that very moment, Loyal Communist Party Member Wang and The Two Round Faced Women walk into the room. Wang claps. Wang: WAHOO PWAPOWSCHIK!! IVAN IS GETTING HIS SWEWVE ON!! Ivan wipes his mouth off and spins, pointing at Wang. Ivan: That is enough out of you!! Ivan hardens himself and stands tall, suddenly all business. Ivan: I will deal with you later, Wang! I told Tempest we would not harm Meanstreak, but that does not mean we cannot interfere with him! Come! We have work to do!! Ivan growls and pushes past Wang, Alexei, and The Women. Wang and Alexei exchange surprised looks and stands for a moment. Suddenly, Ivan’s massive hands shoot through the doorway and grab both men, yanking them through the door. Ivan: NOW!!!
