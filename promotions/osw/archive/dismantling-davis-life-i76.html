Dismantling Davis' Life

2002-05-18
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dismantling Davis' Life Written: May 18, 2002 The camera’s quickly switch backstage to the OSW car lot, where Ivan Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan are seen slowly walking in front of a line of parked cars. Ruslan carries a small clipboard with him as the massive Russian Bear lumbers beside him. Alexei: That is Doug Troy’s….car… Alexei points at a huge white humvee with a license plate reading “DAMN4LFE” on it. Ivan scoffs as they continue to walk… Alexei: And that is Kid Wonders..he..he rents his cars. Alexei points to a nearby, dull red omni with a busted rear windshield, missing hubcap, and rust covered. Ivan pauses and looks at it. Ivan: It is so small! It looks like toy car! Alexei: Da Ivan..just like him…and that is Propaganda’s vehicle… Ivan nods as Alexei points to a large white LCD Van with a license plate that reads: G0D 12 W1TH U. Ivan shakes his head in disgust as they pass it. Alexei: Ah…here it is…. Alexei points to an old, busted up 1994 Pontiac Grand Prix SE. The red paint has seen it’s better days as it has faded considerably. Various nicks and scratches cover the vehicle, showing that it has been well used but perhaps not taken care of to counterbalance the usage. Ivan: This is his vehicle? Alexei: That is correct, Ivan. He is quite attached to the vehicle, it’s been with him for quite some time. Ivan stares at the car as it sits there. His upper lip curls on the right side as he reaches forward and grabs it by the rear bumper and pulls back, dragging the car out of the line of cars. In the background, technicians and staff stare and watch, but none are stupid enough to actually go and try to stop him. Stanislav slams the car back down on the ground and walks around to the front end. He brings his arms over his head and double axehandles the hood, putting a huge hole in the center. Ivan: This is what happens when you attack Ivan Sergeiovich!! Ivan brings his huge foot up over the front bumper and stomps down, pulling the entire bumper off of the front of the car. Alexei smiles wickedly behind Ivan as he tears the hood from the front end and reaches into the guts of the car. Ivan growls as metal is heard bending and cords snap as he tears the entire engine out of the car and chucks it behind him. The engine floats in almost slow motion through the air and lands directly on top of the Omni of Kid Wonder, flattening the roof. Ivan: I will destroy all that is dear to him for what he did to me!! Ivan punches the drivers window and shatters it, before reaching through the window and ripping the steering wheel from the car and whipping it behind him like a discus. He stands by the car and growls… Ivan: This…..is for your lies…. He reaches into the car and grabs the underside of the roof, slowly tearing it upwards…. Ivan: …for your destroying of my name…. Ivan grits his teeth as he tears the roof off of the car and throws it to the ground. Ivan: ….for hurting me…. Ivan kicks the drivers door, driving a hole into the side. He then grabs the backseat of the car and tears it directly from the car and shucks it away. Ivan: ….and for hurting…..for hurting…… Ivan growls and blinks as Alexei looks warily at him from the side. Ivan grinds his teeth as a vein begins to protrude from the side of his head. Ivan growls and roars and then grabs the car on the underside and promptly flips it with tremendous force! The car actually twists in mid air for a moment before falling upside down. Ivan: HEEEEERRRRRR!!!! DYAAHAAHAAHAAHAA!!! Ivan laughs hysterically and pulls the muffler from the car and tosses it away, and then the exhaust pipe. He starts hammering on the underside of the car with his now bloody fists as Alexei reaches over and grabs him by the shoulder. Alexei: Praporschik!! Calm yourself!! I have an answer!! Ivan stops, breathing heavily, sweat pouring off of his face as Alexei produces a small, black ball. He smiles and pushes a button on the side of the ball and drops it in one of the holes in the bottom of the car. Alexei: Lets go Ivan, we are done here…. Ivan nods quickly and turns as the two quickly walk away from the car. The camera stays focused on the car for a moment before it is suddenly engulfed in flames!! Ivan and Alexei are heard laughing as the production crews stare in horror. The camera moves to the side to reveal Kid Wonder’s Omni again. The Omni is in bad shape as well. Flattened by the engine, impaled by the steering wheel, the back end thrashed from the muffler, battered from the hood, and scratched from the exhaust pipe that was thrown, it appears that all the debris found a common place to land. The laugh of Stanislav echoes in the background as we slowly fade away.
