Slam! 26, 27 July 2002

27 July 2002

Jane Reagan’s Voice: So have you considered my offer? The scene fades in to the view of OSW owner Jane Reagan’s red, full lips purring into to the voice piece of a cellular telephone. Panning out, we see the owner’s bright green eyes twinkle inside of her personal office in the backstage area of the Hershey Park Arena, as she leans back in a her comfortable leather chair behind her paperwork filled, oak desk. Jane: (nodding her head) Yes, yes. You would be more than just a mere figurehead. You’d be president both in name, power and responsibility. The auburn-haired owner listens intently to the phone, as her longtime companion Sebastian Toomes enters the shot and begins massaging her shoulders. The site of Toomes causes a surprising pop from the crowd, who are obviously able to see what is unfolding on the Jumbotron in the arena section of the building. Jane’s lips purse into a smile again and she nods her head. Jane: Excellent! I will see you then! With that, Jane flips her cell phone closed, and turns around and wraps her graceful arms around Toomes’ neck. Toomes: I suppose that means we have ourselves our man? Jane winks, then gives Toomes her trademark thumbs up sign. Jane: We have ourselves a President…. Jane pauses to give Toomes a soft, slow peck on the mouth. She then pulls back and looks into Toomes’ eyes. Jane: I already got myself all the man I can handle… A sly smile crosses the face of Toomes, then with a whisk of his left arm, he haphazardly clears all of the paperwork off of Jane’s desk, while lifting the petite owner from her chair onto the desk with his right arm. Jane lets out a giggle, as Toomes pushes her down on the desk…..as the scene fades to black. . . . . . . The usual opening for Old School Wrestling’s Saturday Night Slam begins to roll, showing OSW superstars nailing unwitting opponents with high impact moves. At the point of impact of each move, at Batman-esque SLAM! graphic splashes onto the screen. Wrestlers highlighted are Ivan Stanislav, the pairing of NC-17 and Armour. . . . . . . The show doesn’t open with the usual panning around the arena, pyros and music. There is no burst of excitement or fanfare. In fact, the interior of the Hershey Park Arena is amazingly silent. The camera shifts to the center of the ring, where one man stands alone. That man, is OSW ring announcer, Earl Henrich. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen we now ask that you please rise for a moment of silence, in honor of the life…of Ed. The camera remains focused on a solemn-faced Earl Heinrich as the fans in the arena silently rise from their seats. We briefly cut to the announce table, where Nate, Robbie, and Steven are, for perhaps the first time in OSW history, wordless. A few seconds of complete silence pass by in the arena, until finally… Earl: We will now pay tribute to the memory of Ed by ringing the bell 10 times… During the ringing we ask that you please remain silent. The arena remains eerily quiet, until the silence is cut short by the high, clear ringing of the bell at ringside. Once… The jumbotron comes alive with a shot of Ed raising the UFW Hardcore Championship above his head. Twice… It switches to a shot of a bloodied, yet victorious Ed wearing the PCW Extreme Championship over his shoulder as he stands over a beaten Trashcan Man, the man whose cradle DDT finisher off the ramp two weeks ago ultimately lead to his demise. Three times… A shot of Ed sitting at his OSW Commissioner’s desk, grinning at a furious-looking Jane Reagan. Four times… Ed hitting Meanstreak with the Brink of Insanity during their match at Head on Collision. Five times… Ivan Stanislav and Ed shaking hands in the middle of the ring. Six times… Alan Kriegman laughing evilly over a fallen Ed in an OSW ring… The crowd explodes into a chorus of boos. Nate (breaking the silence): What the hell?! “Back in Black” by AC/DC hits the sound system. Nate: NO!!! FOLKS WERE ARE SUPPOSED TO BE HAVING A TRIBUTE TO ONE OF THE GREATEST COMPETITORS TO EVER STEP INTO A WRESTLING RING, ED, WHO PASSED AWAY IN THE HOSPITAL DUE TO INJURIES HE SUFFERED AT THE HANDS OF THE THINK TANK TWO WEEK’S AGO, BUT NOW THE MAN WHO PULLED THE DAMN PLUG ON HIS LIFE SUPPORT MACHINE’S, ALAN KRIEGMAN IS APPARENTLY ON HIS WAY OUT HERE!!! Robbie: Shhh! The response from the crowd when this happens is too horrendous to be called mere “heel heat”. Something more along the lines of a small riot is incited by this sudden interruption. It only becomes more intense when Alan Kriegman steps out onto the ramp, looking to the fans as if he takes pleasure in their hatred. Nate: THIS IS TOO MUCH, HE CAN’T BE DOING THIS!! WE ENDED LAST WEEK’S SLAM ON A HORRIBLE NOTE, AND THIS WEEK IS STARTING OFF NO BETTER!!! Robbie: Just when you think that man could not get any worse, he goes and pulls something like THIS. God…this man is my hero. Alan Kriegman grins and lets out a chuckle when two lines of police officers begin to file out from under the Jumbotron, one on each side of the evil supergenius and member of the Think Tank. Steven: It’s the fuzz!! Nate: They’re coming out and taking Kriegman away, thank God!! The police officers, however, walk right past Kriegman, and begin lining the ringside area. More men in blue file out and stand at the barriers until the fans are literally blocked from the ringside area by a wall of police. Nate: WHAT THE…?! Fans try to fight past the officers to get at Kriegman as he walks down the ramp, but they are all unsuccessful. Nate: WHY THE HELL IS THAT MURDERER BEING PROTECTED BY THE POLICE?! THIS IS A TERRIBLE WAY TO START THE SHOW, AND DAMN IT, EVEN IF OUR WHORE OF AN OWNER FIRES ME FOR WHAT I AM GOING TO SAY, I COULD CARE LESS!!! CHANGE THE CHANNEL FOLKS!!! DON’T WATCH THIS MANIAC!!! Robbie: I’m tellin’ Jane!!! Nate: I DON’T GIVE A (BLEEP) IF YOU TELL THE DEVIL HIMSELF!!! THIS IS A LOAD OF CRAP AND CHILDREN SHOULD NOT BE SUBJECTED TO IT!!! Meanwhile, at ringside, the evil supergenius smirks as he climbs up to the ring apron, taking a moment to give a wave to the fans before stepping between the ropes into the ring. Shocked by the audacity of Kriegman, Earl Heinrich simply stands there and gawks at him in disgust. Alan walks up to Earl and simply takes the mic from his hand, OSW’s announcer too horrified to do anything but leave the ring, looking back at Kriegman sporadically. Steven: I…I can’t believe this! Nate: I feel ILL because this display. Now alone in the ring, Alan motions to the image on the jumbotron and nods to the outraged fans as they continue to voice their hatred. As “Back in Black” fades out, the arena is left with nothing but the deafening howls of the crowd…until Kriegman raises the mic to his grinning face. The shouts of rage die down enough for us to hear what the man in the black suit has to say. Kriegman: So is the whole interrupting the tribute thing too cliché or what? The crowd explodes into a chorus of boos. Nate: I SHOULD GO UP INTO THE RING AND DOOM DROP THIS SONOVABITCH….RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!! Kriegman smiles evilly, then continues. Kriegman: My friends, I want to tell you all that I am JUST as devastated by the unfortunate passing of Ed as all of you… If not, MORE so than you! He feigns a look of sadness, while fans at ringside continue to try to fight through the barrier of police officers to get to him. Kriegman: There are no words to describe how the death of Ed has affected me as a person. He was one of the true legends in this business, and I daresay that the Hardcore Legend will NEVER be forgotten… Am I right? Nate: DIE, you son of a bitch. DIE. Kriegman stalks around the ring, then raises his index finger to his temple. Kriegman: However, no matter how much I’m going to miss Edward, I must admit that I didn’t come out here to talk about how great a guy he was… Sorry, but I’m just not a “Mourning Person”. The Think Tank member lets out a cackle and slaps his knee at his own joke. Nobody else, however, seems to be amused. Except, of course… Robbie: Hee hee hee hee!!! “Mourning Person”! Get it?! Nate: It was so funny, I forgot to laugh. Kriegman, unlike Nate, is amused by his own wit. Kriegman: Heh heh… No, I’m afraid not, friends. Actually, I’m out here because I’d like to address a certain…rumor…that’s gone floating around as of late. Apparently, for some ungodly reason, you people seem to think that I actually MURDERED Ed! Nate: Oh you’ve GOT to be kidding me… He scoffs, shaking his head and giving a perplexed look. Kriegman: People, I may be capable of many things…but murder is not one of them. Howard, Herbert, and I are geniuses, not barbarians. We don’t stoop to such levels. Frankly, the idea that you all think I murdered Ed is INSULTING. The Think Tank does not murder! Faint “Bullshit” chants begin in the crowd, though most prefer to simply scream in contempt for the evil supergenius. Kriegman (frowning): I expected you all wouldn’t believe me. That’s why I decided to provide you all with some…video evidence. Nate: WHERE IN THE HELL IS LEE HARVEY OSWALD WHEN YOU NEED HIM?!?! The evil supergenius reaches into his suit jacket and pulls out what looks like a remote control. He grins. Kriegman: It was one of the last wishes of the late Ed that this piece of tape be played on OSW television, and I decided who better for the job of presenting it to you all than I? And so, without further adieu… the video will of Ed. Nate: WHAT?! Robbie: I AM STUNNED TOO!! EDDIE COULD WRITE?!?! Steven: Maybe that’s why its on video instead of paper? Nate sighs. Alan gives an evil grin as he raises the remote control to the Jumbotron and presses a button. The Jumbotron crackles to life, fading from the gray and white static of a blank video to the grinning face of the belated Hardcore Legend, Ed, pressed up against the lens. He cocks his head, as if sure it's on, then leans back, revealing the rest of the scene. The camera pans the crowd where some fans are shown crying at the site of the fallen legend. Nate: This is just another sick and sad trick of Alan Kriegman!!! Ed rests comfortably in a big, blue armchair, and is surrounded by rather homey, comfy-looking furniture and decorations. He himself is dressed in his usual garb -- an ubuttoned, single color collared shirt (lime green, in this case), a white T-shirt underneath, and a pair of faded blue denim shorts. His wide smile is framed by a thick goattee, and the rest of his head is covered in a thick mass of unkempt, shoulder-length blonde hair. He winks. Ed: Hey-lo, OSW fans! He pauses, out of sheer habit. The fans in the arena let out a cheer for Ed’s greeting, also out of sheer habit. Ed: The following is a my last will and testament…so if you're watching this right now, I'm probably dead. Hopefully, I died doing some suicidal, insane stunt to garner your love and respect. If not, well, put a round in Osama for me, because that's the only way I'd die outside this ring. He pauses, considering his words, obviously a bit hesitant to speak them. Ed: As I was saying, this is my last will and testament. It’s all legally binding, and on paper, just so you know. This is sort of a humanized, easy to understand version. He stops, a thoughtful expression on his face, then produces a folded piece of notebook paper from his shirt pocket. Ed: Sigh… Ok, first I’d like to do some goodbye. I’m gonna start with-ZIBEEYIBEIABABABABA…. The crowd once again spews forth a chorus of deafening boo’s, when Alan Kriegman presses a button on his remote and the scene on the Jumbotron speeds up, and the voice of Ed begins speaking too quickly to understand. A small icon on the top right corner of the large screen reads “FF” Nate: OH YOU’VE GOT TO BE KIDDING ME!!! Robbie: I LOVE IT!!! The fans continue to voice their outrage. Kriegman merely shrugs. Kriegman: Don’t mind me, I’m just fast forwarding through the boring stuff. Basically this is the part where he says goodbye to all his friends and loved ones. Standard mushy nonsense. Up on the Jumbotron, Ed folds up the sheet of paper, puts it aside, and looks back into the camera before speaking again. Kriegman: Aaannnnddd that should do it. With that, Alan presses another button, and the footage returns to normal speed. Ed: -nother part that I have to talk about…for legal crap, I guess. “It’s called a Do Not Resuscitate” agreement. Basically, it says that if I take, like, a wicked cool bump, but end up some kinda vegetable, that I don’t turn into one of those brain-dead zombies that get kept alive because the hospital has them hooked up to about 20 different machines. If I end up some thoughtless lump after taking a bad spill… please just do me a favor and pull the plug. A gasp of shock explodes from the crowd. Nate: Oh no…NOOOOO!!! THIS CAN’T BE TRUE!!! Ed gives a solemn nod to the camera. Ed: …Right then. Now, onto- He suddenly freezes. Another icon appears, this one reading “PAUSE”. The fans take this opportunity to resume booing. Alan gives an evil grin. Kriegman: A “Do Not Resuscitate” agreement… How very brave of Ed, to not want to remain on life support in the event he were to be incapacitated. Let’s hear it again! Steven: Does this mean what I think it means?! Nate: Sigh… He hits the “rewind” button, and for a brief moment everything moves in reverse on the Jumbotron, before returning to normal. Ed: -20 different machines. If I end up some thoughtless lump after taking a bad spill… please just do me a favor and pull the plug. Nate: NO!!! THIS CAN’T BE!!! KRIEGMAN IS GOING TO GET AWAY WITH MURDER!!!! Robbie: He’s a genius! Alan Kriegman throws his head back and laughs as he rewinds and hits “play” again. Ed: -some thoughtless lump after taking a bad spill…please just do me a favor and pull the plug. Nate: Alan Kriegman is LITERALLY getting away with murder… Kriegman: ONE MORE TIME! Rewind, play. Ed: -just do me a favor and pull the plug. As the image of Ed once again freezes and goes silent, the fans show signs of being more outraged than ever. The evil supergenius cackles again, then regains his composure and smirks. Kriegman: Folks, it doesn’t take an evil supergenius to see that the rumors regarding my murder of Ed are totally false. Don’t you all see? He said so HIMSELF... I was doing him a FAVOR when I yanked the plug on him!! Nate: FUCKING MURDERING SON OF A BITCH!!! Overjoyed by the fact that he has practically incited a riot, Kriegman cannot help but give a big, evil grin. Kriegman: Yes, I did remove Ed from life support…and the best part of all? Robbie: It gets better? Kriegman: …There is not a THING that anyone can do about it, because I was simply following the instructions of his last will and testament! Ladies and Gentlemen, I AM UNTOUCHABLE!!! MUAHAHAHA!!! Another evil laugh follows, drowned out by what could easily have been confused with a clap of rolling thunder from the fans. Kriegman: But WAIT, there’s more! The dark man hits a button, and the video will of Ed once again begins to fast forward. Kriegman: Should be just about…here! It returns to normal speed. Ed: -last thing to take care of before calling it a day…or a night, depending on when you watch this, I guess. My best friend. When I’m gone, I need to make sure that my best friend is taken care of. That he’s in good hands. I’m talking, of course… Ed picks up a rusty old folding chair, wrapped in barbed wire and studded with nails. Ed: …about Bob. I’ll be damned if he’s put in some fricking display case or something. He needs action as much as I do, which means I’m leaving him in the custody of someone that’s gonna make sure he has a lot of fun. That is why I hereby grant custody of Bob to a fellow hardcore icon…and to a dear friend of mine… Nate: Oh GOD please don’t be… Ed: …Trashcan Man. I- The screen goes black, and once again we’re left with nothing but lots of boo’s, and a grinning man in a black suit standing in the ring. Nate: NOOO!!! HE TAPED THIS WILL BEFORE TRASHCAN MAN TURNED TO THE DARKSIDE!!! HAD EDDIE KNOWN HE WOULD NEVER HAVE GIVEN BOB TO THAT SCUMBAG!!!! Kriegman stands amused in the ring. Kriegman: What a generous gesture…and I saw to it that Bob did indeed make it into the right hands. Trash, please come on out here now. Without any music, Trashcan Man comes hobbling out from the back, to a massive explosion of boos holding Bob itself above his head. Nate: THIS IS SO WRONG ON SO MANY LEVELS!!! Robbie: Something so wrong….but feels so RIGHT!!! BWAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Fans shout curses at the mindless slave of Alan Kriegman as he heads straight to the ring, his eyes not once leaving his master. TCM slides into the ring and comes up kneeling before Kriegman, holding Bob in front of him like some kind of demented knight with a sword. Kriegman reaches down and pats Trashy on the head. Kriegman: Well done, Trash. Well done. You heard what Ed said on the Jumbotron, right? The Trashcan Man nods silently, gazing up at Kriegman with adoration. Kriegman: That relic you’re holding…it’s yours now. Ed wanted you to have it if he couldn’t. For a brief moment, Trashy’s eyes flick down to look at the tool of destruction in his hands, then back up to his master. Kriegman: It’s yours. Can I ask you something, Trash? Trashcan Man simple continues gazing. Kriegman: Do you want that, Trash? Or…do you want ME to have it? Without a moment’s hesitation, Trashy holds the weapon up to a smiling Alan Kriegman, who takes it gently from his hands. Grinning happily, Trashcan Man nods to Kriegman, now empty handed. Holding Bob in one hand, Kriegman lowers the mic down to TCM’s face. Kriegman: That’s very nice of you, Trash. Anything else…? TCM: My..my… Nate: I feel ill… TCM: My life for you!! MY LIFE FOR YOU!!! Nate: WHAT IN THE HELL IS GOING ON!!! THIS IS AN OUTRAGE!!! There is a mutual feeling of disgust in the arena as Alan Kriegman raises Bob, formerly his arch rival’s weapon of choise, above his head as Trashcan Man moves forward and hugs his legs tightly. Kriegman: Don’t worry, everyone… I promise that Bob will get a good home. Afterall…I’d hate to do ANYTHING to tarnish the memory of my dear friend Ed! With that, Kriegman lets the mic fall to the mat, then reaches down with his free hand and grabs Trashcan Man by the shoulder, pulling his slave to his feet. We can hear TCM shout “My life for you” to Alan amidst the boo’s. Alan nods approvingly, and exits the ring, TCM following closely behind. Hopefully when we come back we’ll have regained control… Robbie: You GOTTA respect a man that can get away with all this. Nate: I…I… Sigh… We’ll be right back. . . . . . .
