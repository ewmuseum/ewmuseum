Corg and Ritchie Syxx vs. Clay Vendetta and Scott Naket

2002-03-02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Corg and Ritchie Syxx vs. Clay Vendetta and Scott Naket Written: March 2, 2002 Earl: Ladies and gentlemen, Boys and girls, children of all ages, this next tag team match is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring first, reigning form Manchester, UK… Steven is still struggling with what ever is inside. The Doors’ “Peace Frog” begins to play on the arena’s PA system. Earl: Weighing in at 205 pounds and standing at 5 feet 11 inches… The look on Steven’s face is growing more painful. Robbie: Not including the freaks Mohawk. Earl: Ritchie Syxx!!! Ritchie Syxx appears at the top of the entryway. The wrestler pauses for a moment in order to take in the all of the cheers from all of the fans. After a few seconds, Syxx sprints down the ramp and slides under the ropes into the ring. Earl: And his tag partner, reigning from Detroit, Michigan. Standing in at 6 feet tall and weighing in at 172 pounds… Steven: That’s it! I can’t take it anymore! Steven explodes to his feet and begins to yell at Earl. Steven: You suck! And when I say you suck, I mean you really suck! You suck so much that you could suck a golf ball through a garden hose. I don’t know which you suck more at, wrestling or announcing…YOU SUCK! Earl turns quickly and shoots a dirty look at Steven. Steven sits down with a very relieved look on his face. Nate: Feel better? Steven: (relieved) Yeah. Earl finally turns back around after shooting a glare that could stop a brontosaurus in its tracks and begins to finish his job. “Chemical” pierces through the PA system now and Corg quickly appears from the backstage area. He stops at the top of the ramp and stares down at his tag partner for the match with an extreme look of loathing. Corg begins to slowly walk down to the ring never taking his eyes off of Syxx who is returning the same look of distaste. Earl: Corg! Corg climbs up onto the apron and enters through the ropes. He approaches hi stag partner at a slow and menacing pace. Both men finally stand toe to toe now. Corg and Syxx are in each other’s face glaring until they finally break apart towards their respective corner. Earl: And their opponents! Coming to the ring first, weighing in at 225 pounds and standing at 5 feet 11 inches, coming to you from Miami, Florida… The Kansas classic, “Carry on my wayward son” begins to play as The Classic Clay Vendetta appears onto the top of the ramp. Earl: Classic Clay Vendetta! Vendetta heads for the ring at a quick pace all the while gesturing rudely to the boos of the fans. Vendetta climbs into the ring quickly. He turns around quickly and stares at the entranceway waiting for his tag partner to appear. Earl: And his partner, coming to you from Vancouver, British Columbia, standing in at 6 feet 2 inches and weighing in at 240 pounds… “Supermoves” begins to play throughout the arena as Scott Naket appears to the mixed cheers of the fans. Earl: Scott Naket!! Naket hardly waits at all before running down to the ring. He slides under the bottom rope and hops up to his feet. He is in Vendetta’s face. It appears that both men are ready to drop the gloves on each other at any moment. Steven: This is great. Both of these tag teams actually loathe their partners! Ed really knows what he’s doing! Robbie: What? Ed is an idiot! There is no way that this match should have ever been scheduled. This is going to get ugly real fast. Nate: No doubt about that! The question is, which tag team will it be that breaks down first? Whoever takes advantage of that will surely be the winners of this match. Steven: Well, thank you Mister Obvious! Nate: Let’s just get the damn match under way. Inside the ring, both tag team partners are arguing amongst themselves on who will be the one in the ring first. Robbie: Hey, Gaston! Why don’t you get this damn match underway! The French referee gets a shocked and offended look on his face. Naket finally just reaches down and slaps Vendetta’s hand in order to tag himself into the match. Naket sprints across the ring and nails Syxx on the back of the head with a big forearm shot that drops Syxx to the mat. Gaston is too busy acting offended and doesn’t notice the attack. Naket gives Corg a big boot to the midsection now that doubles over the gothic wrestler. Naket grabs Corg by the back of the head and tosses him outside of the ropes to concrete floor. Robbie: Jesus Christ Gaston! Will you ref the match already? Gaston turns around and sees that Corg is on the outside of the ring. As a result of this he thinks that the match is ready to start and he calls for the bell. Nate: Don’t you think that was a little bit harsh? Robbie: No! He’s French, He deserves it! Nate: What the hell are you saying? You’re a racist. Robbie: No I’m not! I just don’t like the French. Steven: Uhm…I think that still makes you a racist, Robbie. Robbie: I am not a racist! I hate everyone equally! Steven: He does have a point…I think. Nate: Arg! Thank God we only have to do this once a week! Inside the ring, Naket is still in control and is laying the boots to Syxx. Syxx using the ropes in an attempt to battle back to his feet. Nate: How is Syxx getting up from this assault? Robbie: Don’t know, but sure wish the idiot would stay down. Syxx gets to his feet to the surprise of Nacket, Syxx is gets back up to his feet and begins to lay into Naket. Both men are exchanging blows in the middle of the ring. Syxx has now got Naket backed up into his own corner and he proceeds to continue laying it on. Naket’s arms are now slumped over the ropes. Vendetta takes this opportunity to tag himself in by slapping Naket’s hand. Vendetta immediately uses the top rope and slings himself over the top rope and delivers a flying spear into the chest of Syxx. Both men crash to the mat. Syxx is holding his chest in pain, but the Classic one is able to get himself to his feet. Nate: And a quick shift in power by here in the match. That was some move by Clay Vendetta. Vendetta pulls Syxx up to his feet by his large spiky Mohawk. Vendetta delivers a perfect DDT on the hurting Syxx. Syxx’s body crumples on top of itself. Vendetta does for the pin. Gaston quickly gets in position to make the count. Nate: This one could be over quick. Robbie: There is a God! 1… 2… NO! Syxx manages to get his shoulder up just in the nick of time. Vendetta slams his right hand against the mat out of frustration. Robbie: Dammit! Vendetta pulls Syxx to his feet. He slings the Mohawked one against the ropes. Syxx hits the ropes and just as he is on his way back to Vendetta, something drops him to the mat right by his corner. Nate: Holy Cow! Corg just tripped up his own partner. Corg begins to pulls his partner out of the ring by his ankles. Just before he drops his partner to the unforgiving pavement floor though he tags himself into the ring. Syxx hits the floor with a loud thud. Corg enters the ring to see Vendetta waiting for him taunting him with his right arm extended out all of the way beckoning for the pale figure to approach. Robbie: Come on already! Someone get a damn pin! I’m getting board. Steven: Will you shut up! This match is a ticking time bomb. This stuff is great. Corg hurls himself at his opponent. Vendetta is caught off guard by this approach. Corg manages to hit Vendetta with a perfect spear. Vendetta falls hard to the mat. Corg gets to his feet only to pull the stunned Vendetta up with him. Vendetta is irishwhipped into the far turnbuckle at an accelerated rate. Vendetta’s body crumples against the unforgiving turnbuckle. Nate: That is some strength shown by Corg! Steven: What the hell is he doing now though? Corg scoops up Vendetta and turns him upside down. Corg tangles Vendetta’s legs under the top rope and leaves him hanging upside down in the corner. Corg crosses to the other corner and seems to be lining Vendetta up. Corg takes off at a full sprint at the helpless Vendetta. Corg flies through the air and delivers a vicious dropkick. The force of the dropkick causes Vendetta to crash to the mat head first out of the tree of woe. Nate: Good God! Robbie: Sweet! Steven: Ha! Gaston: Sac Lebleur! Crowd: OSW! OSW! OSW! OSW! Corg goes for the pin. Gaston is there to make the count. 1… 2… NO! The pin was broken up. Nate: What the hell? Steven: Ladies and Gentlemen the time bomb just went off! Corg looks up to see that the person that broke the pin up was his partner, Syxx. Nate: Syxx doesn’t look too happy about the way that Corg decided to tag himself in. Corg gets to his feet, but is met with a powerful clothesline form Syxx that causes Corg to flip over the top rope down onto the pavement below. Syxx climbs to the top rope. He then flies through the air delivering a Senton Bomb to Corg who was already barely moving. Crowd. OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Nate: That was one hell of a move. Steven: Syxx must have on hell of a pair of balls on him. Robbie: You would like that wouldn’t you? Steven: Shut up! You know what I mean! The stunned Vendetta is starting to get to his feet. Vendetta is laid out by a cruel clothesline from behind by his partner Naket. Vendetta hits the mat hard again. Both of the tag teams have fallen apart. Syxx is outside of the ring beating down Corg, while in the ring Naket is beating Vendetta down. Gaston has a shocked look on his face. Robbie: Hey Frenchy! Call the match already! Gaston signals for the match to be thrown out. The bell rings. NO DECISION Nate: And that’s it ladies and gentlemen, this match has been thrown out! Robbie: Thank GOD! The men continue to brawl with each other without showing any sign of stopping. Security rushes down the ramp in order to stop the fighting. Nate: While security begins to take care of this, let’s go to a commercial. Steven: Get him! Get him! Get him! Ha!
