Stanislav/Krieg Confrontation

2002-06-29
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stanislav/Krieg Confrontation Written: 6-29-02 The show reopens in the backstage area of the Reunion Arena. Voices of technicians and sound techs can be heard close by, our location presumably somewhere near the ramp entrance. Suddenly, as the camera pans round, two massive frames come charging round opposite corners and stumble into each other. The camera pans back, the two figures slightly out of focus at its current position. As the two figures come into full view, they can be seen to be Red Army members, Ivan Stanislav and Michel Krieg. Krieg has on his full wrestling gear, apparently getting ready to head to the ring for his match against Jim Axtell. Ivan: Ah...Krieg. Fancy meeting you here... Krieg: I could say the same for your, Comrade. Ivan straightens up and eyeballs Krieg. Ivan: You do realize, Krieg, that because of your loss at Ascension, Praporschik Stanislav must now destroy El Drago, da? Krieg: Ya. I understand, Ivan. I was also disappointed with Krieg's loss to that dirty wetback. But as you say, it is Ivan's time to destroy... Ivan takes a step closer and narrows his eyes. Ivan: That is right, Comrade. Ivan and Tom have fought together for over a year, you see what sort of a man he is now? You can be just like him...if you follow my example! You seemed to have gone astray...I hope you have righted yourself. Krieg nods in approval. Krieg: Things have been different recently, Ivan. You have seemed to be different man, but I now realise you were focused on Davis. I will follow your footsteps now, Ivan, for I see you as a true leader. Ivan grins and nods. Ivan: Good....goood! Then you watch Ivan destroy El Drago! You see what a true communist is capable of doing! A true communist does not say, 'I quit' but rather smashes the capitalist with his boot heel! You watch tonight, Krieg as Ivan unmasks El Drago. Nodding again, with even more exagerated movements, Krieg grins. Krieg: That sounds great. But there is one thing Krieg can not understand...Krieg tried to take Drago's mask off last night, but you stopped him. Why did you do this? Aren't we supposed to be a group...? Ivan: Do you know what you said, when you fought El Drago? 'I Quit.' Perhaps the most gutless thing Ivan has ever heard. Stanislav gets more stern. Ivan: You did not deserve to have the pleasure of unmasking El Drago, because he bested you. Krieg's grin fades slightly. Krieg: Ah Ivan. You must realise that he is a valid competitor, and that Krieg had no other option than to give up. Would you rather have Krieg injured than save his Communist honour? Ivan nods matter of factly. Ivan: The honor of the Red Army is infinitely more important, than your well being, Krieg. Krieg: So that is the way Ivan works, huh? He doesn't care about his friends and just cares about the outcome? Well, I think Krieg doesn't totally approve with this Ivan... Ivan: To be honest with you, 'Comrade', I think it's time you got back with the Army, and shown your true colours, because as of late, you don't seem like a member of the greatest faction to grace wrestling... Nodding, Krieg grins once more. Krieg: Do not worry Comrade. I will prove my alliance to you and the Army very, very soon... Ivan grits his teeth. Ivan: You had better, Krieg. Your failure has already caused me to take MY time out to handle Drago....I don't want to have to redirect any more of my time to educating you.... Krieg slaps Ivan on the shoulder and grins once more. Krieg: Do not worry about me Ivan. My loyalty to you is unfailing. No more of your time will have to be wasted on my behalf, believe me... With that, Krieg nods at Ivan and continues on his path towards the staging area for wrestlers “on deck”, leaving the Russian Bear to continue on his own travels as the scene fades to black.
