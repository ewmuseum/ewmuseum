"So, Mister Toomes, how are you feeling today?" There was no answer, except a low and short moan from the mouth of the nearly paralyzed Sebastian Toomes. The scene opened up to the hospital room in which former OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian Toomes lay, motionless and battered. A small graphic, reading “Earlier Today” sits on the lower, right hand corner of the screen. A doctor stood next to the bed of Mister Toomes, and began jotting notes down. With every line he wrote, he shook his head in disbelief. Never in his short career had he seen someone this beaten and broken. The doctor surveyed his chart over and over, making sure he didn't miss any details. It was unbelievable, Toomes had numerous stitches, a very battered back, and a serious concussion. "Mister Toomes, what I recommend, and what I will do, is keep you here for at least a month or possibly more. You need lots of rest and medication. Your injuries are very serious, so you'll need to stay away from 'the ring' as long as you can," said the doctor in a very sympathetic voice. Toomes was obviously uneasy about this information and struggled a bit in his bed as he stared up at the doctor in anger. Toomes still couldn't speak all that well because of the pain and the drugs injected in his body. He was very tired, but very determined to get up. The doctor laid his hand upon Sebastian's wrist to assure him that getting up would not be a good idea. The doctor shook his head at Toomes, indicating that Toomes shouldn't move. Toomes, against his will, did so, and laid still in his bed... Suddenly...the camera switched views from the hospital room...to the outside of the hospital. The fans watching the footage on the Jumbotron in the Gund Arena boo wildly at the sight of one of the most hated men in OSW currently. That man was none other than OSW Commissioner Howard Porter. Porter grinned evily as he looked up at the hospital, and back at his work. His work was the payphone, he had ripped a majority of the phone's wires out and began toying with them with some tools. Porter continued fiddling with the wires and finally a large smile came over his face as he picked up the receiver and just grinned in happiness as he spoke to himself… "Ahh, the wonders of technology. It truely is amazing how far geniuses such as myself can go at times...it truely is. And thanks to my genius...I believe that the doctor may have a...'very'...important call to make..." Suddenly, the scene cuts back to the hospital room where Sebastian Toomes still lays still, with the doctor beside him, looking at his notes...when suddenly. The doctor's pager goes off. The doctor looks at his pager, and his eyes widen. Something on that pager was shocking...the doctor took a step back in surprise as he looked down at Toomes and spoke... "Excuse me Mister Toomes, I have a very important call to make. Don't worry, I'll have a nurse come in here and look after you while I am gone. I'll be only ONE minute Mister Toomes, just be patient" The doctor patted Toomes on the shoulder, to replace a handshake, and quickly left the hospital room in a hurry. And there he lay… Alone. Sebastian Toomes, the first OSW World Champion...a man with much honor and respect backstage...was now nothing more...than a vegetable. Toomes slowly closed his eyes, hoping the next moment he opened his eyes, he would be in much less pain and able to walk and get up once again. He was in terrible shape, and hoped it'd go away by the time he woke up... He slowly closed his eyes, and began to drift into dreamland...but something prevented his trip to be too long. A tap on the shoulder. Toomes sighed loudly as he thought to himself, "God damn doctor, what the hell does he want?". Toomes slowly opened his eyes and only saw a little bit of a blur. He slowly raised his hand to his eyes and rubbed them harshly to regain his vision...but did he really want to do that? When his vision became clear...so did his immediate future...for the figure standing right next to him...was the face of the new Monster of OSW, Armour! Toomes' eyes widened in shock and horror as he stared into the monster's eyes, as the monster looked directly into Toomes' eyes and asked in a simpathic way... "Sleep well?" And with that, a fist went flying into Toomes' broken nose. Toomes moaned in pain as Armour grabbed hold of his head and began sending hard right fists into his cranium, over and over...making those concussions even worse. Armour finally stopped his full frontal punching assault, but he wasn't done with Toomes. Armour grabbed Toomes by the throat and hurled him off the hospital bed and into the wall! Toomes' back bounced off the wall and right into the arms of Armour...who lifted Toomes up high in a gorilla press, and slammed Toomes head first into the wall! Meanwhile, fans back in the Gund Arena allow their displeasure at the happenings earlier in the day be heard loud and clear, in the form of a chorus of boos. That was little consolation to Toomes, however, as he crumbled to the floor in a great deal of pain... …but the Animal Called Armour still wasn't finished. He quickly pulled Toomes to his feet and grabbed him by the head. Toomes looked into Armour's angry face with glazed eyes, as Armour ran Toomes skull into the steel siding of the hospital bed, leaving blotches of red stains on the bed and on the floor...and most importantly...a huge blotch of blood all over Toomes head and re-broken nose. Yet the beast was still not satisfied. Armour looked at the fallen Toomes, who was using all his power to even get to his knees...Toomes grabbed Armour’s waist to pull himself up, but all Armour did was spit into Toomes' face and send a hard elbow into the side of Toomes' head. Toomes smacked into the wall, and looked to fall once again, but Armour grabbed Toomes by his long hair and pulled him right back up. Armour lifted Toomes up high in the air with a POWERBOMB. The fans watching on the Jumboron screamed in horror, as Armour CHARGED towards the hospital bed and sent Sebastian Toomes THROUGH the steel barrier AND the bed itself! Toomes body lay a bloodly mess amongst the ruins of a hospital bed and dent steel. Armour looked down at Toomes and spat at his fallen body as the man with all the stroke, “The Scholar” Howard Porter, made his way into the room. Porter's sinister smile appeared in the hospital room, as he patted Armour on the shoulder and nodded in his direction. "Another job done well Armour...you deserve much recognition for this slaughter," stated Howard Porter with the upmost pride in his voice as he patted Armour on the back one last time. But Armour seemed to have blanked out Porter, as he continued staring at the fallen body of Toomes. Porter smiled as he looked down at Toomes. Porter slowly walked over to the fallen body Toomes, and knelt down beside it. Porter grabbed Toomes' hand and held it to his chest, as a mockery to Toomes...as if Porter was saying 'I'm here for you'. Porter smiled at the fallen body of Toomes as he leaned towards Toomes' face and said out loud... "You walked down the wrong pathway Toomes. You should have never crossed the path of the Think Tank...it was the worst mistake of your insignificant life. Don't you get it Toomes? You are worthless now. You're reign of being one of the greats has faded...now...you're just one...of the victims. It looks like THIS star...has fallen." And with that, Porter released the hand of Toomes and allowed it to fall on the unconscience body of the STAR (who has fallen, in Porter's eyes). Porter got up and faced Armour, and motioned toward Armour to leave. Porter didn't wait for Armour and left on his own, but Armour took one last glance at Toomes and slowly followed Porter...leaving a site of mayhem behind.
