Riptah — move list (August 2002)

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Move List Punk-Out: (Gorilla Press into Stunner) Riptah lifts the opponent off the ground and presses them over his head. He then pushes them into the air and catches the on the way down so their chin goes over his shoulder as he sits down. Ghetto Blaster: (Pumphandle Bulldog) Riptah grabs the opponents arm and pulls it between their legs in a pumphandle position. While still holding the opponent’s arm, he uses his other arm to get the opponent in a headlock. He then runs and jumps, driving the opponent’s face into the canvas. Edgetreme: (Sit-down Tombstone Piledriver) Riptah flips their opponent into a sixty-nine position. He then jumps and sits down, driving the opponents head into the canvas. Riptide: (Tiger Driver ’91) Riptah kicks his opponent in the abdomen. He then hooks both arms behind the opponents back and hoists the opponent into the air. He then falls to his knees, driving the head of the opponent into the canvas. Hit n’ Run: (Stunner) Riptah kicks the opponent in the abdomen and spins around, catching the opponent’s head. He jumps and sits down, driving the opponents chin into his shoulder. Sideliner: (Jackhammer) Riptah sets up his opponent in suplex position. He then hoists his opponent off the ground and holds him in the air for a few moments. He then releases his opponent, letting him drop into a powerslam. R.I.P. Bottom: (Rock Bottom) Riptah tucks his head under his opponent’s arm and hooks his head. He then lifts his opponent of the ground and drives him back first into the canvas. Westside Slam 1: (Olympic Slam) Riptah gets under his standing opponent, grabbing his shoulder with one hand and his hip with the other. He pulls his opponent over his own shoulders and elevates him into the air, slamming him onto the canvas in a semi-elevated Samoan Drop. Westside Slam 2: (Hangman’s Tigerbomb) Riptah places his opponents between his legs and hooks both arms. He pulls his opponent over his shoulder in a Powerbomb position and drives his opponent onto his neck by falling onto his knees. Westside Slam 3: (Cradle Piledriver) Riptah lifts his opponent into a Piledriver position, but holds him there for a moment. He then hooks one of his opponents legs and drives him into the canvas by sitting down. 730 Crash: (Triple-Jump DDT) Riptah places a chair in the center of ring. He runs off the ropes and jumps onto the chair. He springboards off the chair only to land on the top-rope. He then springboards off the top-rope to the outside, catching his opponent with a DDT. Riptah’s Ride to Hell: (Inverted DDT) Riptah hooks the opponent’s head with one arm. With his other arm he grabs his opponent’s tights and lifts him into the air. Riptah then drops and drives the opponent’s face into the canvas. Ebonics Lesson: (Sit-Down Reverse DDT) Riptah gets behind his opponent and hooks their head with his arm, bending them backwards. He then sits down, driving the back of his opponent’s head into the canvas. Punksmoker DDT: (Fireman’s Carry DDT) Riptah lifts the opponent onto his shoulders in a Fireman’s Carry position. He then uses his strength to throw his opponent off his shoulders, spins, hooks his head over his mid-air opponent and drives his face into the canvas. Afro-Kick: (Standing Side Kick) While standing, Riptah kicks horizontally at his opponent, catching him in the chin.
