Running in to help Timo, Dame, Ed and Stanislav.

2002-04-13
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Running in to help Timo, Dame, Ed and Stanislav. Written: April 13th, 2002 Hostyle grabs at his head and rolls out of the ring. Bones groggily rises, only to be hit with The Cave In! Bones staggers backwards into the ropes while Hostyle grabs a chair. Timo bounces off the ropes, not knowing that Hostyle is ready to swing and hit him. From the backstage area, Aimz rushes to the ring just behind Hostyle!! Nate: Aimz is here to even the score!! Aimz grabs the chair from Hostyle just as he is about to swing it and takes it from him! Hostyle turns around, only to be crowned by the chair! Timo hits Bones with a crescent kick and drops him, and goes for the pin!! Like…1….like….2…like….3!! FRANKIE BONES HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!! Aimz picks up Drew Hostyle and slides him into the ring! Robbie: Hey! They’re cheating!!! Nate: Wait a minute….forget it, I’m not even wasting my breath… Timo hops to the top rope while Aimz rolls Hostyle into the ring. He leaps and delivers Fist of The Heavens!! Steven: That’s Timo’s finisher!! It could be over!! Timo hooks the leg! Like…1…like…2….like…3!!! DREW HOSTYLE HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!! Bones and Hostyle charge up the entryway and backstage! Nate: The playing field is now even!! Timo gets up from Hostyle just in time to see Switchblade climb into the ring. Switchy makes a beeline for Timo. Bolamba dives to the side for a drop toe hold but Switchblade is able to dodge it! Switchblade spins around and drops to a knee, pinning Timo’s ankle to the mat! Timo screams in pain and kicks Toomes in the face with his free foot. Both men rise and lock up in the center of the ring. They fight for supremacy of the hold. Switchblade goes for a knee but Timo blocks it. The two break the hold and stare each other down. Nate: About even here, eh guys? Switchblade rushes at Timo, unable to control himself. Timo reaches behind him and tags in Stanislav, who Switchblade had not even been paying attention to! Stanislav climbs in the ring just as Switchblade leaps for a cross body block! Stanislav catches him and holds him in his arms for a moment. Switchy kicks and screams as he tries to get free of the iron grasp of Stanislav. Ivan drops to a knee and delivers a backbreaker! Steven: Ouch! That’s gonna leave a mark! Robbie: Stanislav’s a wuss! He’s whipped! From backstage, Jane Reagan walks down the entryway, poised to cause trouble. Aimz and Tempest, still at ringside, stand in her way. Jane points at them and screams. Aimz and Tempest look at one another and then charge up the entryway towards her! Jane screams and takes off backstage. Tempest and Aimz stop, slap each other five, and head back down to ringside! Robbie: How can they do that? She’s the boss! She can walk anywhere, do anything, do anyone…. Stanislav pushes Toomes off of his knee and stands up tall. He dusts off his hands as the crowd cheers loudly for The Russian Bear. He reaches down and lifts Toomes. Toomes shoots his arm out between Ivan’s legs and tags him with a low blow. Stanislav growls but doesn’t seem fazed. Toomes hits him two more times below the belt. Finally having its effect on Ivan his eyes cross and he falls backwards onto his back. Toomes stands up and points at Ivan and laughs. Robbie: HA HA HA! He’s laughing at Ivan!! Tempest reaches under the bottom rope and slams her palms against the mat, trying to rouse Stanislav. Ivan rolls over groggily, but Switchblade is ready for him. As Ivan gets to his hands and knees Switchblade legdrops over the back of his head, driving Stanislav’s face into the mat. Switchblade hops on top of Ivan and wrenches him in a camel clutch. He pulls the massive skull of Stanislav back at a sickening angle and laughs maniacally. Nate: I will give him credit, Sebastian Toomes has got the right idea. Keep Stanislav grounded and put him in a submission! Robbie: That’s why he’s The World Champion! Ivan reaches for the ropes, but even his massive body is too short to reach to the other side of the ring. Toomes nods to himself as Stanislav begins to fade. Tempest screeches on the outside of the ring and stares into Ivan’s eyes as they begin to shut slowly. Robbie: He’s done! Stanislav is done!! Switchblade is going to be the first man to make Ivan submit!! From the center of the ring, roughly where Stanislav is, a low growl is heard. It grows slowly. All the while, Switchblades look of triumph begins to dwindle. Stanislav presses his hands to the mat and with a sudden roar BURSTS to his feet with Switchblade on his back!! Nate: WHOA! Switchblade hangs around the back of Ivan’s head for dear life as Stanislav shifts his weight and sends Switchy careening across the ring into the turnbuckle! Stanislav roars loudly and rushes towards Toomes with his arms over his head and a look of blind rage in his eyes! Toomes ducks out of the way at the last moment, sending Ivan CRASHING into the turnbuckle! The entire ring moves as Stanislav’s body wraps around the turnbuckle! His head snaps forward and hits the steel post! Steven: That can’t be good… Ivan rocks back and falls to the mat in a heap! Switchblade stares in amazement at the fallen Soviet! He looks over at Meanstreak and shrugs and tags him in! Nate: Meanstreak and Stanislav!! Meanstreak rushes towards Stanislav and then steps over him and grabs Timo!! Steven: …maybe not. Switchblade slides out of the ring and waves off the match. He walks around the ring to the entryway and stares at Meanstreak, who now has powerbombed Timo, and Ivan who rolls to his side and shakes the cobwebs free. Switchblade turns and runs up the entryway! Nate: WHAT? HE CAN’T LEAVE THE MATCH!! Robbie: He’s the champ! He can do whatever he wants! Meanwhile, inside the ring, referee Lovejoy has seemingly allowed Meanstreak and Timo to fight. Meanstreak headbutts Bolamba violently and sends him into the ropes. Timo rebounds and leaps for a dropkick! He hits Meanstreak and glances off! Meanstreak roars and grabs Timo by the back of the head, he lifts him high into the air and tosses him stomach first into the turnbuckle. He rushes forward and drives his knee into the back of Bolamba… Crowd: OHHHH!!! Meanwhile, Switchblade makes it to the top of the entryway just as a dark figure on a motorcycle rounds the corner and faces him… Nate: IT’S THE BLACK RIDER!!! Switchblade blinks in horror as the Black Rider pulls his baseball bat out of his trench coat and swings it once and then points it at Switchy! Robbie: NO! Run! Go back to the ring!!! Switchblade screams and backpedals quickly down the entryway. Inside the ring, Meanstreak lifts a now loopy Timo Bolamba onto the top turnbuckle and tosses him in a massive German suplex! Timo lands sickeningly on his head and lays prone in the ring! Nate: Pandemonium has broken loose folks!!! Meanstreak and Timo Bolamba!! Switchblade and The Black Rider!! Ivan Stan…hey, where is Ivan? Just as Nate speaks, the camera switches to Switchblade who continues to backpedal. Suddenly, he stops and runs into a large, black chest…. Robbie: -YELP- Switchblade reaches back behind him and feels the chest of Ivan Stanislav! He turns and looks up at the cold, angry eyes of The Russian Bear!! Beside Ivan stands Tempest. She sticks her tongue out at Switchblade and laughs! Robbie: Run….run anywhere! SECURITY!! Switchblade brings his hands up, but he’s too late. Stanislav wraps his massive paw around the head of Toomes and spins him around to face the ring… Nate: What’s he doing?? Stanislav roars loudly and lifts Toomes high in the air, he drops him and in mid air CRUSHES him with a short arm clothesline!! Steven: THE IRON CURTAIN!!! Toomes flies through the air and slams back first into the steel post with a sickening CLANG! The crowd winces as one as Toomes slides down the pole in a heap! The Black Rider turns on his bike and rides away… Nate: THE BLACK RIDER HAS DONE HIS DAMAGE!! TOOMES ISNT MOVING!! Robbie: Stop yelling you two!! God! Inside the ring, Meanstreak lifts Timo into At Death’s Door. He walks around the ring and roars at the crowd, parading around with the prone Bolamba over his shoulders. Outside, Ivan grabs Toomes and growls at him. Ivan spins Toomes around, looks over his shoulder, and sets him up for The Red Scare!! Robbie: He’s gonna reverse it…right now…. Stanislav roars and tosses Toomes in The Red Scare… Robbie: …alright..so I was wrong….he’ll land though…. Toomes flies over the top rope, into the ring, and hits Meanstreak!!! Meanstreak staggers as he is hit from the 200+ pound projectile that is the OSW World Champion! Meanstreak drops Timo, who lands on his feet and staggers into the corner! Steven: Meanstreak doesn’t look happy! Meanstreak reaches down and grabs Toomes by the neck! Toomes struggles and screams as Meanstreak roars in his face! Behind them, Stanislav climbs into the ring beside Timo! Switchblade points behind Meanstreak to their two enemies. Meanstreak lets go of Switchblade just as Stanislav and Timo rush forward! Stanislav clotheslines Toomes out of the ring while Timo nails Meanstreak with a cross body block! Meanstreak staggers backwards and flips over the top rope with Timo still on him, and both men fall in a heap outside the ring! Nate: Stanislav and Timo have cleared the ring!! Steven: Yeah, but Timo took a big bump too… Timo and Meanstreak roll on the floor and continue to slug it out. Toomes screams as Ivan reaches down from over the top rope and grabs his head! Stanislav hoist Toomes into the ring and tosses him across the ring! Nate: Toomes and Ivan!! Finally!! Ivan points across the ring at Toomes and yells in Russian. The crowd begins to get pumped again… Crowd: IVAN!! IVAN!! IVAN!!! Outside the ring Meanstreak whips Timo into the guardrail and rushes after him. Timo dodges to the side, causing Meanstreak to fly into the guardrail shoulder first! Aimz runs over to Meanstreak and kicks him in the side. Meanstreak shoots to his feet and pushes Aimz away. Ivan allows Switchblade to get up and face him. The Russian Bear roars loudly at Toomes and beckons for him to attack. Switchblade eyes Ivan warily and gets closer. The two lock up! Ivan lifts Toomes into the air with a double chokehold and tosses him across the ring! Toomes rolls and stands again. He circles Stanislav, who stands in the center of the ring. Nate: Switchblade is gonna have to be careful…Ivan doesn’t look happy…. Timo and Meanstreak fight on the entryway now, nearing the backstage area. Aimz hangs on Meanstreak’s back and tries to wrestle him to the ground, but he shrugs and sends her to the entryway. Meanstreak grabs Timo and lifts him high over his head! He tosses Timo at the Jumbotron! Timo’s head smacks off the steel border of the Jumbotron and he falls to the entryway, twisted and bleeding! Aimz screams and runs over to Bolamba! Steven: That’ll leave a mark… In the ring, Stanislav brings his hands away from his body and beckons Toomes to take his best shot. Toomes looks at Ivan, thinking it is a trap… Nate: Ivan Stanislav is a brick wall…he can’t be hurt…. Toomes squints his eye and rushes to the side of Ivan, he kicks hard to the right floating ribs of Ivan and plows into them! Ivan roars suddenly and grabs his side, falling to a knee!! Nate: Ivan’s bad rib!!! Toomes knows a weakness!! Toomes smirks and sidekicks Ivan in his rib again. Ivan roars in pain and rolls over on the mat. Tempest hops to the apron and screams as some blood trickles out of Ivan’s mouth! Toomes turns and begins to walk towards Tempest cockily. Slowly, Ivan rises behind him. Ivan wraps his massive arms around the body of Switchblade and places his massive chin over the top of Switchy’s head…. Nate: What is he doing? Ivan roars as Switchblade screams futilely. Stanislav lifts Toomes up into the air and roars. He slams the tailbone of Toomes into the mat, therefore wrenching his neck due to Ivan’s chin, and thus the entire 400 lbs frame of Stanislav does as well! Meanstreak lifts the prone body of Timo into the air and tosses him backstage and disappears behind as well, Aimz follows, screaming. Steven: Ivan Stanislav and Sebastian Toomes are the only two men left in the ring!! We’re getting a taste of the Pay Per View early!! Ivan rolls Toomes over and puts both hands on his chest…. Like….1….like…..2….kickout by Toomes!! Ivan growls and lifts Switchblade. He sends him into the corner and walks over, favoring his right rib slightly. Ivan puts his massive paw on the chest of Toomes and slaps it with the other hand with a loud THA-WHACK! Toomes falls to his knees and grabs at his chest. Ivan kicks Toomes hard in the side of the head. Nate: Folks! I believe I am getting a message from backstage! There are several backstage incidents occurring at once!! Our view splits to three different camera views: A large singular view over the top half of the screen, two half shots in the lower half. In the upper center, Ivan Stanislav stalks around Toomes and roars at him. In the lower left, Michael Dame and Frankie Bones are brawling just outside of a bathroom! Bones grabs Dame by the head and throws him headfirst into the bathroom door! The door flies open and Dame staggers into the tiled room. Drew Hostyle is seen with Bones as they walk into the bathroom and shut the door! In the lower right, Timo Bolamba lays out in the car lot inside the arena. Meanstreak has a cord wrapped around his throat and is dragging him across the floor! Aimz is seen in the background, seemingly unconscious! Nate: Folks! Mayhem has broken loose throughout the Rupp Arena! The OSW wrestling ring is unable to control these men as they continue their melee backstage!! Inside the ring, Ivan Stanislav lifts Toomes into a powerbomb! Ivan goes to snap Switchy, but he holds on around Ivan’s head. Ivan roars and tries again, but Switchy shoves a thumb in his eye! Ivan staggers back into the turnbuckle and roars. He sloppily throws Switchblade which gives the much more dexterous Toomes time to land on his feet! Switchy charges and drives a shoulder into the rib of Stanislav, eliciting a roar from The Russian Bear once again! Robbie: That’s a true champion! He knows the weak points! He knows his enemies! He’s a strategist!! Nate: He’s a piece of shit, Decker! Toomes laughs and grabs Ivan’s head and bulldogs him hard to the mat. Backstage, the camera switches to inside the bathroom where Drew Hostyle and Frankie Bones hold Dame’s head in the way of a bathroom stall door. Bones laughs and slams the door, slamming it against the head of Dame!! Dame collapses and doesn’t move. Hostyle and Bones slap each other five and leave as Dame is left bleeding from the head. Meanstreak, meanwhile, has Timo up in a double chokehold and is choking the life out of him. Timo fades slowly as Meanstreak throws him up against a truck and dusts his hands off. Suddenly, Ed hobbles behind Meanstreak and slams BOB across his back! Meanstreak roars as little spots of blood form on his back from the chair. He turns, only to get slammed over the head with the lethal chair! Meanstreak falls to a knee! Steven: STOP THE VIOLENCE!! PLEASE!! Back at ringside, Ivan Stanislav lies clutching his side. Blood pours out of his mouth onto the mat as Switchblade stands over him and kicks him repeatedly in the side. Ivan roars in pain, but with every attempt he makes to stand he is kicked back down. Finally, Switchblade himself lifts Ivan up. He sets Ivan up for a suplex and raises his arm high to the crowd. Nate: Switchblade must think he’s got Ivan wore down enough to suplex him! Robbie: Well, he IS the champ, right? Steven: But that doesn’t mean he can… Robbie: YES HE CAN! Switchblade goes for the suplex and gets Ivan up halfway, but the massive Russian falls back to the mat. He tries again, but with less success as well as favoring his back slightly. He tries one final time and a loud roar comes from Stanislav! His roar pierces through the crowd noise to our ears as Ivan reverse the suplex into The Red Scare! Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Nate: RED SCARE!!!! RED SCARE!!!! Ivan falls over as he finishes the move, but Switchy slams into the corner, staggers out, and collapses. Ivan lays on the ground and grabs his side, blood pouring from his throat. He brings one huge hand out and drapes it over Switchblade…. Like….1….like….2…like…3!!! Winners: Ivan Stanislav, Timo Bolamba, Michael Dame, Ed!!! Nate: THEY DID IT!!! THEY BEAT REAGAN AND HER LACKIES!!! Backstage, Meanstreak lies on the floor, his head jammed between the seat and the back of Bob! Timo Bolamba and Ed struggle to hold the roaring Meanstreak, who’s thrashing only cuts his face up more on the barbed wire. Steven: LOOOOOOOOK!!! Suddenly, Aimz appears with a sledgehammer! She swings the hammer up over her head and SLAMS it into the chair! Meanstreak roars once more loudly, and then goes silent, blood trickling from under the chair. Nate: GOOD GAWD!!!! Ed takes Bob and heads towards the ring with Aimz and Timo…. In the arena, the crowd roars in triumph as Tempest runs into the ring and helps Ivan stand! Stanislav raises his hand over his head and accepts the cheers as Tempest wipes the blood from Ivan’s mouth and kisses him. Nate: IVAN STANISLAV DID IT! REAGAN HAS BEEN DEFEATED! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Switchblade rolls out of the ring and into the crowd to escape, being beaten by fans as he does! Steven: Watch him leave in disgrace!! From the backstage area, Timo, Aimz, and Ed head down the entryway, about halfway there Michael Dame appears and staggers to the ring. Stanislav, Tempest, Timo, Aimz, Ed, and Michael Dame stand in the ring as the crowd continues to cheer. The Soviet National Anthem blares over the loudspeaker as The Red Army in it’s entirety heads to the ring and stops. They turn just as Frankie Bones, Drew Hostyle, Hardy, and Jane Reagan appear on the entryway. Krieg, Walczak, and The Two Round Faced Women cross their arms and make a blockade while the others celebrate in the ring. Nate: The Red Army has come to the aid of the good of the OSW and have blocked Reagan! Stanislav, Dame, Bolamba, and Ed are victorious!! The fans have gotten what they deserved tonight folks and the good of the OSW has triumphed!! Reagan screams leaves with her cohorts…. Nate: WHAT A WAY TO END AN ABSOLUTELY INSANE NIGHT!!! WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT EVERYONE!!! GOOD NIGHT!!! The camera shifts towards the ring, as Red Army patrols ringside. In the center of the ring, Ivan and Tempest embrace, with the Russian Bear lifting the little storm high in the air, while Timo, Aimz, Ed, and Dame each ascend the turnbuckles and scream to the crowd….as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears in the lower, central portion of the screen….as the scene fades to black.
