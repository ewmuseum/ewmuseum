Corg — move list (October 2002)

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wrestling Style: Corg has the style of Ku-Moei, which he was taught in Realm 2. 'Ku-Moei', consists of a branch of odd fighting skills. Brutal submissions, fatal aerial stunts, and psychotic kicks. Ku-Moei is only used in a certain stance, the stance of crouching low with your arms seizing the area of your face. It creates a sense of inner mentality, making it easier to focus on the opponent. Set Up Move: ... (back leg sweep to shins, knocking opponent onto their knees ) Finisher: Death Blooms (spinning super kick to the back of opponent's head) Other Moves: Close Combat Moves At Least I Know I'm a Sinner (reverse hurricanrana) Target Audience (running tree of woe dropkick) Introduction to Mayhem (tiger backbreaker) My Decayed Paper Doll (running axe kick) A Lethal Dosage (mid-air superkick) Holidy in Cambodia (flying top rope heel kick) Cross Out the Eyes (running front dropkick to opponent's stomach) The Downward Spiral (implant DDT) Fuck the System (dragon suplex with bridge) My Mirror No Longer Reflects (armbar neckbreaker) At Your Funeral (running side kick) Rape Me (tiger suplex with bridge) Infected (running flying headbutt to opponent's stomach) If I Wore a Mask (flying head scissors) High Risk Moves Set Your Body Ablaze (super frog splash) Total Immortal (dragon attack) Friend of the Enemy (flying crossbody) Bury Me Where I Fall (springboard moonsault) Your God is Nothing (middle rope sunset flip) A Wish for Wings That Work (top rope double stomp) Wait and Bleed (slingshot swanton bomb) Redemption Song (corkscrew moonsault) One Man Army (springboard dropkick) Tell Myself Goodbye (tope rope sunset flip) Submissions Now I'm Nothing (a modified Tazzmission) The Morningstar (Dragon sleeper) Snowblind (abdominal stretch) Suicide is Painless (scissors face lock) The Soft Parade (reverse Indian death lock) Breathe in Bleed Out (STF) A Blister in the Sun (triangle hold) War Within a Breathe (octapus stretch) Anarchist Superstar (the turantula) Hooker With a Penis (rolling cobra clutch) Minus Blindfold (figure four leg lock) Desperation Moves More Human Than Human (suicide dive to opponent on the outside) In the Skin (manible claw) If You Can't Beat 'em, Burn 'em (Corg randomly attempts to light his opponent on fire) A Day in the Strife (spinning kick to the groin) Pieces of You in Me (Corg holds the opponent's mouth open while Shyme gnaws on their tongue)
