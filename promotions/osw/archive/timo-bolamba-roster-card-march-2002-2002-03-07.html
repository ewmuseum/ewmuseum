Timo Bolamba Real Name: Timotoga Aryno Bolamba Birthdate: 08-28-1974 Hometown: Apia, Samoa Height: 6'2' Weight: 245 lbs lbs. Nicknames: 'The Cobra' 'The Samoan Silencer' 'The Human Highlight Reel' Status: Face Manager(s): Gwenneth Cross Current Teams: none Entrance Music: 'Defy You' by The Offspring Wrestling Style: High Flyer, Hardcore Signature Moves: 'Evolution S'- 720 splash from the top rope, many forms of suplexes, knife edge chops, figure four, second rope elbow drop, springboard flying elbow, flying missile dropkick, sleeper, ddt, Samoan Drop Set Up Move: Cave-In (Spinning heel kick to the opponentschest ) Finisher: Fist of The Heavens (Top Rope Summersault into a leg drop, sometimes finished with a chair for added effect.) OSW Title History: none OSW Award History: none Other Federations: PCW, EWA Other Federation Awards: Voted Best Stock Rising two months in a row, voted funniest man in PCW Handler Information Handler Name: Nick B. AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: see email Bio Timo Bolamba spent his life in the gutters, working day to day within the Indy Federations, where he made a name for himself as a great performer, and where he got his nickname 'The Samoan Silencer'. After several years in the hard life, he was given an invitation to join a young upstart federation called EWA, and having nothing better to do with his life, he joined up. Many people thought Timo wouldn't be able to hack it in the world of wrestling again after the paralyzing of his best friend. Timo blamed himself for many years, but through pressure from the very same friend, he persevered and went to EWA with high hopes. Upon arriving, he immediatley made a name for himself, standing up against Fred Ryder and working up the ladder as he did, but sadly, EWA was doomed from the start, and Timo was out of a job once again. That was when the famed PCW came calling for The Samoan, finally his dream was to be fulfilled! He joined PCW in late September, and realized soon that he had every bit as much talent as the men in the ring, and began to exploit them for it. Timo worked his way up the ladder at PCW with hard work and perseverance too, eventually leading him to side with Jeremy Howard and Asylum to win Alliances! But not everything was right in the world of PCW, Timo could see that it needed a change, too many people were in the places of power, people that did not need to be, and he joined up with a rogue group called 'The New Breed' to fight back against these political figures within PCW. New Breed was met with mixed reviews, but they did get the job done, ending Kremmens reign as CEO, injuring Ed, which Timo was never proud of, and single handedly causing the PCW world to realize, like em or hate em, they were here to kick ass. Then on one fateful day, Jason Snow, team leader and Timo's supposed friend turned his back on Timo to gain absolute power within The New Breed. Timo was devastated as the others left shortly after, leaving him high and dry. Timo went on to a few weeks after the fact, but he knew that he had screwed up his dream, he needed to prove himself as a Man again. He was sitting down to a re-run of Bonanza one day when Jane Reagan called, and the rest is history... Promo Archives Sealing the Toomes Erik Estrada is Who? Quotes
