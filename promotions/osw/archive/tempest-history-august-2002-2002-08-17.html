Tempest — history (August 2002)

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Tempest’s past, while shrouded in mystery, has recently been brought at least partly to light. Full of triumph and tragedy, Tempest has survived quite a lot to get to where she is. Born on April 1st, 1976 and raised by a loving father who was a doctor to Hong Kong's poor, she was left alone after his murder, which she witnessed. With nowhere else to turn, she sank into the crime ridden streets of the city and found her childhood martial arts and gymnastics training made her viable as a street fighter. In the seedy clubs and opium dens she fought for years, barely surviving, but honing her craft, and always under the watchful eye of Triad leaders. But she always dreamed of and watched for a way out. While she knew no legitimate master would accept her with her history, she stumbled across professional wrestling. She immediately became a fan of the bright lights and the flamboyant performers, and she knew that no one in that environment would ever find out about her past. She managed to secure a contract in a small indy fed and came to the United States almost by accident. For two years she worked her way through the smaller feds until landing in a couple of high profile tournaments which she won. The victories gave her much needed exposure and thus she was signed by the fWo. Her success there as a two time Cruiserweight champion, and the longest reigning Cruiserweight champion in their history, has only been marred by attempts to use her past to blackmail her. After she was exposed to the public as a pit fighter, and realized that the harsh culture that would have ostracized her like a common thief or prostitute in China didn't exist in the States, she was able to overcome it. Though she was raised in Hong Kong she is clearly of Anglo descent as well. She speaks fluent Chinese, likes hot Szechuan, and works chopsticks with no trouble at all. Her personality is light, glowing and happy, in spite of the tragedy of her past. For her, endless optimism was the only choice to keep facing each day and maintain hope. She's playful and great fun to be around even though she can be quirky and unpredictable. If she is hurt emotionally or just feeling down, she is likely to snap out of it with relative ease. She is the kind of person who asks unexpected and uncomfortable questions. She gets mad, but rarely enraged, and although she generally likes people, she can be a formidable enemy if someone chooses to make one of her. She’s honest and a fair fighter until driven to extremes by cheating or non-competitive assaults. But there are exceptions, most notably her friends. Anyone she truly cares about, she will help or defend, sometimes even illegally. She’s hyper to the point of sleeping only about 4 hours a night and if she’s sitting still, you know there’s something wrong with her. An incorrigible practical joker, she’ll make fun of anyone including herself if it gets a good laugh. She’s like a whirlwind and full of surprises, so you can never tell just what she’ll do next. In the ring she relies on speed and cunning to overcome superior strength and some larger opponents have said that getting hit with those tiny feet is like being shot. But her driving is considered her most dangerous skill. She has an affinity for all things electronic, whether its techno dance music (she loves to dance), odd LCD or neon lighting, computers, games, etc. She's a gifted hacker, and can do almost anything with computer hardware or software. She doesn't like complete darkness, and is likely to have some unusual light source going at all times. Nor does she like complete silence. She has a recurring nightmare in which she is flying through the air as a trapeze artist, she leaps and there is supposed to be someone to catch her, but there isn't and she falls. Ironically, she hates violent storms, especially when the power fails and will become frightened. She also hates hospitals. Her constant companion is Trouble, a young Border Collie, whose herding instincts are probably needed to keep her in line. She can be easily picked out of a crowd by way of her fashion sense which is cyberpunk/rave. Her favorite color is shiny, but she also likes bright colors, preferably in latex, PVC or some other interestingly textured synthetic. She's taken to fringy things lately as well. She also likes feathers and fake fur, and beadwork as well as casual tees and cargos, which are still bright colored, shiny, or interesting in texture. She hates shoes, but wears them outside the house, the funkier the better. She has one tattoo, not usually visible is on her lower back of a single Chinese character. Her measurements are 32-26-30, prompting some to say that would never make it in wrestling, but thankfully fans have still considered her beautiful with out being built like centerfold, and opponents have found her deadly regardless of her size. And it is rumored that she can balance a marble on the end of her nose. All this was almost entirely contradictory to the man that turned out to be her unlikely companion as she entered OSW. Lending her name to the new promotion as a favor to her old friend Jane Reagan was all that Tempest really had in mind when she began appearing on PTC Malice. After exchanging words with Stanislav, somehow Reagan convinced her to take part in a Dating Game, WT style. The contestants turned out to be Tyler Nelson, Dillan Myers and Ivan Stanislav, and Tempest unwittingly picked Stanislav. Shortly after a date that will go down in history as one of the worst ever, Tempest signed for a limited contract with OSW and began appearing on Slam! She quickly gained a following among the OSW fans for her cheery, fun and fearless approach to wrestling. She tracked down Heresian Bloodlust with a camera and got a candid interview with Meanstreak. All the while she was developing a close, if challenging, friendship with Stanislav until disaster struck. In one fell swoop, Jane Reagan turned on the fans and her former friends and allowed Meanstreak to run roughshod over Tempest. Ivan had the chance to save her and refused to do so, but later lied and told Tempest that he had. With her having been injured, she was none the wiser for some time. Stanislav was now becoming an unlikely hero to the people and as Tempest sought her revenge on Meanstreak, someone was trying to reveal the truth to Tempest. To distract Tempest from seeing a tape of the real incident, Ivan kissed her and that turned out to be the beginning of a whole new chapter in both their lives. But despite their defeat of Jane Reagan and Sebastian Toomes for the right to be called the First Couple of the OSW, their time together was about to come to an end. At the Head On Collision PPV, Reno Davis played the tape of the real incident for all to see. Tempest was shocked and hurt, retreating never to be seen again. Until now...
