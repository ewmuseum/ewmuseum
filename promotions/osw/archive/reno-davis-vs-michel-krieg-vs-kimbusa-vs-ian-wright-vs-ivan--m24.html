Reno Davis vs Michel Krieg vs Kimbusa vs Ian Wright vs Ivan Stanislav (Ladder Match)

2002-05-18
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reno Davis vs Michel Krieg vs Kimbusa vs Ian Wright vs Ivan Stanislav (Ladder Match) Written: May 18, 2002 Nate: Ladies and Gentlemen, I have just received word that our next match is just about ready to go. Don’t forget folks, six men will go at it in a ladder match that will declare a number one contender for the International Title!! Steven: This match has the potential to not only be extremely entertaining, but also action packed and exciting. Robbie: Thank you for that insight, Steven. We are all much smarter thanks to your contribution…. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest is a ladder match and will be for a shot at the OSW International Title!! The first wrestler to ascend the ladder and grab the flag of the United Nations suspended by a cable will become the number one contender!! Earl pauses for dramatic effect. The fans just get angry. Earl: Introducing first, standing 6’3” tall and weighing in at 285. IAN WRIGHT!!!! “Hate to say I told you so” by the Hives plays as Ian Wright slowly walks down to the ring and slides under the ropes. Ian looks up at the contract and awaits the coming of his opponents. Nate: This is Wright’s OSW debut. Robbie: He is a gamer. He will do fine. Earl: Introducing next, standing 6’2” tall and weighing in at 500 lbs: KIMBUSA!! “Tiger River” by Thom Brennen plays over the speakers as Kimbusa walks out from the backstage area to a chorus of boos. He ignores the crowd and climbs up the steps and into the ring. Nate: I’m curious as to how a ladder is going to hold the girth of Kimbusa…. Steven: I’d like to contain his girth. The sound of Robbie audibly vomiting in the background can be clearly heard. Earl: Introducing next, standing 6’4” tall and weighing in at 280 lbs: EL DRAGO!!! El Drago rushes down to the ring and leaps to the apron. He glares at Kimbusa and Wright through his mask and then hops into the ring and bounces off the ropes. Earl: Standing at 5’6 and weighing in at 166 lbs: RENO DAVIS!!! Reno Davis heads out from the backstage area and walks down the entryway, slapping some outstretched hands of the fans as he does. Robbie: Gonna be awesome seeing Ivan Stanislav ritualistically kill Reno Davis. Look at that twerp! Earl: And finally, introducing together as per the request of Ivan Stanislav…. Nate: Give me a break…. Earl: Standing at a collective 14’1” tall and weighing in at a combined weight of 747lbs: IVAN STANISLAV AND MICHEL KRIEG!! “The Soviet National Anthem” by The Russian Red Army Choir plays over the loudspeakers as Ivan Stanislav and Michel Krieg emerge from the backstage area. Instantly, inside the ring, Reno Davis and El Drago both point at them and yell as Ivan and Krieg look at one another and smirk. The bell rings as Ivan and Krieg take their time heading to the ring. Wright, however, takes the opportunity to clothesline Davis in the back of the head just as the bell rings! Nate: Here we go!! Stanislav and Krieg burst towards the squared circle as the bell rings and chaos erupts within. Kimbusa rushes towards Drago, but Drago manages to sense him coming and rolls out of the way just as Kimbusa misses a body avalanche. Wright lifts Davis up by the hair and locks him in a hammerlock, wrenching his arm roughly. Robbie: Submission hold? What the heck?! Krieg leaps to the apron in front of Kimbusa, only to be hit in the neck with a hard throat thrust and knocked to the floor! Stanislav pays no attention and lumbers into the ring and bull rushes towards Davis!! Nate: Ivan Stanislav making his intentions clear ladies and gentlemen!! Wright tries to break the hold but isn’t quick enough as Stanislav slams his entire bulk into both Davis and Wright. Both men go sprawling across the ring as Ivan roars animalistically in the center of the ring and points at Davis! Drago walks up behind Ivan and taps him on the shoulder. Ivan turns, thoroughly confused, and looks down at Drago. Nate: Ivan and Drago having a stare down? The massive Russian puts his hand out to let Drago shake it. Drago looks down at the hand, up at Stanislav, and then slaps it away! Drago leaps and drives a knee down into the knee of Stanislav, eliciting a groan from The Russian Bear. Drago kicks Ivan in the side of the head as he doubles over, but it only manages to enrage the Soviet. Ivan grabs Drago by the sides of the head with both hands, lifts him off the ground, and chucks him across the ring before turning his attention back to Davis. Nate: What power. Kimbusa turns around just as El Drago flies into the turnbuckle. The enormous Sumo walks over to the corner and chops Drago HARD in the chest, knocking him to the mat. Michel Krieg grabs Drago’s foot and pulls him out of the ring! Nate: It just sucks to be El Drago, I suppose. Steven: I would suck if I was with El Drago. Ian picks up Davis as the shadow of Ivan Stanislav falls over him. He yelps and pushes Davis towards Ivan and bails out of the ring. Ivan grabs Davis by the throat and lifts him effortlessly into the air and starts to choke him with one outstretched arm! Steven: Ten dollars says if he has the chance, he’ll maim him. Robbie: Twenty bucks says if he has the chance, he’ll kill him! Nate: Thirty bucks says Ian’ll break it up! Sure enough, Ian Wright slides into the ring with a steel folding chair and slams it over the back of Ivan, prompting him to drop Reno to the mat. Nate: Cha-Ching!! Outside, Michel Krieg lifts Drago high over his head and slams him into the steel pole. Drago screams as he is thrown into the pole again and tossed to the floor. Krieg smiles sadistically and bends down to lift Drago, only to be kicked straight in the face! He reels back and accidentally slams his head into the steel post behind him!! Robbie: HA HA!! THAT WAS FUNNY!! HA HA HA!!! Krieg clutches the back of his head, visibly dazed from the sudden shock to his skull. Drago leaps to his feet and sends Krieg into the guardrail and follows it with a huge dropkick to the Germans skull. Drago turns his attention away from Krieg and reaches under the skirting of the ring and drags out a ladder…. Nate: Here we go! Not it gets down and dirty!! Steven: I like that!! Inside the ring, Stanislav turns around to club Wright into the mat, but before he can, Reno Davis kicks Stanislav hard in the back of the knee, dropping him to a knee. Wright SLAMS the chair over the skull of Stanislav, sending the seat of the chair shooting out of the chair and leaving the frame of the chair wrapped around his massive neck! Wright prepares to knee lift Stanislav, but Kimbusa is quick to end that attempt with a huge lariat that sends Wright spinning in mid air and landing hard on his side!! Nate: THUNDEROUS move by Kimbusa!! Davis grabs the legs of the chair and yanks backwards with his foot planted on the upper back of Stanislav and chokes him with the chair. Stanislav reaches back wildly, trying to grab at Davis, but he’s too quick and dodges out of the way and pulls harder as Stanislav’s face turns as red as his suspenders. Kimbusa lifts Wright up and locks up with him, easily beating the smaller man and head butts him in the forehead. Wright staggers back and grabs his forehead. Kimbusa bursts forward with a tremendous amount of speed, only to fall for a drop toe hold by Wright which sends the enormous man to the mat. Wright locks an ankle lock on Kimbusa, eliciting a roar from the sumo. Nate: Good move by Ian Wright! No one really has a chance to get at the flag yet, so he’s wearing down what may be one of the few weak points of Kimbusa!! Drago turns with the ladder and swings it hard, clipping Krieg in the bottom of the chin. Krieg grabs at his chin and howls in pain as blood sprays from his chin and he falls to the floor. Drago slides the ladder into the ring and hops to the apron. He notices Davis and Stanislav and springboards off the top rope, promptly kicking Stanislav hard in the side of the head! Robbie: GET UP IVAN!! C’MON!! Ivan groans and falls to the side, actually tearing the chair from around his neck and leaving Davis with the shredded remains. Davis and Drago both walk over to Stanislav and lift him up. They kick The Russian Bear in the gut and go for a double DDT. They yank hard, but Stanislav doesn’t move. They try again, but the Russian Bear holds fast. They look at one another just as Ivan wraps his arms around both men and flips them up over his shoulders and onto their backs!! Steven: GREAT reversal by the Russian Bear! Kimbusa growls and kicks back hard, sending Wright to his rear end and breaking the hold. Kimbusa gets to his feet and stomps hi foot on the ground to knock the pain out of his ankle. Krieg climbs onto the apron and into the ring. Nate: All the competitors are in the ring, including the ladder!! Krieg grabs the ladder and rushes towards Wright and swings, Wright manages to dive to the side and nails Krieg in the back with a forearm. Wright grabs the ladder and the two wrestle for it in the corner. Stanislav shakes the cobwebs from his head and turns to find Davis on his knees. Stanislav brings his hands high up in a double axe handle and brings them down to break his skull, only for Davis to roll between Ivan’s legs! Ivan staggers into the corner from the miss. Davis dropkicks Stanislav hard in the back, staggering him out of the corner and into a low blow! Ivan falls to his knees and falls face first into the middle turnbuckle!! Nate: The Great Equalizer, nothing you can do to defend it! As Wright and Krieg fight for the ladder in the corner, Kimbusa rushes forward and sandwiches both men in the corner! Krieg and Wright collapse in the corner, crushed by the bulk of Kimbusa. Kimbusa grabs the ladder and sets it up in the center of the ring. He then lifts up El Drago by the head and military presses him up over his head, before dumping him out of the ring to the floor below! Davis turns and bounces off the ropes and hits Kimbusa with a flying shoulder block, but Kimbusa doesn’t move. Davis tries again, only for it to fail again. Davis bounces off one more time, but it chopped down by Kimbusa. Nate: Kimbusa is regulating in the ring folks!! Kimbusa reaches down and picks Davis up and tosses him into the ladder! The ladder teeters to the side and falls sideways to the mat, clipping Wright’s shoulder as it does so! Ian Wright howls in pain and falls to his knees, giving Krieg enough time to grab him in a waist lock and bulldog him to the mat. Stanislav shoots to his feet and pushes Kimbusa! Kimbusa turns and eyes up The Russian Bear…. Nate: Doesn’t look like Ivan wants anyone to fight Davis but himself! Steven: Looks like a stare down in the center of the ring!! Stanislav and Kimbusa jack jaw one another. Ivan’s thick, ruff voice is easily picked up by the camera’s as the two argue louder and louder. Robbie: I never thought I’d say this, but between the two, Ivan gives up one hundred pounds! Kimbusa punches Ivan, Ivan punches back. Both men exchange blows methodically as the entire ring begins to shake in the rhythm of the blows being traded. The crowd cheers louder as the tension builds between two of the largest wrestlers in the OSW and they hammer on one another, neither giving up any ground. Stanislav pokes Kimbusa in the eye suddenly, stopping the melee. Stanislav grabs Kimbusa to military press him!! Nate: Ivan is going to try to military press Kimbusa!! Stanislav growls loudly and roars so loud it drowns out the crowd. His back suspender snaps as his muscles tense and he lifts Kimbusa off the ground and to his shoulders. Ivan shakes as he begins to slowly straighten his arms and successfully picks Kimbusa up over his head!! Steven: THE POWER OF IVAN STANISLAV!! Robbie: Why is he doing that? He’s wearing himself out!!! As if on cue, Davis grabs the ladder and swings it, nailing Stanislav in the gut! Ivan roars and drops Kimbusa, who falls directly onto The Russian Bears shoulders and sends him crumbling to the mat!! Nate/Steven/Robbie: OWWWWW!!! Drago slides into the ring behind Wright and spins him around. The two lock up, but Wright gains the upper hand and arm drags Drago to the mat and follows up with a leaping elbow drop. Krieg rushes towards Davis, but Davis wheels around and hits Krieg in the knee, knocking him over. Kimbusa gathers himself and begins to stand, only to get jabbed in the side by the legs of the ladder. Davis sets the ladder up and begins to climb it slowly! Nate: Davis is climbing the ladder!! Bodies are everywhere!! Stanislav stirs, favoring his back as he gets to a knee. Drago rushes to grab Davis by the leg, but Krieg grabs Drago in route and back body drops him. Davis gets to the second to the top step but still can’t reach, he’s just too short. He gets to the top of the ladder and touches the flag with his fingers, until the ladder seems to float higher into the air, lifting him actually higher than the flag!! Nate: Uh oh!! Stanislav grabs the bottom of the ladder and shuts it, and hoist the ladder, with Davis on top of it, up into the air and THROWS the entire ladder out of the ring! Davis flies from the ladder and literally lands halfway up the entryway!! Steven: OH MY GOD!! RENO DAVIS IS DEAD!!! Kimbusa comes from behind Ivan and grabs him by the waist of his pants and pushes Ivan to the ropes and tosses Ivan out of the ring. Stanislav lands on his right shoulder and growls in pain. Kimbusa turns to Krieg, only to get a hard elbow to the side of the skull for his trouble. Nate: Hey, where is Ian? From the blind side of the ring, another ladder is pulled out from under the ring and Ian Wright slides into the ring. He stands on the second turnbuckle just as Kimbusa hip tosses Krieg in the center of the ring. Wright positions the ladder in front of him, hops on, and rides it down as it CRASHES over the leg of Krieg!! Robbie: That’s gonna leave a mark!! Krieg screams and grabs at his right knee as Wright bounces off and clutches his wrist, damaging it in the fall. Drago and Kimbusa brawl in the corner. Kimbusa shoves Drago to the mat and goes to splash him!! Nate: This will KILL Drago!! Drago is unable to move out of the way, but at the last moment another body flies into the picture and missile dropkicks Kimbusa backwards and through the middle and top rope to the floor! Nate: KID WONDER!! Kid Wonder lands as the crowd cheers loudly. He turns and nods to Drago! Nate: KID WONDER IS HERE!! HE’S HERE TO TAKE OUT KIMBUSA!! Wonder leaps over the top rope and lands on top of Kimbusa as the two begin to brawl on the floor. Stanislav has now recovered as has Davis. The Russian Bear runs up the apron in a blind rage and slams his shoulder into Davis, lifting him off the ground and literally carrying him to a wall in the entryway, Stanislav SLAMS into the wall as both men break through and off camera!! Steven: Hmm…well..there goes two of ‘em! Robbie: Yeah, as well as a wall!! Nate: Folks, the match is beginning to fall apart! Stanislav and Davis are somewhere backstage! Kid Wonder is slugging it out with Kimbusa! That leaves only three men in the ring!! Drago and Wright lift up Krieg and double suplex him. They look at one another and then lock up themselves! Drago sends Wright into the ropes and hits him with a drop toe hold! Wright falls headfirst into the ladder with a CRASH and screams in pain. Kimbusa sends Kid Wonder into the guardrail and nails him with a body avalanche on the outside. From the backstage area, Jesus St. Crux emerges and points down to the ring at Ian Wright, who is recovering from the blow to his head! Nate: Jesus St. Crux is here?! He’s pointing at Ian Wright!! Drago lifts Krieg up and sends him to the ropes. Krieg reverses the Irish Whip and sends Drago to the ropes instead and hits him with a Big Boot to the face, dropping him. Krieg grabs the ladder and sets it up. Wright, meanwhile, is preoccupied with the presence of Jesus St. Crux and gets out of the ring and rushes towards him. Both men start to brawl on the entryway! Steven: Pandemonium!! That’s what it is!! Kimbusa throws Wonder towards the steel post, but Wonder reverses and sends Kimbusa into it instead, busting his forehead open and dropping him like a big, 500 pound bag of rice…. Nate: Matchwriter, that’s a lotta rice! Robbie: What? Nate: Uh..nevermind. Robbie: Who’s the matchwriter? Nate: Just forget about it Robbie, you wouldn’t understand. Krieg lifts Drago and the two lock up. Drago hits a jawbreaker on Krieg, sending more blood flowing from his chin. Krieg growls and throws a wide right, but Drago reverses it into an armbreaker! Krieg’s arm is sent to the mat and wrenched, eliciting a loud roar from the East German. Nate: Speedy Riggs? Referee Speedy Riggs emerges from the backstage area, careful to move out of the way of St. Crux, who is now slamming Wright’s head off of the ground, and heads down to the ring and slides in. Nate: What is Speedy Riggs doing here? We don’t need a referee for a ladder match. Robbie: I guess we do! Why else would he be here? Steven: I smell a rat… Drago looks to Riggs, who merely nods to him and points at his red and yellow referee stripes. Drago ignore Riggs and turns to Krieg. Suddenly, Riggs pulls out a club from his back pocket and raises it over his head awkwardly and rushes forward, breaking it over the head of Drago!! Nate: I KNEW IT!! Drago falls to the mat as Riggs grabs his one hand, hurting it from the blow! Robbie: Riggs is such a wuss… Krieg gets to his feet and starts to climb the ladder. Krieg groggily starts to climb the ladder as Riggs begs him to hurry up, lest Drago wake up! St. Crux, on the entryway, is kicked in the head by Wright and knocked to the ground. Wright sits on top of St. Crux and pounds away at his forehead with lefts and rights, ignoring the going-ons inside the ring. Nate: THIS IS TOTALLY OUT OF CONTROL!!! Wonder slams a set of steel steps over the forehead of Kimbusa and stomps on them, ensuring the Kimbusa will not be moving. Krieg, meanwhile, gets to the top of the ladder and reaches, grabbing for the flag. Drago rolls over and kicks the leg of the ladder, but just as it’s about to teeter away, Riggs grabs the ladder and steadies it just as Krieg wraps the flag around his hand and pulls! Drago kicks the ladder again and it falls, but by now Krieg has the flag and falls from the ladder, flag in hand!! Winner and number one contender for the International Title: Michel Krieg!!
