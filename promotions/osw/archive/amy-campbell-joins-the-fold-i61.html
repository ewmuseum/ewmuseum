Amy Campbell Joins the Fold

2002-06-15
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Amy Campbell Joins the Fold Written: 6-15-02 “What You Say” by Skrape hits the PA, and the Jumbotron shows grainy images of schoolchildren in uniforms, most likely British from the looks of their horrid teeth, filing in and out of classrooms, with images of Sebastian Toomes, Civil Disobedience, and Jane Reagan in a fetching Catholic schoolgirl’s outfit intermingled in the display. Anexplosion of pyro hits, rocking the arena. Nate: Goddamnit! Robbie: CALIFORNIA, IT’S TIME FOR SCHOOL! Sebastian Toomes, Jane Reagan, and Civil Disobedience step out onto the stag. Toomes is nattily dressed in a black suit with a dark green dress shirt and a silver tie, while Civil D are in matching black suits and white shirts, very corporate-like. Jane is dressed in a tight leather mini-skirt and a tight black tank top. Nate: That whore’s gone and gotten herself some more numbskulls to follow her… Robbie: Hey, Civil D needed to do this! How would you feel if your entire career could be summed up as a joke? In my view, they made the best possible decision. Jane and company come down to the ring and climb in between the ropes. Jane snatches the mic from Earl and smiles gleefully out at the crowd. Crowd: SLUT! SLUT! SLUT! Jane: QUIET! Quiet down, everyone… No talking unless you’re called upon… Crowd: SLUT! SLUT! SLUT! Jane grins and continues. Jane: California, home to some of the stupidest people I’ve ever had the misfortune to meet, with one of the worst educational systems in the country, I present to you… THE ELITE! The New School of Old School Wrestling, these are the cream of the crop, the best and brightest stars I’ve ever had the good fortune to ever sign a contract to! The crowd roars its disapproval. Nate: She is out of her knob-bobbing mind!!! Robbie: At least she’s with Seabass, which is a step up from the scumbags she used to shack up with. Steven: IT’S RAININ’ MEN!!! Nate sighs. Back in the ring, Jane twirls her Auburn hair around her index finger, then continues. Jane: How can you deny the evidence in front of you? Sebastian Toomes, my lover, was the first EVER OSW Champion! He destroyed Timo Bolamba at Crowning Glory to become the first ever OSW Champion! He single-handedly destroyed the scourge of PCW, Ivan Stanislav! Beat him quite handily, and the truth is, if that loser Ace Hawk hadn’t cost Sebastian the title, he would still be YOUR OSW Champion! Toomes smiles and plants a kiss on her upturned face. Nate: I am going to be sick… Robbie: The sight of true love turn your stomach there Nate? Nate: True love? That WHORE wouldn’t know the meaning of the phrase, and don’t even get me started on Toomes. Steven: (still euphoric) I’d like for Toomes to get started on… Steven is abruptly silenced in mid-sentence by a teeth rattling shot to the skull, which is followed by the sound of his head hitting the announce table. Robbie: Dude!!! You just knocked Steven out… Nate: (seething) Yeah, and I only find him annoying. Since I despise you, what do you think will happen to you if you continue with your comments? Robbie: (meekly) I’ll be good. Back in the ring, Jane beams and continues. Jane: Also, we are proud to have Civil Disobedience, the first ever OSW Tag Team Champions, making the step up, taking the inititve that half the wrestling world lacks, to prove to the world why they are the best Tag Team in OSW! Trevor and Seth are talents that have been languishing ever since the Vax…a.k.a. Edward… Nate: Allegedly!! Jane: (continuing) …had taken me out of office for a short while like the coward he is, and they’re tired of it! They’re tired of being treated like a comedy act! Seth and Trevor nod and high-five each other. Jane: So, when Ed took me out of office so that he could destroy my creation, this rejection of the corruption and the morally bankrupt PCW, so that he could pursue his TRUE agenda! To bring OSW down to PCW’s level! I should have known he was merely a spy for Nelson… Why else would he have tried to kill me? So that he, along with Nelson and my disgusting husband, The Phantom Booker, could be back on top! Jane grimly looks out over the audience. Jane: Well, this bitch likes it on top! And this bitch is gonna stay on top! Sebastian nods and claps, while Civil D walk around the ring, glaring out at the booing audience. Jane: With The Elite, the best and brightest of OSW, this bitch is gonna stay on top for a long damned time! Jane walks into Toomes’ arms and they embrace briefly as Sebastian takes the mic. Toomes: Listen to yourselves! The crowd screams for his death. Crowd: YOU SUCK! YOU SUCK! YOU SUCK! Steven begins to come back to his senses. Steven: (slurred) I wish he di… Another bone shattering shot to the skull from Nate sends Steven back to la-la land. Back in the ring, Toomes is oblivious to the antics of the announce team. Toomes: Is that the extent of your verbal communication skills? ‘You Suck’? I’m ashamed to call California my home! Massive boos. Toomes: Now, The New School of OSW, The Elite, have a few matches tonight. Trevor and Seth have to put down Heresian Bloodlust and Da Westsidaz, no difficult task I assure you. And I have a tag match against Corg and Ace ‘I Got My Ass Kicked By Toomes Last Saturday Like The Bitch I Am’ Hawk… And I know all you idiot Oakland jackasses are thinking to yourselves… ‘Sebastian, whoever could be yourTag Team partner?’ Toomes pauses and appears to mull over the question. Toomes: Who…who, who, who could be the caliber of wrestler that could have the privledge of tag-teaming with the First Ever Old School Wrestling Champion tonight? Well, that’s a hard question, one that requires some serious thought?I mean, who else in OSW was in the same Primetime Wrestling Academy class as Civil D and me? Which wrestler is one of the fastest rising stars in the business? Who, back in the locker room, could be considered to be one of the best in this promotion? Who has the hearts of thousands and thousands of young fans, a role-model to some, a heart-throb to others? Who…? Guys, can ya help me out? Trevor takes the mic. Trevor: That IS a hard question, Sebastian! The crowd roars as the former fan favorite begins to speak. Trevor: I mean, who else could there be? Who else hasn’t been taken seriously? Who else deserves so much more than what they’ve been getting? Who hasn’t been getting the title shots, the fame and fortune they so richly deserve? Aside from us, I can only think of one other person! Seth steps in and takes the mic. Seth: I see your point, Trevor! I can think of only one wrestler who was IN THE SAME PWA CLASS, who like us, is the most underrated, most talented star in the back…LADIES AND GENTLEMEN… MAY I HAVE THE HONOR OF PRESENTING THE MOST TALENTED, YET MOST HELD-DOWN WRESTLER IN OSW… AMY CAMPBELL! Robbie: Held, strapped, handcuffed… The crowd pops at the mention of Amy’s name, and ‘Only The Strong’ hits the PA as Amy Campbell steps out cautiously onto the stage. Nate: WHAT THE HELL DOES JANE WANT WITH AIMZ? Robbie: Shut up! Quiet, class is in session! Aimz cautiously makes her way down to the ring, carrying a mic. Seth hands over the mic to Toomes, who takes it and regards the red-head. Toomes: Thank you for coming down to the ring, Amy… Now, I’ve noticed something about you, Amy… Despite our history, you’re a good Superstar, yet oddly enough, every time, you’re just put on the arm of whoever. For instance, when you first arrived here in OSW, you were on the arm of that coconut pickin’, Samoan, Timo Bolamba, and now that midget, Reno Davis. Now I don’t care what you do on your off-time, that’s nobody’s business, except… Except you haven’t had a real shot at anything since you’ve been here, haven’t you? Amy frowns and speaks. Aimz: Maybe I haven’t, Toomes, maybe I haven’t. What’s it to you, anyway? Toomes grins. Toomes: Call it classmate solidarity, Amy. Call it loyalty to the girl I spent my PWA training with back in January, and it pains me to see someone who I secretly admire suffering from the oppression of Ed’s reign of terror. How else do you explain the crap that’s been going on here lately? I mean, come on, Amy? You wanna be like every other female wrestler out there, just ‘there’ for the young guys to masturbate to? You wanna be just a curiosity, or a jizz stained picture on a calender? Just another set of tits? Or do you want a serious career, a career that you deserve? Do you wanna reach for the gold, Amy? Aimz continues to frown, thinking it over. Nate: Since when does Toomes care about women’s rights? What the hell happened to them being “wastes of paychecks?” Amy: Again, I havta ask, what’s in it for you, Sebastian? How does the high and mighty OSW Champion play into all this altruism? Robbie: Did Aimz just say altruism? Nate: Yes? Robbie: So someone is putting words in her mouth now, instead of… Nate: (interrupting) One more word and you go night-night… Back in the ring, Jane’s face flushes to anger. She then snatches the mic away from Toomes. Jane: Not so much Sebastian as it is me, Amy. I don’t like you, we all know that. But I recognize talent when I see it, otherwise you wouldn’t be in this federation– you’d be languishing in PCW, playing the T&A role… No self-respecting woman would allow herself to be put in that place, and I happen to think you’ve got enough sense in your head to rise above the rest and do something with your career! So what’s it gonna be, Amy? You gonna be like the other women, being put in your place, or are you gonna rise? Are you gonna be where you belong? In The Elite? Nate: Don’t do it Aimz!!! Don’t do it!!! The crowd roars for Amy to reject the offer, but Amy slowly turns around, thinking.After a moment, she speaks. Amy: So, what exactly are you offering me? What can you give me what I can’t do myself? Janes miles. Jane: I’m giving you the chance to compete for gold, Amy. More than just the Cruiserweight… Tag Team titles… International title… Maybe…maybe even the World Title if you’ve got the guts to go for it. And don’t tell me titles don’t mean a thing to you, Amy, because that’s just an excuse. You wouldn’t be in the business if you were just here to just hang around. You want to win, just like anyone else, don’t you? Amy bites her lip, hesitating. She then nods. Jane: Then what are you waiting for, Amy? Take the chance, seize the moment. A chance like this will never come your way again, honey… Be more than just T&A, be a STAR, Amy… Be the star you know you’re meant to be! Amy looks up at Jane and gets close to her, her eyes locked with Jane’s, her mouth set in a hard line. Robbie: I like where this is going…Cue the cheesy porn music. Nate: Shhhhhh…. Aimz looks at Jane, then out at the booing crowd, then finally nods in agreement. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOO!!! DON’T DO IT!!! Jane turns away with a grin on her face, but Aimz pulls her back by the shoulder. Amy: But I swear to you, Jane, if I get screwed over, if you turn on me, there’s gonna be hell to pay… And Aimz sticks out her hand. Jane looks Aimz up and down, and shakes her hand. The crowd’s roars of hatred and disgust fill the arena. Nate: AIMZ HAS JOINED THE ELITE! Robbie: About time she get some sense about her… Steven: (slurred, groggily) It’s not what you know, but who you know… Nate: I CANNOT BELIEVE JANE HAS TEAMED WITH THE WHORE!!! Robbie: Hey, this is pro-wrestling. You should be used to it. Nate: I am absolutely disgusted as are these fans!!! We need to head to a commercial!!! We’ll be right back!!! The scene shifts to the ring, where Aimz bites her lip, obviously still unsure of her decision to make a deal with the she-devil. Has she signed her soul away?
