Inception

2002-05-29
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Inception Written: 29/05/2002 Nate: Now folks we are just about ready to head to the ring for our… Nate is cut off suddenly and the fans turn to the stage in confusion as the lights dim and the opening notes of “Paradise City” by Guns-N-Roses start to play. Nate: What the hell?!?! A small portion of the crowd reacts to the music as it plays out, while most are confused…until a dark figure steps out from behind the curtain. The fans respond with cheers as none other than-… Nate: ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson!!! He’s the guy that assaulted Twisted Metal, Corg and then The Cult at Ascension!!! Robbie: DAMN HIM!!! HE WILL PAY FOR LOSING HESSIAN AND VAMPIR THE SHOT AT THE TITLE!!!!!!!!! Steven: He’s kinda cute… Robbie: Steven what have I told you about the new guys? Steven sighs, while the crowd react as ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson struts around on the stage, absorbing the cheers from the fans that saw him take out The Cult at Ascension, yes, Gibson, one of the most hated heels in both of his previous federations, is receiving a warm reaction from the OSW fans, most likely due to his assaults on Hessian and Vampir at Ascension. Robbie: Yeah, they cheer for him now! They’ll be crying once he meets Hess and Vamps face-to-face!!! Nate: Oh Robbie let it go, just enjoy the atmosphere! Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, making his way to the ring at this time, ‘OLD SCHOOL’ DAVE GIBSON!!!!!!!!! Holding up a steel chair, Gibson continues to the ring wearing a familiar T-shirt, with the slogan “Shut Up And Wrestle” as found at the seen of previous attacks on both Twisted Metal and Corg. The fans cheer for both Gibson and his steel chair as ‘Old School’ scales the steel steps, clanging the chair off of them, and steps into the ring. The fans familiar with the cocky young North Carolinian are surprised at what they see. Nate: Well obviously Gibson must be out here to explain his actions this previous Sunday!!! Robbie: He’d better… Steven: Wait did I say bugger during the Tag Title match?!?!? Gone is the contemptible sneer so common on Dave Gibson’s face; absent are the looks of loathing to the ringside fans. As he takes the microphone from Earl Heinrich, the fans see that instead, Dave Gibson is actually...smiling. Gibson takes the mic and steps to center ring. Robbie: This better be good… Gibson: For those of you that don’t know me... my name is Dave Gibson, and I am Old School! The fans pop, while Gibson nods his head in acknowledgement, before continuing. Gibson: (continuing) You know, even though I’ve just been in this business for a little over a year, this is already my third federation. I’ve competed in IOW, and I’ve competed in jOlt... and I had plenty of success in both. But you know what? In spite of the titles, the victories, I was never happy. I never felt comfortable. But here, in Old School Wrestling, Dave Gibson finally feels... at home. That’s why earlier today, I met with the owner Jane Reagan and I signed a contract to compete right here, in Old School Wrestling! Nate: ITS OFFICIAL!!! DAVE GIBSON IS UNDER CONTRACT WITH OSW!!! Robbie: (imitating Nate’s tone and excitement level mockingly) ITS OFFICIAL!!! THIS COMMENTATOR COULDN’T CARE LESS!!! Gibson nods, looking around the crowd of cheering fans as they react thusly to his announcement. Gibson: So right now, I’ve got a message to everybody in this federation. This place may call itself Old School Wrestling, but it seems like most of the guys in the back don’t know the first thing about the old school! You want to know what the old school is? It’s WRESTLERS! Real athletes! It’s guys here in Old School Wrestling that bust their asses night in and night out: Guys like Doug Troy and Clay Vendetta! You want to know what it’s not? It’s NOT putting a couple of damn robots in the tag division! It’s NOT some little death-metal would-be rapist masquerading as a wrestler. And it’s DAMN SURE NOT a couple of lummoxes with pituitary problems waddling about the ring, wearing Halloween costumes 12 months of the year, and talking like an extra from a freakin’ Hellraiser movie! Hessian and Vampir: not only are you guys not old school... you make me sick! And if you don’t like it... if you won’t shut up and wrestle… Gibson raises his Steel Chair defiantly above his head, which the fans react to of course… Gibson: …then I’ll MAKE you shut up! Nate: Strong words here from Gibson, he isn’t wasting any time in addressing his problem here! Robbie: Yeah well he bette-… Robbie is cut off suddenly as “Forever Hate You” by Deicide rocks the arena, and the lights turn to blackness, only the strobe lighting on the Jumbotron giving any means of light to the Reunion Arena. Rather than the blazing inferno as made famous by Heresian Bloodlust, The Cult emerges in a cloud of black mist rising up from the pyrotechnics below the stage. Nate: Uh oh…. Robbie: Ratings on the Riiiiiise!!!! Hessian and Vampir Nosferatu stop abruptly on the stage, Hessian removing a microphone from his belt and places it before his stitched up face, as “Forever Hate You” cuts, the lights come back, and The Cult become visible as the last of the black mist disappears from the hell mouth…or entrance. Hessian: ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson…what a pretty little name for such a weak little creature. You do realize upon doing what you did last Sunday, The Cult has no choice but to do away with you and send you back from whence you came…when Heresian Bloodlust was destroyed at Ascension, when it imploded and Da Westsidaz capitalized on Cain’s mistake, well that left the doorway open for Vampir Nosferatu and I to form what is what Heresian Bloodlust was…without the mistakes, without the failure…The Cult is the perfect killing machine, and now ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson, now you’ve made the vital error of disrupting the peace of that killing machine, and now…now we shall get ‘Old School’ on your little carcass… Hessian lowers the microphone and stares at Gibson with those burning red embers of eyes of his. The crowd doesn’t really know how to react to The Cult, but choose to boo them anyway. Hessian hands the microphone to Vampir Nosferatu, who stares around the arena, the crowd shivering in fear as he does so. Vampir: The sun has set fast on your time here young one…the creatures of the darkness you hate so will devour your soul, though certainly not before you have succumb to the almight of what has come to be known as the Apocalyptic Darkness………The Cult… Nate: Well Vampir certainly put Gibson in his place there, and Dave is looking rather mad with the two Heretics… Robbie: This is gonna get good…I know it… Again, Vampir hands the microphone to Hessian, and the two Heretics take a couple of steps forward, while in the ring Gibson holds his chair steadily, obviously frustrated with The Cult. Hessian: Dave Gibson will be another victim at the hands of The Cult, as all were to Heresian Bloodlust. Though you stand in that ring Gibson, you stand mouthing off that you are old school and you are here to defend the ways of old school wrestling…why is it that in my vast knowledge of wrestling, I know, and can execute 60 wrestling moves dear to my heart, whereas you…quite frankly haven’t shown us any sign at all you are all for old school rather than wave your Steel Chair around… Nate: Uh oh…I think Hessian hit a nerve there, Gibson looks a little more than pissed off!!! Robbie: Yeah, so he should, nobody prevents the Hessian from getting what he wants by gum! Steven: I like atmosphere… Gibson parades around the ring with his chair in hand, hitting it off the ropes, then stops dead center and points the chair at The Cult, while holding the mic back up to his lips. Gibson: So, let me get this straight. You claim to be some invulnerable warrior from the dark side, an unstoppable evil beast... and the best thing you can think of is to insult my chair? Is that the best your mighty dark powers can come up with? You know, Hessy... Robbie: Hessy? Steven: That’s what I call him… Gibson: There was a time when guys didn't have to sit around thinking up names for every one of their moves. The wrestling itself was enough! See, unlike you, I don't have to think up names for every move I can do. Unlike you, I don't have to come out here dressed like a cross between Darth Vader, Skeletor and one of the Village People to get a reaction from these fans. You need to be re-educated in what old school wrestling is really all about. And I'm just the guy to do it. You want to say I'm just another victim? Let's see what you can do, pal. No barbed wire gloves, no tricks, no purple midgets running around biting people in the testicles. You may scare some people around here, but you don't... scare... me. So are you going to stand there, and bore us with more of the same... damn... stupid... statements? Or are you going to get in this ring, shut up... and WRESTLE. Gibson awaits a reply, and he gets it… Hessian and Vampir Nosferatu begin walking down to the ring, while Hessian takes the mic. Nate: We may need security out here!!! Hessian: Shut up and wrestle? So be it…next week The Cult, you and the partner of your choice…until then, it’s time for you to shut up….THEN DIE!!! Hessian drops the mic, the static screeching through the arena, and The Cult hit the ring. Nate: THE CULT ARE GOING AFTER DAVE GIBSON!!! RIGHT HERE!!!! RIGHT NOW!!! In the ring, Dave Gibson backs up as Vampir and Hessian climb over the top rope, then charges, smacking first Vampir Nosferatu in the gut with the chair, and then bringing it across the face of Hessian, sending the Goths stumbling backwards. Before Gibson can pull back again, Vampir runs at him, but Gibson falls back, bringing the chair over Vampir’s head as he falls. Hessian meanwhile bounces back off the ropes and runs at Gibson, dropping a large elbow on the chair, which smacks down against Gibson’s chest. Nate: SOMEONE CALL SECURITY!!! THOSE TWO MANIACS KNOW NO HONOR!!!! Robbie: Yes…kill, my pretties, kill! Hessian and Vampir power out of the stinging chair shots and pummel Gibson, forcing him into the corner. Defending himself with the chair, Gibson tries to kick them off as Hessian’s gauntlets dent the chair closer to Gibson’s face. Gibson powers out of the two pummeling Heretics and rolls out of the way as Hessian and Vampir make their way back to their feet. Nate: GIBSON IS FIGHTING VALIANTLY, BUT HIS LUCK MIGHT HAVE JUST RAN OUT!!! Robbie: His luck ran out when he hit Vamps and Hess with that chair at Ascension! Gibson dives at Vampir and Hessian, managing to send them both tumbling with a Diving Shoulder Block. Vampir and Hessian try to make it to their feet but find themselves driven into the mat with a double Bulldog from ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson. The crowd explodes. Nate: By god, Gibson is taking The Cult to School! Robbie: He’ll pay… Steven: Doesn’t look like it now! Vampir and Hessian roll around in the ring, while Gibson grabs his chair again, though as he swings it at Hessian, he is met with a big boot, sending the chair back into his face. The Crowd: Ooooooooooo!!! Gibson falls back into the corner as Vampir Nosferatu and Hessian make their way back to their feet again. Nate Robbie: They just want to beat Gibson with their bare hands, there’s no need for a brawl! The Cult pulls Gibson up to his feet, tossing the chair away, and whips him off the ropes. As Gibson rebounds, both Vampir and Hessian grab him around the throat and hoist him up. Moving towards the announce table side of the ring, The Cult sizes the distance up and pull Gibson up that little bit further, when suddenly the crowd burst out in cheers. Steven: LOOOOOOOOK!!! The camera shifts to the top of the ramp as from behind the curtain emerges ‘Classic’ Clay Vendetta, running to the ring carrying a Steel Chair of his own. Robbie: SOMEONE GRAB ME THE COCKTAIL SAUCE BECAUSE HERE COMES THE SHRIMP!!! Nate: Ya know…(suddenly begins to laugh) Nevermind… Robbie: Hah!!! Vendetta slides into the ring in time for The Cult to spot him and escape. As they back off up the ramp exchanging expletives with Vendetta, ‘Classic’ Clay takes a microphone from ringside. Vendetta: You told ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson to find a partner for next week…Well, he’s got one! Nate: WHOOOOOOOOAAAA!!! NEXT WEEK ON SLAM IT WILL BE CLAY VENDETTA AND DAVE GIBSON AGAINST THE CULT!!! The crowd erupts into a cheer of approval. Vendetta helps Gibson to his feet, then turns back to The Cult. Vendetta: And next week, we’ll beat you so bad…it’ll be a cult classic!!! With that “Carry On My Wayward Son” from Kansas hits the PA system, as Vendetta throws down the microphone and the crowd cheers. Nate: Well we got it sorted now! The Cult and Vendetta and Gibson are on a collision course for Slam! next week!!! Robbie: Two shrimps for the price of one!!! It’ll be Hessian and Vamp’s lucky night!!! Nate: We need to head to a commercial!!! We will be right back after these messages!!!
