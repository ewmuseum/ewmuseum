Corg — history (October 2002)

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History My life… if only you knew. You think your life is hard? You have no idea. I by no means am bragging, for I would trade my life in an instant. I was born in a hospital on January 28 of 1984, surrounded by a neighborhood that could’ve been called ‘Slum-Ville’. Detroit, Michigan, my hometown, the worthless city in which I grew up. But yet when I’m there, I never want to leave. I remember the very first thing I saw when I was born... I saw my older adopted brother, Rhett Locke. He looked at me, in a rather odd mannor, and he smiled. My childhood as you know it was shit. My parents, Mark and Nicole Raiyn, both died in a car accident when I was six or seven. Rhett was hating me more and more for reasons that I did not know. At the time I felt emotionless. My parents were dead, my closest family member hated me, and I was so struck in my mind I was unable to see reality’s truth. It was all foster homes from there. It sucked, strict old Catholics always telling me what to do, although they seemed to praise Rhett. After a year or so of sitting in that shit-hole foster home, Rhett had managed to escape. I didn't see him ever again, that is until I came to OSW. They say the foster homes provide kids a life, even if they look hopeless. Too bad that’s just another lie. My foster home in Southfield, Michigan, was the biggest heap of shit ever. They did nothing for me. And in return, I killed one of my teachers. There was nothing to it. I threw a wooden apple at the back of her head, knocked her out, and beat the shit out of her. Heh, and I was only nine years old… such a pity. I was all on my own now. I went to school, got good grades, and increased my knowledge of the world. It was then that I upbound my beliefs about everything; my beliefs and hatred for the government, the media, and capitalism. It was the smart thing to do and I don’t regret it at all. I don’t remember exactly when, but sometime in my years of being 16 or so, I began training at a kick boxing school. I trained hard, and I enjoyed it. I thought it was cool, getting hit in the face as hard as you can possibly imagine by a twenty-one year old boxing champion. One day, my talents among the school were recognized, and my appearance was well known. I was told that I was being ‘promoted’ to a higher level of training. I began training in the mystical basement of the school, or what they called ‘Realm 2’. There were no lights, only fire-lit torches and rusty floors. I trained for almost a year, learning as much as possible by my Master and trainers. They were completely amazed with my progress… I met a girl shortly after my days of Realm 2, and fell in love with her after the first few minutes of making out with her at a Nine Inch Nails concert in Detroit. Her name was Shyme Orien, my current girlfriend and manager. Not shortly after I met Shyme did I realize my illness. All of my life, I've had depression. But, the depression would soon turn to something much worse; I was bipolar. I didn't care much for my illness, as I had my manics and my depression going on and off. But now I fight in Old School Wrestling, with my rivaled brother Rhett Locke. I was trained to fight, and kill whenever, and I have done just that. I have launched a war, secretly, upon humanity, and I won’t quit until I’ve come to a conclusion. Some would wonder why I chose to pursue this lifestyle. I do not wish to do what I do, it just so happens that I am who I am, and no one will ever change that.
