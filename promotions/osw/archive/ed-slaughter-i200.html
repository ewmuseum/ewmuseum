Ed Slaughter

2002-07-13
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ed Slaughter Written: July 13th, 2002 Kriegman lets out a diabolical laugh as TCM continues to beat the fallen Ed with his trashcan, with Porter smiling widely as he looks on. Steven: WHAT THE HELL IS HAPPENING?! As TCM pulverizes Ed, Alan picks up Bob off the mat and unfolds it, setting the lethal chair down as if it were to be used as furniture, then picks up the microphone. Kriegman: Trash… Trash… Listen to me, Trash. Listen to your master. Nate: WHAT??? Steven: Krieggy could be my master…. Just like that, Trashcan Man ceases his assault and spins back toward Kriegman. It’s now quite obvious what the look on his face is as he stares at the evil supergenius. Trashcan Man is gazing at Kriegman with complete adoration. Kriegman: Come here, Trash. Come to me. TCM slowly steps over to Kriegman, puts down his can, and actually KNEELS before him, looking up at him as if he were God himself. Kriegman: Now say, Trash… Say it just like I asked… The dark man holds the mic in front of Trashcan Man’s bearded face. TCM’s mouth opens, but no words come out. Kriegman: SAY IT, TRASH. TCM (stammering): M….M…My… Kriegman: Say it! TCM: My life..for you… My life for you! MY LIFE FOR YOU!!! Nate: Oh…my…God… TCM: MY LIFE FOR YOU! MY LIFE FOR YOU! Kriegman: That’s enough, my friend. Nate: WHAT IN THE WORLD IS GOING ON?!?!?! ED’S LONGTIME STABLEMATE, TRASHCAN MAN HAS TURNED ON ED AND IS SUBSERVIANT TO THE MAN HE HATES MOST, ALAN KRIEGMAN!?!? WHAT IN THE WORLD IS GOING ON?!?! Trashcan Man moves forward on his knees and tightly hugs the legs of Alan Kriegman, who looks toward Howard and grins evilly. Porter looks on at this scene with pure satisfaction. Nate: WHAT THE HELL HAS ALAN KRIEGMAN DONE TO TRASHCAN MAN?!?! Robbie: I don’t know, but I love it! The crowd, which is already beyond furious, grows even more hostile when Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman suddenly steps out from the back, carrying a steel chair in his hands. Armour and John Mammoth walk on either side of him, each man carrying two chairs of his own. Steven: I’m scared! Hold me, Nate! Nate: NO!!! SOMEONE CALL SECURITY!!! ARMOUR, MAMMOTH AND PHD ARE ON THEIR WAY OUT HERE!!!! PHD, Armour, and Mammoth join Kriegman, Porter, and TCM in the ring. Kriegman grins as TCM remains latched onto his legs. Kriegman: Heh…OK Trash, that’s enough… It’s time. Nate: Time…? Trashcan Man loosens his hold and looks up at Kriegman with wonder. Kriegman: Just like we talked about it, Trash. It’s time. The ring has now become completely crowded. Armour and Mammoth have dropped the 4 chairs are now holding up a semi-concious Ed by the arms, while Kriegman pats TCM on the head like some sort of pet, and Porter simply stands back and watches it all happen. Kriegman: Armour, John…lead the king to his throne. The two monsters nod obediently, carry Ed over to the unfolded Bob… Nate: Oh God, NO… …and trust him down onto the barbed wire-wrapped, nail-studded old chair. The Crowd: Oooooooooooooooo!!! Ed’s head snap’s back and he scowls in agony as he is forced to sit down on the deadly weapon that once led him to the status of ‘Hardcore Legend’. Steven: YEEOOOWWW! Nate: SOMEONE STOP THIS!!! Held down by both Armour and Mammoth, Ed is soon surrounded by Porter, PHD, and Kriegman, who is being clung to by TCM. Alan moves forward and grabs Ed by his now blood-soaked hair, moving his face in close to his old rival’s. Kriegman: This is it, Ed… The game’s over. You lose. No matter what you do, you simply cannot defeat us. But you can still save yourself from complete destruction. There’s only one thing you have to do, Ed… Admit we’ve won. Admit to us, and admit to the world that we’ve beaten you…and we’ll spare you. Nate: THIS IS FUCKING DISGUSTING! SOMEONE DO SOMETHING!!!! Alan literally shoves the microphone in Ed’s face with his free hand, now scowling at the bloodied legend. Kriegman: SAY IT, ED. SAY YOU’VE FAILED. ADMIT YOUR INFERIORITY, OR FACE THE CONSEQUENCES. Struggling to remain conscious, Ed breathes heavily as he looks up at Kriegman…and spits right in his face. The crowd bursts with approval as Alan Kriegman turns away to wipe the loogie from his face, then turns back and smacks Ed hard. Steven: THIS IS PRACTICALLY RAPE!! Kriegman throws himself at Ed, knocking him backwards in the chair and landing on top of him. The evil supergenius begins to slam Ed’s head into the mat repeatedly, filled with rage. Porter and PHD nod to eacother and proceed to pull Kriegman backward, holding him back from attacking Ed any further. Porter commandeers the mic from Alan in the process. Porter: Enough! Enough! Alan, my friend… He’s made his choice. His face red with fury, Alan Kriegman bites his lower lip and nods. His two friends release him, and Kriegman continues to snarl at Ed as Porter stands over his fallen form. Porter: Edward, if you will not lower your head to us…then you leave us with no choice. An example must be made to all of OSW. You will be martyred. Robbie: SWEET!! Nate: NOOO!!! Porter: Armour, John… Proceed as planned. Nate: WHAT PLAN? The two monsters go to work. Armour takes one of the folding chair he brought in, and wraps it around Ed’s right arm…then wraps another around his left arm. Mammoth places each of Ed’s legs inside a chair…then takes the fifth chair from PHD, spreads Ed’s legs, and places the chair over his groin. Nate: Oh sweet Jesus what the hell are they gonna do? Finally, Howard Porter picks up Bob…and very carefully places Ed’s head inside the folded chair. Ed lays on the mat in a horrific manner, with a chair wrapped around each of his limbs, one over his groin, and his own weapon of choice around his neck and covering his face. Porter: Good… Assume positions, my friends. Steven: I CAN’T WATCH!!! The six members of The Think Tank each take a stance at one of the chairs… PHD stands at the right arm, Trashcan Man at the left arm (still staring adoringly at Kriegman), John Mammoth at the left leg, Armour at the right leg, Alan Kriegman at the groin…and Howard Porter standing at the head. Nate: NO! NO! NOOOOO!!!! Porter: On my mark, gentlemen. 3…2…1 The crowd screams as all six men jump simultaneously onto the chairs. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! The full weight of Armour and Mammoth comes down on the chairs wrapped around Ed’s legs, and a sickening snapping can be heard. The same can be said for TCM and PHD with the arms. Kriegman stomps extra hard with both feet on the chair over Ed’s groin, and we can leave that feeling to the imagination. Finally, and most horribly, comes Howard Porter, who lands with both feet on Bob, crushing Ed’s face (and neck) between the nails and barbed wire of the chair. Ed’s back arches, and we can hear what could possibly be a howl of agony, and then the Hardcore legend goes limp. Nate: I…I..I don’t know what I could possibly say to do this justice… Robbie: How about…WOOOOOHOOOOO!!!! Nate: You sicken me almost as much as they do. The chairs are removed from Ed’s limbs, revealing hideous-looking bumps which could only represent fractures. Steven: SOMEBODY STOP THIS!!! Nate: THERE IS NO ONE TO STOP IT!!! TOOMES NEVER MADE IT OUT OF THE HOSPITAL!! IVAN WAS CARTED OUT BY SECURITY!!! VENDETTA, AXTELL AND TRUXELL LEFT TO RESCUE NIKI!!! ST. CRUX IS AT THE HOSPITAL!!! LOCKE LEFT THE BUILDING EARLIER!!!! Steven: THERE HAS TO BE SOMEONE WHO CAN STOP THIS!!! THERE JUST HAS TO BE SOMEONE WHO CAN STOP THE MIGHTY VAX!!! Nate: THIS IS AN OUTRAGE!!!! WE ARE GOING TO LOSE OUR FEED, WITH THIS IMAGE BEING THE LAST THING LEFT IN OUR FINDS MINDS ON AN OTHERWISE SPECTACULAR NIGHT!!! The chair from his groin is removed (again, the results are left to the imagination), and finally, Howard Porter removed Bob from Ed’s head. Whatever remains of Ed’s face is being digitally blurred by the cable provider, because the unrecognizable mask of ripped flesh and gore is simply too much to be aired uncensored. Alan, Howard, and PHD all grin with pleasure at the sight. Ed is not moving. Porter (looking to Herbert, then Alan): Shall we finish the job, gentlemen? Kriegman nods vigorously, with Trashcan Man mimicking him in a way that would be humorous if not for the current circumstances. PHD shows approval as well. Porter: Very well then. Johnathan, if you will… The gargantuan John Mammoth shoots “The Scholar” a glance that could be construed as hatred, and proceeds to pick up the completely limp Ed and sling him over his shoulder like a ragdoll. Howard exits the ring, followed by Kriegman, PHD, Trashcan Man, and Armour. Following behind the new super power is John Mammoth, carrying the unresponsive Ed out with him. All six men walk up the ramp together, led by Porter. Nate: WHERE THE HELL IS SECURITY?! WHAT THE HELL ARE THEY GONNA DO NEXT?! THIS MUST BE STOPPED!!! Debris of all sorts is hurled as the group reaches the top of the ramp, when Mammoth finally tosses Ed to the hard ground like a lifeless sack. Porter: Edward, you DO know what goes with being a martyr, I hope. Bearing his evilest smirk, Alan Kriegman steps forward, his black suit stained with Ed’s blood in several spots. He bends down, grabs his arch rival by the hair once again, and pulls him up. Despite all that has happened to him, Ed still somehow clings to consciousness. With Kriegman clutching him by the hair, he staggers along as the evil supergenius leads him…to the edge of the ramp, which stands 15 feet above an uncovered floor of solid concrete. A virtual army of security guards have swarmed the ringside area, keeping fans from flooding over the barriers to stop this. Steven: Tell me when it’s over, I’m covering my eyes! Alan Kriegman stands at the edge of the ramp, still tightly latched onto Ed’s blood-soaked hair, which was once a sandy blonde. Howard hands the microphone to Alan, who takes it in his free hand, and pulls Ed’s face closer to his once again. Kriegman: Ed…it’s over. We’re giving you one last chance to save yourself. Admit your defeat. Tell the world that we can’t be defeated… Kriegman points to the floor so far beneath them. Kriegman: …or take plunge, and become a martyr. Nate: DO IT, ED. JUST ADMIT THAT YOU CAN’T WIN AND SAVE YOUR GODDAMN LIFE!!! Alan holds the mic up to what remains of Ed’s face. Kriegman: Well? Ed (breathing deeply): I thought I already told you… I’d fucking die before I let you beat me… DO IT. Kriegman grits his teeth and shakes his head at Ed, and moves to throw him over the ledge… The crowd goes silent as Ed teeters over the edge of the ramp…only to be pulled back… by Trashcan Man. Nate: *GASP* Oh THANK GOD… TCM holds Ed’s bloody head in his arms, and looks up at Kriegman with the same look of worship, but is shaking his head to his new master. Alan glares angrily at TCM. Nate: TRASHCAN MAN HAS HAD ENOUGH!! HE’S TAKING A STAND AGAINST THE THINK TANK!!! WE’RE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Trashcan Man then grabs the mic from Kriegman, who stares at him, his lips curled with rage. TCM looks down over the edge, the up at Alan again. TCM: …My life for you! Trashcan Man picks up the limp Ed…setting him up for a cradle piledriver. Nate: NO! WE ARE JUST ARE LOSING OUR FEED RIGHT NOW!!!! Robbie: YES!! DO IT, TRASHY! DO IT!!!! Alan Kriegman makes direct eye contact with TCM, nods with almost fatherly approval, and steps back. Howard Porter stands with PHD and Alan Kriegman on either side of him, with Armour and John Mammoth towering behind them like mountains. Trashcan Man gives one last look to Kriegman… TCM: MY LIFE FOR YOU! MY LIFE FOR YOOOOUUUU!!! Nate: WHOOOOO IS GONNA STOP THESE MANIACS!!!!! Robbie: NNNNNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!! And with Ed locked in a cradle piledriver position, leaps off the edge of the ramp, plummeting 15 feet to the concrete below, as the © 2002 OSW logo appears on the bottom of the screen….as the scene fades to black.
