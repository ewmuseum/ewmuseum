Finale Written: June 23rd, 2002 Nate: Well Ladies and Gentlemen, Ascension has certainly lived up to its name, and events are escalating here tonight in the America West Arena, and next we have a match that is sure to go down as one of the bloodiest tonight, the tag Team Championship Match! Heresian Bloodlust, Da Westsidaz and Civil Disobedience have been at each other’s throats since Da Westsidaz made their impact here in OSW…at Head On Collision! Video footage rolls on the Jumbotron showing the events leading up to tonight’s match. Firstly, Da Westsidaz are shown assaulting Heresian Bloodlust and Civil Disobedience at Head On Collision. Then the following Slam! Heresian Bloodlust are shown interfering in the Civil Disobedience/Westsidaz match. Following that are some clips of other interferences, including Da Westsidaz framing Civil Disobedience for the locker room scandal, Heresian Bloodlust pulling the ring apart during the Civil D/Westsidaz match, and some more backstage antics. Then Civil Disobedience locking Da Westsidaz in their locker room, Da Westsidaz stealing Minion and Caleb, then all three teams falling from the rafters onto the Hell In The Cell, and some in-ring action including Hessian giving Riptah the Eternal Damnation, Da Westsidaz delivering the Westside Bustah, Civil Disobedience beating on both teams with weapons, Minion chokeslamming Short Circuit and hitting the 360º Splash on Seth Masters, then the massacre Vampir Nosferatu laid out in the ring the previous night, with all men covered in blood and thrown around the ring, and Heresian Bloodlust standing defiantly in the ring with Vampir…and finally multiple shots of each team. The video footage ends with headshots of the 6 men, staring with hatred at one another, their Weapon of Choice gleaming in their eyes. The crowd cheer wildly as the video footage ends and the cameras focus back to the announce table. Nate: Well folks what more is there to say than, Let’s get ready to rumble! All three teams have gone through hell to make it to tonight, where nothing but more Hell will be unleashed in the Weapon of Choice Match! Robbie: Yeah and we’re looking at total carnage here tonight! Da Westsidaz chose Baseball Bats, which gives them the reach and power, while Civil Disobedience have opted for Ladders, which gives them the ability for High Impact moves, lots of sickening thuds and of course more reach than a Bat, while the champions, Heresian Bloodlust, the first to choose, chose Barbed Wire Gloves, the same as they used in the Bragging Rights Match. While these gloves; Hessian’s gauntlets being wrapped in barbed wire while Cain has specially designed gloves with barbed wire, don’t have the same reach as Bats or Ladders, at close range they have a devastating effect, so basically this match can go either way! Steven: And of course you can’t count out the addition of Vampir Nosferatu, who, as you know, returned to Slam! last night and has, with Heresian Bloodlust, formed a group of sorts. In this environment it is hard to imagine that anyone could or would want to interfere…but then again Vampir is Vampir… Nate: In any case folks we’re going to get this thing kicked off now, so let’s take it to Earl Heinrich for the next match!
