Unearthing an Imposter

2002-06-15
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Unearthing an Imposter Written: June 15, 2002 The scene shifts back to the ringside announce position of the OSW announce team. Nate: Welcome backe to Slam! Ladies and Gentlemen, I have just received word that the somewhat psychotic Ivan Stanislav has demanded time to speak at this moment and is making his way to the ringside area from backstage as I speak!! Robbie: All hail Ivan Stanislav!! Steven: I've never seen a monkey get spanked like that in all my years..you gotta give the man credit, he's an innovator!! Robbie: That's….that's disgusting Steven. Nate: Regardless, Ivan Stanisla---- Nate's words are cut off suddenly as 'The Soviet National Anthem' by the Russian Red Army Choir explodes throughout the area, drowning out all sound save for the mighty fanfare of the music. Red pyro explodes from the scaffolding around the ring, raining fiery orbs to the mat which only flicker and go out. Flames explode around the entryway as the huge silhouette of a man that can only be Ivan Stanislav melds into the background. With an explosion, the flames go out as Ivan Stanislav, with Alexei Ruslan in tow, stomps down to the ring. Nate: Great intro, I gotta hand it to him! The crowd begins to boo Stanislav, who only gets angrier as he waves them off, deflecting popcorn bags and plastic cups as he marches, undeterred by the general inhospitality of the crowd. Stanislav surges up the metal steps and climbs into the ring. Alexei climbs through the middle rope and yells at a fan for a minute, before fixing his hat and standing beside his massive Comrade. Steven: To think that at one time, Ivan Stanislav was given one of the strongest ovations in OSW's young history! Robbie: To think he's one half of the 'First Couple' of the OSW is even funnier! He's no softy now!! Nate: Ivan Stanislav has been battling demons within himself, outside of himself, and within Russia ever since Tempest left him! He's driven by more than pride now! He's driven by rage, hatred, and vengefulness! Alexei grabs a microphone and hands it to Stanislav. Ivan waits for a moment as the crowd slowly dies down and the music fades away, leaving the people at the mercy of his words…. Ivan: Since I have come here, I have done nothing but fight for you people! I have done nothing but sacrifice my time and energy to better the quality of life and entertainment for the OSW people! And still, this is how you treat my selfless acts!! The crowd reacts harshly to these words and slowly builds up a chant…. Crowd: liar….liar…Liar….LIAR!! Stanislav roars while Alexei covers his ears in an attempt to block out the accursed chant… Nate: That liar chant always gets Ivan worked up…. Steven: Robbie..hold me.. I'm scared! Robbie: Back off queer! Ivan roars loudly as his face turns red and explodes into sound and emotion, very uncommon for Stanislav. Ivan: I did not lie to her!!! I did it for the betterment of us both!!! You people twist the truth and turn you back on me!!! Nate: Stanislav is unable to forget about Tempest…it's blatantly obvious… Ivan puffs out his chest and straightens his back… Ivan: You want to see what a liar is?? You want to hear of a man that is lying to us ALL?! I will tell you who this man is! I will tell you of a man in the OSW that has fooled ALL of us!! Steven: What is Ivan talking about? Ivan: Since he entered the OSW, El Drago was candidate for Red Army. But his inability to accept our cause and his lackluster attitude towards the fellow workers have transformed him from Comrade to enemy! Comrade Ruslan has already taken away his 'ears' in the form of Pablo Rucha..however…I find that El Drago, even without his friend, appears to be understanding awful lot of what goes on around him..and what is said TO him, regardless of his ignorant charade!! Nate: El Drago can't speak anything other than Spanish! That's obvious!! Stanislav is doing nothing more than directing the hostility from himself towards a scapegoat! Ivan: Comrade Alexei! If you will please!! Alexei nods and pulls a paper from the breast pocket of his trench coat and grabs the microphone. He clears his throat… Alexei: Ehem..with pleasure, Praporschik! In my hands is document with transcripts from all of what El Drago has said! The central office of the Russian Federation was more than willing to translate what El Drago has said and the results are abundantly clear: El Drago's speaking ability is nothing more than elementary at best! The crowd dies down just a bit, giving Ruslan more room to speak. Alexei: Furthermore! We have found that his biography is very sparse. Very….incomplete! His hometown? Somewhere in Mexico! Could he not tell someone his hometown? His history? A mystery! He has no story created yet that he thinks will pass off as believable! El Drago is a fake! The experts in Russia have come to unanimous decision and agree that El Drago is not Spanish..but something else! Nate: This is absurd! Stanislav and Ruslan are merely twisting the facts again! Ivan grabs the microphone and grins. Ivan: That is right! He is something else!! He is NOT Mexican! He is an American! Or of English speaking decent! While you people may shun me, I will continue to practice my selfless acts and bring justice to OSW…. Steven: Give me a break! Robbie: This is GREAT!! Ivan: ….and I demand that El Drago bring his lying face to ring and reveal himself for all to see! If not, I will personally reveal who he is!! Come out liar!! Show us who you are!!! Ivan and Alexei turn to the entryway and lean against the ropes, awaiting for El Drago. 'Unleash the Dragon' by Sisco hit's the speakers and the crowd start to cheer on. Nate: Here comes El Drago now to set things straight. Drago pops out from the back an intense stare focused solely on Ivan in the ring. He starts to make his way down but only gets a few steps before the large shadow of Michel Krieg can be seen stalking behind him and the crowds cheers turn to jeers. Steven: He's coming in the rear!!! Drago doesn't even see it coming as Krieg bashes down on him with an axe handle dropping him down to his knees. Stanislav and Ruslan laugh in the ring as Drago hits the mat and Michel Krieg begins to lay waste to him with heavy kicks to his back. Ivan: DYAAHAAHAA!! As you can see....the only truth you people need to know is that no one dares to go against Red Army! No one dares to challenge our power!! Ivan and Ruslan exit the ring and walk over to El Drago and laugh at him while Krieg continues to beat him down. Ivan: You will not be speaking anymore, little dragonfly! You are a disgrace! A liar! A fake! It sickens me, a nationalist in my own right, to see someone such as you slandering a countries name!! Krieg hauls El Drago up to his feet and the weary Mexican takes a wild swing catching the side of Krieg's head. Michel just smiles back as he clasps both hands around Drago's neck and hoists him up into the air and holds him there. Stanislav and Ruslan laugh hysterically and look at Drago. Ivan: I know you understand what I am saying little man. You cannot fool Ivan Sergeiovich. So hear my words: The enemies of one of us, are the enemies of all of us! Congratulations, El Drago. For after I destroy Reno Davis tonight, the full might of my Red Army will bear down upon you and reveal you as the fake that you are!! Krieg drops Drago to the mat and he starts to gasp for air. Krieg looks over at Stanislav and nods as he dusts off his hands and walks toward the ropes. Ivan laughs and points down at the fallen Drago. Ivan: I will spare you today, El Drago, and not rip that wretched mask off of your head. But mark my words....if you do not adhere to OUR ways and reveal yourself of your own volition, this is merely a taste of the pain you will be feeling!! DYAAHAAHAA!! Ivan and Krieg start to leave the ring as Drago wobbles to his feet. Drago: Usted me terminará ahora. Krieg and Ivan turn around a look of rage in their eyes. Drago: Termíneme ahora. Alexei turns and fishes out a pencil, scrawling something on his transcript. Alexei: I will translate this, little boy, and then we will figure out what gibberish you are saying! Alexei laughs and puts the pencil away as the three communists laugh and head back up the entryway. Drago: Communist Pussy Drago stands tall although breathing heavily... Just as Stanislav, Ruslan, and Alexei get to the entryway, Ivan lurches and stops as the three men turn as one, a sudden look of surprise on their faces. Nate: Uh oh.... Ivan tilts his head to the side as Krieg takes a step forward, but Ivan stops him. Ivan: What did you say?? Ivan blinks. Ivan: WHAT DID YOU SAY!!?! Drago doesn't back down as he holds his head high and makes a kissing motion with his lips. Drago: I say Communist pussy, cómo hágalo lo dice, ah sí, you are bitch. Ivan blinks as Drago challenges him. The crowd grows in intensity as Krieg begins to scream in German at Drago. Ivan roars and begins to head down to the ring, fuming! Nate: Stanislav and Krieg are going to take out Drago!! Ruslan, however, rushes in front of Ivan and stops him, jabbering in Russia so that no one can understand his words. Ivan stops and grinds his jaw, before glaring up at Drago. He points. Ivan: You are lucky, liar. Today, Ivan Stanislav preserves your life....next time...we will not be so thoughtful!! Ivan nods to Ruslan and then to Krieg as the three turn and walks backstage... Nate: What did Ruslan tell him? What is going on?? And what is the deal with Ivan's attack upon Drago's person? Robbie: El Drago is a dirty Mexican illegal immigrant!!! Nate: We need to head to a commercial! Hopefully, when we return, we will get this all sorted out!!!
