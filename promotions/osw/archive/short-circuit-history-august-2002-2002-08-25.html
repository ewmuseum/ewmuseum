Short Circuit — history (August 2002)

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Weighing in at 170lbs makes Short Circuit the runt of the tag team. Although runt means he’s smaller, it doesn’t mean he couldn’t hold his own against Riptah in a fight. SC is living proof to the saying, “It’s not the size of the dog in the fight, it’s the size of the fight in the dog.” He usually takes to the air or takes a beating for the team. Being more of a flashy guy, Shorty comes to the ring wearing huge baggy blue jeans, big crazy ass sunglasses (similar to Christian’s), chains, rings and a bright orange tee that reads, “CAUTION: Contents Are Extremely HOT!” Although he usually prefers to leave his hair messed up, he will wear the occasional Westsidaz backwards cap. Marcus Williams comes from a rich, snobby family. Originally from Bakersfield, his father Eric moved the family to Anaheim and founded Williams & Williams International. Short Circuit was raised as a well-mannered member of society and was spoiled to the point that he was disgusted with his own lack of authority figures. The only child in the family, it wasn’t uncommon for Shorty to get whatever he wanted, whether it was clothes, music, cars or even personal trainers. Short Circuit was trained by the best in the following fields: Wrestling, Shoot Fighting, Martial Arts and Wilderness Survival. When SC finally decided he wanted to break into the business, he was more than qualified and after pulling a few strings he immediately got thrown into a respectable Independent Promotion. But don’t let his silver-spoon attitude fool you, he is extremely popular and well-liked and for a good reason. He is very generous and is known for donating to many charitable funds.
