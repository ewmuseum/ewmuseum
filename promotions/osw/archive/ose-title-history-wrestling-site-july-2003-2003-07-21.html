OSE title history (wrestling site) (July 2003)

21 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
March 10, 2002, Crowning Glory PPV, Sebastian Toomes d. Timo Bolamba, after interference from OSW owner Jane Reagan, to win the vacant World Heavyweight Title. May 18th, Saturday Night Slam, Doug Troy d. Sebastian Toomes (c), thanks to interference from Andrew 'Ace' Hawk. June 23nd, 2002, Ascension PPV, Tom Walczak d. Doug Troy (c) July 6th, 2002, Saturday Night Slam, Rhett Locke d. Tom Walczak (c), thanks to interference from Graphic Violence. September 7, 2002, Saturday Night Slam, Howard Porter d. Rhett Locke(c), Ivan Stanislav, White Mexican, Aimz, Propaganda, Frankie Bones, Nick Casey, Mr. President, and Switchblade in the historic Jane Says Match by pinning Aimz after a Think Tank Interference sent Mexi and Ivan to the back to rescue Tempest. October 26, 2002, Saturday Night Slam, Title vacated when Howard Porter (c) loses Contract Match to Dave Hurst. October 27, 2002, Road to Ruins ppv, Ivan Stanislav d. Scott Taylor to win vacant World Heavyweight Title. April 27, 2002, Head On Collision ppv, Tyler Nelson d. Ivan Stanislav (c) in a Ladder Match. May 18, 2003, Sunday Night Slam, Boda d. Tyler Nelson (c), with Nelson retiring following the match. May 5, 2002, Saturday Night Slam, Acting CEO Howard Porter announces creation of OSW Cruiserweight Title. June 23, 2002, Ascension PPV, Corg d. Amy Campbell to win the vacant Cruiserweight Title. October 19, 2002, Saturday Night Slam, Kid Wonder d. Corg (c) to win the Cruiserweight Title. NOTE: Kid Wonder voids Cruiserweight stipulation and renames belt the Soy Boy Memorial Title. March 16, 2003, Crowning Glory 2 PPV, Tyler Nelson d. Kid Wonder (c) for Soy Boy Memorial Title. March 23, 2003, Sunday Night Slam, Nelson renames title the United States Title. May 4, 2003, Sunday Night Slam, Nelson vacates the United States Title on order of Jane Reagan. Nelson had won the World Heavyweight Title from Ivan Stanislav one week prior, May 25, 2003, Sunday Night Slam, Politically Incorrect (William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft) win the Chaos In The Cage Match and are declared co-holders of the United States Champiobship. Powell and Lovecraft made a simultaneous pin on D-Kay Calyx. Since the only rule was that the first pin or submission won the match, both were named co-titleholders. June 29, 2003, Ascension PPV, Azala Zameer defeats Politically Incorrect (c). March 10, 2002, Crowning Glory PPV, Rhett Locke d. Drew Hostyle to win the vacant International Title. May 4, 2002, Saturday Night Slam, Propaganda d. Rhett Locke (c) thanks to interference from Graphic Violence. July 27, 2002, Saturday Night Slam, 'Angeldust' Nick Casey d. Propaganda (c), Lillith and Vampir Nosferatu in a four corners match. September 7, 2002, Saturday Night Slam, Frankie Bones earns the title during the TLC Stipulation of the Jane Says Match taking the title from Nick Casey (c). January 1, 2003, Resolution PPV, Tempest d. Frankie Bones (c). March 16, 2003, Crowning Glory 2 PPV, Kimbusa d. Tempest (c) in an Octagon Match. March 23, 2003, Sunday Night Slam, International title is made defunct, and renamed as OSF World Heavyweight Title.
