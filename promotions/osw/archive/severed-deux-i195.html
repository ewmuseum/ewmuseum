Severed: Deux Written: July 6, 2002 An un-manned security camera in one of the locker-rooms... ''You just can't resist the bait, can you?'' Corg's voice had been coming from somwhere around Amy. She was sitting on the floor of an empty locker-room with headphones barring all sound from getting in. Campbell was, for once, completely obvlivious to any noises outside, or any other presence, just assuming the only person who would've known she was there would be Reno, who'd promised to return shortly with something better than the catering's cheap coffee to drink. Her first mistake? Letting Reno go against his instinct by leaving her alone there. Corg could come in and Amy would be practically defenseless. Of course, he came. The second? Putting the headphones on and allowing herself to doze off to the sounds of KoRn's new 'The Untouchables' album. When Corg came, how would Amy have noticed? This held true, too...she didn't notice when the gothic fighter walked up beside her. Corg continued, speaking to the napping girl, ''You had to come here and watch everybody, just had to see your stable-mates fight for your causes...had to prance around behind your red-headed Romeo, huh?'' Her eyes were closed and she was all but dead asleep, much to Corg's delight as he slid down to kneel beside her, being much more gentle than he'd ever been before when he put one arm at the small of her back, the other slipping underneath her knees as he carefully hoisted her up, carrying her like a small child. Amy stirred at the sudden motion, but it didn't seem she'd come awake, so he continued on... ''It's amazing that you're really so confident. A little bitch like you...you surround yourself with these big, bad men and think you're absolutely fucking immune, don't you?'' Of course, he didn't get an answer...Campbell was still fast asleep. ''You're even happier now, you've got that little redheaded snot to be your Rotweiller...he can just follow you around and keep you safe. Can't be together all the time now, can you? You convinced him you'd be fine, it was just a few minutes alone inside of this nice, secure room...right?'' He lowered his mouth to her ear, using his teeth to tug at one of the rings before he whispered, ''Not so safe now...are we, bitch?'' All that Amy could feel was hot breath against her ear and down her neck as she drifted back from sleep. She didn't open her eyes, though...she was too exhausted, just generally run down from the stress, the constant worries about Corg getting to her. But she was safe here, she thought. Feeling Reno's arms around her, Campbell was content to lean her head against his shoulder. ''Feeling better?'' Corg, seeing Amy's eyes weren't open yet, tried to mimic the deeper tones of Davis' voice. She was too drowsy to tell the difference, anyway. ''Mmhmm.'' She groaned sleepily, nuzzling into the neck of the man she THOUGHT was her boyfriend. ''Must've dozed off...you keep me on my toes, hehe.'' ''Oh, really now? Here I was thinking you liked being on your back more.'' He let his real voice shine through and when he did, Amy's eyes flew open. ''Jesus CHRI---'' Aimz was cut off by Corg's hand slapping down over her mouth. She kicked and punched at him, landing a few good rights to his cheek, but Corg relented... Until she bit his palm. ''Little BITCH!'' He threw her, with tremendous ease, against the white bricked wall. Amy tried to stand up, but slipped over the shirt she'd taken off for comfort and tossed in the corner moments before Corg arrived. He continued to speak as he stepped over to her. ''I'll bet you still have those visions in your mind. The visions of yourself, and I, beating the shit out of you, tearing away at your pretty little thong, and the scent of bleach flowing throughout your nose.'' There was a little bit of blood flowing from Amy's mouth, probably from the collision with the wall. She was trembling, but she tried her best to remain brave. ''Fuck you.'' ''You look edgy, Amy...'' He got a little closer, flicking the knife in his hand open as he cocked his head to the side. ''What's wrong? Were you this scared before Reno fucked your brains out?'' Campbell spit blood at him, it didn't help to sooth the beast at all as he put the knife dangerously near to her throat. ''Were you? C'mon and answer me, whore...'' Amy shook her head, ''No.'' ''I could fuck you, right now,'' Corg smiled, then continued. ''...but that's getting old. I'd rather try something new. Something along the lines of, um... setting your body on fire and lick away at your blood as you fucking burn? Or maybe I could even go 'Ed Gein', and wear your skin as a formal outfit. How about this nice, jagged,'' He thrust the blade upward to accent his descriptions, the very tip now set against the smooth skin at Aimz' throat. ''...shiny knife splinted up your pussy? Anything'll do I guess, since you've already done it all.'' ''What the fuck is wrong with you? You're a...'' She gulped, trying to move her jaw as little as possible for fear of the slick blade against it. ''...a fucking monster! You get some sick thrills from this shit?!'' Corg slapped her clean across the mouth. ''Shut the fuck up or I'll 'violate' that pretty little cunt like I planned to at Ascension, too.'' Feed was lost temporarily at that point…leaving one to wonder what exactly will befall Aimz in the back.
