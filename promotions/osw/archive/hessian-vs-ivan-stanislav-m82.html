Hessian vs. Ivan Stanislav?

2002-02-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hessian vs. Ivan Stanislav? Written: 16/02/2002 The cameras fade back into the MCI center, which is abuzz with excitement. Nate: Welcome back to Slam everyone!!! We are ready for tonight’s main event of the evening and to find out who is the mystery man who agreed to take on Ivan Stanislav!!! Robbie: Cannon Fodder. Nothing more, nothing less. Nate: We shall see!! Earl Henrich is standing in the ring, ready to introduce our the combatants, so lets send it up to the ring!!! Earl: Our next match is scheduled for one fall. First, from Arkhangelsk, Russia...weighing in at 400 pounds, Ivan Stanislav! The Soviet National Anthem hits as the crowd immediately serenades him with their roaring boos. The bear of a man struts forward, fist burying itself into open palm as he prepares for his blind match. As the Russian Red Army Choir continues, so does Ivan. Making his way to the front of the ring and gripping the top rope from the floor with his massive right hand, he pulls himself up. Turning for but a moment to glare out to the fans, Ivan takes no time in turning and bringing his full body over the top rope. He clenches his fists tight and then releases, waiting for that mystery opponent to show its face. Robbie: Ivan's going to completely waste whoever this 'mystery opponent' is...Ed's such a moron. Ivan' gonna wipe the floor with him! Nate: Don't be so sure about that. Ed was looking around last week, but sources say he's found his man. Steven: I wish Eddie would let me find his man... Nate and Robbie sigh. Earl: And his mystery opponent... The arena remains quiet and the crowd is noticably beginning to become restless. Robbie: C'mon, Earl...spit it out. Ivan stares towards the entrance ramp when suddenly, 'I Am Hated' by Slipknot hits the PA system. Robbie: Are you fu--- Steven:(quickly): Is it who we think it is?!? The crowd gives a roaring boo as the massive form of Hessian steps forward, mask shining off the arena lights. Nate: IT’S HESSIAN!!! SATAN’S SERVANT HAS STOOD UP TO TAKE ON THE RUSSIAN BEAR!!! Robbie: If this was the business he had for tonight, he is going to go belly up, literally!!!! Steven: I prefer to be belly down. There is dead air for 1….2…..3…..4…..5 seconds. The hulking Ivan Stanislav stares down at Hessian as he seems to move like a robot towards the Russian Bear. He shakes his head slowly in response to the newfound knowledge of his mystery opponent. Hessian, as Ivan did before him, reaches up with his gloved hand straight to the top rope. Robbie: Is it...cold in the arena? Nate: What the hell are you talking about? Robbie: That big satanic putz is wearing a sweatshirt... Pulling himself up, the Russian darts forward, grasping Hessian by the back of his sweatshirt and flipping the 350 pounder into the ring. Hessian's body hits the mat with a rumble as Ivan trots quickly around him and grasps him by the throat with both hands. Robbie: Look at this...and you tell me that Ivan isn't going to mop the floor with him? Nate: I never said he wasn't...I just said you never know. Steven: I'd like to mop the floor with that little cutie, Minion. They're so cute when they're small... Nate and Robbie: .... Stanislav pulls Hessian straight up into the air with both hands and holds him there, allowing a bellowing laugh/roar to surface from his massive body. Hessian's hands go to his throat and clutch onto those belonging to Ivan Stanislav. Ivan groans aloud as his face turns downward in a grimace. The crowd gasps as Hessian pulls Stanislav's hands free from his throat and brings his feet back to the ground. The Satanic Monster immediately brings a right hand to Stanislav's jaw, causing him to stumble back a few feet. Nate: Hessian sending Ivan back a bit. Robbie: HAH! Hessian's nothing but a sissy...Ivan's just keeping it entertaining. Ivan looks up in disbelief at Hessian as the inch taller man's chest rises and falls with the flow of adrenaline. Stanislav rushes forward and spears Hessian down to the mat. Pinning him down with his weight, Ivan begins drilling fist after fist into the hard mask of Hessian, each shot ringing aloud and causing the Goliath's head to slingshot back into the mat. Even after his hands begin to bleed, Ivan's fists continue their assault upon Hessian. Nate: Ivan's hands are bleeding and he's STILL hitting Hessian! Steven: I-- Robbie immediately cups his hand over Steven's mouth to stop him in mid sentence. Robbie: Believe me...we don't wanna know WHAT you're going to say. Having had enough, the Goliath stretches up as far as he can, locking his legs under the arms of the russian pinning him to the ground like a stuck pig. Hessian pushes up and pulls Ivan straight onto his back as Gaston slides down to make the count. Steven (gasping): OH MY!!! That man sure can stretch! 1 2 ...Stanislav kicks out with authority, sending Hessian's body back and into the ropes with a groan. Nate: SATAN’S SERVANT IS HERE TO FIGHT!!! Hessian struggles to his feet as Ivan angrily stomps to his own. He reaches out a hand and, with a single swat, takes Hessian up and over the top rope and to ringside. Ivan throws his arms up and roars, spouting out mocking words in his own Russian tongue. Robbie: Ivan's whooping Hessian like a pimp would his whore. Nate: ... Hessian tries to pull himself together as Ivan steps over the ropes and hops down to the ground. As Hessian is about to reach full stance again, Ivan brings a hefty boot to his side, causing him to crumple down and back to the ground. Some of the crowd boos, but none of them really seem to care about the thrashing Ivan brings to Hessian. Another boot sends Hessian back down on all fours. The Russian steps back and rushes forward, kicking Hessian straight in the ribs and halfway up the ramp. The huge Goliath rolls back down and climbs up to his feet where Ivan is, arms spread open and waiting. Stanislav grabs Hessian and pulls the 350 pounder over his head in a quick military press. He holds him up for a moment and grunts as Hessian begins to struggle. Ivan groans and attempts to keep hold, but only manages for a few seconds or so before the Goliath breaks free, stumbling to the ring steps. Ivan immediately gives chase and rushes after Hessian. Hessian acts quickly and reverses Ivan's attack, delivering The Grim Reaping and driving Stanislav's exposed head straight into the steel steps. Crowd: OOOOOoooo..... Robbie: Dear God that looked like it hurt. Nate: HESSIAN IS TAKING IT TO THE BEAR!!! Hessian is the first to his feet as Ivan leans against the steel and slowly gets to one knee. The Goliath grabs Ivan by the back of his head and slams it right back down. First, into the steps, then one more time into the ringpost, sending the russian bear down onto his back. Ivan, knowing he is left open, quickly recovers and sits up. Hessian begins slamming the boots down as hard as he can into the Russian barrel of a chest. Robbie: Fight, Ivan!!! Fight!! Hessian stops for a moment to throw his own arms up, giving Ivan that precious second he needed. In a feat of sheer and brute strength, Ivan grabs the boot of Hessian with one hand and higher up on the same leg to his knee with the other and flings Hessian up and over to the opposite end of the ring, Hessian's mask saving the back of his skull from caving in with the contact from the steel steps it meets. The crowd gasps in pain and sympathy for Hessian as Ivan gets to his feet slowly, teeth grinding in rage. Nate: WOW! STANISLAV IS INCREDIBLY STRONG!!! Robbie (standing up and motioning to the crowd): Where's that chant?!?! Crowd (responding immediately): HO-LY SHIT!!! HO-LY SHIT!!!! HO-LY SHIT!!!! Hessian lays there, twitching and slowly moves his arms up to check to see if his mask is still securely clasped to his head. Stanislav begins making his way over to Hessian, exchanging words with a few fans and growling at them as he pulls Hessian up. Into the ring Hessian goes with a push from Ivan. Ivan pulls himself up and begins to step over the top rope just as Hessian speedily gets to his feet. Before Ivan can get both feet over, Hessian grabs him by the back and flips him into the ring, sending the Russian flat to his back. Ivan immediately comes to a crouch and ducks a huge clothesline from Hessian, who uses his momentum to bounce off the ropes and deliver a facebuster to the back of Stanislav. Again, Ivan gets to his feet and again, ducks another clothesline, this time, gripping the back of Hessian's head and bringing him down to the mat in a reverse neckbreaker that shakes the ring. Gaston again hits the mat as Ivan covers Hessian. ...1 ...2 ...Hessian barely kicks out. Robbie: Why is this Frenchie still employed? Slow counts...all those goddamned French people count so goddamned slow, god dammit. Nate: ...that was lovely, Robbie. Nothing like adding some class to OSW's commentating team. Ivan, out of anger, pulls Hessian to his feet and whips him off the ropes. Ivan tries for a swinging lariat, but Hessian somehow manages to fit his enormous body under the arm of Stanislav, hit the opposite ropes, and leap forward, nailing the Russian with a big clothesline that sends him reeling. Nate: HESSIAN IS GETTING A HEAD OF STEAM!!! Hessian kicks Stanislav in the gut and shoves his head in between his legs. Not wasting any time and quickly wrapping his arms around the Russian's waist, the Goliath Hessian groans and tries to bring Ivan up into a powerbomb. Ivan holds on tightly to Hessian's legs and manages to stop the move before it comes to a close and lands back to his feet. Nate: NO ONE WOULD HAVE EVER BELIEVED HESSIAN WOULD BE TONIGHT’S MYSTERY OPPONENT, LET ALONE GIVE STANISLAV A FIGHT LIKE THIS!!! He pulls Hessian up and flips him over in a reversal with a huge back drop. Hessian lays down as Ivan rolls onto his body. Gaston slides in for the count again, his beret barely managing to stay on his head. ...1 ..2 Hessian kicks out after two and pushes up to one knee. Nate: Hessian showing some real determination. He's making things hard for the Russian Bear. Robbie begins pulling his shoe off. He quickly pulls off his sock. Steven: I'll make something hard for the Russian Bear. Then we can both hibernate in my-- Robbie stuffs the sock into Steven's mouth. Steven(showing an eerie amount of interest in being gagged) : Mmmmmmm.... The crowd cheers in astonishment at Hessian's quick recovery. Ivan can't believe it and brings a huge axehandle to the back of Hessian's neck. He pulls Hessian up and whips him into the ropes, catching him with an open arm and wrapping it around his neck. The Russian pulls back and locks The Goliath into a sleeper hold. Hessian struggles to free himself, but Ivan tightens the hold and leans back, lifting Hessian up into the air. Hessian's arms begin to flail madly as The Bear begins to choke the life out of him. Pierre Gaston urges with Stanislav to drop Hessian and to cease the illegal choke. He begins furiously spouting out a count in French as Speedy Riggs bolts out of the entryway. The crowd explodes into a chorus of boos. Robbie: Look at him go!! Speedy slides into the ring and immediately turns Pierre around, arguing a distraction for Stanislav to wrench the move all the more. Stanislav begins to pull harder and lift even more as Hessian's arms discontinue their flailing. Meanwhile, Gaston and Riggs are still going at it. Pierre flails his arms in the typical corny, cliche french fashion and displays his shirt, yelling something about 'Le ref~!' and running his finger through his greasy pencil thin mustache. Riggs shakes his head and sputters out a few disengaging remarks that sends Gaston into a huge french hissy fit. Nate: And what is Pierre Gaston doing!?! Robbie: Probably getting some grapes to crush for his french wine. The ref rolls out of the ring as Riggs steps forward and nods to Ivan. Gaston reenters, wine bottle in hand. Robbie: ... The crowd explodes as he shakes it up and yells 'Oui oui!', the cork of the bottle shooting out into the crowd and wine spewing forth from the neck. Pierre takes a swift chug from the bottle and bashes it down onto Riggs' head. Crowd: OOOH!!!! The crowd roars with enthusiasm while Riggs immediately hits the mat as shards of glass rain down upon him. Gaston holds the broken bottle up as the crowd explodes with excitement. All the while, Ivan Stanislav still holds the prone body of Hessian in the air. Nate: Ivan's such a coward. Always being underhanded in everyone of his matches. Robbie: Bitch, bitch, bitch. Steven(still gagged): Mmmmm..... Gaston, remembering what started the whole disagreement in the first place, steps forward, broken bottle in hand. Ivan lets out a blustering laugh as Gaston shakes the jagged bottle at him, spewing out gibberish in french. He waves the bottle around in his most menacing manner possible and Ivan drops Hessian to the ground, holding his hands up as a truce. Stanislav stalks slowly forward as Gaston waves the bottle about wildly. Nate: He should just run and save himself the trouble!!! The Russian Bear steps over his own communist referee's battered body and swiftly snatches the bottle from the frenchie's hand. Gaston jumps back in fear and shakes his hands, trying to somehow persuade the massive Stanislav to drop the bottle. As Ivan pulls it up, Hessian's arms suddenly appears between the legs of the Russian, giving him a low blow that causes the entire crowd to groan in agony. Ivan's legs slam together and his knees buckle as he falls to the mat. Hessian stands over the hunched Stanislav and takes the bottle from his hand. The Goliath trots over to one of the corners and steps up to the second turnbuckle. Taking what remains of the broken bottle, Hessian holds it up in the air and brings it straight back down to his steel mask, shattering the rest into tiny pieces. Robbie: Are we at some goth concert?! SIT THE F*CK DOWN, YOU BIG COOKY BASTARD!! Steven(remaining gagged): Mmmmm... Taking the opportunity so easily offered to him, Ivan gets up from his position and runs to the corner behind Hessian. He grips the slightly taller man by the back of the head with both hands and then flings him backwards and into the ring facefirst. Ivan walks over to Hessian's body and pulls him up, bringing him right back down with a one handed scoop slam, just to the side of Riggs' body still lying motionless in the ring. The Russian boots his ref out of the ring under the top rope and covers Hessian again. He waits for a moment before looking up to Gaston who is still slouched on the ground, evidently forgetting to count. Ivan growls up at him and Pierre snaps back to life and makes the count. ..1 ..2 Hessian easily kicks out. Ivan roars and stands from his position, glaring at Gaston as he pulls Hessian to his feet. The Russian Bear points at Gaston and then pulls Hessian up with one arm and nails him with the Iron Curtain. Hessian lays there for a moment, chest hunched up and groaning, his hand nursing his tender back. Gaston stares right back at Ivan and watches from the corner. Robbie: That'll teach him, Ivan! Boot him! BOOOOOOT HIM!!! Nate: Calm down there, buddy... Ivan pulls Hessian up and punches him again in the mask, opening up the same cuts again. Hessian's head rolls around groggily as Ivan hoists him up into the suplex position for the Red Scare. Ivan holds up the 350 pounder and trots around the ring, boasting his power and yelling obscenities to the crowd. Nate: LOOK AT THE POWER!!! Robbie: Then witness the end. He stops at Gaston and growls at him. Just as Ivan pulls back to throw the body of the Goliath in his arms, Hessian snaps his head forward and slams it hard against Ivan's. So hard is the Russian hit that he actually drops Hessian and falls to the ground, busted open, and shaking his head. Nate: HESSIAN JUST BROKE THE HOLD AND DOWNED STANISLAV WITH A SINGLE, MASSIVE HEADBUTT!!! Robbie: For God's sake, he's wearing a steel mask!! Nate: Still!! Chairs have hit this man and he's stayed standing! They're steel! Robbie: It's a mask you idgit!! And it's steel! Of course he's down!! Nate: ...you make no sense. Robbie: Yeah, well....nobody likes you. Steven: Mmmmm..... Hessian lays there for a moment when the house speakers suddenly burst to life. An older man's voice is all that is heard. Voice: 'Look, I know the supernatural is something that isn't supposed to happen...but it does happen.' Robbie: Wha...? Just as soon as it sparks up, the audio leaves the arena again. Hessian crouches down and seems to mutter a few things to Stanislav as he pulls him up. Hessian pulls Ivan's body up with surprising ease into the Canadian backbreaker position for the first part of Eternal Damnation. The crowd is in a frenzy. Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOO!!! Nate: HESSIAN HAS STANISLAV SET UP FOR ETERNAL DAMNATION!!! THIS COULD BE A MAJOR UPSET AND THE BIRTH OF A NEW SUPERSTAR RIGHT HERE!!! But, as Gaston stands from his hunched position in the corner, Hessian tosses Stanislav straight up into the air with a twist, catching him on his back in a reverse DVD position. Nate: HE’S GOING FOR A REVERSE DEATH VALLEY DRIVER!!! Steven: Uh…. Hessian wastes but a moment before slamming the Russian's head back down to the mat. The crowd gasps as Hessian drapes himself over Stanislav and hooks the leg. Robbie: NOOOOOOO!!!!! ...1 ..2 ...3!! Winner: Hessian Nate: HESSIAN WINS IT!!! HESSIAN HAS DEFEATED IVAN STANISLAV!!! Robbie: WHAT?!!! WHAT?!? THIS IS A TRAVESTY!!! HOW DID THIS HAPPEN?!! WITH A GODDAMN REVERSE DEATH VALLEY DRIVER?!! AND HESSIAN?!?! MY GOD!!! Nate: HESSIAN HAS BEATEN STANISLAV!!! HESSIAN HAS BEATEN STANISLAV!!! Steven: MMMM!!!! MMM!!!! Mmmm... The crowd remains silent shock as Hessian stands over the crumpled body of Ivan Stanislav. He looks around to the crowd for a moment and then jumps forward, tearing the sweatshirt from his body and revealing an enormously chiseled torso...adorned with a tattoo of barbed wire. Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON!!! The crowd explodes as Hessian pulls the mask from his face and lets loose his tied up hair. The cameras switch and we see the infamous tattoo . MEANSTREAK. Nate: JESUS CHRIST!!! IT’S THE MONSTER!!! MEANSTREAK IS HERE!!! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUT FEED!!! Robbie: NOOOOOOOO!!! Nate: MEAN-(bleeping)-STREAK IS HERE IN OLD SCHOOL WRESTLING!!! WE WILL TRY TO GET THIS ALL SORTED OUT IN TIME FOR PTC SUNDAY RADIO!!! WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT ON SLAM!!! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!!! The crowd explodes as The Monster They Call Meanstreak whips the mask down onto Stanislav's body. 'Super Charger Heaven' by White Zombie hits, the same old man's voice from earlier ringing clearly in the intro. Streak stares down at Stanislav, walking backwards.as he does so, and leans backwards over the ropes, flipping and landing on his feet at ringside. The beast continues walking up the rampway, staring the whole time at Stanislav….as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo hits the lower, central portion of the screen, as the scene fades to black.
