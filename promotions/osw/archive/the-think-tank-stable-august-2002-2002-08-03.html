The Think Tank — stable (August 2002)

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Think Tank Members: ''The Scholar'' Howard Porter , Alan Kriegman , Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman , Trashcan Man , Armour , John Mammoth Status: Heel Combined Weight: 1759 Signature Moves: Bio The Think Tank...the name which has recently resurfaced to strike fear into the hearts of everyone near and dear to Old School Wrestling through their terrorist acts as The Vax. The Think Tank traces it's humble beginnings to the now defunct Ultra Fantasy Wrestling, where founding and current member Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman started his illustrious career, and founding and current member 'The Scholar' Howard Porter saw his flourish. PHD and Porter had seen themselves embedded in a heated feud that climaxed in a dangerous computer store match, where PHD's Brainiacs tag team partner Nick Taylor shoved a stack of computers over and onto Howard, pinning him underneath and letting PHD pick up the victory. Soon after Howard formed a coalition with 'The Demolition Man' Bob Ulrich to help him in his battles with the Brainiacs. This led to a tag match between the men, that led to an ending no one expected. Porter turned on Ulrich and joined PHD and Nick Taylor, and the Think Tank was born. The Think Tank stormed through UFW, with Porter picking up the United States Title in addition to already holding the Rising Star, and the Brainiacs earning the Tag Titles. An added stipulation by the ever thinking Dorkiman allowed any combination of the three men to defend the two belts. Before the Tank was allowed to reach it's full potential, Ultra Fantasy Wrestling folded. However the Tank, being the strong entity that it is, lived on...or, at least 2/3 of it did. Professor Dorkiman and 'The Scholar' moved on to the Millennium Wrestling Federation, while Nick took an extended leave from the stable to attend Dungeons & Dragons camp so he could garner the skills necessary to compete with PHD. Meanwhile in MWF, the Tank was basically on hiatus. Porter and PHD were involved with some other former UFW wrestlers in a battle with the Neo-Underground Organization, or the NUO for short, and their leader, Mike 'Y2K' Kreller. PHD nabbed the MWF Cruiserweight Title while Porter won the World Title, and the Think Tank entity spread strong in MWF. Eventually the Millenium Wrestling Federation was absorbed by the upstart promotion started by Dorkiman, which has grown into perhaps the most recognizable wrestling syndicate in the business, Primetime Championship Wrestling. Porter had already been active in PCW, masquerading as the 'Masked Commissioner,' and that charade culminated at the first PCW Pay-Per-View, Dawning, where Porter was revealed as the Masked Commissioner and, with the aid of PHD, Taylor, and new Think Tank inductee Alan Kriegman, proceeded to beat down newly crowned PCW Champion Kremmen. The Think Tank was back, and it was stronger than ever with the induction of the fourth member, Kriegman. The Think Tank began imposing their will, but soon they met with competition in the form of Team International, led by Ed, who teamed with former PCW World Champ the Chef, Two Ton, and Troy Brown. The feud, which consisted of exploding Dr. Pepper cans, M&M shooters, stolen chairs and spatulas, and paint bombs, was one of the most heated (and sticky) feuds ever in PCW. However members were being weeded out, as Nick Taylor was squashed by the gargantuan Two Ton, Troy Brown disappeared, Howard Porter faded into the cracks, among other things. Soon the battle ended, and the Think Tank experienced a trying time. Porter and PHD were in and out, Nick Taylor was in the hospital, and Kriegman, the newest member, found the weight of the stable on his evil shoulders. Kriegman faced a trying time during the next period of the Think Tank legacy. PHD was kidnapped by Jon Kano, and believe it or not, 'The Scholar' was abducted by aliens. But time passes and things change, and before long, the Think Tank was back. This time repackaged and revamped as Think Tank: The Next Generation. Porter and PHD were back, Kriegman was as strong as ever, and there was a new Tanker, former Just Electrifying Wrestling World Champion The Noble. Things looked good for the Think Tank, but then once again, turmoil from the inside seeped out, in a different form this time though. After The Noble left, the Tank was back to it's old form. The members were showing their old signs of success though, as Porter and Kriegman found themselves in a tag match against then current PCW World Champion Stewart Hix and his partner Slade. However Hix had sold out PCW, making an appearance in another promotion with the belt. The match was turned into a Four Corners match for the World Title, from which Alan Kriegman came out the victor. Then the unthinkable happened. Porter turned his back on his longtime friend, attacking Kriegman in his moment of glory. This led to an eventual World Title match between the two former partners, with PHD serving as referee. After a hardfought, technically sound match, more backs were stabbed, with PHD screwing Porter over, apparantly still holding a grudge from their early UFW days. Yes competition can make us do the craziest of things, but people, especially mentally advanced ones, can look past such inane details. They can look past them in order to coordinate one of the vilest usurptions in the history of the sport. The Vax. Yes the Vax, the dark individual who used, abused, and confused Old School Wrestlng as a whole turned out to be none other than the Think Tank, reunited and stronger than ever. And as if that wasn't enough, the triumvirate has help. Help in the form of three very scary individuals. Armour, Trashcan Man, and John Mammoth. Practically unstoppable? That would be an incredible understatement. Yes, the Think Tank is back, stronger than ever, and ready to conquer Old School Wrestling. The geeks shall inherit the earth! Members Quotes
