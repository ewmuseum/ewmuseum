Rhett Cruisin' Superstars

2002-08-28

Posted by Matt Shivan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recently, OSW World Champion Rhett Locke has been spotted in several instances with another Old School Wrestling personality...one of the roster's few female superstars, in fact. When seen together in a diner just down the street from the infamous Madison Square Garden last Saturday evening after Slam!, the two were reportedly appearing "Rather comfortable with one another." according to a decidedly anonymous source. The young lady accompanying Locke that evening refused to even acknowledge to anybody that she'd even been with the champion that night, or any for that matter, when asked questions by onlookers. Could this be a possible interest to Mr. Locke? The source was surprised to see the girl in question with Rhett, or any male superstar for that matter due to what he called, for fear of retribution on the her part, ''personal reasons''. Rhett, when asked to comment, looked as if he was about to "Crack my skull open.", according to that very same onlooker. When alerted to the situation, none of the ladies in the locker-room stepped forward, but many gave their opinions...one of which was Amy Campbell, who's the current number one contender for Rhett's title... ''Pfft, it's no surprise to me...'' She'd scoffed when referring to her female peers. ''I mean, just look at them...I've got my relationship with Reno, that's fine, but you and I both know that the rest of these women are whoring themselves out to the highest bidder on the roster.'' Tempest, Lillith and Bethany Yamato all chose to keep a stiff tongue on the subject, choosing not to incite any possible backstage qualms. Callisto Bane also commented, simply snorting ''Well OBVIOUSLY it isn't me.'' while pressing an ice pack to wounds endured at the hands of Amy Campbell last Saturday and going on to point out, yet again, that she happens to be the only homosexual, aside from Steven Smith, employed by OSW. OSW prefers not to indulge or meddle in the personal lives of its superstars, so no names other than that of the champ will be "tossed around" at this point. More as, if or when this "friendship" between the champion and one of his many lady challengers develops.
