Severed: Une Written: July 6, 2002 The scene reopens in the private, backstage locker room of Reno Davis. The crowd pops from within the arena pops loudly indicating they can see what is unfolding on the Jumbotron, as it becomes evident that the red-headed maverick is not alone, but with his new-found ladylove, Amy Campbell. Reno looks deep into Aimz’ eyes and brushes the red hair away from her face…a face still pale with the look of stress, which is reasonable considering what she has been through as of late. Reno: Don’t worry about a thing. You are 100% safe now. Aimz smiles weakly and sighs. Aimz: Thanks, but I can really handle myself. I mean, it could happen to anyone. Take a look at Rave… Reno holds up a hand interrupting her. Reno: Don’t compare your situation to that blatant case of self-fulfilling prophesy come true. Reno runs his hand across Amy’s forehead. Reno: You seem a bit clammy. I should go get you some coffee. Aimz smiles weakly. Aimz: Thanks. I’ll go with you… Reno motions her to stay where she is. Reno: Don’t even consider it. I am going to lock the door on my way out, and there is a security guard posted outside. Just sit back and relax. Aimz smiles weakly again, this time both out of admiration, and a bit of disappointment in Reno. Did he really think she was so helpless? Reno reaches into his pocket, and produces a CD case. Reno: (continuing) Besides, how will you enjoy this if you are walking around outside? Aimz looks down at the CD case and smiles. Aimz: HOW DID YOU KNOW I WANTED THIS?!?! Reno winked as he walked out the door. Reno: Nothing ever escapes my notice! I’ll be right back with a nice cup of coffee. Reno gives a wink and exits the room, while Amy pulls her new KorN CD out of its case and nimbly paces it inside her walkman….as the scene fades to black.
