Timo and Aimz, attacked by Stanislav then Meanstreak during a run-in.

2002-03-30
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Timo and Aimz, attacked by Stanislav then Meanstreak during a run-in. Written: March 30th, 2002 'Defy You' by the Offspring suddenly hits the PA system. The crowds boos instantly turn into a roar of excitement as Timo Bolamba and Aimz rush from the entranceway. Nate: IT'S TIMO AND AIMZ!!!! THEY'RE HERE TO STOP THIS COWARDLY ASSAULT!!! Just before Timo and Aimz slide into the ring, Defy You is cut off and the lights slam down as 'Supernova Goes Pop' by Powerman 5000 hits. Nate: WHAT THE HELL?!?! Steven: NOOOO!! IT CAN NOT BE!!! The lights begin to strobe as everyone in the ring, including Timo and Aimz at ringside, all stop what they're doing and turn their attention up the ramp. Some of the crowd begins to boo very loudly. Robbie: WHO THE HELL IS THIS?! The huge figure of Meanstreak slowly trots out onto the stage, face to the ceiling and shadowed with intimidation from the strobing lights. Nate: GOOD GAWD, ITS MEANSTREAK!!!! WHAT THE HELL IS HE DOING OUT HERE??!!?! He slowly turns his head down and looks ahead of him. The lights continue to strobe as he throws his arms back and roars, charging straight towards the ring at an alarming speed. Nate: THE MONSTER IS CHARGING THE RING?!!? Steven: BUT FOR WHO?!?! Robbie: CHAOS!!! I LOVE IT!!! The lights slam up and the music stops just as Meanstreak grips both Timo and Aimz by their necks. Nate: NOOOOOOOOO!!!! Robbie: THERE IS A GOD!!!! The Beast growls and pulls them both into the air. Nate: NO!!! COME ON!!! The crowd is outraged. More debris begins littering the ring. The beast throws both his arms up and tosses Aimz and Timo over the top rope and into the ring. They both tumble in as Streak slides into the ring. Timo slowly comes to his feet as Meanstreak stands straight inside the ring. He grips the Samoan by his ponytail and whips him face first down to the mat and, instead, approaches Aimz. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Robbie: BEAT HER LIKE THE RED HEADED BLOW JOB QUEEN SHE IS!!! The monster pulls Aimz up and clutches her by the jaw. He leans forward and runs his face alongside her cheek, sniffing her. Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS HE DOING?!?! THIS IS DISGUSTING!!! Hostyle and Bones remain holding Tempest's arms tightly as Jane turns to watch Streak from behind Toomes' shoulder. Meanstreak suddenly shoots out his left arm and pulls Timo over with him. He shoves his body in front of Aimz and then pushes them both into one of the free corners. The monster crouches down and pulls them both up, stacked atop one another, into the Death Valley Driver position. Nate: NOOOO!!! MEANSTREAK HAS BOTH TIMO AND AIMZ UP IN THE DVD POSITION!!! The beast walks around the ring and stops at Sebastian Toomes. He looks down at him and growls, the crowd growing restless in response. Suddenly, Toomes nods at the massive beast that is Meanstreak. Nate: WHAAAAAT?!?!?! Immediately Meanstreak suddenly jumps up and releases his arms, flipping both Timo and Aimz over in one quick movement. They both land on his shoulders, face up, Timo on bottom, Aimz on top. Robbie: MEANSTREAK JUST FLIPPED OVER COCONUT AND FIRESNATCH FOR AT DEATH’S DOOR!!! I AM LIKING MEANSTREAK MORE BY THE MOMENT!!! Nate: ARE YOU INSANE?! HE'S ATTACKING TWO DEFENSELESS PEOPLE!!! The beast glares down at Toomes one last time, who once again nods. He then drops to his side, driving both Aimz and Timo into the mat on their heads with a sickening thud. Timo and Aimz’s bodies flop to the mat in unnatural fashion, as the Beast leaps to his feet and lets out a mighty roar. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! The crowd leaps to their feet in rage at the site they just witnessed. The crowd begins booing and chanting to a deafening volume as he stares down at Toomes. The monster's eyes glance over to Reagan and he slowly begins circling them. Robbie: UUUUHHHH??? Streak suddenly lunges behind Reagan and Toomes and grabs them both with one arm underneath their thighs. He slowly lifts them atop his shoulders and throws his head back, letting out a roar as Supernova Goes Pop hits the PA system again. Nate: MEANSTREAK HAS JOINED TOOMES AND REAGAN!?!?! WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON?!!? Robbie: MEANSTREAK GREW A BRAIN AND GOT WITH THE PROGRAM, THAT’S WHAT HELL IS GOING ON!!!! Nate: MEANSTREAK, TOOMES AND REAGAN HAVE GONE TOO FAR!!! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!!! The crowd begins booing even louder as Jane and Toomes both grin ear-to-ear and raise their arms high to the sky in victory. Frankie Bones walks over to Stanislav and grabs half of the destroyed extinguisher. He frees Stanislav and SLAMS the extinguisher across Stanislav’s head one more time. The Russian Bear rocks back into the ropes and slowly teeters, like a felled redwood, and falls straight to the mat. The impact shakes the entire ring as the 400 lbs Russian Bear, for perhaps the first time in his wrestling career, lays unconscious in the ring. Nate: THERE IS NO ONE TO STOP THEM!!! TIMO, AIMZ AND IVAN ARE OUT COLD!!! TEMPEST IS SURROUNDED!!! ED IS HURT!!! Robbie: STOP THEM?!?! NO WAY!!! I CAN’T GET ENOUGH OF THIS!!!! I WANT MORE!!! Nate: WE ARE OUT TIME!!! WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT ON SLAM!!! Meanstreak continues to carry the celebrating Reagan and Toomes around the ring on his shoulders, as “Supernova Goes Pop” continues to blare acrossthe PA system. Elsewhere in the ring, Drew Hostyle kicks Tempest to the mat beside Stanislav. The crowd boos louder as Tempest crawls over to Ivan and grabs his hand, Stanislav, however, does not move…..Tears of worry and rage stream down the cheeks of the “Little Storm” as the 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears in the bottom center of the screen...as the show slowly fades to black.
