The scene fades in from black to reveal an aerial shot of the Reunion Arena, where fans are still billowing in from the parking lot into the arena. The camera begins to pan towards the front doors of the arena, finally passing through them onto the concourse, through the throngs of fans waiting to reach their seats. Moving towards an opening, we can see the light ring, and the raucous crowd inside the arena section of the venue. The camera pans in on the threshold, when the feed is suddenly cut off, by the same loud and annoying static that has been interrupting OSW for the past two to three weeks. The fans look on as the lights in the Reunion arena go dark, and the static slowly begins to subside into the eyes of the beholder of this camera. 'The man behind the static' some may say. We find ourselves behind the Reunion Arena, which was being guarded by three security guards, who were talking and laughing. The figure stood behind the dumpster, but finally rose to his feet...and began walking towards the entrance... The guards didn't notice the man, as this semi-tall man began getting closer and closer to the guards. The height of this man could be guessed as he looked down upon the three guards, who still talked and didn't notice the man...until the man reached for the door knob... Security Guard #1 - Excuse me sir, you are not permitted in this arena...please step away from the door. The man didn't listen and instead began to turn the knob.... Security Guard #2 - HEY PUNK! You heard the man.... The man again didn't listen and was just about to the open the door when....the final security guard, being Moose Malone, pushed the man away with a hard shoulder tackle. The man behind the camera slowly began to laugh, as Malone stood ready...right in front of the door. The other two guards prepared themselves as well...but they were never prepared for him.... WHAM! CRACK! SMASH! In three quick motions with a steel crowbar, the man managed to lay out all three security guards in a matter of seconds. The horror-filled gasps of fans in the arena, who are watching the scene unfold on the Jumbotron, can be heard, as the red blood slowly trickled down the lens of the camera, with the insane laugh of this man echoing outside. The man looked down at all his fallen victims, and again laughed. ??? - "You never had a chance...Nor does anyone else in Old School Wrestling..." With that the man opened the door and entered the rear of the arena with a grim chuckle….as the scene slowly faded to static. The usual opening for Old School Wrestling’s Saturday Night Slam begins to roll, featuring OSW superstars nailing high impact maneuvers on unwitting opponents, with each move causing a Batman-esque SLAM! graphic to splash upon the screen upon completion. Wrestlers highlighted are Rhett Locke, Tom Walczak, Jesus St. Crux and OSW Commissioner, Howard Porter The show reopens in the backstage area of the Reunion Arena. Voices of technicians and sound techs can be heard close by, our location presumably somewhere near the ramp entrance. Suddenly, as the camera pans round, two massive frames come charging round opposite corners and stumble into each other. The camera pans back, the two figures slightly out of focus at its current position. As the two figures come into full view, they can be seen to be Red Army members, Ivan Stanislav and Michel Krieg. Ivan and Tom have fought together for over a year, you see what sort of a man he is now? You can be just like him...if you follow my example! You seemed to have gone astray...I hope you have righted yourself. Krieg nods in approval. Krieg: Things have been different recently, Ivan. You have seemed to be different man, but I now realise you were focused on Davis. I will follow your footsteps now, Ivan, for I see you as a true leader. Ivan grins and nods. Ivan: Good....goood! Then you watch Ivan destroy El Drago! You see what a true communist is capable of doing! A true communist does not say, "I quit" but rather smashes the capitalist with his boot heel! Ivan: Do you know what you said, when you fought El Drago? 'I Quit.' Perhaps the most gutless thing Ivan has ever heard. Stanislav gets more stern. Ivan: You did not deserve to have the pleasure of unmasking El Drago, because he bested you. Krieg's grin fades slightly. Krieg: Ah Ivan. You must realise that he is a valid competitor, and that Krieg had no other option than to give up. Would you rather have Krieg injured than save his Communist honour? Ivan nods matter of factly. Ivan: The honor of the Red Army is infinitely more important, than your well being, Krieg. Krieg: So that is the way Ivan works, huh? He doesn't care about his friends and just cares about the outcome? Well, I think Krieg doesn't totally approve with this Ivan... Ivan: To be honest with you, 'Comrade', I think it's time you got back with the Army, and shown your true colours, because as of late, you don't seem like a member of the greatest faction to grace wrestling... Nodding, Krieg grins once more. Krieg: Do not worry Comrade. I will prove my alliance to you and the Army very, very soon... Ivan grits his teeth. Ivan: You had better, Krieg. Your failure has already caused me to take MY time out to handle Drago....I don't want to have to redirect any more of my time to educating you.... Krieg slaps Ivan on the shoulder and grins once more. Krieg: Do not worry about me Ivan. My loyalty to you is unfailing. No more of your time will have to be wasted on my behalf, believe me... With that, Krieg nods at Ivan and continues on his path towards the staging area for wrestlers “on deck”, leaving the Russian Bear to continue on his own travels as the scene fades to black. The scene reopens inside the arena section of the Reunion Arena as pyros shoot off from the ringposts, ceiling and entryway, while the synthesized sounds of the Who’s “Baba O’Riley” hits the PA system, causing the already loud crowd to explode into a massive pop in honor of the band’s recently deceased bass guitarist, John Entwistle. Singing along with the lyrics that passing fans of the greatest rock and roll band in the history of music probably know by its common, yet incorrect name, Teenage Wasteland, the fans seem united in two things on this evening. Their respect for Entwistle’s accomplishments, and their ravenous appreciation for everything that is Old School Wrestling.
