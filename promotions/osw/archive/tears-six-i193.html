Tears: Six

2002-06-29
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tears: Six Written: June 29, 2002 The smell is degrading, powerful enough to make any man faint. A car swerves in and out of control, up and over the big mounds of trash. Debris flies at the newly painted ‘crap-car’ and stains its finish. An aerial view is seen of the car, as it drives through the garbage dump. A destination point in front of the car, about 500 feet away lays a monstrous trash-compactor. It is underground, with walls that close together and a ceiling that does the same, hanging from above. It it 10 by 10 feet wide and long, made out of steel. For safety, it pokes out 3 feet from the ground so you cannot fall in. Easily. The car pulls up right next to the compactor, as the driver steps out of the vehicle. His face is revealed, and is found to be non-other than OSW’s Corg. He inhales a deep breath as he streaks his hand through his dyed red hair, and walks over to the trunk of his 80’s style Honda. Corg: It’s time… He opens the trunk, as we see a redhead girl lying in the trunk, all wrapped up in duct-tape, with one strip overlapping her mouth. It was Aimz. Corg: Time to die, Aimz. The sick Corg was preparing for something… With minor effort, Corg lifts Aimz out of the trunk, and tosses her over his rather muscular. The trash-compactor was munching trash left and right, pounding it into little pieces of nothing. Corg places Aimz down next to the compactor, right at the edge. Its ‘bottom lip’ started about three feet above the ground, where Aimz lays. Corg: Heh, you see Aimz, it didn’t have to end this way. You could’ve just let this all go before, but no, you had to stay and piss me off. Aimz begins to panic, although she still rests half-conscious. Corg: And now you shall be rewarded for your efforts… As he prepares to dump her into the trash-consuming mouth of the trash-compactor, another figure steps into play. Reno: I’m thinkin’ you oughtta leave her alone right about now buckshot. Corg turns and looks at Davis, a look of surprise, which turns to disgust on his face. Corg: Well it’s Reno “saves the day” Davis again. Let’s see you catch me by surprise this time. Reno: You’re going to pay for what you did. I won’t let you leave here untouched. Corg: Well... Corg kicks Aimz into the trash compactor and presses the crush button, starting up the machine. The walls slowly start to close, along with the ceiling. Aimz lets out a muffled scream and tries to wiggle out of her bondage. Corg looks back at Reno and smiles. Corg: The feeling is mutual, Angel. Corg then charges at Reno, who swings his crutch down at Corg’s left knee, breaking the crutch into splinters. Corg falls sideways, but catches his balance and tackles Reno into the side of the Honda. The Unwanted Angel slams a fist down on the back of Corg’s neck, which is followed by a punch to Reno’s stomach. Both men regain their footing, and Corg kicks Reno in his bad shin, toppling him over. The Silent Killer places a punch right in Davis’ nose, which sends Reno back, holding onto the car for support. Corg walks to the front of the car and grabs a steel pipe, which was on the ground, he turns to walk back but Reno kicks him in the gut, and then swings the car door open right into Corg’s face. Davis leans over, trying to catch his breath as Corg feels the bloody mess that was his face. Outraged, Corg stands up, pipe in hand, and charges at Reno, swinging the pipe down. Davis dodges and the pipe seriously dents the car’s door and roof, jamming the pipe between the twisted metal. Corg fiddles with it for a second but Reno quickly sends both hands flying in a horizontal swing at Corg’s throat. The Anti-God takes a few steps back and regains his composure before Reno could attack again. As Reno darts forward at Corg, He steps aside and side-scissor kicks Reno, sending him down. Corg grabs Reno by the hair and knees him in the face, stunning Davis. Corg then throws Reno towards the trash compactor, which was getting dangerously close to being shut. Reno saw Amy struggling and that orients him enough to hear Corg dashing forward behind him. Reno picks up a nearby bottle and turns, smashing the bottle over Corg’s head as he neared. Corg’s momentum still knocks Reno into the trash compactor, but he brings Corg with him. Both bodies rebound off the dirty steel floor of the trash compactor, which was nearby 5x5 wide now. Reno shook the stars out of his eyes and grabbed Amy, throwing her on his shoulder and climbing up the built in ladder. He ascends the ladder and places Amy on the ground just outside the compactor, but as he tries to get out, he feels a hand grab his foot. Reno doesn’t even look down first; he just drops down, landing on an unsuspecting Corg. Both men hit the floor again but Corg is earliest up this time, as he mounts Reno’s chest and begins to bounce his fists off Reno’s visage. After 7 hits, Davis manages to grab Corg’s hands and flip him off, as the sting of the punches slowly wears off. Reno stands up just as Corg does, and he Flip Kicks Corg as he nears. Corg steps back, dazed, and Reno sprints at the ladder, which he steps up on with one foot, backflips, and kicks Corg in the face with both feet. Corg’s head recoils off the opposite steel wall and he walls forward onto his knees. Reno sends another boot forward into Corg’s face, which sends Corg back up to his feet, then onto his back, unmoving. The Unwanted Angel looks up and sees that the ceiling was near to being closed. Davis lets out a sigh of relief and scales the ladder just as the ceiling closed, meaning the walls were about to crush whatever was inside. He sat down beside Amy and uses the remains of his crutch to rip off the duct-tape. When he finishes, he throws the remains in the trash compactor. Davis then rips the strip of tape from her mouth, causing her to shutter and flinch in pain. Reno: Hey...look at the bright side, At least you don’t have to shave your lip line now. Reno says with a smile, undoing the chains. A screech emits from the compactor and Reno saw that the ceiling had past the ground level. Corg would most likely die if Reno did nothing. He finishes pulling the chains off of Amy and helps her to her feet, where with Reno’s painful slump, they are at eye level. She looks at him and shakes her head in bewilderment. Aimz: You...I was...but... Aimz begins, rubbing her temples, and then her eyes. She gathers herself and looks him in the eyes, Aimz: Why? Reno coughs, spitting up blood and flam. He recovers and looks at her again, opening his mouth to speak. A grinding squeal of metal is heard as the trash compactor comes to a sudden stop, not fully closed yet. Corg stopped the machine from killing him, and Reno didn’t have enough energy to stop Corg again. He puts his finger over Amy’s lips as if to shush her, and then pulls her away as they run off from the scene...
