OSW Tag Team Championship Table Match: Da Westsidaz vs. Civil Disobedience vs. Heresian Bloodlust(c) Written: June 1st, 2002 Da Westsidaz vs. Civil Disobedience vs. Heresian Bloodlust Nate: Moving on...Let’s get back to the action…Table style!!! Steven: They have wood, I have wood!!! We all have… A meaty smack silences Steven in mid-sentence. Nate: Okay, hold onto your seats because next up is the table elimination match. Robbie: And I don’t want to hear you talk about it, so lets just get it started. 'Ante Up Remix' MOP & Funkmaster Flex starts to play and Da Westsidaz make there way down to the ring carrying a table between the two of them. Earl: Coming to the ring first Short Circuit and Riptah… Da Westsidaz!!! Riptah tosses the table into the ring as 'Bounce' by System of a Down starts to play and Seth and Trevor also carrying a table come down towards the ring. Earl: And Coming to the ring second the team of Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke… Civil Disobedience!!!! Seth and Trevor slide the table into the ring and stare at Da Westsidaz as they set the table up in the middle of the ring. Once it’s up they back into the corners and Riptah and Short Circuit pull their table out, setting it up beside Civil D’s. Steven: I wonder what that’s all about. ‘Carmina Burana’ interrupts Steven and the team of Hessian and Azrael Cain come out. Hessian wastes no time as he quickly runs toward the ring, sliding under the ropes and right into a four man stompathon. Cain meanwhile takes his time walking to the ring. Nate: Hessian wasting little time, but Cain doesn’t seem to anxious to get in there. Hessian does his best to get back to his feet, swatting off the smaller men. He powers out of the corner tossing SC and Seth down to the mat with a couple of right hands but Riptah blocks the punch and kicks Hessian into the gut. Robbie: Cain better get in there and help his partner out fast. Cain hops up onto the ring apron and looks on as Riptah struggles to lift the massive Hessian up with the Riptide. He has him a little bit off the ground but Hessian is fighting it hard. Steven: Riptah doesn’t have the strength to get it up tonight. Nate: I don’t know what to say to that… Cain starts to step through the ropes and towards Riptah. Hawke starts to help Riptah pulling at the feet of Hessian and the two men manage to lift him up and Riptah slowly turns towards to two tables. Robbie: Don’t worry, Cain will break this up no problem. Cain sees the possible elimination and now he starts to move but Seth and SC are on top of him and he can’t get to Hessian in time as Riptah and with the aid of Trevor drives him through the tables with the Riptide. Nate: Oh my Hessian is eliminated already!!! Robbie: Cain needs to get his lazy ass in gear. Cain bellows in rage and shoves Seth out of the way. He backhands SC hard, knocking him into the corner and rushes forward towards Trevor! Trevor ducks a wild clothesline by Cain just as Riptah jumps for a dropkick, nailing Cain in the chest. Cain rocks backwards, stunned by the sudden attack, while Trevor surges forward and clotheslines Cain to the mat! Riptah and Trevor nod to one another and lift Cain up. The two go for a double suplex, but Cain blocks it and stays on his feet. The duo try again, but Cain stands his ground again. He shoves Trevor out of the way and grabs Riptah by the waist and belly to belly suplex’s him to the mat!! Nate: Tremendous power by Azrael Cain! He’s able to fight off two men!! Meanwhile, Seth picks Short Circuit up from the mat, who’s brains appear to be more than rattled. Seth locks Short Circuit in a front headlock and wrenches it one twice and promptly DDT’s him to the mat. SC flips over and grabs the top of his head, obviously in a tremendous amount of pain! Steven: It’s pandemonium!!! Cain turns around to see Trevor rushing at him and manages to drop his shoulder and shoulderblock the much smaller man, sending him into the corner. Cain charges forward and drives his shoulder into the abdomen of Trevor once…twice..three times! Riptah sneaks up behind Cain and low blows him, causing the massive man to grab his nether regions and cross his eyes as the wind is knocked out of him. Cain growls and stumbles to the side as Trevor rushes out and back elbows Riptah! Robbie: Ha ha! Can’t trust anyone in a match like this!! Steven: I just like all the men! Seth grabs Short Circuit and whips him towards Cain! SC manages to control himself and cross body blocks Cain, knocking him back into the ropes and teetering over, but SC isn’t heavy enough! Cain grabs SC on the way down and gets ready for a fallaway slam!! Nate: This can be it!! Seth rushes forward with a baseball slide and nails Cain in the ankle, causing him to drop SC and lose his balance! Seth rolls out of the way while Cain tries to stomp on him! Steven: You can see that while the three teams are fighting independently, they are also making a conscious attempt to keep Cain busy and off balance! Riptah staggers back as he is caught completely by surprise from the back elbow by Trevor. Trevor snap suplex’s Riptah to the mat and follows up with a legdrop just in time to see Cain regaining his bearings. Cain runs forward to snatch up Trevor but falls for a drop toe hold! He falls forward, his height working against him as his neck falls over the bottom rope, accidentally choking him! Steven: Whoops! That’s gotta hurt! Cain grabs at his throat as Trevor seizes the opportunity to choke him more, firmly placing his boot over the large neck of Cain. Riptah and Short Circuit walk behind Trevor and stand still as the crowd grows in intensity, seeing what is on the way. Riptah and SC pull their arms back and CRUSH Trevor with a double clothesline, flooring him! Riptah and SC scream to the fans for a moment and then pick up Cain. The two send Cain into the ropes. Riptah turns to deal with Trevor as Short Circuit moves to follow up on the Irish Whip on Cain. Nate: I have got to say, Da Westsidaz appear to be the most cohesive team in the ring currently! The two are showing why they’ve held so many tag titles in their time! Trevor regains his composure and hops to the top turnbuckle, just in time for Riptah to turn around. Trevor leaps and crowns Riptah with a double axehandle, knocking the bigger man to a knee. Trevor rebounds off the ropes and knee lifts Riptah, knocking him to the mat. Trevor, winded from the flurry of moves, staggers wearily to the center of the ring and bumps into Azrael Cain! Nate: This can’t be good! Cain growls and grabs Trevor by the throat and lifts him into the air. Seth notices his friends plight and runs behind Cain, reaches forward, and grabs Cain by the back of the hair and pulls, wrenching Cain’s back. Cain roars in pain and tosses Trevor and spins, clocking Seth in the side of the head! Seth rocks lands hard on his back from the force of the blow. Cain grabs Trevor and sets him up for a powerbomb. But as he positions him, Riptah surges forward with a diving shoulderblock against Cain’s exposed torso, breaking the hold and knocking Cain backwards into the ropes. Cain bounces forwards while Trevor stays doubled over and lifts Cain in a back body drop. Riptah grabs Cain in a neckbreaker position as he is slowly lifted and back body dropped/neckbreakered into the mat!! Crowd: OWWWW!!! Cain flips over from the force of the blow and grabs at his neck while Riptah and Trevor jump at the opportunity to capitalize. Both men grab Cain and lift him into a double backdrop, doing everything and everything to wear Cain down. Cain howls in pain as Trevor sucker punches Riptah!! Steven: That was a cheap move! Robbie: Yeah, but it paid off!! Heh, get it! “Paid” off! That’s a pun! Nate sighs. While Cain lays in the corner, Riptah and Trevor lock up at one end of the ring, Short Circuit and Seth Masters at the other side! Masters whips Short Circuit, not realizing he’s throwing SC towards Riptah and Trevor! Riptah lifts Trevor up in a double choke just as SC slams into his larger partner! Riptah is knocked sideways, and due to his height, flips over the top rope whilst still holding Trevor in his hands!! Nate: OH MY GOD!! Trevor flies through the air and lands headfirst through a table, Riptah lands on his feet, but atop a table, which flattens as the bell rings, signifying both men have been eliminated!! Seth blinks and bites a nail as Short Circuit spins around and glares at Masters and Azrael Cain rumbles to his feet! Robbie: It’s now down to one member of each team. Steven: One member is all I need. SC and Seth charge at each other locking up in the center of the ring. Cain charges too taking both men down and rolling them into the corner with a spear. On the outside Caleb slides a table through the ropes. Nate: This is anyone’s match now. Cain whips Seth out of the corner into the opposite one and then goes to whip SC but he hooks his legs on the ropes and Cain’s momentum trips him up. Seth charges into the corner nailing SC with a splash. Steven: High paced action, just the way I like it. Seth turns his attention towards the table and sets it up in the ring. Cain however is back up and clubs him down to his knees with an axehandle. He then pulls Seth back to his feet and rolls him onto the table. Nate: Here comes Short Circuit. Short Circuit comes flying into the shot trying to splash Cain but he is plucked out of mid air around his neck and the momentum carries him as Cain slams him onto of Seth and through the table. The crowd explodes!!! Winners and Still Tag Team Champions: Heresian Bloodlust View Da Westsidaz 's Biography
