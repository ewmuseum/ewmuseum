Civil Disobedience/Meanstreak vs. Heresian Bloodlust/Ivan Stanislav

2002-03-02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil Disobedience/Meanstreak vs. Heresian Bloodlust/Ivan Stanislav Written: 3-2-02 Nate: Welcome back to Slam!!! Ok folks, we are about ready to blow the roof off this place as the first of our two main events, so let’s not delay and send you it up to Earl Henrich in the ring. Earl: The following match is a six man tag. On their way to the ring, weighing in at a combined 725 pounds, Azrael Cain and Hessian...HERESIAN BLOODLUST!! I Am Hated by Slipknot hits the house speakers as the lights turn down. As the song's lyrics begin, the lights raise behind the massive figures of the tag team known as Heresian Bloodlust. The lights slowly turn up as they make their way down to ringside, Minion the only manager of the two present. Robbie: Cain's gonna tear Meanstreak apart for his idiotic attack towards Bobo. Nate: I believe that's Caleb. Robbie: Bobo, Caleb...what's the difference? The two enormous men pull themselves up onto the top rope and look out to the crowd as the lights strobe. They simultaneously step over the top rope and wander around the ring as Earl Heinrich's voice replaces their music. Earl: AND the third member of the first team, weighing in at an even 400 pounds, IVAN STANISLAV!! The Soviet National Anthem hits in all its splendor as Ivan, followed by Alexei Ruslan, walks out to the top of the entryway. The gargantuan Russian crosses his arms over his barrel chest and scoffs out to the crowd who receive him in a less than splendid chorus of boos. Ivan slowly struts down the ramp, swinging his massive arms slowly and twisting his neck in a counter clockwise motion. He grits his teeth and steps up onto the apron. Alexei stays on the floor and turns out to the crowd as Ivan does the same. Stanislav simply shakes his head and turns his nose up as he steps over the tope rope. Cain and Hessian stay in a separate corner as Ivan seemingly ignores them also. Robbie: WOO!!! I can't get enough of that brilliance right there! Steven: Alexei's a real strategist in the sack, too, so say the rumors. Robbie: I was talking about Ivan, you dolt. The cameras pan out and over to the entranceway, Earl's voice once again filtering through the speakers as 'Pretty Fly For a White Guy' by The Offspring hits. Earl: And, introducing the members of the second team, weighing in at a combined 460 pounds, Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, they are CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE!!! The two members of Civil D trot out onto the ramp, Trevor holding his arms up and Seth grinning like a complete idiot. Seth walks down in front of Trevor and blows kisses out to a few of the female fans, who, in turn, stick out their tongues in pure gagging fashion. Trevor pats his partner on the back and winks at him, shooting a look out to the same ladies. Civil D waits at the bottom of the ramp, bobbing their heads in unison as the lights completely slam down. Earl: And their partner, weighing in at 365 pounds... The intro to Super-Charger Heaven hits and the crowd roars to a deafening level. Earl: MEANSTREAK!!! The lights begin strobing in a chaotic manner as smoke bellows out from beneath the ramp. The Monter They Call Meanstreak walks out through the flashing lights and sucks in some of the smoke, walking straight down to the ramp and not acknowledging anyone around him. Robbie: Let's see if that big tard can actually do anything to Hessian this week. Nate: Shut up, you dumbass...you know it was a tainted win. Robbie: I do not. Nate: sigh... Meanstreak pushes past both members of Civil D, hopping up onto the apron immediately. He throws his arms up, brings his fists down to his chest, and lets out a roar, allowing the smoke to escape from his mouth. The beast makes as if he's about to hop the top rope, when suddenly, 'Food for the Brain' by One Minute Silence hits the PA. The crowd erupts even more, nearly drowning out Nate Dumas's voice. Nate: Ed's coming out here AGAIN?!?! But why?! Robbie: sigh...whoopdee*bleep*indoo. Ed trots out, mic in one hand, Bob in the other. The Hardcore Legend throws his arms up as the crowd cheers and saunters around atop the ramp. He waits for the roaring crowd to calm and then brings the microphone to his lips. Ed: Now, I know, you (pointing to the ring) love to really get involved in matches with your comrade, Ivan Stanislav... The camera shoots to the ring where a stunned Speedy Riggs furrows his brow and frowns, knowing something bad for him is definitely about to happen. Ed: Now, Speedy...I know how much you fancy your big Communist leader and all his colorful pep talks...but I don't think we'll be needing your services in this match EITHER!!! Robbie: OH COME ON!!! HE HAS EJECTED SPEEDY FROM TWO MATCHES IN A ROW!!! Nate: As well he should!!! Ivan throws his head back in fury at the commissioners statements and grips the ropes, shaking them with rage. Cain and Hessian exchange a few worriesome glances and turn their attention back to Ed. Robbie: That stupid idiot Ed is going to ruin everything!!! Ed wanders around atop the ramp once more, grinning like a moron the whole time. He stops his little trip around the stage and then brings the mic back to his mouth. Ed: Instead, I have somebody fair and honest that I think could be an interesting addition to this match. 'Brainless' by The Urge suddenly hits the PA. The crowd nearly explodes, the music of their hero blasting over the speakers. Ed nods and smiles as Doug Troy walks out from the entrance way and appears on the stage. He tugs at his vertical-striped shirt and winks towards the ring. The camera pans in to a wide shot of the faces of Meanstreak and Ivan as they both roar in protest. Nate: Doug Troy is the new referree for this match!!! Robbie: They're taking Speedy out?! How is THAT legal?! Doug Troy climbs quickly into the ring and pushes Speedy back against the rope. The two exchange a few words as Ivan tries to butt in. Doug swiftly shows Ivan his OSW badge and shakes his head and a finger at the Russian Bear. Ivan turns away with a bellowing 'BAH!' as Doug grabs Speedy by the seat of his pants and throws him out of the ring! Robbie: WHAT?!! HE CAN'T TREAT SPEEDY LIKE THAT!! Nate: He certainly just did. Ed grins and steps away from the ramp. Just before he exits the entrance way, he stops. Brainless comes to a fading close as Ed brings the mic to his lips. Ed: Oh, and guys...have a good match. Ed winks and thrusts Bob to the air as the bell rings. Nate: Ed's taking some initiative here and making things fair. Robbie: BAH!!! Speedy's fair! He's completely unbiased! Nate: PBBBBBBBBBBBBTT!!!!!! Robbie: .... Ivan and Hessian stand in their corner and turn towards eachother as Meanstreak paces back and forth across the ring. Civil D climbs into the ring behind him and begin to converse amongst themselves. Ivan shoves Hessian, apparently arguing over who is going to take on Streak. Nate: These morons can't even choose who's going to get in there first. Cain, having had enough, slaps the back of Ivan and climbs into the ring. Stanislav turns around as Cain darts past him and races directly at Civil D. Trevor and Seth look up a second before they are taken to the mat by a huge double clothesline form The Marked Beast. Meanstreak shrugs and rolls his eyes and steps out onto the apron, staring over at Ivan, nostrils flaring. Cain picks up Trevor as Seth rolls out, free of his grasp. Nate: Cripes that was one hell of a clothesline by Cain. Robbie: Hahaha...you don't mess with a man as angry as him. And that sissy boy's about to find that out. Cain pulls Hawke up and holds him above his head in a military press. He walks freely around the ring, pressing Hawke a few times before dropping him to the mat beneath him. Hawke holds his back in pain as Cain pulls him up and drops him quickly with a huge boot to the face. Robbie: Did I not just call that? The Marked Beast pulls Hawke up again and shoves his head in between his legs. He looks out to the crowd who boos in response and shakes his head, pulling Hawke up into a powerbomb. Azrael Cain swings him around once, but a swinging hand from Meanstreak pulls Hawke down and off Cain's back, perhaps causing more harm tho him than saving him from a harsh landing. Cain swivels quickly to Meanstreak and points to him, motioning for him to get into the ring. Meanstreak growls and grins as Doug Troy steps in front of Cain and tells him to back off. Hawke rolls to his feet slowly as Troy screams over at Streak, who swings at him with a huge paw. Nate: Geez...Doug's getting a little too close there... Robbie: Bah!! Meanstreak couldn't hurt a damned fly. Doug's safe. With Doug's back turned, Cain turns around, only to be met with a low blow from Hawke. Trevor brings a fist down onto the back of the Marked Beast's neck and keeps pounding on him. He pulls his face up and swings a right. However, Cain catches his hand and pulls him forward, bringing him down to the ground with another walloping clothesline. Cain throws his arms up and tries to roar, but clutches his crotch and grits his teeth. Nate: Hahahaha...that was comical...doncha think, Robbie? Robbie: Shaddup. Steven: Mmmm...to be the hand of Trevor Hawke... Cain wanders in a circle for a moment and looks over at Meanstreak, again throwing a finger up to him and challenging him to get into the ring. Streak shakes his head and snaps at the Marked Beast as Hawke suddenly rolls towards Cain, hitting his heels with his full body weight and rolling him up. Doug quickly hits the mat and makes the count. ..1 ..2 ..Cain barely kicks out and does so viciously. Hawke is thrown over to the corner, but somehow lands on his feet. The crowd cheers as he races forward and hits the rising Cain directly in the face with a falling dropkick. He begins laying the boots upon Cain's body and pulls him to his feet, attempting to whip him to the ropes. Cain quickly reverses and counters with a whip of his own. As Hawke comes back, Cain swings for a clothesline, but Trevor detects it, ducks underneath and jumps up into the air, clutching Cain's head as he goes. Hawke brings Cain down on the back of his head and kicks up to his feet, throwing his arms up in triumph. The crowd cheers loudly again. Nate: Hawke looking very impressive this evening. Cain looks flustered. Robbie: He's just measuring out the right moment. Steven: And I'm looking to measure something at the right moment this evening, too. Nate: Will you please keep your comments to yourself? Steven: Hey, it's a free country. Nate: Yes, but your comments aren't taken very freely here so shut the hell up. Hawke looks over at his partner Seth and shoots a smile. Seth winks back and snaps to him as Cain suddenly shoots to his feet. Seth motions behind Trevor as he backs up. Hawke looks very confused as he looks back at his partner, a huge grin still plastered on his face. Cain quickly spins Hawke around. Trevor's eyes go wide as Cain grips him by the throat and lifts him with one hand into the air. Doug counts for Cain before he drops him. Robbiee: Resiliant Cain is, hmm? Nate: Sure is, Yoda. Cain spouts another remark in Meanstreak and Seth's general direction and walks over, ignoring DT as he counts. The Marked Beast points at Meanstreak and yells something inaudible, slamming Hawke into the opposite turnbuckle hard. Cain glares at Streak as Trevor holds his back in pain and walks over to Hessian, tagging him in. Hessian glares at Meanstreak and, himself, spouts out taunts for the monster to climb into the ring. He pulls Hawke up into the air by the neck in the same fashion as Cain did and walks over to the middle of the ring. Nate: Hessian's simply toying with Trevor Hawke. Steven: Now that's good watchin'! Hessian growls out another threat to Meanstreak and slams Hawke down onto the mat in a chokeslam, pinning him down with his forearm on Trevor's chest. DT falls for the count. ..1 ...2 ..Hessian pulls Trevor up...by his neck. The Satanic Giant brings Seth up as he stands, the crowd booing defiantly. Hessian tosses Seth into his corner and begins walking towards him. However, before Hessian can reach Hawke, Streak leans down and yanks Trevor up by the back of his neck, pulling him back and over the top rope to the floor beneath them. Nate: Whoa! That was a rather...crude tag by Meanstreak.... Robbie: That big jackasss can never be civil, can he? Meanstreak rushes into the ring and spears Hessian from his feet to the mat. He hops up immediately, holding the 350 pounder in his arms and swings him right back down with a bearhug slam. Hessian roars out in pain as Streak again pulls himself from the mat. Nate: Good Lord!! Again, Streak slams Hessian to the mat in another bearhug slam and jumps to his feet, pounding his chest and roaring to the crowd as they explode in cheers. Alexei hops up onto the ropes and waves his arms, screeching out at Meanstreak. The monster turns to him as Doug Troy does the same. DT begins arguing with Ruslan as he grips his referree's shirt. Meanstreak begins trotting towards Ruslan's direction and Troy's turned back but is unexpectedly taken from his feet by Cain's own spear. Cain pulls Meanstreak up and whips him over to Hessian as he rises. Hessian levels Meanstreak with a monstrous clothesline as Ivan joins Alexei and Doug in their argument. Nate: C'mon, Doug!!! They're doing this on purpose!! Robbie: THAT's the brilliance there, Steven!!! Hessian pulls Meanstreak up and locks his arms behind his back. He slowly lifts Meanstreak into the air as Cain steps forward and begins bringing his fists hard into the beast's abdomen. A few more punches and they drop him to the mat and begin laying the boots to him. Nate: Doug, for the love of God!!! Minion hops up to help, directly behind Alexei. Unfortunately, Doug swings a fist, intended for a ducking Ruslan and clocks Minion straight in the jaw. Minion the Imp flies from the apron and lands flat on his back at ringside, the crowd annointing Doug's unexpected move with a round of cheers. Crowd: DOUG-EY!!! DOUG-EY!!! DOUG-EY!!! Meanwhile Cain and Hessian have picked up Meanstreak and whipped him into his own corner. Hessian tells Cain that he can go on and gives a signal to Ruslan and Ivan as he trots toward Meanstreak. Hessian brings a hard fist into the beast's jaw, another to his abdomen again, and then yet another to his ribs. Streak hangs there on the turnbuckle, groaning in pain as Doug Troy finally fixes himself back on the action. Nate: That double team really took its toll on the monster. Robbie: Such a pansy... Hessian brings another fist down into Meanstreak's jaw as Doug reasserts himself, pushing Hessian back. Seth Masters suddenly taps the monster on the shoulder and quickly leaps to the top turnbuckle. Hessian looks up just as Doug finishes his scolding and turns. Troy ducks from a flying cross bodyblock from masters that brings Hessian hard onto his back. DT turns back and counts as Masters tries his hardest to keep the giant's shoulders down. ..1 ..2 Hessian kicks out with a press that brings Seth back to his feet. As Hessian quickly tries to rise, Seth races full speed at him and jumps, twisting his body to the side and hitting the beast with a side kick. Hessian stumbles back as Seth leaps forward, trying to tackle him by the legs. Hessian, however does not budge as Seth stays wrapped around his legs. He looks behind him and reaches out his hand...Ivan Stanislav responds with a slap to his and climbs ever so slowly into the ring. Nate: OHHHHH NO..... Ivan reaches down to Seth's pants and pries him free from Hessian's legs. Hessian steps out of the ring as Ivan pulls him up to his face. Seth's eyes go wide and he begins to flail his entire body. The Russian Bear simply pulls him up farther and slams him, stomach first, down to the mat. Crowd: OOOOoooooo... Robbie: GWAHAHA!!!! It's Ivan's turn!! Nate: 'GWAHAHA!!!!' That new? Robbie: Yup. Neat, ain't it? Ivan pulls Seth up just as quickly and pulls him up into a vertical suplex. The crowd gasps as Stanislav walks over to the turnbuckle facing Civil D's corner and steps slowly to the second rope. Nate: Uuummm...what in the HELL is Ivan doing? Robbie: I dunno...but... Ivan turns his entire torso towards the announce table. Robbi: Um...move? Nate: Wha? Move? Steven: Yes, you two faggots!!!! MOVE!!!!!! There is a commotion as headphones and chairs hit the ground, Seth Masters body floating through the air and crashing through the announce table as the result of Stanislav's Red Scare!!! Crowd: FUH-KEEN CHRIST!!! FUH-KEEN CHRIST!!! FUH-KEEN CHRIST!!! Seth hardly moves as Doug begins jawjacking a grinning Stanislav. Ivan pushes him aside as he climbs out of the ring. Stanislav slowly walks to the unmoving body of Seth Masters and looks out to the crowd now VERY heavily booing him. He wastes no time in grabbing Masters by the back of his pants with one hand. The Russian Bear walks over to the ring and dumps Masters body into the ring...perhaps a little too far. Seth rolls all the way to the other side of the ring as Ivan takes his time getting into the ring. Meanstreak growls and reaches out as Seth slowly begins moving. Ivan's eyes go wide and he nearly scurries into the ring. Seth hops forward and slaps hands with Meanstreak, the crowd absolutely blowing the roof off the building. Just as Ivan gets into the ring, Hessian and Cain dart towards Civil Disobedience. Trevor pulls Seth off the apron and slaps his face a few times. Masters looks up just in time to scramble drunkedly to his feet. The two members of Civil D start slowly making their way up the ramp as Cain and Hessian give chase. Hessian grabs the back of Hawke and tosses him up the ramp as Cain shoves Masters up past his partner. Back in the ring, Meanstreak and Ivan begin trading fists left and right, neither seeming to budge and inch. Each shot sounds like a thunder clapping and the crowd keeps getting louder. Ivan, growing quickly tired of the stalemate, pulls back as far as he can and swings forward, knocking Streak directly on the jaw and making him stumble back to the corner. Stanislav rushes forward with a clothesline and slams Streak hard as he can. Nate and Robbie manage to get their audio feed back. Nate: Ivan destroyed our table and now these two beasts are going at it!!! Robbie: It's about time!!! KILL HIM!!!!! KILL HIM!!!!!!! Ivan clutches Meanstreak by the throat and slams him back against the turnbuckle. He swings forward with another thundering right as Civil D and Heresian Bloodlust disappear behind the curtain on stage, fists flailing. Nate: Well, there goes the match... Robbie: Ivan's still there!! He can still take this one home!! Steven(unfortunately getting his mic and headphones back): I'd love to take those Civil boys home! Ivan swings one more time, this time the beast shooting up an arm to block and returning with a swing of his own. Ivan tries to make contact again, but, again, is blocked and returned with a fist. Meanstreak shoves Ivan back and pulls himself to the second rope, leaping forward and nailing the Russian Bear with a diving spear. Meanstreak quickly scoops Stanislav up and whips him off the ropes. Doug watches as Streak leaps forward and grabs Stanislav around the waist. He swings him with his momentum and smashes Ivan down straight onto the back of his neck. Nate: Chaotic Peace!!! Robbie: NOO!!!! GET UP!!! Steven: WILL DO!!!! A hefty smack is heard off camera as Meanstreak pulls Ivan up yet again. Stanislav swings slowly and stumbles back and forth trying to maintain his balance. Streak easily avoids the swing and switches behind to Ivan's back. He kicks the Russian HARD in the back of his knee and scoops him up onto his shoulders, slamming the top of Ivan's head down onto the mat in one very fluid motion. Meanstreak falls quickly and hooks the leg as DT counts. ..1 ..2 ..3!!! Winner: Meanstreak and Civil Disobedience Super Charger Heaven hits as Meanstreak leaps to his feet and Doug steps next to him, gripping his forearm and pulling it up in victory. Streak's eyes shoot wide. Nate: Uhhhhhhh.....DOUG...... Robbie: BAH!!! WHO CARES!?! GO TO IVAN!!! SHOOT TO IVAN!!! The beast pulls Doug over to him and into the air. He glares at DT for a moment and then swings him up and onto his shoulders. Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS MEANSTREAK DOING!?!? Doug desparately tries to pull out, but Streak wrenches his neck back hard and plants him with At Death's Door! Nate: OH MY GOD!!!! MEANSTREAK JUST ADD-ED DOUG TROY!!!! HE'S OUT OF CONTROL!!! Robbie: HE'S OUT OF HIS GODDAMNED MIND!!! NO SHIT HE'S OUT OF CONTROL!! Steven: .... Nate: DOUG TROY, IVAN STANISLAV AND MEANSTREAK WILL MEET AT CROWNING GLORY NEXT SUNDAY NIGHT!! WE’VE GOT TO HEAD TO A COMMERCIAL!!! WE WILL BE RIGHT BACK AFTER THESE MESSAGES!!! Meanstreak stands up and surveys the fallen bodies of Ivan Stanislav and Doug Troy, then lets out a roar as the scene fades to commercial.
