One True Love: Serpentina or Olga?

30 July 2003

by Six Finger Cisco

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#122) by Six Finger Cisco, dated 30 July 2003, written from Cisco's mind and past, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Things are starting to change. I look back on the past handful of months and a lot has changed. I’ve come to OSE and made a name for myself. It’s a far cry from what I want to accomplish but you have to start somewhere. To be honest, I didn’t think I would have moved up the roster so quick and be threatening the likes of Boda and Matt Register for their respective titles. Things are not what I thought they would be, they are better. Every week I inch closer to attaining my goal of becoming something in this world and it’s almost scary to think that I’m so close to the top of the mountain. Even my personal life is starting to take a turn for the better. I’ve always had acquaintances in life, but I’ve never had a set of friends quite like Timo, Azala, and Trashy. They’ll always be there for me and it’s the first time in my life that I’ve had three shoulders to lean on. Timo is like a big brother to me. I look up to him and like a little brother I want to be like him. It pains me to see him in the condition that he’s in but he’s strong and I have no doubt that he’ll come out on top, as he always does. He’s been the motivation for me to keep going and has helped me become a superstar with his marketing genius. I wouldn’t be where I am now without him. Azala is like my drinking buddy. I feel like I can tell the big guy anything, and he won’t judge me. He’s the most genuine person that I’ve ever met and I would do anything for him. He’s the type of friend that if you told him you killed someone, he’d help you bury the body. I trust him that much. He keeps me true to myself and makes me appreciate this business. Trash is like a father to me. Not only are we very much alike in our mental state, but he’s the person that I want to be someday. I admire what he’s done in his career and if I could follow in just a few of his footsteps I’d be all set. He’s gone through a lot with Clyde in the past and I know that the two of them are far from done battling. If Clyde hurts him that burned prick will wish he could die. I’ll put him through hell if he does anything to Trashy. TCM keeps my head straight and gives me someone to look up to, and I don’t think I would be the way I am without him. Despite having a set of true friends and a career that I can actually start to be proud of, something has always seemed to be missing. A woman. Someone that will love me for me and be able to look past my sixth finger. I need a woman in my life to complete me. Everything would be perfect if I could find a bride. Now that Timo has this Russian Bride coming to meet me next week I can’t help but wonder if she’s the one. Sure, with a name like Olga she might not be the catch of the day, but while most of me wants to run away, another part wants to be with her. I haven’t even met this woman and already I feel like she could be the one. I haven’t felt this way since......Serpentina.... ~6~ The traveling carnival had stopped for the night and most of the employees were asleep. It was a starry night and the moon illuminated the cloudless sky and cast down a romantic light on the different trailers of the traveling carnival. Six Finger Cisco couldn’t sleep. He had been thinking about his one true love all night and tonight was the night that he was going to find out if she felt the same way. Serpentina slept in her trailer, her blue eyes covered by her soft eyelids while the moon cast a bit of light on the side of her face. Her jet black hair draped over her soft pillow and her bright red lips remained shut as she peacefully breathed in through her nose. She was a sight for sore eyes. A goddess. She was multi-talented and a grade A freak in the sideshow. The way she treated Cisco was what attracted him to her. When she spoke to him, it was like nothing he could explain. He could hear it in her voice that she respected him as a human being and wanted to be his friend. Cisco wanted more than that. He wanted the two of them to be together for the rest of their lives. He felt that strongly about her. This was the first love interest of the young man’s life and to feel that strong about your first is saying something. Six Finger Cisco look down on his goddess through the trailer window as she slept into the night. “ I’m here, my beauty”, the Freak whispered as he walked around to the trailer door and lightly wrapped on it. He heard the sound of Serpentina waking up and treading over to the door. He took a deep breathe as she gazed into his eyes and opened her mouth to speak. “ Cisco, what are you doing?”, she whispered as she pulled him into the trailer by his shirt collar. She was dressed in a silk white night gown, and even in this get-up looked like a prom queen. The Main Attraction’s heart pounded through his chest as he struggled to tell his crush the true reason he was there. “ I...I....ca-.ca-...walked over here to talk with you”, Cisco nervously whispered as Serpentina sat down on her bed and let out a yawn. She put her hands through her long black hair and clicked on a nearby lamp. “ What do you want to talk about?”, she asked, her voice not showing the slightest bit of annoyance. “ I..ne-.ne-...want to know....if..if...I-.I-..you and I...”, he began. Before he could finish she stood up from her bed and clicked the lamp off. Cisco’s hands were sweating and the tingling feeling he got in his stomach was a painful pleasure. She slowly walked over to him and grabbed both of his hands with hers. She leaned in and he closed his eyes. He felt her soft, moist lips press against his and it was heaven. Nothing in the world seemed to matter. It was just the two of them. His dreams had come true and this was the happiest he had ever been. They stopped kissing and Cisco opened his eyes. He looked into her soul through those blue eyes and without saying a word she had answered his question. She loved him just as much as he loved her. This was it, he was going to be with her for the rest of his life.... ~6~ Sometimes I wonder what would have become of Serpentina and I if I had never joined the PWA. I left the traveling carnival to pursue my dream of becoming a wrestler. Some days I regret not staying with her on the carnival but other days I realize that she would want the best for me. As I count down the days until I meet my Russian Bride, I can’t help but wonder if she’ll be anything like Serpentina. Will I be able to love another woman like I did Serpentina, or will I blindly fall in love with this woman named Olga? I can’t predict the future but I do know that I will never stop loving Serpentina. If I can produce the same love for Olga that I did her then all will be fine. I just don’t know that I can do that....
