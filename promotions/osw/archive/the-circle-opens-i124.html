The Circle Opens

2002-06-29
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Circle Opens Written: 6/29/02 Suddenly, all the lights go out, and the arena is plunged into complete and total darkness. Nate: What the hell??? Robbie: Steven if that's your hand on my thigh I am going to rip you a... As quickly as they went out, the arena lights return. The fans react very loudly in a mixture of shock and anger. 'The Scholar' Howard Porter now lies in a heap, looking as if he had been completely devestated during the short time in which the lights had gone out. Nate: OH MY GOD!! THERE HE IS!!! Standing over the fallen Howard Porter is a mysterious figure clad in a long black trenchcoat buttoned all the way up to the neck, black leather gloves, a black ski mask with dark goggles over the eyes, and a low-brimmed black fedora hat. Steven: IT'S THE VAX!! Nate: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THAT IS THE MAN WHOSE VILE MACHINATIONS HAVE PLAGUED OSW SINCE IT'S INCEPTION! THE VAX IS STANDING IN THE RING BEFORE OUR VERY EYES!!! A few swift kicks from the Vax send the presumably unconcious Howard Porter rolling under the bottom rope, spilling over the ring apron and collapsing to the floor, where he lies motionless. Robbie: I DUNNO HOW BUT THE VAX JUST OBLITERATED OUR COMMISSIONER!! The Vax turns away from our out-cold commmissioner and stands accross the ring from Ed, who stares at him with fury, pointing at the masked marauder. Though there is no mic, we can make out the words Ed shouts to the Vax. '...It was YOU!' With that, the Vax makes a running start at Ed and launches himself at the hardcore legend with a picture-perfect spinning heel kick...but Ed ducks under, hoists him up on his shoulders in an electric chair, and the fans let out a DEAFENING roar of approval as Ed hits him with the Brink of Insanity!!! Nate: BRINK OF INSANITY! BRINK OF INSANITY! ED JUST TOOK OUT THE VAX WITH THE BRINK OF INSANITY!!! The Vax hits the mat face first and rolls over on his back, Ed standing over him victoriously. Ed drops to one knee and kneels over the Vax, and the crowd goes wild as he grabs ahold of his black ski mask. Nate: ED IS ABOUT TO UNMASK THE VAX!! THE MYSTERY ENDS NOW!!! Steven: In the words of the gang from Scooby Doo, let's see who you REALLY ARE! Ed is about to yank off the mask of the Vax, when suddenly, the lights go out again. The crowd immediately reacts with a loud chorus of boo's. Nate: NOO! BUT WE WERE SO CLOSE TO FINDING OUT WHO... Seconds later, the lights return, and the fans again cry out in surprise. Nate: WHAT THE HELL?! Steven: It's another one!!! The hardcore legend looks up just in time to see a black wearing, masked figure identical to the one he just took out running at him! Nate: THERE ARE TWO VAXES?! Immediately, Ed springs to his feet, ducks a running clothesline, and executes the Brink of Insanity on the 2nd Vax!!! There is an absolute explosion of cheers ripping through the arena as the 2nd Vax hits the mat. Nate: THAT'S TWO VAXES DOWN! Robbie: I hate to sound like a face, but rip those masks off like old band-aids, Ed!! Surely enough, Ed drops to his knees between the two laid-out Vaxes, and grabs a mask in each hand. Nate: DO IT! DO IT! As the fans cheer him on, Ed begins to pull on the masks, when suddenly...the lights go out again. Robbie: NOW what?! Steven: This is getting older than Amy Campbell's wardrobe. Nate: What the HELL is going on?! The lights remain for a longer period than the first two blackouts, long enough for the fans to voice their displeasure while still surrounded by darkness. When light finally returns... Nate: OH MY GOD! ED, WATCH OUT! Ed, who is still kneeling, does not have enough time to turn around and defend himself from the figure standing directly behind him. This third guest is dressed in the exact same fashion as the first two visitors, but there is something that distinctly sets him apart from them. This Vax is armed. With Bob. Robbie: He's got Bob and he's not afraid to use him!!! The third Vax raises the barbed wire-wrapped, nail-studded chair...and before Ed can defend himself...brings it down right on his head. Nate: NNNOOOOOOO!!!!! Ed's eyes roll up in the back of his head and for a brief moment he teeters on the edge of unconsciousness while still upright on his knees, but a 2nd shot from his own weapon of choice sends him over. He collapses on his side, blood now running down his face. The fans howl with rage as the 3rd Vax tosses Bob aside, and proceeds to help his two colleagues to their feet, both of whom are still reeling from the impact of the Brink of Insanity. Robbie: SO THERE ARE THREE OF'EM! Steven: I smell a gang bang! Nate: THIS IS UNBELIEVABLE! WE THOUGHT ALL ALONG THAT THERE WAS ONLY ONE VAX, BUT THERE ARE THREE!!! Amidst some of the loudest boo's ever broadcasted on OSW TV, the 3rd and tallest Vax turns the bloodied, beaten Ed over onto his belly...and locks him a full nelson camel clutch... Robbie: WAIT A SECOND! Steven: I'D KNOW THAT HOLD ANYWHERE! Nate (in a tone of both denial and disbelief): NO..... This seems to wake Ed up, and he thrashes weakly in pain, but no good comes of it. The other two Vaxes stand on either side of their partner in crime. After a generous period of time, Ed is released from the hold, and he is left lying in a bloody heap, surrounded the three masked marauders standing over him. The Vax which was successful in subduing Ed reaches into his trenchcoat and pulls out a microphone. The Vax (#3): Well Ed... I believe you said something about wanting to know more about me... Or should I say...us. The third Vax removes the fedora hat from his head lets it drop to the mat, then does the same with his black goggles. The Vax (#3): I do hope this will suffice...my friend. There is an instantly loud, hateful response from the arena when he rips off his mask..... Nate (at the top of his lungs): NNNNNOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! .....revealing the spectacled face of OSW Commissioner 'The Scholar' Howard Porter. Nate: OH MY GOD!! I DON'T BELIEVE THIS!!! ED WAS RIGHT ALL ALONG!!! Steven: HOLY CRAP!! THAT TRAITOR!!! Robbie: Hahaha! I LOVE IT!! With the other two Vaxes on either side of him still masked, Porter smiles down at Ed. Nate: THAT SON OF A BITCH!!!! 'The Scholar' Howard Porter: You were a fool to think that you could beat me, Edward. All that hard work, all those sleepless nights dedicated to getting the best of me...when you really had no chance. It was I who masterminded the removal and kidnapping of Jane Reagan, as well as the numerous 'Vax attacks' and sabotages that have occured since the very inception of Old School Wrestling... But I do admit that I could not have done it all by myself... For during my short, yet useful tenure as acting CEO, I took it upon myself to sign a pair of free agents who I am positive will go VERY far in this fine company...and why wouldn't they, with OSW's Commissioner on their side? Please allow me the distinct honor of introducing to OSW my two dear friends, partners in crime, and fellow Vaxes... As the fans continue to roar in perhaps the most overwhelming session of heel heat in OSW history, the smaller of the two remaining masked marauders removes his hat and goggles, and proceeds to remove his ski mask. Steven: WHAT THE?! Robbie: NNNNNNEEEEEEERRRRRRRDDDDD!!! Nate: IT'S PROFESSOR HERBERT D. DORKIMAN! WHAT THE HELL DOES THIS MEAN?! Former UFW Cruiserweight and Tag Team Champion, PCW President, and fWo Survivor II competitor Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman gives his famous bucktoothed grin as he removes a pair of thick, black-rimmed glasses from his trenchcoat and puts them on. Steven: PROFESSOR HERBERT D. DORKIMAN AND PORTER WERE IN CAHOOTS THE WHOLE TIME!!! THEY USED TO BE TAG TEAM PARTNERS YOU KNOW!!! Nate: WHERE THE HELL IS SECURITY!! THESE MEN SENT OUR CEO TO THE HOSPITAL, KIDNAPPPED HER, KEPT HER DRUGGED, FRAMED ED AND HAD HIM SENT TO JAIL, AND COMMITTED COUNTLESS ACTS OF SABOTAGE AGAINST OSW WRESTLERS! SOMEONE GET THEM!! Steven: WHO THE HELL IS THE THIRD GUY!!! 'The Scholar' Howard Porter and Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman both look towards their accomplice and nod to him. He takes his time removing his fedora, and tosses it aside, then proceeds to remove his goggles as well. Nate: Howard Porter and PHD... That means the other one is... No...NO, IT CAN'T BE. Finally, as if the crowd's booing does not get any louder or more hostile, it is somehow taken to another level when the last Vax removes his mask, revealing the evilly grinning, goatee'd face... Steven: HOLY SHIT. Robbie: NO FUCKING WAY. Nate: OOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHHH MMMMMMYYYYYYYYY GOOOOOOOOOD!!! BUT...BUT...BUT HE's SUPPOSED TO BE DEAD!!! Security rushes out of the back to stop fans from hopping the guard rails, because the mere site of the most evil man in PCW history, has driven them into a frenzy. That man? Alan Kriegman. Nate: HOW CAN THIS BE!!! HE DIED OVER SIX MONTHS AGO!!! THIS CAN NOT BE!!! The former PCW World, Continental, and Tag Team Champion throws back his head and lets out his trademark diabolical cackle, as all three members of the infamous Think Tank stand in the center of the ring, laughing evilly at the unmoving Ed. Nate: THE ENTIRE WORLD SAW ALAN KRIEGMAN DIE IN THE PLEASANTVILLE DEATHMATCH AT PCW SHANGHAI'D 2, BUT HERE HE IS ALIVE AND WELL AND HE IS NOW IN OSW!!! Robbie: Dear GOD Nate, stop shouting! Nate: WE'RE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Howard Porter, PHD, and Alan Kriegman stand side by side in front of Ed and, as if this were some sort of hideous play coming to an end, actually take a bow for the fans who continue to voice their trememdous displeasure. Suddenly there is a loud whooshing sound from the top of the arena. The shot moves up as thousands of white flecks begin to gently fall from above. The camera shot zooms in on the ring, which is now layered with tiny white cards. The cards slowly drift through the top of the cell, down into the ring, where the mat soon becomes layered with them. The camera focuses in on the laid-out Ed for a moment, who has become covered with the cards, then focuses in on the writing evident on each one, revealing that they are all printed with the same two words in plain black lettering... 'The Vax' Nate (his voice going hoarse): THE VAX HAS BEEN UNMASKED!!! HOWARD PORTER WAS BEHIND IT ALL ALONG, WORKING WITH HIS TWO THINK TANK COMRADES, PROFESSOR HERBERT D. DORKIMAN AND ALAN KRIEGMAN….(growing breathless) HE….HE….LIVES!!! Robbie: They finished off Ed once and for good, so this isn’t all bad!!! Nate: (ignoring Robbie) THE THINK TANK IS BACK, AND IT'S RIGHT HERE IN OLD SCHOOL WRESTLING!!! WE WILL TRY TO GET THIS ALL SORTED OUT FOR YOU BY THE TIME WE AIR NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT!!! The camera focuses in on 'The Scholar' Howard Porter, Alan Kriegman, and Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman raising each other's arms in victory over the unmoving and bloodied Ed, while the crowd, full of shock and outrage lobs a hailstorm of debris at the cage and the ring area..... as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears in the bottom, central portion of the screen….. as the screen fades to black.
