Hunting for the Mystery Opponent

2002-02-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hunting for the Mystery Opponent Written: Feb 16, 2002 As the cameras fade up we are in the OSW loading bay at the MCI Center in Washington DC. Several crews are working loading in equipment from large trucks down hallways to dressing rooms, to the arena, as well as various boxes full of OSW t-shirts, collectable items, and other paraphernalia. In the lower right portion of the screen the OSW logo slowly appears and the words “Previously Recorded” blinking underneath the logo. From the right side of the screen, we can see that a large roll door has been left open to allow any more trucks to enter into the rear of the building and unload their goods. The sound of a roaring engine is heard.... As technicians continue to load and unload their equipment as well as get their jobs done before Saturday SLAM! airs, they are rudely interrupted.... The engine slowly gets louder and louder as the technicians all stop what they are doing and turn to the disturbance. A large, nondescript van roars through the opening and flies towards the camera. One technician in particular barely dives out of the way and abandons the box that he was carrying. The box nails the front end of the van and bursts apart, sending OSW baseball hats flying in all directions. The van whizzes past at an extremely high speed, so fast that we are unable to see who the driver is and then it stops with a screech, laying tire down behind it. The license plate of this van, however, tells more than any picture could. On the plate itself are the letters S O V I E T. The van jerks as movement is detected from inside and the back doors fly open. At the same time, the driver and passenger side door shoots open and stays open. From the rear of the vehicle, Communist Party Member Wang, The Two Round Faced Women, and Speedy Riggs, wearing his red and yellow stripes, file out and instantly charge towards individual trucks and technicians. From the drivers side of the vehicle, Alexei Ruslan, Ivan Stanislav’s closest friend and Chief of Intelligence, hops out and rushes to a van a few yards away. He opens the back and rushes in, thumbing through boxes and bags. From the passenger side, The Russian Bear, Ivan Stanislav, barrels out and glares around with a look of rage and fury from which the likes that we have never seen! Ivan: ED! WHERE ARE YOU?? WHERE IS YOUR HERO!!! The Two Round Faced Women each grab technicians and throw them against a nearby wall, dashing their heads against the wall and knocking them out completely! Wang rushes to the side of a truck and opens the door, tossing the driver to the ground and climbing in and searching the vehicle. Stanislav roars and runs over to a limousine. He goes to open the back door, but it’s locked. With ease he punches his hand through the window and tears the entire door off of the frame of the car. With nothing more than an afterthought, Stanislav chucks the door across the area and turns as technicians and workers alike flee. Ivan: ED? JANE REAGAN!! YOU WILL NOT THWART PRAPORSCHIK STANISLAV! I WILL FIND YOU!!!! Objects fly from the van that Ruslan searches and finally, the small Russian himself exits from the vehicle, shrugging. Speedy Riggs searches fallen boxes and checks a few nearby rooms, but is sure to stay a safe distance from most of the physical action. The Two Round Faced Women lift the back door of a trucks trailer and search inside. The camera switches to Ivan as he stands in front of the limousine and glares over the hood at the camera. Ivan: Ed! I am going to find you and your friend tonight! And they will not survive the onslaught of Ivan Stanislav! The Russian Bear will find you! The Soviet Machine will never stop!! Stanislav grabs the front of the limousine and lifts, slowly raising the front end up off of the ground. His massive arms tense up as his chest tightens, however, he lifts the front of the vehicle with relative ease. He lifts until his arms are outstretched above his head. The camera now moves to the right of Ivan at a more profile point of view as Ivan looks at the camera. Ivan: You will not stop me, Ed! I will find you! And then, I will finish you and your hero off, once and for all!! Ivan roars and flips the limousine over. The Red Army members all stop and look to Ivan for instructions. He points to various area’s of the building. Ivan: Fan out! Search the area! We will find them before the night is out! Wang, find Shanda Russell! Alexei and Riggs, go to Moose Malone! The camera zooms in on Ivan’s face.... Ivan: And I will see Jane Reagan herself...... Ivan laughs as the group exits in their respective directions instantly...the camera fades out as the OSW logo disappears....
