Robbie: AT DEATH'S DOOR! THIS ONE'S OVER! YOU'LL SEE AMY AT THE HOSPITAL, TIMMY! Nate: YES! TIMO HAS STREAK LOCKED IN A FIGURE FOUR, PUTTING PRESSURE ON THE MONSTER'S BAD KNEES! Robbie: THAT'S CHEAPER THAN BRANDI WILSON! Black leather trenchcoat, pants and boots, white T-shirt and dark helmet hiding his face. The rider surveys the damage and seems pleased, then he hops on his bike. He revs the engine, once, then twice….then suddenly looks over directly at where Toomes is hiding. Robbie: RUN SEA-BASS!!! HE KNOWS YOU ARE THERE!!! Toomes begins to bite his nails as he tries to sink deeper into the shadows. The camera shifts back to the rider, who shakes his helmet covered head, then points his baseball bat directly at Toomes. Nate: THE RIDER KNOWS HE IS THERE!!!! Toomes squints in fear as the rider once again revs his bike, then popping a wheelie, squeals off into the darkness….leaving the petrified Toomes balled up in the fetal position behind the dumpster. Meanwhile, back in the arena… The crowd begins a "TIMO!" chant, gradually building momentum over the length of the hold, while the commentators spout random, inane comments...and then, with a final burst of strength, Streak kicks out. The blast nearly caves in Timo's chest, but the Samoan Silencer holds tight, and despite the now-awkward position, the Monster they call Meanstreak finally blacks out. Nate: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! We focus on the massive, still form behind him, however...or not quite still, as the Monster opens his left eye, clear and showing no signs of pain or even discomfort, and that evil grin of his spreads across his face. Robbie: I almost feel sorry for that dark-skinned bastard. Nate: LOOK OUT, TIMO! LOOK BEHIND YOU! LOOK OUT! Robbie: Someone count the hang time! Nate: ON DEVIL'S WINGS! TIMO HAS JUST BEEN SENT SKYWARD ON DEVIL'S WINGS! Timo smashes face-first into the mat, tilting his neck and eliciting a loud "pop" -- hopefully just cartillege, and not his entire neck. Once again imitating an accordion, the Samoan Silencer bounces away, rolling and tumbling until he comes to rest with his head and neck on the canvas, and the rest of him propped against the ropes and cage. And then, the boos of the crowd turn to cheers, a turn of events obviously not caused by Streak's raised boot. Tipped off by the noise, the Monster turns around...JUST IN TIME TO BE NAILED IN THE FACE WITH BOB THE STEEL-BARBED WIRE-NAIL CHAIR! Nate: IT'S ED! ED -- AND BOB! Robbie: NOOO! WHAT THE HELL IS HE DOING HERE?! AND WHY DOES HE HAVE BOB?! Steven: EDDIE IS BACK! AND SO IS HIS WEAPON! AND THEY MAY HAVE JUST SAVED TIMO BOLAMBA'S LIFE! Robbie: HOW IN THE HELL DID HE GET INSIDE THE CAGE!!! Nate: HE MUST HAVE BEEN HIDING BENEATH THE RING!!! Meanstreak reels backwards from the huge blow, but Ed allows him no chance of escape, raining another massive shot across the Monster's face, and another. The assault continues rapid-fire, driving Streak all the way across the ring from the fallen Timo, while the fans nearly tear the roof off of the place. Nate: LISTEN TO THAT CROWD!!! Finally, with yet another huge shot, the Hardcore Legend and Commissioner of Old School Wrestling drops back, bounces off the ropes, throws Bob out in front of him...AND DRIVES MEANSTREAK RIGHT THROUGH THE WALL OF THE CAGE WITH A MISSILE DROPKICK THROUGH THE BARBED WIRE CHAIR! Nate: HOLY SH-- ED JUST PUT THE MONSTER RIGHT THROUGH THE SIDE OF A STEEL CAGE! Steven: I'VE NEVER SEEN HIM DO ANYTHING LIKE THAT! I DIDN'T KNOW HE COULD DO THAT! On top of the shattered chain-link wall, a bloodied and dazed Meanstreak groans and begins trying to pull the net off of him, but without the support of a steel frame, the fence is more of a steel net. Frustrated and now tangled, the Monster roars out a threat, and redoubles his efforts. Nate: MEANSTREAK IS TRAPPED INSIDE THE WALL OF THE STEEL CAGE! AND TIMO BOLAMBA IS GOING FOR THE WIN!
