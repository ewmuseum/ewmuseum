The camera scene fades in to reveal a a shot of the building that the Old School Wrestling offices are located: a somewhat low-key, six story office building, emblazoned the OSW logo. As the shot fades in, the scene shifts to a shot of a door, upon which a placard hangs: --JANE REAGAN-- ---OSW Owner--- A small OSW graphic in the lower right hand corner of the screen reads “Recorded Earlier” Sauntering down the hall with sunglasses on and wearing a stylish business suit is OSW Superstar, "Classic" Clay Vendetta, with a smirk on his face and briefcase in his hand. Looking confident, Vendetta walks into Reagan's room, without even so much as a knock, and sets his briefcase down on the desk. Reagan, interrupted, stops throwing darts at a picture of Sebastian “Switchblade” Toomes which has been pinned to the wall, and sneers at Vendetta. Noting the television set turned on in the corner of the room, Vendetta covers his eyes... "Wait, wait, let me guess. Must be... curling, right? Or hockey. I'm right, aren't I? Something Canadian." "It's downhill skiing," Reagan replies, "and you're late." "I had to get my files organized," Vendetta says. "As you've been made aware, I have a few complaints that need to be addressed." "I'm aware, but do not really care." "Oh you will care,” scoffs Vendetta. “Well, let's get the non-essential business out there first. I've been informed that Coca-Cola is negotiating, through your office, to have Twisted Metal endorse their product. Need I remind you that the full name of their top-selling beverage is in fact "Coca-Cola Classic", and I think it's only fair that OSW should throw their entire weight into getting a pitchman for the product with charisma, class, and most important, the name of their product proudly worn and honored, right? I mean, it's not "Lugbolt Cola", is it? No. Let's be realistic: is the image you want to present for OSW represented by a couple of malfunctioning, rusted, buggy machines? I didn't think so." "Mr. Vendetta, this is trivial," Reagan says in a disinterested manner, closing her notebook computer and facing Clay directly. "Old School Wrestling makes public the details of its business when it chooses to do so, and you're acting based on rumor and speculation. I recommend you take it up with Coca-Cola if there's a problem and stop wasting my time." Clay scowls back at Reagan, his temper visibly flaring... but composing himself, he continues: "Well, then, MISS Reagan, let me proceed to something which assuredly *isn't* a waste of your time. There's a match scheduled tonight: a large battle royal for one of the spots in OSW's World Title match." "That's correct," says Jane. "But why?" Clay asks. "This match is a farce and deserves to be canceled. What you've got right here is the biggest draw in the history of OSW, and I've been consistently getting the shaft from Old School Wrestling. Take last week, right? The match ends, and the referee decides in favor of yours truly, Clay Vendetta, and I'm *still* not credited for the win? Reagan, if you go overturning every referee's decision, why even keep them on the payroll? That's ridiculous, and you know it." Reagan sighs and rolls her eyes. Vendetta continues. "Now, then. There are 11 competitors in tonight's match, and a true Classic... myself. Out of those 11, let's look at who's going to be bringing in the ratings, huh?" Pulling out a match listing, Vendetta starts pointing out the different competitors. "You've got Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak from the Red Army; and the Olympics have proved, no American likes to watch whiny, bitchy Russians and their Communist buddies." I'm not even going to GET into the pair of Canadian buffoons, who shouldn't be in this country OR this sport... and that brings us down to seven others. Richie Syxx can't be in the main event of a pay-per-view... the viewers can't see past his hair. Then you've got Sebastian Toomes, who's very presence on a card guarantees at least nine boycotts. This guy pisses *everybody* off! He's a pox on the ratings. “What the heck is a pox,” asks Reagan. Vendetta ignores here, simply continuing his rant. “Then after that, a pair of freaks in Azrael Cain and Rhett Locke,” figures Vendetta. “Jane, nobody likes freaks. They scare the kiddies. And of course, 9 and 10 are the old-timers club, Doug Troy and Trashcan Man. Please, Jane. Won't the ring be crowded enough WITHOUT having to wheel their respirators and oxygen tanks in there too?” Jane rolls her eyes, while Vendetta continues to ramble. “Last but not least, your "mystery competitor". Part of the grand scheme to screw me over, I'm sure. Who's it gonna be, Jane? Dudley Do-Right? Let's face it... I know you've got it out for me, but it's very simple... I'm money, Jane. Sheer ratings dynamite, and you just can't deal with it. Tonight, just forget it... I'm going to win anyway, so why not just call the match? You know, be a good girl, give some of the guys the night off, make everyone's lives easier, how about it, huh?" Jane adjusts her reading glasses and shoots Vendetta a serious look. "Are you quite finished?" "I believe so, yes." "Good," Jane says snippily. "First and foremost, Mr. Vendetta, you're damn lucky that I decided to cut you slack and not fine or suspend you for your actions on last week's Slam. Your actions were far beyond out of line, and believe me, if you have another incident like that I will make your stay here in OSW a short one. Now then, as far as your "conspiracy" is concerned... I suggest you spend less time watching Oliver Stone movies and spend more time preparing for your matches, because whether you like it or not, you'll be stepping in against eleven of the finest wrestlers in the business tonight, and abrasive as you are, I'm sure each and every one of them will be looking to throw you beyond the third row. Now then, I suggest you stop wasting my precious time, leave my office and get ready for your match." Vendetta is furious, slamming his fist down on the table without saying a word... grabbing his briefcase and heading for the door, he shouts back "This isn't over, Reagan! This power trip of yours is going to be a short one!" before steaming out the door, slamming it behind him. Reagan rolls her eyes and gets back to her work as the camera cuts away...
