Ed Real Name: Ed Birthdate: 8-30-1973 Hometown: Bakersfield, CA Height: 6'3 Weight: 237 lbs. Nicknames: The Hardcore Legend, the Commish Status: Face Manager(s): Nil Current Teams: Nada Entrance Music: 'Food for the Brain' by One Minute Silence Information Wrestling Style: Hardcore Signature Moves: Running Dropkick, Spinesnapper Stretch (kneels on opponent's back, pulls opponent's head back), top-rope leg drop, diving clothesline, dive over ropes to outside Set Up Move: Clutching Clothesline (grabs opponent's shoulder, clotheslines him in throat ) Finisher: Brink of Insanity (Electric Chair Drop/Sky High Facebuster) OSW Title History: None OSW Award History: OSW Commissioner, Feb. 2002 - Present; Acting CEO of OSW, Apr. 20, '02 - May 4, '02 Other Federations: UFW, PCW Other Federation Awards: PCW Hall of Famer Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio A well-built, solid young man with a shaggy mane of golden brown hair and a thick goattee, Ed seems to be 'just another' wrestler. As he's proven time and time again, however, he's not: he's the Hardcore Legend, baby, and he's got 'it'. Ed began his career in the now-defunct UFW, gaining status as a talented individual who would do damned near anything to make a good match. Unfortunately, this eventually led to a broken leg, which sidelined Ed for the rest of his UFW career, but gave him invaluable experience at helping to run a federation. Finally, though, Ed returned to the ring for Primetime Championship Wrestling, launching himself into a now-legendary stable feud against Alan Kriegman and the Think Tank. Several classic matchups followed, and despite a somewhat shoddy workrate and growing lack of enthusiasm, the Hardcore Legend began to carve a niche for himself that no one else has yet filled. The rest of his story is well-documented, and can be viewed in his biography for the PCW Hall of Fame. It should be noted that, several months after his induction, he finally returned from retirement. Re-establishing himself as a force to be reckoned with, he fought hard, and eventually began crawling up the ladder to square off in a four-way feud between Boda, Clyde, and the then-PCW World Champion, Ivan Stanislav. His return was brought short, however, when Ivan brutally injured the Hardcore Legend's spine, herniating several disks and putting Ed's in-ring return in question. But now, he's back, in power, and fighting desperately to keep his new home from falling completely under Jane Reagan's grasp. Despite misgivings, he's working together with old enemies to combat this menace, and is now stepping back into the ring to make the fight personal. During his rehabilitation, Ed dropped over twenty pounds, most of it excess fat. Though thinner and lighter, the Hardcore Legend is in the best condition of his career, and this could mean plenty of impressive high spots in his near future -- if he survives. Currently, following a fierce battle with the Monster they call Meanstreak at OSW Head on Collission, Ed's on injured reserved with another injury to his spine.
