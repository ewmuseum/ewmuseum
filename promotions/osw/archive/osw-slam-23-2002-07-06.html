The scene opens prior to Slam kicking off, with a small clock in the lower, right hand corner of the screen indicating the time is 8:50 pm EST, a full 10 minutes before our actual time of 9:00 pm EST. Below the small digital clock is an OSW: Filmed Earlier graphic. As the scene fades up, it appears we are on some kind of loading dock, probably behind the Tulsa Convention Center, which just so happens to be the location of tonight’s broadcast of Saturday Night Slam. A car can be seen pulling into the dock, just near a set of steel stairs leading up to the bay where the cameraman is standing. From out of the blue rental car steps former IOW World Heavyweight Champion, Perfection Personfied, Kory Storm. Taking his bag from the back seat of the car, Kory shuts both the driver's side front and back doors, locks the car and slowly walks up the stairs. On the other side of the loading bay, a jeep rolls up and parks in the parking spaces near the arena's outer fence. From inside the care emerges none other than Michel Krieg, dissident member of the Red Army. Like Storm, he takes a kit bag from the back seat of his jeep and walks up similar stairs 50 meters or so away from where Storm is standing as he walks towards him. As Michel walks along the gangway towards Storm and the cameraman, a yellow taxi screeches to a halt just below the bay as a door slams and a man steps away from the cab as it pulls off again with a screeching of tires. That man is happens to be noneother than the Master of Pain, Frankie Bones.
