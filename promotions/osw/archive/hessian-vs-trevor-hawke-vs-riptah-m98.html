Hessian vs. Trevor Hawke vs. Riptah

2002-05-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hessian vs. Trevor Hawke vs. Riptah Written: 23/05/2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam! Right now we’re heading into one of the most brutal feuds in OSW. For weeks now the tag teams of Da Westsidaz, Civil Disobedience and the current OSW Tag Team Champions, Heresian Bloodlust have been at each other’s throats in a war for the very titles Hessian and Cain hold. Robbie: Of course it was last week on Slam! Howard Porter signed that kick-ass match for Ascension, and now Hessian and Cain are going to TEAR THEM SUBURBAN BITCHES A NEW A-HOLE!!! Steven: Of course…tonight we can only expect a massacre! Nate: Well before the crowds get too rowdy let’s get this thing underway, to Earl we go! Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest is a Triple Threat Match, scheduled for one fall. First, making his way to the ring, being accompanied by Seth Masters and hailing from Aurora, Illinois, weighing in at 224lbs…TREVOR HAWKE!!!!!!!!! ‘Bounce’ by System Of A Down hits the PA and Trevor and Seth emerge from behind the curtain to a wave of boos and hisses, as opposed to the normal cheering and yipping that Civil Disobedience receive from the crowds. Strolling down the ramp, Trevor and Hawke flip off the booing crowd members, seemingly careless of the fan’s feelings. Nate: The fans not happy with Trevor Hawke or Seth Masters! Robbie: Well you wouldn’t be happy either if your teenybopping idols turned on you! Steven: They aren’t teenyboppers they just masturbate a lot… Seth and Trevor hit the ring and parade around it mocking the crowds, when the booing is drowned out as “Ante Up Remix” MOP & Funkmaster Flex hits the P.A. Earl: And his opponent…hailing from Bakersfield, California, being accompanied by Short Circuit and Daisy. Weighing in at 290lbs…RIPTAH!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Riptah and Short Circuit charge from through the curtain, as Seth leaves the ring. Behind Da Westsidaz, Daisy emerges, exciting a larger cheer from the already ovating crowd. Riptah slides into the ring and takes Trevor down, and they begin brawling, while from ringside Short Circuit, Daisy and Seth cheer on their respective teammates. Nate: Well Riptah and Trevor are going at it before the final team arrive… Robbie: WOOHOO!!! And we know who they are!!! Earl: AND FINALLY! Being accompanied by Azrael Cain and Minion the Imp! Hailing from Detroit, Michigan, and weighing in at 350lbs…he is one half of the OSW Tag Team Champions, HESSIAN!!! ‘Forever Hate You’ by Deicide begins blaring through the arena and the fans cheering turns to immense hate-filled booing, as the strobe lighting hits and from behind the curtain emerges Hessian, followed by Cain and Minion. Robbie: Isn’t Azrael supposed to be unconscious? Nate: He WAS unconscious! Steven: You never know with these heretics…always so unpredictable! Hessian calmly paces along the stage, eyeing up the brawling men in the ring. Removing his tag title and handing it to Azrael, Hessian takes off for the ring, charging down like a raging bull. Minion follows him, while Azrael calmly walks down to ringside. Ding…ding…ding! Nate: Well the match is officially underway now, with Hessian and Riptah and Trevor Hawke going at it one-on-one-on-one! Steven: Yeah and with Short Circuit, Seth, Azrael, Daisy and MINION at ringside you know this could explode at any moment! In the ring, referee for the bout Betty Lovejoy stands by as Hessian lunges at Riptah, pulling him off of Trevor, and whips him round, executing a quick suplex. Scrambling back to his feet, Hessian grabs Trevor and executes a Stalling Crossed Arms Brainbuster Suplex. Nate: HESSPLEX!!! We’re 4 seconds into the match and already Hessian has one of his signature moves away!!! Robbie: 1 down, 59 to go! Steven: Well after Hessian was eliminated first last week, you gotta believe he’s out for revenge! Hessian roars out and runs at Riptah, kicking Da Westsida in the ribs. Riptah rolls around while Minion and Azrael insult him from ringside. Hessian picks Trevor up and whips him, only to have the Irish whip reversed, sending Hessian flying across the ring. As he bounces off of the ropes Seth lands a hard right to the back of his head, sending Hessian stumbling into a Spinning Heel Kick by Trevor Hawke. Hessian stumbles back, maintaining his balance, while Trevor turns to Riptah, only to be hit with a Standing Side Kick, sending Trevor to the mat. Nate: AFRO-KICK!!! This match is becoming fast-paced! Robbie: Seth Masters, the little cheating bastard! Riptah sees Hessian trying to maintain his balance, and runs off the ropes, charging at Hessian. Riptah goes for the LA X-Press, but Hessian moves out the way, and Riptah dives through the ropes, colliding with Seth Masters and sending them both tumbling to the mats. Nate: OH! Riptah just took out Seth! Robbie: Told ya this is going to get nasty! Steven: No you didn’t… While Riptah and Seth try to recover from the impact, Cain hands the title belts to the time keeper and begins putting the boots to Seth for hitting Hessian, while Minion the Imp sneaks under the ring, and dives out the other end, grabbing Short Circuit and starting a brawl, while Daisy backs off and in the ring Betty Lovejoy stares bimbo-like at a cute guy in the crowd. Steven: BETTY STOP STARING AND DO YOUR FRICKIN’ JOB!!! Nate: Well all hell seems to break loose already!!! Robbie: That’s cos you got Hessian and Cain here!!! WOOHOO!!! In the ring, Hessian picks Trevor up and whips him off the ropes. As Trevor rebounds, Hessian delivers the Fireman’s Carry Gutbuster Drop, sending Trevor crashing into the canvas. Nate: DEVILISH ALLSORTS!!! Robbie: They certainly are! Hessian gets to his feet again and begins trash talking to Trevor, while outside the ring Minion hops up onto the apron, and as Short Circuit turns to him, the Imp grabs him by the throat, and signals for a chokeslam. Robbie: OH MY GOD NO!!!!!!!!! Nate: MINION!!! NO! IS THAT EVEN POSSIBLE!!! Steven: Oh bless him…he’ll never pull it off you know… Robbie: He’s 3’11”, I guess you’re right… Minion squeals out and hoists Short Circuit into the air, then jumps off the apron and slams Shorty into the mats with a devastating Chokeslam… Nate & Robbie & Steven: OH MY GOD!!! MINION JUST CHOKESLAMMED SHORT CIRCUIT!!! HE’S A GODDAMN MONSTER!!! Minion looks to Daisy and begins stalking her as she backs off from the Imp. Meanwhile referee Betty Lovejoy checks her nails. Riptah is pulling himself to his feet as Azrael chokes Seth beside the announce table. Riptah sees Azrael and runs at the Marked Beast, sending him tumbling back with a hard knee to the forehead. Cain rolls around while Riptah sees Hessian’s back turned to him in the ring, and slides back into the match. Nate: Well the ringside antics have simmered down for now, but goddamn Minion chokeslammed Short Circuit!!! Robbie: I’d call that Move of the Week…too bad Minion isn’t a signed wrestler! Steven: Oh you evil little man! Riptah creeps up behind Hessian, and quickly executes The Westside Slam 1 on the Goliath, sending Hessian crashing to the mat next to Seth. Nate: God this match is getting heavy! Riptah kicks Hessian in the ribs, then goes to the second turnbuckle. As Riptah sizes Hessian up, Azrael Cain runs around, hops up onto the apron and pushes Riptah off the turnbuckle, sending him tumbling down onto the mat. Short Circuit sees Cain and runs around the ring to Cain. Short Circuit sizes Cain up and delivers a high dropkick to Cain’s knees, sending the big man crashing down off the apron. Shorty mounts Cain ane begins hitting him with lefts and rights, while Minion the Imp makes his way around the ring to Seth, who is walking over to a recuperating Cain. Nate: Oh no here he goes again! Minion the Imp: NYAAAAAAAAAAH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Minion leaps into the air and dropkicks Seth, merely stunning Trevor Hawke’s fellow suburbanite. As Seth falls, Minion picks himself up and slaps some sense into Cain, then jumps onto Seth and begins pounding at him. Nate: HAHA! Minion is here and he is kicking ass! First Short Circuit and now Seth!!! Who’s next? Robbie: You if you don’t keep yer hat on… In the ring Hessian and Trevor get back to their feet and straight into a brawl. Hessian pounds on Trevor with his steel gauntlets, which are blocked by Trevor. Eventually the steel overcomes flesh, and Hessian plants Trevor with a Full Nelson Slam. Riptah gets back to his feet and grabs Hessian, whipping the tag champ across the ring. As Hessian rebounds off the ropes, Short Circuit slides into the ring, and Da Westsidaz connect with a Double Spear. Nate: FORCEABLE ENTRY!!! DA WESTSIDAZ JUST HIT HESSIAN WITH THE FORCEABLE ENTRY!!! Robbie: DON’T SHOUT SO FRICKIN’ LOUD!!! Steven: BAH!!! Short Circuit slides out of the ring as Riptah covers Hessian and Betty Lovejoy finally gets her ass in gear. 1… 2… 3 NO!!! Hessian kicks out and begins thrashing out as Azrael slides into the ring and knocks Riptah off of his tag partner. Betty Lovejoy puts a finger up to Cain to warn him, but receives a loud roar in her face instead, and backs off into the corner to let the riot begin. Nate: Well Betty Lovejoy has lost control of this match it seems… Robbie: Did she ever have control? Steven: Does she even care??? Azrael grabs Riptah and immediately whips him off the ropes. As Riptah runs back, Hessian Back Body Drops him, but as Cain readies himself to execute the Death Valley Driver he is thrust into the turnbuckle by a missile dropkick from Short Circuit off the other turnbuckle. Nate: OH! Riptah just narrowly missed getting The Heretic’s Wrath there! Steven: Short Circuit was smart there! Short Circuit begins punching at Hessian’s gut, but the Goliath replies with a large forearm to the head. Seth and Minion battle on the outside, with Seth reducing Minion to his bitch. Daisy watches from ringside as in the ring Trevor gets back to his feet and dropkicks Hessian to the knee, sending the big man staggering. Trevor bounces off of the ropes and runs again for a dropkick, but is suddenly caught by Azrael around the throat. Robbie: GO CAINY GO!!! GO!!! GO!!! Cain hoists Trevor into the air and almost throws him out of the ring, Trevor landing on the mats outside with a sickening thud! Nate: Trevor Hawke just chokeslammed out of the ring by Azrael Cain!!! And Seth is still having trouble with Minion!!! Riptah and Short Circuit make their way back to their feet and run at Heresian Bloodlust. Short Circuit hits Cain with a dropkick to the chest while Riptah stuns Hessian with a diving shoulder block. At ringside, Minion gains the advantage and blocks the thumping fists Seth is landing on Minion. The Imp deflects another fist then with a burst of adrenaline thrusts his fist out and smacks Seth square in the nads. Nate: THE JAB OF SATAN!!! JAB OF SATAN!!! MINION WITH THE JAB OF SATAN ON SETH MASTERS!!! Robbie: GO MINION!!!!!!!!! Steven: HAHAAA!!! MINION! THE CROWD ARE GOING NUTS FOR THE LITTLE GUY!!! Crowd: MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! Minion grabs Seth and swings him around, Masters landing on top of the announce table. The crowd cheer a little bit louder, as Minion’s eyes become entranced and he gazes at the top turnbuckle. Nate: Oh god no… Robbie: What the hell is he doing on OUR table??? Steven: Erm…I’m going to move… Nate & Robbie: Good idea… As Minion pulls himself up onto the apron and into the ring, Heresian Bloodlust battle Da Westsidaz in a fistfight. Short Circuit and Azrael Cain brawl while Riptah and Hessian exchange forearms. Trevor meanwhile is coming to his senses on the mats, with Betty Lovejoy checking on him from the ropes. Short Circuit and Azrael battle back and forth, until Azrael pulls back for a clothesline, prompting Short Circuit to duck, and in turn causing Azrael to swing forth his mighty arm…and hitting Betty Lovejoy, sending the bimbo referee crumpling into the corner. Nate: NO! Now there’s no ref! Steven: Was there ever… Robbie: Touché… Azrael roars out in frustration, and as he turns around is confronted with Short Circuit on the top turnbuckle. Shorty dives off and lands on Azrael’s shoulders, then swoops down for the hurricanrana, but Cain catches him and pulls the 170lbs Westsida back up into a vertical position. The crowd let out a whoop as Cain turns to the turnbuckle Shorty just flew off of, and pulls Short Circuit up into powerbomb position. Nate: CAIN’S GOING FOR IT!!! Robbie: He’s going to kill Short Circuit!!! Cain pulls Short Circuit up a little more then drives him into the turnbuckle with a devastating powerbomb! Nate: THE NINTH GATE!!! THE NINTH GATE!!! CAIN HIT THE NINTH GATE ON SHORT CIRCUIT!!! As Cain spouts insults at Short circuit, Trevor slides back into the ring and begins brawling with Cain. Meanwhile, Minion has scaled the turnbuckle to the top. The crowd go nuts for the 3’11” purple monster they call Minion. Nate: Oh god he isn’t… Robbie: YES HE IS!!! Minion gives the Jimmy “Superfly” Snuka gesture, staring at Seth, but as he bends to jump, he freezes suddenly and begins sniffing in the air. Nate: Wha-…what the hell is Minion doing? Robbie: JUMP MINION JUMP!!! DO IT!!! MOVE OF THE YEAR!!! Minion sniffs some more, then his eyes light up suddenly and he turns to the entranceway, and gasps. Minion the Imp: FIRESNATCH~! FIRESNATCH~! FIRESNATCH~! FIRESNATCH~! FIRESNATCH~! FIRESNATCH~! FIRESNATCH~! FIRESNATCH~!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Indeed, Minion points to the stage jumping up and down as the figure of Aimz appears in front of the curtain, next to none other than Sebastian Toomes. Nate: MINION SPOTTED THE INTRUDERS!!! SEABASS AND AIMZ ARE HERE!!! Robbie: More like he smelled it! Steven: Damn I wish I had Minion’s nose… Minion jumps up and down pointing at Aimz and SeaBass, trying to keep his balance on the turnbuckle. SeaBass and Amy notice Minion and hide behind the curtain, as Minion calls for Hessian’s attention. Hessian tries to fistfight Riptah, and eventually lands a throat thrust and looks to Minion. Hessian: WHAT??? Minion the Imp: AIMZ AND SEABASS ARE ON THE STAGE!!! Hessian walks closer to Minion as the crowd cheers louder. Hessian: WHAT???!!! Minion the Imp: AIMZ AND SEABASS ARE ON THE STAGE!!! Hessian turns but no one is there, however Riptah is charging at Hessian. The Goliath turns to grapple with Da Westsida, but in turn nudges Minion, who turns back to the announce table and begins wobbling. Nate: OH NO!!! THE LITTLE MAN IS GONNA GO!!! Minion tries to regain his balance, but still he tips forward evermore. As Seth stirs on the announce table, Minion comes to a decision and capitalises on his wobbling, and flies off of the turnbuckle, spinning 360º in the air and crashing into the announce table, over Seth Masters. A huge crash rings throughout the arena as Minion and Seth lay among the wreckage. Crowd: MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! MINION! Nate: MINION THE IMP!!! MINION JUST BROKE SETH MASTERS IN TWO WITH A 360º SPLASH!!! MINION!!! MINION!!! MINION!!! Robbie: MINION THE IMP JUST BROKE HIS AND SETH’S BODIES IN TWO!!! AND HERE COME AIMZ AND SEABASS NOW!!! Steven: MINION! NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! They’re lying right at our feet and you’re cheering for him??? Robbie: LISTEN TO THE OVATION!!! The crowd go ballistic as Minion lies over the broken body of Seth Masters, and down the ramp Switchblade and Aimz charge. Switchblade slides into the ring and immediately begins helping Trevor with Cain, while Aimz runs around the ring to the announce table. She grabs Seth and pulls him from the wreckage, spitting on Minion as she does…though even in his unconscious state, his long, greenish tongue manages to lick all the spit off of his face. Robbie: HAHAHAHA!!! MINION YOU DAWG!!! Nate: God bless Minion… In the ring, Switchblade and Trevor get the upper hand on Cain, and whip him across the ropes. As he returns, they double dropkick him in the knees, sending Cain falling to the mat yelling like a Grizzly caught in a bear trap and out of the ring. Riptah whips Hessian across the ropes, but he runs into Switchblade and Trevor, who duck immediately, but then, using all their strength, force the 7’2” Satanist over their heads and soaring over the top rope. Hessian lands on his back on the mats, emitting another sickening thud and exciting a gasp from the crowd. Nate: Trevor and SeaBass cleaning house here! Robbie: Aimz has Seth at ringside, he’s looking OK, and thank god Minion is only 79lbs!!! Seth shakes his head and slides into the ring with Aimz. SeaBass and Aimz put the boots to Short Circuit, who still hasn’t recovered from The Ninth Gate, while Civil Disobedience stalk Riptah. Nate: Finally Civil Disobedience ‘bout to kick some shiznit up in da hizouse! Robbie: Nate, what have you been smoking??? Nate: HEY! A 3’11” Imp just flew from the top rope and took out one half of the first ever former Tag Champs!!! I’m buzzing! Riptah thinks his options over, and then with a muffled cry, he rushes Civil Disobedience, only to be flapjacked by Trevor and Seth, and then hit with the Double Flatliner as he falls. Nate: TRENDSETTER!!! CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE JUST HIT RIPTAH WITH TH-… Robbie: TRENDSETTER!!! Nate: Damn you’re interruptions… Civil Disobedience begins punching and kicking at Riptah, while Aimz and SeaBass pick Short Circuit up, and with a kick to the mid-section, Toomes plants Shorty with the Jump Swinging Brainbuster DDT. Nate: Switchblade just planked the little Short Circuit with… Robbie: FUTURE SHOCK!!! Steven: That’s getting annoying… Nate: BOTH OF YOU STOP STEALING MY LINES!!! Steven & Robbie: Lines? Short Circuit rolls out the ring, leaving Aimz, Switchy and Civil Disobedience with Riptah. All at once the four look at each other, then begin a gang attack on Da Westsida. Stirring on the announce table, Minion finally opens his eyes and begins moving, while Daisy tries desperately to revive referee Betty Lovejoy. Nate: Daisy doing her best to revive the ref in hope she’ll stop this slaughter… Robbie: Betty isn’t unconscious she’s just checking the inside of her eyelids for like…guy’s phone numbers or something… Steven: Well whatever, I can see something peeking out that shirt… Nate: Yes Steven, it’s called a-… Robbie: No Nate, don’t give him the satisfaction! Riptah is continually beaten down on by the four, when Daisy enters the ring and runs up to Switchblade, and slaps the former Heavyweight Champ across the back of the head, and proceeds to beat down oh him. While Aimz kicks hell out of Riptah, Civil Disobedience turn and watch as Toomes grabs Daisy by her hair and begins staring evilly into her eyes. Nate: Uh oh, what’s Toomes got planned here? Robbie: I don’t know but Daisy is a fine piece of poon… Steven: I could say the same for Sebastian… Nate: Wait, what’s this! SeaBass begins shouting profanities at Riptah’s woman, while Civil Disobedience looks on. As Aimz kicks Riptah in the ribs, she suddenly hears something before her. Voice: Firesnaaaaaaaatch~! Oh Fiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiresnaaaaaaaatch~! Aimz looks up in time to see Minion on the turnbuckle, grinning cheekily. Minion the Imp: HEADS UP REDBEARD!!! HERE COMES DADDY! Minion flies off the turnbuckle sideways, arms out ready to be caught by Amy, but instead she moves out of the way, and Minion flies into Seth, Trevor and Sebastian. All three men and Minion fall to the mat, and in the rush Daisy manages to free herself from SeaBass’ grip and exit the ring. Minion tramples over the three men back to his feet, and as they lay lying he strolls up to Aimz. Minion the Imp: Hey…y’know I meant to do that… Aimz: GET AWAY FROM ME YOU LITTLE PURPLE F*CKER!!! Minion shrugs then turns away, but as Amy resumes kicking Riptah in, he turns back and reaches out towards Aimz’ ass. Nate: My god, this Imp has more balls than Ron Jeremy! Robbie: I coulda said that… Minion looks at Aimz’ ass, and at once his Imp mind explodes and he finds himself viciously groping the delicious booty. Aimz turns around and sees Minion…and then Minion screams. Nate: Well Minion got him some…but he’s dead now! Robbie: SONOFABITCH!!! WATCH OUT!!! Robbie and Nate and Steven move as a purple haze flies across the ringside area and crashes into the commentator’s chairs. Lying twisted and contorted in a pile of chairs, Minion sighs and shakes his head in pain. Minion the Imp: Oh yeah she wants me… In the ring, SeaBass and Civil D get back to their feet, and look to Aimz. Aimz points to Minion, and ignoring the Imp, all four individuals get back to beating on Riptah. However all at once Toomes, Trevor and Seth are spun around by hands on their shoulders, and knocked to the ground by forearms by Hessian, Cain and Short Circuit! SeaBass and Civil D begin brawling Heresian Bloodlust and Short Circuit. Short Circuit whips Trevor off of the ropes and follows him, dropkicking him out of the ring. As Shorty slides out, Cain kicks Seth in the gut, and then locks him in a pre-suplex position. Slowly turning, Cain uses all of his strength and pulls Seth up by the neck, and plants him into the ring with the Headwhip Suplex, sending Seth rolling out of the ring in the momentum. Nate: These three men are kicking ass! Hessian and SeaBass brawl, but as Hessian goes for the clothesline, Toomes ducks and Hessian’s large arm speeds at Aimz’ throat. Quickly, Hessian deflects the clothesline and spins around, the clothesline degenerating into a forearm, sending SeaBass staggering across the ring and falling out of it. Nate: Well Heresian Bloodlust and Short Circuit have certainly come back into this fight, and now Hessian, Cain and Riptah are alone in the ring with Aimz!!! Aimz looks slightly shocked as Heresian Bloodlust advance on her, though as she backs up she finds herself backed up into the battered, and angry body of Riptah. Heresian Bloodlust step back as Riptah grabs Aimz by the hair. Nate: DON’T HIT A WOMAN!!! Riptah grabs Aimz and hoists her in the air, then runs and throws her out of the ring onto SeaBass. Turning to Nate, Riptah smiles. Riptah: HEY! I never hit her! As Heresian Bloodlust eye up Riptah, the sound of scuffling from outside the ring catches the attention of the three men, who look out of the ring to find Seth and Trevor pulling a large pair of ladders out from under the ring, while Short Circuit has grabbed a pair of baseball bats from the other side of the ring. Hessian and Cain duck as one of the baseball bats flies over the ring and into the hand of Riptah. Heresian Bloodlust escape from the ring and immediately drop and rummage under the ring. As Seth and Trevor enter the ring with the ladders, Short Circuit and Riptah hold their ground at the other end of the ring, and finally Hessian and Cain emerge with their weapons. Robbie: HERESIAN BLOODLUST!!! BARBED WIRE GLOVES!!! HERESIAN BLOODLUST HAVE THEIR BARBED WIRE GLOVES!!! Nate: Those are the same gloves Heresian Bloodlust used to kick ass at the Primetime Central Interfed Pay-Per-View against PCW’s Golden Game, Politically Incorrect and OSW’s own Westsidaz!!! Hessian and Cain enter the ring and stand at another end of the ring, Hessian wearing his barbed wire gloves over his gauntlets, Cain with his barbed wire gloves on over his hands. The three teams stare at each other, not moving. With Da Westsidaz in one corner, Civil D in the other corner, Heresian Bloodlust in another corner and Betty Lovejoy getting back up in the fourth corner, tension is certainly building. Nate: This could explode at any moment…and we need a new table and chairs! Minion the Imp: My god what a sweet ass! Steven: MINION!!! Get off of Robbie’s headset!!! Minion the Imp: Fine the-… Robbie: GIMME THAT BACK YOU BUGGER!!! Then all at once the three teams charge at each other. Bats swinging, Ladders swinging and Barbed Wire Gloves shining. As Heresian Bloodlust charge at both teams, they are floored almost immediately, Hessian by a ladder shot to the gut, Cain by a baseball bat to the thigh. Da Westsidaz swing their bats at Seth and Trevor, but Civil Disobedience with the ladders manage to knock Da Westsidaz to the ground, and land a few more shots to the former 5-Time PCW Tag Champs before the shrill blonde voice of Betty Lovejoy rings out. Betty: HEY! THAT’S LIKE SO NOT ALLOWED!!! YOU’RE ALL DISSERTATED!!! Seth & Trevor: IT’S DISQUALIFIED!!! Betty: Yeah…what-EVER! Civil Disobedience ignores Betty’s call for the DQ and begins pounding away at both tag teams again. Nate: HEY! STOP THAT!!! THOSE WEAPONS ARE FOR THE PPV!!! DAMMIT!!! Robbie: STOP HITTING THE CHAMPS!!! Steven: Hey where’d Minion go? Robbie: Away up the ramp after SeaBass and Aimz I’d imagine! Nate: They left? Steven: Well…no…too late Minion just disappeared behind the curtain! Nate: WHERE THE HELL IS MOOSE AND SECURITY??? STOP THIS ASSAULT!!! Right on cue Moose Malone and his band of wily security guards charge down the ramp and slide into the ring, halting the assault and removing the weapons from the teams in the ring. Nate: GODDAMNIT FINALLY!!! Steven: Yeah! We should go to a commercial and get our table and chairs back while they clear all this mess up!!! Robbie: WOOHOO!!! GO MINION!!!
