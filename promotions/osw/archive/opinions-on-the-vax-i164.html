Opinions on the Vax

2002-07-06
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Opinions on the Vax Written: July 6th, 2002 Nate: Folks, before we head to our next match for the evening, our own Matt Shivan, who has hopefully cleaned himself up, is backstage with the OSW Tag Team Champions, Da Westsidaz, who have a few things to say before their upcoming title defense against Twisted Metal. Matt? The scene flips to the backstage area where the OSW Tag Team Champions are backstage, just about ready for their match. Short Circuit is going through his pre-match ritual of psyching himself up with encouraging, although profanity laden, words. Riptah is talking to Daisy about something when OSW Reporter, Matt Shivan, steps forward cautiously, attempting to get the Tag Champions attention. Matt: Uh…Mr. Riptah….Mr. Circuit….could I…uh…have a word with you two? Riptah looks at the small man, who as usual is scared beyond belief of the professional wrestlers. Riptah looks to Short Circuit, who looks to the belts and shrugs, as if they are obliged to as the Champions. Riptah sighs and removes his sunglasses. Riptah: You come down here all scared and acting like a mouse requesting an interview from us? We’re down here trying to prepare to give out the ass-kicking of a lifetime and you just come and interrupt us? Things don’t work like that when your around us. Next time you come down here with a six-pack and you say, “Excuse me Mr. Williams and Mr. Washington, but I have a deal for you. I’ll give you this six-pack if you help me look better by allowing me to talk to you.” See, it’s tough keeping a job around here if you don’t have a fine rack and nice ass like Shanda, ain’t it dawg? Daisy: Riptah, shut it. Riptah swallows hard, forgetting about the old ball and chain he drags around. He looks back to the interviewer and clears his throat. Riptah: Well, spit it out. Give us some questions. Make it quick and get out of our way, got it you sorry sumbitch? Shivan shivers and tries to sputter the words together. Riptah looks down at the little man and wipes a speck of dust of his gleaming 15lb belt. Shivan regains his composure and professionalism rather quickly and begins the interview. Matt: Well, tonight you two have your first title defense against the bad-luck stricken Twisted Metal. The match was booked by the OSW Commissioner Howard Porter who was revealed as a member of the Vax as a reward for your help. What are your thoughts on the Vax and your match tonight with Twisted Metal? Short Circuit quickly rips the microphone out of Shivan’s hands, who jumps in fear. Short Circuit grins but then realizes he must be serious and turns to the screen. Short Circuit: First of all, all three of those nerds best stay out of our way. They’ve pissed off enough people as it is, so the last people they want to make enemies with is the goddamn OSW Tag Team Champions. They made a big mistake messin’ up Ed like that and I know that whenever he is fit to do so, he’ll come back and kick the livin’ shit out of all of those Star Trek rejects. You don’t connive and shit like that to somebody, it’s not right, y’know? As far as Da Westsidaz are concerned, they’re scum and it’s bad enough they’re here, let alone have booking power with the Commissioner. Short Circuit shakes his head in disgust as Riptah takes the microphone off his hands. Riptah cracks his neck to relax and frowns at the topic at hand. Riptah: It pisses me off to see my dawg Ed get played like that, but it’s not like we can go back in time and change the shit, y’know? But I know Eddie would feel a bit better if he saw us mess up the Vax’s goons here tonight on SLAM! Once we’re done with these two, were going to make sure they don’t come back. They are going to be taking a trip to the scrap heap PERMANENTLY tonight. If the Vax wanted to reward Twisted Metal for all their work they’ve done, they shouldn’t have punished those tin-cans. A title shot ain’t a reward if you’re going up against us. So Ed, we’re going to make you proud tonight by finishing the job you started last week when you trashed these junk-piles. I hope the Vax is watching, so they can remember to steer clear of us. Otherwise, of course, we’ll have to introduce to them that Da Westside is Da Bestside and make sure they don’t forget it. Riptah hands the microphone back to Shivan who straightens his tie. Shivan: Well, are you sure you’re 100%? Two weeks ago you were involved in a brutal match may have been the most challenging match of your careers. I heard that you had a tough time getting your medical releases to defend your titles tonight….Please don’t hurt me. Here, just take my wallet. Riptah rips the microphone out of Shivan’s hand, to a small pop laughing at the humiliation of the OSW Reporter. Shivan trembles as he looks down at his feet, while Riptah keeps his stone serious face. Riptah: Well, it’s either wrestle or lose our jobs so we don’t really have a choice. Besides, were champions. We aren’t about to weasel our way out of challenges. We want competition and I can guarantee you we won’t be hiding every time a challenge comes along. We’re going to kick some ass tonight, whether a doctor says so or not. We’re going to kick ass every night whether it be a broken arm, sore back, barbwire laceration, stroke or amputation. We don’t know how to give up and we won’t give up. You can take that to the bank. Riptah drops the microphone and grins at Shivan who uneasily picks his microphone from the floor. The Tag Champs look at the belts around their waists and high-five each other as they continue the preparation for the proposed ass-kickings they want to give to Twisted Metal. Matt: (wimpering) Back to you guys.
