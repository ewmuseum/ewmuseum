Ivan meets Tempest at her apartment

2002-02-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivan meets Tempest at her apartment Written: Feb 16, 2002 The scene shifts to a hotel lobby, where Ivan Stanislav stands outside of a door. The Russian Bear wears a heavy red military jacket, red pants, yellow gold buttons running up the center of the jacket, and a military hat. A small “Recorded Wednesday” logo appears in the lower, right hand corner of the screen. Ivan- I do not know what I am doing here....grr.... -Stanislav raises his huge fist up to the door and knocks loudly three times, shaking the entire door- Ivan- Hello? -The door opens a crack and Tempest's blue eyes peer out... right at ab level on Ivan. Her eyes travel upward as she tries to see who it is. Tempest- Anyone up there? -Ivan bends at the waist and looks down through the crack- Ivan- Who is this?! -Tempest opens the door, then takes a few paces back, still gazing up. She sweeps back her jet black hair with a smile and waves a little. Her attire is restrained for Tempest, a fuzzy magenta sweater and black vinyl jeans that seem to have a light holographic pattern in them. Tempest- Um, Hi Ivan. I'm Tempest. -Ivan blinks for a moment, surprised at the... beauty of Tempest in person. He shakes his head and regains his composure, standing to his full 7 feet in height.- Ivan- Praporschik Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav!! -Ivan salutes sternly and looks down at Tempest. She snickers and then hops once hoping to actually look him in the eye and failing miserably. Tempest- Um... delighted. Yeah, that's it. Uh... where's the drill sergeant? -She looks around curiously. Ivan grins to himself and reaches down, catching Tempest in the middle of another jump, by the head. He picks her up with one hand, literally palming her entire head and lifting her to his level, roughly two feet in the air. He squints his eyes as he studies her face.- Ivan- Hmmm.... Tempest- Whooooaaaa! Hey! Put me down! -Tempest scowls, a completely adorable expression on her, and kicks her feet. Ivan's arm shakes as she kicks and he unceremoniously drops her to the floor. She manages to land on her feet and dusts herself off. Ivan puts his hands on his hips.- Ivan- So, we are to go to People's Republic of Congo, huh? -Tempest mutters, 'I heard of being swept off your feet, but this is ridiculous.' Then she clears her throat and speaks up. Tempest- Yeah, a war torn, despotic nightmare... what a spot for a romantic getaway. -She rolls her eyes. Ivan laughs, trying to lighten up, and slaps Tempest on the back, knocking her into the door- Ivan- HA HA HA! That is funny! We will go there and create Communist Revolution, nyet?! -Tempest catches herself on the door and frowns up at him.- Tempest- You don't get out much do ya? -Ivan doesn't seem to get the sarcasm and smiles. He bends at the waist and looks straight into her eyes. Ivan's head dwarfs Tempest’s.- Ivan- But of course Ivan does! He is great Russian soldier! Praporschik!! He goes out and does many many things! And what does little storm do? -Tempest eyes him curiously and one eyebrow ticks up ever so slightly as she smirks.- Tempest- I dance the Lambada. You know what that is? -Ivan recoils, now a couple of feet above Tempest- Ivan- I know of Vladimir Lenin....but I have not heard of thisLambada.... Tempest- Somehow I guessed that. -She chuckled and smiles as a small border collie toddles into the room and runs up to her. She scoops him up and snuggles the pup affectionately. Tempest- Oh and of course I play with my puppy! -Ivan looks down at the collie and then back up at Tempest. He clears his throat.- Ivan- Ehem...right..well, I have personal Russian plane that can take us to Congo, if you would wish to take it. I have a jeep outside.... -Ivan shifts his weight and the floor creaks as he shoots the dog a menacing glance. He doesn’t like the show of affection in the slightest.- Ivan- Ehem..if you can dispose of that...thing...we can get going.... -Tempest smiles and lets the dog down as she glances at Ivan with.- Tempest- Ah, better be nice to Trouble. He's my protector after all. -Ivan bursts into laughter- Ivan- DYAAAHAAAHAAA!!!! PROTECTOR?!! -Tempest plants her hands on her hips.- Tempest- What's so funny about that? -Ivan's laugh melts away as he realizes that maybe he was the only one that thought it was funny- Ivan- Uh...well.....never mind. Shall we go tiny storm? Tempest- Yes and.. oopsie, looks like Trouble's living up to his name. -She tried to suppress her laughter as the little dog lifts his leg on Ivan's massive boots. Ivan roars, stumbling backwards and putting a sizable hole in the wall with his back. The dog scurries away with yap that almost sounds like laughter.- Ivan- BWAA! -Tempest sighs.- Tempest- Ya know, I can see this relationship will take some work... preferably by a contractor. -Ivan composes himself and clears his throat. He walks back over to Tempest and places a huge hand on her shoulder, perhaps the most physical motion he has ever done, save for violence. Her shoulder droops slightly under the weight and she chuckles quietly.- Ivan- Uh...shall we...go? -Tempest smiles a little and scoops up her coat, a long black velvet one with glittery purple fluff at the neck and follows him. Tempest- Yes let's. This... should be interesting.
