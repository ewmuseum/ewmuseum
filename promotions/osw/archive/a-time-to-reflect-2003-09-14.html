A Time To Reflect

14 September 2003

by 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#171) by 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst, dated 14 September 2003, written from Hotel Room in Chicago., from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
The moments awaiting my big second round match in the Universal Title Tournament are upon me. I have the butterflies in my stomach and I know that I can do this. But I can’t help thinking about the last Slam. I really flipped out at Gibbo, that’s not like me. I shouldn’t be here really. I should be in the gym or something, getting perfected before the match. Instead I’m in my hotel room, lying in the hard, uncomfortable, cardboard-like bed pulling the sheets over my chest. The lights are turned off as I try to drift off to sleep but can’t, so instead I flick through the endlessly boring and ridiculous television programmes that are showing from the 14-inch set sat in front of me. It’s times like this that I should be preparing for my match, but in reality I think it’s time to reflect. Maybe I shouldn’t have flipped out like that, but it’s just Gibbo has been so selfish lately. He’s so busy scouting that monkey Matt Register that I don’t think he even cares about the rest of us anymore. They might be irritating, but at least The Solution has some respect for the team. Matt Register is a snake, I don’t trust him. There’s no use, the stiff mattress digs into my back and I just can’t get comfortable. So I flap the sheets over and roll off the side of my bed, onto my feet. The floor has only a thin crinkled carpet on it and I feel the coldness of the concrete on the soles of my feet as I step across the room towards the fridge. These hotel rooms are good y’know, they come with self-catering and everything. I pull open the fridge and grab a can of beer, shut the door and head back to bed. Sitting back in bed I open up the can and take a swig of the amber nectar, letting the cool refreshing liquid slide down my throat. Why is Gibbo so desperate to hire Matt Register anyway? Everyone knows he’s just going to tear us apart, but Gibbo just can’t see it. He just can’t see it all coming. He should hire people that we can trust, people that are hungry and want to be a part of Power Play and not just a part of a faction that he can manipulate. People like Jude Kensington. He might swear a lot, but really, is that really so bad? Is it worse than someone that disrespects the stable constantly and has already said he doesn’t want to be a part of a group that are ‘as stable as a sack of water’, I mean at least Jude would have said ‘sack of shit’ right? Jude would be an excellent addition to the stable. He’s the next HUGE star! Not like this Register, who may be having a nice time as Old School champion, but he’ll get his comeuppance eventually and will be nothing. I mean, I hate to admit it, but I’d rather Cisco had beaten him in that Old School Title match, and I don’t think Gibbo should have interfered for that guy’s benefit. I take another swig from the can and flick through a few more channels. The flickering from the lights makes me blink as the contrast from the darkness plays havoc with my eyes. Another commercial for exercise equipment…? These things are just big elastic bands for Christ’s sake! You can quite easily knock one up yourself for a couple of quid, so why spend $45.98 on one? It makes no sense to me. Well, let’s just hope Gibbo comes to his senses, and quick. Because I feel this fort is crumbling and Gibbo just can’t see it. I take the last swig of my beer, place it on the floor and lie down pulling the covers over me once more. Grabbing the remote I switch off the telly. It’ll be fine. Gibbo will come good in the end, Dave. You’ll see…
