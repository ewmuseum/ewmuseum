In the ring, Meanstreak roars out at Ivan to come get him, but Ivan continues to back up the ramp. The crowd is enraged. Debris is not only flying into the ring, but at the Red Army as they back up the ramp. In the ring, Meanstreak uses his off hand to make a throat cutting motion, then the Monster puts Tempest up across his shoulders, with her face and bosom facing the ceiling. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Robbie: MEANSTREAK HAS TEMPEST SET UP FOR AT DEATH’S DOOR!!!! Nate: SOMEONE HAS TO SAVE HER!!! Ivan starts to walk towards the ring again, but Walczak grabs him, while Alexei slaps him across his chiseled jaw and tries to talk sense to him. In the ring, however, it’s a different story, as The Monster They Call Meanstreak roars, then quickly and powerfully rotates his shoulders and wait, planting Tempest onto the canvas, face and torso first via the deadly reverse Death Valley Driver he calls At Death’s Door. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Tempest bounces sickeningly off the mat, her neck twisting violently, and lays, not moving. Streak turns and roars to the crowd as Doug Troy and Tempest lay crushed at his feet. Nate: TEMPEST IS HURT!!! SOMEONE GET THE PARAMEDICS OUT HERE!!! Robbie: SHE SHOULD STAYED IN THE KITCHEN!!! Nate: MEANSTREAK HAS GONE TOO (BLEEPING) FAR!!! HE HAS LOST ALL CONTROL!!!! STANISLAV IS AN UTTER COWARD!!! The Jumbotron shuts off, leaving us with a stunned crowd, Stanislav, and Tempest. Nate: RENO DAVIS HAS SHOWN THE FOOTAGE OF IVAN STANISLAV WHEN HE ABANDONED TEMPEST WEEKS AGO!! Robbie: THAT STUNTED SCUMBAG!!!! HE COULDN’T JUST LET THE MONKEY HAVE HIS MOMENT IN THE SUN!!! Nate: WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT?!?! Ivan stands in horror, dumbfounded as he stares at the Jumbotron and Davis, on the entryway, stops the tape. Tempest stands in complete shock. Her eyes are frozen wide and she stares at the now still image of Ivan with his back turned as he headed up the ramp. Slowly, she blinks and turns toward Ivan. Nate: Looks like Tempest wants some answers!! Ivan blinks and leans over the rope, trying to talk to Tempest. Alexei Ruslan rushes down the entryway, ignoring Davis who smiles from ear to ear. Tempest backs away from the ring, shaking her head. Then she turns and breaks into a run up the ramp, blowing past Alexei Ruslan and then Reno Davis, without stopping. Nate: SHE LEFT!! TEMPEST LEFT HIM!! Ivan roars and looks at Toomes just as Ruslan hops to the apron. Ivan goes to exit the ring but Ruslan points at Ivan to pin Toomes! Steven: It looks like they’re arguing!! Ivan looks over Ruslan’s shoulder as Tempest disappears. He goes to step over the ropes but Ruslan pushes Ivan back in the ring and points at Toomes! Ivan roars as the two men begin to get into a war of words. Nate: Ivan Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan are not seeing eye to eye!! From behind Ivan, Toomes stirs. He groggily looks around and notices that Ivan is completely preoccupied!! Robbie: Toomes is moving!! Toomes crawls weakly towards Ivan and hooks him in a rollup!! Nate: ROLLUP!!! Ivan roars and rolls backwards! 1…. Ivan limply lays there! 2…. Ivan still does not make an attempt to kick out! 3!!! Nate: NO!!!! Robbie: YEEEEEEEEEEEESSSS!!!! WINNER AND STILL OSW WORLD CHAMPION: SWITCHBLADE Nate: SEBASTIAN TOOMES STEALS VICTORY FROM THE JAWS OF DEFEAT TO RETAIN THE OSW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE!!!! Robbie: AND JUSTICE FOR ALL!!!! The crowd is stunned. Ruslan slaps his forehead, knocking his hat off of his head, and falls to the mat stunned. Toomes breaks the hold and rolls over, completely spent. Ivan gets to his feet, ignoring Toomes and heads out of the ring. Ruslan tries one last time to stop Ivan, but Ivan shoves him out of the way and charges up the entryway in chase of Tempest! Nate: He’s going after her!! Ivan is trying to find Tempest!! Reno Davis, however, has mysteriously disappeared! As Ivan runs backstage and abandons the ring we stay focused on Sebastian Toomes, who yanks the World Title off of referee Pierre Gaston and celebrates in the ring! He stands in the center of the ring with the title over his good shoulder and nods to the fans as if he had it in the bag all the time! Nate: I can’t believe this! Ivan had it won!! Steven: LOOK!!! From the backstage area a motor is heard revving as the Masked Biker, wearing a dark, shielded helmet, rounds the corner and flies down towards the ring! Robbie: NOOOOOOOOO! RUN SEABASS!!! RUN!!!! Steven: It’s the Masked Biker!! He has Sebastian Toomes trapped in the ring! Toomes gasps and looks around the ring, trying to find a way to get past the Masked Biker. The angry fans don’t look very promising, however. The Masked Biker stops outside the ring and hops onto the apron. Toomes puts his title down and readies himself for the Biker, who climbs under the bottom rope and points at Toomes! Nate: GOOD GAWD!!! TOOMES AND THE MASKED BIKER ARE GOING TO GO AT IT….RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!!! Robbie: NO FAIR!!! TOOMES IS WORN OUT FROM HIS BATTLE WITH STANISLAV!!! Toomes yells for the Biker to take his helmet off. The Biker points to himself, and then to his helmet. Toomes nods as the Biker looks to the fans as if to ask if they’d like for him to reveal himself! The crowd responds loudly as The Biker turns to Toomes and reaches up to his helmet. Nate: FOLKS! We are about to find out who the Masked Biker that has hounded Sebastian Toomes for weeks!! The Biker grabs at his helmet and slowly pulls it off revealing…. Nate: ANDREW ACE HAWK!!!! Steven: THE LEGEND FROM THE CCW!!! Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! Steven: BOCA!!!!!!!!!! Toomes blinks and drops his title in shock as Andrew Ace Hawk yells at Toomes! Nate: ANDREW ACE HAWK IT THE MASKED BIKER!! THE MENTOR OF SWITCHBLADE HAS BEEN THE ONE TRYING TO DESTROY HIM!!! The crowd is in a frenzy at the site of the CCW Hall of Famer. Hawk locks Toomes in the Harley Grip!! Nate: THE HARLEY GRIP!!! HAWK HAS LOCKED THE HARLEY GRIP ON TOOMES!!! Robbie: SOMEONE CALL SECURITY!!! Toomes struggles weakly as Hawk wrenches on the modified camel clutch on his harder, screaming at him as he does! Nate: ACE HAWK IS TRYING TO INJURE TOOMES!!! Toomes begins to fade until he loses consciousness!! Nate: HE’S OUT COLD!! HE’S OUT COLD!!! LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, ANDREW ACE HAWK IS IN THE OSW AND HE JUST SQUEEZED ALL CONSCIOUSNESS OUT OF HIS FORMER PUPIL, SEBASTIAN TOOMES!!! Steven: LOOK ON THE TRON AT THE BACKSTAGE AREA!!! The camera shifts backstage, where Tempest is seen, having slowed to a jog in the back of the arena. The camera turns to face her and focuses on her tear streaked face. She stops entirely and put her hands over her eyes sobbing. Heavy footsteps are heard as Ivan Stanislav appears from almost nowhere in front of Tempest. Ivan: TEMPEST!! He rushes towards her with his arms outstretched. Tempest looks up and backpedals away from him. Tempest: Leave me alone! You lied to me! I thought everyone was just trying to be mean saying those things about you, but they were true! Ivan shakes his head and slows down, not wanting to appear threatening. He keeps his hands out. Ivan: Nyet! Tempest, I can explain! Do not cry, I do not wish for you to cry..... Tempest backs away snuffling. Tempest: You should have thought of that before you lied to me! How am I supposed to trust you now? Ivan reaches out, his hand shaking, and grabs Tempest by the shoulder, dwarfing her. Ivan: It was different then! Things were different! I did not know...I did not wish to end what we had and tell you truth...I.... Tempest squirms, trying to get free of him. Tempest: But you could have told me the truth in the beginning! I know we were fighting then, I would have looked past that. but now.... let me go! Ivan roars loudly. Ivan: NO!! He grabs her with his other arm and struggles with her as a flash of urgent rage shoots through his face. Ivan: You will stand here and LISTEN TO ME!! Tempest cowers and struggles to free herself. Tempest: No! I've heard enough of your lies! What else are you lying about? Maybe something important, like even caring about me! Ivan grits his teeth. Ivan: I DO care for you Tempest!! Will you listen to yourself!! I am NOT a liar....it is not my fault!! I would not have done that had I not cared for what he have! Tempest: What we have is based on that lie! It's all a lie! She sobs and glares at him through the tears as she struggles. Tempest: LET GO OF ME! Ivan growls as Tempest manages to get free. What we have is based on something...something great Tempest!! Something bigger than both of us!! Tempest looks panicked as she struggles in Ivan's iron grip.
