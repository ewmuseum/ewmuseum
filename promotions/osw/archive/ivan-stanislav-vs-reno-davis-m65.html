Ivan Stanislav vs. Reno Davis

2002-06-15
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivan Stanislav vs. Reno Davis Written: June 15, 2002 Nate: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome back to Saturday Slam! We’re geared up for the Main Event! This is a feud that can be viewed from many different levels! Ivan Stanislav vs. Reno Davis! They are fighting for their pride! They are fighting for revenge! They are fighting for personal vendettas! And they are also fighting for a shot at starting at the top of the mountain at Ascension!! Steven: That was eloquent, Dave! Robbie: Enough with the eloquence! Lets head to the carnage! Reno Davis, the man that took Tempest away from The Russian Bear, must now face his maker! Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest is a No DQ, Falls Count anywhere matchup for the starting position on top of the Mountain at OSW Ascension!! Introducing first, from Moncton, New Brunswick, Canada! He stands 5’6” tall and weighs in at 166 lbs! “The Unwanted Angel” RENO DAVIS!!! “Rocket Science” by The Pimps plays over the loudspeaker as Reno Davis coolly walks down the entryway. The crowd cheers loudly for the Unwanted Angel, but he pays them no heed, his mind completely focused on the match at hand. Davis hops into the ring and stands with his back to the far corner, being sure to watch the entryway. Nate: Davis is a bundle of energy. Robbie: He is going to be a bundle of shatter bones and puss after this match. Earl: And his opponent. He is from Arkhangelsk, Russia. He stands 7’1” tall and weighs in at 400 lbs! Accompanied to the ring by manger Alexei Ruslan, he is “The Russian Bear” Ivan Stanislav!!!! “The Soviet National Anthem” by The Russian Red Army Choir explodes through the entryway as the lights dim to a deep and dark red. Ivan Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan head out from the backstage area as the crowd begins to throw things towards the two communists. Stanislav, from the get go, points at Davis and starts yelling at him while Alexei smugly walks beside Stanislav. Nate: Looks like Stanislav is ready for a fight! Steven: Stanislav is a foot and a half taller than Davis! He outweighs him by over 230 lbs! Robbie: Children, avert your eyes, this is gonna be ugly! Stanislav makes it to the ring and steps up to the apron and into the ring. Henrich bails out of the ring immediately at referee Pierre Gaston warily calls for the bell! Nate: Drivers! Start your engines!! Ivan stands tall and points at Davis and roars, screaming in Russian. Davis straightens up and sizes up the monstrous Russian Bear. Nate: This has been a war of attrition folks! Davis took out Tempest, now Stanislav took out ZaC! This is beyond personal! There is pride involved!! Stanislav berates Davis some more, his harsh, angular voice cutting through the noise of the fans and hitting the camera. Ivan points at Davis again and then points at his chest and slaps it, signaling Davis to give it his best shot! Davis nods and rushes forward with a dropkick. Davis nails Stanislav directly in his barrel chest, but The Russian Bear doesn’t move. Davis lands on his feet and looks up at Ivan, who merely shoves him away! Nate: Stanislav is just toying with Davis! Davis regains his footing as Ivan yells for him to try again. Davis obliges and runs forward with a cross body block. Stanislav stands stock still as Davis feebly bounces off of the impregnable Russian. Ivan picks Davis up by the hair and shoves him away again, laughing in his trademark laugh. Again, Stanislav beckons for Davis to try his best. Behind Davis, however, Alexei Ruslan slinks back and climbs to the apron! Steven: What’s Ruslan doing?! Robbie: It’s no DQ! He can do whatever he wants! Davis is oblivious to Ruslan and runs forward for another dropkick. He kicks Ivan in the stomach, warranting no movement from The Russian Bear again. Ivan grabs Davis just as Ruslan gets into the ring and nods to Ivan. Stanislav shoves Davis backwards into Ruslan’s arms! Ivan bends forward, waiting for Davis to be shoved towards him for a back body drop!! Ruslan struggles, however, and Davis hits a mule kick, doubling over Ruslan! He instead shoves Alexei towards Ivan!! The minute Ivan feels a body hit him, he flips it over his back, except that it’s Ruslan! Alexei flies over the top rope, out of the ring, into the entryway and hits the steel floor hard! Stanislav blinks, realizing that if Davis is standing in front of him….he just flipped….. Ivan: ALEXEI!! Nate: GREAT move my Reno Davis!! Ivan pivots on his heels and looks at his fallen Comrade, who wriggles and rubs his head! Davis sees the opportunity and runs at Ivan, baseball sliding into his ankle! Stanislav roars and whirls around. He brings his boot over the head of Davis and stomps, but Davis moves just in time. Davis rolls to his feet and ducks a wide right from the Russian Bear and moves in close, punching Ivan hard in the lower right ribs. Ivan roars at the tiny wrestler and swings down to clobber him, but Davis scoots to the left and punches Stanislav twice in his left ribs! Steven: Reno Davis’ getting in close where Stanislav might have trouble getting a hold of him! Ivan roars, not fazed by the punches but rather more irritated, and brings his left forearm over the head of Davis, pinning his between the iron forearm of Stanislav and his barrel chest in what appears to be a very sloppy headlock. Ivan roars and laughs while The Unwanted Angel squirms. Stanislav walks around the ring and roars at the crowd whilst holding Davis in his vice grip. Davis grabs Ivan’s leg and tries to unbalance him, but Ivan shrugs it off. Nate: That’s the problem with getting in close with Ivan Stanislav, Steven! Stanislav brings his free hand up an clenches it into a fist. He swings back and NAILS Davis in the crown of the skull, pushing him out of Ivan’s headlock and sending him rolling across the mat!! Crowd: OHHH!! Stanislav roars at Davis and raises his arms over his head. Ruslan wobbles to his feet and clutches at his back but waves Ivan off. Alexei hobbles backstage… Robbie: Ruslan is leaving! Looks like he’s had enough! Stanislav methodically walks over to Davis, who still is seeing stars. He grabs Davis by the hair and lifts him up over his head by the hair and positions him in a one handed military press. Stanislav’s hand virtually envelops the waist of Davis as he walks around the ring, pressing Davis multiple times! Robbie: Look at that! He’s a genius! He’s actually working out WHILE he wrestles Davis! The man’s got it all! Stanislav stands in the middle of the ring and tosses Reno out over the top rope into the entryway with one hand! Davis sails through the air and lands on his side, unmoving! Stanislav laughs and points at himself, then at his open hand and slams his palm into it! Nate: Reno Davis has GOT to find another way to combat Ivan. Toe to toe, power to power, it’s not going to do it! Stanislav climbs out of the ring and in no big hurry walks over to Davis. Davis clambers to his feet and starts to run away from Stanislav, towards the backstage area! Robbie: HA HA! He’s running away!! Stanislav takes off after Davis as he sees him run away. Davis gets to the entryway and leaps into the air and grabs the grating below the Jumboton! Stanislav tries to stop as Davis swings forward, and then back and kicks Ivan square in the jaw! Ivan roars and backpedals, grabbing his face. Davis lands and superkicks Stanislav in the jaw, using his slightly elevated level on the entryway to hit him! Ivan roars and falls onto his back, completely disoriented! Steven: He has Stanislav down! Now’s his chance! Davis grabs both of Ivan’s tree trunk legs and twists them into a figure four leg lock! Davis falls backwards and wrenches the hold, but it doesn’t appear to be doing much damage to Stanislav. Davis arches back more to try to get more pressure. But Stanislav sits up on his elbow and looks at his opponent…. Steven: This can’t be good….not at all… Ivan laughs loudly, stopping Reno’s attempt, and merely flexes his legs and breaks the hold! Davis rolls to his feet as does Stanislav. Ivan rushes forward and wraps both hands around the neck of Davis and easily lifts him high into the air, roaring as he does. Davis kicks weakly as Stanislav chokes the life out of Davis and slams him into a beam that holds the Jumbotron up. Nate: It’s obvious that Ivan isn’t just wrestling to win. He’s wrestling to make a statement against Reno Davis. Davis tries to reach down and stop the choke, but his arms are too short and Stanislav has him lifted too high! Ivan roars and slams him against the beam again and whirls him around like a rag doll, laughing the entire time. Robbie: Ivan’s just playing with him!! Look!! Ivan roars and chucks Davis into the entrance backstage. Davis slams into the wall covering the backstage view and punches through it, sending wood and splinters flying everywhere! Stanislav turns and raises his arms above his head and roars to the crowd, laughing loudly. Steven: Things’re going backstage!! Stanislav roars and turns to walk backstage. From the side of the backstage entrance, a ladder swings into view and slams into Stanislav’s midsection. Ivan roars and tenses up, doubling over just slightly. The ladder wheels back and slams into his forehead, knocking Stanislav onto his back! Davis rounds the corner, holding the ladder, and slams it over Stanislav’s right leg. Ivan roars in pain and rolls over, getting to his hands and knees only to have the ladder slammed over his back. Robbie: Now we get to the nitty gritty! I knew that Davis couldn’t fight fair! Nate: It’s no DQ! Ruslan ran into the ring in the first ten seconds! Robbie: Whatever… Stanislav shrugs off the ladder, which becomes increasingly battered, and gets to his feet. Davis nails Ivan on the chin, opening up a small gash on the Russian Bear. He prods Ivan with the top of the ladder, but Ivan grabs it and slowly crushes the metal seat of the ladder in his bare hands, a sadistic grin on his face as Davis slowly turns pale. Robbie: MUAHAHAAHAHAHAHA!!! Ivan yanks the ladder in his direction, pulling Davis with it. Davis slams into Ivan’s chest and falls on his back while Ivan brings the ladder high over his head, roaring angrily. Davis shoots his foot up, nailing Ivan dead in the crotch. Stanislav’s voice cuts off as he drops the ladder and teeters forward. Davis pushes Ivan away with his feet and clambers to his feet again. Steven: It hurts me thinking of the pain Ivan is going through…he must has beautiful privates. Nate: That’s enough outta you, Smith! Ivan shakes his head and rubs his eyes, relocating his target. Unfortunately, his target had set up the ladder and kicked it in Ivan’s direction! The ladder falls forward and the mangled seat slams into Ivan’s forehead, opening another small cut on his face. Davis runs forward and dives at Ivan’s head, driving his elbow into the face of The Russian Bear. Stanislav continued to reel backwards towards the ring, finally slamming into the apron. Davis leaps for a dropkick, but Ivan catches him and vaults him up over his head. Davis flails in mid air and flies into the ring and lands on his hands and knees. He flips over and grabs at a knee as Ivan shakes his head and rubs the blood from his eyes. Nate: Now THAT was a springboard. Ivan turns and climbs into the ring. He kicks Davis over and points down at him, berating him. Stanislav grabs Davis by the head and lifts him, palming his entire face. He pulls his head back and headbutts Davis in the forehead, sending him sprawling to the corner. The Crowd: Ooooooooo!!! Nate: That’s gotta hurt! Davis flies through the air and slams into the turnbuckle and slinks down to the mat. Stanislav points at him and roars again, Davis, in response, flicks Ivan the finger!! The crowd explodes. Steven: Now he did it! Stanislav blinks and roars angrily. He brings his arms up over his head and rushes forward in a sloppy, angry, enraged attempt to grab him. Davis rolls forward between Ivan’s legs and springs to his feet. Stanislav grabs at the air, only to realize that Davis isn’t there! Davis grabs Ivan’s suspenders, pulls himself to Ivan’s shoulder, and bulldog’s Ivan into the turnbuckle! The crowd is going wild. Nate: WHAT A SEQUENCE!!! Stanislav’s face hits the turnbuckle and spins around, but Davis has already leapt FROM Ivan’s shoulders to the turnbuckle! Davis leaps and nails Ivan with the Shooting Star DDT!! Nate: SHOOTING STAR DDT!! Robbie: NYET!!! Ivan hits the mat hard and rolls over as Davis goes for a cover. 1… 2… Ivan presses Davis and tosses him high into the air and across the ring, effectively kicking out. He sits up angrily and gets to his feet. Davis warily rises as well, amazed at how far he was flung. Davis bounces off the ropes and goes for a spinning heel kick, but Stanislav catches him in mid flight by the front of his shirt. Ivan holds him out at arms length and yells at him and then slaps him in the face twice! Nate: Ivan Stanislav is just brow beating Davis now!! Davis squirms and kicks, but Ivan lifts him up and chokeslams him to the mat with authority. Stanislav picks up Davis again and lifts him high into the air by the head. He drops him and nails him with THE IRON CURTAIN!! Nate: IRON CURTAIN!! Davis soars through the air and hits the turnbuckle with a tremendous amount of force as Stanislav raises his arms again and roars, wiping blood from his chin. Nate: What the?! Suddenly, the lights begin to dim to a dark blue as “Battleflag” by Lo-Fidelity All Stars plays over the PA system. Immediately the crowd explodes. Robbie: WHAT THE HELL?!?!! Nate: THAT’S TEMPEST’S MUSIC!!! Stanislav freezes in his tracks in the center of the ring and turns around as “TEMPEST” appears over the Jumbotron and blinks! The crowd rises to its feet and cheers wildly as Battleflag gains strength and volume!! Nate: TEMPEST IS HERE!! SHES COME BACK TO FACE IVAN!!! RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!!! Sure enough, Tempest appears from the backstage area wearing black boots, red pleather pants, and a red halter top. Her hair is a spikey black with purple highlights. She walks down the entryway slowly, waving to the fans but mainly staring down Ivan!! Stanislav shakes his head and rubs his eyes, not believing what he is seeing. Nate: TEMPEST HAS RETURNED TO OLD SCHOOL WRESTLING!!!! Robbie: SOMEONE CALL SECURITY!!!! Tempest stands at the landing of the entryway, just feet from the ring, and stares at Stanislav who’s face twists from rage to shame to pain as he stares down at her! Nate: IVAN IS STUNNED!!! HE DOES NOT KNOW WHAT TO THINK!!! The lights begin to blink, making it harder to see Tempest on the floor. From the entryway, Alexei Ruslan emerges from the backstage area waving his arms to try to get Ivan’s attention. Ivan, however, is mesmerized by Tempest. Nate: WHAT IS RUSLAN DOING?!?! Ruslan screams and waves his arms more. Realizing that Ivan isn’t paying anything any attention, he takes off down the entryway towards Tempest! Nate: RUSLAN IS GOING AFTER TEMPEST!!! Davis recovers from the Iron Curtain and sizes up Stanislav, who’s back is to him. Ruslan makes it to Tempest and grabs her by the hair, pulling it roughly! Tempest screams and falls backwards as Alexei rips the hair from Tempests’….head?? Nate: WHAT IN THE HELL?!?! Steven: THAT’S NOT TEMPEST!!!! Alexei throws the wig to Ivan, who catches it, stunned. Stanislav’s face is one of horror… Nate: THAT’S NOT TEMPEST AT ALL!!! ITS ACTRESS RACHEL LEE COOK!!! ITS ALL BEEN A SET UP!!! Alexei wrestles with Cook, trying to pin her to the ground. She manages to kick him away and takes off screaming up the entryway. Alexei looks up at Ivan and then takes off after her, screaming the entire time. Stanislav turns slowly just as Davis rushes towards him. Davis nails Stanislav with the Upbeat Tempo!!! Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Nate: UPBEAT TEMPO!!! THIS COULD BE IT!!! Davis makes the cover. 1… 2…. 3!! Winner: Reno Davis Nate: DAVIS WON!!! HE WILL START AT THE TOP OF THE MOUNTAIN!!! Davis rolls off of Ivan quickly and slides out of the ring. Stanislav rolls over, still clutching the wig that looks amazingly similar to Tempests hair close to him. Nate: DAVIS HAS CONQUERED IVAN USING MIND GAMES!!!! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Davis backs up and raises his arms and stares at Stanislav. The crowd remains relatively silent, unable to decide whether to mourn for Ivan, cheer for Reno, or boo The Russian Bear. Stanislav takes a moment and holds the wig close to him, his head bowed in silence, and then glares up at Davis…. Robbie: KILL!!! KILL!!! KILL!!! Ivan tears the wig in half and roars loudly. Davis makes it to the entryway and waits to see what Ivan does. In a blind fit of rage, Stanislav grabs the turnbuckle and tears at it, ripping all three ropes directly from it. He roars again in painful anguish and tears the steel post from the ring, causing the entire structure to buck and sway!! Nate: OH MY GOD!! IVAN STANISLAV JUST RIPPED APART THE RING!! Robbie: KILL DAVIS, NOT THE RING!!! Nate: WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT ON SLAM!!! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!!! The camera shifts back to the ring, Ivan shoulders the huge post and hops off the destroyed ring. He raises the post above his head and lets out a mighty roar….as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears in the lower, central portion of the screen, as the scene fades to black.
