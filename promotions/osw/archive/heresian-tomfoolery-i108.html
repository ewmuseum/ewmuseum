Heresian Tomfoolery

2002-04-27
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Heresian Tomfoolery Written: 27/04/2002 The scene reopens at the ringside position of the OSW announce team. Nate: Welcome back to Slam! It’s been a wild night thus far, and we are just getting started. Robbie: I am telling you, ZaC was the key to the Davis signing. Davis himself was an after thought. Nate: Davis is going to hear you and punch your nose through your skull. Robbie: Well, if ZaC is in the segment, I will take one for the team in order to help ratings. Nate: (sighing) In any event, we are just about… BOOM!!! Nate is cut off suddenly as a towering inferno burst s from the ramp, before the entranceway. “Carmina Burana” blasts across the arena, sending shivers down the crowd’s spines, as they release a flurry of ‘boos’ hisses and insults at the music, as they realise what is about to happen. However the music plays, but no one emerges. Nate: Come out, come out, wherever you are! Steven: I’M RIGHT HERE YOU HEARTLESS ANIMAL! Robbie: *Sigh* Nate: Where are they? Carmina Burana doesn’t stop or decrease in volume, but instead grows louder, as the flames reach higher and higher in front of the Jumbotron…and the arena lights go out. Robbie: Goddamn lights… Steven: Let there be light! Nate: Steven, you are NOT Moses or any other god-knowing entity… Steven: I am god personified… Robbie: *Whispering* “oh god kill me” personified more like… Steven: You say sumptin Robbie? Robbie: Yeah, I wonder where the Duo of Domination are… Suddenly the towering inferno explodes high into the sky, and the extinguishes, and suddenly the ring this time is surrounded by climbing flames, emitting from somewhere under the ring. Steven: WHAT IN TARNATION, HOSS??? Nate: *Nudging Steven* Err…wrong show… Steven: Oh yeah… The flames climb into the sky, and as the shadows dance and flicker on the rafters; shapes can be made out falling fast to the ring… Nate: What the huh… Robbie: Yay!!! Here they come!!! Steven: BOCAAAAAAAAA!!!!!!!!! The flames drop just below the falling shapes, down, down, down…until they hit the ring. Then, they lift into the air again as if by magic, and finally fall majestically to the ring. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome the OSW Tag Team Champions…HERESIAN BLOODLUST!!! The crowd respond by letting out a chorus of hissing boos and the like. Hessian and Cain look around, slightly confused, and snicker as they hear their partners in crime following them…or so they think… Minion & Caleb: aaaaAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!! Hessian and Cain duck, as Minion and Caleb fly past them, flailing from a rope attached to the rafters. Nate: Heh…so a team reaching 700lbs in weight can fly, but a midget and a homeless guy need a big thick rope to swing across… Steven: Pffft…they couldn’t swing if their li-… Robbie: Oh knock it off Smith! Azrael Cain “graciously” accepts a microphone from Earl, and puts it to his lips, as he and Hessian hold their belts up for all to see. Cain: The Russians are coming! The Russians are coming! Run, run for your lives little ones…the Russians are coming………………..……………well bring them on. All you ingrates and brainless rats watch on every week as Hessian and I continue to destroy our competition fight after fight. The Sabbath will be no different. As you peons pray to your god, Hessian and I shall be praying to our own god, praying that we don’t kill his prey before he has the chance to. On Sunday all Hell truly will break loose, and it will be for the Heretic’s pleasure only as we watch Krieg and Walczak cower before our mighty prowess, and then Eternally Damn them from our paths…scum like that don’t deserve to fight the almighty Heretics. Cain passes the microphone to Hessian, who receives a loud boo from the crowd, and waits…waits…waits…and… Hessian: ALL-OF-YOU-SHUT-THE-F*CK-UP-AND-LISTEN-TO-WHAT-I-HAVE-TO-SAY-NOOOOOOOOOW!!!! The arena is silent… Hessian: Now there we have an example of insolence. I, one half of the best team in OSW waits patiently for you, our “fans” to quieten down, and I receive a flurry of abuse…and so I respond thusly. Now, take this little act and magnify it by, oh, say a near-death experience type of magnitude. Now on Sunday, the Sabbath, Heresian Bloodlust face off against a pair of cowards. Oh, so now to be hard and big we have to hide behind a big Russian pussycat. Well I say ‘f*ck off’ to that. Cain and I have put up with a lot of your Communist sh*t for too long, and now on Sunday we will prove indefinitely that we are almighty, and you pair are nothing more than another domino to be pushed over, setting off the chain reaction leading to ultimate destruction. The Apocalypse. It is dawning, and at Head on Collision, Cain, Krieg, Walczak and I are going to collide, and all that’s going to be left is one big mess, two crucified Communists and two Heresian Tag Team Champions…with those few words Cain and I leave you…this town is a f*cking disgrace… Nate: Bah! Steven: That ought to get the fans going. Hessian and Cain again start to levitate as though by magic, and ascend into the rafters. Robbie: Well…quick…yet painless… With those words Robbie turns to his side, and comes face-to-face with a horrifying sight……… Minion & Caleb: aaaaaaaaaAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!! SLAM!!! Minion and Caleb smack into Robbie, knocking him off his chair, as they continue to swing across the arena, mimicking Heresian Bloodlust. Nate: Heh…let’s go to a quick commercial. With those words the stage explodes again in a burst of fire, and as the flames fall, the cinders and swirling flames spell out the name of ‘Heresian Bloodlust’, and down in the pyro chamber, Mike Lasandra giggles satisfactorily.
