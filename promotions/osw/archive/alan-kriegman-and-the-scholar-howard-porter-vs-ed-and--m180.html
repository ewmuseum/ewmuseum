Alan Kriegman and 'The Scholar' Howard Porter vs Ed and a Mystery Partner

2002-07-13
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alan Kriegman and 'The Scholar' Howard Porter vs Ed and a Mystery Partner Written: 7/13/02 Nate: Alright OSW fans… It’s main event time. Our man Ed is gonna try and strike a blow for justice against Commissioner Howard Porter and Alan Kriegman. Robbie: It’s too late, Nate! NOBODY can stop the Think Tank now! Nate: The scary thing is, you just might be right. Steven: I’ve got a bad feeling about this… Robbie: Well you shouldn’t swallow so much! Steven: I’m being serious! Nate: Alan Kriegman and Howard Porter may be geniuses, but that doesn’t mean Ed is an idiot. I doubt he’d go into this match unprepared. I just hope that his mystery partner is enough to help him end the Think Tank’s reign of terror. Alright fans…here we go. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following tag team contest is scheduled for one fall… “Back in Black” by AC/DC hits the sound system. Nate: What the hell…? That isn’t the Think Tank’s theme. Earl: Introducing first… At a total combined weight of 465 lbs… Representing The Think Tank… OSW COMMISSIONER “THE SCHOLAR” HOWARD PORTER AND ALAN KRIEGMAN!!!!! As the screeching voice of Brian Johnson fills the arena, the evil supergenius himself Alan Kriegman slowly walks out from under the jumbotron. Upon the sight of the long-presumed dead member of the Think Tank, the fans immediately erupt into an explosion of hatred. Smirking cockily, Kriegman gives an almost royal wave to the crowd as he walks down the ramp, basking in the hateful energy around him. Nate: Here comes Alan Kriegman, but where the hell is Porter?! Steven: I dunno, but I want to see the Stroke of Genius tonight! Alan enters the ring and walks over to Earl Heinrich, snatching the mic right out of his hands like it was candy from a baby. Nate: What is this scumbag up to? Robbie: Show a little respect for evil itself, Nate. Though the music has stopped, the crowd is booing so loudly that Alan has to almost shout into the microphone to keep from being drowned out. Kriegman: Howard and I decided that this match was too special an occasion to be soiled by the announcing work of Mr. Heinrich. After all, how many times will we get the chance to end the career of Ed before the entire world? In light of the special circumstances surrounding this match, I’ve decided to take the supreme honor of introducing my dear friend and fellow Vax myself. Hit the music! Nate: What a crock. “Genius” by Pitchshifter begins to play, and the boo’s become even louder as “The Scholar” Howard Porter makes his way out into the arena. Kriegman: Ladies and Gentlemen, Boys and Girls, Simpletons of all ages… It is both an honor and a privilege to introduce my partner in this matchup… Weighing in at 235 lbs… He is technically the most technical technicalist in wrestling history, and I am proud to call this man both a friend and a stablemate… The Commissioner of OSW… “THE SCHOLAR” HOWARD PORTER!!! With an air of dignity, Howard Porter calmly walks down the ramp and enters the ring, nodding to Alan with a smile. Kriegman hands the mic over to Porter, and the two share a hearty handshake before The Scholar begins to work the mic. Nate: This should be good… Porter: Thank you, thank you. This match is indeed a very special occasion, as my friend Alan described it. For a match as grand as this, I believe it calls for yet another special announcer…which is why it shall be my pleasure to introduce our first opponent. “Food for the Brain” by One Minute Silence graces the arena, and the fans go wild, despite Porter’s control of the mic. Porter: And now, the opposition! Ladies and Gentleman, I am proud to announce the entrance of one of the most respected men in Old School Wrestling… Both a former commissioner and an eternal Hardcore Legend, this man’s work both in the ring and out has earned him the adoration of the fans and the esteem of his colleagues… For the last time, please give a warm, OSW welcome to none other than EDWARD JONATHAN ED! Robbie: You hear that? FOR THE LAST TIME, Nate! This man knows what he’s talking about! Nate: I was listening, jackass. Wasting no time with his entrance, Ed heads straight to the ring, the nail-studded, barbed-wire steel chair affectionately named “Bob” in his hands. Ed slides under the bottom rope into the ring and gets to his feet, at no point looking away from his two opponents. Kriegman and Porter stand side by side, making no advances on Ed. In fact, the two are actually smiling rather docilely at the Hardcore Legend, who steps forward and yanks the mic from the commissioner’s hands. Ed: Thanks for the intro, Howard. If this really is the last time I’m ever in the ring, then I have a few things that I think I’ll get off my chest before introducing MY partner. The crowd cheers Ed on as he walks back and forth before two of the three men responsible for his fall from grace in OSW. Ed: Y’know…we’ve been through a lot together, us three. I remember back in PCW, waaay back when that old boat first opened for business. You guys along with PHD formed the Think Tank and took over the place as quick as a flash…but I was there too, and I sure as hell didn’t make it very easy for you. I formed my own little group with some of the guys, and we called ourselves…Team International. The longtime PCW fans in the crowd pop at the mention of the name of the old stable. Even Alan and Howard smile and nod in remembrance. Ed: Yeah, those were the days. Me, The Chef, Troy Brown, and Two Ton took you guys on. Think Tank vs Team International. Everyone loved it. Hell, I was fighting a losing battle and even *I* had the time of my life fighting you guys. I hated you three, but they were still good times. You know why? It was fun. Admit it, you guys loved it too. Alan Kriegman and Howard Porter actually chuckle as Ed reminisces before them. Ed: We hated each other’s guts… Especially you, Al! You hated me more than anyone! Me and you, we had ourselves quite a rivalry in the old days. But it was still great. You guys used to make it fun to be the good guy… But things change, don’t they? Here we are, almost two years later. New playground, but the faces haven’t changed. And you know what? It sure isn’t fun anymore. Kriegman gives an ingenuine frown to his old rival. Ed: It used to be a pleasure fighting with you two…but not anymore. You went and pulled that bullshit with The Vax… Hurting people, ending careers, all the political bullshit. You guys took it too far, and you killed all the fun there was in it. I’m not taking you guys on because it’s so much fun anymore… You guys have taken something I loved and ruined it… And for what? So you could get these folks to hate you? Shit, they’d boo you anyway, you didn’t have to do all this… I’m not fighting you guys cause you’re big heels… I’m not fighting you cause everyone hates you and loves me… You two assholes took my business away from me. I CAN’T ENJOY WRESTLING ANYMORE THANKS TO YOU TWO! Nate: And here I thought it was because of chronic masturbation and video games. Robbie: HA! Ed boldly steps forward, putting only inches between himself and his two opponents. Ed: I’M FIGHTING TO TAKE BACK WHAT I LOVE… YOU TWO ARE KILLING THIS BUSINESS, AND I’LL BE DAMNED IF I JUST LET YOU!! I’D FUCKING DIE BEFORE I LET YOU TWO BEAT ME!!!! THIS IS MY LIFE, AND I’M NOT GONNA LET YOU FUCKERS TAKE IT FROM ME!!! Alan and Howard simply smile on at Ed, looking as if they have not a care in the world. Ed: Yeah smile all you want, do the cocky heel thing you guys do so well… Just remember what you’ve done to this business just to get yourselves over. I hope you’re fucking happy. You’re not gonna be smiling much longer. You think you’re gonna end me? Well you just might be doing me a favor, but I’m not gonna give you two the pleasure of thinking that you’ve got me beat. I’ve got a partner that lives and breathes this business just as much as I do, and he’s just as fucking disgusted by you two assholes as I am… Me and him are gonna show you two what this business is all about, and maybe we’ll set you both straight in the process… Say hello to my mystery partner… “While the City Sleeps” by MC 900 Ft. Jesus hits the sound system. Ed: ..TRASHCAN MAN!!! The fans nearly blow the roof off of the arena as they cheer like crazy. Nate: HE’S BACK!!! Robbie: Oh Jesus, God no… Carrying his signature trashcan above his head, former PCW World Champion and short-lived OSW wrestler Trashcan Man comes running out from the back, a crazed look in his eyes. Steven: TRASHY’S BACK! TRASHY’S BACK!! Nate: AND HE LOOKS AS INSANE AS EVER!!! The only man who could possibly be more hardcore than Ed rolls under the bottom rope into the ring, and stands tall next to his fellow extremist. The smug looks on the faces of Kriegman and Porter remain unchanged, despite the fact that Trashcan Man has come back and is brandishing a rusty old garbage can at them next to Ed. Steven: Christ, check out the look on Trashy’s face! He looks like he’s totally lost it! Robbie: Not that he was ever very sane to begin with! Nate: No…something’s wrong. Trashcan Man doesn’t look right… True to Nate’s words, there is definitely something odd about the way Trashcan Man is staring at Alan Kriegman. Ed smirks and looks to TCM, who hasn’t taken his eyes off the evil supergenius. Ed: Alright… Trashy, let’s do this. Ed throws the mic down and raises Bob above his head, TCM doing the same with his garbage can. The only motion made by either Think Tanker is a simple nod…from Alan Kriegman to Trashcan Man. And with that one nod from the man in black, Trashcan Man turns… Nate: NO!!!! …and brings the trashcan down on the head of Ed. Nate: NO!!!!! Bob falls from Ed’s hands as the heavy trash receptacle crashes over his skull, and the hardcore legend manages to give TCM a quick look of confusion before dropping to his knees. Nate: NOOOOOO!!!!!! Robbie: SWERVE!!! The fans react loudly and disapprovingly as TCM smashed the garbage can over Ed’s head a second time, this time causing blood to run down his forehead. Like a young child looking to his father for approval, TCM for some reason turns toward Alan Kriegman, who grins evilly and nods. With that, Trashcan Man gives Ed one more shot upside the head. The hardcore legend lets out a sigh, looks up at TCM as if to ask “Why?”, and collapses to the mat. Nate: AM I FUCKING HALLUCINATING OR IS THIS ACTUALLY HAPPENING?! Kriegman lets out a diabolical laugh as TCM continues to beat the fallen Ed with his trashcan, with Porter smiling widely as he looks on. Steven: WHAT THE HELL IS HAPPENING?! As TCM pulverizes Ed, Alan picks up Bob off the mat and unfolds it, setting the lethal chair down as if it were to be used as furniture, then picks up the microphone. Kriegman: Trash… Trash… Listen to me, Trash. Listen to your master. Nate: WHAT??? Steven: Krieggy could be my master…. Just like that, Trashcan Man ceases his assault and spins back toward Kriegman. It’s now quite obvious what the look on his face is as he stares at the evil supergenius. Trashcan Man is gazing at Kriegman with complete adoration. Kriegman: Come here, Trash. Come to me. TCM slowly steps over to Kriegman, puts down his can, and actually KNEELS before him, looking up at him as if he were God himself. Kriegman: Now say, Trash… Say it just like I asked… The dark man holds the mic in front of Trashcan Man’s bearded face. TCM’s mouth opens, but no words come out. Kriegman: SAY IT, TRASH. TCM (stammering): M….M…My… Kriegman: Say it! TCM: My life..for you… My life for you! MY LIFE FOR YOU!!! Nate: Oh…my…God… TCM: MY LIFE FOR YOU! MY LIFE FOR YOU! Kriegman: That’s enough, my friend. Nate: WHAT IN THE WORLD IS GOING ON?!?!?! ED’S LONGTIME STABLEMATE, TRASHCAN MAN HAS TURNED ON ED AND IS SUBSERVIANT TO THE MAN HE HATES MOST, ALAN KRIEGMAN!?!? WHAT IN THE WORLD IS GOING ON?!?! Trashcan Man moves forward on his knees and tightly hugs the legs of Alan Kriegman, who looks toward Howard and grins evilly. Porter looks on at this scene with pure satisfaction. Nate: WHAT THE HELL HAS ALAN KRIEGMAN DONE TO TRASHCAN MAN?!?! Robbie: I don’t know, but I love it! The crowd, which is already beyond furious, grows even more hostile when Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman suddenly steps out from the back, carrying a steel chair in his hands. Armour and John Mammoth walk on either side of him, each man carrying two chairs of his own. Steven: I’m scared! Hold me, Nate! Nate: NO!!! SOMEONE CALL SECURITY!!! ARMOUR, MAMMOTH AND PHD ARE ON THEIR WAY OUT HERE!!!! PHD, Armour, and Mammoth join Kriegman, Porter, and TCM in the ring. Kriegman grins as TCM remains latched onto his legs. Kriegman: Heh…OK Trash, that’s enough… It’s time. Nate: Time…? Trashcan Man loosens his hold and looks up at Kriegman with wonder. Kriegman: Just like we talked about it, Trash. It’s time. The ring has now become completely crowded. Armour and Mammoth have dropped the 4 chairs are now holding up a semi-concious Ed by the arms, while Kriegman pats TCM on the head like some sort of pet, and Porter simply stands back and watches it all happen. Kriegman: Armour, John…lead the king to his throne. The two monsters nod obediently, carry Ed over to the unfolded Bob… Nate: Oh God, NO… …and trust him down onto the barbed wire-wrapped, nail-studded old chair. The Crowd: Oooooooooooooooo!!! Ed’s head snap’s back and he scowls in agony as he is forced to sit down on the deadly weapon that once led him to the status of ‘Hardcore Legend’. Steven: YEEOOOWWW! Nate: SOMEONE STOP THIS!!! Held down by both Armour and Mammoth, Ed is soon surrounded by Porter, PHD, and Kriegman, who is being clung to by TCM. Alan moves forward and grabs Ed by his now blood-soaked hair, moving his face in close to his old rival’s. Kriegman: This is it, Ed… The game’s over. You lose. No matter what you do, you simply cannot defeat us. But you can still save yourself from complete destruction. There’s only one thing you have to do, Ed… Admit we’ve won. Admit to us, and admit to the world that we’ve beaten you…and we’ll spare you. Nate: THIS IS FUCKING DISGUSTING! SOMEONE DO SOMETHING!!!! Alan literally shoves the microphone in Ed’s face with his free hand, now scowling at the bloodied legend. Kriegman: SAY IT, ED. SAY YOU’VE FAILED. ADMIT YOUR INFERIORITY, OR FACE THE CONSEQUENCES. Struggling to remain conscious, Ed breathes heavily as he looks up at Kriegman…and spits right in his face. The crowd bursts with approval as Alan Kriegman turns away to wipe the loogie from his face, then turns back and smacks Ed hard. Steven: THIS IS PRACTICALLY RAPE!! Kriegman throws himself at Ed, knocking him backwards in the chair and landing on top of him. The evil supergenius begins to slam Ed’s head into the mat repeatedly, filled with rage. Porter and PHD nod to eacother and proceed to pull Kriegman backward, holding him back from attacking Ed any further. Porter commandeers the mic from Alan in the process. Porter: Enough! Enough! Alan, my friend… He’s made his choice. His face red with fury, Alan Kriegman bites his lower lip and nods. His two friends release him, and Kriegman continues to snarl at Ed as Porter stands over his fallen form. Porter: Edward, if you will not lower your head to us…then you leave us with no choice. An example must be made to all of OSW. You will be martyred. Robbie: SWEET!! Nate: NOOO!!! Porter: Armour, John… Proceed as planned. Nate: WHAT PLAN? The two monsters go to work. Armour takes one of the folding chair he brought in, and wraps it around Ed’s right arm…then wraps another around his left arm. Mammoth places each of Ed’s legs inside a chair…then takes the fifth chair from PHD, spreads Ed’s legs, and places the chair over his groin. Nate: Oh sweet Jesus what the hell are they gonna do? Finally, Howard Porter picks up Bob…and very carefully places Ed’s head inside the folded chair. Ed lays on the mat in a horrific manner, with a chair wrapped around each of his limbs, one over his groin, and his own weapon of choice around his neck and covering his face. Porter: Good… Assume positions, my friends. Steven: I CAN’T WATCH!!! The six members of The Think Tank each take a stance at one of the chairs… PHD stands at the right arm, Trashcan Man at the left arm (still staring adoringly at Kriegman), John Mammoth at the left leg, Armour at the right leg, Alan Kriegman at the groin…and Howard Porter standing at the head. Nate: NO! NO! NOOOOO!!!! Porter: On my mark, gentlemen. 3…2…1 The crowd screams as all six men jump simultaneously onto the chairs. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! The full weight of Armour and Mammoth comes down on the chairs wrapped around Ed’s legs, and a sickening snapping can be heard. The same can be said for TCM and PHD with the arms. Kriegman stomps extra hard with both feet on the chair over Ed’s groin, and we can leave that feeling to the imagination. Finally, and most horribly, comes Howard Porter, who lands with both feet on Bob, crushing Ed’s face (and neck) between the nails and barbed wire of the chair. Ed’s back arches, and we can hear what could possibly be a howl of agony, and then the Hardcore legend goes limp. Nate: I…I..I don’t know what I could possibly say to do this justice… Robbie: How about…WOOOOOHOOOOO!!!! Nate: You sicken me almost as much as they do. The chairs are removed from Ed’s limbs, revealing hideous-looking bumps which could only represent fractures. Steven: SOMEBODY STOP THIS!!! Nate: THERE IS NO ONE TO STOP IT!!! TOOMES NEVER MADE IT OUT OF THE HOSPITAL!! IVAN WAS CARTED OUT BY SECURITY!!! VENDETTA, AXTELL AND TRUXELL LEFT TO RESCUE NIKI!!! ST. CRUX IS AT THE HOSPITAL!!! LOCKE LEFT THE BUILDING EARLIER!!!! Steven: THERE HAS TO BE SOMEONE WHO CAN STOP THIS!!! THERE JUST HAS TO BE SOMEONE WHO CAN STOP THE MIGHTY VAX!!! Nate: THIS IS AN OUTRAGE!!!! WE ARE GOING TO LOSE OUR FEED, WITH THIS IMAGE BEING THE LAST THING LEFT IN OUR FINDS MINDS ON AN OTHERWISE SPECTACULAR NIGHT!!! The chair from his groin is removed (again, the results are left to the imagination), and finally, Howard Porter removed Bob from Ed’s head. Whatever remains of Ed’s face is being digitally blurred by the cable provider, because the unrecognizable mask of ripped flesh and gore is simply too much to be aired uncensored. Alan, Howard, and PHD all grin with pleasure at the sight. Ed is not moving. Porter (looking to Herbert, then Alan): Shall we finish the job, gentlemen? Kriegman nods vigorously, with Trashcan Man mimicking him in a way that would be humorous if not for the current circumstances. PHD shows approval as well. Porter: Very well then. Johnathan, if you will… The gargantuan John Mammoth shoots “The Scholar” a glance that could be construed as hatred, and proceeds to pick up the completely limp Ed and sling him over his shoulder like a ragdoll. Howard exits the ring, followed by Kriegman, PHD, Trashcan Man, and Armour. Following behind the new super power is John Mammoth, carrying the unresponsive Ed out with him. All six men walk up the ramp together, led by Porter. Nate: WHERE THE HELL IS SECURITY?! WHAT THE HELL ARE THEY GONNA DO NEXT?! THIS MUST BE STOPPED!!! Debris of all sorts is hurled as the group reaches the top of the ramp, when Mammoth finally tosses Ed to the hard ground like a lifeless sack. Porter: Edward, you DO know what goes with being a martyr, I hope. Bearing his evilest smirk, Alan Kriegman steps forward, his black suit stained with Ed’s blood in several spots. He bends down, grabs his arch rival by the hair once again, and pulls him up. Despite all that has happened to him, Ed still somehow clings to consciousness. With Kriegman clutching him by the hair, he staggers along as the evil supergenius leads him…to the edge of the ramp, which stands 15 feet above an uncovered floor of solid concrete. A virtual army of security guards have swarmed the ringside area, keeping fans from flooding over the barriers to stop this. Steven: Tell me when it’s over, I’m covering my eyes! Alan Kriegman stands at the edge of the ramp, still tightly latched onto Ed’s blood-soaked hair, which was once a sandy blonde. Howard hands the microphone to Alan, who takes it in his free hand, and pulls Ed’s face closer to his once again. Kriegman: Ed…it’s over. We’re giving you one last chance to save yourself. Admit your defeat. Tell the world that we can’t be defeated… Kriegman points to the floor so far beneath them. Kriegman: …or take plunge, and become a martyr. Nate: DO IT, ED. JUST ADMIT THAT YOU CAN’T WIN AND SAVE YOUR GODDAMN LIFE!!! Alan holds the mic up to what remains of Ed’s face. Kriegman: Well? Ed (breathing deeply): I thought I already told you… I’d fucking die before I let you beat me… DO IT. Kriegman grits his teeth and shakes his head at Ed, and moves to throw him over the ledge… The crowd goes silent as Ed teeters over the edge of the ramp…only to be pulled back… by Trashcan Man. Nate: *GASP* Oh THANK GOD… TCM holds Ed’s bloody head in his arms, and looks up at Kriegman with the same look of worship, but is shaking his head to his new master. Alan glares angrily at TCM. Nate: TRASHCAN MAN HAS HAD ENOUGH!! HE’S TAKING A STAND AGAINST THE THINK TANK!!! WE’RE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Trashcan Man then grabs the mic from Kriegman, who stares at him, his lips curled with rage. TCM looks down over the edge, the up at Alan again. TCM: …My life for you! Trashcan Man picks up the limp Ed…setting him up for a cradle piledriver. Nate: NO! WE ARE JUST ARE LOSING OUR FEED RIGHT NOW!!!! Robbie: YES!! DO IT, TRASHY! DO IT!!!! Alan Kriegman makes direct eye contact with TCM, nods with almost fatherly approval, and steps back. Howard Porter stands with PHD and Alan Kriegman on either side of him, with Armour and John Mammoth towering behind them like mountains. Trashcan Man gives one last look to Kriegman… TCM: MY LIFE FOR YOU! MY LIFE FOR YOOOOUUUU!!! Nate: WHOOOOO IS GONNA STOP THESE MANIACS!!!!! Robbie: NNNNNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!! And with Ed locked in a cradle piledriver position, leaps off the edge of the ramp, plummeting 15 feet to the concrete below, as the © 2002 OSW logo appears on the bottom of the screen….as the scene fades to black.
