Caridad Mariposa Real Name: Caridad Torres Birthdate: September 23, 1977 Hometown: San Juan, Puerto Rico Height: 5'3' Weight: 115 lbs. Nicknames: Dadi Status: Face Current Teams: Entrance Music: 'Fever Dog' by Stillwater Information Management Style: Valet Signature Moves: Asia Moonsault Acompanies to Ring: Yes OSW Award History: None Other Federations: UWA Other Federation Awards: None Bio Caridad grew up in San Juan, Puerto Rico, in the typical familial style... well, mostly. She was always a tomboy, because the only kids anywhere near her age growing up were her brothers and their friends. Strangely, she always had way more energy than them, and was always the last to quit playing whatever game was their current fad. Her father started taking her and her brothers to wrestling events when she turned ten. The acrobatics of the mostly Mexican wrestlers fasicnated her, and she begged her father to let her learn the sport, even while her brothers joked at her being only a girl. Her optimism never died though, and eventually her father relented, and found someone willing to teach her the basics. His hope was that the actual wear and tear of the sport would discourage her, but she perservered. While she was a quick learner and quite a looker, Caridad was never able to make it to any of the big federations - they just weren't looking to develop their womens division at that time. She met Jesus St Crux at an indy promotion's show in Miami, Florida. They were booked together for one night as mixed-gender opponents for the promotion's top heel and his valet. There was instant chemistry between them and their styles meshed so well that the promoter didn't mind that they won the match. Shortly after that, Jesus was offered a contract by a major federation, and he asked Caridad to join him as a valet/manager... which had the side benefit of letting them spend more time together. Caridad accepted and helped Jesus through his rocket push, the troubled year after the federation's demise, and has signed on with him again in OSW. She's intrigued that OSW doesn't differentiate between the men and women on its roster, and thinks that perhaps one day she'll step up for an in-ring career for herself. Allies Promo Archives Quotes
