GV/Locke vs Ivan/Davis vs Hawk/Toomes vs Walczak/Troy Written: June 1st 2002 As the scene fades in from the commercial break the camera spins wildly for a moment until the shot finally settles in on the announcers’ table were Nate, Robbie, and Steven are seated and waiting to continue the broadcast. Nate: Welcome back to OSW Slam! Coming up next, we have the most dangerous match in all of wrestling! Robbie: YES! Nate: That’s right; our main event is coming up next! The camera shot changes for the moment to see the steel cage being lowered down from the ceiling to the floor surrounding the ring. Robbie: Hell in the Cell Matches have ended the careers of many a fine wrestlers and tonight, with a little luck it will end the careers of a couple of the jobbers scheduled here. Particularly that damn Doug Troy! Steven: What I wouldn’t give to be locked in a cell with Troy! Oh just the thought of it! Robbie: Shut the hell up! Steven: What? I just want the opportunity to go head to head with the champion of OSW that’s all! Nate: Easy boys. Easy! Robbie: Well anyway, we have been forced to watch a lot of worthless threats going on backstage between the wrestlers but finally we are going to see who has the biggest gahonies! Steven: Gahonies! Nate and Robbie can be heard sighing out of frustration. Nate: Not only is this an eight man tag team match, but the front office also took it upon themselves to match up four teams that are composed of sworn enemies! Steven: This one promises to be a match that no one will ever be able to forget even if they wanted to. Robbie: And I am willing to bet that there are several wrestlers booked in this match that will want to forget about the events of tonight! Especially, that damn Doug Troy! Robbie: Damn right!!! Robbie: Enough exposition already! Let’s just take it to Earl! The camera shot changes to Earl Henrich standing in the middle of the ring holding his mic. He raises it slowly as the fans cheer wildly! Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, Boys and Girl, Children of all ages, this next match is a Tag Team Competition and scheduled for one fall! The referee for this match is Pierre Gaston! Coming to the ring first, weighing in at about 285 pounds, and standing at 6 feet eight inches…Graphic Violence!!! Earl: …Andrew Ace Hawk!!! Robbie: There is another son of a bitch that doesn’t deserve to be in this fed, let alone a shot at being one of the starters at the top of the hill at Ascension. HE HAD NO GAS IN HIS TANK LEFT!!! The crowd gasps and goes silent.. Nate: LOCKE HAD LOST A LOT OF BLOOD AND STAGGERED OFF THE CAGE!!! BOTH MEN LOOK TO BE SERIOUSLY HURT!!!! Steven: EVERYONE IS SERIOUSLY HURT!!!! Nate: WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Robbie: NOOOO!!! IVAN IS ABOUT TO BREAK DAVIS’ NECK!!! Nate: WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT!!! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!!! Paramedics begin to rush to the scene as GV, Locke, Walczak, and Davis all lie motionless, covered in their own blood.. Elsewhere, Hawk and Switchblade trade punches in bloodied exhaustion, while Doug Troy sits a bloodied mess in a chair vacated by one of the announce team, with his belt near the unmoving body of Tom Walczak. Stanislav stands a bloody mess in the ring and laughs at Davis, with his arms held high…as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears on the lower, central portion of the screen…as the scene fades to black.
