Mind Games: Trois

2002-04-06
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mind Games: Trois Written: April 6, 2002 The scene fades into the backstage area, where the Silent Assassin, Corg, skulks along quietly. Corg: Hehehe...Time to die, Rhett. Corg slowly pushed the boiler room door open, it creaked, and Corg peered in. Directly in the center of the boiler room, his victim sat in the rocking chair we saw previously, wielding the bat he had previously, and facing the opposite direction. He has no idea Corg is even there. Corg slowly lifts up the steelchair, and charges Rhett. Locke turns around, to find a steelchair slam into his face! Corg lifts Locke's body up by his long hair, and tosses him into the wall. Continuing the brutal assault on Rhett's limp and bloody carcass with a steel chair! But, as Corg lifts Rhett up once more...he realizes a horrible deed... This man... Corg: THAT'S NOT RHETT! Isn't the man he wants to kill. Corg yells at the top of his lungs, and beats the imposter with that steelchair a few more times. Frustrated, infuriated, the Silent-Killer turns around... And is met with a baseball bat to his temple. The REAL Rhett Locke stood over Corg, and began mercilessly assaulting him. Over and over, that bat crashed into Corg's ribs. CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! Rhett shows no mercy to the man that wants him dead, until Moose Malone and the rest of OSW security is there to pull him away from Corg. Blood covered, the Anti God now realizes he's playing with fire just as well. Rhett: I'M GONNA KILL YOU! SON OF A BITCH! JUST WAIT! JUST WAIT! The International champion continues his ranting, continues his screaming as he is dragged out by a few guys. The bloodied Corg was outsmarted... And he hates it.
