Lights Out For 'Lillith'

2002-06-01
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lights Out For 'Lillith' Written: June 1st 2002 Suddenly, Robbie is interrupted by“Tearing Away” by Drowning Pool blasting over the P.A. system. Nate: At least you were interrupted this time… Robbie: Its cool because its GRAPHIC VIOLENCE!!! The fans jump on their feet, booing immediately following Graphic Violence as he steps from the back and onto the entrance ramp. In his grasp would be that now trademarked sledgehammer, and beside him is a woman, her face shrouded by a brown paper bag. From the black dress, however, and the long raven hair flowing from under the bag, everyone gets the hunch... Everyone knows it's Lillith under that paper bag. Nate: WHAT THE HELL!? How did GV get his hands on Lillith!? Robbie: Haha! You're guess is as good as mine! But it's about time somebody got rid of Rhett, and I think the right man was chosen for the job! GV carries her by the arm down the entrance ramp, and rolls her into the ring. Her arms are tied with a rough rope, so every time she tried to wriggle free, a bad rope burn would follow. His eyes trailblaze to the fans, who send a hell of a verbal assault. He snickers, and gives the fans a 'middle-finger salute'. A microphone is soon tossed to him by Earl Henrich. Graphic Violence: Well LOOK who the cat dragged in! Robbie: WOOOOOOO! The smile of a sadist adorn GV's features, and at this moment it only seems fit. The fans boo even louder, chanting 'G-V-SUCKS! G-V-SUCKS!' Graphic Violence: I couldn't HELP but to notice that Locke was nowhere to be found in the arena tonight. . . Kind of strange how he didn't keep his promise to you all. It's a damn shame he's not a man of his word. It's too bad you can't trust a syllable that flows from his mouth. . . And you all know why don't you. . . You all know why he didn't keep his promise? . . . . . Graphic Violence: Because Rhett Locke is a fucking pussy! Robbie: GV! GV! Nate: Shut up, Decker! Steven: I think it's about time GV's ass got reamed! The crowd nearly tear the roof off with boos, and GV opens his arms wide, warmly accepting and appreciating their brood and hatred. Graphic Violence chuckles, just for a moment, and continues. Graphic Violence: If Rhett had ANY balls, he'd be here to-- “LIFE IS A WATERFALL! WE'RE ONE IN THE RIVER AND ONE AGAIN AFTER THE FALL!” 'Aerials' by System of a Down continues to play, and the fans rise to their feet and shout at the top of their lungs for Rhett, as he limps out. His ribs and knee is taped up, and in his hands he wields a steel chair. His dyed black hair covers a good part of his features, but simply by his skin-tone and clothing the fans in attendance knows it's him. Robbie: Damnit, ALWAYS he ruins the show! ALWAYS! Never fails! Nate: Yes! Rhett Locke is here! Thank god! Steven: ...DAMN I LOVE HIM! . . . . . Rhett Locke: Shut. the. fuck. up. you. dumb. piece. of. SHIT! The fans cheer at the top of their lungs, and a 'Rhett!' chant spreads through the arena. Locke motions for them to die down, and as they do, he continues. Rhett Locke: Now I don't know what's going on in that stupid head of yours, but you sure as hell got the wrong idea. Because you see, GV... I NEVER BACK DOWN, AND I SURE AS HELL KEEP MY PROMISES, (expletive) SO... Nate: Tell him like it is, Rhett! Rhett takes the opportunity to calm down as the fans cheer at the top of their lungs for his verbal assault directed to Graphic Violence. Rhett Locke: So... isn't this the time where I walk to the ring and beat the shit out of you? Yeah, I believe it is. Nate: Rhett and GV are gonna get it on! RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!! Steven: Eh? Robbie: WOOO! GV IS GONNA KICK HIS ASS! Rhett drops the mic, and begins to walk (limp) down to the ring. The chair rests in his hands tightly, but at half-way down the ramp, GV's voice calls. Graphic Violence: Hold it right there, Rhett. Hold it right there! You want to make a fool of me, you little bitch? You want to make a fool of me? Well then how about I make a fool... Graphic Violence: ... OF HER! WHAM! Nate: GOD DAMNIT! THAT'S UNCALLED FOR! Lillith falls to the ground, clutching her ribs after GV slams the sledge into her upper-body. The fans erupt into a chorus of boos, and GV exits the ring and moves through the crowd. As Rhett Locke rushes toward the ring to help Lillith... Graphic Violence: LET THAT BE A LESSON LOCKE! Rhett slides into the ring, the boos still flooding the ring area. And they got even louder when Rhett pulled the bag off... And it wasn't Lillith. She's a fake. It isn't even Lillith. Rhett stands, an epitome of anger and hate, watching his sworn enemy exit through the crowd with the help of security. Nate: OH MY GAWD!!! WHO THE HELL IS THAT?!? Robbie: Low cost prostitute?? Nate: (sighing) This is out of hand! We need to head to a commercial!!! Rhett stares at the “fake” Lillith in shock and disbelief. Rhett couldn't help but grab his hair... …and scream…as the scene fades to commercial.
