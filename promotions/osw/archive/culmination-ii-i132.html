Culmination II Written: May 25th, 2002 A loud belching sound echoes throughout the hallways as Cain and Hessian return from the canteen. Hessian wipes some excess meat from his mouth; while Cain licks his fingers clean, licking all the excess grease and sauce and meat from his fingers and face. Cain: Ohhhh that was a damn fine feast. Hessian: I can tell…you still have half of it on your face. Cain: As long as Minion is not hungry…I’m happy. Cain continues cleaning himself off, while Hessian opens the locker room door, and takes a seat on one of the steel chairs. Cain follows him and slams the door shut by kicking it, neither noticing the note taped to the inside of the door. Cain slumps down on one of the benches and begins picking bits of meat from his teeth. Hessian: Hmm…I wonder what Shanda Russell would look like stewing in a pool of BBQ sauce…makes me hungry just thinking about it. Cain: Hessian, as fair as that little bitch is, aren’t you forgetting something? Hessian begins wiping his face with his gauntleted hands, expecting a small piece of meat to fall from the corner of his mouth. Cain: No, no not that…aren’t we forgetting SOMEONE… Hessian looks around the room, and sighs. Hessian: Oh them, hmph, don’t worry about Minion or Caleb; they’re probably off ogli-……… As Hessian points out the door, his eyes meet with the envelope, and his jaw drops. Slowly getting to his feet, Cain queries as Hessian moves towards the door, then spots the envelope and sits up. Cain: What is that, Hessian? Hessian pulls the envelope from the door and rips it open. Taking the note out, he looks it over, and then looks at Cain. Hessian: I do not (expletive) believe it. I DO NOT (expletive) (expletive) BELIEVE IT!!! Hessian roars out and elbows the door, leaving a nicely shaped hole, and a long crack extending all the way up and down the door. Cain sighs and takes the note from Hessian, and begins reading. Cain: Dear Champs. It has come to our attention that you are looking for a war, so be it, let the games begin. It doesn’t matter that you’re tag team champions, we will beat yo’ asses down…oh my god they actually wrote “yo’”…you have no hope of surviving against us, we have beaten you many times before and we will do it again! Heresian Bloodlust will fall, starting with your little bitch Imp and Hobo. If you want them back then step up and face us like men for once! We’ll be waiting…Signed – The next OSW Tag Champs. Hessian: NOBODY STEALS MY IMP AND GETS AWAY WITH IT!!! Cain: Well who could have written it? It’s actually well composed for either Civil D or Da Westsidaz. Civil D is stupid enough to steal Minion and Caleb, but Riptah and Short Circuit are good enough to steal Minion and Caleb. Then again, Civil D have beaten us many times befo-… Hessian: I DON’T CARE!!! I’M GONNA KILL ‘EM ALL!!! Hessian throws the door open and charges out. Cain listens as Hessian screams bloody murder rampaging through the hallways. Smirking, he wipes the last of the BBQ sauce from his mouth then drops the note and follows his fellow Tag Team Champion after the kidnappers as the scene fades out.
