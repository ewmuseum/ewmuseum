Another METII-related boycott?

22 August 2003

by Dynamic Tension

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own news post (#24) by Dynamic Tension, dated 22 August 2003, from the MET II site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
SACRAMENTO - In the wake of the shocking announcement by Damon O'Halloran and Terlayne Sterling, Into the Void, regarding their status in the 'Most Extreme Team II' tournament, another bombshell was dropped by a participant in the contest. In a press conference held earlier today, one half of the tag team Dynamic Tension, David Fozinelli, announced his intentions to begin a boycott of his own. A boycott of the Piggly Wiggly convenience store on Truxel Road in Sacramento, CA. 'It was a hard decision to make,' Fozinelli elaborated, 'but there comes a point in any situation where a man has to say 'enough.' And unfortunately, I have reached that state of mind with the honorable Mr. Wiggly and his employees.' When asked to elaborate more on the reasoning for the boycott, Fozinelli was quick to list the abuses. 'They have callously and deliberately discriminated against certain members of the community, blocked them from gaining entry into the hallowed halls of consumer goods... they have plundered our seas, ravaged our coasts, burnt our towns, and destroyed the lives of our people.' 'And also,' Fozinelli amended, 'they told me I was too big to ride the Captain Stupendous Rocket to Mars Ride out front. I fit just fine!' Alexander Pimilkin, the manager of the local Piggly Wiggly, disputed Fozinelli's claims. 'That guy? I kicked him out of the store five weeks ago for stealing Ding-Dongs. He's a freaking thief, and if he thinks for a second I'm letting him back into this store, he's even dumber than that goofy grin of his makes him look.' But Fozinelli protested his own innocence in the press conference. 'For no good reason, I have been blacklisted, blackballed, blackeverythinged by this store of deceit and lies, and I will not desist in my boycott until my demands are answered. If places like this are allowed to operate in such a manner, it won't be long before we feel the harsh metal hooves of Piggly Wiggly marching through the streets, his porcine reign of terror a blight upon all that is decent and right in this world, and his hideous, hateful oinks echoing down every street.' When asked about his situation with OSE and the MET II tournament, Foznielli was less flustered. 'I've been having a great time. I mean, I'm anxious to get out there and fight and all, but I understand how these things go. I could use the extra time to get back into fighting shape. Although every time I try to call Rhi[annon Rogers, my partner] to come over to my place to practice our moves, she always says she has a headache or she's lactating or something. Women and their biologies, who understands it? Wait... what was I saying?' The difficulties with Piggly Wiggly and Pimilkin, however, weigh heavily on his mind before his first matchup. 'The many abuses of the Piggly Wiggly cannot and will not stand. I will not rest until I have been given due and just compensation for the pain and anguish I have suffered at the hands of these tyrants. Oh, and free Slurpees. Compensation for pain and anguish, and free Slurpees. That'll do me fine.' Asked about his chances to receive compensation, Foznielli was optimistic. 'Throughout the records of human history, there have been many moments when mankind was held in the grips of tyranny and oppression. There were times when everyone was too afraid to stand up and fight against those who would try to reduce them to lower-class citizens. Everyone... except for one man. In all those moments in history, it just took one man to stand up and fight for the little guy. One man was able to beat back the bad men, and rescue the dignity of the common folk. All of these terrible moments, and just one man was able to save the day for everyone.' 'And that one man... is Batman,' Fozinelli concluded. 'So, I'm gonna try to call him.' Reaching into a pocket of his trenchcoat, Fozinelli then pulled out a flashlight with a crude paper bat shape taped to it and waved it across the ceiling for several minutes while singing the theme song to the 'Batman' television series of the '60s. After several minutes of this, and after all attempts to ask further questions went unanswered except for repeated utterances of 'Da na na na na na na na, BATMAN!', the press conference was assumed over and everyone left.
