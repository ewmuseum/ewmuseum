'Comrade' Ed??

2002-03-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Comrade' Ed?? Written: March 16, 2002 The scene reopens in the backstage area outside of Ed’s makeshift office. A small nail is punched into the door with a small card hanging from it with “Commissioner Ed” scrawled in what appears to be magic marker. From the left, Ivan Stanislav, Alexei Ruslan, and Loyal Communist Party Member Wang walk on camera and stand outside the door. The three pause for a moment and then nod to each other. Stanislav reaches for the doorknob and pushes the door open, walking straight into Ed’s office with Wang and Ruslan in tow. Ed sits behind his desk, papers scattered all over the place and a look of general stress on his face. He looks up from his papers suddenly and his scowl of frustration instantly turns into a disgusted frown, then stands up with his fists resting on the desk. Ed: Stanislav. Here to make my day that much worse? Stanislav smiles arrogantly and pulls up one of the chairs facing the Commissioners desk and sits, crossing a leg nonchalantly. Ruslan and Wang stand to his left and right. Ivan props his elbow on another chair in a very conversational manner. Ivan: Ed, do sit down, do not alarm yourself. Ivan grins and points to his chair. Ed slowly sits in his chair, eyeing Stanislav and his two cohorts. Ed: Let's get this over with. You hear to ask for a match, rant about your life, or punch things and threaten me? Ivan laughs, leans forward, and slaps one of his huge hands on the table, shaking it violently. Ivan: DYAAHAA! Ivan thought he would stop by and pay visit to thank you! Ed: Thank me? Ivan: Da! I wish to thank you for being such short sighted pawn! For being lapdog of Jane Reagan! For bringing Doug Troy in to federation only for him to go renegade! For introducing Meanstreak again to combat me, only to find you cannot control him yourself! Ed growls as Ivan hits a nerve. He tries to cut of Stanislav… Ed: Hey… Ivan: I find it ironic, do you not? Men like Vampir, Great Dragon, Heresian Bloodlust, and Propaganda run amok and wreak havoc now! Meanstreak attacks the innocent! Doug Troy preaches anarchy and our beloved President stabs us all in back and leaves YOU to pick up pieces! Ivan grins ear to ear, as if this is good news to him. He pauses and thinks, and then continues… Ivan: And think, throughout all of this there is only ONE constant factor that can combat such random and…unforeseen violence…. Ivan stands, suddenly growing angry, Ed grits his teeth but stays put…. Ivan: ….US! The Red Army! The only mobilized force of wrestlers present! Ed jumps to his feet and slams his fist on the top of the desk, literally shaking. Ed: If you think for ONE moment I would even THINK about trusting you... Ivan laughs louder as Ruslan and Wang chime in and laugh as well. Ivan: You know you have no other choice, Ed. It is funny how suddenly my cause may not be as ‘evil’ as others present! Tonight, Ed, I will do you favor! Ivan leans forward and puts his huge hands on the desk, talking just above a whisper. Ivan: I will destroy Heresian Bloodlust for you, crush Meanstreak, and continue what I have always done. I will do you great favor, Ed…. Ivan pauses for a moment and glares at Ed, with an underlying venom in his words… Ivan: Tonight, Heresian Bloodlust will become nothing, Meanstreak will be subdued, and I will personally take care of your little…renegade Doug Troy. With those groups finished off, there is only one more target to attack. You know who it is. And then, you will realize what alternatives you truly have…. Ivan starts to back away, glaring at Ed… Ivan: You know what you must do….you just do not wish to admit it….Comrade… Ivan laughs and produces a cassette tape from his pocket and tosses it on the desk. The camera focuses on the words on the tape, “Soviet National Anthem.” Ivan: Perhaps something you should listen to…DYAAAHAAAHAA!! Ivan, Ruslan, and Wang turn and walk out the door, leaving Ed to stare at the tape on the desk….
