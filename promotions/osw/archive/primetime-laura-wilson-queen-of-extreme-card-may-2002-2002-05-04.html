Primetime Laura Wilson Real Name: Laura Wilson Birthdate: April 17, 1979 Hometown: Sidney Australia Height: 5'9 Weight: 145 lbs. Nicknames: The Perfect One, Status: Face Manager(s): Victor Kraatz Current Teams: Entrance Music: 'Girlfight' by Theodore Shapiro Wrestling Style: High Flyer Signature Moves: Moonsault, Ankle Lock, Figure Four Leglock, Rocker Dropper, Shooting Star Set Up Move: Ariel Assult (Corkscrew Moonsault ) Finisher: The Primetime Record (Modified Fisherman's Suplex) OSW Title History: OSW Award History: Other Federations: DiWF Other Federation Awards: DiWF Middleweight Champion Handler Information Handler Name: Bethany AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio It all started in Sidney Australia in the year 1979, because that's when the female legend 'Primetime' Laura Wilson was born. She grew up with three brothers, named: Bryon, Jed, and Mark. They were in their teens by the time that Laura was four. Bryan and Jed watched a bit of the WWF and they wanted to start their own federation, so they made the first Backyard Fed in Sidney. They trained likely people to wrestle for three years, and they had a pretty sucessful run. That's when the faithful day that a talent producer came from Alanta Georgia and sought out the Backyard Federation, and signed Bryan, Mark and Jed to an imediate contract. The Three Brothers left immedietly, to Georgia the next day and said their goodbyes while promising Laura that once she was older, they were grey, and they were sucessful in the Wrestling Busness, that they would reserve a spot for her. Years went by, and by the time that Laura was Nine years old, she had a partial intrest in all types of Martial Arts, Weather it be Ji-Jetsu, or Krate, she loved it all. She was enrolled in Karate and quickly got to the ranks of Black belt in one year. Once she was finished with Karate, at the age of 10, she went to the High Risk Taking path, which involved going into the Outback alone for 8 hours. When she was about 14 her mother died from Lukemia, and her father had left the family when she was still a baby, so the next day she found the backyard federation that her brothers had started. She trained there, until a talent searcher came from DRWF came, and signed her to a contract right away. That's when she met Aimz. She got signed to the DRWF at about the same time, and both became friends. Now, she is looking to get more and more respect in other federations like the DiWF which she is the Middleweight Champion.
