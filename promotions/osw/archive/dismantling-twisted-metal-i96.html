Dismantling Twisted Metal

2002-02-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dismantling Twisted Metal Written: 23/02/2002 A gong and the dark chant, “Carmina Burana” blares through the PA system and the lights go out completely in the arena. Two massive silhouettes appear at the entranceway and quickly make their way down to the ring. Nate: Oh God! We all know what that means. Steven: YES WE DO!! It means the match just got all the more interesting! The lights come back on and Hessian and Azrael Cain are both down by ringside. Hessian is yelling at Lovejoy interrupting the count on the opposite side of the ring distracting her while Cain climbs up into the ring. Nate: This is never good. This usually means that someone is going to the hospital. Steven: Yes it does. And usually it is Civil Disobedience! Ha! Robbie: Betty! Hey, Betty! Quit yelling at Destro over there and look in the ring. BETTY!!!! Cain crosses over to Gunmetal and places the four hundred pound robot on top of Hawke. Hessian starts pointing inside the ring trying to get her attention so she will make the count. Nate: Heresian Bloodlust is costing Civil Disobedience this match! Steven: Yes, they most certainly are! Lovejoy turns around as Cain exits the ring and jumps down to the floor outside the ring. The beautiful bombshell ref looks at Cain and stares at him as he goes around the outside of the ring to Hessian. Lovejoy gets down and begins to make the count. 1… Hessian reaches down to grab the dazed and confused Masters by the hair. Hessian pulls Masters’ head up so he can see Lovejoy make the count on his partner. Masters begins to squirm. 2… 3… Winners of the match: Twisted Metal! Nate: Twisted Metal has done it! They have won their first match in OSW against the formerly undefeated Civil Disobedience! Robbie: Oh don’t get so excited! It’s not like it was legit. That beast, Cain, was the one that placed Gunmetal across Hawke’s chest for the pin. Steven: Yeah we know! That was great! Hessian looks at Masters then slams his head into the ring post. A loud “Clang” noise is heard as Masters’ head bounces and the blood begins to flow from his forehead. Cain and Hessian climb up to the apron and over the top rope to enter the ring. Lovejoy tries to stop them, but both men just walk right by her. Steven: This is going to be great! Hessian Cain look down at Hawke who is now trying to get out from underneath the four hundred pound robot, Gunmetal. Hessian effortlessly heaves the robot off of Hawke. Cain reaches down and hoists Hawke up to his feet. Cain then throws Hawke to Hessian. Hessian irishwhips the suburban wrestler into the ropes who comes back off them at great speed until he is Back Body Dropped by Hessian. Hawke flies through the air until he is caught by Cain right out of mid air and drops Hawke into a Death Valley Driver. Hawke bounces high into the air off the canvas, but lands hard again and isn’t moving. Steven: HA! Heretic’s Wrath! I love that move! Nate: Oh my God! Where the hell is Moose? Steven: Probably hiding. You saw what Cain did to him last week! HA! Someone from the officials’ table tosses a microphone to Hessian. Hessian turns and approaches his partner Cain who is now holding Hawke’s head up so he can hear what Hessian is about to say. Hessian crouches down right in front of Hawke. Hessian: Hello boy! Did you miss me last week? I’m sorry that I couldn’t attend our match, but as you know I was a little preoccupied. Hessian headbutts Hawke in the face with his metal mask. Hawke’s nose begins to bleed profusely. Cain releases his Hawke’s head letting it fall to the mat. Hessian and Cain both rise to their feet now to address the booing fans. Hessian tosses the mic to Cain. Cain: Even though the two of us are preoccupied tonight in far more important matches, we still felt that is was necessary to come down tonight to let Civil Disobedience know we were thinking about them! We just wanted to remind everyone who the best tag team in OSW really is. We felt that it would be only suiting to show up tonight and make sure that despite the fact that we weren’t scheduled to fight you, that you would still lose your match! Chico has climbed into the ring now behind Cain and Hessian. The robot crosses over to Heresian Bloodlust. Robbie: What the hell is that damn robot doing? Chico taps both Cain and Hessian on their shoulders. Both men turn around to see who was foolish enough to interrupt them. Chico looks at them and after a second of uncomfortable ness, Chico extends his hands in order to thank Cain and Hessian for helping them win the match. Nate: I have a feeling that this is going to be bad, very bad. Steven: On the contrary. This is going to be great! Cain and Hessian look at one another for a second. They then both extend their arms and begin to shake Chico’s hands. Robbie: What the hell is going on in there? Both Hessian and Cain suddenly tug on Chico’s arms. Both arms are torn out of their sockets. Sparks are flying out of the holes where Chico’s arms usually are. Cain and Hessian are both holding an arm in their hands. Both men share a look for a moment. They proceed to beat the armless robot with his own severed arms. Steven: Ha! Nate: That just isn’t right! Chico is trying to run away, but without his arms he has no balance. Hessian and Cain continue to pound away at the damaged robot. Chico topples over shortly and falls to the mat as Heresian Bloodlust continue until there is no movement from Chico. Nate: This is disgusting! Someone call the EMT’s!!! Robbie: Forget the EMTs call the morgue. Steven: NOOOO! Crowd: OSW! OSW! OSW! OSW! OSW! EMTs rush down to the ring carrying their equipment…. as the scene fades to commercial.
