Omega Written: March 10, 2002 The blood already flowing from Drew's ear is joined by fresh juice from his eyebrow and nose; soon, both men are nothing but crimson shadows, and one of them continues punching mercilessly away. Finally, satisfied, Locke drags his opponent away from the ropes, and lets himself drop wearily to make the cover. Once again, Betty makes the cover, only this time well away from the growing puddle of plasma. 1... 2... 3! Winner and NEW International Champion: RHETT LOCKE Nate: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! SOME HOW, SOME WAY, RHETT LOCKE HAS DONE IT! HE'S BECOME THE CHAMPION! Robbie: LOOK WHAT HE DID TO DREW HOSTYLE! HE'S A SICK, SICK MAN! Steven: HE'S NEVER CLAIMED TO BE A KNIGHT IN SHINING ARMOR! HE'S A FIGHTER! AND A RED HOT ONE! Nate: RHETT LOCKE HAS WON! HE'S WON! HE'S WON THE TITLE! The referee raises Locke’s hands and hands him the title belt, while the crowd cheers him on. Meanwhile, Hostyle rolls out of the ring and staggers towards the back. Nate: THE CROWD IS PUMPED AND THEY HAVE GOOD REASON TO BE!!! RHETT LOCKE IS THE NEW OSW INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION!!! Suddenly, the crowd’s cheers turn to boos. Steven: LOOOOOOK!!! The camera pans out, as the Silent Killer, Corg, slides into the ring, behind the jubilant, yet excited Rhett Locke. Nate: LOOK OUT!!!! Locke realizes something is wrong, and turns around…..face first into a nasty superkick from Corg. The completely off guard Locke goes down in a heap. Nate: CORG JUST SUCKER KICKED THE NEW INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION!!! Corg licks his black lips and smiles, as the crowd boos. He then kneels down over Locke and pulls a microphone from his pocket. Corg: I finished off once cancer on the womb of OSW tonight in Ritchie Syxx…..Now you are next on my black list. The freakish Corg looks down at the equally “different-looking”, though unmoving Locke. Corg: I’d have to admit, though, I do like your sense of style. Corg looks down at the “Omega Symbol” nose ring, which hangs from the septum of Locke. With won quick yank, he rips the ring from Locke’s nose, sending blood spouting everywhere. The crowd is outraged. Nate: WHERE THE HELL IS SECURITY!!! Corg laughs grimly, as he jams the nose ring forcefully through his own septum, causing blood to gush from his nose. Corg: (laughing) This is only the first of many shiny things I will be taking from you, Missssster Lock-eeeee…..The first of many….. Nate: CORG HAS JUST…GOOD GOD, YOU ALL SAW WHAT JUST HAPPENED!!! THIS IS DISGRACEFUL DISPLAY, WHICH WILL HOPEFULLY BE REMOVED FROM MY BRAIN DURING OUR NEXT COMMERCIAL!!! WE WILL BE RIGHT BACK!!! The scene fades to commercial focused on Corg, who laughs maniacally as the blood from his nose runs into his mouth and “Sweet Dreams' by Marlyn Manson hits the PA system….
