Interupting Shanda.

2002-07-06
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Interupting Shanda. Written: 7/6/02 Shift to the backstage area with Shanda Russell. The reporter stands next to four-time PCW Cruiserweight champion, Kid Wonder. Shanda: I’m standing here with Kid Wonder. Kid, later tonight you will face newcomer Scott Taylor with the number one contendership to Corg’s OSW Cruiserweight title on the line. Now, you’re known for excelling in these type of matches - matches of the high-flying variety - so this is nothing new for you. But your opponent is. So, tell me, what are your thoughts going into tonight? Kid Wonder, decked out in his wrestling gear including blue genie sequin pants, looks down at Shanda as she sticks the microphone in K-Dub’s face. Wonder: My thoughts on what? On the match or on Scott Taylor? Shanda looks a bit surprised. She didn’t expect a question thrown in her direction. Shanda: At…at both if you don’t mind. Wonder: Well, Shanda, my thoughts on the match are I’ve got no thoughts on the match. My mind isn’t on what’s supposed to be a match. My mind is focused on revenge. Revenge against my opponent. Revenge against one Scott Taylor. Shanda: I take it you don’t like Scott very much. Wonder: Don’t like him? Kid Wonder smirks. Wonder: Heh. That’s putting it kindly. Let’s just say I loathe Scott Taylor. Shanda: That bad? Wonder: It’s bad. Now I can understand why he’d attack me. I mean, being wrestling’s premier cruiserweight, I’ve got a giant bulls-eye on my back. Every single person who wants to become someone in the division usually guns for me first. Shanda: But Corg is the… Kid Wonder puts an open palm up to Shanda’s face signaling for her to stop talking. She obliges. Wonder: I know Corg is the champ. Good for him. He beat a girl, Amy Campbell, twice to win the thing. I bet he’s proud of himself. But, you see Shanda, to me, the belt is just a bonus. I know I’m the best cruiserweight around. I don’t need the belt to prove it. If you ever watched that other show, you’d know that. Shanda: I’m not denying what you’re capable of. I’m just saying that… Again, K-Dub cuts her off with his hand. Wonder: The title belt is just a prop. It’s just something that someone carries around to boost their ego. And I won’t lie…I want the thing. But I don’t need some title to prove myself. My actions do that for me. The title just looks good on the résumé. It looks good for those ignorant little people who come from elsewhere, such as Scott Taylor, who can’t be bothered to look at or talk about anything other than gold. Shanda: Speaking of Scott Taylor…your thoughts on him? Wonder: Like I was saying before, I can understand why he attacked me. Hell, I’ve kinda gotten use to these newbies and posers gunning for me. It comes with the job. But then to go and say I use my “stroke” to get where I’m at… Kid Wonder stops talking. Wonder rubs his chin and snickers. Wonder: I dunno what “stroke” Taylor is talking about, but if me using my “stroke” is what he wants, then that’s what I shall do. I’m gonna take my right hand… Kid Wonder raises his right hand for the camera to see. Wonder (continuing): …roll it into a fist… K-Dub makes a fist with his right hand. Wonder (continuing): …and I’m gonna stroke it again and again, as hard as I can, against that little punk’s jaw. KW punches the palm of his left hand with his right fist. Wonder: Let’s see how much Scott likes that. 'Let's see how you like this!' An unseen person speaks, both Kid Wonder and Shanda turn to look in the direction of the new voice. Shanda jumps back as a fist connects with the jawline of The Teen Idol. Kid staggers back holding his jaw as none other than his opponent, Scott Taylor, steps into the scene. Shanda uses her intelligence and gets away from the scene before it becomes too violent. Scott throws another punch at Wonder but Wonder throws up his arm and manages to block the attack. He returns his own right hand and sends Taylor back, he follows up with a kick to the stomach and then he grabs a handful of Taylor's hair whipping him into the chain link fence in the background. Wonder: You're not so tough now, are you Taylor?! Wonder walks over to where Scott is hunched over on the ground, as Wonder kneels down to grab Scott, Scott swings upward holding something in his hands, hitting K-Dub in the head with it. It's a....stereo? Yes, a stereo left behind by Head Audio/Visual Technician Mike Lasandra infact. Kid Wonder falls back hitting the concrete floor hard as Taylor stands to his feet. Scott holds the side of his head as he walks towards the fallen Kid Wonder. Scott pauses above Kid Wonder and looks down at him. He stomps Wonder's body a few times He takes a break from the stomping and kicks him stiffly in the ribs. Scott looks up and notices Shanda Russell standing around the corner watching the second attack on Kid Wonder form the safety of her vantage point. Scott: Hey, Shanda. Get over here and bring that microphone with you! Shanda obliges Scott and slowly walks towards him, she stops infront of him and holds out her microphone. Scott snatches it from her hand and then pushes her away. The young lady staggers back. almost falling to the ground. She leaves before anything else can happen. With Shanda gone Scott looks down at Kid Wonder and kicks him again before speaking into the microphone. Scott: Where's your 'stroke' now K-Dub? You're not so tough are you, Mr Four Time PCW Cruiserweight Champion and PCW Wrestler of The Year 2000? It's the people like you that make me sick! It's the people like you that kept me out of PCW! But guess what? You didn't keep me out of OSW! I'm here Wonderboy, what are you going to do?! Wonder begins to stir again so Scott delivers yet another boot to his jaw, knocking him back down again. Scott: As I was saying before you so rudely interupted me, what are you going to do tonight Wonder? You can't even handle me in a brawl, how are you going to stand up to me in a technical match? I didn't think so... With those words Scott hurls the microphone down at Kid Wonder, the impact of the mic on his chest knocking the breath out of him and making that loud static crackle sound. Scott walks away from the scene, leaving Kid Wonder laying for the second time in two weeks and leaving Shanda Russell stunned.
