Heresian Bloodlust vs. 3B vs. Twisted Metal vs. Jesse Ramey & Michael Dame

2002-03-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Heresian Bloodlust vs. 3B vs. Twisted Metal vs. Jesse Ramey & Michael Dame Written: 23/03/2002 Nate: Assets yes…qualified? Er….In any event, we are just about ready to get underway with our next match of the evening, which will be to determine who the #1 contender for the OSW Tag Team titles will be, Steven: This match is going to involve a lot of wood. There is dead air for 1…..2…..3 seconds. Nate: (clearing his throat) That’s correct, Steven, as it is a four corners, table elimination match featuring Heresian Bloodlust, Twisted Metal, 3B and the team of Michael Dame and Jesse Ramey. Robbie: I like the Brothas from 3B to take this. Toomes’ winning ways have been running off on them, plus they are friends of the man banging the ownerette, so its all but in the bag. Nate: (disgusted) Let’s send it up to Earl Henrich in the ring. The camera shot changes to a smiling Earl Henrich who is standing in the ring waiting to make the introductions for the next match. Earl brings the mic up to his mouth in order to begin. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, this next match is scheduled between four tag teams in order to determine who the number one contender’s for the Tag Team Titles will be. The match is scheduled for one fall… Steven: Why bother Earl? Why don’t you just ask Decker here who is going t win then we won’t have to watch the match. Nate slumps even further into his seat as Decker sits up straight and acts like some sort of omnipotent ruler. Earl lowers his mic and shoots a confused look at Smith. Steven: Well, at least we wouldn’t have to listen to some washed up wrestler that couldn’t hack the difficulties of the ring only to become a ring announcer. I mean come on! And there are people that call me a Queen! HA! Earl’s teeth a grinding in his mouth as he glares at the now laughing Smith. Earl closes his eyes and begins to breathe heavy in order to collect himself. After about five seconds, he continues. Earl: Coming to the ring first, coming to you from Atlantic City, New Jersey and weighing in at a combined weight of 800lbs… “Jesus Built my Hotrod” by Ministry begins to play. The lights dim as two short figures appear on the entryway. Earl: …they’re Chico and Gunmetal…Twisted Metal!!!! The lights come back up to reveal the two robots posing for the booing fans. The robots take a moment of posing like many humans do. They look at each other and give a nod to one another. Chico and Gunmetal quickly run down to the ring. Chico and Gunmetal hop up onto the apron and duck under the ropes. They are in the ring waiting for their opponents. Steven: Are those the winners of the next match, Decker? Robbie: Nope. Steven: Doh! Earl: Coming to the ring next, weighing in at the combined weight of 454 pounds. The tag team composed of Bad Attitude Jessey Ramey and Michael Dame! Coming to the ring first, from Minneapolis, Minnesota… “One” from Metallica begins to play. Earl: He is Michael Dame!!! Dame emerges from the backstage area and waits on the ramp for his partner to come out from the back. Earl: And his partner, coming from Harts, West Virginia… “Snap your fingers, Snap Your Neck” replaces Dame’s music. Earl: Jessey Ramey!!! Ramey appears next to Dame. The two men immediately make their way to the ring as the there is a mix of cheers and boos for the tag team. Nate: And the fans are still unsure how to take this tag team. Steven: Are these the winners? Robbie: No way! You think these guys could actually compete as a tag team? Come one Smithy! Get with it! Steven: Oh, come on! Just tell us already! Nate: Steven, for god’s sake! He doesn’t really know what the outcome of the match is going to be! He is just pulling your chain! Do you understand? Steven: I think so… Earl: The next team to come to the ring…weighing in at the impressive combined weight of 725 pounds! They come from parts unknown… “Carmina Burana” begins to pierce the PA system. The lights dim and the all too familiar firewall appears in front of the entryway as a bloody pentagram spreads across the jumbotron. Two massive silhouettes appear behind the flames. Earl: Azrael Cain and Hessian!!! Heresian Bloodlust!!! Hessian and Cain walk through the flames unscathed. The two giants begin to walk down the steelramp without taking their eyes off of their opponents that are already in the ring. Both men get to the apron and easily climb to the top. Each of them swing their legs over the top rope with ease all the while staring down their opponents. Nate: It seems as if Hessian and Cain are here tonight with only one thing on their minds.’ Robbie: Yeah, winning!!! Steven: So, they are the ones that are going to win this match! Nate groans in utter frustration before he starts to pound away at Smith. Dumas is wielding his clipboard like a claymore sword as he uses the force of both hands on the weapon to beat away at the now screaming Smith. Nate: HE *whack DOESN'T *whack KNOW WHO *whack THE WINNERS *whack ARE!!!!! *whack Steven: Okay…Okay…just stop already! Ouch! Earl: And the final team to come to the ring, coming to you from Brooklyn, New York! Weighing in at a combined weight of 543 pounds… “Fear of a Black Planet” by Public Enemy begins to play as the two figures emerge together from the backstage area. Earl: Two of the three members of Brooklyn Born and Bred! They are Drew Hostyle and Frankie Bones! Bones and Hostyle sprint down the steelramp towards their opponents. Inside the ring though, Dame has been knocked out of the ring by a big boot to the face by Cain, while Hessian is taking it to Chico immediately with repeated punches to the robots face. Nate: Heresian Bloodlust is starting to clean house before the ref has even signaled for the bell! Robbie: Wooooo! Steven: I’m opening bets right now. Who do you think will be the first one injured? Nate: Hey Gaston, you going to ring the bell to get this one started anytime soon? Gaston is standing in the ring with his mouth hanging wide open. Ramey is hit by a clothesline by Gunmetal, which sends the human stumbling backwards into the ropes. Just as Hostyle and Bones are about to get into the ring, Bones is met with a flying Chico sent from Hessian that topples both bodies to the concrete floor with a loud thud. Cain steps out onto the apron as the only tag partner that is not in the ring, but still ready for a tag. Steven: Okay…so you can’t bet on Chico or Bones, but anyone else is still fair game! Nate: It seems as if we are going to see Hessian, Gunmetal, Hostyle, and Ramey all square off first! Robbie: Look at the bodies that are already strewn about! This match is going to be a glorious occasion! Gaston finally signals for the bell to ring in order for the match to officially start. Hostyle runs at the giant Hessian and attempts a spear, but Hessian grabs him out of the air and spins him around slams him into the mat with a perfect Powerbomb. Hessian looks over the stunned Hostyle for a second, but the Goliath is struck from behind by Gunmetal who was just Back Body Dropped by a retaliating Ramey. Hessian stumbles forward only to be tripped to the mat by Hostyle who is still on the mat. Nate: Hostyle finally managed to stop the onslaught from Hessian. Robbie: No way in hell! The Goliath is going to reap all of the souls of those that get in his way! Nate: And what now? Are you suddenly a faithful Satanist, as well as communist? You need to get your affiliations straight here, Decker! Steven: Quit bickering for chrissakes! Will you two look at this match! This is all out Bedlam! Ramey is trying to kick away at the robot, Gunmetal, but each blow hurts Ramey’s foot more and more. Ramey finally gets down on top of the robot and pound away at Gunmetal’s head by bouncing it off of the mat. While on the other side of the ring, Hessian has been placed in a fierce Ankle Lock by Hostyle. Nate: Hostyle has a submission move on the Goliath! This one might be over sooner then we all thought! Steven: No! It can’t end! No one has even gotten maimed yet! Robbie: There is no way that that Ankle Lock is going to cause Hessian to lose! Hessian is facing down on the mat. With a huge display of strength and flexibility Hessian flops over to his back and boots Hostyle in the chest. The force of the kick propels Hostyle backwards into Ramey and Gunmetal. All three of the opponents are on top of one another with a very stunned look on their faces. Robbie: Told you! Steven: Wow! He does know about all of the surprises! Nate: No he doesn’t, he just guessed that Hessian was going to over power that move. Hessian gets back to his feet and tags in his partner, Cain. Both partners walk over to the pile of opponents and begin to kick away at them. Hessian leans over hoists up Ramey with one arm before delivering a One Handed Scoop Slam. Hostyle is on the ground holding his back in agony. Cain grabs pulls Gunmetal to his feet and irishwhips him across the ring. Nate: Incredible strength shown by the big man. The robot is about to get to the ropes, but instead of bouncing off of them, he flips over the top down onto the concrete floor. Gunmetal lands hard onto the floor right by his partner Chico who has finally fought his way back to his feet. Nate: What the?! Steven: It’s Dame! He was holding down the top rope! Hessian is being backed out of the ring by Gaston. The ref is distracted though. He doesn’t see Bones who has climbed up on the turnbuckle at the other side of the ring. Bones leaps off of the post and hits Cain with a Missile Drop Kick. The Beast falls to the mat hard from the kick. Ramey is crawling for Dame. Robbie: Hey, Gaston! Turn around will ya? Bones leans over and helps Ramey get to Dame by kicking him in the ribs twice. Ramey flops over into his corner while Bones is now helping his partner out of the ring. Cain is slowly getting back to his feet and shaking out the cobwebs. Gunmetal has managed to tag in Chico who is now entering the ring. Ramey reaches up and finally tags in Dame. Nate: And here comes Dame. And it looks like he is going right for Bones! Steven: This is going to be great! Come on guys! I want to see some blood! Hostyle is out of the ring. Hessian has stopped distracting Gaston. Nate: And the ref can finally start officiating this damn match! Dame and Bones immediately lock up. Cain is back on his feet and charging at Chico. Dame Irish Whips Bones into the ropes and catches him as he bounces off with a sleeper hold. Cain lunges at Chico and clotheslines him. Robbie: Everything finally slowing down a bit and this actually looks like a wrestling match. Nate: Yeah, who’d a thunk it? Gaston takes a quick glanse at the carnage surrounding the ring and Bones sees his chance. He drills Dame with a mule kick to the junk. Nate: OH, low blow there by Bones. Why does that not surprise me? Robbie: Because you know how good Bones is? Nate: Hardly. Suddenly a small figure sprints out of the entrance way. Robbie: Hey! Speaking of hardly, here comes Hardy, to the rescue. Nate: Oh damn, that little bastard has a steel chair, guess Dame is done for. Robbie: No way, he told Dame he’d prove that he wasn’t really in on it. Nate: I’ll believe it when I see it. Frankie stands defiantely in the ring, taunting the crowd. Meanwhile, Dame cowers near the ropes, apparently the low blow connected just right. Hardy runs up to Dame and begins tapping him in the shoulder. Dame looks at him and Hardy offers the chair, Dame shakes his head, telling Hardy he doesn’t need the chair. As Dame pulls himself up by the ropes, Hardy climbs up onto the ring apron. He insists that Dame take the chair, but Dame animately declines. Dame turns and begins sizing up the still boasting Bones. Hardy stands on the apron, apparently deep in thought. Nate: Oh here we go, he should have never turned his back to the kid. Robbie: Hell, he should have taken the chair. Sure enough, Hardy draws the chair back over his head and drills Dame in the back of the head. Dame drops like a stone and Hardy tosses the chair to Bones. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! The crowd erupts into a chorus of boos. The carnage around the ring is coming to and begin staring at the events taking place in the ring. A deranged look glazes over Frankie Bones’s eyes and he begins nailing Dame with chair shots. After hitting Dame a few good times in the chest, Frankie tosses the chair out of the ring and glances at Hostyle and Hardy. They all nod and Frankie climbs out of the ring. Nate: This man is sick. Where the hell is he going now? Robbie: He’s going to get a chair stupid, to start eliminating people. Frankie, Drew, and Hardy gather outside the ring and begin walking up the entrance way. Nate: WHERE THE HELL ARE THEY GOING!?!?! Robbie: (covering) Perhaps they feel they don’t have to be puppets of the white man’s rules?!?! The camera shifts to the stage at the entrance way, as Bones, Hostyle and Hardy all disappear into the back. Nate: Well I guess 3B have eliminated themselves? It appears as if Chico is taking advantage of the current situation here in the ring. Chico slides back into the ring, and pulls a table in with him. He quickly sets up the table and grabs a battered Michael Dame, placing him on the table. He looks around, shrugs, and backs into the ropes. He bounces off and leaps, performing a text book elbow drop, driving Dame through the table. Michael Dame and Jesse Ramey eliminated. Robbie: Well that looked easy enough. Look at the robot bastard celebrating. Nate: That’s a mistake. As Chico celebrates, he is interupted by a charging Hessian. Hess lifts a big boot and drops Chico easily. Azrael climbs into his corner, as does Gunmetal. Nate: We have a wrestling match again folks. Hessian is taking quick care of Chico in the ring. He lifts his mechanical opponent in the air and body slams him. A few nuts and bolts shoot off the robot. Robbie: Gunmetal needs to get in there and put a stop to this. As if on cue, Gunmetal clanks through the ropes and begins to charge Hessian. He is quickly interupted by a spearing Cain. Nate: OOHH, big hit by Cain there, that couldn’t have felt good for either competitor. Cain is first to stand and he glances at Hessian who is stomping Chico. Cain follows suit and begins stomping Gunmetal, sending him closer to the edge of the ring and finally under the bottom rope. Cain slides out and picks up Gunmetal, he whips him into the guard railing. Robbie: Looks like Heresian Bloodlust has this all but wrapped up. Cain reaches under the ring and grabs a table, the crowd goes nuts. He slides it into the ring to Hessian. Hessian stops the beating on Chico and sets up the table. Nate: You can see Hessian’s thirst for this number one contender spot. Steven: Can you see my thirst for Hessian? Robbie: Who told you to talk? Hessian sets up the table and pulls up Chico. The crowd cheers as he pulls him to him. But Chico retaliates with a quick kick to the gut. Hessian is stunned and Chico grabs his head. Pulling him into suplex position. Directly in front of the table. Robbie: No way, never get the big bastard up. Chico slowly begins raising Hessian up for the suplex. Nate: This is it folks! Twisted Metal is gonna do it!!! Robbie: Noooooooo... At the peak of the suplex, Hessian begins kicking his feet. Chico staggers under the weight. Nate: Do it Chico, do it! The weight becomes too much and Chico drops Hessian to his feat. Hessian quickly kicks Chico in the midsection and pulls him up into a Canadian Backbreaker. Robbie: Ohh, that would hurt Chico’s spine. If he had a spine. With a quickness unparalleled, Hessian delivers the Crucifix Piledriver. Robbie: ETERNAL DAMNATION, ETERNAL DAMNATION! Directly through the table. Heresian Bloodlust pulls it off! Winners and NEW number one contenders: Heresian Bloodlust. Earl: Your winners, and new number one contenders for the tag team titles, Heresian Bloodlust!! Robbie: Did you see the impact of that move? Nate: Yes, very very impressive. Steven: Impressive inDEED. Robbie: Damn, you are such a f... Nate: We’ll be back after these messages.
