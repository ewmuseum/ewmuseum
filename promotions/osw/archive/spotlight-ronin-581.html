Spotlight- Ronin

2002-09-09

Posted by Matt Shivan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello there OSW!! I'm Shanda Russell here with another Wrestler Spotlight! With me today is Matt Register and Jeffrey Wayne of Ronin, the Most Extreme Team participants and winners, and newest signees to OSW!! Gentlemen, thank you for your time! Register: I hope this doesn't take long. Shanda: Uh... yes well anyway, lets just kick this right off, shall we!! Most Extreme Team, a grueling, long, and hard fought battle and you two won! Are you excited to be in the OSW? Register: I'm happy to be out of the MET, that's for damn sure Wayne: I think the excitement sort of died after week 1. Since then it was just going through the motions. Register: It would've been exciting if they'd just stopped the theatrics and handed us the contracts from the beginning. Skip that tedious middle portion. Shanda: Uh... wouldn't you say that the competitors, particularly towards the end of the entire event, gave you a good fight? It wasn't exactly a walk in the park. Register: Wrestling isn't exactly a walk in the park, Shanda. But Jesus, if those six didn't- (Wayne stops him) Wayne: If you wanted an interview about the MET, you should have interviewed us then. It's not like we were preoccupied. Now it's no longer beneficial to us to give the rest of those teams this sort of air time. We won the contract, we're in OSW. This is about us. Shanda: Uh...yes...okay. Well then I'll cut straight to that portion of the interview then. Ronin, a hot, young tag team has just entered into the OSW Tag Team scene. Many people have said that the OSW boasts one of the most competitive and talented tag team scenes in the WT, how do you think Ronin will fare amongst greats such as Da Westsidaz and Civil Disobedience? Register: I think we pretty much answered that question at By Any Means Necessary. I mean, maybe we should check the tape, but it seemed like we were standing over both of those teams at one point or another. Hell, if the Throwbacks knew how to keep their noses where they belong, we could've sent Civil D AND Da Westsidaz to the hospital. Wayne: We came out as the best of over 100 teams trying to get in. We went through eight weeks of proving ourselves. We spent two months licking our chops for this shot. To even question our preparedness for what we're getting ourselves into is an insult. Register: We've been watching teams like them since we started our bid to be here. They saw us once, maybe twice. They don't know us half as much as we know them. Shanda: One question on a lot of people's minds is the transition from somewhat rookie tag teams in the MET to established and highly competitive teams in the OSW. Do you think the transition period between the MET to the OSW will be an easy one? Or difficult? Wayne: I think we made it clear during the competition itself that we were of a higher crop of talent. We were born into this echelon of competition, the level that OSW offers us. Register: We don't have time to sit around and be awestruck with the "big league" teams. We've got momentum behind us, and a reputation of relative dominance to uphold. The brass ring is coming around, and it's ours to grab. We don't get anywhere by asking for autographs. Shanda: What makes Ronin any different from these teams? What will make you shine against them and perhaps help you defeat them, if you do? Wayne: If nothing else, simple drive. We are here to succeed, not to make friends. Register: And let's face it, as much as the whole "youth tested by fire" bit appeals to the kids at home, it's old and tired. We've been around the block. We know the ins and outs of this business. All some of these guys - "legends" in their early 20s - all they know is coming in and being successful. They've got a long way to go before they can really say they've played this game. Wayne: And while we're at it, let's bring up the fact that we're fresh. We're new. The brand new thing for the OSW Tag division. Bottom line is that Riptah and Short Circuit are cruising on PCW royalties, and Civil Disobedience shirts haven't been selling since the first title reign. If they don't want to relinquish the top spot on the mountain, we've got the skills to come take it from them. Shanda: Those are some strong, stabbing words gentlemen. While the Tag Titles are surely something that you both yearn to acquire, what else do you wish to do throughout your tenure in the OSW? Anybody in particular you wish to feud with? Singles Titles or wrestlers you've hungered to fight? Register: After the trial period we had to spend before we got here, we'll do and take whatever we have coming to us. And you know what? Sooner or later, you're going to turn around, and the latest PCW crossover has burned out, another free agent failed to live up to his contract, and who've you got left? The two guys who legitimately busted their asses to be here. We weren't recruited. We didn't have the luxury of sitting back and collecting pay like some of the so-called "stars" seemed to make a habit of. I don't care if the fans hate us. I don't care if YOU hate us. Fact is, we work. You can't ignore that. Wayne: We'll be sticking to the tag scene for now, Shanda, but we're both experienced singles competitors. We've got versatility in that area. Anything that's good for business, you know. Shanda: Well thank you Gentlemen for your time and I wish you luck on your future endeavors.
