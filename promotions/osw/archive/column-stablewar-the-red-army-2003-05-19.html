Stablewar: The Red Army

19 May 2003

by Nate Dumas

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own column (#7) by Nate Dumas, dated 19 May 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Stablewar: The Red Army Hey there once again OSW. Nate Dumas here, as you’d expect, with another analysis of a group of characters: The Red Army. The third and late coming faction in this development, The Red Army has certainly left its mark in WO history. Ivan Stanislav Info: Height: 7’1” Weight: 400 lbs. Style: Powerhouse Title History: OSW World Champion 10-27-02 - 4-27-03 Awards: Longest Reigning OSW World Champion(182 Days) OSW 2002 Wrestler of the Year, OSW 2002 Angle of the Year (Love Story with Tempest), OSW 2002 Promo of the Year 'Belated Birthday', 'First Couple of the OSW' with Tempest, 'The Business' Impact Player of the Month for April '02 Entering into the PCW over two years ago, Ivan Stanislav took the PCW by storm and never stopped. Preaching his Communist Propaganda, those of weak heart joined his cause and helped create his swath of destruction. He shocked the OSW by emerging on the first show, and after a long stint of fighting for good, he’s since fallen back into the Supreme Commander of The Red Army and threatens Old School once again. Ivan Stanislav is the ideological center of The Red Army. Style Built out of solid muscle, Ivan Stanislav is perhaps the most powerful wrestler in the WO physically. Headstrong and stubborn, The Russian Bear is also wily, crafty, and strategically minded. In the ring he overwhelms his opponents with his brutish wrestling style, and outside the ring he politically cripples and physically terrorizes them to no end. Strengths Natural Leader: Ivan has been in the Russian Military for almost his entire life. He knows how to control a large force and coordinate attacks. He sees everything, opponents, opportunities, and dangers and can therefore adjust accordingly. Incredibly Powerful: From flipping cars to bursting through walls to pushing speeding forklifts in the opposite direction, there is little that anyone can do to challenge the physical prowess of Ivan Stanislav. He’s able to eradicate smaller opponents, and nullify larger ones. Set a Goal and It Shall be Achieved: Ivan Stanislav has always had goals, and he’s always ultimately achieved them. He has outlasted all of his opponents. Meanstreak, Doug Troy, Hans Kruger, Mexi, Jeremy Howard, and many others have all tried to stop him, but Ivan Stanislav outlasts, outguns, and outmaneuvers them. Weaknesses Tempest: We’ve seen The Russian Bear cry and at one time he did have a heart. What is the extent of Stanislav’s rebuilding of his defenses from his emotions? With Tempest in the Red Army, it may be even worse. Had she left, Ivan would hate her and thus channel his energy. With her staying, that presence is still felt. Power Hungry: Ivan is, as he says, “The Supreme Commander” of The Red Army. Isn’t that contradictory to being equal to everyone else? In the past, Stanislav has first oppressed his enemies, then his allies. Boda’s felt it before. Will it happen again? Boda Info: Height: 6’11” Weight: 350 lbs. Style: Powerhouse/Brawler Title History: None Awards: None Boda has been in six previous federations before finally joining up in the OSW. And prior to that, he was a professional basketball player! He’s done an awful lot and has gone from being a mere mid card wrestler to one of the top men in this sport today. Boda is no stranger to The Red Army, though he has been on both sides as comrade and enemy. He’s back, however, and going full blown Commie, it would appear. He adds his own cutting edge style to the rigid Communist method. Boda is the contemporary of The Red Army. Style Able to stand toe to toe with the largest opponents, Boda can also break it down into a drag out fight if need be. Unafraid of any and all opponents, Chris Sloboda is a sight to behold when the going gets tough. He operates fluidly as a singles wrestler, tag wrestler, or in a battle royal of sorts and though of large size, is swift of foot and quick of thought as well. Strengths Perfect Ally: Boda is stubbornly true to whatever he is fighting for. And pairing that up against Ivan Stanislav makes a wall of unchanging thought. He’s a great member of any group, being both funny and serious at the same time, and is able to keep morale high and things fresh. Can’t Keep a Good Man Down: Boda has been in a multitude of federations, and nothing has stopped him. He continues to fight and compete and seems to thrive off of that competition. Being both on the side of the fans and against them, Boda is like a train: full speed ahead, and if you slow him, he’ll come back stronger next time. Free Spirited Red: Boda abandons the clunky, old style jargon of Ivan Stanislav for a more contemporary and cutting edge style of talk, dress, and style. The fans gravitate towards Boda and if a wrestling Red Army member is to convert fans, it’ll be Boda. Stanislav is too brash and inflexible, but Boda is able to woo fans to the cause. Weaknesses Fallen Party Member: Ivan Stanislav never forgets a thing, and he certainly didn’t forget the double cross from Boda in the PCW. They get along great on camera, but what about behind the scenes? Is there deep felt hatred for Ivan from Boda? Is Ivan going to try to keep Boda down? Second Fiddle: Boda has shone in the past, but will Ivan Stanislav and The Red Army cloud that ray of light and lessen his appearance? There is an obvious ceiling in The Red Army, will Boda ever be able, or allowed, to rise to the heights of greatness? Politically Incorrect: Reginald Lovecraft and William Powell Info: Lovecraft Height: 6’9” Weight: 298 lbs. Style: Powerhouse/Brawler Title History: None Awards: None Powell Height: 6’1” Weight: 231 lbs. Style: Highflying/Technical/Cheating Title History: None Awards: None The team of Politically Incorrect, in the spirit of Ivan’s communism, will be lumped together because quite frankly, they’re a force to be reckoned with together. Powell and Lovecraft have tagged together for a relatively long period of time and have gone through the PCW and FSW to find their way in the OSW now. However, what appeared to be a turn for the better against Communism, quickly turned into a turn for the worse. Politically Incorrect are the resources of The Red Army. Style Alone, Powell and Lovecraft are capable wrestlers. Powell can fight on the mat as well as in the air, and yet he also has mastered the art of cheating. Lovecraft, on the other hand, is a big and strong man that brutalizes and brawls with the best of them. Couple these two with the strengths of Ivan Stanislav and Boda, and you have an unstoppable force. Strengths American Commies: Going against his previous “anti-American” campaign for entry into The Red Army, Stanislav has allowed PI to join, and it is a plus in the end. Powell and Lovecraft can cater to the demands of America better than Stanislav or even Boda, as they know of better way to manipulate American media and American crowds. Natural Tag Team: Powell and Lovecraft work perfectly together as a tag team, perhaps not as well singularly, but the Red Army can back them up. No other stable has a pair of two people that are a true tag team, giving Powell and Lovecraft an incredible edge. True Politicians: The other members of The Red Army are not true politicians, but Powell and Lovecraft are. They speak with forked tongues, but it sometimes sounds remarkably good for what they preach. They’re wily, sneaky, and are able to woo fans if need be. They know how to lie, and yet make it sound oh so true. Weaknesses Low Men: PI are the low men on the Red Army totem pole. They’ve just joined and have a long way to go, and surely Stanislav is keeping an eye on them. They’ll have to prove their willingness to fight for the cause before any true trust is given. American: Ivan hates America, so does he hate PI? Even Boda has Czech background in him, but do Powell or Lovecraft? Recent reports show that they don’t, will that get them oppressed too? Closing Remarks The Red Army is a cohesive group, yes, but can egos get in the way? I don’t know fans, but this wraps up my three-pronged analysis of these three stables. I don’t know who will win, though I hope it is the Asylum. Only time will tell! I’ll see ya at Slam! Nate Dumas
