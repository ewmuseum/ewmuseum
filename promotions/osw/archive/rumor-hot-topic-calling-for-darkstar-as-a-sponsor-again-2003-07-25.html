Hot Topic calling for DarkStar as a sponsor... Again?

25 July 2003

by DarkStar

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own rumor-board post (#26) by DarkStar, dated 25 July 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
As seen on Hot Topic's main website. The Dreamlined Line of clothing is being offered as a promotion once again. For those who don't know 'Dreamline' was the brand of clothing sponsored and designed by DarkStar and Talena a little bit less then a year ago. Dreamlined was discontinued when DarkStar and his wife apparently 'wised up' in their own words, and then decided to send Hot Topic's CEO through a plate glass window. Thus ending the deal. The pairs run as clothing designers was more then successful, they took advantage of DS's impeccable (albeit gothic) fashion sense and marketed towards the cult fans that would cheer for the Gothic Superstar no matter what he did. It made a ton of money for both the corporation and the Dream Lord himself. At the time, however, DarkStar was a more cocky individual, to the point where his spending and excess was categorized almost every week with some escapade at a strip club or a bar. The near infinite amount of money at his fingertips, thanks to a regrettable descision by him and Clyde to align themselves with a former PCW and OSW World Champion who will remain nameless, made alot of those things popular. The minute that was absolved, DarkStar and Talena had snapped back to 'normal'... that is, assuming you call what they are now, normal. When contacted by officials, Hot Topic CEO Jason McTrendy said that 'Hot Topic was merely clearing out leftover merchandise that hadn't sold during the initial running of the brand. As of now, the company has not struck a deal, nor contacted Gavyn Drazail about a renewing of the sponsorship deal... and most likely would not, after the near killing of myself by that crazy bastard.' Before hanging up, he added; 'He can't hear me... can he? I mean he isn't really a god. That's just something you guys do over there... right?' None of us really had a answer, more on details as they come to us.
