U.S. Title Follow-up

27 May 2003

by Shanda Russell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own news post (#57) by Shanda Russell, dated 27 May 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
President Trashcan Man has had the Old School Entertainment lawyers working nonstop over the past 36 hours, looking for legal backing to nullify Politically Incorrect's 'victory' in the Chaos In The Cage Match. Tuesday morning, Shanda Russell reported that work had failed. 'No proof can be found that Politically Incorrect violated the contract of the match,' she said. 'The only rules were that the first pinfall or submission would win the United States Title, and that the match would be fought under anything-goes rules. 'Nowhere in the contract did it specify that the person who made the pin had to actually be entered in the match--OR that multiple people couldn't make a simultaneous pin.' Upon being notified of the decision, Power Play leader Dave Gibson was upset. 'We should've thought of that first!' Gibson complained. Russell added that, as lawyers and politicians, Powell and Lovecraft no doubt were aware of this fact before they interfered in the match. Making matters worse, there are no rules on the OSE books to address what happens when co-champions claim a singles title. Since there was no legality specifying what would occur in such a case in the Chaos In The Cage match, Powell and Lovecraft would be allowed to defend the U.S. Title as a tag team! This news, obviously, thrilled the Red Army. 'It is happy day for Red Army!' leader Ivan Stanislav proclaimed. 'Not only did new comrades Powell and Lovecraft win U.S. Title--capturing the very symbol of American capitalism and greed--but they will defend as true Communists should: collectively! Comrade Stalin would be proud!' Now that Powell and Lovecraft can defend the U.S. Title as a team, it might mean Politically Incorrect's championship reign will last a long, long time...
