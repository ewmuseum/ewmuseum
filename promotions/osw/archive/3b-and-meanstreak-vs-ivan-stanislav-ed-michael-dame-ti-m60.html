3B and Meanstreak vs. Ivan Stanislav, Ed, Michael Dame, Timo Bolamba Written: April 13, 2002 Nate: Ladies and Gentlemen! Welcome back to Saturday SLAM! We are ready for our main event which is a powder keg just waiting to explode, pitting several OSW superstars against one another!! Steven: I like it when all these men are in the ring. Robbie: That is disgusting, Steven. Steven: Yeah, but it’s the truth! Nate: Well! Without further ado, let’s head to the ring with Earl Henrich! Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen! Welcome to the Saturday SLAM Main event!! The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is an eight man tag match!! Introducing first, weighing in at a combined weight of 1,153 lbs: Switchblade, Frankie Bones, Drew Hostyle, and Meanstreak!! “Get to the Gone” by Static-X plays over the loudspeaker as pyro bursts around the area. Switchblade, Bones, Hostyle, and Meanstreak all walk onto the entryway to a chorus of boos. The four head down to the ring, with Switchblade waving cockily to the crowd. Meanstreak pays them no attention and focuses on the ring the entire time, very quiet for once…which may be scarier than when he’s rampaging. Nate: I can barely hear myself talk!!! Robbie: WHAT??? Nate: I said…. Robbie: WHAT??? The four walk down the entryway poised for their enemies. Timo and Ed slap the hands of the fans as they walk by. Earl Henrich bails quickly, leaving referee Betty Lovejoy to chew her gum in the corner. The bell rings. Nate: And this match is officially underway!! Winners: Ivan Stanislav, Timo Bolamba, Michael Dame, Ed!!! Nate: THEY DID IT!!! THEY BEAT REAGAN AND HER LACKIES!!!
