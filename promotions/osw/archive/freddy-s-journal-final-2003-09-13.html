Freddy's Journal: Final?

13 September 2003

by Freddy

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#169) by Freddy, dated 13 September 2003, written from Within the worn pages of a Demon's diary., from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
June 2nd, 1996 I can’t explain what happened. The rope was around my neck, and everything went black, but when I woke up I was on my bed like it never happened. I think it was this mask that saved me, there’s something about it. Something special. It makes me feel like I can do whatever I want, and no one can stop me. It makes me not be afraid anymore, and now no one will ever laugh at me again. I love this feeling. June 6th, 1996 Today was my first day back to school after I tried to kill myself. I thought it would all change, but they still laughed at me. They even said the mask me look stupider than I already was. They stopped laughing when I destroyed a locker with a kid’s head. Everyone’s afraid of me now, even the teachers don’t tell me what to do. I knew it, this mask was meant for me. It makes me feel like I’m invincible, and now no one can laugh at me, or it’ll hurt them just like it did today. June 19th, 1996 This was the last day of school. Something better happened today than what’s already happened. A teacher told me to take off the mask or go to the principal’s office, and I told him no. This is the first time I’ve said that to anyone, and it felt great. There’s nothing they can do about it. There’s nothing anyone can do about it. I am stronger than anyone else in the world all because of this mask, and now the whole world will feel my pain. April 2nd, 1999 My power keeps growing. This mask feeds me, it makes me whole. Nothing can stop me. Nothing. No one. Not even God could stop me now. All I need is a way to make more people feel my pain. Soon the world will know who I am, and no one will dare to laugh at me. Soon…everyone will know what it’s like to feel the pain I felt. Soon…
