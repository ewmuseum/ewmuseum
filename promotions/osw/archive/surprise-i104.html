Surprise!

2002-03-30
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Surprise! Written: 30/03/2002 The scene returns to the ringside position of the OSW announce team. Steven: (snapping his fingers and waggling his head) You tell him Jesse!! WOO HOO!!! Nate: (sighing) Welcome back to Slam, folks. Steven: (continuing, bursting into song) I wish that I could be Jesse’s girl!!!! Jess… The suddenly sound of fist to skull is heard in all its crunching glory, silencing Steven immediately, save the thump of his head hitting the table a moment later. Robbie: Well, that was… Nate: (enraged) YOU WANT SOME TOO THERE DECKER?!?!! Robbie: (quickly) I’ll be good! Nate: (still furious) Now, during our commercial, our cameras caught what can only be described as spectacle backstage. Robbie: HA! Oh please show it again! HA! That was priceless! HA! The picture on the jumbotron changes to the backstage area where there is a caption on the right hand corner of the screen that reads “During the Break” In the dark hallway, OSW tag team champions, Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke can be seen walking down the hall carrying their duffle bags over their shoulders and the tag belts around their wastes as they are going to their dressing rooms. The crowd pops at the site of the popular kings of surburbia. Masters stops Hawke as they pass a Coke machine. Masters: Hold on man! I need to get a drink. Hawke rolls his eyes at his indulgent friend. Masters puts his dollar in and hits a button. There is no response from the machine though. Masters starts to tap away at the button. Masters: Stupid piece of crap! Give me my damn Diet Coke!!! Masters continues to fiddle with the machine. Suddenly out of the pop machine a massive fist busts through the plastic front and grabs Masters by the throat. Hawke drops his bag in shock. Masters starts to pound away at the huge fist that is wearing black fingerless glove. Hawke: Christ!!! Hawke runs to aid Masters, but another fists bust form within the Coke machine and grabs Hawke by the throat as well!!! The sound of Robbie snickering at the announce desk can be clearly heard in the background. Nate: Shhhhhh! Suddenly, Azrael Cain explodes from within the vending machine and slams Masters and Hawke into the concrete wall with both of his fists closed tightly around their throats. Cain: (maniacally) Hello boys! How are you feeling right now!!?!? A massive silver gauntlet wearing fist appears and grabs Masters by the throat. The fist belongs to the other half of Heresian Bloodlust, Hessian. The mask wearing goliath pulls Masters close his face. Hessian: We just wanted to see if you guys had forgotten about us or not. So we decided to pay you a little visit. Hessian headbutts Masters in the face which causes Masters head to start bleeding profusely. Cain lands a haymaker to Hawke’s jaw. Hessian: You boys have been talking quite a good game since you won those belts at the Pay Per View, haven’t you? That’s pretty brave considering that you won those belts as a result of faulty craftsmanship. This week, there will be no cages and no weapons for you two use for your advantage. This week… Hessian taps the gold belt around Masters’ waste. Hessian: …those will be ours about a half an hour from now!!! Hessian headbutts Masters again. Cain: It seems only fitting that the same weekend you celebrate the sacrifice the son of the Creator made for all of you pathetic creatures will be the same weekend that the dark reign of Heresian Bloodlust begins! You boys better have repented for your sins this week like all good little Christians do because this is your last chance. There will be no mercy shown this week. We are going to finally put you two out of the game! Welcome to Hell boys! Cain and Hessian both deliver headbutts to Masters and Hawke and release their grips on their opponents’ throats. Masters and Hawke drop to the floor with a loud thud. Hessian and Azrael Cain turn and begin walking away down the dark corridor. Their deep laughs filled with hatred can be heard as they disappear into the darkness. The camera settles in on the stunned bodies of Masters and Hawke as it fades to black, but the dark laughter can still be heard as the scene fades. The camera shot returns to the announcers’ table where Steven and Nate are sharing the same shocked look on their faces as before and Robbie is still laughing insanely with tears in his eyes. Robbie: HA! HA! OH God, that is priceless!! Robbie puts his head down and starts to pound away at the table still laughing. Nate: Folks, we have some bills to pay so we will be back after these messages.
