Civil Disobedience vs. Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman/Mystery Partner

2002-07-13
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil Disobedience vs. Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman/Mystery Partner Written: 7-13-02 Nate: That’s just not funny. Well folks, we’ll keep you up to date on Jesus St Crux as we hear more about him. Now we gotta get on with the show. Our next contest should prove to be an interesting one, pitting Elite members Civil Disobedience against one third of the Vax, Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman, and a mystery partner. The back story of this match up goes back two weeks, where, at a house show, Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke took the opportunity to lambast the Think Tank at a house show. This display incited PHD himself to come out in response, challenging them to this match up we are about to see and declaring that he had a big surprise in store for the two suburbanites. Robbie: Nate Dumas, the master of the recap everyone is already aware of. Nate: Blow me, Decker. Steven Smith leans over towards Nate’s lap, before looking up at him innocently. Steven: Can I? Dumas shoves the flaming homosexual away with a look of absolute disgust on his face. Nate: Ugh! Get on with the introductions already, Earl. We switch to a shot of the ring, where ring announcer Earl Henrich is already standing. Earl: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Aurora, Illinois, weighing in at a combined weight of 460 pounds, they are members of the Elite, they are Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke...CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE!!! The opening chords of “What You Say” by Skrape starts to echo throughout the arena. The arena lights dim and white strobe lights begin flashing on the ramp, as the Elite’s entrance video flashes on the big screen. Then, as song shifts to a heavier rift, Seth and Trevor step out from the back, causing the crowd to go wild. The two men are wearing confident expressions as they strut to the ring. Nate: How about these ovations for Civil Disobedience. They’re almost cheering them as loudly as they did before Seth and Trevor joined the Elite and turned their back on them. Robbie: These stupid ass people. One week they love Civil Disobedience, then the next they hate them, and then the next, they love them again. What fickle bastards! Nate: I guess when you go against the Think Tank, the fans are willing to forgive anything. In the ring, Seth runs the rope, limbering himself up for the contest, while Trevor asks Earl Henrich for the microphone. The announcer shrugs, before handing it over to Hawke, who immediately turns his attention to face the locker room’s direction. Trevor: Alright, listen up, you nerdy fuck! Steven: Oh my, such language! Robbie: Shut up, pussy. Trevor: You’ve gone on for about two weeks harping about your huge surprise for us. To tell you the truth, we don’t give a damn who you’ve connived into teaming with you, but frankly, it doesn’t matter. Me and Seth, we’re tag team specialists, and you...well you could bring out the entire crew of the Starship Enterprise and it wouldn’t make a difference. So just bring your buck-toothed ass down to the ring and get ready to have your ass handed to you, dicksmack! Nate: Some choice words by Trevor Hawke, and the fans are eating it up! The crowd lets out another round of cheers as Hawke hands the microphone back to Henrich. He and Trevor lean on the ropes, looking expectantly toward the back as they await the arrival of their opponents. Earl: And their opponents. Introducing first, from Princeton, New Jersey, weighing in at 147 pounds, he is a member of the Think Tank, and the handler of a level 20 Elvish ranger. He is PROFESSOR HERBERT D. DORKIMAN!!! The theme song to the old Doctor Who TV show hits the PA, as PHD steps out from the back to meet loud, loud, LOUD booing from the crowd. Despite the hail of garbage flying in his direction, his lips are turned up into a grin and his eyes are shining beneath his half-inch thick glasses. Robbie: I know that look anywhere! It’s the look of a man who has something up his sleeve. If I were Civil Disobedience, I’d begin fearing for my lives. Coming down to the ring, he makes sure to walk around to the opposite side of the ring from where Seth and Trevor are waiting. Rolling under the bottom ropes, he walks over to Henrich, making sure the announcer stays in between himself and his opponents. Grabbing the microphone away from the man, PHD whirls around to face the crowd. PHD: Greetings, my intellectual inferiors! The Cleveland fans cut Dorkiman off with a loud “NEEEEEEERD” chant. PHD: *Sigh* The plight of the common mongoloids. No matter, though. Dorkiman turns to face Seth and Trevor, who both look ready to plant a foot up his ass. PHD: Civil Disobedience, I think it’s about time for you to roll a d20 for your surprise check. Robbie: What the hell? Nate: It’s Dungeons and Dragons lingo. Robbie: Ah. Dorkiman reaches into the pocket of his lab coat and pulls out a twenty sided die. Shaking it in his hand for a second, he drops it to the mat. As it bounces and settles, the Professor looks down to read it, and a smile comes onto his face. PHD: Oh man! *Snort* A one! That’s a critical failure! Well, in any case, I doubt it would have mattered what the die said. Get ready to pick your jaws off the floor and, more importantly, get ready to meet your doom! Mystery partner...come on out! “Down Fall” by Trust Company plays throughout the arena, as a large form steps out onto the top of the ramp. He is simply massive, standing a good seven foot ,three inches tall. The smark section of the crowd, meaning those that pay attention to wrestling outside of the OSW, let out a collective startled gasp of realization as they recognize the giant of a man standing before them. Nate: Oh my God... Robbie: Is that who I think it is? Nate: I don’t believe it. That’s John Mammoth! John Mammoth is in Old School Wrestling! In the ring, PHD is leaping up and down, clapping his hands and laughing the geekiness laugh imaginable. Both Seth and Trevor are staring at the humongous, 7’3, 500 lb. man who made his mark in Survivor now making his way to the ring in shock, the confident expressions fading from their faces quickly. Robbie: Civil Disobedience can’t believe it! I don’t think I believe it myself! Steven: He’s huge! Just think about the size of that man’s genitalia! Nate: I am in shock. Mammoth is in OSW...which is a surprising enough fact in itself. What’s even more shocking, though, is that he’s teaming with Dorkiman! This doesn’t make any sense at all. Steven: Why’s that? Nate: It’s well known that these two men were at each other’s throats throughout the duration of Survivor II. I can’t think of any possible explanation that would lend any sense to this pairing. All we can hope for is that perhaps more will come to light shortly. Robbie: I...I don’t know what to say! All I know is that this is big, and I feel really, really bad for Seth and Trevor in the near future. And considering I’m the least sympathetic man in the world, you know that’s bad. Stepping over the top rope, Mammoth stands staring at Seth and Trevor with his arms crossed. He towers over everyone else in the ring, looking completely disinterested in the entire situation. Civil Disobedience both stare in disbelief at the giant, before turning to glare at PHD who is still chortling to himself in glee. Nodding to each other, Masters and Hawke make an immediate beeline for the Professor, each delivering a boot to his midsection that crumples him to his knees. Both men grab him, lifting him up in a sort of double press, before carrying and flinging him with a rocket launcher into the corner. Dorkiman lands face first on the turnbuckle, knocking his glasses off before falling to the man where he holds his face in agony. Nate: A smart move by Seth and Trevor. By attacking Dorkiman right away, they save themselves the unenviable task of having to tangle with that monster of a man. Robbie: I’m sure Mammoth will stop this double-team and even the scales soon enough, though. Surprisingly, the Behemoth just stares at Civil Disobedience stomping away on his partner in indifference, before finally stepping over the rope and taking his spot in the corner. Nate: Mammoth has left PHD to the proverbial wolves. Robbie: Well yeah, if waiting for a legal tag to enter into the match counts as leaving him to the wolves Steven: I think it does. Nate: There looks to be more than meets the eye in this situation. In any case, Seth and Trevor are going to town on PHD. The two Elite members are laying into the Professor with a series of hard boots to the midsection. Finally, referee Speedy Riggs orders one of them to the outside, forcing Trevor to leave Seth in the ring alone with PHD. Robbie: It’s about damn time Riggs restored some order to this thing. Nate: Do you think it’s really going to be much better for Dorkiman? Robbie: Perhaps not, but it won’t be AS bad. Dorkiman tries to crawl to his corner for the tag, but Seth will have none of it. Masters drags PHD back to the center of the ring by his foot. Dorkiman gets to his feet in an attempt to scamper away, but Seth pulls him back and catches him with a Russian leg sweep. Keeping PHD’s arm hooked, Masters rolls over and lifts Dorkiman to his feet, only to take him back down with another Russian leg sweep. Still holding on, Masters pulls him up once more, then hits a third Russian leg sweep before finally releasing his opponent. Nate: That was a nice Russian leg sweep series from Seth Masters, especially considering it prevented PHD from getting the tag. Robbie: Civil D wants no piece of John Mammoth, that’s for sure. Steven: I, on the other hand... Robbie: Yuck. Just yuck. Lifting Dorkiman back up, Masters grabs him in a front chancery, twisting it slightly before driving the Professor’s neck down on his shoulder in a half-turn neckbreaker. Seth follows up with a rolling neck snap, causing PHD to go into spasms on the canvas, holding the back of his neck in pain. Nate: That’s some decent neck work by Seth Masters. Picking PHD up again, Seth whips him hard into the corner, following in with a clothesline...that Dorkiman avoids by ducking out of the way. As Masters collides with the turnbuckle, he staggers backward. Seizing the opportunity, PHD crawls over to his corner, reaching out his hand toward Mammoth for the tag. As he gets within range, the Professor looks back to see Seth has him by the foot again. Dorkiman turns back to face Mammoth, waving his hand frantically for the tag. Steven: Hee hee! Look at PHD squirm in there! Robbie: Seth can make him squirm all he likes, but in mere moments John Mammoth is going to get into this match-up! All he has to do is reach out his hand and then we can start booking Civil Disobedience’s funeral! Mammoth, however, doesn’t even bother extending his hand the ten inches needed to make the tag. Instead, PHD is dragged back into the center of the ring, where Masters plants him with a sitdown uranage before making the tag to Hawke. Robbie: WHAT?! Nate: John Mammoth did not even attempt to tag into this match! Robbie: No way, he just screwed PHD over! Hawke remains standing on the outside, while in the ring Seth grabs a waistlock on the Professor. Lifting him off the canvas, Masters delivers a german suplex, bridging long enough for Trevor to springboard over the top rope and connect with a legdrop to Dorkiman’s face. Nate: What a double team maneuver from Civil Disobedience! Robbie: It’s a double team maneuver that shouldn’t have happened to begin with! Mammoth should be in the ring with them right now...not PHD! Seth leaves the ring, allowing Trevor to have his turn in the ring with PHD. Lifting Dorkiman up into vertical suplex position, Hawke carries him over to the ropes, where he drops him midsection first, still hanging onto the maneuver. As the ropes spring the Professor back, Trevor shifts his arms, using PHD’s own momentum to spike him into the mat with a slingshot DDT. Nate: Seth and Trevor are more or less owning PHD in the ring right now. I believe his total amount of offense in this match has been...well, it’s been nonexistent. Steven: He’s looking possibly more pathetic in there than me. Robbie: Now I wouldn’t go that far. Dorkiman looks to be in a world of pain, which only intensifies as Hawke pulls him back onto his feet with an arm wrench. Craning back on the hold, Trevor climbs the turnbuckles, where he proceeds to walk across the top rope until reaching about the center of the ring. Still holding on to the arm wrench, Hawke leaps off, catching his leg on the back of PHD’s head and driving his face into the mat with a modified rocker dropper. Nate: Hawke just nailed the Social Decline! Dragging Dorkiman back to his corner, Trevor tags Seth back in. Entering the ring, Masters drags his thumb across his throat, signaling for the end and bringing the crowd to their feet. Near the corner, Seth sets up PHD for a suplex, while Hawke climbs the turnbuckles. Nate: They’re setting up something here. Robbie: No shit, Sherlock. Hooking the leg of the Think Tank member, Seth hoists him up, cradling him in the air, before smashing him down with a fisherman buster. On the top rope, Trevor leaps off, twisting and turning in mid-air as he nails a corkscrew legdrop. Nate: The Primo Plex and Da Supafly! Steven: Wow, Civil Disobedience are pretty much just going through their moveset on PHD. Robbie: Let’s just be thankful they’re not Hessian, or we’d be here all day. Civil Disobedience lifts Dorkiman up once more, at this point looking to be toying with the man. Lifting him up by the collar of his lab coat, Seth sends him sprawling back down with a hard slap to the face. A boot to the stomach from Trevor sends him rolling across the ring. He keeps rolling, however, all the way into his corner, where he slaps a hand across the knee of Mammoth. The big man looks down at him, seeming to finally pay attention to what’s going on in the ring for the first time this match. Nate: That’s an official tag! Mammoth is in the match! They shouldn’t have been treated the Professor so nonchalantly because now they have to deal with John Mammoth! Robbie: And that’s not a good thing for Civil D. As the beaten, battered, and more or less broken PHD rolls to the outside to lay on the ring apron, Mammoth steps over the top rope to enter the ring. Seth and Trevor are stand in the opposite corner, eyeing the giant apprehensively, but the big man himself just stands there, staring back. He makes no motion to go after the two Elite members. Robbie: What’s going on? He’s just standing there! Steven: Maybe he’s admiring Civil Disobedience like I am right now. They are a couple of cuties after all. Nate: I strongly doubt that. But something is awry here. Mammoth doesn’t seem into this match up at all. PHD has now managed to pull himself to his feet, though he is still wincing as he holds his ribcage. He screams at Mammoth, yelling at him to tear Seth and Trevor to pieces. Mammoth turns to stare at professional wrestling’s most well known nerd, blinking a few times, then turns to charge at Seth and Trevor. The two sububan-ites scatter from the oncoming attack, dashing in opposite directions. As Mammoth puts the breaks on before he hits the corner, he turns to look at his two opponents, who are now standing in opposite corners of the ring, watching him carefully. Nate: Seth and Trevor don’t look to eager to tangle with the big man. Robbie: Seriously, Dumas. Can you blame them? Nate: I can’t say I can. Walking out of the corner, Mammoth stands in the middle of the ring, motioning for Civil Disobedience to come and fight him. The two men refuse to oblige, however, opting instead to begin circling the giant warily. After about ten seconds of this, Mammoth moves to go after Trevor, allowing Seth to dash up from behind and deliver a dropkick to the back...that has absolutely no effect. Nate: John Mammoth did not even feel that dropkick! Steven: Uh-oh, I guess that means Trevor’s in trouble. Mammoth continues moving toward Trevor, as Seth gets off the mat and delivers another dropkick to the back, with similar results. A terrified look appears on Trevor’s face as the big man draws near to him, and he rolls out of the ring to safety before he is forced to tangle with the Behemoth. Steven: Hawke bails to the outside. Robbie: Coward... Nate: If you were in the ring you would do the same thing, Decker. Robbie: But I’m not, am I? Nate: Weren’t you just defending Civil Disobedience a few moments ago? Robbie: Eh, my mood sways with the wind. Mammoth turns to face Seth now, who backs off as he sees the giant’s attention has been turned to him. As John lunges at him, Masters ducks, sliding between the big man’s legs and running to the ropes. Trevor is now back into the ring, running past his partner and around Mammoth to bounce off the ropes on the opposite side of the ring. As the two men approach their opponent from the front and back, Seth attempts a high spinning heel kick, while Trevor hits a rolling leg sweep that connects with the back of Mammoth’s knee. Unfortunately it doesn’t work, as the giant seems not to notice the blow to his leg, concentrating on catching Masters in mid-air. Nate: Mammoth just pulled Masters out of mid-flight! The gargantuan wrestler carries Seth effortlessly to the ropes, despite the futile attempts of Hawke to free his partner by pounding on the giant’s back. As Mammoth reaches the ropes, he hoists Masters high in the air, then proceeds to chuck him out into the audience. The crowd scatters as Seth lands somewhere in the middle of the third row of chairs. Robbie: Holy shit! Steven: BOCA~! Nate: Mammoth has just tossed Seth Masters some twenty feet into the crowd! As John Mammoth turns around, Trevor is waiting for him, delivering a stiff roundhouse kick to the midsection. Besides a small oomph, it has no visible effect. Trevor delivers a second and third kick, bringing more grunts from the big man and little else. On the forth kick it appears he has gone to the well one too many times, however, as Mammoth grabs Hawke’s foot with one hand. Robbie: Do you see that? He’s cupping Trevor’s foot inside of his hand! Inside of his hand! Nate: The longer we watch John Mammoth in the ring, the more amazed we become. The size and strength of this man is just simply unbelievable! Mammoth lifts Hawke’s foot in the air, higher and higher. It’s a testament to Trevor’s flexibility that he’s able to maintain his balance, even as the giant lifts his foot above Hawke’s head level. Deciding not to toy with the smaller man, Mammoth lowers the foot, then with one motion flings it up as hard as he can, sending Trevor flying into the air, flipping backwards. Steven: Johnny just flipped Trevor three hundred and sixty degrees, one handed. Trevor, amazingly, lands on his feet after a full flip, only to walk right into a huge chokeslam. The impact of the maneuver bounces Hawke off of the mat, rolling him backward to end up lieing on his stomach. The crowd let’s out a chant of “Holy shit” as Mammoth looks out at the Cleveland audience with no expression coming into his cold eyes. Nate: ...That was possibly the hardest chokeslam I have ever seen. Robbie: Stop the match! Trevor Hawke has been broken in half! Steven: I reiterate. BOCA~! On the outside, PHD is screaming at Mammoth to finish the match. Looking at Dorkiman, the big man turns his attention back to Hawke. Moving Trevor onto his back with an effortless kick, Mammoth plants a boot on him for the pin. Referee Speedy Riggs dives in for the count. Nate: It’s elementary at this point. There’s no way any man could get up from a impactful maneuver like that. 1... 2... Steven: Look on the top rope! Seth Masters! Robbie: He’s back from his flight so soon? No! Mammoth is knocked backward, as a recovered Seth Masters delivers a jumping side kick from the top rope that staggers the big man all of one step backward. It’s enough to break the pinfall, however, but the comeback effort might as well have been wasted, as the Behemoth storms at Seth and sends him crumpling into the corner with a big boot that nearly takes his head off. Robbie: Well there goes whatever hope Seth had of gaining control of this contest. Nate: Indeed, it seems as if he is just delaying the inevitable at this point. Turning back to Trevor, Mammoth cups his hand around the smaller man’s head and lifts him to his feet. Lifting him up he hoists him into the air in a vertical suplex position, parading around the ring with him before dropping him into a Orange Crush sitdown powerbomb for the pin. Robbie: This has to be it. 1... 2... No! Seth Masters breaks the pinfall again, this time by delivering both feet to Mammoth’s face with a falling dropkick. Pushing Trevor forward, the big man gets to his feet to begin stalking toward Masters. Seth backs away, into the corner, where he sits on the turnbuckle to gain equal height with the approaching man. Seth holds up a fist, as if that would persuade the giant to back away. Suddenly, Mammoth keels over...from a desperation diving lowblow from behind by Trevor Hawke. Nate: Low blow! For the first time in this match, it seems as if John Mammoth is showing a sign of pain! Steven: A nut shot will do that. Trust me, I know all about nut shots! Nate: Now can Civil Disobedience capitalize? This gives Seth the opportunity he needs. Leaping from the top rope, he flies over his Mammoth, flipping down and grabbing his legs in an attempted sunset flip. He pulls, and pulls, and pulls, trying to bring the big man down. He goes down alright, but not in the manner Masters would like him to. Mammoth sits down hard, crushing Masters chest. He motions to Riggs to count the pinfall. Nate/Robbie/Steven: Ooooo, that’s got to hurt! 1... 2... Trevor Hawke tries to push Mammoth off of his partner with what remaining strength he has left, but he doesn’t move the five hundred pounder an inch. 3! Winners: Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman and John Mammoth Nate: It has taken John Mammoth all of five minutes to dispose of the former OSW tag team champions, single-handedly. Simply unbelievable. Robbie: I’m not sure I believe my eyes, myself. Trevor holds his head, his expression a mixture of disappointment and disbelief as Mammoth stands up off of his partner. The Behemoth turns to face PHD, who has now climbed back in the ring clapping his hands excitedly. As the Doctor Who theme song plays over the PA once more, Dorkiman begins to do a dance...a nerdy dance...possibly the nerdiest dance known to man. Steven: Oh no... Nate: Is he doing....? Robbie: The Robot!? Nate: Christ, Civil D should have disposed of him when they had the opportunity! At least it would have spared us from watching this. Mammoth looks at his partner, frowns, then steps over the top rope and heads back to the locker room. Dorkiman is too busy celebrating to even notice, seeming more concerned with swinging his arms and rotating his torso to the chagrin of the fans to even glance over at his partner. Much too busy to notice Civil Disobedience getting back to their feet and approaching him from behind. Nate: Well it seems like they may redeem themselves very shortly, as Mammoth has left Dorkiman alone in the ring once again. Driving a foot into the back of PHD’s knee, Trevor forces Dorkiman to kneel down. Masters runs the ropes and comes back to deliver a hard snap kick to the face, causing the crowd to cheer wildly. Nate: What a kick to the face by Seth Masters! Hoisting the Professor off of the ground, both members of Civil Disobedience whip him into the ropes. The people let out another cheer in anticipation of what’s to come. Their expectations come true, as Seth and Trevor hit their Trendsetter finisher, tossing Dorkiman into the air with a double flapjack, then catching him on the way down in a double flatliner. Robbie: Mammoth doesn’t even know what’s going on in the ring! Steven: And judging from his behavior in the match, I don’t think he really gives a hoot. Mammoth doesn’t even look back once at the act of vengeance taking place in the ring, disappearing behind the curtain having not made a single display of emotion the entire match. In the ring, Seth and Trevor look down at the crumpled frame of PHD in disgust, then leave the ring as well, to an even bigger ovation than the one they arrived to. Robbie: Well after a segment such as that, I don’t really know what to say. Nate: I think we’re all pretty speechless right about now!!! John Mammoth was PHD’s mystery partner. I still cannot believe it!!! I think a commercial break will give us all the opportunity to sort things out!!! The cameras focus over the shoulder of the emotionless John Mammoth, back to where his “partner” PHD lies demolished in ring….as the scene fades to commercial.
