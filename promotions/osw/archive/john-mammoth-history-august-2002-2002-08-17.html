John Mammoth — history (August 2002)

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Gigantism (ji gan'tiz *m) is a condition of abnormal growth that occurs before puberty and results in excessive size and stature. An overproduction of growth hormone is the cause. He has had this desease since birth, and is subject to early death. Jonathan Warren was born with an extremely rare yet currently uprising disease known as 'gigantism', a disease in which your bones NEVER stop growing until you die. The same disease that the late Andre the Giant had all of his life. As a child, the disease never effected him much. He was always picked first to play kickball and other games at recess, and his kind-hearted manner never stopped him from gaining friends. However, their was a darkside, as he met with physicians every-other day. Though, none knew what could help the pour soul become a normal child. But one was differen't than the rest, Dr. Woodrow. He dedicated his entire career to young Jonathan, and placed him on many programs. He also put Jonathan on a special vitamen and iron diet, that would help his bones remain strong. The two still meet to this day, and are the best of friends. The fWo want him to percieve the sterotype giant wrestler: powerful, big, and unstoppable. John though, he doesn't want to be the 'unstoppable' force, he wants to be Jonathan Warren. He wants to be himself. Meanwhile, the curse of this disease haunts him everyday, as he wakes up not knowing if he will be granted another breath to breathe. Physicians say it's only a matter of time until this 38 year old giant dies. Which is exactly why he has tried out for fWo Survivor II. From day one of his life, he could tell you what he wanted to be...a wrestler. His hero, Andre the Giant, had the exact same disease as him. Jonathan was inspired by the man's love for life, his inspiring heart and giant soul that made him the legend that he was. Jonathan wanted to have as big as a heart as Andre did. Times change. As does sports entertainment. the fWo were looking for the cream of the crop, a behemoth of a man that wouldn't take shit from no one... Jonathan Warren, became John Mammoth. However, as a surprise to many, John was quickly eliminated from competition. Mammoth progressed, persevered, and worked even harder to pursue his career. Jonathan travelled the indies for a month or two, until jOlt Wrestling quickly took the opportunity to sign the big man. That's when Mammoth's troubles first began. Paxton Phoenix, another newcomer to jOlt, was jealous of Mammoth's fifteen minutes of fame, and decided that if he beat John, he too would be considered a talented edition to the roster. His first tactic was to try and have John lay down by the power of the almighty dollar... But in the words of Paxton... 'The bastard just won't stay the hell down!' The two had a heated rivalry from that day forward, and after many chairs and tables, jOlt closed briefly. jOlt Wrestling made the decision not to continue with John Mammoth due to a decision made by both Mammoth and jOlt Staff. The fans loved him shortly after he ventured into aWc, taking his down to earth attitude with his no-nonsense talk. However, after grappling on many o' more occasions with wrestlers that time and time again couldn't put on a show, he chose to leave as soon as possible, especially after Kevin Masters passed away. Now, his more than loving and adoring fans are stuck in a pickle. The same fire that was once behind Mammoth's eyes no longer sit. Just cold... Professor Dorkiman's relationship with Mammoth left currently unknown, it is left for the fans to wonder 'Why?'. The only thing we do know, however, is Mammoth isn't the same Mammoth we used to know. After turning his back on the fans, the anguish in his eyes showed it all... He was cold... And he hated it...
