Declaration

2002-08-05
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Declaration Written: 08/05/2002 The scene opens to the popular duo of Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters panting for breath, obviously still trying to hide from Da Westsidaz. They turn the corner and look to each other. Seth: I don’t think Riptah took the sardine comment to well. I guess I should’ve noticed that they had baseball bats and all… Trevor looks down the hall and doesn’t see Da Westsidaz coming. An ethnic voice rips down the hallway though, telling the two that they’re coming. Trevor looks at a Women’s Bathroom and gets an idea. Trevor: Seth, we gotta hide in there. Da Westsidaz will never think to look in there! Seth: But that’s the… Trevor: Do you wanna be embarrassed or let Da Westsidaz’ go Al Capone on our asses? I say we hide in the chick room till the show goes off the air. That way they’ll never find us! Seth: Fine, let’s go already. The longer we wait to decide, the longer it is before Da Westsidaz find us. Trevor: No Seth, the SHORTER it is till Da Westsidaz find us. Seth: Whatever. Civil Disobedience quickly disappear into the Girl’s Washroom. They look around inside and the beautifully cleaned, perfect room. It has it’s own radio, television and everything. It seems completely perfect, like the Washroom From Heaven or something. Trevor: Good lord! The Men’s Bathroom had a dying animal on the floor and this place is perfect?! Talk about sexism. I’m going to have to file a complaint. Seth: I know, really! Look at this place! It even has this neat little envelope dispenser! Seth plays back and forth, taking out multitudes of little, pink envelopes. Trevor: Uh, Seth, I don’t think that’s a… All of a sudden a loud sneeze breaks out in the washroom. Hawke and Masters get on the defensive and look around, but no one is anywhere to be found. The two scan the area and notice two glowing eyes in the vent. Seth kicks the vent real hard and out rolling comes Minion the Imp, holding a tube of KY Jelly in his hands. Seth: AH! Trev, it’s a gremlin!! Trevor: No you idiot, it’s that little puke Minion! What the hell are you doing here?! The little creature snickers evilly at the two faces. Minion: Same thing you’re here for, the POON! And I woulda seen it too if you two clowns didn’t bust in here. Trevor: Poon? No, were not here for that. We’re hiding in here! We were just… A voice is heard and the bathroom door starts to open slowly, so all three of the idiots dive for hiding places. Minion jumps head-first into the toilet and Trevor hides in a stall, leaving Seth to fend for himself. He quickly hides behind the garbage can, but is in clear view as AZRAEL CAIN WALKS IN?? Azrael: Minion? Are you in here? Hessian is going to kill… Both Men and Imp appear from their hiding places and look puzzled at Azrael. Azrael stands in the middle of the room with a sledgehammer, as confused as they are. Civil Disobedience & Minion: You want poon too? Azrael: No you idiots, I’m here to find Minion. It’s obvious where he would go and…WAIT A SECOND! WHAT THE HELL ARE CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE DOING HERE?! Azrael swings his hammer at Trevor, who ducks, so the hammer crashes into a huge center mirror sending glass everywhere. Trevor and Seth boot it out of the washroom and sprint down the hallway and slams into Hessian at full speed. The look up at the masked monster who is holding a large chainsaw wrapped in barbwire. Seth: AH! WHAT THE HELL IS THAT??! Trevor: Let’s not stick around and find out!! The two of them get to their feet and get ready to run only to see Da Westsidaz holding their baseball bats at the end of the hallway. Riptah: You two whiteboys are dead! You hear me?! DEAD! Trevor and Seth look at each other with sad faces as Da Westsidaz charge. In the confusion, Riptah starts to unload on Hessian with the baseball bat. Seth receives a Sledgehammer into the ribs, dropping him like a brick. Trevor starts to give Short Circuit a barrage of rights, staggering him, until a flood of security officials pour into the hallway, dragging the respective parties away from each other. Each member of Civil Disobedience fold up a chair for protection and start yelling across the room at the OSW Tag Champions, while Da Westsidaz try and fight through all the uniforms. Moose is barking orders at the three teams, trying to keep them separated until the Commissioner shows up. Howard Porter: Hey! Knock it off you guys! I book your match and this is how I am compensated? Fine. I’ll give you guys something to chew on for awhile. I’m banning every one of you from the arena tonight, so pack your bags. As for your match, here’s a stipulation for you! “Weapon of Choice” match will suffice I assume? Considering you guys like to disrupt everything using your idiotic antics with weapons and almost destroying my main event a few weeks ago, I think this will be perfect. But, if any of you guys try and pull something stupid like this again, I’ll suspend you all indefinitely! That means no match, no title shot. Da Westsidaz and Civil Disobedience yell in disagreement at Porter, but security holds them back. Hessian and Cain have a smug look on their face, their minds already spinning with plans. Howard Porter: I don’t know what you guys think is funny either because if you mess up, I’ll vacate those titles! I work too hard around here for six people who think they’re hotshots to run around unchecked. You three teams better act accordingly or you’ll be work elsewhere!!! All three teams shake their head in anger, but don’t act out. They all take their leave one by one, as the scene goes to commercial.
