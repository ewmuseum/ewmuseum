Propaganda — history (October 2002)

19 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Standing at a massive 7'0' tall and weighing in at 373lbs. 'The Power himself' Propaganda is not all that meets the eye. He is in fact a die-hard Christian. But isn't it supposed to be that they're against violence. Well this is how Propaganda at the hands of Eric Sagar. As a small boy growing up in Clarksville Propaganda was surrounded in Sin. His mother was a prostitute and his father was a heavy drinker and a gambler. He was the youngest of three brothers; both of his brothers were drug addicts. He himself, smoked, drank, and was taught to be racist. At the age of eleven Propaganda finally found out his father was hitting Propaganda's mother. This went to drastic measures. His mother would be lying on the kitchen floor with a fork stuck in her forehead, or unconscious in the basement under a pile of blood stained clothes. Until she was finally drove out, with all of his anger bottled up he had to unleash it upon some others. So he did, Propaganda's two brothers. Propaganda was the youngest and was the most favoured of the siblings, so he was not beaten. But he knew one day his brothers would run like his mother once did. So Propaganda buffed up, fighting in the streets. As he predicted his brothers left home, so Propaganda was faced alone with his father. One Night his father came home in an angered, drunken straight wanting to hurt someone or something. And all was left was Propaganda, he took of his belt and swung back preparing to his Propaganda who was cowering in the corner. Propaganda notices with his arm back he exposed the most vital organ the human body has to offer the heart. He swung back and hit him hard in the heart. His father fell to the floor. Propaganda was worried 'Had he killed him? Or had he caused him a heart attack? Is he just pretending so that he can surprise him with a bigger beating?' Propaganda panicked, and gathered a few things and ran. He spent the next few years of his life in bar brawls, doing drugs and pretty much hanging around in the wrong crowd. Tennessee Hardcore Wrestling was a budding promotion, they found Propaganda and signed him up. Propaganda was an instant hit, he saw the tapes of the grates like 'Big' John Stud and André 'The Giant' learning how to pull off standard moves with devastation. He worked his way to Television title division. When he was in a local bar on the outskirts of Memphis, when him and water got in an argument. It ended with Propaganda assaulting him with his own metal drink tray. The water has severe injuries, and Propaganda was found guilty of grievous bodily harm and sentenced to 18 months in prison. Local Preacher, Eric Sagar had seen Propaganda's actions in THW and was disgusted. Yet he sore a true soul in Propaganda, and would like for him to say 'No' to this 'disgusting' violence and join Eric Sagar in a campaign against them. So he visited the prison and told him about Jesus, God, and the entire bible. Sagar had Propaganda eating out his hand; he was hanging off his every word. He had found god. Sagar bribed the Parole officer (though he won't deny it) to bring Propaganda's parole earlier then scheduled. He told them how he had found god, and they believed him. They allowed him to be free, so he was and him and Sagar held numerous unsuccessful rallies against THW. This went on for 6 whole months. And got unpopular, so as the old proverb goes 'If you can't beat them, join them.' So that what he did. Eric Sagar managed Propaganda to the THW title! And finally Sagar told Propaganda to attack the Chairman! He did, and the Chairman was unable to continue running THW, and no-one dared take it off him just in case Propaganda and Sagar strike again. So it was officially declared shut. Propaganda and Sagar seeming happy with themselves decided if they could do that to a regional Indy fed with no trouble, why not try to take down a big fed? And they're targets are firmly set on Jane Regan's OSW. Eric Sagar Real Name: Eric Sagar Nick Name: 'The Reverend,' 'The Preacher,' 'Fat Boy!'
