AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: Bio It began on a rainy day in November. Jason Daniel Avondale was born, one month premature, in the back of a filthy taxicab on the side of a crowded American street. He was never told which city, and every time he asked his mother as a youth she never could seem to recall. He was born to a woman named Veronica Jean Avondale, a recovering alcoholic and frequent drug user. Her father had kicked her out of the house when she was just 17, but she didn't mind it very much. She knew how to survive on her own. Veronica was just 19 when she found herself pregnant with Jason. The name of Jason's father was another enigmatic detail that repetitive drug use had erased from her memory. To the best of her recollection, Jason's father was a professional fighter, whom she had met in a bar the night before one of his events. He explained to her that he was only in town for the weekend, and he had to return to Japan on Monday, where he made his living. They made the most of their night together. Veronica had no job, and no home. Though Veronica was by no means a model parent, she always did her best to provide for her young son. She and Jason moved to New York, where there were more than a few men willing to help out a nice young girl with a pretty face. If they were lucky, the two could make themselves at home at the residence of Veronica's current love interest, but some nights were harder than others. Jason can still recall the early memories of sleeping in abandoned buildings and darkened alleyways. His earliest recollection is waking up on cold pavement, surrounded by migrant rats and stray garbage. Nonetheless, Veronica did everything she could to keep Jason happy, healthy, and safe. Because of this, Jason loved her unconditionally. As most boys tend to do, Jason was struggling to find his identity, and as a result he fell into a dark cycle of misbehavior and criminal action. He took up smoking. He began to experiment with drugs. He stole from people and vandalized property. At a young age, he took an interest in the underground music scene, and developed a taste in gothic rock and heavy metal. When he was 13, he began to hang around with other boys who shared these same interests. His eyes were opened to new things. He'd participate in fight clubs in an attempt to make extra cash. Jason was smaller than his friends at the time, and because he was the runt of the group, they were surprised to see how well he held his own in combat. During night time get togethers his friends would divulge upon him their beliefs in Satanism and told him of ancient organizations and rituals. Naturally, he was fascinated, as it was the first time he'd ever experienced religion. He'd spend much of his free time the next few years educating himself on the darker realms of mythology and lore. The next chapter in Jason's life remains partially buried in cloudy gusts of rage and anger. The most Jason can remember is that one day, at the age of 16, he returned from a late night gathering of his friends to the home of his mother's boyfriend, Kyle Porter. Upon his arrival, he found his mother weeping and in pain in the corner of her bedroom. She had been beaten severely, with heavy bruises around her face and back. Her lower lip had been cut open, and large amounts of blood ran down the side of her cheeks. He rushed to his mother and cradled her in his arms, but even the slightest touch of her battered figure made her wince. As he held his mother in his arms, he cried for the first time since he was a small child. Jason's tears soon turned to anger. He regained his composure, and entered the adjacent room, where he found his mother's boyfriend watching television, in the dark. As Jason noticed a drop of his mother's blood on the man's hand, he lost any sense of compassion he ever had. He attacked Porter, maliciously, mercilessly, and with no regard to the consequences he might have to face. He put the man's head through the television set, and then hurled him against the wall, where he proceeded to drive his foot into the side of Porter's head. The next minutes of Jason's life are a blur to him, but moments later he was standing over the near lifeless man, feeling happy ... laughing ... with remnants of blood spattered on his arms, chest, and face. He called help for his mother, kissed her on the forehead, and fled. He spent the next few days roaming the streets, thinking. He had experienced a sensation that he had never known before, and he very much enjoyed it. Jason made the decision to travel to Japan, until his former life in America was forgotten. Once in Japan, Jason rid himself of his former identity. He became known as 'The Magi,' a name chosen after an ancient class of astrologers, interpreters of dreams, and bringers of omens, whose work Jason has become greatly interested in. Several weeks passed before he met up with Shinja Yamamoto, a veteran fighter and experienced trainer who saw great potential in The Magi's ability. He taught The Magi the ways of lethal combat, and the martial arts. As he mastered his abilities, he also fought in Japanese Death Matches, and hardcore professional wrestling circuits. Despite his size, The Magi always left standing, and his vigor and intensity earned him a great deal of respect. He found success, he found a passion, he found a way to take his mind off the pains of his troubled life. Eight years later, at the age of 24, he returned to America with his new identity. He never truly learned what happened to his mother, or what condition she was in, but an old friend had heard that she moved out to the country somewhere, somewhere peaceful, somewhere she could be happy and rest. His friend also mentioned that Kyle Porter was cleared of all the assault charges that were brought against him, after claiming that he and Veronica had both been attacked by the same 'demon' from the shadows that evening. A 'demon' who was never found. Despite the troubles he faced, The Magi continued his career in professional wrestling, turning back to wrestling for the distraction and personal solace. His depression subdued when he found himself in the ring. He had lived a life of pain, and found that returning pain to another human being served as a small sense of retribution for him. Others made quick notice of him, and found his extreme style and tenacity gave him a distinct advantage over his opponents. His undying lust for blood struck fear in many of his opponents, and those who took him lightly were met with an unpleasant surprise. He met a quick rise to the top in the NLW, ICWL, and several other small promotions in the Midwest and East Coast, specializing in hardcore and high risk match-ups. No amount of success he's seen could bring him joy. The Magi remains broken, mysterious, with something to prove. He will always seek revenge from the world that has abandoned him. Most recently he was seen as part of the fWo Survivor competition, taking the opportunity to step in front of the world and reveal himself for the first time. Since his elimination he has avoided the spotlight that other contestants stepped into. Promo Archives Quotes
