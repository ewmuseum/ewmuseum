DT vs. Toomes, OSW World Title

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DT VS TOOMES, OSW WORLD TITLE Nate: Ladies and Gentlemen, we are back on Saturday SLAM and we are about to have our Main Event, Doug Troy versus the OSW World Champion, Switchblade, for the coveted title! Lets head straight to Earl Henrich!! Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the OSW World Title! Introducing first, the challenger, standing 6’1” tall and weighing in at 230 lbs: DOUG TROY!!! ‘Brainless’ by The Urge plays over the loudspeaker as Doug Troy emerges from the backstage area to a chorus of cheers from the fans! The ovation is deafening as Troy walks down the entryway, slapping the occasional fans palm as he does! Nate: The fans are showing a tremendous amount of respect and support for Doug Troy! This is more than I can say for Sebastian Toomes. Earl: And now, introducing the OSW World Champion, he stands 6’2” tall and weighs in at 245 lbs: Switchblade!!!! “King-Size” by Anthrax begins to play as the reception for The World Champion takes a nosedive. Some fans actually throw things at Toomes as he enters from backstage and heads down to the ring. Toomes slides into the ring as referee Pierre Gaston calls for the bell and Henrich bails out of the ring. Nate: And this match is underway! The fans strongly behind Doug Troy and against Switchblade, not that that was much of a surprise, eh gentlemen? Steven: Yep! Robbie: Nope! Nate: Thanks for the commentary, boys. Steven: Sure! Robbie: Uh huh! Nate mutters obscenities under his breath. In the ring, Doug Troy and Toomes stare each other down as the crowd grows in intensity for the tense match. Troy motions for Toomes to come and get him, but Switchblade makes a motion of a belt around his waist and tells Troy to come get him instead. Troy looks to the fans and grins as the fans cheer loudly, and then takes Switchy up on the offer. Troy rushes forward for what appears to be a clothesline, but switches to a baseball slide at the last moment and catches Switchblade completely off guard. Switchblade falls forward on top of Troy! Nate: Nice move by Doug Troy!! Robbie: Luck!!! Troy rolls Switchblade off of him and widens the distance between them both again with a cocky smirk on his face. Switchblade gets up and shakes his ankle, a look of rage on his face. Troy points at Switchblade and tells it’s his turn…. Nate: Some mind games here being played by Doug Troy! It looks like the Master Manipulator is being manipulated! Robbie: He’s playing mental possum. Not to be outdone, Switchblade runs forward to clothesline Troy, but instead runs into an armdrag and is taken down to the mat. Troy is on top of Switchblade instantly and locks on a rear chinlock. Switchblade kicks his legs out to try and catch the bottom rope. He tries once, twice, and on the third time he scooches out enough to get the rope break. Steven: As the World Champion, I think Switchblade should have to stick out every hold instead of going for the cheap ro— Robbie: Shut up Steven! Gaston tells Troy to let go, and he does. Switchblade holds onto the ropes and hauls himself to a standing position. Troy grins wide and stands his ground. Switchblade and Troy lock up in the center of the ring for the first time. Switchy hits Troy with a knee to the gut and follows up with a swinging neckbreaker for good measure. Swtichy bounces off the ropes and leaps for a knee drop, but at the last possible moment Troy slides to the side!! Nate: NO ONE HOME!!! Switchblade BLASTS his knee into the mat and falls to the side, clutching it in agony. Troy climbs to his feet and lifts Switchblade. Switchy gouges Troy in the eye with a thumb and hits a jawbreaker, staggering the veteran! Switchblade snap suplex’s Troy to the mat and goes for a quick pin! 1…kickout by Troy immediately! Nate: Doug Troy has been talking about how the OSW has been corrupt. It’s going to take a lot to put him down! This is the opportunity that he’s been waiting for! Both men get to their feet at the same time and lock up again. Troy with an armbar, reversed into an armbar by Switchy. Troy spins to clothesline Switchblade, but the Champ catches him for a belly to belly suplex. Doug blocks it with his foot and reverses it into a headlock takedown!! Nate: Tremendous succession of moves between challenger and champion! Steven: This isn’t your typical brawl, guys. These two not only want to win, but also show each other who is the true wrestler! Robbie: Which is Switchblade. Switchblade struggles to stand, still in the headlock. Both men get to their feet as Troy wrenches the headlock on harder. Switchblade grunts and shoves Troy forward, breaking the hold. Troy bounces off of the ropes and is back body dropped by Switchblade. Switchy drops an elbow over the neck of Troy and hooks the leg. 1…kickout by Troy! Switchblade lifts Troy and sends him into the corner. He rushes forward, only to be greeted by a boot to the face from Doug. Troy grabs Switchblade by the head and slams his forehead into the turnbuckle. He slams it again, but Switchy gets his boot on the turnbuckle and blocks it and slams Doug’s head into the turnbuckle instead. Robbie: WAHOO!! GO SWITCHBLADE!!! Nate: When are you going to contribute to his match, Robbie? Robbie: When I feel like it! Switchblade throws Troy into the corner and grabs his head and drags his eyes over the ropes! Troy screams and elbows Toomes in the gut. Troy swings to punch Toomes but is wide, obviously blinded from the ropes. Switchblade clotheslines Troy up and over the top rope to the floor and follows him out, yelling at him as he does. Nate: Listen to this crowd!! Crowd: SWITCHBLADE SUCKS!!! SWITCHBLADE SUCKS!!! SWITCHBLADE SUCKS!!! Switchblade screams at the crowd as he gets to the floor, which buys Troy some time to regain his bearings. Toomes stalks over towards Troy, but Troy grabs him by the front of his pants and pulls him into the steel steps, sending him crashing into them and falling in a heap! Nate: Doug Troy played possum and Switchy paid for it!! Troy gets up and points to his head and smiles. He turns to Switchy and sets him up for a piledriver and pulls. Switchblade blocks it. Troy tries again but again Switchy foils the attempt, only this time he reverses the piledriver into a back body drop and drops Troy back first onto the corner of one of the stairs!! The Crowd: OOOOOOOOOOHH!! Switchblade falls to a knee, favoring the same knee that he missed the knee drop and then subsequently hit it into the steps. Troy howls in pain as blood seeps from a wound in his back caused by the jagged corner of the steps. Nate: DT IS JUICING!!! Switchblade stands and reaches under the ring and pulls out a foldout table. He unfolds the legs of the table and sets it up, but by the time he is finished, Troy is to his feet. Doug kicks Switchy in the gut and slides him into the ring. Steven: Smart move by DT!!! Outside leaves more of an advantage for Switchblade!!! Robbie: Inside or outside, this is all according to Seabass’ plan. Troy slides into the ring after Switchblade and sends him into the ropes. Switchblade sneaks in a back elbow on Troy before he’s ready on the rebound and follows up with a hard forearm to the bridge of Troy’s nose. Troy staggers into the corner. Switchy follows and sets him up on the top rope and executes a frankensteiner!! Switchblade hops on the tope rope and leaps off!! Nate: This could be it!! Switchblade goes for a splash, but Troy gets his knees up! Switchblade flips off of Troy’s knees and lands hard on his back. Troy gets to his feet groggily, the frankensteiner taking a lot out of him. Doug goes for the pin. 1…. 2… Kickout by the champ! Nate: Near fall there folks! The match is taking its toll on both men! Troy lifts Toomes and sends him into the ropes, on the rebound, Troy monkey flips Toomes OVER the top rope to the outside!! Steven: GREAT move by Doug Troy!! Troy stands arrogantly and dusts his hands off as the crowd cheers louder for him. Crowd: DT!! DT!! DT!! Switchblade rolls over weakly and snakes a hand under the ring, pulling out a fire extinguisher!! Robbie: HA HA!!! Nate: GASTON, GOD DAMN IT!!! HE HAS A FIRE EXTINGUISHER FOR CHRIST SAKE!!! Toomes leaps to the apron and yells at Doug. Troy turns just as Gaston rushes forward to stop Toomes, but it’s too late. Toomes discharges the extinguisher, sending white powder spraying everywhere, enveloping Gaston and Troy!! As the extinguisher empties, Toomes tosses it away and gets into the ring. Nate: I CANT BELIEVE THIS!! Gaston grabs at his eyes as the powder clings to his greasy beard and staggers into the corner. Troy swings wildly at Toomes, but his vision is horribly impaired!! Toomes points at laughs at Troy and then locks up with him and sets him up for the Future Shock!! Robbie: MUWAHAHAHA!! FUTURE SHOCK!! Steven: LOOOOOOOK!!! The camera swings to a shot of the crowd, as from the crowd, Ace Hawk suddenly emerges and slides into the ring with a chair. Nate: ACE HAWK IS OUT HERE!!! Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! He winds back and SLAMS the chair into the back of Toomes’ head! Toomes lets go of the still blinded DT and stumbles back. The crowd explodes. Robbie: CALL FOR THE DQ DAMN IT!!! Nate: THE REF IS STILL BLINDED FROM THE FIRE EXTINGUISHER!!! Hawk slides out of the ring, as the crowd continues to go wild. Then, Toomes staggers forward into the arms of Troy who instinctively nails THE TAKE!!! Nate: THE TAKE!! THE TAKE!! Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Hawk grins at as the roof almost erupts from the cheers of the fans. Troy drapes an arm over Switchblade. Gaston blinks and regains his vision and drops to the mat… Crowd: 1……… 2………… 3!!!!!! Winner and new OSW World Champion: Doug Troy The crowd explodes. The Crowd: DT!!! DT!!! DT!!! DT!!! DT!!! Nate: DOUG TROY WON IT!! DOUG TROY IS THE NEW OLD SCHOOL WRESTLING WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!! Troy is handed the world title by Gaston and raises it high over his head while Toomes rolls out to the floor! Robbie: HE CHEEEEEEAAAAATED!!!! Nate: THE CROWD FINALLY HAS SOMETHING TO CHEER ABOUT!!!! But suddenly, some of the cheers turn to boos, while other fans erupt into a flurry of confusion. Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS HAPPENING?!?!! Robbie: LOOOOOOOK!!! Troy drapes the belt over his shoulder and waves to the fans when in the blink of an eye, The Polish Punisher, Tom Walczak, appears from the crowd and climbs into the ring with a chair of his own!! Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS WALCZAK OUT HERE FOR?!?!! Robbie: APPARENTLY THAT SODA DOUG OWES HIM!!!! Steven: DUCK DT!!! DUCK!!! Doug however, cannot hear, let alone see anything. Walczak spins Troy around and BREAKS the chair over Doug Troy’s head, sending blood spurting everywhere! The crowd: Ooooooooooooo!!!!! Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Robbie: KILLLL!!! KILLLLL!!! KILL!!!! Walczak grabs Troy and sends him into the ropes, Troy rebounds into a kick to the gut and Walczak sets him up for THE POLISH POWERBOMB!!! Nate: SOMEONE CALL SECURITY!!!! THAT CROMAG IS ATTACKING OUR CHAMPION!!! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Robbie: THIS IS GOING TO BE A KODAK MOMENT!!! Walczak lifts Troy high into the air via a power bomb and releases him OUT OVER to top rope, launching him into the ringside area. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Flash bulbs go off around the arena, as the bloody and helpless DT comes from over 10 feet in the air down through the table that was set up on the floor at ringside up during the title match by Toomes!!! Troy crushes through the wood, then slams into the floor and simply lies there amidst the wreckage of the table. Nate: GOOD GAWD!!! TOM WALCZAK JUST POLISH POWERBOMBED THE NEW OSW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OVER THE TOP ROPE AND THROUGH A TABLE IN THE RINGSIDE AREA!!! Robbie: THERE IS A GOD!!!! Nate: WE ARE OUT OF TIME!!! WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT ON SLAM!!! The crowd is in an outrage. Security rushes out to stop fans from jumping the guardrail, while many other fans begin showing their anger by lobbing garbage and other debris to the ring at the massive Walczak, stares out at the battered and broken Doug Troy, admiring his own handiwork. The camera then shifts to the new OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Doug Troy, who lies unmoving amidst the remnants of the table in the ringside area. The camera pans in on DT’s blood covered face, catching a glimpse of the now blood covered OSW World Heavyweight title still sling over his shoulder, while in the background the deep, croaking laughter of The Polish Punisher Tom Walczak can be heard over the bloody cries of vengeance from among the crowd…as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears on the lower central portion of the screen….as the show fades to black.
