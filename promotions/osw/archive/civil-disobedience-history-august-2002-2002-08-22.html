Civil Disobedience — history (August 2002)

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Every man has a breaking point. It's just a matter of time before a situation becomes too much to deal with. Action must be taken before it's too late. The state of Old School Wrestling making them sick, Civil D did just that. They took action. They formed The Elite. Can you really blame them? With all of the backstage garbage that takes place day in and day out, they had no other choice. Week after week of disrespect, of getting shit on by their fellow roster members, of not being taken seriously by the fans, of being disgusted with the OSW in general...all of these things have led to the formation of the stable that will defy everything that Old School stands for. Along with Ex-World Champion and all around hated asshole Sebastian Toomes and Amy Campbell they have formed the Elite in hopes of shaping the OSW into a more pleasant image in their eyes. After all, they are better than everyone. Doesn't it make sense that they set the standard? They are the shining stars that will define a new era in Old School Wrestling. No longer do they have to hold back how they really feel. They won't have to fake smiles in front of the cameras. They won't have to wave and slap hands with the dumbass fans. They get to be themselves. They're raw, unadulterated egotiscal, son-of-a-bitch selves. Did you really think being cool meant you had to be nice? If you did, you were full of shit. Back in school, the assholes were always the ones placed on a pedestal, and here in OSW Civil Disobedience knows it is no different. It's simple, really. They rock hard, and you don't. But that's just obvious. You didn't come here to read about what you already know. This is a biography and there's a whole back story behind our narcissistic duo. It starts a long time ago, in a suburb far, far away. If you can believe it, these two assholes weren't always assholes. Well Trevor wasn't, at least. Seth never really did have an ounce of morality. Trevor, though...there were high hopes set for Trevor. His parents were extremely wealthy, as his father was a successful entrepreneur, and this wealth led him to experience the best things money could buy. From the finest schools, the most trendy clothes, anything his heart desired was his. The only downside to this was, as with most wealthy families, his parents divorced when he was still young, causing his mother to recieve full custody of his care. From that point on, there wasn't much that could be done to stop him from becoming the one thing that is the antithesis of all 'coolness': The Mama's Boy. Soon, although he recieved whatever he wanted, he had lost his free will in return. A fair deal? No frickin' way. Then along came Seth Masters... Seth had grown up in the same town as Trevor...only he resided on the other side of the tracks. You know, the tacky part of suburbia. His life was rather normal by today's standards, which may account for why he's so f#cked up today. Bored with mundane Aurora, Illinois, he was constantly searching for excitement in all its forms. He wasn't the sharpest knife in the drawer, especially when it came to common sense, but he had a way of looking at the world that made him successful. If he wanted something, he'd take it. If he felt like doing something, he'd do it. All in all, he did whatever the hell he wanted to, living his life by the moment, and he was happy doing so. His self-centered take on life caused him to fall out of touch with reality, which will explain why he now resides in his own little world. Now you're probably asking yourself, 'How the hell did these two guys ever wind up as a team? A guy like Seth would hate a spoiled f#ck like Trevor, right?' And in reply I would say, 'Right, he would.' And Seth did hate Trevor at first. The two met in a self-defense class both were enrolled in at the age of thirteen. Trevor was taking it because his mother wanted him to interact with other 'nice boys his age', while Seth was taking it to more or less know how to kick people's asses. After some initial bickering, Seth realized how pathetic Trevor really was and took it upon himself to straighten out the other boy's priorities. Immediately the two began hanging out (much to the chagrin of Trevor's mother) and slowly but surely, Trevor began to fall into Seth's pit of delinquincy. Ever since, the two have been as tight as Snoop Dogg and the D-R-E And if you don't know how tight that is, that's pretty damn tight. Since then, the two have become obsessed with becoming the 'most bad-ass mofos on the planet' and what better way to do so than in the sport of professional wrestling? I sure as hell can't think of one. Amazingly, Trevor has made it through college, despite Seth's constant ragging on him to drop out. He now can claim a bachelor's degree in Philosophy from the University of Chicago, though it's not like it's going to do him any good in OSW. A stay in the Primetime Wrestling Academy has gotten them a shot at the big time (though it did get Trevor kicked out of his house along the way). These two egomaniacs are set to make complete asses of themselves on the way to the top, and I know I for one will be keeping my eye on them. Until they start making fun of midgets, that is. Even I will not stand for the defamation of the little people. After a month and a half of feuding with the gargantuan team of Azrael Cain and Hessian, Heresian Bloodlust, Seth and Trevor managed to become the first ever OSW Tag Team champions by beating the gothic warriors at the Crowning Glory pay-per-view in a brutal Suburban Death Match. Unfortunately for Civil D however, their reign was a short one. Twenty days later they were cost the belts when unfunny actor/comedian Carrot Top, angry at being bumped from and insulted on their cable access show Seth's World, interfered in their rematch with Heresian Bloodlust. Putting their public access broadcasting rights on the line, Civil Disobedience challenged Carrot Top and his fellow irritating comrade Gilbert Gottfried to a match at Head-On Collision, where they triumphed despite the actions of guest referee David Arquette, thus striking a mighty blow for haters of annoying people everywhere. The victory was bittersweet, however, as later in the night when coming to the rescue of backstage beauty Shanda Russell from the hands of the monstrous Heresian Bloodlust, Civil D fell ambush to an attack from behind, courtesy of returning stars Da Westsidaz. Nobody punks Seth and Trevor out and gets away with it. That's why set out to show Riptah and Short Circuit that its the MID-WESTSIDE that's the best side...and hopefully win a little tag gold from Hessian and Cain along the way. In the midst of all these happenings, an event occurred that changed their mindsets for the better. The event I speak of is none other than the formation of the Elite. One chairshot to the head of Andrew Ace Hawk changed everything. Joining with long time friends, PWA classmates, and fellow superior beings Sebastian Toomes and Amy Campbell they made the best move of their professional careers. For it took this step to break them out of the role they were currently occupying as the federation's comic relief. The Elite made people take them seriously, and in turn they matured because of this. After coming away from their three-way feud dejected and empty-handed, Seth and Trevor are now focusing their sights on a more important task. The tag team titles are important to them, but the number one priority in their eyes is the elimination of the Think Tank from the OSW once and for all. They have come too far to have all their hard-work ruined by the radical group, and if it's the last thing they do, they'll drive them out of wrestling forever. However, if there is a lesson to be learned from the death of Ed, it is this. The troubling thing is...clashing with the Think Tank may very well be the last thing Civil Disobedience does. Ever.
