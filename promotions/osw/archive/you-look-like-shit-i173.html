You Look Like Shit

2002-02-09
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You Look Like Shit Written: February 9, 2002 Nate: We need to head to a commercial. We will be right back after these….Wait a minute, I am being told there is a commotion in the backstage area, so we are going to send you back there right now! The scene shifts to the backstage area as Ritchie Syxx walked down the corridor, staring down at the ground. Ritchie: How the hell could I lose last week to that idiot, Corg... Ritchie’s words trail off Syxx, once he sees Corg walking towards him. Nate: Uh oh…this is not going to be good! Ritchie: (gritting his teeth) You stupid son of a bitch! Corg: (coolly) No, I'm afraid your little piece of [censored], and you know what? Your hair looks like [censored], so why don't you shave it off and give us all a moment to breath a sigh of relief? Ritchie: Go to he... Ritchie’s words trail off when suddenly he is caught off guard by a hard forearm from Corg. Syxx responds back with a hard right, but after a few seconds of brawling, OSW security chief, Moose Malone, comes in, and grabs both collars. Malone: All right that’s enough! Female Voice: (from off camera) You two, save your anger for the ring... The camera pans out to reveal the source of the sultry warning as none other than OSW owner, Jane Reagan. Reagan views the dog collars around each men’s neck, worn as a sign of fashion, Reagan: (continuing)…and while we're at it, why don't we treat you both like the animals you both are? Moose smiles and nods, as the scene fades to commercial.
