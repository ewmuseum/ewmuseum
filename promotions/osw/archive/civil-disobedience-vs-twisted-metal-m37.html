Civil Disobedience vs. Twisted Metal

2002-02-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil Disobedience vs. Twisted Metal Written: 2-23-02 Nate: Well, since we are talking about tag teams, coming up next, we have a tag match between the bad asses from Suburbia, Civil Disobedience and those evil robots, Twisted Metal! Robbie: Do you always sound like some cheesy game show announcer? Nate: What? Steven: This match would be a lot better if Cain and Hessian were scheduled to make an appearance. Robbie: Why? So we can watch them lose for the fourth time. Ha! Steven: Do you want to die? Robbie: No! Steven: Then SHUT UP!!!!! Nate: Alright, let’s take it to good old Earl who is in the ring waiting to announce the wrestlers. The shot changes to Earl Henrich standing in the ring wearing his tuxedo and a very unhappy expression on his face. Steven: Well, get on with it there, Penguin! Steven starts to make sounds like Batman’s arch-nemesis the Penguin. Nate: What’s wrong Earl? Earl: OLD!!?? Nate: Oh...sorry about that Earl. Earl looks satisfied with the apology and turns to begin announcing the next match. Earl: Ladies and gentlemen. Boys and girls! Our next tag match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at a combined weight of 460 pounds. The bad asses from suburbia, Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, Civil Disobedience! “Pretty Fly for a White Guy” pierces the PA system. Masters and Hawke explode onto the entryway. When Masters lands completely on his feet though he flinches from pain in his leg. He immediately begins to rub his thigh. Nate: It seems that Masters is still a bit tender due to the assault from Azrael Cain and his sledgehammer last week. Steven: Serves him right! Masters regains his composure quickly. He and Hawke start to make their way down the steel ramp towards the ring. On their way down the ramp they are slapping hands with all of their fans that line the barriers. Hawke and Masters climb up the steps outside the ring. Hawke leaps up on the turnbuckle trying to get more energy form the crowd, Masters decides to favor his leg and climbs through the ropes to get into the ring. Nate: The way that Masters is favoring that leg, it seems that it is going to be tough for him to fight anywhere close to one hundred percent tonight. Steven: Good! Robbie: Do I really need to threaten you with the respirator thing again? Steven looks at Decker with a shocked and hurt look on his face. Steven: (under his breathe)...no. Robbie: What? I couldn’t here you. Steven: No!!! The shot goes back to Earl Henrich in the ring again getting ready to announce the other half of this match. Earl: And their opponents, weighing in at a combined weight of 800 pounds! Coming form Atlantic City, New Jersey. Making their first in ring appearance in OSW! Chico and Gunmetal, Twisted Metal! “Jesus Built my Hotrod” begins to rock the arena. Both Chico and Gunmetal appear at the top of ramp. As they enter their steps clang against the steel ramp. The fans are booing incessantly. Both robots raise theirs metal appendages into the air and begin to flick off the crowd for booing them. Nate: And here is Twisted Metal! It seems that it is obvious who the crowd favorite is in this one. Robbie: For weighing in at 400 pounds a piece, they’re kinda short don’t you think? Nate: Well, what do you expect form robots. Chico and Gunmetal make their way down the ramp. Every step that they take creates a loud clang sound of metal against metal. Chico stops in mid stride though and looks at a young kid holding a sign that reads “Gears and Bolts can’t Replace Heart! Chico and Gunmetal have the soul of a Quizanart!” Chico holds it up in the air showing the fans. The robot lowers the poster and holds it out in front of him. A small device appears out of his right shoulder and flames spew forth torching the poster into nothing but ash. Nate: Hey, that was uncalled for! Robbie: Oh come on! Quizinart? That sucked! The kid deserved it! Steven: Ha! I think that kid is crying! HA! Twisted Metal continues towards the ring now until they reach the apron. Gunmetal climbs unto the apron outside of the ring, but Chico uses his thrusters in his feet and use them to elevate himself from the floor over the top ropes and into the ring. Nate: Is that legal? Robbie: Sure it is! Leave the bots alone man! Trevor looks at Hawke and points for him to exit the ring. Masters shoots a funny look at his partner, but Hawke doesn't budge, he simply continues to point to the corner. Masters grudgingly exits the ring leaving Hawke and Chico in the ring to be the first ones to face off. Nate: It appears that Trevor Hawke is aware of his partner’s injury and is trying to preserve him. Steven: Ha! That’s it Trevor, get the cripple out of the ring! Nate: What’s the matter Decker? No comments? That’s not like you. Robbie: What?...Oh...Sorry I was just staring...I mean...ah...taking in the radiant beauty of our lovely referee for this match. Betty Lovejoy is in the ring filing away at her nails, completely unaware of the fact that the wrestlers are ready to start the match. Steven: Hey, Lovejoy! Get with the program. Signal for the damn bell already! Women! What the hell do we need them for? Robbie: Well, actually they’re great for... Nate: (interrupting) And it seems that we are ready to get underway here. Lovejoy finally signals for the bell. Hawke and Chico begin to circle each other in the ring trying to size each other up. The two wrestlers lock up. Robbie: Boring! Boring! Boring! Chico quickly overpowers Hawke. The robot picks Hawke up above his head and delivers a wicked bodyslam. Hawke bounces off the canvas about three feet into the air because of the impact. Nate: That is one hell of bodyslam by the robot, Chico. It seems that Civil Disobedience are going to have to use their speed to win this one. Chico leans over to pick up Hawke, but the suburban bad ass roll out of the way and quickly gets to his feet. Chico is still bent over, but manages to turn his head just in time to receive a spinning heel kick from Hawke. A loud clang sound is heard. Chico’s head begins to spin around on its neck. Nate: Quick move from Trevor Hawke. Steven: Ha! Chico looks like the little girl from the Exorcist! Robbie: What the hell is going on? Hawke is staring at the spinning head with look of confusion on his face. He continues to stare in bewilderment until he struck in the face by a right hook by the robot. Hawke falls back on his back from the punch. Chico raises both of his hands up and places them on his head in order to get it to stop spinning. Chico leans over and picks Hawke up by the back of his pants. Once Hawk is on his feet, Chico slings Hawke into the ropes who comes flying back at the android who delivers a back body drop that sends his opponent flying through the air and crashing violently against the canvas. Nate: Some great skill is being shown by Chico in this match. Steven: That’s not skill. That’s a blender in spasms. Robbie: What the hell is this? Battle Bots! Chico slowly approaches Hawke is on the mat clutching his back in pain. Chico grabs Hawke by the hair again and pulls him up the hair. As he does so though, Hawke drop kicks the robot with both feet right in the knee. As a result of the kick, Chico’s leg falls off at the knee. The robot’s red eye lights up in alert before it topples to the ground. Hawke is left clutching both of his feet and rolling around in more pain. Chico reaches out to grabs his amputated leg. Nate: Wait! Chico is trying to get his leg back. Steven: Hey, Betty! Get that thing out of the ring already! That is a possible weapon! Robbie: Is it? I mean, isn’t that still considered a part of his body and can’t he use it in the match then? Lovejoy is in the ring scratching her head not knowing what to do about the situation. Chico finally grabs his leg and crawls towards the hurt Hawke. Nate: Chico’s got his leg and looks like he is going to beat Hawke with it. Steven: Lovejoy, will you do something already? Goddam women, can’t do anything right. Chico is still crawling to Hawke, but before he can beat the senses out of is opponent, Hawke stretches and make sthe tag to his partner, Masters. Nate: I don’t know how he did it, but Hawke probably just saved himself what was most certainly another hospital bill. Steven: Damn!! Robbie: Who the hell are you rooting for in this match anyway, Smith? Steven: Neither of them. I just want to see both of these guys get the crap beaten out of them. That way, they both will be easy pickings for Heresian Bloodlust. BOCA! Masters enters the ring, but noticeably is favoring his injured leg. Masters looks at the robot as if he has no clue how to attack the disabled Chico. The hesitation costs Masters dearly though. Chico pulls his arm back that is wielding his severed leg and slams it right into the injured thigh of Masters. Masters wails in pain and crumples to the mat clutching his thigh in agony. Nate: It seems that Chico did his homework this week. He just nailed Masters in the leg that Cain injured last week. Steven: Look at Masters cry like a little baby. Ha! Look at him! Look! Chico starts to crawl to his corner in order to tag in Gunmetal. Masters is still on the mat in an intense amount of pain. Chico is stalling and still can’t make the tag. Masters is slowly climbing back to his feet. Gunmetal is extending his arm as far as he can. Chico gets an idea though and swings his leg at his partner’s extended hand. The tag is made and Gunmetal is now the official partner in the match. Nate: I guess that was the extra length that Chico needed to make the tag? Robbie: Is that legal? Steven: Damn woman! So help me God! (To Lovejoy) Will you please officiate at least one match during your stay here in OSW? Gunmetal hits the mat running like a locomotive. The robot doesn’t stop until he leaps through the air slams his shoulder into Masters delivering a powerful spear that drops Masters to the canvas again. Gunmetal proceeds to lay punches into Masters in the face. Masters face begins to bounce off the mat repeatedly as a result of the blows being delivered from the enraged robot. Steven: Ha! Look at Masters head! The way that is bounces reminds me of basketball! Nate: There is truly something wrong with you. The assault form Gunmetal is interrupted though by a Springboard clothesline delivered by Hawke. Gunmetal topples off of Masters and falls to his back. Lovejoy gets in Hawke’s face and removes him from the ring. Masters rolls over on his stomach and begins to crawl towards the stunned robot. Nate: It looks like Masters wants to make a pin! Masters is dragging his beaten body across the canvas. Gunmetal is slowly beginning to stir, but he is still noticeably stunned. Masters finally grabs Gunmetal’s leg and rolls over for a pin. Nate: And there’s the pin. Steven: But that damn woman is still messing with Hawke! Robbie: Hey, Lovejoy! You have a pin. Will you count already? Lovejoy turns her head and sees that Masters has a pin on. The girl crosses to the wrestlers and slowly climbs down to her knees in order to count. 1... 2... Thr... No! Gunmetal breaks the pin by tossing Masters off. Masters and Gunmetal are still down on the mat trying to shake the cobwebs out. Robbie: That was a slow count there, Betty. Nate: Both men are down and they don’t look good. Chico is up on the apron again after reattaching his own leg back on. Chico is extending as far as he can over the ropes trying to reach his partner for a tag. Hawke is on the other side of the ring trying to reach Masters. Both Masters and Gunmetal are crawling to their partners. Nate: It looks like whoever makes this tag first is going to win this match. Robbie: Good! Then maybe this match will be over. Gunmetal is reaching. Masters is reaching. Hawke leans over the ropes further. Chico reaches. Both Gunmetal and Masters lunge for their partners. Hawke leaps into the ring and sprints towards Gunmetal who fell inches short of his partner and is laying on the mat trying to get the strength to make the tag. Nate: And they made the tag I don’t believe it. Hawke grabs Gunmetal by the leg and drags him into the center of the ring. Hawke pulls Gunmetal to his feet. Hawke delivers a spinning heel kick to the robot. Gunmetal falls hard on his ass. Hawke leaps through the air and delivers a drop kick with both of his feet right into the robot’s face. Gunmetal is now sprawled out on his back in the middle of the ring. Hakwe climbs the ropes to try a highly dangerous move. Nate: Another high flying technical move. Robbie: But what the hell is he thinking? He is on the corner right in front of Chico! Steven: HA! Hawke looks down only to see Chico looking up at him. Hawke gets an “Oh shit” look on his face. Chico grabs the top rope and then leaps down to the concrete floor out side the ring. The rope stretches down causing Hawke to lose his balance. Hawke’s crotch crashes down into the turnbuckle. Nate, Robbie, and Steven: OOOOOOUUUUCCCCCCHHH!!!!!!! Hawke topples down off the turnbuckle onto his head. Hakwe is rolling round the canvas with one hand on his head and the other on his tender manly region. Gunmetal slowly rolls over and starts to crawl to Hawke. Gunmetal reaches out and grabs hold of Hawke’s leg, but Hawke starts to strike at Gunmetal’s head with his other leg. The repeated kicks start to slowly dent the metal head of the robot. Nate: I can’t believe that Hake still has that kind of fight in him. Robbie: Is Gunmetal going to do what I think he is? Steven: What the hell are you talking about? The red light on Gunmetal’s face begins to flash wildly. Robbie: I think he is. An incredible surge of energy comes out of the robot’s body. The electrical surge is flowing through Hawke’s body and everything else in the area. Hawke’s and Masters’ body begins to go into convulsions from the electricity. Nate: What the hell is that? Robbie: Overload! Overload! The electrical surge ceases. Gunmetal’s head falls to the mat and he is out cold. Masters has fallen off the apron and his laying on the concrete floor while Hawke is out cold in the ring. Nate: But why wasn’t Betty injured by that attack? Robbie: Those shoes she wears must be rubber. Steven: But there is no pin. What the hell is that damn robot doing? Nate: It says here that it takes a while for his programs to reboot. Until that happens he is vulnerable to anything. Betty Lovejoy is in the ring with a very confused look on her face, as usual. Three of the four wrestlers in the match are out cold and barely moving. Nate: Both wrestlers in the ring are down and out. Steven: Hey, Betty do you want to start counting? Lovejoy gets an “oh yeah” look on her face. She slowly begins to count. 1… 2… 3… ah…4… Steven: This might take a while folks, so please stand by. A gong and the dark chant, “Carmina Burana” blares through the PA system and the lights go out completely in the arena. Two massive silhouettes appear at the entranceway and quickly make their way down to the ring. Nate: Oh God! We all know what that means. Steven: YES WE DO!! It means the match just got all the more interesting! The lights come back on and Hessian and Azrael Cain are both down by ringside. Hessian is yelling at Lovejoy interrupting the count on the opposite side of the ring distracting her while Cain climbs up into the ring. Nate: This is never good. This usually means that someone is going to the hospital. Steven: Yes it does. And usually it is Civil Disobedience! Ha! Robbie: Betty! Hey, Betty! Quit yelling at Destro over there and look in the ring. BETTY!!!! Cain crosses over to Gunmetal and places the four hundred pound robot on top of Hawke. Hessian starts pointing inside the ring trying to get her attention so she will make the count. Nate: Heresian Bloodlust is costing Civil Disobedience this match! Steven: Yes, they most certainly are! Lovejoy turns around as Cain exits the ring and jumps down to the floor outside the ring. The beautiful bombshell ref looks at Cain and stares at him as he goes around the outside of the ring to Hessian. Lovejoy gets down and begins to make the count. 1… Hessian reaches down to grab the dazed and confused Masters by the hair. Hessian pulls Masters’ head up so he can see Lovejoy make the count on his partner. Masters begins to squirm. 2… 3… Winners of the match: Twisted Metal! Nate: Twisted Metal has done it! They have won their first match in OSW against the formerly undefeated Civil Disobedience! Robbie: Oh don’t get so excited! It’s not like it was legit. That beast, Cain, was the one that placed Gunmetal across Hawke’s chest for the pin. Steven: Yeah we know! That was great! Hessian looks at Masters then slams his head into the ring post. A loud “Clang” noise is heard as Masters’ head bounces and the blood begins to flow from his forehead. Cain and Hessian climb up to the apron and over the top rope to enter the ring. Lovejoy tries to stop them, but both men just walk right by her. Steven: This is going to be great! Hessian Cain look down at Hawke who is now trying to get out from underneath the four hundred pound robot, Gunmetal. Hessian effortlessly heaves the robot off of Hawke. Cain reaches down and hoists Hawke up to his feet. Cain then throws Hawke to Hessian. Hessian irishwhips the suburban wrestler into the ropes who comes back off them at great speed until he is Back Body Dropped by Hessian. Hawke flies through the air until he is caught by Cain right out of mid air and drops Hawke into a Death Valley Driver. Hawke bounces high into the air off the canvas, but lands hard again and isn’t moving. Steven: HA! Heretic’s Wrath! I love that move! Nate: Oh my God! Where the hell is Moose? Steven: Probably hiding. You saw what Cain did to him last week! HA! Someone from the officials’ table tosses a microphone to Hessian. Hessian turns and approaches his partner Cain who is now holding Hawke’s head up so he can hear what Hessian is about to say. Hessian crouches down right in front of Hawke. Hessian: Hello boy! Did you miss me last week? I’m sorry that I couldn’t attend our match, but as you know I was a little preoccupied. Hessian headbutts Hawke in the face with his metal mask. Hawke’s nose begins to bleed profusely. Cain releases his Hawke’s head letting it fall to the mat. Hessian and Cain both rise to their feet now to address the booing fans. Hessian tosses the mic to Cain. Cain: Even though the two of us are preoccupied tonight in far more important matches, we still felt that is was necessary to come down tonight to let Civil Disobedience know we were thinking about them! We just wanted to remind everyone who the best tag team in OSW really is. We felt that it would be only suiting to show up tonight and make sure that despite the fact that we weren’t scheduled to fight you, that you would still lose your match! Chico has climbed into the ring now behind Cain and Hessian. The robot crosses over to Heresian Bloodlust. Robbie: What the hell is that damn robot doing? Chico taps both Cain and Hessian on their shoulders. Both men turn around to see who was foolish enough to interrupt them. Chico looks at them and after a second of uncomfortable ness, Chico extends his hands in order to thank Cain and Hessian for helping them win the match. Nate: I have a feeling that this is going to be bad, very bad. Steven: On the contrary. This is going to be great! Cain and Hessian look at one another for a second. They then both extend their arms and begin to shake Chico’s hands. Robbie: What the hell is going on in there? Both Hessian and Cain suddenly tug on Chico’s arms. Both arms are torn out of their sockets. Sparks are flying out of the holes where Chico’s arms usually are. Cain and Hessian are both holding an arm in their hands. Both men share a look for a moment. They proceed to beat the armless robot with his own severed arms. Steven: Ha! Nate: That just isn’t right! Chico is trying to run away, but without his arms he has no balance. Hessian and Cain continue to pound away at the damaged robot. Chico topples over shortly and falls to the mat as Heresian Bloodlust continue until there is no movement from Chico. Nate: This is disgusting! Someone call the EMT’s!!! Robbie: Forget the EMTs call the morgue. Steven: NOOOO! Crowd: OSW! OSW! OSW! OSW! OSW! EMTs rush down to the ring carrying their equipment…. as the scene fades to commercial.
