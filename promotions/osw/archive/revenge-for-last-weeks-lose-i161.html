Revenge For Last Weeks Lose

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Revenge For Last Weeks Lose Written: May 25th 2002 The show reopens to reveal a muscular forearm clutching a wooden handle. Moving slowly up the handle, we see that it is topped with an unforgiving steel sledgehammer head. Moving from the evil looking head of the sledghammer, we focus in on the even more evil looking face of Graphic Violence. Graphic Violence smiles as he grips the sledgehammer tightly in his hands. He had planned every thing correctly, it would be his victory tonight. Then he spots his prey. Rhett Locke. Whilst Rhett Locke walks the fluorescent hallway, the smile of a sadist trail blazes across GV's face. The image of Locke suddenly appears in the corner of Graphic Violence's eye, and. . . WHAM! Rhett: SON OF A BITCH! Rhett drops to the floor instantly after the sledgehammer comes crashing into his side. The sledgehammer GV wields like a samurai and his sword becomes an object that begins the decimation of Rhett Locke as it slams into Rhett's ribcage over and over. GV: You thought you had beaten me, didn't you Rhett? You thought that you were one hell of a guy, able to beat Graphic Violence, eh? Well let me tell you something, you stupid piece of shit! NOBODY EVER BEATSME! WHAM! WHAM! the sledgehammer attempted to destroy Rhett's knee, and Locke screamed sounds of horror; he screams sounds of extreme pain. GV: You thought you had made quite an accomplishment, that people would sing your praises for weeks. Nobody will sing your praises again after this Rhett! You know why? Rhett provides no answer. GV: DO YOU KNOW WHY!?! Rhett: NO!. . . GV: Because I'm. . .going. . .to kill you. WHAM! Rhett's knee is battered... WHAM! His ribcage becomes brittle... WHAM! Rhett Locke had been beaten, beaten at the hands of a maniac in Graphic Violence. GV spit in Rhett's face, and chuckles. GV: I win. . .fuckin' albino. Graphic Violence did win, both mentally and physically. Rhett Locke is beaten, battered, bruised, and slain. And as the OSW security and doctors come to check on the decimated Rhett Locke, GV takes his march of victory through the halls. But Graphic Violence's night had just began.
