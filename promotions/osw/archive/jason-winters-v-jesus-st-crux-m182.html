Jason Winters v Jesus St Crux

2002-05-11
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jason Winters v Jesus St Crux Written: May 11, 2002 The scene reopens at the ringside position of the OSW announce team. Nate: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome beack to Slam! Up next, a rematch between Jesus St Crux and Jason Winters - a match interrupted last week by the Northern Wonder, Kanaida! Robbie: Are they still squabbling over that silly defunct title? Nate: I think it's gone beyond just being about a piece of hardware. These men want to prove - to themselves and to the world - that they are among the very best! Steven: Mmm... I certainly think they'd be among the best - Robbie: Please Earl, start talking before Steven finishes that thought. Earl: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is an unsanctioned match for the UWA World Heavyweight Championship! The lights dim, and then the first chords of 'Fever Dog' play. With each drum riff, pyros explode to the side of the entrance ramp, and when the first words are spoken, Jesus and Caridad step out from backstage. Jesus stands at the top of the ramp, looks up at the ceiling, makes the sign of the cross, and then chops his hand downward. Jesus and Caridad run down and slide into the ring as pyros go off at the corners, and then they stand on opposite turnbuckles, and raise their arms as the crowd chants 'Jesus'. Earl: Introducing first, being accompanied by Caridad Mariposa, standing 5'8' and weighting 220lbs, from Juarez, Mexico, Jesus St. Crux! St Crux hops down from the turnbuckle as Caridad exits the ring. He drops into a crouch, staring intently at the entrance as the lights dim. As 'Parabola' plays over the speakers, pyros explode to the side of the entrance, and Jason Winters steps out and begins walking down the ramp. Earl: And the challenger, from Vancouver, British Columbia, standing 6'3' and weighting 237lbs, Jason Winters! The two men stare at each other in the center of the ring. Tentatively, each extends a hand and they briefly shake before circling the ring as Speedy Riggs starts the match. Robbie: Oh no... not respect... I hate when there's respect. The people want blood! Winters starts forward for a grapple, but St Crux is quicker and sidesteps the Canadian and sweeps his legs out from underneath. Continuing his momentum and rolling quickly, Jesus St Crux pushes himself into the air and drops a leg across Winters' neck. Robbie: You need more air than that! Nate: St Crux definately isn't the stronger of the two, but he can do a lot of damage with a flurry of attacks. St Crux gets a leg scissors around Winters' head and starts to twist, rolling Jason Winters onto his stomach. The Mexican releases the hold and spins to his knees, driving several in succession into Winters' kdineys. Jason Winters is pulled to his feet and Irish whipped to the ropes. St Crux charges after him and jumps into a Crucifix position, then continues around and plants the Canadian into the mat with a DDT. Steven: Is he as agile in bed I wonder... Jesus hooks one of Winters' legs, but somehow the Canadian kicks out before the referee can get into position. St Crux pulls Winters to his feet again and sends him back into the ropes. He launches a spinning heelkick, but it's ducked. Winters fires a quick elbow into the Mexican's temple, staggering him long enough for Winters to send St Crux in an Irish Whip off the ropes. He ducks for a back body drop and Jesus jumps and does a head first roll down Winters' back. Standing nearly back to back, both men launch superkicks and catch each other in the back of the head, knocking both men to the mat. Robbie: Heh... rookie mistake... Nate: How so? Robbie: I'm sure it is. St Crux rolls onto his back and shakes his head to clear the cobwebs, then handsprings back to his feet. He races across the ring and uses the rising Winters as a step stool to jump up to the turnbuckle. Winters collapses back to the ground because of the sudden weight, and makes a perfect landing pad as St Crux backflips and hits a moonsault. Robbie: Yeah, yeah... the cruiserweight stuff is for another time and place... Jesus starts to Irish Whip Winters but holds on and reigns the Canadian back in, pulling him down to the mat in a Crucifix Crossface. Jason Winters starts flailing with his arm, trying to make sure the hold isn't cinched in. Eventually, his hand is able to grab the bottom rope, and Speedy Riggs forces St Crux to break the hold. Nate: Jesus St Crux was close, but once again, Jason Winters shows his great ring presence. St Crux stands and starts to pull Winters to his feet as well, but Winters drops his weight to the mat, and pulls St Crux back into the corner hard. He rises underneath the Mexican, driving his shoulder into the crusierweight's gut, and then dropping St Crux onto the top turnbuckle. Without hesitation Winters climbs up beside St Crux, and then slams St Crux down into the mat with a Winter's Discontent. Winters also collapses to the mat and rolls onto his back to catch his breath. Nate: Jason Winters hit the Winter's Discontent! He just needs to roll over for the cover! Speedy Riggs checks on both men, but as he starts to count them both out, Winters rolls onto his stomach and crawls over to St Crux. He grabs a leg and lifts it as he covers with a lateral press. Speedy Riggs drops into position and starts the count. 1... 2... 3 - NO! Jesus St Crux jerks at the last second and Riggs stops the count. Nate: How did St Crux kick out of that?! Robbie: Winters' mistake. He didn't go for the pin right away. Disbelief on his face, Jason Winters stands and whips Jesus St Crux into the ropes. Jesus bounces back towards the center of the ring, and ducks a Winters clothesline. Springing off the other side of the ring, St Crux takes to the air. Winters turns around just in time to take a forearm to the face, and both men crumple to the mat. Nate: What a shot! Jesus caught Winters square in the jaw with that forearm! This may be the shift in momentum that St Crux needs! Robbie: Sure, but he's not going to turn things around unless he picks his Mexican ass up off the mat! Steven: Mmmm... Mexican ass... so plump and juicy, in those nice little tights... they call him Jesus because he's got the butt of a god! Nate: Okay... I don't even know WHERE to begin with THAT one... Jason Winters begins to pull himself up a ring rope, trying to get his feet under him. Jesus, on his knees, pushes himself back up to a vertical base. And in the background, a figure can be seen sprinting down the entrance ramp. Robbie: Deja vu all over again... it's Kanaida! Nate: Thank god! Robbie: Wha.. why? Nate: Anything to get the thoughts that Steven put in my head out of there is welcome, ok? Robbie: Point taken. Kanaida slides into the ring at a full run, and drill Jesus to the mat with a big clothesline. He turns towards Winters and charges, but misses as Jason ducks! Speedy Riggs calls for the bell as Winters backs Kanaida up with a series of chops! Winner: Jesus St Crux Nate: Winters is pushing the big canuck backwards, but with St Crux making it to his feet, the odds are about to change! Jason Winters whips Kanaida hard into the ropes. St Crux steps up beside him. As Kanaida bounces off the ropes, he's met with a double superkick right to the point of the chin! Robbie: Ha! I guess these guys hate Kanaida more than each other? Steven: More likely they're offended at having their matches disrupted. I think they want to settle things between themselves, without any outside interferance. It's an honor thing. Robbie and Nate stare at Steven in shock. Steven: What? I'm not allowed to say something insightful once in a while? Nate: Sure, but it's just so rare... Steven: Whatever. I'm just happy because that big Sid look-a-like isn't hurting my latino sweet! Robbie can be heard audibly gagging. As Kanaida climbs to his feet, the two former opponents drive him backwards with a series of chops, until he's backed into the corner. Backing up a few steps, Winters whips St Crux into the corner, where he spears Kanaida down! As the big man gasps for air, St Crux whips Winters into the corner... and the boots of Jason Winters almost take the Northern Wonder's head off as he flies in with a dropkick! Nate: Wow! For two guys who hate each other, they sure work well together! Robbie: We're about to find out how well, I think. A muscular figure suddenly vaults over the barrier between the crowd and ringside and slides under the bottom rope taking up a position behind Winters and St. Crux. Nate: Why... hey, who's THAT? Steven: Mmmm... beefcake He steps up behind St Crux... and almost rips his head off with an incredible chairshot. Jesus drops insensible to the mat. Steven: Noooo... well, at least it was the BACK of the head, not the face. And at least he's face-down... mmmmmmmm... Jason Winters tries to make it to his feet, but is caught with a chairshot to his left knee. Kanaida rolls out under the bottom rope as Winters staggers around the ring. The crowd howls and throws garbage as the chair finds its mark across the young Canadian's forehead. Like St Crux before him, Winters collapses to the mat. Kanaida stares into the ring, and the first thing that he sees is a reinforced steel chair, with the word NURSE spraypainted on it in red paint. His eyes open in shock, and he looks up into the face of the man standing in the center of the ring, practically daring him to enter. Kanaida's jaw drops for a moment, then he grabs a microphone from ringside. Kanaida: I should have known... nobody else but you would be stupid enough to not only interfere in my business, but to bring Doctor Hardcore's pet chair to the ring. Nice to see you've crawled out from under whatever Irish rock you've been hiding under... MISTER Wright. Robbie: Mr Wright? Shiiiit... this just got interesting! This guy, I can respect! Nate: Another former UWA Champion? Robbie: Damn right. Beat Kanaida for the title, if I remember right. And as big a badass as you'll find in the ring. This guy doesn't give a damn about anything but getting his hand raised! Kanaida begins to walk around the ring, avoiding the garbage raining down from the fans, upset at the disruption of the second matchup of two of OSW's most exciting young stars. Kanaida: And I am really going to look forward to watching OSW's security haul your sorry ass out of here. They may be incompetant, but they can handle the likes of you. Steven: I'd like to handle all of these guys! Robbie: Shut up, Steven. Kanaida, gaining strength after his beating, hops up on the apron, and continues to berate Wright, who just stands in the center of the ring. Glaring. The fans in the arena continue to boo and throw garbage. Kanaida: You, Ian, don't belong here. The UWA is gone, and you should be too. Go back to hiding under the Blarney Stone, and get your sorry ass our of OSW. You do NOT belong here. Ian Wright pulls a microphone out of his back pocket, and raises it to his lips. Wright: Oh, you think so? In that case what the hell... scuse me for a moment. Wright has noticed St Crux has gotten on his knees and is trying to make it to his feet. Wright: Hey Jesus, how're you doing? Miss me? With that he kicks St Crux in the ribs lifting him off the ground in the process. Nate: That sonofabitch. Robbie: What? What? St Crux was gonna jump him from behind. Steven: Hey, if anyone is gonna do any jumping from behind it'll be... Nate: Not now Steven. Wright turns his attention back to Kanaida Wright: Sorry about that OLD *buddy*. In that case what the hell is this? He pulls out a piece of paper, and throws it at Kanaida. Kanaida reads it, and starts shaking his head. Wright: In case your damaged brain doesn't understand what you've got in your hands, that's an OSW contract and its got my name on it. And because you have such a short memory, let me refresh it... I just saved you from getting beaten down by a couple of lightweights. Kanaida crumples the paper in his hand, and steps between the ropes. A few fans begin to cheer, anticipating a fight to replace the match they are missing out on. With rage choking his words, Kanaida points a finger at Wright and spits out five words. Kanaida: I had that under control. Wright laughs. Wright: Oh, right... just like old times, you go fighting blindly only to get your ass kicked. Look, old man... why don't you drag yourself back to the retirement home; I thought that you had lost the taste for this. All the violence, the travel, the pain, the suffering, the injuries, the betrayal - last I heard you said you were never coming back. My advice to you, let me take that belt away from these two idiots, but don't get in my way, cause in many ways we are alike, but in one important way we are not. I don't care what I have to do, I don't care who I have to hurt and how badly. That was always your trouble Kanaida, you were much more moral then I; it led to your downfall as I recall. Kanaida walks over, and gets right into Wright's face. The Irishman and the Northern Wonder go chest to chest, nose to nose. The fans begin a chant of 'Kick his ass! Kick his ass!' Nate: Who are they cheering, anyway? Robbie: Wright, if they've got a brain! Kanaida's sounding more like a wimp by the minute! Kanaida: In the old days, I was your partner because it was either that or retire. Working with you and Doc Hardcore made me sick to my stomach every day... but the thought of retirement made me feel worse. So don't think that we've got that much in common, old 'buddy'. Morals? Damned if I know. My life isn't based around pleasing the fans... it's based on my love of the sport. You and I are a lot alike... we do what we want, when we want it. We have our own agenda, and don't care what gets in our way. But you're right... there are some differences. The first is that I would never stoop as low as you. I still have some respect in me. And second... you are not in my class, Irish. That title should belong to me... you'll be getting your grubby hands on it over MY DEAD BODY. Wright's face darkens, and he lowers his head. Then he looks up into Kanaida's eyes, and grins. Wright: That can be arranged. Ian Wright shoves Kanaida back, and swings Nurse at his head. Kanaida ducks, and spears Wright to the mat. Nurse goes spinning to the canvas as the two men fall out of the ring, swinging wildly. Robbie: What, is the entire roster of a dead federation going to come hunting for this belt? What's going on here? A wild uppercut by Wright sends Kanaida over the barrier, and into the fans along the rampway. Instantly, Moose Malone leads his security team down the aisle at a full run. Nate: We have to go to commercial... we'll have more on this when Slam comes back to (name of the arena)! As the cameras pan back before the commercial break, they pass over Winters and St Crux, still out cold in the center of the ring, with a sturdy steel chair between them. Its dents and cracks are evident, as is the spraypainted name Nurse across the seat.
