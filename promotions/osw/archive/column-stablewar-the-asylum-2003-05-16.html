Stablewar: The Asylum

16 May 2003

by Nate Dumas

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own column (#5) by Nate Dumas, dated 16 May 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Stablewar: The Asylum Greetings OSW fans. I’m Nate Dumas here again with another installment of a special column I am doing entitled “Stablewar.” My previous edition of Stablewar was an overarching description of the three stables that I see poised for collision. But this edition is a bit different. While the overall idea’s of the stables is important, a stable is only as cohesive and successful as it’s players, and that is what I am setting out to analyze next. Our first stable is Asylum. The Asylum is comprised of four members. Trashcan Man, Timo Bolamba, Azala Zameer, and Six Finger Cisco. Trashcan Man Info: Height: 6’3” Weight: 275 lbs. Style: Brawling, Hardcore, Sloppy High Flying Title History: OSW President (Current) Awards: 2 time Impact Player award, 2 time PCW World Champion, 2 time PCW Continental Champion, 3 time PCW Extreme Champion, PCW Hall of Famer, former PCW President, first in PCW to repeat a title reign, record for shortest PCW World title reign (3 minutes), record holder for fastest win in PCW history (defeated Lord Crom Griffin Dagoth in 4 seconds as Mr. Smiley Face to capture PCW Extreme title), 1st ever CWF Inferno Champion. Trashcan Man has a long, long history behind him not only in the OSW, but in the PCW and beyond. As a founding member of the original Asylum, Trashcan Man has seen it all. Currently, Trashcan Man is the President of the OSW and while perhaps new to the whole political schema, he is running things quite smoothly. Trashcan Man also serves as the leader of the new Asylum. The Trashcan Man is the soul of Asylum. Style As his name implies, Trashcan Man isn’t refined. He’s not elegant. He’s not rich. He’s the Trashcan Man and his style reflects that. Arguably the greatest hardcore legend in the WO today, Trashcan Man has the endurance and the tenacity that few other wrestlers can stand up against. While many wouldn’t think it, he is a genius in the ring and versatile as ever. It may not look pretty, but whether it be a high flying move off the top rope, a suplex, or a trashcan shot, it’s certainly going to hurt. His fighting style is a strange mix of raw power and unorthodox tactics, which leaves his opponents second-guessing their stability in the ring. Strengths Iron Will: Trashcan Man has been through a lot. Both physical and emotionally, many can attest to the anguish he has endured. Miraculously, he’s bounced back from all of them to still be his own person and a true leader, rather than a follower. How do you stop a man that had a chip implanted in his head and lived to tell about it? Resourceful: As mentioned above, Trashcan Man is the McGyver of the OSW. He’s able to take a simple weapon and turn it into something lethal in a matter of seconds. Not only that, but his ways of implementing plans are always off the wall and unpredictable, but they make perfect sense to him. Stamina: Trashy has been burned, beaten, bludgeoned, and lord knows what else has happened to him, and he’s still fighting as if he just started wrestling. It only takes one member in a stable with this quality to rally the others to his cause and keep them fighting. No other fighter in any stable in this war, not even Ivan Stanislav, can withstand the brutal punishment that Trashcan Man has sustained. Weaknesses How Many Screws Are Loose: Just how mentally stable is Trashcan Man? He’s been through an awful lot, and that is an understatement. Could the responsibility of being President, running The Asylum, and coping with his own inner demons push him to the breaking point. Unnatural Leader: While it seems to be working thus far, Trashcan Man has usually been a lackey to someone else. How capable of a leader is he? When the going gets tough, can he coordinate a group of people effectively? Timo Bolamba Info: Height: 6’2” Weight: 245 lbs. Style: High Flyer, High Impact Title History: None Awards: Ranked 37th in Month of February W/O Poll, PCW TV Title, PCW Rising Star Champion, PCW Most stock improved for October and November 2001. Voted Funniest man in PCW. Winner of Alliances 2 with Team Asylum. FSW World Champion. Hailing from Samoa, Timo Bolamba has fought in many venues and in many situations. Operating just as effectively in singles or group contests, no one can deny that Timo Bolamba will continue to fight until the bell rings. Timo is a strange mix of intensity and quirkiness. One minute he’s joking and the next he’s pummeling you into the mat. His fiery attitude keeps him going, and it puts fear in the hearts of his adversaries. Timo Bolamba is the drive of Asylum. Style Where the sloppiness and awkwardness of Trashcan Man ends, Timo Bolamba begins. A graceful and artful wrestler inside the ring, watching Timo Bolamba fight is like watching a painter paint a picture. Unfortunately, the red splotches are left from the enemy. Timo Bolamba never backs down from a challenge and refuses to be intimidated. His quick thinking and compact, yet powerful body enables him to strike hard, quick, and with as little hesitation possible. Strengths Rally the Troops: It doesn’t take long for Timo Bolamba to come tearing down the entryway to protect a friend, and it’s that quick thinking that others gravitate towards. In a heartbeat, Timo can rally the troops and they can follow his lead. Unpredictable: If Timo is standing in the middle of the ring, he can go anywhere from there. Straight ahead to attack, to the corner for a highflying maneuver, to the ropes for a rushing move, the possibilities are endless. How do you prepare for such a wide repertoire of moves? Dedicated: Timo trusts himself, he always has, and he is dedicated to whatever cause he is fighting for. To the last man, Timo will stand beside and fight, never running, always trying to gain the upper hand, and never letting his morale break. Weaknesses Headstrong: Charging into battle is sometimes not the best course of action. Timo Bolamba is confident in himself and his abilities, but that doesn’t mean he is always right. Timo will willingly wade into a mass of enemies and fight to the end, but it may be his end. Reckless: Stemming from being headstrong, the style that Timo Bolamba prefers is both high flying and high impact. While the damage they can inflict is great, if an error is made, horrible things can happen. In a war of stables, how effective, or how much will the opportunity present itself to do a highflying move? Azala Zameer Info: Height: 6’10” Weight: 462 lbs. Style: Powerhouse Title History: None Awards: None The African Nightmare, Azala Zameer, was at one time hated in the FSW and wished to destroy America as a whole. But now, the massive African has changed his tone and has lightened his mood, enjoying American life and his newfound Asylum friends. With a heart of gold and teeth to match, Azala Zameer is a devout member of Asylum. Azala Zameer is the ferocity of Asylum. Style Azala Zameer is the powerhouse of Asylum. Weighing in as the largest member in this stable war to date and the heaviest member of the OSW roster, Azala Zameer is an imposing foe to say the least. With power and tenacity that is capable of nullifying two or three other average wrestlers, Azala Zameer is a force to be reckoned with. A master of inertia, when Azala starts to move, virtually nothing short of a tractor trailer can stop him. Strengths Presence: Azala Zameer is simply huge. The mere sight of such a large man barreling down upon an opponent is enough to send someone running away. His piercing blue eyes, golden teeth, and tribal tattoo’s are all part of this strength. Mass: Zameer is the largest member of the OSW roster and very hard to take down. The amount of time and energy needed to topple Azala Zameer could mean the loss of one or two stable members, particularly in Power Play. Agile: Where do stereotypes come from? Well, perhaps Azala could tell us. For a man of his size, Zameer is known to randomly kick high into the air or chop an opponent into the mat. He’s unpredictable and young to boot. Weaknesses Naïve: Azala is an outsider to America and is still learning the ropes of American life. He’s prone to accept the word of others and believe it as true which could make him susceptible to manipulation, or towards getting caught in a trap. How Big is Too Big: Zameer is huge. But that also makes him a huge target. Conventional means of taking him down could be costly, which forces his enemies to cheat to win which could incur a larger chance of injury to Azala, or his allies. Six Finger Cisco Info: Height: 5’11” Weight: 230 lbs. Style: Technical with Hardcore and Speed Title History: None Awards: PWA Graduate, EW Cruiserweight Champion The Freak, as many call him, Six Finger Cisco has used all six fingers to claw his way into the OSE. Starting from humble roots in the PWA, he’s learned the ropes and the tricks of the trade to get to where he has gotten. Though a rocky start befell him in the OSW, with the help of Asylum, Cisco has found his mark. Six Finger Cisco is the spirit of Asylum. Style Adapting to utilize his sixth finger, Cisco literally has an extra weapon at hand, pardon the pun, that gives him an edge against his opponents. In the wild, unexpected battles that are to come, a sixth finger can certainly come in handy. Cisco’s mastery of submission holds rounds out the Asylum, giving them a taste of every style of wrestling. Strengths Freak: No offense to Cisco, but he is a freak. And a freaky one at that! How do you handle a man with six fingers? Submission moves like the mandible claw and holds, as well as eye rakes are instantly made more powerful due to this finger. Cisco’s grip has also got to be quite strong. Young Gun: Cisco hasn’t has the fame of other Asylum members which gives him the chance to truly shine in this war and make a name for himself. After being shunned for so long, the people now love him. This love for Cisco will give him the drive needed to make Asylum successful. Keeps Comin’ Back for More: Cisco has a rough time starting out in the OSW. He had one win and several losses. But you didn’t see him quit. You didn’t see him give up. He continued to bounce back and take the fight to his enemies. That trend won’t change. People may laugh at him and play him off as nothing major, but that tenacity will come in handy, mark my words. Weaknesses Lightweight: Cisco is one of the smallest members in this war and is the smallest member of Asylum. How will he be able to size up against larger, more powerful opponents? Particularly The Red Army, whose average weight is around 320 lbs, can he measure up to those odds? Blown Off: As has been the trend throughout his life, many people blow off Cisco for nothing more than a physical deviant. While this could play into Cisco’s favor, it can also hurt him as he is overlooked in the great scheme of this stable war. Closing Remarks There you have it folks, a breakdown of The Asylum members. They are diverse, they are tenacious, and they have the OSW behind them! Tune in next time as I analyze Power Play!! Nate Dumas
