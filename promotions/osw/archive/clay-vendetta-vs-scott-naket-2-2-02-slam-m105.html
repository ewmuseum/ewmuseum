Clay Vendetta vs. Scott Naket (2/2/02 - Slam!)

2002-02-02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Clay Vendetta vs. Scott Naket (2/2/02 - Slam!) Written: 2/2/02 Clay Vendetta vs. Scott Naket Match Result: Loss Via: Pinfall (schoolboy) Stipulations: None Notes: Vendetta's first match in OSW. Nate: Up next fans we have the self proclaimed Canadian Icon Scott Naket taking on the newcomer, Classic Clay Vendetta. In the ring Ring Announcer Earl Henrich has the mic in hand. Earl – The following is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, weighing in at 240 pounds from Vancouver, British Columbia….Scott Naket. “SuperXXX” by Overseer hits the PA system as Scott Naket comes out from the back to a huge pop. Nate: Scott Naket is trying to resurrect a once promising career here is PCW. Doug: Every fed needs a guy willing to work hard and do his JOB. Nate: (mumbling) Ixnay on the reakingbay ayfabekay. Doug: What!?! I am only saying it is good to know a guy like Naket is available to come in to do a good JOB. Nate sighs. Earl - …And his opponent, weighing in at 225 pounds, from Miami, Florida…Clay Vendetta. “Carry On My Wayward Son” by Kansas rocks the arena PA system as Clay Vendetta comes out to a chorus of boos from the crowd. Doug – BWAHAHAHAHA Steven – (shudders) Ewww, an 80’s flashback. Clay slides into the ring and the bell sounds. Both men step up to each other and stare each other down. A cocky grin slowly starts to appear on Naket’s face. Nate – Naket looking like he’s not impressed with his opponent. Steven – Or maybe he wants to take him out to dinner afterwards Clay, now observing the smirk on Naket’s face takes his right pointer finger and puts it up to his nose and blows, clearing it on Naket’s chest. Doug – Now that’s not right Steven – Hey he had to clear his nose, you can’t expect him not to breathe. Naket looks down on his chest with disgust as he starts to wipe himself clean, Clay seeing that Naket is preoccupied throws a right cross, connecting on the side of Naket’s face, staggering him. Clay follows Naket and delivers a wicked looking European uppercut, again staggering him but Naket refuses to go down. Nate – Naket showing some resilience here. Clay moves behind Naket, locks him in a reverse headlock, Naket trying to claw at Clay’s arms, but it is to no avail as Clay hits a death drop. Naket turns to his side holding his neck. Doug – Yup, that’ll do it. Nate – The youngster concentrating on Scott’s neck. Steven – Where’s that cocky grin now Naket? Clay slowly picks Naket by the hair, lifts him up and hits an atomic drop. Crowd – OOOOOOH Doug – Talk about your trolley horses. Clay grabs Naket by the arm and attempts to Irish whip him into the turnbuckle but Naket reverses and whips Clay into it, then he immediately follows it up with a running dropkick to his chest. Clay keels over clutching his chest and falls to the mat. Nate – Effective counter by Naket has Clay on the rebound. Doug – Stomp a mudhole in him. Naket walks over to the fallen Vendetta, grabs hold of the ropes and proceeds to drop a series of boots down on Clay. After about four boot shots, Scott lets go of the rope and walks around the ring, playing to the cheers of the crowd. Steven – Yessiree, Clay is just setting him up. Nate – Scott Naket with a touch of showboating here. Doug – Naket is trying to make the point that experience at this level is a factor. Naket walks over to Clay and slowly helps him to his feet. He looks him straight in the eye, smiles, and hits a belly-to-belly suplex, throwing Clay halfway across the ring. Naket quickly runs over and delivers an elbow drop across Clay’s chest. He grabs a leg and covers…1…2…kick out by Vendetta. Nate – Naket going for the impressive win but Clay with the kick out. Doug – New federation, fresh start, definitely trying to set the tone. Steven – Clay has got him right where he wants him. Nate and Doug look over at Steven, who ignores them. Naket gets up and picks up Clay and whips him into the robes followed by a reverse elbow, sending Clay down to the mat again. Naket walks over to Clay and starts to taunt him on the ground. Nate – Now you see, instead of finishing someone off he’s gotta go and talk, add further insult to injury. Doug – A foolish mistake made by all, when you think your in control, watch out it may bite you in the ass. Naket continues to taunt Clay when all of a sudden Clay pokes Naket in the eye, causing him to step back holding his eyes. Clay stands up and grabs a hold of Naket and whips him into the ropes and executes a powerslam. Clay picks Naket off the ground and rakes his eyes. Steven – There you go Doug – Oh blatant cheating…wheres the ref calling the dq Nate – The ref is apparently letting them go on. Clay circles around Naket and hits a belly to back suplex followed by another. Nate – The youngster showing some strength Vendetta stands up and drops a quick elbow. He gets back up and runs across the ring, bounces off and hits Naket square in the face with a baseball slide. He covers… 1… 2… Naket raises his shoulder breaking the pin attempt. Doug: So close!!! Clay pounds the mat in frustration and rises to pick Naket up. He lifts him up by the trunks and before Naket is fully standing, he drops a forearm shot across his back. Picks him up again, and delivers another forearm shot. Steven – That’s what I’m talking about, kick the man when he’s down. Clay grabs Naket before he fully goes down and locks him into a dragon sleeper. Nate – This could be it! Clay strengthens the hold as Naket is trying desperately to reach for the ropes. The crowd rises to cheer Naket on. The ref moves over into position to see if Naket reaches the ropes. Clay applies the move harder as he is now fighting Naket’s attempt at breaking free. Naket drops his arms in exhaustion when he decides to try one more attempt and lunges one final time, reaching the ropes and causing the ref to make Vendetta break the hold. Steven – He tapped out…he tapped out! Doug – Noooo, Naket got to the rope in a nick of time. Infuriated, Clay grabs Naket and Irish whips him into the ropes where he hits him with a running spear, nearly breaking Naket in half. He picks him quickly up and lifts him in the air for a spinebuster. Clay then drops down to the mat as well and locks Naket in another Dragon Sleeper but tries to get his leg over and lock in a leg grapevine but Naket counters and rolls Clay up in a small package… 1… 2… 3! The crowd roars to a huge pop. Steven – NOOO! Doug – YESSS! Nate – Clay Vendetta just got countered out of his finisher! THE WINNER OF THE MATCH….SCOTT NAKET Nate – SCOTT NAKET STEALS A WIN FROM THE JAWS OF DEFEAT!!! Doug – Vendetta is NOT a happy camper! Steven – BOOO…BOOO…BOOO… Clay stands up, furious that he lost on a fluke, he begins to throw a temper tantrum in the corner of the ring. He wipes his face in anger a few times and he begins to stare down Naket, who is celebrating to the crowd. Clay then rushes at Naket and hits a bulldog on Naket. He picks him right up and hits a 180 tombstone piledriver. He bends over and starts yelling at Naket. Nate –THIS IS UNCALLED FOR!!! Doug – Who cares!?!? It’s Naket taking a beating! Steven – Yeah, beat him senseless Clay continues to taunt Natet and kicks him in the gut, and again. The referee goes over to try and separate him from Naket but he is met with a punch, knocking him out of the ring. Clay continues to pummel Naket when the crowd suddenly pops. Doug: LOOK!!! The cameras shift to the aisle, to reveal none other than Five Star Wrestling Hall of Famer Mike Marvelous sprinting towards the ring. Nate – IT’S MIKE MARVELOUS…WHAT’S HE DOING HERE IN OSW? Doug – Naket’s own countryman here to save the day!!! Steven – CLAY..BEHIND YOU! Marvelous runs down to ringside and grabs a steel chair as he slides into the ring. Vendetta turns around to see what the commotion is and Marvelous hits him with the chair, wrapping half the steel chair around Clay’s face. The Crowd: Oooooooo!!! Nate: Good GAWWWWD!! Vendetta goes down and goes down hard. The crowd pops even harder. Nate – Clay Vendetta has just been leveled by a steel chair wielding Mike Marvelous!!! Marvelous drops the chair down and yells at Clay while he’s pointing at him. He turns and extends his hand out to Naket to help him up. Nate: MIKE MARVELOUS JUST SAVED HIS FELLOW CANADIAN, SCOTT NAKET, FROM TAKING A BEATING!!! The crowd is in a frenzy cheering Naket and Marvelous. Meanwhile back in the ring, Marvelous begins to help Naket up, only to kick him in the gut, causing him to double over. Nate: WHAT THE HELL!?!? Doug: SWERVE!!! Marvelous immediately follows up by DDT Naket face first onto the steel chair Marvelous used to bash Clay Vendetta. Doug: SWERVE!!! Nate: MIKE MARVELOUS WASN’T OUT HERE TO SAVE SCOTT NAKET AT ALL!!! HE WAS JUST OUT HERE TO CAUSE HAVOK!!! Doug: SWERVE!!! SWERVE!!! Nate: We need to head to a commercial. When we return, we will head to the ring for our main event of the evening. Marvelous jumps up and starts yelling at Naket and Vendetta to a thunderous chorus of boos. He then slides out of the ring and starts to walk up the ramp with a huge smile….as the scene fades to commercial.
