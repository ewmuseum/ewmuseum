How Did It Come To This?

13 September 2003

by 'Sell Out' Dave Gibson

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#170) by 'Sell Out' Dave Gibson, dated 13 September 2003, written from A hotel room somewhere in Miami, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
'You know, you don't have to take that next drink.' But it damn sure helps. *** I glance up, and through my half-closed eyes I see Silvio Rochelli amble across the screen, in mid-beatdown of Jason O'Neil at By Any Means Necessary. I think he's going for a body slam. Christ, I can't tell. This is what passes for 'scouting' anymore. Where I used to watch intently, taking detailed notes on my opponent's strengths and weaknesses, I now sit in front of one my flatscreen TVs--depending on where I'm at--and get shitfaced. I leaned forward in my chair, reaching for the half-empty (yeah, I'm a pessimist, all right) bottle of Bushmill's on the table beside me. My fingers grope clumsily before my hand grabs the bottle and raises it, quivering slightly. No glass for me. I'm way beyond glasses. I'm way beyond a lot of things. I bring the bottle to my lips and tilt it back, feel the acrid liquid pour down my throat and the sweet fiery aftereffect as it pours down my gullet. I swallow twice, and gasp for air, shaking my head back and forth quickly before I set the bottle down again, making sure that it rests safely on the table. It's important to be careful about things like that. Screw the job. Screw mom, screw friends, screw Power Play even. Screw the house, the penthouse apartment in Chicago, the cars, the three-piece suits, and the endorsement contracts. Well, maybe not the contracts. But protect the BOOZE. At all costs. See, what they didn't understand--what they never understand--is that all the things Matt Shivan said? All the stuff about McLeary's? It was all true. But Shivan's too inept to realize what he discovered. He didn't realize that was only the tip of the iceberg. He didn't realize that I have outstanding tabs at more than 15 bars and taverns in Chicago--always circulating, never going to the same one twice in a row, to make sure no one draws a real pattern. Unless there's some bartender convention I don't know about, I know I'm safe. When I'm at a bar, I'm not there to have fun. I keep to myself. If there's a private room, I'm in it. Not to have some 'precious moment' with the latest groupie. Not to do drugs. But to drink. Heavily. Alone. Not because I never knew my dad. Not because it runs in my family--Mom never touches the stuff. I drink to forget. Oh sure, I know what it's cost me. I know why I haven't won a single match legitimately since Ascension. I've seen my physique suffer from the bloat. I've seen my reflexes dull, and my strength diminish from poor nutrition (alcoholics aren't very big eaters, after all. At least I'm not. Food makes me sick. How's THAT for irony?) And now look where I am. I went from the main event at Ascension to getting overlooked come time for By Any Means Necessary. So I go out and find my OWN challenger--and promptly get my ass kicked. Now I'm at the bottom of the card. Back where I started. Back when I was having to play trick-or-treat with no-talent hacks like Vampir Nosferatu and Armour, jobbing to gigantic sides of beef with pitiful technique and worse charisma. Then there's Power Play--or what's left of it. Maybe Dave's right. Hell, I KNOW he's right. Pursuing Register has strained my relationship with the rest of the guys. Now, apparently the only way I can keep Hurst around is to induct that Johnny Rotten wannabe. Things can't keep going this way. I'm THIS CLOSE to becoming OSW's next version of Kory Storm. In my current state, I need reliable people to watch my back more than ever. People like Matt Register. People like the Solution. And as much as it hurts to admit it, people like EDH. It's time to make some changes in Power Play. Drastic changes. But right now... It's time to get drunk. Because right now... that's all I have.
