CORG Real Name: Isiic Raiyn Alias: Corg Nickname: The Quiet Killer Birth Date: January 28, 1984 Height: 6’0 Weight: 178 lbs Hometown: Detroit, Michigan Theme Music: Chemical by No One Wrestling Style: Corg has the style of Ku-Moei, which he was taught in Realm 2. Ku-Moei, consists of a branch of odd fighting skills. Brutal submissions, fatal arial stunts, and psychotic kicks. Ku-Moei is only used in a certain stance, the stance of being on one foot, with your arms seizing the area of your face. It creates a sense of inner mentality, making it easier to focus on the opponent. Gimmick: Behind Corg’s laid back attitude, and gothic dress style, is a brutally black heart infested with nothing. Corg, an anti-God of sort, has no goals in life. He has no aspirations, nothing. Depression and murders, thats it. Corg just likes to kill people, in an unpleasant way. He tortures them, and eventually kills them. He claims that it helps calm his depression, but that is heard to be untrue. He always kills them, for no reason. He is a very quiet, mystical killer, that has no purpose in life. Appearance: Wears white contacts, with black droops around his eyes, along with a pierced left eyebrow. Multiple piercings on his ears, nipples, and chin. Dyed red, short hair, spiked up into random points around his head. A black, studded collar around his neck. He has his lips painted black, along with his finger nails. Skinny build; about average size for his height, very white skin. He wears a black see-thru gothic type long-sleeve shirt, and under that he has see-thru arm pads extending from the top of his elbow to the palm of his hand. He wears studded leather cuffs on his wrists. His tattoos consist of on the right arm, a opened wound. On his back he has a long serpant extending from the right nipple on his chest to the center of his back. A black star covering both of his nipples, and the word kill written on the lower part of his back. He has very baggy black leather pants, with red and black shoes. Ring Enterance: Chemical by No One blasts among the PA, as the lights turn to a dim red, as the sleeping crowd becomes alert, and feels the near presence of the Quiet Killer. The lights flash rapidly, and smoke fills the enterance way, as Corg comes out to a crowd booing him like he's some mastermind killer. Well he is. Corg spreads his arms in a crucifix position, shifting his head downward. He proceeds to walk down to the ring. An endless amount of people are yelling at him in despise. Corg justs pays no attention to them and gets on the apron, makes his way up to the ring. Common Moves: Back leg sweep - side crescent kick - spring board dropkick - arm drag while spin kicking opponent in the air - 360 degree leg drop - swanton bomb - split leg moonsault - super kick - running side kick - spring board senton - hurricanranna, spring board moonsault - top rope flipping leg drop Signature Moves: Remains of Nothing: a fatal Tazzmission a Falling Black Sky: super frog splash Blood from the Heart: opponent is in the tree of woe position and Corg runs from the other end and dropkicks opponent in their face Spreading the Disease: back-ended Dragon sleeper Finisher: The Unheard Noise: inverted DDT Biography: Born as Isiic Raiyn on a cold morning of January 28, 1984. He was an early baby, by 4 months. AKA Corg, caught his first glimpse of the world. The day will never be forgotten. Isiic was born in Detroit, Michigan, where he was loved and cared for by his parents. Throughout Isiic’s early days, he was always a calm and quiet child. Never crying at late night, or getting into things. This made it extremely easy for his parents to raise him. Isiic learned his first word at 2 months old, when he said kill. It was a disturbing thought for his parents to how he learned that word. But none the less, he began talking full sentences at 7 months. Very early. By the age of 2, Isiic was already into the sport of wrestling. Somehow, he started to watch it on TV. His parents couldn’t bare to drag him away from it because Isiic loved to watch it so much. He was hooked. Even as an infant. When he turned 4, he started watching wrestling, listening the wrestlers skin make a smacking sound when they got hit. His parents had bought him the most wrestling toys that they could find [they were crap of course since it was in the dreadful 80s]. Isiic’s life seemed to be going great. He had a loving family, and he had wrestling. He was 7 when he finally got to watch his first ever WWF Pay Per View. WrestleMania 6. Overrated by commercials and advertisements everywhere. Isiic was so excited when his parents let him watch it. Oh he couldn’t wait. But when 7pm struck, he was ready. WrestleMania was underway. His parents went out to get some pizza. It had been hours since they left, but Isiic didn’t seem to care. He just watched and watched the TV, but when it was over, Isiic for the first time realized that he was alone. There was no empty pizza box. Nothing. Midnight came, and Isiic fell asleep. But he was quickly awoken by a knock on the door. 3 thuds as Isiic heard a voice “It’s the Police, open up!”. Isiic got scared, and opened the door. The police explaned to Isiic what had happened. His parents were killed, they didn’t know how or why, the car was undamaged, but his parents were in the car with blood streaming out of their legs. Isiic didn’t really understand, but he also didn’t really care. He wasn’t shocked, sad or anything, he had no emotions. Since Isiic had no family besides his parents, was sent to a foster home, where he would stay for 5 years. When he was 12, he started 6th grade at an elementary school near by. On November 3, 1989, Isiic’s life took an unsuspecting turn. One day at school, as usual Isiic was doing his schoolwork when suddenly his teacher Ms. Blackberry started yelling at him for no reason. She was yelling and yelling, it hurt to hear her voice. When it seemed like she was done, Ms. Blackberry took Isiic’s head and slammed it into the chalk board. Ouch, what a bicth Isiic thought. He passed out for a total of 20 seconds. When he woke up, Ms. Blackberry was teaching class once again. All of the kids were looking at her in fear, wondering why she did what she did. Isiic got up, and saw a wooden apple on Blackberry’s desk. He looks at the apple, and for the first time in his life, realized what happened. His parents were dead. He felt a sudden flame, quiet in his stumache, where he saw his parents, and saw the blood coming out of their bodies. Isiic’s eyes lit up, and tears began strolling down his face. He knew what he had to do. So Isiic got up, took the wooden apple, and drove it into the back of Ms. Blackberry’s head. She fell to the floor in pain as the whole classroom gulped. Isiic got on top of Blackberry, and began beating her with the apple. Blood stained the apple, and Ms. Blackberry had blood rushing out of her head. Some fellow teachers heard the screams, and came running in to see Isiic Raiyn sitting ontop of Sarah Blackberry, with a wooden apple in his hand, and blood pouring out of Blackberry’s head. The police were called, and came to the scene in about 1 minute. They asked the kids what had happened. They later found the cause of Blackberry’s actions, narcotics; cocaine was found in her closet, and she was later tested and turned out to be stoned the whole time. But that didn’t matter, she was vitcim number 3 in Isiic’s life. One in which he didn’t care for. About 4 months later, Isiic’s foster home was sued for lack on positive influence on a child. So Isiic had nowhere to go after this. Detroit wasn’t the best place to be in a crisis like this, and knowing that Isiic was only 12, there wasn’t much hope of surviving. So Isiic knew that the only way that he could get anywhere in life, was through an education. He managed to go to school, get solid As and Bs, and all in the while of living in the abandoned office of a factory. By age 15, Isiic was very smart, very skinny, and very eager to get on with his life. He found a job working as a part-time janitor at a kick boxing school in Southfiled, Michigan. 6 months later, he had accumulated enough money to train at the school for at least 3 years. And so, he paid them the money, and was quickly oriented with the school’s fundementals of fighting, as well as some other disturbing things. After about a month of training, Isiic’s trainer [also known as master] told him that he was of a different calibur than the rest of the fighters. His master immidiately transfered Isiic to a different class, a class that was located in the basement of the training complex. Isiic was to train there for the rest of his training course, or until they thought that he was ready. Ready for the real world, and ready to fight. There were no lights in the basement, only tourches. The tourches, carried around by small guys in hooded robes. Multiple pentagrams surrounded the basement, and the floor was rock hard. Very few boxing rings were around, and very few students were around. Everyone there were at high rankings in the style of Ku-Moei, the fighting style that was taught in Realm 2. He continues to train in Realm 2, improving his dark skills. He has a mysterious sense, and is currently mastering the techniques that are taught in Realm 2, which are unknown by the outside world. “Without question, all hail the one named Corg.” Corg, the anti-God, the Quiet Killer. He was trained to become a fighter, a fighter that makes the human race worse than it already is, for no reason. But, with this senseless life, brings depression to Corg, far too often. And with the depression, comes the killing. He was taught to fight when needed, and to kill whenever. Any that oppose Corg, would be killed. Handler’s Name: Isaac A.
