MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: Email me for it Theme Song: 'Brainless' by The Urge Date of Birth: 8/11/1975 From: Omaha, Nebraska Height: 6'-1" Weight: 230 Wrestling Style: Technical, Speedy Signature Moves: INSERT SIGNATURE MOVES HERE Finisher Move: The Take (Modified Electric Chair Drop (Picks up opponent from behind, lifts opponent into the air, jumps up after them while pushing the opponent's torso forward, then finishes the move in the sitout position while dropping the opponent on their face) Set-Up Move: Usually a reversal of his opponent's move Current Team: None Past Teams & Groups: DAMN w/ Frisco, Guy Leslie & Kid Wonder Title History: PCW World Champion 3-time PCW Hardcore Champion Awards History: Description: Usually clad in jeans and a T-shirt shilling whatever product he happens to be endorsing at that time. History: Douglas Troy, the son of well-known international freelance “problem solvers” Castor and Sasha, was born in an Afghani prison on August 11, 1975. After a week or so in the prison, he and his mother were removed from the prison and transported to Omaha, Nebraska, the place where young Doug would spend most of his young life. Doug showed a fascination with wrestling at an early age while watching such legends as Harley Race and Bob Backlund work their magic in the squared circle. In the Mid ‘80s, Troy watched as Hulkamania ran wild while he parents did “business” in Latin America and the Middle East. His scholarly career went normally enough for him. He graduated in the Top 50 of his class (despite a small debacle in Chemistry class that will not, for legal reasons, be elaborated upon) and traveled to Missouri to attend Truman State University. At times during his college tenure, he attended a small Missouri wrestling school. In 1997, he received his Bachelor of Arts degree in the field of Film. After graduating, he briefly pondered a job in the movie industry, but instead got a letter from a small martial arts and wrestling dojo in Japan. Excited at the opportunity to train abroad, he jumped at the chance. Doug picked things up very quickly at the Tendo-run ‘Anything Goes’ School of Martial Arts and Wrestling, and experienced his first real wrestling match in 1998. His first opponent: Toshiaki Kawada. Needless to say, Troy didn’t fare too well in his matchup. The next couple months were spent in a hospital for young Troy, his neck in a brace after a brutal Kawada powerbomb. When he came back, he quickly decided to AVOID Kawada and instead logged some weeks in the NJPW and some smaller Deathmatch federations. It was there that he developed his finisher, which was found to be extremely effective in hardcore matches. Many an opponent left matches with Troy with their faces and torsos scarred. Even during his Japan days, though, Doug quickly learned the art of playing to the crowd. In late 1999, Doug Troy came back to America to ply his learned skills in a place where hyped-up Japanese announcers didn’t scream “LARIATOOOOOOOOO!” He quickly found a spot in the ACW, a small indy fed known for its quirkyness and brutal hardcore matches. It was there that he joined the stable known as DAMN, a decision that would factor very heavily into his later career. After the ACW folded in early 2000, Troy bounced from federation to federation, each one folding soon after he got there. Convinced that he was an inadvertent death sentence upon any established fed that he joined, he joined the fledgling Primetime Championship Wrestling after being approached by Professor Herbert Dorkiman. From there, it can be said, the rest is history.
