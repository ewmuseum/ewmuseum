Venting Rage

2002-06-29
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Venting Rage Written: 6-29-02 Nate: Welcome back to Slam!!! We have just been informed that our own Matt Shivan is standing by backstage with the Elite!!! Robbie: Finally, some real entertainment!!! Nate: Let’s send it to Matt in the back!!! Cutting to the backstage area, we are greeted with the sight of none other than three fourths of OSW’s Elite. Sebastian Toomes, Seth Masters, and Trevor Hawke do not look too happy at all as they make their way purposefully down the hallway. Well, at least until they are stopped by the presence of OSW’s resident roving reporter, the somewhat geeky Matt Shivan. As the three men glare at this annoyance in their path, a shudder rips through Shivan’s body before he manages to lift his microphone to his lips. Matt: Uh...hey guys...er...do you mind if I have a few words with you? Trevor: Piss off, Shivan. Hawke reaches out to move the reporter aside, grabbing him by the collar and shoving him hard into the wall. Shivan falls on the ground and immediately curls up in fetal position, shivering in fear. Matt: Please, don’t hurt me! Masters looks as if he’s about to drive his boot into Shivan’s mid-section, but Toomes stops him by lifting a hand. Civil Disobedience both turn to look at their stable-mate with confused gazes, to which Switchblade responds with a shrug. Bending over, he lifts Shivan back to his feet with one arm. Toomes: I apologize for the behavior of my two associates. As you can tell, we’re all not in the best of moods today. Matt: Apparently so... Toomes: But don’t think I’ve gone soft by saving your ass a beating. There’s just some shit we need to get off our chest and...well, you’ve got the microphone. Say, can I see that for a second. Shivan looks a bit skeptical, but there’s not much else he can do but hand the microphone over to Toomes. Matt: Uh...sure, I don’t see why not. Switchblade reaches out and takes the mic from the cowering reporter. Toomes: Sweet. He then nods to Seth and Trevor. Toomes: Ok, you two can proceed with the venting of the rage and whatnot. The two members of Civil Disobedience pounce on Shivan, knocking him to the ground with a few well placed blows before stomping the bejesus out of him. Sebastian, on the other hand, turns his attention to Matt’s cameraman, where he begins to address the viewing audience with a somewhat hostile tone. Toomes: It’s pretty damn obvious that Seth and Trevor back there are pissed as hell, and after the shit that went down at Ascension, can you really blame them? Shivan begins crawling away from the two members of Civil Disobedience, getting far enough away to voice a weak protest. Matt: (groaning) Yes... As Shivan crawls his way to Switchblade’s feet, Toomes delivers a kick to his stomach. Toomes: Shut up, pissant. *Ahem* Now where was I? Ah yes, that atrocity of a pay per view. Anyone who tuned in last Sunday already knows that it was possibly the worst night in the history of professional wrestling. I’m not just saying this because I got screwed out of my title shot by some dirty Mexican. Walking up next to Toomes, Trevor Hawke pulls the microphone away for a second to add to the tirade. Trevor: And he’s not just saying that because those Westside fucks took almost twenty minutes to beat me in a two-on-one situation in the tag title match. Sebastian raises an eyebrow in question. Toomes: It took them that long? Hawke shrugs. Trevor: Yeah, they’re not as good as many would like to believe. Toomes: I guess not. Hawke nods, then goes back to join Seth in stomping away on Shivan, who moans in pain like a little sissy. Switchblade returns his attention back to the cameraman once more. Toomes: No, the main reason OSW Ascension will be a night that will live in infamy is because of the actions of one man...one sick, perverted little man...a man that, as of right now, is not long for this world...a man that will soon be crippled by the Elite...a man that goes by the name of...Corg. The crowd erupts in a chorus of boos at the mention of the man, so loudly that it is audible even in the backstage area. Toomes: Yes, I know, I know. You all hate him, AS YOU SHOULD! The sick little bastard tried to rape a woman...and not just any woman, mind you, but our dear friend Amy Campbell...he tried to rape Amy in front of a worldwide audience. And because of that, the perverted fuck has sealed his fate. Me, Seth, Trevor...any one of us, or all of us, it doesn’t matter. One way or another, Corg is as good as dead! Having finished their beating on Matt Shivan, Seth and Trevor are now standing behind Toomes in a display of solidarity. Masters pulls the mic over to himself Seth: Why the man is still in OSW is beyond my understanding. As far as I know, Jane Reagan is working as fast as she can to terminate the man’s working relationship with the company, but with all of these fucking hoops she has to jump through to avoid contract breaches, there’s no way she can get him out of here fast enough. I would assume that the fact that a man would be so demented as to try to pull shit like that at all, let alone in public, is enough to justify his expulsion, but I guess I’m wrong. Hawke grabs the mic from Masters hand to insert his two cents into the situation. Trevor: Which is why we’re going to have to take justice into our own hands. If the system doesn’t allow that fuck-head to get what’s coming to him, we sure as hell will give it to him. That’s the number one item on our agenda. There’s plenty of other dicksmack’s that have to be dealt with...Da Westsidaz, Hessian, Vampir, Jesus St. Crux, and even Rhett Locke...but first things first, we’re going to end Corg’s career. With a nod, Switchblade pulls the microphone back to himself once more. Toomes: Nobody fucks with the Elite and gets away with it! Corg does not have long. It’s just a matter of time. Days, hours, maybe mere minutes before we fuck him up! And fuck him up we shall, by giving him a lesson, he will never, ever forget! The crowd launches into a huge ovation of cheers, which is odd considering Switchblade is the one with the mic in his hand. The reason for their cheers soon becomes apparent, however. It seems that while Toomes was speaking, the Unwanted Angel himself, Reno Davis, has appeared on the scene and is directing a stare of disbelief at the three men in front of the camera. Noticing the newcomer on the scene, Seth shoots him a questioning look. Seth: What the fuck are you looking at? Davis wiggles his fingers in the air in a mocking gesture. Reno: Ooo, beating up Shivan. Yeah, you guys are bad asses. He chuckles mirthlessly, drawing irate glares from the other three men. Reno: You guys are going to fuck up Corg? Phhh. Haven’t you guys fucked up enough lately? I mean, you abandoned Amy in her time of need. The crowd lets out a loud “ooo” upon hearing this, remembering how Civil Disobedience left Aimz to the proverbial wolves last Sunday. Not liking this reminder, Trevor’s face turns a bright red and he launches himself at Davis, trying to take his head off with a right hook that Reno easily dodges. Toomes and Masters pull him back, though, before any real brawl can take form. Hawke shouts at Reno, sounding full of indignation as he points angrily at him. Trevor: You better shut your damn mouth! You have no right to pass judgement on us! You don’t know what we were and were not able to do, you instigating shit! Davis laughs again, blowing Trevor off. Reno: Yeah, whatever. Maybe you boys should go back to your locker room. If anyone’s going to mess that punk-ass goth up, it’s me. Just make sure you stay out of my way tonight. And with that, Davis walks off down the hallway, leaving an angry Trevor still muttering profanities as his stable-mates try to calm him down. The three men glare after him, looking even less happy then they were at the beginning of the segment. Toomes: Hey, don’t pay any attention to that prick. What does he know? Come on, we’ve got shit to do. Let’s go. Nodding their heads reluctantly, Civil Disobedience follows Toomes off screen, leaving us with a shot of the demolished Matt Shivan still groaning on the floor….as the scene fades to commercial.
