Monster Zero Written: June 22, 2002 Toomes sneers and picks up Bones by the head again, however Bones is far too weak to make a comeback. Toomes begins his Jump Swinging Brainbuster DDT… Robbie: FUTURE SHOCK! And Toomes makes the cover! 1… 2… 3! Winner: Sebastian Toomes DING! DING! DING! Nate: TOOMES WILL START ON THE SECOND TIER!!! Toomes gets up and holds his arms up in victory, the fallen Bones lying on the mat, clutching his head (A chairshot, a Leg Drop, and a Future Shock DDT will tend to do things that make you clutch your head in pain). Jane Reagan and Aimz slide in the ring to congratulate him when… Nate: LOOK! Robbie: WHAT’S THAT PASTY FUCK DOING OUT HERE? Indeed, the Pasty Fuck himself, Corg, stands on top of the ramp, holding an official OSW Aimz Blowup doll. He smiles evilly at Aimz, who gets on the ropes and motions him to come down to the ring. Corg smiles and produces a Zippo lighter… Nate: HAH! Robbie: What the hell does a blow-up doll and a Zippo have to do with Toomes’ victory? Steven: Uh, I’m gonna go out on a limb here and say that Aimz is just filled with… HOT air? Robbie: *groan* Steven: Oh, FUCK YOU! THAT WAS THE BEST GODDAMN PUN YOU’VE EVER HEARD! Corg holds the blow-up doll and puts in on the top of the stage. He gets down to his knees, flicking the lighter on and spreads the blow-up doll’s legs. Holding the lighter near his crotch, he simulates a thrust and the lighter’s flame hits the blow-up doll’s crotch, and the blow-up doll EXPLODES, much to the delight of the crowd, who pops loud enough to be heard all the way over to our bruthas holding it down in Compton. Nate: LISTEN TO THIS CROWD! Robbie: Hey…WHERE DID HE GET THAT? Steven: Frankie’s still knocked out! I’m going for it! Corg stands and tosses the burning blow-up doll to the metal stage. Pointing at Aimz, he curses her out. The camera changes to Aimz being held back by Jane and Sebastian. She shrugs them off and makes a break for it, but Sebastian grabs her by the arm. Jane and Sebastian talk to her, trying to calm her down. Corg turns and leaves. Nate: We’ll be right back, folks, on this spectacular Slam before what could be the biggest Pay Per View of the year! Before we fade to commercial, the camera catches Robbie Decker running up the ramp to the melting plastic blow-up doll. Robbie: WHY GOD? He gets to his knees in front of the melting blow-up doll as the scene begins to fade to commercial, leaving nothing but the sound of Robbie’s screaming voice. Robbie: WWWWWHHHHHHHHHHYYYYYYYYYYYY????
