Old School Entertainment — history (October 2002)

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Kid Wonder, (born James Edward Spinks), was born on April 4, 1980 in the teenybopper capital of the World... Orlando, Florida. After spending most of his youth perfecting the arts, (music, drawing, etc.), KW was looking for some sort of challenge. He found it in the art of wrestling. He first discovered the joys of wrestling at the tender age of 15. This led Wonder to do the stupidest thing of his life... backyard wrestling. His backyard experience didn't last long though as he was clocked over the head with a flaming 2x4 that nearly cost him his career before it even got started. Kid Wonder eventually recovered. Then, at the age of 16, sought after some advice. Luckily, he found it n the form of 2 wrestling greats, Y2K and Barbwire. After taking their advice, Wonder immediately went to a local wrestling school in Orlando. Amazingly, standing just 5'9 and anywhere between 165-175 pounds, KW learned very quickly and was on his way. Kid Wonder spent 3 1/2-4 years working the indy circuit in Florida as well as most of the East Coast. He won 2 ECWA Cruiserweight titles and accumulated some big time wins over future stars such as Matt and Jeff Hardy, Shane Helms, Devon Storm, and Shannon Moore. His success in the indies led to a phone call from the Universal Wrestling Alliance and Wonder turning pro. Wonder, since turning pro, has had brief stays in numerous promotions, but most of his stays have been significant. His stint in the UWA began the never-ending feud with Doug Troy. His stint in the Wild Wrestling Federation showed that he could hold his own as Wonder won the United States title in his very first match with the company. And his stay in Kinetic Wrestling showed the World that he has what it takes to be a dominant force in a company. In KW, Wonder won the World, Internet, and Cruiserweight championships solidifying his mark as one of the best wrestlers in the World. But that wasn't enough for the bold 20-year old. He wanted more. So, with this great background, Kid Wonder signed a developmental deal with Primetime Championship Wrestling. His move to the top was now ready to go. Upon word of his signing with PCW, a bit of a buzz started through the internet circle. PCW, a promotion that already had the likes of Kremmen, Reverend, Doug Troy, and Meanstreak, now had signed this young stud. How would Wonder fit into the equation? So, to keep things under wraps some, Wonder donned a mask and called himself the Super Duper Masked Jobber King. During his brief stay in the mask, KW got wins over Flying Tiger and Ultimate Warlord. He also had a couple run-ins with then Extreme champion, Ryan Knox. He even went on to burn both the PCW and MWF versions of the Extreme title in one of his greatest achievements to date. But Wonder wouldn't wear the mask for long. At the first PCW PPV, PCW Dawning, SDMJK ran in during the fatal 4-way for the World Heavyweight title between Meanstreak, the eventual winner Kremmen, Reverend, and Doug Troy. SDMJK went up to the top as Doug Troy hit The Take on Meanstreak. Then, when the time was right, he came off and hit Doug with his So Wonderful finisher costing DT the title. That one act was all that was needed to restart the heated KW/DT feud of the past. Wonder and Troy feuded on and off for months with the teen idol arguably getting the better of Doug Troy. During that time, Wonder’s star rocketed to unthinkable heights for a guy his size. He went on to a couple of title shots at the original Shanghaied and March Madness, but both ended in screwjobs and K-Dub was never able to capture the big prize before the company’s first shutdown in June 2001. After the re-opening, Wonder went on a major slide that never seemed to end. He couldn’t win any matches. And the ones he was winning were struggles for the most part. During that stretch, in September 2001, Kid Wonder fought the legendary Soy Boy in what was dubbed 'a mark’s dream come true'. Wonder and Soy Boy put on a great show, but a Soy Boy flying head scissors was countered by Kid with a vicious piledriver. Soy Boy landed awkwardly on his neck and ended up dying as a result early the next morning. People say that Wonder never got over it, and his lack of focus seemed to prove them right. In March 2002, feeling unappreciated and disrespected by all the older and newer guys in the back, Kid Wonder left the company he, maybe more than anyone else, made and put on the map. Not wanting to be a part of a company that treats him like crap anymore, he left without any remorse or warning sending PCW on a downward spiral near bankruptcy. In April 2002, K-Dub signed on the dotted line and joined PCW’s greatest rival, Old School Wrestling. At first, he didn’t want anything to do with OSW, but eventually signed just to get at some people he doesn’t like. While some people may think the move to OSW will hurt him even more, there are some feel he can reach even greater heights in the fresh company. And, who knows, maybe K-Dub can even make a run at the big prize... the OSW Heavyweight title. With Amanda, his girlfriend, by his side, and an unlimited amount of talent and charisma, Kid Wonder can just taste the gold. If able to do that, Wonder can finally be considered among one of wrestling’s elite and set his place among the immortal in stone. Amanda Real Name: Amanda Lynn Sampson Nick Name: 'Mandy' by some of her friends and people who don't know any better. Deragatory names by her enemies. Catchphrase: 'Now that's wonderful!' Age: 20 (b. 6/4/81) Height: 5'7½ Weight: 115 lbs. Bio Amanda Lynn Sampson was born on June 4th, 1981 in Orlando, Florida. Amanda, the second of four kids, had a normal childhood. Never got involved with drugs or alcohol. Never got into fights. Never got into trouble with the law. She was a good kid. Her 'girl next door' image made her likeable to the people around her. And Amanda's natural beauty made the boys attracted to her. She was just a normal girl with no intentions of getting involved with the wrestling business. By time Amanda was 16, she had developed a close relationship with her brother, Kyle Sampson. Kyle, who is three years older than Amanda, was friends with a girl by the name of Christina 'Christy' Jenkins. Both of them were huge wrestling fans. And on October 22nd, 1997, after much convincing, they took Amanda to her first wrestling event. It was an independent show in Winter Park, Florida. And while Amanda didn't exactly enjoy the show, the trip was worthwhile. After the show, Christy introduced to Amanda to a close friend of hers by the name of James Spinks, better known as Kid Wonder. The two immediately clicked. They started to see some more of one another and eventually became a couple. And the rest is, well, history. Despite the fact she was dating an aspiring wrestler, Amanda had no intentions of getting into the sport herself. It just wasn't her thing. But after her boyfriend was jumped, attacked with a crowbar, and put out of action with a serious knee injury, it allowed Amanda a chance to get closer to her man. And the time she spent with Kid Wonder helped Amanda change her outlook on wrestling. And once her boyfriend was cleared to wrestle again, Amanda found herself going to more and more shows. And with Amanda there, Kid Wonder found himself performing better and better. As if Amanda's presence provided some sort of inspiration. But Amanda still had no plans of getting into wrestling. After graduating from high school in 1999, Amanda was given an interesting proposal. And after some convincing, she decided to become her boyfriend's valet. She started to accompany Kid Wonder to the ring, and in no time he started to perform at a level that no one could imagine. And as his star started to take off, Amanda stood by his side. And she ended up doing something that she never intended on doing. Amanda found herself in the wrestling industry. In April of 2000, Kid Wonder went from the independents to the big time. But it didn't get any bigger than in October 2000 when they went to the new and fresh Primetime Championship Wrestling. And in PCW, Kid Wonder was able to build a legacy. But while he did that, Amanda started to make a name for herself as well. Even though she'd be pushed around by big guys who were too scared of K-Dub, or looking to get at her boyfriend (Ivan Stanislav, Boda, etc.), Amanda's dignity, class, and great personality made her stick out among the valets in the sport. After a while, however, the joy ride started to end. With guys such as Tyler Nelson, and later Duke Williams running the show, they would allow people to run around and take cheap shots at Kid Wonder. She got into this to have fun. And when K-Dub stopped having fun, so did she. And on March 10th, 2002, the couple left the place they helped turn into a household name. Looking to find the fun in the wrestling business again, Amanda decided to try her hand at in-ring action. And what better way to start than the OSW Queen of Extreme tournament. Amanda was the Queen of PCW despite what other people thought or said. But she hit a minor snag by the name of Kimbusa. After bringing Kid Wonder into the fray, and eventually into OSW, Amanda was eliminated from the tournament. Now she serves as a valet once again to her boyfriend. And as the saying goes, if it’s not broke, don’t fix it. The couple have gone back to their old formula in hopes of returning to the glory the couple shared in PCW. Only time will tell if that is the case.
