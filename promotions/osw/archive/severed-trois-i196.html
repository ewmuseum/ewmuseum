Severed: Trois Written: July 6, 2002 The scene fades into the backstage area, as Reno walked up to the door of the locker-room he and Aimz had been relaxing in before, only to find the door locked and the security officials missing. ''Amy?'' He knocked at the steel door with an urgent fist. ''You still there?'' Inside, Corg still had a knife to Campbell's throat as he lead her over near the door, whispering into her ear, ''Tell him everything's fine...you'll..heh....you'll be out in just a minute.'' ''Everything's cool, Reno...'' She called out on command. ''I'm just...uhhh...I'm changing.'' Davis smiled cooly to himself, then shouted back through the door. ''Then what's stopping me from being in there?'' The next voice to come from inside wasn't Amy's... ''What's stopping you? The lock I put on the door before I came in to kill your girlfriend, you dumb shit.'' ''CORG?!'' Reno's face got to be as red as his hair. ''GET THE FUCK OUT HERE AND FIGHT ME!!! LEAVE HER THE HELL ALONE!!'' Davis was bashing against the door, screaming profainities at Corg, who lurked inside with Amy. This hub bub caught the attention of Moose Malone, who was walking the halls and quickly hurried over, shouting something into a walkie talkie before pinning Reno to the wall. ''Calm the hell down, Reno!! What's going on? Who's in there?!'' ''He's in there! FUCK! He's got Amy, too!!'' Moose tried to rationalize, ''Who is it? Corg?'' ''YES, IT'S FUCKING CORG! Get some fucking help to bash this door in!!!'' Security was there in the blink of an eye, obviously responding to Malone's signal from the walkie talkie earlier on. The group of men bashed away at the door, then finally opened it up….only to find a tricky scene inside. Corg kneeled down in the corner, Amy's head in his lap, a knife to her throat as she gazed at the men through half-closed eyes, most likely already suffering from a concussion thanks to her earlier encounter with the wall. Reno was screaming at the top of his lungs at Corg, who just sat there smirking... ''I thought you'd want to see this, Davis! See what happens when you let your girlfriend piss me off? You've got your little 'Unwanted Angel' thing, but this is one pain she brought upon herself...'' As he finished, he slowly raised the knife up over Campbell's chest, making sure to hold her wrists together with his free hand before he hovered his other, knife and all, right over where her heart would lie in her chest. Unfortunately for Corg, just as he was about to drive it into her, Reno, along with several of the other men, who'd heard the noise and arrived moments before, tackled him... It wasn't quite soon enough, though. Corg missed his target when the men jumped him...it was JUST in time for Reno to see the knife drive into the flesh between her shoulder and heart. The bloody scream from Aimz when the knife tore into her was just barely drowned out with the shouts of security, and totally overshadowed by the cursing and howling of Davis as he watched Corg stab the blade into the flesh of the woman he loved. With that….the lights went out….plunging the entire room into darkness, where the only thing evident, where the screams of pain, anguish and confusion. The scene shifts to the ringside position of the OSW announce team. Nate: HOLY JESUS!!! SOMEONE CALL THE COPS!!! Robbie yawns. Steven: MAKE HER BLEED!!!
