Graphic Violence vs Lance Knight

2002-04-28
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Graphic Violence vs Lance Knight Written: April 28th 2002 Nate: Welcome back to Head On Collision!! I can not believe Da Westsidaz are here in Old School Wrestling! Robbie: (ethnically) AWWWWEE YEEEEEAAAAH!!! Nate: Don’t ever do that again… Steven: (singing) Fight the power!!! Fight the power!!! Nate: Don’t ever do that again…Ladies and Gentlemen! Our next match on the card for an already amazing Head-On Collision involves the fWo Survivor star Graphic Violence and a mystery opponent! Any ideas as to who the mystery opponent is? Robbie: I'm hoping it's Steven! Steven: But I'm right here! Robbie: Yeah, but if you're the opponent then you'll get in there and get the shit beat out of you! Nate: Without further adieu, Referee Speedy Riggs is in the ring, Earl Henrich has got the stick, and we're ready for the next match of Head on Collision!! Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen! The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, he hails from New York City and stands 6'8' tall and weighs in at 285 lbs…GRAPHIC VIOLENCE!!! 'I Stand Alone' by Godsmack plays as Graphic Violence heads out of the backstage area and slowly walks down to the ring. Nate: This'll be the first time that Graphic Violence officially wrestles in an OSW wrestling ring; I'm anxious to see if he is able to compete as well as he has in the other federations he's been in. Robbie: I don't think the wrestling rings are very different, Nate. Square, ropes, turnbuckles… Nate: THAT'S NOT WHAT I MEANT!! Earl: And his opponent, from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania! He stands 6'3' tall and weighs in at a weight of 255 lbs: LANCE KNIGHT!! 'Elite' by the Deftones plays through the arena as Lance Knight rushes down to the ring!! The crowd explodes!!! Nate: fWo SUPERSTAR LANCE KNIGHT IS HERE!!! THESE TWO MEN FOUGHT IN FWO SURVIVOR AGAINST ONE ANOTHER!! THEY HATE EACH OTHER!!! Steven: Robbi…Nate….scream…..to…..oud….to…alk!!! Nate: THIS IS AMAZING!! THESE TWO MEN HATE EACH OTHER!! Robbie: I….an't……ear…my…elf…..eak!!! Knight slides into the ring as GV rushes forward and stomps on him instantly. Knight powers up to his feet as the two men brawl wildly in the ring. Henrich bails out of the ring as Speedy Riggs tries to restore order! Nate: WOW!!! GV and Knight shove Speedy away and brawl again. GV finally gets the advantage and knees GV in the gut. He locks him in a butterfly suplex and tosses him across the ring! Knight gets to his feet as GV rushes forward for a clothesline. Knight hits an armdrag and takes GV to the mat and locks on a rear chin lock! Robbie: Fast and furious succession of moves so far! Steven: You all right, Nate? Nate: YES!! GV reaches out to get a rope break, but he's a good foot from the ropes. Knight wrenches the headlock again, but Graphic Violence manages to get to his knees. He drives an elbow into the side of Knight and follows up with another one, breaking the hold. GV grabs Knight for a suplex, but Knight blocks the attempt and reverses into a small package!! 1… kickout by Graphic Violence! Steven: Graphic Violence may be the bigger of the two, but if he's not careful Lance Knight can easily dispatch him. Robbie: Yes, what he said! Graphic Violence and Lance Knight get up at the same time and lock up again. Both men struggle for the advantage. GV wrenches Knight into an armbar. Knight slaps his shoulder and reverses the armbar into an armbar of his own! GV tries to reverse it, but is unable to. Knight drives an elbow into the shoulder of Graphic Violence! Knight quickly releases the hold and grabs GV in a waistlock! Nate: Knight's got the right game plan here! If he can keep Graphic Violence guessing and wear him down, he's well on his way to a victory! Graphic Violence suddenly mule kicks backwards, catching Knight between the legs, and collapsing him! Robbie: HA HA!! I guess Knight guessed wrong that time! Knight rolls on the mat as GV reaches down and lifts him to his feet. GV sends Knight into the nearest corner and follows up with a running knee to the gut. Graphic Violence fires three huge chops to Knight's chest and pulls him out of the corner! Steven: Tremendous flurry of offensive moves by Graphic Violence! He's not going down without a fight! Knight goes for a knee lift but GV is able to block it. He fires an elbow to the side of Knight's head and lifts him into a stalling brainbuster! Graphic Violence holds Knight in the air for the better part of five seconds and then drops him and goes for the cover! 1… 2… …kickout by Knight!! Nate: Lance Knight is a fighter… Steven: (purring) Among other things… Graphic Violence yells and sends Knight into the ropes. Knight rebounds into a massive spinebuster! Graphic Violence goes for the pin again!! 1… 2… …kickout again my Knight!! Robbie: God damn it!!! Stay down you fWo nobody!!! GV gets up and yells at Speedy Riggs, who swears he's been counting at the same speed throughout the match. Knight manages to get his bearing back and hits GV in the back of the knee with a dragon screw leg whip, sending GV to the mat. Knight grabs his leg and locks him in a single leg crab in the middle of the ring!! Nate: LOOK AT THAT POSITIONING!!! Robbie: Ca…n't….he…mys….lf….ag…ain!!! Nate: BEAUTIFUL!!! Graphic Violence screams in pain and presses himself up to crawl to the ropes, but collapses under the weight of Knight! He slams his fist on the mat as Riggs checks for a submission but he denies it. Knight rears back and pulls harder as GV howls in pain and reaches back behind him to grab at Knight, but he isn't positioned correctly to catch anything more than a handful of air. Nate: Graphic Violence can't hold out too long like this! Look at how far back Knight is wrenching that leg!! Steven: Yeah….that looks like it hurts… Robbie: YOU THINK SO? Knight leans back and locks his leg around GV's and converts the hold into an STF!! He pulls back GV's head roughly as Speedy checks for a submission again! Nate: Is he tapping?? Graphic Violence roars and waves Riggs away weakly. Riggs shrugs and backs off as Knight maintains his hold, keeping the larger Graphic Violence grounded. GV fades and nears unconsciousness, prompting Riggs to grab his arm and drop it… Nate: That's one!!! Riggs lifts it and goes to drop it again… Steven: That's two!!! Riggs lifts the arm of Graphic Violence and goes to drop it again, but at the last moment…. Robbie: Surprise…surprise…. GV pushes Riggs away and with a last ditch surge of energy, he manages to power out of the entire hold! Knight rolls off of Graphic Violence and begins to stand as GV rolls towards the apron. Just as GV makes it to the ropes, Knight grabs him by the neck and pulls him to his feet. Nate: Those submission holds took a toll on Graphic Violence. Look how he's favoring his leg and back! Sure enough, Graphic Violence rises gingerly to a vertical base. Knight sends him into the ropes and scoops him up into a backbreaker and dumps him to the mat and goes for the cover!! 1… 2… kickout by Graphic Violence!! Steven: Well, so far I would have to say I am impressed by both men! Nate: You can tell the animosity between these two! Neither are letting up on their attacks! It's all business in the ring! Knight lifts Graphic Violence again. The last kickout seemed to take its toll on GV. Knight sends GV into the ropes again. Knight tries for a wild clothesline, but GV is able to duck it! At the last moment GV lashes out with a mafia kick, clipping Knight in the back and dropping him to the mat!!! Nate: DESPERATION MOVE BY GRAPHIC VIOLENCE!! HE'S STILL IN THIS!!! Steven: I……uck….ay….en…. Robbie: Th….'s…dis….us….ing……ag…..ot…. Knight and Graphic Violence both fall to the mat as Speedy Riggs blinks stupidly for a moment and then begins the mandatory ten-count! 1… 2…. 3…. Knight gets to his hands and knees and shakes his head, rattled from the sudden attack. Graphic Violence rolls over and grabs at his stomach. 4….. 5….. 6…. 7….. Knight gets to a knee, prompting Riggs to stop the count. Knight gets to his feet and rushes at Graphic Violence. GV bursts forward suddenly and hits Knight with a low spear, clipping his knee and dropping him! Graphic Violence gets to his feet, feeling his second wind kick in!! Robbie: Graphic Violence is still in it! I can't believe it!! Graphic Violence lifts Knight into a waistlock and plants him to the mat with a reverse DDT. He yells and sits on the chest of Knight and lays into him with lefts and rights, throwing away the technical aspect of wrestling in order to just inflict pain! Nate: He's becoming unglued, folks! Riggs goes over to break it, but GV pushes him away. Riggs yells and begins to count! 1…. 2…. 3….. 4….. GV dismounts Knight and kicks him hard in the side of the head. He lifts Knight into a powerbomb position and plants him to the mat!! Nate: Tremendous move by Graphic Violence!! At the end of the powerbomb, GV clutches his leg and falls to a knee! He yells and hauls himself back up to his feet and staggers over to Knight, breathing heavily. He slowly lifts Knight. Knight throws a weak punch to the side of GV's head, GV fires back with a weak punch of his own. Both men lock up, resting each other's head on their opponent's shoulder. Steven: Those men are spent…I like it! Robbie: That's disgusting! GV throws another punch but Knight catches his arm and goes for a suplex! He tries to lift the larger GV but GV manages to remain grounded. In desperation, GV DDT's Knight into the mat! Nate: This could be it! It looks like Graphic Violence is getting ready to finish off Lance Knight!! Suddenly, Lillith skulks out from backstage and slowly heads down to the ring. Nate: No good can come of this! Graphic Violence lifts Lance to his knees and then turns as Lillith leaps onto the apron. He drops Lance and turns to her, telling her to get away from the ring! Nate: What is Lillith doing here?? Robbie: She's a weirdo - who knows? Lillith remains on the apron. GV stomps over to her with his arm pulled back, sizing her up to clock her one. Suddenly, Lillith leans forward and spits a blood colored mist into the eyes of Graphic Violence!! The cloud engulfs his head and sends him flailing backwards, palming at his eyes!!! Steven: She blew some sort of liquid into his eyes!!! Nate: Graphic Violence is blind!!! Robbie: And Lance Knight is standing now!! Graphic Violence staggers backwards into Knight, who easily hooks him the Knightmare!! Knight hooks the leg!! 1… 2…. 3!!!!!!! Winner: LANCE KNIGHT!!! Nate: LANCE KNIGHT HAS WON!! HE'S BEATEN GRAPHIC VIOLENCE!! Steven: I….an't…ay…anyt…ng!! Robbie: M….either!! Lillith grins at the camera, blood dripping from her chin and staining her teeth red. She turns and heads away from the ring and back up the entryway, happy with her handiwork. Speedy raises Lance's hand as the winner while Graphic Violence lays in a pool of blood that came from Lillith's mouth! Nate: What a match folks! Lance Knight came to OSW for one night to battle his nemesis, to combat Graphic Violence!! Lillith has cost Graphic Violence his match!! Is it because of his comments towards women? Or is it something deeper? Only time will tell!!
