Coincidence Or Fate? Part 2

24 November 2003

by Angelus Jones

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#225) by Angelus Jones, dated 24 November 2003, written from El Paso, Texas, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
The four men had left the rodeo and after a few blocks turned into a quiet alley and stopping. Angelus stopped just before the entrance and waited, listening. “$500…good deal considering the sedatives were $175!” he could hear one of them say. “Fuck $175 man you know how much we coulda been sued for if they knew that bull was drugged?! I’m just glad it WAS drugged, you see how it kicked off that big guy too??” Jose replies. “Hey hey hey…by the way I’m sure I recognized that guy from somewhere…shit Carlos you said he looked familiar didn’t you!” “I was only joking man…remember a long time back when we lived in Colorado working on that little farm?” Carlos replies. The other three thought for a moment then nodded. “Ever remember the kid that worked with us, the one that we………” “Pfft no chance bro, I know the kid you’re talkin’ about, but I remember seeing him in prison, there was no way he was getting out of there!” replies Jose. “It has been a while, what if he got bailed out or something?!” says Carlos. Angelus inched closer to the alley, gritting his teeth at what he was hearing. “There’s no way after all this time he gets out and by some freakin’ coincidence ends up at the same damn place as us! That’s the kinda thing chu see in movies and shit man, this is real life homez and there ain’t NO WAY that kid got out…” Angelus took a deep breath and walked past the alley to a short gasp from the men inside. Stopping, he turned and peered inside then pointed at Jose. “Hey there, Jose Marcos ain’t it?” Angelus said casually. Carlos nudged Jose, who stepped forward with a sly grin on his face. “That’s me my friend, I saw chu competing there…nice ride.” “Yeah I was about to say the same…how the hell did you manage to stay on that thing as long as me?!” “Ah chu know, been doing this for a while…good tactics. I must be too small and scrawny for a big thing like dat to kick me off!” he chuckled. “Yeah, well nice one anyway. Enjoy the $500!” Angelus replied. Jose nodded and turned back to his comrades as Angelus left the alley. Stopping outside it, his eyes flashed with anger and quietly he turned back into the alley. “Oh by the way boys…” Jose turned around again, looking slightly nervous. “Y-yeah?” He mumbled. “You boys headed anywhere?” Angelus said calmly. Carlos and Jose and the other two looked at each other and shrugged. “Just home I guess, why chu need a ride somewhere homey? Heh heh…” replied Jose. “Actually I do. Where you guys headed?” “We got a place outside of town, where you looking to go?” “I’m headed to Corpus Christi tomorrow actually…you mind if I stay over at y’alls house? I’ll pay!” The four huddle up and begin discussing things out of earshot of Angelus. Quickly they make a decision and turn back to Angelus. “Certainly we can put chu up for the night.” said Jose. Angelus stood grinning at the men as something sinister twinkled in his good eye, and together they all left…
