Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman — history (October 2002)

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman is a nerd. A dork. A geek. Every female's worst blind-date nightmare. Growing up, young Herbie Dorkiman wasn't part of the in crowd, he didn't go to his senior prom, and was ALWAYS chosen last at kickball. He did not aspire to be a professional wrestler as a lad and for that matter he did he engage in any sort of athletics unless mandated through a school required physical education program. What Herbie lacked in physical skills, he made up for with supra-genius level intelligence. After picking up two doctorate degrees from Princeton University, in Physics and Chemistry respectively, the man called PHD by his friends was hired by his alma mater as an Associate Professor in the University's science department. Intially, the university was thrilled with PHD's performance both in the classroom and in the research lab. Unfortunately, PHD is a man with hobbies, hobbies that would eventually lead to his professional downfall. With PHD's new job came expendible income and with expendible income came more comic books, more science fiction videos and more roleplaying games. All night sci-fi movie marathons and weekend long, non-stop Dungeons & Dragons sessions with his college friends 'Obese' Reese Harnes, Barry 'The Claw' Johnson and Noreen Druckenheimer aka Noreen-Noreen the Computer Geek's Dream, took their toll on PHD's work output, and, perhaps, his sense of reality. School officials became worried when PHD began oversleeping causing him to miss his morning class sessions. Their worries only became compounded when he tried to convince his peers at a weekend chemistry retreat that the molecules were held together because 'The Force' chooses to bind them together. Placed on administrative leave from the university so he could 'rest,' PHD now had two things dangerous to any anti-social, supra-genius, dorkozoid: Free time and disability payments. Using his free time to perfect the reception on his five self-designed, hodge-podge satellite dishes, PHD managed to amuse himself with science fiction and fantasy movies 24 hours a day, seven days a week. His comic book collection continued to expand, as did his vast collection of roleplaying game supplements. One day, as a practical joke, his friends toyed with PHD's ramshackle satellite reception network, banning all science fiction programming. Without his belovedsci-fi, he was forced to watch 'programming of the mundane.' After channel surfing for a solid 90 minutes, PHD came across a professional wrestling program. Much to his surprise, it intrigued him. He watched the entire two hour program, and found himself yearning to see more. This led PHD to fiddle with his satellite receivers. Soon, all five of his televisions were tuned to wrestling. PHD watched, studied and formulated, and after 76 hours of extensive research, the five-foot-seven-inch, 147 pound associate professor came to one conclusive discovery: He could dominate the world of wrestling because brains always triumph over brawn. Considering that his own brain was larger and worked in a vastly more efficient manner than that of the average wrestler, it was only natural that PHD pursue a career in professional wrestling. After being laughed at, rejected by and hung up on by all of the major international wrestling organizations for a variety of reasons ranging from the complete absence of natural athetic ability to his lack of precense and charisma, PHD was not daunted. After searching continually for an organization willing to give him an opportunity, he eventually was signed by the 'Legendary' Johnny B. of the Midwest Boxcar and Hobo Regional Railway Wrestling Entertainment Alliance. PHD quickly impressed Johnny B. with his knowledge of the sport and his ability to make the most of what little physical abilities God had granted him. After a few short weeks in Johnny B's organization, PHD had managed to capture thethe MBHRRWEA's Northwest South Dakota Regional Railway Cruiserweight crown. Sensing there was little more he could teach PHD riding the railways wrestling at trainstations and carnival grounds, Johnny B. urged PHD to move on. PHD went on to work to win the Millennium Wrestling Federation cruiserweight title, as well as the Ultra Wrestling Federation cruiserweight and tag team titles. It was in UFW, he met and forged an alliance with his long time friend, Howard Porter. Porter would eventually, follow PHD to a new promotion, called Primetime Championship Wrestling, and together with Alan Kriegman and Nick Taylor, they would go on to form the most dominant stable of PCW's early era. After his time in PCW came to a close, PHD came across the opportunity to try out for fWo's Survivor. Using subliminal messaging on his application video to favoribly manipulate the fWo Survivor selection committee into accepting his application, PHD now spent every waking moment studying video tapes of the other 15 Survivor contenders, searching for weaknesses he could exploit on his way to reaching his ultimate goal: An fWo contract. Unfortunately for PHD, he failed to achieve the goal he set out to achieve, for the first time in his life. Sitting at home and sulking, he was eventually contacted about coming to Old School Wrestling, to rejoin Porter and Kriegman, to become part of the Infamous 'Vax' group in the the second coming of the Think Tank. Will this group of intellectual giants go on to dominate Old School Wrestling, or have their best years past them by? Stay tuned to find out!
