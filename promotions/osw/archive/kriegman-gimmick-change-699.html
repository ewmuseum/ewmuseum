Kriegman Gimmick Change?

2002-08-10

Posted by Matt Shivan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumor has it that Alan Kriegman plans to change his gimmick from the "Evil Supergenius" to the "Dancing Man" Alan Kriegman. "Its been something Alan has been considering for a while," said a source "close to Kriegman" according the popular online wrestlingsheet, The News. "He just wants to freshen up his image." Kriegman has called the report "ridiculous" and promising liquification to the source of the rumor. Kriegman added, "I have not danced anything other than the Mambo in my entire life." OSW officials declined to comment on the report. We will bring you more on this story as it develops.
