Frankie Bones Real Name: Frankie Bones Nick Name: The Legend of Pain Catchphase: "Pain?...Suffering?...What else is new?" Character Handler: Chris A. MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Who We Be" by DMX Date of Birth: 26 From: New York, New York (Bronx) Height: 6'6" Weight: 285 lbs Wrestling Style: Technical Powerhouse Signature Moves: Somoan Drop, Spear, Clothesline, Missle drop kick, series of 5 quick snap suplexes Finisher Move: Bone Yard - tornado reverse DDT Set-Up Move: Troubled Past - whip into corner followed by spear. Current Team: None Past Teams & Groups: Black Attack (PCW) Title History: none Awards History:none Description: Frankie is a black man. He has a large, cut body type very similar to HHH. He has a bald head with a dark chin goatee. He has a large guage earring in each ear. He has the word "PAIN" tattoo on his stomach. His usually clothing is a black wave cap, white t-shirt, black pea coat, blue jeans, and black boots. In the ring he just wears jeans and boots. History: Frankie was born in upstate New York and lived a basic suburban lifestyle. But when he was 2, his house was burned to the ground. His mother and father blamed the white community of the town and decided to move to the big city. After a long job search, his parents decided to sell Crack. Frankie's brother got involved with gangs and was arrested in their apartment, right in front of Frankie's eyes. Later that same day, Frankie's parents were killed by his "Uncle Escobar", again, right in front of Frankie. These two events have haunted Frankie his entire life. Jumping from foster home to foster home, never fitting in. He finally found his place in wrestling and has worked his way to the top. The only way he can silence his demons of the past is to inflict pain on others. His is usually determined on his goals, but sometimes the demons get the best of him and he goes slightly insane.
