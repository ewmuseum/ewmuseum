Ivan Stanislav vs. El Drago

2002-06-29
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivan Stanislav vs. El Drago Written: 6-29-02 Nate: Welcome back to Slam…and would someone PLEASE arrest that sick bastard, Corg? Robbie: She is just playing hard to get… Nate: (shaking his head) I have no time to debate this crap with you. Let’s send it up to the ring for what, on any other night, would be a main event matchup, between Ivan Stanislav and El Drago. Robbie: Comrade Stanislav will crush him like bug. Nate: (ignoring Robbie) Well things sure seem to be heating up in the Red Army and now an unhappy Ivan Stanislav will have to face El Drago. Robbie: If that loser Michel Krieg could have just done his job like he was supposed to, and taken out this filthy Mexican all could have been saved. Nate: El Drago is quite a talent though Robbie, he was the cause of Ivan losing the shot at the World title. Robbie: And don’t think Ivan will forget that either. Nate is cut off as “The Soviet National Anthem” starts to blare over the speakers and Ivan makes his way from the back with Alexei and Wang bringing up the rear. Nate: This will only spell trouble for El Drago if those two are out here. Robbie: He has Rucha with him who has done his fair share of damage if you recall. Ivan climbs into the ring and before Earl can announce him snatches the mic away. Nate: Looks like he’s going to say something. Ivan: Drago, you wanted me one on one… and you earned me one on one..Get out here! Robbie: Time to teach the Mexican some lessons. “Unleash the Dragon starts to play over the speakers and the Mexican Dragon starts to make his way down towards the ring with Pablo Rucha a step behind him. Steven: DAMN I love Spandex. Drago hops into the ring and circles around Ivan and company while Rucha stays on the outside. Ivan puts up his hand in hesitation and motions for Drago to give him room. Robbie: This must be a big plan. Ivan turns to Alexei and Wang, turning his back on Drago. Ivan: Leave me be. Alexei tries to protest but gets a bellow of a response. Ivan: Leave!!! Alexei nods and with Wang they leave the ring and head towards the back. The crowd is silent in shock of Ivan’s orders as is Drago across from him. Nate: This smells like a trap to me, there are only so many times I will fall for a trap from this rat. Robbie: Ivan is being fair and you call him a cheat. No wonder he rarely bothers about you. Drago walks up to Ivan and extends his hand out for the mic. The big Russian hands it over and Drago turns his back on Ivan to approach Drago. Nate: Don’t turn your back!!! The commentators are silent waiting for the cheap shot but there is none. Drago: Sígalos a la espalda y lo se cerciora no es una trampa. Permanezca con ellos. Rucha nods and jogs up the ramp catching up to Alexei and Wang and the three disappear backstage. Robbie: See, if he wanted to cheat he could of right then and there! Nate: I still don’t like this. Drago tosses the mic aside and nods at Ivan that he’s ready. The bell sounds and both men start to circle around each other looking for an opening. Steven: This would be a huge win for El Drago if he can pull this off. Nate: Indeed it would be Steven, for a man to come from no where and defeat the Russian Bear after a couple of months would be amazing. Robbie: And impossible. Drago stops circling and holds a hand up in the air inviting Ivan to lock up. Nate: I can’t say I agree with this move. Ivan stands up and starts to chuckle but that only causes Drago to insist on the lock up more and more. Robbie: This shows you the intelligence of Mexican’s. Ivan shrugs and locks up with one hand and holds the second hand, which Drago snatches. El Drago uses his speed to pull Ivan’s arms downwards surprising everyone including the Russian Bear himself. Nate: I don’t believe it. Drago continues to put all the pressure down to hold Ivan at bay but the Russian’s strength catches up to the Mexican’s quickness and with amazing power he starts lift Drago off the ground in a squat thrust fashion. Robbie: That I believe!! Ivan grunts as he thrusts the Mexican Dragon into the air still supporting him only by his hands and tosses him straight up into the air. He waits for him to come falling down and swats him face first into the canvas. Nate: What a show of strength by Ivan. Steven: I hope El Spandexo didn’t hurt mini spandexo… Nate: STEVEN!!! Ivan bends down to pick up Drago but El Drago spins like a breakdancer and sweeps Ivan’s legs out and he crashes to the ground like a tree. Robbie: Drago’s quickness will only last as long as he can stay out of Ivan’s grasp, which won’t be very long. Ivan roars back to his feet but Drago is there with a drop kick to the face. Amazingly this doesn’t slow Ivan down at all as he takes the blow and continues to rise. Nate: He just no sold that drop kick to the face. Robbie: He’s no sold Meanstreak, you think a 280 pound Mexican can make him sell. Drago bounces back to his feet and starts to wail at Ivan with rights and lefts but it still doesn’t effect the Russian any. Ivan rears back his fist and drives it into Drago’s chest and sends him flying back several feet into the corner. Nate: Commentary tends to get bland during these matches with Ivan’s amazing show of power and Drago’s resilience to bounce back, so why don’t we talk about the Red Army a bit? Robbie: How about you shut up and call the match? Nate: I’m serious, earlier tonight we saw even more of this happening but are they falling apart? Drago fires out of the corner and goes for spear on Ivan but Stanislav steps back cushioning the blow and quickly smashing Drago down to the mat with double axehandle. Robbie: The Red Army will always prevail. It went through traitors such as Boda and the like and still bounced back stronger then ever. Steven: So you’re saying what doesn’t kill it will only make it stronger? Robbie: For once the flamer got something right. Ivan bends over to pull Drago up but the Mexican latches onto Ivan’s leg and won’t let go. It takes several shakes of his leg before Ivan can kick him off but it was all the time Drago needed to regain himself and as Ivan lunges, Drago rolls right hooking Ivan’s arms and pulling him over into a pinning combination. 1. Ivan powers out of the pin. Steven: I guess that’s why I have such a strong Colon then… *SMACK* Robbie: You just had to ruin it didn’t you. Steven: Sowie… Ivan charges at Drago pissed at the early pin attempt and Drago jumps back twisting around and springing off the middle rope into a cross body that hits Ivan and staggers him back but not down. Nate: I see what you were saying Robbie, but it looks as if the Red Army is actually dying. Upon those words the crowd erupts into boos and Alexei Ruslan comes backing out of the entranceway. Robbie: You were saying? Drago stays on the attack hitting Ivan with rights and lefts and staggering the beast, neither man seeing what is happening in the ring. Nate: He’s trying to stop Krieg though! Look Rucha is there too. Indeed Rucha is as both Ruslan and he are yelling at Michel Krieg to get to the back. Michel stops about half way up the ramp when Ivan between blows of Drago catches site of him. Robbie: Yes help is on the way Comrade. Drago still has no idea as he goes to hit Ivan but is blocked and swatted backwards, rolling head over heels. Ivan immediately turns his attention toward Krieg halfway up the ramp and start to yell at him motioning for him to get out of here. Drago meanwhile stumbles forward and sees Ivan hitting him in the back with a drop kick. Robbie: That’s not fair! Nate: It’s not his fault Ivan stopped paying attention. Ivan roars and turns around grabbing Drago and Irish whipping him into the ropes near the stage. Ivan drops for a back body drop and Drago flips over top grabbing the legs and going for the flip into a pin but Ivan is too strong to go down. Robbie: You won’t beat Ivan that way! Krieg turns his back on the match as if he’s leaving and Ruslan and Rucha start to follow him back but that’s when the attack comes from either side with a pair of chairs held by Frankie Bones and Kory Storm connecting on Ivan’s head and causing him to fall back into the pin by Drago. Robbie: WHAT THE HELL!!! Nate: That’s not right at all! Drago holds the pin not knowing anything was wrong and Pierre counts the three as Bones and Storm enter the ring with their chairs. Krieg turns and plows over Rucha and Ruslan with a double clothesline taking both men out and sliding into the ring himself. Steven: I don’t think El Drago wanted to win that way. Drago hops up with his hands in the hair but only momentarily as a chair from the hands of Frankie Bones cracks him against the skull and sends him staggering backwards right into a second chair shot from Storm. Nate: Somebody needs to stop this. Robbie: Comrade Walczak will fix this. Storm and Bones drive Drago into the corner and Bones talks trash as Kory jams a chair into the Dragon’s neck pinning him. Krieg stands over top of the Russian Bear and starts talking trash to him. Nate: Krieg has turned on Ivan and cost him this match against El Drago. Krieg: What happened Ivan, could not get the job done! Michel slaps the sleeping bear however this wakes the Russian up as he grabs a handful of Krieg’s package and tosses him off of him. Michel bounces on the mat holding his jewels as Ivan roars to life. Robbie: Kill, Seek, Destroy!! Bones and Storm charge at Ivan but a boot to the face of Storm sends him flying and then a clothesline to Bones stops his attack too. Nate: Ivan’s fighting them all off. Ivan turns his attention back to Krieg and pulls the wounded German to his feet. Ivan: You dare turn on Ivan! Ivan starts to set Krieg up for the Red Scare when he drops Krieg to the mat at the sound of a steel chair. He turns around to see Drago standing over Frankie Bones. Nate: Drago just cut Bones off at the pass. Ivan nods and grabs a hold of Michel and hoists him up then tosses him into the air towards Drago who takes a baseball swing with the chair connecting against Krieg and knocking him lifeless to the mat. Robbie: Drago and Ivan are working together? Nate: Ivan just Red Scared Michel Krieg into a chair shot by El Drago!!! Steven: And I thought I’d seen everything. Drago tosses the chair out of the ring and pulls Storm up and promptly tosses him over the top rope to the outside. Ivan does the same to Bones and then turns his attention to Drago. Nate: Oh no, what’s going to happen here? Drago turns around and locks eyes with Ivan as the crowd grows silent waiting for what is going to happen next. Robbie: Crush him Comrade. Neither man moves for a very long time until Krieg starts to get back to his feet. Drago darts toward and past Ivan into a baseball slide catching Michel in the midsection and driving him to the outside with a thud. Nate: That was unexpected. Drago looks up to see Ivan’s hand reaching down for him and he accepts the hand up. Nate: Last time this happened Ivan Red Scared El Drago. Drago pulls his hand away and backs towards the ropes flipping over them and to the outside near Earl. He grabs the mic and looks up at Ivan. Drago: Rematch… Cell… Uno on Uno… Ivan nods in agreement for the chance to redeem himself after the cheap victory and the crowd explodes into cheers as “Unleash the Dragon” starts to play again. Robbie: Someone please tell me Ivan just didn’t turn to the good side. Nate: It looks as if he is starting too. Robbie: Come back to the dark side Ivan. Nate: It doesn’t matter what side he’s on as it appears that Ivan and El Drago just agreed to a rematch next week on Slam, however he said Cell which I can only assume would mean a Hell in a Cell match!?! Robbie: I doubt it’s a Cell Phone match, so I’d say it’s a safe bet Dumas. Nate: We’ve still got lots more tonight, we’ll be back in a bit.
