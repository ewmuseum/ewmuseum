OSW: My Battle - Part 2

2002-06-30
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW: My Battle - Part 2 Written: June 30th, 2002 As Slam! returns from commerical break, the feed to the action in the ringside area is cut off by a large amount of static that echoes throughout the arena. The static slowly begins to subside, and finally stops into a clear view of the backstage area. The backstage area seems to be all red, or at least that's what this person is seeing. It is the same camera angle from earlier tonight...except now...whoever this man is....is inside the OSW building. The fans look on excitement, as you can hear the breathing of the man behind the camera, the man..whos eyes we are looking through. The camera continues to be shakey, as the unknown figure looks past a few of the OSW superstars as they walk around the backstage area. Some figures that are seen are Kid Wonder, Frankie Bones, Ian Wright and Jason Winters. The figure's breath is only heard getting louder and louder, as if excited. And finally, the figure notices two figures standing in the distance, the figures of Sebastian Toomes and Amy Campbell. The two are seen just talking over some strategies for tomorrow night, and how their days have been...typical conversation between the partners. But the figure behind this camera angle begins to laugh lowly a bit as he stares at the two wrestlers who disappear behind the “Elite” locker room door. The camera angle looks past the two stars and notices a locker room beside it....one named... Jane Reagan. Suddenly, red blood drips down the lens of the camera...making the scenary red once again. Possibly this is how the secreted individual sees the OSW backstage area. Filled with blood. His eyes are stained with red as he stares closely at the locker room door. His laugh becomes a bit louder, but not loud enough to gain any attention whatsoever. The static begins to return, as we see the camera being set down...and the figure sitting beside it, staring at Reagan's door from behind a soda machine. The figure peaks and watches the door....and begins laughing much more. “Are you my ticket into OSW? Are you my ticket into this battle? Maybe. Maybe I will find out soon. Maybe I won't. But there is a time and a place for everything....a time and a place called the future. What does the future bring for me and this new hunting ground? What does the future hold for me if I enter this place of mayhem and competition? Heh...only fate knows the answer to these things. And I have a feeling fate will answer...very....soon” And suddenly, the static returned, leaving everyone in mystery as to what was going on, and who this figure could possibly be. As this lone, sadistic figure remained in the OSW backstage area. How did he get here? Why is he here? In his words...only fate knew those answers. And the most important question was... Who is he? The scene shifts back to the ringside position of the OSW announce team. Nate: Who in the hell is that?!?! Robbie: I dunno, but I am calling gimmick infringement on behalf of Vampir Nosferatu, who is back baby. Robbie makes a sucking blood noise…a bad one at that. Steven: Turn be on baby!!!
