Quiet Violence Written: May 25, 2002 The scene shifts elsewhere to reveal Graphic Violence stopping at a door backstage that reads “Lillith”. He grins and quietly opens the door to the dressing room. Lillith stands there combing her hair. Graphic Violence walks up behind her, holds her with one arm around her waist and puts his other hand around her mouth. 'Don’t fight, you’ll only hurt yourself' says GV. Lillith kicks and screams, but her cry for help is muffled by GV’s massive hand over her mouth. 'I’m not here to hurt you Lillith, but rather to let you think about something. Rhett Locke…he never mattered to me. He still doesn’t. But for my enjoyment and your emotional suffering, I will continue to beat Locke within an inch of his life. I will continue to do things like I did tonight to him.” GV blows on Lillith’s ear, which only incites Lillith to kick some more. His suffering is your doing, not mine. His pain and his blood is on your hands, not mine. I would of left the OSW after the PPV, but you had to show your pride to the fans and stand up for yourself. It’s your fault that I’m here, and it’s your fault that Rhett Locke is probably in the hospital right now. 'Anytime I want to Lillith, I could end you. I could do it right now if I wanted to. I have you in my grasp, a mere woman. So called ‘Queen of Extreme’ and you’re in my arms a mere woman. I don’t have time to play games with women Lillith. It sickens me to think that you even compete in my ring, but for now I’ll let you be.” Graphic Violence lets Lillith go. Lillith turns around and punches GV in the face. GV rubs his chin and smiles. 'Anytime I want to, you’re done...but for now I think I’ll continue to play with your friend Rhett Locke. See ya around Lillith” Graphic Violence laughs as he leaves the dressing room. Lillith stands there with mixed emotions on her face. Hate, fear, anxiety is all visible. OSW SLAM! fades out to it’s logo and the show heads to commercial.
