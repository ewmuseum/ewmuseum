Wrestler Spotlight: Mexi

2002-09-08

Posted by Matt Shivan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shanda: Hello there OSW, it's me Shanda Russell with a very interesting Wrestler Spotlight in store for you. My guest today, ex PCW superstar: The White Mexican! Mexi, thank you for your time! Mexi: I'm glad to be here Shanda and be able to speak English this time. Shanda: I've got to say that it is rather strange to be sitting here with you. The last time I interviewed you as El Drago I had no idea that it was actually the superstar from the PCW. I applaud your...impersonation of El Drago. Mexi: Well it wasn't much of an impersonation of El Drago, merely a return to my past. Shanda: Oh yes, that is right, I apologize. Where should I start Mexi, there are so many questions that need to be answered. Allow me to ask the first, most obvious question: Why El Drago? Why could you not just enter the OSW as Mexi and start at the top right away? Mexi: I felt I owed it to the fans of PCW to leave the White Mexican there. PCW made him the person he is today and despite the shit that Tyler Nelson put me through at the end, even though the fans would likely understand I didn't feel right. So I went to my roots and became El Drago again. Shanda: Did it bother you at all that perhaps some talent in the OSW did not pay much attention to you because you were portraying a more... naive character and yet you still had your tremendous wrestling ability? Mexi: Those that now me, that if there is one thing that stands out about me it is my sense of humor. I found it humorous that a simple mask could change so many opinions of a man. Shanda: Yes, we all know of your very famous sense of humor. Another major question is that one man was generally the brunt of your jokes, and that man was none other than Ivan Stanislav. He was one of the first men you went after when you entered as El Drago. What is the obsession with Ivan Stanislav, Mexi, a man that seems to hate you? Mexi: Well the jist of it is, when arrived in PCW the Red Army was the biggest, baddest thing around and Ivan was the leader of it. I was young, arrogant and wanting to make a name for myself. So I chose him to be the brunt of most things to get his attention, when it finally looked like I had it, he left for OSW. Now I won't lie and say that didn't have an effect on my decision to go to OSW after PCW wasn't an option, but it was perhaps fate that would have Ivan court me to join the same Red Army in OSW when I arrived. It wasn't planned, or thought out it just happened, so I used it to make a name for El Drago. Defeat the bear and you'll have it made... Of course a strange thing happened and I started to realize we were both in many ways alike and we became friends. And he was the sole reason I chose to reveal the man behind the mask. Shanda: I have got to say that you've put a tremendous amount of faith into Ivan Stanislav, to the point of talking about his personal affairs straight to his face. For most people, they would fear that The Russian Bear would destroy them, but you don't. Why go out of your way for Ivan Stanislav? A large group of people still don't trust him, he's swerved us many times before. Mexi: Perhaps it started out of respect and then it grew into more of that. As I mentioned Ivan and I are at the core so much alike, and have had similar troubles with woman. I ruined my chances with Mona and I still regret doing what I did and pushing her away and I don't want to see a friend do the same. If I have to take some lumps to get it through his head then so be it, what are friends for right? Shanda: Fascinating... I hope you've packed some band-aids then. The heart of this story appears to be Tempest, as she's claimed that you were instrumental in her return to the OSW. What is your relationship with Tempest, exactly? Mexi: I would call her a friend now, nothing more. Shanda: Interesting... well things have certainly picked up with your shocking and amazing unmasking at By Any Means Necessary. But one must wonder what the future has in store for you Mexi? Mexi: There was one thing I never accomplished in PCW and that was to win the World Title, it's one thing I don't want to repeat from PCW. Shanda: What about rumors of Tyler Nelson trying to get into the OSW? Obviously that's a major concern of yours. What if Nelson comes into the OSW? What will you do? Mexi: If he walks through that door, he's gonna have to kill me before I give up this time. OSW is my home now and I won't let a piece of donkey ass like Nelson ruin my house. Shanda: Well, that just about sums it up. Is there anything you have to say to the OSW Fans? Or to any OSW superstars in particular? Mexi: Yah, I do actually. I've seen three OSW stars, I wouldn't call them superstars exactly, walk out on OSW this past week and I have to say good riddance. We have no need for overpriced trash that wants to sit around and screw up an amazing product like they did at BAMN. So to the entire OSW roster, if you don't like it here, make like a coward and flee. Shanda: Very powerful and very true words Mexi. Well, it's a pleasure to have had this time to talk with you. I wish you good luck in your future endeavors.
