Zameer's Unexplained Absence from OSE Cards

10 November 2003

by Azala Zameer

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own news post (#196) by Azala Zameer, dated 10 November 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
For the third straight time in three weeks Azala Zameer has shown up to an OSE event just prior to his match, then has been reportedly seen at an airport immediately following that event. This time the airport was LAX in Los Angeles, California, Sunday night just before midnight catching the red eye out of the country. Still a bit battered from his match with Angelus Jones, and Clyde, and Bethany, Zameer was apparently cleared from the venue in Salt Lake City, Utah with a bruised sternum and mild concussion and left with his entourage shortly there after. An eye witness at the airport said that Zameer was in a somber mood, saying nothing and looking only to the floor as he boarded the plane, but a freelance wrestling magazine reporter was there and managed to get a word with the man known only as “The Ghost”, who was dressed a little more suitable for the rainy weather. “Azala still hasn’t fully grasped the responsibility that comes with his return to the spotlight here in OSE,” he began. “The new focus of Zameer has yet to stand the test of his people, and in winning the hardcore title from Clyde he avenged his name, which was the first step, but the following weeks are to be of more importance to the ultimate goal.” The reporter then managed to ask where it was that they were heading, as the Ghost went to walk away and was met with a crude stare, before he received and answer that most certainly left only more questions. “We are to return to the only place where Azala can complete his return, he has inherited much accountability back home with our recent loss, when he returns next week, he will have achieved adulthood fully. The spirits that allowed him to come back to us course still newly through his veins, and until he learns to accept this and benefit from it, he will not be whole.” “In understanding, Zameer will become stronger than anyone could have ever imagined, he has been chosen by our Gods, he will be born anew” That was the last anyone heard as the party escorting Zameer disappeared into the late night rain and fog. Will Zameer be back next week for Slam? Will he be back to OSE at all? In light of his loss of the Hardcore Championship, clearly controlled by Clyde’s hand, is anyone really safe when Azala returns to rectify the OSE as a whole? Only time will tell now.
