The show fades in from black to the backstage area of the Pepsi Forum in Montreal, Canada, where Ivan Stanislav’s manager/confidant, Alexei Ruslan, stands with his arms crossed over his chest in a secluded hallway. He listens softly as he hears footsteps nearing and ducks into a corner as Pablo Rucha, interpreter for the mysterious El Drago, rounds a corner and walks past the corner where the former Soviet KGB agent lurks. Ruslan hops out of the corner as if he just walked behind Rucha… Alexei: Pablo Rucha?? Rucha turns in surprise as he sees Ruslan who seemingly appeared out of nowhere. Rucha: Yes, that is who my mother birthed me as. Alexei nods and walks over to him, shaking his hand. Alexei: Alexei Gregorovich Ruslan of Russian Red Army! You are interpreter of El Drago, correct? Rucha eyes the Russian carefully as he replies. Rucha: Si. Alexei blinks, confused. Alexei: Si?? Rucha shakes his head in disbelief. Rucha: Perhaps you would prefer, da? Alexei chuckles and lets go of his hand, brushing it off on his jacket in a slight bit of disgust. Alexei: OH! That is how you people say ‘yes’ in Spanish…right. I never wasted my time with that language… Alexei clears his throat. Alexei: Uh..anyway, where is El Drago? I wished to speak with him. Rucha too chuckles in a mocking tone. Alexei swallows and talks with a bit more venom. Alexei: Is there something funny, Mexican? Rucha: If you don't 'waste' time in my native tongue then speak with me for Drago has not ability to understand English. You speak to me and I pass on to Drago, Russian. Alexei levels his voice and speaks coldly. Alexei: Right, you wish to cut to chase, eh? Well then, allow me to give you the message. Ivan Stanislav is interested in Drago’s conscription into his Red Army, as Drago is one of the names of the greatest Russian film characters of all time, Ivan Drago, who killed the black American champion Apollo Creed. Rucha: Che? Alexei: I realize the significance is wasted on someone who values beans over good shock absorbers for his vehicle, but that matters little. Stanislav desires El Drago’s join the ranks of the glorious Red Army, and I suppose, if we must, we could accept you as well…. Rucha: Unless you plan to 'waste' time, you would. Rucha smirks with a look of distain. Rucha: Drago shall receive message, however it will not... how do you say it...be in Russian's favor. Alexei pulls a hand out of his pocket and points at Rucha. Alexei: Do not be foolish my friend, and paint your own pictures. You have seen what has happened to those who oppose us. Rucha: I shall tell him my bendejo amigo. Alexei: (confused by the Spanish) You had better, as it will be easy to supply a Spanish speaking translator from a more leftest regime, nyet? Rucha pulls back his hand and nods slyly, then wanders around the corner, leaving a grim faced Ruslan, seething behind him. After each completed move, a Batman-esque SLAM! graphic splashes onto the screen, accompanied by a shrill cry of a trumpet. A throat is cleared, and the two turn to watch St. Crux’s former UWA colleague and friend-later-turned enemy, Canadian Jason Winters, step into the locker room. A large crowd pop in the background indicates that the happenings in the locker room can be seen by the partisan Canadian crowd on the arena’s Jumbotron. Winters: Hello, Jesus. Long time... The two men pause for a second, and look each other over, perhaps looking for a sign of weakness, perhaps looking for a sign of their destroyed friendship. Either way, the tension of their upcoming OSW debuts, against one another, nonetheless, is evident. He points at a shiny object within as he looks at St Crux questioningly. Slowly, he reaches down, and draws out the UWA World Heavyweight Title belt. Winters: You still carry this? He drops the belt back on top of the gymbag, as an idea seems to form in Winters mind. He raises a hand to silence any answer from the Mexican wrestler in front of him. Winters: I'm trying to move on into the future, but if you want to live in the past, I can live in the past. You know I never got my rematch. Winters taps a finger on the UWA World Heavyweight Title belt. Winters: So, tonight... How about you and me, one-on-one, for the Title. It doesn't matter if it's unsanctioned - the UWA is dead and gone. The only people who'd care are you and I.
