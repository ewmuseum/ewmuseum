The Debut of Randolph.

2002-07-20
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Debut of Randolph. Written: 7/20/02 “Whatever” by Godsmack hits the P.A. and from behind the curtain appears Scott Taylor, he looks over the crowded Igloo and they only boo him heavily. With the same arrogant smile on his face he waves his left hand dismissing them, this only causes them to boo more. Nate: The fans sure aren't giving Scott any respect here tonight. Robbie: Ingrates. This man is the Number One Contender and he deserves a little respect, this is why I hate Pittsburgh. Steven: I hear they have some really good male strip clu-... Nate: Back on track, I think Scott is going to say something. Scott reaches behind his back and removes a microphone from the waistband of his tights. The fans begin too boo louder as he puts the microphone up to his mouth to speak. He looks over the crowd again but they are still booing him. Scott: SHUT THE HELL UP! This only causes them to boo more, just out of spite to get under his skin. A chant of 'Southern Drawl!' breaks out in the crowd, which only makes Scott even more irate. Finally he's had enough and begins to speak over them. Scott: During the course of my stay here in Old School Wrestling, I've come across some problems. What sort of problems, you ask? A problem of ruthless attacks by a man named Kid Wonder. But it's not just Kid Wonder, tonight I have to wrestle a homicidal freak named Corg. God knows what he's capable of doing. That's why tonight I've come prepared. So, allow me to introduce you all to MY new bodyguard! Nate: BODYGUARD?! Robbie: Taylor is packing protection! Steven: I wonder if he's muscular.... Scott steps to the side of the entrance ramp as 'Iron Man' by Black Sabbath begins to play. The fans look around confused as they wait for Scott's security to come out. Scott points to curtain as the bodyguard steps out from behind the curtain. The entire arena instantly bursts into a mixture of boos and laughter as the man appears. Nate: What the hell is this?! Robbie: This is brilliant! I'd bring this guy as back up! Steven: ............. From behind the curtain steps a short and rather obese balding man. He's several inches shorter than Scott but looks to weigh a lot more. A terrible comb over covers his baldhead and his wardrobe is unbelievably tacky. A pair of rather large and bulky white tennis shoes, blue slacks, and a white tee shirt with the words 'Taylor Trained Ninja' written across the front. In his right hand he holds a long katana, made of plastic that is. He holds the sword over his head and lets out a war cry! Scott: Please welcome, my own personal bodyguard, Randolph! Nate: Sigh….Is that Ralphus’ twin brother? The crowd boo as Randolph heads backstage again, in fear of the members of NOW and what they may do in the following match up. Taylor looks at the ring, with a glare of concentrated determination on his face. The fans throw the odd booing jeers at Taylor Made, to which they receive nothing as Taylor simply jogs down to the ring and slides in, looking over the sold-out crowd, then staring at the entrance, awaiting his opponent and a chance at the big time.
