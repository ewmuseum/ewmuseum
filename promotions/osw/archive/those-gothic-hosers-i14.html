Those Gothic Hosers....

2002-02-16

Those Gothic Hosers.... Written: 2-16-02 The camera shot changes to the back stage interview area. Shanda Russell is in the shot with her regular warm and excited smile with her microphone in her hand. Shanda: Thanks guys! Greetings OSW fans. I’m here with Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke who are part of OSW’s most beloved tag team, Civil Disobedience. The camera pans out to show Seth and Trevor standing side by side in their traditional wrestling gear. Trevor is putting on his usual hard ass attitude while Seth is caught staring directly at Shanda’s ample chest. Trevor: How are things going with my favorite OSW reporter? Shanda: Uh...fine. Tonight you two are going to be facing Heresian Bloodlust for the third week in a row, but for the past two weeks you two have spent the evening following your matches in the hospital. What are your thoughts about tonight’s match up? Trevor: You want to know what my thoughts are about this match? You should be more concerned with the thoughts that are going through Hessian and Azrael Cain’s mind right now. In case you have forgotten, those two Goth freaks are yet to pin either of us! Seth: Yeah, tonight we are going to.... Seth is suddenly interrupted by an immense stream of water shooting into the camera’s shot that hits him in the face causing him to fall to the floor. Nate: What the.... Just as Trevor turns his head to see where the intense stream of water is spraying from, he is struck in the face by yet another spray of water. Hawke flies to the floor form the water pressure and land on top of his partner. Both men are now being thoroughly hosed down in front of all of their fans. Steven: Tee hee!! Robbie: Shut up before you require a resperator to breath. The camera pans over he reveal that the two intense streams of water are coming form two fire hoses that are being wielded by the Caleb and Minion, the loyal managers of Heresian Bloodlust. Nate: CALEB AND MINION JUST FIRE HOSED CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE!!! Robbie: That mean’s the Duo of Destruction, Azrael Cain and Hessian can not be far behind!!! Steven: Well… Robbie: (interrupting) RESPERATOR!!!! In the back, the camera focuses in on the laughing faces of Heresian Bloodlust’s managers. Caleb: How are those doctor bills boys? Heh-Heh-Heh. Minion: This should cool down your hotheaded egos! The two managers cut off the hoses. From behind the small henchmen appear the massive warriors, Azrael Cain and Hessian. Nate: THERE THEY ARE!!! Both of the men approach Masters and Hawke quickly. Cain and Hessian immediately lay the boots into their soaking wet opponents. Shanda Russell has a very confused look on her face as all of this is unfolding. She looks at the camera then looks down to see that Caleb and Minion are now standing at both sides of her. Minion: Relax baby! This will only take a minute. Shanda gets a disgusting look on her face. She turns her head to look over at the decrepit hobo, Caleb, receiving her with a toothless smile. Caleb: What are you doing after the show tonight, hot stuff? Heh-Heh-Heh. Shanda is thoroughly freaked out now. She looks at the camera. She slowly takes one step away from the creepy managers, but continues to watch the assault. Robbie: Run ya dumb ho!!! Nate: She’s a very bright young lady. Robbie: But you agree with the ho sentiment then? Nate sighs in Dave Kern like fashion. Hessian reaches down and hoists Masters up by the collar of his t-shirt. Hessian: The Hell’s Angels were a nice ploy last week. You might have gotten the win, but tonight is going to be a different story. You boys have gotten lucky the past two matches that we had. Tonight, your luck has run out! Tonight, you bleed! Steven: A bit of KY Jelly will help avoid flesh tears. Nate: Did I ever mention I am a former World Champion in the ring? Steven: Sure, everyone knows that. Nate: Then do you believe me when I say I will cave your skull with the same ease that a sledgehammer would crush a watermelon? Steven: Er….. Nate: Think about that visual, because if I ever hear you mention flesh tears and KY Jelly again on a broadcast, that is doctors are going to be looking for skull fragments. You understand? Steven swallows hard. Robbie: RESPERATOR!!! In the back, Cain leans over and picks Hawke up and brings him close to his face so that there is now way the stunned Hawke can avoid Cain’s message. Cain: You run around all week making ridiculous claims that you don’t fear us. You say that you don’t need anything to beat us, but your abilities. Your ego has gotten the best of you boy! As I look into your eyes right now I only see fear behind your eyes. I can see the fear as plain as day. And well you should be afraid! Tonight, you two are going to be visiting the surgeons again! But this time, you won’t be able to recover in time to fight next week. We are going to make sure that you two are going to be out of action for a long, long time! Cain and Hessian share a devilish and satisfied look. The two warriors then sling Masters and Hawke into the cement wall. The men crumple against the wall and slide to the floor violently. Cain and Hessian then begin to exit the scene. Hessian extends his arm though and stops Cain as the pass through the camera shot. Cain turns around to see what his partner wants. Hessian: There is some business that Minion and I need to take care of. Cain: What are you talking about? We have a match tonight! Hessian: Calm yourself. We’ll be back in time for our fight. Hessian turns around and begins to walk down the dark hallway. He stops though and summons for his manager, Minion. The little man looks disappointed that he has to leave, but he obeys his master and runs down the hallway in pursuit. Nate: Business?!? What kind of business could Hessian possible have? Robbie: (defensive) Hey, just because he is an oversized freak that wears a Destro mask, does not mean he is not a sought after endorsement commodity. Nate: Suuuuure. If you say so. Cain and Caleb turn and walk away towards their locker room in the opposite direction that Hessian and Minion were walking. The camera shot returns to Shanda Russell standing by the barely moving members of Civil Disobedience. Shanda looks around at the scene around her, but after a moment’s pause she tries to address the camera. Russell: Well...ah...that was...very...interesting...back to you, guys. The scene shifts back to the OSW announce table. Nate: Thanks Shanda. So Heresian Bloodlust gets things started a bit early with Civil Disobedience, who they will take on later in the evening. We are going to head to a commercial. We will be right back after these messages.
