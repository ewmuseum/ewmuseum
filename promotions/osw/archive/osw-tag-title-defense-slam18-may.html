Civil Disobedience vs. Heresian Bloodlust for the OSW Tag Team Titles

18 May 2002

A rematch challenge from Civil Disobedience, the team Heresian Bloodlust had taken the titles from. Ends in chaos as security breaks up the match.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil Disobedience vs. Heresian Bloodlust for the OSW Tag Team Titles Written: 5-18-02 Nate: Welcome back to OSW Slam! Coming up next we have yet another exciting match for you! This one promises to be violent, dangerous, and of course… Robbie: And a definite beat down for those bastards Masters and Hawke delivered generously by the Tag Team Champs themselves! Those two bums don’t even belong in the ring this evening! It’s things like that that hurt this fed! Cain and Hessian are going to ruin those two and hold the titles for even longer! Steven: I’ve seen a lot of dirty things in my life, but this is the raunchiest thing that I have ever witnessed in my life! Robbie: Oh, come on! This probably doesn’t even scratch the surface of the nasty things that you have seen! Steven tries to immediately change the topic! Steven: That’s right folks, coming up next we have the Tag Team Title Match! Nate: Well…so much for building up the tension. Anyway, Decker is right about one thing. If you are just tuning in, Masters and Hawke have seen to it that they get a title shot tonight by locking Riptah and Short Circuit up! They then convinced Commissioner Ed that they should be the ones that take Da Westsidaz’ place in the title match this evening. Robbie: Enough with the exposition, Dumas! Let’s take it to Earl so Masters and Hawke get eh beating that they deserve this evening. The camera shot changes to Earl Henrich waiting in the ring to start the introductions. Earl brings the mic up to his lips and begins. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, Boys and Girls, Children of all ages, the winners of the next match will become…(Dramatic pause)…The Tag Team Champions!!! The crowd explodes with wild cheers! Nate: See guys! That’s how it’s done. Earl: The match is scheduled for one fall! The referee for this match is Caleb the hobo. Caleb appears at the entryway and starts to hop around wildly. The little old man makes his way to the ring quickly as the fans boo his mere presence. Nate: Are you trying to tell me that no one has taken care of that contract stipulation yet? Robbie: Hey! Give the boss a break! It’s not like she has been kidnapped by some deranged lunatic or anything! What the hell do you want her to do!Nate: I meant… Robbie: I mean come on here, Dumas. We’re lucky that we are even able to manage a broadcast with Porter and Ed at the helm of this fed! Nate: I meant that…. Robbie: You can be so heartless some times, Nate! By now Caleb is in the ring up on one of the turnbuckles as the fans are booing wildly. Caleb rips off his trench coat in true Superman fashion to reveal his ref stripes underneath. Steven: I think he only wanted to become the ref, so he could get a newshirt. Robbie: Silence! Caleb is one of the best referees that we have aroundhere. Nate: Christ! Earl continues with the introductions. Earl: And coming to the ring first, the challengers, weighing in at a combined weight of 460 pounds, reigning from Aurora, Illinois…. “Bounce” begins to play on the PA system in the arena as the fans explode in cheers! Earl: They are Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, Civil Disobedience!!! Robbie: Like lambs to the slaughter. Masters and Hawke appear at the top of the steel ramp and begin to work the crowd over for more cheers.Robbie: HA! Look at Caleb! Steven: AHHH! Old man ass!!! Sure enough, Caleb is in the ring mooning Civil Disobedience. Nate: That probably wasn’t the best idea! Masters and Hawke sprint down the ramp towards the little hobo who immediately tries to pull his pants back up and tie his rope around his pants, but the uncoordinated hobo falls hard on the mat chin first. Masters and Hawke are in the ring by now and start to lay the boots into the old man! Robbie: Get up! Steven: Serves him right for showing that damn thing in public! Robbie: Earl! Hurry up and get Cain and Hessian in here! The lights in the arena snap out though and “Carmina Burana” begins to play as the strobe lights flicker throughout the arena. Steven: Too slow Earl! Too Slow! Cain and Hessian sprint down the ramp not waiting for their usual introduction to finish. Both members of Heresian Bloodlust hit the ring and quickly. Cain immediately hits Hawke with a hard Clothesline that drops the challenger to the mat. Before Masters can even turn his head though he is caught with a vicious spear from Hessian. Nate: And here we go! Caleb climbs to his feet very slowly. The hobo is holding his sides in pain, but manages to signal for the bell. Nate: And we are officially under way! Hessian is hitting Masters with a combination of lefts and rights that cause the smaller opponent’s head to bounce off of the mat with each punch. On the other side of the ring, Cain is stomping away at Hawke. Hessian dismounts from Masters and pulls him to his feet. The goliath hoists Masters into the air high over his head only to dump him over the top ropes down onto the concrete floor. Nate: Heresian Bloodlust appears to be in complete control at the moment. Robbie: As it should be! Steven: With Masters out of the ring like that, things don’t look good for Hawke though! LOOK! Cain and Hessian both have a hold of Hawke and manage to hit him with a Double Chokeslam that drives their opponent hard into the mat! Nate: Oh, come on ref! Do something already! Wait a minute! What the hell am I saying? Cain pulls Hawke back up to his feet and grabs hold of the Suburban. Hessian signals for Caleb to come to them. Nate: What are they doing? Caleb pulls his right leg back far only to slam it into the crotch of Hawke causing him to double over in pain immediately. Before ha can hit the ground though the small hobo manages to spit right in Hawke’s face. Nate: This is getting out of control! Robbie: They deserve every bit of this! Hawke is quickly pulled back to his feet by Hessian. The goliath and the marked beast quickly hoist the suburban up into the air again. Robbie: Here it comes! Hawke is planted into the mat again, but this time as a result of a double super crucifix powerbomb from Hessian and Cain. Robbie: HA! BIBLE BASHING!!! Nate: WHAT?! Steven: Um, Nate…that is what Cain and Hessian call that move. Nate: Ahhhh. Back inside the ring Cain immediately rolls Hawke up in a pin. Caleb slides into position to count.1… Nate: This could be it.2… Robbie: HA! That was quick!3…NO!!!! Hawke gets his shoulder up! Nate: How did Hawke manage to get his shoulder up despite that quickcount? Cain rises to his feet and gets into Caleb’s face immediately beginning to scream! Nate: Looks like Cain thinks that that count could have been a little faster. Hessian grabs hold of Cain’s shoulder in an attempt to calm down his partner. Just as Cain and Hessian turn around in order to return their focus on Hawke, but they are greeted by a double low blow from Hawke. The low blow causes both Cain and Hessian to double over in pain. Robbie: That’s not fair! Steven: Here we go!!! Hawke grabs both Hessian and Cain and hits them with a double DDT. Steven: BOCA~!!! Hawke starts to crawl across the mat on his hands in knees trying to get over to Masters. As he crosses the ring though, Caleb starts to kick Hawke as he crawls. Caleb is so busy laying in to Hawk that he doesn’t notice that Masters is on the top ropes now. Nate: It’s Masters! He’s back! Robbie: Dammit!!! Masters flies through the air and hits Caleb with a flying clothesline that crumples the small hobo. Nate: What a clothesline from Masters! Robbie: Oh come on!!! He hit a hobo!!!!! Masters gets back up to his feet and helps Hawke out of the ring before he turns back to his opponents. Masters starts to hit Cain with multiple kicks to the ribs. Cain starts to roll across the ring trying to escape the attack. Nate: The beast better stop rolling in that direction!!! Sure enough, Cain rolls under the ropes and falls hard to the concrete floor. Steven: Well, we all knew he was strong, but we never gave him any credit for his brains! Robbie: Shut Up!!! Masters turns around and rushes at Hessian who is slowly climbing to his feet. As Hessian turns his head though he is caught square in the jaw with a jumping sidekick that topples the goliath. Nate: Masters is certainly fired up for this match. Robbie: It’s all for not! Masters hops up onto the turnbuckle and leaps off only to hit Hessian with a jumping elbow drop into Hessian’s sternum. Masters rises to his feet quickly and starts to work the crowd a little for some cheep pops. Nate: HAAAA! Robbie: Cocky bastard! Masters quickly follows the elbow drop up with a leg drop, but this time no one is home because Hessian managed to roll out of the way. Robbie: Told you he was going to pay. Hessian rolls to his feet slowly, but Masters is up quickly and hits a Russian leg sweep that knocks Hessian down again. Masters goes for an Ankle Lock! Nate: That probably isn’t the smartest thing in the world to do, considering the fact of who the manager is! Robbie: Not only is he cocky, but he’s also a dumbass! Hessian uses his strength to his advantage and rolls over to his back and hits Masters in the chest with a strong kick that sends Masters toppling over onto his back! Robbie: HA! And he’s a wuss too boot! Hessian climbs to his feet and slowly limps over to the corner were Cain is now leaning over the ropes trying to get the tag. Hessian leans and is able to slap his partner in! Robbie: And the beat down is about to commence! Cain rushes across the ring and hits Masters with a spear just as the suburban Bad Ass was getting back to his feet! Cain is quickly on top of Masters and hitting him with lefts and rights! Nate: And the tides have turned in this one as well! Cain continues his barrage until Masters’ face starts to bleed! Cain gets to his feet while pulling Masters up with him. Cain grabs hold of Masters and hits him a vicious powerbomb! Nate: Good God in Heaven! Robbie: HA! Steven: Boca~!!! The crowd erupts into a chorus of boos. Cain straightens himself up again, but he is sent to the mat hard by a leaping dropkick delivered by Hawke! Robbie: Is there no end to their cheating! Nate: Oh come on now! Hawke gets to his feet quickly and stars to lay kicks into Cain’s head until Hawke is sent to the mat hard by a spear from Hessian!!! Nate: And as usual, a Heresian Bloodlust and Civil Disobedience match is resulting in total carnage! Steven: You can’t deny the fact that these two teams absolutely hate each other! Hessian is laying into Hawke, while on the other side of the ring, Cain is being assaulted by Masters! Suddenly, the crowd pops. Steven: LOOOOK!!! Up on the entryway Riptah and Short Circuit are seen rushing down the steel ramp towards the ring! The fans explode in even louder. Nate: DA WESTSIDAZ ARE HERE!!!! THE HAVE BROKEN FREE!!! Robbie: Oh…joy…Da Westsidaz are here… Riptah and Short Circuit hit the ring and immediately start to lay into Hessian and Masters. Shorty is on Hessian’s back trying to choke out the goliath, While Riptah down on Masters with fierce kicks. Nate: This is an all out WAR!!! Caleb is back on his feet now and glances around the ring for a moment. The hobo quickly rushes to the ropes and signals to the official’s table! Robbie: HA!!! Nate: And that’s it! This one is over! Caleb just had this one thrown out! NO CONTEST Steven: OH HELL! I wanted to see some more blood! Nate: Well, it looks like you might get what you are wishing for! Robbie: Look! Inside the ring, Cain just threw Masters out of the ring! Cain rushes at Riptah and spears him off of Hawke. The spear though sent both men through the ropes and crashing to the concrete floor. Robbie: HA!!! Steven: YES!!!!! Hessian is falling to his knees and looks like he is about to pass out until, the chokehold is broken by Caleb smashing one of the title belts off SC’s head. Hawke is rushes at the little hobo, but Caleb manages to see the charging suburban and flees. Nate: And here comes Moose and his boys! The scene shifts to the entryway as Moose Malone and about twelve security goons come rushing down to the ring. Nate: Let’s take it to a commercial break while security tries to break this one up! The scene shifts to the ring where there is absolute pandemonium…then slowly fades to commercial.
