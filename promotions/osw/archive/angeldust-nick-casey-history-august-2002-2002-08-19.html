'Angeldust' Nick Casey — history (August 2002)

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History March 22, 1978. A normal day to anyone else, but the day that Nicholas Casey was brought into the world. He was a very premature baby, and required much care after his birth. It was considered a miracle that he even survived his birth, but soon his childhood came around, and he was just a normal kid. He grew up going to public schools, not really being that rich or that poor, however his father was very religious. He taught Nick everything he knew about religion, and Nick grew up being a devoted Catholic. In high school, Nick wrestled in the two-twelve division, winning three district titles, and one state titles as senior. His other sport was track, which he excelled at more. He ran two events, and did the high jump as well. He medaled in the states in the 200 m dash with a time of 21.1 seconds, and finished 8th in the national 800 m run with a time of 1:53.2. Two very odd events for a man to run, but he did both well. Not only that, but he placed 4th in the nation in high jump as well. He was a very gifted athlete, no doubt, and was offered a scholarship to UCLA, which he took. At UCLA, he continued to have a spectacular athletic record, however while running the half-mile at the national championships, he tripped, and finished last. This was very disappointing for him, as the rain had started to come through. He missed a stop sign, and got t-ed by a pickup truck, causing him to instantly become paralyzed. He was quickly brought to a hospital, where he was pronounced dead at 7:22 PM...however, something on the monitor blipped...Nick was alive again, somehow. The doctors gave him a 1% chance of walking again, and Nick wanted nothing more than to defy those odds. Friends and family helped him to walk, just eight months after being hit by the pickup truck. And exactly one year later, he took his first steps without any help, and he was back to normal. Mentally, he wasn't the same, saying that he had had a conversation with an angel...and it had changed him forever. Almost four years later, Nick graduated with honors from UCLA, and had run in the last race of the season for UCLA track, in a truly remarkable feat. Nick had beaten adversity, and become almost the same athlete that he was four years ago when he was hit by the car. He claimed that an angel had helped him through all of this, and people really didn't care. Nick was Nick again. But what was he to make of all this? He didn't want to be anything else, he just wanted to be. He spent years just living life for the sole purpose of living it, before discovering the sport of wrestling. Nick, being a creative individual, liked the ideas of the angle aspect, and didn't have a problem with the wrestling, so he decided to join this place; MWW. Not the greatest of promotions, but it allowed him for classic wrestling fueds. His matches with people like Gangstar were unforgettable, as blood poured like tears. Not cheap blood from stupid hardcore spots, but simply from giving it all they had, and getting out the next night and doing the same thing. Over, and over again. The same thing with madBlack. His handicapped matches with The Street Thugs. All things that you would watch over and over again, with your jaw touching the floor. But things were unrecognized. He never got a hold of a single title in MWW, even though he was more than deserving. Soon, Angeldust started to take on the more defiant role, screwing up everything the company stood for. He would cold-cock wrestlers for no reason just walking down the hallway. His pretty boy looks didn't match his bad boy attitude. He was finally granted the title match that he so well deserved, against Gangstar. However, it was too late. MWW went bankrupt, and Nick never got his chance to shine. The company owes him over $300,000, and he went back to earn it. Unfortunately, that came the hard way. On the first show, he saved a man by preventing Ethan Ryan Alexander from beating the crap out of him, which ERA didn't like very much. Nick and ERA confronted many times, however, nobody could gain the upper hand. These two spectacular athletes battled time in, and time out, with the score being tied 1-1 with one show left before their first pay-per-view, where the two were set to face off. However, Angeldust never made it to that Reality Check. In fact, he never was even seen. After being locked in Ethan's basement from Christmas until the pay-per-view, Nick obviously had some resentment towards the superstar. The time came closer, however, and Nick was forced to leave his home of so many years after the promotion closed, due to a lack of funding. After being contacted by Mr. Ed, Nick found himself being forced to choose between many prime promotions; EYE Wrestling, Action Wrestling, FEAR, Dynasty, SCCW, and uKw, he finally settled on Old School Wrestling as the most lucritive contract, and the federation most likely to use highly his explosive talents correctly. Through the first few months of his tenure in Old School Wrestling, he has gone up against the likes of Jack Miller, Epitome of Evil, and Vampir Nosfertu. Now he finds himself in a heated feud with the religious zealot Propaganda, where not only his pride, but maybe his first title, is at stake.
