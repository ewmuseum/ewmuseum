Aimz/Timo -vs- Switchblade/Reagan

2002-03-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aimz/Timo -vs- Switchblade/Reagan Written: March 16th, 2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam!! Well, folks, it's time for the main event! Robbie: Also known as, Switchblade and Jane Reagan kicking the crap out of Timo Bolamba. Nate: Let's not forget Timo's mystery partner! Ed booked this match earlier tonight, an intergender tag team matchup -- in fact, the first in OSW history -- and a chance for the Samoan Silencer to get revenge for Crowning Glory! Robbie: Bah. Some people just can't job gracefully. Nate: Tonight, that might apply to our champion! But let's send this one up to Earl Henrich! We cut to the ring, where Earl stands with one hand in his pocket, the mic raised to his lips. Earl: The following match is an intergender tag team matchup! On their way to the ring at this time, they've threatened me with a pay cut if I don't put them over, they are the Owner of Old School Wrestling and the OSW World Heavyweight Champion...SEBASTIAN 'SWITCHBLADE' TOOMES AND JAAAAAAAAANE RRRRREAGAAAAAAN! 'Get to the Gone' by Static-X immediately hits the sound system, and the fans immediately break into a chorus of boos as the First Couple of Old School strut out from the back, arm in arm. Jane leans heavily against her beau, slinking along with one arm waving in mock salute, and the other clamped on Sebo's backside. Steven: *sighing* I envy Jane. For his part, Toomes is holding the heavy OSW Title high overhead, with his other armed wrapped around Jane's waste. Taking their time, the couple finally enter the ring, with Sebastian stepping on the second rope to allow his boss easier entry. The two pose around for a few seconds more, until 'Defy You' by the Offspring finally cuts them off. Earl: And their opponents...first, from the Samoan Islands, weighing in at two hundred and forty-five pounds...TIMO BOLAAAAAAAMBAAAAAAA! The boos turn to cheers as Timo struts out from the back, pumping his arms and gesturing for even more volume. He twists and turns his way down the ramp, pumping up and high-fiving the fans, before finally coming to a stop at the bottom of the ramp. He grins slightly as Switchblade leans against the ropes, taunting him to get in the ring. Earl: And his partner, weighing in at one hundred and forty-three pounds... 'I Stand Alone' by Godsmack hits, and after the moment it takes to realize who it is, half the fans are up on their feet and cheering. The others, not knowing what's going on, figure since everyone else is doing it it must be good, and leap up to join in the screaming. In the ring, Jane's already pale skin tone goes another five shades lighter. Earl: She is a former NTICW superstar...she is... Nate: It can't be... Robbie: Oh, no... Steven: Whee, I'm the same thong size as her... Earl: SHE IS...AAAAAAAAAIIIIIIIMMMMMMMZZZZZZZ-ah! The fans explode, nearly taking the roof off the arena; the commentators mark, sputter, and cheer; Jane begins to tremble violently, while Switchblade tries to calm her down; and Amy Campbell, better known as Aimz, steps out from the back. Nate: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! AIMZ IS HERE! AIMZ IS IN OLD SCHOOL WRESTLING! Robbie: DAMN ED! DAMN HIM! WHY DOES HE KEEP DOING SHIT LIKE THIS?! Steven: LISTEN TO THE CROWD! I DON'T THINK EVEN MEANSTREAK GOT THIS KIND OF REACTION! A massive 'AIMZ!' chant starts up, as Amy Campbell herself grins with excitement and advances down the ring. She high-fives Timo, who returns the favor and shakes her hand. The duo pause for a moment...and then, with a sudden burst of speed, both Timo Bolamba and Amy Campbell dive into the ring and begin laying into their opponents! Nate: AIMZ IS TAKING IT TO JANE! AND TIMO IS GETTING HIS PAYBACK ON SWITCHBLADE! Robbie: FOUL! FOUL! THIS IS FOUL! FOUL BALL, DAMN IT! Timo socks Toomes a few times, then grabs the OSW Champion by the back of the head and hurls him out, right over the top rope, then follows him out. The bell rings as soon as he hits the floor, leaving Aimz and Jane Reagan as the starting combatants. Amy isn't wasting any time, however; after pummelling Jane for a few more seconds, she suddenly grabs the owner of OSW's wrist and pulls forward, flipping Reagan in a tidy Judo flip, and then laying in a few stomps. The crowd is still going nuts about the whole thing. Nate: ED IS GETTING HIS REVENGE! AMY CAMPBELL IS BEATING THE LIVING HELL OUT OF THAT (BLEEPING) WHORE! Steven: FAMILY SHOW! Kicking and wiggling frantically, Jane tries to escape, but just as it seems she's free, Aimz grabs her leg and pulls her back into stomp range. Pierre Gaston walks over and tells Amy to lay off, but doesn't seem particularly upset when Aimz ignores his orders. Cackling in French, he leans against the corner. Robbie: WHAT THE HELL IS PIERRE DOING?! HE'S SUPPOSED TO BE IMPARTIAL! Nate: JANE SHAT ON HIM AT THE PAY PER VIEW, TOO! EVERYONE IN THAT RING WANTS REVENGE ON HER! Outside the ring, Switchblade has apparently crawled off to safety, while Timo prowls around in search of him. As he rounds a corner, however, he's caught off guard -- leaping from the ground with the OSW Title in hand, Sebastian SMASHES the belt into the Samoan's head, knocking him hard to the ground. The Crowd: Oooooooooo!!! Nate: DIRTY MOVE THERE BY THE CHAMP!!! Pierre, too busy watching Jane being physically assaulted to notice the attack, remains blissfully ignorant as Toomes begins raining more belt shots onto Timo's head and shoulders. Nate: DAMN IT! SEBASTIAN TOOMES IS ASSAULTING TIMO WITH AN ILLEGAL WEAPON! Robbie: AND THAT FRENCHIE BASTARD IS TOO BUSY WATCHING JANE'S COURAGEOUS FIGHT TO NOTICE! Courageous is a bit of a misnomer. Clawing desperately at the ring, Jane Reagan tries futiley to drag herself to safety, but just can't escape the wrath of Amy Campbell. Aimz, for her part, seems to be thoroughly enjoying the game, dragging Jane back with hardly any effort and kicking her a few more times before letting her go again. Her game is abruptly cut short, however, as Switchblade dives into the ring and charges her from behind with a HUGE clothesline! Nate: CHEAP SHOT! Steven: And the stomping and kicking wasn't? Nate: AIMZ IS A WOMAN! AND SWITCHBLADE JUST HIT HER WITH EVERY OUNCE OF STRENGTH IN HIS BODY! Robbie: IF SHE WANTS TO BE TREATED LIKE A MAN, WE'LL DO IT! Steven: You'll be we will! She's got a nice ass for a chick! Gloating, basking in the boos of the crowd, Sebastian begins to circle the fallen woman while pumping his arms up and down -- as a result, he's caught completely off balance when Aimz suddenly spins around and takes him down with a spinning kicksweep! The crowd explodes as the stunned Switchblade crashes right down on his jaw, and rolls away with blood running from his mouth. Nate: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! AMY CAMPBELL IS STILL IN THERE FIGHTING! AND SHE BUSTED SWITCHBLADE WIDE OPEN! Steven: Actually, he just bit his tongue. Nate: SHUT UP AND GET OVERLY EXCITED LIKE US! Clutching his face in sheer agony, Sebo slowly gets to his feet, stumbling and bleary. Grinning cockily, Aimz gives an imitation of Jane Reagan's 'thumbs up', then bounces off the ropes and leaps at Switchblade with a hurricanrana -- which is suddenly reversed into a HUGE powerbomb! Nate: NOOOOO! Robbie: HAH! AS IF SOME WOMAN COULD DEFEAT THE OSW CHAMPION! Steven: Pssh! Yeah! As IF! This time, stunned by a move that would have dazed even Meanstreak, Aimz lies still on the ground and twitches slightly. Sebastian, laughing, reaches into his mouth and pulls out a small packet covered with red paste and, with a flourish, wipes off a bit of it to reveal the 'Heinz' logo! Nate: WHAT THE--?! THAT'S NOT BLOOD! THAT'S KETCHUP! Robbie: THE MASTER MANIPULATOR STRIKES AGAIN! HAH! GENIUS-LEVEL IQ MY ASS! SHE'S AS DUMB AS EVERY OTHER PIECE OF T&A IN THE WRESTLING WORLD! Steven: You're just trying to give her promo material, aren't you? Robbie: *with a verbal shrug* Hey, Old School carries on the proud tradition of monthly bonuses for commentators. Still laughing at his own intelligence, Toomes drops to make the cover...and is suddenly kicked in the abdomen by Jane Reagan! Nate: Huh?! Jane begans yelling at her beau, and finally points to what she finds particularly offensive -- one of Switchblade's hands positioned directly over Amy's breasts. He immediately removes the offending appendage, and again drops for the cover; Pierre Gaston begins the count. 1... 2... 3-- NO! The fans explode as, under her own power, Aimz kicks out of the pin with such force that the off-balance and suprised Switchblade is tossed right onto his back! Nate: MY GOD! I DON'T BELIEVE IT! AIMZ JUST KICKED OUT OF THAT POWERBOMB, AND FLIPPED A MAN WHO OUTWEIGHS HER BY OVER ONE HUNDRED POUNDS ON HIS BACK! Shocked, Toomes stands back up, then drops into a preperatory crouch as Amy begins struggling to her feet, pointing a shaky yet determined finger at the now-cackling OSW Champion. Nate: WHAT A FIGHTER! AMY CAMPBELL IS STILL INTENT ON TAKING SWITCHBLADE DOWN! Robbie: Heh. Yeah, right. As Aimz finally pulls herself up with the ropes, Sebastian hops back, takes a step forward, sets up for a superkick -- AND IS SUDDENLY NAILED FROM BEHIND WITH A HURRICANE DDT BY TIMO BOLAMBA! The crowd explodes. Nate: YES! Robbie: NO! Steven: BOCA! Switchblade hits the mat right on his skull and bounces away, before collapsing in a heap with his ass sticking up in the air and his head buried in his own chest. Steven, as you'd expect, is cheering loudly at the sight, but we ignore him. Instead, most of us focus on Pierre Gaston, who's currently telling Timo to get out of the ring. The Samoan Silencer resists for a moment, but finally complies, leaving a disoriented Aimz and a crumpled Toomes in the ring. Robbie: Well, at least Pierre knows how to call SOME things down the middle. Nate: And since when has he not? Robbie: Oh, I dunno... *cough*beginningofthematch*cough* Steven: You should see a doctor about that. Over a minute passes, but Amy seems to finally figure out where she is at about the same time Switchblade uncurls himself and begins crawling towards the ropes. Stumbling, Aimz glances at the OSW Champion as if considering an attack, then shrugs and begins limping towards her partner, holding her sore back. Toomes, meanwhile, drags himself on his elbows towards Jane Reagan, who for some reason seems very anxious to get back in the fight. Then, after an agonizing 'what if they don't make it?' moment, both Aimz and Sebo make the tags at the same time, and Timo Bolamba and Jane Reagan leap into the ring! Nate: AND HERE COMES THE SAMOAN! Robbie: BAH! HERE COMES THE OWNER! AND SHE'S BOUND TO HAVE SOMETHING UP HER SLEEVE! Steven: She's not wearing-- Robbie & Nate: SHUT UP! Indeed, it would seem that way, as Jane charges head-on into the oncoming Samoan. Then, at the last moment, she drops to the ground and wraps her legs around Timo's ankles, tripping him up in a textbook drop toe hold! Robbie: DOWN GOES THE (BLEEP)!!! Nate: I HATE TO ADMIT IT, BUT A NICE MOVE BY JANE REAGAN! Steven: Whoah, where did she learn that?! Nate: SHE'S BEEN MARRIED TO A FORMER PRO WRESTLING! SHE'S PICKED UP A FEW MOVES! Steven: Former pro...? I thought she was married to the Phantom Booker! Robbie: You're an idiot, Steve. By this time, Timo and Jane are both standing again, and the Samoan makes another charge which is met by a second drop toe hold! This time, however, he sees it coming; instead of losing his balance, he throws himself down, grabbing her leg and, in the blink of an eye, twisting it into a figure four leg lock! Nate: HA! JANE JUST GOT REVERSED INTO A FIGURE FOUR! Steven: AND SHE'S USED TO A SPREAD EAGLE! Nate: Nice one, Steve. Steven: *probably sticking his tongue out at Robbie* And you called me an idiot. The Owner of Old School Wrestling begins screaming in pain and clawing towards the ropes, showing suprising resilience in the face of such a painful submission. In the process, her loose gym pants are pulled down, revealing her hot-pink thong, as well as her shapely posterior. The male fans erupt into a huge cheer. Robbie: I...I...I should be pissed, but... Nate: Don't YOU start drooling over that two dollar whore, now. Suddenly, with a desperate burst of strength, Switchblade leaps right over the top ropes and drives his elbows hard into Timo's ribs! The Crowd: Oooooooooooo!!! The Samoan breaks the hold and rolls away, taking his time rising, and leaving everyone in the match but Aimz lying in the ring. Noticing this fact, a slow grin crosses her face, and the camera zooms in as she slowly, achingly scales the turnbuckle and crouches, ready to jump! Nate: This might be it! We might be looking at the Dead Aim! Robbie: I'd like to make a comment about a 'dead Aimz' being better, but between her see-through shirt and the boss' bare ass, I just can't. Steven: I will, then! It'd be better if we were looking at a dead Aimz! Unfortunately, one of her two potential targets is already standing; Sebastian Toomes, weary and battered, leans against the ropes, gasping for air. Nearbye, Timo Bolamba, apparently more injured than it seems he'd be, bends over with his hands on his knees, breathing hard. Nate: Amy's on the top rope, and the only target she has left is Jane Reagan! Robbie: Bah! It'll miss! Watch! Aimz flexes her legs slightly, falls into a crouch...just then Switchblade stumbles his way over in an attempt to protect the downed Regan. Aimz looks for a moment, knowing she can not risk the jump….suddenly Timo spins out of his own crouch to SLAM Switchblade in the chest with a HUGE Cave-In that levels the champion where he stands. The crowd explodes. Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!1 Nate: BOTH SWITCHBLADE AND JANE ARE DOWN!!!! Timo scrambles to the top rope and he and Aimz look at one another then nod. Nate: WHAT ARE THEY GOING TO DO?!?! Simultaneously, both Aimz and Timo leap from their perches on opposite sides of the ring…as flashbulbs go off around the arena. Aimz springs high into the air, flipping a 450 splash and SMASHING into the prone Jane Reagan's chest...while Timo delivers the FIST FROM THE HEAVENS to Toomes in unison. The fans erupt as both Timo and Aimz cover their foes in the center, and Pierre Gaston counts the 1... 2... 3! WINNERS: Timo Bolamba and Aimz The crowd absolutely explodes as both Timo and Aimz hop to their feet in celebration, and Jane and Switchblade slowly take the losers roll out of the ring, to the floor at ringside. Nate: (sceaming over the fans) I DON'T BELIEVE IT! I DON'T BELIEVE IT! TIMO AND AIMZ HAVE WON A CLEAN VICTORY OVER THE OSW WORLD CHAMPION AND JANE REAGAN! Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Nate: IT DOESN'T MATTER! WITHOUT ANY OUTSIDE HELP, WITHOUT ANY OUTSIDE HINDRANCE, TIMO BOLAMBA AND AMY CAMPBELL HAVE JUST SCORED A VICTORY OVER THAT DIRTY WHORE AND HER BOY TOY! Robbie: THIS ISN'T RIGHT! WHERE WERE THE INTERFERENCES?! WHERE WERE THE RUN-INS?! WHERE THE HELL WERE THE USUAL ASPECTS OF AN OSW MAIN EVENT?! Nate: NO IDEA, BUT WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Suddenly, there's a massive, loud creak from the ceiling above. Nate: WHAT THE HELL?!?!?! In the ring, Aimz and Timo look up...JUST AS THE LIGHT RIGGINGS FROM THE ROOF COME CRASHING TOWARDS TO THE RING! Nate: LOOOOOOOOOK OUTTT!!!!! With barely a split second to lose, Campbell and the Samoan dive out through the ropes, as the heavy riggings smash through the ring, splintering and cracking behind them. Toomes, carrying Jane Reagan in his arms as he stumbles away, gapes at the destruction. Robbie: WHAT THE HELL JUST HAPPENED?! Nate: THE LIGHT RIGGINGS JUST FELL OUT OF THE SKY AND DESTROYED THE RING -- AND ALMOST TIMO AND AIMZ! WE CAN NOT HOLD THIS FEED ANY LONGER!!!! THANK GOD NO ONE WAS HURT!!! Robbie: THIS TIME!!! Cameras focus on the mass of twisted steel and wood that now clutter the area where the ring once was, while Timo Bolamba and Aimz hold each other up near the announce table in sheer shock at what they have just witnessed…..as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears in the lower, central portion of the screen…..as the scene fades to black…..or does it? Oddly, the small silver, now somewhat familiar to OSW fans, I-Cybie robotic dog, walks into the black shot, from off camera right, into the center of the shot. It pauses for a moment, and its mechanical head suddenly turns and faces the camera. Its red “eyes light up, and its plastic tail wags as it mechanically yips twice. The camera zooms in on the glowing red eyes of the small, Japanese made mechanical pooch…..as the feed abruptly and disturbingly cuts to static.
