After a second or two, Corg pulls out a table and sets it up near the fallen Locke. Nate: Corg is not done yet! Corg pulls the bloodied Locke up and places him on the table. Corg then whaps the unconscious Locke across the face one more time with the chair, but this time he leaves the chair resting across Locke's face. Nate: Security!!! Steven: What is Corg doing on top of the turnbuckle? Robbie: This should be interesting!!! Corg leaps from the turnbuckle and lands a flawless leg drop on top of the chair that rest on Locke's face! The weight from Corg's move drives Locke's body through the table and crashing to the concrete floor with the silent assassin on top of him. Nate: Dear God in heaven!! Robbie: HA!!! Steven: BOCA~!!! Crowd: OSW!!! OSW!!! OSW!!! Corg rises to his feet and starts screaming at the out cold Locke who still has the chair over his face. After a few moments, Corg ceases his verbal barrage as EMT's coming running to the aid of the injured continental champ. Corg starts to walk up the steel ramp gazing upon the damage that he has just inflicted. Nate: This is insane! We better go to a break while the medics tend to Locke! We'll be back in a few! Nate: Welcome back to Slam! We are just about ready to head to the ring for our Main… Abruptly, "Food for the Brain" by One Minute Silence screeches across the speakers, interrupting Nate mid-sentence. The fans erupt as the instrumentals come together, just as Ed, Commissioner and acting CEO of OSW, steps out from behind the curtains. Nate: IT'S ED!! Robbie: Whoo, there go the ratings. Nate: You do realize he signs your paychecks now, right? Robbie: Erm...YEAH! GO EDDIE!! Steven: I LOVE YOU, EDDIE! High-fiving the fans as usual, Ed takes his time getting into the ring, and grabs a mic from the nearest crew member once he does. He waits a moment for the cheers to die down, and then lifts both his arm and his mic. Ed: Well, before I get started, I have something to say to you all... HEY-LO, FLINT, MICHIGAN! On cue, the fans mark out for the blatant, cheap pop. Ed: ARE YOU ENJOYING SATURDAY NIGHT SLAM?!?! Another huge pop; good crowd tonight, obviously into the show and the acting CEO. Nate: JUST LISTEN TO THAT REACTION FOR OUR ACTING CEO!! Ed allows the face heat to cool down, then lifts the mic again and continues with a grin. Ed: I'd like to welcome and thank you all for showing up; I hope everyone in section C9 enjoys their autographed Civil Disobedience posters! The cameras cut to a section of the crowd, where the fans are cheering wildly and waving large, glossy pictures of Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters. Ed: And, of course, it'd be great if all the kids in the arena were having fun with their Timo Bolamba action figures... This time, there's a loud, albeit high-pitched pop from each and every youth of age 10 and under; our view focuses on the little plastic replicas of the Samoan Silencer each one waves around. Nate: What generosity! Ed has used his new powers as the CEO to act as some kind of battlescarred Santa Claus! Robbie: He's bribing the audience for cheers. How freaking pathetic. Ed: But, down the business. And not the business of running a major federation; I'll have a special announcement on that later. The business I'm talking about is in-ring. In-combat. In-tense. Robbie groans. Ed: Tommorrow night, at Head On Collision, I'll be bashing my thick skull up against Meanstreak's. The match isn't about a title, or a girlfriend, or even a personal hatred. Tommorrow night will be about one thing: destruction. He pauses, pacing to the other side of the ring. Ed: Yeah, I know how cheesy that sounds, but bear with me. When I brought Meanstreak to Old School Wrestling, he was supposed to take care of Ivan -- he was a bad guy back then, remember? Well, he didn't. Instead, he took care of Tempest, and Aimz, and Timo, and anyone else who got in his way. He's returned to his persona of an unstoppable beast, and as countless efforts have shown, that beast can't be brought to submission. Ed's eyes narrow, and his voice drops into a suprisingly dark and ominous tone. Ed: It can only be destroyed. Another pause, and he's pacing again. Ed: What do you do with a rabid dog? You don't tranquilize it and hope it doesn't hurt anyone; you kill it. That's exactly what has to be done with the Monster they call Meanstreak, and that's exactly what I'm going to do. He grins, brightening his demeanor a bit, but then resumes his frown. Ed: Of course, I'm not going to physically destroy him. That's borderline suicidal, right there. I'm going to get inside his head and mindf*ck him. Somewhere, buried in his past, Meanstreak has a secret -- a weakness, a failing, an Achille's heel -- and I'm going to find it. I'm well on my way already; I've only got one piece of the puzzle left to track down, although the most recent tape hasn't been released yet. Once I find what I need, Streak will be slain, in mind if not in body. And that, I'd say, is a lot worse. Once again, he stops, paces a bit, and then lifts the mic. Ed: But, hey, that's enough depressing talk from the Hardcore Legend. Enjoy the rest of Slam, folks; and remember, with me as the acting CEO...this show has "it", baby! "Food for the Brain" hits again as Ed leaves back up the ramp, accompanied by a loud cheer. Nate: Well, the Commish and now acting CEO has spoken!! We need to head to a commercial! When we return, we will head to the ring for our main event of the evening!!! Amy Campbell was partnerless...searching diligently through the locker-rooms of the GM center. The match was moments away and she still had no partner...she was asking people left, right and center but all of the free wrestlers seemed to be on the drive to the pay-per-view slated for the next evening. Eventually, she spun around a corner only to knock into Reno Davis...the redheaded 'punk', someone who could easily be considered her fraternal twin. Aimz: Reno! Hey man... Davis appeared quite confused by the suddenly warm reception. Reno: Uhh...hey. They stood in silence for a moment...Reno with an obvious 'What the hell is going on here?' expression until Campbell spoke up again in explanation. Aimz: See...I've got this pretty...umm...large match tonight...........against Bones and Jobstyle. Since they're all, you know...four times my weight and such...I was wondering if you'd mind being the heavy counterpart for this evening? Hehe. Reno: Uh, well I sort of... He was quickly interrupted...the little redhead wasn't about to take no for an answer. Aimz: Ah, good stuff! The match is up next...get your gear on, man! Reno: Wait I never...*Aimz runs away waving* Shit... It was too late, Aimz was already bouncing down the hallway toward the stage area...apparently we'd see Reno Davis fight tonight, after all….or maybe not. Nate: Welcome back to Slam! Well, folks, our main event is about to get underway! Robbie: Jesus, don't you know any other words? How about "started"? "The match is about to get started." Steven: How about, "the match is about to get hot and--" Steve is abruptly cut off by the thrumming bass of DMX's "Who We Be." The crowd immediately begins booing as two thirds of 3B (the two-thirds not banging Jane Reagan) strut down the ramp: Frankie Bones and Drew Hostyle. Each man walks with a confident gait; hey, they're fighting a woman. Nate: Bones and Hostyle look pretty confident! Robbie: And with good reason! Nate: What good reason? Robbie: No idea. Nate: Well, these two gentlemen will be squaring off with none other than Amy "Aimz" Campbell and the ever-mysterious TBA. "Across the Nation" by Union Underground hits, and the fans cheer hesitantly, not recognizing the music. The volume increases dramatically as Amy steps out from the back, and walks down the ramp with a casual ease, though her eyes remain intensely focused on the grinning Drew Hostyle. Nate: She's staring holes through Drew's head! Robbie: Yeah, and she's sucking holes in it, too. Steven: Eww...I don't want to think about a woman on Drew's dark meat. Suddenly, "Rocket Science" by the Pimps hits, and the crowd explodes as Reno Davis, not even waiting for a dramatic point in the music, sprints down the ramp. Nate: RENO DAVIS IS AIMZ’ MYSTERY PARTNER!!! He high fives Amy as he passes, dives into the ring, and immediately NAILS Frankie Bones with a dropkick that flips him right over the top rope! Drew gapes like a fish as Reno follows Bones down, and slowly turns around to find himself facing an evilly-grinning Amy Campbell. Nate: Run, Drew. Run. Aimz raises her left hand swiftly, drawing Drew's attention, and suddenly NAILS him with a right hook! Hostyle stumbles backwards, holding his jaw and looking incredulously at the woman...who replies with a slight shrug before SMASHING him with a left cross! Steven: Jesus, fight like a woman and SLAP him! You're hurting his beautiful face! Drew steps back and shakes his head, trying to clear out the stars...and then lunges forward, trying to wrap Aimz in a bear hug! Amy's too fast for him, however, and ducks underneath the move to respond with a sharp elbow to the spine. As Hostyle arches his back in pain, Campbell whips around, nailing him with a MASSIVE kick to the crook of the neck! Nate: TIIIIMBEEEER!! Like a fallen tree, Drew hits the mat, and rolls over. Aimz grins for a moment, then drops to cover... 1... 2... 3-- NO! At the last second, Frankie Bones leans in and grabs Amy'z leg, pulling her off of Drew's chest. The big man offers her a malicious smile...just before Reno comes off the apron with a HUGE running moonsault!! Nate: WHAT A MOVE BY RENO DAVIS!! Robbie: Someone make the damned ref get them in their corners! Nate: This isn't a tag team match! This is a preview of tommorrow night's "I Lied" matchup, at Head On Colission! Steven: There are entirely too many women on that card. Amy gets back to her feet, offering a few parting words to Bones, and leisurely turns around...right into a HUGE tackle by Drew Hostyle! Campbell hits the mat hard, and Drew immediately capitalizes, pulling her to her feet. He grins at her dazed expression, and begins making kissy lips towards her. Nate: Drew is just asking to be hurt! Robbie: Bah, that egotistical wench needs to learn her place. Suddenly, Aimz pulls backwards, breaking free of Hostyle's embrace, leaps up, wraps her legs around the man's throat, and NAILS him with a hurricanrana! The fans erupt as Amy stands up and heads for the top rope, while Drew groans and rolls around below. She signals to the crowd, prepares to leap...and is suddenly hurled off the top turnbuckle by Sebastian "Switchblade" Toomes! Nate: WHAT?! WHAT'S HE DOING HERE?! Robbie: Helping his friends, obviously...not that Drew needs it. Toomes dives into the ring, ignoring the angry admonitions of the referee, and begins laying in the boots to the helpless and dazed Campbell. Immediately, the bell rings, and Earl steps up with a microphone. Earl: The winners of this match, via disqualification...AMY CAMPBELL AND RENO DAAAAAAVIIIIIIIS!! The fans are too busy booing to care, however, as Drew and Toomes begin double teaming the red-haired woman with a series of stomps and kicks. The bell continues ringing off-and-on, but neither man seems interested in leaving poor Aimz alone. Outside, Frankie Bones finally seems to be taking control of his fight with the much smaller Davis; we see him run by with Reno's head palmed, and SMASH it into the guard rail, knocking the little man out. Not yet satisfied, Bones kicks his opponent hard in the side, and crawls into the ring, one hand cupped over a bloody forehead. Seeing the others, he walks over to join in the fun. Nate: THIS ISN'T RIGHT! WHERE'S SECURITY?! AMY CAMPBELL IS BEING MAULED BY 3B, AND NO ONE'S TRYING TO SAVE HER!! And then, the Russian National Anthem hits, and the entire arena thunders with cheers. Ivan Stanislav, leading Tom Walczak and Micheal Krieg, sprints down the ramp, sliding under the ropes and smashing like an unstoppable tidal wave against the three members of 3B. Walczak and Drew quickly take their battle to the outside, while Krieg and Bones pummel eachother into a corner. Ivan, however, merely stands nose-to-nose with Toomes, in the center of the squared circle. Nate: THIS COULD GET UGLY!! Steven: NOT WITH SEBBY IN THE RING! The two men continue staring at eachother, their breathing slow and heavy, their fists clenched. Then, abruptly, Toomes goes for a kick to the groin. Ivan easily catches the blow, and tosses Sebastian right on his back. Sebo rolls away, trying to stand, but he takes too long. With one massive blow to the gut, Ivan doubles the smaller man over, lifts him high...AND RED SCARES HIM RIGHT OVER THE TOP ROPE, THROUGH THE ANNOUNCERS' TABLE!!! Robbie: SHIT!! The commentators dive for cover as Toomes crashes through their desk, rolling away from the wreckage as he lands to lie in a crumpled heap below. In the ring, Ivan raises his fists to the sky, while the fans cheer with incredible volume...until another hulking figure lunges out of the crowd, dives into the ring, and stands behind the Russian Bear like some kind of hell-bent demon. Nate: WHAT THE...I CAN'T TELL FROM THIS ANGLE, BUT I THINK IVAN'S GOT A HUGE PROBLEM!! Robbie: AND THE MONSTER THEY CALL MEANSTREAK WILL SOLVE IT!! Then, just as Ivan turns around, he lunges forward...AND IS CUT OFF BY A RUNNING DROPKICK FROM THE ACTING CEO, ED!! Nate: AND NOW EDDIE IS HERE!! THIS MATCH HAS TURNED INTO A COMPLETE CLUSTERF*CK!! Steven: *bored* Just another day at the office. Ed doesn't stop to punch, apparently learning from past mistakes, and isntead circles around as the Monster leaps to his feet and looks around for his attacker. Nate: I CAN FEEL IT!! BRINK OF INSANITY TIME!!
