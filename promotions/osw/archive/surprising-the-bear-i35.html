Surprising the Bear

2002-02-09
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Surprising the Bear Written: Feb 9, 2002 Suddenly, Nate’s words are interrupted as the Soviet National Anthem booms over the sound system and the area lights dim to a deep and dark red. Nate: Oh jeez….Here comes The Red Army! Steven: I think that beard really suits Ivan! Robbie: That’s because Ivan is a man’s man, not some sissy like you or Doug Troy! The camera shifts to the entryway as the massive Soviet, Ivan Stanislav, emerges from the backstage area with Alexei Ruslan in tow. The two men make their way down to the ring and following behind them in equal spaces apart are Tom Walczak, Communist Party Member Wang, Referee Speedy Riggs, and Wang’s Two Round Faced Aids. Stanislav and Ruslan climb into the ring and instantly walk over to ring announcer Earl Henrich. Henrich offers Ivan the microphone, which he takes, and turns to leave… Stanislav grabs Henrich by the back of the jacket and lifts him up easily in the air. He walks over to the announce position and dumps Henrich onto the announce table FROM the ring! Nate: INCOMING!!! Commotion is heard at the announce position as order is attempted to be restored. The communists finally all make it to the ring. Riggs, The Two Round Faced Women, and Wang all perch on a turnbuckle. Stanislav stands in the center of the ring with Tom Walczak to his left and Alexei Ruslan to his right. The national anthem of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics dies down as the roar and the booing of the crowd is left in its place. Nate: That bastard! I have half a mind to go up there and show him what a real wrestler is made of!!!! Robbie: And you have the other half telling you to stay put! Let him do what he wants! It’s Reagan’s fault that he’s here! Steven: I wouldn’t blame Ms. Reagan!! Robbie: It’s her fault that you’re here too! Can I get a witness brotha Nathan! Nate: Amen to that!!! Back in the ring, Ivan brings the microphone to his lips and sneers at the crowd. His breathing can be heard as he looks around, his eyes darting around the room. Stanislav walks to the left and begins to speak…. Ivan: I would like you all to know, first of all, that the OSW is under a communist sphere of influence…. Nate: Oh give me a break. Boo’s continue to erupt throughout the arena, then some kind of chant begins to form. Robbie: Drunken Philly fans, listen to them! They can not even form a chant. God for bid they attempt the wave!!! Nate: What are they chanting? While the crowd tries to get its act together, Ivan continues. Ivan: Because whether Jane Reagan is willing to admit it or not, she begged me to come to the OSW, and I have agreed. However, I have only come here under my own terms, which I will make sure are followed to the full letter of our agreement! And with that in mind, I would also like you all to know that this “Resistance” that was put up against us in the form of Doug Troy and Trashcan Man…. Ivan is interrupted as the crowd’s chant finally comes together. The Crowd: Virgin!!! Virgin!!! Virgin!!! Virgin!!! Virgin!!! In the ring, Ivan’s face turns bright read and he lets out a an enraged howl. Nate: HAH!!!! Robbie: All lies spread by the Capitialistic Media!! LIES!!! Wang tries to calm down Stanislav as Walczak and Ruslan begin shouting out at the fans in the front row. Nate: If it wasn’t true, why is he growing so enraged?!?! Steven: If he’s still a virgin, I am sure I could arrange for… Robbie: (interrupting Steven) Finish that statement, and I will see to it a video tape of this telecast is waiting for Mr. Stanislav after the show!! The sound of Steven swallowing hard can be heard in the background. In the ring, Ivan, though still seething, raises his microphone and continues. Ivan: For this act of insolence, I will see to it your beloved American heroes, Doug Troy and Trashcan Man will be put into intensive care at the local hospital after I crush their skulls like aging pumpkins in January!!! Nate: Aging pumpkins in January? Robbie: I am sure it loses something in the translation. Ivan stops back in the center of the ring. Ivan: What you people see before you is the collective might of all of wrestling! Announcers, referee’s, managers, and wrestlers, all come together to further a fair and just cause! Ivan laughs loudly and points backstage. Ivan: Doug Troy and Trashcan Man were the only two men that could stand up against us, and within one week they both have become nothing more than fodder for our plans! The rest of the roster had better understand the severity of situation, and join the ranks of Red Army before I start eliminating the… Suddenly, Ivan is cut off in midword as 'Food for the Brain' by One Minute Silence hits the speakers. Nate: What the heck?!?! As the music crashes together, pyros go off on either side of the ramp, repeat with each blast of the bass, and then the arena lights come up. Holding his steel-barbed wire-nail chair up high, half-silhouetted in the yellow smoke, is none other then PCW Hall of Famer and Hardcore Legend, Ed. Nate: THAT’S ED!!!! WHAT THE HELL IS HE DOING OUT HERE?!?! Robbie: Killing ratings, that’s what he is doing!!! Steven: He’s got IT baby!!! After the moment it takes the crowd to realize who it is, the explosion of sound is deafening. In the ring, Ivan gazes up at Ed, a man he forced into retirement due to injury, in seething rage. The blond haired Ed returns his gaze, with equal intensity, then pulls out a microphone from the back pocket of his jeans. Nate: Looks like Ed has something to say!! Ivan tries to scream into his microphone, but it would appear his microphone audio is cut off, which serves to only further enrage the 400-pound Russian. Robbie: And the Russians are accused of censorship!!! Steven: Shhhhhh!! I want to hear what Eddie has to say!!! Ed smiles out at the crowd, then winks at a young girl at ringside before finally speaking. Ed: HEY-LO, OSW! The crowd explodes and begins to chant. The Crowd: ED!!! ED!!! ED!!! ED!!! ED!!! Nate: Listen to that crowd!!!! Robbie: Bah! It’s the alcohol talking!!! The camera zooms in on PCW Hall of Famer, as a small tear of happiness runs down the side of his cheek. It's a bit better of a reaction than he expected, apparently. Steven: It's nice to see pro wrestlers showing a bit of their feminine side... Nate: Watch it. The noise begins to die down, but Ed doesn't let it. Instead, as soon as he's even slightly audible over the noise, he shouts at the top of his lungs... Ed: I SAID, HEY-LO OSW~! The roaring is deafening now, and Ed gives a broad, happy smile as he takes in the applaude. Ivan, in the ring, growls something angrily at Alexei, who shrugs helplessly. Obviously pissed, the Russian Bear storms over to the ropes and yanks another microphone out of the hands of a nearby crew member -- who doesn't realize he should let go until he's four feet in the air. Not even noticing, Ivan turns back. Ivan: WHAT do you think you are doing?! This is IVAN STANISLAV’S TIME!!! Ed smiles, bites his lower lip, then shakes his head in disbelief. Ed: Hey, Ivy, guess what? IT ISN'T EVER GONNA BE YOUR TIME! Nate: WHOA!!!! Another incredible cheer, as the big Communist stands with mouth open, resembling a fish, and Ed struggles to hold back an excited chuckle. Ed: You know, I wasn't supposed to be out here until after you finished your rambling, 'I will conquer Old School and then Primetime!' speech, but I'm just too nice a guy to subjugate these fans to that kinda torture. The Russian Bear barks harshly out at Ed. Nate: Ed is treading on dangerous ground here. Its well known his back is in no condition to facilitate a physical altercation with anyone, let alone the likes of Stanislav. Robbie: All the more reason Ivan should put him out of his misery. May the strong survive and the weak be left to the vultures!! Ed: You have no idea how much I'd like to run down that ramp and kick your ass -- me and you, mano e mano, with no Clyde or Boda to save you -- but the doc says, I've got some more rehab to do first. Besides, Trashy and Doug are more than enough to take you down, not to mention the amazing talent that is the young OSW roster! Another loud cheer, this one more of a cheap pop. Ed: No, I can't fight you, but I can do the next best thing. I've got a surprise in store for you -- you and these fans -- and you are not going to like it. Nate: A surprise? Steven: I love it when Boca gives me surprises… Robbie: I’d love it if you would you would shut the hell up! On the ramp, Ed continues. Ivan: Well, get on with it!! Ed: Do NOT interrupt me, Ivan, because ecause you are looking at someone with the power to punish you severely. You see, the all of the front office positions have been announced, save one. A fairly important job, booking matches, setting up interviews, making wrestlers' lives either Heaven or Hell... Nate: WHOA!?!? WHAT IS HE TALKING ABOUT!?!? The crowd again begins to mumble in excitement. We cut to a little kid in the crowd, holding a 'Return of 'The Mack!'' sign in one hand, and an 'I told you so' sign in the other, which is aimed towards a rather stunned-looking group of other kids. Ed continues… Ed: What's that they call it? I forget...let's spell it out. Gimme a C! Gimme an O! Gimme an M! Gimme an ISSONER! What's it spell?! There's a huge intake of breath, as if the fans thing they're supposed to reply, but Ed beats them to the punchline. Ed: EDWARD JOHNATHAN ED, COMMISSIONER OF OLD SCHOOL WRESTLING! Nate: WHAT?! Steven: YOU HEARD HIM! EDDIE'S THE COMMISH! EDDIE'S THE COMMISH! The explosion of energetic cheering damned near collapses the arena, and the ropes are visibly shaking in the ring. The Russian Bear and his cohorts stare in disbelief, covering their ears against the sound. The roaring continues for nearly a minute before it finally dies down to a level where the wrestlers can be heard over it, and Ivan Stanislav takes this time to regain his composure. Ivan: Is this all? You threaten me with the power to make matches? Ivan is not intimidated by this! Ivan-- Ed: YOU HAVE TWO SECONDS TO SHUT UP BEFORE YOU GET BOOKED IN A 12-ON-1 SAMURAI DEATHMATCH! Ivan: Do not presume to threa-- Ed: ONE! Ivan: And what IS a Samu-- Suddenly the camera pans out to reveal Trashcan Man and Doug Troy slide into the ring, having stealthily made their way through the crowd. They come up behind the big man with a double trashcan shot to the back of the head! Alexei stares in disbelief for a moment too long, and finds himself doubled over, lifted, and PLANTED WITH A PYRODRIVER INTO A DENTED GARBAGE CAN! Nate: DOUG TROY AND TRASHCAN MAN!!! Steven: IT'S AN AMBUSH! EDDIE HAS SET IVAN AND ALEXEI UP! A dazed Ivan, dropped to his knees by the double shot, slowly begins to stand...and is SMASHED INTO THE OTHER CAN WITH THE TAKE! As quickly as the pair hit the ring, Trashcan Man and DT slide out of the ring, out of the reach of The Polish Punisher, Tom Walczak. Meanwhile Wang and his assistants check on Ruslan and Stanislav!!! Nate: (barely audible over the cheering) THE ROOF IS COMING OFF THIS PLACE!!! The Hardcore Legend watches it all from the ramp, then raises the mic again and laughs. Ed: You've got enemies in high places now, Ivy, and this one is booking you in a match, next Saturday night on Slam against the ever so infamous MYSTERY OPPONENT!!! Nate: ED HAS A MYSTERY OPPONENT LINED UP FOR IVAN NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT?!?! WHO THE HELL COULD IT BE?!?! Robbie: Who cares?!? Ivan will simply break their back and toss their limp corpse to the wolves!!! Ed: Oh, and Ivan…not only am I the commissioner, but I am the commissioner with IT, baby!!!! The crowd explodes as Ed throws down his microphone and 'Food for the Brain' by One Minute Silence hits the PA system. Nate: WHAT A WAY TO START THE NIGHT!!! ED IS THE COMMISSIONER OF OLD SCHOOL WRESTLING AND HAS BOOKED IVAN STANISLAV IN A MATCH WITH A MYSTERY OPPONENT FOR NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT!!! DOUG TROY AND TRASHCAN MAN HAVE STRUCK THE FIRST BLOW AGAINST THE RED ARMY HERE TONIGHT!!! Robbie: Which spells doom for this company!!! Nate: WE NEED TO HEAD TO A COMMERCIAL!!! WE WILL BE RIGHT BACK RIGHT AFTER THESE MESSAGES!
