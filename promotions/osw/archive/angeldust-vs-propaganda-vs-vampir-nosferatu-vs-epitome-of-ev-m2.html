'Angeldust' vs. Propaganda vs. Vampir Nosferatu vs. Epitome Of Evil

2002-03-30
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Angeldust' vs. Propaganda vs. Vampir Nosferatu vs. Epitome Of Evil Written: March 30, 2002 Nate: Now that that is settled, let’s send it up to ring announcer Earl Henrich in the ring, who is about to announce the principles for our next contest. 'No More Pain' by 2Pac hits, and Epitome of Evil walks down to the ring, giving the audience an apathetic glare. RA: The following contest is a Four Corners Elimination Match! The first competitor, hailing from Dallas, Texas… Weighing in at 345 pounds...EPITOME OF EVIL!!! Nate: Welcome back, folks, and you're in for a helluva match! Four Corners Elimination match! Vampir Nosferatu, Propaganda, Nick Casey, and this man right here, Epitome of Evil! Robbie: I am wowed by EoE's confidence here. Just look at the way he struts down to the ring, like a confident warrior, ready to bitch-slap some rookie punks... Nate: I believe that's called arrogance. Robbie: No, that's what weak people call confident people. Weak people like you. Nate: Well, I'm confident I'll kick your ass if you keep it up. Steven: Personally, I don't care either way. I'm awfully horny though. Robbie: I swear you make my ears bleed with your gay-speak. EoE steps into the ring without pomp and fanfare, and simple runs his hands along the ropes, ready. 'Play God' hits. RA: Coming to the ring, weighing in at three hundred and seventy-three pounds...PROPAGANDA! Propaganda and Eric Sagar walks down to ringside, passing out Gideon Bibles. Robbie: How charitable! That's religious conviction. You know, I was talking to Propaganda in the back, and he was talking about The Great Commission, and how people in Miami need to be Saved! Nate: Oh really. Robbie: Yeah, so I asked what it took to be saved and Eric sat me down and was telling me. I think when I asked if I could still view pornographic materials... I think he got a little upset. Nate: I wonder why. Robbie: Me too! Steven: Personally, I think Propaganda can go fuck himself. If it isn't considered 'sinful', that is... Nate: hmm. That's a strong statement, Steven. Steven: That jag-off told me and Boca we were 'An Abomination Unto The Lord'. So I sprayed his eyes with oven cleaner. Nate: ...Cool! Propaganda and Sagar walk by the announce desk, and sure enough, Sagar's eyes are reddened and irritated. He curses under his breath when he sees Steven. Nate: Wow, you actually sprayed him? Steven: Would I lie? Robbie: Shame on you, Smith! How can you interfere with Propaganda's Holy Mission? 'Sick' by Lagwagon hits, and Nick Casey steps out onto the steps. RA: Their opponents, weighing in at 254 pounds...'ANGELDUST' NICK CASEY! ANC runs down to the ring, sliding underneath the ropes as Propaganda runs towards him and buries his boot right in Nick Casey's face. Robbie: DUDE! That's not very Christian! Nate: And the match hasn't even begun, and Nick Casey is getting the short end of this stick... ANC rolls into a ball to protect himself from Propaganda's booted feet, which stomp and kick all availible exposed body parts. But from behind him, EoE sneaks up and Full Nelson Slams Propaganda into the mat! Nate: Big slam by Epitome of Evil! Robbie: That's blasphemy! Propaganda was merely trying to get Casey to repent! 'Know Your Enemy' hits, and Vampir Nosferatu sprints out of the back, and down to ringside. Once inside the ring, Epitome of Evil lunges for him, but Vampir meets him halfway with a spear. Standing over the crumpled form of EoE, Vampir looks over at Propaganda. A cruel snarl (or is it smile) appears on the ghastly features of the vampire, and he stalks over to Propaganda. Robbie: NO! NO! Propaganda's too young to go to the Lord! Nate: Hopefully, Vampir can beat some sense into him! Eric gets up on the apron, holding up a Bible, screaming at Vampir. Vampir stops and looks at Eric, and slowly stalks towards him... When Nick Casey slams into Vampir from behind with a running dropkick! Vampir lunges into Sagar, who is knocked off the apron and onto the barricade! Steven: How pleasing! Robbie: Bite your tongue, fag. Nate: And Epitome is getting back to his feet now... Dumas is correct. EoE is on his feet, while Vampir turns to face Casey. Casey back off, seeing the huge monster before him. Vampir grabs Casey by the throat, but EoE breaks them apart with a flying lariat, which impacts largely upon Noseferatu. Casey falls to the mat, while EoE and Vampir slug it out. Vampir bodyslams EoE into the mat, and slams a legdrop onto EoE's neck. As Vampir gets back up, he's knocked back down again in pain, courtesy of Propaganda, who slams his boot between Vampir's legs. Vampir and EoE are on the mat, and Propaganda jumps up and down gleefully. Propaganda: PRAISE THE LORD! Only to be clotheslined by ANC. Propaganda falls to the mat, and ANC goes for the quick cover. 1... 2... And Eric Sagar reaches in and pulls Casey's leg, breaking up the pin. Nick Casey turns around and stomps on Sagar's hand... But behind him, Vampir rises. He spins Casey around, and drives his fist right into Casey's throat, nearly lifting him off the ground with the impact! Vampir grabs Propaganda off the ground and puts him into a powerbomb position...He runs forward... Nate: CUBAN UPRISING! CUBAN UPRISING! Vampir Nosferatu just destroyed Propaganda! EoE gets up, holding his head, and sees Propaganda ready to tap out. EoE runs forth and hooks his arm around Vampir's neck, and yanks Vampir off of Propaganda. Propaganda, however, is in a bad way... With EoE forcing Vampir off, Propaganda's neck is now supporting the full weight of his body, thanks to Vampir still holding onto Propagand'a legs. EoE slams his elbow right into Vampir's face, drawing blood, but Nick Casey leaps off the turnbuckle and nails EoE with a hurricanrana! All three men collapse to the mat in a heap! Robbie: This is a train wreck! This is great! Nate: Vampir almost had this won! Robbie: Stop crying, ya big baby! Vampir gets up and DDT's Casey into the mat. Once Nick is taken care of, Vampir rises again and goes over to Propaganda, who is curled up into a ball. Vampir, with a sadistic look of satisfaction on his face, takes Propaganda over to the corner. He preps him for a Top Rope Superplex... But EoE runs and spears them both off the top turnbuckle! Propaganda screams the whole way down, until his scream is cut short when his head bounces off the barricade. Vampir slams off the steel ringsteps and rolls to the ground. Robbie: That's just wrong! The ref needs to take control of the situation! A Christian has just been persecuted! Nate: He's about as Christian as you are! Robbie: That means 'VERY'! Steven: Sigh. Inside the ring, EoE roars and looks at Nick Casey, who is rolling around the mat, holding his head. EoE picks Casey up and Tombstone Piledrives Casey's head into the mat. Casey twitches as EoE picks him up again. EoE grins fiercely as he crushes Casey with a backbreaker. Robbie: EoE is dominating here. Of course, what can we expect? His opponent is named after PCP... Nate: And a very good Faith No More album. Steven: I just... Look! On the outside, Vampir is up, and he powerbombs Propaganda right on the steel ringsteps! Steven: OOOO! Robbie: That's gotta hurt. A lot. Nate: Vampir taking some aggression out on Propaganda! Robbie: Persecution is more like it! Eric Sagar runs up from behind and slams a steel chair onto Vampir's head! Vampir turns around, snarling, and runs after Sagar. Sagar: EEP! Sagar sprints back up the ramp, with a very angry Vampir running after him... The ref begins counting both Propaganda and Vampir Nosferatu out... Meanwhile, EoE throws Casey into the turnbuckle, and begins battering him with powerful elbow strikes. As Casey staggers, EoE picks him up once again and slumps him over his shoulder. Climbing the turnbuckles, he slips Casey into a piledriver position! Nate: No frickin' way! Steven: He's going to piledrive Nick right on the top turnbuckle! Robbie: I think from now I'm going to get a CD player and play Slayer during every Epitome Of Evil Match. This rules... As EoE is precariously balanced on the top turnbuckle, with Casey in the piledriver positon, the camera goes back to the ramp, where Vampir axhandle-smashes Sagar off the top of the stage. Sagar flies down into the sound equipment, and he bounces sickly off a speaker cabinet. Robbie: ANGEL OF DEATH! YES! Nate: Why is EoE taking so long? Just end this! Casey slowly comes to, and EoE gets ready to deliver the deathblow... But Casey wriggles free, and hooks his legs underneath EoE's armpits, and Sunset Flips him off the top turnbuckle! Nate: SWEET ZOMBIE JESUS! EoE and Casey topple off the top turnbuckle, Casey landing hard on the mat while EoE sails onto the barricade, landing on his stomach. Crowd: OS-DUB! OS-DUB! OS-DUB! OS-DUB! Nate: Wow. I can safely say I have never seen that before. Robbie: ANGEL OF DEAAAAATH!!! Steven: I feel a little sick to my stomach... Apparently, EoE does as well, as he vomits noisily on the concrete. He looks like he's in a state of shock from the bump, while Nick Casey scrambles in the ring, exhausted. Robbie: This rules. Nate: EoE is still on the ground, crawling towards the ring... I'm surprised he's still moving... Robbie: That man earned his pay! Finally, EoE enters the ring, gritting his teeth in pain. However, his opponent, Nick Casey, is already on his feet. Casey pulls EoE by the hair, and signals to the crowd for the Angel Driver...And he can't get EoE off his feet! Casey tries again... And EoE takes Casey's head off with a powerful clothesline! Casey flips in the air once, and slams down to the mat hard. EoE falls to his knees, clutching his chest. Nate: I think we've arrived at a stalemate here! Casey is unable to get EoE off his feet, but EoE is too hurt to capitalize! Robbie: Trust me, EoE is gonna wreck PCP here. Casey tries to crawl away, but EoE grabs his ankle, rising back to his feet. Casey turns around and expertly executes an Enziguri, his boot colliding with EoE's jaw. EoE stumbles back, releasing Casey. Casey gets back on his feet, and runs to the ropes. Coming back, he slams both feet into EoE's chest in a Front Dropkick. EoE roars and goes down, clutching his chest. Casey gets back up and climbs the turnbuckle, signaling for the end. Nate: And Casey is gonna do it! Angel's Last Fall! Casey hits the Senton Bomb, and EoE collapses. Casey shakes off the cobwebs and makes the cover... 1! 2! 3! Winner: ‘Angeldust’ Nick Casey
