Stalking 4 Written: June 15, 2002 'Ah look at this, room number 300.' The mystery man was back this week on Slam! The fans have grown tired of just hearing the man and not being able to see his face or any other parts of his body. You could tell those this time there was no camera crew it was only the man and a little hand held camera that he had brought along. 'You know Griff, if you just didn't steal my spot light from me then this would have never had happen. But, no! You have to go out and steal, now you think your Mr. Big man on campus now. Well I got news for you buddy....your not!' With that said the mysterious voice rammed into Griffs locker room door a couple of times before the door swung open. He entered the room with ease and he knew exactly what he wanted to do........trash it. The man started to swing a baseball bat violently around the room breaking various mirrors, mirrors that Griff would probably stare at for hours, just so he could tell himself how great he is. The man broke tables, chairs, a TV and even ripped apart a brand new leather couch. The man started to scream as his baseball bat shattered against the wall leaving a little mark in the wall, but shattering the baseball bat into two's. Finally OSW event security finally made it up to Griff's locker room, but the damaged had already been done. The man dropped the camera as he ran from the security, but before the scene faded out you could hear Moose Malone say 'Don't worry, that guy won't be making anymore entrances on Slam! TV, I got a good look of him.' The scene shifts back to the OSW announce desk at ringside. Nate: WOW!! Griff Truxell’s Secret Admirer, as he is called by the online dirt sheets, just trashed Truxell’s lockerroom!!!
