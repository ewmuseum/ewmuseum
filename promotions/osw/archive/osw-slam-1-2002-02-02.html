The scene fades in from black as "Quiet" from Smashing Pumpkins hits the PA system inside the jam-packed 30,000 seat, indoor, Shanghai International Arena, in Shanghai China. A small graphic in the lower right hand corner of the screen reads “PCW Shanghai-ed, January 28, 2001.” The spotlight hits the entryway as a man with a simple woman’s stocking pulled down over his face to hide his identity emerges from the back, with a tall, slim, auburn-haired beauty at his side. Both individuals are easily recognizable to wrestling fans around the world, but how they came about their fame, an how recently they gained their notoriety in the business differed wildly. The man with the stocking facial covering who also wears a faded black Primetime Indy Circuit T-shirt and a simple pair of blue jeans, is none other than the man, the myth, the legend known only as The Phantom Booker, the architect behind the juggernaut known as Primetime Championship Wrestling. The lovely lass at his side is Jane Reagan, then the Personnel Director of Primetime Championship Wrestling, but in recent days had come to prominence for her role in the 2002 edition of PCW’s Shanghai’ed PPV.
