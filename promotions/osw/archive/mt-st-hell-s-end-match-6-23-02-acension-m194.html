Mt. St. Hell's End Match (6/23/02 - Acension) Written: 6/23/02 Mt. St. Hell's End Match Match Result: Loss Via: Elimination (by Da Westsidaz) Stipulations: Winner receives a World Title shot. Elimination is via being thrown off the mountain. At the end of 60 minutes, the wrestler on top of the mountain is the winner. Notes: Vendetta was the third man eliminated from the match. The show fades back up from commercial to a camera strafing over the 40’ high, 60’ x 60’, monstrosity that is Mt. St. Hell’s End. A steel mountain covered in chain link mesh, with three platforms. One at the base of the mountain, which levels out at 10 feet above the unforgiving concrete of the arena floor. Milling on level one are two dozen of OSW greatest stars, which for the convenience of fans keeping score at home are listed below. Jim Axtell Frankie Bones Amy Campbell Corg Seth Masters Trevor Hawke OSW Cruiserweight Champion, Corg Short Circuit Riptah El Drago Ivan Stanislav Graphic Violence Rhett Locke Hessian Vampir Nosferatu Michel Krieg OSW International Champion, Propaganda Kory Storm Griff Truxell, Jesus St, Crux Gunmetal Chico Doug Troy Clay Vendetta. Panning up the mountain, to the second tier, half way up its treacherous, steel mesh and grate slope, stand four men, three of them, Kid Wonder, Sebastian Toomes and “Angeldust” Nick Casey, earned the right to be here by winning qualifying matches, while the fourth and final man to gain the advantage of starting on high ground, OSW Commissioner, “The Scholar” Howard Porter, merely penciled himself into the spot. Finally, panning to the top, stands the battered, bruised and exhausted form of the OSW’s 170 lb marvel, Reno Davis. Though, beaten and bloodied earlier in the night by Ivan Stanislav, his blue eyes still shine bright with determination and a will to win, because the winner of this match earns themselves a shot at wrestling’s ultimate prize. Davis rifles through a gym bag and pulls out some gauze to cover a still bleeding wound. He then tosses the gym bag to one corner of the tier. The Old School Wrestling World Heavyweight Title, which strangely enough fades into view, as the camera shifts away from Davis, to the ringside position of the OSW announce team, where newly crowned OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Tom Walczak, sits, dressed in street clothes, with the belt in front of him on the announce desk, and headphones on top of his shaven head. Realizing, this tactic will not likely work, El Drago gets to his feet, and backs to the middle of the canvas, and crosses his arms in wait. Nate: WHOA!!! WITH ONLY 45 SECONDS LEFT IN THIS SIXTY MINUTE MATCH FROM HELL, EL DRAGO HAS LET IVAN STANISLAV OFF THE CANVAS AND IS TELLING HIM TO MEET HIM IN THE MIDDLE OF THE TIER TO FINISH THIS THING!!! Robbie: (laughing) THE FOOL!! NOW HE WILL LOOSE!!! Walczak: I agree. The Mexican is dumb for doing this. I would have kept Ivan down. Ivan can hardly believe it, shaking his head in disbelief. The 400 pound Russian Bear climbs to his feet, wriggles his head from side to side, which causes large cracking sounds to spring forth from his 41 year old neck. He straightens out his red suspenders, cracks his knuckles, smiles through a mask of crimson blood and strides to the center of the 15’ x 15’ tier, where El Drago awaits. Nate: 30 SECONDS!!! IT ALL COMES DOWN TO THIS!!!! The crowd is going wild. Ivan and El Drago circle, then lock up. Ivan uses his sizable strength advantage to shove El Drago back 10 feet. Ivan roars and charges in, prompting Drago to surge forward, and baseball slide between through the big man’s legs. Drago pops to his feet behind Ivan, only to find the Bear is not as dumb or as slow as he looks, as a huge elbow to the chops. El Drago stumbles backwards as blood flies from his mouth, but this is for all the marbles, and he knows it. Shaking off the pain, he charges forward, and springs into the air, going for twisting, cross body block. Robbie: LOOOOOK OUT COMRADE!!!! Nate: THIS COULD BE IT!!! ONLY 15 SECONDS LEFT!!! El Drago flies at Ivan, whose eyes grow wide, somewhat surprised by the speed and ferociousness of the attack. He takes a step back as El Drago’s flight continues…. ….catching the freakishly large luchadore in mid-flight in his massive arms. Robbie: YYYEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEESSS!!! Nate: OH MY GOD!!! IVAN STANISLAV JUST SNATCHED THE CHARGING, 280 POUND EL DRAGO IN MID-FLIGHT!!!! THIS IS IVAN’S MATCH TO WIN!!! The crowd is going ballistic. El Drago, who was in the final body block position, flails helplessly, as Ivan’s massive arms grip the Mexican legend’s torso with crushing force. Ivan: DYAAHAHAHAHAHAHHAAHAHAHAHA!!! Nate: 10 SECONDS AND IVAN STANISLAV IS IN COMPLETE CONTROL!!!! Ivan backs towards the edge of the tier, tenses his legs, and lets out a mighty roar, heaving El Drago up and back over his head. Flash bulbs go off around the arena, as Ivan releases El Drago out over the edge… Robbie: YEEEEEESSSSSSSS!!!! Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! El Drago flies out and disappears off the edge of the tier, leaving only Ivan standing triumphantly on top of the mountain….with seven seconds to spare. Suddenly Ivan’s triumphant look gives way to a wide eyed look of shock, panic and surprise as he begins to stumble back towards the edge of the tier. Robbie: WHAT THE HELL?!?! Nate: ON MY GOOOOOOD!!! Ivan continues to stumble back towards the edge of the platform. The camera angle changes from a frontal shot of the scene, to a profile shot, revealing exactly why Ivan is stumbling backwards…. …. The hand of El Drago is clutching a handful of Ivan’s trademark red suspenders. Nate: EL DRAGO HAS IVAN BY THE SUSPENDERS!!! Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! El Drago continues to fly outward and downward, as propelled by Ivan, but with every inch of his flight, causes the now stretched to the max suspenders to drag Ivan along for the ride….ever closer to the edge of the tier. Ivan reaches the end and tries to uses his massive strength to balance on the edge of the tier for just another three seconds. Three seconds he needs to be the only man left at the top when the 60 minute clock runs down… Nate: IVAN NEEDS TO JUST HOLD ON A MOMENT MORE… Three…. Ivan’s boots teeter on the edge of the tier.. Two….. One boot falls off the edge…. One… The spot where Ivan once teetered on the edge, now stands free of Russian boots. Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! The camera shifts to a frontal view of where Ivan Stanislav falls freely through the air. Ivan’s scream of rage fills the arena, as he plummets down towards the second tier, where both he and El Drago crash down upon the steel base of the second tier, unmoving. Blood still flows from the faces of Ivan, whose face is still contorted in shocked rage. El Drago lies motionless and unconscious, with clutching a handful of red suspenders. Nate: IVAN AND EL DRAGO ARE DOWN AND OUT!!! WE’VE PAST THE SIXTY MINUTE TIME LIMIT AND NO ONE IS ON TOP!!! The camera suddenly shifts from the shot of Ivan and El Drago, to the edge of the canvas on the top layer, as a pale, blood spatter hand grasps the top of the canvas. The bell rings. Nate: WE APPARENTLY HAVE A WINNER!!!!! Robbie: WHAT?!?! WHO THE HELL IS THAT?!?! Nate: I DUNNO!!!! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! The camera stays focused on the pale hand, as it scratches the canvas. Another hand reaches up to the top tier, then slowly, black hair matted with blood, edges over the top tier….hair which tops the blood covered face of the new #1 Contender for the Old School Wrestling World Heavyweight Title…. ….Rhett Locke. The crowd explodes into a thunderous pop. Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Nate: HOLY SHIT!!! IT’S RHETT LOCKE!!! IT’S RHETT LOCKE!!! Robbie: WE GATHERED THAT!!! Nate: RHETT LOCKE HAS WON THIS MATCH!!! HE IS THE NEW NUMBER ONE CONTENDER FOR THE TOM WALCZAK’S OSW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE!!! Walczak: I will crush him, like a turtle! Robbie: I DEMAND A PHOTO FINISH ON IVAN’S FOOT LEAVING THE TOP TIER!!! Nate: WE ARE OUT OF TIME!!! WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT ON SLAM!!! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!!! The camera shifts to an exhausted, bloodied and broken Rhett Locke pulls himself up onto the top of the canvas of the top tier, then promptly passes out from blood loss and internal trauma….as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears in the lower, central portion of the screen….as the show fades to black.
