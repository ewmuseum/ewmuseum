Civil Disobedience vs. Azrael Cain/Caleb

2002-02-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil Disobedience vs. Azrael Cain/Caleb Written: 2-16-02 Nate: Alright! We are back and what a match we have for you next. Up next we have the tag match between Civil Disobedience and Heresian Bloodlust. Robbie: Some match. No one has a clue to the location of Hessian and if he doesn’t show, it seems that Ed is going to name a partner for that damn goon Cain. This is going to be a blood bathe. Steven: Blasphemy! Cain can take care of those two idiots without Hessian. And besides, Hessian will show! Nate: Well needless to say, this is going to be on interesting match. Let’s take it to Earl Heinrich in the ring. The camera focuses in on Earl who is wearing his standard tuxedo and big smile for the fans. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, boys and girls! The next match is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring first, from Aurora, Illinois and weighing in at a combined weight of 460 pounds, Civil Disobedience! “Pretty fly for a White Guy blares through the PA system and Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters appear on the entryway! Both men begin jumping around and working the crowd who begin to cheer for the boys from suburbia. Nate: As usual the boys from suburbia are showing a lot of energy before their match. Steven: Too bad that energy is about to get beat out of them for the third time. Robbie: I wonder what body part is going to need medical attention this week? Steven: Ha! Hey Earl, you look like a freakin penguin! Why don’t you quit your damn yapping already and announce the tag team that are going to be the winners this week. The boys run down the steel ramp and dive into the ring awaiting to do battle. Earl: And coming to you from parts unknown, weighing in at.... Ed: Hold on a second there Earl! Commissioner Ed appears on the entryway with a mic in his hand. Steven: Yes! The commissioner is doing my job for me! Shut that damn penguin up! Ed makes his way down to the ring. Ed: Since no one has been able to find Hessian. And seeing how Cain took it upon himself to go on a rampage this week looking for him. I have decided to give Cain another partner this week. This week Heresian Bloodlust will be comprised of Azrael Cain and someone that I think Civil Disobedience will be very interested in fighting this week. Robbie: Who the hell is he talking about? Nate: I think I know! Ed is now in the ring. Ed: Seeing how this...man....has decided to interfere in the past two matches for one reason or another. I think that it is only suiting that he be forced to fight tonight. This evening Cain will be partnered with none other than.... Dramatic pause..... Ed: That little rotten hobo, he calls a manager, Caleb! Nate: WHAT?! Robbie: HA!!! Steven: NO!!!!! The fans erupt in cheers of the thought of the small hobo being tossed around the ring by Masters and Hawke. The lights snap out though and Carmina Burana blasts the PA. The Azrael Cain appears on the entryway. The lights come up before Heresian Bloodlust’s usual intro and the massive beast has a mica in his hand. Cain: You want to bring Caleb into this, Ed! That’s fine with me! I don’t need a partner to take these punks out! We have sent them to the hospital for the past two weeks straight and tonight is only going to be the same! You could match me up with anyone you want and I’m still going to unleash pure and unadulterated carnage. As Cain address Ed and the OSW fans, his five foot tall manager, Caleb appears from the backstage area with Cain’s thirty pound sledgehammer over his shoulder. Cain: As you can see, we are more then up for this challenge! Cain and Caleb charge the ring at full speed. Of course, Cain is the first to reach the apron and he dives in under the ropes. Ed steps out o the ring and proceeds to take a ringside seat at the official’s table. Masters and Hawke immediately lay into Cain before he can get to his feet. Nate: And here we go! Steven: Jesus Christ, Betty, will you get one of those two asses out of the ring. Robbie: Leave the beautiful Ms. Lovejoy alone. The pretty little thing is doing her best out there. The camera shoots to Betty who is just staring at the carnage with a confused look on her face. Steven: At least signal for the damn bell! Betty gets that “oh yeah” look on her face and then proceeds to gesture to the official’s table to ring the bell. Finally, Masters gets to the outside apron as Hawke remains to beat down on Cain. Nate: And now the match has officially started. And it seems that Trevor Hawke is on a mission to send Cain to the hospital for a change. Steven: Never! Cain struggles through the blows and rises to his feet. He then pulls his massive arm back and delivers a huge blow to Hawke’s head. Hawke stumbles backwards in a daze. Cain kicks Hawke in the stomach causing him to double over in pain. Cain then grabs hold of Hawke and delivers a vicious powerbomb. Nate: And the offensive assault by Civil Disobedience is over for the moment. Steven: Did you have any doubt. Ha! Cain then picks Hawke up by the hair and picks his opponent to his feet. Just as Hawke manages to get to his feet, he quickly grabs hold of Cain’s face and delivers a face buster. Cain stumbles backwards grasping his jaw. Robbie: Boring! Boring! Boring! Steven: Shut up! Shut up! Shut up! Nate: There is still fight in that suburban commando yet. Hawke stumbles over to his corner and tags in Masters. Masters jumps up on the top of the turnbuckle and leaps at his wounded opponent. Masters tries to deliver an elbow drop, but he is caught by Cain. Cain then slams Masters hard into the mat. Nate: Good God! Steven: Get him! Send him to the hospital again, Cain! Cain grabs Masters and irishwhips him across the ring. Masters flies into the ropes by Caleb, but before he can come back Caleb leaps on Masters’ back. Caleb begins to gnaw away at Masters’ ear with his toothless mouth. Masters begins to punch away at the skull of the hobo. Caleb falls off Masters’ back and onto the apron. The hobo manages to catch himself though and his hand is hanging over the top of the ropes. Robbie: What the hell was that hobo thinking? Cain runs at Masters and jumps through the air, but Masters manages to avoid the assault. Cain hits the ropes and bounces to the mat as Caleb bounces to the concrete floor. Hawke runs around the outside of the ring and hoists Caleb up to his feet. Hawke then throws Caleb into the ring and begins yelling at referee Lovejoy. Steven: Why did Trevor Hawke throw Caleb into the ring? Nate: It seems that Hawke is telling Betty that there was a tag made when Cain hit the ropes. Betty looks at Trevor Hawke for a moment with a confused look on her face. She then looks at Cain and starts to push him out of the ring. Steven: What does she think she is doing?! There was no tag! Robbie: It looks like civil Disobedience are going to get revenge on that rat for last week. Ha! Steven: NO! Cain is backed up unto the apron by the gorgeous ref. In the ring, Masters grabs the hobo and sets him up. Masters then gives the hobo a wicked super kick that crumples the hobo. Masters the picks Caleb up again. Nate: Caleb looks very shaken up! Robbie: Will someone do something already. I’m falling asleep out here! Cain tries to get in the ring, but is stopped by Hawke tripping the big man causing him to fall to the concrete floor. Hawke starts to lay the boots into Cain. Cain rolls out of the way though and Hawke misses him, but his foot goes into ringpost. A loud clang sound is heard as Hawke’s foot hits the metal post. Nate: What was that noise? Robbie: That sounded like steel hitting against steel! Steven: Those bastards are wearing steel tipped boots! Hey Betty! Will you do something already! In the ring, Masters points at Cain. Masters grabs Caleb and delivers wicked kick to Caleb’s groin with his steel loaded boots. Caleb falls to the mat. His screams fill the arena. Robbie: Finally some action! Steven: Jesus Christ on a cross! Ed! Will you do something already. You’re the damn commissioner! Ed looks at the announcers’ table and shrugs his shoulders. A satisfied smile comes across his face. Masters picks Caleb to his feet. Masters delivers a fisherman buster to the decrepit little man. Nate: Primo Plex! Steven: Cain, get in the damn ring. Cain looks in the ring and sees that his manager is being pinned by Masters and referee Lovejoy is already counting. 1.... Cain climbs to the apron and over the ropes. 2... Cain dives through the air in order to break up the pin. ...3!!!! Cain lands on Masters, but a second late. Winners: Civil Disobedience Nate: They did it! Civil Disobedience has upset Heresian Bloodlust for the third week in a row! Robbie: HA HA HA !!!!!! Steven: What the hell is that? This is a joke! That count was one of the fastest ones that I have ever seen! Cain roars in anger. He then proceeds to punch Masters repeatedly in face. Masters’ wound form last week is busted open again and blood begins to flow from his forehead. Hawke jumps up onto the apron and charges to aid his partner. Steven: That’s right Cain! Make him Bleed! Hawke leaps on Cain’s back and starts to choke the beast. Caleb rolls away from the carnage that is occurring in the ring. The hobo begins to drag himself across the mat to his corner. Hawke is trying to choke out Cain. Robbie: Rip his head off already! Caleb fights to get himself to his feet. He then reaches through the ropes and grabs the sledgehammer that is leaning up against the post. Caleb grasps the weapon and charges at Hawke. The hobo strikes Hawke in the back of his head with the handle of the sledgehammer. Hawke’s grasp is loosened as he falls to the mat holding his head in agony. Nate: All hell has broken loose! Robbie: Sweet! These guys are going to dismember each other. Cain then grabs the sledgehammer from his manager and swings it high in the air. Cain brings the hammer down on Masters thigh. Caleb jumps on top of Hawke and starts to tear away at the wrestler’s steel tip boots. Nate: What is that deranged hobo doing? Caleb finally gets the boot off. The hobo starts to slam the steel tipped boot against Hawke’s face. Hawke’s face begins to gush blood. Steven: End their career! Cain spits on Masters who is thrashing about in pain. The beast walks over to his manager and pulls him off. Cain then spits on Hawke. Caleb throws the boot down on the beaten and bloodied face of Trevor Hawke. Carmina Burana blasts the PA system and manages to drown out the boos of the fans. Cain and the limping Caleb exit the ring and head up the steel ramp leaving the battered and bloodied civil Disobedience in the ring. Nate: That was one of the most savage things that I have ever witnessed! Robbie: That was great! Steven sits with his face in his hands. Steven: Why? Why? Why does it always have to end this way?
