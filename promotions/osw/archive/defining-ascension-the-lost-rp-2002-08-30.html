Defining Ascension (The Lost RP)

Defining Ascension (The Lost RP) Written: Never Note: This RP was done for the OSW Ascension PPV two days after the PPV took place. The handler, who admits to being an idiot, mistook the PPV date thinking it was the coming Sunday instead of the Sunday that just passed. Therefore, the following RP was never posted on the board. There is a saying, or perhaps an objective or goal, that many people have that says, “Climb the ladder to success.” On Sunday night, that saying will be put to the test. The difference is, instead it is not a ladder; it is a dangerous, man-made monstrosity of a mountain entitled Mt. St. Hell’s End. Mt. St. Hell’s End will be the focal point of a wrestling extravaganza called OSW Ascension. Every single brave wrestler on the Old School roster will be a part of the match involving the structure. The goal: Be the one man or woman standing at the very top after 60 minutes of intense fighting. The reward: A shot at one of the most prestigious belts in all of wrestling…The OSW World Heavyweight championship. It will be more than a tough challenge, but the end reward makes it all worth it. The mountain, Mt. St. Hell’s End, has three different levels for the wrestlers to work on. Each of the three is unique in their own special way. Level One The lowest level, Level One, is the largest of the three levels. It needs to be since it will be housing the most wrestlers. This is the toughest of the three levels to succeed at. Level One will be housing most of the lower people. It’ll have your no-names, your dreamers, your wannabes, and your curtain jerkers. The people who don’t matter, or won’t make much of an impact outside of a small whimper will start on Level One. The good thing that Level One does bring is the fact it’s closest to the floor. As a rule states, anyone who hits the floor during the process of the match will be eliminated from the match. With this rule in effect, hopefully the curtain jerkers starting on Level One won’t last long enough to soil what should be a landmark match. Level Two Level Two, or the middle level of Mt. St. Hell’s End, will hold four wrestlers to start, including the four-time PCW Cruiserweight champion, Kid Wonder. This is the second largest of the three levels on the mountain. What Level Two brings to this match is respectability. Anyone starting or finishing here has had to do something good to get to this point. And for that, they’d deserve some respect and props. And that’s all Kid Wonder asks for. All he wants is some respect for everything he has done. But ever since making the jump to OSW, Kid Wonder has been treated like crap by everybody. Be it the wrestlers, the front office, or even security. Everyone who has come in contact with Wonder in the back has treated him as if he were Scott Kilborn. Had Wonder known this would be the thanks he’d get for leaving the ship he captained for so long, he’d have just left all together. So getting up this far is already an accomplishment for the superstar. Level Three This is the level to be at. Level Three is the top of the mountain. The one man standing on Level Three at Mt. St. Hell’s End when the match is done and over will get the title shot. To start off, Reno Davis will be standing at the top of the mountain. But if every wrestler other than Reno has their say, he won’t be there for long. Being the man at the top makes you a marked man. So anyone who has the balls to stand on top of Mt. St. Hell’s End better be prepared. Once you’re there, everyone will be coming for you. Sunday night will be a special night for everyone, even if it is just because of this one match on this one innovative structure. But for some people, it’s a make or break night. For some people, this night, and this match in particular, are the time to showcase what you can or can not do. Time to show whether you are ready to make that move to the top or not. Kid Wonder is one of those people. This is his time to show what the fans know he is capable of. This is Wonder’s time to fulfill all that promise he showed in the early days of PCW. This is K-Dub’s time to finally solidify his place as a wrestling main eventer after years of tweening with it. And with a shot at Doug Troy, Wonder’s long-time rival, on the line, there’s no better time for the teen idol to step up than Sunday night. The goal has been set. Conquer Mt. St. Hell’s End or else. And if Kid Wonder does conquer the mountain, that’s when the new, brighter era in OSW shall begin. View Kid Wonder 's Biography Back
