Segment 1 of 3 — World Tag Team Championship, Suburban Deathmatch

Civil Disobedience (Seth Masters & Trevor Hawke) vs. Heresian Bloodlust (Hessian & Azrael Cain), for OSW's first-ever Tag Team Championship. Civil Disobedience wins — confirmed both by Segment 2 below and, independently, by Hessian's and Trevor Hawke's own later bio-card and roleplay references to the loss.
