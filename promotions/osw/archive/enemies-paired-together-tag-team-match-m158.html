Enemies Paired Together tag team match

2002-05-25
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Enemies Paired Together tag team match Written: May 25th, 2002 Nate: Hey! I didn’t say it! I just repeated it. (attempting to switch gears) Coming up next is a rather unique tag-team match up. El Drago and Michel Krieg are forced to team up against another ill-matched pair, Lillith and Propaganda. Steven: Women have no place in the ring with men! Robbie: Oh God... I actually have to agree with Steven! Steven: Really? In that case, do you- Robbie: Shut up before I hit you, Steven! Nate: Well, before my colleagues start acting like these next two teams more than likely will, lets send it up to Earl. Earl: This tag-team contest is scheduled for one fa- Without warning, 'I Stand Alone' by Godsmack starts playing, cutting off Earl in mid sentence. The house lights dim down as Lillith walks out onto the main stage. With an intent look, she walks down to the ring. Nate: Lillith didn't seem to want to wait for an introduction, she's coming right down! Steven: What's she got in here hands? Nate: Looks like a book of some kind. Robbie: Great, she's going to read us a story. I should've stayed home. Lillith slips through the ropes and walks right up to Earl, who's a bit startled at being singled out. She holds out her hand, asking for the microphone, which he carefully hands her. Robbie: Dammit, she IS going to read us a story. SECURITY! Lillith calmly looks around the arena, taking in the fans, who are cheering her. After a few moments, she holds the book up in the air, and raises the mic up to her ruby red lips. Lillith: This book, is 1000 pages of utter bullshit! The fans pop big, Lillith rarely talks to anyone. Steven: Isn't that the bible? Nate: Sure looks like it. Robbie: She's going to read BIBLE stories? POLICE! Lillith: How people can take this book seriously? It's full of lies, racist slurs, sexist ideas, and even porn! And religions are built around this? Are people really this dense? A slightly quieter response, as quite a few Christians are in the arena. Lillith opens the bible and flips a few pages. Lillith: Here's an example 'The angel of Yahweh appeared to him in a flame of fire out of the midst of a bush. He looked, and behold, the bush burned with fire, and the bush was not consumed. Moses said, I will turn aside now, and see this great sight, why the bush is not burnt. When Yahweh saw that he turned aside to see, God called to him out of the midst of the bush, and said, 'Moses! Moses!' He said, 'Here I am.' ' Lillith drops the book on the mat and walks around a bit. Lillith: What three year old on drugs wrote that?!? Robbie: You know, I changed my mind... I kinda like this. Lillith: So, I figured... if God will talk to some loser named Moses, maybe I can get him to talk to me. Nate: What's she talking about? Steven: Yeah, really, I mean, I shout out for God every night with my 'Man of the Day', and He never answers me... During the silence over that comment, Lillith walks back to stand next to the fallen bible. Reaching into a pocket, she pulls out a match, and strikes it with her thumb. Nate: She wouldn't! Robbie: Oh yes she would! Stevem: BOCA~! Lillith peers into the tiny flame, and what could only be described as an 'evil grin' spread across her face. She looks down at the book at her feet, and drops the match onto it. Almost instantly, the paper ignites, and the book is soon a small fire. Nate: Lillith is burning a bible! Lillith stares down into the flames, and the grin grows wider as she watches the paper turn black under the heat. The sounds of the crowd changes, and Lillith looks up expectantly to the ramp. Nate: Here comes Propaganda and Sagar! Robbie: And they both look pissed! Sagar is screaming at the top of his lungs incoherently, but Propaganda is focused and charges to the ring. He jumps from the floor to the apron, and steps over the top rope. Lillith grabs him by the forearm, and twists, using the 370 lb'ers own weight against him, and flips him over to land on his back - on top of the flaming book! Nate: Propaganda's shirt just caught fire! Lillith's trying to burn The Power Himself! As Propaganda rolls around in the ring, Lillith slips through the ropes, and hops down onto the floor in front of Eric Sagar. Sagar gets a crazed look in his eyes and throws a wild haymaker, but Lillith ducks under it easily. Grabbing his extended arm, she pulls it in front of him, wraps it partway around his neck, and pulls him down hard, slamming his head and shoulders into the floor. Robbie: Lillith takes out Sagar with the Stroke of Midnight, I don't think he's getting up anytime soon. Steven: Oooo! Proppy took off his shirt! Gotta love the skin. Furious, Propaganda strides to the opposite side of the ring, and reaches down, grabbing Lillith by her long hair. She shrieks as she's literally lifted off the ground by her raven black locks up onto the apron. He lets go of her for a moment, and grabs her by the throat. Lifting her over the ropes, he turns and throws her 15 feet across the ring, slamming her into a corner! Lillith then falls face first onto the mat, and doesn't move. Nate: Oh my God! He just threw her like a rag doll! Robbie: Come on, she weights 115 lbs, Steven could throw her like that/ Steven: Nuh-uh, I ain't touchin' no woman! Nate: They have to tag tonight… Sager is slowly climbing back to his feet as, 'Shutdown' by American Head Charge blasts over the PA system and a chorus of boos erupt as Michel Krieg comes sprinting down the ramp, Communist Party Member Wang stepping out from behind the curtain, laughing to himself. Krieg slides under the ropes and smashes Sagar with a massive forearm, flipping the manager over backwards. Robbie: That’s the way to get this match started. Krieg stares at Propaganda as neither man makes a move just glaring. Nate: A title stands between these two men. Betty Lovejoy signals and the bell is rung and both giants collide locking up in the center of the ring. Krieg gains the advantage quickly whipping Propaganda into the ropes and clotheslining him right over the top and to the outside. Steven: This match seems to be underway but where is El Drago? Nate: I don’t know Steven, that’s a good question. Krieg doesn’t stop as he turns his attention towards Lillith and pulls her up to her feet. Sager stumbles over to Propaganda and attends to him as Krieg slaps his massive paw around her tiny neck. Robbie: This is why women shouldn’t be in the ring, they’re just going to get hurt. Nate: Krieg may not even need Drago for this match. Michel toys with Lillith as she squirms trying to get free of his grasp. He flicks her hair playfully before he lifts her up into the air. Instead of just slamming her down he plays with her more as the crowd jeers him. Robbie: Just put her out of OUR misery already. Krieg is just about to slam her down when a carefully placed kick, hits it’s mark freeing her of his grasp and dropping him to his knees grabbing his midsection. Nate: One word, OUCH!! Steven: I’ll rub it better. Lillith struggles to the ropes and starts to her feet Propaganda is back on is feet and with Sager’s guidance he climbs the ropes and gets back into the ring. Robbie: Double team!!! Nate: Where is El Drago? Propaganda glances at both Lillith and Krieg before attacking the later with a stiff kick to the midsection. Propaganda begins to stomp away at the fallen Krieg as the jumbotron flickers and cuts backstage where El Drago is sitting backstage watching the match on a monitor. Two large shadows come into the picture looming over Drago getting his attention immediately. He stands up and snarls a bit as the two shadows become apparent as Alexei and Ivan. Nate: There’s El Drago, but why is he backstage? Ivan: What are you doing comrade? Robbie: It seems the Red Army want that answer too. Drago: Que? Ivan: You dare to sit back here and leave your partner to fight alone. Steven: This could get ugly. El Drago looks back and forth at both men bracing himself for the attack but it doesn’t come. Ivan: I thought better of you Drago, I guess I was mistaken. Nate: That was unexpected! Stanislav and Ruslan retreat leaving a confused El Drago glancing back and forth at the monitor and where the Russians left as the jumbotron returns to normal. In the ring Propaganda has Krieg up and holds him in a full nelson as Lillith scratches at his chest like an angry cat in heat. Steven: If Lillith had a unit I would be turned on right now. Nate: Steven! Lillith finishes with a nasty slap across the face and Propaganda lifts Krieg up hitting him with a Full Nelson Slam and then jumps on top for the cover. 1… 2… As Betty is about to hit the three, the easily distracted ref gets distracted by the blast of fire that erupts from the entranceway. Nate: Looks like El Drago got Ivan’s message. Robbie: I thought he didn’t understand English? Nate: Maybe someone translated… Robbie: Or maybe Drago remembered the last time he crossed the Red Army and thought better of his decision to leave Michel Krieg high and dry. Propaganda breaks the cover and stares toward the entrance waiting for El Drago’s arrival. So much that he hopes out of the ring and starts to walk up the ramp to meet him. Steven: I see the old bait and switch trick coming on here. Sure enough Drago comes from the opposite side jumping over the guardrail and sliding through the ropes into the ring. Lillith charges at him but the quickness of El Drago eludes her and he ducks underneath and grabs her neck dropping her to the mat with a reverse neck breaker. Nate: El Drago is having an immediate effect on this match here. Propaganda dives back into the ring and is met with a drop kick by El Drago sending him right back over the top rope and on the outside from once he came. Meanwhile Krieg is back up to his feet and locks eyes with Drago briefly before being deposited on the out side thanks to yet another drop kick. Robbie: What did he do that for? That’s his partner!!! Nate: There is no love loss between those two and we know Drago didn’t want anything to do with this tag match to begin with. Drago scoops Lillith off the mat but is great with a flip over slam for his trouble and she begins to wrench his arm. Both Propaganda are back on their feet and take their positions on the ring aprons. Krieg snarling towards Drago as Lillith wrenches his arm and Propaganda yelling for a tag. Steven: Wow this actually turned into a tag match. Lillith drops the arm lock and drives a quick knee into Drago’s chest. She turns toward Propaganda and holds her hand up for the tag but instead of slapping his hand she just waves at him. Robbie: Get out of the ring you slut! Lillith turns back to attack El Drago but the Mexican Dragon is already back on his feet and he pulls her down to the mat with a headlock. Steven: She should have made the tag. El Drago holds on with the headlock and lifts the small women up and over with a suplex and makes a cover. 1… 2… Broken up by Propaganda! Nate: A near fall there for El Drago. Krieg stands on the apron literally fuming as Betty orders Propaganda out of the ring but not before several cheap shots on Drago. Robbie: Ha ha! That’s good tag team wrestling by Propaganda. Nate: That’s not how I see it. Robbie: And I don’t care how you see it. Lillith is up again and once again Propaganda is calling for a tag. This time however Sager is up on the apron too screaming at Lillith. Sagar: WHORE!!! LISTEN TO ME WHORE!!! TAG OUT NOW AND GET YOUR WHORE ASS BACK IN THE KITCHEN!!! Lillith gives a smile to Sager and walks toward the corner and Propaganda’s outstretched hand. She however doesn’t just tag him but nails him in the face with a right hand before running beside the ropes and clotheslining Sagar off the apron and down to the ringside floor. Nate: Lillith took offence to Sagar’s comments!! Robbie: Gee you think so? Steven: Did you just say gee? Robbie: Shut up Steven! Steven: And I thought I was gay. *SMACK* Propaganda charges back into the ring and goes straight after Lillith grabbing her around the neck with both hands and lifting the tiny women up into the air. El Drago starts towards Propaganda but is blindsided by his partner. Nate: This isn’t supposed to happen!! The partners are fighting each other. Robbie: This is exactly what was supposed to happen. Ed knew this would happen, it’s like two matches in one. On the outside Wang is choking Sagar. In the ring both Propaganda and Krieg are keeping their smaller opponents ground with huge powerful blows. The bell rings as Betty throws this match out. Steven: I don’t like the guy, but he’s got a point. Krieg pulls Drago to his feet as Propaganda does to Lillith and both men whip their opponent into the ropes. They charge at their opponent but both Lillith and Drago duck at the last second causing Propaganda and Krieg to nail each other with big boots to the face and knocking both to the mat. Nate: Well that backfired. Drago and Lillith roll out of the ring on opposite sides as the crowd cheers they start to back up towards the entrance and back into each other. Startled they turn around ready to attack but upon recognition El Drago lowers his guard and offers his hand. Robbie: I hate this, I really, really hate this. Lillith looks down at the gloved hand and then back at Drago as she hisses at him causing the surprised Mexican to jump back in disbelief. Nate: I don’t think that’s the response he was looking for. In the ring both Propaganda and Krieg are back on their feet, resting in opposite corners, blood dripping from their lips they wipe at it as they stare each other down.
