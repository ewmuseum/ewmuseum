Stanislav berates Meanstreak only to be interrupted

2002-02-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stanislav berates Meanstreak only to be interrupted Written: Feb 23, 2002 Nate’s words are suddenly interrupted out as a HUGE fanfare rocks through the arena and the lights dim down to a deep, dark red and the sound of Russians singing loudly rumbles through the arena. Nate’s voice cuts back in suddenly as the banner of The Red Army appears on the Jumbotron… Nate: ….the heck is going on?! From the backstage area, Ivan Stanislav, Alexei Ruslan, Tom Walczak, Speedy Riggs, Communist Party Member Wang, and The Two Round Faced Women file out and head down to the ring to the yells and screams of the fans, all negative. Stanislav walks up the ring steps and lumbers into the ring, with each thunderous step the ring and the ropes shake, until he finally stops in the center of the ring. Ruslan and Walczak stand at either side of him while Wang, The Women, and Riggs all jaw at the fans nearby. Alexei and Stanislav sing loudly the song of the Soviet Union, saluting the camera as they do. Wang reaches into his jacket pocket and unfurls a huge Soviet Flag, giving the other side to a Round Faced Woman. They hold it up high behind Stanislav, the flag reaching almost to either side of the ring! The fans throw objects at the flag as we can tell from the erratic movements of the flag, but it holds tight. When the song finally subsides, Wang folds up the flag as Ivan produces a microphone from his back pocket. Robbie: Time to hear from the scariest man in the PTC! Steven: What about Meanstreak? Robbie: Meanstreak is an idiot! That there in the ring is a man with a plan!! Stanislav waits for the crowd to die down, which takes about thirty seconds, finally, The Russian Bear of the OSW raises the microphone to his mouth and bellows his decree. Ivan: Last week, you people thought you had seen an upset!! A small pop from the fans…. Ivan: Last week, you people thought you had seen the destruction of The Red Army….last week, you people thought Ivan Stanislav was bested…. Nate: Well, he was! Robbie: Shut up Dumas. Nate: Wheelchair, Robbie. Wheelchair. Stanislav stalks around the ring for a moment, then continues. Ivan: But you see, though Meanstreak may have manifested himself as a force in the OSW, it is merely a false premonition!! For you see, I, Ivan Sergeiovich Stanislav, am a master of history… Robbie: And beating people up, and yelling… Nate: And lying, and cheating… Ivan: …and history, once again, will repeat itself! Those in the PCW that were foolish enough to get in my way were eliminated from this sport! Doug Troy was eliminated, and look at him now, a washed up has been! Ed tried to topple me, and in two matches I took him from a hardcore legend to nothing more than a geriatric! Trashcan Man got in my way, and the onslaught of The Red Army purged him from Primetime Championship Wrestling! Robbie: Y’know, he has a point there. Stanislav growls into the microphone… Ivan: And Meanstreak, the last of many that got in my way, the Monster that was supposed to be unstoppable! The monster that could not be beaten, met a man that was bigger! Stronger! Faster! Smarter! Alexei counts beside Ivan on his hand as Ivan rifles off more traits…. Ivan: I am more intelligent, I am a strategist! I am a man with a mission that cannot, will not, and shall not be stopped! And Meanstreak looked up at me and realized the folly of his plan, and I promptly sent him to the hospital…never to return to the face of wrestling….until now…. Another pop from the crowd! Nate: This is obviously a pro-Meanstreak crowd! Steven: Or every crowd is an anti-Ivan crowd. Ivan: And those men, all injured beyond repair, have returned! Trashcan Man is nothing more than a fly that has been swatted! Doug Troy has been eliminated! Ed has no control over MY Army, and Meanstreak, he is a house of cards about to crumble underneath my great hand! A “Mean-streak” chant bursts out from the crowd. Ivan roars animalistically and stomps his foot on the mat with a thunderous thud. Ivan: Tonight! Ivan Stanislav and Tom Walczak will battle for chance to win OSW World Title! And it is assured that we will be victorious! Meanstreak can try to stop me, and he can try to hinder my progress, but I have my orders, and now, he is enemy number one to The Red Army! MEANSTREAK!! I KNOW YOU CAN HEAR ME!!! The camera zooms in on Ivan’s anger stricken face. Ivan: YOU ARE A NON-PERSON TO US! YOU ARE A DEAD… Suddenly, Ivan’s words are drowned out as “Brainless” by the Urge hits the PA system. Nate: THAT’S DOUG TROY’S MUSIC!!! The crowd immediately recognizes DT’s music and explodes into a monstrous pop as the man once called “The Heart and Soul of PCW” steps out of the backstage area and onto the stage with his trademark, cocky smile on his face and a microphone in his hand. In the ring, Ivan roars in protest, which doesn’t phase DT at all. He simply shakes his head in shock, then raises the microphone to speak. DT: Ya know, Ivan, I think I speak for the entire non-hearing impaired world when I say “Give it a &^$%ing rest already! Nate: WHHOOOOAAA!!! Robbie: Wake me up when Troy is finished crying. In the ring, Ivan is furious, as apparently his microphone has been turned off. DT winks out to the crowd, raises his mic, and continues. DT: You know, guys like you and Meanstreak sucked the marrow out of PCW, but I sure as hell am not going to let that happen here in Old School Wrestling!!! Nate: WHAT?!?! WHAT IS HE SAYING?!?! Ivan looks on in the ring, still seething, wondering what Doug is talking about. Doug, meanwhile, continues. DT: I stood by in PCW and let the “powers that be” push you two dumb apes through the roof, at the expense of younger and better talent. Well, I got news for you. It’s not going to happen here. Not on my watch and not by a long shot!!! The crowd explodes, as Ivan roars in disbelief in the ring. Gone is DT’s smile, as it appears he is all business. DT: So Ivan and Streak, consider yourselves warned because expect to see my ugly mug, in your faces, any time, any place and anywhere in the near future….and to you Ms. Jane Reagan…. Nate: Uh oh….. DT: …I am not going to let you run this company into the ground riding the backs of these two pieces of crap. Had I known these two dumb oxen were going to be here clogging up the main event, I would have stayed retired….but since you lured me out, I am going to take it upon myself, to save this company from being from being destroyed. That’s my take, and I am out!!! With that, “Brainless” by the Urge hits the PA system while the crowd roars in approval.
