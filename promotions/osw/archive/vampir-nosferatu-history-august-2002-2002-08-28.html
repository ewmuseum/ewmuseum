Vampir Nosferatu — history (August 2002)

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History For centuries the struggle of good versus evil has raged on, for centuries an entity known to all as Vampir Nosferatu has walked this planet, waging his own war against all of humanity. But on one fateful night however, everything changed when it was discovered that his long lost brother, Maskimxul, had appeared and was coming to settle unfinished family business. For forty days and forty nights they struggled, a clash heard around the world. During this period inexplicable events occurred on this planet, waves were much more violent, a chain of earthquakes of imaginable magnitude were leveling civilizations, and a fog of war rose from the ground, covering the sun and the moon, dropping temperatures world wide and creating an uncertainty as to what would be the end result, and more importantly, which one of the two will survive. That answer came on the last night of the festival of Endaru, Master Tiamat, the remaining members of the Minion, and droves of brown robed druids waited as the victor would eventually emerge and secure their place within the Minion. Then, on the sixth minute of the sixth hour of the sixth day, a shadowy figure could be seen approaching from within the fog, carrying the unmoving body of another. Speculation ran rampant as to whether or not Lord Nosferatu was indeed victorious or if Maskimxul had prevailed, signaling the end of an era, and the beginning of a new regime. When the shadowy figure approaching through the fog at Piotor Temple became much more detailed, it was then confirmed…it was the end of an era. The bigger Maskimxul emerged from the fog, carrying the lifeless corpse of Vampir Nosferatu. When he crossed the gate of the seven seals, the druids closed the circle and a chant began to arise. Maskimxul then carried Vampir to a stone table, bowed reverently to Tiamat who was standing behind it, and then placed Vampir on the table, in between two black candles. Maskimxul then turned and faced the Minion, observing what he had inherited through battle and was pleased. “Welcome my children, welcome to the new order. Gone are the days where humanity continues to enjoy normalcy and happiness. Gone are the days of playing games or toying with these humans as if they are lab rats in an experiment. It is time for a black cloud to rise above the ashes and envelop this planet, causing fear and horror to become synonymous with their daily lives. I will succeed where my brother has failed, a new era and regime has indeed commenced, one which will delineate the end of humanity and the beginning a true dark period. However, Vampir will live on, as I am sacrificing my body for Vampir to assimilate into. He will gain my size, my strength, my methods, and more importantly he will gain my presence and knowledge within.” Maskimxul then turns towards Tiamat, “It is time.” He then lays down on the stone alter, next to the deceased Vampir and closes his eyes. Two brown robed druids then take a black tarp and cover both men on the table. A third druid walks up and stands next to Tiamat facing him, with an open Necronomicon within his grasp. Tiamat turns and begins to read from the book, causing the members of the Minion to enter into a melodic trance. Once Tiamat concludes the ritual, he takes a chaliced filled with an unknown source of blood and pours it over the black tarp. Suddenly movement can be seen as the body of Maskimxul emerges from the tarp and stands behind the altar. There is something different however, the face of this body is disturbing to the human eye, and the tattoos, are those of Vampir. The body then walks around to the front and reaches into the tarp pulling out the skull that the other body was wearing and he proceeds to put it on. When the mask is firmly in place, his eyes suddenly glow bright red as he looks around the temple, snarling, exposing his long razor sharp teeth. Tiamat, who is still standing behind the altar, is awestruck as Vampir is now standing 6’11” weighing 325 pounds. Then a grin spreads across his face as he begins to realize what this monster is now capable of, the combined entities of Vampir and Maskimxul into one complete persona speaks of unimaginable power and abilities. Tiamat then goes down to one knee, as well as the druids in attendance, looks up at the new and revamped Vampir and speaks, “My lord, where shall we begin?” Vampir continues to scan the crowd and then replies, however his voice is much deeper, much more sinister, “We shall begin on a new battlefield, that battlefield being Old School Wrestling. For too long we stood in the shadows of PCW listening as they blasphemed our names, constantly scheming the demise of Vampir. Now we shall turn the tables, now we will work towards the infinite destruction of PCW, and claim humanity and the civilizations they dwell within as a prize. We will watch as they scramble like chickens with heads cut off as they look for answers. They will be crying, wondering why it is that we have betrayed them after so long. They will learn however, that it was only a matter of time. They grew too comfortable with themselves, too complacent, believing that they could not be touched a notion of false invincibility. Coupled with the loss of Ivan Stanislav, a new day arises in PCW, a day where the uncertainty of the future will take hold and precedence over everything else. They were too concerned about foreign entities invading their precious abode; they were blind and unaware of the evil that was building from within. Now, we turn our sights to OSW, a new playground full of souls to reap. Primetime Central has re-launched, giving us all a bigger battle to wage. The time for games has concluded, we begin the next phase of our master plan now and truly live up to what we have accomplished in the past. Leave no stone unturned, no family untouched, no heart intact, for we are returning back to our roots, and back to when it was fun for us. The blood will flow my children, in OSW they will believe that they know us and what we are capable of, in due time they will learn otherwise. They will come to grips with the cold, hard fact that we have just merely touched the surface of what is coming. As for history, there is none, a new life commences meaning a new beginning, a story untold. Welcome to my new playground my children, welcome to the realm of darkness, welcome to the Minion of Shadows. So it is written, so it shall be executed.”
