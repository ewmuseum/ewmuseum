Throwbacks vs. The Cult (7/6/02)

2002-07-06
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Throwbacks vs. The Cult (7/6/02) Written: July 6, 2002 Nate: With THAT being said, lets send it to the ring for our first match of the evening!!! Robbie: Yeah, The Cult are facing those douchebags Dave Gibson and Clay Vendetta, it’s going to be so bloody! Nate: Well last week folks we saw the official debut of ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson to OSW, and it took nary a few minutes before his first real threat in OSW took its form in the two behemoths that call themselves ‘The Cult’ – Vampir Nosferatu and Hessian! Steven: As you saw during last week’s broadcast, Dave Gibson was preaching the good word of ‘old school’ wrestling, when he was interrupted by the very beasts he eliminated from the Mt. St. Hell’s End Match, who then proceeded to beat down on him, only to be chased away by a chair-wielding Clay Vendetta which in turn led to tonight’s event!!! Robbie: Jesus, take a breath why don’tcha! Steven: I can hold mine for a long time… SMACK! Nate: In any case we’re going to get straight into this match! Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen! The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first… “Forever Hate You” by Deicide hits the P.A., and the arena lights cut out, leaving the booing and hissing crowds in the dark, when the stage begins flashing with white strobe lighting, and the entrance becomes engulfed in black fog. The two giant silhouettes of The Cult storm through the black fog and down to the ring, as the strobe lights cut, the arena lights shine back down and the fans begin their daily regime of hissing and booing at Hessian and Vampir as they leap onto the apron and over the top rope into the ring before Earl Heinrich. Earl: Hailing from Parts Unknown, at a combined weight of 646lbs, they are Hessian and Vampir Nosferatu…THE CULT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The crowd unleash more than a wave of hysterical booing as The Cult simply stand in the ring whispering some final words to each other, as “Forever Hate You” cuts out and is replaced by “Paradise City” by Guns ‘n’ Roses. Earl: And their opponents, first, hailing from Charlotte, North Carolina and weighing in at 228lbs…’OLD SCHOOL’ DAVE GIBSON!!!!!!!!! ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson emerges from behind the curtain to a reasonable ovation. The fans are on their feet, flashing their ‘Shut Up And Wrestle’ T-shirts as Dave Gibson strolls down the ramp, staring into the eyes of Vampir and Hessian as they stalk around the ring. Gibson stops at the end of the ramp as “Paradise City” halts and is replaced with “Carry On My Wayward Son” by Kansas and the crowd continue to cheer. Earl: And his partner, hailing from Miami, Florida, weighing in at 225lbs…’CLASSIC’ CLAY VENDETTA!!!!!!!!! Clay runs down to the ring, and with a nod to Gibson, both men slide into the ring and begin a punch-up with The Cult as the bell rings. Nate: Well folks this battle is underway! Robbie: Yah you damn right that’s what it is! Steven: Let’s see if Gibson can outwrestle the man with more moves than John Travolta! Nate: Hessian? Steven: You daaaaaaaaaamn right! Hessian and Gibson continue to punch it up while Vampir ducks a forearm from Vendetta and whips him off the ropes, clotheslining him upon return. Mounting Vendetta and laying into him with punches the crowd boo Vampir, while Hessian gains the upper hand with a kick to the mid-section of Gibson. Running off the ropes Hessian charges Gibson and goes for a Knee strike, only to fall into the ropes on the other side of the ring as Gibson ducks, nips back up, and dropkicks Hessian, only worsening the impact of Hessian’s groin on the ropes. Nate: THAT’S GOTTA HURT! Robbie: He’s 7’2”; I doubt it would, not this early on… Hessian moves around, trying to prevent the pulsing pain in his groin as Gibson rebounds off of the ropes and dives at Hessian, only to be knocked to the ground with a swinging right gauntlet to the forehead. Referee Pierre Gaston watches on as Vampir grabs Vendetta and again whips him off the ropes, but misses Vendetta on the return with a failed big boot as Clay slides under Vampir and makes a strike at his groin, barely missing as Vampir jumps back out of harm’s way. Steven: This match is picking up early! They better pick a corner and stick to it before Gaston separates them! Vampir drops an elbow on Vendetta and begins choking him as Hessian lifts Gibson up by the shirt, hoists him up over his shoulders and begins bench-pressing the 228lber. Nate: Hessian showing off his strength here! Hessian walks around the ring with Gibson above him, the ‘Old Schooler’ helpless to do anything, when Hessian charges across the ring and throws Gibson over the ropes, across the ringside area and flying into the crowd. A loud roar is heard from Hessian then quickly overshadowed by the screaming of the fans rushing out of the way as another loud SMASH! rings throughout the arena as Dave Gibson crashes into a row of chairs in the 3rd row. Steven: SWEET ZOMBIE JESUS!!! Hessian just threw Gibson over 3 rows into the crowd like he was a damned rag doll!!!!!! Robbie: MWAAAAAAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! HAAAAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!!!!! Nate: HESSIAN WASTING NO TIME IN DISMANTLING GIBSON HERE!!! Hessian storms around the ring, then heads to his own corner leaving Vampir in the ring to work on Vendetta. Clay rolls out of the way of a knee drop from Vampir, getting to his feet as Vampir does. Clay charges Vampir for a spear, but the Soul Stalker counters with a knee strike to Clay’s shoulder, sending CCV to the mat grabbing his shoulder in pain. Nate: Well with Gibson and Hessian out of the ring, Vampir is taking to CCV like a rottweiler to a juicy steak! Robbie: How symbolic of you… Steven: Well when Vampir and Hess are in the house, symbolism ahoy… Hessian shouts to Vampir as CCV is picked back up and hoisted onto Vampir’s shoulders, then promptly driven into Vampir’s knee with a hard shoulderbreaker, whilst outside the ring Gibson slowly picks himself up from the wreckage in the audience. Nate: The Cult are certainly looking to dominate this bout! After Gibson’s harsh words on the previous edition of Slam! he better be able to back them up! Robbie: Look at what Hessian did to him a mere few seconds into the match; the only thing Gibson is backing up is his ass out of this match! Vampir locks CCV in a tight bearhug, shaking the Classic one like a rag doll. Taking a few steps back, Vampir nods to Hessian, and the Titanic Satanic Insaniac quickly rips off the cushion on the top turnbuckle as Pierre Gaston checks on Dave Gibson. Vampir takes a run at the turnbuckle, and drives CCV’s spine into the metal turnbuckle, as Hessian tags himself in, replacing the turnbuckle cushion as CCV falls to the ground. Nate: CHEATERS!!! Robbie: QUICK THINKERS!!! Steven: THE IRISH!!! Nate: Pierre Gaston misses that cheating tactic by the slip of a disc! Hessian and Vampir lift up CCV and whip him off the ropes, and as he returns throwing him over themselves with a hard double back body drop, sending CCV splatting against the mats at ringside! Nate: OOOH!!! A sickening splat there as CCV hits the mat like a fly off of a windscreen! Robbie: Damn we are getting symbolic tonight aren’t we?! Steven: ‘Tis the season… Robbie: ‘Tis summer… Nate: ‘Tis time to watch the match! Hessian and Vampir look to the crowd, absorbing the hate hurled at them in the form of fuckers and assholes and replying with guttural bellowing laughs. The two Heretics stand in the ring watching CCV arching his back in pain as the stinging sensation grows every time his skin hits the cold mats. They laugh as he wriggles around like a helpless little worm, but their laughter soon turns to surprised growls as Dave Gibson flies at them, latching onto them for a Double Bulldog, but instead being grabbed and held in mid-air by The Cult. Nate: Well Dave Gibson is back but I doubt he’ll be able to overcome the sheer power of these two! Robbie: Yeah well Pierre Gaston is doing nada right now to stop this double-team move! Steven: No one is going to interfere with The Cult, I doubt Hessian is over that punch by Cain, you can see the lines in his face where the stitches were holding together those wounds! Robbie: Hmph… The Cult carry Gibson around the ring, then sit him on the top turnbuckle. Hessian holds Gibson in place, wrapping his arms around Gibson’s waist as Vampir backs off. Quickly manoeuvring around Gibson, Hessian manages to wrap his large arms around Gibson’s waist, holding him on his shoulder, while Gibson tries to power out, punching at Hessian in a desperate attempt to escape. Nate: Well Hessian is working up to something…and Gibson knows it! Robbie: Well Hessian has a menagerie of wrestling moves at his feet let the man deicide! Steven: I think you mean decide… Robbie: Ok mom, that’s what I meant! Hessian lets out a massive roar, then throws Gibson into the air, catches him by the shoulders and sends him straight to hell with a Spinning Crucifix Sit-Down Powerbomb! Nate: DISEASED MIND!!! DISEASED MIND! We haven’t seen that move in a long time! Robbie: EXCELSIOR!!! Suddenly, the scene shifts backstage, where Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman is watching the action on a video monitor. His brow suddenly furrows. PHD: THAT’S MY LINE!!!! With that, the scene shift back to ringside. Steven: Hessian took Gibson apa-…WHOA!!! Before Steven can finish off his sentence, Vampir runs at Gibson, still slouched upside down in the sit-down position against Hessian. With a raspy roar, Vampir dives at Gibson, slamming a hard dropkick into the gut of ‘Old School’, merely jolting Hessian a little while Gibson slumps to the mat. Hessian makes the cover and Gaston counts as Vampir exits the ring. 1… 2… … … … NO!!! CCV slides into the ring and kicks Hessian to the forehead, sending the big man tumbling. Nate: Nice teamwork!!! Gibson picks himself up and heads to the corner, where the two exchange words before tagging, and ‘Classic’ Clay Vendetta gets into the ring as the legal man and Gaston allows the action to continue. CCV jumps Hessian, laying into his masked skull with rights and lefts as Hessian tries to get back to his feet. From the corner Vampir watches on as Hessian roars out and jumps to his feet, throwing CCV across the ring. Wasting no time in getting back into the fight, Hessian grabs CCV and throws him into the opposite turnbuckle with a devastating Super Powerbomb. Nate: HIGHWAY TO HELL!!! Hessian is dismantling CCV!!! Robbie: Hell! The Cult is dismantling them alright! Hessian capitalizes on the Highway to Hell, and pulls CCV away from the ropes, then locks him in a Queen Angelito Stretch! Nate: SACRIFICIAL! Hessian has locked CCV in the-… Robbie & Steven: WE KNOW!!! Hessian pulls back on CCV’s neck, Clay’s muffled cries ringing out as Pierre kneels next to him, asking him if he quits. Clay groans out something in the form of a ‘no’ as Hessian reaches back, pulling harder on CCV’s neck. Out of frustration, the Goliath lets CCV go and tags in Vampir. The two behemoths pick CCV up and latch onto his throat, before being kicked square in the groin by Clay, ending both monsters back, flat onto the mat. Nate: OOH! Quick thinking by Clay there, but did Gaston see it? Robbie: It doesn’t matter!!! Clays pulls himself to his feet and begins putting the boots to Hessian, then grabbing Hessian’s ankle and locking in… Nate: ANKELOCK!!! Robbie: Done Classically I might add! Clay twists Hessian’s ankle, the big man trying to twist it back using all the power in his leg. As Clay inches closer to breakage, Vampir knocks him aside with a big boot to the face, sending Clay into the ropes. Hessian pulls himself to the ropes and up to a wobbling standpoint, as Clay charges Vampir, rolling out of the way of a big boot, tagging in Gibson and chop blocking Vampir’s leg, taking the Soul Stalker down. Nate: Well CCV and Gibson may have a strategy worked out here! Robbie: I got a strategy for ya…it’s called RUNTHEFUCKAWAY – BEFORE – THECULT - KILLSYOUBOTH!!! Steven: Sounds iffy… Robbie: It worked for Civil Disobedience… Steven: Not for Aimz… Nate: Ok enough of that! We know where that ended up… Steven: A bulging member springing forth? Robbie: STEVEN!!! Gibson takes Vampir by the legs and applies a Figure Four Leg Lock, while CCV applies an Armbar to the Soul Reaper. Hessian watches on from the turnbuckle, and in a flashing rage forgets the pain in his ankle and runs for CCV. Grabbing Clay by the throat while he still has the Armbar locked in, Hessian lifts Clay up off of his feet, as Gibson unlocks the Figure Four and tries to save Clay, only to have the large gauntlet wrap around his neck too. Hessian hoists Gibson into the air, and promptly slams them into the mat with a hard Chokeslam. Nate: Hessian plants CCV and Gibson with a hard double Chokeslam!!! Robbie: WAIT!!! LOOK!!! Hessian gets back to his feet, still holding on to Gibson and CCV’s throats. His muscles bulge immensely as he pulls the combined 453lbs men up into the air, and Chokeslams them into the ring again, falling flat with his arms draped over them out of tiredness. Pierre Gaston makes the count. 1… 2… NO!!! Nate: Pierre id counting the illegal man!!! Robbie: It doesn’t matter!!! Gibson and Clay suddenly slide around to each side of Hessian and take an arm each, then rush to their feet, turn Hessian so he’s lying on his belly and apply a Reverse Armbar each, pulling back Hessian’s arms. Hessian yells out as CCV and Gibson pull harder, trying to get the Goliath to submit. Nate: Reverse Armbar! Hessian will have to submit now, especially with both arms being pulled in that awkward direction!!! Robbie: Damned new guy! Steven: It’s gonna take a whole lot more pullin’ and yankin’ for Hessian to give up! Pierre checks on Hessian, when Vampir runs into the ring and kicks them both square in the head, knocking them off of Hessian. Pierre wastes no time in rushing Vampir out the ring though as three men take time to recover from their respective blows. As CCV and Gibson try to recover, Hessian slowly pulls himself up with the ropes, leaning against them trying to stretch the pain out from the Armbar. Gibson and CCV capitalise on Hessian’s situation, and grab him, whipping him off the ropes. As Hessian rebounds, CCV and Gibson duck, hoist Hessian up and over the ropes, BUT in mid-air, Hessian wraps his arms around CCV and Gibson’s necks, and as he flies over them from their Back Body Drop, Hessian in turn thrusts them over the ropes and slamming into the mat with a sickening Double Headwhip Suplex! Nate: Hessian capitalises on CCV and Gibson’s Back Body Drop with a Headwhip Suplex in the momentum of it all!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! A sickening thud echoes as all three bodies hit the mat, and Pierre begins a quick count. 1… Nate: Pierre wasting no time in starting a count out!!! 2… 3… Robbie: Wait-a-minute…Vampir is on the move! 4… Vampir slides into the ring and stops behind Pierre Gaston. 5… Gibson pulls himself to his feet and stumbles to the announce tables. 6… Gibson grabs a steel chair, while Vampir ignores Pierre, slides out of the ring, pulls Hessian up and waits as Vendetta gets to his feet. 7… Vendetta turns around only to be grasped around the neck by a pair of large hands, hoisted into the air and splattered into the mats like the aforementioned fly-on-windscreen, as Gibson stalks The Cult around the ring, Pierre notices him out of the corner of his eye. 8… Nate: Pierre doesn’t notice Gibson with the chair! Robbie: SACRE BLEU! Steven: You always were the perfect Frenchie, Robbie… 9… Gibson hears the 9-count, slides into the ring, slams the chair off the mat to get Pierre’s attention and slides back out. Pierre: COORSES! Ah wiz almost at ze ten you leetle zut-monkey! Pierre curses some more and waves an uncaring hand as Gibson stalks Hessian and Vampir, poised with the chair held high above him. A loud thud echoes through the arena suddenly as the chair slams against Hessian’s back, sending the Goliath to his knees, back arched in pain, while Vampir turns around straight into a chair shot to the head. Nate: Gibson pulls a fast one on The Cult with a damn steel chair! Robbie: He will pay… Pierre ignores the action at ringside as Gibson takes the chair to Hessian, beating on his legs, as Hessian tries to crawl away. Meanwhile, CCV rolls around, slowly trying to get back to his feet, as Vampir shockingly sits straight back up. Nate: HEY! Where have we seen that little number before? Robbie: When Aimz sat up rigid after I shoved my-… Steven: DON’T GO THERE!!! Robbie: I was only gonna say…my…ah feck it I got no excuse… Vampir and CCV make their way to their feet, Vampir grabbing Clay instinctively and whipping him down the ringside area and into the announce table. Following closely, Vampir pulls back to deliver a hard forearm, but is instead mule kicked by CCV. Nate: Ok let’s move out of the way, this could get a little awkward! Robbie…Steven…whe-… Robbie and Steven are peeping over the guardrail at the action in fear as Vampir is tossed onto the announce table by CCV, who grabs a steel chair and climbs onto the announce table with Vampir. Turning the Soul Stalker onto his belly, Vendetta sets up Vampir for an STF, but instead takes the steel chair, holds it steady, locking Vampir's legs in place and begins to twist slowly, contorting Vamp's legs in an unusual way, as Vampir yells out, his legs being twisted awkwardly. Nate: DAMN! Vendetta’s working that Corkscrew move on Vampir, and god only knows why that man isn’t tapping out… Robbie: ‘Tisn’t a man…’tis a beast…arrgh! Vendetta then pulls back on the chair, pulling Vamp's legs back and stretching his leg muscles, while Hessian powers to his feet, knocking the chair from Gibson and stumbling around to the announce tables, as Gibson slides into the ring and scales the left turnbuckle nearest the announce tables! Nate: What is Gibson up to? Steven: I don’t know but if Pierre would stop gazing at those topless bimbos in the 7th row and concentrating on the match we’d be happy! Robbie: GAH! Those aren’t bimbos! Those are a bunch of fat guys with ‘OSW’ written on their chests!!! Steven: Well when you’re French you must get it somewhere… Gibson sizes up Hessian, and as the Goliath turns, Gibson flies off the top turnbuckle with a Corkscrew Cross Body, only to be grabbed in mid-air by Hessian!!! Nate: Hessian intercepts Gibson and stops him from hitting that high-risk move!!! Hessian throws Gibson up and catches him on his shoulder, and in the momentum turns to the announce table and slams Gibson on top of Vendetta and Vampir with a hard Wheelbarrow Face First Powerbomb, sending all three men crashing through the announce table!!! Nate: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN HESSIAN HAS JUST DESTROYED OUR ANNOUNCE TABLE WITH THE HELLBOUND ON GIBSON, SENDING ALL OTHER MEN IN THIS MATCH STRAIGHT THROUGH THE TABLE!!! Robbie: Steven, go order us another announce table for the night! Steven: Hey, Reagan’s company, Reagan’s table, Reagan’s job! Robbie: Oh she’s doing the job… Hessian falls to the mats out of tiredness, and all four men lie in the wreckage as Pierre draws his attention back to the match. 1… 2… 3… Gibson begins to move, and rolls out of the wreckage, as does Vampir and Vendetta. Hessian props himself up against the ring, sitting, catching his breath as Gibson stumbles to his feet and falls against the ring. 4… Vampir and Vendetta, out of will, run at the ring and roll in. Nate: Well Pierre ha to stop the count now that the legal men are in the ring! Robbie: You knew that? Nate: Knew what? Robbie: Never mind… As Vampir and Vendetta make their way back to their feet, Vampir grabs Clay and whips him off of the ropes, but as he rebounds, Vendetta clotheslines Vampir, sending both men back out of the ring, and evoking a frustrating growl from the crowd! Nate: Are you kidding me?! Robbie: Damn! Steven: Alright there, Ron Simmons? As Vampir and Clay lay on the mats, tired out, Gibson makes his way back to his feet, and, grabbing the steel chair, swings at Vampir and sends a river of blood flowing down the Soul Stalker’s head, but as he hits Hessian, the Goliath blocks the shot with his arm, while Gibson simply drops the chair and rolls into the ring. Nate: Well it’s been pandemonium to say the least! Gibson is the only one moving, he’s barely able to stand in the ring, but Pierre doesn’t really know what to do… Robbie: COUNT VENDETTA OUT!!! LET THE CULT WIN!!! Steven: They don’t need to let anyone win it for them, they can do it themselves! Gibson stands triumphantly in the ring, pointing at The Cult as though they were inferior. He raises his hands as though he had won the match, to a cheer from the fans, but as Pierre slides out of the ring to check on Hess, Vamp and Clay, a figure suddenly rushes through the crowd, and jumps over the guardrail. Nate: OH MY GOD!!! Robbie: Who the hell is that? Steven: HE’S GOT A BASEBALL BAT!!! The figure, cloaked in black robes and carrying a baseball bat slides into the ring and stops behind Dave Gibson. The crowd go wild, but Gibson takes no notice, until he feels something tapping on his shoulder. Turning slowly, Gibson’s eyes widen in horror as the baseball bat swings hard around, and Gibson falls flat on the mat, a river of red, oozing blood flowing from a ghastly wound in his head. The cloaked figure exits the ring as Pierre slides in. Sneaking around the ring as Pierre scratches his head at Gibson lying in the ring, the cloaked figure slaps Hessian, waking the Goliath up and whispering something to him. Nate: WHO THE HELL IS THAT?! Robbie: IT HAS TO BE SHORT CIRCUIT!!! LOOK AT HIM!!! HE HAS A BASEBALL BAT, HE’S IN BLACK ROBES AND…IT HAS TO BE SHORT CIRCUIT!!! Steven: IT AIN’T SHORT CIRCUIT!!! IT’S GREAT DRAGON!!! Robbie: NO!!!……………………………………It’s Batman! Nate: Whoever it is they’ve taken off up the ramp now as Hessian makes his way back to his feet! Hessian pulls himself up and slides into the ring. Quickly tagging Vampir, Hessian makes himself legal and turns to the middle of the ring. Cocking his head at the pool of blood around Gibson’s head, Hessian watches as the ‘Old School’ warrior, despite his head injury, rolls around and tries to get up. Nate: GODDAMNIT! Even with so much blood lost Gibson is not going to give up!!! Robbie: Too bad he’s dead meat now! Steven: Like Jeeeesus to a Chiiiiild… Hessian stalks Gibson, who turns around to Hessian, only to be kicked in the mid-section by Satan’s Servant. Hessian pulls Gibson up and locks him in the Canadian Backbreaker, parading his bloody carcass around the ring before driving Gibson’s head straight into the mat stained in his own blood with the Crucifix Piledriver! Nate: ETERNAL DAMNATION!!! ETERNAL DAMNATION!!! ETERNAL DAMNATION!!! HESSIAN HITS HIS ETERNAL DAMNATION ON ‘OLD SCHOOL’ DAVE GIBSON!!! Robbie: BOOYAH!!! TAKE THAT YOU LITTLE GIMP!!! Hessian covers Gibson as Pierre drops for the count. 1… 2… … … … … … Steven: Shouldn’t someone mention that Gibson ain’t the legal man? … … … 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Winners: The Cult “Forever Hate You” blasts through the speakers and the crowd go wild as Hessian raises his arms in victory, staring all the while at Gibson, lying motionless in the ring. Meanwhile, Vampir makes it back to his feet, kicks Vendetta in the ribs, and slides into the ring, taking his partners hand in celebration. Nate: WELL FOLKS THE CULT WINS THIS ONE!!! THE FANS ARE DAMN SURE NOT PLEASED, BUT THE CULT HAS WON THE FIRST ROUND OF WHAT PROMISES TO BE A BLOODY FEUD!!! Robbie: But who the hell was that cloaked figure??? Someone is working with The Cult and I damn sure want to know!!! Steven: It certainly wasn’t Minion… Nate: IN ANY CASE FOLKS WE’RE GOING TO GO TO A COMMERCIAL WHILE WE GET THIS TABLE REPLACED, THE BLOOD CLEARED UP AND THESE MONSTERS OUT OF HERE!!!!!!!!!!!! Steven: Ditto… Robbie: What he said… The scene shifts to the ramp, where Vampir Nosferatu, Hessian and their robed “Cultist” stand triumphantly, looking back at the carnage in the ring….as the scene fades to commercial.
