Aimz (w/Reno, ZaC) -vs- Drew Hostyle (w/ Frankie Bones)

2002-04-28
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aimz (w/Reno, ZaC) -vs- Drew Hostyle (w/ Frankie Bones) Written: April 28th, 2002 Nate: Welcome back to Head On Collision!! Robbie: What was up with the cardboard likeness of Aimz? Nate: No idea, but I am sure it seemed very 2D compared to the Amy Campbell blow up doll you sleep with every night. Robbie: That’s because the real thing is a disease-ridden whore. Nate: That is pure speculation and rumor, because of guys like Drew Hostyle besmirching her name! Robbie: Hey, he hit it then talked about it. Drew Hostyle is a man’s man in my eyes. Steven: I wish he would be a man’s man in my… Nate: (interrupting) On THAT note, its time to find out “Who Lied”…as Amy Campbell will take on Drew Hostyle, and its all about honor and pride, as Drew claims he has had Amy, and Amy says its not true…but by the end of the night, one of them is going to admit they lied. Robbie: …and its going to be Aimz to Please. Nate: Ugh…Let’s send it up to Earl Henrich in the ring. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, it's now time for the 'I Lied' Match! The only way to win is to get your opponent to say the words 'I Lied'! 'Accross the Nation' by Union Underground graces the sound system as Aimz steps into the arena. At her side is Reno Davis, who has ZaC the monkey riding on his shoulders. Earl: Now making her entrance... Being accompanied by Reno Davis and ZaC the Monkey... From Nova Scotia... Weighing 143 lbs... AMY CAMPBELL!! Steven: Gotta love the monkey. Nate: I KNEW you were going to say that. Aimz enters the ring while Reno stands on the outside with ZaC. 'Still Fly' by Big Tymers blares through the arena, and the two members of 3B walk out. Earl: Now approaching the ring... Accompanied by Frankie Bones... From Brooklyn, New York...weighing in at 263 lbs... DREW HOSTYLE!! Drew Hostyle cockily (nice wording, considering the buildup to this match) walks to the ring, winking at Aimz and making some rather explicit gestures to her, which only serve to get her even angrier that she already is. Nate: Drew said he did her, Amy said he didn't. Robbie: Aimz could only be so lucky. Drew climbs into the ring, leaving Frankie on the outside, and walks to the center of the ring to meet Aimz. Nate: Drew's got over 100 lbs and a BIG height difference on Aimz. She's gonna have to find some way around his power to beat him. Smirking like he knows exactly what he's doing, Drew closes his eyes sticks his chin out, and then points to it, motioning for Aimz to hit him. Aimz scoffs at Drew's arrogance, then smiles, and kicks him RIGHT BETWEEN THE LEGS. Nate: Gah! I... OH! OH! OH! THAT'S NOT RIGHT! In the ring, Aimz has actually latched onto Hostyle's groin with her right hand, locking him in a horrendous testicular claw. Robbie: That's just WRONG. Steven: That area was meant to be treated nicely! Drew Hostyle falls backward, kicking his legs, his eyes wide open as he actually HOWLS in agony. Aims furiously motions for the ref to get over there and he hands her a mic. Aimz: Say it! Drew: No! Aimz squeezes harder. Aimz: SAY IT! Drew: NNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Frankie Bones, no longer willing to watch his friend go through such torture, hops up to the ring apron and is about to intervene, but is yanked back down by Reno Davis and the two proceed to get into a little brawl at ringside. Frankie shoves Reno backward, only to have ZaC the monkey hop on his back and start climbing around on him, smacking the top of his head head, covering his face, and other stuff that good guy monkeys do to heel black guys. With ZaC serving as a good distraction, Reno gets a nice running start and SPEARS Frankie Bones right into the barricade, knocking him senseless. Back in the ring, Aimz continues to squeeze Drew's 'you know where' like one of those squishy stress balls. Drew is on the verge of tears. Aimz: I SAID SAY IT!!! Drew: WAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHH! Robbie (shuddering): She sure found a way around his strength all right. Nate: Hey, wait a second... It's The Red Army! While Aimz tortures Hostyle in the ring, and Reno and Frankie go at it on the outside, Loyal Communist Party Member Wang and the Two Round Faced Women of The Red Army run down to ringside with large nets in their hands. Nate: Not THIS again... ZaC sees the trio of Communists coming for him, screeches, and bolts. Wang and the two women chase ZaC around the ring, then up the ramp. Reno Davis sees his best friend getting chased by the Red Army, notices Frankie is still seeing stars, looks into the ring and sees Aimz dominating Hostyle, then proceeds to run up the ramp after them. With Davis gone, Frankie Bones quickly hops to his feet. Steven: Looks like Frankie was playing possum! Frankie Bones reaches into his pocket and produces a set of brass knockles, which he slides into the ring. While wailing in pain, Drew sees the knucks coming towards him, catches them, and slides them onto his right hand. Nate: Brass knuckles! That isn't fair, Hostyle has brass knuckles! Robbie: As if what Aimz is doing IS nice... Aimz, however, does not see the knucks. Moments later, Drew Hostyle swings his right hand and nails Aimz right between the eyes with the brass knuckles! Nate: Dear god! His...y'know...finally released, Drew Hostyle sobs in relief as he slowly gets to his feet, doubled over in the pain one feels after having that area abused. He quickly pockets the knucks and walks over to Aimz, who his on her knees, seeing stars. With Aimz on her knees, Hostyle grins, grabs her by the back of the head, and stands directly in front of her, so if you look at it the right way, it looks like she's... Nate: That just sucks! Steven: Hee hee hee... Aimz attempts getting to her feet, but is knocked back down to the ground by a hammering fist from Drew to her back. Drew then pulls her to her feet himself and whips her into the corner. As Aimz struggles to shake off the stub while holding herself up in the corner, Hostyle comes charging in and nails a big body splash on the 143 lb Aimz! Nate: Oh come on, talk about an advantage. Robbie: Too bad she isn't just the submissive type. Give in, bitch!!! Aimz staggers forward, right into a kick to the gut by Drew. Hostyle sets her up for a powerbomb...and just stands there, grinning. Steven: Hee hee hee! Drew starts thrusting his hips forward while Aimz still stands there bent over with her head between his legs (God, I'm getting sweaty just writing this). Finally, Hostyle picks her up and drops her with a powerbomb! With Aimz laying there, Drew grabs the mic, swaggers over to Aimz...and mounts her. Hostyle gets on his knees and sits right on Aimz' chest, with nothing but her head sticking out between his legs. Nate: What a fucking prick Drew Hostyle is. Drew: My turn now, ho... Say it. Hostyle sticks the mic right in Amy Campbell's face. Drew (in a high voice mocking Aimz): *Say it* Amy (breathing heavily): No...way... Hostyle scowls, hauls back, and just SMACKS Amy right accross the face, much to the outrage of the fans. Nate: FUCKING PRICK!!! Drew: I'ma tell you again to motherfuckin SAY IT. Say that you're a lyin' little tramp! Amy: NO! Another smack accross the face sees a little blood drip from Aimz' lower lip. Frustrated, Hostyle gets up off Aimz and viciously drives his large foot right down into her gut. Campbell clutches her lower abdomen as she rolls over in pain. Hostyle drops to his knees, grabs her by the hair, and violently yanks her head up, revealing a mouth with blood-stained teeth. Drew: Now Amy, you and I both know the truth, now I want you to FUCKING SAY IT. Amy responds by snorting and hocking a blood-laced loogie right in the face of Drew Hostyle, which absolutely sets him off. Still holding her hair, Drew smashes her face into the mat, then gets to his feet and begins to kick Aimz' body. He then motions for Frankie Bones enter the ring, and the large tag team partner of Drew Hostyle does so. Bones walks over to a fallen Aimz grabs her by the arms, and holds her up. Drew again smacks Aimz as the fans go into a riot of hatred. Drew: Say yo're a liar! Aimz: *pant*...*pant*... Drew: SAY IT. Aimz: .........FUCK YOU! Robbie: Talks like a whore, acts like a whore… Drew grabs her hair and raises his hand, about to smack her again, when suddenly Amy's foot once again drives itself between his legs! Hostyle drops the mic and clutches his groin. Frankie Bones turns Aimz toward him and latches his right hand around her neck. Nate: Frankie Bones is gonna chokeslam an already weakened Aimz! WHERE THE HELL IS THE REFEREE?! The camera cuts to Referee Betty Lovejoy, who sits on the ring apron, painting her toenails. Nate: ....oh. WAIT A SECOND! LOOK!!! The crowd explodes into cheers as Tempest streaks out from the back, b-lines straight to the ring, and hops to the top turnbuckle. Robbie: NOOOOOOOO!!! Nate: IT’S TEMPEST!!! Frankie sees none of this, and ends up taking a flying dropkick to the back of the head for his ignorance. The crowd roars in approval. Bones releases Aimz and staggers forward, then spins to face Tempest, who is running right at him. She ducks a clothesline, bounces off the ropes and hits a spin-kick to Frankie, which again knocks the big man forward. She then cartwheels right in front of him and then delivers a superkick in an impressive display of agility. Frankie back up into the ropes, then Tempest and Amy lock hands and double-clothesline the big man right out of the ring! The crowd explodes. Nate: YES! Tempest just evened the odds for Aimz! Robbie: Where the hell is Ike Turner when you need him!!! Now revitalized, Amy ducks a running clothesline from Drew Hostyle runs to the corner, and hops to the top turnbuckle. By the time Drew turns to face her, Amy has leapt off the top top and is flying right at him, resulting in a perfect hurricanrana! Aimz gets to her feet drops to her knees by Hostyle...and once again locks on the testicular claw. Nate: OOOOoooooooouch!!! Robbie: NOTE THE PACKAGE ATTACKAGE!!! Drew resumes howling in pain, but is silenced when Aimz reaches over and smacks him with the other hand. Tempest hands Aimz the mic and she practically jams it down his throat. Aimz: Pant...pant.... Now... SAY IT. Drew: I..I... I LIED!!! Nate: YESSSSSS!! Steven: You GO girl! The fans are going CRAZY! Aimz drops the mic, gets to her feet, and smacks hands with Aimz as the crowd cheers them on. Betty Lovejoy hears Drew over the speakers and motions for the bell to be rung as she paints her big right toe a dainty pink. WINNER OF THE MATCH: AMY CAMPBELL Nate: SO AMY CAMPBELL PULLS OFF THE UPSET DEFEATING DREW HOSTYLE!!! Robbie: HE SAID HE WAS FLY, NOT HE LIED!!! Nate: We need to head to a commercial! When we return, the brutality will continue with the Blood Letting matchup between Epitome of Evil and Angeldust Nick Casey!!! Don’t go away, we will be right back!!!
