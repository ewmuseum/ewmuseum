Culmination: Part IV

2002-05-25

Culmination: Part IV Written: 25/05/2002 Sitting in Da Westsidaz’ locker room, Daisy is watching the Civil Disobedience vs. Da Westsidaz match on a portable TV/VCR. The match comes to the end, and Daisy scowls as Azrael Cain delivers that fateful bitchslap to her. Turning the video and TV off, she sits solemnly, checking over her nails, playing with her hair, the usual girly stuff. Daisy: Ugh…damn Satanists…I could be out watching my man training, but I have to wait in this dump…god-……… The locker room door suddenly bursts open and in rushes Short Circuit, closely followed by Riptah. Riptah stops and gasps, then tosses the two burlap sacks into the room and rips the ‘Da Westsidaz’ nameplate off of the door, then runs in and shuts the door. Riptah: Don’t…say…anything… Holding the door handle, Riptah whispers to himself, and sure enough the thumping of the tag team champions’ feet along the hallway grows louder. Cain: Where are they? Hessian: If I knew, they’d be dead by now, keep up, we’ll catch them soon! Riptah listens as Heresian Bloodlust run past the door. Breathing a sigh of relief, he turns to the two burlap sacks. Short Circuit loosens the rope around the top enough do the freaky little purple head of Minion and the scraggy old skull of Caleb can pop out at Da Westsidaz. Minion the Imp: YOU SONS OF BITCHES!!! LET US GO NOW OR THERE’S ABOUT TO BE SOME HELL KICKED UP RIGHT HERE!!! YOU DAMNED WESTSIDAZ HAVE GONE TOO FAR RIGHT NOW!!! CAIN AND HESSIAN ARE GONNA KI-……… Short Circuit chops Minion on the Trapezius and nods at Riptah as the Imp falls unconscious. Caleb sits in his burlap sack biting his lip nervously, as Riptah approaches him. Riptah: Yo homeless boy, you nervous? Caleb: Not really just grooming my lip of its excess skin. Riptah: Caleb, you reek of damned piss and you look like something Minion would crap out after a meal…why in the hell would Heresian B want to hang around you? Caleb: They don’t hang around me, I accompany Cain, you smear of monkey’s menstrual blood. Short Circuit pulls back and backhands the hobo in the face, leaving a red mark on his cheek. Caleb groans, but fixes his glare on Riptah. Riptah: Now the question you gotta axe yourselves is… Minion the Imp: DO YOU HAVE A FIRESNATCH! Riptah: DAMNIT SHORTY YOU’RE SUPPOSED TO HAVE KNOCKED HIM DA (expletive) OUT!!! Short Circuit: I THOUGHT I DID!!! Short Circuit goes to chop Minion again, but the Imp craftily manoeuvres and latches onto Short Circuit’s hand with his sharp little yellow teeth. Shorty yells out as Minion begins tugging at SC’s hand. As Short Circuit yells out in pain, Daisy watches on surprised and Riptah approaches Minion. Grabbing the Imp by the throat and hoisting him into the air to eye-level, Minion shrieks an ungodly shriek, releasing Short Circuit immediately. Riptah: YOU KNOW WHAT WE’RE GONNA DO TO YOU MINION??? WE’RE GONNA MAKE SURE YOU NEVER (expletive) WALK AGAIN UNLESS YOU DAMN WELL (expletive) CALM YOUR IMP ASS DOWN!!!!!!!!! Minion is silent. His little body hangs limp as Riptah grips his throat. Glaring into Minion’s eyes, Riptah becomes increasingly frustrated, then suddenly throws Minion across the room. Daisy yells out and Caleb cries out as Minion’s little 3’11” body smacks brutally against the brick wall, and falls to the ground. Lying in a crumpled heap, Minion groans in pain, as Riptah stands over him defiantly. Riptah: Now your boys Hessian and Cain, they ain’t gonna like what I just did…but you know what? We couldn’t give a damn. Those two oversized freaks are fodder for us, we’re 5-time PCW tag team champions and Hall of Famers, do you really think we can’t take Hessian and Cain? We want those tag titles, and we know we can damn well win them, thing is, we need a little persuasion to get them belts before those two dicksmacks Seth and Trevor do…that’s where you come in. Caleb: Well I got news for ya bud…it’s all fine that you’re 5-time PCW Tag Team Champions, its good that you’re PCW Hall of Famers…but unless you haven’t noticed, this is OSW and facts don’t mean dick, it’s what you can do in the ring that counts, and from what I’ve seen, heh, heh, heh, you two are going to be Heresian Bloodlust’s bitches before the month is out. Riptah cocks his head in amazement at Caleb’s brave words in the face of danger. He growls, then pushes Caleb’s head back into the sack then tighten the top and hoists the sack over his back. Riptah: Shorty, get Minion. I got an idea. Short Circuit walks over to Minion’s crumpled body. Pushing the dazed Imp’s head into the sack, he tighten the rope at the top and drags the sack along the floor to Riptah, who hoists the bag over his other shoulder and gestures for Short Circuit to get the door. Riptah: Daisy, you stay here. Shorty and I are just going out to “address” them freaks. Daisy: WHY! I don’t want to stay in this dump of a locker room! I’ve been here almost the entire night because those friggin’ Satanists are prowling around somewhere! Just let me come with you, PLEASE? Riptah: Ok then you can come with us… Daisy: Yay! Riptah: But don’t come running to me when Hessian grabs you from behind, drags you into a dark corner somewhere and beats the hell out of you, then gets Minion to try and do the dirty with you… Daisy: Rip! That’s a horrible thing to say! Riptah: Well then stay here and not only will you not here me say it, but there’s no chance of it actually happening! Daisy: Fine then……… Riptah nods at Short Circuit and they leave the locker room. Daisy hears the door being locked, and swears out. Frustrated, she sits down on a steel chair and folds her arms. Daisy: That man is really pushing his luck……… As Daisy sighs again, she hears some kind of buzzing sound, and then the lights go out. Grabbing at her hair, Daisy yells out and kicks over the TV/VCR. Daisy: THINGS COULDN’T GET ANY DAMN WORSE!!! Voice: I wouldn’t be too sure of that… Daisy: Who…who’s there? Riptah? Shorty? Anyone? Daisy freezes in the dark. Listening carefully, her senses become fully aware, and she jumps as she hears a creaking from above her. Listening to her own breath, Daisy’s eyes dart from side to side as the creaking slowly manifests into slow footsteps, originating from above the ceiling. Daisy listens as the slow, thumping steps move across the ceiling, then turn and walk back. Pacing steps walking above her in the dark. Daisy: Help……… Daisy whispers in fear as the footsteps become increasingly louder and faster. She can feel her heartbeat rising, as the footsteps move all across the ceiling. Slowly getting to her feet, Daisy grabs her steel chair and folds it, holding it, poised and ready to swing at whatever it is on the roof. The footsteps become ever louder, thumping across the ceiling. Daisy begins panicking as the thumping, pacing footsteps get to her, filling the entire room and echoing. Daisy: stop it…Stop it…Stop It…STOP IT…STOP IT STOP IT STOP IT STOP IT STOP IT STOP IT STOP IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! As Daisy screams out the footsteps stop dead. Listening again to her heartbeat, Daisy relaxes, but as she puts the chair down, a scratching, creaking sound emerges in the corner of the room. Daisy picks up the chair again and poises, scared stiff as whatever is in the ceiling tries to pry away the tiles on the roof. Tears begin to form in Daisy’s eyes, the corner roof panel screeches as the metal scrapes against the other tiles. Daisy’s eyes widen in horror as a low THUMP echoes as something hits the floor. A few seconds later another THUMP, a deeper one sounds as something else hits the floor. Daisy begins breathing heavily as the room again becomes silent. She panics, trying to move but can’t. As she holds the steel chair high above, ready to strike out, a soft breeze blows against her face, causing her eyes to water. Daisy lowers the chair and wipes her eyes clear again. Then all of a sudden a single flame ignites before her, originating from a Zippo. Daisy gasps at the flame, and then screams bloody murder as suddenly it highlights the Skull mask of Hessian. Daisy stares terrified into those glowing, red eyes, frozen in fear. Hessian: Hello Daisy… Hessian lets out a grim chuckle…as the scene fades to black.
