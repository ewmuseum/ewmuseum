Rhett Locke vs Graphic Violence

2002-05-18
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rhett Locke vs Graphic Violence Written: May 18th 2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam! Well ladies and gentlemen, our first main event is about to be underway! Robbie: And from the looks of things, EMTs better be on call. Rhett Locke is going to get destroyed! DESTROOOOOOYED! Nate: Yeah, sure. Whatever guy. Steven: If Graphic Violence wasn't such a bastard, I might just give him a thing or two. Nate: Take it away, Earl... I'm tearing away Pieces are falling I can't seem to make them stay You run away Faster and faster you can't seem to get away Break Earl: Ladies and gentlemen, it is time for your FIRST MAIN EVENT of the evening! Introducing at this time, from New York City, weighing in at a huge 6'8', 285 lbs. He is...GRAPHIC VIOLENCE! The words to 'Tear Away' by Drowning Pool begins to literally boom over the PA system. The fans boo at the top of their lungs as the already much hated Graphic Violence steps out from behind the curtain. However, the very message they give to him is returned with a 'middle finger salute', and with a slight chuckle, Graphic Violence stalks to the ring. Robbie: WOOOOO! Shortly following, the melancholy beginning to 'Aerials' by System of a Down begins to play. Red strobe lights flicker and dance across the arena, until the powercords of the song are played and the words are screamed Life is a waterfall, we're one in the middle and one again after the fall! Swimming the void we here the words, we lose ourselves but we find it all! Because we are the ones that wanna play, always wanna go but you never wanna stay! And we are the ones that want to choose, always wanna play but you never wanna lose... Earl: And his opponent, hailing from Portland, Oregon...he weighs in at 6'1', 218 lbs. He is the former, International Champion...RHETT LOCKE! And there stands Rhett Locke. The fans cheer as he clench his fists, gritting his teeth atop the entrance ramp. The fans cheers become exstatic as he charges toward the ring, and slides under the rope. He gets to his feet and runs, Graphic Violence attempting a huge clothesline, but Rhett's able to duck under. Bouncing off the rope, and using momentum to his advantage, he slides under Graphic Violence's legs. Locke pushes GV from behind, and GV slams into a turnbuckle. He bounced off backwards, and ran right into a drop toe hold from Rhett, and he fell to the floor. Nate: Excellent move by Rhett Locke there! Robbie: Well I'll be damned... Steven: Well I'll be fucked... GV quickly scatters to his feet, and enraged, he begins to throw wild punches all across the board. Tearing away at Rhett with rights and lefts, Locke has no where to go but backward and into a corner. Trapped, Locke is bombarded with pummelling punches from GV, until he literally falls face first out of the corner. Rhett tries his damndest to escape, however, his long hair is once again his disadvantage as GV catches him by it, lifts him high into the air, and drops him down with a beautifully maneuvered brainbuster. The crowd cringed as he was dropped on his cranium, and bounced once or twice. Nate: Good god, that was sheer power on the part of GV Robbie: A graceful monster, a graceful monster. Nate: Sure, Robbie... Steven: Just give me a chance to get my hands on him... I guarantee he won't be so graceful when I shove my cock in his mou-- Nate: Oh my god you sick fuck! Rhett is pulled to his feet once more, and GV grins. Graphic Violence irish whipped Locke so hard into the turnbuckles that Rhett flips over them after the impact and spills out to the floor. Everyone in the arena, including the referee, had to turn their heads during that. GV slides to the outside, and swoops around the ring with the fury of a demon. He shoves the Time Keeper out of his chair, folds it up, and moves back around the ring to Rhett whom seems to be regaining his composure. However, any hopes of a comeback is lost as GV wails away on his opponent! CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! The beating with that chair stops shortly after, briefly, as he slides the chair and Rhett Locke simultaneously in the ring. Nate: HE'S TAKING THIS TOO DAMN FAR! Robbie: AND I LOVE IT! GV holds Locke in a reversed DDT position, apparently going for a DDT on top of that chair. Knowing full well that he risks getting disqualified, he decides to test's the referree's patience, until suddenly... 'I Stand Alone' by Godsmack. Lillith. She charges to the ring, and the referee instantly catches this, while GV suddenly lets the blood soaked Rhett Locke go and watches from afar. Lillith seems to be destracting the referee, and GV will have none of it. Screaming at the top of his lungs 'GET THAT BITCH OUT OF HERE!' Robbie: Amen! Nate: Shut up Robbie! Shut the fuck up! Steven: Uhh....guys... Nate and Robbie: What? Steven: LOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOK! Robbie: TURN AROUND GV! Graphic Violence does turn around, however, he is met with black mist to his eyes, and a huge baseball swing with that steel chair across GV's cranium. CRACK! Locke slides the chair out of the ring, and drops to the mat, draping his arm over GV! Robbie: NO! THAT BITCH! THAT STUPID BITCH! Nate: WOOOOO! LOCKE HAS IT! LOCKE HAS THE MATCH Lillith: Hey ref, turn around dumbass! And the referee did, and soon dropped to the mat... 1... 2... 3... Winner: Rhett Locke Nate: RHETT LOCKE HAS DEFEATED GRAPHIC VIOLENCE WITH THE HELP OF LILLITH!!! PAYBACK IS A BITCH, GV!!! Robbie: LILLITH IS A BIGGER BITCH!!! PROBABLY A DISEASE CARRYING BITCH AT THAT!!!! Nate: THIS MATCH IS FAR FROM OVER!!! WE NEED TO HEAD TO A COMMERCIAL!!! WHEN WE RETURN, WE WILL HEAD TO THE RING FOR OUR SECOND MAIN EVENT OF THE EVENING!!! 'Aerials' once again is played over the sound system, while the referee lifts Rhett's blood drenched arm up in the air. Lillith backs up the entrance ramp, smiling, while Rhett is tossed a towel to wipe away the blood…as the scene fades to commercial.
