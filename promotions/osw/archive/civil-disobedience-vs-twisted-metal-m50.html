Civil Disobedience vs. Twisted Metal

2002-04-20
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil Disobedience vs. Twisted Metal Written: 4-20-02 Earl: The following tag team contest is scheduled for one fall! 'All About the Pentiums' by Weird Al hits the sound system. Nate: We know now that these two are doing the bidding of that mysterious man calling himself The Vax! I wonder if he'll try to make his presence felt in this match tonight, just like he did last week! Robbie: It'd take a mastermind like The Vax to get these two a win. The two robots themselves march out from under the jumbotron and walk down the ramp. Earl: First, making their way to the ring at this time... At a total combined weight of 800 lbs... They are Chico and Gunmetal... TWISTED METAL!!! Chico and Gunmetal stop at the edge of the ring, then use their powerful legs to leap from the floor, over the ropes, and into the center of the ring. Steven: Gotta love the lower body strength. 'Pretty Fly for a White Guy' graces the speakers, and the original OSW Tag Team Champions make their grand entrance. Earl: Now making their way to the ring... Weighing in at Considerably less than their opponents... Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke... CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE!!! The crowd erupts into cheers as the two best friends jive their way down to the ring. Trevor is carrying a black duffel bag over his shoulder. Nate: Here comes the most popular tag team in OSW today! What do you think is in the bag? Robbie: Got me. The duo of Trevor and Seth nonchalantly slide into the ring and take their place in the corner opposite Twisted Metal. Trevor places the duffel bag next to the ring post, and taps the side of his nose while looking at Seth. Robbie: Looks like the two dorks have a plan. Trevor and Seth appear to be conversing in their corner, with Trevor in the ring while Seth stands outside on the apron. Nate: Uh...Trevow..turn around... Steven: He can't hear you. Unfortunately, Steven's words prove to be true, and Trevor is too busy talking with Seth to notice Gunmetal stomp up behind him and pick him up off the ground in his powerful mechanical arms. Robbie: That's what he gets for not paying attention! Gunmetal, while pressing a struggling Seth Masters over his head, marches to the center of the ring and spinning the former tag champ. Slowly gaining speed, Seth eventually becomes nothing but a frighteningly-fast blur being held in the air by Gunmetal. Nate: That can't be too comfortable for Seth Masters! One can hear Seth calling out to his partner as he is twirled in the air by Gunmetal, but his pleas are in vain. The dominating Gunmetal turns toward Trevor's corner, while still spinning Seth at lightning speed, and suddenly HURLS SETH RIGHT INTO THE CORNER!! Masters smashes into the corner with sickening force, and lands in an awkward heap. Trevor begins kicking at Seth for a tag, but Seth can hardly respond. Before Seth can even raise a hand, he is dragged back into the middle of the ring by Gunmetal and once again pressed into the air. Nate: This isn't going good for Civil Disobedience! Meanwhile, Trevor has begun going through the black duffel bag he brought to the ring, and produces a small object about the size of a can of corn, only slightly more dangerous-looking. The object is cylindrical and has a pin sticking out of it on top. Robbie: What on earth...? Just as Gunmetal begins to spin Seth again, Trevor pulls the pin out, winds up, and hurls the object right at Gunmetal! Steven: Wait a second...that's a... KA-BOOM! Steven: ...CHAFF GRENADE!!! The object explodes on impact with a brilliant flash of light. The flash quickly subsides, but the result are thousands of glistening strips of chaff floating through the air, raining down in the ring. Gunmetal immediately drops Seth and begins flailing his robotic limbs as sparks fly from every orfice of his metal body. Steven: That chaff is scrambling Gunmetal's circuts! Look! It's happening to Chico, too! The camera quickly cuts to Chico, who is rolling around on the outside of the ring as if he were on fire. Nate: Steven...how do you know all this? Steven: I used to date a SWAT team member. Tee hee... Nate: ...anyway, It looks like Trevor and Seth have gained control of this match! Back in the ring, Trevor has removed a pair of crow-bars from the duffel bag, and hands one to a revived Seth Masters. The duo look at the steel weapons in their hands, then up at eachother, and nod with a pair of grins on their faces. They then proceed to begin beating Gunmetal senseless with the crowbars. Robbie: What the hell are those two idiots doing?! Where's the ref?! The camera again quickly cuts to the outside of the ring, where referee Betty Lovejoy is busy prancing like a little girl through the drifting strips of chaff. Betty: Oooh, like, snowflakes! She leans her head back and sticks her tounge out to catch one...and begins to gag after doing so. Steven: One time when it's good NOT to swallow, kids. In the ring, Seth and Trevor are wailing away at Gunmetal with their crowbars like there's no tomorrow, but the stout robot remains planted on his feet. One lucky shot from Seth knocks Gunmetal's head clean off his shoulders, but the headless Gunmetal still blindly swings his arms through the air. The members of Civil Obedience step back for a moment to watch a headless robot swing his arms around while shooting sparks in every which way. Seth and Trevor laugh as they dodge the headless Gunmetal's flailing arms...but stop when a third, considerably more annoying laugh chimes in over the speakers. 'HAAAA HAAAA HAAAA HAAAAA HAAAAA!!! YOU GUYS ARE SO FUNNY!!!' The crowd explodes into boo's when the visage of Gilbert Gotfreid appears on the Jumbotron as he laughs in that nasal, hideous laugh. Gilbert: You know something, Civil Disobedience?! You two are nothing but a couple of worthless PUNKS!!! I... Gotfreid goes on about how much he hates Civil Disobedience, while the two friends stand their in the ring glaring at the jumbotron in contempt. This of course means that they don't notice Carrot Top slide into the ring with the head of Gunmetal. Nate: IT’S CARROT TOP!!! HE ISN’T SUPPOSED TO BE HERE!!! Robbie: Is this the way this is supposed to go? Nate: No. The red-headed comedian carefully attached the battered skull back onto the shoulders of the robot, and Gunmetal's eyes light up once again. The raging bot shoves Carrot Top aside and drives his two solid metal fists into the backs of the heads of his opponents. At the speed at which he delivered these punches, it makes perfect sense when Trevor and Seth drop like a pair of rocks. On the outside, Chico has grabbed the frolicing Betty Lovejoy and carries her into the ring with him. Nate: Looks like the chaff's effects have worn off! Carrot Top crawls out of the ring and starts to laugh along with Gilbert, dodging trash thrown by the crowd. In the ring, Gunmetal lets all 400 lbs of his body fall right onto Seth Masters, while Chico does the same to Trevor Hawke. Betty makes the count. 1......2...... Betty: Um... Robbie: 3, ya bimbo! Betty: OH! RIGHT! ....3! Winners: Twisted Metal Nate: So Twisted Metal with the help of Gilbert Gottfried and Carrot Top, pick up the win over the stunned Civil Disobedience. We need to head to a commercial. We will be right back after these messages. The camera shifts to the ring where Civil Disobedience sits stunned at what transpired…as the scene fades to commercial.
