Addressing the Failure

2002-06-15
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Addressing the Failure Written: June 15, 2002 The show fades up from black into a large locker room in the backstage area of the Key Arena, in Seattle, Washington. Long wooden benches line the locker room and face a large blackboard and podium. Behind the benches are a line of lockers, and a door to the far right wall that must be the entrance into the locker room itself. The camera slowly pans around the room, to reveal the Red Army is present within the room. The massive Russian Bear, Ivan Stanislav, stands at the podium, with his ever present confidant, Alexei Ruslan, to his right. The #1 Contender for the OSW World Heavyweight Title, Tom Walczak and referee Speedy Riggs sit on the rightmost bench, while ‘The German War Machine’ Michel Krieg, Loyal Communist Party Member Wang, and The Two Round Faced Women sit on the leftmost bench. Stanislav clears his throat as the Chinese representatives of The Red Army talk to one another, and then quickly silence themselves when Ruslan gives them an angry look as well. The Communists look up at Ivan and wait as he clears his throat again. Ivan: Last week, my Comrades, was a failure. Ivan pauses and lets these words sink in. Ivan: Last week, everything that could go wrong within our Red Army, went wrong. And why? Because we were not functioning as a team! Individualistic wants and needs clouded the collective’s judgment, and we paid for it. Ivan pauses and straightens up as the rest of the Red Army nods in agreement. Ivan: Never again will this happen! We are a team! We are a collective! We are communists!! Your wants, Michel Krieg, are secondary! Tom, your wants, are secondary!! Ivan points at the respective members, who are clenching their fists and tightening their jaws. Ivan: Tonight is a big night! Tonight, El Drago is in for surprise! Tonight, Doug Troy is taught humility! And tonight, most importantly, Reno Davis shall be destroyed!!! Our individual might is strong, our collective might is impregnable! Remember my friends, my comrades, we can only succeed if we all work together!! Come! Let us all stand and sing our glorious anthem! The anthem of the Soviet Union! The guiding light that will lead us to victory!! The group, as one, stands and begins to sing, each in their native tongue. Russian, German, Spanish, and Chinese all blend into one tune as the group sings together….then the scene fades to black.
