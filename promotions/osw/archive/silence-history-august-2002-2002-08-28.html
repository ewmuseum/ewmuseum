Silence — history (August 2002)

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History 1982 is when it happened. My family was visiting China when we entered an open market. I was only 10 when it happened. My father was looking at some cheap watches and my mom was loving some stupid Chinese cloth when we became seperated in the crowd. Now understand this, my family loved me very much but when your plane tickets and passports are getting ready to expire you have to return home. I don't hate my parents because I know they looked for me hard and they even kept in touch with the United States embacy in China until all hope was lost. I was not lost though. A nice old Chinese man found me and delivered me to an old Monk Fellowship that took me in. Being only 10 years old in a foreign country I wasn't used to such kindness and I realized what these monks had to offer, Martial Arts. They began teaching me right away and their style was even weirder then some of their customs. A style that can only be described as a cross between Kempo and Ti-Chi was taught to me and I picked up on it very quick. At the age of 14 I was even making up some of my own moves that could be introduced into the style. This went on for years until I was 20 and my younger brother Cyrus had found me. It seemed that my parents had located me in a small town in China and now my brother had also be lured in by the arts of this temple because he had been told he could find a fighter worthy of compitition here. Well the monks introduced him to me and he reconized me right away since I hadn't changed much. He took me away from my divine life and took me to Japan where he made me fight in underground pit-fighting compititions. I was basically brain dead to the word and didn't know any better. I had no idea that I was hurting these people for good and that some of them would never walk, talk or breath again. Cyrus didn't care and his greed took over when he found Professional Wrestling. He tried to find a trainer but no one would take me so we wrestled on the underground again until Afa The Wild Somoan found me and rescued me from that life and trained me along side of Marauder. At the age of 23 I had already did so much in the fighting world and Tornado Wrestling offered me my first contract under my real name and I turned pro. 7 years later and I am 30 years old. My career got put on the back burner for a while but along side of my former Tag Team partner we are no longer a tag team but a stable and a stable that we declare war on every member of the OSW in a short time.
