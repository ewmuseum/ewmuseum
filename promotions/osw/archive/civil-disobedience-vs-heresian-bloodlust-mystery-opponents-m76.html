Civil Disobedience vs. Heresian Bloodlust (Mystery Opponents)

2002-02-02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil Disobedience vs. Heresian Bloodlust (Mystery Opponents) Written: 02/02/2002 Nate: Welcome back to Saturday Night Slam! The first night of action for the OSW is deemed to be a great one as our last match showed, but now is time for the “mystery” tag match! Doug: What’s so mysterious about it? Some of the roster are still to be announced, its gotta be AFL-CIO!!! Steven: Oh yeah you know they’ve been waiting to beat those bad disobedient civilians down for a while now! Nate: Uhhh…yeah…anyway let’s get this match started and see who it is that Civil Disobedience are booked against! Let’s pass it to Earl. Earl Heinrich: Ladies and Gentlemen, Boys and Girls, coming to the ring first, weighing in at 460lbs. Coming to you form Aurora, Illinois. The tag team of Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke! CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE!!!! No time is wasted as “Pretty Fly For A White Guy” hits the PA and the fans react with a chants and cheers for the young men. Civil Disobedience emerges from the entrance, prancing and dancing around to the music. They look at each other, then to the crowd and make a mad dash for the ring, wasting no time in posing for their fans. The fans now explode for the young superstars. Steven: Well, these young men certainly know how to work the crowd over, that’s for sure. Nate: Yeah well we know who they are but who’s going to come out next? Steven: Our first tag match in the history of OSW and we don’t even know who the hell the other team is. Leave it to a woman to set up a promotional stunt like this. Nate: What is that supposed to mean? Steven: Never mind. Forget it. The arena is still roaring for the first appearance of Civil Disobedience in the OSW ring. Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters are both up on the turnbuckles posing for their cheering fans. Nate: Anyone taking bets on who the opposition is? Doug Troy: I’m putting my money on Da Westsidaz! Steven Smith: I don’t care who the hell it is, but they sure are taking a long time getting their asses out here. Hey! Hey, Earl! Get on with it already! The lights in the arena go out suddenly and an eerie silence falls across the crowd. A Gong sounds, followed by a choir singing a Gregorian chant. Strobe lighting begins flashing all around the arena as the mystery song plays on, and a wall of fire blazes up in front of the Entrance. From behind the blazing wall two massive silhouettes emerge. As the song “Carmina Burana” ascends the two dark giants walk through the wall of fire and stand on the ramp, flashing into view through the strobe lighting. Doug Troy: What the hell?! Nate Dumas: On My God! It can’t be...them...together!? The two silhouettes remain at the top of the ramp, gazing down at the ring. The pyrotechnics cease, but the dark music continues and at the top of the ramp the crowd finally see who the mysterious opponents are. Earl: And announcing their opponents! Weighing in at an astounding 725lbs.! Coming form parts unknown! The Tag Team of HESSIAN AND AZRAEL CAIN!!!! Nate: HESSIAN AND AZRAEL CAIN!!!!!!!!! OH MY HOLY GOD IT’S THE T.S.I. AND THE DARK ANGEL!!!!!!!!!!!!! THE DUO OF DESTRUCTION IS HERE IN OSW!!! THEY HAVE JUMPED SHIP FROM PCW!!! Steven: WOW!!!!!!!!!………Wait I know who they are, but who is the T.S.I.? Nate: The Titanic Satanic Insaniac, Hessian, you fool!!! HESSIAN AND AZRAEL CAIN ARE IN THE OSW!!!!!!!!! Doug: Damn those boys are big, but what self-respecting Satanist turning wrestler isn’t? Steven: Well, the Preacher would be a perfect example of that. Doug: Who?? Steven: The Preacher, you know, tall, pale guy. The guy who defeated you for the PCW World Heavyweight title? Doug: I have no recollection of that individual whatsoever. Nate: Heh… The evil tag team makes their way down the ramp towards the ring. Their eyes never leave their opponents. Trevor and Seth look uncomfortable in the ring as they see the two massive wrestlers walk towards them. Nate: It looks like Civil Disobedience has the fear of God in their eyes. Steven: I actually think that it is the “Fear of Satan,” Nate. Hessian and Azrael Cain climb up to the mat and enter over the ring over the top ropes. “Carmina Burana” ceases, and Azrael Cain looks to Hessian and nods, and then vacates the ring for the apron. Hessian removes his large black cloak as Trevor turns to Seth and then gets on the outside of the ropes. Nate Dumas: Well, it looks like Hessian and Seth Masters are going to be the first ones to face off. Steven: I have a really bad feeling about this! Doug: Let’s just get this damn match underway already. The bell rings, and Hessian cocks his head in wonder at the little 6’0” man they call Seth Masters. Nate: Oh this can’t be good! Hessian is a clear foot taller than Seth! Hessian slowly approaches Seth who looks extremely intimidated for a moment, but he suddenly bites his teeth and lunges at the huge Hessian. Nate: And it seems that Civil Disobedience will be the first to attempt some sort of offense in this match. Seth attempts spear Satan’s mercenary, but Hessian effortlessly catches his smaller opponent in mid air and delivers a DDT. Seth is now on the mat clutching his head in pain. Nate: And it seems that Hessian is the first to attack. Well, at least you have to give the guy some credit for bravery. Steven: I’ll give him credit it for something...Credit for being a damn fool for attempting such a manoeuvre on a massive hell spawn like that. Hessian climbs to his feet and grabs the hurting Seth and pulls him to his feet. Hessian Irish whips his opponent into the ropes. Seth is shot back at the monster. Seth is violently thrown to the mat as a result of a boot to the face from Hessian. Steven: HA! Seth Masters is being thrown around like a rag doll. This is great! Nate: This is getting ugly. It looks like this match is about to be over real soon. Doug: You’re right about the ugly part there, Nate. Take a look at Cain and Hessian. Hessian has to wear a mask and Cain has the kind if face that only a mother could love. Nate: Do you think he had a mother? Nate and Doug share a laugh. Back in the ring Seth Masters is trying to crawl towards his tag partner, Trevor Hawke. Hessian leans over and pulls the smaller Masters to his feet. Hessian decides to give his opponent a hand. He picks the wobbling wrestler to his feet and delivers a running Spike Piledriver. Masters is on his back. Hessian goes for a quick pin. Nate: It looks like this one could be over. Steven: It was over before it started. I mean just look at how big these guys are compared to Civil Disobedience. The ref gets to down on knees and begins to pound the mat. 1..... 2.... No! Masters manages to get his shoulder off the mat at the very last possible moment. Steven Smith: Just stay down, for Christ’s sake! Hessian climbs to his feet and immediately gets into the ref’s face, screaming at the top of his lungs. Nate: Hessian doesn’t look too happy about the count. Hessian pushes the ref away and turns back to take care of Masters. Hessian reaches down for Seth Masters, who crawls through Hessian’s legs and scrambles to the other side of the ring. Hessian doesn’t even bother to look for Seth, and instead tags in his partner, Azrael Cain. Nate: Well, it looks like we’ll finally get to see the other side of this tag team wrestle. Cain steps over the top rope to enter the ring. The dark one grabs Seth by the ankle before he makes the tag to his reaching partner, Trevor Hawke. Cain then lifts the badly beaten Masters into the air and begins to choke him with both if his hands around his opponent’s throat. Steven: HA! Look at Masters’ legs flail! Doug: You know that you are really sick, don’t you. Steven: Cutting off the oxygen supply only serves to enhance the pleasure. Doug and Nate: SHUT UP!!! Cain sees that Hawke still desperately wants into the ring. Cain then looks at the fading Masters and smiles. The massive beast spits in his opponents face and launches him through the air towards his partner. The tag is made and Trevor Hawke rushes at Cain. Hawke runs at his awaiting opponent. Nate: Finally, Trevor is going to get a chance here. Doug: Yeah, like this is going to end up any different. Cain greets the quickly advancing Hawke with a vicious clothesline that causes Trevor to fall to the mat gripping his throat. As Cain reaches down to lift Hawke off of the mat, he is greeted by a quick kick to the head by Trevor. Nate: A quick move by Trevor Hawke there might just be exactly what Civil Disobedience needs here. Hawke begins to strut around the ring for the fans who are now cheering wildly. Steven: Yeah, but the idiot better do something except play the crowd or Cain is likely to nip up and end this short offensive stint of Trevor Hawke’s. Trevor Hawke manages to deliver a quick dropkick to the kneeling Azrael Cain, sending him crashing into the Civil Disobedience corner. Seth Masters grabs hold of Azrael Cain and Trevor Hawke runs at Azrael Cain and delivers spinning heel kick to the warrior, knocking him to the mat. Trevor tags in Seth and they pick up Azrael and Civil Disobedience hit Azrael with a double spinning heel kick. The crowd erupts at the flurry of offense. Nate Dumas: Some quick moves give Civil Disobedience the advantage here! Steven Smith: Yeah, but I wouldn’t want to be on the receiving end of the beating that Cain is going to give them when he gets up! Doug: They need to utilize their quickness to have any chance of surviving in there against these animals. Seth and Trevor both begin to work the crowd together. The arena blows up with excitement for the young wrestlers. Doug: Look at those two work the crowd over. Steven: Yeah, but two bad this won’t last long. After a couple of seconds of working the crowd, Seth tags Hawke back in to the ring, who finally returns his attention to his opponent again. Unfortunate for Hawke though Azrael Cain nips up and catches Trevor Hawke by the throat as he turns around, and drives him into the mat with a hard Chokeslam. The Crowd: Ooooooooooo!!! Without stopping Azrael mounts Trevor and begins punching him hard in the face. Seth tries to coax the ref into stopping the barrage of fists, but the ref watches on as Azrael headbutts Trevor and gets up. Nate: A 6’10” man just nipped up…am I the only one that noticed that? Doug: I don’t know about that, but Azrael is on the second rope and looking angry! Steven: What the hell does nip mean? I thought it meant “nibble.” Hessian roars to the booing crowd as Azrael jumps off the turnbuckle and hits Trevor across the throat with a leg drop. In the momentum Azrael grabs Trevor and pulls him up…then plants him with a powerbomb… Steven: Oh for the love of God! Will somebody stop this match! Nate: Some impressive moves by the beast they call Azrael Cain. Doug: Yeah, if you call a leg drop an impressive move. Nate: Worked for Hogan… Doug: I have no defense to that well placed thrust. Azrael looks to tag in Hessian, and manages to snag the big man as he gets up from the mats. Hessian slides into the ring and lifts Trevor up by his throat, and slams him down onto the mat again with a one-handed chokeslam. Nate: I don’t think I have ever seen such power displayed by a Tag Team before! Hessian leans over to grab up the hurting Hawke, but he is greeted with a low blow from Trevor. The Crowd: Ooooooo!!! Nate: Guuuuuuh!!! Doug: GAAAAAWLLLLLEEEYY!!!! Steven: BOCA!!! Hessian bends over in pain from the blow to his nuts. Hawke stumbles over to his partner and tags in Seth Masters. Steven: What the hell was that? Nate: I think they call it a “low blow.” Trevor grabs the hunched over Hessian and slings him into the turnbuckle by his partner. Hawke then sprints at Hessian and flies through the air to deliver an elbow to his opponent’s throat. Hawke then tags in Masters. Both men then Irish whip Hessian off of the ropes. Hessian comes flying at both members of Civil Disobedience. Trevor and Seth then catch their opponent coming off the ropes in a double flapjack. The men then manage to grab both of Hessians arm and hit a double flatliner. The crowd erupts. Nate: CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE JUST HIT THE TRENDSETTER ON HESSIAN!!! THIS COULD BE IT!!! Seth goes for the cover. Cain enters the ring in an attempt to break the pin up, but he is greeted by a Russian leg sweep. Cain falls to the mat hard. Nate Dumas: This should be it. The ref gets down to make the count on Hessian. 1…. 2…. With a display of awesome strength, Hessian throws Seth off with authority. Masters goes flying through the air and lands on the mat face down with a loud thud. Nate: Hessian just beat the pin after receiving Civil Disobedience’s finisher by shot putting Masters across the ring! Cain slings Trevor out of the ring, while Hessian climbs to his feet and stalks towards the stunned Masters. Cain exits to the apron as Trevor slowly manages to get himself back by his corner. Steven: This is going to be bad! The massive mercenary leans over a pulls Masters to his feet. Hessian then looks to Trevor and points a gauntleted finger at him, to which Trevor replies with a flipping of the bird, much to the delight of the crowd, and displeasure of an already angry Hessian. Hessian lifts Trevor up and sets him up for a powerbomb. Looking around the crowd Hessian hoists Trevor up, and literally tosses him across the ring into Seth. Seth flies off of the mat and flies into the barrier outside of the ring. Nate: The Highway To Hell!!! Hessian has sent Trevor straight into his partner and DAMN that looked painful! Cain enters the ring now and crosses to Hessian who is stalking over to the mangled Hawke. Hessian hoists Hawke into the air and executes a ¾ shoulder mount drop, but before Hawke’s limp body can hit the mat he’s caught by Cain who delivers a ¾-facelock bulldog. Nate: From Hell! From Hell! From Hell! Doug: What the hell are you talking about, boy? Nate: That’s the name of the finisher that they just executed, From Hell. Steven: And how do you know that? Nate: I showed up at the pre-show prep meeting. All three announcers look over to see Caleb, the decrepit homeless manager of Azrael Cain, and Minion, the little hell spawn Hessian calls his manager. Both of the henchmen just return the confused look to the announcers. Minion: What? Caleb: What the hell you two looking at? Heh-Heh. Steven Smith: We’re...not...sure... Minion: Are managers not allowed down to ringside anymore or something? Nate: Let’s just get back to the match…please. In the ring, Hawke is completely laid out flat. Masters’ hand appears on the apron and it slowly begins to pull itself up. Hessian sees Masters trying to get into the ring. He then reaches down and grabs the head of Seth, pulling him through the ropes by force! Hessian begins to lay his gauntlet-covered fists into Masters face, while Cain begins to choke the maimed Hawke. Nate: This should be the end of Civil Disobedience for the evening. Doug: This thing was over the minute that those two gothic goons showed up. Steven Smith: Why is the ref letting this go on? The ref now starts to try and separate Cain’s hands from Hawke’s throat, but this just begins to annoy the warrior who begins to pound Trevor’s head against the mat as well, while Hessian continues to pound away at Seth’s face. Doug: For Christ sakes! What the hell is it going to take for the ref to DQ these guys already? The ref finally manages to remove Cain’s grasp from Trevor’s throat. Cain rises from his opponent and looks at the ref with an annoyed look on his face. The ref begins to stumble backwards away from the approaching menace, but finds himself bumping into the chest of Hessian. The ref slowly turns around to see the masked face of Satan’s mercenary staring down at him form behind his mask. Nate: THIS DOES NOT BODE WELL FOR REFEREE PIERRE GASTON!!! Hessian then grabs the ref and Irish whips him into the ropes. The ref bounces off of the ropes towards Hessian who throws him over his back with a back body drop. The defenseless ref then flies over Hessian’s back only to be caught by Cain in mid-air and thrown in a death-valley driver. Gaston’s body bounces off of the mat high into the air. The crowd is outraged. Nate: COME ON!!! SOMEONE ALL THIS MATCH!! As if from Nate’s lips to the ears of God, the bell rings to signify the Disqualification of Cain and Hessian. Winners via DQ: Civil Disobedience Doug: What the hell was that move called? Nate: It says here “A Heretics Wrath.” Minion: That’s “Heretic’s Wrath.” Caleb: Yeah, drop the ‘A’. Doug: Do us a favor and take a hike from the announce position before I put my hand through that chick-like chest of yours and extract a lung. Caleb hisses in protest. The fans are booing wildly, but inside the ring, Azrael Cain and Hessian are signaling to their managers for a microphone. Caleb immediately responds and tosses a microphone Hessian in the ring. Minion grabs the other mic and stops to address the hate filled crowd. Minion: Ladies and Gentlemen, it is with great pleasure to introduce to you...HERESIAN BLOODLUST!!!!!!!!!! Doug, Nate, and Steven: WHAT?!?!?! Nate: Duo of Domination is a MUCH better name! Doug: Agreed. Steven: 100% better. Caleb: You heard the man, now shut your mouths. Heh-Heh. Earl Heinrich: Ladies and Gentlemen! The winners of the match by disqualification, Civil Diso……… Minion: Shut your damn mouth, Heinrich!
