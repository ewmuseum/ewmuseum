A Discovery!

2002-11-05
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Discovery! Written: 11/05/2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam!! We’re told that there’s action in Heresian Bloodlust’s locker room backstage! Steven: Yes! Gay Satanic Love! Impage! Decker: Shut up. (The camera flips to Heresian Bloodlust walking backstage. Hessian throws a stagehand up against the wall as they pass him. Cain is carrying both tag belts as they make for their door. They come to door and the word spray-painted right on the door says, “DICKSMACKS!” and the name-plate is taken.) Caleb: What’s this, some kind of joke? Hessian: Do these losers ever give up? Civil D is going to learn that their stupid, two-bit insults and dumb-ass coments aren’t going to get them even close to our Tag Titles! Lets get inside and bust there asses! (Hessian kicks the door down and looks inside. Although the Suburban Kings are nowhere to be found, the room is a complete mess. The locker’s are tipped over, the floor is covered with trash and there is spray-paint everywhere. Even their table is flipped over and their stuff is spilt on the floor.) Decker: Hahaha! They’re locker room is trashed! Nate: Sssh… Cain: What is this?! Someone is foolish enough to enter our locker room? Those idiots are going to pay. Minion: Get a load of this B.S.! (Cain drops the titles on the floor and starts looking at the writing. Messages are written all over the wall, not being too kind to the Tag Champions. “Hessian is a DICKSMACK!”, “Suburbia RULES!”, “Heresian Bloodlust are Civil Disobedience’s bitches!” and other rude comments directed toward the Tag Champs. Hessian kicks a garbage can into the wall in a fit of rage. He growls, reading more of the comments sprayed onto the walls. He starts to hunt through the wreckage, in search of some item he desires.) Hessian: Those suburban flies are going to pay in blood! (Hessian finds his target and retrieves a sledgehammer from one of his still standing lockers. He looks at it and passes it to Azrael. Cain looks at it and nods instinctively, knowing what his goliath partner has in mind. Hessian flips a table back on it’s legs and puts the Tag Titles onto it.) Hessian: Caleb and Minion, stay here. If those insects come back again, swat them! Cain: Let’s go find some these two infidels and crush their skulls. They cannot hope to disrespect the tag champions like this and hope to survive. (Hessian pushes the door out of the way as he steams out in search of Civil Disobedience. The managers start to clean the locker room up as Cain follows his fellow monster-partner out of the room, 30lb Sledgehammer in hand.) Nate: Oh man, somebody better tell Civil Disobedience to watch out, Hessian and Azrael look pissed! Decker: Sweet! I’m going to get to see what the insides of a human head look like!
