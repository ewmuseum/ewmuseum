The attack of Ivan-zilla

2002-03-02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The attack of Ivan-zilla Written: March 2, 2002 The scene opens upon a city skyline baked in the light of... fluorescent lighting? The camera backs away from the strangely cardboard looking cityscape as a narration begins. Narrator- It's a lovely day in downtown Tokyo, early spring, a time of new beginnings and enjoyment of life until... A click is audible and then a huge booted foot crashes down onto the buildings, smashing them. Narrator- Until Ivan-zilla wreaks terror across the city! Oh the death! The destruction! The inhumanity of it all! Suddenly the all too familiar roar of Ivan Stanislav seemingly yanks the camera's attention upward, where his enraged face looms over the camera. The voice of Tempest is heard very softly. Tempest- Uh oh... Ivan- WHAT IN LENIN'S NAME?? Stanislav roars and kicks over a few more buildings, effectively destroying the entire city, which actually looks quite good on film. He turns and looks down at the camera with an extremely confused look on his face. Tempest's giggle is heard briefly as she apparently stands and the view shifts. Tempest- That was great! You're a natural for this kind of film Ivan, ya know that? Ivan shakes his head to left and right and blinks several times. He points at the camera and kicks over a skyscraper...or....knee scraper. Ivan- What is all this?! What are you doing?!! Tempest- Can't you tell? I'm makin' a monster movie! Ivan's nostrils flare as his breathing gets heavier, he closes his eyes and grinds his jaw, and then suddenly kicks backwards, slamming his massive boot into the door behind him, tearing it from the wall and sending it careening into the room! Ivan- WHAT DID I TELL YOU ABOUT JOKING AROUND?!! Tempest- (sounding disappointed) But... I'm nowhere near the ring and neither are you. Ivan growls to himself. Ivan- The ring? The camera moves in the vertical plane. Tempest- Yeah, you said I shouldn't mess around with ya and distract you in your matches, to which I agreed. *snicker* But you're not in a match at the moment. Ivan growls. Ivan- You know good and well what I MEANT! He eyes the camera and gets a twisted grin on his face. Ivan- Well then, in that case, perhaps I should show you what Ivan-zilla would do.... Ivan's huge hand shoots out and envelops the lens and pulls the camera away from Tempest. The erratic movements and the growling of Stanislav tell us that he has the camera. He tosses it to the floor and looms over it, the lens staring straight up at the massive Soviet. Ivan- HERE IS WHAT HAPPENS!! Ivan brings his massive boot up in the air and brings it crashing down on the lens, a split second before it cuts to static, a hammer and sickle can be seen on the heel of his boot. The static cuts out and we have another shot now, of Ivan stomping wildly on the camera, busting it to pieces, and Tempest staring wide eyed. He finally stops, panting over the wreckage and Tempest looks at him as her lower lip pooks out in a puppy eyed pout. Tempest- Ivan, I was just having fun... Ivan grunts and kicks the now flat camcorder down the hallway. It slams into a plaster wall and punches a huge hole through it. Ivan- Ivan is having fun too!! DYAAHAA! Ivan turns to her. Ivan- When Ivan said not to joke with him during match, he MEANT the entire card. I must stay focused tonight! Tempest looks down the hall after the remains of the camera sadly and sighs. Tempest- Ok... you stay focused. I'll just go away. You never let me have any fun. Her head droops as she turns and kicks at an imaginary pebble and shuffles away. Stanislav puts his hands on his hips and watches her leave, he shakes his head semi angrily at himself and at the situation. He looks up at the ceiling and mutters something in Russian and turns to walk in the other direction. A technician walks by and is promptly thrown into the wall as he passes by.
