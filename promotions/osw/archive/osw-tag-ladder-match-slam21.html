OSW Tag Team Championship Match: Da Westsidaz vs. Heresian Bloodlust (c)

22 Jun 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW Tag Team Championship Tag Match: Da Westsidaz vs. Heresian Bloodlust(c) Written: June 22nd, 2002 Da Westsidaz vs. Heresian Bloodlust Earl: The following contest is the TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH! Scheduled for one fall. First…making their way to the ring… “Ante Up Remix” by MOP and Funkmaster Flex hits the P.A. and the crowd ovate as Da Westsidaz and Daisy emerge from behind the curtain. Gesturing to the crowd, Riptah and Shorty take in the mass of cheers and chants on their way to the ring. Daisy waves to ogling men in the front rows as Da Westsidaz make their way down the ramp and into the ring. Earl: Hailing from Bakersfield, CA…at a combined weight of 460lbs, Riptah and Short Circuit…DA WESTSIDAAAAAAZ!!!!!!!!! Shorty and Riptah waste no time in preparing for the match while Daisy watches from ringside. Nate: Well Da Westsidaz looked pumped for this match, thank god they aren’t using the weapons tonight or they’d be dead before Ascension… Steven: Hmm…just don’t count out Minion in the main event…he could take the thing! Robbie: Too true…too true… Earl: AND THEIR OPPONENTS!!! A wave of silence befalls the crowd, before the lights dim, “Carmina Burana” invades the ears of the sold-out crowd and the towering inferno of flame rises up in front of the entranceway, provoking an ovation of boos and hisses, cussing and spitting from the crowd. Earl: Hailing from Detroit, MI, at a combined weight of 725lbs…they are the OSW Tag Team Champions, Hessian and Azrael Cain…HERESIAN BLOODLUUUUUUST!!!!!!!!! The towering inferno before the entrance blasts up as two silhouettes, and Minion, appear before it. Slowly they walk through, with Minion screaming as the flames nip at him, and Hessian and Cain enter the arena to a chorus of boos and hisses. Nate: No love shown for Bloodlust here! Robbie: They’re so groovy… Steven: Yeah and I’m a heterosexual! Cain and Hessian unbuckle their shiny title belts and hold them up for the entire crowd to boo at, and then throw them at Minion, who takes them and skitters down to ringside ahead of them. As Hessian and Cain approach the ring and the waiting Westsidaz, Daisy jumps in front of them and begins trash talking. Nate: NOT a smart move on the part of Daisy… Robbie: Shouldn’t Earl show off the tag titles to the crowd? Steven: Meh…let’s get it on! Daisy begins poking at Hessian, who cocks his head at the Westsidaz’ spokesperson, then wraps his large gauntleted hand around her head, and pushes her, causing her to fall over the steel steps. Immediately Da Westsidaz slide out of the ring and begin brawling with Heresian Bloodlust, as Gaston calls for the bell. Ding, ding, and ding! Riptah and Hessian battle it out, with Riptah laying into the big man for Daisy, whilst Cain and Short Circuit exchange rights and lefts. Simultaneously, Cain grabs Shorty and whips him back into the ring, whilst Hessian kicks Riptah to the midsection, then grabs him, hoists him high into the air, and just manages to throw him into the ring. Roaring out, Bloodlust follow, with Shorty and Cain heading to their respective sides, Hessian and Riptah lock up. Nate: Riptah is a well developed athlete…not many people can jostle with a 7’2” monster like Hessian. Robbie: *COUGH* ‘ROIDS! *COUGH* Steven: In any case this match looks to be a good one! Pierre Gaston checks the lock-up, as Riptah quickly locks Hessian in a wristlock, then quickly again into the hammerlock. Hessian turns over and takes Riptah, and whips him off the ropes, hitting him with a clothesline as he returns. Riptah makes his way back to his feet then ducks under a big boot from Hessian, and grabs him from behind. Nate: It’ll be a cold day in hell whe-… Riptah takes Hessian and drills him with a belly-to-back suplex. Hessian scrambles to his feet as Riptah jumps over and tags in Short Circuit. Lifting Hessian up, Da Westsidaz whip Hessian off the ropes, and as he returns plant him with a Double Back Body Drop. Riptah leaves the ring as Shorty puts the boots to Hessian. Robbie: Things getting started quickly here, Da Westsidaz tagging early in the game… Hessian makes his way back to his feet, but as Shorty goes to kick him in the throat, Hessian shoots up and grabs Shorty around his throat. SC yells out as Hessian lifts him up, then tosses him across the ring to his own side, then stumbles across and tags in Cain. The Marked Beast rushes Shorty and wraps a big clothesline around him in the corner. Riptah takes a shot at Cain only to be headbutted off the apron. Daisy rushes to Riptah’s aid whilst Cain whips Shorty off the ropes and plants him with a Back Body Drop of his own. Nate: Dangnabbit! Robbie: Aye? Steven: HOOTS MON! Nate: That’s Scottish! Robbie: And that was redneck… Steven: GOD BLESS AMERICA!!! Cain grabs Shorty and pulls him into the Bloodlust corner, tagging in Hessian. Cain whips SC off the ropes, and as he returns is driven into the mat with a Double Super Crucifix Powerbomb. Nate: BIBLE BASHING!!! Hessian and Cain drop Short Circuit with one of their trademark moves! Robbie: Told you they’d kick ass… Steven: Y’know after what Minion did last week…he’s behaving very well… Robbie: It’s not every day a 3’11” Imp chokeslams someone and dives off the top turnbuckle, breaking our table in two with a 360º splash. Nate: Keep it on the match guys this is interesting… Taking no time to let Shorty recover, Hessian and Cain loom over him, and then suddenly manoeuvre into position, locking in a Crossface Inverted Indian Deathlock Leg Lock and Reverse Ankle Lock. Nate: InSineration!!! Cain and Hessian have Short Circuit locked in another of their trademark moves!!! Robbie: You can tell! The crowd are going ballistic at Heresian B and cheering out f*cking loudly for SC! Shorty yells out in pain as Pierre checks the situation. Screaming out, Shorty tries to wave his arms around, when Riptah slides back into the ring and boots Hessian and Cain in the head, sending both champs stumbling across the ring. Short Circuit rolls to his corner to recover, as Riptah charges at Cain and knocks him out of the ring with a Diving Shoulder Block. Cain falls out before Minion, who immediately tries to get the big man back up. Nate: Wait…where is Caleb? Shouldn’t he be helping in case Cain like…passes out? Robbie: You should know! Steven: Well I think Caleb must be arou-…GAH!!!!!!!!! Steven yells out as Caleb suddenly appears from under the announce table. Grinning at the three commentators, Caleb slips out from under the table and runs around the ring to check on Cain, while Hessian and Riptah jostle in the ring, exchanging punches. Short Circuit makes his way back to his feet and joins in the punch up, and with every Westside punch the crowd ovate just a little more. Nate: Well Shorty is the legal man but Pierre looks confused! Steven: You’d be too in you were French! With another Irish Whip, Da Westsidaz knock Hessian to the ground with a Double Dropkick. While Riptah boots Hessian in the gut, Shorty scales the turnbuckle. Riptah runs over, and Shorty slowly mounts Riptah’s shoulders, trying to maintain his balance as Riptah makes his way slowly across the ring to Hessian. Nate: Da Westsidaz trying a high risk move here by the looks of things! Riptah stops before Hessian, as Shorty dives off of his shoulders, Riptah grips his legs and adds to the impact of SC’s splash with a mat slam, directly onto Hessian. Shorty grabs his gut and rolls to the outside, leaving Riptah and Hessian in the ring again. Nate: Well consistent tagging seems to be the plan for Da Westsidaz, and it’s working as they slow down Hessian and get the fan’s support too!!! Robbie: I don’t care what you say, no one can physically power down Heresian Bloodlust! Riptah pulls Hessian to his feet and whips him off the ropes. As Hessian rebounds Riptah charges him and spears the Goliath to the ground, then covers Hessian. Pierre goes for the count. 1… 2… NO!!! Cain dives back into the ring and knocks Riptah off of Hessian, rolling out to the other side of the ring in the momentum. Nate: What teamwork!!! Minion and Caleb watch on content, holding the titles, as Hessian gets back to his feet and waits as Riptah scrambles back to his feet. As Riptah turns around, Hessian kicks him in the gut and hoists Riptah into the air, then plants him with a Spinning Implant Death Valley Driver! Nate: HELLTER SKELTER!!! HESSIAN JUST PLANTED SHORT CIRCUIT WITH THE HELLTER SKELTER!!! Robbie: I think you’ll find that was Riptah, J.R. Nate: RIPTAH!!! RIPTAH!!! RIPTAH!!! Steven: Hessian hit the move… Nate: Oh who cares they saw it for themselves! Hessian turns in time to see Cain back in the Bloodlust corner, and drags Riptah over, tagging in Cain. Hessian whips Riptah off of the ropes, and as he returns Hessian hoists him up for the Powerbomb, with Riptah falling Cain adds impact to the move with the Shoulder Neckbreaker. Nate: DEMOLITION!!! Heresian Bloodlust ARE taking apart Riptah and Short Circuit! Hessian heads back to his corner as Cain picks Riptah up off the mat, but before he can whip him, Short Circuit reaches in and blind tags Riptah. Cain poises as Riptah rebounds off the ropes, but as he bends over for the Back Body Drop, Riptah kicks Cain in the head, then hoists Cain above him. Nate: Riptah’s a strong guy, he’s holding 375lbs like it was 300lbs! Riptah holds Cain, and then drops him onto Short Circuit’s knee. Nate: Super Gutbuster!!! Steven: PACKIN’ HEAT!!! Robbie: Shorty just took a 375lbs man to his knee; he’s going to have to walk that off!!! Riptah heads to his corner as Short Circuit shakes the stinging from his knee, then puts the boots to Cain. With a swift kick to the gut, Shorty heads to the turnbuckle, and waits as Cain slowly makes his way to his feet. As Azrael turns to the turnbuckle, Short Circuit dives off and smacks Cain in the chin with a Standing Side Kick from the Top Rope! Nate: Short Circuit hits a variation of his Afro-Kick!!! This match is SO not one-sided Robbie, whaddaya say to that? Robbie: If those two weren’t black they’d be dead by now! Steven: WHAT!!!…My god I think I just heard Jordan X Shabazz drop down dead! Short Circuit sees Cain down, then heads back to the same turnbuckle, and sizes up Cain, then dives off and lands a perfect 810º Splash! Nate: CIRCUIT BREAKER!!! CIRCUIT BREAKER!!! SHORT CIRCUIT JUST HIT THE CIRCUIT BREAKER ON AZRAEL CAIN!!!!!!!!! Robbie: Why don’tcha just preach about Barbeque Sauce while you’re at it??? Shorty covers Cain as Gaston begins the count. 1… 2… 3NO!!!!!!!!! Hessian hits a Splash of his own on Shorty, sandwiching him between his and Cain’s body. Nate: What action!!! Pushing SC away, Hessian drags Cain back to the Bloodlust corner, then tags himself in as Gaston tries to keep up with the action. Grabbing Short Circuit, Hessian plants him with a Chokeslam, then in one quick motion pulls him back up by the Throat and drives him back into the mat with another Chokeslam! Robbie: TO HELL AND BACK!!! Hessian is getting some revenge for Bloodlust to-NIGHT!!! Hessian begins taunting Riptah, who runs into the ring, only to be kicked in the stomach by Hessian, who lets out an almighty roar, which in turn receives an ovation of booing from the crowd. Hessian pulls Riptah up onto his shoulder and carries him around the ring, locked in the Canadian Backbreaker. Nate: Oh no…you know what’s coming next… Robbie: BOOYAH!!! Steven: That Imp needs to get himself involved… Hessian parades around the ring with Riptah on his shoulder, then stops in the middle of the ring and drives Riptah’s skull into the mat with the Crucifix Piledriver, sending the Westsida tumbling and bouncing out of the ring in the momentum. Hessian gets to his feet and nudges Cain, who comes around and props himself up in the corner, while Hessian takes Short Circuit by the throat and pulls him to his feet. Nate: This doesn’t look good for Short Circuit, he’s all alone in the ring with Hessian and Cain and that ain’t a good thing! Robbie: No it ain’t HAHAHAAAAA! Hessian and Cain whip Shorty off of the ropes, knocking him to the mat with a Double Big Boot as he returns. Not giving him any time to rest, Cain picks him up and in the blink of an eye hoists him into the air and bombs him into the turnbuckle. Nate: THE NINTH GATE!!! Cain hits the Ninth Gate on Short Circuit and YOU KNOW that is payback for the Circuit Breaker!!! Hessian pulls Short Circuit to his feet and whips him off the ropes again. Short Circuit barely manages to run across the ring, and as he rebounds, Hessian thrusts him into the air over himself with an almighty roar, and roars again to the booing crowd as Azrael Cain almost buries Shorty in the canvas with the Death Valley Driver. Nate: THE HERETIC’S WRATH!!! HERETIC’S WRATH!!! HERESIAN BLOODLUST HAVE DESTROYED SHORT CIRCUIT!!! Steven: Now if only they would pin him or if Gaston would get the illegal man the hell out of there, things would be hunky dory! Nate: They are!!! They’re goi-…WAIT!!! ON THE STAGE!!! LOOK WHO IT IS!!! Hessian drops to the mat and covers Short Circuit as Cain rests in the corner. Pierre Gaston goes for the count… 1… Robbie: IT’S CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE!!! WITH A LADDER!!! THE BASTARDS!!! NOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2……… Steven: THEY’RE IN THE RING!!! THIS THING IS GONNA GET FUGLY NOOOW!!!!!!!!! 3NO!!! Hessian scrambles to his feet, and he and Cain rush Civil Disobedience, only to be smacked with a wide ladder shot sending them falling back into the mat. Nate: THIS IS OUT OF CONTROL!!! As Heresian Bloodlust roll around in pain in the corner, Riptah gets back to his feet, only to be smashed in the face with the top step of the ladder. Laid out next to Short Circuit, Da Westsidaz lie incapacitated. Nate: BASTARDS!!! CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE MUST HAVE THIS MATCH SCREWED UP!!! Pierre Gaston jumps in, but is yelled back into the corner, and forced to watch as first Seth scales the ladder, and dives off, landing on Da Westsidaz with a 360º Splash, sending a sickening thud throughout the arena as flesh hits flesh from the height. While Seth takes the pain of the fall and rolls out of the way, Trevor climbs the ladder, and upon barely hitting the top step, dives off and lands a Twisting Corkscrew Leg Drop across the throats of both Shorty and Riptah. Nate: DA SUPAFLY!!! Trevor just hit Da Westsidaz with Da Supafly!!! The crowd is in a frenzy. Robbie: Goddamnit what are they doing now??? Steven: I dunno but Pierre is going to do something now! Pierre runs across the ring, stopping Civil Disobedience as they grab their ladder. Looking at them, then to the ringside, Pierre shakes his finger in a most French way. Pierre: DQ!!! DQ!!! DQ!!!
