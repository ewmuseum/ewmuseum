Cain's Contract?

2002-04-13
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cain's Contract? Written: 13/04/2002 Nate: Folks, I am being told we our cameras have found the OSW Tag Team Champions, Heresian Bloodlust, in the backstage area, so lets head backstage!!! The camera shot changes to a poorly lit locker room. The massive warriors, Azrael Cain and Hessian can be seen in the dim light and what light there is shining off of the faceplate of Hessian. Cain is pulling his long hair back into a ponytail. The two small managers, Caleb and Minion, can be seen in the background. Caleb: Now listen to me, you two goons. Heh-Heh. Those damn Russians got lucky last week! This week we are going to extract our revenge with the news that we are going to share with all of OSW. Heh-Heh-Heh. Minion: I don't know about you guys, but it seems that you got your ass whooped by that china man last week and that is the reason that we are in the situation that we are in now. Caleb: HE CHEATED, DAMMIT! I already told you that! Heh-Heh-Heh. Minion: Cheated? It looked like he went Kung Fu style on ya and as a result, you got your ass kicked. Caleb begins to shake with rage. Hessian steps in between the two arguing managers. Hessian: That is over and done with! This week, we will get our revenge. I am going to unleash the wrath of hell on Tom Walczak. But Caleb, you were right. Tonight we reveal our little plan to all of those foolish fans of OSW. Nate: PLAN?!!? WHAT PLAN?!?! Robbie: They are going to sell them Amway products. Nate: (sighing) If you do not have anything constructive to add, then don’t speak. Cain: Hessian is right. But first we need to acquire a copy of that contract that Caleb here destroyed in that fire. Cain, Hessian, and Minion all look at Caleb with a disgruntled look on their faces. Caleb: He...it...uh...slipped, that' all...ya. Heh-Heh-Heh. Cain: Minion and Caleb, you two need to...acquire a copy of that 'contract.' Then our plans will come vilision. Caleb: That migh be a little more difficult than one thinks. Heh-Heh. Hessian: What's a matter? Are you scared, you little worm? Caleb: No! Heh. It's just that it might be a little more difficult than you two think! Heh-Heh. Security around here has been a little tight as of late! It might take us some time, that's all. Heh-Heh-Heh. Minion: And why the hell do I need to go? It was his fault that the damn thing was destroyed in the first place! Hessian: Because, knowing Caleb's luck he will most likely ruin yet another copy of the contract. Caleb: HEY.... Cain: Enough of this! Caleb and Minion, GO!!!! Caleb and Minion jump in shock from Cain's order. Cain: NOW!!! Caleb and Minion scurry out the door of the locker room. Cain turns to Hessian. Cain: You just need to know how to talk to him sometimes, but eventually he will do what you ask. Hessian: Finally, things are starting to unfold. This week is going to change the future of Heresian Bloodlust and at the same time, the destiny of Tom Walczak and Michel Krieg. Cain: Those two are going to wish that they never won that match last week! But first we have some business to take care of. We have to fulfill a promise to soeone we made a couple of weeks ago. Hessian nods in complete understanding of what is ally is talking about. Both Hessian and Cain turn and exit the locker room. The camera shot changes back anounce table. Nate, Robbie, and Steven all shared a confused look on their faces. Steven: Well, if that doesn't leave you with some questions. What the hell is going on? Nate: It would appear as if Cain and Hessian are going to have some sort of announcement to make this week about their title match at Head on Collision. Steven: What is in Cain's contract that is so important? Robbie: What about the promise that needs to be fulfilled? Nate: Well, there are certainly a lot of questions up in the air right now. I'm sure that everything will be exposed tonight. Steven: Mmmm….Exposed….
