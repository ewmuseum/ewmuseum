Tears: Cinq Written: June 29, 2002 Reno had made a convenient discovery. He saw Corg throw something in the back of a car, probably his bags, and now he was busy speaking to a security guard. Reno crept up silently, minding the pain in his shin, and inconspicuously slid into the car’s backseat where Reno lay down covered by a blanket. He would follow Corg wherever he went, and then get him for what he did to Aimz. This time, there would be no security guards intervening. He listens as a brief scuffle outside is heard, and then Corg comes back. Corg sits in the car and throws it in reverse, not suspecting Reno at all. Davis smiles to himself underneath the blanket and awaits patiently as Corg pulls out of the parking lot and drives away...
