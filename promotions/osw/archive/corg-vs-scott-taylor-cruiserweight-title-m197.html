Corg vs. Scott Taylor, Cruiserweight Title

2002-07-20
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Corg vs. Scott Taylor, Cruiserweight Title Written: July 20, 2002 From behind the curtain steps a short and rather obese balding man. He's several inches shorter than Scott but looks to weigh a lot more. A terrible comb over covers his baldhead and his wardrobe is unbelievably tacky. A pair of rather large and bulky white tennis shoes, blue slacks, and a white tee shirt with the words 'Taylor Trained Ninja' written across the front. In his right hand he holds a long katana, made of plastic that is. He holds the sword over his head and lets out a war cry! Scott: Please welcome, my own personal bodyguard, Randolph! Nate: Sigh….Is that Ralphus’ twin brother? The crowd boo as Randolph heads backstage again, in fear of the members of NOW and what they may do in the following match up. Taylor looks at the ring, with a glare of concentrated determination on his face. The fans throw the odd booing jeers at Taylor Made, to which they receive nothing as Taylor simply jogs down to the ring and slides in, looking over the sold-out crowd, then staring at the entrance, awaiting his opponent and a chance at the big time. Nate: Well Taylor looks determined to beat Corg for his title. Steven: Of course he’s aware of the fact Corg is a killer, and that’s going to play into how he handles that nasty, evil, evil, evil, evil man! Robbie: T&A! T&A! T&A! Earl: AND HIS OPPONENT! Hailing from Detroit, Michigan and weighing in at 172lbs, he is the OSW Cruiserweight Champion…”The Quiet Killer”…COOOOORG!!!!!!!! “Machines” by Crossbreed blasts through the arena and the fans immediately erupt into a sea of horrifying boos and insults as the OSW Cruiserweight Champion emerges from behind the curtain, with his title around his waist and his girlfriend by his side. Shyme Orien hisses at the crowds to no avail, as they make their way down to the ring, dodging the odd cup of beer and bottle of piss. Making her way to ringside, Shyme offers a few choice last words to Corg before the Cruiserweight Champion enters the ring, hands the title to referee Pierre Gaston and flips off the crowd, snarling as Taylor stares at him, and Shyme watches patiently at ringside. As Gaston shows off the belt to the crowd, the stage is suddenly filled with the loud chanting of women. Nate; Uh oh, here they come…and they don’t sound happy… Robbie: They’re women…when are they ever? Steven: Two words: Multiple Orgasms… Robbie: I always deliver baby! Nate: I’m sure, in any case there are 19 of them there!!! The band of 19 marches down the ramp chanting against Corg. Six of them are clad in jeans, shirt and a coloured bandana. Ten of them are in militant T-Shirts with cut-off shorts and big black boots, while the remaining three are kitted out in polkadot dresses with bangles and earrings and long tied back hair and large signs reading “Down With Corg”, “Old School Women!” and “We’ll Put Corg In It!” Robbie: Well they’re out in protest against the mistreatment of women by Corg, while Firecun-…err I mean, Amy is probably sitting watching this! Steven waves at the camera. Steven: Heyzzies!!! As the 19 NOW members make their way down the ramp, the ground begins to shake, and the audience gasp as suddenly the Jumbotron wobbles, and squeezing through the entrance to the surprise of the crowd is a 20th NOW member. Clad in blue dungarees, with a bowl cut and a massive gut is a woman with more folds than an origami swan, more rolls than 100 Craps tables……… Nate: BIG BESSIE!!! Oh my god they brought Big Bessie out with them!!! Robbie: Who in the ovaries of hell is Big Bessie? Steven: SHE’S THE ONE THAT STOLE MY BAGEL BEFORE THE SHOW!!! The members of NOW, including Big Bessie, look at the ring, then suddenly at once rush Shyme, to the dismay of Corg. As he watches wide-eyed from the ring, they charge her, kicking her to the ground and beating down on her, before throwing her into the steel steps and leaving her crumpled in a heap, ten taking to a side of the ring each, 5 to a side. Pierre Gaston turns his nose up at the action and with a slow nod from Big Bessie, motions at the timekeeper. Ding! Ding! Ding! Corg and Taylor begin circling each other as the NOW members chant from ringside. They lock up, and begin pushing, trying to work each other over to either side of the ring. Corg kicks Taylor to the mid-section and plants him with a hard DDT. As Corg rises, Taylor pins around and leg sweeps him to the mat, nipping up and dropping Corg back to the mat with a fast neckbreaker. Both men nip back up and take a moment to take a deep breath, before charging each other. Corg ducks a clothesline from Taylor and connects with a neckbreaker of his own. Again both men nip up in the momentum as the crowd seemingly excite a cheer for Taylor out of their spite for Corg. The members of NOW jeer at the Silent Killer as yet again Taylor and Corg lock up. Nate: Things getting off quickly here with both men seemingly unaffected by a DDT and two neckbreakers! Robbie: They’re cruiserweights, nothing off of the mat affects them, only turnbuckle moves! Don’t you know anything! Steven: I know Bessie is looking at you like you could deliver to her, Robbie! Robbie: I SHOULD SLAP YOU! Steven: She’d like that I’m sure… Taylor and Corg try to counter out of the lock, with Taylor finally locking Corg in a hammerlock, only to be countered into a top wristlock, and then spun to the mat as Corg powers out of the move. Running off the ropes, Corg barely misses being smacked across the back with one of the large signs, and connects with a hard capoiera roll to a standing moonsault on Taylor. Corg remains strewn across Taylor and motions for Gaston to count. 1… 2… … … 3NO! Taylor throws Corg off of him, and gets to his feet as the members of NOW begin chanting again and Big Bessie licks her lips in the direction of Robbie. Robbie: Why isn’t that thing behind a cage? Steven: I believe it’s called a girdle and…one size doesn’t fit all apparently! Taylor pulls himself back up using the ropes and motions for Corg to pick himself up. Using the ropes for leverage, Taylor charges Corg as he rises and spins around him, whipping him off of the ropes as he spins. Corg rebounds off of the rope, and runs straight into a hard spinebuster by Scott Taylor. Taylor kicks Corg again to make sure he is down then takes to the turnbuckle. Climbing to the top rope, Taylor again excites a small cheer from the crowd, as the members of NOW chant against Corg. Nate: Well Taylor is heading up the turnbuckle and with the members of NOW behind him you know he’s gonna pull off something special! Robbie: WILL YOU TELL BIG BESSIE TO STOP LOKING AT ME LIKE THAT! Steven: I think she likes you…she likes you a lot! Taylor sizes Corg up, and as Corg stops in position, Taylor leaps off of the top rope, and connects with a hard Corkscrew Moonsault into the pin. Gaston counts. 1… 2… 3NO!!! Corg kicks out and hard, sending Taylor tumbling into the ropes. Nate: We almost had a new champion!!! The five members of NOW on that side of the ring gasp and run forward, ready to catch Taylor should he fall. Taylor pulls himself back into the ring as Corg spins to his feet and begins putting the boots to Taylor. Pulling him up, Corg whips Taylor only to be counter-whipped off the ropes. As Corg runs for the ropes, Taylor follows, only to be planted into the mat with a hard and fast springboard DDT from Corg. Nate: The action is getting wild here in the ring and neither man is showing signs of wanting to give up! Steven: I could say the same for Bess-… SMACK!!! Steven: OW! WHAT WAS THAT FOR! Robbie: CURSE BESSIE TO HELL! Big Bessie watches the action from ringside, winking at Decker as the action continues in the ring. Corg wraps his hands around Taylor’s neck, rather than going for the pin as Gaston watches the action. Corg strangles Taylor, however masks it to make it look like a Cobra Clutch, though Taylor’s gags and cries alert Gaston to check the situation, forcing Corg to break the strangle. Slinking back to the turnbuckle, Corg sits at the top, waiting as Taylor gets back to his feet. Turning around Taylor gasps as Corg flies off the top, planting Taylor with a hard hurricanrana, this time forwarding Taylor into a pin. 1… 2… KICK OUT! Nate: Corg wanting to put Taylor away early here in this match! Steven: You’d want to as well had you a 700lbs beast licking her lips in your direction! Robbie: I swear I’ll… Steven: YOU’LL WHAT?! You know Decker once you’ve had big; you’ll never go back! Robbie: I oughta… Corg runs off the ropes, but a recovered Taylor catches him in mid-cross body and in the momentum throws him over the ropes. The members of NOW, including Bessie, swarm to the side Corg is on, only to frown as he grabs the top rope and swings back into the ring. Again in the momentum Corg clips Taylor and sends him tumbling to the mat with a hard kick to the back of the knees. Nate: That’s a variation on that set-up move of Corg’s, it’s a pity he’s gone tumbling into Taylor on the way back into the ring! Steven: It’s a pity that’s because one of them housewives smacked him across the back with that dang sign! One of the NOW members rejoice as Corg arches his back from the wooden sign digging in. Grabbing it and throwing it at Big Bessie, barely phasing every man’s worst nightmare. Corg and Taylor get back to their feet, and back up into each other. Both instinctively stop and in a split second reach back and land a double neckbreaker, sending both of them stunned to the mat…again. Nate: How many neckbreakers is it going to take before they give up! Steven: I don’t know but if that’s how they’re treating the move then it should be renamed to the neckphaserthatwon’tbreakyourneckbutrathergiveyouabadbooboo! Robbie: ……………………Dead air for 1… Nate: 2… Steven: Oh feck you both! Corg and Taylor roll around the ring holding their necks in pain as the crowd jeer at Corg, and the members of NOW chant and boo and hiss at the Anti-God. Pierre Gaston checks on both men, when suddenly Big Bessie begins thumping on the mat, the rest of the NOW members following suit. The ring begins rumbling, as both men stir and slowly make their way back to their feet. Taylor takes the advantage and whips Corg off the ropes, this time ducking as Corg tries to execute a springboard dropkick, only to fly over Taylor and crash in the ring. Taking the opportunity Taylor himself runs off the ropes and connects with a springboard leg drop. Nate: Well folks the action is flying back and forth, and you can rest assured neither man wants to end up at ringside with NOW! Steven: No man wants to end up in the jaws of Big Bessie! Taylor wastes no time in nipping back up and leaping to the top turnbuckle as Corg lies still in the ring. Taylor leaps off, but misses hitting the plancha by a mere second as Corg suddenly rolls out of the way and spins back around and lands a leg drop of is own across the sternum of Taylor. Corg pulls Scott Taylor into the pin as Gaston drops for the count. 1… 2… … … … … … NO!!!!!!!!! Nate: DAMN IT! Corg almost had it there! Robbie: He never had no such thing! You all thought he was going to win! HA! Steven: Biiiiiiiiiiiig Bessie! Robbie: S-STOP THAT!!! Big Bessie begins rubbing her chest and stomach folds in the direction of Robbie, and the sounds of vomiting can be heard as in the ring Corg pulls Taylor to his feet and whips him off the ropes, hitting a Japanese Arm Drag on the return. Taylor rolls and lands back on his feet, ducking a clothesline from Corg then capitalising on it, planting Corg into the mat with a hard sambo suplex! Nate: The folks are going wild here as Taylor and Corg take it out of each other…though we are yet to even see the lumberjacks come into play! Steven: It’s all action here tonight on Slam!, and Baby, that ain’t no B.S.! Robbie: Oh put a Corg in it! Steven: You know I’m sure Aimz has said that very same thing herself… Nate: Oh you’re both a pair of monsters! Robbie: Ye daaaaaaamn riiiiiight! Taylor locks Corg in a Dragon Sleeper, pulling tight as Corg yells out obscenities. Kicking for the ropes, Corg gets ever closer, only to have the sleeper tightened around him. As Gaston checks on the situation, Corg finally manages to get a leg on the ropes, only to have it pushed off in an instant by Big Bessie. Corg rests his leg on the rope again, this time kicking away Bessie’s big paw, and catching the attention of Pierre Gaston, who forces Taylor to let go of the hold. Nate: Well Big Bessie doing her part for NOW! Steven: Mmmmm…Cellulicious! Robbie: Shut up! Shut up! SHUT UP!!! Corg takes his time to recover, as Taylor gets back to his feet and storms around the ring. Taking to a turnbuckle, Taylor perches, waiting as Corg finally gets back to his feet. Taylor stands fully on the top turnbuckle and as Corg turns, flies off, and connects with a missile dropkick, sending Corg tumbling across the ring. Taylor pulls himself up again using the ropes, but falls afoul of a recovered Corg, as the Quiet Killer grabs him and whips him across the ropes. As Taylor rebounds, Corg grabs him and, turning 90°, throws Taylor over the top rope and into the members of NOW with a hard belly-to-belly suplex! Nate: Whoa! Corg using a belly-to-belly suplex there to literally toss Taylor out of the ring like a rag doll! The members of NOW won’t be happy! Robbie: I don’t know, Big Bessie looks Taylor Made herself! The members of NOW suddenly surround Scott Taylor, and begin motioning for Corg to come out and get him. With common sense Corg replies with a shake of his head and a flip of the bird, as Shyme lies unconscious on the steel steps on the other side of the ring. As Corg paces around the ring, the members of NOW help Scott Taylor back to his feet, and hold him as he slowly recovers on the outside. Gaston looks to start a count-out, but remembers it’s a Lumberjack Match and allows Taylor to mingle with the members of NOW, unfortunately including the rebellious Big Bessie. Nate: Well Taylor is playing it smart and recuperating on the outside, though Corg seems reluctant to even dare venture out there with the members of NOW lying in wait! Robbie: You’d think they’d use Shyme to their advantage, but I guess with that frickin’ manatee out there at ringside you don’t need anything but a lure! Taylor shakes the daze of the suplex from his head, and finally takes to the steel steps. Corg steps back as the sign wielding NOW members accompany Taylor up the steps. As he enters the ring they step back, and the action continues immediately as Corg rushes Taylor, only to be whipped off the ropes and planted hard into the mat with a swift Arm-Drag Takedown. Corg is quick back to his feet however, and the two begin brawling. Exchanging lefts and rights they try to gain the advantage as the crowds cheer for the action, the members of NOW jeer at Corg, and Big Bessie licks her lips at Decker…again. Steven: Dude…she SO wants you! Robbie: Well, she ain’t getting any! Steven: Mmmm…Cellulicious! Nate: In any case the action is continuing here, and neither man is backing down from any of the offensive moves being put out by either man here tonight! Corg lands a fast leg drop across the sternum of Taylor, then takes to the top turnbuckle. Taking a quick second to flip off the members of NOW, Corg dives off and connects with the Super Frog Splash! Nate: Set Your Body Ablaze! Steven: NO I WILL NOT!!! Robbie: No, cock jockey, that’s Corg’s high risk move, the forg splash… Nate: Don’t you mean F-r-o-g? Robbie: Meh, Corg, Morg, Forg…does it matter? Corg mounts Scott Taylor and begins laying into him with quick closed fists. Gaston checks on the situation and drops for the count as Corg pins Taylor. 1… 2… … … … … … NO!!! Robbie: HAHAHAHHAHAHAHA!!! You SO thought he’d won it there! Steven: Nuh uh, I knew he didn’t have it won because no one brings out a 700lbs horse with a bowl cut without it being implemented in some form! Nate: In any case pinning predicaments are becoming closer and closer to wins here, so Taylor had better start giving his best! Scott Taylor kicks out, punching Corg in the temple with a closed fist of his own behind Gaston’s back. Pushing the Anti-God off of him, Taylor pulls himself up again using the ropes, and as Corg rises slowly and dazedly, receives a Bulldog from Taylor. Sprawling flat across the mat, Corg shows signs of fatigue as Taylor manages to nip up yet again, and taking to the turnbuckle. Perching, Taylor sizes Corg up and executes a 720° splash, only to have it countered at the last second as Corg nips to his feet and with extreme precision plants Taylor with a face buster! Nate: Corg with a risky manoeuvre there and it pays off as Taylor’s face is driven into the mat!!! Steven: That was nasty, dawg! Robbie: *On a cell phone* Yeah, hello…Save The Whales Foundation? Yeah we have a-… Nate: ROBBIE! HOW COULD YOU!!! Robbie: Easily…just dial-… Steven: Heartless monster… Nate: Keep your eye on the action here folks! With high risk moves like this you know the outcome is going to be one for the books!!! Corg pins Taylor again as Gaston makes the count… 1… 2… KICK OUT!!! Nate: How is Taylor kicking out??!! He should be dead after that move!!!!!!!!! Steven: He’s a former NTICW World Champion…isn’t he? Robbie: I think so… Steven: Yeah so the first thing he isn’t going to do is fluff up a title shot! Corg pulls Taylor to his feet, and pulls back a closed fist, as Gaston watches on. Though as Corg swings at Taylor, his closed fist is caught, and Taylor counters into a Full Nelson. Corg struggles as Taylor drags Corg across the ring and into the turnbuckle. Tightening the hold, Taylor begins climbing to the first…to the second…to the top turnbuckle. He sits with Corg locked in the Full Nelson, Corg failing to pull himself free of Taylor. The crowd begin cheering louder and louder, as Taylor finally leaps off of the top turnbuckle, spinning over Corg in mid-air, the Full Nelson still locked in. As he hits the mat, Taylor kicks Corg in the small of the back and releases the full nelson, sending Corg flying over the top rope and crashing before the entrance ramp in one almighty crash! Nate: OH MY GOD!!! TAYLOR JUST DECAPITATED CORG WITH THAT TOP ROPE SPINNING FULL NELSON LAUNCHING KICK!!!!!!!!! Steven: You made that name up! Robbie: I like it… The crowd however begin ovating suddenly as all 20 members of NOW, including Big Bessie, run around to the front of the ring and begin assaulting Corg. They lay into him with lefts and rights, jamming their shoes and boots into him and slamming their signs down onto him. Big Bessie gets in a few hard shots at his groin, sending Corg reeling in pain and trying to pull away through the crowd to the ring. The crowd goes wild as Pierre Gaston watches the rage unfold from the ring. Nate: Ladies and gentlemen, perhaps the moment we’ve all been waiting for, the destruction of Corg at the hands of some P.O.ed women!!! Steven: He deserves it really, but it’s fun now since Big Bessie is there! Robbie: This is killer T.V.! Pierre Gaston gags as they start concentrating on Corg’s groin, stomping and punching at it, while Corg lies in utter pain. Gaston climbs out of the ring and injects himself into the middle of the fighting, only to be caught up in it and finding himself trying to save his manhood from a vicious woman-handling. Taylor watches from inside the ring, worn away and laughing heartily at the members of NOW taking it to Corg in a vicious style. As Taylor laughs on at Corg, the crowd ovate to an extent that the floor is rumbling, Taylor is clueless however as that ovation is directed at none other than Kid Wonder, who skips over the guardrail behind the announce table and slides into the ring behind Taylor. Nate: KID WONDER!!!!!!!!! Steven: LOOK OUT TAYLOR!!! LOOK BEHIND YOU!!!!!!!!! Robbie: THAT SUMBITCH SPILLED MY DR. PEPPER!!! Nate: Y-what? In the ring, Kid Wonder taps Scott Taylor on the shoulder, and as Taylor Made turns around finds a Superkick planted square in his jaw. Taylor falls to the ground heavily dazed as Kid Wonder heads to the top rope and dives off, hitting the frog splash set-up into the guillotine leg drop, crashing into the throat of Scott Taylor and knocking the man out infinitely. Nate: SO WONDERFUL!!! KID WONDER JUST DESTROYED SCOTT TAYLOR WITH SO WONDERFUL!!!!!!!!! Kid Wonder begins celebrating in the ring, and watching as Pierre Gaston finds himself overpowered and beaten down by several of the NOW members, while the other thirteen including Big Bessie annihilate Corg. Nate: Well Kid Wonder and the members of NOW are the only ones moving, but Kid Wonder isn’t even in this match, so until further notice… Robbie: IT’S A GOOD OL’ FASHIONED BEATDOWN!!! Suddenly, the stage flashes and explodes in a cloud of black pyros, and Kid Wonder’s glee turns to horror as four silhouettes make their way through the mist and down the ramp. Vampir Nosferatu, Hessian, the Cultist and Minion the Imp make their way down the ramp as Kid Wonder hightails it out of the ring and back through the crowds to safety. Nate: THE CULT!!! The Cult are out here now!!! Who next? Robbie: Big Bessie’s trainer? The Cult stops at the bottom of the ramp, and with a nod to one another, suddenly begin laying into the members of NOW! Vampir systematically chokeslams four women at once, following up by throwing them all into the steel stairs, while Hessian begins taking on several of the NOW members, individually destroying them with Eternal Damnation while sustaining bitchslaps to various limbs. The Cultist on the other hand begins spearing down NOW members and pummelling them, while Minion the Imp finds himself face-to-face with none other than Big Bessie. The 3’11” Imp and the 6’4” 700lbs monster stare down one another, before Minion’s eyes widen and he recoils in horror. Minion the Imp: MAN FUCK THAT!!! I may be a lot of things, but I damn sure ain’t no entrée for White Trash Whale!!! Minion turns from Bessie and finds one of the more pretty NOW members at his feet. Reaching down and groping her chest and groin, Minion coughs as Hessian kicks ass in front of him. Minion the Imp: Uhh…mmm…YEAH! TAKE THAT YOU BITCH!!! HAHAHA!!! FEAR THE IMP!!! Oh god you feel good…AHEM! FEEL MY WRATH!!! Oh god feel it…HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!!!! The Cult stands among a field of broken female bodies, to an ovation from the crowd, an ovation of pure hatred for the group as they stand tall above the members of NOW. As Hessian, Vampir, Minion and The Cultist stand before the fallen, a loud grunting echoes from behind them, and they turn to find Big Bessie standing, her bowl cut messed up and her dungarees ruffled. Bessie snorts, and growls, as The Cult stand before her. The great beast of femininity charges The Cult, only to be kicked to the mid-section and pulled into the air by Vampir and Hessian. Holding Bessie high above their heads, The Cult uses all their strength as Bessie’s folds sink to the ground. The Cultist climbs onto the apron and jumps back off, spiking Bessie as The Cult drop to the mats, and a terrifying CRUNCH! echoes throughout the arena as Bessie’s neck breaks, much to the horror of the crowds. Nate: OH MY LORD IN HEAVEN!!!!!!!!! The Cult just executed their finisher: DEADHEADED! and it’s broken the neck of that innocent woman! Robbie: She ain’t innocent!!! Think of all the poor children in Africa then think of how many she’s probably eaten! Corg is out of it on the mats. Hessian wrap an arm around the Anti-God’s body and carries him into the ring, draping Corg’s limp body over that of the recently “So Wonderfuled” body of Scott Taylor. Vamp, meanwhile, brings Pierre Gaston back to his senses, slapping his little French face until the referee opens his eyes and gasps in horror at the pea-headed skin-and-bones skull that makes up Vampir’s head. Vampir points to the ring, and like a little possessed man, Pierre slides in and sees Corg covering Taylor. The crowd is on their feet booing hysterically as Pierre begins the 3-count. 1… 2… … … … … … … … 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Winner: Corg Time: 21:11 Nate: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN THE CULT HAS JUST SAVED COR HIS CRUISERWEIGHT TITLE, PLUS BROKEN THE NECK AND DECIMATED THE MEMBERS OF NOW AND ALL THE WHILE THE CROWD HERE AREGOING FUCKING BALLISTIC!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
