Queen of Extreme: Finals

28 Apr 2002

A Japanese Death Match, at Head on Collision -- the deciding match of OSW's own inaugural Queen of Extreme tournament.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Queen of Extreme: Finals Written: April 28, 2002 Earl: Ladies and gentlemen this next match is the finals of the Queen of the Extreme Tournament and is a Japanese Death Match! “I Stand Alone” by Godsmack hits the speakers and Lillith makes her way down to the ring a gleam of evil intentions in her eyes and a smirk on her face. Nate: This match although picked by Bethany is right up Lillith’s alley. Robbie: I’d like to get inside of Lillith’s tunnel. There is dead air for 1….2….3 seconds. Earl: Coming to the ring now standing five feet and five inches tall and weighing in at a hundred and fifteen pounds… Lillith!!! Lillith climbs up the steps and doesn’t hesitate as she rips her flesh going through the ropes into the ring. Robbie: That’s one sick puppy…I can play submissive… Steven: Wow! We finally have something in common!!! The sound of Robbie audibly vomiting is drowned out by “Terrible Lie” by Nine Inch Nails hitting the PA system, as Bethany Yamato emerges from the back and makes her way down to the ring. Earl: And coming to the ring now and leading the Queen of the Extreme standings with 24 and a half points, she stands six feet and four inches and weighs a hundred and seventy-six pounds…Bethany Yamato!!!! Bethany too makes little notice of the barbwire ropes as she steps over them easier then Lillith with her longer legs and stares back intently at the other finalist. Steven: I don’t know what to say. Nate: Why because your eye candy isn’t out here? Steven: No because I have no gay remarks to make during this match and it would mean I’d just have to do a normal color job. Robbie: I’m becoming a fan of woman’s wrestling already. Nate: Hell for this we even have a woman ref too. Speaking of the dimwitted devil, Betty Lovejoy signals for the bell and then looks for a same place to stay to avoid injury. Steven: I get the feeling that Betty isn’t exactly fond of this match stipulation. Both ladies who come from a martial arts background ready themselves in the ring. Robbie: Everybody HATES Kung Fu fighting… Nate: It’s loves, not hates. Robbie: Not for me it isn’t. Starting slowly the two woman counter each other’s attempts to get the advantage until Lillith slips up and Bethany is able to grab her leg and spin her down to the mat. Steven: Nice takedown by Bethany there. Nate: Are you giving meaningful commentary now? Steven: Everyone makes mistakes. Bethany stays with the advantage as she drives a knee into the back of the gothic one who almost laughs at the attempt to bring her pain. Nate: Bethany is smart to follow up and keep the pressure on Lillith. Yamato still with a knee placed firmly into Lillith’s back starts to pull back on her hair and head increasing the leverage but allowing Lillith to use it against her and sending Bethany flipping over her and into the barbwire ropes drawing blood from a couple of wounds on her back. Robbie: That’s going to leave a mark on that “playboy” body of hers. Steven: (snidely) As opposed to all the other stuff others have left on her disease ridden body since she turned 11. Nate: I am sensing hostility. Lillith gets to her feet and sets herself as Bethany gets up and charges. Lillith calmly ducks and flips Bethany over her and she sails dangerously close to the exploding board propped up in the corner. Steven: This was almost an exploding start to this pay per view. Robbie: Holy cow, Steven just made a pun that had no gay connection. Steven: Almost like the explosion Boca gets… Oh right, women’s wrestling I’ll be good. Nate: Robbie, did you just say ‘Holy Cow?’ Robbie: LOOOK!!! Lillith pulls Yamato up to her feet and quickly flips her back down with a judo flip and holds her arm in a wristlock. Nate: You really need to see be seeing this live to get the full effect of the quickness of these martial arts moves. Robbie: Heh…Even live this is boring. As Lillith twists and puts pressure on Bethany’s wrist her free hand reaches for a nearby board and as she manages to grab it uses a wild swing to catch Lillith in the side of the head with the barbwire end and blood splatters as she falls back to the mat. Nate: GOOD GAWD!!! THIS IS GETTING UGLY ALREADY!!! Steven: They were both ugly people when they woke up, so this is nothing new. Bethany shakes her wrist and rotates it, as she stalks Lillith still holding the cut down 2x4 and drives it but end into her gut pulling off a small piece of her shirt. Robbie: Clothing is one advantage in this match, the more you have the more material that will get between your flesh and the barbwire. Nate: As we are about to see. Bethany raises the board high above her head and starts to bring it down as Lillith manages to hook her leg with her own and a timely pull of Beth’s shirt sends her flying face first into the barbwire ropes and the crowd lets out a gasp as blood sprays upon impact. The Crowd: EWWWWWWEEEEE!!!! Nate: Dear God!!! She went face first into the barbwire ropes, that is going to mess up her face big time!!!! Robbie: Nothing a little plastic surgery can’t fix. Look at what it did for Betty. Nate: Oh you’re not saying… Robbie: Sure am. Nate: No they can’t be. Finally Lillith has caught her breath and is climbing back to her feet and is happily surprised when she catches the glimpse of Bethany struggling to pull her face free of the barbwire. Nate: Awwwwe man!!! That’s going to cause a few nightmares. Robbie: Forget Bethany…Ask Betty if those things are real. Nate: Maybe I will. Robbie: You should. Steven: A gentlemen there is a match going on, let’s try and show a little bit of professionalism please. Nate and Robbie look at one another, then frown and simultaneously look at Steven. Steven: (slinking down in his chair) Boca? Lillith grabs a handful of Bethany’s hair and with a yank and the sickening sound of the flesh tearing from her beautiful face she frees Bethany from the mess of barbwire blood now streaming from several gashes on her face. Nate: Ah! That’s a lot of blood!!! Robbie: How can she possibly continue? Lillith smiles as she lifts the larger woman up and over with another judo flip this time landing her in the middle of the ring. Steven: Pin her and it’s over!!! She stands and looks sadistically over in the corner were one of the two exploding boards lie and starts to pull it free of the corner. Nate: What is she doing!? Robbie: She’s going for the kill!!!! Nate: I see that but why? Robbie: Because she is one sick little puppy!!! Lillith rips the board free and drags it over to Bethany and carefully lies it barbwire and explosives down on top of her. Nate: SOMEONE MAKE THIS STOP!!! She hops up onto the turnbuckle and leaps off with an elbow connecting with the board driving the barbwire into Bethany… BOOOOOOOM!!!! A cloud of smoke follows the explosion making the in ring visibility poor for several seconds and leaving the crowd in silence. Nate: JESUS CHRIST!!! WHAT THE HELL WAS SHE THINKING!!! Robbie: THEY ARE BOTH DEAD!!! Steven: Good…Whores…the both of em. Nate: SOMEONE GET SOME MEDICAL PERSONNEL OUT HERE!!! As the smoke clears from the ring, the mess of what happened is finally visible. Lillith lies on her back beside holding her elbow that seems to be slightly charred from the explosion and Bethany is still under the board which is now in a couple of pieces held together only by the barbwire. Nate: THIS IS OUT OF HAND!!! WHOEVER WINS THIS TRULY DOES DESERVE TO BE CALLED THE QUEEN OF EXTREME!!! Robbie: Can either of them continue?!? Nate: Of course they will!! This is the Queen of the Extreme and there is no way it won’t continue!!! Almost on queue and to the shock of the crowd and announce team the board starts to move as Bethany fights to free herself from the wreck. Nate: HOW IN THE HELL IS SHE STILL MOVING!!! Robbie: I DON’T KNOW, BUT I THINK HER CENTERFOLD CAREER IS OVER!!! Steven: Pffft! As if she had one to begin with! Bethany manages to kick the boards off of her but the barbwire refuses to let go as it stays stuck to her clothing and skin, but she still staggers to her feet. Robbie: Well I’ll be a monkey’s uncle!!! She’s alive!!! Nate: Monkey’s uncle? Holy Cow? What the hell? Robbie: There was a ‘Father Knows Best’ marathon on last night, and the porn channel was out… Steven: My room had plenty of porn going on, and it wasn’t on TV!!! Nate: Spare us the details!!! Lillith too starts to get to her feet but Bethany manages to cut her off with solid right cross to the side of her face knocking her back down to a knee. Nate: I’m at a loss of words here. I just don’t know what to say. Bethany lines her up and drives a knee covered in barbwire into Lillith’s face and the sickening sound of flesh tearing again is heard as she pulls the knee away and Lillith falls back to the mat holding her face. Nate: Both girls had their share of battle wounds before but after this match they will be walking exhibits of a good cosmetic surgeon!!! Bethany starts to try and pull the barbwire off of her but it doesn’t want to let go of her shirt so she does the only thing possible and takes off her top revealing a tank top underneath. Robbie: If she’s smart that will make a pretty unique weapon!!! Bethany must have been thing the same thing as she holds the shirtsleeve and starts to whip Lillith with the barbwire-covered shirt. The Crowd: Ooooooooooo!!! Nate: Gosh, this is brutal!! Bethany gets tired of the whipping after three or four good shots which has ripped the back off of Lillith’s top and a good part of her back as well. She walks up to her and wraps the shirt around her neck and starts to pull back on it choking her and driving the barbwire into her neck as well. Nate: THIS IS OUT OF HAND!!! Robbie: Mercy is for the weak! Nate: And as we can tell by this showing and the showing through out the entire tournament that neither of these women are weak!!! Lillith claws at the shirt around her neck and eventually Bethany lets go as she looks around for another weapon. Steven: I think Bethany maybe taking the name of this match a little to serious. All she has to do is get a three count to win. Robbie: Can’t you see Steven, she doesn’t want to just win, she wants to hand out punishment and then win?? Steven: No one likes catfights. No one likes lesbians. No one likes scantily dressed women in the ring. Nate: Maybe not on planet Homoplex-47, but here on earth, its what makes the world go round!!! Robbie: HA!!! Bethany goes for another barbwire covered board and this time pulls a rag out of her pants. Robbie: THE RAG!!! BETHANY IS ON THE RAG!!!! Steven: EWWWWWWWWWEEE!!!! Nate: NOT THAT KIND OF RAG, YOU NUMBSKULLS!!! Bethany starts to wrap the cloth around the board as the strong smell of kerosene fills the air. Steven: Do you guys smell that? Robbie: Smells like gas…or kerosene?!?! Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! Bethany smirks as she sparks the lighter in her hand and the rag ignites as Lillith staggers to her feet. Nate: SOMEONE STOP THIS CARNAGE!!!! I CAN NOT BELIEVE THIS!!! Robbie: What can’t you believe?!?!? She already choked a woman with barbwire, what’s stopping her from smacking her over the head with a flaming board!?!? Nate: Hopefully security!!! Bethany lines up her target and takes a swing but at the last second Lillith manages to duck while grabbing Bethany’s arm and flipping her over with and judo flip causing the board to sail into the barbwire ropes. The Crowd: OOOOOOOOOooooooooooo!!!! Nate: What a counter by Lillith there!!!! Lillith pulls Bethany up to her feet and whips her into the barbwire ropes which now holds the flaming board and Bethany hits them hard with her right side and the barbwire can’t hold as they give out at the turnbuckles and Bethany falls into a heap of barbwire on the outside. Steven: HOLY VIRGIN BOCA!!! Nate: LILLITH JUST PUT BETHANY OUT OF THE RING!!! WE ARE ONE PIECE OF BARBWIRE SHORT!!!! Lillith smiles as with out hesitation she slides to the outside through the open side and starts to stomp away at Bethany on the ground. Nate: LILLITH IS IN COMPLETE CONTROL!!! Robbie: She likes it that way, believe you me!!! Nate: YOU KNOW NOTHING!!! Steven: LOOOOOOOOOOOK!!! Lillith looks toward the back as a shake in the ground is felt and the mammoth five hundred pound Kimbusa stands on the ramp and as quick as his massive legs will let him heads toward the ring area. Nate: NOOOOO!!! KIMBUSA IS OUT HERE!!!! Steven: HE’S NOT THE ONLY ONE!!! Lillith quickly starts to rip Bethany free from the barbwire mess as Kimbusa gets dangerously close. Lillith turns to face the man that out weighs her four times to one but she gets help in the form of Kid Wonder nailing Kimbusa from the side. The crowd explodes. Nate: KID WONDER JUST INTERCEPTED KIMBUSA!!! KID WONDER AND KIMBUSA ARE GOING TO GO RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!! The crowd does cheer wildly as the little Kid Wonder staggers the giant that is Kimbusa with rights and lefts and Lillith doesn’t waste any time pulling Bethany off the barbwire and rolling her back into the ring. Nate: KIMBUSA AND KID WONDER ARE GOING AT ON THE OUTSIDE!!! WHERE IS MOOSE MALONE!!! Robbie: I gather getting ready for his match against the CCW invaders!!! Bethany is back on her feet and ready for Lillith as she enters the ring, Bethany sees the opportunity and sets up for the spinning heel kick that starts her finisher but Lillith ducks, grabs Bethany around the waste tosses her into the air and catches her with a ¾ facelock neckbreaker driving her hard into the mat. Nate: LILLITH JUST HIT THE DEATH BLOSSOM!!! THIS COULD BE IT!!! Lillith makes the cover…. 1… 2… …Kimbusa makes a last ditch effort to make it to the ring but Betty’s hand falls with the three count before he can break free of Kid Wonder!!! Nate: IT’S OVER!!!
