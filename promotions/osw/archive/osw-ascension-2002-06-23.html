Ivan’s scream of rage fills the arena, as he plummets down towards the second tier, where both he and El Drago crash down upon the steel base of the second tier, unmoving. Blood still flows from the faces of Ivan, whose face is still contorted in shocked rage. El Drago lies motionless and unconscious, with clutching a handful of red suspenders. Nate: IVAN AND EL DRAGO ARE DOWN AND OUT!!! WE’VE PAST THE SIXTY MINUTE TIME LIMIT AND NO ONE IS ON TOP!!! The camera suddenly shifts from the shot of Ivan and El Drago, to the edge of the canvas on the top layer, as a pale, blood spatter hand grasps the top of the canvas. The bell rings. Nate: WE APPARENTLY HAVE A WINNER!!!!! Robbie: WHAT?!?! WHO THE HELL IS THAT?!?! Nate: I DUNNO!!!! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! The camera stays focused on the pale hand, as it scratches the canvas. Another hand reaches up to the top tier, then slowly, black hair matted with blood, edges over the top tier….hair which tops the blood covered face of the new #1 Contender for the Old School Wrestling World Heavyweight Title…. ….Rhett Locke. The crowd explodes into a thunderous pop. Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Nate: HOLY SHIT!!! IT’S RHETT LOCKE!!! IT’S RHETT LOCKE!!! Robbie: WE GATHERED THAT!!! Nate: RHETT LOCKE HAS WON THIS MATCH!!! Robbie: I DEMAND A PHOTO FINISH ON IVAN’S FOOT LEAVING THE TOP TIER!!! Nate: WE ARE OUT OF TIME!!! WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT ON SLAM!!! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!!! The camera shifts to an exhausted, bloodied and broken Rhett Locke pulls himself up onto the top of the canvas of the top tier, then promptly passes out from blood loss and internal trauma….as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears in the lower, central portion of the screen….as the show fades to black.
