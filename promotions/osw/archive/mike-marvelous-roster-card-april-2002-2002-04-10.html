AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio Mike grew up in a small, boring town in Nova Scotia, Canada and he was the middle child of a large family. He was a normal boy and fit in well. He went to school and got good grades, and had a couple friends here and there. When he got to highschool, he was urged by his parents to keep those grades up so maybe one day he could have a respectable Canadian job...like a lumberjack, a mountie, or a moose hunter. But Mike was growing restless inside. He wanted more than the small town suburbia hicksville life. Nobody knew it, but he wanted out. So he joined the school wrestling team and sooner or later, was the best the school had to offer. He was undefeated in scrimmage matchs, and when he was sent to tournaments, he won gold every time. Sooner or later, he won the provincials, and then after that...the nationals. He moved on to St. Francis Xavier University where he was even more successful than highscool, winning every conference championship and tournament they could throw at him. But it all came so easy to Mike. He wanted more. He got invited to the olympics and was going to go, but then he saw a program that grabbed his interest on television. FSW. Five Star Wrestling. Mike was hooked the first momment he saw it. It was wrestling...but also entertainment. And entertainment means one thing. Fame. Mike knew he could wrestle, and he knew he could be an entertainer, so he immediately wrestled a couple dark matchs for FSW and was signed immediately. This is exactly what he wanted. Fame! Glory! Exposure! He wanted everybody in Canada....the US....and THE WORLD to stare at his grinning face via a television set and watch his every move and he does what he does best...wrestling. Mike burnt through the ranks like a forest fire. He was undefeated at the first of his career with a 15 match winning streak until he finally lost a Primetime Championship Triple Threat against Jake Keeton and Marc North, and lost when Marc North pinned Keeton. Mike wasn't technically pinned though, but he still lost his coveted Primetime belt. But he moves on to bigger and better things. He won the tag team belt with 'The Idol' Jacob Jackson, his best friend, as part of a team called 'The Can-Am Connection'. When Mike and Jacob were together, they put a strangle hold on the fed. Jacob won the Extreme and World Title and Mike won the IC title, and they kept their power because Mike interfered whenever it looked like Jacob was going to lose the World Title. But Mike finally got tired of standing in Jacob's shadow while he saved his butt time and time again. The Can-Am Connection lost the belts when Mike walked out and Jacob lost his World Title. Now, without Mike's help, Jacob's career went on a downward spiral until the two finally met in a one on one match where Mike proved who the better half of the Can-Am Connection is...dispite what everybody says: 'Five-Star' Mike Marvelous! And soon after that, after feuds with Creature, Onslaught and Johnny Mayhem, Mike won the World Title. After holding the World Title for a while...Mike realized something. He was grandslam champion. His accomplishments were the greatest EVER in FSW. It was time to move on. So Mike went to PCW. Invaded would be the better word for it. He and Jacob settled their differences and teamed together once again in PCW, along with Jake Keeton, Slade, and Steve Jackson. They were known as the Five Star Five. The team lasted a while, but eventually, egoes exploded and the team split up. Steve Jackson left, Slade and Keeton went off on their own to team with Stewart Hix, and Jacob retired. Mike was on his own. But there was a problem.... ...He still had the FSW Title. So he dicided to put it to good use. So, on behalf of all the little people like his manager Mini-Mike, he went into the PCW ring and announced that he was renaming it the For Short Wrestlers Title and holding a match for it and Spring Fling. Almost half the PCW roster entered midgets into the midget elimination match, but in the end, Kremmy was the victor. Mike moves on, and found himself pitted against many PCW main eventers like Meanstreak, Trashcan Man, and Chef. He suffered many losses and he left the federation to regroup. When he came back, he was drugged by the openly gay faction known as the Ream Team and inducted as their 5th member. But pretty soon, Mike escaped their grasp, and tried to get back to his wrestling career. But his reputation was already buried. Everywhere he turned, he was called gay. And Mike doesn't even know what gay is. He thinks it means excessively happy. Even though everybody thought he was a joke, he still won the PCW T.V. Championship. He later lost it to DarkStar and was beaten within an inch of his life by Dark Age and stuffed in a coffin where he was never seen or spoken to.... ...until now. Mike is back, and this time...he wants the respect he deserves! So whether you think he is a dumb ass Canadian, a Reamer, or just some goofy Canadian guy who is on t.v. only to plug his products like MARVELotion, MARVELicious Soda and MARVELade Sports Beverage...one thing is for sure. He is a man on a mission to regain his integrity, his pride, and maybe get some more gold to add to his lengthy collection. And some say he has a new found mean streak that brings a whole new edge to his in ring style that will prove to make him a very formidible foe... Promo Archives CHICKENS~!
