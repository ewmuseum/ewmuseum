Drago calls out Stanislav

2002-06-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Drago calls out Stanislav Written: June 23, 2002 'Unleash the Drago' by Sisco hits the speakers, interrupting Nate in mid-sentence, but sending the crowd into a chorus of cheers. Nate: Whoa! What's he doing out here? Robbie: Hopefully not another Doug Troy English lesson. El Drago appears from the back but doesn't have his normal energetic walk to the ring. Instead it's a determined strut as he blocks out the cheering fans and gets down to business pulling a mic out. Steven: SPANDEX!!! Nate: Quiet El Drago is going to speak. Drago: Ivan! Here! Now! Nate: WHOA!!! El Drago just called out the Russian Bear! Steven: Looks like Drago doesn’t want to wait for the Main Event tonight Robbie: This guy must really be from Mexico because he hasn't understood an ass kicking yet. 'The Soviet National Anthem' by the Russian Red Army Choir blares over the loudspeaker suddenly! Nate: Here we go! Drago is obviously upset with the accusations that Stanislav made last week! Ivan Stanislav emerges from the backstage area, none to pleased. He roars as steam billows from the ground around him and heads towards the ring, stopping about midway. He reaches behind his back and pulls out a microphone. Robbie: All stand for the mighty Ivan Stanislav!!! Listen to his powerful words! Ivan growls as the crowd boos him. Ivan: What do you want, liar? Come out to show us who you really are?? Drago paces around the ring the crowd cheering in anticipation of what he will say. He walks up to the front of the ropes and steps on the bottom one and pulls the middle of the rope. With his free hands he holds the mic up to his mouth. Drago: Here! Robbie: Someone better call the medics. Ivan looks around, an arrogant sneer on his face. He laughs loudly, his ominous voice echoing through the arena. Ivan: DYAAHAAHAAHAA!!! You want..you want Ivan to fight you now??? DYAAHAAHAA!! Ivan takes two steps closer. Ivan: I would fight you..El Drago. But I do not fight liars! Nate: Who's playing the mind game? Stanislav? Or Drago?? Drago stands unmoving in the ring still holding the ropes open. Drago: Now! Steven: Whoa! The crowd starts to cheer and Drago simply stares at Ivan inviting him into the ring. Nate: This is huge! Steven: I know look at that bulge in *SMACK* Drago: You are Pussy! The crowd laughs and some break out into a 'pussy' chant as Ivan looks around, rubbing his chin. He turns to the crowd. Ivan: SAY WHAT YOU WANT! AT LEAST I HAVE MORALS!! I HAVE VALUES! I AM TRUE TO YOU PEOPLE! IT IS HE THAT IS THE LIAR!! Nate: Morals? I don't call attacking a defenseless chimpanzee moral! Stanislav squints his eyes and glares up at Drago, taking another two steps towards the ring, coming quite close. Ivan: I am not man hiding behind mask, boy! I am not man living a lie!! I am not man trying to be something else! YOU are the coward! You are the fraud!! And Ivan Stanislav will NOT lower himself to your pathetic, impudent level! El Drago nods as he lets go of the ropes. He starts to walk around the ring some more and then stops in the center of the ring. He drops to his knees and hits the mat three times and then stands making a motion of removing his mask. Nate: Did he just do what I think he did? Ivan tilts his head to the side and grins. Ivan: Are you saying, if I defeat you...you..you give up your mask?! Drago simply nods in agreement. Ivan grins and walks up to the ring and climbs in. Drago takes a few steps backwards, sizing him up. Nate: I think Ivan is agreeing!! Ivan and Drago, right here right now!!! Robbie: But the main event??? It’ll be spoiled!!! Ivan leans down to Drago and grins. Ivan: You obviously know what I am saying, oh devoted Spaniard....so allow Ivan to give you his reply. Ivan growls from deep within his throat. Ivan: If you wish to fight me..you have to prove yourself. You defeat Michel Krieg at Ascension, and you can fight Ivan one on one the following Slam, when I have TIME to deal with the likes of you! DYAAHAAHAA!! Ivan laughs right in Drago's face. Nate: Stanislav is blowing off Drago!! Ivan isn't going to fight him! He's passing him off to Krieg!! Ivan: You can wait for me later tonight. Drago stares up into Ivan's eyes and doesn't back down from the giant. Drago: Now! With that Drago nails Ivan with a stiff right to the jaw stunning Ivan but not really fazing him, he looks down at Drago and licks his lips. Ivan: That one was for free. Ivan turns to leave the ring but El Drago doesn't let him pushing him from behind. Steven: That El Drago must have some set of testicles. Nate: He's not letting Ivan go. Ivan turns around and stares at El Drago as the Mexican charges at him with a knee barely effecting Ivan. Robbie: Drago is no match for Ivan. Nate: If little Davis can beat Ivan, I think El Drago could do some damage too. El Drago grabs Ivan and whips him into the ropes and Ivan comes off with authority running right over El Drago. Robbie: See!!! Drago is quick up to his feet and gets right in the face of Ivan once again. This time Ivan doesn't back down throwing a right hand, but Drago ducks underneath the blow and sweeps Ivan's legs out from underneath him. Nate: See!! El Drago runs to the ropes and jumps with a moonsault but the Russian Bear gets the knees up. Robbie: See!! Steven: Enough you two! Drago holds his side on all fours as Ivan gets back to his feet. He looks down at the puny Mexican and starts to give him a few big hoofs to the side when all of a sudden the crowd explodes as Doug Troy comes running down from the back. Nate: The World Champion is here!!! Ivan turns to Doug Troy and waits for him to enter the ring. What he fails to see is Reno Davis come at him from behind. Davis grabs his arm spinning him around and starts to nail him with rights and lefts. Robbie: This was a trap!!! Nate: A cleverly scripted one too. Troy slides into the ring and helps Davis the two double teaming Ivan. Drago staggers back to his feet and Reno and Doug whip Ivan towards Drago who nails him with a drop kick to the jaw. Sending the big Russian backwards but still on his feet. Robbie: This isn’t fair, Davis, Troy and Drago are trying to take the Russian Bear out of the match tonight. Nate: As if Ivan hasn’t done that before. Robbie: Where’s the Red Army??? He staggers right into a double reverse DDT from Davis and Troy. Doug begins to stomp away at Ivan as Davis and Drago hop up the closest turnbuckle. Steven: Wow for once Ivan is on the receiving end of one of these attacks. Robbie: This will only anger the Russian Bear. Davis leaps off first the Upbeat Tempo and just as Drago is about to leap he is sent over to the side by a blur of German efficiency and he crotches himself on the top rope and slowly falls inside the ring. Nate: Michel Krieg is here!!! Krieg steps into the ring and Doug Troy is on him instantly. Meanwhile Davis is struggling to put Ivan in the Straight Jacket but the Russian Bear is fighting it. Robbie: Stop this Krieg!! Krieg grabs Doug by the back of his head and tosses the World Champion out of the ring. He pauses for a second looking at Ivan and then at Drago who is climbing to his feet. He immediately charges for Drago driving him down with a spear and then wailing on the masked man with rights and lefts. Nate: Krieg went after Drago instead of helping Ivan!!! Robbie: What was he doing? Troy is up again on the outside and hops up on the ring apron. The crowd starts to boo again as Tom Walczak appears from the back. Nate: All six men that will meat in the main event are out here right now!!! Troy glances at Drago getting beat down by Krieg and over to Davis and Ivan where Ivan has powered his way out of Reno’s grasp and is choking the smaller man with a forearm under his neck. Steven: Will he help his partners or go for Walczak? Troy turns back to Tom who has stopped halfway up the ramp. Doug Troy hopes into the ropes and stomps Ivan off of Davis and then turns and does the same to Krieg before hoping over the top rope and charging up at Walczak. Nate: He did the right thing, he helped his friends out before going for Walczak!! Tom is waiting for Doug and the two come to blows in the middle of the ramp way. Moose Malone appears from the back with a flood of security guards that do their best to break things up. Robbie: Ah the Fuzz is here. Davis and Ivan are trading blows in the ring as is Krieg and Drago when the security hits. The four men in the ring and the two on the entrance ramp aren’t giving up easily as they claw and toss security personnel. Nate: These six men will meet tonight in that very ring in a special six man tag team match up and as you can see there is a lot of bad blood between all these men especially with Doug Troy and Tom Walczak, Ivan and Reno and El Drago and Michel Krieg. Robbie: That’s right in a few short hours The Red Army will weaken their opponents for the matches at Ascension. Nate: It seems Moose and company has this under control we’ll be back after these messages.
