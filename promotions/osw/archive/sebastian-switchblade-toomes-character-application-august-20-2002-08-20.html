Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes Real Name: Sebastian Toomes Nick Name: Switchblade, which is roughly translated in Woman-speak as 'Asshole'... Catchphase: 'I AM THE STAR! YOU ARE NOTHING!' 'The Shining Star of OSW...WITH CROSS-OVER APPEAL, BABY!' 'I AM THE FUTURE!' 'You know why women like being fingered with these two fingers? Because they're MY two fingers!' Character Handler: Sam AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: 'Get To The Gone' by Static-X Date of Birth: August 12th 1977 From: Santa Monica, California Height: 6'2" Weight: 245lbs Wrestling Style: Suicidal, Hardcore, with a hint of Japanese technical. Not alot, though. But some. But not too much. But not too little. Just enough. Signature Moves: Sex-Plosion! (Osaka Street Cutter) Controlled Burn (This is a very cruel variation to the Sharpshooter. Toomes gets them in a modified Sharpshooter, but doesn't intertwine his leg with his opponent's, so he's got thier legs bunched underneath one arm. He places his knee into their back to apply pressure as a normal Sharpshooter would. But then he raises up, so thier head/face is supporting all the weight, while his leg is cocked, holding the inside of his knee against their back. He swivels slightly, then forcefully drives his knee into thier back, lifting them slightly so while thier neck doesn't snap, thier face is dragged across the mat, which causes a little bit of road rash. When the opponent in swept through, landing on thier backs, he can easily administer it again (Usually only in long matches). This is a heavy duty spot, and it takes alot of strength to pull off, and thus it usually only happens once in a great while.) The Greco-Roman Ballshot/Half Crab (This is move designed only to totally punish an opponent... Basically, all it consists of is putting an opponent in a Half-Crab then smashing their crotch with his fist while they are unable to defend themselves) Finisher Move: Future Shock (Jump Swinging DDT set-up into a Brainbuster) Commonly Used Moves Figure Four Leglock, Crucifix Kneebar, Baseball Slide DDT, Senton Bomb, Swinging Neckbreaker off the barricade, Flying Hurricanrana off the Top Turnbuckle, Shooting Star Plancha, Twisting Senton Inward Somersault Plancha, Running Dropkick, Nagata Lock Full Nelson DDT, Half-Crab, Missile Dropkick, 450 Splash, Evenflow DDT, Brainbuster DDT, Snap Suplex, Farewell, Asai Moonsault Current Team or Stable: I AM Stable... Past Teams & Groups: None Title History: Awards History: Description: Toomes stands about 6'2, weighing in at 245 pounds. His hair, once a lush black hue, is now dyed silver with blue streaks. He wears silver tights with a circuit design running up and down the length of them with blue kneepads and shin guards. On his wrists are chromed wrist guards, and he wears flat gunmetal colored sunglasses. History: Coming from the streets of Santa Monica, Sebastian Toomes first started in Indie promotions in the California area when he was 17, and made a name for himself as a face, calling himself 'Cyclone'. After his 21st birthday, he began training for a CCW contract, debuting in September of 2001. Since then, he has made a name for himself as a suicidal/hardcore superstar who can take grotesque amounts of punishment, but has been criticized for taking too many risks (and getting injured too often). While recovering from injuries (a BRUTAL Glass Table match at the end of November 2001), he began training for his next objective, getting exposure and money in the PTC. It was only a matter of time until he got the exposure he deserved. Hence, OSW. Toomes has two distinct personas. The first is his 'In Character' personality - this personality is brash, loud-mouthed, confident and cocky, and his promos on TV reflect this. Your typical loud-mouthed heel, you know? His other persona, the REAL Sebastian Toomes, is a work in progress. His wrestling career is tumultous, and the cast of characters that run in and out of his life have not made it any better. For instance... Jimmy The Dog Man: His groetesquely hairy friend from back home, his ex-manager, and occasional CCW announcer... Jennifer Kale: His so-called 'valet', this harpy of a woman disproves the long held contention that your valet actually likes you and sleeps with you... Hector Lewis: The gay PTC Talent Manager, he guides Toomes' career with an iron fist and a limp wrist... Diez Nutz and Rod Thicke: Toomes' ex-high-school buddies turned failed pro-wrestlers turned interracial porn stars, these two hope to attain thier shot at the big time by riding on Toomes' coattails... Thusly (is that even a word?), there is two Toomes to deal with, the In Character one the fans see, and the Real Life Toomes, who NOONE wants to see. Not even his valet.
