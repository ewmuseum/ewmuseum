The Cult vs. The Throwbacks Written: 13-07-02 Nate: You’re right. Well folks, it’s time again to head to the ring where the cult will take on the Throwbacks. Steven: Of course last week we saw The Cult pick up a win with an… Robbie: ETERNAL DAMNATION!!! ETERNAL DAMNATION!!! ETERNAL DAMNATION!!! Steven: From Hessian to the latest edition to OSW, ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson. This week now the #1 Contendership for the OSW tag team titles is on the line, and it’ll be a hard fought battle between The Cult and The Throwbacks! Robbie: Da Westsidaz should be on watch, because we all know Hessian is a former tag champion, and it won’t be unfamiliar for him to pick up this Contendership with his partner, Vampir Nosferatu! Nate: Well in any ca-… “Ante Up Remix” by M.O.P. and Funkmaster Flex suddenly hits the P.A., and the crowd erupt for the Tag Team Champions, Riptah and Short Circuit as they make their way down to ringside. Surprised, Earl takes to his duty. Earl: Err…uh…Ladies and Gentlemen…please welcome, the OSW Tag Team Champions, DA WESTSIDAZ!!!!!!!!! The crowd cheer as Riptah and Short Circuit make their way to the ring, carrying their title belts proudly on their shoulders. Dodging the ring, they head over to the announce tables, but rather than take a seat with Nate, Steven and Robbie, they take two steel chairs and sit next to the table, and place their belts on their laps. Nate: What are Da Westsidaz doing down here? Robbie: Well one guess would be to prove Short Circuit isn’t the third cult member…and secondly…they’re scoping out the competition…remember this is a #1 Contender’s match! Steven: In any case we’re going to get this match kicked off…so let’s head to Earl. “Ante Up Remix” cuts out and Earl steps into the ring, as “Paradise City” by Guns ‘n’ Roses hits the P.A. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall and is the OSW Tag Team Title #1 Contender’s Match! First, introducing, at a combined weight of 453lbs…‘Classic’ Clay Vendetta and ‘Old School’ Dave Gibson…they are THE THROOOWBACKS!!!!!!!!! As ‘Paradise City’ blares across the arena, Gibson and CCV emerge from behind the curtain to a reasonable cheer, to which they reply with nothing but the determination on their faces. Gibson scratches his head, a scar left from where he was assaulted by the Third Cultist. Vendetta looks as if he is worried about Niki, but in the same token, seems to be focused on the match.They stop before the ring and stare over at Da Westsidaz, motioning the belts around their waists, before whispering something to each other and entering the ring. As “Paradise City” cuts out CCV takes to the corner, and Gibson stalks around the ring. Earl: And their opponents…at a combined weight of 666lbs… Steven: What the huh! That ain’t no damn coinkydink there!!! Earl: they are Hessian and Vampir Nosferatu………THE CULT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The arena lights cut out, leaving everything in total darkness. As the soft introduction of “The Coming Curse” by Iced Earth plays out through the P.A., black rose petals begin falling from the rafters over the stage, falling gently in time with the music. On the Jumbotron the images of children playing, beautiful scenes of nature, wildlife interacting and other peaceful images play to the music… BOOM! Suddenly the peaceful images on the Jumbotron turn to scenes of death and pain as the guitars kick in and with every riff, the scenes change, the children playing become corpses crucified to a burning cross, the scenes of nature turn to that of disastrous war, and the animals turn to screeching beasts, scavenging and hunting off of one another in a gory battle. The black rose petals falling from the rafters begin exploding left and right, falling and then combusting in a white strobe effect, and as the last of the petals explode, actual strobe lighting around the stage begins going off, while a billowing fog of black smoke rises up from the mouth of the entrance, becoming highlighted by the strobe lighting. As the music intensifies, three shadows appear in the entrance, their massive frames hidden in the smoke. At once, they all walk out…Vampir Nosferatu, Hessian and…the Cultist. All three stand on stage, as the strobe lighting continues with the music. The Cultist is clad in his black robes, hiding any sign of a distinguishing feature beneath. The smoke in the entrance clears as The Cult makes their way down the ramp, and stop before the ring. Vampir stares at Gibson and Vendetta while the Cultist points at Da Westsidaz, staring at them. Hessian says something to Vampir and the Cultist, and as the Cultist stalks around to one side of the ring, The Cult enters the ring, Vampir taking to the corner first, leaving Hessian and Gibson in the ring face-to-face. Nate: Well referee Pierre Gaston has landed the job of this match…let’s see if he can keep things under control… Robbie: The only thing he’s going to control in this match is………erm………his………pssht I dunno… Steven: Well Hessian and Gibson are gonna kick things off…and after that win last week The Cult have to be feeling confident about this one… Hessian nudges Gibson as Da Westsidaz watch on from ringside, whispering to each other occasionally. Pierre stands at the side of them and finally the bell rings. Ding…ding…ding Robbie: It’s about frickin’ time… Hessian and Gibson stand toe to toe, staring into each other’s eyes. Trying to stare each other out, Hessian turns, shaking his head in disappointment, then swings back around with a hard forearm, blocked by Gibson, and sending the two into a brawl. Vampir and CCV watch from their corners as Hessian and Gibson exchange lefts and rights, Gibson trying hard to block Hessian’s gauntleted fists. Gibson ducks a jab and rolls under Hessian, then nips up behind him and mule kicks the Goliath in the back, barely moving the big man. Nate: Well Gibson is taking the offensive here but it’s going to take a whole lot of offensing to get Hessian down! Robbie: Offensing? Hessian turns and grabs Gibson, but ‘Old School’ twists himself out of the hold and counters it into a hammerlock. Hessian arches in pain as Gibson tightens the hold, and Pierre checks Hessian. The Goliath suddenly roars out and pulls Gibson over his head, still with the Hammerlock in hold, and throws him over and across the ring. Gibson lands at his corner and tags in CCV. Nate: Wow! Hessian just overpowered Gibson…literally! Steven: Well when you’re 7’2”…do as the 7’2” do… Gibson and CCV stalk around Hessian, the Goliath refusing to move, simply following each man as they move. Steven: The Throwbacks are doing well this early on…maybe The Cult should have thought twice before coming into this match so cocky… Nate: Exactly, a win last week will have lowered their awareness of what this team stands for… Robbie: Hermaphrodites? As Pierre subdues Vampir and turns back to the action, Hessian begins stomping at CCV and Gibson, and the Cultist watches smugly from ringside. Robbie: You know I could just mosey on over there and yank off his… Steven: Mmmmmm Delicious… Robbie: No you freak…his mask, or robes or whatever he is wearing… As The Cultist watches on, Da Westsidaz exchange their thoughts as Hessian grabs Gibson by the throat, and hoists him high into the air. In one quick motion, he turns towards the announce tables and thrusts Gibson over the top rope…and into Riptah and Short Circuit, sending all three men tumbling backwards into the technical equipment. Nate: WHOA! Look out! That’s twice in two weeks Hessian has manhandled Gibson like he was a child!!! Robbie: What action! Steven: What excitement! Nate: What? Robbie: OH DON’T YOU FRICKIN’ START!!! Hessian roars at Gibson, but as he turns around he is met with a roundhouse kick from CCV, which barely phases him as CCV’s leg bounces back off of Hessian’s raised chest plate armour. Hessian charges CCV, knocking him to the mat with a hard shoulder block. The Goliath stomps Vendetta in the chest and heads over to his own corner, and tags in Vampir. Nate: The Cultist and Jim Axtell continue to brawl here at ringside, yet Da Westsidaz haven’t lifted a finger to attack or aid anyone in this fight… Robbie: After the beating Heresian Bloodlust gave them at the PPV, even Da Westsidaz would be reluctant to interfere with Hessian or Vampir these days. In the ring, Vampir is on his feet, while Vendetta is struggling. Vampir advances on CCV and wastes no time in whipping him across the ropes, driving him into the mat with a devastating Tilt-a-Whirl-Backbreaker. Nate: PENTACIFIX!!! VAMPIR NAILS THE PENTACIFIX ON VENDETTA!!! Robbie: GO VAMPIR!!! Steven: Well when you’re the best…you kick the shit out of the rest… Vampir forgets to pin, and rather shakes some life back into the dazed Gaston, sitting him up in the corner and pointing him towards Vendetta. Vampir walks back and pins Vendetta, and Gaston slouches forward to count. 1… 2… … … … … … 3NO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Vendetta kicks out, and Vampir roars in disgust, throwing himself onto Vendetta and pinning him again… 1… 2… NO!!! Nate: Vendetta is NOT going to give up to a Heretic!!! Robbie: He will lose then he will die… Vampir pushes Clay Vendetta away and grabs his face in frustration. Lying on the mat, Vampir goes through a plan in his head, unbeknownst to him though Vendetta is pulling himself up to the top turnbuckle. Vampir slams a fist into the mat and stands up, but as he turns to Vendetta, ‘Classic’ Clay dives off of the top rope, but is caught in Vampir’s arms. The Soul Stalker parades CCV around the ring, and then stops at the turnbuckle. Almost throwing CCV into the air above him, Vampir catches Vendetta awkwardly and positions him into the powerbomb position. Sitting on the top turnbuckle, Vampir stands and as the crowd boo hysterically, dives off and plants Vendetta with a hard powerbomb, following up almost immediately with the Boston Crab. As Vampir waits for CCV to submit however, the lights in the arena suddenly cut out. Nate: WHAT THE HELL!!! Robbie: DAMMIT!!! Why does this always happen during a Cult match!!! Steven: Maybe because Vampir belongs to the dark side… Robbie: So does Jordan X Shabazz, but the lights don’t damn well cut out on him! Suddenly the arena goes dark, and the Jumbotron bursts to life. Everyone gasps and camera bulbs go off as an image of a gleaming cross appears on the screen, but as Vampir turns to the Jumbotron he is shocked and appalled to find an overlay of Propaganda's face in the background. Vampir suddenly cringes in pain from the sight of the golden cross, with Propaganda’s holy face on it, and as he wipes his beady eyes, he turns around into a kick to the mid-section from Vendetta, who plants him into the mat with a hard Leg-Hook German Suplex. The Cultist tries to stop Vendetta, but barely reaches the apron before being pulled back by Axtell, while Hessian and Gibson are still lying flat out, barely moving on the outside. Da Westsidaz watch on in awe as Vendetta declines the pinning situation, and instead applies the Dragon Sleeper with Leg Grapevine. Nate: CROWD CONTROL!!! VENDETTA LOCKS VAMPIR IN THE CROWD CONTROL!!!!!!!!! IT’S OVER!!! IT’S OVER!!! Robbie: NO!!! VAMPIR ISN’T TAPPING OUT!!! HE WON’T TAP!!! HE CAN’T!!! Steven: HE HAS TO TAP!!!!!!!!! Vampir roars out as Vendetta tightens the hold. The Cultist watches helplessly as Axtell tries to subdue him, and Gibson and Hessian barely stir from ringside. Vampir’s eyes roll into the back of his head as finally Gaston gets into the action. He checks Vampir over. Vendetta tightens the hold to its fullest. Vampir doesn’t show any signs of moving. Gaston lifts his arm. It drops once. The crowd are ecstatic as Vendetta sweats profusely. Gaston lifts Vamp’s arm again. It drops for a second time. The crowd are roaring the roof off of the arena. Gaston lifts Vamp’s arm again. It falls slowly, dropping…dropping…dropping…thunk! it hits the mat. Winners: The Throwbacks Time: 32:18 Nate: WE HAVE NEW NUMBER ONE CONTENDERS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THE THROWBACKS ARE FACING DA WESTSIDAZ FOR THE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIPS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! HELL HAS FROZEN OVER!!!!!!!!! Robbie: NO!!! IT’S NOT FAIR!!! VAMPIR NEVER EVEN TAPPED OUT!!!!!!!!!!! CLAY VENDETTA DIDN’T MAKE VAMPIR TAP!!! THIS IS NOT FAIR!!! NO!!! THEY HAVEN’T WON YET!!! THE CULT DIDN’T LOSE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Nate: OH BLOW IT OUT YER ASS!!!!!!!!! THE REAL WRESTLERS, DAVE GIBSON AND CLAY VENDETTA HAVE BEATEN HESSIAN, VAMPIR AND THE MYSTERY THIRD CULTIST TO ACHIEVE THE NUMBER ONE CONTENDERSHIP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Da Westsidaz nod at each other, then get up and leave, as Clay Vendetta exits the ring and celebrates with Axtell, and the Cultist slides into the ring to check on Vampir. At ringside, Hessian is still out of it as Axtell and Vendetta pull Gibson to his feet and carry him to the back, to an ovation from the fans. Meanwhile Hessian stirs on the outside as Vampir is woken up by the Cultist, but the fans don’t care as they cheer and ovate for the real wrestlers, The Throwbacks. Nate: LADIES AND GENTLEMENT WE HAVE NEW TAG TEAM CONTENDERS!!!!!!!!! THE THROWBACKS ARE THE TAG TEAM TITLE CONTENDERS!!! THEY’LL FACE DA WESTSIDAZ FOR THOSE TAG TEAM TITLES!!!!!!!!! Robbie: WE GET THE FUCKING POINT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THE REAL WINNERS, ARE THE CULT, BECAUSE THEY HAVE NIKI!!! Nate: I DON’T EVEN THINK VENDETTA KNOWS YET!!! Steven: WHOO HOO!!! Ok folks, we’ll be right back after the commercial, and hopefully by then The Cult will have picked up the broken pieces that were aspirations of Tag Team Gold, as well as their bodies, and left while they have their dignity. Nate: Join us after the break, folks!!! We’ll be right back!!! Vendetta, Axtell and Gibson stalk around the ring, calling for the Cult to return….as the scene fades to commercial.
