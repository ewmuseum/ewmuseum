Scott Taylor vs Kid Wonder for the Number One Contendership for the OSW Cruiserweight Title.

2002-07-06
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scott Taylor vs Kid Wonder for the Number One Contendership for the OSW Cruiserweight Title. Written: 7/6/02 Nate: We're about to pick things up with a match that will decide the number one contender for the OSW Cruiserweight Title. Robbie: You mean we have to watch two midget jobbers fly around the ring for twenty minutes? Man, I wish I would have brought a book. Nate: Robbie... Robbie: What? Nate: Nevermind.... Steven: Goodie! Two hard bodied twinks for me to ogle at! SMACK! 'Glory' by Sugar Ray blasts over the PA System and the fans immediately burst into cheers as The Quinnisential Teen Idol himself, Kid Wonder, steps out onto the stage. He looks over the fans as they continue to cheer. As he walks down the ramp towards the ring he slaps hands with as many fans in the front rows as possible. At the ring he jumps up onto the apron, steps in between the ropes, and walks over to the turnbuckle, jumping up to the second. one. The fans continue to cheer, they can't get enough of Kid Wonder. Earl: Making his way to the ring at this time, from Orlando, Florida..he weighs in at 169 pounds..'The Quinnisential Teen Idol' Kid Wonder! Suddenly the fan favorite music is taken over by a loud static sound and then metal rifts kick in as 'Whatever' by Godsmack begins to blare loudly sending the entire arena into a loud chorus of boos and hisses, followed by the lights dimming down. The once beloved fomer World Champion of one of those other shows is now a hated man. A crimson red spotlight begins to fly over the crowd as the camera focuses on it. Fans are pushed aside and Scott Taylor is seen walking inbetween them. He hops over the guardrail and slides in under the bottom rope. Taylor stands up to his feet as K-Dub hops down from the second rope. Earl: Coming to the ring from the crowd, from Baton Rouge, Louisiana..he weighs in at 219 pounds, the former NTICW Heavyweight Champion, Scott Taylor! The bell rings and the match begins with an immediate lock up. Taylor uses his weight advantage over Wonder to push him back into a corner. Speedy Riggs steps in and tells Taylor to break the hold, but he holds on until the count of four. Scott takes a step back and then comes forward hitting Wonder in the stomach with a kick. Wonder holds his stomach and Taylor grabs his wrist whipping him across the ring. Wonder bounces off the ropes and comes back towards Taylor who ducks down but Wonder hops over him, nailing a Sunset Flip. 1...2...Kickout by Taylor. Steven: Woo Hoo! Look at that! I wouldn't mind having that good of a view of Scott's butt! Robbie: Ugh! That's disgusting Smith! Steven: It is not! You just have to understand how good a man's ass can look Robbie. Robbie: No way! No how! NEVER! Nate: Hello? Will you two start paying more attention to the match please? Robbie: Why? Something interesting happening? Nate: Why don't you look at the ring Decker? Taylor rolls backwards to his feet and swings a lariat at Wonder as he is standing back up. Kid ducks the lariat and spins around behind Taylor grabbing him in an armbar, minor reaction from the fans. K-Dub holds onto Taylor's arm and begins to push it upwards applying a minor submission. Scott stomps his foot on the mat and leans forward a little trying to break the hold, to no avail. The fans are still behind Wonder as he holds the submission on Taylor. Speedy Riggs checks in on Scott who just shakes his head. Taylor places his right leg behind Wonder's and leans forward again, this time throwing Wonder off balance enough to escape the hold. Scott spins around behind K-Dub and lifts him up and then drops him down with a sit-out flapjack. The fans show their disapproval as Scott rolls Kid Wonder over onto his back and pushes his legs forward, pinning him. 1...2...Kickout by Wonder. Taylor pulls Wonder to his feet. He whips the smaller KW into the ropes. The teen idol bounces off and is met with a back elbow from the former NTICW champion. Taylor makes the cover. 1………2……kickout by K-Dub. Nate: Scott Taylor really working over Kid Wonder. Robbie: I hope this jobber match ends soon. Steven: I’d love to work Kiddie over. SMACK! Taylor grabs K-Dub and applies a standing leg lock. Wonder quickly scoots to the ropes and latches his arms under them. Referee Speedy Riggs signals for a break. Taylor waits until the mandatory five count is made before breaking the hold. Scott pulls KW away from the ropes, grabs his right leg, and stomps on the inside of the teen idol’s knee. Nate: Scott Taylor trying to ground Kid Wonder by going after that knee. Robbie: Smart strategy. Steven: If I ever got Kid Wonder on the ground…oh the things I can do. Robbie: Can you NOT be gay for one match? Steven: But he’s SO cute. Nate sighs Nate: If you two won’t call the match, I will. Scott spins K-Dub around and puts his right leg on top of the bottom rope. Taylor stands over the top of the knee with one leg on each side. The former NTICW champ then drops down, but Wonder jerks his leg up and catches Taylor right in the sack with a hard kick. Nate: Low blow by Kid Wonder! Robbie: No! Being an expert on bum knees, he is NOT supposed to do that! Steven: He touched another man’s unit. I knew he swung that way! Nate sighs…again Wonder uses the ropes to pull himself off the mat. Taylor, on the other hand, is doubled over in pain…holding his nether regions. K-Dub pushes the larger Taylor back into the turnbuckle. Punch. Chop. Chop. Kid Wonder climbs onto the middle turnbuckle. He makes a closed fist and begins to punch the head of Scott as the crowd counts along. Crowd: 1! 2! 3! 4! 5! 6! Oooh! Taylor with a forearm to Wonder’s nether regions. Nate: Low blow by Scott Taylor! Steven: Don’t hurt Kiddie’s package! I need to use that! With Wonder on the turnbuckle and hurting, Taylor takes his shoulder and powers him over the top rope and down to the arena floor. Nate: Kid Wonder had some momentum, but now Scott Taylor is back in complete control. As Wonder struggles to his feet on the outside, Taylor climbs onto the top turnbuckle. Nate: What is Taylor planning on doing? Robbie: Hopefully it involves breaking his neck. Decker laughs Nate: That’s not even funny. Robbie: Sure it was. If he breaks his neck, we’ll never have to see the jobber again. Nate sighs once more Wonder slowly rises up until he’s on his feet. Once he is, Taylor flies off the top and connects with a spinning heel kick right to the face of Kid Wonder! The crowd pops for the move, but not for the man who did it. Nate: What a move by Scott Taylor! Robbie: No it wasn’t! He’s still moving! Both men are slow to get up after that move by Taylor. Scott, however, is first up. He grabs Wonder by his blond hair as the former PCW star starts rising to his feet. Taylor pulls him over towards where the announce table is positioned. Nate: Looks like they’re coming this way. Steven: WOOHOO!! As Taylor stops in front of the table, K-Dub hits him with an elbow to the gut. And then another one. Taylor lets go of Wonder. Kid grabs Scott and hits him with a side Russian leg sweep outside the ring. Nate: Back and forth these two great athletes go. Robbie: Great compared to what? To a rock? Hardly. Nate: Why can’t you give either of them their proper due? Robbie: Because they suck! Steven: Really? Robbie: Not like that. With Taylor now down, Wonder slides back into the ring. As Scott slowly starts rising, Wonder slowly backs away from the ropes. He then charges and goes for a springboard, but loses his balance and falls back into the ring, landing on his feet. Robbie: What a loser! Nate: What’s he trying to do? Wonder runs at the ropes again, this time gaining more speed. But again, he falls back onto his feet in the ring. Robbie: Boy, he sucks more than I thought! With Taylor up on the outside, Wonder says “screw it” and launches himself over the top rope with a vaulting body press. Taylor, however, has had time to recover and sidesteps the move. Wonder crashes hard onto the arena floor. Robbie: Bwahahahahaha! Steven (concerned): Are you alright, my love? SMACK! Taylor sees the teen idol down and hurting. He stomps on K-Dub a couple times with his boot. Then he walks to where Robbie Decker is sitting. Taylor, without saying anything, pushes Decker out of his seat and takes his chair. Robbie: What the hell? Nate: Looks like you lost your seat, Robbie. Robbie: If it wasn’t for my bum knee, I’d kick that jobber’s ass! Taylor has the chair folded up and in hand as Wonder rises back to his feet. Taylor gets ready to swing it, but is met with a kick to the gut. Scott drops the chair onto the floor. Wonder picks it up. Nate: Kid Wonder has the chair! Robbie: Disqualify him! Riggs, who stands in the ring without making a count, warns Kid Wonder. Speedy tells K-Dub if he uses the chair, he loses the match. Wonder, not listening, swings anyway. Taylor moves at the last second and the chair makes solid contact with the cranium of OSW announcer Robbie Decker. Nate: Robbie Decker has just been decked with the chair at the hands of Kid Wonder! Steven: I’ll revive him! Wonder looks down at Decker and just shrugs, feeling no remorse. He turns around to face Scott, only to be met with a sidekick to the jaw from Taylor. Wonder drops the chair. Taylor grabs K-Dub and rolls him back into the ring. Taylor climbs back in. He then bounces off the far ropes away from K-Dub, rolls, and executes THE THUNDER BOMB!!! Nate: Thunder Bomb! That’s his signature move! This could be over! Taylor, feeling he has the match locked up, makes a non-chalant cover, 1…………2…………3…NO!!! Wonder gets his foot onto the bottom rope just before Riggs could drop his hand a third time. Nate: NO!! WONDER GOT HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES!! THIS ONE ISN’T OVER YET!! Steven: Go Kiddie! Taylor slams his hands on the mat and insists it was a three count. Speedy tells him it was two. Taylor, frustrated, grabs Wonder and drags him to the turnbuckle. Nate: Kid Wonder in all sorts of trouble. Taylor sets him up as he runs towards the middle turnbuckle. He has Wonder’s arms hooked. Taylor leaps off and goes for Facial Reconstruction, but Wonder pushes him away at the last second. Nate: Great counter by Wonder! Taylor, however, lands on his feet. He charges at KW with a lariat, but Wonder ducks and hits referee Speedy Riggs. Riggs goes down in a heap. Nate: Ref bump. Steven: I’ll revive him, too! Nate sighs Taylor turns around, not caring about Riggs. When he does, K-Dub kicks him and hits The Idolizer!! Wonder then hops up to the top and wastes no time in hitting SO WONDERFUL DROPPING HIS LEG ACROSS THE THROAT OF SCOTT TAYLOR!!! Nate: SO WONDERFUL!! THIS MATCH IS OVER!! Wonder makes the cover, but Speedy Riggs is out and can’t make the count. Wonder notices this and gets off of Taylor. He walks over to where Riggs lies. K-Dub nudges the ref with his foot a few times, but Riggs doesn’t get up. This is just the break that Scott Taylor needs to turn things around. He begins to show some signs of life in the ring, rolling over onto his stomach as the effects of 'So Wonderful' are still working. The referee begins to come to and Wonder turns around, heading back over to Taylor who is now on all fours. K-Dub leans down and grabs Taylor by the long braid hanging down his back, however Scott retaliates with a BLATANT LOW BLOW! The fans burst into boos as Kid Wonder drops into a heap infront of them. K-Dub holds his Crown Jewels of India as Taylor gets back to his feet. Scott runs towards the ropes and jumps up to the second one, back flipping off of it and landing across Wonder's chest. Scott grabs one of his legs and the now semi-awake Riggs make the count. Nate: This could be it! Taylor with a second rope moonsault! Steven: Oh! Right on target too! I wonder if he could land on my... Nate: Speedy Riggs is awake and making the count! 1...2...NO! Shoulder up at the last minute! Taylor sets up and holds the side of his head in annoyance, screaming out. He stands to his feet and pulls Wonder up with him, whipping him towards the corner. Scott runs after Kid, Kid Wonder hits the turnbuckle chest first and bounces back into the cheapest move in wrestling...The School Boy! 1...2...Kickout! Once again Taylor yells out his annoyance as Wonder manages to kick out yet again! Scott pulls up K-Dub and hits him with a stiff DDT planting him in the middle of the ring. However Scott begins to slow down a little, taking his time to get back to his feet this time. He walks towards the corner and steps between the ropes and then goes up to the top. Nate: Taylor is wasting too much time here! He has Kid Wonder down but he isn't capitalising on it! Steven: Oh yes! Take your time climbing up there Scotty-poo! Tight Butt! OH YES! Nate: Steven, please. You're supposed to be out here calling a match. Not checking out the wrestlers. Steven: I know, but it's so hard to pay attention when a tight male butt is right infront of you! Nate Sighs. Back in the ring Taylor is now perched on the top rope in a position like he's about to execute a Five Star Frog Splash. From this crouching position Taylor leaps off of the top rope with a Guillotine Legdrop! Nate: Oh my god! Taylor is performing Kid Wonder's finisher! Steven: Fly! Fly through the air my beauty! However the slowing down catches up with Scott. K-Dub sits up and rolls forward letting Taylor hit nothing but mat. Scott holds his lower back and groans loudly in pain at the missed move. Kid Wonder begins to pull himself up to his feet using the ropes as Taylor still recovers from the botched 'So Wonderful.' Steven: Hold on Scott! I'll come give you lip-to-ass! Steven takes off his headset and starts to make a b-line for the ring only to be stopped by Nate. Nate grabs Steven's arm and jerks him back into his seat. Steven puts the headset back on and lets out a heavy sigh. Steven: No fair! Why did you stop me Nate? Nate: You aren't payed to do that Smith. And besides, I couldn't let you interfere in this match anyhow. Not with what's on the line. Steven: MEANIE! Kid Wonder is now up again and seemingly recovered. He helps Scott up to his feet and decks him with a right hand. Scott staggers back and Wonder continues to lay in with the right hands. Scott keeps going back until K-Dub grabs his arm and throws him toward the opposite ropes. Taylor rebounds off of the ropes but he's one step ahead of Kid. He jumps over Wonder telegraphing the back body drop. As Wonder turns around Scott decks him with a standing side kick. Wonder falls back to the mat and Scott leans down and grabs his legs. He flips Wonder over and applies a boston crab, dragging him towards the middle of the ring in the process. The referee checks in with Wonder. Nate: Taylor has applied a Boston Crab, Wonder could be in trouble. Steven: My poor Kid Wonder...this is so sad! Nate: No...I'm not going to say anything. Be quiet Nate. Wonder shakes his head 'No' and starts to claw for the ropes. However the weight advantage that Scott posseses allows him to keep Wonder right where he wants him, in the middle of the ring. But the fighting spirit in the teen idol shows through, he begins to twist and turn in every direction and use his hands to push himself upward. The fans begin to rally into cheers as Wonder pushes himself off of the ground which in turn causes Taylor to stand. The show of strength has caused the fans to nearly erupt. Nate: Kid Wonder is escaping the Boston Crab using pure strength! This is amazing! Steven: WHOO! Push! Push! Finally Taylor looses his footing and falls forward, breaking the hold. Taylor turns around and charges at Kid Wonder with a clothesline. Wonder easily dodges the attack and dropkicks Scott from behind, sending him into the ropes. Scott comes back from the ropes and walks into a standing dropkick from K-Dub. Wonder pins him. 1...2...Almost! Scott kicks out and begins to climb back to his feet. As he reaches his feet Wonder swings an inside kick for his chest. Scott dodges the kick and sweeps the one leg that Wonder is still standing on. Wonder falls down onto his back and Scott grabs his left arm pulling him up. He whips Wonder towards the ropes with an Irish Whip and hits him with a drop toe hold. While K-Dub is still down Scott rolls him up. As Scott sits atop of Wonder he reaches forward and grabs the second rope, pulling it towards him. Nate: WAIT A MINUTE! TAYLOR IS HOLDING ONTO THE ROPES! 1....2....3! Nate: WHAT AN UPSET! SCOTT TAYLOR HAS JUST PINNED KID WONDER! Steven: Who cares about that win? Look at the position that Kid Wonder is in! Nate: Shut up Steven! That doesn't matter! Scott Taylor has just cheated his way into number one contendership! The bell rings and Scott jumps up to his feet celebrating as 'Whatever' begins to play again. Kid Wonder gets up to his feet and Scott jumps between the ropes to the floor outside. The song continues to blare as the fans boo loudly at the low life tactics of the OSW Newcomer. Earl: Here is your winner! And the new number one contender for the OSW Cruiserweight Championship! SCOTT TAYLOR!!! Scott walks backwards up the ramp looking back at the ring where Kid Wonder is standing on the ropes yelling at Scott. Taylor just smiles arrogantly as he disappears behind the curtain. Nate: That is an upset right there. Taylor pinned Wonder. Steven: He may have cheated, but the fact is that Scott is the number one contender now. Nate: Damn..I hope that punk gets his just deserves.
