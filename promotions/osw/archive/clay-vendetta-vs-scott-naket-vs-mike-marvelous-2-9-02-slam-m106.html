Clay Vendetta vs. Scott Naket vs. Mike Marvelous (2/9/02 - Slam!)

2002-02-09
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Clay Vendetta vs. Scott Naket vs. Mike Marvelous (2/9/02 - Slam!) Written: 2/9/02 Clay Vendetta vs. Scott Naket vs. Mike Marvelous Match Result: Draw Via: Triple Count-Out Stipulations: Triple Threat Match Notes: The continuation of the Canadian tri-feud from a week back. Nate: On that note, let’s head to the ring for our next match of the evening. Robbie: How exciting...this is going to be a showstealer...simply magnificent... Nate: You mean you are actually looking forward to this match? Robbie: Oops, let me get back to you Nate...I have to step out of the puddle of sarcasm I am standing in... Nate mutters something under his breath. Nate: Hardy har har...let's go to the ring. Earl: The following match is a triple threat match. Entering the ring first, weighing in at 240lbs, from Vancouver, British Columbia, Scott Naket! 'Supermoves' by Overseer starts playing and Naket walks to the ring with determination in his eyes. He stands at the far left corner of the ring and holds the top rope, stretching his calves. Earl: And making his way to the ring now at 225lbs, from Miami, FL and accompanied by Niki Weathers...'Classic' Clay Vendetta! 'Carry On My Wayward Son' by Kansas blasts over the speakers and Vendetta appears from behind the curtain to a shower of boos accompanied by his lovely valet Niki Weathers. They slowly walk to the ring, Vendetta sporting a confident little smirk the whole trip. Vendetta points at Naket and says something that is inaudible. He gets into the ring and goes into the opposite far right corner of the ring. Naket and Vendetta stare holes through each other from across the ring as Niki Weathers massages Clay's shoulders. Robbie: These two men hate each other...I love it! Nate: Not as much as they hate this next guy... Earl: And entering the ring last, at a weight of 247lbs, from Nova Scotia, Canada and accompanied by Mini-Mike...Mike Marvelous! 'My Hero' by the Foo Fighters hits and Mike Marvelous walks out with Mini-Mike at his side. Mike sports an arrogant grin that he flashes at both men. He is also carrying a Canadian Flag. Steven: There's a fine piece of Canadian Bacon... Nate sighs. Robbie: This job is great. I get to abuse you when you say stupid things...which is every two minutes. Nate: And business is about to pick up in the ring! Sure enough, Mike steps between the ropes, sticks the Canadian Flag between the top turnbuckle and the post, and walks into the center of the ring. Naket and Clay also walk to the center of the ring to meet him. All three men stare each other down. Nate: You can cut the tension with a knife! Suddenly, Naket and Vendetta both turn their eyes to Marvelous and they both deck him with forearms. Whenever Marvelous tries to get up, one of the men stomps him before he can get to his feet. Nate: Clearly they are still upset from Marvelous' attack last Saturday on SLAM! Robbie: And I am still upset that the boys in the back office allowed such a stinker on air... Vendetta and Naket continue to stomp on Mike until suddenly, Vendetta quickly clotheslines Naket to the ground. He gets down on the ground and begins to choke Naket. Naket rolls forward, then rolls back, monkey flipping Vendetta into the turnbuckle. As Vendetta hangs upsidedown on the turnbuckle in the tree of woe, Naket goes for a baseball slide into his face, but Vendetta pulls his head up and Naket slides right out of the ring and cracks his head off the ring apron. Robbie: Haha...what a buffoon! That's what happens when you mess with a Classic! Clay raises his arms and points to his chest as the fans boo. Clay turns around just in time to meet Marvelous' dropkick right in the jaw. Marvelous then whips Clay toward the ropes and meets him with a back body drop, but Clay drops down into a sunset flip. He tries to pulls Mike down into the pin, but Mike catches his balance and instead drops down onto Clay's chest and begins showering him with punches. Nate: Mike with the advantage now! Robbie: What are you talking about? Vendetta is in there with two Canadians. Now tell me who has the advantage... Nate: Vendetta, because we all know it takes three Canadians to screw in a light bulb. Mike continues to hammer on Vendetta and doesn't notice Naket climb onto the apron, pull back the top rope, and launce himself over the rope. Mike turns around just in time to meet a shoulder block by Naket that send him sprawling to the mat. Naket walks over to Marvelous, picks him up, and extends his hand in friendship... Nate: WHAT!?!? ARE NAKET AND MARVELOUS IN CAHOOTS!?!? Robbie: Get out of there, Vendetta! Mike goes to shake Naket's hand, but Naket kicks him in the gut and nails an inverted DDT that leaves Mike standing on his head, before crumbling to the mat. Nate: OH MY GOD! IT WAS JUST A TRICK! THAT'S EXACTLY WHAT MARVELOUS DID TO NAKET LAST WEEK! Robbie: Heh heh...you mark... Naket picks Marvelous up again and whips him towards the ropes and goes for a clothesline, but Marvelous ducks, and when Naket turns around, Mike hits him with a jab, and Naket returns the favor with a jab of his own, which leads to a huge fist fight between the two. Low and behold...Vendetta gets up and slowly sneaks over to the turnbuckle that holds the Canadian Flag. Clay Vendetta: Ahem... Mike and Naket freeze suddenly in the middle of killing each other and look in Clay's direction. Vendetta smiles, waves to them, then takes the Canadian Flag and blows his nose with it. Clay Vendetta: Oh...bless me. Mike and Naket look at each other, then look at Vendetta, then charge at him with a double clothesline, knocking him ass over elbows. Robbie: Noooo! Nate: Well what do you expect!?! That's what you get when you blow your nose in someone's national symbol. Meanwhile in the ring, Naket and Marvelous pick Vendetta up and double suplex him. Then while all this is going on, Niki Weathers climbs onto the apron and calls over referee Pierre Gaston. He turns his attention away from the match and struts over to the beautiful Niki Weathers. Pierre Gaston: Bonjour mon amour... As the ref is distracted, Vendetta pulls out a set of brass knuckles as he is about to recieve a double powerbomb, and he nails Marvelous in the top of the head with it, causing him to let go and drop to the ground. Clay then turns towards Naket and pops him one in the mouth. He looks at Naket's unconsious body, then at Marvelous', and decided to pin both of them. Nate: HE HIT THEM BOTH WITH BRASS KNUCKLES! WHAT A BASTARD! Robbie: Turn around ref! Turn around! But Pierre is still pre-occupied, even though Niki is shouting at him to turn around to count the pin!!! Clay begins pounding his hand on the mat relentlessly and Pierre is about to turn around to count the pin when Mini-Mike jumps up onto the apron and pulls Niki Weathers' skirt down!!! Robbie: HELL YEAH! BACK THAT ASS UP! Nate:...Damn... Steven: What a ho... Mini-Mike: Hehehe... Clay Vendetta: DAMN MIDGET! Nikki Weathers: EEEK! Pierre Gaston: SAC LEBLEUR! While this huge distraction is going on, Marvelous and Naket manage to recover, and Niki also manages to get her skirt back on. When Clay turns around, Marvelous and Naket slowly walk towards him. Clay tries to run to one side of the ring, but Marvelous is there to meet him, and he goes to run to the other side of the ring but Naket grabs him by the head and nails him with the HOTT DROP!!! Nate: It's over!!! Robbie: This is a conspiracy! Throw the match out! Naket goes to cover Vendetta, but Mike pulls him off. Naket yells at Marvelous and pushes him, and Marvelous pushes him back. Marvelous goes to pin Vendetta, but Naket pulls him off. Marvelous answers with a kick to the gut and a TEXTBOOK PLEX on Naket! Pierre counts 1… 2... Broken up by Vendetta. Nate: Close call there! Vendetta points at something out in the crowd which causes Pierre to turn his head, allowing Vendetta to nail Mike with a low blow. Vendetta then hoists Marvelous up and brings him down with a SOUTH BEACH SUPLEX and goes for the pin. 1... 2... Naket breaks the count. Naket clotheslines Vendetta over the top rope and goes over along with him. They both regain footing on the outside and begin to brawl. Meanwhile, Marvelous gets up, shakes the cobwebs out of his head, and then walks towards the announce table. Nate: He's coming over here! Robbie: Get back in the ring and get pinned, Marvelous Marvelous walks over to Steven Smith and smiles at him. Steven: YEAH BABY! Come to papa! Marvelous then grabs Steven by the back of his hair and bounces his face off the announce table. Nate: MARVELOUS JUST GOT A MEASURE OF REVENGE ON STEVEN SMITH FOR THAT WHOLE REAM TEAM ORDEAL 6 MONTHS AGO! Robbie: Talk about holding a grudge... Mike walks away towards his opponents and Steven regains his composure. He points towards Marvelous and screams at the top of his lungs. Steven: OH, GET OVER YOURSELF! I'm glad we kicked your ass to the curb! Boca is better than you could ever be! Robbie: This is wrestling, ya flamer...not Queer as Folk! Watch the damn match!!! Mike gets back in the ring and Naket and Vendetta get back in as well. Naket is the first to his feet, catching Marvelous as he slides in with a big seated dropkick. Steven: And Naket LEVELS Marvelous! Nate: So *this* is what it takes to get you to call a serious match... Trying to seize the opportunity, Vendetta rolls over and covers Marvelous... one, two, but Naket drops an elbow, breaking up the count. Dragging Vendetta to his feet, he whips him against the ropes and lays into him with a HUGE chop! Crowd: WHOOOOOO!! Bouncing him back off the ropes, Naket repeats with another big Canadian chop... Crowd: WHOOOOO!! But by this time Marvelous has gotten to his feet, and poking Naket in the shoulder, turns him around and the two fight back and forth, dueling with open-hand chops to the chest! Crowd: WHOO! WHOO! WHOO! WHOO! WHOO! WHOO! WHOO! Robbie: God, it sounds like an aviary in here. Nate: The two Canadians battle it out with the move they're country has become famous for! Meanwhile, Steven Smith is whistling 'My Heart Will Go On'. Back in the ring, Marvelous gets the upper hand... slapping Naket into the far turnbuckle, getting a running start, and laying into him with a running flying cross-body press! Vendetta, meanwhile, has parked himself into the opposite corner and is using the opportunity to catch a breather. Nate: HUGE flying move by the man formerly known as 'Five-Star', Mike Marvelous! Robbie: Yeah, but do you see what Vendetta's doing over there? Taking a nap! That's why he's better than all of you. Nate: Biased, anyone? Robbie: I'm an objective reporter. I don't have any idea what you're talking about. Stop putting words into my mouth, Dumas! Nate: Actually, I think the match writers did that. Naket rolls out of the ring and Marvelous climbs over the ropes to follow him... and Vendetta follows them outside shortly after. Backing Naket up into the ring apron, Marvelous tries to Irish-whip him into the guardrail, but Naket reverses, sending Marvelous crashing into the barricade. Robbie: See? That's what you get when a Hoser tries a Mick move. Nate: One day on the job and we're already gonna catch flak from the advocacy groups... Naket starts laying into Marvelous with vicious kicks, and Vendetta joins in, double-teaming the downed Canadian. But Vendetta can't continue the onslaught much longer as Mini-Mike comes flying in, chop-blocking Clay in the back of the knees! Robbie: MIDGET VIOLENCE! The shot is enough to distract Vendetta, and as Marvelous tries fighting his way to his feet against Naket, Vendetta turns his attention to the dwarf, grabbing him by the collar and lifting him high into the air! Nate: I don't like where this is going! Robbie: Yeah, and as short as Vendetta is, it's gotta be legal! Hoisting him up and talking trash in the frightened midget's face, Clay drags him into the ring... and lays him out with a HUGE chokeslam! Nate: Oh my God! Mini-Mike just got absolutely laid out by Vendetta!! The hurt midget rolls out of the ring as Marvelous and Naket battle in... Marvelous gets the upper-hand, flattening Naket with a short-arm clothesline, and turns his attention to Vendetta. The two trade punches, and Marvelous gains control, setting Vendetta up for a DDT... but Vendetta reverses the move, and instead of dropping him to the mat, inverts Marvelous, steps through and locks him in CROWD CONTROL! Robbie: CROWD CONTROL! The hoser is FINISHED!!! Marvelous is struggling and thrashing, trying to get to the ropes, but as Vendetta concentrates on applying the pressure to Marvelous' neck, neither sees Naket climbing to the top rope... and the Canadian Icon flies off, LEVELING both with a split-legged moonsault and breaking the hold! Crowd: OLD-SCHOOL! OLD-SCHOOL! OLD-SCHOOL! Nate: Naket with a HUGE moonsault from the top rope and all three men are down and appear to be out!! Robbie: Too bad Naket has air skills similar to Kevin Nash. Referee Pierre Gaston looks down at the three men and is gonna start a 10 count, but Mini-Mike has decided to climb into the ring. Pierre Gaston shakes his head and looks at the little fellow, who walks right over to Clay. Pierre Gaston signals for Mini-Mike to leave the ring, but Mini-Mike just shakes his head and looks down at Clay. Pierre grabs Mini-Mike and demands that he leaves the ring before he asks him to leave. Mini-Mike again shakes his head and looks back at Clay. Pierre demands that Mini-Mike leave the ringside area. Mini-Mike turns his head again to Pierre and looks at him. He then charges Pierre and starts punching straight ahead, which just so happens to be in Pierre's midsection. Nate: OUCH! Robbie: HA! Steven: BOCA! Pierre falls in a heap to the ground, and holds himself, while Mini-Mike starts to lay to boots to him. The crowd, happy that the french referee is out of it, applauds the effort given by the little man, as he leaves the ring. Mini-Mike leaves the ring, and slowly walks by Niki Weathers. For the second time, he pulls down her skirt and laughs. Robbie: YES! Steven: You guys are pigs. Niki, who is now beet red in the face, pulls up her skirt and starts chasing the midget up the aisle. They disappear into the back, and the cameras go back to the ring where all three men are just getting to their feet. Clay gets up first, and charges towards Naket. Naket takes him down with a swift punch to the face. Mike charges at Naket and levels him with a powerful clothesline. Mike turns around and sees Clay back on his feet. He gives him the Canuck Kick, which sends Clay to the mat again. Naket gets up again, and looks over to Mike. Mike responds by giving Naket TING~! Robbie: ARGH! The light hurts my eyes! Nate: For once, I agree. Steven: I haven't seen so much white since my honeymoon with Boca. There is dead air for 1…..2…..3……4 seconds. Nate and Robbie: Shut up! Back in the ring, Naket too is blinded by the set of pearly whites from Mike Marvelous. Marvelous though, runs at Naket and clotheslines him over the top rope. He then walks over to Clay and locks in The MARVELock! Clay tries to fight the hold, but can't, and is a far ways away from the ropes. Marvelous yells at Vendetta to tap, as Clay just reaches for the ropes he can't reach and yells out in pain. Nate: Vendetta is gonna tap!!! Steven: LOOK!! Naket meanwhile, has borrowed a chair from Earl on the outside, and slowly makes his way back to the ring. He notices the referee is down, so he watches from behind Marvelous as Clay continues to try to make his way to the ropes. Vendetta though, gives up all hope and starts to tap fast on the mat. Naket quickly slides into the ring and smashes the back of Mike's head with the steal chair. Crowd: OOOHHHHH! Robbie: That's cheating!!! Nate: Hey, the referee is down. Steven: I go down! SMACKAROO! Back in the ring, Naket throws down the chair as Marvelous just crumbles to the mat out cold. He picks up Clay Vendetta and delivers the ICON MAKER ON THE CHAIR! Crowd: O-S-DUB! O-S-DUB! Naket rolls over and covers Clay. Nate: It's over, Naket's gonna pick up the win! Robbie: Ha, too bad the referee is DOWN! The two look over at Steven Smith, who just sits there and stares back at them. Steven: What?! The two sigh, as Naket starts pounding his fist on the mat, demanding a count. Frustrated, Naket stands up and walks over to the referee, and starts to shake him. Pierre Gaston looks up at Naket, who is now pointing down at Clay Vendetta. Naket walks over and pins Vendetta. Pierre Gaston manages enough strength to stop holding himself, and starts a count. 1… 2… NO! Clay Vendetta gets the shoulder up at the last second! Nate: SO CLOSE!!! Robbie: You can’t keep Vendetta down so easily!!! Naket, now really frustrated, begins pounding the mat with his fist. He looks at Pierre and signals three, but Pierre just crawls back into a ball and begins to hold himself again. Naket shakes his head, and picks up Clay. Naket goes for a punch to Vendetta's head, but Clay blocks it and starts rallying back with punches of his own. He punches Naket over towards the ropes, and with one classic punch, sends Naket flying over the top rope to the outside. He walks over to Mike, who has just begun moving, and picks him up. He runs with him and tosses him over the top rope as well. Clay falls to the mat and rolls out of the ring. He picks up Naket and throws hard into the steal steps. Naket crumples to a heap on the outside. Mike though has gotten to his feet, and turns around. However, Vendetta was ready for him and he hits Mike with the Vendetta Drop on the outside! Clay picks up Marvelous and walks over the announce table. Robbie: You show him who's boss Clay! Clay starts talking trash to Marvelous, and looks at Steven Smith, who his a giddy little smile on his face and is now blushing. Clay smiles as he looks at Mike, and grabs the Canadian Hero's head. He then shoves it right into the groin area of Steven Smith. Nate: AW! Vomiting is heard from Robbie's headset as Steven just stares straight forward with a still smile on his face. Clay lets go and Mike falls to the ground, gasping for air. The crowd begins to cheer, and Clay smiles, raising his arm to the air. But when he turns around, he notices the cheers aren't for him as he sees Scott Naket charging at him. Clay moves out of the way though and throws the already running Naket into the ringside barrier. Meanwhile, Pierre Gaston has gotten back to his feet and has started a count. 1… Clay walks over and picks up Naket. He walks him over to the ringpost and throws him into it. Naket bounces off and falls to the ground. 2… Nate: Clay is in complete control right about now. What do you think Robbie? Robbie: …. Nate: It appears Robbie is cleaning his mouth from the vomit that just came out of it. What do you think about this Steven? The camera shows Steven's face, which is stuck in the same position from when Mike's face was in his crotch. Nate sighs and watches the match. Clay turns around to check on Mike, but Mike meets him with a powerful spear. Clay falls to the ground and starts to eat punch after punch of revenge from Marvelous. 3… Marvelous continues punching Clay, again, and again, and again until he finally rolls off him. He picks up Clay and walks him over to the stairs, where he smashes his face into them. Naket rushes over and turns Mike around. He then kicks him in the gut and gives him a facebuster. 4… Naket grabs the side of the ring and starts to stomp away on Mike. He then rushes over and pushes Earl off the replacement chair he found. Naket folds the chair together and walks over to Marvelous. He raises the chair, but eats it back after a classic dropkick by Clay Vendetta! 5… Nate: That should be a DQ! Robbie: Bah, Naket got the DAMN chair anyway. Naket lays down on the outside, and rolls to his stomach. Clay walks over and locks on CROWD CONTROL on the outside! Naket starts screaming in pain, but there is no way to force Clay out of the hold. Mike Marvelous gets back on his feet and walks over to Clay. 6… Mike, sensing the chance of a lifetime, grabs Clay's foot and locks in the The MARVELock WHILE CLAY HAS NAKET IN CROWD CONTROL! Nate: OH MY GOD! DOUBLE SUBMISSION! Robbie: I can't believe this. Naket is still screaming in pain, as now is Clay, who refuses to let go of Naket's ankle. Mike is smiling, yelling things to both Clay and Naket. 7… Mike continues holding the hold, and looks in the ring at referee Pierre Gaston. He lets go of Clay's ankle, and looks at the two. 8… Mike laughs and walks over towards the ring. He grabs the ropes, but before he can pull himself up and into the ring for victory, both Naket and Clay grab both his feet. Mike flies through the air and lands on his stomach on the outside. Clay looks at Naket and Naket looks back, and then points at Mike. Clay locks on CROWD CONTROL again on Marvelous, while Naket grabs the other ankle and does a version of the anklelock himself. Marvelous now is screaming in pain. 9… Robbie: GET IN THE RING CLAY! Naket and Clay both look at the referee. At the same time, both men let go of Mike's ankle and turn to each other. They double clothesline, and both fall to the ground. All three men are down, as Pierre Gaston rasies his hand once more. 10! The bell sounds. Nate: All three men have been counted out! Robbie: Which is lucky for the Canadian's, because Clay was so close to whipping both of their asses and making them look like buffoons. Nate: What a match though. I think it's safe to say that things are far from over between these three men.
