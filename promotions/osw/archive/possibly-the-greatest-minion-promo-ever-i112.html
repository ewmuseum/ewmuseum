Possibly The Greatest Minion Promo Ever!

2002-11-05
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Possibly The Greatest Minion Promo Ever! Written: 11/05/2002 The scene shifts to the backstage area where an irate El Drago is storming through the backstage area. 'Maldígalos rusos, ellos pagarán por lo que ellos hicieron a Pablo.' Drago mumbles as he comes to a stop in front of a strange creature better known as Minion. '¿Qué el Infierno es usted?' he questions and then pokes the little purple guy with his finger. 'GAH!' Minion yells out in fright as Drago stands before him. 'What the hell do you want?' Drago jumps back in shock from the yell and starts to move in closer for a better look. The two mimicking each other as the masked man then leans back and scratches his head, 'Usted es un wierd la cosa pequeña.' He says and then shrugs his shoulders, '¿Ha visto usted a los Russians? ¿Puede decir usted mí donde encontrarlos?' 'Whoa! Whatchutalkin'bout sucka? Where's your buddy Roachie?' Minion stands almost dumbfounded, and as Drago waits for some sign of a reply, Minion nervously backs off a little, then coughs. 'WHATTY ARE YOO TAWKEEN ABOOT-AY? DONDE EL...MINKY PINKY, CHA CHA CHA...TEQUILA...BOYO.........no?' Drago looks around to see if he is the one being addressed and then realizes that Roachie meant Rucha and he grabs the little beast under the arms and lifts him into the air so he is eye level with the six foot four Mexican. '¿Rucha??? ¿Usted vio el ataque? ¿Dónde los Russians son???' He asks as he shakes Minion like a rag doll. 'Ay ay ay ay ay ay ay ay ay...OK!!!...You goddamn transvestite Spanish mothersucka!' Minion squeals, wriggling free of Drago's grip. Thinking to himself, Minion's eyes light up, and he looks all the way up at Drago. 'Russians you say? Why, you must be so pissed after IVAN KICKED YOUR ASS...erm...YOO LOOKY FOR REVENGEY!' Minion shouts, almost giggling in his speech. '¡IVAN! !!! ¿Dónde está él y esos rusos?' Drago yells down and Minion and goes to grab him again but Minion ducks and runs between Drago's legs. 'Los enanos condenados, vienen aquí usted abigarra.' He says looking upwards at Minion between his legs before turning around and glaring down at the giggling freak. 'Ellos deben pagar por su ataque en Rucha. Yo no esperaré el igual de etiqueta esta noche.' Drago says more calmly but a sullen and serious tone in his voice. 'Hmm...condenados...I remember that fro-...OH!...' Minion breaks out of thought and points defiantly at Drago like he knows Spanish. 'You want to find someone to help you fight Ivan!!!' screams the Imp they call Minion. A smile forms on Drago's face as he misinterprets Minion's understanding. '¡Sí, sí necesito ir el hallazgo Ivan tan yo lo puedo luchar!' he exclaims. '¿Dónde está él? ¿Dónde lo puedo encontrar yo?' He asks anxiously awaiting the answer. 'OK, erm...who would help you...OH I KNOW!!!!!!!!! There's a band of homosexuals just down the corridor...but they can't fight good...erm...' Minion's speech becomes fast and slurred, Drago has a hard time keeping up with him. 'If you want Ivan, you need someone reliable! Erm...Aimz can help you!!! Or somebody...I don't know...GOOO DOWN-AY THE CORRIDOR-AY TO THE LOCKER ROOMIES AND YOU'LL FIND A HELP TO FIGHT IVAN!!!!!!!!!!!!' Drago stares down at Minion and then down the hallway where the locker rooms are. He glances back and forth several times and then points at them, '¿El está en allí?' he asks. 'I don't think Muhammed Ali works for the OSW...unless Porter's been up to his old tricks again.' says Minion, slightly confused. '¿Qué?' Drago says glaring back at Minion who stares back at him. Finally Drago's patience wears thing as he yells out, '¡Dónde están a los RUSSIANS!' 'YES DOWN THE FRICKIN' CORRIDOR YOU SILLY SPANISH SONOFABITCH!!! TAKE YOUR TRANSVESTITE, FUNNY ASS WEARIN' CARCASS DOWN THAT FRICKIN' HALLWAY AND LEAVE ME THE FRICK ALONE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!' screams Minion, hinting at a possible 'Jab of Satan' towards Drago. '¿El está en allí?' He asks again pointing down the hall. Minion begins to go red...a strange thing for a creature of purple origin. With steam pouring out of his pointy ears, Minion sneers at Drago, his patience wearing thin, and then points down the hallway. 'YOU WANT IVAN??? DOWN THE CORRIDOR!!! GO! GO! GO! YOU'LL FIND SOMEONE DOWN THERE TO HELP WITH IVAN!!! I'M NOT ABOUT TO SO GET YOUR GODDAMN ASS DOWN THERE.........before I get HESSIAN...NOW GOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!' screams Minion. Drago nods at Minion and then storms off down the hall towards the locker room. 'El hallazgo Hessian dirá mí donde Russians son.' He says to himself as Minion shakes his head and the camera fades out.
