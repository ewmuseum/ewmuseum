Reno Davis vs

2002-04-27
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reno Davis vs Written: April 27, 2002 Earl: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, representing The Red Army, from Gdansk, Poland, he stands 6’10” and weighs in at 375 lbs: TOM WALCZAK!!! “Up and Gone” by Hoobastank plays throughout the arena as ‘The Polish Punisher’ Tom Walczak, along with Loyal Communist Party Member Wang and The Two Round Faced Women, head out from the backstage area and walk down to the ring. Earl: And his opponent, from Moncton, New Brunswick, he stands 5’6” tall and weighs in at 166 lbs: Reno Davis!!! “Rocket Science” by The Pimps begins to play as Davis, along with his pet monkey ZaC, head down to the ring. Reno points at Wang and then ZaC, giving him a warning to back off. Wang promptly puts a huge steak knife back in his back pocket. Davis climbs into the ring. Referee Pierre Gaston calls for the bell as Earl exits the ring. Walczak points at the much smaller Davis and laughs. Davis glares at Tom while he turns around and talks to Wang, pointing over his shoulder. Nate: Tom Walczak may want to take Reno Davis more seriously, after all he did defeat Michel Krieg last week… Davis walks behind Tom and waits for him to finish. Tom walks right into a drop toe hold and falls to the mat like a ton of brick, unprepared for the move. Reno hops to his feet and laughs as Tom surges to his feet, red in the face. Nate: Some mind games being played by Reno Davis on Tom Walczak? Robbie: Shouldn’t be too hard. Tom rushes forward towards Reno and goes to grab him. Davis ducks to the side and kicks The Polish Punisher in the back. Tom spins as if he was tagged by a flea and grabs Reno by the front of the shirt and hoists him high into the air with ease! Reno flails wildly and is thrown across the ring! Steven: Gotta hand it to him, he’s a strong guy. Robbie: Davis is 166 lbs! Nate: Yeah, but Walczak is 375! Reno gets to his feet as the two men walk to the center of the ring and in a very conventional manner, lock up. Reno appears to try to switch and slink around Tom for a waistlock, but Tom holds hard onto his shoulders and prevents him from moving! Tom grinds his fingers into the shoulders of Reno and drops him to a knee as he screams in pain. Tom stands tall over Davis and laughs as his massive hands envelope the shoulders of Reno and compress them! Nate: That’s an interesting submission move. Robbie: That’s what dumb goons do. Davis howls in agony as Tom laughs aloud. Tom lifts Reno up off the ground by the shoulders and rears back, SLAMMING his forehead into the skull of Davis and knocking him across the ring! Davis grabs his head and kicks his feet in the corner. Nate: That skull put a hole through a concrete wall folks, imagine what damage it could do to another person! Tom runs towards Reno and kicks his foot up to try to boot his face. Reno slides to the side at the last possible moment, causing Tom to miss completely, kick above the middle turnbuckle, and thus drive his groin into it! Tom gasps, as does the crowd, and falls out of the corner to the mat, clutching his crotch! Crowd: Ooooooooo…… Steven: The pain…the pain… Davis sees his opening and leaps to the middle turnbuckle. He leaps off and drives a knee into the stomach of Tom and rolls off and to his feet. Tom grabs his stomach and rolls to the side. Davis slides to the apron, springboard’s off the top rope, and guillotine legdrops Walczak. He hooks the leg! 1…. 2…. …kickout by Walczak! Nate: Near fall for Reno Davis! I’m impressed! Tom gets to his hands and knees while Reno climbs to his feet. Wang screams at ringside to rally Walczak, who appears to not really sure where exactly he is. Reno pulls Tom to his feet and sends him into the ropes. Davis bounces off the opposite side and hits Tom with a dropkick. Tom stumbles backwards into the ropes and bounces off into a DDT! Reno hops to his feet, gaining more and more momentum, and knee drops Tom’s forehead and follows up with another pin attempt! 1… 2… …kickout by Tom! Nate: Reno Davis is going to have to put a substantial beating on Tom Walczak in order to pin him. Davis rushes to the ropes and rebounds off again, only to fall flat on his face! Loyal Communist Party Member Wang appears behind him, whistles, and walks away! Nate: Wang tripped him up!! Tom gets to his feet and shakes his head, trying to clear the cobwebs from it. Davis gets to his feet and turns to yell at Wang. Tom clotheslines Davis hard in the back of the head, knocking him up and over the top rope and to the floor! Instantly, like ants, the three Chinese representatives flock to Reno and begin laying into him with boots. Nate: Hmm… Robbie: Faces, huh? Huh?? Steven: It would appear that some members of the Red Army are more….honorable than others… Tom stands in the ring and waits for his friends to finish with Davis. After a good beatdown, Wang and The Women roll Davis back into the ring and dust off their hands. Walczak grins to himself and walks over to Davis cockily, lifts him with one arm and tosses him across the ring! Tom walks over to Davis, places his massive boot over the neck of The Unwanted Angel, and begins to choke him while using the top rope for leverage! Davis kicks wildly as Referee Gaston counts… 1… 2…. 3…. 4…..Walczak lifts his boot…only to put it over his neck again! 1… 2… 3… 4….Walczak lets off and turns to Gaston. He screams at the Frenchman, giving Davis some time to recover. Davis rises to his feet and creeps up behind the massive Walczak. As Walczak turns to where Davis was, Reno circles around him. Nate: Tom doesn’t know where Davis is! Tom looks around in bewilderment as a boot shoots between his legs and crushes his groin! Gaston looks the other way as Tom’s eyes cross and he falls to his knees. Reno bounces off the ropes behind Tom and dropkicks him in the back of the head. Robbie: Keep an eye on Wang and The Women, looks like they’re still hungry from last week!! Outside the ring, The Two Round Faced Women duck at the corner near the announce position and hide while Wang walks around the other way towards ZaC, who eats peanuts that a fan has given him. Steven: I smell a trap… In the ring, Davis lifts Walczak slowly and clotheslines him back to the mat, apparently setting him up for something. Davis looks to the crowd and raises his arm in the air and rushes to the corner and hops to the top rope. He leaps off the ropes and hits Walczak with The Upbeat Tempo!! Walczak convulses in the ring as Reno rolls to his feet!! Nate: The Upbeat Tempo! Walczak looks like he’s finished!! Outside, Wang rushes ZaC who in turn runs away, straight in the direction of The Two Round Faced Women. The Two Women leap out from the corner and wrap their yellow hands around ZaC’s arms, restraining him! Wang leaps in joy, claps, and orders The Women to take the monkey backstage! Nate: The Two Round Faced Women have ZaC!!!! The Women nod and begin to walk towards the entryway. Davis hears ZaC screech in fear for his life and leans over the top rope in an attempt to grab one of the women. During all of this, Wang produces a frying pan from his jacket!? Nate: Where the hell did he get that? Robbie: The ways of the Chinese are very mysterious…. Wang slides into the ring and winds up, WAILING Davis in the back of the head…. Nate: LOOOOOK OUT!!! CLONG!!! Wang rolls out of the ring and Davis rocks back on his heels and falls to the mat. Wang screams to Walczak to wake up…. Steven: I can’t believe this!! Wang has got the monkey!!! Robbie: That sentence can be taken in so many different ways…especially from you Steven… Walczak rolls over and draps his arm over Davis… 1… 2… 3!!! Winner: Tom Walczak!! The Women continue to drag the fighting primate up the entryway, struggling as he continues to hold onto the grating with his dexterous feet. Walczak slowly rolls out of the ring and staggers up the entryway as well. Wang, however, slides into the ring and raises his arms in triumph. Nate: What the hell is he doing? Robbie: Too many noodles have rotted his brain. Wang flexes for the crowd and twirls the frying pan in his hand as Davis slowly gets to his feet behind him. Nate: Hmm..this is going to be interesting…. Davis grabs Wang suddenly and spins him around, locking him in the Straight Jacket!! Nate: Davis has Wang locked in the Straight Jacket!!!! Wang screams loudly, which prompts The Women to stop and stare up the entryway. Walczak stops as well and turns around to see Wang in agony in the ring! Reno screams to the Women on the entryway. The Two Women look at ZaC, and then at Wang, and then at ZaC, and then at Wang…. Nate: Looks like Davis’ll give Wang for ZaC! Steven: You smell something burning? Wang continues to blubber and whine in the ring as The Women slowly walk down to the ring and let go of ZaC. Davis lets go of Wang, who quickly rolls out of the ring and falls to the floor. The Women grab Wang and drag him up the entryway while Wang points at ZaC and vows revenge. ZaC runs to Davis and hugs him as the crowd cheers loudly… Nate: Well folks! Tom Walczak picked up the win, but thanks to some quick thinking by Reno Davis he stopped The Red Army from having Monkey Stew!! Lets head to a commercial break!
