'Angeldust'/Rhett Locke vs. Epitome of Evil/Corg

2002-04-13
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
'Angeldust'/Rhett Locke vs. Epitome of Evil/Corg Written: April 13, 2002 Nate: I would say we should head to a commercial after that truly disturbing scene, but we are set for our next match of the evening, so we are going to head to the ring for our next match of the evening. 'Chemical' by No One begins to play over the P.A. system, and instantly the arena is shrouded in darkness. Red lights begin flickering across the entrance way and soon makes its way out across the stands as the crowd jeers at the top of their lungs. Earl: Stepping into the ring at this time, at a total combined weight of 517 pounds, they are Epitome of EEEVIL, and COOOOORG! Robbie: WOOO! The fans continue to boo while Corg and EoE merely taunt the fans that adorn them. The boos continue to flood the arena until the mellow, dramatic beginning of 'Aerials' by System of a Down begins to blast through the speakers. The fans cheer in anticipation as strobe lights dance around the audience. LIFE IS A WATERFALL! WE'RE ONE IN THE RIVER AND ONE AGAIN AFTER THE FALL! The fans erupt into a chorus of cheers as Rhett Locke and 'Angeldust' Nick Casey step out, side by side, on the entrance ramp. Robbie: These two stand no chance in hell, Dumas! NO CHANCE IN HELL! Nate: Oh ye of little faith... Earl: Their opponents, at a total combined weight weight of 472 pounds. . .Angeldust, NICK CASEEEY, and OSW International Champion, RHEEEET LOCKE! Both teams are now in the ring, and all respected wrestlers size each other up. Epitome of Evil lets a small chuckle escape and steps out of the ring, and Rhett does too about the same time. Corg and ANC eye each other, and Speedy Riggs rings the bell. Nate: Here we go... Steven: Way to state the obvious, Mr. Dum-ass Robbie: Hehe Nate: Steven. . . Steven: oh don't start that 'i'll kick your ass if I do this say that' bull shit. Robbie: Wow, the guy has balls. Nate: Less than I can say for you. In between the announcers bickering, Corg and ANC have gone toe to toe. Corg soon bull-rushed the taller Nick Casey, but that didn't stop him from giving Angeldust a few good shots. Angeldust easily escapes however, and is soon up on his feet. As Corg stands, he's delivered a stiff dropkick, and the Silent-Killer falls to the mat. Corg quickly scrambles up, and dives to his corner to make the tag to the tallest competitor of the contest, Epitome of Evil. Nate: What a giant! Steven: What a hot tamale! Robbie: Of all the faggot things you've said, that's the most faggot thing I've heard from you in a while. Nate: Agreed. EoE towers over his nemesis in Nick Casey, but Nick stands toe to toe, with no expression of fear on his face. EoE swings a hard fist, Angeldust Evades, and follows with a kick to the back of his shin, taking the monster to a knee. Nate: Angeldust is doing everything he can to win! Steven: I'd to anything I can to win him! ... Robbie: AND LOOK AT CORG~! Corg is swooping around the outside of the ring, with a steel chair in hand. Rhett is seemingly unawair of the Anti-God moving to him. Nate: RHETT, LOOK OUT! SMACK! SMACK! SMACK! Nate: OH MY GOD! Robbie: YES! YES! OOOOOOOH GOD YES! ... Nate: Don't say a word Steven Steven: Sorry, Nate. But I'm afraid Robbie doesn't exactly give me a hard on. ... As Rhett continues to get hammered on the outside, Nick has apparently been taking it to EoE, and delivers the AngelLight- or a head to neck suplex. Nick, perhaps prematurely, goes for the pin. Though, an unlikely savior, the exact opposite even, saves his partner. SMACK! Speedy: 'Ey! Put that down! Nate: *gloomy* Luckily, Speedy doesn't care who wins the damn match. . . Rhett slowly crawls into the ring, and makes his way to his feet. As Epitome of Evil and Corg are literally beating Angeldust, Rhett vaults himself into the ropes, and baseball slides the back of Corg's legs, and Corg drops. Epitome of Evil grabs Rhett by his hair, and tosses him out of the ring! Robbie: CHAOS~! CHAOS I SAY! Nate: THIS IS MADNESS! STOP THE MATCH RIGGS! Steven: BOC-- Nate and Robbie: SHUT UP FAGGOT! Epitome of Evil literally strangles Angeldust Nick Casey from behind, lifts him high into the air, and performs a Reverse Chokeslam. The fans jeer, but Robbie Decker is on his feet, applauding the behemoth of a man. Robbie: Yes! Yes! WOO! Way to go EoE~! Steven: We know what's coming up next.... Nate: *sigh* C'mon Nick... Epitome of Evil: AAAAAAAAAH! One powerbomb. Two powerbomb. And a final, HUGE powerbomb! Robbie: Yes! Yes! EoE has the match won! Steven: I always loved the kinda man that could-- Nate: DON'T COUNT YOUR CHICKENS BEFORE THEY HATCH, DECKER! LOOK AT RHETT LOCKE! Rhett Locke has climbed to the top rope with a steel chair. He sets up Epitome of Evil while he goes for the pin-fall attempt! Speedy: One! ... Two! ...Thr-- SMACK! The Crowd: SCHOOL!!! SCHOOL!!! SCHOOL!!! Nate: RHETT INTERUPTED THE MATCH WITH A HUGE GUILLOTINE LEGDROP, WITH THE CHAIR PLACED UNDER HIS LEG! THE BACK OF EOE'S HEAD IS OPENED UP! Steven: I knew that my-- Robbie: There go the ratings... Epitome of Evil rolls out of the ring, clutching the back of his head. Nick Casey manages to find conciousness, and the highflying 'Angeldust' vaults into the ropes, leaps OVER the third rope and does a moonsault onto his nemesis EOE! Nate and Robbie: Unbe-freakin'-leavable! Steven: Blonde men are so hot when they can fly thr-- Nate and Robbie: Shut up, Smith! Rhett takes a moment to catch his breath as EoE and Nick Casey brawl on the outside... Corg: Once again, Rhett, it's do or die time... Nate: Huh? Robbie: What? Steven: BOCA~? Rhett turns around, and finds Corg standing in the middle of the ring, holding a manilla folder in his hands. Corg sneers wryly, and continues. Corg: Rhett, I know for a fact you don't want the world to know who you really are. . .Or should I say what you really are. Well, I have in this very folder, just *WHAT* you really are, Locke. Rhett shakes his head in utter disbelief. While the announcers sit in silence. Corg: Oh, you don't believe me? Come. . .take a look. Rhett cautiously moves over to Corg, and he opens the folder, revealing a single document. Rhett's eyes grow wide, and he steps back. Rhett: You can't do this! Corg: Oh, but I can. And I will. . .If you don't submit right here, right now. Rhett: ... ...Speedy, ring the god damned bell. Nate: WHAT!?! Robbie: NO WAY! WOO! Speedy Riggs, rather uncaringly, makes a motion for the bell to ring. Soon after, Earl makes the announcement from the outside. Earl: The winners of this match, as the result of a submission. . .EPITOME OF EVIL! AND CORG! Robbie: I can't believe that pussy in Rhett Locke! That quitter! That fake! Nate: Grrr, shut up Decker! You know for a fact Rhett wouldn't give up normally. It's just. . .Whatever's in that folder, for the sake of our International Champion, better not get out in the public. Steven: Which raises the question. . .What's in that folder anyway? ... Robbie: No shit, faggot. Nate: (disgusted) Anyway…We need to head to a commercial. When we return, we will be right back with our next match of the evening.
