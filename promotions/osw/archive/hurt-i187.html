Hurt Written: June 23, 2002 Corg begins scraping Aimz face against the side of the incline, across the steel fence, cutting open the lovely face of OSW’s favorite Diva, while Vampir Nosferatu and Hessian stand guard. The massive duo of Vamp and Hessian say something to Corg, who nods, then the pair make their way up the side of the mountain. Corg punches Aimz in the back of her skull, and rips out a chunk of red hair. Laughing at the site of Aimz’s red locks, Corg’s eyes light up with glee, as he gets an idea. Nate: I DON’T LIKE THAT LOOK!!! Robbie: I do. Kill that cumguzzler. Nate: (furious) Family show!!!! Robbie: Pay Per View!!! I can say whatever the FUCK I want!!!! Nate begins to sigh…but it turns to a gasp. Nate: OH DEAR GOD NO!!!! The camera shifts back to where Corg is brutalizing the unconscious Aimz, as Corg produces a pair of handcuffs from his belt and smiles cruelly. Robbie: SHE LIKES S&M!!!! Corg then laughs, and puts the one side of the cuffs on Aimz’ right wrist, then extends her arm above her head against the incline. He then runs the other cuff through the steel mesh, which covers the sheet metal grating that makes up the actual moutain incline. Nate: SOMEONE HELP AIMZ FOR CHRIST SAKE!!! No one comes to the damsel’s aid, however, as Corg manages to get the cuff through the links on the fencing, and onto Aimz’ other wrist. Nate: CORG HAS NOT ONLY CUFFED AIMZ HANDS TOGETHER, BUT HAS RUN THE CUFFS THROUGH THE FENCE, WHICH WILL NOT ALLOW HER TO MOVE!!! The groggy Aimz lies helpless on her stomach, with her arms cuffed above her head and, through the fencing. Corg lets out a cruel laugh and licks the blood off of Aimz’ face with his sickly black tongue. Nate: THIS IS JUST SICK!!!! Walczak: This tape would sell out in video stores in Poland…. Nate: So would Iron Eagle III, so that ain’t say much!!! Aimz’s face is a bloody mess, as two bites from Corg have opened up more blood letting wounds. Aimz is still had both hands handcuffed to the cage above her, as she faces the incline. Corg licks his lips as its obvious he has more planned for the fly he has caught in his web. Nate: THIS IS SICK!!! SOMEONE THROW IN THE TOWEL FOR AIMZ AND GET HER UNHOOKED FROM THE MOUNTAIN!!! Robbie: Uh…. Nate: WHAT THE HELL!!! The crowd gasps and boos, as Corg suddenly produces a knife. Nate: NOOOOO!!! CORG IS OVER THE LINE!!! CALL THE COPS!!! Corg licks the edge of the knife, cutting his own tongue, then spits the blood into the face of the screaming Aimz. He then thrusts the knife forward at her midsection, but does not cut flesh…instead, slashing Aimz belt. Nate: THIS IS SICK!!! SOMEONE STOP THIS TORTURE!!! Corg removes Aimz’ belt, then reaches infront of her and unbuttons the front of her pants. Nate: JESUS CHRIST!!! Nate throws off his headset. Robbie: NATE COME BACK!!! The scene shifts to ringside, where fans are going ballistic with rage, as Corg yanks down Aimz pants around her ankles, revealing nothing but a thong beneath. Several fans have to be restrained by arena security, from hopping the guardrails and hitting the ring to help Aimz. Unfortunately, OSW announcer, Nate Dumas, who is rushing towards the mountain to save Aimz, is grabbed by three security guys, who are unaware that he is OSW staff. Walczak: This is great!!!! Corg slices away the string of Aimz’s Thong with his knife, then cuts the edges of the undergarmet, leaving her bare assed to the world, with not even a single piece of fabric keeping her vital parts from the rest of the world….or Corg’s bulging member contained in his black, leather pants. Robbie: EVEN I CAN NOT TAKE THIS!!! THIS IS A FUCKING OUTRAGE!!! Aimz screams for help as Corg reaches for his own belt, and begins to unbuckle. The fans are ripping through guardrails at this point and are being beaten back by armed security. The commotion in the crowd has caught the attention of those on other parts of the mountain, even though many of them do not have a clear line of site to what is going on. On top of the mountain, Reno Davis is and Kid Wonder are taking turns keeping Howard Porter down, then notice the commotion in the crowd. Kid Wonder’s eyes then grow wide, as he looks at the Jumbotron in the America West Arena, and sees Corg now unbuttoning his pants with his right hand, while his left hand caresses Aimz’s nude buttocks and other parts in that region, in an attempt to perhaps “get” her in the mood. Kid Wonder slaps Reno on the shoulder, and shows him the tron. Reno’s blue eyes flash red with anger. Robbie: DAVIS IS FURIOUS, AS ANY NORMAL PERSON SHOULD BE!!! Walczak: She’s lovin’ it… Nate: (obviously just returning to ringside after breaking free of security) THIS IS A FUCKING TRAVESTY, AND EVERY MEMBER OF THE OSW ROSTER SHOULD BE OUT HERE RIGHT NOW DESTROYING SECURITY TO GET TO CORG!!! Robbie: This reminds me of the scene from the General’s Daughter… Nate: WHAT A SICK FUCKING THING TO SAY!!!! Robbie: Now that you are back, I need not be facish… Nate sighs. Davis rushes to his gym bag, which is still sitting in the corner of the top tier of the mountain, and this time, he does not pull out gauze….he does not pull out a towel. Instead, he pulls out his trusty, and ever present skateboard, throws it to the canvas, and begins to roll towards the edge with wreckless abandon. Nate: THAT’S SUICIDE!!! Robbie: THAT’S BUYRATES!!! Back down on the first tier tears run down Aimz face, as Corg lowers his leather trousers, and his bulging member springs forth, being digitally blurred by cable and Direct TV providers. Steven: DAMN CENSORSHIP!!! Nate: YOU FREAKISH ASSHOLE!!! YOU CAN SEE THE DAMN THING LIVE, YOU MORON!!! Steven: Oh…. Aimz lets out a scream as Corg approaches her…closer…closer…. Back up top, Reno flies over the edge of the tier, and floats in mid air, catching the second part of the incline, and almost wiping out!!! The Crowd: OOOOOOOOOOooooooo!!!! Nate: HE’S NOT GONNA MAKE IT WITHOUT KILLING HIMSELF!!! A scream from Aimz urges Reno on, as he regains his balances and prepares to hit the lip of the second tier, while traveling at breakneck speeds. Back down below, Corg inches closer, and he bites the back of Aimz neck. Aimz’ eyes grow wide, and she screams…as an airborne Reno Davis comes flying down from 40’ above. His skateboard has long since left his feet, and has been smashed on concrete below, leaving Reno to fly on his own, with nothing to break his fall…. ….except for Corg. Nate: JESUS CHRIST!!!! The Crowd: HOLY SHIT!!! HOLY SHIT!!! HOLY SHIT!!! HOLY SHIT!!! HOLY SHIT!!! Davis slams into Corg, and the pair fly off the first tier, both falling another 10 feet to the floor below, with Davis landing on top of Corg, and Corg therefore taking the full force of Davis’ 40’ decent. Nate: THEY ARE BOTH DEAD!!! THEY HAVE TO BE DEAD!!! Robbie: BOTH BEING DEAD IS A WIN/WIN SITUATION IN MY BOOK!!! No motion can be seen from either man, save the rapid loss of blood that each man is suffering. Nate: SOMEONE CALL THE LOCAL BLOOD BANKS AND GET PARAMEDICS OUT HERE FOR RENO DAVIS WHILE THERE IS STILL A CHANCE TO SAVE HIM!!! Walczak: What about Corg?!?! Nate: FUCK THAT PIECE OF SHIT!!! Steven: Gladly!!!! The camera shifts back to Aimz, who is crying and trembling, bared to the world, bloody, handcuffed and mentally broken. As Vince McMahon says, the show must go on, no matter who dies…
