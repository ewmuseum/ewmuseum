Corg and Andrew Hawk vs. Sebastian Toomes and Aimz

2002-06-15

Corg and Andrew Hawk vs. Sebastian Toomes and Aimz Written: June 15, 2002 On the stage, an explosion of pyro erupts and through the smoke… … Catholic Schoolgirls? Robbie: Oh…oh…OH GOD! Well, rather mature looking Catholic Schoolgirls. The kind that work in Men’s Clubs. And they shimmy and shake down to the ring, slinking down the ramp, leaning over the barricade and showing their ample flesh to the male fans in the front rows. The crowd pops as about two-dozen Schoolgirls come down the ramp, gyrating and dancing to the music, showing off their assets to the fans. Now, these are some incredibly hot women, with extra short skirts, knee-high stockings, and white blouses with no bras, showing some ample cleavage. Now…just think about that for a little while. Then imagine them bumping and grinding shamelessly. Robbie: THIS RULES! Nate: Uh…is it hot in here? Why are my pants feeling small? Steven: -sigh- The girls continue to dance and bump and grind, much to the delight of the fans, as Sebastian Toomes and Amy Campbell slowly walk down to the ring, bemused at the sight. Earl Heinrich: This Tag Team Match is scheduled for one fall! Making their way to the ring, weighing in at a combined weight of three-hundred and eighty five pounds…SEBASTIAN TOOMES AND AMY CAMPBELL! The crowd boos momentarily, then concentrates on the dancing Schoolgirls. Toomes and Campbell slide in the ring, the music still booming through the PA. Amy gets on the turnbuckles and poses while Toomes looks up at the entryway, waiting for their opponents. ‘Chemical’ by No One hits, and Corg sprints down the ramp, pushing a Schoolgirl aside roughly, and he dives in the ring, only to feel the boots on Toomes stomping down on him. Soon after, Ace Hawk, without music, runs down the ramp and follows Corg into the ring. DING! DING! DING! Nate: Well, ahem…I guess the match has begun… Two schoolgirls slink over to the announce table and sit on Robbie’s lap. Robbie: My dream come true! THIS DOESN’T HAPPEN OUTSIDE OF PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING, FOLKS! WOOO! Nate: Why do you get the Catholic Schoolgirls to sit on your lap?… She’s…she’s not wearing any panties… Oh…whew… Robbie: WOOO! Steven: I, for one, am quite sickened by all this heterosexuality. The schoolgirls squirm in Robbie’s lap. Robbie: Wooo! Not so fast, though… Meanwhile, in the ring… Toomes and Corg go at it, locking up – Toomes easily overpowers the lighter Cruiserweight, and transistions from an armlock into a German Suplex, slamming Corg back onto the mat! Corg hits mat and flops over, rolling with the impact, and tags in Ace Hawk! Toomes snarls and taunts Hawk, who rushes over the ropes and charges in, slamming Toomes with a massive clothesline! Toomes flips over in mid-air from the impact, falling to the mat on his stomach. Hawk cocks a leg and slams down onto Toomes’ back with a Elbow Drop. After making contact, he roughly flips Toomes over and begins slugging the former OSW Champion in the face with wicked hard shots with his right hand! Robbie: God, I wish I could concentrate on this match! Nate: Hey, ladies… Uh… Won’t a schoolgirl come and sit on my lap? Steven: I’ll do it, Nate! Nate: Uh, no. Steven: Well, the offer’s open! Nate: *shivers* Ace begins throttling Toomes, choking the life out of him…Until Aimz leaps on the top turnbuckle in her corner and executes a HUGE MOONSAULT, soaring through the air at an unprecedented height, and SLAMS down on Hawk and Toomes. She crumples to the mat as Hawk’s head meets mat, allowing Toomes to slide off and roll away! Robbie: Did you see that? Nate: The moonsault? Robbie: NO! This chick just rubbed my package! Steven: Yet another incredible display of athleticism is ignored in lieu of T&A. Robbie: Do it again! Toomes regains his bearings and picks Hawk up by the head, and suddenly slugs the crap out of him with a left hook. As Hawk reels, Toomes hits a DDT and makes the cover. 1… 2… And Hawk gets a shoulder up! Nate: C’mon, lady! Sit on my lap! Robbie: NO! THEY’RE MINE! Toomes spits and goes to make the tag to Aimz, who has recovered and waits patiently in the corner. Hawk crawls over to his corner, but Aimz comes in and savagely kicks Hawk in the back of the head, dropping him back down to the mat. Hawk tries to get to his feet, but Aimz sweep kicks him, knocking him back to the mat. Hawk takes the bump and recovers, but Aimz locks him in a hammerlock! Steve: Hey guys, they’re wrestling in there…Aimz is actually using moves! Nate: God, look…knuckles… Robbie: Oh yeah, you come here often? Well, let’s go in the back and change that! Wooo! Steven: Feh. Hawk powers out and swings a massive arm backwards, but Aimz actually does the splits and administers a Low Blow! Crowd: OWWWWWWW! Hawk doubles over and staggers over to the corner, slapping in Corg, who fairly flips over the ropes and does a Low Dropkick to Aimz, who is still on the mat after doing the splits… Aimz is knocked over and she howls in pain. Corg picks Amy up by the hair…and does a side kick, driving his boot into her mid-section! As she takes the impact, he savagely uppercuts her, all the while still holding her hair. He does a drop-toe hold and drives her face into the mat, all the while, still clenching a fistful of her hair. Corg grabs her hair and lifts her face off the mat, revealing a busted open forehead. He snarls and curses at her, then begins to repeatedly smash her face into the mat! Steve: Hey, Nate…Robbie… There’s a match going on! Nate: Shut up! Look at these chicks! What sort of strange power does Robbie have over them? Corg lifts her to her feet, and Aimz is dazed and covered in blood… He spits in her face…and Aimz SLAMS her forehead into Corg’s nose! Corg staggers back and Aimz drops to the mat, holding her head! Corg roars in pain and shakes his head as he sees Aimz on her back on the mat, holding her head, dazed. He dives on top of her for the cover! 1… 2… AND AIMZ DRIVES HER THUMBS INTO CORG’S EYES! Corg breaks the cover and grabs Amy’s hair and slams his fist into her nose! They break apart, Amiz clutching her face while Corg thrashes around covering his eyes. Aimz crawls over to her corner and tags in Toomes, while Corg blindly makes his way to Ace, who is screaming to be tagged in. Toomes gets tagged in and he drags Corg away from the corner by the foot. Corg gets to one foot and tries for an Enziguri, but Toomes ducks and forces Corg to the mat, locking in the Greco-Roman Ballshot! He gets Corg in the Half-Crab and balls up his fist, then slams his fist into Corg’s crotch! Corg convulses in pain, and Toomes gets ready to hit Corg again, but Ace Hawk nearly beheads Toomes with another monstrous clothesline! Robbie: Damn, baby… Can’t you wait until the commercial break? The Catholic Schoolgirl smiles and licks her lips. Robbie: Ulp! Toomes hits the mat and rolls away, only to have Hawk drag him back by his foot in an eerie re-enactment of what happened to Corg… Except Toomes gets on his hands in a push-up position and pistons his free foot back, smashing his foot into Hawk’s hand, causing Hawk to let go. Toomes scrambles to his feet, and spears Hawk to the mat. He proceeds to pummel Hawk with closed fists until the ref pulls him off. Hawk gets to his feet and shakes off the hits. Toomes circles him, measuring the CCW veteran… Meanwhile, a mass of Schoolgirls get up on the apron and Referee Pierre Gaston stares at them, his jaw slack. Unnoticed, Aimz hops off the apron and grabs a steel chair. Gaston: Sacre Bleu! Toomes and Hawk lock up, and Hawk powers Toomes down to his knees. He breaks the lock-up and slams his fist into Toomes’ head. As Toomes falls to the mat, Hawk takes a moment to breath… and Aimz slips into the ring with the steel chair! Corg screams at the ref and begins to get into the ring…and the Catholic Schoolgirls spin Pierre Gaston around and they point to Corg! Corg: GODDAMNIT, YOU FRENCH FUCKER! Pierre gets in Corg’s face and argues with the Goth as Aimz measures Hawk with the chair. Corg screams to Hawk, who slowly turns around… BLAM! Aimz BREAKS the chair over Ace’s head, and the veteran falls down to Earth, but Toomes wraps him up in a Small Package! Pierre Gaston turns around, but not before Aimz slides out of the ring with the steel chair. He drops to the mat and makes the count! 1! 2! 3!!! DING! DING! DING! Wiiners: Sebastian Toomes and Amy Campbell! As soon as the bell sounds, the Catholic Schoolgirls, en masse, abruptly turn and being walking up the ramp! Robbie: Wha? NO!!! COME BACK! Nate: What? Oh, damn… We missed the whole match… Steven: Fucking breeders…
