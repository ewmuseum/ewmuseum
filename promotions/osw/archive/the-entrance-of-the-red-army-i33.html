The Entrance of The Red Army

2002-02-02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Entrance of The Red Army Written: Feb 2, 2002 The crowd roars out in shock, disbelief, and disapproval as The Russian Bear of the PCW, Ivan Stanislav, stands in the entryway! The hulking monster, over seven feet in height and an even 400 lbs in weight, roars at the crowd! He stands in his black pants, black shirt, boots, and red suspenders! Nate: PCW’S WRESTLER OF THE YEAR, IVAN STANISLAV IS HERE, LIVE ON SATURDAY NIGHT SLAM!!! JANE REAGAN PROMISED TYLER NELSON WOULD BE SICK WITH GRIEF AFTER TONIGHT AND IT LOOKS LIKE SHE HAS DELIVERED!!! Doug- I can’t believe this!! What is that bastard doing here?!?! Nate: I DUNNO!! From behind Stanislav, his close friend, manager, and fellow Communist, Alexei Ruslan, walks out and stands beside him, carrying a large Soviet flag. Ruslan wears his brown trench coat, hat, brown pants, and dress shoes. Beside him, a Chinaman wearing a Chinese Military uniform and two round faced women emerge carrying flags of the People’s Republic of China! Steven- COMMUNIST PARTY MEMBER WANG AND THE TWO ROUND FACED WOMEN! Nate- There’s still a few more behind that wall of smoke…. PCW referee Speedy Riggs, donning his red and yellow stripes, emerges to the other side of Stanislav. Doug- THIS my friends, is a load of crap! Beside Riggs, Tom Walczak, the Polish Punisher, walks out!! Steven- The Polish Punisher!!! Doug- The Polish Punisher is in the OSW!! Ritchie Syxx and Corg now both stand side by side, staring at Ivan. As one, the Communists head down to the ring! Their feet echo and clatter on the steel grating entryway in unison, like incoming thunder! Nate- This isn’t good folks! This isn’t good at all! Ivan Stanislav is one of the biggest pieces of filth in the world, let alone this sport, today!! Stanislav points up at Syxx and Corg and roars again. Suddenly, Ivan and Walczak burst towards the ring with a speed that belies their size! Ruslan, Wang, The Women, and Riggs wait outside the ring and watch. Ivan and Tom hop to the apron, causing the entire ring to shake! Syxx almost loses his footing, but manages to stay standing as Ivan and Tom climb into the ring itself, point at the two men, and yell in their native languages! Steven- How exactly are you supposed to scare someone when they have no idea what you are saying?? Nate- By weighing in at a combined weight of over 700 lbs and looking like that! Doug- Stanislav is a SCUMBAG!!! Steven- Sore because he beat you and sent you to the hospital, Doug? In the ring, Syxx charges at Ivan and nails a superkick to the barrel chest of The Russian Bear, but Stanislav does not move. Nate: This is gonna be ugly! Corg runs and leaps into the air for a cross body block, only to land in Walczak’s arms and be fallaway slammed OUTSIDE of the ring! Nate: GOOD GAWD!!! As Corg hits the ground, Ruslan picks him up and holds him with Riggs. Wang and The Two Round Faced Women pummel him with martial arts punches to the face! Nate- WHERE THE HELL IS SECURITY WHEN YOU NEED THEM!!! Stanislav envelops Ritchie’s neck with his massive hand and lifts him up off the ground with ease. Stanislav lurches forward, CRUSHING Syxx with a tremendous headbutt! The Crowd: Oooooooo!! Ivan, however, does not let him go! He lifts him higher in the air and faces a turnbuckle. Stanislav roars to the crowd, drops Syxx, and before Syxx even touches the floor, NAILS him with a shortarm clothesline! Syxx is literally knocked through the air, across the ring, and slams into the turnbuckle, crumbling to the mat! Walczak nods and laughs. Doug- That, Nate, was The Iron Curtain that Ivan just did…and well, I hate to admit it….Syxx..he’s a dead man! Nate- This is disgusting!! Security runs down to the ring as Ruslan and Riggs discard the battered remains of Corg and the group holds the five security guards at bay. Stanislav picks up Syxx, his back to the guards. He lifts Syxx in the air with a vertical suplex and releases him! Flashbulbs go off around the arena. Nate: SOMEBODY PUT A STOP TO THIS!!! Stanislav tosses Syxx across the ring, out of the ring, over the communists, and PAST the security guards! Syxx hits side first into the steel grating and doesn’t move! Ivan roars and snaps his fingers….. Steven- STANISLAV JUST THREW RITCHIE SYXX –does math- THIRTY FEET! Nate- He’s mad because…he missed?! Doug- ENOUGH IS ENOUGH!!! Some guards turn to help Syxx and Corg, the others are still held at bay by Wang, The Two Round Faced Women, and Riggs. Ruslan slides into the ring and stands beside Walczak and Stanislav. Ruslan snickers and hands Ivan a microphone… Doug- Look! I don’t want to listen to this guy! Cut the damn feed!!! Ivan stares out across the crowd, as his powerful, booming bass voice rumbles past anything Doug can muster. His thick Russian accent echoes everywhere while the fans and Doug berate him… Ivan- Ivan Stanislav is finally home and that home is among the crumpled bodies of those who oppose me in the OSW ring!!! The crowd is outraged. Nate: THIS CONFIRMS IT!!! IVAN STANISLAV HAS JUMPED SHIP!!! HE HAS LEFT PCW TO COME TO OLD SCHOOL WRESTLING!!! WHAT MUST TYLER NELSON BE THINKING?!?! Doug: Want to know what I am thinking? Nate: This is a family show, so no!!! Ivan growls while Ruslan waves the flag. Walczak stands with his hands crossed over his chest. Ivan then continues. Ivan- Since the beginning of tenure in Primetime Championship Wrestling, Ivan Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan have battled the government of that company! And throughout our time there, we have inflicted damage comparable to anything that Jane Reagan, President of OSW, could muster!! Huge pop for Jane Reagan. Ivan doesn’t even let the fans enjoy a second of it, before continuing. Ivan- And while Ivan Stanislav and Jane Reagan do not see eye to eye, she has decided to sign Ivan Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan to Old School Wrestling contracts!! But furthermore, I have DEMANDED that Speedy Riggs, honorable and excellent in his referee duties, as referee within Old School Wrestling!! Doug- Speedy Riggs doesn’t even know HOW to count to three!! Steven- Sure he does, only when Ivan is pinning someone! Ivan storms around the ring like a caged animal, while his long time associate, Ruslan, continues to wave the flag. Ivan- As well, she has granted the inclusion of Communist Party Member Wang and his Two Round Faced Women, as well as Comrade Walczak!! I have defeated everyone in the PCW, and there was no one else that could stand against me, and therefore I have come here merely to crush some new, young blood! Stanislav laughs loudly as the fans begin to throw objects at Ivan, but he doesn’t care. Ivan- What you see here is already the complete nullification of anything the OSW can do to stop us, and you know why?!? Because the locker room is full of cowards who want to do nothing but avoid Ivan Stanislav!!! Nate: THAT’S A LIE!!! Steven: He scares me…. Ivan scowls out at backstage area and points. Ivan: Are you all so scared of the Russian Bear?!? Does not a single one of you American pigs have the bravery to face the Russian Bear?!? Does no one in Old School… Nate: IVAN STANISLAV WANTS A FIGHT HERE TONIGHT!!! Doug: You know what? I’ve had it. I’ve just had it. Doug throws off his headset, stands up and beckons to the timekeeper. Nate: What’re you doing, Doug? The timekeeper walks over to Doug. Doug says something to the timekeeper, who then gives Doug a microphone. Doug strolls towards the ropes slowly, and raises his microphone. Doug: Hey Yvonne, enough is enough!!! The crowd explodes. Nate: (whispering) What the hell Is he doing?!? Stanislav stares down from the ring with a burning hatred in his eyes. DT shoots him a cocky smirk, then continues. Doug: Now, I realize that you guys in the ring probably wouldn’t hear some of the things I’ve said today until you watched the show later, but I have some stuff that I want to say to you now, right to your faces. One of the reasons that I agreed to sign on to the OSW announcing staff was because Jane told me that she wanted someone big from the PCW Old School to lend some credibility to the federation. Another reason is that OSW reminds me of the feds that I used to work in when I started my career. In short, I wanted to see this fed succeed where the PCW has failed miserably in recent times. During the past few months, the PCW has become a bloated version of itself, completely going with a style over substance approach. It’s no longer about ‘who’s the better wrestler’ in PCW, it’s become all about ‘who can be more political and conniving’, and one of the main causes of that transition is in the ring right now. Doug points at Ivan in the ring while Ivan growls back at the PCW Hall of Famer. Doug: Ivan Stanislav, I have watched you work for months, and quite frankly I’m sick of it. I’m sick of watching you and your cronies run in and ruin perfectly good matches. I’m sick of you running around, yapping about “Russia” and “Capitalists” and “Borchst” or whatever. I’m sick of you bullying everyone around and ruining careers. Remember when you stuck my leg in a folding chair and broke it all those months ago, Ivan? Ivan nods and smiles. DT gives him a disrespecting smirk and head bob in response. Doug: I am glad you do, because rest assure I sure do. You didn’t even have a REASON to do it, but you did it anyway. I thought by getting away from PCW, I could get away from all the crap that you and Tyler Nelson brought upon it. But here you are, poised to bring another federation to ruin. Why? Why do you want to ruin the careers of these kids? These kids are the future of our sport, and here you are throwing them around like rag dolls just because you feel like it? Well, as a great American Sailor once said: “That’s all that I can stands, and I can’t stands no more!” Nate: Uh, did he just quote Popeye? Steven: I’m afraid so… Ivan listens intently in the ring, as DT raises the mic to continue. Doug: Well Ivan, you wanna hurt someone? Ivan nod and cracks his knuckles in the ring. Ruslan continues twave the Russian flag. Doug: Ok then, big fella…Hurt me….IF YOU CAN!!! With that, Doug throws down the microphone and grabs his announcing chair while Ivan, Riggs, Ruslan, Wang and Walczak trash-talk him from the ring. Nate: DOUG, COME TO YOUR SENSES!!! In the ring, the two round-faced women just stand motionless, glaring at Doug. Ivan pantomimes breaking a stick in half to further aggravate Doug. Nate: WE NEED MORE SECURITY OUT HERE!! Doug walks up to the apron with the chair as all three occupants beckon for him to come in to the ring. Steven: I can’t watch!!! Nate: MORE SECURITY!!! NOW!!! Doug pauses while standing on the apron and glares back at them. Slowly, Doug puts one leg through the ropes. Riggs, drunk from the power of having such powerful friends, charges forward. Doug’s ready for this and he introduces the chair to Speedy’s face. The crowd explodes. Doug pulls his other leg through the ropes and comes out swinging as the other Communists descend on him. Chairshot for Wang… The Crowd: OOOOOOoooooooooo!!! Chairshot for Alexei Ruslan! Nate: DOUG TROY IS TAKING IT TO THE RED ARMY!!!! The crowd explodes. The Crowd: DT!!! DT!!! DT!!! DT!!! DT!!! DT!!! Ruslan goes down and rolls out of the ring. Walczak is the next charge In only to also get whacked in the skull with DT’s now, nearly destroyed chair! Steven: LOOK OUT!!! A powerful clothesline from Ivan ends Doug’s rampage, though. Doug hits the ground holding his neck as Ivan and the round-faced womEn start laying the boots to him. The other members of the Red Army take some shots at Doug as they recover. Nate: WHY DID YOU HAVE TO BE A HERO DOUG!!! Steven: It’s the Prison Rule in action, Nate. When it’s six on one, you can bet that the one is gonna be the bitch for the night. In the ring, Walczak gets to his feet picks Doug up and puts his head in between his legs. Steven snickers. Nate: There’s a thin line separating bravery and stupidity. Doug’s finding out which side of the line he’s on right now!!! Walczak drops Doug to the mat via the POLISH POWERBOMB. DT is down and out. Nate: OUR BROADCAST PARTNER, DOUG TROY, IS IN DEFENSELESS IN THE RING AND AT THE MERCY OF IVAN STANISLAV AND THE RED ARMY!!! The crowd boos heavily. Steven: They are gonna kill Doug!!! In the ring, Walczak raises his fists in the air as the crowd’s tune changes from boos to a booming pop! Nate: WHAT THE HELL ARE THEY CHEERING THIS CARNAGE FOR!!! Steven: LOOK!!!! The camera shifts to the ringside area as a lone man sprints towards the ring. Nate: SOMEONE HAS JUST HIT THE RING TO HELP DOUG TROY!!! Steven: WHO IS IT, I CAN’T SEE HIS FACE FROM OUR VANTAGE POINT!!! The man slides into the ring and starts dealing out aluminum justice via his trashcan to the members of the Communist party! Nate: OH MY GOD!!! THAT’S TRASHCAN MAN!!! TRASHCAN MAN IS HERE AND TAKING IT TO THE RED ARMY IN THE RING!!! One by one, like the black ninja of yore, the Communists rush at the hardcore legend, only to get pummeled by the trashcan that Trashcan Man is so proficient at weilding. Wang, both round-faced women, Riggs and Walczak all fall to TCM’s onslaught Nate: TRASHCAN MAN IS CLEANING HOUSE!!! Steven: He’s never cleaned anything his entire life, including himself!!! The only people in the ring are TCM, Ivan and a recovering Doug Troy. TCM and Ivan stare holes at each other, neither one budging. After a long staredown, TCM starts walking toward Ivan. Ivan readies himself for the attack, but in doing do fails to notice that Doug is standing up right behind him. The crowd senses it and begins to pop. TCM stops his advance and smiles right as Doug rears back and kicks Ivan in the crotch! As Ivan roars in pain, and doubles over, and Trashcan Man delivers one…..two…..three crushing blows to ivan’s rock-hard skull, denting the trashcan flat. Nate: TRASHCAN MAN AND DOUG TROY ARE DISMANTLING THE RED ARMY!!! The crowd is going bonkers. Trashcan Man drops his now flat trashcan onto the canvas in front Ivan. Meanwhile, Doug grabs Ivan from behind and starts to strain with effort. Steven: Oh my… Nate: No, he couldn’t… Not with that hurt leg of his… With a final pull, Doug lifts Ivan off the ground and gives him THE TAKE right into the trashcan! Ivan goes down, and slowly rolls out of the ring, to the floor in the ringside area. The crowd explodes. Nate: DOUG TROY JUST NAILED IVAN STANISLAV WITH THE TAKE!!! ALL HELL HAS BROKEN LOOSE!!! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Steven: Nooooooo!!! Nate: IVAN STANISLAV AND TOM WALCZAK ARE HERE IN OSW!!! TRASHCAN MAN AND DOUG TROY ARE BOTH APPARENTLY OUT OF RETIREMENT, AND PREPARED TO MAKE SURE THAT IVAN STANISLAV IS KEPT IN CHECK HERE IN OSW!!! WE ARE OUT OF TIME!!! WE WILL SEE YOU NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT ON SLAM!!! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!!! The camera shifts to the ringside area, Stanislav and the Red Army begin to regroup, and begin jawjacking up at DT and Trashcan Man. In the ring, TCM helps a wobbly Doug to his feet. DT and Trashy lock gazes with Stanislav and Walczak respectively, then they raise each others hands into the air as the crowd roars, as “While the City Sleeps” by MC 900 Ft. Jesus hits the PA system….as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears in the bottom of the center portion of the screen…..and the screen fades to black…..
