AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: Bio Kimbusa was a former Yokozuna Sumo champion in his home nation of Japan. A ferocious warrior, he earned an undefeated record in the art of Sumo. However, controversy arose upon a sudden intensity in Kimbusa's style. His wreckless abandonment of the traditional sumo art, followed immediately by a very suspicious scandal near his home in Japan lead to his total disgrace from the sport. Having lost his livelyhood, Kimbusa fell under the wing of his spiritual guide and mentor, Yoshi Kimawa. Yoshi calmed Kimbusa's rage, and lead him to America to try his hand at a different form of fighting. However, the mystery still remains in Japan concerning the coverup from a terrible event that took place in Kimbusa's home nation only a year ago... A master of martial arts and the artful combat forms of the Orient, Kimbusa is surprisingly numble for weighing in the 500 pound area. A student of spirituality and an enlightened mind, Kimbusa utilizes his distinct size advantage and calm, intelligent approach to the sport, taking an artistic approach to acheive victory. However, something still lurks in his past that he has yet to confront, and it is unknown if his control is complete, or merely superficial.
