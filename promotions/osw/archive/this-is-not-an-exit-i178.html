This is Not an Exit

2002-03-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is Not an Exit Written: March 23, 2002 ...Though, as Walczak just begins to get his momentum back by lifting Rhett up, he notices another gothic figure standing in the entranceway, carrying a baseball bat. Robbie: COOOOOOORG~! Indeed it is. Corg slowly marches down the entranceway, much to the fans’ dismay. They jeer as he slides into the ring. Rhett’s body goes limp and drops when Tom lets him go. Corg slowly lifts the bat over his head! Nate: NO! THIS IS INTERFERENCE! RING THE BELL PIERRE! Robbie: YES! HIT HIM! HIT HIM NOW! Oh, he does hit someone, but certainly not Rhett. Instead, Tom finds himself laying flat on the ground as the baseball bat connects with his chest! Robbie: WHAT~!?! Nate: CORG JUST NAILED TOM WALCZAK WITH THAT BASEBALL BAT!!!! Robbie: NOOOOOO! I THOUGHT CORG WAS ON MY SIDE!! Corg smiles, and simply takes a mic and steps out of the ring. Tom is soon to recover from the hit, and he leans over the rope, taunting Corg. Walczak: Dirty capitalist scum! You thought you pulled one over on me?! The Red Army will not allow! Robbie: Look behind you Walczak! Rhett has slowly regained his feet, and approaches Walczak carefully. Corg spins his finger, motioning for Walczak to turn around. And as he does so… … Black mist blinds Walczak! Robbie: RHETT JUST SPIT BLACK MIST IN WALCZAK’S EYES! THIS IS WRONG! THIS IS AN OUTRAGE! Nate: AND I LOVE IT! Steven: AND I LOVE HIM! Walczak leans against the ropes, trying to wipe away the mist, but he only receives a stiff-kick to his temple, a shot heard all around the arena, and the Polish Punisher falls! 1! 2! 3! Winner and Still International Champion: RHETT LOCKE! Nate: YES! Robbie: NO! Steven: BOCAAAAAA! Corg slowly begins walking up the ramp, speaking into the microphone he retrieved previously. Corg: Why did I do it? Robbie: TRAITOR! Corg: Because if that strap got in the hands of piss-poor communists like Walczak, nobody would ever get a shot at that title. So I thought to myself, “Why not a champion that will put up a god damned fight? And though, I despise you Rhett Locke, I must admit… Corg: You’re one hell of a fighter…but in the end….you will fail at the hands of the Silent Killer…..SOOOOOON!!!! Nate: WHOOOOOAA!! STRONG WORDS FROM CORG!!!! WE NEED TO HEAD TO A COMMERCIAL!!! WHEN WE RETURN, THE IVAN STANILSLAV AND DOUG TROY WILL GO AT IT WITH THE #1 CONTENDERSHIP FOR THE WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE ON THE LINE!!! The crowd jeers Corg as he says those last words and leave, but the jeers turn to cheers once again as “I am Loco” plays over the P.A. once more. The champion has retained his title…..for now.
