Stealing a Match

2002-05-18
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stealing a Match Written: 5-18-02 Somewhere back in the corridors of the Saddledome we move to another camera feed, which picks up Commissioner Ed striding down the hallway, doing something or another. Turning a corner his gaze falls upon the team of Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, Civil Disobedience, coming his way from the opposite end of the hall. Hawke points toward Ed, shouting across the distance seperating them. Trevor: Hey, Ed! You wouldn’t happen to have seen Da Westsidaz running around anywhere, would you? Putting up a hand in defense, Ed shakes his head as Masters and Hawke approach. Ed: Whoa, hold on a second guys. Contrary to what you may believe, I did not write that email that has been circulating around as of late. A confused look appears on Hawke’s face as he and Masters stop about two feet away from Ed. Trevor: What are you talking about? Ed: You know, that email that supposedly has me ordering Riptah and Short Circuit to take you out. Now it’s Masters’ turn to look confused. Seth: What’s an email? Ed: I could have sworn I heard you guys complaining about it the other day. Masters and Hawke’s eyes open wide, and one can almost see the bulb lighting up above their heads. Trevor: Oh wait! I know what you’re talking about. Yeah, that totally slipped my mind. Seth: Are you talking about that letter we were looking at last week? Man, that’s old news. The commissioner looks relieved as he hears their comments. Ed: So you two aren’t suspicious of me at all then, right? Trevor: Oh, we never said that. Civil Disobedience both glare accusingly at Ed, who back away in discomfort. The stares quickly pass however, as Hawke returns to a casual tone. Trevor: But what we originally came to talk to you about was the fact that the tag team title match is only a few minutes away and Da Westsidaz haven’t even shown up at the building yet. Ed: (sighing) Yes, I’m aware of that fact. In fact, I was just on my way to check the parking lot for them. Shifting his eyes back and forth, Masters interjects himself. Seth: Uh...we just came from the parking lot, and we didn’t see anyone. Hawke nods in agreement. Trevor: And if we had seen them you can rest assured we would have given them a stern talking to about the virtue of punctuality. Seth: Really? Why the hell would we stick needles into their neck? Trevor: (sighing) That’s acupuncture, Seth. (turning to Ed) Face it, Ed. Riptah and Short Circuit obviously are not taking this match up seriously. I wouldn’t be surprised if they didn’t show up at all. This type of conduct isn’t a very good way to start your comeback with, you know. Seth: Pretty damn unprofessional too. Trevor: Seriously, though. I know if WE had a big OSW Tag Title match, we’d be here at least two hours early, if not earlier. Ed rolls his eyes. Ed: In my parking spot, more likely than not. Trevor: Well that’s beside the point. Ed: There’s a point to all of this? Trevor: (nodding) Oh, there is. I know watching our promos would make you think to the contrary, but there is definitely a point here. Ed: And are you ever going to tell me? Trevor: Yes. Ed: Well? When? Trevor: Riiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiight now. Ed taps his foot impatiently as he waits for Trevor to speak. Three seconds later, he finally does. Trevor: Ahem...I think you should replace those Westside slackers with a team that actually gives a fuck about their jobs and the OSW title hunt. The fans here in Calgary paid good money to see a tag team title match and I can think of nobody better to give it to them than the trendiest team in the sport today. Ed: Billy and Chuck? Hawke shuts his eyes, wrapping his arms around himself as he shivers in disgust. Trevor: No! God no! I was talking about us, man. So are you going to put us in this match or what? An apprehensive look appears on Ed’s face. Ed: Well... Trevor: Well? Ed: It’s not that you two don’t deserve a shot at the tag titles. You are without a doubt top contenders and deserve a rematch at some point in time. Seth: Like a couple minutes from now, right? Ed shakes his head. Ed: Guys, Heresian Bloodlust didn’t request a match with you two. They wanted a piece of the Westsidaz. Trevor: And the Westsidaz aren’t here! They’re somewhere blowing off this shit right now. Can’t you realize that? Seth: Either that or they’re too fucking scared to step in with the big goth retards. Ed shakes his head once more. Ed: I strongly doubt the second one. Trevor: Look man. Whatever. The match is scheduled to go on any minute now and you have no challengers to the tag team title. Fuck who Bloodlust want to wrestle. Are you going to cancel the match or are you going to put in a team that is truly deserving of this shot. A team that fans want to see. A team that is going to make good and walk away with those belts. Well, what do you say, Ed? It’s us or nothing. Hawke opens his arms up in an offertory gesture, raising his eyebrow and smiling at Ed. He holds this position for five seconds. Ten seconds. Fifteen seconds. He looks to be tiring of having his arms raised at this point. Ed gives him a look of disbelief, shakes his head, and then answers. Ed: (sighing) Despite all the egotistical babble, you do have made some good points. I’ll tell you what. If Da Westsidaz haven’t shown up by time introductions begin, I’ll give you guys the shot. Is that good enough? Trevor: Ed, considering we know for a fact that those ungrateful bastards aren’t going to show up, that offer is great. Hawke and Masters each take turns shaking Ed’s hand excitedly. Ed: Now if you’ll excuse me, I have things to do. Being a commissioner isn’t all sunshine and lollipops you know. Hawke and Masters each step to either side of the commissioner, allowing him to pass in between them. Trevor: Go right ahead, Ed. We won’t hold you up any further. As Ed disappears down the hallway, Hawke and Masters turn to each other with huge grins on their faces, nodding in satisfaction at each other. Seth and Trevor: Primo.
