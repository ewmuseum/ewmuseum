Switchblade (w/ Frankie Bones, Drew Hostyle) -vs- Timo/Aimz

2002-03-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Switchblade (w/ Frankie Bones, Drew Hostyle) -vs- Timo/Aimz Written: March 23, 2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam! The crowd is still buzzing not only from the impromptu appearances by Orlando natives, Kid Wonder and Boda a moment ago, but because the OSW World Title is about to be on the line, in a handicap match nonetheless, as the Champion, Sebastian Toomes, will defend the title against Timo Bolamba and Aimz. Robbie: Seb-ass reeks of confidence. They might as well just call the match before it begins, because he will not be beaten by that coconut picking, islander and his whore of a girlfriend. Nate: On that note, let’s send it up to Earl Henrich in the ring. Earl: Introducing first, the referee for tonight's Main Event, Betty Lovejoy! Betty Lovejoy, her eyes bright with excitement and anticipation, comes through the curtain and totters down the ramp towards the ring. Earl holds the ropes open for her as she climbs through them with extra care. Robbie Decker: Ooh my, Stacey Keibler eat your heart out! Nate Dumas: Blonde, high heels, tight shorts and some hot wax and you are into any bit of fluff that walks on by, eh Decker? Robbie Decker: (eyes locked on Lovejoy's gear) Sorry, I missed that... Nate Dumas: Hey Decker, you hear that she comes from Intercourse? Steven: This is where I finally get to slap one of you across the back of the head, right? 'Get to the Gone' sounds and OSW President Jane Reagan steps through the curtain. She reaches back and holds it apart, allowing OSW Heavyweight Champion Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes to step through. Toomes reaches over and draws Reagan towards him, causing the crowd to voice their immense displeasure, which amplifies in volume as the two share a passionate kiss on the top of the ramp before sauntering arm-in-arm down the ramp towards the ring. Earl: On his way to the ring at this time, standing six foot two and weighing two hundred and forty-five pounds, being led to the ring by Jane Reagan, he is the current and first ever Old School Wrestling Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes! The crowd's 'anti-pop' continues until 'I'm A Cloud' by Boy Hits Car sounds and Amy Campbell, aka Aimz, steps through the curtain and gyrates her way in time to the music down to ringside. Earl: Making her way to the ring for this special handicap match, standing at 5 feet 8 inches tall and weighing in at 143 pounds, Amy Campbell! Nate: These fans love Aimz!!!! Robbie: She’s not a natural red head. Nate: How would you know? Robbie: Everyone has seen the fur around that hamlined taco, and it ain’t red, cuz. There is dead air for 1…..2…..3…..4…..5 seconds before…. 'Defy You' by the Offspring sounds, heralding iminent the entrance of Timo Bolamba, the #1 contender for the OSW Heavyweight Title. The curtain parts for the wild Samoan to burst through. He charges full steam straight towards the ring, slapping a few palms along the way. Earl: On his way to the ring at this time, standing six foot two and weighing two hundred and forty-five pounds, he is the Samoan Silencer, a multiple time former PCW champion, the number 1 contender to the OSW Heavyweight Title...TIIIIIIMO BOLAAAAAAAAAAMBAAAAAAAAAA! Steven: (wiping a single tear from his eye) Guys, don't you think the way he goes 'AaaaaaaaaAAAAAAH!' when saying 'TIIIIIIMO BOLAAAAAAAAAAMBAAAAAAAAAA!' is just simply breathtaking? Robbie: Me jabbing a ball point pen through your windpipe will be breath taking. Timo joins with Aimz at the base of the ringsteps, and they both look into the ring towards Switchblade, who is leaning nonchelantly back in the opposite corner, buffing the OSW Heavyweight Title belt with his left wristband. Timo and Aimz both head up the stairs towards the ring, bumping shoulders with each other. Rather than argue over who gets to go in first, they play a single game of Rock/Paper/Scissors, which Timo wins. Aimz shrugs and gets up onto the apron, while Timo puts a leg through the ropes and starts to enter the ring. Toomes: (taking the mic from Earl) Not so fast... Hold on there... HEY! No Bananas for you to pick here yet boy. Nate: OH COME ON!!! Timo ignores Toomes' taunts and enters the ring, heading straight for him. Toomes begs him off with open hands, causing Timo to slow his advance, and speaks again. Toomes: Before you decide to violate the conditions of your green card and lay a hand on me, I suggest you hear what Jane here has to say... Jane: (taking the mic from Switchblade) Thank you Sebastian. Toomes: No, thank you Jane. Jane: Now, as we know, our *coughs* esteemed commissioner, Ed, booked this match as a handicap match. I'd like to introduce the heavyweight champion's partners for this evening! The curtains part and the duo of Frankie Bones and Drew Hostyle charge down the ramp towards the ring. Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS THIS?!?!? Robbie: SWITCHBLADE NEVER SAID HE WANTED A TWO ON ONE HANDICAP MATCH!!!! Jane Reagan clears the ring. Timo Bolamba briefly looks over his shoulder towards Hostyle & Bones and is clocked over the head by Toomes' Heavyweight Title Belt for his trouble. Hostyle and Bones each grab one of Aimz' ankles and yank her down to ringside and start pounding on her with clubbing fists. Nate: THIS IS BULL(expletive)!! THAT WHORE HAS GONE TOO FAR!!! The crowd is equally outraged. Timo, holding his head, rolls over towards the bottom rope, then starts to pull himself to his feet. The crowd pop turns to a chorus of boos as Toomes pounds on the rising Samoan with lefts and rights. Robbie: THIS IS GREAT!!! Outside the ring, Aimz is whipped shoulder first into the ringsteps, folding her like an accordian, the impact jarring them into the ringpost with a loud crash. The Crowd: Ooooooooooo!!! Nate: OH COME ON!!!! THIS MATCH HASN’T EVEN BEGUN YET!!!! Back inside the ring, Timo finally shrugs off Toomes attack and gathers himself to go on the offensive. Toomes backs into the corner and calls to Hostyle, who leaves Aimz, still in a heap on the floor, at the mercy of Bones and leaps onto the apron for the tag. Bones, almost as an afterthought, turns towards the timekeeper and orders that the bell be rung to start the match. The terrified timekeeper refuses to follow orders from Bones, and points over at referee Betty Lovejoy, who is standing in the ring stunned, not knowing which way to look or where to start officiating. Bones' patience wears thin and he reaches out a meaty paw, snatches the bell and slams it over the timekeeper's head, the ringing noise enough to start the matchclock and focus Betty Lovejoy's attention on the two men in the ring - Sebastian Toomes and Timo Bolamba. Nate: THIS IS THE BIGGEST TRAVESTY I HAVE SEEN IN ALL MY DAYS OF WRESTLING!!!!!!!! Tag - Switchblade out, Hostyle in, Bolamba all over Hostyle. Hostyle ducks a flurry of punches and locks Timo into a side headlock but the wily Samoan takes Hostyle over with a back suplex, sending the air out of Hostyle's lungs with an audible whoosh. The crowd explodes. Nate: TIMO WILL NOT BE DENIED!!!! Timo bounces off the ropes and drops across Drew Hostyle's forehead with a shin, off the ropes again and this time connects with an elbow, causing Hostyle to clutch his head and roll over, kicking the canvas in pain. Nate: TIMO IS ON FIRE!!!! Steven: I’LL SAY!!!! Drew Hostyle gets to his feet but before he can mount any offense is sent to the ropes and taken down again with a drop toe hold. Timo pumps the air to a huge pop from the crowd, then bounces off the ropes and lands a leg drop across the back of Hostyle's neck. In the corner, Sebastian Toomes stomps his feet and bangs his head on the top turnbuckle in frustration. Nate: TIMO IS LOOKING TO END THIS THING EARLY!!!! Robbie: HE’S GOTTA PIN SWITCHBLADE TO WIN THE TITLE, NOT BONES OR HOSTYLE!!!! Outside the ring, Frankie Bones makes to decapitate Aimz with a hades lariat, but Aimz somehow ducks and with a high dropkick to the back of his head sends Bones over the guard rail and into the crowd. Aimz leaps up onto the apron and holds her hand out for the hot tag. The crowd explodes. Nate: HERE COMES AMY CAMPBELL!!!! Timo points at Aimz, her arm hanging out for the tag, and the crowd go nuts. Timo slaps Aimz palm, and Aimz enters the ring, ducking a clothesline from Sebastian Toomes who has neatly stepped around referee Betty Lovejoy, and hoes into the rising Drew Hostyle with a flurry of punches and forearms. Despite this, Hostyle gets to his feet. Aimz reaches out and whips Hostyle to the ropes and quicker than lightning takes him over with a hurricanrana. Aimz backflips to her feet, pumps herself up, then rushes towards Toomes corner and knocks him face first off the apron into the guardrail. Aimz bounces off the ropes again but is brought up short by the slithering arm of Frankie Bones coming in under the bottom rope and tripping her up. Nate: COME ON BETTY, DO YOU DAMN JOB!!! Robbie: She gives good jobs each and every time!!!! Drew Hostyle gets to his feet again, a seriously pissed off look on his face. Seeing Aimz lying motionless face down, his look changes from pissed off to sadistic, and he yanks Aimz to her feet by the hair. Nate: Does he think he’s a tough guy?!?! She’s all of 140 lbs!!! Hostyle ignores the protests of referee Betty Lovejoy to let go of the hair as he reels Aimz in and plants her with a Last Breath powerbomb before Lovejoy can reach the mandatory 5 count. Aimz clutches her belly, fighting for air. With a few well placed kicks that send Aimz body a few feet off the mat with each blow, Hostyle positions Aimz' head under the bottom rope, then springs up to the top rope and lands Mo' Money (Gullotine Leg Drop) across Aimz exposed neck. He then leaps to his feet and poses for the crowd. Nate: This is a travesty!!! Look at Bones celebrating like he’s won the superbowl!!! What a jackass!!! Hostyle tags in Frankie Bones, who sneers over at the enraged Timo Bolamba momentarily before picking up the almost comatose Aimz and lining her up for a suplex. Aimz up.................. Aimz DOWN! A cruel smile crosses Bones' face as he picks up Aimz and snap suplexes her over five times in quick succession. Bones covers Aimz and Betty Lovejoy drops to make the count... 1......... 2......... Bones pulls Aimz up by the hair, then to her feet, then throws her to the corner, following up with a Trouble Past spear, then tags in the Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes. Nate: (solemnly) I think this match is over. Aimz can’t take anymore, and she is not moving after being hit with that nasty spear. Toomes climbs through the ropes and immediately flips off Timo, who is going ballistic in his corner trying to inspire Aimz into some form of movement. Toomes poses to the crowd, completely ignoring the crumpled Aimz. Timo, enraged, comes through the ropes and attacks Toomes, but is sent back to his corner by Lovejoy, allowing Hostyle and Bones to pull Aimz up by the hair to her feet and hold her out to Toomes. Toomes continues to taunt Timo, who is being held in the corner by Lovejoy. Bones and Hostyle let go of Aimz hair, allowing her to stagger out of the corner and try to make the tag. Nate: Oh man, Aimz is out on her feet. Robbie: Welcome to the big leagues, Miss Campbell. Toomes sees the dazed Aimz wandering past him towards Timo, and allows her to get one foot away from Timo's outstretched arm before yanking her backward by the hair, sending her crashing to the mat, butt first. The crowd boos. Nate sighs in disgust. Betty Lovejoy again lectures Toomes on the hair pulling, allowing Timo to encourage Aimz to try to get to her feet. The loud roar from the crowd sees Aimz to her knees, and she starts to crawl, but in the wrong direction. Toomes ignoring her protests, shoves Betty Lovejoy aside and reaches for Aimz, but Aimz crawls between his legs and launches herself through the air and lands a tag on Timo's outstretched arm! The crowd explodes. Nate: TAG OUT TO TIMO!!! TAG OUT TO TIMO!!!! Robbie: WHAT?!?! THAT WASN’T A CLEAN TAG!!! Timo comes into the ring like a house of fire, sending Switchblade to the mat with a flurry of lefts and rights. Timo ducks a lariat by Frankie Bones and dropkicks Drew Hostyle outside the ring, then turns and hits Bones with a German Suplex which sends him rolling out the ring after Hostyle. Nate: TIMO JUST TOOK OUT BONES AND HOSTYLE!!! NOW ITS SWITCHBLADE’S TURN TO FEEL THE PAIN!!! Timo turns towards Toomes but is greeted by a boot to the face. The Crowd: Ooooooooo!!! Timo goes down hard, but gets to his feet immediately and with presence of mind ducks another boot of similar force. Timo and Toomes turn at the same time, but Timo is the quicker of the two and lands the Cave In on Toomes, sending him gasping to the mat. Robbie: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! Nate: THE CAVE IN!!!! THE CAVE IN!!! TOOMES IS DOWN!!!! Timo climbs to the top rope and signals for the Fist of the Heavens. Nate: TIMO IS UP TOP!!! Robbie: GET UP SEA-BASS!!!! Nate: WE COULD HAVE A NEW CHAMPION!!! RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!!! Jane Reagan jumps up onto the apron in order to dislodge Timo, but is in turn taken out by the recovering Aimz. Both women catfight up the aisle, clawing and screeching at each other, much to the delight of the crowd. In the ring, Timo launches himself into the air and lands FIST FROM THE HEAVENS on Toomes! Timo covers Switchblade and calls for Lovejoy to make the count. The crowd explodes. Nate: WE ARE GOING TO HAVE A NEW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!! Betty Lovejoy drops awkwardly to her knees and starts counting out Toomes.... 1...... 2..... .Betty Lovejoy is yanked from position by Drew Hostyle before being able to make the third count, and at the same time Frankie Bones drops an elbow across the back of Timo's head. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!! Betty Lovejoy gets to her feet and calls for the bell. Nate: BETTY JUST DISQUALIFIED SWITCHBLADE!!!! TIMO WINS!!! TIMO WINS!!!! Robbie: Heh…Unless the rules have changed, the title is not changing hands on a Disqualification. Hostyle and Bones help the wheezing Toomes to his feet and all three men start stomping on Timo Bolamba. The crowd is outraged. So is Nate. Nate: THIS IS JUST MORE CRAP FROM A CRAP WEASEL OF A CHAMPION!!! THIS IS A JOKE!!!! Outside the ring, Aimz is taking it to Jane Reagan with punches and kicks. Suddenly, out of a moment of desperation, Jane rakes Aimz with her nails, right in the face, momentarily blinding her. Aimz falls back, but Jane wastes no time, suddenly producing a pair of handcuffs. Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON?!?! Robbie: Bondage?!?! Jane wields the cuffs like and old pro, handcuffing Aimz to a nearby guardrail. Aimz screams out in frustration, kicking and screaming at Jane, who gives the camera her trademark thumbs up signal. Nate: This is a travesty…. In the ring, Toomes makes a breaking motion with his hands and points down at Timo, upon which Bones and Hostyle pick him up, set him up and then drop him hard in a brutal double neck breaker. Timo offers no resistance while the three men continue to perform various combination moves on him designed to damage as many moveable body parts as possible. The crowd is enraged. Police and security officials are shown stopping several fans from jumping the guardrails. Debris is peppering the ring. Nate: 3B AND SWITCHBLADE ARE DISMANTLING TIMO BOLAMBA IN THE RING!!! THIS IS DISGUSTING!!!! Jane Reagan re-enters the ring, upon which the camera pans up the rampway and spies Aimz handcuffed to a guardrail, unable to mount any kind of rescue attempt for her partner Timo Bolamba. Reagan flies into Toomes arms at the exact moment the match result is announced... Earl: Your winner, as a result of a disqualification, the team of Aimz and the Samoan Silencer, Timo Bolamba! Still OSW Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes! The crowd who hadn’t already realized the ultimate outcome, now do, causing more debris and trash to be hurled at the ring in outrage. Upon hearing that he had retained the Heavyweight Title, Toomes celebrates by picking up Timo's legs and locking him in the Controlled Burn while Hostyle, Bones and Jane Reagan celebrate around him. Nate: SOMEONE!!! ANYONE STOP THIS!!!! At that precise moment, “Food for Brain” hits the PA system, bringing a huge pop from the crowd. Steven: LOOOOK!!! The camera shifts to the entryway as OSW Commissioner Ed bursts through the curtain weilding his trusty companion, Bob the barbed-wire wrapped nail embedded, wooden chair. Nate: ED IS ON HIS WAY OUT HERE WITH BOB IN HAND!!!! Ed rushes down the ramp to try to save Timo Bolamba, but suddenly, without warning, a huge explosion of pyrotechnics shoots off from the rampway, engulfing Ed in flames. Nate: GOOOOD GAWD!!!! Ed remembers Dick Van Dyke’s message, and stops, drops and rolls to put out the flames. Bob, however, can not stop, drop and roll, and just begins a slow burn towards ash. Nate: ED IS ON FIRE!!! GOOD GAWD!!! SOMEONE HELP HIM!!! Robbie: If “It” is fire, then Ed certainly has it!!!! Paramedics rush from the back and use fire extinguishers to douse the flames on Ed, but Bob is not so lucky…quickly, not taking any chances for another sudden pyrotechnic “malfunction” the paramedics quickly carry Ed to the back. In the ring, however, the carnage continues, with boot, after boot, after boot, pounding down on the unmoving Timo Bolamba. Nate: THIS IS OUT OF CONTROL!! THE PYROTECHNICS JUST WENT ON THE FRITZ AND SERIOUSLY INJURED THE OSW COMMISSIONER!!! SECURITY IS TOO BUSY HOLDING BACK THE ANGRY FANS, BUT IN TURN THEY CAN NOT HELP TIMO!!!! Robbie: Let him die…No one likes a Samoan anyway. Nate: SOMEONE HAS TO SAVE HIM!!!! Suddenly, the Russian National Anthem hits the PA system. Robbie, Steven and Nate: (simultaneously, surprised) WHAT?!?!?! The cameras shift to the still smoldering entryway as members of the Red Army, Ivan Stanislav, Michel Krieg, Tom Walczak, along with Tempest, dash down the aisle, right past the helpless Aimz. The crowd is confused, but LOUD. Nate: THE RED ARMY IS HEADING TO THE RING!!!! The Red Army hits the ringside area and the new #1 Contender, The Russian Bear Ivan Stanislav heads straight for the OSW Heavyweight Champion, forcing him to release the hold on Timo Bolamba. Nate: IVAN IS GOING RIGHT FOR TOOMES!!!! Toomes quickly exits the ring, grabs the Heavyweight Title Belt from the timekeeper then makes off through the crowd. Nate: TOOMES IS RUNNING SCARED!!!! The crowd is too confused to get a good read on the loud, and excited noise coming from them as a mix of confusion, cheers and boos. Back in the ring, Walczak and Krieg throw Bones and Hostyle over the top ropes, to the floor below. Nate: THE RED ARMY HAS NOT ONLY JUST DRIVEN OFF TOOMES AND 3B, BUT THEY JUST SAVED TIMO BOLAMBA FROM FURTHER HARM!!!! Robbie: I AM RESERVING COMMENT UNTIL I HEAR EXPLAINATIONS!!! NATE: I AM UNSURE OF WHAT TO MAKE OF THIS!!?!?! WE ARE ABOUT TO LOSE OUR FEED!!! Steven: LOOOOOOOK!!!! Suddenly, the crowd’s confusion disappears into pure pop, as the camera shifts to the ring stalking Jane Reagan in the ring. Jane backs away and begs off, and tries to run, but runs right into the chest of Ivan Stanislav. Robbie: SECURITY!!! THE BOSS IS TRAPPED!!!! Jane turns away from Ivan to run again, but turns face first into a flying Tempest, who nails her with the flying headscissors-into-face buster combo. Jane is down and out. Nate: TEMPEST JUST NAILED JANE REAGAN WITH THE SYSTEMS CRASH!!!! GOOD NIGHT SWEETHEART!!!! The crowd explodes. The Crowd: SHE GOT SCHOOLED!!! SHE GOT SCHOOLED!!! SHE GOT SCHOOLED!!! SHE GOT SCHOOLED!!! Nate: WE ARE ABSOLUTELY OUT OF TIME!!! WE WILL HOPEFULLY SORT ALL OF THIS OUT DURING THE WEEK AND HAVE SOME EXPLAINATIONS NEXT SATURDAY NIGHT ON SLAM!!! GOOD NIGHT EVERYONE!!!! The crowd continues to chat, as the Russian National Anthem once again hits the PA system. The camera heads back to the ring, which the Red Army patrol with authority. Walczak and Krieg climb the turnbuckles and hold their arms up high in triumph, while Tempest smiles and jumps into Ivan’s massive arms in the ring. Ivan shows no signs of joy however, just steely determination. His jaw tightens as his eyes go narrow while scanning the crowd for Sebastian Toomes…..and the OSW World Heavyweight Title belt that Ivan so desires…..as the © 2002 Old School Wrestling logo appears in the lower, central portion of the screen….as the scene fades to black.
