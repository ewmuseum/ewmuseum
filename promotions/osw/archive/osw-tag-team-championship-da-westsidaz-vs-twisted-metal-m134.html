OSW Tag Team Championship: Da Westsidaz vs. Twisted Metal

2002-07-06
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OSW Tag Team Championship: Da Westsidaz vs. Twisted Metal Written: July 6th, 2002 Da Westsidaz vs. Twisted Metal “Jesus Built My Hotrod” hits the PA system as Slam returns from commercial. Robbie: TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH! Gunmetal and Chico roll out onto the stage to an indifferent crowd reaction. Nate: Welcome back to Slam!, folks, and Da Westsidaz will be defending their Tag Team Championship belts against Twisted Metal! Robbie: Hopefully, these two will put an end to the inferior human beings that are Riptah and Short Circuit… ALL HAIL TECHNOLOGY! Nate: Hermmm… I guess that’s Robbie’s obligatory Heel reinforcement. Robbie: Damn right. Gunmetal and Chico come to the ring and begin milling around listlessly. Nate: Some people don’t seem too excited to be getting a Tag Team Title match! Robbie: Not ‘people’, you inferior genetic mishap! “Ante-Up” remix hits and Da Westsidaz come out with Daisy, holding up their OSW Tag Team Championship belts, as the crowd goes nuts. Nate: TULSA LOVES THE TAG TEAM CHAMPS! Robbie: Meh. Probably the only black people they’ve ever seen. Steven: I remember the first time I saw a black… Robbie: Stop that. Riptah and Short Circuit make their way to the ring, Chico going to the center to meet Riptah. Gunmetal dutifully drops to the outside and begins whirring his way around to Short Circuit’s corner. DING! DING! DING! Riptah charges Chico, who begins spinning his treads backwards in order to escape, but Riptah slams into the hapless robot, who falls over onto its side. Riptah roars and applies a…a… a sleeper hold. Robbie: What the fuck? Riptah cranks down on the robot’s neck, as Chico politely taps his finger units on Riptah’s arms. Chico: Pardon me, Riptah? Riptah grunts. Chico: Riptah? Riptah: WHAT? Chico: You’re applying a sleeper hold to a robot. Since I do not operate on oxygen, your efforts of cutting off the blood flow to my brain seem to be in vain. Riptah continues to administer the hold for a few seconds until it registers, then utters an expletive. The big man picks Chico back up… Chico: Much obliged! And Riptah gets a taser shot right in the mid-section! Chico rolls his treads up onto Riptah and executes the Lowrider! Nate: Riptah is in trouble! Robbie: Sleeper hold…huh huh… Chico stays on top for the cover…1…2… And the shoulder up by Riptah! Chico rolls over to the corner, but Gunmetal isn’t there! Meanwhile, Gunmetal and Short Circuit are battling it out on the outside, Short Circuit with a sledgehammer, but Gunmetal is rolling around too fast for the smaller Tag Team Champ to catch him. Finally, Daisy runs in front of Gunmetal, her hand outstretched. Daisy: STOP! Gunmetal tilts his head quizzically. Gunmetal: Yes? WHAM! Short Circuit BURIES the sledgehammer into Gunmetal’s head, destroying it. Nate: DAMN! Short Circuit literally DESTROYED Gunmetal! Robbie: Sleeper hold…huh huh huh huh… Nate: Oh shut up. Meanwhile, back in the ring, Chico begins looking around for Gunmetal. Chico: Hey, Gunmetal! Where are ya? I’m looking to make a tag here! Cut back to Gunmetal, with Short Circuit and Daisy dancing triumphantly over his sputtering remains. Chico: Where could he be…? Suddenly, Riptah lifts Chico by the neck and hefts the 400 pound robot OVER HIS HEAD! Nate: Whoa. Robbie: Those big bla.. Nate: ALL RIGHT!! ENOUGH ALREADY!!! Straining, Riptah DVD’s Chico into the mat! After the robot hits the ground, Riptah kneels down, taking a moment to catch his breath before making the cover…1…2… Nate: LOOK! Out of no where, ½ of Civil Disobedience, Trevor Hawke runs in and grabs the referee’s hand! Seth Masters runs in behind the annoyed Riptah and administers the Low Blow on him! Riptah topples over as the bell is rung. Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS CIVIL DISOBEDIANCE DOING OUT HERE?! Robbie: Saving this match! Short Circuit slides in the ring, but is promptly drug back out onto the apron by Sebastian Toomes, who grabs Short Circuit by the shirt and throws him to the ground. Daisy is spun around by OSW owner Jane Reagan, and given a harsh DDT on the ramp! Nate: SHE LEARNED THAT FROM HER HUSBAND!!!! Robbie: NOW SHE ONLY LEARNS FROM TOOMES!!!! Nate: WHOOOOOORE!!! WHOOOOOOOORE!!!! The crowd boos, loud and long as Toomes begins pummeling Short Circuit. Inside the ring, Chico is tossed out and hits the barricade, smashing into bits. Riptah is lying on the ground in a heap. After slamming Short Circuit’s head into the steel steps, Toomes gets into the ring, followed by Jane, Seth hands him a microphone.
