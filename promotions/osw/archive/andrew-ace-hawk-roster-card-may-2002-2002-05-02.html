Andrew Ace Hawk Real Name: Andrew Hawk Birthdate: May 2nd 1978 Hometown: Hollywood, CA Height: 6'6' Weight: 275 lbs. Nicknames: Ace, The Ace of Wrestling, the Living Legend Status: Face Manager(s): Debbie Carmichael Current Teams: none Entrance Music: 'Born to be Wild', by Steppenwolf Information Wrestling Style: Technician, Submissionist, Aerial Signature Moves: Sky Fall: Buff's Blockbuster, german suplex, boston crab, STF, sharpshooter, spinning heel kick, belly-to-back superplex, superkick, big boot to the face, sleeper ddt Set Up Move: Death Dash (Punch to abs, run to the ropes, comes back in high-flying Bulldog ) Finisher: Harley Grip (Modified Camel Clutch) OSW Title History: none so far OSW Award History: none so far Other Federations: CCW, FWF, ASW Other Federation Awards: CCW Heavyweight Champion (2x), CCW Trans-Atlantic Champion (3x), CCW Independent Champion (1x), ASW Heavyweight Champion, FWF Hardcore Champion, 2000 CCW Heavyweight Wrestler of the year Handler Information Handler Name: Andre N. AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: none Bio Life Outside the ring Andrew 'Ace' Hawk is a brazilian by birth. Son of a wrestling champion and a model, Andrew was the oldest of two twin babies that were separated when the other brother supposedly died after the birth. Andrew lived, though, and went home, a nice, big home in Copacabana beach. His childhood was great, as his family had everything, thanks to Ace's father, a very successful brazilian wrestler, a legend in Brazil itself. Brazilian Champion for 13 years, Ace's father was a master of the squared circle. He was a master on submission, always finishing his opponents with simple but very effective moves. His success began reaching some international recognition when he began fighting in Japanese federations and being very successful against the high-flying enduring japanese. Their family had everything: Fame, money, comfort, but that was all about to change... A crooked booker, who wanted to make his own federation the biggest in Brazil, and make its champion the Brazilian champion, challenged Ace's father to a title-unifying match. Ace's father reluctanctly accepted the match, even though he knew, deep down himself, that it'd be hard to have a fair matchup against his opponent, Death Mask, the other Brazilian Champ. Ace, still a young slim 16 year-old, went to see his father, in what he began marketing as his last match. Even though people thought he might not be telling the truth and come back, destiny made it be true. The match day came, and Brazil stopped. More than 30 million people, the largest wrestling audience of all time in Brazil for any wrestling event, turned into the biggest TV channel in Brazil to watch the match. The Maracana Arena was jam-packed with more than 25000 fans. The match came, and it was hard fought, but near the end of the match, something went wrong. Death Mask, supposed to go slow on Ace's father by this time of the match, began hitting him with hard punches and kickes, and the match suddenly became a brawl. The crooked rival promoter slid a chair to Death Mask, who grabbed Ace's father up and gave him a nasty piledriver on the chair, breaking Ace's father neck and finishing his career. Three taps to the mat later, and Death mask would become the new Undisputed Brazilian Champion. Furious about it, Ace decided to challenge Death Mask, a few months later. With almost no wrestling knowledge himself, Ace got severely beaten by Death Mask, who continued as the Brazilian Champ. Things at Ace's house weren't great either. Unable to provide his family's sustain, Ace's family began losing everything they had, until living in friend's houses, with no pride or money. Ace's mother and brothers began working to pay for the bills, but Ace had only one thing on his head: Revenge! A few more months, Ace would meet the man who'd help him change his fortune: Daniel Couto. Daniel was a good friend of Ace's father and also a wrestler in Brazil, who couldn't beat Death Mask. Daniel took Ace, knowing he wanted Death Mask, and began training the kid. Working 16 hours a day, with extensive work schedules, Ace would get fit and ready to face Death Mask once again. This time, he'd do it for his father and for himself. Ace challenged Death Mask, and a $100.000 dollars check was put at stakes by the promoter. The match day came, and the shock was inevitable. After the enduring practices, Ace destroyed Death Mask, running the promoter out of business, and became the Undisputed Brazilian Champion. Now, Ace had given his family something to hang on, but he had to help them in a weekly basis. That's when he decided to move to the United States and fight there. It'd be the only way to help his family again. After the flight, Ace bought a Harley Davidson and began touring the US, trying to find any indy cards open he could to get some money and send it to his family back in Brazil... Enter CCW In one of those rides, Ace found CCW, in Birmingham, Alabama. He decided to join it, and he decided to hide his origin, so he wouldn't give a chance for others to hold him back. Ace fast became a star in CCW, and the money flow helped him and his family a lot. Unfortunately, destiny played a trick with Ace again, as he lost his entire family in a matter of months. First his father, who gave up after several years of suffering. Then, his mother and brothers died in a plane crash back in Cancun. Ace had no family. His only friends were Johnny Atlas, his faithful bodyguard, and Deborah Carmichael, his girlfriend. Ace thought he had no family anymore, but that wouldn't be true. In the end of 2000, a masked man cost Ace the CCW World Title, during the infamous 5-tier cage match. After several attacks, one kidnapping and one career-ending injury inflicted to Johnny, the man revealed himself as Alex Hawk, Ace's lost twin brother, supposed to be dead. The truth was that a poor mother that had lost his newly-born baby made the switch to Alex, giving her dead baby to Ace's parents. Alex, jealous of his brother's success, began a massive campaign against Ace, but eventually, Ace fought him and beat him in a match inside Ace's home. In 2001, after almost a year in CCW, Ace left the company, going straight to the FWF. Ace fought in the FWF for a couple of months, having some success. He became the FWF Hardcore Champion, but after an offer from CCW to come back, Ace decided to leave it. During Ace's first period in CCW, he fought in several other promotions, including ASW, XWA and UWF, having a great deal of success on them. The retirement and the OSW After coming back a third time, in late 2001, Ace thought he had come home, but differences with management made Ace decide it was time to call it quits. He retired from CCW, going to fight on the GTT Tournament, in PTC. After a few weeks, the OSW management gave him a serious look and sent Ace a letter of intent, for an OSW contract. Ace first didn't consider it, but after a few days of close thinking, Ace decided otherwise, signing the contract and becoming an OSWer.
