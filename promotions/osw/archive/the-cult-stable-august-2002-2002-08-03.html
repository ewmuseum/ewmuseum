The Cult — stable (August 2002)

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Cult Members: Hessian , Vampir Nosferatu , Minion the Imp , Bethany Yamato , Sepiroth Du Luc Status: Heel Combined Weight: 1280lbs Signature Moves: Torture Time - Vampir executes and locks the opponent in the Cuban Uprising. Hessian grabs opponent in the Camel Clutch while Vampir has Boston Crab locked in, and Bethany or SDL then baseball slides opponent in the face. Hell On Earth - Double Shoulderbreaker from Hessian and Vampir, with a Leg Drop from Bethany or SDL. Cross'd Out - Vampir and Hessian hold the opponent for a double suplex, but as they drop him, Bethany or SDL comes off of the top rope, dropkicking the opponent's back for an Inverted Brainbuster. Beware! - Vampir spears opponent in the stomach, while Hessian kicks them in the back of head, and as the opponent goes forward, Bethany or SDL connects with a springboard dropkick to the face as the opponent drops to the mat. Slow And Painful - Vampir has the opponent locked in a Boston Crab, while Hessian holds the opponent in an elevated Camel Clutch, and Bethany or SDL drives a missile dropkick to the opponent’s face. Poisonous Sensation - Vampir holds opponent in a Full Nelson as Bethany or SDL low blows the opponent. Vampir then executes a Release Dragon Suplex and Hessian plants an elbow in their stomach in mid air, driving them to the mat. Death In the Afterlife - Vampir holds the opponent in the powerbomb position near the corner. Bethany or SDL leaps off of the top rope, and catches opponent in an Inverted Bulldog, helping Vampir drive the opponent spine-first across Hessian's awaiting knee. BreakFace - Hessian and Vampir have opponent on their knees. The Cult then place a boot to either side of the opponents head, as Bethany or SDL dropkicks them in the face. Spinal Trap - Vampir goes to slingshot the opponent from ground, but as the opponent rises, Hessian clotheslines them, bending them back over Vampir's knees. Bethany or SDL then lands a Senton Bomb on opponent while Vampir still holds them. Jesus Can’t Save You Now - Vampir and Hessian execute the Double Powerbomb, but then lift opponent back up into Powerbomb position, as Bethany or SDL hits an Inverted Bulldog, driving down in a Triple Powerbomb. Nailing Jesus - Hessian and Vampir hold the opponent in double suplex as Bethany or SDL then nails the cross body as they fall. Set Up Move: Judgement Passed - The opponent is in corner – Vampir charges with clothesline, followed by Hessian charging with splash and finally Bethany or SDL charges with a knee to the groin, and the opponent staggers out and caught for the Deadheaded. Finishing Move: Deadheaded - Hessian and Vampir and one of the Cultists deliver a Triple Brainbuster to the two opponents, as Bethany and SDL jumps from the top rope and spike the opponents. Bio The Cult is here. Hessian, Vampir Nosferatu, Minion the Imp, Bethany Yamato, Sepiroth Du Luc and a Cultist are at work in OSW. Their history is as evident as the blood stains on the mat, and their path of destruction is as prominent as the nipples through Aimz' clothing. The Cult is the new, the old, the everything, and if you underestimate them, you'll find yourself trying to claw your way through a piece of wood and 6ft of dirt back to your life of misery and mistakes. Sleep tight, or don't sleep at all, either way, if you're not watching, The Cult will take you... Members Quotes
