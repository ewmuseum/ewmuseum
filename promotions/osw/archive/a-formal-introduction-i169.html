A Formal Introduction

2002-07-06
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Formal Introduction Written: 7/6/02 Nate: PORTER IS ABUSING HIS… Nate is cut off as “I’m With Stupid” by Static-X blasts onto the arena PA system. Robbie: Speak o’ the Devil! Or should I say, Devils! Steven: Well, it IS getting hot in here. An eruption of boos fills the arena as two smiling visages fill the entryway. Beer cups immediately begin flying towards the ring ramp in an angry mass towards the individuals who have plagued OSW since it’s inception. The two men standing at the top of the ramp and smiling are Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman and Alan Kriegman. Next to them on the ground, wagging its mechanical tail, is the robotic I-Cybie. Nate: Looks like it’s explanation time. I don’t think anything they can come up with can cover for the atrocities they committed upon OSW! In fact they shouldn’t even be allowed to! These men should be in jail! Robbie: Alan Kriegman is the most evil man in the history of PCW! Not to mention OSW. Did you expect anything less from him? Momentarily the third member of the group, OSW Commissioner “The Scholar” Howard Porter, hobbles out. Nate: What the hell is this? Porter is wearing a wait brace as he leans on crutches. Robbie: The man has given it his all in two straight main-events! Cut him some slack! Not only that, but he risks getting injured fulfilling his Commissionership duties as well! Nate: Commissioner injuries? Paper cuts and carpal-tunnel syndrome, oooooooh. Porter leans a crutch against his side as each man raises a fist in the air, PHD spreading his fingers in a “Live Long and Prosper” sign. Kriegman and PHD’s other hands sports a microphone, while “The Scholar” has one tucked into his pants. Nate: Oh great, one ticket gets you into a whole Trekkie Convention. Robbie: You better watch it Dumas. Say the wrong thing around these guys and you may find yourself mysteriously “missing.” Nate: The Think Tank were the kinda guys I shoved into lockers in school, I think I’m safe from their reach or power. Suddenly a zapping is heard as Nate jolts in his chair. Nate: Oww! What the hell? Robbie: You were saying? Porter begins hobbling out into the flurry of alcohol rivulets, but then thinks better of it. He turns back to Kriegman and PHD and nods. They both look at each other, nodding in agreement, as each man produces…an umbrella. Robbie: Ha! Try to soak them now ingrates! Outsmarted by the Think Tank again! Nate: Oh please, anyone could have thought of that. Nate jolts in his chair again. Nate: Dammit! Porter tucks both crutches under one arm and begins limping down to the ring, holding the umbrella over his head with his free hand. Alan Kriegman and Dorkiman do likewise, smirking from ear to ear as they do so. They go on ahead of the turtle-esque Commissioner and climb into the ring and begin taunting the crowd. The I-Cybie trots faithfully alongside Howard as he slowly gets to the ring steps and ascends them one by one. Nate: Oh come on! He can’t be hurt THAT bad! Robbie: Hey! Don’t you watch football? Muscle pulls are the hardest thing to recover from! Nate: I was a scholarship football player, you moron. And muscle pulls are tough to recover from for pantywaists. Steve: Hee hee, pantywaist! Robbie: Urgh, it had been nice for a minute there. All three men are now in the ring, along with the yipping I-Cybie. Howard flashes a smile as he raises the mic to his lips first. Porter: First things first…why you ask? Porter takes a look back at his fellow Vaxes and smiles. Porter: BECAUSE WE CAN! The boos explode in the arena as PHD gives a buck-toothed grin and Alan Kriegman lets out his trademark Evil Supergenius laugh. Nate: That’s not a reason! That’s just sick! Robbie: That’s reason enough! They’re above you. Nate: Well, at least they’re above both of us. Robbie: Did I SAY they were above me? Porter: Ah yes, it was planned from the beginning. Quite the work of art when you stop and analyze the situation. Decker begins clapping. Robbie: Bravo! Bravo! Nate: Knock it off! Robbie: Hey, good art deserves applause. Porter: The seeds of fear were planted when the mysterious “accidents” started happening. The falling lights, the “food poisoning.” All we had to do was get a man on the inside to water them and sprout the Venus Fly Trap, and our good friend and patsy Ed played right into that when he appointed me as acting CEO after Jane Reagan was removed from the picture. Nate: A Venus Fly Trap? What the hell? Robbie: It was a good analogy! Steven: Yeah! Like child is to Popsicle as Steven is to P- Robbie: Finish that and it’s lights out. Steven: Whaaat? What’s so bad about me liking Pineapple juice? Robbie: Well, nothing I guess. Steven (softly): Penis. *SMACK* Porter: (continuing)Then once I had started commandeering power, we planted a sample of Ed’s hair with the surveillance video of Jane’s kidnapping! But to make the story even more muddled, Herbert suggested I leave an incriminating document for Ed to find, which would cast doubt for the time being that I was involved with the Vax terror. PHD smiles and winks through his Coke-bottle glasses. Porter: So when Ed returned and accused me of being the real Vax, Alan here simply had to show up on the jumbotron offering us a challenge and… Porter’s jaw drops and he smack his cheek in feign surprise. Porter: Lo and behold! Howard and Ed were BOTH set up by the Vax in an attempt to turn them against each other! So neither one of them could have any hand in it at all! HA! And you fell for it! Every single one of you were completely behind Ed and myself! Robbie: Well, I admit I wasn’t, but I sure am now! VAX! WHOO! Nate: As if all the stunts they pulled weren’t bad enough, they now have to come out here and insult everyone’s intelligence! Porter: And now that we have been unveiled, OSW is ours for the taking! I have the power of the Commissionership to impose and wills or ideas myself, Alan, or Herbert may conjure up! And be warned, the things we brainstorm are nothing to be pooh-poohed! I have to admit however, in spite of our combined intelligences, rivaling any MENSA chapter in the nation, I did wonder if the systematic infiltration of OSW as the Vax would be a hard thing to accomplish, but I never realized how easy it is to manipulate each of you! The crowd begins booing loudly at this, and taunting Porter. Porter: Ah yes, the incredibly intellectual taunts of the plebeians. “The Scholar” sticks his bottom lip out and scrunches one eye. Porter: “Hey! Yous done made funna me! I don’t much ‘preciate it four-eyes.” Porter manages to increase the crowd’s displeasure. Porter: But back to business here. While the three of us collectively collaborated to terrorize OSW…none of it would have been possible without little Rutherford. The I-Cybie mechanically yips as it trots over to Porter and rubs against his leg. Porter: Yes, that’s a good boy. Now then, without further adieu I would like to turn it over to another 1/3 of the Vax, my long time friend and yours, Alan Kriegman. Kriegman: First off, you may have heard that I was dead. But obviously… Alan jumps forward and spreads his arms. Kriegman: TA-DA! Porter and PHD give impressed looks and begin applauding. Kriegman: So I suppose you’re wondering why? Why would someone fake his own death? Well it’s one of the headings in the Evil Supergenius Handbook…”Faking Your Own Death Opens a Wealth of Possibilities For Future Schemes.” Robbie: Yep, it’s right there at the top. Nate: You’ve never seen it! Robbie: I have too! Nate: Have not! Robbie: Have too! Nate: Arrggh! Why am I even arguing with you? There IS no handbook! Kriegman: (continuing) And boy, did it ever. When we decided to influx Old School Wrestling with our own brand of terror, simply for the pleasure and challenge it brought upon us, it made for the perfect alibi. “Well, there’s no way Porter can have any connection with the Vax, because his buddy Alan Kriegman is dead, so who else could it be?” MUAAHAHAHAHHA Well it’s pretty clear now isn’t it? The Evil Supergenius is back, and he’s more evil, more super, and more genius than ever! Robbie: You hear that Dumas? More evil, super, AND genius! Nate: Oh please. Kriegman: Of course had anyone actually thought to LOOK for my body, none of this wouldn’t have been possible, but none of you were smart enough to think of that! So in a way, you brought this upon yourselves! The crowd showers Kriegman in boos for his attempt to pin the Vaxes’ crimes on them. Kriegman: But the important matter now isn’t what has happened in the past, it is what will happen in the future! The domination of the competition in Old School Wrestling by the Think Tank! I am a former PCW World, Continental, AND Tag Champion! Who in OSW can match those claims? Nate: Uh…Ivan Stanislav? Kriegman: NO ONE! That’s who! And I’ll be damned if I’m going to lower my guard to better accommodate the needs of the sub par talent here! They can catch up to me if they don’t want to be left behind in the dust! And when you couple my intellect and talents with those of my partners, we will take over! First OSW…then the World! MUAHAHAHAHHAA…well, unless those damn aliens show up again. Porter bites his lower lip in a look of worry. Steven: Ooh, they got to anally probe Howard, lucky green bastards. Kriegman: Nonetheless, the Tank is back, and OSW will be trampled under its treads! Take it away Herbert. PHD takes the microphone, to a chorus of boos. Nate: Wake me up when he is done. PHD: Salutations and well met fair travelers. I am indeed, Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman, and despite completely destroying everything you all hold dear, I now have a new Prime Directive!!! Nate: What in the hell is he talking about? Robbie: My Geek-to-English translating machine is missing. PHD gives the “Live long and proper” sign to some hecklers at ringside before continuing. PHD: That’s right, a NEW Prime Directive, which is to stop at nothing less than the total and utter destruction, of Civil Disobedience!!! Nate: WHAT?!?! The crowd boos PHD, who responds with a buck-toothed grin. PHD: In case you missed it, I answered on open hail by Civil Disobedience last Thursday night…A hail to meet those metally stunted social deviants in the ring next week on Slam! The crowd cheers, hoping for PHD and whoever his partner is get beaten down next week on Slam. PHD puts his hands on his hips in surprise, then pushes his glasses back up onto his nose. PHD: As I told those two nerf herders, I have a HUGE surprise of MAMMOTH proportions in store for them next week!!! After I am through with them next week, then will neither live long, nor prosper!!! Nate: Big surprise?!?! Robbie: Maybe he’s teaming with that big fat guy he plays D&D with? Nate: I DUNNO!!! PHD lets out a bucktoothed grin as the fans boo. Porter heads over and retrieves the microphone. Porter then raises the microphone and begins to speak once again. Porter: You may be asking, why in the world hasn’t Ms. Reagan just fired me. Well, let me tell… Porter doesn’t get to tell the fans in the arena, or watching at home, because he is interrupted as “Food For the Brain” by One Minute Silence hits and the crowd absolutely explodes. Nate: THAT’S ED’S MUSIC!!! Robbie: Pathetic… The camera shifts to the entryway as the former Commish walks out from the back. He pauses, looking around at the arena crowd, and raises Bob, his nail studded, barb-wire covered chair, which sets off a blast of pyros. Nate: ED IS HERE AND DOESN’T LOOK HAPPY!!! The camera shifts back to the entryway, where Ed asks for the music to be cut. The sound crew obliges. Ed: OSW is experiencing the Revenge of the Nerds… The crowd cheers, as Porter, Kriegman and PHD storm around the ring. Ed doesn’t miss a beat, however. Ed: And they picked the wrong guy to fuck with. The crowd explodes as Ed says this. Crowd: ED! ED! ED! ED! Nate: ED MAKING IT CLEAR HE IS NOT HAPPY FOR HIS ROLE IN THE VAX UNVEILING! Ed: There’s going to be hell to pay for someone, and collection starts now! PORTER…I AM GOING TO DESTROY YOU!!!! Nate: Ed is calling out “The Scholar!” The man whom earned his friendship then betrayed him! Robbie: Ed’s a spineless dog! He’s calling Porter out here when he knows very well that our beloved Commissioner can barely walk! Back in the ring, Porter looks out at Ed and shakes his head. Porter: Please Edward, can we make this quick? The Doctor said I wasn’t to be on my feet too long for fear of reaggravating my hip injury. Robbie: You hear that? Doctor’s orders! Porter: So what exactly did you want Edward? Ed: YOUR ASS ON A STICK! Steven: WOO HOO!!! The arena erupts and Porter furrows his brow. Porter: How eloquent of you Ed. Ed: I want you and that buddy of yours, Alan Kriegman, in the ring…NEXT WEEK! Nate: Ed has challenged Porter and Kriegman to a match next week so he can get some justice!!!! Porter: Kriegman AND myself? You really have gone off the deep end Eddie. How do you expect to take on two men who are your superiors mentally without a partner? The former Commish is seething. Ed: Oh I’ll have a partner alright, and you’ll see who it is next week! Robbie: The wingnut probably means Bob. Nate: Ed has found a mystery man for next week! The last time we witnessed this it was Meanstreak! WHO WILL IT BE THIS TIME!?!? Porter: Edward you dolt, I would love to give you an expert lesson in wrestling next week on Slam, but I simply can’t. I’m sorry but I’m in no condition to wrestle. Nate: Porter is trying to welsh out of another match here tonight!! First he finds a replacement to take on Switchblade, now this!!! Ed: THEN HOW ABOUT I JUST COME DOWN THERE AND TAKE MY SHOTS NOW! Ed begins to stride towards the ring, but is stopped by a sea of security, who rush out of the back. Nate: THIS IS ABSOLUTELY OUT OF HAND!!! NEXT WEEK ON SLAM, IT WILL BE PHD AND HIS “BIG SURPRISE” TAKING ON CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE PLUS FANS WILL GET TO SEE ED AND A PARTNER TO BE NAMED TAKE ON PORTER AND KRIEGMAN!!! WE NEED TO HEAD TO A COMMERCIAL!!! WE WILL BE RIGHT BACK AFTER THESE MESSAGES!!! The scene shifts to the aisle, where OSW security officials try to hold back Ed from getting to the ring, where the smugly smiling trio of PHD, Porter and Kriegman stand pointing and laughing at the former commish….as the scene fades to commercal.
