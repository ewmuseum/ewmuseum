Malcolm Mass — move list (January 2004)

10 January 2004

Wrestling Style: Heavy-Powerful/Super Heavyweight Set Up Move: - (- ) Finisher: Irish Cannonball (After taking his opponent down with some sort of power attack, such as a running lariat, Mass will slowly but surely ascend the turnbuckle and stand on the top ropes. Pushing with his powerful legs, Mass sails through the air and manages to forward roll as he quickly descends through the air, landing heavily on his back right across the opponent’s chest/abdomen, after which he usually hooks the leg for a pin attempt ) Other Moves: Specialty Move(s): 1.) Irish Pride, 2.) Bejeezus, 3.) Black and White [Refers to popular Irish drink, Guiness] Specialty Move Description: 1.) Running Fireman’s Carry turned into a Samoan Drop, 2.) A standard Bearhug held for 15-20 seconds quickly turned into a Belly To Back Suplex, 3.) Stalling Double Underhook Suplex turned into a Sit-Out Slam. 10 Favourite Moves: Swinging Chokeslam, Jumping Spinebuster [Get opp. over his shoulder before jumping in the air and slamming them forward as he lands on top of them], Big Boot, STO, Dropdown Jawbreaker, Running Splash [Usually to an opponent in the corner but can also be done to standing opponent], Jumping Implant DDT, Pull-In Clothesline [Holds victim in a wrist lock before pulling them towards himself and clotheslining them stiffly with the other arm], Tiger Driver, Aeroplane Leg Spin.
