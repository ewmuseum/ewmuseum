Destruction

2002-06-20
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Destruction Written: June 20th, 2002 The show opens as a bright red Porsche comes screeching to a halt in the parking lot of the Igloo Sports Arena, in Pittburgh, Pennsylvania, and as so often the case in professional wrestling, a camera just happens to be there to document the action. The camera shifts to ground level, catching the glimpse of a pair of Air Jordan’s stepping out of the vehicle. The shot slowly pans up to reveal the sneaker, and the foot that currently inhabits it, belong to none other than Andrew “Riptah” Washington, one half of one of the OSW Tag Team Champions, Da Westsidaz. The camera pans to the right, where Riptah’s long time partner, Short Circuit, is shown exiting the passenger side of the vehicle. From inside the arena, we can hear the cheers of the fans, who are obviously watching the happenings in the parking lot on the massive Jumbotron inside the arena. Short Circuit grabs his duffel bag and slings it over his shoulder, as the larger Riptah grabs two duffel bags and carries them off as he kicked his door shut. The pair walk down the parking lot, looking directly at the entrance of the arena. They both take one deep breath, looked at one another, smirk then nod. They knew tonight was another challenge, another defense. This time it was against a team named The Throwbacks, Dave Gibson and Clay Vendetta. Sure, it was a powerful team they were going up against...but they didn't fear anything... The had beaten the best. They had overcome The Blood Brothers. They had beaten the Assassins. The had defeated Civil Disobedience. The had won gold from Heresian Bloodluse. Nothing would get in the way of another win, another successful defense... Or so they thought. Suddenly the Tag Team Champions heard three voices yell out one single command in unison. Like drill army soldiers giving their orders to their troops, it was a command that let Da Westsidaz know...danger was near. The command was... 'NOW!' Da Westsidaz both turn around to face the men who gave the command, but as they turned around...both men were floored and sent down onto the concrete. The sickening sound of flesh against concrete echoed in the parking lot, as Da Westsidaz opened their eyes to see none other than Armour and John Mammoth standing in front of their fallen bodies. From inside the arena, a rousing chorus of boos is heard. The tag team champs than look behind the two monsters to see the three men who commanded the attack...Alan Kriegman, who was holding TCM back, PHD and Howard Porter. Porter looked at the two large men in Armour and Mammoth and stepped forward. 'WHAT ARE YOU WAITING FOR?! DO AS WE PLANNED!' And without any further notice, Armour quickly grabs Short Circuit by the neck and pulls him up off the ground. Armour holds the much smaller SC above his head by his neck, and finally hurled SC into a nearby Lexus, denting the hood. Armour watches as SC lay groaning on the hood, while the nearly 500 pound Mammoth grabs a hold of Riptah and proceeds to slam his head into a nearby pillar. But Mammoth wouldn't allow Riptah to fall...he grabs Riptah by his waist and lifts the 300 pounder up off the ground. Mammoth then proceeds to run with Riptah and crash him HARD into a hood of a car with a Belly to Belly Suplex. Satisified with their handy work, Mammoth and Armour look at one another and nod. Suddenly, Alan Kriegman is seen scurrying behind a car and picking up an object. Kriegman runs back to TCM, who is fuming and ready to pounce, and hands TCM the weapon...the weapon that is...a trashcan. TCM's eyes widen in insanity as he stares at the trashcan and smiles, and than looks back at Kriegman. Alan Kriegman looks back at TCM and whispers, 'Just like we talked about...go for it!' And without any more time wasted, TCM charges with the trashcan firmly in his grasp. Armour and Mammoth looked as TCM charged and quickly pulled SC to his feet and held him upright. TCM leaps in the air, trashcan in hand, and smashes the can over SC's face, causing SC to fall to the concrete hard in a pool of his own blood. Riptah slowly begins to get to his feet, but it did nothing for him...except cause more pain. Immediately after he got to his feet, he felt to large hands grasp tightly around his neck...the hands of Armour and John Mammoth. Porter and PHD yell out to the two men. PHD: MAMMOTH!!! LIKE FINISH HIM!!! Armour and Mammoth nod in agreement and lifted Riptah up above their heads...but instead of slamming Riptah down on the concrete, they look to PHD, who has opened a dumpster and is pointing his pasty fingers at it. PHD: DOCK HIM IN SICK BAY!!!! Armour and Mammoth charge with Riptah in their grasp, landing a DOUBLE CHOKESLAM into the dumpster...slamming Riptah amongst the trash and steel walls. Both Armour and Mammoth nod at a job well done, and then the massive pair notice TCM on the other side. TCM was not giving in, and continued to pound the trashcan over SC's face. TCM had kept it up for nearly 30 seconds, until Kriegman finally pulls him off of SC, while whispering something in TCM's ear. TCM grins insanely as he throws the mangled trashcan down and pulls up the bloodied Short Circuit. TCM walks SC over to a nearby Jeep that was parked next to the open dumpster and rolls SC onto the hood. Mammoth backs away a bit, to give TCM room, as Armour stands next to the dumpster watching the mayhem ensue. TCM places SC between his legs and looked at Kriegman for approval. Kriegman smiles and gives TCM the thumbs up, as TCM lifts SC up then proceeds to excute the PYRO DRIVER INTO THE DUMPSTER, ON TOP OF RIPTAH! The dumpster nearly explodes with sound, as TCM lands his cradle piledriver onto Da Westsidaz. Kriegman applauds the mayhem, and smiled as TCM crawled out of the dumpster. Armour and Mammoth both look at TCM, but then look back over at Porter and PHD, who both smile and yell out, 'Armour...Mammoth...END IT FOR GOOD! MAKE SURE THEY DO NOT SPOIL OUR PLANS!' Immediately Armour stomps off to the corner of the dumpster and pull out two steel chairs. He quickly lobs one to Mammoth, who catches it in his baseball glove sized hands with ease. Both monsters then proceed to climb up the dumpster and lift the chairs above their heads. CRACK! WHACK! SMACK! In one split second, Armour and Mammoth shower Da Westsidaz with a huge amount of sickening chair shots! Kriegman smiles in pleasure, as TCM grasped onto Kriegman's leg as he watched Mammoth and Armour destroy the Tag Team Champions. Every sickening chair shot brings a whole new stain of blood on each chair. The figures of Da Westsidaz finally stopped moving, but the assault still continues. The steel continues to pour down upon the Tag Team Champions. Finally, the two monsters stop their assault as Howard Porter steps forward and raises an open palm above his head. Armour and Mammoth both look to Porter, then finally stop, then climb from the dumpster, with their crimson stained chairs in hand. They then proceed to close the lids to the dumpster. Suddenly, PHD rushes running forward with a steel chain and three separate locks. Immediately PHD wraps the thick chain around the handles of the dumpster's lid, and triple locks it. After locking the final lock, PHD turns to his five comrades, and gives a huge, bucktoothed grin. Porter smiles in response, as the eerie sounds of Alan Kriegman’s trademark laugh serve echo throughout the parking area….as the brutal and disturbing scene fades to black….
