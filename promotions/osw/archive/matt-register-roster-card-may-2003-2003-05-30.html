Matt Register Division: Wrestling Status: Active Real Name: Matt Register (had it legally changed) Birthdate: 7/3/76 Hometown: Tampa, FL Height: 6'3 Weight: 245 lbs. Nicknames: Smart Ass, SAMR, Matty Allignment: Heel Entrance Music: 'Cowboys From Hell' by Pantera Information Combat Style: Technical/High Impact/Innovative Brutality - As would be expected, his style adapts to his opponents', but he's very methodical, waiting for mistakes and then finding new ways to exploit an advantage. Typically he sticks to mat work and chain wrestling with an emphasis on going for the 'home run' big moves. Set Up Move: Move reversal/scoop (He'll either catch the person in a scoop or tilt-a-whirl setup, or reverse a move into the lock. ) Finisher: A Little Dose of Sarcasm (Three-Quarter Nelson Sit-Out Neckbreaker. Like a Greetings From Asbury Park, but with both hands locked over the opponent's head to snap it down as he sits out. ) Title History: Tag Team Champions, October 2002 - Present Award History: MET Winner.
