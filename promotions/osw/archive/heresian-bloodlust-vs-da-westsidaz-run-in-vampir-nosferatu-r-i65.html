Heresian Bloodlust vs. Da Westsidaz Run-in/Vampir Nosferatu Return

2002-06-22
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Heresian Bloodlust vs. Da Westsidaz Run-in/Vampir Nosferatu Return Written: 6-22-02 Hessian pulls Short Circuit to his feet and whips him off the ropes again. Short Circuit barely manages to run across the ring, and as he rebounds, Hessian thrusts him into the air over himself with an almighty roar, and roars again to the booing crowd as Azrael Cain almost buries Shorty in the canvas with the Death Valley Driver. Nate: THE HERETIC’S WRATH!!! HERETIC’S WRATH!!! HERESIAN BLOODLUST HAVE DESTROYED SHORT CIRCUIT!!! Steven: Now if only they would pin him or if Gaston would get the illegal man the hell out of there, things would be hunky dory! Nate: They are!!! They’re goi-…WAIT!!! ON THE STAGE!!! LOOK WHO IT IS!!! Hessian drops to the mat and covers Short Circuit as Cain rests in the corner. Pierre Gaston goes for the count… 1… Robbie: IT’S CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE!!! WITH A LADDER!!! THE BASTARDS!!! NOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 2……… Steven: THEY’RE IN THE RING!!! THIS THING IS GONNA GET FUGLY NOOOW!!!!!!!!! 3NO!!! Hessian scrambles to his feet, and he and Cain rush Civil Disobedience, only to be smacked with a wide ladder shot sending them falling back into the mat. Nate: THIS IS OUT OF CONTROL!!! As Heresian Bloodlust roll around in pain in the corner, Riptah gets back to his feet, only to be smashed in the face with the top step of the ladder. Laid out next to Short Circuit, Da Westsidaz lie incapacitated. Nate: BASTARDS!!! CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE MUST HAVE THIS MATCH SCREWED UP!!! Pierre Gaston jumps in, but is yelled back into the corner, and forced to watch as first Seth scales the ladder, and dives off, landing on Da Westsidaz with a 360º Splash, sending a sickening thud throughout the arena as flesh hits flesh from the height. While Seth takes the pain of the fall and rolls out of the way, Trevor climbs the ladder, and upon barely hitting the top step, dives off and lands a Twisting Corkscrew Leg Drop across the throats of both Shorty and Riptah. Nate: DA SUPAFLY!!! Trevor just hit Da Westsidaz with Da Supafly!!! The crowd is in a frenzy. Robbie: Goddamnit what are they doing now??? Steven: I dunno but Pierre is going to do something now! Pierre runs across the ring, stopping Civil Disobedience as they grab their ladder. Looking at them, then to the ringside, Pierre shakes his finger in a most French way. Pierre: DQ!!! DQ!!! DQ!!! STOP ZE MATCH!!! NO DECISION The bell rings, but that doesn’t stop Civil D knocking Pierre to the side. They make their way over to Hessian and Cain, and begin laying into them with ladder shots. Heresian Bloodlust groan as the ladder lands down on their arms, legs and gut. Then, Seth takes the ladder as Trevor position Cain and Hessian until they are side-by-side, and then lifts up their heads as Seth lays down the ladder under them. Nate: OH F*CK NO!!! THEY CAN’T!!! SOMEBODY STOP THIS!!! Steven: THEY’RE GOING TO KILL THE TAG CHAMPS!!! THIS IS A DEBAUCLE!!! Robbie: GODDAMNIT NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Seth and Trevor grabs the edge of the ladder, sizing up Cain and Hessian’s heads lying in the path of as basic “skull-cracking”. They look at the crowd, who are roaring the roof off of the arena with booing, then begin mimicking a countdown. Civil D: THREE! They pull the ladder up then lower it slightly. Civil D: TWO!! Seth and Trevor again pull and lower the ladder, with some more force this time, and within a few inches of Cain and Hessian’s heads… Civil D: ONE!!! Civil Disobedience repeats the action, this time the ladder reaching within only a few centimetres of Hessian and Cain’s heads. Civil D: ZER-… The lights go out in the arena…everything is pitch black. Nate: WHAT THE HELL HAPPENED??? Steven: I DON’T KNOW!!! THE LIGHTS ARE OUT!!! WHAT THE FRICK HAPPENED??? WHERE’S MINION??? WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON??? Robbie: GET THE FUCKING LIGHTS BACK ON!!! HERESIAN BLOODLUST COULD BE DEAD IN THE RING AND WE’RE SITTING HERE IN THE DARK!!! GODDAMNIT MOTHERFU-… Nate: SSSH!!! Listen! In the ring!!! Suddenly the lights come back on and the arena lights up, and the fans gasp at once…then silence. Nate, Robbie and Steven are silent, staring into the ring in horror at what stands before them. Draped over the turnbuckle is Short Circuit, while tangled in the ropes is Seth. Trevor and Riptah are lying contorted and twisted in the ring, all four men lying in a pool of blood, their own? The crowd stare on, as standing in the middle of this massacre, defiantly stood in the centre of the ring are Caleb…Minion…Azrael Cain…Hessian…and… Nate & Robbie & Steven: VAMPIR NOSFERATU!!! VAMPIR!!! VAMPIR!!! VAMPIR NOSFERATU IS BACK AND HE HAS DECAPITATED DA WESTSIDAZ AND CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE!!! HERESIAN BLOODLUST AND VAMPIR NOSFERATU!!! HOLY JESUS IN HEAVEN!!! VAMPIR NOSFERATU IS BACK!!! Indeed…Vampir Nosferatu stands with Heresian Bloodlust defiantly in the ring, all three men including Minion and Caleb looking out into the sea of amazed fans. Then the entire arena becomes one amazing ovation of boos fit for the apocalypse. Heresian Bloodlust and Vampir Nosferatu meanwhile stand motionless in the ring, looking at the carnage Vampir has created. Nate: OH MY GOD!!! VAMPIR NOSFERATU HAS RETURNED ONE NIGHT BEFORE ASCENSION!!! WHAT THE F*CK DOES THIS MEAN FOR THE TAG MATCH???????????? Robbie: BOOYAH! BOOYAH! BOOYAH! BOOYAH! VAMPIR NOSFERATU AND HERESIAN BLOODLUST!!!!!!!!! THE APOCALYPSE HAS ARRIVED!!! Steven: I think it’s time for a commercial… Nate: VAMPIR NOSFERATU HAS RETURNED AND HERESIAN BLOODLUST HAVE JOINED FORCES WITH THE SOUL STALKER!!! WE NEED TO GO TO A COMMERCIAL!!! The camera shifts to the ring were Vampir Nosferatu emotionlessly stalks around the ring flanked by the OSW Tag Team Champions, Heresian Bloodlust…as the scene fades to commercial.
