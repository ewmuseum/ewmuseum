Reno Davis vs Michel Krieg

2002-04-20
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reno Davis vs Michel Krieg Written: April 20, 2002 Nate: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome back to Saturday Slam! Our next match pits Michel Krieg of The Red Army against the newcomer, Reno Davis!! Steven: I really wanna see Reno’s monkey! Robbie: Alright, that’s the last play on words you’re allowed throughout the duration of this match! Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, the following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, from Berlin, Germany, standing 7’ tall and weighing in at 347 lbs: Michel Krieg!!! “Sonne” by Remmstein blares over the loudspeaker as the swirling hammer and sickle of The Red Army appears on the Jumbotron and pyro bursts from the entryway! Michel Krieg, along with The Two Round Faced Women, walks down the entryway to a mixed reaction of cheers and boos. Krieg climbs into the ring and salutes the crowd. Earl: And his opponent, from Moncton, New Brunswick, he stands 5’6” tall and weighs in at 166 lbs: Reno Davis!!! “Rocket Science” by The Pimps begins to play as Davis, along with his pet monkey ZaC, head down to the ring. Reno climbs into the ring while ZaC stands by the announce position. Robbie: Get that flea ridden thing out of here!! Earl calls for the bell and leaves the ring. Krieg points and laughs at Davis, who is not even half the massive German’s size. Davis growls and flicks off Krieg. Krieg beckons for him to attack him, and the smaller Davis is more than happy to take him up on that offer. Nate: Reno Davis is going to have one hell of a match tonight. Not only having to battle Michel Krieg, but also ward off the attacks of The Two Round Faced Women. This is going to be an uphill battle if I have ever seen one! Davis rushes forward, but his head is palmed by the massive German and shoved across the ring. Krieg looks to the Round Faced Women and nods as Reno deftly rolls back to his feet and stands in a martial arts stance. He lowers his arms and narrows his eyes at Krieg, and then rushes forward again. Krieg shoots his arm out against to push him away, but Reno baseball slides low and kicks Krieg in the ankle! Krieg doubles over, giving Reno just enough time to punch at his throat, catching him between the chin and the chest, dead in the throat! Robbie: Wait a minute?! Krieg wheels back and grabs at his throat, coughing violently. Reno is up on his feet in an instant and grabs Krieg’s arm. He goes for an irish whip, but Krieg remains still. He tries again, but Krieg merely grins and shakes his finger at Reno. The German Machine grabs Reno by the back of the hair and lifts him with on hand and tosses him across the ring. Reno rolls across the ring into the corner! Nate: Reno had him going there for a moment folks, but Michel Krieg is just too powerful! Steven: He had the right idea, going for the legs of the massive man, but I’m not sure if that’ll be enough! Krieg rushes forward, his massive footsteps shaking the wrestling ring as he does, and goes for a knee to the chest of Davis! He nails Reno, slamming his entire 300+ pound bulk into Davis. Davis staggers out of the corner. Krieg lifts Reno with one hand, brings him up over his head, and back down to the mat with a thunderous SLAM!! Krieg laughs and puts his foot on the chest of Davis! 1…Davis gets the shoulder up with ease. Nate: Some cockiness shown by Michel Krieg, eh guys? Robbie: I’d be cocky too if a midget tried to fight me! Steven: But he’s not a midget! Robbie: He is to Krieg! Krieg methodically reaches down and lifts Davis and sends him into the ropes. Davis rebounds! Krieg grabs Davis by the waist and tosses him vertically into the air! Davis flies, but as he begins to fall he twists into some sort of an elbow drop and NAILS Krieg on the crown of his skull! Krieg staggers backwards while Reno falls to the mat and rolls to his feet! Nate: Beautiful reversal by the newcomer!! Reno leaps to his feet and rushes towards Krieg. Krieg begins to get his bearing back, but Reno dropkicks him square in the chest! Krieg rocks back into the ropes and bounces forward. Reno hits a drop toe hold on The German Machine and slaps on an ankle lock! Krieg roars in pain as Reno wrenches the hold harder! Referee Pierre Gaston checks Krieg, who shakes his head in defiance! Nate: He’s got him in the middle of the ring! Krieg can’t get a rope break!! Reno yells loudly as grinds Krieg’s ankle more. Krieg pushes himself up with his hands and roars and begins to crawl towards the ropes… Steven: He’s trying for a rope break! Reno’s not gonna let him though! Reno digs his heels into the mat and pulls back. Krieg’s fingernails drag across the mat as he’s pulled back. Krieg screams loudly, sweat pouring off of his brow. Krieg slams his fist against the mat and digs his forehead into the mat. His shoulders heave as he roars loudly again and pushes hard, literally kicking Reno across the ring!! Nate: WHOA! Krieg staggers to his feet, heavily favoring his right ankle. Reno gets to his feet and rubs his chin with a slight cocky grin on his face. He rushes forward for a dropkick, but Krieg CRUSHES him with a booming clothesline! Reno flips in mid air and lands on his back, gasping for air! Steven: So much for his momentum….. Krieg kicks his right foot to alleviate some of the pain in his right ankle and kicks Reno hard in the gut, flipping him over. Krieg bends down and grabs the waist of Reno’s pants and lifts him. He puts him in a massive bearhug and flips around into a belly to belly suplex! Krieg puts another foot over the chest of Reno! 1…kickout by Reno! Krieg kicks Reno back over and puts his foot on him again! 1…kickout by Reno! Nate: What does Krieg think he is doing? Robbie: Teaching the young’un a lesson! Reno rolls back to his hands and knees and is hoisted up on Krieg’s shoulder in a Canadian Backbreaker! Reno screams in pain, only to be spun around into a reverse shoulder breaker and dumped into the center of the ring! Reno tries to get up, but Krieg is on top of him. He stands him up and throws him into the ropes! Reno charges into one of Krieg’s huge military boots and collapses! Nate: Michel Krieg is dismantling Reno Davis! Krieg lifts Reno again and slaps him in his face, sending him reeling into the corner. Krieg stomps over to the corner and delivers three huge knife edge chops to the chest of Davis, and then sets him up on the top turnbuckle! Krieg turns to The Round Faced Women and salutes them, and turns to face Reno again. However, Reno is now STANDING! Reno leaps off the top turnbuckle and nails Krieg with a tornado DDT! Krieg hits the mat hard and grabs the top of his head, not prepared for the move at all! Nate: Reno Davis has turned the tide!! Robbie: Yeah…for now… Outside the ring, The Two Round Faced Woman have slowly circled the ring together, eyeing up ZaC, who is rustling around under the ring, with just his lower half of his body showing. One of the Women leap to grab him, but ZaC scurries under the ring suddenly! The Woman crawls in after him while the other runs to the other side of the ring! Steven: They’re trying to kill ZaC! Robbie: GOOD! Meanwhile, in the ring, Reno stands over Michel Krieg. Davis runs to the corner, hops on the second turnbuckle, and drops a shin over the forehead of Krieg. He deftly rolls to his feet, stands, and legdrops Krieg with fluid ease! Krieg grabs his neck and kicks at the air while Reno collects himself! Davis rebounds off the ropes and hits Krieg with a splash into a pin!! 1…2..kickout by Krieg!! Nate: Near fall for Reno Davis! He’s got Michel Krieg reeling!! While the Second Round Faced Woman stands on the opposite end of the ring, ZaC appears on another corner, away from the Woman! She runs to catch him as he runs away from her! Just as she is about to get ZaC, the First Round Faced Woman reaches out from under the ring, GRABS her Comrade’s leg, and trips her!! Robbie: YEOWCH! That’s gonna hurt! Krieg wobbles to his feet and shakes the cobwebs free just as Reno stands as well. Reno goes for another dropkick but is caught and thrown hard to the mat. Krieg lines him up for an elbow drop, but Reno rolls at the last possible moment! Steven: Battles inside and outside of the ring!! The Two Round Faced Women rise, standing at the entryway. ZaC stands on the opposite side of the ring. Seeing that ZaC is trapped, one moves right, the other, left…. Krieg walks over to Reno and grabs him by the throat and lifts him. Reno struggles and barely manages to hit Krieg with a knee to the chin! Krieg drops Reno, giving him enough time to whip him into the corner! Reno rushes forward and performs a backflip kick! Krieg staggers out of the corner into a superkick!!! Krieg ponderously falls backwards with a loud thud! Nate: That’s usually a precursor to his finishing move!! The Two Round Faced Women round the ring and stand ten feet away from ZaC on each side!! Steven: They’re gonna beat the monkey!! HEE HEE!! They rush forward quickly, bent at the waist to grab him. At the last moment, ZaC LEAPS vertically! The Two Round Faced Women SLAM into each other and collapse in a heap, with ZaC sitting on top of them!! Robbie: HA HA! The monkey beat them!! Davis drags Krieg out of the corner and slaps him in The Straight-Jacket!! Nate: The Straight Jacket!! He has Krieg in his finisher!! Pierre Gaston runs to check on Krieg. Krieg roars that he won’t give up. Reno yells and locks it on harder, wrenching back the body of the massive German. Again, Krieg shakes his head in defiance. For a good fifteen seconds, Krieg is locked in the hold, and then he goes limp! Nate: I think he passed out!! Gaston lifts Krieg’s hand once… Steven: Krieg doesn’t look to good…. Gaston lifts his hand a second time…. Nate: It fell again!! Gaston lifts it a third time…and his hand hits the mat!! Nate: He lost consciousness!! Reno Davis has defeated the German War Machine!! Davis breaks the hold and leaps up and down, which prompts ZaC to rush into the ring as well. Cheers rock through the arena, as do some boos, amazingly! Krieg lays prone in the ring. Nate: Ladies and Gentlemen, what an upset victory for Reno Davis! And now, a word from our sponsors!
