Clay Vendetta vs. Propaganda (4/27/02 - Slam!) Written: 4/27/02 Clay Vendetta vs. Propaganda Match Result: Loss Via: Pinfall (The Exodus) Stipulations: #1 Contender's Match for the International Title Notes: None of importance. This match took place the night before Head-On Collision. Nate: Welcome back to Slam! Steven: Coming up next, we have a match to decide the number one contender for the Continental Title of OSW. That's right we have two big old sweaty men... Robbie: Ahhhh! Robbie leans over and topples the chair that Steven is sitting in. Robbie: I swear to God, one of these days I am going to quit. Nate: Steven was right though. Coming up next we have a number one's contender match. So, let's take it... Suddenly a voice from backstage interrupts Nate: Voice: Hold on a second there, Dumas! Nate: What the hell? Voice: You think that I am going to let you three sons a bitches announce a match that will decide who will be facing me a week from now do you? The figure that is speaking steps out from the backstage area. Nate: Oh my...It's Rhett Locke!!!! Locke: Yeah that's right asshole! It's me, the intercontinental champion of the world, Rhett Locke!!! Locke is making his way down the ramp towards the ring as he speaks. Locke: Yeah that's right, I am coming down to this shit hole despite the fact I'm not even supposed to be here tonight. Robbie: What the hell is this guy talking about? Locke: What I'm talking about Decker...is that this is my belt and no piece of shit is going to take it from me. Locke has made his way to the ring and starts to walk towards the announcers' table. The continental champion grabs a metal chair and places next to Steven and then proceeds to sit down next to the OSW announce team. Locke: Yeah, that's right boys. you have a new announcer sitting with you slack jawed bitches for this match. And what do you think about that? The camera pans over to Nate, Steven, and Robbie who are sitting in place with their mouths hanging wide open. Locke: That's what I thought. Stupid ass cock suckers. What the hell do they pay you boys for? No response. Locke: Alright...Hey, Earl! do that thing that they pay you for! The camera shot changes to Earl Henrich who is standing in the ring with the same confused look on his face. Earl finally raises the mic up to his mouth in order to begin the match's introduction. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, Boys and Girls, Children of all ages!!! This next match is scheduled to decide the number one contender for the Continental Title. It is scheduled for one fall. The referee for this match is Pierre Gaston. Coming to the ring first, from Clarksville, Tennessee. He is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Sagar! HE is standing in at seven feet tall and weighing in at a hefty373 pounds... 'Play God' by Spineshank begins to play on the speakers hung in the arena. Earl: He is Propaganda!!! There is a mixed reaction from the fans as Propaganda enters the area and raising his hands high into the air while Sagar is close behind him reading from the bible. Locke: And this is what my competition is supposed to be? Some goddam preacher from Tennessee! What the hell would he do to me? Try and save my fucking soul! Propaganda quickly makes his way down to the ring with Sagar following behind. Propaganda climbs into the ring over the top rope and just stairs at the entryway. Earl: And his opponent, coming from Miami, Florida. He is accompanied to the ring by his manager, Niki. He stands at five feet eleven inches and weighs in at 225 pounds... 'Carry On My Wayward Son' by Kansas begins to rock the arena as the fans begin to cheer. Earl: He is Clay Vendetta! Locke: And we are expecting this midget looking motherfucker to pin this giant retard? Christ! This is going to be a massacre. Vendetta charges the ring and dives underneath the ropes. Gaston immediately signals for the bell to officially start the match. Nate: And here we go. Locke: Real original Dumas! Robbie: But seriously, let's talk about your match tomorrow night at Head on Collision, instead of watching these tow idiots beat the hell out of each other. Locke: In my opinion, there is nothing to talk about. That prick, Corg, has been nothing but a pain in my side since Crowning Glory! Tomorrow night, that goddam freak is going to feel what real pain is! Steven leans over to Nate. Steven: (Whispering) This white skinned freak is calling who a freak. Locke's head spins around and he glares at the chuckling Smith. Locke reaches down and topples Steven over for the second time. Locke: Fucking Faggot! Inside the ring, CCV charges at Propaganda. The classic one manages to duck underneath a big boot from the preacher. Vendetta leaps onto the back of the massive preacher and slaps a perfect choke hold on. Nate: Vendetta using his speed here to his advantage! Robbie: Yeah yeah, that's great Nate. But what about Corg. You have to admit that Corg is one hell of a competitor. Locke: One hell of a what? Do you realize what you just said to me! CCV is still holding onto Propaganda's neck. The huge preacher falls to he knee as he starts to try and get CCV off. Gaston is looking for some sort of tap out. Nate: CCV seems as if he means business! Locke: Competitor! Competitor! If you want to call him a competitor, you might as well call that old hag in the front row a competitor! Propaganda quickly falls to his back slamming CCV into the mat hard causing him to release his grip. Propaganda climbs to his feet grasping at his throat. In a fit of rage the preacher starts to lay the boots into the ribs of the classic one. CCV is trying to roll away from the barrage, but Propaganda continues his assault until the classic one gets to the ropes and rolls out of the ring onto the hard concrete floor. Locke: Will you look at this! These are supposed to be the guys that I am expecting to defend my title against next week. This is an outrage! Gaston begins to count out CCV. 1... 2... 3... 4... 5... 6... 7... CCV dives under the ropes only to be met with a vicious clothesline from Propaganda. Vendetta falls to the mat hard and is now grasping at his throat. Propaganda leans over and picks CCV up into the air over his head. WHAM!!! CCV hits the mat hard once again onto his back. Nate: Propaganda is in complete control right now as Vendetta is grasping his back in pain. Locke: You call this wrestling! Neither of these guys are a true fighter! First I will destroy Corg tomorrow then which ever one of these asses wins, I destroy next week! Propaganda gets CCV back up to his feet again and up into the air in order to hit him with a powerbomb! But Vendetta manages to throw his weight forward as he was lifted which sends him to the mat onto his feet. He quickly hits Propaganda with a Russian leg sweep sending the preacher to the mat face first. CCV quickly drops to his knees and grabs hold of Propaganda's ankle in order to slap on a perfect ankle lock. Nate: Great speed and execution from the CCV. Steven: Speed! That is never good! You always want it slow. Especially, at the beginning! WHAP! Steven falls out of his chair again as a result of a fierce slap from Locke. Propaganda is flailing in pain from the ankle lock. The more pain that the preacher seems to be in, the tighter the ankle is twisted. Propaganda is reaching for the ropes. He is using his massive arms to pull himself towards the rope. Nate: This is where the huge size difference is coming into play. Propaganda is dragging Vendetta across the ring. Propaganda reaches with all of his might one more time with every once of strength in his body until... Nate: He got the rope! He got. Gaston just broke up the lock. Locke: Good! The more these two assholes beat the shit out of each other the quicker the victory next week. Robbie: You seem awfully sure of yourself. Locke: Damn right, bitch! I mean come on, who in the hell wins with an ankle lock anymore these days? Vendetta is up on his feet. He quickly jumps up onto the turnbuckle and leaps off trying to hit Propaganda with a double leg drop from the top rope, but the now limping preacher knocks him away as if he was a fly. CCV rolls across the mat and quickly gets to his feet. Propaganda stalks towards Vendetta who is down on one knee catching his breath. As the preacher gets closer, CCV nails the huge man in groin with low blow that doubles Propaganda over. Nate: Oh, come on now! That is a low blow. Steven: Low Blow!!! Robbie: HA!!! Locke: Fucking Cunts! Outside the ring, Niki jumps up onto the apron in an attempt to distract Gaston by shaking her breasts, which works perfectly. CCV sees that Gaston is distracted and proceeds to wrestle the turnbuckle pad until it is off and exposing the unforgiving metal. CCV pulls Propaganda up and starts to pound the preacher's face into the metal repeatedly. Immediately, Propaganda's face is split open in several places causing blood to pour forth from the wounds. Locke: Now this is a little more like it! Steven: He's cut! You see, he's not a machine. He's not a machine! Dead air follows for about five seconds. Robbie: Steven...What have we told you about TRYING TO MAKE JOKES! Robbie leans over to topple the chair. Steven: Not tooooo... Steven hits the ground again. Gaston is still looking at the ample breasts of the bouncing Niki until something pulls her from the apron by hair down onto the floor below. Nate: Sagar! Sagar just assaulted Niki! Locke: What a stupid bitch! Niki screams from fright as she hits the ground. Her high pitched squeal pulls Vendetta's attention away from Propaganda. CCV charges at the ropes in order to aid his darling manager. Before he can get to the ropes though Propaganda grabs hold of CCV and Irish whips him across the ring. As Vendetta comes back towards Propaganda he is hit with a T-Bone Suplex. Nate: Exodus! Propaganda just hit the Exodus! Propaganda goes for the pin. Gaston is feels the loud thud and turns around. The ref quickly slides into position for the count. 1... 2... 3... Winner of the Match: Propaganda!!! Nate: Propaganda has done it. He won the match!
