Michael Dame Real Name: Michael Dame Birthdate: August 11, 1973 Hometown: Minneapolis, Minnesota Height: 6'2 Weight: 249 lbs. Nicknames: Dame Status: Neutral Manager(s): Gloria Current Teams: n/a Entrance Music: One by Metallica Entrance Video Information Wrestling Style: Technical, Martial Artist Signature Moves: Headlock Take Down, Quick Kick to the shin/lower leg Followed by a Clothsline, Snap Suplex, Powerslam, Middle Rope Legdrop, Spinebuster, Powerbomb Set Up Move: Coglini Driver ( Sitdown Butterfly Powerbomb ) Finisher: Damer Kick (Superkick) OSW Title History: n/a OSW Award History: n/a Other Federations: Motor City Wrestling Other Federation Awards: MCW World Heavyweight Champion, MCW World Television Champion Handler Information Handler Name: Dom AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: Bio Michael has received various trainings. When he was 7, he began his interest in wrestling. Immediatly his grandfather, a study of the sport taught him the basics. From there on in, wrestling was all he did. His parents bought him a trampoline, and he immitated various moves he saw on NWA, and WWF programming. The age flew up to 14, and he had perfected 'his' art of wrestling. He joined the wrestling team in his first year of high school. He realized it was a joke, although used the sport for training on his physical endurance. He held up decently in the sport he entitled 'The complete opposite of professional wrestling'. He also took a weekend program, provided by the school, in Karate. May sound basic, but it was far from it. It was listed as Karate, but mixed together various forms of submission, kicks, blows, and maneuvers you would never see in wrestling. He took this this class all through high school. When he was 16, and began to drive he enrolled in one of the longest standing wrestling schools in Michigan 'Top Ropes'. He excelled greatly, and was thrilled about his progress. His knowledge of the Martial Arts served to excell him in all aspects from agility, to a regular punch. His trainer, Don Conlingi, told him he had something special, and that was it. From there on in, wrestling was his life. He even went as far to paying the school's head trainer to stay late, to help him. He trained here non stop, and even did a bit of teaching himself until he was given his chance to become a professional. He recieved a shot in MCW, Motor City Wrestling. The biggest indy league out of the Detroit Area. Traveled far into Minnesota, and was televised in both states. Dame was a bit nervous one his first day, being only 22, but this was soon to change. When he met Gloria. Michael met Gloria the first day he was at MCW. She has been managing for a while now, and everyone knew of her capabilities. She had gotten the short end of the stick in all the cases, and was becoming annoyed. Although with Michael, Right away she saw in him the elite. The exact same thing is trainer saw. She is a determined woman. And for that matter, a trustable woman. And Michael liked this. He liked to have a valued person in his corner, that he didn't fear would turn on him. Gloria got him chances he may have missed out on, and he thanks her countlessly for this. There friendship grew extremly large, but never larger. He was a married man, and Gloria respected that, althogh sometimes she didn't have to. Michael Dame grew over the next three years. All the way to the top, when he won the Motor City Championship! There was no title higher then what he was, and for that matter, no person more popular. His fan base was through the roof, a great number that couldn't be measured. He was a loved man, a respected man, and a well presented man. He was in fact, a role model to everyone. Parents wanted their children to grow up and be like him. He was in fact, the man of the century to everyone that knew of him. Although, this was all soon to change, all over the coarse of a couple hours. In 1997 Michael Dame was a loved man. By his friends, family, especially his wife, and definitly his fans. He was a professional wrestler that loved the sport and loved everything back that has cast emotion on him. He didn't care about the money, cause if he did he could have gotten paid the millions like Steve Austin and The People's Champ. He was happy in Motor City Wrestling, touring the states of Minnesota and Michigan. He was a true face, the way they should be. He performed at the top of his ability even if he was hurt. He strived for more, even if it resulted in injury. Although that was all about to change. He was out late one night. Wrestling a house show for one of the biggest rowds ever in a MCW arena. He was world champion, and being cheered at every corner. He won his match that night, and elected to drive home to see his wife and children. One 4 years and one 9 months. It was late by the time he arrived home, but his night was not about to end. Not by a long-shot. He pulled up to the front of his beautiful house, to find several police cars parked outside. Right away he could feel the emotion on his street and was shocked. He immediatly turned pale and confused. He exited his car and rushed to the front door as suddenly his wife was being hoisted out of the house. She immedietly saw him and started screaming 'There he is, arrest him!'... and 'He's the drug dealer'... Dame's feeling of shock turned quickly into utter confusion. He couldn't believe what he was hearing! The police officers immediatly put him under arrest for the possession and intention to distribute 20 kilo's of cocaine. The car ride has begun, but he hadn't noticed. He still was shocked, and barely was able to mutter 'It wasn't me'... The officers saidnothing. He was put into a holding cell, to where he would spend the night in jail, and in the morning see his lawyer. The night went by with only one word in Dame's head, and that was 'Why?'... He could ask it to everything that has just happened. His children, his wife, his career...every thought ended with the question why?! But there would be no answers. His thoughts were left blank as he was completly mis-informed. Until the morning came, and his lawyer arrived. The Lawyer and himself sit down in a interogation room. About to discuss the matters. His lawyer opens with, 'Do you know why I'm here?' Dame Mutters, 'I do'...pauses...'but I don't'.. The conversation quickly moves to the lawyer informing Dame that he has been under keen obvservation from the FBI for circulating half the distribution of cocaine in the Northern United States. Thoughts quickly raced through his head and he sput out numerous things. 'It wasn't me', and 'It has to be some sort of set up' and of coarse, 'Why?'... The laywer just answered with 'There is no answers for what is going on, but there is facts. 1)There was 20 kilo's of cocaine in your house. 2) Every night at 11 pm a man with your exact description leaves your house. Carrying a breifcase, and continues to a local club, 'The Nightly Dancer', which is also under suspicion of being owned by some high officials in the underground crime ring. And 3) You mostly travel the Northern United States with a lot of money at your dispense. ... So things are not looking good for you. Dame quickly, but reluctantly said 'But what about my wife, you don't think it was her?'... The laywer went on to speak that his wife never leaves the house. The evidence is piled up on him, and barley anything against his wife... He ends his comments with 'What would you belive', and Dame Said... 'I Don't know.' And that's exactly what he knew, nothing. He was not a drug dealer, he was a wrestler and always have been. He was set up, by his wife or someone to take him out of the picture. He hadn't known why, or how, but he known he had been set up. OR had he? Although he asked these questions, he didn't really care. It didn't matter, all that did is when the case came around, it didn't go his way. They fought for all that they could, but he was found guilty and sentence to the maximum sentence of the offense, 7 years. His sentence began right away. All he could think of was two things. How this could happen, and his children. He had a feeling his wife had something to do with it, so he tried to avoid the thought of her. But his two children, one not even able to speak yet, and the other. His star daughter, his first into the world. She was everything, and could never lose her. But there was no choice, two weeks into his sentence his lawyer visited him and informed him his wife was filing a divorce. That the court would award her half of the assets and full custody of the children. Dame could say nothing, but sign off to the papers. He sat in his cell last night and thought about everything. His family, his reputation, his career, what he would do when he left prison. All lost or ruined, but one thing stuck out to him and he thought on it repeatedly. His wife... How she can not say anything to him. Try and see him, and ask him what has happened. He also found it shocking that a divorce was filed 2 weeks AFTER he was sentenced, instead of when it initially happened. But none of it mattered. The only thing in his life was his children and his fans. He had to see his children grow up, and help doing so. He was set on the idea. And his fans had to understand, in some way shape or form that he was not the man they portray him to be. Everyday he worked out. He kept himself in great shape. And made some friends. Things were looking good for him, and they just got a bit better. Fast Forward 4 years. The court has approved Michael Dame for a parole interview in which his initial sentencing of 7 years could be shortend due to good behavior. That interview was a week away. And Mike was ready for it. He explained that he would be working for work in a small wrestling promotion. Working to send his children money for child support and just because he wanted to. He was asked if he would think to attempt another crime and the anser was I never did, and if you belive I did, then I never will again! The parole interviewers approved his parole and he was free to go. The date was June 1st, 2001. He was freed under the sentence of being stricken to the state of Minnesota for six months. He immediatly began to look for work, but he quickly realized his reputation was a lot worse then what he thought. He was a liar, a theif, a drug dealer, a killer to just about everyone that was a fan or knew of him. OR for that matter, knew someone who who him. It was going to be an uphill battle for him, and all he needed was a chance. With the right chance he can prove to people that he is not sick and sadistic, that he really is the guy they once cheered for and loved, and whom his children kissed goodnight after himself driving 4 hours speeding to make it their before they're put to sleep.
