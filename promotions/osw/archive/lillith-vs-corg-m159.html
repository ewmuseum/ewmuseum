Lillith vs Corg

2002-07-07
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lillith vs Corg Written: July 07, 2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam! Robbie: Can we PLEASE see a match? Nate: Yes, we can, and will, as we are just about ready to head to the ring for our big cruiserweight title match, which features Queen of Extreme winner, Lillith, will take on the OSW Cruiserweight Champion, Corg. Robbie: I hope Corg brought condoms. Nate blinks in disbelief. Nate: This could very well be Corg’s last appearance on OSW television, because he will likely be fired after tonight. Robbie: She loved it. She asked for it. Nate: (disgusted) Let’s send it up to the ring and ring announcer Earl Henrich. 'Scum of the Earth' by Rob Zombie suddenly erupts over the speakers, and the fans rise to their feet in great anticipation. The rarely seen, yet giften Lillith steps out from the back recieving a huge reaction from the crowd. Almost ignoring them all, her features remain emotionless and her eyes locked on the squared circle. After sliding into the ring, she leans back into a corner and lifts a single scar covered arm high into the air, much to the fans' delight. Nate: Here comes Lillith! Robbie: Kill Corg, Lillith! Steven: Go to hell, Lillith However, a good reaction from lyrics turn to an almost unimaginable amount of boos as 'Machines' by Crossbreed reverberates throughout the Reunion Arena. Cups filled with beer, eggs, and trash are all hurled at Corg as he steps out from the back. He merely blows a kiss to the crowd, before walking down the entrance ramp. A disgruntled, drunken fan charges the Quiet Killer after jumping the entrance ramp, however he is decked by Corg's clinched right fist. Jeers continue to flood toward the squared circle as Corg enters, however, his legs are sweeped rightout from under him, leaving the fans to cheer and the bell to the ring. Steven: NO! Robbie: Woo! That's it, rock his world Lillith! Nate: ... Steven: ... Robbie: ...Sorry. Corg reaches his feet, ducks a spin kick, and literally slaps the taste out of Lillith's mouth. His trademark sadistic grin spreads across his features as he lays the boots to Lillith's sternum. Lillith soon finds a way out and catches his boot, and frees her left arm to send a punch straight into Corg's testicules! The Anti-God drops to his knees in pain while Lillith reaches her feet. Just as Corg nearly stands, he hits the ground like a ton of bricks as Lillith sends a stiff jumping sidekick straight into Corg's head! Nate: Talk about 'Ouch!' Robbie: No doubt! Corg yelps in pain, holding the back of his head with his face down into the mat. Lillith stoops down, and grabs a handful of spikes, bending his cranium back so he can see her. Lillith: 'Well, you horny little bitch. You want to fuck? DO YOU WANT TO FUCK? Well congratulations... You're fucked!' Steven: YOU GO GUUUUUURL! Lillith slams his face into the canvas over and over again, until blood covers his pale face. As she stands up, the Goth drags Corg with her. While he's hunched over, she whips him into the ropes, and follows up with a roundhouse kick straight to Corg's chest and follows up on the staggering Quiet Killer with another sidekick, straight to Corg's genitals! Corg starts to drop, but she sends him railing backwards with a spin kick to his throat! Nate: Jeez, she's killing him! Robbie: I know, I know! Corg's in hell. Before he even has time to breath she sends stiff sidekicks into his chest, each one leaving red marks seen under his mesh shirt. As Lillith stoops down and begins punching his face in, Corg desperately rakes her eyes with one of the spikes on his bracelet! The fans boo at the top of their lungs as Lillith grabs her face and drops to the mat! Corg slowly stands up, and sends kicks of his own into Lillith's breasts. The Quiet Killer shoves his boot into Lillith's face and puts all his weight on it. Lillith tries to fight out, however, a likely hero would charge toward the ring... Nate: IT'S RENOOOOOOOO! Reno Davis on a skateboard, with a cast on his leg and crutches in hand! The fans' jeers take a suddden jolt of electricity and becomes the loudest cheers of the night. Acrobatically, Reno jumps from his skateboard and hops onto the ring apron! Nate: Corg has no idea Reno's only three feet away! He's too busy taunting Lillith! Robbie: Turn around Corg! Turn around! CRACK! Corg hits the ground after the wooden crutch cracks over his head. Reno enters the ring, and beats the living hell out of Corg with that crutch! Crack, crack, crack, crack, crack! The bell rings, and Corg retains via disqualification, however, that doesn't stop Reno. Davis is acting like a true blue mad man, continues his assault on Corg, but once again, Corg finds a cheap way out by punching Reno on his cast covered leg, causing him to drop like a sack of shit. Like a coward, the Quiet Killer slides out of the ring and grabs his belt. Facing the ring as he backs up the entrance ramp, he doesn't see Civil Disobedience and Sebastian Toomes standing behind him. However, as he lifts his arms into the air, he freezes in motion... His head slowly turns... And takes off running through the crowd, easily escaping a beating….as the scene fades to commercial.
