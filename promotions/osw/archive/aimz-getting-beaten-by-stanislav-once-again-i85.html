Aimz getting beaten by Stanislav........once again.

2002-05-04
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aimz getting beaten by Stanislav........once again. Written: May 4th, 2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam!!! We are back after witnessing the end result of what appeared to be an attack against Aimz by Ivan Stanislav! Robbie: But we have also gotten word from our production truck that the incident was actually caught on tape but due to Davis’ involvement in the ring, it could not be aired! Steven: But we have the tape now!! Lets see what happened!! Nate: That was a nice transition guys, good job! The Jumbotron whirs to life again as we are taken back just minutes ago backstage of Saturday Slam… Amy Campbell, better known as Aimz to the OSW fans, stands backstage around what appears to be the area that all the lighting equipment is stored for the Pepsi Center. She stands in a circle with four other guys playing a game of hacky sack. She laughs and rebounds the bag to another guy with her heel just as a shadow falls on the men and everyone, including Aimz, turns as one and looks. The Rusisan Bear, Ivan Stanislav, stands in front of them and growls as the men gather up their tools and seemingly disappear, leaving Ivan alone with Aimz. Ivan: Amy Campbell….Ivan has been looking for you…. Aimz: Ummm........hi. Whatcha' need? By the way...sorry to hear about Tempest, I think she sorta took things a little too harshly...... Ivan breathes out heavily through his nose. Ivan: Interesting you should bring that up, Amy. If I remember correctly, you were part of the ‘coalition’ with Timo, Dame, Ed…and Tempest…right? Aimz: Yup! I kinda seem to be the only one left...but you've got all hundred and forty three pounds of me if you need backup. Ivan nods as his features harden and walks closer to Aimz. Ivan: That is interesting Aimz, being that you are only one left. It appears that you have remained ‘true’ to the communist cause, if you will, eh? Even after Timo left and Ed disassociated with us and Dame disappeared and….Tempest….left…you stayed close to us. Ivan pauses and swallows audibly. Ivan: However, I also notice that you do not seem to care much for communism, or for the situation within the OSW. So I wonder, why would you seem to stay near Red Army and hold on for so long when all of your ‘friends’ are gone? Ivan takes another loud, heavy step towards Aimz. She backs up nervously and walks into a wall. Aimz: I might not support your screwed up system, but I support your cause. Remember man, I wanna see Toomes, Reagan, Bones, Hostyle and all the other idiots taken down just as badly as you do. Ivan growls. Ivan: Mmmmhmmm. I noticed that you have met with Reno Davis….what is your excuse for that? Aimz: We're into the same culture, same music...nobody else in OSW's down with that kind of thing. I know you don't like him and all...but he's been cool to me, we can relate to one another. It's not like we're best friends or anything. Ivan growls as his upper lip curls and he points a finger at Aimz.. Ivan: You know what I think, Amy Campbell? I think that you didn’t remain close to me because you wanted to ‘bring down’ anyone. I don’t think you stayed near me so that you could continue to fight. I think you did it because you wanted to break up Tempest and I! That is right! You are in league with Reno Davis! YOU HAVE BEEN A COLLABORATOR ALL ALONG!! Aimz: WHAT!? Why would I...DUDE! No no no!!! I'm not into that backstabbing bull!!! Ivan growls as a crazy look flashes over his face and he shoots his massive hand out, grabbing Aimz by the jaw painfully and lifting her up in the air. She kicks and screams as Ivan holds her at arms length from him. Ivan: You wanted to destroy what we had! You wanted to hurt me when I did nothing but protect you! You took her from me! You did this to me!! Aimz: No!!! LET GO!!! Ivan roars and pulls Aimz towards him, only to slam the back of her head into the concrete wall behind him! She groans loudly as her skull bounces off the concrete. Ivan: You will pay for what you have done! You have been ally of Davis all along! Ivan growls and tosses Aimz across the room into a nearby wall where she falls into a pile of large metal lights. Ivan walks over slowly as Aimz groans weakly. Ivan: I will destroy you for what you did to me….I will wipe out all that have tried to hurt Ivan Stanislav and Tempest! Stanislav reaches down and lifts Aimz by her hair with one hand. He rears back with his hammer like fist and SLAMS it into her face. Blood flies from her mouth and nose as she screams weakly and her head falls forward limply. Ivan grins sadistically and punches her again in the face, thoroughly bloodying the rest of her face! Ivan looks over his shoulder and sees the camera and points. Ivan: YOU! GET OVER HERE! I HAVE SOMETHING I WANT YOU TO SEE!! The Jumbotron shuts off suddenly as we are back to the announce position. Nate: Ladies and Gentlemen, that was sickening! We need to head to a commercial. We will be right back after these messages.
