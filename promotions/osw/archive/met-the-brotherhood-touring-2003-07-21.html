The Brotherhood Touring?

21 July 2003

by The Brotherhood

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own news post (#17) by The Brotherhood, dated 21 July 2003, from the MET II site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
MET Competitors Xander Pollack and Garrett Hollister, together known as The Brotherhood, made it incredibly clear to Old School Entertainment when they accepted the invite sent to them regarding the Most Extreme Team contest that they weren't simply wrestlers and often pursued other career options, which could conflict with their responsibilities as far as the competition is concerned. Now it seems as if one of these options has reportedly shown its ugly face. The Brotherhood, who used to be full-fledged members of Action! Wrestling, have reportedly spoken with colleges across the United States about doing some public speaking on their campuses, which sources close to them say they may accept because of their urge to spread the message of who Xander Pollack calls the Great Power. However, when approached with these allegations, Xander, who is the tandem's spokesperson and leader, neither denied or confirmed them, but did respond to OSE officials' questions quite confidently. 'I'm not saying that we do plan to do a tour of schools across the country, and I'm not saying that we don't.' He said over the phone from his home in Boston, which serves as a headquarters of sorts for The Brotherhood. 'If we are though, it won't interfere with The Brotherhood winning this competition, if you can even call it that. We can do two things at once and still do them to our fullest capabilities. After all, we have one thing that these other teams don't, a thing that allows us to take on more things then the average and pitiful human. We have Him on our side!' Old School Entertainment fired back, however, stating clearly to Mr. Pollack that doing things of that nature and magnitude while under temporary contract with the company is a breach of contract and will be cause for punishment. Xander shrugged this off. 'Only the Great Power can tell me what to do and only He can punish me when I don't do it. Anyone else is fair game.' More on this as it develops.
