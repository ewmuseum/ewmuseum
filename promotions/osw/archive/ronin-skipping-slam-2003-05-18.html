Ronin skipping Slam?

18 May 2003

by Ronin

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own news post (#51) by Ronin, dated 18 May 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Word out of Chicago late tonight has the Old School Entertainment Tag Team Champions Ronin not at the new OSE Arena. 'We got a call from Jeffrey Wayne's assistant about two hours ago,' says spokesperson Shanda Russell, 'and he claimed that neither man is going to be there. He said it was because of moving difficulties, they're still finding proper accommodations in the city. I don't believe a word of it, but there's not much we can do now.' Wayne and his partner Matt Register were not booked on the program, and were not needed for any other purposes. However, given their championship status, it is expected of them to be there to set an example. There are several rumors backstage as to their no-show. One is that they are avoiding a possible rematch with Black Attack, who were unable to compete as a team last week due to legal issues. However, the more interesting one has it that the two are trying to avoid any sort of legal or punitive punishments as a result of Register's attack on a fan - namely former OSW Tag Champion Trevor Hawke - during last week's show. Russell did not mention anything about Hawke, whose hometown of Aurora, Ill., is well within driving distance of Chicago. She did, however, squash the chances of ronin escaping punishment. 'We're levying a fine against the team for their absence,' she said. 'It will hopefully teach them a lesson about respect and professionalism for the future.'
