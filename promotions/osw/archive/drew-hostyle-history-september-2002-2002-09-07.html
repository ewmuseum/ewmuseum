Drew Hostyle — history (September 2002)

7 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History As a child in Brooklyn, New York in 1980, Drew Hostyle wasn't like other kids around him. Hell, his whole family wasn't like others. After their father left them at a small age, it was up to Drew to become the man of his own house and take care of his mother and his brother that was two years younger than him, Jordan. After his mother started giving favors to the gansters in a gang called the Benefit. Drew changed his whole outlook on life. He couldn't only attend the school, he had to own it and as an adult he is the same exact way, he MUST own everything he's a part of. Because of his outrageous attics to both his teachers and fellow students, earlier in life he was sent to a juvenile detention home, and later on jail, to have personal guidance with his aggression problems. His mother then died, partly because of the stress she was being put through with Drew's problems, but partly because of reasons that Drew'd never understand within the Beenfit. Also with this he lost custody of his younger brother Jordan and he got adopted by another family who could better provide for him, a white family in Toronto, Canada. The best solution they could come up with to solve Drew's problem in New York, though was wrestling, then he could tear people limb from limb, conquer things and it would all be legal. Drew Hostyle continued to train himself in the art of wrestling even after he was released from jail and began to do it as a profession until he was arrested again for being too brutal during one match-up for an indy federation. After being released from jail a second time, he decided that he'd need to find people who were just extreme as he could be and who wanted dominance just as much as he did. Soon after he met up with a man who would become a long time friend, 'Epic' Carlos Serpico, but after they both went through training in the Primetime Wrestling Academy, where first teacher, the HIT and Oracle both took part in his training there. Epic's and Drew's careers then parted ways and Epic continued his term and soon went on to acheive bigger things, as Drew left for a while to return to the streets and became apart of a 'cult' of some sorts, which had essentially brain-washed him, and he still belongs to it today, trying to overcame the pressures of this gang and sucess in the PTC has both granted him Number#1 ratings while in the PWA a second time with his new headmaster, El Diablo, eventually graduation, and a brief stints in the NTICW and HIW. While in talks with the OSW last year though, in Queens, New York Drew was reunited with his brother. There was no big sobfest, however both had wondered and worried about the other and now they wouldn't have to worry anymore. Taken his newly given role as a big brother again and role model-esque figure, Drew appointed Jordan to the same industry to get him off the same streets that been home ot him for so long. Jordan was given membereship to the Benefit and worked his way through the PWA as Drew'd advised him too. Jordan is now in the NTICW and the two keep in close touch with each other from their seperate federations and homes in Brooklyn and Queens, New York. And now he's in the OSW just to show why he says 'By HOOK or by CROOK, it's ALL in the BOOK!' While in the OSW, Drew's formed a stable with first ever OSW World Champion Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes and Frankie Bones. All three men have and are continuing on their ways to sucess, Drew's been in a number of matches from the OSW's first ever Slam, where he took on 'Black Ice' Matt Blaze and lost. To then going on to face his future partners in Frankie Bones and Switchblade with jesse ramey as his partner. The pairing seemed to be fatal as Drew ended up turning on his team and watched as his partners got awarded the win to the match. He's also been in tag team matches since then with his partner Frankie Bones. The two are currently undefeated in tag team competition and individually Drew's record is nearly as perfect, losing matches to only Matt Blaze and Rhett Locke at the OSW's first ever PPV for him to go on to become International Champion. Current Angle: Drew Hostyle and his partners in crime Frankie Bones, Sebastian 'Switchblade' Toomes, 'The monster they call' Meanstreak and OSW owner, Jane Reagan have united to take down their common enemies in the names of Amy 'Aimz' Campbell, Tempest and The Red Army. The obvious battle over good vs. evil, with Drew on the side of evil is bound to turn ugly in more than one way throughout this lasting feud, and you can only tune in to catch up on the latest.
