Amanda Real Name: Amanda Lynn Sampson Birthdate: June 4, 1981 Hometown: Orlando, Florida Height: 5'7½ Weight: 115 lbs. Nicknames: 'Mandy' by some of her friends and people who don't know any better. Deragatory names by her enemies. Status: Face Manager(s): Current Teams: Entrance Music: 'Glory' by Sugar Ray Wrestling Style: Supportive valet Signature Moves: Set Up Move: Yes ( ) Finisher: No () OSW Title History: OSW Award History: Queen of Extreme 3rd runner-up Other Federations: UWA (Universal Wrestling Alliance) KW (Kinetic Wrestling) CFW (Cloudy Future Wrestling) PCW (primetime Championship Wrestling) Note: All as valet of Kid Wonder Other Federation Awards: PCW manager/valet of the year runner-up (2001) Handler Information Handler Name: AIM Name: MSN Name: Bio Amanda Lynn Sampson was born on June 4th, 1981 in Orlando, Florida. Amanda, the second of four kids, had a normal childhood. Never got involved with drugs or alcohol. Never got into fights. Never got into trouble with the law. She was a good kid. Her 'girl next door' image made her likeable to the people around her. And Amanda's natural beauty made the boys attracted to her. She was just a normal girl with no intentions of getting involved with the wrestling business. By time Amanda was 16, she had developed a close relationship with her brother, Kyle Sampson. Kyle, who is three years older than Amanda, was friends with a girl by the name of Christina 'Christy' Jenkins. Both of them were huge wrestling fans. And on October 22nd, 1997, after much convincing, they took Amanda to her first wrestling event. It was an independent show in Winter Park, Florida. And while Amanda didn't exactly enjoy the show, the trip was worthwhile. After the show, Christy introduced to Amanda to a close friend of hers by the name of James Spinks, better known as Kid Wonder. The two immediately clicked. They started to see some more of one another and eventually became a couple. And the rest is, well, history. Despite the fact she was dating an aspiring wrestler, Amanda had no intentions of getting into the sport herself. It just wasn't her thing. But after her boyfriend was jumped, attacked with a crowbar, and put out of action with a serious knee injury, it allowed Amanda a chance to get closer to her man. And the time she spent with Kid Wonder helped Amanda change her outlook on wrestling. And once her boyfriend was cleared to wrestle again, Amanda found herself going to more and more shows. And with Amanda there, Kid Wonder found himself performing better and better. As if Amanda's presence provided some sort of inspiration. But Amanda still had no plans of getting into wrestling. After graduating from high school in 1999, Amanda was given an interesting proposal. And after some convincing, she decided to become her boyfriend's valet. She started to accompany Kid Wonder to the ring, and in no time he started to perform at a level that no one could imagine. And as his star started to take off, Amanda stood by his side. And she ended up doing something that she never intended on doing. Amanda found herself in the wrestling industry. In April of 2000, Kid Wonder went from the independents to the big time. But it didn't get any bigger than in October 2000 when they went to the new and fresh Primetime Championship Wrestling. And in PCW, Kid Wonder was able to build a legacy. But while he did that, Amanda started to make a name for herself as well. Even though she'd be pushed around by big guys who were too scared of K-Dub, or looking to get at her boyfriend (Ivan Stanislav, Boda, etc.), Amanda's dignity, class, and great personality made her stick out among the valets in the sport. After a while, however, the joy ride started to end. With guys such as Tyler Nelson, and later Duke Williams running the show, they would allow people to run around and take cheap shots at Kid Wonder. She got into this to have fun. And when K-Dub stopped having fun, so did she. And on March 10th, 2002, the couple left the place they helped turn into a household name. Looking to find the fun in the wrestling business again, Amanda has decided to try her hand at in-ring action. And what better way to start than the OSW Queen of Extreme tournament. Amanda was the Queen of PCW despite what other people thought or said. Now she's gonna shoot to become the Queen of OSW, too. Amanda has only been in a few matches in her life. And, realistically, she doesn't stand much of a chance, but that means nothing to her. Amanda has heard the criticism directed towards her boyfriend and seen him overcome it. Now she plans on doing the same thing. Amanda has been around success long enough to know what it takes to be a winner. Whether she succeeds herself remains to be seen. But whatever happens, watching Amanda compete in a wrestling ring, even if it's a one time deal, should be a real treat to watch and something no wrestling fan will soon forget.
