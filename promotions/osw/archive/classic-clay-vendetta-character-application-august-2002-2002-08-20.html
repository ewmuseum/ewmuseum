'Classic' Clay Vendetta — character application (August 2002)

20 August 2002

'Classic' Clay Vendetta Real Name: Clayton Battaglia Nick Name: 'CCV' Catchphrase: "It won't just be a match... it'll be a classic!" Manager: Niki Weathers Description: Tall, blonde, and gorgeous, Weathers stands three inches taller than Vendetta; don't let the hair (or the piercing blue eyes) fool you, though; by all accounts she's the brains in the relationship. Character Handler: Matt D. MSN Account: [detail withheld] AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Carry on Wayward Son" by Kansas Date Of Birth: 3/31/1974 From: Miami, FL Height: 5' 11" Weight: 225 Wrestling Style: Technician with a mean streak (no, not Meanstreak) Signature Moves: the Vendetta Drop (inverted DDT); the South Beach Suplex (leg-hook German suplex); the Classic Anklelock (anklelock (done classically)) Finisher Move: Crowd Control (Dragon Sleeper with a leg grapevine) Set-Up Move: The Throwback (180 vertical brainbuster) Current Team: None Past Teams & Groups: None Title History: None Awards History: None Description: If the picture isn't enough to handle it for you, Vendetta is of medium-build for his short size, and wears long gray tights with 'Classic' emblazoned on the side. The right elbow brace is result of surgery to repair a torn ligament. Vendetta is often seen smirking, both before and after his victories, and wears his dyed-blond hair with plenty of gel for that "classic" sheen. History: Born in March of 1974 as Clayton Battaglia, Clay Vendetta was part of a rich Italian family living in upstate New York. Clay was always a spoiled kid; but athletic, and quickly became a bully amongst the weaker kids, often stealing lunch money just so he could throw it in the trash can and laugh. The smaller kids were afraid of him, and the threats of having his parents sue were enough to scare away the larger ones. Vendetta got into amateur wrestling at an early age and led his high school to three conference championships in the four years he was at the school. Wrestling for FSU, Clay was quickly overmatched. This wasn't a result of his lack of skill so much as him temper, which got him ejected from a team record five bouts in his first season, after which he was asked not to return. After a public shouting match with the coach, he stormed off the squad, lighting his singlet on fire. Though he didn't wrestle, he graduated the school with a degree in communications (though most of the four years were spent partying: his GPA barely squeaking through the graduation requirements). As a college student, Clay often used his large (though short) frame as a bouncer for local clubs and establishments, a career he'd keep as a second job after graduation. With his degree in tow, Clay moved to Miami, working odd jobs but never finding major work. Still, he made enough money to get by working the clubs on South Beach. Battaglia quickly grew restless for competition, though. After dropping the wrestling team in college he had taken basic martial arts (from which he was expelled by the sensai once more for his total disregard for the other students) and joined an underground fighting club. With his new name, Clay Vendetta, he quickly made a reputation for himself as a ruthless fighter with a great grasp of submission tactics. Clayton grew up watching professional wrestling, admiring the work of many of the legends of the time, but wasn't involved with the business until being introduced to the IWA's Tommy Silver one night at a club. Silver, the IWA's Continental champion at the time, offered a bodyguard position to Clay with a hefty salary, and Vendetta quickly accepted. He began work as Silver's ring escort; but when the promoter saw him dispatch some of Silver's opposition he was offered a contract on top of the personal services contract from Silver and began work in the IWA. Silver gave Vendetta the Continental title so he could take a stab at the World Title, and Vendetta successfully defended the title fourteen times before dropping it to Taylor "The Icebreaker" Post in a steel cage. In the meantime, Vendetta was instrumental in getting Silver the IWA World Championship, interfering in a title match against Nightfall so Silver could win the belt. The combined egos of Silver and Vendetta became too much to stay together for long after Vendetta felt he owned a part of the championship; and soon Vendetta cost Silver the title by laying him out mid-match. The fans quickly got behind Silver, but in a triple threat match soon after, Vendetta won the IWA World Championship, making Silver tap out. Contract squabbles followed, and the promoter fired Vendetta in a very public split. Clay bounced through three indy feds afterwards, never gaining much of a foothold with his reputation until he hit the NDWF, a larger indy fed in the Southeast. The NDWF was heavily into a new age style: hardcore and high flying, and it disgusted the very traditional Vendetta. Christening himself "Classic" Clay Vendetta, CCV went on a tear, wining their World Title within six months while ending two careers in the process and injuring three others. His submissions-based style and arrogant attitude won over no fans but did endear himself to one of the valets, the beautiful Niki Weathers, who ditched her boyfriend to accompany Vendetta. Soon Vendetta had become a feared name among many of the indy feds along the East Coast, and the owner of Old School Wrestling found out about Vendetta on a scouting mission. Offering him a lucrative contract (the largest of his career), he signed Vendetta and Weathers to be charter members on the OSW roster, where Vendetta expects nothing less than global acclaim. Interviewed about the signing, CCV had this to say: "Old School Wrestling? Those poor schmucks don't have a clue what Old School is. There's a reason they call me "Classic", and all of OSW is about to find out exactly why I am the one true throwback. CCV is here to stay; and I feel sorry for the rest of the roster."
