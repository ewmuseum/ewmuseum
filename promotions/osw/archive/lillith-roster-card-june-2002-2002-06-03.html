AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio Lillian Duran was your typical outcast teenager. Her dark clothes and strange habits labelled her early on as a freak, and though outwordly she accetped herself regardless of people's opinions, inside she was hurt that people could be so shallow. As her mood became darker, he self-esteem dropped. Rarely did she venture out other than school. She remained locked in her room, sitting in the dark and listening to depressing music. It was during this time she discovered that physical pain could drive away her emotional pain for a short time, and began to periodically cut her body with a striagt-edge razor. Eventually, this became as addicting as a narcotic, and soon her arms were criss-crossed with scars. Sensing something wrong with their daughter, Lillian's parents decided to enrole her into a judo class. Not only would this get her out of the house for a short period each week, but it was hoped that it would strength her self-esteem. The results were from from what was expected - not only did Lillian excel as it, she enjoyed more than anything else she'd ever done. That one night a week grew to 5 per week, as she strived to improve her body and mind. During her senior year, Lillian became aware of what life was like around her. She began to see that people were, on the whole, not very nice to her or anyone else, for that matter. Especially at school. not that she was considered athletic, she tried to join some of the school activities, and was laughed at for being a freak. The last straw for Lillith was when she tried to join the amateur wrestling team, for which there was no women's division. After even the coach of the team laugh at her, she decided then and there she'd had enough. Unfortunately, the boys making fun of her hadn't, and tried to stop her from leaving, resulting in her hurting 4 or 5 before she was beat down. Eventually, the coach regained his sense of responsibility, but the damage had been done. Lillian was now the victim of assault. Once again, her life had taken a turn for the worse, and although she hurt on the outside, it was on the inside where she felt the most pain. Lillian eventually dropped out of her senior year, and dedicated herself to perfecting her chosen art. Taking a small job teaching kids at the YMCA, Lillian eentually rose to the level of Hachidan in Judo (8th degree black belt), when she decided on a career change. Seeing the advertisement for the OSW Queen of Extreme, Lillian decided it was time to take out her frustrations out on other people, and signed up. Promo Archives Women.... evil?
