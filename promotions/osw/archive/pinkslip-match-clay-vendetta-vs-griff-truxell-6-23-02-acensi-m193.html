Pinkslip Match: Clay Vendetta vs. Griff Truxell (6/23/02 - Acension)

2002-06-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pinkslip Match: Clay Vendetta vs. Griff Truxell (6/23/02 - Acension) Written: 6/23/02 Clay Vendetta vs. Griff Truxell Match Result: Win Via: Submission (Crowd Control) Stipulations: 'Pinkslip Match': Win via submission only. Winner gets rights to both Vendetta's '63 Corvette and Truxell's custom limo. Notes: The blow-off match. Nate: Welcome back to Ascension. The medical staff is attending to Jim Axtell in the back, but we’re just about ready for our next match, another OSW first. In fact, this is the first time I’ve seen this match in my history in the business... Robbie, you’ve been around for a while, how about you? Robbie: What are you talking about? I have to sit through jobber matches every single week! Where the hell have YOU been? Nate: Sigh... what I was talking about, ladies and gentlemen, is a “pinkslip match”. Clay Vendetta and Griff Truxell will put an end to their long-brewing feud tonight... and the winner will drive home with BOTH Griff Truxell’s custom Rolls-Royce limousine as well as Clay Vendetta’s vintage 1963 Chevrolet Corvette. Vendetta’s in the ring now, and motioning for a microphone... One is tossed to Clay, who’s standing in his wrestling gear in the center of the ring. Reaching into a back pocket, he pulls out the pinkslip and lays it down in the center of the ring as the crowd cheers... Vendetta: One 1963 Corvette. Truxell, if you want it, tonight, you’re gonna have to take it outta my hands. I’ve invested more time into that car than you’ve done anything in your life, Truxell. I’ll be DAMNED if I’m giving it up to you tonight. Last week on Slam, you went after one of the two most important things in my life... now I’m bringing them both to ringside. The sound of an engine revving can be heard... and from a side entrance a ring of security walks out, encircling the candy-apple-red Chevy Corvette, being driven out into the arena by none other than Niki Weathers, who draws a HUGE ovation from the crowd. Parking the car at a safe distance from the ring, Niki steps out of the car, blows a kiss to the crowd, and sits on the hood. Vendetta: You know, Truxell; even though I wouldn’t object to beating you to within an inch of your life tonight, I’m not a heartless bastard. I mean; tonight I’m just doing you a favor, right? You’ve gotta pay your dues in this business and tonight I’m extracting them from you. But you know something? Even though you’ve cost me matches... jumped me from behind... and attacked Niki like the worthless son of a bitch you are... I’d hate for you to have to walk home tonight. That’s why I brought you a little something. Climbing off the hood, Niki goes around to the trunk of the car, inserting the key and popping it open. Security reaches into the back and produces... ... a rusty moped?? They roll it out as Niki hands the keys to an OSW official as the prize for the winner. Nate: Haha! What an act of courtesy by Vendetta! Robbie: Yeah, well, that midget’s not going to be laughing when he’s riding that thing home tonight and Truxell’s got his car up on eBay. Smirking, Vendetta tosses the microphone out of the ring and stretches against the ropes as “Ante Up” blasts over the speakers. The SchoolTron cuts to a shot of Griff Truxell stepping out of a limousine in the back, having his chaffeur hand the keys to the officials for keeping. The normal smirk on Truxell’s face is replaced by an angry look as he steps out of the curtains... and charging the ring, he slides in, pops up... *DING DING DING* ... and the two immediately start swinging at each other as Referee Pierre Gaston starts the match! Nate: And immediately Vendetta and Truxell being to exchange blows! Robbie: Steven, whatever you’re thinking right now... realize that despite the fact that Nate is an idiot and probably should be slapped himself for setting you up like that... any comments out of your mouth will earn you an abrupt and severe beating. Steven: *gulp*... understood. Nate: While those two continue, I’d like to let everyone know about the rules for this pinkslip match... it’s very simple. No countouts, no disqualifications; the only way to win is to make your opponent surrender the match.... and the pinkslip... by tapping-out. Robbie: So basically, it’s a submission match with cars on the line. Nate: No, it’s an FIRST EVER IN OSW OR PRESUMABLY ANY OTHER FED PINKSLIP MATCH, thank you very much. Robbie: Man, I wish I had a monster truck announcer voice. Back apart from the ringside shenanigans, there’s a heated match going on in the ring, where Griff Truxell has used his height and leverage advantage to power Vendetta into the corner. Laying into him with kneelifts, Truxell rears back and throws a headbutt into Vendetta’s midsection... and goes for another, but Vendetta dodges, leaving Truxell to go headfirst into the turnbuckle! Groggy, Truxell takes a quick step back and is met by Vendetta, who drops low and shoulderblocks Truxell right in the knees! With Truxell down, Vendetta grabs his boot and starts laying in stiff kicks to Truxell’s right leg, pounding away on it as it slams against the mat. After four quick kicks, Vendetta slams the leg down, leaving Truxell clutching at his knee. But Vendetta picks up the assault by lifting Truxell to his feet, and then putting him down again by dropkicking the knee! Nate: Looks like sound psychology to start the match from Vendetta. Robbie: Midget midget midget. Reaching down, Vendetta goes to pick up Truxell by the hair, but Truxell stops him with a quick rake to the face. Robbie: Now *that’s* what I’m talking about. Cheat to win, baby! And it’s allllll legal. Getting to his feet, Truxell whips Vendetta into the ropes and levels him with a lariat! Focusing on Vendetta’s chest, Truxell lays in stomps, drops a pair of quick elbows, and rolls Vendetta over. Nate: It looks like Truxell could be looking to end this match quickly with a Boston crab or an STF! Truxell appear to be going for the latter, but he’s cut short by a Vendetta elbow to the jaw, sending him reeling. Vendetta gets to his feet and looks to clothesline Truxell over the top rope... but Truxell sees it coming and back-body-drops Vendetta over the top rope to the hard floor below! Nate: Whoa! Vendetta went flying there... and it looks like Truxell’s going out after him... the hard way! Climbing out to the ring apron, Truxell heads to the second turnbuckle and comes FLYING off with an elbowdrop... but Vendetta rolls out of the way, leaving Truxell to SPLAT off the ground and both men are down! Nate: Went for the big move there and it cost him... and there’s no count-outs in this one, these two will fight on! Vendetta is first to his feet, dragging Truxell up and rolling him into the ring. Positioning Truxell by the post, Vendetta stays outside of the ring... and grabbing Truxell’s feet, yanks him forward, crotching him HARD on the ringpost! Steven: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Robbie: Ooh! Even those of us who aren’t flaming homosexual queers felt that one. Vendetta stays on the attack, slamming Truxell’s knee against the post as he shouts in pain in the ring. Taking a second or two to soak in the crowd’s cheering, Vendetta hops onto the apron... lies back... and locks Truxell in a ringpost figure-four! Truxell is screaming in pain, but Vendetta doesn’t have the hold cinched on tightly... and Truxell manages to get his hands down and break the hold, sending Vendetta falling back to the cement! Truxell gets to his feet gingerly as Vendetta rolls into the ring... and holding onto the ropes for support, greets Vendetta with a kick to the face as he rolls into the ring! Seizing the opportunity, Truxell drags Vendetta to his feet and LAYS into him with VICIOUS chops, leaving red marks across “Classic”’s chest! Truxell doubles Vendetta over with a quick kick to the gut... and DROPS him with the PISTON DDT! Nate: That’s Truxell’s finisher, but he can’t go for the pin here! It’s submissions only! Realizing the situation, Truxell rolls Vendetta over and cinches in a Boston crab! Vendetta is struggling through the pain as Referee Gaston comes over to check for a submission... but by the time he slides around, Vendetta has a hand on the ropes... but with no DQs, Gaston cannot break the hold, and tells Vendetta as much! Nate: As much as I hate to admit it, that’s the correct call here! Vendetta could be one tap away from losing his Corvette! But Vendetta will not quit... and slowly, but surely, is MAKING HIS FEET! As Truxell looks shocked... Vendetta POWERS to his feet, breaking the hold and throwing Truxell off of his back! Truxell looks stunned, but quickly regains his look of anger as Vendetta slowly recovers on the mat, still looking to be in plenty of pain from the prolonged time in the Boston Crab. Truxell slides out of the ring and heads towards the announce table... stopping short to fold up a chair... and returning with the chair into the ring! Nate: No disqualifications here... that means Truxell can use that chair as he pleases! Robbie: Thank you, Captain Obvious. Heading back towards Vendetta with the chair, Truxell unloads on his legs, hammering him once... twice... THREE times with the chair as Vendetta screams in pain! Still walking with a slight limp from the earlier damage to his knee, Truxell heads over to the ropes... and poses for the fans with the chair, sending down a HUGE negative reaction from the crowd. Robbie: Look at him pose! Vendetta’s in a world of hurt over there... he’s got all the time in the world to! Putting the chair down, Truxell heads back to Vendetta, jawing right in his face... and then getting SPAT at by Vendetta to a HUGE pop from the crowd! Nate: Vendetta just SPAT in Griff Truxell’s face! And Truxell is FURIOUS! Livid in the ring, Truxell looks to move in for the kill and the match... he grabs Vendetta’s right leg, steps over, and applies the FIGURE-FOUR! Vendetta is howling in pain as the time goes on... ... five seconds... ...ten seconds... ... fifteen seconds and even though Gaston is right there, he will NOT give up! Nate: Vendetta in all sorts of pain right now, but he REFUSES to concede this match! I’m afraid Referee Pierre Gaston may have to stop this match before Vendetta is seriously hu... what? What the... signs of life from Vendetta... it looks like he’s trying to REVERSE the figure-four! Robbie: WHAT? HE CAN’T DO THAT! Truxell’s expression goes quickly from confident to confused to downright worried... and finally shocked as Vendetta, using his remaining energy... FLIPS the figure-four! Now it’s Truxell who’s in pain and Vendetta with the upper hand... but Vendetta can’t hold on for long, releasing the hold. Truxell is down in the middle of the ring... and Vendetta struggles to his feet, stumbling over to the ropes... where he recovers the chair dropped by Truxell! Holding the chair up high for the fans, Vendetta signals that enough is enough... and finally regaining the trademark smirk on his face, Vendetta hobbles over... and SLAMS the chair into Truxell’s midsection! Nate: Not only did the tables turn on the figure-four, the tables have turned with the chair now, too, and Vendetta is releasing all the hatred he had for Griff Truxell with that folding chair! WHAM... and another shot clips Truxell in the side of the head, sending him twitching on the canvas! Vendetta then drags Truxell to his feet, where he’s dazed and stumbling, and starts jawing right at Truxell... and though most of it is inaudible, the message gets across... and winding up, Vendetta HAMMERS Truxell in the face with the chair, sending him straight to the mat and bleeding profusely! Nate: WHAT A CHAIRSHOT! TRUXELL IS DOWN AND BUSTED WIDE OPEN! Hobbling over again, Vendetta drags Truxell to his feet, and the two chairshots have Truxell’s face contorted into a crimson mask... but Vendetta takes his head by his arm, cinches in a dragon sleeper, and steps over... Nate: CROWD CONTROL! Vendetta has locked in CROWD CONTROL on Griff Truxell!! Truxell, in the center of the ring, beaten and bloody has no choice... and with Gaston standing right there, taps Vendetta with his free hand... and Gaston signals for the bell! WINNER: Clay Vendetta Nate: He’s done it! Clay Vendetta has done what he said he would and beat cocky upstart Griff Truxell... and is taking his limo home, while Truxell’s gonna be riding home on that moped!! Robbie: Yeah, well, he’s still a midget. As a very tired Vendetta soaks in the cheers from the fans, Niki Weathers rushes in to hug her man... and next over comes the security official with both sets of keys and the pinkslip to Truxell’s limo! Stumbling out of the ring, Vendetta heads through the throngs of fans... and gets into his Corvette, which he revs up... and drives out, with Niki in the passenger’s seat! Nate: A brutal match that was a long time coming, and Vendetta is heading home in style tonight. We’ve gotta take a break, but we’ll be right back! Commercial: This spot brought to you by the Durham, NC Tourist Board. Come see what a great town looks like... when all of its economy collapses. Nate: We’re back, folks, and in the break Truxell was bandaged up... and wait a minute, we’ve got something backstage... The camera cuts to a backstage area, where Shanda Russell can be seen walking around... and then a faint sound growing louder... *putt-put-putt-putt-PUTT-PUTT* ... and it’s Truxell, tooling out of the arena on his newly-acquired rusty moped, on which the exhaust pipe is screeching lightly as it drags across the cement. Russell rushes up... Russell: Mr. Truxell... do you have any comment... Truxell: SHUT UP. And with that he heads out of the arena, doing a brisk... oh, fifteen miles per hour…as the scene fades to commercial.
