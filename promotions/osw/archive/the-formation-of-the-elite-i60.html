The Formation of the Elite

2002-06-08
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Formation of the Elite Written: 6-8-02 The ref breaks them apart, and Ace walks away from Toomes, pumping himself and the crowd up. Toomes tries getting to his feet, but stumbles, clutching onto the ropes for support. Nate: That knee is shot. Toomes gets to his feet unsteadily, but Hawk comes in like a freight train, clobbering Toomes with a massive clothesline! Hawk makes the cover for a… 1 2 Nope, only two, people. Hawk grimaces and rolls Toomes over, locking in The Harley Grip! Nate: HARLEY GRIP! HARLEY GRIP! Toomes is securely locked in that modified Camel Clutch! Steven: Hey, Robbie, do you suppose Nate screams real loud to get us excited? Robbie narrows his eyes and continues to contemplate what he smelled previously. Steven: Meh. Toomes struggles in vain as Ace cranks back relentlessly. Suddenly… Robbie’s eyes fly open. Robbie: I KNEW IT! Trevor Hawke leaps over the barricade and the crowd pops hard for the Civil Disobedience team member. He runs up to the apron and Ace, wary as always, leaps up, releasing Toomes, who flops to the mat. Trevor makes a move to come into the ring, but Ace is ready. Trevor deftly removes his hand before Ace has a chance to stomp it flat. Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON HERE? Robbie: I knew it, I knew it, I knew it! Steven: What are you talking about? Robbie: Soon, my young padawan apprentice, it will become apparent. Nate: WHY THE HELL IS TREVOR HAWKE OUT HERE? Steven: Rights to the last name? The referee runs towards them and separates Trevor and Ace before Ace can hop out of the ring to pound on Trevor. Trevor and the referee argue and Trevor stands on the apron, getting in the referee’s face. Ace smugly turns back to Toomes, who is groggily getting to his feet… Nate: What the hell…LOOK OUT! Seth Masters slides in from the other side of the ring with a steel chair, unnoticed by Ace, who lazily kicks Toomes in the side. Seth runs and SLAMS the steel chair into Hawk’s wounded shoulder! Nate: SETH MASTERS JUST DRILLED ACE HAWK WITH THAT STEEL CHAIR! Robbie: MY HIGHLY ATTUNED JEDI SENSES ARE CORRECT! I STILL GOT IT! Ace falls to the mat, screaming in pain. Seth giggles maniacally, and literally bends the steel chair over Ace’s head, knocking him unconscious. Seth looks around quickly and ducks out of the ring, the steel chair in tow. Nate: GODDAMNIT, ACE HAWK JUST GOT SCREWED! Steven: Robbie, you actually saw this coming? Robbie: Stevie, my boy, the Force flows through all things, and through my Jedi senses, I detected a screw-job. Steven: Wow… Trevor hops off the apron, and Pierre turns around as Toomes stands…grinning ear to ear. Nate: Hey… Toomes stands on his ‘bad’ leg, and inexplicably beings hopping around the ring, waving his arms and laughing. He does this odd little dance around Ace and spits in Ace’s face. He drops and makes the cover… 1 2 3… DING! DING! DING! Winner: Sebastian Toomes Nate: I’m going to be sick. Robbie: Hah hah! I guess that Harley Grip of Ace Hawk has medicinal qualities! Toomes’ knee is fine now! Toomes stands and Civil Disobediance slides into the ring, and they give each other high-fives before they begin to stomp the living crap out of Ace Hawk. Seth does a highly over-exaggerated elbow drop then rolls away to get a mic. Nate: Damnit, what the hell do these jackasses have to do with Switchblade? Robbie: I think they’re going to inform us, eh? Seth grins and begins speaking. Seth: Well, are ya shocked? The crowd boos and they begin throwing refuse into the ring. Seth: I said, ARE YOU SHOCKED? Nate: Jackass… Ace Hawk didn’t deserve this! Seth: Hell, you all shoulda been expecting this! We’re tired of being constantly screwed over around here! Ever since OSW started, we three have had to carry this federation on our backs! Yeah, that’s right! US! And what did we get for it? NOTHING! Trevor stops beating on Ace and comes over to grab the microphone, and Seth takes his place in the mass beating of Ace Hawk. Trevor: We were the first Champions of OSW, the Tag Champs and the OSW Champ! THE FIRST ONES! Yeah, US! We were all in the same class in PWA! But look what’s going on now? We got a pair of Goth dicksmacks, a pair of Go-Bots, and a couple of token black dudes in the Tag Division here, and we got shafted! Noone else deserves the OSW Tag Team Championship Belts, except Civil Disobediance! We were the first…the BEST…THE SHINING STARS! Nate: This is sounding familiar. Toomes takes the mic, grinning, as Trevor goes back to beating Hawk. Toomes: That’s right, you scummy little mouthbreathers! We are the BEST AND THE BRIGHTEST in this whole damn federation! But noone respects that! Noone respects our skill, our ability, our good god-damned looks! We were the founding champions! The best of what Jane brought to he table, and you fans spit on us! You assholes in the back, you spit on us! The crowd screams for Toomes’ untimely and messy death. Toomes: So…it occurred to me that since anti-intellectualism seems to run rampant around here, the former OSW Tag Team Champions and the former OSW World Champion need to give you all…an education! Robbie: TEACH US! TEACH US! Steven: WE’VE BEEN BAD, BAD BOYS AND WE NEED A SPANKING! Robbie: …you ruin everything… Toomes, ignoring the boos and the gay comments, continues. Toomes: An education…taught to you by the First Champions and anyone else willing to listen to reason, to right the wrongs INFLICTED upon us by that geek Porter… That homocidal freak Ed…and the rest of you neaderthals… Welcome, OSW…TO THE NEW SCHOOL! Toomes hands the mic off to Seth, and stomps on Ace’s upturned face. Seth: Class…DISMISSED. Seth throws down the mic and the three of them stalk out of the ring and up the ramp as ‘I Am Hated’ explodes on the PA. Nate: CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE HAS JOINED SEBASTIAN TOOMES! WHAT DOES THIS BODE FOR OSW? Steven: While Nate jizzes his shorts over this admittedly startling development, we’re gonna go to a commerical!
