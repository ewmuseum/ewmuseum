One spooky interview (w/ Clay Vendetta)

2002-07-06
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One spooky interview (w/ Clay Vendetta) Written: July 6, 2002 The camera scene opens up to the locker-room of 'Classic' Clay Vendetta, where Vendetta sits, lacing up his boots. The vintage musical stylings of The Eagles can be heard on a stereo perched on a table next to Vendetta, where a complimentary 6-pack of Classic Coca-Cola keeps it company. Sauntering by is Niki Weathers... Niki: You about ready to go, Clay, baby? Vendetta: Of course. Just gotta lace up my boots, and go take a Trask, and then I'm ready. Niki: Go take a what? Vendetta: A Trask. Take a Damon Trask. Niki: Huh? Vendetta: ...I need to use the bathroom, dear. Niki: Aaaaah. Niki chuckles... as a door slams off-screen. Vendetta looks up quickly with a menacing expression... but the expression on his face changes back to a small smirk when he sees who it is, and the camera pans left to reveal Dave Gibson, grinning in his 'Shut Up and Wrestle' T-shirt. Vendetta: Well, if it isn't one of OSW's biggest moneymakers... oh, and there's somebody wearing it, too. Gibson: Ha, ha. Hilarious, Clay. Vendetta: I do my best. Want a Coke? The wonderful people at the Coca-Cola Company have been comping me for the free plugs. Still waiting on the endorsement deal, though. Gibson: I'll pass, thanks. Anyhow, I notice you haven't bought one of my shirts yet. They're so handsome. 50 percent cotton, you know. Breathable. Vendetta chuckles and looks up from his lacing... Vendetta: Not my style. Walking over to the table, Gibson turns down the music, prompting a somewhat-annoyed look from Vendetta. Gibson: Yeah, someone who comes out to a song by Kansas is gonna talk to me about style. Gee, maybe we should get Styx to record us a theme song while we're at it. Vendetta: I'll choose to ignore that comment. Hey, you seem pretty chipper considering who we're facing tonight... I mean, Team Spooooooooky's got some big guys. Gibson: Who? The Glimmer Twins out there? I couldn't care less. See Clay, they may be bigger. They may be badder. They may be stronger. But we have something they don't. Vendetta: A pituitary disorder? Gibson: Besides that! See Clay, we... are old school. Hessian may know 60 different wrestling moves, but WE know wrestling. Between us, we know every trick in the book. While those two have been lumbering around graveyards, playing with skeletons, practicing their chants and molesting their midgets... Looking back up, Vendetta mouths 'molesting their midgets?' with a confused look on his face, but Gibson continues in stride... Gibson: ...WE have been studying our craft. See you and me, we're the same. We're BOTH throwbacks. And tonight, we're gonna kick The Cult right back to the bad horror movie they staggered out of. Vendetta: Sounds like a plan. Gibson: The only hitch I can see is this: how do we go about intimidating two guys who've seen Nightmare on Elm St. One through Forty-Five? Vendetta: Why, we play their game, of course. Gibson: Huh? Vendetta: You'll see. Hey, Niki, dear? Page Ms. Russell if you could, please, I've got an interview to conduct... The camera fades to black... and a subtitle pops up as the camera fades back in on Vendetta's door: EIGHT SECONDS LATER... Knocking on the door is OSW's favorite junior reporter, Shanda Russell, interview crew in tow. Microphone in hand, Shanda looks poised for the scoop, and Dave Gibson is there to greet her at the door with a... Gibson: Hey. Can I grab that microphone? And the camera crew too. I'm gonna be doing this one. You can watch if you like. With a decidedly bitter look on her face, Russell hands over the microphone. Russell: No, thank you gentlemen. Walking away Shanda can be heard muttering to herself... Russell: Mom always said be a doctor, or a lawyer... nooooooo, I had to work with musclehead guys for a living. Terrific. Taking the microphone and the waiting camera crew inside the door, the home audience gets a great shot of... 'Classic' Clay Vendetta in a 'Scream' mask, prompting confusion from some and laughs from others. An ENORMOUS pop can be heard in the arena, though; for standing next to clay is Niki Weathers, clad in a skimpy 'Red Devil' outfit that's enough to get at least three of the fanboys in the front row to hit puberty instantneously in a flash of light and a puff of brimstone and Clearasil. In fact, somewhere out there, the very sight is enough to make 'Precious' Peter Love just the *tiniest* bit straighter... not that you'd know it. Taking the microphone and looking at the camera crew, Gibson starts his interview... Gibson: Ladies and gentlemen, I'm 'Old School' Dave Gibson, and I'm here for an EXCLUSIVE interview with one of the hottest commodities in the wrestling world today, 'Classic' Clay Vendetta... Vendetta: Ahem... ...he says from behind the Scream mask. Gibson: Ahem? Vendetta: 'CREEPY' Clay Vendetta. BWAHAHAHAHAHAHA! Taken aback at the semi-maniacal laugh, Gibson shoots Vendetta a look. Gibdon: What? Vendetta: You see, I felt I needed a change of gimmick, if you will. I mean, I've made a killing... get it?... as 'Classic', but I think this week, to battle against the FORCES OF EVIL... it's time that 'CREEPY' CLAY VENDETTA showed up. Gibson: And what is that change of gimmick, exactly? Vendetta: Of course, it's the CLASSIC Satanic Jobber... BWAHAHAHA*cough*... aww, damn. Messed that one up. Can I try again? Gibson: Sure thing. Vendetta: BWAHAHAHAHHAHAHHAHAHHAHAHHAHAHAH! Oh man, I felt that one. Much better. Gibson: Nice inflection. Vendetta: Thank you! Gibson: So... is there anything you'd like to say to your opponents tonight, CCV? Vendetta: Of course. *ahem* Nosferaaaaatuuuuuuuu and Hesssssssssssian... ohhhhhhhhhh yes.... toooooooonight... you will taaaaaaaaaaste a thousand bloooooooooooooody asskickings, covvverrrrrrrred in maggggots and taking place in a graaaaaaaaaveyard, 'cause graveyards are SPOOOOOOOOOOOOOKY, BWAHAHAHA! And the maaaaagots will be having their FLESH eaten by a milllllllllion screaming BANSHEE DOGS FROM HELL and yes, I mayyyyy be just a wrestler, but you must fear my DAAAAAAAARK AND EVVVVVVILLLLL POWERS LIKE PUTTING ON WOMEN'S CLOTHING... for THAT is where the energy of the Saaaaaataaaanic Jooooobbbbberr is channelled from... raaaaaaging homosexuality... BWAHAHAHAHA!! And after that I will turn the SCREAMING BANSHEE DOGS on your moooooooothheeeerrrs.... Gibson: WHOA. Whoa. You're scaring me here, Clay. Vendetta: Oh. Sorry. Just trying to get into The Cult's mindset, that's all. Alright, I'll wrap it up. Can at I least hit my tagline? Gibson: Yeah, sure, but make it quick. Vendetta: Alright. *ahem* So Vaaaaaampiiiir and Hesssssssian... tooooonight... it won't just be the destruction... naaaaaay, the crucifixxxxxxxtion of a pair of Satanic Jobbers... it'll be a CULT CLASSIC!!! BWAHAHAHHAHAHHAHHAHHA! Gibson: And... that's a wrap. Pulling off the Scream mask, Vendetta is all smirks. Vendetta: Think I captured the spirit correctly? Gibson: Perfectly. The two men get to their feet, and Gibson walks towards the door... Gibson: Ready? Vendetta: Yeah... just as soon as I take a Trask. Gibson: A what? Vendetta sighs... as the scene fades to black.
