Civil Disobedience vs. Frankie Bones/Drew Hostyle for the OSW Tag Team Titles (Suburban Deathmatch)

2002-03-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil Disobedience vs. Frankie Bones/Drew Hostyle for the OSW Tag Team Titles (Suburban Deathmatch) Written: 3-16-02 Robbie: I just think that tonight deserves to be something special. This next match was set immediately after Bones and Hostyle attacked Civil Disobedience after they had just won the Tag Titles in our first ever Suburban Death Match. This match is huge. Steven: BOCA! Nate and Robbie let loose an audible groan. Robbie: That’s it! That’s it! Let’s just take it to Earl Goddammit! The camera shot changes to Earl Henrich standing in the middle of the ring waiting to make the introductions for the next match. Earl brings the mic up to his lips to begin the tag teams announcements. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen! Boys and Girls! Our next match is for the Tag Team Titles. The match is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring first, coming from Brooklyn, New York and weighing in at a combined weight of 543 pounds! Two members of 3B! “Who We Be” begins to play on the PA system. Frankie Bones and Drew Hostyle come out of the backstage area and explode out onto the entryway. The tag team are welcomed with the boos of the fans that are cursing them as they make their way down to the ring. Earl: Frankie Bones and Drew Hostyle!!! Bones and Hostyle make it to the ring and immediately climb the apron and crouch under the ropes. Bones and Hostyle taunt the crowd that is still booing them incessantly. Earl begins to announce their opponents. Earl: And their opponents! Coming to you from the suburbs of Aurora Illinois! Weighing in at the combined weight of 460pounds! They are the first ever OSW Tag Team Champions! “Bounce” begins to play which sparks the arena into an explosion of cheers for their champions. Earl: Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke…Civil Disobedience!!!! Masters and Hawke leap out onto the entryway proudly wearing the gold Tag Titles around their wastes. The fans continue to cheer and hold up signs for the tag team as Masters and Hawke make their way to the ring slapping the hands of the fans that line the steelramp. Nate: Tonight we are going to see Civil Disobedience defend their precious titles for the first time. And it appears as if they are ready for the challenge. Robbie: It doesn’t matter how ready they are! Those two punks barely beat Cain and Hessian for the titles at Crowing Glory! It’s about time that their luck has run out. 3B is going to make fast work of these two and before long, 3B is going to hold two out of three of OSW’s titles! Masters and Hawke get into the ring and leap up onto the turnbuckles. Both men remove their belts and hold them up in the air as flash bulbs begin to twinkle throughout the arena. The fans cheer wildly, but before Masters and Hawke can get themselves down off the turnbuckles they are pulled down by Hostyle and Bones. Bones is laying the punches into Masters as Hawke is planted hard onto the mat as Hostyle just slammed him from the turnbuckle. Nate: And Bones and Hostyle are refusing to wait for the signal from Referee Lovejoy to start this one. They have decided to start the match right away. Steven: Dammit, Lovejoy! Will you signal for the damn bell already? Lovejoy signals for the bell. It rings so that the match is now officially underway. Hostyle has pulled Hawke to his feet now and tossed him through the ropes down onto the hard concrete outside of the ring. Lovejoy has picked up the belts and secured them away from the match. Nate: Hostyle has made quick work of Hawke already! It will probably be a little while before he is back up. Robbie: Good! Hostyle has stepped out of the ring as his partner, Bones, is still laying haymakers into Masters’ face. Bones wraps up Masters now and irishwhips him against the ropes causing Masters to be propelled off the ropes and into a big boot from Bones. Masters is on the mat with a dazed look on his face. Nate: It is obvious that Masters and Hawke have their hands full tonight. Steven: Well, no wonder. Look at the match last week. You are actually lucky that he they are able to fight this week after the beating that they received from Heresian Bloodlust during the title match. Robbie: Lucky? What the hell are you on. We have been cursed to this week. Bones leans over to pick up Masters. Once Masters is on his feet, he somehow manages to deliver a spinning Heel Kick to Bones’ chin. Bones grasps his chin and falls to the ground in shock from the speed of that particular move. Nate: And Masters has just dropped Bones! That is unbelievable! Were did that come from! Robbie: Who cares! Bones leans over and reaches for a tag from his partner. Hostyle is reaching for Bones, but before the tag can be made, Masters follows up with a leg drop that drives Bones’ face into the mat. Steve: Ouch! Nate: This is a side to Masters that we rarely get to see! Robbie: Yeah, because he is usually too busy blocking punches with his face! HA! Masters goes for a cover. Lovejoy is there to make the count. 1… 2… Bones manages to get his shoulder up. Nate: And Bones denies the victory just in time. Masters gets to his feet and begins to pull Bones up. As Bones is getting up to his knees his manages to lunge at Maters and hits him with a strong clothesline that topples Masters to the mat. Both men are on the mat. Bones gets to his feet first and goes for the tag. Nate: And a quick tag from Bones to put his partner in the ring. Robbie: And now we get to see Hostyle let some of his aggression from loosing the Continental Title last week out on Masters! Hostyle is climbs in the ring and hits Masters with a spear just as he was getting back to his feet. Masters topples backwards again. Hostyle gets on top of Masters and begins to lay punches into his opponents face. Steven: Why do they always go for that poor boys face? That is just wrong! Robbie: Shut the hell up Smith! Hostyle gets Masters up to his feet. He then grabs Masters into a Fireman’s carry and drops him down into a Gut Buster. Nate: Cracka Cracker! Good God, this one might be over! Hostyle goes for a pin. Lovejoy is there to start the count. 1… 2… No! The pin is broken up by Hawke who has managed to get himself back into the match by hitting Hostyle in the back of the head with an Elbow Drop. Nate: And that is why these two are champions! Robbie: That’s right! Because they cheat! Nate: What? Robbie: You heard me! They cheat! Nate: You must be delusional! Hostyle is grasping at his head. Hawke sprints across the ring and leans into the ropes. But before he springs back towards Hostyle he hits Bones in the face with an elbow that drops Bones from the apron down onto the floor outside of the ring. Hawke is on his way back towards Hostyle. Before he gets there, he leaps into the air and lands with a Knee Drop to the back of Hostyle’s leg. Hostyle’s body flops in pain. Nate: Hawke is on a mission to defend these titles tonight. Robbie: Come on! Be serious for a second. He is bailing out Masters again just like he has to do every time they fight! Hawke climbs out of the ring and begins to reach for a tag. Masters is on all fours trying to get to his partner. He lunges and makes the tag. Hawke hits the mat again and runs at Hostyle. Hawke leaps through the air again and tries to his Hostyle with another elbow drop, but this time no one is home. Nate: And Hawke just misses with another devastating blow. Steven: What did you just say? Robbie: Dumas, what the hell have I told you about saying things like that. Think before you open your damn unoriginal mouth! Nate: I can’t help it the little pervert can spin anything into something sexual. Hawke is holding his elbow in pain, but he gets back to his feet quick. He eyes up Hostyle who is trying to make a tag to Bones. Hawke starts to climb the ropes. He leaps through the air and hits Hostyle with a Drop Kick. The momentum from the move that gave Hostyle exactly what he needed to make the tag. Unfortunately for Hostyle though, face down on the mat grasping his head in pain. Nate: Hawke might be in trouble if he doesn’t do something quick. Bones climbs into the ring over top of his hurting partner. Bones runs at Masters and tries to hit him with a clothesline from hell, but he is met with a Hip Toss that flips Bones over onto his back. Hawke leans down to pick up Bones, but when he is about to get a hold on him, he is met with a kick to the skull by Bones. The kick topples the stunned Hawke over. Bones gets up to his feet. He starts to kick away at Hawke. Nate: And the tides in this match have turned yet again. Robbie: Will you quit trying to make it seem like it is some sort Soap Opera? Nate: But I thought that you wanted to have some more Flare! Robbie: Not hat kind of Flare! Inside the ring, Bones leans over to get Hawke back up. Bones grabs hold of Hawke and hits him with a perfectly executed Reverse DDT. Nate: We might have new Tag Champions already. Bones goes for the pin. Masters hits the ring running in order to break up the pin, but before he can do so he is hit with a running spear from Hostyle that causes both men to fall outside of the ring. Bones still has the pin held on, but Lovejoy is watching the wrestlers outside of the ring as Hostyle and Masters are up and exchanging punches. Steven: That damn woman is doing again! She is costing Bones and Hostyle this match because she is not paying attention to the guys in the damn ring. While Lovejoy is distracted, there is some movement that in the crowd that he cannot see. Nate: What the hell is that? Robbie: Don’t you mean, “Who the hell is that?” A man climbs over the barrier and slides under the bottom rope without the ref seeing him. Nate: Lovejoy will you turn around already? Bones does not see the predator that is behind him right now. Nate: It’s Michael Dame! What the hell is he doing out here? Robbie: Dammit Bones! Turn around! Bones begins to sense that something is behind him. Just as Bones turns around he is hit with a Super Kick. Bones is dropped to the mat by Dame’s super kick. Dame then leans over and pulls Masters over Bones’ body. Robbie: He cheated for god’s sake! Will someone call the match already! Dame runs over to Lovejoy and makes her aware of the pin. The ref begins to make the count. 1… 2… 3… Match Result: Civil Disobedience Robbie: That damn woman blows more matches then any of those… Robbie looks over to his fellow announcers and decided stop that last statement so that he doesn’t open a floodgate of comments from them because of what he was about to say. Steven: Than any what, Decker? Robbie: Nothing! Nate and Steven begin to laugh outrageously. Hawke is in the ring pulling Masters back up to his feet so that they can celebrate the defense of their titles. Hostyle is in hot pursuit of Dame after he got Bones up to his feet. This moment is interrupted though by the loud gong that begins an all too familiar chant. Nate: Oh, this is never good! Robbie: You’re so wrong. This is always great! That song means that things just got all the more interesting! “Carmina Burana” Blasts the arena’s PA system as Hessian and Azrael Cain appear at the entryway. Nate: Heresian Bloodlust are here! Cain and Hessian sprint down the steelramp as Hostyle and Bones begin to pursue after Dame through the crowd. Hessian and Cain slide under the mat together. Both of them lunge at Civil Disobedience and strike both of Tag Champs down with spears to the midsection. Cain and Hessian get back to their feet and start to stomp away on Masters and Hawke. Nate: Heresian Bloodlust is on the warpath! Robbie: It’s about time! Steven: Boca! Hessian and Cain get back on their feet. Hessian has Masters by throat, while Cain is holding Hawke the same way. Both of the giants look at one another and nod. Both of them lift Masters and Hawke up into the air for a couple of seconds before Chokeslamming them simultaneously into the mat. Nate: Someone get security in here! Hessian and Cain look down on Masters and Hawke for a second before they turn to leave. Both of them exit back up the steelramp, still staring at Civil Disobedience in the ring who are not moving at all. Nate: That was uncalled for! Robbie: But completely fun to watch. Steven: BOCA! Nate: Let’s head to a commercial.
