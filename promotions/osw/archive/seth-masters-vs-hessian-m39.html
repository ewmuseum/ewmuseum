Seth Masters vs. Hessian

2002-03-09
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seth Masters vs. Hessian Written: 3-9-02 Nate: Welcome back to Slam!! Our first match or the evening should be something to see! These two teams will be facing off tommorrow night at Crowning Glory, but we're about to get a preview of that right here tonight! Robbie: Translation: we get to see the Heresian Bloodlust destroy two pathetic jobbers. 'Pretty Fly for a White Guy' by blasts over the speakers with heavy bass, and the crowd immediately erupts in 'SE-ETH MASTERS!' chants. Civil Disobedience enters soon after, and the cheering grows even louder as Seth bows and waves to his fans, and Trevor looks slightly annoyed. After a few moments of enjoying the adoration of the fans, the two men head down the ramp, with Trevor stopping just short of the ring as Seth slides in and strikes a pose. Earl Heinrich: The following is a standard match! In the ring at this time, weighing two hundred and thirty-six pounds, from Aurora, Illinois -- SETH MASTERS! The pop is cut short, however, as 'I Am Hated' by Slipknot hits the speakers; the crowd begins booing loudly as Hessian, accompanied by Azrael Cain and Caleb, storms down the ramp like some legendary, pissed off god of old. The group stops at Trevor, standing strong in their path, and immediately a brawl breaks out as Azrael and Hessian begin laying in the punches to Seth Master's smaller, smarter, and weaker partner. Nate: What the-- They're not even letting this match start! Seth doesn't hesitate -- with a roar belying his size, he leaps right over the top rope and lands on the heads of his two taller foes. Heresian Bloodlust goes down hard, but Hessian is back up quickly, throwing a punch that Seth somehow manages to block. The next one, however, is ducked under, and there's obviously no escape from the third -- until Trevor Hawke charges in to spear the big man in the stomach! Nate: Great teamwork by Civil D! Robbie: BAH! You mean, great BLATANT CHEATING by Civil D! Steven: No, I think he really meant the great teamwo-- Robbie: Shut up. Like a felled tree, Hessian toppled backwards, taking Trevor down with him. Seth stops to congratulate his now winded and dazed partner on the move, but is suddenly struck from behind by a punch to the head, as Azrael Cain joins the melee! Nate: CHEAP SHOT! Robbie: And the spear wasn't? In the ring, the helpless generic ref waves his arms for Seth and Hessian to get into the ring, but to no avail. The brawl continues at full tilt, with Azrael and Caleb pounding away at Seth Masters, and Trevor, somehow, some way, laying it in to Hessian. Steven: This match never even got under way, and I have a feeling it never WILL get under way! Finally, Hessian rolls free of the Mighty Hawke, stopping only to elbow the little guy in the gut, grab him by his neck and pants, and smash him into the guard rail like some sort of battering ram. Trevor hits the floor, stunned, while Hessian begins searching around for a weapon. Spotting a pretty young woman in the crowd, he marches over to her and, somehow, it seems like he's smiling at her from under the mask. She smiles back, obviously thinking the whole thing fake -- and is suddenly deposited on her rump as the big man yanks her chair out from under her, folds it up, and turns back to the brawl. Nate: This looks bad for Seth Masters! Robbie: Kill him! Maybe then, Ed will schedule them against some non-jobbers for Crowning Glory! Steven: Knowing him, he'd just replace Seth with Meanstreak! Nate: That'd work pretty well -- we all know how terrified Hessian is of the Monster! Robbie: Bah, we all know it's Streak who's terrified of Hessian! Steven: Uh-oh! This looks bad! Back at the brawl, Seth has somehow managed to fight his way back to the feet, though a shadow now looms over him from behind -- Hessian, with the steel chair raised high. The famous half of Civil D gawks at the impressive sight, then suddenly slams his fist into Azrael's stomach, doubling the giant over, and ducks for cover -- just as the steel chair descends. Instead of splattering the little Illinoisan, it smashes right into the back of Cain's head, dropping him heavily to the ground. Hessian glances around, stunned, just as Seth comes back up with a HUGE superkick right to the big guy's throat! Nate: THAT'S GOTTA HURT! Robbie: DAMN! Come on, Heresian Bloodlust! You can squash these insects! Gasping for air, Hessian stumbles in a half-circle -- right into a FLYING TRASHCAN SHOT FROM OFF THE GUARD RAIL! Nate: GOOOOD GAWD!!!! Like some kind of short-haired, geeky Jeff Hardy, Trevor Hawke comes flying off the barrier and drives the can right into Hessian's steel-masked face, and while the blow doesn't damage his facial features, it does knock the already dazed giant off his feet, to land in a heap next to his partner. Nate: They've done it! Civil Disobedience has brought the giants down! Robbie: BAH! Let's see them do it again at Crowning Glory! Nate: Be careful what you wish for, my friend! Still standing, though obviously winded, Seth and Trevor high-five eachother and turn back to their fallen foes. Suddenly, they notice something -- a lone, small figure, cowering behind the dazed Azrael Cain. The two members of Civil D glance at eachother, and slow grins spread over their faces. 'Caleb,' they mouth simultaneously. The midget, already traumatized so many times in his OSW career, squeaks slightly. Nate: This looks bad for Caleb! Steven: *sobbing* Haven't they hurt the little guy enough? Seth turns to his partner, his grin even broader, and suddenly slams him in the chest with his palms. 'TREVOR!' he screams, still barely audible because of the crowd's cheering. 'WHAT?!' comes the shouted reply. 'GET THE TABLE!' The crowd's eruption is nearly deafening, as Trevor and Seth flip open the apron and drag out a long, wooden table. Caleb, for some reason not running, is quickly caught by the pair and lifted high overhead...when suddenly, Azrael Cain and Hessian, back on their feet though we somehow didn't notice, charge in. Robbie: GET THEM! Immediately, Civil D drop the midget, and duck under the apron. The Heresians, thinking their opponents are running away, stupidly drop to peer in after them -- and receive two hard chairshots, right in the face! Nate: HA! The chairshots continue, driving the big men back, and up the ramp, with Civil D weilding them like a pair of lightsabers against a two overpowered Sith. Finally, Seth rears back and SMASHES his chair across the side of Hessian's head, throwing the chair out of his grasp but laying the giant low. Azrael Cain soon follows his comrade, and Seth and Trevor hold their arms high as the crowd roars in approval. Then, with a quick nod to eachother, they race back down the ramp to capture the stunned Caleb, who's trying to squeeze under an apparently filled section of the ring. Robbie: DAMN IT! THEY'LL PAY! OH, THEY'LL PAY! Each man grabs one of the midget's legs and hoists him up; Seth steps up onto the apron, curling the little guy and lifting him high; Trevor raises his arms, and then chops them downward. A moment later, Caleb smashes through the table, landing in a crumpled heap and bouncing slightly, so that only his legs and feet remain in the rubble. Nate: CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE JUST POWERBOMBED CALEB THROUGH A TABLE! Robbie: THEY WILL PAY FOR THIS! THEY HAVE ONE DAY TO LIVE, AND THEN THEY WILL PAY! Steven: WHY DO THEY HAVE TO KEEP HURTING THAT POOR LITTLE GUY?! CRUEL BASTARDS! Nate: WE NEED TO HEAD TO COMMERCIAL!!! WE WILL BE RIGHT BACK AFTER THESE MESSAGES!!! Up on the ramp, Hessian and Azrael Cain are back to their feet, and throw a few curses at Civil D before retreating and leaving their helpless, battered, and unconcious midget manager behind. In the ring, Seth and Trevor run amuck, posing and grinning, basking in the flashbulbs and cheers of the OSW fans, as “Pretty Fly for a White Guy” once again hits the PA system, as the scene fades to commercial.
