Lillith vs Jesus St Crux vs Frankie Bones

2002-07-13
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lillith vs Jesus St Crux vs Frankie Bones Written: July 13th, 2002 Earl: The following contest is scheduled for one fall, and is a triple threat match for the number one contendership to the International Title! As 'Scum of the Earth' plays over the arena soundsystem, Lillith steps out and starts walking down the ramp. The reaction from the crowd is mixed, some booing, some cheering, but all are loud. Earl: Introducing first, from Twilight, Pennsylvania, standing 5'5' and weighing 115 pounds: Lillith! Steven: Boo! Hiss! Get the woman out of there! I wanna see some P and A! Nate: P and A? What's P- Robbie: Don't even dare answer that, Steven! Lillith climbs through the ropes and paces around the ring a bit, all the while giving Earl a stare that makes the man noticeably shift uncomfortably. The music changes, replaced by 'Bloodline'. Frankie Bones steps out to the boos of the crowd, and he smiles... an evil smile, considering his earlier actions. Ignoring the crowd, he stalks down to the ring straight for Lillith. Earl: The second challenger, from Brooklyn, New York, at 6'6' and 280 pounds: Frankie Bones! Bones moves right past referee Betty Lovejoy, who, as normal, is blissfully unaware of the action going on around her. Frankie moves to get in Lillith's face, but the OSW Queen of Extreme is the faster, sidestepping the Brooklyn native, and flipping him throw the air with a hip toss. Frankie slides out of the ring with his momentum to regroup. Nate: Lillith shows right away that she's not going to take any crap from Frankie Bones! Robbie: Bah! Frankie eats as much as Lillith weighs each meal! Earl: Finally, their opponent, from Juarez, Mexico, standing 5'8' and weighing 220 pounds: Jesus St Crux! 'Fever Dog' plays in the air, and Jesus St Crux steps out onto the ramp. He wears his usual wrestling pants, but also has a large amount of bandages wrapped around his waist. Caridad Mariposa stands next to him, a concerned look on her face. Robbie: St Crux has no chance! Bones already demoralized him earlier. He's just showing how stupid he is by painting a big bull's eye on himself with those bandages! Nate: St Crux is a competitor though. Even with the pain of that branding, he's going to take the fight to Frankie Bones as often as necessary! Steven: Besides, he's still got a great ass. St Crux turns to Caridad and says something, then walks down to the ring on his own. Caridad, a worried expression still evident, remains standing at the top of the ramp for a short while, then slowly seems to comply with Jesus' request and she goes backstage. Steven: That's right! No women allowed! Robbie: I would agree with Steven, but I just know he's going to follow that up with something gay. Nate: That's a safe bet. Steven: And it's a safe bet that there's going to be some loving in my hotel room tonight! Jesus gingerly slides into the ring as Earl exits it. Betty Lovejoy continues to preen her nails, until Earl snaps his fingers in front of her, to bring at least closer to our own world. She calls for the bell to begin the match as Frankie hurries around the outside to grab St Crux's leg before the Mexican stands inside the ring. He hauls St Crux back out of the ring, and immediately slams several punches into the bandages, right on target to where St Crux was branded only a short while ago. Robbie: That's it! Kill him! As St Crux crumples to the floor outside, Lillith charges the ropes above them. She latches on and spins herself through, whipping her heels in a large arc and clipping Bones on the back of the head. Surprised he stumbles forward a few steps, and his forehead collides with the steel ring post. Somehow, Frankie remains standing, although he's dazed enough to forget about St Crux just long enough for the Mexican to roll away, and scramble into the ring. Robbie: Ha! See, he's running! Steven: He's coming for me! Nate: Not quite. St Crux is going for a suicide plancha! Jesus St Crux bounces off the far ropes and runs back to springboard off the ropes above Frankie, but he's too intent on his target, and Lillith surprises him with a spinning heelkick, that sends the now off-balance St Crux crashed to the mat. Lillith pulls him to his feet and Irish whips him to a corner, but Bones has regained his senses, and he pulls Lillith's feet from underneath her when she moves to charge St Crux, causing her to faceplant into the mat. Nate: Frankie Bones is back in the ring, now. And - Oh! That's unnecessary! Bones grabs a handful of Lillith's hair, with the blissfully unaware Betty Lovejoy right beside them. He snarls, and then grinds her face into the mat, causing her to thrash a bit in pain. Jesus charges out of the corner at him, but Bones is ready, catching St Crux in a bear hug, and then slamming the Mexican into the mat with a punishing spinebuster. Nate: Now what's Bones doing? Bones drives a knee into the back of Lillith's head, then climbs on top of her, placing his knee on the back of her neck to pin her to the mat. He then reaches over to the prone St Crux, and starts tearing away at the bandages. Betty actually seems to notice the display, and taps Bones on the shoulder. Betty: Umm... Hey! Like you're not supposed to do that. Frankie glares at her, backing her off. The fans continue to boo his systematic dismantling of St Crux - who's now without the bandages. Bones starts hammering St Crux with his fists, paying particular attention to the 'FB' brand. The fans start cheering however, as Caridad Mariposa runs down the entrance ramp to the ring. Robbie: Hey now! She has no business at ringside! Caridad stops at the apron, alternating between yelling at Frankie Bones and cheering on Jesus. Frankie turns to glare at her, and shifts just enough that Lillith is able to get an arm free... an arm that she immediately uses to punch Bones in the nether region. The Brooklyn native crumples over and falls off her, which lets Lillith get to her feet. Nate: And now Lillith goes for a measure of revenge! Lillith sends Frankie Bones to the ropes, and on the return sends him to the mat with an arm-drag takedown. She drops a quick leg across his neck, and then rolls over for a cover. Noticing that Betty Lovejoy isn't making a move to count the pin, Lillith instead pulls Frankie to his feet. Robbie: Hey, St Crux is getting up! That's not fair! Nate: The man was branded earlier! What are you talking about fair! Lillith sends Bones into a corner, but St Crux charges in instead, jumping over the top rope and catching Bones' head as Frankie stumbles from the corner. St Crux seems to land on the apron, but he continues down to the floor outside, hanging Bones' neck on the top rope. Disoriented from the double team, Bones staggers into the center of the ring where he's snapped to the mat by a Stroke of Midnight. Lillith levels a malevolent glare at Betty Lovejoy, who decides it's in her best interest to remember to make the count as Lillith covers Frankie Bones. Like 1... Like 2... Like... the pin is broken by St Crux pulling Lillith off of Frankie. Lillith backs Jesus up a step with a knife edge chop, to which St Crux responds with a chop of his own. They trade chops for a bit, until both notice Frankie regaining his feet. Both stop and turn to Frankie, and as if reading each other's minds, they prepare to lift Bones for a flapjack. As they try to muscle Frankie into the air, the Brooklyn native claps both his arms together, smashing the heads of his two smaller opponents together. Robbie: That's my boy! He's a thinker despite what anybody says! Frankie kicks Lillith to send her from the ring then turns to focus on St Crux. Two forearms slam into St Crux's kidneys, and then the Mexican is whipped into a corner. Frankie charges, and crushes his shoulder into the cruiserweight's abdomen, sending more pain flaring from the brand burned in earlier. Robbie: Troubled Past! Troubled Past! Yeah! Kill him! Steven: I can ease their pain... or at least replace it with the good kind... Frankie catches St Crux as he starts to collapse, and then props him back in the corner. He sneers, and his evil expression tells anybody who's looking (well, except Betty, but she's clueless so we'll forgive her) that he's not finished yet. He moves to spear St Crux again, but Caridad jumps on the apron near him, causing Frankie swing at her. She drops down to the floor to dodge the swing, and Frankie swears at her, telling her to watch out. Robbie: Watch it Frankie! She's only trying to distract you! Nate: And she's doing a good job! Steven: That Jezebel! Women are the devil's tool! Bones dismisses Caridad, then charges St Crux for another spear, but this time St Crux is able to jump into the air over Bones, and the Brookyln native slams his shoulder into the steel ringpost between the turnbuckles. Jesus wraps his legs around Bones' waist, and then flips overtop, rolling up the bigger man with a sunset flip into a pin. Like 1... Like 2... Like... Frankie pushes with his legs, launching St Crux back into the corner. Jesus braces his injured abdomen, but steps forward and grabs Bones' legs before he can stand. Looking for a bit of revenge, St Crux jumps, and drops both legs between Bones', causing Frankie to howl in pain. Steven: Not the love stick! St Crux stands and moves to grab Frankie's head, but Lillith slips back into the ring behind and grabs the Mexican's head, hauling him around and planting him into the mat with a DDT. Lillith pulls St Crux to his feet and sets him up for a Stroke of Midnight, but Frankie bowls them over with a double clothesline. He lifts Jesus in a bear hug, squeezing the cruiserweight right where the brand left its mark. Bones charges into a corner, and nearly flattens St Crux there. Nate: Frankie Bones' power may just be too much for both Lillith and St Crux here tonight! Robbie: Damn straight! Kill them both! Frankie charges to clothesline Lillith, but she's too quick and ducks underneath the powerful arm. Grimacing through pain, Jesus lifts himself up to the second turnbuckle, and jumps out at Lillith, but instead of hitting her, he handsprings off her shoulder and slams into Frankie with a modified senton bomb. St Crux's momentum carries him forward and out of the ring, where Caridad rushes to his side to check on him. Robbie: Get her away from the ring! She's interfering! Nate: She's checking on St Crux! The man's been brutalized already! Lillith somehow manages to keep her balance after the Mexican leapt from her shoulders, and she notes that Frankie doesn't see her as he slowly regains his feet. Frankie locates St Crux outside of the ring, and takes a step towards him, but Lillith turns him around and quickly snaps him into the air with a Death Blossom. She rolls on top as Betty gently drops to the mat to count (without breaking a nail). Like 1... Like 2... Like 3! Winner: Lillith Robbie: What?! Frankie was robbed! Nate: And Lillith is now the number one contender to Propaganda's International Title!
