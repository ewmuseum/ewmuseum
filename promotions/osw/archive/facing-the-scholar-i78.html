Facing the Scholar

2002-05-18
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Facing the Scholar Written: May 18, 2002 The scene reopens backstage of OSW SLAM as the camera is focused on a large steel door with the nameplate, “CEO PORTER” engraved on a golden plate. Heavy footsteps are heard as a shadow falls on the nameplate and the camera pans around, revealing the source of the shadow. Ivan Stanislav Stanislav stands in front of the door and looks at the camera, an eye twitching as he does, and then grabs the handle and pushes it open with a growl. The inside of the room is a typical makeshift office complete with desk, leather chair, and a the new CEO: “The Scholar” Howard Porter. Porter sits in his chair filling out a form of some kind. He glances up and fixes his glasses as Stanislav walks into the room slowly, a wide smile on his face. The Scholar: Greetings and salutations my fine nostalgic-ridden employee, how can I be of service to you? Stanislav pauses for a moment and smiles wide. Ivan: Comrade Porter! I must say it is very nice to see you back in wrestling business!! I was curious if perhaps you could help me with small, minute, and otherwise simple problem!! The Scholar: Nothing is small, minute, and simple when it comes to you Ivan. Ivan nods slowly, and thinks, not sure if that is a compliment or not. He shrugs it off and smile a smile that is obviously forced. Ivan: First off, allow me to congratulate you on your introduction into OSW! I find it relieving to find that there is someone in power that will do right the injustices down to federation! Secondly, I would like to offer you an chance to become even more prestigious and join my glorious Red Army! The Scholar: Well Ivan, as much as the opportunity to become a blundering sidekick of yours appeals to me, I'm afraid I'm going to have to pass. Ivan grinds his jaw and squints an eye…. Ivan: Right…well, you had your chance. But if I may get to the meat of the matter. I want a match with Reno Davis. As said before, a small problem for you…something I am sure two men who are on good terms with one another can solve. After all, when you left PCW, we were not necessarily enemies….. The Scholar: True Ivan, but I have come here to do a job. I have come here to do what is best for Old School Wrestling. And I don't feel that booking a match between you and Reno is in the best interest of OSW. For one, if the difference in size ratio causes something unfortunate and unexpected to happen, we could have a whole matter of liability on our hands, and that wouldn't be good for the collective, would it Ivan? And isn't that what is important, the good of everyone? Ivan’s right eyebrow shoots up as he growls and suddenly gets very angry. He points his finger into the desk, caving a hole into the wood. Ivan: Listen to me, Howard Porter. When Reagan was in power, what she ‘thought’ could not stop me from doing what I’ve wanted. I want a match with Reno Davis, and I will get a Reno Davis. I am offering this one chance for you to do what I say, and if you don’t, then I will take matters into my hands. There is nothing that you and Ed can do to stop me…if need be, I will wipe out every wrestler in OSW in order to get at him…. The Scholar: That sounds like a challenge Mr. Stanislav. Ivan: That is Praporschik Stanislav...and there is no challenge to it Howard Porter. Ivan will do what Ivan wants. The Scholar: Well as long as I'm in power, Ivan will do what 'The Scholar' wants. Ivan roars and slams his fist into the desk, collapsing a leg and sending books sliding off the desktop. Ivan: YOU LISTEN TO ME LITTLE MAN!! I can crush you right now if I wish and not bat an eye!! You deny me what I want, and I’ll make your life living hell!! The time for politics is over! It is now time for WAR! Ivan Stanislav and his Red Army are ready to do whatever it takes!! The Scholar: Listen Ivan, you go on MY terms. I may just be acting CEO, but that also means I have full power while I'm here. You want to test me? You want to try and push me to the edge? It wouldn't be a sound decision on your part, if you fancy yourself an intelligent field general. Don't even try to outsmart me Ivan, because it can't be done. And the next time you hospitalize someone, I'm taking it out of your pay! Stanislav scoffs. Ivan: Take it out of my pay? Go ahead! I have no use for your money anyway! The Motherland takes care of me! Ivan turns on his heels and walks to the door. He pauses and turns around. Ivan: You think you’re so smart? Well here is history lesson that you should remember: Tyler Nelson tried to stop me, and I buried him! History can repeat itself, Howard Porter! You’re quite stupid for a “Scholar.” Just try to stop me Porter. Just try to get in my way. I have given you your chance, now, if you are smart man, you will stay out of my way!!! DYAAHAAHAA!! Ivan bursts into laughter and opens the door. He laughs in the doorway and slams it shut, pinching the door into the wall as he pulls it harder than usual and wedges it shut. The Scholar fixes his tie and sits down. The Scholar: Things never change…. Porter pushes his glasses back up onto his nose as the scene fades to black.
