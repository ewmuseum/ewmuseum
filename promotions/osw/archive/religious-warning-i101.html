Religious Warning!

2002-02-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Religious Warning! Written: 16/02/2002 The scene reopens at the ringside position of the OSW announce team. Nate: Welcome back to Slam!!! Apparently heightened security is truly still a number one priority since the 9-11 attacks. Robbie: Meanstreak should just be barred from the building. The guy is a ticking time bomb waiting to go off! Nate: I would have to agree with that. Now fans, my understanding is that our own Shanda Russell is standing by backstage with a very special guest!!! Robbie: Who? Nate: Well, whoever it is, let’s take it backstage to Shanda who is standing by. Shanda? The camera shot changes to the backstage interview area. The delightful Ms. Russell is standing in the area with a microphone waiting for her cue from her cameraman. Finally, she gets the cue and she begins. Shanda: Good Evening OSW fans. Tonight, I am here with one of OSW’s newest wrestlers. He made his surprising entrance at Crowning Glory last week when he attacked Vampir Nosferatu and the Epitome of Evil backstage with a steel chair and tonight he was victorious in his OSW debut! That’s right ladies and gentlemen; I am standing here with the bible thumping Southerner himself, Propaganda!! The camera pans over to capture the humungous holy man standing by Shanda. The camera is struggling to capture both Shanda and Propaganda in the shot. Propaganda’s glasses are reflecting the lights that brighten the interview area. Shanda begins her questioning. Shanda: Propaganda, last week you made your presence felt at Crowning Glory by attacking both Vampir and EoE. And then after laying waste to them, you sent your holy message to all of OSW. How do you actually expect to wipe out all of the evil from this federation? Propaganda reaches to take the microphone from the young reporter, but before he can get his massive hand on the mic, something streaks through the camera shot and strikes Propaganda to the ground. The camera pans over quickly to see Hessian on top of Propaganda pounding away on his face. Nate: HESSIAN IS ATTACKING PROPAGANDA!!! Hessian climbs to his feet and pulls Propaganda up by his collar as Azrael Cain appears in the shot at a slow cocky walk. Hessian slams the stunned Propaganda up against the wall. Hessian’s green eyes appear as if they are enflamed with hatred. Hessian brings his masked face up close to Propaganda’s. Hessian: Welcome to OSW, Holy man. You think that you can rid this federation of all the evil and hatred that Heresian Bloodlust has worked so hard to establish. This federation is heading to an apocalypse and who do you think that you are? This is the time of Heresian Bloodlust and Cain and I are going to see to it that no one gets in our way. Especially not some bible thumping worshiper of a dead god! Hessian head butts Propaganda in the face with the forehead of his steel mask. He then releases his grip on Propaganda so that Cain can get a word or two in. Cain: What the hell do you think you’re doing? You come into our federation and think that you can destroy evil and all those that worship it. You think that you are powerful enough to stop Heresian Bloodlust? Your religion and your god are dead. People only practice the faith because it makes them think that there is someone out there that offers them true and undying love. Well, let me tell you something, No One Loves You! We are giving you this chance to leave this federation, but if you continue to annoy us with your mission of holiness, than we are going to see to be forced to prove to you that your lord is dead. Cain reaches out and gives another right hook across the face Propaganda. Blood begins to flow from the large man’s mouth. Hessian grabs hold of Propaganda one more time. Hessian: You have been warned. You either make yourself scarce or feel the wrath of Heresian Bloodlust. Cain kicks Propaganda in the gut that causes him to double over. Hessian and Cain both grab hold of Propaganda. Both men hoist the large preacher of the word up over their heads and smash him into the concrete floor with a vicious two man powerbomb. Heresian Bloodlust exit the camera shot and walk down the dark hallway. The camera shot is now on the very still Propaganda who is still lying on the floor. The shot changes again back to the announcers’ table. Nate: Well, it certainly seems as if Heresian Bloodlust is here to take care of business. Robbie: And that business is pain! Alright! Steven: Look at that bible thumping hypocrite! BOCA! Nate: Well, needless to say, Cain and Hessian better be ready for battle this week because tonight there is going to be war when they take on both Ivan and Meanstreak!
