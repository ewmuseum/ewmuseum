Jesus St Crux v Jason Winters

2002-05-02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jesus St Crux v Jason Winters Written: May 2, 2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam! Up next - the debut of two men very familiar with each other if not with the fans. Robbie: For their crappy indy belt at that? Nate: I have already explained the circumstances surrounding this match. Robbie (sarcastically): Suuure. That makes this match all that much more tolerable. Just go Earl... Earl: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is an unsanctioned match for the UWA World Heavyweight Championship! The lights in the arena go dim, as 'Parabola' plays over the speakers. Pyros explode to the side of the entrance, as Jason Winters steps out and begins walking down the ramp. Earl: Introducing first, from Vancouver, British Columbia, standing 6'3' and weighing 237lbs, Jason Winters! The crowd cheers as Jason slides into the ring and mounts one of the far turnbuckles. He raises his arms in the air triumphantly. Steven: Mmmm... eye candy... The lights dim, and then the first chords of 'Fever Dog' play. With each drum riff, pyros explode to the side of the entrance ramp, and when the first words are spoken, Jesus and Caridad step out from backstage. Jesus stands at the top of the ramp, looks up at the ceiling, makes the sign of the cross, and then chops his hand downward. Jesus and Caridad run down and slide into the ring as pyros go off at the corners, and then they stand on opposite turnbuckles, and raise their arms as the crowd chants 'Jesus'. Earl: And his opponent, being accompanied by Caridad Mariposa, standing 5'8' and weighting 220lbs, from Juarez, Mexico, Jesus St. Crux! Nate: Jesus St Crux is making his first wrestling appearance on television in almost a year. In fact, his last match was against Jason Winters where St Crux won that very Title! Robbie: That now-defunct Title. Don't give it any more credit than it deserves. Steven: Why are Latinos so hot? Think Boca would mind if I ?saw the light? with Jesus? Robbie: Shut up, Steven. Nate: Anyways... Jason Winters is also making his first in-ring appearance since seemingly dying on another telecast. Caridad Mariposa exits the ring, as Jason Winters and Jesus St Crux begin circling each other warily, neither really wanting to make the first move. Referee Speedy Riggs calls for the bell as fingers are clenched and released, until finally the two men stop and quickly close into a grapple. St Crux is just a split second faster and drops to a knee as he brings Winters over his head with a fireman's carry takedown. Nate: St Crux is to his feet and bounces off the ropes. Dropkick to the back of Winters' head as he stands! St Crux starts to pull Winters to his feet, but the spry Canadian lunges forward and drives a shoulder into the Mexican's gut. Robbie: The ring rust shows early on St Crux! Jason Winters slams a second shoulder into St Crux for good measure as he regains his footing. He sends St Crux off the ropes and launches a spinning heel kick for the Mexican's head, but St Crux ducks underneath. Winters makes up for it a second later, connecting a superkick with St Crux's jaw as the Mexican comes off the ropes again. Nate: Jason Winters hooks the leg for the early pin. 1... 2... St Crux kicks out. Nate: Perhaps too early. Steven: That little Latino has a bunch of hepa!!! Winters drives a knee into St Crux, doubling over the smaller man as they stand. Latching on a side headlock, Winters runs for a few steps and plants St Crux into the mat with a bulldog. St Crux slowly rolls over onto his back as Winters climbs onto the second turnbuckle. Robbie: St Crux should have stayed down in Mexico... Nate: Winters drops off the second turnbuckle, and drives an elbow - No! St Crux rolls out of the way! Clutching his elbow, Jason Winters struggles to his feet. St Crux runs past him and springboards off the turnbuckles back through the air at the Canadian and rolls him up in a Crucifix Cradle. 1... 2... Jason Winters rolls through the pin. Nate: Surprise almost catches Jason Winters, but he's able to kick out! Robbie: Wake me up when it is finished. Steven: Look at the sweat glisten off of Jesus?s body. St Crux pulls Winters to his feet and backs the Canadian into the corner with a series of knife-edge chops, each accompanied by a WOO! from the crowd. An Irish Whip sends Winters across the ring, but when St Crux follows across and tries for a monkey flip, Winters holds the ropes and only St Crux falls to the mat. Robbie: That Winters... always thinking. Steven: Thinking is overrated. Latinos, like my Boca, like to act on impulse, passion and pure, unadulterated, emotion. The sound of Robbie audibly vomiting can be heard in the background. Winters grabs St Crux's legs, but the Mexican starts kicking. He regains his feet, well, one foot, as Winters is able to keep hold of the other. St Crux bounces once one his free foot, then launches an inverted enziguri that clips Winters on the side of the head. Nate: Jason Winters braces himself on the ropes, but he doesn't go down! Jesus St Crux grabs Winters' arms, and quickly snaps the Canadian's head into the mat with the Last Rites. St Crux covers with a lateral press while hooking one of Winters' legs. 1... 2... Speedy Riggs breaks up the pin, pointing at Jason Winters' foot sitting on the bottom rope. Nate: Great ring presence by Jason Winters. St Crux pulls Winters to his feet again and Irish Whips him across the ring, but the Canadian is able to stop in his tracks as St Crux charges. Winters sends St Crux into the air with a flapjack, hanging the crusierweight across the ropes. The Crowd: Ooooooooo!!! Robbie: LOOK!!! The camera shifts to the entryway as large man runs down the ramp and pushes Caridad aside as he slides into the ring. Unaware, Winters finishes the Cold Front by tossing St Crux into the corner. Speedy Riggs tries to stop the intruder, by he's brushed aside before a HUGE uppercut clocks Jason Winters. Steven: OH MY GOD! IT'S SID VISCIOUS! Nate: You idiot... it's Kanaida! Another former UWA World Heavyweight Champion! Steven: Too bad... Sid's very gentle you know... Speedy Riggs calls for the bell as he quickly escapes from the ring. Winner by DQ: Jason Winters Nate: Jason Winters wins by disqualification, but even if this had been a sanctioned title match, a title doesn't change hands on a disqualification! Hey, what's Kanaida doing now? Robbie: Off-hand... I'd say he's having fun. Kanaida pulls St Crux from the corner and gives him a stiff uppercut for good measure, then pulls Winters to his feet. The Northern Wonder doubles both men over and grabs them around their waists. Nate: What in the hell!!! After taking a deep breath, the big man tenses and lifts both men into the air. They hang there for a second before being powerbombed into the mat with Kanaida dropping as well for added momentum. The crowd explodes. Nate: GOOD GAWD!!! KANAIDA JUST HIT BOTH MEN WITH A POWERBOMB AT ONCE!!! WHAT POWER!!! Steven: I'll say!!! The crowd is still going nuts. The Crowd: OSW!!! OSW!!! OSW!!! OSW!!! OSW!!! OSW!!! Nate: A debut with impact for all three men! Kanaida stands between Winters and St Crux. He stares at them both for a bit until his gaze shifts to the UWA World Heavyweight Title. Steven: Kanaida wants that belt almost as much as I want him right now!!! Robbie: The question is why in the hell would he want that belt at all?!? Nate: Pride! Something you do not posess!!! While we wait for the carnage in the ring to be cleared, let's break to commerical.
