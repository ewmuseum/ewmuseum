Earl: Introducing the challengers, making their way to the ring... from Des Moines, Iowa, at a total combined weight of 541 pounds, William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft, POLITICALLY INCORRECT! Powell and Lovecraft are all smiles as they make their way to the ring, making 'I want the belt' motions as they approach the squared circle. Nate: The challengers look confident, and for good reason! I'd be surprised if Propaganda could even compete tonight. And Hessian's one hell of a tough guy, but I don't think even he can beat Politically Incorrect by himself, ESPECIALLY with Ronin as the referees. As if magically on cue, the house lights darken and 'Feel A Fear' hits. The fans respond with a loud but mixed reaction, as Hessian stalks out onto the stage, his championship belt strapped around his massive waist. Hessian steps to his left, and the fans' mixed reaction turns to one primarily of cheers as Propaganda walks out, wearing the other tag team title belt, and displaying a huge blood-soaked bandage wrapped around his head. Earl: And their opponents... at a total combined weight of 723 pounds, the OSW World Tag Team Champions... HEAVEN AND HEEEEELL!!! Hessian and Propaganda look at one another, then grimly start toward the ring, unbuckling their title belts and letting them fall on the stage side by side as they advance down the ramp. In the ring, Powell and Lovecraft are angrily speaking to Ronin and pointing at the stage. Register and Wayne seem nonplussed as well, but merely gesture toward the advancing champions. Nate: The challengers seem upset at Ronin! I'm sensing some collusion between PI and our special guest referees! Powell and Lovecraft both shoot Ronin a dirty look, then roll out of the ring and sprint up the aisle toward the champs. Register turns and indifferently signals for the bell seconds before the four men come within reach of each other. DING DING DING! At the last second, Hessian steps in front of Propaganda. He blocks Powell's first punch, tags Lovecraft with a backhanded blow then decks Powell with a big roundhouse right! He then turns and grabs Lovecraft, and throws him headfirst into the railing. Propaganda starts to move in, but Hessian growls at his partner, and God's Ultikmatum takes a reluctant step back. Nate: Who would've thought we'd ever see this? Hessian, the man that tried to crucify Propaganda less than two months ago, now is protecting God's Ultimatum! Robbie: Only because he loves holding gold, Nate! Hessian doesn't give a damn about Propaganda otherwise. Hessian throws Powell against the railing next to Lovecraft. Then, with a guttural roar, he charges with a double clothesline. The members of PI duck, however, hoist Hessian and Hot Shot him across the steel rail! Steven: OUCH! That didn't work! Hessian drops to one knee, clutching at his throat. Powell stays on top of Hessian, stomping him while Lovecraft approaches Propaganda. Proppa-G motions for the Governor to come on, but Lovecraft catches Propaganda with a boot to the gut, followed by a big hook to the temple that puts Propaganda on one knee. The big man struggles back to his feet, but that just allows Lovecraft to tag him with a vicious right-left combination, as Powell takes a sound cord and wraps it around Hessian's throat, proceeding to try and throttle the Goliath. Robbie: Looking good for the challengers right now! Nate: Propaganda does not seem himself. I think it's obvious he sustained a concussion during that attack by Ronin earlier tonight! Powell reaffirms his grip around Hessian's throat, and doesn't notice the Goliath reach up and grip the cord with both gauntleted hands. Suddenly, the big man jerks forward, connecting on a super snapmare that sends Powell over the rail and crashing into the seats! Meanwhile, Lovecraft is battering Propaganda back up the rail, with Register and Wayne now lurking in the aisle, waiting to count a fall. Propaganda, however, starts fighting back with thunderous blows of his own. After landing a couple, however, Lovecraft ducks, and Propaganda misses badly. Lovecraft pops back to his feet, grabs Proppa-G in an inverted facelock and connects on a reverse DDT! He makes the cover. One... Two... Hessian runs in and stomps Lovecraft to break the count by Register! Wayne, however, heads in and tries to separate Hessian from the combat. Nate: Come on, what is this? It's no DQ, what does The Legend think he's doing? Hessian grabs Wayne and tosses him out of the way effortlessly, as Lovecraft goes for a second cover on Propaganda. One... Hessian yanks Lovecraft up, and lifts him in a fireman's carry position, tucking Lovecraft's head. Nate: Hessian I think is going for Hellter Skelter! As Hessian starts to spin the Governor, however, Register reaches out and low blows the Goliath! Nate: COME ON! The fans boo as Hessian drops to one knee and Lovecraft slides out of the big man's grip. Meanwhile, Wayne is helping Powell back over the rail, as Propaganda sits up weakly. Register reaches over the rail and pulls out a steel chair. He turns, and BLASTS the defenseless preacher right in the head with it. Nate: GOOD GOD! GOOD GOD! HE JUST ANNIHILATED PROPAGANDA WITH THAT CHAIR! Robbie: Haha, this is great! Nate: Dammit, this is like a 4 on 2 match! I respect the skills of Hessian and Propaganda--though I don't always agree with their tactics--but even they can't beat these four men at once with God's Ultimatum in such bad shape! Steven: These four guys hated each other a couple of weeks ago, but they seem to have buried the hatchet to try and destroy Heaven & Hell! Powell and Lovecraft now are laying the boots to Hessian. Robbie: Smart psychology, though, as they try to take out Hessian before trying to pin Propaganda! PI get Hessian to his feet, and both ensnare him in front facelocks and start to go for a suplex... but Hessian blocks! They try again... but Hessian blocks again! He starts to try and reverse it... when Register nails Hessian in the kidneys with the edge of the chair! This staggers and surprises Hessian enough for PI to lift Hessian, and connect on a double brainbuster! Nate: AND HESSIAN GOES HEADFIRST INTO THE RAMP! The fans are really booing now, as Ronin stand over Hessian and Propaganda. Wayne starts pretending to check and see if Propaganda can continue, then gives the Smart Ass a comical thumbs-up. Meanwhile, Propaganda rolls over onto his side, and fresh blood is seen seeping from under the bandage into his eyes. Lovecraft heads over and covers Proppa-G. One... Two... Thre--PROPAGANDA KICKS OUT! Lovecraft looks up, incredulous, as the fans go crazy. Steven: Listen to the cheers for Propaganda's guts! Lovecraft covers again, and Register counts faster. OneTwoThre--PROPAGANDA KICKS OUT! Nate: HE DID IT AGAIN! Finally Powell rolls to his feet, and dips into his tights... to withdraw a pair of handcuffs. Nate: Oh, now this can't be good! Powell turns and snaps one of the cuffs around Hessian's wrist, then snaps the other around the ringside railing. Nate: Oh no... Wayne turns and motions to Lovecraft and Powell. Politically Incorrect get the bloody battered Propaganda to his feet and start leading him up the ramp. The knees of God's Ultimatum buckle, but PI hoist Propaganda back onto his feet somewhat and now are dragging him up the ramp. Steven: I think they're gonna throw him off the stage! Robbie: Haha, remember: Falls count anywhere! Lovecraft and Powell reach the stage, then throw Propaganda in a heap onto it. Register and Wayne follow a couple of steps behind, smirking and sizing each other up for the belts, it seems. Finally, the politician duo pull Propaganda to his feet. They turn and start dragging him toward the edge of the stage. Nate: OK, a match is one thing! But someone has to stop this! It's 10 feet to the floor! In Propaganda's condition, this could kill him! Robbie: ... What's your point? Suddenly, the house lights go out. Nate: What the--? Steven: Robbie, don't touch me there! Robbie: Heh, keep dreamin', nancy boy. The fans are buzzing in anticipation, when suddenly, a white light flashes through the entire arena. Steven: GAH! Nate: A PIERCING white light is filling the arena--much as it did during the Tag Rumble! The white light flashes again, then suddenly... a section of the stage bursts into flame! White flame. Ronin and PI instinctively leap back, as a chorus begins to sing a hymn at near deafening volume. Nate: What in the world is this?!?! The fire builds with the music, and Ronin and PI are all looking this way and that, trying to anticipate an attack. Then, suddenly, the fire goes out, and the house lights come up. There, standing beside an empty hole in the stage... is... Nate: HOLY SHIT! Steven: Literally! Nate: IT'S THE REVEREND! JON HULL IS HERE! HE'S HERE IN OSW! Robbie: WHOOOOOA! Only now do Ronin and PI realize who is amongst them. Hull turns toward Propaganda and raises his left hand, then toward Hessian and raises his right. Instantly, both of them reach their feet! Nate: OH MY GOD! Propaganda, seemingly unfazed by his beating, advances toward the members of PI. He grabs them from behind, and starts slamming their heads together with repeated double noggin knockers. Meanwhile, Hessian actually rips loose a section of the guardrail and starts hustling up the aisle, wielding it like a weapon. Register and Wayne turn and start to accost Hull, when the Reverend's fists lash out like lightning... and both members of Ronin end up flat on their backs! Nate: REVEREND JUST DECKED RONIN! Hessian reaches the stage, still attached to the section of guardrail as Propaganda releases the members of PI, sending them sprawling. Hessian swings the guardrail at Powell... but he ducks, and it smashes into the back of Wayne, knocking him sprawling forward to the stage, dazed! Nate: OH MY! Propaganda hooks Powell, and connects on his release T-Bone suplex... sending Powell into the hole after him! Nate: EXODUS! EXODUS! EXODUS SENDS POWELL INTO THE HOLE! Hull raises his arms to Hessian and Propaganda, who raise their arms in salute to the Reverend. Robbie: We wondered what sort of force could get Hessian and Propaganda to unite, I think we just got our answer! Propaganda and Hessian turn, arms still raised, and grab Lovecraft by the throat. The two men cinch their grips, and connect on a vicious double chokeslam on the unforgiving steel stage! Propaganda then makes the cover and Register, looking at the fate of Wayne and Powell, drops down, turning to stare murderously at Hull. Without looking at Lovecraft's shoulders once, Register counts slowly. One. Two. Three. Nate: HEAVEN & HELL RETAIN! AGAINST ALL ODDS, THE CHAMPS KEEP THE TITLES! Hull steps forward, plucking the title belts up off the stage. He turns and hands them to Hessian and Propaganda as Register, watching them all the while, helps his partner to his feet and starts backing toward the entrance. Nate: We have found the answer! The force that drives Hessian and Propaganda to merge has been revealed! And his name... IS THE REVEREND JON HULL! Fans, for Robbie Decker and Steven Smith this is Nate Dumas... GOOD NIGHT! Hull raises the arms of Hessian and Propaganda in victory as Goliath and God's Ultimatum raise their belts with their free arms, bellowing in fury as the OSW copyright logo appears on the screen.
