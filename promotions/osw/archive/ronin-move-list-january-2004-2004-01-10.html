Ronin — move list (January 2004)

10 January 2004

Tag Team Set Up Move: 10 punch in the corner. (Register irish whips the opponent into the corner, climbs to the second turnbuckle and delivers 10 consecutive punches to his face before getting down. As the dazed opponent stumbles out, Register slips behind them and prepares for the electric chair lift. ) Tag Finisher: Seppuku (Register gets the opponent in the electric chair (Hart Attack/Doomsday Device) position and faces the turnbuckle. Wayne leaps off and executes a somersault neckbreaker as Register falls backwards. Sometimes, on opponents who are easy to lift, Register will replace the electric chair setup with a powerbomb setup, so he will throw them down as Wayne executes the neckbreaker, making it much more forceful. {Electric Chair or Powerbomb/Top Rope Blockbuster Combo} ) Other Tag Team Moves: Double Teams: Total Elimination Homage - (spinning heel kick/sweep combo) Dosun - (Wayne gets the opponent in a corner and monkeyflips him out, where Register catches him, spins 180 degrees, and delivers a sit-out powerbomb) Chikuso - (Register executes a modified Queen Angelito stretch in the middle of the ring, and Wayne jumps from a top turnbuckle with a guillotine leg drop) Boko-Boko - (Wayne picks the opponent off the ground by his legs in a wheelbarrow suplex position, allowing Register the take their head and execute a 3/4 turn neckbreaker) Akumu - (one man locks in a figure four perpedicular to a turnbuckle, allowing the other to climb the ropes and execute a shooting star press) Zuki-Zuki - (Wayne grabs downed opponent, stomach-up, by their feet and executes a catapult towards a corner, while Register jumps off that corner's turnbuckle and delivers a missile dropkick) Itami - (the opponent is sprawled out on the mat, face up, perpendicular to a turnbuckle. Register climbs for a moonsault, and Wayne hooks under his arms facing the opposite direction. Both men jump, with Wayne executing a crucifix powerbomb on Register, tossing him even harder in a moonsault onto the opponent) Spike Vertebreaker Partner-Assist Springboard Hurricanrana - Opponent mounted on the corner, Register drops to all fours, Wayne bounces off him and delivers a hurricanrana, usually to the outside. Alternate Finisher: Culture Shock - (One man lifts a downed opponent onto their shoulders in a fireman's carry. The other climbs the nearby turnbuckle. At once, the opponent is thrown off of the shoulders into a flapjack/jumping DDT combo.) Jeffrey Wayne Wrestling Style: Technical Sadist, with a bit of flying Set Up Move: Hostile Takeover (double ace crusher - stunner without release followed by diamond cutter ) Finisher: (double underhook facebuster (yes, it's the Pedigree) ) Other Moves: IMPACT FINISHER: The Business (see listing) SUBMISSION FINISHER: The LegendLock (crippler crossface) SIGNATURE MOVES: Hostile Takeover (double ace crusher - see listing) Crossfire (spinning double hotshot) Superstar Press (shooting star press - formerly a finisher) StunPlex (hits a stunner on his knees, pivots and executes a belly-to-belly suplex on dazed opponent) IMPACT MOVES shining wizard dragon screw northern lights bomb curtain call wheelbarrow powerbomb double arm DDT superkick belly-to-belly suplex snap suplex hot-shot spinebuster enzuigiri dragon suplex (on smaller opponents) diamond dust michinoku driver SUBMISSION MOVES reverse figure four leglock texas cloverleaf STF TOP ROPE MOVES top rope tiger driver top rope armbreaker (to set up LegendLock) top rope armdrag (to set up LegendLock) moonsault CHEATS low blow (if ref is distracted) Singerapore Cane shot (if the moment presents itself) Matt Register Wrestling Style: Technical/High Impact/Innovative Brutality Very methodical, waiting for mistakes and then finding new ways to exploit an advantage. Typically he sticks to mat work and chain wrestling with an emphasis on going for the 'home run' big moves. Set Up Move: (... ) Finisher: Anything he damn well pleases. (see moveset for common trademarks ) Other Moves: Psychology: Against Wrestlers Smaller Than/The Same Size As Himself (roughly) – He’ll stick to a mostly grounded offense, going with technical and power moves, usually taking the chance to string together moves for a more high-impact offence. He’ll take more time to show off and taunt here, too, and it’s in these matches when he tends to get ahead of himself and make mistakes. Too much focus on trying to hurt his opponent, and not enough on beating them. Against Wrestlers Much Larger - Much more calculating and cautious. He’ll fly a lot more, and rely on strikes (punches, kicks, forearms, the like) for most of his offense. For the most part, he’ll take more punishment in this matches (as everyone would) and get his shots in on mistakes made by his opponent. These are also the matches where he’ll focus on one area and break it down. Submissions are prominent against big opponents. He’ll look for the easiest out possible. Moves (Use your judgement on what he can hit on guys of varying sizes) A Little Dose of Sarcasm: (his old finisher) Three-Quarter Nelson Sit-Out Neckbreaker. Like a Greetings From Asbury Park, but with both hands locked over the opponent's head to snap it down as he sits out. If he hits A Little Dose of Sarcasm against smaller guys, he’ll actually mount the opponent on his shoulder, either via a scoop slam or tilt-a-whirl-type setup or something like that. He’ll hold them on his shoulder for a bit before dropping in the sit out…except late in big matches when he’s desperate to hit it. ALDOS against big guys is more quick and to the point. Since he can’t lift the huge guys without help from someone else, he’ll usually lock in the ¾ Nelson quick and drop. In this case, it looks more like a Diamond Cutter or Diamond Dust floatover with the ¾ nelson lock, falling in an Ace Crusher/Stunner. The Smart Remark: ½-Nelson lock from behind, lifted slightly with the free hand on opponent’s back, then dropped like a uranage (Rock Bottom). {1/2-Nelson Uranage Slam} Michinoku Buster: Fisherman’s Buster dropped between the legs in a sit out position. {Sit-Out Falcon-Arrow Fisherman’s Buster - Ki Crusher '99.} DVDDT: DVD setup, with the legs thrown off and the opponent pulled over by his head as Register drops, scissoring his legs to land in a Reverse DDT. Amethyst Fusion: Scoop slam into a quick Emerald Fusion/Northern Lights Bomb (Snow Plow) type of finish. Basically like a Simonizer without a pause in between the scoop and the drop. WiseCrack: Trap-arm swinging neckbreaker, a Roll The Dice-type move from a regular standing facelock with the appropriate arm locked as well. Pumphandle Cradle Piledriver Electric Chair Drop onto a turnbuckle Powerbomb to tossing Flatliner Rolling Suplexes: He'll hit three in a row, but not three of the same kind. Any three, switching his lock as he pulls the dazed guy up. Options are: Northern Lights, T-Bone, German, Belly-to-Belly, Exploder, Half Nelson, Dragon, Tiger, Sambo and Back Suplexes. Roaring Elbow Running inside knee lift Rainbow Kick: facing away (or angled away) from opponent, he kicks one leg up and out, swing his body around to face them, then brings the other leg behind it in an arc, coming down with a roundhouse-style finish on the side of the opponent’s head. Springboard Rounding Splash Savage Elbow: Easily enough, a Macho Man-style top rope elbowdrop, including the pre-move-double-arm-point-to-the-heavens. Texas Cloverleaf Deviled Crab: crossover half-crab. He locks the opponent’s right leg under his right arm, pulling it across his body and elevating them (if that makes sense). Against bigger guys, he’ll look for the Lance Storm baseball-slide-and-grab-the-leg setup. The Laughing Stock: Queen Angelito stretch rolled over the guy's head into a modified surfboard. STF/Kataha jime combo Buffalo sleeper Half-nelson sleeperhold Assumption: He stands on the second rope, outside, locking his legs in the ropes. The opponent is underneath him. He applies a full nelson and lifts them off the ground, leaving them in a hanging full nelson with the grip held tight to avoid their arms falling out limply. If he can't lift the guy, he'll merely bring them to the ropes with him and kick them off, holding them there. Obviously he has to be able to at least suspend them.
