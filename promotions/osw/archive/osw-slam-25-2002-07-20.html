Slam! 25, 20 July 2002

20 July 2002

The show opens as a bright red Porsche comes screeching to a halt in the parking lot of the Igloo Sports Arena, in Pittburgh, Pennsylvania, and as so often the case in professional wrestling, a camera just happens to be there to document the action. The camera shifts to ground level, catching the glimpse of a pair of Air Jordan’s stepping out of the vehicle. The shot slowly pans up to reveal the sneaker, and the foot that currently inhabits it, belong to none other than Andrew “Riptah” Washington, one half of one of the OSW Tag Team Champions, Da Westsidaz. The camera pans to the right, where Riptah’s long time partner, Short Circuit, is shown exiting the passenger side of the vehicle. From inside the arena, we can hear the cheers of the fans, who are obviously watching the happenings in the parking lot on the massive Jumbotron inside the arena. Short Circuit grabs his duffel bag and slings it over his shoulder, as the larger Riptah grabs two duffel bags and carries them off as he kicked his door shut. The pair walk down the parking lot, looking directly at the entrance of the arena. They both take one deep breath, looked at one another, smirk then nod. They knew tonight was another challenge, another defense. This time it was against a team named The Throwbacks, Dave Gibson and Clay Vendetta. Sure, it was a powerful team they were going up against...but they didn't fear anything... The had beaten the best. They had overcome The Blood Brothers. They had beaten the Assassins. The had defeated Civil Disobedience. The had won gold from Heresian Bloodluse. Nothing would get in the way of another win, another successful defense... Or so they thought. Suddenly the Tag Team Champions heard three voices yell out one single command in unison. Like drill army soldiers giving their orders to their troops, it was a command that let Da Westsidaz know...danger was near. The command was... "NOW!" Da Westsidaz both turn around to face the men who gave the command, but as they turned around...both men were floored and sent down onto the concrete. The sickening sound of flesh against concrete echoed in the parking lot, as Da Westsidaz opened their eyes to see none other than Armour and John Mammoth standing in front of their fallen bodies. From inside the arena, a rousing chorus of boos is heard. The tag team champs than look behind the two monsters to see the three men who commanded the attack...Alan Kriegman, who was holding TCM back, PHD and Howard Porter. Porter looked at the two large men in Armour and Mammoth and stepped forward. "WHAT ARE YOU WAITING FOR?! DO AS WE PLANNED!" And without any further notice, Armour quickly grabs Short Circuit by the neck and pulls him up off the ground. Armour holds the much smaller SC above his head by his neck, and finally hurled SC into a nearby Lexus, denting the hood. Armour watches as SC lay groaning on the hood, while the nearly 500 pound Mammoth grabs a hold of Riptah and proceeds to slam his head into a nearby pillar. But Mammoth wouldn't allow Riptah to fall...he grabs Riptah by his waist and lifts the 300 pounder up off the ground. Mammoth then proceeds to run with Riptah and crash him HARD into a hood of a car with a Belly to Belly Suplex. Satisified with their handy work, Mammoth and Armour look at one another and nod. Suddenly, Alan Kriegman is seen scurrying behind a car and picking up an object. Kriegman runs back to TCM, who is fuming and ready to pounce, and hands TCM the weapon...the weapon that is...a trashcan. TCM's eyes widen in insanity as he stares at the trashcan and smiles, and than looks back at Kriegman. Alan Kriegman looks back at TCM and whispers, "Just like we talked about...go for it!" And without any more time wasted, TCM charges with the trashcan firmly in his grasp. Armour and Mammoth looked as TCM charged and quickly pulled SC to his feet and held him upright. TCM leaps in the air, trashcan in hand, and smashes the can over SC's face, causing SC to fall to the concrete hard in a pool of his own blood. Riptah slowly begins to get to his feet, but it did nothing for him...except cause more pain. Immediately after he got to his feet, he felt to large hands grasp tightly around his neck...the hands of Armour and John Mammoth. Porter and PHD yell out to the two men. PHD: MAMMOTH!!! LIKE FINISH HIM!!! Armour and Mammoth nod in agreement and lifted Riptah up above their heads...but instead of slamming Riptah down on the concrete, they look to PHD, who has opened a dumpster and is pointing his pasty fingers at it. PHD: DOCK HIM IN SICK BAY!!!! Armour and Mammoth charge with Riptah in their grasp, landing a DOUBLE CHOKESLAM into the dumpster...slamming Riptah amongst the trash and steel walls. Both Armour and Mammoth nod at a job well done, and then the massive pair notice TCM on the other side. TCM was not giving in, and continued to pound the trashcan over SC's face. TCM had kept it up for nearly 30 seconds, until Kriegman finally pulls him off of SC, while whispering something in TCM's ear. TCM grins insanely as he throws the mangled trashcan down and pulls up the bloodied Short Circuit. TCM walks SC over to a nearby Jeep that was parked next to the open dumpster and rolls SC onto the hood. Mammoth backs away a bit, to give TCM room, as Armour stands next to the dumpster watching the mayhem ensue. TCM places SC between his legs and looked at Kriegman for approval. Kriegman smiles and gives TCM the thumbs up, as TCM lifts SC up then proceeds to excute the PYRO DRIVER INTO THE DUMPSTER, ON TOP OF RIPTAH! The dumpster nearly explodes with sound, as TCM lands his cradle piledriver onto Da Westsidaz. Kriegman applauds the mayhem, and smiled as TCM crawled out of the dumpster. Armour and Mammoth both look at TCM, but then look back over at Porter and PHD, who both smile and yell out, "Armour...Mammoth...END IT FOR GOOD! MAKE SURE THEY DO NOT SPOIL OUR PLANS!" Immediately Armour stomps off to the corner of the dumpster and pull out two steel chairs. He quickly lobs one to Mammoth, who catches it in his baseball glove sized hands with ease. Both monsters then proceed to climb up the dumpster and lift the chairs above their heads. CRACK! WHACK! SMACK! In one split second, Armour and Mammoth shower Da Westsidaz with a huge amount of sickening chair shots! Kriegman smiles in pleasure, as TCM grasped onto Kriegman's leg as he watched Mammoth and Armour destroy the Tag Team Champions. Every sickening chair shot brings a whole new stain of blood on each chair. The figures of Da Westsidaz finally stopped moving, but the assault still continues. The steel continues to pour down upon the Tag Team Champions. Finally, the two monsters stop their assault as Howard Porter steps forward and raises an open palm above his head. Armour and Mammoth both look to Porter, then finally stop, then climb from the dumpster, with their crimson stained chairs in hand. They then proceed to close the lids to the dumpster. Suddenly, PHD rushes running forward with a steel chain and three separate locks. Immediately PHD wraps the thick chain around the handles of the dumpster's lid, and triple locks it. After locking the final lock, PHD turns to his five comrades, and gives a huge, bucktoothed grin. Porter smiles in response, as the eerie sounds of Alan Kriegman’s trademark laugh serve echo throughout the parking area….as the brutal and disturbing scene fades to black…. . . . . . . The usual opening for Old School Wrestling’s Saturday Night Slam, featuring OSW superstars executing high impact moves on unwitting opponents, with each move causing a Batman-esque SLAM! graphic to splash onto the screen, begins to roll. Wrestlers highlighted are Rhett Locke, Ivan Stanislav, Reno Davis and Amy Campbell. . . . . . . The scene reopens backstage at the Igloo, and it’s a room simply labeled: “The Throwbacks”. Note, if you will, the absence of any shiny logos or even a star: no, the label looks simply written on a piece of first-aid tape with a Sharpie. Fitting. As the camera moves into the room, a few things are visible; mostly, the team of “Classic” Clay Vendetta and “Old School” Dave Gibson, which elicits a BIG pop in the Pittsburgh crowd for the number-one contenders to the OSW Tag Team Championship. But also in the room, curiously, is a large mat set up on the floor, a duffel bag at Clay’s feet, and a large, wooden black box lying on the ground. Both men are sitting on benches in the room; Gibson lacing up his boot tightly, Vendetta rustling through the duffel bag. Gibson: So what the hell did you get here anyway? Vendetta is still poking through the bag as he replies... Vendetta: It’s for training. Gibson: For Da Westsidaz tonight? The crowd pop is TREMENDOUS for the current OSW Tag Champs, who the fans, hope will be able to go tonight. Vendetta: Nope. I’ve got a ton of respect for Riptah and Short Circuit, Dave. Sure, they might be a little... unorthodox. Hell, a little thuggish, even. But they get the job done. They’ve got arguably the best tag-team resume of all time. But us, Dave... we’ve got skill and talent on our sides... and arguably the best technical team ever assembled. Should be a hell of a match... but we’ll wrestle, and they’ll wrestle, and the better team should come out. Damn straight I think we’re that team, though. Finally finding what he was looking for, Vendetta produces a crowbar from the bag. Vendetta: This isn’t about Da Westsidaz, though. This is about the big, Gothic pain in our asses... those B-movie rejects in The Cult. You see, I pulled a few strings and managed to find somebody who might be able to help us ascertain a weak point in The Cult. At the very least, he’ll be good training. Gibson looks puzzled, but Vendetta, whistling as he pries, wrenches the top off from the box. A groaning noise comes out, and a form sits straight up, Undertaker-style, brushing packing peanuts off of his chest. Getting up to his feet, the large man dressed in black a black robe, wearing a skull mask rises in a flurry of Styrofoam... and in the outside crowd a BIG pop can be heard from a loyal group of fans who made the transition from PCW as they instantly recognize who it is. The recognition is made by Dave Gibson, as well... Gibson: Dude... that’s DARK GIMMICK~! MONSTER pop outside, as those who didn’t instantly recognize the man recognize the name. Gibson: How the hell did you get him? Vendetta: Well, it’s funny you ask. I was talking to Kid Wonder one day in the locker rooms and it turns out that he acquired DG in some bet on a match a year or two back. Won him from Clyde, I think. Anyhow, he doesn’t have a lot of use for him, so he rents him out... you know, parties, barmitzphahs, anyplace that could use an authentic Dark Gimmick. Makes a hefty amount of side cash, too. So I figured “this is perfect” and rented him for the week. Gibson: Wow. So... uh... what does he do? Vendetta holds up a red-and-black leather bound book, upon which can be clearly read in Gothic red handwriting: “INSTRUCTIONS”. Vendetta: I’ve been reading the manual. For one, he’s good at ritual sacrifice. Rooting again through the bag, Vendetta produces a stuffed chicken and a large meat cleaver. Placing the chicken on the bench, Vendetta hands the cleaver to Dark Gimmick... *THWOCK!* With one clean stroke, the chicken’s head bounces cleanly off to the floor below. Reaching down to the chicken, Gimmick plucks a large handful of feathers, and in the blink of an eye, arranges them into a crude pentagram on the bench. Wiping off his hands, DG goes back to his stern, emotionless stance. Vendetta: He’s also a card-carrying member of the Satanic Jobber’s Union. DG reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a wallet, presenting it to Gibson. Shuffling through it, Gibson pulls out a black card... Gibson: Yup, that looks legit, alright. Wow, even signed in real goat’s blood, it says... that’s impressive. Does he give interviews? Vendetta: Of course! Reaching back into the duffel bag, Vendetta grabs a microphone and hands it to the monster. In a pitch somewhere between James Earl Jones and Barry White, Gimmick growls... Dark Gimmick: You.... will... fall... before... the... might... of... Dark... Gimmick.... grrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrr. Peon... tonight... you... will... taste... your... DOOOOOOM!! Doooooooom.... doooooom.... dooomity dooooom dooooom..... Vendetta snatches away the microphone and tosses it back into the bag. Dark Gimmick crosses his arms once again. Vendetta: He’ll go on like that forever, if you let him. Anyhow, here’s the real reason why I got him: training. Here, head over to the mat. Gibson and Dark Gimmick walk over and stand on opposite ends of the mat. Vendetta stands in between them with the duffel bag, then takes a few steps back to the edge of the practice mat. Gibson gets into a ready stance, as Gimmick... well, stands and stares. Vendetta: Alright, Dave, he’s just like Hessian and Nosferatu... he’s got an arsenal, man. Flipping through the “manual”, Vendetta comes to a page which he’s marked with a Post-It note. Reading from the book... Vendetta: “Dark Gimmick features over 200 moves, all of which have been given ludicrous names. These 200 moves feature over 50 finishers, thus completely negating the point of have a signature move. With Dark Gimmick, EVERY move is a signature move!” See? Just like The Cult! So I want you to take a few of his finishers... don’t worry, Dave, it’ll be okay. Dark Gimmick, go easy, just go through the motions. Dark Gimmick: Yessss.... master. Vendetta: And don’t say that. It scares the piss outta me. Gibson looks... well, nonplussed to say the least. Gibson: So you just want me to lie down and take this guy’s moves? Vendetta: Basically. Hey, how are you going to learn the counters? Commitment to the craft, my friend. Commitment to the craft. Gibson: *sigh* Fine, fine, let’s get this over with. Dark Gimmick takes two steps forward... and executes a weak clothesline that barely knocks Gibson off of his feet. Turning towards Vendetta, DG tilts his head in a creepy manner... Dark Gimmick: That one... is called... THE HANGMAN’S YARDARM OF THE APOCALYPSE. Vendetta: Uh... terrific. What else have you got? Turning back towards Gibson, Dark Gimmick drops a weak elbow across his chest as Gibson rolls his eyes. Getting slowly back to his feet, Dark Gimmick tilts his head eerily again... Dark Gimmick: That move... is known as... DEATH BY PESTILENCE-ENGULFED METEOR SKULLS. Now even Vendetta is rolling his eyes. Gimmick is unfazed, though, dropping a leg across Gibson before sitting back up and tilting his head again... Dark Gimmick: I call... that one... SATAN’S FLAMING SWORD OF UTTER DISARRAY SKEWERING CUTE LITTLE BABY BUNNIES. Gibson shoves his way out from underneath Dark Gimmick, clearly annoyed. Gibson: Jesus Christ, “flaming” is right... *THUD!* The sound is that of Dark Gimmick’s head hitting the mat as the monster collapses. Looking at him, Gibson shakes is head... then realizes something is amiss. Checking his pulse... Gibson: CLAY! He’s DEAD! Vendetta: Oh, yeah, it happens. Gibson jumps to his feet, clearly stunned, his mouth agape and his eyes frantic... Gibson: What the HELL do you MEAN “IT HAPPENS???” We’ve got a DEAD BODY here! Vendetta leans over him and kicks Dark Gimmick... no response. Reaching into a duffel bag and pulling out a black body bag, Vendetta starts to tuck DG’s feet in. Vendetta: Yeah, calm down, man. He is undead, after all. He’ll be fine in a day or two, forget about it. KW told me this might happen... he just said to put Dark Gimmick into the bag and drop him off outside his locker room, and he’d handle things. Zipping DG up, Vendetta heads back to the bench and gathers the crowbar to head back into the duffel bag. Looking less stunned and more annoyed, Gibson turns to him: Gibson: Hey. We’ve got a tag team championship match tonight; some of the biggest men in OSW want a piece of us aside from that... don’t you think you could be taking this a little more seriously? *WHAM!!... clink, clink, clink...* In a fit of rage, Vendetta SPIKES the crowbar to the floor, and it skitters across the concrete as Clay turns to Gibson, eyes ablaze... Vendetta: Seriously? Don’t EVER accuse me of not taking things seriously. You should know DAMN well that NOBODY touches my woman and gets away with it. The last guy who tried that found himself at the end of his career, and the last words on his resume read “Loss: submission, to Clay Vendetta”. Tonight, there are ONLY two things on my agenda: gold, and revenge. Da Westsidaz are a hell of a team, and if I can’t get the gold... well, damn, I’ll be disappointed... but revenge? I’m not leaving Pittsburgh until I get it. Reaching back into the duffel bag one more time, Vendetta, still enraged, produces two more items: matching baseball bats, in what’s quickly becoming a Throwback trademark. Grabbing one bat and extending the other, handle-first to Gibson, Vendetta speaks, softly... Vendetta: Tonight, we go hunting... Taking the bat with a smile on his face, Gibson heads to the door, and Vendetta is soon behind him... Gibson: That’s what I’m talking about. *WHAM!* Vendetta leaves a parting shot on the doorframe with his bat as he passes through it... and as the Throwbacks go stalking their prey, the camera focuses on the dented door...and the scene fades to black. . . . . . . The scene reopens inside the arena section of the Igloo in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, as pyros shoot off from the ceiling, ringposts and entryway, while “Born in the US” by Bruce Springsteen hits the PA system. Cameras zoom around the arena, taking samples of the crowd. The first stop for the cameras is on a group of fans holding up posters on former fWo Superstar and Pittsburgh native, Lance Knight. Among the young men holding up posters of the now-unemployed night, several of the youths signs which read “HEY JANE!!! HIRE LANCE!!!” Quickly moving away from the scene suggesting OSW should hire overpriced, former fWo superstars, the camera settles in on a group of Pittsburgh area sanitation workers out to support their man, Trashcan Man, who are not only wearing their bright orange work uniforms, but have brought the tools of their trade with them as well: Aluminum Trashcans. Shifting away from the painful reminder of what took place out in the parking lot at the start of the show, we shift to ringside, where one of the biggest nerds in baseball history, former Pittsburgh Pirates close Kent Tekulve, sits in the front row with three of his dorkfied children, holding up brand new OSW Professor Herbert D. Dorkiman Wrestle buddies. Finally, mercifully, the cameras shift to the ringside position of the OSW announce team. Nate Dumas: Hello everyone and welcome to this edition of OSW’s Saturday Night Slam!!! I am Nate Dumas and I am joined here at ringside by my partners in crime, Steven Smith and Robbie Decker!!! We are coming to you live from the sold out Igloo in the steel city of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and folks do we have a great lineup for you tonight, in which every single OSW title is on the line!!! Robbie: Assuming Da Westsidaz can even make it to their match… Nate: Agreed. That attack was one of the biggest mockeries to our sport I have ever laid witness too!!! Now folks, not only do we have tag team titles on the line tonight, but Corg will defend his cruiserweight title against Scott Taylor, while OSW International Champion, Propaganda, will defend the International strap against OSW’s Queen of Extreme, Lillith. Finally, in our main event, we will have the return match between OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Rhett Locke, and the man he took the title from, Tom Walczak!!! Guys, who do you like in these matchups? Robbie: Are we just stalling for time, while security searches for Da Westsidaz? Nate: (carefully) Er…no. I legitimately want to know your thoughts. Robbie: Thanks for confirming my suspicions…I, of course, like Corg to kick the crap out of that NTICW nobody, Propaganda to demolish Lillith within 30 seconds of the opening bell, and the Polish Punisher to powerbomb the obviously gay Rhett Locke quickly, to regain his World Title. Nate: (sighing) Steven? Steven: Corg can stick me any time, so I like him to beat Taylor, who is obviously not into guys. Propaganda will defeat Lillith with the help of his delightfully squat and experience manager, Bob Sagar, and I gotta agree with Jeff in the fact that Rhett Locke is screaming to allow me to help him get in touch with his feminine side, but we differ because we do think he will defeat the smoothly shaven and delightfully rugged Tom Walczak to retain his title. Nate: This is like pure death….Well, folks we are just about set to get underway for our first match of the evening, but first, perhaps we should discuss…wait a minute…oh, thank God…..I am being told we have some activity in the backstage area, so we are going to send you to the back right now!!! . . . . . . The camera slides around to a small lockerroom tucked back in the backstage confines of The Igloo... and poking into the door labeled “Jim Axtell”, the camera zooms in on the former security guard, watching the night’s proceedings from a small television monitor. However, the television is focused on the ring, keeping Axtell from realizing that he’s been snuck up on... *tap tap tap* The sound of wood-on-metal... or more precisely, a baseball bat on a doorframe... makes Axtell jump and wheel around, ready to fight... until he sees that the men in the doorway are “Classic” Clay Vendetta and “Old School” Dave Gibson... and the pop for the Throwbacks can be heard rattling through the arena watching on the Jumboron nearby. Relaxing... but still wary... Axtell returns to his seat. Robbie: Oh good grief. Somebody shoot me. Nate: Shhhhh!!! In the doorway, Vendetta’s usual smirk is replaced by an expression that’s all business. Ditto for Gibson standing behind him. Leaning on the bat, Vendetta looks at Axtell, looks at the floor, then back to Axtell before talking... Vendetta: Let me make one thing clear to you, Jim. We don’t owe you thanks. Hell, we don’t owe you anything. I don’t know what you were doing out there last week; and frankly, I don’t give half a damn about your reasons. But before I start sounding like an unappreciative bastard... we don’t owe you thanks... but you get them, anyway. Axtell nods his head, still wary, as Vendetta continues. Vendetta: What I saw last week was the same thing I saw when Gibson here walked through the doors of OSW... a certain... hmmm... spirit, if you will. A commitment to the idea that nobody’s bigger than the game... and a commitment to perfection of the art. I can’t imagine you truly grasp any of that... after all, a few weeks back you were just a rent-a-cop. But I like your style, Axtell. And I’m gonna make you an offer. Axtell: I’m listening. Robbie: I’m not. Nate: Shut the hell up. Vendetta eyes Axtell. Vendetta: You gave us a hand last week. This week, I’m asking a favor. We don’t need anybody to watch our backs... but partners in hunting are always appreciated. I think you know who the targets are. And in return? Teaching, Jim. I know you’re a security guard first and a wrestler second. We can change that. You’re looking at the two most technically savvy wrestlers to step into OSW’s squared circle. Maybe we could get a little of it to rub of on you. I’ve seen you out there. You’ve got what it takes. You just need polish. I’m offering you an apprenticeship of sorts, Axtell... ...and from behind his back, Vendetta produces a sledgehammer... and hands it to Axtell. Vendetta:... and I’m offering you a weapon of choice. Call it a token or our appreciation. Turning the hammer over in his hands, Axtell thinks for a moment, then reaches down, tightens the laces on his boots, and gets to his feet. Walking towards the door, he says, simply... Axtell: So where do we start? Vendetta smirks, and wordlessly the three men leave the room to head down the hall, bats in hand. As they round the corner, though, a single person can be seen behind them, looking out from a doorway... and the figure follows them down the hall, maintaining his distance... Nate: WOAH!!! THE THROWBACKS JUST ADDED A BIT ON MANPOWER IN THE FORM OF JIM AXTELL!!! Steven: I need to add a bit more man power to my love life… Nate: SOMETHINGS ARE BETTER THOUGHT NOT SAID!!!! Nate: (sighing) Looks like this thing isn’t going to… The “Ante Up” remix suddenly hits the PA system, bringing the crowd to its feet. Nate: WOAH!!! Worst of all is his neck, held in a heavy neckbrace. Kriegman turns to his servant, the Trashcan Man, who gazes at the motionless body of his former friend as if in a trance. “Paralyzed from the neck down, Trash. You know what this means?” It was the cradle piledriver that did him in. The Pyro Driver, TCM’s signature move, executed from the top of the ramp, 15 feet to the concrete floor below. Some claim to have actually heard the crunching sound of Ed’s neck breaking on impact. Trashcan Man shifts his gaze from Ed to his new master. “I..I pleased you? Did I make you happy?” he asks. “No, Trash.” He smiles His smile suddenly changes to a scowl, and Kriegman smacks Trashy hard across the face. TCM lets out a cry not unlike that of a child being slapped by his father for the first time, and spins away from the man in the black suit. “It MEANS, Trash,” Alan snarls “that you didn’t get the job done!” Trashcan Man slowly turns back toward his master, and we can see that there are actually tears welling up in his eyes. “I..I…” “NO excuses, Trash,” Says Alan while pointing to Ed. “I wanted him DEAD. You failed me, Trash. You FAILED ME.” This does it for the Trashcan Man, and tears actually begin to run down his face. He drops to his knees and looks down at the feet of his master, clutching his hands to the side of his head. “I..I…I’m sorry master…I didn’t mean to make you angry..” he gasps. “I thought the bad man would die, I heard his neck…I heard it make that sound…” Looking down at his blubbering companion, Alan Kriegman gives a wretchedly evil grin. The REAL “bad man” kneels down to face Trashy, takes the side of his bearded face in one hand, and raises it up so they may make eye contact. TCM looks truly devastated, hardly able to look into Alan’s eyes. The warm, fatherly smile has returned. “It’s alright, Trash. I forgive you. I know you won’t disappoint me again.” He says with that horrible smile. Trashcan Man’s face brightens. Not with joy or happiness, but with relief. He looks like he’s been granted a letter of pardon after getting strapped into the electric chair. “Thank you…! I, I promise, I-“ Kriegman raises an index finger to his mouth. “Shhhhh… If you really want to thank me, Trash, you know what you should say.” The Trashcan Man knew, and he said just that. “My life for you! My life for you!! My life-“ “Shhhh. That’s enough, Trash. Go over and stand by the window for a moment while I have a little chat with Edward.” Kriegman commands, and with that, TCM gets up and walks over to the window across the room, where he stands…just staring out at the moon. Kriegman gets up and casually saunters over to the hospital bed, where his arch nemesis lay comatose, alive only because of the technology around him. For the first time, the camera focuses in on a shot of Ed’s face. Though he was never a handsome man, Ed’s face had always been intact. No longer is this the case. After his face being crushed between the mat and his own weapon of choice (Bob, the nail studded, barbed wire wrapped chair), there is now little more than a stitch-ridden, swollen mask of pink and purple skin. What remains of his visage becomes darkened in the shadow of the evil supergenius standing over him. “You always were a tough one, Ed” he softly says to the unresponsive Ed. “But this is just getting ridiculous. You never did know when to just quit. Here you are, in the very mouth of death, on the verge of plunging into the black once and for all…and yet you still fight.” Alan grabs ahold of Ed’s blonde locks in his right hand, and moves his mouth up to Ed’s ear. “Hopefully there’s enough of you left in there to hear me,” he whispers. “I’m sure the last thing going through your mind before it all went to black was why your dear old friend sided with your worst enemy… How did I do it, you thought. I could see it in your eyes, my friend. At that point you knew you were beaten, you just couldn’t put aside your pride…and look where it got you. Nothing can save you now, Ed. You breathe only because these machines allow it.” He whispers as he motions to the life support systems by the bed. “I’m going to put it all to an end for you, Ed. You have no life left to live. Your business has been taken from you, your friends have betrayed you, and your own body has been destroyed. There’s nothing left for you on this mortal coil, my friend… but before I make it all over, I’m going to put your soul at ease. I’ll answer that last question that ran through your mind. What is my secret to controlling Trashcan Man? I hope you’re listening…” Alan Kriegman moves his mouth as close to Ed’s ear as possible without touching it, and begins to whisper too softly for us to hear. Though it may be a coincidence, the unresponsive Ed begins to flinch as he whispers, as if he were having a bad dream. When Kriegman ceases whispering, the sporadic flinching stops as well. “There you have it,” he says loudly and clearly. “That should put all your questions to rest.” Kriegman smiles and pats Ed on the head. “Farewell, my friend.” The dark man moves away from the bed and approaches the various life support systems being used to keep Ed alive. Without any speaking, all we can hear once again is the steady pulsing of Ed’s heart monitor. Beep… Beep… Beep… The scene begins to fade to black as Kriegman kneels down, and grabs at a single electrical cord and gives it a pull…leaving the scene to fade to black, with nothing but the eerie sound of silence to serve as audio…
