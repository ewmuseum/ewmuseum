10 August 2002

The scene opens in the rain-slicked VIP parking area of the Fleet Center in Boston, as a sole security guard sits under and awning by a rear entrance to the building. From off camera, the sound of a slamming car door is heard, which catches the attention of the security official, who glances out towards the approaching cameraman, giving us the impression that we are viewing the rear door of the arena not through the lens of the camera, but through the eyes of the individual approaching the security guard. Rising from his chair, the security guard, now about 20 feet away from the approaching individual calls out. Security Guard: (Boston accent) You there!! Construction entrance is on the other… Suddenly the security guard squints, as if he has recognized the approaching man. Our suspicion is confirmed as the guard’s eyes light up and a wide smile crosses his face. Security Guard: (marking out uncontrollably) Holy shit!!! I didn’t recognize you from a distance and carrying the building materials!!! What the hell are you doing here!?!!?! WOW!! I am like your biggest fan!!! Fumbling through his yellow, nylon security jacket, the man pulls out an crumpled envelope and a pen, and nervously holds it out to the stranger. Security Guard: Wow!!! Can I have your autograph, because the guys are never gonna believe I actually met a fucking legend… Suddenly the jubilation in the man’s eyes turns to shock and horror. There is a suddenly crash, and the sound of hard substance to skull, accompanied by the thump of the security guard’s limp body hitting the ground. The visitor looks down at the unconscious and bleeding man. The stranger’s tan, steel-toed boot step over the man’s body, and into the rear of the arena….as the scene fades to black. Scene fades up backstage of Saturday Slam outside of Ivan Stanislav’s locker room. The Russian Bear stands in front of his door apparently waiting for someone. Ivan: Come on Riggs… where are you…. Ivan taps his enormous foot on the floor for a minute until finally Speedy Riggs, clad in his red and yellow officiating shirt, comes walking around the corner. Stanislav motions for Riggs to come over and speaks to him in a hushed tone. Ivan: It is about time you got here! What has been taking so long?! Speedy: Sorry Ivan! I was signing all those stupid autographs for the President. Ivan growls and nods. Ivan: Da…you are officiating match for Tempest tonight, correct? Riggs nods slowly to Ivan. Speedy: Yes Ivan, I am. What’s up? Ivan looks around and then lowers his head down close to the smaller Cuban. Ivan: I want you to watch Corg. I do not want anything to happen to Tempest, understand me? Riggs nods slowly. Speedy: Of course Ivan. I understand. Ivan: If he tries anything strange I want you to make sure you stop it. Speedy swallows. Speedy: Uh… me… stop Corg? Ivan: That is correct. You will do this for me, correct? She is too important to have defiled by him. Speedy: Sure Ivan, I… I’ll do it. Ivan: Good… and another thing…. Just as he leans over, Tempest rounds the corner coming the opposite way. She smiles warmly at Ivan and heads over. Ivan notices her and suddenly changes his tone with Riggs. Ivan: Right Riggs! So, go and uh….get those documents for me and I will be right over to uh… see you! Riggs blinks and then nods quickly. Riggs: Oh uh… yeah… right! Documents!!! He turns and clumsily walks away, bumping into a water fountain before rounding the corner. Ivan stands tall and positions himself in front of Tempest, blocking her path with his broad body. Ivan: Tempest… I uh… wanted to speak to you. Tempest smiles widely and hops a little on her heals. Tempest: Good! I'm glad. I wanted to talk to you too. Ivan smiles crookedly and tilts his head to the side, warming up just slightly and then gets a bit colder once again. Ivan: Tempest, your match tonight is against Corg. I do not want you in that ring alone with him. Corg is a dangerous man and therefore I am asking you to allow me to accompany you to wrestling ring, just to be safe. Tempest sighs and stands tall... which is sort of meaningless next to Ivan. Tempest: Ivan, I appreciate that you're worried. but I can’t very well win the match if I'm not alone with him in the ring. I don't want you there to throw things off. I mean think about the fact that this is my re-debut! I have to be seen as someone who can fight her own battles. Ivan closes his eyes slowly. Ivan: Please… let me come to the ring Tempest. If anything, it will throw off Corg and he will not try anything out of the ordinary. The people know you can fight your own battles. This is only precautionary measure. He opens his eyes again and breathes slowly, trying to keep his cool. Tempest nods, turning her best puppy eyed gaze to him. Tempest: I know that Ivan, but if it's precautionary, why not just be there backstage or something? Surely you of all people understand the symbolism. If you're right there it's like I'm saying I'm scared. Ivan shakes his head quickly, somewhat confused. Ivan: Nyet! How many times has Alexei accompanied me?? No one says that Ivan is scared! Tempest… I am going to ask you one more time, allow me to go to wrestling ring with you… please? Tempest shakes her head, still trying to smile. Tempest: It's not the same Ivan and you know it. I want you around, believe me. But... not out there. Not when I have to prove something to people. Ivan breathes quickly and stands up straight. His arm almost reflexively shoots out against the wall and presses against it, crackling the plaster. Ivan: NO! Tempest jumps as she’s blasted by Ivan’s breath. Ivan: I have asked you twice now for permission to go to wrestling ring and you have denied me. If I need to I will handle this myself!! Ivan notices Tempest’s surprise and tries to calm himself. Ivan: Tempest… you have not seen what he has done to people. You do not understand! Tempest bites her lip and steps forward, putting her hand on his arm. Tempest: Ivan please... I'm grateful that you're concerned. And if anything happens after the bell, I'd be happy to have help. But I have to fight the match alone. I need it and the fans need it. Ivan recoils suddenly as she touches his arm and takes two large steps back. He almost shivers and shakes his head. Ivan: You… you do not understand! You want to fight that match alone? Go ahead! Why should Ivan even care!?! Go ahead and fight Corg Tempest! Deny my help!! Ivan turns and roars angrily. He slams into the fountain that Riggs had hit and knocks it completely out of the wall, sending a jet of water shooting across the hallway. He storms around the corner as his heavy footsteps slowly fade away. Tempest sighs and her shoulder slump sadly. Tempest: Maybe he'll feel better after the match. . . . . . . The usual opening from OSW’s Saturday Night Slam! begins to roll, featuring OSW superstars nailing high impact maneuvers on unwitting opponents, with a Batman-like Slam! graphic appearing on the screen upon the impact of each move. Wrestlers highlighted are OSW World Heavyweight Champion Rhett Locke, Corg, and Civil Disobedience. Pyros shoot off from the ringposts, ceiling and entryway and “Bulls on Parade” by Rage Against the Machine hits the PA system. Cameras scan the arena taking samples of the jam-packed and raucous crowd. The first stop for our roving cameras is on a group of Canadian youths who, besides getting the crap booed out of them by the Boston crowd, are holding up flags and posters of their superstar of choice, OSW super diva, Amy Campbell. The camera is not focused on the Canadians for more than three seconds when a huge “CANADA SUCKS” chant breaks out, and debris begins getting hurled at the Canadian fans. Wanting to avoid further incident, the director quickly, and wisely, shifts the scene to ringside, where Boston Red Sox superstars Manny Ramirez and Pedro Martinez, which causes the crowd to immediately stop booing, and suddenly burst out into cheers. Within seconds, the cheers then give away to chants of… “BEAT NEW YORK!!! BEAT NEW YORK!!! BEAT NEW YORK!!!!” Unfortunately for Ramirez and Martinez, they have no idea what the hell the fans are saying, because they aren’t chanting in Spanish. Finally and mercifully, the camera pans away from the confused gentlemen from the Caribbean, to the ringside position of the OSW announce team. Robbie: Well it’s just that thus far I’ve been thoroughly unimpressed by Garvin, and I’ve yet to see Callisto in action. Unlike some people, Dumas, wrestlers have to earn my interest and respect before I toss it out at them. Nate: Well Earl is in the ring for introductions right now, so maybe you won’t have to wait long before they take it from you. Robbie: What? My interest and respect? Phhh...we’ll see. Nate: Let’s go to Earl for the introductions. The camera shifts to the ring where ring announcer Earl Henrich is ready to announce the principals. Earl: The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, from Tampa, Florida, weighing in at 242 pounds, he is “The Original” JEEEEEEEEFF GAAAAAARVIN!!! “Drag You Down” by Finger Eleven echoes throughout the arena as Garvin appears at the top of the entrance ramp. The crowd gives him a lukewarm reaction as he walks down to the ring, because frankly, he has done nothing to impress them thus far. Undeterred, he climbs up the ring steps and through the ropes to take his position in the ring. He straightens his elbow pads on his arms as he waits for his opponent to make her way to the ring. Robbie: Guys, can someone tell me what’s so original about this guy? Because I’m not seeing it. Steven: Well...maybe he’ll be the first one in the OSW to lose to Callisto Bane. That would be original. Robbie: If that’s all he has going for him in the originality department, then that’s pretty sad. Nate: No argument from me there. Earl: And his opponent, from Parts Unknown, weighing in at 285 pounds, she is the “Olden Beast” CALLISTOOOOO BAAAAAAAANE!!! “Mouth Full of Poison” by Kittie replaces Garvin’s theme, announcing the arrival of Callisto Bane at the Boston Fleet Center. Coming out from the back, she pauses at the top of the ramp, her six foot six inch frame creating an impressive sight. The fans seem somewhat in awe of this giant of a woman, yet overcome their initial shock to jeer her anyway, remembering her actions last week in the near-crucifixion of Dave Gibson. She glares angrily at the people at ringside, a sadistic expression coming over her face, before rolling into the ring. Getting to her knees, she points a finger in the direction of Garvin, then slides it across her throat in a warning gesture of what she has in store for him. Steven: Holy moley! This girl is a biggun! Think she can strap it? The sound of Robbie audibly vomiting can be head in the background. Nate: Callisto Bane isn’t your normal female competitor by any means. She has both the height and weight advantage over Garvin in this contest. And from what we’ve seen from her so far, she’ll be hungry for blood. Robbie: The blood of the innocent? Nate: Is there any other? Robbie: Oh boy! *snickers deviously* Well in that case, this match may not be so bad after all. An apprehensive look coming over his face, Garvin manages to shake it off and, after the bell rings, walks to the center of the mat to lock up with Bane. She doesn’t seem to interested in the lock-up, however, and instead opts to drive a knife edge thrust into the throat of the “Original” one. Jeff falls to his knees, his hands around his larynx as he gasps for air, but Callisto gives him little time to catch his precious breath. Grapping his head with both hands, she drives his face into her knee. As Garvin falls back on the canvas, his mouth is bloody mess. Nate: Less than a minute has passed in this match-up and Garvin is already spitting up blood. Robbie: You were right for once, Dumas. It didn’t take Callisto long before she captured my interest AND respect. The former 21w star sputters and chokes as he tries to pry Bane’s hands from around his throat, but his efforts yield no results. His face turns blue from lack of oxygen, and just when it looks as if he’s about to pass out, Callisto releases her hold. Nate: Bane is just sadistic in there! Steven: Can she str… Robbie: (growling) I only have bile to vomit now, so shut the hell up! Garvin sucks in deep breaths of air, writhing around on the canvas. Looking down at her prey, Callisto lets out a hearty laugh as she grabs his head and forces him back down to the canvas. The blood from his mouth has now trickled down the sides of his mouth, and Bane streaks her fingers through it, a smile crossing her face as she does so. She stares at the blood of her opponent as it drips down her hand contentedly. Her demented aesthetic sense allows Garvin the opportunity to sit up off of the mat. His eyes are wide open with shock, and he is visibly shaking as he tries to get his wits about him. Nate: Am I the only one that sees that this match is getting out of control? Robbie: No. Nate: Am I the only one that sees something wrong with that? Robbie: Yes. Bane turns her attention away from her blood-covered hand to stare back down at Garvin again. Steven: I know! At this point I would have been slithering all over Garvin’s body like... Robbie: Ugh...please stop... Grabbing Garvin around the neck in a dragon sleeper hold, Callisto lifts him back up to his feet. She takes a hold of his tights with her free hand, pulling him up for what appears to be a reverse vertical suplex, but instead repositions her body so that Garvin falls back down in an elevated neckbreaker. Bane gives Jeff no time to recover from this move, picking him up again immediately, stepping through the ropes and dragging him out to the ring apron with her. Nate: This is not going to be good... Robbie: You’re right, it’s going to be great! Placing Jeff’s head between her legs, Callisto lifts him up for a piledriver. Running the length of the apron with Garvin in this position, she leaps off as she reaches the end, sits out, and executes a jumping piledriver from the apron to the arena floor. A chant of O-S-Dubbya fills the Fleet Center as the fans are on their feet at the sight of the carnage. Robbie: HOLY SHIT! Nate: My god...this could be the end of Jeff Garvin’s career, taking place before our very eyes.
