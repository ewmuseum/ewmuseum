The scene fades in to the gurgling sound of a bubbling Culligan water cooler. As the camera zooms out to the view around the standard area of office chat, you see two figures around it, one on the left, and one on the right, both leaning casually against the wall. However as the view comes in focus, the realization is made that these aren’t the typical beings you see around the water cooler in an OSW office hall. The two are stout and shiny, and altogether not human at all. They are Gunmetal and Chico, the robotic tag team of Twisted Metal, made famous from their time in PCW. They are not, however, holding a complimentary cup of purified water, but rather each is holding a lighted cigarette. Chico puts the small roll of paper to a tiny tube in his torso. The soft whirring of a fan is heard as a deep drag is taken. “Y’know, though the smoke is hot, I feel somehow… cooler? Hmm, an interesting experience… how about you?” queries Chico as he drops the cigarette to his side. “Indeed, Chico,” agrees Gunmetal. He puts the cigarette to his own tube and goes through the same noise motions as Chico while the expelled smoke wafts from a myriad of vents. “But you would think there would be some more perks to this new gig than a single packet of cigarettes.” “More perks, what do you mean?” inquires Chico. “More money, plus we now get iron oxide insurance AND full collision coverage, the suckers!” “I do not know, I guess I was hoping to be plated in gold that is all. It does not seem too much to—E-mail senses tingling!” Gunmetal suddenly jerks up straight as he cuts himself off mid-sentence. “Message forthcoming!” Chico takes another drag of his cigarette before looking to see how much is left. “Well, let’s have a look at it,” he says nonchalantly. Doors on Gunmetal’s stomach slide open revealing a screen. Chico presses a few of the buttons on Gunmetal’s abdomen below the screen. “Let’s see now, inbox…new messages…” Chico clicks on the link before crossing his arms and tapping his foot impatiently with a loud thunking. “Stupid turtlesque server, I thought we got you upgraded to T1?” Gunmetal shrugs as the message finally comes up. “Ah here we are,” Chico exclaims with renewed interest. “Who is it from?” inquires Gunmetal. “I dunno, it says the sender is unknown and the sender’s e-mail has been encrypted so it won’t show up.” “Well then hurry up and read it.” “Don’t get your circuits in a tangle,” retorts Chico before revealing the email’s contents. “To Whom It May Concern: It has come to my attention that you gentlebots may have exactly the tools I need to complete a specific task that I have undertaken. I realize your services do not come without a price, so it may be of some interest to you that I have something that may be…of use to you. If I have piqued your curiosity, come and meet with me and we will discuss my intentions. Further information on location and time will be divulged in future online mailings. Thank you for you for your time.” “No signature?” asks Gunmetal. “What do you expect, ‘my secret name is actually X?’ It’s a SECRET!” responds Chico. “So what do we do?” “I guess we tell Cobol,” says Chico, referring to their robotic commander. “Do we have to?” “No, but he knows the password to the account, so he’ll find out eventually. Might as well bag the brownie points while we can.” “Excuse me,” comes a nasally voice from behind. Chico rotates his head around on his shoulders to see a young OSW worker addressing him. Chico turns the rest of his body around to face the bony young man who wears brown pants and a matching tie with a white shirt. He has a mop of red hair to go with his freckles that clash with his black-framed glasses. “There’s no smoking in here I’m afraid,” he whines as he points to a sign above the water cooler with a crossed out cigarette. “NO FUMAR,” it proclaims. “So? We don’t have any Fumar, so go file something or whatever it is you middle management drones do, we’re busy doing… things!” snarls Chico. “Important things!” chimes in Gunmetal. “Yes, important things!” “You’re endangering my lungs as well as everyone within a twenty-foot radius of you, that’s what it’s to me. Now I can point you direction to the door leading outside if you’d like,” the nasal intern says as he puffs his chest out a little to try and fake boldness. “I’ll show you what I’d like.” Chico grabs the young man by the tie with one arm and flings him up to the ceiling. The intern hits it with a thump and falls to the floor, broken chunks of cheap ceiling tile all over him. Chico takes a final drag of his cigarette and leans over, putting it out on the young man’s tie, as Gunmetal does likewise. They begin walking down the hall, leaving the intern lie. “Geez,” says Chico. “They complain when we smoke, they complain when we don’t smoke, humans just need to make up their stupid minds.” “I agree fully, but I did enjoy extinguishing the smoke tube,” concurs Gunmetal as they walk down the hall and around the corner. A hand that extends from a black suit jacket sleeve reaches back from the keyboard in front of the monitor and begins stroking a small mechanical Japanese dog.
