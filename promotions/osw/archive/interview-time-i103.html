Interview Time Written: 23/03/2002 The scene reopens backstage, where the sound of deep hissing and content silence fill a small, dimly lit hallway. The constant annoyance of dripping water in the background adds to the noise in the hall, whilst various OSW trashcans and barbed wire steel chairs serve as eye candy for any fan or wrestler with a lust for blood. In front of this barrage of on-display weaponry stands none other than OSW’s ace reporter – Shanda Russell. Kitted out in her usual on-screen attire, Shanda holds the microphone close to her mouth, trembling slightly, as by her side, on either side stands Hessian, Minion the Imp and Caleb, whilst brooding in the background, toying with one of the barbed wire steel chairs is ‘The Marked Beast’ Azrael Cain. Shanda Russell: Hello OSW viewers, I’m Shanda Russell, here with possibly the most dominating force in the OSW – Heresian Bloodlust. Now first of all, Hessian, how on earth did you and Azrael lose to Civil Disobedience at Crowning Glory when you’re both such big, bad…men? Hessian: T’was a mere misfortune. A simple spanner thrown into the cogs of the machine of destruction that is Heresian Bloodlust, but we shan’t dwell. Our time will come, it’s best to let the children play with their newfound toys for a little while before we snatch them up away from them, and ready ourselves for the repercussions. Shanda Russell: Ok, and what are your views on your upcoming #1 Contender’s Match against Twisted Metal, 3B and Jesse Ramey and Michael Dame? Before Hessian can reply, Minion jumps up and grabs Shanda’s wrist, pulling the mic down to his mouth. Minion the Imp: What in the hell kind of a match is this? #1 Contender’s Table Match??? Dammit they must be looking for ratings and nothing else; I’ve never seen an OSW Tag team Championship #1 Contender’s Table Match featuring two Satanists, two Robots, two Hood rats, one man and a Trannie in a pear tree??? That’s right, Azrael, Hessian, Chico, Gunmetal, Frankie, Drew, Michael and that sexshiftin’ son-of-a-bitch Jesse Ramey all in one ring, surrounded by tables and if all hell don’t break loose THEN THE IMP IS GONNA START WHOOPIN’ SOME ASS HIMSELF!!!!!!!!! Minion takes a well needed breathe… Minion the Imp: Civil Disobedience got lucky at Crowning Glory and now the Heresian Bloodlust has to wait while more undeserving fiends get a chance at the titles that Heresian Bloodlust and Civil Disobedience put a reputation to! Who fought in the first OSW title match? They did! Dropouts from PCW, Hood scum and two nobodies will NOT stop Azrael Cain and Hessian from finally achieving the OSW tag Team Titles from those little pretty boy b*st*rds Trevor and Seth, Seth who I’m sure is looking forward to a Global Title Final including him and the T.S.I. here, Hessian. But that’s many rounds and many weeks from now so tonight right here right NOW we’re going to straighten the whole mess out! Hessian! The Behemoth that strikes fear into any, and all, and the man that killed the Monster, Hessian’s plan is simple; storm through 6 other men like a tornado and get that shot at Civil Disobedience that he needs so damn badly, that’ll show Seth and Trevor the error of their ways… Shanda Russell: Minion………… Minion the Imp: AZRAEL CAIN HAS A SIMPLE GOAL!!! It is the destruction of all who oppose him man or machine, AND……… Minion turns to Cain, who is still fiddling with the chair. Minion the Imp: You best not be going fruity there Az, staring at blonde’s is fine but when you’re in a table match against 6 other men INCLUDING a guy that, if you turn the right way, will do you worse than Gary Glitter on Viagra!!! It’s very simple when the cards are all laid out. Twisted Metal, 3B and Dame-y and Ramey all together in one ring, it’s a recipe for disaster and when the element of Hessian and Azrael Cain is added then that recipe becomes a 100% Disasterpiece…………… Shanda Russell:……………If you say so. So Caleb, how do you see Heresian Bloodlust in the hunt for the tag titles? Caleb: Well what can you say? Heh-heh. Azrael and Hessian are two of the biggest, baddest creatures in Old School. They said Hessian couldn’t beat The Monster They Call Meanstreak…he did it. They said Azrael couldn’t beat the Russian Bear and he did! People are underestimating these two Heretics and it won’t be long before they come back and bite you in the ass! Heh-heh! Shanda Russell: Ok that’s enough of the managers…so… Shanda looks past Hessian to Azrael, who is running his finger along the sharp metal of the barbed wire chairs. Shanda Russell: Cain?………Cain?………Azrael? Ah forget it then; you’ll probably just lose to Ramey like Minion said anyway… Azrael’s head snaps up and cocks to one side. The chair drops to the ground, as he trundles over to Shanda and stares her eye-to-eye. Azrael Cain: Lose? For millennia and beyond I have suffered for…reasons best left to themselves…and now that suffering has surpassed its worst the time to deal out that same suffering to others has begun, and with more intense suffering comes winning, and if we must lower ourselves to the rank of sheer brutality just to exact revenge on those Suburban weasels. You cannot comprehend the wrath of the Heretic, so next time you assume that Bloodlust will fail, think again, especially when there is a raging Satanist next to you with a barbed wire steel chair in the vicinity…ok? Shanda winces as Azrael picks up the chair and throws it into a pile of OSW brand barrels, sending them crashing everywhere, the sound of the barbed wire snapping off of the chair and springing everywhere. As Azrael looms over Shanda in an intimidating way, Moose Malone of OSW Security appears as if out of nowhere, and immediately puts himself between Shanda and The Marked Beast. Moose: Don’t think I won’t hesitate to knock you flat if you even think of touching her, Cain… Azrael: Ahh neither of you are worth it…go book a pension old man… Azrael walks off, leaving Moose and Shanda in the company of Hessian, Minion and Caleb. Caleb: Err…heh-heh…I best be catching up with Azrael… Minion the Imp: Uhhh…yeah I need to go Imp up for later on…toodlepips! Minion and Caleb chase off after Azrael, leaving Hessian, hissing deeply through his mask. Hessian: Sorry to have cut this so short dear Shanda…it seems Cain is ready for battle tonight, I can only say, Bring on the Tables! Hessian flips his cape over his body and calmly wanders off after Azrael, Minion and Caleb down the dark hallway, leaving Shanda and Moose rather flustered, as the scene fades to commercial.
