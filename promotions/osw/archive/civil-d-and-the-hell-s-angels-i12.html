Civil D and the Hell's Angels

2002-02-09
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil D and the Hell's Angels Written: 2-9-02 Nate: I am being told our Shanda Russell is standing by backstage, who has a report on the popularity of PCW. Shanda! The scene cuts from the ring to the parking lot area in the back of the arena. Here we see OSW reporter Shanda Russell standing next to a barricade holding back hoards of screaming fans who missed out on buying tickets for seats. She’s looking attractive as always in a pair of tight blue pants and tank top, and the catcalls from the horde behind her only reinforce this idea further. As the crowd flails their arms wildly, she smiles at the camera, sweeping her hand across the mass of people. Shanda: Hi, I’m Shanda Russell, here backstage at the Blue Horizon in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. This may only be this promotion’s second show, but, as you can see, we’ve had no problem packing in the bodies tonight. Just look at...and listen to...all of these people. The crowd goes nuts once more at their undeserved television exposure. The sound is reminiscent of the entirity of New England when they realized their football team had sold their souls to the devil for a Superbowl victory. It doesn’t last very long, however, as some gruff voices can be heard sounding from towards the back of the swarm of people. The crowd’s noise dies down, and you can see the mob part as a swath is cut right down the middle. As the people closest to the barricade move apart, three rough looking bearded men in leather jackets come to the front, stepping over the barrier to stand next to Shanda. She looks a bit uneasy at the sight of these unexpected visitors, but maintains a smile none the less. She glances at chief of security, Moose Malone, motioning for him to come to her aid. Shanda: It appears as if I’ve been joined by some...er...outgoing fans. Hey Moose, can I get some help here. Moose walks over to her, nodding in acknowledgement at the muscular men standing next to her. He leans over and whispers into Shanda’s ear, nods at the men again, then walks away again. Shanda’s eyes open wide in fear as contemplates what Moose told her. Shanda: You...you guys are Hell’s Angels? The men nod in agreement, the corners of their mouths turned up in amusement. Shanda: Moose...are you sure you don’t want to do something about this? Moose shrugs halfheartedly, while Shanda begins to shiver in fear. As she contemplates what to do, one of the men reaches out and snatches the microphone out of her hand. He pounds on it a few times before smiling and holding it up to his mouth. Biker: Is this thing on? Heheheh. He snickers and exchanges a laugh with the other two men, who seem to be enjoying the havoc they’ve begun to cause. The man with the microphone lifts it to his mouth again. Biker: Man, I can’t believe this fed is finally open. Shit, I can’t believe we’re actually on TV right now. Old School kicks ass!!!! He pumps his fist in the air, as his friends hoot and holler with him. Shanda pulls the microphone back to her hesitantly, speaking softly into it. Shanda: So you fellows are fans of OSW? Biker: Hell yeah! We ain’t never seen a show yet, but once we heard that Hessian and Azrael Cain came here we fired up our hogs and headed out. Now here we are, ready to see those bad-ass motherf#ckers kick some ass, old school style! The three bikers begin to laugh loudly again, exchanging high fives. As the man with the microphone turns to speak again, he pauses and looks up to see Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, the team of Civil Disobedience, have entered the area. Seth, who is wearing a pair of jean shorts and a black Slayer shirt, is currently shooting an accusing glare toward the man who was speaking. and he raises his hand in protest. Nate: What he heck are they doing there? Seth: Whoa, whoa, whoa! Hold on a second, here! Seth and the man stare at each other for a few seconds, as tension builds in the air. The other two bikers exchange looks, as if wondering if they should get ready to bash in some skulls, while Trevor starts to tug on Seth’s arm to pull him away from an inevitable ass-kicking. Seth pushes him away, continuing to stare at the large biker, then deciding to walk towards him. As he draws close to him, the two stand nose to chest as Seth cranes his neck back to look up at the other man. It looks almost as if a fight is about to break out when...the two men embrace. Seth: Hey, Uncle Jake! How have you been? Jake the Biker: Aw, I’ve been alright I guess. So what have you been up to. The two men pull apart and Seth gives his uncle a questioning look as he points at himself with his index finger. Seth: Hello, I’m a wrestler! Isn’t that why you’re here? To cheer me on? Jake the Biker: Actually...er...I didn’t know that. See, I don’t talk much to yer mother no more. Seth: No biggie. But anyways, since you’re here, I’ve been thinking maybe you can help me out. Seth leans over and starts to whisper into his uncle’s ear. The Hell’s Angel’s face scrunches up as he listens, and he complains out loud in a whiny voice. Jake the Biker: Aw, but Seth...we like those guys! Seth: *sigh* Fine, fine. Trev...break out the Chuckie Cheese gift certificates. Trevor walks over to the gathering and reaches into his pocket. After a few seconds he pulls out a handful of slips of paper. He counts out about ten of them and slips the wad into the pocket of Seth’s uncle’s jacket. Uncle Jake shrugs and shakes Seth’s hand. Jake the Biker: Well boy, you got yourself a deal. Everyone manages a smile, even a dumfounded Shanda Russell, as the scene shifts back to the OSW announce desk.
