'Copasetic' Lennox Lanthrop — character application (August 2002)

'Copasetic' Lennox Lanthrop Real Name: Lennox Lanthrop Nick Name: 'Copasetic' Lennox Lanthrop Character Handler: jordan AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Control" by Puddle of Mudd Date of Birth: 22 From: St. Cloud Minnesota Height: 6'6 Weight: 268 Wrestling Style: Powerhouse-Brawler Signature Moves: Dangerous DDT Amazing Power Clothesline T-Bone Suplex Hard Running DDT Fisherman DDT Huge Sidewalk slam Finisher Move:"Enigmatic Epitome" --- Lennox drags his opponent down and hooks their arms, much like the beginning of a Pedigree. He then proceeds to lift them up into a powerbombing position. He then violently thrusts them down, their shoulder striking Lennox's knee with dangerous velocity. They often are knocked out by this move, but if they aren't; its assured Lennox usually grabs the three count. Set-Up Move: Violent Backwards-Thrusting Snap German Suplex Current Team: None Past Teams & Groups: None. Title History: Fury: 1st and only Junior Champion LoW: Network Title #1 Contender (2) Awards History: None Description: Lennox Lanthrop is a very, very handsome (sexy?) young man....he has slightly tanned skin.... •Head -Baby Blue Eyes -Medium Nose -Silver ring in left ear -Million Dollar Smirk on face -Full Lips -Black, spiky hair with periodic gray streaks....long, thin black sideburns.. -Black goatee on chin only....with gray streaks here too... -Long, well structured and somewhat narrow face... •Body -Well toned, extremely muscular -Shirtless... -small barcode tatoo on his ripped left arm... -fingers taped periodically taped like they do in the NFL....white tape.... -Baggy, realistic gray jeans/cargo pants -White, somewhat big K-Swiss shoes...with 3 gray stripes going diagonally down the sides of the shoes. History: A new world. Bright lights, beautiful pigments and hues. These were ultimaetly brought to Lennoxes bright blue eyes upon birth. The white, clean hospital room. The clean-shaven, young doctors lifted the cute baby, wrapped in a soft blue blanket. They carried him over, and placed him in his excited mothers' arms. He giggled and cooed, happy to finally be in the world that was so unknown and new....he never knew that this warm, inviting atmosphere would soon diminish, leaving him in a world of hate, neglect, and downright disrespect. Lennox is now two months old. He is in his St. Cloud home. Quaint to say the least; a baby blue, one story house. After dinner time one evening, Mr. Bill Lanthrop bends over a play-pin and picks up the young Lennox, his head already full of thick black hair. He smiles at the toddler Lennox... He walks backwards, and his happy face soon turned to shock. He trips over one of Lennoxes play toys and he falls backwards, dropping the small Lennox. Lennox lands right on his face, and Mrs. Lanthrop let out a loud cry. But Lennox gets to a crawling position, his nose bloodied. The scariest thing, however, is Lennoxes' loud, persistant laughing. Lennox went to school, but didn't do so well. He got extremely poor grades. He tried to fit in and was successful, but the teachers were puzzled as to why Lennox befriended all the girls and no little boys. He would have tea parties and play Barbies with all the girls, and he just wouldn't look twice in the direction of a male. Some now would say he was quite the stud, him finding favor from the females at such an early age. His teachers, very confused, gave Lennoxes parents a call. They were appauled...and yanked Lennox out of the elementary private school. Oh, but they weren't angry with Lennox, it was those nuns that were soon found to be lesbians. Lennox was then home-schooled. He didn't like school, so at age 16, he stopped....abruptly, yes. He wasn't book-smart...however he was extremely talented mentally. His parents agreed. He was an extremely fit boy, and still interacted and had fun with the other kids by City-sponsored baseball and basketball leagues. Once again, he was quite the ladies man. Upon his 18th birthday, he faced a dilemma. All his friends were going to college, and he refused to follow suit. Lennox then moved out, living alone.... Times were rough. He lived in a less-than-savory apartment with infestations of roaches and 'holy' walls. He wasn't very organized, as he went through job after job...coming home every evening to a new daily stench in his room. His life took a turn for the worst. Lennox never felt bad about anything...ever. It was an odd experience, until luck came to his aid. There was a local wrestling promotion, the DWRCFCVA; they were willing to pay good wages for athletic men. He watched pro wrestling on t.v.... "...I mean, how hard could it be?" ...and so it was, and Lennoxes career in the big leagues began. Through stressful beatings and strenuous diligence and attitude, he quickly rose to the top of the game...until his last federation closed. He will be forever changed after a long lasting feud with a rival german beast. This man has changed Copasetic for the better....because at 22, Lennox is no longer a 'kid' in the game...hes a man. Hes more conservative, serious and focussed on his goals...he is admired by fans, and recognized by many...the fan support is there along with his already matured being .... ...He was left jobless after his last fed's closure...its time Lennox traced back to his roots...all the way back to the 'old school'. Entrance Description: The lights dim greatly, as "Control" by Puddle of Mudd strikes up and the fans rise in their seats....erupting in cheers already. A huge, silvery explosion erupts on the stage, literally shaking the house...as grey lights shine from underneath the steel ramp. 'Copasetic' Lennox Lanthrop swiftly walks onto the stage, to even a bigger ovation. He then continues his intent descent to the ring, his head turning side to side periodically, smiling at the fans.... Upon reaching ringside, Lennox stops. He looks around at the fans...not smiling, just soaking it all in as he notices the vast applause and approval towards him...its then he climbs up onto the apron, and he climbs in the ring through the ropes....He casually walks to the other side of the ring, as he leans against a turnbuckle, his arms crossed as he waits for his opponent.
