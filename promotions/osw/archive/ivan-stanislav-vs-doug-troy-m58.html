Ivan Stanislav vs. Doug Troy

2002-03-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivan Stanislav vs. Doug Troy Written: March 23, 2002 Nate: Welcome back to OSW Slam! Our next match is going to decide who the number one contender for the OSW World Title is! Steven: Come on Decker, who is going to win? Nate sighs. Nate: For the last time Steven, Nate is not omnipotent. He doesn’t know anymore than you are I know. Nate looks Decker up and down for a second. Nate: In fact I am willing to bet that he knows less than us. Considerably less. Robbie: Hey! Nate: Let’s just take it to Earl for the introductions! The camera shot changes to Earl Henrich standing in the ring waiting to make the introductions for the match. Earl raises the mic and begins. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, Boys and Girls, our next match is scheduled for one fall and the winner of the match will be named the number one contender for the OSW World Title! The fans cheer wildly. Earl: Coming to the ring first, from Omaha Nebraska, standing at 6’1” and weighing 230 pounds… “Brainless” by the Urge begins to blast the PA system as all of the fans in the arena rise to their feet and begin to cheer wildly. Earl: Doug Troy!!! Troy appears on the entryway and makes his way down the ramp slapping hands with all of the fans that line it! Troy leaps up onto the apron and raises his hands in order to get the fans more involved in the excitement of the match. Nate: Doug sure knows how to work over the fans, doesn’t he? Steven: Doug can work me over any… Decker grabs Smith by the collar and brings his face close to his own. Decker begins to warn Smith very sinisterly. Robbie: Please…not tonight…not during this match…please. Troy ducts between the ropes and immediately sprints to one of the turnbuckles. He effortlessly leaps up onto the turnbuckle and raises his arms into the air in order to get more pop from the crowd. DT’s music is cut short though as it is rudely interrupted by the Soviet National Anthem. All of the lights go red in the arena as the cheers from the fans quickly change from into hateful boos. Nate: It appears as if Ivan Stanislav is above an introduction these days. Decker quickly climbs to his feet and salutes the hammer and sickle that has appeared on the jumbotron. Robbie: Ivan no longer needs an introduction. The man is a warrior for the people! Nate: What the hell are you talking about? Robbie: I can’t believe this! All of these damn rednecks are booing their champion! This man fights for the little guy! Nate: The man fights to destroy the little guy! Robbie: Nonsense! Ivan Stanislav is making his way to the ring. He is being followed by his loyal comrade Alexei Ruslan who is carrying a massive Soviet Union flag on a long pole and waving it about. Ivan gets to the apron and pulls himself up. He lifts his massive left leg up over the top rope. His equally large right leg follows. Ivan is in the ring staring down Doug Troy who is returning the glare with one of pure hatred. Nate: And there is no love between these two opponents! Robbie: The only thing that I criticize Comrade Stanislav for is not permanently crippling Dougy Boy! That way we wouldn’t have to put up with his crap anymore! Ivan and Troy are still staring each other down as the lights return to normal. Referee Gaston enters the ring and immediately signals for the bell. Ivan rushes at Troy without a moment of hesitation. Nate: Here we go! Troy quickly ducts underneath the reaching arms of the Russian. Troy manages to get behind the Ivan and up onto his back. DT places a perfect sleeper hold on the bear. Nate: Doug seems to being using his quickness to his advantage here tonight! Robbie: He is just avoiding the inevitable! Ivan is going to crush him like the annoying gnat that he is. Troy is using all of his strength to keep the sleeper hold around Ivan’s neck. The bear is swatting at DT trying to get the hold off. Ivan is spinning around trying to get his massive hands on Troy. Steven: This looks like something in a damn cartoon! The fans are cheering wildly. Ivan is still reaching, but he is starting to waver in part from being dizzy and in part from the lack of oxygen that he is getting. Suddenly, a look comes across the Bear’s face like he just got a devilish idea. Nate: I don’t like the look of that expression on Ivan’s face! Ivan leaps up into the air with the last bit if strength that he can muster. Nate: I was afraid of this! Steven: This is going to be messy! Robbie: This is going to be great! Ivan lands on his back with his legs sticking up straight into the air on to of Troy causing him to release his sleeper hold. Nate: Troy has to be almost dead! That was four hundred pounds landing on top of him! Ivan rolls off of DT who is on the mat holding his gut in agony. Ivan gets to his feet and leans over to grab Troy. Ivan’s massive hand closes around Troy’s neck. The bear hoist Troy off of the mat by his throat and holds him high in the air. Troy’s legs begin to flail more violently as he gets further and further off of the mat. Nate: Listen to these fans boo! There is a lot of hatred for the bear down here in Florida! Robbie: These damn rednecks don’t know what the hell they are doing! They’re booing their champion! Ivan looks at Troy for a moment and lets him continue to flail about in his hand. Ivan starts to pat DT on the top of his head patronizing him. Ivan pulls back his free hand and punches Troy in the face, sending him hurtling through the air and crashing into the steel ring post. DT’s body crumples against the metal and he falls limply to the mat. Nate: Oh my God! Robbie: HA! Ivan begins to yell at the fans that hare now throwing things into the ring. Alexei continues to wave the massive flag in the air taunting the people. Nate: Ivan might want to be careful here. Doug Troy is not someone that you want to turn your back on in a match. Ivan turns around and walks over to the mangled Doug Troy. Ivan reaches down to grab Troy again by the throat, but instead he is welcomed by a kick to the groin. The fans cheer wildly! Nate: And a quick kick to Ivan’s lower sensitive manly region! Robbie: Huh? Ivan’s Lower sensitive manly region? Steven: OOoooOOO!!! Ivan’s lower sensitive manly region!!!! Ivan is doubled in agony. The fans are going wild. DT lunges forward and grabs Ivan by the ankle causing Ivan to fall hard to the mat. DT places a perfect ankle lock on the Russian Bear who is now in an incredible amount of pain from the hold. Nate: I can’t believe it! Doug Troy might become the number one contender here! Robbie: BAH!!!! Ivan is holding his head in his hands. Nate: Wait a minute look up at the entryway! Robbie: HA! Tom Walczak appears form the backstage area and quickly makes his way down the ramp to help his fellow communist. Something stops him though on his way down to the ring. Nate: What is all that commotion in the seats? Robbie: What the hell is Walczak thinking? He needs to help his fellow communist! Walczak looks on in shock as two figures hurdle the security barrier. Nate: Is that who I think it is? Robbie: What the hell are they doing here? Steven: BOCA~! Walczak is hit with a fierce spear from the larger of the two men that drops him to the steelramp violently. The crowd EXPLODES into a huge pop as the identities of the men are revealed. Nate: GOOD GAWD!!! BODA AND KID WONDER ARE HERE!!! THE PCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION JUST BURIED TOM WALCZAK!!!! Robbie: WHAT THE HELL?!?! SECURITY!!!! Walczak is trying to fight off Boda, but the kicks from Kid Wonder are too much for him. Inside the ring though Ivan is still caught in the ankle lock. Ivan is thrashing about in pain. Gaston is distracted by what is going on the ramp. Alexei leans under the ropes and grabs hold of DT’s leg and manages to pull him off Ivan. DT turns around and starts screaming at the small Russian. Nate: Gaston, will you turn around! There still is a match going on here! Robbie: HA! Ivan is going to tear Troy apart then he is going to rip Boda and Wonder limb from limb! Troy is still in the corner yelling at Ruslan and doesn’t see that Ivan has climbed back to his feet. Just as Troy turns around he is caught with a short arm clothesline. Troy bounces off of the ropes and comes back at Ivan. The Russian tries to hit Troy with a stiff right haymaker, but DT ducks the punch and gets behind Ivan long enough to hit him with the Take! Nate: The Take! The Take! Troy just hit Ivan with the Take! This one is over. Robbie: NOOOOO!!!! Troy rolls Ivan over and starts to pin the stunned bear. Gaston though is still staring at the brawl that is going on the steelramp. The fans are cheering wildly. The loud noise from the fans though gets the Frenchman’s attention. Gaston turns around to make the count. 1… 2… The count is stopped by a stiff blow to the back of the referee’s head from the flagpole wielded by Ruslan. Gaston falls to the mat out cold. Nate: What the hell is that? Robbie: That my friend is the perfectly oiled machine that is communism! Nate: That is bullshit! That’s what that is! Steven: uhm…excuse me Nate…but ah…this is a family show. Nate: What? Steven starts laughing uncontrollably. Steven: I have always wanted to say that to you! BOCA~! Up on the ramp Wonder and Boda both have Walczak up in the air and bring him crashing down onto the hard steelramp with a double powerbomb. Robbie: WHERE THE HELL IS SECURITY?!?!?! Inside the ring, Troy is going after Ruslan for stealing the win form him. Ruslan is cowering in the corner poking at Troy with the flagpole trying to fend him off. DT is about to get his hands on slimy little Russian, but he is stopped by the massive hand of the Russian bear. Nate: OH God! Ivan is up and there is no ref in the ring. Someone better get out here, quick! Ivan hoists Troy up into the air and delivers the Red Scare! Nate: Will someone get in here! This place is a battle zone! Robbie: HA! No, no, this is good! Doug Troy deserves this! Steven: BOCA~!!!!! Ivan gets down and makes the pin, but Gaston is still unconscious. Nate: Looks like Ruslan’s interference is going to cost him dearly. Robbie: Will somebody get in here!!! Steven: BOCA~! Ivan looks around and sees that there is no ref. The Russian leans over grabs Troy and picks him up to his feet. Ivan Irishwhips DT off of the ropes. Troy is flying back at Ivan who is just waiting for his victim. Ivan scoops up Troy and delivers a second Red Scare. Troy hits the mat hard and is not moving. Nate: Someone stop this! The bear is out of control! Steven: What is that running down the ramp? Robbie: It’s Speedy Riggs! Speedy Riggs manages to dodge Boda and Wonder’s attempts at stopping him. Ivan dives on top of Troy. Riggs flies into the ring and begins to count. 1… 2… 3… Winner and new #1 Contender for the World Heavyweight Title: Ivan Stanislav Robbie: HA! Ivan is the new Number One Contender! I told you!! I told you all!!! Steven: Wow!! Decker you have a gift! Ivan leaps out of the ring and charges at Boda and Wonder who flea from the enraged bear. Alexei and Riggs are helping Walczak to his feet, while inside the ring, Doug Troy is barely moving. Nate: This was a sham! Robbie: No my friend! Like I said before, this is the well oiled machine that is communism. Nate: Let’s take it a commercial break, while we try and put some pieces of this puzzle together.
