Reno Davis — history (August 2002)

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Reno Vaugn Davis was never very popular with anyone, be it his friends, his parents, girls, and even God. Everyone seemed to dislike him for various reasons. Some didn’t like his freakish blue eyes, which contained no pupils. Others thought (especially in the 80’s) his naturally neon red hair was sort of scary or stupid looking. Davis was also never really big, standing at 4’9, 69 lbs in grade 9 and only growing to 5’6, 166 lbs by graduation. His people skills were never that great, and he was destined to live forever alone in the world. On July 24th, 1980, Reno Davis was born to Paul and Rebecca Davis. Both were very wealthy on their own, and combined together were supposed to give Reno a very cushy life growing up. Paul was a successful defense lawyer, whereas Rebecca was a published author who was popular by another name, and was constantly writing. So from an early age, his parents neglected Reno. At age 13, Davis made his first friend in the form of a half dead monkey in a trashcan near his house. Kids who were dropping rocks and throwing stones at him surrounded the little monkey, who was barely clinging to life. Reno chased them off with a baseball bat and took the monkey home, later naming him ZaC. On October 9, 1993 a friendship that’s gone on for 9 years unbroken or compromised began, making Reno a little closer to being an actual kid. ZaC lived in secrecy for 2 years as Reno hid him from his father inside the house. It wasn’t hard, since Paul was barely ever home, and when he was he just yelled or hit Reno to relieve any frustration or anger bottled up. Reno took the hits for his whole life, and didn’t really think much of them anymore, but ZaC did. The little monkey latched onto Paul’s crotch one faithful Saturday morning when Paul backhanded Reno on the side of the face (For not running home immediately when he called for him). The truth was that Reno’s shirt was stuck in the fence, and Davis made sure the shirt was not ripped as it came out. He knew he’d get it worse if he ripped a shirt. Paul was not impressed and after tetanus shots and a bit of recovery time, he took ZaC for a drive in the car. Reno thought that his father and ZaC had made up, but he noticed the rifle in the backseat. He knew where his father was taking ZaC, as he’d taken other animals there as well and Reno ended up biking there and burying them. When Reno arrived on his bike, the gun was aimed at ZaC’s head. Davis rushed in front of the monkey and stood there, blocking the shot. After a few threats from his father didn’t work, Reno convinced his father that the monkey mattered to him. Paul slammed the butt of the gun into Reno’s jaw, breaking it and then he shot the bike’s tires, blowing them into pieces. Paul left Reno there to walk 3 miles home with the little monkey. Reno didn’t walk home though. Oak Park was not a popular hang out like it is today. It was where the lost youths hung out, the quote unquote “punks”. Reno, afraid to go home, ended up passing through there all bloodied up. He met his good friend Brent, who took him in and gave him a place to stay. Reno spent the rest of his high school days downtown at Oak Park and his nights at Brent’s house. Had it not been for Brent, Reno probably wouldn’t be a wrestler right now, as Brent was the son of Jean Doiron, the former professional boxer turned trainer. Jean took Reno and whipped pumped him from his 100 lbs physique, to 160 lbs of muscle, which is quite a jump in only 2 and a half years. Davis’ record ending high school was 24-6-0, holding 2 titles in the middleweight division. Upon graduation, Reno had a choice: He could take a chance and turn pro, or go into some other field of education. He chose neither. Reno found a small ad looking for pro wrestlers, saying that they’d pay 50 dollars a match. With his boxing experience, Reno thought it’d be easy, so he signed up to the federation called Five Star Wrestling. He met someone who he believed to be a friend in Griff Truxell. Griff used Reno to fight for him and after 3 matches (and 3 losses) Griff shunned Reno. The promoters thought this was great and started scheduling Reno to fight Griff, who was actually well trained in wrestling. Reno went down easily in the first match, and the second. The promoters were not impressed, as Griff’s Piston DDT finisher was hit within a few minutes of each match, causing a pin fall each time. Five Star Wrestling soon released Reno, and closed down a week later. This didn’t stop Davis, as he faced bad luck so often in his life that it was second nature for him to fail or lose. He knew, or thought he knew, that God was always there for him and would never let Reno down in the end. It was only later that he realized differently. Wrestling didn’t leave a sour taste in Reno’s mouth as expected, and he joined up with another federation called Professional Organized Wrestling, or POW. Reno learned how to suplex from Griff in FSW, and made that his finisher in POW, which didn’t really work. After 5 matches, Reno hadn’t won a single match and he was ready to call wrestling quits. That’s when Kyle Noiles rolled along. Kyle assured Reno that he had the talent to be a wrestler, just not the training. They traveled together and Kyle taught Reno the basics of wrestling, and after only a few weeks, he was teaching Reno advanced moves. Kyle and Reno had the same style, moves and attitude after awhile and became a tag team, winning the titles in their first match and holding them until both men left POW months later. Big men always seemed to like picking on Reno, and he was constantly wrestling with some injury or another in every match. His record jumped to 13-7-1 with a 12 win-streak going into the Intercontinental Title tourney. Kyle was asked to go into the newly funded No Holds Barred division. Reno advance almost effortlessly through the tourney, making each man tap out to the Queen Angelito Stretch that Noiles taught him (Reno named it the Straight-Jacket). After 3 weeks of fighting, Reno faced Frank Swallows in the finals and defeated him in a very technical submission match. Reno became the InteRENOcontinental Champion. Kyle had no luck at the PPV though, as he lost his No Holds Barred Title to a new wrestler named: ‘Freight’. Rebecca Davis passed away in 1999, causing Reno a lot of grief. She had really been the only parent he had, as she at least gave him some attention once and awhile. Reno’s lack of attention made him lose the Intercontinental title and he went into a slump. Kyle was still having troubles in the No Hold’s Barred division, but the promoters wouldn’t allow him to go back to his former Intercontinental division. Kyle Noiles soon added a more aggressiveness to his style, giving him his title shot again against Freight. He won, and kept the title for quite some time. However, the spotlight that used to shine on Kyle drifted over to Reno, who regained the Intercontinental title for the second time, and was catching the interest of various scouts. One in particular was Stevie Roberts, a ‘fans Wrestling organization’ scout. He spoke to Reno about participating in a competition known as fWo Survivor. Kyle grew jealous, thinking that if he were allowed back in the Intercontinental title division, he would be in Reno’s shoes. After all, it was he who trained Reno how to wrestle. Kyle broke off his side of the tag team and Reno decided to vacate his side as well. Kyle continuously attacked Reno backstage and during matches, trying to get noticed by the fWo scout. Reno grew confused and frustrated at his former mentor’s actions and confronted him, with the scout looking on. Kyle challenged Reno to a Title vs Title Submission match at the next televised event (POW couldn’t afford PPV deals, and so they aired a television event once a month as their sort of PPV). Reno accepted, and they were set. Both competitors were not allowed to attack each other, or else a suspension would come, making their chances of being fWo bound impossible. After 3 weeks of build up, the televised event “Enraged” aired as Reno ‘The Flare’ Davis and Kyle ‘Twisted’ Noiles fought. The match was long and hard, and by the end both were injured and fatigued. The battle leaked out onto the streets, as both men used whatever they could find to injure the other further. Reno slapped on The Straight-Jacket inside a nearby 7-11 and made Kyle beg to release the hold. He did. Stevie Roberts approached Reno after the match and offered a contract to sign, thus starting Reno’s indefinite rise to fame and glory. Kyle, however, was a broken man and quit the federation that night, disappearing for years to come. Reno also left the federation the following week, thanking the fans for being there for him and his friends for helping him out. His two closest wrestling friends: Frank Swallows and Freight (Mikey Thomas), all embraced in the ring as Reno vacated the title and left. Survivor was only the beginning of the strenuous world of wrestling however, as he received a good allowance from the fWo and got posh transportation and hotels. The backstage atmosphere wasn’t as nice though, as the 12 men he participated with were all cutthroats and the only friendliness ever brought forth was totally faked. Well, except for Reno’s, as he tried his best to befriend everyone and was thought as too stupid to be worried about. Slowly each man was picked off until the finals, where the two quiet wrestlers throughout the competition, Ruben Ross and Reno Davis, were left. Reno and Ruben fought in an exhibition round for pure sport, with Reno Davis coming on top with the win, however it was useless as Ruben Ross won the voting by one vote the next day. Reno was so close, but so far. He awaited a call from the fWo, since Kodiak Vic Creed, Juno Law, and Red Dragon all made it into the organization. The call never came, so Reno went elsewhere. He joined a few up and coming wrestling associations, but most failed immediately, sending Reno onto the street. Joe Campbell, an unknown man trying to get his name out, called Reno up one day and asked him to join a fighting organization called ‘The Asylum’. It was in America, had television and PPV deals, and most importantly, a very good salary. Reno signed up, not knowing what he was into. He fought hard and won often, even capturing the United Kingdom Championship on February 4, 2001 from Nerva. A fellow fighter by the name of Boomer McAugust took a keen liking to Reno after the two survived a roof collapsing on top of them in a mall during a snow storm, and after Reno won the title, he asked if he’d join him. Davis didn’t like how Boomer operated and kindly said no, driving Boomer insane. The two fought for a while until March 19 when Reno got his own car door slammed onto his neck by Boomer. Boomer took the UK Title and Reno, with an injury that still nags him today, left the world of shoot fighting. Pinnacle Of Insane Wrestling, or PIW, picked Reno up after his neck injury died down a bit, giving Reno a second shot at national television fame. He won 8 straight matches and then met up with Griff Truxell again. Griff convinced Reno that he’d changed and apologized for prior treatment. The two started up the tag team called as ‘Empiric Reality’ and as was accustom to Reno, won the titles in their first match. Soon after starting in PIW, Reno’s father Paul died of lung cancer a week before Reno’s match with Chris Mezz for the US Title. He lost the match, as he was very distracted. Griff called Reno a baby and said that the old man was better off dead anyway for the way he treated Reno. Reno didn’t believe it and got into a fight with Griff, causing the break up of Empiric Reality. Reno soon left PIW and it closed down a month or so later. Reno did absolutely nothing, as his heat from Survivor had burnt out and no one wanted him. He thought back to the nickname that Kyle once gave him, that being ‘The Unwanted Angel’. He called him that due to the fact that no matter how loyal Reno was to God, God repaid him with bad luck and hardships. Reno re-thought his life. He realized the reason he was put onto this world was to show that beauty lied on the inside, no matter how different or scary the outside was. He was an angel sent down in human form from God. Unfortunately, God only realized afterwards that his human race had drifted too far away from him, and even the religions that worshipped him were way off. He made a mistake sending Reno to earth, and tried to take him back, failing each time. Reno would not allow God to take Reno back, instead he would rub God’s mistake in his face until the day Reno finally did die. Thus, The Unwanted Angel was born. His luck grew worse, as Reno announced his intentions to God, who did not enjoy the news. An organization called jOlt Wrestling picked Reno up, where he spent 20 weeks or memories he’d rather forget. Once again he was placed side by side with Griff Truxell, but luckily spent very little time around him. Reno accomplished very little in the first 20 weeks, winning only 4 matches of the 10 he fought. It was a black period for Reno. Things picked up soon, as Reno won a shot at the International Title, but dimmed, as after 4 weeks he never got his shot. A man named Azrael Ravenell entered jOlt and won the International title in his first match, angering Reno. Davis challenged Azrael to a title match, which turned into a best of 3 matches tournament. The first match was a ladder match where Azrael picked up the win with a pretty entertaining back and forth match. The second match was a Ladder match, where Reno picked up the win. At the PPV, Reno and Azrael squared off in a Submissions match where Reno gained a victory, but not without Azrael smashing a cinder block over his knee, giving Reno a permanent injury. He was hurt so badly that at the next show, Reno had to vacate the title. jOlt Wrestling went on for a few months before closing, then re-starting for 5 weeks and closing again. It took longer for Reno to come out of this injury than any other. Reno wrestled in Real Action Wrestling for a while; trying to gain back his in-ring talent until the day Old School Wrestling gave him a call. They offered a contract and Reno took it without a question. His first day in Old School Wrestling didn’t go very well, as he lost ZaC and found him in the locker room of The Red Army. Reno saw all the communist stuff and gave a very long lecture to them members, putting them in awe. The little angel got his ass kicked later on that night by Michel Krieg and Tom Walczak, who also gave ZaC quite the beating. He defeated Michel Krieg the next week, but lost to Walczak the week after due to interference. That’s when he ran into Tempest. She was dating Ivan Stanislav, the leader of The Red Army, and Reno felt a soft spot for her. When Davis found out that Stanislav had left her to be brutalized by Meanstreak, and then he lied to her about it, he exposed Ivan. Tempest left in a hurry, making Reno lose a friend and Ivan lose a love. The battle between Ivan and Reno began there, as Ivan destroyed Reno’s 1994 Pontiac Grand Prix, attacked his (then friend) Amy Campbell, and then he went too far and seriously injured ZaC. Reno went off the deep end and two weeks after, defeated Ivan for a spot on top of Mount St. Hell at Ascension. Ivan got back at Reno by flipping a truck onto him one week before the Ascension PPV. While in the hospital, Reno and Amy realized their love for each other, but Reno couldn’t allow her to be hurt anymore by his curse from God, as anyone who ever became close to Reno was seriously hurt in someway. A major argument ensued with the end result of Amy punching a hole in the wall and leaving. Reno then met a proud man named Rent-A-Hero, who was consequently Amy Campbell’s other love. The two bonded well and gave each other help on how to fix each other’s relationships, not knowing they both had the same girl. Rent-A-Hero took Reno’s advice and proposed to Amy Campbell, who refused, wrecking Rent-A-Hero. Reno’s curse destroyed Rent-A-Hero, creating his alter ego: Rent-A-Villain. Ascension rolled around and Davis fought valiantly against Ivan Stanislav, taking a massive beating throughout the whole match. The Unwanted Angel managed to escape with his life, but not with a win as Ivan totally destroyed him during and after the match. Reno bandaged himself up and walked into the Ascension match with his chin up. He fought and survived through ¾ of the Ascension match up, creating a temporary alliance with Kid Wonder, a fellow cruiserweight. Aimz didn’t have the luck Reno did, as her current rival at that time, Corg, handcuffed her to the side of the mountain and began to mutilate her before he started to undress both he and her. Kid Wonder notified Davis about the events, which led Reno to grab his skateboard and ollie off the mountain towards Corg. He flew down the mountain 40 feet before landing on Corg, knocking out and eliminating both men, saving Amy from being raped. Reno was bruised terribly, but still managed to make it to the next Slam event, where he met Corg face to face... To be continued....
