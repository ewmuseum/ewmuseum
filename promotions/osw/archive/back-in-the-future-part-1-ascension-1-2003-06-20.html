Back in the Future (Part 1) (Ascension #1)

20 June 2003

by 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own roleplay post (the site calls them Promos) (#71) by 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst, dated 20 June 2003, written from Washington D.C, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
At Ascension, I have to defeat a shedload of people to become the Old School Champion. Now I know that the title doesn't fit my description. The 'old' part doesn't anyway. Of course the 'champion' does. But Exceptional Dave Hurst is definitely not old. In fact, EDH is quite new. Hell, he's achieved a lot in his spell. Howard Porter is no more thanks to me. That was my debut match too! Tom Walczak got his arse handed to him and has never been the same since. Azala Zameer lost his nashers thanks to me, and although he's having a lot of success and gaining a lot of fans, everyone knows it's only for his golden falsies he has in. Then there's Six Finger Cisco, who I haven't managed to brush off as of yet. The jammie bastard always pulls off something flukey to escape defeat. But Exceptional Dave Hurst is used to a challenge. Exceptional Dave Hurst is used to bringing things up a notch. Otherwise, I wouldn't be here today. But Six Finger Cisco is going to take the beating of his life at Ascension. Six Finger Cisco is going to be brutalised at Ascension. And if I don't finish off the son of a bitch in our singles match that night, I'll sure as hell beat seven shades of shit out of him in the Ascension match, I can assure you of that! -----=====|=====----- Darkness is beginning to fall and the lamp posts are starting to light, shining yellow beams across the street and giving a strange yellow tint to the pavement as I walk along it. Ahead of me, sitting next to a lamppost, is a small black box. The box has a kind of shiny gleam to it, drawing me closer. I walk up to it, kneel down and gaze at the wonder of the shiny black box. I wonder what’s inside. There’s a white button on the front of the box and in black lettering it says ‘Press Me’. Should I press it? I’m not sure if I should. I don’t know what’s inside it, and the ‘Press me’ button is a little peculiar. Ah, what the hell, what can happen, eh? I reach forward to press the button, as my finger just touches the button there is a loud popping noise, deafening me for a short moment. I crease my eyes and hold my ears for a moment to regain my hearing then as I look in front of me, the box has vanished! In the place of where the box used to be is a scruffy-looking man in his mid-20s with a bum-fluff beard and a shaven head. I look up at him with curiosity. Where has the box gone? And what is this guy doing in his place? All is answered in a single sentence. “Hi, I’m Bobby, the genie of the shiny black box!” says the scruffy guy. Eh? Excuse me? What does he mean ‘the genie of the shiny black box’? This isn’t a fairy tale you know! So I ask him “What do you mean ‘the genie of the shiny black box?’” Bobby smirks, ducks his head and scratches his eyebrow. This is obviously not a good question to ask him. “It’s a long story, and not somethin’ I’d like to talk about, mate. If it’s okay with you,” Bobby says in a strong Londoner accent. “No problem, mate.” I reply. I guessed as much, these ‘genie in the lamp’ stories normally have stupid backlogged plots that bore the viewer. “What do you want, anyway?” I ask. “That’s not your question, mate” says Bobby “Ya see. You freed me from the box. So, I have to grant ya three wishes, innit?” “Erm… right… So you can get me anything I like?” I ask. “I can get ya anything, mate! I just get it off the internet!” says Bobby with a cheesy grin of pride appearing on his face. As was apparent from the start, this guy isn’t your average genie, if there is such a thing. I expected someone floating in the air with his legs crossed or something. This guy is just a glorified software pirate. “Ha, you couldn’t get anything, Bob.” I reply, challenging him as the greatest pirate in the Universe. “No mate. I can get absolutely anythin’. I’m a genie, me. You can ask me to get anything, and I can get it. Not the best quality, mind. But I can get it for you, for free!” “Okay…” I say as I ponder what unbelievable thing I can ask for, “How about… a time machine?!” “Time machine, eh?” he says as if he might be able to get it. Some people are so stupid, “Yeah, I can get you that. I’ll start downloading it now, just a sec.” What the hell is he going on about? How the hell can you download a flaming time machine from the internet?! “How in the hell do you get a time machine off of the internet?!” I ask him. “Off of share reactor, innit?” he says, letting his dialect shine through. “Right… well that’s great… then…” Bobby pulls out from his coat a laptop lays it on the floor. Then he starts tapping away at the keys a furious rate. All of a sudden, a puff of white smoke explodes out of the floor and as the smoke clears, a grandfather clock appears in its place. “A grandfather clock, what’s this?” I ask Bobby. “I asked for a time machine.” “Well it is a time machine, innit?” Bobby says “It measures time, doesn’t it?” “I want to TRAVEL through time!” “Oh, do ya?” Bobby says looking surprised, which is a surprise in itself. Surely this idiot aught to know if I wanted a clock, I’d actually ask for a clock. “I have that on CD!” Bobby exclaims. Now this is really weird. How the hell do you hold a time machine on a CD for Christ’s sake?! Bob opens up the CD tray on his laptop then places a CD into it and lets it close. A few seconds later another puff of smoke covers the grandfather clock and a red telephone box appears in its place. “THIS IS A PHONE BOX!” I cry out at the deranged excuse for a genie. Bob holds his hands out in reassurance. “It’s okay,” he says “it is a time machine, and a Tardis.” What in God’s name is a Tardis, for crying out loud?! So I ask him, “A what?” to which he replies, “I don’t know, that’s what it said on the CD!” Anyway, Bob ushers me into the Tardis. I open the door to the phone box-looking contraption and step inside. Looking around, amazingly this phone box seems very spacious! It is pitch black inside, apart from a glowing piece of machinery on the side of the cabinet. “How does this thing work?” I shout, expecting the genie to respond. A huge face of Bob suddenly appears above me in a weird Mork and Mindy kind of a way. “Well…” he says “the way I always get these things working is to just try it out and see if it works!” before his head fades back into the darkness. Somehow I’m not sure about all this, but this idiot has made a phone box appear out of nowhere, so I’m entitled to believe any crap that comes out of his mouth. So I look at the machinery in front of me, which is letting off an orange-tinted glow. There are a bunch of levers and stuff, which I’m not really interested in. There is a keyboard there with an LCD display and ‘Date of Destination =’ written on it. So I guess they just want me to key in the date I want to travel to and hit the enter key. So I’ll ponder about what I’m going to do. Cisco is the freak of all freaks with the extra digit and all that stuff. He’s getting so cocky and big for his boots lately. He honestly thinks that he stands a chance at Ascension against the likes of me! Just because he cheated in the Gladiator challenge and managed to pull off a fluke draw against me. Ever since then he thinks he’s a contender and stops at nothing to make me look a monkey in front of the whole of Old School Wrestling. Well I’ve had enough of it. I’m not going to stand here and take this kind of crap from him. At Ascension he’s getting obliterated. At Ascension there’s no way I’m going to let him win that match. Hey, hang on! I want Six Finger Cisco to lose this match… I have a time machine… think of the possibilities! So now the question is how exactly am I going to use the time machine to fuck up Cisco before he gets to Ascension…? I’ve got it! I’m going to go back to the beginning of all this! The gladiator challenge matches! And the pugel stick duel! I’m going to travel back to just before the duel and tell myself exactly what cheating tactics that freak is going to pull. I mean, moving from one podium to the other is not allowed, IT’S THE RULES! So I set the date. I tap 4th May 2003, 4.00pm into the machine. That should give me enough time to warn me of the cheating bastard before it’s too late. And let me have a bit of fun too. The ‘Mork and Mindy’ style head of Bob appears overhead once again. I look up to see what the idiot has to say this time. “Good luck, mate!” says Bob as a huge fist appears in front of me. I put my fist out to touch his, returning the gesture. “Safe!” says Bob in his typical Londoner dialect as his face and fist fades once again back into the darkness. I whack the enter key on the keypad in eager anticipation of what is to come. I’m really hyped about this whole time travel thing. Hang on a second! The damned thing isn’t working! Whoa! What’s that! There’s an ear-splitting rumble and the phone booth is starting to shake violently, I’m barely able to keep my balance as the thing shakes back and forth like a small earthquake. The blackness surrounding me and the controls are starting to change, they are starting to light up and glow. There is a bellowing light-yellow light shining all around me. Am I dying? Am I going to heaven? That’s what it seems like as the holy light shines down on me. Looking around me it’s like a misty, light space going off into the distance. Looking below me, it is like the floor is made of glass, and I can see more lighted mist shining off into the distance from below me. The light starts to dim and the tremors subside gradually until it reaches a standstill... -----=====|=====----- Has it worked? Have I managed the impossible and travelled back in time? There's only one way to find out. I grab hold of the door and pull it open. Stepping outside into the sunlight it takes a while for my eyes to adjust. When my sight is regained I look and find out that everything is as it was. 'It didn't fucking work!' I scream. As I said that Bobby's head appeared 'Mork and Mindy' style once again, this time in the sky. 'What do ya mean it didn't work?' Bobby says in a bemused tone of voice 'course it worked, innit?' he says idiotically in his ridiculous dialect. 'I didn't work! Why is there exactly the same stuff still here then? The only thing that's different is this breeze that's picked up!' I ask. ''Eh? What breeze? Anyway, because you only travelled back a month and a bit, mate! This stuff would still be here then!' he said matter-of-factly. 'Okay... so how do I know that I travelled then?' 'You ask someone, innit?' So I walk over to the nearest passer-by and ask 'Excuse me, mate. What's today's date?' 'Fuck you!' the guy says as he walks away, turning and sticking a middle finger up. So I try again on the next passer-by, this one is an elderly woman 'Excuse me, love. What's today's date?' 'Get lost, ya pervert!' she says. A child walks past, takes one look at me and runs off screaming. The parent of the child walks past with a sickened look on her face. 'You're sick!' she says as she walks off. 'What's going on?!' I shout out as Bobby interrupts by coughing violently to catch my attention. 'What?!' I ask. 'Mate, I forgot to tell ya! When ya travel through time, you travel, but only you.' He is speaking as if he's really embarrased but I have no idea why. 'What's that supposed to mean?' I ask him. 'Well... your clothes didn't travel with you...' This is not a good moment to realise that it is not, in fact, a breezy day. I slowly-but-surely tilt my head downwards to take a look at the inevitable truth that I am in fact, stark-bollock naked. 'Oh shit!' I scream as I get sight of my own penis. I'm not scared of it, but when you see it in a public area, you can't help but feel slightly scared of it. This is the point where I run for cover, or out of daylight at least. What am I going to do!? Dear God, what am I going to do!? TO BE CONTINUED...
