The Entrance Written: June 23rd, 2002 The scene fades in from black to reveal a limousine pulling up behind the Arena in Oakland, in Oakland, California. The long, black, vehicle stops and out steps am overweight, balding, middle-aged chauffeur, who steps to the back of the vehicle, and opens the rear door. Within seconds, former OSW World Heavyweight Champion, Sebastian Toomes steps out of the from the luxury vehicle, now with that old look in his eye; one that personifies arrogant confidence and cockiness in one glance. Brushing away his silvery locks from his face, he stops and extends his hand back into the limo, and which is grasp by the silk smooth digits of none other than Toomes’ lady-love, OSW owner, Jane Reagan. Jane steps out of the vehicle, revealing her silky smooth legs and well toned features, which are only accentuated by a lovely looking, Catholic-school-esque outfit she is wearing. Seabass kisses Jane hand and looks into her eyes, while Jane takes her free hand and strokes Toomes’ cheek. Jane then sniffs the air. Jane: Ahhhh….Smell all that money. Toomes smiles. Toomes: Don’t sniff too hard, because you might smell the faggots from across the Bay. Jane shrugs. Jane: Darling, gay money spends the same as straight money. Now come on, its time for you to begin your rapid ascension back up to your rightful place at the top of OSW. Toomes grins evilly. Toomes: I love you. Jane winks and leads Seabass towards the a now-open door into the backstage area. Suddenly, the angle of the shot changes, from about three feet away from the initial conversation, to a good 30-40 feet away, from a location that appears to be from around the edge of a dumpster. This shot is different, however, than the initial shot, as this appears to be from the perspective of someone’s eyes, as if we are seeing what someone else sees, as opposed to an actual shot from the camera. From the “eyes” of whoever is secreted behind, we see Toomes lead Jane through a gauntlet of security guards, into the rear of the area…when suddenly, some scurrying to the side of the shot is heard. The “camera” pans towards the noise, to reveal a large rat on the ground, behind the dumpster. The rat skitters across the rain and grease slickened ground, searching for a morsel, and doesn’t even sense the end of its parasitic existence is near… …in the form of a size 13, black, combat boot.
