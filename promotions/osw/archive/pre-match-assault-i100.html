Pre-Match Assault

2002-10-03
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pre-Match Assault Written: 10/03/2002 Nate: (sighing) I suppose that will have to do. Now fans, not only do we have the World Title on the line here tonight, but the Rhett Locke will battle Drew Hostyle for the OSW International title while Heresian Bloodlust will take on Civil Disobedience in a Surburban Death Match with the tag team straps on the line. Robbie: Hostyle is going to crush Locke, and hell will freeze over before Cain and Hessian lose to those snot nosed punks from Civil Disobedience. Nate: Speaking of Civil Disobedience, I’m just getting word that Seth Masters and Ethan Hawke have just entered the building, so we are going to send you to the back!!! The camera shot changes backstage as Masters and Hawke are walking through the entryway to the backstage area with gym bags over their shoulders. From inside the arena, there is a huge pop from the fans, who are obviously watching from the arena’s Jumbotron. Both men have a confident stride in their step and their heads are held high. Seth: Tonight’s our night! I can feel just feel it! Trevor: Yeah, that is if you don’t screw something up and I have to bail you out. Seth: When do you ever have to bail me out? Trevor: Well, do you remember… Something stops Masters in his tracks. He extends his arm out across Trevor’s chest. Trevor: What the hell are you doing? Seth: Shhh! Look! Masters points down the hallway in the direction of two small women facing the other direction. Seth: It seems that these lovely ladies are lost. We should help them out. Trevor: Jesus, do you always have to think with your dick? Steven: I always thing about his… Nate: SHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! Seth: Shhh! Come on! Masters walks down the hallway towards the two small women who still haven’t noticed Masters and Hawke approaching. Masters and Hawke approach quickly and in no time they are directly behind the little ladies. Masters clears his throat. Seth: Excuse me ladies, may the two of us be of assistance to you. You seem to be lost. Masters nudges Hawke and winks. Hawke just rolls his eyes. Little lady 1: Oh no thank you, we seem to have found exactly what we’re looking for. Heh-Heh-Heh. Seth: What the… The two little women turn around quickly and begin to spray something in the eyes of both Masters and Hawke. Both men wince in pain as the substance gets into their eyes. Seth: Mace!! AHH!! Trevor: Dammit, Masters! The two little women remove their wigs as they continue to spray Masters and Hawke to reveal that these to women are no ladies at all, but in fact they are Caleb and Minion, the loyal managers of Heresian Bloodlust. Nate: IT’S THE SCUMBAG MANAGERS OF HERESIAN BLOODLUST!!! Robbie: I love it when a plan comes together!!! A chorus of boos explodes from the crowd. Minion: HA! Take that bitches! Caleb: Revenge is a bitch, ain’t it pretty boys! Heh-Heh-Heh. Masters and Hawke are on the ground now trying to blindly kick at Caleb and Minion. Hessian and Azrael Cain appear from behind the two little drag queens. Nate: SOMEONE CALL SECURITY!!! CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE IS HELPLESS!!! Robbie: Actually, its better that they can not see, because they now avoid having to look at impending doom bearing down upon them. Hessian turns to Cain and chuckles. Hessian: Okay, you were right. Sometimes the little guys do have some good ideas. Cain and Hessian shove Caleb and Minion out of their way. Both of them lean over and pick Masters and Hawke up and slam them against the wall. Hessian: I give you credit for showing up tonight. I thought you two would wuss out. I guess you do have some balls after all. It’s crying shame though, seeing how the two of you are going to lose them tonight… Steven: I have a jar full of formaldehyde right here!! There is dead air for 1…..2…..3 seconds. Hessian growls. Hessian: You should have stayed home with your mommies. At least then you would have some sort of future. Cain steps forward and chimes in. Cain: Tonight, all of your dreams are going to be crushed. The two of you are going fall and I mean fall hard. Your end is near. Embrace the time that you have left. Cain punches Masters in the nose, while Hessian head butts Hawke in the face. Both of them release the temporarily blinded Masters and Hawke, who crash to the ground. Cain: The clock is ticking boys. Cherish every second. You don’t know which one will be your last. Cain and Hessian turn to leave. As the walk down the corridor Minion and Caleb begin to follow. The small hobo that is Caleb stops though and turns back to Civil Disobedience. He waddles back to them and gets in Masters face. The hobo kicks Masters in the groin. Nate: OH COME ON!!! THIS IS TOTALLY OUT OF HAND!!! Caleb: That’s for last night. Heh-Heh. And this…Heh-Heh…well this for being you. Heh-Heh-Heh. Caleb starts to gnaw at Masters nose with his toothless mouth. A collective grown erupts from the crowd. Cain: Caleb!! Caleb stops gnawing. Caleb: Who’s the bitch now? Heh-Heh-Heh. The hobo then waddles into the darkness down the hallway after Minion, Hessian, and Cain. The camera shot changes to the announcers’ table after staying on Masters and Hawke struggling to get back to their feet. Nate: Did I just see that? Tell me I just didn’t see Minion and Caleb in miniskirts. Robbie: Afraid so. Steven: There goes my libido.
