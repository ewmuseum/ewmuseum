Mystery Solved!

2002-05-11
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mystery Solved! Written: May 11th, 2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam! I’m getting word our camera have caught up with the Tag Champions! Decker: It’s Hawke and Masters last moments on earth! (Heresian Bloodlust travels through the hallway swiftly, throwing everyone in their way to the wall. A table stands in Hessian’s way, so he merely flips it on it’s side and they continue on. The feverish search through the corridors comes to an end, however, as the goal is in sight. The two arrive at Civil Disobedience’s locker room and Hessian tries to open the door ever so carefully, not to tip off their prey. He groans as he realizes the former Champions had locked the door, so he turns to his partner, Cain.) Hessian: Get it open. (Cain smashes the sledgehammer over the knob, blowing all incognita they had, backs off and kicks the door wide open. Trevor Hawke looks up from reading a magazine to see the commotion, but Cain ram the sledgehammer sideways into his face meets his concern. He falls on the floor, grabbing his face as blood flows freely from it. Seth jumps from his chair and dives to aid his fallen companion. Cain scouts the move and bats at the aid, but Masters ducks it. The hammer thuds, denting the locker which stalls the might Azrael for a moment, allowing Seth to lay a few right hands to his attacker, just enough to knock the hammer from his hands. Masters knocks the beast down with a final right hook and turns exhausted, but forgets the other monster. Hessian drives the “Fist of Rage” right in the throat of Seth Masters, additionally painful thanks to the Steel Gauntlet worn by the Gothic Warrior. Masters falls to one-knee, gasping for breath and clutching his throat, at the complete mercy of the sadistic Hessian.) Nate: This isn’t fair! Civil Disobedience just wrestled a match against Da Westsidaz, you can’t expect them to even have a fighting chance against these two monsters from Hell. Decker: They shouldn’t have messed up the Tag Champs locker room! Take a lesson kiddies, this is how real revenge is taken. (Hessian half-heartedly grabs Seth’s head and pulls him to his feet. The hulking beast grabs the cruiserweight from Aurora by the throat and stares him right in the eyes. He hoists him into the air with one hand and drives him into the cement creating a sound of his back slamming unto the concrete floor hair-raisingly loud. Masters groans in pain, losing consciousness as a bloodied companion staggers to his feet. Unfortunately, Azrael was already fully recovered from the fist fight. Cain grins and drives his fingers into the mouth of Trevor Hawke. Cain’s patented “Famine” leaves Trevor helpless and he eventually succumbs, slowly falling unto his back, fighting with all his will to stay conscious. Meanwhile, Hessian makes his way across the locker room to a lone table and wipes a variety of garbage from it. He pulls it into the center of the room and raises the limp body of Masters on-top of the tool of destruction.) Nate: Oh no, I can’t watch. These two sadistic monsters might seriously hurt these two! You can’t expect them to have a fighting chance after what they’ve went through over the past week! Steven: You know, once I rolled Boca onto the table and… *SMACK!* (Cain finally releases the Claw, but it is obviously not out of mercy. The sizable beast spares no time bringing the former Tag Champion to his feet, grabbing him by his face and using some colorful language describing their actions. Hessian makes his way over as well and the two use their almost unlimited strength to hoist Trevor Hawke into mid-air. He two then drive him into his tag partner, sending them both through the table in a scene of carnage.) Nate: OH MY GOD! HERESIAN BLOODLUST JUST SENT CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE THROUGH A TABLE! Decker: Oh man, not even Civil D really deserved that. Wait a minute, weren’t they the reason we had to see Carrot Top! ARGHH! Find another table! (Cain spits on the limp bodies and a grin fills his face. He walks over and retrieves his weapon of choice. He returns and raises the corrupted hammer into the air. He is about to finish off the initial Tag Champions, permanently when a deep, ethnic voice breaks the silence.) Voice: AWWEEE YEAH! It’s gothic-bashing time!!! (Hessian is the first to turn around, only to be met by direct chair shot into his skull. Although the steel mask detoured the blow slightly, it was still enough too stagger him. Cain turns with his weapon in hand, but another chair shot rings across his unprotected forehead, rattling the beast enough to send him sprawling head over heels.) Nate: THAT’S SHORT CIRCUIT AND RIPTAH! DA WESTSIDAZ ARE HERE TO HELP CIVIL D! Decker: I don’t know whether to boo or mark out…My bruthas or the DA MEN~! Oops, don’t tell Marx I called them that. (The crowd goes nuts as Da Westsidaz stand ready with the chairs again, preparing for a counter attack. Hessian regains his footing, but Short Circuit drives the chair into his forehead once again, denting the steel facemask. Hessian still isn’t knocked from his feet, but Da Westsidaz prove to much. The duo slam the chairs together in a conCHAIRto fashion, crunching Hessian’s skull and distorting his mask and leaving him barely standing. He staggers for a moment, but then drops to all fours as blood streams from his masks’ mouth area.) Decker: GOD! He’s spitting up blood! Nate: I think Da Westsidaz remember the multiple hammer shots Hessian took six days ago! Why else would they focus of Hessian’s head? (Cain rises, still holding what is turning out to be a nasty welt. Da Westsidaz, like assassins, strike quickly. Short Circuit drives the front-end of the steel chair into Azrael’s abdomen, winding him. He bends over gasping for breath and Short Circuit looks to Riptah, who nods. The crowd goes wild, as they know what is coming. Riptah grabs the beast by the waist and his arms constrict, while the muscles on his body bulge. Riptah seemingly easily hoists Azrael Cain up in a flapjack and Short Circuit catches his neck delivering THE WESTSIDE BUSTAH ON THE CONCRETE FLOOR! Cain, as on instinct, shakes a little, obviously in a lot of pain and very unconscious. The crowd goes wild at ringside after witnessing Da Westsidaz clean house.) Nate: Da Westsidaz still know how to do it! Steven: REALLY?! (Hessian rises to his feet, the crimson blood streaking down his mask. He staggers for a moment, but falls to his knees, the loss of blood too much for even the large hulk. Riptah then retrieves a bottle of alcohol from his bag, sitting in the door way. He looks at the helpless monster, as if measuring him up.) Decker: Jesus, that is one huge bottle of ABSOLUT~! VODKA! (Riptah takes the lid off the vodka and takes a swill of it. He takes the strong taste down like a man, then puts the lid back up. He bends over and stares Hessian right in the eyes, smiling right in the face of the Tag Champion. Riptah rises back up and stalls for a moment. Then suddenly with a quick movement, he SHATTERS THE BOTTLE OVER THE SKULL OF HESSIAN!) Decker: JEEESUS!!! (Hessian falls, unconscious and defeated. He lays in a pool of his own blood, alcohol and glass while his partner lays motionless a few feet from him. Short Circuit steps over Hessian and looks at Civil Disobedience, who are just starting to move slowly, groaning and slipping in and out of consciousness in the table wreckage. After a moment, Riptah passes a small bottle to Short Circuit, who shakes it quickly.) Nate: What the… (Each Westsidah bends down over top of their respective tag team and starts to spray paint out of the bottles on to them. Riptah writes, “DA WESTSIDE!” over top of Heresian Bloodlust’s still bodies as Short Circuit writes “IS DA BESTSIDE!” over top of the wreckage on the tables.) Nate: Whoa, does this mean what I think it means? Decker: That Da Westside really IS Da Bestside? Nate: No, Da Westsidaz have spray-paint bottles…Oh shit! THEY were the ones that trashed the Tag Champions locker room! Well, I don’t think this will bode well for Da Westsidaz next week when Civil D and the tag champions figure out what happened! Decker: Hell yeah, there’s going to be hell to pay next week! (Da Westsidaz keep on laughing while referees and trainers rush the room, checking on everyone. The two legends walk confidently out of the room as the scene cuts to a commercial.)
