Shutting up Toomes

2002-04-27
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shutting up Toomes Written: April 27, 2002 The crowd begins to cheer as a red flag appears behind the hammer and sickle and another explosion rocks through the arena as the massive Ivan Stanislav emerges from the backstage area with a microphone in his hand!! Steven: IVAN STANISLAV IS HERE! Robbie: I don’t see the leash that Tempest has on him anywhere! What’s going on? The crowd instantly rallies behind Stanislav and begins to chant his name!! Crowd: IVAN!!! IVAN!!! IVAN!!! Nate: LISTEN TO THIS CROWD!! I CAN BARELY HEAR MYSELF TALK!! Robbie: As if that’s a bad thing… Ivan raises his microphone. Ivan: Sebastian Toomes!!!! We are tired of your lies!! The crowd cheers in unison as Ivan slowly lumbers towards the ring. Ivan: Sebastian Toomes!!!! We are tired of your excuses!! Ivan stops halfway down the entryway. Ivan: Sebastian Toomes!!!! We are tired of your stupid stories!! Ivan continues to walk closer to the ring and stops. He points at Toomes…. Ivan: But most of all, Switchblade, WE ARE TIRED OF YOU!!! The crowd explodes in agreement as Sebastian Toomes looks around in amazement, as if the crowd had somehow betrayed him. Ivan stands tall outside the ring… Ivan: I have risen to dethrone you from your pedestal in which you put yourself, and you are running scared, Switchblade!! You have made an enemy out of someone that Jane Reagan cannot stop! You have not only angered one person, but The Red Army as a whole and The Russian Federation as well! Ivan reaches up and pulls himself up to the apron. Switchblade backs off slightly as Ivan leers at him. Ivan: We have grown tired of your talking and your nonchalant attitude! I have been sworn protector of the people and intend to carry out my portion of obligation! Tomorrow, you and I must battle in ring at Head on Collision in which you will try to defend that title against the might of The Russian Bear… Ivan climbs into the ring, the mat shaking as he does. Switchblade gives Ivan a wide berth. Nate: IVAN IS IN THE RING!!! THEY COULD GO, RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!! Ivan rubs his chin, then raises the microphone to continue. Ivan: Do you think you can, Switchblade? Do you think that your feeble body can defend against the might of Ivan Stanislav? I have sworn my service to the people, and I ask, do the people wish Switchblade Toomes to be OSW World Champion? Crowd: NO!!! Nate: Resounding answer from the crowd! Ivan Stanislav is definitely rallying the people behind him! Robbie: The people are fickle, Nate! Steven: Yeah, but so are you! Robbie: That’s beyond the point! Switchblade gasps in disbelief. Switchblade: I’m YOUR rising star, Stanislav! You can’t talk to me like that!! Ivan: A rising star must always fall sooner or later! And it is time to fall hard, Toomes! Stanislav growls and motions for Toomes to step forward. Ivan: Why should we wait one more night when we can finish this right now? Toomes steps forward and yells back Switchblade: Alright Stanislav! Lets go!! Nate: FOLKS! IVAN STANISLAV AND SWITCHBLADE AREN’T GOING TO WAIT UNTIL HEAD ON COLLISION! THEY ARE GONNA GO!!! RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!! Stanislav and Toomes charge and one another and begin to brawl as the crowd goes ballistic. Toomes, however, is unable to stand toe to toe with Stanislav and begins to wear as the massive fists of Stanislav hammer into him. Stanislav sends Toomes into the ropes, on the rebound he lifts Toomes over his head in a military press and dumps him to the mat! The crowd explodes. Nate: STANISLAV JUST DUMPED THE WORLD CHAMPION LIKE A SACK OF POTATOES!!!! Ivan raises his arms high in the air, much to the delight of the fans who cheer back at him. Security pours out from backstage as Stanislav allows Toomes to slowly stand. Steven: HERE COMES SECURITY!!! Toomes gets to his feet and rushes at Stanislav who stands tall when Security slides under the ropes and cuts off Switchblade! Ivan roars and rushes to hit him, only for around ten guards to pile on him and push him away! Nate: NOOOOOOOOO!!! LET THEM GO AT IT!!!! Steven: Look at all those men! Robbie: I wanna see Switchblade kick Ivan’s ass!! Nate: I wanna see Ivan kick Switchblade’s ass!! Switchblade jumps and tries to get through the men, but its worthless. Ivan roars and with a surge of energy shoves all ten guards off of him! They fall back on the mat as Ivan tosses the three guards off of Switchblade and the two hammer at each other again!! Nate: Security can’t stop these men!! More are coming out!! Sure enough, about ten more guards head down the entryway as five men literally jump on the back of Stanislav. Ivan growls loudly as Switchblade pokes him in the eye and kicks Ivan dead in the kneecap! Ivan drops to a knee as two guards grab Switchblade and drag him away from Ivan. Steven: (singing) IT’S RAININ’ MEN!!! HALLELUJAH, IT’S RAININ’ MEN!!! Nate: Folks! There must be thirty security guards in the ring!! Three more men pile up on the World Champion as Ivan gets to his feet, dwarfing the men around him. However, even Stanislav cannot push through as five men a piece grab his legs, three grab each arm, and the rest push with all their might to get him away!! Ivan’s voice booms throughout the area… Ivan: YOU ARE DEAD MAN TOOMES!! YOU ARE DEAD!!! Nate: ALL HELL HAS BROKEN LOOSE HERE!!! WE NEED TO HEAD TO A COMMERCIAL!!! Robbie: LUCKY FOR IVAN!!! SEABASS WAS ABOUT TO MURDILATE HIM!!! Nate: MURDILATE ISN’T EVEN A WORD!!! WE WILL BE RIGHT BACK AFTER THESE MESSAGES!!! DON’T GO AWAY!!!!
