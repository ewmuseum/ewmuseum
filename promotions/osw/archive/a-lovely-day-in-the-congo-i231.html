A Lovely Day in the Congo

2002-02-16
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Lovely Day in the Congo Written: 2/16/02 A lovely Day in the Congo Slam 2/16 Scene opens up in the city of Kinshasa. The sun shines brightly in the bright blue sky as Ivan Stanislav is seen walking down a sidewalk alongside some dilapidated buildings. Stanislav wears his black shirt, red suspenders, pants and boots. As the camera gets closer we begin to hear Ivan talking to himself…with no Tempest in sight…. Ivan: ....now...Stalin created Machine Tractor Stations to collectivize the Pogroms in 1932 and create a more collective Soviet Union in order to fulfill first Five Year Plan. Ivan grins to himself, almost as if he was there when Stalin issued the beginning of the MTS's, he pauses and looks to see Tempest is not there! Ivan: What the.... Ivan looks around, shoving a few people out of his way as he searches for the bombshell. Ivan: Where did she go? Ivan growls, rolling over a small car and looking under it. Tempest creeps up silently in the crowded street and hops onto packing crate. It's beside a building and she's hidden around the corner. As he nears, Tempest leaps out, at his height. Tempest: BOO!! Ivan: DAAA! Ivan roars and backs into a tree imbedded in the sidewalk and knocks it over. People scatter. He growls and fixes a suspender. Ivan: What are you doing?! Tempest grins and laughs as she hops up and down a little. Tempest: Just trying to liven things up a little. Ivan: Liven things up? But I was telling you about history of Communism! You must appreciate that, no? After all, you live in Communist China. Ivan grins at the mentioning of the People's Republic. He looks down at Tempest with his hands on his hips. Tempest: I live in New York. I left Hong Kong when they turned it over. She looks at him curiously. Ivan blinks, trying to think of a good excuse for her leaving. Ivan: Wh..why would you leave great Communist Country? Ivan looks thoroughly confused, this thinking is completely alien to him. Tempest chuckles and shakes her head. Tempest: Cuz when the communists show up, you know the party's over. Of course, Hong Kong was always run by the Triads and still is, hardly matters who's in power. Ivan bites his lower lip. Ivan: You're.....not a devoted communist? Her eyelids flutter in exasperation.- Tempest: Um... no. Ivan blinks, not really sure how to take any of this. Finally, he shrugs it off. Ivan: Right....well..let us head on through this town, I will show you how to incite revolution! Ivan smiles, thinking she'll get a charge out of that. Tempest looks vaguely worried. Tempest: Um, revolution? Ivan begins to walk, unable to get off of the communist subject. Ivan: Yes, when you see how devote communists act, you will be awe inspired! You will go running back to the People's Republic of China! Thinking fast, Tempest catches his arm... as best she can. Tempest: Hold on a second there, I have a better idea. Why don't we duck in that movie theater and enjoy a flick? We could get out of the sun... and no one would see you with me. She rolls her eyes looking away from him. Ivan looks down at her Ivan: Not see you with me? You should be proud to be with great Russian hero! But..if you insist.... Tempest: Oh I am! Which is uh... er... why I want to spend some time alone with you instead of in a crowd. She grins in a way that's just slightly doubtful about whether she's telling the truth. Ivan thinks for a second, and then realizes the subtext. He opens his eyes wide. Ivan: Oooohhh!! Da...da, I understand. Well then, let us go see this movie! ~Later~ As a soft set of violins plays, Tempest watches the screen, fully engrossed in the chick flick of the week, while Ivan Stanislav simmers next to her. He grinds his jaw as a large empty group of seats are behind him, since he blocks the view everywhere. He looks over at Tempest. Ivan: How long is this movie? It is not even in any language that I know! Tempest snuffles slightly. Tempest: I'm not sure, but it's very touching, don't you think? Ivan raises an eyebrow, thinking...... Ivan (thinking to himself) * It is boring! I should be starting my revolution!! I should be inspiring the people!!!* Ivan looks down at Tempest, and keeps thinking Ivan: Uh....I...I suppose it can be touching.... Ivan shrugs, swallowing his pride and trying, oh so hard, to be nice. Tempest's eyes tear up ever so slightly and she turns to Ivan... and blows her nose on the shoulder of his uniform. Ivan's eyes widen as he looks down at his precious shirt. He mouths something. Ivan's jaw slowly begins to jut from his skull and his temples on the sides of his head begin to move as he stares at Tempest, who still blows her nose. Ivan: It is not that sad! She sighs and seems happier, smiling lightly. Tempest: You're right, it's not. Ivan smiles, knowing what will cheer her up. He brings a huge arm around her and almost crushes her up against his barrel chest. Ivan: There there Tempest, think of the lives of those Russians during Imperial era? Many starved to death, did not make any money, and were ostracized by the bourgeois capitalist Russians! And then, great Vladimir Ulianov, Vladimir Lenin that is, came and delivered them to the Soviet Union!! Tempest looks at him and her eyelids droop, partly in disbelief. Ivan: I know you are amazed, I can see it in your eyes, but it is true! And luckily, they followed him, they followed him to the death, and then great Stalin took over! Ivan grins, a tear in his own eye. Ivan: And he lead us to fight in Second Great Fatherland War in which we, the mighty Russians of Soviet Union, ousted the fascist Germans and protected the world! Tempest yawns and looks mildly annoyed at being caught in the grip of the human bench vise. Ivan: Just as we did in ancient times against the Mongols, and the French when Napoleon thought he could control the world, and the third time was Hitler! We, the great Russians, have been protectors of world many times over!! A few people turn around and give Ivan a dirty look as he gets louder and more excited. Tempest's eyelids droop further until she can no longer resist the urge coming over her... to sleep. Her head plops against Ivan's shoulder. Ivan keeps talking, not realizing she's asleep. Ivan: And that was great time! And then Khrushchev came and brought America to her knees! And Brezhnev and those after him! Ivan smiles, thinking she's now snuggling with him. Ivan: That is right Tempest, do not be afraid! Never be afraid of anything so long as a Russian is nearby..... Ivan pauses for a moment, thinking that this is quite a genuine moment, not realizing she's asleep. Ivan: ....do not worry, Ivan will protect you, do not be afraid! Tempest: srk... bebebebe..... srk.... bebebe Ivan pauses as he hears her snore..he stops, and looks down at her. His face drains away from what looked like almost tenderness. Stanislav shakes his head and mutters to himself in Russian. He rolls his eyes, realizing she heard nothing that he said. He shrugs and keeps his arm on her whilst suffering through the movie that he cannot even understand. Ivan sighs and shakes his head. Ivan: Bah..who is Ivan kidding....grr..... Ivan shakes his head and rolls his eyes, muttering to himself in Russian as the camera fades out.
