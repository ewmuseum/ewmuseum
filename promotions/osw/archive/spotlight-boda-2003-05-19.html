Spotlight: Boda

19 May 2003

by Shanda Russell

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own news post (#53) by Shanda Russell, dated 19 May 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
Shanda: Hi there OSW. Shanda Russell here with a very special Wrestler Spotlight for you today! I’m sitting here with the newly crowned OSW World Heavyweight Champion: Boda! He’s a veteran wrestler and a member of the Red Army, and now being World Champion is just icing on the cake! Thanks for the time Chris! Boda: Well, you're welcome. Of course, being The World Champion now makes my time more precious. So you should feel special that I made this time for you. Shanda: Riiight. But anyway, right to business. You’ve been in the OSW for a few months now, what do you think? Boda: What do I think of the OSW..... hmmm..... well, I think that me being in Old School Wrestling has made this a better place. I came to OSW, and reformed The Red Army with Ivan, and together, we've taken over OSW. OSW was a dying federation until The Red Army made its triumphant return a few months ago. And now, look at it, the ratings are through the roof, and it's all because of me joining forces with Ivan Stanislav. Shanda: You sound like you and your ‘comrades’ are the sole reason that OSW is doing so well. Boda: Well, do you see any other group making a stance in OSW? Huh? I mean Power Play is nothing more than a bunch of idiots, and Asylum.... well, we all know that their collective IQ is like 5. Sure they are all wrestlers, and individually at some point has had great careers, but The Red Army has only the cream of the communist crop, and the collective force of The Red Army is the mere reason that OSW is where it is today. Shanda: I would venture to say that President Trashcan Man has had something to do with that, not your commie friends. But I don’t want to argue with you. And speaking of The Red Army, weren’t you beaten to a bloody pulp by “Comrade Supreme Commander Stanislav” in PCW? Boda: Listen, Shanda, I am a man.... don't make me lower myself and smack you around for spreading that Capitalist propaganda. Trashcan Man had done nothing right since becoming President. Wait.... that ain't right.... he did book Nelson vs. Boda and that ultimately led to me being the World Champion. TCM isn't the one putting asses in the seats. I am. Ivan is. The Red Army is selling out the arenas, because no one can get enough of us. We are what is just and right with OSW. And as far as my being beaten in PCW by Ivan Stanislav, that is water under the bridge. Ivan and I will always be friends. He did what he had to do in PCW because that is the way Ivan was raised. He and I had a long discussion before I returned by his side. Ivan is the epitome of communism's past, I am the future of communism. It's all good now. Shanda: The future of Communism? That’s a strong statement. Isn’t that sorta partitioning yourself from the collective? Boda: No. I do what it takes to better the collective. Ivan and I are on the same page, but we are from different generations. I am the one that the young communist look up to. I am hip to today's communists. Shanda: Okay… if you say so. But moving right along, Azala Zameer. Why did you turn on him so coldly? After all the faith he had in you! Boda: Because he was a pawn. Yes we became friends, but I never felt the closure that me and Ivan have. I gave Zameer the perfect opportunity, to join us, to learn the way of Communism, but he refused to accept it. So, at SLAM, he had to accept the steel chair. At first, Shanda, I did refuse to listen to Ivan when it came to matters of Zameer and Boda. But when Azala would not join me and become a communist...... remember he mocked Ivan and pissed him off...... I made a decision to end our friendship. Shanda: That’s horrible. How can you live with yourself? People used to cheer for you. Now they hate you! Boda: So.... I hate people too. Maybe you've forgotten, but I was booed when I had to wrestle Shaquille O'Neal. I was booed when I wrestled Miyagawa. I've been booed for a long time. It's second nature Shanda. It doesn't even grate on me anymore. And the fans, they don't mean squat to me. I don't need them. And besides, if tomorrow I turned over a new leaf, and beat Ivan down, I'd be a hero again. Well, that makes the fans two faced, and I don't need them. Either like me like this, or don't like me at all, I don't care. I still get the commercial deals, I still get the calls from George Lucas, Steven Spielberg. It wouldn't break my heart if another fan never asked me for another autograph. Shanda: I think you’re lying. Boda: And I think you need to loosen up, and be the next recipient of a Bodagasm. But then again.... you might not like outies, you look more like an innie type of girl. Shanda: Don’t flatter yourself. Okay, Commie boy, do you really think you can take over the entire OSW? That’s ludicrous, don’t you think? What stops TCM from just firing your butt? Boda: This World Title. No matter what I do, firing the World Champion because you can't beat him or his comrades. It would not make for a good business decision. We can take over OSW, because there is no one that can stop us all. If I refuse to wrestle, refuse to show up to company business, then TCM may try to fire me, but not because of my idealism. Shanda: So, you’ve got the belt, what do you see in the future for you? Boda: A lot of victories over a lot of losers. I see a record setting title reign, longer than, if I may dare say, the last one that my comrade Ivan currently holds. Shanda: One can only hope you’re wrong… which you probably are. Any words to anyone in particular before I wrap up this horrible interview? Boda: HORRIBLE? You think this is horrible? Well, by the looks of you, the 'HOR' part is right. But moving on, I have nothing in particular to say to anyone, but instead, I have something to say to everyone in OSW. And that is this.... I am your World Champion. Like it or not, you all have to live with it, because not one of you can stop me and strip this title from me. I've got the support of an Army, and that makes me even more dangerous than you all will ever realize.
