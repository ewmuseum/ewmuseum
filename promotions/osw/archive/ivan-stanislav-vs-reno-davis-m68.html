Ivan Stanislav vs. Reno Davis

2002-06-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivan Stanislav vs. Reno Davis Written: June 23, 2002 Nate: The action at Ascension just continues to build folks as we keep moving along through the night! The next match is sure to be a crowd pleaser as Ivan Stanislav and Reno Davis officially fight one another for the one thing they’ve always wanted: Retribution. Steven: Inside of Mount St. Hells End, Ivan Stanislav and Reno Davis will be encased in the concrete floored, steel walled confines of the mammoth structure where two men enter, and only one man will exit! Nate: The seeds of this rivalry….this hatred, can be traced back to our last Pay Per View, Head on Collision, when Ivan Stanislav battled Switchblade for the OSW World Heavyweight Title… ~~Clips of the Ivan Stanislav/Switchblade match play, ending in Ivan Stanislav looming over a battered and beaten Switchblade.~~ Steven: Ivan Stanislav had Switchblade beaten and was prepared to give the crowd what they wanted, a legitimate champion for the belt….but things were about to take a turn… ~~Still of Ivan Stanislav standing in the ring, watching the infamous “secret footage” of his match in which he abandoned Tempest. Reno Davis stands on the entryway with the remote control.~~ Nate: Reno Davis played the video, which prompted Stanislav to be confronted by Tempest, who was his then unlikely girlfriend. Steven: She left him, and he got pinned. The ensuing confrontation between the two was anything but pretty… ~~Clip of Ivan trying to console a teary Tempest in which she finally turns and runs away into a car and drives off, leaving Stanislav alone.~~ Robbie: …and the chick moved on to greener pastures!! Nate: After seemingly vanishing to his Arkhangelsk base, Stanislav returned the next week with a vendetta on his hands and soundly destroyed Amy Campbell and set his sights on Reno Davis. Steven: After first denouncing the fans, of which Stanislav claimed to fight for, and declaring war on Reno Davis and El Drago, the pain within The Russian Bear pushed him over the edge. Stanislav and Davis clashed on a few occasions, but nothing was resolved… ~~Still of Stanislav throwing a brown bag, which held ZaC in it, against the steel cage.~~ Robbie: …until Ivan decided to fight fire with fire, and take out someone that Reno liked! Needless to say, the little spud couldn’t take a dose of his own medicine! Nate: Ivan and Reno fought one on one for a spot to start at the top of the mountain at Ascension. The physically superior Stanislav made short work of Davis and as he was about to pin him….a face from the past emerged… ~~Clip of the Tempest lookalike, played by Rachel Leigh Cook, plays.~~ Steven: A decoy in the form of a Tempest lookalike caught Ivan’s attention long enough to be pinned, throwing salt in his slowly healed, or perhaps quickly infected wound in his heart. The blows had gone from professional to strictly personal. ~~Clip of Stanislav flipping a car over on a fallen Reno Davis, his maniacal laughter in the background.~~ Nate: Which takes us to a week ago, in which Stanislav laid Davis to waste under over a ton of twisted metal. The camera pans to Mount St. Hells End….. Nate: Now, these two men fight for the ultimate prize: Pride. The wounded heart of Ivan Stanislav against the damaged body of Reno Davis. Now, a man who thinks vengeance will heal his heart goes up against a man who’s personal life has been brutally destroyed and it will all take place within the heart of the monstrosity known as Mount St. Hells End! The camera pans around to Mount St. Hells End, where Earl Henrich stands in front. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen!! The following contest is scheduled for one fall and will take place WITHIN Mount St. Hells End!! Introducing first, from Moncton, New Brunswick, Canada! He stands 5’6” tall and weighs in at 166 lbs!! He is The Unwanted Angel: RENO DAVIS!! “Rocket Science” by The Pimps begins to play as Reno Davis emerges from the backstage area. A large metal roll door on one side of the mountain opens, giving Davis entry inside of the steel behemoth. Davis pauses as he looks around and then walks into the mountain. Nate: One of the two men are now within the walls of Mount St. Hells End…. Earl: And his opponent, hailing from Arkhangelsk, Russia. He stands 7’1” tall and weighs in at 400 lbs. He is the Russian Bear of The Red Army: IVAN STANISLAV!! “The Soviet National Anthem” by the Russian Red Army Choir begins to play as Ivan Stanislav, flanked by Loyal Communist Party Member Wang and Alexei Ruslan, head out from backstage and walk towards the mountain. Nate: Even Ivan looks tiny compared to the mountain!! Stanislav pauses before he enters the mountain and turns to Wang and Alexei. The three share a few words before Ivan nods and his two smaller friends leave the area. Stanislav walks into the mountain as the roll door slams shut behind him… Nate: And this match is underway!! The scene shifts to inside of Mount St. Hells End. The dirty steel walls are lined periodically with lights connected by extension cords which cling to the rivets in the steel walls by hooks. Steel beams are piled up in various areas of the large room. Lights flicker and hang from the ceiling, led to about ten feet in the air by long, stringy wires. Nate: This is going to be anything but a hospitable match for both men! Stanislav blinks as his eyes adjust to the lower level of light. The long expanse of concrete floor, spanning roughly forty feet in all directions, leaves many dark areas where Davis could be hiding. Stanislav takes a few heavy footsteps deeper into the room and looks around. Ivan: Where are you little angel!! Stanislav’s heavy voice echoes within the hollow base of the mountain as he looks around for Davis. A shadow flicks across a light from behind Ivan, prompting him to spin and look. Ivan squints and grinds his jaw just as an elbow flies from the left side of the screen and nails Ivan in the side of the head! Ivan growls and shakes off the blow as Reno Davis gets to his feet and rolls backwards, keeping a safe distance from Ivan. Nate: Hit and run is the best move for Reno Davis. Standing toe to toe with Ivan normally is a detrimental move, let alone with concrete floors and steel walls! Ivan roars and swings at Davis, barely missing him. Davis grabs both of Ivan’s legs and pulls hard, trying to trip him, but The Russian Bear keeps his ground! Stanislav reaches down and lifts Davis up over his shoulder in a Canadian Backbreaker. Ivan laughs and runs at a wall, bent to smash Davis’ head against the wall. Davis wriggles free at the last moment as Ivan charges headfirst into the hard steel wall. CLONG!! Stanislav’s skull bangs off the metal interior wall and snaps back. He whirls around, a look of twisted rage on his face. Davis dropkicks Ivan in the chest, knocking Stanislav back into the wall again and hitting the back of his head against the sloped wall. Stanislav growls and shoves Davis away, knocking him back on his rear end. Davis rolls backwards and back to his feet and runs at Ivan again, but The Russian Bear easily catches him in mid air! Robbie: CRUSH HIM!! Ivan turns to the sloped wall and presses Davis against it, literally crushing him between his massive hands and the cold steel. Davis groans as Ivan pushes harder, gritting his teeth and compacting his body, before letting go and dropping Reno over seven feet to the hard concrete below. Reno hits with a hard smack on his chest and spits into the air as Stanislav grabs him by the hair and slowly lifts him. Nate: Stanislav has slowed the momentum down in this match. That won’t bode well for Davis. Stanislav holds Davis up by the neck and pushes him against the wall, sizing him up. He brings his fist back and SLAMS it into Reno’s face. Ivan’s fist eclipses Reno’s head, busting open his forehead, lip, and nose! Robbie: AAHAA!!! Ivan growls and punches Reno again, who weakly struggles against the vice-like grip of The Russian Bear. Ivan brings his fist back for a third time and punches. Reno slides out of his grip at the last moment as Ivan misses the punch and hits the hard steel wall! The wall dents as Stanislav’s fist sinks into the thick steel. Ivan roars in pain! Nate: Folks! We can see the dent from our location! The power behind that punch!! Ivan spins around as Davis runs away and leaps over a stack of beams and disappears from sight. Ivan cracks his knuckles and nears the beams as a bucket flies into view and hits Ivan in the head. Ivan back steps from the blow as the bucket lands with a hard thud…. Steven: There was dried concrete in that bucket!! Davis leaps onto the beams and into the air. He grabs a hanging light and swings, kicking Ivan in the face with both feet. Stanislav staggers backward and catches himself just as Davis rushes forward and knees Ivan in the stomach. Stanislav doubles over more from surprise than real pain, giving Davis enough time to DDT him!!! Nate: He’s going for a DDT on the concrete!! Reno pulls, but Ivan doesn’t move. He tries again, but the weight of Reno isn’t enough to move Ivan. Ivan pulls back and slams a fist into Reno’s stomach, doubling him over. Ivan laughs loudly and clubs him over the back with a forearm, knocking The Unwanted Angel straight to the floor! Ivan reaches into his back pocket and chuckles…. Steven: What’s Ivan got there?! Ivan pulls out a pair of handcuffs and roughly grabs both of Davis’ wrists. He cuffs him behind the back and laughs loudly. Ivan: DYAAHAAHAA!! What is wrong Unwanted Angel? Did you not think Ivan would come prepared!!! DYAAHAAHAA!! Nate: Ivan Stanislav has handcuffed Reno Davis!!! Robbie: No DQ! Ivan can do what he wants!!! Steven: This is sick! Doesn’t Ivan Stanislav have enough stipulations on this match!! Nate: Apparently not! Davis wriggles and squirms as Ivan grabs him by the cuffs and lifts him into the air, dangling him in front of him. Ivan growls and whirls around, whipping Davis through the air! He sails through the darkened confines of Mount St. Hells End and slams into the steel wall and lands unceremoniously on the concrete. He wriggles quickly, trying to free himself. Ivan laughs loudly and grabs the bucket of dried concrete. Steven: I can’t believe this, isn’t there SOME kind of rule? Robbie: Nyet!! Ivan throws the bucket hard and nails Reno in the stomach, spinning him around on the cold, dusty concrete. Ivan looms over him and picks him up again, this time by the waist of the pants. He throws Reno vertically as high as he can…. Robbie: ..which is pretty damn high! Reno flies roughly twenty feet into the air and falls clumsily, hitting a light on his way down and landing on his side with a loud SMACK. Ivan laughs loudly again and rolls him over, putting a foot on Reno’s chest. Referee Betty Lovejoy emerges. Like…1…like…2…. Reno rolls over weakly, breaking the sloppy cover! Nate: Reno Davis should have just kept the shoulder down and save himself a lot of trouble. Ivan reaches down and lifts Reno again. He pushes Reno back a few steps and sizes him up. Ivan rushes forward with a clothesline, but Reno manages to duck low and run away, his arms still behind his back. Ivan skids to a halt and turns around to chase after Reno again. Reno leaps into the air and brings his legs up and his arms under as he manages to switch his arms from behind him, to in front of him in mid air! Nate: A true testament to the agility and dexterity of Reno Davis! Robbie: Yeah, and the malleability too! He’s gonna be bounced like a basketball soon! Ivan runs at Reno as he turns and double axehandles Ivan with his wrists, cutting Stanislav’s angular cheekbone with the corner of the cuffs. Reno jams a shoulder into Ivan’s knee, dropping him to a knee, and clubs Ivan over the top of the head with the handcuffs. He jumps behind Ivan with blinding speed and brings the chain holding his arms together around Ivan’s neck! Steven: He’s choking out Ivan!! GREAT!! Robbie: DQ!! That’s illegal!! Ivan growls as the chain wraps around his neck tighter. He grabs at it, but his fingers are too big to wrap in between his neck and the chain. Reno puts his foot on Ivan’s back and pulls harder as Ivan’s head turns purple! Ivan reaches back, but is unable to grab Reno either. Suddenly, the chain snaps as Ivan shoots forward and smacks his cheek on the concrete. Reno flies backwards off of Ivan onto his back! Nate: Reno broke the chain with Ivan’s huge neck!! Ivan sputters and coughs, some blood welling out of his mouth as he does. Reno leaps to his feet and jumps in the air, clubbing Ivan over the back of the neck while he’s down. Ivan growls and gets to his hands and knees, which gives Reno enough time to drop a knee over the back of his head and send his forehead into the concrete with a sickening thud! Blood splatters on the concrete as Ivan rolls over, a new gash formed on his forehead. Steven: The blood is being spilt now. Neither man’ll give up in this match!! Reno walks over to a corner and produces a shovel that appeared to be caked in mortar. He walks back as Ivan gets to a knee. He pulls back hard and nails Ivan in the back of the head, knocking the head of the shovel off and sending it flying into the darkness as Ivan remains unmoving! Reno stares wide eyed at the broken shovel. Robbie: HA HA! HE NO SOLD A SHOVEL SHOT!! Ivan turns his head slowly, his face twisted in rage as Davis swings the wooden handle. Ivan blocks it with his forearm, smashing it to splinters, and surges forward towards Davis, butting his head up into his chest and knocking him backwards over the pile of beams. Ivan gets up and shakes his head, trying to get the cobwebs out. He reaches up above his head and grabs one of the hanging lights. Stanislav pulls hard, ripping the cord in half, sending sparks flying and creating a bit more darkness in the dank room. Steven: That crazy Ivan..always using weapons. Nate: Crazy Ivan! Ha! That’s a play on words! Robbie: Hey! I was supposed to say that! Ivan wraps the cord around his fist and swings the steel light over his head as Reno stands. Stanislav whips the light at Reno, hitting him in the back of the head and dropping him. The Russian Bear drops the light and walks over the beams, looming over the fallen Reno Davis. He cracks his knuckles, a sadistic grin on his face, and reaches down to grab Davis. Reno rolls over quickly and throws a handful of sand up in Ivan’s eyes! Nate: Possum! He was playing possum! Stanislav howls in pain as the sand stings his eyes and the wound on his head. Reno gets up and easily dodges a wide fist from Stanislav. Ivan swings again, obviously blind from the sand. Reno hops on the pile of beams and bulldogs Ivan into the concrete. Stanislav roars and pushes up to his feet, unfazed by the attack, but stumbles into the steel wall of Mount St. Hells End and wobbles backward! Nate: HA HA! Now THAT was funny!! Robbie: It was not! Reno Davis is cheating!! Stanislav blinks a few times and swings again, missing Reno by about a foot and a half. Reno leaps and punches Ivan in the throat. Stanislav coughs and manages to grab Reno by the shirt! Reno puts a foot on Ivan’s knee, giving him enough time to springboard out of Ivan’s grasp and wrap his legs around Ivan’s head! Reno tries for a hurricanrana, but instead he merely hangs with his legs wrapped around Ivan’s neck!! Robbie: Close your eyes kids, this aint gonna be pretty! Ivan roars and pulls Reno up into a powerbomb position. Reno’s head slaps off the steel wall as he gets into the position, giving Ivan more momentum to toss Reno into the concrete! Reno bounces off of the concrete with a bone jarring thud and does not move! Ivan shakes his head and rubs his eyes with his shirt, untucking it and letting it drape between his suspenders. Steven: I think Reno Davis is dead. He hit that concrete with a tremendous amount of force! Stanislav appears to get ready to pin Reno, but instead steps over him and towards the I-Beams…. Robbie: Ever see a fly get swatted? Nate: Yeah. Robbie: Well consider this swatting a fly..but of Biblical proportions! Ivan looks down at the beam and spreads his legs slightly, squaring himself, and lifts it slowly, his muscles tensing as he does. He picks the huge, rusty beam up and puts it under and arm before turning around slowly. Reno gets up and turns just in time to see Ivan with the massive support beam. Ivan swings slowly as it grows with speed a loud WHOOSH echoes through the area as the beam slams into Reno’s gut! Reno flies backward and skids on the floor before hitting into the far wall!! Robbie: And he’s out of the park!!! Nate: Ivan Stanislav is a monster! He’s not fighting to win…. Robbie: He’s fighting to maim!! Ivan brings the heavy beam up over his head and chucks it towards Reno! It clangs off the ground, not making it as far as expected, and sends concrete flying from the floor. Reno yells as it flips and the end slams over Reno’s shin! Reno howls in pain as the beam may have broken it!! Nate: Ladies and Gentlemen, Ivan Stanislav just threw an I-Beam….probably weighing over 500 lbs, onto Reno Davis’ shin! I doubt he’ll be able to stand, let alone compete!! Ivan slowly walks over to Davis, wiping sand and blood from his eyes. Ivan: Inside the Mountain, no one can hear you scream, Reno Davis. Reno wails in pain again as Ivan drags Reno out from under the beam and sets him up for a piledriver! Ivan pulls Reno up and jumps, driving Reno’s head into the concrete with the force of the 400 pound Russian Bear! Reno’s head bleeds from the crown while Ivan gets up and dusts off his hands. Ivan: You took her from me….now I will right that wrong…. Nate: I don’t think Ivan understands that Reno is not the problem, HE is the problem!! Robbie: That’s okay! This is awesome!! Ivan lifts Reno, who’s leg bleeds badly and sets him up for the Red Scare!! Nate: Red Scare inside of Mount St. Hells End!! Stanislav executes the Red Scare, sending Davis flying through the air into the wall! Davis bounces off and falls heavily onto the concrete. Ivan lumbers over to Davis and puts his boot over his chest. Like…1….like…..2……..like…..3!!! Winner: Ivan Stanislav Nate: Ivan Stanislav has won! He has gotten his revenge against Reno Davis through trickery, treachery, and barbary!! Steven: Is Barbary a word? Nate: No, but it gets the point across!! Robbie: It doesn’t look like Ivan’s done yet!! As if he had heard Robbie, Ivan bends down and lifts a weak, battered, and bleeding Davis up to his level… Ivan: I am not done yet…. Referee Betty Veronica Lovejoy tries to stop Ivan, but he backhands her to the floor!! Nate: He just hit Lovejoy!! Robbie: He’s not gonna hurt anything! The makeup covers up her entire face anyway! Ivan growls and looks into Reno’s glazed over eyes. Ivan: Revenge is a dish…best served COLD DAVIS!! Ivan rushes towards a wall and slams Davis into it, and then spins and slams him into another hard steel wall. He roars and chucks him into yet another wall and rushes over, stomping him hard over the small of his back! Nate: He got the win, what else does he want!? Steven: I think we are seeing all of Ivan Stanislav’s frustrations being unleashed right now. Stanislav grabs Davis and lifts him into the air by the head, only to drop him and nail him with the Iron Curtain!! Nate: IRON CURTAIN!! Davis flies through the air and slams into the roll door that served as the entrance into the massive Mount St. Hells End. Davis weakly struggles to fight against Ivan, but he’s consumed with rage. The Russian Bear grabs Davis and throws him into the roll door three more times, causing it to clang and bend. Finally, Ivan spins him around and Red Scares him into the roll door, sending The Unwanted Angel THROUGH the steel door like a bullet and into the crowds view!! Steven: BOCAAAA!!!! Stanislav tears down the rest of the door as paramedics rush to help Davis. Ivan kicks them away and lifts Davis by the back of the neck, blood covering The Unwanted Angel’s entire body! He parades him around like a fallen trophy to the crowd… Nate: Does this look like a man that just freed his tortured soul? Robbie: No, but it looks great regardless!! Nate: We need to head to a commercial. We will be back right after these messages. Ivan throws Davis’ unmoving body to the ground and spits on it and grinds his heel into his chest before walking off, leaving Davis unconscious…..
