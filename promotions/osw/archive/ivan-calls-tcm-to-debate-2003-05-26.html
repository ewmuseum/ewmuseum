Ivan calls TCM to debate?

26 May 2003

by Ivan Stanislav

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own news post (#56) by Ivan Stanislav, dated 26 May 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
An enraged Ivan Stanislav was quick to talk to reporters after Sunday Slam and made his intentions clear: He wants Trashcan Man in the ring… but in a political debate?!? “Trashcan Man has sat behind desk long enough and hidden from Praporschik Stanislav! It is not time that he show his political mettle and get in ring with Stanislav on next Slam! Trashcan Man thinks he is able to run OSW? Then he must prove it not only to Ivan, but to the world!!” That wasn’t all, however. “Because Ivan cannot trust Trashcan Man, however, he must ask that The Asylum is not present or near the ring for our debate! Though they are liars and cheaters, this opportunity to finally have dialogue with Trashcan Man cannot be ignored! Consequently, Ivan will not have Red Army present, who would normally serve as protection from the miscreants, as an act of trust and goodwill!” Is Ivan Stanislav delusional? Well, that’s another question entirely. But one thing is for sure: Stanislav wants to get at Trashcan Man. What will Trashcan Man say? It’s certainly a trap… but if he denies Stanislav, will he sacrifice boasting power to Ivan? More news as it becomes available.
