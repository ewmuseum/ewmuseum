Shooting Bullets #27

2002-09-08

Posted by Matt Shivan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I know it's been a while since you heard from me. Since June to be exact. Where have I been? Enjoying a little vacation. yes, even us great columnists need some down time every now and then. So what have I been doing? Only going to Westminster and laughing at my Ravens as they've built a solid mound of suck that they're gonna put out there for the upcoming season. But that is for another time. I've returned a little earlier than I was going to for a very special reason. And, in case you couldn't tell, I didn't even put up the whole disclaimer shit. Probably because I think everyone should read this and see what someone who's been around the block has to say. Yeah, I've been around the block. This is my 27th column. I did 23 for PCW. I did three for Wrestling Tattler. Now I'm doing my 27th column exclusively for OSW. Should you be honored? If you want. But should you read? Of course. Reading about a guy ranting on a trio of fuckwads is always quality entertainment. Now, in case you missed it, an entire stable just got up and left without even a hint of notice to the OSW front office. The extent of their notice, from what I understand was a small note after a house show. They didn't contact anyone who NEEDED to know of their departure. They just got up and left. If you don't know who I'm talking about, I'm talking about Rhett Locke, Graphic Violence, and Silence. I'll start with Silence. Silence...you ain't shit. As far as I'm concerned, you never were shit. And to quote someone I talked to earlier today, "I don't know how NC-17 became legends anyway." Fact is, you were barely a spec of dust on the radar. I never noticed your arrival into OSW. I never cared about your arrival into OSW. And I won't care about your departure from OSW. To me, you were just Graphic Violence's partner. And don't look at that as a good thing. To be looked at as someone's partner, and then post some bullshit goodbye in public like you're actually meaningful is so egotistical that it makes me absolutely sick. It makes me want to puke. Why you were even brought in is beyond me. I guess OSW was really desperate for a tag scene. Next up is Rhett Locke. Yeah, Rhett might've just packed up and walked out on OSW *WHILE* champion. But, still, he's not the biggest asshole of the trio. However, at the same time, any person that leaves AS CHAMP is a complete jackass. I don't care if you hate everybody on the staff. I don't care if you're not having fun anymore. But, Jesus fucking Christ, you owe it to the OSW locker room, people who you call "friends", to drop the title with dignity. If you want to leave after you drop the title, then that's your business. But to just go and say that you're leaving while champ is complete and utter bullshit. I wish you fucking hard times for as long as you do this. And if you go somewhere else and have the audacity to display the OSW title there, then that is fucking sick. And that'd show you for the type of fucking person you are. To me, just by walking out you're the next Stewart Hix. Others might not think that, but I do. So do whatever you want. Don't have fun somewhere else. Just be sure to leave the title in OSW where it belongs. You know, for the people that actually deserve to hold it. That brings me to you, Graphic Violence. I save you for last because you, sir, are a complete and total asshole. I don't even need to go on about how large of a jerkoff you are. From what I understand your own talk speaks for itself. I have my sources, and I have my contacts, and from what I heard through the grapevine you’ve said that you felt you didn’t need to give the Front Office a heads up because you weren’t booked on Saturday because you had no angle or storyline. By no angle, do you mean the Rhett Locke swerve at BAMN just a FEW DAYS AGO!?! That sounds like an angle to me. And from that, a whole bunch of storylines could develop. And as for not being booked...BIG FUCKING DEAL!! So you weren't booked. There have been greater travesties in life. Hell, since you weren't booked, maybe you could've taken the free time to try and discover a workrate. Had you done that a month sooner, maybe your feud with Rhett wouldn't have blown as much as it did. And as for the heads up, it's called RESPECT!! Show some fucking respect and at least let someone who needs to know that you want to leave. That way, they can actually plan something out instead of being forced to scramble like you, as well as your little buddies, have forced them to do. But, then again, where you come from I doubt respect was even in the vocabulary. When you've got an enormous ego, you don't know how to show respect. But no, from what I’ve heard it doesn’t stop there, I also heard from a very reliable source that there would have been nothing anyone in the OSW could have said that could have changed that over-inflated mind of yours. For the record pal, nobody in their right mind would've convinced you to stay. If you wanted out, that's your own deal. But, as I already said, the least you could do is show some fucking respect and inform someone that you want to leave. Don't just leave a note saying you're leaving and hope the right people see it. Actually TELL them. That's the least fucking thing you could've done. Furthermore, and this is the best part, I hear that your belief was that slugging Commissioner Porter in the face was a wonderful way to make the exit for your character, and also because you felt slighted without having a title, an angle, or a feud. Slugging the commish in the face? Sounds like a way to build heat with Porter. Not holding a title? No. But you were with Rhett. Had Locke not been a bitch and quit too, you'd have really screwed him over. Or what if the angle isn't done and you win the title. Then what? You'd be an even bigger bitch than you already are. Not in a storyline with any wrestler? Of course not! Locke and Silence quit! And God forbid you actually work on your own! No current future storyline? First off, current is now and future is, well, the future. It's either one or the other. It can't be both at the same time like you wrote it. But as for the stupid thing you said, perhaps you weren't watching the same PPV the rest of us were. YOU AND RHETT LOCKE COMPLETED A "MASTER PLAN" THAT TURNED RHETT HEEL AND JOINED THE TWO OF YOU TOGETHER!! AND YOU ALL HAVE THE BELT!! Sorry, but if that's not a storyline, than I don't know what is. So many fucking things could've come from that it's scary. But no! You weren't booked on Saturday, so you feel there's nothing in the future for you. Boo fucking hoo! If you want to quit, that's your deal. But to post that lame ass post you did was completely bullshit. If you can't see how you screwed some people over, then maybe you should get your fucking head out of your fucking ass, wipe the bullshit from your eyes, and actually look. You, as well as Rhett, screwed EVERYONE over! Shit! Now that I'm done with those three fuckwads, here's fair warning to everyone. If you decide to involve yourself around the title, or decide to do something totally drastic at a PPV or whatever, then QUIT a few days later, you are a complete and utter asshole. And anyone I deem an asshole will receive the same harsh treatment our little trio of jerkoffs just got. Sure, they might just be words, but words are more powerful than you think...especially when directed at the right people. *sigh* I'm getting too old for this. I'll probably take another short break. And for good cause. Just from listening to those three, total ignorance might be spreading and I sure as hell don't want to catch it. I'm out. Truthfully Yours, Shooter
