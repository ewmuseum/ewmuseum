Crazy Cunuck #6

2002-08-28

Posted by Matt Shivan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hellllooooooooo OSW! Da Canuck is back with just in time for *name withheld due to Court order* predictions! The card has its ups and its downs, but overall I think it will turn out to be an okay event. This is one of the first times in awhile when the mid-card matches have much more of an appeal than the upper-card, but enough aboot that. You came hear to see predictions, eh? Oh yeah, just so I don’t have to waste time, these matches all have No DQ. MET Final Task Redemption Song vs. Total Violation vs. All About the Benjamins vs. American Ronin Now this bugs me. Ronin owns the whole competition through hard work, dedication and just pure talent, but it all means nothing because whoever wins this gets enough points to win period. All the other contests mean nothing because of this event. Oh well, can’t ask for anything. Besides, the Benjamins can’t rely purely on the attractiveness of their gimmicks so American Ronin plows through the competition and beats out the Jobbers, Comic Relief Jobbers and Lower Card team. WINNER: American Ronin Handicap Street Fight Dave Gibson, Jim Axtell and Clay Vendetta vs. The Cult Well, set your VCRs. I think we get to witness THREE awesome things in this match. The first? Clay Vendetta wrestling. The guy rules, get used to it. The second? The not-so-sad farewell of SDL and Vampir and finally, and I’m really praying for this, the return of Leadership to the Cult. Hessian should be back, even Bigger & Badder and dispose of his two weaker leaders, SDL and Vampir, so he can reign over the Cult. But, barring Hessian’s decision whether or not to rip into the Throwbacks, the Old Schoolers take this one home. WINNER: Gibson, Axtell and Vendetta Climbing to Heaven Match Propaganda vs. Nick Casey Please Propaganda… Please?? This feud has just confused me for the most part. The title changed hands from Proppa-G to Casey, now it’s vacated and now these two are fighting for it at BAMN? Huh? Well, everyone knows Crack Kills, so there’s no way this druggy is going to take the big boy from the South. Propaganda in a squash. Now would you please throw Frankie into a program with this title? WINNER: Propaganda OSW Cruiserweight Championship Match Reno Davis vs. Corg Whoa! Reno and Corg are fighting each other? How?! Corg can barely walk and won’t after Mammoth gets through with him on SLAM! and Davis’ monkey is dying or something. If you ask me, Reno shouldn’t be in this match. I half-expected Aimz to take his place, making this an anticipated return match from Ascension, but what can you do? Anyways, this should be a good match none-the-less. Hopefully it can tell it’s own story. As for the winner? Well, let’s flip a penny. Heads, Corg. WINNER: Corg OSW Tag Team Championship Triple Threat Civil Disobedience vs. NC-17 vs. Mammoth and PHD All three teams have a legitimate chance to pull in the titles. Needless to say they all deserve it. Civil D is on one helluva roll lately and is definitely at the top of their game. Hawke and Masters are the most skilled of the three teams, but not necessarily the best. PHD and Mammoth have the numbers on their side, plus Mammoth is a tank. He alone can carry PHD to the titles, but I don’t think that’s tonight. NC-17 are legends. GV is ferocious and has a killer instinct. Although I’m not to high on Silence, both men in my opinion have the best chance of winning. So I’m going to say NC-17 in the match of the night. WINNER: NC-17 TLC Contract Match Kid Wonder vs. Scott Taylor Another one that could be the match of the night in my opinion. Wonder and Taylor have really opened a few cans on each other in the last couple weeks, bringing this feud to the boiling point. Taylor has been on one helluva rise lately and has apparently rubbed some big people the right way. But, I don’t see Kid Wonder losing Amanda, even if it stops Scotty’s push. Scott loses, but gains some heat back after assaulting Wonder post-match. WINNER: K-Dub Dog-Collar Match Jesus St. Crux vs. Frankie Bones This slightly picked up on SLAM! I like the way they threw Maurice Fountaine into the mix there, it’s something new. Actually, this feud is a lot better than I thought it would be. But the Master of Pain is going to bring a new meaning of PAIN to Jesus and probably destroy him. Look for Fountaine to get involved somehow though. WINNER: Frankie Bones Singles Match Tempest vs. Alan Kriegman Hmmm… this match doesn’t really excite me. Tempest’s cute antics are a little out of place when she’s feuding with the Think Tank. You’d think that she wouldn’t be fooling around with novels and tinkering with iCybies of the people who murdered Ed? Oh well. Since the Think Tank is going down in every other match, I expect them to at least win this one. Plus with the No DQ it makes perfect sense for TCM to show up and give Kriegman the win. WINNER: Alan Kriegman Glass Table Match Armour vs. Toomes, Special Referee Howard Porter Toomes kills Armour in relative ease. Porter tries to cost him the match, but Toomes gives the fans what they want to see and opens a can on Porter. Hopefully this one between Seabass and Porter continues to a meeting between the two at the next PPV. We all want to see Porter back in the ring full-time so give in already and book the match. WINNER: Toomes Single Match El Drago vs. Ivan Stanislav Well, nothing bad can come out of the two of them fighting again. The matches before this were awesome and this will be awesome. What genius by Porter to pull something like this to take out his enemies! Anyways, even though it’s a good and bad booking at the same time, at least we get to see them duke it out again. I say that Drago picks up the win because he’s been really impressive as of late. However, I think he should lose Rucha and do English on his own now… Oh well, Drago beats in a fair fight. WINNER: El Drago OSW World Championship, Barbwire Rope Match Graphic Violence vs. Rhett Locke I think Kid Wonder said it best, "Has this match lost heat or what?" Well Jimmy, I agree with you. I haven’t seen such a good feud in it’s first half go to the shits this bad since… well, since ever! I think we all know the exact spot where the feud went to the dogs. When Rhett Locke started crying in the ring. How can you take this guy serious now? It makes OSW look weak. But now, he HAS to win against GV. Violence is good, but if I was incharge of booking, I wouldn’t want him holding my most prestigious title YET. I can guarantee you he’ll hold it somewhere down the road, but right now I’d rather have someone else beat our crybaby "Alternative Warrior". Hopefully he finds some way to beat the meanest sumbitch in OSW. WINNER: Rhett Locke As for the PPV overall, it’s alright. But can you really expect it to live up to Ascension!? Well, most of the lower matches look good but some of the uppercard haven’t got much luster. I’ve heard some rumours about possible feuds going in to next month and they look real interesting, so expect a rant-filled, cynical column next month! Until then drink your Molson, eat your poutine and DON’T BLAME CANADA!
