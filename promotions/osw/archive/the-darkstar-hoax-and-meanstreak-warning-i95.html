The DarkStar Hoax and Meanstreak Warning

2002-02-23
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The DarkStar Hoax and Meanstreak Warning Written: 23/02/2002 Nate: Well ladies and gentlemen tonight on OSW Saturday Night Slam! We have one helluva main event for you, as you know the Monster they call Meanstreak returned to our television screens but one week ago, and it is tonight he was called upon for a bout of revenge by the man he attacked, and who was supposed to be Ivan Stanislav’s mystery opponent last week, Hessian. Robbie: That’s great, a retard and a freak in the same ring! Ratings AHOY!!! Steven: Oh be nice! Erm…jut out of curiosity, which one’s the freak and which one’s the…retard? Robbie: Does it really matter? Nate: Uh…yeah I’ll see you in hell Robbie, anyway folks next up we have… BOOM!!! Pyros go off on the stage, first across it, then around it, then up the Jumbotron, and a final huge explosion around the Jumbotron. No music plays, however the lights in the arena black out and a single spotlight shines down in front of the entrance…awaiting someone. Robbie: Uh…who…who is this? Nate: I don’t know I don’t have anything on my schedule…unless… Steven: OH MY GOD IT CAN’T BE!!! LOOK ON THE STAGE!!! Sure enough, on the stage, stands a man, fully cloaked except for his spiky blue hair and a pale white face. The man throws open his cloak, and the crowd go into an uproar as the familiar attire shows through the cloak. Nate: OH MY GOD IT CAN’T BE!!! Robbie: THAT TRAITOR!!! Steven: HE’S SUPPOSED TO BE DEAD DAMMIT!!! On the stage, looking across the crowd, with a demonic twinkle in his eye is none other than the man himself… Nate: DARKSTAR!!! DARKSTAR HAS JUMPED SHIP TO OSW!!! OH MY GOD I CAN’T BELIEVE IT!!! DarkStar struts down to the ring, accompanied by strobe lighting, and an arena full of confused fans. DarkStar scales the ringside steps and enters the ring… Nate: Oh my god Jane Reagan pulled a fast one here… Steven: Tyler’s gonna be piiiissed by this! DarkStar looks the crowd of shocked fans over…and then begins wobbling. He falls over, landing on his side on the mat, but howls out as his ribs appear to wriggle and move of their own accord. Nate: Oh no don’t tell me Clyde is here too! Steven: No silly look at his pants! DarkStar suddenly halves in two! Each half begins moving around, until finally all is revealed. The face of DarkStar, a mask, is pulled off and out pops the head of Caleb, whilst out from inside the pants appears Minion the Imp. The crowd boo hysterically as the two managers’ of Heresian Bloodlust stumble around and look at the fans. A quick glance to each other and they begin laughing insanely at all the duped people of Baltimore. Robbie: Well I’ll be a Monkey’s lover…those little sh!ts……… Steven: Minion and Caleb! What are those little funny midgets doing out here? Nate: Only time will tell……… Minion waddles over to the side of the ring and prompts for a microphone from the timekeeper of all people. The crowd begin booing louder as Minion walks to the center of the ring and puts the microphone to his lips. Minion the Imp: Ladies and Gentlemen I’ll make this short…Impin’ Ain’t Easy… Nate: Hey he’s right on both counts there! Steven: Quiet! That squeaky voice is SOOO funny! Robbie: I’ll bet those rubber pants are SOOO funny too…eh? Steven: Oh no, Mike Lasandra let me borrow these for tonight…it’s a bit chilly here at the ol’ announce table tonight. Minion the Imp: Tonight every one of you will watch and cringe as Azrael Cain dominates the 12-man battle royal! Ivan Stanislav himself will not prevent the Dark Angel from achieving his goal…but there’s another goal that is to be achieved tonight…and it involves a Monster…and a Goliath. Robbie: No prizes for guessing who the f*ck they are then, huh? Nate: Family show, family show! Robbie: BAH! Children these days are little punk bastards; they don’t have any good role models any more! Steven: You know I did a bit of……… Nate: Keep it to yourself Steven… Minion the Imp begins waddling around the ring, as Caleb sits on the second turnbuckle watching. Minion the Imp: The Monster they call Meanstreak will be taken to Hell and Back ladies and gentlemen, and why am I so confident? The Monster assaulted Hessian FOR NO DAMNED REASON!!! And that in itself is reason enough for Hessian to tear the Monster apart…when Hessian enters this ring tonight, Meanstreak should remember to pay attention, because someone who can master 55 devastating moves and have the balls to demand a match with the most formidable man in Primetime history has to be a man of both brains and brawn…so yes…we will see Meanstreak versus Hessian…Satan vs. Monster…Goliath vs. Crazy wench…but what we will have to WAIT and see is the vanquishing of that very Monster, and then we can all boogie through the night… Nate: Little sucka sure does know how to talk… Robbie: Oh! So THAT’S what those sounds are coming out of his mouth? OF COURSE HE CAN TALK HE’S ALSO TALKING UTTER CRAP!!! Minion the Imp: It doesn’t matter if you can lift 700lbs soda can machines, it doesn’t matter if you can roar like a Howler Monkey, all that matters is that you can hang with the biggest and baddest there is to hang with…and now people……… A slight pause and a field of silence fill the arena, as Minion steps to the side of the ring, and watch the middle of the ring. Nate: What the hell is going on here??? Robbie: I don’t know they’re just staring at the middle of the ring… And then the lights in the arena go out again. The crowd begin booing, but the boos turn to eerie silence as in the middle of the ring, a small orange light begins flickering in the middle of the ring. The crowd look intrigued as slowly the light begins to expand until it is 4ft in diameter. The light under the mat begins to pulsate and “throb”, until finally with an immense BOOM! KICK! -WHAM! -KABOOM! The center of the ring explodes, and huge, lapping flames rise up out of the middle of the ring. Nate: OH MY GOD! THE RING IS ON FIRE!!! Steven: Looks like a job for Scooby Doo! Robbie: There’s a flaming great hole in the ring dammit where’s the fire brigade? Nate: In their firehouse reading dirty magazines…why? Robbie goes to smack Nate, as the flames get more intense, and barely light up the front row in the darkness of the arena. Still the flames climb up as Minion grins from jug ear to jug ear. Then from below the ring, inside the huge ring of fire, a black shape rises up. Slowly, and seemingly motionlessly, the figure rises up out of the flames, as they begin to slightly lower. Nate: God only knows how much this cost Lasandra… Robbie: Why does his name keep popping up? Steven you didn’t…? Steven: Oh no I like the suits but the boy is weird… The flames stop at several feet, as the figure rises fully out of the ring. Where the wide gaping hole in the ring used to be, there is now a new piece of mat, and standing on it is a huge black figure. The flames die down, and leave the arena in full darkness again. Steven: I can’t see a damned thing here! Nate: You should’ve eaten your carrots as a child! Steven: Meh…I was more of a cauliflower person… Robbie: I wonder why. Total darkness in the ring…nothing can be seen. The suddenly two little glowing embers of light appear at the height the flames first dropped to. They begin flickering very slowly, and then open wider. A single spotlight appears in the ring, and the crowd burst out in hysterical booing as the massive body of Hessian stands there, in total silence. His silver gauntlets and steel skull glisten in the light, but the rest of his black attire remains its usual black self. Nate: HESSIAN!!! Steven: Hessian in an OSW ring…again…who’d have thought it!!! Robbie: I don’t know but after that little fire stunt I’m looking forward to the main event… Nate: As you should be… The green eyes blink, the silver shines, and Minion walks over to Hessian and hands him the microphone. As Hessian raises it to his masked mouth, the sound of heavy breathing can be heard throughout the arena. Hessian: From the depths of Hell I have arisen, for tonight sees the destruction of the Monster they call Meanstreak…to me he is my prey, my goal, my target. I don’t need to lift soda machines, or be thrown from the Kremlin, or win a World Title 3 times to impress the fans…but defeating someone who has will be most exciting. If I can destroy the Monster himself, everyone will realise just who I am, what I am, and that………is Ethan Giroux’s Final Nightmare… Minion takes the microphone from Hessian and stands to the side of the ring, as again the same little light appears in the middle of the ring directly under Hessian’s feet. It expands and brightens, and eventually bursts through the ring, engulfing Hessian in a ring of fire. Everyone boos loudly as he slowly drops down into the depths below the ring, and all that is left is a flaming circle in the ring. Nate: Well folks with those few words Hessian is obviously ready for possibly the biggest fight of his life… Robbie: Meanstreak is still gonna crush him though… Steven: Well lads I do believe the night must continue, so would a commercial break be in order? Nate: Yeah, why not, here’s a word from our sponsors.
