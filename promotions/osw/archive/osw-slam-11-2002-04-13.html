As the camera’s fade up we are inside what appears to be a transformed locker room. The benches that normally line the locker room have been torn from the floor and set in three rows alongside one another, facing a white brick wall. Upon the wall is the large, red flag of the Soviet Union and in front of it a table with various documents spread along the surface of the table. The entire Red Army, along with Tempest, are present in the room. Ivan Stanislav stands behind the table, facing the benches with his hands behind his back and a military cap upon his head. Alexei Ruslan stands beside him carrying a small briefcase. On the center bench sits Loyal Communist Party Member Wang, flanked by both Round Faced Women, all three of which have napkins tied around their necks. Speedy Riggs sits on the bench to the far left with Michel Krieg, while Tom Walczak sits to the far right. Behind them all stands Tempest, who carries a flagpole with the Soviet Flag atop it. She stands at mock attention, as if truly in the army, and salutes with her right hand while grinning. Ivan: Comrades, I call this meeting to order! Alexei takes a step back, giving Ivan room to walk back and forth behind the table, which he does in a very slow, methodical manner. Ivan: Tonight, my Comrades, we have several goals set forth in front of us, from which we will succeed in them ALL! For too long have we had to listen to the babble of Jane Reagan and her cheating ways! For too long have we had to stomach the injustices put forth by the administration of OSW! For too long have we had to watch this fine sport known as wrestling be degraded into nothing more than X Rated television show!! Ivan stopped behind the center of the table…. Ivan: Tonight, we take BACK the sport from which we have worked so hard to create! Tonight, we destroy Jane Reagan and her cohorts, we silence Heresian Bloodlust, we demolish Reno Davis!! And in the aftermath, The Red Army will stand tall and honorable over the corpses of our fallen foes! The Red Army as one cheers as Ivan nods angrily. Tempest hops up and down behind the group and claps. Ivan: First order of business!! Comrade Walczak!!! The Polish Punisher stands from his bench and turns to the crowd. Tom: Tonight, Hessian from Heresian Bloodlust will go up against The Red Army! As we have shown them last week, The Red Army WILL acquire tag titles at any cost, including the complete destruction of one half of the champions themselves! Tonight, The Hessian will be reduced to nothing more than a conquest to be pinned up against the wall of The Red Army, for tonight, we will bury him for sure!! Again, the group cheers loudly, with occasional claps from Ruslan and Riggs. Ivan: Meanwhile, my friends, we have been plagued with what appears to be a monkey of some sort. And while you may believe I am talking about a certain ape named ZaC, you are wrong, for I am speaking about Reno Davis, the man that thinks we, “Need to be taught a lesson.” Ivan laughs loudly, as does the rest of the group, except for Tempest who scratches her head uncertain of what they're talking about. Ivan: Comrade Krieg, representing what should be East Germany, will silence him tonight, will you not, Krieg? Krieg stands quietly and looks to the group… Michel: Since I have come to The Red Army, all have been humbled in our wake. All have respected what it is we have set out to do in fear of our might. Reno Davis, a young, tiny little man, believes he can come into OUR federation and tell us how to act? I think not. WE think not! Tonight, The German War Machine is going to make sure that Reno Davis and his little friend ZaC, are permanently put on Endangered Species list…. Wang stands up suddenly just as Krieg sits down, as Tempest starts to fidget. Wang: And as for wittle monkee, we can assuwe you that he will not be pwobwem! We alweady have napkins for monkee’s bwains, and we will be eating them up soon!! HO HO HAA!! Ivan: Da, that is correct. During the night, I want that monkey found and eradicated. Eat whatever you wish of him, but be sure to exterminate him. We will show Davis what happens when you tamper with The Red Army! Tempest smiles and look around restlessly as Ruslan steps forward and clasps his hands together. Alexei: Finally, our great leader, Praporschik Ivan Stanislav, must battle alongside three men in which he is unsure of, against the forces of bourgeois evil: Jane Reagan and her minions! Sebastian Toomes, the man that has permanently tainted OSW World Title, is about to know what true fear is…is that correct, Comrade? Stanislav growls and steps forward again. Ivan: Jane Reagan has shown us what she truly is, and where her loyalties lie! She has shown the World what type of a person she is and who can be trusted! And now, finally, the people have turned to us, the fighters of the people, to protect them from her swift and unjust hand! Myself, Tempest, and everyone else within this room have been victimized by her callous ways, and it is time to destroy them in one fell swoop! The group stands as one as Ivan tilts his chin up higher. Ivan: Tonight, my friends, the revolution begins! Tonight, we turn the tide and rally our strength and beat back the oppressors! Tonight, a curtain of The Red Army will cover the OSW, red with the blood of it’s enemies!! Starting with Krieg, the row of communists march out of the room. Tempest marches over to Ivan and takes his arm as herself, Stanislav, and Ruslan follow the group out….as the scene fades to black. Papers are scattered on the desktop, and Jane is working away at her computer.
