Destruction...Announcement...Recruitment...Repeat... Written: 20-07-02 Suddenly, the stage flashes and explodes in a cloud of black pyros, and Kid Wonder’s glee turns to horror as four silhouettes make their way through the mist and down the ramp. Vampir Nosferatu, Hessian, the Cultist and Minion the Imp make their way down the ramp as Kid Wonder hightails it out of the ring and back through the crowds to safety. Nate: THE CULT!!! The Cult are out here now!!! Who next? Robbie: Big Bessie’s trainer? The Cult stops at the bottom of the ramp, and with a nod to one another, suddenly begin laying into the members of NOW! Vampir systematically chokeslams four women at once, following up by throwing them all into the steel stairs, while Hessian begins taking on several of the NOW members, individually destroying them with Eternal Damnation while sustaining bitchslaps to various limbs. The Cultist on the other hand begins spearing down NOW members and pummelling them, while Minion the Imp finds himself face-to-face with none other than Big Bessie. The 3’11” Imp and the 6’4” 700lbs monster stare down one another, before Minion’s eyes widen and he recoils in horror. Minion the Imp: MAN FUCK THAT!!! I may be a lot of things, but I damn sure ain’t no entrée for White Trash Whale!!! Minion turns from Bessie and finds one of the more pretty NOW members at his feet. Reaching down and groping her chest and groin, Minion coughs as Hessian kicks ass in front of him. Minion the Imp: Uhh…mmm…YEAH! TAKE THAT YOU BITCH!!! HAHAHA!!! FEAR THE IMP!!! Oh god you feel good…AHEM! FEEL MY WRATH!!! Oh god feel it…HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!!!!! The Cult stands among a field of broken female bodies, to an ovation from the crowd, an ovation of pure hatred for the group as they stand tall above the members of NOW. As Hessian, Vampir, Minion and The Cultist stand before the fallen, a loud grunting echoes from behind them, and they turn to find Big Bessie standing, her bowl cut messed up and her dungarees ruffled. Bessie snorts, and growls, as The Cult stand before her. The great beast of femininity charges The Cult, only to be kicked to the mid-section and pulled into the air by Vampir and Hessian. Holding Bessie high above their heads, The Cult uses all their strength as Bessie’s folds sink to the ground. The Cultist climbs onto the apron and jumps back off, spiking Bessie as The Cult drop to the mats, and a terrifying CRUNCH! echoes throughout the arena as Bessie’s neck breaks, much to the horror of the crowds. Nate: OH MY LORD IN HEAVEN!!!!!!!!! The Cult just executed their finisher: DEADHEADED! and it’s broken the neck of that innocent woman! Robbie: She ain’t innocent!!! Think of all the poor children in Africa then think of how many she’s probably eaten! Corg is out of it on the mats. Hessian wrap an arm around the Anti-God’s body and carries him into the ring, draping Corg’s limp body over that of the recently “So Wonderfuled” body of Scott Taylor. Vamp, meanwhile, brings Pierre Gaston back to his senses, slapping his little French face until the referee opens his eyes and gasps in horror at the pea-headed skin-and-bones skull that makes up Vampir’s head. Vampir points to the ring, and like a little possessed man, Pierre slides in and sees Corg covering Taylor. The crowd is on their feet booing hysterically as Pierre begins the 3-count. 1… 2… … … … … … … … 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Winner: Corg Time: 21:11 Nate: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN THE CULT HAS JUST SAVED COR HIS CRUISERWEIGHT TITLE, PLUS BROKEN THE NECK AND DECIMATED THE MEMBERS OF NOW AND ALL THE WHILE THE CROWD HERE AREGOING FUCKING BALLISTIC!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Robbie: WHOO HOO!!! THE CULT! THE CULT! THE CULT!!! GET JIGGY HYA!!! Steven: Poor NOW members…who’s going to pay for the surgery? Robbie: WHO CARES!!! THE CULT IS HERE AND GODDAMN THEY ARE EVIL!!! The Cult however group in the ring, rather than leaving up the ramp. Standing amongst the broken bodies outside the ring and the beaten bodies of Corg and Taylor, Hessian, Vampir Nosferatu, Minion and the Cultist stare across the booing crowd, while Earl Heinrich does the smart thing and tosses a microphone into the ring. Minion bends over to pick it up, then hands it to Hessian as EMTs emerge from the back, cluttering around the NOW members and checking on their physical state. Nate: Well The Cult have some business at hand it seems… Robbie: Ya don’t say… Steven: Minion looks so groovy since his plastic surgery. Robbie: He had what now? In any case The Cult are about to speak, t’would be wise to listen. Hessian points amongst the bodies surrounding the ring, and puts the mic to his healing face, the scars from Ascension still ripe in his flesh. Hessian: Before you lies our meaning. These bodies you see before you, broken necks, cracked ribs, battered bodies all stand for what we are…you see, as we stand before you, The Cult has yet to be perceived as somewhat of a threat. Sure, Vampir and I can dismantle and destroy a pairing of rejects from the old testament of wrestling, sure, we can abuse innocent women and break their necks, sure we can attack people from behind but where is the real competition? Steven: I wonder where he’s going with this… Robbie: Probably something devious no doubt… Hessian: Albeit Heresian Bloodlust was the pinnacle of the tag team division in the past, but this is a new time, and in this new time, there is going to be a new threat…therefore we come before you hordes of steaming rat’s piss of a people to declare that The Cult, Vampir Nosferatu and I WILL be competing in the aforementioned Tag Team Tournament and Vampir Nosferatu and I WILL destroy everyone and everything in it…and Vampir Nosferatu and I WILL win……… Nate: THE CULT HAS THROWN ITS NAME IN THE HAT FOR THE TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT!!! Hessian looks over the crowds as they hiss and jeer at The Cult. Handing the microphone to Vampir Nosferatu, Hessian watches as the EMTs carry out the members of NOW, and Corg and Scott Taylor stir at their feet. Vampir: The Throwbacks have brought unto themselves a plague much worse than one can contemplate…The Black Plague, The 10 Plagues, all were feeble compared to the plague that stands before you now…if you feared nothing before, you’re going to die of fright now…prepare for the worst, for The Cult is the coming curse, and we shan’t stop until our names have been driven into your minds, along with the insurmountable pain we will drive into your souls. Before you is the new disease, before you is the new threat………before you is The Cult! Vampir hands the microphone to Minion, and suddenly grabs Scott Taylor, tossing him out of the ring and into the last few remaining members of NOW. In the ring now are The Cult, along with Corg, managing to sit up, holding his head in pain. At ringside, Shyme begins to stir, and too sits up, a small cut opened on her forehead. In the ring, The Cult stand aside as Corg shakily makes his way to his feet using the ropes for leverage, and stands before The Cult, staring back at him. Nate: Well what’s going on here then? The Cult and Corg…the Cultist is here…therefore Corg cannot be The Cultist… Robbie: Excellent deduction dear Nate… The Cult closes in on Corg as Minion hands the microphone back to Vampir. Hessian takes Corg by the arm and presents him before The Soul Stalker, as Corg stares into the grotesque life form before him. Vampir: Corg…think back to your early days. Think back to the time when you flew high amongst the others, as a Rising Star. Think back to the days of NTICW. Remember them Corg? Corg pulls his arm away from Hessian’s grasp and stares down the 7’2” behemoth, then nods at Vampir with some kind of a grin on his face, as Shyme enters the ring carrying his Cruiserweight Title. Vampir: Remember the Dark Prophecy, Corg? Remember the faction of evil which reigned over the NTICW before its own demise? Remember the union of Hessian, the Zombie and Corg? Remember? Corg again nods, taking his Cruiserweight Title from Shyme as she stands by his side. Vampir: Well think again Corg…you see, moments ago The Cult, out of our own good will, decided upon evening the score, and helping you to retain that gold around your waist…now what we ask in return is……… Nate: OH MY GOD!!! Robbie: HE ISN’T!!!! Steven: Erm…I bet $5 he is… Vampir: Corg…join the ranks of The Cult…bring your evil to us and together we will rain down as acid upon the OSW…join us as one and unleash your evil upon them all…join us as The Cult… Corg cocks his head as Vampir retracts the microphone and awaits Corg’s reply. The crowd are rocking the arena with an ovation of boos and hisses, as the darkness envelops the ring. Corg looks at Hessian, then the Cultist, then Minion and Vampir…and simply turns, leaving the ring with Shyme and The Cult with no reply. Corg and Shyme take up off the ramp, leaving everyone to wonder, as The Cult talk amongst themselves, and slowly make their way back up the ramp, cued out by “The Coming Curse”. Nate: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN THE CROWD HERE AT THE IGLOO ARE GOING NUTS AS THE CULT HAS JUST INIVITED CORG INTO THEIR RANKS, YET THEY LEAVE WITHOUT A REPLY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Robbie: THE CULT COULD HAVE CORG AS ONE OF THEIR OWN!!! WE COULD FIND OUT VERY SOON IN THE COMING WEEKS WHETHER OR NOT THEIR RECRUITMENT IS SUCCESSFUL!!!!!!!!! Steven: UNTIL THEN LADIES AND GENTS, LET’S TAKE YOU TO A COMMERCIAL!!!!!!!!!
