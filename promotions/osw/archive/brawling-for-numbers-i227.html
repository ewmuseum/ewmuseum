Brawling For Numbers!

2002-07-20
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brawling For Numbers! Written: 20-07-02 Slam fades in from commercial to the backstage area of the Igloo. Matt Shivan poured an extra spoonful of sauce onto his spaghetti. He sat down at the table and rubbed his hands together contentedly as the cameraman watched. Shivan: Want some? [Camera shakes back and forth] Shivan: I figured we’d stop and grab a bite since it’s been sort of slow lately. You’re not filming this, right? I hate when people watch me eat. [Camera shakes back and forth again] Shivan: Good. [Gets a gigantic forkful of spaghetti and puts it in his mouth.] Robbie: C’mon, are we that desperate to fill the show that we have to watch Matt eat? Shivan freezes in mid-chew, pointing with a shaking hand at something over the cameraman’s shoulder. [Camera pans left, to reveal the Cult—Hessian, Vampir, Minion and the masked Cultist—standing near the craft services buffet line.] Shivan: [mouth full] Oh fuck. [Opens mouth in fear and spaghetti drops out onto table.] Robbie: DUDE! SICK! Cameraman: Oh crap. [Pans right to see the Throwbacks—Gibson, Vendetta and Axtell—standing at the other end of the catering room, wielding their baseball bats.] Cameraman: Wow! This is great! Isn’t it, Matt? Matt? [Camera pans back left to see Matt crawling under the table.] Nate: Courageous as always... [Cameraman backs up, zooming out until he gets both factions in the same shot.] Vendetta: [points with the bat] All right, you bastards, it’s time to pay. No one touches Niki and gets away with it. Vampir: [chuckles] Weapons matter not children, you'll always be the little pathetic rat to our bloodthirsty rattlesnake, and its time for din dins. Gibson: We’ll see if you’re laughing once I ram this bat down your throat. Hessian: You came looking for us? Well you've done one thing for us, the only thing left for us to do now.....is finish you off! Minion: FUCK THIS! I'm outta here...where'd that fat Bessie chick go? [glances to catering spread] Ooh, cheese-filled crab puffs... Steven: Mmmm, crab puffs. With that, the Throwbacks raise the bats and charge. The Cult advances more slowly, as Minion waddles to the buffet table. Robbie: HERE WE GO! Gibson goes at Hessian, Vendetta at Vampir and Axtell at the Cultist. Steven: This is just like West Side Story! Gibson takes a Sosa-esque swing at Hessian, who ducks it and tackles Gibson. Hessian lifts Gibson off the ground and throws him through a table. Hessian stays on top of Gibson, pounding away with his massive hands. Vampir grabs CCV by the throat, only to have the hold broken by a kick below the belt. Even that staggers the monstrous individual, as CCV backs up and... CRUNCH! Nate: OH MY! Vendetta just drilled Vampir in the side of the head with the bat! The Cultist, meanwhile, has revealed the black baseball bat he used against Gibson. He and Axtell are basically sparring with the weapons like they would with swords. Axtell: Fuck this! Axtell drops his bat, and hoists the Cultist over one shoulder. He turns and charges the wall, driving the Cultist into it. Meanwhile, CCV has run over to Hessian, who has pressed Gibson over his head. Snarling, Hessian charges and throws Gibson at the catering table! Minion: ACK! Minion grabs the tray of cheese-filled crab puffs and scoots out of the way a second before Gibson crashes through the table, sending food and drink flying everywhere. Steven: Mmmm, crab puffs. Axtell has grabbed a chair, and whacks the Cultist with it in the back, before rapping him in the head. Axtell places the chair over the fallen Cultist’s head, and raises the bat when Hessian assaults Axtell from behind. Grabbing Axtell by the throat, Hessian effortlessly lifts him off the ground and throws Axtell against the unyielding wall. Axtell hits it hard and slumps to the floor. Nate: GOOD LORD! Robbie: Hessian’s gone nuts! Meanwhile, Vendetta and Vampir are pounding on one another. Hessian helps the Cultist up and they advance toward CCV as well. In the background, someone is watching from the shadows. Seeing the Cult approach, CCV backs up until he is against the far wall, grabbing Gibson’s forgotten bat to get two weapons. Hessian stalks straight for CCV, grabbing a table and flinging it out of the way. The action reveals Shivan cowering on all fours. Shivan: AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! Robbie: Check for a urine stain! Shivan crawls away, sobbing in terror as Hessian and the Cultist head on, undistracted. Nate: This does not look good for CCV! The Cult closes in around CCV, as the figure in the shadows moves closer. Robbie: Who the hell is that? Hessian: We will give you a choice, Vendetta. Lay down your weapons, and we will allow you to join us. Pledge to serve The Cult, and you shall survive. If not, we must—urk! CCV answers by ramming the end of one bat into Hessian’s groin! The move leaves him open to Vampir, who delivers a clubbing forearm blow to the back of Vendetta’s head. CCV falls, and Vampir and the Cultist start to stomp the Throwback. CCV tries to fend them off with the one bat he still has, but Vampir bats it away, sending it clattering across the room... where it lands at the feet of the figure watching from the shadows. The man steps out... to reveal... Nate: OH MY GAWD!!! THAT’S ‘THE ORIGINAL’ JEFF GARVIN FROM 21W!!!! WHAT THE HELL IS HE DOING HERE?!?!? Garvin picks up the bat lying at his feet and takes in the scene. Dave Gibson, only now crawling out of the remnants of the catering table. Jim Axtell, struggling to pull himself up after being thrown like a rag doll into the wall. The Cult, brutalizing Clay Vendetta as a group. Minion, trying to see how many cheese-filled crab puffs he can fit into his mouth at once. Steven: Mmmm, crab puffs. Garvin raises the bat... and pauses. Nate: What the hell is he waiting on? Robbie: You don’t know much about Jeff Garvin, do you? He’s gutless. He’ll never— Before Robbie can finish, Garvin charges over to the Cult and... THWACK! Robbie: HEY! Garvin DRILLS Hessian in the back of the head, actually breaking the bat over his massive skull! The crowd roars in approval!!! Nate: DOWN GOES HESSIAN! Garvin turns and attacks the Cultist, tying him up in a modified half nelson and pulling him to the floor. Nate: GARVIN! Jeff Garvin has come to The Throwbacks’ rescue! Robbie: What an idiot! Vampir is left with Vendetta, but not for long as a staggering Gibson heads over... with a semi-full pot of hot coffee and throws it on Vampir’s bare back! Vampir: YEEEEAAAARRRRGGH! As Vampir cringes, Gibson leaps onto Vampir’s back and applies a Cobra Clutch, with bodyscissors. Nate: SILENCER! SILENCER! Gibson has his move hooked! Garvin and the Cultist are slugging it out, as Vampir thrashes wildly in the Silencer. Hessian has started to stand, but Vendetta tackles him, shoving Hessian onto and through the only still-standing catering table. Robbie: Mental Note: Don’t bother trying to get something to eat after the show tonight. Vampir’s struggles have nearly ceased, as he’s almost unconscious. Axtell, now standing, is limping over to the fray using a bat as a crutch. Nate: The Throwbacks have... they’ve gotten the better of the Cult in this fight! They REFUSE to give up! Suddenly, from the ceiling above, a dark silhouette drops down behind Axtell. Robbie: What the hell? Dressed identical to the Cultist, the newcomer grabs Axtell around the throat and brandishes a lethal-looking dagger, which he holds against Axtell’s jugular vein. Nate: It... It... A SECOND CULTIST?!? Steven: A knife? It must be Corg! Vendetta immediately steps away from Hessian, who struggles to his feet. He gives a look to Garvin and Gibson. Garvin backs off from the Cultist while Gibson, reluctantly, releases Vampir from the Silencer. Hessian heads over and helps Vampir up. Hessian: Now, my pitiful adversaries, you see the power of the Cult. Our numbers, our influence... it exceeds what your pitiful intellects can conceive. It is a lesson that, inevitably, you would learn . But for... your actions, there must be a price. Perhaps it will cost you... your new ally. The second Cultist poises the knife against Axtell’s throat, as Gibson, CCV and Garvin watch helplessly. Hessian: [chuckles] No. No, I think not. The second Cultist releases Axtell who falls, gasping, to all fours. Hessian: Perhaps we will wait. Yes... Perhaps we will settle this matter next week... on Slam. The four of us against the four of you... assuming you can fight us without using baseball bats and... [chuckles] coffee. Gibson: Any time, any place. We’ll be there. Hessian: Yes, I’m sure... and we will be there as well. To dance on your freshly dug graves. Never taking their eyes off the Throwbacks, The Cult backs away into the shadows, followed by Minion, who’s holding the last two crab puffs. Steven: Mmmm, crab puffs. Robbie: Wow, another Cultist! Those Throwbacks don’t know who they’re messing with. Nate: I think you’re ignoring what we witnessed!!! The Throwbacks and their new allies stood up to the Cult again! Man, what a fight! And what a match for next week! The Throwbacks and Jeff Garvin will meet the Cult in an eight man tag!!! We need to head to a commercial!! When we return, we will head to the ring for our International Title matchup!!!
