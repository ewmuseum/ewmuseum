Civil Disobedience vs. Team Annoying

2002-04-28
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil Disobedience vs. Team Annoying Written: 4-28-02 The camera pans around the jam packed arena that is filled with thousands of screaming OSW fans who have flocked from all across the world to come and watch Head on Collision. Many of the people are holding up signs while others are cheering wildly. One thing is certain though. Everyone here is witnessing one hell of a show. After a few seconds, the camera finally settles in one the announcers' table where the crackpot OSW announce team is sitting. All three of the announcers are wearing a big smile across their faces. Nate: And finally Head on Collision is here and coming up next we have a match that will go down in history for all time! Coming up next we have Civil Disobedience taking on what we have dubbed team annoying, which is comprised of Gilbert Godfrey and Carrot Top! Steven: Oh for the love of God! I think that it is time to start drinking! I don't think I can take this! Robbie: For once you are going to hear me root for those damn jerks from the Suburbs, Hawke and Masters! Nate: I don't think there is a single person in the world that wants to see Team Annoying win this match. It all started a couple of weeks ago when CD mocked the wanna be comedian Carrot Top on their public access cable program. And since then things have escalated to dangerous levels of annoyance for all of the fans of OSW! Robbie: And here we are! Forced to watch those pains in the ass from Suburbia take on Carrot Top and Gilbert Godfrey! What the hell is the front office thinking? This is going to kill our ratings! I think I would rather listen to Steven rate every member of the roster all night then allow this match to go on! Steven: Well, okay let's start at the top... Robbie: SHUT UP! Nate: Alright guys just calm down for a second. Take some solace in the fact that you are going to get to so Carrot Top and Good Old Gilbert get completely beat down! Robbie and Steven: Good Point! Nate: Now that we all agree on that, let's get to the introductions for the match. Earl it's all yours! The camera shifts to Earl Henrich standing in the middle of the ring with his mic in his hand waiting to make the introductions. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, Boys and Girls, Children of all Ages! This next match up is scheduled for one fall. The referee for this match is... Voice from backstage: Stop right there! Nate: What the... Robbie: HUH? Steven: Oh God! It can't be!!! Voice: You think that my boys didn’t come prepared did you? Steven: Anybody but him! Please anyone but him! Voice: There is anew ref scheduled for this match! Steven: This is my worst nightmare come true! Voice: And that new Referee is ME!!! From the backstage area the identity of the voice that is striking fear into the heart of Steven Smith is revealed when David Arquette emerges onto the entryway wearing referee stripes. Steven: NOOOOOooooOOOOoooo!!!! Robbie: Just when you think that things can't get any worse! Nate: Who let David Arquette ref this match? Robbie: This is nuts. David: That's right people, it is I, former WCW WorldChampion, David Arquette! I am the special guest referee for this match. The fans are booing wildly as Steven is now in the fetal position on the floor underneath the announcers' table and Robbie's head is in his hands out of frustration. Arquette starts to make his way down towards the ring as the fans are booing for him. The annoying entertainer finally gets into the ring and starts to prance around the mat to the regret of the fans. Nate: Okay! Now this is getting ridiculous! Steven! Steven! Will you get the hell up! What the hell is a matter with you? Steven is under the table still shaking. Robbie looks around the table for something that might bring Steven out of this seizure brought on by the mere presence of David Arquette. A glass of water catches Robbie's eye. He quickly grabs the glass and dumps it on Steven. This works like a charm and Steven slowly starts to climb back in his seat. Steven: Not him! This has just become the night of my life! Steven returns to his normal announcing position. Nate: Let’s just take it to earl for the introductions shall we? The camera shot changes to earl standing in the ring trying to ignore the always annoying David Arquette. Earl raises the mic up to his lips to begin the match’s introductions. Earl: Coming to the ring first from Hollywood California, Gilbert Gottfried and Carrot Top! Carrot Top and Gottfried appear from the backstage area in a mass of exuberant flailing limbs and hair as the bounce around at the entryway. Carrot Top is just wearing a pair of orange briefs with a carrot on the front, while Gottfried is dawning a pair of tight purple jam pants. They quickly make their ways down to the ring to stand side by side with Arquette. Earl rolls his eyes at the nuisance that is team annoying. Earl manages to muster enough composure despite his surroundings. Earl: And their opponents, from Aurora, Illinois. And weighing in at combined weight of 460 pounds! “Bounce” by System of a Down begins to blast the PA system to the delight of the fans. Earl: They are Trevor Hawke and Seth Masters, Civil Disobedience!!!! Masters and Hawke blast out of the backstage area with great energy as the arena explodes with cheers for them. Masters and Hawke sprint down the ramp towards the ring and dive underneath the ropes to begin their assault. Nate: And here we go! Robbie: Kill em!!!! Steven: Kill Arquette! Masters dives at Carrot Top and hits him with a spear. But before Hawke can get involved Arquette gets in his way and forces Hawke outside onto the apron. Nate: Hey Arquette! What about Gottfried? Masters is on top of CT and pounding his head into the mat, but Gottfried pulls a chair into the ring and slams it across his Masters back. Nate: What the hell is that? Steven: Damn him! I knew that he would do this! I knew it! Robbie: Do what? Steven: He is allowing Carrot Top and Gottfried to be in the ring at the same time, but forcing Masters and Hawke to fight under normal Tag Stipulations!!! Nate: How do you know this? Steven: I almost had to work with him on that horrible movie “Ready to Rumble!” Nate: What? I didn’t know that you were supposed to be in that film? Steven: Yes, I was that is until I figured out that he was in the film. I could only make it through three days of shooting before I had to quit the job! That son of a bitch ruined my acting career! Inside the ring now, Gottfried is slamming away at Masters with the chair, while Arquette pretends to not know what is going on. Gottfried finally tosses the chair outside the ring. Gottfried and Carrot Top pull Masters to his feet and get him up for a double powerbomb. Masters though manages to flip over their backs. Masters lands on his feet and grabs both of the not so funny comedians by the back of their heads and slams them together. Gottfried and CT fall to the mat like a couple of cartoon characters. Nate: This is all getting very confusing. Robbie: Shhh! You hear that? Nate and Steven: What? Robbie: That is the sound of our rating dropping through the floor! Robbie makes the sound of something falling and crashing very loudly. Masters is kicking away at Carrot Top now and not showing any sign of stopping until Arquette gets in his way and forces Masters away from the fallen comedians. Out of pure frustration, Masters walks over and tags in Hawke. Nate: Masters is walking okay considering that he just got his back beat in with a steel chair. Robbie: Did you see who was holding the chair! That annoying over the hill prick can’t hurt a professional wrestler! Gottfried and CT are getting up as Hawke charges at them. Hawke hits Gottfried with a clothesline first and follows that up with a spinning heel kick to CT’s jaw. Both men are down once again and the fans are cheering wildly. Nate: Get em! Hawke immediately gets down and slaps on a half elevated crab on carrot Top. Nate: And this could be it! Arquette gets down and pulls Hawke off of Carrot Top saying that Carrot Top was touching the ropes. Robbie: He was no where near the ropes! What the hell is this guy thinking. Gottfried is up now, but he is now crawling underneath the ring as if he is looking for something. Gottfried emerges with two trashcan lids in his hand. Nate: What the hell does he think he is going to use those for? Robbie: Oh God NO! Steven and Nate: WHAT? Robbie: He is going to do that ridiculous Mickey Mouse impression! I can just sense it! Sure enough Gottfried is back in the ring and prancing around with the garbage can lids up to his ears as if he was Mickey Mouse. Robbie: OH GOD! Gottfried starts to prance around in front of Hawke who just looks completely unamused at the entire ordeal. Hawke is suddenly hit the face with one of the can lids, then the other one, then the other, and the other, and the other. Gottfried is attacking Hawke with both of the garbage lids in a combination of right and lefts all the while he is still prancing. Nate: This is insane! Robbie: Kill him already Hawke! Steven: damn you Arquette! Damn you to hell! Carrot Top has rolled out of the ring now and he is looking for something underneath the apron as well. Nate: Now what the hell is HE doing? Hawke finally falls to his back holding his head after a multiple shots from the lids. Gottfried goes for the pin and Arquette is there to make the count. 1…2…NO! Hawke kicks out. Nate: That was an awfully quick count there! Steven: What do you expect! Arquette is probably going through withdrawal right now. It ahs probably been fifteen minutes since his last hit of Coke! Robbie: What? Steven: The man is an addict! Look at him! He always seems as if he needs a hit of something! Hawke lunges at Masters and makes the tag. Masters immediately leaps up onto the turnbuckle and hurls himself at the Gottfried by performing a sonton from the top rope. And sure enough, Masters nail Gottfried who falls to the mat a shaken mess. Nate: That’s it! The fans are going crazy at the spectacle of have Civil Disobedience beat down on what could possibly be the two most not funny people in the world. Masters is up right away and goes for a pin. Arquette pretends that he is in the corner checking the strength of the turnbuckle instead of paying attention. Steven: I know you’re watching there Arquette! We all know the truth! Masters slams the mat in a fury and gets to his feet, but before he can turn Arquette around he is nailed in the back of the head with a fire extinguisher wielded by Carrot Top who has just entered the ring again. Carrot Top drops the extinguisher and goes for a quick pin. Arquette turns around quickly and starts to make the count. 1…2…No! Another kick out by Civil Disobedience! Nate: I have seen some quick counts in my day, but this is getting ridiculous! Robbie: What do you mean getting? Nate: Good point! Masters lunges for a tag and clearly slaps Hawke’s hand, but when Hawke tries to get into the ring he is stopped by Arquette who claims that they didn’t actually tag! Steven: you son of a bitch! You know they tagged! You know it! Masters is now at the mercy of Carrot Top and Gottfried who is back on his feet. Gottfried picks up the extinguisher and manages to spray its contents all over Masters! Masters is now covered in head to toe in a flame retardant substance. Masters is getting to his feet, but he blinded by the chemicals that cover. He is stumbling around the ring trying to find one of the comedians. Masters is stopped in his tracks though by a double garbage can shot by both Gottfried and CT. Nate: I swear! After tonight, I am going to have a talk with someone in the front office and asking them seriously what the hell they were thinking! Masters is on his back again, but this time it is Carrot Top that goes for the pin. Arquette spins around and tries to count again. 1…2…NO! Yet another kick out! Masters tries to get to the corner for a tag, but he is stopped by yet another shot to the back of the skull by Gottfried who doesn’t seem too happy. They go for another pin. Arquette starts to count. 1…2…NO! Masters rolls out and this time manages to make the tag again in order to get out of the ring. Steven: Oh, so you will allow that one huh Arquette! Nate: I have never seen you so militant before in my life! Robbie: I guess that just goes to show! Nate: Goes to show what? Robbie: Never piss off a flamer, like Smith! Hawke enters the ring and charges at Gottfried and hit him with a spinning heel kick to the chin. Carrot Top looks stunned by what happened and immediately gets down on his knees in fear of Hawke. Hawke is standing over him with no look of pity on his face. Nate: Carrot Top is pleading with the angered Suburban Bad Ass! Steven: Not going to happen! Hawke turns his back and starts to look at the crown for a moment as if he is saying “What should I do?” Hawke is done joking around though after a second and simply turns to CT and bitch slaps the comedian. The fans cheer wildly with joy at the sight. Hawke leans over and pulls Carrot Top to his feet and picks the man into the air. Hawke manages to lift Carrot Top into the air and bring him crashing to the mat as a result of a spinning fisherman’s suplex! Nate: I think that Seth has just killed Carrot Top! Robbie: One can only hope! Hawke goes for a pin, but Arquette refuses to count for Civil Disobedience! Hawke slams his fist hard into the mat out of rage towards the referee. Hawke gets to his feet and starts to stalk towards the now backing up Arquette. Steven: YES! KICK HIS ASS! Hawke grabs hold of the strung out comedian and irishwhips him across the ring towards Masters who is in the ring now. Masters hits the charging Arquette with a fierce spinning heel kick that drops the wanna be ref down and out! Steven: BOOOOOOCCCCAAAAA~! Nate: Arquette is out cold! This is not good for team annoying one bit. Masters charges a Gottfried who has the fear of God in his eyes. Masters grabs hold of Gottfried and nails him with a fisherman buster! Nate: Primo Plex! Masters has hit the Primo Plex! Hawke pulls himself up onto the top rope. He signals to the crowd who immediately begin to cheer wildly. Nate: You all know what is coming now, don’t you! Hawke leaps from the turnbuckle and performs a corkscrew legdrop that hits Carrot Top right across the throat! Nate: Da Supafly!!! Robbie: YES!!! The arena is going nuts now as Masters and Hawke pull Carrot Top up to his feet. Nate: I don’t think that they are done yet! Both, Masters and Hawke, irishwhip Carrot Top across the ring. Carrot top comes flying back at Civil Disobedience with great speed! Masters and Hawke hit CT with a double flap jack, but on the way down they hit him with a double flatliner before he hits the mat. Nate: The Trendsetter! Ha! They have done it! Masters and Hawke each place a foot on top of Carrot Top and work the crowd for more cheers. Suddenly out of the back, referee Gaston explodes down the ramp and dives under the rope. Gaston starts to count just for the effect. All of the fans in the arena are counting along with Gaston. 1… PAUSE 2… PAUSE 3… Winners of the Match: Civil Disobedience Masters and Hawke each leap up onto a turnbuckle and begin to work the crowd over for some more pops! Nate: They did it! They beat Team Annoying! Steven: Die Arquette! DIE!!!! Robbie: HA! Nate: Well, we need to head to a commercial while we clean up the hacks!
