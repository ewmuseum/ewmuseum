Make Me Bleed

2002-07-20
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Make Me Bleed Written: July 20, 2002 The scene fades back in from commercial to reveal Corg drifting backstage, with his cruiserweight title is over his shoulder as he leans against the wall, catching his breathe. Corg: Oh… shit. Out of the corner of his eye, he sees a figure standing behind him. The figure looks female, but he is startled when he sees that she is carrying a lead pipe. Aimz. Aimz charges at Corg and before Corg can even react he feels the pipe driving straight into his spine. WHACK! Corg swings a fist at Aimz, nailing her straight in the jaw, but he goes down as Aimz continues to beat him with the pipe. Aimz: You...little...piece...of...shit! WHACK! WHACK! WHACK! Aimz continues to hit Corg with the pipes. The pipe’s usual chrome reflection has now turned to blood, thanks to Aimz. Panting hard, Aimz steps back as she stares at her adversary. Corg puts one hand over he knee, and attempts to lift himself up, but he is taken down by another shot from Aimz with the pipe. This can’t be happening… Aimz face has now turned into a crazed, psychotic face, with a hint of blood dripping from her lip where Corg had hit her. Aimz: You’re pathetic! Fucking hunter, your ankles caught in the bear trap now, huh? A wad of spit lands on Corg’s face, obviously coming from Amy. No… no… Aimz just smiles, sadistically like Corg would, and swings the pipe at him once more. Corg: Ooh, fuck! Aimz continues to bash Corg with the pipe, and Corg can’t stand it. It’s not the fact that he’s in pain, but it’s the fact that he’s losing. Corg is losing his battle.
