Xavier The Red?

29 July 2003

by Jane Reagan

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The OSE site's own rumor-board post (#28) by Jane Reagan, dated 29 July 2003, from the OSW site as it stood in the Wayback capture.
With many people speculating as to who was last to see OSW owner Jane Reagan, who's been missing since Slam! went off air, the number of names being tossed around has grown... With a very surprising addition. Those suspected are people who've been working closely or have been associated with Miss Reagan recently. Although the Red Army are chief suspects, the name of Xavier Kannon has found itself thrown into the mix. Over the last 3 weeks, Kannon's supposed signing to OSW has been he cause of debate, with Jane constantly denying any link to the former PCW Champion... Is this how he planned his arrival, by removing the woman who controls contracts and signings? Or is there something more sinister at play... Although the pair are thought to be on nowhere near friendly terms, Xavier is known to be an admirer of the Russian Bear, Ivan Stanislav; Once referring to him as the 'Alpha-Male'. Could Kannon and Stanislav be in cahoots? Or is the innocent name of Kannon once again being mis-used?
