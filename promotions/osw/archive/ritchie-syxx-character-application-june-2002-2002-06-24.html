Title History: None Awards History: None Description: See that pic up there? That's them. History: Our story starts a long time ago, in a suburb far, far away. If you can believe it, these two idiots weren't always idiots. Well Trevor wasn't, at least. Seth never really did have a brain. Trevor, though...there were high hopes set for Trevor. His parents were extremely wealthy, as his father was a successful entrepreneur, and this wealth led him to experience the best things money could buy. From the finest schools, the most trendy clothes, anything his heart desired was his. The only downside to this was, as with most wealthy families, his parents divorced when he was still young, causing his mother to recieve full custody of his care. From that point on, there wasn't much that could be done to stop him from becoming the one thing that is the antithesis of all "coolness": The Mama's Boy. Soon, although he whatever he wanted, he had lost his free will in return. A fair deal? No frickin' way. Then along came Seth Masters... Seth had grown up in the same town as Trevor...only he resided on the other side of the tracks. You know, the tacky part of suburbia. His life was rather normal my today's standards, which may account for why he's so f#cked up today. Bored with mundane Aurora, Illinois, he was constantly searching for excitement in all its forms. He wasn't the sharpest knife in the drawer, especially when it came to common sense, but he had a way of looking at the world that made him successful. If he wanted something, he'd take it. If he felt like doing something, he'd do it. All in all, he did whatever the hell he wanted to, living his life by the moment, and he was happy doing so. His self-centered take on life caused him to fall out of touch with reality, which will explain why he now resides in his own little world. Now you're probably asking yourself, "How the hell did these two guys ever wind up as a team? A guy like Seth would hate a spoiled f#ck like Trevor, right?" And in reply I would say, "Right, he would." And Seth did hate Trevor at first. The two met in a self-defense class both were enrolled in at the age of thirteen. Trevor was taking it because his mother wanted him to interact with other "nice boys his age", while Seth was taking it to more or less know how to kick people's asses. After some initial bickering, Seth realized how pathetic Trevor really was and took it upon himself to straighten out the other boy's priorities. Immediately the two began hanging out (much to the chagrin of Trevor's mother) and slowly but surely, Trevor began to fall into Seth's pit of delinquincy. Ever since, the two have been as tight as Snoop Dogg and the D-R-E And if you don't know how tight that is, that's pretty damn tight. Since then, the two have become obsessed with becoming the "most bad-ass mofos on the planet" and what better way to do so than in the sport of professional wrestling? I sure as hell can't think of one. Amazingly, Trevor has made it through college, despite Seth's constant ragging on him to drop out. He now can claim a bachelor's degree in Philosophy from the University of Chicago, though it's not like it's going to do him any good in OSW. A stay in the Primetime Wrestling Academy has gotten them a shot at the big time (though it did get Trevor kicked out of his house along the way). These two egomaniacs are set to make complete asses of themselves on the way to the top, and I know I for one will be keeping my eye on them. Until they start making fun of midgets, that is. Even I will not stand for the defamation of the little people. 'Angeldust' Nick Casey Amy Campbell Azrael Cain Corg Civil Disobedience
