Ian Wright Real Name: Birthdate: 12/3/79 Hometown: Clane, Ireland Height: 6'3'' Weight: 285 lbs. Nicknames: Status: Heel Manager(s): Current Teams: Entrance Music: 'Hate to say I told you so' by The Hives Information Wrestling Style: European with some Judo elements mixed in Signature Moves: The Full clutch, a submission hold which is a camel clutch with Wright holding his opponent in a Full Nelson grip. Dragon Suplex; T-bone Suplex; Northern Lights Suplex; German Suplex; Top Rope Kneedrop; Dragon Screw Leg Whip; Head And Arm Suplex; Powerslam;Short-arm Clothesline; Single Arm DDT; Snap Suplex; Spinebuster; Uranage; DDT Set Up Move: The Wrights Out can be performed in a number of ways. It is primrarily done when both wrestlers are standing facing each other. It can also be done as the opponent returns after an Irish whip to the ropes. It can be performed as a counter move as well, mainly when the opponent throws a high overhand strike, it can also be used as a hip toss counter ( ) Finisher: The Wrights Out (Wright reaches under and inside his opponents armpit, he places his hand in the middle of the opponents back and picks his opponents legs up so that Wright is carrying his opponent horizontally across his lower back. He pauses for a moment before diving face first towards the mat, before he hits the ground he spins round so that the first thing to hit the ground is the back of his opponents head as Wright's weight comes down on top of him ) OSW Title History: OSW Award History: Other Federations: UWA Other Federation Awards: UWA wrestler of the year 2000, UWA World Champion, UWA canadian champion Handler Information Handler Name: Ian Wright AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio Ian Wright is a young wrestler from Ireland, but his youth belies years of experience from all over the world. He got into the sport much the same way as William Regal did, he started off in carnival fairgrounds at 16. For months and months he got the hell beaten out of him, never going more then a couple of minutes in the ring with the much more experienced, skilled and stronger men that he had to fight. But he never gave up, even then he had an unflinching will to win, to be the best. Eventually over time he started to pick up the skills that he needed to compete in the ring, he started lasting longer and longer in his matches, he was causing difficulty for his opponents, but that first victory still eluded him. Eventually after a year at the carvinals he finally won his first match, granted in order to do it he had to tear apart the knee of his opponent, thus ending his career but a win was a win. At this point he came to a realisation, he had the tools, in fact he had had them for months, but he had always been too nice about his matches, he had always been concerned about the health of his opponents after the match, and because of this he had never been willing to perform moves on them that would endanger them. He came to a realisation that this would have to change. Cut to today and Wrights whole attitude to life and his career has been based on that realisation. In the ring he is sadistic, vicious. He will do anything to win a match, and he doesn't care about his opponent while doing it. He will risk any injury (to his opponent) to get the win, they got in the ring, they know the risks. Outside the ring he cares about no one, all he wants to be is the best. He doesn't fraternise with the other wrestlers, he may have to hurt them someday, being friends with them will only make it more difficult if he has to face them in the ring. And that after all is his goal in life, to proove to everyone that he is the very best there is.
