Civil Disobedience vs. Heresian Bloodlust for the OSW Tag Team Titles

30 Mar 2002

Slam 9. Civil Disobedience's first Tag Team Championship defense — and last.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Civil Disobedience vs. Heresian Bloodlust for the OSW Tag Team Titles (Suburban Deathmatch) Written: 3-30-02 Nate: We are just about ready to head to the ring for our next match of the evening, in which Civil Disobedience will defend the OSW Tag Team titles against Heresian Bloodlust. Robbie: Now this is more like it! Just jump right into the announcements. Less banter. Nate: Let’s take it to Earl who is standing by in the ring to make the announcements. The camera shot changes to OSW’s Earl Henrich who is standing in the ring waiting to make the title match’s introductions. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, Boys and Girls, Children of all Ages, this next match is scheduled for one fall for the OSW Tag Team Belts! The referee for this match Speedy Riggs!! Coming to the ring first, from unknown regions and weighing in at a combined weight of 725 pounds… The lights in the arena snap out as the fans start to boo wildly. A large bloody pentagram seeps across the jumbotron as “Carmina Burana” begins to play on the PA system. A strobe light starts flickering and large wall of fire explodes at the entryway as two huge silhouettes appear behind the flames. Robbie: I still don’t see why everyone continues to boo these guys? I mean these guys are great!! Nate: Gee, do you think it could have anything to do with the fact that they want nothing more than to condemn the entire population of earth to Hell? Robbie thinks for a moment. Robbie: …nah, there has to be some other reason. Earl: They are Azrael Cain and Hessian, Heresian Bloodlust!!! Cain and Hessian emerge from the flames unscathed. They proceed down the ramp to the ring without paying any notice to the fans screaming at them. Their managers, Caleb and Minion are following closely behind them. The small hobo, Caleb, is carrying Cain’s thirty-pound sledgehammer over his shoulder. Nate: And sure enough, there are those two little bastards following behind Cain and Hessian. Robbie: Shut up Dumas!! Those two are the best managers in this fed. Nate: They are constantly cheating for Heresian Bloodlust. Robbie: you just proved my argument right there. Cain and Hessian are now in the ring. Both of them remove their cloaks and are staring at the entryway for their opponents to come out. Nate: Well, these two certainly seem to be ready for the match. Steven: Well, they have been ready for every other match with Masters and Hawke up until this point and they are still to get a victory. Robbie: They were robbed at the Pay per View and you guys now it!!! The camera shot returns to Earl Henrich. Earl: And now introducing their opponents, coming to you from Aurora, Illinois and weighing in at a combined weight of 460 pounds! They are the OSW Tag Team Champions… “Pretty Fly for a White Guy” replaces “Carmina Burana” on the Arena’s speakers. The music can hardly be heard over the cheers of the fans. Earl: Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, Civil Disobedience!!! Masters and Hawke leap out onto the entryway as the arena practically explodes from the wild cheers for the tag champs. Masters and Hawke begin working the crowd for more cheers as the slap hands with all of their fans lining the steelramp. Nate: This is the classic battle of Good versus Evil! Steven: Strength versus Speed! Robbie: Warriors versus nursing puppies!! Nate: What!? Robbie: Masters and Hawke are going down this week and that is all there is to it! Masters and Hawke are now in the ring and each of them are up on a turnbuckle holding the tag belts high in the air for their fans to see. Nate: You think that they would have learned by now. Steven: Learned what? Both Masters and Hawke are ripped from the top of the turnbuckles and slammed into the canvas by Hessian and Cain. The tag belts go flying to the mat, but they are quickly scooped up by Riggs who also signals for the bell to ring. Nate: To never turn there back on Heresian Bloodlust! Robbie: HA!!! Cain is stomping away at Masters, while Hessian is pounding his steel gauntlets into the face of Hawke. Cain pulls Masters to his feet and hoists him up over his head. He presses Masters three times before tossing him out of the ring down onto the concrete floor below. Nate: Good Lord! Look at that! Cain steps out of the ring and stands on the apron. Riggs is on top of Hessian trying to pull the goliath off of Hawke. Hessian finally stops the pounding and pulls Hawke to his feet only to deliver a clothesline that drops Hawke back to the mat. Robbie: It has been entirely to long since I have seen a good match like this. These guys should fight every week! Steven: I don’t think Hawke or Masters’ bodies could take that! Robbie and Steven: HA! Hessian is stomping away at Hawke. Hawke is trying to roll away from the kicks, but every other one is making contact. Finally, Hawke gets to the ropes and manages to roll out of the ring down to the floor. Robbie: Cowards!!! Hessian raises his arms in fury calling for Hawke to get back in the ring. The goliath’s attention is focused on the Hawke. He doesn’t see that Masters is back in the ring. Masters runs at Hessian and manages to spear Hessian in the back of the right knee before rolling back out of the ring. Hessian drops to the mat holding his knee in pain. Nate: And a quick move by Masters probably just saved his partner. Robbie: Hey Riggs, call the match even there, buddy. He was cheating! Hawke is up on the top turnbuckle now. Nate: Dangerous maneuver from Hawke! Hawke leaps high from the top rope and delivers knee to the chest of Hessian. The masked warrior is rolling on the mat in pain. Hawke makes a quick tag to his partner. Masters gets into the ring and goes for Hessian’s knee immediately. Nate: It seems that Masters and Hawke have a plan for this match. Masters is trying to get a sharp shooter on Hessian, but he unfortunately manages to nail Masters with a kick to the chest with his other leg before the move is locked on. Masters hits the mat from the kick. Hessian is limping to his corner for a tag. Robbie: Come on Hessian, get the tag. Hessian reaches and manages to get the tag. Cain enters over the top rope. He lunges at Masters who ducks under the fallen angel’s grasp and crawls between his legs. Cain turns around and his met with a spinning heel kick to the chin. Masters winds up and hits Cain with another. Nate: Cain is still on his feet! Masters looks frustrated in the ring. He winds up and hits Cain with a third spinning heel kick that makes contact with Cain’s chin again. Masters runs at Cain and starts to lay punches into the warrior’s abdominals. The stunned Cain is back into the ropes. Hawke jumps up and gets Cain into a sleeper hold from the apron. Cain is grasping at Hawke. Masters steps back and runs at Cain and hits him with a forearm to the face. Nate: It would seem that Civil Disobedience has Cain and Hessian on the run here! Robbie: No way in hell! They are just giving these guys a chance to get some licks in before the destroy them! Steven: I don’t think that is what’s really going on here, Decker! Robbie: Don’t you dare get started Smith!!! Go back to your hole!!! Cain roars loudly in the ring. He pulls himself forward flipping Hawke over the ropes and into the ring causing him to release the sleeper hold. Hawke hits the mat hard. Cain lunges at Masters and hits him with a clothesline that drops Masters back to the mat as well. Cain turns around and sees that Hawke is back on his feet, so Cain hits him with a clothesline that drops him to the mat as well. Masters is back up too, but his fate is the same as his partner as he gets dropped to the mat with another clothesline from the infuriated Cain. Nate: Cain is clearing house! Robbie: What did I tell you!! Steven: BOCA~!!! Cain pulls Hawke up and tosses him out of the ring to the concrete floor. He grabs Masters and hoists him up into the air. Cain presses him again three times. The beast tosses Masters out of the ring unto his partner Hawke. Nate: Good God!!! Cain goes over and tags in Hessian. Both of them exit the ring and cross to Hawke and Masters. Cain picks Masters up and puts him on top of the announcers’ table. Nate: HEY! Get the hell out of here! Cain signals to Hessian who is up on the apron of the ring. Nate: Oh this can’t be happening! Steven: OH GOD! Hessian leaps into the air from the apron. Nate: IN COMING!!! Steven: MOMMY!!! Robbie: AHHH!!! There is a loud crash as Hessian drives his elbow into Masters’ chest. The massive weight of Hessian and the force of the elbow drop has caused the announcers’ table to explode into millions of pieces all over the floor. Nate: Masters isn’t moving! Our table is toast!!! Cain is now getting Hawke up and placing him on the security barrier. Hessian is back up on the apron. Nate: Heresian Bloodlust is dismantling Civil Disobedience!!! Cain signals for Hessian again. Hessian leaps through the air again with his elbow ready to make contact. Nate: Good God!! Before Hessian can hit though Hawke has managed to roll off of the barrier. Hessian hits the barrier with his elbow. The goliath let’s out a mighty roar in pain as crashes to the floor on top of Hawke. Riggs has finally begun to count out both teams. 1… 2… 3… Masters is now beginning to crawl towards the ring holding his chest in pain. 4… Nate: There are tears in Masters’ eyes. 5… Cain slides back into the ring. 6… Masters has his arm up on the apron. Cain has begun taunting the wounded wrestler. 7… 8… Nate: THE TITLEs CAN NOT CHANGE HANDS ON A COUNT OUT!!! 9… Before Riggs can get to ten though, someone has climbed the barrier and managed to toss Masters back into the ring. Nate: Who the hell is that? Robbie: Oh God, please say it isn’t him! Steven: Will you look at the flaming red hair!!! Nate: MY GOD!!! ITS ACTOR/COMEDIAN CARROT TOP!!! HE JUST THREW THE INJURED SETH MASTERS BACK IN THE RING, TO BEAT THE COUNT!!! Robbie: HA!!! THAT WILL LIKELY COST CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE THE TITLES!!! The crowd erupts into a resounding chorus of boos. Carrot Top is outside of the ring taunting Masters who is at the mercy of Azrael Cain. Carrot Top has crawled into the ring now and starts to beat away at Masters. Nate: CARROT TOP IS LAYING A BEATING ON SETH MASTERS!!! THIS IS AN OUTRAGE!!! Cain has a confused look on his face at the red headed lunatic. Nate: Carrot Top is here to get revenge on Civil Disobedience for the way that they treated him on their talk show this week! Robbie: HA…Masters is…HA…getting beat down…by…by…HA…Carrot Top! Caleb is distracting Riggs on the other side of the ring, so that the beating can continue. Carrot Top is up on his feet now and running around the ring like a lunatic. Cain grabs hold of the not so funny comedian and tosses him aside. Hessian is climbing back into the ring. Nate: Masters is at Cain and Hessian’s mercy! This is going to be ugly! Cain reaches down and pulls Masters to his feet only to Irishwhip him across the ring. Masters bounces off of the ropes and he is back body dropped by Cain. Before Masters can hit the mat Hessian catches Masters in midair and delivers a death valley driver. Nate: The Heretics Wrath!!! Heresian Bloodlust just hit Masters with the Heretics Wrath!!! Robbie: Ha!!! Cain goes for the cover. Riggs is there to make the count. 1… Nate: I can’t believe it!!! 2… Steven: BOCA~!!! 3!!! Robbie: YES!!! Winners of the match and New Tag Team Champions: Heresian Bloodlust Nate: We have new tag team champions here in OSW!!! The fans are booing wildly as Riggs hands Hessian and Cain the belts and raise their hands in the air. Nate: I can’t believe! Robbie: I knew it! I knew this day would come!!! Nate: How could this happen on Easter weekend?! Carrot Top is running around outside of the ring shaking his head so that his wild red hair is flying about in every direction. Carrot Top grabs and puts him into the ring. Nate: I don’t think Carrot Top is done yet! He wants Cain and Hessian to dismantle Masters and Hawke. Cain and Hessian look at one another and share a “Why not” shrug. They both toss their belts to their managers who immediately begin to taunt the booing fans with them. Cain reaches down and hoists Hawke up to his feet. Cain then tosses Hawke into the ropes with and Irishwhip. Hawke comes flying back at Cain who delivers another back body drop so that Hessian can finish Hawke off with the Heretic’s Wrath! Nate: Oh God! Heresian Bloodlust is dismantling Civil Disobedience, at the behest of CARROT TOP!!! WHAT THE HELL HAS HAPPENED HERE!!!! The goliath and the beast begin stomping away at Masters and Hawke. Then, suddenly, The Soviet National Anthem begins to blare though through the PA system. Nate: OH MY GOD!!! The camera heads to the entry way as Tom Walczak and Michel Krieg hit the steelramp at a full sprint. Cain and Hessian turn around quickly to see who is coming down the ramp. Nate: KRIEG AND WALCZAK ARE ON THEIR WAYS TO THE RING, RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!! Steven: I think they are here to help out Civil Disobedience! Robbie: Someone get security in here! Walczak and Krieg immediately begin to lay fists into Cain and Hessian. Cain and Krieg are exchanging blows, while Hessian and Walczak are doing the same. Nate: This is unbelievable! After a moment of exchanging blows, it is Krieg and Walczak that manage to get the upper hand. Cain and Hessian are tossed out of the ring down to the floor. The fans are not certain as to who to cheer for in this melee. Robbie: I don’t get it! Krieg and Walczak both reach a hand down to Masters and Hawke. Nate: OH MY!!! THE RED ARMY HAS SAVED CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE FROM A SURE BEATING AND ARE EXTENDING A HAND OF FRIENDSHIP TO THE KINGS OF SURBURBIA!!! Hawke and Masters look up at the two massive Communists, then at each other. The pair then roll out of the ring, without reciprocating the show of friendship. Nate: WOAH!!! CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE JUST BLEW OFF THE OVERTURE OF FRIENDSHIP FROM THE RED ARMY!!! Robbie: I still don’t get it!! What the hell are Krieg and Walczak doing out here? Very slowly the fans are beginning to cheer for the members of the Red Army that just came to Civil Disobedience’s aid. The Soviet National Anthem begins to play as Cain and Hessian exit with the belts. Nate: This is one of the most bizarre turn of events that I have ever seen. Listen to the fans! They are cheering for the Red Army! The fans are cheering wildly now as all four of the wrestlers climb the turnbuckles to work the crowd. Nate: Let’ s go to a commercial while we try to sort this damn thing out. Robbie: I still don’t get it!!! The camera shifts to the ring where Walczak and Krieg stand on the turnbuckles, soaking in the apprehensive cheers of the crowd.
