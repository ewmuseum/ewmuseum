Drew Hostyle Birth Name: Dante Williams Ring Name: Andrew Copeland Nick Name(s): Drew Hostyle, OSW's Hoodrat Hugh Hefner Catchphase(s): The streets are watchin', better not turn ya back. By HOOK or by CROOK, it's all ALL in the book. Character Handler: Korey R. MSN Account: N/A AIM Address: [detail withheld] Theme Song: "Right Now, Later On" Fabolous Date of Birth: Feb. 8th, 1980 From: Brooklyn, New York Height: 6'3 Weight: 263 lbs. Wrestling Style: Hardcore Brawler, and submissionist when need be. Signature Moves: Gangsta Lean: STF submission The Last Breath: Gutwrench sitdown powerbomb The Hook-Up: Locks the opponent's arms in the ropes then does 5 chops The Ladies Choice: Hardcore Holly's Shattered Dreams Reverse Death Valley Driver DDT Mo' Money: Gullotine Leg Drop to the outside while opponent's head is exposed from under the ring ropes Holla: 10 punches from the top rope Strictly Business: Sharpshooter Finisher Move: The Hostyle Takeover: A powerbomb into facebuster from the top rope Set-Up Move: Code Red: Superkick Current Team: None Past Teams & Groups: None Title History: None Awards History: PWA Number#1 Student PWA Graduate Description: You see that one good looking dude, in that picture up there? That's me. History: As a child, Drew Hostyle wasn't like other kids, he couldn't only attend the school, he had to own it and as an adult he is the same exact way, he MUST own everything he's a part of. Because of his outrageous attics to both his teachers and fellow students, earlier in life he was sent to a juvenile detention home, and later on jail, to have personal guidance with his aggression problems. The best solution they could come up with to solve Drew's problem was wrestling, then he could tear people limb from limb, conquer things and it would all be legal. Drew Hostyle continued to train himself in the art of wrestling even after he was released from jail and began to do it as a profession until he was arrested again for being too extreme during one match-up for an indy federation. After being released from jail a second time, he decided that he'd need to find people who were just extreme as he could be and who wanted dominance just as much as he did. Soon after he met up with a man who would become a long time friend, "Epic" Carlos Serpico, but after they both went through training in the Primetime Wrestling Academy, their careers parted ways and Epic continued his term and soon went on to acheive bigger things, as Drew left for a while to return to the streets and became apart of a "cult" of some sorts, which had essentially brain-washed him, and he still belongs to it today, trying to overcame the pressures of this gang and sucess in the PTC has both granted him Number#1 ratings while in the PWA, eventually graduation, and a brief stints in the NTICW and HIW. And now as he's looking to join the OSW, he plans to show exactly why he says "By hook or by crook, it's ALL in the book".
