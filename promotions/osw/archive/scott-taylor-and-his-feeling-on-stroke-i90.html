Scott Taylor and his feeling on 'Stroke.'

2002-06-29
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scott Taylor and his feeling on 'Stroke.' Written: 6/29/02 'Glory' by Sugar Ray unexpectedly plays on the sound system. The crowd, recognizing the music, instantly pops. The Dallas audience rises to their feet and applauds, hoots, and cheers wildly as, from behind the curtain, Kid Wonder emerges. Robbie: What the hell is that runt doing out here??? Nate: I dunno. He's not scheduled to be here. Steven: But I'm sure glad he showed up. Teehee! Wonder casually walks down the ramp and towards the ring. He doesn't stop to chat with fans, slap hands with them, or anything. All we see in front of us is a focused Kid Wonder as he rolls into the ring. The teen idol immediately motions for a microphone. Nate: Looks like Kid Wonder has something to say. Steven: Goodie! I hope he tells everyone how much he loves me! Robbie: Ugh! After being handed a mic by the trusty ring announcer, K-Dub motions for the crowd to quiet down some. Once they oblige, Wonder puts the mic up to his mouth and begins talking. Wonder: You know, for a city that plays home to that super ass wad, Tyler Nelson… Eruption of boos from the Dallas crowd at the mention of the PCW scumbag owner’s name. Wonder (continuing): …and close to the home of that other ass wad, Jon Hull… Mixed reaction for the PCW Hall of Fame member. Wonder (still continuing): …this city ain’t half bad. The crowd pops recognizing Wonder’s attempt at a cheap pop. Robbie: The only pops this fruit can get are cheap pops. Steven: I’d like to pop my… Steven is interrupted when Robbie SMACKS him. In the ring, Kid Wonder slowly paces back and forth while addressing the fans. Wonder: But I didn’t come out here to suck up to you all. I came out here to express my concern. I came out here to express dissatisfaction with my mistreatment since coming to OSW. Steven: I’ll never mistreat you my sweet. Nate sighs Wonder: On average, I’m being booked about once a month. Robbie: That’s one time too many for me. Nate: Show some respect, Decker. Robbie: What’s to respect about a cookie cutter? Wonder: Now when I came in, I didn’t expect too much. Being the new guy and all, it kinda comes with the turf. But as the weeks went on, I found myself being less and less involved. Not as much by my choosing as by the choosing of the poor management of OSW. You’d think being the guy to put the final nail in the rival’s casket would make them appreciate me some. Robbie: Does any of this have a point? Or has he just come out here to cry us a river to drown Steven in. K-Dub stops pacing. He scratches the bridge of his nose with his free hand. The crowd is relatively quiet, waiting for Wonder to continue. Wonder: Then came this past Sunday at Ascension. Loud pop from the crowd just at the mention of the great event that was OSW Ascension. Wonder: I took part in one of the most hellacious matches to ever take place on any wrestling show. Of course, I’m talking about the Mt. St. Hell’s End match. Even louder pop from the crowd as they remember the big main event from this past Sunday. Wonder: Not only do I compete in that match…not only do I do well in that match…but I arguably do better than anyone else in that match! I stood on top of that mountain longer than anybody else. And if not for my own move, I might’ve been up there at the end, too. Pop from the crowd at the thought of KW standing on top of the mountain at the end. Wonder lets out a small smile as the crowd keeps cheering. Wonder: I’m not gonna complain about not winning because there was no shady ending to the match. I wasn’t there at the end and I’ll leave it at that. Robbie: And for the OSW title’s sake, I’m glad he wasn’t. Nate: What do you mean? Kid Wonder would make a credible OSW champion. Robbie: No he wouldn’t. Nate: Yes he would. Robbie: No he wouldn’t! Nate: Yes he would! Steven: He’d make a credible partner for me. Nate and Robbie: Shut up, Steven! Wonder: Heh. You’d think making that big of an impact on the biggest match in OSW history would earn a man a little respect. You’d think it’d earn me at least a spot on the card this week. But low and behold, I’m not booked. Robbie: And for good reason, too. Wonder gets a smirk on his face. Wonder: So since OSW management didn’t want to put me on the show, I’ve had to put myself on there. Wonder runs and hops onto the middle turnbuckle. He looks right out towards the fans in Reunion Arena. Wonder: You all wanna see Kid Wonder in a match tonight? Huge pop from the crowd. They sound like they want an impromptu match. Kid Wonder hops off the turnbuckle. He walks and stands at the ropes, leaning forward against them. He looks out towards the entrance while still holding the mic. Wonder: I know a lot of you in the back love talking smack about me behind my back. Well, here I am. So if any of you jackasses back there really want a piece of me, I’m not going anywhere. Come on and bring it. Let’s see what you’re really made of. K-Dub continues to lean against the ropes looking at the entrance ramp. The fans are still cheering loudly awaiting someone to answer the cal. Will it be Tom Walczak? Rhett Locke? Maybe El Drago? Maybe even the Cruiserweight Champion himself, Corg? Robbie: Is he done with his bitching yet? I want to see a match, damnit! Nate: Don't you get it Robbie? Wonder is trying to prove a point. He's trying to say 'Look, I'm here in OSW. Let me wrestle more than one show per month.' Robbie: And that is supposed to concern me? Steven: Mmm. You guys should look at the ring more. That is one hell of a view. Robbie: SHUT UP STEVEN! As Wonder continues to hold his place in the ring there is a disturbance in the crowd. Fans are being pushed to the side forming a walkway of sorts. There is a little cheering heard, but it's so low that it would be hard for the crew to pick it up. Suddenly someone appears in the front row next to the announce position. The person jumps over the guard rail and sprints towards the ring. The mystery person is wearing a pair of black denim jeans and a plain red tee shirt, long black hair is hanging down covering his face, making it hard for the cameras and the announcers to get a clear look at him. Robbie: Who the hell is that?! Steven: I sure want to know. He's such a cutie, especially from this angle. Robbie: Damn you, Steven! Will you stop looking at the wrestler's asses already? Steven: Sorry, I just can't help myself sometimes. Nate: I know who that is! That's the former NTICW World Champion, Scott Taylor! Robbie: What's an NTICW guy doing here? Taylor slides in under the bottom rope and approaches Wonder. Wonder continues to look out at the entrance ramp completely unaware of the person behind him. Taylor has his knees bent and looks as if he's ready to kick K-Dub's head off at any moment. Wonder: Come on. I know you guys aren't afraid of me. Someone better come out here, or I'm going back there! Nate: Turn around Wonder! Turn around! Robbie: Yes, do turn around! Do turn around! As if Nate and Robbie's words have some sort of subliminal quality to them Wonder does turn around. Wonder has little time to react as he sees the crouching Scott Taylor waiting. Taylor lunges forward with his right leg, aiming a super kick at the jaw of The Teen Idol. Foot connects with chin as Kid Wonder goes flying back hitting the mat. The crowd erupts into boos at this sight. Robbie: OH! WONDER'S HEAD! FIFTH ROW! CHEAP SECTION! Nate: That's not funny Robbie! Robbie: Yes it is! That NTICW guy just super kicked Wonder to hell and back. Nate: I don't know what's going on here. But Scott Taylor has just came in from the crowd and attacked Kid Wonder! Steven: I always wanted to get Kid Wonder down on the floor.... Robbie and Nate: Quiet Steven! In the ring, the attack continues. Taylor grabs Wonder by his right leg and pulls him towards the center of the ring. He then runs towards the ropes and goes down into a roll as he returns towards the fallen Teen Idol. As Taylor reaches his feet again he uses the momentum to hurl his body backwards, landing a Standing Shooting Star Press on Wonder. Robbie: Ow. This Taylor kid may be a gold digger but he's sure teaching Kid Wonder a lesson. Nate: You call sneak attacking a fan favorite gold digging? Robbie: Damn straight. What else would he be attacking this idiot for? Steven: Wow! I've got to learn how to do that! Nate: Steven, you aren't going to use that for wrestling either, are you? Steven: Nope. You can just imagine what I'd use that for. At the same time both Nate and Robbie undergo a cold chill and then slide their chairs away from Steven a little. Back in the ring Taylor is still in control of the attack. He grabs a handful of Wonder's blonde hair and jerks him up to his feet by it. As Wonder stands Taylor kicks him in the stomach and then grabs his arms, spinning him around into a reverse double underhook. The fans are still showing their outage by booing Taylor. Taylor shows no remorse and ignores the fans. He jumps to the second rope and uses it a springboard, jumping from it and spinning around as he drops Kid Wonder flat on his face. Taylor stands to his feet and walks over to where Kid Wonder dropped the microphone, he bends over and picks it up. However he doesn't speak just yet. He walks back over to where Kid Wonder lays and looks down at him, getting in one good kick to the ribs. Taylor: Hey Wonder, like a washed up boy band. You've fallen flat on your face. The fans illicit a cheap heat pop. Taylor: You want to come out here and talk about not having matches and not being used properly? Looks like you just got some good OSW air time you teeny bopper. Robbie: Hah! Taylor called him a Teeny Bopper! Nate: This in't right Robbie and you know it isn't. Taylor: I'll tell you what Wonder Boy, you want someone to pay attention to you? You want someone to give you some air time? Well junior, you've had your attention. You have the stroke and the name value. And it's people like you that have kept that from people like me. But not anymore Wonder. Now like the old saying goes. You better be careful what you wish for, becuase you just mgiht get it. Your wish has just come true. The staticy beginning of 'Whatever' by Godsmack begins to play as Taylor drops the microphone. He looks down at Wonder who is still not moving. Just to add insult to injury Taylor stomps on Wonder a few more times. Taylor smiles at the havoc he has created, throwing his arms up into the air and taking in the heat as if he's feeding off of it. The song continues to play as he steps through the ring ropes and heads up the ramp. Nate: Scott Taylor is here guys, and it looks like he's ready to stay. Robbie: Gold Digger, but a very entertaining one. So I guess he's ok, even though he used to be from NTICW. Steven: The fresh meat is of a good cut, I can see. I'm pleased. Steven is SMACKED by Robbie Decker again. Steven: Owies! Robbie: Serves you right.
