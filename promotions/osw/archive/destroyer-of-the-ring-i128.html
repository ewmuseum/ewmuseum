Destroyer of the Ring...

2002-05-05
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Destroyer of the Ring... Written: May 5th, 2002 The two tiny managers of Heresian Bloodlust, Caleb and Minion, crawl over the security barrier and immediately make a dash for the ring. Instead of crawling under the ropes though they both dive under the apron. Nate: What the hell are they doing? Steven: I'm not sure, but it always seems that we're asking that very week about those two! Robbie: Screw that! When those two appear that usually means Cain and Hessian are nearby! Someone's going to DIE!!! Riptah hasn't noticed that the two managers are here though and he continues his assault on Masters by starting to lay the boots to the suburban's ribcage! Nate: Where are those two little things? Riptah leans over and pulls Masters up to his feet, but Masters delivers a quick to the big man's abdomen and hits him a Russian leg sweep. Riptah hits the mat hard. Nate: Masters is in control! Robbie: Will those two idiots do something already? Steven: Here they come! Caleb and Minion appear from underneath the mat on opposite sides of the ring. Each of them have a rope in their hands that are tied to something underneath the ring. Nate: Why do they have those ropes? Steven: Screw that! What are those ropes tied to? Inside the ring, Masters is down on top of Riptah now and beating down Da Westsida! Meanwhile Hawke's head is being slammed into the turnbuckle by Short Circuit! Caleb and Minion tug on the ropes sharply. Nothing happens though. Caleb and Minion look frustrated. Minion gets an idea though and whistles to someone on the other side of the barrier. Nate: Who is he signaling to? The Goliath, Hessian appears from behind the barrier though and crawls over top only to take the rope from Minion's hand. Cain appears from backstage area sprinting down the ramp. He gets to Caleb and steals the rope from the small hobo. Cain and Hessian look at one another and tug on the ropes with great force. Nate: What the hell?! There is a large snap sound heard underneath the ring though! Steven: Oh God! Suddenly, the center of the ring collapses! Civil Disobedience and Da Westsidaz are launched into the air and crash down onto the now slanted mat only to slide to the center of the ring. Nate: What the hell? Steven: Can someone please tell me what just happened? Robbie: HA! Cain and Hessian release their grips on the ropes and approach the ring. Caleb pulls Cain's sledgehammer out from behind the barrier and hands it to the beast. Minion hands Hessian a steal chair! Cain and Hessian climb up into the collapsed ring. Nate: Heresian Bloodlust is here to get some revenge for last week! Robbie: Suddenly, this match just got very interesting! Steven: This is going to be ugly! Short Circuit is climbing to his feet, but he is quickly slammed back down to the mat as a result of a chair shot from Hessian. Riptah lunges at Hessian, but his efforts are quickly thwarted by a shot to the back from Cain and his sledgehammer. Cain and Hessian look onto Masters and Hawke! Robbie: Someone is going to die! Cain pulls the sledgehammer back and brings it down onto the gut of Masters that causes him to spit blood out of his mouth. Hawke rolls over only for his eyes to grow very wide as he sees Hessian standing over him with the steel chair pulled back over his head. Hessian brings the chair crashing down onto Hawke's head repeatedly rendering him unconscious. The fans are booing wildly at the top of their lungs! Nate: There is no doubt about who got the biggest response from the crowd this week! Robbie: They just decimated Civil Disobedience and Da Westsidaz!! Steven: BOCA~! Daisy rushes to the ring, but she is quickly stopped by Caleb and Minion. Caleb pulls a mic out of his pocket and tosses it to Hessian. Hessian catches the mic in order to address the crowd. Hessian: That's right all of you damn peon! It's your Tag Team Champions! We just thought that we would come out and greet all of fans! The boos are echoing throughout the arena. Hessian: Last week, Cain and I fulfilled our promise of a Dark Sabbath for the Red Army! Nate: Yeah, but it didn't hurt that that little hobo was the ref. Robbie: Show some respect to the champs, Nate! Hessian: We laid waste to Civil Disobedience in order to win these belts, we our titles against Propaganda and the Global Champion Vampir Nosferatu, and we ruined Krieg and Walczak last week in order to retain our belts! And then our victory is cut short by the likes of these four men! Tonight, we have shown everyone that there is no one that can stop us! Hessian tosses the mic to Cain. Cain: Week in and week out, we have promised Carnage! And week in and week out we have delivered on our promises! If the acting president Ed wants to bring the five time PCW champions to OSW in an attempt to remove these titles from our wastes so be it! That is just more fuel for the fire! There is no team in the OSW that can stop our reign of terror! Robbie: HA! Cain: We have just show you all what happens to those that interfere in our affairs! Last week these punks decided to get themselves some attention by jumping us after our victory. Cain lays a fierce kick into Masters head and then continues. Cain: This is only the beginning of our reign of terror. If you want these titles boys, you are going to need more than steel chairs and your skills to stop us! When you step into the ring with Heresian Bloodlust, you better come prepared for a war! Because a war is what you are going to get! Cain drops the mic on top of the bodies of the fallen tag teams. 'Et Fortuna' from Carmina Burana begins to blast the sound system of the arena as the fans boos are still echoing. Cain and Hessian exit the ring. As the pass over their victims, Cain and Hessian make sure to lay one more kick in for good measure and spit on the tag teams. Nate: Well, it seems that Civil Disobedience and Da Westsidaz hit a nerve last week! Robbie: Yeah and seems that Cain and Hessian just cut that nerve out! HA! Steven: Uh, what about Daisy? Hessian and Cain stop where Minion and Caleb are holding onto Daisy who is fighting with all of her strength to get free, but to no avail. Cain raises his massive hand up and starts to caress the young ladies face with it. She is still trying to fight to get free. Nate: Oh come on! Haven't they already proved their point? Robbie: Nope! Steven: Kill the Skirt! A slow smile stretches across Cain's face. Cain pulls his hand back only to bring it crashing back into Daisy's face with fierce bitch slap that sends her collapsing to the concrete floor crying hysterically. Nate: Good God! Robbie: HA! Steven: Fucking Skirts! Hessian and Azrael Cain begin to walk up the ramp with Minion and Cable following closely behind them. Before they disappear into the backstage area though they stop and the entryway and remove their belts. Cain and Hessian hold their titles up high into the air in order to egg the crowd's anger on further. After a moment, all four of the demonic figures walk back into the backstage area. Nate: Well, Heresian Bloodlust certainly made their presence felt! Robbie: Their presence? They also made a steel chair, sledgehammer and a fierce bitch slap felt tonight! Steven: That was great! Nate: Well, let's get to a commercial while the crew tries to get the ring ready for the next match! Robbie and Steven's laughter can be heard as they the scenes fades to black.
