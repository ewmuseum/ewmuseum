Corg vs. Bethany Yamato vs. Aimz

Corg vs. Bethany Yamato vs. Aimz Written: June 8, 2002 ‘Chemical’ by No One hits and the crowd of hip Seattle goers….as much as wrestlng fans can be considered ‘hip’ not so much as boos him as they kind make a indescipherable pop for the Goth. Corg sneers at them and slides into the ring. Earl Heinrich: This Three-Way Dance is scheduled for one fall… Making his way to the ring, hailing from Detroit, Michigan, weighing in one hundred and seventy-two pounds… He is the Silent Killer…CORG! ‘Terrible Lie’ by Nine Inch Nails hits and Bethany Yamato stalks down the ramp, and as always, the crowd pops for women who have posed nude. Earl: His first opponent, hailing from Los Angeles, California…weighing in at one hndred and seventy-six pounds… The Midnight Angel… BETHANY YAMATO! Robbie: THAT”S A BIG BITCH~! Nate: Huh? Robbie: Dude, she weighs more than Corg! That’s one hefty chick! Nate: That’s muscle, you moron. Robbie: More like retained Twinkies! Nate: Sigh… ‘Only The Strong’ by FLAW hits, and Amy Campebell appears on the stage, and the crowd pops hard for the…’plucky little redheaded girl’. Robbie: Is it me, or does this chick change entrance music every ten fucking minutes? Nate: I think it’s just you. Aimz runs down and slides into the ring…only to be brutally kicked in the head by Yamato, who is in turn speared by Corg. Robbie: Ugh, just a trainwreck. Corg begins smashing his fists into the back of Yamato’s head, who falls off Aimz in a heap. Corg kneels down and smashes his knee into Yamato’s head, drawing a sympathetic response to Bethany’s pain. Nate: Well, no dancing around the subject here… Aimz crawls away, holding her cheek while Corg just beats the living crap out of Bethany. Aimz turns her head to see this, and shakily gets to her feet, Bethany’s kick doing a little more damage than expected. As Corg continues the beatdown, Aimz leaps on the ropes and splashes down on Bethany and Corg. Nate: Big high-risk offense from Aimz, and she may have just bought Bethany some much needed time! Robbie: Looks like Bethany’s gonna need more than time – she’s gonna need a plastic surgeon! Aimz rolls off the heap of bodies, and unfortunately for Bethany, Aimz weight crushed Corg down on top of her. Corg gets to his feet, unfettered, and kicks a boot into Aimz’s midsection, flopping her over. Aimz’s face contorts in pain and she exhales through gritted teeth as the wind is knocked out of her… Corg stands over her, triumphantly… Until Bethany hits the Low Blow. Corg crumples like a rag doll with a strangled screech, hitting the mat. Robbie: Owww… That was uncalled for. Corg lies on the mat, whimpering. Steven: You don’t suppose she might of…disturbed a relatively new piercing…could she? Robbie: Oh…Oh God, that’s horrid. I’ve seen pictures of dude’s piercing their junk…. Nate: So what you’re saying is that you look at pictures of dude’s dicks, am I correct? Steven: And, your point is? Robbie: I DO NOT LOOK AT DUDE’S JUNK JUST TO LOOK AT THEM! I WAS RESEARCHING AN IMPORTANT TOPIC! Nate: Yeah, right…. Corg lies on the mat in a heap, and Bethany stalks over his quivering form to attack Aimz, who retaliates with a sweep kick, knocking Bethany on her ass. Aimz crawls on top of her and begins pummeling Bethany with a flurry of punches. Nate: Corg doesn’t look too healthy here… Robbie: Well, he is pasty white. Steven: That might be from too much blunt trauma to the sack. Nate: Nevertheless, he is not in fighting condition right now. Robbie: Just goes to show you, never trust a woman to treat your dick with respect. Steven: INDEED! That’s a MAN’S job! Robbie shivers uncontrollably. Bethany and Aimz roll on the mat, clawing and punching each other. Nate: This is more of a cat fight than a wrestling match! Robbie: Understandably so. These two cock-hungry bitches… Nate: That’s enough Dirty talk, Robbie. Robbie: What? Bethany kicks Aimz of her and Bethany falls to the mat, only to regroup and spring at Aimz, who drives her knee into Bethany’s face. Bethany jerks away, clutching what looks like a busted open nose. Corg slowly rises to his feet, spins Aimz around… Nate: DEATH BLOOMS! DEATH BLOOMS! Robbie: You sound like a cultist. Nate: CORG HAS THAT FATAL SUBMISSION MOVE LOCKED IN ON AIMZ! THERE IS NO WAY SHE’S GONNA GET OUT OF THAT! To be sure, Aimz is flagging in the hold, Corg screaming as he maintains the lock. The referee kneels down to check on Bethany’s broken nose, who has slipped out of the ring, holding a wad of cloth up to her face, the cloth soaked through with blood. The ref slides out of the ring and checks on her, instructing her to tilt up her head… Meanwhile, Aimz begins slapping her hand against Corg’s arm, making the sign of submission, however the ref isn’t there to see it. Corg angrily throws Aimz to the mat and calls out to the ref. Robbie: Corg had this match won, just technicalities kept the referee out of the ring… Nate: Hey, you win some, you lose some. The ref turns around and argues with Corg, who is cursing heartily at the official. Steven: LOOK! The announcers turn their heads, and Seth Masters leaps out of the crowd and runs into the ref. He begins screaming at the ref, and pointing at the ring. Seth: DO YER JOB, DICKSMACK! Nate: Wow, Seth sure is passionate about the way the match is going!!! Robbie: Heh. He said ‘dicksmack’. Steven: (purring) Yes he did….grrrr…. Meanwhile, Trevor Hawke slides in opposite to Corg. He helps Aim to her feet and hands her a small object. Nate: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON HERE? WHY IS CIVIL DISOBEDIANCE HELPING OUT AMY CAMPBELL? Robbie: Maybe they just got nothing better to do tonight? Nate: Yeah, right! This is a screwing-to-be! Steven: Anything involving Aimz is a screwing to be…. Nate: Heh…That’s pretty witty… Steven: I thought so. Corg turns around and Aimz is ready. She uses the object and sprays something into Corg’s eyes! Robbie: Uh… BLINDING MIST~! Sure is BLINDING MIST~! Corg staggers back, frantically wiping away the acidic compound that Amy sprayed directly into his eyes. He staggers back, bounces slightly off the ropes…and catches a MASSIVE superkick to the face, connecting solidly with his jaw, dropping him like a steer hit with a poleax. The Crowd: Ooooooooo!!! Nate: Its hard for me to side with Corg, but what the hell is going on here?!?! Robbie: It’s a load of crap whatever it is… Seth spins the ref around and points at Amy Campbell making the cover. The ref slides in, and when the ref is making the count, Seth glowers at Bethany. He kicks her in the stomach and hits the Primo Plex! Bethany crashes down onto the concrete with a sickening thud! 1! 2! 3! Winner: Amy Campbell DING! DING! DING! Nate: CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE JUST SCREWED CORG AND YAMATO! I AM NOT SURE WHAT TO SAY?!?! Robbie: Heh heh… Is there anyone who hasn’t screwed Yamato? Steven coughs. Nate: WHAT DOES THIS MEAN, CIVIL D AND AIMZ TOGETHER? Robbie: Calm down, Dumas! It’s just a screwing, you see this everyday! Well, maybe YOU don’t… Nate: Civil Disobedience had no right to come down here and interfere in this match, even if Corg and Bethany Yamato are the filth of the earth!!! We need to head to a commercial!!! Hopefully this will all be sorted by the time we return. We’ll be right back!!! The camera shifts to the ring as Seth and Trevor slide into the ring and help a battered and tired Amy Campbell to her feet. Her eyes thoughtfully regard the two former OSW Tag Team Champions as the scene fades to commercial.
