Jesus St Crux v Kid Wonder

2002-05-18
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jesus St Crux v Kid Wonder Written: May 18, 2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam! We are just about ready to head to the ring for our first match of the evening, which is a first round cruiserweight tournament match, featuring two of the preeminent lightweight wrestlers in the world today!!! Steven: And two of the hottest men in OSW at that, as Kid is so smooth and cute, while the fire of Latino blood pumps through the veins of Jesus St Crux. Nate: Robbie? Robbie: Yes? Nate: Don't ever let Steven follow my lead ins with commentary again. Robbie: Sorry, I was eating a soft pretzel. Nate: (sighing) Why do I even bother? Let's send it up in the ring for our opening bout. Suddenly, 'Glory' by Sugar Ray begins to blast over the speakers. And with the music, to the delight of many fans, Kid Wonder and Amanda step out from the back. Wonder races down to the ring and slides under the ropes, while Amanda takes her time and waves to a few perverted marks cheering for her. Steven: Wooo hoooo!!! Kiddy!!! Robbie: Wake me up when this match is over. 'Fever Dog' by Stillwater plays over the P.A. shortly after, and sending most of the female audience into a pure ecstatic state, Jesus St Crux steps out from the backstage. With a smile he walks down the ramp, as Caridad follows behind him. Jesus vaults over the top rope, and after landing softly on his feet, he taunts Wonder by spreading his arms out and offering Wonder to make the first move. . . . . . And he does, bouncing off the ropes, and launching a huge dropkick. However Jesus side-steps, bounces off the ropes himself, and does a baseball slide straight into Wonder's head. Wonder grabs his head and rolls in pain, while Jesus tucks, rolls, and does a huge splash after jumping in the air from the roll onto Kid Wonder's body. With a slight grin, Jesus steps out of the ropes and onto the apron. He climbs to the top turnbuckle, non chalantly. Nate: LOOK AT WONDER! Robbie: OH HELL! St Crux squeals in pain after Wonder, who had been playing possum, fell into the ropes, causing St Crux to fall groin first on the turnbuckle. The Crowd: Ooooooooooooo!!! Nate: Ouch!!! Robbie: YOWZA!!!! Steven: NOT THE LOVE SOURCE!!! With one swift movement, Wonder jumps on the turnbuckle, and launches off, moonsaulting onto Jesus. Staying on top of his fallen victim, Wonder motions for Referee Pierre Gaston to make the count Pierre: Une! Du! Tro- Kickout. Nate: Almost a quick ending to this match Robbie: Damnit Gaston, count faster. Steven: These two...I dunno who to pick. Both so vagrant, so hot...so unimaginably sexy that I just want to take my fat co-- Nate: Hey, Steven. Shut. The. (bleep). Up. Wonder takes the aftermath opportunity by quickly lifting Jesus St Crux up, and irish-whipping him into the rope. St Crux, conscious enough to make a comeback, does so with a vengeance as he leaps into the air and onto Wonder's shoulders, following up with a huge flying headscissor takedown. The fans cheer for the fast paced action, while St Crux runs into the ropes, bouncing on the second rope, then the third, only to spin into a modified form of his well-known somersault legdrop. Nate: HOLY HELL! Steven: If St Crux can do moves like THAT in the ring, I wonder what moves he can pull under the covers. Dead air for 1...2...3...4...5 seconds. Robbie: I think I'm gonna throw-up Nate: Ditto. St Crux had gone for the cover, however Kid Wonder was just able to kick out. Frustrated and tired, St Crux lifts Kid Wonder, and holds him high for a Crucifix Powerbomb. However, Kid Wonder breaks free, reversing the move into a hurracanrana. Kid Wonder hooks St Crux's legs from behind, but at the count of two, St Crux flips Wonder over into a sunset flip's pin position. Before the count of three, Kid Wonder rolls backwards and out of the pin. Following up, Wonder grabs St Crux's legs, flips over him, and tries for a three count once more. But to no avail. Nate: Great Action by both men! Kid Wonder, outraged, stands up and begins derailing St Crux with rights and lefts, St Crux returns with a kick, but it's caught by Kid Wonder. The crowd is going wild!! Inverted Enziguri! The sheer sound of the kick around the back of Kid Wonder's head was heard around the world, while Wonder dropped like a sack of shit to the mat. Nate: This match is definately Jesus' Robbie: Thank god it's nearly over... Steven: I hope my baby is okay! However, before St Crux could make the count, the sound of 'Hate to Say I Told You So' by the Hives blasts over the P.A. system. St Crux instantly looks up, knowing full well who is coming to spoil the broth. Ian Wright. Robbie: It's about time that talent got introduced to this damn match! The 6'3' descendent of Ireland rushes to the ring. St Crux motions to the referee, and Pierre Gaston does his job. However, Jesus is also too busy making sure that Ian doesn't enter the ring. . . Nate: LOOKOUT! Kid Wonder runs Jesus into the ropes, and he himself rolls him up. Pierre catches this... 1... 2... 3! Winner: Kid Wonder Ian smiles as he walks back up the entrance ramp. Nate: Damnit, no! Wright ruined a great match!!! Robbie: Quit pissing yourself, at least we can move on to the next match, which will most likely be as equally crappy. Nate: Jerkoff. Steven: Eh? Nate: SHUT UP! Amidst the bickering and Kid Wonder's celebration, Queen of Extreme Bethany Yamato has found her way to the ring. Steven: LOOK!!! She climbs up onto the apron, and as Kid Wonder comes to stop her, he was slapped in the face. Kid Wonder, with the rage of a dragon. . . *SMACK!* Back-hands the hell out of her, all the way off the apron. Steven: Yeah, bitchslap that whore!!! Nate: LOOK OUT WONDER! KW: OOMPH~! Out of no where, the massive 500 pound sumo wrestler, Kimbusa splashes into Kid Wonder. His massive body literally sandwiches Wonder between him and the turnbuckle. Nate: YAMATO WAS JUST A DISTRACTION FOR KIMBUSA TO ATTACK KID WONDER!!! THIS IS AN OUTRAGE!!! Robbie: WOOOOOO! KIMBUSAAAAAA~! Nate: DAMN YOU ROBBIE! STRAIGHT TO HELL! STRAIGHT TO HELL! Kimbusa and Bethany Yamato (whom still has Kid Wonder's handprint on her cheek) celebrate in the ring as 'Tiger River' plays over the P.A. system. Nate: Kid Wonder might have won the match, but the victory certainly belongs to Kimbusa. We need to head to a commercial. We will be right back after these messages. The scene shifts to Kimbusa and Bethany Yamato, as they make their way up the ramp, and head towards the back, as the scene fades to commercial.
