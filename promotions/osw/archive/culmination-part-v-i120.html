Culmination: Part V

2002-05-25
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Culmination: Part V Written: 25/05/2002 The fans in the arena buzz about the night so far on Slam! With all the action so far on Slam! and the brutal main event on the way the adrenaline is evident in the sold out Pyramid. Nate: Well folks it’s been a wild night so far on Slam! and we’re almost ready for our main event. With so much tension between all the teams, from Ivan and Reno to Rhett and GV, you know there’s going to be blood lost and names taken in this formidable match up! Robbie: That and they’re going to be shut inside that big ol’ Cell hanging above the ring there. Steven: The tension is so thick! You could cut it with a-……… Robbie: Less of that Smith!!! Nate: In any ca-… Nate is cut off suddenly as ‘Ante Up Remix’ by MOP & Funkmaster Flex booms throughout the arena. The fans burst into an uproar as Riptah and Short Circuit emerge from behind the curtain to a loud ovation. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome, PCW Fall of Famers, DA WESTSIDAZ!!!!!!!!! Steven: Da Westsidaz? They’re not scheduled… Robbie: Bah! Riptah is carrying the two burlap sacks containing the fidgeting Caleb and the still-dazed Minion. Short Circuit looks over the crowds and gestures to them, receiving a loud cheer. Nate: Well Da Westsidaz are on their way out here, but what the hell is that Riptah is carrying? Robbie: Hey…no…they wouldn’t… Short Circuit slides into the ring, while Riptah throws the two burlap sacks into the ring, then climbs up the steel steps, and enters the ring. Earl graciously hands Riptah his microphone then exits the ring, as Riptah kicks the two burlap sacks and puts the mic to his lips. Riptah: It seems to me, that Heresian Bloodlust and Civil Disobedience are both underestimating Shorty and I. Nate: Riptah isn’t wasting any time getting to the point anyway… Riptah: Sure, Civil Disobedience and Shorty and me had a fight, how did that end? DQ via Hessian and Cain. Da Westsidaz are then scheduled to fight Heresian Bloodlust FOR THE OSW TAG TEAM TITLES!!! How did that end? IT ENDED WITH DA WESTSIDAZ BEATING THE HELL OUT OF ALL OF THEM!!! Civil D lock us in our locker room, every single week it’s been nothing but backstage antics or disqualifications, well now it’s going to get more serious. Riptah takes a breath and looks at the burlap sack, then motions for Short Circuit to open them. Pulling at the rope, the bodies of Caleb and Minion fall out, and the crowd gasp at the mistreatment of the lovable duo. Riptah: Y’see Cain, Hessian…you two are yet to actually face Shorty and I, I mean OK, the DQ’s and such count for something, but not a show of skills or talent. So far we’ve been playing games and now it’s going to get a little rough. There’s one thing we want boys, and you know what it is…now give it to us or your Imp and Hobo suffer………I’m waiting… Riptah pockets the mic, and then begins kicking Caleb in the ribs, while Short Circuit grabs Minion, but as he goes to lift him in the air, Minion lunges forward and grabs Short Circuit by his testicles. Nate: IMPIN’ AIN’T EASY!!! OH MY GOD MINION JUST LATCHED ONTO SHORT CIRCUIT’S NADS AND SC IS IN INTOLERABLE PAIN!!! Short Circuit doubles over, trying to cradle his scrotum, while Minion squeezes SC’s family jewels. Seeing this, Riptah runs over to Minion, and swiftly kicks the Imp in the gut, sending him flying across the ring and into the turnbuckle. The crowd gasp, then go silent, as Riptah grabs the microphone and yells at the Jumbotron. Riptah: I’LL KILL THEM!!! GET YOUR ASSES OUT HERE AND GIE US AN ANSWER DAMMIT! WE WANT A FAIR MATCH FOR THO-………… Daisy: HELP ME!!! OH GOD PLEASE HELP ME SOMEBODY!!!!!! AAAAAAAAAAAAAARGH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The Jumbotron suddenly fizzes up, and shows the image of Daisy, being held by the hair by Cain. In the darkness, Daisy screams, tears flooding down her eyes, standing in awkwardly. Riptah gasps, his jaw dropping at what he sees before him. Suddenly, the masked head of Hessian comes into view, and the crowd boo hysterically. Hessian: Mmmmmm…how sweet she does smell. You know I wonder what she tastes like… Hessian opens his mouth and leans towards Daisy’s face, and a rather large, red tongue protrudes from Hessian’s mouth, and moves up Daisy’s face, from her jaw to her ear. Licking at her, a trail of warm, saliva glimmers in the camera’s light, as Hessian pulls away and licks his lips. Nate: SOMEONE CALL THE COPS!!! Steven: Look at the size of his tongue!!! There is dead air for 1…2…3… seconds. Hessian: She tastes so sweet…and you know Riptah, for all that you have done to Minion and Caleb, Daisy has a hell of a debt to repay for your insolence. Then again, why hurt her when we could please ourselves wi-… Riptah: YOU (expletive) (expletive) (expletive) (expletive) (expletive)!!! LEAVE HER ALONE!!! WHERE THE HELL ARE YOU!!! I’LL KILL YOU FOR THIS YOU (expletive)!!! Steven: I hope he don’t talk to his momma with that mouth! Robbie: Is that even possible… Nate: Pray for Daisy…this could get ugly… Cain releases Daisy, who stumbles, but is caught by Hessian, who grabs her by the hair, and begins stroking her face lovingly. Cain: What was that Riptah? Where are we? Well tell you what, if you let Minion and Caleb go, I’ll tell you where we are. Then you can get your precious Daisy back…before Hessian decides to show her some real lovin’… Nate: Whoa! Strong words by Cain, that’s gotta hurt Riptah’s dignity! Steven: Heh…wouldn’t it surprise you that Minion is the mo-… Robbie: WE KNOW!!! Just shut up! Riptah: FINE THEN!!! MINION, CALEB, GET YOUR (expletive) ASSES OUT OF HERE!!! Caleb climbs to his feet, and runs over to Minion. Picking the dazed Imp up, they leave the ring and slowly make their way up the ring, and through the curtain, as the crowd mourn their pain. Riptah: OK I let them go now GIMME BACK DAISY!!! Cain: Oh yeah Daisy…you want her…well… Riptah: TELL ME NOW!!! WHERE THE HELL ARE YOU!!! GIMME BACK MY DAISY!!! YOU HURT HER IN ANY WAY AND I’LL KILL YOU I SWEAR!!! Cain: You want to know where we are…well just look up… Riptah’s eyes widen in horror, and he slowly glances up at the ceiling, past the Hell in a Cell to the rafters, and gasps as looking back down at him are Hessian and Cain. Nate: OH MY GAWD!!!! HERESIAN BLOODLUST IS ON TOP OF THE CELL!!!! Daisy screams down to Riptah, while Hessian holds her by the throat over the edge of one of the beams, and Cain waves smugly down at Riptah. Looking up the ramp to the Jumbotron, Short Circuit notices Minion and Caleb making their way up a large iron ladder leading to the rafters. Short Circuit: Riptah look! Riptah sees Minion and Caleb, and wasting no time, darts through the ropes and races up the ramp, closely followed by Short Circuit. Racing past the curtain, Riptah and Short Circuit stop and look to the right, and spot the huge ladder nestled between some technical equipment. Looking up, Riptah spots Minion and Caleb just reaching the rafters, and carefully moving across to where Hessian and Cain are. Nate: Does anyone else think that ladder shouldn’t be here? Robbie: I assume it was put there for Heresian Bloodlust… Steven: Of course it’s not meant to be there…but it is, and that’s what counts! Riptah scales the ladder, followed by Short Circuit. As they climb higher and higher, the crowd become increasingly tense, as the risk factor increases with the height. Waiting at the top, in the rafters, Cain and Hessian watch as Minion and Caleb finally stop in front of them. Hessian: Minion, are you all right? Minion the Imp: I’M VINCE McMAHON DAMMIT!!! Cain: Yeah, he’s OK… Caleb: Oh I’m OK, don’t worry about ol’ Caleb, I’ll pull through anything. Only got a few bruised ribs, nothing to worry about. Caleb will pu-……… Cain: Nobody gives a (expletive) about you, thief! Caleb: Bugger…thought you’d have forgotten… Riptah and Short Circuit near the top of the ladder. Climbing furiously up the iron structure to the iron beams of the rafters. Seeing red, Riptah growls and snorts as he climbs up, while the cameraman beside Heresian Bloodlust backs off to a suitable distance and begins filming. Nate: This is going to get really ugly… Robbie: Don’t worry, they’re men they can take it… Steven: Ohhhh you set yourself up there Decker! Robbie: DAMMIT!!! Finally Da Westsidaz reaches the rafters, and leap across the beams to Heresian Bloodlust, while down at the base of the ladder, two other figures brood. Nate: Da Westsidaz are confronting Heresian Bloodlust at a dangerous location! Robbie: Can you smell the ratings! Steven: No but I can smell something else! Calm down Decker it’s not like this is a big thing or anything! Up in the rafters, Riptah and Short Circuit stop a couple of beams before Hessian and Cain, and Minion and Caleb. On the Jumbotron the crowds watch as the cameraman films them from a safe distance, comfortably picking up the sounds. Riptah: Hessian let her go. Hessian: Well OK, you did let Minion and Caleb go… Hessian releases Daisy, but before she can run to Da Westsidaz he grabs her by the neck and holds her over the rafters and she screams out, looking down the 80ft to the ring below. Riptah yells out and jumps one beam next to Heresian Bloodlust. Riptah: LET HER GO DAMMIT!!! Hessian: Really? Can I? Short Circuit: LET HER GO YOU (expletive)!!! Hessian sighs, then pulls Daisy back over onto the beam and lets her go. Breathing heavily and shaking she runs across the beam, skips over to the one Da Westsidaz are standing on, and latches onto Riptah’s arm, crying. Cain: Please, get that little hussy out of here! Riptah kisses Daisy on the head, and then looks up at Cain. Short Circuit and Riptah exchange glances, then Riptah looks at Daisy again. Riptah: Daisy, I need you to go over and wait beside that cameraman, Shorty and me got to take care of some business. Please, come on Daisy; go over there out of the way. Daisy lets go of Riptah and makes her way across the rafters to the cameraman, and waits patiently by his side, while Da Westsidaz and Heresian Bloodlust face off. Nate: This is going to end soon and it’s going to be bloody! Robbie: WOOO!!! Steven: I can’t watch…but I’m paid to so HA! Up in the rafters Short Circuit, Riptah, Hessian and Cain stand before each other, a hatred for each man coursing through their veins. Cain: Well………you said you wanted it…well you can have it. Let’s see…the almighty Westsidaz would like a shot at our tag team titles, a fair shot…what do you think Hessian? Hessian: I think that we should just set ourselves a little match at Asce-……… Voice: HOLD UP DICKSMACKS!!!!!!!!! The voice, belonging to Seth Masters, echoes aro8und the rafters. Da Westsidaz turn to look, as Seth makes his way across the rafters, while Trevor finds his way up the ladder. Struggling to get up, Trevor kicks the ladder, and the crowd gasp as the massive iron ladder falls forward onto the Jumbotron, then slides to the side, clattering against the ground. Trevor: Oops… Nate: Oh how convenient! Now they’re all trapped up there!!! Robbie: Oh my god could it GET any better??? Well…3 of the best tag teams in wrestling today…stuck in the rafters…no way to escape…my guess would be YEAH! Heresian Bloodlust, Da Westsidaz and Civil Disobedience stand looking at each other, while the crowds all boo and cheer hysterically. Seth: No way!!! You are SO not gonna call a match without us being here dammit!!! We were the first frickin’ Tag Champs in OSW! We get NO RESPECT!!! Caleb: Stop masturbating so much and maybe we will give you some respect! Heh! Heh! Heh! Cain: Ok, so be it! Da Westsidaz thinks they can steal Minion and Caleb without any repercussions? Civil Disobedience thinks they deserve everything in the world and Hessian and I are left with a scabby little whore to get our own back! Short Circuit: Look you dumb bitch! Every week we get screwed over ‘cos of some damned interference or you two freaks disqualify us and we haven’t gotten a clean win yet! Trevor: Yeah! Dammit we beat Hessian and Cain at Crowning Glory for those titles and we never even got a rematch!!! Short Circuit: Well freaks, wha-……… Cain: DAMN IT ALL TO HELL!!! Da Westsidaz wants the tag titles? Civil Disobedience wants the tag titles? THEN HESSIAN AND I WILL GLADLY DECIMATE YOU FOR THEM!!! Hessian: Gladly! Heresian Bloodlust will gladly take on Da Westsidaz and Civil Disobedience…AND WE’LL DO IT AT ASCENSION!!! Nate: WELL I’LL BE DAMNED!!! It looks like it’s going to be Heresian Bloodlust vs. Da Westsidaz vs. Civil Disobedience at Ascension!!! Now they need to book the match indefinitely! Robbie: Stipu-… Hessian: However! We’ll leave the stipulation for the coming weeks…keep you all on your feet! Robbie: Oh… Nate: Well that certainly puts a little spin on things… As Heresian Bloodlust turn away to leave, both Cain and Hessian turn back at the last second and knock Da Westsidaz back with two hard rights. Short Circuit and Riptah fall back from their beam onto one of the conjoining platforms. Civil Disobedience look at each other, then dive onto the platform and begin beating down on Da Westsidaz. Nate: THIS IS NEITHER THE PLACE NOR THE TIME FOR A BRAWL!!! Hessian and Cain walk over a beam to another towards the platform, but are stopped in horror as they watch Minion and Caleb leap through the air and land in the middle of Da Westsidaz and Civil Disobedience. Minion begins tugging at Short Circuit’s ears, whilst Caleb slaps Trevor about the face, jiving and punching at him. Robbie: My god…for a homeless man he is kicking ass! Steven: Minion is such a badass! Seth and Riptah begin trading punches, with the Westsida gaining the advantage. As Seth pulls back for a forearm, Riptah knocks him to the platform with a hard, fast spear. Nate: LA X-PRESS!!! Robbie: Why are you looking up Steven? The fight is on the Jumbotron… Steven: I think I can see up Minion’s cloak… Nate & Robbie: SHUT THE HELL UP MAN!!! Minion jumps on Short Circuit and begins smacking him about the face. Then, why SC is dazed, Minion sits on Short Circuit’s head, his cloak covering SC’s head…and then there is the most horrifying sound known to man…ringing out throughout the arena. Robbie: I’m guessing Short Circuit just had a face full of Sour Grapes… Steven: Ugh…even I don’t want to think about that. Hessian and Cain watch on, laughing as Minion and Caleb continue to beat down on Short Circuit and Trevor. Riptah gets up, pulling Seth with him, and then hoists him above his head. Nate: OH HELL NO!!! Robbie: OHHH HEEEEEEEEELL YEEEEAAAAAAH!!! Riptah holds Seth, while Trevor grabs Caleb by the throat and gets up, choking out the Hobo. Minion is pulled to his feet by Short Circuit, who grabs the Imp in a Bear Hug. As Da Westsidaz, Civil Disobedience and Minion and Caleb battle it out on the platform, Hessian and Cain look at each other, then below, a the ring all the way away down below them, then back at each other. Nate: What the… Robbie: I don’t like the looks of this… Steven: GATHP!!! Hessian and Cain take a few steps back, and as Riptah readies to slam Seth onto the platform, he, Short Circuit and Trevor gasp as Hessian and Cain roar out, and leap across the beams towards the platform. The crowd yell out hysterically, as Heresian Bloodlust crash onto Da Westsidaz and Civil Disobedience. Minion and Caleb go flying to the edge, but manage to grab on as they go. Slowly pulling himself back up, Caleb makes it back onto the platform, however Minion is slipping slowly. Steven: NO MINION NOOO!!! Minion’s little Imp hands slowly slide away from the ledge, and the arena falls silent as the 3’11” Imp they call Minion loses his grip and falls down, down, down… Steven & Robbie & Nate: OH MY GOD!!!!!!!!! As Minion falls, the Impish shrieking echoes out throughout the arena, when suddenly…POOF!…Minion’s cloak expands out like a parachute, and the Imp begins slowly floating to the ground. It is at this point that the entire arena bursts out into obscene laughter. Nate: Oh god…are those… Robbie: He never! Steven: HE’S WEARING A PAIR OF AIMZ’ UNDERWEAR!!! Minion immediately turns bright red, and begins kicking, which only makes him float more slowly to the ground. Nate: Hide the women and put the children to bed! This just got FUGLY! Robbie: It’s so ugly…it’s FUGLY! Steven: So Nate implied… Minion finally hits the ground, then pulls his cloak down and skitters up the ramp and into the backstage area. On the platform, Hessian and Cain get up and grab both Trevor and Short Circuit. Hoisting them high into the air by the throats, Hessian and Cain drive Trevor and Short Circuit back onto the metal platform with thunderous chokeslams, while Daisy and the cameraman watch from the distance. Nate: This is too much! That platform is designed to hold rafters together, not support over 700lbs!!! Robbie: And that’s a bad thing why? Steven: It’s obvious we’re reaching the climax of this brawl! Robbie: I swear……… Steven: I KNOW! Riptah and Seth get to their feet again, only to be grabbed by Cain and Hessian. Cain lifts Seth up and drives him onto the metal with a powerbomb, shaking the platform. Hessian pulls Riptah up onto his shoulders, and holds the Westsida in the Canadian Backbreaker. Riptah yells out in pain, when Hessian drops and drives Riptah into the iron of the platform with a devastating Crucifix Piledriver. Nate: ETERNAL DAMNATION AND THE NINTH GATE ON THE PLATFORM!!! Robbie: What’s that creaking noise? Hessian and Cain stumble as the platform suddenly begins creaking and moaning. They watch as the metal attached to the rafters begins to slowly bend, and watch wide-eyed as it suddenly snaps, and tilts heavily to one side. Nate: THEY SHOULD HAVE EXPECTED THIS!!! The platform begins to buckle, and before they can do anything, Heresian Bloodlust, Civil Disobedience, Da Westsidaz and Caleb are thrown from the platform as it jolts and buckles. Nate: OH MY GOD IN HEAVEN NO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Robbie: AW HELL!!! THEY’RE FALLING!!!! Steven: ACTUNG LEIBER!!! Crowd: OSW! OSW! OSW! OSW! OSW! OSW! OSW! Down, down they fall, Hessian, Cain, Caleb, Riptah, Short Circuit, Seth and Trevor. Flailing and falling, the tag teams yell out when suddenly the arena is filled with a sickening thump…as every man lands on the roof of the Hell in the Cell, suspended 40ft above the ring. Nate: NOOOOOOOOOOOO-………………well I’ll be damned… Robbie: WHAT! That’s must hurt like a bitch!!! Steven: Oh Jesus, Mary and Joseph! How the hell does a Cell hold that much weight! Nate: Well they all seem to have landed on the steel and Caleb is on the mesh…thank god, or we’d have had a ruined main event! Robbie: GET SOME DAMNED EMTs OUT HERE FOR THE TAG CHAMPS!!!! The entranceway suddenly floods with people, as EMTs, security guards, trainers and even Minion sprint down to the ring. The Cell begins to lower as they surround the ring, and on top of the Cell the fallen bodies lie crumpled like a car crash. Nate: Well goddamn folks! Heresian Bloodlust have claimed they want Civil Disobedience and Da Westsidaz at Ascension, but withheld the stipulation, and in an impromptu brawl all three teams and Caleb have fallen 30ft or so from the rafters on top of the Cell which is needed for the main event…and now we wait as the Cell lowers and these men are taken away. Robbie: Basically the (expletive) just hit the fan! Steven: I hate when exit remnants get on my sheets. Nate: (disgusted) Sigh…this is absolutely out of control here tonight. We need to head to a commercial while all this is sorted out!!! When we return, we will head to the ring for our main event of the evening!!!
