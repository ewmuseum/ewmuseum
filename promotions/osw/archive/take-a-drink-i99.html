Take A Drink!

2002-02-03
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Take A Drink! Written: 02/03/2002 Nate: Welcome back to Slam!!! We are just about ready to head to the ring for our next match, but I am being told that our cameras have caught up with Meanstreak in the back. The cameras switch to the backstage area where The Monster They Call Meanstreak is trudging down the hall. He stops at the soda machine and glares at it, blinking at the Dr. Pepper insignia covering it's front. Meanstreak(mumbling): Dr. Pepper makes the world taste better, boyo... Robbie: Wooooo...we get to watch this big moron talk to himself and jab buttons on a soda machine. Talk about ratings. The camera pans out a bit and the crowd begins to boo as Meanstreak, out of boredom, begins tapping the buttons on the machine, hoping for a soda to roll out. Hessian and Cain step forward, the cause of the crowd's booing. Nate: HESSIAN AND CAIN ARE BACK TO DEAL OUT MORE DAMAGE TO MEANSTREAK!!! Hessian glances over to Cain as Streak continues mumbling something about 'boyo' over and over. Hessian jumps forward and slams the face of Meanstreak into the machine, pinning him against it. Cain steps forward and pushes his weight against the monster as well. Cain: I let you off easy a few minutes ago. Not this time, BOY-O… Meanstreak grunts and grits his teeth, growling like the madman he is. He tries to push back, but is again slammed against the soda machine. Hessian: I beat you once...I can do it again, 'Monster'. Hessian pulls Meanstreak back and spins the 365 pounder around, driving a fist straight to his ribs. The beast groans in pain and begins to double over, but is taken right back against the machine with a right from Cain. Cain: I assure you Caleb will be pleased with watching you bl-- Before Cain can finish his sentence, Meanstreak snaps his head forward, headbutting Cain. Azrael stumbles backward and holds his forehead as Hessian looks back to him. Nate: Meanstreak does not look happy... Meanstreak pulls his free arm to Hessian's and pries it off his body, twisting it to the side and whipping Hessian straight into the wall. Cain growls and swings and Meanstreak, hitting him straight on and causing the monster to stumble back into the soda machine, rocking it back and forth. Nate: These men don't look like they're going to keep a lid on this thing much longer! Cain leaps forward and tries to drive a fist into Streak's face, but instead, the beast ducks, Cain's hand slamming through the plastic cover of the machine. Nate: Meanstreak almost had his head taken off!!! Streak grips Azrael in a military press and lifts the huge man over his head. The crowd roars in unison with Meanstreak as he tosses Cain straight up into the ceiling with a variation of his move 'Fearing the Light'. Cain's body easily clears the fragile drop ceiling as Meanstreak steps to the side of the machine and out of the way. For a few seconds, the floor is littered with broken pieces of the ceiling before Cain's body comes crashing back down through the ceiling and slams down face first to the floor. Nate: HOLY SH*T!!!!! Meanstreak, meanwhile, grabs one side of the 700 pound soda machine and begins lifting it. Hessian, still recovering from the whip against the wall, notices this and quickly rolls out of the way before the Dr. Pepper machine slams into the ground and spills the cans everywhere. Robbie: So much for that machine... Meanstreak growls at the two members of Heresian Bloodlust as he walks away. The camera focuses in on them as they collect themselves and share a confused look. The scene shifts back to the ringside position of the OSW announce team.
