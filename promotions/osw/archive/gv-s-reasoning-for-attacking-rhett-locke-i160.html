GV's Reasoning For Attacking Rhett Locke

2002-05-18
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GV's Reasoning For Attacking Rhett Locke Written: May 18th 2002 Suddenly 'Tearing Away' by Drowning Pool begins to blare over the PA system, interrupting Nate’s rant in mid-sentence. Nate: What the hell?!?! The crowd rises to their feet as the camera swings to the entryway as Graphic Violence comes out from behind the curtains wearing blue jean shorts and a black hooded sweatshirt with a bulls-eye on the front and begins heading towards the ring. Nate: What the hell is this jackass doing out here?!?! Robbie: Giving the fans what they want!!! Steven: When is he going to give me what I want?!? Robbie: If it is a shattered skull, I am sure I can arrange that. Me and GV are tight. Steven: Tight in a figura…. Nate: (interrupting) Steven, if you finish that sentence, I am going to cripple you…RIGHT HERE!!! RIGHT NOW!!! Steven wimpers submissively as the camera shifts to ringside as GV climbs into the ring, much to the disgust of the fans, and grabs a microphone. He just glares at the crowd and begins to speak. Graphic Violence: I'm sorry to disappoint you all by being here tonight. I was just going to do the PPV, boost this piece of shit federation's ratings through the roof and move on. The boos grow louder as the OSW fans express their distaste for Graphic Violence. GV snarls at some hecklers at ringside before continuing. Graphic Violence: But that all changed when a certain bitch had to come down to the ring and cost me my match with Lance Knight. That little slut Lillith…' The crowd immediately begins to cheer for Lillith since she cost GV his match. GV grits his teeth and nods. Graphic Violence: Go ahead, cheer for her. Because sooner or later she'll have taken her last breath. Nate: Takes a “big” man to threaten a 115 lb woman…Pffft. Steven: Women are the bane of society!!! Burn them all!!! Nate sighs. In the ring, GV stalks like a caged animal, before continuing. Graphic Violence: So why did I cost Rhett Locke his title last week if I have a problem with Lillith? That is the question that's been on everyone's pathetic little minds since last week. The answer is quite simple. I'm not going to waste my time beating a woman into a bloody pulp. I wouldn't even break a sweat. It's just too easy for me to take Lillith out. Women don't belong in a mans world, and in this ring it's MY world and little slut bitches don't belong in it! Once again more boos are heard throughout the arena. Mainly to GV's comments about women wrestlers. Steven: YOU GO BOYFRIEND!!! Robbie: Jesus, would you stop turning his legit statements about not having T&A in the workplace, into the Battlecry of the Homosexual? Steven: Nope. Back in the ring, GV soaks in the boos, before continuing. Graphic Violence: So I decided to go after the ones Lillith cares for. Make her feel the emotional pain as I run down each and every person that she holds dear to her. I start with Rhett Locke, and I start tonight. GV begins to walk away but abruptly stops. He grins and lifts the mic to his lips one last time. Graphic Violence: Of course, once there's been enough blood shed…there are ways to make it all go away Lillith. There are ways' GV grabs his crotch and smiles as he drops the mic and walks back up the entrance ramp much to the dismay of the crowd. Robbie: GV is my kind of guy!!! Steven: I hope he is MY kinda guy too!!! Nate: I am gonna be sick. We need to head to a commercial. When we return, we will head to the ring for our first match of the evening. The camera shifts to the still smiling Graphic Violence, as he flips off the crowd from the entryway….as the scene fades to commercial.
