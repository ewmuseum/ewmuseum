Carrot Top's Arrival

2002-04-06
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Carrot Top's Arrival Written: 4-6-02 Nate: I am being told our own Shanda Russell is standing by backstage with…Carrot Top. Robbie: Jesus, someone just shoot me now. Steven: Someone will be shooting all over me later on TONIGHT!!! There is dead air for 1…2…3…4…5 seconds before the camera shifts to the backstage area, where we are met with a view of Shanda Russell looking more irritated than an armless man with an itchy nose...and that’s pretty damned irritated. We aren’t left to speculate the cause of her discomfort for long, as the camera pans to the left to reveal the obvious cause of her annoyed state: actor/comedian Carrot Top. The crowd in the Gulf Center immediately erupts in a chorus of boos at the sight of him, the majority jeering not only because of the man’s actions on last week’s Slam, but also because of the fact that they realize they will have to listen to his annoying voice very shortly. Carrot Top is dancing around, acting like the idiot that he is, causing Shanda to sigh deeply before deciding to start her interview. Shanda: I am backstage here with Carrot Top, the man of a thousand bad jokes. Carrot Top: Correction, Ms. Russell. I am now officially up to a thousand and thirteen. Pretty impressive, no? Instead of the smile he’s expecting, Shanda answers him with a roll of her eyes. Shanda: I think I’m going to agree with you on the no part. Carrot Top: I’m always hard at work thinking up of new ways to impress my fans. I’m not the kind of guy who rests on his laurels...or his hardies. Another roll of the eyes from Shanda. Shanda: Wow. You just keep cracking away at that humor thing and some day you might eventually get the hang of it. The red-head with the afro puts his hands over his heart in a display of mock offense. Carrot Top: You’re hurting me, pretty lady. You can’t tell me that what I did to Civil Disobedience last week wasn’t funny. I think it was some of my best work to date. Shanda: Ah yes, the reason I was stuck with this assignment. Last week on Slam you played a vital role in helping Heresian Bloodlust take the OSW Tag Team titles off of the former champions, Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke. I believe we have some footage of that. We switch to a feed showing Carrot Top running out of the audience and laying boots into a beaten Seth Masters outside of the ring. The comedian tosses Masters under the bottom ropes and continues to lay in punches before turning him over to Hessian and Cain, who spike him with Heretic’s Wrath for the win. We cut back to Shanda and Carrot Top, the latter of which is laughing at the top of his lungs at what he just saw. Carrot Top: Oh man! Ha ha ha! I kill myself! Did you see the way Seth’s head bounced off the mat? That was great! Shanda: The question that is on everyone’s minds is...why? Carrot Top: Why did I do it? Why did I cost them the match? That’s like asking why birds have feathers, why dolphins live in the sea, why 1-800-COLLECT advertising executives take enough acid to convince them that hiring me as a spokesperson is a good idea, why... Shanda: Are you going anywhere with all of this? Carrot Top: Not really. Ha ha ha ha! Another roll of the eyes, courtesy of Shanda. Shanda: So are you actually going to answer my... The Top holds a hand out, stopping Shanda in mid-sentence. He drops the mirthful look, now taking up a serious tone and expression. Carrot Top: If you want an answer, I’ll give you an answer, darn it. I cost Civil Disobedience that match because they deserved to lose those belts. I have never felt more disrespected in my entire life, and coming from me you know that has to mean something. Shanda: Disrespectful? Hmmm, yeah that sounds like Civil Disobedience all right. But they act that way towards everybody, yet you’re the first to interject yourself into their professional careers. Any further explanation? Carrot Top: Those jerkwads had the audacity to book me on their cable access show and then bump me because of time restraints?! After that, I had to get even somehow! Do you know what a slap in the face that is! That is the type of thing can ruin careers. If I get bumped off of Seth’s World, then Rosie O’Donnell and Jay Leno won’t think two seconds about doing the same thing. So you see, I was only evening the score. You people can’t be mad at me for doing that. They injure my career, so I injure theirs. It makes so much sense! After a polite nod, Shanda turns to face the camera as she runs through the motions to end the interview. Shanda: I suppose that explains your actions. Well, Carrot Top, I can’t say it was a pleasure talking to you, but... A hand sticking out from Carrot Top interrupts her in mid-sentence. Carrot Top: Oh, you’re not getting out of this interview this easy, sister. I’m not done ranting yet. The crowd jeers loudly after being informed of this fact. Loud enough to be heard in the backstage area. Carrot Top ignores the collective shouted protest, grabbing the microphone away from Shanda and continuing in spite of it. Carrot Top: If it was just getting bumped from their show, I might be able to handle things, but there’s more. On top of that, those two hacks don’t understand how funny I am. I’m funny, darn it! I’m funny, I’m funny, I’m funny! Why don’t they see that? Carrot Top seems to be losing it, as he jumps up and down, throwing a fit. After a few seconds of this, he grabs a tuft of his red afro and points at it with his other hand. Carrot Top: Look at this ridiculous haircut. You don’t think I have it to impress the womenfolk, do you? No, I do it for the chuckles it causes when people lay their eyes on me for the first time. I’ve put so much into this business, and for Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke to demean all my work like that just makes me so mad. As Carrot Top pauses to catch his breath, Shanda grabs the mic from him slowly, bringing it up to her mouth. Shanda: If you’re done with your tantrum, I’d like to... She jerks away in fear as Carrot Top snatches the microphone back. Carrot Top: Wait, Shanda. I’m not done yet. You see, things aren’t as bad as I’m making them out to be. Sure, I was disrespected by those guys, but there’s always a silver lining in the clouds. When I came out of the crowd last week, I realized that I had more television exposure in that one segment than I would have had in ten collect calling commercials. That got me to thinking...maybe it would be best for my career if I appeared in OSW on a weekly basis. Anticipatory groans can be heard from the Gulf Center crowd. Carrot Top: So that’s exactly what I’m going to do! You people are the luckiest wrestling fans in the world, because you will get to see my face appear on your television sets EVERY SINGLE WEEK! The people in the arena erupt in another chorus of boos. The angry murmurs refuse to subsidize, carrying on for well over a minute before Shanda takes the mic away from Carrot Top. She doesn’t look too happy at what she just heard, either, but carries on with her job none the less. Shanda: That’s quite the bombshell you just dropped, Carrot Top. Carrot Top: And I’ll tell you another thing...wait, Civil Disobedience aren’t booked in a match tonight, are they? Shanda: I don’t think so. Carrot Top: Good. And I’ll tell you another thing. Civil Disobedience may have thought they got off easy last week being on the receiving end of mere beat down at my hands, but there is much, much more to come. I am intent on making their lives miserable, on humiliating them, on making them the laughing stock of the wrestling world. I... A high pitched voice, obviously a falsetto, calls out from off screen. Voice: Excuse me, Mr. Carrot Top. Can I have an autograph. Carrot Top: You hear that, Shanda? People are already coming up to me asking for auto...Wait a second. No one has ever asked for my autograph before. I think that... Turning around, Carrot Top’s sentence is interrupted by the loud crack of a steel chair across his face. As the actor/comedian slumps down into a heap on the ground, we are met with a shot of Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke, with chairs in hand, standing behind him with vengeful smiles on their faces. Trevor glances at his chair, now-dented beyond use, and tosses it to the side. He pulls Carrot Top off the ground by his hair, leveling a few shots at the man’s face, before dragging him away. Seth heads in the same direction, but not before turning at waving at Shanda. Seth: I love you Shanda! Whoooo! As Seth disappears, Shanda rolls her eyes for the umpteenth time this segment, before collecting herself and turning to the camera. Shanda: From the looks of it, they seem to be heading out to ringside. That’s your territory, boys. I’m turning it over to you. The camera cuts to a shot of the entrance ramp and, sure enough, it’s only seconds before Civil Disobedience appears at the top, each with a hand clutching Carrot Top by the hair. The crowd is cheering wildly...whether its for the appearance of Civil D or the fact that Carrot Top isn’t talking anymore is up in the air...but one way or another they are going absolutely bonkers. Nate: Looks like Seth and Trevor are coming our way, and they’ve got one annoying comedian in tow. Robbie: Oh gee, lucky us. That was sarcasm if you didn’t catch it. Steven: Poor Carrot Top! I hope Civil Disobedience doesn’t hurt him too bad. I may never have the chance to see if the carpet matches the drapes. Awkward silence fills the broadcast table. Seth and Trevor drag Carrot Top down to the ring, despite his wails of protest. Rolling him into the squared circle underneath the bottom rope, Seth joins him in the ring, still wielding his chair, while Trevor walks around to take the microphone away from announcer Earl Henrich. Entering the ring as well, Hawke lifts the mic to his mouth, apparently having a few words to say. Robbie: God, listening to Carrot Top was bad enough, but now these guys are going to open their mouths. When will the torture end!? Nate: As soon as we find a new broadcast commentator to replace you. Robbie: What was that? Nate: Shhh, be quiet. He’s about to say something. Trevor: You know, usually me and Seth here are pretty carefree guys. We don’t really get pissed off about stuff when things go wrong. We’re the type of people that can take things in stride. I mean, so what if Heresian Bloodlust sneak attacks us in the back every week in repetitive pointless segments. You don’t hear us bitching and moaning about it. Trevor pauses, looking around the arena as he collects his thoughts. Robbie: Not bitching, my ass. What does he call what he’s doing right now!? B-I-T-C-H-I-N-G!!! Nate: You’re no one to talk, Robbie. You haven’t stopped bitching since the show went on the air. Robbie: I...well I guess you have a point. Trevor: No, we don’t get mad. We get even. Which brings me to the purpose of our coming out here today. Turning around, Trevor points a finger in Carrot Top’s direction, shaking it in a scolding manner. A look of sheer terror is painted across the comedian’s face as he meets Hawke’s eyes. Getting to his feet, he tries to flee the ring. He spins around to leave, but is met with a fist to the jaw from Seth Masters, who was standing behind him. The crowd pops big time as they can smell the blood about to be spilled. Trevor: (Still pointing at Carrot Top) You! Heheheh. You little shit. Did you think you could do what you did last week on Slam and not suffer the consequences? Carrot Top nods his head up and down, then realizes that is the wrong thing to do. Shaking it off, he moves his head left and right in a vehement sign of denial, getting on his hands and knees to plead for mercy. Of course he gets none. Trevor lifts him up by his hair and delivers a right cross, catching Carrot Top across the nose. He falls sprawled out on the mat once more, flipping over quickly and crawling into the corner. Robbie: Civil Disobedience are such wimps. Two on one against an untrained wrestler. Oh, they’re soooooo tough. I’d like to see them bully around a real man. Nate: You’re not trying to tell me that Carrot Top doesn’t deserve every damn bit of this beat down. Robbie: Actually, yeah. I am. Nate: He cost Seth and Trevor their match against Heresian Bloodlust! And have you seen Member of the Board? My god, that movie was terrible! Steven: Well, if he deserves punishment, I’d be more than happy to give him a thorough spanking. More awkward silence. Robbie: Can’t you at least try to act straight every now and then? By now, Trevor Hawke has drug Carrot Top back to the center of the ring, where he is continuing to berate him. Trevor: Now as I said, we’re not ones to bitch and moan, but damn it man...I miss our tag team titles, and it’s all your fault! Lifting his foot, Trevor drives a boot into the side of Carrot Top’s head. As the man’s head hits the mat once more, a smile comes over Trevor’s face. He looks over at Seth across the ring, raising an eyebrow. Trevor: Seth, do you have anything to add before we show this dicksmack what Old School is all about? Thinking to himself for a moment, Seth nods his head. He walks over to Trevor and takes the microphone from his partner. He lays down on his hands and knees, getting up in Carrot Top’s face. Seth: Just one little thing...you’re about to find out why you don’t F**K with the suburbs! Masters drives the microphone into the red-head’s skull, then drops it, lifting Carrot Top from the mat. Pushing him to the corner, he nods at Hawke, who has now picked up the steel chair from the ground. Seth proceeds to whip Carrot Top out of the corner with authority, sending him flying right into a chair shot from Trevor that nearly takes his head off. Nate: Baseball season is starting early here in OSW with Trevor Hawke hitting a home run shot off of Carrot Top’s skull! Robbie: Meh. Cowards. Picking Carrot Top off the mat again, Seth delivers an inverted DDT, putting him down and out permanently it seems. The crowd cheers wildly at the violent act of revenge, and Seth and Trevor are playing it up big time. Picking the mic up again, Trevor climbs the turnbuckle, soaking up the crowds ovation. Trevor: Yeah, you like that, don’t you Mississippi? (More cheers) I know I sure as hell do! Do you think this punk-ass dicksmack has had enough? An empathic “no” is emitted from the crowd, bringing a smile to Trevor’s face. Climbing down from the turnbuckle, he confidently walks over to the fallen comedian. Steven: Ooo, the people seem to be out for blood tonight. Nate: And that is not good news for Carrot Top at all. Picking Carrot Top up again by his hair, Trevor stares into his face, a sneer coming over his usually jovial face. Trevor: You want to hurt careers, dude? I’ll show you how to hurt careers. Seth, get me the clippers. Reaching into his pocket Seth pulls out a pair of hair clippers, and is quick to hand them over to his partner. Nate: Are those...hair clippers? Is Civil D going to shave Carrot Top’s head?! Robbie: Those...those bastards! Flipping the clippers on, Trevor raises them high in the air, for all the people in the arena to see. Trevor: You know, Carrot Top...of all the people I know in show business, you have the least talent of the bunch. Really, I’m not joking. You’re more annoying than funny, and I know for a fact that ninety nine percent of the population agrees with me. Right people? More cheers, courtesy of the crowd. Trevor: When I’m right, I’m right. Face it, man. Nobody likes annoying celebrities. They’re a pox on our culture. If we could take them out one at a time, the world would be a better place. With that stated, there’s no better place to start than you, buddy. It’s the least we can do for your unwanted help in our match last week. Carrot Top, though out of it, still manages to shake his head in a pained denial of his actions. Trevor: Your career has been built upon the fact that you tell bad jokes and have a stupid haircut. Hell, your stage name is even modeled off that same stupid haircut. If we were to take away the hair...well, all you’d have left were the bad jokes...and that type of thing doesn’t cut it in show business. So Carrot Top, I guess you had better kiss your fame and notoriety goodbye, because you’re just going to be just another annoying punk when I’m done with you. Bringing the hair clippers down toward Carrot Top’s head slowing, the people in the Gulf Center begin the infamous “Na na na na” chant with a twist. It goes a little something like this. Crowd: “Na na na na. Na na na na. Hair hair hair...goodbye!” Robbie: I can’t watch this. These people are making me sick. Nate: Well if one good thing comes out of this, it will be we’ll never have to listen to that man’s irritating voice again. Steven: Will the same thing work if we shave your head, Nate? Robbie: Ha! The clippers come closer and closer to Carrot Top’s head, and one can almost see a tear forming in his eye. The more they draw near, the bigger the grin on Trevor’s face becomes. The blades are about a foot away...nine inches...six...three...one! The clippers are just about to cut through those preposterous red locks when... Voice: WHAT DO YOU TWO THINK YOU’RE DOING?! What can only be described as the most annoying voice in the world rings out across the public address system. It is almost impossibly annoying, in fact, with the undesirable blend of tone and timber causing a chain reaction to go through out the arena. As it crosses each person’s auditory canals, they wind up falling to the ground in spasms. Even, Trevor and Seth are not immune to this, as they collapse to the mat as well, holding their ears in pain. Carrot Top seems to be the only one not affected by this, and he takes the opportunity to roll to the outside of the ring, escaping from immediate danger. Robbie: Ah, my ears! I think they’re bleeding! Nate: (Covering his ears) WHAT?! Robbie: I said: Ah, my...oh nevermind. The source of the voice reveals himself shortly after. Walking out from the back to a chorus of boos, which pretty damn loud when you consider the crowd’s incapacitated state, is the only man you could expect to have a voice like that: Gilbert Godfrey. Unfortunately with a microphone in hand. Gilbert: SHAME ON YOU, SETH MASTERS! SHAME ON YOU, TREVOR HAWKE! PICKING ON POOR CARROT TOP LIKE THAT! Trevor and Seth are on their knees in the ring, with their hands cupped over their ears. They manage to glare angrily down the ramp at Godfrey, put pained expressions are still painted across their faces in spite of this. Carrot Top manages to limp his way up the ramp and when he reaches Godfrey, he wraps his arms around him in a thankful embrace. The crowd is not happy about this at all. Gilbert: YOU TWO ARE TRAINED PROFESSIONAL WRESTLERS AND YOU’RE DOUBLE TEAMING MY GOOD FRIEND! AM I SUPPOSED TO JUST SIT IN THE BACK AND WATCH THIS HAPPEN? I DON’T THINK SO! Carrot Top hugs Gilbert again, drawing more jeers from the crowd. Gilbert: ANNOYING PEOPLE HAVE FEELINGS, TOO! YOU JUST REMEMBER THAT NEXT TIME YOU FEEL LIKE ATTACKING A POOR DEFENSELESS CELEBRITY! In the ring, Civil Disobedience are sporting typical W.T.F. looks, not pleased at all that their revenge scheme was foiled by this Hollywood Squares regular. Their moods are not helped much by an indignant Carrot Top ripping the microphone from Godfrey’s hands. Carrot Top: You want to shave MY HEAD?! Oh, no...this isn’t over by a long shot. This isn’t over by a loooooooooooong shot! You two are lucky Gilbert here saved your asses from my wrath. Next week you may not be so lucky. Gilbert: I DON’T KNOW WHY DO YOU INSIST ON ANTAGONIZING THEM, CT. LET’S JUST GO TO THE BACK AND GET OURSELVES SOME NICE MOCHA FRAPPACHINOS. Turning around, Gilbert and Carrot Top walk off into the back, but not before Carrot Top turns around and flicks off Seth and Trevor on last time. To say the least, they’re not happy, as the two annoying celebrities disappear into the back. Trevor kicks the bottom rope in frustration, before he and Seth roll to the outside, looking disappointed. Nate: Well that was certainly an interesting segment. Seth Masters and Trevor Hawke exacting a bit of revenge from Carrot Top’s hide, before he was saved by fellow celebrity Gilbert Godfrey’s annoying as hell voice. Robbie: At least justice was preserved. Those bullying asses in Civil Disobedience didn’t get to have their way tonight. Carrot Top is still proudly sporting those stupid red locks. Steven: Hmm...I’m still wondering about Mr. Top's carpet. Nate: Carrot Top AND Gilbert Godfrey in the OSW? Who would have thunk it? Robbie: Obviously some moron!!!! Nate: I gotta agree with you there!!! That’s a little to much to digest all at once. I think it would be best if we went to commercial. We will be right back!!!
