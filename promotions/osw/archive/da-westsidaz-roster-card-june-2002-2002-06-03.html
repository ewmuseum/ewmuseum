RIPTAH Real Name: Andrew Washington Nick Name: Riptah, Rip, Ripdude Age: 23 Wrestling Style: Powerhouse, Hardcore, DDT Innovator Riptah's Description & Brief History: The 6’4” 290lb monster known as Riptah is as bad-assed as they come. With a mix of power and agility, he is the balanced member of Da Westsidaz. A clean, mostly shaven head and wrap-around sunglasses are a staple look for Riptah that he is most oftenly shown in. Along with his black jeans and Nike Air Estivals, Riptah is usually either wearing a Westsidaz Football Jersey or a white beater. Although he us able to take to the air on occasion, he is better used as the powerhouse of the team, whether it be attacking with his vicious rights and lefts, powerslaming an opponent or bashing someone over the head with a steel chair. Riptah grew up in a pretty poor section of Los Angeles and was born Andrew Aleksander Washington. His parents had decent jobs in a local bar, but six kids is a little much to raise on that kind of salary. Riptah is one of five boys and one girl in the family. His two older brothers are T.J. Washington and Sean Washington. He has a twin brother named Jesse and two younger brothers, Mike and Jordan. His youngest brother Mike is 14 and his oldest brother T.J. is 30. Riptah’s little sister, Cita, is 16. The family was mostly distant, hanging out with their own crowds at school, except for one thing. They all loved wrestling. Sean was always the strongest, followed by Andrew, who was always gunning for his superior brother. Cita spent, and still does, most of her time beating on Mike. Andrew’s school years weren’t anything out of the ordinary until High School, where he really excelled in Football until an untimely suspension after he put his own Quarterback through a table of Gatorade. Afterwards Riptah just DJed or served as a Bouncer until he decided to get into a wrestling school. He got signed by the PWN, met SC and the rest is history... Riptah’s Signature Moves: Punk-Out: (Gorilla Press into Stunner) Riptah lifts the opponent off the ground and presses them over his head. He then pushes them into the air and catches the on the way down so their chin goes over his shoulder as he sits down. Ghetto Blaster: (Pumphandle Bulldog) Riptah grabs the opponents arm and pulls it between their legs in a pumphandle position. While still holding the opponent’s arm, he uses his other arm to get the opponent in a headlock. He then runs and jumps, driving the opponent’s face into the canvas. Edgetreme: (Sit-down Tombstone Piledriver) Riptah flips their opponent into a sixty-nine position. He then jumps and sits down, driving the opponents head into the canvas. Riptide: (Tiger Driver ’91) Riptah kicks his opponent in the abdomen. He then hooks both arms behind the opponents back and hoists the opponent into the air. He then falls to his knees, driving the head of the opponent into the canvas. Hit n’ Run: (Stunner) Riptah kicks the opponent in the abdomen and spins around, catching the opponent’s head. He jumps and sits down, driving the opponents chin into his shoulder. Sideliner: (Jackhammer) Riptah sets up his opponent in suplex position. He then hoists his opponent off the ground and holds him in the air for a few moments. He then releases his opponent, letting him drop into a powerslam. R.I.P. Bottom: (Rock Bottom) Riptah tucks his head under his opponent’s arm and hooks his head. He then lifts his opponent of the ground and drives him back first into the canvas. Westside Slam 1: (Olympic Slam) Riptah gets under his standing opponent, grabbing his shoulder with one hand and his hip with the other. He pulls his opponent over his own shoulders and elevates him into the air, slamming him onto the canvas in a semi-elevated Samoan Drop. Westside Slam 2: (Hangman’s Tigerbomb) Riptah places his opponents between his legs and hooks both arms. He pulls his opponent over his shoulder in a Powerbomb position and drives his opponent onto his neck by falling onto his knees. Westside Slam 3: (Cradle Piledriver) Riptah lifts his opponent into a Piledriver position, but holds him there for a moment. He then hooks one of his opponents legs and drives him into the canvas by sitting down. 730 Crash: (Triple-Jump DDT) Riptah places a chair in the center of ring. He runs off the ropes and jumps onto the chair. He springboards off the chair only to land on the top-rope. He then springboards off the top-rope to the outside, catching his opponent with a DDT. Riptah’s Ride to Hell: (Inverted DDT) Riptah hooks the opponent’s head with one arm. With his other arm he grabs his opponent’s tights and lifts him into the air. Riptah then drops and drives the opponent’s face into the canvas. Ebonics Lesson: (Sit-Down Reverse DDT) Riptah gets behind his opponent and hooks their head with his arm, bending them backwards. He then sits down, driving the back of his opponent’s head into the canvas. Punksmoker DDT: (Fireman’s Carry DDT) Riptah lifts the opponent onto his shoulders in a Fireman’s Carry position. He then uses his strength to throw his opponent off his shoulders, spins, hooks his head over his mid-air opponent and drives his face into the canvas. Afro-Kick: (Standing Side Kick) While standing, Riptah kicks horizontally at his opponent, catching him in the chin. Riptah’s Set Up Move: LA X-Press: (Spear) Riptah runs from a short distance and dives into his opponent, driving his shoulder into his opponent’s abdomen. Riptah’s Finishing Move: RDD: (Any DVD Variation) Riptah places his opponent’s on his shoulder’s in a Fireman’s Carry position. He then drives his the back of his opponent’s neck into any object. ie. The wall, the canvas, a windshield, a table, a chair, a window, etc. SHORT CIRCUIT Real Name: Marcus Williams Nick Name: Short Circuit, SC, Shorty Age: 21 Wrestling Style: Highflying, Suicidal, Innovative Short Circuit's Description & Brief History: Weighing in at 170lbs makes Short Circuit the runt of the tag team. Although runt means he’s smaller, it doesn’t mean he couldn’t hold his own against Riptah in a fight. SC is living proof to the saying, “It’s not the size of the dog in the fight, it’s the size of the fight in the dog.” He usually takes to the air or takes a beating for the team. Being more of a flashy guy, Shorty comes to the ring wearing huge baggy blue jeans, big crazy ass sunglasses (similar to Christian’s), chains, rings and a bright orange tee that reads, “CAUTION: Contents Are Extremely HOT!” Although he usually prefers to leave his hair messed up, he will wear the occasional Westsidaz backwards cap. Marcus Williams comes from a rich, snobby family. Originally from Bakersfield, his father Eric moved the family to Anaheim and founded Williams & Williams International. Short Circuit was raised as a well-mannered member of society and was spoiled to the point that he was disgusted with his own lack of authority figures. The only child in the family, it wasn’t uncommon for Shorty to get whatever he wanted, whether it was clothes, music, cars or even personal trainers. Short Circuit was trained by the best in the following fields: Wrestling, Shoot Fighting, Martial Arts and Wilderness Survival. When SC finally decided he wanted to break into the business, he was more than qualified and after pulling a few strings he immediately got thrown into a respectable Independent Promotion. But don’t let his silver-spoon attitude fool you, he is extremely popular and well-liked and for a good reason. After he was hired to OSW, he bought every member of Team Westsidaz a $50 000+ vehicle. Short Circuit’s Signature Moves: Diffused: (Shooting Star Press) Short Circuit knocks his opponent down and goes to the top-rope. He then jumps off the top-rope and back flips in mid-air, landing stomach-first onto his opponent. Educated Feet: (Kicking Frenzy) Short Circuit starts with a kick to the mid-section, then kicks out his opponent’s knees, making him collapse in a praying position. Short Circuit then roundhouses his opponent, knocking him to the floor. Short Changed: (Flipping Neckbreaker) Short Circuit goes to the top-rope while his opponent is facing him. He jumps off the top-rope and flips over his opponent, hooking the back of his head on the down, hitting a neck breaker. Simply Stunning: (Flipping Stunner) Short Circuit goes to the top-rope while his opponent has his back to him. He jumps off the top-rope and flips over his opponent, hooking his head on the way down, driving his opponent’s chin into his shoulder. DJ Deathlock: (Tarantula) Short Circuit has his back to his opponent while they are both in the corner. He then pushes himself off the ground using the ropes, hooking his own legs around his opponent’s arms. He then crawls over the top-rope and uses his arms to hook his opponent’s legs and stretches his opponent. Bling Bling: (Reverse Frankensteiner) The opponent goes to the top, but Short Circuit kicks one of his legs out. The opponent lands in a sitting position, facing the outside of the ring. Short Circuit jumps onto his opponent’s shoulders and leans back, throwing his opponent off the top, onto the canvas moonsault-like. Last, Best West: (Frog Splash) Short Circuit knocks his opponent down and goes to the top-rope. He jumps off the top-rope and makes a frog-taunt by putting his hands between his legs and pulling them out quickly. He lands stomach-first onto his opponent. Caution: (Tazzmission) Short Circuit hooks one of his opponent’s arms and his opponent’s neck with his arms. He starts to choke the opponent, falls backwards and hooks his legs around the opponent’s midsection, choking him. The opponent usually taps out or passes out during the move. Short Fuse: (450) Short Circuit knocks his opponent down and to the top-rope. He jumps off the top-rope and front-flips in the air, landing stomach-first onto his opponent. Afro-Kick: (Standing Side Kick) While standing, Short Circuit kicks horizontally at his opponent, catching him in the chin. Short Circuit’s Set Up Move: Shock Value: (Tornado Reverse DDT into a Dragon Sleeper) Short Circuit goes to the top-rope while his opponent has their back to him. He jumps off the top rope and hooks his hand over his opponent’s head. Using his momentum, he spins and drives the back of his opponent’s head into the canvas. He still has his hand hooked around his opponent’s head and moves it down to around his neck and hooks the opponent’s hand with his free-hand, putting the opponent to sleep. Short Circuit’s Finisher Circuit Breaker: (810-Degree Splash) Short Circuit knocks his opponent down and goes to the top-rope. He jumps off the top-rope and twists 360 degrees twice in the air and lands stomach first onto his opponent. DAISY Real Name: Chantal Holden Nick Name: Daisy, Daze Catchphrase: 'Uh...My boyfriend is a 290lb black man with a chip the size of texas on his shoulder, so you might NOT wanna touch my ass again.' Age: 19 Height: 5'7' Weight: 121 lbs Description: Young, Beautiful, Innocent and BLONDE are some words to describe Daisy. Although not the brightest woman in the world, the 20 year old fox has some very valuable assets that she uses to aid Da Westsidaz. Whether it be the usual lap dance to the official, distracting the other team or even sliding in a brick-loaded purse, she tries to help out the two as much as she can. Known obviously for her T&A, Dayz usually wears her tight, hot-pink leather pants to the ring, accompanied by her tight white tank-top, cut-off coincidently just after her two most prized possessions. Remember boys, her boyfriend is 290lb black man who has been known to break jaws of people that glance at her. Born Chantal Holden, Daisy grew up in San Diego. Nothing out of the ordinary except for that she was a model from the age of 7. When she turned sixteen she got a gig as an interviewer and reporter at Pro-Wrestling Nation. There she met and fell in love with Riptah and since then things have been peachy. Finishing Move: Bitch Slap Finishing Move Description: Daisy reels back and slaps her opponent in the face.
