Seth Masters vs. Azrael Cain

2002-09-02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seth Masters vs. Azrael Cain Written: 09/02/2002 Nate: Well, come up next is a match that will even entertain you two. Robbie: I find that hard to believe. Nate: Let’s take it to Earl Henrich in the ring. The camera zooms in on Earl in the center of the ring wearing a large smile as usual. The fans erupt as the Earl brings the mic up to his lips to announce the next match. Earl: Ladies and Gentlemen, boys and girls, children of all ages.... Steven: Get on with already Henrich! For Christ’s sake! Earl Henrich’s face is filled with confusion and humiliation. The mic is still up to his mouth, but he has become speechless. Earl: ...ah....well...yeah. Steven: Is this what they pay the big bucks for earl? Earl finally pulls himself back together and proceeds to announce the next match. Earl: Coming up next is a single match scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring first, weighing in at 236 pounds and standing at 6’ 0”... “Pretty fly for a White Guy” blares through the arena’s PA system. Out form the back comes the Seth Masters who immediately begins to work the crowd over. Masters is jumping up and down at the entrance with Trevor on crutches behind him. The fans are exploding with excitement. Earl: Seth Masters! Steven: Oh great! We get to sit here and watch the other half of Civil Disobedience get manhandled! HA! Robbie: You though that they would have learned their lesson last week. Look at Trevor. His knee is the size of a softball. Nate: Show so compassion guys. The man just got his knee drained because of the beating that he received from Heresian Bloodlust. He’s lucky to be here. Robbie and Steven look at each other sharing a half smile and the same look. Masters and Hawke are now making their way down to the ring. Steven and Robbie: Yeah, but we’re not! Both announcers begin to howl at their own joke. Nate: I have never seen you two get along so well. The two announcers continue to laugh hysterically at their joke. Nate: I’m glad that two people’s suffering is what it takes to bring you close. Please Earl, save me here! Earl: And his opponent, coming from regions on known and standing at 6’10”, weighing in at an astounding 375 pounds... The lights in the arena snap out. The eerie chant begins to play that is Carmina Burana. A strobe light is turned on and begins to flick in the arena. Nate: And we all now who this is going to be! The fans let loose a deafening boo as a row of flames ignite at the entryway and the two massive silhouettes of Azrael Cain and Hessian appear behind them. The dark figures walk through the flames unscathed as the lights come back to normal. Earl: Azrael Cain! Hessian and Cain take a moment to absorb the boos of the thousands of screaming fans. The men share a look. Cain starts for the ring at a dead sprint. Referee, Pierre Gaston is in between Masters and the charging Cain. Gaston has the look of a deer in headlights as the huge beast rushes at Masters. Nate: It looks like Cain is going to get this match started right away. Robbie: Good old Pierre better get the hell out of his way or he is going to be the first one to get a beating tonight. At the very last possible moment, Pierre dives out of the way. The traumatized referee signals for the bell to ring in order to start the match. Nate: And here we go! Cain lunges at Masters in an attempt to give him a spear. Masters sees this coming though and manages to greet Cain with an elbow to the face. The fans cheer to see Cain drop to the mat holding his nose. Nate: And a great move to avoid Cain’s spear puts Masters in the driver seat for a moment. Robbie: Kill that gothic freak! Steven: Shut up! You think a pussy move like an elbow to the face is going to stop Azrael Cain! Get up Cain and show this pansy what’s up! Robbie and Nate begin to laugh uncontrollably. Steven: What? Robbie: You...HA HA HA...calling Masters...HA HA HA...a...HA HA HA HA HA... pansy? HA HA HA HA HA! Steven: You son of a bitch! SMACK!! In the ring, Masters picks Cain up by the hair and delivers a spinning heel kick to the beast’s head. Cain wobbles, but doesn’t fall to the mat. Masters looks on in shock then delivers another spinning heel kick to Cain, but still he doesn’t drop. Finally on the third heel kick Cain falls to the mat. Nate: Masters just took Cain down! Oh my God! Steven: Yeah, but it took three spinning heel kicks! Cain is going to kill this guy. Robbie: If he ever shakes the cobwebs out. Masters pulls Cain to his feet again. This time he irishwhips him into the ropes. The massive Cain flies into the ropes and starts to come back at Masters, whose attention is so focused on his opponent that he doesn’t see Hessian moving in behind him. Before Cain gets to Masters, Hessian reaches under the ropes and grabs hold of Masters’ ankle. Masters is up ended in the ring as Cain leaps through the air and delivers a viscous elbow drop to Masters’ back. Nate: Did anyone notice how far the mat drooped when Cain hit that elbow drop? Steven: HA! I told you! I told you all! Cain hoists Masters up to his feet and up again into a gorilla press slam. Masters is launched through the air and lands violently on the mat. Cain doesn’t miss a beat and proceeds to get Masters up again. This time Cain grabs Masters by the throat and throws him to the mat with a huge chokeslam. Nate: This could be it folks. Robbie: Only if Masters is even more of a pussy then we thought before. Cain refuses to go for the pin right now though and pulls Masters up again. Nate: This isn’t going to be good for Civil Disobedience. Steven: Yeah, but it’ll be good for us if both members get injured and we don’t have to put up with these wiggers anymore. Robbie: Did you just say wigger? Nate: Let’s just get back to the ring. Cain places a mandible claw on Masters who begins to flail in pain. Nate: That’s Cain’s submission move, famine. Cain’s grip is constricting tighter and tighter by the moment. Pierre is checking to see if Masters is ready to tap out, but the wrestler refuses to give in to the pain. Masters just keeps reaching for the ropes that just seem a couple of inches out of reach. Nate: I can believe that Master’s hasn’t tapped yet! This is unbelievable. Robbie: Oh come on. You’re acting like it hurts to have large things in your mouth. You know it doesn’t! Just ask Steven. He has large things in his mouth all the time. Steven: Yeah….Hey! Smack!!! With an incredible show of strength and desperation, Masters manages to grasp hold of the bottom rope. The ref is immediately on top of it and tries to separate Cain fist from Masters mouth. Pierre begins to count. 1… 2… 3… 4… Cain let’s go of Masters’ mouth and climbs to his feet. Cain picks Masters up to his feet by his hair, but Masters gives him a low blow causing Cain to double over in pain. Steven: Disqualify that son of a bitch, Pierre! Robbie: Oh please, like you have never given one of those! Masters begins to lay into Cain with the fists. Cain blocks one and with one punch he drops him masters to the mat. Masters gets back up again, but to the same result. And for a third time Masters gets to his feet, but is only greeted with a big boot to the face from Cain. Nate: It looks like Masters is on a mission tonight to prove that size doesn’t matter. Steven: But it does matter! It does! Get him Cain! Robbie: You sick bastard! Masters slowly climbs to his feet only to be welcomed with a spear. Cain climbs on top of Masters and begins to deliver fists to his face. Pierre immediately begins to try and separate Cain and Masters, but as a result he doesn’t see the small groups of Hell’s Angels making their way down to the ring. Nate: Wait a minute. Who the hell is making their way down to the ring. The three large Hell’s Angels slowly creep up behind Hessian who is to busy yelling at the ref for breaking up Cain’s assault. The bikers then begin to beat down on the Hessian severely outside of the ring. The three large men quickly overcame Hessian’s power. Nate: Good god! That is masters, uncle! What the hell are they doing down here? Robbie: Who the hell cares? They are beating down that idiot Hessian. Steven: Hey Pierre! Get on the damn ball already! Look outside the ring! Inside the ring, Pierre has managed to separate Cain and Masters. The two wrestlers immediately lock up again, but Pierre’s attention is now drawn to the outside of the ring where Hessian is getting assaulted. Pierre is trying to get the bikers away from Hessian and doesn’t see Trevor Hawke dropping his crutches and getting into the ring. Nate: And this is going to be trouble for the big Cain! Robbie: Get him! Get him! Hawke hits Cain form behind and then irishwhips him into the ropes. Cain flies into the ropes and is slingshot back at both members of Civil Disobedience. Hawke and Masters catch their opponent coming off the ropes in a double flapjack before each grabbing an arm to hit a double flatliner. Nate: Trendsetter! Trendsetter! Robbie: YES! Steven: NO! Trevor gets out of the ring as quickly as his busted up leg will take him. Masters makes the tag as his partner is getting the attention of the ref. Steven: NO! The members of Hell’s Angels stop the attack on Hessian and begin to exit up the ramp laughing hysterically. Hessian gets to his feet though and bellows a fierce roar and runs after the bikers back into the backstage area. Pierre drops to his knees and begins to count. 1.... 2.... Steven: NOOOO!!!!! 3..... Nate: Seth Masters has done it! Seth Masters has proven to all men that size really doesn’t matter! Robbie: You are a little to excited about that there Nate. Steven: But it does matter! It does! “Pretty Fly for a White Guy” blares as Masters is greeted by his partner. They both celebrate the victory wildly as the fans cheer. Masters calls for a mic, which is tossed to him in the ring. Masters pulls the mic to his lip to address the screaming fans...
