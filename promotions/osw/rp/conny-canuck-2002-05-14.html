Date: 05/14/02 “Flight 1923 to Detroit is now boarding.” The announcement causes Short Circuit to shake to life. He blinks and groggily stands to his feet, stretching for a moment. He’s dressed in a LA Lakers jersey and a huge pair of baggy jeans, covering his shoes. He peers through his yellow crazy glasses and looks around, trying to find his partner in the huge facility. The Pearson Internation Airport is busy with people running everywhere, carrying bags and the like. He yawns and asks questionably, “Was that us?” “No. That was Motor City, we haven’t been called yet.” “A’ight dawg. I’m beat, wake me when it’s time to go.” Short Circuit sits back down on the bench, yawning. He closes his eyes and tries to fall back asleep, but is kicked in the ankle by his partner. Riptah is reading a WrestlingTattler magazine with all sorts of pictures on the front. He’s got his eye on the columns, trying to figure out what is going on the wrestling world. He looks up from his magazine at Short Circuit, with a concerned tone in his voice. “Don’t be nappin’. We have to keep our eyes open, even here. I know it ain’t the ring, but we pissed a lot of people off on SLAM! Civil Disobedience are probably pissed outta their minds. We set ‘em up. They have to have it out for us. Plus we played those bros, Heresian B, for fools y’know. They probably ain’t to happy either.” Riptah takes black sunglasses from his pocket and puts them onto his face. He’s wearing an OSW tee shirt and black jeans, but doesn’t really look out of place in the busy airport. Amidst the busy crowd, a beautiful blonde woman, who is immediately recognized as Daisy, Da Westsidaz’ valet walks slowly toward the two. She is dragging a wheeled suitcase toward the two and takes her seat on Riptah’s lap. Her eye looks completely healed, but she still dons a small pair of sunglasses. “It’s still about a half-hour till our flight is prepared, so we have tons of time. By the way, I found out Heresian Bloodlust really wants your heads. Apparently they were pretty pissed off about the way you totally kicked their asses and want you in the ring,” informs the diva, “I don’t think they think the match you guys got coming up at the inter-federation Pay Per View is enough, heh.” “Oh shit! SC, I forgot about that match! We gotta take on Heresian Bloodlust and those other clones in a few weeks too! Oh well, I assume Cain and his buddy are putting the titles on the line. Stupid ass-spears, I hope they realize that Caleb won’t be reffin’ the iPPV match. They have a one-up on us in OS-Dub, but they are going to be in a different world when they realize that Caleb can’t ref outside of OSW. But screw the outside, we have to worry about the present. So, they want us in the ring, eh? Well they can bring it.” Riptah scratches his eye-brow, thinking about the upcoming weeks. Riptah continues flipping through the magazine as Daisy cuddles up on his shoulder. The road has fatigued everyone since they haven’t been like this in awhile. Short Circuit stirs and looks up. “Yeah, Heresian Bloodlust is as big and bad-assed as they come, but we’ve been in matches like this before. Two big guys may be harder to knock down or to penetrate than most, but that won’t bug us. We played ‘em for fools and proved that we can take them out. It’ll be tough, but these boys are tough, not unstoppable. We’ve proved it before and we’ll prove it again. Size only matters in your pants, not you arms. Saturday, you boys will be graced by Da Westsidaz presence. You say you’re out for revenge? Can that even be possible? Rip, if they got revenge for Head on Collision and we got revenge after they beat up Daisy, can they even be able to get revenge from us again? I mean, they got revenge then we got revenge already. Can the circle keep going on and on? I’m confus…” “Shut the fuck up. Your being annoying.” “Sorry Rip, but these guys can’t take a hint. They think they can mow down whoeva the hell they want just because they’re big and bad. Well they ain’t going to mow down us, I can guarantee ya that. They need a frickin’ special ref every match so that proves they ain’t as big and bad as they thought. Just two big cowards, I tell ya…” Short Circuit gets up and stretches again. He does a few phantom kicks, trying to loosen up a little, but also trying to show off his martial arts to any nearby ladies. Of course, they ignore him. “Y’know what? I’m frickin’ thirsty. Excuse me for a moment.” Short Circuit runs across the busy crowd to a coke machine. He quickly hits the Coca-Cola button and slides in his American money. He hits the button and suddenly three cokes come out of the machine. Puzzled, he checks his change to see if he put in too much money. He shrugs, finding he still has all his money left but a dollar. “Excuse me sir, but your taking quite a long machine at the pop machine, eh?” A female voice politely interjects in Short Circuit’s thought process. “Pop?! EH?! Huh…Oh YEAH! We’re in Canada! That’s why I got three sodas, our money is worth triple the amount of there’s! Whoooooo! Canada rules!” He turns 180 degrees and runs right into the person talking to him. He falls onto his back, as does the person he ran into. Shorty grabs his forehead in pain but shakes it off quickly, kick-flipping to his feet. He looks to see if the woman is alright and notices something very interesting. 5’4” Big Breasted Brunette Perfect Tanned Complexion J-Lo Ass “Oh shit, I do love Canada….Hey, wanna see why Da Westside is Da Bestside honey??” “Flight 1700 to Calgary is now boarding…”
