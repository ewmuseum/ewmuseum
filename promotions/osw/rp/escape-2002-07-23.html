Escape

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.23.2002 My parents always thought highly of me, even when they as a couple were engaged in brutal arguments. In my mother's suicide note, she gave reason as to why she killed my family along with herself. Reading it as a naive child, I never saw the hidden meanings. Was the young Rutherford heir just too ignorant to understand? Hardly. The question is, and always has been, did I care about why she sprayed the crimson blood of the only humans I had ever cared for? Not then. Hell, maybe not even now. I often look back and see my Nana, the dear old lady who for so long tried to protect me from the harsh words and physical battles, and I still can't find a place in my black heart for her memory. That was then. Yet, as Kory Storm, I always need a method to my madness. Old School Wrestling needs a different angle every Saturday, so they can give the fans a reason why I torture them. Does Jane Reagan care if I'm tortured?! Do the writers, who have driven so-called 'wrestlers' to stabbing fits and sexual innuendo, give a fuck if I don't have a reason? Hell no. For I don't matter. Only you ignorant souls matter. As you shovel cheap arena food into your already bulging mouths, I give my all in the ring. For what? It's not like there are perks for men who are hated. I can't see the smiles after I sign an autograph. I can't feed my ego with the endless amounts of cheering when my music begins to blare through every ear. As of last week, I was convinced that I had given my blood for the last time. The freak was leaving the circus ... simply to see how they did without it. I would care for you no longer, while every other disgusting waste of life would start draining their spirits by doing what I had to. Enter Stanislav. Between school and my dysfunctional family, I had always found time as a child to watch wrestling. My father frowned on the entire lifestyle... 'Daddy! Daddy!' Even my voice was innocent. 'I know exactly what I want to become as an adult!' He intimidated me back then, just as his memory does now. The booming voice replying in excitement. 'I knew it! A director like your dear old dad, eh?' 'No.' 'A Lawyer?' 'Nope.' 'Doctor?' 'One more guess!' 'Journalist?' 'Not even close.' He slammed his fist on the desk then, the thud still echoes in the cages of my mind. 'Damn it boy! I hate this guessing games! Tell me now!' I could smell the vodka on his breath. 'I-I'm ... going to be ... a professional ... w-w-wrestler.' 'Not under my roof you little bastard.' I didn't need his support. I was confident then, just as my alter persona shows now to the masses. In my dark bedroom, night after night, I would study wrestling tapes and watch my new career goal on live television. My mind began to realize each detail of the business, analyzing my icons with every move. Dreams Can Come True. I saw the fan favorite as my father when I broke into professional wrestling. The enemy. The object of pain and violence. Yet, even as I carefully constructed the character which is today Kory Storm, I could not hate one man, whether he was fan favorite or the object of hated. Ivan Stanislav. He moved with such power, and he commanded respect. I wanted to be an icon ... I wanted to be like him ... but break his mold in the process. Never did I expect that I would take the dream so far as Kory Storm. I aligned myself with two pawns in my life's game ... waging war with the establishment. Kory Storm fought battles and was determined to bring down the 'Bear'. Inside, however, Cory Rutherford ached. Addiction and politics had long held me back, now it was pain. Pain ... just as I felt after the murders. It carved a hole into my soul ... I couldn't continue. Making one last stride torward my ultimate battle, he injured me. I then yearned for the escape I had wanted since childhood. Denied. Introducing you all to my only real friend, one Christopher Kingsley, I made the decision to take one week off. I claimed injury, that I couldn't work. But emotion was the true factor. OSW saw right through me. Who cares about the man behind the mask? Still ... no one. Yet, each and every ignoramus wanted the freak to return to the ring, to be commanded by a higher master. I was to learn the error of my ways. Kory Storm Vs. Ivan Stanislav My icon destroyed me that night. Sitting here in my defeat, refusing to leave what pride I have left in Pittsburgh, I realize each reason. For she killed herself to protect me from her. The Fault Is Mine They booked me in order for my attitude to be suppressed. The Fault Is Mine My feelings seem to stem from each of the above. Meaning that my pain, and my frustration, is my doing. However, I'm willing to admit my mistakes. Can he admit his? He stood and watched while the girl was destroyed The fault is his. He beats me ... just forcing me to come back again and brutalize him The fault ... and Ivan Stanislav's bloodshed ... is his. Life is a bitch ... But being even with an enemy is bigger.
