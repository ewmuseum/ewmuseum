A Painful Victory

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/24/02 The water in the hot tub whirls around Jim’s body like some sort of liquid masseuse, trying to soothe his aching muscles. The OSW training room is empty now, as the post-Ascension rush has subsided and most of the talent has gone home. Alone in the hot tub sits the OSW security guard turned wrestler Jim Axtell, still nursing various injuries incurred in his battle with Kory Storm over the last several weeks. Ring battles and sledgehammer shots have quickly broken Jim into the sport of professional wrestling, and have also quickly broken his body as well. The Ascension main event didn’t exactly help to heal Jim’s wounds. It was a good showing for Jim, though. He won a hard fought battle with Kory Storm, and made a good showing of himself by not being eliminated in the Mt. St. Hell main event. He didn’t win, but he didn’t lose either. He didn’t quit, and he gave 100% just like he always does. Hopefully that has earned him some respect from people like Kory Storm, Frankie Bones, and Michel Krieg. People who seem to be uneasy with the success Jim Axtell has had due to working hard and giving his all every time. Maybe if they didn’t always look for the easy way out all the time, taking bribes, taking cheap shots…. Taking short-cuts. As Jim sits in the hot tub, he slowly slides down until his head is resting on the edge of the tub. His eyes close and he tries to block out the pain he feels inside. Bruised sternum, cracked ribs, ruptured blood vessels, pounding headache. “What a first couple of weeks this has been,” mutters Jim to himself. His eyes are still shut and he seems content to soak his worries away. His rest is interrupted by a familiar, booming voice. “Ya did good out there tonight, kid.” Jim’s eyes spring open and he quickly sits up in the hot tub. He looks to his right and sees Moose Malone walking toward him with a warm smile on his face. Jim smiles as well, fighting the urge to frown from the pain of the bruises and cuts on his face. “Thanks, Moose,” replies Jim. “I owe a lot of that to you.” “Nah,” Moose says as he waves his hands at Jim dismissively. “That was all you out there tonight. Ya did the boys proud.” Moose stands near the hot tub and folds his massive arms across his thick chest. The OSW security t-shirt is stretched to it’s capacity over the muscular frame of the head of security. Moose is still in great shape for his age as we all know. “You taught me a lot of things in the short time I worked for you,” says Jim. “You told me it would be tough as hell and you weren’t kidding. If it wasn’t for your guidance I might not have made it through the first week, let along as far as I have gotten. For that, I thank you.” Moose pats Jim on the shoulder, inducing a cringe from the battered wrestler. Moose chuckles deeply. “Sorry kid, forgot you had your ass handed to you tonight.” Moose pauses as Jim shoots him a playful glare. “It’s not going to get any easier from here on out, you know?” The playfulness leaves Jim as quickly as it came over him. The words from Moose weigh on Jim as he realizes that he has new challenges ahead of him, and some old scores to settle up on. “Yeah,” Jim starts. “Krieg is a big step up on the ladder. He is in the Red Army and one of the fiercest competitors in OSW.” Moose points a stern finger at Jim, causing him to pay close attention. “I’m tellin’ you what, kid, don’t you pay no attention to all that stuff going on in the Red Army right now. Those commies stick together when it comes to outside affairs. They handle the dirty laundry from the inside. You better keep your eye out for them the whole time on Saturday.” Jim nods his head in agreement. “I know, Moose. If there’s one thing I’ve learned is to have my head on a swivel at all times. You never know what can happen here in OSW.” Moose grins satisfactorily. “You’re gonna be alright, kid. You got all the heart in the world, and the talent is coming around. Someday soon you are gonna be something to reckon with.” Moose winks at Jim and then turns to leave. He exits the training room, leaving Jim all alone once again. The hot tub continues it’s healing ways on Jim’s body and ‘The Axphixiator’ relaxes and enjoys the opportunity to rest. It’s something that he won’t get much of as the weeks ahead unfold.
