Book 1 - MTV's Becoming

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 1, 2002 Click. Click. Click. Graphic Violence sat in his hotel room, looking for something good to watch. Nothing entertaining was on. Television is a barren wasteland these days. Click. “What the fuck!?” said Graphic Violence as he heard his name mentioned on an OSW promo show. There he was, Howard Porter, imitating Graphic Violence to the worst of his abilities. Oh is that who you are? Fucking nobody? Fuck you and your fucking mother. Yeah I fucking say fuck a lot, what's it fucking to you? Maybe I'm just a fucking badass. Maybe rage burns with fire behind these fucking silver rimmed fucking spectacles. You're a fucking nobody, always have been, always will be. Who's a fucking nobody? You, you who's fucking reading this you dumb fuckwad. Yeah you. You're a fucking nobody. I'm fucking better than you, and if fucking having to say fuck forty fucking times gets the point across that I'm better than you, then so fucking be it. “So that’s how he wants it to be huh? I already have to deal with Rhett Locke, the entire tag team division, Nate Dumbass and now Howard Porter.” GV rose from his bed, turned off the television and threw the remote on the bed. He picked up the telephone and began to dial. “I’ll do one better Porter. I’m not gonna waste my time in the ring with you. I’ll just insult your manhood. Hello, MTV? This is Graphic Violence. I want to do one of your shows and I want to do it now. I can be there in 2 hours. Yes. Don’t worry. I’m gonna do it for free. Start advertising because your ratings are about to go through the roof” Two and a half hours later at the MTV studios in New York “Mr Violence, you don’t know how much this means to us” says the MTV producer. “Not a problem. Anyway I can help out my favorite television program. Now you know the show I want to do right?” The producer looks at his clip board, then back at Graphic Violence with a puzzled look on his face. “You want to do MTV’s Becoming? I would have thought you were here for TRL or something like that.” GV just laughs. “I’m not here to promote OSW. I’m here to become Howard Porter. Now let’s get to it.” Graphic Violence is surrounded by cameras and the show begins to record. “OH MY GOD, I’M BECOMING HOWARD THE DUCK, err I mean PORTER!!” Graphic Violence yells as these little MTV teenie boppers do. He is taken to a dressing room where he meets a woman. “Hello sir, my name is Vanessa and I’m going to be giving you your wardrobe. Now I got a picture here of Mr. Potter. Wait, his name is Porter. He’s not that wimpy wizard is he? I don’t know OSW stuff. Anyways here are your black pants, white shirt, tie and glasses. Then we’ll give you a wig.” GV looks in confusion. “Baby, it’s quite clear you don’t watch the OSW. This isn’t his attire.” “But I have his picture…” She is cut off by Graphic Violence who is also waving his finger in her face to tell her no. “Listen you cute little thing, I’m in the OSW. That picture is of him at a funeral. Now let me just see your wardrobe area and I’ll pick out the correct attire. I wouldn’t want to represent Howard Porter in a…wrongful manner.” The woman smiles. “Oh why yes. I understand. Let me show you to the dressing room.” The monitor begins to read the following: Three Two One And now, Graphic Violence as…Howard Porter MTV fades up, and the face of Graphic Violence can be seen. He’s wearing overly large black glasses with tape in the middle. The camera slowly begins to fade out and we can see his entire body. He’s wearing a woman’s body cast with no clothing. It’s like looking at a manican. “Hello everyone. My name is Howard Porter. I know that I’m naked right now, but I thought I should let the truth be told. I’m really a woman with a mans head.” A gasp is heard in the background. “Yes it’s true. I mean, it’s pretty clear by the way I wrestle…but I thought I should finally come out in the open. I just kind hide it anymore. I’m just so embarrassed. I sucked a lot of dick to get to the OSW and it’s not something that I’m proud of. But I have to do what I have to do.” More gasps from the crowd. “I just hope Graphic Violence takes it easy on me when he sees this. I know I pissed him off earlier and I’m so sorry. I open up my legs to you GV. I’m your bitch as it is already.” Graphic Violence beings to rip the wig and glasses off with rage. He then rips the rubber body cast off and throws it to the side. “Listen up Porter. If you want to take a shot at me, you do it face to face. You don’t pull this shit just to try to get noticed. You’re in the dark right now in OSW. No one knows who you or, nor do they actually give a flying fuck. You’re just like the rest of the idiots in the wrestling world. Just like Rune Winters. You need to take a shot at the big dog in order for the nobody to get people talkin’ about him. Do me a favor Porter, get off my jock. Because I don’t waste my time with fuckin’ pussies.” “Alright, thank you Mr. Violence. We’ll have to add a few bleeps in there. But I’m sure this is going to give us our best show yet.” Graphic Violence smirks. “You’re damn right it’s going to. I just want that to air tonight. So make it happen.”
