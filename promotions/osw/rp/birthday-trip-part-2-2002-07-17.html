Birthday TRIP (Part 2)

17 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 17th, 2002 ((Note: Today is my birthday, this RP is set on Monday evening, July 15th. I'll build up to the 17th eventually. ;) )) They've ignored you... He made them. He took you and broke you... When you're not on their screens, when they can't see your tits, you're at the back of their minds. Let the living die. He's alive, and he's only keeping himself that way to kill you off. His purpose is to hurt you...Reno got involved, his purpose is to hurt Reno...you don't want that, do you? Do something about it...you know what you're thinking, you'd love to make him bleed, to break him in half. What you want is something you've never had: Revenge. Why deny yourself of it? She felt asleep, but knew she wasn't...dozing off in the seat of a cheap commercial flight, sitting next to Reno about 36 hours before she'd turn twenty, Amy felt like she was slowly going crazy. She'd finally come to the realisation that Reno couldn't save her from what seemed to be the world working against her every time, nobody could but, with Davis in mind, she glanced over at him where he sat at her right, flipping through a Modern Drummer magazine while he shifted uncomfortably in the seat, just assuming she was napping beside him. She didn't know much about his past, he rarely spoke of it, if at all...but the scars were there to prove that, physically, he'd been through a lot more than she'd ever dream of. He kept his cool through most of it, she knew Reno had his own internal struggles going on and prayed she'd somehow helped to ease them, but he'd never been hunted...not like this. He'd challenged Corg to a match at By Any Means Necessary, that match should have been hers, of course, but she couldn't be angry...he was fighting for whatever honor she had left. Besides, the Cruiserweight strap would look MUCH better on him...heh, she was just too skinny. ''Speaking of skinny...'', she thought upon a quick inspection of her wrists and forearms, Amy'd been slightly shocked by exactly how much weight she'd lost over the last little while, it was almost as if she was just barely skin and bone now. It was weight she couldn't afford to lose...she hadn't had a match in weeks, she wasn't even to the level of a God damned valet. She hadn't gotten NEAR a ring in weeks and prayed that would change for the better soon. If it didn't, she was basically fucked...being on the shelf doesn't get you anywhere near the pay and practice you get when you're in the ring and she was definitely in need of a little practice. That voice or whatever it was that had run through her head was un-clear, blurred...she wasn't even sure if it was a voice, an image, text or what...which was the main reasoning behind her newest stance on her sanity, but it had said that when she wasn't on screen, she wasn't in anybody's mind...she wasn't getting title shots, she wasn't earning title shots, she wasn't even earning her continued contract to keep her on the roster, for Christ's sake. The voice, be it insanity slowly setting in or just her own subconcious pattern of thought, was entirely too right. Unfortunately, she'd been thinking like this for more than a week now and the even more unfortunate part was that it had been driving her up the proverbial wall. Corg had launched her into the hospital. Nine days alone in a little, bland, completely sterile room leave you nothing but time to think...too much time to think, as had become apparent. Thinking had always driven her to depression, for some reason...the general consensus within herself was that she over-thinks just about everything, she drives herself to near madness when she sits alone ponder something for even an hour, let alone over a week. The question Amy had to ask herself now was had she driven herself just over that line? For the last few weeks, she'd been trying NOT to think of what Corg had done and was doing to her, but she was being hunted and was now entirely helpless for the most part. She could avoid it for a minute, a few days even...but eventually he'd get to her and at the moment he struck, there would absolutely nothing there to stop him. He'd make sure of that. When you see him next, do what you feel like... Humans are animals, you're allowed to act on your instincts... Seek and destroy, right? You won't be caught, you know they can't pin it on you, there are so many people after him. If it works for him, it works for you... Implied consent, don't you remember? You know he's thought the same way this whole time, give him a taste of his own fucking sour medicine! 'Implied Consent' was all she could think about, it was the only reason she was this worried, but it would also be her escape if it came down to harming Corg in the almost primal way she was feeling inclined towards. He could do ANYTHING to her, she couldn't have him punished unless she did it herself. Lawyers were useless, Reno couldn't be around all the time...it was up to her to keep herself alive and that sure as hell wasn't proving to be very easy. She'd never been entirely attached to what she thought or felt, but everything seemed to be making her angry all of a sudden. It was as if the crying baby at the back of the jet made her want to claw her own eyes out, like the fact that Reno's nails needed to be cut was driving her up the wall, they were scratchy and annoying as hell most of the time, but that was beside the point because what made her angriest was Corg... He was hunting her, he wanted nothing more than to see or deal her demise and it was making her want nothing more than to see his. She felt crazy for wanting that, though. Sure, people have said 'God, I wish they would just DIE!' about bosses, girlfriends, boyfriends, brothers and sisters before...but how many, upon deeper thought into that, would have actually carried out their wish? Only people like Corg, and she hated him for what he was...she refused to become that. She was at the point where she would have done something, though. She'd thought about it over and over again...promised herself if she actually had done anything, she wouldn't get caught. She thought of escape routes, loopholes, clauses, weapons she could use...it was becoming more of a grand scheme than an amusing little thought, that's what made her feel like her sanity was slipping. She wouldn't admit it to herself but, in truth, it WAS slipping...and Corg was holding the rope that pulled it. It was too true: She thought about what she'd do to Corg when she got the chance almost more than she thought of Reno, more than she thought of music and more than she thought of her closest friends. Aimz wouldn't come to terms with the fact that she actually thought these things, though...she'd rather call it a 'voice' in her head, some sort of viral sickness...unfortunately, she still knew that it was brutally honest conscience. She really did want to kill somebody, for the very first time in her life. But when she snapped back into reality, she found herself sweaty and nautious, her head pressed back against the stiff seat of the plane with one of Reno's hands cupping hers. ''Are you SURE it was Vodka you drank? How much of it did you have?'' He looked even more worried than before. Davis didn't like flying at all, mainly because he was forced to leave ZaC, his best friend, in a pen in the cargo bay...but he apparently liked flying with what appeared to be an ill girlfriend even less. ''You look sick.'' 'Wow...master of the obvious,' She thought. ''Yeah, I'm just kind of sleepy...must be the pain killers the nurses were giving me, puts me right out.'' ''Why don't you sleep? It looked like you were halfway gone for a minute...you uncomfortable? I don't blame you.'' He grinned, folding up the magazine for a moment while he cocked his head at her. ''Just ask the attendant for a pillow.'' ''No. Sleeping isn't my thing right now, too much to think about.'' ''Sleeping's gonna have to be your 'thing' soon enough, Amy...'' She could feel Davis squeezing her hand urgently. ''...the lack of it's making you sick.'' She just sighed and managed to bring her leg up onto the edge of the seat so she had a knee to rest her chin on, ''A lot of things are making me sick.'' ''Like...?'' ''I think you know.'' She groaned, rolling her injured shoulder a little. ''Gothic weenies, mostly. He makes me so fucking sick, Reno...I just want to......ugh, I won't say that.'' Tell him...you show him everything and usually tell him even more. He deserves to know this... She just wished something would silence that conscience. ''Won't say what?'' He inched a little closer to her, just enough so he could speak in lower tones. ''You want to hurt him, I know. It's not like I couldn't have gue---'' He's a big boy, he knows what it's like... ''I want him dead.'' Amy finally said it, sighing with a bit of relief. ''I just want to see him fucking burn...do you ever feel like that?'' She'd expected to find Reno looking disturbed by her true thoughts, but instead he chuckled and put a hand on her knee reassuringly. ''Come on, of course I do! Everybody does...'' He smiled a bit at how innocent someone as usually strong as Amy could be at times. ''We all have stuff like that go through our head sometimes. You're pissed, I'm pissed...I think we both wanna mutilate him right now, huh? You just need some rest, lighten up for a few days. I'm here to keep him away, just relax and enjoy the time off, alright?'' It's as if it had become Reno's turn to do what Aimz had done for him a few weeks back: Smile and assure that everything’s fine, even though you know it's far from it. None-the-less, he was doing a bang-up job at it, almost cheering her up a bit. ''Yeah...'' She tried to let all thoughts of Corg drain from her head when she met Reno's almost loving eyes. ''...yeah, I can do that.'' ''Good. Remember, it's YOUR damned birthday...don't let him ruin it on thought alone, got that?'' ''Yup,'' Campbell managed a weak smile, trying to hide that the worry still clawed at her. ''This'll be nice...I'll show you around.'' ''There's plenty of time to see the sights, it's just a bunch of lobster and rocky sand anyway.'' He nudged her elbow, knowing the one girlfriend he'd come to know so well would probably cut in to defend her home province. ''What I'm concentrated on is having some fun.'' ''Fun...yeah, fun.'' She winked, knowing she couldn't be her usual self without adding the usual suggestive innuendo that had become almost an instinctive addition to any of their conversations. She brushed off Davis' rib about Nova Scotia pretty swiftly afterwards, mumbling, ''...at least my province HAS scenery...yours is just a parking lot between Quebec and the outside world.'' The playful argument over who's home land was superior would continue for the rest of the flight. Unconsciously, it seemed like Campbell managed to cover up the worry and rather aggressive train of thought she'd been subjected to for that hour or so, but it was the rest of the trip that would tell the tale...
