Getting Better All The Time Part 3

Seattle, Washington. That's where I was going to get my hands on Hawk. Finish this thing, once and for all. Get him out of my hair, out of my company, out of my life. What bothers me is that everyone jumped on the bandwagon of him being a 'teacher', which would imply that he knew more than me, was somehow my better. A grave miscalculation on their part. And noone else in the locker room wanted to jump in on this, either. Noone wanted to help me. Which was going to change. There were like-minded individuals running around, it was just a matter of finding them, which he did... But things would materialize soon. They always did. Whether it be severe pain, drug overdoses, penis-biting hallucinations, or the general downward spiral of his federation in general, I will always perservere. You know why? Because I know what it's like to have to suffer through the indies, and I ain't going back to that. Hawk is nothing. An ill-conceived Ed ploy to get me out of the title scene. But Ed got his, didn't he? And now all there was is to eliminate Hawk from the mix as well. Now that his friend in high places is kaput, Hawk will go the way of the dodo... And strap matches. Why did Hawk think he had a chance in OSW? Because he was in high places at CCW? And through simple word association, Ed and others in charge thought it would be nice to simply through my lot in with a fuckstick like Hawk? No...no no no. I had worked too hard to get thrown into a crap feud with Hawk. Yeah, things have been very bad since I lost the title, but no way in fuck was I going to let Hawk get the satisfaction of waddling out of the ring on Saturday with a win over me. No, no no... This was the end of the line, Hawk. Now you get what's due. Enough of this self-pity crap for me. Enough of the painkillers and the hallucinatory psycho-chicks, enough of the crap. Time to get back to work. Time to put Ace Hawk out.
