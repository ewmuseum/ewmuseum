I will forever hide...

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/20/02 'Many of us spend our whole lives running from feeling with the mistaken belief that you cannot bear the pain. But you have already borne the pain. What you have not done is feel all you are beyond the pain.' Bartholomew Here I lie... Under a veil of toughness. A rough exoskeleton of rage. A quickly thrown together facade of anger. The layers I use to hide myself. You think you know me? You think you know a single thing about me? It would take you years to fathom the depths of my being. Am I psychotic? Full of delusions of grandeur? Think that, if it helps you sleep at night. As for me, I cannot run from the truth in such a way. These things I say, the things I proclaim, are facts. I know this, because I tackle them every second of every day. Every waking minute, every sleeping hour. And all I have found are these damn layers. Sparking, off the cuff reactions. Emotions that take control. Pure anger, unadulterated pain, overwhelming join, these things control me, they make me who I am. But they change, as easily as they come, they are gone. So which is the real me? Who the fuck am I? So you still think you know me? You still think you know all about me? Because if you do, then tell me, and end this lifelong search. Until that day, until I know myself, I will embrace these layers. I will lay underneath, and hide behind them, saving the true me. The only thing I know about the true me is this... It cannot be healthy. For I have no desire to live beyond the pain... I gave that up long ago. I live embracing the pain. Yet I still ask, who the fuck am I? Then again, who the fuck are you?
