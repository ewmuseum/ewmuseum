PWA: The Class of....

4 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.04.02 “...Going Down...It’s Going Down......The rhythm projects...” “...You may not believe it, but I don’t...” “...You’re so hot, teasing me, so you feel that I can’t...” “...Eins...Twei…Drie...Vier...” Nodding his head in approval, Krieg took his finger away from the CD player in his Ford Pickup and smiled as his favourite Rammstein song came on, which also happened to be his OSW theme tune. “Sonne” started to get into full swing as the German industrialist battered out heavy metal chords from Krieg’s stereo, “This is what you call good music,” Krieg said to his passenger, Turning round to him, Krieg’s passenger showed her disapproval for his German orientated taste of music, “Man, this sucks big time,” declared Krieg’s female associate as Krieg’s face showed an expression of shock, “Ah, you Americans. You not know good music when you hear it. That is problem with American youth: too engrossed in own culture to appreciate others,” responded the German, obviously not too pleased with his driving buddy’s response, The pickup slowly ground to a halt as Krieg stopped his traditional American automobile outside what looked to be an traditional American café, with a flagpole sporting the Stars and Stripes, 20c machines outside the door. The usual kind of things you’d expect to see in a suburban café or bar. Stepping out of the driver’s side, Krieg slammed his door shut, the whole car shaking slightly, as his passenger stepped down onto the pavement. Her combat trousers and Vans sneakers pretty much gave away her identity as the OSW’s most influential female, Aimz, stepped out of the pickup. Looking around, Aimz took in the exterior of the café as Krieg walked around to the pavement, grinning ever so slightly as he met Aimz, “What did I tell you Aimz? We can be friends…Krieg likes you, you know that, ya,” asked Krieg, a bit of schoolboy embarrassment showing through his light red cheeks, “Uhm…Yeah, Michel. I know you like me, but I’m with Timo, you see,” said Aimz, as she attempted to reach over Krieg’s shoulder but just settled with his waist, “I like you, and you’re a sweeter guy than I thought a rough, tough German Communist like you might be, but you see: me and Timo share a bond, ye know? I just can’t break that bond, man. You cool with that,” queried Amy, as she tried to let the big German down softly, “Ya, ya. Krieg is fine. He just likes good woman. My day with woman shall come, you not worry,” called Krieg as he started to walk towards the door of the café, Holding it open for Aimz, Krieg stood inside the café beside his OSW co-member and looked around, as if looking for someone or something. Spotting a man sitting at a table, Krieg lunges over with massive steps and greets him, “Ah, Will. It is good to see you friend. How have you been,@ Former NTICW star, Will Freeman, stands up and slaps Krieg’s hand as he stares up at his former PWA tag team partner, “Great man! How’s the OSW going,” “Ah…so, so. Oh ya, this is Amy Campbell,” said Krieg, swinging a massive paw in Aimz’ direction as she stepped forward to greet Freeman, “Nice to meet you Will, man,” said Aimz as she gave Freeman a small hug before backing away again, “You likewise,” replied Freeman, wearing a pair of black Gucci shades, a silver Armani shirt and a pear of black Levi’s, As all three sat down, a chewing gum-chewing waitress sauntered over to their table with her notepad, “What can I get for ya’ll,” asked the waitress in a deep Kentucky accent, “I’ll have a lemon soda,” replied Aimz, “A root beer for me,” said Krieg, half-grinning at the waitress, “Just a Coke for me, thanks,” answered Will, “They’ll be over shortly,” muttered the sloppy waitress as she walked back over to her less-than-tidy bar area, Tutting, Krieg spoke softly to the two others, “Ah, you can not find good help these days, yah,” “Oh Krieg. Stop complaining about the service. Let’s just enjoy the day, Ok,” said Aimz, trying to turn Krieg’s negatives into positives, “Yah, Ok…So Will, have you spoken to any of the other guys since the PWA,” “Yeah. Me and Jordan Crazy, or X Shabazz as he’s now called, stayed in touch, since we both went to the NTICW, but besides that, there wasn’t much contact between me and the other guys. The PWA’s a changed place now, huh,” “Yeah,” replied Aimz, “I remember what it was like back when I was there. Nothing like what it is now, I can tell you that,” Looking at his watch, Krieg turned to look towards the door, “They should be here by now,” Just as he said it, none other than the NTICW’s Jordan X Shabazz walks through the door, grinning from ear to ear on seeing his old buddy Michel Krieg, “Yo man! What’s going down? How are you, G,” shouted Jordan as he walked towards Krieg, “I am fine, my African-American friend,” responded Krieg, outstretching his massive right hand, The two men shook hands and then walked over to the table together. After making a brief introduction between Jordan and Aimz, they all sat down again, the PWA Reunion looking to be getting off to a good start, “So, Aimz, what’s it like being Timo’s best friend? Is he like, well cool,” asked Freeman, still a junior in the game of Wrestling, “Well, we’ve like known each other forever, and they do say friends make the best lovers, and I reckon me and Timo are proving that, ye know,” Krieg looked at his watch again and turned to look at the door. As he did, he started to turn his head around again, but then did a double take to see who was standing at the door, Is that Ben Pailthorpe Standing up, Krieg went to walk over to Pailthorpe, but as he did, Ben just smiled, waved and disappeared from the doorway. When Krieg went marching over to the door to look for Pailthorpe, a smallish male came walking around the corner and walked right into him. As both men went to shout at the other, they realised who the other man was and started chatting. Krieg and Charlie Loc began walking back over to the table, “Guys, this is Charlie Loc as most of you know. Charlie, this is Jordan X Shabazz, Will Freeman and the beautiful Amy Campbell…You can call her Aimz if you please,” “Ah, it is the ever gorgeous Amy Campbell,” said Charlie as he kissed Amy’s hand, Grimacing, Amy pulled her hand back, “Leave me alone, sleaze,” Turning to Krieg, Loc laughs, “You said she was feisty, but I didn’t think she’d be this feisty,” Growling at Krieg, Aimz stands up and begins to walk towards the bathroom, “I’m going to powder my nose. Hopefully, when I come back, that sleaze-ball will be gone,” Chuckling, Krieg talks to the others, “Ah guys. I see Aimz doesn’t like Charlie too much, yah,” “Ah, she just wants some of my Vietnamese strudel, that’s all. It’s only natural,” Laughing, Shabazz intervenes, “Yo dawg. Don’t be too quick to jump to conclusions. I hear Aimz likes a bit of chocolate log, if you catch my drift,” A bit displeased, Krieg speaks up for his OSW buddy, “Guys, guys. It’s Aimz we’re talking about here. She is a distinguished female wrestler, not some stupid bimbo like the ones in the PCW,” Taken back slightly by Krieg’s defensiveness for Aimz, the group stays silent for a second before Freeman chirps up, “So…” “So…” replied Loc, “So…” echoed Jordan, “Bah, stop repeating each other!…Once Aimz comes back, we’ll head to the hotel and you can meet some of the Army and then we’ll head to Slam tomorrow evening after some major site seeing and some heavy duty shopping. I hope you can all stay ‘til next week, yah,” asked Krieg, “Yeah man, I’m cool with it,” said Freeman, “Me too,” replied Loc, “Snap,” cracked Shabazz with a smile on his face, Krieg grinned as Aimz came back to the table, looking a little more happy than before she left the group to go to the bathroom. All 4 men stood up and smiled in her direction as they headed out of the café to continue their “PWA Reunion” Fade.
