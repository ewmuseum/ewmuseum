Beginning, Middle, & End

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.04.2002 '...It has to start somewhere. It has to start somehow. What better place then here? What better time than now?....' - Rage Against the Machine Every situation sparks from another. You must accept that fact, for it is just how our twisted world works. Humanity, just like the Earth, spins on an axis ... the propaganda and the proverbial riding crops of America forcing mankind to spin in the direction which the leaders say to. Just when you think there is control and a certain amount of substance in your life, they sweep it out from under your feet like the leaves blow across an empty street in Autumn. Once again, we as a civilization must deal with what has been normal since birth. We cannot rebel at risk of being labeled inferior, or worse, being destroyed. Not in body. No, what happens is much worse. They kill your mind until it is reduced to mere cinders of what it was, and thus ... without intelligence, you can no longer rebel. It's a constant circle. It was written in the corners of my mind, the mind which has been shadowed by the ideals of so many others, that I would be the one who could put the stick in the wheel which turns. Me, Cory Rutherford. Are You Sure? No. Is my name Kory Storm? I still have no fucking idea. But with what has happened today, I cannot worry about my insecurities anymore. The flag which is being celebrated outside of these concrete walls is pulled over every eye who sleeps on these grounds. Grounds which so many fought for. Grounds which I thought meant something. However, the blood given by those who made America possible has long been soaked into the ground ... replaced by dollars and cents. Why not jump on the bandwagon? I didn't think twice ... I jumped. I became what you ignorant citizens see every week in the ring which so many of you flock to. I became the anti-hero, yet you still spent what you earned to listen to me berate you. Every night, I felt as if I was supporting a dream which I've always knew didn't exist. I was contributing to Capitalism at it's finest. What Do You Know About Capitalism? Everything. I was born into it. Raised amongst people who knew nothing of love, but plenty about hatred and advancement by betrayal. In fact, in the end, betrayal and greed was what did them in. Thus, Capitalism ended my life without my permission, and as I ran away from it all ... I realized exactly that fact. Could I survive without money? Doubtful. Could I hate what I do to earn my survival? Of course, and I thought that whenever I found a job, I'd despise it. However, what I found was the exact opposite. I found something which combined the factors that shaped me as a child. Pain and money. Professional wrestling. I opened up my eyes and heart, letting this circus in, to this day ... it's never been let out. That was my beginning, but soon comes the end. Why? Am I the only one who sees a pattern with this question and my life? I didn't think so ... maybe you people are smarter than I thought. Wait a minute ... no ... you latch onto people like George Bush and Jim Axtell, how could you be anything but ignorant? This is another question with an answer only able to be formed in the eye of whoever chooses to ponder it. I always find myself laying in bed at night, tears sometimes streaming down my face from the memories, and I wonder. I'm the martyr ... I fight for what is right. You all are blind to this, claiming that I'm vindictive. I see it differently. I aligned myself with two of the most hated men in Old School Wrestling for one reason, and one reason only. Personal gain. And if I'm benifited by my gain, than you all will be too. Everything I do is in the best intrest of humanity as a whole. But you all sheild yourself from reality ... choosing to act as I used to and wish. Wish for a bette tomorrow. In a civilization filled with nuclear scare and tortured children with guns, your wish will float into the air just as every obese woman in West Virginia would float to a swimming pool. Don't like what I say, or how I say it? Fine. Hide from the truth as long as you would like, but when it finally reaches you ... it will be even more terrible than you expected. Just as the led sheep are eventually brought to slaughter, you too will be. Not just from me ... that personal pleasure is reserved for Jim Axtell and the person who feeds off of your welfare, Jesus St. Crux. Do You Really Think That You Can Help By Insult? Yes. I won't be contained in this place by Saturday, and then the government, along with each idiot who opposes me will fall. Not in a Communistic way as Ivan 'I'll Hold Back Every Wrestler In OSW' Stanislav. But in the ways which you all will no doubt despise. Do I care? No.
