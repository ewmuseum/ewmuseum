Strain

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-14-02 “Damn it!” Yells a familiar voice, “I told you once, I don’t wish to have this god forsaken wheelchair in my presence!” Slowly running around and into the mind, as we listen ever so carefully to the thoughts running through the mind. A sudden jolt, a sudden spark shown flashing through the almost completely covered terrain. Scenes of morbid visions and great pain staking agonies. “Get me a walker! A cane! Get me anything damn it! Just get this thing out of my sight!” Jesse shouts as his veins almost pop out of his neck. A doctor runs into the scene and finds Jesse yelling at a nurse who is almost in tears, “Mr. Ramey, please clam yourself down.” Jesse twists himself to the doctor and stares at him with the sinister look upon his face, “Me? You want me to settle down?” Jesse asks in a very evil way as his voice begins to shoot through the roof once more. “I will not settle down until you get me some other than this contraption to move myself around in!” Jesse picks up a metal bedpan from his bed and tosses it at the wheelchair. “I will not lower myself to pushing myself around in that thing!” Jesse lowers his voice and grabs the doctor by his shirt collar, “Either you go and find me a walker right now, or else I’ll kick your ass right here. Now, do you comprehend that?” Jesse asks as he releases the doctor. The doctor begins to back up as he continues to stare Jesse right in the eyes, the fear growing more and more as he reaches down and tries to bring the young nurse, who is now in tears along with him. “Ye…Ye…Yes, sir.” He says as he makes his way out of the room. “Then be gone!” Jesse yells as the doctor makes his way out of the room. He twists himself around to the window and tries to pull himself over into a chair that is pointing toward the window and gives an astonishing look out into the city of Chicago. Finally managing to make his way over to the chair, Jesse begins to look out the window and just look. “Do these people not know who I am?” Jesse asks as he runs his hand through his hair, now with the light shinning on his face, his full beard and blood shot eyes are in complete vision. “Do they not know that this,” Jesse pauses and just begins to shake his head, “My life! It’s all I have,” as he hands his head low and a tear begins to fill his eye. A knock at the door, “Mr. Ramey,” a soft voice comes. “We have your walker, sir. The doctor and nurse on patrol didn’t wish to burden you any longer, so they asked me to come and give it to you.” “Just leave it and thank you.” Jesse says as he begins to wave his hand in the air, motioning for her to leave. “I was informed that I was to make sure you could make your way to the walker and walk freely, sir. I’m not allowed to leave until you do so.” The nurse says protesting. “Did you not understand what I just said or am I going to have to tell you just like I told your co-workers?” Jesse says as he tries to turn around in his chair and very quickly slips and falls into the floor with an enormous thud. “God damn it!” Jesse shouts as he tries to make his way back up. The nurse runs over to Jesse, “Sir, please let me help you.” “Back up!” Jesse screams. “What?” The nurse asks. “I said, back up! I don’t need your help and I sure as hell don’t need anyone else’s help.” Jesse says as he begins to lift himself up in the bed, “I can do it myself.” Jesse slides across the bed and grabs the walker and places it in front of him. He pushes one leg out and then the other. He pushes himself up with a strain and a sudden bursting yelp as he begins to slowly walk and smile at the nurse. “I don’t need you, or anyone else.” ~The End~
