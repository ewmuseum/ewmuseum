Damn Kids...

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 May 2002 We see the sun illuminating a bright blue days sky, the camera lowers to see in the middle of a street is a sea of cars all shapes and sizes in the compounds of an approximate five foot high steel barriers. But on the hood of the rusty, old, LDV Van sits the OSW International Champion; The Power Himself; God's Ultimatum: Propaganda. Next to him sits his manager; Eric Sagar, with the International Title resting on his lap. 'Lovely day.' Sagar states. 'Wouldn't you say Props?' Propaganda nods; he seems to be bedazzled with the sun's rays. The glasses magnify the thoughts in his eyes. 'What is wrong?' concerns the 5'4' preacher. 'Well my brother…' roars the power himself 'It's that Banshee.' 'Lillith?' Eric queries. 'Yes.' Propaganda states 'She's… something else.' 'Don't worry about it.' Eric Sagar smirks. 'She's just another stepping stone - to be squashed by the mighty foot of Christianity. Another Satanist to be destroyed at our hands. If they don't step in line, and see the error of their ways they shall fall, to the power of Christ.' 'No… Not that.' Propaganda moans. 'Then what is it my brother?' Sagar queries. 'She's a woman.' Propaganda gulps. 'You're not…' Sagar shoots upright and looks at Propaganda in horror. 'You know are you?' 'Oh heavens no!' Declares Propaganda. 'Good' Sagar sighs in relief. 'It's just, I've never fought a women before.' Propaganda mumbles. 'Oh don't worry, it's like fighting a skinny man who's very bad at wrestling. The Women's place in the kitchen Props. You need not worry.' Sagar scoffs. 'Yes, but she is no ordinary woman, is she Eric?' Propaganda states. 'No, she is a banshee! Though she should prove no threat to someone as righteous as you.' Sagar scoffs. 'True…' Propaganda mumbles. His face goes red then he quickly snatches the title from Sagar's lap and leaps to his feet. He places it over his shoulder and looks at Sagar and smiles proudly. 'I'M THE INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION OF OLD SCHOOL WRESTLING!' He roars. 'I'M GOD'S ULTIMATUM! THE POWER HIMSELF! AND NOBODY… BUT NOBODY CAN DENY THAT!' 'A-Men, My brother.' Sagar yells jumping to his feet! Then a boy who looks in his late teens walks past with baggy jeans, an 'Offspring' T-shirt and a baseball cap compressing his curly ginger hair. He has a face full of freckles and spots. His smile has a missing tooth in the centre. 'Yo-Yo-Yo.' Shouts the young boy. 'Are you the Proppa-G from OSW?' slurps the boy. 'It's Propaganda.' Corrects Sagar. 'And yes he is.' 'Ah, Wicked man! Wicked! Who's is tha' scrubby old van?' 'THAT HAPPENS TO BE MY VAN!' Yells the international champion. 'I HAVE HAD IT FOR TWENTY YEARS AND YOU HAST NO RIGHT TO DECLARE IT AS SCRUBBY!' He roars with anger shocking the boy. 'And you say wicked…' Mocks Sagar 'Well the wicked witch - Lillith shall fall, wicked is not a good thing my dear boy! You shall see my boy, you shall see.' He cackles evilly. 'Damn… you guys couldn't suck more if you had a straw!' The boy struts off cockily. 'BURN IN HELL!' Yells Propaganda - this startles the boy causing him to sprint away. 'Fools.' Declares Sagar. 'A-MEN!' Roars God's ultimatum as both men look at each smiling.
