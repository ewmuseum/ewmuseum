Large and in charge? Waste of fucking air...why does God let people get fat? Eugh.

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 17th, 2002 ((Note: For some reason I just got a grudge against fat people.......uhhh....right.)) I'm not apologizing. Would things sound better if I were a man? You're the one with the problem. Why don't you just deal with it? Would you like me better if I showed you my tits? We all feel the same way. I have no regrets. Just look in the mirror. I don't have to justify anything. I'm not like you. Why should I be? Fuck you too. Deal with it. You know what pisses me off? When everybody treats you differently just because of choice. Okay, so I hate anybody who speaks in ebonics, and that's a choice they make...but who DOESN'T hate those retards? Sure, I hate anybody who listens to Britney Spears, but who doesn't have an agenda against any girl under 14? Yeah yeah yeah, so I hate anybody who's anti-drug...but..well....they're just fucking morons! Oh, the goths...can't forget the goths. I hate those whiney little brats the most. When you've got every parenting group in the world after you, why not go a step further and have all their kids hate you too? Exactly! Fucking simple-minded peons suddenly despise me because I'm doing what comes naturally...displaying my talent, actually winning for once. That's right, kids...Amy Campbell is no longer in 'He said, she said' fights with nimrods who have enough free time on their hands to record their mothers in audio tapes that are supposedly of me having sex with their big, dark, dumb asses. Of course, I'm giving you parents another thing to bitch about right now...I'm HOPEFULLY going to be piss drunk after I top off this half-quart of over-proof rum. That's right, kiddies...Amy Campbell endorses Bacardi 151! It's the good stuff...nice, white, over-proof rum...just about as strong as you can get. See, for the last fifteen minutes, I've been sitting in this absolutely disgusting, dank little pub chasing it with some sort of carrot juice. The juice is disgusting, the 151 is...well...making my eyes water...yum yum, chugging cups of what may as well be puppy vomit. But hey, it's getting me drunk...the taste won't matter in a few minutes. But let's hope I can start taking it straight soon...forget the rum, this carrot juice is taking quite a toll on my stomach. Like, what the fuck...it's HEALTHY! Eugh! Ack...this dude beside me smells like..........what the FUCK does this lardy smell like? God knows...it's horrible, though. I can only thank God that the divinely bitter smell of the Bacardi is drowning it out. ''Hey there, cutie.'' Did he just speak to me? GOD!!! His breath wafted over here. Yeah, I had to look at him for that split second to nod, then turn back to my wonderful drinking. Yet, he relented. ''What's a sweet little thing like you doing in a part of town like this?'' ''Not you, so stop before you embarass yourself.'' The words basically slipped out of me...I wasn't in my right mind. But this was a fat, disgusting bastard and, in ANY state of mind, a fat guy is still a fat guy...meaning? He's gross!! ''ExcUSE me?'' ''You didn't hear? Chicks don't dig mountain ranges.'' I was pissed, as usual...being pissed wasn't helping, though. God only knew if this guy had a knife or a glock on him...but that buldge under his coat was most likely just a third tit or a container of Chunky Munky ice cream. ''There's a gym down the street. Sweat a little, maybe some of that's just water weight.'' Breath alert. This jiggly puff needed a Tic Tac in the worst way. ''You should have a lil' respect! It's more to love for someone worthwhile!'' ''Sure.'' I smiled, then easily went back to frowning amongst my slurred words. ''More for whatever circus reject comes by next time. Bye!'' I left five Canadian dollars on the dusty bar. Heh, there was gonna be one pissed employee when he saw that...damn us Canadians and our 'Two cents to the dollar' money. I walked out with a bottle of Bacardi that was almost half-empty, too...err...well...staggered out, but I'm more graceful than that, so if you ask me, I was striding out on a golden fucking pony. Yeah yeah...I was mean to the fat guy. Did it matter? He was fat, people that large are just wasting my oxygen to fuel their giant, useless masses of flesh. Don't like that? I'll pull 'A Corg', then... Bite me.
