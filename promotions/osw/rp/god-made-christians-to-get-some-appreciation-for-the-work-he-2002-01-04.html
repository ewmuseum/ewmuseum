God made Christians to get some appreciation for the work he does...then he made Satanists to fight the Christians so not only would he get some entertainment, but also to get rid of the Christians that had opinions...Old Age Pensioners I think they'

4 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-04-02 Place: Detroit, Michigan Date: Monday, 1st April 2002 Time: 11:48am EST ----------------------------------- “Hessian! Hessian! Dammit wake up man!” The shrill voice of Minion ring throughout the Chamber as Hessian awakes from a nightly slumber. The new OSW Tag Team Champion and Global Title Semi Finalist sits up in his 8ft Stone sarcophagus slightly disorientated. The GoliaTh shakes his head, and then looks down to his left…to Minion. “What in the Hell is wrong?” enquires the Satanic Behemoth. “I was taking a brisk morning walk, when I passed two guys…” “Oh Minion…you didn’t pay them to slap each other for you, did you?” Hessian butts in. “No! I walked past them, and as they were looking through the newspaper I overheard them………” “…Talking sweet nothings into each others ears?” again Hessian butts in, much to the frustration of Minion. “No dammit!!! They said that THE DEVIL was delivering a speech in the Joe Louis Arena!” spurts Minion excitedly. “Why the Joe Louis Arena?” asks Hessian. “Ahem…because it’s 5:32am GMT and you can’t think of any Conference centres in Detroit, so you went with the lazy bastard option…” replies Minion. “Ok, that sounds right. So what are you planning to do Minion?” Hessian tiredly pulls himself up and out of his sarcophagus. Stretching and cricking the bones in his neck, Hessian looks across to his Tag Team strap hanging on the wall, freshly pried from the cold, sticky hands of Civil Disobedience. Hessian lifts his shirt up to reveal a rather large bandage covering a hidden wound sustained on Slam! Tearing the corner of the bandage away, Hessian examines his abdominal wound. Blood. Exactly what he wants. Hessian inhales, holds it, then thrusts his index and middle fingers into the wound, and immediately withdraws. He keels over in sheer pain, having worsened the wound. Quickly smoothing the bandage back over the wound, Hessian takes his bloody fingers, and smears the excess on the shining gold of the belt. Minion looks on, bewildered. “Hessian…why???” he asks, oblivious as to Hessian’s painful action. “I felt like it………” replies Hessian, the groaning in his speech evident of his pain. Hessian holds up his belt and chuckles in pain, as before him, scrawled on the belt is a rough imprint of his Insignia – THE Angel of Death. ”Anyway…fancy getting the Devil’s autograph?” Hessian asks, before hanging his belt back on the wall, and standing fully on his feet. “Ok…let us go then.” replies Minion. ----------------------------------- Through the streets of Detroit the two members of Heresian Bloodlust stroll, Hessian in pain, and Minion in wonder, as various blondes strut past, flaunting their curves and fancies. Never a man to take a cab, Hessian strolls on, the pain of his wound vibrating through his whole body. Closer and closer Hessian and Minion get to the Joe Louis Arena, the thoughts of the Devil himself ripe in their minds. “So Minion…” asks Hessian, “What exactly did you hear those two men say about the Devil?” He yawns deeply, and awaits a reply. “They just said he was delivering a speech that would change the face of the modern age as of today, and that there would be free punch and pie.” replies Minion. “Sounds like the Devil.” adds Hessian. “By the by, where exactly did Azrael and Caleb go after Slam?” asks Minion. “Azrael claimed there was no time to waste in preparing Caleb for the fight next week…” “Homeless motherf*cker gets all the fun…” Minion replies under his breath. ----------------------------------- Finally Hessian and Minion stop outside the Joe Louis Arena. 12:17am They enter the vast arena, careless of any costs they may have to make. Into the large lobby they walk, past gawping businessmen, surprised Promoters and intimidated Sportsmen. Stopping at the ticket office, Minion jumps up onto the counter, and finds himself staring deep into the eyes of another behemoth: “Bessie the Ticket Collector”. “May I help you?” asks Bessie. “Yes actually, a couple of things. Firstly, why is this RP 3 frickin’ pages long on MS Word when it was supposed to be 1 page? Also, can you tell us where the Devil is giving his speech today?” asks Minion. “Firstly…I don’t think your exceptionally large friend there can write a short RP, and secondly, the “Devil” as you call him is giving a presentation in Hall 4…two tickets, 1 adult and 1 minority, that’ll be $34.99 please…” replies Bessie. “Is there even a Hall 4 in the Joe Louis Arena??? I thought it was just…an arena…?” asks Minion. “How should I know? Your exceptionally large friend over there hasn’t been to Detroit before, much less the Joe Louis Arena, plus Hall 4 sounds less generic.” replies Bessie, simply not caring about the questions asked by the Imp. “Ah, very good. Ok thank you.” says Minion, throwing the $35 oh-so PWAishly to Bessie, before grabbing the tickets and running over to Hessian. “Ok I got them let’s go.” ----------------------------------- Hessian and Minion waltz down a hallway, taking in the fresh pine smell. On and on they go, until they stop outside the door for Hall 4. They hand their tickets to the pimply-faced collector kid, but before they go in Hessian looks to Minion in a confused fashion. “I never knew there was a Hall 4 in the Joe Louis Arena…” he asks. “Look its 6:05am GMT, let’s just go in and see the Devil.” replies Minion. Hessian and Minion wait for a couple of seconds, adrenaline pumping through their veins, chills running down their spines at the prospects of meeting the big man himself, the man, the myth, the legend… The PFCK opens the door for them, and as Hessian and Minion step inside, they hear the echoing, evil voice of the most evil man ever, the Devil. “Ladies and Gentlemen. I am Bill Gates…today I would like to unveil to you our new project…new for Windows XP V.2.0, Microsoft WWF - We Won’t Fuck-it-up-this-time…………………………” Fin
