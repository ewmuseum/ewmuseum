Introspection

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 9, 2002 Jesus St Crux sat at a table by himself, a half empty glass of beer sitting in front of him. He didn't pay attention to the other patrons in Mohammed's Tap and Grill; he was a bit too focused on current events. Current events in the OSW that were just a tad to similar to events nearly a year ago in another federation. The Mexican took another swig of his beer and then leaned back, his gaze casting over the old UWA Towers building across the street. 'Always with the controversy...' Jesus stared at the glass for a second then set it back down on the table. He didn't want to go through the controversy again. Jesus enjoyed the sport of wrestling; the challenge of matching skill, speed, and strength to see on any particular day who's the better man. In Mexico, that's how things work, there's a honor between the wrestlers. But here in America... interferences ran rampant through the UWA and controversy abounded; sometimes, his passions got the better of him and he joined the... insanity. The UWA had eventually folded, and after a while, Jesus had signed with OSW, believing the tagline - 'The Way Wrestling Was Meant To Be'. Shaking his head, Jesus finished his beer. The way wrestling was meant to be... His first match in the new federation, Jesus St Crux had hoped to make a fresh start, even if it was against the friend who betrayed him in the waning days of the UWA. But Kanaida... The Northern Wonder... Jesus St Crux started tapping his glass against the table, as if using the beat to gather his thoughts. It was happening again, people interfering in other people's matches. For apparently no better reason than... than... nothing Jesus could think of. He replaced the galls on the table and dropped a five from his wallet to cover it. At least he and Winters had agreed on one thing. Kanaida's interference wasn't going to change things. They were going to finish what was started; they were going to have a one-on-one match, no matter how often they had to try. Old School Wreslting... Jesus was going to do his best to make sure it became wrestling the way it was meant to be.
