In case you ever thought Aimz WASN'T a dweeb...(Fixed)

18 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 18th, 2002 ''Well, he is pretty hot...'' Amy Campbell's voice rang out, clear as a bell, into the reciever of a silver Nokia cell phone. Her feet, complete with 'rocker'-style leather, fire-tipped calve buckle boots, were propped against the dark dashboard of a little silver Audi TT coupe, one arm splayed out the window, swinging leisurely at the elbow. ''...what? No...I've got Rentie, I don't ne---'' She paused, interrupted by the seemingly chirpy voice barely audible on the other line, adjusting the waist of her Guess cargo khakis. ''...welllll...I could definitely use to get a bit more...ummm...action...on the road and all, plus he's a really sweet guy...'' ''But...'' This time it was Amy to interrupt. ''...it's a screwy thing to have on your mind, right now I've really gotta think of how I can keep away from that Stanislav maniac.'' Again, she silenced...waiting out, listening to the voice on the line. ''Yeah, I'll be at Slam to help keep shit in line, make sure nobody gets too messed up and all. Take care, 'kay?'' Another pause, this time as the pleasantries were dispensed with. ''...yep, see ya!'' If she hadn't been talking to either Bre, Renee or another one of her girly druggie buddies still stuck in their High School antics, it hadn't been very obvious.Campbell had never really been one to 'Talk boys', but she'd been doing so...though she kept slightly vague with details, probably not wanting to reveal exactly WHO she was talking about, as if it weren't painfully obvious to the world already. Awww...so what? She had a little 'thing' for Reno Davis, one of those schoolgirl-ish crushes, if you will. What was the best part? As far as anybody could tell, he had absolutely NO clue what she thought about him. Well what the hell did you expect, anyway? He was a real punker, he had great taste, better looks and a fuzzy monkey...what more could a girl like Amy ask for? Oh shut up, she hadn't seen exactly how sizeable his 'endowments' were...so MAYBE she had something else to ask for... BLAH! He was at her hotel, had to get a drive back with her after Ivan mangled the poor guy's car... What in the name of God was running around her head? This was insanity...but almost the type she liked...definitely an intruiging situation. But, in a sudden turn of events...and a glance at the dashboard radio clock... ''Oh shit, he's gonna be ready in half an hour!!!!'' She ended up bolting the little red Jetta back to the Holiday Inn, it was just a fluke that the girl wasn't pulled over for speeding...this was quite the evening! When Amy made it to the parking lot, she didn't even lock the car...just jumped out, ran into the lobby and practically flew into her room, grabbing a luggage case. With that opened, the redhead dumped every single article of clothing inside onto the cheapo bed... The shower was running, which meant he wouldn't be long now... ''Tight...gotta have ti---'' She looked over a pair of uncomfortable looking vinyl pants, ''...no, too tight.'' ''Short? No!!!....must...be...perfect......'' By the time she found the right pants, the grey carpeting was covered in various materials. ''Muahaaaa!!!!!!! Plaid...he's punk, he'll like it...yaaaaay...'' Okay, so maybe she was getting a little TOO girly. None-the-less, she grabbed the gold, red, green and white plaid, semi-tight pants as she tore the 'Guess?' khakis off... Alrighty, the pants were in place... ''Shirt!'' And the search was on... This was getting a little too crazy, she had even turned down her favorite Metallica hoodie...trading the usual, rather extravagant, apparel for a simple black long sleeved t-shirt...chosen probably for the cling-factor. Or not... Just as she put the shirt on and sat down on a little fake leather sofa in the corner of the room, flicking the TV on, the door to the bathroom swung open, revealing Reno Davis wearing a towel around his waist. He shook his head violently to dry his hair and then looked over at Aimz. Long red locks hung down over his bright blue eyes, accenting his sharp featured face. Reno looked around the room and saw Aimz's clothes thrown everywhere. He rubbed his eyes a little and shrugged it off, walking forward towards the couch where Aimz sat. He flopped down right beside her, dawned in only a towel. Aimz watched the TV and pretended not to notice Reno, who didn't seem to care about his lack of clothing. ''Hey.'' ''Ummm...uhh...'' 'Regain your composure, damnit!', she ordered herself. ''...hey. Didja' sleep well?'' Well, if we didn't think she could get flakier...that question probably topped the ladder of lame. Reno shrugged and slid his legs over Aimz's, laying his head against the cushioned couch. His towel slid open a little bit, but Reno didn't notice. ''I guess so, I'm still a little sore from Ivan's bullshit though.'' 'He's just BEGGING me to jump him! AAAAARRRRGH!!!!!!!' ''Ah...yeah, I'm pretty used to getting my ass kicked...pain isn't a new concept, hehe.'' Hey missy, keep the eyes on his! Holy shit, she was acting like a teenaged boy! ''So...err...yeah....'' ''Yyyup...What the hell are you watching?'' Oh, how badly she wanted to say something so incredibly disturbed....................but instead, she was forced to actually look at the TV for a minute, lowering her head in slight shame. ''Apparently I'm watching...Spongebob Squarepants.....................'' ''Cool...'' And the awkward silence ensued...only until Amy forced herself to pick things up... ''You're Straight-Edge, right?'' 'Uh huh.' ''Well...'' She shifted a bit. ''I've been thinking about going that way for certain. I mean, I'm part way there and all...I've got the no booze, no drugs thing down...but doesn't it also mean no sex? That's a little harsh, don't you think?'' He laughed. ''Heh...No, it's no sex until you're ready to handle the consequences of it.'' We've discovered a term foreign to Amy Campbell! ''Con..se......quen...ces?'' ''Yeah, I mean it would suck if you had to stop wrestling because you got pregnant.'' ''Pah! In my world, contraceptives were invented for two reason...to piss Catholics off and to keep celebrities, like wrestlers, safe from these consequence thingies.'' The redhead smiled at Reno, who's hair color was actually identical. ''I don't think I ever need to worry about pregnancy when I don't want it or anything like that...thank God for technology. And now that I know Straight-Edge means you CAN have sex...I'm SO there! Baha!'' Reno laughed and re-adjusted his position on the couch. ''Hey, pass me the remote.'' Reno grabbed the remote control and started flicking through the channels. He stopped changing channels when he saw the old school Spider-Man cartoon. ''Whoo yeah! Spider-Man rules!'' ''Yep...'' Aimz smiled at Davis yet again. ''...I reeeeeally wanna see the movie, but most of my friends despise the thought, apparently it's too mainstream...damn them......'' ''I saw it opening night! Sucker...'' 'Sucker'? She wished! Reno continued to watch as The Vulture magically fired a rocket out of his wing and hit the ground near Spidey. Spider-Man flew into a wall and watched as the Vulture flew away. ''Dammit! Use a Spider-Tracer!'' Davis leaped up from the couch and started to yell at the TV, then the towel dropped from his waist, much to Aimz' wide-eyed amusement... ''You know.........'' Campbell was stumbling for words, ''A lot of people would do a lot of things if put in this situation.....unfortunately rape's illegal, and my record's big enough as is.'' She laughed, trying to lighten her own mood more than his. ''Uh...Can I have that back?'' The Unwanted Angel's angel was quickly covered by his left hand as he reached with his right towards Amy, who was holding his towel. 'Damnit! I hoped he wouldn't notice it was gone. Errr...uhhh...' ''Hmm? Oh, this!'' She gestured with his towel before tossing it at him. ''Uhhh...yeah!'' Davis reached out with both hands and caught the towel, which flew above his head. ''Thanks...'' Davis then realized where his hand WASN'T. ''...uh...shit. Could her eyes have POSSIBLY bugged out more? She had finally been able to judge, nothing more to ask! Muahaaaa... ''Again...'' She gulped, still quite astounded. ''You are VERY lucky my criminal record's as big as it is.........'' Reno wrapped the towel around his waist, his face as bright as his hair. He backed away uncomfortably and tried to nonchalantly walk back to the bathroom, but he bumped into several things along the way. The bathroom door swung shut and Aimz was alone again. ''Well.........that went........well.......'' She was smacking herself on the inside, really. ''..........time to call Bre and announce what a dork I've been today!''
