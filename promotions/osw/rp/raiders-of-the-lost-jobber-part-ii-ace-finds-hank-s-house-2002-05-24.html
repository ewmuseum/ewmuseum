Raiders of the lost jobber Part II: Ace finds Hank's house

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 24th 2002 Date: 8 days ago **I remember those days when Ace got lost in the desert, on search for the lost jobber...** 'Who???' **HANK, damn it, HANK~!** 'Ah, cool...' **Now, that was only the end of his journey. The start had been many weeks early, when he decided he needed to find Hank to know how to be a jobber. Only that way he could beat Troy. But the truth is, the journey didn't make him any good. Sebastian Toomes, the OSW biggest pussy, managed to steal Ace's victory. In the end, that proved costly. Now, both men, men that hate each other, have to face 6 other men, complete OSW superstars... With Toomes tagging with him! Just the thought, as I remember, made me mad. What the hell were the OSW suits thinking? They knew of the bad blood between these two, they knew all about it going back to 2001, and now... They were forced to team up? I was simply mad at it...** **And yet, Ace was calm...** **The scene changes from the desert shot to a shot of a big forest area somewhere in America. The day is cloudy and rainy, thunders and lightnings can be heard on the distance. The rain, like sharp cold needles, pierce the leaves and strike the floor, in a moronic symphony of sounds. The camera shows a man in the distance, walking with a rain coat covering his body and a backpack on his back. His step is difficult, but he manages to dribble the slippery stones and leave beds around. After a few seconds of walking through the trees, big old oaks, strong and thick, the man stands in front of a small wooden house. The camera starts moving around to catch his face and finds out he's none other than CCW living legend, Andrew 'Ace' Hawk, who's not been around for the past 2 weeks, except for the SLAM appearances. The scene shows him walking towards the house, as he finally is relieved, as we can see from his expression. The house looks abandoned, though. Ace knocks on the door, no answer. He knocks on it again, again no answer. Finally, he decides to try the knob, and the door opens. Ace walks in fast, tyring to dodge the rain. The cabin is a rustic cabin, with almost no furniture. We can see a brick fireplace across the room, completely out. It's dirty, though, showing the cabin has been used a lot. To the left, a small round wooden table and chair, a few papers over it. To the right, a small bed and a Bowflex. Ace looks around, when suddenly a phone rings. Ace reaches for his backpack, where he gets a sattelite cell phone from it. He turns it on:** 'Hello' 'Hi, Ace', answers a feminine voice in the back. 'Oh, hi, Debbie. How are you?', answers Ace, with a renewed happiness when he hears about Debbie Carmichael, CCW superstar and his former girl. 'I'm fine, Ace... I'm worried about you. You haven't been around... Where are you now?', answer Debbie on the other side of the phone, as Ace slowly walks to the wooden table and sits down. 'I'm looking for the lost jobber...', answers Ace, closing his eyes, almost knowing the answer... 'Who??', answers Deb, making the fatidic question. 'Hank. That PCW guy that defeated Doug Troy... He's famous and all...', answers Ace, visibly annoyed. 'Ah, Hank... I heard of him... Why the hell are you looking for him? It doesn't make any sense...', asks Deb, clueless. 'It's simple, girl. Hank is a guy who defeated the OSW Heavyweight Champion. I don't care what he did before or after that, he managed to defeat Doug Troy. Now, I want that OSW Title as much as anyone, that's why I'm going for the Ascencion triple cage, but until I find him and ask him how the hell he did it, there's no way I'll have a fair match against Doug in the future. He was a CCW fan, I was never a PCW fan...', answers Ace, in a down voice, as he starts looking at the papers in front of him. He finds one interesting and reads it out loud: 'Listen to this: Hank's mother was a waitress at Hotel California' 'Nice fact to know about, Ace... I'm here, worried about your safety, and you come and talk about Hank's momma? Damn, you're stupid.', answers Debbie, a little mad... 'Don't worry, I'm just...' Ace opens his eyes, as he looks at a small piece of paper in front of him. 'What's it, Ace?', Deb answers, getting no response from the shocked Ace... 'Venice...', Ace struggles to answer, staring at the piece of paper in front of him. 'What???', Debbie asks, not understanding it. 'Hank... is in Venice.', Ace answers it. 'What are you talking about?', Debbie asks. 'Deb, I need to go. I found him, I need to talk to him. We'll talk later.' **Ace hungs up on Deb and gets up, rushing, grabbing an envelope on the table before he zooms out. The camera zooms on the letter, and the letter says:** Montana, March 23rd 2002 Dear Danielle, My wrestling days are over. PCW has finally killed me yet again, and this time it's a done deal. They fired me and now I'm here, with my career done in the US. I want to go and find you, so we can go back to the days of our lives, and I'm gonna do it. I have found a small apartment in Venice, and there, I'm gonna find work on the European wrestling circuit. The address is the return address on the envelope. As soon as I can find a job in Europe, we can start planning on our wedding. I'll see you in two weeks. Love you Hank. **The scene fades to Ace, running through the woods, as Dick narrates it** **As Ace's friend, I remember him, as he finally found where Hank could be. Although he was a little mad because he didn't find him in Montana, we all knew he was actually glowing from the inside, finally realizing he could find Hank and ask him the magic question. How he could beat Troy? Now, looking back at this whole thing, I look at Montana and realize it was nothing near what he was going to have to go through. You don't know what I'm talking about? You'll see...**
